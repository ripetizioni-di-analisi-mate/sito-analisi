The midpoint of segment $AB$ is $M(2, 1)$, and point $A$ has coordinates $(-1, 4)$. What are the coordinates of $B$?

- $(-4, 7)$
- $(3, -3)$
- $\left( \dfrac{1}{2}, \dfrac{5}{2} \right)$
- $(5, -2)$

## Explanation

The coordinates of the **midpoint** are the averages of the corresponding endpoint coordinates, so writing $B(x, y)$ gives $\dfrac{-1 + x}{2} = 2$ and $\dfrac{4 + y}{2} = 1$. It follows that $x = 5$ and $y = -2$. In other words, going from $A$ to $M$ increases the horizontal coordinate by $3$ and decreases the vertical coordinate by $3$. The same displacement takes us from $M$ to $B$. The coordinates of point $(3, -3)$ give the displacement itself, and point $\left( \dfrac{1}{2}, \dfrac{5}{2} \right)$ is the midpoint of $A$ and $M$.
