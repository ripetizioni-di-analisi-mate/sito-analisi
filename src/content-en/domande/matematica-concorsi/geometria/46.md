Two lines intersect, and one of the angles measures $137^\circ$. What is the measure, in degrees, of the adjacent angle with which it forms a straight angle?

- $137$
- $53$
- $43$
- $47$

## Explanation

The two outer rays are opposite, so they form a straight angle of $180^\circ$. The adjacent angle measures $180^\circ-137^\circ=43^\circ$.
