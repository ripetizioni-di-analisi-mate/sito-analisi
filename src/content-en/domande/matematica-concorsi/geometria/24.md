A circular track has radius $50$ m. An athlete completes $4$ laps. Using $\pi \approx 3.14$, how many meters does the athlete travel?

- $628$ m
- $2512$ m
- $314$ m
- $1256$ m

## Explanation

In one lap the athlete travels the **circumference** of the track, $2\pi r = 2 \cdot 3.14 \cdot 50 = 314$ m. In $4$ laps the athlete therefore travels $4 \cdot 314 = 1256$ m. The value $2512$ m uses the formula $2\pi r$ with the diameter of $100$ m in place of the radius, and $628$ m uses $\pi r$, omitting the factor $2$. The value $314$ m is the length of one lap.
