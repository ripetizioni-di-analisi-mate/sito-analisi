A right triangle has an acute angle of $30^\circ$ and a longer leg measuring $6\sqrt{3}$ cm. What is its perimeter?

- $(18 + 6\sqrt{3})$ cm
- $(9 + 9\sqrt{3})$ cm
- $(18 + 18\sqrt{3})$ cm
- $(12 + 6\sqrt{3})$ cm

## Explanation

A right triangle with acute angles of $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$ is half an equilateral triangle. If $c$ is the hypotenuse, the shorter leg is $\dfrac{c}{2}$ and the longer leg is $\dfrac{c\sqrt{3}}{2}$. From $\dfrac{c\sqrt{3}}{2} = 6\sqrt{3}$ we get $c = 12$ cm, so the shorter leg measures $6$ cm. The perimeter is $6 + 6\sqrt{3} + 12 = 18 + 6\sqrt{3}$ cm. The value $(9 + 9\sqrt{3})$ cm treats $6\sqrt{3}$ as the hypotenuse, and $(18 + 18\sqrt{3})$ cm treats it as the shorter leg.
