A rectangular screen has a width-to-height ratio of $4 : 3$ and a diagonal of $100$ cm. What is its perimeter?

- $280$ cm
- $140$ cm
- $200$ cm
- $350$ cm

## Explanation

Let $4k$ be the width and $3k$ the height. The diagonal is the hypotenuse of the right triangle with the width and height as its legs. By the **Pythagorean theorem**, the diagonal measures $\sqrt{16k^2 + 9k^2} = 5k$. From $5k = 100$ we get $k = 20$, so the screen measures $80$ cm by $60$ cm and its perimeter is $2 \cdot (80 + 60) = 280$ cm. The value $140$ cm is the semiperimeter. The value $200$ cm treats the diagonal as the sum of the width and height. The value $350$ cm takes the diagonal as the width and uses a height of $75$ cm.
