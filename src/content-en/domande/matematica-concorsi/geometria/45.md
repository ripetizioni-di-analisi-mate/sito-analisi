The line $y = -2x + 8$ and the coordinate axes form a triangle. What is its area?

- $32$
- $8$
- $12$
- $16$

## Explanation

The line crosses the $y$-axis at $(0, 8)$ because its intercept is $q = 8$. It crosses the $x$-axis where $-2x + 8 = 0$, at point $(4, 0)$. The triangle therefore has a right angle at the origin and legs of lengths $4$ and $8$, so its area is $\dfrac{4 \cdot 8}{2} = 16$. The value $32$ omits division by $2$, and $12$ adds the leg lengths instead of multiplying them.
