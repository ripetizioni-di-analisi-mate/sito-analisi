Two lines intersect at a point and form four angles. One angle is three times an adjacent angle, one beside it with which it shares a side. What is the measure of the smaller of the two angles?

- $135^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $22.5^\circ$

## Explanation

The nonshared sides of two adjacent angles formed by intersecting lines lie on the same straight line. The angles therefore form a straight angle and are supplementary. Let $x$ be the smaller angle. Then $x + 3x = 180^\circ$, so $x = 45^\circ$ and the other angle measures $135^\circ$. The value $90^\circ$ comes from $x + 3x = 360^\circ$, which assigns a full turn to just two of the angles.
