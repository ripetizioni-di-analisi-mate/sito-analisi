Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One interior angle measures $64^\circ$. What is the measure, in degrees, of the other interior angle on the same side of the transversal?

- $26$
- $116$
- $64$
- $126$

## Explanation

Same-side interior angles between parallel lines are supplementary. The required angle measures $180^\circ-64^\circ=116^\circ$.
