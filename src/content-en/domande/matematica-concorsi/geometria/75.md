A rectangular room measures $10$ m by $15$ m. What area in square centimeters does it occupy on a floor plan drawn to a scale of $1:250$?

- $24$
- $12$
- $60$
- $6$

## Explanation

The actual lengths are $1000$ cm and $1500$ cm. Dividing each by $250$ gives $4$ cm and $6$ cm on the plan. The area on the plan is therefore $4\cdot6=24\ \mathrm{cm}^2$.
