A regular hexagon has side length $6$ cm. What is the distance in centimeters between two opposite sides?

- $12$
- $6\sqrt{3}$
- $3\sqrt{3}$
- $6$

## Explanation

Joining the center to the vertices gives six equilateral triangles with side length $6$ cm. By the **Pythagorean theorem**, the distance from the center to a side is $\sqrt{6^2-3^2}=3\sqrt{3}$ cm. Opposite sides are twice this far apart, or $6\sqrt{3}$ cm.
