At twelve o'clock the minute and hour hands of a clock coincide. If both move continuously at constant rates, after how many minutes will they first form a right angle?

- $18$
- $15$
- $\dfrac{180}{13}$
- $\dfrac{180}{11}$

## Explanation

The minute hand advances $6^\circ$ per minute and the hour hand $0.5^\circ$ per minute. Their angular separation therefore increases by $5.5^\circ$ per minute. They first form a right angle after $90/5.5=\dfrac{180}{11}$ minutes.
