The base of a rectangle increases by $20\%$ and its height decreases by $20\%$. How does its area change?

- It stays the same
- It increases by $4\%$
- It decreases by $4\%$
- It decreases by $2\%$

## Explanation

The base is multiplied by $1.2$ and the height by $0.8$. Since the area is base times height, the area is multiplied by $1.2 \cdot 0.8 = 0.96$ and thus **decreases** by $4\%$. The answer “It stays the same” adds the two percentage changes, $+20\% - 20\% = 0$, whereas the scale factors for two quantities in a product must be multiplied.
