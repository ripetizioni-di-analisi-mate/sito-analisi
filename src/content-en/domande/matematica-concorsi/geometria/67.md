A rectangular frame has an outer perimeter of $58$ cm. The long side is $7$ cm longer than the short side. What is the length of the long side in centimeters?

- $11$
- $22$
- $25$
- $18$

## Explanation

The long and short side lengths add up to the semiperimeter, $29$ cm. If $x$ is the length of the long side, then $x+(x-7)=29$ and $x=18$.
