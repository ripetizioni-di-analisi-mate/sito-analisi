A rectangular sheet of cardboard measures $15$ cm by $12$ cm. A square of side length $2$ cm is cut from each of its four corners. What is the remaining area in square centimeters?

- $148$
- $172$
- $156$
- $164$

## Explanation

The original rectangle has area $15\cdot12=180\ \mathrm{cm}^2$. Removing the four squares subtracts $4\cdot2^2=16\ \mathrm{cm}^2$, leaving $164\ \mathrm{cm}^2$.
