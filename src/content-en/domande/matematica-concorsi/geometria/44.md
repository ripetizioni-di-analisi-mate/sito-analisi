Which of the following points lies on the line $y = 3x - 5$?

- $(4, 7)$
- $(7, 4)$
- $(0, 5)$
- $(2, -1)$

## Explanation

A point lies on the line if its coordinates satisfy the equation. For $(4, 7)$ we have $3 \cdot 4 - 5 = 7$, so the point lies on the line. The point $(7, 4)$ interchanges the horizontal and vertical coordinates, and indeed $3 \cdot 7 - 5 = 16$. The point $(0, 5)$ reverses the sign of the y-intercept, which is $-5$. For $(2, -1)$, we get $3 \cdot 2 - 5 = 1$, which differs from $-1$.
