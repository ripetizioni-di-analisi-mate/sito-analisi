Four rays with a common endpoint divide a full turn into four consecutive sectors. The first three measure $55^\circ$, $110^\circ$, and $85^\circ$. What is the measure of the fourth sector in degrees?

- $125$
- $90$
- $70$
- $110$

## Explanation

The four sectors together form an angle of $360^\circ$. The fourth measures $360^\circ-(55^\circ+110^\circ+85^\circ)=110^\circ$.
