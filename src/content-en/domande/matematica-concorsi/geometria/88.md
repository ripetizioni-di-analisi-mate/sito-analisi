A rectangular prism measuring $30$ cm by $18$ cm by $12$ cm is divided entirely into identical cubes. Their edges are parallel to the edges of the prism, have a whole-number length in centimeters, and are as long as possible. How many cubes are formed?

- $30$
- $15$
- $36$
- $60$

## Explanation

The edge length of a cube must divide all three dimensions. The greatest common divisor is $6$ cm, so $5$, $3$, and $2$ cubes fit along the respective edges. This gives $5\cdot3\cdot2=30$ cubes in all.
