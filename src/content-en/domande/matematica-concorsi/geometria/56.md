A parallelogram has area $96\ \mathrm{cm}^2$. The side chosen as its base measures $8$ cm. What is the corresponding height in centimeters?

- $8$
- $6$
- $16$
- $12$

## Explanation

The area of a parallelogram is its base times the corresponding height. Dividing $96$ by $8$ gives a height of $12$ cm.
