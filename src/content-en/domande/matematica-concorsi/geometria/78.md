A rectangular container has an internal base measuring $50$ cm by $30$ cm. A total of $12$ liters of water, equivalent to $12\,000\ \mathrm{cm}^3$, is poured in. What depth does the water reach in centimeters?

- $8$
- $4$
- $15$
- $12$

## Explanation

The internal base has area $50\cdot30=1500\ \mathrm{cm}^2$. Dividing the volume by this area gives a water depth of $12\,000/1500=8$ cm.
