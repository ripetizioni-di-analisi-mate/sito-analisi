A pointer moves from $24^\circ$ to $61^\circ$ on the same scale, rotating toward increasing values without completing a full turn. Through how many degrees does it rotate?

- $37$
- $24$
- $85$
- $49$

## Explanation

Both readings on the scale are measured from the same starting point. The angle through which the pointer turns is $61^\circ-24^\circ=37^\circ$.
