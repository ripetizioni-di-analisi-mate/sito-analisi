All four vertices of a square lie on a circle of radius $5$ cm. What is the area of the square?

- $100\ \text{cm}^2$
- $25\ \text{cm}^2$
- $200\ \text{cm}^2$
- $50\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The diagonals of the square meet at the center of the circle and have endpoints on the circle. Each is therefore a diameter of length $10$ cm. As with any rhombus, the area of a square is half the product of its diagonals, so the area is $\dfrac{10 \cdot 10}{2} = 50\ \text{cm}^2$. The value $100\ \text{cm}^2$ takes the diameter as the side length, giving the area of the square whose sides are tangent to the circle. The value $25\ \text{cm}^2$ uses the radius as the side length.
