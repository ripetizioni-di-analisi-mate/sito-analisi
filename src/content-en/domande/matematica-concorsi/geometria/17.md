A parallelogram has a base of $15$ cm, a slanted side of $10$ cm, and a height of $8$ cm relative to that base. What is its area?

- $150\ \text{cm}^2$
- $60\ \text{cm}^2$
- $75\ \text{cm}^2$
- $120\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The **area of a parallelogram** is the base times its corresponding height, so it equals $15 \cdot 8 = 120\ \text{cm}^2$. The slanted side is not needed. The value $150\ \text{cm}^2$ multiplies the base by the slanted side, which is longer than the height. The value $60\ \text{cm}^2$ divides by $2$ as in the area formula for a triangle.
