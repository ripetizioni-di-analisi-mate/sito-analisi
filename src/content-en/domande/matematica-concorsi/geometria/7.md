Each base angle of an isosceles triangle measures $52^\circ$. What is the measure of the vertex angle?

- $128^\circ$
- $64^\circ$
- $76^\circ$
- $38^\circ$

## Explanation

In an isosceles triangle the base angles are equal, and the **sum of the interior angles** is $180^\circ$. The vertex angle is therefore $180^\circ - 2 \cdot 52^\circ = 76^\circ$. The value $128^\circ$ subtracts just one base angle from $180^\circ$. The value $64^\circ$ interchanges the roles of the angles by treating $52^\circ$ as the vertex angle and calculating $\dfrac{180^\circ - 52^\circ}{2}$.
