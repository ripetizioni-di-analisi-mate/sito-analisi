In the following expressions, $r$ and $h$ are lengths in centimeters. Which expression can represent a volume in $\text{cm}^3$?

- $2\pi r h$
- $\dfrac{\pi r^2 h}{3}$
- $4\pi r^2$
- $\pi r + 2h$

## Explanation

A volume is a product of three lengths, and $\dfrac{\pi r^2 h}{3}$ contains the three length factors $r \cdot r \cdot h$. In fact, it is the volume of the cone. The expressions $2\pi r h$ and $4\pi r^2$ are products of two lengths, so they give areas in $\text{cm}^2$, the lateral surface area of a cylinder and the surface area of a sphere, respectively. The expression $\pi r + 2h$ is a sum of lengths, so it gives a length in cm.
