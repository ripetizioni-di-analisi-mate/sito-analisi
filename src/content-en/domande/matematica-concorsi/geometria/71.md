A decoration is shaped like a semicircle with radius $7$ cm. What is its perimeter in centimeters, counting both the arc and the straight diameter?

- $7\pi$
- $7\pi+14$
- $7\pi+7$
- $14\pi+14$

## Explanation

The arc of the semicircle is half the circumference, or $7\pi$ cm. The diameter adds $14$ cm.
