The diagonals of a rhombus measure $30$ cm and $16$ cm. What is its perimeter?

- $92$ cm
- $46$ cm
- $34$ cm
- $68$ cm

## Explanation

The diagonals of a rhombus are perpendicular and bisect each other, so each side is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs $15$ cm and $8$ cm long. By the **Pythagorean theorem**, a side measures $\sqrt{225 + 64} = \sqrt{289} = 17$ cm, and the perimeter is $4 \cdot 17 = 68$ cm. The value $46$ cm is the sum of the diagonals, and $92$ cm is the perimeter of a rectangle with those diagonals as its side lengths. The value $34$ cm counts two sides instead of four.
