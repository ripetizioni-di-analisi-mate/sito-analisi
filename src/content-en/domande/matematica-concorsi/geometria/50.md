Three straight rods are needed to make the frame of a triangular sign. Which triple of lengths, in the same unit, can form a closed triangle with positive area?

- $6,\ 8,\ 13$
- $5,\ 6,\ 12$
- $4,\ 7,\ 11$
- $3,\ 5,\ 9$

## Explanation

The **triangle inequality** requires the sum of the two shorter sides to exceed the longest. The triple $6$, $8$, $13$ satisfies $6+8>13$.
