An isosceles triangular support has two equal sides of length $13$ cm and a perimeter of $36$ cm. What is its base length in centimeters?

- $8$
- $23$
- $12$
- $10$

## Explanation

The two equal sides have a combined length of $2\cdot13=26$ cm. The base measures $36-26=10$ cm.
