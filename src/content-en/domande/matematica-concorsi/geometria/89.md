Points $A=(-3,2)$ and $B=(5,2)$ are the endpoints of the base of a triangle. The third vertex has coordinates $C=(1,k)$, with $k>2$. If the triangle has area $28$, what is $k$?

- $5$
- $9$
- $7$
- $14$

## Explanation

The horizontal base has length $8$, and the distance from $C$ to the base is $k-2$. The equation $8(k-2)/2=28$ gives $k=9$.
