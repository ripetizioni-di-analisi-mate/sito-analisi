A tent is shaped like a right triangular prism. The base triangle has a side of $2$ m and an altitude of $1.5$ m relative to that side. The tent is $3$ m long. What is its volume?

- $9\ \text{m}^3$
- $3\ \text{m}^3$
- $4.5\ \text{m}^3$
- $1.5\ \text{m}^3$

## Explanation

The volume of a **prism** is the base area times the height of the prism, which here is the length of the tent. The triangular base has area $\dfrac{2 \cdot 1.5}{2} = 1.5\ \text{m}^2$, so the volume is $1.5 \cdot 3 = 4.5\ \text{m}^3$. The value $9\ \text{m}^3$ omits division by $2$ when calculating the triangle's area, and $1.5\ \text{m}^3$ also divides by $3$, as if the solid were a pyramid.
