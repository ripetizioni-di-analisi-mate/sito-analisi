What is the slope of the line through $(1, 2)$ and $(4, 11)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $-3$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

The **slope** is the change in the vertical coordinate divided by the change in the horizontal coordinate, giving $m = \dfrac{11 - 2}{4 - 1} = \dfrac{9}{3} = 3$. The value $\dfrac{1}{3}$ reverses numerator and denominator, while $9$ uses only the change in the vertical coordinate. The line rises from left to right because the point with the larger horizontal coordinate also has the larger vertical coordinate. The slope is therefore positive, ruling out $-3$.
