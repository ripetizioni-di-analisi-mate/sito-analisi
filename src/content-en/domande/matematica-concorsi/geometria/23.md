A circle has diameter $14$ cm. What is its area?

- $49\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $196\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $14\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $28\pi\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The **area of a circle** is $\pi r^2$, and the radius is half the diameter, or $7$ cm. The area is therefore $49\pi\ \text{cm}^2$. The value $196\pi\ \text{cm}^2$ squares the diameter instead of the radius. The value $14\pi\ \text{cm}^2$ is the numerical value of the circumference $\pi d = 14\pi$ cm, which measures length.
