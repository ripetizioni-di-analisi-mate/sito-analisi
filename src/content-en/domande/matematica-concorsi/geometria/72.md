A disk has radius $9$ cm. A sector with central angle $120^\circ$ is cut from it. What is the area of the sector in square centimeters?

- $27\pi$
- $18\pi$
- $36\pi$
- $54\pi$

## Explanation

The central angle is one third of a full turn. The sector therefore has area $\pi\cdot9^2/3=27\pi\ \mathrm{cm}^2$.
