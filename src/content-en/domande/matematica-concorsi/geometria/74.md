Two similar polygons have perimeters of $30$ m and $45$ m. The smaller polygon has area $36\ \mathrm{m}^2$. What is the area of the larger polygon in square meters?

- $54$
- $90$
- $81$
- $72$

## Explanation

The scale factor is the ratio of the perimeters, $45/30=3/2$. The ratio of the areas is $(3/2)^2=9/4$, so the larger area is $36\cdot9/4=81\ \mathrm{m}^2$.
