The space diagonal of a cube measures $6\sqrt{3}$ cm. What is its volume?

- $216\ \text{cm}^3$
- $648\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}^3$
- $24\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}^3$
- $162\sqrt{6}\ \text{cm}^3$

## Explanation

The **space diagonal of a cube** with edge length $l$ is $l\sqrt{3}$, so $l\sqrt{3} = 6\sqrt{3}$ gives $l = 6$ cm. The volume is therefore $l^3 = 216\ \text{cm}^3$. The value $648\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}^3$ uses the diagonal as the edge length, and $24\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}^3$ uses one third of the diagonal as the edge length. The value $162\sqrt{6}\ \text{cm}^3$ uses the formula $l\sqrt{2}$, which gives the diagonal of a face.
