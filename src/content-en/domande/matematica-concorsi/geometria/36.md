A closed can, including its bottom and lid, is a cylinder of base radius $3$ cm and height $10$ cm. What is its total surface area?

- $60\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $69\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $78\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $90\pi\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The total surface area of a **cylinder** is its lateral surface area plus the areas of both bases. The lateral surface area is $2\pi r h = 2\pi \cdot 3 \cdot 10 = 60\pi\ \text{cm}^2$, and each base has area $\pi r^2 = 9\pi\ \text{cm}^2$, so the total surface area is $60\pi + 2 \cdot 9\pi = 78\pi\ \text{cm}^2$. The value $60\pi\ \text{cm}^2$ gives only the lateral surface area. The value $69\pi\ \text{cm}^2$ counts only one base, as for an open can. The value $90\pi$ is the volume $\pi r^2 h$, measured in $\text{cm}^3$.
