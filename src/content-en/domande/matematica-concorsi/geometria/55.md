A wall is 4 m wide and 3 m high. A diagonal divides it into two triangles. What is the perimeter of either triangle in metres?

- $7$
- $12$
- $10$
- $14$

## Explanation

The diagonal is $\sqrt{4^2+3^2}=5$ m. The triangle's boundary includes the diagonal and two sides of the rectangle, so its perimeter is $5+4+3=12$ m.
