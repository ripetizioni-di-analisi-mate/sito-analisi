A rectangle has sides of $4$ m and $3$ m. One of its diagonals divides it into two triangles. What is the perimeter of each of the two triangles, in meters?

- $7$
- $12$
- $14$
- $19$

## Explanation

Each triangle has as sides two consecutive sides of the rectangle and the diagonal. The two sides of the rectangle are perpendicular, so by the **Pythagorean theorem** the diagonal measures $\sqrt{4^2+3^2}=\sqrt{25}=5$ m. Therefore the perimeter of each triangle is $4+3+5=12$ m. The value $7$ ignores the diagonal, the value $14$ is the perimeter of the rectangle and the value $19$ adds the diagonal to the perimeter of the rectangle.
