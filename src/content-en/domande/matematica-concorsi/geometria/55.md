A storeroom has a rectangular floor. Its diagonal measures $17$ m and one side measures $15$ m. How long is the other side in meters?

- $2$
- $8$
- $12$
- $16$

## Explanation

The diagonal is the hypotenuse of the right triangle formed with the sides. The missing side measures $\sqrt{17^2-15^2}=\sqrt{64}=8$ m.
