The interior angles of a convex polygon add up to $1980^\circ$. How many sides does the polygon have?

- $11$
- $12$
- $13$
- $22$

## Explanation

The **sum of the interior angles** of a convex polygon with $n$ sides is $(n - 2) \cdot 180^\circ$. From the equation $(n - 2) \cdot 180^\circ = 1980^\circ$ we get $n - 2 = 11$, so $n = 13$. The value $11$ gives the number $n - 2$ of triangles formed by drawing diagonals from one vertex, omitting the final addition of $2$. The value $22$ divides the sum by $90^\circ$ instead of $180^\circ$.
