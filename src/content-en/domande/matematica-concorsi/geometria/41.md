A rectangular prism has a base measuring $4$ cm by $3$ cm and a space diagonal of $13$ cm. How tall is the prism?

- $12$ cm
- $6$ cm
- $\sqrt{194}$ cm
- $\sqrt{153}$ cm

## Explanation

The **space diagonal of a rectangular prism** with dimensions $a$, $b$, $c$ is $\sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}$, so $16 + 9 + c^2 = 169$. It follows that $c^2 = 144$, and therefore the height is $c = 12$ cm. The same result follows in two steps. The diagonal of the base is $5$ cm, and the height is the other leg of a right triangle with hypotenuse $13$ cm, as in the triple $(5, 12, 13)$. The value $6$ cm subtracts the lengths instead of their squares, while $\sqrt{194}$ cm adds the squares instead of subtracting them.
