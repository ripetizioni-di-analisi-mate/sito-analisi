A regular square pyramid has base side length $10$ cm and slant height $13$ cm, where the slant height is the altitude of a lateral face. What is its volume?

- $1200\ \text{cm}^3$
- $400\ \text{cm}^3$
- $\dfrac{1300}{3}\ \text{cm}^3$
- $1300\ \text{cm}^3$

## Explanation

The height of the pyramid, half the side length of the base, and the slant height form a right triangle with the slant height as its hypotenuse. By the **Pythagorean theorem**, the height is $\sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = \sqrt{144} = 12$ cm. The volume of the **pyramid** is one third of the product of base area and height, so it equals $\dfrac{100 \cdot 12}{3} = 400\ \text{cm}^3$. The value $1200\ \text{cm}^3$ omits division by $3$, and $\dfrac{1300}{3}\ \text{cm}^3$ uses the slant height in place of the height.
