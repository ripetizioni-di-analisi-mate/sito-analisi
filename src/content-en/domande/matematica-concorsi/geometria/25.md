In a circle of radius $9$ cm, an arc subtends a central angle of $40^\circ$. How long is the arc?

- $9\pi$ cm
- $\pi$ cm
- $2\pi$ cm
- $4\pi$ cm

## Explanation

The arc is the fraction $\dfrac{40^\circ}{360^\circ} = \dfrac{1}{9}$ of the circumference, which measures $2\pi \cdot 9 = 18\pi$ cm. The arc is therefore $\dfrac{18\pi}{9} = 2\pi$ cm long. The value $9\pi$ is the area of the sector, $\dfrac{1}{9} \cdot 81\pi$, which measures area rather than length. The value $4\pi$ cm uses the diameter in place of the radius in $2\pi r$.
