Two rectangular signs are similar. The smaller has sides of $12$ cm and $20$ cm. The shorter side of the larger sign measures $18$ cm. What is the length of its longer side in centimeters?

- $30$
- $36$
- $26$
- $24$

## Explanation

The ratio of the corresponding shorter sides is $18/12=3/2$. Applying it to the longer side gives $20\cdot3/2=30$ cm.
