A diagonal is counted just once, even if its endpoints are listed in reverse order. How many diagonals does a convex polygon with $9$ vertices have?

- $18$
- $27$
- $54$
- $36$

## Explanation

From each vertex, $9-3=6$ diagonals can be drawn, since the vertex itself and its two neighbors are excluded. Dividing $9\cdot6$ by $2$ gives $27$ diagonals.
