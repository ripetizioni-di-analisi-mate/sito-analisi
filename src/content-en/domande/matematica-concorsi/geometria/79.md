In the Cartesian plane, point $A=(7,-2)$ is reflected across the vertical line $x=3$. What are the coordinates of the reflected point?

- $(1,-2)$
- $(-1,-2)$
- $(-7,-2)$
- $(7,2)$

## Explanation

Point $A$ lies four units to the right of the line. Its reflection is four units to the left and has the same vertical coordinate, so its coordinates are $(-1,-2)$.
