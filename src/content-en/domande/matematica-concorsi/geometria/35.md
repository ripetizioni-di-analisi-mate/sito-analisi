A rectangular aquarium has internal dimensions of $60$ cm, $30$ cm, and $40$ cm. How many liters of water does it hold when full?

- $720$ liters
- $7.2$ liters
- $7200$ liters
- $72$ liters

## Explanation

The volume of a **rectangular prism** is the product of its three dimensions, $60 \cdot 30 \cdot 40 = 72\,000\ \text{cm}^3$. Since $1$ liter is $1\ \text{dm}^3$, or $1000\ \text{cm}^3$, the aquarium holds $\dfrac{72\,000}{1000} = 72$ liters. The same result follows by expressing the dimensions in decimeters because $6 \cdot 3 \cdot 4 = 72\ \text{dm}^3$. The values $720$ liters and $7.2$ liters come from a factor-of-$10$ error in conversion.
