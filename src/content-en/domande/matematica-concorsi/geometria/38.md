A sphere has surface area $100\pi\ \text{cm}^2$. What is its volume?

- $\dfrac{500}{3}\pi\ \text{cm}^3$
- $500\pi\ \text{cm}^3$
- $\dfrac{4000}{3}\pi\ \text{cm}^3$
- $\dfrac{125}{3}\pi\ \text{cm}^3$

## Explanation

The surface area of a **sphere** is $4\pi r^2$, so the equation $4\pi r^2 = 100\pi$ gives $r^2 = 25$ and a radius of $r = 5$ cm. The volume is $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = \dfrac{4}{3}\pi \cdot 125 = \dfrac{500}{3}\pi\ \text{cm}^3$. The value $\dfrac{4000}{3}\pi\ \text{cm}^3$ finds the radius from $\pi r^2 = 100\pi$ using the area formula for a circle, giving $r = 10$ cm. The value $500\pi\ \text{cm}^3$ omits the factor $\dfrac{1}{3}$ in the volume formula.
