A circular flower bed of radius $5$ m is surrounded by a path $2$ m wide. The outer edge of the path is a circle with the same center as the flower bed. What is the area of the path?

- $4\pi\ \text{m}^2$
- $24\pi\ \text{m}^2$
- $20\pi\ \text{m}^2$
- $28\pi\ \text{m}^2$

## Explanation

The path is the region between the outer circle of radius $5 + 2 = 7$ m and the flower bed of radius $5$ m. Its area is the difference of their areas, $49\pi - 25\pi = 24\pi\ \text{m}^2$. The value $4\pi\ \text{m}^2$ equals $\pi \cdot (7 - 5)^2$, obtained by subtracting the radii before squaring. The value $20\pi\ \text{m}^2$ multiplies the circumference of the flower bed by the width of the path, while $28\pi\ \text{m}^2$ multiplies the outer circumference by that width.
