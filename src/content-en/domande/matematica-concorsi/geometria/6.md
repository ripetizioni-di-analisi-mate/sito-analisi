The interior angles of a triangle are in the ratio $2 : 3 : 4$. What is the measure of the largest angle?

- $160^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $80^\circ$
- $40^\circ$

## Explanation

The **sum of the interior angles** of a triangle is $180^\circ$. The ratio $2 : 3 : 4$ divides this sum into $2 + 3 + 4 = 9$ equal parts of $20^\circ$ each. Thus the angles measure $40^\circ$, $60^\circ$, and $80^\circ$. The largest is therefore $80^\circ$. The value $160^\circ$ divides $360^\circ$ instead of $180^\circ$, using the angle sum of a quadrilateral.
