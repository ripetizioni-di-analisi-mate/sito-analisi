An isosceles right triangle has area $50\ \mathrm{cm}^2$. What is the length of its hypotenuse in centimeters?

- $5\sqrt{2}$
- $10$
- $10\sqrt{2}$
- $20$

## Explanation

The equal legs have length $a$, so the area satisfies $a^2/2=50$ and the legs measure $10$ cm. The hypotenuse of an isosceles right triangle is $\sqrt{2}$ times the length of a leg, so it measures $10\sqrt{2}$ cm.
