A square frame has outer side length $30$ cm and a border $3$ cm wide on all sides. What is the area of the border alone in square centimeters?

- $171$
- $324$
- $288$
- $360$

## Explanation

The border takes up $3$ cm at each end of a side, so the inner square has side length $24$ cm. The area of the border is $30^2-24^2=324\ \mathrm{cm}^2$.
