Two sides of a triangle measure $8$ m and $5$ m. Which of the following measures, in meters, cannot be the length of the third side?

- $4$
- $8$
- $12$
- $3$

## Explanation

By the **triangle inequality** each side of a triangle is less than the sum of the other two and greater than their difference. So the third side $d$ must satisfy $8-5<d<8+5$, that is $3<d<13$. The measures $4$, $8$ and $12$ belong to this interval, while the measure $3$ does not. With a third side of $3$ m the three vertices would be collinear, so no triangle would be formed.
