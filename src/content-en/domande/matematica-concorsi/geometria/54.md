On a plan, a route consists of two consecutive, perpendicular segments of lengths $9$ m and $12$ m. What is the straight-line distance in meters between the start and end points?

- $18$
- $13$
- $21$
- $15$

## Explanation

The two segments are the legs of a right triangle. By the **Pythagorean theorem**, the distance is $\sqrt{9^2+12^2}=15$ m.
