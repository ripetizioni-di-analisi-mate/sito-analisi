A path follows two consecutive sides of a triangle, 8 m and 5 m long. The included angle is unknown. Which distance in metres between the endpoints is impossible for a nondegenerate triangle?

- $4$
- $8$
- $12$
- $3$

## Explanation

The triangle inequality gives $8-5<d<8+5$. The value 3 is the limiting case in which the three points are collinear.
