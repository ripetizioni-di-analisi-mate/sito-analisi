Two complementary angles are in the ratio $4 : 5$. What is the measure of the smaller angle?

- $50^\circ$
- $40^\circ$
- $80^\circ$
- $100^\circ$

## Explanation

Two **complementary** angles add up to $90^\circ$. The ratio $4 : 5$ divides the sum into $4 + 5 = 9$ equal parts of $\dfrac{90^\circ}{9} = 10^\circ$ each, so the smaller angle measures $4 \cdot 10^\circ = 40^\circ$ and the larger angle measures $50^\circ$. The values $80^\circ$ and $100^\circ$ divide $180^\circ$ in the ratio $4 : 5$, treating the angles as supplementary.
