A closed rectangular box has edge lengths $4$ cm, $6$ cm, and $9$ cm. What is its outer surface area in square centimeters?

- $216$
- $204$
- $228$
- $114$

## Explanation

Opposite faces are congruent. The total surface area is $2(4\cdot6+4\cdot9+6\cdot9)=228\ \mathrm{cm}^2$.
