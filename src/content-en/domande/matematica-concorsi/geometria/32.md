The radii of two spheres are in the ratio $2 : 3$. The smaller sphere has volume $32\ \text{cm}^3$. What is the volume of the larger sphere?

- $48\ \text{cm}^3$
- $108\ \text{cm}^3$
- $72\ \text{cm}^3$
- $288\ \text{cm}^3$

## Explanation

Any two spheres are similar, and the scale factor from the smaller to the larger is $k = \dfrac{3}{2}$. By the rule for the **ratio of the volumes** of similar figures, the volume is multiplied by $k^3 = \dfrac{27}{8}$, so the larger sphere has volume $32 \cdot \dfrac{27}{8} = 108\ \text{cm}^3$. The value $48\ \text{cm}^3$ multiplies by $k$, and $72\ \text{cm}^3$ multiplies by $k^2 = \dfrac{9}{4}$, the surface area factor.
