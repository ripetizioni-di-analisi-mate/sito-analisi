A cylindrical container with an internal diameter of $12$ cm holds water to a depth of $15$ cm. All the water is poured into a second cylinder with an internal diameter of $18$ cm. What depth does it reach in the second cylinder, in centimeters?

- $\dfrac{15}{2}$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{20}{3}$
- $10$

## Explanation

The initial volume is $\pi\cdot6^2\cdot15=540\pi\ \mathrm{cm}^3$. Dividing by the area of the new base, $81\pi\ \mathrm{cm}^2$, gives $\dfrac{20}{3}$ cm.
