A ladder $2.5$ m long leans against a vertical wall and stands on a horizontal floor. At first the foot of the ladder is $0.7$ m from the wall. If the foot is moved out to $1.5$ m from the wall, by how many meters does the point where the ladder touches the wall move down?

- $0.4$
- $0.5$
- $1$
- $0.8$

## Explanation

The ladder, the wall and the floor form a right triangle in which the ladder is the hypotenuse. By the **Pythagorean theorem**, at first the ladder touches the wall at a height of $\sqrt{2.5^2-0.7^2}=\sqrt{5.76}=2.4$ m. After the move the height is $\sqrt{2.5^2-1.5^2}=\sqrt{4}=2$ m. So the point of contact moves down by $2.4-2=0.4$ m. The value $0.8$ is the displacement of the foot, and the value $0.5$ takes the length of the ladder as the initial height.
