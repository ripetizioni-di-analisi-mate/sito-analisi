A ladder $25$ m long leans against a vertical wall. Initially its foot is $7$ m from the wall. If the foot is moved out to $15$ m from the wall, by how many meters does the point where the ladder touches the wall drop?

- $4$
- $5$
- $3$
- $8$

## Explanation

The **Pythagorean theorem** gives the two heights. They are $\sqrt{25^2-7^2}=24$ m and $\sqrt{25^2-15^2}=20$ m, a difference of $4$ m.
