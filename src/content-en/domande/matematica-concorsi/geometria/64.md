A right triangle has sides of $9,12,15$ cm. A similar triangle has a 10 cm hypotenuse. What is the radius of its circumcircle?

- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The midpoint of the hypotenuse is the circumcentre of a right triangle. Its radius is therefore half of 10 cm.
