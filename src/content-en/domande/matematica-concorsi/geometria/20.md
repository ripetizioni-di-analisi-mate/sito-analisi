A rectangular plot measures $250$ m by $80$ m. What is its area in square kilometers?

- $0.02\ \text{km}^2$
- $0.2\ \text{km}^2$
- $2\ \text{km}^2$
- $20\ \text{km}^2$

## Explanation

The area is $250 \cdot 80 = 20\,000\ \text{m}^2$. Since $1$ km equals $1000$ m, we have $1\ \text{km}^2 = 1000 \cdot 1000\ \text{m}^2 = 1\,000\,000\ \text{m}^2$, so the area is $\dfrac{20\,000}{1\,000\,000} = 0.02\ \text{km}^2$. The same result follows by converting the sides first to $0.25$ km and $0.08$ km and multiplying. The value $20\ \text{km}^2$ divides by $1000$, using the length conversion factor instead of the area conversion factor.
