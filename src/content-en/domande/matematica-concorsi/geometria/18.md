An isosceles trapezoid has bases of $22$ cm and $10$ cm and legs of $10$ cm. What is its area?

- $128\ \text{cm}^2$
- $256\ \text{cm}^2$
- $160\ \text{cm}^2$
- $96\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The altitudes drawn from the endpoints of the shorter base cut off two equal segments on the longer base, each $\dfrac{22 - 10}{2} = 6$ cm long. Each segment is a leg of a right triangle whose hypotenuse is a slanted side of the trapezoid. By the **Pythagorean theorem**, the height is $\sqrt{100 - 36} = 8$ cm. The **area of the trapezoid** is therefore $\dfrac{(22 + 10) \cdot 8}{2} = 128\ \text{cm}^2$. The value $160\ \text{cm}^2$ uses the slanted side as the height, and $256\ \text{cm}^2$ omits division by $2$.
