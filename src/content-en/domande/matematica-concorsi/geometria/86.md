In triangle $ABC$, choose point $D$ on side $AB$. Through $D$, draw the line parallel to $BC$, which meets $AC$ at $E$. If $AD=6$ cm, $DB=9$ cm, and the area of $ABC$ is $150\ \mathrm{cm}^2$, what is the area of quadrilateral $DBCE$ in square centimeters?

- $126$
- $96$
- $114$
- $90$

## Explanation

Triangles $ADE$ and $ABC$ are similar with scale factor $AD/AB=6/15=2/5$. The area of $ADE$ is $150\cdot(2/5)^2=24\ \mathrm{cm}^2$. Subtracting this from the area of $ABC$ gives $126\ \mathrm{cm}^2$.
