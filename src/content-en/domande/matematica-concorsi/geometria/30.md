A rectangular photograph measuring $10$ cm by $15$ cm is enlarged without distortion until its shorter side measures $20$ cm. What is the area of the enlargement?

- $300\ \text{cm}^2$
- $600\ \text{cm}^2$
- $1200\ \text{cm}^2$
- $500\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

An enlargement without distortion preserves similarity, and here the scale factor is $k = \dfrac{20}{10} = 2$. The longer side therefore becomes $15 \cdot 2 = 30$ cm and the new area is $20 \cdot 30 = 600\ \text{cm}^2$, or $k^2 = 4$ times the original area of $150\ \text{cm}^2$. The value $300\ \text{cm}^2$ doubles the area instead of quadrupling it. The value $500\ \text{cm}^2$ also adds $10$ cm to the longer side, making it $25$ cm and distorting the photograph.
