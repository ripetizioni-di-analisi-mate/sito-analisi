Points $A=(-1,2)$ and $B=(3,4)$ are consecutive vertices of a rectangle. The next vertex after $B$ is $C=(2,6)$. What are the coordinates of the fourth vertex $D$?

- $(-2,4)$
- $(0,0)$
- $(-2,6)$
- $(1,8)$

## Explanation

In a rectangle, the displacement from $B$ to $C$ is the same as the displacement from $A$ to $D$. It equals $(-1,2)$, so starting at $A=(-1,2)$ gives $D=(-2,4)$.
