Which polynomial do you get by subtracting $x^2 - 5x + 4$ from $2x^2 - 3x + 1$?

- $x^2 - 8x + 5$
- $x^2 + 2x - 3$
- $x^2 + 2x + 5$
- $3x^2 - 8x + 5$

## Explanation

Write the difference as $2x^2 - 3x + 1 - (x^2 - 5x + 4)$. The minus sign before the second polynomial changes the signs of all its terms. This gives $2x^2 - 3x + 1 - x^2 + 5x - 4$, and combining **like terms** gives $x^2 + 2x - 3$. The polynomial $x^2 - 8x + 5$ changes only the sign of $x^2$, and $x^2 + 2x + 5$ leaves the constant $4$ unchanged.
