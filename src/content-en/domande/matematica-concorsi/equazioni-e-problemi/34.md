At a store, a hat and a T-shirt together cost $23$ euros, a T-shirt and a sweatshirt cost $41$ euros, and a hat and a sweatshirt cost $36$ euros. How much does a T-shirt cost?

- $9$ euros
- $27$ euros
- $14$ euros
- $11.50$ euros

## Explanation

Each item appears twice in the sum of the three prices, so $23 + 41 + 36 = 100$ euros is twice the combined price of a hat, a T-shirt, and a sweatshirt. The three items therefore cost $50$ euros together. Subtracting the combined price of a hat and a sweatshirt leaves the price of the T-shirt, $50 - 36 = 14$ euros. The amounts $9$ euros and $27$ euros are the prices of the hat and the sweatshirt. The amount $11.50$ euros splits the price of a hat and a T-shirt in half as if they cost the same.
