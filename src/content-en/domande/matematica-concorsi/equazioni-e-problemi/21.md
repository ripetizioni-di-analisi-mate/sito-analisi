What is the solution of $4\,(x + 3) - 2x = 20$?

- $8$
- $4$
- $16$
- $\dfrac{17}{2}$

## Explanation

By the distributive property, $4\,(x + 3) = 4x + 12$, so the equation becomes $2x + 12 = 20$. By the **addition property of equality**, move $12$ to the right and change its sign to get $2x = 8$. Dividing by $2$ gives $x = 4$. Substitution gives $4 \cdot 7 - 8 = 20$. The value $8$ is the right-hand side of $2x = 8$, one step short of the solution. The value $\dfrac{17}{2}$ applies the factor $4$ only to $x$, giving $4x + 3$.
