A scale displays the true weight $x$, plus 10% of $x$, plus a fixed error of 2 kg. It currently shows 24 kg. What will the reading in kilograms be for the same object once only the fixed error is removed?

- $20$
- $22$
- $21.6$
- $24.2$

## Explanation

The original reading satisfies $1.1x+2=24$. Removing the fixed term leaves $1.1x=22$, which still differs from the true weight.
