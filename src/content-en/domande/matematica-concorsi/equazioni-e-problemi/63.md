$140\%$ of a number exceeds $90\%$ of the same number by $6$. What is the number?

- $3$
- $12$
- $120$
- $1.2$

## Explanation

Calling the number $x$, $140\%$ of $x$ is $1.4\,x$ and $90\%$ of $x$ is $0.9\,x$, so the equation is $1.4\,x=0.9\,x+6$. Moving the term $0.9\,x$ to the left side gives $0.5\,x=6$. Therefore the number is $x=\dfrac{6}{0.5}=12$. The value $3$ comes from multiplying $6$ by $0.5$ instead of dividing it, and the values $120$ and $1.2$ from an error by a factor of $10$ in the position of the decimal point.
