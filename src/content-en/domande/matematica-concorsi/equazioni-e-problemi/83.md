A service charges a fixed fee of 6 euros plus 3 euros for each of the first 5 applications and 2 euros for each additional application. What is the smallest number of applications for which the total exceeds 30 euros?

- $11$
- $8$
- $9$
- $10$

## Explanation

Including the fixed fee, the first five applications cost 21 euros. Four additional applications bring the total to 29 euros and five bring it to 31, so the minimum is 10 applications.
