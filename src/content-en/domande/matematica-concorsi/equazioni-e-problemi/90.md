A clerk processes 18 applications in 5 hours of actual work. Simple applications take 10 minutes each and complex ones take 25 minutes each. How many simple applications did the clerk process?

- $12$
- $8$
- $9$
- $10$

## Explanation

Eighteen simple applications would take 180 minutes. The 5 working hours total 300 minutes, and the extra 120 minutes correspond to 8 complex applications at 15 extra minutes each. The number of simple applications is therefore $18-8=10$.
