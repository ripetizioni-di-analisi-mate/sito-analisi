A shipment contains 17 boxes. The small ones weigh 2 kg each and the large ones weigh 5 kg each. They weigh 58 kg in all. How many large boxes are there?

- $8$
- $9$
- $6$
- $12$

## Explanation

If all the boxes were small, they would weigh $17\cdot2=34$ kg. Each large box replaces a small one and adds 3 kg, so the extra 24 kg corresponds to 8 large boxes.
