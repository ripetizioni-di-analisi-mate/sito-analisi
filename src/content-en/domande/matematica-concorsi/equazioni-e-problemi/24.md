A print shop incurs a fixed cost of $120$ euros to produce a booklet plus $0.50$ euros for each copy printed. It sells each copy for $2$ euros. What is the smallest number of copies it must print and sell to make a profit of at least $300$ euros?

- $210$
- $200$
- $80$
- $280$

## Explanation

For $n$ copies, revenue is $2n$ euros and cost is $120 + 0.5\,n$ euros, so profit is $2n - 120 - 0.5\,n = 1.5\,n - 120$ euros. The condition $1.5\,n - 120 \geq 300$ gives $1.5\,n \geq 420$, or $n \geq 280$. For $280$ copies, revenue is $560$ euros and cost is $260$ euros, giving a profit of exactly $300$ euros, so the minimum is $280$. The value $210$ ignores the cost per copy, and $200$ ignores the fixed cost. The value $80$ is the number of copies at which profit is zero.
