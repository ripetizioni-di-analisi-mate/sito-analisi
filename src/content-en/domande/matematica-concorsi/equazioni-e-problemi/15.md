Five consecutive natural numbers have a sum of $115$. What is the product of the smallest and the largest?

- $528$
- $525$
- $529$
- $621$

## Explanation

The sum of an odd number of consecutive integers equals the middle integer times the number of integers, so the middle one is $\dfrac{115}{5} = 23$. The five numbers are therefore $21$, $22$, $23$, $24$, and $25$. The product of the smallest and largest is $21 \cdot 25 = 525$. The value $528 = 22 \cdot 24$ is the product of the two numbers next to the middle one, and $621 = 23 \cdot 27$ treats $23$ as the smallest of the five numbers.
