What is the solution of $0.2\,x + 1.5 = 0.5\,x - 0.3$?

- $4$
- $6$
- $60$
- $0.6$

## Explanation

Multiplying every term by $10$ clears the decimals and gives $2x + 15 = 5x - 3$. Rearranging gives $15 + 3 = 5x - 2x$, or $3x = 18$, so $x = 6$. Checking gives $1.2 + 1.5 = 2.7$ and $3 - 0.3 = 2.7$. The value $4$ moves $-0.3$ without changing its sign, and the values $60$ and $0.6$ misplace the decimal point by a factor of $10$.
