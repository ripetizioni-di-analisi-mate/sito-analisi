Two notebooks and three pens cost 14 euros, while one notebook and three pens cost 10 euros. How much does one pen cost?

- $2$ euros
- $3$ euros
- $4$ euros
- $1$ euro

## Explanation

Subtracting the totals gives the price of one notebook, 4 euros. That leaves 6 euros for three pens in the second purchase, so one pen costs 2 euros.
