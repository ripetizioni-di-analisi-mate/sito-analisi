One person earns 36 euros plus 8 euros per hour worked. Another earns 12 euros plus 12 euros per hour worked. After how many hours of work will they have earned the same amount, assuming both work the same number of hours?

- $8$
- $6$
- $4$
- $12$

## Explanation

The initial difference is $36-12=24$ euros. The second person gains 4 euros per hour on the first, so their earnings are equal after $24/4=6$ hours.
