Which number satisfies the inequality $7 - 3x > 19$?

- $-4$
- $-3$
- $5$
- $-5$

## Explanation

Moving $7$ to the right gives $-3x > 12$. Dividing both sides by the negative number $-3$ **reverses** the inequality sign, giving $x < -4$. Among the choices, only $-5$ is less than $-4$, and indeed $7 + 15 = 22 > 19$. The number $-4$ gives $7 + 12 = 19$, which is no greater than $19$. The numbers $-3$ and $5$ satisfy $x > -4$, the result of dividing by $-3$ without reversing the sign.
