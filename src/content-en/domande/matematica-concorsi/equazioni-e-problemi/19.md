For $a = -2$ and $b = 3$, what is the value of $a^2 - 2ab + 3b$?

- $17$
- $1$
- $-7$
- $25$

## Explanation

The square of $-2$ is $(-2)^2 = 4$ because the product of two negative numbers is positive. The term $-2ab$ equals $-2 \cdot (-2) \cdot 3 = 12$, and $3b$ equals $3 \cdot 3 = 9$. The **value of the expression** is therefore $4 + 12 + 9 = 25$. The value $17$ comes from writing $a^2 = -4$, and $1$ comes from evaluating $-2ab$ as $-12$ without accounting for the sign of $a$.
