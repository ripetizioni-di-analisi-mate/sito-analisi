Which equation has solutions $x = 3$ and $x = -7$?

- $x^2 - 4x - 21 = 0$
- $x^2 + 4x + 21 = 0$
- $x^2 + 4x - 21 = 0$
- $x^2 - 10x - 21 = 0$

## Explanation

Two numbers with sum $s$ and product $p$ are the solutions of the equation $x^2 - s\,x + p = 0$. Here the sum is $3 + (-7) = -4$ and the product is $3 \cdot (-7) = -21$, so the equation is $x^2 + 4x - 21 = 0$. Expanding the product $(x - 3)(x + 7)$ gives the same result. The equation $x^2 - 4x - 21 = 0$ uses the sum without the minus sign as the coefficient of $x$, and its solutions are $7$ and $-3$.
