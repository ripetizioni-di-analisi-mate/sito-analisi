The equation $4x^2 + 7x - 12 = 0$ has two distinct real solutions. What is their product?

- $3$
- $-12$
- $-\dfrac{7}{4}$
- $-3$

## Explanation

By the formulas for the **sum and product of the solutions**, the product of the solutions of $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ is $\dfrac{c}{a}$. Here $c = -12$ and $a = 4$, so the product is $\dfrac{-12}{4} = -3$. The value $-12$ omits division by $a = 4$, and $-\dfrac{7}{4}$ is the sum of the solutions, which equals $-\dfrac{b}{a}$.
