Which pair of numbers solves the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x - 2y = 1 \\
3x + y = 17
\end{cases}
$$

- $x = 3$, $y = 1$
- $x = 5$, $y = 2$
- $x = 4$, $y = 5$
- $x = 2$, $y = 5$

## Explanation

The first equation gives $x = 2y + 1$. Using **substitution**, write this expression in place of $x$ in the second equation. This gives $3\,(2y + 1) + y = 17$, or $7y = 14$, so $y = 2$ and $x = 5$. The pair $x = 3$, $y = 1$ satisfies only the first equation, since $9 + 1 = 10$. The pair $x = 4$, $y = 5$ satisfies only the second, since $4 - 10 = -6$.
