Two classrooms have a total of 34 seats. The first classroom has 6 more seats than the second. How many seats does the first have?

- $17$
- $28$
- $14$
- $20$

## Explanation

Subtracting the 6 extra seats leaves 28 seats divided into two equal groups. The second classroom has 14 seats and the first has $14+6=20$.
