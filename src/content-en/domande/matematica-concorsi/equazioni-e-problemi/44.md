A mother is $40$ years old and her three children are $10$, $7$, and $3$. In how many years will the mother's age equal the sum of her children's ages?

- $10$
- $20$
- $5$
- $50$

## Explanation

In $x$ years, the mother will be $40 + x$, while her children's ages will sum to $20 + 3x$ because *each* of the three children will be $x$ years older. The equation $40 + x = 20 + 3x$ gives $2x = 20$, so $x = 10$. In $10$ years, the mother will be $50$ and the children will be $20$, $17$, and $13$, with a sum of $50$. The value $20$ is the current difference between the mother's age and the sum of her children's ages. It decreases by $2$ years each year because the mother's age increases by $1$ and the sum of the children's ages increases by $3$. The value $5$ moves $x$ without changing its sign, giving $4x = 20$.
