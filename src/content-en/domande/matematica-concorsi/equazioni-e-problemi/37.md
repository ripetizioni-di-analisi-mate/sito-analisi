Which number satisfies the system
$$
\begin{cases}
3x - 5 > 7 \\
2x + 1 < 13
\end{cases}
$$

- $4$
- $5$
- $6$
- $7$

## Explanation

A number satisfies the system if it satisfies *both* inequalities. For $x = 5$, we get $15 - 5 = 10 > 7$ and $10 + 1 = 11 < 13$, so $5$ satisfies the system. For $x = 4$, the first inequality becomes $7 > 7$, which is false. For $x = 6$, the second becomes $13 < 13$, which is also false. The number $7$ satisfies only the first inequality, since $15 > 13$. Solving the two inequalities gives $x > 4$ and $x < 6$. Among the choices, only $5$ lies strictly between $4$ and $6$.
