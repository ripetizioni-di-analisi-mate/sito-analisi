For $x \neq 2$ and $x \neq -2$, which expression is equal to the fraction $\dfrac{x^2 - 4x + 4}{x^2 - 4}$?

- $\dfrac{x + 2}{x - 2}$
- $1$
- $x - 1$
- $\dfrac{x - 2}{x + 2}$

## Explanation

The numerator is the square of a binomial, $x^2 - 4x + 4 = (x - 2)^2$, and the denominator is a difference of squares, $x^2 - 4 = (x + 2)(x - 2)$. For $x \neq 2$, the common factor $x - 2$ is nonzero, so it can be canceled and the fraction equals $\dfrac{x - 2}{x + 2}$. The fraction $\dfrac{x + 2}{x - 2}$ writes the numerator as $(x + 2)^2$, whose cross term is $+4x$. The value $1$ writes the denominator as $(x - 2)^2$. The expression $x - 1$ cancels the term $x^2$ in the numerator with the corresponding term in the denominator to get $\dfrac{-4x + 4}{-4}$. Only factors common to the numerator and denominator can be canceled.
