For which real value of $k$ does the system of equations $2x+3y=7$ and $4x+ky=12$ have no solution?

- $3$
- $6$
- $2$
- $7$

## Explanation

For the lines to be parallel, the coefficient of $y$ in the second equation must be twice 3. At $k=6$, the left-hand side doubles, but the constants require two different results, 14 and 12.
