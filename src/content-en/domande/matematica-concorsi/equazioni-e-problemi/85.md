Two full-price tickets and one reduced ticket cost 23 euros. A cashier wants to infer both prices from a second purchase. Which purchase gives independent information?

- Four full and two reduced
- One full and two reduced
- Six full and three reduced
- Eight full and four reduced

## Explanation

The first equation has coefficients $(2,1)$. Only $(1,2)$ is not a multiple of that pair and can determine both prices.
