Of two positive numbers, the smaller is $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of the larger, and their difference is $18$. What is the larger number?

- $27$
- $45$
- $30$
- $90$

## Explanation

Let $y$ be the larger number. The smaller is $\dfrac{3}{5}\,y$, and their difference is $y - \dfrac{3}{5}\,y = \dfrac{2}{5}\,y$. The equation $\dfrac{2}{5}\,y = 18$ gives $y = 18 \cdot \dfrac{5}{2} = 45$. The smaller number is therefore $27$, and indeed $45 - 27 = 18$. The value $30$ treats $18$ as $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of the larger number, and $90$ treats it as $\dfrac{1}{5}$ of the larger number.
