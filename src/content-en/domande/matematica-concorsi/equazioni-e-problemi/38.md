Three brothers divide an inheritance. The first receives $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the inheritance, the second receives $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of what remains after the first brother takes his share, and the third receives the remaining $30\,000$ euros. What is the total inheritance?

- $60\,000$ euros
- $72\,000$ euros
- $40\,000$ euros
- $45\,000$ euros

## Explanation

After the first brother takes his share, the fraction of the inheritance left is $\dfrac{2}{3}$. The second brother receives $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of this amount, or $\dfrac{1}{4} \cdot \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{1}{6}$ of the inheritance, leaving $\dfrac{2}{3} - \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ for the third brother. Since $30\,000$ euros is half the inheritance, the total is $60\,000$ euros. The amount $72\,000$ euros treats the second share as $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of the entire inheritance, which would leave $\dfrac{5}{12}$ for the third brother. The amount $45\,000$ euros treats $30\,000$ euros as $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of the inheritance.
