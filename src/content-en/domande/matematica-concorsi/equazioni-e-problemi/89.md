In a two-digit number, the ones digit is twice the tens digit. Reversing the digits increases the number by 27. What is the original number?

- $48$
- $63$
- $24$
- $36$

## Explanation

Reversing the digits increases the number by nine times the difference between the digits. The difference is $27/9=3$, and since the ones digit is twice the tens digit, the digits are 3 and 6.
