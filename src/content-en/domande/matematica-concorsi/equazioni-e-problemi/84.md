For the same print run, print shop A charges a fixed fee of 18 euros plus $0.30$ euros per copy. Print shop B charges $0.55$ euros per copy with no fixed fee. What is the smallest whole number of copies for which A is strictly cheaper?

- $73$
- $72$
- $71$
- $66$

## Explanation

The condition $18+0.30x<0.55x$ simplifies to $18<0.25x$. From $x>72$, the smallest whole number of copies is 73.
