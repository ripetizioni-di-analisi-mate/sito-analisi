For what value of $k$ does the equation $x^2 - 6x + k = 0$ have a repeated root?

- $k = 9$
- $k = 36$
- $k = -9$
- $k = 3$

## Explanation

The two solutions coincide when the **discriminant** is zero. Here $\Delta = 36 - 4k$, so $\Delta = 0$ when $k = 9$, and the equation becomes $x^2 - 6x + 9 = (x - 3)^2 = 0$, with the single solution $x = 3$. The value $k = 36$ omits the factor $4$ from the discriminant. The number $3$ is the repeated root of the equation when $k = 9$.
