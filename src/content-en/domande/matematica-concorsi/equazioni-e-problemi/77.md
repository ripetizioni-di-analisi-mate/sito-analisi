A warehouse contains 17 packages. Each delivery adds 6 packages. What is the smallest number of deliveries needed to have more than 47 packages?

- $7$
- $5$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

After five deliveries, the warehouse contains $17+5\cdot6=47$ packages. It takes a sixth delivery to exceed the threshold.
