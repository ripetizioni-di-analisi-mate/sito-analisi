The product of the roots of $x^2-5x+k=0$ is negative. What follows?

- The roots are real and have opposite signs
- Both roots are negative
- One root is zero
- The roots are equal

## Explanation

Their product is $k<0$, so the discriminant $25-4k$ is positive. Real numbers with a negative product have opposite signs.
