For what real value of $k$ does the equation $x^2-8x+k=0$ have exactly one distinct real solution?

- $16$
- $4$
- $64$
- $-16$

## Explanation

A quadratic equation has exactly one distinct real solution when its discriminant is zero. Here the discriminant is $64-4k$, which equals zero when $k=16$.
