The product of the solutions of the equation $x^2-5x+k=0$ is negative. Which of the following statements is true?

- The solutions are real and of opposite sign
- Both solutions are negative
- One solution is zero
- The two solutions coincide

## Explanation

The **product of the solutions** of $ax^2+bx+c=0$ is $\dfrac{c}{a}$, so here it equals $k$ and the condition is $k<0$. With $k<0$ the **discriminant** $\Delta=25-4k$ is greater than $25$, so the equation has two distinct real solutions. Two real numbers with a negative product have opposite signs, so one solution is positive and the other is negative. The solutions coincide only for $\Delta=0$, and one solution is zero only for $k=0$.
