The trinomial $x^2+8x+3$ is written in the form $(x+h)^2+k$. What is the value of $k$?

- $-5$
- $3$
- $19$
- $-13$

## Explanation

Expanding the square gives $(x+h)^2+k=x^2+2hx+h^2+k$. The coefficient of $x$ must be $8$, so $2h=8$ and $h=4$. The constant term must be $3$, so $h^2+k=16+k=3$, and therefore $k=-13$. The value $19$ comes from adding $16$ to the constant term instead of subtracting it, and the value $-5$ from taking $h^2=8$ instead of $h^2=16$.
