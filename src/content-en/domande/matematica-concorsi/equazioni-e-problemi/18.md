Three friends share an expense of $168$ euros. The second pays twice as much as the first, and the third pays $12$ euros less than the second. How much does the third friend pay?

- $60$ euros
- $72$ euros
- $36$ euros
- $33$ euros

## Explanation

Express the second and third shares in terms of the first, so the unknown $x$ is the first share. The second pays $2x$ and the third $2x - 12$, so $x + 2x + (2x - 12) = 168$, or $5x = 180$ and $x = 36$. The third friend therefore pays $2 \cdot 36 - 12 = 60$ euros. The amounts $36$ euros and $72$ euros are the first and second shares. The amount $33$ euros subtracts $12$ euros from the first share instead of the second.
