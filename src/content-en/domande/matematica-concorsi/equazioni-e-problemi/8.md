A group of friends rents a house for $1200$ euros and divides the cost equally. If $2$ more friends joined them, each would pay $50$ euros less. How many friends are in the original group?

- $8$
- $4$
- $6$
- $24$

## Explanation

With $n$ friends, each pays $\dfrac{1200}{n}$ euros. With $n + 2$ friends, each would pay $\dfrac{1200}{n + 2}$ euros. The difference between the shares is $50$ euros. Multiplying by $n\,(n + 2)$ gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
1200\,(n + 2) - 1200\,n &= 50\,n\,(n + 2) \\[6pt]
2400 &= 50\,n\,(n + 2) \\[6pt]
n^2 + 2n - 48 &= 0 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The solutions are $n = 6$ and $n = -8$ because their sum is $-2$ and their product is $-48$. The number of friends is positive, so there are $6$ friends. Checking gives $200 - 150 = 50$ euros. The value $8$ is the number of friends in the second case. The value $4$ omits the factor $2$, giving $1200 = 50\,n\,(n + 2)$. The value $24$ is the quotient $\dfrac{1200}{50}$, which represents no quantity in the problem.
