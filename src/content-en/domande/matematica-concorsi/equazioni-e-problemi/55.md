After paying a flat fee of 18 euros, a buyer still owes 24 euros for 6 identical passes. What is the price of each pass?

- $4$ euros
- $6$ euros
- $7$ euros
- $3$ euros

## Explanation

The remaining balance of 24 euros covers exactly six passes. Each pass costs $24/6=4$ euros.
