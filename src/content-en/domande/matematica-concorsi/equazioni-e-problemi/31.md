Which equation has no real solutions?

- $x^2 - 4x - 5 = 0$
- $x^2 + 4x + 4 = 0$
- $x^2 - 4x + 5 = 0$
- $x^2 - 5 = 0$

## Explanation

A quadratic equation has no real solutions when the **discriminant** $\Delta = b^2 - 4ac$ is negative. For $x^2 - 4x + 5 = 0$, we get $\Delta = 16 - 20 = -4 < 0$, so there are no real solutions. For $x^2 - 4x - 5 = 0$, we get $\Delta = 16 + 20 = 36$, and the solutions are $5$ and $-1$. For $x^2 + 4x + 4 = 0$, we get $\Delta = 0$, and the solution is $x = -2$, counted twice. The equation $x^2 - 5 = 0$ has solutions $\sqrt{5}$ and $-\sqrt{5}$ even though its constant term is negative.
