Three offices divide 84 binders among them. The second receives twice as many as the first, and the third receives 4 more than the second. How many binders does the third office receive?

- $32$
- $16$
- $40$
- $36$

## Explanation

If the first receives $x$ binders, the other two receive $2x$ and $2x+4$. The total $5x+4=84$ gives $x=16$, so the third receives 36.
