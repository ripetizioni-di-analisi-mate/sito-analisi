To solve the equation $(x-2)^2=x-2$, both sides are divided by $x-2$. Which solution of the original equation is lost in this step?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Moving all terms to the left side and factoring out $x-2$ gives $(x-2)(x-2-1)=0$, that is $(x-2)(x-3)=0$. By the **zero-product property** the solutions of the original equation are $x=2$ and $x=3$. Dividing by $x-2$ is allowed only for $x\neq2$ and leads to the equation $x-2=1$, whose only solution is $x=3$. Therefore this step loses the solution $x=2$, while $3$ is the solution that remains.
