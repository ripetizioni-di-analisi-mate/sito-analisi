In the polynomial $(x-1)(x-4)$, $x$ is replaced by $x+2$. What is the sum of the roots of the resulting polynomial?

- $5$
- $7$
- $1$
- $9$

## Explanation

After the substitution the polynomial becomes $(x+2-1)(x+2-4)=(x+1)(x-2)$. By the **zero-product property** its roots are $x=-1$ and $x=2$, so their sum is $-1+2=1$. Each root of the new polynomial is the corresponding root of the original polynomial decreased by $2$, so the sum is $1+4-2\cdot2=1$. The value $5$ is the sum of the original roots $1$ and $4$, and the value $9$ comes from increasing each root by $2$ instead of decreasing it.
