For what real values of $x$ is $(2x+1)(x-3)$ equal to zero?

- $\left\{-\dfrac{1}{2},-3\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{1}{2},3\right\}$
- $\left\{-\dfrac{1}{2},3\right\}$
- $\{-1,3\}$

## Explanation

The product is zero when at least one factor is zero. The first factor gives $x=-1/2$, and the second gives $x=3$.
