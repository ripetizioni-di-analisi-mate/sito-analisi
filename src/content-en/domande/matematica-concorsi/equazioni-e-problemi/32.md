A company divides a fund among its employees. If each received $150$ euros, $200$ euros would be left over. If each received $170$ euros, the company would be $160$ euros short. How much is in the fund?

- $2700$ euros
- $2900$ euros
- $3060$ euros
- $500$ euros

## Explanation

Let $n$ be the number of employees. The fund equals $150n + 200$ euros and also $170n - 160$ euros. Setting the expressions equal gives $20n = 360$, so $n = 18$. The fund therefore contains $150 \cdot 18 + 200 = 2900$ euros, and indeed $170 \cdot 18 - 160 = 3060 - 160 = 2900$. The amounts $2700$ euros and $3060$ euros are the payouts in the two scenarios before adjusting for the surplus or shortfall. The amount $500$ euros comes from writing $170n + 160$, which gives $n = 2$.
