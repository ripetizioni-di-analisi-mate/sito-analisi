The area of a trapezoid is $S = \dfrac{(B + b)\,h}{2}$, where $B$ and $b$ are the bases and $h$ is the height. Which formula gives the shorter base $b$?

- $b = \dfrac{2S}{h} - B$
- $b = \dfrac{2S - B}{h}$
- $b = \dfrac{S}{2h} - B$
- $b = \dfrac{2S}{h} + B$

## Explanation

Treat the formula as an equation in the unknown $b$. Multiplying both sides by $2$ gives $2S = (B + b)\,h$, and dividing by $h$ gives $B + b = \dfrac{2S}{h}$. Subtract $B$ from both sides to get $b = \dfrac{2S}{h} - B$. When $B = 8$, $b = 4$, and $h = 5$, the area is $S = 30$, and indeed $\dfrac{60}{5} - 8 = 4$. The formula $b = \dfrac{2S - B}{h}$ subtracts $B$ before dividing by $h$, and $b = \dfrac{S}{2h} - B$ divides by $2$ instead of multiplying.
