A rectangle's base is 5 meters longer than its height, and its area is 84 square meters. What is its height?

- $6$ meters
- $9$ meters
- $7$ meters
- $12$ meters

## Explanation

Let $h$ be the height. The area gives $h(h+5)=84$. The equation factors as $(h-7)(h+12)=0$, and the only positive solution is 7 meters.
