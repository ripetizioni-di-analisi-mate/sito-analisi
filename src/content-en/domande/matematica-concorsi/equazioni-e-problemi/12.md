What are the solutions of $2x^2 - 50 = 0$?

- $x = 5$ and $x = -5$
- $x = 5$
- $x = 25$ and $x = -25$
- $x = 0$ and $x = 25$

## Explanation

The quadratic equation is **missing a term**, the one containing $x$. Rearranging gives $x^2 = \dfrac{50}{2} = 25$, and the numbers whose square is $25$ are $5$ and $-5$. Giving only $x = 5$ overlooks the negative root. The pair $x = 25$ and $x = -25$ comes from stopping at $x^2 = 25$ without taking the square root.
