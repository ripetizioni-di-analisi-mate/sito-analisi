Two teams start with the same number of workers. After 6 workers transfer from the first to the second, the second has twice as many as the first. How many workers are there in all?

- $18$
- $30$
- $36$
- $24$

## Explanation

If each team starts with $x$ workers, the teams have $x-6$ and $x+6$ workers, respectively, after the transfer. The equation $x+6=2(x-6)$ gives $x=18$, so there are 36 workers in all.
