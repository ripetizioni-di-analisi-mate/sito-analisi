Adding a number to twice itself and then subtracting $12$ gives the original number plus $20$. What is the number?

- $16$
- $32$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{32}{3}$

## Explanation

Let $x$ be the number. The statement gives the equation $x + 2x - 12 = x + 20$. Rearranging gives $2x = 32$, so $x = 16$. Substitution gives $16 + 32 - 12 = 36$ and $16 + 20 = 36$. The value $32$ is the right-hand side of $2x = 32$, and $4$ moves $-12$ without changing its sign, giving $2x = 8$.
