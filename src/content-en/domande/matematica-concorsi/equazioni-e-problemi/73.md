On a 20-question test, all questions are answered. Each correct answer earns 3 points, and each wrong answer costs 1 point. What is the smallest number of correct answers needed to score at least 32 points?

- $12$
- $14$
- $13$
- $11$

## Explanation

With $x$ correct answers, there are $20-x$ wrong answers. The score is $3x-(20-x)=4x-20$ and is at least 32 when $x\ge13$.
