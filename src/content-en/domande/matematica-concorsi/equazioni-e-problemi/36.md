The digits of a two-digit number add up to $11$. Reversing the digits gives a number that exceeds the original by $27$. What is the original number?

- $74$
- $38$
- $47$
- $56$

## Explanation

Let $a$ be the tens digit and $b$ the ones digit. The number is $10a + b$, and reversing the digits gives $10b + a$. The two conditions give the system
$$
\begin{cases}
a + b = 11 \\
(10b + a) - (10a + b) = 27
\end{cases}
$$
The second equation simplifies to $9\,(b - a) = 27$, or $b - a = 3$. Together with the first, it gives $b = 7$ and $a = 4$. The number is therefore $47$, and indeed $74 - 47 = 27$. The number $74$ subtracts in the opposite order. The digits of $38$ and $56$ add up to $11$, but these numbers satisfy only the first condition, since $83 - 38 = 45$ and $65 - 56 = 9$.
