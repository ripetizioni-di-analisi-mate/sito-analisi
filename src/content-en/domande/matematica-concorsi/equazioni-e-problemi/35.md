A parking lot charges $2$ euros for the first hour and $1.50$ euros for each additional hour. Which expression gives the cost in euros of parking for $n$ whole hours, where $n \geq 1$?

- $1.5\,n + 2$
- $3.5\,n$
- $1.5\,n + 0.5$
- $2n + 1.5$

## Explanation

There are $n - 1$ hours after the first, so the cost is $2 + 1.5\,(n - 1)$ euros. Expanding the product gives $2 + 1.5\,n - 1.5 = 1.5\,n + 0.5$. For $n = 3$, parking costs $2 + 1.50 + 1.50 = 5$ euros, and indeed $1.5 \cdot 3 + 0.5 = 5$. The expression $1.5\,n + 2$ counts $n$ hours after the first, one too many. The expression $3.5\,n$ charges the sum of the two rates for every hour.
