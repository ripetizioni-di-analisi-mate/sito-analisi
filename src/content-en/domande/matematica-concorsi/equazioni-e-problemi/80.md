How many real solutions does the system of equations $2x-y=4$ and $6x-3y=12$ have?

- Two
- Infinitely many
- None
- One

## Explanation

Multiplying the first equation by 3 gives the second. Every pair of the form $(x,2x-4)$ satisfies both, so there are infinitely many solutions.
