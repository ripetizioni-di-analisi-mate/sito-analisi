A binding service charges a setup fee of 4 euros plus 2 euros per booklet. If $x$ is the number of booklets, which equation represents a total cost of 18 euros?

- $4+18x=2$
- $4x+2=18$
- $4+2x=18$
- $6x=18$

## Explanation

The setup fee is charged once, so it stays at 4. The charge for the booklets is $2x$, and the sum must be 18.
