Two numbers $a$ and $b$ have sum $a + b = 7$ and product $ab = 10$. What is $a^2 + b^2$?

- $49$
- $39$
- $69$
- $29$

## Explanation

By the **square of a binomial** formula, $(a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$, so $a^2 + b^2 = (a + b)^2 - 2ab$. Substituting the given values gives $a^2 + b^2 = 49 - 20 = 29$. To check, the numbers with sum $7$ and product $10$ are $2$ and $5$, and $4 + 25 = 29$. The value $49$ is the square of the sum, which also includes twice the product. The value $39$ comes from subtracting $ab$ instead of $2ab$.
