Which pair consists of two numbers with sum $17$ and product $72$?

- $6$ and $12$
- $7$ and $10$
- $8$ and $9$
- $4$ and $13$

## Explanation

When **checking the choices**, look for a pair that satisfies *both* conditions. The numbers $8$ and $9$ have sum $17$ and product $72$. The numbers $6$ and $12$ have product $72$ and sum $18$. The pairs $7$ and $10$, and $4$ and $13$, both have sum $17$, but their products are $70$ and $52$, respectively. Solving $x^2 - 17x + 72 = 0$ gives the same result. The equation has $\Delta = 289 - 288 = 1$ and solutions $\dfrac{17 \pm 1}{2}$, or $8$ and $9$.
