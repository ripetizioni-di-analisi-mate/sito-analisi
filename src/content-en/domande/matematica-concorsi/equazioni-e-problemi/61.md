The number $x$ is the solution of the equation $3x+2=11$. Which of the following equations in the unknown $y$ has twice $x$ as its solution?

- $6y+4=22$
- $3y+2=22$
- $6y+2=11$
- $3y+4=22$

## Explanation

Twice $x$ is written $y=2x$, so $x=\dfrac{y}{2}$. Substituting into the given equation gives $\dfrac{3y}{2}+2=11$. Multiplying both sides by $2$ gives $3y+4=22$. Indeed, the given equation has solution $x=3$, and its double $y=6$ satisfies $3\cdot6+4=22$. The equation $6y+4=22$ comes from multiplying the given equation by $2$ without substituting $x=\dfrac{y}{2}$, and its solution is $y=3$.
