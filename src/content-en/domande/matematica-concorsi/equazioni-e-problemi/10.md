Half a number plus one-third of the number equals $35$. What is the number?

- $70$
- $42$
- $87.5$
- $21$

## Explanation

Let $x$ be the number. Then $\dfrac{x}{2} + \dfrac{x}{3} = 35$. Multiply every term by $6$ to get $3x + 2x = 210$, so $5x = 210$ and $x = 42$. Checking gives $21 + 14 = 35$. The value $87.5$ adds the fractions as $\dfrac{1 + 1}{2 + 3} = \dfrac{2}{5}$ by adding the numerators and the denominators separately. The value $21$ is half of $42$.
