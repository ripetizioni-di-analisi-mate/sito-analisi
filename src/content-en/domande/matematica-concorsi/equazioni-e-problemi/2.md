The numbers $x$ and $y$ satisfy $x + y = 12$ and $x - y = 4$. What is the product $x\,y$?

- $48$
- $32$
- $128$
- $8$

## Explanation

Using **elimination**, adding the two equations gives $2x = 16$, so $x = 8$. Subtracting them gives $2y = 8$, so $y = 4$. Their product is therefore $x\,y = 8 \cdot 4 = 32$. The value $48$ comes from the product $(x + y)(x - y)$, which equals $x^2 - y^2$, and $128 = 12^2 - 4^2$ is four times the required product because $(x + y)^2 - (x - y)^2 = 4xy$.
