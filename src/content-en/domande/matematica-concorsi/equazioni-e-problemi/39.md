Anna is $7$ years older than Marco. In $5$ years, their ages will add up to $45$ years. How old is Marco now?

- $19$ years
- $21$ years
- $14$ years
- $26$ years

## Explanation

Let $m$ be Marco's current age. Anna is now $m + 7$ years old. In $5$ years, their ages will be $m + 5$ and $m + 12$ because *each* increases by $5$, so $2m + 17 = 45$. This gives $2m = 28$, or $m = 14$. The age $19$ is Marco's age in $5$ years. It is also the result of leaving out the $5$ years and writing $2m + 7 = 45$. The age $21$ is Anna's current age.
