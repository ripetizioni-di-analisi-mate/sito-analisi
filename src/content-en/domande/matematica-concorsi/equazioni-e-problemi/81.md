What is the set of real solutions of $\dfrac{x^2-9}{x-3}=6$?

- $\varnothing$
- $\{3\}$
- $\{-3\}$
- $\{-3,3\}$

## Explanation

The denominator rules out $x=3$. For the other values, the left-hand side simplifies to $x+3$, and the equation would require precisely the excluded value $x=3$. The solution set is therefore empty.
