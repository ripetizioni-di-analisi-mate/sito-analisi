What are the solutions of $(2x - 6)(x + 4) = 0$?

- $x = -3$ and $x = 4$
- $x = 6$ and $x = -4$
- $x = 3$ and $x = -4$
- $x = 3$ and $x = 4$

## Explanation

By the **zero-product property**, a product is zero if and only if at least one factor is zero. From $2x - 6 = 0$ we get $2x = 6$, so $x = 3$, and from $x + 4 = 0$ we get $x = -4$. The pair $x = 6$ and $x = -4$ omits division by the coefficient $2$. The pair $x = -3$ and $x = 4$ changes the signs of both values. The pair $x = 3$ and $x = 4$ has the wrong sign for the second solution, since at $x = 4$ the factor $x + 4$ equals $8$.
