A freight elevator can carry at most $600\ \text{kg}$. A worker weighing $80\ \text{kg}$ rides with boxes that weigh $25\ \text{kg}$ each. What is the greatest number of boxes the worker can take in one trip?

- $21$
- $24$
- $20$
- $27$

## Explanation

With $n$ boxes, the load is $80 + 25n$ kilograms, so the elevator's limit gives the **inequality** $80 + 25n \leq 600$. This gives $25n \leq 520$, or $n \leq 20.8$. The number of boxes is an integer, so the maximum is $20$, with a load of $580\ \text{kg}$. With $21$ boxes, the load would be $605\ \text{kg}$. The value $24$ divides $600$ by $25$ without counting the worker, and $21$ rounds $20.8$ up. The value $27$ adds the worker's weight instead of subtracting it, since $\dfrac{680}{25} = 27.2$.
