For what value of $k$ does the following system have no solution?
$$
\begin{cases}
2x + 3y = 5 \\
4x + ky = 7
\end{cases}
$$

- $k = 3$
- $k = -6$
- $k = 6$
- $k = 12$

## Explanation

Multiply the first equation by $2$ to get $4x + 6y = 10$. Subtract the second equation from this result to eliminate $x$, leaving $(6 - k)\,y = 3$. When $k \neq 6$, this equation has a unique solution $y$, so the system has a unique solution. When $k = 6$, the result is $0 = 3$, which is false. The system is therefore **inconsistent**. The lines $4x + 6y = 10$ and $4x + 6y = 7$ are parallel. The value $k = 3$ compares the coefficients of $y$ while ignoring the factor of $2$ between the coefficients of $x$.
