One shelf holds 42 folders and another holds 18. How many folders must be moved from the first shelf to the second so both hold the same number?

- $18$
- $12$
- $24$
- $6$

## Explanation

Moving $x$ folders gives counts of $42-x$ and $18+x$. Setting them equal gives $2x=24$, so 12 folders must be moved.
