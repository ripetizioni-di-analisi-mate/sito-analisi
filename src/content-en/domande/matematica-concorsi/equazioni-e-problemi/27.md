What is $98^2$?

- $10\,004$
- $9804$
- $9596$
- $9604$

## Explanation

Write $98 = 100 - 2$ and use the **square of a binomial** formula $(a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2$ with $a = 100$ and $b = 2$. This gives $10\,000 - 2 \cdot 100 \cdot 2 + 4 = 10\,000 - 400 + 4 = 9604$. The value $10\,004$ omits twice the product, and $9804$ subtracts $200$ instead of $400$, omitting the factor $2$ from twice the product.
