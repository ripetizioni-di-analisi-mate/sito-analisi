For which positive value of $k$ is the trinomial $x^2 + kx + 49$ the square of a binomial?

- $7$
- $49$
- $14$
- $98$

## Explanation

The trinomial contains the squares $x^2$ and $49 = 7^2$, so it can only be the square of $x + 7$ or $x - 7$. Since $(x + 7)^2 = x^2 + 14x + 49$, the term $kx$ equals **twice the product**, $2 \cdot x \cdot 7 = 14x$, so the required positive value is $k = 14$. The value $7$ omits the factor $2$ from twice the product, and $98$ uses $49$ in place of its square root $7$ when calculating that term.
