On a test with $40$ questions, each correct answer earns $1$ point and each wrong answer costs $0.25$ points. A candidate answers every question and scores $25$ points. How many answers were correct?

- $28$
- $35$
- $25$
- $12$

## Explanation

If $x$ answers are correct, $40 - x$ are wrong because the candidate answers every question. The score gives the equation $x - 0.25\,(40 - x) = 25$, or $1.25\,x - 10 = 25$, so $1.25\,x = 35$ and $x = \dfrac{35}{1.25} = 28$. Substitution gives $28 - 0.25 \cdot 12 = 28 - 3 = 25$. The value $35$ deducts $0.25$ points for each of the $40$ questions, including the correct ones. The value $12$ is the number of wrong answers.
