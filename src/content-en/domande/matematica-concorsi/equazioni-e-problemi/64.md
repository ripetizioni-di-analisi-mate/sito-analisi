Which statement describes the real solutions of $4(x-1)+2=4x-2$?

- The only solution is 0.
- Every real number is a solution.
- There are no real solutions.
- The only solution is 1.

## Explanation

The left-hand side expands to $4x-4+2=4x-2$. Both sides are equal for every real value of $x$.
