Which of the following lines is exactly $1$ unit from the origin?

- $3x-4y+4=0$
- $3x-4y+10=0$
- $3x-4y=0$
- $3x-4y+5=0$

## Explanation

The denominator of the distance is $\sqrt{3^2+(-4)^2}=5$ for every line. At the origin, the numerator is the absolute value of the constant, so it must equal $5$. Among the options, $3x-4y+5=0$ has the required constant. A constant of $4$ would give distance $\dfrac45$, while a zero constant would place the line through the origin. The sign of the constant does not affect the distance.
