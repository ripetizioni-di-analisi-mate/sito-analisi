In a rectangle with sides parallel to the axes, opposite vertices are $A(-2,1)$ and $C(4,5)$. What is the length of diagonal $AC$?

- $\sqrt{10}$
- $2\sqrt{13}$
- $10$
- $52$

## Explanation

The sides from $A$ measure $6$ and $4$. The diagonal is the hypotenuse, so $AC=\sqrt{36+16}=\sqrt{52}=2\sqrt{13}$. The other vertices can be read from the mixed coordinates, for instance $(4,1)$. The move from $A$ to that point is horizontal, and the move on to $C$ is vertical. The lengths are not simply added because the diagonal is a slanted segment.
