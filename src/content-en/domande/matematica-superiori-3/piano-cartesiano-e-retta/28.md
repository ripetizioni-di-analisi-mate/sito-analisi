On a line with slope $-\dfrac{3}{2}$, the horizontal coordinate increases from $1$ to $5$. What is the change in the vertical coordinate?

- $-6$
- $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $6$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The horizontal change is $5-1=4$. The vertical change is the slope times that value, so $\left(-\dfrac{3}{2}\right)4=-6$.
