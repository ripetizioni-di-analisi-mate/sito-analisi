On a line with slope $-\dfrac{3}{2}$, the horizontal coordinate increases from $1$ to $5$. What is the change in the vertical coordinate?

- $-6$
- $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $6$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The horizontal change is $5-1=4$. The vertical change is the slope times that value, so $\left(-\dfrac{3}{2}\right)4=-6$. The negative sign means the vertical coordinate falls by six units, while the size of the change is $6$. The intercept is unnecessary because it cancels when subtracting the two endpoint outputs. Only $m(5-1)$ remains, with $m=-\dfrac32$.
