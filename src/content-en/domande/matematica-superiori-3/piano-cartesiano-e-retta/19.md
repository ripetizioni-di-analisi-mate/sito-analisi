The midpoint of $A(1,a)$ and $B(7,4)$ lies on the $x$-axis. What is $a$?

- $-4$
- $4$
- $-2$
- $0$

## Explanation

For the midpoint to lie on the $x$-axis, its vertical coordinate must be zero. Thus $\dfrac{a+4}{2}=0$ and $a=-4$. The horizontal coordinates give $\dfrac{1+7}{2}=4$ in every case. The mean vertical coordinate is zero only when the two vertical coordinates are opposites. With $a=-4$, the midpoint is $(4,0)$ and belongs to the requested axis without being the origin.
