The segment $AB$ has midpoint $M(2,1)$ and endpoint $A(-1,4)$. What are the coordinates of $B$?

- $(-4,7)$
- $(1,5)$
- $(5,-2)$
- $(3,-3)$

## Explanation

The displacement from $A$ to $M$ is $(3,-3)$. Adding the same displacement to $M$ gives $B=(2+3,1-3)=(5,-2)$. The midpoint divides the segment into two equal displacements. Check the result using $\dfrac{-1+5}{2}=2$ and $\dfrac{4-2}{2}=1$, exactly the stated coordinates of $M$. Taking $(3,-3)$ as the endpoint would confuse displacement components with position coordinates.
