If all the coefficients of a line's equation are multiplied by $-3$, how does the distance from $P$ change?

- It triples.
- It changes sign.
- It stays the same.
- It becomes one third.

## Explanation

The equation still describes the same line. In the distance formula, both the absolute-value numerator and the denominator are multiplied by $3$, which cancels.
