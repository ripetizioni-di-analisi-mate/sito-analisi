Find the intersection of $x=3$ and $y=2x-1$.

- $(3,7)$
- $(3,5)$
- $(3,-1)$
- $(5,3)$

## Explanation

The first line fixes $x=3$. Substitution into the other equation gives $y=2\cdot3-1=5$, so the point is $(3,5)$. The number $5$ is the vertical coordinate, since it follows after the vertical line fixes the input. The pair $(5,3)$ would reverse those roles and would fail the first equation. Since just one output corresponds to the only allowed input, the intersection is unique.
