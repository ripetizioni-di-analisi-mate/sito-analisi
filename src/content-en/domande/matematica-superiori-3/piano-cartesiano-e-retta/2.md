What is the midpoint of the segment with endpoints $A(-1,5)$ and $B(4,-2)$?

- $\left(\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{3}{2}\right)$
- $(3,3)$
- $\left(-\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{3}{2}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{5}{2},-\dfrac{7}{2}\right)$

## Explanation

Average the horizontal coordinates, $\dfrac{-1+4}{2}$, and the vertical coordinates, $\dfrac{5-2}{2}$. Both averages equal $\dfrac{3}{2}$. The displacement from $A$ to $B$ is $(5,-7)$, so reaching the midpoint means adding half of each component to $A$. This gives $-1+\dfrac52=\dfrac32$ and $5-\dfrac72=\dfrac32$, confirming both averages.
