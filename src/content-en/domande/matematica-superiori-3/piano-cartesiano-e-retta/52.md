What is the distance from $P(2,3)$ to $x+y-5=0$?

- $1$
- $0$
- $5$
- $\sqrt2$

## Explanation

The value $2+3-5$ is zero, so $P$ lies on the line. The point itself is then the foot of the perpendicular, and the distance is zero. The distance formula confirms the result because its numerator is $0$ while its denominator is $\sqrt2>0$. Zero distance means precisely that the point lies on the line. Here there is no positive-length segment to construct from the point to the line.
