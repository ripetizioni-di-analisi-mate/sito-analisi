Two lines pass through $A(1,2)$ and $B(3,0)$ respectively and both have slope $-1$. How are they related?

- They meet only at the origin.
- They coincide.
- They are perpendicular.
- They are distinct and parallel.

## Explanation

From $y=-x+q$ and $A(1,2)$, we get $q=3$. Point $B(3,0)$ also gives $q=3$, so both equations are $y=-x+3$. The common slope fixes direction. Deciding whether the lines are distinct also requires position, given here by intercept $3$ in both cases. Direct substitution confirms that $(3,0)$ satisfies the line constructed through $(1,2)$.
