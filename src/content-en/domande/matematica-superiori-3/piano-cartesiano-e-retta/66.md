Two lines pass through $A(1,2)$ and $B(3,0)$ respectively and both have slope $-1$. How are they related?

- They meet only at the origin.
- They coincide.
- They are perpendicular.
- They are distinct and parallel.

## Explanation

From $y=-x+q$ and $A(1,2)$, we get $q=3$. Point $B(3,0)$ also gives $q=3$, so both equations are $y=-x+3$.
