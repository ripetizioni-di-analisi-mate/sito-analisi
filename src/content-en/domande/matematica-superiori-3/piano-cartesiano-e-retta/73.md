The lines $3x+4y=8$ and $3x+4y=18$ are distinct. What is their distance?

- $\dfrac{10}{7}$
- $5$
- $10$
- $2$

## Explanation

Each point of the first line satisfies $3x+4y=8$. In the distance formula for the second line, the numerator is therefore $|8-18|=10$. The denominator is $5$, so the distance is $2$.
