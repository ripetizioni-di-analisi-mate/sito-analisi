Which line perpendicular to $3x+4y=12$ passes through the origin?

- $3x-4y=0$
- $4x-3y=0$
- $4x+3y=0$
- $3x+4y=0$

## Explanation

The coefficients $(3,4)$ and $(4,-3)$ have dot product $3\cdot4-4\cdot3=0$. The constant is zero, so $4x-3y=0$ passes through the origin. The given line has slope $-\dfrac34$, so its perpendicular must have slope $\dfrac43$. With zero intercept, its slope-intercept form is $y=\dfrac43 x$. Multiplying by $3$ and rearranging gives an equivalent standard form.
