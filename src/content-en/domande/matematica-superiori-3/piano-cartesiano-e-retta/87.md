What positive value of $c$ makes the line $y=c$ a distance $3$ from $P(4,1)$?

- $4$
- $3$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The vertical distance is $|1-c|$. Setting it equal to $3$ gives $c=4$ or $c=-2$, and the only positive value is $4$. The two possible lines lie three units above and below height $1$. The word positive refers to $c$, while the distance is $3$ in both cases. This selects only the horizontal line above $P$.
