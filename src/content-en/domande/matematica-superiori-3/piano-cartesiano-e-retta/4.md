Find the line parallel to $y=-3x+2$ through $P(2,1)$.

- $y=-3x+2$
- $y=-3x+1$
- $y=3x-5$
- $y=-3x+7$

## Explanation

The slope must remain $-3$. Substituting the coordinates of $P$ gives $1=-3\cdot2+q$, so $q=7$. Check the two requirements separately. The slope $-3$ ensures the same direction, and the intercept $7$ makes $1=-6+7$ true. The intercepts differ: the given line at $x=2$ gives $-6+2=-4$, so it does not pass through $P$.
