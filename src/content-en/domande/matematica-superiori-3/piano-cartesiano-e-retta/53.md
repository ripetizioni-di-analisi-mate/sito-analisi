What is the distance from the origin to $x+y-4=0$?

- $\sqrt2$
- $2\sqrt2$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The formula gives $\dfrac{|0+0-4|}{\sqrt{1^2+1^2}}=\dfrac{4}{\sqrt2}$. Multiplying the numerator and denominator by $\sqrt2$ gives $2\sqrt2$. The graph gives the same value. The perpendicular through $O$ is $y=x$ and meets the line at $(2,2)$, since $2+2=4$. Thus the length of the segment from the origin is $\sqrt{2^2+2^2}=\sqrt8=2\sqrt2$.
