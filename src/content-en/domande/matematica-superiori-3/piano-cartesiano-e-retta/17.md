Which point is farthest from the origin?

- $(5,0)$
- $(0,-4)$
- $(2,-3)$
- $(4,4)$

## Explanation

The squared distances for $(4,4)$, $(5,0)$, $(0,-4)$ and $(2,-3)$ are $32$, $25$, $16$ and $13$, respectively. Taking the square root preserves order, so $(4,4)$ is farthest. The largest single absolute coordinate occurs at $(5,0)$, but both components contribute to the distance. At $(4,4)$ two squared contributions of $16$ give a sum larger than the square $25$ from the component $5$ alone.
