Are the points $A(0,1)$, $B(2,5)$ and $C(3,7)$ collinear?

- Yes, all lie on $y=2x+1$.
- No, because $C$ does not lie on $y=2x+1$.
- Yes, all lie on $y=x+1$.
- No, because lines $AB$ and $BC$ have opposite slopes.

## Explanation

Line $AB$ has slope $\dfrac{5-1}{2-0}=2$ and intercept $1$, so it is $y=2x+1$. At $x=3$ the output is $7$, so $C$ lies on it.
