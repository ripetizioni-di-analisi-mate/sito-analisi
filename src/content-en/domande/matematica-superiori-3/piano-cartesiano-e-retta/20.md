What is the reflection of $P(3,-1)$ through the centre $M(-2,2)$?

- $(1,1)$
- $(-5,3)$
- $(-7,5)$
- $(7,-5)$

## Explanation

The centre is the midpoint of $P$ and its image. The coordinates are therefore $(2(-2)-3,2\cdot2-(-1))=(-7,5)$. From $P$ to the centre the displacements are $-5$ horizontally and $3$ vertically. Repeating them from $M$ gives $(-2-5,2+3)=(-7,5)$. Both components matter because the symmetry is about a point, the centre.
