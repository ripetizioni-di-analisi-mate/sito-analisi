Two nonvertical lines have the same slope. What information guarantees that they coincide?

- They have at least one point in common.
- Their intercepts are different.
- One passes through the origin.
- Both slopes are negative.

## Explanation

If $(x_0,y_0)$ lies on both lines, the intercept is $q=y_0-mx_0$ for each. Since both slope and intercept agree, the equations describe the same line.
