Two nonvertical lines have the same slope. What information guarantees that they coincide?

- They have at least one point in common.
- Their intercepts are different.
- One passes through the origin.
- Both slopes are negative.

## Explanation

If $(x_0,y_0)$ lies on both lines, the intercept is $q=y_0-mx_0$ for each. Since both slope and intercept agree, the equations describe the same line. Since the slopes already agree, the only unknown is the difference between the constants. A common point makes it zero. Knowing, for instance, that both slopes are negative indicates only a descending direction and does not fix the intercept.
