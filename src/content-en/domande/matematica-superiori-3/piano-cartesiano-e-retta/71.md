What is the midpoint of the axis intercepts of $x+2y=6$?

- $\left(\dfrac32,3\right)$
- $(6,3)$
- $(3,3)$
- $\left(3,\dfrac32\right)$

## Explanation

Setting $y=0$ gives $(6,0)$, and setting $x=0$ gives $(0,3)$. The average horizontal coordinate is $3$ and the average vertical coordinate is $\dfrac32$.
