For which $k$ does $kx+(k-2)y=4$ describe a vertical line?

- $2$
- $-2$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

A vertical line requires a zero coefficient of $y$ and a nonzero coefficient of $x$. From $k-2=0$ we get $k=2$, making the equation $2x=4$. After substitution the horizontal coordinate is fixed at $2$, while $y$ can be any real number. That characterises a vertical line. Making the coefficient of $x$ zero instead by taking $k=0$ would give $-2y=4$, a horizontal line.
