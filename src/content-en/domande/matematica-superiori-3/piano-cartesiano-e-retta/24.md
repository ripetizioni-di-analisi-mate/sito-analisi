An equation of a line is $-6x+2y+8=0$. Which slope-intercept equation is equivalent?

- $y=3x+4$
- $y=-3x+4$
- $y=6x-8$
- $y=3x-4$

## Explanation

Isolating the term with $y$ gives $2y=6x-8$. Dividing both sides by $2$ gives $y=3x-4$. Division by $2$ applies to both the term in $x$ and the constant. Setting $x=0$ in the original equation gives a check, since $2y+8=0$ gives $y=-4$, the same intercept as in slope-intercept form.
