What is the number $N$ of points shared by the lines $x+2y=4$ and $2x+4y=9$?

- $N=2$
- Infinitely many
- $N=1$
- $N=0$

## Explanation

Multiplying the first equation by $2$ gives $2x+4y=8$. The same left-hand side cannot equal both $8$ and $9$, so the lines are distinct and parallel. The coefficients of $x$ and $y$ are proportional, so the directions agree. The constants are not in the same proportion, so the lines do not coincide. Subtracting after doubling would give $0=1$, ruling out every common point.
