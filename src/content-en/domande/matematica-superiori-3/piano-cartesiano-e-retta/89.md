For the line $y=mx+1$, the vertical separation between the points with horizontal coordinates $0$ and $2$ is $6$. What are the possible values of $m$?

- Only $3$
- $-3$ and $3$
- Only $-3$
- $-6$ and $6$

## Explanation

The vertical coordinates are $1$ and $2m+1$, so their absolute difference is $|2m|$. From $|2m|=6$ we get $m=-3$ or $m=3$.
