Segments $AB$ and $CD$ have the same midpoint. Given $A(0,2)$, $B(4,6)$ and $C(1,1)$, find $D$.

- $(1,3)$
- $(5,7)$
- $(3,7)$
- $(2,4)$

## Explanation

The midpoint of $AB$ is $(2,4)$. To obtain the same averages with $C(1,1)$, the coordinates of $D$ must be $2\cdot2-1=3$ and $2\cdot4-1=7$. The new coordinates do give $\dfrac{1+3}{2}=2$ and $\dfrac{1+7}{2}=4$. The first sum fixes the horizontal position of $D$, and the second fixes the vertical position. Having the same midpoint imposes both averages at once.
