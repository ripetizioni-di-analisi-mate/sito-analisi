Find the foot of the perpendicular from $P(1,5)$ to the line $y=x$.

- $(5,5)$
- $(3,3)$
- $(2,2)$
- $(1,1)$

## Explanation

The perpendicular has slope $-1$ and passes through $P$, so $y-5=-(x-1)$, or $y=-x+6$. Setting $y=x$ gives $2x=6$ and the foot is $(3,3)$. The foot has to satisfy both equations, so a point such as $(1,1)$ is insufficient even though it lies on $y=x$. From $P$ to $(3,3)$ the displacement is $(2,-2)$, with slope $-1$, confirming the required right angle.
