For $P(0,3)$, which of $r:x=2$ and $s:y=-1$ is closer?

- $r$, at distance $3$
- They are equally far away.
- $r$, at distance $2$
- $s$, at distance $2$

## Explanation

The distance to the vertical line is $|0-2|=2$. The distance to the horizontal line is $|3-(-1)|=4$. Therefore $r$ is closer. The foot on the vertical line $r$ is $(2,3)$, while the foot on the horizontal line $s$ is $(0,-1)$. Only the horizontal coordinate changes in the first case. Only the vertical coordinate changes in the second. The lengths satisfy $2<4$.
