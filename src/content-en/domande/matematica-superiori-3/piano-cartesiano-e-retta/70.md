For $P(0,3)$, which of $r:x=2$ and $s:y=-1$ is closer?

- $r$, at distance $3$
- They are equally far away.
- $r$, at distance $2$
- $s$, at distance $2$

## Explanation

The distance to the vertical line is $|0-2|=2$. The distance to the horizontal line is $|3-(-1)|=4$. Therefore $r$ is closer.
