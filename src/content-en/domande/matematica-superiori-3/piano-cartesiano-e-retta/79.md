Point $P(2,3)$ is reflected across the line $x=0$. What is its image?

- $(3,2)$
- $(2,-3)$
- $(-2,3)$
- $(-2,-3)$

## Explanation

The reflection axis is vertical. The perpendicular segment is horizontal and has midpoint $(0,3)$, so the horizontal coordinate changes from $2$ to $-2$ and the vertical coordinate stays $3$.
