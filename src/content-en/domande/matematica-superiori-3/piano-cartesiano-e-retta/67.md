For which $c$ does the line $y=-x+c$ pass through the intersection of $x=1$ and $y=4$?

- $4$
- $5$
- $-3$
- $3$

## Explanation

The intersection is $(1,4)$. Substitution into the required line gives $4=-1+c$, so $c=5$. Use both coordinates of the point determined by the original lines. The value $4$ is the output at $x=1$. The term $c$ is instead the output at $x=0$, so it must be one unit greater because the slope is $-1$.
