Which line has the same direction as $2x+y=4$ and passes through the midpoint of $A(0,0)$ and $B(2,4)$?

- $x+2y=4$
- $2x+y=2$
- $2x+y=8$
- It coincides with $2x+y=4$.

## Explanation

The midpoint is $(1,2)$, and $2\cdot1+2=4$. The line with the required direction through that point is therefore the given line itself. For an explicit check, keep the part $2x+y$ and find a right side that makes the equation true at $(1,2)$. Substitution gives exactly $4$. Only one line with that direction passes through the midpoint.
