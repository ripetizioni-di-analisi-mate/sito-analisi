For which $k$ does $2x+ky=6$ pass through the origin?

- $k=6$
- For no $k$
- $k=0$
- For every $k$

## Explanation

Substituting $x=0$ and $y=0$ makes the left side zero. The equation would require $0=6$ for every value of $k$. The parameter multiplies only $y$, which is zero at the origin regardless of $k$. The term $2x$ is also zero. Thus the constant $6$ prevents the line from crossing the origin, even though the coefficient $2$ ensures it represents a line for every $k$.
