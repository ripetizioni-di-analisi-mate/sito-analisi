For which values of $t$ does $P(t,2)$ lie at distance $5$ from $A(1,5)$?

- $\{-3,5\}$
- $\{-2,4\}$
- $\{-4,4\}$
- $\{5\}$

## Explanation

The vertical displacement is $2-5=-3$. Thus $(t-1)^2+9=25$, giving $t-1=\pm4$ and $t\in\{-3,5\}$. The horizontal component must have length $4$, since $25-9=16$ is its square. It can have either sign, and the two resulting points lie on opposite sides of input $1$. Both give legs of lengths $3$ and $4$.
