For $A(2,-1)$ and $B(6,1)$, what is the vertical coordinate of their line at $x=0$?

- $2$
- $-2$
- $0$
- $-1$

## Explanation

Between the points, the change in $y$ is $2$ and the change in $x$ is $4$, so $m=\dfrac12$. Moving from $x=2$ to $x=0$ decreases the output by $1$, from $-1$ to $-2$.
