On the integer-coordinate grid, start at $(1,-1)$ and move to another grid point on $3x+2y=1$. Which displacement $(\Delta x,\Delta y)$ uses the smallest possible positive horizontal step?

- $(1,-\dfrac32)$
- $(3,-2)$
- $(2,3)$
- $(2,-3)$

## Explanation

Subtracting the equations at the two points gives $3\Delta x+2\Delta y=0$. With integer coordinates $\Delta x$ must be even. Its least positive value is $2$, which forces $\Delta y=-3$.
