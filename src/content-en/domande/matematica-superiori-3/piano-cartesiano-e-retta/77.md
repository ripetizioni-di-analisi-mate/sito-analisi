For which value of $k$ are the lines $(k+1)x+2y=4$ and $3x+ky=4$ parallel and distinct?

- $2$
- $3$
- $-2$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The lines $a_1x+b_1y=c_1$ and $a_2x+b_2y=c_2$ have the same direction if $a_1b_2-a_2b_1=0$. Here the condition is $(k+1)k-3\cdot2=0$, that is, $k^2+k-6=0$, whose solutions are $k=2$ and $k=-3$.

For $k=2$ both equations become $3x+2y=4$, so the lines coincide. For $k=-3$ we get $-2x+2y=4$ and $3x-3y=4$, that is, $y=x+2$ and $y=x-\dfrac43$, which have the same slope and different intercepts. Therefore the lines are parallel and distinct only for $k=-3$.
