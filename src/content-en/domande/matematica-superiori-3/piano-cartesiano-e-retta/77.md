For which value of $k$ do $kx+2y-4=0$ and $x+y-2=0$ coincide?

- $-2$
- $1$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

Multiplying the second equation by $2$ gives $2x+2y-4=0$, with its $y$ coefficient and constant already matching the first equation. Thus $k=2$ is required. The same direction requires proportional coefficients of $x$ and $y$. The coefficient of $y$ is $2$ in the first equation and $1$ in the second, fixing the scale factor. The constants also agree with that factor, as required for the lines to coincide.
