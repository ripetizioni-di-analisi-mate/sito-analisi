A tank starts with $12$ litres and loses $2$ litres per minute at a constant rate. After how many minutes is it empty?

- $4$
- $10$
- $24$
- $6$

## Explanation

The volume is $V(t)=12-2t$, with negative slope because the volume decreases. The horizontal intercept follows from $12-2t=0$ and is $t=6$. The number $12$ is the initial volume, and $2t$ is the amount lost after $t$ minutes. At $t=6$, the loss is $2\cdot6=12$ litres. From then on, the linear loss model no longer describes the actual volume.
