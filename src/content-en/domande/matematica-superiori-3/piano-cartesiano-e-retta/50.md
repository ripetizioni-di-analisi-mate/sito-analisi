Find the distance from $P(4,-2)$ to the horizontal line $y=1$.

- $4$
- $1$
- $5$
- $3$

## Explanation

The perpendicular segment is vertical and ends at $(4,1)$. Its length is $|1-(-2)|=3$. From the point below the line, move up to output $1$ without changing the input $4$. Any other point on the line would add a horizontal displacement to the vertical one and lengthen the segment. So the requested distance is just the difference in vertical coordinates.
