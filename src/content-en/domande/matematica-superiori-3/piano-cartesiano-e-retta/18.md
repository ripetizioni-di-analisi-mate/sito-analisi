On a square grid, two points differ by $6$ units in one direction and $8$ in the perpendicular direction. How long is the direct route?

- $14$
- $2$
- $10$
- $100$

## Explanation

The direct distance is the hypotenuse of a triangle with legs $6$ and $8$, giving $\sqrt{36+64}=10$.
