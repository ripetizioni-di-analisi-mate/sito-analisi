On a square grid, two points differ by $6$ units in one direction and $8$ in the perpendicular direction. How long is the direct route?

- $14$
- $2$
- $10$
- $100$

## Explanation

The direct distance is the hypotenuse of a triangle with legs $6$ and $8$, giving $\sqrt{36+64}=10$. The sum $6+8=14$ instead describes the route that goes all the way along one direction and then along the other. The direct route is the segment joining the points. It is shorter than the two consecutive sides, as $10<14$ confirms.
