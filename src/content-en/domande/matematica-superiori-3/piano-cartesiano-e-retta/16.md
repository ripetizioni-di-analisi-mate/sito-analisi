Which point of the $x$-axis is equidistant from $A(-2,1)$ and $B(4,1)$?

- $(2,0)$
- $(1,0)$
- $(0,1)$
- $(-1,0)$

## Explanation

Both points have vertical separation $1$ from the $x$-axis. Equating the squared distances gives $(x+2)^2=(x-4)^2$, hence $x=1$. Expanding gives $x^2+4x+4=x^2-8x+16$, so $12x=12$. The vertical coordinate is zero because the point lies on the horizontal axis. Indeed, $(1,0)$ is $\sqrt{3^2+1^2}$ from both points, with opposite horizontal components.
