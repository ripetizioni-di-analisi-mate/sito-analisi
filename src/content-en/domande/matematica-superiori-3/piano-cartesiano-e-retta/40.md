The line $r$ is horizontal. Which equation describes a line perpendicular to $r$ and passing through $P(-2,5)$?

- $y=-2$
- $x=-2$
- $y=5$
- $x=5$

## Explanation

The direction perpendicular to a horizontal line is vertical. Fix the horizontal coordinate of $P$, which is $-2$.
