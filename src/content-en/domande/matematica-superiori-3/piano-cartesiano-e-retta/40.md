The line $r$ is horizontal. Which equation describes a line perpendicular to $r$ and passing through $P(-2,5)$?

- $y=-2$
- $x=-2$
- $y=5$
- $x=5$

## Explanation

The direction perpendicular to a horizontal line is vertical. Fix the horizontal coordinate of $P$, which is $-2$. A vertical line through $P$ contains every point of the form $(-2,y)$. The given output $5$ is one of them. The equation $y=5$ would pass through the same point, but it would describe another horizontal line, with the same direction as $r$.
