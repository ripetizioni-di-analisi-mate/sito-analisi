For which $c$ do the lines $2x-y=3$ and $4x-2y=c$ coincide?

- $6$
- $3$
- $\dfrac32$
- $-6$

## Explanation

The right-hand side must double along with the left-hand side. Multiplying the first equation by $2$ gives $4x-2y=6$. With $c=6$, dividing the entire second equation by $2$ recovers the first. For any other $c$, the left sides would still be proportional, but a point on the first line would always give $6$ for the left side of the second. Equality with $c$ would therefore fail.
