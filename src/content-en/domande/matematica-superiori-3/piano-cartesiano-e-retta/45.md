What is the intersection of $2x+y=7$ and $x-y=2$?

- $(2,3)$
- $(3,-1)$
- $(3,1)$
- $(1,3)$

## Explanation

Adding the equations eliminates $y$ and gives $3x=9$. Setting $x=3$ in the second equation gives $y=1$. The point $(3,1)$ satisfies $2\cdot3+1=7$ and $3-1=2$. Adding was useful because the coefficients of $y$ were opposites. Once the input is known, one equation gives the output and the other checks it.
