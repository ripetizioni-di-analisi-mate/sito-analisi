Which line has negative slope and meets the $y$-axis above the origin?

- $y=x+2$
- $y=-x-2$
- $y=2$
- $y=-x+2$

## Explanation

In $y=mx+q$, we need $m<0$ and $q>0$. For $y=-x+2$, the slope is $-1$ and the intercept is $2$, so both conditions hold. On the graph, negative slope means the output falls as we move right. A positive intercept places the vertical-axis crossing above the origin. The line $y=-x-2$ satisfies the first requirement but meets the axis at $(0,-2)$, so it fails the second.
