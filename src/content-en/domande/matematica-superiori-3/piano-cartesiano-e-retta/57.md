A triangle has a horizontal base from $A(-1,2)$ to $B(5,2)$ and third vertex $C(3,6)$. What is its area?

- $24$
- $18$
- $12$
- $10$

## Explanation

The base is $5-(-1)=6$, and the height is the distance from $C$ to $y=2$, which is $6-2=4$. The area is $\dfrac{6\cdot4}{2}=12$.
