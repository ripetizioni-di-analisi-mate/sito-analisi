A triangle has a horizontal base from $A(-1,2)$ to $B(5,2)$ and third vertex $C(3,6)$. What is its area?

- $24$
- $18$
- $12$
- $10$

## Explanation

The base is $5-(-1)=6$, and the height is the distance from $C$ to $y=2$, which is $6-2=4$. The area is $\dfrac{6\cdot4}{2}=12$. The foot of the altitude is $(3,2)$, which shares the horizontal coordinate of $C$. It lies between the base endpoints because $-1<3<5$. The product $6\cdot4$ would be the area of the rectangle of that base and height, so the triangle requires division by $2$.
