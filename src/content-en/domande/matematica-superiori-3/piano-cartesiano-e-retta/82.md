Let $P(2,0)$ and the line $x-y=0$. What is the distance from $P$ to the line?

- $2$
- $1$
- $2\sqrt2$
- $\sqrt2$

## Explanation

The numerator is $|2-0|=2$ and the denominator is $\sqrt{1^2+(-1)^2}=\sqrt2$. The distance is $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt2}=\sqrt2$. A geometric check uses the perpendicular of slope $-1$ through $P$. Its equation is $y=2-x$, which meets $y=x$ at $(1,1)$. Both displacement components from $P$ to that point have magnitude $1$, so the distance is $\sqrt{1+1}=\sqrt2$.
