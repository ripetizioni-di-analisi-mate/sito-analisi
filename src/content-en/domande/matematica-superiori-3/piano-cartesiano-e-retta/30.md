For which $a$ do the points $(a,1)$ and $(4,5)$ determine a vertical line?

- $a=1$
- $a=4$
- $a=0$
- $a=5$

## Explanation

The different vertical coordinates ensure that the points are distinct. Their line is vertical when the horizontal coordinates agree, so $a=4$. With $a=4$, the two distinct points have input $4$, so the equation is $x=4$. If $a\neq4$, the difference in inputs is nonzero and the slope is given by $\dfrac{5-1}{4-a}$. A vertical line requires that division to be undefined.
