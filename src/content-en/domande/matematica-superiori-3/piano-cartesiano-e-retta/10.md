For which value of $k$ does $P(-2,3)$ lie on the line $kx+y-7=0$?

- $k=-2$
- $k=2$
- $k=5$
- $k=-5$

## Explanation

Substituting $x=-2$ and $y=3$ gives $-2k+3-7=0$. Therefore $-2k=4$ and $k=-2$. The negative horizontal coordinate is the factor multiplying $k$, so its sign must be kept when solving for the parameter. With $k=-2$, the left-hand side gives $(-2)(-2)+3-7=0$. The opposite value would not place the same point on the line.
