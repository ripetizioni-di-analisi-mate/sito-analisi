In a triangle with vertices $A(0,0)$, $B(8,0)$ and $C(2,4)$, what is the equation of the median to $AB$?

- $y=2x$
- $x=4$
- $y=-2x+8$
- $y=-\dfrac12x+5$

## Explanation

The median passes through $C$ and the midpoint $(4,0)$ of $AB$. Its slope is $\dfrac{0-4}{4-2}=-2$, so $y=-2(x-4)=-2x+8$.
