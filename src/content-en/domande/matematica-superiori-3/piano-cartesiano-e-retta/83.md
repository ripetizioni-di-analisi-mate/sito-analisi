For which $k$ are $(0,0)$, $(2,3)$ and $(4,k)$ collinear?

- $\dfrac32$
- $6$
- $3$
- $12$

## Explanation

Between the first two points the slope is $\dfrac32$. The third point has horizontal coordinate $4$, so on the same line its vertical coordinate is $\dfrac32\cdot4=6$. The equation can be written as $y=\dfrac32x$ because one point is the origin. Moving from horizontal coordinate $2$ to $4$ therefore doubles the vertical coordinate as well. As a check, the slope from the second to the third point is $\dfrac{6-3}{4-2}=\dfrac32$.
