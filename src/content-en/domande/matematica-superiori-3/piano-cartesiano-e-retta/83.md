On a graph, one centimetre on the horizontal axis represents $2$ units of $x$ and one centimetre on the vertical axis represents $5$ units of $y$. On the paper a line rises $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ for every centimetre to the right. What is its slope in the coordinates $x$ and $y$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac52$
- $\dfrac25$
- $10$

## Explanation

A displacement of $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ to the right on the paper corresponds to $\Delta x=2$, and a displacement of $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ upwards corresponds to $\Delta y=5$. The slope is the ratio of the vertical displacement to the horizontal one, hence
$$
m=\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}=\frac{5}{2} .
$$
The value $1$ is the slope measured in centimetres on the paper, which depends on the scales chosen for the axes. The value $\dfrac25$ is obtained by swapping the two displacements in the ratio.
