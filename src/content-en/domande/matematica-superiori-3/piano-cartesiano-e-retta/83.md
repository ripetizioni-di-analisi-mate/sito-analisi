On a graph, one centimetre along the horizontal axis represents $2$ units of $x$, and one centimetre along the vertical axis represents $5$ units of $y$. A line rises one centimetre on paper for every centimetre to the right. What is its slope in the data coordinates?

- $1$
- $\dfrac52$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac72$

## Explanation

The movement on paper corresponds to $\Delta x=2$ and $\Delta y=5$. The slope in data units is $\Delta y/\Delta x=\dfrac52$.
