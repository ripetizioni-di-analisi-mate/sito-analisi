Which displacement takes $A(-1,3)$ to $B(2,-4)$?

- $(-3,7)$
- $(3,7)$
- $(1,-1)$
- $(3,-7)$

## Explanation

Subtract the starting coordinates from the final coordinates. The result is $(2-(-1),-4-3)=(3,-7)$. The first component is positive because the point moves right. The second is negative because the move is seven units down. Adding $(3,-7)$ to the coordinates of $A$ gives $(-1+3,3-7)=(2,-4)$, confirming the required direction.
