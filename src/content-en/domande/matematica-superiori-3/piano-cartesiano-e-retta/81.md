Which equation represents the vertical line that bisects the segment joining $A(-3,2)$ and $B(5,2)$?

- $x=1$
- $x=2$
- $y=2$
- $y=1$

## Explanation

The midpoint's horizontal coordinate is $\dfrac{-3+5}{2}=1$. The vertical line through that midpoint has equation $x=1$. The midpoint is therefore $(1,2)$. Every point on the vertical line through it shares input $1$, while the vertical coordinate can vary. The line $y=2$ also passes through the midpoint, but contains the whole segment and is horizontal.
