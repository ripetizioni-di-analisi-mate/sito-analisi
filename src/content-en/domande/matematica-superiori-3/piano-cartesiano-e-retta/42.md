For which $k$ is $kx+y+2=0$ perpendicular to $2x-y+1=0$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-2$
- $2$

## Explanation

The dot product of the coefficients is $2k-1$. It must be zero, so $k=\dfrac{1}{2}$. With this value, the first line has slope $-\dfrac12$, while the second gives $y=2x+1$. Their product is $-\dfrac12\cdot2=-1$. Since both coefficients of $y$ are nonzero, the slope check applies to both lines.
