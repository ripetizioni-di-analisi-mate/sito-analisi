What is the constant vertical coordinate of the line perpendicular to $x=-2$ through $P(4,1)$?

- $y=1$
- $y=-1$
- $y=-2$
- $y=4$

## Explanation

The line $x=-2$ is vertical. Its perpendicular is horizontal and its constant vertical coordinate must equal the vertical coordinate of $P$, which is $1$. All points on the required line therefore have the form $(x,1)$, with unrestricted horizontal coordinate. In particular, $(4,1)$ satisfies this condition. The line with fixed input $4$ would also pass through $P$, but it would be vertical and parallel to the given line.
