Let $P(0,0)$ and $r:x+y=2$. Which listed point of $r$ is nearest to $P$?

- $(1,1)$
- $(2,0)$
- $(0,2)$
- $(3,-1)$

## Explanation

The line $r$ has slope $-1$. The perpendicular from $P$ is $y=x$, which meets $r$ at $(1,1)$ since $1+1=2$. The foot of the perpendicular is the nearest point.
