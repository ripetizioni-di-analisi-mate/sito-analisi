Given $3x+4y=0$, which listed parallel line is at distance $3$ from it?

- $3x+4y-15=0$
- $3x+4y+5=0$
- $3x+4y-3=0$
- $3x+4y-10=0$

## Explanation

The origin lies on the given line. Its distance to the parallel line $3x+4y+c=0$ is $\dfrac{|c|}{5}$, so $|c|=15$ is required. The line $3x+4y-15=0$ is in the parallel family and gives $\dfrac{|-15|}{5}=3$. The opposite constant would produce a parallel line on the other side at the same distance, but among the proposed lines, the constant $-15$ meets the requirement.
