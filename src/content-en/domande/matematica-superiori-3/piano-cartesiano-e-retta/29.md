The points $A(1,2)$, $B(3,6)$ and $C(4,y)$ lie on the same line, with $y$ unknown. Find $y$.

- $8$
- $4$
- $7$
- $10$

## Explanation

From $A$ to $B$, the slope is $\dfrac{6-2}{3-1}=2$. From $B$ to $C$, the horizontal coordinate increases by $1$, so the vertical coordinate increases by $2$ to $8$. The full equation gives another check. At $A$, slope $2$ gives $2=2\cdot1+q$, so $q=0$ and the line is $y=2x$. Substituting $x=4$ gives the same value as the displacement calculation.
