Which equation describes the line obtained by shifting $y=2x+1$ upwards by $3$ units?

- $y=2x-2$
- $y=2x+7$
- $y=2x+4$
- $y=5x+1$

## Explanation

The horizontal coordinate stays the same and every output increases by $3$. The slope is still $2$ and the intercept becomes $1+3=4$.
