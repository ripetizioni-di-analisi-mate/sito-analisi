Where does $y=-2x+6$ meet the angle bisector $y=x$?

- $(1,1)$
- $(0,6)$
- $(2,2)$
- $(3,0)$

## Explanation

At the intersection $x=y$, so $x=-2x+6$, giving $3x=6$. Therefore $x=y=2$. The point $(2,2)$ satisfies the first equation because $-2\cdot2+6=2$. It also lies on the bisector because its coordinates agree. The intercept $(3,0)$ is on the first line but outside the bisector, so it cannot be a point common to both.
