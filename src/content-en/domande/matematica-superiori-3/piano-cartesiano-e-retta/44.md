Where do the lines $x=2$ and $y=-3$ meet?

- $(2,-3)$
- $(-2,-3)$
- $(-3,2)$
- $(2,3)$

## Explanation

The first equation fixes the horizontal coordinate at $2$ and the second fixes the vertical coordinate at $-3$. The point satisfying both is $(2,-3)$. The vertical line contains pairs of the form $(2,y)$, and the horizontal line contains pairs of the form $(x,-3)$. A pair on both lines must meet both conditions at once. Changing a sign or swapping the coordinates makes at least one condition fail.
