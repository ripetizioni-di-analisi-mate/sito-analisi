Which formula represents the line of slope $2$ through $P(a,b)$ for every real pair $(a,b)$?

- $y-b=2(x+a)$
- $y+b=2(x-a)$
- $y-a=2(x-b)$
- $y-b=2(x-a)$

## Explanation

The vertical displacement from $P$ to a point $(x,y)$ is $y-b$. The horizontal displacement is $x-a$, so the vertical displacement is $2(x-a)$.
