Which equation is satisfied by every point on the line $2x+3y=6$?

- $4x+3y=12$
- $2x-3y=6$
- $-4x-6y=-12$
- $-4x-6y=12$

## Explanation

The equation $-4x-6y=-12$ is obtained by multiplying both sides by $-2$, which preserves exactly the same solutions. The operation is reversible because $-2$ is nonzero. Dividing the new equation by $-2$ recovers the original one. Negating just one side would instead move the line, as can be checked at its point $(3,0)$.
