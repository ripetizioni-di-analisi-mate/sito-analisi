Lines $y=2x+1$ and $y=-x+7$ meet at $P$. How far is $P$ from the $x$-axis?

- $2$
- $1$
- $7$
- $5$

## Explanation

Equating the outputs gives $3x=6$, so $x=2$ and $y=5$. The distance from the $x$-axis is the absolute value of the vertical coordinate, which is $5$. This gives $P=(2,5)$. The perpendicular segment reaches the horizontal axis at $(2,0)$. It is vertical, so the input $2$ identifies the foot but does not contribute to the length. The vertical coordinates differ by exactly $5$.
