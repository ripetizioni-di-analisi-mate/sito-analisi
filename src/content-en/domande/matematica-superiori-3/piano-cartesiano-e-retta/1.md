Find the distance between $A(-2,1)$ and $B(2,-2)$.

- $AB=1$
- $AB=25$
- $AB=5$
- $AB=7$

## Explanation

The displacements are $2-(-2)=4$ and $-2-1=-3$. By the Pythagorean theorem, the distance is $\sqrt{4^2+(-3)^2}=\sqrt{25}=5$. The two displacements give the legs of a right triangle, with lengths $4$ and $3$. The sum $4+3=7$ would measure the route along the two legs, whereas the problem asks for the direct segment between the endpoints.
