Find the distance from $P(1,2)$ to $3x-4y+15=0$.

- $d=\dfrac{10}{7}$
- $d=2$
- $d=\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $d=10$

## Explanation

The absolute value in the numerator is $|3-8+15|=10$. The denominator is $\sqrt{3^2+(-4)^2}=5$, so the distance is $\dfrac{10}{5}=2$. The numerator measures the discrepancy in the line equation at the given point. The denominator converts it to a length because it is the length of the normal vector $(3,-4)$. Dividing by $3+4=7$ would confuse the sum of the absolute components with their vector length.
