For which value of $b$ do the lines $2x+by=1$ and $4x-6y=5$ have the same direction?

- $0$
- $-6$
- $-3$
- $3$

## Explanation

For the same direction, the coefficients of $x$ and $y$ must be proportional. To obtain $4$ from $2$, double both coefficients, so $2b=-6$ and $b=-3$.
