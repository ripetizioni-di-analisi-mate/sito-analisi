Let $P(2,2)$ and $Q(-1,3)$. How do their distances from the origin $O$ compare?

- $OP>OQ$
- $OP=OQ$
- $OP<OQ$
- $OP=0$

## Explanation

We have $OP^2=2^2+2^2=8$ and $OQ^2=(-1)^2+3^2=10$. Since the distances are nonnegative, $8<10$ implies $OP<OQ$. The horizontal coordinate of $Q$ has smaller magnitude than that of $P$, but the vertical coordinate of $Q$ contributes $9$ instead of $4$. The sums of the squares therefore provide the comparison because they account for displacement in both directions.
