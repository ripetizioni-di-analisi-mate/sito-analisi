Which statement about the slope holds for every pair $A$ and $B$ with distinct horizontal coordinates when the points are swapped in both the numerator and denominator?

- It always becomes zero.
- It changes sign.
- It becomes its reciprocal.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

Both the difference of the vertical coordinates and the difference of the horizontal coordinates change sign. Their quotient therefore stays the same.
