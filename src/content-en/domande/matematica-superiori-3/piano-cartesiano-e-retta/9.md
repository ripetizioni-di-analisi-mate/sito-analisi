Read the slope of the line through $A$ and $B$ from the graph.

::grafico{f="-2*x+2" legenda=0 punti="-1,4,pieno,A;2,-2,pieno,B" segmenti="-1,4,2,4;2,4,2,-2" xmin=-3 xmax=4 ymin=-4 ymax=6 uguale=1 titolo="Points $A$ and $B$ lie on the line."}

- $m=-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $m=\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $m=-2$
- $m=2$

## Explanation

From $A(-1,4)$ to $B(2,-2)$ the horizontal displacement is $3$ and the vertical displacement is $-6$. The slope is $\dfrac{-6}{3}=-2$.
