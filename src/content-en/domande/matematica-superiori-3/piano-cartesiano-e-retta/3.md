Write the slope-intercept equation of the line through $A(-1,2)$ and $B(3,4)$.

- $y=\dfrac{1}{2}x+\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $y=\dfrac{1}{2}x+2$
- $y=2x+4$
- $y=-\dfrac{1}{2}x+\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The slope is $\dfrac{4-2}{3-(-1)}=\dfrac12$. Requiring the line to pass through $A$ gives $2=-\dfrac12+q$, so $q=\dfrac52$.
