Find the point where the line $2x-3y+6=0$ meets the $x$-axis.

- $(3,0)$
- $(0,2)$
- $(-3,0)$
- $(0,-2)$

## Explanation

On the $x$-axis, $y=0$. The equation becomes $2x+6=0$, so the horizontal coordinate is $-3$. The requested point is $(-3,0)$. Substitution checks both coordinates because $2(-3)-3\cdot0+6=0$. Setting $x=0$ would instead give $(0,2)$, a point on the same line but on the other axis.
