For which $k$ are $y=kx+1$ and $y=3x-5$ parallel?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $-5$
- $1$

## Explanation

The slopes are $k$ and $3$, so the same direction requires $k=3$. The intercepts $1$ and $-5$ differ, making the resulting lines distinct. With the resulting slope, at the same input the outputs are $3x+1$ and $3x-5$, which always differ by $6$. They therefore cannot be equal. With any slope other than $3$, the two lines would instead have exactly one common point.
