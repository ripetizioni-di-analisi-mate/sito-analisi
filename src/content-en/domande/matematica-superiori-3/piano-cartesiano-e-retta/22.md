Let $A(2,4)$ and $B(-6,-2)$. Which displacement takes $A$ to the midpoint?

- $(-4,-3)$
- $(-2,1)$
- $(4,3)$
- $(-8,-6)$

## Explanation

From $A$ to $B$, the displacement is $(-8,-6)$. Half of each component gives $(-4,-3)$. The displacement keeps the direction from $A$ towards $B$, so both components remain negative. Adding them to $A(2,4)$ gives midpoint $(-2,1)$. That point is a position, whereas the question asks for the components to add to reach it.
