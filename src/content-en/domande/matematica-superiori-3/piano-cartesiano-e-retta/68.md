Which system represents two lines that meet in exactly one point for every $k\in\mathbb R$?

- $x=k$ and $y=2x+1$
- $y=kx+1$ and $y=x$
- $x=k$ and $x=1$
- $y=2x+k$ and $y=2x+1$

## Explanation

The vertical line $x=k$ fixes the horizontal coordinate, which gives the unique output $2k+1$ in the other line. In each other system, choosing $k=1$ gives coincident lines or distinct parallel lines. The lines $x=k$ and $y=2x+1$ always give $(k,2k+1)$. At $k=1$, however, $x=k$ and $x=1$ coincide, as do $y=2x+k$ and $y=2x+1$. At that same value, the equations $y=kx+1$ and $y=x$ give distinct parallel lines.
