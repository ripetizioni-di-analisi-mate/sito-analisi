A line perpendicular to $y=x$ forms a triangle with the axes whose vertices are $(0,0)$, $(4,0)$ and $(0,4)$. What is its equation?

- $y=4$
- $y=-x+4$
- $y=x+4$
- $y=-x-4$

## Explanation

The slope perpendicular to $1$ is $-1$. The vertical intercept of the triangle is $4$, so the line is $y=-x+4$. The hypotenuse must also pass through the horizontal intercept $(4,0)$. The formula gives $-4+4=0$ there. The slopes $1$ and $-1$ have product $-1$, so the equation satisfies both the stated vertices and the perpendicularity condition.
