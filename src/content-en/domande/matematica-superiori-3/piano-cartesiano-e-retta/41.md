Which line is perpendicular to $3x+4y-1=0$?

- $4x-3y+5=0$
- $4x+3y+5=0$
- $6x+8y+5=0$
- $3x-4y+5=0$

## Explanation

Coefficients $(3,4)$ and $(4,-3)$ give $3\cdot4+4(-3)=0$. Hence the directions meet at a right angle. Slope-intercept form gives another check. The given line has slope $-\dfrac34$, while $4x-3y+5=0$ has slope $\dfrac43$. Their product is $-1$. The constant changes the line's position while leaving its direction unchanged.
