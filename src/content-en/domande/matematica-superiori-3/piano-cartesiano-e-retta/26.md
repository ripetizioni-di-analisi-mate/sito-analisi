A line intersects the axes at $(4,0)$ and $(0,2)$. What is its equation?

- $y=\dfrac12x+2$
- $y=-\dfrac12x+2$
- $y=-2x+2$
- $y=-\dfrac12x+4$

## Explanation

The vertical intercept is $2$. Using both points gives slope $\dfrac{0-2}{4-0}=-\dfrac12$, which determines the equation. This gives $y=-\dfrac12x+2$. The vertical intercept checks $(0,2)$. Substituting $x=4$ gives output $-\dfrac12\cdot4+2=0$, checking the point on the other axis too. The slope ratio divides the vertical change by the horizontal change.
