A line passes through $(0,4)$ and $(4,0)$. Which point is at distance zero from it?

- $(1,2)$
- $(2,2)$
- $(4,4)$
- $(0,0)$

## Explanation

The equation of the line is $x+y=4$. Only $(2,2)$ satisfies it, so that point is at distance zero. The equation follows from slope $-1$ and vertical intercept $4$. At $(2,2)$ the coordinates add to $4$, while at a point such as $(4,4)$ they add to $8$. Zero distance is equivalent to membership on the line, so checking its equation suffices.
