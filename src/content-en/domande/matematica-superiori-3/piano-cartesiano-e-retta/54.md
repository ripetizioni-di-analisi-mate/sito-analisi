Which points of the $y$-axis are at distance $2$ from the line $y=1$?

- $(0,-2)$ and $(0,2)$
- $(-1,0)$ and $(3,0)$
- $(0,-1)$ and $(0,3)$
- Only $(0,3)$

## Explanation

A point on the $y$-axis has coordinates $(0,y)$. Its distance from the horizontal line is $|y-1|$, so $y-1=-2$ or $y-1=2$.
