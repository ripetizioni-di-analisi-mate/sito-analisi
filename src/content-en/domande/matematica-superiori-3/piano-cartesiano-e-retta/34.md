Which listed line is distinct from and parallel to $2x+3y-1=0$?

- $4x+6y-2=0$
- $3x-2y-5=0$
- $2x-3y-1=0$
- $4x+6y-5=0$

## Explanation

Doubling the original equation gives $4x+6y-2=0$. Replacing the constant by $-5$ preserves the coefficients of $x$ and $y$ and shifts the line, which remains parallel. The point $(2,-1)$ distinguishes the lines, since it satisfies $2x+3y-1=0$. Substitution in $4x+6y-5=0$ gives $8-6-5=-3$. The point therefore belongs to the first line but not the second, which has the same direction.
