Two price rules in euros are $C_1(n)=3n+2$ and $C_2(n)=2n+6$. For how many visits do they give the same cost?

- $6$
- $4$
- $14$
- $2$

## Explanation

For the charges to agree, set $3n+2=2n+6$. Subtracting $2n$ and $2$ from both sides gives $n=4$. The value found is a nonnegative integer and therefore an allowed number of visits. Substitution gives $3\cdot4+2=14$ and $2\cdot4+6=14$. The number $14$ is the common charge, while the question asks for the number of visits, $4$.
