A horizontal hyperbola has vertices $(\pm2,0)$ and foci $(\pm\sqrt{13},0)$. What is the conjugate semiaxis?

- $9$
- $3$
- $\sqrt{17}$
- $\sqrt{11}$

## Explanation

The coordinates give $a=2$ and $c=\sqrt{13}$. Since $c^2=a^2+b^2$, we get $b^2=13-4=9$ and hence $b=3$.

The value $9$ is the square of the semiaxis, so its length is the positive root. The relation checks by computing $2^2+3^2=13$. The focus is $\sqrt{13}>2$ units from the center and lies beyond the vertex.
