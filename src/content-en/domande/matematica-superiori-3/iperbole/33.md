The lines $y=3x$ and $y=-3x$ are asymptotes of the same horizontal hyperbola. What is $\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac19$
- $9$
- $\sqrt3$

## Explanation

The positive slope is $\dfrac ba=3$. Squaring gives $\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}=9$.

For example, choosing $a=2$ would give $b=6$, so the ratio of squares would be $\dfrac{36}{4}=9$. The asymptotes fix the semiaxis ratio while leaving their scale free. The value $3$ is the rising slope.
