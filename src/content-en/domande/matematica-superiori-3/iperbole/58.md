A linear fractional graph has asymptotes $x=-2$ and $y=3$ and passes through $(0,1)$. What is the product $(x+2)(y-3)$ on the branches?

- $4$
- $-4$
- $-2$
- $2$

## Explanation

The product of the relative coordinates is constant. Substituting $x=0$ and $y=1$ gives $K=2\cdot(-2)=-4$.

The function is therefore $y=3-\dfrac4{x+2}$. At $x=0$ the reciprocal term is $-2$, returning $y=1$. The point lies to the right of the vertical asymptote and below the horizontal one, confirming that the product is negative.
