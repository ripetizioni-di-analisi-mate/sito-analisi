A linear fractional graph has asymptotes $x=-2$ and $y=3$ and passes through $(0,1)$. What is the product $(x+2)(y-3)$ on the branches?

- $4$
- $-4$
- $-2$
- $2$

## Explanation

The product of the relative coordinates is constant. Substituting $x=0$ and $y=1$ gives $K=2\cdot(-2)=-4$.
