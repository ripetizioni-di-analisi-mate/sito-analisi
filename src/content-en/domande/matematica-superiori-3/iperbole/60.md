A rectangle has area $36\,\mathrm{m}^2$ and base $x>0$. For what interval of base lengths is its height at least $9\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $0<x\leq4$
- $x\geq4$
- $0<x<4$
- $0<x\leq9$

## Explanation

The height is $\dfrac{36}{x}$. Since $x>0$, multiplying $\dfrac{36}{x}\geq9$ by $x$ gives $36\geq9x$, or $x\leq4$. Equality gives a height of exactly $9\,\mathrm{m}$.
