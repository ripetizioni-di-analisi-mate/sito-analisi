What is the upper vertex of the hyperbola $16y^2-9x^2=144$?

- $(0,4)$
- $(3,0)$
- $(0,9)$
- $(0,3)$

## Explanation

Dividing by $144$ gives $\dfrac{y^2}{9}-\dfrac{x^2}{16}=1$. The transverse axis is vertical. Setting $x=0$ gives $y^2=9$, so the positive ordinate is $3$.

Substituting $(0,3)$ into the original equation gives $16\cdot9=144$. The other vertex is $(0,-3)$. The denominator $16$ belongs to the negative term and gives the horizontal half-side of the rectangle, so its square root $4$ is not a vertex ordinate.
