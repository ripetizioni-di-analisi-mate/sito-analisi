On the graph of $y=1-\dfrac{8}{x-2}$, which listed point is on the branch to the right of the vertical asymptote?

- $(6,3)$
- $(-2,3)$
- $(6,-1)$
- $(2,1)$

## Explanation

To the right of $x=2$ we need $x>2$. At $x=6$ we get $y=1-\dfrac84=-1$. The point $(-2,3)$ lies on the left branch.

When $x>2$, the term $-\dfrac8{x-2}$ is negative, so the whole right branch is below $y=1$. A point at abscissa $6$ and ordinate $3$ is therefore in the wrong region. The point with abscissa $2$ is outside the domain.
