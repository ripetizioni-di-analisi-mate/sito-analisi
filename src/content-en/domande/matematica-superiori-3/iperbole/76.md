If $(x-2)^2-(y+1)^2=-9$, in which directions do the branches open?

- To the right and left.
- Only along $y+1=x-2$.
- Only upward.
- Upward and downward.

## Explanation

Dividing by $-9$ gives $\dfrac{(y+1)^2}{9}-\dfrac{(x-2)^2}{9}=1$. The $y$ term is positive, so the vertices lie above and below the center.

The original form also gives $(y+1)^2=9+(x-2)^2\geq9$. The allowed ordinates are therefore $y\geq2$ or $y\leq-4$. Equality occurs at $x=2$, the vertical line through the center containing both vertices.
