On the hyperbola $xy=18$, what happens to $y$ when a positive abscissa $x$ triples?

- It triples.
- It decreases by $3$.
- It stays the same.
- It becomes one-third of its previous value.

## Explanation

The new ordinate is $\dfrac{18}{3x}=\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac{18}{x}$. This keeps the product equal to $18$.

For example, $(2,9)$ lies on the curve, and tripling its abscissa gives $(6,3)$. Both products are $18$. Subtracting a fixed amount from the ordinate would not preserve the product for every $x$, because the variables are inversely proportional.
