Which point is missing from the line containing the graph of $f(x)=\dfrac{4x-12}{x-3}$?

- $(4,3)$
- $(-3,4)$
- $(3,4)$
- $(3,0)$

## Explanation

The factor $x-3$ cancels for $x\ne3$, leaving $f(x)=4$. The line is $y=4$ and the point with abscissa $3$ has ordinate $4$. That point remains excluded by the original domain.

Cancellation preserves every allowed value. For example, $f(2)=4$ and $f(4)=4$, so near $3$ the ordinate stays constant. At $x=3$ a point on the line must have $y=4$, so the point with ordinate zero is not the one removed from the line.
