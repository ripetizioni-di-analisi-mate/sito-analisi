Where does the graph of $f(x)=\dfrac{2x+7}{3x-2}$ meet the $y$-axis?

- $\left(-\dfrac72,0\right)$
- $\left(0,\dfrac72\right)$
- $(0,7)$
- $\left(0,-\dfrac72\right)$

## Explanation

At $x=0$ the denominator is $-2$, so $0$ is in the domain. The function value is $f(0)=-\dfrac72$.

The point therefore has first coordinate zero and second coordinate $-\dfrac72$. Swapping them would give a point on the horizontal axis. The ordinate is negative because the numerator $7$ is positive and the denominator $-2$ is negative.
