For a centered horizontal hyperbola, what happens if $a$ and $b$ are replaced by $2a$ and $2b$?

- The asymptotes stay the same and the focal distances from the center double.
- The slopes of the asymptotes double and the focal distance stays the same.
- The transverse axis becomes vertical.
- The foci move to the center.

## Explanation

The positive slope remains $\dfrac{2b}{2a}=\dfrac ba$ and the center is still the origin. The focal distance becomes $\sqrt{(2a)^2+(2b)^2}=2c$.
