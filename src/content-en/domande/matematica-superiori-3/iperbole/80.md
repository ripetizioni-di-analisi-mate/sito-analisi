On which interval is the graph of $f(x)=\dfrac{2x-4}{x+1}$ below the $x$-axis?

- $x<-1$
- $-1<x<2$
- $x>2$
- $-1<x\leq2$

## Explanation

The numerator changes sign at $2$ and the denominator at $-1$. Between those values the numerator is negative and the denominator positive, so $f(x)<0$. Outside that interval the signs agree.
