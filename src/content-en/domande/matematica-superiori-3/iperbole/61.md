Which part of $xy=24$ describes the base and height of a rectangle with area $24\,\mathrm{cm}^2$?

- The branch in the third quadrant.
- The branch in the first quadrant.
- Both branches.
- The positive $x$-axis.

## Explanation

The base and height are both positive lengths. This selects the points with $x>0$ and $y>0$, namely the branch in the first quadrant. The branch in the third quadrant satisfies the equation but has negative coordinates.
