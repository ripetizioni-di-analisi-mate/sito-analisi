Which equation represents the two asymptote lines of $4x^2-y^2=36$?

- $4x^2-y^2=0$
- $4x^2+y^2=0$
- $x^2-4y^2=0$
- $4x^2-y^2=1$

## Explanation

The quadratic part factors as $(2x-y)(2x+y)$. Setting it equal to zero gives the lines $y=\pm2x$.

Both lines must pass through the origin. For example, $(1,2)$ gives $4\cdot1^2-2^2=0$. Using $1$ on the right would instead give a new hyperbola. A zero right-hand side allows factorization into the two lines.
