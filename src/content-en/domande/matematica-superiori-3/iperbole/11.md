Find the rising asymptote of $\dfrac{(x-2)^2}{9}-\dfrac{(y+1)^2}{4}=1$.

- $y-1=\dfrac23(x-2)$
- $y+1=\dfrac32(x-2)$
- $y+1=\dfrac23(x-2)$
- $y+1=-\dfrac23(x-2)$

## Explanation

The center is $(2,-1)$. The vertical half-side is $2$ and the horizontal one is $3$, so the positive slope is $\dfrac23$. The line of that slope through the center is $y+1=\dfrac23(x-2)$.
