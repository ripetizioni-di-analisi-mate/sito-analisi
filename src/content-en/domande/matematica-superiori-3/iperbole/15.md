Two foci are $10$ units apart. At which constant is the locus with absolute distance difference equal to that constant a hyperbola?

- $0$
- $10$
- $12$
- $6$

## Explanation

The definition requires $0<2a<10$. The value $0$ gives the perpendicular bisector of the segment joining the foci, $10$ gives two rays, and $12$ exceeds the distance between the foci.
