Which equation gives a hyperbola with a vertical transverse axis and the same asymptotes as $\dfrac{x^2}{9}-\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1$?

- $\dfrac{y^2}{9}-\dfrac{x^2}{4}=1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{4}-\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$
- $\dfrac{y^2}{4}-\dfrac{x^2}{9}=1$
- $\dfrac{y^2}{4}+\dfrac{x^2}{9}=1$

## Explanation

Keeping the same denominator with each variable preserves the rectangle and its diagonals. Reversing both signs makes the $y$ term positive.
