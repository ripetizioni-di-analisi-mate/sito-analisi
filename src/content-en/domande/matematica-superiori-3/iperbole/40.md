Which condition on $x$ and $y$ describes the branch of $xy=20$ in the first quadrant?

- $y=\dfrac{20}{x}$ with $x<0$.
- $y=-\dfrac{20}{x}$ with $x>0$.
- $y=\dfrac{20}{x}$ with $x>0$.
- $y=20x$ with $x>0$.

## Explanation

The product gives $y=\dfrac{20}{x}$ for $x\ne0$. Both coordinates are positive exactly when $x>0$.
