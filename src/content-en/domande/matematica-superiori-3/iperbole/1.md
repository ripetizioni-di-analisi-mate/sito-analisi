For $F_1=(-6,0)$, $F_2=(6,0)$, and $P=(2,0)$, find the absolute difference between the distances from $P$ to the foci.

- $|PF_1-PF_2|=2$
- $|PF_1-PF_2|=4$
- $|PF_1-PF_2|=8$
- $|PF_1-PF_2|=12$

## Explanation

The distances are $|2+6|=8$ and $|2-6|=4$, so their absolute difference is $|8-4|=4$. The point lies on the branch closer to $F_2$.

Because $P$ lies between the foci, $8+4=12$ is the length of the entire focal segment. The question asks by how much one distance exceeds the other. The value $8$ measures only the distance from $F_1$.
