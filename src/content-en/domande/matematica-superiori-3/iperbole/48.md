For $\dfrac{(x-1)^2}{4}-\dfrac{(y+2)^2}{25}=1$, what is the vertical extent of the reference rectangle?

- From $y=-5$ to $y=5$.
- From $y=-4$ to $y=0$.
- From $y=-7$ to $y=3$.
- From $y=-3$ to $y=7$.

## Explanation

The center has ordinate $-2$ and the vertical half-side is $\sqrt{25}=5$. The edges thus have ordinates $-2-5=-7$ and $-2+5=3$.
