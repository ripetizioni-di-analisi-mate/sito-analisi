The line $y=x$ is an asymptote of $x^2-y^2=16$. What value does the left-hand side take at points on the line?

- $16$
- $0$
- $1$
- $2x^2$

## Explanation

On $y=x$, we have $x^2-y^2=x^2-x^2=0$. The hyperbola requires the value $16$, so no point on the line is on the curve.

The same value holds on the other asymptote $y=-x$ because $(-x)^2=x^2$. The squares cancel on both lines. On the curve, however, the square of the abscissa exceeds the square of the ordinate by $16$.
