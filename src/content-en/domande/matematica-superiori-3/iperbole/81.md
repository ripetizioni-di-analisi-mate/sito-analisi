Which ordered pair gives the two values of $y$ on $xy=-8$ when $x$ takes the values $-2$ and $4$, in that order?

- $(-4,2)$
- $(2,-4)$
- $(-2,4)$
- $(4,-2)$

## Explanation

Divide the product $-8$ by each abscissa. This gives $y=\dfrac{-8}{-2}=4$ and then $y=\dfrac{-8}{4}=-2$. The points lie in different quadrants.
