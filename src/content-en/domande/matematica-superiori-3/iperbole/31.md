Where do the asymptotes of $\dfrac{(x+3)^2}{16}-\dfrac{(y-2)^2}{25}=1$ intersect?

- $(3,-2)$
- $(-3,2)$
- $(-3,-2)$
- $(1,2)$

## Explanation

The asymptotes are the diagonals of the rectangle centered where $x+3=0$ and $y-2=0$. They therefore meet at $(-3,2)$.

The shift from the origin-centered form is $3$ units left and $2$ up. The two lines can be written as $y-2=\pm\dfrac54(x+3)$. At $(-3,2)$ both differences vanish, so the point lies on both lines.
