Which statement is true of the graph of $f(x)=\dfrac{x+2}{x}$?

- It crosses the $y$-axis and the $x$-axis is excluded from the graph.
- It crosses the $x$-axis and the $y$-axis is excluded from the graph.
- It crosses both axes.
- The origin lies on the graph.

## Explanation

At $x=-2$ the numerator is zero and the denominator is nonzero, so $(-2,0)$ is on the graph. Every point on the $y$-axis instead has $x=0$, which is outside the domain.
