Which point is the upper-right corner of the rectangle determining the asymptotes of $\dfrac{y^2}{25}-\dfrac{x^2}{9}=1$?

- $(5,3)$
- $(3,5)$
- $(0,5)$
- $(9,25)$

## Explanation

The rectangle extends to $x=\pm3$ and $y=\pm5$. Its upper-right corner has both coordinates positive, so it is $(3,5)$.
