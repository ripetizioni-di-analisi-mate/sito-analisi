How many points of the right branch of $x^2-y^2=4$ lie on $y=0$?

- $0$
- $2$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

We have $x^2=4$, so the full hyperbola meets the line at $(\pm2,0)$. Only $(2,0)$ has positive abscissa and belongs to the right branch.

The ordinate is zero on the horizontal axis, leaving exactly the two vertices. Restricting to the right branch selects abscissa $2$ and excludes $-2$. Without that restriction the count would change because the left vertex lies on the line too.
