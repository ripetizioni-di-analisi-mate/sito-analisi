In the family $f_q(x)=\dfrac{2x+q}{x+3}$, for what $q$ does the graph lie on a horizontal line with one point missing and no vertical asymptote?

- $-6$
- $3$
- $2$
- $6$

## Explanation

The numerator must vanish at $-3$, the zero of the denominator. Thus $-6+q=0$ and $q=6$. In this case $2x+6=2(x+3)$ and the graph coincides with $y=2$ for $x\ne-3$.
