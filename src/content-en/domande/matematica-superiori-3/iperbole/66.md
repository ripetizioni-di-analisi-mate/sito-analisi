At which point does $y=4-x$ touch $xy=4$?

- $(4,0)$
- $(0,4)$
- $(-2,-2)$
- $(2,2)$

## Explanation

Substitution gives $x(4-x)=4$, so $(x-2)^2=0$. The repeated abscissa is $2$, and the line gives $y=2$.

The pair $(2,2)$ satisfies both the product $2\cdot2=4$ and the line $2=4-2$. The intercepts $(4,0)$ and $(0,4)$ lie on the line but have product zero. The curve point $(-2,-2)$ lies on the opposite branch and misses the given line.
