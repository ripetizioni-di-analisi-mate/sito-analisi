Find the equation of the hyperbola with vertices $(\pm4,0)$ and rising asymptote $y=\dfrac{x}{2}$.

- $\dfrac{x^2}{16}-\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{16}-\dfrac{y^2}{64}=1$
- $\dfrac{y^2}{4}-\dfrac{x^2}{16}=1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{4}-\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$

## Explanation

The vertices give a center at the origin, a horizontal transverse axis, and $a=4$. The slope of the rising asymptote is $\dfrac{b}{a}=\dfrac12$, so $b=2$ and the denominators are $16$ and $4$.
