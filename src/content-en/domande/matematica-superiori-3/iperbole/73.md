If $a=3$ and $c=5$, how long are the vertical sides of the reference rectangle of the horizontal hyperbola?

- $4$
- $6$
- $8$
- $10$

## Explanation

We get $b=\sqrt{c^2-a^2}=\sqrt{25-9}=4$. The rectangle extends from $y=-4$ to $y=4$, so each vertical side has length $2b=8$.

The number $4$ is the distance from the center to either horizontal edge. A full vertical side adds both of those distances. The measure $6$ belongs to a horizontal side instead, because its length is $2a=6$.
