A hyperbola has center $(1,1)$ and upper vertex $(1,4)$. Which line contains both foci?

- $y=1$
- $y=4$
- $x=4$
- $x=1$

## Explanation

The center and vertex lie on the transverse axis, here the vertical line with abscissa $1$. Both foci lie on that line.

The vertex is $3$ units above the center with the same abscissa. Their joining segment is therefore vertical. The lines $y=1$ and $y=4$ each pass through just one of the two points and are horizontal.
