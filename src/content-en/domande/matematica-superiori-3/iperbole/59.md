A bus costs $180$ euros and each participant pays an additional $5$ euros for admission. What value does the per-person cost approach as the number $n$ of participants grows, under the model $5+\dfrac{180}{n}$?

- $0$ euros
- $180$ euros
- $185$ euros
- $5$ euros

## Explanation

The bus share $\dfrac{180}{n}$ approaches zero as the number of participants grows. Admission remains $5$ euros per person, so that is the value the cost approaches.
