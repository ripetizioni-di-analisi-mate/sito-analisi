Find the domain of $f(x)=\dfrac{x+3}{2x-8}$.

- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-3\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{4\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-4\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-3,4\}$

## Explanation

The denominator is $2(x-4)$, so the ratio is defined for every real number other than $4$. The numerator's zero $-3$ is allowed and gives $f(-3)=0$.

At $x=4$ the numerator equals $7$. The ratio is therefore undefined there and the factor $x-4$ cannot be canceled. For example, at $x=-4$ the denominator is $-16$ and the function is defined, even though this input is the opposite of the excluded one.
