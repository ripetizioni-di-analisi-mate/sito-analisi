Find the domain of $f(x)=\dfrac{x+3}{2x-8}$.

- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-3\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{4\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-4\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-3,4\}$

## Explanation

The denominator is $2(x-4)$, so the ratio is defined for every real number other than $4$. The numerator's zero $-3$ is allowed and gives $f(-3)=0$.
