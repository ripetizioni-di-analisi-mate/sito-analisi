Which value is missing from the range of $f(x)=3-\dfrac{4}{x+1}$?

- $-1$
- $3$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

The term $\dfrac{4}{x+1}$ is nonzero on the domain, so $f(x)\ne3$. Conversely, each $y\ne3$ gives the admissible solution $x=-1-\dfrac4{y-3}$.

For example, height $0$ is reached when $x+1=\dfrac43$, which gives $x=\dfrac13$. The number $-1$ is an abscissa excluded from the domain, while $3$ is an ordinate excluded from the range. The two exclusions concern different coordinates.
