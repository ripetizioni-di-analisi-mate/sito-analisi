What number is added to $x^2-10x$ to obtain a perfect square?

- $5$
- $25$
- $-25$
- $100$

## Explanation

Half the coefficient of $x$ is $-5$. The square $(x-5)^2$ is $x^2-10x+25$, so the term to add is $25$.

In $(x-h)^2=x^2-2hx+h^2$, the term $-2hx$ matches $-10x$ when $h=5$. The missing term is therefore $h^2=25$. Adding only $5$ would preserve the linear term but would not produce the square of the binomial.
