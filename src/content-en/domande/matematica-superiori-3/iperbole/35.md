Which vertical line is tangent to the left branch of $\dfrac{x^2}{16}-\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$?

- $x=-4$
- $x=4$
- $x=-3$
- $x=-5$

## Explanation

The vertical tangent passes through the left vertex $(-4,0)$. Substituting $x=-4$ gives $y^2=0$, and the entire left branch stays on the left side of the line.
