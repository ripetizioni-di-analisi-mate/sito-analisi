Which vertical line is tangent to the left branch of $\dfrac{x^2}{16}-\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$?

- $x=-4$
- $x=4$
- $x=-3$
- $x=-5$

## Explanation

The vertical tangent passes through the left vertex $(-4,0)$. Substituting $x=-4$ gives $y^2=0$, and the entire left branch stays on the left side of the line.

For comparison, $x=-3$ would give $\dfrac{y^2}{9}=\dfrac9{16}-1<0$, so it misses the curve. At $x=-5$ the right-hand side becomes $\dfrac{25}{16}-1>0$ and there are two intersections. Tangency occurs at the intermediate value where the square is zero.
