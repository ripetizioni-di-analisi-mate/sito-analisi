Which horizontal hyperbola has the same asymptotes as $\dfrac{x^2}{4}-\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$ and vertices twice as far from the center?

- $\dfrac{x^2}{8}-\dfrac{y^2}{18}=1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{16}-\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$
- $\dfrac{y^2}{36}-\dfrac{x^2}{16}=1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{16}-\dfrac{y^2}{36}=1$

## Explanation

The vertices require $a=4$ and horizontal orientation. The slope remains $\dfrac32$, so $b=6$ and the denominators are $16$ and $36$.
