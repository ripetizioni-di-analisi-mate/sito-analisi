For which value of $p$ does $\dfrac{p x-6}{x-2}$ define a constant function on its domain?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $6$
- $-6$

## Explanation

The numerator must equal $p(x-2)=px-2p$, so $-2p=-6$ and $p=3$. The graph is $y=3$ for $x\ne2$.

Division gives $\dfrac{px-6}{x-2}=p+\dfrac{2p-6}{x-2}$. The part that varies with $x$ disappears only when $2p-6=0$. The abscissa $2$ is excluded even in the constant case because it is a zero of the original denominator.
