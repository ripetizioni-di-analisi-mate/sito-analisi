A point $P$ satisfies $PF_1-PF_2=4$ for $F_1=(-5,0)$ and $F_2=(5,0)$. What is its abscissa if $PF_1+PF_2=15$?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $6$
- $\dfrac35$

## Explanation

The product of the sum and difference is $15\cdot4=60$. It also equals $PF_1^2-PF_2^2=(x+5)^2-(x-5)^2=20x$. Therefore $x=\dfrac{60}{20}=3$.

The individual distances provide a check on the data. Half the sum of $15$ and $4$ is $\dfrac{19}{2}$, and half their difference is $\dfrac{11}{2}$. The larger distance is from $F_1$, so the point lies to the right of the $y$-axis, consistent with the sign of $x$.
