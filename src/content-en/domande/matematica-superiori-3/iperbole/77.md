How does $y=\dfrac6x$ differ from $\dfrac{x^2}{9}-\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1$ with respect to vertical lines?

- Every vertical line meets both curves exactly once.
- The line $x=0$ meets $y=\dfrac6x$ once.
- Every vertical line meets $y=\dfrac6x$ at most once, while some meet the other hyperbola twice.
- Every vertical line meets the other hyperbola at most once.

## Explanation

For $x\ne0$ the ratio gives a single ordinate. In the other equation, for example at $x=6$, we get $y^2=12$ and hence two ordinates.
