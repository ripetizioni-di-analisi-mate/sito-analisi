For $\dfrac{x^2}{25}-\dfrac{y^2}{144}=1$, what is the distance between the right vertex and the right focus?

- $5$
- $12$
- $18$
- $8$

## Explanation

The vertex has abscissa $a=5$. The focus has abscissa $c=\sqrt{25+144}=13$. Both have ordinate zero, so the distance is $13-5=8$.

Along the right half-axis, the abscissas are $5$ and $13$. The requested distance is their difference because both points are on the same side of the center. The value $18$ would be the distance from the right vertex to the left focus.
