For $\dfrac{x^2}{25}-\dfrac{y^2}{144}=1$, what is the distance between the right vertex and the right focus?

- $5$
- $12$
- $18$
- $8$

## Explanation

The vertex has abscissa $a=5$. The focus has abscissa $c=\sqrt{25+144}=13$. Both have ordinate zero, so the distance is $13-5=8$.
