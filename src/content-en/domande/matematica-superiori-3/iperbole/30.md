For the hyperbola $x^2-y^2=9$, what is the vertical gap between the rising asymptote and the upper point of the curve at $x=5$?

- $1$
- $4$
- $5$
- $9$

## Explanation

The rising asymptote is $y=x$, so its ordinate above $x=5$ is $5$. The upper ordinate of the curve is $\sqrt{25-9}=4$. The gap is $5-4=1$.
