Which point in the figure lies on the other branch from $P$ and is its reflection through the center $C$?

::grafico{f="-1+6/(x-1)" xmin=-5 xmax=7 ymin=-8 ymax=6 uguale=1 asintoti="x=1;y=-1" legenda=0 punti="1,-1,vuoto,C;3,2,pieno,P;-1,-4,pieno,Q;4,1,pieno,A;-2,-3,pieno,B;5,0.5,pieno,R" titolo="Points on the branches and center $C$."}

- $A$
- $B$
- $R$
- $Q$

## Explanation

The center is $C=(1,-1)$ and $P=(3,2)$. The vector from $C$ to $P$ is $(2,3)$ and the vector from $C$ to the reflected point is $(-2,-3)$. The resulting point is $(-1,-4)$, labeled $Q$.
