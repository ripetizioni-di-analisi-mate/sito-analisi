Which rectangular hyperbola passes through $(2,3)$ and has the same asymptotes as $xy=4$?

- $xy=6$
- $xy=4$
- $(x-2)(y-3)=6$
- $x^2-y^2=6$

## Explanation

The stated asymptotes are the coordinate axes, so the required curve has the form $xy=K$. The point $(2,3)$ gives $K=2\cdot3=6$.
