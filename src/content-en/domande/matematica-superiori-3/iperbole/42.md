Take a point with positive coordinates on $xy=12$ and project it orthogonally onto both axes. What is the area of the rectangle formed by the point, its two projections and the origin?

- $12$
- $6$
- $24$
- It depends on the point

## Explanation

The side lengths are $x$ and $y$, both positive on this branch. Their product $xy$ is constant on the hyperbola and equals $12$.
