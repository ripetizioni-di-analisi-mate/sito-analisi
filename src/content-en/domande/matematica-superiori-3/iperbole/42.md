Which rectangular hyperbola with centre at the origin and foci on the $x$-axis passes through $P(5,4)$?

- $x^2-y^2=9$
- $x^2-y^2=41$
- $y^2-x^2=9$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{25}-\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$

## Explanation

A rectangular hyperbola has equal semi-axes, so with centre at the origin and foci on the $x$-axis its equation is $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}-\dfrac{y^2}{a^2}=1$, that is, $x^2-y^2=a^2$. Since $P$ lies on the curve, its coordinates satisfy the equation, therefore $a^2=5^2-4^2=9$. The required hyperbola is thus $x^2-y^2=9$, with vertices $(\pm3,0)$.

The equation $\dfrac{x^2}{25}-\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$ has different semi-axes and does not pass through $P$, because at $P$ the left-hand side equals $1-1=0$. The equation $x^2-y^2=41$ comes from adding the squares of the coordinates instead of subtracting them.
