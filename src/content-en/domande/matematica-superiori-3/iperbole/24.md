How far apart are the foci of $\dfrac{y^2}{9}-\dfrac{x^2}{16}=1$?

- $5$
- $8$
- $10$
- $14$

## Explanation

We have $c=\sqrt{9+16}=5$. The foci lie on opposite sides of the center along the $y$-axis and are $2c=10$ units apart.

The foci are $(0,-5)$ and $(0,5)$. Because they have the same abscissa, the length of the segment between them is $|5-(-5)|=10$. The number $5$ measures only the distance from the center to one focus.
