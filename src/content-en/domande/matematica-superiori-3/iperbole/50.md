Which decomposition of the numerator gives $\dfrac{3x+1}{x-2}=3+\dfrac{7}{x-2}$?

- $3x+1=3(x-2)+1$
- $3x+1=3(x-2)-5$
- $3x+1=3(x-2)+7$
- $3x+1=(x-2)+3$

## Explanation

The multiple $3(x-2)$ gives $3x-6$. Adding $7$ gives $3x+1$. Dividing by $x-2$ produces the required form for $x\ne2$.
