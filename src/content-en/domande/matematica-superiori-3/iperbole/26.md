With $a$ fixed, which change increases the eccentricity of a hyperbola?

- Decrease the conjugate semiaxis $b$.
- Translate the center along the $x$-axis.
- Increase the conjugate semiaxis $b$.
- Swap the two coordinates.

## Explanation

We have $e^2=1+\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}$. With $a$ fixed, increasing $b>0$ increases $e$. Translation and swapping the coordinates both preserve lengths.
