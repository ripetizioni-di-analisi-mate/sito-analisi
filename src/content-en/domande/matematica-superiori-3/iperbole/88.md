A student looking for the positive asymptote slope of $\dfrac{x^2}{16}-\dfrac{y^2}{81}=1$ gets $\dfrac{81}{16}$. What is the actual slope?

- $\dfrac49$
- $\dfrac{81}{16}$
- $\dfrac94$
- $\dfrac{16}{81}$

## Explanation

The ratio of denominators is the square of the slope. The half-side lengths are $4$ and $9$, so the positive slope is $\dfrac94$.
