A rectangular hyperbola has the coordinate axes as asymptotes and passes through $P=(-4,-3)$. Find its equation.

- $xy=-12$
- $xy=7$
- $xy=-7$
- $xy=12$

## Explanation

With the coordinate axes as asymptotes, the form is $xy=K$. Substituting $P$ gives $K=(-4)(-3)=12$. The positive product places the other branch in the first quadrant.
