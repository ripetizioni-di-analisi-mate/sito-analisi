Which equation has asymptotes $x=3$ and $y=-2$ and passes through $(4,2)$?

- $y=-2+\dfrac4{x-3}$
- $y=2+\dfrac4{x-3}$
- $y=-2+\dfrac4{x+3}$
- $y=-2-\dfrac4{x-3}$

## Explanation

The asymptotes give $y=-2+\dfrac K{x-3}$. Substituting $x=4$ and $y=2$ gives $2=-2+K$, so $K=4$.

At the given point the offsets from the center $(3,-2)$ are $1$ and $4$, with product $4$. Choosing $-\dfrac4{x-3}$ would keep the same asymptotes but would give ordinate $-6$ at $x=4$. The point therefore determines the sign as well as the magnitude of the parameter.
