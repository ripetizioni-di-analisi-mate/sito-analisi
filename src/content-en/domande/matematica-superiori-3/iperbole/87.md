For what $t$ is $(t,-2)$ on the curve $(x-3)(y+1)=10$?

- $7$
- $-7$
- $13$
- $-13$

## Explanation

The second coordinate relative to the center is $-2+1=-1$. Thus $(t-3)(-1)=10$, giving $t-3=-10$ and $t=-7$.

The point found gives $(-7-3)(-2+1)=(-10)(-1)=10$. The given ordinate is below the center’s ordinate $-1$, so obtaining a positive product also requires the abscissa to lie left of the center’s abscissa $3$. The sign of $t-3$ can thus be checked before calculating.
