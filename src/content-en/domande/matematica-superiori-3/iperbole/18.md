Which points of the line through the foci belong to the locus $|PF_1-PF_2|=6$ if the foci are $(-3,0)$ and $(3,0)$?

- The points with $|x|\leq3$.
- Only the points with $|x|=3$.
- The points with $|x|\geq3$.
- Every point on the line.

## Explanation

For $x\geq3$, the difference is $(x+3)-(x-3)=6$. By symmetry $x\leq-3$ also works. Between the foci, the absolute difference is $2|x|<6$.
