A hyperbola has foci $(0,\pm5)$ and an absolute difference between the distances of $8$. Find its eccentricity.

- $e=\dfrac85$
- $e=\dfrac45$
- $e=\dfrac54$
- $e=\dfrac58$

## Explanation

The foci are $5$ units from the center, so $c=5$. The difference is $2a=8$, so $a=4$. The ratio $\dfrac ca$ is $\dfrac54$.
