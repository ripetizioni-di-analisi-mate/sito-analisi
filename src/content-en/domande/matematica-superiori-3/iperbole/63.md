For $y=4+\dfrac{K}{x-2}$, the branches lie above and to the left, and below and to the right, of the center. Which value of $K$ is consistent?

- $-6$
- $6$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

To the left of the center $x-2<0$, and above the center $y-4>0$. Their product $K$ is negative. If $K=0$, the graph is a horizontal line with one point excluded.
