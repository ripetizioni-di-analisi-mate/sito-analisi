For which abscissas is $f(x)=\dfrac{4}{x-1}$ negative?

- $x<1$
- $x>1$
- $x\ne1$
- $x\leq1$

## Explanation

The numerator is positive, so the ratio has the sign of its denominator. The ratio is negative for $x-1<0$, or $x<1$. The point $x=1$ is excluded from the domain.

For example, $f(0)=-4$ is negative, while $f(2)=4$ is positive. The abscissa $1$ separates the two intervals but lies on neither part of the curve. The numerator never vanishes, so the function has no zeros.
