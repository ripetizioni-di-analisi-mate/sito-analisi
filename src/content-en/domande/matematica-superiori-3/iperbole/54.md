For $f(x)=-2+\dfrac{3}{x+4}$, in which half-plane is the branch with $x>-4$?

- $y>-2$
- $y<-2$
- $y>3$
- $y=-2$

## Explanation

On that branch $x+4>0$, so $\dfrac3{x+4}>0$. Adding it to $-2$ gives a value greater than $-2$.

For $x=0$ on the right branch, $f(0)=-2+\dfrac34=-\dfrac54$. This point is above $y=-2$ even though its ordinate is negative. The comparison must therefore use the height of the horizontal asymptote, distinct from the height of the $x$-axis.
