Let $P=(5,2)$ lie on the right branch of a horizontal hyperbola centered at the origin. What is its image under reflection through the center?

- $(5,-2)$
- $(-5,2)$
- $(2,5)$
- $(-5,-2)$

## Explanation

The origin is the midpoint of the segment from $P$ to its image, so both signs change. The new point is on the left branch.
