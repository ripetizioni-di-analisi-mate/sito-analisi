How many points lie on both $x=4$ and $\dfrac{x^2}{25}-\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$?

- $N=1$
- $N=2$
- $N=4$
- $N=0$

## Explanation

Substituting $x=4$ gives $\dfrac{y^2}{9}=\dfrac{16}{25}-1=-\dfrac{9}{25}$. The left-hand side is always greater than or equal to zero, so the intersection is empty.
