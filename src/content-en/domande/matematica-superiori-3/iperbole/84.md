For $\dfrac{x^2}{16}-\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$, which point lies on the right branch?

- $(0,3)$
- $(-4,0)$
- $(4,0)$
- $(4,3)$

## Explanation

The right branch requires $x\geq4$. At $x=4$ the positive term equals $1$, so $y=0$. The point $(-4,0)$ is the left vertex.

The point $(4,3)$ does have abscissa at the edge of the right branch, but substitution gives $1-1=0$. The required value is $1$, so at that abscissa the ordinate is zero. The point $(0,3)$ is also excluded because its abscissa lies between the vertices.
