For $x^2-y^2=4$, which point is its intersection with $y=-x+4$?

- $\left(\dfrac32,\dfrac52\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac52,\dfrac32\right)$
- $(2,2)$
- $\left(-\dfrac52,\dfrac32\right)$

## Explanation

Substitution gives $x^2-(-x+4)^2=8x-16=4$. Therefore $x=\dfrac52$ and $y=4-\dfrac52=\dfrac32$.
