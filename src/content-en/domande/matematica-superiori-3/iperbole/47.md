After completing the square, what does $x^2-y^2-4x-2y+3=0$ represent?

- Two intersecting lines.
- A hyperbola with a horizontal transverse axis.
- A hyperbola with a vertical transverse axis.
- A single line.

## Explanation

This gives $(x-2)^2-(y+1)^2=0$. The equality holds if $x-2=y+1$ or $x-2=-(y+1)$. The lines intersect at the center $(2,-1)$.
