For what $p$ is $(x-1)^2-(y+2)^2=p$ a pair of lines?

- $1$
- $-1$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

When $p=0$, the left-hand side is a difference of squares equal to zero and factors into two linear factors. For every $p\ne0$, dividing by $p$ instead gives a standard hyperbola equation.
