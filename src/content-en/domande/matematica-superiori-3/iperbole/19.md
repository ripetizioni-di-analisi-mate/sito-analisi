A student assigns vertices $(\pm5,0)$ to $\dfrac{y^2}{4}-\dfrac{x^2}{25}=1$ because $25>4$. What are the actual vertices?

- $(0,\pm2)$
- $(0,\pm5)$
- $(\pm2,0)$
- $(\pm5,0)$

## Explanation

The positive term is the $y$ term, so the transverse axis is vertical. Setting $x=0$ gives $y^2=4$. Setting $y=0$ leaves the left-hand side at most zero.
