For $x^2-y^2=9$, which of the lines through the origin meets both branches?

- $y=x$
- $y=2x$
- $y=\dfrac{x}{2}$
- $y=-x$

## Explanation

With $y=mx$, the equation becomes $(1-m^2)x^2=9$. For real values of $x$ we need $1-m^2>0$, or $|m|<1$. Among the listed slopes only $\dfrac12$ qualifies.

The line $y=\dfrac x2$ gives $\dfrac34x^2=9$, so there is one positive root and one negative root. The line of slope $2$ instead gives $-3x^2=9$. For slope $1$ or $-1$ the left-hand side is zero throughout the line, because these are the asymptotes.
