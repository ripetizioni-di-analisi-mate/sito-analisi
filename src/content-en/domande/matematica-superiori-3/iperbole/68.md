For $x^2-y^2=9$, which of the lines through the origin meets both branches?

- $y=x$
- $y=2x$
- $y=\dfrac{x}{2}$
- $y=-x$

## Explanation

With $y=mx$, the equation becomes $(1-m^2)x^2=9$. For real values of $x$ we need $1-m^2>0$, or $|m|<1$. Among the listed slopes only $\dfrac12$ qualifies.
