Identify the quadrants containing the branches of $xy=-15$.

- Second and fourth.
- First and third.
- First and fourth.
- Second and third.

## Explanation

The product is negative, so the coordinates have opposite signs. For example, $(-3,5)$ is in the second quadrant and $(3,-5)$ is in the fourth.

Solving for the ordinate gives $y=-\dfrac{15}{x}$. When $x<0$, $y$ is positive. When $x>0$, $y$ is negative. In the first and third quadrants the coordinates have the same sign, which would give a positive product.
