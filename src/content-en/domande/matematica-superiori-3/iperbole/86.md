The line $y=m x$ is an asymptote of $\dfrac{y^2}{a^2}-\dfrac{x^2}{b^2}=1$. If $a=2b$, which value can $m$ have?

- $-2$
- $-\dfrac12$
- $0$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Setting the quadratic expression equal to zero gives $m^2=\dfrac{a^2}{b^2}=4$. The slopes are therefore $2$ and $-2$.
