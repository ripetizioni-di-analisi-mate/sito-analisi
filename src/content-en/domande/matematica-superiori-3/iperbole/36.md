Which horizontal line above the $x$-axis intersects $\dfrac{y^2}{9}-\dfrac{x^2}{4}=1$ at two points with abscissas $\pm2$?

- $y=3$
- $y=3\sqrt2$
- $y=2\sqrt3$
- $y=6$

## Explanation

For $x=\pm2$, we get $\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1+\dfrac44=2$. The possible ordinates are $\pm3\sqrt2$. The line above the $x$-axis takes the positive ordinate.
