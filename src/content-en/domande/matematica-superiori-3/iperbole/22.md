For what $b>0$ does the hyperbola $\dfrac{x^2}{4}-\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$ pass through $(\sqrt5,1)$?

- $4$
- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $2$

## Explanation

Substituting the coordinates gives $\dfrac54-\dfrac{1}{b^2}=1$, so $\dfrac{1}{b^2}=\dfrac14$. It follows that $b^2=4$ and the positive root is $2$.
