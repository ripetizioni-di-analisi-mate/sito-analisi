The average cost per item is $C(n)=4+\dfrac{96}{n}$ euros. What is the smallest number of items $n$ for which the cost is at most $12$ euros?

- $8$
- $13$
- $12$
- $24$

## Explanation

Subtracting $4$ gives $\dfrac{96}{n}\leq8$. Since $n>0$, we get $96\leq8n$, or $n\geq12$. The smallest allowed integer is $12$.
