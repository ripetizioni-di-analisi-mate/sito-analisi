Where does $y=-4$ intersect $x^2-y^2=9$?

- $(5,\pm4)$
- $(\pm5,4)$
- $(\pm\sqrt7,-4)$
- $(\pm5,-4)$

## Explanation

With $y=-4$, the equation becomes $x^2=9+16=25$, so $x=\pm5$. Both points have the same ordinate $-4$.

Substituting either pair gives $25-16=9$. The line is below the $x$-axis, so the second coordinate is negative at both points. The abscissa changes sign because the horizontal line crosses one branch on the left and one on the right.
