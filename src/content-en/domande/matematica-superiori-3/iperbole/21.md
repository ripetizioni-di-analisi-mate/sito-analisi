Which standard equation is equivalent to $4x^2-9y^2=-36$?

- $\dfrac{y^2}{4}-\dfrac{x^2}{9}=1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{9}-\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1$
- $\dfrac{y^2}{9}-\dfrac{x^2}{4}=1$
- $\dfrac{y^2}{4}+\dfrac{x^2}{9}=1$

## Explanation

Dividing every term by $-36$ makes the coefficient of $y^2$ positive and the coefficient of $x^2$ negative. This gives $-\dfrac{x^2}{9}+\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1$.
