How many common points do $y=\dfrac{12}{x}$ and $y=x+1$ have?

- $2$
- $0$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

For $x\ne0$, equating the ordinates gives $x^2+x-12=0$. The factorization $(x+4)(x-3)=0$ gives two distinct, admissible abscissas.

The line gives ordinates $-4+1=-3$ and $3+1=4$. As a check, the products $(-4)(-3)$ and $3\cdot4$ both equal $12$. The points are in the third and first quadrants, respectively, so there is one on each branch.
