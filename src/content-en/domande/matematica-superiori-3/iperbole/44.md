What equation results from translating $xy=-6$ by the vector $(2,-3)$?

- $(x+2)(y-3)=-6$
- $(x-2)(y+3)=6$
- $(x-2)(y+3)=-6$
- $(x-2)(y-3)=-6$

## Explanation

The coordinates before translation are $x-2$ and $y+3$. Substituting them in the product $xy=-6$ gives $(x-2)(y+3)=-6$.
