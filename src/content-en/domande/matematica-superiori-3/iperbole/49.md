What happens to eccentricity when a hyperbola is translated by $(5,-2)$?

- It increases by $5$.
- It is multiplied by $\sqrt{29}$.
- It becomes $1$.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

Translation moves the center, vertices, and foci together. It preserves the distances $a$ and $c$, so the ratio $\dfrac ca$ stays the same.

For example, a vertex at distance $a$ from the center remains $a$ units away after the shift. The components of the translation vector cancel when the coordinates of the two points are subtracted. The number $\sqrt{29}$ is the length of the shift, not a scale factor.
