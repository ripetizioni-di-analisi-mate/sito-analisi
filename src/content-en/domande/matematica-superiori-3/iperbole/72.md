For two foci $8$ units apart, what is the locus where the absolute difference between the distances is zero?

- The line through the two foci.
- The perpendicular bisector of the segment joining the foci.
- The circle with the segment joining the foci as its diameter.
- Only the midpoint of the two foci.

## Explanation

A zero difference means that the two distances are equal. The points equidistant from two distinct points form the perpendicular bisector of their segment.
