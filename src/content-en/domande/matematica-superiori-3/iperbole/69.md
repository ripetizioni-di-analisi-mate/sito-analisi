What is the midpoint of the chord cut by $y=-1$ on the curve $(x-2)^2-(y+4)^2=16$?

- $(2,-1)$
- $(2,-4)$
- $(7,-1)$
- $(-3,-1)$

## Explanation

With $y=-1$ we get $(x-2)^2=25$, so the abscissas are $2-5=-3$ and $2+5=7$. Their average is $2$ and their common ordinate is $-1$.

The endpoints are $5$ units left and right of the vertical line through the center. The midpoint therefore shares the center’s abscissa but has ordinate $-1$ instead of $-4$. The chord lies on the given line and has length $7-(-3)=10$.
