A rectangular frame has an interior area of $48\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. How tall is it if its base is $16\,\mathrm{cm}$? Give the height in centimetres.

- $h=4$
- $h=3$
- $h=6$
- $h=8$

## Explanation

With $x$ the base and $y$ the height, the product of the measurements is $xy=48$. On the branch with positive coordinates, $x=16$ gives $y=\dfrac{48}{16}=3$.
