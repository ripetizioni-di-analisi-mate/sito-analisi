For what $q$ does the line $y=x+q$ have no intersection with $x^2-y^2=4$?

- $q=1$
- $q=0$
- $q=-1$
- $q=2$

## Explanation

Substitution gives $-2qx-q^2=4$. If $q\ne0$, this is a linear equation with one solution. If $q=0$, it becomes $0=4$, so the line is the asymptote and misses the hyperbola.
