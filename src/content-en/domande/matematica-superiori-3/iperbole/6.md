Find the distance between the two intersection points of $x=5$ and $x^2-y^2=9$.

- $d=4$
- $d=16$
- $d=8$
- $d=32$

## Explanation

Substituting $x=5$ gives $25-y^2=9$, so $y=\pm4$. The points have the same abscissa and are $|4-(-4)|=8$ units apart.

The points are $(5,4)$ and $(5,-4)$, both on the right branch. The distance is measured vertically. There are $4$ units from the first point to the $x$-axis and another $4$ from the axis to the other point. The value $4$ is thus half the requested length.
