The hypotenuse of a right triangle has length $\sqrt{x^2+9}$ with $x>0$. What must $x$ be for the hypotenuse to have length $5$?

- $16$
- $\sqrt{34}$
- $4$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The equation is $\sqrt{x^2+9}=5$. Squaring gives $x^2+9=25$, hence $x^2=16$. Take the positive root because $x$ is a side length.
