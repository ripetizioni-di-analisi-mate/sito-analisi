What is the real domain of $\sqrt{x-4}$?

- $(4,+\infty)$
- $[4,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-\infty,4]$

## Explanation

The radicand must be greater than or equal to zero. Thus $x-4\geq0$. At $x=4$, the square root equals $0$ and is defined. For $x<4$ the radicand is negative and the root is not real. For $x>4$ the root is positive and defined. Including $4$ gives the ray $[4,+\infty)$, closed on the left.
