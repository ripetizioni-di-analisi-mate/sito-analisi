What is $\sqrt{(-6)^2}$?

- $-6$
- $36$
- $\sqrt6$
- $6$

## Explanation

The radicand equals $36$. The square root denotes the nonnegative number whose square is $36$, so it is $6$. The number $-6$ also has square $36$, but it is the negative solution of $y^2=36$. The square-root sign denotes just the nonnegative solution, so here it gives $|-6|=6$.
