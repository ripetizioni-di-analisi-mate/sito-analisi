Solve $\sqrt{x+5}-\sqrt{x}=1$.

- $\{16\}$
- $\{1\}$
- $\{4\}$
- $\{9\}$

## Explanation

For $x\geq0$, rewrite the equation as $\sqrt{x+5}=1+\sqrt{x}$. Squaring both nonnegative sides gives $x+5=1+2\sqrt{x}+x$, so $\sqrt{x}=2$. At $x=4$ we have $3-2=1$. The terms in $x$ cancel, leaving $4=2\sqrt{x}$. The cross term is what retains the root. Since $2$ is also positive, the second squaring gives $x=4$.
