For which real values of $k$ does $\sqrt{x-1}=k$ have a solution?

- $k\in\mathbb R$
- $k\geq0$
- $k\leq0$
- $k>0$

## Explanation

The square root is nonnegative, so we need $k\geq0$. For every such $k$, $x=1+k^2$ provides a solution. In particular $k=0$ gives $x=1$. At the constructed value, $x-1=k^2$, whose square root is $|k|$. This equals $k$ for every nonnegative value, including the boundary zero.
