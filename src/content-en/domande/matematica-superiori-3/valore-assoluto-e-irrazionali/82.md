A controller uses the formula $R(t)=\sqrt{t-1}$ only while its reading is *closer to zero than to four*. What further upper bound must $t$ satisfy in addition to $t\geq1$?

- $t<3$
- $t\leq5$
- $t<5$
- $t<17$

## Explanation

The midpoint of $0$ and $4$ is $2$, so a nonnegative reading is closer to zero when it is less than $2$. From $\sqrt{t-1}<2$ we get $t-1<4$, that is, $t<5$. At $t=5$ the distances would be equal.
