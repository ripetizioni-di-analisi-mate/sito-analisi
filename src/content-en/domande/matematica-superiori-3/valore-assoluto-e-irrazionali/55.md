Solve $\sqrt{x-2}\leq3$.

- $[2,11]$
- $(2,11]$
- $(-\infty,11]$
- $[2,5]$

## Explanation

The domain requires $x-2\geq0$. Since the right-hand side is $3>0$, squaring gives $x-2\leq9$. The intersection of the conditions is $[2,11]$. At $x=2$ the square root is zero, and at $x=11$ it reaches $3$. To the left of $2$ the expression is undefined. To the right of $11$ it is defined but exceeds the threshold.
