The expression $\dfrac{x-4}{\sqrt x-2}$ simplifies to $\sqrt x+2$. Which point on the graph of the simplified formula is missing from the original graph?

- $(4,4)$
- $(2,4)$
- $(4,0)$
- $(0,2)$

## Explanation

For $x\geq0$, factor $x-4=(\sqrt x-2)(\sqrt x+2)$. Cancelling the common factor requires $x\ne4$. At $x=4$ the simplified formula would give vertical coordinate $\sqrt4+2=4$, but the original quotient is undefined.
