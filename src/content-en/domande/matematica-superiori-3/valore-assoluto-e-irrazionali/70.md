The real number $x$, rounded to the nearest integer, gives $20$ and also satisfies the inequality $|x-18|<2$. Which of the following numbers can $x$ be?

- $20.6$
- $19.8$
- $19.2$
- $20.1$

## Explanation

The inequality $|x-18|<2$ is equivalent to $-2<x-18<2$, that is $16<x<20$. The numbers $20.6$ and $20.1$ are greater than $20$, so they do not satisfy the inequality. The number $19.2$ satisfies the inequality, but it rounds to $19$. The number $19.8$ belongs to the interval $(16,20)$ and rounds to $20$, so it is the only possible value among those listed.
