A thermometer rounds the actual temperature to the nearest whole degree. Its display reads $20\,^\circ\mathrm C$. Which actual temperature could have produced that reading and also satisfies $|T-18|<2$, with $T$ in degrees Celsius?

- $20.6\,^\circ\mathrm C$
- $19.8\,^\circ\mathrm C$
- $19.2\,^\circ\mathrm C$
- $20.1\,^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

The distance constraint requires $16<T<20$. The value $19.8$ lies in this interval and rounds to $20$. The value $19.2$ would display as $19$, while both values above $20$ violate the distance constraint.
