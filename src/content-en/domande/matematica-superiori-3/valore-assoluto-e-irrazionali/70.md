A temperature $T$ must stay strictly less than $2\,\mathrm{^\circ C}$ from $18\,\mathrm{^\circ C}$. Which reading in degrees Celsius is allowed?

- $15$
- $19$
- $16$
- $20$

## Explanation

The constraint $|T-18|<2$ is equivalent to $16<T<20$. The readings $16$ and $20$ differ by exactly $2$, while $15$ differs by $3$. Only $19$ remains.
