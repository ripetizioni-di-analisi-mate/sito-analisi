Which set is the real domain of $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{(x-3)^2}}$?

- $(3,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $[3,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{3\}$

## Explanation

The square in the radicand is nonnegative for every $x$. In the denominator, however, we need $\sqrt{(x-3)^2}=|x-3|\neq0$. Only $x=3$ is excluded.
