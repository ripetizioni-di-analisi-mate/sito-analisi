Solve $\sqrt{3-x}\leq2$.

- $[-1,+\infty)$
- $(-1,3]$
- $(-\infty,3]$
- $[-1,3]$

## Explanation

We need $0\leq3-x\leq4$. The first condition gives $x\leq3$ and the second $x\geq-1$. Both endpoints are allowed. The intersection is $[-1,3]$. At $x=3$ the radicand is zero and the root is $0\leq2$. At $x=-1$ the radicand is $4$ and equality holds. The domain gives the upper endpoint and the threshold gives the lower one.
