A classmate obtains $x\geq-1$ from $3-x\leq4$ and proposes it as the solution of $\sqrt{3-x}\leq2$. What interval remains after checking the radicand?

- $[-1,3)$
- $[-1,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,3]$
- $[-1,3]$

## Explanation

The real square root requires $3-x\geq0$, so $x\leq3$. Intersecting this restriction with $x\geq-1$ gives $[-1,3]$. At the right endpoint the radical equals zero, which satisfies the comparison with $2$.
