What is $|-7|$?

- $-7$
- $7$
- $0$
- $49$

## Explanation

The number $-7$ is seven units from zero. Thus $|-7|=7$. Since $-7<0$, the definition gives its opposite, $-(-7)=7$. This is the same distance as for the point $7$ on the other side of the origin.
