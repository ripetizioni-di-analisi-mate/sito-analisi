In $\sqrt{x+1}=|x-1|$ both sides are nonnegative on the domain. Which pair of horizontal coordinates is obtained without introducing extraneous solutions?

- $-3,\ 0$
- $-1,\ 3$
- $0,\ 1$
- $0,\ 3$

## Explanation

The domain is $x\geq-1$. On this domain squaring is reversible and gives $x+1=(x-1)^2$, or $x(x-3)=0$. Both results lie in the domain and are therefore solutions.
