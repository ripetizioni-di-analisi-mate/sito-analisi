An integer $n$ is chosen among those satisfying $|2n-1|<6$. What is the sum of the smallest and largest allowed integers?

- $1$
- $0$
- $6$
- $5$

## Explanation

The double inequality $-6<2n-1<6$ is equivalent to $-\dfrac52<n<\dfrac72$. The extreme integers are therefore $-2$ and $3$, whose sum is $1$.
