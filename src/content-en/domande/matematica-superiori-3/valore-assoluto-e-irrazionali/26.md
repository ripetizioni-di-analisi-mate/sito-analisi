Solve $|2x-6|<10$.

- $(-2,8)$
- $[-2,8]$
- $(-8,2)$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(8,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Dividing by $2$ gives $|x-3|<5$. The unknown must be less than $5$ units from $3$, so $-2<x<8$. At the left boundary, $2x-6$ equals $-10$, and at the right boundary it equals $10$, so both absolute values are $10$. The strict inequality excludes both endpoints and leaves the open interval.
