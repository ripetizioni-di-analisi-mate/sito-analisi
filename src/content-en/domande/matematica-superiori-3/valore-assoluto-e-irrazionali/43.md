What is the real domain of $\sqrt{2-x}+\sqrt{x-3}$?

- $\varnothing$
- $(-\infty,2]\cup[3,+\infty)$
- $(2,3)$
- $[2,3]$

## Explanation

The first square root exists for $x\leq2$, and the second for $x\geq3$. No real number satisfies both conditions. The domain is empty. The intersection of the rays $(-\infty,2]$ and $[3,+\infty)$ is empty. The endpoints do not help either. At $x=2$ the second radicand is negative, and at $x=3$ the first is negative.
