For which $k$ does the domain of $\sqrt{2-x}+\sqrt{x-k}$ consist of a single point?

- $k=2$
- $k=-2$
- $k=3$
- $k=0$

## Explanation

The first radical requires $x\leq2$, and the second requires $x\geq k$. For $k<2$ the domain is a segment of positive length, and for $k>2$ it is empty. The conditions select the single point $x=2$ when $k=2$.
