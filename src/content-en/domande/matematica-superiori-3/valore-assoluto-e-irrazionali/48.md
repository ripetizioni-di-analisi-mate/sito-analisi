Solve $\sqrt{x+3}=\sqrt{5-x}$.

- $\varnothing$
- $\{-1\}$
- $\{1\}$
- $\{4\}$

## Explanation

The common domain is $[-3,5]$. Both sides are nonnegative roots, so set the radicands equal. From $x+3=5-x$ we get $x=1$, which belongs to the domain. In the equation of the radicands, add $x$ and subtract $3$ to get $2x=2$. At $x=1$, $x+3=4$ and $5-x=4$, so both roots equal $2$.
