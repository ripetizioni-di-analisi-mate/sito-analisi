In $\sqrt{x^2-4}=\sqrt{x-2}$, equating the radicands gives $x=-1$ or $x=2$. Which conclusion follows from the common domain?

- Only $x=-1$ remains.
- Both are excluded.
- Only $x=2$ remains.
- Both remain.

## Explanation

The second radical requires $x\geq2$. The first is also defined on this ray, since $x^2-4\geq0$. The candidate $2$ gives two zero radicals, while at $-1$ both radicands are negative.
