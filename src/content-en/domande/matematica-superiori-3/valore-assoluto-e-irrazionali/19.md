At which point does the graph of $y=|2x-6|$ meet the $x$-axis?

- $(6,0)$
- $(0,3)$
- $(-3,0)$
- $(3,0)$

## Explanation

On the $x$-axis the y-coordinate is zero, so solve $|2x-6|=0$. This implies $2x-6=0$ and gives $x=3$. Substituting $x=3$ gives $|6-6|=0$. The argument has a unique zero because the coefficient of $x$ in $2x-6$ is nonzero. The point on the graph is therefore $(3,0)$.
