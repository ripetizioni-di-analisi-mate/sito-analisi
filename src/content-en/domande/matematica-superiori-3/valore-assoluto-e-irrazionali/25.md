For which $k$ does $|x-5|=k$ have exactly one real solution?

- $k>0$
- $k<0$
- $k=0$
- $k=5$

## Explanation

If $k>0$, the two points are $5-k$ and $5+k$. If $k<0$, a distance cannot take that value. Only $k=0$ gives one solution, the center $5$. On the graph, the line at height $k=0$ meets $y=|x-5|$ only at the vertex. Any line at positive height instead crosses both branches of the curve.
