To represent $|4-2x|>2$, start with the real line and erase the points that fail the inequality. Which part must be erased, including its endpoints?

- $[2,6]$
- $[1,3]$
- $[-1,1]$
- $[-3,-1]$

## Explanation

Since $|4-2x|=2|x-2|$, the distance from $x$ to $2$ must exceed $1$. Every point between $1$ and $3$ has distance at most $1$. Erasing $[1,3]$ leaves the two open rays that solve the inequality.
