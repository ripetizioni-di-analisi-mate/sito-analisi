Solve $|4-2x|>2$.

- $(-\infty,-1)\cup(1,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,1)\cup(3,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,1]\cup[3,+\infty)$
- $(1,3)$

## Explanation

Since $|4-2x|=2|x-2|$, we require $|x-2|>1$. The points are more than $1$ unit from $2$, so they are before $1$ or after $3$. At $x=1$ and $x=3$, the original expression equals exactly $2$, so both points are excluded. The center $x=2$ instead gives zero and is not part of the solution.
