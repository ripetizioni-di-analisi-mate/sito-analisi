What is the solution set of the equation $|x-2|=4$?

- $\{2,6\}$
- $[-2,6]$
- $\{-2,6\}$
- $\{6\}$

## Explanation

The solutions are the two points at distance $4$ from $2$. We have $x-2=4$ or $x-2=-4$, so $x=6$ or $x=-2$. On the number line, $4$ is added to or subtracted from $2$. Both cases pass the check $|6-2|=|-2-2|=4$. The center $2$ itself has distance zero.
