Solve $|x|+|x-2|=6$.

- $[-2,4]$
- $\{0,2\}$
- $\{-2,4\}$
- $\{-3,3\}$

## Explanation

The sum is $2$ on the segment $[0,2]$. To the left it equals $2-2x$, and $2-2x=6$ gives $x=-2$. To the right it equals $2x-2$, and $2x-2=6$ gives $x=4$. Both values lie in their respective branches.
