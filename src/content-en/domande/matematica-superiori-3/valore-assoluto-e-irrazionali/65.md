The function $f(x)=|x|+|x-2|$ takes the value $6$ at one point to the left of $0$ and one to the right of $2$. How far apart are these solutions on the line?

- $2$
- $4$
- $6$
- $8$

## Explanation

On the left the sum is $-x+(2-x)=2-2x$, which equals $6$ at $x=-2$. On the right it is $x+(x-2)=2x-2$, which equals $6$ at $x=4$. The distance between the two points is $4-(-2)=6$.
