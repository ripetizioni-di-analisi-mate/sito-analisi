Solve $|x^2-4|\geq4$.

- $[-2\sqrt2,2\sqrt2]$
- $\{0\}$
- $(-\infty,-2\sqrt2]\cup\{0\}\cup[2\sqrt2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-2\sqrt2]\cup[2\sqrt2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The condition $x^2-4\leq-4$ gives $x=0$. The other branch is $x^2-4\geq4$, or $x^2\geq8$. This gives the two exterior intervals, together with the isolated point $0$.
