A table lists the solutions of $|x^2-4|\geq4$ as two rays extending outwards from $-2\sqrt2$ and $2\sqrt2$, including their endpoints. Which isolated point must be added?

- $x=\sqrt2$
- $x=-2$
- $x=0$
- $x=2$

## Explanation

Besides the branch $x^2-4\geq4$, which gives the two rays, we must examine $x^2-4\leq-4$. This branch is equivalent to $x^2\leq0$, true only at $x=0$. At that point the absolute value is exactly $4$.
