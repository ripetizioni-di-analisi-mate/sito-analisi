On the number line, you walk from $2$ to $5$ via $x$. Which stopping points make the total length $|x-2|+|x-5|=3$?

- $[2,5]$
- $\{2,5\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac72\right\}$
- $(-\infty,2]\cup[5,+\infty)$

## Explanation

If $2\leq x\leq5$, the two legs have lengths $x-2$ and $5-x$, which add to $3$. A stop outside the segment adds twice the distance to the nearer endpoint to those $3$ units.
