Solve $|x-2|+|x-5|=3$.

- $[2,5]$
- $(2,5)$
- $(-\infty,2]\cup[5,+\infty)$
- $\{2,5\}$

## Explanation

Between $2$ and $5$, the distances add to the length of the segment, which is $3$. Moving away from the segment by $d>0$ makes the sum $3+2d>3$. Thus precisely the points of $[2,5]$ solve the equation. For example, at $x=3$ the sum is $1+2=3$. To the left of the segment, at $x=1$, it is instead $1+4=5$. At each endpoint, $2$ and $5$, one distance is zero and the other is $3$, so both are included.
