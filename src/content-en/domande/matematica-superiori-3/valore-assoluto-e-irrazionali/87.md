At which points does $|x-2|=|x+2|$ hold?

- $\{0\}$
- $\mathbb R$
- $\{-2,2\}$
- $[-2,2]$

## Explanation

The absolute values measure distances from $2$ and $-2$. The midpoint is $0$, and it is the only equidistant point. To its left or right, one distance is smaller than the other.
