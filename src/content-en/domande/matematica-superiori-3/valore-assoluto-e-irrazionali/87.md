On a line, choose a point $x$ whose distance to $2$ is at most half its distance to $-2$. Which interval meets the requirement?

- $\left[\dfrac23,6\right]$
- $[0,6]$
- $\left[-2,\dfrac23\right]$
- $\left(-\infty,\dfrac23\right]\cup[6,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The condition is equivalent to $2|x-2|\leq|x+2|$. Both sides are nonnegative, so we can pass to $4(x-2)^2\leq(x+2)^2$. The difference of squares factors as $(x-6)(3x-2)$, which is nonpositive between its zeros, including the endpoints.
