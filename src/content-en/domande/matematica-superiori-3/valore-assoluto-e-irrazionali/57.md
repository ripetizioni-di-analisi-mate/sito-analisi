On the number line, the value $\sqrt{x+2}$ must be at least $-3$. What is the left endpoint of the ray satisfying the requirement?

- $0$
- $7$
- $-3$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Wherever it exists, the radical is at least zero and therefore exceeds $-3$. It remains to impose $x+2\geq0$, giving $x\geq-2$. At the endpoint the radical is zero.
