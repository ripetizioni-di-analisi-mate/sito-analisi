Between which consecutive integers does $\sqrt{15}+\sqrt{17}$ lie, given that both radicals exceed $3$?

- $6$ and $7$
- $7$ and $8$
- $8$ and $9$
- $9$ and $10$

## Explanation

Since $\sqrt{15}>3$ and $\sqrt{17}>4$, the sum exceeds $7$. Its square equals $32+2\sqrt{255}$, which is less than $32+2\sqrt{256}=64$. The sum is positive and therefore less than $8$.
