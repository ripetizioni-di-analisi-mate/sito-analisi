How many real solutions does $|x^2-1|=-1$ have?

- One.
- None.
- Four.
- Two.

## Explanation

The left-hand side is an absolute value, so it is always nonnegative. It cannot equal $-1$. For example, at a point where $x^2-1=0$, the left-hand side reaches its minimum, which is $0$. The height $-1$ is below that minimum, and the graph cannot reach it.
