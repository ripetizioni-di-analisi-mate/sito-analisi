Solve $\sqrt{x+2}>x$.

- $[0,2)$
- $(-1,2)$
- $[-2,2]$
- $[-2,2)$

## Explanation

On the domain $x\geq-2$, every value with $x<0$ satisfies the inequality. For $x\geq0$, squaring gives $(x-2)(x+1)<0$, hence $0\leq x<2$. The union of both cases is $[-2,2)$. The point $x=-2$ is allowed because $0>-2$. The point $x=2$ is excluded because both expressions equal $2$. The negative root of the polynomial is not an endpoint of the second branch.
