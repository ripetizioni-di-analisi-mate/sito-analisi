Which set consists of exactly the points in $\mathbb R$ at which the inequality $|x^2-9|>0$ is false?

- $\{3\}$
- $\{-3,3\}$
- $\{-9,9\}$
- $[-3,3]$

## Explanation

An absolute value is positive wherever its argument is nonzero. The zeros of $x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3)$ are $-3$ and $3$. For instance, $x=0$ lies between the zeros and gives absolute value $9$, so it must be retained.
