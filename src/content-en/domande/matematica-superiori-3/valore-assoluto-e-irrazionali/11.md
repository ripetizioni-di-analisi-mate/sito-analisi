Solve $|x^2-9|>0$.

- $\mathbb R$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-3,3\}$
- $\{-3,3\}$
- $(-\infty,-3)\cup(3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The absolute value is positive whenever its argument is nonzero. The zeros of $x^2-9$ are $-3$ and $3$, so only those two values are excluded. Indeed, $x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3)$, so the product is zero only at those two values. The value $x=0$ is allowed too, since $|0-9|=9$. The solution contains the middle interval and both exterior portions.
