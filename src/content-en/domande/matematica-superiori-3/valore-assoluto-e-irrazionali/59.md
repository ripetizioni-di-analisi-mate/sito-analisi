Solve $\sqrt{x^2-4}\geq3$.

- $(-\infty,-\sqrt{13})\cup(\sqrt{13},+\infty)$
- $[-\sqrt{13},\sqrt{13}]$
- $(-\infty,-\sqrt{13}]\cup[\sqrt{13},+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-2]\cup[2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Squaring gives $x^2-4\geq9$, which also makes the root defined. Thus $x^2\geq13$ and the two exterior rays are allowed, including their endpoints.
