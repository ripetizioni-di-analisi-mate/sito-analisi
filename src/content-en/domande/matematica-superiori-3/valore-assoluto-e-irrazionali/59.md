A cord must measure $\sqrt{x^2-4}$ metres and be at least $3$ metres long. What is the smallest integer value of $|x|$ that allows the construction?

- $3$
- $2$
- $4$
- $13$

## Explanation

The requirement is equivalent to $x^2-4\geq9$, which also guarantees that the radical exists. Therefore $|x|\geq\sqrt{13}$. Since $3^2<13<4^2$, the first allowed integer absolute value is $4$.
