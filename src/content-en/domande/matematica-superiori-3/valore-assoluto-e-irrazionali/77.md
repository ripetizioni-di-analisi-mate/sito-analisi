For which value of $k$ does $\sqrt{x-2}$ equal $0$ at $x=k$?

- $4$
- $-2$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

A square root is zero where its radicand is zero. Thus $k-2=0$ and $k=2$. At that point the square root is defined and equals $0$. The domain is $x\geq2$. For $x>2$, the radicand is strictly positive, as is its square root. Thus the zero is also the left endpoint of the function’s domain.
