Which domain restriction on $y=(x-2)^2$ makes $x=2-\sqrt y$ its inverse formula?

- $x\geq0$
- $x\ne2$
- $x\leq2$
- $x\geq2$

## Explanation

The equation gives $|x-2|=\sqrt y$. On the ray $x\leq2$ we have $|x-2|=2-x$, so $x=2-\sqrt y$. Every $y\geq0$ then determines exactly one point on that branch.
