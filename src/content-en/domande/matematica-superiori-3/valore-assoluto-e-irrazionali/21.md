Two parts can have deviations $e_1$ and $e_2$ from their nominal lengths, with $|e_1|\leq2$ mm and $|e_2|\leq3$ mm. What is the smallest guaranteed upper bound on $|e_1+e_2|$?

- $5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The triangle inequality gives $|e_1+e_2|\leq|e_1|+|e_2|\leq5$ mm. When both deviations are positive and maximal, $e_1=2$ mm and $e_2=3$ mm, their sum reaches that bound.
