Solve $|2x-3|=0$.

- $\left\{\dfrac32\right\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\{0\}$
- $\left\{-\dfrac32,\dfrac32\right\}$

## Explanation

When the absolute value is zero, its argument is also zero. The only equation is $2x-3=0$, which gives $x=\dfrac32$. In this case the two sign possibilities coincide because the opposite of $0$ is $0$. At $x=\dfrac32$ the argument is $2\cdot\dfrac32-3=0$. There is therefore only one distinct value.
