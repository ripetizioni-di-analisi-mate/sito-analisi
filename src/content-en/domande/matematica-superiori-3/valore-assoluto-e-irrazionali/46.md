A square on a floor has area $A$ and side $\sqrt A$. If the area rises from $A$ to $A+1$, which formula for the increase in side length shows that the increase becomes smaller as $A\geq0$ grows?

- $\sqrt{2A+1}$
- $\dfrac1{\sqrt{A+1}+\sqrt A}$
- $\dfrac1{\sqrt{A+1}-\sqrt A}$
- $\dfrac1{2A+1}$

## Explanation

The increase is $\sqrt{A+1}-\sqrt A$. Multiply the numerator and denominator by $\sqrt{A+1}+\sqrt A$ to obtain a numerator of $1$, since $(A+1)-A=1$. The positive denominator grows with $A$, so the quotient decreases.
