Solve $\sqrt{5-x}=0$.

- $\{-5\}$
- $\{5\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $(-\infty,5]$

## Explanation

The square root is zero only when its radicand is zero. This gives $5-x=0$, so $x=5$. The domain includes this value. For $x<5$ the radicand is positive and gives a root greater than zero. For $x>5$ it is negative and outside the domain. The only intersection with the horizontal axis is at the boundary $x=5$.
