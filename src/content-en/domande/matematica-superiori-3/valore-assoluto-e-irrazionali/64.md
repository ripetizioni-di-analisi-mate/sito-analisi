Solve $|x|+|x-2|=1$.

- $\varnothing$
- $\{1\}$
- $[0,2]$
- $\{0,2\}$

## Explanation

Between $0$ and $2$, the two distances add to $2$. Outside that segment the sum is greater than $2$. It never reaches the required value $1$. The three branches confirm this. For $x<0$ the sum is $2-2x>2$, and for $x>2$ it is $2x-2>2$. The minimum is therefore $2$ throughout the middle segment.
