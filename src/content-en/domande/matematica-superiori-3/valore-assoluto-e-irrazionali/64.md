Each measurement $x$ is replaced by $3x-7$ before distances are calculated. Two transformed measurements are $12$ apart. How far apart were the original measurements?

- $4$
- $36$
- $5$
- $19$

## Explanation

For two readings $u,v$ the new distance is $|(3u-7)-(3v-7)|=3|u-v|$. The shared translation cancels, and the factor $3$ multiplies the distance. To recover the initial distance, divide $12$ by $3$.
