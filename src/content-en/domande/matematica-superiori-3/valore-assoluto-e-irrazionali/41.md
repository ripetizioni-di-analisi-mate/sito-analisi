For which values of the parameter $k$ can $\dfrac1{\sqrt{(x-2)^2+k}}$ be evaluated for *every* real $x$?

- $k>0$
- $k<0$
- $k\ne0$
- $k\geq0$

## Explanation

The minimum of the radicand is attained at $x=2$ and equals $k$. Since the radical is in the denominator, even this minimum must be strictly positive. With $k>0$ the radicand stays positive for every $x$.
