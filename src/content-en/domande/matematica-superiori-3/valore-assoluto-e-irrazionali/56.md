A square has real area $A>0$. For which areas does the numerical value of the side $\sqrt A$ exceed the numerical value of the area, using the same reference unit of length?

- $0<A<1$
- $0<A\leq1$
- Every $A>0$.
- $A>1$

## Explanation

Since $A>0$, we can divide $\sqrt A>A$ by $\sqrt A$ to obtain $1>\sqrt A$. Squaring gives $A<1$. Together with the initial assumption, this yields the open interval $(0,1)$.
