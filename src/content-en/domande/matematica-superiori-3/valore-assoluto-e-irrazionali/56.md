Solve $\sqrt{4-x}<2$.

- $(0,4]$
- $[0,4]$
- $(0,4)$
- $[4,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The domain requires $x\leq4$. Squaring the strict inequality gives $4-x<4$, or $x>0$. At $4$ the square root is zero and the comparison holds, while at $0$ there is equality. Both sides are nonnegative, so squaring does not reverse the inequality. Intersecting $x>0$ with the domain gives $x\in(0,4]$.
