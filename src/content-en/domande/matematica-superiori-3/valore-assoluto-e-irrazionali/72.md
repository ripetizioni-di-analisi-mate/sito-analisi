On the real line consider the points $A$ with coordinate $0$ and $B$ with coordinate $5$. For which $x$ is the point with coordinate $x$ strictly closer to $A$ than to $B$?

- $\left(-\infty,\dfrac52\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac52,+\infty\right)$
- $(0,5)$
- $\left(-\infty,\dfrac52\right]$

## Explanation

The distances of the point with coordinate $x$ from $A$ and from $B$ are $|x|$ and $|x-5|$, so we solve the inequality $|x|<|x-5|$. Both sides are non-negative, so squaring them gives the equivalent inequality $x^2<x^2-10x+25$. Subtracting $x^2$ from both sides gives $10x<25$, that is $x<\dfrac52$. At the midpoint $x=\dfrac52$ the two distances are equal, so the endpoint is excluded and the required set is $\left(-\infty,\dfrac52\right)$.
