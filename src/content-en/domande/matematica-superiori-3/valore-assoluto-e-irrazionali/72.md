Two sensors on the same line are at $0$ and $5$. For which $x$ is the sensor at $0$ strictly closer?

- $\left(-\infty,\dfrac52\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac52,+\infty\right)$
- $(0,5)$
- $\left(-\infty,\dfrac52\right]$

## Explanation

Compare the distances using $|x|<|x-5|$. Squaring both nonnegative sides gives $x^2<x^2-10x+25$, so $x<\dfrac52$. At the midpoint the distances are equal, and the comparison is strict. Subtracting $x^2$ gives $10x<25$. The coefficient $10$ is positive, so the inequality keeps its direction. At $x=0$ it is immediately clear that the first sensor is closer.
