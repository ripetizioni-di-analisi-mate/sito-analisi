What is the vertex of the graph of $y=|x-2|+1$?

- $(-2,1)$
- $(2,0)$
- $(1,2)$
- $(2,1)$

## Explanation

The absolute value reaches its minimum when $x-2=0$. At $x=2$ it is zero, so the y-coordinate is $1$. The vertex is $(2,1)$. To the left and right of $2$ the absolute value is positive, so the ordinate is greater than $1$. The x-coordinates $1$ and $3$ both give height $2$. The vertex is the lowest point between the two branches.
