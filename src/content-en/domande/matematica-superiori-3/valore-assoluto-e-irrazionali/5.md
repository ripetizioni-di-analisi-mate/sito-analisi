Solve $\sqrt{2x-1}+1=4$.

- $\{5\}$
- $\{4\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac52\right\}$
- $\{9\}$

## Explanation

Subtracting $1$ gives $\sqrt{2x-1}=3$. Since $3>0$, squaring gives the equivalent equation $2x-1=9$. This yields $x=5$, where the radicand is $9$. Adding $1$ to the second equation gives $2x=10$. For $x=5$ the original expression is $\sqrt9+1=4$. Its nonnegative radicand confirms that the solution belongs to the domain.
