The distance between $a$ and $b$ is $|a-b|$. Which other expression represents it for all real $a,b$?

- $|a+b|$
- $b-a$
- $a+b$
- $|b-a|$

## Explanation

Swapping the points changes the displacement $a-b$ to its opposite $b-a$. The two displacements have the same absolute value, so the distance is the same.
