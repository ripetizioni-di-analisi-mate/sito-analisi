The distance between $a$ and $b$ is $|a-b|$. Which other expression represents it for all real $a,b$?

- $|a+b|$
- $b-a$
- $a+b$
- $|b-a|$

## Explanation

Swapping the points changes the displacement $a-b$ to its opposite $b-a$. The two displacements have the same absolute value, so the distance is the same. For example, when $a=2$ and $b=5$ the displacements are $-3$ and $3$, both with absolute value $3$. The expression $b-a$ alone would not always be a distance, because it can be negative.
