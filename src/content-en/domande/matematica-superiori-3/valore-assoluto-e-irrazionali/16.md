Which formula equals $|3-x|$ for $x>3$?

- $-x-3$
- $3-x$
- $x+3$
- $x-3$

## Explanation

If $x>3$, the argument $3-x$ is negative. Its absolute value is its opposite, $-(3-x)=x-3$. For example, at $x=5$ the argument is $-2$ and its absolute value is $2$, equal to $5-3$. The condition $x>3$ matters because to the left of $3$ the formula would be $3-x$.
