Which number satisfies $|3x+1|<5$?

- $-2$
- $3$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

From $-5<3x+1<5$ we obtain $-6<3x<4$, so $-2<x<\dfrac43$. Of the given options, only $1$ is inside. In particular, $-2$ gives equality and is excluded. Substituting the allowed value gives $|3\cdot1+1|=4<5$. With $x=2$ the argument is $7$, beyond the threshold.
