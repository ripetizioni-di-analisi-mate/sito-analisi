What is the minimum of $|x-6|$ for $x\in[-1,2]$?

- $7$
- $6$
- $0$
- $4$

## Explanation

Every point of the interval lies to the left of $6$. The distance is therefore $6-x$, which decreases as $x$ increases. The largest allowed value is $2$, so the minimum is $6-2=4$. At the other endpoint, $x=-1$, the distance is $7$. On this interval the distance decreases from $7$ to $4$ without reaching zero, because the reference point $6$ is outside the interval.
