What is the minimum of $|x-1|+2|x-3|$?

- $3$
- $4$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

Between $1$ and $3$, the expression is $5-x$ and decreases to $2$ at $3$. To the left, it is $7-3x$ and decreases to $4$ at $1$. To the right, it becomes $3x-7$ and increases from $2$. Thus the minimum is $2$.
