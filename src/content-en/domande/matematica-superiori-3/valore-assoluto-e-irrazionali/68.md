A courier must choose a base $x$ on a straight road. One trip goes to $1$ and two go to $3$, each one way from the base. Which location minimises the total $|x-1|+2|x-3|$?

- $x=\dfrac73$
- $x=2$
- $x=3$
- $x=1$

## Explanation

The sum $|x-1|+|x-3|$ is at least the distance $2$ between the destinations. There is one more term, $|x-3|$, which is nonnegative. At $x=3$ the first sum is $2$ and the extra term is zero, so the minimum is attained there.
