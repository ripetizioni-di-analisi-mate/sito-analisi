Solve $\sqrt{x+1}=x-1$.

- $\{0\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\{3\}$
- $\{0,3\}$

## Explanation

The right-hand side must be nonnegative, so $x\geq1$. Squaring gives $x+1=(x-1)^2$, or $x(x-3)=0$. Of $0$ and $3$, only $3$ satisfies the constraint. The other candidate $0$ gives $\sqrt1=1$ on the left and $-1$ on the right. At $x=3$, instead, $\sqrt4=2=3-1$. The check distinguishes equality of squares from equality of the original sides.
