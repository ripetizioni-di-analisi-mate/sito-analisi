When solving $|x-2|=x$, the branch $x\geq2$ leads to $x-2=x$. Which solution comes from the other branch?

- $x=-1$
- $x=0$
- $x=2$
- $x=1$

## Explanation

The equality $x-2=x$ would require $-2=0$, so the first branch is empty. For $x<2$ we have $|x-2|=2-x$. The equation $2-x=x$ gives $x=1$, which lies on this branch.
