Solve $|x-2|=x$.

- $\varnothing$
- $\{2\}$
- $\{0,1\}$
- $\{1\}$

## Explanation

Since absolute value is nonnegative, we need $x\geq0$. If $0\leq x<2$, we have $2-x=x$ and hence $x=1$. If $x\geq2$, we would have $x-2=x$, which is impossible. Only $1$ remains. As a check, at $x=1$ we have $|1-2|=1$. At $x=2$ the left-hand side is zero and the right-hand side is $2$, so the boundary between the branches adds no solution.
