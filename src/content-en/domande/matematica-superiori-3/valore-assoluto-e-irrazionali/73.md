A target is at distance $|s-12|$ from position $s$. Which positions give a distance of exactly $5$?

- $\{5,12\}$
- $\{7,17\}$
- $\{17\}$
- $[7,17]$

## Explanation

The target is at position $12$. The two allowed displacements are $-5$ and $5$, so the positions are $12-5=7$ and $12+5=17$. The first position is to the left of $12$ and the second is to the right. The checks are $|7-12|=|-5|=5$ and $|17-12|=|5|=5$. The equality excludes intermediate positions.
