A real number $x$ is at distance at most $2$ from $14$ and at most $2$ from $17$. Which set contains exactly the possible values of $x$?

- $[12,19]$
- $[15,16]$
- $[14,17]$
- $\{15\}$

## Explanation

Both conditions must hold together, so $x$ is a solution of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
|x-14|\leq2\\
|x-17|\leq2
\end{cases}
$$
The first inequality is equivalent to $12\leq x\leq16$. The second is equivalent to $15\leq x\leq19$. Therefore the solutions of the system form the intersection of the two intervals, that is $[15,16]$.
