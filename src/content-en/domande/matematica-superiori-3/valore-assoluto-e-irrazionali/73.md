A sensor can have an absolute error of at most $2$ units. Two such sensors give readings of $14$ and $17$ for the same quantity. What interval for the true value is consistent with both readings?

- $[12,19]$
- $[15,16]$
- $[14,17]$
- $\{15\}$

## Explanation

The reading $14$ allows true values from $12$ to $16$. The reading $17$ allows values from $15$ to $19$. Since both error bounds must hold, the remaining interval is their intersection, $[15,16]$.
