Two instruments display $p=\sqrt{a+b}$ and $q=\sqrt{a-b}$, with both radicands nonnegative. The readings are $p=5$ and $q=3$. What is $b$?

- $2$
- $16$
- $17$
- $8$

## Explanation

Squaring the readings gives $a+b=25$ and $a-b=9$. Subtracting the second relation from the first eliminates $a$ and leaves $2b=16$, so $b=8$. With $a=17$ the radicands are indeed $25$ and $9$.
