Solve $\sqrt{3x+4}=4$.

- $\{2\}$
- $\{0\}$
- $\{12\}$
- $\{4\}$

## Explanation

Since $4>0$, squaring gives $3x+4=16$. Thus $3x=12$ and $x=4$. The original square root equals $4$ at this value. Equality of the squares is reversible because both the root and the number $4$ are nonnegative. At $x=4$, $3\cdot4+4=16$, so the radicand is allowed.
