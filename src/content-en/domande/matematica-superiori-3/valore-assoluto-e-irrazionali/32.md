An alarm is triggered when $|T-12|\geq3$. To implement the same rule using direct tests on the temperature $T$, which two tests should be connected by “or”?

- $T\leq9$, $T\geq15$
- $T<9$, $T>15$
- $T\geq9$, $T\leq15$
- $T\leq-3$, $T\geq3$

## Explanation

The alarm is triggered at a distance of at least $3$ from $12$. On the lower side this means $T\leq12-3=9$, and on the upper side it means $T\geq12+3=15$. Equality triggers the alarm at both thresholds.
