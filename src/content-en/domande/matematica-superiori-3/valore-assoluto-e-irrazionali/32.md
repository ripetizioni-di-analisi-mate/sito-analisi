Solve $|x+3|\geq2$.

- $(-\infty,-5]\cup[-1,+\infty)$
- $[-5,-1]$
- $(-\infty,1]\cup[5,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-5)\cup(-1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

We need $x+3\leq-2$ or $x+3\geq2$. The two branches give $x\leq-5$ or $x\geq-1$, including both equality points. The value $-3$ is the center, with zero deviation, which is too small to be allowed. The two points exactly $2$ units from $-3$ separate the middle interval from the exterior rays.
