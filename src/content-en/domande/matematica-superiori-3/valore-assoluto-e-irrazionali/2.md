Solve $|2x-1|\leq5$.

- $(-\infty,-2]\cup[3,+\infty)$
- $[-2,3]$
- $(-2,3)$
- $[-3,2]$

## Explanation

The compound inequality is $-5\leq2x-1\leq5$. Adding $1$ gives $-4\leq2x\leq6$, so $-2\leq x\leq3$. Then divide by $2$, which is positive, so the directions stay the same. At $x=-2$ we have $|-5|=5$, and at $x=3$ we have $|5|=5$. Both endpoints are allowed.
