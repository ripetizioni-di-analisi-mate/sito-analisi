To solve $|2x-1|\leq5$, Elena imposes only $2x-1\leq5$ and obtains $x\leq3$. Which set remains after the missing condition is added?

- $(-\infty,3]$
- $[-2,3]$
- $[-2,+\infty)$
- $[-3,3]$

## Explanation

The absolute value also requires $2x-1\geq-5$. Adding $1$ and dividing by $2$ gives $x\geq-2$. Its intersection with $x\leq3$ includes both endpoints.
