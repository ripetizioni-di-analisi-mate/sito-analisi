All you know is that $|a-b|=4$. Which value can you assign with certainty to $(a-b)^2$?

- $4$
- $8$
- $-16$
- $16$

## Explanation

The difference can equal either $4$ or $-4$. Its square is the same in both cases and equals $4^2=16$.
