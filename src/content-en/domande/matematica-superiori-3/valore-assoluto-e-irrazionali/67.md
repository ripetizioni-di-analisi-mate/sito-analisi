What is $|x+1|-|x-2|$ for $x>2$?

- $1$
- $-3$
- $3$
- $2x-1$

## Explanation

For $x>2$, both arguments are positive. This gives $(x+1)-(x-2)=3$. To the right of $2$, both distances increase by the same amount when $x$ increases. Their difference therefore remains the distance between $-1$ and $2$, namely $3$.
