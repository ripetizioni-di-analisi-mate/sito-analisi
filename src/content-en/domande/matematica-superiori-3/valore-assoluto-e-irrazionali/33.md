Two posts on a straight road are $4$ m apart. Which pair of distances from a person to the posts, in metres, is impossible even if the person can stand off the road?

- $2$ and $7$
- $4$ and $4$
- $2$ and $2$
- $1$ and $5$

## Explanation

The distances from a single point can differ by at most the distance between the posts. For the pair $2,7$ the difference is $5>4$. Two distances of $2$ occur at the midpoint, distances of $1$ and $5$ on the road's extension, and two distances of $4$ at the third vertex of an equilateral triangle.
