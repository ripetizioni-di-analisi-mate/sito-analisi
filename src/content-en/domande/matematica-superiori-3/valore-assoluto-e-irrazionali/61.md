Solve $\sqrt{x-1}<\sqrt{5-x}$.

- $[1,3]$
- $(1,3)$
- $[1,3)$
- $(3,5]$

## Explanation

The common domain is $[1,5]$. Since both sides are nonnegative, compare the radicands. This gives $x-1<5-x$, so $x<3$. The domain excludes values below $1$. At $x=1$ the first root is zero and the second is $2$, so the lower boundary is included. At $x=3$ both equal $\sqrt2$, and the strict inequality excludes that point.
