On which interval do both curves $y=\sqrt{x-1}$ and $y=\sqrt{5-x}$ exist with the first strictly below the second?

- $(-\infty,3)$
- $(1,3]$
- $[1,3)$
- $(3,5]$

## Explanation

The common domain is $[1,5]$. There the radicals have the same order as their radicands, so we require $x-1<5-x$, or $x<3$. The coordinate $1$ is allowed, and $3$ is where the curves intersect.
