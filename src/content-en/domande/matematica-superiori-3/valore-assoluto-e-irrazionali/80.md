What is $\sqrt[3]{-27}$?

- No real number.
- $-3$
- $3$
- $-9$

## Explanation

A cube root preserves the sign. The cube of $-3$ equals $-27$. We seek a solution of $y^3=-27$. The cube of $3$ is positive, while $(-3)(-3)(-3)=-27$. Cubing is strictly increasing on the reals, so this solution is unique.
