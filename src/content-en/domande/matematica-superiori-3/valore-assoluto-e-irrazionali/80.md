Compare $p=\sqrt[3]{x^3}$ and $q=\sqrt{x^2}$ for the same real $x$. For which inputs are the two readings strictly different?

- $x>0$
- $x<0$
- Every $x\in\mathbb R$.
- $x\ne0$

## Explanation

Cubing and taking the cube root are inverse operations on the whole real line, so $p=x$. The principal square root instead gives $q=|x|$. These outputs differ when $x<0$ and agree at zero and for all positive inputs.
