What is the reference point for the distance $|x+4|$?

- $-2$
- $-4$
- $0$
- $4$

## Explanation

We have $|x+4|=|x-(-4)|$. This is the distance between $x$ and $-4$, where the expression is zero. Another way to find the center is to solve $x+4=0$. The symmetric points $-5$ and $-3$ give arguments $-1$ and $1$, respectively, so both have distance $1$ from that center.
