The graph shows $y=\sqrt{x}$ and $y=2$. For which $x$ is the curve on or above the line?

::grafico{f="sqrt(x);2" xmin=-1 xmax=9 ymin=-1 ymax=4 punti="0,0,pieno;4,2,pieno" titolo="Square root and horizontal line"}

- $(4,+\infty)$
- $[4,+\infty)$
- $[0,4]$
- $[2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The graphs meet when $\sqrt{x}=2$, or $x=4$. The square root is increasing for $x\geq0$, so the points above the line have x-coordinates greater than $4$. The threshold is included.
