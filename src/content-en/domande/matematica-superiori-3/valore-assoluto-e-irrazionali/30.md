If $a<0$, what is the solution set of $|x-1|<a$?

- $(-\infty,1)$
- $\varnothing$
- $\mathbb R$
- $\{1\}$

## Explanation

We have $|x-1|\geq0$ for every $x$. No nonnegative number is less than $a<0$. Even at the center $x=1$, where the absolute value is smallest, we have $0>a$. A negative threshold lies below every ordinate of the graph. The inequality is impossible and the solution set is empty.
