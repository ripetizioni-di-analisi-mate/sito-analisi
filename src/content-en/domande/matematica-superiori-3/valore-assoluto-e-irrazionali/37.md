How many integers solve $|x|<3$?

- $5$
- $4$
- $6$
- $7$

## Explanation

The integers strictly between $-3$ and $3$ are $-2$, $-1$, $0$, $1$ and $2$. Both endpoints are excluded. There are five integers. The admissible real set is $(-3,3)$. The check $|\pm3|=3$ shows why those two integers are not counted. The integers in between consist of two opposite pairs and zero.
