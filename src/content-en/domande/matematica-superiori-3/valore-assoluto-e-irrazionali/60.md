Solve $\sqrt{x+1}\leq x+1$.

- $\{-1\}\cup[0,+\infty)$
- $(-1,0]$
- $[0,+\infty)$
- $[-1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

On the domain $x\geq-1$, the right-hand side is nonnegative. Squaring gives $x+1\leq(x+1)^2$, or $x(x+1)\geq0$. Intersecting with the domain leaves the point $-1$ and the ray starting at $0$. Between $-1$ and $0$, the product $x(x+1)$ is negative. For example, at $x=-\dfrac12$ we compare $\sqrt{\dfrac12}$ and $\dfrac12$, with the first larger. At $-1$, both sides are zero.
