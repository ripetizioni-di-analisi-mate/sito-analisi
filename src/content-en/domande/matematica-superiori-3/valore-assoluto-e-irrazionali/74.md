Time $t$ follows the model $t=\sqrt{2s}$, where $s\geq0$ is the distance traveled. For which $s$ is $t\leq4$?

- $[0,8]$
- $(0,8)$
- $[8,+\infty)$
- $[0,2]$

## Explanation

Since both sides are nonnegative, $\sqrt{2s}\leq4$ is equivalent to $2s\leq16$. With $s\geq0$, this gives $[0,8]$, including the threshold where time is $4$.
