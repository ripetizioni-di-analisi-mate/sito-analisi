In a model the time is $t=\sqrt{2s}$ with $s\geq0$. A measurement gives $3\leq t<4$. What range of $s$ is consistent with the measurement?

- $\left[\dfrac92,8\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac92,8\right]$
- $[9,16)$
- $\left[\dfrac32,2\right)$

## Explanation

Both endpoints and the time are nonnegative. Squaring gives $9\leq2s<16$. Division by $2$ preserves the ordering and keeps only the left endpoint included.
