Solve $3\sqrt{x-2}=6$.

- $\{14\}$
- $\{8\}$
- $\{6\}$
- $\{4\}$

## Explanation

First divide by $3$ to get $\sqrt{x-2}=2$. Squaring gives $x-2=4$, so $x=6$. The domain requires $x\geq2$, and after division the threshold is $2>0$, so squaring is valid. As a check, at $x=6$ we have $3\sqrt{6-2}=3\cdot2=6$.
