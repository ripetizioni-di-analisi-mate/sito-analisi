A rectangle has positive side lengths $x$ and $x+1$ and diagonal $\sqrt{13}$. Which pair of side lengths fits these conditions?

- $3,\ 4$
- $\sqrt6,\ \sqrt7$
- $2,\ 3$
- $1,\ 4$

## Explanation

The Pythagorean theorem requires $x^2+(x+1)^2=13$, which reduces to $x^2+x-6=0$. Factoring gives $(x-2)(x+3)=0$, with $x=2$ the only positive length. The sides are therefore $2$ and $3$.
