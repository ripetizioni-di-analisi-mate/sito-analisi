To plot the solutions of $|x^2-4|<1$, compare the parabola $y=x^2$ with a horizontal strip. Which strip gives exactly the required horizontal coordinates?

- $3\leq y\leq5$
- $3<y<5$
- $y<3$ or $y>5$
- $-1<y<1$

## Explanation

The distance of $x^2$ from $4$ must be less than $1$, which means $3<x^2<5$. On the parabola the open strip selects the horizontal coordinates between $-\sqrt5$ and $-\sqrt3$ or between $\sqrt3$ and $\sqrt5$, excluding the endpoints.
