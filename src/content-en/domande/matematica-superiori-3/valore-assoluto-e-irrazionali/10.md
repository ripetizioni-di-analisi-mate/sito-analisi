Solve $|x^2-4|<1$.

- $(-\sqrt3,\sqrt3)$
- $(-\sqrt5,-\sqrt3)\cup(\sqrt3,\sqrt5)$
- $[-\sqrt5,-\sqrt3]\cup[\sqrt3,\sqrt5]$
- $(-\sqrt5,\sqrt5)$

## Explanation

The comparison is equivalent to $-1<x^2-4<1$, or $3<x^2<5$. The distance from zero lies between $\sqrt3$ and $\sqrt5$, with both thresholds excluded. This gives two symmetric intervals. With $x=\pm2$, the square is $4$ and the argument of the absolute value is zero. For $x=0$, instead, the absolute value is $4>1$. The lower bound $x^2>3$ excludes the middle portion.
