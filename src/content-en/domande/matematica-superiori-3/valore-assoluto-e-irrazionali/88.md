For $x<0$, which expression equals $\sqrt{x^2}+x$?

- $2x$
- $-2x$
- $0$
- $x$

## Explanation

The square root is $|x|=-x$ on the stated interval. Adding $x$ gives $-x+x=0$. The square root must be nonnegative, so for $x<0$ it gives the opposite of $x$. For example, at $x=-3$ we get $\sqrt9-3=3-3=0$. The same cancellation occurs throughout the interval.
