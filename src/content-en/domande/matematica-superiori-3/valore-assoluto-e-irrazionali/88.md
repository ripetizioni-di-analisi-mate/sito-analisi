The device $R(x)=\dfrac{x+|x|}{2}$ receives a real signal $x$. What happens if the processed signal is passed through the device a second time?

- $R(R(x))=2R(x)$ for every $x$.
- $R(R(x))=|x|$ for every $x$.
- $R(R(x))=R(x)$ for every $x$.
- $R(R(x))=x$ for every $x$.

## Explanation

For $x<0$ we have $x+|x|=0$. For $x\geq0$ we have $R(x)=x$. The first pass therefore returns a nonnegative number, which the second pass leaves unchanged.
