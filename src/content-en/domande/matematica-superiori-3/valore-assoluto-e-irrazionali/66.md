A program uses $2x-4$ to compute $|x-1|+|x-3|$. Starting at which horizontal coordinate is this formula always correct?

- $4$
- $1$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

For $x\geq3$ both arguments are nonnegative and their sum is $(x-1)+(x-3)=2x-4$. Immediately to the left of $3$, for $1<x<3$, the second argument is negative and the sum is constant at $2$. Thus the earliest possible starting point is $3$.
