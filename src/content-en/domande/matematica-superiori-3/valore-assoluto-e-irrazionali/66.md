Solve $|x-1|+|x-3|\leq4$.

- $[1,3]$
- $(0,4)$
- $[0,4]$
- $(-\infty,0]\cup[4,+\infty)$

## Explanation

In the middle branch $[1,3]$, the sum is $2$. For $x<1$ we have $4-2x\leq4$, so $x\geq0$. For $x>3$ we have $2x-4\leq4$, so $x\leq4$. Joining the branches gives $[0,4]$. At both boundaries the sum is $4$. An interior point such as $x=2$ gives $1+1=2$. The condition therefore gives a full interval including both boundaries.
