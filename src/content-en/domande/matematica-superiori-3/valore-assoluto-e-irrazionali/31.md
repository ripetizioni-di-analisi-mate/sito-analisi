In the graph, for which x-coordinates is $|x+1|<2$?

::grafico{f="abs(x+1);2" xmin=-5 xmax=3 ymin=-1 ymax=5 punti="-3,2,pieno;1,2,pieno" titolo="Absolute value and threshold"}

- $[-3,1]$
- $(-1,3)$
- $(-3,1)$
- $(-\infty,-3)\cup(1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The curve is below the line at height $2$ between the two intersections. Their x-coordinates are $-3$ and $1$, so take the open interval $(-3,1)$.
