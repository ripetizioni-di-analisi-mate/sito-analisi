A point $x$ must be at a distance from the origin that differs from $3$ by less than $1$. Which set satisfies $\bigl||x|-3\bigr|<1$?

- $[-4,-2]\cup[2,4]$
- $(2,4)$
- $(-4,4)$
- $(-4,-2)\cup(2,4)$

## Explanation

Writing $d=|x|$ turns the constraint into $2<d<4$. To the right of the origin this gives $2<x<4$, and to the left it gives $-4<x<-2$. All four boundary coordinates produce a difference of $1$ and are excluded.
