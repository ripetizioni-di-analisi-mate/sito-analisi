Solve $\sqrt{(x-3)^2}=2$.

- $[1,5]$
- $\{1\}$
- $\{5\}$
- $\{1,5\}$

## Explanation

The square root of the square is $|x-3|$. The equation asks for points at distance $2$ from $3$, namely $3-2=1$ and $3+2=5$. If $x=1$, the inner expression $x-3$ is $-2$, and the square root of its square is $2$. At $x=5$, the inner expression is $2$, giving the same root. Both signs are needed.
