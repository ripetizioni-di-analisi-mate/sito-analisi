Points on the number line satisfying $|x+2|=|x-4|$ are equally distant from two reference points. Where is the boundary between positions closer to the first and positions closer to the second?

- $x=1$
- $x=2$
- $x=3$
- $x=-1$

## Explanation

The reference points are $-2$ and $4$. On the line the only point equally distant from both is their midpoint, with coordinate $\dfrac{-2+4}{2}=1$. This boundary lies $3$ units from either reference point.
