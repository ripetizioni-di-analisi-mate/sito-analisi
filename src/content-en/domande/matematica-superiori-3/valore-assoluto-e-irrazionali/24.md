Solve $|x+2|=|x-4|$.

- $\{1\}$
- $\{-2,4\}$
- $[-2,4]$
- $\{-1\}$

## Explanation

The two quantities are the distances from $-2$ and $4$. The only equidistant point is their midpoint, $\dfrac{-2+4}{2}=1$. Checking at $x=1$ gives $|1+2|=3$ and $|1-4|=3$. Since both absolute values are nonnegative, squaring is valid and gives $(x+2)^2=(x-4)^2$, hence $12x=12$.
