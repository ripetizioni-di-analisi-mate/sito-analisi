What is the real domain of $\sqrt{\dfrac{x-2}{x+5}}$?

- $[2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-5)\cup[2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-5]\cup[2,+\infty)$
- $(-5,2]$

## Explanation

The fraction must be nonnegative. The linear factors have the same sign for $x<-5$ or $x>2$. At $2$ the radicand is zero and the square root exists. At $-5$ the fraction is undefined. On the middle interval $(-5,2)$ the numerator is negative and the denominator positive, so the quotient has no real square root. The two allowed portions form $(-\infty,-5)\cup[2,+\infty)$.
