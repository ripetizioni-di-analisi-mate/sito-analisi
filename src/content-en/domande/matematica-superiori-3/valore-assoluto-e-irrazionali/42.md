A graph of $\sqrt{\dfrac{x-2}{x+5}}$ has two branches. Which of the boundary coordinates $-5$ and $2$ is the horizontal coordinate of an actual point on the graph?

- Only $-5$.
- Only $2$.
- Neither.
- Both.

## Explanation

At $x=2$ the quotient is zero and the radical exists, so the graph includes $(2,0)$. At $x=-5$ the denominator is zero and the quotient is undefined. The sign of the quotient allows the intervals $x<-5$ and $x\geq2$.
