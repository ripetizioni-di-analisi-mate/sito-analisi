Two streetlights are $10\,\mathrm m$ apart on the same straight sidewalk. A point must be at most $6\,\mathrm m$ from each streetlight. How long is the allowed segment?

- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $4\,\mathrm m$
- $12\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Place the streetlights at $0$ and $10$. The two constraints give $[-6,6]$ and $[4,16]$. Their intersection is $[4,6]$, whose length is $6-4=2\,\mathrm m$.
