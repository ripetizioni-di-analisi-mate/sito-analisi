How many real solutions does $\sqrt{x^2+4}=-2$ have?

- One.
- Infinitely many.
- None.
- Two.

## Explanation

The radicand is at least $4$. Therefore the square root is at least $2$ and cannot equal $-2$. At the minimum, $x=0$, the square root is $2$. Squaring immediately would give $x=0$, but substituting it would require $2=-2$. The sign on the right rules out the candidate.
