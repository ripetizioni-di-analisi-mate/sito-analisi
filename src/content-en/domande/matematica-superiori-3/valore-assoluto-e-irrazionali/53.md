A nested radical occurs in $v=\sqrt{\sqrt{x}}$ for $x\geq0$. For what interval of $x$ does $1\leq v\leq2$ hold?

- $[1,16]$
- $[1,4]$
- $[0,16]$
- $[1,8]$

## Explanation

All terms are nonnegative, so both inequalities may be squared. This first gives $1\leq\sqrt{x}\leq4$, then $1\leq x\leq16$. Square roots and squares preserve order on this ray.
