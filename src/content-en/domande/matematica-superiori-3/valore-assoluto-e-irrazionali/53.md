Solve $\sqrt{x} + \sqrt{x}=8$.

- $\{16\}$
- $\{8\}$
- $\{4\}$
- $\{32\}$

## Explanation

The two terms are equal, so their sum is $2\sqrt{x}$. Dividing by $2$ gives $\sqrt{x}=4$, and squaring gives $x=16$. Adding two copies of the same root leaves its radicand unchanged. On the domain $x\geq0$, the new equality can be squared. The check gives $\sqrt{16}+\sqrt{16}=4+4=8$.
