What is the minimum of $D(x)=\max\{|x+2|,|x-6|\}$? The two quantities inside the maximum are the distances to $-2$ and $6$.

- $8$
- $4$
- $2$
- $6$

## Explanation

The two points are $8$ apart. If both distances from $x$ were less than $4$, their sum would be less than $8$, which is impossible. At the midpoint $x=2$ both distances equal $4$, so the lower bound is attained.
