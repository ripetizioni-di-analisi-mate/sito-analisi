On which interval is $|x-1|+|x-4|$ constant?

- $[4,+\infty)$
- $[1,4]$
- $(-\infty,1]$
- $[0,5]$

## Explanation

Between $1$ and $4$, the distances from $1$ and $4$ add to $4-1=3$. Outside the segment, moving changes both distances in the same direction, so the sum varies. On the middle interval, $(x-1)+(4-x)=3$. To the left the formula is $5-2x$, and to the right it is $2x-5$. Both exterior branches have nonzero slopes.
