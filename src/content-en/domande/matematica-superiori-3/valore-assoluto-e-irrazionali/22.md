How many real solutions does $|x^2-5|=4$ have?

- Four.
- Two.
- None.
- One.

## Explanation

The two cases are $x^2-5=4$ and $x^2-5=-4$, so $x^2=9$ or $x^2=1$. Each positive square gives two roots. The four values $-3$, $-1$, $1$ and $3$ are distinct. With $x=\pm3$ the argument is $4$. With $x=\pm1$ it is $-4$. Both pairs give absolute value $4$, and none of their values belongs to both cases.
