A horizontal line $y=h$ meets the curve $y=|x^2-1|$ at three distinct points. Which height $h$ is possible?

- $1$
- $2$
- $\dfrac12$
- $0$

## Explanation

At $h=1$ we obtain either $x^2=2$ or $x^2=0$, giving the three horizontal coordinates $-\sqrt2$, $0$ and $\sqrt2$. For $0<h<1$ both squares are positive, giving four points. For $h>1$ only the outer pair remains, while $h=0$ gives the two zeros of the curve.
