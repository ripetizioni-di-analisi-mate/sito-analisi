The input $x=1$ was excluded from $\sqrt[3]{x-4}$ because the radicand is negative. What value does the expression actually take there?

- No real value.
- $-\sqrt[3]{3}$
- $\sqrt[3]{3}$
- $-3$

## Explanation

At $x=1$ the radicand is $-3$. A radical with an odd index allows negative radicands. The number $-\sqrt[3]{3}$ is the answer because its cube equals $-3$.
