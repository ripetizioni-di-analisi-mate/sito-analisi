What is the real domain of $\sqrt[3]{x-4}$?

- $(-\infty,4]$
- $\mathbb R$
- $[4,+\infty)$
- $(4,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The index $3$ is odd. Every real number has a real cube root, including negative numbers and zero. The expression $x-4$ is defined for every $x$. For example, $x=3$ gives radicand $-1$, whose cube root is $-1$. The point $x=4$ gives zero. Both are allowed, so no sign restriction reduces the domain.
