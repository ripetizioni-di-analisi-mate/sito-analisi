Two real square roots, $\sqrt{x+1}$ and $\sqrt{7-x}$, are equal. What is their common value?

- $\sqrt3$
- $4$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

Equating the radicands gives $x+1=7-x$, so $x=3$. The common radicand is $4$, so the positive square root is $2$. The common domain is $[-1,7]$, so $3$ is allowed. Substituting gives $\sqrt{3+1}=\sqrt{7-3}=\sqrt4$. The question asks for the value of the roots, which is $2$, rather than the x-coordinate where they are equal.
