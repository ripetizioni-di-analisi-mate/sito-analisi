Two squares have nonnegative areas $x+1$ and $7-x$. They are to form a rectangle by sharing an entire side of the same length. What will the resulting rectangle's perimeter be?

- $6$
- $8$
- $12$
- $16$

## Explanation

Equal side lengths require equal areas. Solving $x+1=7-x$ gives $x=3$, so each square has area $4$ and side length $2$. The rectangle measures $4$ by $2$ and has perimeter $2(4+2)=12$.
