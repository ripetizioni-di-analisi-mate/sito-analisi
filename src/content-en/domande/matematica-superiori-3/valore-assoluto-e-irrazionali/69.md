Two instruments read $2$ and $8$. Choose a reference $x$ to minimise the larger of the errors $|x-2|$ and $|x-8|$. What is this minimum error?

- $3$
- $0$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

The readings are $6$ units apart, so an interval of radius less than $3$ cannot contain both. At their midpoint $x=5$, both errors equal $3$.
