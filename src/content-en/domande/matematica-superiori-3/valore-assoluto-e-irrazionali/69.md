A frame of length $x$ is accepted if it differs from $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ by at most $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. Which interval contains exactly the allowed lengths in millimeters?

- $[48,52]$
- $(48,52)$
- $(-\infty,48]\cup[52,+\infty)$
- $[50,52]$

## Explanation

The allowed difference in absolute value is $|x-50|\leq2$. The interval is centered at $50$ with radius $2$. The phrase “at most” includes the measurements $48$ and $52$.
