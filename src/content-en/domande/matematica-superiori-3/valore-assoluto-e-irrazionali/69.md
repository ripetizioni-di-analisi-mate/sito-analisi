A frame is accepted if its length $x$ differs from $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ by at most $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. Which interval contains exactly the accepted lengths, in millimetres?

- $[48,52]$
- $(48,52)$
- $(-\infty,48]\cup[52,+\infty)$
- $[50,52]$

## Explanation

The condition is written $|x-50|\leq2$, because $|x-50|$ is the distance of the length $x$ from the value $50$. This inequality is equivalent to $-2\leq x-50\leq2$. Adding $50$ to all three sides gives $48\leq x\leq52$.

The phrase "at most" also includes a deviation equal to $2$, therefore the endpoints $48$ and $52$ belong to the set and the interval is $[48,52]$. The set $(-\infty,48]\cup[52,+\infty)$ instead describes the lengths with a deviation of at least $2$.
