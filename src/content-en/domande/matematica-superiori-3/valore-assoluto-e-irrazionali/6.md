Squaring $\sqrt{x+6}=x$ gives two candidates, $-2$ and $3$. Which set survives a check in the original equation?

- $\varnothing$
- $\{-2\}$
- $\{-2,3\}$
- $\{3\}$

## Explanation

At $x=-2$ the radical equals $\sqrt4=2$, while the right-hand side equals $-2$. At $x=3$ both sides equal $3$. Squaring had erased the difference in sign for the first candidate.
