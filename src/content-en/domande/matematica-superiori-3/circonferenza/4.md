Write the equation of the circle with diameter endpoints $(-2,0)$ and $(4,8)$.

- $(x-3)^2+(y-4)^2=25$
- $(x-1)^2+(y-4)^2=100$
- $(x+1)^2+(y+4)^2=25$
- $(x-1)^2+(y-4)^2=25$

## Explanation

Averaging the coordinates gives $C(1,4)$. The diameter has length $\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=10$, so the radius is $5$. Hence the right-hand side of the equation is $25$.
