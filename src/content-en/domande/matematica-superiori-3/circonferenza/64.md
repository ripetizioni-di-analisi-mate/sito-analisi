A circle is tangent to both axes and its centre is in the first quadrant. What relation holds for its centre $(h,k)$?

- $h=2k>0$
- $h+k=0$
- $h=k>0$
- $h=-k$

## Explanation

The centre's distances to the $y$-axis and $x$-axis are $h$ and $k$, respectively, since both are positive. Both equal the radius by tangency, so $h=k>0$.
