What is the area of the smallest axis-aligned square containing $x^2+y^2=9$?

- $9$
- $36$
- $12$
- $18$

## Explanation

The $x$- and $y$-coordinates range from $-3$ to $3$. The smallest square therefore has sides on $x=\pm3$ and $y=\pm3$. Each side has length $6$, giving area $36$.

The circle reaches each side of the square at an endpoint of a horizontal or vertical diameter. A square of side less than $6$ could not contain both $(-3,0)$ and $(3,0)$, for example. This also proves that the square is minimal.
