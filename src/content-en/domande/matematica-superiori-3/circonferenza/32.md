How many points in the closed disk $x^2+y^2\leq25$ have $x$-coordinate $6$?

- Two.
- One.
- None.
- Infinitely many.

## Explanation

If $x=6$, the left-hand side is $36+y^2\geq36$. Since $36>25$, no $y$-coordinate satisfies the inequality.

The line $x=6$ is $6$ units from the centre. Its nearest point to the origin is $(6,0)$, and even that point lies more than the radius $5$ away. All other points on the line are farther still.
