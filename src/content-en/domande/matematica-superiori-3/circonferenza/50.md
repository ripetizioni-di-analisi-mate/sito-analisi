An idealised wheel is the disk $x^2+y^2\leq25$. A cut parallel to the $x$-axis at height $-4$ gives a straight cross-section. How long is it?

- $10$
- $3$
- $8$
- $6$

## Explanation

On the line $y=-4$, the inequality is $x^2+16\leq25$, so $-3\leq x\leq3$. The cross-section is the horizontal segment of length $3-(-3)=6$.
