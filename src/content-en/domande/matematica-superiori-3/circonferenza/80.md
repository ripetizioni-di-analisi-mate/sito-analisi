What is the intersection of the two closed disks centred at $(0,0)$ and $(6,0)$ with radius $3$?

- The entire line $x=3$.
- No point.
- The entire segment from $(0,0)$ to $(6,0)$.
- The single point $(3,0)$.

## Explanation

Membership of the first disk requires $x\leq3$, while the second requires $x\geq3$. Thus $x=3$. In the first inequality $9+y^2\leq9$ forces $y=0$.
