What is the maximum of $x+y$ on the circle $x^2+y^2=8$?

- $\sqrt8$
- $2$
- $8$
- $4$

## Explanation

Since $(x-y)^2\geq0$, we have $(x+y)^2=2(x^2+y^2)-(x-y)^2\leq16$. The value $4$ is reached at $(2,2)$, which lies on the curve.

Equality in the upper bound requires $x-y=0$. On that line the circle equation reduces to $2x^2=8$, giving $x=2$ or $x=-2$. The first gives sum $4$, while the second gives $-4$, the minimum.
