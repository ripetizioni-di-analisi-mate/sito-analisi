Two circles have the same centre and radii $7$ and $4$. A point is $5$ units from that centre. Where is it?

- Inside the larger circle and outside the smaller one.
- Inside both circles.
- On the smaller circle and inside the larger one.
- Outside both circles.

## Explanation

The distance $5$ is greater than $4$ and less than $7$. The point is between the circles, outside the smaller disk and inside the larger one.
