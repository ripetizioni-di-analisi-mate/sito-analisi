Which point on $x^2+y^2=25$ is closest to $P(0,8)$?

- $(0,0)$
- $(0,-5)$
- $(0,5)$
- $(5,0)$

## Explanation

For every point $Q$ on the circle, $PQ\geq PC-CQ=8-5=3$. The point $(0,5)$ lies on the circle between the centre and $P$, and reaches the distance $3$.

The ray from the origin through $P$ reaches the boundary before it reaches $P$, precisely at height $5$. The opposite point $(0,-5)$ is $13$ units from $P$. The two extreme positions are therefore distinguished by the direction of the radius.
