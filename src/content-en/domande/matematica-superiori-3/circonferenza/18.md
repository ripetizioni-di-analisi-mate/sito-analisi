In the family $x^2+y^2-2x+6y+t=0$, what changes as $t$ varies over values that give a circle?

- The centre changes and the radius stays fixed.
- The radius changes and the centre stays fixed.
- The centre and radius both stay fixed.
- The centre and radius both change.

## Explanation

Completing the squares gives $(x-1)^2+(y+3)^2=10-t$. The same two binomials always give the centre $(1,-3)$. The value $10-t$ varies and is the square of the radius.
