Two circles of radii $5$ and $2$ have centres that are $1$ unit apart. How are they positioned?

- The smaller circle lies strictly inside the larger one.
- They lie outside one another.
- They are internally tangent.
- They intersect in two points.

## Explanation

By the triangle inequality, a point on the smaller circle is at most $1+2=3$ units from the larger circle's centre. Since $3<5$, the whole smaller circle lies inside.
