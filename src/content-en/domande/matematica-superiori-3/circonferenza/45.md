Two tangents are drawn to a circle from an exterior point. How do the segments from the exterior point to the two points of contact compare?

- They cannot be compared without the radius.
- The segment closer to the centre is longer.
- They have equal length.
- The lengths are in the ratio $2\colon1$.

## Explanation

The radius is perpendicular to the tangent at each point of contact. The two right triangles share the hypotenuse joining the centre to the exterior point and have a leg equal to the radius. By the Pythagorean theorem, the other legs are equal.
