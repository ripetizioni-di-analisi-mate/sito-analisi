How many points of $x^2+y^2=25$ also lie on the positive $x$-axis?

- One.
- Two.
- Infinitely many.
- None.

## Explanation

The positive half-axis requires $y=0$ and $x>0$. The equation gives $x=\pm5$, but only $x=5$ is positive. The point is $(5,0)$.

The value $-5$ solves the system with the full $x$-axis, but lies on the opposite half-axis. Requiring positivity therefore selects just one of the two solutions that would result from fixing $y=0$ alone.
