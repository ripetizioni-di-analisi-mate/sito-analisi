For which values of $s$ does the line $y=s$ cut a chord of length $8$ on $(x-1)^2+(y-2)^2=25$?

- $s=-1$ or $s=5$
- $s=-2$ or $s=6$
- $s=-3$ or $s=3$
- $s=5$ only

## Explanation

The half-chord measures $4$, so its distance from the centre is $\sqrt{25-16}=3$. The distance to the horizontal line is $|s-2|$, hence $s=2\pm3$.
