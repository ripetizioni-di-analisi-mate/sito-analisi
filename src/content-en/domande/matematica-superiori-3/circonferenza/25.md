What equation results from reflecting the circle $(x-5)^2+(y-2)^2=9$ across the $y$-axis?

- $(x+5)^2+(y+2)^2=9$
- $(x-2)^2+(y-5)^2=9$
- $(x-5)^2+(y+2)^2=9$
- $(x+5)^2+(y-2)^2=9$

## Explanation

Reflection across the $y$-axis changes the sign of the centre's $x$-coordinate and preserves its $y$-coordinate and the radius. The new centre is $(-5,2)$.
