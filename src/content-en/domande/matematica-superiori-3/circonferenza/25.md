What equation results from reflecting the circle $(x-5)^2+(y-2)^2=9$ across the $y$-axis?

- $(x+5)^2+(y+2)^2=9$
- $(x-2)^2+(y-5)^2=9$
- $(x-5)^2+(y+2)^2=9$
- $(x+5)^2+(y-2)^2=9$

## Explanation

Reflection across the $y$-axis changes the sign of the centre's $x$-coordinate and preserves its $y$-coordinate and the radius. The new centre is $(-5,2)$.

To obtain the new equation, substitute $-x$ for $x$ in the original. The square $(-x-5)^2$ becomes $(x+5)^2$, while $(y-2)^2$ stays the same. The point $(8,2)$ reflects to $(-8,2)$, which satisfies the new equation.
