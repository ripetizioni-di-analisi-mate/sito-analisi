When $y=mx+q$ is substituted into the general form of a circle with unit squared coefficients, what is the coefficient of $x^2$?

- $1+m^2$
- $1+q^2$
- $m^2$
- $1+m$

## Explanation

The term $x^2$ already present is joined by the term $m^2x^2$ from expanding $(mx+q)^2$. The linear terms of the circle give at most linear terms in $x$.
