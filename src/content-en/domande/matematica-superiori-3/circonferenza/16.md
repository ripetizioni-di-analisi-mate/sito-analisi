Which equation represents a real circle with positive radius?

- $x^2+y^2-4x+5=0$
- $x^2+2y^2-8x+6=0$
- $x^2+y^2-4x+4=0$
- $2x^2+2y^2-8x+6=0$

## Explanation

Dividing $2x^2+2y^2-8x+6=0$ by $2$ and completing the square gives $(x-2)^2+y^2=1$. The expression with $x^2+2y^2$ has unequal squared coefficients, while $x^2+y^2-4x+4=0$ and $x^2+y^2-4x+5=0$ give right-hand sides $0$ and $-1$, respectively.
