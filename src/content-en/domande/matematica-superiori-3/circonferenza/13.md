How far apart are the two horizontal tangents to $(x+4)^2+(y-1)^2=36$?

- $d=6$
- $d=7$
- $d=12$
- $d=10$

## Explanation

The radius is $6$. The horizontal tangents have $y$-coordinates $1-6=-5$ and $1+6=7$, so their distance is $7-(-5)=12$.

The centred form contains the square $(y-1)^2$. Its largest possible value is $36$, attained when $x=-4$. Both extreme points are $6$ units from the centre, so the vertical separation of the tangent lines is the diameter.
