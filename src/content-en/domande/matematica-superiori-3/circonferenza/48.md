Two chords of the same circle have lengths $6$ and $8$. Which is closer to the centre?

- The answer depends on the radius.
- The one of length $6$.
- They are the same distance away.
- The one of length $8$.

## Explanation

For fixed radius, $d^2=r^2-\left(\dfrac L2\right)^2$. The longer chord has a larger squared half-length and therefore a smaller distance.
