For which $d>0$ do circles of radii $7$ and $2$ have exactly two common points when their centres are $d$ units apart?

- $5\leq d\leq9$
- $d>9$
- $5<d<9$
- $2<d<7$

## Explanation

For two intersections, the sides of lengths $d$, $7$ and $2$ must form a non-degenerate triangle. Thus $7-2<d<7+2$, or $5<d<9$. The endpoints give tangency.

When both inequalities are strict, the third vertex can be constructed on either side of the segment between the centres. The constructions are reflections of each other and give two distinct intersections. If the triangle flattens at $5$ or $9$, only one vertex remains.
