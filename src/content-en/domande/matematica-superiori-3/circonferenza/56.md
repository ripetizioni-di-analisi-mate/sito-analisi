For which $d>0$ do circles of radii $7$ and $2$ have exactly two common points when their centres are $d$ units apart?

- $5\leq d\leq9$
- $d>9$
- $5<d<9$
- $2<d<7$

## Explanation

For two intersections, the sides of lengths $d$, $7$ and $2$ must form a non-degenerate triangle. Thus $7-2<d<7+2$, or $5<d<9$. The endpoints give tangency.
