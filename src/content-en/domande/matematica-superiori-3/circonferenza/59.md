The circle $x^2+y^2=16$ is externally tangent to $(x-6)^2+y^2=r^2$. What is $r>0$?

- $10$
- $2$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The centres are $6$ units apart and the first radius is $4$. For external tangency the radii sum to $6$, so $r=6-4=2$.

The contact point lies between the two centres. On the segment from $(0,0)$ to $(6,0)$ it is therefore at $(4,0)$. Its distances to the endpoints are $4$ and $2$, as the radii require.
