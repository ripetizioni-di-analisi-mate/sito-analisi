A circle centred at the origin is tangent to $x=5$. What is the area of its disk?

- $10\pi$
- $100\pi$
- $5\pi$
- $25\pi$

## Explanation

The distance from the origin to the line is $5$. By tangency this equals the radius, so the area is $\pi r^2=\pi\cdot25$.

The contact point is $(5,0)$ because it is the projection of the origin onto the vertical line. This gives radius $5$. Area scales with the square of the radius, leading to $25\pi$, which measures the disk.
