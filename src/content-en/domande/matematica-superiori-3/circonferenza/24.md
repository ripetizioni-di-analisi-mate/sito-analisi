What is the intersection of the closed disk $(x+2)^2+(y-1)^2\leq 4$ with the $y$-axis?

- Only the points $(0,-1)$ and $(0,3)$.
- No point.
- The whole segment from $(0,-1)$ to $(0,3)$.
- The single point $(0,1)$.

## Explanation

On the $y$-axis we have $x=0$. The inequality becomes $4+(y-1)^2\leq4$, so the square must vanish. Thus $y=1$.
