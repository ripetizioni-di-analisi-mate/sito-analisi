How many circles of radius $3$ pass through $(-2,0)$ and $(2,0)$?

- Two.
- Infinitely many.
- One.
- None.

## Explanation

The centre lies on the perpendicular bisector $x=0$. Writing it as $(0,k)$, its squared distance from $(2,0)$ is $4+k^2$. This must equal $9$, so $k=\pm\sqrt5$ and there are two centres.

The two values of $k$ have opposite signs and give distinct centres. The given points are $4$ units apart, less than the diameter $6$. Their chord therefore misses the centre, and the two possible centres lie on opposite sides of the chord.
