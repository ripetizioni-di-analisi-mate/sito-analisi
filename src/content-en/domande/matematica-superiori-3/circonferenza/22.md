Which locus consists of points inside the disk at distance $2$ from the circle $x^2+y^2=9$?

- $x^2+y^2=4$
- $x^2+y^2=1$
- $x^2+y^2=25$
- $x^2+y^2=9$

## Explanation

For an interior point the minimum distance to the boundary is $3-CP$, reached along the radius through $P$ if $P\neq C$. The same formula holds at the centre. Requiring $3-CP=2$ gives $CP=1$.
