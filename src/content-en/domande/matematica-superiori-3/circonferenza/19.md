What is the radius after moving the circle $(x+1)^2+(y-3)^2=7$ by $4$ units right and $2$ units down?

- $\sqrt3$
- $\sqrt{13}$
- $7$
- $\sqrt7$

## Explanation

The same translation applies to the centre and all points, so their distances are unchanged. The square of the radius is still $7$, while the centre becomes $(3,1)$.
