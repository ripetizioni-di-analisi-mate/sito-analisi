Which statement holds for the radical axis of two disjoint circles with distinct centres?

- It is always empty.
- It is a line that meets neither circle.
- It is a circle of radius zero.
- It meets each circle at two points.

## Explanation

Distinct centres guarantee a line after subtraction. If this line met one circle, the two polynomials would have the same value at the intersection and the point would lie on the other circle as well.
