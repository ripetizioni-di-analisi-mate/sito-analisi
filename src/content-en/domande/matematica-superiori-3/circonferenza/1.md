What is the centre of $x^2+y^2-8x+6y-11=0$?

- $(-4,3)$
- $(4,-3)$
- $(8,-6)$
- $(-3,4)$

## Explanation

In $(x-4)^2$ the linear term is $-8x$. In $(y+3)^2$ it is $6y$. The binomials vanish at $x=4$ and $y=-3$, so the centre is $(4,-3)$.

The constants to add are $16$ and $9$. The centred form is $(x-4)^2+(y+3)^2=11+16+9=36$. Find the coordinates by setting the differences to zero. For the vertical coordinate, that difference is $y+3$.
