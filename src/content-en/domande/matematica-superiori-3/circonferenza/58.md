Two circles of radius $5$ have centres $(0,0)$ and $(8,0)$. What is the length of the segment between their common points?

- $6$
- $8$
- $10$
- $3$

## Explanation

With equal radii the common points have $x$-coordinate $4$, halfway between the centres. In the first circle $y^2=25-16=9$, so the vertical distance between $(4,3)$ and $(4,-3)$ is $6$.
