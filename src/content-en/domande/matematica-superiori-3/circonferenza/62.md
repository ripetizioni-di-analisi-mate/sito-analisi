What value of $s$ makes $(x-s)^2+y^2=16$ coincide with $x^2+y^2=16$?

- $8$
- $16$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

The circles already have equal radius $4$. To coincide they must have the same centre. Thus $(s,0)=(0,0)$ and $s=0$.

The first centre is found by setting $x-s$ and $y$ to zero. If $s$ differed from zero, the smallest and largest horizontal coordinates, $s-4$ and $s+4$, would also shift. Only for $s=0$ do they agree with $-4$ and $4$.
