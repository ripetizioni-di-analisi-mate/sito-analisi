The points $A(1,2)$ and $B(1,8)$ lie on a circle whose centre has $x$-coordinate $5$. What is the radius?

- $4$
- $7$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

The chord $AB$ is vertical and its perpendicular bisector is $y=\dfrac{2+8}{2}=5$. The centre is therefore $(5,5)$, and its distance from $A$ is $\sqrt{(5-1)^2+(5-2)^2}=5$.
