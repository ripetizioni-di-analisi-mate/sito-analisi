Which pair of points is common to $x^2+y^2=25$ and $x^2+(y-6)^2=25$?

- $(3,-4)$ and $(3,4)$
- $(-4,3)$ and $(4,3)$
- $(-5,0)$ and $(5,0)$
- $(-3,4)$ and $(3,4)$

## Explanation

Subtracting gives $-12y+36=0$, so $y=3$. From $x^2=25-9=16$ we get the $x$-coordinates $-4$ and $4$.
