The centre of a circle lies on the $x$-axis, and the circle is tangent to $x=2$ and $x=10$. What is its equation?

- $(x-6)^2+y^2=16$
- $x^2+(y-6)^2=16$
- $(x-4)^2+y^2=36$
- $(x-6)^2+y^2=64$

## Explanation

The centre is equidistant from the two vertical lines, so its $x$-coordinate is $\dfrac{2+10}{2}=6$. Its distance to either line is $4$, the radius. The centre has $y$-coordinate zero.
