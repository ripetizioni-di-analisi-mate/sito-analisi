Find the radical axis of $x^2+y^2=16$ and $(x-5)^2+y^2=1$.

- $y=4$
- $x=4$
- $x=\dfrac52$
- $x=1$

## Explanation

Subtracting the first equation from the second gives $(x-5)^2-x^2=1-16$. Expanding yields $-10x+25=-15$, so $x=4$. The point $(4,0)$ is also the point of external tangency.
