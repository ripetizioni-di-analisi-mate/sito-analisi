Consider the circles that are tangent to the $x$-axis and pass through $P(0,4)$. Which equation describes the locus of their centres?

- $y=2$
- $y=\dfrac{x^2}{8}-2$
- $y=\dfrac{x^2}{8}+2$
- $y=\dfrac{x^2}{4}+4$

## Explanation

Let $C(x,y)$ be the centre of one of these circles. Since the circle is tangent to the $x$-axis, its radius equals the distance from $C$ to the axis, that is, $|y|$. Since the circle passes through $P$, the radius also equals $CP=\sqrt{x^2+(y-4)^2}$.

Equating the squares of the two distances gives $x^2+(y-4)^2=y^2$, hence $x^2-8y+16=0$. Therefore the locus of the centres is the parabola $y=\dfrac{x^2}{8}+2$. Among these centres, only the centre $(0,2)$ of the circle with diameter $OP$ lies on the line $y=2$.
