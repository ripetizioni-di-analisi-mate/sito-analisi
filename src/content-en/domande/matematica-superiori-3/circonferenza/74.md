The centres of circles tangent to the $x$-axis and passing through $P=(0,4)$ form a locus. What is its equation?

- $y=2$
- $x^2+y^2=4$
- $y=\dfrac{x^2}{8}+2$
- $y=\dfrac{x^2}{4}+4$

## Explanation

The distances from the centre $(x,y)$ to the axis and to $P$ are $|y|$ and $\sqrt{x^2+(y-4)^2}$. Both equal the radius. Equating their squares and simplifying gives $8y=x^2+16$.
