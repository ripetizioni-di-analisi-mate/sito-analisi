A circle passes through $(0,0)$, $(4,0)$ and $(2,4)$. Why is the segment from $(0,0)$ to $(4,0)$ not a diameter?

- Its midpoint is equidistant from the first two points.
- The circle cannot have horizontal diameters.
- Its midpoint is $4$ units from the third point and $2$ from the first two.
- The first two points lie on a horizontal line.

## Explanation

If the segment were a diameter, its midpoint $(2,0)$ would be the centre. Its distance to $(2,4)$ is $4$, while its distance to $(0,0)$ is $2$. A centre must be the same distance from all points of the circle.
