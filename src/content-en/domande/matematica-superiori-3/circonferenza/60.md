What is the point of internal tangency of $x^2+y^2=25$ and $(x-2)^2+y^2=9$?

- $(3,0)$
- $(2,3)$
- $(5,0)$
- $(-5,0)$

## Explanation

The second centre is $2$ units to the right of the first, and its radius is $3$. Its rightmost point is therefore $(2+3,0)=(5,0)$, which is $5$ units from the first centre.
