Rewriting $x^2+y^2-6x+2y+22=0$ gives $(x-3)^2+(y+1)^2=-12$. What does this mean?

- The locus contains only the centre.
- The real locus is empty.
- The locus is a circle of radius $\sqrt{12}$.
- There are two diametrically opposite points.

## Explanation

The left-hand side is a sum of real squares and is always greater than or equal to zero. No real pair can make it equal to $-12$.
