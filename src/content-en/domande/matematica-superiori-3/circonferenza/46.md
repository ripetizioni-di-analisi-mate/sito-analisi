Let $P(13,0)$ be outside the circle $x^2+y^2=25$. How long is the segment from $P$ to a point of tangency?

- $13$
- $18$
- $8$
- $12$

## Explanation

In the right triangle formed by the centre, point of contact and $P$, the hypotenuse measures $13$ and the radius measures $5$. The remaining leg is $\sqrt{13^2-5^2}=\sqrt{144}=12$.
