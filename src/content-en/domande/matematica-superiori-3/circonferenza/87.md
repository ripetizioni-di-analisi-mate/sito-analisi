If the distinct centres of two circles lie on $y=x$, what is the slope of their radical axis?

- It is undefined.
- $-1$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

Subtracting the general forms gives a line whose normal vector is parallel to the vector joining the centres. That vector is parallel to $(1,1)$, so the radical axis has slope $-1$.
