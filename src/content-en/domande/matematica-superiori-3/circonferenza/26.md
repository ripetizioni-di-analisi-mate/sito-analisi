A diameter has endpoints $A(-2,1)$ and $B(4,5)$. Which other point on the circle has $x$-coordinate $-2$?

- $(-2,-1)$
- $(-2,3)$
- $(-2,5)$
- $(-2,7)$

## Explanation

The centre has $y$-coordinate $\dfrac{1+5}{2}=3$. Reflection across $y=3$ preserves the $x$-coordinate of $A$ and changes its $y$-coordinate from $1$ to $2\cdot3-1=5$.
