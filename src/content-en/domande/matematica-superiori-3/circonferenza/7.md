A chord of a circle of radius $13$ is $5$ units from the centre. What is its length?

- $L=12$
- $L=26$
- $L=16$
- $L=24$

## Explanation

The perpendicular from the centre meets the chord at its midpoint. So the half-chord is $\sqrt{13^2-5^2}=\sqrt{144}=12$. The full chord measures $24$.
