Find the tangent to $(x-1)^2+(y+2)^2=25$ at $P(4,2)$.

- $4x-3y=10$
- $3x+4y=25$
- $3x+4y=20$
- $3x-4y=4$

## Explanation

The vector from the centre $(1,-2)$ to $P$ is $(3,4)$ and has length $5$. The tangent is perpendicular to that vector and passes through $P$, so $3(x-4)+4(y-2)=0$, or $3x+4y=20$.
