The boundary of a circular track is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from its centre. Does a straight path $8\,\mathrm{m}$ from the centre meet the boundary?

- No, the line misses the circle.
- Yes, at one point.
- Yes, at two points.
- Yes, at every point of the line.

## Explanation

The shortest distance from the centre to the path is $8\,\mathrm{m}$. All its points are therefore farther than $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from the centre. None lies on the boundary.
