Find the common points of $(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2=13$ and the vertical line $x=4$.

- $(4,-3)$ and $(4,3)$
- $(4,-2)$ and $(4,4)$
- $(4,-4)$ and $(4,2)$
- $(4,-4)$ only

## Explanation

Substituting $x=4$ gives $4+(y+1)^2=13$. Thus $y+1=\pm3$ and the two $y$-coordinates are $-4$ and $2$. Both $x$-coordinates are $4$.
