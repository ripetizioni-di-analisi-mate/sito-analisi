What is the coefficient of the linear term in $x$ in the general form of the circle with centre $(-3,2)$ and radius $5$?

- $-6$
- $2$
- $6$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The centre gives the square $(x+3)^2$, which expands to $x^2+6x+9$. The radius term is constant and does not change the coefficient of $x$.
