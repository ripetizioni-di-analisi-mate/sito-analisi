At which point does the tangent $y=7$ touch $(x-4)^2+(y-2)^2=25$?

- $(4,2)$
- $(4,7)$
- $(9,7)$
- $(7,4)$

## Explanation

The perpendicular projection of the centre $(4,2)$ onto the horizontal line preserves the $x$-coordinate $4$ and changes the $y$-coordinate to $7$. It is $5$ units from the centre and is therefore on the circle.
