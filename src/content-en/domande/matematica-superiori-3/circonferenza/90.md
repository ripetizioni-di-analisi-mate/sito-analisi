Besides $P(3,4)$, which point lies on both circles in the figure?

::grafico{curve="5*cos(t),5*sin(t),0,6.283185307;6+5*cos(t),5*sin(t),0,6.283185307" xmin=-6 xmax=12 ymin=-6 ymax=6 uguale=1 punti="0,0,pieno,C_1;6,0,pieno,C_2;3,4,pieno,P" legenda="x^2+y^2=25;(x-6)^2+y^2=25" titolo="The circles have centres $C_1$ and $C_2$."}

- $(3,0)$
- $(-3,-4)$
- $(4,3)$
- $(3,-4)$

## Explanation

Both circles are symmetric about the $x$-axis because their centres lie on it. The reflection of $P(3,4)$ is $(3,-4)$, so that point lies on both circles.
