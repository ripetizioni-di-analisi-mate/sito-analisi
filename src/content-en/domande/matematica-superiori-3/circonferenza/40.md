Which line is a secant to $x^2+y^2=4$?

- $3x+4y=15$
- $3x+4y=10$
- $3x+4y=5$
- $x=-2$

## Explanation

The radius is $2$. The lines $3x+4y=q$ are $\dfrac{|q|}{5}$ units from the origin, so for $q=5$ the distance is $1<2$. The cases $q=10$ and $x=-2$ are tangent. For $q=15$ the line misses the circle.
