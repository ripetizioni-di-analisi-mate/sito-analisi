What value must $r>0$ have for $5x+12y=65$ to be tangent to $x^2+y^2=r^2$?

- $5$
- $65$
- $13$
- $25$

## Explanation

The radius must equal the distance from the origin. This distance is $\dfrac{65}{\sqrt{5^2+12^2}}=\dfrac{65}{13}=5$.

In the distance formula, $65$ is the absolute value of the constant after substituting the centre. The denominator is the length of $(5,12)$, namely $13$. The point of contact is the foot of the perpendicular from the origin.
