Reflect the circle with centre $(2,-1)$ and radius $3$ in the line $x=5$. What is the equation of its image?

- $(x-8)^2+(y+1)^2=9$
- $(x+2)^2+(y+1)^2=9$
- $(x-3)^2+(y+1)^2=9$
- $(x-8)^2+(y-1)^2=3$

## Explanation

The reflected centre has horizontal coordinate $2\cdot5-2=8$. Its vertical coordinate and radius are unchanged, and the right-hand side is the radius squared.
