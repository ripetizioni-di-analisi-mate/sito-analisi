What is the smallest possible radius of a circle passing through $A(1,2)$ and $B(7,10)$?

- $5$
- $10$
- $5\sqrt2$
- $7$

## Explanation

The distance between the two points is $AB=\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=10$. The segment $AB$ is a chord of the circle and every chord is at most as long as the diameter, so $2r\geq10$, that is, $r\geq5$.

The value $r=5$ is obtained with the centre at the midpoint $M(4,6)$ of $AB$, because then $AB$ is a diameter. Therefore the smallest radius is $5$. The value $10$ comes from using the distance $AB$ as the radius instead of the diameter.
