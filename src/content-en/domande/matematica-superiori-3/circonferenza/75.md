How many circles of radius $2$ pass through $(0,0)$ and $(6,0)$?

- None.
- Infinitely many.
- Two.
- One.

## Explanation

The points are $6$ units apart. On a circle of radius $2$, the greatest distance between two points is the diameter $4$. Since $6>4$, no circle meets the requirement.
