For what value of $m$ does the locus $x^2+y^2-4x+6y+m=0$ consist of a single point?

- $m=1$
- $m=-13$
- $m=5$
- $m=13$

## Explanation

Completing the squares gives $(x-2)^2+(y+3)^2=4+9-m$. The sum of squares must be zero for there to be a single point. Thus $m=13$ and $(2,-3)$ is the only point.
