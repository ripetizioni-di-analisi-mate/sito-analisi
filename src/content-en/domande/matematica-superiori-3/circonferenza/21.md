A circle tangent to the $x$-axis has centre $(-2,3)$. What is its equation?

- $(x+2)^2+(y-3)^2=9$
- $(x-2)^2+(y+3)^2=9$
- $(x+2)^2+(y-3)^2=3$
- $(x+2)^2+(y-3)^2=4$

## Explanation

The $x$-axis has equation $y=0$. The centre is $|3-0|=3$ units from it, which equals the radius because of tangency. Thus the right-hand side is $9$.

The foot of the perpendicular from $(-2,3)$ to the $x$-axis is $(-2,0)$. It is the point of tangency, and substituting it into $(x+2)^2+(y-3)^2=9$ indeed gives $0+9=9$.
