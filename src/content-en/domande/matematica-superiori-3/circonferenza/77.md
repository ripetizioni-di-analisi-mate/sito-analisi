Which inequality describes the open disk with centre $(3,-2)$ and radius $4$?

- $(x+3)^2+(y-2)^2<16$
- $(x-3)^2+(y+2)^2\leq16$
- $(x-3)^2+(y+2)^2<16$
- $(x-3)^2+(y+2)^2<4$

## Explanation

The open disk contains only points strictly less than the radius from the centre. The squared distance is $(x-3)^2+(y+2)^2$, so it is compared with $4^2=16$ using a strict inequality.
