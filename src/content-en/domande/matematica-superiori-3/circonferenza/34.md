A point $P$ lies on the circle with centre $(2,3)$ and radius $5$. Which point of the circle is farthest from $P$ if $P=(6,6)$?

- $(6,0)$
- $(-2,6)$
- $(-2,0)$
- $(2,3)$

## Explanation

The maximum distance occurs along the diameter through $P$. For every $Q$ on the curve, $PQ\leq PC+CQ=10$, with equality when $C$ lies between the two points. The vector opposite to $(4,3)$ leads from $C$ to $(-2,0)$.
