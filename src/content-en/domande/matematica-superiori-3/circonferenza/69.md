A line cuts a circle at $A(-1,2)$ and $B(5,2)$. Which point is the projection of the centre onto the line?

- $(2,2)$
- $(-1,2)$
- $(3,2)$
- $(2,0)$

## Explanation

The perpendicular from the centre bisects the chord. Hence the projection is the midpoint $M\left(\dfrac{-1+5}{2},2\right)=(2,2)$.
