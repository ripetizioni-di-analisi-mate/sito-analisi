Two concentric circles of radii $2$ and $6$ bound a ring. A third concentric circle divides the ring into two parts of equal area. What radius must it have?

- $2\sqrt5$
- $4$
- $\sqrt{10}$
- $4\sqrt2$

## Explanation

The two areas are $\pi(r^2-4)$ and $\pi(36-r^2)$. Equating them gives $2r^2=40$. The positive radius is $2\sqrt5$.
