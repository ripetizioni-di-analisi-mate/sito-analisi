For which $t$ is the point $(t,2)$ strictly inside the disk bounded by $(x-1)^2+(y+2)^2=25$?

- $t<-2$ or $t>4$
- $-2<t<4$
- $-2\leq t\leq4$
- $-3<t<3$

## Explanation

Inside, the squared distance is less than $25$. Substituting $y=2$ gives $(t-1)^2+16<25$, so $|t-1|<3$. The boundary values $-2$ and $4$ are excluded because they give points on the circle.
