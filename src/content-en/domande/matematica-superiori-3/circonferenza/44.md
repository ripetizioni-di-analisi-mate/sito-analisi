The point $(1,2)$ is proposed as a point of tangency to $x^2+y^2=9$. Which check immediately decides whether this is possible?

- Compute $1^2+2^2$ and compare it with $9$.
- Compute $1+2$ and compare it with $\sqrt9$.
- Compute $2-1$ and compare it with $0$.
- Compute the product $1\cdot2$ and compare it with $9$.

## Explanation

A point of tangency must lie on the curve. Here the squared distance from the centre is $1^2+2^2=5$, which differs from $9$, so the point is inside and cannot be the point of contact.
