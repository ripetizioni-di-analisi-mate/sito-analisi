On a map, a fountain is at $(-1,2)$ and a circular fence centred on the fountain has radius $4$. A sign is at $(2,5)$. Where is it?

- Outside the fence.
- On the fence.
- Inside, on a vertical diameter.
- Inside, on the horizontal diameter.

## Explanation

The two differences in coordinates are $3$ and $3$. The squared distance is $3^2+3^2=18$, greater than the squared radius $16$. The sign is therefore outside.
