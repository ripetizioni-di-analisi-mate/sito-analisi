At which points on $x^2+y^2=25$ is $y=x$?

- $\left(\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2},\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2}\right)$ and $\left(-\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2},-\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2}\right)$
- $(0,0)$ only
- $\left(\dfrac52,\dfrac52\right)$ and $\left(-\dfrac52,-\dfrac52\right)$
- $(5,5)$ and $(-5,-5)$

## Explanation

Substitution gives $2x^2=25$. Thus $x=\pm\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2}$, and at each point $y$ has the same value as $x$.
