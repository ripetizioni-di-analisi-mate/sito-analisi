Which condition is impossible for a circle of positive radius?

- Being tangent to two distinct parallel lines.
- Passing through three distinct collinear points.
- Having its centre at the origin.
- Passing through two distinct points.

## Explanation

The intersection of a circle and a line gives a quadratic equation with at most two distinct real solutions. Three distinct points on the same line would require three solutions.
