The line $y=x$ is a secant to $x^2+y^2=18$. What is the distance between its two intersections?

- $6$
- $18$
- $6\sqrt2$
- $3\sqrt2$

## Explanation

The line passes through the centre $(0,0)$, so the chord is a diameter. The radius is $\sqrt{18}=3\sqrt2$, hence the distance is $6\sqrt2$.
