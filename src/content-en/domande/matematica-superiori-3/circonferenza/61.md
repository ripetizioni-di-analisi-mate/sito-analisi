Given the first circle $x^2+y^2=9$, for what value of $q$ is the radical axis with $(x-4)^2+y^2=q$ the line $x=3$?

- $1$
- $9$
- $25$
- $16$

## Explanation

Subtraction gives $-8x+16=q-9$. At $x=3$ the left-hand side is $-8$, hence $q-9=-8$ and $q=1$.

The line is vertical because the terms in $y$ cancel. The value $q=1$ is positive and gives a circle of radius $1$. On $x=3$ the point $(3,0)$ satisfies both equations since $(3-4)^2=1$.
