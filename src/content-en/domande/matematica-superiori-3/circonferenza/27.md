The centre of a circle is $(3,-1)$ and one diameter endpoint is $(7,2)$. What is the other endpoint?

- $(-1,-4)$
- $(-1,4)$
- $(11,5)$
- $(1,-4)$

## Explanation

To find the opposite endpoint, subtract from the centre the displacement from the centre to the first endpoint, which is $(4,3)$. This gives $(3-4,-1-3)=(-1,-4)$.
