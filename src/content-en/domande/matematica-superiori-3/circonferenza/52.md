A secant line rotates about the centre of the circle while keeping a fixed distance from the centre. What is preserved?

- The slope of the line.
- The $x$-coordinates of both endpoints in every case.
- The length of the chord.
- The chord midpoint coordinates in every case.

## Explanation

The length is $2\sqrt{r^2-d^2}$ and depends only on the radius $r$ and distance $d$. Both quantities stay fixed during the rotation.
