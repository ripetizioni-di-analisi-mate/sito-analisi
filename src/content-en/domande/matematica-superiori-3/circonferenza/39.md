After substituting a line into a circle, the result is $(x-2)^2=0$. How many distinct intersection points are there?

- Two.
- It depends on the radius.
- Zero.
- One.

## Explanation

The square is zero only for $x=2$. For a line of the form $y=mx+q$, this $x$-coordinate gives a single $y$-coordinate. The root is repeated, but there is one point.
