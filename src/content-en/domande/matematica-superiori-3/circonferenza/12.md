Two circles have radii $8$ and $3$, and their centres are $5$ units apart. How many common points do they have, and how are they positioned?

- Two common points.
- No common points.
- One point of internal tangency.
- One point of external tangency.

## Explanation

The distance between the centres is $5=8-3$. The radius-$3$ circle lies inside the radius-$8$ circle and touches it at the point on the line of centres beyond the smaller circle's centre.
