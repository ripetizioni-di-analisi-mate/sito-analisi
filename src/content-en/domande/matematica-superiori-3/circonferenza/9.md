What is the centre of the circle through $(0,0)$, $(6,0)$ and $(0,2)$?

- $(6,2)$
- $(2,1)$
- $(3,1)$
- $(3,2)$

## Explanation

The perpendicular bisector of the horizontal segment is $x=3$, while that of the vertical segment is $y=1$. The centre is their intersection. All three points have squared distance $10$ from $(3,1)$.
