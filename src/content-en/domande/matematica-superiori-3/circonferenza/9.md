What is the centre of the circle through $(0,0)$, $(6,0)$ and $(0,2)$?

- $(6,2)$
- $(2,1)$
- $(3,1)$
- $(3,2)$

## Explanation

The perpendicular bisector of the horizontal segment is $x=3$, while that of the vertical segment is $y=1$. The centre is their intersection. All three points have squared distance $10$ from $(3,1)$.

Equal distances from $(0,0)$ and $(6,0)$ put the horizontal coordinate halfway between theirs. Equal distances from $(0,0)$ and $(0,2)$ fix the vertical coordinate in the same way. As a check, the three displacement vectors all have component magnitudes $3$ and $1$.
