The rightmost point of a circle of radius $4$ is $(7,-1)$. What is its centre?

- $(3,-1)$
- $(7,-5)$
- $(7,3)$
- $(11,-1)$

## Explanation

The right endpoint of the horizontal diameter is $4$ units to the right of the centre and has the same $y$-coordinate. Thus the centre has $x$-coordinate $7-4=3$ and $y$-coordinate $-1$.
