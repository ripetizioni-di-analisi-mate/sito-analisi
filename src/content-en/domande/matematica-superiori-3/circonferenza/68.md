The equation $x^2+y^2-4x+2y+F=0$ passes through the origin. What follows?

- $F=0$
- $F=-2$
- $F=5$
- $F=4$

## Explanation

At the origin all terms containing $x$ or $y$ vanish. The left-hand side is therefore $F$ and must be zero.

Substituting $(0,0)$ directly gives $0+0-0+0+F=0$. With $F=0$, the centred form is $(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2=5$. At the origin the left-hand side equals $4+1=5$, confirming membership.
