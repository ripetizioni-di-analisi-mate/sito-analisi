For which values of $t$ does the line $x=t$ miss $(x+1)^2+(y-2)^2=16$?

- $-5<t<3$
- $t<-3$ or $t>5$
- $t\leq-5$ or $t\geq3$
- $t<-5$ or $t>3$

## Explanation

Its horizontal distance from the centre is $|t+1|$. The line misses the circle when $|t+1|>4$. At $t=-5$ and $t=3$ there is still one point because the line is tangent.
