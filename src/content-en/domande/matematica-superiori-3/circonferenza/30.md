A circle passes through $(0,0)$, $(4,0)$ and $(2,2)$. Which segment is a diameter?

- The segment from $(0,0)$ to $(2,2)$.
- None of the three.
- The segment from $(0,0)$ to $(4,0)$.
- The segment from $(4,0)$ to $(2,2)$.

## Explanation

The midpoint $(2,0)$ is $2$ units from each of the three points. Since the points are non-collinear, the centre is unique and is $(2,0)$. The horizontal segment passes through it and has endpoints on the circle.
