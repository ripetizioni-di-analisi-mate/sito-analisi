Where does the line $4x-3y=25$ touch $x^2+y^2=25$?

- $(4,3)$
- $(-4,3)$
- $(3,-4)$
- $(4,-3)$

## Explanation

The radius at the point of contact is parallel to the normal vector $(4,-3)$. This vector has length $5$ and its endpoint satisfies $4\cdot4-3(-3)=25$. Hence the point of contact is $(4,-3)$.
