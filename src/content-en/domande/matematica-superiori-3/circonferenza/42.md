Where does the line $4x-3y=25$ touch $x^2+y^2=25$?

- $(4,3)$
- $(-4,3)$
- $(3,-4)$
- $(4,-3)$

## Explanation

The radius at the point of contact is parallel to the normal vector $(4,-3)$. This vector has length $5$ and its endpoint satisfies $4\cdot4-3(-3)=25$. Hence the point of contact is $(4,-3)$.

The positive right-hand side selects the direction of the vector. The opposite point $(-4,3)$ is still on the circle but gives $-25$ on the left, so it belongs to the other parallel tangent. The values $4$ and $-3$ satisfy both equations.
