Which coordinate stays unchanged when a point on $(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2=9$ is reflected across $x=2$?

- Neither, for every point.
- The $y$-coordinate.
- Both, for every point.
- The $x$-coordinate.

## Explanation

The line $x=2$ is vertical. The reflection replaces $x$ with $4-x$ and leaves $y$ unchanged. The square of $x-2$ stays the same, so the reflected point lies on the curve.
