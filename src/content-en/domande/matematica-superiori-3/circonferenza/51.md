How far from the centre must a chord's line be for the chord to have length equal to the radius $r>0$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}r$
- $\dfrac r2$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}r$
- $r$

## Explanation

If the chord measures $r$, the half-chord measures $\dfrac r2$. The Pythagorean theorem gives $d^2=r^2-\dfrac{r^2}{4}=\dfrac{3r^2}{4}$. The distance is the non-negative square root.
