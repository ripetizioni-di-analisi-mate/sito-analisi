For which $q$ is the line $y=2x+q$ tangent to $x^2+(y-1)^2=5$?

- $q=-6$ or $q=4$
- $q=-4$ or $q=6$
- $q=-5$ or $q=5$
- $q=1$

## Explanation

The distance from $(0,1)$ to $2x-y+q=0$ is $\dfrac{|q-1|}{\sqrt5}$. Setting it equal to the radius $\sqrt5$ gives $|q-1|=5$, so $q=1\pm5$.
