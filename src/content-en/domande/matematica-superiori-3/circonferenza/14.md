The circles $x^2+y^2=9$ and $x^2+y^2-4y+m=0$ both pass through $(0,3)$. What is $m$?

- $m=3$
- $m=12$
- $m=-3$
- $m=-9$

## Explanation

The point satisfies the first equation because $3^2=9$. In the second, substitution gives $0+9-12+m=0$, hence $m=3$. At this value the second circle has radius $1$ and centre $(0,2)$.
