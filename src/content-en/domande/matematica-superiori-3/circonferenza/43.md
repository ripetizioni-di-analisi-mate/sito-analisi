What is the slope of the tangent to $x^2+y^2=25$ at $(-3,4)$?

- $-\dfrac43$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac43$
- $-\dfrac34$

## Explanation

The radius from the origin to the given point has slope $-\dfrac43$. The slopes of two perpendicular oblique lines multiply to $-1$, so the tangent has slope $\dfrac34$.
