A circle has centre $(1,1)$, radius $\sqrt2$, and a tangent at $(2,2)$. What is the $y$-intercept of this tangent?

- $2$
- $4$
- $1$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The radius to the point of contact has components $(1,1)$, so the tangent is $(x-2)+(y-2)=0$. Setting $x=0$ gives $y=4$.
