A circle of radius $10$ contains a chord of length $16$. What is the distance from the centre to the line of the chord?

- $8$
- $6$
- $12$
- $4$

## Explanation

In the right triangle formed by the radius and half-chord, the radius is the hypotenuse and the half-chord measures $8$. The remaining leg is $\sqrt{10^2-8^2}=\sqrt{36}=6$.
