Which equation describes the circle with centre $(-3,2)$ through $(-3,-2)$?

- $(x-3)^2+(y+2)^2=16$
- $(x+3)^2+(y-2)^2=16$
- $(x+3)^2+(y-2)^2=4$
- $(x+3)^2+(y+2)^2=16$

## Explanation

The points have the same $x$-coordinate, so the radius is the absolute difference between their $y$-coordinates, $|-2-2|=4$. The centre makes $x+3$ and $y-2$ zero. The squared radius is $16$.

The result is $(x+3)^2+(y-2)^2=16$. Substitution of the given point yields $0+(-4)^2=16$, while both squared terms vanish at the centre. The number $4$ is a length and must be squared on the right-hand side.
