What are the minimum and maximum $x$-coordinates on the circle $(x+2)^2+(y-7)^2=16$?

- $-2$ and $6$
- $-16$ and $16$
- $-6$ and $2$
- $3$ and $11$

## Explanation

The $x$-coordinate of the centre is $-2$ and the radius is $4$. The $x$-coordinate can be at most $4$ units from the centre. The extremes $-2-4=-6$ and $-2+4=2$ occur when $y=7$.
