What results from subtracting the equations $x^2+y^2=9$ and $x^2+y^2=16$?

- The line $y=x$.
- An impossible equality and no common point.
- An identity valid for all points.
- The $x$-axis as the radical axis.

## Explanation

The quadratic terms cancel completely, leaving $0=7$, which is impossible. The circles have the same centre and different radii, so they have no common point.
