The figure shows the circle centred at $C$ and the line through $A$ and $B$. Which segment gives the distance from the centre to the line?

::grafico{f="3" curve="5*cos(t),5*sin(t),0,6.283185307" xmin=-6 xmax=6 ymin=-6 ymax=6 uguale=1 punti="0,0,pieno,C;0,3,pieno,H;-4,3,pieno,A;4,3,pieno,B" segmenti="0,0,0,3;0,0,4,3" legenda="y=3;x^2+y^2=25" titolo="The horizontal line passes through $A$ and $B$."}

- $HB$
- $CB$
- $AB$
- $CH$

## Explanation

Distance to a line is measured along the perpendicular. Since the line is horizontal, the required segment is the vertical segment from the centre to $H$.
