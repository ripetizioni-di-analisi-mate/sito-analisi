A chord of the circle $x^2+y^2=100$ has midpoint $M(0,6)$. What is the equation of the line containing it?

- $y=-6$
- $y=6$
- $x=6$
- $x=0$

## Explanation

The segment from the origin to $M$ is vertical and perpendicular to the chord. The chord therefore lies on the horizontal line through $M$, namely $y=6$.
