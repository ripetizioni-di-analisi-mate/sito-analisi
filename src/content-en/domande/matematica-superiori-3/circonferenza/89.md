Which statement about the point of contact in the figure is correct?

::grafico{f="(25-3*x)/4" curve="5*cos(t),5*sin(t),0,6.283185307" xmin=-6 xmax=7 ymin=-6 ymax=7 uguale=1 punti="0,0,pieno,C;3,4,pieno,P" segmenti="0,0,3,4" legenda="3x+4y=25;x^2+y^2=25" titolo="The line meets the circle at $P$."}

- The segment $CP$ is parallel to the line.
- The length $CP$ is less than the radius.
- The segment $CP$ is perpendicular to the line.
- The line passes through $C$.

## Explanation

The point $P$ lies on the circle, so $CP$ is a radius. At tangency, the radius to the point of contact is perpendicular to the line.
