How is the graph of $y=2^{-x}$ obtained from the graph of $y=2^x$?

- Reflection across the $x$-axis.
- Reflection across the line $y=x$.
- A shift one unit to the right.
- Reflection across the $y$-axis.

## Explanation

Replacing $x$ by $-x$ reverses the signs of the horizontal coordinates and leaves the vertical coordinates unchanged. This is reflection across the $y$-axis.
