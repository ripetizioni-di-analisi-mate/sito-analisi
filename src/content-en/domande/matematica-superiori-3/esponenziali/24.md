How is the graph of $y=2^{-x}$ obtained from the graph of $y=2^x$?

- Reflection across the $x$-axis.
- Reflection across the line $y=x$.
- A shift one unit to the right.
- Reflection across the $y$-axis.

## Explanation

For every point $(u,2^u)$ on the original graph, the new function takes the same height at $x=-u$, since $2^{-(-u)}=2^u$. The horizontal coordinates change sign and the heights remain the same. This is reflection across the $y$-axis. For example, $(1,2)$ becomes $(-1,2)$, while $(0,1)$ stays on the line of reflection.
