Evaluate $\dfrac{2^{-3}\,2^5}{2^4}$.

- $\dfrac{2^{-3}\,2^5}{2^4}=4$
- $\dfrac{2^{-3}\,2^5}{2^4}=1$
- $\dfrac{2^{-3}\,2^5}{2^4}=\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac{2^{-3}\,2^5}{2^4}=\dfrac1{16}$

## Explanation

The product in the numerator adds its exponents, and division subtracts the denominator exponent. This gives $2^{-3+5-4}=2^{-2}=\dfrac14$.
