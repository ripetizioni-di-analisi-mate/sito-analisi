Evaluate $\dfrac{2^{-3}\,2^5}{2^4}$.

- $\dfrac{2^{-3}\,2^5}{2^4}=4$
- $\dfrac{2^{-3}\,2^5}{2^4}=1$
- $\dfrac{2^{-3}\,2^5}{2^4}=\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac{2^{-3}\,2^5}{2^4}=\dfrac1{16}$

## Explanation

The numerator is a product of powers of $2$, so add exponents $-3$ and $5$ to obtain $2^2$. Division by $2^4$ subtracts another $4$ from the exponent, leaving $2^{-2}=\dfrac14$. The ratio of $4$ to $16$ confirms the result. The negative exponent gives the reciprocal, so the final value stays positive.
