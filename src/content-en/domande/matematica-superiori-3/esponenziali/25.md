The graph shows $y=2^x$ and $y=3^x$. At which $x$-values does the graph of $3^x$ lie above that of $2^x$?

::grafico{f="2^x;3^x" xmin=-2 xmax=2 ymin=-0.3 ymax=7 punti="0,1" legenda="2^x;3^x" titolo="Graphs of powers with bases two and three"}

- For every $x$.
- $x>0$
- $x<0$
- $x\geq0$

## Explanation

For $x>0$ the ratio $\dfrac{3^x}{2^x}=\left(\dfrac32\right)^x$ exceeds $1$. At zero the ratio is $1$, so the intersection is excluded.
