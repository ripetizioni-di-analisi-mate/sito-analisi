The graph shows $y=2^x$ and $y=3^x$. At which $x$-values does the graph of $3^x$ lie above that of $2^x$?

::grafico{f="2^x;3^x" xmin=-2 xmax=2 ymin=-0.3 ymax=7 punti="0,1" legenda="2^x;3^x" titolo="Graphs of powers with bases two and three"}

- For every $x$.
- $x>0$
- $x<0$
- $x\geq0$

## Explanation

Compare the heights using the ratio $\dfrac{3^x}{2^x}=\left(\dfrac32\right)^x$, which is valid since $2^x>0$. The base $\dfrac32$ exceeds $1$, so the ratio exceeds $1$ exactly for $x>0$. At $x=0$ both curves have height $1$ and meet. At negative inputs the ratio is below $1$ and the order reverses. At $x=-1$, for example, compare $\dfrac13$ and $\dfrac12$.
