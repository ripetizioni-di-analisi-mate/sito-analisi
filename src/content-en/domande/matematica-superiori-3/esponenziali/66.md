A quantity falls by $10\%$ on two consecutive days. What is the overall percentage decrease?

- $10\%$
- $81\%$
- $20\%$
- $19\%$

## Explanation

Each day leaves $90\%$ of the value at the start of that day. After two days the remaining fraction is $0{,}9\cdot0{,}9=0{,}81$. The decrease is $1-0{,}81=0{,}19$, or $19\%$ of the initial value. For instance, starting from $100$ loses $10$ first and then $9$, because day two starts at $90$.
