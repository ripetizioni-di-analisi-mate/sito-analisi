If $N(t)=N_0q^t$ with $N_0,q>0$ and $q\ne1$, which ratio equals $q^3$ for every $t$?

- $\dfrac{N(t+3)}{N(t)}$
- $\dfrac{N(t+1)}{N(t)}$
- $\dfrac{N(t)}{N(t+3)}$
- $\dfrac{N(3t)}{N(t)}$

## Explanation

Dividing $N_0q^{t+3}$ by $N_0q^t$ cancels $N_0$ and subtracts the exponents. This leaves $q^{t+3-t}=q^3$.
