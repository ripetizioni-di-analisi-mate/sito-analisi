Two tanks lose water at different rates. After $n$ hours they contain $80\left(\dfrac12\right)^n$ and $40\left(\dfrac14\right)^n$ litres. What fraction of the water present after the first hour remains after the second?

- $\dfrac9{20}$
- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac5{12}$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

After one hour the tanks hold $40$ and $10$ litres, a total of $50$. After two hours they hold $20$ and $\dfrac52$, a total of $\dfrac{45}{2}$. The ratio of the totals is $\dfrac{45}{2}\div50=\dfrac9{20}$.
