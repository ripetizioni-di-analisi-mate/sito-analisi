For which $x$ does $3^x+1\leq1$ hold?

- For no $x$.
- For $x\leq0$.
- For $x<0$.
- For $x=0$.

## Explanation

Subtracting $1$ from both sides gives $3^x\leq0$. Every real power of base $3$ is strictly positive, including at negative exponents. No $x$ therefore satisfies the inequality. The graph of $3^x+1$ approaches $y=1$ from above for increasingly negative inputs, without ever reaching that height. Height $1$ is thus an asymptote. It is never attained as a minimum.
