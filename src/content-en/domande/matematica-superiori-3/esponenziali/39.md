Solve $5^{1-2x}\geq125$.

- $(-\infty,-1)$
- $[-1,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,1]$
- $(-\infty,-1]$

## Explanation

Because $125=5^3$ and $5^u$ is increasing, comparing exponents preserves the direction and gives $1-2x\geq3$. Subtracting $1$ gives $-2x\geq2$. Dividing by $-2$ now reverses the inequality, giving $x\leq-1$. The endpoint $-1$ gives exponent $3$. At $x=0$ the exponent is only $1$, below the threshold. The first transformation uses monotonicity. The inequality is reversed later, when dividing by the negative coefficient.
