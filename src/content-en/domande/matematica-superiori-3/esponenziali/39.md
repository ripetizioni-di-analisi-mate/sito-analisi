An archive uses two independent compression stages. In each cycle the first keeps $\dfrac34$ of the data and the second keeps $\dfrac23$ of what it receives. Which fraction of the original data remains after $n$ complete cycles?

- $\dfrac12\,n$
- $\left(\dfrac5{12}\right)^n$
- $\left(\dfrac1{12}\right)^n$
- $\left(\dfrac12\right)^n$

## Explanation

In one cycle the retained fraction is $\dfrac34\cdot\dfrac23=\dfrac12$. Each cycle acts on the data left by the preceding cycle. After $n$ cycles there have been exactly $n$ multiplications by $\dfrac12$.
