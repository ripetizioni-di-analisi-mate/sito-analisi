A fabric is washed several times. In each wash a first stage removes $\dfrac14$ of the dirt present and a second stage removes $\dfrac13$ of the remaining dirt. Which fraction of the initial dirt remains after $n$ washes?

- $\dfrac12\,n$
- $\left(\dfrac5{12}\right)^n$
- $\left(\dfrac1{12}\right)^n$
- $\left(\dfrac12\right)^n$

## Explanation

The first stage leaves $\dfrac34$ of the dirt present and the second leaves $\dfrac23$ of the dirt it receives. In one wash the remaining fraction is therefore $\dfrac34\cdot\dfrac23=\dfrac12$. Each wash acts on the dirt left by the previous one, so after $n$ washes the initial dirt has been multiplied $n$ times by $\dfrac12$ and the remaining fraction is $\left(\dfrac12\right)^n$.
