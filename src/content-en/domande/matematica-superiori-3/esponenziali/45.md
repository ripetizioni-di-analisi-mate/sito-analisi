Which condition is equivalent to $a^x>1$ for $0<a<1$?

- $x>0$
- $x\geq0$
- $x\ne0$
- $x<0$

## Explanation

Compare $a^x$ with $a^0=1$. For $0<a<1$ the exponential is strictly decreasing, so $a^x>a^0$ is equivalent to $x<0$. For instance, $a^{-1}=\dfrac1a>1$ because $a<1$. A positive exponent gives a value below $1$, and exponent zero gives equality, which the question excludes. The reversal comes from the fractional base, even when neither power is negative.
