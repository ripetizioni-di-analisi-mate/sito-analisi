The equation $4^x-10\cdot2^x+16=0$ has two real solutions $x_1,x_2$. What is $x_1+x_2$?

- $16$
- $4$
- $8$
- $10$

## Explanation

With $u=2^x$, the polynomial $u^2-10u+16$ has positive roots $2$ and $8$. Their product is $16=2^{x_1}2^{x_2}=2^{x_1+x_2}$. Since $16=2^4$, the exponents sum to $4$.
