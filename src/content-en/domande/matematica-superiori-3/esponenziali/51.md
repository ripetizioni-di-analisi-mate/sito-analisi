Solve $4^x+2^x-2=0$.

- $\{-2,1\}$
- $\{0\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\{1\}$

## Explanation

Since $4^x=(2^x)^2$, putting $u=2^x$ gives $u^2+u-2=(u+2)(u-1)=0$. The roots are $-2$ and $1$. The first lies outside the range of the exponential, leaving $u=1$. From $2^x=1=2^0$ we get $x=0$, and $4^0+2^0-2=0$ checks it. The number $1$ is the root in $u$. The other root cannot be converted with a real logarithm because its value is negative.
