Which curve approaches $y=0$ as $x$ increases without bound?

- $y=\left(\dfrac14\right)^x$
- $y=4^x$
- $y=4^{x-1}$
- $y=1+4^{-x}$

## Explanation

With base $\dfrac14$ between $0$ and $1$, its powers approach zero as the exponent increases. The curve $1+4^{-x}$ has the same exponential part, but the constant term shifts the asymptote to $y=1$. The powers $4^x$ and $4^{x-1}$ have base greater than $1$ and grow without bound to the right.
