Which curve approaches $y=0$ as $x$ increases without bound?

- $y=\left(\dfrac14\right)^x$
- $y=4^x$
- $y=4^{x-1}$
- $y=1+4^{-x}$

## Explanation

A positive base below $1$ gives values arbitrarily close to zero when the exponent increases. The curve $1+4^{-x}$ instead approaches $y=1$ because of the added constant.
