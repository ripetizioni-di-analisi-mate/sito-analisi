Solve $2^{x^2}\geq8$.

- $(-\infty,-\sqrt3]\cup[\sqrt3,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-3]\cup[3,+\infty)$
- $[\sqrt3,+\infty)$
- $[-\sqrt3,\sqrt3]$

## Explanation

Rewrite the threshold $8$ as $2^3$. Because $2^u$ increases, this gives $x^2\geq3$. The distance of $x$ from zero must be at least $\sqrt3$. The solution is $(-\infty,-\sqrt3]\cup[\sqrt3,+\infty)$. Include both endpoints because both square to $3$ and give the same power $8$. The inner interval would give powers below $8$, for instance $2^{0^2}=1$.
