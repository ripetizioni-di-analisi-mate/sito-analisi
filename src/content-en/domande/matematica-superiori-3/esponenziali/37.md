On $0\leq x\leq4$, the function $f(x)=2^{x(4-x)}$ increases and then decreases. What is its maximum value?

- $16$
- $4$
- $8$
- $256$

## Explanation

The exponent can be written as $x(4-x)=4-(x-2)^2$. It attains its maximum of $4$ at $x=2$. Since $2^u$ is increasing, the function reaches its maximum at the same input and equals $2^4=16$.
