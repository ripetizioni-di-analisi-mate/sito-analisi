Solve $2^{x^2}\geq8$.

- $(-\infty,-\sqrt3]\cup[\sqrt3,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-3]\cup[3,+\infty)$
- $[\sqrt3,+\infty)$
- $[-\sqrt3,\sqrt3]$

## Explanation

The increasing exponential allows us to compare $x^2$ with $3$. Since $x^2\geq3$ exactly when $|x|\geq\sqrt3$, the solution has two rays.
