What is the value of $81^{\frac12}$?

- $81^{\frac12}=3$
- $81^{\frac12}=9$
- $81^{\frac12}=-9$
- $81^{\frac12}=40{,}5$

## Explanation

The exponent $\dfrac12$ denotes the nonnegative square root, so $81^{\frac12}=\sqrt{81}=9$ since $9^2=81$. The equation $u^2=81$ would also admit $u=-9$. Here we are evaluating a fractional power, which for a positive base has one positive value. A fraction in the exponent calls for a root. Dividing $81$ by $2$ gives a different operation.
