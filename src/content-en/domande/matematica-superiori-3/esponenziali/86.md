At which $x$ does $\dfrac{9^x}{3^{x+1}}=27$ hold?

- $6$
- $2$
- $4$
- $3$

## Explanation

Write $9^x=3^{2x}$. In the quotient, subtract the entire exponent of the denominator to get $3^{2x-(x+1)}=3^{x-1}$. Since $27=3^3$, we have $x-1=3$ and hence $x=4$. As a check, the base-$3$ exponents are $8$ in the numerator and $5$ in the denominator, giving $3^{8-5}=27$. Grouping $2x-(x+1)$ is necessary because subtraction changes the sign of the constant $1$ as well.
