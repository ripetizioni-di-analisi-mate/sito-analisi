Let $F(x)=C\cdot2^x$ with $C>0$. For which value of $C$ does $F(r+s)=F(r)F(s)$ hold for every pair $r,s$?

- $C=\dfrac12$
- Every $C>0$.
- $C=1$
- $C=2$

## Explanation

The left-hand side is $C\,2^{r+s}$. The product on the right is $C^2\,2^{r+s}$. Dividing by the positive power leaves $C=C^2$, whose only solution under $C>0$ is $C=1$.
