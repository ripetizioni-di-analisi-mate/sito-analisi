Find the range of $g(x)=5-3^x$.

- $(-\infty,5)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(5,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,5]$

## Explanation

Every power $3^x$ is positive, so $5-3^x<5$. To see that every smaller height is reached, fix $y<5$ and solve $3^x=5-y$. The argument is positive, giving $x=\log_3(5-y)$. Thus the range is $(-\infty,5)$. Height $5$ would require a power equal to zero and is the least upper bound of the range. The function decreases because the growing exponential term is subtracted.
