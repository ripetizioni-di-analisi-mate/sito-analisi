A quantity starts at $96$ and halves every $4$ hours. What is its value after $12$ hours?

- $24$
- $48$
- $12$
- $8$

## Explanation

After $12$ hours, $\dfrac{12}{4}=3$ half-life periods have elapsed. The value is $96\left(\dfrac12\right)^3=96\cdot\dfrac18=12$. The same result follows by tracking the values at four-hour intervals. After $4$ hours there are $48$ units, after $8$ hours there are $24$, and after $12$ hours there are $12$. The exponent counts four-hour periods, rather than the hours themselves.
