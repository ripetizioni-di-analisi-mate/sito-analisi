The amount of a drug in the blood halves every $4$ hours. At $8$ p.m. there are $15\,\mathrm{mg}$ in the blood. How many milligrams were there at noon?

- $120\,\mathrm{mg}$
- $\dfrac{15}{4}\,\mathrm{mg}$
- $60\,\mathrm{mg}$
- $30\,\mathrm{mg}$

## Explanation

From noon to $8$ p.m. there are $8$ hours, that is two intervals of $4$ hours. The amount halves in each interval, so at $8$ p.m. it is one quarter of the amount at noon. Therefore there were $15\cdot4=60\,\mathrm{mg}$ at noon.
