A counter loses half its value every $4$ hours. The model started at noon, and at $8$ p.m. the counter reads $15$. What did it read at noon?

- $120$
- $\dfrac{15}{4}$
- $60$
- $30$

## Explanation

From noon to $8$ p.m. there are $8$ hours, or two halving intervals. The final value is therefore one quarter of the initial value. Working backwards from $15$ gives $15\cdot4=60$.
