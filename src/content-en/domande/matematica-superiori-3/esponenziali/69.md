A quantity grows from $100$ to $144$ in two months with a constant monthly factor $q>0$. What is the monthly percentage increase?

- $12\%$
- $44\%$
- $22\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

The final-to-initial ratio is $q^2=\dfrac{144}{100}=1{,}44$. The monthly factor is positive, so take the positive root to find $q=1{,}2$. The monthly increase is $q-1=0{,}2$, or $20\%$. The $44\%$ applies to both months together because the second increase acts on the first month's value. Indeed, two increases of $20\%$ take $100$ to $120$ and then to $144$.
