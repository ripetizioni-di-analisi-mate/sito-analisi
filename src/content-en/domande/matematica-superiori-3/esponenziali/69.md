A quantity grows from $100$ to $144$ in two months with a constant monthly factor $q>0$. What is the monthly percentage increase?

- $12\%$
- $44\%$
- $22\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

The ratio $\dfrac{144}{100}=1{,}44$ equals $q^2$. The positive square root gives $q=1{,}2$. The monthly increase is $q-1=0{,}2$, or $20\%$.
