Three consecutive readings of $F(n)=C\,a^n+k$ are $F(0)=5$, $F(1)=8$ and $F(2)=14$. What is the constant offset $k$?

- $5$
- $6$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

The successive differences are $3$ and $6$. In the model these are $C(a-1)$ and $Ca(a-1)$, so their ratio gives $a=2$. The first difference then gives $C=3$, and $C+k=5$ yields $k=2$.
