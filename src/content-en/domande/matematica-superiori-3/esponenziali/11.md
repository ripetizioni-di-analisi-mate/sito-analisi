In $3^{\frac1{x+1}}\geq9$, the fact that $3^u$ is increasing requires $\dfrac1{x+1}\geq2$. Which interval accounts for both the sign of the denominator and equality at the threshold?

- $\left(-1,-\dfrac12\right]$
- $\left[-1,-\dfrac12\right]$
- $\left(-\infty,-\dfrac12\right]$
- $\left[-\dfrac12,+\infty\right)$

## Explanation

A reciprocal at least as large as $2$ is positive, so $x+1>0$. Multiplication by this positive denominator gives $1\geq2(x+1)$, or $x\leq-\dfrac12$. The domain excludes $-1$, and $-\dfrac12$ gives exactly $9$.
