Solve $3^{\frac1{x+1}}\geq9$.

- $\left(-1,-\dfrac12\right]$
- $\left[-\dfrac12,+\infty\right)$
- $\left[-1,-\dfrac12\right]$
- $(-\infty,-1)$

## Explanation

Since $9=3^2$, on the domain $x\ne-1$ we need $\dfrac1{x+1}\geq2$. Moving all terms left gives $\dfrac{-2x-1}{x+1}\geq0$. The factors have the same sign only between $-1$ and $-\dfrac12$, including the zero of the numerator.
