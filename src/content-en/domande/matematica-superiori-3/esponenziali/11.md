Solve $3^{\frac1{x+1}}\geq9$.

- $\left(-1,-\dfrac12\right]$
- $\left[-\dfrac12,+\infty\right)$
- $\left[-1,-\dfrac12\right]$
- $(-\infty,-1)$

## Explanation

The domain excludes $x=-1$. Rewriting $9$ as $3^2$ and using the increasing exponential gives $\dfrac1{x+1}\geq2$. For $x<-1$ the fraction is negative, so the inequality fails. For $x>-1$ multiply by the positive denominator to obtain $1\geq2(x+1)$, or $x\leq-\dfrac12$. The interval is $\left(-1,-\dfrac12\right]$, and its right endpoint gives exactly $9$. The branch $x<-1$ cannot reach $9$, because the power is below $1$ there.
