How many solutions does $4^x-c\cdot2^x+1=0$ have if $c>2$?

- Infinitely many.
- Two distinct solutions.
- One.
- None.

## Explanation

The quadratic $u^2-cu+1$ has discriminant $c^2-4>0$. Its roots have product $1$ and sum $c>2$, so both are positive and distinct. Each corresponds to exactly one $x$.
