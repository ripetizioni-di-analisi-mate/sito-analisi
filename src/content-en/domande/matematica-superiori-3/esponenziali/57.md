The parameter $c$ is negative. How many intersections can $y=4^x+1$ and $y=c\,2^x$ have?

- Infinitely many.
- None.
- Exactly two.
- Exactly one.

## Explanation

For every $x$ the first expression exceeds $1$. The second is the product of the negative number $c$ and the positive number $2^x$, so it is negative. The vertical coordinates have opposite signs at every input, ruling out intersections.
