The curve $y=2^x$ passes through $(2,4)$. If the curve is shifted $3$ units left and $5$ units up, where does that point go?

- $(5,9)$
- $(-1,-1)$
- $(-1,9)$
- $(2,9)$

## Explanation

A shift to the left subtracts $3$ from the horizontal coordinate and a shift up adds $5$ to the vertical coordinate. This gives $(2-3,4+5)=(-1,9)$. Indeed, the translated curve has equation $y=2^{x+3}+5$ and takes the value $9$ at $x=-1$.
