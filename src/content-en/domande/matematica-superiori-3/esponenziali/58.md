What is the greatest lower bound of the values of $f(x)=3^{x-2}+7$?

- $0$
- $2$
- $7$
- $8$

## Explanation

Since $3^{x-2}$ is positive, every value of $f$ exceeds $7$. Moving left, the exponent decreases without bound and the power approaches zero. Values can be made arbitrarily close to $7$ from above, although that height is never attained. No number above $7$ is a lower bound, so the greatest lower bound of the range is $7$.
