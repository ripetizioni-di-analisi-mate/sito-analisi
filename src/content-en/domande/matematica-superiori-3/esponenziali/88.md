Let $f(x)=3\cdot2^x+1$. What is $f(x+1)-f(x)$?

- $1$
- $2^x+1$
- $3\cdot2^{x+1}$
- $3\cdot2^x$

## Explanation

Subtract the two values $3\cdot2^{x+1}+1$ and $3\cdot2^x+1$. The constant terms cancel. Since $2^{x+1}=2\cdot2^x$, their difference becomes $3\cdot2^x(2-1)=3\cdot2^x$. For example, from $x=0$ to $x=1$, the value rises from $4$ to $7$, an increase of $3$. The increase therefore depends on the starting input. At greater inputs the difference increases, because it contains a base-$2$ exponential times a positive coefficient.
