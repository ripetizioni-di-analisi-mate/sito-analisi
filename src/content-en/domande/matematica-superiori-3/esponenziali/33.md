What is the exact solution of $5^x=7$?

- $x=\log_7 5$
- $x=\log_5 7$
- $x=\dfrac75$
- $x=\ln7-\ln5$

## Explanation

We are looking for the exponent of base $5$ that gives $7$. Since $7$ is positive, the definition of the logarithm gives $x=\log_5 7$. This is exact even without an integer exponent. Indeed, $5<7<25$ and the exponential increases, so the exponent is between $1$ and $2$. The same value is $\dfrac{\ln7}{\ln5}$.
