Solve $27^{x-1}=9$.

- $\left\{\dfrac32\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac23\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac53\right\}$
- $\{3\}$

## Explanation

Since $27=3^3$ and $9=3^2$, we have $(3^3)^{x-1}=3^2$. The power-of-a-power law gives $3^{3(x-1)}=3^2$, so $3(x-1)=2$. Divide by $3$ and add $1$ to obtain $x=\dfrac53$. To check, the original exponent is then $\dfrac23$, and $27^{\frac23}=(\sqrt[3]{27})^2=9$. The exponent $\dfrac23$ is the value of $x-1$, so add $1$ to get the unknown. This separates the solution $\dfrac53$ from taking the cube root during the check.
