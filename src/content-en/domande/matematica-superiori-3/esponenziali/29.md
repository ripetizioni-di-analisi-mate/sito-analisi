Two real exponents satisfy $2^r=3$ and $3^s=4$. What is their product $rs$ without evaluating the exponents separately?

- $3$
- $\dfrac32$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Raising both sides of the first equality to the power $s$ gives $2^{rs}=3^s$. By the second condition the right-hand side is $4=2^2$. A positive base different from $1$ takes the same value only at equal exponents, so $rs=2$.
