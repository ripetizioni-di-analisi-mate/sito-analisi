An exponential equation has been reduced to $2^{x^2-4x}=1$. What is the mean of its two solutions, which is also the horizontal coordinate of the exponent's axis of symmetry?

- $4$
- $-2$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

A power of base $2$ equals $1$ only when its exponent is zero. The equation $x(x-4)=0$ has solutions $0$ and $4$. Their mean is $2$, also the centre of the square in $x^2-4x=(x-2)^2-4$.
