For $a>0$ and $a\ne1$, which expression equals $a^{2x}a^{3-x}$ for every $x$?

- $a^{x+3}$
- $a^{3x-3}$
- $a^{2x(3-x)}$
- $a^{3x+3}$

## Explanation

The product contains two powers of the same positive base. Add the exponents to get $a^{2x}a^{3-x}=a^{2x+(3-x)}=a^{x+3}$. The term $-x$ reduces $2x$ to $x$. For example, at $x=1$ the original exponents are $2$ and $2$, whose total $4$ agrees with $x+3$. Multiplying the exponents would apply if one power were raised to another power.
