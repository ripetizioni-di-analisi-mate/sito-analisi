An initial quantity of $70$ units doubles every $4$ hours. How many units does the model predict after $8$ hours?

- $N=630$
- $N=280$
- $N=140$
- $N=560$

## Explanation

The quantity doubles once every $4$ hours. After $8$ hours, $\dfrac84=2$ periods have passed, so the combined factor is $2^2=4$. Starting at $70$, the first doubling gives $140$ and the second gives $280$. The intermediate value $140$ therefore belongs to hour four, while the question asks about hour eight.
