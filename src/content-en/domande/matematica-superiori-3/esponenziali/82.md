A sequence satisfies $u_0=12$ and $u_{n+1}=\dfrac32u_n$ for $n\geq0$. Which formula gives $u_n$?

- $\left(\dfrac32\right)^{12n}$
- $12+\dfrac32n$
- $12\left(\dfrac32\right)^n$
- $12\left(\dfrac23\right)^n$

## Explanation

Starting at $12$, multiply by $\dfrac32$ a total of $n$ times to reach the term with index $n$. This gives $12\left(\dfrac32\right)^n$.
