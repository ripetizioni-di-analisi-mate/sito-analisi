A sequence satisfies $u_0=12$ and $u_{n+1}=\dfrac32u_n$ for $n\geq0$. Which formula gives $u_n$?

- $\left(\dfrac32\right)^{12n}$
- $12+\dfrac32n$
- $12\left(\dfrac32\right)^n$
- $12\left(\dfrac23\right)^n$

## Explanation

The term at index zero is $12$. Increasing the index by one multiplies by $\dfrac32$, so reaching index $n$ multiplies by that factor $n$ times. Thus $u_n=12\left(\dfrac32\right)^n$. The first checks are $u_1=18$ and $u_2=27$. An additive model would have a fixed increase, whereas here the ratio between consecutive terms is fixed.
