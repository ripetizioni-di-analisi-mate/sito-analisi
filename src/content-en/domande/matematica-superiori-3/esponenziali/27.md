How does $f(x)=7^x$ change when $x$ is replaced by $x+2$?

- The value is squared.
- The value increases by $49$.
- The value is multiplied by $49$.
- The value is multiplied by $14$.

## Explanation

Replacing $x$ with $x+2$ gives $7^{x+2}=7^x\cdot7^2=49f(x)$. The factor $49$ is the same for every $x$. For instance, from $x=0$ to $x=2$ the value changes from $1$ to $49$, and from $x=1$ to $x=3$ it changes from $7$ to $343$. Both ratios are $49$. This corresponds to applying the factor $7$ twice, once for each unit.
