How does $g(x)=4-\left(\dfrac12\right)^x$ vary?

- It increases up to $x=0$ and then decreases.
- It is constant.
- It is strictly increasing.
- It is strictly decreasing.

## Explanation

The power with base $\dfrac12$ is decreasing. Its negative is increasing, and adding the constant $4$ does not change monotonicity.
