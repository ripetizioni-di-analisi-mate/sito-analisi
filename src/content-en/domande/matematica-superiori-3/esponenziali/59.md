Which function results from shifting $y=3^x$ two units right and one unit down?

- $y=3^{x+2}-1$
- $y=3^{x-2}+1$
- $y=3^{x-2}-1$
- $y=3^x-3$

## Explanation

If the original input is $u$, shifting right gives $x=u+2$. Thus the old input to put in the exponent is $u=x-2$. Shifting down subtracts $1$ from the height and gives $y=3^{x-2}-1$. As a check, $(0,1)$ moves to $(2,0)$ and the new function is indeed zero at $x=2$.
