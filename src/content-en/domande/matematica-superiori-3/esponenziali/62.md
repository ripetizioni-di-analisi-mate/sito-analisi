The graph of $y=C\cdot2^x+k$ has asymptote $y=3$ and passes through $(0,7)$. What is $C$?

- $4$
- $10$
- $7$
- $3$

## Explanation

For increasingly negative $x$, $2^x$ approaches zero. Thus the function has asymptote $y=k$, so the given line gives $k=3$. The point $(0,7)$ then gives $7=C\cdot2^0+3=C+3$, hence $C=4$. The coefficient $C$ is the signed vertical distance from the asymptote $3$ to the intercept $7$. Consequently $C$ is positive and the curve approaches $y=3$ from above.
