Solve $9^x-4\cdot3^x+3=0$.

- $\{0,1\}$
- $\{1\}$
- $\{-1,0\}$
- $\{1,3\}$

## Explanation

We have $9^x=(3^x)^2$. Substituting $u=3^x>0$ makes the left-hand side $u^2-4u+3=(u-1)(u-3)$. The roots $1$ and $3$ are positive, so both are attainable as powers of $3$. From $3^x=1=3^0$ we get $x=0$. From $3^x=3^1$ we get $x=1$. The roots of the quadratic are values of $u$. The solution set is $\{0,1\}$. Substituting $x=1$ gives $9-12+3=0$.
