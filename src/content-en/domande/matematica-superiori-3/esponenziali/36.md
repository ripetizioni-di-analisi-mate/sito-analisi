Solve $1^x=1$ over the reals.

- $\varnothing$
- $\{1\}$
- $\{0\}$
- $\mathbb R$

## Explanation

A base of $1$ gives $1^x=1$ for every real exponent, so the solution set is $\mathbb R$. The rule allowing exponents to be equated depends on injectivity and does not apply here because the function is constant. For example, both $x=0$ and $x=2$ satisfy the equation despite being different exponents.
