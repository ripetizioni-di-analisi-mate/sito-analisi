What is the range of $y=4^x$ for $x\in\mathbb R$?

- $[1,+\infty)$
- $[0,+\infty)$
- $(0,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$

## Explanation

Every real exponent gives $4^x>0$, so negative values and zero never occur. Conversely, given any $y>0$, the exponent $x=\log_4 y$ satisfies $4^x=y$. The range is therefore $(0,+\infty)$. For instance, values between zero and one are obtained at negative exponents, and $x=-1$ gives $\dfrac14$. The horizontal axis is the asymptote on the left, and its height is outside the range.
