What is $f(0)$ if $f(x)=a^x$ with $a>0$ and $a\ne1$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac1a$
- $a$
- $0$

## Explanation

Substituting $x=0$ gives $a^0=1$. This also follows from $\dfrac aa=a^{1-1}=a^0$, since $a$ is nonzero. So $(0,1)$ lies on the graph for every admissible base. The value $a$ occurs at $x=1$, while the value $\dfrac1a$ occurs at $x=-1$. These are points with different horizontal coordinates. The vertical intercept is shared by all exponential graphs with positive base.
