Solve $3^{\frac1{x-2}}=1$.

- $\varnothing$
- $\{0\}$
- $\{3\}$
- $\{2\}$

## Explanation

The domain requires $x\ne2$. Writing the right-hand side as $3^0$ gives $\dfrac1{x-2}=0$. On its domain we can multiply by $x-2$, which would give the impossible equality $1=0$. So there is no solution. The fraction can approach zero when $|x|$ is large but stays nonzero for every allowed input.
