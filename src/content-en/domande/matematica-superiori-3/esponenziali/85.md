For $3^{\frac1{x-2}}=1$, the exponent would have to equal zero. Which property of the reciprocal decides whether a solution exists?

- Its numerator is $1$, so it is nonzero wherever it is defined.
- It equals zero where its denominator is zero.
- It is less than $1$ throughout its domain.
- It is positive throughout its domain.

## Explanation

A defined quotient is zero only if its numerator is zero. Here the numerator is the constant $1$, and $x=2$ is outside the domain. Therefore no real $x$ gives the required exponent.
