Which integer solutions does the inequality $\left(\dfrac12\right)^{x^2-1}>1$ have?

- None.
- Only $x=0$.
- All integers except $0$.
- $x=-1$ and $x=1$.

## Explanation

The power exceeds $1$ when $x^2-1<0$. The endpoints $-1$ and $1$ are excluded, so the only integer in $(-1,1)$ is $0$.
