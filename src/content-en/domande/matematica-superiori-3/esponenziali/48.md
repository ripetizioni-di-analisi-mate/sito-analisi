Which integer solutions does the inequality $\left(\dfrac12\right)^{x^2-1}>1$ have?

- None.
- Only $x=0$.
- All integers except $0$.
- $x=-1$ and $x=1$.

## Explanation

Since $1=\left(\dfrac12\right)^0$, the decreasing exponential gives $x^2-1<0$. Thus $x^2<1$, so the real solution interval is $(-1,1)$. The only integer strictly between the endpoints is $0$. At $x=0$ the exponent is $-1$ and the power is $2$, whereas integers $-1$ and $1$ give equality. Integers with absolute value greater than $1$ instead give a positive exponent.
