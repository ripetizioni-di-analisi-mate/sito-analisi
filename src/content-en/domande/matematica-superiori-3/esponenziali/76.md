Which expression is equal to $9^{x+\frac12}$ for every $x$?

- $9^x\cdot\dfrac12$
- $3\cdot9^x$
- $3^{x+1}$
- $9^x+3$

## Explanation

In the sum $x+\dfrac12$, both terms are exponents, so the power splits into the product $9^x\cdot9^{\frac12}$. Since $9^{\frac12}=\sqrt9=3$, this gives $3\cdot9^x$. As a check, both expressions give $3$ at $x=0$. Treating the fractional exponent as a factor $\dfrac12$ outside the power already gives a different result there.
