Let $f(x)=2^x$. At $x=3$ compare the composition $f(f(x))$ with the square $(f(x))^2$. What is the ratio of the first quantity to the second?

- $8$
- $4$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

First compute $f(3)=8$. The composition equals $f(8)=2^8$, whereas the square equals $8^2=2^6$. The ratio is $2^{8-6}=4$.
