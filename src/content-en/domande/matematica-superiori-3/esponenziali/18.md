Which expression factors $2^{x+1}+2^x$ for every $x$?

- $2^{2x+1}$
- $3\cdot2^x$
- $4\cdot2^x$
- $2^x+2$

## Explanation

Since $2^{x+1}=2\cdot2^x$, the sum is $2\cdot2^x+1\cdot2^x$. Factoring out $2^x$ gives $2^x(2+1)=3\cdot2^x$. Adding exponents is a rule for products of powers. Applying it to the two terms of this sum, for example at $x=1$, would give $8$ instead of $2^2+2=6$. Since $2^x>0$, dividing the sum by $2^x$ checks the coefficient and gives $2+1=3$.
