Which comparison between powers of $3$ is correct?

- $3^{-\frac12}=-3^{\frac13}$
- $3^{-\frac12}>3^{\frac13}$
- $3^{-\frac12}<3^{\frac13}$
- $3^{-\frac12}=3^{\frac13}$

## Explanation

The function $3^x$ is increasing and $-\dfrac12<\dfrac13$. The same order therefore holds for the powers.
