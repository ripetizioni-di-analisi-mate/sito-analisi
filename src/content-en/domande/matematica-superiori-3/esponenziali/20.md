Which comparison between powers of $3$ is correct?

- $3^{-\frac12}=-3^{\frac13}$
- $3^{-\frac12}>3^{\frac13}$
- $3^{-\frac12}<3^{\frac13}$
- $3^{-\frac12}=3^{\frac13}$

## Explanation

The exponents satisfy $-\dfrac12<\dfrac13$. The function $u\mapsto3^u$ is increasing, so $3^{-\frac12}<3^{\frac13}$. A second check uses $3^0=1$. The negative exponent gives $3^{-\frac12}=\dfrac1{\sqrt3}<1$, while the positive one gives $\sqrt[3]3>1$. The two powers are positive and lie on opposite sides of level $1$. The comparison therefore holds without approximating either of the two roots.
