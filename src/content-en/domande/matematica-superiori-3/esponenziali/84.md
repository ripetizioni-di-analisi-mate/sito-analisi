A warehouse's stock halves every night. Each morning, after the halving, $12$ crates always arrive. How many crates must there be one morning for the stock to remain the same on every subsequent morning?

- $12$
- $36$
- $6$
- $24$

## Explanation

If the morning stock is $Q$ crates, the next morning there are $\dfrac Q2+12$. For the stock to stay constant, we need $Q=\dfrac Q2+12$, giving $Q=24$. Each night then leaves $12$ crates and the delivery restores the stock to $24$.
