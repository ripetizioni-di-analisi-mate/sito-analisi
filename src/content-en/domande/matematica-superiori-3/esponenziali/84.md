What is $t$ when $120\left(\dfrac13\right)^t=40$?

- $3$
- $-1$
- $\dfrac13$
- $1$

## Explanation

Divide by the initial value $120$ to get $\left(\dfrac13\right)^t=\dfrac{40}{120}=\dfrac13$. The base is positive and differs from $1$, so the exponential is one-to-one. Since $\dfrac13=\left(\dfrac13\right)^1$, we get $t=1$. One period leaves a third of the quantity. Two periods would leave a ninth of the original amount. As a check, at $t=2$ the value would be $\dfrac{40}{3}$, already below the required level.
