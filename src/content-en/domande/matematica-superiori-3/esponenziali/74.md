Two quantities both start at $50$. Each day the first grows by $10$ units and the second is multiplied by $1{,}2$. What is the second minus the first after two days?

- $0$
- $-2$
- $2$
- $10$

## Explanation

With a fixed rise of $10$ units, the first quantity is $50+2\cdot10=70$ on day two. Multiplying the second twice by $1{,}2$ gives $50(1{,}2)^2=72$. Subtracting the first from the second gives $2$. Both equal $60$ after day one. The difference appears on day two, when $20\%$ of $60$ is $12$.
