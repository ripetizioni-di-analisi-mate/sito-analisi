Two quantities both start at $50$. Each day the first grows by $10$ units and the second is multiplied by $1{,}2$. What is the second minus the first after two days?

- $0$
- $-2$
- $2$
- $10$

## Explanation

The first model gives $50+2\cdot10=70$. The second gives $50(1{,}2)^2=72$. The difference in the order asked for is $72-70=2$.
