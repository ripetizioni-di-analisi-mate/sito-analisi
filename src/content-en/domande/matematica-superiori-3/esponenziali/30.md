The product $5^{x+1}\cdot5^{x-2}$ must equal $1$. What is the arithmetic mean of the two exponents when the constraint holds?

- $0$
- $\dfrac12$
- $-\dfrac12$
- $1$

## Explanation

The product is a power of $5$ whose exponent is the sum of the two exponents. To obtain $1=5^0$, that sum must vanish, so its half is zero as well. Indeed, at $x=\dfrac12$ the exponents are $\dfrac32$ and $-\dfrac32$.
