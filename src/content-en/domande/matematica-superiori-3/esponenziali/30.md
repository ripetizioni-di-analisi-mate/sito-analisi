Solve $5^{x+1}\cdot5^{x-2}=1$.

- $\left\{\dfrac12\right\}$
- $\{0\}$
- $\left\{-\dfrac12\right\}$
- $\{1\}$

## Explanation

In the product the exponents add, giving $5^{(x+1)+(x-2)}=5^{2x-1}$. For this power to equal $1=5^0$, its exponent must be zero. From $2x-1=0$ we get $x=\dfrac12$. At that value the original exponents are $\dfrac32$ and $-\dfrac32$, so the factors are reciprocals and their product is $1$. Requiring both factors to equal $1$ would be too strong. They only need to be reciprocals.
