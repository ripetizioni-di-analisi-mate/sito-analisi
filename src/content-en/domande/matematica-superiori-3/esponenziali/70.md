A quantity doubles every $3$ hours according to $N(t)=N_0\cdot2^{\frac t3}$. In how many hours is it eight times its initial value?

- $6$
- $3$
- $24$
- $9$

## Explanation

Dividing by the positive initial value gives $2^{\frac t3}=8=2^3$. The exponent counts periods of $3$ hours, so $\dfrac t3=3$ and $t=9$ hours. Each period multiplies by $2$. After the second the factor is $4$, while after the third it becomes the required factor $8$. Before the third doubling, the model is below the threshold because it increases over time.
