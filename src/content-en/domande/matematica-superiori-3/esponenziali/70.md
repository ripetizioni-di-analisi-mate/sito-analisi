A quantity doubles every $3$ hours according to $N(t)=N_0\cdot2^{\frac t3}$. In how many hours is it eight times its initial value?

- $6$
- $3$
- $24$
- $9$

## Explanation

The factor $8$ is $2^3$, so the exponent $\dfrac t3$ equals $3$. Three periods of $3$ hours have elapsed.
