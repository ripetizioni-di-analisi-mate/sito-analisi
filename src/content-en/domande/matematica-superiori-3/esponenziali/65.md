An initial quantity of $500$ grows by $20\%$ in each of two periods. What is the final value?

- $600$
- $620$
- $720$
- $700$

## Explanation

Each period adds $20\%$ of the value at the start of that period, so multiply by $1{,}20$. After the first increase the value is $500\cdot1{,}20=600$. The second increase is $600\cdot0{,}20=120$, giving $720$ in total. Using an increase of $100$ twice would give $700$ and model a fixed increase of $100$ each period.
