An initial quantity of $500$ grows by $20\%$ in each of two periods. What is the final value?

- $600$
- $620$
- $720$
- $700$

## Explanation

Each increase multiplies by $1{,}20$, so the value is $500(1{,}20)^2=500\cdot1{,}44=720$. The second percentage is applied to the increased value.
