A fund grows by $10\%$ at the end of each year. Just after the first increase, another $100$ units are deposited. Starting with $100$ units, how much is there just after the second increase?

- $221$
- $242$
- $231$
- $220$

## Explanation

The initial $100$ becomes $110$ at the end of the first year. The deposit raises the amount to $210$ before the second increase. At the end of year two there are therefore $210\cdot1.10=231$ units.
