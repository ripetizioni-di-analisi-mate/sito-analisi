Solve $\left(\dfrac12\right)^{x-1}>\dfrac14$.

- $(-\infty,3]$
- $(-\infty,1)$
- $(3,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,3)$

## Explanation

Write the threshold as $\dfrac14=\left(\dfrac12\right)^2$. For this base, a larger power corresponds to a smaller exponent. This gives $x-1<2$, and hence $x<3$. At $x=3$ the power equals exactly $\dfrac14$, which the strict comparison excludes. For example, $x=2$ gives $\dfrac12$, which is above the threshold. At $x=4$ the exponent is $3$ and the height is $\dfrac18$, below the threshold.
