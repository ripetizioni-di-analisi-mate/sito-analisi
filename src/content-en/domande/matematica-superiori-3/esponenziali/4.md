Solve $\left(\dfrac12\right)^{x-1}>\dfrac14$.

- $(-\infty,3]$
- $(-\infty,1)$
- $(3,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,3)$

## Explanation

The threshold is $\left(\dfrac12\right)^2$. The base gives a decreasing function, so values above the threshold correspond to exponents smaller than $2$. From $x-1<2$ we obtain $x<3$.
