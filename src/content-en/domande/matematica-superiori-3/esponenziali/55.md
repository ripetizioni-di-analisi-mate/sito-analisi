If $u=2^x$ and $u+\dfrac1u=3$, what is $4^x+4^{-x}$ without finding $x$?

- $5$
- $3$
- $9$
- $7$

## Explanation

The required expression is $u^2+\dfrac1{u^2}$. The square of $u+\dfrac1u$ is $u^2+2+\dfrac1{u^2}$. Subtracting the cross term $2$ from $3^2=9$ therefore gives $7$.
