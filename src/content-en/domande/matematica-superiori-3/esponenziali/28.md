Which property of $a^x$ allows us to pass from $a^u=a^v$ to $u=v$ for $a>0$, $a\ne1$?

- The intersection with the $y$-axis.
- The existence of an asymptote.
- Injectivity.
- Positivity.

## Explanation

An injective function assigns different values to different inputs. Equality of the powers thus requires the exponents to agree.
