Which power is equal to $\sqrt[3]{5^2}$?

- $5^{\frac23}$
- $5^{-\frac23}$
- $5^6$
- $5^{\frac32}$

## Explanation

The cube root corresponds to exponent $\dfrac13$ since the radicand $5^2$ is positive. The power-of-a-power law gives $(5^2)^{\frac13}=5^{2\cdot\frac13}=5^{\frac23}$. As a check, cubing $5^{\frac23}$ yields $5^2$, the given radicand. The denominator $3$ determines the root, while the numerator preserves the exponent $2$ of the radicand.
