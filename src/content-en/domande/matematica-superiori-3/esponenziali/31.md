How many real solutions does $2^x=-4$ have?

- Two.
- One.
- None.
- Infinitely many.

## Explanation

The value of $2^x$ is positive for every real $x$. Negative exponents give reciprocals of positive powers, for example $2^{-2}=\dfrac14$. The sign of the exponent therefore does not make the power negative. The graph stays above the horizontal axis, while the line $y=-4$ is below it. They have no intersection.
