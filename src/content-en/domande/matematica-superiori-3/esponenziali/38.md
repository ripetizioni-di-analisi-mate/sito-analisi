Solving $y=3^{\sqrt x}$ for $x$ gives $x=(\log_3 y)^2$. From what value of $y$ upwards does this inversion give points on the original graph?

- $3$
- $1$
- $9$
- $0$

## Explanation

The original equation gives $\log_3 y=\sqrt x$, which is nonnegative. The base-$3$ logarithm is increasing, so this constraint means $y\geq1$. For every $y$ in that interval, squaring gives a valid input, and its square root recovers the logarithm.
