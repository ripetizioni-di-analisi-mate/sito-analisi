Solve $3^{\sqrt x}<9$.

- $[0,4]$
- $[0,4)$
- $(-\infty,4)$
- $(0,4)$

## Explanation

The root in the exponent requires $x\geq0$. With $9=3^2$ and an increasing base, this gives $\sqrt{x}<2$. Both sides are nonnegative, so squaring is equivalent to $x<4$ on the domain. The solution is $[0,4)$. At $x=0$ the power is $1$ and satisfies the comparison. At $x=4$ it is $9$ and is excluded.
