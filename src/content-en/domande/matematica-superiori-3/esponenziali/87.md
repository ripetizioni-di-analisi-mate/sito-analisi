Two points on $y=3^x$ have vertical coordinates $p$ and $q$ with $q=9p$. What horizontal distance separates them, independently of $p>0$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $6$
- $9$

## Explanation

If the inputs are $r<s$, the ratio of the outputs is $\dfrac{3^s}{3^r}=3^{s-r}$. Since $9=3^2$, injectivity requires $s-r=2$. The initial coordinate cancels from the ratio.
