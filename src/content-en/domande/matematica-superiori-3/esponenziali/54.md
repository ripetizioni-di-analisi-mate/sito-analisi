With $u=2^x$, the expression $4^x-5\cdot2^x+4$ factors as $(u-1)(u-4)$. At which horizontal coordinates does it change sign as $x$ increases?

- $1$ and $4$
- $0$ and $2$
- $0$ and $4$
- $1$ and $2$

## Explanation

The two factors have simple zeros at $u=1$ and $u=4$, so the product changes sign when either value is crossed. The variable $u=2^x$ increases, crossing $1$ at $x=0$ and $4$ at $x=2$.
