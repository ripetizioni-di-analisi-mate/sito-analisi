Solve $4^x-5\cdot2^x+4\leq0$.

- $[1,4]$
- $[0,2]$
- $(-\infty,0]\cup[2,+\infty)$
- $(0,2)$

## Explanation

For $u=2^x>0$ the left side is $(u-1)(u-4)$. The product is negative between the roots, where the factors have opposite signs, and zero at the endpoints. We obtain $1\leq2^x\leq4$. The increasing base converts the heights to exponents $0$ and $2$, giving $0\leq x\leq2$. The interval $[1,4]$ concerns the substitution variable.
