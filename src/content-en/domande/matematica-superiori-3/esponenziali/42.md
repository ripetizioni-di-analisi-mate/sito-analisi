Solve $2^{|x|}\leq16$.

- $[-16,16]$
- $[-4,4]$
- $(0,4]$
- $(-\infty,-4]\cup[4,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Write $16=2^4$. A power of base $2$ increases with its exponent, so $2^{|x|}\leq2^4$ is equivalent to $|x|\leq4$. The absolute value is the distance from zero, so $-4\leq x\leq4$. Both endpoints have exponent $4$ and give height $16$. They are included because equality is allowed. The heights are symmetric because replacing $x$ by $-x$ leaves the absolute value unchanged.
