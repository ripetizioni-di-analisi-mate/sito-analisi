The threshold $2^{|x|}\leq16$ admits only some odd integers. How many odd integers are admitted?

- $2$
- $4$
- $9$
- $5$

## Explanation

Since $16=2^4$ and $2^u$ is increasing, $|x|\leq4$ is required. The odd integers between $-4$ and $4$ are $-3,-1,1,3$. Both endpoints are even and do not affect the count.
