A value decreases by $15\%$ each day. What factor multiplies the value from one day to the next?

- $q=0{,}15$
- $q=1{,}15$
- $q=0{,}95$
- $q=0{,}85$

## Explanation

A $15\%$ decrease leaves $100\%-15\%=85\%$ of the previous value. The daily multiplier is therefore $\dfrac{85}{100}=0{,}85$. For example, starting from $200$, the first day loses $30$ units and leaves $170=200\cdot0{,}85$. The factor $0{,}15$ would calculate only the amount lost, not the value remaining at the end of the day.
