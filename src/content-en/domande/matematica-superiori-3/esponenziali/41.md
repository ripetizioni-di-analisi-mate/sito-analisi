For which $x$ does $4^x<2^x$ hold?

- $x>0$
- $x<0$
- $x\leq0$
- $x\ne0$

## Explanation

Since $4^x=2^{2x}$, we may divide by $2^x$, which is positive for every $x$. The comparison becomes $2^x<1=2^0$. Base $2$ exceeds $1$, so $x<0$. Input $0$ would give equality and is excluded. At $x=-1$, for example, the powers are $\dfrac14$ and $\dfrac12$, in the required order. At positive inputs the order reverses, as at $x=1$, where $4>2$.
