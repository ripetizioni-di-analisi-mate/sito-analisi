Suppose $f(x)=a^x$ with $a>0$ and $f(3)=5$. Which expression equals $f(7)$ without approximating $a$?

- $35$
- $25\sqrt[3]5$
- $5\sqrt[7]3$
- $125$

## Explanation

Since $a^3=5$, we have $a=\sqrt[3]5$. Writing $7=3+3+1$ gives $a^7=25\sqrt[3]5$.
