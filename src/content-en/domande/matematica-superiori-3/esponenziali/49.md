The graph shows $y=\left(\dfrac12\right)^x$ and the line $y=1$. At which $x$-coordinates is the curve below the line?

::grafico{f="0.5^x;1" xmin=-2 xmax=3 ymin=-0.2 ymax=4.5 punti="0,1" legenda="\left(\frac12\right)^x;1" titolo="Comparing the exponential with level one"}

- $x\geq0$
- $x<0$
- $x>0$
- $x\ne0$

## Explanation

The curve and line meet at $x=0$, since $\left(\dfrac12\right)^0=1$. The function is strictly decreasing, so it is below the line to the right of that point and above it to the left. For instance, at $x=1$ the height is $\dfrac12$ and at $x=-1$ it is $2$. The answer is $x>0$, excluding the intersection.
