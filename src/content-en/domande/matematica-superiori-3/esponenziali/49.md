The graph shows $y=\left(\dfrac12\right)^x$ and the line $y=1$. At which $x$-coordinates is the curve below the line?

::grafico{f="0.5^x;1" xmin=-2 xmax=3 ymin=-0.2 ymax=4.5 punti="0,1" legenda="\left(\frac12\right)^x;1" titolo="Comparing the exponential with level one"}

- $x\geq0$
- $x<0$
- $x>0$
- $x\ne0$

## Explanation

The curve crosses $y=1$ at $x=0$, since a zero exponent gives $1$. As it is decreasing, it lies strictly below the line for $x>0$.
