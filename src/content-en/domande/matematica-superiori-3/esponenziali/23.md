A curve of the form $y=a^x$ goes through $(2,25)$, with $a>0$. What is the base?

- $-5$
- $\dfrac15$
- $25$
- $5$

## Explanation

The point $(2,25)$ means that squaring the base gives $25$, so $a^2=25$. The equation has roots $5$ and $-5$, but the model requires $a>0$. This leaves $a=5$, which indeed satisfies $5^2=25$. Its reciprocal $\dfrac15$ would give height $\dfrac1{25}$ at the same input and would miss the given point.
