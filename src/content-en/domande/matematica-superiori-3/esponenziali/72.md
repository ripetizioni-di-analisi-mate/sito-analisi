A value follows $N(t)=80\cdot2^t$ and is observed at nonnegative integer times. When does it first exceed $320$?

- At $t=4$.
- At $t=3$.
- At $t=2$.
- At $t=1$.

## Explanation

We have $N(2)=320$, so time $2$ reaches the threshold without exceeding it. The model is increasing and $N(3)=640$, so the first qualifying time is $t=3$.
