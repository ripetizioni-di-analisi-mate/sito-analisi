For which values of $k$ does $3^x=k$ have a real solution?

- $k\geq1$
- $k>0$
- $k\ne0$
- $k\geq0$

## Explanation

The power $3^x$ is strictly positive for every real exponent, so $k\leq0$ cannot be attained. For each $k>0$, the exponent $\log_3 k$ gives exactly $k$ and is unique by injectivity. For example, $k=\dfrac13$ is allowed and gives $x=-1$. Solutions therefore exist for values of $k$ between zero and one as well.
