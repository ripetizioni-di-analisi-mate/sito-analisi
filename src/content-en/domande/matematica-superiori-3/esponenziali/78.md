For $a>0$, which condition is equivalent to $a^3<a$?

- $a=1$
- For every $a>0$.
- $a>1$
- $0<a<1$

## Explanation

Since $a>0$, divide by $a$ without reversing the inequality to get $a^2<1$. Squaring is increasing on positive values, so this is equivalent to $a<1$. The complete condition is $0<a<1$. At $a=1$ there is equality, while at $a=\dfrac12$ its cube is $\dfrac18$, below $\dfrac12$. The negative root of $a^2=1$ does not bound the domain because the base is positive.
