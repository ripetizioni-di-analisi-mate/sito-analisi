Measurements give $10$, $15$ and $22{,}5$ units at times $t=0,1,2$. Which model reproduces the three values?

- $N(t)=15(1{,}5)^t$
- $N(t)=10(0{,}5)^t$
- $N(t)=10(1{,}5)^t$
- $N(t)=10+5t$

## Explanation

The consecutive ratios are $\dfrac{15}{10}=1{,}5$ and $\dfrac{22{,}5}{15}=1{,}5$. The factor is constant, so with value $10$ at time zero the model is $N(t)=10(1{,}5)^t$. The consecutive differences are $5$ and $7{,}5$. The data therefore do not follow linear growth with a fixed increase. With a fixed rate, every new value is $50\%$ larger than its predecessor.
