Measurements give $10$, $15$ and $22{,}5$ units at times $t=0,1,2$. Which model reproduces the three values?

- $N(t)=15(1{,}5)^t$
- $N(t)=10(0{,}5)^t$
- $N(t)=10(1{,}5)^t$
- $N(t)=10+5t$

## Explanation

The ratio of consecutive values is $1{,}5$ in both cases, so the factor is constant. The initial value is $10$, giving $N(t)=10(1{,}5)^t$.
