A price first increases by $25\%$ and then decreases by $20\%$. How does the final price compare with the initial price?

- It is $5\%$ lower.
- It is $45\%$ of the initial price.
- It equals the initial price.
- It is $5\%$ higher.

## Explanation

The factors are $1{,}25$ and $0{,}8$. Their product is $1{,}25\cdot0{,}8=1$, so the final price equals the initial price.
