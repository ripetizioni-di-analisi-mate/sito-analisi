A price first increases by $25\%$ and then decreases by $20\%$. How does the final price compare with the initial price?

- It is $5\%$ lower.
- It is $45\%$ of the initial price.
- It equals the initial price.
- It is $5\%$ higher.

## Explanation

The increase multiplies the price by $1{,}25$ and the decrease multiplies the new price by $0{,}80$. The overall factor is $1{,}25\cdot0{,}80=1$, so the original price is recovered. For example, $100$ rises to $125$, and $20\%$ of $125$ is $25$. The second percentage is therefore based on the increased price.
