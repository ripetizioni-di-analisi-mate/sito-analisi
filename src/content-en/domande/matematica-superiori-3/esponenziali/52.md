At what height does the lowest horizontal line meeting the curve $y=4^x-4\cdot2^x+5$ touch it at a single point?

- $y=1$
- $y=2$
- $y=5$
- $y=0$

## Explanation

Completing the square in the variable $2^x$ gives $y=(2^x-2)^2+1$. Thus $y\geq1$, and equality occurs only when $2^x=2$, at $x=1$. The line at height $1$ contains just that point of the curve.
