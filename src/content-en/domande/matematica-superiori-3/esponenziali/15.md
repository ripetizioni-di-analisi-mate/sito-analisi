Evaluate $32^{-\frac25}$.

- $\dfrac14$
- $-\dfrac14$
- $4$
- $\dfrac1{16}$

## Explanation

Recognise $32=2^5$. Raising $2^5$ to $-\dfrac25$ multiplies the exponents, giving $5\cdot\left(-\dfrac25\right)=-2$. Hence $32^{-\frac25}=2^{-2}=\dfrac1{2^2}=\dfrac14$. The calculation can also be read in terms of roots. The fifth root of $32$ is $2$, squaring gives $4$, and the negative sign says to take the reciprocal. The result is positive despite the negative exponent, since the reciprocal of $4$ is positive.
