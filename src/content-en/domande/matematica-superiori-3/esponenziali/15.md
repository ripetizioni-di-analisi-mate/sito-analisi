A spreadsheet divides $32^{-2}$ by $5$ to obtain $32^{-\frac25}$. Which sequence of operations correctly evaluates the power?

- Take the fifth root, square the result and take its reciprocal.
- Divide $32$ by $5$ and square the reciprocal.
- Take the square root, raise the result to the fifth power and take its reciprocal.
- Take the fifth root, square the result and change its sign.

## Explanation

The denominator of the exponent gives the root index, so $\sqrt[5]{32}=2$. The numerator $2$ requires squaring, which gives $4$. The negative sign in the exponent requires the reciprocal and leads to $\dfrac14$.
