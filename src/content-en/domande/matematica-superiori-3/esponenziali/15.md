Which sequence of operations correctly evaluates the power $32^{-\frac{2}{5}}$?

- Take the fifth root, square the result and take its reciprocal.
- Divide $32$ by $5$ and square the reciprocal.
- Take the square root, raise the result to the fifth power and take its reciprocal.
- Take the fifth root, square the result and change its sign.

## Explanation

The denominator $5$ of the exponent is the root index, so $\sqrt[5]{32}=2$. The numerator $2$ is the power to which the result is raised, which gives $2^2=4$. Since the exponent is negative, the reciprocal is taken last, and therefore $32^{-\frac{2}{5}}=\dfrac14$.
