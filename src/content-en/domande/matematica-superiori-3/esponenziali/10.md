Solve $2^{x+2}-2^x=24$.

- $\{2\}$
- $\{3\}$
- $\{4\}$
- $\{8\}$

## Explanation

Write $2^{x+2}=2^x\cdot2^2=4\cdot2^x$. The difference becomes $(4-1)2^x=3\cdot2^x$. The equation therefore requires $2^x=\dfrac{24}{3}=8=2^3$, so $x=3$ by injectivity. At that value the original terms are $2^5=32$ and $2^3=8$, whose difference is $24$. Factoring works because the two terms differ only by a factor of $4$. It separates the one exponential term from its numerical coefficient.
