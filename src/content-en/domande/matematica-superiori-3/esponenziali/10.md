In $2^{x+2}-2^x=24$ a classmate subtracts the exponents and obtains $2^2=24$. What value of $x$ follows if you factor out $2^x$?

- $x=4$
- $x=3$
- $x=8$
- $x=2$

## Explanation

Write $2^{x+2}=4\cdot2^x$, so the difference becomes $(4-1)2^x=3\cdot2^x$. Dividing $24$ by $3$ leaves $2^x=8=2^3$. The power function with base $2$ is one-to-one, giving $x=3$.
