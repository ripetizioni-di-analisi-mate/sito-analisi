Suppose $0<a<1$ and $r<s$. How do $a^r$ and $a^s$ compare?

- The comparison depends on the sign of $r$.
- $a^r<a^s$
- $a^r>a^s$
- $a^r=a^s$

## Explanation

For a base between zero and one the exponential function is strictly decreasing, so the smaller exponent gives the larger value.
