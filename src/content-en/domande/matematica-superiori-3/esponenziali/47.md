Suppose $0<a<1$ and $r<s$. How do $a^r$ and $a^s$ compare?

- The comparison depends on the sign of $r$.
- $a^r<a^s$
- $a^r>a^s$
- $a^r=a^s$

## Explanation

From $r<s$ we obtain $s-r>0$. With $0<a<1$, the power $a^{s-r}$ is below $1$. Now $a^s=a^r a^{s-r}$ and $a^r$ is positive, so $a^s<a^r$. The comparison depends on the difference between the exponents. For example, with $a=\dfrac12$, exponents $-2$ and $-1$ give $4$ and $2$, respectively. The reasoning also applies to positive exponents, because it depends only on the positive difference $s-r$.
