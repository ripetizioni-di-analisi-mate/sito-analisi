A sequence at integer times is $1,-2,4,-8,\ldots$ A real curve $y=a^x$ is proposed for all times, including half integers, matching those values. What obstacle already occurs at $x=\dfrac12$?

- The value at $0$ requires $a=0$, which has no reciprocal.
- At $x=\dfrac12$ the model must have an integer output.
- The value at $1$ requires $a=-2$, which has no real square root.
- The base would have to change between $1$ and $2$.

## Explanation

At $x=1$ the data give $a^1=-2$, fixing the base. The integer powers of $-2$ do produce the sequence. At time $\dfrac12$, however, a real number whose square is $-2$ would be needed, and there is no such number.
