In a model $N(t)=N_0q^t$, with $q>0$, suppose $N(0)=36$ and $N(3)=972$. What is $q$?

- $27$
- $9$
- $\dfrac13$
- $3$

## Explanation

Since $q^0=1$, the data $N(0)=36$ give $N_0=36$. After three periods $36q^3=972$, so $q^3=\dfrac{972}{36}=27$. Taking the cube root gives $q=3$. The check is $36\cdot3\cdot3\cdot3=972$. The number $27$ is the overall factor across three periods, while $3$ is the factor for one period. The ratio is positive and consistent with growth, since the value after three periods exceeds the initial value.
