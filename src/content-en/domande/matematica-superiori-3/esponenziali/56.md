Solve $2^x+2^{-x}=2$.

- $\{0\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\{1\}$
- $\{-1,1\}$

## Explanation

Let $u=2^x>0$. Then $2^{-x}=\dfrac1u$ and the equation becomes $u+\dfrac1u=2$. Multiplication by $u$ is valid because $u$ is positive. We get $u^2-2u+1=(u-1)^2=0$, hence $u=1$. This requires $x=0$, which gives two terms equal to $1$. This is the only value where the reciprocal terms agree. Any value other than $1$ leaves a positive square after multiplication by $u$.
