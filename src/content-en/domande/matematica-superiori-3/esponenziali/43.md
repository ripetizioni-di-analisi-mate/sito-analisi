Let $0<q<1$. Given that $q^r=8$ and $q^s=2$, what is $q^{\frac{r+s}{2}}$?

- $5$
- $16$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The required value is positive. Its square is $q^{r+s}=q^r q^s=16$, so its value is $4$. The midpoint of the exponents gives the geometric mean of the two outputs.
