Solve $\left(\dfrac14\right)^x<\dfrac12$.

- $\left(-\infty,\dfrac12\right)$
- $\left[\dfrac12,+\infty\right)$
- $(2,+\infty)$
- $\left(\dfrac12,+\infty\right)$

## Explanation

The threshold $\dfrac12$ is $\left(\dfrac14\right)^{\frac12}$. For this decreasing exponential, being below the threshold is equivalent to $x>\dfrac12$.
