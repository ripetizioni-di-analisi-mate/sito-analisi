With $u=2^x$, the equation $4^x-8\cdot2^x=0$ becomes $u(u-8)=0$. Which set of exponents is obtained on returning to $x$?

- $\{0,3\}$
- $\{0,8\}$
- $\{8\}$
- $\{3\}$

## Explanation

The substitution produces only values $u>0$, so the factor $u$ cannot vanish. The second factor gives $2^x=8=2^3$. Zero is a root of the polynomial in $u$, but no real exponent produces it.
