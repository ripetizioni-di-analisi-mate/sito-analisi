Solve $\left(\dfrac13\right)^{x^2}\leq\dfrac1{81}$.

- $(-\infty,-2]\cup[2,+\infty)$
- $[-2,2]$
- $[2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Write $\dfrac1{81}$ as $\left(\dfrac13\right)^4$. Since the base is less than $1$, the power on the left is at most the threshold when the exponent is at least $4$. We need $x^2\geq4$, so $x\leq-2$ or $x\geq2$. Both endpoints give equality. The value $0$ is excluded because it gives power $1$.
