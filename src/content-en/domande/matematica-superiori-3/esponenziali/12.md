Do the solutions of $\left(\dfrac13\right)^{x^2}\leq\dfrac1{81}$ lie inside or outside the interval between $-2$ and $2$? Which set includes the correct boundary points?

- $(-\infty,-2]\cup[2,+\infty)$
- $[-2,2]$
- $[2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The right-hand side is $\left(\dfrac13\right)^4$. The power decreases as its exponent increases, so $x^2\geq4$ is required. This means $|x|\geq2$ and includes both boundary points, where equality holds.
