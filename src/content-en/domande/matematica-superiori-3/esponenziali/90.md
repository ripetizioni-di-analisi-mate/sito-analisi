At which point do the graphs of $2^x$ and $4^x$ meet?

- They meet at $(1,0)$.
- They meet at $(0,1)$.
- They meet at $(1,2)$.
- They have no point in common.

## Explanation

Writing $4^x=2^{2x}$, equality of the values implies $x=2x$. This gives $x=0$ and the common value is $1$.
