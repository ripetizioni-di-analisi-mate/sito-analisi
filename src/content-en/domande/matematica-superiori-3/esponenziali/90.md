If $f(x)=a^x$ with $a>0$, which pair of values could belong to the same function at $x=-1$ and $x=1$, in that order?

- $\dfrac13,\ 2$
- $\dfrac23,\ \dfrac32$
- $2,\ 2$
- $-\dfrac12,\ -2$

## Explanation

The values at $-1$ and $1$ are $\dfrac1a$ and $a$, both positive and with product $1$. The pair $\dfrac23,\dfrac32$ meets both requirements and corresponds to the base $\dfrac32$. Two negative numbers can also have product $1$, but a power with positive base stays positive.
