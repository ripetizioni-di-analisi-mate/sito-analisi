At which point do the graphs of $2^x$ and $4^x$ meet?

- They meet at $(1,0)$.
- They meet at $(0,1)$.
- They meet at $(1,2)$.
- They have no point in common.

## Explanation

At an intersection the functions have the same input and the same output. Set $2^x=4^x=2^{2x}$. Injectivity of the base-$2$ exponential gives $x=2x$, hence $x=0$. Substitution gives height $2^0=1$, so the point is $(0,1)$. The linear equation for the exponents excludes other intersections. At $x=1$, for example, the heights are $2$ and $4$, so they differ.
