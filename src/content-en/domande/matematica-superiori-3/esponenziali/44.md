Solve $\left(\dfrac12\right)^{2-x}\leq1$.

- $\mathbb R$
- $(-\infty,2]$
- $[2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,2)$

## Explanation

Write $1=\left(\dfrac12\right)^0$. With base $\dfrac12$, heights at most $1$ correspond to exponents at least zero. Thus $2-x\geq0$, giving $x\leq2$. At $x=2$ the exponent vanishes. For $x>2$ it becomes negative and the power is the reciprocal of a value below $1$. The other side can be checked at $x=1$, where the height is $\dfrac12$ and the inequality holds.
