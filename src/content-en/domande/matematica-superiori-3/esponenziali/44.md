A chain of comparisons gives $\left(\dfrac12\right)^u<\left(\dfrac12\right)^v$ and $\left(\dfrac12\right)^v\leq\left(\dfrac12\right)^w$. Which ordering of the exponents is guaranteed?

- $u<v\leq w$
- $u>v\geq w$
- $u\geq v>w$
- $u>v>w$

## Explanation

A base between $0$ and $1$ reverses the direction of each comparison when passing to the exponents. The strict inequality requires $u>v$. The second comparison includes equality, so it allows $v=w$ and gives $v\geq w$.
