Solve $e^{x+1}>0$.

- $\varnothing$
- $(-1,+\infty)$
- $(0,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$

## Explanation

The exponent $x+1$ is defined for all real numbers. The base-$e$ exponential has only positive values. For instance, at $x=-2$ the exponent is $-1$ and the power is $\dfrac1e>0$. At $x=-1$ the exponent is zero and the power is $1$. The inequality therefore holds throughout $\mathbb R$.
