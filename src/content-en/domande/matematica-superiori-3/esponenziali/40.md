A table gives $f(0)=2$, $f(1)=6$ and $f(2)=15$. What prevents a single model $f(x)=C\,a^x$ from fitting all three values?

- The initial value differs from $1$.
- All three values are positive.
- The first input is zero.
- The ratios $6/2$ and $15/6$ are different.

## Explanation

In an exponential of the stated form every unit step multiplies the value by the same $a$. The first ratio is $3$ and the second is $\dfrac52$. With $C=2$ and $a=3$, the third value would therefore have to be $18$.
