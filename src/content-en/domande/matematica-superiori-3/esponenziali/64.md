Which line is the asymptote of $g(x)=2+3\cdot4^{-x}$?

- $y=5$
- $y=3$
- $y=2$
- $x=2$

## Explanation

The term $4^{-x}=\left(\dfrac14\right)^x$ approaches zero as $x$ increases without bound. Multiplying by $3$ changes its distance from zero, but its value still approaches zero. The constant term $2$ remains, so the asymptote is $y=2$. The value $5$ is the height at $x=0$, before the exponential term decreases. For example, at $x=1$ the distance from the asymptote drops to $\dfrac34$.
