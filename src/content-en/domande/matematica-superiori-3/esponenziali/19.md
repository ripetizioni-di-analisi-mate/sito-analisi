Evaluate $\left(\dfrac49\right)^{-\frac12}$.

- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac94$
- $-\dfrac32$

## Explanation

The square root of $\dfrac49$ is $\dfrac23$, since $2^2=4$ and $3^2=9$. The exponent $-\dfrac12$ gives the reciprocal of the root, so the result is $\dfrac32$. Taking the reciprocal first also gives $\sqrt{\dfrac94}=\dfrac32$. As a check, the base is below $1$, so a negative exponent produces a value above $1$.
