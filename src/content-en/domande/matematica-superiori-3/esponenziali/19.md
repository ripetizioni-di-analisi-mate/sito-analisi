To rewrite a power, someone proposes the identity $(a+b)^x=a^x+b^x$ for all $a,b>0$ and all real $x$. Which choice provides a counterexample?

- $a=2,\ b=5,\ x=1$
- $a=1,\ b=2,\ x=2$
- $a=1,\ b=2,\ x=1$
- $a=3,\ b=3,\ x=1$

## Explanation

With $a=1$, $b=2$ and $x=2$ we get $(1+2)^2=9$, while $1^2+2^2=5$. One admissible case with different results suffices to disprove the identity. When $x=1$, both sides always equal $a+b$.
