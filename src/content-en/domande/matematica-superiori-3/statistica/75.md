Group A has mean duration $12$ minutes and group B has mean duration $18$. The combined mean is $16$. How do the group sizes compare?

- Group A, with twice as many observations.
- The groups are the same size.
- Group B, with three times as many observations.
- Group B, with twice as many observations.

## Explanation

Writing the sizes as $n_A$ and $n_B$, the weighted mean gives $12n_A+18n_B=16(n_A+n_B)$. Thus $2n_B=4n_A$ and $n_B=2n_A$.
