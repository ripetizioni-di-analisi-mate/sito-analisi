Which point, added to $(1,2)$, $(2,3)$ and $(3,4)$, would most clearly conflict with their increasing relationship?

- $(8,0)$
- $(4,5)$
- $(0,1)$
- $(2,3)$

## Explanation

The original points follow $y=x+1$. The alternatives $(4,5)$, $(0,1)$ and $(2,3)$ lie on that line. The point $(8,0)$ has a high x-coordinate and a low y-coordinate compared with the observations.
