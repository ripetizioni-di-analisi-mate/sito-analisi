The data $x$ have mean $8$ and variance $5$. Define $y=x-8$. Which statement holds for $y$?

- The mean is $0$ and the variance is $0$.
- The mean is $0$ and the variance is $5$.
- The mean is $8$ and the variance is $5$.
- The mean is $0$ and the variance is $25$.

## Explanation

Subtracting $8$ takes the mean to $0$. The scale factor is $1$, so the variance remains $5$.
