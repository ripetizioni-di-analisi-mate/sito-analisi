Two series have the same mean, with standard deviations of $2$ and $5$. What is the ratio of the second variance to the first?

- $\dfrac52$
- $\dfrac{25}{4}$
- $\dfrac94$
- $\dfrac4{25}$

## Explanation

The variances are the squares of $2$ and $5$, so the requested ratio is $\dfrac{5^2}{2^2}=\dfrac{25}{4}$.
