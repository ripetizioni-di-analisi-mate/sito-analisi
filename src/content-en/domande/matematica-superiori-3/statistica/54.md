The observed pairs are $(-1,2)$, $(0,0)$ and $(1,2)$. Why is covariance zero?

- The contributions at opposite x-coordinates cancel.
- All the y-coordinates are equal.
- Both variances are zero.
- The three points lie on a vertical line.

## Explanation

The mean x-coordinate is $0$. The outer points have horizontal deviations $-1$ and $1$ and the same vertical deviation. The middle point has zero horizontal deviation.
