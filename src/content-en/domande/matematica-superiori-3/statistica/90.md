At $x=1,2,3,4$, a line gives residuals $2,-2,-2,2$. Which observation is consistent with these data?

::grafico{xmin=-0.45 xmax=5 ymin=-3.4 ymax=3.65 nomi="x,residual" punti="1,2;2,-2;3,-2;4,2" segmenti="1,0,1,2;2,0,2,-2;3,0,3,-2;4,0,4,2" titolo="The horizontal axis marks residual zero."}

- The line overestimates at the extremes and underestimates in the middle.
- The line underestimates at the extremes and overestimates in the middle.
- The line passes through all four points.
- The residuals all have the same sign.

## Explanation

A positive residual means the observation lies above the prediction. This happens at $x=1$ and $x=4$, while the residuals at $x=2$ and $x=3$ are negative.
