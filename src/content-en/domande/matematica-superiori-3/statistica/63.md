Using the line $\hat y=-2x+14$, predict $y$ at $x=3$. What is the prediction?

- $6$
- $11$
- $20$
- $8$

## Explanation

Substituting $x=3$ gives $-2\cdot3+14=-6+14=8$.

The value $14$ is the intercept, the prediction at input zero. The slope $-2$ subtracts two units for every unit increase in the input. After three units the change is therefore $-6$. The result is the line's prediction and leaves the actual measurement, if there is one, unknown.
