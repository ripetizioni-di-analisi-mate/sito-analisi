Out of $30$ measurements, $9$ are at most $2$ and $21$ are at most $5$. How many are greater than $2$ and at most $5$?

- $9$
- $12$
- $21$
- $30$

## Explanation

The $21$ measurements at most $5$ include the $9$ measurements at most $2$. The difference $21-9=12$ counts the observations in the required interval.
