Out of $30$ measurements, $9$ are at most $2$ and $21$ are at most $5$. How many are greater than $2$ and at most $5$?

- $9$
- $12$
- $21$
- $30$

## Explanation

The $21$ measurements at most $5$ include the $9$ measurements at most $2$. The difference $21-9=12$ counts the observations in the required interval.

The first cumulative count includes values equal to $2$, so those are removed. The value $5$ belongs to the second cumulative count and remains. The counts outside the interval are $9$ to the left and $30-21=9$ to the right, giving the same check $30-9-9=12$.
