In $2,4,4,7,9$, the value $9$ is replaced by $30$. Which pair of outcomes is correct?

- Both median and mean increase.
- The median increases and the mean stays the same.
- The median remains $4$ and the mean increases.
- Both median and mean stay the same.

## Explanation

The third observation in order is still $4$. The sum increases by $30-9=21$ with the same number of observations. The mean increases, while the median remains $4$.
