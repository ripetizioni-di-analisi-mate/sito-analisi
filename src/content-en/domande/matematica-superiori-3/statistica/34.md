A survey counts $8$ responses for bus, $8$ for train and $4$ for car. What are the modes?

- Bus and train.
- Bus only.
- Train only.
- Car.

## Explanation

The highest frequency is $8$. Two categories reach it, so the distribution has two modes.

A mode is a value or category of greatest frequency, so it is defined for categorical variables such as means of transport. The two counts of $8$ match and exceed $4$. No ordering between bus and train enters into deciding the modes.
