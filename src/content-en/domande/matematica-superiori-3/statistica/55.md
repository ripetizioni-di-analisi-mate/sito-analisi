The pairs $(1,3)$, $(1,5)$ and $(1,7)$ are known. Is the linear correlation coefficient defined?

- It is zero because the x-coordinates are equal.
- It is $1$ because the points are collinear.
- It is $-1$ because the line is vertical.
- It is undefined because the standard deviation of $x$ is zero.

## Explanation

The denominator is $\sigma_x\sigma_y$. The x-coordinates are constant, so $\sigma_x=0$ and the ratio is undefined.
