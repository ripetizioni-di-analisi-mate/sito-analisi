In which data set is the mean equal to the median?

- $2,5,9$
- $2,3,9$
- $2,5,8$
- $3,4,8$

## Explanation

In $2,5,8$, both extremes are $3$ away from the middle value and their deviations cancel. The mean and median are both $5$. The distances differ in each remaining set.

For three ordered observations the median is the middle value. The mean equals it when the deviations of the largest and smallest values add to zero. Here $\dfrac{2+5+8}{3}=5$. Replacing $8$ with $9$, for example, would leave the median unchanged while raising the mean.
