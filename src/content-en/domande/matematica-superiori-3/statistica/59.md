Which quantity stays the same when the x- and y-coordinates of each pair are swapped?

- The mean of the first coordinate, always.
- Covariance.
- The variance of the first coordinate, always.
- The regression slope, always.

## Explanation

The product of the two deviations is commutative, so its value stays the same after the swap. Means, variances and slopes can change.
