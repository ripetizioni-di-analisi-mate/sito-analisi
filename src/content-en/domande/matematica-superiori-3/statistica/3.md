The scores $3$, $7$ and $9$ occur $5$, $1$ and $4$ times, respectively. What is the median?

- $M=3$
- $M=7$
- $M=9$
- $M=5$

## Explanation

The middle positions are the fifth and sixth. The value $3$ occupies the first five, while $7$ occupies the sixth. The median is $\dfrac{3+7}{2}=5$.

There are $5+1+4=10$ observations, so the midpoint lies between two observations. The cumulative count of $5$ at value $3$ identifies only the first middle position. The result $5$ can be the median even though no observation has that value.
