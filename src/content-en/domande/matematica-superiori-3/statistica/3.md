The scores $3$, $7$ and $9$ occur $5$, $1$ and $4$ times, respectively. What is the median?

- $M=3$
- $M=7$
- $M=9$
- $M=5$

## Explanation

The middle positions are the fifth and sixth. The value $3$ occupies the first five, while $7$ occupies the sixth. The median is $\dfrac{3+7}{2}=5$.
