A thermometer records temperatures with mean $15$ and standard deviation $2$. A correction adds $4$ to every reading. What are the corrected mean and standard deviation?

- $(19,6)$
- $(15,6)$
- $(15,2)$
- $(19,2)$

## Explanation

The mean increases by $4$. Each observation and the mean shift by the same amount, so deviations stay the same and the standard deviation remains $2$.
