Four temperatures have mean $21$. After adding $1$ to each measurement, by how much does their sum increase?

- $1$
- $21$
- $22$
- $4$

## Explanation

Each of the four observations adds $1$ to the sum. The total increase is therefore $4\cdot1=4$.

Before correction the sum is $4\cdot21=84$. The mean rises to $22$, giving a new sum of $4\cdot22=88$. The difference $88-84=4$ applies to the total. The value $1$ is the increase in each individual reading and also the increase in the mean.
