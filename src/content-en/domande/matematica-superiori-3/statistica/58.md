A covariance of $2$ is recalculated after multiplying every x-coordinate by $3$ and adding $10$ to every y-coordinate. What is the new covariance?

- $2$
- $12$
- $6$
- $18$

## Explanation

Horizontal deviations triple, while vertical deviations stay the same after the shift. Every product triples and the new covariance is $3\cdot2=6$.
