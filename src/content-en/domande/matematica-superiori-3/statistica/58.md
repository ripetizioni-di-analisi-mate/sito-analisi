A covariance of $2$ is recalculated after multiplying every x-coordinate by $3$ and adding $10$ to every y-coordinate. What is the new covariance?

- $2$
- $12$
- $6$
- $18$

## Explanation

Horizontal deviations triple, while vertical deviations stay the same after the shift. Every product triples and the new covariance is $3\cdot2=6$.

For the new x-coordinates we have $3x_i-3\bar x=3(x_i-\bar x)$. For the new y-coordinates the term $10$ cancels because it is also added to the mean. Since the number of pairs stays the same, multiplying every product of deviations by $3$ multiplies their mean by the same factor.
