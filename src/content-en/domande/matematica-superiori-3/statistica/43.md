The values $2$ and $8$ each occur twice. What is their descriptive variance?

- $3$
- $12$
- $18$
- $9$

## Explanation

The mean is $5$. Every observation is $3$ units from the mean, so all four squared deviations are $9$ and their mean is $9$.

To find the mean, weight each value by its frequency and divide by four. The two $2$s give deviation $-3$ and the two $8$s give deviation $3$. The opposite signs would cancel in a direct sum of deviations. Squaring instead gives numerator $4\cdot9=36$, to be divided by $4$.
