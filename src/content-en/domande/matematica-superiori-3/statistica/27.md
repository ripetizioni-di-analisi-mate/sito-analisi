A group of $8$ people has mean waiting time $15$ minutes, and a group of $12$ has mean $10$ minutes. What is the combined mean?

- $11\,\mathrm{min}$
- $12\,\mathrm{min}$
- $12{,}5\,\mathrm{min}$
- $13\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

The totals are $8\cdot15=120$ and $12\cdot10=120$ minutes. There are $20$ observations, so the mean is $\dfrac{240}{20}=12$ minutes.

The two total waiting times coincide even though the group means differ. The second group contains more people, so the combined mean must be closer to $10$ than to $15$, as it is for $12$. The value $12{,}5$ would weight the two groups equally.
