Two observations are in $[0,4)$ and two in $[4,8)$. What can be concluded about the mean using the class midpoints?

- The exact mean is $4$.
- The estimate is $8$.
- The estimate is $4$, while the exact mean depends on the four observations.
- The exact mean is always above $4$.

## Explanation

The midpoints are $2$ and $6$, both with weight $2$. The estimate is $\dfrac{2\cdot2+2\cdot6}{4}=4$. The exact mean requires the sum of the actual observations.
