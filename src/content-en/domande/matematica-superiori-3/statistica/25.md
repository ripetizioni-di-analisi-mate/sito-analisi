The duration classes are $[0,5)$, $[5,10)$ and $[10,15)$. Which interval contains $10$ minutes?

- $[10,15)$
- $[0,5)$
- $[5,10)$
- Both $[5,10)$ and $[10,15)$.

## Explanation

The left endpoint is included and the right is excluded. The value $10$ is the left endpoint of $[10,15)$.
