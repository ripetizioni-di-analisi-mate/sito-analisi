The duration classes are $[0,5)$, $[5,10)$ and $[10,15)$. Which interval contains $10$ minutes?

- $[10,15)$
- $[0,5)$
- $[5,10)$
- Both $[5,10)$ and $[10,15)$.

## Explanation

The left endpoint is included and the right is excluded. The value $10$ is the left endpoint of $[10,15)$.

To belong to $[5,10)$ a time must satisfy $5\le t<10$. At $t=10$ the second condition is false. The next interval requires $10\le t<15$, which the threshold satisfies. This way a measurement at the common boundary is counted exactly once.
