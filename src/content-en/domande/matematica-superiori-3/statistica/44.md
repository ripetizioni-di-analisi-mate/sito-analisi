A data set has mean $5$ and descriptive variance $4$. What is the mean of the squared observations?

- $9$
- $21$
- $29$
- $25$

## Explanation

Add the square of the mean to the variance. This gives $4+5^2=29$.

In the variance formula, the mean of squares appears together with the square of the mean. To isolate the first quantity, move the term $-25$ to the other side of the equation. The result must be at least $25$, since otherwise subtracting the square of the mean would give a negative variance.
