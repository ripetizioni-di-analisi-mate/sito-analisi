The class $[0,5)$ contains $10$ of $50$ measurements. In a histogram with total area $1$, what is its height?

- $0{,}2$
- $0{,}1$
- $0{,}04$
- $2$

## Explanation

The area represents the proportion $\dfrac{10}{50}=0{,}2$. Dividing it by the width $5$ gives a height of $0{,}04$.
