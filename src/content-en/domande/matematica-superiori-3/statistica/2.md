Four journeys have a mean duration of $14\,\mathrm{min}$. The first three last $12$, $13$ and $15$ minutes. How long does the fourth take? Give the duration in minutes.

- $t_4=16$
- $t_4=14$
- $t_4=15$
- $t_4=18$

## Explanation

Four times with mean $14$ sum to $4\cdot14=56$ minutes. The first three add up to $40$ minutes, so the fourth lasts $56-40=16$ minutes.
