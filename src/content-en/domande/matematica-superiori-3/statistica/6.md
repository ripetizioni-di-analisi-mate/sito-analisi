What is the descriptive covariance of $(1,4)$, $(2,2)$ and $(3,0)$?

- $\operatorname{cov}(x,y)=\dfrac43$
- $\operatorname{cov}(x,y)=-\dfrac23$
- $\operatorname{cov}(x,y)=-2$
- $\operatorname{cov}(x,y)=-\dfrac43$

## Explanation

Both means equal $2$. The products of the deviations are $(-1)\cdot2=-2$, $0$ and $1\cdot(-2)=-2$. Dividing their sum by $3$ gives $-\dfrac43$.
