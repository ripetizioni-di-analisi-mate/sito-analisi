What is the descriptive covariance of $(1,4)$, $(2,2)$ and $(3,0)$?

- $\operatorname{cov}(x,y)=\dfrac43$
- $\operatorname{cov}(x,y)=-\dfrac23$
- $\operatorname{cov}(x,y)=-2$
- $\operatorname{cov}(x,y)=-\dfrac43$

## Explanation

Both means equal $2$. The products of the deviations are $(-1)\cdot2=-2$, $0$ and $1\cdot(-2)=-2$. Dividing their sum by $3$ gives $-\dfrac43$.

The products use deviations of the two coordinates because the data come in pairs. Both outer points have deviations of opposite signs. The middle point matches the point of means and contributes zero to the numerator, but it still counts in the three pairs used as the divisor.
