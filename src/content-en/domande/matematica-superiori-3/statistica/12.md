The regression line is $\hat y=2x+3$. What is the residual $y-\hat y$ for the observed pair $(4,9)$?

- $y-\hat y=-2$
- $y-\hat y=2$
- $y-\hat y=9$
- $y-\hat y=11$

## Explanation

At $x=4$, the prediction is $2\cdot4+3=11$. The observation is $9$, so the residual is $9-11=-2$ and the point lies below the line.

The first coordinate identifies the prediction to compare with the observation. Subtract the value on the line from the measurement at the same input. The vertical distance between the points is $2$, but the **residual** retains the sign and shows that the line overestimates the observed value.
