The regression line is $\hat y=2x+3$. What is the residual $y-\hat y$ for the observed pair $(4,9)$?

- $y-\hat y=-2$
- $y-\hat y=2$
- $y-\hat y=9$
- $y-\hat y=11$

## Explanation

At $x=4$, the prediction is $2\cdot4+3=11$. The observation is $9$, so the residual is $9-11=-2$ and the point lies below the line.
