Apply $y=ax+b$ to data with positive standard deviation. For which values of $a$ is the standard deviation of $y$ zero?

- Only $a=1$.
- Only $a=-1$.
- Only $a=0$.
- Every positive $a$.

## Explanation

We have $\sigma_y=|a|\sigma_x$. With $\sigma_x>0$, the product vanishes exactly when $a=0$, in which case every observation becomes $b$.
