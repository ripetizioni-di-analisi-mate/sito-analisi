Compare $14$ out of $20$ with $28$ out of $40$. Which statement is correct?

- The second percentage is twice the first.
- The percentages match because both numerator and denominator doubled.
- The first percentage is twice the second.
- The second percentage exceeds the first by $14$ points.

## Explanation

The fractions are $\dfrac{14}{20}=\dfrac7{10}$ and $\dfrac{28}{40}=\dfrac7{10}$. Both correspond to $70\,\%$.
