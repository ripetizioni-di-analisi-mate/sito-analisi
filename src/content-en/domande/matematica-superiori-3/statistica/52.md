A point has horizontal deviation $-3$ and vertical deviation $2$ from their respective means. What is its contribution to the sum used for covariance?

- $-6$
- $6$
- $-1$
- $-5$

## Explanation

The sum uses products of deviations. Here the signs are opposite, giving $(-3)\cdot2=-6$.

The horizontal deviation places the point to the left of the mean of $x$, while the vertical one places it above the mean of $y$. These opposite signs give a negative contribution. The value $-1$ would be the sum of the deviations, while the covariance numerator uses one product for each pair.
