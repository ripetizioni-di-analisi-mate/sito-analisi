The median of the four ordered observations $2,5,a,12$ is $7$. What is $a$?

- $7$
- $8$
- $10$
- $9$

## Explanation

The middle observations are $5$ and $a$. The equation $\dfrac{5+a}{2}=7$ gives $a=14-5=9$.

With four observations, average the second and third positions. For their midpoint to be $7$, the two values must lie at the same distance from $7$. The value $5$ lies $2$ to the left, so $a=9$ lies $2$ to the right and satisfies the given order.
