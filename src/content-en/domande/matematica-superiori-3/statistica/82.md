The three deviations from the mean are $-4$, $1$ and $d$. What is $d$?

- $3$
- $-3$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

Deviations from the mean sum to zero. The equation $-4+1+d=0$ gives $d=3$.

The sum of deviations is the sum of the observations minus three times their mean. These two quantities agree by the definition of the mean. The first two deviations total $-3$, so the third must be positive and of equal magnitude to cancel them.
