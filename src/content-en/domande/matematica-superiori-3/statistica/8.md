In a table, the values $1$, $3$ and $6$ have frequencies $2$, $5$ and $3$. What is the mean of the observations?

- $\bar x=\dfrac{10}{3}$
- $\bar x=3$
- $\bar x=\dfrac72$
- $\bar x=5$

## Explanation

There are $10$ observations. The weighted sum is $2\cdot1+5\cdot3+3\cdot6=35$, so the mean is $\dfrac{35}{10}=\dfrac72$.

The rows of the table collect repeated observations. For instance, the value $3$ adds $15$ to the sum because it occurs five times. Averaging only the distinct values would give $\dfrac{10}{3}$ and weight each row equally. The stated mean weights each observation equally.
