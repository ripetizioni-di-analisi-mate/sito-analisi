On a cumulative plot, the count is $7$ at both thresholds, $x=2$ and $x=3$. How many observations lie in $(2,3]$?

- $1$
- $0$
- $7$
- $14$

## Explanation

The number in $(2,3]$ is the difference between the cumulative counts at those thresholds. It equals $7-7=0$.

The first cumulative value already includes observations equal to $2$. The second would add those above $2$ and at most $3$. The plot stays at the same level between the thresholds, so the added group is empty. All seven observations already counted lie at or below the first threshold.
