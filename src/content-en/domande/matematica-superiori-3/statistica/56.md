Three pairs with distinct x-coordinates satisfy $y=5-2x$ exactly. What is their correlation?

- $0$
- $-1$
- $1$
- $-\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Deviations in $y$ are $-2$ times those in $x$, so covariance is $-2\sigma_x^2$ and $\sigma_y=2\sigma_x$. Their ratio is $-1$.

The x-coordinates are distinct, so $\sigma_x>0$ and the ratio is defined. The mean of $y$ is $5-2\bar x$, so subtracting the respective means gives $y_i-\bar y=-2(x_i-\bar x)$. The magnitude $2$ cancels in the ratio, leaving the negative sign of the slope.
