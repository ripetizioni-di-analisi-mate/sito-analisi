The correlation between $x$ and $y$ is $-\dfrac34$. What is the correlation between $x$ and $-2y+5$?

- $r=-\dfrac34$
- $r=\dfrac38$
- $r=\dfrac34$
- $r=0$

## Explanation

The transformation multiplies the deviations of $y$ by $-2$, so covariance is multiplied by $-2$. The new standard deviation of $y$ is multiplied by $2$. The coefficient therefore changes sign and equals $\dfrac34$.

Adding $5$ shifts the values and their mean by the same amount and cancels in the deviations. The negative factor instead reverses the signs of the deviations in $y$. The two factors of magnitude $2$ cancel in the ratio, leaving the opposite of the original coefficient.
