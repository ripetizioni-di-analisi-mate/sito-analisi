A class has $25$ students, of whom $36\,\%$ chose the lab. How many chose it?

- $4$
- $9$
- $11$
- $16$

## Explanation

The count is $\dfrac{36}{100}$ of the total of $25$. This gives $\dfrac{36\cdot25}{100}=9$.

The $36\,\%$ gives the count per hundred students at the same proportion. The class has $25$ students, a quarter of one hundred, so the requested count is one quarter of $36$. Checking in the reverse direction gives $\dfrac9{25}=\dfrac{36}{100}$.
