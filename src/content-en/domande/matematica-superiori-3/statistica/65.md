A point lies on the fitted line $\hat y=2x+3$ at x-coordinate $1$. What is its residual?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $5$

## Explanation

The prediction is $2\cdot1+3=5$. Because the point lies on the line, its y-coordinate is $5$ and its residual is $5-5=0$.

At the same input the observed and predicted y-coordinates coincide because the point is on the line. The residual measures their difference. The slope $2$ instead describes a change as the input changes, and the y-coordinate $5$ locates the point. Neither number measures the deviation.
