The three observations $4,4,4$ have mean $4$. What is their descriptive standard deviation?

- $\dfrac43$
- $0$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Every deviation $4-4$ is zero. The mean of the squares and its square root are therefore both zero.

The number $4$ is the common location of the data. Standard deviation describes spread around that location. Since no observation has positive distance from the mean, all three contributions to the numerator are zero. Moving all three values together would change their mean but would leave the spread at zero.
