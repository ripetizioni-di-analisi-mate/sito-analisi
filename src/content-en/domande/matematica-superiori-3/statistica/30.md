In a record, the values $2$ and $5$ occur $n$ and $4$ times, respectively. If the mean is $4$, what is $n$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The sum is $2n+20$ and there are $n+4$ observations. The equation $2n+20=4(n+4)$ gives $2n=4$, so $n=2$.

The value $2$ occurs $n$ times, so it contributes $2n$ to the numerator and $n$ to the denominator. The required mean must be closer to $5$ because the $5$s are more frequent. When $n=2$, six observations add up to $24$, confirming a mean of $4$.
