At school A, $18$ out of $30$ students choose the vegetarian menu. At school B, $20$ out of $40$ do so. By how many percentage points is the proportion higher at school A?

- $d=2$
- $d=10$
- $d=5$
- $d=20$

## Explanation

The two proportions are $\dfrac{18}{30}=60\,\%$ and $\dfrac{20}{40}=50\,\%$. Subtract the percentages to get a gap of $60-50=10$ percentage points.
