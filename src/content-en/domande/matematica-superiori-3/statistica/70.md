A model uses data collected between $5$ and $20$ degrees. Which request is an extrapolation?

- A prediction at $7$ degrees.
- A prediction at $25$ degrees.
- A prediction at $12$ degrees.
- A prediction at $18$ degrees.

## Explanation

Only $25$ lies outside $[5,20]$. Evaluating the line there extends the relationship beyond the observed maximum.
