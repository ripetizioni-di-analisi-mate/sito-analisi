Five values have mean $8$ and one is $14$. What mean results from removing that value?

- $5{,}2$
- $7$
- $10$
- $6{,}5$

## Explanation

The original sum is $5\cdot8=40$. Removing $14$ leaves $26$ across four values, so the mean is $\dfrac{26}{4}=6{,}5$.

Both the sum and the number of terms change here. The value $5{,}2$ would come from dividing $26$ by $5$, as if the observation had been replaced by zero. Removing a value above the original mean must reduce the mean, as the change from $8$ to $6{,}5$ shows.
