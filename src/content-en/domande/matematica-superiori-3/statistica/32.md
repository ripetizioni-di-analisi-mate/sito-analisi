A $3$-credit exam has grade $24$. A $9$-credit exam has grade $28$. What is the credit-weighted mean?

- $27$
- $25$
- $26$
- $28$

## Explanation

The weighted points are $3\cdot24+9\cdot28=324$. Dividing by $12$ credits gives $27$.

The nine credits give the second exam three times the weight of the first. For three of the twelve credits the grade lies four points below $28$. This lowers the mean by $\dfrac{4\cdot3}{12}=1$, in agreement with the result $27$. The unweighted average would give $26$.
