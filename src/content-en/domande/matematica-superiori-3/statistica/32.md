Thirty-two journey times are rounded to the nearest minute. What is the largest possible absolute difference between the mean of the rounded times and the mean of the exact times, allowing ties at half a minute?

- Half a minute
- $16$ minutes
- One minute
- $\dfrac{1}{64}$ of a minute

## Explanation

Each error has magnitude at most half a minute, so their mean obeys the same bound. It is attained if all times are half-minute ties rounded the same way.
