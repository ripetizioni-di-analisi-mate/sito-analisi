One exam is worth $3$ credits and has grade $24$. Another is worth $9$ credits and has grade $28$. What is the credit-weighted mean of the grades?

- $27$
- $25$
- $26$
- $28$

## Explanation

In the weighted mean each grade is multiplied by its number of credits and the sum is divided by the total number of credits. The weighted sum is $3\cdot24+9\cdot28=72+252=324$ and the credits are $3+9=12$. Therefore the weighted mean is
$$
\bar x=\frac{324}{12}=27 .
$$
The value $26$ is the simple mean of the two grades, which gives the same weight to both exams even though the second has three times the credits of the first.
