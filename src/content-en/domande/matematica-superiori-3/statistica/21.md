A bar chart shows $12$, $13$ and $14$ votes with its vertical axis starting at $11$. Which statement interprets the data correctly?

- The last value is three times the first.
- The first and last values differ by two votes.
- The first value is one vote.
- The middle value is half the last.

## Explanation

The visible parts of the first and last bars measure $12-11=1$ and $14-11=3$. The actual counts are $12$ and $14$, so their difference is $2$ votes.
