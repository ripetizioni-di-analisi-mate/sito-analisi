The data $y_1,\dots,y_n$ are obtained from the data $x_1,\dots,x_n$ by the transformation $y_i=-3x_i+4$. The standard deviation of the data $x_i$ is $2$. What is the standard deviation of the data $y_i$?

- $2$
- $10$
- $18$
- $6$

## Explanation

The mean of the transformed data is $\bar y=-3\bar x+4$, so each deviation from the mean becomes $y_i-\bar y=-3(x_i-\bar x)$. The term $4$ does not appear in the deviations, so it does not change the spread. The variance is therefore multiplied by $(-3)^2=9$ and the standard deviation by $|-3|=3$. It follows that the standard deviation of the data $y_i$ is $3\cdot2=6$.
