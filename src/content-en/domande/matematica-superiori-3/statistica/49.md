A sensor returns $y=-3x+4$. If the standard deviation of $x$ is $2$, what is that of $y$?

- $2$
- $10$
- $18$
- $6$

## Explanation

The distances from the mean are multiplied by $|-3|=3$. This gives $\sigma_y=3\cdot2=6$.

An observation's deviation from the new mean is $-3$ times its original deviation. The sign reflects the reversed order, while standard deviation uses nonnegative distances. A factor of $9$ applies to the variance. For standard deviation the factor is $3$, and the term $4$ cancels.
