Two regressions have covariance $4$. The x variances are $2$ in case A and $8$ in case B. What is the ratio $m_A:m_B$?

- $1:4$
- $2:1$
- $4:1$
- $1:1$

## Explanation

The slopes are $m_A=\dfrac42=2$ and $m_B=\dfrac48=\dfrac12$. Hence $\dfrac{m_A}{m_B}=4$.

At fixed covariance, slope is inversely proportional to the variance of the inputs. In case B the variance is four times that of A, so its slope is a quarter as large. The requested ratio puts A first and B second, giving $4:1$.
