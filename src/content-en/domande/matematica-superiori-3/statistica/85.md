A fitted model is $\hat y=3x+2$. Every observed y-coordinate increases by $5$, then the regression is fitted again. What is the new line?

- $\hat y=3x+7$
- $\hat y=8x+2$
- $\hat y=3x+2$
- $\hat y=15x+10$

## Explanation

Adding a constant to $y$ leaves its deviations from the mean unchanged, so covariance and slope stay the same. The mean y-coordinate rises by $5$, so the intercept becomes $2+5=7$.

Geometrically, every observed point moves up by the same amount. Translating the line up by $5$ as well preserves every vertical difference between a point and its prediction. The resulting line is $\hat y=3x+7$. Increasing the slope would instead change the deviations differently at different inputs.
