A model uses data with x-coordinates from $3$ to $11$, inclusive. Starting at x-coordinate $14$, what is the smallest decrease that brings the prediction within the observed range?

- $d=3$
- $d=1$
- $d=8$
- $d=11$

## Explanation

As the x-coordinate decreases from $14$, the first value in the interval it reaches is the upper endpoint $11$. The smallest decrease is $14-11=3$. Any smaller decrease would leave the x-coordinate outside the interval.
