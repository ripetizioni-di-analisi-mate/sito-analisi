Two adjacent intervals have widths $2$ and $6$ and absolute frequency densities $3$ and $1$. What density results from merging them?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $\dfrac32$

## Explanation

The frequencies are $2\cdot3=6$ and $6\cdot1=6$. The merged class contains $12$ observations over width $8$, so its density is $\dfrac{12}{8}=\dfrac32$.
