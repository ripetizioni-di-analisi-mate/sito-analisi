In warmer months, water consumption and fan sales rise together. Which observation explains the association without attributing a direct effect to one of the two variables?

- Buying a fan always increases water consumption.
- Both variables must have the same mean.
- Temperature may increase both values.
- Both variables have zero variance.

## Explanation

Temperature is a common factor. An increase can raise both quantities even without a direct effect between them.
