The variance of some length measurements is $9\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. What is the standard deviation?

- $3\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $81\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Standard deviation is the square root of variance. The square root of $9$ is $3$, and the square root of $\mathrm{cm}^2$ gives centimetres.

The square in the variance comes from squaring each deviation before averaging. The square root restores the unit of length. The result is positive because it measures the size of deviations. Checking in reverse, $(3\,\mathrm{cm})^2=9\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ matches the given value.
