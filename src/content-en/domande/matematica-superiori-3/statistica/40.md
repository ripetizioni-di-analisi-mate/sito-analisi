The variance of some length measurements is $9\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. What is the standard deviation?

- $3\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $81\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Standard deviation is the square root of variance. The square root of $9$ is $3$, and the square root of $\mathrm{cm}^2$ gives centimetres.
