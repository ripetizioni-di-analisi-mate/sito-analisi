Two histogram classes have absolute frequency densities $3$ and $2$ and widths $2$ and $4$, respectively. Which contains more observations?

::grafico{xmin=-0.55 xmax=7.1 ymin=-0.4 ymax=4.3 nomi="x,density" poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,3 0,3;2,0 6,0 6,2 2,2" titolo="The taller rectangle has the smaller width."}

- The second, with $8$ observations.
- The first, with $6$ observations.
- The second, with $4$ observations.
- They contain the same number.

## Explanation

The areas are $3\cdot2=6$ and $2\cdot4=8$. The second class is wider and has the larger area.
