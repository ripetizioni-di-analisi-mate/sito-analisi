Waiting times are $6,7,7,8,40$ minutes. Which measure describes the centre by position and is less sensitive to the largest value?

- The mean, equal to $13{,}6$ minutes.
- The median, equal to $7$ minutes.
- The range, equal to $34$ minutes.
- The maximum, equal to $40$ minutes.

## Explanation

The median is the third observation in order. Replacing $40$ with another time above $8$ leaves that middle observation at $7$.
