In four pairs, the products of deviations are $-6$, $0$, $2$ and $8$. What is the descriptive covariance?

- $0$
- $4$
- $1$
- $-1$

## Explanation

Covariance is the mean of the four signed products. Their sum is $4$, so the result is $\dfrac44=1$.

Keep the sign of each product. Here the positive contributions sum to $10$, the negative one subtracts $6$, and the zero product leaves the sum unchanged. The pair giving zero still counts in the divisor because we are averaging the contributions of all four observations.
