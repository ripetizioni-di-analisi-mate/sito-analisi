A regression has $\bar x=0$, $\bar y=4$ and $m=-3$. What does the line predict at the mean x-coordinate?

- $4$
- $-3$
- $0$
- $7$

## Explanation

The line passes through $(\bar x,\bar y)=(0,4)$. Its prediction at $x=\bar x$ is therefore $\bar y=4$.

The constant term can also be found from $q=\bar y-m\bar x$. Here the product with $\bar x=0$ vanishes, so the intercept equals the mean y-coordinate. The negative slope will affect predictions for inputs other than zero, but its term vanishes at the requested point.
