Two candidate lines have residuals $(-1,1,0)$ and $(0,-2,2)$. Which has the smaller sum of squared residuals?

- The second, with sum $2$.
- The first, with sum $0$.
- Both have sum $0$.
- The first, with sum $2$.

## Explanation

Add the squares. The first line gives $1+1+0=2$ and the second gives $0+4+4=8$.
