Two candidate lines have residuals $(-1,1,0)$ and $(0,-2,2)$. Which has the smaller sum of squared residuals?

- The second, with sum $2$.
- The first, with sum $0$.
- Both have sum $0$.
- The first, with sum $2$.

## Explanation

Add the squares. The first line gives $1+1+0=2$ and the second gives $0+4+4=8$.

Squaring removes the negative signs. The two nonzero deviations for the first line have magnitude one. Those for the second have magnitude two, so each contributes four times as much. The signed sums are both zero, but this tie disappears when we measure squared deviations instead.
