The three pairs are $(1,4)$, $(2,4)$ and $(3,4)$. Which line minimises the squared residuals of $y$ on $x$?

- $\hat y=2$
- $\hat y=x+2$
- $\hat y=4x$
- $\hat y=4$

## Explanation

Every observation has y-coordinate $4$. The horizontal line $\hat y=4$ gives only zero residuals, so the sum of squares reaches its minimum of $0$.
