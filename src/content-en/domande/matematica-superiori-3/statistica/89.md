The observed pairs are $(2,1)$, $(2,4)$ and $(2,7)$. Why is there no unique slope for the regression of $y$ on $x$?

- The variance of $y$ is zero.
- All pairs have equal y-coordinates.
- Any vertical line gives zero horizontal residuals and is therefore the solution.
- Every line through $(2,4)$ gives the same predictions at the observed x-coordinates.

## Explanation

All x-coordinates are $2$, so only the prediction at $x=2$ matters. The sum of squares is minimised by predicting the mean y-coordinate $4$. Lines with different slopes can all pass through $(2,4)$.
