A table gives $\sigma_x=2$ and $\sigma_y=4$. Which proposed covariance is incompatible with $|r|\le1$?

- $-8$
- $0$
- $10$
- $6$

## Explanation

The denominator of $r$ is $2\cdot4=8$. For $|r|\le1$, the absolute covariance must be at most $8$. Of the proposed values, only $10$ exceeds that limit.
