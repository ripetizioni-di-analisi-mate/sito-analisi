A table gives $\sigma_x=2$ and $\sigma_y=4$. Which proposed covariance is incompatible with $|r|\le1$?

- $-8$
- $0$
- $10$
- $6$

## Explanation

The denominator of $r$ is $2\cdot4=8$. For $|r|\le1$, the absolute covariance must be at most $8$. Of the proposed values, only $10$ exceeds that limit.

The value $-8$ satisfies the bound because it would give $r=-1$, of magnitude exactly one. A covariance of $10$ would instead give $r=\dfrac{10}{8}=\dfrac54$. The check uses absolute value, so a negative sign alone does not make a covariance incompatible.
