A table lists three relative frequencies, $0{,}18$, $0{,}42$ and $f$. If its categories cover all observations without overlap, what is $f$?

- $0{,}24$
- $0{,}58$
- $0{,}60$
- $0{,}40$

## Explanation

The three proportions add up to $1$. The first two add to $0{,}60$, so the third is $1-0{,}60=0{,}40$.
