Some measurements in metres have variance $0{,}04\,\mathrm{m}^2$. What is the variance in square centimetres?

- $4\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $400\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The conversion multiplies each numerical value by $100$ and each squared deviation by $100^2$. Thus $0{,}04\cdot100^2=400$.
