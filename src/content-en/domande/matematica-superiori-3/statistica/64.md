At $x=2$, the line predicts $5$ and the residual is $3$. What y-coordinate was observed?

- $2$
- $3$
- $8$
- $5$

## Explanation

The residual is $y-\hat y$. Thus $y=\hat y+e=5+3=8$.

A positive residual places the observation above the point on the line at the same input. Here the point on the line has y-coordinate $5$, and the measurement is three units higher. As a check, $8-5=3$ gives the stated residual. Subtracting $3$ from the prediction would correspond to a negative residual.
