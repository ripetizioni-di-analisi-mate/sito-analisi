A plot has x-coordinates in hours and y-coordinates in kilometres. What units does covariance have?

- Kilometres.
- Kilometres divided by hours.
- No units.
- Hours times kilometres.

## Explanation

Each term multiplies a deviation in hours by one in kilometres. The mean of the products retains the unit $\mathrm{h}\cdot\mathrm{km}$.
