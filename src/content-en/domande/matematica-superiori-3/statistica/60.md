A plot has x-coordinates in hours and y-coordinates in kilometres. What units does covariance have?

- Kilometres.
- Kilometres divided by hours.
- No units.
- Hours times kilometres.

## Explanation

Each term multiplies a deviation in hours by one in kilometres. The mean of the products retains the unit $\mathrm{h}\cdot\mathrm{km}$.

Dividing the sum by the number of pairs does not change the units, since the count has no dimension. Unlike a slope calculation, this does not divide length by time. Correlation instead divides by the product of the two standard deviations, which cancels the units.
