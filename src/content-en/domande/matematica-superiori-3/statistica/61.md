The regression of $y$ on $x$ has $\sigma_x^2>0$, $\bar y=4$ and zero covariance. What is the equation of the line?

- $\hat y=x$
- $\hat y=4$
- $\hat y=0$
- $x=\bar x$

## Explanation

Zero covariance divided by positive variance gives slope zero. The intercept is $\bar y=4$, so the line is horizontal.

Since the slope is zero, every input receives the same prediction. Passing through the point of means requires that constant prediction to be $4$. A vertical line would instead have many y-coordinates at the same input and would not represent a prediction of $y$ from $x$.
