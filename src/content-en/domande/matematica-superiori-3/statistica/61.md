The regression of $y$ on $x$ has $\sigma_x^2>0$, $\bar y=4$ and zero covariance. What is the equation of the line?

- $\hat y=x$
- $\hat y=4$
- $\hat y=0$
- $x=\bar x$

## Explanation

Zero covariance divided by positive variance gives slope zero. The intercept is $\bar y=4$, so the line is horizontal.
