For five measurements, the sum of the values is $20$ and the sum of their squares is $90$. What is the descriptive variance?

- $\sigma^2=4$
- $\sigma^2=14$
- $\sigma^2=2$
- $\sigma^2=18$

## Explanation

The mean is $\dfrac{20}{5}=4$ and the mean of the squares is $\dfrac{90}{5}=18$. The variance is the difference $18-4^2=2$.

The formula subtracts the **square of the mean**, so the quantity to subtract is $16$. Subtracting $4$ directly would give $14$ and compare different quantities. The count of $5$ is the divisor for both sums because the calculation describes all five recorded measurements.
