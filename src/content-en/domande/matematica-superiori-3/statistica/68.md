In the regression of $y$ on $x$, along which direction are deviations from points to the line measured?

- Horizontally, retaining each point's y-coordinate.
- Perpendicularly to the line.
- Vertically, retaining each point's x-coordinate.
- Always along the angle bisector.

## Explanation

The fitted value $mx_i+q$ uses the same x-coordinate $x_i$ as the observation. The difference $y_i-\hat y_i$ is therefore vertical.
