The transformation $y=ax+7$ is applied with $a>0$. The variance rises from $3$ to $12$. What is $a$?

- $3$
- $4$
- $2$
- $9$

## Explanation

Variance is multiplied by $a^2$. Since $a>0$ and $a^2=\dfrac{12}{3}=4$, the factor is $a=2$.

The value $7$ shifts every datum and the mean and therefore cancels in the deviation. The factor $a$ is squared because the comparison is between variances. The ratio $4$ is not the scale factor for distances. Taking a square root gives the magnitude, and $a>0$ selects the positive value.
