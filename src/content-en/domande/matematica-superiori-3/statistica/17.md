Two groups of $20$ and $40$ members have $8$ and $12$ votes in favour. What is the combined proportion in favour?

- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac7{20}$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac25$

## Explanation

There are $8+12=20$ votes in favour among $20+40=60$ members. The proportion is $\dfrac{20}{60}=\dfrac13$. Simply averaging the two proportions gives $\dfrac7{20}$ because that method gives equal weight to the two groups.
