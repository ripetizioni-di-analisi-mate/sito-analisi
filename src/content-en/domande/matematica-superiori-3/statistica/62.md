The regression line has slope $3$ and the point of means is $(2,5)$. What is the intercept?

- $-1$
- $1$
- $5$
- $11$

## Explanation

The line passes through $(2,5)$, so $5=3\cdot2+q$. This gives $q=5-6=-1$.

The intercept is the prediction at $x=0$. Starting from the point of means, going from $x=2$ to $x=0$ reduces the input by two units. With slope $3$ the predicted output therefore drops by $6$, from $5$ to $-1$.
