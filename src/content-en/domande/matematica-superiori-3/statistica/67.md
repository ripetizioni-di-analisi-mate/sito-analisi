The least squares fit is $\hat y=2x+1$ for points $(1,2)$, $(2,7)$ and $(3,6)$. What is the sum of the residuals?

- $2$
- $0$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The predictions are $3$, $5$ and $7$. The signed residuals are $-1$, $2$ and $-1$, which sum to $0$.

The positive middle residual balances the combined magnitude of the two negative residuals. This agrees with the property of a fitted line with an intercept, which passes through the point of means. The sum of squared residuals would instead equal $1+4+1=6$ and measures a different quantity.
