In a histogram, a class of width $4$ has area $12$ and the total area is $30$. What fraction of the observations belong to the class?

- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac2{15}$
- $\dfrac35$

## Explanation

The fraction is the class area divided by the total area. This gives $\dfrac{12}{30}=\dfrac25$.
