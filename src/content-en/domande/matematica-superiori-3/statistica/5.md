An instrument corrects the values using $y=2x-5$. If $x$ has mean $12$ and standard deviation $3$, what is the standard deviation of $y$?

- $\sigma_y=6$
- $\sigma_y=1$
- $\sigma_y=3$
- $\sigma_y=12$

## Explanation

The deviation from the mean changes according to $y_i-\bar y=2(x_i-\bar x)$. The constant term cancels and the standard deviation doubles, so it equals $2\cdot3=6$.
