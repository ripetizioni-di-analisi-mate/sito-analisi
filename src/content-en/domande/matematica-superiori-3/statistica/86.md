A model predicts consumption in litres as a function of time in hours and has slope $4$. How should the slope be interpreted?

- The predicted initial consumption is $4$ litres.
- Predicted consumption quadruples each hour.
- Consumption is measured at $4$ hours per litre.
- Predicted consumption increases by $4$ litres for every additional hour.

## Explanation

The slope is the change in $\hat y$ when $x$ increases by one unit. The unit of $x$ is an hour and the unit of $\hat y$ is a litre.
