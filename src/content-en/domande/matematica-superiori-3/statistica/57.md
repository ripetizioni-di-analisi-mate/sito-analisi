The covariance is $3$ and the standard deviations are $2$ and $3$. What is $r$?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

The product of the standard deviations is $6$. The coefficient is $\dfrac36=\dfrac12$.

Correlation compares covariance with the product of the two scales of spread. The divisor $6$ is positive because both standard deviations are positive. The positive sign of the covariance is therefore retained. One half satisfies $|r|\le1$, unlike $\dfrac32$.
