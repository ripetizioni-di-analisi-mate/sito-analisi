For the ordered data $2,4,5,7,8,12,16$, find the interquartile range by excluding the median from both halves.

- $\mathrm{IQR}=7$
- $\mathrm{IQR}=9$
- $\mathrm{IQR}=8$
- $\mathrm{IQR}=14$

## Explanation

The overall median is $7$. The two remaining halves are $2,4,5$ and $8,12,16$. Their middle values are $4$ and $12$, so $Q_3-Q_1=12-4=8$.
