Ten measurements have mean $6$. One reading recorded as $7$ is corrected to $12$. What is the new mean?

- $6$
- $7{,}2$
- $6{,}5$
- $11$

## Explanation

The original sum is $60$. The correction adds $12-7=5$ and there are still ten measurements. This gives $\dfrac{65}{10}=6{,}5$.

The corrected value replaces the old one, so the denominator stays at $10$. The new value adds $5$ units to the total, which across ten observations increases the mean by $\dfrac5{10}=0{,}5$. Adding this change to the original mean of $6$ gives the same result.
