Ten measurements have mean $6$. One reading recorded as $7$ is corrected to $12$. What is the new mean?

- $6$
- $7{,}2$
- $6{,}5$
- $11$

## Explanation

The original sum is $60$. The correction adds $12-7=5$ and there are still ten measurements. This gives $\dfrac{65}{10}=6{,}5$.
