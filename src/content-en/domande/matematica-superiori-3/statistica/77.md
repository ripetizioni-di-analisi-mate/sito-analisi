In a scatter plot, $\bar x=4$ and $\bar y=6$. Which of the four indicated points has a positive product of deviations?

::grafico{xmin=-0.4 xmax=7.7 ymin=-0.45 ymax=10.6 punti="3,5;3,8;6,5;4,6" asintoti="x=4;y=6" titolo="The dashed lines pass through the means $4$ and $6$."}

- $(3,5)$
- $(3,8)$
- $(6,5)$
- $(4,6)$

## Explanation

At $(3,5)$ both deviations are $-1$ and their product is $1$. The two points with opposite signs give negative products, and the product at $(4,6)$ is zero.
