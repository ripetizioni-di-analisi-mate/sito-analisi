Among $40$ households, $31$ have at most two bicycles. The households with more than two bicycles are $p\,\%$ of the total. What is the value of $p$?

- $p=9$
- $p=22.5$
- $p=31$
- $p=77.5$

## Explanation

The group above the threshold contains $40-31=9$ households. Its percentage is $100\cdot\dfrac9{40}\,\%=22.5\,\%$.

Households with exactly two bicycles are among the $31$ already counted. The requested group is the complement within the total of $40$. To check, households with at most two bicycles account for $77.5\,\%$, and $100-77.5=22.5$ is the value of $p$.
