Among $40$ households, $31$ have at most two bicycles. What percentage have more than two? Give the number that appears before the % sign.

- $p=9$
- $p=22{,}5$
- $p=31$
- $p=77{,}5$

## Explanation

The group above the threshold contains $40-31=9$ households. Its percentage is $100\cdot\dfrac9{40}\,\%=22{,}5\,\%$.
