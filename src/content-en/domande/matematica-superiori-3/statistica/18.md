The cumulative frequencies at $0$, $1$ and $2$ mistakes are $4$, $11$ and $18$. How many people made exactly one mistake?

- $4$
- $11$
- $7$
- $15$

## Explanation

The cumulative count at $1$ includes people who made at most one mistake. Subtracting the cumulative count at $0$ leaves $11-4=7$ people.
