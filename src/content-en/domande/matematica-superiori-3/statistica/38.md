The distributions $1,3,4,5,7$ and $1,3,4,5,19$ have the same median. Excluding it from both halves, how much larger is the interquartile range in the second?

- $0$
- $3$
- $6$
- $12$

## Explanation

The lower quartile stays at $\dfrac{1+3}{2}=2$. The upper quartile is the mean of $5$ and the maximum, so it increases by half of $19-7$, which is $6$.
