Two classes have the same mean. Class A has greater variance. What does this difference describe?

- A necessarily has more students.
- The mean squared deviation is larger in A.
- Every student in A has a higher value.
- A necessarily has a higher median.

## Explanation

Variance is the mean of the squared deviations from each class's mean. A larger variance means a higher average of those squares, while group sizes and medians may be equal.
