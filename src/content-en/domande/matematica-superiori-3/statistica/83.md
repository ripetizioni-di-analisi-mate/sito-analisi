The quartiles are $Q_1=4$ and $Q_3=9$. Add $10$ to every observation. What is the new interquartile range?

- $9$
- $15$
- $19$
- $5$

## Explanation

The order stays the same and each quartile increases by $10$. The difference becomes $(9+10)-(4+10)=5$.

Adding the same constant to all the data keeps each sorted position associated with the same observation. The quartile values become $14$ and $19$. Both increase by ten, so their difference stays at $19-14=5$. The original interquartile range is likewise $9-4=5$.
