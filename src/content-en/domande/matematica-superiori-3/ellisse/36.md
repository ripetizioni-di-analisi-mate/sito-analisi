If $(4,-2)$ belongs to an ellipse centred at the origin with horizontal and vertical axes, which other point has the same $x$-coordinate?

- $(4,2)$
- $(2,4)$
- $(-4,2)$
- $(-4,-2)$

## Explanation

The square of the $y$-coordinate stays the same when its sign changes. Keeping the $x$-coordinate therefore amounts to reflecting the point across the $x$-axis, giving $(4,2)$.
