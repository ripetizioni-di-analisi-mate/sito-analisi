For which $s$ is $(s,0)$ inside the region bounded by $\dfrac{(x+2)^2}{16}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$?

- $-6<s<2$
- $-6\leq s\leq2$
- $s<-6$ or $s>2$
- $-4<s<4$

## Explanation

At $y=0$, the condition for being inside is $\dfrac{(s+2)^2}{16}<1$. Thus $|s+2|<4$, equivalent to $-6<s<2$. The endpoints are on the curve.

The line $y=0$ crosses the ellipse along its horizontal axis, centred at $s=-2$ with half-length $4$. The interior region is the segment without its endpoints. At the centre the left-hand side is zero, so the centre is included.
