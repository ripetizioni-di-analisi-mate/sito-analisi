Which ellipse is more elongated if both have $a=5$? The first has $b=4$ and the second has $b=3$.

- The second, with eccentricity $\dfrac45$.
- The second, with eccentricity $\dfrac35$.
- The first, with eccentricity $\dfrac45$.
- Both have eccentricity $\dfrac15$.

## Explanation

For the first ellipse, $c=\sqrt{25-16}=3$, while for the second $c=\sqrt{25-9}=4$. Dividing by $5$ gives $\dfrac35$ and $\dfrac45$. With equal width, the curve with $b=3$ is lower.
