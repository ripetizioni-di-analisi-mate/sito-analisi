The figure shows the foci $F_1$, $F_2$ and point $P$ at the upper endpoint of the minor axis. What is the length of $PF_1$?

::grafico{curve="5*cos(t),3*sin(t),0,6.283185307" xmin=-7 xmax=7 ymin=-4 ymax=4 uguale=1 punti="-4,0,pieno,F_1;4,0,pieno,F_2;0,3,pieno,P" segmenti="-4,0,0,3;4,0,0,3" legenda="\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1" titolo="$F_1$ and $F_2$ are symmetric about the $y$-axis."}

- $4$
- $3$
- $5$
- $7$

## Explanation

The point $P$ is equidistant from the foci, so the distances are equal and sum to $2a=10$. Each is $5$. The figure shows legs $4$ and $3$, which give the same hypotenuse $\sqrt{4^2+3^2}=5$.
