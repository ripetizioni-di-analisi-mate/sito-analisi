The foci are $10$ units apart and the required sum is $8$. How many points meet the condition?

- None.
- Infinitely many along a segment.
- Two.
- One.

## Explanation

For every point, the sum of distances to the foci is at least their separation. Since $8<10$, no point satisfies the condition.

The direct distance between the foci is the segment of length $10$. Passing through a point $P$ gives a two-part path of length $PF_1+PF_2$, which can have length $10$ when $P$ lies on that segment. The value $8$ is below this minimum.
