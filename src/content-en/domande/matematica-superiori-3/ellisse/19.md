What is the width-to-height ratio of the smallest axis-aligned rectangle containing $\dfrac{x^2}{9}+\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$?

- $\dfrac43$
- $\dfrac9{16}$
- $\dfrac{16}{9}$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

The extreme $x$-coordinates are $-3$ and $3$, while the extreme $y$-coordinates are $-4$ and $4$. The rectangle is $6$ units wide and $8$ high. The ratio is $\dfrac68=\dfrac34$.
