What is the semi-major-axis length if $b=6$ and $e=\dfrac45$?

- $6$
- $8$
- $15$
- $10$

## Explanation

The relation $b=a\sqrt{1-e^2}$ becomes $6=a\sqrt{1-\dfrac{16}{25}}=\dfrac35a$. Solving gives $a=10$.

Dividing by $\dfrac35$ gives $a=6\cdot\dfrac53=10$. As a check, $c=\dfrac45\cdot10=8$, so $10^2-8^2=36=b^2$. The number $20$ would measure the full axis, twice the required semi-axis.
