What condition on $q$ makes $4x^2+9y^2=q$ a non-degenerate ellipse?

- $q>0$
- $q\geq0$
- $q=0$
- $q<0$

## Explanation

The left-hand side is non-negative. For $q=0$ the origin is the only point. For $q>0$, division by $q$ gives positive denominators $\dfrac q4$ and $\dfrac q9$, so both semi-axis lengths are positive.
