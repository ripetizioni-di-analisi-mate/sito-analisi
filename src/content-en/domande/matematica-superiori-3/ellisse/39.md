What are the minimum and maximum $x$-coordinates of $\dfrac{(x+1)^2}{4}+\dfrac{(y-2)^2}{25}=1$?

- $-2$ and $2$
- $-3$ and $1$
- $-3$ and $7$
- $-6$ and $4$

## Explanation

The horizontal semi-axis is $\sqrt4=2$, even though it is shorter than the vertical one. The extreme $x$-coordinates are $-1-2=-3$ and $-1+2=1$.
