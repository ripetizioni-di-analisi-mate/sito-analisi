Which tangent to $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$ has the same slope as the tangent at $\left(3,\dfrac{12}{5}\right)$ and touches at a different point?

- $9x+20y=0$
- $9x-20y=75$
- $20x+9y=-75$
- $9x+20y=-75$

## Explanation

The given tangent is $9x+20y=75$. Central reflection moves the contact point to $\left(-3,-\dfrac{12}{5}\right)$ and preserves the slope. It reverses the sign of the right-hand side of the equation.
