Which points are common to the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{4}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$ and the circle $x^2+y^2=4$?

- $(0,-3)$ and $(0,3)$
- $(-2,0)$ and $(2,0)$
- No point.
- $(0,-2)$ and $(0,2)$

## Explanation

On the circle, $x^2=4-y^2$. The first equation becomes $1-\dfrac{y^2}{4}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$, which requires $y=0$. The circle equation then gives $x=\pm2$.
