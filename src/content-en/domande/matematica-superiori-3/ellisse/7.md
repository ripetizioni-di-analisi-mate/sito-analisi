What is the tangent to $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$ at $\left(-3,\dfrac{12}{5}\right)$?

- $-9x+20y=25$
- $-3x+12y=25$
- $9x+20y=75$
- $-9x+20y=75$

## Explanation

The ratios of squared coordinates sum to $\dfrac9{25}+\dfrac{16}{25}=1$. The coefficients in the tangent formula are $-\dfrac3{25}$ and $\dfrac4{15}$. Multiplying the equation by $75$ gives the required equation.
