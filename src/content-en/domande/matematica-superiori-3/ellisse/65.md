An elliptical window is $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ wide and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ high. Only its height is doubled. What is the new direction of the major axis?

- The two axes have the same length.
- Oblique.
- Vertical.
- Horizontal.

## Explanation

The new height is $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ while the width stays $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. Since the height exceeds the width, the major axis is vertical.
