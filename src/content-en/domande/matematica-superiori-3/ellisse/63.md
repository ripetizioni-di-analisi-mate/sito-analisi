What is the area of the smallest axis-aligned rectangle containing the ellipse with semi-axes $5$ and $2$ parallel to the coordinate axes?

- $14$
- $10$
- $40$
- $20$

## Explanation

The sides of the rectangle must extend to the extreme points. The side lengths are therefore twice the semi-axes, namely $10$ and $4$. The area is $40$.

The rectangle contains the entire curve because the coordinates relative to the centre are bounded by the semi-axes. Any narrower or shorter rectangle would leave out at least one of the four endpoints. The one reaching all endpoints therefore has minimum area $10\cdot4$.
