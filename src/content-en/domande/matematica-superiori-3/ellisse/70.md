Which equation describes a circle as a case of an ellipse centred at the origin?

- $\dfrac{x^2}{16}-\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{16}+\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{16}+\dfrac{y^2}{16}=0$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{16}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$

## Explanation

With equal semi-axes of length $4$, multiplying by $16$ gives $x^2+y^2=16$. This is the circle centred at the origin with radius $4$.
