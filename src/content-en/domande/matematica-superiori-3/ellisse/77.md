Which statement holds for the two ellipses in the figure?

::grafico{curve="4*cos(t),3*sin(t),0,6.283185307;4*cos(t),2*sin(t),0,6.283185307" xmin=-5 xmax=5 ymin=-4 ymax=4 uguale=1 punti="-4,0;4,0;0,3;0,2" legenda="\dfrac{x^2}{16}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1;\dfrac{x^2}{16}+\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1" titolo="Both curves are centred at the origin."}

- The taller curve has a vertical major axis.
- The shorter ellipse has greater eccentricity.
- The two curves have the same foci.
- The shorter ellipse has smaller eccentricity.

## Explanation

The semi-major axis is $4$ in both cases. The shorter ellipse has $b=2$ while the other has $b=3$. In the formula $e=\sqrt{1-\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}}$, the lower curve therefore has the greater value of $e$.
