What is the $y$-intercept of the tangent to $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$ at $\left(3,\dfrac{12}{5}\right)$?

- $\dfrac{15}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{15}$
- $\dfrac{12}{5}$
- $3$

## Explanation

The tangent has equation $\dfrac{3x}{25}+\dfrac{4y}{15}=1$. On the $y$-axis set $x=0$, so $y=\dfrac{15}{4}$.

Multiplication by $75$ gives the equivalent equation $9x+20y=75$. Setting $x=0$ gives $20y=75$, so the axis intersection is $\left(0,\dfrac{15}{4}\right)$. The value $\dfrac{12}{5}$ is the vertical coordinate at the contact, where the horizontal coordinate is $3$.
