A stretch maps the unit circle to an ellipse with semiaxes $2$ and $3$ by $(X,Y)=(2x,3y)$. A circle tangent has slope $-1$. What is the slope of the corresponding ellipse tangent?

- $-\dfrac32$
- $-\dfrac23$
- $-1$
- $\dfrac32$

## Explanation

Along the original line $\Delta y=-\Delta x$. After stretching, the slope becomes $\dfrac{3\Delta y}{2\Delta x}=-\dfrac32$. The image line remains tangent because it meets the transformed curve only at the contact point.
