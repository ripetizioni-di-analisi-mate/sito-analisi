A horizontal ellipse has semi-major axis $10$ and eccentricity $\dfrac35$. What is its semi-minor axis?

- $b=6$
- $b=4$
- $b=8$
- $b=64$

## Explanation

Since $c=ae$, we find $c=6$. Thus $b=\sqrt{a^2-c^2}=\sqrt{100-36}=8$.

The square to subtract is $6^2$ because the distance from a focus to the centre is $6$, half the focal separation. At the minor-axis endpoint the geometric check gives $\sqrt{8^2+6^2}=10$, the distance from either focus to that endpoint.
