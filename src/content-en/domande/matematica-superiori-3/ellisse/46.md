An ellipse has horizontal major axis, centre at the origin and focus $(2,0)$. Which extra piece of information is enough to find the semi-minor axis?

- The other focus is $(-2,0)$.
- The centre is the midpoint of the foci.
- The foci lie on the $x$-axis.
- The length of the major axis.

## Explanation

The given focus yields $c=2$. The length of the major axis determines $a$, and hence $b=\sqrt{a^2-4}$. The other facts already follow from the initial data.
