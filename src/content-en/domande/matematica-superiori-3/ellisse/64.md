An arch is the upper half of an ellipse of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above a base $8\,\mathrm{m}$ wide. What is the horizontal distance from the centre of the base where the arch has height $\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\sqrt3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The ellipse has horizontal semi-axis $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and vertical semi-axis $3\,\mathrm{m}$. At the given height, $\dfrac{y^2}{9}=\dfrac34$, so $\dfrac{x^2}{16}=\dfrac14$. The horizontal distance is $|x|=2\,\mathrm{m}$.
