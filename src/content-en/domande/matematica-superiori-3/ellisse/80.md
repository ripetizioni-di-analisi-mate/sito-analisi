Which quantity can be determined from the eccentricity of an ellipse alone?

- The exact separation of the foci.
- The ratio of $b$ to $a$.
- The coordinates of the centre.
- The length of $a$.

## Explanation

The relation $b=a\sqrt{1-e^2}$ gives $\dfrac ba=\sqrt{1-e^2}$. Ellipses at different scales and with different centres can have the same eccentricity.
