The major-axis vertices of an ellipse are $(1,2)$ and $(9,2)$, and one focus is $(8,2)$. What is the square of the semi-minor axis?

- $7$
- $25$
- $9$
- $16$

## Explanation

The centre is $(5,2)$, the midpoint of the vertices. It is $4$ units from a vertex and $3$ from the focus. Thus $b^2=4^2-3^2=7$.
