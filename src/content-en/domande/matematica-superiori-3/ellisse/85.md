An ellipse centred at the origin has horizontal major axis, focus $(3,0)$, and point $(0,4)$ on the curve. What is the sum of distances to the foci?

- $6$
- $5$
- $8$
- $10$

## Explanation

The other focus is $(-3,0)$. The point $(0,4)$ is $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$ units from each focus. The constant sum is therefore $5+5=10$.
