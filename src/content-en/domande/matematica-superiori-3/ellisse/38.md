Where is the centre of the ellipse $9x^2+4y^2-18x+16y-11=0$?

- $(1,-2)$
- $(9,-8)$
- $(-1,2)$
- $(2,-1)$

## Explanation

Factoring gives $9(x^2-2x)$ and $4(y^2+4y)$. The completed squares contain $(x-1)^2$ and $(y+2)^2$. The binomials vanish at $(1,-2)$, the centre of the ellipse.

The added terms after factoring out the coefficients are $9\cdot1=9$ and $4\cdot4=16$. Completing the squares gives $9(x-1)^2+4(y+2)^2=36$. The signs in the centre's coordinates are the ones that make the differences vanish.
