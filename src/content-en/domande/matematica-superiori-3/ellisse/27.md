Which value of $e$ corresponds to the case where the two focal distances are equal for every point on the curve?

- $0$
- $\dfrac12$
- $1$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

If the foci are distinct, only points on their perpendicular bisector are equidistant. For every point on the curve to have this property, the foci must coincide and hence $c=0$. Then $e=0$.
