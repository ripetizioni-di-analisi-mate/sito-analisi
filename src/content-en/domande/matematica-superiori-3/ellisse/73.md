Which point of $\dfrac{(x-1)^2}{9}+\dfrac{(y-1)^2}{4}=1$ is at maximum horizontal distance from the centre and has positive $x$-coordinate?

- $(4,1)$
- $(1,-1)$
- $(1,3)$
- $(3,1)$

## Explanation

The maximum horizontal distance is the horizontal semi-axis $3$ and occurs at $y=1$. The endpoint $x$-coordinates are $1-3=-2$ and $1+3=4$. Only $4$ is positive.
