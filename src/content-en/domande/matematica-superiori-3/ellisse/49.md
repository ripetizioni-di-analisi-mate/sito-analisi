Where are the points of an ellipse that are equidistant from its two distinct foci?

- At the foci themselves.
- At the endpoints of the major axis.
- At the endpoints of the minor axis.
- At the centre only.

## Explanation

The points equidistant from the foci lie on the perpendicular bisector of the segment joining them. This line is the minor axis and meets the ellipse at its two endpoints.
