Why is the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{16}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$ not the graph of a single function $y=f(x)$?

- At $x=0$ there are two different $y$-coordinates.
- The $y$-coordinate is $0$ for every $x$.
- There are no points at $x=0$.
- The ellipse has no symmetry.

## Explanation

Substituting $x=0$ gives $y^2=9$, so $y=-3$ and $y=3$. A function assigns a single value to each $x$-coordinate in its domain.
