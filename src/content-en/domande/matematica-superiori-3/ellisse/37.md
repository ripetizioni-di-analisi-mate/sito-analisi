What is the vertical axis of symmetry of $\dfrac{(x+3)^2}{16}+\dfrac{(y-2)^2}{9}=1$?

- $y=2$
- $x=3$
- $x=2$
- $x=-3$

## Explanation

Reflection across a vertical line changes $x+3$ to its opposite and preserves $y$. The fixed value of $x$ satisfies $x+3=0$, so the line is $x=-3$.
