For which $t$ does the vertical line $x=t$ meet $\dfrac{(x+1)^2}{9}+\dfrac{(y-2)^2}{16}=1$ at exactly two points?

- $-3<t<3$
- $-5<t<3$
- $-4\leq t\leq2$
- $-4<t<2$

## Explanation

Two distinct $y$-coordinates require $1-\dfrac{(t+1)^2}{9}>0$. This gives $|t+1|<3$, or $-4<t<2$. At the endpoints the vertical line has just one common point.
