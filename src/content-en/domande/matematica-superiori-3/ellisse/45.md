An ellipse centred at the origin has horizontal major axis and passes through $(6,0)$ and $(0,2)$. What is the distance between the foci?

- $8\sqrt2$
- $2\sqrt{10}$
- $4\sqrt2$
- $12$

## Explanation

The points on the axes give $a=6$ and $b=2$. Thus $c=\sqrt{36-4}=\sqrt{32}=4\sqrt2$, and the distance between the foci is $2c=8\sqrt2$.
