An ellipse centred at the origin has horizontal major axis and passes through $(6,0)$ and $(0,2)$. What is the distance between the foci?

- $8\sqrt2$
- $2\sqrt{10}$
- $4\sqrt2$
- $12$

## Explanation

The points on the axes give $a=6$ and $b=2$. Thus $c=\sqrt{36-4}=\sqrt{32}=4\sqrt2$, and the distance between the foci is $2c=8\sqrt2$.

The implicit form confirms that intersecting the curve with the horizontal axis means setting $y=0$, so $(6,0)$ is a vertex. The point $(0,2)$ is the endpoint of the vertical semi-axis. The foci have opposite horizontal coordinates $\pm4\sqrt2$, so their separation is twice $c$.
