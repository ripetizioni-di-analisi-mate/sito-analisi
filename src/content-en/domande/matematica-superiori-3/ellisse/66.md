At the point closest to a focus of an ellipse, what is the distance to that focus if $a=7$ and $c=5$?

- $12$
- $2$
- $7$
- $5$

## Explanation

The sum of distances is $14$ and the absolute difference is at most $10$. The smaller distance is therefore at least $\dfrac{14-10}{2}=2$. At the major-axis vertex on the same side as the focus, the distance is $7-5=2$.
