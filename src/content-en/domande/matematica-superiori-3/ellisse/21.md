Which equation gives an ellipse with vertical major axis and centre at the origin?

- $9x^2+4y^2=36$
- $9x^2+9y^2=36$
- $9x^2-4y^2=36$
- $4x^2+9y^2=36$

## Explanation

Dividing $9x^2+4y^2=36$ by $36$ gives $\dfrac{x^2}{4}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$. The larger denominator is under $y^2$, so the major axis is vertical.
