The graph shows a vertical tangent and a horizontal tangent. Where do the two lines intersect?

::grafico{curve="3*cos(t),2*sin(t),0,6.283185307;3,t,-3,4;t,2,-4,5" xmin=-4 xmax=5 ymin=-3 ymax=4 uguale=1 punti="3,0,pieno,P;0,2,pieno,Q" legenda="\dfrac{x^2}{9}+\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1;x=3;y=2" titolo="The tangents pass through the vertices $P$ and $Q$."}

- $(0,2)$
- $(2,3)$
- $(3,2)$
- $(3,0)$

## Explanation

The vertical tangent fixes the $x$-coordinate at $x=3$ and the horizontal tangent fixes the $y$-coordinate at $y=2$. The point satisfying both conditions is $(3,2)$.
