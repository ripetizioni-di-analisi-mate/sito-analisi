If the distance from a point on an ellipse to one focus increases by $2$, what happens to the distance to the other focus?

- It decreases by $2$.
- It increases by $2$.
- It decreases by $1$.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

The two distances always sum to $2a$. Keeping the sum fixed means a $2$-unit increase in one distance must be matched by a $2$-unit decrease in the other.

Writing $d_1$ and $d_2$ for the initial distances, the second is $d_2=2a-d_1$. After the change it becomes $2a-(d_1+2)=d_2-2$. Such a change is possible only if the new point stays within the distance range allowed by that ellipse.
