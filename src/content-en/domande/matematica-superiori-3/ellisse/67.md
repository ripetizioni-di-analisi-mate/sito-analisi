If the distance from a point on an ellipse to one focus increases by $2$, what happens to the distance to the other focus?

- It decreases by $2$.
- It increases by $2$.
- It decreases by $1$.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

The two distances always sum to $2a$. Keeping the sum fixed means a $2$-unit increase in one distance must be matched by a $2$-unit decrease in the other.
