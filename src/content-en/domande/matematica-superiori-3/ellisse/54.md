How many common points do $\dfrac{x^2}{9}+\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1$ and $x+y=5$ have?

- One.
- Two.
- Zero.
- Four.

## Explanation

On the ellipse we have $x\leq3$ and $y\leq2$. Reaching the sum $5$ would require both extremes, giving $(3,2)$. That point gives $1+1=2$ on the left and does not lie on the curve.
