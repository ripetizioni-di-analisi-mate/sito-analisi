What is the greatest possible distance from a point on an ellipse to one of its foci when $a=8$ and $c=3$?

- $16$
- $13$
- $8$
- $11$

## Explanation

For every point the sum of the distances is $16$ and their difference is at most $6$. The greater distance is therefore at most $\dfrac{16+6}{2}=11$. At the major-axis vertex opposite the focus, the distance is exactly $8+3=11$.
