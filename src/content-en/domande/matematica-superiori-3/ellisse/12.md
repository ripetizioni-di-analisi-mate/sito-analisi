The ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$ has been moved $3$ units left and $2$ units up. What is its rightmost vertex?

- $(2,2)$
- $(-2,2)$
- $(8,2)$
- $(2,-2)$

## Explanation

The original vertex is $(5,0)$. Apply the same displacement $(-3,2)$ to that point, giving $(5-3,0+2)=(2,2)$.
