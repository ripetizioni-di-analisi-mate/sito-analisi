The ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$ has been moved $3$ units left and $2$ units up. What is its rightmost vertex?

- $(2,2)$
- $(-2,2)$
- $(8,2)$
- $(2,-2)$

## Explanation

The original vertex is $(5,0)$. Apply the same displacement $(-3,2)$ to that point, giving $(5-3,0+2)=(2,2)$.

Another method is to find the new centre $(-3,2)$ and add the horizontal semi-axis $5$ to its horizontal coordinate. The vertical coordinate remains that of the centre. Since a translation adds the same amount to all horizontal coordinates, the right-hand vertex still has the largest one.
