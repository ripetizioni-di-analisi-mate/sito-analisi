For which positive $q$ is the line $y=q$ tangent to $\dfrac{(x-1)^2}{16}+\dfrac{(y+2)^2}{25}=1$?

- $q=7$
- $q=1$
- $q=5$
- $q=3$

## Explanation

The vertical semi-axis measures $5$, and the centre has $y$-coordinate $-2$. The horizontal tangents occur at the extreme heights $-2\pm5$, namely $3$ and $-7$. The only positive one is $3$.
