Which line contains both foci of $\dfrac{(x-1)^2}{16}+\dfrac{(y+3)^2}{25}=1$?

- $x=-1$
- $y=3$
- $y=-3$
- $x=1$

## Explanation

The larger denominator is under the term in $y$. The focal line is therefore vertical and passes through the centre $(1,-3)$. Its equation is $x=1$.
