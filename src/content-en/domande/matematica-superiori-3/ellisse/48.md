Which pair of distances can both belong to one point on an ellipse with $a=5$ and $c=3$?

- $6$ and $6$
- $9$ and $1$
- $1$ and $1$
- $7$ and $3$

## Explanation

The sum must be $10$ and the absolute difference must be at most $6$. For $7$ and $3$ the difference is $4<6$. A triangle with side lengths $7$, $3$ and $6$ exists, so there is such a point.
