What value of $m$ puts $(3,2)$ on $\dfrac{x^2}{18}+\dfrac{y^2}{m}=1$ with $m>0$?

- $m=9$
- $m=8$
- $m=4$
- $m=2$

## Explanation

Substitution gives $\dfrac9{18}+\dfrac4m=1$. Thus $\dfrac4m=\dfrac12$, and multiplying by $2m$ gives $m=8$.

The value $m=8$ is admissible because it is strictly positive. The given coordinates do indeed make each ratio equal to $\dfrac12$. The denominator $m$ is the square of the vertical semi-axis, whose actual length is therefore $\sqrt8$.
