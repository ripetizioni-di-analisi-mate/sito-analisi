The ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{9}+\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1$ is translated by the vector $(-2,3)$. What is the new equation?

- $\dfrac{(x-2)^2}{9}+\dfrac{(y+3)^2}{4}=1$
- $\dfrac{(x+2)^2}{9}+\dfrac{(y-3)^2}{4}=1$
- $\dfrac{(x+2)^2}{4}+\dfrac{(y-3)^2}{9}=1$
- $\dfrac{(x+3)^2}{9}+\dfrac{(y-2)^2}{4}=1$

## Explanation

The centre moves from $(0,0)$ to $(-2,3)$. Use the displacements $x-(-2)=x+2$ and $y-3$. The semi-axis lengths and directions remain unchanged.
