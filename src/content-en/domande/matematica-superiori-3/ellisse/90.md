Which relation necessarily holds for a point $P$ outside the region bounded by $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$?

- $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}>1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}-\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}>1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}<1$

## Explanation

Rescaling the coordinates to $\dfrac xa$ and $\dfrac yb$ maps the curve to the boundary of the unit disk. An outside point is more than $1$ unit from the origin in those coordinates, so the sum of squares exceeds $1$.
