Which value can be the eccentricity of a non-circular ellipse?

- $1$
- $0$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

For distinct foci we have $0<c<a$. Dividing by $a>0$ gives $0<e<1$. The value $\dfrac23$ is the only one in this interval.

At $e=0$ the foci would coincide, giving a circle. At $e=1$ we would have $c=a$ and obtain only the segment between the foci. For values above $1$, the sum $2a$ would be smaller than the separation $2c$.
