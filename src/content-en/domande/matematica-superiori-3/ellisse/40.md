An ellipse has centre $(2,-3)$ and the right endpoint of its horizontal axis is $(7,-3)$. What is the left endpoint?

- $(-7,-3)$
- $(-5,-3)$
- $(-3,-3)$
- $(2,2)$

## Explanation

The centre is the midpoint of the endpoints. The right endpoint lies $7-2=5$ units from it. To the left the $x$-coordinate is $2-5=-3$, and the $y$-coordinate is still $-3$.
