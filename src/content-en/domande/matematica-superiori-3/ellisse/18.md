The diagram marks the centre $C$ and the vertex $V$. Which segment measures the semi-major axis?

::grafico{curve="4*cos(t),2*sin(t),0,6.283185307" xmin=-5 xmax=5 ymin=-4 ymax=4 uguale=1 punti="0,0,pieno,C;4,0,pieno,V;-4,0,pieno,U;0,2,pieno,W" legenda="\dfrac{x^2}{16}+\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1" titolo="Key points of an ellipse with a horizontal major axis."}

- $UV$
- $CW$
- $CV$
- $WU$

## Explanation

The semi-major axis is half the axis joining $U$ and $V$. Since $C$ is the midpoint, the required length is $CV$.
