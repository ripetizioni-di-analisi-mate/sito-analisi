What equation results from exchanging the coordinates $x$ and $y$ in the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1$?

- $\dfrac{x^2}{5}+\dfrac{y^2}{2}=1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{4}+\dfrac{y^2}{25}=1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{4}-\dfrac{y^2}{25}=1$

## Explanation

Exchanging the coordinates moves the denominator $25$ under $y^2$ and $4$ under $x^2$. The semi-axis lengths remain $5$ and $2$, while their directions change.
