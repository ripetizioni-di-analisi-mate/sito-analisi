What is the length of the vertical chord of $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$ on the line $x=3$?

- $\dfrac85$
- $\dfrac{16}{5}$
- $\dfrac{32}{5}$
- $8$

## Explanation

At $x=3$ we get $y^2=16\left(1-\dfrac9{25}\right)=\dfrac{256}{25}$. The $y$-coordinates are $\pm\dfrac{16}{5}$ and their positive difference is $\dfrac{32}{5}$.
