Which ratio holds on an ellipse of eccentricity $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$?

- $\dfrac ba=2$
- $\dfrac ba=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$
- $\dfrac ba=\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac ba=\dfrac34$

## Explanation

From $b=a\sqrt{1-e^2}$, we get $\dfrac ba=\sqrt{1-\dfrac34}=\dfrac12$. The ratio is positive because both semi-axis lengths are positive.

The identity follows by dividing $b^2=a^2-c^2$ by the positive quantity $a^2$. This gives $\left(\dfrac ba\right)^2=1-e^2=\dfrac14$. The square root is $\dfrac12$, so the full minor axis also measures half the major axis.
