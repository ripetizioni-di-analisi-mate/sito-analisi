Which equation has the same points as $\dfrac{(x-1)^2}{4}+\dfrac{(y+2)^2}{9}=1$?

- $9(x+1)^2+4(y-2)^2=36$
- $9(x-1)^2+4(y+2)^2=1$
- $9(x-1)^2+4(y+2)^2=36$
- $4(x-1)^2+9(y+2)^2=36$

## Explanation

Multiply every term by the least common multiple $36$. The first square has coefficient $36$ divided by $4$, namely $9$. The second has coefficient $36$ divided by $9$, namely $4$.
