Which quantity involving the foci stays fixed if an ellipse is rotated in the plane without distortion?

- The $x$-coordinates of the foci under every rotation.
- The direction of the focal segment under every rotation.
- The $y$-coordinates of the foci under every rotation.
- The distance between the foci.

## Explanation

A rotation is an isometry and preserves the distance between every pair of points, including $F_1F_2=2c$. The foci move with the curve.
