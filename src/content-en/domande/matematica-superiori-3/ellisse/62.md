The foci of an ellipse are $(0,1)$ and $(0,5)$. What is the $y$-coordinate of its centre?

- $4$
- $6$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The centre is the midpoint of the foci. Its $y$-coordinate is therefore $\dfrac{1+5}{2}=3$. The value $2$ is the distance from each focus to the centre.
