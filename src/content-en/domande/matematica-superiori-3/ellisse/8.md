What is the locus of points satisfying $PF_1+PF_2=8$ if $F_1(-4,0)$ and $F_2(4,0)$?

- A circle of radius $4$.
- The entire line through the foci.
- The closed segment joining the foci.
- The origin only.

## Explanation

The given sum equals the distance between the foci. Equality in the triangle inequality holds exactly along the segment joining the foci, including its endpoints.
