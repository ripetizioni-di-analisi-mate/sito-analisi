What locus results when the sum of distances to two distinct foci equals the distance between the foci?

- A circle.
- Two ellipses.
- The empty locus.
- A line segment.

## Explanation

The triangle inequality requires $PF_1+PF_2\geq F_1F_2$. Equality holds only on the segment between the foci. A smaller sum gives the empty locus. For a non-degenerate ellipse, the sum must exceed the distance between the foci.
