A closed string $26\,\mathrm{cm}$ long goes around two nails $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart and the tip of a pencil. Moving the pencil while keeping the string taut traces an ellipse. How long is its major axis?

- $26\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $13\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The taut string forms a triangle with vertices at the two nails and at the pencil tip $P$. The side between the nails always uses $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ of string, so the two sides starting from $P$ measure $26-6=20\,\mathrm{cm}$ together.

The nails are the foci $F_1$ and $F_2$, therefore every traced point satisfies $PF_1+PF_2=20\,\mathrm{cm}$. By the definition of an ellipse this sum is $2a$, that is, the length of the major axis. Therefore the major axis is $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ long and the semi-major axis $10\,\mathrm{cm}$.
