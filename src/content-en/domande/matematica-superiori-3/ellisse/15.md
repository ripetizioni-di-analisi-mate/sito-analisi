A closed loop of string $26$ cm long is stretched into a triangle around two pins $6$ cm apart and a pencil point. What is the major-axis length of the ellipse traced by the pencil?

- $26$ cm
- $13$ cm
- $10$ cm
- $20$ cm

## Explanation

The side between the pins uses $6$ cm of string. The other two sides, from the pencil to the foci, therefore sum to $20$ cm, the major-axis length.
