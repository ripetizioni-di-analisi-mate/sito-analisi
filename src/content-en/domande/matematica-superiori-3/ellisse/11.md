A dome is modelled by the upper half of $\dfrac{x^2}{64}+\dfrac{y^2}{36}=1$, with $x$ and $y$ in metres. What is the height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from the centre of the base? Give the height in metres.

- $h=3$
- $h=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$
- $h=6$
- $h=3\sqrt3$

## Explanation

At $x=4$, $\dfrac{y^2}{36}=1-\dfrac{16}{64}=\dfrac34$. On the upper half take the positive root, so $y=3\sqrt3\,\mathrm{m}$.
