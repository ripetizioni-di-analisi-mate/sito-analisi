Substituting $y=mx+q$ into an ellipse gives an equation in $x$ with zero discriminant. How is the line positioned?

- It meets the ellipse at two points.
- It coincides with an axis of symmetry.
- It is tangent.
- It misses the ellipse.

## Explanation

The quadratic coefficient is positive because it is the sum of a positive term and a non-negative term. A zero discriminant gives a single real root, hence a single intersection.
