Which focus of $\dfrac{(x-2)^2}{25}+\dfrac{(y+1)^2}{9}=1$ has the larger $x$-coordinate?

- $(4,-1)$
- $(6,-1)$
- $(2,3)$
- $(7,-1)$

## Explanation

The centre is $(2,-1)$ and the major axis is horizontal. We have $c=\sqrt{25-9}=4$, so the right-hand focus is $4$ units to the right of the centre.

Add $4$ only to the centre's horizontal coordinate, giving $(2+4,-1)=(6,-1)$. The point $(7,-1)$ is $5$ units from the centre and is a vertex. The vertical coordinate $-1$ is common to all points on the focal axis.
