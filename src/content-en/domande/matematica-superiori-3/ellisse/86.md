How does the denominator of the term in $y$ change if $\dfrac{x^2}{9}+\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$ is stretched vertically by a factor of $2$?

- It becomes $8$.
- It becomes $64$.
- It becomes $32$.
- It stays $16$.

## Explanation

The vertical semi-axis grows from $4$ to $8$. The denominator is the square of that semi-axis, so it becomes $8^2=64$.
