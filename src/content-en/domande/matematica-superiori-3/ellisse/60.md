For the tangent at $P_0(x_0,y_0)$ to $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$, which formula gives the pair of coefficients of $x$ and $y$ for every point on the ellipse when the right-hand side is $1$?

- $(x_0,y_0)$
- $\left(\dfrac{x_0^2}{a^2},\dfrac{y_0^2}{b^2}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{x_0}{a},\dfrac{y_0}{b}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{x_0}{a^2},\dfrac{y_0}{b^2}\right)$

## Explanation

The formula is $\dfrac{xx_0}{a^2}+\dfrac{yy_0}{b^2}=1$. The coefficients are the point’s coordinates divided by the respective squares of the semi-axes.
