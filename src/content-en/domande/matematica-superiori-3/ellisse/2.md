Find the focus with larger $y$-coordinate for $\dfrac{x^2}{20}+\dfrac{y^2}{36}=1$.

- $(0,4)$
- $(4,0)$
- $(0,8)$
- $(0,6)$

## Explanation

The major axis is vertical because $36$ is the denominator under $y^2$. The focal distance from the centre is $\sqrt{36-20}=4$. The upper focus is therefore $(0,4)$.
