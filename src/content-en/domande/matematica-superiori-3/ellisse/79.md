If $\dfrac{(x-1)^2}{9}+\dfrac{(y-2)^2}{4}=1$ is reflected across the $x$-axis, what are the coordinates of the new centre?

- $(-1,-2)$
- $(2,1)$
- $(-1,2)$
- $(1,-2)$

## Explanation

Reflection across the $x$-axis preserves the first coordinate and changes the sign of the second. The original centre is $(1,2)$, so the reflected centre is $(1,-2)$.
