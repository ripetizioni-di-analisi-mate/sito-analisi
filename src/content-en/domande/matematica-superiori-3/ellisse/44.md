What is the equation of the ellipse with centre $(1,2)$, horizontal semi-axis $4$ and vertical semi-axis $6$?

- $\dfrac{(x-1)^2}{36}+\dfrac{(y-2)^2}{16}=1$
- $\dfrac{(x-1)^2}{16}+\dfrac{(y-2)^2}{36}=1$
- $\dfrac{(x+1)^2}{16}+\dfrac{(y+2)^2}{36}=1$
- $\dfrac{(x-1)^2}{4}+\dfrac{(y-2)^2}{6}=1$

## Explanation

The variables are the displacements $x-1$ and $y-2$ from the centre. The square of the horizontal semi-axis, $16$, goes under the first squared term. Its vertical counterpart, $36$, goes under the second.
