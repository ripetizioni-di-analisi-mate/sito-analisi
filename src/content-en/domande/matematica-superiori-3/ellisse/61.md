Which transformation maps $\dfrac{x^2}{9}+\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1$ to the unit circle $u^2+v^2=1$?

- $u=\dfrac x2$, $v=\dfrac y3$
- $u=3x$, $v=2y$
- $u=\dfrac x3$, $v=\dfrac y2$
- $u=\dfrac x9$, $v=\dfrac y4$

## Explanation

Since $\dfrac{x^2}{9}=\left(\dfrac x3\right)^2$ and $\dfrac{y^2}{4}=\left(\dfrac y2\right)^2$, divide the coordinates by the semi-axis lengths.
