Find the equation of the ellipse with foci $(0,-3)$ and $(0,3)$ and focal distance sum $10$.

- $\dfrac{x^2}{16}+\dfrac{y^2}{25}=1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{9}+\dfrac{y^2}{25}=1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{16}+\dfrac{y^2}{100}=1$

## Explanation

The centre is the midpoint of the foci, the origin. We have $c=3$ and $a=\dfrac{10}{2}=5$, so $b^2=25-9=16$. The foci are aligned vertically, hence $25$ is the denominator under $y^2$.
