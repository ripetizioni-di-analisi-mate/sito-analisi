Which line is a secant to $\dfrac{x^2}{16}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$?

- $y=-3$
- $x=3$
- $y=4$
- $x=4$

## Explanation

For $x=3$ we have $\dfrac9{16}<1$, leaving a positive value for $\dfrac{y^2}{9}$ and giving two $y$-coordinates. The lines $x=4$ and $y=-3$ are tangents, while $y=4$ misses the ellipse.
