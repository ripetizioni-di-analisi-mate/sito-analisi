Which point of $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$ lies on the tangent $x=-5$?

- $(-5,3)$
- $(-5,0)$
- $(-5,-3)$
- $(0,-5)$

## Explanation

In the ellipse equation the ratio $\dfrac{x^2}{25}$ already equals $1$ for $x=-5$. The other ratio must therefore be zero, leaving $0$ as the only possible $y$-coordinate.

This gives $(-5,0)$, the left endpoint of the horizontal axis. For $x<-5$ the horizontal term would already exceed $1$, whereas for $-5<x<5$ it would allow two vertical coordinates. The case $x=-5$ is exactly the tangency threshold.
