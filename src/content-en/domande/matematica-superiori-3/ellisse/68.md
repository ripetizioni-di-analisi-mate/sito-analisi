Which comparison is correct if two ellipses share the same $a$ and the first has greater $c$?

- The first has smaller eccentricity.
- The first has smaller $b$.
- They have the same $b$.
- The first has greater $b$.

## Explanation

Since $b^2=a^2-c^2$, increasing $c$ with fixed $a$ lowers $b^2$. Because $b$ is positive, it decreases as well.
