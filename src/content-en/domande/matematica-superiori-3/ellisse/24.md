An ellipse has centre $(1,-2)$, vertical semi-major axis $5$, and $e=\dfrac45$. What are its foci?

- $(-3,-2)$ and $(5,-2)$
- $(1,-6)$ and $(1,2)$
- $(1,-7)$ and $(1,3)$
- $(1,-4)$ and $(1,0)$

## Explanation

We get $c=ae=4$. The foci keep the $x$-coordinate $1$, and their $y$-coordinates are $-2\pm4$.
