What is the coefficient of $x^2$ after substituting $y=2x+q$ into $\dfrac{x^2}{4}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$?

- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{17}{36}$
- $\dfrac{13}{36}$
- $\dfrac{25}{36}$

## Explanation

The term $\dfrac{(2x+q)^2}{9}$ contributes $\dfrac49x^2$, and we add $\dfrac14x^2$. The coefficients sum to $\dfrac{16+9}{36}=\dfrac{25}{36}$.
