Which pair of lines is tangent to $\dfrac{(x+2)^2}{9}+\dfrac{(y-1)^2}{16}=1$ and parallel to its major axis?

- $x=-5$ and $x=1$
- $x=-6$ and $x=2$
- $y=-3$ and $y=5$
- $x=-3$ and $x=3$

## Explanation

The major axis is vertical since $16>9$. The parallel tangents are vertical and pass through the extreme $x$-coordinates $-2-3=-5$ and $-2+3=1$.
