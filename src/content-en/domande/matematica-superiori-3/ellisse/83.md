An ellipse has $c=4$ and $e=\dfrac12$. What is the length of its major axis?

- $8$
- $16$
- $24$
- $4$

## Explanation

From $e=\dfrac ca$ we get $a=\dfrac ce=\dfrac{4}{\frac12}=8$. The axis length is $2a=16$.

The value $4$ is the distance from the centre to a focus, half the total focal separation. Since $e=\dfrac12$, it is also half the semi-major-axis length. The semi-axis measures $8$ and the full axis measures twice that.
