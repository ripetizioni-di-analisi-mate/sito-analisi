What locus results if the sum of distances to two coincident foci is $14$?

- A circle of radius $14$.
- A line.
- A circle of radius $7$.
- A single point.

## Explanation

Since the foci coincide, both distances equal the same distance $d$. The equation $2d=14$ gives $d=7$.

There is a single centre, at the point shared by the two foci. The locus of all points at the same distance $7$ from that centre is a circle. In particular, $14$ is the sum of two equal distances, each half that length.
