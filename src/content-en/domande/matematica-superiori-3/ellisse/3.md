What is the eccentricity of the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{9}+\dfrac{y^2}{25}=1$?

- $e=\dfrac45$
- $e=\dfrac43$
- $e=\dfrac35$
- $e=\dfrac{16}{25}$

## Explanation

The semi-major axis is $5$, and $c=\sqrt{25-9}=4$. The required ratio is $e=\dfrac ca=\dfrac45$.

The number compared with $c$ is the semi-major axis $a=5$, which is vertical here since the larger square is under $y^2$. The horizontal semi-axis measures $3$. The fraction $\dfrac45$ lies between $0$ and $1$, as an ellipse requires.
