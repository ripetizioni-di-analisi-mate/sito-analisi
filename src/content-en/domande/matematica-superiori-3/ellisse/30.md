How is $P(4,2)$ positioned relative to the region bounded by $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$?

- On the curve.
- Outside.
- Inside.
- It is a focus.

## Explanation

Compute $\dfrac{16}{25}+\dfrac49=\dfrac{244}{225}>1$. Since the value exceeds $1$, the point lies outside the bounded region.

The test is equivalent to rescaling the coordinates of $P$ to $\left(\dfrac45,\dfrac23\right)$ and comparing its distance from the origin with $1$. Its squared distance exceeds $1$, so the transformed point is outside the unit disk and $P$ lies outside the ellipse.
