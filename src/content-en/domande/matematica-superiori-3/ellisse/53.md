Why can the tangent formula not be applied at $(1,1)$ for $\dfrac{x^2}{4}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$?

- The two semi-axes would have to be equal.
- The $y$-coordinate would have to be zero.
- The point lies on the major axis.
- The point is not on the ellipse.

## Explanation

Substituting $(1,1)$ gives $\dfrac14+\dfrac19=\dfrac{13}{36}$, which is less than $1$. The point is inside, while the tangent formula at a point requires a point on the curve.
