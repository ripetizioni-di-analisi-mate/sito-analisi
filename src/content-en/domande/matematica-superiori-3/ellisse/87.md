Which point on $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$ has positive $y$-coordinate and is equidistant from the two foci?

- $(3,0)$
- $(0,3)$
- $(0,-3)$
- $(5,0)$

## Explanation

The foci are $(\pm4,0)$ and their perpendicular bisector is $x=0$. Substituting into the ellipse gives $y=\pm3$. The positive $y$-coordinate selects $(0,3)$.
