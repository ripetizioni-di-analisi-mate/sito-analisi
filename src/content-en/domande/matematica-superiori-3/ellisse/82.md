For which $t>0$ does $P(t,t)$ belong to the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{9}+\dfrac{y^2}{36}=1$?

- $\sqrt5$
- $\dfrac{6}{\sqrt5}$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

Substitution gives $t^2\left(\dfrac19+\dfrac1{36}\right)=1$, so $t^2=\dfrac{36}{5}$. The positive solution is $\dfrac{6}{\sqrt5}$.
