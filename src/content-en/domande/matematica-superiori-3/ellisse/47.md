For the ellipse with $a=6$ and $c=4$, someone claims a point on the curve is $11$ units from one focus and $1$ from the other. Which conclusion is correct?

- It is possible only on an axis.
- It is impossible because $11+1=12$.
- It is impossible because $11-1>8$.
- It is possible because $11+1=12$.

## Explanation

The two distances have the required sum $2a=12$, but their absolute difference is at most the focal separation $2c=8$. Here the difference is $10$, so there can be no such triangle.
