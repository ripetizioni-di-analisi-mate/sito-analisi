What is the length of the major axis of $4x^2+9y^2=144$?

- $L=12$
- $L=36$
- $L=8$
- $L=6$

## Explanation

Divide by $144$ to get $\dfrac{x^2}{36}+\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$. The semi-major axis is $\sqrt{36}=6$, so the full axis measures $12$.

The term in $x^2$ has the larger denominator, so the major-axis endpoints lie on the horizontal axis. Setting $y=0$ gives $x=\pm6$. Their distance is $6-(-6)=12$, whereas $36$ is the square of the semi-axis length.
