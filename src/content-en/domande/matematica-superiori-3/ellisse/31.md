Which point lies on $\dfrac{x^2}{9}+\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$?

- $\left(\dfrac95,\dfrac{16}{5}\right)$
- $(4,0)$
- $(3,4)$
- $(0,3)$

## Explanation

At $\left(\dfrac95,\dfrac{16}{5}\right)$ the ratios are $\dfrac9{25}$ and $\dfrac{16}{25}$, with sum $1$. The point $(3,4)$ gives sum $2$, while $(0,3)$ and $(4,0)$ give $\dfrac9{16}$ and $\dfrac{16}{9}$.
