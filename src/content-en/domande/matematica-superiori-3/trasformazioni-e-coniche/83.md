After translation by $(1,2)$, what is the perpendicular bisector of the segment from $(0,0)$ to $(4,0)$?

- $x=3$
- $x=2$
- $y=2$
- $x=1$

## Explanation

The translated endpoints are $(1,2)$ and $(5,2)$. The segment remains horizontal and the new midpoint is $(3,2)$, so the perpendicular bisector is $x=3$.

The original perpendicular bisector can also be translated directly. It was the line $x=2$, and every point gains $1$ in abscissa under the translation. The vertical component $2$ moves points along the new line without changing their common abscissa.
