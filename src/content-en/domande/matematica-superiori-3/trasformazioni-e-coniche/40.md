What locus does $x^2+4y^2=-1$ define in the real plane?

- It is an ellipse.
- It is just the origin.
- It is empty.
- It is a pair of lines.

## Explanation

The left-hand side is a sum of nonnegative terms, so it cannot equal $-1$. No real pair satisfies the equation.

The least possible value of the left-hand side is zero, attained at the origin. Even the origin therefore fails to give the required $-1$. A right-hand side of $0$ would give only $(0,0)$, while a positive right-hand side would give an ellipse.
