Which negative scale factor makes the area nine times its original value?

- $-9$
- $-3$
- $-\dfrac13$
- $-\sqrt3$

## Explanation

Area is multiplied by $\lambda^2$. Solving $\lambda^2=9$ gives $\lambda=\pm3$, so the negative scale factor is $-3$.

For example, a square of side $2$ becomes a square of side $6$, so the area changes from $4$ to $36$. The negative sign reverses directions from the center. Using factor $-9$ would instead multiply the area by $81$.
