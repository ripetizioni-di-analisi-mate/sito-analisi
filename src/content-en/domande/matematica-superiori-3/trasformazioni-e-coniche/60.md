Which point lies on both $y=x^2-2$ and the circle $x^2+y^2=8$?

- $(2,2)$
- $(2,-2)$
- $(0,-2)$
- $(1,-1)$

## Explanation

At $(2,2)$ we have $2=4-2$ and $2^2+2^2=8$. The point $(2,-2)$ is on the circle, but the parabola has ordinate $2$ at $x=2$.

Substituting the parabola’s ordinate into the circle gives $x^2+(x^2-2)^2=8$. Setting $s=x^2$ produces $(s-4)(s+1)=0$. Discarding $s=-1$ leaves abscissas $\pm2$ and ordinate $2$. Among the listed pairs is $(2,2)$.
