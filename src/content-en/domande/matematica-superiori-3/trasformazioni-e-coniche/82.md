A point is equidistant from $F=(2,0)$ and the line $x=-2$. What is the equation of the locus?

- $y^2=-8x$
- $y^2=8x$
- $x^2=8y$
- $x^2+y^2=4$

## Explanation

The squares of the distances are $(x-2)^2+y^2$ and $(x+2)^2$. Equating them and canceling $x^2+4$ gives $y^2=8x$.

Because $y^2\geq0$, the resulting parabola has $x\geq0$ and opens toward the focus. For a check, $x=2$ gives $(2,\pm4)$, both $4$ units from the focus and at distance $|2+2|=4$ from the directrix.
