Reflection in $y=x$ swaps the coordinates. Is the image of **every** point of $x^2+4y^2=4$ on the same conic, and why?

- Yes, because the conic passes through $(0,1)$
- Yes, because both terms have degree two
- Yes, because it is centred at the origin
- No, as $(2,0)$ shows

## Explanation

Point $(2,0)$ satisfies the equation, but its reflection $(0,2)$ makes the left-hand side $16$. This single point disproves invariance.
