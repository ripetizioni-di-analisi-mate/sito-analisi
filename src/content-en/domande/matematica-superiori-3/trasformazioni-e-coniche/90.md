Where do $y=x^2$ and $y=-2x-1$ touch?

- $(1,1)$
- $(-1,-1)$
- $(0,-1)$
- $(-1,1)$

## Explanation

Equating the ordinates gives $x^2+2x+1=0$, or $(x+1)^2=0$. The contact point has $x=-1$ and $y=(-1)^2=1$.

Substituting the abscissa in the line gives $-2(-1)-1=1$, the same ordinate given by the parabola. The point $(0,-1)$ is the line’s intercept, while the parabola at $x=0$ has ordinate zero. The point of tangency must satisfy both equations.
