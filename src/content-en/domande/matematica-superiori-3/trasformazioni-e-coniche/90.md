The line $y=x+5$ is tangent to the ellipse $x^2+4y^2=20$. What is the point of contact?

- $(4,9)$
- $(-4,-1)$
- $(-5,0)$
- $(-4,1)$

## Explanation

Substituting $y=x+5$ into the equation of the ellipse gives $x^2+4(x+5)^2=20$, that is, $5x^2+40x+80=0$. Dividing by $5$ we get $x^2+8x+16=(x+4)^2=0$, which has the double root $x=-4$. The double root confirms the tangency and gives the $x$-coordinate of the point of contact.

Substituting $x=-4$ into the equation of the line gives $y=1$, therefore the point of contact is $(-4,1)$. The point $(-4,-1)$ lies on the ellipse but not on the line.
