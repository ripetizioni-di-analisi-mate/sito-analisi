For which $q$ is the horizontal line $y=q$ tangent to $y=-2(x-1)^2+3$?

- $q=1$
- $q=-2$
- $q=-3$
- $q=3$

## Explanation

The parabola has maximum $3$ at $(1,3)$. The equation $-2(x-1)^2+3=q$ has a repeated root only when $(x-1)^2=0$, namely for $q=3$.

For $q<3$ the equation becomes $(x-1)^2=\dfrac{3-q}{2}>0$ and there are two points. For $q>3$ its right-hand side is negative and there are none. Only at the maximum $q=3$ do both solutions coincide at the vertex’s abscissa.
