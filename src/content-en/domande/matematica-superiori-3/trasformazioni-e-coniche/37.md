With two dilations about the same center and scale factors $-2$ and $-\dfrac12$, where does each point end up?

- At its reflection through the center.
- At the center.
- Twice as far from the center.
- At its starting point.

## Explanation

The vector from the center is first multiplied by $-2$ and then by $-\dfrac12$. Their product is $1$, so the final vector equals the original one.
