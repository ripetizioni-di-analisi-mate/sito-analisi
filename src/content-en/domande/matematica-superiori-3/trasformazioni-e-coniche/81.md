What is the perpendicular bisector of the segment from $(0,0)$ to $(6,8)$?

- $4x-3y=0$
- $3x+4y=50$
- $3x-4y=25$
- $3x+4y=25$

## Explanation

Equating $x^2+y^2$ and $(x-6)^2+(y-8)^2$ cancels the squared terms and gives $12x+16y=100$. Dividing by $4$ gives $3x+4y=25$.

The midpoint $(3,4)$ satisfies $3\cdot3+4\cdot4=25$. The form $y=-\dfrac34x+\dfrac{25}{4}$ gives slope $-\dfrac34$, whose product with the segment’s slope $\dfrac43$ is $-1$. This verifies the perpendicular line through the midpoint.
