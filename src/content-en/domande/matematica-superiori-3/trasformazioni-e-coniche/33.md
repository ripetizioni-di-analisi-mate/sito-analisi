A circle of radius $6$ is dilated by the scale factor $-\dfrac13$. What is its new radius?

- $-2$
- $18$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The radius is a distance and is multiplied by the absolute value of the scale factor. This gives $6\cdot\dfrac13=2$. The negative sign concerns the direction of vectors from the center.
