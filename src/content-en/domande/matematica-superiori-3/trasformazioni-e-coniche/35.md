Which equation describes the image of $y=x^2$ under dilation about the origin by factor $3$?

- $y=\dfrac{x^2}{3}$
- $y=3x^2$
- $y=\dfrac{x^2}{9}$
- $y=(x-3)^2$

## Explanation

The original coordinates are $\dfrac{x}{3}$ and $\dfrac{y}{3}$. The equation $\dfrac{y}{3}=\left(\dfrac{x}{3}\right)^2$ gives $y=\dfrac{x^2}{3}$.

The point $(1,1)$ moves to $(3,3)$. The new formula contains it because $y=\dfrac{3^2}{3}=3$. The expression $y=\dfrac{x^2}{9}$ would account for the change in abscissa but would give original ordinate $1$ instead of image ordinate $3$.
