Which equation describes the image of $y=x^2$ under dilation about the origin by factor $3$?

- $y=\dfrac{x^2}{3}$
- $y=3x^2$
- $y=\dfrac{x^2}{9}$
- $y=(x-3)^2$

## Explanation

The original coordinates are $\dfrac{x}{3}$ and $\dfrac{y}{3}$. The equation $\dfrac{y}{3}=\left(\dfrac{x}{3}\right)^2$ gives $y=\dfrac{x^2}{3}$.
