Translate $y=x^2$ by $(-3,2)$. What is the new axis of symmetry?

- $x=-3$
- $x=3$
- $y=2$
- $x=2$

## Explanation

The original axis $x=0$ shifts $3$ units to the left. The equation $y=(x+3)^2+2$ has vertex $(-3,2)$ and a vertical axis.

The new axis is vertical and passes through the vertex. The points at abscissas $-4$ and $-2$ have the same ordinate, since each is $1$ unit from the axis. Raising the vertex ordinate to $2$ does not change the direction of the axis.
