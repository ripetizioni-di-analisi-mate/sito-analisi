What is the center of $-x^2-4y^2+6x-8y-9=0$?

- $(3,-1)$
- $(-3,1)$
- $(3,1)$
- $(6,-2)$

## Explanation

Completing the squares gives $-(x-3)^2-4(y+1)^2+4=0$. The center makes both squares vanish, so it is $(3,-1)$.

The centered form is $(x-3)^2+4(y+1)^2=4$. The squares measure offsets from the intersection of $x=3$ and $y=-1$. The point $(3,1)$ would give $y+1=2$, so it would not make the vertical offset from the center vanish.
