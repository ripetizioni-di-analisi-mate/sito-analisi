A conic is reflected first in the line $x=1$ and then in the line $x=4$. Which single transformation maps every point of the conic to its final image?

- The translation by the vector $(6,0)$
- The translation by the vector $(3,0)$
- The translation by the vector $(-6,0)$
- The reflection in the line $x=\dfrac52$

## Explanation

The reflection in the line $x=h$ maps the point $(x,y)$ to $(2h-x,y)$. The first reflection therefore maps $(x,y)$ to $(2-x,y)$. The second maps $(2-x,y)$ to $(8-(2-x),y)=(x+6,y)$.

Therefore the composition of the two reflections is the translation by the vector $(6,0)$, whatever the conic. The vector $(3,0)$ is the displacement from the first line to the second, which is doubled in the composition.
