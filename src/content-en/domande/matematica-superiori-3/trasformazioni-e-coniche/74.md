What is the equation of the locus of points equidistant from $A=(-3,0)$ and $B=(1,0)$?

- $x=-1$
- $x=1$
- $y=-1$
- $y=0$

## Explanation

The segment is horizontal and its midpoint has abscissa $\dfrac{-3+1}{2}=-1$. The perpendicular line through that point is $x=-1$.

Equality of squared distances gives $(x+3)^2+y^2=(x-1)^2+y^2$. This leaves $6x+9=-2x+1$, hence $8x=-8$. The ordinate drops out because the fixed points have the same ordinate. Every point on the resulting vertical line therefore satisfies the condition.
