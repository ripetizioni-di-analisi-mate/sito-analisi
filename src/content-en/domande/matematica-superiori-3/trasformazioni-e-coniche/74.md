An entire conic is first reflected across $x=1$ and then across $x=4$. Which single transformation has the same effect on every point?

- Translation by $(6,0)$
- Translation by $(3,0)$
- Translation by $(-6,0)$
- Reflection across $x=\dfrac52$

## Explanation

The vertical coordinate stays fixed. The horizontal coordinate first becomes $2-x$ and then $8-(2-x)=x+6$, independently of the shape of the conic.
