What is the vertex of the parabola $x^2-6x+2y+5=0$?

- $(3,2)$
- $(-3,2)$
- $(3,-2)$
- $(2,3)$

## Explanation

Writing $x^2-6x=(x-3)^2-9$ gives $2y=4-(x-3)^2$. The square vanishes at $x=3$, where $y=2$.

Since the square is nonnegative, $y=2-\dfrac12(x-3)^2$ shows that height $2$ is the maximum. It occurs just once, at $x=3$. Substituting the point in the original equation gives $9-18+4+5=0$.
