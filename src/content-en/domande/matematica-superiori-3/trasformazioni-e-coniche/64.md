To solve $x^2+y^2=5$ and $y=x^2+1$, set $s=x^2$. Before solving the system, what domain for $s$ is imposed by this substitution alone?

- $s>0$
- $s\leq0$
- $s\in\mathbb R$
- $s\geq0$

## Explanation

The variable $s$ represents the square of a real number. Any negative root must be discarded, while $s=0$ is allowed as a square.

Every $s>0$ corresponds to two real abscissas $\pm\sqrt{s}$, while $s=0$ corresponds only to abscissa zero. Thus the substitution alone allows all of $[0,+\infty)$. The system may narrow that set once the other equations are imposed.
