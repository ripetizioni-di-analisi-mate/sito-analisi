Which formula gives the distance from $P=(x,y)$ to the line $x=-2$?

- $|x-2|$
- $|x+2|$
- $|y+2|$
- $\sqrt{x^2+(y+2)^2}$

## Explanation

The perpendicular segment to the given line is horizontal and joins $(x,y)$ to $(-2,y)$. Its length is $|x-(-2)|=|x+2|$.

For example, from $(1,4)$ the foot of the perpendicular is $(-2,4)$, giving distance $3$. From $(-4,4)$ the foot stays the same and the distance is $2$. The absolute value makes the distance positive even for a point to the left of the line.
