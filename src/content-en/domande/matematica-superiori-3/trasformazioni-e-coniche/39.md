Which type of transformation multiplies distances by $3$?

- Dilation with scale factor $-3$.
- Translation by $(3,0)$.
- Reflection across $x=3$.
- Point reflection through $(3,0)$.

## Explanation

Translations and reflections preserve distances. Dilation with factor $-3$ multiplies distances by $|-3|=3$ and reverses vectors from the center.
