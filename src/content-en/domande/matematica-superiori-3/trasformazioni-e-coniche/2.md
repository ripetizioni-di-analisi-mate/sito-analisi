Translate $x^2+y^2=16$ by $(-2,3)$ and write the new equation.

- $(x-2)^2+(y+3)^2=16$
- $(x+2)^2+(y-3)^2=4$
- $(x-2)^2+(y-3)^2=16$
- $(x+2)^2+(y-3)^2=16$

## Explanation

The new center is $(-2,3)$ and the radius stays $4$. The coordinates relative to the center are $x+2$ and $y-3$.
