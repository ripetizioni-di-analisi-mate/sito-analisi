What transformation results from reflecting across the $x$-axis and then across the $y$-axis?

- Reflection across $y=x$.
- Reflection across the $x$-axis alone.
- Point reflection through the origin.
- The identity transformation.

## Explanation

The first reflection maps $(x,y)$ to $(x,-y)$. The second gives $(-x,-y)$, the reflection through the origin.
