For which $t$ does $(x+1)^2+4(y-2)^2=t$ describe a nondegenerate ellipse?

- $t<0$
- $t=0$
- $t\ne0$
- $t>0$

## Explanation

With $t>0$, division by $t$ gives the sum of two squared terms with positive denominators equal to $1$. With $t=0$ there is one point, and with $t<0$ the locus is empty.
