Which point is fixed under dilation about $(2,-3)$ with scale factor $-4$?

- $(0,0)$
- $(-8,12)$
- $(-2,3)$
- $(2,-3)$

## Explanation

The vector from the center to itself is zero and remains zero after multiplication by $-4$. Every nonzero vector changes, so the center is the only fixed point.
