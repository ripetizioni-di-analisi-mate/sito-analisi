Which horizontal lines are tangent to $x^2+(y-1)^2=9$?

- $y=-3$ and $y=3$
- $y=-2$ and $y=4$
- $y=-4$ and $y=2$
- $y=1$ and $y=3$

## Explanation

The circle has center ordinate $1$ and radius $3$. The horizontal tangents are $3$ units from the center, so their ordinates are $1-3=-2$ and $1+3=4$.
