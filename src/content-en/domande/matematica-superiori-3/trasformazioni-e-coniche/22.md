Which equation describes $x=y^2$ translated by $(0,-4)$?

- $x=(y-4)^2$
- $x+4=y^2$
- $x=y^2-4$
- $x=(y+4)^2$

## Explanation

The original point has ordinate $y+4$ and the same abscissa $x$. Substituting into the original equation gives $x=(y+4)^2$.

The original vertex is $(0,0)$ and moves to $(0,-4)$. That point satisfies the new equation because $y+4=0$. Writing $x=y^2-4$ would instead shift the vertex’s abscissa and describe a horizontal translation.
