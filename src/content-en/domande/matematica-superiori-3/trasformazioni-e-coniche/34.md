A dilation about the origin maps $(2,-4)$ to $(-3,6)$. Find the scale factor.

- $\dfrac32$
- $-\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac23$
- $-\dfrac32$

## Explanation

The ratio of the abscissas is $\dfrac{-3}{2}$ and the ratio of the ordinates is $\dfrac6{-4}=-\dfrac32$. The negative sign agrees with the opposite rays.
