After subtracting $x^2+y^2=9$ from $(x-6)^2+y^2=9$, what abscissa must the common points have?

- $0$
- $3$
- $6$
- $9$

## Explanation

The squared terms cancel, leaving $-12x+36=0$, so $x=3$. The first equation then gives $y=0$, so there is exactly one common point.

Both circles have radius $3$ and their centers are $6$ units apart. The point $(3,0)$ is halfway between the centers and is $3$ units from each. Every other point on the line $x=3$ lies farther away.
