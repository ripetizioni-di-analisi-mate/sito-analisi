For which $c$ do $y=x^2$ and $y=c-x^2$ meet on the $y$-axis?

- $c=1$
- $c=-1$
- $c=2$
- $c=0$

## Explanation

On the $y$-axis, $x=0$. The first parabola gives $y=0$ and the second gives $y=c$, so they match when $c=0$.

The common point is exactly $(0,0)$. With $c>0$ there would be two intersections with opposite abscissas because equating ordinates gives $2x^2=c$. Neither would lie on the $y$-axis. With $c<0$ there would be no real intersections.
