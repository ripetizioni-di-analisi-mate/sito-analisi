A circle of radius $3$ is dilated by factor $2$. What is the ratio of the new circumference length to the original length?

- $4$
- $2$
- $6$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The radius changes from $3$ to $6$, so the ratio between lengths $2\pi\cdot6$ and $2\pi\cdot3$ is $2$. The factor $4$ applies to the areas.
