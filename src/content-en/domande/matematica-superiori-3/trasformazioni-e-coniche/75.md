Apply $(x,y)\mapsto(2x,y-3)$ to each point of $y=x^2$. Which equation describes the resulting curve in the new coordinates $(X,Y)$?

- $Y=4X^2-3$
- $Y=\dfrac{X^2}{4}-3$
- $Y=\dfrac{X^2}{4}+3$
- $Y=2X^2-3$

## Explanation

Recover $x=\dfrac X2$ and $y=Y+3$ from the transformed coordinates. The original equation becomes $Y+3=(\dfrac X2)^2$.
