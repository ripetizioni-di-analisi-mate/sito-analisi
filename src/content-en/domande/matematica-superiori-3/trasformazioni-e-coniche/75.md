The transformation $(x,y)\mapsto(X,Y)=(2x,\,y-3)$ is applied to every point of the parabola $y=x^2$. What is the equation of the image curve?

- $Y=4X^2-3$
- $Y=\dfrac{X^2}{4}-3$
- $Y=\dfrac{X^2}{4}+3$
- $Y=2X^2-3$

## Explanation

To write the equation of the image we express the old coordinates in terms of the new ones, so from $X=2x$ and $Y=y-3$ we get $x=\dfrac X2$ and $y=Y+3$. Substituting into the equation $y=x^2$ gives
$$
Y+3=\left(\frac X2\right)^2=\frac{X^2}{4} .
$$
Therefore the image curve is $Y=\dfrac{X^2}{4}-3$. The equation $Y=4X^2-3$ comes from substituting $x=2X$, that is, from applying the transformation in the opposite direction.
