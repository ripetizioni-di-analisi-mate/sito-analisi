Which equation describes points equidistant from $F=(0,1)$ and the line $y=-1$?

- $x^2=-4y$
- $x^2=4y$
- $y^2=4x$
- $x^2+y^2=1$

## Explanation

Equality of distances gives $x^2+(y-1)^2=(y+1)^2$. The $y^2$ terms and constant $1$ cancel, leaving $x^2=4y$.

The value of $y$ must be at least zero because $x^2\geq0$. The curve opens toward the focus, which lies above the directrix. The point $(2,1)$ satisfies the definition because it is $2$ units from the focus and $2$ from $y=-1$.
