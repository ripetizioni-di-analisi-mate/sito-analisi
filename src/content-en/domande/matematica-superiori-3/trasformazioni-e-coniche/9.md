What locus does the equation $4(x-1)^2+9(y+2)^2=0$ describe?

- Only the point $(-1,2)$.
- An ellipse.
- The empty locus.
- Only the point $(1,-2)$.

## Explanation

Both terms are greater than or equal to zero, so both must vanish. This gives $x=1$ and $y=-2$.

The pair $(1,-2)$ satisfies the equation because both terms are zero. At every other pair at least one offset is nonzero, making at least one term positive. An ellipse with two positive semiaxes would require a positive right-hand side.
