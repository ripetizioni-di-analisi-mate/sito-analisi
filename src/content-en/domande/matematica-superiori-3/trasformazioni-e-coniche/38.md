For a dilation about $(1,1)$ by factor $2$, which original point has image $(7,5)$?

- $(3,2)$
- $(4,3)$
- $(13,9)$
- $(8,6)$

## Explanation

The image vector relative to the center is $(6,4)$. Halving it gives $(3,2)$, then adding the center gives $(4,3)$.

To check, subtract the center from $(4,3)$ and double $(3,2)$ to get $(6,4)$. Adding the center returns $(7,5)$. The pair $(3,2)$ is only the displacement from the center to the original point.
