Which quadratic equation describes a parabola with a vertical axis?

- $2x^2-4x+3=0$
- $2y^2-x+3=0$
- $2x^2+2y^2-3=0$
- $2x^2-y+3=0$

## Explanation

From $2x^2-y+3=0$ we can isolate $y=2x^2+3$, which has a vertical axis. From $2y^2-x+3=0$ we instead isolate $x=2y^2+3$, giving a parabola with a horizontal axis. The equation $2x^2+2y^2-3=0$ describes a circle.
