When $t<0$, in which directions do the branches of $9(x-1)^2-(y+2)^2=t$ open?

- To the right and left.
- Upward and downward.
- Only upward.
- The equation describes a single point.

## Explanation

Dividing by $t<0$ makes the coefficient of $(y+2)^2$ positive. The transverse axis is therefore the vertical line $x=1$, and the branches are on opposite sides of $y=-2$.
