The line $x=2$ is tangent to $x^2+y^2=4$. What is the image line when the line and circle are both translated by $(-1,3)$?

- $x=3$
- $y=3$
- $x=1$
- $x=-1$

## Explanation

Every point on the line loses one unit in its abscissa, so $2$ becomes $1$. The contact point $(2,0)$ moves to $(1,3)$ and translation preserves tangency.
