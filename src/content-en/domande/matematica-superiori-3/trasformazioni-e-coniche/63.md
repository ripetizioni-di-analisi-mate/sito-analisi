Which marked point lies on both curves in the figure?

::grafico{f="x^2;2-x^2" xmin=-2.5 xmax=2.8 ymin=-2.7 ymax=4 uguale=1 punti="0,0,pieno,A;1,1,pieno,B;0,2,pieno,C;2,-2,pieno,D" titolo="Two curves and four marked points."}

- $B$
- $A$
- $C$
- $D$

## Explanation

The point $B=(1,1)$ lies on both curves: $1=1^2$ and $1=2-1^2$. Each other marked point lies on at most one curve.
