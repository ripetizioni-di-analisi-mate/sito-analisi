For $A=(2,0)$ and $B=(0,2)$, which line is the locus of points $P$ with $PA=PB$?

- $y=-x+2$
- $x=1$
- $y=1$
- $y=x$

## Explanation

Equality of squared distances gives $(x-2)^2+y^2=x^2+(y-2)^2$. Cancellation leaves $-4x=-4y$, so $y=x$.

Segment $AB$ has slope $-1$, because going from $A$ to $B$ decreases the abscissa by $2$ and increases the ordinate by $2$. Its perpendicular bisector has slope $1$ and passes through the midpoint $(1,1)$. The line $y=x$ satisfies both conditions.
