What is the image center of a circle centered at $(2,1)$ after reflection across $x=4$ followed by translation by $(1,-3)$?

- $(5,-2)$
- $(7,-2)$
- $(7,4)$
- $(3,-2)$

## Explanation

The reflection gives $(8-2,1)=(6,1)$. Adding $(1,-3)$ then gives $(7,-2)$.

The center’s first image must be two units right of $x=4$, because the original center is two units left. Only then is $1$ added to the reflected abscissa. Reversing the operations would give abscissa $3$ first and then $5$ by reflection.
