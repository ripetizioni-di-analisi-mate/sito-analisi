What equation results from reflecting $y=2x^2$ across $y=x$?

- $y=-2x^2$
- $x=-2y^2$
- $y=\dfrac{x^2}{2}$
- $x=2y^2$

## Explanation

The coordinates are swapped. The original ordinate becomes $x$ and the original abscissa becomes $y$, giving $x=2y^2$.

The original point $(1,2)$ moves to $(2,1)$. The new form checks by $2=2\cdot1^2$. The axis becomes horizontal because the vertical line through the vertex is reflected to a horizontal line. Changing just the sign of $y$ would leave the axis vertical.
