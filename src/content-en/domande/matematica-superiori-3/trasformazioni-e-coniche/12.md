Find the line containing the intersections of $x^2+y^2=10$ and $(x-2)^2+y^2=10$.

- $x=2$
- $x=1$
- $y=1$
- $y=0$

## Explanation

Subtracting the first equation from the second gives $-4x+4=0$, hence $x=1$. Substitution gives $y^2=9$, so the line contains the two points $(1,\pm3)$.
