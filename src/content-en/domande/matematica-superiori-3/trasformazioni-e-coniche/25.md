Which line reflects $P=(-2,4)$ to $P'=(6,4)$?

- $x=2$
- $y=4$
- $x=4$
- $x=-2$

## Explanation

Their joining segment is horizontal. Its midpoint is $(2,4)$, so the line perpendicular to it at that point is $x=2$.

Both points are $4$ units from the line $x=2$ and have the same ordinate. The line $y=4$ contains the points themselves and would leave them fixed. The required reflection must swap the two points along their horizontal joining segment.
