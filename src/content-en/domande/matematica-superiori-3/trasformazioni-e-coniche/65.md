Find the ordinate of the tangency point of $y=x^2$ and $y=6x-9$.

- $9$
- $3$
- $-9$
- $18$

## Explanation

Equating the ordinates gives $x^2-6x+9=(x-3)^2=0$. The repeated root is $3$, so $y=3^2=9$.

The line gives the same height there because $6\cdot3-9=9$. The value $3$ is the abscissa that makes the square vanish, and it must be substituted to obtain the ordinate. The difference stays a nonnegative square at all abscissas.
