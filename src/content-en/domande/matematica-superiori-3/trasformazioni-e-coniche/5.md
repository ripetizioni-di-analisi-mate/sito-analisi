Find the reflection of $(2,-1)$ across the line $x=5$.

- $(-2,-1)$
- $(8,1)$
- $(3,-1)$
- $(8,-1)$

## Explanation

The point is $3$ units to the left of $x=5$. The image keeps the same ordinate and lies $3$ units to the right, at abscissa $5+3=8$.

To check the point, verify that the mean of abscissas $2$ and $8$ is $5$. Simply changing the sign of the abscissa would give $-2$, which is reflection across the $y$-axis instead of the given vertical line.
