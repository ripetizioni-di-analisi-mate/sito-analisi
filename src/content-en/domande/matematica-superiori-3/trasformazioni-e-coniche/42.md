What locus does $(x-2)^2-9(y+1)^2=0$ describe?

- Two intersecting lines.
- Two parallel lines.
- A parabola.
- Only the point $(2,-1)$.

## Explanation

The difference of squares vanishes when $x-2=3(y+1)$ or $x-2=-3(y+1)$. The two distinct lines meet at $(2,-1)$.

The first case gives $y+1=\dfrac{x-2}{3}$ and the second $y+1=-\dfrac{x-2}{3}$. The slopes are different, so the lines are not parallel. The point $(2,-1)$ satisfies both, but either factor can vanish at other points as well.
