What is the right-hand side $R$ in the form $4(x-2)^2+(y+3)^2=R$ of $4x^2+y^2-16x+6y+9=0$?

- $25$
- $7$
- $-16$
- $16$

## Explanation

The $x$ square produces constant $-16$, the $y$ square produces $-9$, and $+9$ is already present. Their sum is $-16$, so the right-hand side is $16$.
