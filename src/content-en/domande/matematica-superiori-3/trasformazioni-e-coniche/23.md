What is the image of $(3,-2)$ under reflection across $y=x$?

- $(2,-3)$
- $(-2,3)$
- $(-3,-2)$
- $(3,2)$

## Explanation

Reflection across $y=x$ swaps the coordinates. The midpoint of $(3,-2)$ and $(-2,3)$ is $(\dfrac12,\dfrac12)$, on the mirror line.

The segment joining the point and its reflection has coordinate changes $-5$ and $5$, so its slope is $-1$. It is perpendicular to $y=x$, whose slope is $1$. Changing only one sign would preserve one coordinate, as in reflection across a coordinate axis.
