Complete the square in $4x^2-16x$. Which expression results?

- $4(x-2)^2-4$
- $4(x-4)^2-64$
- $4(x-2)^2-16$
- $4(x+2)^2-16$

## Explanation

Factoring out $4$ leaves $x^2-4x=(x-2)^2-4$. The subtracted term is multiplied by $4$, giving $16$.

Expanding $4(x-2)^2$ gives $4x^2-16x+16$, so $16$ must be subtracted from the entire expression. Subtracting only $4$ would leave an extra $12$ and change the polynomial. Plugging in $x=0$ also checks that its original value must be zero.
