If $Ax^2+Cy^2+Dx+Ey+F=0$ is a circle of positive radius, which condition on the quadratic coefficients is necessary?

- $A=C\ne0$
- $A=-C\ne0$
- $AC<0$
- $A=0$

## Explanation

In a circle the squares of both coordinates relative to the center have the same coefficient. Expanding preserves that equality, so $A=C\ne0$.

The condition is necessary because the same distance from the center applies in every direction. By itself, it does not guarantee a positive radius. For example, $x^2+y^2=-1$ has equal coefficients, but no point in the real plane satisfies it.
