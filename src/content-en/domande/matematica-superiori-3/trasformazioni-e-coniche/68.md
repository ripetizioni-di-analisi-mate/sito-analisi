Substituting a line into a nondegenerate conic gives $2x^2-4x+2=0$. What does this equation indicate?

- A tangency at abscissa $2$.
- Two distinct intersections.
- A tangency at abscissa $1$.
- No intersection.

## Explanation

The left-hand side is $2(x-1)^2$. The quadratic coefficient is nonzero and the only root is repeated, so the contact has abscissa $1$.
