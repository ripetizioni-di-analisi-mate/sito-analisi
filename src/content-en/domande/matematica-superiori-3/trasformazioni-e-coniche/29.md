Which point in the figure is the reflection of $P$ across $x=1$?

::grafico{xmin=-4 xmax=5 ymin=-3 ymax=4 uguale=1 asintoti="x=1" punti="3,2,pieno,P;-1,2,pieno,A;3,-2,pieno,B;-2,2,pieno,D;-1,-2,pieno,E" titolo="Five points and the vertical line $x=1$."}

- $B$
- $D$
- $A$
- $E$

## Explanation

The point $P=(3,2)$ is two units to the right of the line. Its reflection keeps $y=2$ and has abscissa $1-2=-1$, so it is $A$.
