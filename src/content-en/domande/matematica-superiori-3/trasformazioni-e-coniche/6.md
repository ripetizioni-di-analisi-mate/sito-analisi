Dilate $P=(4,3)$ about $C=(2,1)$ by the scale factor $-2$.

- $(-8,-6)$
- $(6,5)$
- $(-2,-3)$
- $(-4,-4)$

## Explanation

The vector from $C$ to $P$ is $(2,2)$. Multiplying by $-2$ gives $(-4,-4)$, which is then added to $C$. The point is $(-2,-3)$.

The point lies on the same line as $P$ and $C$, on the opposite side of the center from $P$. Its distance from $C$ doubles, as the change from $(2,2)$ to $(-4,-4)$ shows. The pair $(-4,-4)$ is the relative vector, not the image point.
