Which line of slope $2$ is tangent to $y=(x-1)^2$?

- $y=2x-1$
- $y=2x+3$
- $y=2x-3$
- $y=2x$

## Explanation

With $y=2x+q$, we get $(x-1)^2-2x-q=x^2-4x+1-q$. The discriminant is $16-4(1-q)=12+4q$, which vanishes at $q=-3$.

At $q=-3$ the ordinate difference is $x^2-4x+4=(x-2)^2$, so the only contact is $(2,1)$. The line $y=2x-1$ would be tangent to $y=x^2$, whose vertex is at the origin, whereas the given parabola is shifted right.
