Which conic consists of the points $5$ units from $(2,-1)$?

- $(x+2)^2+(y-1)^2=25$
- $(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2=5$
- $(x-2)^2-(y+1)^2=25$
- $(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2=25$

## Explanation

The square of the distance from the given point is $(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2$. Requiring distance $5$ sets this sum equal to $5^2=25$.
