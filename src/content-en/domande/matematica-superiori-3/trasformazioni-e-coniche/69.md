Why is the single common point of $y=x+1$ and $x^2-y^2=1$ insufficient to conclude that the line is tangent?

- Substitution gives a linear equation.
- The resulting equation is quadratic with a repeated root.
- The curve is degenerate.
- The two equations coincide.

## Explanation

Substitution gives $x^2-(x+1)^2=1$, so $-2x-1=1$. The quadratic terms cancel, so the sole abscissa $-1$ is a simple root.
