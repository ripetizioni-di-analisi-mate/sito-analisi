Under translation by $(3,-2)$, which original point has image $(4,1)$?

- $(7,-1)$
- $(1,-1)$
- $(4,1)$
- $(1,3)$

## Explanation

Apply the inverse translation by $(-3,2)$. This gives $(4-3,1+2)=(1,3)$.

The image must lie three units right and two down from the point being sought. Indeed $(1+3,3-2)=(4,1)$. Applying the same vector to the image would give $(7,-1)$, farther forward in the direction of the shift.
