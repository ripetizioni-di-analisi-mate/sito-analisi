Write the image equation of $y=(x-2)^2+1$ under reflection across the $x$-axis.

- $y=(x+2)^2+1$
- $y=-(x-2)^2+1$
- $y=-(x-2)^2-1$
- $y=(x-2)^2-1$

## Explanation

Replace the original $y$ by $-y$. Thus $-y=(x-2)^2+1$, or $y=-(x-2)^2-1$. The vertex $(2,1)$ moves to $(2,-1)$.

For example, the original point $(3,2)$ must become $(3,-2)$. The new expression gives $-(3-2)^2-1=-2$. If the constant term kept its positive sign, the vertex would stay at ordinate $1$ and would not be the required reflection.
