What are the horizontal and vertical semiaxes, in that order, of $9(x+2)^2+4(y-1)^2=36$?

- $(3,2)$
- $(4,9)$
- $(2,3)$
- $(9,4)$

## Explanation

Dividing by $36$ gives denominator $4$ under the $x$ square and $9$ under the $y$ square. Their positive square roots are $2$ and $3$.
