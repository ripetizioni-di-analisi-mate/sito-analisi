What are the common points of $y=x^2+1$ and $y=9-x^2$?

- $(\pm2,4)$
- $(\pm\sqrt8,9)$
- $(\pm2,5)$
- $(\pm4,5)$

## Explanation

Equating the ordinates gives $2x^2=8$, hence $x=\pm2$. In the first equation both abscissas give $y=4+1=5$.

The other equation gives the same ordinate because $9-(\pm2)^2=9-4=5$. The abscissas have opposite signs by symmetry, but the ordinate stays the same because both formulas depend on $x^2$. The number $4$ is the squared abscissa, not the final ordinate.
