What is the center of the conic $9x^2+4y^2+36x-8y+4=0$?

- $(-2,1)$
- $(2,-1)$
- $(-4,2)$
- $(-2,-1)$

## Explanation

Factoring and completing the squares gives $9((x+2)^2-4)+4((y-1)^2-1)+4=0$. Both squares vanish at $(-2,1)$.

The centered form is $9(x+2)^2+4(y-1)^2=36$, so the ellipse is symmetric about $x=-2$ and $y=1$. The center is their intersection. The point $(-2,-1)$ does not make the second square vanish, because $-1-1=-2$.
