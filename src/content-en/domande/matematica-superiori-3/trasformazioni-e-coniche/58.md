Which points lie on both $x^2+y^2=5$ and $x^2-y^2=3$?

- $(\pm1,\pm2)$ with independent signs.
- $(\pm2,\pm1)$ with independent signs.
- $(\pm4,\pm1)$ with independent signs.
- Only $(2,1)$ and $(-2,-1)$.

## Explanation

Addition gives $2x^2=8$, so $x^2=4$. Subtraction gives $2y^2=2$, so $y^2=1$. Both equations depend only on the squares, so every sign combination is allowed.
