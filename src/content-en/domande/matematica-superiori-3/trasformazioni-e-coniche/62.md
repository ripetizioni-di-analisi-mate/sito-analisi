Subtracting the equations of two circles with distinct centers gives a line. Why must the system retain the equation of one of the circles as well?

- The line always contains only the common points.
- Subtraction erases the coordinates of every common point.
- The line can also contain points outside both circles.
- The line must be parallel to a tangent to each circle.

## Explanation

Every solution of the system lies on the line, but a point on the line can be a different distance from the center than the radius. The equation of one circle also imposes that distance.
