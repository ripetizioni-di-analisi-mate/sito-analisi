First reflect $(1,2)$ across the $x$-axis, then translate by $(0,3)$. Where does the point end up?

- $(1,-5)$
- $(1,5)$
- $(-1,1)$
- $(1,1)$

## Explanation

Reflection gives $(1,-2)$. Translating that point adds $3$ to the ordinate, so the result is $(1,1)$. Reversing the order would give a different point.
