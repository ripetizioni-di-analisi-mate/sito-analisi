After translating $xy=8$ by $(2,-1)$, which listed point lies on the image curve?

- $(2,-1)$
- $(4,-3)$
- $(2,4)$
- $(4,3)$

## Explanation

For $(4,3)$ the original coordinates are $(4-2,3+1)=(2,4)$. Their product is $8$, so the point satisfies $(x-2)(y+1)=8$.

The point $(2,-1)$ is where the translated asymptotes meet, so its product of offsets is zero. At $(2,4)$ the first factor is zero as well. The pair $(4,-3)$ instead has product $2(-2)=-4$, opposite in sign to the required constant.
