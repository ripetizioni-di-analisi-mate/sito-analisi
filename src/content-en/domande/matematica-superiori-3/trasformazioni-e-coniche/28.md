Which point is the reflection of $P=(5,-1)$ through $C=(2,3)$?

- $(-5,1)$
- $(-1,7)$
- $(3,-4)$
- $(1,-7)$

## Explanation

The center is the midpoint. The image coordinates are $2\cdot2-5=-1$ and $2\cdot3-(-1)=7$.

The vector from $C$ to $P$ is $(3,-4)$. Its opposite, added to $C$, gives $(-1,7)$. The pair $(-5,1)$ would come from changing both original signs without taking account of the given center.
