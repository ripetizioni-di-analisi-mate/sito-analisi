Which translation takes $y=2x^2$ to $y=2(x+3)^2-4$?

- $(-3,-4)$
- $(3,-4)$
- $(-3,4)$
- $(3,4)$

## Explanation

The vertex moves from $(0,0)$ to $(-3,-4)$ and the quadratic coefficient stays $2$. Thus $h=-3$ and $k=-4$.

To check the direction, track $(1,2)$, which moves to $(-2,-2)$ under the vector found. At $x=-2$ the new expression gives $2(-2+3)^2-4=-2$. The plus sign inside the square indicates a shift to the left.
