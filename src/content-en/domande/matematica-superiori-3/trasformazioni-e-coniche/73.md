Which line through $P=(0,-1)$ is tangent to $y=x^2$ and has positive slope?

- $y=-2x-1$
- $y=x-1$
- $y=2x-1$
- $y=2x+1$

## Explanation

The line has form $y=mx-1$. The system gives $x^2-mx+1=0$, so tangency requires $m^2-4=0$. The positive slope is $m=2$.

The line for $m=2$ is $y=2x-1$. The difference $x^2-(2x-1)=(x-1)^2$ vanishes at $x=1$, giving contact at $(1,1)$. The value $m=-2$ would also give a tangent through the given point, but with negative slope.
