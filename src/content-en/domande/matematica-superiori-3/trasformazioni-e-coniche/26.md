Reflect the circle $(x-1)^2+(y+2)^2=9$ across the $y$-axis. Where is the image center?

- $(1,2)$
- $(-1,2)$
- $(-1,-2)$
- $(-2,1)$

## Explanation

The initial center is $(1,-2)$. Reflection across the $y$-axis changes only the abscissa’s sign, giving $(-1,-2)$.

Substituting $-x$ for $x$ gives $(-x-1)^2+(y+2)^2=9$. Since $(-x-1)^2=(x+1)^2$, both squares vanish at the new center $(-1,-2)$. The radius remains $3$ because reflection preserves all distances.
