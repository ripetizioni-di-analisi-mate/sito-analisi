Find the vertex of the horizontal parabola $2y^2+8y-x+5=0$.

- $(-3,-2)$
- $(3,-2)$
- $(-2,-3)$
- $(-3,2)$

## Explanation

This gives $x=2((y+2)^2-4)+5=2(y+2)^2-3$. The square vanishes at $y=-2$, where $x=-3$.

The equation gives the abscissa as a function of the ordinate. Since the squared term has positive coefficient, $x=-3$ is its minimum and the parabola opens right. Swapping the vertex coordinates would give a different point and a different position of the horizontal axis.
