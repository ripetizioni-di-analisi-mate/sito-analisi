A shape has area $20\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. What is the area, in square centimetres, of its image under a dilation with scale factor $-\dfrac12$?

- $A=10$
- $A=5$
- $A=-10$
- $A=80$

## Explanation

Every length is halved and the area is multiplied by $\left(-\dfrac12\right)^2=\dfrac14$. The product is $20\cdot\dfrac14=5$.

To check the factor, consider a rectangle with sides $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The new lengths are $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $\dfrac52\,\mathrm{cm}$, so their product is $5\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. The sign reverses directions from the center, while the area remains positive.
