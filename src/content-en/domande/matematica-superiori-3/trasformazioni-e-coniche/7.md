A shape has area $20\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. Find its area after dilation by the scale factor $-\dfrac12$. Give the area in square centimetres.

- $A=10$
- $A=5$
- $A=-10$
- $A=80$

## Explanation

Every length is halved and the area is multiplied by $\left(-\dfrac12\right)^2=\dfrac14$. The product is $20\cdot\dfrac14=5$.
