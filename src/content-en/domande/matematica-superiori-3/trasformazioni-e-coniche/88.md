When a conic centered at $(1,-2)$ is reflected across $y=x$, where does its center go?

- $(-1,2)$
- $(2,-1)$
- $(-2,1)$
- $(1,2)$

## Explanation

Reflection swaps the coordinates of every point. In particular, the image center has abscissa $-2$ and ordinate $1$.

The segment joining the original and reflected centers has midpoint $\left(-\dfrac12,-\dfrac12\right)$, on $y=x$. The two centers therefore lie on opposite sides of the bisector. Changing the signs of the coordinates would instead give reflection through the origin.
