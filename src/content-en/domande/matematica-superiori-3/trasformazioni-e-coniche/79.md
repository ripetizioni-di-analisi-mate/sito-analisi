What is the locus of points equidistant from the lines $y=1$ and $y=5$?

- $y=3$
- $y=2$
- $x=3$
- $y=4$

## Explanation

The distances are $|y-1|$ and $|y-5|$. Equating their squares gives $(y-1)^2=(y-5)^2$, or $8y=24$, so $y=3$.

At $y=3$ there are two vertical units to both the lower and upper lines. The distances stay equal for all $x$ because the lines are horizontal. The locus therefore has no single fixed abscissa.
