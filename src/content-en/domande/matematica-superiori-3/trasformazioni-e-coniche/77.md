What type of locus has a sum of distances to two foci of $12$, when the foci are $10$ units apart?

- A hyperbola.
- A parabola.
- An ellipse.
- A pair of lines.

## Explanation

The focal definition of an ellipse requires a constant sum greater than the distance between the foci. Here $12>10$, so the locus is an ellipse.

We have $2a=12$ and $2c=10$, so $a=6$ and $c=5$. The square of the minor semiaxis is $a^2-c^2=36-25=11>0$. If the sum were $10$, only the segment joining the foci would remain, by equality in the triangle inequality.
