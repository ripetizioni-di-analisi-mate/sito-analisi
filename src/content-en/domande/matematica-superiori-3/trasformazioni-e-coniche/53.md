For which values of $t$ is the locus $x^2+y^2-4x+2y+t=0$ empty?

- $t\geq5$
- $t<5$
- $t=5$
- $t>5$

## Explanation

The equation becomes $(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2=5-t$. The locus is empty exactly when $5-t<0$, so $t>5$. At $t=5$ the locus contains just the center.

For $t<5$ the locus is a circle of radius $\sqrt{5-t}$. Increasing $t$ to $5$ reduces the radius to zero and leaves $(2,-1)$. That point satisfies the equation, so $5$ is excluded from the interval of parameter values giving an empty locus.
