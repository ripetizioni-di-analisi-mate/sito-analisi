For which $t$ do the circle $x^2+y^2=4$ and the line $y=t$ have two common points?

- $-2\leq t\leq2$
- $-2<t<2$
- $t>2$
- $-4<t<4$

## Explanation

Substituting gives $x^2=4-t^2$. Two distinct roots require $4-t^2>0$, or $|t|<2$. At either endpoint the line is tangent.

For example, at $t=0$ the line contains a diameter and meets the curve at $(\pm2,0)$. At $t=2$ only $(0,2)$ remains, so the endpoint must be excluded. Lines with $|t|>2$ lie beyond the highest or lowest points.
