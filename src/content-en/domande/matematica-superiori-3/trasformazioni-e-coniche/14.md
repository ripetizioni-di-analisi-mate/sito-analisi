What is the locus of points equidistant from $(2,-1)$ and $(2,7)$?

- $x=2$
- $y=3$
- $y=4$
- $y=-3$

## Explanation

The segment is vertical and its midpoint is $(2,\dfrac{-1+7}{2})=(2,3)$. Its perpendicular bisector is therefore the horizontal line $y=3$.

To check the line algebraically, one sets $(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2=(x-2)^2+(y-7)^2$. The $x$ term cancels, and simplifying gives $16y=48$. The abscissa is unrestricted, so the locus contains the whole horizontal line, not just the midpoint.
