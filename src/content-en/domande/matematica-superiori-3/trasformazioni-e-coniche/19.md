Points $P=(1,3)$ and $Q=(4,7)$ undergo the same translation. How far apart are their images?

- $5$
- $7$
- $\sqrt7$
- $25$

## Explanation

A translation preserves coordinate differences. The distance follows from $Q-P=(3,4)$ and equals $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$.

Adding the absolute differences gives $3+4=7$, the length of a path made of one horizontal and one vertical segment. The direct segment is the hypotenuse of the right triangle with those legs. The shift shared by both endpoints preserves that triangle’s shape.
