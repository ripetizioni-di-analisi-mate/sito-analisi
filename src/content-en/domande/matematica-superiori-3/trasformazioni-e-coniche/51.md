What is the radius of the circle $x^2+y^2+2x-6y-6=0$?

- $16$
- $4$
- $\sqrt6$
- $2$

## Explanation

Completing the square gives $(x+1)^2-1+(y-3)^2-9=6$, so $(x+1)^2+(y-3)^2=16$. The radius is $\sqrt{16}=4$.

The center is $(-1,3)$. For a check, $(3,3)$ lies four units to its right and gives $9+9+6-18-6=0$ in the original equation. The number $16$ is the square of the radius, so it represents a squared distance.
