Find the image of $P=(-3,4)$ under translation by $(2,-5)$.

- $(-5,9)$
- $(-1,-1)$
- $(-1,9)$
- $(2,-5)$

## Explanation

Add corresponding components. The abscissa is $-3+2=-1$ and the ordinate is $4-5=-1$.

The horizontal component of the shift is positive and the vertical component negative. Moving right from $-3$ gives $-1$, and moving down from $4$ gives $-1$. Subtracting the original coordinates from the resulting ones recovers exactly $(2,-5)$.
