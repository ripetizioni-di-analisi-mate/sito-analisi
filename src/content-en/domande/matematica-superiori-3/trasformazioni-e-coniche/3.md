Which vector translates a circle’s center $(1,-4)$ to its image center $(-2,5)$?

- $(-3,9)$
- $(3,-9)$
- $(-1,1)$
- $(-3,1)$

## Explanation

The original center coordinates are subtracted from the image center coordinates. This gives $(-2-1,5-(-4))=(-3,9)$.

Horizontally the move is from $1$ to $-2$, or $3$ units left. Vertically it is from $-4$ to $5$, or $9$ units up. The opposite vector would send the image back to its original center.
