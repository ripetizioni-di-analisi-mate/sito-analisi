When completing the square in $-3y^2+12y$, which constant remains outside $-3(y-2)^2$?

- $12$
- $-12$
- $4$
- $-4$

## Explanation

Write $-3[y^2-4y]=-3[(y-2)^2-4]$. The outside term is $(-3)(-4)=12$.

Substituting $y=2$ checks the value. The square $(y-2)^2$ is zero, while the original polynomial gives $-3\cdot4+12\cdot2=12$. Thus the outside constant must be exactly $12$, with a positive sign.
