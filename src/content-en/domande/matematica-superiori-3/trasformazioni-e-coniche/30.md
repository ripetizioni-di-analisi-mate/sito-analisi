Which reflection transforms $xy=6$ into $xy=-6$ and preserves the abscissa of every point?

- Reflection across the $x$-axis.
- Reflection through the origin.
- Reflection across $y=x$.
- Reflection across $y=-x$.

## Explanation

Reflection across the $x$-axis replaces $y$ by $-y$, giving $-xy=6$, equivalent to $xy=-6$. Changing both signs or swapping the coordinates preserves the product.
