For what value of $k$ is the range of $f(x)=\sqrt{x}+k$ on $[0,+\infty)$ equal to $[-2,+\infty)$?

- $-2$
- $2$
- $0$
- $4$

## Explanation

The square root on $[0,+\infty)$ takes every nonnegative value and equals $0$ at $0$. Adding $k$ shifts the range to $[k,+\infty)$, so $k=-2$. The desired minimum is $-2$, and it is included in the range. It must therefore equal the function's value at $x=0$. Once $k=-2$ is chosen, each $y\geq-2$ is attained at input $(y+2)^2$, which gives output $y$.
