Let $f(x)=2x+1$ for $0\leq x<1$ and $f(x)=4-x$ for $1\leq x\leq3$. Find the maximum of $f$.

- $\max f=3$
- There is no maximum.
- $\max f=4$
- $\max f=1$

## Explanation

The first piece satisfies $2x+1<3$. On the second piece, $4-x$ decreases and its largest value is $4-1=3$, attained at $x=1$.
