If $f$ is even, $f(-3)=4$ and $g(x)=f(x)-x$, what is $g(3)$?

- $-1$
- $1$
- $7$
- $-7$

## Explanation

Evenness gives $f(3)=f(-3)=4$. The second rule therefore gives $g(3)=4-3=1$. Evenness applies to $f$, so it lets us move from a negative input to the corresponding positive one. The subtracted term in the definition of $g$ is the requested input $3$. Using $-3$ there as well would give $g(-3)=7$.
