The area in square metres of a rectangle is $A(x)=x(6-x)$ with $0<x<6$. What is its maximum value?

- $9$
- $18$
- $12$
- $6$

## Explanation

Completing the square gives $A(x)=9-(x-3)^2$. The square is smallest at the allowed input $x=3$, and the area reaches $9$ there. At $x=3$ the other side is also $6-3=3$, so the rectangle of maximum area is a square. At every other allowed length the subtracted term is positive, so the area is strictly below $9$.
