For the relation $x^2+y^2=9$, which pair of points shows that the entire curve is not the graph of a function of $x$?

- $(3,0)$ and $(-3,0)$
- $(0,0)$ and $(0,3)$
- $(0,3)$ and $(3,0)$
- $(0,3)$ and $(0,-3)$

## Explanation

At $x=0$ the equation becomes $y^2=9$ and gives two outputs, $3$ and $-3$. The vertical line $x=0$ therefore meets the curve twice.
