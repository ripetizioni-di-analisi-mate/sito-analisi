For $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$ and $g(t)=\dfrac{1}{t}$, which pair gives the correct formula and domain of $f\circ g$?

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{t}}$ on $[0,+\infty)$
- $\dfrac{1}{t}$ on $(0,+\infty)$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{t}}$ on $(0,+\infty)$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{t}}$ on $\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$

## Explanation

First compute $\dfrac{1}{t}$, which requires $t\neq0$. Its square root exists if $\dfrac{1}{t}\geq0$, which is equivalent to $t>0$ once $t\neq0$ is imposed.
