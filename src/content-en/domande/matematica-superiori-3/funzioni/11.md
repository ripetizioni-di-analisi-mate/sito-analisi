Let $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$ and $g(t)=t^2+1$. For which real $x$ is the composition $g\circ f$ defined?

- $(0,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $[0,+\infty)$
- $[1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The composition first requires $x\geq0$ to evaluate $\sqrt x$. The outer function accepts every real value. The resulting formula is $x+1$, defined as a composition only for $x\geq0$. At the boundary $x=0$, the inner function gives $0$ and the outer one gives $g(0)=1$, so the endpoint is included. At $x=-1$ the square root $\sqrt{x}$ is not defined over the reals, even though the simplified expression $x+1$ could be evaluated.
