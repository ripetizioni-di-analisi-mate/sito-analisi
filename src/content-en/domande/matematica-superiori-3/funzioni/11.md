Let $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$ and $g(t)=t^2+1$. Find the domain of $g\circ f$.

- $(0,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $[0,+\infty)$
- $[1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The composition first requires $x\geq0$ to evaluate $\sqrt x$. The outer function accepts every real value. The resulting formula is $x+1$, defined as a composition only for $x\geq0$.
