Let $f(x)=x^2$ and $g(t)=\dfrac{1}{t+1}$. What is the real domain of $g\circ f$?

- $[0,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-1\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$

## Explanation

The outer function excludes $-1$, while the inner function returns $x^2\geq0$. Thus the denominator $x^2+1$ is strictly positive for every $x$.
