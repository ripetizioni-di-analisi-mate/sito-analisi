Let $f(x)=x^2$ and $g(t)=\dfrac{1}{t+1}$. What is the real domain of $g\circ f$?

- $[0,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-1\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$

## Explanation

The outer function excludes $-1$, while the inner function returns $x^2\geq0$. Thus the denominator $x^2+1$ is strictly positive for every $x$. In particular, $x=-1$ must not be removed from the composition's domain. At that input the intermediate value is $f(-1)=1$ and the outer function gives $g(1)=\dfrac12$. The forbidden input to $g$ is never attained by $f$.
