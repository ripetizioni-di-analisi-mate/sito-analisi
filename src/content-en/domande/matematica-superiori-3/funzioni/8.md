Find the range of $f(x)=x^2$ on $(-2,1]$.

- $[0,1]$
- $(0,4)$
- $[0,4)$
- $[0,4]$

## Explanation

The minimum is $0$, attained at $x=0$. Every $y\in[0,4)$ is attained at $x=-\sqrt y\in(-2,0]$. The value $4$ would require $x=\pm 2$, both outside the domain. Throughout the domain, $|x|<2$, so $x^2<4$. The input $x=1$ is included and gives $1$, but the negative part of the domain also gives outputs greater than $1$, for example $\left(-\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2=\dfrac{9}{4}$.
