For which values of $a$ is $2$ excluded from the domain of $f(x)=\dfrac{x-a}{x^2-a}$?

- $a\neq4$
- $a=2$
- $a=4$
- $a=-4$

## Explanation

The input $2$ is excluded when the denominator at $2$ is zero, so $2^2-a=0$. This gives $a=4$. The numerator does not determine exclusions.
