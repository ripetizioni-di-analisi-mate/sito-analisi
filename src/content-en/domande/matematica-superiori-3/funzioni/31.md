Let $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x^2+1}$ with domain $[-1,3]$. How is $f$ classified on this domain?

- Both even and odd
- Even only
- Odd only
- Neither even nor odd

## Explanation

The domain contains $3$ but not $-3$, so it is not symmetric about $0$. The square in the formula alone is insufficient to make the function even. The formula would give equal outputs at opposite inputs on all of $\mathbb R$. In the given domain, however, $x=3$ has value $\dfrac{1}{10}$ and the point with the opposite input is missing. The restricted graph therefore loses both symmetries.
