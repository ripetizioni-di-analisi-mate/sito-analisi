Let $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x^2+1}$ with domain $[-1,3]$. How is $f$ classified on this domain?

- Both even and odd
- Even only
- Odd only
- Neither even nor odd

## Explanation

The domain contains $3$ but not $-3$, so it is not symmetric about $0$. The square in the formula alone is insufficient to make the function even.
