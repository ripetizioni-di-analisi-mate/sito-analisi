Classify $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2}{x^2-1}$ as even, odd, both or neither on its real domain.

- Odd only
- Neither even nor odd
- Both even and odd
- Even only

## Explanation

The domain excludes $\pm1$ and is symmetric. Replacing $x$ with $-x$ leaves both squares unchanged, so $f(-x)=f(x)$. Also $f(2)=\dfrac{4}{3}\neq 0$, so the odd-function identity does not hold.
