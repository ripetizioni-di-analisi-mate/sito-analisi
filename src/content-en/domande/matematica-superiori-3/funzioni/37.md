The graph of $f$ shows its vertex and zeros. For which value of $c$ does $f(x)=c$ have exactly one solution?

::grafico{f="-x^2+4" xmin=-3 xmax=3 ymin=-3 ymax=5 punti="-2,0;2,0;0,4" titolo="The graph is symmetric about the $y$-axis."}

- $0$
- $1$
- $4$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The line $y=4$ meets the parabola only at its vertex. At each other listed height, the horizontal line meets one point to the left of the vertex and one to the right.
