Let $f\colon\{-1,0,1,3\}\to\{0,1,3,9\}$ be given by $f(x)=x^2$. Which element of the codomain has no preimages?

- $1$
- $3$
- $0$
- $9$

## Explanation

The four outputs are $f(-1)=1$, $f(0)=0$, $f(1)=1$ and $f(3)=9$. The range is $\{0,1,9\}$, so $3$ is never attained. Another way to look for a preimage of $3$ is to solve $x^2=3$. Both candidates $\pm\sqrt{3}$ are real, but neither is in the given domain. Membership in the codomain means being an allowed output, with no guarantee it is attained.
