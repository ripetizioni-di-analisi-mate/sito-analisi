Two functions must agree on their entire domains to be equal. How are $f(x)=\sqrt{x^2}$ and $g(x)=x$ related on $\mathbb R$?

- They agree exactly when $x\leq0$.
- They agree only at $x=0$.
- They agree exactly when $x\geq0$.
- They agree for every $x$.

## Explanation

The square root is nonnegative, so $\sqrt{x^2}=|x|$. Absolute value equals $x$ for $x\geq0$ and equals $-x\neq x$ for $x<0$. For instance, at $x=-2$ the radicand is $4$, so $f(-2)=2$ while $g(-2)=-2$. At $x=0$ both rules give $0$, so the set of inputs where they agree contains its left endpoint.
