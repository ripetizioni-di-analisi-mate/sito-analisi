An odd function has domain $\mathbb R$ and $f(2)=7$. What is $f(-2)+f(0)$?

- $0$
- $-7$
- $-14$
- $7$

## Explanation

Oddness gives $f(-2)=-f(2)=-7$. Also $f(0)=-f(0)$, so $f(0)=0$. Their sum is $-7$. The symmetry is about the origin, so $(2,7)$ corresponds to $(-2,-7)$. Any point on the vertical axis must coincide with its own reflection through the origin. Since the domain contains $0$, that point is the origin.
