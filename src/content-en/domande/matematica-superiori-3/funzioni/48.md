The function $f$ is injective and its graph contains $(2,-5)$. Which point must belong to the graph of its inverse?

- $(5,-2)$
- $(-2,5)$
- $(2,5)$
- $(-5,2)$

## Explanation

From $f(2)=-5$ we get $f^{-1}(-5)=2$. On the graph, the original vertical coordinate becomes the new horizontal coordinate. The inverse takes an output of $f$ as its input and returns its unique preimage. The given point guarantees that $-5$ is a valid inverse input. Swap the coordinates, keeping the sign of each number.
