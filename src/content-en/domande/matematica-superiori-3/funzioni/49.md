What is the domain of the inverse of $f(x)=\sqrt{x-2}+1$ on $[2,+\infty)$?

- $(1,+\infty)$
- $[1,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $[2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The function is strictly increasing and attains $1$ at $x=2$. For every $y\geq1$, $f(2+(y-1)^2)=y$. Its range, which is the domain of its inverse, is therefore $[1,+\infty)$.
