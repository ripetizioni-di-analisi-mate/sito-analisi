A price rule $C(n)=7+2n$ euros is defined for $n\in\{0,1,2,3\}$ visits. What is its range?

- $\{2,4,6,8\}$
- $\{0,1,2,3\}$
- $\{7,9,11,13\}$
- $[7,13]$

## Explanation

Substitute only the four allowed numbers. The charges are $7$, $9$, $11$ and $13$. An intermediate charge such as $8$ would require half a visit, which is outside the domain. Consecutive prices differ by $2$, because each extra visit costs $2$ euros. The amount $7$ is attained even with no visits. A line could interpolate the four outputs, but the model assigns charges only to the stated integer visit counts.
