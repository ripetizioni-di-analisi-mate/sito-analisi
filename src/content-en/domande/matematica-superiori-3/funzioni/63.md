What is the range of $f(x)=x^2+1$ for $x\in[1,3]$?

- $[1,10]$
- $[2,9]$
- $[1,3]$
- $[2,10]$

## Explanation

On $[1,3]$ the square is strictly increasing. The endpoints are $1^2+1=2$ and $3^2+1=10$. For each output $y$ between these values, $\sqrt{y-1}$ is an input in the interval.
