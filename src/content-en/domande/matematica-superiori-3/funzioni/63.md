What is the range of $f(x)=x^2+1$ for $x\in[1,3]$?

- $[1,10]$
- $[2,9]$
- $[1,3]$
- $[2,10]$

## Explanation

On $[1,3]$ the square is strictly increasing. The endpoints are $1^2+1=2$ and $3^2+1=10$. For each output $y$ between these values, $\sqrt{y-1}$ is an input in the interval. This checks both directions. Every allowed input gives an output between $2$ and $10$, and the square root gives a preimage for every output in that interval, including the endpoints. The range is therefore $[2,10]$.
