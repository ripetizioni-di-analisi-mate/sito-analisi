Evaluate $f(-3)$ for $f(x)=x^2+2x-5$.

- $f(-3)=8$
- $f(-3)=-20$
- $f(-3)=-2$
- $f(-3)=10$

## Explanation

Substituting $-3$ into both terms gives $(-3)^2+2(-3)-5=9-6-5=-2$. The linear term is negative. Here the horizontal coordinate is given and the output is to be found. The sign of $-3$ stays inside the power, so the square is $9$, while the product with $2$ stays negative. The corresponding point on the graph is $(-3,-2)$.
