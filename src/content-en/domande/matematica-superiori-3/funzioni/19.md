The line shown belongs to the graph of $f$, apart from the open circle. Which real number is missing from the domain of $f$?

::grafico{f="x+3" xmin=-3 xmax=4 ymin=-1 ymax=7 punti="1,4,vuoto" titolo="The open point is at $(1,4)$."}

- $1$
- $4$
- $0$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The domain consists of the horizontal coordinates of points on the graph. The open point has horizontal coordinate $1$, and the line $x=1$ contains no other point of the graph, so $1$ is excluded.
