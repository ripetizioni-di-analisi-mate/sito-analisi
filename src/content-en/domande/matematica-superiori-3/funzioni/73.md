For the function $f(x)=\dfrac{x}{x^2}$, which pair of statements is correct?

- The domain is $\mathbb R$ and the function has no zeros.
- The domain excludes $0$ and there are two zeros.
- The domain is $\mathbb R$ and the function has one zero.
- The domain excludes $0$ and the function has no zeros.

## Explanation

The denominator vanishes at $0$, which is the only candidate from the numerator. On the domain, the quotient equals $\dfrac{1}{x}$ and is always nonzero.
