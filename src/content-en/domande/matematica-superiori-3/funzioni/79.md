If $f$ has range $[1,5]$, what is the range of $g(x)=3-2f(x)$?

- $[-10,-2]$
- $[1,7]$
- $[-7,1]$
- $[-1,7]$

## Explanation

The transformation $v\mapsto3-2v$ is decreasing, so the smaller endpoint comes from $v=5$ and equals $3-10=-7$. The larger comes from $v=1$ and equals $3-2=1$. The endpoints exchange roles because the coefficient $-2$ reverses order. For each $y$ between $-7$ and $1$, the value $\dfrac{3-y}{2}$ is between $1$ and $5$ and is therefore taken by $f$. The range is the whole interval $[-7,1]$.
