A function satisfies $f(f(x))=x$ on all of $\mathbb R$. If $f(4)=-2$, which pair must also be on its graph?

- $(-4,2)$
- $(2,-4)$
- $(-2,4)$
- $(-2,-2)$

## Explanation

Substituting $x=4$ into the identity gives $f(-2)=4$, so the required pair is $(-2,4)$.
