What are the zeros of $f(x)=|x+1|-2$?

- $-3$ and $1$
- Only $-1$
- Only $1$
- $-1$ and $3$

## Explanation

The equation is $|x+1|=2$. The distance from $x$ to $-1$ is $2$, so $x=-1-2=-3$ or $x=-1+2=1$. Substituting the inputs found gives absolute-value arguments $-2$ and $2$, respectively, so both give output $0$. The input $x=-1$ makes the inner argument zero, but it is the minimum of the whole graph, with output $-2$.
