A quantity is given by $s(t)=t(4-t)$ for $0\leq t\leq4$. At what times does it take the value $3$?

- Only $t=3$
- Only $t=2$
- $t=0$ and $t=4$
- $t=1$ and $t=3$

## Explanation

The equation $t(4-t)=3$ is equivalent to $t^2-4t+3=0$, or $(t-1)(t-3)=0$. Both times $1$ and $3$ are allowed.
