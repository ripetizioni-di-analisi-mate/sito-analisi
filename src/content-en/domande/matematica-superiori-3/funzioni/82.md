Let $f(x)=x^2$ with $x\geq 0$, and let $g(x)=f(x-1)$. What is $g^{-1}(9)$?

- $-2$
- $3$
- $10$
- $4$

## Explanation

The new function is $(x-1)^2$ for $x\geq1$. The equation $(x-1)^2=9$ gives $x=4$ and $x=-2$, but only $4$ lies in the domain. On $[1,+\infty)$ the square of $x-1$ is strictly increasing, so the inverse exists on the range. The excluded solution came from argument $x-1=-3$, which lies outside the domain of $f$. Checking the branch is therefore necessary.
