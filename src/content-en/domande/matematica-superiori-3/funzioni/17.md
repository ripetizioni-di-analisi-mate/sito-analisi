A pass costs $5$ euros, plus $3$ euros for each of the first $2$ visits and $2$ euros for each subsequent visit. What is the total cost for $5$ visits?

- $15$ euros
- $20$ euros
- $11$ euros
- $17$ euros

## Explanation

The first rate applies to $2$ visits and the second to the remaining $5-2=3$. The cost is $5+3\cdot2+2\cdot3=17$ euros. The fixed charge is paid once. The first two visits add $6$ euros and the later visits add another $6$ euros. Applying the reduced rate to all five visits would give $15$ euros and would treat even the first two as later visits.
