For which $x$ does $\dfrac{x^2-4}{x-2}\leq0$ hold, taking the domain into account?

- $[-2,2)$
- $(-\infty,-2]$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(2,+\infty)$
- $\{-2,2\}$

## Explanation

On the domain $x\neq 2$, cancel the factor $x-2$ and solve $x+2\leq0$. The set $(-\infty,-2]$ does not contain the excluded point $2$.
