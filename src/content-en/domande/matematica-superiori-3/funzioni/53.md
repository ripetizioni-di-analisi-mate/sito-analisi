Let $f(x)=x^2-4x+4$. On which of the listed intervals is it injective?

- $(-\infty,3]$
- $[2,5]$
- $[1,3]$
- $[0,4]$

## Explanation

The form $(x-2)^2$ is strictly increasing for $x\geq2$, hence injective on $[2,5]$. Each other interval contains both $1$ and $3$, whose common image is $1$.
