Let $f(x)=x^2-4x+4$. On which of the listed intervals is it injective?

- $(-\infty,3]$
- $[2,5]$
- $[1,3]$
- $[0,4]$

## Explanation

The form $(x-2)^2$ is strictly increasing for $x\geq2$, hence injective on $[2,5]$. Each other interval contains both $1$ and $3$, whose common image is $1$. For two points to the right of $2$, compare their nonnegative distances from $2$. Increasing the input increases the distance and therefore its square. An interval crossing $2$ can instead contain two inputs equally far from the vertex, such as the given pair.
