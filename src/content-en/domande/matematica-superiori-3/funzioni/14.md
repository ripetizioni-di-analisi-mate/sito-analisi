Write the function obtained by reflecting the graph of $f(x)=x^2-2x$ across the $x$-axis and then shifting it $3$ units up.

- $-x^2+2x-3$
- $-x^2+2x+3$
- $-x^2-2x+3$
- $x^2+2x+3$

## Explanation

Reflecting across the $x$-axis reverses the sign of the entire output, giving $-(x^2-2x)=-x^2+2x$. The shift then increases every output by $3$. To check the order, follow the point $(0,0)$ on the original graph. The reflection leaves it in place and the shift takes it to $(0,3)$. Thus the vertical intercept of the resulting formula must be $3$.
