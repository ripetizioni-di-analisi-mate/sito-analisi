Write the function obtained by reflecting the graph of $f(x)=x^2-2x$ across the $x$-axis and then shifting it $3$ units up.

- $-x^2+2x-3$
- $-x^2+2x+3$
- $-x^2-2x+3$
- $x^2+2x+3$

## Explanation

Reflecting across the $x$-axis reverses the sign of the entire output, giving $-(x^2-2x)=-x^2+2x$. The shift then increases every output by $3$.
