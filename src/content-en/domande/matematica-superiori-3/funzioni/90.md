Let $f(x)=x^2-4$ on $\mathbb R$. For which of the listed functions $g$ are both zeros of $f$ also zeros of $g\circ f$?

- $g(t)=\sqrt{t+2}$
- $g(t)=2t$
- $g(t)=t+1$
- $g(t)=t^2-1$

## Explanation

At both zeros of $f$, the output is $0$. At both inputs of $f$, the composition takes the value $g(0)$, so it requires $g(0)=0$. Only $g(t)=2t$ satisfies this condition. The original zeros are $-2$ and $2$, so they supply the same input to $g$. It suffices to evaluate each suggested rule at $0$. The rules $t+1$, $t^2-1$ and $\sqrt{t+2}$ give $1$, $-1$ and $\sqrt{2}$ at $0$, respectively, all nonzero.
