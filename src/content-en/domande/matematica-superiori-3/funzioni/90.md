Let $f(x)=x^2-4$ on $\mathbb R$. For which of the listed functions $g$ are both zeros of $f$ also zeros of $g\circ f$?

- $g(t)=\sqrt{t+2}$
- $g(t)=2t$
- $g(t)=t+1$
- $g(t)=t^2-1$

## Explanation

At both zeros of $f$, the output is $0$. At both inputs of $f$, the composition takes the value $g(0)$, so it requires $g(0)=0$. Only $g(t)=2t$ satisfies this condition.
