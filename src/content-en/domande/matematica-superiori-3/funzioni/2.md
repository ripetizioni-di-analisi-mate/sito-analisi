Find the real domain of $f(x)=\dfrac{\sqrt{x+2}}{\sqrt{3-x}}$.

- $[-2,3)$
- $[-2,3]$
- $(-2,3)$
- $(-2,3]$

## Explanation

The requirements are $x+2\geq 0$ and $3-x>0$, because the square root in the denominator must be nonzero. Their intersection is $[-2,3)$. The endpoints show which brackets to use. At $x=-2$ the quotient is $\dfrac{0}{\sqrt{5}}=0$. At $x=3$ the numerator is $\sqrt{5}$ but the denominator is zero, so that point must be excluded.
