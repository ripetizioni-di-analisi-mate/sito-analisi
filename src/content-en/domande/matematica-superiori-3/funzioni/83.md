The graph of $f$ has one zero at $x=2$ and passes through $(0,4)$. For $g(x)=f(x-1)$, which pair gives its zero and another point on the graph?

- $x=3$ and $(0,5)$
- $x=3$ and $(1,4)$
- $x=1$ and $(-1,4)$
- $x=2$ and $(1,4)$

## Explanation

To reuse the original horizontal coordinate $u$, require $x-1=u$, so $x=u+1$. The points $(2,0)$ and $(0,4)$ become $(3,0)$ and $(1,4)$.
