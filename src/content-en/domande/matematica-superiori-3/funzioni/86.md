The hire cost is $C(t)=4+3t$ euros for $t\geq0$, where $t$ is time in hours. For how many allowed values of $t$ is the cost $2$ euros?

- Infinitely many
- None
- Two
- One

## Explanation

The equation $4+3t=2$ gives $t=-\dfrac{2}{3}$, outside the domain. In fact every allowed charge is at least $4$, the charge at $t=0$.
