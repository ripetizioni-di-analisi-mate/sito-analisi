A car park charges $C(t)=2t+3$ euros for stays of $0<t\leq4$ hours. What is the set of possible charges?

- $(3,8]$
- $[3,11]$
- $(0,4]$
- $(3,11]$

## Explanation

The function is strictly increasing. The charge $3$ would correspond to the excluded time $0$, whereas $11$ is attained at $t=4$. For any charge $y\in(3,11]$, the time $\dfrac{y-3}{2}$ is allowed.
