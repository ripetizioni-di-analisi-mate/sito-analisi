Use the graph to determine the values of $x$ for which $f(x)<0$.

::grafico{f="(x+2)*(x-1)" xmin=-4 xmax=3 ymin=-3 ymax=6 punti="-2,0;1,0" titolo="Graph of the function $f$."}

- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(1,+\infty)$
- $(0,1)$
- $(-2,1)$
- $[-2,1]$

## Explanation

The graph lies below the $x$-axis between the intercepts with horizontal coordinates $-2$ and $1$. At the intercepts $f(x)=0$, so the strict inequality excludes them. Read the horizontal coordinates of the lower part of the graph, since the sign concerns the output associated with each of them. The origin is not an endpoint. At $x=0$ the graph is below the axis, and that input belongs to $(-2,1)$.
