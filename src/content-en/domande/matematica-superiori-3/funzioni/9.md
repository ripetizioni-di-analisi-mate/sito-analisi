Use the graph to determine the values of $x$ for which $f(x)<0$.

::grafico{f="(x+2)*(x-1)" xmin=-4 xmax=3 ymin=-3 ymax=6 punti="-2,0;1,0" titolo="Graph of the function $f$."}

- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(1,+\infty)$
- $(0,1)$
- $(-2,1)$
- $[-2,1]$

## Explanation

The graph lies below the $x$-axis between the intercepts with horizontal coordinates $-2$ and $1$. At the intercepts $f(x)=0$, so the strict inequality excludes them.
