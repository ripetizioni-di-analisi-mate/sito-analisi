A student claims that $f(x)=(x-2)^2$ is increasing on all of $\mathbb R$. Which comparison disproves the claim?

- $2<3$ and $f(2)>f(3)$
- $1<2$ and $f(1)>f(2)$
- $2<3$ and $f(2)<f(3)$
- $1<2$ and $f(1)<f(2)$

## Explanation

We have $f(1)=1$ and $f(2)=0$. Increasing the input from $1$ to $2$ therefore decreases the output, which disproves increase on the whole real line.
