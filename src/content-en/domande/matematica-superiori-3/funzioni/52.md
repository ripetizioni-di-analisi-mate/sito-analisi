Temperature $F$ in degrees Fahrenheit is obtained from Celsius temperature $C$ using $F(C)=\dfrac{9}{5}C+32$. What Celsius temperature corresponds to $68$ degrees Fahrenheit?

- $36$
- $20$
- $10$
- $100$

## Explanation

To recover $C$, solve $68=\dfrac{9}{5}C+32$. Subtracting $32$ gives $36=\dfrac{9}{5}C$, hence $C=36\cdot\dfrac{5}{9}=20$.
