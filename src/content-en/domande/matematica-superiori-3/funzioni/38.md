One device computes $f(x)=x+1$. A second computes $g(x)=x^2$. What is $(g\circ f)(2)$ minus $(f\circ g)(2)$?

- $8$
- $-4$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

In the first order, $2$ goes to $3$ and then to $9$. In the second, it goes to $4$ and then to $5$. The difference is $9-5=4$.
