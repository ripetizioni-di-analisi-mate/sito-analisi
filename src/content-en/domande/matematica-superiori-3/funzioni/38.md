One device computes $f(x)=x+1$. A second computes $g(x)=x^2$. What is $(g\circ f)(2)$ minus $(f\circ g)(2)$?

- $8$
- $-4$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

In the first order, $2$ goes to $3$ and then to $9$. In the second, it goes to $4$ and then to $5$. The difference is $9-5=4$. The two calculations are $(2+1)^2=9$ and $2^2+1=5$. In the first, the extra unit goes inside the square. In the second, it is added after squaring. The question asks for the difference of those final outputs in the stated order.
