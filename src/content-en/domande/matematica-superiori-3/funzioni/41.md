Given $f(x)=x+2$ and $g(t)=t^2-1$, find the real solutions of $(g\circ f)(x)=0$.

- $\{-3,-1\}$
- $\{-1,1\}$
- $\{1,3\}$
- $\{-2\}$

## Explanation

The condition is $(x+2)^2-1=0$, or $x+2=\pm1$. Subtracting $2$ from both values gives $-3$ and $-1$. The zeros of the outer rule are the intermediate values $-1$ and $1$. To obtain the actual inputs, take their respective preimages under $f$. Both functions are polynomials, so neither of the two inputs found is excluded by a domain restriction.
