Given $f(x)=\sqrt{2-x}$ and $g(t)=3t+1$, what is the domain of $f\circ g$?

- $\left(-\infty,\dfrac{1}{3}\right]$
- $\left(-\infty,\dfrac{1}{3}\right)$
- $(-\infty,2]$
- $\left[\dfrac{1}{3},+\infty\right)$

## Explanation

The square root requires $2-g(t)\geq0$. Substituting $g(t)=3t+1$ gives $1-3t\geq0$, hence $t\leq\dfrac{1}{3}$.
