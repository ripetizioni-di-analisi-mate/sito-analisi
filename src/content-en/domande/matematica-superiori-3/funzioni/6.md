Find $f^{-1}(y)$ and its domain for $f(x)=3-2x$ on $[0,2]$.

- $\dfrac{3-y}{2}$ on $[0,2]$
- $\dfrac{3-y}{2}$ on $[-1,3]$
- $3-2y$ on $[-1,3]$
- $\dfrac{y-3}{2}$ on $[-1,3]$

## Explanation

Solving $y=3-2x$ gives $x=\dfrac{3-y}{2}$. The function is strictly decreasing and takes every value between $f(2)=-1$ and $f(0)=3$, so the inverse has domain $[-1,3]$.
