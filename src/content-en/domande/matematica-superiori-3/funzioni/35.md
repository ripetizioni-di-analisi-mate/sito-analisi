Let $f(x)=|x|+x$ on $\mathbb R$. Which pair of values shows that it is neither even nor odd?

- $f(-1)=-2,\ f(1)=2$
- $f(-1)=0,\ f(1)=2$
- $f(-1)=0,\ f(1)=0$
- $f(-1)=2,\ f(1)=2$

## Explanation

Since $|-1|=|1|=1$, we get $f(-1)=0$ and $f(1)=2$. Zero equals neither $2$ nor its opposite. Evenness would require $f(-1)=f(1)$, while oddness would require $f(-1)=-f(1)$. One pair that contradicts both equalities suffices because each identity would have to hold throughout the domain.
