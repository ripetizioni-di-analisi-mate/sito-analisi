The function $f$ has a zero at $x=-4$. Where is the corresponding zero of $g(x)=3f(x-2)$?

- $x=2$
- $x=-2$
- $x=-6$
- $x=-12$

## Explanation

Set $x-2=-4$, so $x=-2$. At this input, $g(-2)=3f(-4)=3\cdot0=0$. Vertical scaling preserves the zero. The horizontal shift is two units to the right, so the new input is larger than the old one. Multiplying a zero output by $3$ leaves it zero. Thus the original pair $(-4,0)$ becomes $(-2,0)$.
