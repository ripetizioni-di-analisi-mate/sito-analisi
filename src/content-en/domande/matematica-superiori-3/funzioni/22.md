What is the real domain of $f(x)=\sqrt{\dfrac{1-x}{x+2}}$?

- $(-2,1]$
- $[-2,1]$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup[1,+\infty)$
- $(-2,1)$

## Explanation

The denominator excludes $-2$. The quotient is positive on $(-2,1)$, where both factors are positive. At $x=1$ the quotient is zero, so the square root is also defined there.
