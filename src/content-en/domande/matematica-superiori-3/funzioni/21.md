What is the real domain of $f(x)=\sqrt{(x-1)(3-x)}$?

- $(-\infty,3]$
- $(1,3)$
- $[1,3]$
- $(-\infty,1]\cup[3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Between $1$ and $3$ both factors are nonnegative. Outside that interval, one is positive and the other is negative. At either endpoint the product is zero, so both endpoints are included.
