For $f(x)=\dfrac{3}{x+2}$, which output $y$ satisfies $f^{-1}(y)=1$?

- $3$
- $0$
- $1$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The equality $f^{-1}(y)=1$ is equivalent to $y=f(1)$. Substituting $1$ into the formula gives $y=\dfrac{3}{1+2}=1$. Here $1$ is an allowed input, since the denominator there is $3$. The inverse works in the opposite direction and takes the produced output as input. The point $(1,1)$ provides a check, as it stays unchanged when its coordinates are swapped.
