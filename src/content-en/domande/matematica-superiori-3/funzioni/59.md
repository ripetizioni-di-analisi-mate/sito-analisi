The graph of $f$ is shifted $3$ units left and then reflected across the $x$-axis to obtain $g$. Which rule describes $g$?

- $g(x)=-f(x-3)$
- $g(x)=f(x)-3$
- $g(x)=-f(x+3)$
- $g(x)=f(-x+3)$

## Explanation

To move a point with horizontal coordinate $u$ from $u$ to $u-3$, the new rule uses input $x+3$. Reflection across the $x$-axis then reverses the sign of the output, so $g(x)=-f(x+3)$.
