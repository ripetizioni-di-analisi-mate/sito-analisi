Let $f(x)=x^2$ with $D_f=(-\infty,0]$. What is $f^{-1}(16)$?

- $4$
- $16$
- $-16$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The solutions of $x^2=16$ are $\pm4$. The only one in the domain, where $x\leq0$, is $-4$. On the nonpositive half-line, the distance from zero is $-x$ and decreases as $x$ increases, so the square strictly decreases. That is why there is just one preimage in the domain. The check is $(-4)^2=16$.
