Find all preimages of $5$ for $f(x)=(x+1)^2+1$.

- $\{-3,1\}$
- $\{-1,3\}$
- $\{-5,3\}$
- $\{1\}$

## Explanation

The equation is $(x+1)^2=4$, so $x+1=2$ or $x+1=-2$. Subtracting $1$ gives $1$ and $-3$. The square represents the squared distance of $x$ from $-1$. Both inputs found are $2$ units from $-1$ and give $4+1=5$. The answer with only the positive input misses the left branch.
