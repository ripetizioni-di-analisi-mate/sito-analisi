Let $f(x)=x^2+1$ be defined on $\mathbb R$. Which pair of $x$-values shows that $f$ is not injective?

- $-2$ and $2$
- $-2$ and $0$
- $0$ and $1$
- $1$ and $2$

## Explanation

A function is injective if distinct $x$-values have distinct images. The $x$-values $-2$ and $2$ are distinct and have the same image, because $f(-2)=f(2)=4+1=5$. Therefore $f$ is not injective.

In the other pairs the images are different, for example $f(-2)=5$ and $f(0)=1$. On the graph, the line $y=5$ meets the parabola at the two points $(-2,5)$ and $(2,5)$.
