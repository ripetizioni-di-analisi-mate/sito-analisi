Let $f(x)=x^2+1$ on $\mathbb R$. Which pair shows that $f$ is not injective?

- $-2$ and $2$
- $-2$ and $0$
- $0$ and $1$
- $1$ and $2$

## Explanation

The inputs $-2$ and $2$ are different and each gives $4+1=5$. Thus a single output has more than one preimage. On the graph, the horizontal line $y=5$ meets the parabola twice. This single pair suffices to disprove injectivity. Both points can belong to the graph of the same function because their horizontal coordinates are different.
