A function from $\{1,2,3\}$ to $\{a,b,c\}$ satisfies $f(1)=a$ and $f(2)=a$. How many of the three possible values of $f(3)$ make it surjective?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

Two outputs, $b$ and $c$, are still missing. The one remaining input can cover at most one of them, so no completion is surjective.
