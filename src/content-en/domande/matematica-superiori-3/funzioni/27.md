For which values of $x$ is $f(x)=\dfrac{(x-1)^2}{x+2}$ strictly positive?

- $(-\infty,-2)$
- $[-2,1)\cup(1,+\infty)$
- $(-2,1)\cup(1,+\infty)$
- $(-2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The numerator is positive when $x\neq1$, and the denominator is positive when $x>-2$. Keep only the inputs that satisfy both conditions. For $x<-2$ the denominator is negative and the square positive, so the quotient is negative. The value $1$ makes the numerator zero, so it is also removed, leaving $(-2,1)\cup(1,+\infty)$.
