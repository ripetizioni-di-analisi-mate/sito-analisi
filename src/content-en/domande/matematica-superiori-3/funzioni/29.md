Let $f(x)=x^2+kx+4$ on $\mathbb R$. For which value of $k$ is it even?

- $0$
- $4$
- $1$
- $-1$

## Explanation

Substituting $-x$ gives $x^2-kx+4$. The difference $f(-x)-f(x)=-2kx$ must be zero for every $x$. At $x=1$, this forces $k=0$, which makes the difference identically zero.
