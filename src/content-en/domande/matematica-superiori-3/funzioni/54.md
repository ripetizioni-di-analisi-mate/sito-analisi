Which function reflects $f(x)=x^2+x$ across the $y$-axis?

- $g(x)=-x^2-x$
- $g(x)=-x^2+x$
- $g(x)=x^2-x$
- $g(x)=x^2+x$

## Explanation

Reflecting across the $y$-axis reverses the sign of the input. This gives $g(x)=f(-x)=(-x)^2+(-x)=x^2-x$. One check is to follow the point $(1,2)$. Reflection gives $(-1,2)$ because the output is unchanged. The new rule evaluates to $(-1)^2-(-1)=2$, as expected. Negating the entire expression would reflect across the other axis.
