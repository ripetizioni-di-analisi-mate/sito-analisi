Which function has a zero at $x=0$ and a negative value at both $x=-1$ and $x=1$?

- $f(x)=-x^2$
- $f(x)=x$
- $f(x)=x^2$
- $f(x)=x^2-1$

## Explanation

For $f(x)=-x^2$, we get $f(0)=0$ and $f(\pm1)=-1$. The even power gives the same output on both sides, and the leading minus sign makes it negative. The rule $x^2$ instead gives output $1$ at both points. The function $x$ has opposite signs at $-1$ and $1$, and $x^2-1$ already misses the initial requirement because it gives $-1$ at zero.
