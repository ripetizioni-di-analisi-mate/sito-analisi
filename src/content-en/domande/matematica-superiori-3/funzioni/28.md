Which function has a zero at $x=0$ and a negative value at both $x=-1$ and $x=1$?

- $f(x)=-x^2$
- $f(x)=x$
- $f(x)=x^2$
- $f(x)=x^2-1$

## Explanation

For $f(x)=-x^2$, we get $f(0)=0$ and $f(\pm1)=-1$. The even power gives the same output on both sides, and the leading minus sign makes it negative.
