Let $f(x)=3x-2$ and $g(x)=x^2+1$. What is $(f\circ g)(-2)$?

- $(f\circ g)(-2)=13$
- $(f\circ g)(-2)=7$
- $(f\circ g)(-2)=10$
- $(f\circ g)(-2)=65$

## Explanation

First compute $g(-2)=(-2)^2+1=5$. Applying $f$ to that value gives $f(5)=3\cdot 5-2=13$. The order is fixed by $(f\circ g)(x)=f(g(x))$. The number $5$ is the intermediate result. The value $65$ would come from reversing the order, since $f(-2)=-8$ and $g(-8)=65$.
