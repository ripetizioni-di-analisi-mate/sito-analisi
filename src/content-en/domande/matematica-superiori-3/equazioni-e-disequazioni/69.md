In the model $h(t)=1+4t-t^2$, with $t\geq0$ in seconds and $h$ in meters, at which times is $h(t)\geq4$?

- $(1,3)$
- $[0,4]$
- $[1,3]$
- $[0,1]\cup[3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The difference $h(t)-4$ is $-t^2+4t-3=-(t-1)(t-3)$. It is nonnegative on $[1,3]$. Both endpoints are allowed and correspond to a height of exactly $4$ meters. For example, at $t=2$ the height is $1+8-4=5$. At $t=0$ it is $1$, and the constraint fails. The nonnegative-time condition already holds throughout the interval obtained.
