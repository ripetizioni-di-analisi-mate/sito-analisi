What is the sum of the real roots of $x^2-7x+10=0$?

- $7$
- $-10$
- $10$
- $-7$

## Explanation

In the form $(x-r)(x-s)$, the coefficient of $x$ is $-(r+s)$. Here it is $-7$, so $r+s=7$. The constant term is instead $rs=10$. Here both facts can be checked by finding the roots $2$ and $5$ from the factorization $(x-2)(x-5)$. Their sum is $2+5=7$.
