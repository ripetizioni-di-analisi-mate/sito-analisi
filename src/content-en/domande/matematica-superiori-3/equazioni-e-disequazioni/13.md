For which values of $x$ does $\dfrac{x-1}{x+2}\leq-1$ hold?

- $(-\infty,-2)\cup\left[-\dfrac12,+\infty\right)$
- $\left(-\dfrac12,+\infty\right)$
- $\left(-2,-\dfrac12\right]$
- $\left[-2,-\dfrac12\right]$

## Explanation

Adding $1$ gives $\dfrac{2x+1}{x+2}\leq0$. The quotient is negative between $-2$ and $-\dfrac12$, where the signs are opposite. Include $-\dfrac12$ because the numerator is zero. At $-2$ the denominator is zero, so that value is excluded. At $x=-1$ the transformed numerator is $-1$ and the denominator is $1$, confirming the required sign inside the interval.
