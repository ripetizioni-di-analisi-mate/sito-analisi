For which (x) does the fraction $\dfrac{x^2-1}{x+1}$ have the same value as $x-1$?

- Only for $x=-1$.
- For every real $x$.
- For every $x\neq-1$.
- Only for $x=1$.

## Explanation

The numerator is $(x-1)(x+1)$. For every $x\neq-1$, the factor $x+1$ cancels and leaves $x-1$. At $-1$ the fraction is undefined.
