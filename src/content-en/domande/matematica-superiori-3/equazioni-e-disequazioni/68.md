A simple revenue model is $R(x)=x(12-2x)$ with $0<x<6$. For which values of $x$ is $R(x)\geq16$?

- $[2,4]$
- $(2,4)$
- $(0,2]\cup[4,6)$
- $(0,6)$

## Explanation

The difference $R(x)-16$ factors as $-2(x-2)(x-4)$. It is nonnegative between the roots, both of which are admissible. Thus the required interval is $[2,4]$.
