For which $k$ would the excluded point $x=2$ be the only zero of the numerator of $\dfrac{x-k}{x-2}$?

- $2$
- $1$
- $0$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The numerator $x-k$ is zero only at $x=k$. For this point to coincide with the excluded value from the denominator, we need $k=2$. Substituting $x=2$ into the numerator gives $2-k$. For the resulting parameter, the fraction equals $1$ throughout its domain but is still undefined at the common zero.
