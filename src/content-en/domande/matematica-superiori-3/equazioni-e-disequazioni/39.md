What is the solution set of the equation $\dfrac{x-2}{x^2+1}=0$?

- $\{2\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\{-1,1\}$
- $\{0,2\}$

## Explanation

Since $x^2+1\geq1$, the denominator is positive for every $x$. The fraction is zero when $x-2=0$, so at $x=2$. The inequality $x^2+1\geq1$ follows from $x^2\geq0$. Substituting $2$ gives $\dfrac05=0$. At every other value the numerator is nonzero, so the quotient is nonzero as well.
