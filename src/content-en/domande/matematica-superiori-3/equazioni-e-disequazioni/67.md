A ticket costs $x$ euros. Under the model $n(x)=12-2x$ for the number of tickets sold, how many euros are collected when $x=2$?

- $12$
- $20$
- $8$
- $16$

## Explanation

Here $n(x)$ counts the tickets sold. For $x=2$, we have $n(2)=12-4=8$. Revenue is the price times the number of tickets, or $2\cdot8=16$.
