For which value of $k$ is the line $y=k$ tangent to the parabola $y=x^2-4x+7$?

- $7$
- $2$
- $4$
- $3$

## Explanation

Equal y-coordinates give $(x-2)^2=k-3$. There is a single intersection x-coordinate, with a double root, when $k-3=0$. Thus $k=3$. Tangency occurs at $(2,3)$. At a height greater than $3$ the square must equal a positive number, so there are two intersections. At a height less than $3$ there are none.
