In the graph the curve represents $y=(x-1)^2$ and the line represents $y=4$. What is the solution set of $(x-1)^2>4$?

::grafico{f="(x-1)^2;4" xmin=-2.5 xmax=4.5 ymin=-1 ymax=10 punti="-1,4,pieno;3,4,pieno" titolo="Intersections at height $4$"}

- $(-1,3)$
- $[-1,3]$
- $(-\infty,-1)\cup(3,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-1]\cup[3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The intersections have x-coordinates $1-2=-1$ and $1+2=3$. Outside that interval the curve has y-coordinate greater than $4$. The intersections are excluded because equality holds there.
