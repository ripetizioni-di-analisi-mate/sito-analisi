A rectangular garden has area $32\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The longer side is twice the shorter one. What is the shorter side length in meters?

- $8$
- $4$
- $16$
- $\sqrt{32}$

## Explanation

Let $x>0$ denote the shorter side. The area condition is $x(2x)=32$. Thus $x^2=16$, and the positive length is $4$. The value $-4$ has the same square but is not an admissible length. With $x=4$, the sides are $4$ and $8$. Their ratio is $2$ and their product is $32$.
