Solve the system $\begin{cases}x^2-11x+28\leq0,\\ x>5.\end{cases}$

- $[4,7]$
- $(5,7)$
- $(5,7]$
- $[5,7]$

## Explanation

The trinomial equals $(x-4)(x-7)$, so it is nonpositive on $[4,7]$. The values must also be strictly greater than $5$. The intersection is $(5,7]$, with $7$ included because it makes the trinomial zero and exceeds $5$.
