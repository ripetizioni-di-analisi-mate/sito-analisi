How many real solutions does $\dfrac4{x+1}=\dfrac2{x-1}$ have?

- One.
- None.
- Two.
- Infinitely many.

## Explanation

On the domain $x\neq\pm1$, multiply by $(x+1)(x-1)$. This gives $4(x-1)=2(x+1)$, or $2x=6$. The value $3$ is in the domain, so there is one solution. The step is reversible because both factors are nonzero in the domain. At $x=3$, the two fractions are $\dfrac44$ and $\dfrac22$, both equal to $1$.
