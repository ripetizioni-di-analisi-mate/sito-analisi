For which $k$ does $x^2-2x+k=0$ have no real roots?

- $k<1$
- $k=1$
- $k>1$
- $k\geq1$

## Explanation

Completing the square gives $(x-1)^2=1-k$. The square cannot take a negative value, so the solution set is empty when $1-k<0$. The condition $1-k<0$ gives $k>1$. At $k=1$, we would instead have $(x-1)^2=0$, with the single root $1$. That is why $k=1$ is excluded from the requested set.
