For which $k$ is the solution set of $x^2-2x+k\le0$ an interval of length exactly $6$?

- $-5$
- $1$
- $-8$
- $10$

## Explanation

Completing the square gives $(x-1)^2\le1-k$. When the right-hand side is positive, the interval is centred on $1$ and has length $2\sqrt{1-k}$. A length of $6$ requires $1-k=9$.
