For which $k$ does $(k-3)x=5$ have no solution?

- $3$
- $5$
- $-3$
- $0$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $x$ vanishes when $k=3$. The equation then becomes $0=5$, which is false for every $x$. For $k\neq3$ we can divide by $k-3$. Whenever $k\neq3$, the unique solution is $x=\dfrac{5}{k-3}$. For example, $k=4$ gives $x=5$. The only case with no solution is the value that makes the coefficient zero.
