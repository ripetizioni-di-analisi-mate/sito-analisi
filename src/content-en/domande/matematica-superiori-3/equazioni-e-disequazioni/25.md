For which $k$ is the double root of $2x^2+kx+8=0$ negative?

- $4$
- $-8$
- $8$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The discriminant is $k^2-64$, so the candidates are $k=8$ and $k=-8$. The double root is $-\dfrac{k}{4}$, which is negative only for $k=8$. At $k=8$ we get $x=-2$ and the polynomial is $2(x+2)^2$. At $k=-8$ we instead obtain $2(x-2)^2$, whose zero is positive.
