What is the solution set of $-2(x-3)^2<0$?

- $\mathbb R$
- $\varnothing$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{3\}$
- $\{3\}$

## Explanation

For $x\neq3$ the square is positive, and multiplying it by $-2$ gives a negative number. At $x=3$ the result is zero, which the strict inequality excludes. Dividing by $-2$ gives the equivalent inequality $(x-3)^2>0$. A square is strictly positive at every point other than the unique zero of the expression being squared.
