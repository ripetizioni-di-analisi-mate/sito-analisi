Suppose $S_1=[-2,1]$ and $S_2=(1,5)$. What is $S_1\cap S_2$?

- $\{1\}$
- $[-2,5)$
- $(1,5)$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The first set ends at $1$. The second contains only numbers strictly greater than $1$. No number belongs to both sets. The only possible point of contact is $1$, which the second interval excludes. Belonging to both would require $x\leq1$ and $x>1$ simultaneously. The intersection is therefore empty.
