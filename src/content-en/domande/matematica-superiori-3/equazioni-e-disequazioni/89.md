If $x^2+bx+c=0$ has one root equal to zero and another equal to $-3$, what are $b$ and $c$?

- $b=-3$ and $c=0$.
- $b=3$ and $c=-3$.
- $b=0$ and $c=-3$.
- $b=3$ and $c=0$.

## Explanation

The given roots produce the factors $x$ and $x+3$. The product is $x^2+3x$, so $b=3$ and $c=0$.
