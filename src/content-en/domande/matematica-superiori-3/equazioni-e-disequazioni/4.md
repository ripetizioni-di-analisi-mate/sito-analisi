Solve $(x-4)(x-7)>0$.

- $(-\infty,4]\cup[7,+\infty)$
- $(4,7)$
- $(-\infty,4)\cup(7,+\infty)$
- $[4,7]$

## Explanation

For $x<4$ both factors are negative, and for $x>7$ both are positive. Between $4$ and $7$ they have opposite signs. Exclude the zeros because the product must be strictly positive. At $x=5$ the product is $1\cdot(-2)=-2$. A single negative factor is therefore enough to rule out the entire middle interval.
