A rectangle is $x+4$ meters long and $x$ meters wide. If its area is greater than $12\,\mathrm{m^2}$, which condition describes exactly the admissible lengths?

- $x<-6$ or $x>2$.
- $x\geq2$
- $0<x<2$
- $x>2$

## Explanation

We have $x(x+4)-12=(x-2)(x+6)$. Since $x>0$, the factor $x+6$ is positive, leaving the condition $x-2>0$. At $x=2$, the area is $2\cdot6=12$, so the strict inequality excludes that boundary. The other algebraic branch, $x<-6$, is incompatible with the domain of the side lengths.
