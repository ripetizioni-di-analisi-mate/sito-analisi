What is the larger real solution of $(3x-1)(2x+4)=0$?

- $-2$
- $2$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The first factor is zero when $3x=1$, and the second when $2x=-4$. Between $\dfrac13$ and $-2$, the larger number is $\dfrac13$. The second case gives $x=-2$ after dividing $-4$ by $2$. The first root is positive, so it is greater. At $x=\dfrac13$ the factor $3x-1$ equals zero, as the zero product requires.
