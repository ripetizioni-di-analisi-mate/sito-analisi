Which number satisfies $x^2-6x+8\leq0$?

- $1$
- $0$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

The trinomial factors as $(x-2)(x-4)$. It is nonpositive on $[2,4]$. Of the given values, $3$ is the only one in that interval. In particular $3$ gives $(3-2)(3-4)=-1$. The number $5$ instead gives $3\cdot1=3>0$, and $0$ and $1$ lie to the left of both roots, where the product is positive.
