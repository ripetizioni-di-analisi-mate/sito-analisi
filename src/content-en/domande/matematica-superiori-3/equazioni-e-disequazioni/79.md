How many integers are in the solution set of $(x+2)(x-3)<0$?

- $6$
- $3$
- $5$
- $4$

## Explanation

The factors have opposite signs for $-2<x<3$. The integers in the open interval are $-1$, $0$, $1$ and $2$, so there are four. The integers $-2$ and $3$ are excluded because each makes one factor zero. Before $-2$ both factors are negative, and after $3$ both are positive, so the product is positive on both exterior intervals.
