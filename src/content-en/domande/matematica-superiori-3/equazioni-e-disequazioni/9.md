What is the solution set of the equation $\dfrac{x+1}{x-2}=1+\dfrac{3}{x-2}$?

- $\{2\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{2\}$
- $\mathbb R$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

For $x\neq2$, the right-hand side can be written as $\dfrac{x-2+3}{x-2}=\dfrac{x+1}{x-2}$. The equality holds throughout the domain. The denominator is zero at $2$, so that value is excluded from the solution. At $x=0$, for example, both sides equal $-\dfrac12$. The simplified forms are identically equal throughout the original domain.
