Solve $\dfrac{x+1}{x-2}=1+\dfrac{3}{x-2}$.

- $\{2\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{2\}$
- $\mathbb R$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

For $x\neq2$, the right-hand side can be written as $\dfrac{x-2+3}{x-2}=\dfrac{x+1}{x-2}$. The equality holds throughout the domain. The denominator is zero at $2$, so that value is excluded from the solution.
