What distinguishes $x=4$ from $x=-2$ in $f(x)=\dfrac{x-4}{x+2}$?

- The function is undefined at both points.
- At $4$ the function equals zero, and at $-2$ it is undefined.
- At $4$ the function is undefined, and at $-2$ it equals zero.
- The function equals zero at both points.

## Explanation

At $4$ the numerator is $0$ and the denominator is $6$, so the quotient is zero. At $-2$ the denominator is $0$, so the quotient is undefined.
