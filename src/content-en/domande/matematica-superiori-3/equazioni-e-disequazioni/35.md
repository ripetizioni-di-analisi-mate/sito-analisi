What is the solution set of $3x^2+1<0$?

- $\mathbb R$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$
- $\{0\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The square is always nonnegative. Therefore $3x^2+1\geq1$ for every $x$, and the inequality never holds. The lowest possible value is reached where $x^2=0$, at $x=0$. Even there the value is $1$. Writing the condition as $3x^2<-1$ gives the same impossibility, since the left-hand side is nonnegative.
