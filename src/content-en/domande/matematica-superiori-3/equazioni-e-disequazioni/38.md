For which $x$ does $\dfrac{(x-1)(x+2)}{x-1}=0$ hold?

- $\{1\}$
- $\{-2\}$
- $\{-2,1\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The denominator rules out $1$. At any other value the quotient equals $x+2$, which is zero only at $-2$. At $x=-2$, the factor $x-1$ equals $-3$, so the original fraction is defined and equals zero. At $x=1$, the numerator and denominator being zero simultaneously does not give a value for the fraction.
