For which value of $a$ is $(x-2)(x-a)$ positive both immediately to the left and immediately to the right of $2$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $0$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The factor $x-2$ changes sign at $2$. For the product to keep its sign, the second factor must also be zero and change sign at $2$. With $a=2$ the product is $(x-2)^2$, which is positive on both sides.
