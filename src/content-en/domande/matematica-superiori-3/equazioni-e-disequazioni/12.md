Solve $(x+2)^2(x-1)>0$.

- $[1,+\infty)$
- $(1,+\infty)$
- $(-2,1)$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The square is positive for $x\neq-2$. For the product to be positive, we therefore need $x-1>0$, or $x>1$. Every value satisfying this condition is already different from $-2$.
