Two consecutive positive integers have product $30$. What is the larger one?

- $6$
- $15$
- $10$
- $5$

## Explanation

Writing the integers as $x$ and $x+1$ gives $x^2+x-30=(x-5)(x+6)=0$. Since $x$ is positive, it equals $5$. The next integer is $6$. The second root would be $-6$, giving the pair $-6$ and $-5$. Their product is the same, but they fall outside the conditions of the problem. For the allowed pair, $5\cdot6=30$.
