Both real roots of $x^2+bx+c=0$ are negative. What are the signs of $b$ and $c$?

- $b>0$ and $c>0$.
- $b<0$ and $c<0$.
- $b<0$ and $c>0$.
- $b>0$ and $c<0$.

## Explanation

The sum of the roots is negative and equals $-b$, so $b>0$. The product of two negative numbers is positive and equals $c$, so $c>0$. Writing the factors as $(x-r)(x-s)$ gives the cross terms $-rx-sx$. The coefficient is positive because $r<0$ and $s<0$. The constant term $rs$ is the product of two numbers with the same sign.
