Solve $\begin{cases}\dfrac{x^2-9}{x-3}>0,\\ x\leq4.\end{cases}$

- $(-3,3)\cup(3,4]$
- $(-3,4]$
- $[-3,3)\cup(3,4]$
- $(3,4]$

## Explanation

For $x\neq3$, the fraction equals $x+3$. Thus $x>-3$ with $3$ excluded. The other condition is $x\leq4$, so the result is $(-3,3)\cup(3,4]$. At $x=-3$ the quotient is zero, so the strict inequality excludes it. At $x=4$ the quotient equals $7>0$ and the other condition allows equality.
