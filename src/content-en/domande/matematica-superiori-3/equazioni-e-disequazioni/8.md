A rectangle has area $28\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, and its longer side is $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ longer than its shorter side. What are the two side lengths in centimeters, from shortest to longest?

- $(2,14)$
- $(-7,-4)$
- $(3,6)$
- $(4,7)$

## Explanation

The shorter length $x>0$ satisfies $x(x+3)=28$. The polynomial $x^2+3x-28$ equals $(x-4)(x+7)$, so the only positive root is $4$. The longer side has length $4+3=7$.
