Solve $\begin{cases}x(x-3)>0,\\ x^2<9.\end{cases}$

- $(-\infty,0)\cup(3,+\infty)$
- $(0,3)$
- $(-3,0]\cup\{3\}$
- $(-3,0)$

## Explanation

The product is positive for $x<0$ or $x>3$. The square is less than $9$ for $-3<x<3$. These sets overlap only between $-3$ and $0$. The branch $x>3$ lies entirely outside the interval allowed by the square. At $x=0$ the product is zero, and at $x=-3$ the square equals $9$. Therefore the solution is $(-3,0)$.
