A rectangle has perimeter $18\,\mathrm m$ and area $14\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What is the shorter side length in meters?

- $1$
- $2$
- $9$
- $7$

## Explanation

The side lengths add to $9$, so $x(9-x)=14$. Moving all terms to one side gives $(x-2)(x-7)=0$. The lengths are $2$ and $7$, so the shorter one is $2$.
