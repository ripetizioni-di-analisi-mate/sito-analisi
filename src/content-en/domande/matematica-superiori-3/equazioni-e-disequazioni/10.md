What is the solution set of the equation $3x^2-12x=0$?

- $\{0,4\}$
- $\{0,-4\}$
- $\{-2,2\}$
- $\{4\}$

## Explanation

Factoring out $3x$ gives $3x(x-4)=0$. The first factor is zero at $0$ and the second at $4$. Dividing by $x$ at the start would remove $0$, which satisfies the equation. To check the two solutions, at $x=0$ each term is zero. At $x=4$ the terms are $48$ and $-48$, so their sum is zero.
