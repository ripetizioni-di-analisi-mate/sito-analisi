Which set solves $x(x-3)\geq x+5$?

- $(-\infty,-1]\cup[5,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-5]\cup[1,+\infty)$
- $[-1,5]$
- $(-\infty,-1)\cup(5,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Subtracting $x+5$ gives $x^2-4x-5=(x+1)(x-5)$. The product is positive outside the roots and zero at $-1$ and $5$, so the endpoints are included. At $x=0$, the original sides equal $0$ and $5$, so the middle point is excluded. At $x=5$ both sides equal $10$, so that boundary belongs to the solution, as does the other zero.
