Solve $-x^2+11x-28\leq0$.

- $(4,7)$
- $(-\infty,4]\cup[7,+\infty)$
- $[4,7]$
- $(-\infty,4)\cup(7,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The trinomial equals $-(x-4)(x-7)$. Dividing by $-1$ gives $(x-4)(x-7)\geq0$. The factors have the same sign outside $[4,7]$, and the product is zero at the endpoints, so $4$ and $7$ are included.
