How many real roots does $4x^2+4x+2=0$ have?

- Infinitely many.
- One double root.
- None.
- Two distinct roots.

## Explanation

The discriminant is $4^2-4\cdot4\cdot2=-16$. Since it is negative, the equation has no real roots. Completing the square also gives $4x^2+4x+2=(2x+1)^2+1$. The left-hand side is at least $1$ for every real value of the unknown, so it cannot reach zero.
