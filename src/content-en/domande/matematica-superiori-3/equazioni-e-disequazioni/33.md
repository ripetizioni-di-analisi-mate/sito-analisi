A parabola has zeros at $-2$ and $4$ and passes through $(0,8)$. Where is its polynomial positive?

- $[-2,4]$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(4,+\infty)$
- $(-2,4)$
- $(0,4)$

## Explanation

The polynomial is $a(x+2)(x-4)$. At $0$, we have $-8a=8$, so $a=-1$. The product of the linear factors is negative between the roots, and the negative coefficient makes the polynomial positive on $(-2,4)$.
