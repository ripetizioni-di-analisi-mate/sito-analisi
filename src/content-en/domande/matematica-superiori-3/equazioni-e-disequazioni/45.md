The expression $\dfrac{x^2-1}{x-1}$ simplifies to $x+1$. At which value does the line representing $x+1$ have a point missing from the graph of the fraction?

- $x=-1$
- $x=2$
- $x=0$
- $x=1$

## Explanation

At $x=1$ the line has y-coordinate $2$, while the fraction has a zero denominator. At every other value the factor $x-1$ can be canceled and the graphs agree. To see why, write $x^2-1=(x-1)(x+1)$. Cancellation is valid under the condition $x\neq1$. The missing point therefore has coordinates $(1,2)$ in the graph of the simplified expression.
