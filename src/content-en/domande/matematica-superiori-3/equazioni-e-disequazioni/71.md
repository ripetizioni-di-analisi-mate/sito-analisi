A rectangle has area $x(8-x)\,\mathrm{m^2}$ with $0<x<8$. How many values of $x$ give an area of exactly $16\,\mathrm{m^2}$?

- Infinitely many.
- One.
- None.
- Two.

## Explanation

The equation $x(8-x)=16$ is equivalent to $x^2-8x+16=(x-4)^2=0$. The only root, $4$, belongs to the domain.
