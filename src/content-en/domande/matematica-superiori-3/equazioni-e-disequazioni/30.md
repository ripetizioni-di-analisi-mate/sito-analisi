The product $(x-2)(x-3)(x-4)$ is negative at $x=0$. What is its sign on $(2,3)$?

- Zero.
- Positive.
- Negative.
- It changes within $(2,3)$.

## Explanation

Between $0$ and any point in $(2,3)$, only the simple zero $2$ is crossed. The factor $x-2$ becomes positive, while the other two stay negative. So the product becomes positive. To check, take $x=\dfrac52$. The three factors then equal $\dfrac12$, $-\dfrac12$, and $-\dfrac32$, so the product is $\dfrac38>0$. There are no other zeros inside the interval.
