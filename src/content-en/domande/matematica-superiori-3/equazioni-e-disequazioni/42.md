Solve $\dfrac{1}{(x-2)^2}>0$.

- $\mathbb R$
- $(-\infty,2)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{2\}$
- $(2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The numerator is positive and the square in the denominator is positive for every $x\neq2$. At $2$ the quotient is undefined. Both intervals on either side of $2$ are in the solution set.
