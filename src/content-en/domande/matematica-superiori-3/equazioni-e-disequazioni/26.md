What is the positive real root of $x^2+2x-2=0$?

- $-1+\sqrt2$
- $-1+\sqrt3$
- $1+\sqrt3$
- $\sqrt6-1$

## Explanation

Completing the square gives $(x+1)^2=3$. The two roots are $-1\pm\sqrt3$. Since $\sqrt3>1$, the positive one is $-1+\sqrt3$. The square is obtained by adding $1$ to both sides of $x^2+2x=2$. The second root, $-1-\sqrt3$, is negative because it adds two negative terms.
