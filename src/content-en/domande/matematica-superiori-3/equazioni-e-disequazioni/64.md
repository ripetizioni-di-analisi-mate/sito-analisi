A rectangle has perimeter $18\,\mathrm m$. If one side is $x$ meters long, which interval contains exactly the admissible values of $x$?

- $\left(0,\dfrac92\right)$
- $(0,18)$
- $[0,9]$
- $(0,9)$

## Explanation

The semiperimeter is $9\,\mathrm m$, so the other side is $9-x$ meters long. The conditions $x>0$ and $9-x>0$ give $0<x<9$. The perimeter is twice the sum of the side lengths. At $x=0$ the first side would be zero, while at $x=9$ the second would be zero. Thus the domain is the open interval $(0,9)$.
