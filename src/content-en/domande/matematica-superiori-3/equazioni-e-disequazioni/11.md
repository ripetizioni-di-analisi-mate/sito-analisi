For which values of $x$ does $x^2<2x+3$ hold?

- $(-3,1)$
- $(-\infty,-1)\cup(3,+\infty)$
- $[-1,3]$
- $(-1,3)$

## Explanation

Moving all terms to the left gives $x^2-2x-3=(x+1)(x-3)<0$. The factors have opposite signs between the roots $-1$ and $3$. The endpoints give equality, so they are excluded. At $x=0$ the parabola has y-coordinate $0$ and the line has y-coordinate $3$, so the parabola is below the line on that interval. The solution is $(-1,3)$.
