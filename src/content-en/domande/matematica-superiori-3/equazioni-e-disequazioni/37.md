The graph shows $y=x^2-4$ and the line $y=0$. For which values of $x$ is the parabola below the line?

::grafico{f="x^2-4;0" xmin=-3.5 xmax=3.5 ymin=-5 ymax=7 punti="-2,0,pieno;2,0,pieno" titolo="Parabola and horizontal axis"}

- $[-2,2]$
- $(-2,2)$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(2,+\infty)$
- $\{-2,2\}$

## Explanation

The curves meet at $x=-2$ and $x=2$. Between these values, $x^2<4$, so $x^2-4<0$. The y-coordinates are equal at the two intersections. The vertex has x-coordinate $0$ and y-coordinate $-4$, an example of a point below the line. To the left of $-2$ and to the right of $2$, we have $x^2>4$, so the parabola lies above the line.
