What is the solution set of the system $\begin{cases}x^2\leq4,\\x\geq2\end{cases}$?

- $\varnothing$
- $\{2\}$
- $[-2,2]$
- $[2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The first condition gives $[-2,2]$, and the second gives $[2,+\infty)$. The only common value is the endpoint $2$, which belongs to both sets. The first condition requires a distance from the origin of at most $2$. The value $x=2$ has square $4$ and also satisfies the second condition. For $x>2$ the square exceeds $4$.
