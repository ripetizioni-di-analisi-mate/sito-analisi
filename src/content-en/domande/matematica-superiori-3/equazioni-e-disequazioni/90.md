Two consecutive integers satisfy $n^2+(n+1)^2=25$. What is the sum of all possible values of the smaller integer $n$?

- $-1$
- $0$
- $5$
- $-5$

## Explanation

The equation reduces to $n^2+n-12=0$, or $(n+4)(n-3)=0$. The smaller integer can be $-4$ or $3$, whose sum is $-1$.
