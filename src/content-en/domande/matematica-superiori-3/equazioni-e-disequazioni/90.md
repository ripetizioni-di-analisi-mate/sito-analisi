For which $a$ is $x^2+ax+4$ zero at $x=-2$?

- $4$
- $0$
- $-4$
- $2$

## Explanation

Substituting $x=-2$ gives $4-2a+4=0$, so $a=4$. In this case the trinomial is $(x+2)^2$. On substitution, the square of $-2$ supplies the first $4$ and the linear term is $-2a$. With the value found, the factor $x+2$ occurs twice, so $-2$ is a double root.
