What is the union of $(-\infty,-1)$ and $[-1,2]$?

- $(-\infty,2]$
- $(-\infty,-1)\cup(-1,2]$
- $(-\infty,2)$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

Every number below $-1$ belongs to the first set, and the numbers from $-1$ to $2$, including both endpoints, belong to the second. So the union includes $-1$ and extends through $2$. For an endpoint to belong to the union, it is enough that it belong to either set. In particular, the second interval supplies $-1$, so there is no missing point between the two portions.
