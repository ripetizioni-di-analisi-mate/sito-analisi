In $\dfrac{x}{x-1}<2$, for which values of $x$ does multiplying by $x-1$ reverse the inequality?

- $x=1$
- $x<1$
- For every $x\neq1$.
- $x>1$

## Explanation

The inequality reverses when the multiplier is negative. Here $x-1<0$ is equivalent to $x<1$. At $x=1$ the fraction is undefined.
