For which values of $x$ does $(x-1)^2(x-4)^2\leq0$ hold?

- $[1,4]$
- $\{1,4\}$
- $\mathbb R$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

Each factor is nonnegative, so the product is nonnegative. To satisfy $\leq0$ it must be zero. This occurs at $1$ or $4$. At every other point both squares are positive, and so is their product. For example, $x=2$ is between the zeros and gives $1\cdot4=4>0$. The solution set therefore contains only two isolated points.
