How many points do the graphs $y=(x-2)^2+3$ and $y=3$ have in common?

- Infinitely many.
- None.
- Two.
- One.

## Explanation

Equating the y-coordinates gives $(x-2)^2=0$, so $x=2$ and $y=3$. The system has just one pair, $(2,3)$. The number $3$ is the minimum height of the parabola, since the added square is positive for every $x\neq2$. The horizontal line therefore passes through the vertex and meets no other point of the curve.
