Which quadratic polynomial has roots $-1$ and $3$ and coefficient of $x^2$ equal to $2$?

- $2x^2+4x-6$
- $2x^2-4x-6$
- $x^2-2x-3$
- $2x^2-4x+6$

## Explanation

The factors are $x+1$ and $x-3$. The required leading coefficient gives $2(x+1)(x-3)$, which expands to $2x^2-4x-6$. The factor in front of the product changes the coefficients while keeping the same two roots. Expanding gives the cross terms $-3x$ and $x$ inside the product, which become $-4x$ after multiplication.
