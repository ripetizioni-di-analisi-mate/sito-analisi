For $x<-1$, what is the sign of $-(x+1)(x-2)^2$?

- Zero.
- It changes within the interval.
- Negative.
- Positive.

## Explanation

The factor $x+1$ is negative and $(x-2)^2$ is positive for $x<-1$. The product before the minus sign is negative, so the full expression is positive. The only zero of the square is $2$, outside the given interval. At $x=-2$ we get $-(-1)\cdot16=16$, consistent with the signs of the same factors throughout the interval.
