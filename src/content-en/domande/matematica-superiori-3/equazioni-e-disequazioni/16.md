For which value of $x$ is the right-hand side of $x+1=\dfrac{3}{2x-1}$ undefined?

- $\dfrac12$
- $0$
- $1$
- $-\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The fraction is undefined where $2x-1=0$. Adding $1$ and dividing by $2$ gives $x=\dfrac12$. All other values make the denominator nonzero. For example, at $x=0$ the right-hand side equals $-3$. Finding where the fraction is defined comes before looking for values that make the two sides equal.
