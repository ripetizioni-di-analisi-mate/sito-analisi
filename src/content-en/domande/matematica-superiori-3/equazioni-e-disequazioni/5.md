Solve $\dfrac{x^2-16}{x-4}\geq0$.

- $[-4,+\infty)$
- $[-4,4)\cup(4,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-4]\cup(4,+\infty)$
- $(-4,4)\cup(4,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The domain excludes $4$. For $x\neq4$, factoring the numerator as $(x-4)(x+4)$ reduces the fraction to $x+4$. The condition $x+4\geq0$ includes $-4$, while $4$ remains outside the domain. At $x=-4$ the numerator is zero and the denominator is $-8$, so equality is valid. At $x=4$ the denominator is also $0$.
