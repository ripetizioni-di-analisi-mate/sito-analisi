A rectangle has area $12\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Let $x$ be the shorter side length. Given that $x>0$, which condition is equivalent to the other side being at least as long as the first?

- $x\leq12$
- $x^2\leq12$
- $x^2=12$
- $x^2\geq12$

## Explanation

The other side has length $\dfrac{12}{x}$. Since $\dfrac{12}{x}\geq x$ and $x>0$, multiplying by $x$ gives $12\geq x^2$ without reversing the inequality.
