Which value of $c$ makes $x^2+6x+c$ a perfect square?

- $3$
- $-9$
- $9$
- $36$

## Explanation

The linear term of $(x+a)^2$ is $2ax$, so here $2a=6$ and $a=3$. The constant term of the square is $a^2=9$. Expanding $(x+3)^2$ gives $x^2$, the cross term $2\cdot x\cdot3=6x$, and $3^2=9$. The constant term is therefore determined by the coefficient of the linear term.
