Which operation is reversible for every $x\in\mathbb R$ in $x(x-1)=0$?

- Divide both sides by $x-1$.
- Divide both sides by $2$.
- Multiply both sides by $0$.
- Divide both sides by $x$.

## Explanation

The number $2$ is nonzero for every value of $x$. Multiplying by $2$ always recovers the original equation. The factors involving the variable are zero at $0$ and $1$, respectively.
