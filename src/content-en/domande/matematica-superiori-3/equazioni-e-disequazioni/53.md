Which pair solves the system $\begin{cases}y=x^2,\\y=3x-2\end{cases}$?

- $(2,2)$
- $(2,4)$
- $(0,-2)$
- $(1,4)$

## Explanation

Substituting $3x-2$ for $y$ in the first equation gives $(x-1)(x-2)=0$. For $x=2$, both equations give y-coordinate $4$. The other solution is $(1,1)$, which is not among the listed pairs.
