Solve $(2x+1)^2=9$.

- $\{-2,1\}$
- $\{-1,2\}$
- $\{1\}$
- $\{-2,2\}$

## Explanation

The equations $2x+1=3$ and $2x+1=-3$ give $x=1$ and $x=-2$, respectively. Both values produce a square equal to $9$. Subtracting $1$ before dividing by $2$ gives $2x=2$ and $2x=-4$, respectively. The second equation corresponds to the case in which the expression being squared is negative.
