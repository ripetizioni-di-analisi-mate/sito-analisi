What is the solution set of $\dfrac{(x-4)^2}{x^2-16}=0$?

- $\{-4\}$
- $\{-4,4\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\{4\}$

## Explanation

The numerator is zero only at $4$. At $4$ the denominator equals $4^2-16=0$, so the quotient is undefined. There are no zeros in the domain. The denominator factors as $(x-4)(x+4)$, so it also excludes $-4$. At every other point the fraction equals $\dfrac{x-4}{x+4}$. The only zero of the simplified expression remains outside the original domain.
