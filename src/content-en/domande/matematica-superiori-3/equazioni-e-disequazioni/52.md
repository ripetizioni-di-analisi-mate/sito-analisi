Which numbers satisfy at least one of $x^2>9$ and $x<-1$?

- $(-1,3)$
- $(-\infty,-3)$
- $(-\infty,-1)\cup(3,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-3)\cup(3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The first condition gives $(-\infty,-3)\cup(3,+\infty)$. The branch $(-\infty,-3)$ is contained in $(-\infty,-1)$. Taking the union leaves $(-\infty,-1)$ and $(3,+\infty)$.
