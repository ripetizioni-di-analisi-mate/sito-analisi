The chart describes the sign of a rational expression. The symbols $0$ and $\times$ indicate a zero and a point outside the domain, respectively. Which set solves $f(x)\leq0$?

$$
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
x & (-\infty,-2) & -2 & (-2,1) & 1 & (1,+\infty)\\ \hline
f(x) & + & 0 & - & \times & +
\end{array}
$$

- $[-2,1]$
- $(-\infty,-2]\cup(1,+\infty)$
- $(-2,1)$
- $[-2,1)$

## Explanation

The minus sign in the chart corresponds to $(-2,1)$. The value $-2$ is a zero and satisfies $\leq0$. The value $1$ is outside the domain and remains excluded.
