One root of $2x^2-5x-3=0$ is $3$. What is the other?

- $\dfrac12$
- $-\dfrac32$
- $-1$
- $-\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The product of the two roots is $\dfrac{-3}{2}$. If the other root is $r$, then $3r=-\dfrac32$, so $r=-\dfrac12$. The ratio uses the coefficient of $x^2$, which is $2$. This can be checked by factoring the polynomial as $(x-3)(2x+1)$. Setting $2x+1$ equal to zero gives the same value.
