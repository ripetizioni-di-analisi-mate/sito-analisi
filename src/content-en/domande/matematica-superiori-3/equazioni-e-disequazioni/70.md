A square of side length $x$ has area at most $25\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and perimeter greater than $8\,\mathrm m$. Which measurements $x$ are possible, in meters?

- $[2,5]$
- $(0,5]$
- $(2,5)$
- $(2,5]$

## Explanation

Since $x>0$, the condition $x^2\leq25$ is equivalent to $0<x\leq5$. The inequality $4x>8$ requires $x>2$. The intersection is $(2,5]$.
