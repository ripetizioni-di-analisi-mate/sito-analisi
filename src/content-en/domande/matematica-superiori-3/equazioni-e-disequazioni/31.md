What sign does $x^2-4x+6$ have for every real $x$?

- Negative near $2$.
- Strictly negative.
- Strictly positive.
- Zero at $2$ and positive elsewhere.

## Explanation

The trinomial equals $(x-2)^2+2$. Since the square is nonnegative, the sum is at least $2$ and hence positive. The minimum is reached at $x=2$, because there $x-2=0$. Even at that point the value is $2>0$. Therefore the entire graph is above the horizontal axis, including its vertex.
