What sign does $x^2-4x+6$ have for every real $x$?

- Negative near $2$.
- Strictly negative.
- Strictly positive.
- Zero at $2$ and positive elsewhere.

## Explanation

The trinomial equals $(x-2)^2+2$. Since the square is nonnegative, the sum is at least $2$ and hence positive.
