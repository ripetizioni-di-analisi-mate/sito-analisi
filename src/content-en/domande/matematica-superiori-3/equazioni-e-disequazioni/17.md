What is $b$ if $x=2$ is a root of $x^2+bx-10=0$?

- $6$
- $7$
- $3$
- $-3$

## Explanation

Substituting $x=2$ gives $4+2b-10=0$. Therefore $2b=6$ and $b=3$. The term $2b$ comes from multiplying $b$ by the given root. As a check, when $b=3$ the trinomial is $(x-2)(x+5)$ and the first factor is zero at $2$.
