Solve $-(x-1)(x+3)\ge0$.

- $(-3,1)$
- $(-\infty,-3]\cup[1,+\infty)$
- $[-1,3]$
- $[-3,1]$

## Explanation

Between $-3$ and $1$ the factors have opposite signs, so the product with the leading minus is positive. At the roots it is $0$ and outside it is negative, giving $[-3,1]$.

To check, use $x=0$, where the product with the leading minus equals $-(-1)\cdot3=3$. At $x=2$ it equals $-1\cdot5=-5$. The leading minus changes the sign of the product of the factors, and equality keeps the endpoints included.
