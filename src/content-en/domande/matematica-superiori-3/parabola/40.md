What are the ordinates of the two points on $x^2=8y$ that are $2$ units from the axis?

- $-\dfrac12$ and $\dfrac12$.
- Both are $\dfrac12$.
- Both are $2$.
- Both are $4$.

## Explanation

The axis is $x=0$, so the x-coordinates are $2$ and $-2$. In both cases $x^2=4$, so $8y=4$ and $y=\dfrac12$.
