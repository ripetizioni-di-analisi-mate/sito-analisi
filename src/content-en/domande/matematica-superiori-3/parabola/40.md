What are the ordinates of the two points on $x^2=8y$ that are $2$ units from the axis?

- $-\dfrac12$ and $\dfrac12$.
- Both are $\dfrac12$.
- Both are $2$.
- Both are $4$.

## Explanation

The axis is $x=0$, so the x-coordinates are $2$ and $-2$. In both cases $x^2=4$, so $8y=4$ and $y=\dfrac12$.

The points are therefore $\left(-2,\dfrac12\right)$ and $\left(2,\dfrac12\right)$. The distance to the axis is horizontal and fixes the absolute value of the x-coordinate. The ordinates are equal because $x^2$ takes the same value with both signs. In the graph the two points are on a single horizontal line.
