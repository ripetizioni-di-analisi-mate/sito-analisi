A horizontal line meets $y=x^2+3$ at x-coordinates $-2$ and $2$. What is the equation of the line?

- $y=5$
- $y=7$
- $y=4$
- $y=1$

## Explanation

Substituting $x=2$ gives $y=4+3=7$ on the parabola. The square is the same at $x=-2$, so the horizontal line through both points is at height $7$.

A horizontal line has equation $y=q$ and zero slope. The common ordinate therefore determines $q$ directly. As a check, substituting $y=7$ into the parabola gives $x^2=4$, whose solutions are the two given x-coordinates. The height $4$ would omit the constant term $3$.
