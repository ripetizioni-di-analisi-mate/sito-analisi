A horizontal line meets $y=x^2+3$ at x-coordinates $-2$ and $2$. What is the equation of the line?

- $y=5$
- $y=7$
- $y=4$
- $y=1$

## Explanation

Substituting $x=2$ gives $y=4+3=7$ on the parabola. The square is the same at $x=-2$, so the horizontal line through both points is at height $7$.
