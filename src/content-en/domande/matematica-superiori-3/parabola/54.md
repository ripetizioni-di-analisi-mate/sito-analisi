How many tangents to $y=x^2$ pass through $(2,4)$?

- $0$
- $2$
- $1$
- Infinitely many.

## Explanation

Requiring $y=2tx-t^2$ to pass through $(2,4)$ gives $4=4t-t^2$, or $(t-2)^2=0$. There is one x-coordinate of contact, $t=2$.

The given point is already on the parabola because $2^2=4$. The only tangent through it touches at that point. Setting $t=2$ gives $y=4x-4$, and its difference from the quadratic is $(x-2)^2$, confirming tangency. The value $t=2$ counts as one line even though it is a double root.
