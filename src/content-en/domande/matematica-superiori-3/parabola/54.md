The tangents to $y=x^2$ at horizontal coordinates $1$ and $3$ intersect at $T$. What is the vertical distance between $T$ and the parabola at this same horizontal coordinate?

- $2$
- $3$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

At horizontal coordinate $t$ the tangent is $y=2tx-t^2$, since the difference from the parabola is $(x-t)^2$. The lines $y=2x-1$ and $y=6x-9$ meet at $(2,3)$, while the parabola contains $(2,4)$.
