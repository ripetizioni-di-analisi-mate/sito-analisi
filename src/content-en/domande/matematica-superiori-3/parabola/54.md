The tangents to the parabola $y=x^2$ at the points with $x$-coordinates $1$ and $3$ meet at $T$. What is the vertical distance between $T$ and the point of the parabola with the same $x$-coordinate as $T$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

The tangent to $y=x^2$ at the point with $x$-coordinate $t$ is $y=2tx-t^2$, because the difference $x^2-(2tx-t^2)=(x-t)^2$ has a double root at $t$. For $t=1$ and for $t=3$ we get the lines $y=2x-1$ and $y=6x-9$.

Equating the $y$-coordinates gives $2x-1=6x-9$, hence $x=2$ and $T=(2,3)$. The point of the parabola with $x$-coordinate $2$ is $(2,4)$, therefore the vertical distance is $4-3=1$.
