What is the parabola with vertical axis, roots $0$ and $4$, and maximum value $8$?

- $y=-2x(x-4)$
- $y=2x(x-4)$
- $y=-x(x-4)$
- $y=-2x(x+4)$

## Explanation

The given roots yield $y=ax(x-4)$. The vertex has x-coordinate $2$, so $8=a\cdot2\cdot(-2)=-4a$, giving $a=-2$.

Check the maximum by rewriting the product as $-2\bigl((x-2)^2-4\bigr)=8-2(x-2)^2$. The subtracted term is nonnegative, so the greatest ordinate really is $8$. A positive choice of $a$ would retain the roots and give a minimum at the vertex.
