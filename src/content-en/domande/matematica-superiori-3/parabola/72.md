What is the vertex of the parabola with focus $(-2,1)$ and directrix $x=4$?

- $(2,1)$
- $(1,1)$
- $(-3,1)$
- $(1,0)$

## Explanation

The horizontal axis is $y=1$. The vertex is the midpoint of $(-2,1)$ and $(4,1)$, so its x-coordinate is $\dfrac{-2+4}{2}=1$.

Projecting the focus to the directrix is a horizontal movement and leaves the ordinate at $1$. From the vertex $(1,1)$, the focus is $3$ units left and the directrix $3$ units right. This checks both data and shows why the vertex x-coordinate is between $-2$ and $4$.
