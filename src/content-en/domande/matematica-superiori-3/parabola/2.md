For which values of $x$ does $y=(x-4)^2-4$ lie above the x-axis?

- $(2,6)$
- $(-\infty,2)\cup(6,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,2]\cup[6,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,0)\cup(8,+\infty)$

## Explanation

We need $(x-4)^2>4$, so the distance from $x$ to $4$ must exceed $2$. The two possibilities are $x<2$ and $x>6$. At $2$ and $6$ the graph is on the axis.
