What condition makes $y=mx+1$ tangent to $y=x^2+5$?

- $m=\pm2$
- $m=0$
- Any value of $m$.
- $m=\pm4$

## Explanation

The intersection equation is $x^2-mx+4=0$. The discriminant is $m^2-16$, which vanishes exactly when $m=\pm4$.

At $m=4$ the difference factors as $(x-2)^2$, while at $m=-4$ it is $(x+2)^2$. Both lines pass through $(0,1)$ and touch on opposite sides of the axis. At $m=0$ the polynomial would be $x^2+4$, which has no real roots.
