What is the focus of $(y-2)^2=12(x+3)$?

- $(-6,2)$
- $(-3,5)$
- $(9,2)$
- $(0,2)$

## Explanation

The vertex is $(-3,2)$ and the horizontal displacement to the focus is $p=3$ because $4p=12$. Thus $F=(-3+3,2)$.

The directrix is the vertical line $x=-3-3=-6$. The focus has greater x-coordinate than the vertex because $p>0$ and the curve opens to the right. For example, $(0,8)$ satisfies the equation and is $6$ units from both $(0,2)$ and the vertical line $x=-6$.
