A parabola is formed by shifting $y=x^2$ three units right and two units down. What is its equation?

- $y=(x+3)^2-2$
- $y=(x-3)^2-2$
- $y=(x-3)^2+2$
- $y=(x+2)^2-3$

## Explanation

The shift moves the vertex from $(0,0)$ to $(3,-2)$ and preserves $a=1$. The vertex form is $y=(x-3)^2-2$.

To recover the original x-coordinate, subtract the horizontal shift to get $x-3$. Then subtract $2$ from its ordinate. For example, $(1,1)$ moves to $(4,-1)$, and the new equation gives the ordinate $(4-3)^2-2=-1$ at that point.
