What is the range of $f(x)=3(x+2)^2-5$ on $\mathbb R$?

- $(-\infty,-5]$
- $(-5,+\infty)$
- $[-5,+\infty)$
- $[-2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The square takes every value from $0$ upwards, so $3(x+2)^2-5$ takes every value from $-5$ upwards. The endpoint is included because $f(-2)=-5$.
