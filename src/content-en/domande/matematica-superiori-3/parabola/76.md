Two positive numbers add to $14$. What is their maximum product?

- $28$
- $49$
- $196$
- $42$

## Explanation

Writing the numbers as $x$ and $14-x$ gives the product $x(14-x)=49-(x-7)^2$. The maximum $49$ occurs at $x=7$.

Both numbers must be positive, so $0<x<14$. The value $7$ is in this interval and gives $14-7=7$ for the second number. The product is $49$ and decreases by $(x-7)^2$ whenever the two parts of the sum differ.
