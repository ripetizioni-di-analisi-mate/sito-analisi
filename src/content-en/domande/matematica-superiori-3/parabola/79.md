A product is given by $P(n)=25-(n-2{,}5)^2$ with $n\in\{1,2,3,4\}$. Which values give the maximum?

- Only $n=2$.
- Only $n=3$.
- $n=2$ and $n=3$
- $n=1$ and $n=4$

## Explanation

Of the permitted values, $2$ and $3$ are both $0{,}5$ from $2{,}5$. The square subtracted is therefore smallest and equal in the two cases.

For $n=2$ and $n=3$ the term subtracted is $0{,}25$ and the product is $24{,}75$. The other two permitted values are $1{,}5$ from the centre and subtract $2{,}25$. At the vertex $n=2{,}5$ the value would be $25$, but that value of $n$ is outside the given set.
