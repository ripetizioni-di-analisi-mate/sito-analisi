What is the length of the vertical chord of $x=y^2-1$ on the line $x=3$?

- $2$
- $8$
- $4$
- $2\sqrt3$

## Explanation

The equation $3=y^2-1$ gives $y=\pm2$. The endpoints have the same x-coordinate, so the length is the difference $2-(-2)=4$.

The full coordinate pairs are $(3,-2)$ and $(3,2)$. The midpoint $(3,0)$ lies on the horizontal axis $y=0$. Each endpoint is $2$ units from it, so adding the distances gives $4$. Taking only the positive root would give the distance from the midpoint and omit half the chord.
