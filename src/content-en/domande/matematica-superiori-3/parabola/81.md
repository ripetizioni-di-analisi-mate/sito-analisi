An arch has cross-section $y=9-x^2$ and the ground is $y=0$. What is its width at ground level?

- $3$
- $6$
- $9$
- $18$

## Explanation

Setting $9-x^2=0$ gives $x=-3$ and $x=3$. Both endpoints are on the ground, so the width is $3-(-3)=6$.

The vertex is $(0,9)$, so its axis is the vertical line $x=0$. Each foot lies $3$ units from the axis on an opposite side. The number $3$ is therefore the half-width, while $9$ is the maximum height. They describe different directions in the cross-section.
