A point is $3$ units from the focus and $4$ from the directrix. What is its relation to the parabola determined by them?

- The point is on the locus.
- The point is outside the locus.
- It is the vertex of the parabola.
- It lies on the locus if the directrix is horizontal.

## Explanation

The definition requires the two distances to be equal. Since $3\ne4$, the point fails the condition regardless of the orientation of the directrix.
