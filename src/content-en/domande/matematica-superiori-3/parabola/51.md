What is the horizontal tangent to $y=-x^2+4x+1$?

- $y=4$
- $y=5$
- $y=1$
- $x=2$

## Explanation

Completing the square gives $y=5-(x-2)^2$. The horizontal line at the vertex height gives the double root $2$, so it is $y=5$.

A horizontal line has the same ordinate at every point. For instance, height $4$ would give $(x-2)^2=1$ and thus two intersections. Height $5$ gives $(x-2)^2=0$ instead. The axis $x=2$ is vertical and passes through the same vertex.
