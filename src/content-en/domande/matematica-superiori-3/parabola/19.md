On which of these intervals is $f(x)=-3(x-4)^2+2$ strictly increasing?

- $[4,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,4]$
- $\mathbb R$
- $[3,5]$

## Explanation

To the left of $4$, the distance $4-x$ decreases as $x$ increases. The term subtracted from $2$ decreases, so $f$ increases up to the vertex.

For example, moving from $x=3$ to $x=4$ reduces the distance from the axis from $1$ to zero, and the ordinate rises from $-1$ to $2$. To the right of the vertex the distance increases again, so $[3,5]$ contains both a rise and a fall.
