On which of these intervals is $f(x)=-3(x-4)^2+2$ strictly increasing?

- $[4,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,4]$
- $\mathbb R$
- $[3,5]$

## Explanation

To the left of $4$, the distance $4-x$ decreases as $x$ increases. The term subtracted from $2$ decreases, so $f$ increases up to the vertex.
