What directrix results from reflecting $y=x^2$ across the x-axis?

- $y=-\dfrac14$
- $x=\dfrac14$
- $y=\dfrac14$
- $y=4$

## Explanation

The reflected parabola is $y=-x^2$, so $4p=-1$ and $p=-\dfrac14$. The vertex stays at the origin, hence the directrix is $y=-p=\dfrac14$.

Reflection sends $(x,y)$ to $(x,-y)$. The original directrix was $y=-\dfrac14$, so all its points move to height $\dfrac14$. The focus also moves to its opposite ordinate below the vertex. Reflection preserves distances, so it preserves the defining condition of the locus.
