The point $P(2,1)$ lies on the parabola with focus $F(0,1)$ and directrix $y=d$, with $d<1$. Find $d$.

- $d=-1$
- $d=-2$
- $d=0$
- $d=3$

## Explanation

The horizontal segment $PF$ has length $2$. The distance from $P$ to the directrix is $1-d$ because $d<1$. The equation $1-d=2$ gives $d=-1$.
