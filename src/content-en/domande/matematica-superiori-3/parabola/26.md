A parabola with vertical axis has vertex $(2,3)$. Which of these points cannot lie on it?

- $(3,3)$
- $(3,4)$
- $(3,2)$
- $(0,7)$

## Explanation

In $y=a(x-2)^2+3$, point $(3,3)$ would require $3=a+3$, hence $a=0$. The other points give nonzero coefficients.

If $x\ne2$, the square is positive and its product with $a\ne0$ is nonzero. The ordinate must therefore differ from $3$. At $(3,4)$ and $(3,2)$ the changes in height are $1$ and $-1$, consistent with opening up or down.
