The graph shows $V(0,0)$ and $F(0,1)$. What is the equation of the directrix?

::grafico{f="x^2/4" xmin=-3.6 xmax=3.6 ymin=-2 ymax=4 uguale=1 punti="0,0,pieno,V;0,1,pieno,F" legenda=0 titolo="The axis of the parabola passes through V and F"}

- $y=1$
- $x=-1$
- $y=-1$
- $y=-2$

## Explanation

The distance between $V$ and $F$ is $1$. The directrix is perpendicular to the vertical axis and is equally far from the vertex on the opposite side from the focus.
