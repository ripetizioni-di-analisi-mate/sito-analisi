In the graph, $V(2,3)$ is the vertex and $A(1,2)$ lies on the curve. Which point is the reflection of $A$ across the axis?

::grafico{f="-(x-2)^2+3" xmin=-1 xmax=5 ymin=-3 ymax=5 punti="2,3,pieno,V;1,2,pieno,A" legenda=0 titolo="Parabola with vertex V and point A"}

- $(1,4)$
- $(3,4)$
- $(-1,2)$
- $(3,2)$

## Explanation

The axis is $x=2$. Point $A$ is one unit to the left, so its reflection is one unit to the right and keeps ordinate $2$.
