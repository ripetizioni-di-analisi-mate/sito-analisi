Which line is tangent to $y=-x^2$ at height $-4$ and negative x-coordinate?

- $y=-4x+4$
- $y=4x-4$
- $y=4x+4$
- $y=-2x-4$

## Explanation

From $-x^2=-4$ and $x<0$ we get $t=-2$. In the tangent formula $a=-1$, so the slope is $-2t=4$ and the intercept is $t^2=4$.

The two points at height $-4$ have x-coordinates $-2$ and $2$. Requiring a negative x-coordinate selects the first and gives a positive slope. Substituting $x=-2$ into the line gives $-8+4=-4$. Also, $-x^2-(4x+4)=-(x+2)^2$ confirms that the line touches without crossing the curve.
