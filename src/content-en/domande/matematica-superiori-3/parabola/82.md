Revenue is modelled by $R(p)=p(30-2p)$ with $0\le p\le15$. Which price $p$ maximizes revenue?

- $\dfrac{15}{2}$
- $15$
- $\dfrac{15}{4}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The roots are $0$ and $15$ and the coefficient of $p^2$ is negative. The vertex therefore gives a maximum at the midpoint $\dfrac{0+15}{2}=\dfrac{15}{2}$.

To check, write $R(p)=\dfrac{225}{2}-2\left(p-\dfrac{15}{2}\right)^2$. The term with a minus sign is zero at that price and negative at every other value. Since $\dfrac{15}{2}$ is between $0$ and $15$, the price is permitted by the model.
