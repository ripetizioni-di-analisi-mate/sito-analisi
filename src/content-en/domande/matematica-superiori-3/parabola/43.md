For which values of $q$ does $y=2x+q$ cut $y=x^2$ at two distinct points?

- $q>-1$
- $q\ge-1$
- $q<-1$
- $q>1$

## Explanation

Equating the expressions gives $(x-1)^2=q+1$. Two values of $x$ at a positive distance from $1$ require $q+1>0$.

At $q=-1$ the distance from $1$ would be zero, so the two roots would merge and the line would be tangent. For $q<-1$ the square would have to be negative. This is why the values producing two distinct intersections exclude the endpoint $-1$.
