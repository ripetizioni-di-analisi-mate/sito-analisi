Which coefficient makes $y=2x^2+bx+8$ tangent to the x-axis at a positive x-coordinate?

- $-8$
- $8$
- $-4$
- $4$

## Explanation

The discriminant must satisfy $b^2-64=0$, so $b=\pm8$. The x-coordinate of contact is $-\dfrac b4$, which is positive when $b=-8$.

The two choices making the discriminant zero give the quadratics $2(x-2)^2$ and $2(x+2)^2$. Both touch the axis, to the right and left of the origin respectively. The question specifies the first location, so the linear term must be $-8x$.
