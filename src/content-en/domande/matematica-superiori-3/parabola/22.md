A parabola has roots $-1$ and $5$. What is the x-coordinate of its vertex?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The axis of symmetry lies halfway between the roots. Its x-coordinate is $\dfrac{-1+5}{2}=2$.

Both roots are $3$ units from that x-coordinate. The form $a(x+1)(x-5)=a\bigl((x-2)^2-9\bigr)$ also shows that the square is zero at $x=2$. The vertex height depends on $a$, which remains free here. The two intercepts already determine the axis.
