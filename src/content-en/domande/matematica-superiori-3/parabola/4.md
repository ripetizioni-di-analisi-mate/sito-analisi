Find the equation of the parabola with focus $F(3,4)$ and directrix $y=0$.

- $(x-3)^2=16(y-2)$
- $(x-3)^2=-8(y-2)$
- $(x-3)^2=8(y-2)$
- $(x-3)^2=8(y-4)$

## Explanation

The vertex is halfway between $F$ and $(3,0)$, so it is $(3,2)$. The displacement from vertex to focus is $p=2$, so the coefficient on the right is $4p=8$.
