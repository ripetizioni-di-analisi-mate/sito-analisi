Find the parabola with vertical axis through $(0,3)$, $(1,2)$ and $(2,5)$.

- $y=x^2-2x+3$
- $y=2x^2-x+1$
- $y=-2x^2+x+3$
- $y=2x^2-3x+3$

## Explanation

The point with x-coordinate $0$ gives $c=3$. The other two give $a+b=-1$ and $2a+b=1$. Subtracting yields $a=2$ and then $b=-3$.

In the third condition, substituting $x=2$ gives $4a+2b+3=5$. First subtract $3$, then divide by $2$. The coefficients found give $2-3+3=2$ at $x=1$ and $8-6+3=5$ at $x=2$.
