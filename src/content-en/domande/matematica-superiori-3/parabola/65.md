Which x-coordinates have at least one point on $x=4-(y-1)^2$?

- $x<4$
- $x\ge4$
- $y\le4$
- $x\le4$

## Explanation

Isolating the square gives $(y-1)^2=4-x$. The right-hand side must be nonnegative, so $x\le4$. The vertex is at $x=4$.

At $x<4$, two ordinates can actually be constructed, $1+\sqrt{4-x}$ and $1-\sqrt{4-x}$. At $x=4$ they coincide at $1$, so the endpoint is included. For $x>4$, the right-hand side would be negative and cannot equal a real square.
