The parabolas $y=a(x-1)^2+2$ with $a>0$ all share one point. Which one?

- $(1,2)$
- $(0,2)$
- $(2,1)$
- $(1,0)$

## Explanation

At $x=1$ the term that depends on $a$ is zero. At every other x-coordinate the square is positive and the ordinate changes with $a$.

For example, with $a=1$ and $a=2$, the difference between ordinates at the same x-coordinate is $(x-1)^2$. The two parabolas have the same height only at $x=1$, where it is $2$. Every other positive value of $a$ still gives that height at $x=1$, so the point is common to the whole family.
