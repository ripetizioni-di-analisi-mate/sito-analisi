Which of the listed lines is tangent to $y=x^2$ and has y-intercept $-9$?

- $y=3x-9$
- $y=6x-9$
- $y=9x-9$
- $y=-3x-9$

## Explanation

An intercept of $-9$ requires $-t^2=-9$, so $t=\pm3$. The possible slopes are $\pm6$. The listed line with one of those slopes has slope $6$.

A direct check uses the intersection polynomial $x^2-6x+9=(x-3)^2$. The root is double, so the line touches at $x=3$. In general the intercept $-9$ also permits a second tangent of slope $-6$. The question asks which listed line meets both conditions.
