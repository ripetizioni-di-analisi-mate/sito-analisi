Which of the listed lines is tangent to $y=x^2$ and has y-intercept $-9$?

- $y=3x-9$
- $y=6x-9$
- $y=9x-9$
- $y=-3x-9$

## Explanation

An intercept of $-9$ requires $-t^2=-9$, so $t=\pm3$. The possible slopes are $\pm6$. The listed line with one of those slopes has slope $6$.
