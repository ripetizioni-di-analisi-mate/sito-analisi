The parabolas $y=x^2-4$ and $y=-x^2+4$ meet on the x-axis. How far apart are their intersection points?

- $2$
- $4$
- $8$
- $2\sqrt2$

## Explanation

Equating the expressions gives $2x^2=8$, hence $x=\pm2$. Both ordinates are $0$, so the distance is $2-(-2)=4$.

The two ordinates are opposite at every x-coordinate because one parabola is the reflection of the other across the x-axis. They can agree only when both are zero. The points are $(2,0)$ and $(-2,0)$. Their ordinate is the same, so the distance is the absolute difference of their x-coordinates.
