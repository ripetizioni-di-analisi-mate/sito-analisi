Which formula describes the upper branch of $x=2(y+1)^2-3$?

- $y=-1+\sqrt{\dfrac{x+3}{2}}$
- $y=-1-\sqrt{\dfrac{x+3}{2}}$
- $y=1+\sqrt{\dfrac{x+3}{2}}$
- $y=-1+\sqrt{2(x+3)}$

## Explanation

We get $(y+1)^2=\dfrac{x+3}{2}$. The upper branch has $y\ge-1$, so take $y+1$ equal to the nonnegative square root.
