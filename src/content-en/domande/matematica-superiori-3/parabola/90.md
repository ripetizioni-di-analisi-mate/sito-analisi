Which condition on $c$ gives two distinct tangents to $y=x^2+2$ through $(0,c)$?

- $c\le2$
- $c>2$
- $c<2$
- $c=2$

## Explanation

The tangent at x-coordinate $t$ has intercept $2-t^2$. We need two distinct solutions of $t^2=2-c$, so $2-c>0$ and $c<2$.

At $c=2$, the only solution is $t=0$, the contact at the vertex. For $c>2$, the right-hand side $2-c$ is negative and yields no real solutions. For $c<2$, the two contacts have opposite nonzero x-coordinates, so the slopes $2t$ of the two lines are different.
