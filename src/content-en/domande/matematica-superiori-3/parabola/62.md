What are the coordinates of the vertex of $x=2(y+3)^2-4$?

- $(-4,-3)$
- $(-3,-4)$
- $(4,3)$
- $(-4,3)$

## Explanation

The square is zero when $y=-3$. There $x=-4$, so writing the x-coordinate first gives $V=(-4,-3)$.

Since $2(y+3)^2\ge0$, the minimum x-coordinate is $-4$, reached only at ordinate $-3$. Ordinates $-2$ and $-4$ both give $x=-2$, to the right of the vertex. This check shows that the axis of symmetry is horizontal and has equation $y=-3$.
