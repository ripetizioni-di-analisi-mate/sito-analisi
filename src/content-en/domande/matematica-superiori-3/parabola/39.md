Which expression equates the distances from $P(x,y)$ to $F(2,3)$ and to the directrix $y=-1$?

- $\sqrt{(x-2)^2+(y-3)^2}=|x+1|$
- $\sqrt{(x+2)^2+(y+3)^2}=|y-1|$
- $\sqrt{(x-2)^2+(y-3)^2}=|y-1|$
- $\sqrt{(x-2)^2+(y-3)^2}=|y+1|$

## Explanation

Distance to the focus uses the differences $x-2$ and $y-3$. The directrix is horizontal, so its distance uses only the ordinate and equals $|y-(-1)|$.
