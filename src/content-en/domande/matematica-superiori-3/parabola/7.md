Find the directrix of $(y-2)^2=-12(x+1)$.

- $x=-4$
- $y=5$
- $x=2$
- $x=11$

## Explanation

The vertex is $(-1,2)$ and $4p=-12$, so $p=-3$. The directrix is vertical at x-coordinate $h-p=-1-(-3)=2$.

The axis is horizontal because the squared expression is a difference of ordinates. With $p=-3$ the focus has x-coordinate $h+p=-4$ and still has ordinate $2$. The vertex is halfway from the focus to the perpendicular foot on the directrix, whose x-coordinate must therefore be $2$.
