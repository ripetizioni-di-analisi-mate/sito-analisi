A chord of $y=x^2+1$ joins the points with x-coordinates $0$ and $4$. At which x-coordinate is the tangent parallel to the chord?

- $2$
- $0$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

The endpoints are $(0,1)$ and $(4,17)$, so the slope of the chord is $\dfrac{17-1}{4-0}=4$. The tangent has slope $2t$, giving $t=2$.
