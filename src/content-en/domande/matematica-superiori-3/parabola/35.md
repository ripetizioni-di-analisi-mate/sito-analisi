Which pair is a focus and directrix for $y=x^2$?

- $F(0,1)$, $y=-1$
- $F\left(\dfrac14,0\right)$, $x=-\dfrac14$
- $F\left(0,\dfrac14\right)$, $y=-\dfrac14$
- $F\left(0,-\dfrac14\right)$, $y=\dfrac14$

## Explanation

Compare $x^2=y$ with $x^2=4py$. From $4p=1$ we get $p=\dfrac14$. The focus is above the origin and the directrix below it, at the same distance.
