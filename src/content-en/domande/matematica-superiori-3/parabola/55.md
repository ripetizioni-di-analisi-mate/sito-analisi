What is the product of the slopes of the tangents to $y=x^2$ through $(0,-1)$?

- $4$
- $-1$
- $0$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The intercept condition $-t^2=-1$ gives $t=1$ or $t=-1$. The slopes are $2$ and $-2$, so the product is $-4$.

The lines are $y=2x-1$ and $y=-2x-1$. Their common intercept ensures that they pass through the assigned point. Their contacts $(1,1)$ and $(-1,1)$ reflect across the axis of the parabola. The slopes therefore have equal magnitudes and opposite signs, making their product negative.
