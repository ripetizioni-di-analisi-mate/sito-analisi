How many points do $y=x^2+2$ and $y=1$ have in common?

- $1$
- $2$
- $0$
- Infinitely many.

## Explanation

Equating ordinates gives $x^2=-1$, impossible over the reals. The parabola's minimum is $2$, so the line is below the whole curve.

Raising the line to height $2$ would reach the vertex and give $x^2=0$, with a double root. At height $1$, the minimum difference in ordinates is still $2-1=1$. That positive difference rules out any intersection.
