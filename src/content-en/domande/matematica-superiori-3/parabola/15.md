Which value of $u$ is needed to write $x^2+6x+2=(x+u)^2+v$?

- $6$
- $3$
- $-3$
- $9$

## Explanation

The expansion contains the term $2ux$, so $2u=6$ and $u=3$. The number $9$ is the square of $u$.

After finding $u$, the constant terms give $9+v=2$, so $v=-7$. Indeed $(x+3)^2-7=x^2+6x+9-7$. The value $u=-3$ would produce the linear term $-6x$, whereas the coefficient of $x$ here is positive.
