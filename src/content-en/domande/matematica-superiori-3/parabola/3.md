Find the parabola with vertical axis and vertex $V(4,-3)$ that passes through $P(6,5)$.

- $y=4(x-4)^2-3$
- $y=2(x+4)^2-3$
- $y=\dfrac12(x-4)^2-3$
- $y=2(x-4)^2-3$

## Explanation

The vertex determines the form $y=a(x-4)^2-3$. Passing through $P$ requires $5=4a-3$, giving $a=2$.

The change in ordinate is $5-(-3)=8$, while the squared horizontal displacement is $(6-4)^2=4$. Dividing the first by the second gives the coefficient. The point with x-coordinate $2$ is equally far from the axis and the formula gives it ordinate $5$ as well.
