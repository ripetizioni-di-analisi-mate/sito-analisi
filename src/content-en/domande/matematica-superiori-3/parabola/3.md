Find the parabola with vertical axis and vertex $V(4,-3)$ that passes through $P(6,5)$.

- $y=4(x-4)^2-3$
- $y=2(x+4)^2-3$
- $y=\dfrac12(x-4)^2-3$
- $y=2(x-4)^2-3$

## Explanation

The vertex determines the form $y=a(x-4)^2-3$. Passing through $P$ requires $5=4a-3$, giving $a=2$.
