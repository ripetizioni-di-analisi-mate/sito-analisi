A fountain's height is $h(t)=6-(t-2)^2$ in metres. When is it highest over $3\le t\le4$?

- $t=2$
- $t=4$
- $t=\dfrac72$
- $t=3$

## Explanation

Among the permitted times the distance from $2$ is smallest at $t=3$. There the square subtracted from $6$ is smallest and the height is largest.

The unconstrained model would give its maximum at $t=2$, which the question excludes. At the permitted endpoints, $h(3)=6-1=5$ and $h(4)=6-4=2$. Between those times the square increases, so the height decreases throughout the interval and the earliest time gives the maximum.
