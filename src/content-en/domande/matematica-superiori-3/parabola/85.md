Which pair of points attains the minimum of $g(x)=9-(x-2)^2$ on $[0,4]$?

- $(1,8)$ and $(3,8)$
- $(0,5)$ and $(4,5)$
- $(0,0)$ and $(4,0)$
- The minimum occurs only at $(2,9)$.

## Explanation

The value is smallest where $|x-2|$ is largest. On $[0,4]$ the greatest distance is $2$ at $0$ and $4$, where $g(x)=9-4=5$.
