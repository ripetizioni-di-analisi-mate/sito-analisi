Where does $x=-3$ meet $y=x^2+2x$, and what kind of intersection is it?

- $(-3,3)$, intersection with a vertical line that is not tangent.
- $(-3,3)$, point of tangency.
- $(-3,15)$, point of tangency.
- The vertical line misses the parabola.

## Explanation

Setting $x=-3$ gives $y=9-6=3$. The vertical line fixes one point because the graph has one ordinate for each x-coordinate. The tangent there instead has slope $2(-3)+2=-4$.
