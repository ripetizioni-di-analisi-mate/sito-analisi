Find the vertex and axis of $y=x^2-8x+13$.

- $V(4,3)$, $x=4$
- $V(-4,-3)$, $x=-4$
- $V(4,-3)$, $x=4$
- $V(8,13)$, $x=8$

## Explanation

Half of $-8$ is $-4$, so add and subtract $16$. This gives $y=(x-4)^2-3$, with axis $x=4$ and minimum height $-3$.

Completing the square leaves the quadratic unchanged because $13=16-3$. The coefficient of the square is positive, so no point can have ordinate below $-3$. Substituting into the original expression gives $f(4)=16-32+13=-3$. The vertex coordinates and the equation of the axis describe two different objects.
