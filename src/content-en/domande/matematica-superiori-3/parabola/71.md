Which horizontal line is the axis of symmetry of $x=5-(y+4)^2$?

- $y=4$
- $y=5$
- $y=-5$
- $y=-4$

## Explanation

At $y=-4+s$ and $y=-4-s$ the square is the same, $s^2$. The reflected points have equal x-coordinates, so the axis is $y=-4$.

The square vanishes at $y=-4$, giving vertex $(5,-4)$. The heights $-3$ and $-5$ are each one unit from $-4$ and give $x=4$. The midpoint of the vertical segment between the points is therefore precisely on the line $y=-4$.
