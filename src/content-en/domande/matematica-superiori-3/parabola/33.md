The focus of a parabola with vertical axis is $(1,5)$ and the vertex is $(1,3)$. Which way does it open and what is $p$?

- Downward, $p=-2$.
- Upward, $p=2$.
- Upward, $p=8$.
- Downward, $p=2$.

## Explanation

The displacement from vertex to focus is $5-3=2$, so $p>0$. The equation $(x-1)^2=4p(y-3)$ then gives $y\ge3$.
