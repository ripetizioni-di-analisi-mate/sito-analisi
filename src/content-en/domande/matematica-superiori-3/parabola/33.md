The focus of a parabola with vertical axis is $(1,5)$ and the vertex is $(1,3)$. Which way does it open and what is $p$?

- Downward, $p=-2$.
- Upward, $p=2$.
- Upward, $p=8$.
- Downward, $p=2$.

## Explanation

The displacement from vertex to focus is $5-3=2$, so $p>0$. The equation $(x-1)^2=4p(y-3)$ then gives $y\ge3$.

The number $p$ is a signed displacement along the vertical through the vertex. Here the focus is two units above it, so the directrix is two units below, at height $1$. The coefficient $4p=8$ appears in the locus equation and describes the same opening without equalling the focal displacement.
