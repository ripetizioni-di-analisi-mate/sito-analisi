For which $t$ does the tangent to $y=x^2-4x+1$ at x-coordinate $t$ pass through the origin and have slope less than $-4$?

- $1$
- $-1$
- $-2$
- $0$

## Explanation

Passing through the origin requires zero intercept, so $1-t^2=0$ and $t=\pm1$. The slopes $2t-4$ are $-6$ and $-2$. Only $t=-1$ satisfies the condition.
