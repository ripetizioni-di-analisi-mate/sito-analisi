Find the tangent to $y=2x^2-x+1$ at the point with x-coordinate $2$.

- $y=7x-7$
- $y=7x+7$
- $y=8x-9$
- $y=3x+1$

## Explanation

The parabola minus its tangent must equal $2(x-2)^2=2x^2-8x+8$. Subtracting this from the quadratic gives $y=7x-7$. At $x=2$ both curves have ordinate $7$.
