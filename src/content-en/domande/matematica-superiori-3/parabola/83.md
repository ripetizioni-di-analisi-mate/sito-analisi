A $24\,\mathrm{m}$ fence covers all four sides of a rectangle. A second fence of the same length covers three sides against a wall. What is the ratio of the second maximum area to the first?

- $2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $4$

## Explanation

The first area is $x(12-x)$, with maximum $36\,\mathrm{m}^2$. The second is $x(24-2x)$, with maximum $72\,\mathrm{m}^2$. The ratio is $\dfrac{72}{36}=2$.
