There are $18\,\mathrm{m}$ of fencing for three sides against a wall. If $x$ is the side perpendicular to the wall, what is the domain of the area for positive side lengths?

- $0\le x\le9$
- $0<x<18$
- $0<x\le9$
- $0<x<9$

## Explanation

Both side lengths must be positive. The parallel side uses the remaining length $18-2x$, so require $x>0$ and $18-2x>0$.

The factor $2$ appears because there are two sides perpendicular to the wall. Rearranging $18-2x>0$ gives $2x<18$. Dividing by $2$ yields $x<9$. At $x=9$ the parallel side would have length zero. At $x=0$ the other sides would have zero length. Both endpoints are therefore excluded.
