What are the ordinates of the points on $x=(y+2)^2$ with x-coordinate $9$?

- $-3$ and $3$
- $-7$ and $5$
- $-5$ and $1$
- $1$ and $5$

## Explanation

We need $(y+2)^2=9$, so $y+2$ is $3$ or $-3$. Subtracting $2$ yields $1$ and $-5$.

Both ordinates are $3$ units from the horizontal axis $y=-2$. Check them with $(-5+2)^2=9$ and $(1+2)^2=9$. The two points are $(9,-5)$ and $(9,1)$. The vertical line with x-coordinate $9$ therefore intersects both branches of the parabola.
