What is the maximum value of $y=-2(x-1)^2+6$ for $3\le x\le4$?

- $6$
- $-12$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

On the interval $x-1$ is positive and smallest at $x=3$. The square is multiplied by $-2$, so the largest value of $y$ is $y(3)=-2\cdot4+6=-2$.

The value $6$ would occur at $x=1$, which is outside the required interval. At its endpoints, $y(3)=-2$ and $y(4)=6-18=-12$. Between them the square increases with $x$, so the left endpoint gives the maximum on the interval.
