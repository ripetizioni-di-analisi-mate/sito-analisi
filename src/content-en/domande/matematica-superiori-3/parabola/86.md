The width of an arch $y=H-x^2$ at height $y=5$ is $4$. What is $H$?

- $7$
- $21$
- $9$
- $1$

## Explanation

By symmetry the endpoints at height $5$ have x-coordinates $-2$ and $2$. Substituting $x=2$ and $y=5$ gives $5=H-4$, so $H=9$.

The given height determines a horizontal chord. The axis goes through the chord's midpoint, so each endpoint is half the width from the axis, namely $2$. With $H=9$, substitution of $x=-2$ again gives $9-4=5$, confirming both endpoints.
