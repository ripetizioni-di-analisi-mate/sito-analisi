Which line passes through $(1,1)$ and also crosses $y=x^2$ at a second distinct point?

- $y=3x-2$
- $y=2x-1$
- $x=1$
- $y=3x-1$

## Explanation

The line $y=3x-2$ passes through $(1,1)$ and gives $x^2-3x+2=(x-1)(x-2)=0$. The points have different x-coordinates, $1$ and $2$.

At $x=2$ the line has ordinate $3\cdot2-2=4$ and the parabola has ordinate $2^2=4$. The tangent $y=2x-1$ also passes through $(1,1)$, but gives only the double root $x=1$. The task requires two different roots of the intersection polynomial.
