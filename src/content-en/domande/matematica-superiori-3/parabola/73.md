The point $(6,3)$ lies on $x=a(y-1)^2-2$. What is $a$?

- $4$
- $-2$
- $2$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Substituting $x=6$ and $y=3$ gives $6=4a-2$. Then $4a=8$, so $a=2$.

The given point's ordinate is $2$ units from the horizontal axis $y=1$, so the square is $4$. Its x-coordinate increases by $6-(-2)=8$ from the vertex. This yields the coefficient $\dfrac84=2$. The reflected ordinate $-1$ gives the same displacement and identifies the second point on the vertical line $x=6$.
