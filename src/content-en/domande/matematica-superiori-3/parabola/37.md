Given $F(0,2)$ and $d:y=-2$, for which values of $x$ does a point with ordinate $2$ lie on the locus?

- $x=\pm4$
- $x=\pm2$
- $x=0$
- $x=\pm8$

## Explanation

The focus has ordinate $2$, so the distance to it is $|x|$. The distance to the directrix is $|2-(-2)|=4$. Thus $|x|=4$.
