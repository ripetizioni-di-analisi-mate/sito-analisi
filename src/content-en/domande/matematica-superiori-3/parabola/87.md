A parabola $y=a(x-1)^2+4$ passes through $(3,0)$. Where else does it meet the x-axis?

- $(-3,0)$
- $(1,0)$
- $(-1,0)$
- $(5,0)$

## Explanation

The root $3$ is two units to the right of the axis $x=1$. The other root is two units to the left, at $1-2=-1$.

The given point also determines the coefficient. From $0=4a+4$ we get $a=-1$, so the curve is $y=4-(x-1)^2$. At $x=-1$, the square is $4$ and the ordinate is zero. The vertex is halfway between the two x-intercepts.
