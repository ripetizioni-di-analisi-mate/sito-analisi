The parabola $y=a(x-1)^2+4$ passes through $(3,0)$. At which other point does it meet the $x$-axis?

- $(-3,0)$
- $(1,0)$
- $(-1,0)$
- $(5,0)$

## Explanation

The parabola has axis of symmetry $x=1$, so its two points on the $x$-axis are symmetric with respect to this line. The point $(3,0)$ lies at distance $2$ from the axis, on its right, therefore the other point has $x$-coordinate $1-2=-1$.

The same result follows by computing $a$, since $0=4a+4$ gives $a=-1$ and the parabola is $y=4-(x-1)^2$. At $x=-1$ we have $(x-1)^2=4$, so $y=0$ and the second point is $(-1,0)$.
