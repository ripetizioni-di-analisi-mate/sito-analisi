A parabola has directrix $y=1$ and vertex $(2,4)$. How far is its focus from the directrix?

- $3$
- $5$
- $8$
- $6$

## Explanation

The vertex is $4-1=3$ units from the directrix. The focus is equally far from the vertex on the same axis, so the total distance is $3+3=6$.

The perpendicular to the directrix passes through $(2,4)$ and meets it at $(2,1)$. Since the vertex is the midpoint between this foot and the focus, the focus has height $4+3=7$. The requested distance can thus be found directly as $7-1=6$.
