Which polynomial must result from subtracting the tangent at x-coordinate $3$ from $y=-2x^2+x+4$?

- $(x-3)^2$
- $-2(x-3)^2$
- $-2(x+3)^2$
- $-2(x-3)$

## Explanation

Subtracting a line preserves the quadratic coefficient, $-2$. To have a double root at $3$, the polynomial must therefore be $-2(x-3)^2$.
