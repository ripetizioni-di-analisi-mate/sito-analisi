For which slopes $m$ does $y=mx$ meet $y=x^2-1$ at two distinct points?

- Only $m=0$.
- Only $m>0$.
- Every $m\in\mathbb R$.
- Only $|m|>2$.

## Explanation

The intersection equation is $x^2-mx-1=0$. The discriminant $m^2+4$ is strictly positive for every real $m$.

The term $m^2$ is nonnegative, so the discriminant is at least $4$ and cannot vanish. The product of the roots also shows why they are distinct, since it is $-1$. The real roots have opposite signs and give two points on either side of the vertical axis $x=0$.
