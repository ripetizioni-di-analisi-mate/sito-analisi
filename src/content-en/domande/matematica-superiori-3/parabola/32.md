Which parabola has the focus farthest from its vertex?

- $y=\dfrac14 x^2$
- $y=2x^2$
- $y=-x^2$
- $y=4x^2$

## Explanation

The distance is $\dfrac{1}{4|a|}$. The smallest absolute coefficient is $\dfrac14$, which gives the largest distance, $1$.

To connect the coefficients, $x^2=4py$ means that in $y=ax^2$ we have $4p=\dfrac1a$. A small coefficient magnitude therefore increases the focal distance. With $a=-1$ the parabola opens downward, but its focus is only $\dfrac14$ of a unit from its vertex.
