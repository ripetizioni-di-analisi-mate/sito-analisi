What must hold if $y=ax^2+bx+c$ touches the x-axis at $(0,0)$?

- $a=b=0$
- $a=c=0$
- $c=0$ and $b\ne0$
- $b=c=0$

## Explanation

Passing through the origin gives $c=0$. The intersection must be double at $x=0$, so the polynomial is $ax^2$ and $b=0$ as well.

Indeed, with $c=0$ the root equation is $x(ax+b)=0$. If $b$ were nonzero, there would be a second root $-\dfrac ba$ distinct from zero. Both roots coincide at zero only if $b=0$, and $a$ stays nonzero because the graph is a parabola.
