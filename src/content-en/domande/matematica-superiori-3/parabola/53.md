How many tangents to $y=x^2$ pass through $(0,4)$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The tangent at x-coordinate $t$ has intercept $-t^2$. Passing through $(0,4)$ would require $-t^2=4$, which has no real solutions.

Every tangent has intercept at most zero because $t^2$ is nonnegative. The horizontal vertex tangent meets the y-axis at $(0,0)$, the highest point reached there by a tangent. The given point has ordinate $4$, which is above that height.
