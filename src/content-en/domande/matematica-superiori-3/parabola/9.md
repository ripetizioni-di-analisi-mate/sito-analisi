The graph of $y=3x^2+bx+1$ has axis $x=-2$. What is the value of $b$?

- $b=-12$
- $b=6$
- $b=-6$
- $b=12$

## Explanation

The axis has x-coordinate $-\dfrac{b}{2\cdot3}=-\dfrac b6$. Set it equal to $-2$ and multiply by $-6$ to obtain $b=12$.

With this value the quadratic is $3x^2+12x+1=3(x+2)^2-11$. The square is zero at $x=-2$ and is the same at points equally far from $-2$. Taking $b=-12$ would instead produce the square $(x-2)^2$ and move the axis to $x=2$.
