What follows if three distinct points used to reconstruct $y=ax^2+bx+c$ lie on a nonvertical straight line?

- The system gives $b=0$.
- Any $a$ is possible.
- The system gives $a=1$.
- The system gives $a=0$.

## Explanation

The line and a polynomial of degree at most two through the points would have a difference with three distinct roots. That difference is zero, so the polynomial is the line and $a=0$.
