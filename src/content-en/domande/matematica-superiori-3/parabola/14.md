Find the tangent to $y=x^2+2x$ parallel to $y=6x+7$.

- $y=6x+4$
- $y=6x-4$
- $y=4x-6$
- $y=6x-2$

## Explanation

The slope at x-coordinate $t$ is $2t+2$. Setting $2t+2=6$ gives $t=2$. The point of tangency is $(2,8)$, so the intercept is $8-6\cdot2=-4$.

Parallelism concerns the slope. To fix the intercept, the point where the line touches is also needed. Check by subtracting the line from the parabola. The difference is $x^2+2x-(6x-4)=(x-2)^2$, which has its double root at the required point.
