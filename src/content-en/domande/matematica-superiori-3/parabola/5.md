Find the points common to $y=x^2-2$ and $y=x$.

- $(-2,-2)$, $(1,1)$
- $(-1,1)$, $(2,4)$
- $(-1,-1)$, $(2,2)$
- $(0,-2)$, $(2,2)$

## Explanation

Equating ordinates gives $x^2-x-2=(x-2)(x+1)=0$. The x-coordinates are $-1$ and $2$. Because $y=x$ on the line, the ordinates equal the x-coordinates.
