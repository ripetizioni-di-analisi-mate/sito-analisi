In the model $h(t)=4-(t-2)^2$, how long does the height exceed $3$?

- $2$ units of time.
- $1$ unit of time.
- $3$ units of time.
- $4$ units of time.

## Explanation

The threshold requires $(t-2)^2<1$, so $1<t<3$. The duration is $3-1=2$.

Writing the inequality as $|t-2|<1$ shows that the eligible times are less than one unit away from $2$. At times $1$ and $3$ the height equals $3$ exactly, so they are excluded. The duration of the open interval is still the difference between its endpoints.
