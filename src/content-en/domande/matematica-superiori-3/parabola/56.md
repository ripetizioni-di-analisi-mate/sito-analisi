The tangent to $y=x^2$ is perpendicular to $y=\dfrac12x+3$. What is the x-coordinate of tangency?

- $1$
- $-1$
- $-\dfrac14$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The slope perpendicular to $\dfrac12$ is $-2$. On the parabola the tangent at x-coordinate $t$ has slope $2t$. Thus $2t=-2$ gives $t=-1$.

The slopes of perpendicular lines with finite nonzero slopes have product $-1$. Here $\dfrac12\cdot(-2)=-1$. At $t=-1$ the point on the parabola is $(-1,1)$ and the tangent is $y=-2x-1$, which passes through it with the required slope.
