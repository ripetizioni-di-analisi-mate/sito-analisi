Which system makes $y=ax^2+bx+c$ pass through $(0,2)$, $(1,5)$ and $(-1,0)$?

- $\begin{cases}c=2\\a+b+c=5\\a-b+c=0\end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases}c=2\\a+b+c=5\\-a-b+c=0\end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases}b=2\\a+b+c=5\\a-b+c=0\end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases}c=2\\a+b=4\\a-b=0\end{cases}$

## Explanation

At $x=0$ only $c$ remains. At $x=1$ the terms are $a$, $b$ and $c$. At $x=-1$ the square is still $1$, while the linear term is $-b$.
