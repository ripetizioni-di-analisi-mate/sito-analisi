Suppose $f(1)=f(5)$ and $f$ is quadratic. What is $f(0)$ if $f(6)=8$?

- $-8$
- $6$
- The information is insufficient.
- $8$

## Explanation

The x-coordinates $1$ and $5$ have equal ordinates, so their midpoint $3$ is the x-coordinate of the axis. The values $0$ and $6$ are also equally far from $3$, so $f(0)=f(6)=8$.
