The line $y=-2x+3$ cuts a chord on $y=x^2$. What is the positive difference between the endpoints' x-coordinates?

- $3$
- $2$
- $4$
- $4\sqrt5$

## Explanation

The equation is $x^2+2x-3=(x+3)(x-1)=0$. The x-coordinates are $-3$ and $1$, so the requested difference is $1-(-3)=4$. The sloping segment itself is longer.

The difference of the x-coordinates is the horizontal projection of the chord. Its endpoints have ordinates $9$ and $1$ because the line has slope $-2$. To find the actual distance, use both coordinate differences to get $\sqrt{4^2+8^2}=4\sqrt5$.
