How many values of $y$ satisfy $x=(y-1)^2+2$ when $x=2$?

- $0$
- $2$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

Substitution of $x=2$ gives $(y-1)^2=0$. The square vanishes only at $y=1$, the ordinate of the vertex.

The vertical line $x=2$ passes through the vertex of a parabola opening to the right. A greater value would make the right-hand side of $(y-1)^2=x-2$ positive and give two ordinates. At the exact value $2$, those ordinates coincide and determine a single point.
