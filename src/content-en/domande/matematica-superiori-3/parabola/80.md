In the cross-section shown, $A(1,10)$ and $B(5,10)$ are at the same height. With axes measured in metres, how wide is the arch at that height?

::grafico{f="-2*(x-3)^2+18" xmin=-0.6 xmax=6.7 ymin=-1 ymax=21 punti="0,0;6,0;3,18,pieno,V;1,10,pieno,A;5,10,pieno,B" legenda=0 titolo="The arch cross-section with A and B at the same height"}

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The width at the specified height is the horizontal distance from $A$ to $B$. Subtract the x-coordinates to obtain $5-1=4\,\mathrm{m}$.
