A rectangular enclosure against a wall has $16\,\mathrm{m}$ of fencing for the remaining three sides. What is its maximum area? Give the area in square metres.

- $A_{\max}=16$
- $A_{\max}=32$
- $A_{\max}=64$
- $A_{\max}=24$

## Explanation

If $x$ is a side perpendicular to the wall, the third side is $16-2x$. The area is $x(16-2x)=32-2(x-4)^2$ and reaches $32\,\mathrm{m}^2$ when $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$.
