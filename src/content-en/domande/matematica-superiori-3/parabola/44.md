The line $y=4x-3$ cuts a chord on $y=x^2$. What is its midpoint?

- $(2,4)$
- $(1,1)$
- $(3,9)$
- $(2,5)$

## Explanation

The equation $x^2-4x+3=0$ has roots $1$ and $3$. The points are $(1,1)$ and $(3,9)$, so their coordinate averages are $2$ and $5$.

The midpoint lies on the chord. Substituting $x=2$ into its line gives $4\cdot2-3=5$. On the parabola, the same x-coordinate gives $2^2=4$. Using the parabola for the midpoint's ordinate would move the point off the chord.
