Solve $\log_2(x-2)+\log_2(x+2)=\log_2 5$.

- $\{-3\}$
- $\{\sqrt5\}$
- $\{3\}$
- $\{-3,3\}$

## Explanation

Before combining the logarithms, require $x-2>0$ and $x+2>0$, giving $x>2$. On that domain their sum is $\log_2(x^2-4)$. Equality with $\log_2 5$ gives $x^2-4=5$, hence $x=\pm3$. At $x=-3$ both original arguments are negative. At $x=3$ they are $1$ and $5$, whose product is $5$. The final solution set has just one element even though the quadratic has two roots.
