Let $f(x)=\log_{10}x$. Which of the two changes $|f(3)-f(2)|$ and $|f(1)-f(2)|$ is larger?

- $|f(3)-f(2)|$
- $|f(1)-f(2)|$
- The two changes are equal.
- The comparison requires the numerical value of $\log_{10}2$.

## Explanation

By the quotient property, $|f(3)-f(2)|=\log_{10}\dfrac32$ and $|f(1)-f(2)|=\log_{10}2$. The base-ten logarithm is increasing and $2>\dfrac32$, so $\log_{10}2>\log_{10}\dfrac32$. Therefore the larger change is $|f(1)-f(2)|$, even though both arguments are at distance $1$ from $2$.
