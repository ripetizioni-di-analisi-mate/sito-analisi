A sensor reports $J=\log_{10}R$ for each positive reading $R$. Two equally sized deviations around $R=2$ are tested by changing the reading to $1$ and then to $3$. Which test gives the larger absolute change in $J$ relative to the reading at $R=2$?

- The change from $2$ to $3$.
- The change from $2$ to $1$.
- The two changes have equal absolute values.
- The comparison requires the exact numerical value of $\log_{10}2$.

## Explanation

Moving down to $1$ gives an absolute change of $\log_{10}(2/1)$. Moving up to $3$ gives $\log_{10}(3/2)$. The base-ten logarithm is increasing, so the first change is larger because $2>\dfrac32$.
