Which evaluation of $\log_2(18+14)$ is correct?

- $2\log_2 16=8$
- $\log_2 18+\log_2 14$
- $\log_2 18-\log_2 14$
- $\log_2 32=5$

## Explanation

The argument contains a sum, so first calculate $18+14=32$. Since $32=2^5$, the logarithm is $5$. Applying the product law to the two addends would instead give $\log_2 18+\log_2 14=\log_2(18\cdot14)=\log_2 252$. Because the base-$2$ logarithm is one-to-one and $252\ne32$, the two values are distinct. Doubling $\log_2 16$ would give $8$, because it would mean taking the logarithm of $16^2$.
