Which evaluation of $\log_2(18+14)$ is correct?

- $2\log_2 16=8$
- $\log_2 18+\log_2 14$
- $\log_2 18-\log_2 14$
- $\log_2 32=5$

## Explanation

First add $18$ and $14$ to get $32=2^5$. The law that splits a logarithm into two addends applies to a product, whereas the argument here is a sum.
