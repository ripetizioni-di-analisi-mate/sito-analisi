Which expression is equal to $\log_5\dfrac1{125}$?

- $\dfrac13$
- $-3$
- $3$
- $-\dfrac13$

## Explanation

The denominator is $125=5^3$. Taking the reciprocal reverses the sign of the exponent, so $\dfrac1{125}=5^{-3}$. The logarithm is $-3$. Comparison with $1$ also shows its sign. Base $5$ gives an increasing logarithm and the argument is between $0$ and $1$, so its logarithm is negative.
