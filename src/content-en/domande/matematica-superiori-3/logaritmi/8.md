A population follows the model $N(t)=200\cdot3^{\frac t2}$, where $t$ is time in hours. After how many hours does it reach $1800$?

- $t=9$
- $t=4$
- $t=2$
- $t=6$

## Explanation

The initial quantity is $200$, so reaching $1800$ means multiplying it by $\dfrac{1800}{200}=9$. The model gives $3^{\frac t2}=9=3^2$, so $\dfrac t2=2$. The exponent counts two-hour periods, giving $t=4$ hours. The quantity is $600$ after the first period and $1800$ after the second. Counting four periods instead of four hours would multiply by $3^4$ and exceed the target.
