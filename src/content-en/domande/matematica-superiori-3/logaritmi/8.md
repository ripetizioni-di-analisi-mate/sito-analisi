A population follows the model $N(t)=200\cdot3^{\frac t2}$, where $t$ is time in hours. After how many hours does it reach $1800$?

- $t=9$
- $t=4$
- $t=2$
- $t=6$

## Explanation

Dividing by $200$ gives $3^{\frac t2}=9=3^2$. The exponential is one-to-one, so $\dfrac t2=2$ and $t=4$.
