How does $\log_3 x$ change when its argument goes from $x$ to $3x$, for every $x>0$?

- It increases by $3$.
- It triples.
- It increases by $1$.
- It decreases by $1$.

## Explanation

For $x>0$, $\log_3(3x)=\log_3 3+\log_3 x=1+\log_3 x$. Multiplying each argument by $3$ raises the graph by one unit.
