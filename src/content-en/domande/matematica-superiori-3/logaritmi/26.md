For which $k$ does $\log_5 15-\log_5 k=1$ hold, with $k>0$?

- $3$
- $10$
- $5$
- $75$

## Explanation

For $k>0$, the quotient law gives $\log_5\dfrac{15}{k}=1$. By definition the argument must equal $5^1=5$. Multiplying by $k$ gives $15=5k$, so $k=3$. Check in the original difference, where $\log_5 15-\log_5 3=\log_5 5=1$. For the base-$5$ logarithm to be positive, the quotient must exceed $1$, so the solution must also satisfy $k<15$.
