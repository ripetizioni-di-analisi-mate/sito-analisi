What is the domain of $\log_2(\log_3 x)$?

- $(0,1)$
- $(0,+\infty)$
- $[1,+\infty)$
- $(1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The inner logarithm exists for $x>0$. The outer logarithm takes the value of $\log_3 x$ as its argument, which must be strictly positive. Since the base-$3$ logarithm increases and is zero at $1$, the additional restriction is $x>1$. The endpoint $1$ is excluded because it would give the outer logarithm argument zero.
