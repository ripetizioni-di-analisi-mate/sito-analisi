Solve $\log_2(x^2-1)\leq3$.

- $(-3,-1)\cup(1,3)$
- $[-3,-1)\cup(1,3]$
- $[-3,3]$
- $(-\infty,-3]\cup[3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Base $2$ is greater than $1$, so the logarithm is increasing and the argument can be at most $2^3=8$. The argument must also be strictly positive. Thus $0<x^2-1\leq8$, or $1<|x|\leq3$. Include $-3$ and $3$, which give equality, and exclude $-1$ and $1$, which give a zero argument. The input $x=0$ is excluded even though it satisfies $x^2\leq9$, since it makes the argument negative.
