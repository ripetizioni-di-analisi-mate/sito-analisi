In $\log_2(x^2-1)\leq3$, comparison with $2^3$ gives $x^2\leq9$. Which part of $[-3,3]$ remains after imposing the domain as well?

- $[-3,3]$
- $[-3,-1)\cup(1,3]$
- $(-1,1)$
- $[-3,-1]\cup[1,3]$

## Explanation

For the logarithm to exist, its argument must satisfy $x^2-1>0$, or $|x|>1$. This removes the whole interval $[-1,1]$. The inputs $-3$ and $3$ remain because the logarithm equals exactly $3$ at both.
