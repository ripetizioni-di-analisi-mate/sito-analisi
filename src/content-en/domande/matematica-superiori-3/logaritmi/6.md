Solve $\log_2(x^2-1)\leq3$.

- $(-3,-1)\cup(1,3)$
- $[-3,-1)\cup(1,3]$
- $[-3,3]$
- $(-\infty,-3]\cup[3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Base $2$ gives an increasing logarithm. On its domain the inequality requires $0<x^2-1\leq2^3$, meaning $1<x^2\leq9$. This is equivalent to $1<|x|\leq3$.
