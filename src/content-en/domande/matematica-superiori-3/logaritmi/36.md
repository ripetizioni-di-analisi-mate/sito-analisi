What is the domain of $\ln(9-x^2)$?

- $(0,3)$
- $[-3,3]$
- $(-3,3)$
- $(-\infty,-3)\cup(3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The argument $9-x^2$ must be strictly positive. The inequality is equivalent to $x^2<9$, or $|x|<3$. The domain is therefore $(-3,3)$. The value $x=0$ is allowed and gives argument $9$. At both endpoints the argument is zero, so the interval is open. Outside the interval $x^2>9$, so the argument becomes negative on both sides.
