On a scale $L=10\log_{10}(I)$ with $I>0$, the intensity increases by a factor of $10$. How much does $L$ increase?

- $10$
- $100$
- $1$
- $20$

## Explanation

The new level is $10\log_{10}(10I)$. Subtract the original level $10\log_{10}I$ and use the quotient law, since $I>0$. The difference is $10\log_{10}\dfrac{10I}{I}=10\log_{10}10=10$. The multiplier on the intensity thus becomes a level increase that has the same value for every initial $I$. For example, with $I=1$ the level changes from $0$ to $10$.
