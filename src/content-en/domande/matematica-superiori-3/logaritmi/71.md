On a scale $L=10\log_{10}(I)$ with $I>0$, the intensity increases by a factor of $10$. How much does $L$ increase?

- $10$
- $100$
- $1$
- $20$

## Explanation

The difference in levels is $10\log_{10}\dfrac{10I}{I}=10\log_{10}10=10$. The ratio is $10$ for every initial intensity.
