What is the domain of $f(x)=\dfrac1{\ln(x-1)}$?

- $(2,+\infty)$
- $(1,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{2\}$
- $(1,2)\cup(2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The logarithm is defined when $x-1>0$, so $x>1$. As it occurs in the denominator, we also need $\ln(x-1)\ne0$. The only argument with zero logarithm is $1$, so exclude $x=2$. The domain is $(1,2)\cup(2,+\infty)$. At $x=1$ the logarithm is undefined. At $x=2$ it is defined and makes the denominator zero.
