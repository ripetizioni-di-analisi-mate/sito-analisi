What is the domain of $f(x)=\dfrac1{\ln(x-1)}$?

- $(2,+\infty)$
- $(1,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{2\}$
- $(1,2)\cup(2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

We need $x-1>0$ for $\ln(x-1)$ to be defined. Also, the denominator is zero when $x-1=1$, meaning $x=2$. Remove $2$ from the interval $x>1$.
