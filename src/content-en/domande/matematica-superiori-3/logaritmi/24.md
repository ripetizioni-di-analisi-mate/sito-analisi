For $x\ne0$, which identity holds for $\ln(x^2)$?

- $\ln(x^2)=(\ln|x|)^2$
- $\ln(x^2)=2\ln x$
- $\ln(x^2)=2\ln|x|$
- $\ln(x^2)=\ln(2|x|)$

## Explanation

Because $x^2=|x|^2$ and $|x|>0$, the power law applies to $|x|$. The expression $2\ln x$ is defined only on the positive part of the domain.
