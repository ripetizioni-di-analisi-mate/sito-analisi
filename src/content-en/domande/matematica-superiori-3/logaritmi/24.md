For $x\ne0$, which identity holds for $\ln(x^2)$?

- $\ln(x^2)=(\ln|x|)^2$
- $\ln(x^2)=2\ln x$
- $\ln(x^2)=2\ln|x|$
- $\ln(x^2)=\ln(2|x|)$

## Explanation

For every $x\ne0$, the number $|x|$ is strictly positive and $x^2=|x|^2$. Applying the power law to $|x|$ gives $\ln(x^2)=2\ln|x|$. For example, at $x=-2$, this yields $\ln4=2\ln2$. The expression $2\ln x$ is defined only for $x>0$ and would lose every negative input. The original argument is a square, so its symmetry about zero must survive the transformation.
