A principal grows according to $C(t)=500(1{,}05)^t$, with $t$ in years. What is the exact real-valued time at which it reaches $600$?

- $\dfrac{1{,}2}{1{,}05}$
- $\dfrac{\ln(1{,}2)}{\ln(1{,}05)}$
- $\ln(1{,}2)-\ln(1{,}05)$
- $\dfrac{\ln(1{,}05)}{\ln(1{,}2)}$

## Explanation

Dividing by the initial capital gives $(1{,}05)^t=\dfrac{600}{500}=1{,}2$. Taking natural logarithms gives $t\ln(1{,}05)=\ln(1{,}2)$. Since $\ln(1{,}05)>0$, division gives $t=\dfrac{\ln(1{,}2)}{\ln(1{,}05)}$. This time is measured in years, and the quotient keeps its exact value without rounding to a whole year. The logarithm of $1{,}05$ is in the denominator because it is the base of the power with the unknown exponent.
