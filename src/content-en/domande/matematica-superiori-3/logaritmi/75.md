A principal grows according to $C(t)=500(1{,}05)^t$, with $t$ in years. What is the exact real-valued time at which it reaches $600$?

- $\dfrac{1{,}2}{1{,}05}$
- $\dfrac{\ln(1{,}2)}{\ln(1{,}05)}$
- $\ln(1{,}2)-\ln(1{,}05)$
- $\dfrac{\ln(1{,}05)}{\ln(1{,}2)}$

## Explanation

The required factor is $\dfrac{600}{500}=1{,}2$. Taking natural logarithms in $(1{,}05)^t=1{,}2$ gives $t\ln(1{,}05)=\ln(1{,}2)$.
