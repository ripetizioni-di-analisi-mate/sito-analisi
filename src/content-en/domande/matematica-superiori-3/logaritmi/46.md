Which formula represents the graph of $y=\log_2x$ shifted $3$ units right and $1$ unit up?

- $y=\log_2(x+1)-3$
- $y=\log_2(x-3)-1$
- $y=\log_2(x-3)+1$
- $y=\log_2(x+3)+1$

## Explanation

A point at horizontal coordinate $u$ on the original graph moves to $x=u+3$, so $u=x-3$. The new function evaluates the logarithm at $x-3$ and adds $1$ to its height. This gives $y=\log_2(x-3)+1$. For example, $(1,0)$ moves to $(4,1)$, and the new expression gives exactly $1$ at $x=4$. The asymptote also moves from $x=0$ to $x=3$, at the boundary of the new domain.
