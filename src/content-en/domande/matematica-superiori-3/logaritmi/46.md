Which formula represents the graph of $y=\log_2x$ shifted $3$ units right and $1$ unit up?

- $y=\log_2(x+1)-3$
- $y=\log_2(x-3)-1$
- $y=\log_2(x-3)+1$
- $y=\log_2(x+3)+1$

## Explanation

A shift to the right replaces $x$ with $x-3$ in the argument. The upward shift adds $1$ to the value of the logarithm.
