In the quotient $\dfrac{2^{\log_2 u+\log_2 v}}{u}$, fix $v>0$ and allow $u>0$ to vary. Which quantity does the quotient equal throughout?

- $\dfrac1v$
- $u$
- $v$
- $u+v$

## Explanation

In the numerator, the sum of logarithms combines to $\log_2(uv)$. Exponentiation with base $2$ recovers $uv$. Dividing by the positive factor $u$ leaves the fixed value $v$.
