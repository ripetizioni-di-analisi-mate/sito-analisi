Evaluate $2^{\log_2 5+\log_2 3}$.

- $8$
- $5$
- $15$
- $30$

## Explanation

Since $5$ and $3$ are positive and the logarithms have a common base, the sum is $\log_2(5\cdot3)=\log_2 15$. The power $2^{\log_2 15}$ therefore gives $15$ by the logarithm's definition. The same result follows by splitting the power of the sum into two factors, $2^{\log_2 5}=5$ and $2^{\log_2 3}=3$.
