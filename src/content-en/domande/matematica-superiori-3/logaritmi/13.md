Solve $(\log_3 x)^2+\log_3 x-2=0$.

- $\{3\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac13,9\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac19,3\right\}$
- $\{-2,1\}$

## Explanation

For $x>0$ set $u=\log_3 x$, which can take every real value. The equation becomes $u^2+u-2=(u+2)(u-1)=0$. From $u=-2$ we get $x=3^{-2}=\dfrac19$, and from $u=1$ we get $x=3$. The negative root is a value of $u$. In terms of $x$ it corresponds to a positive argument less than $1$. Substituting the two values of $x$ indeed gives back $u=-2$ and $u=1$.
