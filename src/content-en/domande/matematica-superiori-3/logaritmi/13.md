Solve $(\log_3 x)^2+\log_3 x-2=0$.

- $\{3\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac13,9\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac19,3\right\}$
- $\{-2,1\}$

## Explanation

With $u=\log_3 x$, we obtain $(u+2)(u-1)=0$. Its roots are $-2$ and $1$, so $x=3^{-2}=\dfrac19$ or $x=3^1=3$. Both belong to the domain $x>0$.
