Solve $\log_2(\log_3 x)=1$.

- $\{9\}$
- $\{6\}$
- $\{3\}$
- $\{2\}$

## Explanation

The domain of the composition requires $\log_3 x>0$, so $x>1$. The outer equation gives $\log_3 x=2^1=2$. Applying the definition to the inner logarithm gives $x=3^2=9$. The candidate lies in the domain, and $\log_2(\log_3 9)=\log_2 2=1$ checks it. The two bases are used in two distinct steps. The last check has $2$ as the outer argument, so its positivity is also confirmed.
