What is the largest $x$ for which $\log_{\frac13}(x+2)>0$ if $x$ must be an integer multiple of $\dfrac14$?

- $-1$
- $-2$
- $-\dfrac54$
- $-\dfrac74$

## Explanation

The base is less than $1$, so a positive logarithm is equivalent to $0<x+2<1$. The multiples of $\dfrac14$ in $(-2,-1)$ are $-\dfrac74$, $-\dfrac32$ and $-\dfrac54$. The largest is $-\dfrac54$.
