A quantity follows $Q(t)=80\left(\dfrac12\right)^{\frac t3}$ with $t$ in hours. After how many hours does it fall to $10$?

- $9$
- $6$
- $8$
- $3$

## Explanation

Dividing the threshold by the initial value gives $\dfrac{10}{80}=\dfrac18$. Since $\dfrac18=\left(\dfrac12\right)^3$, equality requires $\dfrac t3=3$. The exponent counts halving periods, each of which lasts $3$ hours. The time is therefore $9$ hours. The three steps go from $80$ to $40$, then $20$ and finally $10$. After only three hours, a single halving has occurred and the quantity is still $40$.
