A quantity follows $Q(t)=80\left(\dfrac12\right)^{\frac t3}$ with $t$ in hours. After how many hours does it fall to $10$?

- $9$
- $6$
- $8$
- $3$

## Explanation

The quantity must fall to $\dfrac18$ of its initial value. Since $\dfrac18=\left(\dfrac12\right)^3$, three half-life periods of $3$ hours each are needed.
