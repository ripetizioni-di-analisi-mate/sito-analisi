To find the halving time we reach $q^t=\dfrac12$, with $0<q<1$. Which formula gives the exact value of $t$ for every allowed $q$?

- $\dfrac{\ln(1/2)}{\ln q}$
- $\ln\left(\dfrac{1}{2q}\right)$
- $\dfrac{\ln2}{\ln q}$
- $\dfrac{\ln q}{\ln(1/2)}$

## Explanation

Taking natural logarithms gives $t\ln q=\ln(1/2)$. Divide by $\ln q$, which is negative and nonzero. The numerator is negative as well, so the quotient that solves the equation is positive.
