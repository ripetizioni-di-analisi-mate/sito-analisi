Which ratio represents $\log_7 2$ using natural logarithms?

- $\dfrac{\ln 2}{\ln 7}$
- $\ln 7-\ln 2$
- $\dfrac{\ln 7}{\ln 2}$
- $\ln\dfrac27$

## Explanation

Let $u$ be the exponent satisfying $7^u=2$. Taking natural logarithms gives $\ln(7^u)=u\ln7=\ln2$. Since $\ln7>0$, we can divide and find $u=\dfrac{\ln2}{\ln7}$. The value is less than $1$ because $2<7$. This distinguishes the quotient from its reciprocal. The ratio is also above zero because $\ln2$ and $\ln7$ are both positive.
