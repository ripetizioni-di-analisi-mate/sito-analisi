A table rounds $\ln a=0.004$ to $0.00$ and $\ln b=1$ to $1.00$. Only these rounded values are entered in the formula for $\log_a b$. What happens?

- The calculation attempts division by zero although the exact value is finite.
- The result is exactly $0$.
- The true base $a$ equals $1$ and the logarithm does not exist.
- The result is exactly $1$.

## Explanation

Change of base requires $\dfrac{\ln b}{\ln a}$. With exact values the ratio is $1/0.004=250$. After rounding, the denominator becomes zero and the quotient of the displayed readings is undefined.
