The endpoint conditions for $\log_{x+1}(3-x)$ suggest the interval $(-1,3)$. Which single value must also be removed because of the base of the logarithm?

- $x=1$
- $x=2$
- $x=-\dfrac12$
- $x=0$

## Explanation

The base is positive for $x>-1$, and the argument is positive for $x<3$. The base must also differ from $1$. Solving $x+1=1$ identifies the excluded input $x=0$, leaving $(-1,0)\cup(0,3)$.
