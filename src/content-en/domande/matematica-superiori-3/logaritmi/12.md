Find the domain of $f(x)=\log_{x+1}(3-x)$.

- $(0,3)$
- $(-1,3)$
- $(-\infty,3)\setminus\{0\}$
- $(-1,0)\cup(0,3)$

## Explanation

The base $x+1$ imposes two conditions, $x+1>0$ and $x+1\ne1$. They give $x>-1$ and $x\ne0$. The argument $3-x$ must also be positive, so $x<3$. The domain is $(-1,0)\cup(0,3)$. At the interior point $x=0$ the argument is $3$, but the base is $1$ and the logarithm is undefined. At $x=-1$ the base is zero, while at $x=3$ the argument is zero.
