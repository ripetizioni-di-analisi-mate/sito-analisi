Find the domain of $f(x)=\log_{x+1}(3-x)$.

- $(0,3)$
- $(-1,3)$
- $(-\infty,3)\setminus\{0\}$
- $(-1,0)\cup(0,3)$

## Explanation

The base must be positive and different from $1$, so $x>-1$ and $x\ne0$. The argument is positive for $x<3$. Their intersection is $(-1,0)\cup(0,3)$.
