The point $(2,9)$ lies on the graph of $y=3^x$. Which point therefore lies on the graph of the inverse function?

- $(-2,9)$
- $(2,-9)$
- $(2,9)$
- $(9,2)$

## Explanation

The point $(2,9)$ means that $3^2=9$. The inverse therefore takes $9$ as input and returns the exponent $2$, in other words $\log_3 9=2$. The point on the inverse graph is $(9,2)$. Geometrically the horizontal and vertical coordinates are exchanged, as in reflection across the line $y=x$. The numerical check also respects the inverse's domain, because the new horizontal coordinate $9$ is positive.
