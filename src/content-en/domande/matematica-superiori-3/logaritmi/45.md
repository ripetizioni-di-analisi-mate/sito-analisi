The point $(2,9)$ lies on the graph of $y=3^x$. Which point therefore lies on the graph of the inverse function?

- $(-2,9)$
- $(2,-9)$
- $(2,9)$
- $(9,2)$

## Explanation

From $3^2=9$ we obtain $\log_3 9=2$. To move to the graph of the inverse, exchange the coordinates to get $(9,2)$.
