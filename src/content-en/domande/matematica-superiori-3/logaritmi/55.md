A student writes $\ln(x+y)=\ln x+\ln y$ for $x,y>0$. With $x=3$, for which single value of $y$ does this happen to give a true equality?

- $3$
- $1$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac32$

## Explanation

The right-hand side is $\ln(3y)$. Injectivity of the logarithm makes the equality equivalent to $3+y=3y$, giving $y=\dfrac32$. At that point the sum and product both equal $\dfrac92$.
