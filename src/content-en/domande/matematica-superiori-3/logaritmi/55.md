Solve $\log_2(x+1)-\log_2(x-1)=2$.

- $\{3\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac35\right\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\left\{\dfrac53\right\}$

## Explanation

The common domain is $x>1$. The difference is the logarithm of the quotient, so $\dfrac{x+1}{x-1}=2^2=4$. Multiplying by $x-1$ gives $x+1=4x-4$, or $3x=5$. At $x=\dfrac53$ the quotient is $\dfrac{\dfrac83}{\dfrac23}=4$, so the candidate lies in the domain and satisfies the equation. The denominator is positive on the domain, so multiplication is reversible and introduces no spurious roots.
