Which condition gives the domain of $\ln\dfrac{x+3}{x+3}$?

- $x\ne3$
- $x>-3$
- $x\ne-3$
- $x\in\mathbb R$

## Explanation

The denominator $x+3$ is zero at $x=-3$, so that value is excluded. At every other $x$ the fraction simplifies to $1$. Since $1>0$, the logarithm is defined and equals zero throughout the domain. Cancelling before imposing the restriction would hide the excluded point. The graph agrees with the horizontal axis and has a missing point at $(-3,0)$.
