Which condition gives the domain of $\ln\dfrac{x+3}{x+3}$?

- $x\ne3$
- $x>-3$
- $x\ne-3$
- $x\in\mathbb R$

## Explanation

The quotient is $1$ whenever $x+3\ne0$. At $x=-3$ the denominator is zero and the original expression is undefined, even though the simplified expression is $\ln1$.
