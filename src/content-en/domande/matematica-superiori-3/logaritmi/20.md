Evaluate $\log_4(\sqrt8)$.

- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac43$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Rewrite the argument and base as powers of $2$. We have $\sqrt8=(2^3)^{\frac12}=2^{\frac32}$ and $4=2^2$. Setting $u=\log_4(\sqrt8)$ gives $2^{2u}=2^{\frac32}$. Thus $2u=\dfrac32$ and $u=\dfrac34$. The result is between $0$ and $1$, as expected from $1<\sqrt8<4$. As a check, $4^{\frac34}=(2^2)^{\frac34}=2^{\frac32}$, precisely the value of the original root.
