Suppose $\log_2 3=p$. Which equation must the quantity $z=2^{p/2}$ satisfy without approximating $p$?

- $2z=3$
- $z^2=3$
- $z^2=6$
- $z^3=2$

## Explanation

By definition $2^p=3$. Squaring $z=2^{p/2}$ gives $z^2=2^p=3$. Since $z>0$, the represented value is the positive square root of $3$.
