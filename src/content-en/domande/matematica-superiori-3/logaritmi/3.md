Evaluate $\log_4(2^5)$.

- $\log_4(2^5)=5$
- $\log_4(2^5)=10$
- $\log_4(2^5)=\dfrac52$
- $\log_4(2^5)=\dfrac25$

## Explanation

Let $u$ be the required logarithm. Its definition gives $4^u=2^5$. Since $4=2^2$, the left-hand side is $2^{2u}$. Equating the exponents of the two powers of $2$ gives $2u=5$, so $u=\dfrac52$. Substitution checks this value since $4^{\frac52}=(\sqrt4)^5=2^5$. The value is between $2$ and $3$, as the argument $32$ lies between $4^2=16$ and $4^3=64$.
