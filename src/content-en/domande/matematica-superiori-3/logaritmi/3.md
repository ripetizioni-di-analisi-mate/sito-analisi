Setting $u=\log_4(2^5)$ translates the definition into $4^u=2^5$. Which value of $u$ makes the exponents equal after rewriting both sides in base $2$?

- $10$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac52$
- $3$

## Explanation

Since $4=2^2$, the left-hand side becomes $2^{2u}$. Powers of base $2$ are one-to-one, so $2u=5$. Dividing by $2$ gives $u=\dfrac52$.
