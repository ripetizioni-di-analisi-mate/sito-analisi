Evaluate $\log_2 192-\log_2 3$.

- $\log_2 192-\log_2 3=6$
- $\log_2 192-\log_2 3=-6$
- $\log_2 192-\log_2 3=7$
- $\log_2 192-\log_2 3=64$

## Explanation

Both arguments are positive and the logarithms have the same base. The subtracted term provides the denominator, so $\log_2 192-\log_2 3=\log_2\dfrac{192}{3}=\log_2 64=6$. The number $64$ is the argument obtained by division. The logarithm gives the exponent, as confirmed by $2^6=64$. Writing $192=3\cdot2^6$ makes it clear why the term $\log_2 3$ cancels after the transformation.
