To find $\log_2 192-\log_2 3$ without evaluating the two logarithms separately, which power of $2$ should you identify?

- $192/3=2^6$
- $192-3=2^6$
- $192/3=2^8$
- $192\cdot3=2^6$

## Explanation

The difference of logarithms with the same base is the logarithm of the quotient of their arguments. Dividing $192$ by $3$ gives $64=2^6$, so the result is $6$.
