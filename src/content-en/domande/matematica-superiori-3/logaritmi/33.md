It is known that $\log_a b$ is irrational, with $a,b>1$. Which equality of powers can be ruled out for every pair of positive integers $m,n$?

- $a^{mn}=(a^m)^n$
- $a^m=a^n$
- $b^m=b^n$
- $a^m=b^n$

## Explanation

If $a^m=b^n$, taking logarithms to base $a$ would give $m=n\log_a b$. This would imply $\log_a b=\dfrac mn$, a rational number, contradicting the assumption. Equal powers of the same base are possible when $m=n$, and the power-of-a-power rule always holds.
