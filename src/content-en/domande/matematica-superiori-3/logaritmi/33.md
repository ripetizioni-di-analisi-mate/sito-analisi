Evaluate $\dfrac{\log_2 7}{\log_4 7}$.

- $1$
- $4$
- $\dfrac12$
- $2$

## Explanation

Both logarithms have argument $7$, so in natural logs they are $\dfrac{\ln7}{\ln2}$ and $\dfrac{\ln7}{\ln4}$. Dividing by the second quotient means multiplying by its reciprocal. Cancel $\ln7$, which is nonzero, to find $\dfrac{\ln4}{\ln2}=2$. The base-$4$ logarithm is therefore half the other one. Indeed, $\ln4$ is twice $\ln2$, because $4=2^2$.
