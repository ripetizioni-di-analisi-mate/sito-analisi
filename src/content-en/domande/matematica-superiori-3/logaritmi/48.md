The figure shows $y=\log_2 x$ and $y=\log_{\frac12}x$. What is the $x$-coordinate of their intersection?

::grafico{f="log(x)/log(2);log(x)/log(0.5)" xmin=-0.2 xmax=4 ymin=-3 ymax=3 legenda="\log_2 x;\log_{\frac12}x" titolo="Two logarithms with reciprocal bases"}

- $\dfrac12$
- $1$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

Since $\ln(\frac12)=-\ln2$, the logarithms are opposites for every $x>0$. They agree only when both equal zero, at $x=1$.
