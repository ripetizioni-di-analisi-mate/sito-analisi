The figure shows $y=\log_2 x$ and $y=\log_{\frac12}x$. What is the $x$-coordinate of their intersection?

::grafico{f="log(x)/log(2);log(x)/log(0.5)" xmin=-0.2 xmax=4 ymin=-3 ymax=3 legenda="\log_2 x;\log_{\frac12}x" titolo="Two logarithms with reciprocal bases"}

- $\dfrac12$
- $1$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

The change-of-base formula gives $\log_{\frac12}x=\dfrac{\ln x}{-\ln2}=-\log_2 x$ for $x>0$. Equality requires a number to equal its opposite, so both logarithms must be zero. That happens only at $x=1$. This is the point $(1,0)$ where the curves meet on the horizontal axis. They have opposite signs to the left of the intersection, as well as to the right.
