Solve $\ln(x-2)=\ln(2-x)$.

- $\{2\}$
- $(-\infty,2)$
- $(2,+\infty)$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The first argument requires $x-2>0$, equivalent to $x>2$. The second requires $2-x>0$, meaning $x<2$. Both conditions must hold at once and their intersection is empty. Equating the arguments directly would give $x=2$, where both arguments are zero and neither logarithm is defined. At every other input, if one argument is positive the other is negative.
