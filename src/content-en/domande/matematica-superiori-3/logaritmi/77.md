A student divides $(\ln x)^2=\ln x$ by $\ln x$ and obtains only $x=e$. Which solution was lost?

- $x=1$
- $x=-1$
- $x=e^2$
- $x=0$

## Explanation

Division removes the case $\ln x=0$, which occurs at $x=1$. Factoring gives $\ln x(\ln x-1)=0$, yielding either $x=1$ or $x=e$. Both values belong to the domain $x>0$.
