For $x>0$, when does $\log_{\frac12}x\geq\log_2x$ hold?

- $0<x<1$
- $x\geq1$
- For every $x>0$.
- $0<x\leq1$

## Explanation

The bases are reciprocals, so $\log_{\frac12}x=-\log_2x$ for $x>0$. The inequality is equivalent to $-\log_2x\geq\log_2x$, or $\log_2x\leq0$. Because $\log_2x$ is increasing and is zero at $x=1$, the solution is $0<x\leq1$. Include $1$ because the two logarithms agree there. For example, at $x=\dfrac12$ the heights are $1$ and $-1$, in the required order.
