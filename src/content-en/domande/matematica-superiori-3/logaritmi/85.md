For $x>0$, when does $\log_{\frac12}x\geq\log_2x$ hold?

- $0<x<1$
- $x\geq1$
- For every $x>0$.
- $0<x\leq1$

## Explanation

The change-of-base formula gives $\log_{\frac12}x=-\log_2x$. The inequality is therefore equivalent to $\log_2x\leq0$. Base $2$ is greater than $1$, so $0<x\leq1$.
