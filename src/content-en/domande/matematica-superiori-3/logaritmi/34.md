If $u>1$ and $\log_2 u=p$, what is $\log_4(u^3)$?

- $\dfrac{3p}{2}$
- $3p$
- $6p$
- $\dfrac{2p}{3}$

## Explanation

Change the base from $4$ to $2$ to use the given value $p$. The numerator is $\log_2(u^3)=3\log_2 u=3p$ because $u>1$. The denominator is $\log_2 4=2$, so $\log_4(u^3)=\dfrac{3p}{2}$. The factor $3$ comes from the exponent of the argument and the divisor $2$ from that of the base. Since $p>0$, the result is positive. At $u=2$, this gives $\log_4 8=\dfrac32$.
