If $\log_3 x=2\log_3 y$, with $x,y>0$, which relation follows?

- $x=2y$
- $x=3y^2$
- $x=\sqrt y$
- $x=y^2$

## Explanation

For $y>0$, the power law gives $2\log_3 y=\log_3(y^2)$. Equality with $\log_3 x$ implies equality of their positive arguments, so $x=y^2$. For example, $y=3$ gives $x=9$, with $2\log_3 3=2$ and $\log_3 9=2$. The coefficient $2$ becomes the exponent of the argument. The relation is also sufficient, because substituting $x=y^2$ gives back the original equality.
