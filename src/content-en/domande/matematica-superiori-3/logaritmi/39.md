What is the domain of $\sqrt{\log_3 x}$?

- $(0,1]$
- $(0,+\infty)$
- $(1,+\infty)$
- $[1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The inner logarithm $\log_3 x$ requires $x>0$. Its value is under a square root and must be nonnegative. Since $\log_3 1=0$ and the logarithm increases, this gives $x\geq1$. The endpoint $1$ is included because the square root of zero is defined. For $0<x<1$ the logarithm is negative. For example, $x=9$ gives $\sqrt2$, confirming that the composition is defined to the right of $1$.
