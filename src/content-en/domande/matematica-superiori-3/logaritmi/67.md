The curve $y=\log_2(x^2+1)$ touches the level $y=0$. At how many different integer inputs is the vertical coordinate *strictly positive* but less than $1$?

- Two.
- None.
- Four.
- One.

## Explanation

The condition $0<\log_2(x^2+1)<1$ is equivalent to $1<x^2+1<2$. An integer square strictly between $0$ and $1$ would be needed. The input $x=0$ gives the lower level and $x=\pm1$ gives the upper level, both excluded.
