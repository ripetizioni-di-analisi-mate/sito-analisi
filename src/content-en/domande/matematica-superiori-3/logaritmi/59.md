How many real solutions does $(\log_3 x-2)^2+1=0$ have?

- None.
- Two.
- Infinitely many.
- One.

## Explanation

For $x>0$, the quantity $\log_3 x-2$ is real. Its square is at least zero, so $(\log_3 x-2)^2+1\geq1$. The minimum $1$ occurs when $\log_3 x=2$, which is at $x=9$. As even the minimum exceeds zero, the equation has no real solutions. A repeated zero would require the minimum to be zero, whereas here it is strictly positive.
