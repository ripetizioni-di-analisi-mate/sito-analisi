How many solutions does $\ln(x-2)=\ln(k)$ have on $x>2$ if $k>0$?

- None for $0<k<1$.
- One only when $k=1$.
- One for every $k>0$.
- Two for $k>1$.

## Explanation

Injectivity gives $x-2=k$, so the unique candidate is $x=k+2$. Since $k>0$, we have $x>2$ and the candidate lies in the domain.
