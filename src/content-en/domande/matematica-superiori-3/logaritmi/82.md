Let $a,b>0$ satisfy $\ln a+\ln b=\ln12$ and $\ln a-\ln b=\ln3$. What is the smaller of $a$ and $b$?

- $4$
- $3$
- $2$
- $6$

## Explanation

The sum of the logarithms gives $ab=12$, and the difference gives $\dfrac ab=3$. Substituting $a=3b$ into the first relation yields $3b^2=12$. Positivity gives $b=2$ and therefore $a=6$.
