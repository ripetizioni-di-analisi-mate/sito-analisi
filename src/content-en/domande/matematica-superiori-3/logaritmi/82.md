How many solutions does $\ln(x-2)=\ln(k)$ have on $x>2$ if $k>0$?

- None for $0<k<1$.
- One only when $k=1$.
- One for every $k>0$.
- Two for $k>1$.

## Explanation

The arguments $x-2$ and $k$ are positive under $x>2$ and $k>0$. Because $\ln$ is one-to-one, the equation is equivalent to $x-2=k$, so its sole candidate is $x=k+2$. As $k>0$, that candidate is always greater than $2$ and does give argument $k$. There is therefore one solution for every allowed $k$.
