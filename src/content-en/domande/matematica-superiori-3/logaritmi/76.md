On a scale $m=-\log_{10} c$ with $c>0$, what happens to $m$ if $c$ falls to one tenth of its value?

- It increases by $10$.
- It increases by $1$.
- It decreases by $1$.
- It becomes one tenth of its value.

## Explanation

The new argument is $c\cdot10^{-1}$. Its logarithm is $\log_{10}c-1$. The outer minus sign turns that decrease into an increase of $1$.
