On a scale $m=-\log_{10} c$ with $c>0$, what happens to $m$ if $c$ falls to one tenth of its value?

- It increases by $10$.
- It increases by $1$.
- It decreases by $1$.
- It becomes one tenth of its value.

## Explanation

The new value of $c$ is $c\cdot10^{-1}$. By the product law, $\log_{10}(c\cdot10^{-1})=\log_{10}c-1$. The definition of $m$ changes the sign of the entire logarithm, so the new value is $-\log_{10}c+1=m+1$. The difference is $1$ for every positive $c$, regardless of the initial level. For example, with $c=1$ the value goes from $m=0$ to $m=1$.
