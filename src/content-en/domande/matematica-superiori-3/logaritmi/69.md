Three positive readings $A,B,C$ are each transformed by taking their base-$2$ logarithm. The arithmetic mean of the three transformed readings is $3$. If $A=4$ and $B=8$, what was the original reading $C$?

- $8$
- $15$
- $16$
- $4$

## Explanation

The three logarithms must sum to $3\cdot3=9$. The first two transformed readings are $2$ and $3$, so $\log_2 C=9-2-3=4$. Returning to the original scale gives $C=2^4=16$.
