In a model $N(t)=100\cdot2^t$, measurements are taken only at whole hours. What is the first hour with $N(t)\geq600$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

The initial value is $100$, so the threshold requires a factor of at least $6$. Since $2^2=4$ and $2^3=8$, we have $N(2)=400<600<N(3)=800$. The function increases, so no earlier whole hour works. The first is $3$. The exact time of equality is $\log_2 6$, between $2$ and $3$. The observation at $2$ is too early, even though the integer part of the exact time is $2$.
