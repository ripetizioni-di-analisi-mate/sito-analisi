In a model $N(t)=100\cdot2^t$, measurements are taken only at whole hours. What is the first hour with $N(t)\geq600$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

The ratio of threshold to initial value is $6$. Since $2^2=4<6<8=2^3$ and the model is increasing, the first whole-hour observation that qualifies is $3$.
