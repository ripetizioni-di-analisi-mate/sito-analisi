Solve $\log_x 16=2$.

- $\{-4,4\}$
- $\{8\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac14\right\}$
- $\{4\}$

## Explanation

Here $x$ is the base, so $x>0$ and $x\ne1$. The exponential form is $x^2=16$, with candidates $-4$ and $4$. Only $4$ is positive and different from $1$. Checking $4^2=16$ gives $\log_4 16=2$. The negative candidate is not an admissible base. The condition $x\ne1$ is separate from positivity and is also satisfied by $4$.
