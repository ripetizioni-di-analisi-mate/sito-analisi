For $x>2$, write $\ln(x+2)+\ln(x-2)$ as a single logarithm. Which expression is correct?

- $\ln(x^2-4)$
- $\ln(x^2+4)$
- $\ln\dfrac{x+2}{x-2}$
- $\ln(2x)$

## Explanation

For $x>2$ both arguments are positive, so the product law applies. The product of $x+2$ and $x-2$ is $x^2-4$.
