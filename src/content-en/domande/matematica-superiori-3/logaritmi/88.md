The graph of $y=c+k\log_2 x$ passes through $(1,3)$ and $(4,9)$. What is the vertical coordinate at $x=8$?

- $15$
- $12$
- $10$
- $18$

## Explanation

At the first point $\log_2 1=0$, so $c=3$. At the second point $\log_2 4=2$, and $9=3+2k$ gives $k=3$. At $x=8$, with $\log_2 8=3$, the formula returns $3+3\cdot3=12$.
