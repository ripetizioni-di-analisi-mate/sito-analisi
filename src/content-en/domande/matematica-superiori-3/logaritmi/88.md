A model $y(t)=3\cdot2^t+1$ reaches the level $13$. What is $t$?

- $4$
- $2$
- $3$
- $1$

## Explanation

The equation is $3\cdot2^t+1=13$. First subtract $1$ to remove the fixed amount. Dividing by $3$ gives $2^t=\dfrac{13-1}{3}=4$. Since $4=2^2$, $t=2$. Checking in the original formula gives $3\cdot4+1=13$. Dividing $13$ directly by $3$ would keep the fixed amount in the quotient. The model is increasing, so this is the only time when the level is attained exactly.
