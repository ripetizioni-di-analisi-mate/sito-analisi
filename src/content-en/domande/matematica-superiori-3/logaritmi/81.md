How do $\log_2 7$ and $\log_3 7$ compare?

- $\log_2 7=-\log_3 7$
- $\log_2 7=\log_3 7$
- $\log_2 7>\log_3 7$
- $\log_2 7<\log_3 7$

## Explanation

With a common base the numerator is $\ln7>0$ in both cases. Since $0<\ln2<\ln3$, the first ratio has the smaller positive denominator and hence the larger value.
