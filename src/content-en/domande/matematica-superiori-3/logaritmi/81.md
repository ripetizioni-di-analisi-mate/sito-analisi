How do $\log_2 7$ and $\log_3 7$ compare?

- $\log_2 7=-\log_3 7$
- $\log_2 7=\log_3 7$
- $\log_2 7>\log_3 7$
- $\log_2 7<\log_3 7$

## Explanation

Changing base gives $\dfrac{\ln7}{\ln2}$ and $\dfrac{\ln7}{\ln3}$. The shared numerator is positive and the denominators satisfy $0<\ln2<\ln3$, so the first quotient is larger. In terms of powers, base $2$ needs a larger exponent than base $3$ to produce the same argument $7$. If the argument were between $0$ and $1$, the numerator would be negative and the comparison of the ratios would reverse.
