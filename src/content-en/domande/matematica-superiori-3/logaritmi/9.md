Find the base $a>0$, $a\ne1$, for which $\log_a 9=-2$.

- $a=3$
- $a=9$
- $a=\dfrac19$
- $a=\dfrac13$

## Explanation

By definition $\log_a 9=-2$ is equivalent to $a^{-2}=9$. Taking reciprocals gives $a^2=\dfrac19$. Of the two real roots, keep the positive one, $a=\dfrac13$, because a logarithmic base must be positive. It differs from $1$ and satisfies $\left(\dfrac13\right)^{-2}=9$. The base $3$ would give $\dfrac19$. The sign of the exponent thus also predicts that the base lies between $0$ and $1$.
