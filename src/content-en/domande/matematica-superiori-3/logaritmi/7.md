Solve $\log_{\frac12}(x-4)\geq-1$.

- $(4,5]$
- $(-\infty,6]$
- $[6,+\infty)$
- $(4,6]$

## Explanation

The level $-1$ is attained in base $\dfrac12$ at argument $\left(\dfrac12\right)^{-1}=2$. Since the logarithm is decreasing, a value at least $-1$ corresponds to an argument at most $2$. Positivity gives $0<x-4\leq2$. The interval is $(4,6]$, with $6$ included because it gives exactly $-1$. For instance, $x=5$ is inside the interval and gives argument $1$, so its height is zero.
