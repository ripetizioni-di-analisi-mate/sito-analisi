What is the range of $y=\log_5(x+3)-2$?

- $(0,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-3,+\infty)$
- $(-2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The domain is $x>-3$, but the range concerns the heights attained. For a fixed real height $y$, the equation $y=\log_5(x+3)-2$ gives $x+3=5^{y+2}$. This power is positive for every $y$, so $x=5^{y+2}-3$ lies in the domain. Every height therefore has a corresponding input and the range is $\mathbb R$. For example, $y=-2$ is attained at $x=-2$, where the argument is $1$.
