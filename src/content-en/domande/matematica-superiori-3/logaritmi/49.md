What is the range of $y=\log_5(x+3)-2$?

- $(0,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-3,+\infty)$
- $(-2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

For $x>-3$, the argument $x+3$ takes every positive value. The logarithm therefore takes every real value. Subtracting $2$ from each still gives range $\mathbb R$.
