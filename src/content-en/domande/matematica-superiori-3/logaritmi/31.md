For $a,b>0$ with $a,b\ne1$, what is $\log_a b\cdot\log_b a$?

- $-1$
- $2$
- $0$
- $1$

## Explanation

Changing base gives $\log_a b=\dfrac{\ln b}{\ln a}$ and $\log_b a=\dfrac{\ln a}{\ln b}$. The hypotheses exclude $1$ for both bases, so $\ln a$ and $\ln b$ are nonzero. The two factors are reciprocals and their product is $1$, even when both logarithms are negative. For example, with $a=2$ and $b=8$ the two factors are $3$ and $\dfrac13$.
