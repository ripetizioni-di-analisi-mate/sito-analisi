Which step preserves the solutions of $\log_2(x-1)+\log_2(x-3)=0$?

- $(x-1)+(x-3)=1$ with $x>3$.
- $(x-1)(x-3)=1$ with $x>3$.
- $(x-1)(x-3)=0$ with $x>3$.
- $(x-1)(x-3)=1$ with $x\in\mathbb R$.

## Explanation

The sum requires both arguments to be positive, so $x>3$. On that domain the sum is the logarithm of the product, which is zero when the product equals $1$.
