Solve $\log_3(2x-1)=\log_3(x+3)$.

- $\{2\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\{4\}$
- $\{-4\}$

## Explanation

Both logarithms require $2x-1>0$ and $x+3>0$, hence $x>\dfrac12$. On this interval the base-$3$ logarithm is one-to-one and equality reduces to $2x-1=x+3$, giving $x=4$. Substitution gives two arguments equal to $7$, so the candidate is admissible. The step is reversible on the domain. The linear equation has one root, so the value found is the unique solution.
