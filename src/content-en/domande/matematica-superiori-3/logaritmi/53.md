The functions $f(x)=\log_3(2x-1)$ and $g(x)=\log_3(x+3)$ agree at one point. What is its vertical coordinate?

- $4$
- $7$
- $\log_3 7$
- $\log_3 4$

## Explanation

The logarithm is one-to-one, so the two arguments agree at the common point. From $2x-1=x+3$ we get $x=4$, where both arguments equal $7$. Applying the logarithm to that positive value gives the vertical coordinate.
