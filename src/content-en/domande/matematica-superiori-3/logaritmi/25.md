Given $p=\log_2 3$, which expression in terms of $p$ is equal to $\log_2 12$?

- $p+2$
- $p+4$
- $p-2$
- $2p$

## Explanation

To use $p=\log_2 3$, write $12$ as a product of $3$ and a power of the base. Since $12=3\cdot4=3\cdot2^2$, we have $\log_2 12=\log_2 3+\log_2(2^2)=p+2$. The factor $4$ adds $2$ to the logarithm because multiplying by $2^2$ raises the exponent of base $2$ by $2$. In exponential form, $2^{p+2}=2^p\cdot4=3\cdot4$, recovering argument $12$.
