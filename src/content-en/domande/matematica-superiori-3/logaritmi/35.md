What is the sign of $\dfrac{\ln 7}{\ln(\frac13)}$?

- Zero.
- Positive.
- Negative.
- Indeterminate.

## Explanation

The natural logarithm increases and is zero at $1$. Because $7>1$, the numerator $\ln7$ is positive. Because $0<\dfrac13<1$, the denominator $\ln(\frac13)$ is negative and nonzero. The quotient is therefore negative. By the change-of-base formula it is also $\log_{\frac13}7$, which is negative because its base lies between $0$ and $1$.
