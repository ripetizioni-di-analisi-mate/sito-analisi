To compare $y=8x^2$ with experimental data, both coordinates are changed to $X=\log_2 x$ and $Y=\log_2 y$, with $x,y>0$. Which line should result?

- $Y=3+X^2$
- $Y=3+2X$
- $Y=6X$
- $Y=8+2X$

## Explanation

Taking the logarithm of the product gives $Y=\log_2 8+\log_2(x^2)$. The first term equals $3$ and the second is $2\log_2 x=2X$. The transformed data should therefore lie on a line with intercept $3$ and slope $2$.
