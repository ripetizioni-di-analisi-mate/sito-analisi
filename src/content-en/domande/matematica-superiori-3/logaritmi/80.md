Two positive quantities $u$ and $v$ may vary independently with $1\leq\log_{10}u\leq2$ and $3\leq\log_{10}v\leq5$. Which interval contains *exactly* the possible values of $\log_{10}(u/v)$?

- $[-3,-2]$
- $[-4,-1]$
- $[-1,4]$
- $[4,7]$

## Explanation

The logarithm of the quotient is $\log_{10}u-\log_{10}v$. The minimum comes from subtracting $5$ from $1$, and the maximum from subtracting $3$ from $2$. The two logarithms vary independently over their intervals, so every intermediate value occurs as well.
