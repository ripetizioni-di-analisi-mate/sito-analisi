Which ordering is correct?

- $\log_2 5<\log_2 3<\log_2 9$
- $\log_2 3<\log_2 5<\log_2 9$
- $\log_2 3<\log_2 9<\log_2 5$
- $\log_2 9<\log_2 5<\log_2 3$

## Explanation

All three arguments are positive and satisfy $3<5<9$. Base $2$ exceeds $1$, so the logarithm is strictly increasing and preserves both inequalities. This gives $\log_2 3<\log_2 5<\log_2 9$. No approximations are needed, because the direction follows from the shared base. A reciprocal base such as $\dfrac12$ would reverse the order of the three values.
