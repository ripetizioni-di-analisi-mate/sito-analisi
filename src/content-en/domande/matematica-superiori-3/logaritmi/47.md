A scale assigns a positive quantity $I$ the score $L=\log_{10}(I/I_0)$ relative to $I_0>0$. If the reference becomes $100I_0$ with $I$ fixed, how does the score change?

- It increases by $2$
- It decreases by $2$
- It is divided by $100$
- It stays the same

## Explanation

The ratio inside the logarithm is divided by $100$. The quotient rule subtracts $\log_{10}100=2$ from the previous score.
