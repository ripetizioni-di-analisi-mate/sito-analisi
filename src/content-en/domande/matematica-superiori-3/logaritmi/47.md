At what $x$ does the graph of $y=\log_2(x-2)-1$ cross the $x$-axis?

- $5$
- $4$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

Points on the horizontal axis have height zero. Thus $\log_2(x-2)-1=0$ is equivalent to $\log_2(x-2)=1$. The argument is $2^1=2$, giving $x=4$. At $x=3$ the logarithm is zero but the function is $-1$ because of its vertical shift. The intersection point is $(4,0)$. The input $4$ satisfies $x>2$, as required for the argument to be positive.
