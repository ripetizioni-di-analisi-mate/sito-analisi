The graph shows $y=\log_2(x-1)$ and the line $y=1$. At which $x$-coordinates is the curve above the line?

::grafico{f="log(x-1)/log(2);1" xmin=0 xmax=6 ymin=-3 ymax=3 asintoti="x=1" punti="3,1" legenda="\log_2(x-1);1" titolo="The logarithm at level one"}

- $(1,3)$
- $(1,+\infty)$
- $(3,+\infty)$
- $[3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The graph is defined for $x>1$. To find its intersection with the line, set $\log_2(x-1)=1$, so $x-1=2$ and $x=3$. Because the curve increases, it is above level $1$ to the right of $3$ and below it to the left. The intersection itself is excluded because the curve must lie strictly above.
