The graph shows $y=\log_2(x-1)$ and the line $y=1$. At which $x$-coordinates is the curve above the line?

::grafico{f="log(x-1)/log(2);1" xmin=0 xmax=6 ymin=-3 ymax=3 asintoti="x=1" punti="3,1" legenda="\log_2(x-1);1" titolo="The logarithm at level one"}

- $(1,3)$
- $(1,+\infty)$
- $(3,+\infty)$
- $[3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The intersection has $x$-coordinate $3$ because $\log_2(3-1)=1$. The curve is increasing, so it is strictly above the level for $x>3$.
