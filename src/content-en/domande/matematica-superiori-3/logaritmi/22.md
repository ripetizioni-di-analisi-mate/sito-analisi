Which formula for $3\ln x-2\ln y$ holds on the whole domain $x,y>0$?

- $\ln(x^3-y^2)$
- $\ln\dfrac{x^3}{y^2}$
- $\ln\dfrac{3x}{2y}$
- $\ln\dfrac{y^2}{x^3}$

## Explanation

The power law gives $3\ln x=\ln(x^3)$ and $2\ln y=\ln(y^2)$. The subtraction puts $y^2$ in the denominator.
