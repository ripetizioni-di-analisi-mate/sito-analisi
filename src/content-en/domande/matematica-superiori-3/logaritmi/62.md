For which $x$ is $\log_{\frac12}(3-x)$ at least as large as $\log_{\frac12}(x+1)$?

- $[1,3)$
- $(-1,1]$
- $[1,3]$
- $(1,3)$

## Explanation

Since $3-x>0$ and $x+1>0$, the common domain is $-1<x<3$. The base is less than $1$, so a greater logarithm corresponds to a smaller argument. This gives $3-x\leq x+1$, or $x\geq1$, which must be intersected with the domain.
