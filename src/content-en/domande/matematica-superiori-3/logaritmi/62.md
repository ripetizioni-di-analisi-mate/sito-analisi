For which $x$ is $\log_{\frac12}(3-x)$ at least as large as $\log_{\frac12}(x+1)$?

- $[1,3)$
- $(-1,1]$
- $[1,3]$
- $(1,3)$

## Explanation

The conditions $3-x>0$ and $x+1>0$ give $-1<x<3$. The base $\dfrac12$ makes the logarithm decreasing, so the comparison becomes $3-x\leq x+1$, meaning $x\geq1$. Intersecting with the domain gives $[1,3)$. At $1$ both functions have argument $2$ and at $3$ the first has argument zero. At $x=2$ the arguments are $1$ and $3$, so the first logarithm is greater.
