Which property characterises the graph of $y=\log_a x$ for every admissible base $a$?

- It is always increasing.
- It passes through $(0,1)$.
- Its range is $(0,+\infty)$.
- It passes through $(1,0)$.

## Explanation

From $a^0=1$ we get $\log_a 1=0$, so $(1,0)$ belongs to every graph in the family. The domain is $x>0$ and the direction of change depends on the base.
