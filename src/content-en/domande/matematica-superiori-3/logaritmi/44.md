Which property characterises the graph of $y=\log_a x$ for every admissible base $a$?

- It is always increasing.
- It passes through $(0,1)$.
- Its range is $(0,+\infty)$.
- It passes through $(1,0)$.

## Explanation

Every admissible base $a$ satisfies $a^0=1$, hence $\log_a 1=0$. Every curve in the family passes through $(1,0)$ regardless of the base. The point $(0,1)$ belongs to the exponential, the inverse of the logarithm. The logarithm has domain $(0,+\infty)$, range $\mathbb R$ and increases or decreases according to $a$. None of the logarithmic curves meets the vertical axis, since zero lies outside the domain.
