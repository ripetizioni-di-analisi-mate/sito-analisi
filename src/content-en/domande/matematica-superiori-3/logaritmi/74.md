A substance halves each day according to $S(t)=96\left(\dfrac12\right)^t$. What is the first whole day with $S(t)<12$?

- $8$
- $3$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Dividing $12$ by $96$ gives $\dfrac18$, the third power of $\dfrac12$. Thus $S(3)=12$. The model decreases and the inequality is strict, so day $3$ is not yet admissible. On the next day the quantity halves again and is $6$. The first whole day that meets the condition is day $4$.
