An index is defined by $J=\log_{10}R$ with $R>0$. The index lies in $[-1,2)$. What is the ratio of the upper bound to the lower bound of the allowed interval of $R$?

- $100$
- $10$
- $3$
- $1000$

## Explanation

Raising $10$ to the endpoint values gives the interval $[10^{-1},10^2)$ for $R$. Its bounds are $\dfrac1{10}$ and $100$. Their ratio is $100\div\dfrac1{10}=1000$, even though the upper bound is never attained.
