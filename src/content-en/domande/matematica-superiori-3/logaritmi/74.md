A substance halves each day according to $S(t)=96\left(\dfrac12\right)^t$. What is the first whole day with $S(t)<12$?

- $8$
- $3$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

We have $S(3)=96\cdot\dfrac18=12$. The threshold is strict, so day $3$ is excluded. The model is decreasing and $S(4)=6$, making day four the first qualifying day.
