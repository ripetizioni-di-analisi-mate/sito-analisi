Two measurements give levels $L_1$ and $L_2$ on a scale $L=10\log_{10}\dfrac{I}{I_0}$ with the same reference $I_0>0$. If $L_2-L_1=30$, what is $\dfrac{I_2}{I_1}$?

- $3$
- $1000$
- $100$
- $30$

## Explanation

Both levels use the same reference $I_0$, which cancels in the quotient of the arguments. This gives $L_2-L_1=10\log_{10}\dfrac{I_2}{I_1}$. As the difference is $30$, the logarithm is $3$ and the ratio is $10^3=1000$. The number $3$ is the exponent of ten, whereas $1000$ is the intensity multiplier.
