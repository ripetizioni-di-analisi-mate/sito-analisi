A sound meter reads $30\,\mathrm{dB}$ with a source on and $20\,\mathrm{dB}$ of background alone with it off. Using the same reference $I_0$, the level is $10\log_{10}(I/I_0)$ and intensities add. What is the ratio of the intensity of the *source alone* to the background intensity?

- $100$
- $9$
- $\dfrac32$
- $10$

## Explanation

The reading with the source on corresponds to $10^3I_0$ and the background to $10^2I_0$. Subtract the intensities to isolate the source, obtaining $900I_0$. The ratio to the background is therefore $\dfrac{900I_0}{100I_0}=9$.
