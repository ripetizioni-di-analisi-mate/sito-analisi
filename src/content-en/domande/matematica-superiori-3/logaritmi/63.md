What is the solution set of the inequality $\ln(x^2)\geq0$?

- $(-\infty,-1]\cup[1,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$
- $[1,+\infty)$
- $[-1,1]$

## Explanation

The domain is $x\ne0$. Since $\ln$ increases and $\ln1=0$, the inequality is equivalent to $x^2\geq1$. The distance of $x$ from zero must be at least $1$, so $x\leq-1$ or $x\geq1$. Both endpoints are included and give logarithm zero. All the resulting inputs already lie in the domain.
