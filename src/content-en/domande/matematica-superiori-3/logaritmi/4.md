Find the domain of $f(x)=\log_3\dfrac{x-4}{x+1}$.

- $(-\infty,-1]\cup[4,+\infty)$
- $(4,+\infty)$
- $(-1,4)$
- $(-\infty,-1)\cup(4,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The quotient is positive when the factors have the same sign. Both are negative for $x<-1$ and both are positive for $x>4$. Exclude $-1$, which makes the denominator zero, and $4$, which makes the argument zero.
