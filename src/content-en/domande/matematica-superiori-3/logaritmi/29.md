To evaluate $\log_5 25$ with the $\ln$ key, which step is correct?

- $\ln\dfrac{25}{5}=2$
- $\ln 25-\ln 5=2$
- $\dfrac{\ln 25}{\ln 5}=2$
- $\dfrac{\ln 5}{\ln 25}=2$

## Explanation

In the change-of-base formula, the logarithm of the argument goes in the numerator and that of the base in the denominator. Since $25=5^2$, we have $\ln25=2\ln5$, so the ratio is $2$.
