To evaluate $\log_5 25$ with the $\ln$ key, which step is correct?

- $\ln\dfrac{25}{5}=2$
- $\ln 25-\ln 5=2$
- $\dfrac{\ln 25}{\ln 5}=2$
- $\dfrac{\ln 5}{\ln 25}=2$

## Explanation

Set $u=\log_5 25$, so $5^u=25$ by definition. Applying $\ln$ to both positive sides gives $u\ln5=\ln25$, hence $u=\dfrac{\ln25}{\ln5}$. Since $25=5^2$, the numerator is $2\ln5$ and the quotient equals $2$. Reversing the ratio would give the reciprocal $\dfrac12$. The denominator may be used because $\ln5$ is positive. The check $5^2=25$ connects the result with the definition.
