Comparing $\log_2(x+3)$ and $\log_2(2x-1)$ gives $x<4$ after the logarithms are removed. What is the smallest integer also allowed by the common domain?

- $-2$
- $1$
- $0$
- $3$

## Explanation

The arguments must satisfy $x+3>0$ and $2x-1>0$. The stronger restriction is $x>\dfrac12$, giving the allowed interval $\left(\dfrac12,4\right)$. Its integer values are $1,2,3$, and the smallest is $1$.
