Solve $\log_2(x+3)>\log_2(2x-1)$.

- $\left[\dfrac12,4\right]$
- $\left(\dfrac12,4\right)$
- $(-3,4)$
- $(4,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The common domain requires $x+3>0$ and $2x-1>0$, so $x>\dfrac12$. The base-$2$ logarithm is increasing, so compare the arguments in the same direction. From $x+3>2x-1$ we get $x<4$. The solution is $\left(\dfrac12,4\right)$. At $4$ the arguments agree, and at $\dfrac12$ the second is zero. At $x=2$, for example, the arguments are $5$ and $3$, in the required order.
