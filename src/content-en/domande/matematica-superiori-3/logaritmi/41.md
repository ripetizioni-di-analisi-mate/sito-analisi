What is the domain of $\ln(x-1)+\ln(5-x)$?

- $(1,5)$
- $(-\infty,1)\cup(5,+\infty)$
- $(1,+\infty)$
- $[1,5]$

## Explanation

Both logarithms must exist. From $x-1>0$ we get $x>1$, and from $5-x>0$ we get $x<5$. Their intersection is $(1,5)$. At $x=1$ the first argument is zero and at $x=5$ the second is zero. One zero argument is enough to make the sum undefined. At $x=3$ both arguments are $2$, which also shows that the intersection is nonempty.
