Solve $\log_2\dfrac{x-1}{x+2}<0$.

- $(1,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-2)$
- $(-2,1)$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The quotient is positive for $x<-2$ or $x>1$. On that domain the quotient must be less than $1$. Since $\dfrac{x-1}{x+2}-1=\dfrac{-3}{x+2}$, this happens when $x>-2$. The intersection leaves $x>1$.
