Solve $\log_9 x+\log_3 x=3$.

- $\left\{\dfrac19\right\}$
- $\{9\}$
- $\{27\}$
- $\{3\}$

## Explanation

The bases are related by $9=3^2$. On $x>0$, setting $u=\log_3 x$ gives $\log_9 x=\dfrac{u}{\log_3 9}=\dfrac u2$. The equation becomes $\dfrac u2+u=3$, so $u=2$. Therefore $x=3^2=9$. At that point the original logarithms are $1$ and $2$, respectively. The fact that base $9$ equals $3^2$ divides the exponent by $2$.
