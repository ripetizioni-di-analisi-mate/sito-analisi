In $\log_9 x+\log_3 x=3$ the terms have different bases. What is the value of the term $\log_9 x$ at the solution?

- $\dfrac32$
- $1$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

If $u=\log_9 x$, then $x=9^u=3^{2u}$ and $\log_3 x=2u$. The equation becomes $u+2u=3$, giving $u=1$. The corresponding value $x=9$ is positive and satisfies the domain condition.
