Under which condition on $a$ does $\log_a 5<0$ hold?

- $a>5$
- $0<a<1$
- $1<a<5$
- $a=1$

## Explanation

Use $\log_a 1=0$ for comparison. Since $5>1$, when $a>1$ the logarithm preserves order and gives $\log_a 5>0$. When $0<a<1$ the function is decreasing and gives $\log_a 5<0$. For example, with $a=\dfrac15$, $\log_{\frac15}5=-1$. The interval $0<a<1$ is the set of all the required bases. Base $1$ is excluded regardless of the logarithm's argument.
