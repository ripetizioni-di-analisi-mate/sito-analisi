Which set describes the solutions of $\log_{\frac15}x\leq1$?

- $\left(\dfrac15,+\infty\right)$
- $\left(0,\dfrac15\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac15,+\infty\right)$
- $[5,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Write $1=\log_{\frac15}(\frac15)$. The function is decreasing, so values less than or equal to $1$ come from arguments greater than or equal to $\dfrac15$.
