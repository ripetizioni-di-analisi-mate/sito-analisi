Which set describes the solutions of $\log_{\frac15}x\leq1$?

- $\left(\dfrac15,+\infty\right)$
- $\left(0,\dfrac15\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac15,+\infty\right)$
- $[5,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Write level $1$ as $\log_{\frac15}(\frac15)$. Since the base is between $0$ and $1$, a logarithm value at most $1$ corresponds to an argument at least $\dfrac15$. That condition already ensures $x>0$. The endpoint $\dfrac15$ is included, whereas for $0<x<\dfrac15$ the logarithm exceeds $1$. At $x=1$ we get zero, which satisfies the inequality and lies on the allowed branch.
