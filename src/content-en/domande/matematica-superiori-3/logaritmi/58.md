What is the solution set of $\ln(x^2)=0$?

- $\{-1,1\}$
- $\{-1\}$
- $\{1\}$
- $\{0\}$

## Explanation

The argument $x^2$ is positive for every $x\ne0$. The natural logarithm is zero exactly when its argument is $e^0=1$, so $x^2=1$. The two solutions are $x=-1$ and $x=1$, both in the domain. The base remains $e$ even at $x=-1$, because the argument is the square of $x$.
