What is the value of $\log_3 12+\log_3\dfrac94$?

- $3$
- $2$
- $27$
- $9$

## Explanation

The two arguments are positive and both terms use base $3$. Their sum is the logarithm of the product, which is $12\cdot\dfrac94=3\cdot9=27$. Since $27=3^3$, the answer is $3$. Cancelling the denominator $4$ against the factor $12$ reveals the power without approximating the individual logarithms. The product $27$ is the argument of the combined logarithm, and the answer is its base-$3$ exponent.
