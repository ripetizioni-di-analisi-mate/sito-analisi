What is the inverse of $f(x)=2^x+3$, defined for $x\in\mathbb R$?

- $f^{-1}(x)=\log_2(x-3)$ for $x>3$.
- $f^{-1}(x)=\log_2 x-3$ for $x>0$.
- $f^{-1}(x)=\log_2(x-3)$ for $x\ne3$.
- $f^{-1}(x)=\log_2(x+3)$ for $x>-3$.

## Explanation

From $y=2^x+3$ we have $y-3=2^x>0$, hence $x=\log_2(y-3)$. The domain of the inverse is the range of the original function, namely $y>3$.
