For which $x$ is $\log_{x^2} 4$ defined?

- $\mathbb R\setminus\{0,1\}$
- $(0,+\infty)\setminus\{1\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-1,0,1\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-1,1\}$

## Explanation

The argument $4$ is positive for every $x$. The conditions therefore concern the base $x^2$, which must be positive and different from $1$. The first excludes $x=0$, and the second excludes $x=-1$ and $x=1$. Negative values other than $-1$ can still give an admissible base. For example, $x=-2$ gives base $4$.
