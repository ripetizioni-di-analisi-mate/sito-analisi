For which $x$ is $\log_{x^2} 4$ defined?

- $\mathbb R\setminus\{0,1\}$
- $(0,+\infty)\setminus\{1\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-1,0,1\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-1,1\}$

## Explanation

The argument $4$ is always positive. The base $x^2$ must be positive and different from $1$, so $0$, $-1$ and $1$ are excluded.
