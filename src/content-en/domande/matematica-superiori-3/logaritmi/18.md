For how many integer bases $b>1$ is $\log_b64$ an integer greater than or equal to $2$?

- $2$
- $6$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

If $b^n=64=2^6$ with $n\geq2$, the integer base $b$ can contain only the prime factor $2$. Writing $b=2^k$ gives $kn=6$. The allowed divisors for $n$ are $2,3,6$, giving the three bases $8,4,2$, respectively.
