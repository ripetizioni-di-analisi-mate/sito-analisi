What is the vertical asymptote of $g(x)=\log_2(x+3)-2$?

- $x=-3$
- $x=3$
- $y=-2$
- $x=0$

## Explanation

The domain starts where $x+3=0$. As $x$ approaches $-3$ from the right, the argument tends to zero and the logarithm decreases without bound. Shifting the graph down by $2$ units leaves the asymptote $x=-3$ unchanged.
