What is the vertical asymptote of $g(x)=\log_2(x+3)-2$?

- $x=-3$
- $x=3$
- $y=-2$
- $x=0$

## Explanation

The argument $x+3$ is positive only to the right of $-3$. If $x=-3+2^{-n}$ for a positive integer $n$, the height is $\log_2(2^{-n})-2=-n-2$. These inputs approach $-3$ from the right while the heights decrease without bound. The asymptote is the vertical line $x=-3$. The term $-2$ shifts the graph vertically. The asymptote is vertical because the horizontal coordinates approach $-3$ while the heights become unbounded.
