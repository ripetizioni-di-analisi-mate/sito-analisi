Solve $\ln(x+2)-\ln(x-1)\geq\ln 2$.

- $[4,+\infty)$
- $(1,4]$
- $(-\infty,4]$
- $(1,4)$

## Explanation

Both logarithms are defined for $x>1$. Their difference is the logarithm of the quotient and, since $\ln$ is increasing, we need $\dfrac{x+2}{x-1}\geq2$. The denominator is positive on the domain, so $x+2\geq2x-2$, giving $x\leq4$. At $x=4$ the quotient is $2$ and equality is allowed. The solution is $(1,4]$. For instance, $x=2$ lies in the solution set and gives a quotient of $4$.
