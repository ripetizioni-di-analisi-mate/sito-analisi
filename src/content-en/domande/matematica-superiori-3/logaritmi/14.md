Solve $\ln(x+2)-\ln(x-1)\geq\ln 2$.

- $[4,+\infty)$
- $(1,4]$
- $(-\infty,4]$
- $(1,4)$

## Explanation

The domain is $x>1$. The natural logarithm is increasing, so the comparison becomes $\dfrac{x+2}{x-1}\geq2$. On the domain $x-1>0$, giving $x+2\geq2(x-1)$ and therefore $x\leq4$.
