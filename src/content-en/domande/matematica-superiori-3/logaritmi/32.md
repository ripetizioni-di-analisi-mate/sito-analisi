Two instruments use different bases and return the same reading $\log_4 u=\log_8 v$, with $u,v>1$. Which relation holds between the original quantities?

- $u^3=v^2$
- $u^2=v^3$
- $u=v$
- $u^4=v^8$

## Explanation

If the common reading is $h$, then $u=4^h=2^{2h}$ and $v=8^h=2^{3h}$. The cube of $u$ and the square of $v$ both equal $2^{6h}$. The assumption $u,v>1$ excludes the case in which all powers equal $1$.
