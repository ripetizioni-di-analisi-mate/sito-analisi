What is $\log_3 5\cdot\log_5 9$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $9$
- $1$

## Explanation

Express both logarithms in one base. The product becomes $\dfrac{\ln5}{\ln3}\cdot\dfrac{\ln9}{\ln5}$. The factor $\ln5$, which is nonzero, cancels. This leaves $\dfrac{\ln9}{\ln3}=\log_3 9=2$. The intermediate number $5$ cancels because it is the first logarithm's argument and the second logarithm's base. The final check is $3^2=9$, which identifies the exponent after the intermediate base cancels.
