Without calculating the exact value, which interval contains $\log_{\frac19} 6$?

- $(-1,0)$
- $(0,1)$
- $(1,2)$
- $(-2,-1)$

## Explanation

For base $\dfrac19$, the exponents $-1$ and $0$ give $9$ and $1$, respectively. Since $1<6<9$ and the exponential is decreasing, the exponent that gives $6$ lies between $-1$ and $0$.
