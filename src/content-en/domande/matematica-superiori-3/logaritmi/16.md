Without calculating the exact value, which interval contains $\log_{\frac19} 6$?

- $(-1,0)$
- $(0,1)$
- $(1,2)$
- $(-2,-1)$

## Explanation

For $a=\dfrac19$ we have $a^{-1}=9$ and $a^0=1$. Since $1<6<9$, the exponent giving $6$ lies between those giving the two boundary values. The exponential is decreasing, so the value $9$ gives the left endpoint $-1$ and $1$ gives the right endpoint $0$. Both endpoints are excluded because $6$ differs from both values.
