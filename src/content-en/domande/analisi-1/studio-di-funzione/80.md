What is the pair of limits at $(-\infty,+\infty)$ for $\dfrac{x - 3}{\left|{x - 3}\right| + 1}$?

- $\left( 0, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 1, \  -1\right)$
- $\left( 1, \  1\right)$
- $\left( -1, \  1\right)$

## Explanation

The denominator is asymptotic to the absolute value of the numerator. The ratio approaches its sign on each branch.
