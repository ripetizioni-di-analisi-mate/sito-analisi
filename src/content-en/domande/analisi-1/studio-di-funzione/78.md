For which $k$ does $y=k$ intersect the graph of $\left(x - 4\right)^{2} + 7$ in two distinct points?

- $\left[7, \infty\right)$
- $\left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(7, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 7\right)$

## Explanation

The vertex has height $7$. Above the vertex each horizontal line crosses both branches.
