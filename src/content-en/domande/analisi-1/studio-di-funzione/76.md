For which $k$ does $y=k$ intersect the graph of $\left(x - 2\right)^{2} + 3$ in two distinct points?

- $\left(2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right)$
- $\left(3, \infty\right)$
- $\left[3, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The vertex has height $3$. Above the vertex each horizontal line crosses both branches.
