The trinomial $f(x) = x^2 - 6x + 10$ takes a smallest value on $\mathbb{R}$. What is it?

- $1$
- $3$
- $4$
- $10$

## Explanation

The derivative $f'(x) = 2x - 6$ is negative for $x < 3$ and positive for $x > 3$, so $f$ decreases on $(-\infty, 3]$ and increases on $[3, +\infty)$. Therefore $x = 3$ is the **absolute minimum point** and the minimum value is $f(3) = 9 - 18 + 10 = 1$. The same value is given by the vertex formula $-\dfrac{\Delta}{4a} = -\dfrac{36 - 40}{4} = 1$.
