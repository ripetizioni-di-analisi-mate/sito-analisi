What is the pair (left derivative, right derivative) at $4$ for $e^{28 - 7 x} \left|{x - 4}\right|$?

- $\left( -1, \  1\right)$
- $\left( -7, \  7\right)$
- $\left( 1, \  -1\right)$
- $\left( 1, \  1\right)$

## Explanation

In the difference quotient, the absolute value produces the sign of $x-4$. The exponential tends to one on either side.
