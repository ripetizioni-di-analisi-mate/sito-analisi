What is the pair (assigned value, slope) of the continuous extension at $4$ of $\dfrac{x^{2} - 16}{x - 4}$?

- $\left( 8, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 8, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 0, \  1\right)$

## Explanation

For $x\ne 4$ cancel the common factor. The line $x + 4$ gives the value and slope of the extension.
