What is the maximal interval of strict increase for $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x - 3 \right)}}{x - 3}$?

- $\left[e + 3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(3, e + 3\right)$
- $\left(3, e + 3\right]$

## Explanation

The domain begins to the right of $3$. The derivative is $- \dfrac{\ln{\left(x - 3 \right)}}{\left(x - 3\right)^{2}} + \dfrac{1}{\left(x - 3\right)^{2}}$, positive up to $e + 3$. The right endpoint can be included in the monotonicity interval.
