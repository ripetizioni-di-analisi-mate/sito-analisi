What is the pair (abscissa of the only zero, ordinate of the horizontal asymptote) for $\dfrac{x - 2}{x^{2} + 3}$?

- $\left( -2, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 0, \  2\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  1\right)$

## Explanation

The denominator is positive. The numerator vanishes at $2$ and has lower degree than the denominator, so the limit at infinity is zero.
