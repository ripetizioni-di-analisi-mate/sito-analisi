What are the abscissas of the global maximum and minimum, in that order, for $x e^{- 4 x^{2}}$?

- $\left( \dfrac{1}{8}, \  - \dfrac{1}{8}\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4}, \  - \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4}\right)$
- $\left( - \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4}, \  \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4}\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{1}{2}, \  - \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$

## Explanation

The derivative has the sign of $1 - 8 x^{2}$ and the function tends to zero at both ends. The positive extremum is the maximum.
