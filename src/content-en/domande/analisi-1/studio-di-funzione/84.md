What are, in this order, the left and the right derivative of the function $f(x) = |\ln x|$ at the point $x = 1$?

- $1$ and $1$
- $0$ and $0$
- $-1$ and $1$
- $1$ and $-1$

## Explanation

For $0 < x < 1$ we have $\ln x < 0$, so $f(x) = -\ln x$ and $f'(x) = -\dfrac{1}{x}$; for $x > 1$ we have $f(x) = \ln x$ and $f'(x) = \dfrac{1}{x}$. The function is continuous at $1$, therefore the one-sided derivatives coincide with the one-sided limits of $f'$, that is, $-1$ from the left and $1$ from the right. The two derivatives are different, so $x = 1$ is a **corner point**.
