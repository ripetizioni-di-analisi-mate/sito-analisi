What are the relative minimum points of the function $f(x) = x^4 - 8x^2$?

- Only $x = 0$
- Only $x = 2$
- $x = -2$, $x = 0$ and $x = 2$
- $x = -2$ and $x = 2$

## Explanation

The derivative $f'(x) = 4x^3 - 16x = 4x(x - 2)(x + 2)$ vanishes at $-2$, $0$ and $2$. The second derivative is $f''(x) = 12x^2 - 16$, with $f''(\pm 2) = 32 > 0$ and $f''(0) = -16 < 0$. By the **second derivative test**, $x = -2$ and $x = 2$ are relative minimum points and $x = 0$ is a relative maximum point. The symmetry of the result agrees with the fact that the function is even.
