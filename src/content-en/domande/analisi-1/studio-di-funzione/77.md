For which $k$ does $y=k$ intersect the graph of $\left(x - 3\right)^{2} + 5$ in two distinct points?

- $\left(-\infty, 5\right)$
- $\left[5, \infty\right)$
- $\left(3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(5, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The vertex has height $5$. Above the vertex each horizontal line crosses both branches.
