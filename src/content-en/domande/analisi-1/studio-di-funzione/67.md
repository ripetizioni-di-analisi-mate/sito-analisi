What is the pair (left derivative, right derivative) at $2$ for $e^{6 - 3 x} \left|{x - 2}\right|$?

- $\left( -1, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 1, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 1, \  -1\right)$
- $\left( -3, \  3\right)$

## Explanation

In the difference quotient, the absolute value produces the sign of $x-2$. The exponential tends to one on either side.
