How many real solutions does the equation $e^{x} = 3x$ have?

- None
- One
- Two
- Three

## Explanation

The solutions are the zeros of $g(x) = e^{x} - 3x$. The derivative $g'(x) = e^{x} - 3$ is negative for $x < \ln 3$ and positive for $x > \ln 3$, so $g$ decreases on $(-\infty, \ln 3]$, increases on $[\ln 3, +\infty)$ and has minimum $g(\ln 3) = 3 - 3\ln 3$. Since $\ln 3 > 1$, the minimum is negative. Moreover, $g(x) \to +\infty$ both as $x \to -\infty$ and as $x \to +\infty$, so by the **intermediate zero theorem** and strict monotonicity $g$ vanishes exactly once on each of the two intervals. Therefore the equation has **two solutions**.
