What are the coordinates of the global maximum of $e^{- 7 \left(x - 4\right)^{2}}$?

- $\left( -4, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  7\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  1\right)$

## Explanation

The exponent is nonpositive and is zero only at $4$. The exponential is increasing, so the maximum is one.
