The function $f(x) = e^{2x} - 2e^{x}$ has a single stationary point. On which maximal interval does it decrease?

- $[0, +\infty)$
- $(-\infty, 0)$
- $(-\infty, \ln 2]$
- $(-\infty, 0]$

## Explanation

The derivative is $f'(x) = 2e^{2x} - 2e^{x} = 2e^{x}\left(e^{x} - 1\right)$. The factor $2e^{x}$ is always positive, so the sign of $f'$ is that of $e^{x} - 1$, negative for $x < 0$ and positive for $x > 0$. By the monotonicity test $f$ is **decreasing** on $(-\infty, 0]$ and increasing on $[0, +\infty)$, with absolute minimum $f(0) = -1$.
