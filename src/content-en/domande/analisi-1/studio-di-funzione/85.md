What is the pair (assigned value, slope) of the continuous extension at $2$ of $\dfrac{x^{2} - 4}{x - 2}$?

- $\left( 0, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  0\right)$

## Explanation

For $x\ne 2$ cancel the common factor. The line $x + 2$ gives the value and slope of the extension.
