For which values of $a$ and $b$ is the function defined by $f(x) = x^2$ for $x \leq 1$ and by $f(x) = ax + b$ for $x > 1$ differentiable on $\mathbb{R}$?

- $a = 2$ and $b = -1$
- $a = 2$ and $b = 1$
- $a = 1$ and $b = 0$
- $a = -1$ and $b = 2$

## Explanation

Away from $x = 1$ both expressions are differentiable, so it is enough to impose at $1$ continuity and equality of the one-sided derivatives. Continuity requires $1^2 = a + b$, the left derivative equals $2 \cdot 1 = 2$ and the right derivative equals $a$. The conditions to be satisfied together are
$$
\begin{cases} a + b = 1 \\[4pt] a = 2 , \end{cases}
$$
so $a = 2$ and $b = -1$. With these values the straight piece $y = 2x - 1$ is the tangent line to the parabola at the point $(1, 1)$.
