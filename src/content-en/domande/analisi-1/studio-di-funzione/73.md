What is the maximal interval of convexity containing the minimum of $\ln{\left(\dfrac{\left(x - 2\right)^{2}}{9} + 1 \right)}$?

- $\left[-1, 5\right]$
- $\left(-1, 5\right)$
- $\left[2, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-3, 3\right]$

## Explanation

The minimum is at $2$. The second derivative has the sign of $9 - \left(x - 2\right)^{2}$, which is nonnegative from $-1$ to $5$.
