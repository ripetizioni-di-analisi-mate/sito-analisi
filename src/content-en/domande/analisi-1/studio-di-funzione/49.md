What is the maximal interval of strict increase for $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x - 2 \right)}}{x - 2}$?

- $\left(2, 2 + e\right]$
- $\left(2, \infty\right)$
- $\left[2 + e, \infty\right)$
- $\left(2, 2 + e\right)$

## Explanation

The domain begins to the right of $2$. The derivative is $- \dfrac{\ln{\left(x - 2 \right)}}{\left(x - 2\right)^{2}} + \dfrac{1}{\left(x - 2\right)^{2}}$, positive up to $2 + e$. The right endpoint can be included in the monotonicity interval.
