What is the absolute minimum value of $\left(x - 3\right)^{4} - 5 \left(x - 3\right)^{2}$?

- $0$
- $- \dfrac{25}{4}$
- $\dfrac{25}{4}$
- $-25$

## Explanation

Setting $t=(x-a)^2$ with $a=3$, the problem becomes minimizing $t^{2} - 5 t$ for $t\geq0$. Its vertex is at $\dfrac{5}{2}$.
