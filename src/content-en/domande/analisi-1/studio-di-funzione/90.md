What is the pair (abscissa of the only zero, ordinate of the horizontal asymptote) for $\dfrac{x - 4}{x^{2} + 7}$?

- $\left( 0, \  4\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  0\right)$
- $\left( -4, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  1\right)$

## Explanation

The denominator is positive. The numerator vanishes at $4$ and has lower degree than the denominator, so the limit at infinity is zero.
