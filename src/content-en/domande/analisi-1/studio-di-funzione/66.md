What is the exact positivity set of $\dfrac{x - 4}{x + 7}$?

- $\left(-\infty, -7\right) \cup \left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-7, 4\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -7\right) \cup \left[4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(4, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The critical values are the pole $-7$ and zero $4$. The two factors have the same sign outside them.
