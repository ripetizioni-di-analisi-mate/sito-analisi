The graph of the function $g(x) = x \sin x$ is symmetric with respect to which element of the plane?

- The origin
- The $x$-axis
- The line $y = x$
- The $y$-axis

## Explanation

For every $x \in \mathbb{R}$ we have
$$
g(-x) = (-x)\sin(-x) = (-x)(-\sin x) = x \sin x = g(x) ,
$$
because the sine function is odd. Hence $g$ is **even** and its graph is symmetric with respect to the $y$-axis. The graph is not symmetric with respect to the origin, because $g\!\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right) = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $g\!\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right) = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ have the same sign.
