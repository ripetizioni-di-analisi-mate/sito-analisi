Among all rectangles with perimeter $20\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the largest area?

- $5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $100\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $25\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

If one side measures $x$ centimeters, the other measures $10 - x$, because the sum of two adjacent sides is half the perimeter. The area is therefore $A(x) = x(10 - x) = 10x - x^2$, with $0 < x < 10$. The derivative $A'(x) = 10 - 2x$ is positive for $x < 5$ and negative for $x > 5$, so $x = 5$ is the **absolute maximum point** on $(0, 10)$. The largest area is $A(5) = 25\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, attained by the square with side $5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
