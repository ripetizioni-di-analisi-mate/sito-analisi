What is the maximal interval of strict increase for $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x - 4 \right)}}{x - 4}$?

- $\left[e + 4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(4, e + 4\right)$
- $\left(4, e + 4\right]$

## Explanation

The domain begins to the right of $4$. The derivative is $- \dfrac{\ln{\left(x - 4 \right)}}{\left(x - 4\right)^{2}} + \dfrac{1}{\left(x - 4\right)^{2}}$, positive up to $e + 4$. The right endpoint can be included in the monotonicity interval.
