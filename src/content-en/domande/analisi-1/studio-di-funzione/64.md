What is the exact positivity set of $\dfrac{x - 2}{x + 3}$?

- $\left(-\infty, -3\right) \cup \left[2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -3\right) \cup \left(2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-3, 2\right)$

## Explanation

The endpoints to check are the pole $-3$ and zero $2$. The two factors have the same sign outside the interval they delimit.
