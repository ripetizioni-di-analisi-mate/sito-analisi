What is the pair (abscissa, ordinate) of the absolute maximum of $e^{- 5 \left(x - 3\right)^{2}}$?

- $\left( 3, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  5\right)$
- $\left( -3, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  0\right)$

## Explanation

The exponent is nonpositive and is zero only at $3$. The exponential is increasing, so the maximum is one.
