What is the equation of the tangent line to the graph of $f(x) = x^3 - 3x^2 + 2$ at its inflection point?

- $y = 3x - 3$
- $y = 2$
- $y = -3x + 3$
- $y = -3x$

## Explanation

The second derivative $f''(x) = 6x - 6$ vanishes at $x = 1$ and changes sign, so $x = 1$ is the only inflection point, with $f(1) = 1 - 3 + 2 = 0$. Since $f'(x) = 3x^2 - 6x$, the slope of the tangent at the inflection point is $f'(1) = -3$. Therefore the tangent has equation $y = 0 - 3(x - 1)$, that is, $y = -3x + 3$.
