For $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-1}{x^2+1}$, which pair gives, in order, the number of $x$-intercepts and the height of the horizontal asymptote?

- $(1,1)$
- $(2,1)$
- $(2,-1)$
- $(2,0)$

## Explanation

The denominator is positive for every $x$, so the $x$-intercepts occur where $x^2-1=0$. Their abscissas are $-1$ and $1$. The numerator and denominator have the same degree and leading coefficient, so the limit at both infinities is $1$.
