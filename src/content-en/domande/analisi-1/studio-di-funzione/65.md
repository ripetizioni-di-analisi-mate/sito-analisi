What is the exact positivity set of $\dfrac{x - 3}{x + 5}$?

- $\left(3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-5, 3\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -5\right) \cup \left[3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -5\right) \cup \left(3, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The endpoints to check are the pole $-5$ and zero $3$. The two factors have the same sign outside the interval they delimit.
