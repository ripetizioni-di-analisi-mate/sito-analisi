What are the inflection points of the graph of $f(x) = x^4 - 4x^3 + 10$?

- $(0, 10)$ and $(2, -6)$
- Only $(2, -6)$
- $(0, 10)$ and $(3, -17)$
- $(2, -6)$ and $(3, -17)$

## Explanation

The derivatives are $f'(x) = 4x^3 - 12x^2 = 4x^2(x - 3)$ and $f''(x) = 12x^2 - 24x = 12x(x - 2)$. The second derivative is positive for $x < 0$ and for $x > 2$, and negative for $0 < x < 2$, so it changes sign both at $0$ and at $2$. Therefore both points are **inflection points**. The ordinates are $f(0) = 10$ and $f(2) = 16 - 32 + 10 = -6$. Since $f'(0) = 0$, the point $(0, 10)$ is an inflection point with horizontal tangent. The point $x = 3$ is stationary with $f''(3) = 36 > 0$, so it is a relative minimum point.
