On which interval is the function $f(x) = (x + 1)\,e^{-x}$ concave?

- $[1, +\infty)$
- $(-\infty, 0]$
- $(-\infty, 2]$
- $(-\infty, 1]$

## Explanation

By the product rule $f'(x) = e^{-x} - (x + 1)\,e^{-x} = -x\,e^{-x}$ and, differentiating again, $f''(x) = -e^{-x} + x\,e^{-x} = (x - 1)\,e^{-x}$. Since $e^{-x} > 0$, we have $f''(x) \leq 0$ if and only if $x \leq 1$. By the characterization of convexity through the second derivative, $f$ is **concave** on $(-\infty, 1]$ and convex on $[1, +\infty)$.
