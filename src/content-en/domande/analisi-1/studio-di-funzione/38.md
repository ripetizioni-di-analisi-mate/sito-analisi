The arctangent function, $y = \arctan x$, is concave on a single maximal interval. Which one?

- $(-\infty, 0]$
- $(0, +\infty)$
- $\mathbb{R}$
- $[0, +\infty)$

## Explanation

We have $f'(x) = \dfrac{1}{1 + x^2}$ and therefore
$$
f''(x) = -\frac{2x}{(1 + x^2)^2} .
$$
The denominator is positive, so $f''(x) \leq 0$ if and only if $x \geq 0$. By the characterization of convexity through the second derivative, $f$ is **concave** on $[0, +\infty)$ and convex on $(-\infty, 0]$. The endpoint $0$ belongs to the interval, because concavity on an interval allows $f'' = 0$ at the endpoints.
