What is the value of the relative minimum of the function $f(x) = x^3 - 3x^2 - 9x + 5$?

- $10$
- $-27$
- $-22$
- $3$

## Explanation

The derivative $f'(x) = 3x^2 - 6x - 9 = 3(x + 1)(x - 3)$ is positive for $x < -1$ and for $x > 3$, and negative for $-1 < x < 3$. By the **first derivative test**, $x = -1$ is a relative maximum point and $x = 3$ is a relative minimum point. The relative minimum is $f(3) = 27 - 27 - 27 + 5 = -22$, while the relative maximum is $f(-1) = 10$.
