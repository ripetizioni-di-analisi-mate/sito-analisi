What is the absolute minimum value of $\left(x - 4\right)^{4} - 7 \left(x - 4\right)^{2}$?

- $0$
- $- \dfrac{49}{4}$
- $\dfrac{49}{4}$
- $-49$

## Explanation

Setting $t=(x-a)^2$ with $a=4$, the problem becomes minimizing $t^{2} - 7 t$ for $t\geq0$. Its vertex is at $\dfrac{7}{2}$.
