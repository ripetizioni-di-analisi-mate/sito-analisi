On which maximal interval is the function $f(x) = \dfrac{\ln x}{x}$ strictly increasing?

- $[e, +\infty)$
- $(0, 1]$
- $[1, e]$
- $(0, e]$

## Explanation

The domain is $(0, +\infty)$. By the quotient rule,
$$
f'(x) = \frac{\frac{1}{x} \cdot x - \ln x}{x^2} = \frac{1 - \ln x}{x^2} .
$$
Since $x^2 > 0$, the sign of $f'$ is that of $1 - \ln x$, which is positive for $\ln x < 1$, that is, for $0 < x < e$. By the **monotonicity test**, $f$ is strictly increasing on $(0, e]$ and strictly decreasing on $[e, +\infty)$.
