As $x \to +\infty$ the graph of $f(x) = x \arctan x$ has an oblique asymptote. What is its equation?

- $y = \dfrac{\pi}{2}\,x + 1$
- $y = \dfrac{\pi}{2}\,x - 1$
- $y = \dfrac{\pi}{2}\,x$
- $y = -\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,x - 1$

## Explanation

The slope is $m = \lim\limits_{x \to +\infty} \dfrac{f(x)}{x} = \lim\limits_{x \to +\infty} \arctan x = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. For $x > 0$ we have $\arctan x - \dfrac{\pi}{2} = -\arctan \dfrac{1}{x}$, so
$$
f(x) - \frac{\pi}{2}\,x = -x \arctan\frac{1}{x} = -\frac{\arctan\frac{1}{x}}{\frac{1}{x}} .
$$
Since $\dfrac{1}{x} \to 0^+$ and $\dfrac{\arctan t}{t} \to 1$ as $t \to 0$, the intercept is $q = -1$. Therefore the oblique asymptote as $x \to +\infty$ is $y = \dfrac{\pi}{2}\,x - 1$; the function is even, so as $x \to -\infty$ the asymptote is the symmetric line $y = -\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,x - 1$.
