What is the pair (inflection abscissa, tangent slope) for $- 7 x + \left(x - 4\right)^{3} + 31$?

- $\left( -4, \  -7\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  7\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  -7\right)$

## Explanation

The second derivative $6 \left(x - 4\right)$ changes sign at $4$. The first derivative at that point is $-7$.
