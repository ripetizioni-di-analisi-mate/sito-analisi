What is the maximal interval of convexity containing the minimum of $\ln{\left(\dfrac{\left(x - 4\right)^{2}}{49} + 1 \right)}$?

- $\left[-3, 11\right]$
- $\left[-7, 7\right]$
- $\left[4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-3, 11\right)$

## Explanation

The minimum is at $4$. The second derivative has the sign of $49 - \left(x - 4\right)^{2}$, which is nonnegative from $-3$ to $11$.
