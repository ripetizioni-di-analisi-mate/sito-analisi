What is the pair (abscissa of the inflection point, slope of the tangent) for $- 5 x + \left(x - 3\right)^{3} + 22$?

- $\left( -3, \  -5\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  -5\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  5\right)$

## Explanation

The second derivative $6 \left(x - 3\right)$ changes sign at $3$. The first derivative at that point is $-5$.
