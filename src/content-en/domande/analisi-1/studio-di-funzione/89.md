What is the pair (abscissa of the only zero, ordinate of the horizontal asymptote) for $\dfrac{x - 3}{x^{2} + 5}$?

- $\left( 0, \  3\right)$
- $\left( -3, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  0\right)$

## Explanation

The denominator is positive. The numerator vanishes at $3$ and has lower degree than the denominator, so the limit at infinity is zero.
