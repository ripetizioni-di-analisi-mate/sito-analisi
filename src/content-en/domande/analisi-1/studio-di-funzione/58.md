Where is the polynomial $f(x) = x^4 - 4x$ strictly increasing? Give the maximal interval.

- $[1, +\infty)$
- $(-\infty, 1]$
- $(1, +\infty)$
- $(-\infty, -1] \cup [1, +\infty)$

## Explanation

The derivative is $f'(x) = 4x^3 - 4 = 4\left(x^3 - 1\right)$. Since the function $x^3$ is strictly increasing, $f'(x) > 0$ if and only if $x^3 > 1$, that is, $x > 1$. By the **monotonicity test** $f$ is strictly increasing on $[1, +\infty)$, with the endpoint $1$ included because $f$ is continuous at $1$, and it is strictly decreasing on $(-\infty, 1]$.
