What is the pair (abscissa, ordinate) of the absolute maximum of $e^{- 3 \left(x - 2\right)^{2}}$?

- $\left( 2, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  3\right)$
- $\left( -2, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  1\right)$

## Explanation

The exponent is nonpositive and is zero only at $2$. The exponential is increasing, so the maximum is one.
