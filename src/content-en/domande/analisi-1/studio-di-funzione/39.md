A function $f$ is convex and differentiable on $\mathbb{R}$, with $f(1) = 2$ and $f'(1) = 3$. Which inequality holds for every function with these properties?

- $f(3) \geq 8$
- $f(3) \leq 8$
- $f(3) = 8$
- $f(3) \geq 11$

## Explanation

The tangent line to the graph at $x = 1$ is $y = 2 + 3(x - 1)$. Since $f$ is convex and differentiable, by the theorem on the **position relative to the tangent** the graph lies above each of its tangent lines, that is, $f(x) \geq 2 + 3(x - 1)$ for every $x$. For $x = 3$ this gives $f(3) \geq 2 + 6 = 8$. Equality holds for the convex function $f(x) = 3x - 1$, while for $f(x) = 3x - 1 + (x - 1)^2$ we get $f(3) = 12$; therefore neither $f(3) \leq 8$ nor $f(3) = 8$ nor $f(3) \geq 11$ holds for every function.
