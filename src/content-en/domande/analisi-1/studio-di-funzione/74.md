What is the maximal interval of convexity containing the minimum of $\ln{\left(\dfrac{\left(x - 3\right)^{2}}{25} + 1 \right)}$?

- $\left[-2, 8\right]$
- $\left[3, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-5, 5\right]$
- $\left(-2, 8\right)$

## Explanation

The minimum is at $3$. The second derivative has the sign of $25 - \left(x - 3\right)^{2}$, which is nonnegative from $-2$ to $8$.
