What is the abscissa of the absolute maximum on $\mathbb R$ of $\left(x - 4\right) e^{28 - 7 x}$?

- $11$
- $\dfrac{27}{7}$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{29}{7}$

## Explanation

The derivative is a positive exponential times $29 - 7 x$. The latter changes from positive to negative at $\dfrac{29}{7}$.
