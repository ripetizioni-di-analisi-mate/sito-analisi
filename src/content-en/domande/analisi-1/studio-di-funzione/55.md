What is the pair (inflection abscissa, tangent slope) for $- 3 x + \left(x - 2\right)^{3} + 11$?

- $\left( -2, \  -3\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  -3\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  3\right)$

## Explanation

The second derivative $6 \left(x - 2\right)$ changes sign at $2$. The first derivative at that point is $-3$.
