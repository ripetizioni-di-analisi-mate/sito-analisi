Which line does the graph of $f(x) = 3 + 2\arctan x$ approach as $x \to -\infty$?

- $y = 3 + \pi$
- $y = 3 - \dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $y = 3$
- $y = 3 - \pi$

## Explanation

As $x \to -\infty$ we have $\arctan x \to -\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, so
$$
\lim_{x \to -\infty} \bigl(3 + 2\arctan x\bigr) = 3 + 2\left(-\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = 3 - \pi .
$$
The limit is finite, therefore the line $y = 3 - \pi$ is a **horizontal asymptote** as $x \to -\infty$. As $x \to +\infty$ the horizontal asymptote is instead $y = 3 + \pi$.
