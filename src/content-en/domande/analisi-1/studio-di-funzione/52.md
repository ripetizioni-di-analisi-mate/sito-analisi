What is the absolute minimum value of $\left(x - 2\right)^{4} - 3 \left(x - 2\right)^{2}$?

- $-9$
- $- \dfrac{9}{4}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$

## Explanation

Setting $t=(x-a)^2$ with $a=2$, the problem becomes minimizing $t^{2} - 3 t$ for $t\geq0$. Its vertex is at $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
