What is the abscissa of the global maximum on $\mathbb R$ of $\left(x - 3\right) e^{15 - 5 x}$?

- $\dfrac{16}{5}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{14}{5}$
- $8$

## Explanation

The derivative is a positive exponential times $16 - 5 x$. The latter changes from positive to negative at $\dfrac{16}{5}$.
