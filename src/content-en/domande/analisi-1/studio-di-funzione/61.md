What is the range on $\mathbb R$ of $\dfrac{x^{2}}{x^{2} + 3}$?

- $\left[0, \infty\right)$
- $\left[0, 1\right)$
- $\left(0, 1\right)$
- $\left[0, 1\right]$

## Explanation

The minimum is zero, attained at zero. Writing $\dfrac{x^{2}}{x^{2} + 3}$ as $1 - \dfrac{3}{x^{2} + 3}$ shows that one is approached as the magnitude of $x$ grows.
