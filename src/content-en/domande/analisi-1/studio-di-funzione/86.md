What is the pair (assigned value, slope) of the continuous extension at $3$ of $\dfrac{x^{2} - 9}{x - 3}$?

- $\left( 0, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 6, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 6, \  0\right)$

## Explanation

For $x\ne 3$ cancel the common factor. The line $x + 3$ gives the value and slope of the extension.
