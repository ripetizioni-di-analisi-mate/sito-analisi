On which maximal interval is the function $f(x) = x + 2\cos x$, considered on $[0, 2\pi]$, decreasing?

- $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{3}, \dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac{7\pi}{6}, \dfrac{11\pi}{6}\right]$
- $\left[0, \dfrac{\pi}{6}\right]$ and $\left[\dfrac{5\pi}{6}, 2\pi\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{5\pi}{6}\right]$

## Explanation

The derivative is $f'(x) = 1 - 2\sin x$, which is negative when $\sin x > \dfrac{1}{2}$. On $[0, 2\pi]$ this inequality is satisfied for $\dfrac{\pi}{6} < x < \dfrac{5\pi}{6}$. By the **monotonicity test**, $f$ is decreasing on $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{5\pi}{6}\right]$ and increasing on $\left[0, \dfrac{\pi}{6}\right]$ and on $\left[\dfrac{5\pi}{6}, 2\pi\right]$.
