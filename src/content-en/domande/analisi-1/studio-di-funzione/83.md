What are the abscissas of the absolute maximum and minimum, in that order, for $x e^{- 3 x^{2}}$?

- $\left( - \dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{6}, \  \dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{6}\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{1}{6}, \  - \dfrac{1}{6}\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}, \  - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{6}, \  - \dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{6}\right)$

## Explanation

The derivative has the sign of $1 - 6 x^{2}$ and the function tends to zero at both ends. The positive extremum is the maximum.
