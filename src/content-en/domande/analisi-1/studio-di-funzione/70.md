What is the abscissa of the global maximum on $\mathbb R$ of $\left(x - 2\right) e^{6 - 3 x}$?

- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The derivative is a positive exponential times $7 - 3 x$. The latter changes from positive to negative at $\dfrac{7}{3}$.
