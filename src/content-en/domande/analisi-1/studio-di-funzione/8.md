For the function $f(x) = \arctan x - \dfrac{x}{2}$, what is the value of the relative maximum?

- $\dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4} - 1$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4} - \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2} - \dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The derivative is
$$
f'(x) = \frac{1}{1 + x^2} - \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1 - x^2}{2(1 + x^2)} ,
$$
which is positive for $-1 < x < 1$ and negative for $|x| > 1$. Therefore, by the **first derivative test**, $x = 1$ is a relative maximum point and $x = -1$ is a relative minimum point. The relative maximum is $f(1) = \arctan 1 - \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{\pi}{4} - \dfrac{1}{2}$. Since the function is odd, the relative minimum is $f(-1) = \dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
