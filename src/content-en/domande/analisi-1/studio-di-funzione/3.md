For the function $f(x) = x^2 e^{x}$, which point is a relative maximum point?

- $x = -2$
- $x = 0$
- $x = 2$
- $x = -2 - \sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

By the product rule, $f'(x) = 2x\,e^{x} + x^2 e^{x} = x(x + 2)\,e^{x}$. Since $e^{x} > 0$, the sign of $f'$ is that of $x(x + 2)$, which is positive for $x < -2$ and for $x > 0$, and negative for $-2 < x < 0$. Therefore $f$ increases to the left of $-2$ and decreases to the right, and by the **first derivative test** $x = -2$ is a relative maximum point, with $f(-2) = \dfrac{4}{e^2}$. The point $x = 0$ is instead a relative minimum point, because at $0$ the derivative changes from negative to positive.
