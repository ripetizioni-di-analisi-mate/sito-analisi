On every interval $[2^n,2^{n+1}]$, where $n=0,1,\ldots$, a continuous nonnegative function has integral $3^{-n}$. What is its integral over the entire half-line $[1,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac32$
- $3$
- $+\infty$, because the interval of integration is unbounded.

## Explanation

By additivity the integral up to $2^{N+1}$ is the sum of the first $N+1$ terms of a geometric progression with ratio $\dfrac{1}{3}$. The areas are nonnegative, so the limit along these endpoints gives the total integral, equal to $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
