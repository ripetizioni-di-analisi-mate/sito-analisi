What is the exact set of real values of $A$ for which $\int_{1}^{\infty}x^{- A} \ln{\left(1 + \dfrac{1}{x^{2}} \right)}\,dx$ converges?

- $\left(-1, \infty\right)$
- $\left(3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -1\right)$
- $\left[-1, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

At infinity the logarithm is asymptotically equivalent to $\dfrac{1}{x^{2}}$. The resulting power is integrable if $A+2>1$.
