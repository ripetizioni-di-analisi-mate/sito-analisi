What is $\int_{0}^{1}- x^{3} \ln{\left(x \right)}\,dx$?

- $- \dfrac{1}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{25}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$

## Explanation

Integration by parts gives a zero boundary contribution because $x^4\ln x$ tends to zero at zero. What remains is $\dfrac{1}{4}$ times the integral of $x^{3}$.
