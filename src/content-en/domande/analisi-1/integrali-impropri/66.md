What is the exact set of real values of $A$ for which $\int_{0}^{1}x^{- A} \left(1 - \cos{\left(4 x \right)}\right)\,dx$ converges?

- $\left(3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 2\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right]$

## Explanation

The numerator is asymptotic to $8 x^{2}$. Apply the power test at zero to exponent $2-A$, which must exceed $-1$.
