- $\alpha \geq -1$
- $\alpha > -1$
- $\alpha > 1$
- $\alpha < -1$

## Explanation

For $x \geq 1$ the integrand is positive for every $\alpha$, and the only critical point is $+\infty$. As $x \to +\infty$ one has $1 + x^{2} \sim x^{2}$, so the integrand is asymptotic to $\dfrac{1}{x^{\alpha + 2}}$. The benchmark integral $\displaystyle\int_1^{+\infty} \frac{dx}{x^{\alpha + 2}}$ converges if and only if $\alpha + 2 > 1$, that is $\alpha > -1$. Therefore, by the **limit comparison test**, the given integral **converges if and only if** $\alpha > -1$.
