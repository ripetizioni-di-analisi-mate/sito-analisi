- It is indeterminate, because $\cos x$ has no limit as $x \to +\infty$
- Converges, because $\left| \dfrac{\cos x}{x^{3}} \right| \leq \dfrac{1}{x^{3}}$ and the benchmark integral with $\alpha = 3$ converges
- Its behavior cannot be determined with the comparison tests, because the integrand changes sign
- Diverges, because $\displaystyle\int_1^{+\infty} \cos x\,dx$ does not converge

## Explanation

The integrand changes sign, so the comparison tests are applied to its absolute value. Since $|\cos x| \leq 1$, for $x \geq 1$ one has $\left| \dfrac{\cos x}{x^{3}} \right| \leq \dfrac{1}{x^{3}}$. The benchmark integral $\displaystyle\int_1^{+\infty} \frac{dx}{x^{3}}$ converges, since $\alpha = 3 > 1$, therefore by the **comparison test** the integral of the absolute value converges. Hence the given integral converges absolutely and, since absolute convergence implies convergence, it **converges**.
