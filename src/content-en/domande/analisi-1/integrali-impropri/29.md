Determine the behavior of
$$
\int_1^{+\infty} \frac{\arctan x}{x^{2}}\,dx .
$$

- Diverges, because $\arctan x$ does not tend to zero as $x \to +\infty$
- Diverges, because the integrand is asymptotically equivalent to $\dfrac{1}{x}$
- Converges, because for $x \geq 1$ the integrand lies between $0$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{2x^{2}}$
- Diverges, because for $x \geq 1$ the integrand is less than $\dfrac{\pi}{2x}$

## Explanation

For $x \geq 1$ one has $\dfrac{\pi}{4} \leq \arctan x < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, so $0 \leq \dfrac{\arctan x}{x^{2}} \leq \dfrac{\pi}{2x^{2}}$. The integral of $\dfrac{\pi}{2x^{2}}$ over $[1, +\infty)$ converges, because it is the comparison integral with $\alpha = 2$ multiplied by the constant $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Therefore the given integral **converges** by the **comparison test**. The equivalence $\arctan x \sim x$ holds as $x \to 0$, while as $x \to +\infty$ the function $\arctan x$ tends to $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
