What is $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{x^{2} + 16}\,dx$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{8}$
- $\pi$
- $- \dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

An antiderivative is $\dfrac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{4} \right)}}{4}$. The arctangent approaches opposite values of magnitude $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ at the two endpoints.
