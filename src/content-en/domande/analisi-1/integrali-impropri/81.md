Give the set of real parameters $\alpha$ for which
$$
\int_0^{1} \frac{dx}{x^{\alpha} + x^{2\alpha}}
$$
is a convergent integral.

- $\alpha \leq 1$
- $\alpha > 0$
- $\alpha > 1$
- $\alpha < 1$

## Explanation

For $\alpha = 0$ the integrand equals $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and the integral is ordinary. For $\alpha < 0$ both powers tend to $+\infty$ as $x \to 0^{+}$, so the integrand tends to $0$, is bounded and the integral is ordinary. For $\alpha > 0$ we have $\dfrac{x^{2\alpha}}{x^{\alpha}} = x^{\alpha} \to 0$, so $x^{\alpha} + x^{2\alpha} \sim x^{\alpha}$ and the integrand is asymptotic to $\dfrac{1}{x^{\alpha}}$, with convergent integral if and only if $\alpha < 1$. Therefore the integral **converges** if and only if $\alpha < 1$.
