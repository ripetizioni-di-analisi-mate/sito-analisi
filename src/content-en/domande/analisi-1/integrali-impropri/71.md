What is the exact set of real values of $A$ for which $\int_{0}^{1}x^{- A} \left(- 4 x + e^{4 x} - 1\right)\,dx$ converges?

- $\left(-\infty, 3\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right)$
- $\left(3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 1\right)$

## Explanation

The constant and linear terms cancel. The numerator is asymptotic to $8 x^{2}$, so $2-A>-1$ is required.
