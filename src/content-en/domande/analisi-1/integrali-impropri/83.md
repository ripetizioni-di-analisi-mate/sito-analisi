What is the exact set of real values of $A$ for which $\int_{1}^{\infty}x^{A} \left(1 - \cos{\left(\dfrac{3}{x} \right)}\right)\,dx$ converges?

- $\left(-\infty, 1\right]$
- $\left(1, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 1\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 2\right)$

## Explanation

For large $x$, $1-\cos(3/x)$ is asymptotic to $\dfrac{9}{2 x^{2}}$. The resulting power must have exponent less than $-1$.
