Determine for which $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$ the following integral converges:
$$
\int_0^{+\infty} \frac{dx}{x^{\alpha}} .
$$

- $\alpha > 1$
- $\alpha < 1$
- $0 < \alpha < 1$
- For no value of $\alpha$

## Explanation

The integral is split at the point $1$ into the integrals $\displaystyle\int_0^{1} \frac{dx}{x^{\alpha}}$ and $\displaystyle\int_1^{+\infty} \frac{dx}{x^{\alpha}}$. By the theorem on comparison integrals the first converges if and only if $\alpha < 1$, the second if and only if $\alpha > 1$. The two conditions are never satisfied together, so for every $\alpha$ at least one of the two pieces does not converge. Therefore the integral **converges for no value** of $\alpha$.
