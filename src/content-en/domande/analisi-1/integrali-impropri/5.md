To approximate $\int_0^1(1-x)^{-\dfrac{1}{2}}\,dx$, stop at $1-\delta$ with $0<\delta<1$. Which value of $\delta$ leaves an omitted area of $\dfrac{1}{10}$?

- $\dfrac1{400}$
- $\dfrac{1}{100}$, because the omitted integral would be $\sqrt{\delta}$.
- $\dfrac1{20}$
- $\dfrac1{10}$

## Explanation

With $u=1-x$ the omitted area is $\int_0^\delta u^{-\dfrac{1}{2}}\,du=2\sqrt{\delta}$. Setting $2\sqrt{\delta}=\dfrac{1}{10}$ gives $\delta=\dfrac{1}{400}$.
