What is the exact set of real values of $A$ for which $\int_{0}^{1}x^{- A} \left(- 3 x + e^{3 x} - 1\right)\,dx$ converges?

- $\left(-\infty, 3\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 1\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right)$
- $\left(3, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The constant and linear terms cancel. The numerator is asymptotically equivalent to $\dfrac{9 x^{2}}{2}$, so $2-A>-1$ is required.
