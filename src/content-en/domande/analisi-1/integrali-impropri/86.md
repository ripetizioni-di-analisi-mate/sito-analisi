What is the exact set of real values of $A$ for which $\int_{0}^{1}\dfrac{x^{A}}{\sqrt[3]{1 - x}}\,dx$ converges?

- $\left(-1, \infty\right)$
- $\left(0, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-1, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -1\right)$

## Explanation

Near one the exponent $- \dfrac{1}{3}$ exceeds $-1$, so the integral converges there. At zero the critical factor is $x^A$, requiring $A>-1$.
