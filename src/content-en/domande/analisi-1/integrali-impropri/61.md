What is the exact set of real values of $A$ for which $\int_{0}^{\infty}\dfrac{x^{A}}{x^{3} + 1}\,dx$ converges?

- $\left(-\infty, 2\right)$
- $\left(-1, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-1, 2\right)$
- $\left[-1, 2\right]$

## Explanation

Near zero the integrand is asymptotic to $x^A$, and at infinity to $x^{A-3}$. The power tests require $A>-1$ and $A<2$.
