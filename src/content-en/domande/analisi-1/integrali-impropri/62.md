What is the exact set of real values of $A$ for which $\int_{0}^{\infty}\dfrac{x^{A}}{x^{4} + 1}\,dx$ converges?

- $\left(-1, 3\right)$
- $\left[-1, 3\right]$
- $\left(-1, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right)$

## Explanation

Near zero the integrand is asymptotic to $x^A$, and at infinity to $x^{A-4}$. The power tests require $A>-1$ and $A<3$.
