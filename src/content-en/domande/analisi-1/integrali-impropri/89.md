What is $\int_{0}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{\left(x + 4\right)^{3}}\,dx$?

- $\dfrac{1}{32}$
- $\dfrac{1}{192}$
- $\dfrac{1}{128}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Substitute $u=x+4$ and integrate $\dfrac{1}{u^{3}}$ from $4$ to infinity. The upper contribution from the antiderivative is zero.
