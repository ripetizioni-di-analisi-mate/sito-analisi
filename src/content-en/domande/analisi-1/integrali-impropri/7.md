## Explanation

For $x \geq 1$ the integrand is continuous and positive, so the only critical point is $+\infty$. As $x \to +\infty$ the numerator is asymptotic to $x^{2}$ and the denominator to $x^{4}$, so the integrand is asymptotic to $\dfrac{1}{x^{2}}$. The benchmark integral $\displaystyle\int_1^{+\infty} \frac{dx}{x^{2}}$ converges, since $\alpha = 2 > 1$, therefore the given integral **converges** by the **limit comparison test**.
