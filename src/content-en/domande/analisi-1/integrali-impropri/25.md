Which of the following integrals converges?

- $\displaystyle\int_0^{1} \frac{dx}{x^{2}}$
- $\displaystyle\int_1^{+\infty} \frac{dx}{\sqrt{x}}$
- $\displaystyle\int_0^{1} \frac{dx}{x^{\frac{3}{4}}}$
- $\displaystyle\int_1^{+\infty} \frac{dx}{x}$

## Explanation

By the theorem on benchmark integrals, $\displaystyle\int_0^{1} \frac{dx}{x^{\alpha}}$ converges if and only if $\alpha < 1$, while $\displaystyle\int_1^{+\infty} \frac{dx}{x^{\alpha}}$ converges if and only if $\alpha > 1$. On $(0, 1]$ the exponent $\dfrac{3}{4}$ is less than $1$, therefore $\displaystyle\int_0^{1} \frac{dx}{x^{\frac{3}{4}}}$ **converges**; the exponent $2$ instead gives a divergent integral. On $[1, +\infty)$ the exponents $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $1$ do not exceed $1$, hence both integrals diverge.
