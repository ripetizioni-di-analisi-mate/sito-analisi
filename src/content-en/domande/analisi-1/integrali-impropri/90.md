What is $\int_{0}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{\left(x + 5\right)^{4}}\,dx$?

- $\dfrac{1}{375}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2500}$
- $\dfrac{1}{1875}$

## Explanation

Substitute $u=x+5$ and integrate $\dfrac{1}{u^{4}}$ from $5$ to infinity. The upper contribution from the antiderivative is zero.
