What is $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{x^{2} + 9}\,dx$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $\pi$
- $- \dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

An antiderivative is $\dfrac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{3} \right)}}{3}$. The arctangent approaches opposite values of magnitude $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ at the two endpoints.
