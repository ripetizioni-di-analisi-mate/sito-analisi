What is $\int_{0}^{1}- x \ln{\left(x \right)}\,dx$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

Integration by parts gives a zero boundary contribution because $x^2\ln x$ tends to zero at zero. What remains is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ times the integral of $x$.
