What is the exact set of real values of $A$ for which $\int_{1}^{\infty}x^{A} \left(1 - \cos{\left(\dfrac{2}{x} \right)}\right)\,dx$ converges?

- $\left(-\infty, 1\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 1\right]$
- $\left(1, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 2\right)$

## Explanation

For large $x$, $1-\cos(2/x)$ is asymptotically equivalent to $\dfrac{2}{x^{2}}$. The resulting power must have exponent less than $-1$.
