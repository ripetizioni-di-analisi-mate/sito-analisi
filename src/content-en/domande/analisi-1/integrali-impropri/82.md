What is the area of the region between the graph of $y = e^{-|x|}$ and the $x$-axis? The area is given by
$$
\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} e^{-|x|}\,dx .
$$

::grafico{f="exp(-abs(x))" area="exp(-abs(x));0;-6;6" xmin=-6 xmax=6 ymin=-0.2 ymax=1.3 titolo="The graph of $y = e^{-|x|}$ is symmetric with respect to the $y$-axis"}

- $1$
- $0$
- $2$
- The integral diverges

## Explanation

The singular points are $-\infty$ and $+\infty$, so the integral is split at $0$. For $x \geq 0$ we have $e^{-|x|} = e^{-x}$ and
$$
\int_0^{t} e^{-x}\,dx = 1 - e^{-t} \to 1 \qquad (t \to +\infty) .
$$
The integrand is even, therefore the integral over $(-\infty, 0]$ also converges and equals $1$. Both pieces converge, so the integral **converges** and equals $1 + 1 = 2$.
