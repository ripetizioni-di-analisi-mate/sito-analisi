What is $\int_{0}^{1}- x^{2} \ln{\left(x \right)}\,dx$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

Integration by parts gives a zero boundary contribution because $x^3\ln x$ tends to zero at zero. What remains is $\dfrac{1}{3}$ times the integral of $x^{2}$.
