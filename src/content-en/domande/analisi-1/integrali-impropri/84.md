How many singular points does the integral
$$
\int_0^{+\infty} \frac{dx}{x^{2} - 3x + 2}
$$
have?

- $2$
- $1$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

From the factorization $x^{2} - 3x + 2 = (x - 1)\,(x - 2)$ it follows that the integrand is unbounded near $1$ and near $2$, so these two interior points are singular. At $0$ the integrand equals $\dfrac{1}{2}$, therefore $0$ is not a singular point. The endpoint $+\infty$ is infinite, so it is a singular point. The singular points are therefore **three**, namely $1$, $2$ and $+\infty$.
