What is the exact set of real values of $A$ for which $\int_{1}^{\infty}x^{- A} \ln{\left(1 + \dfrac{1}{x^{4}} \right)}\,dx$ converges?

- $\left[-3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(5, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -3\right)$

## Explanation

At infinity the logarithm is asymptotic to $\dfrac{1}{x^{4}}$. The resulting power is integrable if $A+4>1$.
