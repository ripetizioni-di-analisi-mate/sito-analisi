What is the exact set of real values of $A$ for which $\int_{e^{5}}^{\infty}\dfrac{\ln{\left(x \right)}^{- A}}{x}\,dx$ converges?

- $\left[1, \infty\right)$
- $\left(0, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 1\right)$
- $\left(1, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

With $u=\ln x$ the integrand becomes $u^{-A}$. The power is integrable at infinity for $A>1$.
