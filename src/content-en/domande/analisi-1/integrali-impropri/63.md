What is the exact set of real values of $A$ for which $\int_{0}^{\infty}\dfrac{x^{A}}{x^{5} + 1}\,dx$ converges?

- $\left(-1, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 4\right)$
- $\left[-1, 4\right]$
- $\left(-1, 4\right)$

## Explanation

Near zero the integrand is asymptotically equivalent to $x^A$. At infinity it is asymptotically equivalent to $x^{A-5}$. The power tests require $A>-1$ and $A<4$.
