## Explanation

The integrand is unbounded near $0$, so the critical point is $0$. An antiderivative of $x^{-\frac{2}{3}}$ is $3\,x^{\frac{1}{3}}$, therefore
$$
\int_t^{1} \frac{dx}{x^{\frac{2}{3}}} = 3 - 3\,t^{\frac{1}{3}} .
$$
Since $t^{\frac{1}{3}} \to 0$ as $t \to 0^{+}$, the integral **converges** and equals $3$.
