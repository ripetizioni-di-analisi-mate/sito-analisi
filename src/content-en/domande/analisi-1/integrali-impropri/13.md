- $\alpha < 2$
- $\alpha < 1$
- $\alpha \leq 2$
- $\alpha > 2$

## Explanation

For $x \in (0, 1]$ the integrand is positive for every $\alpha$. By the standard limit of the exponential, $e^{x} - 1 \sim x$ as $x \to 0$, so the integrand is asymptotic to $\dfrac{x}{x^{\alpha}} = \dfrac{1}{x^{\alpha - 1}}$. The benchmark integral $\displaystyle\int_0^{1} \frac{dx}{x^{\alpha - 1}}$ converges if and only if $\alpha - 1 < 1$, that is $\alpha < 2$. Therefore, by the **limit comparison test**, the given integral **converges if and only if** $\alpha < 2$.
