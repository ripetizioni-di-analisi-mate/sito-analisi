## Explanation

The integrand is continuous on $[2, +\infty)$, so the only critical point is $+\infty$. An antiderivative of $x^{-4}$ is $-\dfrac{1}{3x^{3}}$, therefore
$$
\int_2^{t} \frac{dx}{x^{4}} = \frac{1}{24} - \frac{1}{3t^{3}} .
$$
As $t \to +\infty$ the term $\dfrac{1}{3t^{3}}$ tends to $0$, hence the integral **converges** and equals $\dfrac{1}{24}$.
