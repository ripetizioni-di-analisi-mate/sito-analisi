- $3$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

An antiderivative of $e^{-3x}$ is $-\dfrac{e^{-3x}}{3}$, so
$$
\int_0^{t} e^{-3x}\,dx = \frac{1}{3} - \frac{e^{-3t}}{3} .
$$
Since $e^{-3t} \to 0$ as $t \to +\infty$, the integral **converges** and equals $\dfrac{1}{3}$.
