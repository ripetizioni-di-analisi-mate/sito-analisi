A device reports positions at unit time intervals. Suppose $s_0=0$, $s_1=3$ and $s_{n+2}-2s_{n+1}+s_n=2$. What is the displacement $s_4-s_3$?

- $8$
- $6$
- $12$
- $9$

## Explanation

Set $d_n=s_{n+1}-s_n$. The relation becomes $d_{n+1}-d_n=2$ with $d_0=3$. The displacements over the first four intervals are thus $3,5,7,9$, and the required one is $9$.
