The power $(-3)^n$, with negative base, appears in $a_n = \dfrac{(-3)^n + 2^n}{3^n}$. What is the behavior of the sequence?

- It converges to $0$
- It converges to $1$
- It diverges to $+\infty$
- It has no limit

## Explanation

Dividing each term by $3^n$ we obtain
$$
a_n = (-1)^n + \left( \frac{2}{3} \right)^{n} .
$$
The second term is a geometric sequence with ratio $\dfrac{2}{3}$, so it tends to $0$. Therefore the terms of even index tend to $1$ and those of odd index to $-1$, and the sequence **has no limit**.
