For $a_n=\dfrac{(-1)^n}{n+1}$ with $n\geq1$, what is the smallest closed interval containing every term?

- $\left[-\dfrac12,\dfrac13\right]$
- $\left[-\dfrac12,\dfrac12\right]$
- $\left[-\dfrac13,\dfrac12\right]$
- $\left[0,\dfrac12\right]$

## Explanation

The absolute values decrease with the index. The lowest negative term is therefore the first odd one, $a_1=-\dfrac12$, and the largest positive term is the first even one, $a_2=\dfrac13$. Both endpoints are attained and no narrower interval can contain them.
