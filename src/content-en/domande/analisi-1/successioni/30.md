An approximation satisfies $a_n=L+\dfrac6n+\dfrac4{n^2}$. What is the signed error $(2a_{2n}-a_n)-L$?

- $\dfrac2{n^2}$
- $\dfrac6n$
- $-\dfrac4n$
- $-\dfrac2{n^2}$

## Explanation

Substituting $2n$ gives $a_{2n}=L+\dfrac3n+\dfrac1{n^2}$. Doubling and subtracting $a_n$ cancels the terms of order $\dfrac{1}{n}$. The result is $L-\dfrac2{n^2}$, with a negative error.
