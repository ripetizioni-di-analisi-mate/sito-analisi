For $n\geq1$, write $\ln(n+1)-\ln n=\int_n^{n+1}\dfrac{1}{t}\,dt$. Which lower and upper bounds come from the two rectangles of width one?

- $\dfrac1n\leq\ln(n+1)-\ln n\leq\dfrac1{n-1}$ for $n>1$
- $\dfrac1{n+1}\leq\ln(n+1)-\ln n\leq\dfrac1n$
- $n\leq\ln(n+1)-\ln n\leq n+1$
- $1\leq\ln(n+1)-\ln n\leq n$

## Explanation

The function $\dfrac{1}{t}$ decreases for $t>0$. Its minimum on the integration interval is $\dfrac{1}{n+1}$ and its maximum is $\dfrac{1}{n}$. Multiplying these heights by the unit width bounds the area under the graph.
