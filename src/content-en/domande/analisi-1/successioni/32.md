A sequence starts at $a_1=0$ and satisfies $a_{n+1}=a_n+\dfrac{1}{n(n+1)}$. What value does it approach?

- $0$
- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $+\infty$

## Explanation

The increment is $\dfrac1n-\dfrac1{n+1}$. Adding the increments from the first through index $n-1$ cancels every intermediate term and leaves $a_n=1-\dfrac1n$. The limit is $1$.
