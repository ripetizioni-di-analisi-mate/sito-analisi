Suppose $b_n\ne0$ and $\dfrac1{b_n}\to0$. Which property is necessary even when the sign of $b_n$ changes?

- $b_n\to-\infty$
- $b_n$ is eventually positive.
- $|b_n|\to+\infty$
- $b_n\to+\infty$

## Explanation

Given $M>0$, eventually $|\dfrac{1}{b_n}|<\dfrac{1}{M}$. Taking reciprocals of the positive quantities gives $|b_n|>M$. The sign is unrestricted, as illustrated by $b_n=(-1)^n n$.
