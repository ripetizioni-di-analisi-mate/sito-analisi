Let $a_n=\dfrac{2^n n!}{n^n}$ for $n\geq1$. To which number does the ratio $\dfrac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}$ tend, determining the behavior of $a_n$?

- $\dfrac2e$
- $2e$
- $\dfrac1e$
- $2$

## Explanation

The factor $n+1$ from the factorial cancels one of the $n+1$ factors in the denominator. This leaves $2\left(\dfrac{n}{n+1}\right)^n=\dfrac{2}{(1+\frac{1}{n})^n}$. The ratio tends to $\dfrac2e<1$, so the positive sequence tends to zero.
