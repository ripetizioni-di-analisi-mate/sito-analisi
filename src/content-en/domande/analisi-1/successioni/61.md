An oscillator is sampled at $a_n=\cos(\dfrac{n\pi}{3})$ for $n\geq0$. After how many steps does the complete pattern of values first repeat?

- $3$
- $6$
- $2$
- $12$

## Explanation

For a positive integer period $p$, the value $a_0=1$ must first return. This requires $\cos(\dfrac{p\pi}{3})=1$, so $\dfrac{p\pi}{3}$ must be a multiple of $2\pi$. The smallest choice is $p=6$, and cosine's periodicity then repeats every term.
