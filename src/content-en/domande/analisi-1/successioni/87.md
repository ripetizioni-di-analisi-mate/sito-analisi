For $n\geq1$ let $a_{n+2}=a_{n+1}+a_n$. Only $a_1=1$ is given. Which additional piece of information uniquely determines the entire sequence?

- The sequence is increasing.
- All terms are positive.
- The terms are integers.
- The value $a_3=5$.

## Explanation

At $n=1$ the rule says $a_3=a_2+a_1$. Knowing $a_3=5$ gives $a_2=4$, fixing the two initial values needed for the recurrence. The qualitative conditions allow several choices for $a_2$.
