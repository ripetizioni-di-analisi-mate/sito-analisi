Evaluate $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to+\infty} n(e^{\frac{3}{n}}-1)$.

- $3$
- $e^3$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

Set $t=\dfrac3n$, so $t\to0$ and $n=\dfrac3t$. The product becomes $3\dfrac{e^t-1}{t}$. The ratio tends to the derivative of the exponential at zero, which is one, leaving $3$.
