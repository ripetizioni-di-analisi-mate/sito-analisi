Two calculations assign the same sequence the limits $2$ and $5$. What is the largest common radius $\varepsilon$ for which the open intervals centered at $2$ and $5$ are disjoint?

- $3$
- $\dfrac72$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

We need $2+\varepsilon\leq5-\varepsilon$. Equality is allowed because the touching point belongs to neither open interval. Thus $2\varepsilon\leq3$ and the largest radius is $\dfrac32$. A tail of the sequence could not lie in both intervals.
