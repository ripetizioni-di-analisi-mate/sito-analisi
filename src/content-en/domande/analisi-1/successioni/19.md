The sequence $a_n = \sqrt{n + \sqrt{n}} - \sqrt{n}$ contains a square root inside another square root. What is its limit?

- $0$
- $1$
- $+\infty$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The difference is in the form $+\infty - \infty$. Multiplying and dividing by $\sqrt{n + \sqrt{n}} + \sqrt{n}$, the numerator becomes $(n + \sqrt{n}) - n = \sqrt{n}$, so
$$
a_n = \frac{\sqrt{n}}{\sqrt{n + \sqrt{n}} + \sqrt{n}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 + \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{n}}} + 1} ,
$$
where in the last step the numerator and the denominator were divided by $\sqrt{n}$. Since $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \to 0$, the limit equals $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
