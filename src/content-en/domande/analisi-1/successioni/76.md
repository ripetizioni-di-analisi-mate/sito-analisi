Let $u_n=\dfrac1n$ and $v_n=\dfrac1n+\dfrac1{n^2}$. The two readings approach each other. What does the difference of their reciprocals $\dfrac1{u_n}-\dfrac1{v_n}$ approach?

- $+\infty$
- $-1$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

The second reciprocal is $\dfrac{n^2}{n+1}$. Subtracting it from $n$ over a common denominator gives $\dfrac{n}{n+1}\to1$. The small difference in the original readings is amplified because both readings approach zero.
