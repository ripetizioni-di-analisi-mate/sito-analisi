A plot of a sequence shows $a_2=5$ and $a_3=1$. A student joins the points and reads off $3$ at the horizontal coordinate $2.5$. What does this $3$ represent?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=4 ymin=0 ymax=6 punti="2,5,pieno;3,1,pieno" linee="2,5 3,1" nomi="n,a" titolo="Two terms joined by the segment drawn by the student"}

- The signed change from $a_2$ to $a_3$.
- A value of the linear interpolation.
- The limit of the even-indexed terms.
- An additional term of the sequence.

## Explanation

The indices of the sequence are integers. The intermediate point belongs to the segment added to the plot, so it describes an extension chosen by the student. The two original data points give a signed change of $1-5=-4$.
