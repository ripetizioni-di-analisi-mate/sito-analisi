An algorithm produces a time $t_n$ satisfying $t_n\geq n-\sqrt n$ whenever $n\geq4$. What follows even without an upper bound?

- $t_n\to+\infty$
- $\dfrac{t_n}{n}\to1$
- $t_n$ is increasing.
- $t_n-n\to0$

## Explanation

For $n\geq4$ we have $\sqrt n\leq \dfrac{n}{2}$, so $t_n\geq \dfrac{n}{2}$. The right-hand side eventually exceeds every threshold, and so does $t_n$. The bound leaves both the growth rate and the ordering of consecutive terms undetermined.
