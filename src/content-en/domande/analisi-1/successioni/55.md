The base and the exponent of $\left( \dfrac{n^2 + n}{n^2 + 4} \right)^{3n}$ contain polynomials in $n$. What is the value of the limit?

- $e$
- $1$
- $e^3$
- $+\infty$

## Explanation

The base tends to $1$ and, for $n > 4$, it can be written as
$$
\frac{n^2 + n}{n^2 + 4} = 1 + \frac{n - 4}{n^2 + 4} = 1 + \frac{1}{a_n}, \qquad a_n = \frac{n^2 + 4}{n - 4} \to +\infty .
$$
Then $\left( 1 + \dfrac{1}{a_n} \right)^{a_n} \to e$. The exponent is $3n = a_n \cdot \dfrac{3n}{a_n}$, and $\dfrac{3n}{a_n} = \dfrac{3n(n - 4)}{n^2 + 4} \to 3$. Therefore the sequence tends to $e^3$.
