Suppose $a_1=-12$, $a_2=4$ and $|a_n-3|<1$ for $n\geq3$. What is the smallest $K$ for which the data guarantee $|a_n|\leq K$ at every index?

- $3$
- $12$
- $4$
- $16$

## Explanation

The tail terms lie between $2$ and $4$, so their absolute values are less than $4$. The larger absolute value among the first two terms is $12$. This bound covers every term and is already necessary for $a_1$.
