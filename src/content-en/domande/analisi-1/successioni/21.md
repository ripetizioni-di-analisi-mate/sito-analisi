We know that $b_n\to3$. To divide by $b_n$ along the tail, we want to guarantee $b_n>2$. Which condition on the distance from $3$ implies this inequality for every term satisfying that condition?

- $|b_n-3|>1$
- $|b_n-3|<4$
- $|b_n-3|<1$
- $|b_n-3|<3$

## Explanation

The condition $|b_n-3|<1$ is equivalent to $2<b_n<4$ and holds eventually by convergence. Division by $b_n$ is therefore valid along the tail. A radius larger than one would allow values below $2$ as well.
