The numerator of $a_n = \dfrac{\sqrt{4n^2 + 1} + n}{3n - 2}$ contains a square root. What is the limit of the sequence?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$

## Explanation

For $n \geq 1$ we have $\sqrt{4n^2 + 1} = n \sqrt{4 + \dfrac{1}{n^2}}$. Dividing the numerator and the denominator by $n$ we obtain
$$
a_n = \frac{\sqrt{4 + \dfrac{1}{n^2}} + 1}{3 - \dfrac{2}{n}} \to \frac{2 + 1}{3} = 1 .
$$
The square root contributes $2n$ and not $4n$, because $\sqrt{4n^2} = 2n$.
