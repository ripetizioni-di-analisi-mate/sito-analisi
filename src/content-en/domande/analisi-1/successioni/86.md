Two readings start at $u_0=2$ and $v_0=8$. At every step $2u_{n+1}+v_{n+1}=2u_n+v_n$. If the two sequences converge to a common value $L$, what is $L$?

- $12$
- $4$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

The combination $2u_n+v_n$ keeps its initial value $2\cdot2+8=12$. At the limit the same combination becomes $2L+L=3L$. Thus $3L=12$, giving the common value $4$.
