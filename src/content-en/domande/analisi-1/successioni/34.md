Approximations to an unknown value $L$ satisfy $|a_k-L|\leq\dfrac1k$. For $m,n\geq100$, which bound on $|a_m-a_n|$ follows from the triangle inequality?

- $|a_m-a_n|\leq\dfrac1{m+n}$
- $|a_m-a_n|\leq\dfrac1{10000}$
- $a_m=a_n$
- $|a_m-a_n|\leq\dfrac2{100}$

## Explanation

Insert $L$ between the two terms to obtain $|a_m-a_n|\leq|a_m-L|+|a_n-L|$. Each distance is at most $\dfrac1{100}$. The bound measures how far two readings can differ without needing to know $L$.
