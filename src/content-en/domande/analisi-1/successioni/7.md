A sequence satisfies $a_n = 7$ for $n \leq 1000$ and $a_n = \dfrac{2n}{n + 1}$ for $n > 1000$. What is $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to +\infty} a_n$?

- $7$
- $2$
- $1$
- The limit does not exist

## Explanation

The limit depends only on what happens eventually, so the first $1000$ terms, which are finitely many, do not change it.

For $n > 1000$ we have
$$
a_n = \frac{2n}{n + 1} = \frac{2}{1 + \dfrac{1}{n}} ,
$$
and the denominator tends to $1$. Therefore $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to +\infty} a_n = 2$.
