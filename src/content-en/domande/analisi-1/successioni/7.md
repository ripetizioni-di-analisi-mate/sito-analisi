Evaluate $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to+\infty}\sqrt[n]{4^n+5^n}$.

- $5$
- $1$
- $9$
- $\sqrt{20}$

## Explanation

The larger term is $5^n$, so $5^n\leq4^n+5^n\leq2\cdot5^n$. Taking $n$th roots gives bounds of $5$ and $5\sqrt[n]{2}$. Both tend to $5$, so the root between them has the same limit.
