For $n\geq1$, what is $\left\lfloor\sqrt{n^2+2n}\right\rfloor-n$? The floor brackets denote the greatest integer less than or equal to the enclosed number.

- $2$
- $0$
- $-1$
- $1$

## Explanation

We have $n^2\leq n^2+2n<(n+1)^2$, since the expression is exactly one short of the next square. Its square root is therefore at least $n$ and less than $n+1$. Its floor is $n$ and the requested difference is zero.
