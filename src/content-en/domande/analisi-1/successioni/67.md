For $n>1$, find the exponent $p_n$ that makes $(\sqrt[n]{n})^{p_n}=e$ exactly. What is $p_n$?

- $n\ln n$
- $\dfrac{\ln n}{n}$
- $\dfrac1{\ln n}$
- $\dfrac n{\ln n}$

## Explanation

The logarithm of the base is $\dfrac{\ln n}{n}$. Taking logarithms of the equality gives $p_n\dfrac{\ln n}{n}=1$. The logarithm is nonzero for $n>1$, so the unique solution is $\dfrac{n}{\ln n}$.
