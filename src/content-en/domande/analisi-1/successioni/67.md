The logarithm of a power appears in $\dfrac{\ln\left( n^5 \right)}{\sqrt{n}}$. What is the limit?

- $5$
- $+\infty$
- $10$
- $0$

## Explanation

By the properties of logarithms $\ln\left( n^5 \right) = 5 \ln n$, so
$$
\frac{\ln\left( n^5 \right)}{\sqrt{n}} = 5 \cdot \frac{\ln n}{n^{\frac{1}{2}}} .
$$
By the **hierarchy of infinities** $\dfrac{\ln n}{n^{\alpha}} \to 0$ with $\alpha = \dfrac{1}{2}$, so the limit equals $5 \cdot 0 = 0$.
