We want to sample a sequence without repeating or reversing indices. For $k\geq1$, which rule always defines a subsequence?

- $n_k=k+\lfloor \dfrac{k}{2}\rfloor$
- $n_k=3+(-1)^k$
- $n_k=1$
- $n_k=\lceil \dfrac{k}{2}\rceil$

## Explanation

In the rule $k+\lfloor \dfrac{k}{2}\rfloor$, the first term increases by one and the second never decreases. The index therefore increases by at least one at every step. Rounding $\dfrac{k}{2}$ up instead produces repeated indices, such as $1,1,2,2$.
