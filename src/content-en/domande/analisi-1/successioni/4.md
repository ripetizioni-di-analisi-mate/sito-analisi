What is $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to+\infty}\big(\sqrt{n^2+5n}-n\big)$?

- $\dfrac52$
- $5$
- $0$
- $+\infty$

## Explanation

Multiplying and dividing by the conjugate gives $\dfrac{5n}{\sqrt{n^2+5n}+n}$. Divide the top and bottom by $n$. The denominator becomes $\sqrt{1+\frac{5}{n}}+1$ and tends to $2$, so the limit is $\dfrac52$.
