A sequence has $a_1=100$ and $a_n=\dfrac{1}{n}$ for $n\geq2$. What is the limit of $s_N=\sup\{a_n:n\geq N\}$ as $N\to+\infty$?

- $100$
- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $0$

## Explanation

For $N\geq2$ the isolated value $100$ lies outside the tail. The remaining terms decrease, so their supremum is $\dfrac{1}{N}$, attained at the first index of the tail. It follows that $s_N\to0$.
