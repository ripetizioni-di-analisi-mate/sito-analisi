Let $u_n\to2$ and $v_n\to5$. For the sequence $m_n=\max\{u_n,v_n\}$, which equality holds eventually?

- $m_n=u_n+v_n$
- $m_n=v_n$
- $m_n=5$
- $m_n=u_n$

## Explanation

The difference $v_n-u_n$ tends to $3$, which is positive. Hence $v_n>u_n$ from some index onward and the maximum equals $v_n$. A limiting value of $5$ does not require $v_n$ ever to equal that value.
