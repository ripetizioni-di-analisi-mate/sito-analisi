The plot shows four successive readings $a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4$. What is the total variation $\sum_{k=1}^3|a_{k+1}-a_k|$?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=6 punti="1,2,pieno;2,5,pieno;3,4,pieno;4,1,pieno" nomi="n,a" titolo="Four samples in index order"}

- $-1$
- $1$
- $7$
- $3$

## Explanation

The successive vertical coordinates are $2,5,4,1$. Their changes are $+3,-1,-3$, so the sum of their absolute values is $3+1+3=7$. Subtracting the first reading from the last would give $-1$ and would lose the intermediate reversals.
