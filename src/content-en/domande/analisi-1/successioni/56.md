A signal $a_n$ equals $1$ when $n$ is a positive perfect square and $0$ otherwise. What is the limit of the average of the first $N$ signals?

- $1$
- The average has no limit.
- $\dfrac12$
- $0$

## Explanation

There are $\lfloor\sqrt N\rfloor$ squares from $1$ to $N$. The average is therefore $\dfrac{\lfloor\sqrt N\rfloor}{N}$, between zero and $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt N}$. The spikes persist in the original sequence, but their share of the average tends to zero.
