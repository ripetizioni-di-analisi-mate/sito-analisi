In each of $3n$ independent trials, a signal occurs with probability $\dfrac2n$, where $n>2$. What is the limiting probability of receiving no signal?

- $e^{-6}$
- $e^{-2}$
- $0$
- $1-e^{-6}$

## Explanation

In one trial the probability of no signal is $1-\dfrac2n$. Independence makes the probabilities multiply, giving $\left(1-\dfrac2n\right)^{3n}$. Its logarithm tends to $-6$ because $\dfrac{\ln(1+t)}{t}\to1$ as $t\to0$. The probability therefore tends to $e^{-6}$.
