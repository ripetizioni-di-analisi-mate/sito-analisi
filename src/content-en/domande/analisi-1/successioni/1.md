Let $a_n=\dfrac{4n-1}{2n+1}$. What is the smallest integer $N\geq0$ such that $|a_n-2|<\dfrac15$ for every integer $n>N$?

- $7$
- $15$
- $8$
- $6$

## Explanation

Subtracting $2$ and taking the absolute value gives $\dfrac{3}{2n+1}$. This distance is less than $\dfrac15$ when $2n+1>15$, starting at $n=8$. Since the condition applies to $n>N$, the last index to exclude is $7$.
