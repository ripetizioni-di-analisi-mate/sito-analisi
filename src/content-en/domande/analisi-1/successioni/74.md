The plot shows the values $y_n=\log_2 a_n$ of a positive signal. The points lie exactly on the line $y=3-n$. What happens to $a_n$ at each step?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=6 ymin=-3 ymax=4 punti="0,3,pieno;1,2,pieno;2,1,pieno;3,0,pieno;4,-1,pieno;5,-2,pieno" nomi="n,y" titolo="Base-two logarithm of the signal"}

- It is halved.
- It doubles.
- It decreases by one unit.
- It remains equal to $3$.

## Explanation

The difference between consecutive logarithmic coordinates is $-1$. Hence $\log_2(\dfrac{a_{n+1}}{a_n})=-1$ and the ratio of two successive signal values is $2^{-1}$. A constant difference in the logarithm becomes a constant factor in the signal.
