For $n\geq1$, we want to prove by comparison that $a_n=\dfrac{5n+2}{n^2+1}\to0$. Which bound holds for every $n\geq1$ and suffices to prove the claim?

- $0\leq a_n\leq\dfrac7n$
- $\dfrac7n\leq a_n\leq8$
- $0\leq a_n\leq\dfrac1n$
- $0\leq a_n\leq7n$

## Explanation

For $n\geq1$ we have $5n+2\leq7n$ and $n^2+1\geq n^2$. The quotient is positive, so it is at most $\dfrac{7n}{n^2}=\dfrac7n$. Both ends of the bound tend to zero. An upper bound such as $7n$ would not yield this conclusion.
