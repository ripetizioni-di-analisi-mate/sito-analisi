For $n\geq3$, what is $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to+\infty}\dfrac{n!}{(n-3)!\,n^3}$?

- $0$
- $6$
- $+\infty$
- $1$

## Explanation

The factorial quotient leaves exactly $n(n-1)(n-2)$. Dividing each factor by $n$ gives $1\cdot(1-\dfrac{1}{n})(1-\dfrac{2}{n})$. All three factors tend to one.
