Two exponents tending to zero are applied to the same base $a_n=2^{-n}$. What are the limits of $a_n^{\frac1n}$ and $a_n^{\frac2n}$, in that order?

- $(1,1)$
- $\left(\dfrac12,\dfrac14\right)$
- $(0,0)$
- $\left(\dfrac12,\dfrac12\right)$

## Explanation

The base is positive, so raising a power to another power multiplies the exponents. This gives $2^{-n\cdot\frac1n}=2^{-1}$ and $2^{-n\cdot\frac2n}=2^{-2}$ for every $n$. The resulting sequences are constant but have different values. The form $0^0$ alone does not determine a limit.
