The ratio test is applied to the sequence $a_n = \dfrac{1}{5^n} \dbinom{2n}{n}$. What result is obtained?

- $\ell = \dfrac{4}{5}$, so $a_n \to 0$
- $\ell = \dfrac{2}{5}$, so $a_n \to 0$
- $\ell = 1$, and the test gives no information
- $\ell = 4$, so $a_n \to +\infty$

## Explanation

We have $\dbinom{2n}{n} = \dfrac{(2n)!}{(n!)^2}$, so
$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{1}{5} \cdot \frac{(2n + 2)!}{(2n)!} \cdot \frac{(n!)^2}{\left( (n + 1)! \right)^2} = \frac{1}{5} \cdot \frac{(2n + 2)(2n + 1)}{(n + 1)^2} = \frac{1}{5} \cdot \frac{2(2n + 1)}{n + 1} .
$$
The ratio tends to $\ell = \dfrac{4}{5}$, which is less than $1$. Therefore, by the **ratio test**, $a_n \to 0$. The value $4$ is the limit of the ratio between two consecutive binomial coefficients, without the factor $\dfrac{1}{5}$.
