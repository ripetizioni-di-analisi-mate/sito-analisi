At small angles in radians, a sensor loses a fraction $1-\cos x$ of its signal. If $x=\dfrac{2}{n}$, what number does the loss multiplied by $n^2$ approach?

- $2$
- $1$
- $0$
- $4$

## Explanation

Use $1-\cos(\dfrac{2}{n})=2\sin^2(\dfrac{1}{n})$. Multiplying by $n^2$ gives $2[n\sin(\dfrac{1}{n})]^2$. The standard sine limit in radians gives $n\sin(\dfrac{1}{n})\to1$, so the rescaled loss tends to $2$.
