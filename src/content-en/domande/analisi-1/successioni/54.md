From $a_n$ form $b_k=a_{2k+1}$, then form $c_j=b_{3j-1}$. Which original index supplies $c_j$?

- $6j-3$
- $5j$
- $6j+1$
- $6j-1$

## Explanation

Substitute the entire index $k=3j-1$ into $2k+1$. This yields $2(3j-1)+1=6j-1$. The order of substitution matters because the two index transformations have different constant terms.
