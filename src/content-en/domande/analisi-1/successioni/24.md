An experimental constraint guarantees only $1\leq a_n\leq2$. Which sequence satisfies the constraint and shows that convergence does not follow?

- $a_n=2-\dfrac1n$
- $a_n=1+\dfrac1n$
- $a_n=\dfrac{3+(-1)^n}{2}$
- $a_n=\dfrac32$

## Explanation

The expression with numerator $3+(-1)^n$ alternates between $1$ and $2$. It stays in the interval but has two subsequences with different limits. Each of the other formulas has a finite limit.
