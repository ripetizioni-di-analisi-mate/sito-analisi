An iteration converges to $L$ and satisfies $|a_{j+1}-a_j|\leq\dfrac{3}{2^j}$ for every $j\geq1$. Adding the bounds on the changes from step $n$ onward, what upper bound does this give for $|L-a_n|$?

- $\dfrac3{2^n}$
- $\dfrac1{2^{n+1}}$
- $\dfrac3{4^n}$
- $\dfrac6{2^n}$

## Explanation

Getting from $a_n$ to the limit involves every subsequent change. Their total absolute size is at most the geometric sum with first term $\dfrac{3}{2^n}$ and ratio $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Dividing the first term by $1-\dfrac{1}{2}$ gives $\dfrac{6}{2^n}$.
