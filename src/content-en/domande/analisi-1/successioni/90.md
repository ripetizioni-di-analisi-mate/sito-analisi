For every $n\geq1$, $a_n$ is the positive solution of $a_n^2+n a_n=1$. Without solving the quadratic equation, what is $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to+\infty}n a_n$?

- $0$
- $+\infty$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

Positivity gives $0<n a_n=1-a_n^2\leq1$, hence $0<a_n\leq\dfrac{1}{n}$. It follows that $a_n\to0$. Returning to $n a_n=1-a_n^2$, we see that the right-hand side tends to one.
