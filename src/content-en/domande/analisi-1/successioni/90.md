The semiperimeter of the regular polygon with $n$ sides inscribed in the circle of radius $1$ is $p_n = n \sin \dfrac{\pi}{n}$. What value does $p_n$ tend to?

- $1$
- $\pi$
- $2\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

With the infinitesimal sequence $\varepsilon_n = \dfrac{\pi}{n}$ we have $n = \dfrac{\pi}{\varepsilon_n}$, so
$$
p_n = \pi \cdot \frac{\sin \varepsilon_n}{\varepsilon_n} .
$$
By the standard limit $\dfrac{\sin \varepsilon_n}{\varepsilon_n} \to 1$, we obtain $p_n \to \pi$. The result is consistent with geometry, because the semiperimeter of the circle of radius $1$ equals $\pi$.
