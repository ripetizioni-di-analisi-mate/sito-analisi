What does the $n$-th root of a product, $\sqrt[n]{n^2 \cdot 4^n}$, tend to?

- $1$
- $4$
- $16$
- $+\infty$

## Explanation

By the properties of roots
$$
\sqrt[n]{n^2 \cdot 4^n} = \left( \sqrt[n]{n} \right)^{2} \cdot \sqrt[n]{4^n} = \left( \sqrt[n]{n} \right)^{2} \cdot 4 .
$$
Since $\sqrt[n]{n} \to 1$, the first factor tends to $1$, and the limit equals $4$.
