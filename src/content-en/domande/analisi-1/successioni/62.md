Every approximation lies in $(0,1)$, and the approximations converge. Which set contains exactly all possible limits of sequences satisfying these conditions?

- $[0,1]$
- $(0,1)$
- $[0,1)$
- $\{0,1\}$

## Explanation

The weak inequalities $0\leq L\leq1$ pass to the limit. A constant sequence realizes each interior point. The sequences $\dfrac{1}{n+2}$ and $1-\dfrac{1}{n+2}$ realize the endpoints as limits without ever reaching them.
