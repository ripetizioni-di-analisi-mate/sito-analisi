The sequences $n^{10}$, $10^n$, $n!$ and $n^n$ all diverge to $+\infty$. Which list orders them by increasing order of infinity?

- $10^n$, $n^{10}$, $n!$, $n^n$
- $n^{10}$, $n!$, $10^n$, $n^n$
- $n^{10}$, $10^n$, $n!$, $n^n$
- $n^{10}$, $10^n$, $n^n$, $n!$

## Explanation

By the **hierarchy of infinities**, with $\alpha = 10$ and $a = 10$,
$$
\frac{n^{10}}{10^n} \to 0, \qquad \frac{10^n}{n!} \to 0, \qquad \frac{n!}{n^n} \to 0 .
$$
The ratio between each sequence and the next one tends to $0$, so the order by increasing infinity is $n^{10}$, $10^n$, $n!$, $n^n$. For the first values of $n$ the terms are ordered differently; for example, for $n = 10$ we have $n^{10} = 10^n = 10^{10}$, which exceeds $10!$. The order of infinity, however, depends only on the limit of the ratios.
