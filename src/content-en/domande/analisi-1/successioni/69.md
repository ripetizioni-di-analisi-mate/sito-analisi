For $a_n=2^n(2+(-1)^n)$, the $n$th root tends to $2$. Does the ratio $\dfrac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}$ also tend to $2$?

- Yes, it is always $2$.
- No, it tends to zero.
- No, it alternates between $\dfrac23$ and $6$.
- Yes, it increases monotonically toward $2$.

## Explanation

The parenthesized factor changes from $3$ to $1$ after an even index and from $1$ to $3$ after an odd one. In the ratio, the fixed factor $2$ is therefore multiplied by $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and by $3$, respectively. The two results differ, so the ratio has no limit.
