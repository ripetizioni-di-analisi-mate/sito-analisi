What is the maximum value taken by the terms of $a_n = \dfrac{3^n}{n!}$, with $n \geq 1$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{27}{8}$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$

## Explanation

The terms are positive and the ratio between two consecutive terms is
$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{3^{n+1}}{(n + 1)!} \cdot \frac{n!}{3^n} = \frac{3}{n + 1} .
$$
The ratio is greater than $1$ for $n = 1$, equal to $1$ for $n = 2$ and less than $1$ for $n \geq 3$. Hence $a_1 = 3 < a_2 = a_3 = \dfrac{9}{2}$, and from $n = 3$ on the terms decrease. Therefore the **maximum** of the terms is $\dfrac{9}{2}$, attained for $n = 2$ and $n = 3$. The value $\dfrac{27}{8}$ is the term $a_4$.
