For $n\geq1$, let $u_n=\sqrt n\,(\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt n)$. Its limit is $\dfrac12$. Which inequality follows by applying the mean value theorem to $f(x)=\sqrt x$ on $[n,n+1]$?

- $u_n\leq0$
- $\dfrac12<u_n<1$
- $0<u_n<\dfrac12$
- $u_n\geq1$

## Explanation

There is a point $\xi_n$ between $n$ and $n+1$ such that the increase in the square root equals $\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{\xi_n}}$. Because $\sqrt{\xi_n}>\sqrt n$, multiplying by $\sqrt n$ gives a positive number strictly less than $\dfrac12$.
