The plot shows eight successive signal samples. An alarm triggers on the second of two consecutive samples outside the closed band $[2,4]$. At which index does it first trigger?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=9 ymin=0 ymax=6 punti="1,3,pieno;2,5,pieno;3,3,pieno;4,4,pieno;5,5,pieno;6,1,pieno;7,3,pieno;8,1,pieno" asintoti="y=2;y=4" nomi="n,a" titolo="Samples and the boundaries of the accepted band"}

- $6$
- $8$
- $5$
- $2$

## Explanation

Sample $2$ is above the band, but sample $3$ returns inside it. Sample $4$ lies on the boundary and is still accepted. Sample $5$ is above $4$ and sample $6$ below $2$, so the first consecutive pair is completed at index $6$.
