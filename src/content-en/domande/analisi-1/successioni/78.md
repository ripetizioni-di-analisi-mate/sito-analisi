A sequence has a convergent subsequence. Which example shows that this information alone does not imply that the entire sequence is bounded?

- $a_{2k}=0,\ a_{2k-1}=k$
- $a_n=\dfrac{1}{n}$
- $a_n=(-1)^n$
- $a_n=n$

## Explanation

The even indices in the piecewise sequence give the constant zero subsequence. The odd terms are $1,2,3,\ldots$ and exceed every upper bound. This single example satisfies both conditions required to disprove the converse.
