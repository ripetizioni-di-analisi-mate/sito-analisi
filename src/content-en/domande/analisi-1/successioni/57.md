All infinitely many readings lie in the closed interval $[-2,5]$. What does the Bolzano–Weierstrass theorem guarantee without any information about their order?

- All the readings converge to the midpoint.
- A subsequence converges to a point in $[-2,5]$.
- Some value must occur infinitely often.
- A subsequence must converge to an endpoint.

## Explanation

Boundedness guarantees a convergent subsequence. Its limit remains in the interval because the inequalities $-2\leq a_{n_k}\leq5$ pass to the limit. The theorem specifies neither the point nor the behavior of the whole sequence.
