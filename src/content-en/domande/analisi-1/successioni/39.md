To study the monotonicity of $a_n = \dfrac{3n - 1}{n + 2}$, the difference $a_{n+1} - a_n$ is computed. Which expression is obtained?

- $\dfrac{7}{(n + 2)(n + 3)}$
- $\dfrac{5}{(n + 2)(n + 3)}$
- $-\dfrac{7}{(n + 2)(n + 3)}$
- $\dfrac{7}{(n + 2)^2}$

## Explanation

We have $a_{n+1} = \dfrac{3(n + 1) - 1}{(n + 1) + 2} = \dfrac{3n + 2}{n + 3}$. Over a common denominator
$$
a_{n+1} - a_n = \frac{(3n + 2)(n + 2) - (3n - 1)(n + 3)}{(n + 2)(n + 3)} = \frac{(3n^2 + 8n + 4) - (3n^2 + 8n - 3)}{(n + 2)(n + 3)} = \frac{7}{(n + 2)(n + 3)} .
$$
The difference is positive for every $n$, so the sequence is **strictly increasing**.
