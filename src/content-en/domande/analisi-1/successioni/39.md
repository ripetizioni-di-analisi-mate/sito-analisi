A bisection method starts with an interval of length $8$ and keeps the half containing the solution at each step. What is the minimum number of halvings that guarantees the midpoint error is at most $\dfrac1{16}$?

- $5$
- $7$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

After $k$ halvings the length is $\dfrac{8}{2^k}$. The distance from the midpoint to the solution is at most half this length, so we need $\dfrac{4}{2^k}\leq\dfrac{1}{16}$. This is equivalent to $2^k\geq64$, whose smallest solution is $k=6$.
