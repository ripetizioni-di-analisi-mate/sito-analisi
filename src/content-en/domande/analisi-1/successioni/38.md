A nondecreasing sequence is observed only at indices $2^k$. If $a_{2^k}\to7$, what follows for the sequence at all indices?

- The whole sequence might tend to $8$.
- $a_n=7$ eventually.
- All unsampled terms are equal.
- $a_n\to7$

## Explanation

For $2^k\leq n\leq2^{k+1}$, monotonicity gives $a_{2^k}\leq a_n\leq a_{2^{k+1}}$. As $n$ grows, so does $k$. Both bounds tend to $7$, so every intermediate term approaches the same number.
