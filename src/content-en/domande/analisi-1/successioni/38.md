Which statement about the monotonicity of $a_n = \dfrac{n}{2^n}$, with $n \geq 1$, is true?

- It is strictly decreasing
- It is decreasing, but not strictly
- It is strictly increasing
- It is not monotone

## Explanation

The terms are positive, so monotonicity is studied by comparing with $1$ the ratio
$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{n + 1}{2^{n+1}} \cdot \frac{2^n}{n} = \frac{n + 1}{2n} .
$$
The ratio equals $1$ for $n = 1$ and is less than $1$ for $n \geq 2$, because $n + 1 < 2n$ is equivalent to $n > 1$. Therefore $a_{n+1} \leq a_n$ for every $n$ and the sequence is **decreasing**. It is not strictly decreasing, because $a_1 = a_2 = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
