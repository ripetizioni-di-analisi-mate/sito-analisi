Comparing two growth rates leads to $\dfrac{\ln(\ln n)}{\ln n}$ for $n>1$. What is its limit?

- $-1$
- $1$
- $0$
- $+\infty$

## Explanation

Set $x=\ln n$. As $n\to+\infty$, we also have $x\to+\infty$, and the ratio becomes $\dfrac{\ln x}{x}$. The logarithm grows more slowly than $x$, so the ratio tends to zero.
