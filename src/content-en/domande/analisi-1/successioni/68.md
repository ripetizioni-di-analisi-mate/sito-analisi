An instrument truncates each positive reading to two decimal places. It receives $a_n=\dfrac1{10}(1-\dfrac1n)$ for $n\geq2$. What value does it eventually display?

- $0.08$
- $0.10$
- $0.09$
- $0.01$

## Explanation

For $n\geq10$ the reading is at least $0.09$ and strictly less than $0.10$. Throughout this interval truncation to two decimal places gives $0.09$. Rounding to the nearest hundredth would instead give $0.10$ for $n>20$.
