Under the $n$-th root of $\sqrt[n]{5n^3 + 2}$ there is a polynomial. What is the limit of the sequence?

- $5$
- $1$
- $+\infty$
- $0$

## Explanation

For $n \geq 1$ we have $5n^3 \leq 5n^3 + 2 \leq 7n^3$, so
$$
\sqrt[n]{5} \left( \sqrt[n]{n} \right)^{3} \leq \sqrt[n]{5n^3 + 2} \leq \sqrt[n]{7} \left( \sqrt[n]{n} \right)^{3} .
$$
By the limits of **$n$-th roots**, $\sqrt[n]{5} \to 1$, $\sqrt[n]{7} \to 1$ and $\sqrt[n]{n} \to 1$, so the two outer sequences tend to $1$. By the squeeze theorem also $\sqrt[n]{5n^3 + 2} \to 1$.
