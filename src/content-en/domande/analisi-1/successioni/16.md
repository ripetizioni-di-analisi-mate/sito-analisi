The first hundred measured terms are all zero. Which statement correctly describes the limits compatible with these measurements?

- If it converges, its limit must be nonnegative.
- All its terms must have absolute value at most one.
- It must tend to zero.
- It can converge to any prescribed real number.

## Explanation

Choose a real number $L$ and set every term from the hundred and first onward equal to $L$. This matches all one hundred measurements and the resulting sequence tends to $L$. The available data therefore impose no particular value on the limit.
