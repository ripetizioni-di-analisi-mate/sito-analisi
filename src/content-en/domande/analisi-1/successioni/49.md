A positive cost after $n$ steps is $C_n=7^n n^4$. What per-step growth factor is obtained from the limit of $\sqrt[n]{C_n}$?

- $1$
- $7$
- $28$
- $+\infty$

## Explanation

The root is $7\exp(4\dfrac{\ln n}{n})$. The exponent in the second factor tends to zero because $\dfrac{\ln n}{n}\to0$. The polynomial contribution thus has a per-step factor of one, leaving $7$.
