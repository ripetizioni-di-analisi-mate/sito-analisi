The sequence $x_n = n^2 - n$ diverges to $+\infty$. What is $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to +\infty} \arctan\left( n^2 - n \right)$?

- $+\infty$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

By the limit of the arctangent function we have $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to +\infty} \arctan x = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. By the **bridge theorem**, for every sequence $x_n \to +\infty$ we therefore have $\arctan x_n \to \dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

Since $x_n = n(n - 1) \to +\infty$, it follows that $\arctan\left( n^2 - n \right) \to \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The limit cannot be $+\infty$, because the arctangent takes only values between $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
