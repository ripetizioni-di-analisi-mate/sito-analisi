Two methods approximate the same number with positive errors $e_n=\dfrac1{n+1}$ and $f_n=\dfrac1{n(n+1)}$. Which ratio justifies choosing the second method for sufficiently large indices?

- $\dfrac{e_n}{f_n}\to0$
- $\dfrac{e_n}{f_n}\to1$
- $\dfrac{f_n}{e_n}\to1$
- $\dfrac{f_n}{e_n}\to0$

## Explanation

Dividing $f_n$ by $e_n$ cancels the common factor $\dfrac1{n+1}$ and leaves $\dfrac1n$. At $n=100$, for example, the second error is one hundredth of the first. This ratio tends to zero as the index grows.
