For $a_n=(-1)^n\dfrac{2n+1}{n+2}$, consider first the even-indexed terms and then the odd-indexed ones. What is the ordered pair of their limits?

- $(-2,2)$
- $(2,2)$
- $(2,-2)$
- $(0,0)$

## Explanation

The fraction tends to $2$ because both the numerator and the denominator are linear. The factor $(-1)^n$ equals $1$ at even indices and $-1$ at odd indices. The two subsequences therefore tend to $2$ and $-2$, ruling out a common limit.
