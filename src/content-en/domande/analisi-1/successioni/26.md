Suppose $a_n^2\to9$ and $a_n\leq0$ eventually. What is the limit of $a_n$?

- $-3$
- $-9$
- $3$
- It cannot be determined.

## Explanation

Continuity of the square root gives $\sqrt{a_n^2}=|a_n|\to3$. Along the tail $a_n=-|a_n|$, so the limit is $-3$. The given sign selects one of the two roots.
