Suppose $a_n>0$ and $a_{n+1}\leq\dfrac34 a_n$ for $n\geq N$. What bound follows after another $k$ steps, where $k\geq1$?

- $a_{N+k}\leq a_N(\dfrac{3}{4})^{N+k}$
- $a_{N+k}\leq a_N-\dfrac{k}{4}$
- $a_{N+k}\leq a_N(\dfrac{3}{4})^k$
- $a_{N+k}\leq \dfrac{a_N}{4k}$

## Explanation

The first step introduces one factor of $\dfrac34$, and the second introduces another. Iterating the inequality $k$ times starting from $a_N$ gives exponent $k$. The resulting bound tends to zero as $k$ grows.
