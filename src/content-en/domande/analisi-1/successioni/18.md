Which observation, if proved, suffices to disprove the statement $a_n\to4$?

- Infinitely many indices satisfy $|a_n-4|\geq\dfrac1{10}$.
- No term equals $4$ exactly.
- The terms cross the level $4$ infinitely often.
- One term is more than $10$ away from $4$.

## Explanation

For convergence to $4$, all terms beyond some index must lie at distance less than $\dfrac1{10}$. Infinitely many indices outside this interval prevent any such cutoff. A single distant term or crossings of decreasing amplitude are compatible with the limit.
