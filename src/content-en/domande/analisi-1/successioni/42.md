A sensor follows the rule $a_{n+1}=q a_n+c$, with unknown constants $q$ and $c$. The first three readings are $2,6,8$. What is the additive constant $c$?

- $4$
- $2$
- $\dfrac12$
- $5$

## Explanation

Subtract consecutive updates to eliminate the additive term. The second increment, $8-6=2$, is $q$ times the first, $6-2=4$, so $q=\dfrac12$. Now $6=2q+c=1+c$, giving $c=5$.
