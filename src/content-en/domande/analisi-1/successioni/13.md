What can be said about the boundedness of the terms of $a_n = 3n - n^2$, with $n \geq 1$?

- They are bounded above with maximum $2$ and not bounded below
- They are bounded above with maximum $\dfrac{9}{4}$ and not bounded below
- They are bounded
- They are not bounded above

## Explanation

We have $a_{n+1} - a_n = 3 - (2n + 1) = 2 - 2n$, which is zero for $n = 1$ and negative for $n \geq 2$. Hence $a_1 = a_2 = 2$ and the following terms decrease, so the **maximum** of the terms is $2$.

The number $\dfrac{9}{4}$ is the maximum of the function $3x - x^2$, attained at $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$, which is not an index. Moreover $3n - n^2 = n(3 - n) \to -\infty$, so the terms are not bounded below.
