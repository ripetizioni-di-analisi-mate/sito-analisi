The sequence $a_1=2$, $a_{n+1}=\sqrt{3a_n}$ is increasing and stays in $[2,3]$. To which fixed point does it converge?

- $0$
- $\sqrt6$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

An increasing sequence bounded above has a limit $L$. Continuity of the square root gives $L=\sqrt{3L}$, or $L(L-3)=0$. The bound $L\geq2$ rules out zero, leaving $L=3$.
