For $n\geq2$, set $P_n=\prod_{k=2}^{n}(1-\dfrac1{k^2})$. What is the limit of $P_n$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

Write each factor as $\dfrac{k-1}{k}\dfrac{k+1}{k}$. The first ratios multiply to $\dfrac1n$ and the second ratios to $\dfrac{n+1}{2}$. Thus $P_n=\dfrac{n+1}{2n}\to\dfrac12$.
