The sine in $a_n=\dfrac{n\sin n}{n^2+1}$ keeps oscillating. What is the limit of $a_n$?

- $1$
- $0$
- The limit does not exist.
- $-1$

## Explanation

The absolute value of the sine is at most one. The oscillation therefore has amplitude at most $\dfrac{n}{n^2+1}$, which tends to zero. The entire sequence tends to zero even though the factor $\sin n$ does not settle down.
