For which real value of $k$ does the sequence $(n+2)^2-n^2-kn$ have a finite limit?

- $4$
- $2$
- $0$
- $-4$

## Explanation

Expanding the square gives $(4-k)n+4$. Any nonzero coefficient of $n$ makes the absolute value grow without bound. With $k=4$ the linear term disappears and the sequence is constantly $4$.
