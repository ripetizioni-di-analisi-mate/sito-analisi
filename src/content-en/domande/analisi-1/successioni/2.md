Find the limit of $\dfrac{n^2-3n+1}{4n^2+n}$ as $n\to+\infty$.

- $-\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac14$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

Divide the numerator and denominator by $n^2$. The result is $\dfrac{1-\frac{3}{n}+\frac{1}{n^2}}{4+\frac{1}{n}}$. The terms with $n$ in the denominator tend to zero and the denominator tends to $4$, so the ratio tends to $\dfrac14$.
