A sequence of integers converges to $7$. What must happen to its terms?

- They may keep alternating between $6$ and $8$.
- All terms from some index onward equal $7$.
- The terms must be strictly increasing.
- Every term, including the first, must equal $7$.

## Explanation

Choose the radius $\dfrac12$ in the definition of a limit. Beyond some cutoff every term lies between $6.5$ and $7.5$. The only integer in that interval is $7$, so the tail is constant.
