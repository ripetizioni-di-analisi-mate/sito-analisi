Two sequences satisfy $a_n \to 2$ and $b_n \to -3$. What does $\dfrac{a_n^2 - b_n}{a_n + b_n}$ tend to?

- $7$
- $-7$
- $-1$
- $1$

## Explanation

By the rules for limits of sums, products and quotients, the numerator tends to $2^2 - (-3) = 7$ and the denominator tends to $2 + (-3) = -1$.

Since the limit of the denominator is different from $0$, the quotient tends to
$$
\frac{7}{-1} = -7 .
$$
