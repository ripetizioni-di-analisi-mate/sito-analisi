Bernoulli's inequality gives $1.01^{n} \geq 1 + \dfrac{n}{100}$ for every $n$. What is the smallest $n$ for which this estimate ensures $1.01^{n} > 10$?

- $n = 900$
- $n = 901$
- $n = 1001$
- $n = 91$

## Explanation

The estimate ensures $1.01^{n} > 10$ when $1 + \dfrac{n}{100} > 10$, that is, when $\dfrac{n}{100} > 9$, or $n > 900$. For $n = 900$ the right-hand side equals $1 + 9 = 10$, which is not greater than $10$. Therefore the smallest index is $n = 901$.

By the same argument, for every $M > 0$ we have $1.01^{n} > M$ for every $n > 100(M - 1)$. This is how Bernoulli's inequality proves that the geometric sequence with ratio $q > 1$ diverges to $+\infty$.
