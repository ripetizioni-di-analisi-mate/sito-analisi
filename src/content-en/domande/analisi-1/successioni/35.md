At which integer index $n\geq1$ does $a_n=\dfrac{n^2}{2^n}$ attain its maximum?

- $2$
- $3$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

The ratio of consecutive terms is $\dfrac{(1+\frac{1}{n})^2}{2}$ and decreases as $n$ grows. At $n=2$ it is $\dfrac98>1$, whereas at $n=3$ it is $\dfrac89<1$. Thus the third term exceeds both the earlier and the later terms.
