What is $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to+\infty}n\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-\arctan n\right)$ when angles are measured in radians?

- $+\infty$
- $0$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

For $n>0$, the angles $\arctan n$ and $\arctan(\dfrac{1}{n})$ are complementary, as seen by exchanging the legs of a right triangle. The product becomes $\dfrac{\arctan(\frac{1}{n})}{\frac{1}{n}}$. This ratio tends to one because the derivative of arctangent at zero is one.
