The rule $a_{n+1}=2a_n+1$ holds from $n=0$. Which initial value gives a bounded sequence?

- $a_0=-\dfrac12$
- $a_0=1$
- $a_0=-1$
- $a_0=0$

## Explanation

The quantity $a_n+1$ doubles at each step because $a_{n+1}+1=2(a_n+1)$. Thus $a_n+1=2^n(a_0+1)$. Boundedness requires $a_0+1=0$, in which case every term is $-1$.
