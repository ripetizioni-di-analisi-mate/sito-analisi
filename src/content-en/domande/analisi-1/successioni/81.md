Use the usual real roots, with nonnegative even roots. Which statement describes $a_n=\sqrt[n]{(-2)^n}$?

- It equals $2$ for even $n$ and $-2$ for odd $n$.
- It is defined only for even $n$.
- It always equals $2$.
- It always equals $-2$.

## Explanation

For even $n$, the power is positive and the principal root gives $2$. For odd $n$, the power is negative and has a unique real root, namely $-2$. Canceling root and power without checking parity would lose this distinction.
