A model predicts a reading of $n$ when the actual reading is $a_n=n+\sqrt n$. How do the absolute error $|a_n-n|$ and the error relative to the prediction $\dfrac{|a_n-n|}{n}$ behave?

- The absolute error tends to $+\infty$ and the relative error to $0$.
- The absolute error tends to zero and the relative error to one.
- Both tend to $+\infty$.
- Both tend to zero.

## Explanation

The absolute difference is $\sqrt n$ and grows without bound. Dividing by the prediction gives $\dfrac{\sqrt n}{n}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt n}\to0$. An error can therefore grow in absolute units while becoming small relative to the scale of the data.
