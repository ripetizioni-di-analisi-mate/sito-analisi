A signal alternates exactly between $2$ and $8$. Each sample is replaced by the arithmetic mean of itself and the next two samples. What values does the filtered signal take?

- $4$ and $6$
- $3$ and $7$
- Always $5$.
- $2$ and $8$

## Explanation

The two possible windows are $(2,8,2)$ and $(8,2,8)$. Dividing their sums $12$ and $18$ by three gives $4$ and $6$. The three-term window reduces the amplitude but contains an odd number of samples, so some oscillation remains.
