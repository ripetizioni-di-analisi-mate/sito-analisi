A population is alternately multiplied by $2$ and by $8$. Which positive constant factor per period produces exactly the same effect after any even number of periods?

- $16$
- $4$
- $5$
- $8$

## Explanation

Over two periods the total multiplier is $2\cdot8=16$. The positive constant factor $q$ must therefore satisfy $q^2=16$, giving $q=4$. Repeating the pair of periods preserves equality over every even duration.
