The sum of the first $n$ terms is $S_n=n^2$ for every $n\geq1$, with $S_0=0$. What is the tenth term?

- $21$
- $100$
- $19$
- $10$

## Explanation

The tenth sum contains exactly one more term than the ninth. Thus $a_{10}=S_{10}-S_9=100-81=19$. The value $100$ includes the preceding nine terms as well.
