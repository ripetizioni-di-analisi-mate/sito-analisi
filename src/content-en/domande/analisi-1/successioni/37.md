Readings $x_1,x_2,\ldots$ remain between $0$ and $1$. Define the running record by $r_n=\max\{x_1,\ldots,x_n\}$. Why do the records converge?

- They are nondecreasing and bounded above.
- The value $1$ must be attained.
- The original readings must converge.
- Each record increases by at least a fixed amount.

## Explanation

Adding one reading keeps the previous maximum available, so $r_{n+1}\geq r_n$. Also, every record is at most $1$. The monotone convergence theorem gives convergence even when the limiting record is below $1$.
