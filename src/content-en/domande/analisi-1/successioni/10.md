To study $\dfrac{n^4+3^n}{n!+2^n}$, replace each sum by its dominant term. Which ratio is asymptotically equivalent to the original one as $n\to+\infty$?

- $\dfrac{n^4}{n!}$
- $\dfrac{n^4}{2^n}$
- $\dfrac{3^n}{n!}$
- $\dfrac{3^n}{2^n}$

## Explanation

In the numerator $\dfrac{n^4}{3^n}\to0$, while in the denominator $\dfrac{2^n}{n!}\to0$. Factoring out $3^n$ and $n!$ gives $\dfrac{3^n}{n!}$ multiplied by a factor tending to one. This is precisely the condition for asymptotic equivalence.
