The terms of $a_n = \dfrac{3n - 1}{n + 1}$, with $n \geq 1$, satisfy $|a_n| \leq K$ for every $n$. What is the smallest possible value of $K$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $3$

## Explanation

We write
$$
a_n = \frac{3(n + 1) - 4}{n + 1} = 3 - \frac{4}{n + 1} .
$$
Since $\dfrac{4}{n + 1}$ is positive and decreasing, the terms increase starting from $a_1 = 1$ and stay less than $3$. Hence $|a_n| = a_n < 3$ for every $n$, and $K = 3$ satisfies the condition.

Moreover $a_n \to 3$, so for every $K < 3$ there are terms greater than $K$. The smallest possible value is therefore $K = 3$, which is the supremum of the terms and is not one of them.
