For the sequence $a_1 = 2$, $a_{n+1} = 2a_n - 1$ the equation $L = 2L - 1$ has the unique solution $L = 1$. What is the limit of the sequence?

- $1$
- $2$
- $+\infty$
- The limit does not exist

## Explanation

Subtracting $1$ from both sides of the recurrence we obtain $a_{n+1} - 1 = 2(a_n - 1)$. Hence $a_n - 1 = 2^{n-1}(a_1 - 1) = 2^{n-1}$, that is,
$$
a_n = 1 + 2^{n-1} ,
$$
which **diverges** to $+\infty$. The equation $L = 2L - 1$ only says that, *if* the limit exists and is finite, it equals $1$. Here no finite limit exists, because the sequence is increasing and not bounded above.
