For which $x\in\mathbb R$ does the sequence $(x-1)^n$ have a finite limit?

- $0\leq x<2$
- $0<x<2$
- $0<x\leq2$
- $0\leq x\leq2$

## Explanation

Set $q=x-1$. For $|q|<1$ the powers tend to zero. At $q=1$ they are constant, at $q=-1$ they oscillate, and for $|q|>1$ their absolute values grow without bound. Thus $-1<q\leq1$, or $0<x\leq2$.
