What is the smallest bound obtained for the first-order Maclaurin remainder of $e^{4 x}$ at $\dfrac{1}{4}$ using the maximum of $|f\prime\prime|$ on the entire interval?

- $\dfrac{e}{6}$
- $\dfrac{e}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $e$

## Explanation

The second derivative is increasing and equals $16 e$ at the right endpoint. Multiply by $\dfrac{1}{32}$ in the Lagrange remainder.
