What is the derivative of $f(x) = \arcsin(2x)$ for $|x| < \dfrac{1}{2}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1 - 4x^2}}$
- $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}}$
- $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{1 - 4x^2}}$
- $\dfrac{2}{1 + 4x^2}$

## Explanation

The derivative of $\arcsin t$ is $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1 - t^2}}$, and here the argument is $t = 2x$, with derivative $2$. Evaluated at $t = 2x$, the outer derivative equals $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1 - (2x)^2}} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1 - 4x^2}}$. By the **chain rule** we multiply by $2$, so $f'(x) = \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{1 - 4x^2}}$. The condition $|x| < \dfrac{1}{2}$ ensures that $1 - 4x^2 > 0$.
