What is the coefficient of $x^2$ in the Maclaurin polynomial of $e^{2 x} \cos{\left(3 x \right)}$?

- $-5$
- $\dfrac{13}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

The quadratic contributions from the exponential and cosine are $2$ and $- \dfrac{9}{2}$. The cosine has zero linear coefficient.
