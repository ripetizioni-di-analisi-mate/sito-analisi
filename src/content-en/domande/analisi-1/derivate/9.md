For $f(x) = x^2$ on the interval $[1, 3]$, which point $c$ satisfies the conclusion of Lagrange's theorem?

- $c = 2$
- $c = 4$
- $c = \sqrt{5}$
- $c = \dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

The function $f$ is a polynomial, so it is continuous on $[1, 3]$ and differentiable on $(1, 3)$; the hypotheses of **Lagrange's theorem** are therefore satisfied. The slope of the line through the endpoints of the graph is
$$
\frac{f(3) - f(1)}{3 - 1} = \frac{9 - 1}{2} = 4 .
$$
Since $f'(c) = 2c$, the conclusion $f'(c) = 4$ becomes $2c = 4$, so $c = 2$. The point belongs to the interval $(1, 3)$, as the theorem guarantees.
