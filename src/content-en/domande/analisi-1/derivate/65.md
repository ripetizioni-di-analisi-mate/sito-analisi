The function $f$ is differentiable on $\mathbb{R}$, with $f(1) = 2$ and $f'(1) = 3$. What is the tangent line to the graph of $g(x) = f(x)^2$ at the point with abscissa $1$?

- $y = 12x + 4$
- $y = 6x - 2$
- $y = 12x - 10$
- $y = 12x - 8$

## Explanation

We have $g(1) = f(1)^2 = 4$. By the derivative of a composite function $g'(x) = 2f(x)\,f'(x)$, so $g'(1) = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 = 12$. The tangent line at the point with abscissa $1$ is $y = g(1) + g'(1)\,(x - 1) = 4 + 12\,(x - 1)$, that is, $y = 12x - 8$.
