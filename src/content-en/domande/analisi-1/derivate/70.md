What is the absolute minimum value of $f(x) = e^{x} - x$ on $\mathbb{R}$?

- $1$
- $0$
- $e - 1$
- The function has no absolute minimum

## Explanation

We have $f'(x) = e^{x} - 1$, which is negative for $x < 0$, zero at $x = 0$ and positive for $x > 0$. So $f$ is decreasing on $(-\infty, 0]$ and increasing on $[0, +\infty)$, therefore $x = 0$ is the absolute minimum point. The minimum value is $f(0) = e^{0} - 0 = 1$.
