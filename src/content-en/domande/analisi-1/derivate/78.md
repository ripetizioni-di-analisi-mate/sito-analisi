Which polynomial of degree two in $x - 1$ is the Taylor polynomial of $\ln x$ centered at $x_0 = 1$?

- $(x - 1) - \dfrac{(x - 1)^2}{2}$
- $(x - 1) + \dfrac{(x - 1)^2}{2}$
- $(x - 1) - (x - 1)^2$
- $x - \dfrac{x^2}{2}$

## Explanation

We have $f(1) = 0$, $f'(x) = \dfrac{1}{x}$ and $f''(x) = -\dfrac{1}{x^2}$, so $f'(1) = 1$ and $f''(1) = -1$. By **Taylor's formula** the polynomial is
$$
f(1) + f'(1)\,(x - 1) + \frac{f''(1)}{2}\,(x - 1)^2 = (x - 1) - \frac{(x - 1)^2}{2} .
$$
The same result follows from the expansion of $\ln(1 + t)$ with $t = x - 1$.
