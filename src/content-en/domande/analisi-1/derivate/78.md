What is $\lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{\dfrac{32 x^{3}}{3} - 4 x + \sin{\left(4 x \right)}}{x^{5}}$?

- $\dfrac{128}{15}$
- $\dfrac{8}{15}$
- $- \dfrac{128}{15}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The first- and third-degree terms cancel. The fifth-degree coefficient of the sine is $\dfrac{128}{15}$.
