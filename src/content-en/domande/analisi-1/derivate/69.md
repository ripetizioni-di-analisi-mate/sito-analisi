What is the coefficient of $x^2$ in the Maclaurin polynomial of $e^{4 x} \cos{\left(5 x \right)}$?

- $-9$
- $\dfrac{41}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{9}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$

## Explanation

The quadratic contributions from the exponential and cosine are $8$ and $- \dfrac{25}{2}$. The cosine has zero linear coefficient.
