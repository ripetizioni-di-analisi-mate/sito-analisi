The function $g(x) = \bigl| x^3 - x \bigr|$ is differentiable at $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$, where $x^3 - x \neq 0$. What is the value of $g'\!\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)$?

- $-\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{7}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

For $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ we have $x^3 - x = \dfrac{1}{8} - \dfrac{1}{2} = -\dfrac{3}{8} < 0$. Since $x^3 - x$ is continuous, it is negative in a whole neighborhood of $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and in that neighborhood $g(x) = -(x^3 - x) = x - x^3$. Therefore near $\dfrac{1}{2}$ we have $g'(x) = 1 - 3x^2$, and
$$
g'\!\left( \frac{1}{2} \right) = 1 - \frac{3}{4} = \frac{1}{4} .
$$
