For which $k$ is the junction at zero differentiable between $\sin{\left(2 x \right)}$ on the left and $3 x^{2}$ plus $kx$ on the right, with $f(0)=0$?

- $3$
- $0$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Both expressions tend to zero. The one-sided derivatives are $2$ and $k$, so they must coincide.
