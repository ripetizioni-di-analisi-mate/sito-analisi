What is the derivative of $\dfrac{x}{x^{2} + 4}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{x^{2} + 4}$
- $\dfrac{4 - x^{2}}{\left(x^{2} + 4\right)^{2}}$
- $- \dfrac{4 - x^{2}}{\left(x^{2} + 4\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{4 - x^{2}}{x^{2} + 4}$

## Explanation

The numerator in the quotient rule simplifies to $4 - x^{2}$. Square the denominator.
