The function $f(x) = x^2 \ln x$ is the product of a power and a logarithm. Which function is its derivative for $x > 0$?

- $2x \ln x$
- $x$
- $2$
- $2x \ln x + x$

## Explanation

By the **product rule** with $u(x) = x^2$ and $v(x) = \ln x$ we have $f'(x) = u'(x)\,v(x) + u(x)\,v'(x)$. Since $u'(x) = 2x$ and $v'(x) = \dfrac{1}{x}$, we obtain
$$
f'(x) = 2x \ln x + x^2 \cdot \frac{1}{x} = 2x \ln x + x .
$$
