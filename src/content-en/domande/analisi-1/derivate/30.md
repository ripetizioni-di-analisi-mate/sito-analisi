For $f(x) = \ln x$ on the interval $[1, e]$, which point $c$ satisfies the conclusion of Lagrange's theorem?

- $c = e - 1$
- $c = \dfrac{1}{e - 1}$
- $c = \dfrac{e + 1}{2}$
- $c = \sqrt{e}$

## Explanation

The function $\ln x$ is continuous on $[1, e]$ and differentiable on $(1, e)$, so the hypotheses of **Lagrange's theorem** are satisfied. The slope of the line through the endpoints of the graph is
$$
\frac{\ln e - \ln 1}{e - 1} = \frac{1}{e - 1} .
$$
Since $f'(c) = \dfrac{1}{c}$, the conclusion $\dfrac{1}{c} = \dfrac{1}{e - 1}$ gives $c = e - 1$. The point belongs to $(1, e)$, because $e - 1 \approx 1.72$.
