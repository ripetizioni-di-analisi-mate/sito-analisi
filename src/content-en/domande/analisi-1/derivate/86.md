What is the ordered pair (left derivative, right derivative) at $3$ of $\left(x + 4\right) \left|{x - 3}\right|$?

- $\left( -7, \  7\right)$
- $\left( 7, \  -7\right)$
- $\left( 7, \  7\right)$
- $\left( -4, \  4\right)$

## Explanation

The difference quotient is $x + 4$ on the right and its negative on the left. Take the limits at $3$.
