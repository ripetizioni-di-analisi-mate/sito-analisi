The cube root $f(x) = \sqrt[3]{1 + x}$ is a power of $1 + x$ with exponent $\dfrac{1}{3}$. Which Maclaurin polynomial of order $2$ does it have?

- $1 + \dfrac{x}{3} - \dfrac{2x^2}{9}$
- $1 + \dfrac{x}{3} + \dfrac{x^2}{9}$
- $1 + \dfrac{x}{3}$
- $1 + \dfrac{x}{3} - \dfrac{x^2}{9}$

## Explanation

We write $f(x) = (1 + x)^{\alpha}$ with $\alpha = \dfrac{1}{3}$. We have $f'(x) = \alpha\,(1 + x)^{\alpha - 1}$ and $f''(x) = \alpha(\alpha - 1)\,(1 + x)^{\alpha - 2}$, so $f(0) = 1$, $f'(0) = \dfrac{1}{3}$ and $f''(0) = \dfrac{1}{3} \cdot \left( -\dfrac{2}{3} \right) = -\dfrac{2}{9}$. By **Taylor's formula** the coefficient of $x^2$ is $\dfrac{f''(0)}{2!} = -\dfrac{1}{9}$. Therefore
$$
T_2(x) = 1 + \frac{x}{3} - \frac{x^2}{9} .
$$
