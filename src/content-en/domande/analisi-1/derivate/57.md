The function $f(x) = 3^{x^2}$ is an exponential with base $3$ and exponent $x^2$. What is $f'(x)$?

- $\ln 3 \cdot 3^{x^2}$
- $2x \cdot 3^{x^2}$
- $x^2 \cdot 3^{x^2 - 1}$
- $2x \ln 3 \cdot 3^{x^2}$

## Explanation

The derivative of $a^{t}$ is $a^{t} \ln a$. By the **derivative of a composite function** with argument $t = x^2$, we multiply $3^{x^2} \ln 3$ by the derivative of the argument, which is $2x$. Therefore $f'(x) = 2x \ln 3 \cdot 3^{x^2}$.
