What is the derivative of $f(x) = \sin^2(3x)$?

- $6\sin(3x)\cos(3x)$
- $2\sin(3x)\cos(3x)$
- $-6\sin(3x)\cos(3x)$
- $6\sin(3x)$

## Explanation

The function is the composition of three functions: the square, the sine and $x \mapsto 3x$. By the **chain rule** applied repeatedly, from the outside in, we differentiate the square and obtain $2\sin(3x)$, then the sine and obtain $\cos(3x)$, and finally the argument $3x$, with derivative $3$. Multiplying the three factors we have
$$
f'(x) = 2\sin(3x) \cdot \cos(3x) \cdot 3 = 6\sin(3x)\cos(3x) .
$$
