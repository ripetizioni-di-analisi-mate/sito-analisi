Near $x = 0$, $\sin(2x)$ is approximated by a polynomial of degree at most $3$. Which Maclaurin polynomial of order $3$ is obtained?

- $2x - \dfrac{x^3}{3}$
- $2x - 8x^3$
- $2x - \dfrac{4}{3}\,x^3$
- $2x + \dfrac{4}{3}\,x^3$

## Explanation

We substitute $2x$ for $x$ in the expansion $\sin x = x - \dfrac{x^3}{6} + o(x^3)$. We obtain
$$
\sin(2x) = 2x - \frac{(2x)^3}{6} + o(x^3) = 2x - \frac{8x^3}{6} + o(x^3) .
$$
Therefore the polynomial of order $3$ is $2x - \dfrac{4}{3}\,x^3$.
