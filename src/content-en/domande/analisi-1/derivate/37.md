The numerator $\sin x - x \cos x$ is infinitesimal as $x \to 0$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x - x \cos x}{x^3}$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The numerator and the denominator tend to $0$. From the expansion $\cos x = 1 - \dfrac{x^2}{2} + o(x^2)$ and the rule $x \cdot o(x^2) = o(x^3)$ we obtain $x \cos x = x - \dfrac{x^3}{2} + o(x^3)$. With the expansion $\sin x = x - \dfrac{x^3}{6} + o(x^3)$ the numerator becomes
$$
\sin x - x \cos x = -\frac{x^3}{6} + \frac{x^3}{2} + o(x^3) = \frac{x^3}{3} + o(x^3) .
$$
Therefore the limit equals $\dfrac{1}{3}$.
