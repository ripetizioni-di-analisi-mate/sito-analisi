For the function $f(x) = x\,e^{-x}$, what is the equation of the horizontal tangent line to the graph?

- $y = e$
- $y = \dfrac{1}{e}$
- $y = -e$
- $y = 0$

## Explanation

The tangent line is horizontal at the points where $f'(x) = 0$. By the product rule and the chain rule we have $f'(x) = e^{-x} - x\,e^{-x} = (1 - x)\,e^{-x}$. Since $e^{-x} > 0$ for every $x$, the derivative vanishes only at $x = 1$. At that point $f(1) = e^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{e}$, so the tangent line is the line $y = \dfrac{1}{e}$.
