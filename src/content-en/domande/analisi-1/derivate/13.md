## Explanation

The denominator is $x^3$, so the logarithm is expanded up to order $3$ with the **Maclaurin expansion** $\ln(1 + x) = x - \dfrac{x^2}{2} + \dfrac{x^3}{3} + o(x^3)$. Substituting, the numerator becomes
$$
x - \left( x - \frac{x^2}{2} + \frac{x^3}{3} + o(x^3) \right) - \frac{x^2}{2} = -\frac{x^3}{3} + o(x^3) ,
$$
because the terms of first and second degree cancel. Since $\dfrac{o(x^3)}{x^3} \to 0$, the limit equals $-\dfrac{1}{3}$.
