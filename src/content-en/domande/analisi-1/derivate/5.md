- $y = x$
- $y = x + 1$
- $y = 2x$
- $y = 0$

## Explanation

The tangent line at the point with abscissa $x_0 = 0$ has equation $y = f(0) + f'(0)\,x$. We have $f(0) = 0 \cdot e^{0} = 0$. By the **product rule** $f'(x) = e^{x} + x\,e^{x} = (1 + x)\,e^{x}$, so $f'(0) = 1$. Therefore the tangent line is $y = 0 + 1 \cdot x$, that is, $y = x$.
