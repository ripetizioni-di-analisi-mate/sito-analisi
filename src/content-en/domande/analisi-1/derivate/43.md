What is the Maclaurin polynomial of order $2$ of $f(x) = e^{x} \cos x$?

- $1 + x + x^2$
- $1 + x + \dfrac{x^2}{2}$
- $1 + x - x^2$
- $1 + x$

## Explanation

As $x \to 0$ we have the expansions $e^{x} = 1 + x + \dfrac{x^2}{2} + o(x^2)$ and $\cos x = 1 - \dfrac{x^2}{2} + o(x^2)$. In the product the terms of degree $3$ and $4$ are $o(x^2)$, so
$$
e^{x} \cos x = 1 + x + \frac{x^2}{2} - \frac{x^2}{2} + o(x^2) = 1 + x + o(x^2) .
$$
The two second-degree terms cancel, therefore $T_2(x) = 1 + x$.
