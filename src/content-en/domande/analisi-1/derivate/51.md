What is the derivative of $e^{4 x} \sin{\left(5 x \right)}$?

- $5 e^{4 x} \cos{\left(5 x \right)}$
- $4 e^{4 x} \sin{\left(5 x \right)}$
- $- 4 e^{4 x} \sin{\left(5 x \right)} + 5 e^{4 x} \cos{\left(5 x \right)}$
- $4 e^{4 x} \sin{\left(5 x \right)} + 5 e^{4 x} \cos{\left(5 x \right)}$

## Explanation

The product rule gives one term from differentiating the exponential and another from differentiating the sine. Their sum is $4 e^{4 x} \sin{\left(5 x \right)} + 5 e^{4 x} \cos{\left(5 x \right)}$.
