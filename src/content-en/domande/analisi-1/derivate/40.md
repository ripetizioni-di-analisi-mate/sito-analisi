Let $f(x) = x\,e^{x}$. What is the value of the tenth derivative $f^{(10)}(0)$?

- $1$
- $0$
- $10!$
- $10$

## Explanation

By the product rule $f'(x) = e^{x} + x\,e^{x} = (x + 1)\,e^{x}$. Since the derivative of $(x + k)\,e^{x}$ is $e^{x} + (x + k)\,e^{x} = (x + k + 1)\,e^{x}$, at each differentiation the constant in the first factor increases by $1$, so $f^{(n)}(x) = (x + n)\,e^{x}$ for every $n \geq 1$. Therefore $f^{(10)}(x) = (x + 10)\,e^{x}$ and $f^{(10)}(0) = 10$.
