What is the equation of the tangent line to the graph of $f(x) = \ln x$ at the point with abscissa $x_0 = e$?

- $y = \dfrac{x}{e}$
- $y = \dfrac{x}{e} + 1$
- $y = e\,x - e^2 + 1$
- $y = x - e + 1$

## Explanation

The tangent line at the point with abscissa $e$ has equation $y = f(e) + f'(e)\,(x - e)$. We have $f(e) = \ln e = 1$ and, since $f'(x) = \dfrac{1}{x}$, the slope is $f'(e) = \dfrac{1}{e}$. Hence
$$
y = 1 + \frac{1}{e}\,(x - e) = 1 + \frac{x}{e} - 1 = \frac{x}{e} .
$$
The tangent line passes through the origin, because the term $1$ cancels with $-\dfrac{e}{e}$.
