For which value of $k$ is the line $y = kx$ tangent to the graph of $f(x) = e^{x}$?

- $k = e$
- $k = 1$
- $k = \dfrac{1}{e}$
- $k = e^2$

## Explanation

Since $f(x_0) = f'(x_0) = e^{x_0}$, the tangent line to the graph at the point with abscissa $x_0$ is $y = e^{x_0} + e^{x_0}\,(x - x_0)$. This line passes through the origin if and only if $e^{x_0} - x_0\,e^{x_0} = 0$, that is, $e^{x_0}\,(1 - x_0) = 0$. Since $e^{x_0} > 0$, the only solution is $x_0 = 1$. The tangent at the point with abscissa $1$ is $y = e + e\,(x - 1) = e\,x$, therefore $k = e$.
