What angle does the tangent line to the graph of $f(x) = \ln x$ at the point $(1, 0)$ form with the positive $x$-axis?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The slope of the tangent line at the point with abscissa $1$ is $f'(1)$. Since $f'(x) = \dfrac{1}{x}$, we have $f'(1) = 1$. The slope of a line is the tangent of the angle $\vartheta$ that the line forms with the positive $x$-axis, so $\tan \vartheta = 1$. Therefore $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
