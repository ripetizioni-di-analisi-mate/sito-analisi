The tangent line to the graph of $f(x) = x^3$ at the point with abscissa $1$ meets the graph at a second point. Which one?

- $(2, 8)$
- $(-2, -8)$
- $(-1, -1)$
- $(0, -2)$

## Explanation

Since $f(1) = 1$ and $f'(x) = 3x^2$, the tangent at the point with abscissa $1$ is $y = 1 + 3\,(x - 1) = 3x - 2$. The intersections with the graph are the solutions of $x^3 = 3x - 2$, that is, of $x^3 - 3x + 2 = 0$. The polynomial has the double root $x = 1$, which corresponds to the point of tangency, and it factors as
$$
x^3 - 3x + 2 = (x - 1)^2\,(x + 2) .
$$
Therefore the second intersection has abscissa $x = -2$ and ordinate $(-2)^3 = -8$.
