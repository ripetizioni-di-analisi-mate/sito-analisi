The derivatives of $f(x) = \tan x$ evaluated at $0$ determine its Maclaurin polynomial. What is the one of order $3$?

- $x + 2x^3$
- $x - \dfrac{x^3}{3}$
- $x + \dfrac{x^3}{3}$
- $x + \dfrac{x^3}{6}$

## Explanation

We have $f'(x) = 1 + \tan^2 x$, hence $f''(x) = 2\tan x\,(1 + \tan^2 x)$ and
$$
f'''(x) = 2\,(1 + \tan^2 x)^2 + 4\tan^2 x\,(1 + \tan^2 x) .
$$
At $x = 0$ we obtain $f(0) = 0$, $f'(0) = 1$, $f''(0) = 0$ and $f'''(0) = 2$. By **Taylor's formula** the polynomial is $x + \dfrac{2}{3!}\,x^3 = x + \dfrac{x^3}{3}$.
