What is the derivative of $e^{3 x} \sin{\left(4 x \right)}$?

- $3 e^{3 x} \sin{\left(4 x \right)}$
- $4 e^{3 x} \cos{\left(4 x \right)}$
- $3 e^{3 x} \sin{\left(4 x \right)} + 4 e^{3 x} \cos{\left(4 x \right)}$
- $- 3 e^{3 x} \sin{\left(4 x \right)} + 4 e^{3 x} \cos{\left(4 x \right)}$

## Explanation

The product rule gives one term from differentiating the exponential and another from differentiating the sine. Their sum is $3 e^{3 x} \sin{\left(4 x \right)} + 4 e^{3 x} \cos{\left(4 x \right)}$.
