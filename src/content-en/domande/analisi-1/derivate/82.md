The Maclaurin polynomial of order $4$ of a function $f$ is $T_4(x) = 1 - x^2 + \dfrac{x^4}{3}$. Which statement about the point $x = 0$ is true?

- $x = 0$ is a local maximum point
- $x = 0$ is a local minimum point
- $x = 0$ is not a stationary point
- $x = 0$ is a stationary point that is not an extremum

## Explanation

By **Taylor's formula** with Peano remainder we have $f(x) = 1 - x^2 + o(x^2)$ as $x \to 0$. The polynomial has no first-degree term, so $f'(0) = 0$ and $x = 0$ is a stationary point. Moreover $f(x) - f(0) = -x^2 + o(x^2) = x^2\,\bigl( -1 + o(1) \bigr)$, and the factor $-1 + o(1)$ is negative in a neighborhood of $0$. Therefore $f(x) \leq f(0)$ in a neighborhood of $0$, and $x = 0$ is a **local maximum point**.
