What is the ordered pair (left derivative, right derivative) at $2$ of $\left(x + 3\right) \left|{x - 2}\right|$?

- $\left( 5, \  5\right)$
- $\left( -5, \  5\right)$
- $\left( 5, \  -5\right)$
- $\left( -3, \  3\right)$

## Explanation

The difference quotient is $x + 3$ on the right and its negative on the left. Take the limits at $2$.
