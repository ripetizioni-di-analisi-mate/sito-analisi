What is the derivative of $f(x) = \arctan \sqrt{x}$ for $x > 0$?

- $\dfrac{1}{1 + x}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x}\,(1 + \sqrt{x})}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x}\,(1 + x)}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x}\,(1 + x)}$

## Explanation

The outer function is the arctangent, with derivative $\dfrac{1}{1 + t^2}$, and the argument is $t = \sqrt{x}$. Evaluated at $t = \sqrt{x}$, the outer derivative equals $\dfrac{1}{1 + (\sqrt{x})^2} = \dfrac{1}{1 + x}$. By the **chain rule** we multiply by the derivative of the argument, $\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}$, so
$$
f'(x) = \frac{1}{1 + x} \cdot \frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}\,(1 + x)} .
$$
