The function $f$ is differentiable on $\mathbb{R}$ and $x_0 = 2$ is a local maximum point of $f$. Which statement is certainly true?

- $f''(2) < 0$
- $f'(x) > 0$ for every $x < 2$
- $f'(2) = 0$
- $f(2) > 0$

## Explanation

The point $2$ is interior to the domain $\mathbb{R}$ and $f$ is differentiable at $2$, so by **Fermat's theorem** $f'(2) = 0$. The other statements may be false. The function $f(x) = -(x - 2)^4$ has a maximum at $2$, with $f''(2) = 0$ and $f(2) = 0$. The function $f(x) = \cos\bigl( \pi (x - 2) \bigr)$ has a local maximum at $2$ and is decreasing on the interval $[0, 1]$.
