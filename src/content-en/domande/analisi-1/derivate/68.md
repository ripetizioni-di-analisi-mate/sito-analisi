What is the coefficient of $x^2$ in the Maclaurin polynomial of $e^{3 x} \cos{\left(4 x \right)}$?

- $-7$
- $\dfrac{25}{2}$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{7}{2}$

## Explanation

The quadratic contributions from the exponential and cosine are $\dfrac{9}{2}$ and $-8$. The cosine has zero linear coefficient.
