As $x \to 0$, what is the order of the infinitesimal $\sin x - x$ with respect to $x$?

- $1$
- $3$
- $2$
- $5$

## Explanation

From the Maclaurin expansion $\sin x = x - \dfrac{x^3}{6} + o(x^3)$ we obtain $\sin x - x = -\dfrac{x^3}{6} + o(x^3)$. Therefore $\dfrac{\sin x - x}{x^3} \to -\dfrac{1}{6}$, a finite nonzero limit. The infinitesimal is therefore of order $3$ with respect to $x$.
