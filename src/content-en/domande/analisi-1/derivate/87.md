What is the ordered pair (left derivative, right derivative) at $4$ of $\left(x + 5\right) \left|{x - 4}\right|$?

- $\left( -5, \  5\right)$
- $\left( 9, \  -9\right)$
- $\left( -9, \  9\right)$
- $\left( 9, \  9\right)$

## Explanation

The difference quotient is $x + 5$ on the right and its negative on the left. Take the limits at $4$.
