For which $k$ is the junction at zero differentiable between $\sin{\left(4 x \right)}$ on the left and $5 x^{2}$ plus $kx$ on the right, with $f(0)=0$?

- $0$
- $-4$
- $5$
- $4$

## Explanation

Both expressions tend to zero. The one-sided derivatives are $4$ and $k$, so they must coincide.
