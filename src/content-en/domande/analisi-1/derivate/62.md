The line $y = 3x - 2$ is tangent to the graph of a differentiable function $f$ at the point with abscissa $x_0 = 1$. What are the values of $f(1)$ and $f'(1)$?

- $f(1) = 1$ and $f'(1) = 3$
- $f(1) = 3$ and $f'(1) = 1$
- $f(1) = -2$ and $f'(1) = 3$
- $f(1) = 1$ and $f'(1) = 1$

## Explanation

The tangent line at the point with abscissa $1$ is $y = f(1) + f'(1)\,(x - 1)$, so it has slope $f'(1)$ and passes through the point $(1, f(1))$. The slope of the line $y = 3x - 2$ is $3$, therefore $f'(1) = 3$. At $x = 1$ the line takes the value $3 - 2 = 1$, therefore $f(1) = 1$. The constant term $-2$ is the ordinate of the point where the line meets the $y$-axis.
