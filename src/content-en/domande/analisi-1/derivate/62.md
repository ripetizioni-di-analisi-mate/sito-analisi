For which $k$ is the junction at zero differentiable between $\sin{\left(3 x \right)}$ on the left and $4 x^{2}$ plus $kx$ on the right, with $f(0)=0$?

- $3$
- $-3$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

Both expressions tend to zero. The one-sided derivatives are $3$ and $k$, so they must coincide.
