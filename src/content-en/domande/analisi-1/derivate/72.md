Among all rectangles with area $16\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, what is the perimeter of the one with minimum perimeter?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $34\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

If one side measures $x > 0$, the other measures $\dfrac{16}{x}$ and the perimeter is $p(x) = 2x + \dfrac{32}{x}$. We have $p'(x) = 2 - \dfrac{32}{x^2}$, which is negative for $0 < x < 4$, zero at $x = 4$ and positive for $x > 4$. So $p$ has its absolute minimum at $x = 4$, that is, for the square of side $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. The minimum perimeter is $p(4) = 8 + 8 = 16\,\mathrm{cm}$.
