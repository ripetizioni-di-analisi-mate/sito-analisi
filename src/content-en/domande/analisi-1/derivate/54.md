For $x \neq 0$, what is the expression of the derivative of the cube root $f(x) = \sqrt[3]{x^2}$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3\sqrt[3]{x}}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\sqrt[3]{x^5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt[3]{x}}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\sqrt[3]{x}$

## Explanation

We write $f(x) = x^{\frac{2}{3}}$. By the rule $(x^{\alpha})' = \alpha\,x^{\alpha - 1}$ with $\alpha = \dfrac{2}{3}$ we have $f'(x) = \dfrac{2}{3}\,x^{-\frac{1}{3}}$, because $\dfrac{2}{3} - 1 = -\dfrac{1}{3}$. Since $x^{-\frac{1}{3}} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt[3]{x}}$, the derivative is $\dfrac{2}{3\sqrt[3]{x}}$.
