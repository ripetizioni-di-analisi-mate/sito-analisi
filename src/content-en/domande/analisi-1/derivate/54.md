What is the derivative of $\dfrac{x}{x^{2} + 5}$?

- $\dfrac{5 - x^{2}}{x^{2} + 5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x^{2} + 5}$
- $\dfrac{5 - x^{2}}{\left(x^{2} + 5\right)^{2}}$
- $- \dfrac{5 - x^{2}}{\left(x^{2} + 5\right)^{2}}$

## Explanation

The numerator in the quotient rule simplifies to $5 - x^{2}$. Square the denominator.
