The polynomial $f(x) = (x - 1)(x - 2)(x - 3)$ vanishes at $x = 1$. What is the slope of its graph at that point?

- $0$
- $2$
- $-2$
- $6$

## Explanation

We write $f(x) = (x - 1)\,g(x)$ with $g(x) = (x - 2)(x - 3)$. By the **product rule** we have $f'(x) = g(x) + (x - 1)\,g'(x)$. For $x = 1$ the second term vanishes, so $f'(1) = g(1) = (1 - 2)(1 - 3) = 2$. The value $f(1) = 0$ gives no information about the derivative.
