What is the smallest bound obtained for the first-order Maclaurin remainder of $e^{2 x}$ at $\dfrac{1}{2}$ using the maximum of $|f\prime\prime|$ on the entire interval?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $e$
- $\dfrac{e}{6}$
- $\dfrac{e}{2}$

## Explanation

The second derivative is increasing and equals $4 e$ at the right endpoint. Multiply by $\dfrac{1}{8}$ in the Lagrange remainder.
