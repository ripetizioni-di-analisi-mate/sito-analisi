What is the derivative of $e^{2 x} \sin{\left(3 x \right)}$?

- $2 e^{2 x} \sin{\left(3 x \right)}$
- $2 e^{2 x} \sin{\left(3 x \right)} + 3 e^{2 x} \cos{\left(3 x \right)}$
- $3 e^{2 x} \cos{\left(3 x \right)}$
- $- 2 e^{2 x} \sin{\left(3 x \right)} + 3 e^{2 x} \cos{\left(3 x \right)}$

## Explanation

The product rule gives one term from differentiating the exponential and another from differentiating the sine. Their sum is $2 e^{2 x} \sin{\left(3 x \right)} + 3 e^{2 x} \cos{\left(3 x \right)}$.
