The function $f$ is differentiable on $\mathbb{R}$, with $f(0) = 1$ and $|f'(x)| \leq 2$ for every $x$. What is the smallest interval that certainly contains $f(3)$?

- $[1, 7]$
- $[-5, 7]$
- $[-1, 3]$
- $[-6, 6]$

## Explanation

By the **mean value theorem** on the interval $[0, 3]$ there exists $c \in (0, 3)$ with $f(3) - f(0) = 3\,f'(c)$. Since $|f'(c)| \leq 2$, we have $|f(3) - 1| \leq 6$, that is, $-5 \leq f(3) \leq 7$. The functions $f(x) = 1 - 2x$ and $f(x) = 1 + 2x$ satisfy the hypotheses and take the values $-5$ and $7$ at $3$, so no interval smaller than $[-5, 7]$ certainly contains $f(3)$.
