What is the coefficient of $x^4$ in the Maclaurin polynomial of $\ln{\left(3 x^{2} + 1 \right)}$?

- $-9$
- $- \dfrac{9}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Substitute $3 x^{2}$ in the logarithm expansion. The fourth-degree term comes from the quadratic term in that argument.
