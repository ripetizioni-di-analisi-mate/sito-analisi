The function $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{1 + x}$ is defined for $x \neq -1$. Which polynomial $T_3$ approximates it near $0$ with remainder $o(x^3)$?

- $1 + x + x^2 + x^3$
- $1 - x + x^2 - x^3$
- $1 - x + \dfrac{x^2}{2} - \dfrac{x^3}{6}$
- $1 - x$

## Explanation

We substitute $t = -x$ in the expansion $\dfrac{1}{1 - t} = 1 + t + t^2 + t^3 + o(t^3)$. Since $(-x)^k = (-1)^k x^k$, we obtain
$$
\frac{1}{1 + x} = 1 - x + x^2 - x^3 + o(x^3) .
$$
Therefore the polynomial of order $3$ is $1 - x + x^2 - x^3$, with coefficients of alternating signs.
