The functions $f$ and $g$ are differentiable on $\mathbb{R}$, with $g(1) = 2$, $g'(1) = 3$, $f'(1) = 5$ and $f'(2) = -1$. What is the value of the derivative of $f \circ g$ at the point $x = 1$?

- $15$
- $-1$
- $-3$
- $5$

## Explanation

By the **derivative of a composite function** we have $(f \circ g)'(1) = f'\bigl(g(1)\bigr) \cdot g'(1)$. Since $g(1) = 2$, the derivative of $f$ must be evaluated at the point $2$, where it equals $f'(2) = -1$. Therefore $(f \circ g)'(1) = -1 \cdot 3 = -3$. The datum $f'(1) = 5$ plays no role in the computation.
