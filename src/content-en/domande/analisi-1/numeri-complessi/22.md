For $z = 2 - 5i$, which equality is true?

- $z\, \bar{z} = -21$
- $z\, \bar{z} = 29$
- $z - \bar{z} = -10$
- $z + \bar{z} = 4i$

## Explanation

The conjugate of $z$ is $\bar{z} = 2 + 5i$. By the identity $z\bar{z} = |z|^2$ one has $z\, \bar{z} = 2^2 + 5^2 = 29$, which is a positive real number. The value $-21 = 4 - 25$ comes from writing $(5i)^2 = 25$ by mistake. Moreover $z - \bar{z} = 2i \operatorname{Im}(z) = -10i$ and $z + \bar{z} = 2 \operatorname{Re}(z) = 4$, so the last two equalities are false.
