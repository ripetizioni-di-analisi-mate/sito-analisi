Which set of the complex plane do the numbers $z \neq 0$ with principal argument $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ form?

- The line $y = x$
- The half-line $y = x$ with $x > 0$
- The half-line $y = x$ with $x < 0$
- The half-line $y = -x$ with $x > 0$

## Explanation

A number $z \neq 0$ with argument $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ can be written $z = \rho\left(\cos\dfrac{\pi}{4} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)$ with $\rho > 0$, so $x = y = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\rho > 0$. As $\rho > 0$ varies one obtains all the points of the half-line $y = x$ with $x > 0$, origin excluded. The points with $y = x$ and $x < 0$ have instead principal argument $-\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$.
