Let $z$ and $w$ be complex numbers with $|z| = 2$, $\arg z = \dfrac{\pi}{5}$, $|w| = 3$ and $\arg w = \dfrac{3\pi}{10}$. What is $zw$?

- $6i$
- $5i$
- $6\, e^{-i \frac{\pi}{10}}$
- $6$

## Explanation

By the product formula in trigonometric form the modulus of $zw$ is $|z|\, |w| = 6$ and one of its arguments is
$$
\frac{\pi}{5} + \frac{3\pi}{10} = \frac{2\pi}{10} + \frac{3\pi}{10} = \frac{\pi}{2} .
$$
Hence
$$
zw = 6 \left( \cos\frac{\pi}{2} + i \sin\frac{\pi}{2} \right) = 6i .
$$
