What is the minimum degree of a polynomial with real coefficients that has among its roots $i$, $1 + i$ and $2$?

- $3$
- $4$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

The non-real roots of a polynomial with real coefficients come in conjugate pairs, so together with $i$ and $1 + i$ also $-i$ and $1 - i$ are roots. The polynomial therefore has at least the five distinct roots $i$, $-i$, $1 + i$, $1 - i$, $2$, and by the fundamental theorem of algebra its degree is at least $5$. Degree $5$ is attained by $\left(z^2 + 1\right)\left(z^2 - 2z + 2\right)(z - 2)$, which has real coefficients.
