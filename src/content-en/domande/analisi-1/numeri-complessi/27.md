Which set in the complex plane is described by the inequality $|z + 2| < |z|$?

- The half-plane $\operatorname{Re}(z) > -1$
- The half-plane $\operatorname{Re}(z) < -1$
- The half-plane $\operatorname{Re}(z) < -2$
- The half-plane $\operatorname{Im}(z) < -1$

## Explanation

The left-hand side is the distance of $z$ from the point $-2$ and the right-hand side is the distance of $z$ from the origin, so the inequality describes the points closer to $-2$ than to $0$. Setting $z = x + iy$, both sides are nonnegative, therefore the inequality is equivalent to the one between their squares
$$
(x + 2)^2 + y^2 < x^2 + y^2 .
$$
Simplifying one obtains $4x + 4 < 0$, that is $x < -1$. The set is therefore the half-plane $\operatorname{Re}(z) < -1$, bounded by the perpendicular bisector of the segment with endpoints $-2$ and $0$.
