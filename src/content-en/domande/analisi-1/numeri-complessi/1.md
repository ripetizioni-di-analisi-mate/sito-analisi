- $8 - 3i$
- $-4 - 3i$

## Explanation

Expanding the product and using $i^2 = -1$ one obtains
$$
(2 + 3i)(1 - 2i) = 2 - 4i + 3i - 6i^2 = 8 - i .
$$
By the square of a binomial $(1 + i)^2 = 1 + 2i + i^2$, and since $i^2 = -1$ one has $(1 + i)^2 = 2i$. Therefore
$$
z = 8 - i - 2i = 8 - 3i .
$$
