What is the imaginary part of $z = (3 - 2i)^2$?

- $-12$
- $-12\, i$
- $5$
- $-6$

## Explanation

By the square of a binomial $(3 - 2i)^2 = 9 - 12i + 4i^2$, and since $i^2 = -1$ one obtains $z = 5 - 12i$. The imaginary part of $a + ib$ is the real number $b$, so $\operatorname{Im}(z) = -12$. The expression $-12i$ is the term $ib$ of the algebraic form, while $5$ is the real part of $z$.
