What is the imaginary part of $z = (4 - i) - 2(1 - 3i)$?

- $5$
- $5i$
- $2$
- $-7$

## Explanation

Since $-2(1 - 3i) = -2 + 6i$, one adds the real parts and the imaginary parts separately and obtains $z = (4 - 2) + (-1 + 6)\,i = 2 + 5i$. The imaginary part is the real number that multiplies $i$, therefore $\operatorname{Im}(z) = 5$.
