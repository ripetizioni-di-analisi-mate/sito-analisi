The complex number $z$ has modulus $2$. What is $|z - 1|^2 + |z + 1|^2$?

- $10$
- $8$
- $6$
- The value depends on the argument of $z$

## Explanation

By the identity $|w|^2 = w\bar{w}$ one has
$$
\begin{gathered}
|z - 1|^2 = (z - 1)(\bar{z} - 1) = |z|^2 - (z + \bar{z}) + 1, \\[4pt]
|z + 1|^2 = (z + 1)(\bar{z} + 1) = |z|^2 + (z + \bar{z}) + 1 .
\end{gathered}
$$
Adding, the terms $z + \bar{z}$ cancel and one obtains $2|z|^2 + 2$. Since $|z| = 2$, the sum equals $2 \cdot 4 + 2 = 10$ for every $z$ of modulus $2$.
