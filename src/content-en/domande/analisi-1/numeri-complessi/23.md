For $z = 1 + 2i$, what is the real part of $\dfrac{z}{\bar{z}}$?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $1$
- $-\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The numerator and the denominator are multiplied by $z$, which is the conjugate of $\bar{z}$. Since $\bar{z}\, z = |z|^2 = 5$, one obtains
$$
\frac{z}{\bar{z}} = \frac{z^2}{|z|^2} = \frac{(1 + 2i)^2}{5} = \frac{1 + 4i - 4}{5} = -\frac{3}{5} + \frac{4}{5}\, i .
$$
Therefore the real part of $\dfrac{z}{\bar{z}}$ is $-\dfrac{3}{5}$.
