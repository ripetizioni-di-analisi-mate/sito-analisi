For $w = 3 - 2i$, what is the algebraic form of $\overline{iw}$?

- $2 + 3i$
- $-2 + 3i$
- $2 - 3i$
- $-2 - 3i$

## Explanation

Since $i^2 = -1$, the product is $iw = 3i - 2i^2 = 2 + 3i$. The conjugate is obtained by changing the sign of the imaginary part, so $\overline{iw} = 2 - 3i$. The same result follows from the property $\overline{iw} = \bar{i}\,\bar{w} = -i(3 + 2i) = 2 - 3i$.
