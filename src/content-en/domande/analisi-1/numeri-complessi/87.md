Which $z=x+iy$ satisfy $\operatorname{Im}(z^2)=0$ and $\operatorname{Re}(z^2)<-16$?

- $x=0,\ 0<|y|<4$
- $x=0,\ |y|\geq 4$
- $y=0,\ |x|>4$
- $x=0,\ |y|>4$

## Explanation

The equation $2xy=0$ puts the point on an axis. On the imaginary axis the square is $-y^2$, so we require $|y|>4$.
