- $\dfrac{5}{3} - \dfrac{10}{3}\, i$
- $\dfrac{11}{5} - 2i$
- $2 - 3i$

## Explanation

The numerator and the denominator are multiplied by the conjugate of the denominator, that is by $2 - i$. The denominator becomes $(2 + i)(2 - i) = 4 - i^2 = 5$, which is a real number. The numerator becomes
$$
(4 - 3i)(2 - i) = 8 - 4i - 6i + 3i^2 = 5 - 10i .
$$
So $z = \dfrac{5 - 10i}{5} = 1 - 2i$.
