- $z = 3 + 3i$ and $z = -3 + 3i$
- $z = 3i$ and $z = -3i$
- The equation has no solutions
- $z = 3i$

## Explanation

Setting $z = x + iy$ with $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$, one has $|z|^2 = x^2 + y^2$ and $z - \bar{z} = 2iy$. The equation becomes $x^2 + y^2 + 2iy = 9 + 6i$, so it is equivalent to the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 + y^2 = 9 \\[4pt]
2y = 6
\end{cases}
$$
From the second equation one obtains $y = 3$. Substituting into the first one obtains $x^2 = 0$, that is $x = 0$, therefore the only solution is $z = 3i$.
