For which value of the real parameter $k$ are the solutions of the equation $z^2 + 2z + k = 0$ non-real complex numbers with modulus $\sqrt{5}$?

- $k = \sqrt{5}$
- $k = 4$
- $k = 25$
- $k = 5$

## Explanation

The discriminant is $\Delta = 4 - 4k$, and the solutions are non-real complex numbers if and only if $\Delta < 0$, that is for $k > 1$. In that case the solutions are
$$
z = \frac{-2 \pm i\sqrt{4k - 4}}{2} = -1 \pm i\sqrt{k - 1} .
$$
The square of their modulus is $1 + (k - 1) = k$, so the condition $|z| = \sqrt{5}$ is equivalent to $k = 5$. The value $k = 5$ satisfies $k > 1$, therefore it is valid, and the solutions are $-1 \pm 2i$.
