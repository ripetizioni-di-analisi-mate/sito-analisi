What is the exponential form, with the principal argument, of $z = \dfrac{6\, e^{i \frac{5\pi}{6}}}{2\, e^{-i \frac{\pi}{3}}}$?

- $3\, e^{i \frac{\pi}{2}}$
- $3\, e^{-i \frac{5\pi}{6}}$
- $12\, e^{i \frac{\pi}{2}}$
- $4\, e^{-i \frac{5\pi}{6}}$

## Explanation

In a quotient the moduli are divided and the arguments are subtracted, so the modulus of $z$ is $\dfrac{6}{2} = 3$ and an argument of $z$ is
$$
\frac{5\pi}{6} - \left(-\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \frac{5\pi}{6} + \frac{2\pi}{6} = \frac{7\pi}{6} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{7\pi}{6}$ does not belong to the interval $(-\pi, \pi]$, therefore $2\pi$ is subtracted and one obtains the principal argument $-\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$. Hence $z = 3\, e^{-i \frac{5\pi}{6}}$.
