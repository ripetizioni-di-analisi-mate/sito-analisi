Which set in the complex plane is described by the equation $\operatorname{Re}\left(z^2\right) = 0$?

- The imaginary axis
- Only the line $y = x$
- The two lines $y = x$ and $y = -x$
- The circle with center at the origin and radius $1$

## Explanation

Setting $z = x + iy$, one has $z^2 = x^2 - y^2 + 2ixy$, so $\operatorname{Re}\left(z^2\right) = x^2 - y^2$. The equation becomes $x^2 - y^2 = (x - y)(x + y) = 0$, which is satisfied if and only if $y = x$ or $y = -x$. Therefore the set is the union of the two bisectors of the quadrants.
