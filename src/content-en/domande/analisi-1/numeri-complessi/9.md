- $z = \pm 2$ and $z = \pm i\sqrt{2}$
- $z = \pm 2$
- $z = \pm 2i$ and $z = \pm \sqrt{2}$
- $z = \pm 2$ and $z = \pm \sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

Setting $t = z^2$, the equation becomes $t^2 - 2t - 8 = (t - 4)(t + 2) = 0$, so $t = 4$ or $t = -2$. From the equation $z^2 = 4$ one obtains $z = \pm 2$. The complex numbers whose square is $-2$ are $\pm i\sqrt{2}$, therefore from the equation $z^2 = -2$ one obtains $z = \pm i\sqrt{2}$. There are four solutions, in agreement with the **fundamental theorem of algebra** for a polynomial of degree $4$.
