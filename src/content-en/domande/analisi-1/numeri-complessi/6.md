How many complex numbers are both sixth roots and fourth roots of unity, that is, solutions of both $z^6 = 1$ and $z^4 = 1$?

- $1$
- $4$
- $2$
- $10$

## Explanation

Let $z$ be a solution of both equations. Since $z^4 = 1$, the number $z$ is nonzero and the first equation can be divided by the second:
$$
z^2 = \frac{z^6}{z^4} = \frac{1}{1} = 1 .
$$
Therefore $z = 1$ or $z = -1$. Conversely, $1$ and $-1$ have sixth power and fourth power equal to $1$, because the exponents $6$ and $4$ are even. Hence the required numbers are exactly $1$ and $-1$, that is, $2$.
