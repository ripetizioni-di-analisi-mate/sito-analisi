Among the cube roots of $w = -27i$, which one has positive real part?

- $\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} + \dfrac{3}{2}\, i$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} - \dfrac{1}{2}\, i$
- $\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} - \dfrac{3}{2}\, i$
- $\dfrac{3}{2} - \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\, i$

## Explanation

The number $w$ has modulus $27$ and lies on the negative imaginary semi-axis, so $w = 27\, e^{-i \frac{\pi}{2}}$. By the formula for the **$n$-th roots** the cube roots are
$$
z_k = 3\, e^{\,i \left( -\frac{\pi}{6} + \frac{2k\pi}{3} \right)}, \qquad k = 0, 1, 2,
$$
that is the numbers with modulus $3$ and arguments $-\dfrac{\pi}{6}$, $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $\dfrac{7\pi}{6}$. Among these arguments only $-\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ has positive cosine, so the only root with positive real part is
$$
z_0 = 3 \left( \cos\frac{\pi}{6} - i \sin\frac{\pi}{6} \right) = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} - \frac{3}{2}\, i .
$$
