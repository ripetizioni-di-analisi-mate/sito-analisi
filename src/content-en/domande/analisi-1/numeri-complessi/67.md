What is the center-radius triple $(x_C,y_C,r)$ for $|2z-(4 + 6 i)|=2$?

- $\left( 2, \  3, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  2, \  1\right)$
- $\left( -2, \  -3, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  3, \  2\right)$

## Explanation

Divide by $2$. The modulus measures the distance from $2 + 3 i$, and the radius is $1$.
