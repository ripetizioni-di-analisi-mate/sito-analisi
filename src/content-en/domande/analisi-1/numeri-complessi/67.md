Among the following numbers, only one has modulus $1$. Which one is it?

- $\dfrac{1 + i}{2}$
- $e^{i\frac{\pi}{7}}$
- $1 + i$
- $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{7} + \sin\dfrac{\pi}{7}$

## Explanation

By Euler's formula $e^{i\frac{\pi}{7}} = \cos\dfrac{\pi}{7} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{7}$, so its modulus is
$$
\sqrt{\cos^2\frac{\pi}{7} + \sin^2\frac{\pi}{7}} = 1 .
$$
The numbers $1 + i$ and $\dfrac{1 + i}{2}$ have modulus $\sqrt{2}$ and $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$. The number $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{7} + \sin\dfrac{\pi}{7}$ is real and positive, and its square equals $1 + 2\sin\dfrac{\pi}{7}\cos\dfrac{\pi}{7} = 1 + \sin\dfrac{2\pi}{7} > 1$, therefore its modulus is greater than $1$.
