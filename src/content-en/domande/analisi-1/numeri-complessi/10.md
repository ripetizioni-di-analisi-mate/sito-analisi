- $\pm \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} - \dfrac{1}{2}\, i$ and $i$
- $\pm \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} + \dfrac{1}{2}\, i$ and $-i$
- $\pm \dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\, i$ and $-i$
- $\pm \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} + \dfrac{1}{2}\, i$ and $i$

## Explanation

The number $i$ has modulus $1$ and argument $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, so $i = e^{i \frac{\pi}{2}}$. By the formula for the **$n$-th roots** the solutions are
$$
z_k = e^{\,i \left( \frac{\pi}{6} + \frac{2k\pi}{3} \right)}, \qquad k = 0, 1, 2,
$$
with arguments $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$, $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$ and $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$. In algebraic form one obtains $z_0 = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} + \dfrac{1}{2}\, i$, $z_1 = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} + \dfrac{1}{2}\, i$ and $z_2 = -i$.
