What is the exponential form, with the principal argument, of $z = 3 - 3\sqrt{3}\, i$?

- $6\, e^{-i \frac{\pi}{6}}$
- $36\, e^{-i \frac{\pi}{3}}$
- $6\, e^{-i \frac{\pi}{3}}$
- $6\, e^{i \frac{2\pi}{3}}$

## Explanation

The modulus is $\rho = \sqrt{9 + 27} = \sqrt{36} = 6$. Since $a = 3 > 0$ and $b = -3\sqrt{3} < 0$, the number lies in the fourth quadrant. Since $a > 0$, the principal argument coincides with the arctangent of $\dfrac{b}{a}$, with no correction:
$$
\theta = \arctan\frac{-3\sqrt{3}}{3} = \arctan\left(-\sqrt{3}\right) = -\frac{\pi}{3} .
$$
Hence $z = 6\, e^{-i \frac{\pi}{3}}$.
