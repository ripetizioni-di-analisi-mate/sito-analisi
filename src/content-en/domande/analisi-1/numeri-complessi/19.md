A spreadsheet calculates $u=\dfrac{z}{w}$ with $w\ne0$. Given $|z|=6$ and $|w|=3$, which proposed result can be ruled out from its modulus alone?

- $-2i$, because its modulus would be $4$.
- $-2$
- $1+i$
- $\sqrt3+i$

## Explanation

The quotient must have modulus $\dfrac{6}{3}=2$. The modulus of $1+i$ is $\sqrt2$. All three other proposals have modulus $2$, so this check does not distinguish between them.
