What is the algebraic form of the reciprocal of $z = 3 - 4i$?

- $\dfrac{3}{5} + \dfrac{4}{5}\, i$
- $\dfrac{3}{25} - \dfrac{4}{25}\, i$
- $\dfrac{3}{25} + \dfrac{4}{25}\, i$
- $\dfrac{1}{3} - \dfrac{1}{4}\, i$

## Explanation

By the reciprocal formula one has $\dfrac{1}{z} = \dfrac{\bar{z}}{|z|^2}$. The conjugate of $z$ is $\bar{z} = 3 + 4i$ and the square of the modulus is $|z|^2 = 9 + 16 = 25$. Hence
$$
\frac{1}{3 - 4i} = \frac{3 + 4i}{25} = \frac{3}{25} + \frac{4}{25}\, i .
$$
The denominator is $|z|^2 = 25$, while the modulus $|z| = 5$ would give a number of modulus $1$.
