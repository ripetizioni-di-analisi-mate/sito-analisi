What is the triple $(x_C,y_C,r)$ giving the center and radius of the locus $|3z-(9 + 15 i)|=6$?

- $\left( 3, \  5, \  2\right)$
- $\left( 5, \  3, \  2\right)$
- $\left( -3, \  -5, \  2\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  5, \  6\right)$

## Explanation

After dividing by $3$, the modulus measures the distance from $3 + 5 i$, and the radius is $2$.
