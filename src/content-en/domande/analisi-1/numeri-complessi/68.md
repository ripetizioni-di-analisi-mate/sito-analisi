The number $z$ satisfies $z^2=-9$ and has negative imaginary part. Which movement of $z$ in the complex plane represents multiplication by $i$?

- From the negative imaginary axis to the positive real axis.
- From the negative imaginary axis to the negative real axis.
- From the negative real axis to the positive imaginary axis.
- From the positive imaginary axis to the positive real axis.

## Explanation

The sign of the imaginary part selects $z=-3i$. Multiplication by $i$ rotates counterclockwise through a right angle and gives $i(-3i)=3$, on the positive real axis.
