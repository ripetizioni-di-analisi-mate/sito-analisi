Which complex numbers have square equal to $-9$?

- $3i$ and $-3i$
- $3$ and $-3$
- $9i$ and $-9i$
- No complex number

## Explanation

The complex numbers whose square is the negative real number $t$ are $\pm i\sqrt{-t}$. With $t = -9$ one obtains $z = \pm i\sqrt{9} = \pm 3i$. The two solutions are conjugate, as happens for every quadratic equation with real coefficients and negative discriminant.
