With the substitution $w = z - 1$ the equation $(z - 1)^3 = 8$ becomes $w^3 = 8$. Which complex numbers $z$ solve it?

- $z = 3$
- $z = 3$ and $z = \pm i\sqrt{3}$
- $z = 2$ and $z = -1 \pm i\sqrt{3}$
- $z = 3$ and $z = 1 \pm i\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Setting $w = z - 1$, the equation becomes $w^3 = 8$, whose solutions are the cube roots of $8$, that is $w_k = 2\, e^{\,i\frac{2k\pi}{3}}$ with $k = 0, 1, 2$. Hence $w_0 = 2$ and
$$
w_{1,2} = 2\left(-\frac{1}{2} \pm \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\, i\right) = -1 \pm i\sqrt{3} .
$$
Since $z = w + 1$, the solutions are $z = 3$ and $z = \pm i\sqrt{3}$.
