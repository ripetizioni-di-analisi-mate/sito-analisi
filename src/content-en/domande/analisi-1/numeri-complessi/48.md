Which of the following products is a purely imaginary number?

- $(1 + 2i)(1 - 2i)$
- $(2 + i)^2$
- $(1 + i)(1 + 2i)$
- $(1 + 2i)(2 + i)$

## Explanation

A number is purely imaginary if its real part is zero and its imaginary part is nonzero. Expanding the products and replacing $i^2 = -1$ gives
$$
\begin{gathered}
(1 + 2i)(2 + i) = 2 + i + 4i + 2i^2 = 5i, \qquad (1 + 2i)(1 - 2i) = 1 - 4i^2 = 5, \\[6pt]
(2 + i)^2 = 4 + 4i + i^2 = 3 + 4i, \qquad (1 + i)(1 + 2i) = 1 + 2i + i + 2i^2 = -1 + 3i .
\end{gathered}
$$
Therefore the only purely imaginary product is $(1 + 2i)(2 + i) = 5i$.
