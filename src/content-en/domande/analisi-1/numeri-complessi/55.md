For every complex number $z$ with $|z| = 1$, which number is the reciprocal $\dfrac{1}{z}$ equal to?

- $z$
- $\bar{z}$
- $-\bar{z}$
- $-z$

## Explanation

Since $|z| = 1$, the number $z$ is nonzero and the reciprocal exists. By the property of the modulus $z\bar{z} = |z|^2 = 1$. Dividing by $z$ gives $\dfrac{1}{z} = \bar{z}$, which is the reciprocal formula $\dfrac{1}{z} = \dfrac{\bar{z}}{|z|^2}$ in the case $|z| = 1$. The other equalities do not hold for every $z$ of modulus $1$. For $z = i$ one has $\dfrac{1}{z} = -i$, while $z = -\bar{z} = i$; for $z = 1$ one has $\dfrac{1}{z} = 1$, while $-z = -1$.
