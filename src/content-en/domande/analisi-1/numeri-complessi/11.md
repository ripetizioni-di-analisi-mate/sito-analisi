- $2 - i$
- $2 - \dfrac{1}{5}\, i$
- $2 + i$
- $-10 - \dfrac{1}{5}\, i$

## Explanation

The equation contains $\bar{z}$, so one sets $z = x + iy$ with $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$ and substitutes $\bar{z} = x - iy$. The left-hand side becomes $2(x + iy) + 3(x - iy) = 5x - iy$, therefore the equation is equivalent to the system
$$
\begin{cases}
5x = 10 \\[4pt]
-y = -1
\end{cases}
$$
which has solution $x = 2$, $y = 1$. Hence $z = 2 + i$.
