With $z=x+iy$, which expression for $y$ describes $|z-4|=|z-7i|$?

- $- \dfrac{4 x}{7} - \dfrac{33}{14}$
- $\dfrac{4 x}{7} + \dfrac{33}{14}$
- $\dfrac{4 x}{7} - \dfrac{9}{14}$
- $\dfrac{4 x}{7} + \dfrac{75}{14}$

## Explanation

Squaring both moduli gives $- 8 x + 14 y - 33=0$. Solve for $y$.
