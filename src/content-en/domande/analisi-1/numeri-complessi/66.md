What is the sum of the five fifth roots of $w = 2 + 3i$?

- $2 + 3i$
- $5$
- $-2 - 3i$
- $0$

## Explanation

The five roots are obtained from one of them, $z_0$, by rotating it repeatedly by $\dfrac{2\pi}{5}$, so they are $z_0 q^k$ with $q = e^{i\frac{2\pi}{5}}$ and $k = 0, \dots, 4$. Since $q \neq 1$, by the sum of the geometric progression
$$
z_0\left(1 + q + q^2 + q^3 + q^4\right) = z_0\, \frac{1 - q^5}{1 - q} .
$$
Since $q^5 = e^{2\pi i} = 1$, the sum equals $0$. In the complex plane the roots are the vertices of a regular pentagon centered at the origin.
