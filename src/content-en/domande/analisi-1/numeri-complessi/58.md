What is the principal argument, in $(-\pi, \pi]$, of $z = -5i$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\pi$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The number $-5i$ has real part $0$ and imaginary part $-5 < 0$, so in the complex plane it lies on the negative imaginary semi-axis. For a number with zero real part and negative imaginary part the principal argument is $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Indeed
$$
-5i = 5\left(\cos\left(-\frac{\pi}{2}\right) + i\sin\left(-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)\right).
$$
