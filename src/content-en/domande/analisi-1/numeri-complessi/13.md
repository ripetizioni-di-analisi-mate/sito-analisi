- The circle with center $(1, -2)$ and radius $3$
- The circle with center $(-1, 2)$ and radius $9$
- The circle with center $(-1, -2)$ and radius $3$

## Explanation

In the complex plane the modulus $|z - z_0|$ is the distance of the point $z$ from the point $z_0$. Since $z + 1 - 2i = z - (-1 + 2i)$, the equation is equivalent to $|z - (-1 + 2i)| = 3$. The points that satisfy it are therefore those at distance $3$ from the point $-1 + 2i$, that is from the point $(-1, 2)$. Therefore the locus is the circle with center $(-1, 2)$ and radius $3$.
