The figure shows the numbers $z = 4 + i$ and $w = -1 + 2i$ as vectors starting at $0$. Which number corresponds to the fourth vertex of the parallelogram whose sides are these two vectors?

::grafico{xmin=-2 xmax=5 ymin=-1 ymax=4 uguale=1 nomi="Re,Im" vettori="0,0,4,1,z;0,0,-1,2,w" legenda=0 titolo="The vectors of the numbers $z = 4 + i$ and $w = -1 + 2i$"}

- $5 - i$
- $\dfrac{3}{2} + \dfrac{3}{2}\, i$
- $3 + 3i$
- $-5 + i$

## Explanation

By the **parallelogram rule**, the vertex opposite the origin in the parallelogram with sides $z$ and $w$ represents the sum $z + w$. The sum is computed by adding real parts and imaginary parts separately:
$$
z + w = (4 - 1) + i(1 + 2) = 3 + 3i .
$$
The number $\dfrac{3}{2} + \dfrac{3}{2}\, i$ is the midpoint of the diagonal, that is, the center of the parallelogram, while $5 - i$ and $-5 + i$ are the differences $z - w$ and $w - z$.
