With $z=x+iy$, which expression for $y$ describes $|z-3|=|z-5i|$?

- $\dfrac{3 x}{5} + \dfrac{18}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3 x}{5} + \dfrac{8}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3 x}{5} - \dfrac{2}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{3 x}{5} - \dfrac{8}{5}$

## Explanation

Squaring both moduli gives $- 6 x + 10 y - 16=0$, from which one solves for $y$.
