The six sixth roots of $w = 5 - 2i$ are the vertices of a regular hexagon centered at the origin. What is the angle, with vertex at the origin, formed by two consecutive roots?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{12}$

## Explanation

The roots are $z_k = \sqrt[6]{|w|}\; e^{\,i\frac{\varphi + 2k\pi}{6}}$ with $k = 0, \dots, 5$, where $\varphi$ is an argument of $w$. Going from $k$ to $k + 1$ the argument increases by $\dfrac{2\pi}{6} = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$, whatever $\varphi$ is. Therefore two consecutive roots form an angle of $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, and the result does not depend on $w$.
