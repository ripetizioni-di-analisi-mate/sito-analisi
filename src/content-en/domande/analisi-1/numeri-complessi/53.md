Which of the following numbers is equal to its own conjugate?

- $i^2$
- $i$
- $1 + i$
- $i^3$

## Explanation

A complex number is equal to its own conjugate if and only if it is real. Since $i^2 = -1$, the number $i^2$ is real and therefore equals its conjugate. The other numbers have nonzero imaginary part, so they differ from their conjugates: the conjugate of $i$ is $-i$, that of $1 + i$ is $1 - i$, and since $i^3 = -i$ the conjugate of $i^3$ is $i$.
