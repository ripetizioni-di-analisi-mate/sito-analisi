The set $\mathbb{C}$ consists of the ordered pairs of real numbers, with the product $(a, b) \cdot (c, d) = (ac - bd,\; ad + bc)$. What is $(2, 3) \cdot (1, -1)$?

- $(5, 1)$
- $(2, -3)$
- $(-1, 1)$
- $(5, -5)$

## Explanation

With $a = 2$, $b = 3$, $c = 1$ and $d = -1$ the first component is $ac - bd = 2 + 3 = 5$ and the second is $ad + bc = -2 + 3 = 1$. Hence the product is the pair $(5, 1)$. In algebraic form this is the computation $(2 + 3i)(1 - i) = 2 - 2i + 3i - 3i^2 = 5 + i$.
