What is the algebraic form of $z = \dfrac{5}{2 - i} + \dfrac{5i}{2 + i}$?

- $3 + 3i$
- $3 + i$
- $1 + 3i$
- $15 + 15i$

## Explanation

The numerator and the denominator of each fraction are multiplied by the conjugate of its denominator. Since $(2 - i)(2 + i) = 4 + 1 = 5$, one has
$$
\frac{5}{2 - i} = \frac{5(2 + i)}{5} = 2 + i, \qquad
\frac{5i}{2 + i} = \frac{5i(2 - i)}{5} = 2i - i^2 = 1 + 2i .
$$
Therefore $z = (2 + i) + (1 + 2i) = 3 + 3i$.
