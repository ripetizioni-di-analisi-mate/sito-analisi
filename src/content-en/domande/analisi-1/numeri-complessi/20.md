For $z = 2 - i$, what is $z^2 - 4z$?

- $-3$
- $-5 + 4i$
- $-5$
- $-5 - 8i$

## Explanation

By the square of a binomial $z^2 = 4 - 4i + i^2$, and since $i^2 = -1$ one has $z^2 = 3 - 4i$. Moreover $4z = 8 - 4i$. Therefore
$$
z^2 - 4z = 3 - 4i - 8 + 4i = -5 ,
$$
which is a real number.
