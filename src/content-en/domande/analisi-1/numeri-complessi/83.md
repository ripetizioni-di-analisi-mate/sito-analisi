What is the real factorization of the polynomial $z^3 - 1$?

- $(z - 1)^3$
- $(z - 1)\left(z^2 + z + 1\right)$
- $(z + 1)\left(z^2 - z + 1\right)$
- $(z - 1)\left(z^2 - z + 1\right)$

## Explanation

The roots of $z^3 - 1$ are the cube roots of unity, $1$ and $z_{1,2} = -\dfrac{1}{2} \pm \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\, i$. The two non-real roots are conjugate, and their factor is
$$
(z - z_1)(z - \bar{z}_1) = z^2 - 2\operatorname{Re}(z_1)\, z + |z_1|^2 = z^2 + z + 1 ,
$$
since $\operatorname{Re}(z_1) = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $|z_1| = 1$. The factor $z^2 + z + 1$ has discriminant $-3 < 0$, so the real factorization is $z^3 - 1 = (z - 1)\left(z^2 + z + 1\right)$.
