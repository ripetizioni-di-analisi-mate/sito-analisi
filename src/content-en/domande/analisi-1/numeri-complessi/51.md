Which complex numbers $z$ and $w$ satisfy both equations of the following system?
$$
\begin{cases}
z + w = 3 + i \\[4pt]
z - iw = 1 - i
\end{cases}
$$

- $z = 2$, $w = 1 + i$
- $z = 1 - i$, $w = 2 + 2i$
- $z = 1 + i$, $w = 2$
- $z = 3 - i$, $w = 2i$

## Explanation

From the first equation one obtains $z = 3 + i - w$. Substituting into the second gives $3 + i - w - iw = 1 - i$, that is $(1 + i)\,w = 2 + 2i$. Since $2 + 2i = 2(1 + i)$ and $1 + i \neq 0$, one has $w = 2$, and hence $z = 3 + i - 2 = 1 + i$.
