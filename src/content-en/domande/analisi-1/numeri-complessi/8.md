- $2 \pm 10i$
- $-2 \pm 5i$
- $2 \pm 25i$
- $2 \pm 5i$

## Explanation

The equation has real coefficients and discriminant $\Delta = 16 - 4 \cdot 29 = -100 < 0$, so it has two complex conjugate solutions. By the quadratic formula
$$
z = \frac{4 \pm i\sqrt{100}}{2} = \frac{4 \pm 10i}{2} .
$$
Dividing both $4$ and $10i$ by $2$ one obtains $z = 2 \pm 5i$.
