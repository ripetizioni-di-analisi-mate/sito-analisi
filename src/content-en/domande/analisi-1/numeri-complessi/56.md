The conjugate of a complex number $z$ is $\bar{z} = 2 - 3i$. What is $\overline{z^2}$?

- $-5 + 12i$
- $13 - 12i$
- $-5$
- $-5 - 12i$

## Explanation

The conjugate of a product is the product of the conjugates, so $\overline{z^2} = \bar{z} \cdot \bar{z} = (\bar{z})^2$. Therefore, since $i^2 = -1$,
$$
\overline{z^2} = (2 - 3i)^2 = 4 - 12i + 9i^2 = -5 - 12i .
$$
The number $-5 + 12i$ is instead $z^2$.
