The complex number $z$ has positive imaginary part and satisfies the following system.
$$
\begin{cases}
z + \bar{z} = 0 \\[4pt]
|z| = 3
\end{cases}
$$
What is $z$?

- $-3i$
- $3$
- $9i$
- $3i$

## Explanation

By the properties of the conjugate $z + \bar{z} = 2\operatorname{Re}(z)$, so the first equation is equivalent to $\operatorname{Re}(z) = 0$ and $z = iy$ with $y$ real. Then $|z| = |y|$, and the second equation gives $|y| = 3$, that is, $y = 3$ or $y = -3$. Since the imaginary part of $z$ is positive, $y = 3$ and therefore $z = 3i$.
