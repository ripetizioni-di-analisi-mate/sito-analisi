What is the modulus of the cube roots of $w = 8i$?

- $2$
- $8$
- $2\sqrt{2}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$

## Explanation

The $n$-th roots of a number $w \neq 0$ all have modulus $\sqrt[n]{|w|}$. The number $8i$ has modulus $8$, so its three cube roots have modulus $\sqrt[3]{8} = 2$. In the complex plane they therefore lie on the circle with center at the origin and radius $2$.
