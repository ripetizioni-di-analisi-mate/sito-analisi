With $z=x+iy$, which expression for $y$ describes $|z-2|=|z-3i|$?

- $\dfrac{2 x}{3} + \dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{2 x}{3} - \dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{2 x}{3} + \dfrac{11}{6}$
- $- \dfrac{2 x}{3} - \dfrac{5}{6}$

## Explanation

Squaring both moduli gives $- 4 x + 6 y - 5=0$, from which one solves for $y$.
