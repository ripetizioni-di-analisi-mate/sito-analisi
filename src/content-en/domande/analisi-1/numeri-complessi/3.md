- $2\sqrt{2} \left( \cos\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right) + i \sin\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right) \right)$
- $8 \left( \cos\dfrac{3\pi}{4} + i \sin\dfrac{3\pi}{4} \right)$
- $2\sqrt{2} \left( \cos\dfrac{5\pi}{4} + i \sin\dfrac{5\pi}{4} \right)$
- $2\sqrt{2} \left( \cos\dfrac{3\pi}{4} + i \sin\dfrac{3\pi}{4} \right)$

## Explanation

The modulus is $\rho = \sqrt{(-2)^2 + 2^2} = \sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2}$. Since $a = -2 < 0$ and $b = 2 \geq 0$, the number lies in the second quadrant and the principal argument is
$$
\theta = \arctan(-1) + \pi = -\frac{\pi}{4} + \pi = \frac{3\pi}{4} .
$$
The arctangent alone would give $-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, which is the argument of a point in the fourth quadrant. Hence
$$
z = 2\sqrt{2} \left( \cos\frac{3\pi}{4} + i \sin\frac{3\pi}{4} \right) .
$$
