Which region of the complex plane does the following system describe?
$$
\begin{cases}
|z| < 2 \\[4pt]
\operatorname{Re}(z) > 0
\end{cases}
$$

- The open half-disk of radius $2$, centered at the origin, contained in the half-plane $x > 0$
- The open half-disk of radius $2$, centered at the origin, contained in the half-plane $y > 0$
- The open half-disk of radius $4$, centered at the origin, contained in the half-plane $x > 0$
- The strip $0 < x < 2$

## Explanation

Setting $z = x + iy$, the inequality $|z| < 2$ says that the distance of $z$ from the origin is less than $2$, so it describes the open disk with center at the origin and radius $2$. The inequality $\operatorname{Re}(z) > 0$ is $x > 0$ and describes the half-plane to the right of the imaginary axis. The two conditions must hold together, therefore the region is the right half of the disk, without its boundary.
