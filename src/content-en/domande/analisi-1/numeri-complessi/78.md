Setting $z = x + iy$, the equation $|z| = \operatorname{Re}(z) + 1$ represents a curve. What is its Cartesian equation?

- The parabola $x^2 = 2y + 1$
- The two lines $y = 1$ and $y = -1$
- The line $x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$
- The parabola $y^2 = 2x + 1$

## Explanation

The equation is $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2} = x + 1$. The left-hand side is nonnegative, so every solution satisfies $x + 1 \geq 0$, and under this condition the equation is equivalent to the equation between the squares
$$
x^2 + y^2 = x^2 + 2x + 1 ,
$$
that is, to $y^2 = 2x + 1$. Every point of this parabola has $x = \dfrac{y^2 - 1}{2} \geq -\dfrac{1}{2}$, so $x + 1 > 0$ and the condition is always satisfied. Therefore the curve is the whole parabola $y^2 = 2x + 1$, with vertex at $\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}, 0\right)$.
