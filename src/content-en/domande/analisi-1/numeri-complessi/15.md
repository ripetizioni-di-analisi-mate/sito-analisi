What is the value of $i^{2027}$?

- $i$
- $-1$
- $-i$
- $1$

## Explanation

The powers of $i$ repeat with period $4$, because $i^4 = \left(i^2\right)^2 = (-1)^2 = 1$. Since $2027 = 4 \cdot 506 + 3$, one has
$$
i^{2027} = \left(i^4\right)^{506} \cdot i^3 = i^3 .
$$
Finally $i^3 = i^2 \cdot i = -i$, so $i^{2027} = -i$.
