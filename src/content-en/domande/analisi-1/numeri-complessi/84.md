What is the sum of the four roots in $\mathbb{C}$, counted with multiplicity, of the polynomial $p(z) = 2z^4 - 6z^3 + z - 5$?

- $-3$
- $6$
- $3$
- $-\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

By the fundamental theorem of algebra $p(z) = 2(z - z_1)(z - z_2)(z - z_3)(z - z_4)$. Expanding the product, the coefficient of $z^3$ is $-2(z_1 + z_2 + z_3 + z_4)$. The coefficient of $z^3$ in $p$ is $-6$, so $-2(z_1 + z_2 + z_3 + z_4) = -6$ and the sum of the roots is $3$.
