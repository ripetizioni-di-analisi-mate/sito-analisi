In the complex plane, at what distance from the origin is the point representing $5 - 12i$?

- $169$
- $17$
- $\sqrt{119}$
- $13$

## Explanation

The modulus of $z = a + ib$ is $\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$, here with $a = 5$ and $b = -12$. Hence $|z| = \sqrt{25 + 144} = \sqrt{169} = 13$. The number $169$ is the square of the modulus, that is $z\bar{z}$.
