What is the triple $(x_C,y_C,r)$ giving the center and radius of the locus $|4z-(16 + 28 i)|=12$?

- $\left( -4, \  -7, \  3\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  7, \  12\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  7, \  3\right)$
- $\left( 7, \  4, \  3\right)$

## Explanation

After dividing by $4$, the modulus measures the distance from $4 + 7 i$, and the radius is $3$.
