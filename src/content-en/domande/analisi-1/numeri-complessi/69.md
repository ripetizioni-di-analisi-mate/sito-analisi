Which complex number $z$ satisfies the equation $3z - 2i = z + 4$?

- $2 - i$
- $4 + 2i$
- $2 + i$
- $1 + \dfrac{1}{2}\, i$

## Explanation

The equation is of first degree in $z$, and in $\mathbb{C}$ the same algebraic rules as in $\mathbb{R}$ hold. Moving the terms with $z$ to the left-hand side and the others to the right-hand side gives $2z = 4 + 2i$. Dividing by $2$ gives $z = 2 + i$. Indeed $3(2 + i) - 2i = 6 + i$ and $(2 + i) + 4 = 6 + i$.
