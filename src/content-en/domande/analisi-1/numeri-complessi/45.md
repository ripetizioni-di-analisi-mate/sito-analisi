What is the polynomial of degree three with real coefficients, with coefficient of $z^3$ equal to $1$, that has $2$ and $1 - i$ among its roots?

- $z^3 - 2z - 4$
- $z^3 - 2z + 4$
- $z^3 - 4z^2 + 4z$
- $z^3 - 4z^2 + 6z - 4$

## Explanation

The polynomial has real coefficients and $1 - i$ is one of its roots, so by the complex conjugate root theorem $1 + i$ is also a root. The three roots are therefore $2$, $1 - i$ and $1 + i$, and the polynomial is
$$
p(z) = (z - 2)(z - 1 + i)(z - 1 - i) .
$$
The product of the two conjugate factors is $(z - 1)^2 - i^2 = z^2 - 2z + 2$, so
$$
p(z) = (z - 2)\left(z^2 - 2z + 2\right) = z^3 - 4z^2 + 6z - 4 .
$$
