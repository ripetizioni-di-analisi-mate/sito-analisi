## Explanation

The number $1 - i$ has modulus $\sqrt{2}$ and lies in the fourth quadrant, with principal argument $\arctan(-1) = -\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. So $1 - i = \sqrt{2}\, e^{-i \frac{\pi}{4}}$, and by **De Moivre's formula**
$$
(1 - i)^{10} = \left(\sqrt{2}\right)^{10} e^{-i \frac{10\pi}{4}} = 32\, e^{-i \frac{5\pi}{2}} .
$$
Since $-\dfrac{5\pi}{2}$ and $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ differ by $2\pi$, one has $e^{-i \frac{5\pi}{2}} = e^{-i \frac{\pi}{2}} = -i$. Therefore $(1 - i)^{10} = -32i$.
