For $z_1 = 1 + 2i$ and $z_2 = 3 + i$, which statement is true?

- $z_1 < z_2$, because $1 < 3$
- $z_1 > z_2$, because $2 > 1$
- $\operatorname{Re}(z_1) > \operatorname{Re}(z_2)$
- $|z_1| < |z_2|$

## Explanation

No ordering compatible with the operations is defined on $\mathbb{C}$, so the expressions $z_1 < z_2$ and $z_1 > z_2$ have no meaning for non-real complex numbers. Inequalities make sense between real quantities, such as the real part or the modulus. One has $\operatorname{Re}(z_1) = 1 < 3 = \operatorname{Re}(z_2)$, therefore the statement $\operatorname{Re}(z_1) > \operatorname{Re}(z_2)$ is false. Since $|z_1| = \sqrt{1 + 4} = \sqrt{5}$ and $|z_2| = \sqrt{9 + 1} = \sqrt{10}$, the inequality $|z_1| < |z_2|$ holds.
