What is the modulus of $z = \dfrac{(3 + 4i)^2}{1 - i}$?

- $\dfrac{25}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5\sqrt{2}}{2}$
- $25\sqrt{2}$
- $\dfrac{25\sqrt{2}}{2}$

## Explanation

The modulus of a product is the product of the moduli and the modulus of a quotient is the quotient of the moduli, so $|z|$ is computed from the moduli of $3 + 4i$ and $1 - i$. One has $|3 + 4i| = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$ and $|1 - i| = \sqrt{2}$. Therefore
$$
|z| = \frac{|3 + 4i|^2}{|1 - i|} = \frac{25}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{25\sqrt{2}}{2} .
$$
