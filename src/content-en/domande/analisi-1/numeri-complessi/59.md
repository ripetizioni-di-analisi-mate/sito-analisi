Which of the following numbers is equal to $e^{i\frac{7\pi}{3}}$?

- $e^{-i\frac{\pi}{3}}$
- $e^{i\frac{4\pi}{3}}$
- $e^{i\frac{7\pi}{6}}$
- $e^{i\frac{\pi}{3}}$

## Explanation

Two numbers $e^{i\theta}$ and $e^{i\varphi}$ are equal if and only if $\theta - \varphi$ is an integer multiple of $2\pi$. Since $\dfrac{7\pi}{3} - \dfrac{\pi}{3} = 2\pi$, one has $e^{i\frac{7\pi}{3}} = e^{i\frac{\pi}{3}}$. For the other exponents the differences are $\dfrac{8\pi}{3}$, $\pi$ and $\dfrac{7\pi}{6}$, which are not integer multiples of $2\pi$.
