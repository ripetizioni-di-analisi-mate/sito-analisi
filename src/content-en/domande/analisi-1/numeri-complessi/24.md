For $w = 2 + 3i$, what is the modulus of $z = \dfrac{w}{\bar{w}}$?

- $1$
- $13$
- $\dfrac{1}{13}$
- $\sqrt{13}$

## Explanation

The conjugate $\bar{w}$ is the reflection of $w$ across the real axis, so it has the same distance from the origin, that is $|\bar{w}| = |w| = \sqrt{13}$. The modulus of a quotient is the quotient of the moduli, therefore
$$
|z| = \frac{|w|}{|\bar{w}|} = \frac{\sqrt{13}}{\sqrt{13}} = 1 .
$$
The argument holds for every $w \neq 0$, so the number $\dfrac{w}{\bar{w}}$ always lies on the unit circle.
