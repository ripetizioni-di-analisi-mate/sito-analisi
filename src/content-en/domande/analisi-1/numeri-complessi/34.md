What is the value of $\left(\sqrt{3} + i\right)^6$?

- $64$
- $-64i$
- $64i$
- $-64$

## Explanation

The number $\sqrt{3} + i$ has modulus $\sqrt{3 + 1} = 2$ and lies in the first quadrant, with argument $\arctan\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$. Hence $\sqrt{3} + i = 2\, e^{i \frac{\pi}{6}}$, and by **De Moivre's formula**
$$
\left(\sqrt{3} + i\right)^6 = 2^6\, e^{i \frac{6\pi}{6}} = 64\, e^{i\pi} .
$$
Since $e^{i\pi} = -1$, one obtains $\left(\sqrt{3} + i\right)^6 = -64$.
