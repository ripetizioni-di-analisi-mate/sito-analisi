What is the remainder of the division of the polynomial $z^3 + 1$ by $z - i$?

- $1 - i$
- $1 + i$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

Dividing $p(z) = z^3 + 1$ by $z - i$ gives $p(z) = (z - i)\, q(z) + r$ with $r$ constant. Substituting $z = i$ the first term vanishes, so the remainder is $r = p(i)$. Since $i^3 = i^2 \cdot i = -i$, one has $p(i) = 1 - i$. The remainder is different from $0$, therefore $i$ is not a root of $z^3 + 1$.
