For $z = 3 + 2i$, with respect to what is the point $-\bar{z}$ the reflection of $z$ in the complex plane?

- With respect to the imaginary axis
- With respect to the real axis
- With respect to the origin
- With respect to the line $y = x$

## Explanation

The conjugate of $z$ is $\bar{z} = 3 - 2i$, so $-\bar{z} = -3 + 2i$, that is the point $(-3, 2)$. This point has the same ordinate as $(3, 2)$ and the opposite abscissa, therefore it is the reflection of $z$ across the imaginary axis. The reflection of $z$ across the real axis is $\bar{z}$, and the reflection through the origin is $-z$.
