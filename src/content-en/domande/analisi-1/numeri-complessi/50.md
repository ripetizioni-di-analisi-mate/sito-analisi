Expanding $(1 + i)^3$ with the formula for the cube of a binomial, which number is obtained?

- $-2 + 2i$
- $4 + 4i$
- $2 - 2i$
- $-2 - 2i$

## Explanation

By the formula for the cube of a binomial $(1 + i)^3 = 1 + 3i + 3i^2 + i^3$. Since $i^2 = -1$ and $i^3 = i^2 \cdot i = -i$, one obtains
$$
(1 + i)^3 = 1 + 3i - 3 - i = -2 + 2i .
$$
