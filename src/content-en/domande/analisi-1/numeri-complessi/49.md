Which real numbers $x$ and $y$ satisfy the equality $x(1 + i) + y(2 - i) = 4 + i$?

- $x = -2$, $y = 3$
- $x = 2$, $y = 1$
- $x = 1$, $y = 2$
- $x = 2$, $y = -1$

## Explanation

The left-hand side equals $(x + 2y) + i(x - y)$. Two complex numbers are equal if and only if they have the same real part and the same imaginary part, so the equality is equivalent to the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2y = 4 \\[4pt]
x - y = 1
\end{cases}
$$
Subtracting the second equation from the first gives $3y = 3$, that is $y = 1$, and hence $x = 2$.
