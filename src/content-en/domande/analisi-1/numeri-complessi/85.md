The polynomial $p(z) = \left(z^2 + 1\right)^2 (z - 3)$ has degree $5$. How many distinct roots does it have in $\mathbb{C}$, and what is the multiplicity of the root $i$?

- Only one distinct root, $3$, and $i$ is not a root
- Five distinct roots, and $i$ has multiplicity $1$
- Three distinct roots, and $i$ has multiplicity $1$
- Three distinct roots, and $i$ has multiplicity $2$

## Explanation

Since $z^2 + 1 = (z - i)(z + i)$, one has $p(z) = (z - i)^2 (z + i)^2 (z - 3)$. The distinct roots are therefore $i$, $-i$ and $3$, and the factors $z - i$ and $z + i$ appear with exponent $2$. Therefore $i$ is a root of multiplicity $2$, like $-i$, and the sum of the multiplicities $2 + 2 + 1 = 5$ equals the degree.
