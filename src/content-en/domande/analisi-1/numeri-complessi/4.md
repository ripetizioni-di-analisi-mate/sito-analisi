- $2\, e^{i \frac{\pi}{6}}$
- $2\, e^{-i \frac{5\pi}{6}}$
- $4\, e^{-i \frac{5\pi}{6}}$
- $2\, e^{i \frac{5\pi}{6}}$

## Explanation

The modulus is $\rho = \sqrt{3 + 1} = 2$. Since $a = -\sqrt{3} < 0$ and $b = -1 < 0$, the number lies in the third quadrant and the principal argument is
$$
\theta = \arctan\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} - \pi = \frac{\pi}{6} - \pi = -\frac{5\pi}{6} .
$$
The value $-\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$ belongs to the interval $(-\pi, \pi]$, so it is the principal argument. Therefore $z = 2\, e^{-i \frac{5\pi}{6}}$.
