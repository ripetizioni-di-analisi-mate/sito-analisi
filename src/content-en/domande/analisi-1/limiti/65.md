What is $\lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{- 3 x + \ln{\left(3 x + 1 \right)}}{x \sin{\left(5 x \right)}}$?

- $0$
- $- \dfrac{9}{10}$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}$
- $- \dfrac{9}{5}$

## Explanation

The first nonzero term of the numerator is $- \dfrac{9 x^{2}}{2}$. The sine in the denominator is asymptotic to $5 x$.
