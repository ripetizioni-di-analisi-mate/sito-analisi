Which of the following functions is $o(x)$ as $x \to +\infty$?

- $x + \sin x$
- $\sqrt{x} \, \ln x$
- $x \ln x$
- $2x$

## Explanation

A function $f$ is $o(x)$ as $x \to +\infty$ if $\dfrac{f(x)}{x} \to 0$.

For $f(x) = \sqrt{x} \, \ln x$ we have $\dfrac{\sqrt{x} \, \ln x}{x} = \dfrac{\ln x}{\sqrt{x}}$, which tends to $0$ by the **hierarchy of infinities**. Therefore $\sqrt{x} \, \ln x = o(x)$.

For the other functions the ratio with $x$ tends to a limit different from $0$. Indeed $\dfrac{x + \sin x}{x} \to 1$, $\dfrac{x \ln x}{x} = \ln x \to +\infty$ and $\dfrac{2x}{x} = 2$.
