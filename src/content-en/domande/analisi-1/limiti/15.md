What is the set of accumulation points of $A = (0, 1) \cup \{2\}$?

- $[0, 1] \cup \{2\}$
- $[0, 1]$
- $(0, 1)$
- $(0, 1) \cup \{2\}$

## Explanation

Every point of $[0, 1]$ has points of $(0, 1)$ different from itself in every neighborhood, so it is an accumulation point of $A$. This also holds for $0$ and $1$, which do not belong to $A$. The neighborhood $\left(\dfrac{3}{2}, \dfrac{5}{2}\right)$ of $2$ contains no points of $A$ other than $2$, therefore $2$ is an isolated point of $A$ and is not an accumulation point. Points outside $[0, 1] \cup \{2\}$ have a neighborhood containing no points of $A$. Hence the set of accumulation points is $[0, 1]$.
