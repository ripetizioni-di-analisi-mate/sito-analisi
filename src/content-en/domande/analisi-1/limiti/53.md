What is $\lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{- 3 x + e^{3 x} - 1}{1 - \cos{\left(5 x \right)}}$?

- $\dfrac{18}{25}$
- $\dfrac{9}{25}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{25}{9}$

## Explanation

The quadratic terms are $\dfrac{9 x^{2}}{2}$ and $\dfrac{25 x^{2}}{2}$. The limit is the ratio of their coefficients.
