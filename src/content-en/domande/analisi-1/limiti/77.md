Which of the following functions is continuous at every point of $\mathbb{R}$?

- $f(x) = \ln\left(x^{2}\right)$
- $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{e^{x} - 1}$
- $f(x) = \tan x$
- $f(x) = \dfrac{x^{3}}{1 + x^{2}}$

## Explanation

The quotient of two continuous functions is continuous where the denominator does not vanish. Since $1 + x^{2} \geq 1$ for every $x$, the function $\dfrac{x^{3}}{1 + x^{2}}$ is **continuous** on all of $\mathbb{R}$.

The other functions are not defined on all of $\mathbb{R}$, so they are not continuous at every point of $\mathbb{R}$. The function $\ln\left(x^{2}\right)$ is not defined at $0$, the function $\dfrac{1}{e^{x} - 1}$ is not defined at $0$ because $e^{0} - 1 = 0$, and $\tan x$ is not defined at the points $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$.
