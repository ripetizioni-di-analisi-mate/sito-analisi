## Explanation

At $x = 2$ the numerator and the denominator both equal $0$, so the limit takes the indeterminate form $\dfrac{0}{0}$.

Since $x^2 - 4 = (x - 2)(x + 2)$ and $x^2 - 3x + 2 = (x - 2)(x - 1)$, for $x \neq 2$ we have
$$
\frac{x^2 - 4}{x^2 - 3x + 2} = \frac{x + 2}{x - 1} .
$$
The function $\dfrac{x + 2}{x - 1}$ is continuous at $x = 2$, therefore the limit equals $\dfrac{2 + 2}{2 - 1} = 4$.
