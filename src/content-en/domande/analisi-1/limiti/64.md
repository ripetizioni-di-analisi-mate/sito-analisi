What is $\lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{- 2 x + \ln{\left(2 x + 1 \right)}}{x \sin{\left(3 x \right)}}$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{4}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{2}{3}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The first nonzero term of the numerator is $- 2 x^{2}$. The sine in the denominator is asymptotic to $3 x$.
