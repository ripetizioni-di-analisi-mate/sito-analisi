- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

As $x \to 0$ the numerator and the denominator tend to $0$, so the limit is in the indeterminate form $\dfrac{0}{0}$. For $x \neq 0$ we write
$$
\frac{1 - \cos 2x}{x^2} = \frac{1 - \cos 2x}{(2x)^2} \cdot 4 .
$$
With the change of variable $y = 2x \to 0$, the first factor tends to $\dfrac{1}{2}$ by the **fundamental sine limit** $\displaystyle\lim_{y \to 0} \frac{1 - \cos y}{y^2} = \frac{1}{2}$. Therefore the limit equals $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 4 = 2$.
