What is $\lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{- 4 x + \sin{\left(4 x \right)}}{x^{3}}$?

- $- \dfrac{2}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{32}{3}$
- $\dfrac{32}{3}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The linear term cancels. The first remaining term is $- \dfrac{32 x^{3}}{3}$, and the remainder divided by $x^3$ tends to zero.
