In numerical evaluation of $\sqrt{x+5}-\sqrt{x}$ for very large $x$, subtracting nearby numbers can lose digits. Which equivalent expression avoids that subtraction?

- $\dfrac{5}{2\sqrt{x+5}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x+5}+\sqrt{x}}$
- $\dfrac{5}{\sqrt{x+5}+\sqrt{x}}$
- $\sqrt5$

## Explanation

Multiplying the difference by the sum of the radicals gives $(x+5)-x=5$. Dividing by the positive sum gives an identity valid for every $x\ge0$.
