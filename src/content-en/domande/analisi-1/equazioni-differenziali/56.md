What is the solution of the problem $y' + \dfrac{3 y}{x}=\dfrac{1}{x^{4}}$ with $y(1)=5$?

- $\dfrac{5 - \ln{\left(x \right)}}{x^{3}}$
- $x^{3} \left(\ln{\left(x \right)} + 5\right)$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x \right)} + 5}{x^{3}}$
- $\dfrac{5}{x^{3}}$

## Explanation

The integrating factor on $x>0$ is $x^{3}$. The product has derivative $1/x$ and equals $5$ at one.
