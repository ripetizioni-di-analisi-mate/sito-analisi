What is the solution of the problem $y'' + 4 y=0$ with $y(0)=3$, $y'(0)=10$?

- $- 5 \sin{\left(2 x \right)} + 3 \cos{\left(2 x \right)}$
- $10 \sin{\left(2 x \right)} + 3 \cos{\left(2 x \right)}$
- $5 \sin{\left(2 x \right)} + 3 \cos{\left(2 x \right)}$
- $3 \sin{\left(2 x \right)} + 5 \cos{\left(2 x \right)}$

## Explanation

At zero, cosine is one and sine is zero. The derivative of the sine term at zero is its coefficient times $2$.
