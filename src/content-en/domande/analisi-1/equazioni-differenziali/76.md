The graph in the figure is a solution of one of the following equations. Which one?

::grafico{f="exp(-x)*sin(3*x)" xmin=-0.3 xmax=5 ymin=-0.8 ymax=0.8 legenda=0 titolo="A nonzero solution of the equation"}

- $y'' - 2y' + 10y = 0$
- $y'' + 9y = 0$
- $y'' + 2y' + 10y = 0$
- $y'' + 2y' + y = 0$

## Explanation

The graph crosses the $x$-axis infinitely many times and the amplitude of the oscillations decreases as $x$ increases. The solutions oscillate only if the characteristic equation has complex roots $\lambda \pm i\mu$, and the amplitude $e^{\lambda x}$ decreases only if $\lambda < 0$.

For $y'' + 2y' + 10y = 0$ the roots are $-1 \pm 3i$, which satisfy both conditions. For $y'' - 2y' + 10y = 0$ the roots are $1 \pm 3i$ and the amplitude grows; for $y'' + 9y = 0$ the roots are $\pm 3i$ and the amplitude is constant; for $y'' + 2y' + y = 0$ the root $-1$ is a real double root and the solutions $(C_1 + C_2 x)\, e^{-x}$ vanish at most once.
