What is the solution of the problem $y'=4 y \cos{\left(x \right)}$ with $y(0)=7$?

- $7 e^{4 \sin{\left(x \right)}}$
- $7 e^{- 4 \sin{\left(x \right)}}$
- $4 \sin{\left(x \right)} + 7$
- $7 e^{4 \cos{\left(x \right)} - 4}$

## Explanation

The antiderivative of the coefficient of $y$ is $4 \sin{\left(x \right)}$. The exponential is one at zero.
