What is the solution of the problem $y'=2 y \cos{\left(x \right)}$ with $y(0)=3$?

- $3 e^{- 2 \sin{\left(x \right)}}$
- $2 \sin{\left(x \right)} + 3$
- $3 e^{2 \cos{\left(x \right)} - 2}$
- $3 e^{2 \sin{\left(x \right)}}$

## Explanation

The antiderivative of the coefficient of $y$ is $2 \sin{\left(x \right)}$. The exponential is one at zero.
