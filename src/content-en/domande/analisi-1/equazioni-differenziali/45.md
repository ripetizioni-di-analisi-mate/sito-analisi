On which maximal interval is the solution of the Cauchy problem
$$
\begin{cases}
y'' + y = \dfrac{1}{x} \\[8pt]
y(1) = 0 \\[4pt]
y'(1) = 1
\end{cases}
$$
defined?

- $(0, +\infty)$
- $\mathbb{R}$
- $\mathbb{R} \setminus \{0\}$
- $(1, +\infty)$

## Explanation

The forcing term $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{x}$ is continuous on the intervals $(-\infty, 0)$ and $(0, +\infty)$, and the initial point $x_0 = 1$ belongs to the second one. By the theorem on the **second-order Cauchy problem**, the problem has one and only one solution, defined on the whole interval that contains $x_0$ and on which $f$ is continuous. So the solution is defined on $(0, +\infty)$. The set $\mathbb{R} \setminus \{0\}$ is not an interval, and at $x = 0$ the forcing term is not defined.
