Which function satisfies the equation $y'' - 2y' + 10y = 0$ together with the conditions $y(0) = 1$ and $y'(0) = 4$?

- $y = e^{x}\left(\cos(3x) + \dfrac{4}{3}\sin(3x)\right)$
- $y = e^{-x}\left(\cos(3x) + \dfrac{5}{3}\sin(3x)\right)$
- $y = e^{2x}\left(\cos(6x) + \dfrac{1}{3}\sin(6x)\right)$
- $y = e^{x}\bigl(\cos(3x) + \sin(3x)\bigr)$

## Explanation

The characteristic equation $r^2 - 2r + 10 = 0$ has discriminant $\Delta = 4 - 40 = -36 < 0$ and roots $1 \pm 3i$, so the general solution is $y = e^{x}\bigl(C_1\cos(3x) + C_2\sin(3x)\bigr)$. The condition $y(0) = 1$ gives $C_1 = 1$.

By the product rule
$$
y' = e^{x}\bigl((C_1 + 3C_2)\cos(3x) + (C_2 - 3C_1)\sin(3x)\bigr) ,
$$
so the condition $y'(0) = 4$ gives $C_1 + 3C_2 = 4$, hence $C_2 = 1$. The solution is $y = e^{x}\bigl(\cos(3x) + \sin(3x)\bigr)$.
