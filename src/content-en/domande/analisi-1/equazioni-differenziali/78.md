What is the solution of the problem $y'' + 16 y=0$ with $y(0)=7$, $y'(0)=12$?

- $12 \sin{\left(4 x \right)} + 7 \cos{\left(4 x \right)}$
- $3 \sin{\left(4 x \right)} + 7 \cos{\left(4 x \right)}$
- $7 \sin{\left(4 x \right)} + 3 \cos{\left(4 x \right)}$
- $- 3 \sin{\left(4 x \right)} + 7 \cos{\left(4 x \right)}$

## Explanation

At zero, cosine is one and sine is zero. The derivative of the sine term at zero is its coefficient times $4$.
