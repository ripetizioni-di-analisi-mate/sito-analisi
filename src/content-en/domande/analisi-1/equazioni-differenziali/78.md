The equation $y''' - 3y'' + 3y' - y = 0$ has constant coefficients. Which family describes all of its solutions?

- $y = \left(C_1 + C_2 x + C_3 x^2\right) e^{x}$
- $y = \left(C_1 + C_2 x + C_3 x^2\right) e^{-x}$
- $y = \left(C_1 + C_2 x\right) e^{x} + C_3 e^{-x}$
- $y = C_1 e^{x} + C_2 e^{2x} + C_3 e^{3x}$

## Explanation

By the expansion of the cube of a binomial, the characteristic equation is $r^3 - 3r^2 + 3r - 1 = (r - 1)^3 = 0$. Hence $r = 1$ is a root of **multiplicity** $3$ and gives the solutions $e^{x}$, $x\, e^{x}$ and $x^2 e^{x}$.

The general solution is their linear combination, that is, $y = \left(C_1 + C_2 x + C_3 x^2\right) e^{x}$.
