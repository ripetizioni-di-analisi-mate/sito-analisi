What is the maximal interval of existence for $y'=3 y^{2}$ and $y(0)=5$?

- $\left(-\infty, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{1}{15}\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{1}{15}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{15}, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Separating the variables gives $\dfrac{5}{1 - 15 x}$. The interval containing zero ends where the denominator vanishes, at $\dfrac{1}{15}$.
