What is the maximal existence interval for $y'=3 y^{2}$ and $y(0)=5$?

- $\left(-\infty, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{1}{15}\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{1}{15}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{15}, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Separation gives $\dfrac{5}{1 - 15 x}$. The interval containing zero ends at the denominator’s zero, $\dfrac{1}{15}$.
