- $y = e^{x} \left( C_1 \cos x + C_2 \sin x \right)$
- $y = e^{-2x} \left( C_1 \cos x + C_2 \sin x \right)$
- $y = e^{-x} \left( C_1 \cos x + C_2 \sin x \right)$
- $y = e^{-x} \left( C_1 \cos(2x) + C_2 \sin(2x) \right)$

## Explanation

The characteristic equation is $r^2 + 2r + 2 = 0$, with discriminant $\Delta = 4 - 8 = -4 < 0$. Hence the roots are complex conjugates, equal to
$$
r = \frac{-2 \pm 2i}{2} = -1 \pm i ,
$$
that is, $\lambda = -1$ and $\mu = 1$. Therefore the general solution is $y = e^{-x} \left( C_1 \cos x + C_2 \sin x \right)$ with $C_1, C_2 \in \mathbb{R}$.
