- $y = C_1 e^{-2x} + C_2 e^{-3x}$
- $y = C_1 e^{2x} + C_2 e^{3x}$
- $y = C_1 e^{-x} + C_2 e^{6x}$
- $y = C_1 e^{x} + C_2 e^{6x}$

## Explanation

The characteristic equation associated with the equation is $r^2 - 5r + 6 = 0$. The discriminant is $\Delta = 25 - 24 = 1 > 0$, so the roots are real and distinct. Since $r^2 - 5r + 6 = (r - 2)(r - 3)$, the roots are $r_1 = 2$ and $r_2 = 3$. Therefore the general solution is $y = C_1 e^{2x} + C_2 e^{3x}$ with $C_1, C_2 \in \mathbb{R}$.
