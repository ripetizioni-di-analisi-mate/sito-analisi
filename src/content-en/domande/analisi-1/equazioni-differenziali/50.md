What is the solution to $y'=3 y \cos{\left(x \right)}$ with $y(0)=5$?

- $3 \sin{\left(x \right)} + 5$
- $5 e^{3 \sin{\left(x \right)}}$
- $5 e^{3 \cos{\left(x \right)} - 3}$
- $5 e^{- 3 \sin{\left(x \right)}}$

## Explanation

The antiderivative of the coefficient of $y$ is $3 \sin{\left(x \right)}$. The exponential is one at zero.
