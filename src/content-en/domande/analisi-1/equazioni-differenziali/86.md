Which of the following functions is a particular solution of $y'' - 9 y=5 e^{3 x}$?

- $\dfrac{5 x e^{3 x}}{6}$
- $- \dfrac{5 x e^{3 x}}{6}$
- $\dfrac{5 x e^{3 x}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5 e^{3 x}}{6}$

## Explanation

The exponential solves the homogeneous equation. Applying the operator to $x e^{3 x}$ leaves $6 e^{3 x}$.
