The characteristic equation of $y^{(4)} - y = 0$ has four roots. Which family contains all solutions?

- $y = C_1 e^{x} + C_2 e^{-x} + C_3 x\, e^{x} + C_4 x\, e^{-x}$
- $y = C_1 e^{x} + C_2 e^{-x} + C_3 \cos x + C_4 \sin x$
- $y = \left(C_1 + C_2 x + C_3 x^2 + C_4 x^3\right) e^{x}$
- $y = C_1 + C_2 x + C_3 x^2 + C_4 x^3$

## Explanation

The characteristic equation is $r^4 - 1 = 0$. Since $r^4 - 1 = (r^2 - 1)(r^2 + 1) = (r - 1)(r + 1)(r^2 + 1)$, the roots are $1$, $-1$ and the pair $\pm i$, all simple.

The real roots give $e^{x}$ and $e^{-x}$, the pair $\pm i$ gives $\cos x$ and $\sin x$. The solution space has dimension $4$, so the general solution is the linear combination of these four functions.
