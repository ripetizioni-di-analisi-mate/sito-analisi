What is the solution to $y'' + 9 y=0$ with $y(0)=5$, $y'(0)=12$?

- $5 \sin{\left(3 x \right)} + 4 \cos{\left(3 x \right)}$
- $4 \sin{\left(3 x \right)} + 5 \cos{\left(3 x \right)}$
- $- 4 \sin{\left(3 x \right)} + 5 \cos{\left(3 x \right)}$
- $12 \sin{\left(3 x \right)} + 5 \cos{\left(3 x \right)}$

## Explanation

At zero, cosine is one and sine is zero. The derivative of the sine term at zero is its coefficient times $3$.
