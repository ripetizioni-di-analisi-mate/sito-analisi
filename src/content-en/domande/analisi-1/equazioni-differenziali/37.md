Which function is a particular solution of the equation $y'' + y' = 2x$?

- $y = x^2 - 2x$
- $y = 2x - 2$
- $y = x^2 + 2x$
- $y = x^2$

## Explanation

The characteristic equation $r^2 + r = r (r + 1) = 0$ has the roots $0$ and $-1$. The forcing term is a polynomial of degree $1$ and the associated number $0$ is a simple root, so by the **resonance rule** the polynomial trial function of degree $1$ must be multiplied by $x$, and we look for $y_p = x (a x + b) = a x^2 + b x$. Since $y_p' = 2ax + b$ and $y_p'' = 2a$, substituting we obtain $2ax + 2a + b = 2x$. Equating the coefficients we get $a = 1$ and $2a + b = 0$, so $b = -2$. Therefore a particular solution is $y_p = x^2 - 2x$.
