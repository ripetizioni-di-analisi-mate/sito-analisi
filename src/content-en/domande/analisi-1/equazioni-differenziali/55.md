What is the solution to $y' + \dfrac{2 y}{x}=\dfrac{1}{x^{3}}$ with $y(1)=3$?

- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x \right)} + 3}{x^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{3 - \ln{\left(x \right)}}{x^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{3}{x^{2}}$
- $x^{2} \left(\ln{\left(x \right)} + 3\right)$

## Explanation

The integrating factor on $x>0$ is $x^{2}$. The product has derivative $1/x$ and equals $3$ at one.
