- $y = C_1 \cos(2x) + C_2 \sin(2x) - 2x \cos(2x)$

## Explanation

The characteristic equation $r^2 + 4 = 0$ has the roots $\pm 2i$, so the general solution of the homogeneous equation is $C_1 \cos(2x) + C_2 \sin(2x)$. The number associated with the forcing term $8 \sin(2x)$ is $\pm 2i$, which is a simple root of the characteristic equation, therefore by the resonance rule we look for $y_p = x \left( a_0 \cos(2x) + b_0 \sin(2x) \right)$. Substituting into the equation we obtain
$$
y_p'' + 4 y_p = -4 a_0 \sin(2x) + 4 b_0 \cos(2x) = 8 \sin(2x) ,
$$
so $a_0 = -2$ and $b_0 = 0$. Hence $y_p = -2x \cos(2x)$ and the general solution is $y = C_1 \cos(2x) + C_2 \sin(2x) - 2x \cos(2x)$.
