The substitution $z=\dfrac{1}{y}$ in a Bernoulli equation gives the solution $z(x)=x^2-1$. What is the maximal interval containing $x=0$ on which this formula yields a finite solution for $y$?

- $\mathbb R$, because the polynomial $z(x)$ is defined for every real input.
- $(0,1)$
- $(-1,1)$
- $(1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Returning to the original variable gives $y=\dfrac{1}{x^2-1}$. The zeros of $z$ are poles of $y$. The component of the domain containing zero is therefore $(-1,1)$.
