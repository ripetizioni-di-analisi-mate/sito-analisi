Which equation results from $y' + \dfrac{3 y}{x}=5 y^{2}$ under $w=1/y$, where $x>0$ and $y\ne0$?

- $w'-\dfrac{3w}{x}=-5$
- $w'-\dfrac{3w}{x}=5$
- $w'+\dfrac{3w}{x}=5$
- $w'+\dfrac{3w}{x}=-5$

## Explanation

Divide by $y^2$ and use $w\prime=-y\prime/y^2$. Multiplying by negative one gives $w'-\dfrac{3w}{x}=-5$.
