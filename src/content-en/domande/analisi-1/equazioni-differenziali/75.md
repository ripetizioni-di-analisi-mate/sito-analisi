The family $y = C_1 e^{x} + C_2 e^{-3x}$, with $C_1, C_2 \in \mathbb{R}$, is the general solution of a homogeneous second-order equation with constant coefficients. Which one?

- $y'' - 2y' - 3y = 0$
- $y'' + y' - 3y = 0$
- $y'' + 2y' + 3y = 0$
- $y'' + 2y' - 3y = 0$

## Explanation

The general solution contains $e^{x}$ and $e^{-3x}$, so the roots of the characteristic equation are $r_1 = 1$ and $r_2 = -3$. The characteristic equation is therefore
$$
(r - 1)(r + 3) = r^2 + 2r - 3 = 0 ,
$$
and the corresponding differential equation is $y'' + 2y' - 3y = 0$.
