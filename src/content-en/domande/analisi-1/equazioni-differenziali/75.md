What is the solution of the problem $- 8 y' + y'' + 16 y=0$ with $y(0)=7$, $y'(0)=31$?

- $\left(3 x + 7\right) e^{4 x}$
- $\left(7 - 3 x\right) e^{4 x}$
- $7 e^{4 x}$
- $\left(31 x + 7\right) e^{4 x}$

## Explanation

The characteristic root $4$ is double. The linear factor in front of the exponential has constant term $7$ and slope $3$, set by the initial derivative.
