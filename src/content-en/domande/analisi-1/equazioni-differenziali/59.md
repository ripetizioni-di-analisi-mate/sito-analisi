What is the solution of the problem $y'=\dfrac{3 x}{y}$ with $y(0)=5$?

- $3 x^{2} + 5$
- $\sqrt{25 - 3 x^{2}}$
- $\sqrt{3 x^{2} + 25}$
- $- \sqrt{3 x^{2} + 25}$

## Explanation

Multiply by $2y$ to obtain the derivative of $y^2$. After integration take the positive square root required by the initial value.
