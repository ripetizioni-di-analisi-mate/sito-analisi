What is the exact set of constant-solution values of $y'=\left(y - 3\right) \left(y + 5\right)$?

- $\left\{3, 5\right\}$
- $\left\{-5, 3\right\}$
- $\left\{-3, 5\right\}$
- $\left\{3\right\}$

## Explanation

A constant solution has zero derivative. Set the product to zero to obtain $3$ and $-5$.
