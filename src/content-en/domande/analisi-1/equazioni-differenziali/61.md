What is the maximal existence interval for $y'=2 y^{2}$ and $y(0)=3$?

- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{1}{6}\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{1}{6}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{6}, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Separation gives $\dfrac{3}{1 - 6 x}$. The interval containing zero ends at the denominator’s zero, $\dfrac{1}{6}$.
