What is the solution of the problem $6 y' + y'' + 9 y=0$ with $y(0)=5$, $y'(0)=-11$?

- $\left(5 - 4 x\right) e^{- 3 x}$
- $5 e^{- 3 x}$
- $\left(4 x + 5\right) e^{3 x}$
- $\left(4 x + 5\right) e^{- 3 x}$

## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial has a double root $-3$. Differentiate both the linear factor and the exponential and apply the derivative condition.
