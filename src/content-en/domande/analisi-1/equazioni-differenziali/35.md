For which values of the parameter $k \in \mathbb{R}$ does the general solution of the equation $y'' + 4y' + k\, y = 0$ contain the sine and cosine functions?

- $k < 4$
- $k \geq 4$
- $k > 16$
- $k > 4$

## Explanation

Sine and cosine appear in the general solution when the roots of the characteristic equation $r^2 + 4r + k = 0$ are complex conjugates, that is, when the discriminant is negative. The discriminant is $\Delta = 16 - 4k$, so $\Delta < 0$ if and only if $k > 4$. For $k = 4$ the root $r = -2$ is a real double root and the general solution is $\left( C_1 + C_2 x \right) e^{-2x}$, which contains no trigonometric functions. Therefore the required values are $k > 4$.
