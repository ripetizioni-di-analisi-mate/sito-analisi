What is the order of the differential equation $(y')^3 + x\, y'' = \sin y$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $1$
- $6$

## Explanation

The order of a differential equation is the highest order of the derivatives of the unknown function that appear in it.

The equation contains $y$, the first derivative $y'$ raised to the third power and the second derivative $y''$, so the derivative of highest order is $y''$. The exponent $3$ of $y'$ is a power, not a derivative, therefore it does not change the order, and the equation has **order** $2$.
