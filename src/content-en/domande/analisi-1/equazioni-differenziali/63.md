What is the maximal interval of existence for $y'=4 y^{2}$ and $y(0)=7$?

- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{1}{28}\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, \infty\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{28}, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{1}{28}\right)$

## Explanation

Separating the variables gives $\dfrac{7}{1 - 28 x}$. The interval containing zero ends where the denominator vanishes, at $\dfrac{1}{28}$.
