For which values of the parameter $k \in \mathbb{R}$ are all solutions of the equation $y'' + k\, y' + 4y = 0$ bounded on the half-line $[0, +\infty)$?

- $k \geq 0$
- $k > 0$
- $k \geq 4$
- $-4 < k < 4$

## Explanation

The characteristic equation is $r^2 + kr + 4 = 0$, with discriminant $\Delta = k^2 - 16$, sum of the roots $-k$ and product $4$.

For $k > 0$ real roots are both negative, because their product is positive and their sum is negative, and complex roots have real part $-\dfrac{k}{2} < 0$; so every solution tends to $0$ as $x \to +\infty$ and is bounded. For $k = 0$ the solutions are $C_1 \cos(2x) + C_2 \sin(2x)$, which are bounded as well.

For $k < 0$ the sum of the roots is positive, therefore at least one root has positive real part and the corresponding solution is unbounded. Hence all solutions are bounded if and only if $k \geq 0$.
