What is the solution to $4 y' + y'' + 4 y=0$ with $y(0)=3$, $y'(0)=-1$?

- $\left(5 x + 3\right) e^{- 2 x}$
- $\left(3 - 5 x\right) e^{- 2 x}$
- $\left(5 x + 3\right) e^{2 x}$
- $3 e^{- 2 x}$

## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial has repeated root $-2$. Differentiate both the linear factor and the exponential and apply the derivative condition.
