What is the solution of the problem $y' + \dfrac{4 y}{x}=\dfrac{1}{x^{5}}$ with $y(1)=7$?

- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x \right)} + 7}{x^{4}}$
- $\dfrac{7 - \ln{\left(x \right)}}{x^{4}}$
- $x^{4} \left(\ln{\left(x \right)} + 7\right)$
- $\dfrac{7}{x^{4}}$

## Explanation

The integrating factor on $x>0$ is $x^{4}$. The product has derivative $1/x$ and equals $7$ at one.
