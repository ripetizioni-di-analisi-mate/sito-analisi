A solution of $y''-y=0$ satisfies $y(0)=2$. To remove the growing mode as $x\to+\infty$, which value of $y'(0)$ is needed?

- $2$
- $0$, differentiating only the growing term and omitting the decaying one.
- $-1$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The general solution is $Ae^x+Be^{-x}$. The growing mode disappears for $A=0$, giving $B=2$ from the initial value. Differentiating leaves $y'(0)=-2$.
