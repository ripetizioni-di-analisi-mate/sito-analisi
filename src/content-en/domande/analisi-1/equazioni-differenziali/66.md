What is the exact set of constant-solution values of $y'=\left(y - 4\right) \left(y + 7\right)$?

- $\left\{4, 7\right\}$
- $\left\{-7, 4\right\}$
- $\left\{-4, 7\right\}$
- $\left\{4\right\}$

## Explanation

A constant solution has zero derivative. Set the product to zero to obtain $4$ and $-7$.
