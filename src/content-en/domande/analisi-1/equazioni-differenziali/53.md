Which function solves the initial value problem $y' = 2x$, $y(1) = 0$?

- $y = x^2$
- $y = x^2 + 1$
- $y = x^2 - 1$
- $y = 2x - 2$

## Explanation

The equation asks for a function whose derivative is $2x$, so the general solution consists of the antiderivatives of $2x$, that is, $y = x^2 + C$ with $C \in \mathbb{R}$.

The initial condition $y(1) = 0$ gives $1 + C = 0$, therefore $C = -1$. The solution is $y = x^2 - 1$, defined on all of $\mathbb{R}$.
