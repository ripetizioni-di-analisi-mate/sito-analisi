- $y = C_1 e^{2x} + C_2 e^{-2x} - 2x^2 - 1$
- $y = C_1 e^{2x} + C_2 e^{-2x} - 2x^2$
- $y = C_1 e^{2x} + C_2 e^{-2x} - 2x^2 + 1$
- $y = C_1 \cos(2x) + C_2 \sin(2x) - 2x^2 - 1$

## Explanation

The characteristic equation $r^2 - 4 = 0$ has the distinct real roots $\pm 2$, so the general solution of the homogeneous equation is $C_1 e^{2x} + C_2 e^{-2x}$. The forcing term is a polynomial of degree $2$ and $0$ is not a root of the characteristic equation, therefore by the method of undetermined coefficients we look for $y_p = a x^2 + b x + c$. Since $y_p'' = 2a$, substituting we obtain $-4a x^2 - 4b x + 2a - 4c = 8x^2$, and equating the coefficients
$$
\begin{cases}
-4a = 8 \\[4pt]
-4b = 0 \\[4pt]
2a - 4c = 0
\end{cases}
\quad\implies\quad a = -2, \quad b = 0, \quad c = -1 .
$$
Therefore $y_p = -2x^2 - 1$ and the general solution is $y = C_1 e^{2x} + C_2 e^{-2x} - 2x^2 - 1$.
