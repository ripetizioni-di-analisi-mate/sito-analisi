Which of the following functions is a particular solution of $y'' - 16 y=7 e^{4 x}$?

- $\dfrac{7 x e^{4 x}}{8}$
- $\dfrac{7 x e^{4 x}}{4}$
- $- \dfrac{7 x e^{4 x}}{8}$
- $\dfrac{7 e^{4 x}}{8}$

## Explanation

The exponential solves the homogeneous equation. Applying the operator to $x e^{4 x}$ leaves $8 e^{4 x}$.
