What is the set of characteristic roots of $- 8 y' + y'' + 15 y=0$?

- $\left\{8, 15\right\}$
- $\left\{-3, 5\right\}$
- $\left\{-5, -3\right\}$
- $\left\{3, 5\right\}$

## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial factors as $\left(t - 5\right) \left(t - 3\right)$. The roots are distinct.
