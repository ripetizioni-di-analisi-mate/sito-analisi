All nonzero solutions of the equation $y'' + 4\pi^2 y = 0$ are periodic functions. What is their minimal period?

- $1$
- $2\pi$
- $\pi$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The characteristic equation $r^2 + 4\pi^2 = 0$ has the complex roots $\pm 2\pi i$, so the general solution is $y = C_1 \cos(2\pi x) + C_2 \sin(2\pi x)$.

The functions $\cos(2\pi x)$ and $\sin(2\pi x)$ have minimal period $\dfrac{2\pi}{2\pi} = 1$. Every nonzero solution can be written as $R\cos(2\pi x - \varphi)$ with $R > 0$, therefore its minimal period is $1$.
