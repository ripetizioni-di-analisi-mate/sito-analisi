What is the solution to $- 4 y' + y'' + 4 y=0$ with $y(0)=3$, $y'(0)=11$?

- $\left(5 x + 3\right) e^{2 x}$
- $\left(11 x + 3\right) e^{2 x}$
- $\left(3 - 5 x\right) e^{2 x}$
- $3 e^{2 x}$

## Explanation

The characteristic root $2$ is repeated. The linear factor in front of the exponential has intercept $3$ and slope $5$, set by the initial derivative.
