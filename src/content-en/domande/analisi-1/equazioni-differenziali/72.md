What is the set of characteristic roots of $- 11 y' + y'' + 28 y=0$?

- $\left\{11, 28\right\}$
- $\left\{-4, 7\right\}$
- $\left\{4, 7\right\}$
- $\left\{-7, -4\right\}$

## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial factors as $\left(t - 7\right) \left(t - 4\right)$. The roots are distinct.
