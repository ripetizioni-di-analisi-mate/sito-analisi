What is the solution of the problem $8 y' + y'' + 16 y=0$ with $y(0)=7$, $y'(0)=-25$?

- $\left(3 x + 7\right) e^{4 x}$
- $7 e^{- 4 x}$
- $\left(3 x + 7\right) e^{- 4 x}$
- $\left(7 - 3 x\right) e^{- 4 x}$

## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial has a double root $-4$. Differentiate both the linear factor and the exponential and apply the derivative condition.
