Which of the following functions is a particular solution of $y'' - 4 y=3 e^{2 x}$?

- $\dfrac{3 e^{2 x}}{4}$
- $- \dfrac{3 x e^{2 x}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3 x e^{2 x}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3 x e^{2 x}}{2}$

## Explanation

The exponential solves the homogeneous equation. Applying the operator to $x e^{2 x}$ leaves $4 e^{2 x}$.
