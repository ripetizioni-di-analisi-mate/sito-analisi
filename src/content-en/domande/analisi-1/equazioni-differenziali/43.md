What is the solution of the initial value problem
$$
\begin{cases}
y'' + 9y = 0 \\[4pt]
y(0) = 1 \\[4pt]
y'(0) = 6
\end{cases}
$$

- $y = \cos(3x) + 2 \sin(3x)$
- $y = \cos(3x) + 6 \sin(3x)$
- $y = \cos(3x) + 18 \sin(3x)$
- $y = \cos(3x) + \dfrac{1}{2} \sin(3x)$

## Explanation

The characteristic equation $r^2 + 9 = 0$ has the roots $\pm 3i$, so the general solution is $y = C_1 \cos(3x) + C_2 \sin(3x)$. The first condition gives $y(0) = C_1 = 1$. Since $y' = -3 C_1 \sin(3x) + 3 C_2 \cos(3x)$, the second condition gives $y'(0) = 3 C_2 = 6$, so $C_2 = 2$. Therefore the solution of the problem is $y = \cos(3x) + 2 \sin(3x)$, defined on all of $\mathbb{R}$.
