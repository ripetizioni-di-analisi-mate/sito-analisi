What is the solution to $- 6 y' + y'' + 9 y=0$ with $y(0)=5$, $y'(0)=19$?

- $5 e^{3 x}$
- $\left(5 - 4 x\right) e^{3 x}$
- $\left(19 x + 5\right) e^{3 x}$
- $\left(4 x + 5\right) e^{3 x}$

## Explanation

The characteristic root $3$ is repeated. The linear factor in front of the exponential has intercept $5$ and slope $4$, set by the initial derivative.
