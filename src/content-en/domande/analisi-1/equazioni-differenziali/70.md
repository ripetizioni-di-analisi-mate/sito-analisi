What is the set of characteristic roots of $- 5 y' + y'' + 6 y=0$?

- $\left\{-2, 3\right\}$
- $\left\{-3, -2\right\}$
- $\left\{5, 6\right\}$
- $\left\{2, 3\right\}$

## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial factors as $\left(t - 3\right) \left(t - 2\right)$. The roots are distinct.
