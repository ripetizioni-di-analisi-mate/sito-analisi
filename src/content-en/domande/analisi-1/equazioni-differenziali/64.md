What is the exact set of constant-solution values of $y'=\left(y - 2\right) \left(y + 3\right)$?

- $\left\{2, 3\right\}$
- $\left\{-3, 2\right\}$
- $\left\{-2, 3\right\}$
- $\left\{2\right\}$

## Explanation

A constant solution has zero derivative. Set the product to zero to obtain $2$ and $-3$.
