What is the domain of $f\circ g$ with $f(x)=\sqrt{x - 3}$ and $g(x)=8 - \left|{x - 6}\right|$?

- $\left[3, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-5, 5\right]$
- $\left(1, 11\right)$
- $\left[1, 11\right]$

## Explanation

The square root requires $g(x)\geq 3$. The distance from $x$ to $6$ must be at most $5$.
