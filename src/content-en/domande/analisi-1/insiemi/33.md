- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$

## Explanation

The equation is equivalent to $\sin x = -\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} = -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$. Since the sine is odd and $\sin \dfrac{\pi}{4} = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$, we have $\arcsin\left( -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right) = -\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. The solutions of $\sin x = m$ are $x = \arcsin m + 2k\pi$ or $x = \pi - \arcsin m + 2k\pi$, so here $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$ or $x = \pi + \dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi = \dfrac{5\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$. The solutions $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$ are those of the equation $\cos x = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.
