What is $f^{-1}(y)$ if $f(x)$ is given by $\dfrac{4 x + 9}{x + 5}$?

- $\dfrac{9 - 5 y}{y + 4}$
- $\dfrac{9 - 5 y}{y - 4}$
- $\dfrac{5 y + 9}{y - 4}$
- $- \dfrac{9 - 5 y}{y - 4}$

## Explanation

From $y=\dfrac{4 x + 9}{x + 5}$ we obtain $x \left(y - 4\right)=9 - 5 y$. Divide by $y - 4$.
