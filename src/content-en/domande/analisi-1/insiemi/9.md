Which of the following numbers lies between $\sqrt{2}$ and $1.42$?

- $1.414$
- $1.4$
- $1.415$
- $1.425$

## Explanation

The given numbers are positive, and the function $t \mapsto t^2$ is strictly increasing on $[0, +\infty)$; therefore comparing a positive number with $\sqrt{2}$ is equivalent to comparing its square with $2$.

We have $1.415^2 = 2.002225 > 2$, so $1.415 > \sqrt{2}$. Moreover $1.415 < 1.42$, therefore $1.415$ lies between $\sqrt{2}$ and $1.42$.

On the other hand $1.414^2 = 1.999396 < 2$, so $1.414 < \sqrt{2}$. The number $1.414$ is obtained by truncating the decimal expansion $\sqrt{2} = 1.41421\ldots$, and every truncation is less than $\sqrt{2}$; $1.4$ is also a truncation. The number $1.425$ is greater than $1.42$.
