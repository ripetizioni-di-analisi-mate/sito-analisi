What is $(\inf A,\sup A)$ for $A=\{\dfrac{\left(-1\right)^{n} n}{n + 7} + 2:n\geq1\}$?

- $\left( \dfrac{15}{8}, \  3\right)$
- $\left( 1, \  2\right)$
- $\left( 1, \  3\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  3\right)$

## Explanation

The ratio $\dfrac{n}{n + 7}$ increases to $1$. Odd indices approach $1$ and even indices approach $3$.
