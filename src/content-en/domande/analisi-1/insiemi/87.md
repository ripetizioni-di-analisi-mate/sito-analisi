For which real numbers $x$ does the sum of the distances of $x$ from $0$ and from $2$ equal $4$, that is, $|x| + |x - 2| = 4$?

- $x = -1$ or $x = 3$
- Only $x = 3$
- $x = 1$ or $x = 3$
- $x = -3$ or $x = 3$

## Explanation

The two absolute values change expression at $x = 0$ and at $x = 2$, so three cases are studied.

For $x \geq 2$ the equation becomes $x + x - 2 = 4$, that is, $x = 3$, which satisfies $x \geq 2$. For $0 \leq x < 2$ it becomes $x + 2 - x = 4$, that is, $2 = 4$, which is false, so in this case there are no solutions. For $x < 0$ it becomes $-x + 2 - x = 4$, that is, $x = -1$, which satisfies $x < 0$.

Therefore the solutions are $x = -1$ and $x = 3$. For the points of the interval $[0, 2]$ the sum of the distances equals $2$, so the solutions are the two outer points at distance $1$ from the interval.
