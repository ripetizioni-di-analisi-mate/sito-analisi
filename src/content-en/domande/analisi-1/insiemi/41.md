What is the value of $\log_4 8 + \log_2 \dfrac{1}{4}$?

- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $0$

## Explanation

With the **change of base** to base $2$ we have $\log_4 8 = \dfrac{\log_2 8}{\log_2 4} = \dfrac{3}{2}$. Since $\dfrac{1}{4} = 2^{-2}$, we have $\log_2 \dfrac{1}{4} = -2$. Hence the sum equals $\dfrac{3}{2} - 2 = -\dfrac{1}{2}$. The value $-\dfrac{4}{3}$ is obtained by swapping numerator and denominator in the change of base, that is, with $\log_4 8 = \dfrac{2}{3}$. The value $\dfrac{7}{2}$ is obtained instead with $\log_2 \dfrac{1}{4} = 2$, that is, with the wrong sign.
