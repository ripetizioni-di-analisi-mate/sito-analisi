Given $A = \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ and $B = \{2, 3, 5\}$, which set is $A \setminus B$?

- $\{5\}$
- $\{0, 1\}$
- $\{2, 3\}$
- $\{0, 1, 5\}$

## Explanation

The difference $A \setminus B$ consists of the elements of $A$ that do not belong to $B$. The elements $2$ and $3$ of $A$ also belong to $B$, so they are excluded. The elements $0$ and $1$ do not belong to $B$, therefore $A \setminus B = \{0, 1\}$. The set $\{5\}$ is the difference $B \setminus A$, and $\{2, 3\}$ is the intersection $A \cap B$.
