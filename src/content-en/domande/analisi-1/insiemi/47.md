Which figure in the Cartesian plane represents the Cartesian product $\{1, 2\} \times [0, 1]$?

- Two vertical segments, on the lines $x = 1$ and $x = 2$, with $0 \leq y \leq 1$
- Two horizontal segments, on the lines $y = 1$ and $y = 2$, with $0 \leq x \leq 1$
- The filled rectangle $[1, 2] \times [0, 1]$
- The four points $(1, 0)$, $(1, 1)$, $(2, 0)$, $(2, 1)$

## Explanation

The Cartesian product $\{1, 2\} \times [0, 1]$ is the set of ordered pairs $(x, y)$ with $x \in \{1, 2\}$ and $y \in [0, 1]$.

The first coordinate can only be $1$ or $2$, so the points lie on the two vertical lines $x = 1$ and $x = 2$. The second coordinate runs over the whole interval $[0, 1]$, therefore on each line we obtain the segment with $0 \leq y \leq 1$.

Hence the figure consists of **two vertical segments**. The filled rectangle is the product $[1, 2] \times [0, 1]$, in which the first coordinate also runs over an interval.
