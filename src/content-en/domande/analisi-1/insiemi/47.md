What is the natural domain of $\dfrac{\sqrt{\left(8 - x\right) \left(x - 3\right)}}{x - 6}$?

- $\left(3, 6\right) \cup \left(6, 8\right)$
- $\left[3, 6\right)$
- $\left[3, 6\right) \cup \left(6, 8\right]$
- $\left[3, 8\right]$

## Explanation

The radicand is nonnegative between $3$ and $8$, including the endpoints. The denominator excludes $6$.
