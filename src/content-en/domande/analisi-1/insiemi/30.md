- $0 < x < 1$
- $x < 0$ or $x > 1$
- $x < 1$ or $x > 3$
- $x > 1$

## Explanation

Since $9^x = (3^x)^2$, with the substitution $t = 3^x$ the inequality becomes $t^2 - 4t + 3 > 0$. The trinomial has roots $t = 1$ and $t = 3$, so the inequality is satisfied for $t < 1$ or $t > 3$. The base $3$ is greater than $1$, therefore the condition $3^x < 1 = 3^0$ is equivalent to $x < 0$ and the condition $3^x > 3$ is equivalent to $x > 1$. Hence the solutions are $x < 0$ or $x > 1$.
