What is the solution set of $2^{2 x} - 20 \cdot 2^{x} + 64 < 0$?

- $\left(4, 16\right)$
- $\left[2, 4\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 2\right) \cup \left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(2, 4\right)$

## Explanation

Set $u=2^x$. The quadratic has roots $4$ and $16$ and is negative between them. The function $2^x$ is increasing.
