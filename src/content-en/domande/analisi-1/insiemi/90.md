For which $x \in \mathbb{R}$ is the distance of $x$ from $2$ less than or equal to the distance of $x$ from $0$, that is, $|x - 2| \leq |x|$?

- $x \leq 1$
- $x > 1$
- $0 \leq x \leq 2$
- $x \geq 1$

## Explanation

Both sides are greater than or equal to $0$, and squaring is strictly increasing on $[0, +\infty)$, so the inequality is equivalent to $(x - 2)^2 \leq x^2$.

Expanding gives $x^2 - 4x + 4 \leq x^2$, that is, $-4x + 4 \leq 0$, that is, $x \geq 1$.

The point $x = 1$ is the midpoint between $0$ and $2$ and is included, because at it both distances equal $1$.
