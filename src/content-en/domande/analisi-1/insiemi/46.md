What is the natural domain of $\dfrac{\sqrt{\left(7 - x\right) \left(x - 2\right)}}{x - 4}$?

- $\left(2, 4\right) \cup \left(4, 7\right)$
- $\left[2, 4\right)$
- $\left[2, 7\right]$
- $\left[2, 4\right) \cup \left(4, 7\right]$

## Explanation

The radicand is nonnegative between $2$ and $7$, including the endpoints. The denominator excludes $4$.
