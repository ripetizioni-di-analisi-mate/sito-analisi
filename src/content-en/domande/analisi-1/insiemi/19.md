- $-80$
- $10$
- $40$
- $80$

## Explanation

By the **binomial theorem** with $a = 2x$ and $b = -1$, the general term of the expansion of $(2x - 1)^5$ is $\dbinom{5}{k} (2x)^{5-k} (-1)^k$. The power $x^3$ is obtained for $5 - k = 3$, that is, for $k = 2$. The corresponding term equals $\dbinom{5}{2} \cdot 2^3 x^3 \cdot (-1)^2 = 10 \cdot 8 \cdot x^3 = 80x^3$, so the coefficient is $80$. The sign is positive because the exponent of $-1$ is even.
