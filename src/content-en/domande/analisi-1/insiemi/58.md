What is the range on $\mathbb R$ of $\left|{x - 2}\right| + 4$?

- $\left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left[2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 4\right]$
- $\left[4, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The absolute value reaches zero at $2$ and takes every nonnegative value. The minimum is $4$.
