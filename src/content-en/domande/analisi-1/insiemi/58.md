What is the value of the binomial coefficient $\dbinom{8}{6}$?

- $56$
- $336$
- $20160$
- $28$

## Explanation

By the symmetry property $\dbinom{n}{k} = \dbinom{n}{n - k}$ we have $\dbinom{8}{6} = \dbinom{8}{2}$.

Simplifying the factorials,
$$
\binom{8}{2} = \frac{8!}{2!\,6!} = \frac{8 \cdot 7}{2} = 28 .
$$
The number $20160$ is $\dfrac{8!}{2!}$, that is, the quotient in which the factor $6!$ is missing from the denominator.
