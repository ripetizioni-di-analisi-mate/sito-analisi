What is the solution set of $\left\lfloor{3 x}\right\rfloor + 8 = 6$?

- $\left\{- \dfrac{2}{3}\right\}$
- $\left[-2, -1\right)$
- $\left[- \dfrac{2}{3}, - \dfrac{1}{3}\right)$
- $\left(- \dfrac{2}{3}, - \dfrac{1}{3}\right]$

## Explanation

The floor equals $6$ when $6\leq 3 x + 8<7$. Subtract $8$ and divide by $3$.
