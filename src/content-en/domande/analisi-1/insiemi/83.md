What are the solutions of the equation $|2x + 1| = 5$?

- $x = -3$ or $x = 2$
- Only $x = 2$
- $x = -2$ or $x = 3$
- $x = -3$ or $x = 3$

## Explanation

A number has absolute value $5$ if and only if it equals $5$ or $-5$, so the equation is equivalent to $2x + 1 = 5$ or $2x + 1 = -5$.

The first gives $2x = 4$, that is, $x = 2$; the second gives $2x = -6$, that is, $x = -3$. Therefore the solutions are $x = -3$ and $x = 2$.
