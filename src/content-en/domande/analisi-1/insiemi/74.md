Which of the following functions, defined on $\mathbb{R}$, is injective?

- $x^2 + x$
- $|x - 1|$
- $\cos x$
- $x^3 + x$

## Explanation

The functions $x^3$ and $x$ are strictly increasing on $\mathbb{R}$, so their sum $x^3 + x$ is strictly increasing as well. A strictly monotonic function is injective, therefore $x^3 + x$ is **injective**.

The other three take the same value at two distinct points: $x^2 + x$ equals $0$ at $x = 0$ and at $x = -1$, $|x - 1|$ equals $1$ at $x = 0$ and at $x = 2$, $\cos x$ equals $1$ at $x = 0$ and at $x = 2\pi$. Hence none of them is injective.
