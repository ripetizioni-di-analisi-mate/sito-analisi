What is $A\setminus B$ if $A=\left[-8, 3\right)$ and $B=\left(-3, 6\right]$?

- $\left[-8, -3\right]$
- $\left[-3, 3\right)$
- $\left[-8, -3\right)$
- $\left[-8, 3\right)$

## Explanation

Points of $A$ greater than $-3$ belong to $B$. The point $-3$ is excluded from $B$ and remains in the difference.
