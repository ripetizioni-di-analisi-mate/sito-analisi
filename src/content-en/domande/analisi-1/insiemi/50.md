What is the natural domain of $\ln{\left(\left(x - 3\right) \left(x + 8\right) \right)}$?

- $\left(-8, 3\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -8\right] \cup \left[3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -8\right) \cup \left(3, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The logarithm requires a positive argument. The factors have the same sign to the left of $-8$ and to the right of $3$.
