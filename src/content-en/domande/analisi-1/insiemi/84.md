What is the solution set of $\left\lfloor{4 x}\right\rfloor + 9 = 5$?

- $\left[-4, -3\right)$
- $\left[-1, - \dfrac{3}{4}\right)$
- $\left(-1, - \dfrac{3}{4}\right]$
- $\left\{-1\right\}$

## Explanation

The floor equals $5$ when $5\leq 4 x + 9<6$. Subtract $9$ and divide by $4$.
