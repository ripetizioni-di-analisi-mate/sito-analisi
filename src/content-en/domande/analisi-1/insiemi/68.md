At how many points of the interval $[0, 2\pi]$ is the function $f(x) = \tan 2x$ not defined?

- $2$
- $4$
- $8$
- $1$

## Explanation

The tangent is defined when its argument is different from $\dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$. Here the argument is $2x$, so the excluded points are those with
$$
2x = \frac{\pi}{2} + k\pi , \qquad\text{that is}\qquad x = \frac{\pi}{4} + k\,\frac{\pi}{2} , \qquad k \in \mathbb{Z} .
$$
The interval $[0, 2\pi]$ contains the values obtained with $k = 0, 1, 2, 3$, that is, $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$, $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$, $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$; with $k = 4$ we get $\dfrac{9\pi}{4} > 2\pi$. Hence there are $4$ points.

Imposing the condition $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$, which concerns $\tan x$ and not $\tan 2x$, one would find only the two points $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$.
