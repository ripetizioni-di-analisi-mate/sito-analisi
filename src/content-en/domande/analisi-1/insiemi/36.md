Let $A = \{1, 2, 3\}$. Which of the following statements is true?

- $\{2, 3\} \subseteq A$
- $\{2\} \in A$
- $2 \subseteq A$
- $\{1, 2\} \in A$

## Explanation

The elements of $A$ are the three numbers $1$, $2$ and $3$. Both elements of the set $\{2, 3\}$ belong to $A$, so $\{2, 3\} \subseteq A$ is **true**. The symbol $\in$ relates an element to a set and no element of $A$ is a set, therefore the statements $\{2\} \in A$ and $\{1, 2\} \in A$ are false. The symbol $\subseteq$ relates two sets and $2$ is a number, so the statement $2 \subseteq A$ is also false. The correct statements involving the number $2$ are $2 \in A$ and $\{2\} \subseteq A$.
