For which values of $x$ is the function $f(x) = \arccos(\ln x)$ defined?

- $[-1, 1]$
- $\left( \dfrac{1}{e},\ e \right)$
- $\left[ \dfrac{1}{e},\ e \right]$
- $(0, e]$

## Explanation

The logarithm requires $x > 0$, and the arccosine requires its argument to belong to $[-1, 1]$:
$$
\begin{cases}
x > 0 \\[4pt]
-1 \leq \ln x \leq 1
\end{cases}
$$
Since the exponential with base $e$ is strictly increasing, the second condition is equivalent to $e^{-1} \leq x \leq e^{1}$, and these values also satisfy $x > 0$.

Hence the domain is $\left[ \dfrac{1}{e},\ e \right]$. The interval $[-1, 1]$ is the domain of the arccosine, that is, the set of admissible values for $\ln x$ and not for $x$.
