- $[-1, 3]$
- $[0, 2]$
- $[-3, 1]$
- $(-1, 3)$

## Explanation

The arcsine is defined on $[-1, 1]$, so the condition is $-1 \leq \dfrac{x - 1}{2} \leq 1$. Multiplying all three sides by $2$, which is positive, gives $-2 \leq x - 1 \leq 2$. Adding $1$ to all three sides yields $-1 \leq x \leq 3$. The endpoints are included, because $\arcsin(-1)$ and $\arcsin 1$ are defined; therefore $D = [-1, 3]$.
