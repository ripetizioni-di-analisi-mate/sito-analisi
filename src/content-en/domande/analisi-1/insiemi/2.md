Given the intervals $A = (-3, 2]$ and $B = [0, 4)$, which set is $A \setminus B$?

- $(-3, 0]$
- $(2, 4)$
- $[0, 2]$
- $(-3, 0)$

## Explanation

The difference $A \setminus B$ contains the points of $A$ that do not belong to $B$. The points of $A$ with $x \geq 0$ belong to $B$, because $[0, 2] \subseteq [0, 4)$. What remains are the points of $A$ with $x < 0$, that is, those with $-3 < x < 0$. The point $0$ is excluded, since $B$ contains its own left endpoint, and the point $-3$ is excluded because it does not belong to $A$. Therefore $A \setminus B = (-3, 0)$.
