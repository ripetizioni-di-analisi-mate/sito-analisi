What is the set of upper bounds of $A = [1, 4) \cup \{5\}$?

- $[5, +\infty)$
- $[4, +\infty)$
- $(5, +\infty)$
- $\{5\}$

## Explanation

A number $M$ is an upper bound of $A$ if $a \leq M$ for every $a \in A$. Since $5 \in A$, every upper bound satisfies $M \geq 5$. Conversely, every $M \geq 5$ is an upper bound, because the elements of $[1, 4)$ are less than $4$ and the only other element of $A$ is $5$. Hence the set of upper bounds is $[5, +\infty)$. The number $4$ is excluded, since $5 \in A$ and $5 > 4$.
