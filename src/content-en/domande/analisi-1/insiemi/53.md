What is the solution set of $2^{2 x} - 72 \cdot 2^{x} + 512 < 0$?

- $\left(-\infty, 3\right) \cup \left(6, \infty\right)$
- $\left(3, 6\right)$
- $\left(8, 64\right)$
- $\left[3, 6\right]$

## Explanation

Set $u=2^x$. The quadratic has roots $8$ and $64$ and is negative between them. The function $2^x$ is increasing.
