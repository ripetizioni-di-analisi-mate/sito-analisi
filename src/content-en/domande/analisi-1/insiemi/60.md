What is the range on $\mathbb R$ of $\left|{x - 4}\right| + 5$?

- $\left(5, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 5\right]$
- $\left[5, \infty\right)$
- $\left[4, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The absolute value reaches zero at $4$ and takes every nonnegative value. The minimum is $5$.
