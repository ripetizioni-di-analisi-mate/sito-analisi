What is the value of $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{7} \binom{7}{k}$?

- $256$
- $128$
- $64$
- $49$

## Explanation

By the binomial theorem with $a = b = 1$,
$$
\sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} = (1 + 1)^n = 2^n .
$$
With $n = 7$ the sum equals $2^7 = 128$. The sum has $8$ terms, from $k = 0$ to $k = 7$, but the exponent of $2$ is $n = 7$.
