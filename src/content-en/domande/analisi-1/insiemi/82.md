What is the solution set of $\left\lfloor{2 x}\right\rfloor + 7 = 4$?

- $\left\{- \dfrac{3}{2}\right\}$
- $\left[- \dfrac{3}{2}, -1\right)$
- $\left[-3, -2\right)$
- $\left(- \dfrac{3}{2}, -1\right]$

## Explanation

The floor equals $4$ when $4\leq 2 x + 7<5$. Subtract $7$ and divide by $2$.
