What is the value of $\lfloor -2.5 \rfloor + \lfloor 2.5 \rfloor$, where $\lfloor t \rfloor$ denotes the integer part (floor) of $t$, that is, the largest integer less than or equal to $t$?

- $0$
- $-2$
- $1$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The largest integer less than or equal to $2.5$ is $2$, so $\lfloor 2.5 \rfloor = 2$.

The integers less than or equal to $-2.5$ are $-3, -4, -5, \dots$, and the largest of them is $-3$. Therefore $\lfloor -2.5 \rfloor = -3$, and not $-2$, which is greater than $-2.5$.

Hence the sum equals $-3 + 2 = -1$.
