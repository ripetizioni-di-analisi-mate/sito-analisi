- $(-\infty, -1) \cup (3, +\infty)$
- $(-1, 3)$
- $[-1, 3)$
- $(-1, 3]$

## Explanation

The logarithm is defined for positive arguments, so the condition is $\dfrac{x + 1}{3 - x} > 0$. The numerator is positive for $x > -1$ and the denominator is positive for $x < 3$, therefore for $-1 < x < 3$ the quotient is positive. For $x < -1$ the numerator is negative and the denominator positive; for $x > 3$ the numerator is positive and the denominator negative. The endpoints are excluded, because at $x = -1$ the argument equals $0$ and at $x = 3$ the denominator vanishes. Hence $D = (-1, 3)$.
