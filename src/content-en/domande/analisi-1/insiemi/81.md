What is the domain of $f\circ g$ with $f(x)=\sqrt{x - 4}$ and $g(x)=9 - \left|{x - 5}\right|$?

- $\left[-5, 5\right]$
- $\left[4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(0, 10\right)$
- $\left[0, 10\right]$

## Explanation

The square root requires $g(x)\geq 4$. The distance from $x$ to $5$ must be at most $5$.
