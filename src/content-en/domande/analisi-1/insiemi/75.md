What is $A\setminus B$ if $A=\left[-9, 4\right)$ and $B=\left(-4, 5\right]$?

- $\left[-9, -4\right]$
- $\left[-9, -4\right)$
- $\left[-4, 4\right)$
- $\left[-9, 4\right)$

## Explanation

Points of $A$ greater than $-4$ belong to $B$. The point $-4$ is excluded from $B$ and remains in the difference.
