For $n \geq 1$, which expression is equal to $\dfrac{(n+1)! - n!}{(n-1)!}$?

- $n(n+1)$
- $1$
- $n^2 + n - 1$
- $n^2$

## Explanation

By the **recursive formula** for the factorial we have $(n+1)! = (n+1) \cdot n!$, so the numerator can be written as $(n+1) \cdot n! - n! = n! \,\bigl( (n+1) - 1 \bigr) = n \cdot n!$. Applying the recursive formula again we have $n! = n \cdot (n-1)!$, therefore the numerator equals $n^2 \,(n-1)!$. Dividing by $(n-1)!$ we obtain $n^2$. For example, with $n = 3$ we have $\dfrac{4! - 3!}{2!} = \dfrac{24 - 6}{2} = 9 = 3^2$. The value $n(n+1)$ is the quotient $\dfrac{(n+1)!}{(n-1)!}$ alone, without the contribution of the term $-n!$.
