What is $(\inf A,\sup A)$ for $A=\{\dfrac{\left(-1\right)^{n} n}{n + 9} + 4:n\geq1\}$?

- $\left( 3, \  5\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  4\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  5\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{39}{10}, \  5\right)$

## Explanation

The ratio $\dfrac{n}{n + 9}$ increases to $1$. Odd indices approach $3$ and even indices approach $5$.
