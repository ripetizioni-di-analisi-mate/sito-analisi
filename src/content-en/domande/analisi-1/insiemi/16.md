What is the smallest $n \in \mathbb{N}$ for which $3^n > 2^n + n$ holds?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

For $n = 0$ both sides equal $1$, so the strict inequality is false. For $n = 1$ we get $3 > 3$, which is also false. For $n = 2$ we have $9 > 4 + 2 = 6$, which is true. Therefore the smallest natural number is $n = 2$, which is the **base case** of the induction used to prove the inequality for every $n \geq 2$.
