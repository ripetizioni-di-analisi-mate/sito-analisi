Which real numbers are excluded from the natural domain of $f(x) = \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{1}{x - 1}}$?

- Only $x = 1$
- All $x \leq 1$
- None
- All $x < 1$

## Explanation

The cube root has odd index, so it is defined on all of $\mathbb{R}$ and imposes no condition on the sign of the radicand.

The only condition concerns the fraction, whose denominator must be different from $0$, that is, $x \neq 1$. Therefore only $x = 1$ is excluded from the domain, and the domain is $\mathbb{R} \setminus \{1\}$.

Excluding all $x \leq 1$ would mean treating the root as if it had even index.
