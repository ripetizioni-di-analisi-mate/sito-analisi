Five people sit on five chairs arranged in a row. Two of them, Anna and Bruno, want to occupy two adjacent chairs. In how many different ways can the five people sit?

- $120$
- $48$
- $24$
- $72$

## Explanation

Anna and Bruno are treated as a single block, which occupies two adjacent chairs. There are four objects to arrange, the block and the other three people, and the factorial counts their orderings, which are $4! = 24$.

Inside the block Anna and Bruno can be arranged in $2! = 2$ ways. Hence the number of ways to sit is $2 \cdot 24 = 48$.

The number $120 = 5!$ counts all the orderings of the five people, and the difference $120 - 48 = 72$ counts those in which Anna and Bruno are not adjacent.
