Given $A = \{1, 2\}$ and $B = \{0, 1\}$, to which of the Cartesian products $A \times B$ and $B \times A$ does the pair $(0, 1)$ belong?

- Only to $A \times B$
- To both
- Only to $B \times A$
- To neither

## Explanation

A pair $(a, b)$ belongs to $A \times B$ if and only if $a \in A$ and $b \in B$. The first coordinate of $(0, 1)$ is $0$, which does not belong to $A$, so $(0, 1) \notin A \times B$. In $B \times A$ the first coordinate belongs to $B$ and the second to $A$; since $0 \in B$ and $1 \in A$, the pair $(0, 1)$ belongs to $B \times A$. Pairs are ordered, therefore $A \times B$ and $B \times A$ are different sets.
