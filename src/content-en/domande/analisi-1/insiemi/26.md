The function $f(x) = e^{2x} + 1$ is strictly increasing on $\mathbb{R}$. What is its range?

- $(0, +\infty)$
- $(1, +\infty)$
- $[1, +\infty)$
- $\mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

As $x \in \mathbb{R}$ varies, the number $2x$ takes all real values, and the exponential has range $(0, +\infty)$; hence $e^{2x}$ takes exactly the values in $(0, +\infty)$. Adding $1$ gives exactly the numbers greater than $1$, therefore the range of $f$ is $(1, +\infty)$. The value $1$ is excluded, because $e^{2x} > 0$ for every $x$ and hence $f(x) > 1$. The set $(0, +\infty)$ is the range of $e^{2x}$, before the upward translation by $1$.
