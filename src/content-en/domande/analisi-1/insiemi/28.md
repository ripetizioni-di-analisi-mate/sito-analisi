The graph of $f(x) = 3 - (x + 1)^2$ is obtained from that of $y = x^2$ by two translations and one reflection. What is the vertex of the parabola?

- $(-1, 3)$
- $(1, 3)$
- $(-1, -3)$
- $(1, -3)$

## Explanation

The graph of $(x + 1)^2$ is that of $x^2$ translated to the left by $1$, so the vertex moves from $(0, 0)$ to $(-1, 0)$. The graph of $-(x + 1)^2$ is the reflection of the previous one across the $x$-axis, and the vertex stays at $(-1, 0)$. Adding $3$ translates the graph upward by $3$, therefore the vertex is $(-1, 3)$. Indeed $f(x) \leq 3$ for every $x$, with equality only for $x = -1$.
