In the Venn diagram a region built from the sets $A$, $B$, $C$ is shaded. Which expression describes the shaded region?

::venn{tipo="tre" zone="a,ab,ac" titolo="Shaded region"}

- $A \setminus (B \cup C)$
- $A \cap (B \cup C)$
- $A \setminus (B \cap C)$
- $A \,\triangle\, (B \cap C)$

## Explanation

The shaded region is contained in $A$ and consists of three zones: the elements that lie only in $A$, those that lie in $A$ and in $B$ but not in $C$, and those that lie in $A$ and in $C$ but not in $B$.

The only zone of $A$ left white is that of the elements common to all three sets, that is, $A \cap B \cap C$. Hence the region is $A$ without the elements that lie both in $B$ and in $C$, that is, $A \setminus (B \cap C)$.

The set $A \setminus (B \cup C)$ contains only the zone of the elements that lie only in $A$. The set $A \cap (B \cup C)$, on the other hand, contains the central zone, which is white in the figure.
