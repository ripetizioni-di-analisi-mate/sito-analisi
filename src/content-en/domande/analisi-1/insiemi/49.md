What is the natural domain of $\ln{\left(\left(x - 2\right) \left(x + 7\right) \right)}$?

- $\left(-\infty, 2\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -7\right) \cup \left(2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-7, 2\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -7\right] \cup \left[2, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The logarithm requires a positive argument. The factors have the same sign to the left of $-7$ and to the right of $2$.
