What is the value of $\dbinom{9}{3} + \dbinom{9}{4}$?

- $210$
- $120$
- $252$
- $330$

## Explanation

By Stifel's formula, $\dbinom{n}{k} + \dbinom{n}{k + 1} = \dbinom{n + 1}{k + 1}$; with $n = 9$ and $k = 3$ we get
$$
\binom{9}{3} + \binom{9}{4} = \binom{10}{4} = \frac{10 \cdot 9 \cdot 8 \cdot 7}{4 \cdot 3 \cdot 2} = 210 .
$$
The direct computation confirms the value, because $\dbinom{9}{3} = 84$ and $\dbinom{9}{4} = 126$.
