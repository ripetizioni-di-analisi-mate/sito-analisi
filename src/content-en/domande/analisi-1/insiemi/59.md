What is the range on $\mathbb R$ of $\left|{x - 3}\right| + 6$?

- $\left(6, \infty\right)$
- $\left[3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 6\right]$
- $\left[6, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The absolute value reaches zero at $3$ and takes every nonnegative value. The minimum is $6$.
