For every $n \geq 1$, the sum $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n} \left( \frac{1}{k} - \frac{1}{k + 1} \right)$ has a closed-form expression. Which one?

- $0$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{n}{n + 1}$
- $\dfrac{n - 1}{n}$

## Explanation

Writing the terms one after the other gives
$$
\left( 1 - \frac{1}{2} \right) + \left( \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3} \right) + \cdots + \left( \frac{1}{n} - \frac{1}{n + 1} \right) .
$$
Each fraction $\dfrac{1}{k}$ with $2 \leq k \leq n$ appears once with the sign $+$ and once with the sign $-$, so it cancels.

The first term $1$ and the last term $-\dfrac{1}{n + 1}$ remain, therefore the sum equals $1 - \dfrac{1}{n + 1} = \dfrac{n}{n + 1}$.
