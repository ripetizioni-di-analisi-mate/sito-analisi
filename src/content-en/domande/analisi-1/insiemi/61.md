A set has $6$ elements. How many of its subsets have at least $2$ elements?

- $63$
- $15$
- $57$
- $42$

## Explanation

A set with $6$ elements has $2^6 = 64$ subsets in all, because $\dbinom{6}{k}$ counts those with $k$ elements and $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{6} \binom{6}{k} = 2^6$.

We remove the subsets with fewer than $2$ elements: the empty set, which is $\dbinom{6}{0} = 1$, and the subsets with one element, which are $\dbinom{6}{1} = 6$.

Hence the subsets with at least $2$ elements are $64 - 1 - 6 = 57$. The number $15 = \dbinom{6}{2}$ counts only those with exactly $2$ elements, and $42$ counts those with more than $2$ elements.
