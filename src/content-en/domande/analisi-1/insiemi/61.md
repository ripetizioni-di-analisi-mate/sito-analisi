What is $f^{-1}(y)$ if $f(x)$ is given by $\dfrac{2 x + 7}{x + 4}$?

- $\dfrac{7 - 4 y}{y - 2}$
- $\dfrac{4 y + 7}{y - 2}$
- $- \dfrac{7 - 4 y}{y - 2}$
- $\dfrac{7 - 4 y}{y + 2}$

## Explanation

From $y=\dfrac{2 x + 7}{x + 4}$ we obtain $x \left(y - 2\right)=7 - 4 y$. Divide by $y - 2$.
