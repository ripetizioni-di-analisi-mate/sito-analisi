In the proof by induction of the equality $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n} (2k - 1) = n^2$, one assumes that it holds for $n$. Which expression is equal to $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n+1} (2k - 1)$?

- $n^2 + 2n + 1$
- $n^2 + 2n - 1$
- $n^2 + 2n$
- $n^2 + 2n + 3$

## Explanation

The sum up to $n + 1$ is the sum up to $n$ plus the term with $k = n + 1$, which equals $2(n + 1) - 1 = 2n + 1$. By the inductive hypothesis the sum up to $n$ equals $n^2$, so $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n+1} (2k - 1) = n^2 + 2n + 1$. Since $n^2 + 2n + 1 = (n + 1)^2$, the equality also holds for $n + 1$, and the **inductive step** is verified. The result $n^2 + 2n - 1$ is obtained by adding the term $2n - 1$, which is already included in the sum up to $n$.
