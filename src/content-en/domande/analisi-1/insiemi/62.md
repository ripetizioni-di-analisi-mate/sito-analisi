What is $f^{-1}(y)$ if $f(x)$ is given by $\dfrac{3 x + 8}{x + 6}$?

- $\dfrac{8 - 6 y}{y + 3}$
- $\dfrac{8 - 6 y}{y - 3}$
- $- \dfrac{8 - 6 y}{y - 3}$
- $\dfrac{6 y + 8}{y - 3}$

## Explanation

From $y=\dfrac{3 x + 8}{x + 6}$ we obtain $x \left(y - 3\right)=8 - 6 y$. Divide by $y - 3$.
