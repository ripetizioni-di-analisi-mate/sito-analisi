What is the solution set of $2^{2 x} - 48 \cdot 2^{x} + 512 < 0$?

- $\left(16, 32\right)$
- $\left[4, 5\right]$
- $\left(4, 5\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 4\right) \cup \left(5, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Set $u=2^x$. The quadratic has roots $16$ and $32$ and is negative between them. The function $2^x$ is increasing.
