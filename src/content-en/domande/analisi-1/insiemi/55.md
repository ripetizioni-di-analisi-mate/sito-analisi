Which estimate of $(1.02)^{50}$ follows from Bernoulli's inequality $(1 + x)^n \geq 1 + nx$, valid for $x \geq -1$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$?

- $(1.02)^{50} \geq 2$
- $(1.02)^{50} \leq 2$
- $(1.02)^{50} \geq 51$
- $(1.02)^{50} \geq 3$

## Explanation

We apply the inequality with $x = 0.02$, which satisfies the hypothesis $x \geq -1$, and with $n = 50$.

The right-hand side equals $1 + 50 \cdot 0.02 = 1 + 1 = 2$, so Bernoulli's inequality gives $(1.02)^{50} \geq 2$.

The inequality only provides a lower bound. The actual value is about $2.69$, therefore the estimates $(1.02)^{50} \geq 3$ and $(1.02)^{50} \geq 51$ are false.
