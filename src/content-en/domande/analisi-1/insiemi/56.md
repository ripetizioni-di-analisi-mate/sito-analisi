What is the value of the sum $\displaystyle\sum_{k=21}^{60} k = 21 + 22 + \cdots + 60$?

- $1830$
- $1620$
- $3240$
- $1410$

## Explanation

We write the sum as the difference of two sums of the first natural numbers:
$$
\sum_{k=21}^{60} k = \sum_{k=1}^{60} k - \sum_{k=1}^{20} k .
$$
By the formula for the sum of the first $n$ natural numbers, $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{60} k = \frac{60 \cdot 61}{2} = 1830$ and $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{20} k = \frac{20 \cdot 21}{2} = 210$.

Hence the sum equals $1830 - 210 = 1620$. The sum to subtract stops at $20$, because the term $21$ is part of the required sum.
