What is the natural domain of $\dfrac{\sqrt{\left(9 - x\right) \left(x - 4\right)}}{x - 5}$?

- $\left[4, 5\right) \cup \left(5, 9\right]$
- $\left(4, 5\right) \cup \left(5, 9\right)$
- $\left[4, 9\right]$
- $\left[4, 5\right)$

## Explanation

The radicand is nonnegative between $4$ and $9$, including the endpoints. The denominator excludes $5$.
