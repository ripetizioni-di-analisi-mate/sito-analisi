At which points of the interval $[0, 2\pi)$ do the functions $\cos 2x$ and $\cos x$ take the same value?

- $0$, $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$
- $0$, $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, $\dfrac{5\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$
- $0$, $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

With the double-angle formula $\cos 2x = 2\cos^2 x - 1$ the equation becomes $2\cos^2 x - \cos x - 1 = 0$, which is quadratic in $t = \cos x$.

The roots of $2t^2 - t - 1 = 0$ are $t = 1$ and $t = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, so $\cos x = 1$ or $\cos x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.

In $[0, 2\pi)$ the equation $\cos x = 1$ has the single solution $x = 0$. The equation $\cos x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ has the two solutions $x = \arccos\left( -\dfrac{1}{2} \right) = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ and $x = 2\pi - \dfrac{2\pi}{3} = \dfrac{4\pi}{3}$. Therefore the solutions are $0$, $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$.
