What is the domain of $f\circ g$ with $f(x)=\sqrt{x - 2}$ and $g(x)=7 - \left|{x - 4}\right|$?

- $\left[-1, 9\right]$
- $\left[-5, 5\right]$
- $\left[2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-1, 9\right)$

## Explanation

The square root requires $g(x)\geq 2$. The distance from $x$ to $4$ must be at most $5$.
