What is $A\setminus B$ if $A=\left[-7, 2\right)$ and $B=\left(-2, 4\right]$?

- $\left[-7, 2\right)$
- $\left[-7, -2\right]$
- $\left[-2, 2\right)$
- $\left[-7, -2\right)$

## Explanation

Points of $A$ greater than $-2$ belong to $B$. The point $-2$ is excluded from $B$ and remains in the difference.
