The graph in the figure is obtained from that of $y = \sqrt{x}$ by a horizontal and a vertical translation, and it passes through the points $(1, 2)$ and $(5, 4)$. Which function is it the graph of?

::grafico{f="sqrt(x-1)+2" xmin=-1 xmax=7 ymin=-1 ymax=5 punti="1,2,pieno;5,4,pieno" legenda=0 titolo="The graph passes through $(1, 2)$ and $(5, 4)$"}

- $y = \sqrt{x + 1} + 2$
- $y = \sqrt{x - 2} + 1$
- $y = \sqrt{x - 1} + 2$
- $y = \sqrt{x + 2} - 1$

## Explanation

The graph of $\sqrt{x}$ starts at the origin. Here the starting point is $(1, 2)$, so the graph of $\sqrt{x}$ has been shifted to the right by $1$ and upward by $2$.

By the rules for transformations, the shift to the right by $1$ replaces $x$ with $x - 1$, and the upward shift by $2$ adds $2$ to the value of the function. We obtain $y = \sqrt{x - 1} + 2$.

The second point confirms the choice, because $\sqrt{5 - 1} + 2 = 2 + 2 = 4$.
