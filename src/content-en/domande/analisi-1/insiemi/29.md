- $x < -\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $x > \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $x > -\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $x < \dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

Since $9 = 3^2 = \left( \dfrac{1}{3} \right)^{-2}$, the inequality can be written as $\left( \dfrac{1}{3} \right)^{2x - 1} < \left( \dfrac{1}{3} \right)^{-2}$. The base $\dfrac{1}{3}$ is less than $1$, so the exponential is strictly decreasing and the inequality is equivalent to $2x - 1 > -2$, with the direction reversed. Therefore $2x > -1$, that is, $x > -\dfrac{1}{2}$. The result $x > \dfrac{3}{2}$ is obtained with the incorrect equality $9 = \left( \dfrac{1}{3} \right)^{2}$.
