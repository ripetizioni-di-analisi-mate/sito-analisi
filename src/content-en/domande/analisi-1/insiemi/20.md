## Explanation

By definition $\dbinom{n}{2} = \dfrac{n!}{2!\,(n-2)!} = \dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}$, so the equation becomes $\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2} = 36$, that is, $n(n-1) = 72$. The equation $n^2 - n - 72 = 0$ has solutions $n = 9$ and $n = -8$. Since $n$ is a natural number with $n \geq 2$, the only acceptable solution is $n = 9$. Indeed $\dbinom{9}{2} = \dfrac{9 \cdot 8}{2} = 36$.
