## Explanation

The function is defined where the argument of the root is greater than or equal to $0$ and the denominator is different from $0$, so the conditions to be satisfied simultaneously are
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 - 9 \geq 0 \\[4pt]
x - 5 \neq 0
\end{cases}
$$
The first inequality is satisfied for $x \leq -3$ or $x \geq 3$, with the endpoints included because the root of $0$ is defined. The second condition excludes only the point $x = 5$, which belongs to $[3, +\infty)$. Hence $D = (-\infty, -3] \cup [3, 5) \cup (5, +\infty)$.
