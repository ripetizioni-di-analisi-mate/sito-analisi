What is the coefficient of $x^3$ in the expansion of $(1 + x)^5 (1 - x)$?

- $10$
- $20$
- $0$
- $-10$

## Explanation

By the binomial theorem, $(1 + x)^5 = 1 + 5x + 10x^2 + 10x^3 + 5x^4 + x^5$.

In the product with $1 - x$ the term of degree $3$ arises in two ways: $1 \cdot 10x^3$ and $(-x) \cdot 10x^2$. Hence the coefficient of $x^3$ is $\dbinom{5}{3} - \dbinom{5}{2} = 10 - 10 = 0$.

The value $10$ is obtained if only the product with $1$ is considered, the value $20$ if the contribution of $-x$ is added instead of subtracted.
