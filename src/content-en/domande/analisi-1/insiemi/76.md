Which of the following functions is bounded on its natural domain?

- $x \sin x$
- $\dfrac{x}{x^2 + 1}$
- $e^{-x}$
- $\ln(1 + x^2)$

## Explanation

From $(|x| - 1)^2 \geq 0$ it follows that $x^2 + 1 \geq 2|x|$, so
$$
\left| \frac{x}{x^2 + 1} \right| = \frac{|x|}{x^2 + 1} \leq \frac{1}{2}
$$
for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$. Therefore $\dfrac{x}{x^2 + 1}$ is **bounded**.

The other three are not bounded above. At the points $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$ we have $x \sin x = x$, which exceeds any number for $k$ large enough; at the points $x = -n$ we have $e^{-x} = e^{n} > n$; finally $\ln(1 + x^2) \geq M$ as soon as $x^2 \geq e^{M} - 1$.
