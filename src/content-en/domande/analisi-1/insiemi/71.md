What is $(\inf A,\sup A)$ for $A=\{\dfrac{\left(-1\right)^{n} n}{n + 8} + 3:n\geq1\}$?

- $\left( 2, \  3\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  4\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{26}{9}, \  4\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  4\right)$

## Explanation

The ratio $\dfrac{n}{n + 8}$ increases to $1$. Odd indices give values tending to $2$, and even indices give values tending to $4$.
