What is the natural domain of $\ln{\left(\left(x - 4\right) \left(x + 9\right) \right)}$?

- $\left(-9, 4\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -9\right) \cup \left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 4\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -9\right] \cup \left[4, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The logarithm requires a positive argument. The factors have the same sign to the left of $-9$ and to the right of $4$.
