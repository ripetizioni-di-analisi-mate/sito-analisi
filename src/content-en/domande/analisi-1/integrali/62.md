Which expression equals $\int \dfrac{1}{x^{2} + 9}\,dx$, up to a constant?

- $\operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{3} \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{3} \right)}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(x \right)}}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{3} \right)}}{3}$

## Explanation

Setting $t=x/3$, the denominator becomes $9 t^{2} + 9$. The derivative of $\arctan t$ is the reciprocal of $1+t^2$.
