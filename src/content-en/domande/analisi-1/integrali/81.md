On $[0, 3]$ the graph of the function $f$ is the polygonal line in the figure, joining the points $(0, 2)$, $(1, 2)$ and $(3, -2)$. Let $F(x) = \displaystyle\int_0^x f(t)\, dt$. What is the value of $F(3)$?

::grafico{xmin=-0.6 xmax=3.6 ymin=-2.6 ymax=2.8 nomi="t,y" linee="0,2 1,2 3,-2" punti="0,2,pieno;1,2,pieno;2,0,pieno;3,-2,pieno" legenda=0 titolo="The graph of $y = f(t)$ for $0 \leq t \leq 3$"}

- $4$
- $2$
- $3$
- $1$

## Explanation

The value $F(3)$ is the integral of $f$ over $[0, 3]$, that is a signed area, and by **additivity** it is split into the pieces $[0, 1]$, $[1, 2]$ and $[2, 3]$. On $[0, 1]$ the function equals $2$ and the contribution is the area of the rectangle, that is $2$. On $[1, 2]$ the graph lies above the axis and bounds a triangle with base $1$ and height $2$, which contributes $1$. On $[2, 3]$ the graph lies below the axis and bounds a triangle of area $1$, which contributes $-1$. Therefore $F(3) = 2 + 1 - 1 = 2$.
