The integral of a function $f$ integrable on $[0, 3]$ equals $5$ over the whole interval and equals $\displaystyle\int_2^3 f(x)\, dx = 2$ over the piece $[2, 3]$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_0^2 \bigl( 2 f(x) + 1 \bigr)\, dx$?

- $8$
- $7$
- $15$
- $6$

## Explanation

By **additivity** with respect to the interval $\displaystyle\int_0^3 f(x)\, dx = \int_0^2 f(x)\, dx + \int_2^3 f(x)\, dx$, so $\displaystyle\int_0^2 f(x)\, dx = 5 - 2 = 3$. By **linearity**
$$
\int_0^2 \bigl( 2 f(x) + 1 \bigr)\, dx = 2 \int_0^2 f(x)\, dx + \int_0^2 1\, dx = 6 + 2 = 8 .
$$
