- $\dfrac{1}{4} \arctan \dfrac{x + 2}{2} + c$
- $\dfrac{1}{2} \arctan \dfrac{x - 2}{2} + c$
- $\dfrac{1}{2} \arctan \dfrac{x + 2}{2} + c$
- $\dfrac{1}{2} \arctan (x + 2) + c$

## Explanation

The trinomial $x^2 + 4x + 8$ has discriminant $16 - 32 < 0$, so it has no real roots and we **complete the square**:
$$
x^2 + 4x + 8 = (x + 2)^2 + 4 .
$$
The integral is therefore reducible to the immediate integral $\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{u^2 + k^2}\, du = \frac{1}{k} \arctan \frac{u}{k} + c$ with $u = x + 2$ and $k = 2$. Hence the result is $\dfrac{1}{2} \arctan \dfrac{x + 2}{2} + c$.
