Let $f$ be continuous on $[0, 2]$ with $|f(x)| \leq 3$ for every $x \in [0, 2]$. Which statement is true for every function with these properties?

- $\left| \displaystyle\int_0^2 f(x)\, dx \right| \leq 3$
- $\left| \displaystyle\int_0^2 f(x)\, dx \right| \leq 6$
- $\displaystyle\int_0^2 f(x)\, dx \geq 0$
- $\displaystyle\int_0^2 f(x)\, dx = 6$

## Explanation

By the **absolute value inequality** and by the monotonicity of the integral
$$
\left| \int_0^2 f(x)\, dx \right| \leq \int_0^2 |f(x)|\, dx \leq \int_0^2 3\, dx = 6 .
$$
The constant function $f(x) = 3$ satisfies the hypotheses and has integral $6$, so the bound with $3$ is false for this function. The function $f(x) = -3$ satisfies the hypotheses and has integral $-6$, so the other two statements are false.
