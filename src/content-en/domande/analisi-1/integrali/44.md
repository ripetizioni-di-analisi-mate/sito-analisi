Compute the area of the region between the graph of $y = x^3 - x$ and the $x$-axis for $-1 \leq x \leq 1$.

- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$

## Explanation

Since $x^3 - x = x(x - 1)(x + 1)$, the function is nonnegative on $[-1, 0]$ and nonpositive on $[0, 1]$. By **additivity** we split the interval at the point $0$ and on $[0, 1]$ we integrate $-(x^3 - x)$, so
$$
\mathcal{A} = \int_{-1}^{0} \left( x^3 - x \right) dx - \int_{0}^{1} \left( x^3 - x \right) dx = \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{2} .
$$
The integral of $x^3 - x$ over $[-1, 1]$, instead, equals $0$, because the two contributions are opposite.
