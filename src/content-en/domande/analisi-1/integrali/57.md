Which expression is an antiderivative on $x>0$ of $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x \right)}}{x^{4}}$?

- $- \dfrac{- \dfrac{\ln{\left(x \right)}}{3} - \dfrac{1}{9}}{x^{3}}$
- $\dfrac{- \dfrac{\ln{\left(x \right)}}{3} - \dfrac{1}{9}}{x^{3}}$
- $\ln{\left(x \right)} + \dfrac{- \dfrac{\ln{\left(x \right)}}{3} - \dfrac{1}{9}}{x^{3}}$
- $- \dfrac{\ln{\left(x \right)}}{3 x^{3}}$

## Explanation

Integrate by parts, differentiating the logarithm and integrating the negative power of $x$. The remaining integral is another power integral.
