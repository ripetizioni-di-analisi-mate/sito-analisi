For $F(x)=\int_{3x}^{5x}\ln{\left(t + 1 \right)}\,dt$, what is $F\prime(1)$?

- $- \ln{\left(4 \right)} + \ln{\left(6 \right)}$
- $- 5 \ln{\left(4 \right)} + 3 \ln{\left(6 \right)}$
- $- 3 \ln{\left(4 \right)} + 5 \ln{\left(6 \right)}$
- $3 \ln{\left(4 \right)} + 5 \ln{\left(6 \right)}$

## Explanation

The fundamental theorem gives the upper endpoint contribution minus the lower endpoint contribution. Multiply each integrand value by the derivative of its endpoint.
