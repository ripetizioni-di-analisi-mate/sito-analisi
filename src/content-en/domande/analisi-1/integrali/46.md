Which expression equals $\int \dfrac{x}{2 x^{2} + 3}\,dx$, up to a constant?

- $x + \dfrac{\ln{\left(2 x^{2} + 3 \right)}}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{\ln{\left(2 x^{2} + 3 \right)}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(2 x^{2} + 3 \right)}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(2 x^{2} + 3 \right)}}{2}$

## Explanation

The derivative of $2 x^{2} + 3$ is $4 x$. The substitution leaves the reciprocal of the new argument.
