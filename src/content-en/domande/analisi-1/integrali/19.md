Compute
$$
\int \frac{e^{\sqrt{x}}}{\sqrt{x}}\, dx .
$$

- $e^{\sqrt{x}} + c$
- $2 e^{\sqrt{x}} + c$
- $\dfrac{1}{2} e^{\sqrt{x}} + c$
- $2 \sqrt{x}\, e^{\sqrt{x}} + c$

## Explanation

The integral is reducible to the immediate integral $\displaystyle\int f'(x)\, e^{f(x)}\, dx = e^{f(x)} + c$ with $f(x) = \sqrt{x}$, therefore
$$
\int \frac{e^{\sqrt{x}}}{\sqrt{x}}\, dx = 2 \int \frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}\, e^{\sqrt{x}}\, dx = 2 e^{\sqrt{x}} + c .
$$
