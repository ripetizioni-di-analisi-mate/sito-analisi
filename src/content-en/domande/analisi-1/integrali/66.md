What is $\int_{0}^{1} x e^{4 x^{2}}\,dx$?

- $- \dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{e^{4}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{e^{4}}{8}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{8} + \dfrac{e^{4}}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8} + \dfrac{e^{4}}{8}$

## Explanation

Set $t=4 x^{2}$. The limits of integration become zero and $4$, and the remaining factor is $\dfrac{1}{8}$.
