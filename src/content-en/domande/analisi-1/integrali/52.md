Which expression equals $\int x e^{2 x}\,dx$, up to a constant?

- $\dfrac{x e^{2 x}}{2}$
- $\left(\dfrac{x}{2} + \dfrac{1}{4}\right) e^{2 x}$
- $- \left(\dfrac{x}{2} - \dfrac{1}{4}\right) e^{2 x}$
- $\left(\dfrac{x}{2} - \dfrac{1}{4}\right) e^{2 x}$

## Explanation

Integrate by parts, differentiating $x$ and integrating the exponential. Subtract $\dfrac{e^{2 x}}{4}$ from the boundary term.
