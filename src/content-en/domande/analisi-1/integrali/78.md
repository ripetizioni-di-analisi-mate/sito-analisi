For $F(x)=\int_{4x}^{7x}\ln{\left(t + 1 \right)}\,dt$, what is $F\prime(1)$?

- $- 4 \ln{\left(5 \right)} + 7 \ln{\left(8 \right)}$
- $- 7 \ln{\left(5 \right)} + 4 \ln{\left(8 \right)}$
- $4 \ln{\left(5 \right)} + 7 \ln{\left(8 \right)}$
- $- \ln{\left(5 \right)} + \ln{\left(8 \right)}$

## Explanation

The fundamental theorem gives the upper endpoint contribution minus the lower endpoint contribution. Multiply each integrand value by the derivative of its endpoint.
