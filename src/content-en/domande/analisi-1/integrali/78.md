For which value of $x > 0$ does $\displaystyle\int_0^x (2t + 1)\, dt = 6$ hold?

- $2$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\sqrt{6}$
- $3$

## Explanation

An antiderivative of $2t + 1$ is $t^2 + t$, which equals $0$ at $t = 0$. By the **Newton–Leibniz formula** one therefore has $\displaystyle\int_0^x (2t + 1)\, dt = x^2 + x$.

The equation $x^2 + x = 6$ is equivalent to $(x - 2)(x + 3) = 0$, so its solutions are $x = 2$ and $x = -3$. Since $x > 0$, the required value is $x = 2$.
