Which expression equals $\int x e^{3 x}\,dx$, up to a constant?

- $- \left(\dfrac{x}{3} - \dfrac{1}{9}\right) e^{3 x}$
- $\left(\dfrac{x}{3} + \dfrac{1}{9}\right) e^{3 x}$
- $\dfrac{x e^{3 x}}{3}$
- $\left(\dfrac{x}{3} - \dfrac{1}{9}\right) e^{3 x}$

## Explanation

Integrate by parts, differentiating $x$ and integrating the exponential. Subtract $\dfrac{e^{3 x}}{9}$ from the boundary term.
