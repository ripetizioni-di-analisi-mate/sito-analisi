Compute the area of the region bounded by the graph of $y = \dfrac{1}{x}$, the line $y = x$ and the line $x = 2$.

- $\dfrac{3}{2} + \ln 2$
- $2 - \ln 2$
- $\dfrac{3}{2} - \ln 2$
- $\ln 2 - \dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

For $x > 0$ the equation $\dfrac{1}{x} = x$ gives $x^2 = 1$, so $x = 1$. On $[1, 2]$ one has $x \geq \dfrac{1}{x}$, so the line lies above the hyperbola and
$$
\mathcal{A} = \int_1^{2} \left( x - \frac{1}{x} \right) dx = \left[ \frac{x^2}{2} - \ln x \right]_1^{2} = (2 - \ln 2) - \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2} - \ln 2 .
$$
