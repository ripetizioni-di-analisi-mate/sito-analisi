What is the area between the $x$-axis and the graph on $\left[0, 2\right]$ of $- x^{2} + 2 x$?

- $- \dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$

## Explanation

The parabola is nonnegative between its zeros. Integrating the polynomial gives $\dfrac{4}{3}$.
