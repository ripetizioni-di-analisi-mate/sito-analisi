Which expression equals $\int \dfrac{1}{x^{2} + 4}\,dx$, up to a constant?

- $\dfrac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(x \right)}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{2} \right)}}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{2} \right)}}{2}$
- $\operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{2} \right)}$

## Explanation

Set $t=x/2$. The denominator becomes $4 t^{2} + 4$. The derivative of $\arctan t$ is the reciprocal of $1+t^2$.
