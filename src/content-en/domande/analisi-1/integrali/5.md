- $\arctan \sqrt{x} + c$
- $\dfrac{1}{2} \arctan \sqrt{x} + c$
- $2 \ln\left( 1 + \sqrt{x} \right) + c$
- $2 \arctan \sqrt{x} + c$

## Explanation

We apply **integration by substitution** with $t = \sqrt{x}$, that is $x = t^2$, so $dx = 2t\, dt$ and $1 + x = 1 + t^2$. The integral becomes
$$
\int \frac{1}{t \left( 1 + t^2 \right)} \cdot 2t\, dt = \int \frac{2}{1 + t^2}\, dt = 2 \arctan t + c .
$$
Returning to the variable $x$ with $t = \sqrt{x}$, one obtains $2 \arctan \sqrt{x} + c$.
