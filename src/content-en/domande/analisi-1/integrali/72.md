Let $f(x) = e^{x}$ for $x < 0$ and $f(x) = 1 + x$ for $x \geq 0$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{-1}^{1} f(x)\, dx$?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2} - \dfrac{1}{e}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{e}$
- $3 - \dfrac{1}{e}$

## Explanation

The function $f$ is continuous, because $e^0 = 1 = 1 + 0$. By **additivity** the interval is split at the point $0$, where the expression of $f$ changes:
$$
\int_{-1}^{1} f(x)\, dx = \int_{-1}^{0} e^{x}\, dx + \int_0^{1} (1 + x)\, dx = \left( 1 - \frac{1}{e} \right) + \frac{3}{2} = \frac{5}{2} - \frac{1}{e} .
$$
