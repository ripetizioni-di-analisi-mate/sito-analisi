What is the pair (minimum, maximum) of the integrand used to bound $\int_0^{5} e^{-3x}\,dx$?

- $\left( e^{-15}, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 0, \  1\right)$
- $\left( e^{-15}, \  5\right)$
- $\left( 1, \  e^{-15}\right)$

## Explanation

The exponential is decreasing. Its minimum is at the right endpoint and its maximum is at zero.
