Which integral results from $\int_1^{5}x\cos(3x^2)\,dx$ under $t=3 x^{2}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}\int_{0}^{75}\cos t\,dt$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}\int_{3}^{75}\cos t\,dt$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\int_{3}^{75}\cos t\,dt$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}\int_{1}^{5}\cos t\,dt$

## Explanation

We have $dt=6x\,dx$. The limits in $t$ are $3$ and $75$.
