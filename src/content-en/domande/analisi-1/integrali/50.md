Which expression equals $\int \sin^{5}{\left(3 x \right)} \cos{\left(3 x \right)}\,dx$, up to a constant?

- $- \dfrac{\sin^{6}{\left(3 x \right)}}{18}$
- $\dfrac{\sin^{6}{\left(3 x \right)}}{18}$
- $\dfrac{\sin^{6}{\left(3 x \right)}}{6}$
- $\dfrac{2 \sin^{6}{\left(3 x \right)}}{9}$

## Explanation

Use the sine as the new variable. The cosine times the coefficient of $x$ is the derivative of the sine.
