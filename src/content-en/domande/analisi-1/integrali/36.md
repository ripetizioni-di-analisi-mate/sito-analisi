What is the average value of $f(x) = \sin x$ on the interval $[0, \pi]$?

- $\dfrac{2}{\pi}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The average value of $f$ on $[a, b]$ is the number $\dfrac{1}{b - a} \displaystyle\int_a^b f(x)\, dx$. By the **Newton–Leibniz formula**
$$
\int_0^{\pi} \sin x\, dx = \bigl[ -\cos x \bigr]_0^{\pi} = 1 + 1 = 2 .
$$
Since the interval has length $\pi$, the average value equals $\dfrac{2}{\pi}$.
