Which integral results from $\int_1^{3}x\cos(2x^2)\,dx$ under $t=2 x^{2}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}\int_{2}^{18}\cos t\,dt$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}\int_{1}^{3}\cos t\,dt$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}\int_{0}^{18}\cos t\,dt$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\int_{2}^{18}\cos t\,dt$

## Explanation

We have $dt=4x\,dx$. The bounds in $t$ are $2$ and $18$.
