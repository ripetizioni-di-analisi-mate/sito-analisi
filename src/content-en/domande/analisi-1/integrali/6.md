- $e^x + \ln\left( e^x + 1 \right) + c$
- $\ln\left( e^x + 1 \right) + c$
- $e^x - \ln\left( e^x + 1 \right) + c$
- $\dfrac{e^{2x}}{2} - e^x + \ln\left( e^x + 1 \right) + c$

## Explanation

We apply **integration by substitution** with $t = e^x$, so $dt = e^x\, dx$ and $e^{2x}\, dx = e^x \cdot e^x\, dx = t\, dt$. The integral becomes $\displaystyle\int \frac{t}{t + 1}\, dt$.

Since $\dfrac{t}{t + 1} = 1 - \dfrac{1}{t + 1}$, one has
$$
\int \frac{t}{t + 1}\, dt = t - \ln|t + 1| + c .
$$
Returning to the variable $x$, and since $e^x + 1 > 0$, the result is $e^x - \ln\left( e^x + 1 \right) + c$.
