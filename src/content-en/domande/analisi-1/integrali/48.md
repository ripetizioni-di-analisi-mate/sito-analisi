Which expression equals $\int \dfrac{x}{4 x^{2} + 7}\,dx$, up to a constant?

- $- \dfrac{\ln{\left(4 x^{2} + 7 \right)}}{8}$
- $x + \dfrac{\ln{\left(4 x^{2} + 7 \right)}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(4 x^{2} + 7 \right)}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(4 x^{2} + 7 \right)}}{8}$

## Explanation

The derivative of $4 x^{2} + 7$ is $8 x$. The substitution leaves the reciprocal of the new argument.
