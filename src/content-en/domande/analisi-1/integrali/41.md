Let $H(x) = \displaystyle\int_{x^2}^{3x} \ln(1 + t)\, dt$ for $x > 0$. What is the value of $H'(1)$?

- $4 \ln 2$
- $\ln 2$
- $8 \ln 2$
- $5 \ln 2$

## Explanation

The function $\ln(1 + t)$ is continuous for $t > -1$ and the limits $x^2$ and $3x$ are differentiable, so the formula for the **derivative of an integral function with variable limits** applies:
$$
H'(x) = 3 \ln(1 + 3x) - 2x \ln\left( 1 + x^2 \right) .
$$
For $x = 1$ one obtains $H'(1) = 3 \ln 4 - 2 \ln 2 = 6 \ln 2 - 2 \ln 2 = 4 \ln 2$.
