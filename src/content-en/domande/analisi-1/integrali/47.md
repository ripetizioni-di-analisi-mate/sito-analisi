Which expression equals $\int \dfrac{x}{3 x^{2} + 5}\,dx$, up to a constant?

- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(3 x^{2} + 5 \right)}}{6}$
- $- \dfrac{\ln{\left(3 x^{2} + 5 \right)}}{6}$
- $x + \dfrac{\ln{\left(3 x^{2} + 5 \right)}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(3 x^{2} + 5 \right)}}{3}$

## Explanation

The derivative of $3 x^{2} + 5$ is $6 x$. The substitution leaves the reciprocal of the new argument.
