## Explanation

The parabola intersects the $x$-axis at the points where $4 - x^2 = 0$, that is for $x = -2$ and $x = 2$. On $[-2, 2]$ one has $4 - x^2 \geq 0$, so the area is the integral of the function over this interval. By the **fundamental formula of calculus**
$$
\mathcal{A} = \int_{-2}^{2} \left( 4 - x^2 \right) dx = \left[ 4x - \frac{x^3}{3} \right]_{-2}^{2} = \left( 8 - \frac{8}{3} \right) - \left( -8 + \frac{8}{3} \right) = \frac{32}{3} .
$$
