Let $f(x) = x$ on the interval $[0, 2]$ and let $P$ be the partition of $[0, 2]$ into four intervals of equal length. What is the value of the lower sum $s(P)$?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The partition is $0 < \dfrac{1}{2} < 1 < \dfrac{3}{2} < 2$, and each interval has length $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Since $f$ is increasing, the infimum of $f$ on each interval is its value at the left endpoint, so $m_1 = 0$, $m_2 = \dfrac{1}{2}$, $m_3 = 1$ and $m_4 = \dfrac{3}{2}$. Therefore
$$
s(P) = \frac{1}{2} \left( 0 + \frac{1}{2} + 1 + \frac{3}{2} \right) = \frac{3}{2} .
$$
