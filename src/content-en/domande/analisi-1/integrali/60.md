Which expression is an antiderivative on $x>0$ of $\dfrac{1}{\left(x + 4\right) \left(x + 7\right)}$?

- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 4 \right)}}{3} - \dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 7 \right)}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 4 \right)}}{3} + \dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 7 \right)}}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 4 \right)}}{3} + \dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 7 \right)}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4 \ln{\left(x + 4 \right)}}{3} - \dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 7 \right)}}{3}$

## Explanation

Partial fractions give $- \dfrac{1}{3 x + 21} + \dfrac{1}{3 x + 12}$. Each term has a logarithmic antiderivative.
