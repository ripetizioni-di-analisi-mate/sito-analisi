Which expression equals $\int \dfrac{1}{x^{2} + 16}\,dx$, up to a constant?

- $\dfrac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{4} \right)}}{4}$
- $\operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{4} \right)}$
- $- \dfrac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{4} \right)}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(x \right)}}{4}$

## Explanation

Setting $t=x/4$, the denominator becomes $16 t^{2} + 16$. The derivative of $\arctan t$ is the reciprocal of $1+t^2$.
