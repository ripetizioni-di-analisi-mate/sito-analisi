In the first quadrant the parabola $y = x^2$, the line $y = 2 - x$ and the $x$-axis enclose the shaded region of the figure. What is its area?

::grafico{f="x^2;2-x" area="x^2;0;0;1|2-x;0;1;2" punti="1,1,pieno;2,0,pieno" xmin=-0.6 xmax=2.8 ymin=-0.5 ymax=2.6 titolo="The colored region is bounded by $y = x^2$, by $y = 2 - x$ and by the $x$-axis"}

- $\dfrac{7}{6}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$

## Explanation

In the first quadrant the equation $x^2 = 2 - x$ gives $x = 1$, and the line meets the $x$-axis at the point $x = 2$. For $0 \leq x \leq 1$ the region is bounded above by the parabola, while for $1 \leq x \leq 2$ it is bounded above by the line. By **additivity** the area is the sum of the two contributions:
$$
\mathcal{A} = \int_0^{1} x^2\, dx + \int_1^{2} (2 - x)\, dx = \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{6} .
$$
