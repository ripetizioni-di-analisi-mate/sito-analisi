Compute
$$
\int \frac{x}{(x - 1)^2}\, dx .
$$

- $\ln|x - 1| + \dfrac{1}{x - 1} + c$
- $\ln|x - 1| - \dfrac{1}{x - 1} + c$
- $-\dfrac{1}{x - 1} + c$
- $\ln|x - 1| - \dfrac{1}{2(x - 1)^2} + c$

## Explanation

The denominator has the real root $x = 1$ with multiplicity $2$, so the decomposition into **partial fractions** has the form
$$
\frac{x}{(x - 1)^2} = \frac{A}{x - 1} + \frac{B}{(x - 1)^2}, \qquad x = A(x - 1) + B .
$$
For $x = 1$ one obtains $B = 1$, and comparing the coefficients of $x$ gives $A = 1$. Since the integral of $\dfrac{1}{(x - 1)^2}$ is $-\dfrac{1}{x - 1}$, the result is $\ln|x - 1| - \dfrac{1}{x - 1} + c$.
