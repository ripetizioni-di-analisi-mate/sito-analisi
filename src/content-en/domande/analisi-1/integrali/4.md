- $x \arctan x + \dfrac{1}{2} \ln\left( 1 + x^2 \right) + c$
- $x \arctan x - \dfrac{1}{2} \ln\left( 1 + x^2 \right) + c$
- $x \arctan x - \ln\left( 1 + x^2 \right) + c$
- $x \arctan x - 2 \ln\left( 1 + x^2 \right) + c$

## Explanation

We write $\arctan x = 1 \cdot \arctan x$ and apply **integration by parts** with $f(x) = \arctan x$ and $g'(x) = 1$, so $f'(x) = \dfrac{1}{1 + x^2}$ and $g(x) = x$. The formula gives
$$
\int \arctan x\, dx = x \arctan x - \int \frac{x}{1 + x^2}\, dx .
$$
The last integral is reducible to the immediate integral $\displaystyle\int \frac{f'(x)}{f(x)}\, dx = \ln|f(x)| + c$ with $f(x) = 1 + x^2$, therefore it equals $\dfrac{1}{2} \ln\left( 1 + x^2 \right)$. The absolute value is omitted because $1 + x^2 > 0$, hence the result is $x \arctan x - \dfrac{1}{2} \ln\left( 1 + x^2 \right) + c$.
