Compute the area of the region bounded by the graphs of $y = e^{x}$ and $y = e^{-x}$ and the line $x = 1$.

- $e + \dfrac{1}{e} - 2$
- $e - \dfrac{1}{e}$
- $e + \dfrac{1}{e}$
- $e - \dfrac{1}{e} - 2$

## Explanation

The two graphs intersect where $e^{x} = e^{-x}$, that is at $x = 0$, so the region corresponds to $0 \leq x \leq 1$. On this interval $e^{x} \geq e^{-x}$, therefore, by the formula for the **area between two curves**,
$$
\mathcal{A} = \int_0^1 \left( e^{x} - e^{-x} \right) dx = \bigl[ e^{x} + e^{-x} \bigr]_0^1 = e + \frac{1}{e} - 2 .
$$
