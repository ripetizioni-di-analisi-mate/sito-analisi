What is the area between the $x$-axis and the graph on $\left[0, 4\right]$ of $- x^{2} + 4 x$?

- $- \dfrac{32}{3}$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}$
- $\dfrac{32}{3}$
- $\dfrac{64}{3}$

## Explanation

The parabola is nonnegative between its zeros. Integrating the polynomial gives $\dfrac{32}{3}$.
