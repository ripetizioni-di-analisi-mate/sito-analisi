Which expression equals $\int x e^{4 x}\,dx$, up to a constant?

- $\left(\dfrac{x}{4} - \dfrac{1}{16}\right) e^{4 x}$
- $- \left(\dfrac{x}{4} - \dfrac{1}{16}\right) e^{4 x}$
- $\left(\dfrac{x}{4} + \dfrac{1}{16}\right) e^{4 x}$
- $\dfrac{x e^{4 x}}{4}$

## Explanation

Integrate by parts, differentiating $x$ and integrating the exponential. Subtract $\dfrac{e^{4 x}}{16}$ from the boundary term.
