Which expression is an antiderivative on $x>0$ of $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x \right)}}{x^{2}}$?

- $- \dfrac{\ln{\left(x \right)}}{x}$
- $- \dfrac{- \ln{\left(x \right)} - 1}{x}$
- $\dfrac{- \ln{\left(x \right)} - 1}{x}$
- $\ln{\left(x \right)} + \dfrac{- \ln{\left(x \right)} - 1}{x}$

## Explanation

Integrate by parts, differentiating the logarithm and integrating the negative power of $x$. The remaining integral is another power integral.
