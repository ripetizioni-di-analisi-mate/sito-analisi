Which expression equals $\int \sin^{7}{\left(4 x \right)} \cos{\left(4 x \right)}\,dx$, up to a constant?

- $\dfrac{5 \sin^{8}{\left(4 x \right)}}{32}$
- $- \dfrac{\sin^{8}{\left(4 x \right)}}{32}$
- $\dfrac{\sin^{8}{\left(4 x \right)}}{8}$
- $\dfrac{\sin^{8}{\left(4 x \right)}}{32}$

## Explanation

Use the sine as the new variable. The cosine times the coefficient of $x$ is the derivative of the sine.
