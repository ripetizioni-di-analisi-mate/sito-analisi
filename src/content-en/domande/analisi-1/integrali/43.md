Compute the area of the region between the parabola $y = 2x^2$ and the line $y = x + 1$.

- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{8}$

## Explanation

The intersections are the solutions of $2x^2 = x + 1$, that is of $2x^2 - x - 1 = 0$, so $x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $x = 1$. Between the two intersections the difference $x + 1 - 2x^2$ has constant sign and at $x = 0$ it equals $1$, so on $\left[ -\dfrac{1}{2}, 1 \right]$ the line lies above the parabola. Therefore, by the formula for the **area between two curves**,
$$
\mathcal{A} = \int_{-\frac{1}{2}}^{1} \left( x + 1 - 2x^2 \right) dx = \left[ \frac{x^2}{2} + x - \frac{2x^3}{3} \right]_{-\frac{1}{2}}^{1} = \frac{5}{6} + \frac{7}{24} = \frac{9}{8} .
$$
