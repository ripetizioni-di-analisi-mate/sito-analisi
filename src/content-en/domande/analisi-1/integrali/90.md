Which integral results from $\int_1^{7}x\cos(4x^2)\,dx$ under $t=4 x^{2}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}\int_{4}^{196}\cos t\,dt$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}\int_{1}^{7}\cos t\,dt$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}\int_{0}^{196}\cos t\,dt$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}\int_{4}^{196}\cos t\,dt$

## Explanation

We have $dt=8x\,dx$. The limits in $t$ are $4$ and $196$.
