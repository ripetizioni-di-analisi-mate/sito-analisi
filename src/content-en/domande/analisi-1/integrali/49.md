Which expression equals $\int \sin^{3}{\left(2 x \right)} \cos{\left(2 x \right)}\,dx$, up to a constant?

- $\dfrac{\sin^{4}{\left(2 x \right)}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3 \sin^{4}{\left(2 x \right)}}{8}$
- $\dfrac{\sin^{4}{\left(2 x \right)}}{8}$
- $- \dfrac{\sin^{4}{\left(2 x \right)}}{8}$

## Explanation

Use the sine as the new variable. The cosine times the coefficient of $x$ is the derivative of the sine.
