On $(-3, +\infty)$, what are the antiderivatives of $\dfrac{1}{x^2 + 6x + 9}$?

- $-\dfrac{1}{x + 3} + c$
- $\dfrac{1}{x + 3} + c$
- $\ln\left( x^2 + 6x + 9 \right) + c$
- $\arctan(x + 3) + c$

## Explanation

The trinomial $x^2 + 6x + 9$ has discriminant $36 - 36 = 0$, so it is the square $(x + 3)^2$ and has the single root $x = -3$ with multiplicity $2$. The integral is therefore reducible to the standard integral of a power:
$$
\int (x + 3)^{-2}\, dx = \frac{(x + 3)^{-1}}{-1} + c = -\frac{1}{x + 3} + c .
$$
