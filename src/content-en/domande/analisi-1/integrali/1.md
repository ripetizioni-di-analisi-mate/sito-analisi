- $\dfrac{x^4}{2} - x^3 - \dfrac{1}{x^2} + c$
- $\dfrac{x^4}{4} - \dfrac{x^3}{3} + \ln|x| + c$
- $\dfrac{x^4}{2} - x^3 + \ln|x| + c$
- $\dfrac{2x^4}{3} - \dfrac{3x^3}{2} + \ln|x| + c$

## Explanation

By the **linearity** of the integral, each term is integrated separately and the coefficients are brought outside the integral.

From the formula $\displaystyle\int x^{\alpha}\, dx = \frac{x^{\alpha+1}}{\alpha+1} + c$ one obtains $\displaystyle\int x^3\, dx = \frac{x^4}{4}$ and $\displaystyle\int x^2\, dx = \frac{x^3}{3}$, while $\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{x}\, dx = \ln|x|$. Therefore
$$
\int \left( 2x^3 - 3x^2 + \frac{1}{x} \right) dx = 2 \cdot \frac{x^4}{4} - 3 \cdot \frac{x^3}{3} + \ln|x| + c = \frac{x^4}{2} - x^3 + \ln|x| + c .
$$
