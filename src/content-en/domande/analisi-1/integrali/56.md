For $I_n=\int_0^1x^n e^x\,dx$ and $n\ge1$, which relation determines $I_n$ from $I_{n-1}$?

- $I_n=e+nI_{n-1}$
- $I_n=nI_{n-1}$
- $I_n=e-nI_{n-1}$
- $I_n=e-I_{n-1}$

## Explanation

Integrate by parts, differentiating $x^n$ and integrating $e^x$. The boundary term is $[x^ne^x]_0^1=e$ because $n\ge1$. The remaining term is $-n\int_0^1x^{n-1}e^x\,dx$.
