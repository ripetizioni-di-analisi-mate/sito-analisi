Which expression is an antiderivative on $x>0$ of $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x \right)}}{x^{3}}$?

- $- \dfrac{- \dfrac{\ln{\left(x \right)}}{2} - \dfrac{1}{4}}{x^{2}}$
- $- \dfrac{\ln{\left(x \right)}}{2 x^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{- \dfrac{\ln{\left(x \right)}}{2} - \dfrac{1}{4}}{x^{2}}$
- $\ln{\left(x \right)} + \dfrac{- \dfrac{\ln{\left(x \right)}}{2} - \dfrac{1}{4}}{x^{2}}$

## Explanation

Integrate by parts, differentiating the logarithm and integrating the negative power of $x$. The remaining integral is another power integral.
