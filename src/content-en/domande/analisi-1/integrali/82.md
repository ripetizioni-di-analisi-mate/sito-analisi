What is the pair (minimum, maximum) of the integrand used to bound $\int_0^{3} e^{-2x}\,dx$?

- $\left( 1, \  e^{-6}\right)$
- $\left( 0, \  1\right)$
- $\left( e^{-6}, \  1\right)$
- $\left( e^{-6}, \  3\right)$

## Explanation

The exponential is decreasing. Its minimum is at the right endpoint and its maximum is at zero.
