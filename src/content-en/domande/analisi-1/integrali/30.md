Compute
$$
\int \frac{x + 5}{x^2 + x - 2}\, dx .
$$

- $2 \ln|x - 1| - \ln|x + 2| + c$
- $2 \ln|x - 1| + \ln|x + 2| + c$
- $\ln|x - 1| - 2 \ln|x + 2| + c$
- $2 \ln|x + 1| - \ln|x - 2| + c$

## Explanation

The denominator factors as $x^2 + x - 2 = (x - 1)(x + 2)$, so it has two simple real roots and the integrand is decomposed into **partial fractions**:
$$
\frac{x + 5}{(x - 1)(x + 2)} = \frac{A}{x - 1} + \frac{B}{x + 2}, \qquad x + 5 = A(x + 2) + B(x - 1) .
$$
For $x = 1$ one obtains $6 = 3A$, so $A = 2$; for $x = -2$ one obtains $3 = -3B$, so $B = -1$. Therefore the integral equals $2 \ln|x - 1| - \ln|x + 2| + c$.
