Let $F(x) = \displaystyle\int_0^x \left( t^2 - 4 \right) e^{-t}\, dt$. Which point is a relative minimum point of $F$?

- $x = -2$
- $x = 0$
- $F$ has no relative minimum points
- $x = 2$

## Explanation

The integrand is continuous on $\mathbb{R}$, so by the **fundamental theorem of calculus** $F$ is differentiable and $F'(x) = \left( x^2 - 4 \right) e^{-x}$. Since $e^{-x} > 0$, the sign of $F'$ is the sign of $x^2 - 4$, which is negative for $-2 < x < 2$ and positive for $x < -2$ and for $x > 2$.

Therefore $F$ is increasing on $(-\infty, -2]$, decreasing on $[-2, 2]$ and increasing on $[2, +\infty)$. Hence the **relative minimum** point is $x = 2$, while $x = -2$ is a relative maximum point.
