Which description is correct for $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \left(2 n + 5\right)^{n} \left(5 n + 3\right)^{- n}$?

- Diverges to negative infinity
- The partial sums oscillate without a limit
- Diverges to positive infinity
- Converges to a finite sum

## Explanation

The $n$th root of the general term tends to $\dfrac{2}{5}$, which is less than one. The root test applies.
