A sequence $(b_n)$ has first term $b_1 = 5$, and the telescoping series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (b_n - b_{n+1})$ converges with sum $3$. What is the limit of $b_n$?

- $8$
- $-2$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The series is **telescoping**, so its partial sums are $s_N = b_1 - b_{N+1} = 5 - b_{N+1}$.

It follows that $b_{N+1} = 5 - s_N$. Since $s_N \to 3$, we have $b_{N+1} \to 5 - 3 = 2$, so $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to +\infty} b_n = 2$.

The value $8$ is obtained by writing the partial sums as $b_{N+1} - b_1$, that is by reversing the difference.
