The series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n$ converges with sum $5$, and $a_1 = 2$. What is $\displaystyle\sum_{n=2}^{\infty} a_n$?

- $3$
- $5$
- $7$
- $-3$

## Explanation

Let $s_N = a_1 + a_2 + \cdots + a_N$ be the partial sums of the given series and $t_N = a_2 + \cdots + a_N$ those of the series starting at $n = 2$. For every $N \geq 2$ we have $t_N = s_N - a_1 = s_N - 2$.

Since $s_N \to 5$, we get $t_N \to 5 - 2 = 3$, so the series starting at $n = 2$ converges with sum $3$.

Removing finitely many terms leaves the **behavior** of the series unchanged and changes the *sum*, so the answer $5$ is wrong.
