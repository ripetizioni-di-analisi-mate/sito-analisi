Which description is correct for $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \dfrac{\cos{\left(4 n \right)}}{n^{2} + 9}$?

- The partial sums oscillate without a limit
- Converges conditionally
- Diverges to positive infinity
- Converges absolutely

## Explanation

The absolute value of the cosine is at most one. The absolute values of the terms are bounded by the terms of the convergent series with general term $1/n^2$.
