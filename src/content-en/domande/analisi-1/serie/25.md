For the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n$ we have $a_n \geq 0$ for every $n$, and the partial sums satisfy $s_N \leq 3$ for every $N$. Which statement is true?

- The series converges and its sum is less than or equal to $3$
- The series converges and its sum is $3$
- The series may be indeterminate
- Without knowing $a_n$ the behavior cannot be determined

## Explanation

Since $a_n \geq 0$, we have $s_{N+1} = s_N + a_{N+1} \geq s_N$, so the partial sums are increasing.

An increasing sequence that is bounded above converges to its supremum, by the theorem on **monotone sequences**. Therefore the series converges, and the sum is the supremum of the $s_N$, which is less than or equal to $3$.

The sum can be strictly less than $3$. For example $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{2^n}$ has partial sums less than $1$, hence less than $3$, and sum $1$.
