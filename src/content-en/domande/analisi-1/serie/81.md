Which description is correct for $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} 4^{- n} n^{- n} n!$?

- Diverges to negative infinity
- The partial sums oscillate without a limit
- Converges to a finite sum
- Diverges to positive infinity

## Explanation

In the consecutive-term ratio the factorial contributes $n+1$. The ratio simplifies to $(n/(n+1))^n/4$, which tends to $\dfrac{1}{4 e}$.
