- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The partial fraction decomposition gives
$$
\frac{1}{(2n-1)(2n+1)} = \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{1}{2n-1} - \frac{1}{2n+1} \right) .
$$
Setting $b_n = \dfrac{1}{2n-1}$, one has $b_{n+1} = \dfrac{1}{2n+1}$, so the series is **telescoping**.

Therefore the partial sums are
$$
s_N = \frac{1}{2} \left( 1 - \frac{1}{2N+1} \right) ,
$$
and since $\dfrac{1}{2N+1} \to 0$, the sum of the series is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
