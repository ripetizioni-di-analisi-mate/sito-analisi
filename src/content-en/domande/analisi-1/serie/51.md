What is the sum of $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} - \dfrac{1}{n + 9} + \dfrac{1}{n + 4}$?

- $\dfrac{2131}{2520}$
- $- \dfrac{1879}{2520}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1879}{2520}$

## Explanation

Common reciprocal terms cancel in the partial sums. At the beginning, reciprocals from $5$ through $9$ remain, while the trailing terms tend to zero.
