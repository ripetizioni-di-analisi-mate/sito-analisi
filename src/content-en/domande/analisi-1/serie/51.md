Which expression do the partial sums $s_N$ of Mengoli's series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n(n+1)}$ have?

- $s_N = 1 - \dfrac{1}{N+2}$
- $s_N = 1 - \dfrac{1}{N}$
- $s_N = \dfrac{1}{N+1}$
- $s_N = 1 - \dfrac{1}{N+1}$

## Explanation

Since $\dfrac{1}{n(n+1)} = \dfrac{1}{n} - \dfrac{1}{n+1}$, the series is **telescoping** with $b_n = \dfrac{1}{n}$, and in the sum of the first $N$ terms all intermediate terms cancel:
$$
s_N = \left( 1 - \frac{1}{2} \right) + \left( \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3} \right) + \cdots + \left( \frac{1}{N} - \frac{1}{N+1} \right) = 1 - \frac{1}{N+1} .
$$
For $N = 1$ the formula gives $\dfrac{1}{2}$, which is exactly the first term; the expression $1 - \dfrac{1}{N}$ would give $0$ instead.
