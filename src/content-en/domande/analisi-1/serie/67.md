For which real values of $A$ does $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \dfrac{3^{- n} \left(A - 2\right)^{n}}{n^{2}}$ converge?

- $\left(-1, 5\right)$
- $\left[-1, 5\right)$
- $\left(-1, 5\right]$
- $\left[-1, 5\right]$

## Explanation

The ratio test applies inside $|A-2|<3$. At either endpoint the absolute terms are $1/n^2$, so both endpoints are included.
