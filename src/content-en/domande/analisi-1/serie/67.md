The series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{3n^2 - 1}{n^4 + n}$ has positive terms. How does it behave?

- It diverges to $+\infty$
- It is oscillatory
- It converges conditionally but not absolutely
- It converges

## Explanation

In the numerator $3n^2$ dominates and in the denominator $n^4$ dominates, so
$$
\frac{3n^2 - 1}{n^4 + n} \sim \frac{3n^2}{n^4} = \frac{3}{n^2} .
$$
The series $\displaystyle\sum \frac{1}{n^2}$ converges, because it is a generalized harmonic series with $\alpha = 2 > 1$, hence by the **limit comparison test** the series **converges**.
