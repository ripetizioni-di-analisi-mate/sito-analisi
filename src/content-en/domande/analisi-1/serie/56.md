Which description is correct for $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} e^{\dfrac{3}{n}} - 1 - \dfrac{3}{n}$?

- Converges to a finite sum
- Diverges to positive infinity
- The partial sums oscillate without a limit
- Diverges to negative infinity

## Explanation

Subtracting the linear exponential term leaves a term asymptotic to $\dfrac{9}{2 n^{2}}$. The comparison series with exponent two converges.
