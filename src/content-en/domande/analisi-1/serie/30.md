The ratio test is applied to the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{3^n\, n!}{n^n}$. Which statement is true?

- The ratio tends to $\dfrac{3}{e}$ and the series converges
- The ratio tends to $3$ and the series diverges to $+\infty$
- The ratio tends to $\dfrac{3}{e}$ and the series diverges to $+\infty$
- The ratio tends to $3e$ and the series diverges to $+\infty$

## Explanation

Since $(n+1)! = (n+1)\, n!$ and $(n+1)^{n+1} = (n+1)(n+1)^n$, we have
$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{3^{n+1} (n+1)!}{(n+1)^{n+1}} \cdot \frac{n^n}{3^n\, n!} = 3 \left( \frac{n}{n+1} \right)^{\! n} = \frac{3}{\left( 1 + \dfrac{1}{n} \right)^{n}} .
$$
The denominator tends to $e$, so the ratio tends to $\dfrac{3}{e}$.

Since $e < 3$, we have $\dfrac{3}{e} > 1$, therefore by the **ratio test** the series **diverges** to $+\infty$.
