Using the ratio test, what can be concluded about the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{2^n}{n^2}$?

- The series converges
- The ratio test gives no information
- The series is oscillatory
- The series diverges to $+\infty$

## Explanation

The general term is positive, and the ratio is
$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{2^{n+1}}{(n+1)^2} \cdot \frac{n^2}{2^n} = 2 \left( \frac{n}{n+1} \right)^{\! 2} \to 2 .
$$
Since $2 > 1$, by the **ratio test** the series **diverges** to $+\infty$. Consistently with this, the general term tends to $+\infty$ by the hierarchy of growth rates.
