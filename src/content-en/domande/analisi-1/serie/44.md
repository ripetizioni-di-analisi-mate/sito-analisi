What is the sum of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n\, \pi^{2n}}{(2n)!}$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $-1$
- $e^{-\pi}$

## Explanation

For every $x \in \mathbb{R}$ the **Taylor series** of the cosine holds:
$$
\cos x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n\, x^{2n}}{(2n)!} .
$$
The given series is obtained by substituting $x = \pi$, so its sum is $\cos \pi = -1$.

The value $0$ is $\sin \pi$, which corresponds to the series with the odd powers $x^{2n+1}$ and the factorials $(2n+1)!$.
