For which real values of $A$ does $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \dfrac{9^{- n} \left(A - 4\right)^{n}}{n}$ converge?

- $\left[-5, 13\right]$
- $\left(-5, 13\right]$
- $\left[-5, 13\right)$
- $\left(-5, 13\right)$

## Explanation

The ratio test gives $|A-4|<9$. The left endpoint gives the alternating harmonic series, and the right endpoint gives the harmonic series.
