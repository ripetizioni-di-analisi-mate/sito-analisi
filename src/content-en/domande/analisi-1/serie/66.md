The denominator of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{2^n + n}$ contains an exponential and a polynomial. What can be concluded about the behavior of the series?

- It converges
- It diverges to $+\infty$
- It converges with sum $1$
- It is oscillatory

## Explanation

For every $n \geq 1$ we have $2^n + n > 2^n$, so
$$
0 < \frac{1}{2^n + n} < \frac{1}{2^n} .
$$
The series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{2^n}$ is geometric with ratio $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and converges with sum $1$, hence by the **comparison test** the given series **converges**.

The comparison only shows that the sum is less than $1$, so $1$ is not the sum.
