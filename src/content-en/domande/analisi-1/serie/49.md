What is the sum of $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} - \dfrac{1}{n + 5} + \dfrac{1}{n + 2}$?

- $\dfrac{19}{20}$
- $\dfrac{47}{60}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{47}{60}$

## Explanation

Common reciprocal terms cancel in the partial sums. At the beginning, reciprocals from $3$ through $5$ remain, while the trailing terms tend to zero.
