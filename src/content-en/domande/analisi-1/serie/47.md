What is the sum of $\sum_{n=2}^{+\infty} \left(- \dfrac{3}{7}\right)^{n}$?

- $\dfrac{9}{70}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$
- $\dfrac{9}{28}$

## Explanation

The first term is $\dfrac{9}{49}$ and the ratio is $- \dfrac{3}{7}$. The sum is the first term divided by one minus the ratio.
