The repeating decimal $0.666\ldots$ is the sum of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{6}{10^n}$. Which fraction is it equal to?

- $\dfrac{20}{3}$
- $\dfrac{6}{11}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The series is geometric with ratio $q = \dfrac{1}{10}$, with $|q| < 1$, and its first term, for $n = 1$, equals $\dfrac{6}{10}$.

Hence the sum is the first term divided by $1 - q$:
$$
\frac{\dfrac{6}{10}}{1 - \dfrac{1}{10}} = \frac{6}{9} = \frac{2}{3} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{3}{2}$ is obtained by taking $\dfrac{6}{10}$ as the ratio instead of as the first term.
