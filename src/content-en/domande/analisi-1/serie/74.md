Which description is correct for $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \dfrac{\cos{\left(3 n \right)}}{n^{2} + 7}$?

- Diverges to positive infinity
- Converges absolutely
- Converges conditionally
- The partial sums oscillate without a limit

## Explanation

The cosine magnitude is at most one. The term magnitudes are bounded by the convergent series with terms $1/n^2$.
