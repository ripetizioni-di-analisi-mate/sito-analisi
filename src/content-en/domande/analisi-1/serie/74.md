What is the behavior of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdots (2n-1)}{n!\; 3^n}$, whose numerator is the product of the first $n$ odd numbers?

- Converges
- Diverges to $+\infty$
- The ratio test gives no information
- Is oscillatory

## Explanation

Going from $a_n$ to $a_{n+1}$, the numerator gains the factor $2n + 1$ and the denominator gains the factors $n + 1$ and $3$. Hence
$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{2n+1}{3(n+1)} \to \frac{2}{3} .
$$
Since $\dfrac{2}{3} < 1$, by the **ratio test** the series **converges**.
