What is the behavior of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\cos n}{n \sqrt{n}}$?

- Converges absolutely
- Converges conditionally but not absolutely
- Is indeterminate
- Diverges to $+\infty$

## Explanation

The sign of $\cos n$ varies without a simple pattern, so we study the series of absolute values. Since $|\cos n| \leq 1$, we have
$$
0 \leq \left| \frac{\cos n}{n \sqrt{n}} \right| \leq \frac{1}{n^{\frac{3}{2}}} .
$$
The series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^{\frac{3}{2}}}$ converges, because $\alpha = \dfrac{3}{2} > 1$, so by the comparison test the series of absolute values converges.

Therefore the given series **converges absolutely**, and absolute convergence implies convergence.
