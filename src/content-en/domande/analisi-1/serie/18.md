A geometric series of positive terms has sum 12. Removing the first two terms leaves a tail with sum 3. What is the common ratio?

- $\dfrac14$, treating the tail as if only the first term had been removed.
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

Skipping two terms multiplies every term, hence the sum, by $q^2$. Thus $12q^2=3$. Positivity of the ratio selects $q=\dfrac{1}{2}$.
