What is the sum of the terms $e^{-1} + e^{-2} + e^{-3} + \cdots$, that is of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} e^{-n}$?

- $\dfrac{e}{e - 1}$
- $\dfrac{1}{e - 1}$
- $\dfrac{1}{e + 1}$
- $e - 1$

## Explanation

Since $e^{-n} = \left( \dfrac{1}{e} \right)^{\! n}$, the series is a **geometric series** with ratio $q = \dfrac{1}{e}$. Since $e > 1$, we have $0 < q < 1$, so the series converges.

The series starts at $n = 1$, hence the sum is the first term $\dfrac{1}{e}$ divided by $1 - q$, and equals
$$
\frac{\dfrac{1}{e}}{1 - \dfrac{1}{e}} = \frac{1}{e - 1} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{e}{e - 1}$ is the sum of the same series starting from $n = 0$.
