What is the sum of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=2}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n}{3^n}$?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

Since $\dfrac{(-1)^n}{3^n} = \left( -\dfrac{1}{3} \right)^{\! n}$, the series is a **geometric series** with ratio $q = -\dfrac{1}{3}$. We have $|q| < 1$, so the series converges.

The index starts at $n = 2$, therefore the first term is $q^2 = \dfrac{1}{9}$ and the sum is the first term divided by $1 - q$:
$$
\sum_{n=2}^{\infty} \left( -\frac{1}{3} \right)^{\! n} = \frac{\dfrac{1}{9}}{1 + \dfrac{1}{3}} = \frac{\dfrac{1}{9}}{\dfrac{4}{3}} = \frac{1}{12} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{3}{4}$ is obtained by starting at $n = 0$, and $-\dfrac{1}{4}$ by starting at $n = 1$.
