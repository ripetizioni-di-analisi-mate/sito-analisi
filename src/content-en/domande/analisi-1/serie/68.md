For which real values of $A$ does $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \dfrac{2^{- n} \left(A - 3\right)^{n}}{n^{2}}$ converge?

- $\left[1, 5\right]$
- $\left(1, 5\right]$
- $\left(1, 5\right)$
- $\left[1, 5\right)$

## Explanation

The ratio test applies inside $|A-3|<2$. At both endpoints the series of absolute values has general term $1/n^2$, so both endpoints are included.
