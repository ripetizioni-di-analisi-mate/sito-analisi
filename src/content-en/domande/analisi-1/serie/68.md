About the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n$ it is known only that $0 \leq a_n \leq \dfrac{1}{n}$ for every $n$. What can be concluded?

- The series converges by the comparison test
- The series either converges or diverges to $+\infty$, and the comparison does not establish which of the two cases occurs
- The series diverges to $+\infty$ by the comparison test
- The series may be oscillatory

## Explanation

Since $a_n \geq 0$, the series either converges or diverges to $+\infty$, and the oscillatory case does not occur.

The comparison test with $\sum \dfrac{1}{n}$ gives no information, because the harmonic series diverges and a series whose terms are smaller than those of a divergent series may either converge or diverge.

Both cases actually occur. With $a_n = \dfrac{1}{n^2}$ the series converges, while with $a_n = \dfrac{1}{2n}$ the series diverges, and in both cases $0 \leq a_n \leq \dfrac{1}{n}$ holds.
