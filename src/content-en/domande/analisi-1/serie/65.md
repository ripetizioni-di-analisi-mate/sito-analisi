For which real values of $A$ does $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \dfrac{7^{- n} \left(A - 3\right)^{n}}{n}$ converge?

- $\left(-4, 10\right]$
- $\left(-4, 10\right)$
- $\left[-4, 10\right)$
- $\left[-4, 10\right]$

## Explanation

The ratio test gives $|A-3|<7$. The left endpoint gives the alternating harmonic series, and the right endpoint gives the harmonic series.
