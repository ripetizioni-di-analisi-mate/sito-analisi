Writing the general term as a power of $n$, what is the behavior of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{\sqrt[3]{n^2}}$?

- Converges
- Is oscillatory
- Diverges to $+\infty$
- Converges conditionally but not absolutely

## Explanation

Since $\sqrt[3]{n^2} = n^{\frac{2}{3}}$, the series is the **generalized harmonic series** with $\alpha = \dfrac{2}{3}$.

Since $\dfrac{2}{3} \leq 1$, the series **diverges** to $+\infty$. The terms are positive, so the series cannot be oscillatory.
