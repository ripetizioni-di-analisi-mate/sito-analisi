With $b_n = \dfrac{n}{n+1}$, what is the sum of the telescoping series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (b_n - b_{n+1}) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left( \frac{n}{n+1} - \frac{n+1}{n+2} \right)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $0$
- $-1$

## Explanation

Setting $b_n = \dfrac{n}{n+1}$, the general term is $b_n - b_{n+1}$, so the series is **telescoping** and
$$
s_N = b_1 - b_{N+1} = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{N+1}{N+2} .
$$
Since $b_{N+1} \to 1$, the sum is $\dfrac{1}{2} - 1 = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.

The result agrees with the sign of the terms: the sequence $\dfrac{n}{n+1}$ is increasing, so every term $b_n - b_{n+1}$ is negative. The value $\dfrac{1}{2}$ is obtained by taking $b_1$ as the sum, that is by assuming $b_n \to 0$.
