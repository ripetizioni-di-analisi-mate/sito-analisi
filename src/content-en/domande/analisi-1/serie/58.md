For a series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n$ the partial sums are known, $s_N = \dfrac{N^2}{N^2+1}$ for every $N \geq 1$. What is the value of the third term $a_3$?

- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The partial sum $s_3$ contains the terms $a_1, a_2, a_3$, while $s_2$ contains $a_1, a_2$, so $a_3 = s_3 - s_2$.

Since $s_3 = \dfrac{9}{10}$ and $s_2 = \dfrac{4}{5}$, one obtains
$$
a_3 = \frac{9}{10} - \frac{8}{10} = \frac{1}{10} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{9}{10}$ is the partial sum $s_3$, and $1$ is the sum of the series.
