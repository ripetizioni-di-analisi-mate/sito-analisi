Which description is correct for $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} - n + \sqrt{n^{2} + 2}$?

- Diverges to negative infinity
- Converges to a finite sum
- Diverges to positive infinity
- The partial sums oscillate without a limit

## Explanation

Rationalizing gives $\dfrac{2}{n + \sqrt{n^{2} + 2}}$. This is asymptotic to $\dfrac{1}{n}$, a multiple of the harmonic term.
