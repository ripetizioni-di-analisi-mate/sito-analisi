Which description is correct for $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} - n + \sqrt{n^{2} + 4}$?

- The partial sums oscillate without a limit
- Converges to a finite sum
- Diverges to positive infinity
- Diverges to negative infinity

## Explanation

Rationalizing, the general term is $\dfrac{4}{n + \sqrt{n^{2} + 4}}$. It is asymptotically equivalent to $\dfrac{2}{n}$, a multiple of the general term of the harmonic series.
