A power series centered at $x_0 = 1$ has radius of convergence $R = 2$. At which of the following points does the series certainly converge?

- $x = 2.5$
- $x = 3$
- $x = -1.5$
- $x = 4$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the **radius of convergence**, the series converges absolutely where $|x - 1| < 2$, that is for $-1 < x < 3$, and does not converge where $|x - 1| > 2$.

The point $x = 2.5$ satisfies $|2.5 - 1| = 1.5 < 2$, so the series certainly converges there. The point $x = 3$ is an endpoint of the interval, where the behavior depends on the series, while at $x = -1.5$ and at $x = 4$ the distance from the center exceeds $2$ and the series does not converge.
