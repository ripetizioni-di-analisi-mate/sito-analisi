For which real values of $A$ does $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \dfrac{5^{- n} \left(A - 4\right)^{n}}{n^{2}}$ converge?

- $\left(-1, 9\right]$
- $\left(-1, 9\right)$
- $\left[-1, 9\right]$
- $\left[-1, 9\right)$

## Explanation

The ratio test applies inside $|A-4|<5$. At both endpoints the series of absolute values has general term $1/n^2$, so both endpoints are included.
