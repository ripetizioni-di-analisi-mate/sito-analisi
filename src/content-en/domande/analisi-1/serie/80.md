Which description is correct for $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} 3^{- n} n^{- n} n!$?

- Diverges to positive infinity
- The partial sums oscillate without a limit
- Converges to a finite sum
- Diverges to negative infinity

## Explanation

In the consecutive-term ratio the factorial contributes $n+1$. The ratio simplifies to $(n/(n+1))^n/3$, which tends to $\dfrac{1}{3 e}$.
