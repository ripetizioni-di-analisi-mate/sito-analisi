Recognizing an elementary Taylor series, what is the value of $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{2^n}{n!}$?

- $2e$
- $e^2 - 1$
- $e^2$
- $\sqrt{e}$

## Explanation

For every $x \in \mathbb{R}$ the Taylor series $e^x = \displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n!}$ holds.

With $x = 2$ one obtains exactly the given series, hence its sum is $e^2$. The value $e^2 - 1$ would be the sum starting from $n = 1$, while the series also contains the term with $n = 0$, which equals $1$.
