- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}$

## Explanation

The series is a **geometric series** with ratio $q = \dfrac{2}{5}$. Since $|q| < 1$, the series converges.

The index starts at $n = 0$, so the first term is $q^0 = 1$ and the sum is the first term divided by $1 - q$:
$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \left( \frac{2}{5} \right)^{\! n} = \frac{1}{1 - \dfrac{2}{5}} = \frac{1}{\dfrac{3}{5}} = \frac{5}{3} .
$$
Therefore the sum is $\dfrac{5}{3}$.
