Which description is correct for $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \ln{\left(\dfrac{n + 4}{n + 5} \right)}$?

- Diverges to negative infinity
- Converges to a finite sum
- The partial sums oscillate without a limit
- Diverges to positive infinity

## Explanation

In the sum through $N$ the middle logarithms cancel. This leaves $\ln{\left(5 \right)}$ minus the logarithm of $N+5$.
