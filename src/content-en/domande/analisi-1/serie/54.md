The differences $\arctan(n+1) - \arctan n$ are positive and tend to $0$. What is the sum of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \bigl( \arctan(n+1) - \arctan n \bigr)$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

The partial sums are computed by adding the differences, which cancel in pairs:
$$
s_N = \sum_{n=1}^{N} \bigl( \arctan(n+1) - \arctan n \bigr) = \arctan(N+1) - \arctan 1 .
$$
Since $\arctan(N+1) \to \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $\arctan 1 = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$, the series **converges** with sum $\dfrac{\pi}{2} - \dfrac{\pi}{4} = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

Here the difference is $b_{n+1} - b_n$ with $b_n = \arctan n$, so the sum is the limit of $b_n$ minus the first term $b_1$; the value $-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ is obtained by reversing this difference.
