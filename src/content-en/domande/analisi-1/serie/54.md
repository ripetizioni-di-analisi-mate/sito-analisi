The series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(b_n-b_{n+1})$ converges and $b_1=0$. All $b_n$ with $n\ge2$ are doubled, while $b_1$ is left unchanged. Which relationship holds for every series satisfying these data?

- The new sum is twice the original sum.
- The new sum is minus twice the original one, reversing the difference in the new terms.
- The new sum is always zero.
- The new sum equals the original sum plus 2.

## Explanation

The original partial sum is $b_1-b_{N+1}=-b_{N+1}$. The modified one is $-2b_{N+1}$. Both expressions converge, so the same identity holds between the sums of the series.
