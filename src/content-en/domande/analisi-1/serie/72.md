The general term of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=2}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(\ln n)^n}$ is an $n$-th power. What is the behavior of the series?

- Diverges to $+\infty$
- Converges
- The root test gives no information
- Is oscillatory

## Explanation

The general term is positive for $n \geq 2$ and is an $n$-th power, so the **root test** applies:
$$
\sqrt[n]{a_n} = \frac{1}{\ln n} \to 0 .
$$
Since $0 < 1$, the series **converges**.
