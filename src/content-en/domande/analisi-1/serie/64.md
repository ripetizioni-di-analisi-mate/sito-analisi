The series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n$ converges, and $s_N$ denotes its $N$-th partial sum. What is $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to +\infty} (s_{2n} - s_n)$?

- The sum of the series
- $0$
- $+\infty$
- It depends on the series

## Explanation

Denoting by $s$ the sum of the series, we have $s_N \to s$. The sequences $s_{2n}$ and $s_n$ are subsequences of $s_N$, so both tend to $s$.

By the algebra of limits, $s_{2n} - s_n \to s - s = 0$, whatever the convergent series is.
