For which real values of $A$ does $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \dfrac{5^{- n} \left(A - 2\right)^{n}}{n}$ converge?

- $\left(-3, 7\right]$
- $\left(-3, 7\right)$
- $\left[-3, 7\right]$
- $\left[-3, 7\right)$

## Explanation

The ratio test gives $|A-2|<5$. The left endpoint gives the alternating harmonic series, and the right endpoint gives the harmonic series.
