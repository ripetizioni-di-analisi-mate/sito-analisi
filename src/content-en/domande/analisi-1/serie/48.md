What is the sum of $\sum_{n=2}^{+\infty} \left(- \dfrac{4}{9}\right)^{n}$?

- $\dfrac{9}{13}$
- $\dfrac{16}{45}$
- $- \dfrac{4}{13}$
- $\dfrac{16}{117}$

## Explanation

The first term is $\dfrac{16}{81}$ and the ratio is $- \dfrac{4}{9}$. The sum is the first term divided by one minus the ratio.
