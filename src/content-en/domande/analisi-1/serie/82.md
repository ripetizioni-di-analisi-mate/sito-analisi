What upper bound on the error is obtained using the first omitted term after 2 terms of $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \dfrac{\left(-1\right)^{n}}{\left(n + 5\right)^{2}}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{49}$
- $\dfrac{1}{64}$

## Explanation

The absolute values decrease to zero. The alternating series test uses the absolute value of the term with index $3$.
