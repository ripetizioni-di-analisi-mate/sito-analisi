The sequence $(a_n)$ is defined by $a_1 = 1$ and $a_{n+1} = \dfrac{n}{2n+1}\, a_n$ for every $n \geq 1$. What is the behavior of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n$?

- Diverges to $+\infty$
- It cannot be determined without the explicit formula of $a_n$
- Converges
- Is oscillatory

## Explanation

Since $a_1 > 0$ and every factor $\dfrac{n}{2n+1}$ is positive, all terms are positive. The ratio can be read directly from the definition:
$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{n}{2n+1} \to \frac{1}{2} .
$$
Since $\dfrac{1}{2} < 1$, by the **ratio test** the series **converges**, with no need for an explicit formula for $a_n$.
