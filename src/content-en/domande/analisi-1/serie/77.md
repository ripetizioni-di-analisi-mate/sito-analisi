Which description is correct for $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \left(3 n + 7\right)^{n} \left(7 n + 2\right)^{- n}$?

- Converges to a finite sum
- Diverges to positive infinity
- The partial sums oscillate without a limit
- Diverges to negative infinity

## Explanation

The $n$th root of the term tends to $\dfrac{3}{7}$, below one. Apply the root test.
