For which real values of $A$ does $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \dfrac{n^{A}}{n^{5} + 1}$ converge?

- $\left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 4\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 5\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 4\right]$

## Explanation

The summand is asymptotic to $n^{A-5}$. The power series of reciprocal terms converges if the exponent is below $-1$.
