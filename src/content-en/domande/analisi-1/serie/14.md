- $[-2, 2]$
- $[-2, 2)$
- $\left[ -\dfrac{1}{2}, \dfrac{1}{2} \right)$

## Explanation

The coefficients are $c_n = \dfrac{1}{n\, 2^n}$, so
$$
\frac{|c_{n+1}|}{|c_n|} = \frac{n\, 2^n}{(n+1)\, 2^{n+1}} = \frac{n}{2(n+1)} \to \frac{1}{2} .
$$
Therefore the **radius of convergence** is $R = 2$, and the series converges absolutely for $|x| < 2$ and does not converge for $|x| > 2$.

For $x = 2$ the series becomes $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n}$, that is the harmonic series, which diverges. For $x = -2$ the series becomes $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n}{n}$, which converges by the **Leibniz test**, because $\dfrac{1}{n}$ is positive, decreasing and tends to $0$.

Hence the set of convergence is $[-2, 2)$.
