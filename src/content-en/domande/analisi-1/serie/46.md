What is the sum of $\sum_{n=2}^{+\infty} \left(- \dfrac{2}{5}\right)^{n}$?

- $\dfrac{4}{35}$
- $\dfrac{4}{15}$
- $- \dfrac{2}{7}$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}$

## Explanation

The first term is $\dfrac{4}{25}$ and the ratio is $- \dfrac{2}{5}$. The sum is the first term divided by one minus the ratio.
