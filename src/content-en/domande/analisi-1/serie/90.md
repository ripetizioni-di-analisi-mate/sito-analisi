The power series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} c_n x^n$ has coefficients $c_n = 1$ for even $n$ and $c_n = 2$ for odd $n$. What is its radius of convergence?

- $R = 1$
- $R = 2$
- $R = \dfrac{1}{2}$
- The radius cannot be determined, because $\dfrac{|c_{n+1}|}{|c_n|}$ has no limit

## Explanation

The ratio $\dfrac{|c_{n+1}|}{|c_n|}$ equals alternately $2$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so it has no limit and the ratio test cannot be used.

The limit of the root, instead, exists. Indeed $\sqrt[n]{|c_n|}$ equals $1$ for even $n$ and $\sqrt[n]{2}$ for odd $n$, and since $\sqrt[n]{2} \to 1$ we have $\sqrt[n]{|c_n|} \to 1$. Hence the radius of convergence is $R = 1$.

The radius exists for every power series; the lack of a limit for the ratio only prevents computing it with the ratio.
