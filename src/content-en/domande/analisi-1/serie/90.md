For what exponent $p$ does the ratio of the term of $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \dfrac{4 n + 5}{n^{3} + n}$ to $1/n^p$ tend to a positive finite value?

- $2$
- $1$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The numerator and denominator have degrees one and three. Multiplication by $n^2$ gives the limit $4$.
