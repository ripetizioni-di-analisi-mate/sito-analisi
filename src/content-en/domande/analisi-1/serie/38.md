Consider the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (-1)^n b_n$ with $b_n = \dfrac{2 + (-1)^n}{n}$. Which hypothesis of the Leibniz test is not satisfied?

- $b_n \geq 0$ for every $n$
- $b_n \to 0$
- All the hypotheses are satisfied
- $b_{n+1} \leq b_n$ for every $n$

## Explanation

Since $1 \leq 2 + (-1)^n \leq 3$, we have $\dfrac{1}{n} \leq b_n \leq \dfrac{3}{n}$, so $b_n \geq 0$ and, by the squeeze theorem, $b_n \to 0$.

The sequence is not decreasing, because $b_1 = 1$ and $b_2 = \dfrac{3}{2}$; more generally $b_{2k} = \dfrac{3}{2k}$ is greater than $b_{2k-1} = \dfrac{1}{2k-1}$ for every $k \geq 1$. Therefore the **monotonicity** hypothesis is not satisfied, not even eventually.

Hence the Leibniz test does not apply. In this case the series does not converge, because $(-1)^n b_n = \dfrac{2(-1)^n}{n} + \dfrac{1}{n}$ is the sum of the term of a convergent series and the term of the harmonic series.
