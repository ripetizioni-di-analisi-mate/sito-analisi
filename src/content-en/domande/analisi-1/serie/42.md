For which $x \in \mathbb{R}$ does the power series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n^n x^n$ converge?

- For every $x \in \mathbb{R}$
- Only for $x = 0$
- For $|x| < 1$
- For $|x| < \dfrac{1}{e}$

## Explanation

The coefficients are $c_n = n^n$, so $\sqrt[n]{|c_n|} = n \to +\infty$. By the root formula for the radius, with $\ell = +\infty$ the **radius of convergence** is $R = 0$.

Therefore the series does not converge at any point $x \neq 0$. Indeed for $x \neq 0$ the general term $(n x)^n$ has absolute value tending to $+\infty$, so the necessary condition is not satisfied.

At $x = 0$ all the terms are zero and the series converges. Consequently the series converges **only for** $x = 0$.
