- Converges conditionally but not absolutely
- Diverges to $+\infty$
- Is indeterminate

## Explanation

The general term is positive and is an $n$-th power, so the **root test** applies. The $n$-th root of the general term is
$$
\sqrt[n]{\left( \frac{2n+1}{3n+2} \right)^{\! n}} = \frac{2n+1}{3n+2} .
$$
The numerator and the denominator have degree $1$, therefore the root tends to $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Since $\dfrac{2}{3} < 1$, the series **converges**.
