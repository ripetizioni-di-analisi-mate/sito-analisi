What is the radius of convergence $R$ of the power series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (\arctan n)^n\, x^n$?

- $R = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $R = 0$
- $R = +\infty$
- $R = \dfrac{2}{\pi}$

## Explanation

The coefficients $c_n = (\arctan n)^n$ are positive for $n \geq 1$ and are $n$-th powers, so one computes the limit of their $n$-th root:
$$
\sqrt[n]{|c_n|} = \arctan n \to \frac{\pi}{2} .
$$
By the theorem on the **ratio and root tests for the radius**, the radius of convergence is the reciprocal of this limit, hence $R = \dfrac{2}{\pi}$.

The value $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ is obtained by taking as radius the limit of the root instead of its reciprocal.
