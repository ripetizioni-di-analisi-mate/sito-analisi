At its center $x = 2$, what is the sum of the power series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{n+3}{n+1}\, (x-2)^n$?

- $0$
- $3$
- $1$
- The series does not converge at $x = 2$

## Explanation

The point $x = 2$ is the center of the series. For $n \geq 1$ we have $(x - 2)^n = 0$, while for $n = 0$ the convention $(x - 2)^0 = 1$ holds.

Hence at $x = 2$ only the term with $n = 0$ remains, that is the coefficient $c_0 = \dfrac{0 + 3}{0 + 1} = 3$, and the series converges with sum $3$.
