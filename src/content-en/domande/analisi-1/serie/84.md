What upper bound on the error is obtained using the first omitted term after 4 terms of $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \dfrac{\left(-1\right)^{n}}{\left(n + 9\right)^{2}}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{14}$
- $\dfrac{1}{196}$
- $\dfrac{1}{25}$
- $\dfrac{1}{169}$

## Explanation

The absolute values decrease to zero. The alternating series test uses the absolute value of the term with index $5$.
