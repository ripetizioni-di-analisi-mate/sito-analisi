The terms of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \cos \frac{n\pi}{2}$ repeat with period $4$. How does the series behave?

- It is oscillatory
- It converges with sum $0$
- It converges with sum $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- It diverges to $-\infty$

## Explanation

The terms repeat with period $4$ and are $0, -1, 0, 1, 0, -1, 0, 1, \dots$. The partial sums are therefore $0, -1, -1, 0, 0, -1, -1, 0, \dots$ and take both the value $0$ and the value $-1$ infinitely many times.

Hence the partial sums have no limit, and the series is **oscillatory**. Consistently with this, the general term does not tend to $0$, so the series does not converge, and the partial sums are bounded, so the series does not diverge.
