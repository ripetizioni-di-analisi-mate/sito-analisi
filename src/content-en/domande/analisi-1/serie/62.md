For which real values of $A$ does $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \dfrac{n^{A}}{n^{7} + 1}$ converge?

- $\left(-\infty, 6\right]$
- $\left(6, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 6\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 7\right)$

## Explanation

The general term is asymptotically equivalent to $n^{A-7}$. The series of reciprocal powers converges if the exponent is below $-1$.
