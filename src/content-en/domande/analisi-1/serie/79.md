Which description is correct for $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} 2^{- n} n^{- n} n!$?

- Diverges to positive infinity
- Converges to a finite sum
- Diverges to negative infinity
- The partial sums oscillate without a limit

## Explanation

In the consecutive-term ratio the factorial contributes $n+1$. The ratio simplifies to $(n/(n+1))^n/2$, which tends to $\dfrac{1}{2 e}$.
