In the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{100^n}{n!}$ the first terms are increasing. Which statement is true?

- The series diverges to $+\infty$, because the first terms increase
- The ratio test gives no information, because the ratio exceeds $1$ for the first indices
- The series converges, because the ratio $\dfrac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}$ tends to $0$
- The series converges with sum $e^{100}$

## Explanation

The ratio is
$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{100^{n+1}}{(n+1)!} \cdot \frac{n!}{100^n} = \frac{100}{n+1} .
$$
For $n < 99$ the ratio exceeds $1$, which explains why the first terms increase. The **ratio test**, however, uses the limit, which equals $0 < 1$, hence the series **converges**. The behavior of a series does not depend on its first terms.

The sum is obtained from the Taylor series of the exponential with $x = 100$; since the term with $n = 0$ is missing, it equals $e^{100} - 1$.
