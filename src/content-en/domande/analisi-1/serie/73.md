The terms of a series are positive and $\dfrac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}\le\dfrac{1}{2}$ for every $n\ge5$. If $a_5=8$, what upper bound for the tail $\sum_{n=6}^\infty a_n$ follows from these data?

- $4$
- $2$
- $8$
- $1$

## Explanation

We have $a_6\le4$, $a_7\le2$ and so on. The tail is bounded above by $4+2+1+\cdots=8$. The progression attaining equality at every step shows that a smaller upper bound is not guaranteed.
