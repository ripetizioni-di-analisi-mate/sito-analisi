- Is indeterminate
- Converges conditionally but not absolutely
- Converges

## Explanation

The general term $a_n = \dfrac{(2n)!}{(n!)^2\, 5^n}$ is positive, so the **ratio test** applies. Since $(2n+2)! = (2n+2)(2n+1)\,(2n)!$ and $(n+1)! = (n+1)\, n!$, one has
$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{(2n+2)(2n+1)}{(n+1)^2 \cdot 5} = \frac{2(2n+1)}{5(n+1)} .
$$
The numerator and the denominator have degree $1$, therefore the ratio tends to $\dfrac{4}{5}$. Since $\dfrac{4}{5} < 1$, the series **converges**.
