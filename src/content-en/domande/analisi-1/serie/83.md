What first-omitted-term error bound is obtained after 3 terms of $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \dfrac{\left(-1\right)^{n}}{\left(n + 7\right)^{2}}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{100}$
- $\dfrac{1}{121}$
- $\dfrac{1}{11}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$

## Explanation

The magnitudes decrease to zero. The alternating series bound uses the magnitude of the term with index $4$.
