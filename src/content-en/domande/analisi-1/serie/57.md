Which of the following sequences is oscillatory, that is, has no limit?

- $a_n = \sin(n\pi)$
- $a_n = \dfrac{(-1)^n}{n}$
- $a_n = \cos(n\pi)$
- $a_n = \left( 1 + \dfrac{1}{n} \right)^{\! n}$

## Explanation

For every integer $n$ we have $\cos(n\pi) = (-1)^n$, so the sequence takes alternately the values $-1$ and $1$ and has no limit.

On the other hand $\sin(n\pi) = 0$ for every $n$, so that sequence converges to $0$. Since $\left| \dfrac{(-1)^n}{n} \right| = \dfrac{1}{n} \to 0$, the sequence $\dfrac{(-1)^n}{n}$ also converges to $0$, and $\left( 1 + \dfrac{1}{n} \right)^{\! n}$ converges to $e$.
