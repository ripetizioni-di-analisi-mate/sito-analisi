What is the sum of $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} - \dfrac{1}{n + 7} + \dfrac{1}{n + 3}$?

- $\dfrac{743}{840}$
- $\dfrac{319}{420}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $- \dfrac{319}{420}$

## Explanation

Common reciprocal terms cancel in the partial sums. At the beginning, reciprocals from $4$ through $7$ remain, while the trailing terms tend to zero.
