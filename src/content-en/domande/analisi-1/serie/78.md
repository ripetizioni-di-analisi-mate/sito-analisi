Which description is correct for $\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty} \left(4 n + 9\right)^{n} \left(9 n + 5\right)^{- n}$?

- Diverges to negative infinity
- Diverges to positive infinity
- The partial sums oscillate without a limit
- Converges to a finite sum

## Explanation

The $n$th root of the general term tends to $\dfrac{4}{9}$, which is less than one. The root test applies.
