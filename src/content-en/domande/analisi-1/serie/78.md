What can be said about the alternating series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n}{n!}$?

- Converges absolutely
- Converges conditionally but not absolutely
- Is oscillatory
- Diverges

## Explanation

The series of absolute values is $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n!}$. By the ratio test
$$
\frac{n!}{(n+1)!} = \frac{1}{n+1} \to 0 < 1 ,
$$
so the series of absolute values converges and the given series **converges absolutely**. From the Taylor series of the exponential with $x = -1$ its sum is $\dfrac{1}{e}$.
