From the series with terms $a_n=\dfrac{3^{n-1}}{4^n}$, $n\ge1$, keep only the odd-indexed terms. What is their sum?

- $\dfrac47$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$, starting at index $2$ and then skipping one index each time.
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Consecutive selected indices differ by two, so the ratio becomes $(\dfrac{3}{4})^2=\dfrac{9}{16}$. The first term is $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and the sum is $\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{4}}{1-\dfrac{9}{16}}=\dfrac{4}{7}$.
