- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

Since $3^{n-1} = \dfrac{3^n}{3}$, the general term can be written as
$$
\frac{3^{n-1}}{4^n} = \frac{1}{3} \left( \frac{3}{4} \right)^{\! n} .
$$
So the series is $\dfrac{1}{3}$ times a **geometric series** with ratio $q = \dfrac{3}{4}$, with $|q| < 1$, and by the linearity of the sum the constant $\dfrac{1}{3}$ can be taken outside.

The index starts at $n = 1$, therefore the first term of the geometric series is $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and its sum is
$$
\frac{\dfrac{3}{4}}{1 - \dfrac{3}{4}} = 3 .
$$
Consequently the sum of the given series is $\dfrac{1}{3} \cdot 3 = 1$.
