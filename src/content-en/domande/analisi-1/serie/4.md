- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$

## Explanation

The difference between $\dfrac{1}{n(n+1)}$ and $\dfrac{1}{(n+1)(n+2)}$ is $\dfrac{(n+2) - n}{n(n+1)(n+2)} = \dfrac{2}{n(n+1)(n+2)}$, so
$$
\frac{1}{n(n+1)(n+2)} = \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{1}{n(n+1)} - \frac{1}{(n+1)(n+2)} \right) .
$$
Therefore the series is **telescoping** with $b_n = \dfrac{1}{2\, n(n+1)}$.

Since $b_n \to 0$, the sum is $b_1 - 0 = \dfrac{1}{2 \cdot 1 \cdot 2} = \dfrac{1}{4}$.
