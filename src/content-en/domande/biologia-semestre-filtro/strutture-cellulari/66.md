After fusion, which system allows SNARE reuse by disassembling their complexes?

- The NSF ATPase, recruited by SNAP proteins.
- SNARE pairing on opposing membranes before fusion.
- COPII assembly extracting SNAREs from their complexes.
- Rab and its effectors, directly separating SNARE helices by consuming GTP.

## Explanation

After fusion, SNAREs are together in the same membrane. NSF, with SNAP, uses ATP to separate the complex and make components available for new cycles. This recycling differs from initial pairing between SNAREs on opposing membranes.
