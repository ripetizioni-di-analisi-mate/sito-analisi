During apoptosis, phosphatidylserine increases in the outer leaflet. Which membrane property is changed?

- The phosphatidylserine head-group charge, which becomes positive.
- Total phosphatidylserine amount, which must increase without redistribution.
- Only lateral phosphatidylserine mobility, without movement between leaflets.
- The asymmetry of lipid distribution.

## Explanation

Phosphatidylserine is normally enriched in the cytosolic leaflet. Its external exposure reduces that asymmetric distribution and can favour recognition by phagocytes. The observation concerns lipid positioning between leaflets, without implying a change in DNA or protein synthesis.
