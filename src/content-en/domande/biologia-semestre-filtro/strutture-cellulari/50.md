Which signal-receptor association is correct for peroxisomal import?

- PTS1 with PEX5; PTS2 with PEX7.
- PTS1 with SRP; PTS2 with importin.
- PTS1 with PEX7; PTS2 with PEX5.
- PTS1 with TOM; PTS2 with KDEL.

## Explanation

The two classes of peroxisomal signals use dedicated receptors. PEX5 recognizes many carboxy-terminal PTS1 signals, whereas PEX7 participates in PTS2 recognition. Nuclear and secretory-pathway signals belong to different systems, even though they share the general principle of selective targeting.
