A cargo-exportin-Ran-GTP complex reaches the cytosol. Which event normally favours cargo release?

- Hydrolysis of Ran-bound GTP, stimulated by RanGAP.
- ATP hydrolysis by the exportin as the driver of release.
- Cytosolic exchange of GDP for GTP on Ran, catalysed by RanGEF.
- Proteolytic cleavage of the NES during every export cycle.

## Explanation

In classical export, Ran-GTP stabilizes the complex in the nucleus. In the cytosol RanGAP stimulates hydrolysis, changing Ran's state and promoting dissociation. RanGEF instead promotes nucleotide exchange and maintains nuclear Ran-GTP; the two activities occupy different compartments.
