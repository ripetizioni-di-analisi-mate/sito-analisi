A peroxisomal protein retains its folded structure during import. Which property makes this observation plausible?

- The peroxisomal pathway can import already folded cargo.
- Import depends on a translocon accepting only fully extended chains.
- PEX5 must first completely denature every cargo to transport it.
- Import occurs only during synthesis, before the cargo can fold.

## Explanation

The ability to import folded proteins distinguishes the peroxisomal pathway from many mitochondrial translocations. Cargo is recognized through signals and receptors and transferred by specialized machinery. A membrane's presence alone therefore does not impose the same conformational requirement on every organelle.
