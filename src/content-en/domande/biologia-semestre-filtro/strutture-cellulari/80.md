In reconstituted microtubules, an associated protein reduces catastrophe frequency without moving along the track. Tubulin amount and GTP availability remain equal. Which function is directly supported?

- Directional cargo transport by a motor moving along the microtubule.
- Regulation of microtubule stability by a MAP.
- Nucleation of new microtubules as the direct explanation for transitions observed in existing ones.
- Reversal of each microtubule’s polarity by exchanging its ends.

## Explanation

Catastrophes are transitions from growth to shortening. Reducing their frequency in existing microtubules supports a stabilizing function without requiring cargo transport or new nucleation. MAPs can regulate polymer dynamics and interactions; the absence of movement along the track and the stated controls help distinguish this activity from a motor’s function.
