A cotransporter accumulates glucose using favourable Na⁺ entry. Which intervention directly reduces the energy source for cotransport?

- Maintaining the Na⁺ gradient and adding functional cotransporters.
- Increasing only external glucose while keeping the Na⁺ gradient unchanged.
- Dissipating the Na⁺ electrochemical gradient.
- Increasing the favourable Na⁺ gradient.

## Explanation

In secondary active transport, favourable movement of one solute powers unfavourable movement of another. Dissipating the sodium gradient reduces the energy available to accumulate glucose. ATP hydrolysis occurs in the pump supporting the gradient, without necessarily being a step of the cotransporter itself.
