Lysosomal V-ATPase is inhibited without initially changing hydrolase numbers. Which effect is plausible?

- Unchanged hydrolytic activity because preserving enzyme numbers is sufficient.
- Increased pH accompanied by generally increased acid-hydrolase activity.
- Increased luminal pH and reduced efficiency of many acid hydrolases.
- Lower pH because inhibition prevents protons from leaving the lysosome.

## Explanation

V-ATPase uses energy to maintain an acidic lumen. If pumping is inhibited, leaks and exchanges can raise pH, reducing activity of enzymes adapted to acidity. Function therefore depends both on enzyme presence and on compartment conditions.
