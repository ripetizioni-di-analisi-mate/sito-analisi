For a ΔF508 CFTR variant, quality control removes many molecules before they complete the secretory pathway. Which consequence is most directly expected?

- A larger fraction of mature surface CFTR per molecule synthesized.
- Reduced delivery of CFTR channels to the plasma membrane.
- Reduced CFTR gene transcription as a necessary effect of folding quality control.
- Increased conductance of each individual channel as a direct consequence of quality control.

## Explanation

A folding defect can cause retention and removal during maturation. The immediate consequence is reduced delivery of CFTR to the surface, even if the gene is transcribed and protein synthesized. The example distinguishes amount produced from functional amount correctly localized.
