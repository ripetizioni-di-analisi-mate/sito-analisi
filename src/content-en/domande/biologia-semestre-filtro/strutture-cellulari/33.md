Where does glycolysis occur in an animal eukaryotic cell?

- In the intermembrane space.
- In the endoplasmic reticulum lumen.
- In the mitochondrial matrix.
- In the cytosol.

## Explanation

Glycolysis converts glucose into pyruvate in the cytosol and supplies two net ATP per glucose. Pyruvate can then enter the mitochondrion for aerobic oxidation. Distinguishing locations explains why a cell without mitochondria can still produce glycolytic ATP.
