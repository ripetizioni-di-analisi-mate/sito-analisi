A multipass mitochondrial carrier with internal signals must enter the inner membrane. Which complex is typically involved after TOM?

- TIM23 as the pathway for cleavable amino-terminal presequences.
- OXA as an insertase from the matrix side after local translation.
- SAM.
- TIM22.

## Explanation

The TIM22 pathway inserts several multipass carriers into the inner membrane using signals that may be internal to the chain. TIM23 is associated with many presequence-containing precursors. Recognizing cargo class avoids assigning every mitochondrial protein to the same final step.
