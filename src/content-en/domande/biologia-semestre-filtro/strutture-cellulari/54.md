Chains of an incompletely assembled immunoglobulin are retained in the reticulum. Which interpretation is consistent?

- Retention shows that the signal peptide prevented entry into the reticulum.
- Retention indicates a permanent reticulum destination even if assembly is completed.
- Quality control limits export of incorrectly assembled products.
- Chain assembly normally occurs only after secretion, so retention prevents the physiological step.

## Explanation

The chains have already been synthesized, so the issue concerns maturation and assembly. Reticulum systems can retain them during folding and direct them to degradation if the defect persists. Quality control reduces export of incomplete molecules into the secretory pathway.
