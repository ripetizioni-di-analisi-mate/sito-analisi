Selective blockade of COPII assembly causes early accumulation of a correctly folded secretory cargo. Which compartment is most directly involved?

- The mature secretory-granule compartment as the first blocked step.
- The endoplasmic reticulum.
- The early endosome.
- The trans-Golgi network as the first blocked step.

## Explanation

COPII participates in formation of carriers leaving the reticulum. If this step is blocked, cargo can enter and fold in the reticulum but cannot proceed normally towards the Golgi. The result identifies an export defect, distinct from initial recognition by SRP.
