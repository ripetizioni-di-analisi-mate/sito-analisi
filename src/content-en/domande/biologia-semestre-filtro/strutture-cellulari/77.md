Which component promotes microtubule nucleation at the centrosome?

- The gamma-TuRC complex.
- Arp2/3.
- Formins.
- The motor kinesin responsible for anterograde transport.

## Explanation

The gamma-TuRC complex provides an organization favouring initiation of the tubulin polymer. Nucleation differs from end elongation and cargo transport along the microtubule. The centrosome thus coordinates organization of many microtubules in numerous animal cells.
