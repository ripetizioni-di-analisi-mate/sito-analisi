Which pair correctly associates a protein with mitochondrial fusion?

- DRP1: outer-membrane fusion; OPA1: inner-membrane fusion.
- OPA1: outer fusion; mitofusins: inner fusion.
- Mitofusins: outer-membrane fusion; DRP1: inner-membrane fusion.
- Mitofusins: outer membrane; OPA1: inner membrane.

## Explanation

Fusion must coordinate both membranes. Mitofusins participate in the outer step, whereas OPA1 acts in the inner one. DRP1 is associated mainly with fission; confusing the processes would reverse predictions about network fragmentation after a selective defect.
