A protein with a presequence is destined for the mitochondrial matrix. Which sequence of steps is typical?

- TOM in the outer membrane, then SAM for release into the matrix.
- TOM in the outer membrane, then TIM23 in the inner membrane.
- TOM in the outer membrane, then OXA as an entry channel from the intermembrane side.
- TOM in the outer membrane, then TIM22 for complete translocation into the matrix.

## Explanation

The protein must cross two membranes. TOM provides outer entry for many cytosol-synthesized proteins; TIM23 participates in the presequence pathway towards the matrix. The other sequences describe different destinations and do not deliver that cargo to the mitochondrial matrix.
