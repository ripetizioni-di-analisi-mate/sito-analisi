A PEX-gene variant impairs peroxisomal biogenesis and several metabolic pathways. Which connection is correct?

- It implies depletion of all substrates while import and assembly remain normal.
- It can belong to Zellweger spectrum disorders.
- It demonstrates a defect confined to catalase without effects on other peroxisomal functions.
- It necessarily indicates an isolated defect of one hydrolase with normal biogenesis.

## Explanation

Peroxins participate in biogenesis and import, so a defect can reduce several organelle functions simultaneously. Zellweger spectrum disorders include PEX-gene defects. The association concerns the cellular mechanism and does not amount to diagnosis from a single sign.
