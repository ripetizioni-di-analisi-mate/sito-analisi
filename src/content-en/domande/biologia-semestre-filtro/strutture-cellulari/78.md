A microtubule rapidly changes from growth to shortening after losing its stabilizing cap. What is this transition called?

- Nucleation.
- Treadmilling as the definition of the transition.
- Rescue.
- Catastrophe.

## Explanation

Catastrophe is the transition to rapid shortening in dynamic instability. GTP hydrolysis and loss of the stabilizing cap make this transition more likely. Rescue instead describes return from shortening to growth, so it indicates the opposite direction.
