Why must the inner mitochondrial membrane have low spontaneous proton permeability?

- To preserve the gradient that can power ATP synthase.
- To permit ATP synthesis even when the gradient is completely dissipated.
- To prevent proton return even through ATP synthase.
- To make pH and voltage identical on both sides.

## Explanation

If protons returned freely to the matrix, the chain-generated gradient would dissipate without effective coupling to ATP synthesis. Membrane selectivity allows return to be regulated through specific proteins. Metabolites can still cross through dedicated carriers.
