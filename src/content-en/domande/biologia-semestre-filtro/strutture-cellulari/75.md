The PINK1-Parkin pathway promotes removal of damaged mitochondria in a cell model. Which term describes this selectivity?

- Reticulophagy, selecting portions of the endoplasmic reticulum.
- Mitophagy.
- Pexophagy, selectively targeting peroxisomes.
- Nonselective autophagy, sampling cytoplasm without enriching damaged mitochondria.

## Explanation

Mitophagy is selective mitochondrial removal through the autophagic-lysosomal system. PINK1 and Parkin can contribute to recognition of damaged mitochondria. The term identifies the cargo; it does not imply that every form of mitophagy in every context necessarily uses the same regulators.
