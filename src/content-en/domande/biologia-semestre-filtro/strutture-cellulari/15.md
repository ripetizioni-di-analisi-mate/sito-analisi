At equal temperature and mean tail length, the proportion of phospholipids with cis double bonds increases. Which direct effect is expected?

- Greater tail packing and lower fluidity.
- Less tail packing and greater fluidity.
- Unchanged fluidity, because only tail length, not geometry, matters.
- Looser packing but lower fluidity because of the double bonds.

## Explanation

Cis double bonds introduce bends that make contact between neighbouring tails less regular. Under the specified equal conditions, packing decreases and fluidity increases. The comparison holds temperature and tail length constant specifically to isolate the effect of unsaturation.
