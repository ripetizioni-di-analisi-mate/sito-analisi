A soluble reticulum-resident protein loses its KDEL signal and is secreted more readily. Which signal function is supported?

- Initial nascent-chain recognition by SRP.
- Anterograde reticulum exit through direct KDEL recognition by COPII.
- Retrieval from the Golgi to the reticulum after escape.
- Sorting from the Golgi to lysosomes through the mannose-6-phosphate receptor.

## Explanation

A resident protein can leave the reticulum with anterograde traffic. KDEL enables recognition for retrieval, contributing to stable localization. Losing this signal favours progression towards secretion without necessarily implying defective synthesis or initial reticulum entry.
