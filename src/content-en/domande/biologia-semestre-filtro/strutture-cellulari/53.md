Which process connects glycosylation with folding control in the reticulum?

- Immediate complete removal of every oligosaccharide before folding control.
- Mannose-6-phosphate addition as the signal initiating the calnexin cycle in the reticulum.
- Interaction of glycoproteins with calnexin and calreticulin.
- Binding of luminal glycans to cytosolic ribosomes to control folding.

## Explanation

Calnexin and calreticulin recognize specific oligosaccharide states and participate in glycoprotein quality control. Sugars can therefore contribute to maturation as well as alter the final product's properties. This control occurs in the reticulum and precedes sorting of many proteins through the Golgi.
