A peroxisomal protein ends in SKL. Adding a long tail after SKL reduces its import. Which explanation is consistent?

- Any internal SKL signal is equivalent to a terminal one, so its position cannot explain the result.
- The tail converts SKL into a PTS2 signal directly recognized by PEX7.
- The modification impairs carboxy-terminal presentation of the PTS1 signal.
- PEX5 recognizes SKL only at the amino terminus.

## Explanation

PEX5 recognizes many PTS1 signals exposed at the carboxy terminus. Moving SKL inside the chain can hinder recognition even though the sequence remains present. Peroxisomes import cytosol-produced proteins and do not possess their own translation machinery.
