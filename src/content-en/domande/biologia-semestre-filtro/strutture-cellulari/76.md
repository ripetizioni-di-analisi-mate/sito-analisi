Which description distinguishes chaperone-mediated autophagy from macroautophagy?

- Cargoes are ubiquitinated and degraded exclusively by the cytosolic proteasome.
- Specific proteins cross the lysosomal membrane without enclosure in an autophagosome.
- Cargoes are enclosed in double-membrane autophagosomes before degradation.
- Cargo enters through lysosomal-membrane invagination enclosing a portion of cytosol.

## Explanation

In the chaperone-mediated pathway, specific protein cargoes are recognized and transferred across the lysosomal membrane. Macroautophagy instead involves formation of an autophagosome enclosing material. The distinction concerns delivery mode, while both pathways can contribute to cellular turnover.
