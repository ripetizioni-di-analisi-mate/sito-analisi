Which statement correctly distinguishes export of most mRNAs?

- Most mRNA export occurs by free diffusion without receptors or remodelling.
- Most mRNAs use a protein NES on the mRNA as the exportin-recognized signal.
- It uses NXF1-NXT1 and particle remodelling, distinct from the classical Ran-dependent exportin cycle.
- NXF1-NXT1 loads mRNA exclusively through direct binding to Ran-GTP.

## Explanation

Mature mRNAs are exported as ribonucleoprotein particles with dedicated factors, including NXF1-NXT1. Remodelling on the cytoplasmic side contributes to directionality. Assigning every nuclear export process indiscriminately to Ran-dependent exportins would confuse pathways that use the same pore but different mechanisms.
