Which organization is common in the axoneme of motile cilia and eukaryotic flagella?

- Nine peripheral doublets without central microtubules as the most common motile axoneme model.
- Nine peripheral doublets and two central microtubules.
- Nine peripheral triplets and no central structure as the typical motile axoneme model.
- Nine peripheral triplets and two central microtubules.

## Explanation

The nine-plus-two arrangement describes axonemal microtubules associated with motors and linkages. It is common in motile eukaryotic structures. Different arrangements exist, and primary cilia are often nine-plus-zero; the model should not become a universal rule for every cilium.
