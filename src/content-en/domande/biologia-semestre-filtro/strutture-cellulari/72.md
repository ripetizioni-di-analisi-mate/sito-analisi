A phagocyte engulfs a bacterium by extending membrane around the particle. Which cytoskeletal system directly supports this remodelling?

- Actin networks.
- Axonemal microtubules organized into doublets.
- Centrosomal microtubules as the main scaffold of the phagocytic cup.
- Cortical intermediate-filament networks as the protrusive polymer system.

## Explanation

Phagocytosis requires protrusions and cortical remodelling supported by actin polymerization and organization. The phagosome can then mature and interact with degradative compartments. Bacterial recognition, engulfment and degradation are distinct steps, each with appropriate components.
