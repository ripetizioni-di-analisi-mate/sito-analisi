Which association describes a peroxisomal function?

- Catalase generating H₂O₂ as the required product of detoxification.
- Oxidases producing H₂O₂ and catalase helping transform it.
- Fatty-acid oxidation occurring only if a respiratory chain is present in the peroxisome.
- Oxidases transferring all electrons to a respiratory chain coupled to ATP synthase.

## Explanation

Peroxisomes host oxidative reactions that can produce hydrogen peroxide. Catalase limits its accumulation by transforming it. The organelle has no DNA or ribosomes of its own, and its energetic organization does not match mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation.
