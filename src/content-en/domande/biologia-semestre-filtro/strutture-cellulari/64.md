A vesicle forms normally, but a Rab defect reduces its tethering to the target compartment. Which phase is most directly altered?

- Cargo concentration during budding as the only possible affected phase.
- Target recognition and tethering.
- ATP hydrolysis to disassemble SNAREs after completed fusion.
- SRP-mediated cotranslational entry of cargo into the reticulum.

## Explanation

Rab proteins are small GTPases that recruit effectors and contribute to target-interaction specificity. The question states that vesicle formation remains normal, locating the problem at a later phase. Budding, tethering and fusion are distinct steps, even though coordinated.
