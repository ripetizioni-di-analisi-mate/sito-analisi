Which structural continuity is present in the nuclear envelope?

- A third membrane separates the reticulum lumen from the perinuclear space.
- The perinuclear space is continuous with the cytosol through nuclear pores.
- The outer membrane is continuous with the endoplasmic reticulum.
- The nucleoplasm is continuous with the reticulum lumen through the perinuclear space.

## Explanation

The envelope has two membranes that connect at pores. The outer membrane continues into the reticulum and the perinuclear space is continuous with its lumen. Nucleoplasm remains distinct from the reticulum lumen; continuity concerns specific membranes and spaces, not fusion of every compartment.
