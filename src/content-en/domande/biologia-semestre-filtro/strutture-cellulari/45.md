Selective inhibition of mitochondrial fission leaves fusion active. Which network change is consistent in a simplified model?

- A tendency towards more connected and elongated structures.
- A decrease in mean mitochondrial length without reduced fusion.
- Necessarily unchanged shape, because keeping mitochondrial mass constant is sufficient.
- A tendency towards more numerous fragments, as when fission predominates.

## Explanation

Fission separates network portions, while fusion joins them. Reducing only the former activity can shift the balance towards connection and elongation. This is a prediction of the stated model; the real phenotype also depends on distribution, turnover and other cellular regulators.
