Which substance crosses a protein-free lipid bilayer most readily, other conditions being equal?

- Urea.
- Na⁺.
- Molecular oxygen.
- Glucose.

## Explanation

Oxygen is small and nonpolar, so it can partition into the hydrophobic region of the bilayer. An ion and charged macromolecules face a much larger energy barrier. The absence of proteins excludes channels, carriers and translocases that could facilitate other crossings.
