Which complex promotes assembly of beta-barrel proteins in the outer mitochondrial membrane?

- TIM22.
- SAM.
- OXA.
- TIM23.

## Explanation

SAM participates in insertion and assembly of beta-barrel proteins in the outer membrane. TIM22 and OXA act in inner-membrane pathways, with different cargoes and directions. PEX5 instead recognizes peroxisomal signals. Cargo destination thus distinguishes systems sharing the principle of selective targeting.
