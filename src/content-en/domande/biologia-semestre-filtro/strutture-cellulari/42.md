A subunit synthesized in the matrix must be inserted into the inner mitochondrial membrane. Which system can act from the matrix side?

- SAM in the outer membrane.
- TOM as an outer-membrane channel.
- TIM22 as the translocase for carriers arriving from the intermembrane space.
- OXA.

## Explanation

OXA promotes insertion into the inner membrane from the matrix side and also acts on subunits synthesized by mitochondrial ribosomes. The direction therefore differs from initial entry of a cytosolic precursor through TOM. The other structures do not perform this insertion.
