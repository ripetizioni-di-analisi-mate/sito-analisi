IκB is degraded after a signal activating NF-κB. Which consequence directly concerns transport?

- The NF-κB–IκB complex must remain intact during signal-induced import.
- The signal retains NF-κB in the cytosol by increasing NLS masking.
- NF-κB can become available for nuclear import.
- Degradation removes the NF-κB NLS and thereby activates import.

## Explanation

IκB helps retain and regulate NF-κB. Its removal allows the import machinery to recognize the factor, leading to nuclear accumulation. This control changes cargo accessibility and availability without turning the pore into a vesicular system.
