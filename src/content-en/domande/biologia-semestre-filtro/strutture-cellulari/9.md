During synthesis of a secreted protein, a hydrophobic signal sequence emerges. Which component recognizes it in the classical cotranslational pathway?

- SRP.
- Importin.
- PEX5.
- Mannose-6-phosphate receptor.

## Explanation

SRP recognizes the emerging signal and promotes ribosome targeting to its receptor on the reticulum. The chain can then cross the translocon during synthesis. Importins, PEX5 and mannose-6-phosphate receptors instead recognize cargoes and signals in different pathways: nuclear, peroxisomal and lysosomal sorting.
