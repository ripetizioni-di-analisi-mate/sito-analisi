The inner mitochondrial membrane potential is dissipated while ATP is initially available. Which process can be directly impaired?

- Import of many precursors with positively charged presequences.
- Cytosolic presequence recognition, because it directly requires the inner-membrane electric field.
- Cytosolic chaperone binding to the precursor, because every chaperone is directly powered by that potential.
- Initial diffusion of small metabolites through outer-membrane porins, through loss of its energy source.

## Explanation

The relatively negative matrix favours transfer of positively charged presequences across the inner membrane. Removing that electrical contribution can block import even before ATP is depleted. The experiment distinguishes the voltage requirement from the requirement for ATP-dependent chaperones.
