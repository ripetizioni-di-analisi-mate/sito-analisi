A glucose carrier shows a rate plateau when external concentration becomes very high. Which interpretation is most direct?

- Available carrier sites and cycles become limiting.
- Saturation proves that internal glucose concentration necessarily equals the external concentration.
- The plateau proves that the carrier directly hydrolyses ATP.
- Carrier number automatically increases enough to eliminate saturation.

## Explanation

Carrier number is finite and each cycle takes time. At high concentrations many sites are occupied, so additional substrate increases flux only slightly. Saturation is consistent with carrier-mediated transport; alone it does not establish whether the process is passive or energy-coupled.
