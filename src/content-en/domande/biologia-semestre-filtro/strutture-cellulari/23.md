CFTR binds and hydrolyses ATP while chloride passes through it down its gradient. How should chloride flow be classified?

- Passive through a regulated channel.
- Simple diffusion through the bilayer, because transport is not against the gradient.
- Primary active transport against the gradient.
- Secondary active transport obligatorily coupled to Na⁺.

## Explanation

ATP regulates the channel's gating cycle, while anion flow follows its electrochemical gradient. Energy used to regulate a protein must be distinguished from energy coupled to uphill transport. CFTR belongs to the ABC family but functions as a channel.
