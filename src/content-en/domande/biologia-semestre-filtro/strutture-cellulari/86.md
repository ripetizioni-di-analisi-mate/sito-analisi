Loss of dystrophin makes the muscle membrane more vulnerable during contraction. Which function explains the connection?

- Active calcium reuptake into the sarcoplasmic reticulum.
- Calcium binding as the switch moving tropomyosin.
- Linking the actin system to membrane complexes connected to the matrix.
- Direct generation of actin-myosin sliding through its own ATPase cycle.

## Explanation

Dystrophin contributes to force transmission and mechanical stability between cytoskeleton and membrane. If that connection is impaired, contraction can stress a more fragile membrane. The defect concerns structural integration without making dystrophin the contractile motor itself.
