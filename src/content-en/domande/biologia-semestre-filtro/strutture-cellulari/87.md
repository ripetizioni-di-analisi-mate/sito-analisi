Which regulator family coordinates actin contractility, protrusions and polarity?

- Small GTPases of the Rho family, including Rho, Rac and Cdc42.
- Rab proteins, as principal regulators of vesicle tethering to compartments.
- Ran, coordinating the states of nuclear-transport complexes.
- Sar1 and ARF, involved in coat formation during secretory trafficking.

## Explanation

Rho, Rac and Cdc42 regulate effectors modifying actin organization and dynamics. Their spatial activities help coordinate contractile structures, protruding edges and polarity. The outcome depends on effectors and context; the names do not imply one identical effect in every cell.
