Which route describes passage of a secretory cargo through the Golgi?

- Entry at cis and obligatory return to the reticulum before every secretion.
- Entry at the cis side and sorting at the trans-Golgi network.
- Entry at trans and retention at cis as the final destination of all secretory cargo.
- Entry at the trans-Golgi and secretory exit from the cis side.

## Explanation

The cis side receives traffic from the reticulum; modifications and cargo progression occur through the Golgi. The trans-Golgi network sorts towards different destinations. Compartment polarity helps interpret a temporal labelling sequence in a pulse-chase experiment.
