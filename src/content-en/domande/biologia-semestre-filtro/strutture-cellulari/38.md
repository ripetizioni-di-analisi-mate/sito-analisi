Why should the yield of about 30–32 ATP per glucose not be treated as an identical constant in every cell?

- Shuttles, transport and degree of coupling modify the balance.
- ATP yield depends only on glucose consumed, so variability must be measurement error.
- The two-ATP difference always comes from a different net glycolytic ATP yield.
- Every NADH produces exactly three ATP independently of the shuttle and proton leaks.

## Explanation

The balance depends on how cytosolic reducing equivalents reach the chain and on system costs and losses. The estimate is useful for comparing aerobic metabolism with glycolysis alone, but describes a yield model.
