Which system helps transmit forces between the cytoskeleton and nuclear structures across the envelope?

- Continuity between the reticulum and outer nuclear membrane without protein bridges across the perinuclear space.
- LINC complexes.
- The nuclear pore, through transient importin-nucleoporin interactions.
- The nuclear lamina alone, without proteins spanning the envelope.

## Explanation

LINC complexes connect components on the two sides of the nuclear envelope, integrating the cytoskeleton with nuclear structures. They therefore enable continuity in force transmission. This concerns specific mechanical connections, distinct from macromolecular passage through the nuclear pore.
