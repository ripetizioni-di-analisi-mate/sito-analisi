Which coat is associated with retrieval of components from the Golgi to the reticulum?

- Clathrin.
- COPII.
- COPI.
- Retromer, as the selector of retrograde Golgi-to-reticulum transport.

## Explanation

COPI participates in retrograde routes to the reticulum and traffic within the Golgi. Retrieval compensates for loss of resident components during forward flow. COPII instead is associated with reticulum export; distinguishing directions helps locate a trafficking defect.
