What is oxygen's direct role in the mitochondrial respiratory chain?

- It directly accepts pumped protons in the intermembrane space without receiving electrons.
- It directly oxidizes ADP to ATP at the ATP synthase catalytic site.
- It donates the initial electrons that the chain transfers to NAD⁺.
- It accepts electrons at the end of the chain and is reduced to water.

## Explanation

Oxygen receives electrons at complex IV and forms water with protons. Availability of the final acceptor allows electron flow and indirectly supports the proton gradient. ATP synthase uses that gradient to phosphorylate ADP; oxygen and phosphate have different roles.
