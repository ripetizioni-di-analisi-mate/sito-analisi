A cell contains many autophagosomes. Which additional measurement best distinguishes increased formation from slowed degradation?

- Measuring only structure number in a single image.
- Measuring only total marker amount without tracking turnover.
- Measuring material flux through degradation by comparing conditions with and without lysosomal blockade.
- Equating every marker increase with more completed degradation.

## Explanation

The observed number depends on the difference between structure production and removal. Accumulation can therefore result from faster entry or slower exit. Flux comparison, with controls for degradative blockade, separates interpretations that a single static image leaves compatible.
