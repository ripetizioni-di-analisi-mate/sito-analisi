A protein increases water permeability without increasing Na⁺ permeability. Which function is compatible?

- Aquaporin channel.
- Voltage-gated Na⁺-selective channel.
- Obligate Na⁺-glucose cotransporter.
- ATPase pump exchanging Na⁺ for K⁺.

## Explanation

Aquaporins provide selective pathways for water. Greater water flow does not require simultaneous sodium entry: channel selectivity distinguishes permeating species. Net water direction still depends on osmotic and pressure conditions, not merely on channel presence.
