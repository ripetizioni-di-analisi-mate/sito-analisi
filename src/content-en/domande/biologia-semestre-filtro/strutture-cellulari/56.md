Misfolded proteins accumulate in the reticulum. Which initial response can reduce the load on the compartment?

- Increasing secretory-protein translation before increasing folding capacity.
- A UPR that reduces some synthesis and increases quality-control and folding capacity.
- Reducing chaperones while maintaining synthesis, to speed escape from quality control.
- Blocking defective-cargo degradation without reducing entry into the reticulum.

## Explanation

The UPR coordinates adaptations to reticulum capacity. Reducing arrival of new chains and increasing chaperones and quality control can help restore homeostasis. If stress is severe or persistent, outcomes can differ; the option describes an adaptive response, not a guarantee of survival.
