A microtubule has its minus end near the centrosome and its plus end towards the periphery. Where does cytoplasmic dynein carry cargo?

- Towards the nearest cargo, independently of track orientation.
- Towards the centrosome.
- Towards the periphery.
- In both directions with equal probability, because ATP use does not provide directionality.

## Explanation

Cytoplasmic dynein moves towards microtubule minus ends. In the specified arrangement, that end is near the centrosome, so movement is centripetal. The answer depends on track orientation and motor identity; intermediate filaments are not dynein tracks.
