In a secretory cell, increased cytosolic Ca²⁺ induces fusion of already mature granules. Which step is most directly regulated?

- Translation of new molecules as the only source of rapid release.
- Initial reticulum import of that cargo as a step still awaiting completion.
- Exocytosis of already stored cargo.
- Gene transcription as an immediate requirement of every fusion.

## Explanation

Cargo is already present in mature granules, so rapid release can depend on control of fusion. Increased calcium acts on the regulated exocytosis system. Assigning the immediate effect to new transcription or translation would ignore the stored pool specified in the question.
