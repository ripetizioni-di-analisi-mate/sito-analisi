Why is Ran-GTP normally more abundant in the nucleus than in the cytosol?

- Nuclear RanGEF and cytosolic RanGAP maintain different nucleotide states.
- The pore admits Ran-GTP but prevents it from leaving with receptors.
- GTP hydrolysis occurs mainly in the nucleus and nucleotide exchange in the cytosol.
- Nuclear RanGAP exchanges GDP for GTP, while cytosolic RanGEF stimulates hydrolysis.

## Explanation

RanGEF promotes nucleotide exchange in the nucleus, while RanGAP stimulates GTP hydrolysis in the cytosol. Spatial separation of these activities maintains asymmetry. This difference controls cargo association and release, giving directionality to import and export cycles.
