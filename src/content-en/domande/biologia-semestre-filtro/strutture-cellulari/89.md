Which property distinguishes intermediate filaments from actin and microtubules?

- They have a GTP cap regulating dynamic instability.
- They have plus and minus ends used by myosins.
- They are apolar and contribute mechanical resistance.
- They are the canonical track for plus-end kinesins.

## Explanation

Intermediate-filament assembly produces a structure lacking the polarity typical of the other two networks. Keratins and lamins support cells and the nucleus against deformation. This mechanical role differs from providing tracks for motors moving on actin or microtubules.
