Which pair correctly associates a track with a motor family?

- Microtubules and myosins as the canonical association.
- Actin and myosins.
- Actin and dyneins.
- Keratins and kinesins.

## Explanation

Myosins interact with actin and use ATP to generate movement or tension. Kinesins and dyneins instead use microtubules. Intermediate filaments contribute mechanical resistance but are not tracks for these motor families; correct network association helps predict inhibitor effects.
