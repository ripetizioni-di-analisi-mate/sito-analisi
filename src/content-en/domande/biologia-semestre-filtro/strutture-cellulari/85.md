During sarcomere contraction, the distance between Z lines decreases. Which change is predicted by the sliding-filament model?

- Filament overlap increases while filament lengths remain substantially unchanged.
- Overlap decreases as Z lines approach in the stated model.
- A-band length decreases in the same proportion as the distance between Z lines.
- Thin filaments must shorten in the same proportion as the sarcomere.

## Explanation

Myosin heads produce relative movement of thin and thick filaments. Greater overlap brings Z lines closer. The model explains shortening of the unit without requiring each filament to be degraded and rebuilt during every contraction.
