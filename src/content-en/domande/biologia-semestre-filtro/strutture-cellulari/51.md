A specialized cell expands its smooth reticulum. Which activity is compatible with this expansion?

- Degradation of endocytosed cargo by acid hydrolases in its lumen.
- Protein N-glycosylation linked to ribosomes attached to its surface.
- Hydrolase sorting through mannose-6-phosphate addition.
- Lipid metabolism, calcium handling or detoxification.

## Explanation

Smooth reticulum participates in functions varying with cell specialization, including lipid synthesis and calcium regulation. Expansion of a compartment can reflect its activity. Rough reticulum is distinguished by ribosomes associated with its cytosolic surface, not autonomous ribosomes in the lumen.
