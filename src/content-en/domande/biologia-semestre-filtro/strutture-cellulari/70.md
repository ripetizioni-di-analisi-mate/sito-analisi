Activated EGF receptor is directed to multivesicular bodies and lysosomes. Which effect is consistent?

- Increased surface receptors through predominant recycling of the activated complex.
- Reduced receptor availability and attenuation of signalling.
- Degradation of the ligand alone with complete receptor recovery at the surface.
- Retention of the active receptor in recycling vesicles with unchanged sensitivity to new stimuli.

## Explanation

Sending the receptor for degradation reduces the molecules available for further responses. Endosomal sorting can therefore regulate signal duration and intensity. This differs from the LDL receptor, which in the classical pathway is predominantly recycled after cargo release.
