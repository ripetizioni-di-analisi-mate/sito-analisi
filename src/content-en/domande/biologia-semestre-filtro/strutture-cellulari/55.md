A persistently misfolded reticulum protein is retrotranslocated, ubiquitinated and degraded by the proteasome. Which pathway is described?

- Macroautophagy with obligatory autophagosome enclosure.
- KDEL-mediated retention through retrieval from the Golgi, without proteasome involvement.
- Lysosomal degradation after mannose-6-phosphate-mediated protein delivery.
- ERAD.

## Explanation

ERAD links recognition of a defect in the reticulum with removal through the ubiquitin-proteasome system. Retrotranslocation makes cargo accessible to cytosolic degradation machinery. This differs from secretion and from delivery to a lysosome through an autophagosome.
