Which pair of sites can use clathrin in cellular trafficking?

- Reticulum exit sites and COPII carriers travelling to the Golgi.
- Golgi-to-reticulum retrieval and intra-Golgi COPI traffic.
- Secretory-granule fusion and autophagosome-lysosome fusion, as the coat joining the membranes.
- Plasma membrane for endocytosis and certain routes from the trans-Golgi.

## Explanation

Clathrin contributes to coats participating in cargo selection and carrier formation. It can act at the surface and in intracellular routes from the trans-Golgi. A coat identifies certain trafficking steps, while fusion requires further recognition systems.
