A protein accumulates in granules and is rapidly released only after a stimulus. Which secretion mode is described?

- Constitutive secretion with an obligatory stimulus required for each granule.
- Continuous constitutive secretion without granule storage.
- Regulated secretion.
- Granule endocytosis from the surface as the origin of the observed release.

## Explanation

A stored granule pool ready for release identifies regulated secretion. The stimulus controls fusion of already loaded vesicles. Constitutive secretion instead proceeds continuously without requiring that type of prior storage and signal for each release.
