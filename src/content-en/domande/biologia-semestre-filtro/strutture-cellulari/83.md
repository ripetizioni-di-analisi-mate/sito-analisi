Which component promotes a branched actin network at a cell edge?

- Myosin II as the network branch nucleator.
- Arp2/3.
- Proteins capping plus ends, as the sole mechanism creating new branches.
- Formins directly nucleating lateral branches on existing filaments.

## Explanation

Arp2/3 promotes branch nucleation from existing filaments. A branched network can support broad cell-edge protrusions. Formins favour unbranched actin filaments; myosin II generates tension and capping proteins regulate elongation. None of these roles is the branch-nucleation activity of Arp2/3.
