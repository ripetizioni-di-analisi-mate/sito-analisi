In the classical neuronal action potential, which event explains the rapid rising phase?

- Simultaneous closure of all Na⁺ channels before depolarization.
- Increased Na⁺ permeability through opening of voltage-gated channels.
- Immediate increase in Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase activity alone.
- Opening only K⁺ channels with positive-charge efflux.

## Explanation

Na⁺ channel opening allows rapid positive-charge entry under the typical ionic conditions of a resting neuron. Depolarization opens further channels and supports the rise. Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase maintains gradients over longer periods but does not directly generate that rapid phase.
