A cytoplasmic organelle is enclosed in a double-membrane compartment and then delivered to the lysosomal system. Which process is described?

- Macroautophagy.
- Endocytosis of an initially extracellular cargo.
- Microautophagy as simple lysosomal-membrane invagination.
- Chaperone-mediated autophagy without vesicle formation.

## Explanation

The double-membrane autophagosome is characteristic of macroautophagy. Its formation isolates portions of cytoplasm and allows delivery for lysosomal degradation. Cargo identity can make the process selective, as in mitophagy, without changing the principle of the described pathway.
