Which activity is directly associated with the nucleolus?

- rRNA production and processing and initiation of ribosomal subunit assembly.
- N-glycosylation of secreted proteins.
- Complete translation of all cytosolic proteins.
- Assembly of respiratory complexes in the inner membrane.

## Explanation

The nucleolus concentrates components of ribosome biogenesis without a surrounding membrane. rRNA and ribosomal proteins assemble into subunit precursors that mature and are exported. Cytosolic translation occurs on ribosomes in the cytosol, N-glycosylation begins in the reticulum lumen, and respiratory-complex assembly involves the inner mitochondrial membrane.
