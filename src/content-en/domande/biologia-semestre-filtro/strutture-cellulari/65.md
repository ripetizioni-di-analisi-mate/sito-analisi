Vesicles reach their target but fail to fuse after the appropriate SNAREs are inactivated. Which function is lost?

- Initial target recognition by Rab effectors.
- Cargo selection and membrane curvature by the COPII coat.
- Disassembly of cis-SNARE complexes by NSF ATPase after fusion.
- Membrane apposition through pairing of fusion proteins.

## Explanation

SNAREs on the membranes form complexes promoting close apposition and fusion. Arrival at the target therefore does not guarantee cargo delivery. The experiment separates vesicle movement from the final step of bilayer fusion.
