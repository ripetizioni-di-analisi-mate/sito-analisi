Two sides have equal K⁺ concentration, but the inside is electrically negative. When K⁺-selective channels open, which initial force acts on the cation?

- No force because concentrations are equal.
- An outward chemical force caused by different concentrations.
- An electrical force always directed outwards.
- An inward electrical force.

## Explanation

Equal concentrations initially remove the chemical difference. The voltage difference remains, however: a positive charge is favoured towards the negative side. The electrochemical gradient therefore cannot be inferred from concentration alone; it also includes the electrical contribution.
