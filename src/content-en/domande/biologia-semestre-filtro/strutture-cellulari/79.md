A cargo is associated with a protein identified only as a member of the kinesin family. Can one infer that it will move towards the cell periphery?

- Membership of the kinesin family implies movement towards the centrosome.
- ATP consumption makes transport direction independent of microtubule polarity.
- The specific motor direction and microtubule orientation are needed.
- Every kinesin moves towards the plus end and therefore always towards the periphery.

## Explanation

Many kinesins are plus-end-directed, but the family includes motors with different properties, including minus-end-directed motors. Moreover, microtubule plus ends do not always point towards the periphery in every cell type. Inferring a spatial destination therefore requires both motor and track information; the family name alone is insufficient.
