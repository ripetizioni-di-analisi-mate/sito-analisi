An immunoglobulin enters the basolateral side of an epithelial cell and is released from the apical side through vesicles. Which process is described?

- Recycling to the basolateral entry surface.
- Immunoglobulin sorting to lysosomes for complete degradation.
- Biosynthetic secretion of an immunoglobulin newly translated by the same cell.
- Transcytosis.

## Explanation

Transcytosis moves cargo between distinct surfaces of a polarized cell, combining endocytosis, internal trafficking and exocytosis. Cargo remains separated from the cytosol within pathway compartments. Cell polarity distinguishes this destination from simple return to the entry surface.
