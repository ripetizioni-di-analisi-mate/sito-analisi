Which respiratory-chain complex transfers electrons without pumping protons?

- Complex IV.
- Complex III.
- Complex II.
- Complex I.

## Explanation

Complex II links succinate oxidation with electron transfer to ubiquinone and does not pump protons. Complexes I, III and IV instead contribute to proton translocation. Thus electrons entering downstream of complex I produce a lower ATP yield in the usual model.
