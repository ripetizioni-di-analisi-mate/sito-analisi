A peroxisomal defect alters very-long-chain fatty acid breakdown. Which other function can be directly involved?

- Chylomicron assembly in the reticulum lumen.
- Pyruvate oxidation to acetyl-CoA in the mitochondrial matrix.
- Synthesis of particular lipids, such as plasmalogens.
- Triacylglycerol synthesis in the endoplasmic reticulum.

## Explanation

Peroxisomes participate both in breakdown of specific fatty acids and in plasmalogen biosynthetic steps. A biogenesis defect can therefore alter several functions. The other activities belong respectively to the nucleolus, Golgi and centrosome; assigning them to peroxisomes would confuse compartments with different machinery.
