In normal codon-anticodon pairing, which codon position pairs with the base at the anticodon’s 5′ end?

- The second codon base.
- The position changes randomly at each codon because of wobble.
- The first codon base, towards its 5′ end.
- The third codon base, towards its 3′ end.

## Explanation

Codon and anticodon are antiparallel. The anticodon’s 5′ end therefore faces the codon’s 3′ end, its third base. Some wobble pairings can be tolerated at this pair, depending on the tRNA. Antiparallel orientation establishes the positional correspondence before considering which pairings are allowed.
