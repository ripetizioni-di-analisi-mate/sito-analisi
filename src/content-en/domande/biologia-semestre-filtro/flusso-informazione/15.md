A mixture contains replicative DNA polymerase, a single-stranded template and all dNTPs, but no primer. Which component is missing for elongation to begin?

- A ligase able to select and incorporate the first two dNTPs.
- A template-paired 3′-OH end.
- An origin site recognised by DNA polymerase, sufficient to replace the primer.
- A free new-chain 5′-phosphate, with no need for a 3′-OH.

## Explanation

Replicative polymerase extends an existing chain by adding nucleotides to its 3′-OH. The template determines the sequence but does not itself provide the new chain end to extend. A complementary primer supplies that end. Ligase joins already adjacent ends; recognising an origin does not replace the chemical requirement for a primer.
