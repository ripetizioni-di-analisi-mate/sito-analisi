RNA polymerase II is selectively blocked. Which production directly decreases immediately?

- Addition of poly-A tails by pre-existing poly-A polymerase.
- Production of the nucleolar major-rRNA precursor.
- Production of tRNAs and 5S rRNA.
- Nuclear mRNA precursor production.

## Explanation

Nuclear protein-coding genes are transcribed by polymerase II. Blocking it therefore directly reduces formation of their RNA precursors, as well as some non-coding RNAs produced by the same enzyme. Other processes may be affected later, but the immediate effect concerns the blocked polymerase’s direct products.
