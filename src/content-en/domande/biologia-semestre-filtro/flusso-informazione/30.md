Fully heavy DNA is replicated once in a light medium. Only DNA of intermediate density is observed. Which conclusion follows from this first result?

- An intermediate band proves that each molecule contains two entirely new strands.
- The result is expected from the conservative model retaining an intact parental double helix.
- The classical conservative model is excluded; more data are needed to distinguish semiconservative and dispersive models.
- The result alone already distinguishes semiconservative from dispersive replication.

## Explanation

In the conservative model, the heavy double helix remains together alongside an entirely light double helix, predicting two distinct densities. A single intermediate density is compatible with semiconservative hybrid molecules. This one round alone does not yet distinguish semiconservative replication from an appropriate dispersive model; further observations are needed.
