An inhibitor blocks primase after a fork is already active. In the basic model, which event repeatedly requires the blocked activity?

- Relaxation of torsional stress ahead of helicase.
- Sealing phosphodiester bonds after gap filling.
- Removal of an incorrect 3′ nucleotide during proofreading.
- Initiation of new Okazaki fragments.

## Explanation

Each Okazaki fragment begins as a new chain and requires a primer. Once initiated, the leading strand can continue from its existing end. Blocking primase therefore repeatedly compromises initiation on the lagging strand. In a real system, fork activities are coordinated, so the defect may also slow the entire complex.
