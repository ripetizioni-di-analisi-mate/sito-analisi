Immediately after peptide bond formation and before translocation, which tRNA carries the extended chain?

- No tRNA, because every bond releases the chain into the cytoplasm.
- The tRNA in the A site.
- The P-site tRNA, because transfer occurs only after translocation.
- The uncharged E-site tRNA, before exit.

## Explanation

The reaction transfers the chain from P-site tRNA to the amino acid attached to A-site tRNA. The extended chain therefore remains on A-site tRNA until translocation moves it towards P. Free protein release occurs at termination, not during every peptide-bond cycle.
