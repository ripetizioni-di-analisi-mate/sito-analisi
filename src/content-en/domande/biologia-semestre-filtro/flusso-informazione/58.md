In the standard code, CAA specifies glutamine and UAA is a stop. C → U editing changes a CAA codon to UAA in the messenger without altering DNA. What effect can this produce?

- Unchanged glutamine incorporation because DNA retains CAA.
- Translation termination at that position.
- Addition of an extra amino acid before normal continuation.
- Loss of an intron without changing codon meaning.

## Explanation

Editing directly changes the RNA sequence. Given the supplied codon meanings, the substitution creates a stop signal where an amino acid was previously specified. The chain can therefore terminate early. This case shows how a translated product can differ even without a mutation in the corresponding genomic sequence.
