A metazoan miRNA recognises a target 3′ UTR with partial complementarity. Which effect is typical and compatible with this pairing?

- Obligatory immediate cleavage of every target by any Argonaute.
- Translational repression and/or mRNA destabilisation.
- A necessary translation increase whenever the miRNA binds the 3′ UTR.
- Selective removal of the 3′ UTR by splicing without destabilising the rest of the messenger.

## Explanation

Many metazoan miRNAs guide complexes that reduce translation efficiency and promote target deadenylation and degradation. Endonucleolytic cleavage requires more specific conditions, including suitable complementarity and a competent Argonaute. Partial pairing alone therefore does not justify claiming that every target is immediately cleaved.
