A sequence lies in the first exon, upstream of the initial AUG, and is present in mature mRNA. Which region does it belong to?

- An intron retained by definition only in pre-mRNA.
- The promoter, because every sequence upstream of AUG is a promoter.
- 5′ UTR.
- The coding region, because every exon is fully translated.

## Explanation

The sequence remains in the messenger but precedes the start of the coding region. It therefore belongs to the 5′ untranslated region, or 5′ UTR. An exon is defined by its presence in mature RNA and may include translated and untranslated parts. Being exonic alone therefore does not make a sequence protein-coding.
