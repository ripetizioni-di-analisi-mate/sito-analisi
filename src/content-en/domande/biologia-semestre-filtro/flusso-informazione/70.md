UUU and UUC both encode phenylalanine. If a substitution changes only UUU to UUC and does not affect splicing, amount or mode of translation, what is its effect on the primary sequence?

- A stop necessarily appears at that position.
- The reading frame shifts by one base.
- Phenylalanine remains at that position.
- Two phenylalanine residues are incorporated instead of one.

## Explanation

The two codons have the same meaning in the specified code, so the substitution is synonymous with respect to amino acid sequence. The question excludes effects at other levels to isolate this consequence. A synonymous variant can generally have regulatory effects, but those cannot automatically be assigned to this case.
