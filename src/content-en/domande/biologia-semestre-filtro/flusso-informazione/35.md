A mutation reduces recruitment of transcription machinery near a gene’s initiation site without altering its coding region. Which element is directly consistent with the defect?

- A miRNA-binding site in the mature mRNA’s 3′ UTR.
- The stop codon, whose direct role is to recruit RNA polymerase.
- An amino acid charging site on tRNA.
- The promoter.

## Explanation

The promoter contributes to positioning and recruiting the transcription complex. Altering it can therefore reduce RNA synthesis while leaving the encoded protein sequence unchanged. The other structures concern products or later steps and do not correspond to the DNA element described.
