In the classical lac operon model, with intact regulators and operator, which condition favours the highest transcription?

- Lactose available and glucose scarce.
- Lactose absent and glucose scarce.
- Lactose absent and glucose abundant.
- Lactose and glucose both abundant.

## Explanation

Lactose allows allolactose formation, which reduces repressor binding to the operator. Glucose scarcity also favours the cAMP signal and activation by CAP-cAMP. Both effects are needed for high expression. Abundant glucose removes this strong positive activation; without lactose, the repressor remains effective even if CAP-cAMP is available.
