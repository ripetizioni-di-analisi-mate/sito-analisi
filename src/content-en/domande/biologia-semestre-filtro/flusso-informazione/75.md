A UAA codon reaches the A site during standard translation. Which event allows chain release?

- The ribosome always skips UAA and continues to the physical RNA end.
- A release factor promotes hydrolysis of the polypeptide-tRNA bond.
- The release factor adds a terminal amino acid and maintains the chain’s attachment to tRNA.
- A charged UAA-specific tRNA adds the final amino acid to the chain.

## Explanation

In the standard code, a stop does not specify an amino acid carried by a normal tRNA. A release factor recognises the signal and promotes hydrolytic separation of the chain from tRNA. This explains why the stop codon counts towards nucleotide length but not towards the number of incorporated amino acids.
