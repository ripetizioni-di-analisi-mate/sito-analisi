After primer removal, a gap of ten nucleotides remains. In a simplified model, which activity order is needed to obtain continuous DNA?

- Ligase alone, incorporating the ten missing dNTPs.
- Sealing by ligase before the missing nucleotides are present.
- Filling by polymerase, followed by sealing by ligase.
- Filling by primase, then ligase, retaining RNA in the final product.

## Explanation

The gap contains positions lacking nucleotides, so a polymerase must first fill it using the template. After filling, the ends are adjacent, but one phosphodiester bond may remain interrupted. Ligase seals that nick. Confusing a gap with a simple nick incorrectly assigns a synthesis function to ligase.
