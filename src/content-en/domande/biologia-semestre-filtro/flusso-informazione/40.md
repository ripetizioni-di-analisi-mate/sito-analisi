The CAP-binding site at the lac promoter is mutated; LacI and the operator are normal. With lactose present and glucose scarce, what is expected relative to the intact system?

- Transcription necessarily reaches its maximum because removing LacI is sufficient.
- Scarce glucose makes CAP binding unnecessary for its positive action.
- The CAP-site mutation directly prevents allolactose from binding LacI.
- Normal strong activation by CAP-cAMP is lost.

## Explanation

Allolactose can still reduce repression, but CAP-cAMP no longer finds a functional site at which to contribute activation. The system can therefore remain below the fully induced level. The mutation is localised to activator binding and does not automatically imply defects in other interactions.
