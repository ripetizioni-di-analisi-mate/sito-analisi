During tRNA charging by its synthetase, which ATP conversion is typical?

- No ATP consumption: attachment to tRNA requires only base complementarity.
- ATP → AMP and pyrophosphate.
- ATP → ADP as the only amino acid activation step.
- GTP → GDP instead of ATP in the synthetase-catalysed reaction.

## Explanation

The synthetase first activates the amino acid by forming an aminoacyl-AMP intermediate and releasing pyrophosphate. The amino acid is then transferred to tRNA, and AMP is released. Pyrophosphate hydrolysis favours the process. Charging prepares the energy and identity of the substrate used during protein synthesis.
