An intracellular receptor binds a steroid hormone and increases coactivator recruitment at target DNA sequences. At which level does this effect act directly?

- Transcriptional regulation.
- Aminoacyl-tRNA selection at the A site.
- Removal of introns from pre-mRNA.
- Protein degradation by the proteasome.

## Explanation

The described receptor changes the activity of a complex associated with DNA regulatory elements. The direct effect therefore concerns transcription of target genes. Any protein changes arise through later RNA-processing and translation steps; they are not the initial molecular contact specified in the question.
