Two lac operon copies share the same cytoplasm and functional LacI. Only copy A’s operator can no longer bind LacI. Without inducer, what follows for negative regulation?

- Only B escapes repression because it captures repressor away from A.
- A becomes repressed again through B’s normal operator without changing A’s sequence.
- Both escape repression because the operator mutation acts in trans.
- A escapes LacI binding, while B can remain repressed.

## Explanation

The operator is cis-acting: its sequence remains physically associated with copy A. The recognition defect does not alter B’s intact operator, which can still bind the diffusible repressor. Loss of repression does not by itself imply maximal transcription, because positive control by CAP-cAMP also remains.
