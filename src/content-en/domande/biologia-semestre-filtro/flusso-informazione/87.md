A protein variant is present in normal amounts but forms aggregates and has greatly reduced enzymatic activity. Which defect most directly explains the observed combination?

- Reduced translation initiation efficiency as the only alteration.
- Defective folding or maturation.
- Reduced gene transcription as the only alteration.
- Increased complete protein degradation as the only alteration.

## Explanation

Amount measures how many molecules are present, whereas activity also depends on structure and maturation. Aggregation and reduced activity are compatible with exposed inappropriate surfaces and an altered functional site. Measured abundance alone does not determine the current synthesis or degradation rate. Amount and function must therefore be evaluated separately.
