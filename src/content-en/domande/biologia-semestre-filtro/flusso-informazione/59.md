A population of mRNAs shows poly-A tail shortening followed by cap loss. Which consequence is compatible with normal turnover?

- A necessary increase in cap-dependent translation initiation efficiency.
- Greater accessibility to messenger degradation systems.
- Obligatory acceleration of the corresponding gene’s transcription.
- Greater end protection caused by cap removal.

## Explanation

The tail and cap participate in interactions that protect and regulate mRNA. Deadenylation and decapping can facilitate exonuclease access and thus messenger removal. The process concerns RNA stability; the DNA sequence from which it was transcribed can remain unchanged.
