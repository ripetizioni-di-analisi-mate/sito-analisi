A small RNA guides a catalytically competent Argonaute towards an mRNA with extensive complementarity. Which information mainly determines target specificity?

- mRNA length alone, independently of sequence.
- The cap, sufficient to select one unique messenger.
- The amino acid meaning of codons, even without complementarity.
- The small RNA sequence and its pairing to the target.

## Explanation

The guide selects the target through base complementarity. The protein complex supplies effector activities; under the stated conditions, the recognised RNA can be cleaved. Changing the guide sequence can therefore change the target, provided pairing and context remain suitable. A cap or a particular length alone cannot distinguish one target sequence from all others.
