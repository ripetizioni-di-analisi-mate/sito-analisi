Ignoring wobble, which anticodon recognises the codon 5′-AUG-3′ if the anticodon must also be written 5′ → 3′?

- 5′-CAT-3′
- 5′-UAC-3′
- 5′-AUG-3′
- 5′-CAU-3′

## Explanation

The complement paired to the codon is 3′-UAC-5′ because codon and anticodon have opposite orientations. To write the anticodon in the required direction, read this sequence from the other end, obtaining 5′-CAU-3′. Merely substituting complementary bases without reversing the sequence leaves the wrong orientation. The exercise uses the ordinary RNA alphabet, U rather than T; modified bases are outside this model.
