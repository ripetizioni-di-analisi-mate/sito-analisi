In the cap-dependent eukaryotic model, how is a suitable start AUG normally located?

- The small subunit with initiation factors scans from the 5′ end towards an AUG in a favourable context.
- Every AUG necessarily has the same selection probability regardless of context.
- The large subunit binds the stop and traverses mRNA 3′ → 5′ until AUG.
- The cap directly recognises every tRNA anticodon throughout the chain.

## Explanation

In the stated mechanism, cap recognition contributes to recruitment and the preinitiation complex scans the messenger. AUG context affects recognition efficiency. The question limits the model to cap-dependent translation: other initiation mechanisms exist, but the requested model depends on 5′ recruitment, scanning and AUG context.
