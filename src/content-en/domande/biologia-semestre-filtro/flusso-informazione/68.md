The sequence 5′-AUGGCUAAA-3′ is read from its first nucleotide. Which codon grouping respects this frame?

- AUG | GCU | AAA
- A | UGG | CUA | AA
- AUGG | CUAA | A
- AU | GGC | UAA | A

## Explanation

The starting point fixes the first group of three bases. Proceeding from the first nucleotide gives AUG, then GCU and finally AAA. Starting at the second or third nucleotide would produce another frame with different triplets. Grouping does not arise from physical separators between codons; it is maintained by the ribosome’s reading process.
