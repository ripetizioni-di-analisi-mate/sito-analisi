A locus is transcribed into a functional tRNA without producing a protein. How is it classified?

- It is a gene because a functional product can be RNA.
- It must be an intron removed from a protein-coding gene.
- It must be a promoter region because it produces an untranslated RNA.
- It is a gene only if the same RNA is then translated.

## Explanation

The concept of a gene includes regions contributing to a functional RNA or protein product. After processing, tRNA performs its function as RNA, connecting codon recognition to amino acid delivery. Lack of translation makes it neither an intron nor evidence of absent function.
