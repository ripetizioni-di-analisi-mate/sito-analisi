A pre-tRNA contains extra end sequences and bases that are not yet modified. Which process can turn it into a functional tRNA?

- Translation of pre-tRNA into an adaptor protein.
- Adding a cap and long poly-A tail as the only universal requirement.
- Obligatory retention of every precursor end sequence.
- End processing and base modifications, with any additional specific steps.

## Explanation

The initial transcript must acquire ends and a structure suitable for recognition by synthetases and the ribosome. Cleavage and modifications contribute to this maturation; some tRNAs also require intron removal or CCA addition. The mature RNA itself performs the final function, rather than a protein translated from tRNA.
