A double helix has both strands labelled. It undergoes three complete rounds of semiconservative replication without the label. With no degradation or recombination, what fraction of the final double helices contains an initial strand?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The number of double helices becomes $2^3=8$. The two initial strands remain intact and are found in two separate molecules, each paired with a new strand. There are therefore always two molecules containing an initial strand after the first round. The required fraction is $\dfrac{2}{8}=\dfrac{1}{4}$. We are counting hybrid molecules, not individual labelled nucleotides.
