Mutated LacI binds the operator normally but cannot bind allolactose. With lactose available and glucose scarce, which prediction is consistent?

- LacI can no longer bind DNA because its DNA-binding site is its allolactose-binding site.
- Glucose scarcity directly removes LacI from the operator even without inducer binding.
- The repressor can remain bound and prevent normal induction.
- Lactose always induces the system independently of recognition by LacI.

## Explanation

Induction requires allolactose to alter LacI affinity for the operator. If LacI cannot recognise the inducer but retains DNA binding, that step is lost and repression can persist. CAP-cAMP activity does not directly remove the repressor: the two systems act through different interactions.
