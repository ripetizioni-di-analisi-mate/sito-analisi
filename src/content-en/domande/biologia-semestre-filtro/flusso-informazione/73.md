A synthetase recognises the correct tRNA but sometimes activates an amino acid similar to the right one. Which activity can reduce the error before translation?

- Faster release of every charged product without passage through the editing site.
- Increased delivery to the ribosome of tRNAs charged with the similar amino acid.
- Editing that hydrolyses incorrect intermediates or mischarged tRNAs.
- Protection of all amino acid–tRNA bonds from hydrolysis, including incorrect ones.

## Explanation

Many aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases have checks beyond initial recognition. Hydrolysis of an incorrect product reduces the chance that tRNA will carry the wrong amino acid to the ribosome. This matters because ribosomal recognition mainly evaluates codon-anticodon pairing rather than re-establishing the identity of the attached amino acid.
