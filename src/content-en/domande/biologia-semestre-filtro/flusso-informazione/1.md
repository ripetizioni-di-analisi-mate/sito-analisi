A segment of the template strand is written 3′-TACGGTATT-5′. Which RNA is transcribed, written 5′ → 3′?

- 5′-UACGGUAUU-3′
- 5′-UUAUGGCAU-3′
- 5′-AUGCCAUAA-3′
- 5′-ATGCCATAA-3′

## Explanation

Polymerase reads the template 3′ → 5′ and builds the transcript 5′ → 3′, so the supplied orientation allows us to proceed from left to right. Applying T–A, A–U, C–G and G–C pairing gives AUG CCA UAA. RNA contains uracil instead of thymine. Reversing the result without changing the end labels would produce a different sequence.
