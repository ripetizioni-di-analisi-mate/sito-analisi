Five ribosomes simultaneously occupy the same mRNA and move in the same reading frame. What does this structure represent?

- Five obligatorily different frames on the same mRNA.
- A polysome, with one chain being synthesised by each ribosome.
- One shared chain transferred from one ribosome to the next.
- A complex in which the second ribosome can initiate only after the first terminates.

## Explanation

A polysome permits multiple translation events on the same messenger. Each ribosome retains its own tRNA carrying a growing chain and travels along mRNA 5′ → 3′. The chains may have different lengths because the ribosomes started at different times, even while reading the same coding region.
