After a promoter mutation, a gene’s nascent RNA decreases while messenger half-life remains unchanged. Which step is directly supported as altered?

- Transcriptional RNA production.
- Mature-messenger degradation.
- Intron removal from the precursor.
- Export of mature messenger from the nucleus.

## Explanation

Nascent RNA measures molecules being produced, so its decrease supports a transcriptional effect. Unchanged half-life makes accelerated degradation of mature messenger alone less plausible. The promoter location is consistent with an initiation defect; the result does not require a change in the coding sequence.
