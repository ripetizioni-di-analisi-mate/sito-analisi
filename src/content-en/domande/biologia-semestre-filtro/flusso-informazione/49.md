An enhancer far from a promoter increases transcription of a gene. Which mechanism makes interaction between proteins bound at the two elements possible?

- Three-dimensional DNA folding can bring them together.
- The intervening DNA must be deleted at every transcription event.
- The enhancer must be transcribed as part of the coding region to act.
- Action requires the enhancer and promoter to be adjacent in the linear sequence.

## Explanation

Distance along the sequence does not necessarily equal distance in space. Chromatin folding can bring an enhancer and promoter together, enabling interactions between factors and cofactors. This allows long-range regulation without removing the intervening segment. Specificity also depends on chromatin organisation and the proteins present.
