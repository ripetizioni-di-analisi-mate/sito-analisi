A selective inhibitor immediately reduces the nucleolar precursor yielding 18S, 5.8S and 28S. Which RNA polymerase is directly involved?

- Poly-A polymerase.
- RNA polymerase II.
- RNA polymerase I.
- RNA polymerase III.

## Explanation

Polymerase I produces the precursor of the major nuclear rRNAs, which is then processed through cleavage and modifications. 5S rRNA is an exception and depends on polymerase III. Distinguishing precursor synthesis from its processing connects the defect to the correct enzyme class.
