A 903-nucleotide mRNA segment extends from the first nucleotide of the initial AUG to the last nucleotide of the stop codon, both included. In the standard code, without recoding or internal stops, how many amino acids are incorporated?

- 299
- 903
- 300
- 301

## Explanation

The segment contains $\dfrac{903}{3}=301$ codons. The start codon specifies the methionine incorporated at the beginning of the chain; the stop is recognised by a release factor and adds no amino acid. Only the stop is therefore subtracted: $301-1=300$. The question concerns amino acids incorporated before any later protein modifications or cleavage.
