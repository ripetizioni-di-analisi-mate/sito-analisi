With four available bases and codons containing three positions, how many different triplets are possible?

- 64
- 61
- 20
- 12

## Explanation

Each of the three positions allows four independent choices. The number of combinations is therefore $4\cdot4\cdot4=4^3=64$. In the standard code, three combinations are stop signals and 61 specify amino acids. Thus 61 counts sense codons, whereas the question asks for all possible triplets, including stops.
