The amount of an mRNA doubles, but neither nascent RNA nor degradation is measured. Which conclusion is justified?

- Half-life has certainly doubled and transcription is certainly unchanged.
- Transcription rate has certainly doubled.
- The result may reflect greater synthesis, less degradation, or both.
- Translation has certainly doubled independently of every other control.

## Explanation

mRNA abundance results from the balance between production and removal. A higher level alone does not identify which flux changed. A longer half-life can increase abundance even with unchanged production. Distinguishing the explanations requires synthesis or decay measurements in addition to counting the molecules present.
