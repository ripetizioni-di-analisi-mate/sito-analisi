One nucleotide is inserted into a coding region after AUG, with no downstream compensation. What effect on reading is expected?

- A reading-frame shift in codons downstream of the insertion.
- Replacement of only one amino acid with no other possible consequences.
- Addition of exactly one amino acid with the frame always unchanged.
- Obligatory appearance of a stop exactly at the insertion point.

## Explanation

Codons are groups of three nucleotides. Inserting one changes the grouping of subsequent bases because the change is not a multiple of three. Downstream codons can therefore change, and a premature stop may appear. This is not equivalent to simply adding one amino acid, which would require an inserted triplet in the appropriate frame.
