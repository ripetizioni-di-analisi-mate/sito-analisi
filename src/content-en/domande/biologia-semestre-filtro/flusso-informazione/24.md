In an experiment, primers are removed and gaps filled normally, but newly formed fragments remain separated by backbone nicks. Which activity is most directly suspect?

- DNA ligase.
- Helicase, responsible for separating templates.
- Primase, responsible for producing primers.
- RNA-primer removal activity.

## Explanation

Primer removal and gap filling are already complete, so the defect concerns the step joining adjacent ends. DNA ligase forms the final phosphodiester bond, making the backbone continuous. A defect in the gap-filling polymerase would also be expected to leave gaps; the question specifically excludes that observation.
