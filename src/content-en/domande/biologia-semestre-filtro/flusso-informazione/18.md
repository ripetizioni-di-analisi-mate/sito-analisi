Helicase opens DNA, but the region still in a double helix accumulates torsional stress. Which activity directly addresses this problem?

- Production of new lagging-strand primers.
- Sealing nicks between already filled Okazaki fragments.
- Exonucleolytic removal of an incorrect nucleotide from the new-chain 3′ end.
- Transient DNA cleavage and resealing by topoisomerases.

## Explanation

Opening the double helix changes DNA winding ahead of the fork. Topoisomerases allow stress to relax through controlled transient breaks, which are then resealed. Helicase separates the strands, whereas this activity handles the topological consequences of opening. Primer production, proofreading and nick sealing solve different problems within the same replication process.
