Which component performs the central catalytic role in ribosomal peptide bond formation?

- The large-subunit rRNA.
- Large-subunit proteins, as in a conventional protein enzyme.
- Small-subunit rRNA in the decoding centre.
- Aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase, remaining in the ribosome to form every peptide bond.

## Explanation

The peptidyl-transferase centre consists mainly of large-subunit rRNA. Ribosomal proteins contribute to the complex’s structure and operation, but the specified central catalytic role belongs to RNA. The ribosome therefore illustrates RNA-based catalysis, distinct from mRNA’s informational role.
