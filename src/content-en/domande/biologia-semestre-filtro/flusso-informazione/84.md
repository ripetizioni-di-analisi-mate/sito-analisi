Which enzyme-function association describes the ubiquitination pathway?

- E1 activates ubiquitin, E2 conjugates it, and E3 contributes to substrate selection and transfer.
- E1 activates ubiquitin, E2 recognises every substrate and E3 removes the ubiquitin chain.
- E1 selects the substrate, E2 activates ubiquitin and E3 directly degrades the protein into peptides.
- E3 activates ubiquitin as the universal first step, then E1 transfers it directly to the proteasome.

## Explanation

Initial activation consumes energy and makes ubiquitin available for the subsequent transfer cascade. E2 and E3 ligases cooperate in transferring it to selected substrates; catalytic details vary among E3 classes. The functional sequence connects signal activation to target specificity without confusing it with transcription or translation.
