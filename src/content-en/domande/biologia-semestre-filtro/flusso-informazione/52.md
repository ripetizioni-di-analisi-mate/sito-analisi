After treatment, mRNA number and half-life remain unchanged, protein degradation rate is unchanged, and amino acid incorporation into the target protein increases. Which level is directly supported?

- A necessary transcription increase as the only explanation of the data.
- Increased protein synthesis through translation.
- Increased mRNA stability.
- Increased protein stability.

## Explanation

Incorporation of new amino acids measures protein-chain production. With messenger amount and protein removal controlled, the result supports an increase in translation efficiency or frequency. The stated measurements distinguish this effect from increased protein stability or higher mRNA abundance.
