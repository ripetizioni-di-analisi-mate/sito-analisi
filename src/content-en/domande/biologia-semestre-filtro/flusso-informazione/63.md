A nucleolar perturbation reduces rRNA precursor processing and ribosomal subunit assembly. Which function is directly affected?

- Only amino acid charging of tRNAs, without involving ribosomes.
- Peptide bond formation in the already assembled small subunit.
- Addition of poly-A tails to pre-mRNAs.
- Production of mature ribosomal components.

## Explanation

The nucleolus hosts transcription and maturation of important rRNAs and stages of ribosome assembly. Subunits are then exported and participate in cytoplasmic translation. A nucleolar defect can therefore reduce ribosome availability without implying that protein synthesis normally takes place inside the nucleolus.
