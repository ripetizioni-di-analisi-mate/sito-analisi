RNA polymerase III activity is selectively reduced in a eukaryotic cell. Which pair of products is directly affected?

- Pre-mRNAs and several miRNAs.
- 18S rRNA and 28S rRNA.
- tRNAs and 5S rRNA.
- Pre-mRNAs and 28S rRNA.

## Explanation

Polymerase III transcribes tRNA genes and the 5S rRNA gene, among others. The common precursor of the major rRNAs, which yields 18S and 28S, is produced by polymerase I, whereas pre-mRNAs depend on II. A reduction in tRNAs can eventually affect protein synthesis, but that is a downstream effect, not a direct polymerase product.
