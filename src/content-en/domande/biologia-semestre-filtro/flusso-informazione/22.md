A fork moves to the right. The upper template runs from 3′ on the left to 5′ on the right. Which description is correct for the new chain paired to this template?

- It is lagging because all upper templates produce fragments.
- It grows rightwards by adding nucleotides to its own 5′ end.
- It must grow continuously 3′ → 5′ to follow the fork.
- It can grow continuously to the right, with an extending 3′ end.

## Explanation

The upper template is read 3′ → 5′ when moving to the right. The complementary chain therefore grows 5′ → 3′ in the same direction as fork movement and can be leading. The growing end is always the 3′ end. Answering requires both template and fork directions, not merely the upper position in the diagram.
