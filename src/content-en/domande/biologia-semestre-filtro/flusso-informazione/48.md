A eukaryotic gene is transcribed normally, but its promoter contains no TATA box. Which conclusion is correct?

- Transcription obligatorily requires conversion of an enhancer into TATA.
- Absence of TATA proves that the gene has no promoter.
- The gene must be transcribed by polymerase III solely because TATA is absent.
- Functional promoters can have other sequence organisations.

## Explanation

The TATA box occurs in some eukaryotic promoters and can be recognised by TBP within the initiation complex. Other promoters use different combinations of elements. Its absence therefore demonstrates neither absence of a promoter nor impossibility of transcription.
