In the translation initiation complex, which ribosomal site accommodates initiator tRNA?

- The A site, like every incoming elongator tRNA.
- The E site, from which all tRNAs begin an E → P → A path.
- No site until the first peptide bond forms.
- The P site.

## Explanation

Initiator tRNA is positioned in the P site, paired to the start codon. This leaves the A site available for the aminoacyl-tRNA carrying the next amino acid. Thus entry of new tRNAs into the A site during elongation does not describe the initial placement of the first tRNA.
