A coding-strand segment is 5′-ATGGAATGA-3′. Without editing, what is the corresponding RNA sequence written 5′ → 3′?

- 5′-ATGGAATGA-3′
- 5′-UACCUUACU-3′
- 5′-AUGGAAUGA-3′
- 5′-UCAUUCCAU-3′

## Explanation

The coding strand has the same sequence as the transcript when both are written in the same direction, except that T is replaced by U. Polymerase uses the other, complementary strand as template. Directly complementing the coding strand would instead produce a sequence related to the template, not the requested transcript.
