Which observation supports RNA functions beyond carrying coding information?

- Catalytic RNAs must first be translated into proteins performing the same reaction.
- rRNAs function solely by encoding ribosomal proteins.
- Some RNAs contribute directly to catalytic centres, as in the spliceosome.
- Every snRNA is a messenger specifying a spliceosomal subunit.

## Explanation

snRNAs contribute to splice-site recognition and organisation of the spliceosome’s catalytic centre. Catalytic RNAs called ribozymes also exist. These roles show that RNA can have structural or catalytic functions as well as informational ones. mRNA is only one class of cellular RNA.
