In an experimental system, only the template region of telomerase RNA is changed while the enzyme remains active. Which change can result directly?

- A different sequence in the added DNA repeats.
- A change in primer sequence without any new telomeric DNA.
- An obligatory reversal of DNA synthesis from 5′ → 3′ to 3′ → 5′.
- Automatic replacement of pre-existing telomeric repeats.

## Explanation

The specified RNA region is read as the template for adding telomeric nucleotides. Changing it can therefore alter the complementary sequence of newly synthesised DNA. The direct effect concerns the enzyme's product, not existing genomic sequences at other loci. Assuming the enzyme remains active separates the sequence effect from possible catalytic inhibition.
