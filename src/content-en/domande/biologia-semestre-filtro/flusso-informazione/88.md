After a proteasome inhibitor, a protein increases while nascent RNA, mRNA amount and measured protein synthesis remain unchanged. Which explanation is most directly supported?

- Increased transcription of the gene.
- Increased mRNA stability causing protein accumulation.
- Increased degradation, which alone explains accumulation with unchanged synthesis.
- Reduced degradation of the protein.

## Explanation

Protein abundance results from production minus removal. If measurements keep production unchanged and the intervention blocks a removal system, reduced degradation directly explains accumulation. Attributing it to transcription would conflict with unchanged nascent RNA measurements. The case distinguishes post-translational regulation from transcriptional control.
