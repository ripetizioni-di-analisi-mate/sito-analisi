A protein is ubiquitinated, but linkage type and signal organisation are unknown. Which conclusion avoids an incorrect generalisation?

- Monoubiquitination and polyubiquitin chains always have the same functional meaning.
- The effect may depend on the signal and need not be proteasomal degradation.
- Every single ubiquitin is sufficient to always impose immediate degradation.
- Chain type is irrelevant because all receptors recognise every signal identically.

## Explanation

The meaning of ubiquitination depends on ubiquitin number, arrangement and linkages, as well as substrate context. Some signals favour degradation; others regulate trafficking or signalling. Without that information, a unique fate cannot be inferred. The observation concerns a protein modification and does not demonstrate a DNA change.
