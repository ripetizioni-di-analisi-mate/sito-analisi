A protein is modified with SUMO. Which consequence is scientifically justified as a general possibility?

- SUMOylation is always equivalent to a K48-linked polyubiquitin chain.
- Interactions or localisation may change without obligatory proteasomal degradation.
- SUMO must always be removed before it can affect a protein interaction.
- SUMOylation always directs the protein to the lysosome.

## Explanation

SUMO is a ubiquitin-like protein conjugated to targets through specific machinery. The modification can create or alter interaction surfaces and influence localisation or activity. Similarity to ubiquitin does not make all signals equivalent, so proteasomal fate does not automatically follow from SUMOylation.
