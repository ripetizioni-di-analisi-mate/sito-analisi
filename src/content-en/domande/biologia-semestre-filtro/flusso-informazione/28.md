Somatic cells with critically short telomeres stably stop proliferating. Which interpretation is consistent without claiming more than the data show?

- Telomeric damage can contribute to replicative senescence.
- The observation shows that all senescence depends exclusively on telomeres.
- The arrest proves that every cell has already acquired tumour-forming capacity.
- The arrest proves that telomerase activity is necessarily high.

## Explanation

Critically short or unprotected telomeres can activate damage responses favouring stable proliferative arrest. This makes a telomeric contribution to the observed senescence plausible. Other stresses can also cause senescence, and a senescent cell is not automatically a tumour cell. The observation therefore does not demonstrate that the mechanism is exclusive.
