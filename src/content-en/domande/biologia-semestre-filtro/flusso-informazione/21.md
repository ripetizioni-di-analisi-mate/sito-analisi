A polymerase retains its incorporation speed while bound to DNA but dissociates much more often after losing the sliding clamp. Which property has directly decreased?

- Catalytic speed during each bound interval.
- Fidelity, defined as the number of nucleotides made before dissociation.
- Processivity.
- Synthesis direction, switching from 5′ → 3′ to 3′ → 5′.

## Explanation

Processivity measures how many nucleotides are incorporated during one binding event on the template. More frequent dissociation reduces that number even if catalytic speed while bound remains unchanged. The sliding clamp favours retention of the complex on DNA. Processivity and incorporation speed therefore describe different properties.
