Which pair of directions correctly describes translation elongation?

- mRNA read 5′ → 3′; protein synthesised from N to C.
- mRNA read 3′ → 5′; protein from N to C.
- mRNA read 5′ → 3′; protein from C to N.
- mRNA read 3′ → 5′; protein from C to N.

## Explanation

The symbols 5′ and 3′ describe nucleic acid ends, whereas N and C describe the polypeptide’s amino and carboxyl ends. The ribosome advances towards the messenger’s 3′ end and adds each new residue to the chain’s carboxyl terminus. The two conventions concern different molecules and must be distinguished.
