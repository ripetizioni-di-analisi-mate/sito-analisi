Helicase functions at a fork, but the proteins coating single-stranded DNA are missing. Which problem becomes more likely in newly opened regions?

- Failure of primer removal because these proteins directly perform RNase activity.
- Reannealing or secondary structure formation that hinders copying.
- Failure of double-stranded DNA relaxation because these proteins are topoisomerases.
- Failure of nick sealing because these proteins catalyse the final phosphodiester bonds.

## Explanation

Single-stranded DNA-binding proteins stabilise the exposed template and limit interactions that would make it less accessible. Without them, complementary segments can reanneal or fold, hindering replication. Template chemical polarity remains unchanged: the problem concerns physical availability to the enzymes copying it.
