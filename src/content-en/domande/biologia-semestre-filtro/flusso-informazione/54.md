In a normal pre-mRNA, which sequence of events produces the polyadenylated 3′ end?

- Addition of adenines at the 5′ end, followed by cap removal.
- Precursor cleavage, followed by enzymatic adenine addition.
- Intron removal by the spliceosome, which directly synthesises the poly-A tail.
- Obligatory transcription of a long genomic thymine tract without precursor cleavage.

## Explanation

Recognition of processing signals leads to pre-mRNA cleavage. Poly-A polymerase then extends the new 3′ end with adenines without using a DNA sequence as a template for the tail. Polymerase II transcription termination is linked to this processing but does not simply coincide with the last adenine added.
