A mature tRNA loses its 3′ CCA end while the anticodon remains intact. Which function is directly impaired?

- Hydrolysis of the polypeptide–tRNA bond catalysed during termination.
- Amino acid attachment to the 5′ end, which is the normal acceptor end.
- Codon reading, because the anticodon is identical to CCA.
- Amino acid attachment to tRNA.

## Explanation

The amino acid is attached to the tRNA’s 3′ acceptor end, which terminates in CCA. The anticodon has a different function, codon recognition, and the question preserves it. Loss of the charging site therefore prevents normal function as a charged adaptor even though the codon-recognition region remains present.
