After synthesis stops completely, a protein decreases from 120 to 60 units in the first 2 hours and then to 45 in the next 2 hours. Which conclusion is consistent with these data?

- A constant absolute loss of 60 units every 2 hours describes both intervals.
- The two intervals are inconsistent with a constant 2-hour half-life.
- A constant 2-hour half-life predicts 45 units after 4 hours.
- A constant 4-hour half-life describes both intervals exactly.

## Explanation

The remaining fraction is $\dfrac{60}{120}=\dfrac{1}{2}$ in the first interval and $\dfrac{45}{60}=\dfrac{3}{4}$ in the second. Equal intervals therefore do not show the same decay factor. A constant 2-hour half-life would give 30 units after 4 hours, not 45. Constant absolute loss also fails: the decreases are 60 and 15 units. These data alone do not identify the cause of the change in removal.
