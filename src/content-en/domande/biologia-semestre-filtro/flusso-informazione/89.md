Large protein aggregates are enclosed in structures that reach lysosomes. Which pathway is consistent with this observation?

- Cytosolic proteasomal degradation of the intact aggregate without unfolding.
- Secretory export that necessarily releases intact aggregates outside the cell.
- Only chaperone-assisted folding, without lysosomal transport or degradation.
- Autophagy with lysosomal degradation.

## Explanation

Autophagy routes cytoplasmic material, including some aggregates and organelles, towards lysosomes. The proteasome instead processes substrates that can be recognised and unfolded to pass through its proteolytic system. The described lysosomal destination therefore identifies a distinct pathway that also contributes to proteostasis.
