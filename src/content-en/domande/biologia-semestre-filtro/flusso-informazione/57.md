Two tissues have the same gene sequence, but one produces mRNA containing exons 1-2-3 and the other exons 1-3. Which mechanism directly explains the difference?

- Alternative splicing that skips exon 2.
- Editing individual bases in exon 2 without removing nucleotides.
- Shortening the poly-A tail after processing.
- Obligatory deletion of exon 2 from the second tissue’s DNA.

## Explanation

Identical DNA means a genomic deletion is not required to explain these products. Different splice-site selection can join exon 1 directly to exon 3 in the second tissue, while the first also retains exon 2. Differently expressed regulatory factors can determine the choice. The variation occurs during RNA processing.
