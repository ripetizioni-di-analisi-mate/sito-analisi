An n+1 gamete fuses with a normal n gamete. What chromosome complement is expected in the zygote?

- n+1
- 4n
- 2n+1
- 2n-1

## Explanation

Fertilisation combines the gamete complements: $(n+1)+n=2n+1$. The affected chromosome has three copies rather than two. This is aneuploidy, not duplication of the whole complement as in tetraploidy.
