In a normal mitotic cycle of a diploid somatic cell, what conclusion follows from measuring 4C DNA in the whole cell alone?

- It identifies G2 and excludes every mitotic stage
- It identifies ongoing S phase
- It identifies M and excludes G2
- It does not distinguish G2 from M

## Explanation

G2 and mitosis before cellular separation can share the same total DNA content. Another indicator, such as chromosome condensation, is needed to distinguish them. Ploidy, DNA quantity and DNA organisation provide different information.
