Spermiogenesis transforms spermatids into sperm. Which change correctly describes it?

- Remodelling without a third meiotic division
- Homologue separation in the first meiotic division
- Sister separation in the second meiotic division
- Premeiotic replication producing primary spermatocytes

## Explanation

Spermatids have completed meiosis and are already haploid. Maturation changes cell shape and organisation without a third division to reduce the chromosome complement further. Division and differentiation are distinct processes.
