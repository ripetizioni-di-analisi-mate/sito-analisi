Which complex is most directly associated with mitotic entry in the animal-cell model?

- Cyclin E–CDK2
- Cyclin B–CDK1
- Cyclin D–CDK4/6
- Securin–inhibited separase

## Explanation

Cyclin B–CDK1 activation promotes mitotic events, including nuclear and spindle reorganisation. Cyclin D–CDK4/6 supports G1, and cyclin E–CDK2 the transition towards S. Securin–separase controls sister separation.
