Which combination of capacities defines a stem cell?

- Self-renewal and generation of differentiated progeny
- Many divisions followed by exhaustion without self-renewal
- Rapid division as the sole requirement, regardless of progeny fates
- Differentiation capacity without maintenance of cells with the same properties

## Explanation

Self-renewal maintains cells with stem-cell capacity over time, while differentiation produces specialised types. Division rate is not the defining criterion: some stem cells are quiescent. Differentiation alone, without maintenance of the stem-cell compartment, does not demonstrate self-renewal.
