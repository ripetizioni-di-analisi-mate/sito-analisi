One pair fails to separate in meiosis I; meiosis II is normal. How are the four products classified for that chromosome?

- Four normal n products
- One n+1, one n-1 and two n
- Two n and two n+1
- Two n+1 and two n-1

## Explanation

Both homologues enter one cell after division I and the other receives neither. Division II separates sisters but does not correct the partition: two products have an extra copy and two lack one.
