Non-degradable securin retains its inhibitory function. Which step is directly obstructed?

- Initial APC/C activation even when the checkpoint has been satisfied
- Kinetochore attachment to the already formed spindle
- Initial chromosome condensation in prophase
- Effective separase activation

## Explanation

While the inhibitor remains bound and functional, separase cannot normally release sisters through cohesin cleavage. Spindle presence or APC/C activation is insufficient to overcome the substrate’s resistance to degradation.
