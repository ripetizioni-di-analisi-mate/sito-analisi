Two cell types respond to Fas: one requires mitochondrial amplification, while the other can activate executioners effectively without it. Must BAX/BAK blockade have identical effects?

- No; dependence on the mitochondrial step differs
- Yes, because every extrinsic pathway always ignores mitochondria
- No, because blockade must affect the second type more strongly, even though it does not require MOMP
- Yes, because every caspase-8 signal must pass through the apoptosome

## Explanation

The question gives a causal difference between the models. Blocking a necessary step in the first can reduce its response more than in the second. Results should not be generalised without specifying the cell type’s amplification requirements.
