Which enzymatic activity belongs to APC/C?

- Protease directly cleaving cohesin
- Ubiquitin ligase
- DNA ligase joining replication fragments
- Phosphatase removing CDK1 phosphates

## Explanation

APC/C promotes ubiquitylation of selected substrates, including securin and mitotic cyclins. Tagging promotes proteasomal degradation. Separase, Cdc25 and DNA ligase perform different actions; identifying the enzyme prevents reversing the sequence.
