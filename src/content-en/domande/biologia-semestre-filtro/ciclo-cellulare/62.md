Eight primary spermatocytes complete both divisions without loss. How many spermatids are expected in the model?

- 8
- 64
- 32
- 16

## Explanation

Each primary spermatocyte produces two cells after division I and four products after division II. Thus $8\cdot4=32$. Absence of loss is a problem assumption, not a universal prediction of yield in a real population.
