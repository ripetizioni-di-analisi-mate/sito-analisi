How many duplicated homologous chromosomes and chromatids does a meiotic tetrad contain?

- 4 chromosomes and 4 chromatids
- 2 chromosomes and 4 chromatids
- 2 chromosomes and 2 chromatids
- 4 chromosomes and 8 chromatids

## Explanation

A bivalent forms when two already duplicated homologues pair. Each has two chromatids, making four altogether. Tetrad refers to those four chromatids, not four independent chromosomes.
