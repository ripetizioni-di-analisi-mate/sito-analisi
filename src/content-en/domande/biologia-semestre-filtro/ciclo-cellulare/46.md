Which event is normally absent between meiosis I and meiosis II?

- Spindle reorganisation
- Possible changes in nuclear organisation
- Preparation for sister separation
- Another complete DNA replication

## Explanation

One S phase precedes both divisions. Another replication between I and II would prevent the expected reduction to 1C after division II. The other events concern division organisation and can occur.
