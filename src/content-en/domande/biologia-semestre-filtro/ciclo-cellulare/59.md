At which stage do human primary oocytes undergo their prolonged arrest before maturation resumes?

- G1 before any replication
- Prophase I
- Mitotic anaphase
- Metaphase II after ovulation

## Explanation

Primary oocytes begin meiosis before birth and arrest in prophase I after S phase. Metaphase-II arrest instead belongs to the secondary oocyte. Identifying the cell stage prevents assigning both pauses to the same phase.
