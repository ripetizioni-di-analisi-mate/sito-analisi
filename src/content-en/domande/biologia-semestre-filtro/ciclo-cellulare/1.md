A diploid human cell has completed S but has not separated chromatids. Which count is correct?

- 46 chromosomes and 46 DNA molecules
- 23 chromosomes and 46 DNA molecules
- 92 chromosomes and 92 DNA molecules
- 46 chromosomes and 92 DNA molecules

## Explanation

S duplicated each DNA molecule, producing two sister chromatids per chromosome. There are still 46 chromosomes until sister centromeres separate, but 92 DNA molecules. Replication is not a reductional division and does not halve the chromosome complement.
