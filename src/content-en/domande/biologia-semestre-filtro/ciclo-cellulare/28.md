A chromosome is aligned, but one kinetochore is unattached. Which conclusion follows with a functional checkpoint?

- Alignment proves that APC/C–Cdc20 is already fully active
- Anaphase can remain delayed
- Tension on any other chromosome necessarily silences the unattached kinetochore
- The missing attachment directly activates separase

## Explanation

Alignment describes position, not the quality of every connection. An unattached kinetochore can maintain signalling that inhibits APC/C–Cdc20. An overview image therefore cannot prove that every requirement has been met.
