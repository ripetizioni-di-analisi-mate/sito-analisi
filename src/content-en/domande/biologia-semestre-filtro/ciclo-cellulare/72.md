Which statement about necrosis avoids an overly rigid classification?

- Every regulated death is necessarily apoptosis
- Necrotic morphology always proves the absence of regulatable molecular components
- Regulated forms with necrotic morphology also exist
- Phosphatidylserine exposure alone excludes every death with necrotic morphology

## Explanation

Necrotic morphology typically includes swelling and loss of integrity, but does not always mean absence of molecular regulation. Defining a mechanism requires more than a programmed-versus-accidental dichotomy. Apoptosis and regulated death are not exact synonyms.
