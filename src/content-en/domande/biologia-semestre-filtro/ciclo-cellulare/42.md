A cell from a $2n=10$ species completes normal meiosis I. Which combination applies to each product?

- 5 chromosomes, 10 chromatids, 2C
- 5 chromosomes, 5 chromatids, 1C
- 10 chromosomes, 20 chromatids, 4C
- 10 chromosomes, 10 chromatids, 2C

## Explanation

The haploid number is five. After meiosis I each chromosome retains two chromatids, giving ten chromatids and 2C DNA. The cell is already haploid although the second division has not yet occurred.
