Which pattern is typically compatible with early apoptosis?

- Marked swelling with early loss of integrity and content leakage
- Mitotic condensation with chromosomes aligned on a functional spindle, as the only finding
- Immediate membrane rupture preceding every other change
- Chromatin condensation with initially intact membrane

## Explanation

Apoptosis involves coordinated structural changes and can initially preserve membrane integrity, facilitating fragment clearance. Integrity can be lost later, so the measurement’s timing matters.
