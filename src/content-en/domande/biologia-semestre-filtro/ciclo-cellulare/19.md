Functional loss of a gene that restrains proliferation is consistent with which category?

- Gain of function of a proto-oncogene
- Activation of the still-intact inhibitory gene
- Tumour-suppressor inactivation
- A transient ligand increase with preserved gene function

## Explanation

A tumour suppressor restrains processes promoting transformation or supports integrity and damage responses. Loss of function can remove a brake. This principle does not automatically establish every downstream change or a clinical outcome.
