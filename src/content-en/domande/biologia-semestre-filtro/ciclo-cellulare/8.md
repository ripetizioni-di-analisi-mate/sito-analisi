Which Cdc25 action promotes CDK1 activation in the mitotic-control model?

- Removal of inhibitory CDK1 phosphates
- Addition of inhibitory CDK1 phosphates
- Degradation of CDK1-bound cyclin B
- Removal of the activating CDK phosphorylation

## Explanation

Cdc25 is a phosphatase. Removing specific inhibitory phosphates promotes cyclin–CDK activity. The meaning depends on the modified site: removing a phosphate does not always inactivate a protein.
