Caspases act directly primarily on which class of substrates?

- Glucose through kinase activity
- Proteins
- DNA through nuclease activity
- Lipids through phospholipase activity

## Explanation

Caspases are proteases that cleave selected protein substrates. Apoptotic DNA fragmentation involves nucleases regulated downstream. A final effect on DNA does not give the caspase the enzymatic activity of a nuclease.
