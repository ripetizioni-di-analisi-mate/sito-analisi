A cell is positive for both annexin V and a membrane-impermeant dye. Which conclusion does not require prior knowledge of its timeline?

- It exposes phosphatidylserine and does not exclude the dye
- It certainly began with primary necrosis
- It certainly has an intact membrane
- It has never activated caspases

## Explanation

Double positivity describes the current state, not automatically the order of events. It can occur in late apoptosis or other integrity-loss contexts. A time series and complementary measurements help distinguish routes.
