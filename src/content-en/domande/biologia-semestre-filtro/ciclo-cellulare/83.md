What can follow from BID cleavage by caspase 8?

- Necessarily prevent BAX/BAK activation
- Link extrinsic signalling to mitochondrial amplification
- Directly replace FADD in Fas binding to its ligand
- Directly activate caspase 9 without APAF1 or other components

## Explanation

BID links the pathways in several cell types. Intrinsic and extrinsic routes should therefore not be imagined as always isolated. Dependence on this link varies with context and must be specified in experiments.
