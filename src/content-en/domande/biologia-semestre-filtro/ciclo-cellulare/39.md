A proteasome inhibitor prevents destruction of already ubiquitylated securin, which retains its inhibitory binding to separase. Which prediction follows in the model?

- Ubiquitylation alone must immediately inactivate securin
- APC/C must lack activity because its substrate persists
- Tagging can occur without releasing separase
- Separase replaces the proteasome in securin degradation

## Explanation

Substrate tagging and destruction are separate steps. If securin persists and retains inhibition, separase can remain blocked despite ubiquitylation. The case tests the causal sequence rather than a simple protein association.
