In a $2n=6$ species, a cell completes meiosis I and each of its two daughters completes meiosis II, without losses. How many cellular products form in total?

- 8
- 2
- 4
- 6

## Explanation

The two divisions first produce two cells and then four products if both complete the process. The value $2^3=8$ would describe possible maternal/paternal combinations for three pairs without recombination across many meioses. Combination number and the products of one event are distinct quantities.
