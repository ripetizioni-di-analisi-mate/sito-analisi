Between which structures does meiotic crossing over typically generate new allele combinations?

- Sister chromatids of the same duplicated chromosome
- Non-sister chromatids of homologous chromosomes
- Chromatids of non-homologous chromosomes as the normal event
- The two strands of the same double helix within one chromatid

## Explanation

Non-sister chromatids belong to the two homologues of a pair. Exchange between corresponding regions can recombine alleles along a chromosome. This differs from assortment, which distributes whole homologues towards the poles.
