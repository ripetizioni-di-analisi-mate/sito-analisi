In open mitosis of many animal cells, which change allows spindle access to chromosomes?

- Cohesin proteolysis instead of nuclear reorganisation
- Complete chromosome decondensation before capture
- Premature assembly of a continuous nuclear lamina
- Disassembly of the nuclear envelope and lamina

## Explanation

In open mitosis, envelope and lamina disassembly removes the physical separation obstructing kinetochore access. Lamin phosphorylation contributes to this process. Cohesin cleavage concerns sister separation, whereas lamina reassembly belongs to mitotic exit.
