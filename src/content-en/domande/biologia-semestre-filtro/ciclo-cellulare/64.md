Which event has occurred in a secondary spermatocyte but not yet been completed in a primary spermatocyte at metaphase I?

- Meiotic homologue separation
- Crossing over and initial homologue pairing
- Premeiotic DNA replication
- Final separation of all sisters

## Explanation

A secondary spermatocyte is a meiosis-I product with one set of still-duplicated chromosomes. Sister separation belongs to meiosis II and produces spermatids. The stage name must not be confused with completed maturation.
