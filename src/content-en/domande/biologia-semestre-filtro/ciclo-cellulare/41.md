Which event directly reduces ploidy from diploid to haploid in normal meiosis?

- Homologue separation in meiosis I
- Chromosome condensation in prophase
- DNA replication before meiosis
- Sister separation in meiosis II

## Explanation

Ploidy counts homologous chromosome sets. Division I sends one homologue of each pair to each product, reducing two sets to one. Division II reduces DNA by separating sisters, without another reduction in set number.
