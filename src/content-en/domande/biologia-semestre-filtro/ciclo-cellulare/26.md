Which spindle microtubules interact with the cortex and help orient the spindle?

- Kinetochore and interpolar microtubules together
- Only interpolar microtubules
- Astral microtubules
- Only kinetochore microtubules

## Explanation

Astral microtubules extend from the pole towards the periphery and interact with the cortex, contributing to spindle orientation. Interpolar microtubules overlap centrally; kinetochore microtubules connect chromosomes. Position and interaction partner distinguish these functions.
