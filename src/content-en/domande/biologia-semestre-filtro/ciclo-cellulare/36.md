Why are cyclin B degradation and securin degradation functionally different?

- They release separase and reduce CDK1 activity, respectively
- Both directly increase CDK1 activity
- Both directly inhibit separase
- They reduce mitotic activity and release separase, respectively

## Explanation

The substrates control different targets. Destroying cyclin B helps reduce CDK1 activity and exit mitosis; destroying securin permits separase action. Coordinated degradation connects segregation and exit without making the regulators interchangeable.
