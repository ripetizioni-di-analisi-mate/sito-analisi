Two samples contain equal cyclin B, but only the first has inhibitory CDK1 phosphorylation. With other conditions equal, which prediction follows?

- The second may have greater CDK1 activity
- The first must have greater activity because every phosphorylation is activating
- The second must have lower activity because Cdc25 is a phosphatase
- Both must have identical CDK1 activity

## Explanation

Cyclin abundance is equal, but the first sample retains inhibitory CDK1 phosphorylation. With other conditions equal, the second may therefore be more active. The regulatory sign depends on the site: Cdc25 promotes activation by removing inhibitory phosphates, whereas phosphorylation at other sites can be activating.
