Mitogens are withdrawn after the restriction point, with intact DNA and adequate nutrients. Which outcome fits the classical model?

- Obligatory immediate G1 arrest in the current cycle
- Loss of every possibility of arrest after subsequent damage
- Continuation of the cycle already initiated
- Indefinite proliferation in subsequent cycles without external signals

## Explanation

The restriction point marks a change in mitogen dependence for the current cycle. Passing it does not remove DNA checkpoints or guarantee indefinite proliferation in future cycles.
