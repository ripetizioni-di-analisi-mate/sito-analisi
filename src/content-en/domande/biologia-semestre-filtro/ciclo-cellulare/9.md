In an isolated cyclin–CDK complex, cyclin is degraded while CDK remains present. What direct effect is expected on complex activity?

- A direct increase in the activity of that free CDK
- Obligatory maintenance of the same activity because CDK is still present
- Reduced activity of the complex
- Reactivation of the complex without any need for cyclin

## Explanation

Cyclin is a regulatory subunit needed for the corresponding complex’s activity. Removing it enables rapid change without necessarily destroying the kinase. Selective degradation therefore helps order cell-cycle phases.
