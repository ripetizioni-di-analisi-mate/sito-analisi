Which statement correctly describes chromosome movement in the spindle?

- It depends exclusively on motors while microtubules remain static
- Motors and microtubule dynamics cooperate
- It depends exclusively on depolymerisation without motor contributions
- It depends on the actin–myosin ring directly pulling each kinetochore

## Explanation

Kinesins and dyneins generate forces; microtubule growth and depolymerisation also contribute to movement. Kinetochore connections transmit these forces to chromosomes. The actin–myosin ring instead separates the cytoplasm and does not replace the spindle system.
