An experiment finds abundant cyclin but little phosphorylation of a CDK substrate. Which additional check best addresses the discrepancy?

- The CDK’s inhibitory and activating state
- The amount of cyclin alone in a second identical sample
- Total DNA content as sufficient proof of CDK activity
- Cell number as sufficient proof of CDK activity

## Explanation

Regulator abundance does not establish catalytic activity. Inhibitory phosphorylation, missing activating modifications or protein inhibitors can limit the complex. Measuring these factors directly addresses the discrepancy between amount and function.
