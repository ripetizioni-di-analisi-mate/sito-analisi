Which statement distinguishes assortment from crossing over?

- Assortment distributes homologues; crossing over exchanges non-sister chromatid segments
- Assortment exchanges chromosome segments; crossing over orients whole bivalents
- Both change only whole-chromosome combinations without internal exchanges
- Both generate new combinations exclusively through replication errors

## Explanation

The two sources of variation act at different levels. Bivalent orientation determines which homologues enter products; recombination can make each homologue a mosaic of segments. These combinations do not require another DNA replication.
