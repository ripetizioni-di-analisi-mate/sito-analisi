What general role do BH3-only proteins play in the intrinsic-pathway model?

- They directly cleave executioner-caspase protein substrates
- They alone form the apoptosome instead of APAF1
- They regulate the balance of pro- and anti-apoptotic members
- They bind cytochrome c and transfer electrons to the respiratory chain

## Explanation

BH3-only proteins transmit stress signals through BCL2-family interactions. Outcomes can favour BAX/BAK activation and MOMP. They should not be confused with enzymes executing downstream proteolytic cleavage.
