A clone divides repeatedly and then exhausts without maintaining cells with the same capacity. What is missing to demonstrate durable stemness?

- Evidence that at least one cell completed mitosis
- Evidence of long-term self-renewal
- Evidence of a high initial division count
- Evidence only of transient expression of a progenitor marker

## Explanation

A progenitor can proliferate extensively for a limited period. Stemness also requires sustained capacity over time, not merely initial production of many cells. A large division count does not replace that functional evidence.
