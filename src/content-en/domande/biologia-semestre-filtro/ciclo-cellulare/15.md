Which distinction between the restriction point and DNA checkpoints is correct?

- Proliferative commitment and DNA integrity are distinct controls
- Passing the restriction point makes arrest impossible
- The restriction point certifies that replication of the whole genome is already complete
- An active DNA checkpoint proves that the cell never received mitogens

## Explanation

A cell can commit to proliferation and later encounter damage that arrests its cycle. The first control concerns mitogen dependence; checkpoints coordinate progression with conditions such as DNA integrity and replication completion.
