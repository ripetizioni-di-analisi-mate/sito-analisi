One protein complex promotes chromosome compaction, another maintains sister cohesion. Which pair matches these roles?

- Cohesin; condensin
- Condensin; cohesin
- Condensin; separase
- Cohesin; securin

## Explanation

Condensins contribute to compact chromosome organisation; cohesins connect sister chromatids. These are coordinated but different functions: condensing a structure is not equivalent to keeping two copies connected.
