In a stem-cell population, some divisions yield two stem cells and others two differentiating daughters. Which conclusion is correct?

- Every division must be asymmetric
- Population balance can support self-renewal
- Divisions yielding two stem cells must increase the population without limit under every condition
- Symmetric divisions exclude self-renewal

## Explanation

Maintenance can emerge from a balance of different symmetric outcomes. Every individual division need not yield exactly one stem and one differentiated cell. Population behaviour must be distinguished from one division’s outcome.
