In a sample, p53 increases but p21 does not accumulate. Which conclusion avoids overinterpretation?

- The p53 increase alone proves that the p21 branch functions
- Absence of p21 alone proves that p53 cannot regulate any other gene
- The connection leading to p21 may be impaired
- Failure of p21 accumulation proves that DNA is already fully repaired

## Explanation

Measuring an upstream regulator does not guarantee a particular downstream effect. p21 transcription, translation or stability may be altered; controls are needed to locate the defect. The finding neither excludes other p53 functions nor establishes complete DNA repair.
