Which comparison among BCL2-family members fits the mitochondrial model?

- BAX/BAK oppose MOMP; BCL2/BCL-XL promote it
- All these members promote MOMP with the same role
- BAX/BAK promote MOMP; BCL2/BCL-XL can oppose it
- BCL2 and BCL-XL oppose only caspase 8 without acting on mitochondrial control

## Explanation

The family includes pro- and anti-apoptotic functions. Their interaction balance influences permeabilisation before apoptosome assembly. Membership in one family does not imply identical effects on cell fate.
