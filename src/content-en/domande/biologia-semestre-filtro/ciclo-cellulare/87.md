DNA fragmentation follows caspase activation during apoptosis. Which enzymatic interpretation is correct?

- Caspases can regulate downstream nucleases
- Nucleases must be the same proteins as caspases because they activate sequentially
- All caspases directly cleave DNA phosphodiester bonds
- Caspase activation proves that no nuclease contributes to fragmentation

## Explanation

The cascade can activate fragmentation machinery through proteolytic cleavage of regulators. Nucleases then act on DNA. Temporal association does not assign every downstream enzymatic activity to the first enzyme.
