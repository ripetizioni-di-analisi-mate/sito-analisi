Which order of prophase-I stages is correct?

- Zygotene, leptotene, diplotene, pachytene, diakinesis
- Diakinesis, leptotene, pachytene, zygotene, diplotene
- Leptotene, zygotene, pachytene, diplotene, diakinesis
- Pachytene, diplotene, leptotene, zygotene, diakinesis

## Explanation

The sequence describes chromosome organisation, pairing, recombination stages and later partial homologue separation with chiasmata retained. Ordering them links structure to function and avoids placing pairing resolution before pairing is established.
