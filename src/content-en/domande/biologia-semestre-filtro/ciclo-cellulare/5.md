A checkpoint detects incomplete replication before mitosis. Which response limits transmission of incompletely copied DNA?

- Delay entry into M phase
- Prematurely activate cyclin B–CDK1
- Degrade securin to begin anaphase immediately
- Initiate cytokinesis before segregation

## Explanation

Delaying M-phase entry avoids starting segregation with an incompletely copied genome and allows time to manage replication stress. Activating CDK1 or advancing anaphase and cytokinesis would instead move division forward without resolving the problem. The checkpoint coordinates processes; it does not directly synthesise the missing sequences.
