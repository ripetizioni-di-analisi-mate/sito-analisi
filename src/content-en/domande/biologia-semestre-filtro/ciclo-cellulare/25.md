NDC80 is impaired, but tubulin polymerises. Which defect is most directly expected?

- Loss of sister cohesion with normal spindle attachments
- Unstable kinetochore–microtubule attachment
- A defect limited to astral microtubule contact with the cortex
- A defect limited to overlap between interpolar microtubules

## Explanation

NDC80 participates in the kinetochore–microtubule interface. The spindle can retain fibres while failing to transmit forces correctly to chromosomes. The question explicitly separates attachment failure from tubulin’s ability to polymerise.
