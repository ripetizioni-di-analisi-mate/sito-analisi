Apoptotic-body clearance is inefficient. Which consequence is possible?

- Guaranteed indefinite preservation of membrane integrity
- Late loss of integrity and secondary necrosis
- Automatic return to proliferation after chromatin condensation
- Exclusion of any content release even after fragment degradation

## Explanation

Initial membrane preservation does not guarantee indefinite fragment integrity. If not cleared, fragments can deteriorate and release contents. Timing and phagocytic capacity therefore influence the final observed pattern.
