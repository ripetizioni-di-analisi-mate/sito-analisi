Which description correctly defines interphase in a proliferating cell?

- It comprises G1 and G2 but excludes S phase
- It comprises S phase only
- It comprises G1, S and G2
- It comprises G2 and the whole of mitosis

## Explanation

Interphase includes preparation, replication and post-replication stages before mitosis. It is not an inactive interval: metabolism, gene expression and DNA replication occur within it. M phase is distinct from this group of phases.
