Which role links chiasmata to correct homologue segregation?

- They eliminate the need for arm cohesion
- Together with cohesion they support homologue connections
- They keep homologues joined until the end of meiosis II
- They replace homologue attachment to microtubules

## Explanation

Chiasmata reflect chromatid exchanges and, with cohesion, help keep the bivalent connected until the appropriate time. This supports homologue orientation. The spindle remains necessary for segregation.
