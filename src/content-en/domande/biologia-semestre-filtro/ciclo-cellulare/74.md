Which initiator caspase is recruited in the canonical apoptosome model?

- Caspase 3
- Caspase 7
- Caspase 8
- Caspase 9

## Explanation

The apoptosome recruits and activates procaspase 9. Caspases 3 and 7 are major downstream executioners; caspase 8 is an initiator associated with the extrinsic death-receptor model. Distinguishing activation complexes prevents the initiators from being interchanged.
