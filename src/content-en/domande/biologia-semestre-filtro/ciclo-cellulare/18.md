A growth-signalling receptor signals without ligand because of an activating mutation. Which mechanism does this illustrate?

- Loss of receptor function that eliminates all signalling
- A normal receptor response still strictly dependent on ligand
- Tumour-suppressor inactivation as the sole direct explanation of the stated variant
- Potentially oncogenic gain of function

## Explanation

The receptor acquired activity disconnected from normal regulation. This illustrates possible oncogenic activation, not necessarily increased protein abundance. Full transformation requires broader assessment of cellular properties.
