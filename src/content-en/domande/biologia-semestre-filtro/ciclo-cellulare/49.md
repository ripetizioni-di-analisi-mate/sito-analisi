For a $2n=6$ species, ignoring crossing over, how many maternal/paternal homologue combinations are possible across many gametes?

- 8
- 6
- 12
- 64

## Explanation

There are three independently assorting pairs. Each offers two choices of homologue origin, giving $2^3=8$. This counts possible combinations across many meioses, not the products generated by a single meiotic cell.
