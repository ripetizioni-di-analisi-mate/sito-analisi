Which sister-kinetochore arrangement characterises correctly bioriented mitotic metaphase?

- Both sisters lacking attachments
- Both sisters attached to the same pole
- Sisters attached to opposite poles
- Attachment of the same kinetochore to microtubules from both poles

## Explanation

Biorientation directs the two sisters towards opposite destinations and prepares segregation. Attaching both to the same pole does not achieve that geometry. Having attachments is insufficient; the pole of origin also matters.
