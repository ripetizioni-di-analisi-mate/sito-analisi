Which comparison correctly identifies what segregates in mitosis and meiosis I?

- Sisters in mitosis; homologues in meiosis I
- Sisters in both, with no differences
- Homologues in both, with sisters always associated
- Homologues in mitosis; sisters in meiosis I

## Explanation

Mitosis normally preserves ploidy by distributing sister copies to daughters. Meiosis I reduces chromosome sets by separating duplicated homologues. Meiosis II resembles mitosis more closely in sister separation but begins with already haploid cells.
