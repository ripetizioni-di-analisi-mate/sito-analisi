Which description normally applies to an ovulated human secondary oocyte?

- Metaphase II, n duplicated chromosomes, 2C
- G1, 2n chromosomes, 2C
- Completed meiosis, n chromosomes, 1C
- Prophase I, 2n chromosomes, 4C

## Explanation

Division I has separated homologues and made the cell haploid. Sisters remain associated because division II is arrested. DNA content is therefore 2C: haploid does not necessarily mean one DNA molecule per chromosome.
