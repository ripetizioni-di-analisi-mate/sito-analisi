In the Rb–E2F model, what follows from reduced inhibitory Rb binding to E2F?

- Direct repression of all E2F target genes
- Immediate separase activation through securin degradation
- Possible increased expression of S-phase genes
- Obligatory G1 arrest caused by stronger Rb–E2F binding

## Explanation

E2F regulates genes involved in S-phase entry and progression. Reducing inhibitory Rb binding can therefore increase their expression, whereas strengthening that binding would have the opposite effect. Separase acts later in segregation control and is not directly activated by E2F release.
