A short DNA-precursor pulse labels cells with DNA content between 2C and 4C. Which process is directly detected?

- DNA synthesis during S phase
- Chromatid duplication during G2
- Chromosome condensation during mitotic prophase
- Sister-chromatid separation in anaphase

## Explanation

The precursor enters newly synthesised DNA. Intermediate DNA content is consistent with ongoing replication. The label does not directly measure condensation or cytoplasmic division; those processes require different observations.
