After S, a diploid cell is 2n,4C. Why is it not automatically called 4n?

- The two chromatids have already been assigned to different cells
- The number of DNA molecules did not change
- C denotes chromosome number independently of replication
- Chromatids doubled, not homologous chromosome sets

## Explanation

Each homologue produced a sister copy retained in association before segregation. Ploidy describes homologous sets and remains two; C describes DNA amount and doubles. Using both symbols prevents conflating different counts.
