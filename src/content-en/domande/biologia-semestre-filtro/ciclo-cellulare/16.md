After DNA damage, p53 and p21 increase. Which causal chain describes possible arrest?

- p53 degrades p21 and directly activates every CDK
- p53 induces p21, which restrains cyclin–CDK complexes
- p21 stimulates CDK2 and accelerates S-phase entry
- p21 increases E2F-target transcription by inactivating Rb

## Explanation

p53 can induce p21 transcription; p21 inhibits selected CDK complexes and slows progression. This gives the damage response time but does not make p53 or p21 the enzymes that directly repair every lesion.
