After DNA damage, one cell temporarily arrests while another activates apoptosis. Which explanation fits p53 function?

- Outcome depends on damage, duration and cell state
- Arrest proves there was no damage
- Every p53 increase must immediately produce the same outcome
- p53 can regulate only p21 and no apoptosis-related genes

## Explanation

p53 participates in a network supporting arrest, repair, senescence or apoptosis. A shared regulator does not impose identical responses under different conditions. The case requires context rather than assigning p53 one obligatory outcome.
