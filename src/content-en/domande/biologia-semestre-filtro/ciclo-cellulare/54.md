Which structure supports close homologue pairing during prophase I?

- NDC80 complex
- APC/C complex
- Actin–myosin ring
- Synaptonemal complex

## Explanation

The synaptonemal complex organises close homologue pairing in prophase I. NDC80 instead forms the microtubule connection; APC/C regulates degradation of cell-cycle proteins; the contractile ring acts in cytokinesis. The connected partner and phase distinguish these complexes.
