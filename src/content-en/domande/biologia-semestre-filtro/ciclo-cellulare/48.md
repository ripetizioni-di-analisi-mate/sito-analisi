Arm cohesion is removed in meiosis I while centromeric cohesion is protected. What does this difference permit?

- Separation of all sisters before homologues
- Keeping bivalents intact until meiosis II is completed
- Homologue separation while sisters remain joined at the centromere
- Simultaneous separation of homologues and sisters in division I

## Explanation

Arm connections must be released to resolve the bivalent, but sisters must stay associated until meiosis II. Centromeric protection, involving shugoshin, separates the events in time. Removing all cohesion simultaneously would disrupt that sequence.
