A cell reversibly enters G0 while remaining metabolically active. Which conclusion is appropriate?

- It is arrested in G2 after completing replication
- It is quiescent, not necessarily dead
- It must be senescent and unable to re-enter the cycle
- It has begun irreversible apoptotic execution

## Explanation

G0 describes exit from the proliferative cycle. Some cell types can re-enter after appropriate signals. Quiescence does not mean absence of metabolism and is not universally equivalent to apoptosis, senescence or irreversible differentiation.
