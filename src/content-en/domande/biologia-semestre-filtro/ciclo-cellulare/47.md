Which sister-kinetochore arrangement distinguishes meiosis I from mitotic metaphase?

- In meiosis I sisters are oriented towards the same pole
- In meiosis I sisters biorient to opposite poles as in mitosis
- In meiosis I both homologues normally move towards the same pole
- In meiosis I centromeric cohesion must be removed before alignment

## Explanation

Division I must separate homologues while retaining sister association. Sister coorientation and protected centromeric cohesion help achieve this. In division II, sisters can instead biorient and separate.
