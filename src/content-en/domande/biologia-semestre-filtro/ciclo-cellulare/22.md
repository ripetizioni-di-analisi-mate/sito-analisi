In an experimental model, A resumes proliferation after repair; B remains viable in durable arrest with multiple senescence markers; C activates executioner caspases and forms apoptotic bodies. Which assignment is consistent?

- A apoptosis; B transient arrest; C senescence
- A senescence; B apoptosis; C transient arrest
- A transient arrest; B apoptosis; C senescence
- A transient arrest; B senescence; C apoptosis

## Explanation

A resumes proliferation after repair, so its arrest is transient. B remains viable but shows durable arrest with the specified markers, consistent with senescence. C activates executioner caspases and forms apoptotic bodies, supporting apoptosis. Viability, duration and markers distinguish the three outcomes.
