What identifies the kinetochore relative to the centromere?

- The DNA region on which the protein complex assembles
- The whole duplicated chromosome
- The complex joining chromatids along their arms
- A protein assembly for spindle attachment

## Explanation

The kinetochore is a protein complex assembled on the centromeric region and forms the microtubule interface. Centromere and kinetochore are therefore related but not identical. Cohesin maintains sister association; a chromatid instead comprises one chromosome copy and many associated structures.
