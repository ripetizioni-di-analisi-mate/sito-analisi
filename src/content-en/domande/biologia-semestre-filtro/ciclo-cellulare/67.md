A cell generates derivatives of all three embryonic germ layers. Which term describes this potential without itself implying development of a complete organism?

- Multipotency restricted to one tissue compartment
- Pluripotency
- Unipotency
- Automatically proven totipotency

## Explanation

Pluripotency includes generating derivatives of all three germ layers. Multipotency describes a more restricted range of fates, whereas unipotency concerns one differentiated lineage. Demonstrating derivatives of three germ layers is insufficient to establish every capacity needed to develop a complete organism.
