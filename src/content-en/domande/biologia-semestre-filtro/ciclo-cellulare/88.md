After severe damage, an inhibitor reduces some apoptotic markers. Which additional observation is needed to support lasting cellular recovery?

- A decrease in the same markers at the already-observed time point
- Viability and function maintained over time compared with appropriate controls
- Confirmation of the initial inhibitor concentration alone
- An image of chromatin before treatment alone

## Explanation

Reducing an execution marker does not establish long-term functional recovery. Damage may persist and other death outcomes may occur. Viability and function must be measured over time with appropriate controls. Repeating the immediate marker, confirming dose or observing chromatin before treatment does not test that recovery.
