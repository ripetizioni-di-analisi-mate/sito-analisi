In the high-risk HPV model, which viral-protein association is correct?

- E6: Rb pathway; E7: p53 pathway
- E6 and E7: both exclusively the p53 pathway
- E6: p53 pathway; E7: Rb pathway
- E6 and E7: both exclusively the Rb pathway

## Explanation

E6 and E7 can interfere with the p53 and Rb networks, respectively, weakening cellular controls. This illustrates a molecular mechanism and does not imply that an individual infection inevitably causes malignant transformation.
