MOMP denotes permeabilisation of which structure?

- Plasma membrane during any ion transport
- Mitochondrial inner membrane during normal ATP synthesis
- Nuclear envelope during every prophase
- Mitochondrial outer membrane

## Explanation

MOMP affects the outer membrane and can release intermembrane-space proteins. It is not equivalent to normal proton flow through ATP synthase in the inner membrane. Confusing them would conflate metabolism with death signalling.
