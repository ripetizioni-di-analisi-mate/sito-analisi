Which property distinguishes interpolar from kinetochore microtubules?

- They form the direct connection to each kinetochore
- They terminate exclusively at the cell cortex
- They overlap with microtubules from the opposite pole
- They overlap only with microtubules oriented in the same direction

## Explanation

Antiparallel overlap enables motor interactions contributing to spindle dynamics. This differs from direct kinetochore attachment. Functional overlap between fibres from opposite poles is antiparallel; cortical contact instead distinguishes astral microtubules.
