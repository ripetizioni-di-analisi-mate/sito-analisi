In an appropriate context, what combination is indicated by annexin-V positivity and membrane-impermeant dye negativity?

- Exposed phosphatidylserine with membrane still excluding the dye
- Unexposed phosphatidylserine with membrane permeable to the dye
- Exposed phosphatidylserine with membrane already permeable to the dye
- No phosphatidylserine exposure and an intact membrane

## Explanation

The signals measure different properties: phosphatidylserine exposure and dye access to the interior. Their combination is compatible with early apoptosis, but controls and other markers matter. It does not prove every molecular pathway step by itself.
