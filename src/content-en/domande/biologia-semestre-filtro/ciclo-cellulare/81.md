In the Fas model, which adaptor supports the initiator-caspase activation complex?

- APAF1
- BID
- FADD
- BCL-XL

## Explanation

FADD participates in the death-receptor complex and promotes recruitment of initiator procaspases, including procaspase 8. APAF1 instead belongs to the mitochondrial-pathway apoptosome. Distinguishing the complexes clarifies the two signal-entry routes.
