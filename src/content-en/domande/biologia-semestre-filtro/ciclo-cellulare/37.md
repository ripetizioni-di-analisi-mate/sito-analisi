After normal segregation, the actin–myosin ring fails to contract, leaving two nuclei in one cytoplasm. What is directly impaired?

- Chromosome condensation in prophase
- Sister-chromatid separation
- Cytokinesis
- Initial kinetochore capture

## Explanation

The question separates successful chromosome segregation from physical cytoplasmic separation. The contractile ring acts in the latter process. Binucleation therefore does not necessarily imply incorrect chromosome distribution.
