Meiosis I is normal; nondisjunction of one chromosome occurs in only one division II. What fraction of the four products is normal for that chromosome?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac34$
- $0$

## Explanation

The unaffected meiosis-II cell produces two normal products. The affected cell produces one $n+1$ and one $n-1$. The normal fraction is therefore $\dfrac{2}{4}=\dfrac12$. This depends on only one of the two meiosis-II cells being affected.
