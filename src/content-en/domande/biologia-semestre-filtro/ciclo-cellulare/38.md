In human mitotic anaphase, after centromere separation but before cytokinesis, how many chromosomes are counted in the whole normal cell?

- 23, all still duplicated
- 92, destined for two groups of 46
- 184, destined for four groups
- 46, destined for two groups of 23

## Explanation

Once sister centromeres separate, each former chromatid counts as a daughter chromosome. The whole-cell count is 92, while each pole receives 46. No new replication occurred; the chromosome-counting unit changes during separation.
