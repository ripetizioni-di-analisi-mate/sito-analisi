Securin binds separase before anaphase. What is the relevant regulatory consequence in the model?

- It limits premature cohesin cleavage
- It replaces cohesin as the structural connection between chromatids
- It directly accelerates cohesin cleavage
- It promotes separase degradation before anaphase

## Explanation

Securin restrains separase before the appropriate time. Its removal allows the protease to act on cohesin. Securin is not the structural connection between sisters; it regulates the enzyme that removes that connection.
