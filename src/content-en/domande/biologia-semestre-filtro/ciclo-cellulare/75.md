Which pair represents executioner caspases in the described apoptotic cascade?

- 3 and 9
- 8 and 9
- 7 and 8
- 3 and 7

## Explanation

Caspases 3 and 7 act as executioners and cleave many substrates. Caspases 8 and 9 instead have initiator roles in extrinsic and intrinsic models. The other pairs either mix at least one initiator with an executioner or contain two initiators.
