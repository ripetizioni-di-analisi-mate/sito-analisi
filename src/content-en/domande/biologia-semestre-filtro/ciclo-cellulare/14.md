Mutant Rb binds E2F and remains inhibitory despite active CDKs. Which prediction follows directly?

- CDK activation alone guarantees complete replication
- E2F must be free because CDK is present
- Increasing mitogens necessarily removes the mutant brake
- E2F may remain restrained despite upstream signalling

## Explanation

The variant interrupts the target’s response to its upstream regulator. Signalling can reach the CDK without relieving E2F inhibition. Activity in one part of a network does not prove completion of every downstream step.
