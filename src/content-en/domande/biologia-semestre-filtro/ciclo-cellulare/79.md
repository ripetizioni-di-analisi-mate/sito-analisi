Why does cytosolic cytochrome c have a different significance from intermembrane-space cytochrome c?

- Interactions must remain identical because the protein sequence has not changed
- The total protein amount must increase before release
- In the cytosol it can assemble an apoptosome alone without APAF1
- Location changes available partners and possible function

## Explanation

In mitochondria, cytochrome c participates in electron transport; in the cytosol it can interact with APAF1 and contribute to apoptosis. Functional change follows compartment and interaction changes without requiring a new protein sequence.
