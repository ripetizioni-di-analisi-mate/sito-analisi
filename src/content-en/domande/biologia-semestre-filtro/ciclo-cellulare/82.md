A ligand activates Fas and a FADD-containing complex. Which initiator caspase is directly associated with this pathway?

- Caspase 8
- Caspase 3
- Caspase 7
- Caspase 9

## Explanation

The extrinsic model can activate caspase 8 through the receptor complex. The mitochondrial pathway may subsequently amplify the signal in some contexts. The initiating step should not be replaced by the other pathway’s initiator.
