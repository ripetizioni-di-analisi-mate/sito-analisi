Two daughters with the same DNA receive different niche signals and adopt different fates. Which explanation does not require new mutations?

- Niche signals can affect only division rate, never the transcriptional programme
- Necessary loss of genes that are not expressed
- The same sequence necessarily imposes the same transcriptional state
- Different regulation of gene expression

## Explanation

Signals, contacts and position can change transcriptional programmes without changing DNA sequence. Niche context therefore contributes to maintenance or differentiation. The same genetic information does not mean identical activity of every gene.
