Phosphorylation activates protein A but inhibits protein B. Which conclusion is consistent?

- Phosphorylation must always inhibit every target
- Every phosphorylation must activate every protein
- The effect depends on the protein and modified site
- The direction of the response is independent of the modified site

## Explanation

The phosphate group can change interactions and conformations in different ways depending on structural context. For A, the new state favours the measured activity, whereas for B it reduces it. The data therefore show that the chemical name of a modification is insufficient to predict its functional outcome. The site and system studied must be known.
