A viral particle has an envelope. Which statement correctly describes its composition?

- A covering composed exclusively of capsid proteins
- A lipid envelope acquired from cellular membranes and containing viral proteins
- A peptidoglycan network replacing the capsid
- A membrane made only of lipids and necessarily lacking proteins

## Explanation

The envelope derives from host membranes during the viral cycle and contains viral proteins involved, for example, in recognising new cells. It is distinct from the protein capsid. Its lipid nature explains the sensitivity of many enveloped viruses to treatments that disrupt such membranes, without making it a bacterial wall or a cellular organelle.
