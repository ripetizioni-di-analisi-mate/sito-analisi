An enzyme is added to a reversible reaction while temperature and initial concentrations are maintained. Which quantity remains unchanged by catalysis alone?

- The time needed to approach equilibrium
- The initial rate under every condition
- The activation barrier of the catalysed pathway
- The free-energy difference between reactants and products

## Explanation

The enzyme provides a pathway with a lower barrier but does not change the initial and final energy states. Free-energy change and equilibrium therefore remain the same under the same conditions. Rate can increase and the time to approach equilibrium can decrease. Catalysis and thermodynamic favourability describe different aspects of the reaction.
