A test tube contains ATP and reactants for an unfavourable synthesis, but lacks a mechanism linking the two reactions. Which conclusion is correct?

- ATP presence removes the need for every intermediate or chemical transfer
- The mere presence of ATP does not guarantee coupling to the synthesis
- Independent reactions are already coupled merely because they share a solvent
- Every reaction present automatically becomes favourable

## Explanation

Coupling requires a mechanistic connection, such as a shared intermediate or an appropriate chemical transfer. Two simultaneous but independent reactions do not thereby form a coupled process. ATP availability is useful in many systems, but it does not replace the enzymes and steps that actually transfer energy to the conversion considered.
