A cell maintains internal pH within a range while external pH changes moderately. The process stops when ATP becomes unavailable. Which interpretation is most consistent?

- Stability may depend on regulated processes that consume energy
- A stable value proves that no ions cross the membrane
- ATP dependence proves that maintenance is entirely passive
- Recovery requires internal and external pH to coincide at every instant

## Explanation

Maintaining pH does not imply that molecules are immobile. Transport and reactions may compensate for the disturbance; ATP dependence suggests an energetic contribution to regulation. The finding alone does not identify a particular transporter. Further measurements would be needed for that, whereas active homeostasis is already consistent with the experiment.
