A reaction cleaves a bond by using a water molecule. What is this type of transformation called?

- Phosphorylation
- Oxidation
- Condensation
- Hydrolysis

## Explanation

In hydrolysis, water participates in bond cleavage and contributes to groups appearing in the products. In condensation, bond formation is instead accompanied by elimination of a small molecule. These descriptions concern the chemical transformation; determining rate and direction under cellular conditions also requires catalysis and energy balance.
