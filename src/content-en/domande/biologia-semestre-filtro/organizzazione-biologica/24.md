During phage infection, extracellular particle numbers initially remain low and then rise sharply together with cell lysis. Which sequence explains the finding?

- Exclusive prophage persistence without particle production
- Continuous release without lysis from the beginning of the experiment
- Intracellular production and assembly followed by release
- Autonomous multiplication of virions in cell-free medium

## Explanation

The lytic cycle includes an interval during which components and particles are produced inside cells. Lysis releases the progeny and causes the increase in particles measured outside. The finding does not require autonomous virion division in the medium: multiplication depends on the host and on assembly of components produced during infection.
