A membrane that allows free DNA through but prevents contact separates donors and recipients. Transfer stops; purified DNA does not transfer the trait. Which hypothesis calls for direct testing of contact machinery?

- Transformation
- Transduction
- Vertical transmission
- Conjugation

## Explanation

The results are consistent with contact-dependent transfer and therefore motivate testing conjugation. A further experiment could specifically inactivate the conjugative apparatus while preserving cell viability and contact. Stating this as a hypothesis avoids treating the barrier experiment alone as definitive molecular identification. The free-DNA control makes transformation less plausible under the tested conditions.
