Which process belongs predominantly to anabolism?

- Synthesis of a macromolecule from precursors with energy input
- Hydrolysis of a protein into fragments
- Breakdown of a reserve with energy recovery
- Cleavage of a polysaccharide into smaller units

## Explanation

Anabolism includes building processes that require energy and, in many pathways, reducing power. Macromolecule synthesis fits this function. The other options describe breakdown. The two categories are connected in cells because intermediates and energy obtained through catabolism can support biosynthesis.
