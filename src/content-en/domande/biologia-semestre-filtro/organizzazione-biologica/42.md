A bacterial cell replicates its chromosome and divides into two daughter cells that inherit it. How is this passage of information classified?

- Bacteriophage-mediated transduction
- Conjugation through contact between separate cells
- Transformation by free DNA outside the cell
- Vertical transmission

## Explanation

DNA passes from a mother cell to its descendants during division, so transmission is vertical. Horizontal mechanisms introduce material from other lineages through free DNA, contact or phage vectors. Chromosome replication is consistent with division and does not require any of those transfer processes to be assumed.
