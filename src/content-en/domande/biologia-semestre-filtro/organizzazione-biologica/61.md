Which interactions directly stabilise alpha helices and beta sheets in the basic model of protein structure?

- Interactions exclusively between side chains, without backbone involvement
- Disulfide bonds between cysteine residues
- Hydrogen bonds between peptide-backbone groups
- Peptide bonds between consecutive residues

## Explanation

Secondary structures depend on regular patterns of hydrogen bonding involving peptide-backbone groups. Side chains influence which conformations are possible, but they do not replace these bonds in the model. Peptide bonds maintain the covalent chain, while hydrogen bonds contribute to its local organisation.
