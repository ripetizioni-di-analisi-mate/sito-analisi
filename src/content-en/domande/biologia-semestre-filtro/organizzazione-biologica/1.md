8 units of a substance enter a cell per minute and 3 are consumed. Assuming constant flows and no outflow, how much does the internal amount increase in 8 minutes?

- 64 units
- 40 units
- 24 units
- 88 units

## Explanation

The net balance per minute is inflow minus consumption, so $8-3=5$ units. Over 8 minutes, $5\cdot8=40$ units accumulate. Multiplying only inflow by time would ignore consumption; adding the flows would incorrectly count the substance used as another inflow. The conclusion requires the flows to remain constant.
