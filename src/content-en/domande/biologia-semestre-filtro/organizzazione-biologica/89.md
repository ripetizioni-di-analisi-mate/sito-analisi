A small RNA reduces expression of a target without being translated. Which role is consistent with a miRNA?

- Regulation of expression through interaction with target RNA
- Role as a translated template for a new polypeptide chain
- Obligatory structural function in the large ribosomal subunit
- Amino-acid carriage through an anticodon

## Explanation

miRNAs are noncoding RNAs that participate in regulatory complexes and can reduce expression of specific targets. Their function depends on recognising RNA without requiring translation into proteins. This case shows why a functional RNA is not necessarily a new messenger for protein synthesis.
