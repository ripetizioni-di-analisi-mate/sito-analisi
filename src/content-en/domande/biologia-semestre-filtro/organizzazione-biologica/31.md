An aerobic bacterium consumes oxygen and produces ATP by oxidative phosphorylation despite lacking mitochondria. Where can its respiratory chain be located?

- In the cytosol, as an entirely soluble system
- In the plasma membrane
- In the peptidoglycan network
- In the polysaccharide capsule

## Explanation

The bacterial plasma membrane can contain systems that transfer electrons and contribute to ATP production. Mitochondria are eukaryotic compartments, whereas in a bacterium the required separation can be provided by the cell membrane. Absence of an organelle therefore does not imply absence of the biochemical process carried out by analogous molecular systems.
