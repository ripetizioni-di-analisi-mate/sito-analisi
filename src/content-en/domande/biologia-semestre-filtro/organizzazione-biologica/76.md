Which bond forms the covalent backbone of a DNA strand by joining consecutive nucleotides?

- Phosphodiester bond
- N-glycosidic bond between base and sugar
- Hydrogen bond between complementary bases
- Base-stacking interaction

## Explanation

Phosphodiester bonds join consecutive nucleotides along the sugar–phosphate backbone. An N-glycosidic bond instead joins a base to its sugar within one nucleotide. Base pairing and stacking contribute to double-helix structure but do not replace the covalent linkage between nucleotides of the same strand.
