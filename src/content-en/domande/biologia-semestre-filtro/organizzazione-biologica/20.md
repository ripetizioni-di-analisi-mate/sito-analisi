Comparing ribosomal RNAs helps reconstruct relatedness because these molecules provide which combination of features?

- Absence of conserved regions, preventing recognition of homologous sequences
- Sequences determined solely by environment and unrelated to heredity
- Identical sequences in every group with no informative differences
- A shared function and comparable sequences that vary during evolution

## Explanation

The molecules compared must be recognisable as homologous and contain informative differences. Ribosomal RNAs perform a conserved function, but their sequences can diverge among evolutionary lineages. A sequence identical in every group would not distinguish different branches. Analysis requires appropriate comparisons and is not equivalent to morphological resemblance alone.
