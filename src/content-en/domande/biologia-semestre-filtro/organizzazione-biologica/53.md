Glycogen and cellulose both contain glucose units. Which difference can explain their different functions and digestibility?

- Absence of covalent bonds between glucose units in glycogen
- Peptide bonds between glucose units in cellulose
- Bond type and chain organisation
- Identical numbers of glucose units always making properties identical

## Explanation

The same unit can be linked in different ways. Bond configuration and branching affect shape, properties and enzyme recognition. Glycogen is a branched reserve, while cellulose has a structural role. Knowing only the monomer is therefore insufficient to predict all polymer properties.
