Two cultures with the same cell count receive the same purified DNA. Only the competent culture acquires the trait. Which property differs directly in the experimental model?

- Ability to inherit its own chromosome during division
- Ability to take up free DNA under the conditions considered
- Ability to transfer a plasmid through contact
- Ability to receive DNA through a transducing phage

## Explanation

Competence denotes the ability to take up free DNA using appropriate cellular systems. With DNA dose and cell count controlled, the difference is consistent with this transformation requirement. Conjugation and transduction require different mechanisms and are not established by the result. A complete experimental conclusion also checks viability and maintenance of acquired DNA.
