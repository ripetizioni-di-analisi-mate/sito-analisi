An animal virus acquires an envelope while passing through a cellular membrane containing its glycoproteins. Which mode of exit is described?

- Budding
- Envelope fusion during host entry
- Endocytosis that internalises the particle
- Lysis without membrane acquisition

## Explanation

During budding, the particle becomes surrounded by a portion of membrane containing viral proteins. This explains the lipid origin of the envelope. Endocytosis and fusion during entry instead describe routes into the host. Lysis without membrane acquisition would not explain the observed envelope. Budding nevertheless does not establish that infection causes no harm.
