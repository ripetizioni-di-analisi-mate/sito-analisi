Bacterial DNA is transferred to a recipient inside purified bacteriophage particles. Which process is described?

- Transduction
- Transformation by unprotected free DNA
- Vertical chromosome transmission during division
- Conjugation through direct donor–recipient contact

## Explanation

The DNA vehicle is a bacteriophage, which identifies transduction. Transformation instead requires uptake of free DNA by competent cells; conjugation requires transfer machinery and contact. Vertical transmission accompanies descent from a mother cell. The same new trait may arise through different routes, distinguished by their mechanism.
