In a rooted tree, A and B share node X; C diverges from their ancestor at the older node Y. Which relationship follows from the topology?

- C is necessarily less evolved
- The horizontal distance between tips determines relatedness
- A descends from present-day organism B
- A and B are more closely related to each other

## Explanation

Relatedness is inferred from the most recent common ancestor. A and B share X, which is later than Y, whereas their common ancestor with C is Y. Tips represent the lineages considered, so present-day B should not be identified as the ancestor of A. Rotating branches preserves this relationship even if the graphical distance between tips changes.
