Which pair of activities can reversibly regulate protein acetylation?

- Protease and peptidase
- Ubiquitin ligase and deubiquitinase
- Acetyltransferase and deacetylase
- Kinase and phosphatase

## Explanation

An acetyltransferase transfers an acetyl group, while a deacetylase can remove it. Their opposing activities allow the modification state to change. Kinases and phosphatases concern phosphate, whereas ligases and deubiquitinases concern ubiquitin. Proteases and peptidases cleave peptide bonds rather than forming a pair that adds and removes acetyl groups.
