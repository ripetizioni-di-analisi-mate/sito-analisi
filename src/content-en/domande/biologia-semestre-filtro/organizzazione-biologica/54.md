A molecule has a polar head and nonpolar hydrocarbon regions. Which organisation in water is consistent with a membrane phospholipid?

- Heads facing water and nonpolar regions associated inside the bilayer
- All polar heads buried and nonpolar tails exposed to water
- Identical affinity for water in heads and tails
- Stable random distribution of tails in water without association

## Explanation

The molecule is amphipathic because it contains regions with different affinities for water. In a bilayer, heads interact with aqueous compartments while tails associate internally. This arrangement reduces exposure of nonpolar regions to water. Reversing heads and tails or assigning them identical affinity would erase the chemical difference explaining the organisation.
