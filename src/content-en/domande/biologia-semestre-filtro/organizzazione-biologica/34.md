An experimental agent specifically hydrolyses peptidoglycan. Which cellular target does it directly affect?

- Outer-membrane lipopolysaccharide as the sole substrate
- Chromosomal phosphodiester bonds
- The plasma membrane’s phospholipid bilayer
- The bacterial cell-wall network

## Explanation

Peptidoglycan forms a network of sugar chains linked through peptide components in the walls of many bacteria. Its hydrolysis directly changes that structure and may weaken osmotic resistance. Nucleic acids and lipids are different molecular classes, so specific action on peptidoglycan does not automatically imply their degradation.
