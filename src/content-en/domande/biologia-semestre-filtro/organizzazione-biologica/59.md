A monomeric enzyme brings together three amino acids that are distant in sequence at its active site. Which structural level mainly explains their proximity in space?

- Secondary structure considered without overall folding
- Tertiary structure
- Quaternary structure
- Primary structure

## Explanation

Overall folding of one chain brings together regions distant in sequence and defines tertiary structure. Primary structure gives residue order; secondary structure concerns local motifs and alone does not describe the entire site arrangement. Because the enzyme is monomeric, no association of multiple chains, which defines quaternary structure, is required.
