An isolated single DNA strand contains 40% adenine. Which statement cannot be inferred without further information?

- That thymine on the same strand must also be 40%
- That the measured value concerns a single strand
- That the remaining bases together account for 60%
- That the sample contains adenine

## Explanation

Equality between A and T and between G and C follows from pairing and applies to the total in double-stranded DNA. An isolated strand may have an asymmetric composition. Knowing that A is 40% leaves 60% for other bases without fixing how much of that remainder is thymine. Specifying the sample is therefore decisive.
