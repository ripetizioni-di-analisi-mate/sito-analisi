A DNA fragment contains 12 A–T pairs and 8 G–C pairs. How many hydrogen bonds directly involve these canonical pairs?

- 48
- 56
- 40
- 60

## Explanation

Each A–T pair contributes 2 hydrogen bonds and each G–C pair contributes 3. The count is therefore $12\cdot2+8\cdot3=24+24=48$. Pairs, rather than isolated bases, are counted: doubling again would count the same bonds twice. The result is not a complete calculation of double-helix stability energy.
