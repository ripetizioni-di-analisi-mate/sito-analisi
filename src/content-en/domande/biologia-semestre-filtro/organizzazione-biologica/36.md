Loss of flagella reduces movement in a bacterial culture without immediately blocking protein synthesis. Which function is directly linked to the observed defect?

- Polypeptide-chain synthesis
- Crystal-violet retention during Gram staining
- Motility
- Conjugative DNA transfer

## Explanation

Flagella are structures involved in motility in many species. The measured change is consistent with their loss, whereas translation depends on ribosomes and other protein-synthesis factors. Linking the defect to the relevant structure avoids assigning every cellular function to flagella. The experiment does not imply that all bacteria possess flagella.
