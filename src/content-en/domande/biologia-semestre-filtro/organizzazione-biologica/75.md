Which pair contains two purines found in nucleic acids?

- Thymine and uracil
- Cytosine and thymine
- Uracil and cytosine
- Adenine and guanine

## Explanation

Adenine and guanine are purines; cytosine, thymine and uracil are pyrimidines. The distinction depends on base structure and remains valid whether the base is part of a free nucleotide or a nucleic acid. In ordinary DNA, each canonical base pair joins a purine with a pyrimidine.
