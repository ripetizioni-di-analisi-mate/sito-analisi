In a eukaryotic cell, a confined acidic environment promotes material degradation without acidifying the whole cytosol to the same extent. Which organisational principle is illustrated?

- Free membrane permeability to every substance and ion
- Instantaneous pH equilibrium across every cellular space
- Complete absence of transport between organelles and cytosol
- Compartmentalisation

## Explanation

Membranes allow local environments with different compositions and pH while permitting regulated transport. The lysosome provides an example of an acidic compartment suited to particular degradative activities. Spatial separation does not imply complete isolation: material trafficking and transport regulation are needed for the system to function.
