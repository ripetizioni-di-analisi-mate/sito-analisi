Which statement about cholesterol is consistent with its cellular functions?

- It is a phospholipid with two tails and a phosphate head
- It is a storage polysaccharide equivalent to glycogen
- It contributes to animal membranes and is a precursor of signalling molecules
- It is a triacylglycerol made of glycerol and three fatty acids

## Explanation

Cholesterol belongs to the sterols and contributes to the properties of animal membranes. Its structure also underlies several signalling molecules. It is neither a triacylglycerol nor a phospholipid with two tails and a phosphate head. Glycogen is instead a polysaccharide: a general storage function does not make different molecules structurally identical.
