A cell expresses the receptor recognised by a virus but produces no new virions. Which conclusion is justified?

- The block must affect only exit of already assembled virions
- The receptor may enable an initial step without guaranteeing the entire cycle
- The receptor alone guarantees expression, assembly and release
- Replication of the viral genome must already be complete

## Explanation

Receptor recognition contributes to attachment or entry. Replication, expression, assembly and exit may require additional factors that are absent or blocked in the cell considered. Receptor availability is therefore insufficient to infer productive infection. The final observation must be interpreted by distinguishing the different stages of the cycle.
