An RNA molecule catalyses a purified reaction with no proteins present. Which conclusion is supported?

- All RNAs have the same catalytic activity
- The RNA must have been translated before every catalytic event despite the absence of proteins
- The result proves that every protein lacks catalytic activity
- Catalytic RNAs called ribozymes exist

## Explanation

Activity attributed to purified RNA shows that an RNA molecule can contribute directly to catalysis. The term ribozyme identifies this case. Many enzymes are proteins, but catalytic RNAs make it false that every biological catalyst must be a protein. The experiment does not imply that every RNA is catalytic.
