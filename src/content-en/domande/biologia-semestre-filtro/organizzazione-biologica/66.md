A treatment prevents selective recognition of a target by its E3 ligase. Which process is directly affected?

- Acetyl-group removal by a deacetylase
- Target ubiquitination
- Direct proteolysis of the target chain by the ligase
- Phosphate removal by a phosphatase

## Explanation

In the ubiquitin system, E3 ligases contribute to target selection and ubiquitin transfer. Blocking this interaction can therefore reduce target modification. The final effect depends on that ubiquitination’s role. Phosphatases and deacetylases act on other modifications; the ligase is not a protease directly hydrolysing the target chain.
