A sample contains a pentose sugar with a hydroxyl group at position 2′. Which nucleic acid contains it in the ordinary cellular model?

- Only free nucleosides, whereas RNA would always lose that hydroxyl group
- Both DNA and RNA, because their sugars would be identical at position 2′
- DNA containing deoxyribose
- RNA

## Explanation

RNA ribose has a hydroxyl group at position 2′. DNA deoxyribose instead has hydrogen in place of that hydroxyl group. The difference contributes to distinct chemical properties and remains in the sugars of nucleotides incorporated into RNA. Polymer formation does not systematically remove the 2′ hydroxyl as the free-nucleoside-only alternative would suggest.
