A recipient acquires a plasmid carrying a gene that inactivates antibiotic X. Which conclusion is supported without additional assumptions?

- Resistance is guaranteed even if the gene is never expressed
- It necessarily becomes resistant to all antibiotics
- It may acquire resistance to X if the gene is maintained and expressed
- The gene can function only if the entire plasmid replaces the chromosome

## Explanation

The acquired trait depends on gene function, maintenance and expression in the recipient. Inactivating X may support resistance to X under appropriate conditions without implying resistance to all antibiotics. A plasmid can be maintained as an extrachromosomal element: it need not replace the chromosome to express its gene.
