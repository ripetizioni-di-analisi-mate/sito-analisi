After an enzyme acts, a protein loses a phosphate group by hydrolysis. Which activity is described?

- Deubiquitinase
- Deacetylase
- Kinase
- Phosphatase

## Explanation

A phosphatase catalyses hydrolytic removal of phosphate groups. Here it reduces phosphorylation without by itself determining whether activity increases or decreases. A kinase adds phosphate, whereas deacetylases and deubiquitinases remove different groups. Identifying the group lost distinguishes these post-translational modifications.
