A bacterium without a capsule grows and divides in an appropriate medium. What follows?

- A capsule is not a universal component required by every bacterium
- Capsules have no function in any environment
- The preserved membrane necessarily performs every capsule function
- A capsule is obligatory for chromosome replication in every medium

## Explanation

Capsules have protective or adhesive roles in many bacteria but are not obligatory in every species. The observation shows that this culture can grow without a capsule under the stated conditions. It does not show that capsules are useless in every environment. Capsule and plasma membrane are distinct structures, so presence of the latter does not contradict absence of the former.
