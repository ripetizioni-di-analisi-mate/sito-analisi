A kinase uses ATP to modify a residue in a target protein. Which group does it transfer in this case?

- A ubiquitin molecule
- A phosphate group
- An acetyl group
- An adenylyl group

## Explanation

Kinases catalyse phosphate-group transfer, often from ATP to a substrate. The modification may change the protein’s charges, conformation or interactions. Its functional effect depends on the target and site, so universal activation cannot be inferred. Protein modification is distinct from synthesis of a new chain.
