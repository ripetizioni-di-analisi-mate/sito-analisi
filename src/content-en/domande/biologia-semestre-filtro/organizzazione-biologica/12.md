A DNA strand is 5′-AGTC-3′. What is the complementary strand also written from 5′ to 3′?

- 5′-GACT-3′
- 5′-CUGA-3′
- 5′-TCAG-3′
- 5′-AGTC-3′

## Explanation

Pairing the bases in the same spatial arrangement gives 3′-TCAG-5′. To rewrite that strand from 5′ to 3′, it must be read in the opposite direction, giving 5′-GACT-3′. TCAG labelled 5′ to 3′ would not respect antiparallel orientation. Uracil is also not the base expected in the ordinary DNA described in the exercise.
