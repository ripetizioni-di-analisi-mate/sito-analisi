A group of cells adheres stably, communicates and coordinates proliferation and specialisation. Which evolutionary transition can this combination support?

- Multicellular organisation
- Complete independence of cells from every reciprocal signal
- Necessarily identical and uncontrolled proliferation of every cell
- Aggregation without any coordination as the only interpretation

## Explanation

Adhesion keeps cells associated, communication coordinates responses, and regulation of proliferation and differentiation supports distributed functions. These properties can contribute to multicellularity. Physical proximity alone, without coordination, would be weaker evidence. Complex organisation depends on cooperation among the processes described while retaining individually bounded cells.
