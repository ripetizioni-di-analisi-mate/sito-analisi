Competent bacteria acquire a trait from purified DNA. DNase added before contact with the cells prevents acquisition. Which mechanism is directly supported?

- Transformation
- Capsid-protected transduction
- Binary fission
- Conjugation

## Explanation

The transferred material is free DNA, and its extracellular degradation prevents the recipient from changing. These findings correspond to transformation of competent cells. Conjugation would require a donor and a contact apparatus; in transduction, DNA is carried by phage particles. Cell division alone does not explain acquisition from purified DNA.
