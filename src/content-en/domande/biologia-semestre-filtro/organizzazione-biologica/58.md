A polypeptide chain contains two regions that fold into recognisable units with different functions. Which term describes these regions?

- Subunits consisting of distinct polypeptide chains
- Quaternary structures
- Protein domains
- Individual secondary-structure elements

## Explanation

A domain is a region with recognisable structural organisation, often associated with a function; one chain may contain several. A subunit is instead a separate chain in a complex, whose association defines quaternary structure. A domain can contain several secondary elements, such as helices and sheets, organised into a larger unit.
