A protein receives carbohydrate groups during maturation. Which term directly identifies this modification?

- Glycosylation
- Ubiquitination
- Acetylation
- Lipidation

## Explanation

Glycosylation adds carbohydrate groups that may affect protein folding, stability or recognition. Lipidation adds lipid components, acetylation adds acetyl groups, and ubiquitination adds ubiquitin. The nature of the group attached to the target therefore distinguishes these modifications, even when their functional effects can overlap.
