Which pair belongs to the endomembrane system and displays structural continuity?

- Nuclear envelope and endoplasmic reticulum
- Golgi apparatus and lysosomes
- Endoplasmic reticulum and Golgi apparatus
- Lysosomes and plasma membrane

## Explanation

The outer nuclear membrane is continuous with the endoplasmic reticulum. The other pairs participate in membrane trafficking but do not normally form one continuously connected enclosure. Vesicular transport links distinct compartments and is not equivalent to the structural continuity between endoplasmic reticulum and nuclear envelope.
