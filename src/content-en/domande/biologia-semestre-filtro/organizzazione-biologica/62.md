A linear, unbranched chain contains 31 amino acids. How many peptide bonds link consecutive residues?

- 62
- 31
- 32
- 30

## Explanation

The first residue requires no link to a preceding residue. Each of the following 30 adds one bond to the chain, so the total is $31-1=30$. This relation applies to an open linear chain as specified. Closing the chain into a cycle would change the count, but that is not part of the exercise.
