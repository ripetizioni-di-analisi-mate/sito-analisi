A bacterium has an outer membrane containing lipopolysaccharide and a thin peptidoglycan layer. Which organisation is consistent with these findings?

- A bacterial cell completely lacking a wall
- An archaeal wall lacking bacterial peptidoglycan
- A Gram-negative-type envelope
- A typical Gram-positive envelope with a thick wall

## Explanation

The combination of an outer membrane containing lipopolysaccharide and thin peptidoglycan characterises a Gram-negative envelope. Peptidoglycan lies between plasma and outer membranes. In the typical Gram-positive model the layer is thick and that outer membrane is absent. An archaeal wall instead differs in composition from bacterial peptidoglycan; complete wall absence would contradict the observation.
