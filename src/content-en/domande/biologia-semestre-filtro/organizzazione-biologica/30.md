An inhibitor prevents only fusion between a viral envelope and a cellular membrane. Which step is directly affected in a virus using this route?

- Capsid assembly through direct cleavage of capsid proteins
- Entry of viral contents into the cell
- mRNA translation through direct ribosome inhibition
- Viral DNA synthesis through direct polymerase inhibition

## Explanation

Fusion joins the viral envelope with a host membrane, allowing viral contents to access the appropriate compartment. If the virus depends on that route, blocking fusion can prevent entry. Not all viruses use the same entry mechanism; the effect should not automatically be extended to non-enveloped particles or alternative routes.
