The RNA of a positive-sense single-stranded virus can be used directly by ribosomes after uncoating. Which property explains this possibility?

- It is complementary to mRNA and must always be copied before initial translation
- It must first be converted into chromosomally integrated DNA to become translatable
- Its sense is compatible with mRNA
- It must obligatorily undergo nuclear splicing to become readable

## Explanation

Positive sense means that the sequence can function as mRNA and allow initial synthesis of viral proteins. This does not mean that new genomes are produced without polymerases: RNA replication requires appropriate enzymes and often a complementary intermediate. Initial translation and genome replication are two different operations.
