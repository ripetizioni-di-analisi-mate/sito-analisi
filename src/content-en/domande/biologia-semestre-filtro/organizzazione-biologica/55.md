Which description corresponds to a triacylglycerol?

- Glycerol linked to two fatty acids and a phosphate-containing head
- A sterol structure with four fused rings
- Glycerol linked to three phosphate groups without fatty acids
- Glycerol esterified with three fatty acids

## Explanation

The name indicates three fatty acids attached to glycerol through ester bonds. This structure suits lipid storage. Glycerol with two fatty acids and a phosphate-containing head instead matches a glycerophospholipid model. Sterols have a ring system: they too are lipids, but their architecture differs.
