A conversion with $\Delta G=+40\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}$ is coupled to two reactions, each with $\Delta G=-25\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}$. What is the balance, assuming the stated ratio?

- $+90\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}$
- $-65\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}$
- $+15\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}$

## Explanation

The coupling ratio requires two favourable contributions. The balance is $40+2(-25)=40-50=-10\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}$. Counting only one contribution would give $+15$ but would ignore the stated stoichiometry. The negative sign concerns the overall process under the described conditions and does not remove the need for a mechanism that implements coupling.
