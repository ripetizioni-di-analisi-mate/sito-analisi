Which activity defines reverse transcription in the retroviral cycle?

- Synthesis of RNA using DNA as a template
- Synthesis of DNA using only DNA as a template
- Insertion of proviral DNA into a chromosome without synthesis from RNA
- Synthesis of DNA using RNA as a template

## Explanation

Reverse transcriptase copies information from RNA into DNA. The resulting DNA may then be integrated in a separate step involving integrase. Ordinary transcription instead produces RNA from DNA, while replication copies DNA from DNA. Correctly identifying template and product distinguishes these processes and helps locate an experimental block in the cycle.
