A temperate phage persists as integrated DNA and is inherited by daughter cells without continuous virion production. What is this situation called?

- The eclipse phase of a lytic cycle
- Chronic infection with continuous particle release
- Generalised transduction
- Lysogeny

## Explanation

The phage genome is maintained as a prophage and replicated with the host genome: this inherited persistence describes lysogeny. The eclipse phase is instead an early stage of lytic production; transduction transfers bacterial DNA through phages. Continuous release is excluded by the findings. Later induction may initiate a lytic cycle without being required at every division.
