An RNA contributes to ribosome structure and catalysis in protein synthesis. Which category does it belong to?

- rRNA
- tRNA
- mRNA
- miRNA

## Explanation

Ribosomal RNAs are structural and functional ribosome components; in the large subunit, RNA contributes to catalysis of peptide-bond formation. tRNA instead carries an amino acid and interacts with a codon. Being RNA alone does not determine a molecule’s function: its category and structure provide the required information.
