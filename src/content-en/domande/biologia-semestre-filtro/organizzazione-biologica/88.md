An intermediate obtained by breaking down a nutrient is used to synthesise an amino acid. Which relationship between metabolic pathways does this show?

- Catabolism can supply precursors to anabolism
- A catabolic intermediate must always be discarded without reuse
- Catabolic origin makes the compound impossible to use in biosynthesis
- Catabolism and anabolism never share intermediates

## Explanation

Metabolic pathways form a network. A breakdown process can make available both energy and molecular skeletons for biosynthesis. Classification into catabolism and anabolism distinguishes the predominant role without rigidly separating every intermediate. The case directly connects a breakdown pathway with a building pathway.
