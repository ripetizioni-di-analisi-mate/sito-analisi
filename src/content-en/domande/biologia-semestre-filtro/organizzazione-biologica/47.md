Which observation provides particularly informative evidence for the bacterial origin of mitochondria?

- Import of proteins synthesised in the cytosol, considered alone
- Size resemblance between organelles and bacteria alone
- The phylogenetic relationship between mitochondrial and bacterial genes
- Generic membrane presence, also shared with other compartments

## Explanation

Sequence comparisons can reconstruct specific evolutionary relationships and support descent from a bacterial ancestor. Small size or the generic presence of membranes alone are too widespread to support that conclusion. Endosymbiosis is supported by several consistent lines of evidence, including organellar genomes, translation and molecular relationships.
