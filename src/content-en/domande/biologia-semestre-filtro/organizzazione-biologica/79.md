Which RNA carries an amino acid and participates in codon recognition during translation?

- rRNA
- mRNA
- tRNA
- snRNA

## Explanation

A tRNA connects amino acid carriage with codon recognition through its anticodon. Correct association between tRNA and amino acid depends on dedicated enzyme systems. mRNA instead provides the sequence to be read, while rRNAs participate in the ribosome. These are distinct roles performed by molecules in the same general class.
