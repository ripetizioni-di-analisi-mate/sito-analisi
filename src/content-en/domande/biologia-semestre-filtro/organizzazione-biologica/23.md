Which pair of components is present in the general model of a virion, whether enveloped or non-enveloped?

- Capsid and autonomous translation machinery
- Both DNA and RNA obligatorily forming the genome of the same particle
- Genome and an obligatory envelope
- Genome and capsid

## Explanation

The general model includes a genome protected by a protein capsid. An envelope is an additional component present only in some families, so it is not part of every virion model. Translation depends on host cellular machinery. A viral genome may be DNA or RNA: both classes are not required to form the genome of the same particle.
