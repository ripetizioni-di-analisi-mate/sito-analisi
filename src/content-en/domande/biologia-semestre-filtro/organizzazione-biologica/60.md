A functional complex consists of four associated polypeptide chains. Which level describes their organisation relative to one another?

- Only the local arrangement of alpha helices along one chain
- Quaternary structure
- Only the residue sequence of one chain
- Only tertiary folding of an isolated subunit

## Explanation

Quaternary structure concerns association between polypeptide subunits. Each chain also has its own sequence and folding, so the levels refer to different aspects of the same complex. The term quaternary does not mean that four chains must always be present: it also applies to associations with other numbers of subunits.
