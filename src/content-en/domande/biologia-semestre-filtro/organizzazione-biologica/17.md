Which observation would most directly distinguish a growing cell culture from isolated virions in a cell-free medium?

- Presence of structural proteins
- Synthesis of new proteins using the cells’ own ribosomes
- Presence of nucleic acids
- Possibility of heritable variation

## Explanation

Cells possess ribosomes with which they synthesise proteins. Isolated virions instead depend on cellular machinery to produce new proteins and particles. Both can contain nucleic acids and proteins, and viruses can evolve. These properties alone therefore do not distinguish the two samples as directly as cellular translation activity does.
