A protein acquires ubiquitin, but the experiment measures neither the type of modification nor the protein’s fate. Which conclusion would be excessive?

- The protein will certainly be degraded immediately
- Ubiquitin has been covalently attached to the protein
- Ubiquitin linkage type can affect signal meaning
- Measuring protein fate can clarify the modification’s function

## Explanation

Ubiquitin can participate in different signals depending on the number, linkages and context of attached groups. Some modifications promote proteasomal degradation, while others serve different functions. Without information about ubiquitination type and target fate, certain immediate degradation exceeds the available evidence. The modification nevertheless remains an observable property of the protein.
