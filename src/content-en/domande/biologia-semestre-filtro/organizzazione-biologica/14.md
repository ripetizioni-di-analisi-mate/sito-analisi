Two reactions are coupled one to one. Under the stated conditions, their free-energy changes are $+14$ and $-20\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}$. What is the overall balance?

- $-6\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}$
- $-34\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}$
- $+34\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}$
- $+6\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}$

## Explanation

Free-energy changes add with their signs in the stated stoichiometric ratio. Thus $\Delta G_{\mathrm{tot}}=14-20=-6\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}$. The negative value makes the coupled process favourable under the stated conditions but does not determine its rate. Adding absolute values would lose information about the energetic direction of the two contributions.
