Two cells from the same individual have essentially the same genome, but one produces large amounts of a protein that is almost absent from the other. Which explanation is consistent with differentiation?

- Obligatory alteration of the entire gene sequence for every expression difference
- Different programmes of gene expression
- Necessary gene loss in every cell with low expression
- Obligatorily identical production of every protein because the genome is shared

## Explanation

Possessing a gene does not mean producing the same amount of its product in every cell. Expression regulation and molecular stability allow different functions from largely shared genetic information. The finding is therefore consistent with differentiation. It does not require gene loss or an obligatory sequence change whenever protein abundance changes.
