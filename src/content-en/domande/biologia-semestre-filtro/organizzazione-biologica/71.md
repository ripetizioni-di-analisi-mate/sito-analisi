A reaction is thermodynamically favourable but very slow. Which change can accelerate it without changing its equilibrium?

- Reducing reactant availability while keeping other conditions equal
- Adding an appropriate catalyst
- Increasing the forward activation barrier
- Stabilising products by changing their free energy

## Explanation

An appropriate catalyst provides a lower-barrier pathway without changing the energies of initial and final states. Stabilising products would instead change the thermodynamic balance. Raising the barrier hinders conversion, while reducing reactants does not provide an easier pathway. Thermodynamic favourability and rate remain distinct kinds of information.
