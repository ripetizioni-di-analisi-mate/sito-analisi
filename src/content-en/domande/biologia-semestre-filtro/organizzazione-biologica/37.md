A prokaryote has membrane lipids with ether bonds and a wall lacking typical bacterial peptidoglycan. Which group is supported by these features?

- Gram-negative bacteria identified by an outer membrane containing lipopolysaccharide
- Gram-positive bacteria identified by thick peptidoglycan
- Archaea
- Eukaryotes identified by a nuclear envelope

## Explanation

Ether-linked lipids and absence of typical bacterial peptidoglycan are consistent with archaea. Prokaryotic organisation is shared with bacteria but does not erase molecular differences between the groups. Classification does not require the organism to live in an extreme environment: many archaea occupy ordinary environments.
