A recipient takes up free DNA. Which observation would best distinguish heritable transformation from merely transient entry?

- Recipient growth in a medium that does not require the new trait
- Persistence of the acquired DNA and trait in descendants after several divisions without renewed exposure
- Persistence of free DNA in the culture medium
- Detection of DNA in the recipient immediately after entry

## Explanation

Initial entry does not establish DNA maintenance or heritability of the trait. Following both DNA and trait over several divisions without renewed exposure tests persistence in descendants. DNA remaining in the medium or growth under nonselective conditions does not demonstrate that transmission. Integration or appropriate replication can enable maintenance.
