Why does the endosymbiotic origin of mitochondria not automatically show that the nucleus also derives from an engulfed bacterium?

- Every cellular compartment necessarily retains an autonomous bacterial genome
- Two membranes always require the same bacterial ancestor
- Evidence specific to one organelle cannot be extended without testing to every other organelle
- Every form of compartmentalisation can be explained only by bacterial engulfment

## Explanation

An evolutionary reconstruction requires evidence relevant to the structure considered. The bacterial origin of mitochondria is supported by molecular relationships and other specific findings. Formation of the nucleus and endomembranes involves additional hypotheses and evidence. Generic similarities between compartments therefore do not justify replacing investigation with automatic extension of the same explanation.
