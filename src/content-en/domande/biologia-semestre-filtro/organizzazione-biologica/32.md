Under the microscope, a cell has DNA in a cytoplasmic region without a nuclear envelope. What is this region called in the prokaryotic model?

- Nucleoid
- Plasmid
- Nucleolus
- Nucleus

## Explanation

The nucleoid is the region containing the prokaryotic chromosome without being enclosed by a nuclear envelope. DNA can nevertheless be organised and associated with proteins. Absence of an envelope does not mean that every component is randomly dispersed. Nucleus and nucleolus refer to other levels of organisation and are not synonyms for nucleoid.
