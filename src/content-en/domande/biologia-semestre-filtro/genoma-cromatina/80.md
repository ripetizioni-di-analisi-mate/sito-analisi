A chromosome region is highly compact in one cell type and accessible in another, with unchanged sequence. Which category is consistent with this reversibility?

- Constitutive heterochromatin with invariant compaction across the compared cell types.
- Facultative heterochromatin.
- Euchromatin remaining accessible in both cell types.
- A compaction state determined exclusively by sequence and unable to change.

## Explanation

Cell-type-dependent variation is consistent with a facultative chromatin state. An unchanged sequence allows regulatory changes without requiring DNA loss. The classification applies to the observed region; it does not imply that every compact genomic region behaves in the same way.
