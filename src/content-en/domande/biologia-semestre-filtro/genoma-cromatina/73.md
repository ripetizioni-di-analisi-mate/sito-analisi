An insertion occurs only in a clone of adult skin cells. Which conclusion about transmission to offspring is justified?

- Presence in the somatic clone does not demonstrate presence in gametes.
- Its presence in a diploid cell implies that half the gametes carry it.
- Transmission to the clone's daughter cells is equivalent to transmission to the individual's offspring.
- Expansion of the clone demonstrates that the insertion was already present in the zygote.

## Explanation

Skin cells belong to the somatic lineage. An event restricted to that clone may create mosaicism, but does not demonstrate that gamete DNA carries the same insertion. Discussing inheritance through reproduction requires distinguishing somatic mutations from germline events.
