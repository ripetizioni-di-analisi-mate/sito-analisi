A microsatellite has a 4-bp repeat unit. Two alleles differ by three repeat units and have identical flanking regions. By how much do their fragment lengths differ?

- 7 bp.
- 12 bp.
- 3 bp.
- 4 bp.

## Explanation

Each additional unit contributes 4 bp. Three units therefore produce a difference of $3\cdot4=12$ bp. Identical flanking regions contribute equally to both fragments and do not change the difference. This describes allele length, without implying a different chromosome count.
