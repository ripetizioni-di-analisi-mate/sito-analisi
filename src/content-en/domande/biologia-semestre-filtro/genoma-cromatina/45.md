Which observation supports regulatory memory maintained through cell divisions?

- The state persists in descendants after stimulus removal, without detected sequence changes.
- A histone modification appears during stimulation without being followed after stimulus removal.
- Expression differs at one measurement without analysing descendant cells.
- DNA has undergone a deletion removing the promoter.

## Explanation

Persistence after stimulus removal distinguishes maintenance from a continuously induced response. Absence of sequence differences supports a regulatory explanation, while still requiring appropriate controls. A deletion would be a genetic change; a single instantaneous observation does not demonstrate transmission of the state.
