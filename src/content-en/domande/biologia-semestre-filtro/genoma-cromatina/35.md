A technique observes irregular chromatin in intact cells, without regular 30-nm fibres. Which statement is compatible with this result?

- Every regular fibre seen in vitro nevertheless proves the same structure in every cell.
- Nucleosomes are necessarily absent.
- A 30-nm fibre is not a universally obligatory compaction step.
- Chromatin must lack any organisation.

## Explanation

The result concerns higher-order chromatin organisation under the conditions studied. Present nucleosomes can arrange irregularly without forming a regular fibre. The conclusion limits the universality of the hierarchical model; it neither denies structures seen under other conditions nor removes nucleosome organisation.
