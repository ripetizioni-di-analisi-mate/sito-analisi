A non-autonomous element mobilises only when an external source supplies missing proteins. Which experimental result supports this dependency?

- The source is inactivated, but no control condition is compared.
- Insertions appear with the active source and decrease when it is inactivated.
- More element RNA is measured without searching for new insertions.
- Only membership of different sequence families is verified.

## Explanation

The comparison alters the availability of required activities and measures the relevant outcome: new insertions. With controls for viability and element expression, loss of mobility after source inactivation supports functional dependence. RNA abundance or membership of different families alone does not demonstrate that the supplied proteins are needed for insertion.
