Which component forms the direct connection between the centromeric region and spindle microtubules?

- Linker histone H1 alone.
- Telomerase.
- The shelterin complex.
- The kinetochore.

## Explanation

The kinetochore is a protein complex assembled at the centromere and contains components that bind microtubules. The centromere identifies the chromosome region, while the kinetochore provides the attachment machinery. Shelterin protects ends, telomerase extends telomeric repeats and H1 contributes to linker-DNA organisation: none replaces the kinetochore in spindle attachment.
