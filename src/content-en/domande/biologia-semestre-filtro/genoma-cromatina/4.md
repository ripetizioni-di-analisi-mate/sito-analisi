A human chromosome retains terminal TTAGGG sequence but loses shelterin-mediated protection. Which consequence is plausible?

- Guaranteed telomere lengthening, even without a telomeric synthesis mechanism.
- Complete protection guaranteed by TTAGGG sequence alone.
- Recognition of the end as damage and possible fusions.
- Loss of telomeric synthesis alone, with no effect on end recognition.

## Explanation

Telomere protection depends on the association between DNA and proteins. A remaining terminal sequence can become unprotected when shelterin is impaired. The end may then trigger damage responses and be joined to other ends. The presence of the repeat alone does not demonstrate a functional telomere.
