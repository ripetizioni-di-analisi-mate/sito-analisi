Which alteration is most directly consistent with loss of sister-chromatid cohesion?

- A histone H1 defect in stabilising linker DNA.
- A telomerase defect in end maintenance.
- A defect in cohesin complexes.
- A shelterin defect in end protection.

## Explanation

Cohesins help keep sister chromatids associated after replication. A defect in their function can therefore impair cohesion and segregation. Telomere maintenance and repeat copy number are other aspects of the genome, but do not provide the direct linkage between sisters.
