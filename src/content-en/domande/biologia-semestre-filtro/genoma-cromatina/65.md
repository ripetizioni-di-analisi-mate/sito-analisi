A repeated element contains mutations inactivating proteins required for movement. Which conclusion is correct?

- It necessarily becomes a tandem repeat.
- Every family copy must have the same mutation.
- It can no longer be inherited by daughter cells through chromosome replication.
- It can remain a repeated sequence even if the copy is no longer autonomous.

## Explanation

Classification as a repeat depends on the presence of similar copies. Autonomy instead depends on an individual copy encoding functional activities. An inactivated copy can persist in DNA and be inherited through normal replication without independently generating new insertions.
