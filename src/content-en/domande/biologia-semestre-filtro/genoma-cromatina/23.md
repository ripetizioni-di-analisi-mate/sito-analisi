Which histone is replaced by CENP-A in centromeric nucleosomes containing this variant?

- H3.
- H2A.
- H4.
- H1.

## Explanation

CENP-A is an H3 variant and contributes to specialised centromeric chromatin. H1 is a linker histone outside the octamer. H2A and H4 are histone-core components but are not the histone replaced by CENP-A. The distinction therefore concerns the composition of some centromeric nucleosomes, not the presence or absence of every other histone in the region.
