Which sequence shows three consecutive units of a 3-base tandem repeat?

- CAGTTACAG.
- CAGCAGCAG.
- CCCAAAGGG.
- CAGCAG.

## Explanation

The required unit is three bases long and must occur three times without interruption. CAGCAGCAG divides into CAG, CAG and CAG. CAGTTACAG contains two copies separated by another sequence, whereas CAGCAG contains only two units. Arrangement is inferred from the sequence, not from total base count alone.
