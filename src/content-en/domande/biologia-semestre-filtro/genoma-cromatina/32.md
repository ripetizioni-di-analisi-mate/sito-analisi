Mitotic compaction increases while total DNA content remains constant. Which conclusion is correct?

- DNA has been shortened through deletions despite unchanged total DNA content.
- Spatial organisation changes without a necessary loss of sequence.
- DNA molecule number must double during compaction.
- Compaction is sufficient to demonstrate a second round of DNA replication.

## Explanation

Compaction describes the space occupied by chromatin and its organisation. A chromosome can occupy less space while retaining the same molecules and DNA length. Constant DNA content is consistent with more compact folding, but does not support gene loss or a reduction in chromosome sets.
