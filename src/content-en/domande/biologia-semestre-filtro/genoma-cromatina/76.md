Which result favours the hypothesis that two similar genes arose through duplication rather than being merely two alleles?

- They differ by one base, without genomic-position data.
- They occupy corresponding positions on the two homologous chromosomes.
- Both occur at distinct loci in the same haploid set.
- They are two different reads of the same locus without evidence of a second locus.

## Explanation

Two sequences at distinct loci within one set are not alternative alleles of the same locus on the two homologues. Their similarity and placement can support a duplication origin, to be tested evolutionarily. Repeating a technical read does not create a new biological locus.
