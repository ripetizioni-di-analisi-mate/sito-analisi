A complex moves a nucleosome over a previously exposed promoter. Which outcome is plausible?

- Reduced access for some transcription factors.
- Increased binding of every factor because the covered site is always more accessible.
- Certain RNA increase independently of factor binding.
- Obligatory promoter activation because every remodelling event activates.

## Explanation

Remodelling changes particle positions and can expose or cover sites. Here, coverage may obstruct some factors and favour repression. Describing every remodelling event as activating would therefore be incorrect: the outcome depends on the direction of movement and the affected site.
