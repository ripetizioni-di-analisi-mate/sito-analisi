In an experiment H1 is removed while the octamers remain intact. Which statement is correct?

- Removing H1 necessarily eliminates every octamer.
- H1 must be replaced by H3 to reconstitute an eight-histone octamer.
- Every octamer necessarily loses two H3 molecules.
- Nucleosome core particles can remain present, although stabilisation and compaction may change.

## Explanation

H1 associates with entry and exit regions and linker DNA. It is not one of the eight histone-core subunits. Removing it can therefore alter higher-order organisation or stability without requiring the loss of the H2A-H2B-H3-H4 octamer stated to remain intact.
