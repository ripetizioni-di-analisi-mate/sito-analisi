A diploid cell progresses from G1 to G2 without division or abnormalities. Which pair of changes is correct?

- DNA remains $2C$; ploidy changes from $2n$ to $4n$.
- DNA from $2C$ to $4C$; ploidy from $2n$ to $4n$.
- DNA from $2C$ to $4C$; ploidy remains $2n$.
- DNA from $2C$ to $C$; ploidy remains $2n$.

## Explanation

During S phase the molecules are replicated, so DNA content doubles. The same two sets of homologues remain, so ploidy stays diploid. The notation $C$ describes DNA amount; $n$ describes the chromosome number in one set. Confusing these quantities leads to the erroneous answer $4n$.
