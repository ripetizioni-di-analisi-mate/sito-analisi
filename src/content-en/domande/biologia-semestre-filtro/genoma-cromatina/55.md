Sequencing identifies a base substitution that destroys a regulatory binding site. How is this alteration directly classified?

- Maintenance methylation that does not alter sequence.
- Only nucleosome repositioning without a genetic change.
- A histone modification leaving bases identical.
- A DNA-sequence mutation.

## Explanation

The substitution is documented in the DNA bases, so it is a genetic variation. It may have regulatory consequences, such as reduced factor binding, but its effect on expression does not change the nature of the initial alteration. Genetic changes and epigenetic states can affect the same gene through different mechanisms.
