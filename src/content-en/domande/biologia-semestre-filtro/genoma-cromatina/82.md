Which result directly demonstrates a change in an element's copy number rather than merely increased RNA?

- Greater element-promoter accessibility without sequence data.
- New DNA insertion junctions, with confirmed retention of all pre-existing copies.
- More element RNA without analysing DNA.
- Greater element-RNA stability without new genomic junctions.

## Explanation

RNA can increase through transcription or stability without a new insertion. New genomic junctions demonstrate new sites occupied by the element. Confirming that all previous copies are also retained establishes an increased total copy number, excluding simple relocation or compensating losses. The comparison must concern comparable samples and genome amounts.
