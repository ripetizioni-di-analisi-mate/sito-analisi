Partial nuclease digestion produces main bands at 180, 360 and 540 bp. What average nucleosome spacing is suggested?

- 180 bp.
- 1,080 bp.
- 360 bp.
- 147 bp.

## Explanation

The bands differ by $360-180=180$ bp and $540-360=180$ bp. The regular interval indicates fragments containing one, two and three repeats. The estimated repeat length is therefore 180 bp, including the average linker. Partial digestion does not necessarily isolate only the 147 wrapped bp.
