In a hypothetical genome of $10^6$ bp, $2\cdot10^4$ bp directly encode protein sequences. What percentage is this?

- $20\%$.
- $0{,}2\%$.
- $50\%$.
- $2\%$.

## Explanation

Divide the coding portion by the total and multiply by 100: $\dfrac{2\cdot10^4}{10^6}\cdot100=2\%$. This concerns the stated hypothetical genome, not a universal estimate. The remainder may include regulatory regions, introns, non-coding RNAs and repeats; absence of function cannot be inferred.
