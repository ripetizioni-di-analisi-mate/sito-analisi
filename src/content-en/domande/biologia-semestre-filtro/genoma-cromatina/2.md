In a species with $2n=12$, how many chromatids are present in an abnormality-free somatic cell in G2?

- $48$.
- $6$.
- $24$.
- $12$.

## Explanation

The cell retains the 12 chromosomes of its diploid complement. In G2 replication is complete and each chromosome contains two sisters, giving $12\cdot2=24$ chromatids. A value of 48 would count the strands of the double helices separately, whereas each chromatid contains one double-stranded molecule.
