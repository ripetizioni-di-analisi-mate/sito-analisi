A new genomic site contains a copy resembling mature mRNA, without the source gene's introns. Which origin is compatible?

- Direct duplication of source-gene DNA retaining all its introns.
- Reverse transcription of a processed RNA and insertion of the DNA copy.
- RNA splicing that automatically removes the same introns from chromosomal DNA.
- Transposition of the intact genomic gene without removing its introns.

## Explanation

A processed RNA has already had its introns removed. If reverse-transcribed, the DNA copy can retain that absence and insert elsewhere. The result is consistent with a processed-copy origin; determining function or inactivity requires further information about the locus and expression.
