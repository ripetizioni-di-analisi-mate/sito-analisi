In proliferating cells, maintenance methylation is blocked for several divisions, with no de novo methylation. What trend is expected for an initially methylated pattern?

- Automatic doubling of methyl groups at every S phase.
- Obligatory enzymatic removal of every parental methyl group before each S phase.
- Progressive dilution of methylation through replication.
- Complete pattern maintenance through base complementarity alone.

## Explanation

Parental strands can retain methyl groups while new strands remain unmodified when maintenance is blocked. In subsequent generations the fraction retaining the pattern decreases. This is passive replication-associated dilution; it does not necessarily require direct enzymatic removal of existing methyl groups.
