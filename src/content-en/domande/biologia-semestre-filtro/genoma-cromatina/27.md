A sample contains 25 canonical nucleosomes. How many histone molecules belong to their octamers altogether?

- $200$.
- $225$.
- $50$.
- $25$.

## Explanation

Each octamer contains eight molecules: two copies of four histones. The total is therefore $25\cdot8=200$. H1 may associate with linker DNA but remains outside the octamer; adding one H1 per particle would give 225 associated proteins, which is not the requested count.
