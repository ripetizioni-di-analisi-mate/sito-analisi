A sequence has 12 adjacent copies of a 20-bp unit. Which description follows certainly, independently of thresholds used for mini- and microsatellites?

- It is a tandem array measuring 32 bp in total.
- It is a tandem array measuring 20 bp in total.
- It is a 240-bp tandem array.
- It is a set of 12 interspersed copies despite their adjacency.

## Explanation

Adjacency defines the tandem array and $12\cdot20=240$ bp gives its length. Labels based on unit size may depend on the classification used, but these two structural facts remain valid. A repeated sequence alone implies neither transposition activity nor coding capacity.
