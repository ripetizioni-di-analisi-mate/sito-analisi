Which set describes the minimal requirements of a linear eukaryotic chromosome maintained through divisions?

- Origins and telomeres, with guaranteed segregation without a centromere.
- Functional origins and centromere, without any end-protection system.
- A centromere replacing every replication and protection requirement.
- Replication, a functional centromere and telomere protection.

## Explanation

Stability requires DNA to be copied, distributed and protected at its ends. Origins, centromere and telomeres address these different problems. A coding sequence may be expressed, but alone provides neither chromosome segregation nor end protection for a linear molecule.
