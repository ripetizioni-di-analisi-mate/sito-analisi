Which step distinguishes retrotransposition from a DNA transposition mechanism involving excision and insertion?

- Possible alteration of the target genomic sequence.
- Requirement for enzymatic activities.
- DNA synthesis from an RNA intermediate.
- Presence of an insertion site.

## Explanation

Both processes can involve enzymes and a new genomic site. The distinguishing feature of a retrotransposon is an RNA intermediate followed by reverse transcription. DNA transposition by excision and insertion instead transfers material through a DNA intermediate.
