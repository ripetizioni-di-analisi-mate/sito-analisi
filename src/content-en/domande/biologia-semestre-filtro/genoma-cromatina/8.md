A promoter becomes accessible after a nucleosome moves, while the DNA sequence remains identical. Which process describes the change?

- Histone acetylation demonstrated by nucleosome position alone.
- A point mutation in the promoter.
- DNA methylation as the only explanation for the new position.
- Chromatin remodelling.

## Explanation

Repositioning changes particle locations and DNA access. This is a remodelling effect, which can consume ATP. A point mutation would require a sequence difference. Increased accessibility may favour factor binding, but does not by itself demonstrate that the gene is transcribed.
