A sample has abundant H3K9me3 in compact repetitive regions. Which association is consistent?

- A state typical of active H3K4me3-marked promoters, regardless of the modified residue.
- A necessarily identical effect to H3K4me3 in every context.
- An accessible chromatin state caused by lysine neutralisation through methylation.
- Heterochromatic organisation in those regions.

## Explanation

H3K9me3 is a histone modification frequently associated with heterochromatin. Its location in compact repetitive regions is therefore consistent with that state. The result does not describe the entire genome or assign the same transcriptional level to every gene in the cell.
