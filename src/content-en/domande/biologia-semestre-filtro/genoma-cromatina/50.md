A selective deacetylase inhibitor increases histone acetylation. Which conclusion about individual genes' RNA is most appropriate?

- Effects may vary because different factors and regulatory contexts contribute.
- Every gene's RNA must decrease because acetylation increases histone positive charge.
- Every gene increases expression by the same proportion.
- RNA can change only if the promoter sequence also changes.

## Explanation

Deacetylases remove acetyl groups, so blocking them may raise acetylation levels. RNA also depends on factors, signals, local accessibility and degradation. The biochemical change does not specify one quantitative outcome for every gene and is not itself a sequence change.
