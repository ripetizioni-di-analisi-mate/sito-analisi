A 3,600-bp region is idealised as 18 complete nucleosome repeats. Assuming 147 wrapped bp per repeat, how long is the average linker?

- 147 bp.
- 53 bp.
- 200 bp.
- 347 bp.

## Explanation

The repeat length is $\dfrac{3600}{18}=200$ bp. Each repeat includes 147 wrapped bp plus the linker, so the latter measures $200-147=53$ bp. The calculation explicitly uses complete repeats; in a real fragment, ends and variable linkers would require additional information.
