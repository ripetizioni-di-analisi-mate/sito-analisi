Which chemical change accompanies acetylation of a histone lysine?

- Reduction of its positive charge and creation of a site recognised by specific proteins.
- Conversion of lysine's positive charge into a negative charge.
- Addition of methyl groups without changing the amino group's charge.
- Increased lysine positive charge and direct strengthening of attraction to DNA.

## Explanation

Acetylation modifies lysine's amino group and neutralises its positive charge under the conditions considered. It can also promote recruitment of proteins recognising acetylated residues. These effects contribute to its frequent association with accessible chromatin without alone guaranteeing transcription of every nearby gene.
