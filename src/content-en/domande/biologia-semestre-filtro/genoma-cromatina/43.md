After treatment a population has more RNA, but the RNA is also more stable. Which measurement helps assess transcriptional activity directly?

- The amount of gene DNA alone.
- The amount of nascent RNA produced in a defined interval.
- RNA half-life alone, without measuring production.
- Total pre-existing RNA alone.

## Explanation

Total RNA depends on both production and degradation. Increased stability can cause accumulation without increased synthesis. Measuring nascent RNA over a defined interval informs more directly about production; this control avoids automatically attributing every RNA increase to a more active promoter.
