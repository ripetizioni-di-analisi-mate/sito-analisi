Which property contributes to the interaction between DNA and histones?

- All DNA phosphates are positive and lysines are negative.
- Negatively charged DNA phosphates and many positively charged histone residues.
- Peptide bonds between DNA phosphates and histone tails.
- DNA and histones always both have zero charge.

## Explanation

The DNA phosphate backbone is negatively charged. Lysine and arginine are common in histones and carry positive charges under the conditions considered, contributing to electrostatic attraction. Other interactions also stabilise the complex; the explanation does not require DNA and proteins to have the same type of monomer.
