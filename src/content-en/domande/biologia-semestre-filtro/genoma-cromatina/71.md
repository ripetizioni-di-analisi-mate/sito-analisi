A new insertion is detected in an exon. Which assessment is scientifically appropriate?

- It always causes a frameshift regardless of length and position.
- It cannot alter the product because every exonic insertion is removed by splicing.
- It cannot alter the product if the inserted length is a multiple of three.
- It may alter the gene product; the effect must be evaluated from the site and sequence.

## Explanation

The insertion may interrupt a coding region or affect transcript processing. However, consequences and severity depend on position, sequence and genetic context. Location provides a reason to investigate without automatically determining a particular clinical outcome.
