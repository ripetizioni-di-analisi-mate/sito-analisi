Extensive digestion leaves protected fragments of about 147 bp, whereas partial digestion showed bands every 200 bp. Which interpretation reconciles the data?

- The 200-bp bands measured wrapped DNA alone, without any linker.
- Extensive digestion removed much of the linker DNA.
- The linker is 147 bp long and the wrapped DNA is 53 bp long.
- The 147-bp fragments contain only linker after removal of wrapped DNA.

## Explanation

Nuclease reaches linker DNA more readily than DNA wrapped around the octamer. Partial digestion retains multiples of the repeat, whereas more extensive digestion leaves the protected core. The difference $200-147=53$ bp is consistent with the model's average linker, without requiring changes in histone proteins.
