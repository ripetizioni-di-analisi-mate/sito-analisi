Which result directly distinguishes nucleosome repositioning from a DNA mutation?

- Factor binding decreases without sequencing its binding site.
- Gene RNA abundance decreases without examining DNA or nucleosomes.
- Accessibility changes without checking the DNA sequence.
- Positions change while sequencing confirms identical bases.

## Explanation

A mutation changes sequence. If the bases are identical and independent mapping shows different nucleosome positions, the data support chromatin reorganisation. Changes in RNA, factor binding or accessibility can instead result from either sequence changes or chromatin regulation; alone they do not distinguish the possibilities.
