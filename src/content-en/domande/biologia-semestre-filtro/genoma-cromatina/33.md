Which function is particularly associated with condensins during mitosis?

- Organisation and compaction of mitotic chromosomes.
- Restoration of methylation on new DNA strands after S phase.
- Maintenance of sister cohesion as their main characteristic function.
- Assembly of the direct bridge between centromeric DNA and spindle microtubules.

## Explanation

Condensins are SMC complexes contributing to mitotic chromosome structure, including loop organisation. This concerns the arrangement of replicated material. Telomerase instead adds telomeric sequences; assigning that task to condensins would confuse physical organisation with DNA synthesis.
