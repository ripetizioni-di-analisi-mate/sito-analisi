A treatment reduces the signal of a histone modification but also reduces total nucleosome number. Which control is needed to interpret modification per nucleosome?

- Compare only the raw modification signal.
- Normalise only to cell number, ignoring histone abundance per cell.
- Normalise to an appropriate measure of histone or nucleosome abundance.
- Normalise only to total DNA, ignoring changes in nucleosome density.

## Explanation

Total signal depends both on how many particles are present and on what fraction carries the modification. If particle number falls, signal may decrease even with an unchanged modified fraction. An appropriate total-histone reference helps distinguish nucleosome loss from a change in their modification.
