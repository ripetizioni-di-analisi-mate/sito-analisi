In a sample, methylated promoters are associated with little RNA. Which additional step strengthens a causal hypothesis?

- Measure total RNA alone without controlling its synthesis and degradation.
- Compare the same samples again without intervening on methylation.
- Select only low-RNA samples and measure their methylation once.
- Alter methylation in a targeted way with appropriate controls and measure expression.

## Explanation

Correlation does not establish event order or exclude common causes. A targeted intervention tests whether changing the modification changes expression, while controlling sequence, specificity and indirect effects. This strengthens causal inference compared with observing two variables that vary together.
