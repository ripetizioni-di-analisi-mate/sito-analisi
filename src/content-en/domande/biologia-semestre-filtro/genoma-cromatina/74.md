Which pair of categories uses different criteria and can describe the same sequence?

- An autonomous retrotransposon unable to produce its own reverse transcriptase.
- Interspersed and derived from retrotransposition.
- Single-copy and four-copy in the same genome at the same time.
- Allele of the same locus and a distinct locus in the same comparison.

## Explanation

“Interspersed” describes copy distribution, whereas “derived from retrotransposition” describes origin. A family can therefore have both features. Separating the criteria avoids treating categories answering different questions as alternatives: where copies occur and how they arose.
