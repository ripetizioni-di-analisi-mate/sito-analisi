An Alu element is transcribed, but no new insertions are detected during observation. Which conclusion is correct?

- Transcription alone demonstrates complete element autonomy.
- Absence of detected insertions proves the element cannot mobilise under any other condition.
- Every RNA molecule must correspond to an insertion.
- Transcription alone does not show that retrotransposition has been completed.

## Explanation

Transcription is one step, followed by availability of enzymatic activities, reverse transcription and insertion. Detectable RNA does not guarantee that all steps occur during the period examined. Detection sensitivity and observation duration also limit conclusions about absence of events.
