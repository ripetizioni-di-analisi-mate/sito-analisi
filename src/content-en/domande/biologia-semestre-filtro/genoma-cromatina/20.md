Which observation shows that gene number alone cannot predict genome size?

- Genomes with the same gene count necessarily have identical repeats.
- Every gene always has the same length in every organism.
- Two genomes with similar gene numbers may differ greatly in introns and repeats.
- Non-coding DNA has negligible length in every eukaryotic genome.

## Explanation

Total size includes coding regions, introns, regulatory sequences and repeats. Variation in non-coding components can greatly change length without a corresponding change in gene number. Counting genes and measuring base pairs therefore provide different kinds of information.
