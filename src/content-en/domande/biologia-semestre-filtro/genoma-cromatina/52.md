H3K4me3 and H3K9me3 are both histone methylations. Why cannot the same regulatory effect be inferred?

- H3K4me3 adds four methyl groups and H3K9me3 adds nine.
- H3K4me3 modifies DNA, whereas H3K9me3 modifies a protein.
- The two methylations neutralise lysine's positive charge to different extents.
- They affect different residues, recognised in different contexts and by different proteins.

## Explanation

The modification's position determines which interactions may be favoured. H3K4me3 is often associated with active promoters, whereas H3K9me3 is frequently associated with heterochromatin. The word methylation alone is therefore incomplete: substrate, residue and context must be specified.
