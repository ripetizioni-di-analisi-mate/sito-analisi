In normal mitotic anaphase, after sister separation but before cytokinesis, how many independent chromosomes are counted in the entire diploid human somatic cell?

- 46, distributed into two groups of 23.
- 184, because each strand is a chromosome.
- 92, distributed into two groups of 46.
- 23, counting each homologous pair only once.

## Explanation

After separation, each chromatid is considered an independent chromosome. The still-undivided cell therefore contains 92, but each pole receives 46. Specifying the compartment prevents incorrectly assigning 92 chromosomes to each future daughter nucleus. The strands of a double helix are not counted separately.
