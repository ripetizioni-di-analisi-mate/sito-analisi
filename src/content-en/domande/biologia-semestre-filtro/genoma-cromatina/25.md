Which component supplies telomerase with the template for adding repeats?

- The mRNA encoding the protein subunit of telomerase.
- An RNA primer just synthesised by primase.
- The complementary telomeric DNA strand.
- An RNA within the complex.

## Explanation

Telomerase is a ribonucleoprotein complex. Its protein component catalyses DNA synthesis and part of the complex's RNA acts as the template. This RNA is distinct from the mRNA encoding the protein subunit. A primer provides an end to extend, whereas the template specifies which bases to add: confusing these roles assigns the template function to a primer or to complementary DNA.
