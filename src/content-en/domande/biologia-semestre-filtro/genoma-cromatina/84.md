Two repeat copies have identical sequences but one is methylated and the other is not. Which information necessarily remains the same?

- The order of bases in the compared sequences.
- All protein interactions of the two copies.
- Transcription level under every condition.
- The genomic position of the copies.

## Explanation

Sequence identity concerns base order. Methyl groups may differ and influence regulatory interactions. The copies may also occupy different loci. The same sequence therefore does not automatically imply the same chromatin state, position or expression.
