Which description correctly distinguishes shelterin from telomerase?

- Shelterin contributes to end protection; telomerase extends telomeric sequences.
- Telomerase protects every end even when shelterin is completely absent.
- Both extend telomeres using the same RNA template.
- Shelterin synthesises repeats; telomerase prevents their fusion without synthesising DNA.

## Explanation

Chromosome ends must be protected, and their sequence may require maintenance. Shelterin contributes to protection through protein–DNA interactions. Telomerase adds repeats using an RNA template. Distinguishing the tasks explains why measurable telomere length alone does not guarantee proper protection.
