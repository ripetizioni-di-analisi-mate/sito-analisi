A DNA segment reads CACACACACA. How many consecutive CA units does it contain?

- $2$.
- $5$.
- $10$.
- $4$.

## Explanation

The sequence contains 10 bases and each CA unit contains 2. The number of units is therefore $\dfrac{10}{2}=5$. The copies are adjacent, forming a tandem repeat. Counting every base as a repeat would confuse total length with the number of specified units.
