A retroviral sequence occurs at the same locus in many cells of an organism and is transmitted to offspring. Which origin is compatible?

- Integration restricted to an adult skin clone without gamete involvement.
- Exclusive maintenance of viral DNA as an episome without chromosomal integration.
- Transient viral RNA without DNA integration.
- An ancestral germline integration.

## Explanation

Transmission through gametes requires integration into the germline in a relevant generation. Copies can then be inherited as part of chromosomal DNA. The presence of an endogenous retrovirus does not establish that the sequence still produces an infectious virus today.
