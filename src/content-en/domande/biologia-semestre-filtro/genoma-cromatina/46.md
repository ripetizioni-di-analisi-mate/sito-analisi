In many human CpG-rich promoters, a stable increase in methylation is associated with which state?

- No possible transcriptional change while promoter bases remain the same.
- Reduced transcription of the associated gene.
- Increased accessibility identical to that produced by histone acetylation.
- Increased transcription through obligatory recruitment of activators.

## Explanation

Methylation of many CpG promoters can hinder factor binding or favour recruitment of repressive complexes. It is therefore often associated with lower transcription. The conclusion is restricted to the stated context and does not automatically extend to every methylated cytosine in every genomic region.
