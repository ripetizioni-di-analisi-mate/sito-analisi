Which region is a typical example of constitutive heterochromatin?

- A promoter switching between compact and accessible states during differentiation.
- The entire inactive X chromosome as an exclusively constitutive example.
- Every temporarily inactive promoter in any cell.
- A stably compact, repeat-rich pericentromeric region.

## Explanation

Constitutive heterochromatin is associated with stably compact, often repetitive regions such as many pericentromeric regions. A temporarily silent gene is not automatically constitutive heterochromatin. Classification requires considering state stability and context as well as RNA abundance at one time point.
