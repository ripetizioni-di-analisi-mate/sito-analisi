Which example shows that a sequence not encoding protein can have a defined function?

- The coding region of a gene translated into a haemoglobin chain.
- The mRNA exons specifying actin's amino-acid sequence.
- A gene producing rRNA required by the ribosome.
- A codon sequence translated into collagen protein.

## Explanation

rRNA is a functional product participating in ribosome structure and catalysis. Its gene is transcribed, but the functional product is RNA rather than an amino-acid sequence. This distinguishes “gene” from “protein-coding gene” and shows the limitation of equating non-coding with useless.
