A signal proportional to DNA measures 80 units in G1. What value is expected in G2 in the same normal cell line?

- 160 units.
- 80 units.
- 320 units.
- 40 units.

## Explanation

The comparison uses the same cell line and a dye response proportional to DNA. Complete replication changes $2C$ to $4C$, so the signal also doubles: $80\cdot2=160$. Chromosome number remains unchanged until sister separation; the measurement tracks total DNA rather than that count.
