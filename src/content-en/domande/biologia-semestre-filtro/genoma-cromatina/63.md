Two fragments measure 142 and 154 bp with identical flanks; the repeat unit is 3 bp long. How many extra units does the second contain?

- $4$.
- $12$.
- $51$.
- $3$.

## Explanation

The length difference is $154-142=12$ bp. Each unit contributes 3 bp, so the copy-number difference is $\dfrac{12}{3}=4$. Identical flanking regions cancel. The measurement gives the difference between alleles, not the absolute repeat count of either allele.
