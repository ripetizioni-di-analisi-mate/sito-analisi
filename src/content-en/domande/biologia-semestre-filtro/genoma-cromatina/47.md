Methylation is measured within a gene body, without promoter data. Which interpretation is correct?

- Position must be considered before inferring an effect on expression.
- The presence of methyl groups alone demonstrates an obligatory transcription increase.
- The gene is certainly completely silenced.
- Every gene-body methyl group necessarily has the same effect as at a CpG promoter.

## Explanation

The association between methylation and repression described for many promoters cannot be transferred unconditionally to a gene body. Meaning depends on location and context. Mapping must therefore be combined with expression measurements and other controls, rather than treating methyl groups as a universal switch.
