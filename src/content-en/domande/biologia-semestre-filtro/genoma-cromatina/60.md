After duplication, two gene copies are expressed in different tissues. Which change can explain this without necessarily changing the encoded proteins?

- An obligatory change to both proteins' amino-acid sequences.
- Conversion of both copies into pseudogenes that are never transcribed.
- An identical copy-number increase in every tissue, sufficient by itself to determine tissue specificity.
- Divergence of regulatory regions.

## Explanation

Expression patterns also depend on promoters and regulatory elements. Two copies can retain similar coding sequences yet respond to different factors if their transcription-controlling regions diverge. This distinguishes protein function from where and when the protein is produced.
