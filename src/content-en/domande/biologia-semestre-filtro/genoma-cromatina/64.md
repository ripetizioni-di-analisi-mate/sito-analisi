Copies of the same family are separated by thousands of bases of other sequences. How is their arrangement described?

- Interspersed repeats.
- Contiguous tandem repeats.
- Single-copy sequences lacking other similar copies.
- Allelic variants of the same locus on the two homologues.

## Explanation

The stated feature is dispersal of copies at non-adjacent positions, defining an interspersed arrangement. Tandem repeats instead require consecutive units. This criterion concerns copy location and alone does not establish whether the elements can still mobilise.
