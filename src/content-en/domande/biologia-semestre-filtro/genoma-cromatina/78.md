To compare accessibility, two promoters receive nuclease under the same conditions and with equal DNA amounts. A naked-DNA control excludes intrinsic cleavage differences between the sequences. In chromatin, promoter A is cut more rapidly. Which initial interpretation is reasonable?

- A necessarily produces more protein in the same proportion as the increased cleavage.
- A is more accessible to the nuclease under the assay conditions.
- A certainly lacks every histone.
- A necessarily has a higher transcription rate.

## Explanation

Greater nuclease sensitivity informs about DNA availability for cutting, within the assay's specificity limits. It is consistent with greater accessibility but does not directly measure protein output or complete absence of nucleosomes. Separate measurements are needed to connect accessibility, transcription and translation.
