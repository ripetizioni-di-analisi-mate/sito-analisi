Why are numerous copies of the major rRNA genes consistent with high ribosome production?

- Copy number alone determines rRNA abundance independently of copy activity.
- Each gene copy is consumed after producing one rRNA molecule.
- They provide multiple transcribable units for producing abundant ribosomal RNA.
- All copies must be transcribed at the same rate under every condition.

## Explanation

Ribosomes require large amounts of rRNA. Multiple gene units provide more potentially transcribable templates, alongside regulation of individual units. The DNA template is not consumed by transcription. Different copies can also have different activities: copy number expands production capacity but does not alone determine actual output.
