An artificial linear chromosome has a functional centromere and active origins but unprotected ends. Which defect remains to be addressed?

- End protection against damage responses and fusions.
- Initiation of replication at the origins already stated to be active.
- Kinetochore assembly at the centromere already stated to be functional.
- Pairing with a homologue as an obligatory requirement for every mitotic segregation.

## Explanation

Origins and centromere address copying and segregation. They do not replace the system protecting the ends of a linear molecule. The artificial chromosome therefore also requires functional telomere protection; possessing two requirements does not make the third unnecessary.
