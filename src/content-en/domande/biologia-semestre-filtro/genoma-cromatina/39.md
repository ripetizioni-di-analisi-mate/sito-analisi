Much of the inactive X is compact, but some of its genes are expressed. Which description is correct?

- It is facultative heterochromatin with genes that may escape silencing.
- It is constitutive heterochromatin, irreversibly identical in every cell type.
- Expression of one gene proves that no X region is heterochromatic.
- Every X-linked gene must have identical activity.

## Explanation

X inactivation affects a broad regulatory domain, but some genes can escape. Facultative heterochromatin describes a state dependent on cellular or developmental context. The chromosome's name does not justify inferring absolute silencing at every locus.
