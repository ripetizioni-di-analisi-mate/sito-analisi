Which statement distinguishes DNA methylation from histone methylation?

- The former modifies only proteins; the latter modifies only DNA bases.
- Both modify histone-tail lysines exclusively.
- Both modify DNA CpG cytosines exclusively.
- The former modifies DNA bases; the latter modifies amino-acid residues in proteins.

## Explanation

A methyl group may be added in both cases, but the substrates differ. Human DNA methylation often concerns CpG cytosines; histone methylation specifies residues such as lysines and arginines. Distinguishing substrates avoids automatically transferring effects and mechanisms between the processes.
