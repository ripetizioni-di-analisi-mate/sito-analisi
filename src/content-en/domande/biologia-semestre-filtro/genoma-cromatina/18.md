An unreplicated chromosome contains one DNA molecule. How many polynucleotide strands make up that molecule?

- Four strands, because it is diploid.
- One strand, because the chromosome is not duplicated.
- Eight, including the strands of both homologues after replication.
- Two complementary strands.

## Explanation

Chromosomal DNA is double-stranded even before replication. An unduplicated chromosome therefore contains one molecule with two complementary strands. S phase produces two double-stranded molecules; it does not simply turn a single-stranded molecule into a double helix.
