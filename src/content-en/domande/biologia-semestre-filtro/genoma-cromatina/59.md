A gene-like copy has lost the ability to produce the original functional protein but is still transcribed. Which classification remains possible?

- A gene that must produce the original protein because it is transcribed.
- A pseudogene, possibly with other roles requiring investigation.
- A gene certainly lacking any molecular activity.
- An allele with an unchanged ability to produce the original functional protein.

## Explanation

Loss of the original functional product is compatible with classification as a pseudogene. Transcription may persist, and some pseudogenes have regulatory roles. The term therefore does not justify declaring an absolute absence of function; specific data about the product and its effects are needed.
