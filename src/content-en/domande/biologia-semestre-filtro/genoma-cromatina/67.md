A new retrotransposon copy inserts while the original remains at its locus. What is the immediate effect on copy number, assuming no losses?

- It doubles regardless of the copy number before the event.
- It increases by one copy.
- It necessarily remains identical.
- It decreases by one copy.

## Explanation

The process retains the source and adds an insertion. If there were $k$ copies before, this single event produces $k+1$, provided there are no losses. The copied intermediate allows the increase; merely relocating an excised copy would not give the same balance.
