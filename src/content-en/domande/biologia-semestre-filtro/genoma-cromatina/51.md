In the notation H3K4me3, what does the number 4 indicate?

- The number of acetyl groups added.
- The number of methyl groups added to lysine.
- The position of the lysine in the histone H3 sequence.
- The number of H3 copies in each octamer.

## Explanation

H3 identifies the histone, K denotes lysine and 4 specifies its position in the sequence. The me3 portion instead indicates three methyl groups on that residue. Distinguishing position and modification type allows marks such as H3K4me3 and H3K9me3 to be compared without assigning them the same meaning.
