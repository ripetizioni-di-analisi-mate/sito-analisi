A researcher measures greater factor binding after local nucleosome removal. Which control reduces the hypothesis that the result simply reflects more available factor?

- Compare conditions with equivalent factor amount and activity.
- Increase the factor only in the nucleosome-free sample and attribute everything to chromatin.
- Change factor abundance and nucleosome position simultaneously without separate comparisons.
- Measure only final binding without knowing available factor abundance.

## Explanation

If factor abundance rises, binding may increase even without changed accessibility. Keeping amount and activity equivalent helps isolate the effect of nucleosome arrangement. Sequence and site controls remain necessary, but this comparison directly addresses the proposed alternative explanation.
