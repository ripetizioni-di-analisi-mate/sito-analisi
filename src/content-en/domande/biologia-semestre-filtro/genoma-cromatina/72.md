A genomic insertion leaves a nearby gene's coding sequence and splicing unchanged but reduces its transcription. Which mechanism is consistent with these data?

- A frameshift within the coding region.
- An amino-acid substitution caused by mutation of a gene codon.
- Alteration of a regulatory region controlling gene transcription.
- Intron retention caused by defective splicing.

## Explanation

Expression also depends on regulatory sequences outside the coding region. A new insertion can alter binding sites or functional distances, changing RNA production without changing encoded amino acids. Comparisons with the insertion-free locus are needed to identify the mechanism.
