Two samples show the same gene sequence but different expression. Which conclusion is justified by these data alone?

- Heritable epigenetic memory is automatically demonstrated.
- The difference may depend on regulation or RNA stability, which require investigation.
- A different transcription rate is demonstrated, excluding differences in degradation.
- A sequence change in the compared region is demonstrated.

## Explanation

Identical sequence with different expression excludes a difference in the bases actually compared, but leaves several mechanisms open. Regulatory factors, chromatin and RNA stability may contribute. Establishing epigenetic memory additionally requires testing persistence of the state in descendant cells.
