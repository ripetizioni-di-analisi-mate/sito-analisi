A gene has an accessible promoter but produces little RNA because a required factor is absent. Which conclusion does this illustrate?

- Accessibility still imposes maximal transcription independently of factors.
- Promoter exposure alone substitutes for binding of the missing factor.
- Accessibility can be favourable without being sufficient for transcription.
- Euchromatin is always completely devoid of nucleosomes.

## Explanation

An accessible site permits binding, but the required factor must also be present and active. The example separates physical availability of DNA from availability of the regulatory machinery. Transcription depends on a combination of conditions; classification as open chromatin alone does not guarantee abundant RNA.
