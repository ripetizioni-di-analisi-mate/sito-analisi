An amplified fragment contains 100 bp of total flanking sequence and 8 repeats of 3 bp. What is its length?

- 300 bp.
- 108 bp.
- 24 bp.
- 124 bp.

## Explanation

The repeated segment measures $8\cdot3=24$ bp. The fragment also includes flanking regions, so total length is $100+24=124$ bp. The value 24 counts only the microsatellite; 108 adds the number of units without multiplying by unit length.
