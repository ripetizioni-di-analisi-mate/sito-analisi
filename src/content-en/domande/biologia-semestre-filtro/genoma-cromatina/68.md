A truncated LINE-1 copy lacks a region required for reverse transcription. What can be concluded about its autonomy?

- Membership of LINE-1 does not guarantee that this copy is autonomous.
- Its sequence can no longer be replicated with the chromosome.
- Every LINE-1 copy must be autonomous by definition.
- Loss of autonomy proves that no family copy is active.

## Explanation

The name identifies the family, whereas autonomy requires functional machinery encoded by the copy. Truncations or mutations may remove that capacity. The sequence can still be copied with the chromosome: ordinary DNA replication and autonomous generation of new insertions are distinct processes.
