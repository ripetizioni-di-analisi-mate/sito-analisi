An enzyme removes acetyl groups from histone tails. Which functional class does it belong to?

- Histone acetyltransferases.
- Histone deacetylases.
- Histone methyltransferases.
- Histone demethylases.

## Explanation

The class is identified by the reaction: a deacetylase removes acetyl groups, whereas an acetyltransferase transfers them to a substrate. The two activities can help regulate tail modifications dynamically. Removing the modification is not equivalent to cutting away the entire histone protein.
