Two alleles differ by one base but occupy the same locus on the two homologues. Which term correctly describes their relationship?

- Two orthologous genes defined by a species split.
- Two sister chromatids produced by the same replication.
- Two paralogous genes necessarily at distinct loci.
- Variants of the same gene at the same locus.

## Explanation

The decisive information is the corresponding position on the two homologues. Sequence differences at that locus define alleles. Paralogues instead arise from duplications producing distinct loci. Sequence similarity alone is insufficient to choose the term without considering location and origin.
