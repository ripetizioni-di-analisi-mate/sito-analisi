Meiosis I is normal. In only one of the two cells, meiosis II shows nondisjunction of one chromosome; everything else proceeds normally. What are the four products for that chromosome?

- Two with an extra copy and two lacking a copy.
- Four normal products.
- One normal product and three aneuploid products.
- Two normal, one with an extra copy and one lacking a copy.

## Explanation

The cell that completes meiosis II normally produces two normal gametes. In the other, both chromatids move to one pole: one product receives two copies and the other none. The pattern with all four products aneuploid instead corresponds to meiosis I nondisjunction in the simple model.
