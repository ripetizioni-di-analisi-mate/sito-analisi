With complete linkage and no recombination between the loci, which gametes does AB/ab produce?

- Only AB and aB.
- AB and ab.
- AB, Ab, aB and ab in equal proportions.
- Only Ab and aB.

## Explanation

Phase indicates that A lies on the same homolog as B, and a on the same homolog as b. Without recombination in the interval, gametes retain these combinations. Ab and aB would require an exchange separating the linked alleles. With regular segregation, the two parental classes are equally likely.
