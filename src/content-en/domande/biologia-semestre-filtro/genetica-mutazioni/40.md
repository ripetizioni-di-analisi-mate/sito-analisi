A pigment appears only in A_B_; aa or bb blocks the same pathway. In AaBb × AaBb with independent loci, what is the pigmented : unpigmented ratio?

- $1:1$
- $9:3:4$
- $3:1$
- $9:7$

## Explanation

Pigment requires at least one A and at least one B. Each locus has probability three quarters, so $P(\mathrm{A\_B\_})=\dfrac9{16}$. All other genotypes, fraction $\dfrac7{16}$, interrupt at least one step. The ratio follows from the requirement for both functions.
