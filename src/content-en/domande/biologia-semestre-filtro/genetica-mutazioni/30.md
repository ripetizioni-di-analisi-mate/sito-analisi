Two Aa parents have already had three aa children. In the Mendelian model with independent pregnancies, what is the probability that the fourth child is aa?

- $1$
- $0$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

The parents remain Aa and each fertilization combines A and a gametes with the same probabilities. Outcome aa requires a from both, so $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac14$. Previous results do not force the ratios to compensate within a small family.
