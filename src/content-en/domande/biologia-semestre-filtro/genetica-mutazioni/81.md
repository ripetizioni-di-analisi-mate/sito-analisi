An inherited variant is present from birth, but the trait appears only in adulthood. Which statement is consistent?

- Adult onset excludes inheritance.
- The variant necessarily arises on the day of manifestation.
- Every genetic trait must be visible at birth.
- Presence of the genotype and age of manifestation are distinct aspects.

## Explanation

DNA can contain the variant from the zygote onward, while manifestation depends on time-dependent processes or age-related conditions. Adult onset is therefore compatible with inheritance. Assessing penetrance also requires specifying the age at which the trait is observed.
