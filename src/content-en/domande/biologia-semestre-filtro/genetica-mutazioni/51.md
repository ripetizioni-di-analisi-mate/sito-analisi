Among 200 individuals with the same genotype, 150 show the defined trait at the same age. What is the observed penetrance?

- 100%.
- 75%.
- 50%.
- 25%.

## Explanation

Penetrance uses carriers of the genotype as the denominator and those showing the phenotype as the numerator. Thus $\dfrac{150}{200}=0{,}75$. The 50 without manifestation constitute the 25% complementary to the 75% penetrance. The estimate concerns the age and criteria used in the observation.
