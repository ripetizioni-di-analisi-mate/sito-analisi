In a testcross, one recombinant class has greatly reduced survival before counting. What limitation applies to frequency calculated from survivors alone?

- It necessarily equals the exact meiotic frequency.
- It may underestimate the frequency of recombinant gametes produced.
- It necessarily increases the absolute number of counted recombinants.
- It demonstrates complete linkage even if recombinant survivors exist.

## Explanation

The count records recovered products after selection rather than all formed gametes directly. If some recombinants are lost, the numerator is reduced unrepresentatively. The usual estimate requires comparable class viability and identification, or experimental correction of the distortion.
