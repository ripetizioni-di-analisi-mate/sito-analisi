After UV exposure, many helix-distorting photoproducts persist in DNA. Which pathway normally removes a short tract containing these lesions?

- Direct joining of a double-strand break.
- Polymerase proofreading during synthesis.
- Nucleotide excision repair.
- Base excision repair removing an individual base.

## Explanation

The lesion alters the local structure of the double helix. Nucleotide excision removes the damaged tract; synthesis uses the complementary strand and ligase seals the interruption. End joining instead concerns double-strand breaks and does not describe this removal of photoproducts.
