A variant in one gene affects both a connective-tissue property and an eye function. Which term describes the effect on multiple traits?

- Pleiotropy.
- Multiple allelism, meaning several variants of the same locus in the population.
- Demonstrated epistasis between two different genes.
- Polygenic inheritance of one trait, demonstrated by this finding alone.

## Explanation

Pleiotropy concerns one gene contributing to several phenotypic manifestations. The description follows from the one-to-many relationship between gene and traits. A polygenic trait represents the other direction, with several loci contributing to one trait, and requires different evidence.
