A confirmed carrier of an autosomal dominant variant does not show the trait at the age considered. Which hypothesis can explain this without denying the variant's presence?

- Loss of the ability to transmit the allele when the trait is absent.
- Incomplete or age-dependent penetrance.
- Necessary incompatibility between any dominant variant and an absent phenotype.
- Complete penetrance at the observed age without exceptions.

## Explanation

Allele presence is a molecular finding, whereas phenotype also depends on conditions and age. Incomplete penetrance allows carriers without manifestation. The allele can still be transmitted through gametes: absence of the trait does not erase the genotype.
