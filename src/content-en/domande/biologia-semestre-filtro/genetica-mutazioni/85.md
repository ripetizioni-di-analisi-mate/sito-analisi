A mother $X^AX^a$ and a father $X^AY$ are considered under an X-linked recessive model in which heterozygotes are unaffected. What fraction of daughters is expected to be affected?

- $\dfrac12$
- $0$
- $1$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

Every daughter receives $X^A$ from the father. From the mother she receives $X^A$ or $X^a$, so she can be $X^AX^A$ or $X^AX^a$. None has two a copies in the stated model. Half the daughters are carriers, but the question asks for the affected fraction.
