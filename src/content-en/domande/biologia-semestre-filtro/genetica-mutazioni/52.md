All carriers of a variant show a trait, but its intensity varies greatly. Which description correctly distinguishes the two aspects?

- Complete penetrance and variable expressivity.
- Incomplete penetrance and constant expressivity.
- Variable penetrance defined by intensity only among those showing the trait.
- Complete penetrance and constant expressivity.

## Explanation

Presence of the trait in every carrier indicates complete penetrance under the defined conditions. Differences in intensity instead concern expressivity. These answer different questions: whether the trait appears and how it manifests when present. Variable intensity alone does not reduce penetrance.
