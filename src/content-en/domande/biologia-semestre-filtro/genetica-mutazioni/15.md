Two codons, GAA and GAG, both specify glutamate. A GAA → GAG substitution in a translated region is classified as:

- Frameshift.
- Missense.
- Synonymous with respect to the protein sequence.
- Nonsense.

## Explanation

The classification compares the amino acids specified by the two codons. Because both indicate glutamate, the substitution is synonymous. The number of bases remains three, so the frame does not shift. Any effect on splicing or RNA stability requires separate evidence.
