A deletion removes exactly one complete internal codon from a translated region. Which conclusion is justified without knowing the protein's function?

- Three amino acids are lost while preserving the frame.
- The downstream frame is preserved, but the functional effect remains to be assessed.
- Protein function is certainly preserved.
- The frame shifts for every subsequent triplet.

## Explanation

Removing three nucleotides preserves the alignment of subsequent triplets and removes the amino acid encoded by the codon. Its importance depends on its position in the protein. A preserved frame does not guarantee preserved activity, especially if the residue contributes to the active site or structure.
