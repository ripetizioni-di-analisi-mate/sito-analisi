Which step explains absence of direct father-to-son transmission of a variant in the X-specific region in the usual model?

- The son receives paternal X and maternal Y.
- The son receives paternal Y and maternal X.
- The father transmits his X to half his sons.
- The variant enters the son’s X but is always silenced because it is paternal.

## Explanation

The paternal sex chromosome contributing to the son's XY complement is Y. The son's X therefore comes from the mother. The reasoning concerns the X-specific region stated in the question; a shared phenotype in father and son can have other genetic or environmental causes.
