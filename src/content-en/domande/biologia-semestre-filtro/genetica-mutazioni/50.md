Two DNA intervals each have a physical length of one million bases but different genetic distances. Which explanation is compatible?

- Repulsion phase increases genetic distance compared with coupling phase.
- Genetic distance is always proportional to length with the same coefficient.
- Centimorgans directly measure the number of bases.
- Recombination frequency varies among genomic regions.

## Explanation

A physical map measures length in bases, whereas a genetic map derives from recombination. Regions with different exchange propensities can therefore have equal physical extent and different genetic distance. Conversion requires local information rather than a universal constant ratio. Phase distinguishes parental from recombinant combinations, but does not by itself determine the distance between loci.
