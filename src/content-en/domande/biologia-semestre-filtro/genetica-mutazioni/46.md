Two markers show an expected recombination frequency of 50%. Which conclusion is justified by this result alone?

- They are necessarily adjacent.
- Every meiosis has exactly one crossover between the markers.
- They necessarily lie on different chromosomes.
- They behave as unlinked; they may lie on different chromosomes or far apart on the same one.

## Explanation

A 50% value makes parental and recombinant combinations equally likely in the model. Assortment of different chromosomes can produce it, but so can multiple exchanges between distant loci on the same chromosome. Establishing physical location requires additional markers or sequence information.
