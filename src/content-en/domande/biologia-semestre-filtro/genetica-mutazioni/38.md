An assay distinguishes proteins produced by alleles M and N. The heterozygote shows both recognizable proteins. Which relationship describes the result at this level?

- Codominance.
- Complete recessiveness of both alleles for the measured product.
- Incomplete dominance with one intermediate product.
- Epistasis between two distinct loci demonstrated by the result alone.

## Explanation

The result shows both distinguishable allelic contributions simultaneously, so it describes codominance for the molecular trait measured. The two products remain distinguishable: the assay does not show a single intermediate product. Dominance relationships must always refer to the phenotype observed, here the assay's protein composition.
