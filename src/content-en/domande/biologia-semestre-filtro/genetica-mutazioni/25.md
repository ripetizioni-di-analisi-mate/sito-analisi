A damaged base is removed and the original sequence is restored before replication. Which distinction is correct?

- The lesion necessarily becomes a synonymous mutation.
- The restored sequence still contains a stable substitution.
- The damage was repaired without necessarily leaving a stable mutation.
- Restoration necessarily transfers the lesion to the germ line.

## Explanation

Damage is a chemical or structural alteration of DNA. If the system restores the correct sequence before the alteration is fixed, it can prevent a stable mutation. The initial event must therefore be distinguished from the outcome of replication and repair.
