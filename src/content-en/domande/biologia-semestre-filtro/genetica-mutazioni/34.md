An AaBbCc individual produces gametes with independent assortment of the three loci. What is the probability of gamete AbC?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac1{64}$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac18$

## Explanation

The gamete must receive A, b and C. Each choice has probability $\dfrac12$ and the loci assort independently, so $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac18$. A gamete contains one copy per locus; a two-parent Punnett square is not required for this question.
