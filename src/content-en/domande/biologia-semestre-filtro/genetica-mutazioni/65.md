Mitotic nondisjunction occurs during embryonic development. Cells with different karyotypes are observed in the organism. Which term describes this coexistence derived from the same zygote?

- Identical heterozygosity in all cells.
- Population multiple allelism with no differences among cells.
- Chimerism necessarily arising from two different zygotes.
- Mosaicism.

## Explanation

The postzygotic error generates different cell lineages within the same individual. Descendants of each lineage may retain their chromosome complement, producing a mosaic. Tissue distribution depends on when and in which cell the error occurred.
