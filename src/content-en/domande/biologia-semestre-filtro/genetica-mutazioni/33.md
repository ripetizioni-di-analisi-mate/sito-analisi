An unaffected individual, born to Aa × Aa parents for a fully penetrant recessive trait, has a partner known to be Aa. Excluding new mutations, what is the probability of an aa child?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac1{12}$

## Explanation

The unaffected individual is Aa with probability $\dfrac23$; if AA, this individual cannot produce aa with the partner. In the Aa × Aa case, the probability is $\dfrac14$. The overall probability is therefore $\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac14=\dfrac16$, separating genotype uncertainty from segregation.
