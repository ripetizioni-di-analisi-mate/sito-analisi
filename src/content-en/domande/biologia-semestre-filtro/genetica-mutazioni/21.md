A glycosylase recognizes an oxidized base and separates it from deoxyribose. Which pathway has begun?

- End joining after a double-strand break.
- Base excision repair.
- Nucleotide excision through initial removal of an entire tract.
- Mismatch repair of a post-replication mismatch.

## Explanation

Removal of the individual base by a glycosylase is the characteristic step in base excision repair. An abasic site forms; other enzymes then restore the tract and seal the strand. Nucleotide excision instead recognizes lesions through a different system and removes a nucleotide-containing tract.
