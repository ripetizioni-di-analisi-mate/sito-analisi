A father affected by an X-linked dominant trait has children with a noncarrier mother. In a complete-penetrance model, which children receive the paternal variant?

- All daughters and no sons.
- No children of either sex.
- Half the daughters and half the sons.
- All sons and no daughters.

## Explanation

The father supplies his X to every daughter and his Y to every son. The variant on his X therefore goes to all daughters. With complete penetrance, they show the trait; sons receive the maternal X and do not inherit that variant from the father.
