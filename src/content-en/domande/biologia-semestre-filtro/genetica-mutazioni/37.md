In a plant species, RR flowers are red, rr white and Rr pink. In Rr × Rr, what fraction is expected to be pink?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac12$
- $1$

## Explanation

Pink identifies heterozygote Rr, formed by R from the first parent and r from the second or vice versa. Each combination has probability one quarter, adding to one half. Incomplete dominance changes the phenotypic ratio, while segregation retains the one-to-two-to-one genotypic ratio.
