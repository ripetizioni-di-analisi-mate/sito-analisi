Which comparison correctly distinguishes homologous chromosomes from sister chromatids?

- Homologs are always newly synthesized copies of the same chromosome.
- Sisters necessarily come from different parents.
- Homologs carry the same loci; sisters result from replication of one chromosome.
- Homologs must have identical alleles at every locus.

## Explanation

A homologous pair normally includes one maternal and one paternal copy, with the same loci but possibly different alleles. Sister chromatids instead form by duplicating one chromosome. The distinction allows tracking of the structures that separate in meiosis I and meiosis II.
