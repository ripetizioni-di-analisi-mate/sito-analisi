In one meiosis of an AB/ab double heterozygote, a single crossover between loci A and B involves two nonsister chromatids. How many of the four chromatids become recombinant for those loci?

- Three.
- Two.
- Four.
- One.

## Explanation

The reciprocal exchange alters the two participating chromatids. The other two do not participate and retain parental combinations. Thus two of four products are recombinant in this meiosis; this does not imply that every meiosis in the individual has the same event.
