A promoter variant reduces RNA polymerase recruitment without changing the coding sequence. Which primary effect is consistent with the data?

- Automatic increase in stability of every protein produced.
- Reduction in codon number in every transcript through deletion.
- Mandatory replacement of the first amino acid.
- A reduced amount of transcript.

## Explanation

The promoter contributes to transcription initiation. Less efficient recruitment can reduce RNA production while leaving the codon sequence unchanged. Inferring changes in the protein sequence would require additional evidence; the alteration here primarily concerns the amount of product.
