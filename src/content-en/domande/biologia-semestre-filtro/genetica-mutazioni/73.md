In one individual, one tissue has 20% variant mitochondrial DNA molecules and another has 80%. Which concept describes the mixture of normal and variant molecules?

- Complete linkage between two nuclear loci.
- Heteroplasmy.
- Homoplasmy for a single mitochondrial sequence.
- Heterozygosity at a diploid autosomal locus.

## Explanation

Heteroplasmy is coexistence of mitochondrial DNA with different sequences within a cell or individual. Proportions can differ among tissues and contribute to functional differences. The percentages concern mitochondrial molecules, so they do not describe the two copies of an autosomal locus.
