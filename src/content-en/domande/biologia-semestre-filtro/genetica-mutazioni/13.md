A mother $X^AX^a$ and a father $X^AY$ are considered for an X-linked recessive trait with complete penetrance. Assuming equally likely sexes, what is the probability of an affected son in one pregnancy?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac14$
- $1$

## Explanation

Both a son, probability $\dfrac12$, and maternal transmission of $X^a$, probability $\dfrac12$, are required. The result is therefore $\dfrac14$. A probability of one half instead applies to children already known to be male; this question concerns a pregnancy of unknown sex.
