In the classical ABO model, which blood groups can occur among offspring of $I^AI^B$ × $ii$?

- Only AB.
- A and B, each with probability $\dfrac12$.
- AB and O, each with probability $\dfrac12$.
- A, B, AB and O in equal proportions.

## Explanation

The AB parent supplies $I^A$ or $I^B$, while the O parent always supplies i. Only $I^Ai$ and $I^Bi$ therefore result. An AB child would require an A allele and a B allele from different parents; the ii parent can supply neither.
