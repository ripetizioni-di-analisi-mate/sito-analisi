For two nearby loci, a testcross shows 8% recombinants. Ignoring multiple exchanges, what is the approximate genetic distance?

- 8 cM.
- 16 cM.
- 0.08 cM.
- 4 cM.

## Explanation

For a short interval, one centimorgan corresponds approximately to 1% recombination. Thus 8% gives about 8 cM. This measure derives from meiotic events; it does not specify a number of bases because recombination rate varies along the genome.
