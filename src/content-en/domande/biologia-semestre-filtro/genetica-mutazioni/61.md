An individual carries a balanced reciprocal translocation, with no detectable net loss of material. Which reproductive consequence remains possible?

- Production of some gametes with unbalanced material.
- The carrier will necessarily transmit the translocation to every child.
- Rearranged chromosomes will always return to their original arrangement during meiosis.
- Every gamete will necessarily be balanced like the carrier.

## Explanation

Balance describes the material present in the observed individual. During meiosis, pairing and segregation of rearranged chromosomes can produce combinations with duplications and deletions. Thus absence of net loss in the carrier does not guarantee that every gamete is balanced.
