Two unaffected parents have an aa child for an autosomal recessive trait with complete penetrance. Excluding new mutations, which parental genotypes are necessary?

- Both aa.
- Both Aa.
- Both AA.
- One AA and one aa.

## Explanation

The aa child received a from both parents, so each must carry a. Because the parents are unaffected and aa always shows the trait, each must also have A. Both must therefore be Aa. The conclusion explicitly depends on complete penetrance and absence of new mutations.
