Two variants of the same gene both lie on the maternal copy; the paternal copy is intact. Which statement correctly describes their phase?

- They are in trans.
- They constitute homozygosity for each variant.
- They necessarily belong to two distinct loci.
- They are in cis.

## Explanation

Cis phase means that the variants lie on the same homolog. Trans phase would require one variant on each copy. The distinction changes the functional interpretation of a gene associated with a recessive trait because one of the two copies remains intact in the described case.
