A variant introduces a premature stop. Which statement avoids inferring more than the result permits?

- Every transcript is degraded with identical efficiency regardless of stop position.
- It always produces a stable protein of the same length.
- It may reduce product length or RNA amount, depending on position and cellular surveillance.
- RNA amount always remains identical and only the final amino acid changes.

## Explanation

A premature stop can terminate translation early. Some transcripts are also recognized and degraded by surveillance systems in a context-dependent manner. Nonsense classification alone therefore does not specify the amount, stability or activity of the final product.
