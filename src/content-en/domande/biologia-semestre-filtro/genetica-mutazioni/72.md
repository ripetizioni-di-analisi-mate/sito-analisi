Two people with the same genotype show different values of a trait when raised in different environments. Which conclusion is justified?

- Phenotype depends only on genotype and must be identical.
- Phenotype can also depend on the environment.
- Phenotypic difference alone demonstrates a DNA-sequence change.
- The trait cannot have any genetic component.

## Explanation

The comparison is compatible with an environmental contribution to trait expression. It does not imply that genes are irrelevant, because the same genotype can respond differently to conditions. Separating contributions and interactions requires controlled comparisons beyond observation of just two individuals.
