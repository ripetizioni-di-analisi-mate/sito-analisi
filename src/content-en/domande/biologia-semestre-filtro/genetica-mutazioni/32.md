Two Aa parents have two independent children. What is the probability that at least one has genotype aa?

- $\dfrac9{16}$
- $\dfrac1{16}$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac7{16}$

## Explanation

The complementary event is that neither child is aa. Each child is non-aa with probability $\dfrac34$, so both are non-aa with probability $\dfrac9{16}$. Subtracting from one gives $1-\dfrac9{16}=\dfrac7{16}$. Simply adding two quarters would count the case with both aa twice.
