In Aa × Aa, genotype aa occurs with probability $\dfrac14$. For three independent children, what is the probability that exactly one is aa?

- $\dfrac{37}{64}$
- $\dfrac1{64}$
- $\dfrac9{64}$
- $\dfrac{27}{64}$

## Explanation

For one fixed order, such as aa followed by two non-aa children, the probability is $\dfrac14\left(\dfrac34\right)^2=\dfrac9{64}$. The aa child can occupy three distinct positions. Adding them gives $3\cdot\dfrac9{64}=\dfrac{27}{64}$. The factor three counts the compatible orders.
