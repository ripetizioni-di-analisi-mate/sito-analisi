Why does availability of the sister chromatid favor homologous recombination in S and G2 phases?

- It allows ends to be joined without using sequence information.
- It prevents every possibility of error.
- It eliminates every need for DNA synthesis.
- It supplies a homologous sequence that can serve as a template.

## Explanation

After replication, a nearby copy of the region exists and can reconstruct missing information. This makes the sister chromatid a suitable template. Repair may include synthesis and other steps; template availability improves expected accuracy without guaranteeing infallibility.
