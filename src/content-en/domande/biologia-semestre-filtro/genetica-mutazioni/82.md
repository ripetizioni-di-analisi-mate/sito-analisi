A new pathogenic variant arises in the zygote and is absent from the parents. Which description correctly distinguishes genetic origin from inheritance from the parents?

- A de novo variant necessarily makes the condition nongenetic.
- Every genetic condition must result from a variant already inherited from a parent.
- The condition can be genetic while resulting from a de novo variant.
- Absence in the parents proves that DNA does not contribute to the condition.

## Explanation

A genetic cause concerns the effect of a genetic-material variant. The variant may be inherited or arise as a new event. If it appears in the zygote and is retained in relevant cell lineages, it can affect the individual without previously being present in the parents.
