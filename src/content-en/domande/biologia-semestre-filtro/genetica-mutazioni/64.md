A single nondisjunction in meiosis I sends both homologs into one cell; meiosis II proceeds normally. What distribution is expected among the four products for that chromosome?

- Three normal and one with two extra copies.
- Four normal.
- Two with an extra copy and two lacking a copy.
- Two normal, one with an extra copy and one lacking a copy.

## Explanation

After the meiosis I error, one cell contains both homologs and the other none. Normal sister separation in meiosis II preserves that difference in the products. Two disomic and two nullisomic gametes therefore result for the affected chromosome, with no normal products in this model.
