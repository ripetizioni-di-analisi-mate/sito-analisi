After replication, a G–T pair remains in a tract where the new strand can be identified. Which system specializes in correcting this mismatch?

- Nucleotide excision of a helix-distorting lesion.
- Joining the ends of a double-strand break.
- Mismatch repair.
- Codon-anticodon pairing in the ribosome.

## Explanation

The problem is a mismatch that escaped replication. Mismatch repair acts on the new-strand tract using the other strand as a reference. Distinguishing the correct strand prevents fixing the error as a new complementary pair; this case does not require an initial double-strand break.
