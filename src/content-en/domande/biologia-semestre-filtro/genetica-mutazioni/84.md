A small pedigree shows an affected mother and an affected daughter, with no other data. Which conclusion is methodologically sound?

- Mitochondrial inheritance is uniquely demonstrated.
- Several models may fit; further evidence is needed to distinguish them.
- Autosomal dominant inheritance is the only compatible model.
- X linkage is excluded by the presence of an affected daughter.

## Explanation

Mother-to-daughter transmission fits several models, including autosomal dominant, some recessive configurations, X-linked and mitochondrial inheritance. Without other relatives, genotypes or penetrance information, one cannot identify a unique model. Compatibility and demonstration therefore have different meanings.
