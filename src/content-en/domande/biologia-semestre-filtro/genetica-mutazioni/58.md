A human cell has three complete haploid sets, totaling 69 chromosomes. Which term describes it?

- Normally diploid.
- Triploid.
- Trisomic for just one chromosome.
- Monosomic.

## Explanation

Three complete sets correspond to $3n$, hence triploidy, a form of polyploidy. Trisomy instead adds one copy of a single chromosome to an otherwise diploid set. The distinction depends on the extent of the change: an entire set or a specific chromosome.
