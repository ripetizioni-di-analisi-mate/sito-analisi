A trait depends on a variant in the Y-specific region, with complete penetrance. What transmission is predicted from a carrier father in the usual model?

- To all daughters and no sons.
- To half the sons and no daughters.
- To half the children of either sex.
- To all sons and no daughters.

## Explanation

The father transmits Y to sons and X to daughters. A variant in the Y-specific region therefore follows the male line. The assumption excludes pseudoautosomal regions, whose recombination and transmission require a different description.
