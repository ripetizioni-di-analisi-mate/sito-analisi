A variant appears after fertilization in a progenitor cell of part of the skin. Which distribution is compatible with its somatic origin?

- Transmission to half the gametes inferred from the skin location alone.
- Presence in every cell of the organism from the zygote onward.
- Presence only in the original cell, without transmission to mitotic daughters.
- Presence in some descendant skin cells.

## Explanation

Descendant cells can inherit the variant during subsequent divisions, forming a population different from other cells in the individual. This is possible somatic mosaicism. Involvement of gametes requires involvement of the germ line and does not necessarily follow from a skin-limited variant.
