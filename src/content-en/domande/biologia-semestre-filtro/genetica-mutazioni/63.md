An autosomal segment normally present in two total copies is duplicated on one homolog. How many total copies of the segment are then present in the unreplicated diploid cell?

- Two.
- One.
- Three.
- Necessarily four.

## Explanation

The unchanged homolog retains one copy. The altered homolog contains the original and its duplication, hence two. The total is $1+2=3$. The question concerns a segment, so increased dosage does not imply duplication of the entire chromosome set.
