Two AB parents, in the classical model without rare phenotypes, have what probability of a group O child?

- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $0$

## Explanation

Both parents produce only $I^A$ or $I^B$ gametes. Children can be $I^AI^A$, $I^AI^B$ or $I^BI^B$, but not ii because neither parent carries i. The zero probability depends on the stated ABO model and exclusion of rare expression variants.
