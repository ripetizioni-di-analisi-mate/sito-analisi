In the classical ABO model, a group A person and a group B person have a group O child. What are the parental genotypes?

- $I^Ai$ and $I^Bi$.
- $I^AI^A$ and $I^BI^B$.
- $I^AI^B$ and $ii$.
- $I^Ai$ and $I^BI^B$.

## Explanation

The group O child is ii and received an i allele from each parent. The group A parent must also carry $I^A$, so is $I^Ai$; likewise, the group B parent is $I^Bi$. The deduction assumes ordinary antigen expression and excludes rare phenotypes.
