A double-heterozygote testcross yields 440 Ab/ab, 440 aB/ab, 60 AB/ab and 60 ab/ab. Assuming equally viable classes, which heterozygous-parent phase best fits?

- AB/ab with zero recombination.
- AB/ab, with parental AB and ab.
- Independent assortment with four equally likely classes.
- Ab/aB.

## Explanation

The most frequent classes identify parental combinations when recombination frequency is below one half and recovery is undistorted. Ab and aB therefore lie on the two homologs. AB and ab total 120 of 1000, consistent with 12% recombination.
