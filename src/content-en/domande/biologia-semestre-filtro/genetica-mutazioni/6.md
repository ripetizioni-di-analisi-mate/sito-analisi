In the AaBb × AaBb cross, with independent loci and Mendelian segregation, what is the probability of genotype aaBb?

- $\dfrac1{16}$
- $\dfrac3{16}$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

At locus A, aa requires two a gametes and has probability $\dfrac14$. At locus B, Bb can arise from maternal B and paternal b or the reciprocal case, so its probability is $\dfrac12$. Independence allows the product $\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac18$.
