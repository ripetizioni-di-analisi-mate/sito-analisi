In a three-locus testcross, the most frequent parental classes are ABC and abc; the classes assigned to double crossing over are AbC and aBc. Which gene is in the middle?

- B.
- The order cannot be inferred even when double-crossover classes are known.
- A.
- C.

## Explanation

Compare ABC with AbC and abc with aBc: only the B allele changes, while the outer alleles retain their parental combination. Two exchanges, one in each interval flanking the middle gene, produce exactly this result. The order is therefore A–B–C or its equivalent reverse C–B–A.
