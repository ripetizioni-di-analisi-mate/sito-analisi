Why is an aabb individual useful as the partner in a testcross of AaBb?

- It produces only ab gametes, making the other parent's gametes identifiable.
- It makes the other parent’s four gametes equally likely.
- It transmits AB and ab in equal proportions.
- It blocks crossing over in the other parent.

## Explanation

The tester's contribution is constant: a at the first locus and b at the second. Differences among offspring genotypes therefore reflect combinations produced by the double heterozygote. This allows estimation of recombinant frequency, assuming correct identification and equal recovery of classes.
