Two broken DNA ends are processed and joined without an extended homologous template. Two bases are missing at the junction. Which description is consistent?

- End joining with a small deletion.
- Mismatch repair of an incorrect pair after replication.
- Base excision initiated by a glycosylase at an oxidized base.
- Homologous recombination that fully copies a template.

## Explanation

The described process directly joins the ends after processing. Loss of two bases is compatible with a mutagenic outcome of end joining. The pathway can also be accurate with suitable ends, so its name does not imply that every event produces the same deletion.
