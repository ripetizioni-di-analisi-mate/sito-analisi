A protein works as a complex of several subunits. A mutant subunit is incorporated and impairs the normal subunits in the complex. Which mechanism is described?

- Haploinsufficiency due solely to insufficient normal-product amount.
- A dominant-negative effect.
- Complete recessiveness with normal heterozygote function.
- Codominance without interference between the two products.

## Explanation

The mutant product interferes with the normal product through the shared complex. It can therefore impair function even in a heterozygote, beyond simply losing its own activity. This defines a dominant-negative effect; it does not follow merely from allele frequency or uppercase notation.
