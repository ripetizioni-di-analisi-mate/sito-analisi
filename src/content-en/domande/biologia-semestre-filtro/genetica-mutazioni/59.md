A karyotype shows 47 chromosomes, including three separate copies of chromosome 21. Which description matches the result?

- Translocation trisomy 21 as the only explanation.
- Free trisomy 21.
- Monosomy 21.
- Complete triploidy.

## Explanation

The count increases by one and the extra chromosome is a 21. This is aneuploidy for that chromosome, described as free trisomy 21. Triploidy would require three complete sets. The three separate copies here distinguish free trisomy from an extra dose of chromosome 21 material due to translocation.
