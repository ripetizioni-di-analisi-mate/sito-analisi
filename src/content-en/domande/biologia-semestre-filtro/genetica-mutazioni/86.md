One parent is $v_1/+$ and the other $v_2/+$ for the same gene associated with a recessive trait, with two different inactivating variants. What is the probability of a compound-heterozygous $v_1/v_2$ child, assuming Mendelian segregation?

- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac12$
- $0$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

The first parent transmits $v_1$ with probability $\dfrac12$ and the second transmits $v_2$ with probability $\dfrac12$. The combination requires both events, giving $\dfrac14$. The variants differ but affect the two copies of the same gene, so the child is a compound heterozygote.
