In Aa × Aa, AA is lethal before birth, whereas Aa and aa have equal survival. Among live births, what fraction is expected to be Aa?

- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

At conception, the fractions are one quarter AA, one half Aa and one quarter aa. Aa and aa have initial weights of one half and one quarter; equal survival probability preserves their 2:1 ratio. Normalizing these weights, the fraction of Aa among live births is $\dfrac{\frac12}{\frac34}=\dfrac23$. The denominator changes because the sample is restricted to live births.
