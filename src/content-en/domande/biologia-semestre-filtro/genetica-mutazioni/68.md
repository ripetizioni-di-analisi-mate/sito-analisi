In the X-linked recessive model, a daughter is $X^aX^a$. Excluding new mutations, which paternal contribution is necessary?

- An $X^a$ chromosome.
- Either $X^A$ or $X^a$ with equal compatibility.
- An $X^A$ chromosome, because maternal a is sufficient.
- A Y chromosome carrying variant a.

## Explanation

The daughter has two X chromosomes and, in the usual model, one comes from the father. To have a on both copies, she must therefore receive $X^a$ from him too. A carrier mother alone cannot produce this genotype if the father supplies an X with A and new mutations are excluded.
