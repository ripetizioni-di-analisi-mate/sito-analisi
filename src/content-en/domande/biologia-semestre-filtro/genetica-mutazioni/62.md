Chromosome-segment order changes from A–B–C–D–E to A–D–C–B–E, without a change in amount. Which rearrangement describes the change?

- Inversion of segment B–C–D.
- Duplication of B–C–D.
- Deletion of B–C–D.
- Translocation of B–C–D to a nonhomologous chromosome.

## Explanation

Segment B–C–D occurs once in both sequences, but has reversed order D–C–B in the product. This identifies an inversion. Determining whether it is paracentric or pericentric would require knowing the centromere's position relative to the inverted segment.
