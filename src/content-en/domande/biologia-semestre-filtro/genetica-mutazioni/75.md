A variant is detected in a child but not in the parents' blood. Which limitation prevents concluding that no parental gamete can carry it?

- Every transmissible variant must be present in all somatic cells.
- Absence from blood excludes any recurrence through gametes.
- The blood result necessarily describes all germ cells.
- Possible germ-line mosaicism not represented by the blood sample.

## Explanation

The result concerns the cells and sensitivity of the tested sample. A variant confined to part of the germ line may be absent or undetectable in blood while present in some gametes. This possibility explains the logical limitation without assigning a specific clinical probability to the case.
