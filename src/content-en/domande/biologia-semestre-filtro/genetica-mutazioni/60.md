A banding karyotype is normal. Which alteration can still be present without being detected by this observation?

- A single-base substitution in a gene.
- Monosomy of an entire autosome in every observed cell.
- Complete triploidy in every observed cell.
- An entire extra chromosome 21 in every observed cell.

## Explanation

Banding compares number and structure on a scale far larger than one nucleotide. A point substitution can therefore leave the image unchanged and require sequence analysis. A normal test excludes only abnormalities detectable by that method and sample.
