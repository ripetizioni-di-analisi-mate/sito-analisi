Two cultures receive the same mutagen treatment. One repairs lesions effectively and the other has a defective repair pathway. Why can the final number of variants differ?

- Repair changes only phenotype while leaving all lesions identical.
- Initial lesions can have different probabilities of being removed or fixed as mutations.
- Every lesion is already a stable mutation at exposure.
- Equal dose always imposes the same final mutation number.

## Explanation

Exposure describes the event producing damage, whereas the outcome also depends on repair, replication and survival. A defective pathway can leave more lesions available for erroneous replication. Experimental comparisons must also control dose and growth conditions.
