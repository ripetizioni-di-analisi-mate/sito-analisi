A quantitative value depends on many loci and on diet. Which prediction is more consistent than a simple single-gene model?

- Possible continuous variation without one unique Mendelian ratio.
- No hereditary contribution because the environment is involved.
- A three-to-one phenotypic ratio independent of environment.
- One locus inferable from continuity of values alone.

## Explanation

Many genetic and environmental contributions can combine to produce a quantitative distribution. Environmental involvement does not eliminate genetic effects. A three-to-one ratio requires a specific single-locus model with complete dominance, which does not describe the stated data.
