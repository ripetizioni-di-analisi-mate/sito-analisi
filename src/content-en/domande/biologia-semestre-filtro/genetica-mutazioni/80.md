A variant makes a receptor active even without its ligand, whereas the normal receptor requires the ligand. Which functional change is described?

- Activity unchanged from the normal receptor in the absence of ligand.
- Reduced sensitivity requiring more ligand for activation.
- Gain of function through constitutive activity.
- Complete loss of receptor function.

## Explanation

The receptor acquires activity under a condition in which the normal form remains inactive. This is gain of function, here through constitutive activity. The functional finding alone does not establish the sequence change that caused it or the number of bases involved.
