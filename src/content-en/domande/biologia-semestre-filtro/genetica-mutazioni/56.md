A normal human somatic cell is in G2, before chromatid separation. How many chromosomes and nuclear DNA molecules does it contain?

- 46 chromosomes and 46 DNA molecules.
- 23 chromosomes and 46 DNA molecules.
- 46 chromosomes and 92 DNA molecules.
- 92 chromosomes and 92 DNA molecules.

## Explanation

S phase replicated each chromosome into two sister chromatids. While sisters remain joined before separation, each pair constitutes one duplicated chromosome. The chromosome count remains 46, whereas each chromatid contains one DNA molecule and the total is 92.
