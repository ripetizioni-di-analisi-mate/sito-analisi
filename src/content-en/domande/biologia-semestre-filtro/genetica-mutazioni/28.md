A rare allele produces the same phenotype in the heterozygote as in the homozygote carrying it; the other homozygote has a different phenotype. What does this observation indicate for that trait?

- Dominance of the allele, independently of its rarity.
- Recessiveness inferred from allele rarity.
- Incomplete dominance with an intermediate phenotype.
- Codominance with two distinguishable phenotypic contributions.

## Explanation

Dominance compares phenotypes of genotypes at the same locus. The heterozygote sharing the phenotype considered with one homozygote identifies dominance in that context. Population frequency and effects on fitness are different properties and require other evidence.
