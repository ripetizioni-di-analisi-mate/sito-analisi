In the Aa × aa cross, with Mendelian segregation, which genotypic distribution is expected?

- All AA.
- Three quarters Aa and one quarter aa.
- Half Aa and half aa.
- One quarter AA, half Aa and one quarter aa.

## Explanation

The aa parent always supplies a. The Aa parent supplies A or a with equal probability, producing Aa and aa respectively. AA is impossible because the aa parent cannot provide an A gamete. The one-to-two-to-one ratio belongs to a different cross, Aa × Aa.
