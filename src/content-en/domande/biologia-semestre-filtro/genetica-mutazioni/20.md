In a family with a repeat-expansion disorder, repeat number increases and onset tends to occur earlier in successive generations. Which phenomenon describes this pattern?

- Genomic imprinting.
- Incomplete penetrance.
- Incomplete dominance.
- Anticipation.

## Explanation

Anticipation describes earlier onset or greater severity in successive generations, observed in some repeat-expansion disorders. Repeat instability provides a possible mechanism. The relationship between repeat number and manifestation remains locus-specific and is not identical across all disorders.
