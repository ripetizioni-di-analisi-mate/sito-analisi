In a model, A_ prevents pigmentation; among aa individuals, B_ gives black and bb brown. In AaBb × AaBb with independent loci, what fraction lacks pigment?

- $\dfrac9{16}$
- $\dfrac{12}{16}$
- $\dfrac4{16}$
- $\dfrac1{16}$

## Explanation

All genotypes with at least one A lack pigment, whatever B is. In Aa × Aa, A_ has probability three quarters, or twelve sixteenths. The remaining aaB_ and aabb classes occupy three and one sixteenths respectively, producing the twelve-to-three-to-one ratio.
