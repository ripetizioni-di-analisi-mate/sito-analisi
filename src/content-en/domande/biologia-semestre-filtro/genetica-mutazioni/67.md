For an X-linked recessive trait in the classical model, the father is $X^aY$ and the mother $X^AX^A$. Which genotypes are expected in the daughters?

- All $X^AX^a$.
- All $X^aX^a$.
- Half $X^AX^A$ and half $X^AX^a$.
- Half $X^AX^A$ and half $X^aX^a$.

## Explanation

Every daughter receives the paternal X carrying a and a maternal X always carrying A. All daughters are therefore heterozygous. Genotype $X^AY$ instead describes a son; the father supplies Y to sons rather than daughters in this model.
