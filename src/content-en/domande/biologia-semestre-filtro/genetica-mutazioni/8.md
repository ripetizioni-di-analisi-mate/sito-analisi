In AaBb × AaBb, the loci are independent. A_B_ is purple, A_bb is red and aa__ is white. What fraction of individuals is expected to be white?

- $\dfrac4{16}$
- $\dfrac9{16}$
- $\dfrac3{16}$
- $\dfrac1{16}$

## Explanation

White requires aa, whatever the genotype at locus B. The probability of aa is $\dfrac14$, or $\dfrac4{16}$. Counting only aabb would give one sixteenth and omit aaBB and aaBb, which are also white under the stated epistasis.
