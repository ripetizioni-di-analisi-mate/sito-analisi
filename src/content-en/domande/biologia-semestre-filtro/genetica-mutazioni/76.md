For an autosomal recessive trait, two Aa parents have children with equally likely sexes and segregation independent of sex. What is the probability of aa among daughters only?

- $\dfrac12$
- $0$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac18$

## Explanation

The event is already conditioned on being a daughter. Because the locus is autosomal and segregation is independent of sex, the probability of aa remains $\dfrac14$. An additional $\dfrac12$ factor would be needed for the joint probability of an aa daughter in a pregnancy of unknown sex.
