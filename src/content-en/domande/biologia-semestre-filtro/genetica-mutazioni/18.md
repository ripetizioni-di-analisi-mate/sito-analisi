A variant changes a splice site and mature RNA lacks an exon present in the normal transcript. Which step is directly involved?

- Codon recognition by tRNA.
- Base correction during replication.
- Pre-mRNA processing.
- Homolog segregation during meiosis I.

## Explanation

Splicing selects and joins the regions retained in the mature transcript. An altered site can change exon recognition and cause exon skipping. Consequences for the frame depend on the length and position of the lost sequence, so they cannot be inferred from the word “splicing” alone.
