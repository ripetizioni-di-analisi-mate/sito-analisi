A woman inherited a variant in an imprinted gene from her father. If she transmits it to a child, what parental origin does that copy have in the child?

- Maternal.
- Maternal only if the grandmother also carried the variant.
- Paternal in sons and maternal in daughters.
- Paternal, because the grandfather’s origin remains unchanged.

## Explanation

Parental origin refers to the parent transmitting the copy in the generation considered. Germ-line marks are re-established according to the sex of the individual producing gametes. The woman's transmitted copy therefore has maternal origin in her child, even though she inherited it from her father.
