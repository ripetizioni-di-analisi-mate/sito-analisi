A selective PI3K inhibitor reduces PIP3 but leaves a measurable ERK response after insulin. Which interpretation is compatible?

- PI3K must be downstream of ERK because ERK persistence alone establishes the order of all components.
- PIP3 is an obligatory intermediate in every receptor-activated branch, including the ERK branch.
- The receptor can activate multiple branches, including Ras-MAPK.
- PIP3 amount alone permits quantitative estimation of ERK activity under every condition.

## Explanation

Insulin signalling can distribute across different branches. A decrease in the PI3K product can therefore coexist with detectable ERK signalling, depending on network context and interactions. The finding does not justify assigning every insulin effect to one messenger. ERK persistence shows that reducing one branch does not eliminate every response; it does not order the entire network or make PIP3 a universal ERK measurement.
