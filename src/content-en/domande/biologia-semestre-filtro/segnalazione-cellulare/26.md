A small cytosolic dye passes between contacting cells without being secreted into the extracellular space. Which structure can directly explain the transfer?

- A gap junction.
- Hemidesmosome.
- Adherens junction.
- Tight junction.

## Explanation

Gap junctions create channels between cytoplasms and can allow small molecules compatible with their permeability to pass. Mechanical adhesion structures do not automatically form an intercellular pore. The dye's size and properties must still be suitable for the channel. A paracellular barrier limits the external route; a mechanical attachment transmits forces. Neither provides the required cytoplasmic channel.
