Which order matches the canonical kinase cascade downstream of Ras?

- Raf → ERK → MEK.
- Raf → MEK → ERK.
- ERK → Raf → MEK.
- MEK → ERK → Raf.

## Explanation

Ras-GTP promotes Raf activation, Raf acts on MEK, and MEK activates ERK. This order predicts which responses are lost when a component is blocked and which blocks can be bypassed by a downstream component active independently of its predecessor. The comparison must preserve activation order rather than merely list the three kinases present in the pathway.
