In a model without saturation, each of 4 active receptors activates 5 enzymes and each enzyme produces 20 messengers. How many messengers are produced?

- $29$.
- $100$.
- $400$.
- $80$.

## Explanation

First, the number of active enzymes is $4\cdot5=20$. Each produces 20 messengers, giving a total of $20\cdot20=400$. The yields of the steps are multiplied because each upstream component acts on several downstream components. The result applies to the stated model without limiting factors.
