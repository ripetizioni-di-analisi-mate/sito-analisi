Which substrate does adenylyl cyclase use to produce cAMP?

- ADP.
- ATP.
- Non-cyclic AMP as the sole substrate of the canonical reaction.
- GTP.

## Explanation

Adenylyl cyclase produces cyclic adenosine monophosphate from ATP. GTP instead serves as the substrate for cGMP production by guanylyl cyclase. Distinguishing adenosine from guanosine keeps enzyme, substrate and messenger consistent. Adenine in ADP or AMP is insufficient: ATP is the substrate of the canonical cyclisation reaction.
