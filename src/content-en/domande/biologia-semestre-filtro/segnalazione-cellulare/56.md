Soluble guanylyl cyclase can no longer bind NO through its haem group. With NO availability unchanged, which direct response is impaired?

- Increased cAMP in response to a Gs-coupled GPCR.
- IP3 and DAG production in response to PLC activation.
- Increased cGMP in response to a natriuretic peptide activating its own membrane receptor cyclase.
- The NO-dependent increase in cGMP.

## Explanation

The defect affects NO recognition by the enzyme that produces cGMP. The ligand may reach the cell but cannot normally activate that target. The defect concerns NO recognition by soluble cyclase; sharing the product cGMP does not mean sharing the natriuretic-peptide receptor.
