A Ras variant reduces GTP hydrolysis without preventing GTP binding. Which alteration is most directly favoured?

- Ras-GDP accumulation, interpreting hydrolysis as the process forming GTP.
- Unchanged termination upon ligand dissociation, independently of the nucleotide still bound.
- Loss of the active state because GTP hydrolysis would be required to bind each effector.
- Persistence of the Ras-GTP state.

## Explanation

Hydrolysis is one of the steps returning Ras to the GDP-bound state. Reducing it can prolong the active GTP-bound state if the other requirements are met. The GTP-bound conformation enables effector interactions; hydrolysis promotes termination, so reducing it prolongs this state.
