Which combination is typically required for activation of conventional PKCs in the studied model?

- DAG without any calcium requirement, as in novel PKCs.
- DAG and calcium, with the appropriate membrane requirements.
- Calcium without DAG as the complete and sufficient condition in the model.
- cAMP alone, confusing PKC with PKA.

## Explanation

Conventional PKCs respond to DAG and calcium in the appropriate lipid context. The qualifier conventional correctly limits the statement, because other PKC isoforms have different requirements. Applying the same activators to every PKC would introduce an error. Novel PKCs respond to DAG without the same calcium requirement; cAMP instead regulates canonical PKA.
