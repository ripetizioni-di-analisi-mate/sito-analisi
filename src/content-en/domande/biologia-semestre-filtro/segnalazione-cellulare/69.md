In the EGFR–Grb2–SOS–Ras connection, which component acts as the Ras GEF?

- ERK.
- Grb2.
- SOS.
- Raf.

## Explanation

SOS promotes GDP–GTP exchange on Ras, increasing the Ras-GTP fraction. Grb2 helps connect to the receptor complex as an adaptor, whereas ERK is a downstream kinase. Distinguishing adaptor, GEF and kinase avoids assigning the same activity to all components. Grb2 is an adaptor; Raf and ERK are cascade kinases. Nucleotide exchange distinguishes SOS from those other components.
