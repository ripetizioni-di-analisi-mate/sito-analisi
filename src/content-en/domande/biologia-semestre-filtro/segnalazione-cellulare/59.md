An intracellular receptor directly activates an enzymatic pathway without binding DNA. Which example is compatible?

- A steroid receptor acting directly as a DNA-bound transcription factor.
- Soluble guanylyl cyclase activated by NO.
- A nicotinic receptor recognising acetylcholine outside the cell.
- An EGF receptor binding ligand on the extracellular side.

## Explanation

Target location and response type are separate criteria. Soluble guanylyl cyclase recognises NO and produces cGMP, initiating an enzymatic pathway. This shows why the term intracellular receptor does not always imply DNA binding or direct transcriptional control. The other alternatives describe direct transcriptional control or surface receptors, whereas NO recognises an intracellular enzyme.
