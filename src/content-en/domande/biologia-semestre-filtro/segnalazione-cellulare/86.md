A messenger is produced at $30\,\mathrm{pmol/min}$ and removed at $18\,\mathrm{pmol/min}$. If the rates remain constant for 2 minutes, by how much does its amount increase?

- $96\,\mathrm{pmol}$.
- $60\,\mathrm{pmol}$.
- $24\,\mathrm{pmol}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{pmol}$.

## Explanation

The net rate is $30-18=12\,\mathrm{pmol/min}$. Over 2 minutes, the increase is $12\cdot2=24\,\mathrm{pmol}$. Adding the two rates would treat removal as a second source; using production alone would ignore the stated degradation.
