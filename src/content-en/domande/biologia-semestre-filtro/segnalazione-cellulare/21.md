Cells of the same type are grown on surfaces with equal fibronectin but different stiffness. A change in FAK activity suggests which connection?

- Between substrate mechanics and adhesion signalling.
- Between different fibronectin concentrations and FAK activity, despite equal ligand levels.
- Between stiffness and obligatory abolition of every integrin–matrix interaction.
- Between FAK activity and signalling independent of every cytoskeletal connection.

## Explanation

The ligand is kept equal while a mechanical property changes. A difference in adhesion signalling is therefore consistent with mechanotransduction. Controls for other variables are required to attribute causality to stiffness. Controlling fibronectin separates mechanical properties from ligand amount; it does not impose a universal loss-of-adhesion effect.
