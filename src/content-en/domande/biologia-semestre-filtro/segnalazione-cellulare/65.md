A rod's cation channels remain open even when cGMP decreases. Which effect of the light cascade is directly hindered?

- Retinal isomerisation, necessarily blocked.
- Reduction of inward current and the resulting hyperpolarisation.
- PDE6-mediated cGMP degradation, necessarily abolished.
- Initial transducin activation, necessarily prevented.

## Explanation

The defect lies in the link between messenger and channel. The upstream cascade may lower cGMP, but the channel does not convert that change into closure. Inward current persists, hindering the component of hyperpolarisation caused by its reduction. The perturbation affects downstream electrical execution; it does not necessarily disable steps preceding the cGMP decrease.
