A cell produces IP3 normally, but its reticular IP3 receptors do not open. Which direct response is reduced?

- Direct PKA activation by cAMP.
- IP3-mediated calcium release from the reticulum.
- Opening of plasma-membrane voltage-gated calcium channels in response to depolarisation.
- DAG production during phospholipase-C cleavage of PIP2.

## Explanation

The defect lies in the IP3 target channel, downstream of messenger production. Even with IP3 present, opening and calcium flow through that channel are reduced. The finding does not require inhibition of the upstream reaction that generates IP3. The IP3 receptor controls release from the reticulum; the defect does not directly establish loss of the cAMP pathway, voltage-gated channels or upstream DAG production.
