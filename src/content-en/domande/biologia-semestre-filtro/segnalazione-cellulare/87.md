In the model $f=\dfrac{[L]}{K_d+[L]}$, where $f$ is the occupied receptor fraction, what is $f$ when $[L]=K_d$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$.
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- $1$.
- $2$.

## Explanation

Substituting $[L]=K_d$ gives $f=\dfrac{K_d}{2K_d}=\dfrac{1}{2}$, with $K_d>0$. The model describes simple equilibrium binding; the fraction lies between zero and one. Half occupancy does not automatically imply half the maximal biological response.
