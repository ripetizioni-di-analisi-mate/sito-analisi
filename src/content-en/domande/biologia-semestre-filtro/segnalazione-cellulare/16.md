One fibronectin domain binds an integrin and another interacts with the matrix. Which function does this arrangement support?

- A continuous channel directly joining two cytoplasms.
- Adhesive connection between cell and matrix.
- Direct fibronectin binding to actin across the bilayer, without integrins.
- A paracellular barrier made exclusively of fibronectin.

## Explanation

Different domains allow the same molecule to interact with a cell receptor and extracellular components. Fibronectin can therefore contribute to an adhesive connection. The connection crosses the membrane through integrin; extracellular fibronectin replaces neither a gap-junction channel nor a tight junction.
