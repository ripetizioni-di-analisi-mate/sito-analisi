A scaffold protein brings together three kinases in the same cascade. Which effect can it directly favour?

- Continuous activation of all three kinases even without any regulatory signals.
- Selective encounters between pathway components.
- Loss of selectivity because every scaffold necessarily mixes components from all pathways.
- A direct increase in synthesis of all three kinases as a consequence of their binding to the scaffold alone.

## Explanation

Spatial organisation increases the likelihood that appropriate components interact in the correct compartment. A scaffold can therefore contribute to cascade specificity. This is an organisational role and does not automatically imply catalysis or amplification under every condition. Bringing components together changes interaction probabilities; it does not equal synthesising them or keeping them active without regulation.
