A steroid effect requires new synthesis of a target protein. Which treatment can prevent it while preserving hormone-receptor binding?

- Inhibition of target-protein degradation while leaving its synthesis intact.
- Stimulation of target-gene transcription while keeping translation functional.
- Inhibition of translation of the new protein.
- Increased stability of the corresponding mRNA without blocking the ribosome.

## Explanation

Receptor binding precedes production of the required protein. If translation is blocked, the new product does not accumulate even when the receptor recognises the hormone normally. This separates signal reception from execution of a response requiring protein synthesis. Stabilising mRNA or protein does not itself prevent the required production, and promoting transcription does not block translation. The necessary step identified by the finding is synthesis of the new protein.
