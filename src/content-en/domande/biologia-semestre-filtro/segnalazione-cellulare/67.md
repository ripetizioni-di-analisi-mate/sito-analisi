A response decreases during constant ligand exposure while cell viability remains normal. Which further measurement most directly tests GPCR uncoupling?

- Total receptor number alone, without measuring coupling.
- Extracellular ligand concentration alone, already held constant.
- Only a terminal kinase's activity, which may also change because of downstream defects.
- G-protein activation after receptor stimulation.

## Explanation

Uncoupling concerns the receptor's ability to activate the G protein. Measuring this step tests the mechanism more directly than a distant endpoint. Normal viability excludes a general explanation based on cell loss, but does not by itself identify the cause of desensitisation. A present receptor may be uncoupled; a reduced final effector alone does not locate the defect at receptor–G-protein coupling.
