A cell binds fibronectin through integrins but transmits little force to its substrate after losing talin. Which step is impaired?

- Availability of the extracellular fibronectin site, which would be completely abolished.
- Connection of the adhesion complex to actin.
- The connection of desmosomal cadherins to intermediate filaments.
- Tight-junction sealing as the sole matrix attachment.

## Explanation

Ligand binding remains detectable, so the defect need not lie in fibronectin recognition. Talin helps connect integrins to actin. Weakening this connection can reduce force transmission while leaving receptors that can still bind the matrix. Extracellular binding is stated to persist; desmosomes and tight junctions serve different functions from integrin-actin anchoring.
