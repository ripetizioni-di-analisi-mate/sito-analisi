A secreted factor activates a receptor on the same cell that produced it. Which mode is described?

- Paracrine, with targets exclusively different from the signalling cell.
- Autocrine.
- Endocrine because every secreted factor must enter the blood.
- Contact-dependent, with ligand necessarily tethered to the membrane.

## Explanation

The producing cell is also a target of the signal, making the communication autocrine. The term describes the relationship between source and target. It requires neither blood transport nor a specialised synaptic junction, although other targets may also respond to the same factor. Secretion alone does not define the mode: here, the producer also being the target identifies autocrine signalling.
