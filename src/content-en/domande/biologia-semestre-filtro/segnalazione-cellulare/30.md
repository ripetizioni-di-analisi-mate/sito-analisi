A hormone travels in the blood and reaches many tissues, but only some respond. Which explanation is most appropriate?

- Every exposed cell must respond identically regardless of receptors.
- Blood hormone amount alone determines response without intracellular components.
- Presence of the same hormone forces every tissue to express the same receptor.
- Targets must have appropriate receptors and response machinery.

## Explanation

Ligand distribution is not the same as the ability to respond. The receptor enables recognition, and intracellular components determine transduction. A living cell can therefore encounter the hormone without producing the measured response if one of these elements is missing. Endocrine distribution may be broad while recognition and response networks remain cell-type specific.
