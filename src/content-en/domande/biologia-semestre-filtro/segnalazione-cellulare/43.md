A Gi-coupled GPCR inhibits the adenylyl cyclase present in the cell. With cAMP degradation unchanged, which trend follows?

- Increased cAMP production, assigning Gi the same effect as Gs.
- Unchanged cAMP production because the cyclase would regulate only messenger degradation.
- Instant removal of existing cAMP, as if inhibiting synthesis were equivalent to removing it.
- Reduced cAMP production.

## Explanation

The stated effector is adenylyl cyclase, which synthesises cAMP. Inhibiting it reduces messenger production. The direction depends on the specified coupling: assigning the stimulatory Gs effect to every GPCR would give the opposite prediction. Gi reduces synthesis through the cyclase. Reduced production does not instantly eliminate existing messenger, whose fate also depends on degradation.
