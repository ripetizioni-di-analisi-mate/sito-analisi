A cell with active Ras shows phosphorylated ERK even without EGF. Which conclusion is supported without adding further data?

- EGF binding remains obligatory for any observed ERK activation.
- The cell is necessarily already fully malignantly transformed.
- The studied branch can be activated downstream of the receptor without that ligand.
- Phosphorylated ERK proves that the EGF receptor is the only active component.

## Explanation

Ras lies downstream of the receptor and can sustain cascade activation when active independently of ligand. The finding establishes the behaviour of the measured branch. It does not establish every property of tumour transformation, which requires information about other cellular controls. The marker informs about the measured branch; it establishes neither residual EGF dependence nor every property of transformation.
