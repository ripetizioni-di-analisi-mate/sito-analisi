An epithelium maintains distinct apical and basolateral domains. Which structure helps restrict lateral mixing of membrane components between them?

- Tight junction.
- Gap junction.
- Desmosome.
- Hemidesmosome.

## Explanation

Tight junctions contribute both to the paracellular barrier and to separation of membrane domains. These functions support epithelial polarity. Communicating channels and mechanical attachments have other functions; domain separation is the tight junction's lateral barrier role.
