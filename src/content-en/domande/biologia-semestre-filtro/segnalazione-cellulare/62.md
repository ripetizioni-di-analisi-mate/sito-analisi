Which initial event links light absorption to rhodopsin activation?

- Direct PDE6 activation by the photon without rhodopsin or transducin.
- An initial cGMP increase activating rhodopsin.
- A change in retinal configuration.
- Initial cation-channel opening as the cause of photon absorption.

## Explanation

Retinal is the chromophore associated with opsin. Light absorption changes its configuration and induces the active receptor state. The transducin-PDE6 cascade follows this event and can use existing proteins without waiting for new synthesis. The photophysical event precedes the cascade: PDE6, cGMP and channels are subsequent steps, not the initial light-recognition event.
