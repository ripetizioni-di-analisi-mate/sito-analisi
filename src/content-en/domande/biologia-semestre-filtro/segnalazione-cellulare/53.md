A steroid hormone increases gene expression through an intracellular receptor. Which event directly belongs to this mechanism?

- Transcriptional regulation by the receptor complex and its cofactors.
- Increased translation of pre-existing mRNAs without changing gene transcription.
- Activation of an existing enzyme through a surface receptor, without acting on the gene.
- Slower target-protein degradation as a direct activity of the receptor–DNA complex.

## Explanation

The receptor recognises the hormone and contributes to transcriptional control through DNA and cofactors. The response therefore also depends on chromatin context and expressed proteins. Protein amount can change at several levels, but the described genomic mechanism involves transcriptional control, distinct from translation, protein stability and rapid surface effects.
