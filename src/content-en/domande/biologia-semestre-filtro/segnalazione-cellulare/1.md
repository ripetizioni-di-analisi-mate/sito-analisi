A cartilage sample loses proteoglycans while its collagen network initially remains intact. Which consequence is most directly expected?

- Greater water retention because negative charges decrease.
- Unchanged compression resistance because it depends exclusively on collagen.
- Reduced water retention and resistance to compression.
- Obligatory loss of tensile strength without effects on hydration.

## Explanation

Glycosaminoglycan chains carry negative charges and help retain water. Their loss changes the hydrated gel that resists compression. The finding therefore concerns a function distinct from the tensile strength provided by collagen fibres, which the question states are initially preserved. Preserved collagen does not replace the hydrated gel's function, and fewer fixed charges do not imply greater water retention.
