Two samples contain equal total GLUT4. Only one increases glucose uptake after insulin. Which measurement best tests a translocation defect in the other?

- Total GLUT4 amount alone, already found to be equal.
- The fraction of GLUT4 exposed at the membrane before and after signalling.
- GLUT4 mRNA alone, without determining protein localisation.
- ERK activity alone, without measuring surface transporters.

## Explanation

Total amount does not describe distribution between vesicles and surface. Measuring the exposed fraction directly tests the proposed trafficking step. A normal result would then point towards other defects, such as transporter function or subsequent metabolic steps. The suspected defect concerns protein redistribution; mRNA, total content and another signalling branch do not directly measure that trafficking.
