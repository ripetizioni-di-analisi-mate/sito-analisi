Grb2 is removed from a model in which it is the only link between EGFR and SOS. Which event is directly reduced after EGF?

- Initial extracellular EGF binding to its receptor.
- Ras GTP hydrolysis accelerated by a functioning GAP.
- Recruitment of SOS to the receptor complex.
- PIP3 recognition by the Akt PH domain at equal available PIP3.

## Explanation

The sole-link assumption makes Grb2 necessary for the stated recruitment. Its removal separates the activated receptor from SOS and may reduce Ras activation. This transmission defect does not automatically imply that EGF can no longer bind the receptor. In the model, Grb2 connects EGFR to SOS; removing it interrupts GEF recruitment without directly establishing defects in ligand binding, a GAP or a PH domain.
