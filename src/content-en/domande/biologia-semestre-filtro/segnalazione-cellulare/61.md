After prolonged stimulation, both surface and total receptor numbers decrease. Which fate is compatible with the second finding?

- Redistribution alone without any net protein loss.
- Synthesis exceeding degradation as the sole explanation of the reduced total.
- Simple G-protein uncoupling with receptor abundance necessarily unchanged.
- Degradation of some receptors.

## Explanation

Internalisation alone can lower surface receptors while preserving the total. A decrease in total number instead requires net protein loss, consistent with degradation exceeding replacement. The two measurements therefore distinguish redistribution from a change in overall amount. Uncoupling and internalisation can reduce response without lowering the total; the second finding instead requires a negative protein balance.
