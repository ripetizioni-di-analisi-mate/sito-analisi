Why does opening a cation channel not by itself determine the exact current?

- Current also depends on electrochemical gradients and membrane potential.
- Current depends only on channel number, independently of potential.
- Every open cation channel necessarily produces inward current at every potential.
- Opening guarantees the same current even when ion concentrations change.

## Explanation

Opening provides a passage, but the force driving ions depends on concentrations and the electric field. The same channel can therefore produce different currents at different potentials. Conductance and electrochemical driving force are distinct; near reversal potential, net flux may fall even with open channels.
