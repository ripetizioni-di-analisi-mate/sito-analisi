A ligand remains tethered to cell A's membrane and activates cell B's receptor only when the surfaces touch. Which mode is described?

- Contact-dependent signalling.
- Paracrine diffusion of a free ligand.
- Direct ligand transfer between cytoplasms through a gap junction.
- Endocrine signalling through blood.

## Explanation

Tethering prevents the ligand from freely diffusing to distant targets. Contact between the surfaces allows it to meet the receptor. This geometry defines the mechanism; secretion or transport through the circulation need not be assumed. Recognition occurs between surfaces; it is not cytoplasmic transfer through a communicating channel.
