A response returns to baseline after ligand removal while receptor number stays constant. Which mechanism can explain the recovery?

- Removal of second messengers and inactivation of cascade proteins.
- Ligand dissociation alone, even if downstream second messengers and effectors remain active.
- G-protein deactivation alone, even if the messenger controlling the response is not removed.
- Receptor return to the surface alone while cascade activity remains high.

## Explanation

Termination can occur through GTP hydrolysis, target dephosphorylation and messenger removal. These processes allow recovery without reducing receptor number. The finding therefore shows that response termination and receptor degradation are distinguishable processes. Interrupting one step or changing trafficking does not guarantee recovery if downstream signalling persists; return to baseline requires termination of the steps maintaining the response.
