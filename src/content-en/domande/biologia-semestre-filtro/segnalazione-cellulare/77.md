An RTK binds ligand normally, but its kinase domain is inactive. Which initial downstream event is directly impaired?

- Ligand recognition by the extracellular domain.
- Hydrolysis of GTP already bound to functioning Ras through its GTPase activity.
- Binding of a protein to an already phosphorylated peptide supplied separately from the receptor.
- Phosphorylation of appropriate substrates and formation of kinase-dependent recruitment sites.

## Explanation

Ligand recognition is preserved, but the activity transferring phosphates to targets is missing. Connections dependent on those sites are therefore impaired. This separates the extracellular binding domain from the catalytic function transmitting the signal inside. The inactive domain fails to generate required downstream phosphorylation signals. It does not eliminate preserved external binding or other proteins' ability to recognise an existing phosphate.
