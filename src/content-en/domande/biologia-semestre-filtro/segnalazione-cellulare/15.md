Which pair correctly matches a matrix component to its predominant function?

- Elastin, reversible elastic deformation.
- Collagen, the principal hydrated gel formed by glycosaminoglycan chains.
- Proteoglycans, the main fibrillar network resisting tension.
- Fibronectin, the main component responsible for fibre recoil.

## Explanation

Elastin contributes to structural recoil after deformation. Collagen is primarily associated with tensile strength, proteoglycans with hydration, and fibronectin with adhesive interactions. The other pairs exchange matrix functions: hydration for proteoglycans, tension resistance for collagen and adhesive interactions for fibronectin.
