Which pair correctly distinguishes an adherens junction from a desmosome?

- Actin in the first, intermediate filaments in the second.
- Intermediate filaments in the first, actin in the second.
- Actin in both, without any intermediate-filament connection.
- Intermediate filaments in both, without any actin connection.

## Explanation

Both structures can use cadherins for cell-cell adhesion, but they connect to different cytoskeletal systems. An adherens junction is associated with actin; a desmosome with intermediate filaments. Identifying the filament distinguishes structures that share an adhesive function. The adhesion receptor alone is insufficient: the connected filament type separates the two cell–cell attachments.
