In which tissues is insulin-dependent GLUT4 translocation a classic example of a rapid response?

- Hepatocytes and pancreatic beta cells as classic sites of insulin-driven GLUT4 translocation.
- Red blood cells and blood–brain barrier endothelium as classic sites of insulin-dependent GLUT4.
- Skeletal muscle and adipose tissue.
- Enterocytes and proximal renal tubular cells as sites of the same GLUT4 translocation.

## Explanation

Skeletal muscle and adipocytes have trafficking systems that increase surface GLUT4 after insulin signalling. This example concerns particular cells and tissues. It does not automatically extend to every mode of glucose transport in every organ. The other tissues predominantly use different glucose systems; intestinal and renal absorption also involves sodium-dependent cotransporters.
