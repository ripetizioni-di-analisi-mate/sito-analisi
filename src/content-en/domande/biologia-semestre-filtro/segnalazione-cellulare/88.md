A control produces 80 response units; a treatment produces 20. What is the percentage reduction relative to control?

- $25\%$.
- $75\%$.
- $60\%$.
- $400\%$.

## Explanation

The decrease is $80-20=60$ units. Relative to control, this is $\dfrac{60}{80}\cdot100\%=75\%$. The value $25\%$ instead describes the remaining response. Distinguishing the amount remaining from the amount lost avoids reversing the interpretation of inhibition.
