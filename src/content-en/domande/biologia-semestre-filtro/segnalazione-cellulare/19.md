Which composition identifies a typical membrane integrin?

- Two identical alpha subunits without a beta subunit.
- Three alpha, beta and gamma subunits as in a G protein.
- Six connexins organised into a hemichannel.
- One alpha and one beta subunit.

## Explanation

Integrins are alpha-beta heterodimers. Subunit combinations contribute to ligand specificity and adhesive functions. A connexon instead forms part of a gap junction and is not the receptor that connects a cell to the matrix. The alpha-beta pair distinguishes an integrin from both the G-protein trimer and the connexon hexamer.
