A neurotransmitter is released into the cleft of a specialised junction and acts on the postsynaptic membrane. Which mode is described?

- Contact-dependent because the neurotransmitter must remain tethered to the presynaptic membrane.
- Autocrine because the target must be the signalling cell.
- Synaptic.
- Endocrine because every chemical signal travels through blood.

## Explanation

The specialised release site and postsynaptic membrane identify synaptic communication. Other modes also use chemical messengers, so chemical nature alone does not distinguish them. Here, the signal crosses a short cleft organised for neural communication. At a chemical synapse, the messenger is released into the cleft; the specialised junction distinguishes this mode from generic local diffusion.
