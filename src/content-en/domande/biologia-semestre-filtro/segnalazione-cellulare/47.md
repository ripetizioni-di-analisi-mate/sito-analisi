A cAMP-degrading phosphodiesterase is inhibited while messenger production continues. Which change is favoured?

- Decreased cAMP, confusing inhibition of degradation with inhibition of synthesis.
- Unchanged concentration, assuming synthesis rate alone determines messenger level.
- A shorter peak, assigning phosphodiesterase a protective role against cAMP degradation.
- Greater cAMP accumulation or persistence.

## Explanation

With synthesis continuing, reducing a removal route favours messenger accumulation or prolongs its duration. The prediction uses the production-removal balance. The quantitative result also depends on other degradation routes and regulatory mechanisms. A phosphodiesterase catalyses messenger removal; inhibiting it reduces an outgoing balance term without itself blocking synthesis.
