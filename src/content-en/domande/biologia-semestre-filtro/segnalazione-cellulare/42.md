Which statement about the Gβγ complex is correct?

- It has only a structural role and does not participate in enzyme or channel regulation.
- It can regulate intracellular effectors.
- It contains the site exchanging GDP for GTP during trimer activation.
- It must split into isolated beta and gamma subunits to regulate an effector.

## Explanation

After heterotrimeric G-protein activation, Gβγ can also interact with effectors. Assigning all signalling to the alpha subunit would omit this branch. The nucleotide-binding site is in Gα; beta and gamma operate as a complex that can regulate effectors without splitting into individual subunits.
