A selective MEK inhibitor reduces phosphorylated ERK while Ras-GTP stays high. Which block location is consistent?

- Upstream of Ras, with obligatory loss of Ras-GTP.
- Downstream of Ras and upstream of ERK.
- Downstream of ERK, without any direct effect on its phosphorylation.
- Only in ligand binding to the receptor, with every intracellular step intact.

## Explanation

Ras-GTP measurement shows that upstream activation persists. Loss of phosphorylated ERK places the defect in subsequent transmission, consistent with MEK. Blocking a downstream step does not require every upstream component to be switched off as well. High Ras-GTP with low phosphorylated ERK separates upstream activation from the interrupted kinase step.
