Which measurement is most directly consistent with a rod hyperpolarising in response to light?

- The potential changes from $-40$ to $-65\,\mathrm{mV}$.
- The potential changes from $-40$ to $-20\,\mathrm{mV}$.
- The potential changes from $-65$ to $-40\,\mathrm{mV}$.
- The potential stays at $-40\,\mathrm{mV}$.

## Explanation

Hyperpolarisation means that the internal potential becomes more negative relative to its initial value. The specified change is $-65-(-40)=-25\,\mathrm{mV}$. The reverse change makes the membrane less negative and describes depolarisation. A less negative value describes depolarisation; a constant value describes no potential change.
