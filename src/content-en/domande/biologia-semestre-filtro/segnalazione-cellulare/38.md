Messenger concentration remains stable while synthesis and degradation continue at equal rates. How is this interpreted?

- A dynamic steady state.
- Chemical equilibrium established by the constant concentration alone.
- Progressive accumulation obtained by adding synthesis and degradation rates.
- Progressive decline because any degradation implies a net loss.

## Explanation

Concentration depends on the difference between production and removal rates. If these rates are equal, net change is zero even though molecules continue to be produced and degraded. A constant value therefore does not establish that messenger metabolism has stopped. Removal is subtracted from production. A steady state with turnover does not alone establish chemical equilibrium; a cellular system may sustain continuous fluxes with material and energy inputs.
