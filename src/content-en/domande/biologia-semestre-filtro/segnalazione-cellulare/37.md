In a pathway A → B → C, a selective B inhibitor abolishes the C response even when A is active. Which conclusion is supported in this linear model?

- B is required to transmit that signal from A to C.
- The finding proves that activating B is sufficient for the response without checking other requirements.
- The result proves that A cannot be activated when B is inhibited.
- The result identifies direct physical binding between A and C.

## Explanation

The signal starts at A but does not reach C when B is blocked. B is therefore required for transmission along the stated route. The result concerns this pathway and response; it does not show that B is required for every cellular function. Necessity and sufficiency are different conclusions: the blockade shows that B is needed along the stated route, but does not establish physical binding or all conditions sufficient for the response.
