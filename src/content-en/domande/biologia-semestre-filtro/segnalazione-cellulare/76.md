Why is ERK phosphorylation often measured after EGF stimulation?

- It can indicate MAPK-branch activation in a controlled experimental context.
- It directly measures EGFR affinity for EGF.
- It determines total EGFR number from phosphorylated-ERK signal intensity alone.
- It proves that branch activation is sufficient for proliferation in every context.

## Explanation

ERK is a downstream cascade component, and its phosphorylation state can inform on branch activation. Proliferation is a more complex outcome dependent on other signals and controls. A pathway marker therefore does not provide a complete prediction of cell fate. Receptor affinity and abundance require their own measurements; a downstream signal may also depend on amplification, feedback and the state of intermediate components.
