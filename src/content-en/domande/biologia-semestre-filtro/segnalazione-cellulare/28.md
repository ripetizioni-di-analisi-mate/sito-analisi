A mutation alters desmoglein and weakens adhesion between cells under tension. Which complex is directly involved?

- An E-cadherin–actin adherens junction.
- An integrin-based hemidesmosome.
- An integrin–actin focal adhesion.
- Desmosome.

## Explanation

Desmogleins are specialised cadherins in desmosomes. These complexes connect intermediate filaments in adjacent cells and distribute mechanical forces through the tissue. Hemidesmosomes also associate with intermediate filaments, but anchor a cell to the matrix through integrins. Desmoglein identifies the desmosomal cadherin family; resistance to force in general does not by itself distinguish every attachment.
