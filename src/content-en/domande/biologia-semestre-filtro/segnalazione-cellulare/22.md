Which connection characterises an epithelial hemidesmosome?

- Integrins, actin and matrix, as in a focal adhesion.
- Cadherins, actin and an adjacent cell.
- Connexons and two cytoplasms.
- Integrins, intermediate filaments and basal lamina.

## Explanation

A hemidesmosome anchors the cell to the basal lamina. Specific integrins participate in external binding, and adaptor proteins connect the complex to intermediate filaments. A cadherin-actin connection between cells instead belongs to adherens junctions. Focal adhesions share matrix attachment but use actin; intermediate filaments distinguish the hemidesmosome in this comparison.
