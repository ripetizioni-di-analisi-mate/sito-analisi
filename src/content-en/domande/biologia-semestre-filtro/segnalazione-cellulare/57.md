Which observation directly distinguishes a ligand-gated receptor-channel from a receptor that modifies a channel through a cascade?

- The ligand is present in extracellular fluid.
- The response is rapid, without establishing which intermediates are required.
- The final current crosses a channel even if the ligand binds a separate GPCR.
- The ligand binds the same receptor complex that forms the regulated pore.

## Explanation

In a receptor-channel, recognition and ion conduction are functions of the same receptor complex. In a metabotropic cascade, receptor and channel are linked by intermediates. A metabotropic pathway may also be rapid and end at a channel; the distinction concerns the identity of the ligand-recognising complex.
