After calcium release from the reticulum, SERCA takes the ion back up. What function does this step serve?

- It directly expels calcium from cytosol into extracellular space.
- It opens a passive channel to release more calcium from the reticulum.
- It eliminates IP3 through degradation without transporting ions.
- It lowers cytosolic calcium and replenishes the reticular store.

## Explanation

SERCA uses energy to transport calcium from the cytosol into the reticulum. This contributes both to termination of the cytosolic signal and to replenishment of the store for subsequent responses. Restoration of the gradient is therefore part of reversible signalling. Reticular uptake, extracellular extrusion and messenger degradation can contribute to recovery, but they are different steps.
