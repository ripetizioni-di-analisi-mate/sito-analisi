Which transformation is catalysed by class I PI3K in the studied insulin pathway?

- GTP to cGMP.
- PIP2 to PIP3 by phosphorylation.
- PIP2 to IP3 and DAG by cleavage.
- ATP to cAMP.

## Explanation

PI3K adds a phosphate to the phosphoinositide head group and generates PIP3. The product remains in the membrane and can recruit proteins. Cleavage that releases IP3 and leaves DAG is instead catalysed by phospholipase C, so the reactions have different products and spatial consequences.
