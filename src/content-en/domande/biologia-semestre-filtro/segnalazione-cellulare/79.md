What role do proteins such as IRS play in insulin signalling?

- Transporters moving glucose directly across the membrane.
- Lipid phosphatases directly converting PIP3 to PIP2.
- Substrates that, after phosphorylation, help recruit downstream components.
- Alternative extracellular receptors binding insulin instead of its RTK.

## Explanation

IRS links receptor activity to proteins in the intracellular network. Phosphorylation creates interactions that support recruitment, including the PI3K branch. Its function is therefore signal transmission and organisation, distinct from glucose transport across the membrane. Transport belongs to GLUT4 and PIP3–PIP2 conversion to PTEN; IRS is an intracellular substrate organising downstream connections.
