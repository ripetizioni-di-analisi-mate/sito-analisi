A cell contains 1000 total GLUT4 transporters; surface transporters increase from 100 to 300 without a change in the total. Which description is correct?

- The surface fraction increases by $20\%$ relative to its initial value.
- The surface fraction increases by 200 percentage points.
- The intracellular fraction falls from $30\%$ to $10\%$.
- The surface fraction rises from $10\%$ to $30\%$.

## Explanation

The fractions are $\dfrac{100}{1000}=10\%$ and $\dfrac{300}{1000}=30\%$. The surface number triples, but the total stays constant. The surface fraction rises by 20 percentage points, which differs from the relative increase in the exposed number. The relative increase is $\frac{300-100}{100}\cdot100\%=200\%$; the intracellular fraction instead falls from $90\%$ to $70\%$. Percentage points, relative change and measured compartment must be distinguished.
