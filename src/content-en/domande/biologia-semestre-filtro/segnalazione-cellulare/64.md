In a rod model, transducin cannot activate PDE6. Which immediate consequence of light is reduced?

- Initial retinal isomerisation following photon absorption.
- The cascade-mediated decrease in cGMP.
- Initial transducin activation by rhodopsin.
- Guanylyl-cyclase synthesis of cGMP from GTP.

## Explanation

Transducin lies upstream of PDE6. If it cannot activate the phosphodiesterase, the light-induced degradation of cGMP is reduced. Failure to activate PDE6 reduces cGMP degradation; it does not imply blockade of initial steps and is not equivalent to inhibiting the cyclase synthesising the nucleotide.
