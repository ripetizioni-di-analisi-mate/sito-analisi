Two cells have the same receptor, but one modifies a metabolic enzyme and the other activates a gene. Which difference can explain the outcomes?

- The same receptor forces every cell to possess identical effectors.
- Response outcome depends only on the ligand, without intracellular contributions.
- Different intracellular effectors and regulators.
- Different outcomes always require a receptor mutation.

## Explanation

The receptor initiates the response, but downstream components determine its effects. One cell may express different enzymes or regulatory factors from the other. Sharing a receptor therefore does not imply identical intracellular networks or require mutations. Changing the downstream context is enough to allow different outcomes without postulating mutated receptors.
