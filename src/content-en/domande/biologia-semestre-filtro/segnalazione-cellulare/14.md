After a few minutes of insulin stimulation, a muscle cell displays more GLUT4 while its total GLUT4 content remains unchanged. Which process explains the finding?

- New GLUT4 synthesis as the sole explanation for an unchanged total.
- Translocation of existing transporters to the membrane.
- Redistribution from the surface to intracellular vesicles.
- Increased activity of each transporter without any localisation change.

## Explanation

Unchanged total content indicates redistribution, consistent with translocation of GLUT4-containing vesicles. More transporters are exposed without requiring new synthesis during the observation period. The finding therefore distinguishes altered trafficking from an increase in total protein amount. The observation concerns exposed number: altered activity per molecule alone does not explain the measured increase in surface transporters.
