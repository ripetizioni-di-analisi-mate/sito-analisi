A GAP increases GTP hydrolysis on a functional G protein. Which state is favoured?

- The GDP-bound state.
- A GDP-free state through accelerated exchange with GTP.
- A stabilised GTP state through inhibited hydrolysis.
- A GTP-bound state through direct phosphate addition to GDP.

## Explanation

Hydrolysis converts bound GTP to GDP and inorganic phosphate. Accelerating this step favours return to the GDP-bound state, associated with termination in this cycle. Exchange that enables new GTP binding is instead promoted by a GEF. Exchange and hydrolysis have different roles: a GAP accelerates the latter and does not act as a GDP kinase.
