An inactive heterotrimeric G protein contains GDP-bound Gα. Which other subunits belong to the canonical complex?

- Gβ and Gγ.
- Two additional Gα subunits.
- One Ras subunit and one Gβ as the obligatory composition.
- A GEF and a GAP as permanent structural subunits.

## Explanation

The heterotrimeric complex contains alpha, beta and gamma. The guanine nucleotide is bound to the alpha subunit, while beta and gamma form a complex that can also participate in signalling. Ras is instead an example of a monomeric G protein. GEFs and GAPs regulate the cycle, whereas alpha, beta and gamma identify the trimer's own subunits.
