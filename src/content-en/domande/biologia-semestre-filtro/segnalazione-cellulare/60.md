An internalised GPCR is returned to the membrane after ligand removal. Which process describes this return?

- De novo synthesis of new receptors in the endoplasmic reticulum.
- Sorting internalised receptors towards lysosomal degradation.
- Receptor recycling.
- Endocytosis of receptors present at the surface.

## Explanation

The receptor retains its identity and is moved from intracellular compartments to the surface. This is recycling and can help restore sensitivity. Degradation instead removes the protein and would require new synthesis to restore the same receptor number. Return of internalised molecules distinguishes recycling from delivery of new proteins, degradation and transport in the opposite direction.
