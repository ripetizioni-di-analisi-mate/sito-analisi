After a health inspection, a local newspaper writes: ‘Some restaurants on Via Dante passed the inspection.’ If the newspaper's sentence is true, which statement necessarily follows?

- At least one restaurant on Via Dante passed the inspection.
- At least one restaurant on Via Dante did not pass the inspection.
- Most of the restaurants on Via Dante passed the inspection.
- No restaurant outside Via Dante passed the inspection.

## Explanation

In the language of logic, the newspaper's sentence states that the set of restaurants on Via Dante that passed the inspection contains at least one element. So **at least one restaurant on the street passed the inspection**. The sentence is also true if every restaurant on the street passed the inspection, so it does not follow that some restaurant failed it. For the same reason the sentence does not indicate what portion of the restaurants on the street passed the inspection. Restaurants on other streets are outside the scope of the sentence, so their outcome is not established.
