A four-story building, with floors numbered 1 to 4, houses the Purchasing, Budget, Contracts and Management offices, one per floor. Management is on floor 4. Purchasing is on a lower floor than Contracts. Budget is not on floor 1. Which statement is necessarily true?

- Budget is on floor 2.
- Purchasing is on floor 1.
- Contracts is on floor 3.
- Budget is on a lower floor than Contracts.

## Explanation

With Management on floor 4, the other three offices occupy floors 1, 2 and 3. Floor 1 cannot go to Budget, nor to Contracts, which must have Purchasing below it. So **Purchasing is on floor 1** in every consistent case. Two cases remain: Budget on 2 with Contracts on 3, or Contracts on 2 with Budget on 3. The second case falsifies the statements that Budget is on floor 2, that Contracts is on floor 3 and that Budget is lower than Contracts.
