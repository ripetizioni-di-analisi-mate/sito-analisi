Four friends arrive at a party at different times. Bea arrives before Carlo, and Dino arrives after Carlo. About Elsa it is known only that she does not arrive first. Evaluate I ‘Bea arrives before Dino’ and II ‘Dino arrives last’.

- I and II true.
- I true, II false.
- I cannot be determined, II true.
- I true, II cannot be determined.

## Explanation

Bea arrives before Carlo and Carlo arrives before Dino, so by transitivity Bea arrives before Dino, and I is true. Carlo, Dino and Elsa cannot arrive first, so the first place goes to Bea. The order Bea, Elsa, Carlo, Dino satisfies the facts and makes II true. The order Bea, Carlo, Dino, Elsa also satisfies the facts and makes II false, so II **cannot be determined**.
