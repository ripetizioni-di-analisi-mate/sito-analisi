In a poll the East district appoints six delegates and the West district appoints two regardless of population. All delegates follow their district majority. East chooses P by 51 to 49; West chooses Q by 400 to 100. What follows from counting individuals and delegates?

- Q has more individual votes, but P has more delegates.
- P wins on both counts.
- The two districts cancel each other out on both counts.
- Q wins on both counts.

## Explanation

Individual votes total 151 for P and 449 for Q. East's majority nevertheless gives P six delegates, while West's majority gives Q two.
