Four speakers, $A,B,C,D$, sit at two tables, with two speakers at each table. $A$ is at the east table. $B$ and $C$ sit together. Who sits with $A$?

- $C$
- $A$'s tablemate cannot be determined.
- $D$
- $B$

## Explanation

The pair $B,C$ fills a table, so they use the table other than $A$'s. Only $D$ remains, and must therefore sit with $A$.
