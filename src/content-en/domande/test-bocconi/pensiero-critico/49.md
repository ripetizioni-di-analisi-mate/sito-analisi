A company estimates late deliveries by counting complaints about lateness. Complaints double after it introduces a shorter form. Which information would most strengthen the view that the frequency of late deliveries really increased?

- The complaint button is now more visible.
- Delivery timestamps collected in the same way for equal numbers of shipments show more late deliveries.
- Some customers prefer writing a short complaint.
- The new form has fewer compulsory fields.

## Explanation

The timestamps directly measure lateness on a comparable base. The other facts concern how easy it is to report lateness.
