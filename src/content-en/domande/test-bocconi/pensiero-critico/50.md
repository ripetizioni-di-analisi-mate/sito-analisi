A group claims that adding a bench in the shade is enough to increase use of a square in any district. Which observation, if verified, directly refutes that claim of sufficiency?

- A shaded bench was added to a square and use of the square did not increase.
- One square was already busy before the installation.
- Two districts installed the same bench model.
- A square with no shaded bench is popular.

## Explanation

A condition claimed to be sufficient must produce the outcome whenever it is present. A shaded bench followed by unchanged use meets the condition without the promised increase.
