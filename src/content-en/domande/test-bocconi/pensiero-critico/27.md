A loading platform can take at most ten metric quintals (100 kilograms each) in total. A supervisor tests two crates separately and finds that the platform can take either one. He concludes that he can load both at once. Which example agrees with both tests but disproves his conclusion?

- The crates weigh four and five quintals.
- The crates weigh three and seven quintals.
- Each crate weighs six quintals.
- Each crate weighs two quintals.

## Explanation

To disprove the conclusion, each weight must be at most ten but their sum must exceed ten. Two crates weighing six quintals each meet both conditions. The other pairs have totals within the platform’s capacity.
