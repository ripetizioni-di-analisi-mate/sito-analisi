The new rule applies to bookings made from 1 September onwards. Under this rule, bookings for more than two people require a deposit. One booking for three was made on 1 September. Another booking for three was made on 31 August. Which conclusion follows?

- The new rule requires a deposit for 31 August only.
- The new rule waives the deposit for both.
- The new rule requires a deposit for 1 September. It does not determine the requirement for 31 August.
- The new rule requires a deposit for both.

## Explanation

1 September is included, and three people exceed the threshold of two. The 31 August booking is outside the date range. The passage does not specify the previous policy.
