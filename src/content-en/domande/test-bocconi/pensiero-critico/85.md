A panel chooses the quickest project among those within a spending cap. The author of a project already excluded for exceeding the cap promises to halve its duration at the same cost and says that will secure selection. Which step is overlooked?

- Halving a duration makes all projects tie.
- The spending cap applies before durations are compared.
- All durations are compared before costs are considered.
- Every faster project automatically costs less.

## Explanation

The project must first enter the eligible set to compete on duration. An unchanged cost still exceeds the cap, whatever the new duration.
