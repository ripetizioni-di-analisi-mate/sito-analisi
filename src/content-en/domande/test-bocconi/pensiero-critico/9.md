A group starts with 17 registered members. Cancellations may be filled by new registrants, at most one per cancellation. There are four cancellations and two replacements, with no other changes. How many people are registered at the end?

- 17
- 19
- 15
- 13

## Explanation

The four cancellations take the total from 17 to 13. Two replacements actually join, bringing the count up to $13+2=15$.
