A clinic is open on exactly three days a week, chosen among Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday, and no two of its opening days are consecutive. Evaluate I ‘The clinic is open on Monday’, II ‘The clinic is closed on Thursday’, and III ‘The clinic is open on Tuesday’. Which are necessarily true?

- I only.
- All three.
- II only.
- I and II only.

## Explanation

Between two opening days there is at least one closed day. So from the first to the third opening day, both included, there are at least $3+2=5$ days. Since exactly five days are available, the first opening day is Monday and the third is Friday. The second cannot be Tuesday or Thursday, which are consecutive to Monday and Friday, so it is Wednesday. The clinic is then open on Monday and closed on Tuesday and Thursday, so I and II are true and III is false. The answer is **I and II only**.
