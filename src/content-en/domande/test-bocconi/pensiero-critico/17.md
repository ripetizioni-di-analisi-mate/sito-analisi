Credit is granted only if the applicant has a guarantee, a deposit or both. One applicant has neither. Which conclusion is necessary?

- Credit is granted because both requirements are missing.
- Credit cannot be granted.
- The applicant can obtain credit by paying a higher rate.
- The outcome depends on which requirement is missing.

## Explanation

If credit were granted, at least one of the two conditions would have to hold. Both are false, so granting credit is ruled out by contraposition.
