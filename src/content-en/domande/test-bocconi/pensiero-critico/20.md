The organizers of a demonstration have to seat all 24 expected spectators in one room. Room A has 18 available seats. The number of available seats in room B is between 24 and 30, inclusive. Evaluate I ‘Room A can seat all the expected spectators’ and II ‘At least six seats remain unused in room B if everyone expected attends’.

- I false, II true.
- I and II false.
- I true, II cannot be determined.
- I false, II cannot be determined.

## Explanation

Seating everyone expected requires more than 18 places, so I is false. The upper bound of 30 allows B to have 30 seats, with six unused, or 24 seats, with none unused. II therefore cannot be determined.
