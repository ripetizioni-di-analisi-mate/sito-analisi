Two groups of twenty people take the same test. At least twelve pass in group X, and exactly eleven pass in group Y. Which conclusion is certain?

- Exactly twelve people pass in X.
- The difference in passes is necessarily one person.
- The pass rate in Y can exceed the pass rate in X.
- The pass rate in X is higher than in Y.

## Explanation

The denominators are the same. Even at its minimum, X has twelve passes and exceeds Y's eleven. X's actual number of passes can be greater than twelve.
