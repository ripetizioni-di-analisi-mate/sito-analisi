In a hall a reserved ticket expires if its holder has not arrived by 7 pm. The manager wants to know how many seats became available again at 7 pm specifically because of that rule. Which comparison isolates expiries from seats never booked or bookings cancelled earlier?

- Count all empty seats at 7 pm.
- Count every booking made since the morning.
- Subtract all admissions that evening from seating capacity.
- Compare reservations still valid just before 7 pm with those recorded as expiring for non-arrival at 7 pm.

## Explanation

Start with tickets that could still expire and identify the event that released them. Aggregate counts mix seats with different histories.
