Four friends sit in a row of five chairs numbered 1 to 5, and one chair is left empty. Ugo sits on chair 1. Vera and Zeno sit on adjacent chairs. Teo does not sit next to Ugo. Evaluate I ‘Chair 2 is empty’, II ‘Teo sits on chair 3’, and III ‘Chair 5 is empty and Teo sits on chair 3’. Which are consistent with the facts?

- I only.
- All three.
- I and II only.
- II and III only.

## Explanation

Teo cannot sit on chair 2, which is next to Ugo's. The arrangement with Teo on chair 3, Vera and Zeno on chairs 4 and 5 and chair 2 empty satisfies every fact, so I and II are consistent. In III, chairs 2 and 4 are left for Vera and Zeno, because chair 1 is Ugo's, chair 3 is Teo's and chair 5 is empty. Chairs 2 and 4 are not adjacent, so III is not consistent. The answer is **I and II only**.
