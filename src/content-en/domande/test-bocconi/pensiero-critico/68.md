In a reading room it is forbidden to photograph documents without written permission. A visitor obtains permission and chooses only to take notes. An attendant scolds her for taking no photographs. What has the attendant confused?

- The date of issue with the date of expiry.
- Permission to act with an obligation to act.
- The validity of written permission with that of oral permission.
- A ban on taking notes with a ban on taking photographs.

## Explanation

Permission lifts the photography ban for the visitor. The rule imposes no duty to exercise that permission.
