For each shift there is at least one available technician. The director deduces that a single technician could cover every shift. Which example shows the mistake?

- There is just one shift and two technicians can cover it.
- Ada is available for both shifts.
- Three technicians are available on every shift.
- The only technicians are Ada, available only in the morning, and Hugh, available only in the afternoon. Those are the only two shifts.

## Explanation

With Ada available only in the morning and Hugh only in the afternoon, every shift is covered but neither technician can cover both. The order of “for each shift” and “there is a technician” matters.
