‘The town should postpone the renovation of the market square until September. In July and August the square hosts the evening market, which brings many visitors to the shops in the town center. A summer construction site would force the market to move to a side street, and in the years when the market was moved, the shops' takings fell.’ Which statement expresses the main conclusion of the argument?

- The renovation of the square should be postponed until September.
- The evening market brings many visitors to the shops in the town center.
- A summer construction site would force the market to move to a side street.
- In the years when the market was moved, the shops' takings fell.

## Explanation

The main conclusion is the statement that the argument sets out to support, while the other sentences are its reasons. The evening market and the visitors it brings show why the square matters in summer. The summer construction site and the drop in takings in the years of the move show the harm of work carried out in July or August. These pieces of information support the opening proposal, and none of them is derived from the others. The main conclusion is therefore that **the renovation of the square should be postponed until September**.
