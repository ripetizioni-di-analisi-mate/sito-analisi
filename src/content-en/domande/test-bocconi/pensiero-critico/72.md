A bookbinder measures a single copy and writes, “All volumes in this edition are 24 centimetres tall.” Which additional fact, if verified, makes the generalization valid?

- The ruler has also been used on other editions.
- The measured volume was chosen at random.
- The edition contains many copies.
- All finished copies have exactly the same height and the measured volume belongs to the edition.

## Explanation

A single measurement extends to every copy when membership and identical height are established. Random selection alone does not guarantee uniformity.
