A meeting lasts exactly 90 minutes and starts between 2:00 p.m. and 2:30 p.m., inclusive. Which of the following statements cannot be true?

- The meeting ends at 3:45 p.m.
- The meeting ends at 3:20 p.m.
- At 3:55 p.m. the meeting is still in progress.
- At 2:10 p.m. the meeting is already in progress.

## Explanation

Adding 90 minutes to the earliest and latest start times, the meeting ends between 3:30 p.m. and 4:00 p.m. An end at 3:20 p.m. falls before 3:30 p.m., so it **cannot be true**. An end at 3:45 p.m. corresponds to a start at 2:15 p.m., which is consistent. With a start at 2:30 p.m. the meeting is in progress at 3:55 p.m., and with a start at 2:00 p.m. it is in progress at 2:10 p.m.
