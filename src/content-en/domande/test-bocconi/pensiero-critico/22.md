Of three restaurants A, B and C, exactly one is open today. If A is open, B is open too. C is closed. Evaluate I ‘A is closed’, II ‘B is open’, and III ‘B and C are both open’. Which are necessarily true?

- I and II only.
- I only.
- II and III only.
- All three.

## Explanation

Opening A would require B to be open at the same time, violating the exactly-one constraint. A is therefore closed. Since C is closed too and one must be open, B is open. C's closure makes III false.
