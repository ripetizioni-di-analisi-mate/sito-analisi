A platform features every verified record that is not a duplicate. Verified duplicates may be featured for archival reasons. All that is known about record K is that it is verified and is a duplicate. Which judgment is supported?

- K must be featured.
- K must be removed from the platform.
- K cannot be featured.
- Whether K is featured cannot be determined.

## Explanation

K is a duplicate, so the first rule leaves its outcome open. The second rule allows a case where K is featured for archival reasons and a case where K is not featured.
