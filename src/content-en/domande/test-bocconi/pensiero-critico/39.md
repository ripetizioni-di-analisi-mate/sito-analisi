A ticket assigned today has a number from 90 to 96 inclusive. The number is odd and greater than 92. Which pair contains exactly the numbers that are still possible?

- $91$ and $93$
- $94$ and $96$
- $95$ and $97$
- $93$ and $95$

## Explanation

Above 92 and up to 96 lie 93, 94, 95 and 96. Only 93 and 95 are odd among them. Both meet every condition.
