A poll codes “poor, fair, good, excellent” as 1, 2, 3, 4. The categories establish only an order of preference. An editor says that going from poor to good is twice the improvement of going from good to excellent. What is missing from that argument?

- A category below poor.
- An even number of participants.
- A justification that equal numerical gaps represent equal improvements.
- Confirmation that excellent comes after good.

## Explanation

The codes order responses without measuring the distance between categories. Comparing improvements by taking ratios of differences would require comparable intervals.
