In a hotel, every room with a balcony has a sea view. No first-floor room has a sea view. Room 12 is on the first floor. Evaluate I ‘Room 12 has a balcony’, II ‘Some first-floor room has a balcony’, and III ‘Every room with a sea view has a balcony’. Which are necessarily false?

- I and II only.
- I only.
- I and III only.
- All three.

## Explanation

A first-floor room has no sea view, so it cannot have a balcony, because every room with a balcony has a sea view. This applies to room 12, so I is false. The same argument applies to every first-floor room, so II is false too. III is true in a hotel where the rooms with a balcony are exactly the sea-view rooms, and false if there is a sea-view room without a balcony. The necessarily false propositions are therefore **I and II only**.
