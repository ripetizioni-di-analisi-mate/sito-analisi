At a company, the average salary in department A is higher than in department B. Which statement necessarily follows?

- Every employee in A earns more than every employee in B.
- The total salaries of A exceed the total salaries of B.
- At least one employee in A earns more than the average salary of B.
- The highest-paid employee of the two departments works in A.

## Explanation

Let $m_B$ be the average salary of B. If every employee in A earned at most $m_B$, the average of A would be at most $m_B$, contrary to the facts. So **at least one employee in A earns more than $m_B$**. The average does not determine individual salaries, so an employee in B may earn more than everyone in A. Total salaries also depend on the number of employees, and a much larger department B may have a larger total.
