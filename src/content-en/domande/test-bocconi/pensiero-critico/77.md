A speaker gives three tutors' free times. Ada and Bea are free together on Monday, Bea and Carlo on Tuesday, Carlo and Ada on Wednesday. He concludes that there must be a time when all three are free. Which further fact would prove this conclusion false?

- The listed meetings are the only possible ones for each pair and take place on three different days.
- The pairs can also meet in three different rooms.
- The tutors do not all work full-time.
- Each tutor has at least one day off.

## Explanation

If each pair can meet only on its own distinct day, no time can belong to all three pairs. Merely knowing that each pair can meet does not establish a common time.
