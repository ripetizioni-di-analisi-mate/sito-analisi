A manager says, “None of the three workshops passed both tests.” Which situation is compatible with this statement?

- Every workshop passed at least one test and none passed both.
- Two workshops passed both the first and the second.
- Exactly one workshop passed both tests.
- Every workshop passed the first and one also passed the second.

## Explanation

The statement rules out both successes in the same workshop. Each workshop could therefore have passed exactly one test.
