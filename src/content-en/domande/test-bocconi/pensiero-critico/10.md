What is the negation of ‘Every received publication has already been read’?

- No received publication has been read.
- At least one received publication has been read.
- At least one publication that was not received has been read.
- At least one received publication has yet to be read.

## Explanation

One unread publication within the group is enough to disprove a proposition about every received publication. The other received publications may have been read.
