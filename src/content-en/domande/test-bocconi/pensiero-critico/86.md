‘Some architects at the firm speak German. Some people who speak German have worked in Vienna. So some architects at the firm have worked in Vienna.’ Which of the following arguments contains the same error?

- All fruit at the market is organic. Some fruit at the market is imported. So some organic products are imported.
- All fruit at the market is organic. All organic products are certified. So all fruit at the market is certified.
- Some fruit at the market is organic. Some organic products are imported. So some fruit at the market is imported.
- No fruit at the market is imported. Some organic products are fruit from the market. So some organic products are not imported.

## Explanation

The two premises about the architects refer to two groups of German speakers, and nothing guarantees that they overlap. The German-speaking architects could all lie outside the group of people who worked in Vienna, so the conclusion does not follow. The argument about organic and imported fruit has **the same error**, because the organic fruit at the market may be different from the imported organic products. In the other three arguments the conclusion follows from the premises. For example, if all fruit at the market is organic and some of it is imported, an imported fruit from the market is also organic, so it is an imported organic product.
