A box contains exactly four objects, each made entirely of wood or entirely of metal. Two are wooden. At least one object is reinforced, but the material of the reinforced objects is unspecified. Evaluate I ‘There are exactly two metal objects’ and II ‘There is a reinforced wooden object’.

- I true, II cannot be determined.
- I and II true.
- I and II false.
- I cannot be determined, II true.

## Explanation

The two nonwooden objects must be metal, so I is true. The reinforcement condition is met by reinforcing a wooden object or by reinforcing a metal object only. These cases give opposite results for II.
