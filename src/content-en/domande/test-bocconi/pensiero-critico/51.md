Two stores of the same chain, North and South, are in the same city. In October, 120 customers of the North store and 90 customers of the South store bought an umbrella. The owner concludes that the proportion of customers who bought an umbrella was higher at the North store. Which piece of information is needed to assess the conclusion?

- The average price of umbrellas at each store.
- The number of umbrellas sold at each store in September.
- The number of rainy days in the city in October.
- The total number of customers of each store in October.

## Explanation

A store's proportion is the ratio of the customers who bought an umbrella to the store's total number of customers. The data give only the numerators, 120 and 90. With 600 customers at North and 300 at South the proportions would be $\dfrac{120}{600}=20\%$ and $\dfrac{90}{300}=30\%$, so the conclusion would be false. With equal totals it would be true, so **the total number of customers of each store** is needed. Price, September sales and rainfall, which is the same for both stores in the same city, play no part in the October proportions.
