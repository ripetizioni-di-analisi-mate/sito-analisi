An association concludes, “The installation will pay for itself within the year: it saves 5 euros per operating hour and costs 2,000 euros initially.” Ignore all other costs. Which assumption is missing?

- The savings will be received in 400 equal instalments.
- The old installation cost exactly 2,000 euros.
- The installation will run for at least 400 hours within the year.
- Every day will include the same number of operating hours.

## Explanation

Covering 2,000 euros requires $2000\div5=400$ hours of savings. How the hours are distributed across days or payments does not determine whether the total is reached within the year.
