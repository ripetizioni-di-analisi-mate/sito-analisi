A laboratory log records “missing” if a sensor fails to transmit and “zero” if it transmits a zero reading. A chart displays both entries as 0. What must be done before counting hours with zero consumption?

- Treat every missed transmission as positive consumption.
- Count all chart zeroes and divide the number by two.
- Look only for positive numerical values.
- Use the log to distinguish zero readings from missed transmissions.

## Explanation

A missed transmission leaves consumption unknown; a zero reading documents zero consumption. The chart merges these situations, while the log retains the distinction.
