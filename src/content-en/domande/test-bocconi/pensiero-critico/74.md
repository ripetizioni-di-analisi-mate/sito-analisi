A warehouse receives 50 pairs of shoes and then ships 30 pairs. The stock held before the delivery is unknown, and no other movements are recorded. Evaluate I ‘After the shipment the warehouse holds at least 20 pairs of shoes’ and II ‘After the shipment the warehouse holds exactly 25 pairs of shoes’.

- I true, II cannot be determined.
- Neither I nor II can be determined.
- I true, II false.
- I cannot be determined, II false.

## Explanation

Let $s$ be the number of pairs held before the delivery, with $s\geq 0$. After the delivery and the shipment there are $s+50-30=s+20$ pairs. Since $s\geq 0$, there are at least 20 pairs, so I is true. The value 25 corresponds to $s=5$, which is consistent, while $s=0$ gives 20 pairs. II therefore **cannot be determined**.
