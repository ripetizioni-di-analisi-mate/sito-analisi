Each team has exactly one leader. Two distinct teams share the same leader, and there are no other teams. Which proposition follows?

- There is a person who leads every team.
- There are two distinct team leaders.
- Every member of the two teams leads at least one team.
- Nobody leads two teams.

## Explanation

The shared leader holds that role for each of the two teams. Since there are no other teams, that person leads every team.
