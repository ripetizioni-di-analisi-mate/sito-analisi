A sample is eligible if and only if it is dry and weighs less than $10\,\mathrm{g}$. Sample G is dry and ineligible. What follows about its mass?

- It can take any positive value.
- It is at least $10\,\mathrm{g}$.
- It is exactly $10\,\mathrm{g}$.
- It is less than $10\,\mathrm{g}$.

## Explanation

The dry condition holds. If the mass were also below $10\,\mathrm{g}$, the sufficient condition would be met and G would be eligible. Its mass is therefore $10\,\mathrm{g}$ or greater.
