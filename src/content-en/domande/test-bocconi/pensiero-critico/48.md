At a company, in the year after two days of remote work per week were introduced, sick-leave absences fell by one fifth compared with the previous year. Management attributes the drop to remote work. Which piece of information most strengthens this explanation?

- In the same year, seasonal flu was less widespread than usual in the region.
- In a questionnaire, employees said they appreciate remote work.
- In the previous year, sick-leave absences had been higher than the industry average.
- In the same year, absences stayed the same in a department with similar duties that was excluded from remote work.

## Explanation

An alternative explanation of the drop is a cause that affects the whole company in the same year, such as a milder flu season. The department with similar duties is exposed to the same causes and was excluded from remote work. Since absences did not fall there, the observed drop is concentrated where remote work was introduced, and management's explanation **is strengthened**. The less widespread flu instead offers an alternative cause and weakens the explanation. Employees' appreciation does not concern absences, and an unusually high previous year makes the drop explainable even without remote work.
