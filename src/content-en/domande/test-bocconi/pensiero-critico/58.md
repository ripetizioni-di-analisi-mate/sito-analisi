Samples must be handed over after weighing and before signing. Signing must come before photography. Which additional constraint would make the whole procedure impossible?

- Weigh before taking the photographs.
- Take the photographs before weighing.
- Sign after weighing.
- Hand over the samples before taking the photographs.

## Explanation

The required order is weighing, handover, signing and photography. Placing photography before weighing would create a cycle.
