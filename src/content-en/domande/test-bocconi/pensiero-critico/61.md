In a vote between two proposals, the one with more yes votes passes; in a tie the current regulations remain. A councillor says, “If Blue does not pass, Green is bound to pass.” Which outcome disproves the claim?

- Blue and Green each get seven yes votes.
- Green gets five yes votes and Blue gets six.
- Green passes by one vote.
- Blue gets seven yes votes and Green gets eight.

## Explanation

With seven votes each the tie rule keeps the current regulations in force. Blue fails but Green also fails, exactly the outcome the councillor rules out.
