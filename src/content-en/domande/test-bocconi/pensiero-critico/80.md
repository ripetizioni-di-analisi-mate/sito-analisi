A material is chosen using strictly positive weights for durability and savings. Higher scores are better on both criteria and the total is the weighted sum. X scores 8 and 7, Y scores 8 and 5, and Z scores 9 and 4. Which comparison is settled even without the weights?

- X always outscores Z.
- X always outscores Y.
- Z always outscores X.
- Y always outscores Z.

## Explanation

X and Y tie on durability, but X has two additional savings points multiplied by a positive weight. Comparisons with Z trade a gain on one criterion for a loss on the other.
