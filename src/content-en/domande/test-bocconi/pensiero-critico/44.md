A gym has one hundred members in March and also in April. In March, 30 attend in the morning. In April, 40 attend in the morning. By how many percentage points does the morning share increase?

- 10
- 30
- 40
- 70

## Explanation

The two proportions are $30\%$ and $40\%$ because the total is one hundred in each month. The difference is $40-30=10$ percentage points.
