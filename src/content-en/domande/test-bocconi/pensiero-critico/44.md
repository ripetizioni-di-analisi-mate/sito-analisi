The same hundred gym members are observed in March and April. Thirty attend in the morning in March and forty in April. The manager claims that exactly ten people started attending in the morning. What can the two counts actually establish?

- At least ten people started, and there may have been more if others stopped.
- Exactly ten people started, regardless of turnover.
- No March morning attendee could have stopped.
- Twenty members certainly attend only in the afternoon in April.

## Explanation

If any of the thirty March attendees stopped, new attendees first have to replace them to reach forty. There are exactly ten starters only if all thirty stayed. For example, five departures require fifteen starters.
