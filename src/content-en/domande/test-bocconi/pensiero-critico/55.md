Three delegations each send at least one person. Each person belongs to exactly one delegation. Five people are present in all. Which statement is certainly false?

- One delegation has three people present.
- One delegation has just one person present.
- Every delegation has at least two people present.
- Two delegations have two people each.

## Explanation

Two people per delegation would require at least six people. The distributions $3,1,1$ and $2,2,1$ show that the other possibilities are compatible.
