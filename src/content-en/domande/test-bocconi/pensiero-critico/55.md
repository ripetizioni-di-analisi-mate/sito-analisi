In a cultural association, no founding member pays the annual fee. Some members of the board pay the annual fee. Which statement necessarily follows?

- No board member is a founding member.
- Some board members are not founding members.
- Some founding members are not on the board.
- Every member who pays the fee is on the board.

## Explanation

Consider a board member who pays the fee; such a member exists by the premises. If that member were a founding member, they would not pay the fee, since no founding member pays it. So that member **is not a founding member**, and at least one board member is not a founder. The other board members could be founders, so ‘no board member is a founder’ does not follow. The passage says nothing about founders outside the board or about paying members outside the board.
