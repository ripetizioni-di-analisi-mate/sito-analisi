A club accepts a drawing when it meets at least two of three criteria: legibility, originality and precision. Otherwise it rejects it. A rejected drawing is known to be legible. Which judgment follows?

- It is original though imprecise.
- It misses at least one criterion but could be original.
- It meets two of the three criteria.
- It lacks both originality and precision.

## Explanation

Legibility already counts as one criterion. Any second criterion would have secured acceptance, so both remaining criteria must be absent.
