Anna is taller than Bruno. Carla is shorter than Bruno. Dario is taller than Anna. Ernesto is shorter than Carla. Who is the second tallest of the five?

- Bruno
- Dario
- Carla
- Anna

## Explanation

Dario is taller than Anna, who is taller than Bruno. Bruno is taller than Carla, who is taller than Ernesto. Since the relation ‘taller than’ is transitive, the order from tallest to shortest is Dario, Anna, Bruno, Carla, Ernesto. So the second tallest is **Anna**.
