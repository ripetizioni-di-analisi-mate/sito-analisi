At the start of a shift there are 22 containers in a holding area. Five arrivals are recorded during the shift. Nineteen remain at the end. Every departure involves a container in the area. How many departures were recorded altogether?

- 5
- 27
- 8
- 3

## Explanation

The initial total and the arrivals give 27 before departures are subtracted. Since the final count is 19, there were $22+5-19=8$ departures.
