A newsletter has 44 distinct contributors. Eighteen wrote an article, and fifteen proofread drafts. Seven did both. How many did neither?

- 26
- 25
- 18
- 11

## Explanation

Adding the two groups counts the seven contributors in both groups twice. Those who did at least one task total $18+15-7=26$, leaving $44-26=18$ who did neither.
