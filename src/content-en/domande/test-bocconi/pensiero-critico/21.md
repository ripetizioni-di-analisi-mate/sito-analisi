A service receives one hundred reports in April and eighty in May. Thirty April reports are duplicates. Twenty May reports are duplicates. How many fewer nonduplicate reports arrived in May?

- 30
- 0
- 10
- 20

## Explanation

After removing duplicates, $100-30=70$ reports remain in April and $80-20=60$ in May. The decrease for the requested group is $70-60=10$.
