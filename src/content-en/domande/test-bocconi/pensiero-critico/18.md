Four stations $P,Q,R,S$ are visited once each. $S$ is before $Q$, and $Q$ is before $R$. Which positions are ruled out for $Q$?

- First and fourth.
- Second only.
- Third only.
- Second and third.

## Explanation

$Q$ needs a station before it and one after it, so it cannot be first or fourth. The orders $S,Q,R,P$ and $P,S,Q,R$ make second and third possible, respectively.
