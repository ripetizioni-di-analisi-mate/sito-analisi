In a meeting report, every contribution containing a proposal also has a summary note. No contribution with a summary note appears in the reports section. Contribution B appears in that section. Contribution C contains no proposals. Evaluate I ‘B has no summary note’, II ‘B has no proposals’, and III ‘C has no summary note’. Which are necessarily true?

- II only.
- I and III only.
- All three.
- I and II only.

## Explanation

Appearing in the reports section rules out a summary note, so I is true. The absence of a note rules out any proposal, which would require a note. II is also true. C could have a note even without proposals, so III cannot be determined.
