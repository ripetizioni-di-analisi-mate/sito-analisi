Ada's badge is activated when the office records the second of two required documents. The first was recorded on Thursday. The other will be recorded on either Friday or Monday. Which statement follows?

- The badge is still inactive on Thursday.
- The badge will be activated on Friday.
- The badge will be activated on Monday.
- Activation could occur on Wednesday.

## Explanation

The last document is recorded after Thursday in either case. The badge is therefore still inactive that day. The passage permits either of the two subsequent dates.
