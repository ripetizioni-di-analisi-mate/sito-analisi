A town compares two projects by adding the residents’ average rating to the cost in thousands of euros and choosing the larger total. At equal ratings, which effect conflicts with the stated aim of containing expenditure?

- The cheaper project is always selected.
- A costlier project automatically earns a larger total.
- Residents’ ratings count twice as much as cost.
- The rule necessarily produces a tie.

## Explanation

When ratings stay fixed, adding a larger cost increases the total to be maximized. Cost enters the score in the opposite direction to the stated aim.
