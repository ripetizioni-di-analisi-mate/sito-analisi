A public library that used to close at 7 p.m. has extended its opening hours until 10 p.m. In the first month, 400 entries were recorded after 7 p.m. The councilor in charge concludes that the extension brought 400 new users to the library. Which assumption does the councilor's conclusion require?

- In the same month, morning entries stayed the same.
- Each evening entry is by a different person who did not previously use the library.
- The library increased the number of seats.
- The extended hours required new staff.

## Explanation

The data count entries, while the conclusion speaks of new people. The same person may come in on several evenings, and an evening visitor may be a regular user who has simply changed schedule. In either case there would be fewer than 400 new users. The conclusion therefore requires that **each evening entry be by a different person who is new** to the library. Morning entries, seating and staff do not change the count of new users.
