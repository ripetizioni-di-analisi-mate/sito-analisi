A canteen wants to know whether all employees like its new menu. It asks only those who return to the counter for a second helping. Almost everyone surveyed gives a favourable rating. Which step in “almost all employees like the menu” is unsupported?

- Generalising from a sample selected by a choice related to liking the food.
- Counting second helpings as portions actually served.
- Calculating the favourable share among those actually surveyed.
- Distinguishing favourable and unfavourable survey responses.

## Explanation

Returning for a second helping may itself depend on liking the food. The replies describe those who returned and do not establish the share among other employees.
