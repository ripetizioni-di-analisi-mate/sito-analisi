A survey of many gyms in a region finds that members renew their memberships more often at gyms with evening classes than at the others. The manager of a gym without evening classes concludes that introducing them will increase renewals at the gym. Which piece of information most weakens the conclusion?

- The gyms in the survey added evening classes when their members were already renewing more often than at other gyms.
- Members of the manager's gym have long been asking for evening classes.
- Gyms with evening classes and the others charge similar fees.
- At gyms with evening classes, many members attend those classes.

## Explanation

The conclusion attributes the higher renewal rate to evening classes. If that rate was already higher before the classes were introduced, the observed difference has another source. In that case the survey shows no effect of evening classes on renewals, so the manager's prediction has no basis in the data. Members' requests, similar fees and good attendance are instead consistent with an effect of the classes and make it more credible.
