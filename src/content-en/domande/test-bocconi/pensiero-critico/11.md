A participant receives a card if and only if they submit exactly one of the paper and digital forms. Nila submits both. Orso submits only the paper form. Who receives a card?

- Orso only.
- Nila only.
- Both.
- Neither.

## Explanation

The criterion is necessary and sufficient. Nila submits two forms and fails the requirement of exactly one. Orso submits just one, so he meets the criterion and receives the card.
