If a room was prepared for a screening or a lecture, it was opened. Room N remained closed. Which conclusion is necessary?

- Which of the two events N was prepared for cannot be determined.
- N was prepared for neither event.
- N was prepared for at least one of the two events.
- N was prepared for exactly one event.

## Explanation

Either preparation would meet the condition implying that the room was opened. The fact that it remained closed therefore rules out both preparation for a screening and preparation for a lecture.
