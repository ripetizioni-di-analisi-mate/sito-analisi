Twenty-four players take part in a single-elimination tennis tournament. Each match is played between two players, there are no draws, and the loser leaves the tournament. In rounds with an odd number of players, one of them advances to the next round without playing. How many matches are played in total, including the final?

- 12
- 46
- 23
- 31

## Explanation

Each match eliminates exactly one player, the loser. The tournament ends when one player is left, so $24-1=23$ players are eliminated. Since each elimination corresponds to one match, there are **23** matches. Counting round by round confirms the result, because $12+6+3+1+1=23$ matches are played, with one player advancing without a match when three players are left.
