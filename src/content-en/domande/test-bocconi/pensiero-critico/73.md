A contract promises “delivery within three days on average.” The four actual delivery times are 1, 1, 3 and 7 days. A customer argues that the seven-day delivery alone proves a breach. Which assessment respects the clause?

- Every delivery taking more than three days necessarily breaches the clause.
- The clause can be checked only when all deliveries take the same time.
- The mean is three days, so the data meet the clause even though one delivery took seven days.
- The median is two days, so the mean cannot be three.

## Explanation

The total is twelve days for four deliveries, giving a mean of three. The contract limits the mean; an individual maximum would require a different clause.
