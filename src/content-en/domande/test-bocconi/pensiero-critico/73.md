At Naples airport, every flight of the airline Aerea Sud departs from terminal 1. Flight V departs from terminal 2 of an Italian airport. Evaluate I ‘V is not an Aerea Sud flight departing from Naples’ and II ‘V is not an Aerea Sud flight’.

- I true, II cannot be determined.
- I and II true.
- I cannot be determined, II true.
- I false, II cannot be determined.

## Explanation

If V were an Aerea Sud flight departing from Naples, it would depart from terminal 1. V departs from terminal 2, so by contraposition I is true. The rule concerns only Naples airport. An Aerea Sud flight departing from terminal 2 of another airport satisfies all the facts and makes II false. A flight of another airline also satisfies the facts and makes II true, so II **cannot be determined**.
