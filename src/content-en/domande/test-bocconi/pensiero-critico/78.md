A technician considers only two hypotheses for a scale. H1 predicts a fixed excess of 2 grams at every load. H2 predicts a 10% excess. A 20-gram weight reads 22. Which test distinguishes the hypotheses even if the scale displays whole grams only?

- Change the container while keeping the total load at 20 grams.
- Place two 10-gram weights on the scale together.
- Weigh the same 20-gram sample again.
- Weigh a certified 100-gram sample.

## Explanation

At 100 grams H1 predicts 102 and H2 predicts 110. The other tests maintain a 20-gram load, for which both hypotheses predict a reading of 22.
