An ice cream kiosk had the same number of customers in June and in July, while its takings in July were higher than in June. Which statement necessarily follows from the data?

- The price of ice cream rose from June to July.
- In July the kiosk sold more ice creams than in June.
- Every customer in July spent more than every customer in June.
- The average spending per customer was higher in July than in June.

## Explanation

The average spending per customer is the month's takings divided by the number of customers. The number of customers is the same in both months and July's takings are higher, so **the average spending in July is higher**. Higher takings may come from higher prices or from more ice creams sold at the same prices, so neither a price increase nor an increase in ice creams sold is necessary. The average does not determine what individual customers spent, so in July a customer may have spent less than some customer in June.
