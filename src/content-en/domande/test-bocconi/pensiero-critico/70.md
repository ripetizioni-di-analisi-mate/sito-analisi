In every week of July, a café took in more on Saturday than on Sunday. Which conclusion is justified by this fact?

- In every week of the year the café takes in more on Saturday than on Sunday.
- Saturday is the day of the week with the highest takings.
- In no week of July did Sunday's takings exceed Saturday's.
- In August the café will take in more on Saturday than on Sunday.

## Explanation

The fact compares two days, Saturday and Sunday, and concerns only the weeks of July. Since Saturday took in more in each of those weeks, **in no week of July did Sunday exceed Saturday**. Extending the comparison to other weeks of the year or to August goes beyond the period observed. The fact says nothing about takings from Monday to Friday, so it does not establish which day has the highest takings.
