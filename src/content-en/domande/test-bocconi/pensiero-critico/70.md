Three panels rank three logos. From best to worst, their orders are A, B, C; B, C, A; and C, A, B. Logos are compared in pairs, one vote per panel. What is the outcome?

- B beats both A and C.
- A beats B, B beats C and C beats A.
- Every comparison ends in a tie.
- A beats both B and C.

## Explanation

Panels one and three prefer A to B, one and two prefer B to C, and two and three prefer C to A. The three majorities form a cycle.
