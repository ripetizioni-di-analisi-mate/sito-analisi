A courtyard can only be reached through two gates, E and F. A supplier enters the courtyard while maintenance is in progress at gate E. Gate E remained locked throughout maintenance. What follows?

- The gate used by the supplier cannot be determined.
- The supplier used F.
- The supplier used E.
- Both gates were open.

## Explanation

The supplier entered while E was locked, so E is ruled out. Since there are only two gates into the courtyard, F is the only entrance consistent with the facts.
