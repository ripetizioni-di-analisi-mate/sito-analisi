At a university, the number of engineering graduates rises in one year from 400 to 450, while the university's total number of graduates rises from 2000 to 3000. Which statement is correct?

- Both the number and the share of engineering graduates increase.
- The share of engineering graduates stays at $20\%$.
- The share falls from $20\%$ to $15\%$ because the number of engineering graduates decreases.
- The number of engineering graduates increases and their share falls from $20\%$ to $15\%$.

## Explanation

The number of engineering graduates goes from 400 to 450, so it increases. The share in the first year is $\dfrac{400}{2000}=\dfrac{1}{5}=20\%$. The share in the following year is $\dfrac{450}{3000}=\dfrac{3}{20}=15\%$. Hence **the number increases and the share falls**, because the total number of graduates grows proportionally more than the number of engineers.
