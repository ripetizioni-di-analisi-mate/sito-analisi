Five people each claim to have shaken hands with exactly three of the other four, at most once per pair. Why is the account impossible?

- The individual counts would sum to 15, yet every handshake contributes twice.
- Each person would have to shake hands with all the others.
- Five people can have at most five handshakes.
- The account would require a pair to shake hands twice.

## Explanation

Adding the five counts of three gives an odd number. Each handshake involves two people, so the sum of the individual counts must be even.
