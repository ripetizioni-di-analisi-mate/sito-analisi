Which statement contradicts ‘No painting in storage has a legible signature’?

- A painting in storage bears an illegible signature.
- At least one painting in storage has a legible signature.
- At least one painting outside storage has a legible signature.
- No painting in storage has been photographed.

## Explanation

The statement requires the sets of stored paintings and paintings with legible signatures to be disjoint. A painting with both properties makes their intersection nonempty and contradicts the statement.
