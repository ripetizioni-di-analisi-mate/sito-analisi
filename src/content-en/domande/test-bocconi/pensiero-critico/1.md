Twenty-five distinct items need to be checked before closing. At least 18 have already been checked by 3 p.m. What is the largest number that could still require a check at 3 p.m.?

- 18
- 8
- 6
- 7

## Explanation

The number awaiting a check is largest when the number already checked is the smallest allowed, namely 18. The maximum is therefore $25-18=7$, attained when exactly 18 have been checked.
