Four singers, Alba, Bruno, Cleo and Dante, perform one after another. Alba performs before Bruno and Cleo before Dante, not necessarily immediately before. Which additional piece of information is enough to establish the complete order of the performances?

- Bruno performs immediately before Cleo.
- Dante performs last.
- Alba performs first.
- Bruno performs before Dante.

## Explanation

If Bruno performs immediately before Cleo, Alba precedes the pair Bruno, Cleo and Dante follows it, so the order is **Alba, Bruno, Cleo, Dante**. With Dante last, the first three places allow the orders Alba, Bruno, Cleo, then Alba, Cleo, Bruno and finally Cleo, Alba, Bruno. With Alba first, the last three places allow the orders Bruno, Cleo, Dante, then Cleo, Bruno, Dante and finally Cleo, Dante, Bruno. If Bruno performs before Dante, Dante follows both Bruno and Cleo, so he is last, and the same three orders as in the case with Dante last remain. Therefore the only piece of information that determines the order is that Bruno performs immediately before Cleo.
