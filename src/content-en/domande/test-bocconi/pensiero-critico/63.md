A magazine quotes committee minutes: “The committee examined the project and found it feasible.” The original continues, “provided the room is made available free of charge.” What error does the cut introduce?

- It portrays as opposed a committee that favoured the project in all circumstances.
- It turns a conditional judgment into an unqualified one.
- It attributes the judgment to a different body.
- It reverses the order of the review and the judgment.

## Explanation

The free-room requirement limits the judgment. Omitting it suggests that feasibility was also established if rent has to be paid.
