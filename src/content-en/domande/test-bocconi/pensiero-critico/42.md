Five cards $A,B,C,D,E$ are in a row. $B$ occupies the middle position. $A$ and $D$ are the same distance from $B$ on opposite sides. $C$ is immediately to the left of $B$. Which card is fourth from the left?

- $D$
- $C$
- $E$
- $A$

## Explanation

$B$ is third and $C$ second, so $A$ and $D$ cannot occupy both slots next to $B$. The only unoccupied symmetric positions are 1 and 5. Slot 4 remains for $E$.
