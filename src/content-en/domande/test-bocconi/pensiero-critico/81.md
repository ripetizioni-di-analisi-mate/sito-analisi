An archive tests a stamp reader. It flags 9 of the 10 sheets that really are stamped, as well as 11 of the 990 unstamped sheets. The director claims that “90% of flagged sheets are stamped.” Which fraction addresses exactly what she claims?

- 9 stamped sheets out of 20 flagged sheets.
- 9 flagged sheets out of 10 stamped sheets.
- 11 false flags out of 990 unstamped sheets.
- 20 flags out of 1,000 sheets.

## Explanation

The director is talking about flagged sheets, so the denominator includes the nine correct flags and the eleven false flags. The relevant share is 9 out of 20, or 45%.
