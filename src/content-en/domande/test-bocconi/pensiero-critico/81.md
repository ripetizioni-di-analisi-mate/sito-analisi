A town introduced a speed limit of $30\,\mathrm{km/h}$ on the streets of the town center. The following year, accidents with injuries in the center fell by one third, and the councilor in charge attributes the drop to the new limit. Which piece of information most strengthens the councilor's explanation?

- In the same year, new cameras were installed at the traffic lights in the center.
- After the limit was introduced, the measured average speed of cars in the center decreased.
- Outside the center, where the limit did not change, accidents with injuries fell by the same proportion.
- Many residents of the center had asked for the new limit.

## Explanation

The councilor's explanation presupposes that the limit reduced the speed of cars in the center. If the average speed had stayed the same, the limit could not explain the drop in accidents. The decrease in the measured average speed shows that the cause indicated by the councilor actually operated, so the explanation **is strengthened**. The new cameras instead offer an alternative cause of the drop. An equal drop outside the center, where the limit did not change, points to a cause affecting the whole town and weakens the explanation. The residents' requests do not concern the number of accidents.
