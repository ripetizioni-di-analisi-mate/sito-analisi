Every loan from the historical collection must be picked up at the desk. No loan that must be picked up at the desk is placed in the lockers. Volume K is on loan from the historical collection. What follows about whether Volume K is in the lockers?

- K is not in the lockers.
- K is in the lockers.
- The facts establish that K has already been collected.
- Whether K is in the lockers cannot be determined.

## Explanation

K belongs to the historical collection loans, so it must be picked up at the desk. The set of loans that must be collected at the desk is disjoint from the set placed in the lockers. K is therefore outside the lockers.
