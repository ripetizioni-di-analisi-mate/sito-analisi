Two people are “project colleagues” if they share at least one team. A coordinator assumes the relation is transitive. Which complete list of teams provides a counterexample?

- Three teams consist of the three possible pairs from Ada, Beppe and Cleo.
- A single team contains Ada, Beppe and Cleo.
- One team contains Ada and Beppe, another Beppe and Cleo.
- Ada and Beppe form the only team; Cleo works alone.

## Explanation

In the two teams that share only Beppe, Ada works with Beppe and Beppe with Cleo, yet Ada and Cleo do not work together. Both premises of transitivity hold while its conclusion fails.
