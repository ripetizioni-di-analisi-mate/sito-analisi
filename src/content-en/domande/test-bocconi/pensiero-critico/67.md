At a company, only employees with at least five years of service may request extended leave. Elena has worked at the company for eight years. What does the rule establish for Elena?

- Elena may request extended leave.
- Elena may not request extended leave.
- The rule does not establish whether Elena may request extended leave.
- Elena must request extended leave.

## Explanation

The word ‘only’ signals a necessary condition, so the rule states that anyone who may request extended leave has at least five years of service. Elena satisfies this condition, so the rule does not exclude her. The rule does not state that length of service is enough, so other requirements may apply to Elena. For this reason **the rule does not establish whether Elena may request the leave**, let alone require her to request it.
