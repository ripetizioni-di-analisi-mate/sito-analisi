There are four numbered lockers and four distinct instruments. Each instrument goes in a locker and no locker contains two instruments. What follows?

- Locker 1 can be empty if another contains two instruments.
- The largest instrument must go in locker 4.
- Swapping two instruments must leave one locker empty.
- Every locker contains exactly one instrument.

## Explanation

Four instruments require four distinct places. Since there are just four lockers, all four are occupied exactly once.
