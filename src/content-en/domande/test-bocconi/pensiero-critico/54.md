A parking lot has 40 spaces. At 10 a.m., at least 31 and at most 36 spaces are occupied. Which statement about the free spaces at 10 a.m. is possible but not necessary?

- At least 4 spaces are free.
- 10 spaces are free.
- Exactly 6 spaces are free.
- At most 9 spaces are free.

## Explanation

The free spaces are $40$ minus the occupied spaces. Since between 31 and 36 spaces are occupied, between $40-36=4$ and $40-31=9$ are free. The statements ‘at least 4’ and ‘at most 9’ hold in every consistent case, so they are necessary. Ten free spaces would require 30 occupied spaces, so that statement is impossible. **Six free spaces** correspond to 34 occupied spaces, a consistent case, while 33 occupied spaces leave 7 free, so the statement is possible without being necessary.
