Five runners, $F,G,H,J,K$, cross the finish line of a race, with no ties. $G$ finishes before $H$ and after $F$. $K$ finishes immediately after $H$. $J$ does not finish last. Which finishing order is consistent with the facts?

- $F,G,H,K,J$
- $F,J,G,H,K$
- $J,G,F,H,K$
- $F,G,H,J,K$

## Explanation

The order $F,J,G,H,K$ satisfies every constraint, because $F$ precedes $G$, $G$ precedes $H$, $K$ immediately follows $H$ and $J$ is second. In the order $F,G,H,K,J$ runner $J$ is last, so the fourth constraint is not satisfied. In the order $J,G,F,H,K$ runner $G$ finishes before $F$. In the order $F,G,H,J,K$ runner $J$ is between $H$ and $K$, so $K$ does not finish immediately after $H$. The only consistent order is therefore **$F,J,G,H,K$**.
