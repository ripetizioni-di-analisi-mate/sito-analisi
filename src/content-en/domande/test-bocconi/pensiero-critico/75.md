A sports center has 80 people enrolled in the swimming course and 50 enrolled in the volleyball course. The director states that 130 different people attend at least one of the two courses. Which piece of information is needed to check the statement?

- The number of courses offered by the center.
- The number of people enrolled in both courses.
- The proportion of women enrolled in each course.
- The number of people enrolled in the two courses the previous year.

## Explanation

The sum $80+50=130$ counts twice those enrolled in both courses. The number of different people is therefore $130$ minus the number enrolled in both. The director's statement is true only if nobody attends both courses. **The number of people enrolled in both courses** is therefore needed. The other pieces of information do not change the count of distinct people.
