An organiser claims that every repeat performance had at least one spectator attending for the second time. Which evidence suffices on its own to refute her?

- No spectator attended all performances.
- For one repeat performance the complete attendance history shows that every spectator was attending for the first time.
- A spectator who attended two performances did not attend a third.
- Each of three performances had at least one first-time spectator.

## Explanation

One repeat performance without a second-time spectator is sufficient. The other observations allow different people to satisfy the condition at different performances.
