Two rules concern a parcel. If it goes by express courier, it gets a red label. If it gets a red label, it passes desk 2. It is known to have passed desk 2. Which additional fact would allow us to conclude that it went by express courier?

- Some express parcels pass desk 2.
- Parcels without a red label also arrive at desk 2.
- All parcels that passed desk 2 went by express courier.
- All express parcels pass desk 2.

## Explanation

The missing implication runs from passing the desk to express shipment. Knowing that some express parcels or other parcels reach the desk does not determine the type of this parcel. Repeating that all express parcels pass the desk only preserves the original direction of implication.
