Every reader in group A is also in group B. At least one reader in group B is outside group C. Which statement is necessarily true?

- Group A contains a reader outside C.
- Group A is empty.
- Groups A and C are disjoint.
- Group B contains a reader outside C.

## Explanation

The reader in B and outside C is given. That reader may also be in A, but the inclusion $A\subseteq B$ allows them to be in B and outside A. The relationship of A to C remains open.
