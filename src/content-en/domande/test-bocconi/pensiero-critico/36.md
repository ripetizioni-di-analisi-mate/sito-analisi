An exhibition contains exactly ten objects. Three are identified as reproductions. The other seven may be originals or reproductions, and their types are unspecified. Evaluate I ‘At least three objects are reproductions’ and II ‘Eight objects are originals’.

- I true, II false.
- I true, II cannot be determined.
- I and II true.
- I cannot be determined, II false.

## Explanation

The three known reproductions guarantee I. At most all seven remaining objects could be originals. Eight originals are impossible, so II is false.
