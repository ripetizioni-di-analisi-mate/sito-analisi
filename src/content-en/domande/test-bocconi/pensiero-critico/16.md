A newspaper publishes a story without naming its source. Two blogs repeat it, both saying they got it from that newspaper. A reader counts three independent confirmations. What must be established before there is a second confirmation?

- That the two blogs have different titles.
- That the second blog posted later than the first.
- That the three accounts use exactly the same words.
- That at least one other source verified the event without relying on the same article.

## Explanation

All three available accounts trace back to the same story. Changing the title, date or wording adds no separate check on the event. A source that verified it independently would provide a second confirmation.
