A manager signs four documents $P,Q,R,S$ one after another. $P$ is signed before $Q$, not necessarily immediately before. $R$ is signed immediately after $S$. How many signing orders are consistent with these constraints?

- 6
- 2
- 3
- 12

## Explanation

Since $R$ immediately follows $S$, the pair $S,R$ forms a single block. Three items remain to be ordered, $P$, $Q$ and the block, which can be arranged in $3\cdot 2\cdot 1=6$ ways. In half of them $P$ precedes $Q$, so the consistent orders number $\dfrac{6}{2}=3$. They are $S,R,P,Q$, then $P,S,R,Q$ and finally $P,Q,S,R$, so the answer is **3**.
