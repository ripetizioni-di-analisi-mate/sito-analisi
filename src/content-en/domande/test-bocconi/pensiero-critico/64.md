Four cards show A, K, 4 and 7 respectively. Each has a letter on one side and a number on the other. To test the rule “if one side has a vowel, the other side has an even number,” which is the smallest set of cards that must be turned over?

- K and 7.
- A only.
- A and 7.
- A and 4.

## Explanation

A could have an odd number behind it and 7 could have a vowel behind it. A visible consonant or even number cannot conceal a violation of this rule.
