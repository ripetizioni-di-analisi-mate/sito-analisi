A library survey asks, “How satisfied are you with the quiet and the availability of power outlets?” A reader likes the quiet but finds too few outlets. Which change would make this reader’s answer interpretable?

- Replace “satisfied” with “very satisfied”.
- Increase the number of points on the single scale from five to ten.
- Ask for separate ratings of quiet and of outlets.
- Ask one hundred more users for a single rating.

## Explanation

One answer merges two features the reader judges differently. Separate questions measure the two judgments; more scale points or respondents do not resolve the ambiguity.
