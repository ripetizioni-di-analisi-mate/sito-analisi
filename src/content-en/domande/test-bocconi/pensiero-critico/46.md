A theatre opens its bar earlier and, in the same week, introduces student discounts. Purchases before shows increase. The manager attributes the rise to the new hours. Which comparison would best separate the effect of the hours from that of the discount?

- The highest and lowest bar prices after the change.
- Comparable evenings assigned different opening times but the same discount.
- Total receipts in the weeks before and after both changes.
- The number of students in town and the number of shows scheduled.

## Explanation

Assigning different hours to comparable evenings keeps the discount fixed while opening time varies. Comparing the two weeks leaves both changes combined.
