The town has replaced every streetlight on Via Roma with an LED streetlight. Each LED streetlight uses less energy per hour than the one it replaced. The town concludes that the energy used each night to light Via Roma will decrease. Which assumption does the conclusion rely on?

- LED streetlights last longer than the ones they replaced.
- LED streetlights cost less to buy than the old ones.
- The number of lit streetlights and the hours they stay on each night will not increase.
- Residents prefer the light of the LED streetlights.

## Explanation

The energy used in one night depends on the hourly consumption of each streetlight, on the number of lit streetlights and on the hours they stay on. The premise concerns only the hourly consumption of a single streetlight. If there were more streetlights or they stayed on longer, the total consumption could increase. The conclusion therefore rests on the assumption that **the number of streetlights and the hours of operation do not increase**. Lifespan, purchase cost and residents' preferences play no part in the energy used.
