Reviews $A,B,C,D,E$ occupy five consecutive slots, one each. $E$ occupies the first slot, $D$ the second, and $A$ is no later than third. $C$ is after $B$. Which review is second to last?

- $D$
- $B$
- $C$
- $A$

## Explanation

The first two slots are taken and $A$ must be no later than third, so it is third. The fourth and fifth slots remain for $B$ and $C$. Since $B$ precedes $C$, it is second to last.
