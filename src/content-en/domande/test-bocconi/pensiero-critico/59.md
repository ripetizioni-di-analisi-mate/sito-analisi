In a ranking without ties, Lea is ahead of Nico and Nico is ahead of Olga. Piero is ahead of Olga but otherwise unrestricted. Which single piece of information would suffice to identify the first of the four?

- Lea is ahead of Piero.
- Lea is ahead of Olga.
- Nico is ahead of Olga.
- Piero is ahead of Olga.

## Explanation

Only Lea and Piero could lead the group. Knowing Lea is ahead of Piero removes the only uncertainty about first place.
