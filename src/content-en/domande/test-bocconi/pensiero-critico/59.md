To obtain a scholarship for a master's program, a student must be enrolled in the program and have a family income below a set threshold. Enrolling in the program requires a degree. Giulia has obtained the scholarship. Which statement is certain?

- Giulia has the lowest family income among the enrolled students.
- Every graduate with income below the threshold obtains the scholarship.
- Giulia has a degree.
- Giulia graduated with top marks.

## Explanation

Since Giulia obtained the scholarship, she satisfies both necessary conditions, so she is enrolled in the program and her income is below the threshold. Since she is enrolled, she satisfies the necessary condition for enrollment, that is, **she has a degree**. The passage gives only necessary conditions, so it does not establish that a degree and a low income are enough to obtain the scholarship. Her degree mark and a comparison with other students' incomes are not among the facts.
