A newspaper asks whether a new timetable is “better” and cites only on-time departures. Commuters had defined “better” as reducing total time from home to work, including connections. What measures their criterion most directly?

- How many trains leave their first station on time.
- Mean departure delay for trains with no connections.
- Actual door-to-door times for the same trips before and after the change.
- How many printed timetables are handed out at stations.

## Explanation

The commuters’ criterion includes waiting and connections as well as departure. Comparing the same complete trips captures these components on a common basis.
