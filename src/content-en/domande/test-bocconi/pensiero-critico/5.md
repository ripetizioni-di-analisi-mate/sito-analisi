In an internal competition, all projects from the North division are assessed in person, except joint projects. Joint projects with two coordinators are assessed remotely. Project N is a joint project from the North division with two coordinators. Which assessment method applies to N?

- Both methods are mandatory.
- The method cannot be determined.
- Remote assessment.
- In-person assessment.

## Explanation

Being a joint project places it within the division's exception. Its two coordinators put it under the second rule, which assigns remote assessment.
