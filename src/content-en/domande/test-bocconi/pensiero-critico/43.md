A list publishes the names of the four people who completed a test within twenty minutes. Sam is on the list. The passage states that the result depends on a separate check not published in the list. Evaluate I ‘Sam finished within twenty minutes’ and II ‘Sam passed the test’.

- I and II true.
- I true, II false.
- I false, II cannot be determined.
- I true, II cannot be determined.

## Explanation

By the definition of the list, Sam met the time limit, so I is true. The separate check could give a pass or a fail with the same completion time. II therefore cannot be determined.
