A woodshop wants to cut overtime by buying a faster saw. A report shows that the existing saw is often idle while pieces wait for the only varnishing booth, which stays busy until the end of the shift. Which prediction is best supported?

- The existing booth is too fast for the saw.
- The faster saw will cut the varnishing time per piece.
- Faster cutting might add to the queue without reducing overtime caused by varnishing.
- Every minute saved in cutting removes a minute of overtime.

## Explanation

The queue forms before the already busy booth. Producing cut pieces faster cannot increase the capacity of the stage delaying their completion.
