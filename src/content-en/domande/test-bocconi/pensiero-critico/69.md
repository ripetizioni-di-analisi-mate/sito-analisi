Every examination board at an institute must include at least one external teacher. The chemistry board consists of three teachers. Two of them, Rossi and Verdi, are internal teachers. What can be said about the third teacher, Bianchi?

- Bianchi is an internal teacher.
- Bianchi's status cannot be determined.
- Bianchi may be internal if the board has an external chair.
- Bianchi is an external teacher.

## Explanation

The constraint ‘at least one external teacher’ applies to the board as a whole. The chemistry board has three teachers, and two of them are internal. If Bianchi were also internal, the board would have no external teacher and the constraint would not be satisfied. So **Bianchi is an external teacher**. An external chair would have to be one of the three teachers, and Rossi and Verdi are internal, so in that case too the external member would be Bianchi.
