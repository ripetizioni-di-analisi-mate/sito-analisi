A proposal would close two outlying service desks. Its sponsor documents an assured saving and concludes that closure is preferable, while acknowledging longer journeys for some residents. Which value premise connects the evidence to the conclusion?

- For this decision, the saving justifies the additional inconvenience to those residents.
- Closure will raise expenditure.
- Every resident lives near the centre.
- Both desks open at the same time.

## Explanation

The saving establishes one consequence. To prefer closure despite the other acknowledged consequence, the argument must give the saving sufficient weight relative to the inconvenience.
