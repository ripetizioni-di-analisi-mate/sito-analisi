An urn contains 10 balls, each red, blue or green, and there is at least one ball of each color. There are more red balls than blue ones and more blue balls than green ones. Evaluate I ‘There are at least 5 red balls’, II ‘There are at most 2 green balls’, and III ‘There are 4 blue balls’. Which are necessarily true?

- I only.
- I and II only.
- All three.
- II and III only.

## Explanation

Let $r$, $b$ and $v$ be the numbers of red, blue and green balls, with $r+b+v=10$ and $r>b>v\geq 1$. If $v\geq 3$, then $b\geq 4$ and $r\geq 5$, so there would be at least $12$ balls, contrary to the facts. Therefore $v\leq 2$, and II is true. If $r\leq 4$, then $b\leq 3$ and $v\leq 2$, so there would be at most $9$ balls. Therefore $r\geq 5$, and I is true. The choices $r=5$, $b=4$, $v=1$ and $r=5$, $b=3$, $v=2$ both satisfy the facts, so III cannot be determined. The necessarily true propositions are **I and II only**.
