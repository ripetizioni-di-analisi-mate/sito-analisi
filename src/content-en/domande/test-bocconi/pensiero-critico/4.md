Four distinct recordings $A,B,C,D$ occupy slots 1 through 4 in a running order. $D$ precedes $C$. $C$ precedes $A$ and $B$. Exactly one recording lies between $C$ and $B$. What is the order of the recordings?

- $D,B,C,A$
- $D,C,A,B$
- $D,C,B,A$
- $D,A,C,B$

## Explanation

Since $D$ precedes $C$ and $C$ precedes both other recordings, $D$ is first and $C$ second. $B$ must be two positions after $C$, so $B$ is fourth. $A$ takes the only remaining slot, third.
