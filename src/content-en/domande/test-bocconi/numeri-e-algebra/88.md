The numbers of rows and columns in a room may be any two positive integers whose sum is $15$. What is the greatest possible number of seats in a full rectangular grid?

- $60$
- $56$
- $54$
- $49$

## Explanation

With $r$ rows the product is $r(15-r)=(\dfrac{15}{2})^2-(r-\dfrac{15}{2})^2$. Maximising it requires an integer as close as possible to $7.5$. Both $r=7$ and $r=8$ give $7\cdot8=56$ seats.
