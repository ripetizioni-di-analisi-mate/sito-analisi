For which real numbers $x$ does $|x|>x$ hold?

- $(-\infty,0]$
- $(-\infty,0)$
- $\varnothing$
- $\mathbb R$

## Explanation

For $x\ge0$ we have $|x|=x$, so the strict inequality is not satisfied. For $x<0$ we have $|x|=-x>0>x$, so the inequality is satisfied. The solutions are therefore the numbers in $(-\infty,0)$.
