Let $A$ and $B$ be subsets of a nonempty universal set $U$. Which condition is equivalent to $(A\cap B)^c=U$?

- $A\cap B=\varnothing$
- $A=B$
- $A\cup B=\varnothing$
- $A\cup B=U$

## Explanation

If the complement of the intersection is all of $U$, every element of $U$ is outside the intersection, so it is empty. Conversely, the complement of the empty set is $U$.
