Evaluate $\left(\dfrac12+\dfrac13\right)\div\left(1-\dfrac35\right)$.

- $\dfrac{25}{12}$
- $\dfrac56$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac5{12}$

## Explanation

The sum is $\dfrac56$ and the difference is $\dfrac25$. Dividing by $\dfrac25$ means multiplying by $\dfrac52$, so the quotient is $\dfrac{25}{12}$.
