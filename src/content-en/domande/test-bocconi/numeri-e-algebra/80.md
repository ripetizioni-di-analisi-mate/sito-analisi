Solve the relation $a=\dfrac{b+x}x$ for $x$, with $x\ne0$ and $a\ne1$.

- $x=\dfrac b{a-1}$
- $x=\dfrac b{1-a}$
- $x=\dfrac b{a+1}$
- $x=\dfrac{a-1}b$

## Explanation

Multiplying by $x$ gives $ax=b+x$. Moving the terms containing $x$ to the left gives $x(a-1)=b$. Since $a\ne1$, we may divide by $a-1$, so $x=\dfrac b{a-1}$.
