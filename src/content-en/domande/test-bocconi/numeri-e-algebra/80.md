Two balances $a,b$ satisfy $ab<0$ and $a+b<0$. What follows with certainty?

- Both balances are negative.
- Balance $a$ is negative.
- The positive balance has the larger absolute value.
- The negative balance has the larger absolute value.

## Explanation

A negative product requires opposite signs. Writing the balances as $p$ and $-q$ with $p,q>0$, a negative sum means $p-q<0$. Thus $q>p$ whichever balance carries the minus sign.
