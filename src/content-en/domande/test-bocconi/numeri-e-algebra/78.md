A travel pass costs $18$ euros and includes the first $5$ rides. Each subsequent ride costs $1.50$ euros. Which expression gives the total cost in euros for every integer number $n\ge0$ of rides, once the pass has been bought?

- $18+1.5(n-5)$
- $18+1.5\max(0,n-5)$
- $18+1.5\min(n,5)$
- $18+1.5\max(5,n)$

## Explanation

The base price applies even when fewer than five rides are taken. The number of chargeable rides is zero through $n=5$ and $n-5$ thereafter. It is therefore $\max(0,n-5)$, multiplied by $1.5$ and added to $18$.
