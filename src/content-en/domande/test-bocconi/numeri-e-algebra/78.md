Find the real solutions of $\dfrac x{x-2}=\dfrac2{x-2}+3$.

- $\{2\}$
- $\{4\}$
- $\{-2\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The equation is defined for $x\ne2$. Multiplying by $x-2$ gives $x=2+3(x-2)$, that is, $-2x=-4$, so $x=2$. This value is excluded from the domain, therefore the equation has no solutions.
