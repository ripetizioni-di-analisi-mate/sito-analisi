A complete list of pairings has the form $A\times B$ and contains $(r,2)$ and $(s,5)$, where $r$ and $s$ denote two distinct labels, neither of which is a number. Which other two pairs must appear?

- $(2,r),\ (5,s)$
- $(r,5),\ (s,2)$
- $(r,s),\ (2,5)$
- $(r,r),\ (s,s)$

## Explanation

The given first coordinates guarantee $r,s\in A$. The second coordinates guarantee $2,5\in B$. A Cartesian product contains every pairing with its first entry in $A$ and its second in $B$, including the two cross-pairings.
