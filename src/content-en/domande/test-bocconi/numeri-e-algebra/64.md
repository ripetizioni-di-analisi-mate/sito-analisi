For which positive value of $k$ is the trinomial $9x^2+kx+16$ the square of a binomial?

- $12$
- $7$
- $24$
- $48$

## Explanation

Since $9x^2=(3x)^2$ and $16=4^2$, with $k>0$ the binomial is $3x+4$. By the identity $(a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2$ the first-degree term is $2\cdot3x\cdot4=24x$, so $k=24$.
