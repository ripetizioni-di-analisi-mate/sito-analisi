Which of the following numbers lies between $7$ and $8$?

- $\sqrt{50}$
- $\sqrt{48}$
- $\sqrt{65}$
- $2\sqrt{17}$

## Explanation

A positive number lies between $7$ and $8$ exactly when its square lies between $49$ and $64$. The squares of the four numbers are $50$, $48$, $65$ and $4\cdot17=68$. Only $50$ lies between $49$ and $64$, so the required number is $\sqrt{50}$.
