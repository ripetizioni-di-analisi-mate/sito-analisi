A machine accepts only $20$- and $50$-cent coins. A customer inserts exactly six coins. Which total could the customer have paid?

- $1.40$ euros
- $2.50$ euros
- $2.10$ euros
- $1.90$ euros

## Explanation

Six $20$-cent coins would total $120$ cents. Replacing one with a $50$-cent coin adds $30$ cents. Among the choices only $210=120+3\cdot30$ can be reached.
