Mix $3\,\mathrm{L}$ of solution at $10\,\mathrm{g/L}$ with $2\,\mathrm{L}$ at $25\,\mathrm{g/L}$. The volumes add. What is the final concentration?

- $40\,\mathrm{g/L}$
- $16\,\mathrm{g/L}$
- $17.5\,\mathrm{g/L}$
- $35\,\mathrm{g/L}$

## Explanation

The solute masses are $30\,\mathrm{g}$ and $50\,\mathrm{g}$. The total volume of $5\,\mathrm{L}$ contains $80\,\mathrm{g}$, so the concentration is $16\,\mathrm{g/L}$.
