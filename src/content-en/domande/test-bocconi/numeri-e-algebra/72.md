A tank contains $12\,\mathrm L$ of solution, one quarter of which is dissolved salt by volume. Only water evaporates and volumes are additive. How many litres of water must evaporate for the salt fraction to become one third?

- $1\,\mathrm L$
- $3\,\mathrm L$
- $9\,\mathrm L$
- $4\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

The salt volume stays at $\dfrac{12}{4}=3$ litres. For this to be one third, the final total volume must be $3\cdot3=9$ litres. The water lost is therefore $12-9=3$ litres.
