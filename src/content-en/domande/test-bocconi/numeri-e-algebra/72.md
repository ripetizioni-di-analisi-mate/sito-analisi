Write $\sqrt2\cdot\sqrt[3]2$ as a single radical.

- $\sqrt[6]{32}$
- $\sqrt[6]{4}$
- $\sqrt[5]{4}$
- $\sqrt[6]{2}$

## Explanation

With rational exponents, $\sqrt2=2^{\frac12}$ and $\sqrt[3]2=2^{\frac13}$. The product is therefore $2^{\frac12+\frac13}=2^{\frac56}$. By the definition of a rational exponent, $2^{\frac56}=\sqrt[6]{2^5}=\sqrt[6]{32}$.
