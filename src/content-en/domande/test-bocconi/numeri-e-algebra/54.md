A shop gives a $10\,\%$ discount on the entire order only when the subtotal reaches $60$ euros. Items worth $57$ euros are in the basket. How does adding a $3$ euro item change the amount due?

- It falls by $3$ euros.
- It falls by $6$ euros.
- It stays the same.
- It rises by $3$ euros.

## Explanation

Before the addition the subtotal is below the threshold, so the payment is $57$ euros. The new subtotal reaches $60$, making the payment $60\cdot0.90=54$ euros. The change is $54-57=-3$ euros.
