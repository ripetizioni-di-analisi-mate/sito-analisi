Six workers, all working at the same rate, finish a job in $10$ days. In how many days do $15$ workers at that rate finish the same job?

- $25$
- $5$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The job corresponds to $6\cdot10=60$ days of work by a single worker. The product of the number of workers and the number of days therefore stays equal to $60$, so the two quantities are inversely proportional. With $15$ workers it takes $\dfrac{60}{15}=4$ days.
