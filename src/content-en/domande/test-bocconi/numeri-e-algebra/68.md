To solve $x(x-4)=2x$, a student immediately divides both sides by $x$ and gets $x=6$. Which solution was lost, and why?

- $0$, because the division requires $x\ne0$.
- $2$, because it appears on the right-hand side.
- $-6$, because the opposite sign must also be included.
- $4$, because it makes the factor $x-4$ zero.

## Explanation

Moving everything to the left gives $x(x-4)-2x=x(x-6)=0$. The solutions are $0$ and $6$. The initial division was valid only for $x\ne0$, so it discarded the first solution.
