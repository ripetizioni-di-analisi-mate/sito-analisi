The product of two consecutive positive integers is $156$. What is their sum?

- $12$
- $13$
- $25$
- $1$

## Explanation

If the numbers are $n$ and $n+1$, then $n(n+1)=156$, that is, $n^2+n-156=0$. The trinomial factors as $(n-12)(n+13)$, because $-12+13=1$ and $-12\cdot13=-156$. Since $n>0$, we get $n=12$, so the numbers are $12$ and $13$ and their sum is $25$.
