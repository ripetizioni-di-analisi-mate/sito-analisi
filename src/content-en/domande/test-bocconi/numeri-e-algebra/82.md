For every odd integer $n$, which of these numbers always divides $n^2-1$?

- $16$
- $3$
- $8$
- $5$

## Explanation

The factors $(n-1)$ and $(n+1)$ are consecutive even integers. One is a multiple of $4$ and the other of $2$, so their product is divisible by $8$. For instance $n=3$ gives $8$, ruling out all three other proposed divisors.
