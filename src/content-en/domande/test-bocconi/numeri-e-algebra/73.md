A workshop can outsource a component at $9$ euros per piece, or make it for a setup fee of $50$ euros plus $4$ euros per piece. What is the least number of pieces for which making them costs strictly less?

- $9$
- $11$
- $10$
- $13$

## Explanation

If $n$ pieces are required, the comparison is $50+4n<9n$. This gives $50<5n$, hence $n>10$. At $10$ pieces the costs are equal, and the first integer that yields a saving is $11$.
