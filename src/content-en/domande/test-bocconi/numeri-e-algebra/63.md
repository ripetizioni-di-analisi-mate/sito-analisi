How many elements are in the intersection of the set of positive divisors of $24$ and the set of positive divisors of $36$?

- $11$
- $6$
- $4$
- $12$

## Explanation

The positive divisors of $24$ are $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, $6$, $8$, $12$ and $24$. Among them, $8$ and $24$ do not divide $36$, while the other six do. The intersection therefore has $6$ elements.
