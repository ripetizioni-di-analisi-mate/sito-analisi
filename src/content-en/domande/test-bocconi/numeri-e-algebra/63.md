Three archives contain $20$, $18$ and $15$ records. Their three pairwise intersections contain $6$, $5$ and $4$ records, and $2$ records appear in all three. How many different records are there in total?

- $36$
- $38$
- $42$
- $40$

## Explanation

The three sizes sum to $53$. Subtracting the pairwise intersections gives $53-15=38$, but each record common to all three was counted three times and then subtracted three times. Adding the two common records back gives $40$.
