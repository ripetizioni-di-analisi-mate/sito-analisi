The share of subscribers who pay annually rises from $40\,\%$ to $50\,\%$. By how much does the share grow relative to its initial value?

- $125\,\%$
- $10\,\%$
- $20\,\%$
- $25\,\%$

## Explanation

The difference is $10$ percentage points. To express it as relative growth, divide by the initial share. This gives $\dfrac{50-40}{40}=\dfrac{1}{4}=25\,\%$.
