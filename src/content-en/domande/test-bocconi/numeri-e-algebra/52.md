The unemployment rate of a region rises from $4\,\%$ to $5\,\%$. By how much has the rate grown in relative terms?

- $1\,\%$
- $20\,\%$
- $5\,\%$
- $25\,\%$

## Explanation

The difference between the two rates is $1$ percentage point. The relative growth is computed with respect to the initial rate, so it equals $\dfrac{5-4}4=\dfrac14=25\,\%$.
