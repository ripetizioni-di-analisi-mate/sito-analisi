Of $52$ participants, $31$ attend group $A$, $29$ attend group $B$ and $16$ attend both. How many attend neither?

- $36$
- $16$
- $8$
- $24$

## Explanation

There are $31+29-16=44$ participants in at least one group, because the $16$ in both must be counted once. Outside the union there are $52-44=8$ people.
