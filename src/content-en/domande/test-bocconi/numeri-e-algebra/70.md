Write as a fraction in lowest terms the number $\left(\dfrac23\right)^{-2}\cdot\left(\dfrac32\right)^{-1}$.

- $\dfrac{27}8$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac8{27}$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

A negative exponent denotes the reciprocal of the power. Thus $\left(\dfrac23\right)^{-2}=\left(\dfrac32\right)^2=\dfrac94$ and $\left(\dfrac32\right)^{-1}=\dfrac23$. The product is $\dfrac94\cdot\dfrac23=\dfrac32$.
