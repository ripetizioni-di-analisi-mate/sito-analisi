A machine shares a mass $m$ equally among $n$ containers. The mass is between $48$ and $54$ grams inclusive, and $n$ may be $6$, $7$ or $8$ independently of $m$. What is the largest possible mass per container?

- $8\,\mathrm{g}$
- $6\,\mathrm{g}$
- $9\,\mathrm{g}$
- $6.75\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The mass per container is $\dfrac{m}{n}$. For positive quantities it increases with $m$ and decreases as $n$ increases. The two favourable extremes can occur together, giving a maximum of $\dfrac{54}{6}=9$ grams.
