Let $U=\{1,2,\ldots,9\}$ be the universal set. For $A=\{2,4,6,8\}$ and $B=\{1,2,3,4,5\}$, find $A^c\cap B$.

- $\{1,3,5,7,9\}$
- $\{1,3,5\}$
- $\{6,8\}$
- $\{2,4\}$

## Explanation

We want the elements of $B$ outside $A$. Of the elements of $B$, $2$ and $4$ also belong to $A$. The remaining elements are $1$, $3$ and $5$.
