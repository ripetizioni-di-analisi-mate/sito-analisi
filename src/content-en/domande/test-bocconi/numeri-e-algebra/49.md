Evaluate $\dfrac{0.0006\cdot4\cdot10^5}{8\cdot10^{-2}}$.

- $3\cdot10^3$
- $3\cdot10^2$
- $3\cdot10^4$
- $3\cdot10^{-1}$

## Explanation

Write $0.0006=6\cdot10^{-4}$. The numerator is therefore $6\cdot4\cdot10^{-4+5}=24\cdot10^1$. The quotient of the coefficients is $\dfrac{24}8=3$ and the quotient of the powers is $10^{1-(-2)}=10^3$. Hence the result is $3\cdot10^3$.
