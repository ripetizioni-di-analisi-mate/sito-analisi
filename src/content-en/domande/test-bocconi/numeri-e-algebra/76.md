Two nonzero numbers $u,v$ are the roots of $2x^2-5x+1=0$. Which equation has $\dfrac{1}{u}$ and $\dfrac{1}{v}$ as its roots?

- $y^2-5y+2=0$
- $2y^2-y+5=0$
- $y^2+5y+2=0$
- $2y^2+5y+1=0$

## Explanation

Write $y=\dfrac{1}{x}$. The original equation becomes $\dfrac{2}{y^2}-\dfrac{5}{y}+1=0$. The value $y=0$ is excluded, so multiplying by $y^2$ is valid. Rearranging gives $y^2-5y+2=0$.
