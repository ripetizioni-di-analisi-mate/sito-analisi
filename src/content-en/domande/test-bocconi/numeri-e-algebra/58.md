In a class of $30$ students, $18$ passed the mathematics exam and $20$ passed the economics exam. What is the minimum number of students who passed both exams?

- $2$
- $12$
- $18$
- $8$

## Explanation

Let $A$ and $B$ be the sets of students who passed the two exams. We have $\lvert A\cup B\rvert=18+20-\lvert A\cap B\rvert$, and the union has at most $30$ elements. Hence $38-\lvert A\cap B\rvert\le30$, that is, $\lvert A\cap B\rvert\ge8$. The value $8$ occurs when every student in the class passed at least one of the two exams.
