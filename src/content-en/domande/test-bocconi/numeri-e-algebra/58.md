Four fifths of a road are level. A cyclist covers all of the level part and then half of the remaining part. The rider still has $3\,\mathrm{km}$ to go. How long is the road?

- $15\,\mathrm{km}$
- $30\,\mathrm{km}$
- $24\,\mathrm{km}$
- $60\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

After the level section, $\dfrac{1}{5}$ of the road remains. The half of this still to be covered is $\dfrac{1}{10}$ of the whole road. If one tenth is $3\,\mathrm{km}$ long, the road is $30\,\mathrm{km}$ long.
