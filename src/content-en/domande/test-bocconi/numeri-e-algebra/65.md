A cube model grows from side length $n$ to side length $n+1$, where $n$ is a positive integer. Which expression directly gives the number of unit cubes added?

- $3n^2+1$
- $3n+1$
- $3n^2+3n+1$
- $n^3+1$

## Explanation

The volumes before and after are $n^3$ and $(n+1)^3$. Expanding the latter gives $n^3+3n^2+3n+1$. The volume added is therefore $3n^2+3n+1$.
