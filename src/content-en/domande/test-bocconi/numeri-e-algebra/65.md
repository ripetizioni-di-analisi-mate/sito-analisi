Factor $x^3-2x^2-9x+18$ into first-degree factors.

- $(x+2)(x-3)(x+3)$
- $(x-2)(x-3)^2$
- $(x-2)(x-3)(x+3)$
- $(x-2)(x+3)^2$

## Explanation

Factoring $x^2$ out of the first two terms and $-9$ out of the last two gives $x^2(x-2)-9(x-2)$. The factor $x-2$ is common, so the polynomial equals $(x-2)(x^2-9)$. Finally, $x^2-9$ is a difference of squares, so $x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3)$.
