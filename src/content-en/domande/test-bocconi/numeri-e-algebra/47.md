One automatic check starts at opening time and runs every 12 minutes. A second check starts 6 minutes after opening and runs every 18 minutes. How many minutes after opening do the checks first coincide?

- $36$
- $24$
- $42$
- $18$

## Explanation

The first check runs at minutes $0,12,24,\ldots$ The second runs at minutes $6,24,42,\ldots$ The first common entry is $24$. The least common multiple of the periods would give the time between successive coincidences.
