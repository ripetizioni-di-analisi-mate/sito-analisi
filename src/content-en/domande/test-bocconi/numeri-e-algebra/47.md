Let $a$ and $b$ be real numbers with $a<b<0$. Which inequality is always true?

- $a^2<b^2$
- $\dfrac1a>\dfrac1b$
- $-a<-b$
- $ab<0$

## Explanation

Since $a$ and $b$ are both negative, the product $ab$ is positive. Dividing $a<b$ by $ab>0$ preserves the direction, so $\dfrac1b<\dfrac1a$. For example, with $a=-4$ and $b=-2$ we get $\dfrac1a=-\dfrac14$, which is greater than $\dfrac1b=-\dfrac12$. The same numbers give $a^2=16>b^2=4$ and $-a=4>-b=2$.
