The numbers $2^{30}$, $3^{20}$, $5^{10}$ and $4^{14}$ are arranged from smallest to largest. Which number comes last?

- $3^{20}$
- $2^{30}$
- $5^{10}$
- $4^{14}$

## Explanation

Rewrite the powers with exponent $10$ where possible. We have $2^{30}=(2^3)^{10}=8^{10}$ and $3^{20}=(3^2)^{10}=9^{10}$, while $4^{14}=2^{28}$ is less than $2^{30}$. Since the bases satisfy $9>8>5$, the power $9^{10}$ exceeds $8^{10}$ and $5^{10}$. Hence the greatest number is $3^{20}$.
