An archive contains $2.4\cdot10^8$ bytes. A network transmits $6\cdot10^5$ bytes per second at a constant rate. Which time in seconds is correctly written in scientific notation?

- $4\cdot10^2$
- $4\cdot10^3$
- $4\cdot10^{-2}$
- $1.44\cdot10^{14}$

## Explanation

Time is the ratio of quantity to rate. It is $(\dfrac{2.4}{6})\cdot10^{8-5}=0.4\cdot10^3$. Moving the coefficient into the interval from $1$ up to $10$ gives $4\cdot10^2$ seconds.
