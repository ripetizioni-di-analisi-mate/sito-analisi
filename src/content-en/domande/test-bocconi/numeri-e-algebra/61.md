The sets $A$ and $B$ contain, respectively, the multiples of $2$ and the multiples of $3$ between $1$ and $12$. With respect to the universe $U=\{1,2,\ldots,12\}$, find $(A\cup B)^c$.

- $\{1,5,7,11\}$
- $\{6,12\}$
- $\{1,5,7\}$
- $\{1,3,5,7,9,11\}$

## Explanation

An element lies outside the union if it lies outside both sets, so $(A\cup B)^c=A^c\cap B^c$. We therefore look for the elements of $U$ that are multiples of neither $2$ nor $3$. These elements are $1$, $5$, $7$ and $11$.
