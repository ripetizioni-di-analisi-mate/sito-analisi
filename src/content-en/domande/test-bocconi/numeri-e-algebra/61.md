In an archive, filter $A$ flags files missing a signature and filter $B$ flags those missing a date. The quick review accepts files missing exactly one of those items. Which filter selects files for quick review?

- $(A\cap B)^c$
- $A\cup B$
- $(A\cup B)\setminus(A\cap B)$
- $A\setminus B$

## Explanation

The union contains files with at least one missing item. Within it, those in $A\cap B$ must be excluded because both items are missing. This keeps both signature-only and date-only omissions.
