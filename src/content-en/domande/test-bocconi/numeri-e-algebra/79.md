A shop requires a deposit of one third of the price and then takes $6$ euros off the outstanding balance. The balance actually paid equals the deposit. What was the original price?

- $12$ euros
- $9$ euros
- $18$ euros
- $24$ euros

## Explanation

For a price $p$, the deposit is $\dfrac{p}{3}$ and the discounted balance is $\dfrac{2p}{3}-6$. Equating them gives $\dfrac{p}{3}=6$, hence $p=18$. The deposit is then $6$ euros and the reduced balance is another $6$.
