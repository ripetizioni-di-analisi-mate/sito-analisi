A company has a fixed cost of $1\,200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and a cost of $8\,\mathrm{EUR}$ for each item produced, and it sells each item for $14\,\mathrm{EUR}$. How many items must it produce and sell for the revenue to equal the total cost?

- $150$
- $86$
- $600$
- $200$

## Explanation

With $q$ items the total cost is $1\,200+8q$ and the revenue is $14q$. The equality $14q=1\,200+8q$ is equivalent to $6q=1\,200$, so $q=200$.
