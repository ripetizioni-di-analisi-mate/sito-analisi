What is the distance between $-\dfrac58$ and $\dfrac34$ on the number line?

- $\dfrac{11}8$
- $\dfrac98$
- $\dfrac18$
- $-\dfrac{11}8$

## Explanation

The distance is the absolute value of the difference. Since $\dfrac34=\dfrac68$, the difference is $-\dfrac{11}8$ and the distance is $\dfrac{11}8$.
