Which number belongs to $\left(-\dfrac35,-\dfrac12\right]$?

- $-\dfrac23$
- $-\dfrac9{20}$
- $-\dfrac{11}{20}$
- $-\dfrac35$

## Explanation

The endpoints are $-\dfrac{12}{20}$ and $-\dfrac{10}{20}$. The number $-\dfrac{11}{20}$ lies between them. The left endpoint is excluded.
