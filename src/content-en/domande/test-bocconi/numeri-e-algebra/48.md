Without approximating $\sqrt5$, find the value of $|2-\sqrt5|+|\sqrt5-3|$.

- $-1$
- $2\sqrt5-5$
- $5-2\sqrt5$
- $1$

## Explanation

Since $4<5<9$, we have $2<\sqrt5<3$. Hence $2-\sqrt5$ is negative and $|2-\sqrt5|=\sqrt5-2$. The number $\sqrt5-3$ is negative as well, so $|\sqrt5-3|=3-\sqrt5$. The sum is $\sqrt5-2+3-\sqrt5=1$.
