An archive number is $7^{2026}$. Which digit belongs in the units box?

- $7$
- $1$
- $9$
- $3$

## Explanation

The units digits of the first four powers of $7$ are $7,9,3,1$. Multiplying by $7$ again restarts the cycle. Since $2026=4\cdot506+2$, the required digit is the second in the cycle, namely $9$.
