Let $A=\{1,2,3\}$ and $B=\{1,2,3,4\}$. How many pairs $(a,b)\in A\times B$ satisfy $a<b$?

- $9$
- $6$
- $12$
- $3$

## Explanation

For $a=1$ the admissible values of $b$ are $2$, $3$ and $4$. For $a=2$ they are $3$ and $4$, while for $a=3$ only $4$ remains. There are therefore $3+2+1=6$ pairs.
