A report prints $0.\overline{27}$ as the share of correct answers. Which fraction in lowest terms represents the printed value exactly?

- $\dfrac7{25}$
- $\dfrac29$
- $\dfrac{27}{100}$
- $\dfrac3{11}$

## Explanation

Set $x=0.\overline{27}$. The repeating block has two digits, so $100x=27.\overline{27}$. Subtracting the original equation gives $99x=27$. Dividing numerator and denominator of $\dfrac{27}{99}$ by $9$ yields $\dfrac{3}{11}$.
