One reader reports $30\,^\circ\mathrm C$ with a maximum absolute error of $2\,^\circ\mathrm C$. A second reader measures the same sample at $33\,^\circ\mathrm C$ with a maximum error of $3\,^\circ\mathrm C$. Which temperatures, in degrees Celsius, are consistent with both readings?

- $[28,36]$
- $[28,30]$
- $[30,32]$
- $[32,36]$

## Explanation

The first reading allows temperatures from $28$ to $32$ degrees inclusive. The second allows $30$ to $36$. Satisfying both requires their intersection, $[30,32]$.
