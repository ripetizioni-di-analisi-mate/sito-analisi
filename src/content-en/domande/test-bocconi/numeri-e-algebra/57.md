The pair $(2,-1)$ is a solution of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
ax+3y=5,\\
x+by=4.
\end{cases}
$$
What is $a+b$?

- $6$
- $-1$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

A pair is a solution of the system if it satisfies both equations, so we substitute $x=2$ and $y=-1$. The first equation becomes $2a-3=5$, hence $2a=8$ and $a=4$. The second equation becomes $2-b=4$, hence $b=-2$. Therefore $a+b=4+(-2)=2$.
