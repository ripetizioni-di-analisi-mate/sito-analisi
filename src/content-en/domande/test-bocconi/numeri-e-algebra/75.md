For which real values of $x$ is the expression $\dfrac{\sqrt{x+3}}{x-1}$ defined?

- $(-3,1)\cup(1,+\infty)$
- $[-3,1)\cup(1,+\infty)$
- $[-3,+\infty)$
- $(1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The radicand must be nonnegative and the denominator must be nonzero, so
$$
\begin{cases}
x+3\ge0,\\
x-1\ne0.
\end{cases}
$$
The first condition gives $x\ge-3$ and the second excludes $x=1$. Therefore the domain is $[-3,1)\cup(1,+\infty)$.
