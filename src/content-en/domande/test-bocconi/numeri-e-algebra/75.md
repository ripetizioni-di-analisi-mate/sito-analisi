To divide a budget, the first department receives $\dfrac1{1\cdot2}$ of the total, the second $\dfrac1{2\cdot3}$, and so on through the forty-ninth, which receives $\dfrac1{49\cdot50}$. Given $\dfrac1{n(n+1)}=\dfrac1n-\dfrac1{n+1}$, what fraction of the budget remains?

- $\dfrac1{49}$
- $\dfrac{49}{50}$
- $\dfrac1{2450}$
- $\dfrac1{50}$

## Explanation

The sum of the shares starts with $1-\dfrac12+\dfrac12-\dfrac13$ and ends with $\dfrac1{49}-\dfrac1{50}$. All intermediate terms cancel. Together the departments receive $1-\dfrac1{50}$, leaving $\dfrac1{50}$ of the budget.
