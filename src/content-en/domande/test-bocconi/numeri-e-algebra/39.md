Solve $|x-4|=2x-1$.

- $\left\{\dfrac53\right\}$
- $\{3\}$
- $\{-3\}$
- $\left\{-3,\dfrac53\right\}$

## Explanation

For $x\ge4$, the equation becomes $x-4=2x-1$, giving $x=-3$ outside that case. For $x<4$, it becomes $4-x=2x-1$, so $x=\dfrac53$. Both sides equal $\dfrac73$ there.
