A printer has 84 postcards and 126 stickers. All are used to make the greatest possible number of identical packs. How many stickers go in each pack?

- $6$
- $2$
- $42$
- $3$

## Explanation

The number of packs must divide both stocks. The greatest common divisor is $42$, since $84=2\cdot42$ and $126=3\cdot42$ and the quotients $2$ and $3$ are coprime. Sharing $126$ stickers among $42$ packs puts $3$ in each.
