Among the numbers $\dfrac57$, $0.72$, $\dfrac{13}{18}$ and $\dfrac7{10}$, which is the greatest?

- $\dfrac57$
- $0.72$
- $\dfrac{13}{18}$
- $\dfrac7{10}$

## Explanation

Write the numbers in decimal form. We have $\dfrac57=0.714\ldots$ and $\dfrac{13}{18}=0.722\ldots$, while $\dfrac7{10}=0.7$. Since $0.722\ldots>0.72>0.714\ldots>0.7$, the greatest is $\dfrac{13}{18}$.
