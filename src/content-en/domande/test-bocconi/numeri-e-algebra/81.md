A depot must serve two sites on the same road, at kilometre markers $-2$ and $10$. Its position $x$ should minimise the larger of the two distances. What is the smallest possible value of that distance?

- $12\,\mathrm{km}$
- $4\,\mathrm{km}$
- $8\,\mathrm{km}$
- $6\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

The sites are $12$ kilometres apart. If both depot distances were below $6$, their sum would be less than $12$, which is impossible. At the midpoint $x=4$ each distance is $6$, so the lower bound is attained.
