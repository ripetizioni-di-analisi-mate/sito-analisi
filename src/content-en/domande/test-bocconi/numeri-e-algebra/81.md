Find all real solutions of the equation $(x^2-4)(x^2+1)=0$.

- $\{-2,2\}$
- $\{2\}$
- $\{-2,-1,1,2\}$
- $\{-1,4\}$

## Explanation

A product equals zero exactly when at least one of its factors equals zero. The factor $x^2-4=(x-2)(x+2)$ vanishes at $x=2$ and at $x=-2$. The factor $x^2+1$ is greater than or equal to $1$ for every real $x$, so it never vanishes. Therefore the solutions are $-2$ and $2$.
