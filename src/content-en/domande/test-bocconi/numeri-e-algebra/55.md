One machine could complete an order on its own in 6 hours and another in 3 hours. The first works alone for an hour, then both work together at constant rates. What is the total elapsed time?

- $2\,\mathrm{h}$
- $1\,\mathrm{h}\ 40\,\mathrm{min}$
- $3\,\mathrm{h}\ 30\,\mathrm{min}$
- $2\,\mathrm{h}\ 40\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

After one hour, $1-\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{5}{6}$ of the order remains. Together the machines produce $\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{1}{2}$ an order per hour. The remaining time is $\dfrac{\dfrac{5}{6}}{\dfrac{1}{2}}=\dfrac{5}{3}$ hours, or one hour and forty minutes, on top of the first hour.
