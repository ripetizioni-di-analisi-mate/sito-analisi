At a university, $30\,\%$ of the students are non-local. Among the non-local students, $40\,\%$ live abroad, and every student who lives abroad is non-local. What percentage of all the students live abroad?

- $70\,\%$
- $12\,\%$
- $10\,\%$
- $18\,\%$

## Explanation

The reference value of the $40\,\%$ is the group of non-local students, which is $30\,\%$ of all students. Hence the students living abroad are $\dfrac{40}{100}\cdot\dfrac{30}{100}=\dfrac{12}{100}$ of the total, that is, $12\,\%$.
