A menu offers seven different side dishes. Any subset may be chosen, but salad is compulsory and potatoes are excluded. How many selections are possible?

- $32$
- $128$
- $64$
- $5$

## Explanation

The choices for salad and potatoes are fixed. Each of the other five dishes may be included or left out. The five independent decisions produce $2^5=32$ subsets.
