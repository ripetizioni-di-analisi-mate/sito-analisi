A cyclist covers $1.8\,\mathrm{km}$ at a constant speed of $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How many seconds does the journey take?

- $150\,\mathrm{s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{s}$
- $216\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The distance is $1\,800\,\mathrm{m}$. Dividing the distance by the metres covered each second gives $\dfrac{1\,800}{12}\,\mathrm{s}=150\,\mathrm{s}$.
