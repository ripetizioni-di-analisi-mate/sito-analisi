A subscription price rises by $15\,\%$ and then falls by $20\,\%$ from its new price. What is the overall percentage change, including its sign?

- $-12\,\%$
- $-8\,\%$
- $+8\,\%$
- $-5\,\%$

## Explanation

The successive factors are $1.15$ and $0.8$. Their product is $0.92$, so the final price is $92\,\%$ of the original price. The change is $-8\,\%$.
