For which values of $x$ is the trinomial $x^2-x-6$ positive?

- $(-2,3)$
- $(-\infty,-3)\cup(2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-2]\cup[3,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The trinomial factors as $(x+2)(x-3)$, so it vanishes at $x=-2$ and at $x=3$. The product is positive when the two factors have the same sign, that is, for $x<-2$ or for $x>3$. The zeros are excluded because the inequality is strict.
