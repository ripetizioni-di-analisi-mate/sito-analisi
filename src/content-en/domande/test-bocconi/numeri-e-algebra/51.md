For which integer $n$ does the fraction $\dfrac n7$ lie between $\dfrac35$ and $\dfrac56$?

- $4$
- $5$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

The denominators are positive, so we can compare cross products. The condition $\dfrac35<\dfrac n7$ is equivalent to $21<5n$, that is, $n>\dfrac{21}5$. The condition $\dfrac n7<\dfrac56$ is equivalent to $6n<35$, that is, $n<\dfrac{35}6$. The only integer between $\dfrac{21}5$ and $\dfrac{35}6$ is $5$.
