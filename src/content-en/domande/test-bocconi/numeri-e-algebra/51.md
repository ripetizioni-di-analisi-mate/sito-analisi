Two rolls are labelled 8 and 12 metres, rounded to the nearest metre with halves rounded up. What is the exact interval of possible combined lengths, in metres?

- $(19,21]$
- $[19.5,20.5)$
- $[19,21)$
- $[20,21)$

## Explanation

The actual lengths lie in $[7.5,8.5)$ and $[11.5,12.5)$. Their sums start at $19$ inclusive and approach $21$ without reaching it. Varying the two lengths within their intervals gives every intermediate value.
