The remainder when $P(x)$ is divided by $x^2-1$ has the form $ax+b$. Given $P(1)=7$ and $P(-1)=1$, what is the remainder?

- $4x+3$
- $7x+1$
- $6x+1$
- $3x+4$

## Explanation

At $1$ and $-1$ the divisor vanishes, so $P$ and the remainder agree. Thus $a+b=7$ and $-a+b=1$. Adding gives $b=4$, and then $a=3$.
