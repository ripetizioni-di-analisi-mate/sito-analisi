A prize of $360\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is shared among three people in parts proportional to $2$, $3$ and $4$. How much does the person with the largest share receive?

- $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $144\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $160\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

There are $2+3+4=9$ parts in total, so each part is worth $\dfrac{360}9=40\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The largest share corresponds to $4$ parts and is therefore $4\cdot40=160\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
