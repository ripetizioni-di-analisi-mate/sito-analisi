Two boxes hold tokens in the ratio $3:2$. After 7 tokens are moved from the first box to the second, the boxes hold equal numbers. How many tokens are there in all?

- $35$
- $49$
- $70$
- $84$

## Explanation

Writing $u$ for one part gives initial quantities $3u$ and $2u$. The transfer implies $3u-7=2u+7$, so $u=14$. The five parts total $5\cdot14=70$.
