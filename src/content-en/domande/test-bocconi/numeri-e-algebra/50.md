What is the smallest positive integer $k$ for which $180k$ is the square of an integer?

- $5$
- $10$
- $2$
- $45$

## Explanation

Factorising gives $180=2^2\cdot3^2\cdot5$. Every prime exponent in a square is even. One factor of $5$ is missing, and indeed $180\cdot5=900=30^2$.
