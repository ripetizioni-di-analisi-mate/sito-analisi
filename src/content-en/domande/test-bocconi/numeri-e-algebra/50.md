Which number on the number line is at the same distance from $-\dfrac23$ and from $\dfrac54$?

- $\dfrac7{12}$
- $\dfrac{23}{24}$
- $\dfrac7{24}$
- $\dfrac{23}{12}$

## Explanation

The point equidistant from two numbers is their arithmetic mean. With the denominator $12$ the two numbers become $-\dfrac8{12}$ and $\dfrac{15}{12}$, so their sum is $\dfrac7{12}$. Half of the sum is $\dfrac7{24}$. Indeed, the distance from $\dfrac7{24}$ to each of the two numbers is $\dfrac{23}{24}$.
