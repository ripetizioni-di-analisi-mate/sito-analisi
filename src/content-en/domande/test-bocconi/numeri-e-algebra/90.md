Find the value of $k$ for which the system
$$
\begin{cases}
2x+ky=3,\\
4x+6y=5
\end{cases}
$$
has no solutions.

- $k=-3$
- $k=12$
- $k=2$
- $k=3$

## Explanation

The system has no solutions when the two equations represent distinct parallel lines. For $k=3$, twice the first equation is $4x+6y=6$, which is incompatible with $4x+6y=5$. For $k\ne3$ we have $2\cdot6-4k\ne0$, so the coefficients of $x$ and $y$ are not proportional and the lines have exactly one common point.
