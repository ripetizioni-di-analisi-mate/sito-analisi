A retailer sells two items for $120$ euros each. The first makes a profit of $20\,\%$ of its purchase cost, and the second a loss of $20\,\%$ of its purchase cost. What is the overall outcome?

- A profit of $10$ euros.
- A loss of $24$ euros.
- A loss of $10$ euros.
- Break-even.

## Explanation

The first item cost $\dfrac{120}{1.2}=100$ euros. The second cost $\dfrac{120}{0.8}=150$ euros. Total revenue is $240$ euros against a total cost of $250$, giving a net loss of $10$ euros.
