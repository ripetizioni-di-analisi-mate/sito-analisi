A tariff rounds each call up to a whole minute. Three calls last $25$, $25$ and $70$ seconds. How many more minutes are billed than if the total duration were rounded just once?

- $2$
- $1$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

Rounded separately, the calls are billed as $1+1+2=4$ minutes. Adding the seconds first gives $25+25+70=120$, exactly $2$ minutes. The difference between the two methods is $4-2=2$.
