Which real numbers $x$ satisfy the inequality $|3-2x|\le5$?

- $[-4,1]$
- $(-1,4)$
- $[-1,4]$
- $(-\infty,-1]\cup[4,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The condition $|u|\le5$ is equivalent to $-5\le u\le5$, so $-5\le3-2x\le5$. Subtracting $3$ gives $-8\le-2x\le2$. Dividing by $-2$ reverses the inequalities, so $-1\le x\le4$.
