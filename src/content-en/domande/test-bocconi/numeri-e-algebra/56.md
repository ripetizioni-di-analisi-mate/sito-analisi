A laboratory has $17$ tubes and must supply at least $100$. It can buy only whole boxes of $12$. How many tubes will remain after delivering exactly $100$ if it buys as few boxes as possible?

- $13$
- $1$
- $7$
- $11$

## Explanation

The shortfall is $100-17=83$. Six boxes would give only $72$, whereas seven give $84$. The new stock is $17+84=101$, leaving one tube after delivery.
