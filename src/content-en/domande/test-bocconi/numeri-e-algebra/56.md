A tap delivers $12$ liters of water per minute. How many cubic metres of water does it deliver in one hour?

- $7.2\,\mathrm{m}^3$
- $72\,\mathrm{m}^3$
- $0.072\,\mathrm{m}^3$
- $0.72\,\mathrm{m}^3$

## Explanation

An hour has $60$ minutes, so the tap delivers $12\cdot60=720\,\mathrm{L}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{m}^3=1\,000\,\mathrm{dm}^3$ and $1\,\mathrm{L}=1\,\mathrm{dm}^3$, we get $\dfrac{720}{1\,000}\,\mathrm{m}^3=0.72\,\mathrm{m}^3$.
