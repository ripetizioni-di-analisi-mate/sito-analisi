Compute $\dfrac1{x-1}-\dfrac1{x+1}$ for $x\ne\pm1$.

- $\dfrac2{x^2-1}$
- $\dfrac{-2}{x^2-1}$
- $\dfrac{2x}{x^2-1}$
- $\dfrac1{x^2-1}$

## Explanation

The common denominator is $(x-1)(x+1)=x^2-1$. The numerator becomes $(x+1)-(x-1)=2$, so the difference equals $\dfrac2{x^2-1}$.
