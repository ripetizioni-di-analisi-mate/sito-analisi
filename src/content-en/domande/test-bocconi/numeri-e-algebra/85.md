Find the set of real numbers satisfying $(x-1)^2(x+4)\le0$.

- $(-\infty,-4]$
- $[-4,1]$
- $(-\infty,-4]\cup\{1\}$
- $(-\infty,1]$

## Explanation

The factor $(x-1)^2$ is positive for $x\ne1$ and equals zero at $x=1$. For $x\ne1$ the product therefore has the sign of $x+4$, so it is less than or equal to zero for $x\le-4$. At $x=1$ the product equals zero and the inequality is satisfied. The solutions are $(-\infty,-4]\cup\{1\}$.
