Two instruments each measure a length with an absolute error of $1\,\mathrm{cm}$. The reference lengths are $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $80\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the ratio of the first relative error to the second?

- $1$
- $4$
- $60$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

Relative error is absolute error divided by the reference length. The relative errors are $\dfrac{1}{20}$ and $\dfrac{1}{80}$. Taking their ratio in the stated order gives $\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{20}}{\dfrac{1}{80}}=4$.
