A piggy bank holds $14$ coins, some worth $2\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the others worth $5\,\mathrm{EUR}$, for a total of $43\,\mathrm{EUR}$. How many $5\,\mathrm{EUR}$ coins are there?

- $9$
- $5$
- $7$
- $6$

## Explanation

Let $x$ be the number of $2\,\mathrm{EUR}$ coins and $y$ the number of $5\,\mathrm{EUR}$ coins. Then
$$
\begin{cases}
x+y=14,\\
2x+5y=43.
\end{cases}
$$
Subtracting twice the first equation from the second gives $3y=15$, so $y=5$. There are $9$ coins worth $2\,\mathrm{EUR}$, and indeed $18+25=43$.
