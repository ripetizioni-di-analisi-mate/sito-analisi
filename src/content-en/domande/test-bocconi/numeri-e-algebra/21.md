A rectangular container has dimensions $20\,\mathrm{cm}$, $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $40\,\mathrm{cm}$. The first is doubled, the second is halved and the third stays fixed. What is the ratio of final volume to initial volume?

- $2$
- $\dfrac12$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

Volume is the product of the three side lengths. The changes multiply those factors by $2$, $\dfrac12$ and $1$, so volume is multiplied by $2\cdot\dfrac12\cdot1=1$.
