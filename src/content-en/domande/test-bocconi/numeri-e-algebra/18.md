After a $12.5\,\%$ discount, a ticket costs $70\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What was the price before the discount?

- $61.25\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $78.75\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $84\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The discount is $\dfrac18$ of the original price. Thus $70\,\mathrm{EUR}$ represents $\dfrac78$ of the price and the original amount is $70\cdot\dfrac87=80\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
