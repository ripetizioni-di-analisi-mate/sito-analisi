A $102\,\mathrm{cm}$ bar is divided into four equal pieces using three cuts. Each cut removes $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ of material. How long is one piece?

- $25.35\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $25.50\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $25.30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The three cuts remove $6$ millimetres, or $0.6$ centimetres. This leaves $102-0.6=101.4$ centimetres of usable bar. Each of the four pieces measures $\dfrac{101.4}{4}=25.35$ centimetres.
