For $A=\{\varnothing,\{2\}\}$, which statement is true?

- $\{2\}\subseteq A$
- $\{2\}\in A$
- $2\in A$
- $\varnothing\notin A$

## Explanation

The listed elements are the empty set and the singleton $\{2\}$. Because $2$ itself is absent, $\{2\}$ is an element of $A$ and is not a subset of it.
