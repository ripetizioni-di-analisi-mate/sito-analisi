A code consists of one letter from $3$ letters and one digit from $5$ distinct digits, including zero. The second position cannot contain zero. How many codes are allowed?

- $14$
- $12$
- $7$
- $15$

## Explanation

Four digits remain available. Each letter can be paired with any of the four, so the Cartesian product has $3\cdot4=12$ elements.
