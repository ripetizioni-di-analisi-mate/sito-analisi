What is the set of real solutions of the inequality $|2x+7|>-3$?

- $\varnothing$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-5,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-5)\cup(-2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The absolute value of a real number is greater than or equal to zero, so $|2x+7|\ge0$ for every real $x$. Since $0>-3$, it follows that $|2x+7|>-3$ for every $x$. Therefore the solution set is $\mathbb R$.
