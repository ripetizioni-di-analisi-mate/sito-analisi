The extra cost of a process is modelled by $x^2-10x+k$. It must always be non-negative and must vanish at exactly one value of $x$. Which value of $k$ is required?

- $5$
- $25$
- $100$
- $10$

## Explanation

Completing the square gives $(x-5)^2+k-25$. Its minimum occurs at $x=5$ and equals $k-25$. For exactly one zero and no negative values the minimum must be $0$, which gives $k=25$.
