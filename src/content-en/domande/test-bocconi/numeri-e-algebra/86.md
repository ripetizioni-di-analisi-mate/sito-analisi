Solve the system of inequalities
$$
\begin{cases}
2x-3>1,\\
x^2\le16.
\end{cases}
$$

- $[-4,4]$
- $(2,+\infty)$
- $[2,4]$
- $(2,4]$

## Explanation

The first inequality is equivalent to $2x>4$, that is, $x>2$. The second is equivalent to $|x|\le4$, that is, $-4\le x\le4$. The values satisfying both conditions are those with $2<x\le4$.
