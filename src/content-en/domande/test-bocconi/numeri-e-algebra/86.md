A program classifies an expression as rational if its value can be written as a ratio of integers. Which expression should it classify as rational?

- $\sqrt{12}+\sqrt3$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{12}}{3}$
- $\sqrt{12}\,\sqrt3$
- $\sqrt{12}-\sqrt3$

## Explanation

Since $\sqrt{12}=2\sqrt3$, the product is $2(\sqrt3)^2=6$. The sum is $3\sqrt3$, the difference is $\sqrt3$ and the quotient is $\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{3}$. They are nonzero rational multiples of the irrational number $\sqrt3$.
