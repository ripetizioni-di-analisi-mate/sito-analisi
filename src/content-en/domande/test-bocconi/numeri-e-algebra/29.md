Which simplification of $\dfrac{x^2-9}{x^2-5x+6}$ preserves the original domain?

- $\dfrac{x+3}{x-2},\ x\ne2$
- $\dfrac{x+3}{x-3},\ x\notin\{2,3\}$
- $\dfrac{x-3}{x-2},\ x\notin\{2,3\}$
- $\dfrac{x+3}{x-2},\ x\notin\{2,3\}$

## Explanation

The numerator is $(x-3)(x+3)$ and the denominator is $(x-3)(x-2)$. Cancel $x-3$ while keeping both original exclusions, $3$ and $2$.
