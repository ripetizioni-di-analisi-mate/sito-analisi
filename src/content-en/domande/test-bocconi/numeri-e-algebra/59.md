Let $A=[-1,4)$ and $B=(2,6]$. Find $A\setminus B$.

- $[-1,2]$
- $[-1,2)$
- $(4,6]$
- $[-1,6]$

## Explanation

The set $A\setminus B$ contains the elements of $A$ that do not belong to $B$. A number in $A$ also belongs to $B$ exactly when it is greater than $2$, so the numbers with $-1\le x\le2$ remain. The endpoint $2$ is included because $2\notin B$.
