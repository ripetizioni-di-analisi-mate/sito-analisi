For $a<b<0$, which chain correctly orders the four expressions shown?

- $\dfrac1b<\dfrac1a<0<-a$
- $\dfrac1a<\dfrac1b<0<-a$
- $-a<0<\dfrac1b<\dfrac1a$
- $\dfrac1b<0<\dfrac1a<-a$

## Explanation

The product $ab$ is positive. Dividing $a<b$ by $ab$ gives $\dfrac{1}{b}<\dfrac{1}{a}$. Both reciprocals are negative, while $-a$ is positive, giving the stated order.
