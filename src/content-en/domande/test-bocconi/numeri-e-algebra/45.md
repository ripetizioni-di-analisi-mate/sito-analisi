How many ordered real pairs $(x,y)$ satisfy $x^2+y^2=25$ and $xy=12$?

- $4$
- $8$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

We have $(x+y)^2=25+2\cdot12=49$, so the sum is $7$ or $-7$. With product $12$, the unordered pairs are $\{3,4\}$ and $\{-3,-4\}$. Each has two orders, giving four pairs.
