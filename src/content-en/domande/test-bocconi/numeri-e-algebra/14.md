Find the real roots of $2x^2-7x+3=0$.

- $\left\{-\dfrac12,-3\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac32,2\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac12,3\right\}$
- $\{1,3\}$

## Explanation

Expanding $(2x-1)(x-3)$ yields the quadratic, as the linear term is $-6x-x=-7x$. The two factors vanish at $\dfrac12$ and $3$.
