Find the real domain of $\sqrt{(x-2)(x+5)}$.

- $[2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-5]\cup[2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-5)\cup(2,+\infty)$
- $[-5,2]$

## Explanation

The product is positive when both factors have the same sign, so outside the interval between $-5$ and $2$. At the endpoints it is zero and the root is also defined there.
