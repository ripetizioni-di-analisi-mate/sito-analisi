A spare-parts price index is $125$ in May and $155$ in July on the same base. What would the July index be if May were the new base period?

- $130$
- $124$
- $155$
- $120$

## Explanation

The index on the new base is $100\cdot\dfrac{155}{125}=124$. The ratio uses May's level as the denominator.
