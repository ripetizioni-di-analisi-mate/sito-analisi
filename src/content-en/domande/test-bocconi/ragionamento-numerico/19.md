A laboratory records requests received through two channels. Each request is classified as confirmed or unconfirmed.

| Channel | Confirmed | Unconfirmed |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Online | 48 | 12 |
| In person | 36 | 24 |

What fraction of the *confirmed* requests arrived in person?

- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$

## Explanation

Of the $48+36=84$ confirmed requests, $36$ arrived in person. The fraction is $\dfrac{36}{84}=\dfrac{3}{7}$.
