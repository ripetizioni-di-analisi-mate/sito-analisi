A report shows $63\%$ of $320$ participants as satisfied. The percentage has been rounded to the nearest whole percent, with halfway values rounded up. Which count is consistent with the published figure?

- $204$
- $206$
- $203$
- $199$

## Explanation

The exact rate is at least $62.5\%$ and below $63.5\%$. The count is therefore at least $200$ and less than $203.2$. Of the proposed counts, $203$ is consistent.
