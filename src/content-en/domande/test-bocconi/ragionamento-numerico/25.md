A train has $120$ seats. For the first $20\,\mathrm{km}$, exactly $90$ seats are occupied throughout. For the final $10\,\mathrm{km}$, exactly $60$ are occupied throughout. Utilisation is occupied seat-kilometres divided by available seat-kilometres. What is the utilisation?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

There are $90\cdot 20+60\cdot 10=2{,}400$ occupied seat-kilometres out of $120\cdot 30=3{,}600$ available. The proportion is $\dfrac{2{,}400}{3{,}600}=\dfrac{2}{3}$.
