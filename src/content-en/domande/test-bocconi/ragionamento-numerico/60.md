The chart shows the cases closed each day by an office, from Monday (day $1$) to Friday (day $5$). On which day does the number of cases closed since the start of the week first exceed $80$?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=6 ymin=0 ymax=36 poligoni="0.7,0 1.3,0 1.3,18 0.7,18;1.7,0 2.3,0 2.3,25 1.7,25;2.7,0 3.3,0 3.3,14 2.7,14;3.7,0 4.3,0 4.3,30 3.7,30;4.7,0 5.3,0 5.3,22 4.7,22" etichette="1,20,18;2,27,25;3,16,14;4,32,30;5,24,22" titolo="Cases closed on days 1 to 5: 18, 25, 14, 30 and 22."}

- Wednesday
- Tuesday
- Thursday
- Friday

## Explanation

The total since the start of the week is obtained by adding the bars day by day. It is $18$ at the end of Monday, $18+25=43$ at the end of Tuesday, $43+14=57$ at the end of Wednesday and $57+30=87$ at the end of Thursday. The total therefore stays below $80$ until Wednesday and first exceeds it on Thursday.
