A packaging line repeats the pattern A, B, A, C. An inspector takes the first package and every fourth one thereafter, at positions 1, 5, 9 and so on through position 33. What percentage of the sampled packages are type A?

- $100\,\%$
- $50\,\%$
- $75\,\%$
- $25\,\%$

## Explanation

Four positions separate successive samples, exactly one full cycle. The inspector always selects the first position of the cycle, which contains A. Thus 100% of the sample is A even though A occupies only half the positions on the line.
