A ferry company publishes the paying passengers on one route over one week. A note states that children under $4$ travel free and are not included in the table; $40$ of them boarded during the week.

| Direction | Crossings | Paying passengers |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Outbound | 10 | 850 |
| Return | 10 | 750 |

On average, how many people boarded each crossing, including children under $4$?

- $80$
- $164$
- $85$
- $82$

## Explanation

The table excludes children under $4$, so the people who boarded are the $850+750=1{,}600$ paying passengers plus the $40$ children given in the note, that is $1{,}640$. There are $10+10=20$ crossings, because the outbound and the return crossings are distinct. The mean per crossing is therefore $\dfrac{1{,}640}{20}=82$ people. Without the children one would get $\dfrac{1{,}600}{20}=80$, which is the mean of the paying passengers only.
