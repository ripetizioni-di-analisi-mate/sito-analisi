Each complete block of five group tickets includes one free ticket. The others cost 6 euros each. One order covers 23 visitors. How much is payable?

- $114\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $108\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $138\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The 23 tickets contain four complete blocks of five. Four admissions are free, so the cost is $19\cdot6=114$ euros.
