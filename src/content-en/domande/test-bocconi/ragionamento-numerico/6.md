A neighbourhood has $84$ reduced-price subscriptions, exactly $35\%$ of its subscriptions. How many full-price subscriptions does it have, assuming every other subscription is full price?

- $210$
- $240$
- $156$
- $126$

## Explanation

The total is $\dfrac{84}{0.35}=240$. Subtracting the $84$ reduced-price subscriptions gives $240-84=156$ full-price subscriptions.
