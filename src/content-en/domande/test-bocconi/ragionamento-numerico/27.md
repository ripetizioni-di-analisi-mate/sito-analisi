A subscription price falls by $25\%$. By what percentage must it then rise from the reduced price to return to its original level?

- $25\%$
- $20\%$
- $33\dfrac{1}{3}\%$
- $75\%$

## Explanation

On an initial base of $100$, the price falls to $75$. Returning to $100$ requires an increase of $25$ on a base of $75$. The ratio is $\dfrac{25}{75}=\dfrac13$.
