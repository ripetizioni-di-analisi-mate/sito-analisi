An item costs a retailer $72\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and sells for $96\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What percentage of the selling price is the margin, defined as the selling price less the cost?

- $24\%$
- $33\dfrac{1}{3}\%$
- $75\%$
- $25\%$

## Explanation

The margin is $96-72=24\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The selling price is the base because the question asks for the margin as a share of that price. Hence $\dfrac{24}{96}=25\%$.
