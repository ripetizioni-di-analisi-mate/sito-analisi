In a class of $48$ students, $37$ attend a language workshop and $29$ attend a science workshop. What is the *minimum* possible number who attend both?

- $29$
- $18$
- $19$
- $11$

## Explanation

If all $11$ students outside the language workshop attend science, the other $29-11=18$ science students also attend language. This minimises the overlap because every place outside the first group is already used.
