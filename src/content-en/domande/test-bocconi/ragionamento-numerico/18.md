A warehouse has $75$ containers. The Blue mark appears on $43$, the Green mark on $32$ and both marks on $14$. How many bear *exactly one* mark?

- $61$
- $33$
- $75$
- $47$

## Explanation

The Blue-only group contains $43-14=29$ containers. The Green-only group contains $32-14=18$. Their sum is $29+18=47$.
