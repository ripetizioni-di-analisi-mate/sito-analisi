A sample contains 20 small shops and 20 large shops. Annual consumption averages 5 units per small shop and 11 per large shop. These averages are used to estimate the citywide mean. Small shops outnumber large ones four to one in the city. What is the estimate per shop?

- $7$
- $9.8$
- $6.2$
- $8$

## Explanation

The city has four small shops per large one. Its estimated mean is $(4\cdot5+11)\div5=6.2$ units, using city weights.
