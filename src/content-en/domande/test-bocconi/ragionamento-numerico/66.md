An infographic represents turnover with discs whose *area* is proportional to the amount. Disc A is 6 centimetres in diameter and represents 40 thousand euros. Disc B is 9 centimetres in diameter. How many thousand euros does B represent?

- $80$
- $90$
- $135$
- $60$

## Explanation

The diameter ratio is $\dfrac96=\dfrac32$. The area ratio is its square, $\dfrac94$, so the second turnover is $40\cdot\dfrac94=90$ thousand euros.
