A market records stalls over two days. Each stall belongs to exactly one category on each day.

| Day | Food | Crafts | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Saturday | 37 | ? | 64 |
| Sunday | 31 | 39 | 70 |

What is the total number of craft stall appearances, counting a stall present on both days once per day?

- $66$
- $64$
- $68$
- $39$

## Explanation

Saturday has $64-37=27$ craft stalls. Sunday's $39$ count towards the same total of appearances. The total is $27+39=66$.
