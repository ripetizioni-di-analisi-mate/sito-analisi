A team starts with $24$ measurements averaging $18$. It removes $6$ whose mean was $27$. What is the mean of the remaining measurements?

- $15$
- $16$
- $19$
- $17$

## Explanation

The original sum is $24\cdot 18=432$. Removing $6\cdot 27=162$ leaves a sum of $270$ for $18$ measurements, so the mean is $15$.
