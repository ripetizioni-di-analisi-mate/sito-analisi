Higher scores rank first in a competition. Ties go to the earlier submission. The pairs (points, minute submitted) are Ada (82, 17), Bruno (84, 23), Ciro (82, 15) and Dora (80, 9). What is the sum of the submission minutes for the top two entrants?

- $40$
- $32$
- $24$
- $38$

## Explanation

Bruno leads with 84. Of the two entrants on 82, Ciro submitted earlier, so the two submission minutes sum to $23+15=38$.
