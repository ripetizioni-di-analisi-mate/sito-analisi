A hotel classifies its $80$ rooms by type and by view. Each room belongs to exactly one row and one column.

| Rooms | Sea view | Inner side | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Single | 14 | ? | 30 |
| Double | ? | ? | 50 |
| Total | 38 | ? | 80 |

How many double rooms are on the inner side?

- $16$
- $36$
- $26$
- $42$

## Explanation

The “Sea view” column totals $38$ and there are $14$ single rooms with a sea view, so there are $38-14=24$ double rooms with a sea view. There are $50$ double rooms in all, so $50-24=26$ of them are on the inner side. The result agrees with the “Inner side” column, which contains $80-38=42$ rooms, of which $30-14=16$ are single; indeed $16+26=42$.
