A company owns 40 reusable scaffolding units. Today 9 are with customers, 4 are in the workshop and the rest are available. Tomorrow customers return 3 ready for use, and 2 are released by the workshop. A new order needs 30 units. How many will remain available after filling it?

- $0$
- $2$
- $8$
- $5$

## Explanation

Today $40-9-4=27$ units are available. Five become available tomorrow, bringing the total to 32. Filling the order leaves two.
