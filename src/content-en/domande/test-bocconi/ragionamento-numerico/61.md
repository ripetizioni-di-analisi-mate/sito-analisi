The chart shows, for five consecutive years, the annual sales in thousands of units of two products A and B. In which year is the gap between the sales of A and those of B largest?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=6 ymin=0 ymax=45 linee="1,20 2,26 3,30 4,35 5,38;1,14 2,18 3,27 4,28 5,36" punti="1,20,pieno;2,26,pieno;3,30,pieno;4,35,pieno;5,38,pieno;1,14,vuoto;2,18,vuoto;3,27,vuoto;4,28,vuoto;5,36,vuoto" etichette="0.55,21,A;0.55,13,B" titolo="Sales in thousands of units in years 1 to 5. A (filled points): 20, 26, 30, 35, 38. B (open points): 14, 18, 27, 28, 36."}

- In year $4$
- In year $2$
- In year $5$
- In year $1$

## Explanation

The gap in each year is the difference between the vertical coordinates of the two series. From year $1$ to year $5$ it is $20-14=6$, $26-18=8$, $30-27=3$, $35-28=7$ and $38-36=2$ thousand units. The largest value is $8$, so the gap is largest in year $2$. In year $5$ both products sell more, but the two series are closer together.
