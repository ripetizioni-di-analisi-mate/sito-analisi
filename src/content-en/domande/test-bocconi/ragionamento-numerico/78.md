In a company $40\%$ of the employees work on site and, of these, $25\%$ come to work by car. There are $30$ employees who work on site and come by car. How many employees does the company have?

- $120$
- $75$
- $750$
- $300$

## Explanation

The on-site employees who come by car are $25\%$ of $40\%$ of the total $N$, that is $0.25\cdot 0.4\cdot N=0.1N$. The condition $0.1N=30$ gives $N=300$. The value $120$ is the number of on-site employees, since $\dfrac{30}{0.25}=120$.
