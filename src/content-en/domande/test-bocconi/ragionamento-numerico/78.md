A department has three identical machines. Two active machines suffice to meet its target in any hour. On Monday they are stopped for one, two and three hours respectively, with no overlaps, during an eight-hour shift. For how many hours are *all three* available?

- $3$
- $6$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

The disjoint stoppages cover $1+2+3=6$ hours. During the remaining $8-6=2$ hours all three machines are active.
