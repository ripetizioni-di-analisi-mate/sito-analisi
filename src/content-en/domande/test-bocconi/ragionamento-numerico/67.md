In a histogram, bar area gives frequency. The interval from 0 to 2 has height 6, the one from 2 to 6 has height 4, and the one from 6 to 10 has height 3. What share falls from 2 inclusive to 6 exclusive?

- $30\%$
- $25\%$
- $40\%$
- $50\%$

## Explanation

The bar areas are $2\cdot6=12$, $4\cdot4=16$ and $4\cdot3=12$. The middle interval accounts for 16 out of 40 observations, or 40%.
