A delivery firm compares two vans over the same week.

| Van | Kilometres | Diesel (L) | Deliveries |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | 450 | 36 | 60 |
| B | 600 | 42 | 90 |

By how many litres per $100\,\mathrm{km}$ does the fuel consumption of van A exceed that of van B?

- $1\,\mathrm{L}$
- $6\,\mathrm{L}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{L}$
- $0.13\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The required unit is litres per $100\,\mathrm{km}$, so the diesel and the kilometres are needed, while the deliveries play no part. Van A uses $\dfrac{36}{450}\cdot 100=8\,\mathrm{L}$ per $100\,\mathrm{km}$. Van B uses $\dfrac{42}{600}\cdot 100=7\,\mathrm{L}$ per $100\,\mathrm{km}$. The gap is therefore $1\,\mathrm{L}$ per $100\,\mathrm{km}$.
