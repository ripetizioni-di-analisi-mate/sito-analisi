A van travels $60\,\mathrm{km}$ at an average of $40\,\mathrm{km/h}$ on the outward journey and covers the same route at $60\,\mathrm{km/h}$ on the return. What is the average speed for the whole trip, excluding stops?

- $52\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $48\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $45\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $50\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

The outward trip takes $\dfrac{60}{40}=\dfrac{3}{2}$ hours and the return takes one hour. Divide the $120\,\mathrm{km}$ by the total time of $\dfrac{5}{2}$ hours, giving $48\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
