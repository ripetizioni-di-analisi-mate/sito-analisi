A helpline answers $72$ calls during a four-hour shift, including one hour of scheduled downtime with no calls. What is the mean number of calls answered per hour of actual service?

- $36$
- $18$
- $20$
- $24$

## Explanation

The service is active for $4-1=3$ hours. Dividing the calls by the active hours gives $\dfrac{72}{3}=24$ calls per hour.
