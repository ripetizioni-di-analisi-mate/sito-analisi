A nursery's fixed budget used to buy exactly $50$ pots. The unit price index rises from its initial level of $100$ to $125$. By what percentage does the number of pots the budget can buy fall?

- $25\%$
- $10\%$
- $12.5\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

With a fixed budget, the number of pots is inversely proportional to the unit price. The budget now buys $50\cdot\dfrac{100}{125}=40$ pots. The fall is $\dfrac{50-40}{50}=20\%$.
