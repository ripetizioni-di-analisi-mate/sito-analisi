A delivery service records $1.2$ thousand kilometres for its North route and $680\,\mathrm{km}$ for its South route. How many kilometres did it travel on the two routes in total?

- $692\,\mathrm{km}$
- $1\,880\,\mathrm{km}$
- $1\,240\,\mathrm{km}$
- $6\,920\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

The first route is $1.2\cdot 1{,}000=1{,}200\,\mathrm{km}$. Adding $1{,}200+680$ gives $1{,}880\,\mathrm{km}$.
