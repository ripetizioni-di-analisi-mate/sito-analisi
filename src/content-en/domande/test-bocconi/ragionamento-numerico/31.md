An item costs $171\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at a $10\%$ discount. What would it cost with a $10\%$ increase on the original price instead of the discount?

- $190\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $209\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $188.10\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $205.20\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The original price is $\dfrac{171}{0.9}=190\,\mathrm{EUR}$. A $10\%$ increase gives $190\cdot 1.1=209\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
