A shop records inventory at original cost. Its book value falls from 600 to 480 euros with no purchases or losses; the entire fall comes from sales. The items sold fetched twice their recorded cost. What were the sales receipts?

- $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $240\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $960\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The $600-480=120$ euro decrease is the cost of the goods sold. Receipts at twice that cost are $2\cdot120=240$ euros.
