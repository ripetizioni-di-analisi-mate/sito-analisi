The mean of $10$ measurements recorded by a laboratory is $45$. It turns out that a measurement equal to $72$ had been recorded as $27$. What is the corrected mean of the $10$ measurements?

- $49.5$
- $40.5$
- $90$
- $4.5$

## Explanation

The recorded sum is $10\cdot 45=450$. The correction adds $72-27=45$ to the sum, so the corrected sum is $495$. The number of measurements is still $10$, so the corrected mean is $\dfrac{495}{10}=49.5$.
