Of the members of a library, $70\%$ are women. Of the women members $40\%$ are under $25$, and of the men members $10\%$ are under $25$. What percentage of the members are under $25$?

- $25\%$
- $50\%$
- $31\%$
- $19\%$

## Explanation

Out of $100$ members, $70$ are women and $30$ are men. The women under $25$ number $0.4\cdot 70=28$ and the men under $25$ number $0.1\cdot 30=3$. So $31$ members out of $100$ are under $25$, that is $31\%$. The simple mean of the two rates, $25\%$, would give the same weight to two groups of different sizes.
