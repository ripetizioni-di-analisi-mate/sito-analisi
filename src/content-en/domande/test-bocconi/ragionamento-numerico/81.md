Eighteen panels 50 centimetres wide are fitted in a row. A single 2-centimetre seal goes between each neighbouring pair, with no seals at the outer ends. What is the total length in centimetres?

- $938$
- $918$
- $936$
- $934$

## Explanation

The panels take up $18\cdot50=900$ centimetres. There are 17 internal gaps, contributing another $17\cdot2=34$ centimetres.
