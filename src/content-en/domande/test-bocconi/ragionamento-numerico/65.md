Two teams of painters work on different days. Team A, of $3$ people, paints $144\,\mathrm{m^2}$ in $6$ hours; team B, of $2$ people, paints $120\,\mathrm{m^2}$ in $8$ hours. Each person works for the whole time stated. By how many square metres per person-hour does team A's output exceed team B's?

- $9\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

Person-hours are the number of people times the hours worked, so team A uses $3\cdot 6=18$ of them and team B $2\cdot 8=16$. Team A's output is $\dfrac{144}{18}=8\,\mathrm{m^2}$ per person-hour and team B's is $\dfrac{120}{16}=7.5\,\mathrm{m^2}$ per person-hour. The gap is therefore $8-7.5=0.5\,\mathrm{m^2}$ per person-hour.
