Seven workstations must be divided proportionally among departments with 51, 29 and 20 employees. Each department first gets the integer part of its theoretical quota, and remaining stations go to the largest fractional remainders. What allocation results in the given order?

- 4, 2, 1
- 3, 2, 2
- 4, 1, 2
- 3, 3, 1

## Explanation

There are $100$ employees, giving theoretical quotas $3.57$, $2.03$ and $1.40$. The integer parts allocate six stations. The largest remainder is $0.57$, so the seventh station goes to the first department.
