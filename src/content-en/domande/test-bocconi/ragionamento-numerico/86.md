A table groups payments as “under 10 euros”, “10 to under 20” and “20 or more”. A payment first recorded as 19 euros is corrected to 20. How does the *number in the last band minus the number in the middle band* change?

- It stays unchanged.
- It increases by 1.
- It decreases by 2.
- It increases by 2.

## Explanation

The last band gains one payment and the middle band loses one. In the difference the first change adds one and the second adds one more.
