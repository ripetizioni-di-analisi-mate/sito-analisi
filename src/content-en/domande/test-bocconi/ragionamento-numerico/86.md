In a call centre the mean waiting time is $12$ minutes in the morning shift and $18$ minutes in the afternoon shift. Both shifts received at least one call. Which of the following values is impossible as the mean waiting time of all the day's calls?

- $11$ minutes
- $13$ minutes
- $15$ minutes
- $17$ minutes

## Explanation

The mean for the day is the mean of the two shifts weighted by the number of calls in each. With positive weights a weighted mean lies between the smallest and the largest value, so here it lies strictly between $12$ and $18$ minutes. Hence $11$ minutes is impossible. The values $13$, $15$ and $17$ are obtained, for example, with $5$ and $1$, with $1$ and $1$, and with $1$ and $5$ calls in the morning and afternoon respectively.
