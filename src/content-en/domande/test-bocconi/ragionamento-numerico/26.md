In district A, $54$ of $900$ households apply for a grant. In district B, $63$ of $1{,}400$ households apply. How many more applications per thousand households are recorded in A than in B?

- $15$
- $9$
- $12$
- $45$

## Explanation

Per thousand households, A records $\dfrac{54}{900}\cdot 1{,}000=60$ applications and B records $\dfrac{63}{1{,}400}\cdot 1{,}000=45$. The difference is $15$.
