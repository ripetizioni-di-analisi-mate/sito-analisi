Five stalls’ sales, listed from smallest to largest, total $60$ items. The third stall sold $12$ and the last sold $20$. What is the greatest possible number sold by the first stall?

- $8$
- $6$
- $10$
- $12$

## Explanation

The third and fourth stalls each sold at least $12$ items, and the last sold $20$. This leaves at most $60-12-12-20=16$ items for the first two. The smaller of them can reach $8$, as shown by the ordered sales $8,8,12,12,20$.
