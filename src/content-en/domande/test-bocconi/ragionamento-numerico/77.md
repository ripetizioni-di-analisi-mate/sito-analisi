A vertical chart uses a scale where equal intervals correspond to equal multiplicative factors. Two marks are labelled 16 and 100. What value belongs to a mark exactly halfway up between them?

- $42$
- $40$
- $58$
- $64$

## Explanation

Writing $m$ for the middle reading, the ratios must match: $\dfrac{m}{16}=\dfrac{100}{m}$. Thus $m^2=1600$, giving $40$ on the positive scale. Going from $16$ to $40$ and from $40$ to $100$ applies the same factor of $2.5$.
