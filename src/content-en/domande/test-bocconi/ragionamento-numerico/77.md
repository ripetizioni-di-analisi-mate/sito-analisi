With the same base year 2020, the bread price index goes from $120$ in 2022 to $150$ in 2024, while the milk price index goes from $80$ to $96$. Which statement about the change in prices between 2022 and 2024 is true?

- Milk rises by a larger percentage than bread.
- Bread and milk rise by the same percentage.
- Bread rises by $25\%$, more than milk.
- Bread rises by $30\%$, more than milk.

## Explanation

The ratio between two values of the same series equals the ratio between their indices, because the base cancels out. For bread this gives $\dfrac{150}{120}=1.25$, that is a $25\%$ increase. For milk it gives $\dfrac{96}{80}=1.2$, that is a $20\%$ increase. Bread therefore rises more than milk. The $30$ index points gained by bread correspond to $25\%$, because the base of the comparison is the index $120$.
