A survey of $300$ people reports three possible answers with shares of $34\%$, $33\%$ and $34\%$, each rounded to the nearest whole percent; the published shares therefore add up to $101\%$. Each person gave exactly one answer. Which set of counts is consistent with the published shares?

- $101$, $98$ and $101$
- $102$, $99$ and $102$
- $100$, $100$ and $100$
- $103$, $97$ and $100$

## Explanation

The three answers are mutually exclusive, so the counts add up to exactly $300$; hence the set with sum $303$ is ruled out. The percentage share of $c$ people out of $300$ is $\dfrac{100c}{300}=\dfrac{c}{3}$. A share rounded to $34\%$ requires $33.5\le\dfrac{c}{3}<34.5$, that is $100.5\le c<103.5$, while a share rounded to $33\%$ requires $97.5\le c<100.5$. The set $101$, $98$, $101$ satisfies both conditions. The two remaining sets contain $100$, which corresponds to $33\%$, or $97$, which corresponds to $32\%$.
