A tank holds at most 90 litres and contains 72. First 25 litres are poured in, with any excess spilling over. Then 12 litres are drawn out. How many litres remain?

- $83$
- $85$
- $78$
- $90$

## Explanation

Only $90-72=18$ of the 25 litres fit. The tank is full after the overflow, and the withdrawal leaves $90-12=78$ litres.
