An organisation receives $780\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Materials cost between $470$ and $510\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and transport between $50$ and $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$. These are the only expenses. Which statement about the net balance is guaranteed?

- The net balance is at least $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
- The net balance is exactly $235\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
- The net balance is at least $190\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
- The net balance is at most $240\,\mathrm{EUR}$.

## Explanation

For the smallest balance, subtract the highest costs. This gives $780-510-80=190\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The lowest costs would yield $260\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
