A service counts distinct users each week. There are 120 in the first week and 150 in the second. Of the first week’s users, 40% return in the second week. How many second-week users were absent in the first week?

- $198$
- $102$
- $90$
- $72$

## Explanation

There were $120\cdot0.40=48$ returning users. Of the 150 in the second week, $150-48=102$ are new relative to the first.
