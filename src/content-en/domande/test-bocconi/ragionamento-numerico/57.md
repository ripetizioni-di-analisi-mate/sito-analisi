A transport company classifies one week's journeys by punctuality.

| Line | Late | On time | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Urban | 30 | 270 | 300 |
| Suburban | 20 | 80 | 100 |

Which statement is true?

- Urban journeys have a higher share of delays than suburban ones.
- Of the late journeys, $60\%$ are on urban lines.
- $30\%$ of the urban journeys are late.
- Late suburban journeys make up $20\%$ of the late journeys.

## Explanation

There are $30+20=50$ late journeys, so the urban share among them is $\dfrac{30}{50}=60\%$. Among urban journeys, however, the share that is late is $\dfrac{30}{300}=10\%$, while among suburban journeys it is $\dfrac{20}{100}=20\%$. Suburban journeys therefore have the higher share of delays, even though most late journeys are urban. Late suburban journeys make up $\dfrac{20}{50}=40\%$ of the late journeys.
