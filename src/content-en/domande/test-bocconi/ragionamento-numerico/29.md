An association sets aside $35\%$ of its $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ fund for a project. Two sevenths of that allocation go towards materials. How much does it spend on materials?

- $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $175\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $210\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $84\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The project receives $0.35\cdot 600=210\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Materials account for $\dfrac{2}{7}\cdot 210=60\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
