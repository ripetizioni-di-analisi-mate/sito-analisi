Eight athletes in one group average 12 points. Four athletes in another group average 18. An athlete with 12 points moves from the first group to the second. Which pair gives the new averages in the same order?

- $12$ and $18$
- $12$ and $15$
- $10.5$ and $18$
- $12$ and $16.8$

## Explanation

The first group loses an athlete with its average score, so its average remains 12. In the second group the sum rises from 72 to 84 and the count from four to five, giving $84\div5=16.8$.
