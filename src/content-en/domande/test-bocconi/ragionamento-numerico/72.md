In a sports club, members under $30$ make up $40\%$ of the membership. Six new members join, all under $30$, and the share of members under $30$ rises to $50\%$. How many members did the club have before the new members joined?

- $30$
- $60$
- $36$
- $12$

## Explanation

Let $n$ be the initial number of members. Members under $30$ number $0.4n$ before and $0.4n+6$ after, out of a total of $n+6$ members. The new share requires $0.4n+6=0.5(n+6)$, that is $0.4n+6=0.5n+3$. It follows that $0.1n=3$, so $n=30$.
