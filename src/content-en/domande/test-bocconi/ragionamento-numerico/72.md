A two-good price index keeps the initial basket quantities fixed. At initial prices A accounts for 75% of spending and B for 25%. A rises in price by 8% and B falls by 4%. What is the percentage change in the basket cost?

- $+4\%$
- $+5\%$
- $+2\%$
- $+7\%$

## Explanation

Per initial 100 euros, A costs 6 euros more and B costs 1 euro less. The net increase is $6-1=5$ euros, or 5% of the initial spending.
