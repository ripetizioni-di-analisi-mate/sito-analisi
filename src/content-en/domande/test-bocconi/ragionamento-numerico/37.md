Four tests have an average score of $10$ points. A test of the same weight is added. What score must the fifth test have for the new mean to be $12$ points?

- $12$
- $18$
- $14$
- $20$

## Explanation

The known sum is $4\cdot 10=40$ points. For five tests, a mean of $12$ requires $5\cdot 12=60$ points. The added test must therefore score $60-40=20$ points.
