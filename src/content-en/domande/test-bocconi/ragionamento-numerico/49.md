A thermometer assigns 10 minutes to each reading. The high tariff applies only when the reading is strictly above 18 degrees. The six readings are 18, 19, 20, 18, 17 and 19 degrees. For how many minutes does the high tariff apply?

- $30$
- $40$
- $50$
- $20$

## Explanation

The two readings of 19 and the reading of 20 exceed 18 degrees. Three intervals of 10 minutes give 30 minutes.
