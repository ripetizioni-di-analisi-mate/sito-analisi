A weather station measures a wind speed of $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The strong-wind alert is triggered above $50\,\mathrm{km/h}$. By how many kilometres per hour does the measured wind exceed the alert threshold?

- $10\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $35\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $1.1\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $4\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

An hour contains $3{,}600$ seconds and a kilometre contains $1{,}000$ metres, so $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ corresponds to $\dfrac{3{,}600}{1{,}000}=3.6\,\mathrm{km/h}$. The measured wind is therefore $15\cdot 3.6=54\,\mathrm{km/h}$. The excess over the threshold is $54-50=4\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
