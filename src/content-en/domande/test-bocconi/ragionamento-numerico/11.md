The chart shows files scanned over time. The horizontal axis is measured in minutes. How many files are scanned between the fourth and ninth minute?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=10 ymin=0 ymax=50 linee="0,0 4,20 7,29 9,39" punti="4,20,pieno;7,29,pieno;9,39,pieno" titolo="Cumulative files. The total is 20 at 4 minutes, 29 at 7 minutes and 39 at 9 minutes."}

- $19$
- $10$
- $9$
- $39$

## Explanation

Subtract the total at minute four from that at minute nine. The cumulative increase is $39-20=19$ files.
