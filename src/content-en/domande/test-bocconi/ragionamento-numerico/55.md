Two schools take part in a mathematics competition. At the Fermi school, which has $240$ students, $25\%$ of the students take part. At the Volta school, which has $160$ students, $40\%$ take part. How many students from the two schools take part in the competition altogether?

- $65$
- $124$
- $160$
- $100$

## Explanation

Each percentage refers to the students of its own school. So $0.25\cdot 240=60$ students take part from Fermi and $0.4\cdot 160=64$ from Volta. The two schools have different students, so the participants number $60+64=124$.
