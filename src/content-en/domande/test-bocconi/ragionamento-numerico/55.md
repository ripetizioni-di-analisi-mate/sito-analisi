An electricity bill with no fixed charge reports 50 kilowatt-hours in total and a cost of 12 euros. Energy costs 20 cents per kilowatt-hour at the standard rate and 30 cents at peak times. How many kilowatt-hours were used at peak times?

- $10$
- $20$
- $40$
- $30$

## Explanation

At the standard rate all usage would cost 10 euros. The extra 2 euros represent 10 extra cents for each of 20 peak kilowatt-hours.
