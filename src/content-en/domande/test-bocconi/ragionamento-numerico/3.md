The table gives the volume of water in a tank being filled, at three times.

| Hours from start | Volume (L) |
| --- | ---: |
| 0 | 27 |
| 3 | 51 |
| 5 | 63 |

By how many litres per hour does the average rate of increase over the first three hours exceed that over the last two?

- $12\,\mathrm{L/h}$
- $8\,\mathrm{L/h}$
- $6\,\mathrm{L/h}$
- $2\,\mathrm{L/h}$

## Explanation

Over the first three hours the volume increases by $51-27=24\,\mathrm{L}$, so the average rate is $\dfrac{24}{3}=8\,\mathrm{L/h}$. Over the last two hours the volume increases by $63-51=12\,\mathrm{L}$, so the average rate is $\dfrac{12}{2}=6\,\mathrm{L/h}$. Therefore the first rate exceeds the second by $8-6=2\,\mathrm{L/h}$.
