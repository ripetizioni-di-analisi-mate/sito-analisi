The three-day moving average of sales rises from 24 for Monday through Wednesday to 28 for Tuesday through Thursday. Monday’s sales were 19 copies. How many were sold on Thursday?

- $28$
- $23$
- $35$
- $31$

## Explanation

The three-day total rises by $3(28-24)=12$. Tuesday and Wednesday are in both totals, so Thursday exceeds Monday by 12 copies and reaches 31.
