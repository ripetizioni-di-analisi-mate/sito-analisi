A survey interviews $250$ of the $6{,}000$ commuters of a town: $90$ of the people interviewed say they use the train. Which statement follows from the data?

- Exactly $1.5\%$ of the town's commuters use the train.
- $36\%$ of the town's commuters use the train.
- $36\%$ of the people interviewed use the train.
- $2{,}160$ of the town's commuters use the train.

## Explanation

The share $\dfrac{90}{250}=36\%$ has the interviewees as its base, so it describes with certainty only the group interviewed. Extending it to all $6{,}000$ commuters, which gives $0.36\cdot 6{,}000=2{,}160$, is an estimate that depends on how well the sample represents the town. The ratio $\dfrac{90}{6{,}000}=1.5\%$ counts only the interviewees who use the train. Hence the share for the whole town is at least $1.5\%$ and may be higher.
