Delivery times for 30 parcels are whole numbers of days. Seven arrived within one day, 17 within two, 25 within three and all within four. What is the median delivery time?

- $4$ days
- $2$ days
- $1$ day
- $3$ days

## Explanation

The middle entries in order are the fifteenth and sixteenth. Both fall between positions eight and seventeen, occupied by two-day deliveries.
