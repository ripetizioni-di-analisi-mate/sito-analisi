In a batch of $47$ devices, at least $70\%$ must pass an inspection. What is the greatest number that can fail while the threshold is still met?

- $33$
- $15$
- $14$
- $13$

## Explanation

At least $33$ must pass because $0.7\cdot 47=32.9$. At most $47-33=14$ devices can therefore fail.
