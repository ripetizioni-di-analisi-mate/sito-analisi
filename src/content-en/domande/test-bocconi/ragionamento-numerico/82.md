A warehouse must ship exactly 42 spares in sealed packs of 6 or 9. Only three packs of 9 are available, with ample packs of 6. What is the fewest packs that can fulfil the order?

- $6$
- $4$
- $5$
- $7$

## Explanation

With at most five packs, the largest load available is $3\cdot9+2\cdot6=39$, which is too small. With six packs, two packs of $9$ and four of $6$ give $18+24=42$. Thus six is the minimum.
