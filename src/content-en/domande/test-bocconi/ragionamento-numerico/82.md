A town has $36{,}000$ residents and in one year spends $540{,}000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ on waste collection. Each resident belongs to exactly one household, and households have $2.4$ members on average. What is the annual spending per household?

- $15\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $36\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $6.25\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $86.40\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The required spending has households as its base, so their number is needed. Since each resident belongs to exactly one household and households have $2.4$ members on average, there are $\dfrac{36{,}000}{2.4}=15{,}000$ households. The spending per household is therefore $\dfrac{540{,}000}{15{,}000}=36\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The figure $15\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is instead the spending per resident, since $\dfrac{540{,}000}{36{,}000}=15$.
