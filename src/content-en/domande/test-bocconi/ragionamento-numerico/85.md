The recorded values are 2, 3, 4, 5 and 16. The 16 was an error and is corrected to 6. By how much do the mean and median decrease?

- Mean by 10, median by 0.
- Mean by 2, median by 0.
- Mean by 0, median by 2.
- Mean by 2, median by 2.

## Explanation

The sum falls by 10 across five values, so the mean decreases by 2. The middle value is 4 both before and after the correction.
