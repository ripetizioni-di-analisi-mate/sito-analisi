Two offices record the cases completed on time, split by type.

| Office | Simple on time | Simple in all | Complex on time | Complex in all |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | 45 | 50 | 45 | 150 |
| B | 170 | 200 | 5 | 20 |

Which statement is true?

- B has a higher on-time rate for both simple and complex cases.
- A has the higher overall on-time rate.
- A has a higher rate for both types, while B has the higher overall rate.
- A's overall rate is the simple mean of its two rates.

## Explanation

For simple cases A has $\dfrac{45}{50}=90\%$ and B has $\dfrac{170}{200}=85\%$; for complex cases A has $\dfrac{45}{150}=30\%$ and B has $\dfrac{5}{20}=25\%$. A therefore has the higher rate for both types. Overall A completes $\dfrac{90}{200}=45\%$ of its cases on time and B $\dfrac{175}{220}\approx 79.5\%$. The overall comparison is reversed because three quarters of A's cases are complex, while B handles almost only simple cases. The simple mean of A's rates, $60\%$, differs from $45\%$ because the two types have different sizes.
