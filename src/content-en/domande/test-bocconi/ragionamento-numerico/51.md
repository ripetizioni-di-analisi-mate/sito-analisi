An expense sheet lists “Paper 26”, “Ink 14”, “Materials 40”, and “Services 18”, in thousands of euros. Materials is the subtotal for paper and ink. A reviewer mistakenly adds all four rows. By how many thousand euros does this exceed the correct expense?

- $58$
- $40$
- $18$
- $98$

## Explanation

The correct expense is $40+18=58$. Also adding paper and ink counts $26+14=40$ thousand euros a second time.
