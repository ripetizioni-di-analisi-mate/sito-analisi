An annual index is 100 in 2020 and 125 in 2022. A second series sets 2022 equal to 100 and gives 108 for 2024. What is the 2024 index on the first series’ 2020 base? Both series measure the same quantity.

- $108$
- $117$
- $135$
- $133$

## Explanation

From 2022 to 2024 the factor is $108\div100=1.08$. Applied to 125 on the old base, it gives $125\cdot1.08=135$.
