The chart shows the enrolments in an evening course in months $1$ to $4$. Between which two consecutive months is the percentage increase in enrolments largest?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=76 poligoni="0.7,0 1.3,0 1.3,40 0.7,40;1.7,0 2.3,0 2.3,50 1.7,50;2.7,0 3.3,0 3.3,60 2.7,60;3.7,0 4.3,0 4.3,66 3.7,66" etichette="1,44,40;2,54,50;3,64,60;4,70,66" titolo="Course enrolments in months 1 to 4: 40, 50, 60 and 66."}

- Between month $2$ and month $3$.
- Between month $3$ and month $4$.
- The increase is the same from month $1$ to $2$ and from month $2$ to $3$.
- Between month $1$ and month $2$.

## Explanation

The absolute increases are $50-40=10$, $60-50=10$ and $66-60=6$ enrolments. Each percentage change uses the starting month as its base, giving $\dfrac{10}{40}=25\%$, $\dfrac{10}{50}=20\%$ and $\dfrac{6}{60}=10\%$. The same increase of $10$ enrolments gives a higher percentage on the smaller base. Hence the largest percentage increase is between month $1$ and month $2$.
