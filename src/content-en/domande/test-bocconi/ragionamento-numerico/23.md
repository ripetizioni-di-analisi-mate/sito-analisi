A bar chart shows costs of $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $132\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The vertical axis starts at $108\,\mathrm{EUR}$. By what percentage does the higher cost exceed the lower one?

- $100\%$
- $20\%$
- $10\%$
- $12\%$

## Explanation

The costs are measured from zero. The difference is $132-120=12\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the change is $\dfrac{12}{120}=10\%$. The visible bar length doubles because of the axis limit.
