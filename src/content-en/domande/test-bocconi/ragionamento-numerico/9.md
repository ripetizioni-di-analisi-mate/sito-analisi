A machine prints $42$ labels in $18$ minutes and runs at a constant rate. How many labels does it print in one hour?

- $126$
- $180$
- $140$
- $120$

## Explanation

It prints $42\div 3=14$ labels in $6$ minutes. An hour has ten six-minute intervals, so it prints $140$ labels.
