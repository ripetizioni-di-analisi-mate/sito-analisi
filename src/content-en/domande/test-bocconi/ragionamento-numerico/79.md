The average price of a bus ticket was $2.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in the base year. Today the index of that price, with base $100$ in the base year, is $84$. What does the ticket cost on average today?

- $2.98\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2.10\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2.34\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1.66\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

An index of $84$ means that the current price is $84\%$ of the base-year price. The price is therefore $2.50\cdot 0.84=2.10\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The fall of $16$ index points thus corresponds to $0.16\cdot 2.50=0.40\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
