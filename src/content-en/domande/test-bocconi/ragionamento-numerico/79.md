Preparing a sample takes 8 minutes. As soon as that ends, a 12-minute scan and a 5-minute weighing operation can start together on separate instruments. The final seal takes 4 minutes and may be applied only once both checks are finished. What is the shortest possible total time in minutes?

- $24$
- $17$
- $21$
- $29$

## Explanation

After the first $8$ minutes, the checks run in parallel. The scan finishes last and requires $12$ minutes of elapsed time, during which the weighing operation also finishes. The minimum time is $8+12+4=24$ minutes.
