In one year the registered users of an app go from $40{,}000$ to $100{,}000$. By what percentage has the number of users increased?

- $250\%$
- $60\%$
- $150\%$
- $40\%$

## Explanation

The absolute increase is $100{,}000-40{,}000=60{,}000$ users. The percentage change divides the increase by the starting value, so it equals $\dfrac{60{,}000}{40{,}000}=1.5$, that is $150\%$. The figure $250\%$ is instead the ratio of the final number to the initial one, since $\dfrac{100{,}000}{40{,}000}=2.5$.
