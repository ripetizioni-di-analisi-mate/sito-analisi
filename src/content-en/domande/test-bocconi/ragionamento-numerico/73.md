A travel survey says that 75% of participants take at most 40 minutes and a further 15% take over 40 but at most 50 minutes. Of 200 participants, how many take *more than 50 minutes*?

- $50$
- $20$
- $180$
- $30$

## Explanation

The two classes are disjoint and cover $75\%+15\%=90\%$ of the participants. The remaining 10% of 200 is 20 people.
