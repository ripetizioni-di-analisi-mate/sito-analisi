In the base year a household spends $1{,}000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ a month: $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ on food and $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ on transport. Relative to the base year, the food price index is $105$ and the transport price index is $165$. If the household buys the same quantities, what is the index of its total monthly spending?

- $117$
- $135$
- $153$
- $170$

## Explanation

With the same quantities, spending on food becomes $800\cdot 1.05=840\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and spending on transport $200\cdot 1.65=330\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Total spending is therefore $840+330=1{,}170\,\mathrm{EUR}$, and compared with the $1{,}000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ of the base year the index is $117$. The result is the mean of the two indices weighted by the spending shares $0.8$ and $0.2$, so it is closer to the food index.
