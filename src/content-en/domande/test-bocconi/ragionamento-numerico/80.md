A report compares service counters using a score of $100-2t$, where $t$ is waiting time in minutes. A’s average score exceeds B’s by 6 points. How do their average waiting times compare?

- A’s average wait is 12 minutes shorter.
- A’s average wait is 3 minutes longer.
- A’s average wait is 3 minutes shorter.
- A’s average wait is 6 minutes shorter.

## Explanation

The constant 100 cancels in the difference. Each minute less adds two points, so six more points mean three minutes less.
