The Aurora shop issued $3{,}600$ receipts in the first quarter of the year. The Borgo shop issued $1{,}300$ receipts in March alone. Which statement is guaranteed by these data?

- Aurora issued more receipts than Borgo in March.
- Borgo issued more receipts than Aurora in the first quarter.
- Aurora's monthly average over the quarter is lower than Borgo's receipts in March.
- Aurora issued $1{,}200$ receipts in March.

## Explanation

The first quarter contains three months, so Aurora's monthly average is $\dfrac{3{,}600}{3}=1{,}200$ receipts. Since $1{,}200<1{,}300$, this average is lower than Borgo's March count. The data do not say how Aurora's $3{,}600$ receipts are spread over the three months, so its March count is unknown. For the same reason Borgo's receipts in January and February are unknown, and the comparison over the whole quarter is not determined.
