A receipt totals 46 euros, including a 6-euro deposit for three identical crates. The customer returns two empty crates and receives their deposits back. What is the total net payment for the goods and the crate kept?

- $44\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $46\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $42\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $40\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

One crate carries a deposit of $6\div3=2$ euros. The two returns yield a refund of 4 euros, making the net payment $46-4=42$ euros.
