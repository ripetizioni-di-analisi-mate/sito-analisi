In a library's register, Monday has $42$ loans, Tuesday shows zero and Wednesday has a dash meaning “missing data”. Which result is guaranteed?

- The three-day total is at least 42.
- Wednesday has at least 42 loans.
- The three-day total is exactly 42.
- The three-day mean is 14.

## Explanation

The two observed days sum to $42$. Wednesday can have any non-negative whole-number count, so $42$ is a lower bound for the total.
