A cooperative mixes $12$ litres of concentrate costing $14\,\mathrm{EUR}$ per litre with $8$ litres costing $9\,\mathrm{EUR}$ per litre. Two litres are lost during transfer, with no refund. What is the cost per litre remaining?

- $\dfrac{120}{11}\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $12\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $\dfrac{40}{3}\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $\dfrac{23}{2}\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The original cost is $12\cdot 14+8\cdot 9=240\,\mathrm{EUR}$. There are $20-2=18$ litres left, so the cost per remaining litre is $\dfrac{240}{18}=\dfrac{40}{3}\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
