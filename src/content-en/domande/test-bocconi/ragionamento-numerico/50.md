An exhibition is open for $24$ days in October and $30$ days in November. It has $4{,}320$ visitors in October and $5{,}100$ in November. How does the mean number of visitors per opening day change from October to November?

- It decreases by $10$ visitors.
- It increases by $26$ visitors.
- It increases by $32.5$ visitors.
- It increases by $780$ visitors.

## Explanation

The two months have a different number of opening days, so the comparison requires the mean of each month. In October the mean is $\dfrac{4{,}320}{24}=180$ visitors per opening day. In November the mean is $\dfrac{5{,}100}{30}=170$ visitors per opening day. Hence the mean decreases by $180-170=10$ visitors. The monthly total, on the other hand, grows by $5{,}100-4{,}320=780$ visitors, because November has $6$ more opening days.
