A complaints table counts a report once per reason given. The only reasons are delay and damage. It records 26 mentions of delay and 14 of damage across 32 distinct reports, each with at least one reason. How many extra rows would an export create by using one row per reason instead of one per report?

- $12$
- $40$
- $8$
- $18$

## Explanation

One row per reason gives $26+14=40$ rows. There were 32 reports, so eight extra rows represent the reports with both reasons.
