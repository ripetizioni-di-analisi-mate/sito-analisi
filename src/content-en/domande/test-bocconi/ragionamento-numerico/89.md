A monthly index rounds each reading to the nearest integer, with halves rounded up. Its published value is 50 in both March and April. Which change in the exact reading from March to April is impossible?

- An increase of three quarters of a point.
- A decrease of half a point.
- An increase of exactly 1 point.
- No change.

## Explanation

Both readings lie from $49.5$ inclusive to $50.5$ exclusive. The distance between any two values in that interval is strictly less than one point.
