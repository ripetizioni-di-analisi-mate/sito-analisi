A company offers the employees of four departments membership of a pension fund. In department A $47$ of $95$ employees join, in B $52$ of $103$, in C $61$ of $124$ and in D $38$ of $77$. In which department did more than half of the employees join?

- A
- C
- B
- D

## Explanation

A fraction $\dfrac{a}{b}$ with $b>0$ exceeds $\dfrac{1}{2}$ if and only if $2a>b$. For A we have $2\cdot 47=94<95$, for B $2\cdot 52=104>103$, for C $2\cdot 61=122<124$ and for D $2\cdot 38=76<77$. So only in department B do the employees who joined exceed half of the department's employees.
