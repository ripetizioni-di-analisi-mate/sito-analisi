An exchange office sells dollars for 0.95 euros each and buys them back for 0.90 euros each. With no other fees, a customer buys dollars with 190 euros and immediately sells them all back. How many euros does the customer lose?

- $5$
- $19$
- $0$
- $10$

## Explanation

The customer buys $190\div0.95=200$ dollars. Selling them back yields $200\cdot0.90=180$ euros, a loss of 10 euros.
