A company's turnover goes from $200$ to $230$ thousand euros. Over the same period the turnover of product line X goes from $50$ to $68$ thousand euros. What percentage of the overall increase in turnover is due to line X?

- $36\%$
- $15\%$
- $9\%$
- $60\%$

## Explanation

The overall increase is $230-200=30$ thousand euros and that of line X is $68-50=18$ thousand euros. The question compares these two increases, so the required share is $\dfrac{18}{30}=60\%$. The figure $36\%$ is instead the growth of line X relative to its own initial turnover, since $\dfrac{18}{50}=36\%$.
