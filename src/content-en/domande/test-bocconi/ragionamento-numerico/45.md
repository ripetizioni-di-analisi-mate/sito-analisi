A business receives a grant of $40\%$ of eligible expenditure, capped at $170\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Its eligible expenditure is between $400$ and $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What are the lowest and highest possible grants?

- $160\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $170\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $170\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in both cases
- $160\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $170\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Taking $40\%$ of $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ gives $160\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $40\%$ of $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ gives $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The cap reduces the latter amount to $170\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
