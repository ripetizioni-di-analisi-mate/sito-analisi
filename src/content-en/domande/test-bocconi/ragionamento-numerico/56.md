Parts must pass two checks in succession. The first passes 72 of the initial 80 parts. The second rejects one quarter of the parts it receives. How many parts are rejected altogether?

- $18$
- $20$
- $28$
- $26$

## Explanation

The first check rejects $80-72=8$ parts. The second rejects a quarter of 72, or 18. The total is $8+18=26$.
