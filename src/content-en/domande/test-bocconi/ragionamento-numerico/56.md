The staff summary of a plant lists the workers by department. Each worker belongs to exactly one department and one shift.

| Item | Workers |
| --- | ---: |
| Production, shift A | 18 |
| Production, shift B | 22 |
| Production total | 40 |
| Logistics | 15 |
| Administration | 9 |

How many workers does the plant have?

- $104$
- $49$
- $64$
- $55$

## Explanation

The “Production total” row combines the two shifts, since $18+22=40$. Adding all the rows would therefore count the production workers twice. The total is obtained from the production subtotal and the other two departments, and equals $40+15+9=64$.
