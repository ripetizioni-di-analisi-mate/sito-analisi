A retailer has replied to $24$ complaints. Each concerns a different order. There were between $300$ and $480$ orders in the same period. Which interval necessarily contains the percentage of orders with a complaint that received a reply?

- From $6\%$ to $8\%$, inclusive.
- From $5\%$ to $8\%$, inclusive.
- From $8\%$ to $10\%$, inclusive.
- From $4\%$ to $7\%$, inclusive.

## Explanation

The largest denominator gives $\dfrac{24}{480}=5\%$ and the smallest gives $\dfrac{24}{300}=8\%$. All intermediate denominator values give rates between these limits.
