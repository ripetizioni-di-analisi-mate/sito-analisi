Two company fleets record their accidents over one year.

| Fleet | Vehicles | Kilometres driven (millions) | Accidents |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | 30 | 2 | 6 |
| B | 25 | 1 | 4 |

Which statement is true?

- B has more accidents both per vehicle and per million kilometres.
- A has more accidents both per vehicle and per million kilometres.
- A has more accidents per million kilometres, B has more per vehicle.
- A has more accidents per vehicle, B has more per million kilometres.

## Explanation

Per vehicle, A records $\dfrac{6}{30}=0.2$ accidents and B records $\dfrac{4}{25}=0.16$, so A's value is higher. Per million kilometres, A records $\dfrac{6}{2}=3$ accidents and B records $\dfrac{4}{1}=4$, so B's value is higher. The ranking changes with the denominator because each vehicle of A drives on average more kilometres than each vehicle of B.
