A device checks a series of readings. It replaces an interior reading by the mean of its two neighbours only if the neighbours differ by at most 1 and both differ from the central reading by at least 5. The original readings are 10, 11, 25, 12, 13. What corrected value replaces 25?

- $12$
- $13$
- $14.2$
- $11.5$

## Explanation

The neighbouring readings are $11$ and $12$, separated by $1$. Their differences from $25$ are $14$ and $13$, both at least $5$. The replacement therefore applies and gives $\dfrac{11+12}{2}=11.5$.
