A student has passed three exams: one worth $12$ credits with a mark of $30$, one worth $3$ credits with a mark of $18$ and one worth $3$ credits with a mark of $24$. What is the credit-weighted mean of the marks?

- $24$
- $30$
- $21$
- $27$

## Explanation

Each mark is multiplied by the credits of its exam, so the weighted sum is $12\cdot 30+3\cdot 18+3\cdot 24=360+54+72=486$. The credits total $12+3+3=18$. The weighted mean is therefore $\dfrac{486}{18}=27$. The simple mean of the three marks, $24$, would give the $12$-credit exam the same weight as the $3$-credit exams.
