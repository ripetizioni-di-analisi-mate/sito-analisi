A final mark gives the written exam twice the weight of the oral exam. Applicants need both a weighted average of at least 70 and an oral mark of at least 60. With a written mark of 77, what is the minimum whole-number oral mark that suffices?

- $63$
- $60$
- $56$
- $70$

## Explanation

The average requires $2\cdot77+x\ge210$, or $x\ge56$. The separate oral minimum is 60, so 60 is the least mark satisfying both requirements.
