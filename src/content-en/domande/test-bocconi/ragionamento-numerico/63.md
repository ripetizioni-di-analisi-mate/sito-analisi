The chart shows the number of members of two gyms, A and B, at the start and at the end of a $4$-month period. The two segments are parallel. Which statement is true?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=150 linee="0,100 4,130;0,40 4,70" punti="0,100,pieno;4,130,pieno;0,40,pieno;4,70,pieno" etichette="4.4,130,A;4.4,70,B" titolo="Members: A goes from 100 to 130, B from 40 to 70."}

- B grows by $75\%$, A by $30\%$.
- A and B grow by the same percentage, because the segments are parallel.
- A grows by a larger percentage than B, because it starts from a higher value.
- B grows by $70$ members.

## Explanation

Parallel segments have the same slope, so the two gyms gain the same number of members, $30$ in $4$ months. The percentage change divides the increase by the starting value. For A this gives $\dfrac{30}{100}=30\%$ and for B $\dfrac{30}{40}=75\%$. Two equal absolute increases therefore give different percentages when the bases are different.
