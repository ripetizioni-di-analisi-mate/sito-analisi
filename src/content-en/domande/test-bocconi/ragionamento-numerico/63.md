A table groups customers by purchases made during the month: 10 customers made one purchase, 6 made two and 4 made three. One recorded purchase is chosen uniformly at random. What is the probability that its customer made three purchases?

- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac{6}{17}$
- $\dfrac3{17}$

## Explanation

The four customers in the third group account for $4\cdot3=12$ purchases. There are $10+12+12=34$ purchases in all, giving $\dfrac{12}{34}=\dfrac6{17}$.
