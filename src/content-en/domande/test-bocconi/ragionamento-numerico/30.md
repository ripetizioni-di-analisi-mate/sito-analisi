Two outlets compare quantities delivered in two consecutive weeks.

| Outlet | First week | Second week |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| North | 60 | 75 |
| South | 100 | 120 |

By how many percentage points does the North outlet's growth rate exceed the South outlet's?

- 20 percentage points
- 5 percentage points
- 15 percentage points
- 25 percentage points

## Explanation

The North grows by $\dfrac{15}{60}=25\%$. The South grows by $\dfrac{20}{100}=20\%$. The difference between the rates is $5$ percentage points.
