A museum records exactly one booking type per reservation.

| Day | Group | Individual | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Friday | 28 | 42 | 70 |
| Saturday | 33 | 37 | 70 |
| Sunday | 19 | 41 | 60 |

Of the Saturday or Sunday bookings, how many are individual?

- $120$
- $52$
- $78$
- $130$

## Explanation

Combine the individual cells for Saturday and Sunday because they are different days. The total is $37+41=78$.
