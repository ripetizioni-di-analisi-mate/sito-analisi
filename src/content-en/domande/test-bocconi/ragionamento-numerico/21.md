A probe records a remaining charge of $84$ units at hour two, $70$ at hour three and $40$ at hour six. The model joins consecutive readings by straight segments. At what time does the model place a reading of $55$ units?

- $3.5$
- $4.5$
- $4$
- $5.5$

## Explanation

The level $55$ lies between $70$ and $40$, so the relevant segment runs from hour three to hour six. The fall from $70$ to $55$ is half the full drop of $30$ on that segment. Half of three hours after hour three gives hour $4.5$.
