A battery life indicator was recorded at four times.

| Time (hours) | Remaining battery life (units) |
| --- | ---: |
| 0 | 100 |
| 2 | 84 |
| 3 | 70 |
| 6 | 40 |

In which interval between successive readings was the mean hourly decrease greatest?

- From hour three to hour six.
- From hour two to hour three.
- From the start to hour two.
- The mean decrease is equal across the three intervals.

## Explanation

The mean decreases are $\dfrac{16}{2}=8$, $\dfrac{14}{1}=14$ and $\dfrac{30}{3}=10$ units per hour. The largest is $14$ between hours two and three.
