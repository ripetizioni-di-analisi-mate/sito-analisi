A print shop needs 8 minutes to set up each run and one minute per 12 sheets printed. It prints 144 blue sheets and then 96 white sheets in two separate runs. How many minutes does the job take?

- $20$
- $28$
- $40$
- $36$

## Explanation

Printing the 240 sheets takes $240\div12=20$ minutes. The two setups take another $2\cdot8=16$ minutes.
