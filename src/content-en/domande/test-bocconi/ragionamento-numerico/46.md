An agricultural cooperative records one day's deliveries: $3.2$ tonnes of wheat and $28$ quintals of barley. By how many kilograms does the wheat delivery exceed the barley delivery?

- $400\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2{,}920\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3{,}172\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

One tonne equals $1{,}000\,\mathrm{kg}$, so the wheat delivered weighs $3.2\cdot 1{,}000=3{,}200\,\mathrm{kg}$. One quintal equals $100\,\mathrm{kg}$, so the barley weighs $28\cdot 100=2{,}800\,\mathrm{kg}$. The two masses are now in the same unit, and their difference is $3{,}200-2{,}800=400\,\mathrm{kg}$.
