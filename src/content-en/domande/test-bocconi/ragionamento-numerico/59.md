A laboratory allocates 126 thousand euros this year in nominal terms. Each unit of an unchanged basket of goods now costs 5% more than last year. How many thousand euros at last year’s prices does the new allocation represent?

- $121$
- $132.3$
- $119.7$
- $120$

## Explanation

One euro at the old prices corresponds to $1.05$ euros at current prices. The allocation at constant prices is $126\div1.05=120$ thousand euros.
