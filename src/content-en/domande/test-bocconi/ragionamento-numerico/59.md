A probe measures the temperature of the water in a pan $0$, $4$ and $10$ minutes after the hob is switched on. The chart joins the three measurements with segments; the horizontal axis shows minutes and the vertical axis degrees Celsius. What temperature does the drawn segment give at the eighth minute?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=11 ymin=0 ymax=50 linee="0,10 4,30 10,42" punti="0,10,pieno;4,30,pieno;10,42,pieno" titolo="Temperature in degrees Celsius: 10 at 0 minutes, 30 at 4 minutes, 42 at 10 minutes."}

- $38\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$
- $50\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$
- $33.6\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$
- $42\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The eighth minute lies on the segment from $(4,\,30)$ to $(10,\,42)$. Along that segment the temperature rises by $42-30=12\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$ in $6$ minutes, that is by $2\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$ per minute. Four minutes pass between the fourth and the eighth minute, so the segment gives $30+4\cdot 2=38\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$. The value is an estimate, because measurements were taken only at $0$, $4$ and $10$ minutes.
