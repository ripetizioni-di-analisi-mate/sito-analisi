A car park contains 31 cars at 8 a.m. The table shows arrivals and departures before each successive hour.

| Interval | Arrivals | Departures |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 8–9 | 7 | 3 |
| 9–10 | 10 | 2 |
| 10–11 | 2 | 11 |
| 11–12 | 5 | 2 |

At which end-of-interval time are there the most cars?

- 12:00
- 10:00
- 11:00
- 9:00

## Explanation

Update occupancy by adding arrivals and subtracting departures. At 9, 10, 11 and 12 there are $35$, $43$, $34$ and $37$ cars. The highest count is at 10.
