Four companies report shipping expenditure and the number of parcels sent. Which row gives the highest *average* cost per parcel?

| Company | Cost (thousand euros) | Parcels (hundreds) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| A | 3.6 | 9 |
| B | 2.4 | 4 |
| C | 1.8 | 6 |
| D | 4.0 | 8 |

- D
- C
- A
- B

## Explanation

The mean costs in euros are $4$, $6$, $3$ and $5$, respectively. For example, B gives $2{,}400$ divided by $400$, so B has the highest average cost.
