A council has $61$ members. A resolution is valid if at least half of the members are present, and it is passed if at least three fifths of those present vote in favour. What is the smallest number of votes in favour with which a resolution can be passed?

- $37$
- $19$
- $31$
- $16$

## Explanation

Half of $61$ is $30.5$, so at least $31$ members must be present. The number of votes in favour required grows with the number present, so the minimum occurs with $31$ present. Three fifths of $31$ is $\dfrac{93}{5}=18.6$. Since votes are whole numbers, at least $19$ are needed.
