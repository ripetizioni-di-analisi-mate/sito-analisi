A motor takes in 80 units of energy and transfers 60 as useful output. A downstream device puts half the 20 units the motor would have wasted to useful work. What percentage of the original energy is useful in the overall system?

- $87.5\%$
- $100\%$
- $75\%$
- $50\%$

## Explanation

Recovery adds 10 units to the motor’s 60 useful units. The system makes use of 70 out of the original 80 units, or $87.5\%$.
