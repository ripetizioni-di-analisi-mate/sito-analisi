A theatre publishes its ticket sales as chain indices: each year the index compares the tickets with those of the previous year, set equal to $100$. Because of restoration work the 2024 index is $50$; the 2025 index is $180$. What is the 2025 ticket index with base 2023 equal to $100$?

- $90$
- $130$
- $360$
- $180$

## Explanation

A chain index of $50$ means that the 2024 tickets are $0.5$ times those of 2023. An index of $180$ means that the 2025 tickets are $1.8$ times those of 2024. Compared with 2023, the 2025 tickets are therefore $0.5\cdot 1.8=0.9$ times as many, so the index with base 2023 is $90$. Adding the changes of $-50$ and $+80$ points would give $130$, but the two changes are calculated on different bases.
