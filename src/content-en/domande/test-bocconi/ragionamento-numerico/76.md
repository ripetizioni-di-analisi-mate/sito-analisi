A trial account charges 1% of the overdrawn amount at the end of any day with a negative closing balance. Charges are tracked separately. The account starts with 32 thousand euros, pays out 50 thousand on day one, receives 27 thousand on day two and pays out 21 thousand on day three. How many euros of charges accrue over the three days?

- $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $180\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $390\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The closing balances are $-18$, $9$ and $-12$ thousand euros. The charge applies only to the overdrafts of $18\,000$ and $12\,000$ euros. One percent of the resulting $30\,000$ euros is $300$ euros.
