A regional funding call received $1{,}500$ applications in 2024 and accepted $20\%$ of them. In 2025 there were $1{,}000$ applications and the share accepted rose to $26\%$. Which statement is true?

- The number of accepted applications rose by $30\%$.
- The share accepted rose by $6$ percentage points and the number of accepted applications fell by $40$.
- The share accepted rose by $6\%$.
- The number of accepted applications did not change.

## Explanation

Accepted applications number $0.2\cdot 1{,}500=300$ in 2024 and $0.26\cdot 1{,}000=260$ in 2025, so they fall by $40$. The share goes from $20\%$ to $26\%$, so it rises by $26-20=6$ percentage points. In relative terms the share grows by $\dfrac{6}{20}=30\%$. The number of accepted applications falls all the same, because the total number of applications dropped by one third.
