Two registers show the same payment. Register X shows Monday at 23:50; Y shows Tuesday at 01:50 because its time zone is two hours ahead of X. Other Monday payments in X total 240 euros. The payment in question was 35 euros. What is Monday’s total in X’s time zone, counting each payment once?

- $275\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $240\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $310\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $205\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

In X’s time zone the 35-euro payment belongs to Monday. The Y entry is the same payment, so the total is $240+35=275$ euros.
