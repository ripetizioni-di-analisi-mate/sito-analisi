A four-digit counter returns to 0000 immediately after 9999. It reads 9984 at opening and 0037 at closing. It rolled over exactly once and advances by one per admission. How many admissions were there?

- $53$
- $54$
- $21$
- $52$

## Explanation

Going from 9984 to 0000 takes $10000-9984=16$ admissions, including the one that resets the display. Another 37 give a total of $53$.
