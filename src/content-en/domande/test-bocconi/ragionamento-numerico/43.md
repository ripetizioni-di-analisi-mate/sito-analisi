Group A has $7$ failed inspections out of $20$. Group B has $15$ out of $42$. How much must be added to Group A's rate to obtain Group B's rate, expressing the difference as a fraction of 1?

- $\dfrac{1}{140}$
- $\dfrac{1}{70}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{140}$
- $\dfrac{1}{14}$

## Explanation

Simplifying $\dfrac{15}{42}$ gives $\dfrac{5}{14}$. Over a common denominator of $140$, the difference is $\dfrac{50-49}{140}=\dfrac{1}{140}$.
