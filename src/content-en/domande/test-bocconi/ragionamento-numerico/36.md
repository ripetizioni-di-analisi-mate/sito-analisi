In the morning, $35$ of $50$ jobs were completed on time. In the afternoon, $27$ of $30$ jobs were completed on time. What is the overall proportion of jobs completed *late*?

- $30\%$
- $20\%$
- $77.5\%$
- $22.5\%$

## Explanation

The numbers of late jobs are $50-35=15$ and $30-27=3$ in the two shifts. The total is $18$ out of $80$, giving $22.5\%$.
