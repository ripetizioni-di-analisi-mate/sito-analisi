A sheet for 100 packs of finished product gives 125 kilograms of dough before baking and 100 kilograms afterwards. Sixty identical packs are needed. How many kilograms evaporate during baking if the yield is unchanged?

- $75$
- $12$
- $15$
- $25$

## Explanation

For 100 packs the loss is $125-100=25$ kilograms. For 60 packs it is $\dfrac{60}{100}\cdot25=15$ kilograms.
