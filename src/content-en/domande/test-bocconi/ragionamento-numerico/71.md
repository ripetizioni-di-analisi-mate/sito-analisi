A shop applies a $30\%$ discount to a coat and, at the till, a further $20\%$ discount on the already discounted price. What single discount on the list price is equivalent to the two discounts?

- $50\%$
- $56\%$
- $44\%$
- $24\%$

## Explanation

The first discount leaves $70\%$ of the list price to pay, so it multiplies the price by $0.7$. The second discount applies to the reduced price and multiplies it by $0.8$. The final price is therefore $0.7\cdot 0.8=0.56$ times the list price. The equivalent single discount is thus $1-0.56=0.44$, that is $44\%$.
