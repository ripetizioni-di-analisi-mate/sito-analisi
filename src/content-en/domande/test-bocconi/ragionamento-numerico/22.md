Two rivers are measured at 8 a.m. and noon. River A moves from $40$ to $60$ flow units and river B from $70$ to $50$. Straight segments connect the readings on a chart. Which statement is certain for the *drawn segments*?

- A lies above B at 8 a.m.
- The two segments have the same signed slope.
- The segments cross between 8 a.m. and noon.
- B lies above A at noon.

## Explanation

At 8 a.m. we have $40<70$, while at noon $60>50$. The vertical difference changes sign and varies linearly, so it is zero once within the interval.
