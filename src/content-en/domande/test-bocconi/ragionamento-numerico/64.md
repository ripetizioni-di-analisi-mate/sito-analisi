Two couriers make 18 deliveries each. A map uses one symbol per three deliveries by the first courier and one per two by the second. How many more symbols are drawn for the second courier?

- $3$
- $9$
- $0$
- $6$

## Explanation

The couriers make the same number of deliveries, but the keys differ. The map uses $18\div3=6$ symbols for the first and $18\div2=9$ for the second, a difference of three.
