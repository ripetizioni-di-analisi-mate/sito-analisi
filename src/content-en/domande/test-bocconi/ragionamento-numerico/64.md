The chart shows the customers of a service desk grouped by waiting time, in $10$-minute classes: $0$ to $10$, $10$ to $20$, $20$ to $30$ and $30$ to $40$. Each customer belongs to exactly one class, and a customer who waits exactly $20$ minutes is counted in the class from $20$ to $30$. What fraction of the customers waited at least $20$ minutes?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=45 ymin=0 ymax=22 poligoni="0,0 10,0 10,12 0,12;10,0 20,0 20,18 10,18;20,0 30,0 30,9 20,9;30,0 40,0 40,6 30,6" etichette="5,13.5,12;15,19.5,18;25,10.5,9;35,7.5,6" titolo="Customers per waiting-time class in minutes: 12, 18, 9 and 6."}

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{15}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

There are $12+18+9+6=45$ customers in all. The customers who waited at least $20$ minutes are those in the last two classes, that is $9+6=15$. The required fraction is therefore $\dfrac{15}{45}=\dfrac{1}{3}$.
