Relative to the same base year, a business has a revenue index of $112.5$ and an index of $125$ for items sold. What is its revenue-per-item index on that base?

- $100$
- $110$
- $112$
- $90$

## Explanation

Mean revenue is total revenue divided by items sold. Its change factor is therefore $\dfrac{112.5}{125}=0.9$. The index is $90$.
