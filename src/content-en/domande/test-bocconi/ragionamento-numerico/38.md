A lab buys $198$ vials at $19.80\,\mathrm{EUR}$ each. What is the total cost rounded to the nearest thousand euros?

- $10\,000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3\,000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $4\,000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $5\,000\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The total is below $200\cdot 20=4{,}000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and above $190\cdot 19=3{,}610\,\mathrm{EUR}$. It is therefore in the interval that rounds to $4{,}000\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
