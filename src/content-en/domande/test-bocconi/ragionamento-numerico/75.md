Only two lists are contesting a runoff. So far 48 votes have been counted for A and 42 for B; 10 valid votes remain. What is the fewest of those 10 that A must receive to win with *more* votes than B, regardless of the others?

- $2$
- $1$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

If A gets two and B gets eight, both finish on 50. If A gets three, the totals become 51 and 49, making three the minimum.
