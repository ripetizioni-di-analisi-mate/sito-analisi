A restaurant receipt shows a total of $73.20\,\mathrm{EUR}$, VAT included. VAT is $22\%$ of the amount before VAT. What is the amount before VAT?

- $16.10\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $51.20\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $89.30\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

If $P$ is the amount before VAT, the total is $P+0.22P=1.22P$. The condition $1.22P=73.20$ gives $P=\dfrac{73.20}{1.22}=60\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The VAT contained in the receipt is therefore $73.20-60=13.20\,\mathrm{EUR}$, which equals $0.22\cdot 60$. The figure $22\%$ of the total, $16.10\,\mathrm{EUR}$, is a different quantity, because its base is the amount including VAT.
