A line produces 9 boxes in its first three hours. A chart shows only that total, with no intermediate readings or assumption about the rate. Hourly counts are whole numbers. Which statement is guaranteed?

- At least one hour produced more than 3 boxes.
- At least one hour produced at least 3 boxes.
- Every hour produced exactly 3 boxes.
- The last hour produced at least 3 boxes.

## Explanation

If every hour produced at most two boxes, the total could be at most six. Nine boxes require at least one hour with three or more, and $3,3,3$ shows that exceeding three is optional.
