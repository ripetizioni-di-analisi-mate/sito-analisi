For a school trip a school hires $52$-seat coaches. Each coach must carry $2$ accompanying teachers, who take two of those seats. There are $151$ students taking part. What is the smallest number of coaches needed?

- $3$
- $2$
- $5$
- $4$

## Explanation

Each coach has $52-2=50$ seats left for students. Three coaches therefore carry $150$ students, which is not enough for $151$. Hence $4$ coaches are needed. The quotient $\dfrac{151}{52}\approx 2.9$ would lead to $3$ coaches, because it ignores the seats taken by the teachers.
