> The service uses only vans fitted with an enclosed equipment cabinet. In the larger vans the cupboard is separate from the refrigerated compartment. The new van is in service and its specification lists an enclosed cabinet.

Which conclusion about the new van's size is supported?

- The refrigerated compartment proves that the new van is the largest.
- Only the smaller vans have an enclosed cabinet.
- The cabinet identifies the van as one of the larger ones.
- The cabinet alone leaves the size undetermined.

## Explanation

An enclosed cabinet is required in every van in service. For larger vans the passage specifies a separate refrigerated compartment, a detail absent from the specification quoted.
