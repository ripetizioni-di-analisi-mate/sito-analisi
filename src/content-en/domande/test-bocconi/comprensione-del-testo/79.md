> Only two species of fish live in a mountain lake, the trout and the char. The char stays in deep water all year round, below twenty metres. In July an angler caught a fish at a depth of five metres.

What follows from the information in the passage?

- The fish caught is a char.
- The fish might belong to a third species.
- The fish caught is a trout.
- In July trout go down below twenty metres.

## Explanation

Only two species live in the lake, and the char stays below twenty metres all year round. A fish caught at five metres therefore cannot be a char. Since the only other species in the lake is the trout, the fish **is a trout**.
