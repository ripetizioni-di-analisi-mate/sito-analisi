> A guide’s notebook records when each group reaches the lookout. If a group turns back early, its line stays blank. Last week five lines were blank. A storm prevented the guide from recording two arrivals confirmed by photographs.

For how many of the five entries can an early turnaround already be ruled out?

- For at least two.
- For all five.
- For exactly three.
- For none.

## Explanation

The photographs confirm two arrivals despite the blanks. The other three blank lines lack equivalent confirmation.
