> The plan to repair a pier involves closing one half at a time. Shutting the whole pier would shorten the work. Keeping half of it open, however, allows one berth to remain in service throughout.

Which statement best expresses the reason for the choice?

- The proposal is intended to state the exact length of the repairs.
- The plan accepts a longer period of work to keep one berth available.
- The whole pier would remain available during repairs.
- The partial closure kept the estimated cost of the work down.

## Explanation

Closing just one half keeps a berth open. A total closure would take less time, so the plan gives priority to keeping the service available.
