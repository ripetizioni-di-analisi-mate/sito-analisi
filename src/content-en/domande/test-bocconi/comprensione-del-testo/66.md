> A bookshop says that since September the share of orders collected in store has risen from one half to two thirds. A reader concludes that more parcels were handed over at the counter. The report gives no total for either period.

Which totals would make the reader’s conclusion false while preserving both shares?

- 120 orders initially, then 60.
- 90 orders initially, then 120.
- 60 orders initially, then 120.
- 120 orders in both periods.

## Explanation

With 120 initial orders and 60 subsequent orders, collections fall from 60 to 40. In the other three cases the number collected rises.
