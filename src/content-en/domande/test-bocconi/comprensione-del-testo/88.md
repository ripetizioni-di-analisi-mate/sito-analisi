> The regional seed bank keeps local varieties of beans, corn and wheat. The seeds are dried, closed in sealed packets and stored at minus eighteen degrees. Every ten years part of each batch is sown to check that the seeds still germinate. Farmers can request small amounts of seed to grow in their own fields.

According to the passage, why is part of each batch sown every ten years?

- To supply seeds to the farmers who request them.
- To check that the seeds are still able to germinate.
- To obtain new local varieties.
- To free up space in the sealed packets.

## Explanation

The third sentence links the sowing every ten years to a specific purpose, namely checking that the seeds still germinate. Supplying seeds to farmers is described in the last sentence as a separate service. The stated purpose of the sowing is therefore **to check the ability to germinate**.
