> A record says, “After the move the workshop has two lathes.” An early draft said “two more lathes”, but there is no inventory of the previous equipment.

If the current total is two, what fact would justify the deleted wording?

- One of the current lathes is second-hand.
- The two current lathes are of the same make.
- Two lathes were already available before the move.
- The workshop had no lathes before the move.

## Explanation

“Two more” denotes the difference from the previous number. With two now, a difference of two requires a previous total of zero.
