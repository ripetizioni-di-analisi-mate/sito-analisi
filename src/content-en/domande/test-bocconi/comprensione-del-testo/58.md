> A school has 150 students in its lower years and 300 in its upper years. In the lower years 30% of the students sing in the school choir, while in the upper years the share is 20%. The choir director notes that the lower years are the more involved group.

According to the figures in the passage, which statement about the choir members is correct?

- The lower years provide more choir members than the upper years.
- The two groups provide the same number of choir members.
- The upper years provide more choir members than the lower years.
- The choir has 50 members in all.

## Explanation

In the lower years there are $150\cdot\dfrac{30}{100}=45$ choir members, in the upper years $300\cdot\dfrac{20}{100}=60$. The upper years have a lower share and twice the base, so they **provide more choir members**. The director's remark concerns the share of students involved and is consistent with these numbers. The total number of choir members is $45+60=105$, while 50 comes from adding the two percentages.
