> “I was on the ferry by the first bell,” says Mina. Her brother remembers seeing her on the pier when the village’s first bell rang. The ship’s log says the ferry bell called passengers ten minutes after the village bell.

Which reading makes the memories compatible?

- Mina means the ship’s bell and her brother means the village bell.
- Both mean the ship’s bell at the same instant.
- Both mean the village bell at the same instant.
- The ship’s bell rang before the village bell.

## Explanation

Her brother could have seen her before she boarded. Ten minutes later Mina could already be on the ferry when its own bell rang.
