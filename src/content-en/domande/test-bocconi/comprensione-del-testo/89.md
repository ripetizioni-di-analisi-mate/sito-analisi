> The volleyball team changed coach in December. In the second half of the season it won twelve matches out of fifteen, against six out of fifteen in the first half. The new coach introduced two weekly sessions of fitness training. In January, moreover, the first-choice setter returned after being out since October with an injury.

Which statement respects what the passage says about the improvement in results?

- The passage attributes the improvement to the setter's return.
- The passage states that the fitness training doubled the number of wins.
- The passage reports several changes in the team without attributing the improvement to any one of them.
- The passage indicates that the team lost more matches in the second half of the season than in the first.

## Explanation

The passage lists three changes, namely the new coach, the training sessions and the setter's return. No sentence links the improvement to any of these changes with a *because* or a *thanks to*, so the passage **reports the changes without naming their cause**. Wins go from six to twelve out of fifteen, so defeats drop from nine to three.
