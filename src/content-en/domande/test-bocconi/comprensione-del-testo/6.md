> The cooperative's ranking includes only applications accompanied by proof of residence. Yesterday the office received an application with all of the income documents and without proof of residence. The deadline for supplying missing documents has already passed.

What follows for the application received yesterday?

- The application is included because the income documents are complete.
- The ranking puts it first among late applications.
- The office can still wait for the missing document.
- The application is excluded from the ranking.

## Explanation

Proof of residence is required to appear in the ranking. Since it is missing and the deadline for adding documents has passed, the application is excluded.
