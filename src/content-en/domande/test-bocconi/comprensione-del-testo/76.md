> The technical sheet says, “The prototype can run on a single battery for up to six hours.” An advertisement says, “One battery gives you at least six hours of operation.”

What change adds a guarantee that the technical sheet does not provide?

- It changes the unit of time.
- It changes the number of batteries required.
- It states that the prototype is already on sale.
- It turns a possible maximum into a promised minimum.

## Explanation

“Up to six” sets an upper limit. “At least six” promises that operation will never fall short of that figure.
