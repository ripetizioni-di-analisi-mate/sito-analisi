> The foundation awards a scholarship only if two independent teachers recommend the candidate. The teachers assess the school record, the cover letter and the interview. Luca's application has been recommended by both teachers.

What can be said about Luca?

- Luca will certainly receive the scholarship.
- Luca meets the foundation's condition, but the passage does not guarantee him the scholarship.
- Luca cannot receive the scholarship.
- Luca's application will have to be assessed by a third teacher.

## Explanation

The phrase *only if* sets a necessary condition, so without both recommendations the scholarship is not awarded. The passage does not state that both recommendations are enough, so the foundation might require further conditions. Luca therefore meets the condition, but **the award of the scholarship is not guaranteed** by the text.
