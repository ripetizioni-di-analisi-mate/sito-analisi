> A survey of 1,200 workers in a textile district found that 40% also hold a second job. Of these, most give the need to supplement their income as the main reason. A smaller share mentions an interest in another profession. The survey collected no data on the hours of the second jobs.

Which statement goes beyond the survey's data?

- More than half of the workers surveyed have only one job.
- For most people with a second job, the main reason is financial.
- Some workers have a second job out of interest in another profession.
- People with a second job spend on average less than ten hours a week on it.

## Explanation

$40\%$ have a second job, so $60\%$ have only one, which is more than half. The second and third sentences support the statements about the prevailing financial reason and about interest in another profession. The last sentence, on the other hand, makes clear that the survey collected no data on hours, so an average number of hours per week **goes beyond the data**.
