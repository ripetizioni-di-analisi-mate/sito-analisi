> An article describes the houses in a village first in an estate agent’s words, then in a resident’s. The agent calls the rooms “cosy” and the resident calls them “cramped”. Both point to the same plan with the same dimensions.

Why retain both expressions?

- To show that one of the measurements is wrong.
- To indicate that walls were moved between the visits.
- To show two assessments of the same dimensions.
- To distinguish the ground floor from the upper floor.

## Explanation

The common plan fixes the dimensions. “Cosy” and “cramped” present them with different connotations.
