> On weekdays the street is reserved for buses until nine. Deliveries are allowed after nine, except on Wednesdays, when the market stalls occupy the street until eleven.

What does the phrase beginning “except” do?

- It introduces an exception to the rule on deliveries.
- It explains why buses run only on Wednesdays.
- It allows deliveries only on market days.
- It extends the market hours to the entire week.

## Explanation

The general rule allows deliveries after nine. Wednesday is treated separately because the street is occupied for longer.
