> A small dairy sells almost all of its output in its own region. Its main cheese is struggling to break into Northern European markets, where consumers know little about that kind of ageing. The dairy has therefore organised tastings in specialty shops in Hamburg and Copenhagen.

In this passage, what does “break into” mean when said of the cheese?

- Be shipped across national borders.
- Win customers in those markets.
- Pass health checks at the border.
- Lower its price to compete with local cheeses.

## Explanation

The difficulty is linked to the fact that consumers in Northern Europe know little about that kind of ageing. The tastings, presented as the dairy's response, serve to make the cheese known to those consumers. “Break into” therefore refers to **the product gaining customers** in those markets, while the passage says nothing about shipping, checks or prices.
