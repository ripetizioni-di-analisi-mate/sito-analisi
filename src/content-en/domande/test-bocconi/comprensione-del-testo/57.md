> The house sheet reports “ninety admissions”. Asked about the number of people, the usher explains that anyone stepping out during the interval received a fresh ticket on returning. Nine tickets were for returns. Every other ticket records one person’s first entry.

How many distinct people does the sheet document?

- Ninety.
- Eighty-one.
- Ninety-nine.
- Seventy-two.

## Explanation

The nine returns count people already included. Ninety minus nine first entries remain, one per person.
