> Visitors are given a map at the greenhouse entrance. The attendant gives an audio guide to each visitor with an evening booking. No audio guides are available for other visitors. In the afternoon, a gardener accompanies the groups instead.

Who is given an audio guide?

- Groups accompanied by the gardener.
- Unaccompanied afternoon visitors.
- Visitors who book an evening visit.
- All visitors who receive a map.

## Explanation

The second sentence reserves audio guides for visitors who book an evening visit. The map and the accompanying gardener are separate services.
