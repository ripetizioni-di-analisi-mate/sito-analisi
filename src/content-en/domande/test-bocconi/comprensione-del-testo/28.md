> The department collected observations on group study in two courses. In the first, shared notes helped students revise. In the second, the group used its meetings to coordinate an experimental project. The report considers the purposes of the activities separately.

Which summary captures both observations?

- The report measures a rise in grades due to the notes.
- The shared notes were used only for the experimental project.
- Group work served different purposes in the two courses, one involving revision and the other a project.
- The experimental project replaced group study in both courses.

## Explanation

The first course concerns revision aided by the notes. The second involves coordinating a project. The summary must preserve the distinction between the two purposes.
