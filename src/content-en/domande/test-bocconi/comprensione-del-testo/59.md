> “Between the end of rehearsals and the doors opening, we have a thirty-minute window to tune the piano.” The theatre has also replaced the glass in the hall, but the piano remains on stage.

Which word can replace “window” in the first sentence?

- Booking.
- Pane.
- Interval.
- Casement.

## Explanation

The two events bound the time available for tuning. The glass belongs to a separate piece of news.
