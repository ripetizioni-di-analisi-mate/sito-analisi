> The water stored in the reservoir was sufficient for regular irrigation. Because the main pump was undergoing maintenance, the more distant plots received their water a day after the nearby ones. The planting schedule stayed the same.

What does the passage give as the reason for the delay at the more distant plots?

- Maintenance of the main pump.
- A change in the planting schedule.
- Increased use of water by the nearby plots.
- A shortage of water in the reservoir.

## Explanation

The clause beginning with *because* gives the stated cause, which is maintenance. The first sentence specifies that there was enough water.
