> A small business compares two applications for recording hours. The one with the lower purchase price requires staff to enter each business trip manually. The other imports approved trips automatically. The final comparison therefore includes staff time as well.

What is the purpose of the passage?

- To explain why the comparison between the applications includes staff time.
- To recommend the application with the lower purchase price.
- To measure the average duration of a business trip.
- To specify which business trips can be approved.

## Explanation

The passage compares manual entry with automatic import and connects that difference to staff time. The final sentence explains the extra criterion used for the comparison.
