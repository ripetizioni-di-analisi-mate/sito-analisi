> The report on courtyard plants starts with the presence of high walls, which shorten the hours of sunlight. It then describes a building where pots are moved monthly to make use of the spots that remain sunny in the afternoon. It presents this case before the figures for the plants that flowered.

What role does the example of the building play?

- It explains why the walls of the building were made higher.
- It summarises the number of plants that had already flowered.
- It illustrates an adaptation to the light conditions described at the start.
- It indicates that all courtyards have the same number of hours of sunlight.

## Explanation

The beginning presents the limitation caused by the walls. Moving the pots in the example makes use of the remaining light, so it illustrates an adaptation.
