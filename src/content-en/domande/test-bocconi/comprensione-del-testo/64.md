> The church's façade is plain, with few cornices, no statues and a single rose window above the main door. Inside, by contrast, the side chapels are clad in gilded stucco and every wall is covered in frescoes. The contrast surprises many visitors entering for the first time.

What does “plain” mean when said of the façade?

- Built from cheap materials.
- Having little decoration.
- Small in size.
- Left unfinished.

## Explanation

Right after the adjective, the passage lists few cornices, no statues and a single rose window, that is, decorative elements kept to a minimum. The next sentence contrasts this façade with an interior full of stucco and frescoes. *Plain* therefore means **sparingly decorated**, while the passage says nothing about materials, size or unfinished work.
