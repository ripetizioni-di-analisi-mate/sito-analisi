> A team and a choir share a hall. The team minutes say, “The choir wrote to us: ‘We rehearse on Mondays; you can train on Tuesdays.’ We agreed.”

Who does “we” refer to inside the inner quotation?

- The members of the choir.
- Only the authors of the team minutes.
- The members of the team.
- Both groups together.

## Explanation

The inner quotation reports the choir’s words. The team speaks again in “We agreed”.
