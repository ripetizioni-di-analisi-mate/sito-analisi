> To open a new file, the officer must receive both the project and the expenses form. For files already open, only the expenses form can be updated. Updates must quote the file number.

Which statement accurately gives both the document that can be updated and the requirement for an update?

- Only the expenses form may be updated, and the open file's number must be quoted.
- Both documents may be updated, and the open file's number must be quoted.
- Only the project may be updated, and the open file's number must be quoted.
- The expenses form may be updated by quoting the name of the project.

## Explanation

The second sentence identifies the document that may be updated. The third requires the file number. The answer must preserve both details.
