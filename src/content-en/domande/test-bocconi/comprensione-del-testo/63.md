> The narrator sees a “Silence” sign outside a workshop. Hammer blows can be heard through the open gate. “An excellent place for a reading room,” he tells his companion, covering his ears.

Which detail makes an ironic reading of the comment plausible?

- The workshop might contain books.
- The sign is written on one line.
- He covers his ears while praising the location.
- His companion stands outside the gate.

## Explanation

The noise and the gesture indicate discomfort at odds with literal praise of a spot for quiet reading.
