> A farming cooperative installed moisture sensors in half of its tomato fields. In those fields irrigation started only when the soil was dry, and water consumption fell. The yield per hectare stayed the same as in the fields without sensors.

Which statement correctly reports both results?

- In the fields with sensors less water was used, with the same yield per hectare.
- In the fields with sensors less water was used and the yield per hectare rose.
- In the fields with sensors both water consumption and the yield per hectare stayed the same.
- In the fields with sensors irrigation was more frequent and the yield per hectare fell.

## Explanation

The second sentence reports that water consumption fell in the fields with sensors. The third states that the yield per hectare stayed the same as in the other fields. A correct answer must keep **both results**, so any option that changes either one contradicts the passage.
