> To contain waiting times, the clinic has added two opening hours on Saturday mornings. Those hours are used mainly for routine check-ups, which take less time than first appointments. After three months the average wait for a check-up went from six weeks to four.

What does “contain” mean in this passage?

- Limit.
- Include.
- Calculate.
- Record.

## Explanation

The clinic adds opening hours, and the last sentence reports a wait that fell from six weeks to four. The aim of the measure is therefore to keep waiting times from growing and to bring them down. In this context *contain* means **limit**, while the sense of *include* concerns what is inside a container or a list.
