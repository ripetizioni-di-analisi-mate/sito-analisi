> A report places two sentences together: “The bakery changed its hours in March. Deliveries increased in April.” An editor inserts “therefore”. The manager deletes it because online sales also began during that period.

What extra claim did “therefore” introduce?

- It placed March before April.
- It distinguished deliveries from in-store collection.
- It said online sales began the previous year.
- It attributed the increase in deliveries to the change in hours.

## Explanation

The separate sentences date the events. “Therefore” would present the second as a consequence of the first without separating the possible effect of online sales.
