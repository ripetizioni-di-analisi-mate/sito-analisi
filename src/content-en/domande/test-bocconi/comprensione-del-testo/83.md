> To estimate how much time the teenagers of a region spend on social media, the researchers chose a sample of 800 students from twenty different schools. The schools were drawn at random from all those in the region, so as to include both large cities and small towns. The results will be published in the spring.

What does the word “sample” mean in the passage?

- The group of people studied in order to draw conclusions about a larger whole.
- The student who achieved the best result.
- A small amount of material taken for a laboratory test.
- The set of all teenagers in the region.

## Explanation

The researchers want to learn about the behaviour of teenagers across the whole region and choose 800 students, drawn so as to represent cities and towns. *Sample* therefore means **the group observed**, from which estimates for the whole population are derived. The set of all teenagers is instead the population that the sample is meant to represent.
