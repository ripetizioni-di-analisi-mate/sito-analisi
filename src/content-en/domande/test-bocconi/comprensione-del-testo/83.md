> The restoration report says, “In June the south wing was still closed.” It says nothing further about that wing.

What information does “still” convey?

- The wing was to reopen exactly on 1 July.
- The closure was first decided in June.
- The north wing was closed in June as well.
- The closure was already in effect before the point described in June.

## Explanation

“Still” presents the closure as continuing up to the point described. It does not specify a reopening date.
