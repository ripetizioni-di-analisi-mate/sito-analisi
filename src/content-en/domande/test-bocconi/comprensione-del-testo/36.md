> A school keeps its musical instruments in the classroom for everyday use. Some instruments, however, are needed by two classes at the same time. When the school brought in bookings for shared instruments, it was able to keep the others freely available.

Which choice expresses the main idea?

- Booking was limited to the instruments for which requests overlapped.
- The school stopped making instruments available for daily use.
- The music timetable was changed for all classes.
- Instruments for daily use were moved out of the classroom to make space for another class.

## Explanation

The passage describes overlapping demand for some instruments and explains the solution applied to that group. The others remain available without a booking.
