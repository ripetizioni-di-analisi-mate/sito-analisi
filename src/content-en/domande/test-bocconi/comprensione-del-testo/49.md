> A bulletin said that ferries would call at Porto Alto and Riva. A correction states, “Delete the reference to Riva. Times for Porto Alto are unchanged.” The bulletin listed no other calls.

Which information from the first announcement can still be used to plan a journey?

- The time already published for Porto Alto.
- The Riva time as the new time for Porto Alto.
- The crossing time between Porto Alto and Riva.
- A departure from Riva at the original time.

## Explanation

The correction expressly retains the Porto Alto times. It instructs readers to remove the entry for Riva.
