> In an experiment, several bee colonies were kept in greenhouses at different temperatures. In the warmer greenhouses the bees started collecting pollen earlier in the morning. The total number of flights per day, on the other hand, remained similar in all the greenhouses. The researchers are now checking whether the same happens outdoors.

Which statement contradicts the passage?

- In the warmer greenhouses the bees began collecting earlier.
- The effect of temperature outdoors has yet to be checked.
- In the warmer greenhouses the bees made many more flights per day.
- The colonies in the experiment lived in greenhouses at different temperatures.

## Explanation

The third sentence states that the number of flights per day remained similar in all the greenhouses. A much higher number of flights in the warm greenhouses **therefore contradicts the passage**. The other three statements restate the earlier start of collection, the ongoing outdoor check and the setup of the experiment.
