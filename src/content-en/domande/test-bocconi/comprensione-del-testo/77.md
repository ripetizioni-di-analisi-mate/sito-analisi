> The reporter writes, “Before the gate closed, Nora managed to deliver the letter.” The next paragraph opens the following morning, when Nora returns to ask if the letter has been read.

Which event is already complete when the gate closes?

- The recipient’s reading of the letter.
- Nora’s delivery of the letter.
- The recipient’s reply.
- The following morning’s request for confirmation.

## Explanation

“Managed to deliver” presents the delivery as completed before the gate closed. Whether the letter has been read remains to be checked the next day.
