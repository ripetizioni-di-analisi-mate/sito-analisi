> The new condominium rules devote three pages to the colour of curtains and not a single line to the maintenance of the lift, which has been out of service for two months. Meanwhile, the residents on the fifth floor keep climbing the stairs with their grocery bags.

What tone does the author take towards the rules?

- Neutral, because the passage only describes what the rules contain.
- Approving, because the rules take care of the building's appearance.
- Alarmed, because the lift has become dangerous.
- Critical, because the rules neglect a practical problem.

## Explanation

The author sets the three pages on curtains beside the absence of any line about the broken lift. The second sentence, with *meanwhile*, shows the consequences of that gap for the residents on the fifth floor. The juxtaposition therefore expresses **a critical judgement** of the rules' priorities. The passage speaks of a lift that is out of service, not of a danger, so an alarmed tone finds no support.
