> Technicians check the water level in the tanks every day. They record pH weekly and inspect the tank walls once a month. The three checks have separate records.

Which measurement is recorded every week?

- The height of the tank walls.
- The pH.
- The water level.
- The interval between inspections.

## Explanation

The second sentence gives a weekly schedule for pH. The level is checked daily and the walls once a month.
