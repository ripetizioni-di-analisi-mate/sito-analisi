> A magazine gives two durations for restoring a painting: “twelve days of work in the studio” and “eight weeks from collection to return”. It explains that the painting spent several weeks waiting for a solvent delivery.

Which question does the eight-week figure answer?

- How long was the owner without the painting?
- How many days of direct work were paid for?
- For how many hours was the solvent applied?
- How many weeks did transport alone take?

## Explanation

Collection and return bound the owner’s time without the painting. This includes waiting even when nobody is working on it.
