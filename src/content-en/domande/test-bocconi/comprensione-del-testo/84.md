> Many people believe that the cheapest plane tickets are always found by booking many months in advance. An analysis of one airline's prices on a hundred routes shows instead that, for domestic flights, the lowest price appeared on average between six and eight weeks before departure. For intercontinental flights the most favourable booking time was earlier.

Why does the author report the airline analysis?

- To explain how airlines set their prices.
- To challenge a widespread belief with observed data.
- To indicate that booking at the last minute pays off.
- To show that intercontinental flights cost more than domestic ones.

## Explanation

The first sentence reports a widespread belief, namely that it *always* pays to book many months in advance. The analysis, introduced by *instead*, indicates that for domestic flights the lowest price appeared between six and eight weeks before departure. The author therefore cites it **to challenge that belief**. Six to eight weeks is still booking in advance, so the passage does not support booking at the last minute.
