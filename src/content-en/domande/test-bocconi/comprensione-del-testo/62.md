> A report says, “In the morning the hall hosts the choir, in the afternoon the quartet.” A student must supply the missing words without changing the programme.

How should the second part be completed?

- In the afternoon the choir hosts the quartet.
- In the afternoon the quartet hosts the choir.
- In the afternoon the quartet leaves the hall.
- In the afternoon the hall hosts the quartet.

## Explanation

The two time phrases introduce parallel clauses. The second leaves out the subject “the hall” and the verb “hosts”. The quartet replaces the choir as the object.
