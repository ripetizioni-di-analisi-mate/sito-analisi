> In the 1950s and 1960s many Italian cities dismantled their tram lines and replaced them with buses. Buses needed no tracks and could easily change route. Decades later some of those cities built new tram lines, having recognised the limits of that choice for carrying large numbers of passengers.

What does the expression “that choice” refer to?

- Building the new tram lines.
- The buses' ability to change route.
- Replacing trams with buses.
- Carrying large numbers of passengers.

## Explanation

The cities that build new tram lines recognise the limits of a decision made earlier. The first sentence describes exactly such a decision, dismantling the trams and switching to buses. *That choice* therefore refers to **replacing trams with buses**, while the new lines are the consequence of recognising those limits.
