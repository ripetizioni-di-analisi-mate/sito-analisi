> The team compared a new notification system with the previous one at three clinics. Waiting time fell at two of them. At the third, the front-desk staff also changed during the trial. The report keeps the results for the third site separate.

Which statement preserves both the number of sites with shorter waits and the reason the third is considered separately?

- Waits fell at two of the sites, and the third is treated separately because the staff changed there as well.
- The staff changed at the two sites that reduced waits.
- The third site dropped the results because it cut the trial short.
- Waits fell at all three sites, and one was treated separately because it used the old system.

## Explanation

The second sentence gives the number of sites with shorter waits. The third describes the difference in conditions that explains how the report treats them.
