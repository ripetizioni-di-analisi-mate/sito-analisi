> The director writes, “Even at the cost of losing an exhibition wall, I would agree to a second exit here. Groups leaving could then avoid crossing paths with those coming in.”

How does she view the loss of the wall?

- As something that would prevent the exit from being useful.
- As something that has already happened because the wall blocks the entrance.
- As a drawback she would accept to improve the movement of groups.
- As the purpose for which she wants the second exit.

## Explanation

“Even at the cost” introduces the drawback she acknowledges. The advantage that motivates her choice follows.
