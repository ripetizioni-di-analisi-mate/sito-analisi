> An observatory has installed a new telescope on a hill far from the city lights. In the first nights the images showed very faint stars that could not be seen from the old site. The technicians point out that the test nights were all moonless and the air was very dry.

What does the last sentence do?

- It explains why the telescope was installed on a hill.
- It refutes the result obtained in the first nights.
- It limits the scope of the result to the conditions in which it was obtained.
- It adds a new result obtained on the test nights.

## Explanation

The second sentence reports a result, namely that very faint stars were visible. The last sentence describes the conditions of the test nights, which were especially favourable for observing. The result is therefore established for those conditions, and the sentence **limits its scope** without denying it.
