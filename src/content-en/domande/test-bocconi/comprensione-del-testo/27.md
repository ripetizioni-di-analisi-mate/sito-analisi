> The system distinguishes between newly placed orders and orders already in transit. For the latter, it also gives the carrier’s name. For each carrier, the system displays the expected arrival time.

Which orders does “the latter” refer to?

- All orders on the two lists.
- Only orders that are late.
- Orders just placed.
- Orders already in transit.

## Explanation

The second group mentioned consists of orders already in transit. The carrier and arrival time are information relevant to orders that have been dispatched.
