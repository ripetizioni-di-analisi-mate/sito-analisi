> “I put the empty jar on the sill. Two hours earlier I had filled it with blackberries. Now the cloth below the window was purple.” The narrator stops there.

Which event is narrated after an event that actually happened later?

- Washing the cloth.
- Putting the empty jar down.
- Filling the jar.
- Noticing the purple cloth.

## Explanation

The filling is narrated in the second sentence. “Two hours earlier” places it before the action described in the first.
