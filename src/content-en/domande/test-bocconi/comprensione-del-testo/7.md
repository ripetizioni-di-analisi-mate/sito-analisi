> Of the 200 households invited, 80 responded to the consortium's survey. Of those who responded, 25% suggested weekly collections. The consortium will use the responses to discuss a possible change.

How many households suggested weekly collections?

- $80$
- $50$
- $120$
- $20$

## Explanation

The $25\%$ applies to the 80 households that responded. One quarter of 80 is 20.
