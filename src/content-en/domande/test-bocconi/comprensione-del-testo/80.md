> The first incandescent light bulbs on the market had a carbon filament and gave a weak, yellowish light. In the early twentieth century the tungsten filament came into use, making their light brighter for the same power consumption. This type of bulb remained the most common in homes for most of the century.

What does “their” refer to in the second sentence?

- The tungsten filament.
- The incandescent light bulbs.
- The carbon filament.
- The early twentieth century.

## Explanation

In the second sentence *their* goes with *light*, so it indicates whose light becomes brighter. The tungsten filament is what brings about the change, so it is not the thing whose light changes, and the carbon filament is what gets replaced. The first sentence describes the light of the bulbs, so *their* refers to **the incandescent light bulbs**.
