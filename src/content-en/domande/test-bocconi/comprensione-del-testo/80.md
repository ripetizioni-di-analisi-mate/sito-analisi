> “Leave the panel for at least two hours after the final coat. Only then remove the tape.” The painter finishes an initial coat at ten and the final one at eleven.

Which event starts the wait that matters for removing the tape?

- Completion of the first coat at ten.
- The start of the first coat.
- Completion of the final coat at eleven.
- The moment when the tape is removed.

## Explanation

“After the final coat” sets the starting point. The two hours run from completion of the last application.
