> The neighbourhood branch was busier after a study room opened. In the same week, the main branch was closed while its floors were replaced. The report therefore calls the visitor figures “provisional”.

What role does the closure of the main branch play in the report's reasoning?

- It establishes that reader numbers fell at both branches.
- It identifies a circumstance that may have influenced the figures for the neighbourhood branch.
- It explains why the report declines to record visitor numbers.
- It proves that the study room caused the entire increase.

## Explanation

The closure coincides with the week measured. The report mentions it before calling the figures provisional because visitor numbers could also depend on that circumstance.
