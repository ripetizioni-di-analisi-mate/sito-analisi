> A researcher writes, “Had the author been born on the coast, he might have described the sea differently.” The previous sentence says he was born inland, and the article makes no comparison with other writers’ texts.

What status does the different description of the sea have?

- It is a difference established by comparing two writers.
- It is a revision the author made to his own text.
- It is a possibility imagined under circumstances different from the actual ones.
- It is a fact inferred from his actual birthplace.

## Explanation

The hypothetical construction changes the birthplace, and “might” qualifies the consequence. The passage presents a conjecture.
