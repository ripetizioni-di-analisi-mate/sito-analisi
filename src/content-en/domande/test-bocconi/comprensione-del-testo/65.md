> For the end-of-year trip a class can rent one of two buses. The first costs 600 euros and has 40 seats, the second costs 420 euros and has 25 seats. Thirty students are going on the trip, and they split the rental cost equally.

How much does each student pay for the rental?

- 15 euros.
- 14 euros.
- 16.80 euros.
- 20 euros.

## Explanation

The second bus has 25 seats, so it cannot carry 30 students. The class must therefore rent the first, and each student's share is $\dfrac{600}{30}=20$ euros. The figure of 14 euros divides the second bus's price by 30 without considering the seats, 15 euros divides the first bus's price by its 40 seats, and 16.80 euros divides the second bus's price by its 25 seats.
