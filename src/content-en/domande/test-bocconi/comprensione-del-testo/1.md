> The shop has changed how it packs orders for home delivery. For example, preserves are now placed in cardboard dividers made from the boxes that come from suppliers. The monthly report records a fall in purchases of packing materials.

What does the second sentence do?

- It compares the strength of two materials.
- It explains how customers choose their preserves.
- It gives an example of the change.
- It quantifies the monthly saving.

## Explanation

The first sentence introduces the new packing method. The second shows how a particular product is packed, so it illustrates the change.
