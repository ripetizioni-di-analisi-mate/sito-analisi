> Deer bones with cut marks have been found in a cave in the Apennines. The researchers believe the marks were made by stone tools during butchering. Dating of the bones is still under way, and no tools have been found in the cave so far.

Which piece of information does the passage present as established?

- There are cut marks on the deer bones.
- The marks were made by stone tools.
- The age of the bones has already been determined.
- Stone tools have been found in the cave.

## Explanation

The first sentence reports a find, namely deer bones with cut marks, and presents it as a fact. The origin of the marks is introduced by *believe*, so it is a hypothesis of the researchers. Dating is still under way and no tools have been found in the cave so far, so the only **established** information is the presence of the marks.
