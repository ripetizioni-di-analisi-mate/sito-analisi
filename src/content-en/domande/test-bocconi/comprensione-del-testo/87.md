> A contract bases a translation fee on the pages of the text supplied by the client. The translation takes up more space because the notes have been expanded. The translator wants to charge for the final page count.

Which contractual phrase settles the disagreement?

- “The notes have been expanded”.
- “The translation takes up more space”.
- “Of the text supplied by the client”.
- “The translator wants to charge”.

## Explanation

The contractual qualifier specifies which text is to be counted. The remarks about greater length concern a different document.
