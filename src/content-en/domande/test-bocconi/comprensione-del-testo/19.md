> Before opening, the scale must be calibrated with reference weights. An attendant will compare the reading on the dial with the value marked on the weights and adjust the instrument.

What operation does “calibrated” refer to?

- Replacing the dial with one of a different size.
- Measuring samples with no stated weight.
- Increasing the scale's maximum capacity.
- Adjusting the scale using known values as a reference.

## Explanation

The second sentence describes a comparison with values already marked on the weights, followed by adjustment of the instrument. That is what *calibrated* means here.
