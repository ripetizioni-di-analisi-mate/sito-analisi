> A municipal log says, “Three signs were removed during the night.” The next day a newspaper writes, “Council workers removed three signs.” The log does not say who was on duty and the newspaper says it relied on the log alone.

What information appears for the first time in the newspaper?

- The number of signs removed.
- The fact that a removal took place.
- The fact that more than one sign was involved.
- The stated identity of the people who carried out the removal.

## Explanation

The passive wording of the log gives the action, time and number of signs, but leaves the actor unnamed. The newspaper assigns the action to council workers, whom the log does not identify.
