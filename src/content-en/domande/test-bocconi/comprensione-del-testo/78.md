> In many varieties the fig tree bears two crops a year. The first fruits, called breba figs, ripen in early summer on the branches that grew the previous year. The main figs ripen between August and September on the new branches.

On which branches do the breba figs ripen?

- On the branches that grew the previous year.
- On the current year's new branches.
- On both kinds of branches.
- On the branches pruned in spring.

## Explanation

The second sentence gives the name breba figs to the first fruits and places them on the branches that grew the previous year. The new branches appear in the third sentence, which concerns the figs that ripen between August and September. The two pieces of information concern different fruits, so the breba figs ripen **on the previous year's branches**.
