> The rules describe a day ticket: “The first entry must be validated at the desk. For later entries that day, show the stamp.” A sign summarises this as “Get a stamp at the desk for every entry.”

Which detail restores the distinction lost on the sign?

- Obtain the stamp on first entry and show it on subsequent entries that day.
- A different coloured stamp is issued on each return.
- The same stamp is valid the following day.
- First entry is allowed even if the visitor skips the desk.

## Explanation

The rules distinguish obtaining the stamp from using it. The sign makes repeated validation appear necessary.
