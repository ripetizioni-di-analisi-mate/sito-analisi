> During the January sales an electronics store cut the price of a television by 40%. In March it raised the discounted price by 40%. A customer claims that in March the television costs the same as before the sales.

If the television cost 500 euros before the sales, how much does it cost in March?

- 500 euros.
- 300 euros.
- 700 euros.
- 420 euros.

## Explanation

The $40\%$ discount brings the price to $500\cdot\dfrac{60}{100}=300$ euros. The $40\%$ increase is calculated on the discounted price, so the new price is $300\cdot\dfrac{140}{100}=420$ euros. The customer's claim is false, because the two percentages have different bases and the 120-euro increase is smaller than the 200-euro discount.
