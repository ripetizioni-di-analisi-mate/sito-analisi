> The centre can move only activities with fewer than twenty participants into its indoor classrooms. The painting class is due to have twenty-four participants. The report does not say how many participants are expected in the music class.

What follows about the activities that can be moved?

- The painting class is above the stated limit.
- The music class will be moved indoors.
- Both classes can move into the same room together.
- The stated limit is twenty-four participants per class.

## Explanation

The rule applies to activities with fewer than twenty participants. The painting class is due to have twenty-four. The figure needed to make the same comparison for music is missing.
