> An interview starts with “Of course I’ll go back there,” then turns to questions about work. An editing note says the opening question, which was not recorded, named a place. No place is mentioned later in the audio.

What can the note establish without reconstructing missing words?

- The speaker had returned there on the day of the interview.
- The place was in the city where the interview was recorded.
- “There” referred to a place named by the interviewer.
- The place was the previous workplace.

## Explanation

The note explains the reference of “there”. It does not record the place name or the date of a return.
