> On a path Marta sees three blue posts and writes “three blue markings visible from the junction”. A colleague suggests “three blue posts along the whole path”. The record specifies that Marta stayed at the junction.

What check would the addition “along the whole path” require?

- Count the posts again from the same spot.
- Inspect the sections out of sight from the junction as well.
- Measure the height of the three visible posts.
- Ask what make of paint was used.

## Explanation

The original record covers what can be seen from the junction. A total for the whole path requires examining the rest as well.
