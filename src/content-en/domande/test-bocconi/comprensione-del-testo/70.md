> An inn advertises “Rooms from 65 euros”. A guest is offered an 82-euro room and says the sign should say 82. The innkeeper shows her a 65-euro room on the price list as well.

What does the sign communicate according to the list?

- The lowest price offered for a room.
- The average price of the occupied rooms.
- A ceiling above which the inn must grant a discount.
- The price of every room available that day.

## Explanation

“From” introduces a minimum price. The 65-euro entry makes the sign compatible with the 82-euro offer.
