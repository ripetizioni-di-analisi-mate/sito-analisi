> In several European cities, last-mile deliveries, from the warehouse to the customer's door, are increasingly made with pedal-assist bicycles fitted with a cargo box. These vehicles can enter restricted traffic zones and stop right outside front doors. For heavy or bulky parcels, however, companies still use vans.

Which title best suits the passage?

- The disappearance of vans from European cities
- The rules of restricted traffic zones
- Cargo bikes for light deliveries in the city
- How a pedal-assist bicycle is built

## Explanation

The passage describes the spread of cargo bikes in urban deliveries, states their advantages and makes clear that vans remain in use for heavy parcels. A suitable title must therefore name the bikes and **their limit to light loads**. The disappearance of vans contradicts the last sentence, while restricted traffic zones are a detail and the construction of the bicycles is a topic the passage does not address.
