> The council tried two ways of announcing the closure of an underpass. Road signs help people who are already in the area. Updated notices on the traffic map let people choose another route before leaving. The report therefore proposes keeping both.

Which idea explains the final proposal?

- Road signs were quicker to update.
- The two kinds of notice reach travellers at different stages.
- People mostly check the map once they reach the underpass.
- Diversions depend on the position of parked cars.

## Explanation

One method reaches people already near the underpass. The other lets them choose a route before leaving. That difference explains why the report proposes using both.
