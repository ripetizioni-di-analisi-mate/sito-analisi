> The report estimates that plaster dries in three days in rooms with ventilation. For closed rooms, it proposes checking on day four and defers the estimate until that check. The timing is therefore determined in different ways in the two cases.

Which statement preserves the difference in certainty between the two types of room?

- The report establishes that plaster dries in exactly three days in ventilated rooms and four days in the others.
- The report gives an estimate for ventilated rooms and puts off estimating for the other rooms until the check.
- The report defers all estimates until the check on day four.
- For closed rooms, the check on day four confirms an estimate already made.

## Explanation

Three days is an estimate for ventilated rooms. Day four is the proposed date of a check in closed rooms, for which the estimate is still pending.
