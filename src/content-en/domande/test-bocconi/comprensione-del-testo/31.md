> In the trial month, the supermarket sold 80 packs of a baked product. Sales rose by 10% in the following month. Over the same period the price of a pack stayed unchanged.

How many packs were sold in the second month?

- $90$
- $8$
- $88$
- $72$

## Explanation

The $10\%$ increase on 80 packs is 8 packs. Adding 8 to the initial 80 gives 88.
