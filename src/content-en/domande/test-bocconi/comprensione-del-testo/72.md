> A consumer association asked 500 people how they get to work. Each person named a single means of transportation, and the table gives the shares of the answers.

| Means | Share |
| --- | ---: |
| Car | 45% |
| Public transportation | 30% |
| Bicycle | 15% |
| On foot | 10% |

How many of the people surveyed use neither the car nor public transportation?

- 75.
- 125.
- 275.
- 50.

## Explanation

People who use neither the car nor public transportation go by bicycle or on foot, that is, $15\%+10\%=25\%$ of those surveyed. $25\%$ of 500 is $\dfrac{500}{4}=125$. The figures 75 and 50 count only one of the two categories, while 275 corresponds to the $55\%$ who do not use the car.
