> A call for entries says, “Candidates who have already filed their drawing will receive a receipt.” A revision says, “All candidates have already filed their drawing and will receive a receipt.” Three candidates still need to file.

Why does the revision change the notice?

- It changes the item candidates have to file.
- It says the three candidates have lost the right to file.
- It gives everyone a property that originally restricted who would receive a receipt.
- It moves up the date by which receipts must be sent.

## Explanation

In the notice the relative clause selects those who have filed. The revision attributes filing even to the three who have yet to do it.
