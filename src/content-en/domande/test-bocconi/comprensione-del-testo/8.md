> The theatre sells tickets for the morning performance both at the box office and online. For the evening performance, only tickets still available at 5 p.m. are sold at the box office. Online sales remain open until one hour before the start.

Which statement accurately gives the box-office sales rule for the evening performance?

- Tickets for the evening performance are sold only at the box office.
- The box office sells only tickets still available at 5 p.m.
- Online sales for the evening performance end at 5 p.m.
- All ticket sales stop at 5 p.m.

## Explanation

The second sentence concerns the evening performance and specifies which tickets are sold at the box office. The final sentence gives a separate deadline for buying online.
