> A dressmaker notes three measurements. “I’ll use the measurement taken after washing unless the fabric shrinks again in the second wash. In that case I’ll measure again.” The second wash leaves the fabric at its size after the first.

Which measurement will guide the cut according to the note?

- The one taken before either wash, when the fabric was still dry.
- The one taken after the first wash.
- The average of the three recorded measurements.
- A fourth measurement required in every case.

## Explanation

A fresh measurement is called for only if the fabric shrinks further. Since that does not happen, the measurement taken after the first wash stands.
