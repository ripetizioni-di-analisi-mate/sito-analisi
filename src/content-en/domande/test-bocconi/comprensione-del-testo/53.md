> The city marathon offers three routes, of 42, 21 and 10 kilometres. Only adult athletes with a medical certificate for competitive sport may enter the full race. For the half marathon the competitive-sport certificate is enough, while the 10-kilometre run is open to anyone with a non-competitive certificate.

What do the rules require of a sixteen-year-old who wants to run the half marathon?

- Being an adult and having the competitive-sport certificate.
- A medical certificate for competitive sport.
- Only the non-competitive certificate.
- Membership in a sports club.

## Explanation

The half marathon is the 21-kilometre route, and the third sentence says the competitive-sport certificate is enough to take part in it. Adult age is required only for the full race, so the runner's age does not matter for the half marathon. The non-competitive certificate is enough only for the 10-kilometre run.
