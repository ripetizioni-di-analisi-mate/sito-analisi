> In the first three months, 150 of the 200 distributed samples were returned. Of the returned samples, 30 had no label. The laboratory analyses only labelled samples.

How many returned samples are usable under the stated rule?

- 170.
- 150.
- 50.
- 120.

## Explanation

The rule applies to the 150 samples that were returned. Subtracting the 30 without labels leaves 120.
