> The specification gives a nominal frame length of twenty centimetres. The drawing states “tolerance of one millimetre either way”. The supplier rejects a frame two millimetres too long.

What does “tolerance” denote on the drawing?

- The difference between the shortest and longest frames received.
- The minimum amount of material lost in cutting.
- The actual length of every frame delivered.
- The permitted deviation from the nominal length.

## Explanation

The drawing defines how far a frame may deviate from twenty centimetres. The rejected frame exceeds that margin.
