> In recent years the city has planted trees along its main avenues and replaced part of the asphalt in school playgrounds with grass. On summer days the ground temperature in the renovated playgrounds has dropped by a few degrees compared with before. This persuaded the city council to extend the work to public car parks.

What does “This” refer to?

- The drop in ground temperature in the renovated playgrounds.
- The planting of trees along the avenues.
- The decision to work on the car parks.
- The number of summer days.

## Explanation

The pronoun *this* takes up a fact already mentioned, and the sentence before it reports the drop in temperature in the renovated playgrounds. A measured result is what can persuade the council to repeat the work elsewhere. The decision about the car parks is instead the effect described in the same sentence as *this*, so it cannot be what the pronoun refers to.
