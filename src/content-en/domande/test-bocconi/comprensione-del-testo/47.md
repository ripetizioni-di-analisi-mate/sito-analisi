> A poster announces a bookbinding workshop and a printmaking workshop. “They will take place in the north room and the courtyard, respectively. If it rains we will move the latter into the greenhouse.” Rain is forecast all day.

Under the wet-weather plan, where will the bookbinding workshop take place?

- In the greenhouse.
- In the north room.
- Partly in the north room and partly in the greenhouse.
- In the courtyard.

## Explanation

“Respectively” pairs bookbinding with the north room. Only the latter workshop, printmaking, moves in wet weather.
