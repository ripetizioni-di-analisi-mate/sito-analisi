> A historian writes, “We can trace the square’s name back to 1760, the year of the first deed to record it. Earlier deeds may yet turn up.” A translator writes, “The square was given its name in 1760.”

Which distinction has been lost?

- The distinction between the square’s name and a nearby street’s name.
- The distinction between the date of the deed and the date of its translation.
- The distinction between a calendar year and a school year.
- The distinction between the earliest known record and the date of naming.

## Explanation

The date belongs to a document known today. The name could already have been in use when the deed was drawn up.
