> A canteen's menu always included a single dairy-free dish. During a trial a different dairy-free dish was offered each day at the same price. Participants reported which dishes they would choose again. The canteen used their responses to schedule two recurring options.

Which summary gives both the purpose of the trial and how its results were used?

- The canteen tested different prices and kept the highest one.
- The canteen replaced all its dishes with the single option that received most votes.
- The canteen collected preferences about dishes to choose two recurring options.
- The trial identified how many participants were intolerant to dairy.

## Explanation

The dish changes during the trial while the price remains the same. As the final sentence says, the responses are used to select two options.
