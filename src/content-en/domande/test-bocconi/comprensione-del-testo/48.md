> In November an olive mill pressed the olives of three members of a cooperative. From the first member's olives it obtained 120 litres of oil, from the second member's 160 litres. The mill's register records that the three members obtained an average of 150 litres each, but the third member's line is illegible.

How many litres of oil did the third member obtain?

- 170.
- 150.
- 140.
- 130.

## Explanation

An average of 150 litres for three members corresponds to a total of $3\cdot 150=450$ litres. The first two members obtained $120+160=280$ litres together, so the third obtained $450-280=170$ litres. The figure 150 is the average itself and 140 is the average of the two known members only. The first member is 30 litres below the average and the second 10 litres above, so the third must be 20 litres above the average. The figure 130 comes from placing the third member 20 litres below the average instead.
