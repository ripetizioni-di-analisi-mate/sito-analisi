> “Let’s shorten the captions: nobody reads them,” says a councillor. The attendant replies that in the portrait room visitors stop at the labels and follow the lines with a finger. She adds that she cannot see the other rooms from her post.

Which part of the proposal can the attendant directly challenge?

- The possibility of shortening the texts in the other rooms.
- The councillors’ choice to visit the museum.
- The idea that the portrait-room captions are long.
- The claim that no visitor reads any caption.

## Explanation

The readers seen in one room provide counterexamples to “nobody”. The observation does not measure the effect of shortening a text.
