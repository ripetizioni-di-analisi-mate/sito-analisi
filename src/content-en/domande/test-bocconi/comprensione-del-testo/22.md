> The magazine gives access to its full archive only to annual subscribers. All registered users can read the current month's articles. Gianni accesses the full archive through his account.

What can be inferred about Gianni?

- He is a monthly subscriber with temporary permission.
- He is the only user who can read the current month's articles.
- He is an annual subscriber.
- He reads the archive through someone else's account.

## Explanation

Access to the full archive is reserved for annual subscribers. Gianni accesses it through his own account, so he belongs to that group.
