> A review calls the new hall “quieter”. The previous sentence describes the murmur at readings in the old venue. The next one says that chairs could still be heard moving in the new hall.

What is the implied point of comparison in “quieter”?

- The volume of the reader’s voice.
- The noise in the new hall when it is empty.
- The noise at readings in the old venue.
- The noise made by one chair.

## Explanation

The preceding sentence supplies the reference situation. The chairs show how a comparatively quiet hall can still have some audible noise.
