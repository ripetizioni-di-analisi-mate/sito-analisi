> Many small shops now accept card payments even for purchases of a few euros. The fees the shopkeeper pays the bank on each transaction reduce the profit on these purchases. Several shopkeepers point out, however, that in the past customers without cash often left without buying anything. For these shops, cards therefore bring in sales that would otherwise be lost.

What role does the second sentence play in the passage's reasoning?

- It explains why customers prefer to pay in cash.
- It presents a drawback that the passage weighs against an advantage.
- It states the conclusion the passage reaches.
- It reports the shopkeepers' opinion of banks.

## Explanation

The second sentence names a cost for the shopkeeper, namely the fee paid on each transaction. The third sentence, introduced by *however*, reports the sales that cards make it possible to recover, and the last one draws the conclusion. The second sentence therefore presents **the drawback** that the rest of the passage weighs against an advantage.
