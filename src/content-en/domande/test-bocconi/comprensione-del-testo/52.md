> Some economists propose cutting VAT on appliance repairs in order to extend the life of the devices. According to critics, the cut would cost the state revenue without changing consumers' habits. Supporters reply that the choice between repairing and buying new often depends on a difference of a few tens of euros. Even a small drop in the price of repairs could therefore shift some of those decisions.

How is the reasoning in the passage organised?

- A problem, two alternative solutions and the choice of the better one.
- A statistic, its explanation and a forecast.
- A general rule followed by three examples.
- A proposal, an objection and a reply to the objection.

## Explanation

The first sentence presents the economists' proposal. The second reports the critics' objection, namely the loss of revenue with no effect on habits. The last two sentences give the supporters' reply, which challenges precisely the part of the objection about habits. The passage therefore follows the pattern **proposal, objection, reply**.
