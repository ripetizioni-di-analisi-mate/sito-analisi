> An audio guide places a village “on the right of the river as you look towards the sea”. Anna listens on the bridge, facing the mountains where the river begins.

How can Anna use the guide’s frame of reference?

- Follow the bank up into the mountains.
- Turn towards the river mouth before identifying the right-hand side.
- Choose the bank on which the bridge stands.
- Use her own right without turning.

## Explanation

The guide uses the right-hand side of someone looking out to sea. Anna faces the source, so she needs to face the opposite way.
