> The chair catalogue lists only items restored after 2000. The green chair is absent. A dealer uses its absence to claim it has never been restored.

Which document could refute him and still be consistent with the catalogue?

- A recent photograph of the green chair on display.
- A 1996 receipt for a red chair.
- An unaccepted 2010 estimate for restoring the green chair.
- A 1996 invoice for restoring the green chair.

## Explanation

A restoration in 1996 would refute “never” and would fall outside the catalogue’s period. The other documents do not establish a restoration of the green chair.
