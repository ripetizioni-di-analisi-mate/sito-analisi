> The theatre opened in 1842 with an opera by a local composer. In 1878 a fire destroyed its stage, and the rebuilding took six years. During the work the companies performed in the hall of the nearby academy, which since 1870 had also housed a singing school.

Which of these events happened first?

- The fire that destroyed the stage.
- The opening of the singing school in the academy.
- The end of the theatre's rebuilding.
- The companies' first performance in the academy's hall.

## Explanation

The academy has housed the singing school since 1870. The fire dates from 1878, and the performances in the hall begin after it, during the work. The rebuilding lasts six years, so it ends in 1884. The earliest of the events listed is therefore **the opening of the singing school**.
