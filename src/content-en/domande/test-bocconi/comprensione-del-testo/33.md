> The article opens with a photograph of a closed level crossing. It explains that the photo shows a single observation and puts it alongside a log showing when each closure began and ended. The conclusion compares the total duration of closures on the two days observed.

What does adding the log to the photograph achieve?

- It allows the paper used to print the photograph to be dated.
- It removes the duration of closures from the comparison.
- It shows that the crossing is always closed during the hours shown in the photograph.
- It provides the time data needed for the final comparison.

## Explanation

The photograph records a single state. The log gives the closure times, which allow the durations to be added up and the two days to be compared.
