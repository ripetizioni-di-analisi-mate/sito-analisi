> The foreman reads aloud, “Bring the long rope, Leo.” Leo sets off. In a copy without the comma, Marta wonders whether the name tells her who usually brings the rope.

What is the function of “Leo” in the foreman’s version?

- It identifies the owner of the rope.
- It names the person being addressed.
- It signs a description written by Marta.
- It specifies which rope is called Leo.

## Explanation

The comma marks the name as a direct address accompanying the command. Leo’s subsequent action supports that reading.
