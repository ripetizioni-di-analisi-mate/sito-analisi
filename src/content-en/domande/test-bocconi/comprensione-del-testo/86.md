> According to the historian Elena Rossi, the fall in the village's population in the seventeenth century was caused mainly by an outbreak of plague. Her colleague Marco Bianchi instead considers the closure of the nearby mines decisive, since it left many families without work. Both cite the parish registers, but they read them differently. Rossi focuses on deaths, Bianchi on families moving to other parishes.

Which sentence correctly describes the content of the passage?

- The author shows that plague was the main cause of the decline.
- The parish registers disprove both explanations.
- Two scholars explain the same decline differently, starting from the same sources.
- The two scholars agree on the cause and disagree about the date of the decline.

## Explanation

The passage reports two explanations of the same decline, one based on the epidemic and one on the closure of the mines. Both scholars use the parish registers, but they look at different data in them. The author reports the two positions without taking sides, so the correct description is **the disagreement between two readings of the same sources**.
