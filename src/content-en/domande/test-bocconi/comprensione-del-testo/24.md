> The network of workshops lists the actual duration of repair jobs observed in town. For jobs outside town, it publishes an estimate that includes travel time. The catalogue was updated at the end of October.

Which statement preserves the distinction between the two types of figure?

- Outside town the catalogue estimates only the time spent on the repair at the customer's premises.
- In town the figures are observed durations, while the estimates for jobs outside town also include travel.
- In and outside town, the figures are all observed durations calculated in the same way.
- For jobs in town the catalogue gives an estimate with travel time.

## Explanation

The first two sentences distinguish an observation from an estimate and specify which measure includes travel. The update date applies to the catalogue.
