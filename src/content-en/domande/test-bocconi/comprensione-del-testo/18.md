> The archive puts the index of the tapes in a sound collection online. The recordings can be heard on site by appointment. Digital copies available for research can be consulted at the same workstation and remain at the archive.

What does the passage say is available online?

- The appointment schedule.
- The index of the tapes.
- The digital copies for research.
- The full recordings.

## Explanation

The first sentence reports the online publication of the index. The following sentences put both listening and consulting copies on site.
