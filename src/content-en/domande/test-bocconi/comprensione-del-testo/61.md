> Every year the trunks of many trees form a new ring. Favourable years produce wide rings, dry years narrow ones. By comparing the sequence of rings in an old beam with that of trees of known age from the same area, one can determine in which years the tree the beam came from was growing. In this way the timber used in a building can be dated.

What is the main purpose of the passage?

- To show that dry years have become more frequent.
- To describe techniques for making strong beams.
- To compare the growth of different tree species.
- To explain how tree rings make it possible to date wood.

## Explanation

The first two sentences present the phenomenon, namely annual rings of different widths. The third describes the comparison between ring sequences, and the last states its application. The passage therefore explains **a method for dating** wood, while the frequency of dry years, the making of beams and the comparison of species lie outside the text.
