> The weekly paper runs the headline “The district has chosen its new emblem”. The article says that children chose their favourite symbol during a workshop in one class. The district council will receive the drawing the following week.

Which headline preserves both who made the choice and its status?

- All schoolchildren vote for the district symbol.
- One class chooses an emblem to propose to the council.
- Council adopts emblem chosen by children.
- District postpones a vote already under way.

## Explanation

The vote took place in one class. Delivery to the council is still in the future, so the drawing remains a proposal.
