> The library holds newspapers, illustrated periodicals and leaflets. Newspapers can be consulted on film. A selection of illustrated periodicals can be handled in the original, and leaflets can be viewed as scans on site.

For which material does the passage say at least some items can be consulted in the original?

- Illustrated periodicals.
- Both newspapers and leaflets.
- Leaflets.
- Newspapers.

## Explanation

A selection of periodicals can be consulted in the original. Newspapers are on film and leaflets are available as scans.
