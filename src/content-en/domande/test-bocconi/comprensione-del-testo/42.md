> In her diary for 1911 Ada writes, “Tomorrow we leave for Genoa.” In the 1980 edition a note below the sentence reads, “The journey was postponed by a week.” The preface attributes all notes printed beneath the diary pages to the editor.

Who supplies the information about the postponement?

- The editor of the 1980 edition.
- Ada at the time she announced the departure.
- The person who travelled with Ada.
- A publisher whom Ada quotes in the diary.

## Explanation

The forecast appears in the diary, whereas the postponement is reported in a note. The preface attributes notes of this kind to the editor.
