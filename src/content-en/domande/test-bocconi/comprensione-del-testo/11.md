> The heading “recovered materials” appears for the first time in the accounts. Stocks returned from building sites are entered under this heading if they can still be used.

What does “heading” mean in this passage?

- The name of the supplier of the stocks.
- A note written by the inspectors.
- A category under which items are entered in the accounts.
- The full budget for one building site.

## Explanation

Stocks meeting the condition are entered under the same label. The term therefore refers to a category in the accounts.
