> The consortium received 115 requests. Twenty concerned products already in stock. Of the remaining 95 requests, 45 were sent to local suppliers. The rest went to the national network.

How many requests went to the national network?

- 70.
- 65.
- 45.
- 50.

## Explanation

After setting aside the requests for products already in stock, 95 remain. Subtracting the 45 for local suppliers leaves 50 for the national network.
