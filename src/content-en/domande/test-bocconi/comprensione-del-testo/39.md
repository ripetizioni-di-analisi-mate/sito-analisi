> In the script Lia’s first line is “I’ll be back tomorrow.” The words “closes the window gently” follow in italics and in brackets, then comes another line in quotation marks. An introductory note distinguishes spoken lines from acting directions.

How should the actor playing Lia perform the italicised passage?

- Say “closes the window gently” as part of the first line.
- Repeat “I’ll be back tomorrow” in a quieter voice.
- Close the window before the first line.
- Gently close the window after speaking the line.

## Explanation

Quotation marks enclose the character’s spoken words. The bracketed italic text describes an action and follows the first line, so the actor should perform it at that point.
