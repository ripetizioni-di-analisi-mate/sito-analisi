> The company describes the project as “on hold”, since it is waiting for an opinion before deciding whether to buy the machinery. The reviewer lists that opinion among the steps leading up to the final decision.

What does “on hold” mean in the passage?

- Already approved by the reviewer.
- Well into the installation of the machinery.
- Awaiting a step before the decision can be made.
- Already rejected in a final decision.

## Explanation

The purchase is still to be decided, and the opinion is required before that decision. The project is therefore waiting.
