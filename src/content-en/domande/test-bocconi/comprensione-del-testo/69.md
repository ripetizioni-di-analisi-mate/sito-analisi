> A recipe says, “Divide the dough and fold the nuts into one half. Rejoin the halves only after the first rise.” Paul folds in the nuts, immediately rejoins the halves, and then leaves the dough to rise.

Which timing relationship in the instructions has he reversed?

- Preparing the nuts and preparing the flour.
- The first rise and the rejoining of the halves.
- Dividing the dough and adding the nuts.
- Adding the nuts and cutting the baked loaf.

## Explanation

The recipe places rejoining after the first rise. Paul rejoins the halves before it, while preserving the order of dividing and adding nuts.
