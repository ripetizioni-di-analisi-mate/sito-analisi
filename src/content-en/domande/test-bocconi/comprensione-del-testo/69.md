> The new insulating panel costs more than traditional rock wool. It cuts heating consumption enough to recover the price difference within a few winters. Several builders already offer it for renovations.

Which expression, placed at the start of the second sentence, would make its link with the first explicit?

- For example.
- As a result.
- That is.
- However.

## Explanation

The first sentence presents a drawback of the panel, its higher price. The second presents an advantage that offsets that drawback, so the link between the two sentences is **a contrast**. *However* introduces exactly this kind of information, which limits or balances what precedes it. *For example*, *as a result* and *that is* would present the saving as an instance, an effect or a rewording of the higher price.
