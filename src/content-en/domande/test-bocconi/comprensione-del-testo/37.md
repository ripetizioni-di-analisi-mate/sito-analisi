> The organisers had estimated that arrivals would be spread evenly. Their report acknowledges that the peak came in the final twenty minutes. It adds, however, that only one gate was open for entry in those twenty minutes, because the others had been reserved for departures.

What information does the final sentence add?

- The reason all spectators decided to arrive late.
- Confirmation that arrivals were spread evenly.
- Proof that departures ended before the peak.
- An operating condition that applied during the peak.

## Explanation

The last sentence gives the number of gates admitting people at the peak. It describes how access was organised and leaves individual spectators' reasons open.
