> In the first year, 12% of students at the centre took part in an optional workshop. In the second year, the share was 18%. The report gives no total enrolment figures for the two years.

By how many percentage points did the share change?

- It rose by 50 percentage points.
- It rose by 18 percentage points.
- It rose by 12 percentage points.
- It rose by 6 percentage points.

## Explanation

The difference between the two shares is $18-12=6$ percentage points. A figure of $50\%$ would express the increase relative to the initial share.
