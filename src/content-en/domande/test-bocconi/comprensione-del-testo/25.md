> The new application counts available rooms using confirmed bookings. Over the weekend, the count differed from the number of rooms actually free. Two groups had cancelled by phone, and their cancellations were entered on Monday. The manager therefore introduced an evening check of phone cancellations.

What does the third sentence do?

- It gives a fact that accounts for the discrepancy in the count.
- It lists all the ways to book a room.
- It reports an increase in bookings made on Monday.
- It gives the outcome of the evening check.

## Explanation

The third sentence says that two cancellations had taken place but were entered after the weekend. That delay accounts for the difference mentioned in the second sentence.
