> A chain of bookstores moved its children's books from the back of the stores to the entrance. In the following three months sales in that section grew, while sales in the other sections remained stable. During the same period no new children's series came out.

Which conclusion is supported by the passage?

- Moving any section to the entrance makes its sales grow.
- The growth is due to new children's series coming out.
- The new location may have boosted sales of children's books.
- The chain's total sales fell.

## Explanation

After the move, sales grow only in the section that was moved, and the passage rules out an alternative explanation, namely the release of new series. These facts make a link between location and sales plausible, so **the cautious conclusion is supported**. Extending the result to any section goes beyond the case observed, and total sales cannot have fallen, since one section grew and the others remained stable.
