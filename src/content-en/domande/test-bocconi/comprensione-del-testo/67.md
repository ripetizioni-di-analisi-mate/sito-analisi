> The review quotes the phrase “a sea of copper”, then notes that the painter worked only with blue pigments. An opening note says that quotations come from the painter’s letters.

Which reading preserves the distinction between quotation and observation?

- The painter said he used copper and blue together.
- The copper image is the painter’s; the reviewer reports the pigment information.
- The reviewer found metallic copper among the pigments.
- Both expressions are quoted words of the painter.

## Explanation

Quotation marks attribute the verbal image to the painter. The next sentence gives information about materials in the reviewer’s voice.
