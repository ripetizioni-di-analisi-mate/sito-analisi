> The register of a rural school from 1910 lists 38 pupils in first grade and 12 in fourth grade. The fourth-grade pupils are all boys. The teacher notes that in spring many children left lessons to help their families in the fields.

Which statement is consistent with the register?

- Fourth grade had more pupils than first grade.
- There were some girls in fourth grade.
- Attendance at lessons rose in spring.
- There were also girls in first grade.

## Explanation

The register states the pupils' sex only for fourth grade, so the presence of girls in first grade is possible and **consistent with the text**. The other three statements contradict the passage. Fourth grade has 12 pupils against 38 in first grade, its pupils are all boys, and in spring many children left lessons.
