> The contents page promises “the reasons for the theatre’s closure”. The chapter first describes damage to the roof, then failed attempts to find temporary premises. The last page lists shows rescheduled in other towns.

Which page least directly fulfils the promise in the contents?

- The list of shows rescheduled elsewhere.
- The description of the roof damage.
- The explanation of why the attempts failed.
- The account of the search for temporary premises.

## Explanation

The damage and the failed search help explain the closure. The list of shows documents an organisational consequence.
