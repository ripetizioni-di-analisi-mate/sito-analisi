> The committee allocated 18 stations to science projects. Eight stations are near the entrances, and three of these have a network cable. The other ten are in the main hall and use the wireless network.

How many stations near the entrances have a network cable?

- Three.
- Ten.
- Eighteen.
- Eight.

## Explanation

The passage says eight stations are near the entrances and limits the figure for network cables to three of those eight.
