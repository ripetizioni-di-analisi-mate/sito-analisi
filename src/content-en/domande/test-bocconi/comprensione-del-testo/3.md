> The agency had sent the quote and the contract. The client approved the quote. The client then asked for the contract to be translated before signing it. This request postponed the appointment.

What does “this request” refer to?

- The translation of the contract before signing.
- Moving the appointment.
- Sending the contract together with the quote.
- The revision of the quote that had been approved.

## Explanation

The previous sentence reports the request to translate the contract before signing. Postponing the appointment was the effect of that request.
