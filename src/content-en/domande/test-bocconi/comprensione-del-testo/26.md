> The club takes volleyball court bookings only through its website. It also takes bookings by phone for its tennis courts. Payment for either type of court is made at the club entrance.

Which activity can be carried out by phone?

- Paying for use of a court.
- Booking a tennis court.
- Booking a volleyball court.
- Completing any booking and its payment.

## Explanation

Booking by phone is mentioned in the sentence about tennis. The final sentence says payments are made at the entrance.
