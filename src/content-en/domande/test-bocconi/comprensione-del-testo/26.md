> A manual gives two glossary entries. “Dredger: a machine that removes sediment from the bottom.” “Scouring excavation: an excavation intended for scouring.” A reader wants to understand the word “scouring” without consulting another page.

Why is the second entry less helpful to that reader than the first?

- It assigns two incompatible functions to a dredger.
- It repeats the word that needs explaining without describing the operation.
- It lists the entries by date instead of by name.
- It describes work that always requires a dredger.

## Explanation

The first entry explains the function in words other than the machine’s name. Understanding the second entry already requires knowing what “scouring” means. The explanation is circular.
