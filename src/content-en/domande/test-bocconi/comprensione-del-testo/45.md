> A manufacturer changed a vacuum's filter to speed up replacement. In the manual, the new procedure reduces the number of steps from four to two. The manufacturer makes clear that the recommended replacement frequency remains the same.

What does the final sentence clarify in relation to the preceding ones?

- The change helps only people who rarely use the vacuum.
- The new filter takes four steps if replaced frequently.
- The procedure becomes quicker, while the recommended time between replacements stays the same.
- The manufacturer halved the replacement frequency.

## Explanation

The manual reduces the number of steps for a single replacement. The final sentence clarifies that the schedule for replacing the filter stays the same.
