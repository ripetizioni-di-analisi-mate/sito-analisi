> The depot received the frames in May. In June it started fitting the wheels after obtaining the required certification. The summary gives the two dates as separate milestones.

What relationship does the passage state between certification and fitting the wheels?

- Certification allowed the frames to arrive in May.
- Certification was required and was obtained before the fitting began.
- Certification was obtained at the exact moment the wheels were fitted.
- Certification was issued after the fitting was complete.

## Explanation

Fitting starts *after* the required certification is obtained, so the passage states both the requirement and the order of events.
