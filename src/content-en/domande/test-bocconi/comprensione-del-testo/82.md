> In an Italian sailing manual, “P” in the winds table stands for ponente (west). In the later duty register, “P” stands for pomeriggio (afternoon). A note beside a list of names says, “For this page’s abbreviations see the duty register.”

How should a P beside a name be read?

- It indicates a westerly wind.
- It indicates afternoon duty.
- It indicates that the name appears in both tables.
- It means afternoon duty in a westerly wind.

## Explanation

The cross-reference selects the relevant register. The meaning supplied for the winds table belongs to a different section.
