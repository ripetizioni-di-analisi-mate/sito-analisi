> In the earliest photographs of cities the streets often look deserted. Exposure times could last minutes, and people in motion left no sharp image on the plate. Only the buildings remained visible, along with anyone who stood still for a long time, such as a passerby having his shoes shined.

How is the second sentence related to the first?

- The second describes a consequence of the deserted streets.
- The second gives an example of a deserted street.
- The second explains the fact described in the first.
- The second contradicts the first.

## Explanation

The first sentence reports a fact, namely the empty streets in the earliest photographs. The second says that exposures lasted a long time and that people in motion did not leave a sharp image. The long exposures are therefore **the cause** of the deserted look of the streets, even though there is no explicit connective between the two sentences.
