> The nursery mixed dry leaves into the clay soil. The new mixture drains better, as shown by the shorter time it takes water to flow to the bottom of the pot.

What improvement is described by “drains better”?

- The pot stays warmer during sunny hours.
- The mixture holds all the water at the surface.
- Leaves break down more quickly.
- Water moves through the mixture to the bottom more quickly.

## Explanation

The second sentence says how the improvement was identified, namely by the shorter time water takes to reach the bottom.
