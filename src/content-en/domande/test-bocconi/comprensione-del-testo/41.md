> The writer describes a workshop that started cataloguing spare parts by diameter. She then recounts the difficulty of finding the right part when several shared a diameter but used different fittings. The last part presents the dual index introduced afterwards.

What function does the middle of the passage serve?

- It lists the advantages of the dual index after it was adopted.
- It explains how to determine the prices of spare parts.
- It identifies the limitation of the original classification rule.
- It establishes that recording diameter is pointless.

## Explanation

Classifying by diameter puts parts with different fittings in the same group. The middle identifies the problem that the later index addresses.
