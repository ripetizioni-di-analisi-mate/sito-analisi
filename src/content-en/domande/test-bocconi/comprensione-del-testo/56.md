> The market rules define “opening days” as days on which stalls are set up. A note says, “Notice on display for the first two opening days in November.” Stalls are scheduled for 3, 10 and 17 November.

When must the notice still be visible for the second required day?

- 10 November.
- 2 November.
- 4 November.
- 17 November.

## Explanation

Only stall days count. The 3rd is the first and the 10th the second.
