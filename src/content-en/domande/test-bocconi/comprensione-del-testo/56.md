> The letters kept in the archive of a Venetian trading company record both the date of dispatch and the date of arrival. Letters sent from Alexandria took about a month in summer and almost twice as long in winter. In the winter months the company cut back on business that required quick decisions by its agents in Egypt.

What does the passage suggest about the company's conduct?

- It stopped trading with Egypt during the winter.
- It sent more letters in winter to make up for the delays.
- It preferred its agents in Alexandria to those in other cities.
- It adapted its business to the time letters took to travel.

## Explanation

The second sentence states that in winter letters from Alexandria took almost twice as long. The third reports that precisely in the winter months the company cut back on business requiring quick decisions. Taken together, the two facts therefore suggest that the company **adapted its business to the time letters took**. The passage speaks of cutting back some business, so stopping all trade with Egypt goes beyond the text.
