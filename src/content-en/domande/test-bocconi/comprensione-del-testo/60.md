> During the excavation of an ancient Roman villa, a student noticed some fragments of painted plaster in a drainage channel. The archaeologist in charge decided to have all the soil from the channel sifted. The museum's restorer then reassembled the recovered fragments, reconstructing part of a decoration with birds and branches.

Who reconstructed the decoration?

- The student who noticed the fragments.
- The museum's restorer.
- The archaeologist in charge of the excavation.
- The workers who sifted the soil.

## Explanation

The passage assigns a different action to each person. The student notices the fragments and the archaeologist decides to have the soil from the channel sifted. The last sentence assigns the reassembly of the fragments to the museum's restorer, so the restorer is the one who **reconstructed the decoration**.
