> A guidebook marks one path in black and another in grey. Its key says that these colours distinguish routes walked by the author from routes described by other people, in that order. It recommends sturdy shoes for both.

What can be attributed to the author on the basis of the grey line?

- She walked that path only in sturdy shoes.
- She excluded the grey path from the itinerary.
- She judged the grey path less safe.
- She reported someone else’s description of that path.

## Explanation

Grey identifies the source of the description. The footwear recommendation applies to both routes.
