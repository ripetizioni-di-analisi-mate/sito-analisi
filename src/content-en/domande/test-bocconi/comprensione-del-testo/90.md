> The film follows three generations of a fishing family in a small port on the Adriatic. The cinematography alternates long shots of the sea with close-ups of faces. The director, however, closes the story with a rushed ending, which resolves in a few minutes conflicts built up over two hours.

Which expression in the passage conveys a negative judgement by the author?

- “long shots”
- “close-ups of faces”
- “small port”
- “rushed ending”

## Explanation

*Rushed* indicates excessive haste, and the rest of the sentence justifies it with the contrast between a few minutes of ending and two hours of build-up. The adjective therefore expresses **a negative assessment** of the ending. *Long shots*, *close-ups of faces* and *small port* instead describe the cinematography and the setting without judging them.
