> The story closes with Marta slipping a letter into her brother’s coat pocket. In the final line he takes his raincoat instead and closes the door.

What remains open at the end of the scene?

- Whether and when the brother will find the letter left in the other garment.
- Which garment Marta put the letter in.
- Whether Marta had already written the letter.
- Whether the brother took his raincoat.

## Explanation

The text shows where Marta leaves the letter and which garment her brother takes. It does not narrate a later discovery.
