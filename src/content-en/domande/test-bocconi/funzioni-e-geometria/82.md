Two straight trails of length $5$ and $8$ kilometres start at the same lodge and form an angle of $60^\circ$. What is the straight-line distance between their far ends?

- $7\,\mathrm{km}$
- $3\,\mathrm{km}$
- $\sqrt{89}\,\mathrm{km}$
- $\sqrt{129}\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

The required side faces the angle between the known lengths. The cosine rule gives $d^2=5^2+8^2-2\cdot5\cdot8\cos60^\circ=89-40=49$. The positive distance is $7$ kilometres.
