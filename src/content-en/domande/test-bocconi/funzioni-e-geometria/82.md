For which values of $c$ does the line $x=c$ intersect the circle $x^2+y^2-6x+2y-6=0$ in two points?

- $-1<c<7$
- $-1\leq c\leq 7$
- $-7<c<1$
- $3-\sqrt6<c<3+\sqrt6$

## Explanation

Completing the squares gives $(x-3)^2+(y+1)^2=6+9+1=16$, so the center is $(3,-1)$ and the radius is $4$. The vertical line $x=c$ is at distance $|c-3|$ from the center. A line is secant when its distance from the center is less than the radius, therefore we need $|c-3|<4$, that is, $-1<c<7$. For $c=-1$ and $c=7$ the line is tangent.
