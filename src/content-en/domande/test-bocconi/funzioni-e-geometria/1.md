What is the domain of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{9-x^2}}$?

- $(-3,3)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-3,3\}$
- $(-\infty,-3)\cup(3,+\infty)$
- $[-3,3]$

## Explanation

The square root is in the denominator, so its radicand must be strictly positive. The condition $9-x^2>0$ is equivalent to $|x|<3$, with both endpoints excluded.
