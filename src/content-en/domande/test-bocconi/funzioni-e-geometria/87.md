Two sides of a triangular sail are $6$ and $10$ metres long and enclose an angle of $30^\circ$. How much fabric is needed for one face, ignoring hems?

- $30\,\mathrm m^2$
- $15\sqrt3\,\mathrm m^2$
- $15\,\mathrm m^2$
- $60\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

Using the $10$ metre side as the base, the altitude is the perpendicular component of the other side, $6\sin30^\circ=3$ metres. The area is therefore $\dfrac{10\cdot3}{2}=15$ square metres.
