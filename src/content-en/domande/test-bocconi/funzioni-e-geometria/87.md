On the interval $[0,4\pi)$, at how many points does the function $\sin x$ take the value $\dfrac13$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

Let $\alpha=\arcsin\left(\dfrac13\right)$, which is an angle between $0$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. On $[0,2\pi)$ the sine equals $\dfrac13$ at $\alpha$ and at $\pi-\alpha$, which are distinct because $\alpha\neq\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The sine has period $2\pi$, so on $[2\pi,4\pi)$ the solutions are $\alpha+2\pi$ and $3\pi-\alpha$. There are therefore $4$ solutions.
