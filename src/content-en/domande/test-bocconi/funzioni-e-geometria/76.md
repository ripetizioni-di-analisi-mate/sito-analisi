Three consecutive vertices of a parallelogram are $A=(1,2)$, $B=(4,3)$ and $C=(6,7)$. Where is the fourth vertex $D$?

- $(3,6)$
- $(9,8)$
- $(5,5)$
- $(-1,-2)$

## Explanation

In a parallelogram the displacement from $A$ to $D$ equals that from $B$ to $C$. The latter is $(6-4,7-3)=(2,4)$. Adding it to $A$ gives $(1+2,2+4)=(3,6)$.
