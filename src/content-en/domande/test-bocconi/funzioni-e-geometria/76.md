On the segment with endpoints $A=(-2,1)$ and $B=(10,7)$, the point $P$ is chosen so that $AP=\dfrac13AB$. What are the coordinates of $P$?

- $(4,4)$
- $(6,5)$
- $\left(\dfrac83,\dfrac83\right)$
- $(2,3)$

## Explanation

The displacement from $A$ to $B$ has components $10-(-2)=12$ and $7-1=6$. The point $P$ is obtained from $A$ by one third of this displacement, that is, by components $4$ and $2$. Hence $P=(-2+4,\,1+2)=(2,3)$. The point $(4,4)$ is instead the midpoint, which corresponds to half the displacement.
