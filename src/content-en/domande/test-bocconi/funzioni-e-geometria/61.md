Which real numbers $x$ satisfy $x^{-2}>4$?

- $-\dfrac12<x<\dfrac12$
- $x<-\dfrac12$ or $x>\dfrac12$
- $-2<x<2$ with $x\neq 0$
- $-\dfrac12<x<\dfrac12$ with $x\neq 0$

## Explanation

The power $x^{-2}=\dfrac{1}{x^2}$ is defined for $x\neq 0$. For these values $x^2>0$, so multiplying by $x^2$ turns the inequality $\dfrac{1}{x^2}>4$ into $1>4x^2$. It follows that $x^2<\dfrac14$, that is, $|x|<\dfrac12$. Excluding $x=0$, where the power is not defined, gives $-\dfrac12<x<\dfrac12$ with $x\neq 0$.
