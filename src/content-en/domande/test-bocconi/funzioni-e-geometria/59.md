The graph in the figure has its vertex at $(1,-2)$ and passes through $(0,0)$ and $(2,0)$. Which equation does it correspond to?

::grafico{f="2*abs(x-1)-2" xmin=-1.5 xmax=3.5 ymin=-3 ymax=3.5 punti="0,0,pieno;1,-2,pieno;2,0,pieno" legenda=0 titolo="A V-shaped graph with vertex $(1,-2)$ meeting the $x$-axis at $(0,0)$ and $(2,0)$."}

- $y=2|x-1|-2$
- $y=|x-1|-2$
- $y=2|x+1|-2$
- $y=|2x-1|-2$

## Explanation

The vertex at $(1,-2)$ indicates a curve of the form $y=a|x-1|-2$, obtained from $y=a|x|$ by a translation. Passing through $(0,0)$ gives $0=a\cdot|0-1|-2$, so $a=2$. The curve $y=2|x-1|-2$ also passes through $(2,0)$, because $2\cdot|2-1|-2=0$. The curve $y=|x-1|-2$ has the same vertex, but at $x=0$ it equals $-1$.
