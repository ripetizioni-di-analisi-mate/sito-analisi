Two positive intensities differ by $2$ units. Their base-$2$ logarithms sum to $3$. What is the higher intensity?

- $4$
- $8$
- $2$
- $6$

## Explanation

The logarithm of the product is $3$, so the product is $2^3=8$. Calling the lower intensity $u$ gives $u(u+2)=8$, or $(u-2)(u+4)=0$. Positivity requires $u=2$, and the higher intensity is $u+2=4$.
