A scale is defined by $S(x)=\log_b x$, with $b>0$ and $b\ne1$. A sample of value $9$ gives a reading of $-2$. What base does the scale use?

- $\dfrac19$
- $3$
- $-3$
- $\dfrac13$

## Explanation

The reading means $b^{-2}=9$. Taking reciprocals gives $b^2=\dfrac{1}{9}$. A logarithm base is positive, so of the two algebraic roots only $b=\dfrac{1}{3}$ remains.
