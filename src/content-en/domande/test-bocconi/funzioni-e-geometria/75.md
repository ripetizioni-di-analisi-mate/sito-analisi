What is the equation of the line through $(1,2)$ and $(3,8)$?

- $y=3x+2$
- $y=3x-1$
- $y=\dfrac13x+\dfrac53$
- $y=-3x+5$

## Explanation

The slope is $m=\dfrac{8-2}{3-1}=3$. The line through $(1,2)$ with slope $3$ is $y-2=3(x-1)$, so $y=3x-1$. Substituting $x=3$ gives $y=8$, therefore the second point also lies on the line.
