In a right-triangular roof section, the altitude from the right angle divides the hypotenuse into lengths of $4$ and $9$ metres. What is the altitude?

- $5\,\mathrm m$
- $6.5\,\mathrm m$
- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $\sqrt{97}\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The altitude forms two similar right triangles. Writing it as $h$, the corresponding sides give $\dfrac{4}{h}=\dfrac{h}{9}$. Thus $h^2=36$ and its positive length is $6$ metres.
