With $f(x)=2x-1$ and $g(x)=x^2+1$, which expression is obtained for $(g\circ f)(x)$?

- $2x^2+1$
- $4x^2+2$
- $4x^2-4x+2$
- $4x^2-4x$

## Explanation

In the composition $g\circ f$, first $f$ is applied and then $g$ is applied to the result, so $(g\circ f)(x)=(2x-1)^2+1$. Expanding the square gives $4x^2-4x+1+1=4x^2-4x+2$. The expression $2x^2+1$ corresponds to the opposite order, namely $f(g(x))=2(x^2+1)-1$.
