Received intensity strictly decreases as distance from a transmitter increases. The displayed score strictly decreases as intensity increases. How does the score vary with distance?

- It strictly decreases.
- It alternates between increasing and decreasing.
- It strictly increases.
- It stays constant.

## Explanation

Take two distances in increasing order. The second gives a lower intensity, and the display assigns a higher score to a lower intensity. The two reversals of order make the composite function increasing.
