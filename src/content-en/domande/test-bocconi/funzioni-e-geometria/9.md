What is the range of $f(x)=3-2x$ on the domain $[-2,4)$?

- $[-5,7)$
- $(-5,7)$
- $(-5,7]$
- $[-2,4)$

## Explanation

The negative slope makes the function decreasing. Its maximum $7$ is attained at $x=-2$. The input $4$ is excluded, so the corresponding output $-5$ is excluded.
