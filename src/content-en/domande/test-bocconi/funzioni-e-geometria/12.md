What is the area of the sector of a circle of radius $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ with central angle $80^\circ$?

- $4\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $72\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $36\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $18\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The angle is the fraction $\dfrac{80}{360}=\dfrac29$ of a full turn. The sector takes up the same fraction of the disk's area, so its area is $\dfrac29\cdot81\pi=18\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2$.
