The curve in the figure has equation $y=k\cdot a^x$, with $k>0$ and $a>0$, and passes through the points $(0,3)$ and $(1,6)$. What is the value of $y$ at $x=3$?

::grafico{f="3*2^x" xmin=-2.5 xmax=2 ymin=-0.8 ymax=13 punti="0,3,pieno;1,6,pieno" legenda=0 titolo="The curve passes through $(0,3)$ and $(1,6)$."}

- $18$
- $24$
- $12$
- $27$

## Explanation

At $x=0$ we have $a^0=1$, so the $y$-value $3$ gives $k=3$. At $x=1$ we have $3a=6$, therefore $a=2$. The equation of the curve is $y=3\cdot 2^x$, and at $x=3$ we get $y=3\cdot 8=24$.
