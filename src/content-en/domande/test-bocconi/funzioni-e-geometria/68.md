A convex quadrilateral has all four sides tangent to the same inscribed circle. Three consecutive sides measure $9$, $12$ and $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. How long is the fourth side?

- $7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $11\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $13\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $19\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The two tangent segments from each vertex to the circle have equal lengths. Either pair of opposite sides therefore counts one tangent length from each of the four vertices. If the fourth side is $x$ centimetres long, then $9+10=12+x$, giving $x=7$.
