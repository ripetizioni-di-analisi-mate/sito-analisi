- $4\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

Each quarter circle has a central angle of $90^\circ$, so it covers $\dfrac14$ of a circle of radius $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. The radius is half the side, therefore two quarter circles centered at consecutive vertices touch only at the midpoint of the side and do not overlap. Together the four quarter circles have the area of a whole circle, that is, $\pi\cdot 2^2=4\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. Hence the remaining area is $(16-4\pi)\,\mathrm{cm}^2$.
