A courier starts at $A=(0,3)$, must touch the road $y=0$, and then reach $B=(8,3)$. Straight travel is possible between stops. What is the shortest total length?

- $8$
- $14$
- $10$
- $11$

## Explanation

Reflect $B$ in the road to obtain $B'=(8,-3)$. From any point on the road, $B$ and $B'$ are equally far away. The straight segment $AB'$ crosses the road and attains the minimum, with length $\sqrt{8^2+6^2}=10$.
