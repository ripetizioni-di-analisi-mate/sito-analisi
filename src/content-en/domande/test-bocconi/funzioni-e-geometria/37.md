What is the area of the triangle bounded by the coordinate axes and the line $2x+3y=12$?

- $24$
- $6$
- $10$
- $12$

## Explanation

Setting $y=0$ gives the intercept $(6,0)$, and setting $x=0$ gives $(0,4)$. These points and the origin form a right triangle with area $\dfrac{6\cdot4}{2}=12$.
