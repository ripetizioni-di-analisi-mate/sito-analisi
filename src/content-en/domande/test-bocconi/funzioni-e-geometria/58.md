Three consecutive readings at equally spaced times are $12$, $18$ and $27$. Which rule continues to preserve the same ratio between consecutive readings?

- Multiply by $\dfrac{2}{3}$ at each step.
- Multiply by $\dfrac{3}{2}$ at each step.
- Add $9$ at each step.
- Add $6$ at each step.

## Explanation

The ratios are $\dfrac{18}{12}=\dfrac{3}{2}$ and $\dfrac{27}{18}=\dfrac{3}{2}$. Multiplication by that factor preserves the ratio at each step. The additive increases, by contrast, change from $6$ to $9$.
