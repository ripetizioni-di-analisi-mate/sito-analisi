A circular flower bed of radius $3\,\mathrm m$ is surrounded by a path of uniform width. The path alone has area $16\pi\,\mathrm m^2$. How wide is the path?

- $5\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $4\,\mathrm m$
- $8\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

With outer radius $R$, the path has area $\pi(R^2-9)$. Setting this to $16\pi$ gives $R^2=25$, hence $R=5$. The width is the difference of the radii, $5-3=2$ metres.
