A diagonal divides a trapezoid with bases of lengths $11\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ into two triangles. What is the difference between their areas?

- $24\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $32\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

Each triangle uses one of the trapezoid's bases. Both have height $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ because the bases are parallel. Their area difference is $\dfrac{(11-5)\cdot 4}{2}=12\,\mathrm{cm}^2$.
