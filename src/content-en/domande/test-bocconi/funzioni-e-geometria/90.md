Which expression is equal to $(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^2-\sin(2\theta)$ for every $\theta$?

- $1$
- $0$
- $\cos(2\theta)$
- $1-2\sin(2\theta)$

## Explanation

Expanding the square gives $\sin^2\theta+2\sin\theta\cos\theta+\cos^2\theta$. By the Pythagorean identity $\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$, and by the double-angle formula $2\sin\theta\cos\theta=\sin(2\theta)$. Hence the square equals $1+\sin(2\theta)$ and the expression equals $1$ for every $\theta$.
