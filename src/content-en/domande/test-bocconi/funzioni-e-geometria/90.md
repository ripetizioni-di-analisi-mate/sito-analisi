A graph shows tap flow in litres per minute against time. In the first $4$ minutes the flow rises linearly from $0$ to $6$, then stays at $6$ for another $2$ minutes. How many litres are delivered?

- $24\,\mathrm L$
- $36\,\mathrm L$
- $18\,\mathrm L$
- $30\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

Volume is the area under the flow-versus-time graph. The rising part forms a triangle of area $\dfrac{4\cdot6}{2}=12$ litres. The constant part forms a rectangle of area $2\cdot6=12$ litres, giving $24$ litres in all.
