Each side of a rectangle is increased by $50\%$. By what percentage does its area increase?

- $50\%$
- $125\%$
- $100\%$
- $225\%$

## Explanation

Each length is multiplied by $\dfrac32$. The product of the side lengths is therefore multiplied by $\dfrac94$. The increase over the original area is $\dfrac94-1=\dfrac54$, or $125\%$.
