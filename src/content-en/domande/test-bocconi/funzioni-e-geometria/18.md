Let $f(u)=\sqrt{2-u}$ and $g(x)=(x-1)^2$. What is the domain of $f\circ g$?

- $[1-\sqrt2,1+\sqrt2]$
- $[-\sqrt2,\sqrt2]$
- $(-\infty,2]$
- $(1-\sqrt2,1+\sqrt2)$

## Explanation

For the composition, require $2-(x-1)^2\geq 0$. This gives $|x-1|\leq\sqrt2$. The endpoints are allowed because the square root equals zero there.
