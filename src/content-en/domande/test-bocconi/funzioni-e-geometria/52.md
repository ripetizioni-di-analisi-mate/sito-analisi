A service assigns the integer $n$ to every input in $[n,n+1)$, for any integer $n$. What can be inferred from the output $-2$ alone?

- $x=-2$
- $-3<x\le-2$
- $-2<x\le-1$
- $-2\le x<-1$

## Explanation

The output gives the interval label, so substitute $n=-2$ into $[n,n+1)$. This gives $[-2,-1)$, including its left endpoint. All points in that interval produce the same output.
