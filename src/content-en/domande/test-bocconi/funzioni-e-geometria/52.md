The function $f(x)=x+5$, composed with a function $g$, gives $(f\circ g)(x)=x^2+2$ for every $x$. What is the formula of $g$?

- $g(x)=x^2+7$
- $g(x)=x^2-3$
- $g(x)=(x-5)^2+2$
- $g(x)=\dfrac{x^2+2}{x+5}$

## Explanation

By definition $(f\circ g)(x)=f(g(x))=g(x)+5$, because $f$ adds $5$ to its input. The equality $g(x)+5=x^2+2$ therefore gives $g(x)=x^2-3$. Indeed $f(x^2-3)=x^2-3+5=x^2+2$. The formula $(x-5)^2+2$ would instead solve $g(f(x))=x^2+2$.
