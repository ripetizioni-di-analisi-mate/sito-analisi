A chord of a circle of radius $13\,\mathrm{cm}$ is $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center. What is its length?

- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $26\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The perpendicular from the center bisects the chord. The radius is the hypotenuse and the distance from the center is one leg, so half the chord is $\sqrt{169-25}=12\,\mathrm{cm}$. The full length is $24\,\mathrm{cm}$.
