Which set of points can be the graph of a function?

- $\{(2,1),(3,2),(2,3)\}$
- $\{(-1,0),(0,-1),(-1,1)\}$
- $\{(-2,4),(0,4),(1,-1)\}$
- $\{(0,2),(0,3),(1,2)\}$

## Explanation

A graph assigns a unique $y$-coordinate to each $x$-coordinate. The inputs $-2,0,1$ are distinct. The inputs $-2$ and $0$ are allowed to produce the same output $4$.
