A ramp $25\,\mathrm{m}$ long rises by $7\,\mathrm{m}$. What angle does it make with the horizontal?

- $\arcsin\left(\dfrac{24}{25}\right)$
- $\arcsin\left(\dfrac{7}{25}\right)$
- $\arctan\left(\dfrac{7}{25}\right)$
- $\arccos\left(\dfrac{7}{25}\right)$

## Explanation

The rise is the leg opposite the angle and the ramp is the hypotenuse. Their ratio is therefore the sine of the acute angle. Taking the inverse sine gives $\arcsin\left(\dfrac{7}{25}\right)$.
