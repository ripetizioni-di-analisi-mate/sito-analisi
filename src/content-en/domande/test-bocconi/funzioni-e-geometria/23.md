For which inputs does the graph of $y=x^3$ lie strictly above the graph of $y=x^2$?

- $x<0$
- $0<x<1$
- $x>1$
- $x\geq 1$

## Explanation

The difference of the outputs is $x^3-x^2=x^2(x-1)$. The factor $x^2$ is positive for $x\neq 0$. The difference is therefore positive when $x>1$, while $x=0$ and $x=1$ give equality.
