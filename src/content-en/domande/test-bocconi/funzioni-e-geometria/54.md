A vehicle travels $6\,\mathrm{km}$ east from a depot and then backtracks $2\,\mathrm{km}$. A graph shows distance from the depot against total kilometres travelled. What is the slope of the second part of the graph?

- $-1$
- $1$
- $0$
- $-\dfrac13$

## Explanation

At the turning point the graph is at $(6,6)$. By the end the vehicle has travelled $8$ kilometres but is only $4$ from the depot, giving $(8,4)$. The slope is $\dfrac{4-6}{8-6}=-1$.
