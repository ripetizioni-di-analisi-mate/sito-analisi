An entrance is at $(4,0)$ and a main corridor follows the line $y=x$. Where should the shortest straight link from the entrance meet the corridor?

- $(1,1)$
- $(0,0)$
- $(4,4)$
- $(2,2)$

## Explanation

The shortest route is perpendicular to $y=x$, so through the given point it has slope $-1$ and equation $y=4-x$. At the intersection $x=4-x$, giving $x=y=2$.
