For which value of $a$ do the points $(1,1)$, $(3,5)$ and $(a,9)$ lie on the same line?

- $17$
- $4$
- $5$
- $9$

## Explanation

The line through $(1,1)$ and $(3,5)$ has slope $\dfrac{5-1}{3-1}=2$, so its equation is $y-1=2(x-1)$, that is, $y=2x-1$. The point $(a,9)$ lies on this line if $9=2a-1$, therefore $a=5$.
