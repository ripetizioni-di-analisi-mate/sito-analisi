A rectangle has perimeter $20\,\mathrm{cm}$. If one side is $x\,\mathrm{cm}$ long, the area is $A(x)=x(10-x)$. What is the domain of $A$ as a model of the rectangle?

- $\mathbb R$
- $[0,10]$
- $(0,+\infty)$
- $(0,10)$

## Explanation

The other side is $10-x$ long, because the sum of two adjacent sides is half the perimeter. Both sides must be positive, so
$$
\begin{cases}
x>0\\
10-x>0
\end{cases}
$$
It follows that $0<x<10$. The polynomial $x(10-x)$ is defined on all of $\mathbb R$, but the model describes a rectangle only for $x\in(0,10)$.
