What is $\arcsin\left(\sin\dfrac{5\pi}{6}\right)$?

- $-\dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$

## Explanation

The sine of $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$ is $\dfrac12$. The inverse sine returns the angle in $\left[-\dfrac{\pi}{2},\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$ with that sine, namely $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$.
