The mass of a sample after $t$ hours is $64\cdot 2^{-\frac{t}{3}}\,\mathrm{g}$. After how many hours is it $16\,\mathrm{g}$?

- $6$
- $3$
- $12$
- $2$

## Explanation

The ratio of final mass to initial mass is $\dfrac{16}{64}=\dfrac14=2^{-2}$. Thus $-\dfrac{t}{3}=-2$ and the time is $6$ hours.
