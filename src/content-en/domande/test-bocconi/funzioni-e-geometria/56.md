Which $x$ satisfy the inequality $|2x-3|\leq 5$?

- $[-4,1]$
- $[-1,4]$
- $(-1,4)$
- $(-\infty,-1]\cup[4,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The inequality $|u|\leq 5$ is equivalent to $-5\leq u\leq 5$, so $-5\leq 2x-3\leq 5$. Adding $3$ to every part gives $-2\leq 2x\leq 8$. Dividing by $2$ yields $-1\leq x\leq 4$. The endpoints are included because at them $|2x-3|=5$.
