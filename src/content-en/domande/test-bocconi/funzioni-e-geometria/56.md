A meter uses the scale $L=10\log_{10}(\dfrac{I}{I_0})$. One source reads $30$ units higher than another. What is the ratio of the greater intensity to the smaller?

- $3$
- $30$
- $100$
- $1000$

## Explanation

Subtracting the two readings cancels the reference $I_0$ and gives $30=10\log_{10}(\dfrac{I_2}{I_1})$. The logarithm of the ratio is $3$. The intensity ratio is therefore $10^3=1000$.
