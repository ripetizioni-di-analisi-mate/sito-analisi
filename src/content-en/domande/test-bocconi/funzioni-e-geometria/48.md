A device updates a reading by the rule $f(x)=6-x$. It starts at $x=2$. What does it read after exactly $101$ updates?

- $0$
- $2$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

The first update gives $6-2=4$, and the second returns to $6-4=2$. The two readings then repeat. As $101$ is odd, the reading matches the first update, namely $4$.
