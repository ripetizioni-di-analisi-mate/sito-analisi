What is the perimeter of a semicircle of radius $4\,\mathrm{cm}$, including the straight diameter?

- $4\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(8\pi+8)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(4\pi+8)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(4\pi+4)\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The arc is half of $2\pi\cdot4$, or $4\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$. The boundary also includes the diameter of length $8\,\mathrm{cm}$, giving the total.
