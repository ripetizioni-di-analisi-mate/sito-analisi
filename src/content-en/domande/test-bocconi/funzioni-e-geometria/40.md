On a map with the $x$-axis pointing right and the $y$-axis pointing up, point $P=(4,3)$ rotates $90^\circ$ anticlockwise about the pivot $C=(1,2)$. What are the new coordinates of $P$?

- $(-3,4)$
- $(2,-1)$
- $(4,1)$
- $(0,5)$

## Explanation

The vector from the pivot to the point is $(4-1,3-2)=(3,1)$. An anticlockwise quarter turn takes $(u,v)$ to $(-v,u)$, giving a new vector of $(-1,3)$. Adding the pivot coordinates gives $(1-1,2+3)=(0,5)$.
