A robot follows the boundary of a convex regular polygon and turns $24^\circ$ in the same direction at every vertex. How many sides does it traverse in one circuit?

- $12$
- $24$
- $7.5$
- $15$

## Explanation

During one circuit the robot turns through a total of $360^\circ$. Each turn is an exterior angle of $24^\circ$. The number of turns, equal to the number of sides, is $\dfrac{360}{24}=15$.
