A parabola with a vertical axis has vertex $(1,-4)$ and passes through $(3,4)$. What is its equation?

- $y=x^2-2x-3$
- $y=2x^2-4x-2$
- $y=2x^2+4x-2$
- $y=4x^2-8x$

## Explanation

A parabola with vertex $(1,-4)$ can be written in the form $y=a(x-1)^2-4$. Passing through $(3,4)$ gives $4=a\cdot 2^2-4$, so $4a=8$ and $a=2$. Expanding $2(x-1)^2-4$ gives $y=2x^2-4x-2$.
