A walkway rises $3$ metres for every $4$ metres of horizontal run. A redesign doubles the rise while leaving the run unchanged. If $\theta$ is the new angle with the ground, what is $\tan\theta$?

- $\dfrac65$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac32$

## Explanation

The tangent of the angle to the horizontal is the rise divided by the horizontal run. The new rise is $6$ metres and the run remains $4$. Thus $\tan\theta=\dfrac{6}{4}=\dfrac{3}{2}$.
