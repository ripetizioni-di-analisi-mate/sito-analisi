The line $y=x+1$ divides a map into two zones. A route is the straight segment between two stops. Which route crosses the line at a point strictly inside its segment?

- From $(0,0)$ to $(2,2)$.
- From $(0,3)$ to $(2,1)$.
- From $(0,2)$ to $(2,4)$.
- From $(0,1)$ to $(2,2)$.

## Explanation

The quantity $y-x-1$ is positive above the line and negative below it. Between $(0,3)$ and $(2,1)$ it goes from $2$ to $-2$, so it vanishes at an interior point. For the other segments the endpoint values have the same sign, or one is zero at the endpoint itself.
