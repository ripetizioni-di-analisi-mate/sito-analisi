The line $y=mx+2$ passes through the midpoint of the segment with endpoints $(1,4)$ and $(5,8)$. What is the value of $m$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac43$
- $2$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

The midpoint has coordinates $\left(\dfrac{1+5}{2},\dfrac{4+8}{2}\right)=(3,6)$. The line passes through this point if $6=3m+2$, so $3m=4$ and $m=\dfrac43$. The value $1$ is the slope of the segment, which plays no role here.
