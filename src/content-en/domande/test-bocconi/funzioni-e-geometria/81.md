At which points does the line $y=x+1$ meet the circle $x^2+y^2=25$?

- $(4,5)$ and $(-3,-2)$
- Only at $(3,4)$
- $(3,4)$ and $(-4,-3)$
- $(-3,4)$ and $(4,-3)$

## Explanation

Substituting $y=x+1$ into the equation of the circle gives $x^2+(x+1)^2=25$, that is, $2x^2+2x-24=0$. Dividing by $2$ gives $x^2+x-12=0$, which factors as $(x+4)(x-3)=0$. The $x$-coordinates are therefore $3$ and $-4$, and the line assigns them the $y$-coordinates $4$ and $-3$.
