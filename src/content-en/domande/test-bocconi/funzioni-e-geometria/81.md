A scaled copy of the parabola $y=x^2$ is obtained by doubling both coordinates of every point. Which equation describes the copy in the new coordinates $(X,Y)$?

- $Y=X^2$
- $Y=2X^2$
- $Y=\dfrac{X^2}{2}$
- $Y=4X^2$

## Explanation

The new coordinates are $X=2x$ and $Y=2y$. To express $Y$ in terms of $X$, substitute $x=\dfrac{X}{2}$ into $Y=2x^2$. This gives $Y=2(\dfrac{X}{2})^2=\dfrac{X^2}{2}$.
