An angle $\theta$ lies in the fourth quadrant and satisfies $\cos\theta=\dfrac{8}{17}$. What is $\sin\theta$?

- $-\dfrac{8}{17}$
- $\dfrac{15}{17}$
- $-\dfrac{9}{17}$
- $-\dfrac{15}{17}$

## Explanation

By the Pythagorean identity, $\sin^2\theta=1-\dfrac{64}{289}=\dfrac{225}{289}$. Sine is negative in the fourth quadrant, so it equals $-\dfrac{15}{17}$.
