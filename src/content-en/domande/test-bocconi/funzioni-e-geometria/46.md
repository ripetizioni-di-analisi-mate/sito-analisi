A scale gives an affine response. It reads $7$ units at $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $16$ units at $5\,\mathrm{kg}$. What should it read at $4\,\mathrm{kg}$?

- $15$
- $13$
- $14$
- $12$

## Explanation

An increase of $3$ kilograms raises the reading by $9$ units. The slope is therefore $3$ units per kilogram. Moving from $2$ to $4$ kilograms adds $2\cdot3=6$ units to the original $7$.
