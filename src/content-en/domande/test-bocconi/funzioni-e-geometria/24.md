Given $f(x)=x^{-3}$ for $x>0$, by what factor is the value of $f$ multiplied when the input changes from $x$ to $3x$?

- $\dfrac19$
- $\dfrac{1}{27}$
- $27$
- $\dfrac13$

## Explanation

The exponent law gives $(3x)^{-3}=3^{-3}x^{-3}$. The factor $3^{-3}$ equals $\dfrac{1}{27}$.
