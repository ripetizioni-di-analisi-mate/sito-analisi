The figure shows the graph of the parabola $y=ax^2+bx+c$. Which statement about the coefficients is true?

::grafico{f="-x^2-2*x+2" xmin=-4 xmax=2.5 ymin=-3 ymax=4.2 legenda=0 titolo="A parabola with a vertical axis."}

- $a>0$ and $c>0$
- $a<0$ and $c<0$
- $a>0$ and $c<0$
- $a<0$ and $c>0$

## Explanation

The vertex is the highest point of the curve, so it is a maximum point and therefore $a<0$. For $x=0$ we have $y=c$, so the parabola crosses the vertical axis at the point $(0,c)$. In the figure this point lies above the horizontal axis, therefore $c>0$.
