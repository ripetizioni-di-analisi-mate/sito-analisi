At 2:40 the hands of an analogue clock move continuously, each at a constant speed. What is the smaller angle between them?

- $140^\circ$
- $160^\circ$
- $180^\circ$
- $200^\circ$

## Explanation

The minute hand travels $6^\circ$ per minute, reaching $40\cdot6^\circ=240^\circ$ clockwise from twelve. The hour hand starts at $60^\circ$ at two o’clock and advances by $\dfrac{40}{60}\cdot30^\circ=20^\circ$. Their angular separation is $240^\circ-80^\circ=160^\circ$, less than half a turn.
