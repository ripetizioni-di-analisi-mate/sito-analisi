Two circles have radii of $6$ and $2$ centimetres. Their centres are $4$ centimetres apart. How do their boundaries meet?

- They intersect at two points.
- They are internally tangent.
- They are concentric.
- They are externally tangent.

## Explanation

The centre distance equals the difference of the radii, $6-2=4$. The point of the smaller circle farthest from the larger centre is $4+2=6$ away and lies on both boundaries. The smaller disc lies inside the larger one and there is just one point of contact.
