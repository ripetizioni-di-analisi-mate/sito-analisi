A graph of $f$ has just three zeros, $-3$, $0$ and $2$. What are all the zeros of the folded signal $g(x)=f(\lvert x\rvert)$?

- $\{-2,0,2\}$
- $\{-3,0,2\}$
- $\{0,2\}$
- $\{-3,-2,0,2,3\}$

## Explanation

The input to $f$ is $\lvert x\rvert$, which can only be non-negative. Of the original zeros, only $0$ and $2$ are possible inputs. The first gives $x=0$, and the second gives $x=-2$ or $x=2$.
