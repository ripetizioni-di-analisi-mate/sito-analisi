A parabolic arch meets the ground at horizontal positions $0$ and $6$ metres. Halfway across, at $x=3$, it is $9$ metres high. How high is it at $x=1$?

- $3\,\mathrm m$
- $8\,\mathrm m$
- $5\,\mathrm m$
- $6\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The zeros let us write the height as $h(x)=a x(6-x)$. The midpoint measurement gives $9=9a$, hence $a=1$. One metre from the end, $h(1)=1\cdot5=5$ metres.
