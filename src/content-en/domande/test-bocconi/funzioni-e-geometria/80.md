For which values of $k$ does the line $y=k$ have no points in common with the parabola $y=x^2-4x+1$?

- $k>-3$
- $k\leq -3$
- $k<1$
- $k<-3$

## Explanation

Completing the square gives $x^2-4x+1=(x-2)^2-3$. The parabola therefore has vertex $(2,-3)$ and takes exactly the values greater than or equal to $-3$. The horizontal line $y=k$ meets it if and only if $k\geq -3$, and for $k=-3$ it is tangent at the vertex. Hence there are no common points for $k<-3$.
