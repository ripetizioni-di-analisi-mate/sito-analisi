What is the range of $g(x)=2-\sqrt{x+1}$ when $x\in[-1,8]$?

- $(-\infty,2]$
- $[0,3]$
- $[2,5]$
- $[-1,2]$

## Explanation

On this interval $x+1$ ranges from $0$ to $9$, so the square root takes every value from $0$ to $3$. Subtracting these values from $2$ gives all outputs from $-1$ to $2$.
