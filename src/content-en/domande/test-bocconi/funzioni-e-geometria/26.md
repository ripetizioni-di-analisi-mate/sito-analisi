At which $x$-coordinates does the curve $y=(x+1)^4+2$ meet the line $y=18$?

- $-5$ and $3$
- $-3$ and $1$
- $1$ only
- $-1$ and $3$

## Explanation

At an intersection, $(x+1)^4=16=2^4$. Since the exponent $4$ is even, $x+1$ can be $-2$ or $2$. The two $x$-coordinates are therefore $-3$ and $1$.
