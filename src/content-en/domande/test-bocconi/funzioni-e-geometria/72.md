A vertical pole $1.5\,\mathrm m$ tall casts a $2\,\mathrm m$ shadow. At the same time on the same level ground, a vertical tree's shadow is $3\,\mathrm m$ longer than its height. How tall is the tree?

- $4\,\mathrm m$
- $12\,\mathrm m$
- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $9\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The sun's rays form similar triangles, so the height-to-shadow ratio is $\dfrac{1.5}{2}=\dfrac{3}{4}$. For the tree of height $h$ this gives $\dfrac{h}{h+3}=\dfrac{3}{4}$. Solving $4h=3h+9$ yields $h=9$.
