What is the area of an equilateral triangle with side $6\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $18\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $18\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $6\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $9\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The height divides the triangle into two right triangles with hypotenuse $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and one leg of $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. By the Pythagorean theorem the height is $\sqrt{36-9}=\sqrt{27}=3\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}$. The area is therefore $\dfrac{6\cdot 3\sqrt3}{2}=9\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}^2$.
