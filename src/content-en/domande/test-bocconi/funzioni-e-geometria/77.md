Which statement is true for the triangle with vertices $A=(0,0)$, $B=(5,0)$ and $C=(1,2)$?

- The triangle has a right angle at $C$.
- The triangle has a right angle at $A$.
- The triangle has a right angle at $B$.
- The triangle is not a right triangle.

## Explanation

The side $AC$ has slope $\dfrac{2-0}{1-0}=2$ and the side $BC$ has slope $\dfrac{2-0}{1-5}=-\dfrac12$. The product of the two slopes is $-1$, so $AC$ and $BC$ are perpendicular and the right angle is at $C$. The side $AB$ is horizontal, while $AC$ and $BC$ are not vertical, therefore the angles at $A$ and at $B$ are not right angles.
