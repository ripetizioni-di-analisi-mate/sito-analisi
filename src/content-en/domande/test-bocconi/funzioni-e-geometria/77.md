A stop must be placed on the $x$-axis at the same distance from offices $A=(0,2)$ and $B=(4,4)$. What is the stop's $x$-coordinate?

- $\dfrac72$
- $\dfrac52$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

The stop is $(x,0)$. Equating the squared distances gives $x^2+4=(x-4)^2+16$. Expanding yields $4=-8x+32$, so $x=\dfrac{28}{8}=\dfrac{7}{2}$.
