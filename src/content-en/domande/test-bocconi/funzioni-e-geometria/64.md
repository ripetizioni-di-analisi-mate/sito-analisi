How many real solutions does the equation $\log_2\left(x^2\right)=4$ have?

- $1$
- $2$
- $0$
- $4$

## Explanation

The argument $x^2$ is positive for every $x\neq 0$, so the domain is $\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$. In exponential form the equation becomes $x^2=2^4=16$, hence $x=4$ or $x=-4$. Both values belong to the domain, therefore there are $2$ solutions. The rewriting $2\log_2 x=4$ holds only for $x>0$ and would lose the solution $x=-4$.
