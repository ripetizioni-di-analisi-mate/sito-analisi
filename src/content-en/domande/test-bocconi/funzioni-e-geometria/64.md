A rectangular notch $4$ centimetres wide and $2$ deep is cut into the middle of a long side of a $10$ by $6$ centimetre card. What is the perimeter of the remaining card?

- $28\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $32\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $36\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The original perimeter is $2(10+6)=32$ centimetres. The notch removes a segment of length $4$ and adds one of the same length at its base. Its two sides add $2$ centimetres each, bringing the total to $36$.
