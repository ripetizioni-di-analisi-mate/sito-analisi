For which real numbers $x$ is the distance from $1$ greater than the distance from $-3$, that is, $|x-1|>|x+3|$?

- $x>-1$
- $-3<x<1$
- $x<1$
- $x<-1$

## Explanation

The number $|x-1|$ is the distance of $x$ from $1$ and $|x+3|=|x-(-3)|$ is the distance of $x$ from $-3$. The point equidistant from $-3$ and $1$ is their midpoint $-1$. The points to the left of $-1$ are closer to $-3$ than to $1$, while those to the right of $-1$ are closer to $1$, so the inequality holds exactly for $x<-1$. For example, at $x=-2$ we have $|x-1|=3$ and $|x+3|=1$.
