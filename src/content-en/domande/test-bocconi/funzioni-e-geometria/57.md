An object's temperature is modelled by $T(t)=20+60a^t$. After one hour it is $50$ degrees. What does the model predict after two hours?

- $30$ degrees
- $25$ degrees
- $35$ degrees
- $40$ degrees

## Explanation

The varying part is the excess above $20$ degrees. After one hour it has fallen from $60$ to $30$, so $a=\dfrac{1}{2}$. After two hours the excess is $60(\dfrac{1}{2})^2=15$, to be added to the baseline of $20$.
