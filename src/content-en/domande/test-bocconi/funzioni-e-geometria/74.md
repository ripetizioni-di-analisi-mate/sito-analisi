The sides of a triangle measure $6\,\mathrm{cm}$, $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the height relative to the $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ side?

- $\dfrac{48}{5}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{12}{5}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{24}{5}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Since $6^2+8^2=100=10^2$, the triangle is a right triangle and the $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ side is the hypotenuse. Taking the legs as base and height, the area is $\dfrac{6\cdot 8}{2}=24\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. The same area is obtained with base $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $h$, so $\dfrac{10h}{2}=24$ and $h=\dfrac{24}{5}\,\mathrm{cm}$.
