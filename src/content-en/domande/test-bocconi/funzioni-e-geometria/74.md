The top side of a rectangle $8$ centimetres wide and $3$ high slides horizontally $2$ centimetres to the right. The bottom side stays fixed and the other two sides become slanted. Which quantity stays the same?

- The length of the longer diagonal.
- The area.
- The measure of every interior angle.
- The perimeter.

## Explanation

The new parallelogram keeps its base of $8$ and perpendicular height of $3$, so its area stays at $24$ square centimetres. The slanted sides exceed $3$ centimetres, the angles change and the longer diagonal spans a greater horizontal distance.
