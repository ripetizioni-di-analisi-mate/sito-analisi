A craftsperson wants to join three rods into a triangle with a strictly obtuse interior angle. Which set of lengths in centimetres allows this?

- $3,4,5$
- $3,4,8$
- $4,4,5$
- $4,5,8$

## Explanation

To form a triangle, the longest side must be less than the sum of the other two. For $4,5,8$, we have $8<9$ and also $8^2>4^2+5^2$; the cosine rule makes the angle opposite $8$ obtuse. The other length sets give a right triangle, no triangle and an acute triangle, respectively.
