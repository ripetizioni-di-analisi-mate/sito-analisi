What is the period of the function $y=3\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi x}{2}\right)+1$?

- $\pi$
- $2$
- $4\pi$
- $4$

## Explanation

For $y=A\cos(bx)+c$ the period is $\dfrac{2\pi}{|b|}$. Here $b=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, so the period is $2\pi\cdot\dfrac{2}{\pi}=4$. The coefficients $3$ and $1$ change the amplitude and the vertical position and leave the period unchanged.
