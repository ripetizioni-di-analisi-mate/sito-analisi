The horizontal line $y=k$ must meet the circle $(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2=9$ at just one point and pass above its centre. What is $k$?

- $-4$
- $3$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The circle has centre $(2,-1)$ and radius $3$. Its horizontal tangents pass through its highest and lowest points. The upper one has vertical coordinate $-1+3=2$.
