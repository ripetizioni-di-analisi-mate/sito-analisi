From a point on the ground $30\,\mathrm{m}$ from the base of a building, the top is seen at an angle of $30^\circ$ above the horizontal. How tall is the building?

- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30\sqrt3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\sqrt3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\sqrt3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The height $h$ of the building is the leg opposite the $30^\circ$ angle and the distance of $30\,\mathrm{m}$ is the adjacent leg. The tangent is the ratio of the opposite leg to the adjacent leg, so $h=30\tan 30^\circ$. Since $\tan 30^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}$, we get $h=10\sqrt3\,\mathrm{m}$.
