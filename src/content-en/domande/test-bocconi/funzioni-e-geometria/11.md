What is the domain of $\log_3(5-2x)+\log_3(x+1)$?

- $\left(-1,\dfrac52\right)$
- $\left(-\infty,\dfrac52\right)$
- $\left[-1,\dfrac52\right]$
- $(-1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Both arguments must be positive at the same time. From $5-2x>0$ we get $x<\dfrac52$, while $x+1>0$ gives $x>-1$. The domain is the intersection of these conditions.
