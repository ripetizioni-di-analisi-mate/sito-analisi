For which $x$ does the inequality $3^{x^2-1}<27$ hold?

- $x<2$
- $x<-2$ or $x>2$
- $-2<x<2$
- $-2\leq x\leq 2$

## Explanation

Since $27=3^3$ and the exponential function with base $3$ is increasing, the inequality is equivalent to $x^2-1<3$. Hence $x^2<4$, that is, $|x|<2$. The solutions are therefore $-2<x<2$, with the endpoints excluded because at them the two sides are equal.
