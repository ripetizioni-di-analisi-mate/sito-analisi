A bolt head is a regular hexagon of side $4\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the distance across two opposite parallel sides?

- $2\sqrt3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $4\sqrt3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Joining the centre to the vertices forms six equilateral triangles of side $4$. The distance from the centre to a side is their altitude, $\sqrt{4^2-2^2}=2\sqrt3$. Two such altitudes span the opposite sides.
