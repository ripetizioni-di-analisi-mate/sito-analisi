The point $(2,7)$ lies on the graph of a one-to-one function $f$. Which point certainly lies on the graph of $y=f^{-1}(x)+1$?

- $(2,8)$
- $(7,2)$
- $(7,3)$
- $(8,2)$

## Explanation

Since $f(2)=7$, we have $f^{-1}(7)=2$, so the point $(7,2)$ lies on the graph of the inverse. Adding $1$ to the value of the inverse shifts its graph up by one unit. Therefore at $x=7$ the new function equals $2+1=3$ and the required point is $(7,3)$.
