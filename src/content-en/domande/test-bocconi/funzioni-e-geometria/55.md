A rectangular frame is $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ wide and $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ high, with a border of uniform thickness $x$. For which values of $x$, in centimetres, do both the border and the opening have strictly positive dimensions?

- $0<x<10$
- $0<x<6$
- $0\le x\le6$
- $0<x<12$

## Explanation

The opening has sides $20-2x$ and $12-2x$. Both must be positive, giving $x<10$ and $x<6$. The thickness must also be positive, so the physical domain is $0<x<6$.
