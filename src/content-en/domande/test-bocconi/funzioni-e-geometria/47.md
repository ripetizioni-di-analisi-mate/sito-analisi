A charge in euros is $C(x)=2x+3$ up to and including $x=4$, and $C(x)=x+k$ for $x>4$. The graph must have no jump as $x$ passes $4$. What must $k$ be?

- $3$
- $4$
- $7$
- $11$

## Explanation

At the join the first piece has value $2\cdot4+3=11$. From the right, values approach $4+k$. Matching the two requires $4+k=11$, which gives $k=7$.
