Which values does the function $f(x)=\dfrac{4}{x^2+1}$ take as $x$ ranges over $\mathbb R$?

- $(0,+\infty)$
- $[0,4]$
- $(0,4)$
- $(0,4]$

## Explanation

Since $x^2\geq 0$, the denominator is at least $1$, so $f(x)\leq 4$, with equality at $x=0$. The denominator is positive, therefore $f(x)>0$ for every $x$. Every value $y$ with $0<y\leq 4$ is attained, because the equation $\dfrac{4}{x^2+1}=y$ gives $x^2=\dfrac{4}{y}-1$, which is a nonnegative number. Hence the range is $(0,4]$.
