Which formula gives $f^{-1}(y)$ for $f(x)=3+\log_2(x-1)$, defined for $x>1$?

- $2^{y-3}-1$
- $2^{y+3}+1$
- $2^{y}-2$
- $1+2^{y-3}$

## Explanation

Set $y=3+\log_2(x-1)$ and isolate the logarithm, which gives $\log_2(x-1)=y-3$. By the definition of logarithm this equality is equivalent to $x-1=2^{y-3}$. Hence $x=1+2^{y-3}$. The inverse is defined for every $y\in\mathbb R$, because the range of $f$ is all of $\mathbb R$.
