A price $p$ receives a $20\,\%$ reduction and a fixed $10$ euro surcharge. How many euros dearer is it to apply the surcharge after the reduction instead of before it?

- $10$
- $0$
- $2$
- $8$

## Explanation

With the surcharge last the payment is $0.8p+10$. With it first the payment is $0.8(p+10)=0.8p+8$. The difference is $2$ euros for every starting price.
