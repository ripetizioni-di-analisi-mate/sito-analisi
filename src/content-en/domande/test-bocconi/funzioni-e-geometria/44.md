For which $\theta\in[0,2\pi)$ does $\tan\theta=-1$?

- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$ and $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$ only
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

The reference angle is $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. The quotient of sine and cosine is negative when their signs differ, in the second and fourth quadrants. The required angles are $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$ and $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$.
