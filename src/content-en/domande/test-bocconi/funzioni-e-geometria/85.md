How many degrees is an angle of $\dfrac{7\pi}{12}$ radians?

- $75^\circ$
- $210^\circ$
- $105^\circ$
- $120^\circ$

## Explanation

Since $\pi$ radians correspond to $180^\circ$, one radian corresponds to $\dfrac{180^\circ}{\pi}$. Hence the angle measures $\dfrac{7}{12}\cdot 180^\circ=7\cdot 15^\circ=105^\circ$.
