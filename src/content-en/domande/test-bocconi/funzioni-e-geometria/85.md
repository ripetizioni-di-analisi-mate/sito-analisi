A water level is modelled by $h(t)=4+2\cos(\dfrac{\pi t}{6})$, with $t$ in hours. At what first positive time does it reach its minimum?

- $12\,\mathrm h$
- $6\,\mathrm h$
- $3\,\mathrm h$
- $2\,\mathrm h$

## Explanation

The cosine coefficient is positive, so a minimum requires the cosine to equal $-1$. The first positive argument giving that value is $\pi$. Solving $\dfrac{\pi t}{6}=\pi$ gives $t=6$ hours.
