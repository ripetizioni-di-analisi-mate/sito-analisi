Which statement describes the line $x=0$ relative to the parabola $y=(x-1)^2$?

- It does not meet the parabola.
- It is tangent at $(0,1)$.
- It meets the parabola at $(0,1)$ in a direction different from that of the tangent.
- It passes through the vertex.

## Explanation

Substituting $x=0$ gives the point $(0,1)$. The tangent to the parabola there has finite slope, while $x=0$ is vertical. The line crosses the graph in a direction different from that of the tangent.
