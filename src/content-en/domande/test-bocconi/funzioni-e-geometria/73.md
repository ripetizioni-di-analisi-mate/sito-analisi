A right trapezoid has bases of $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a slanted side of $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its area?

- $56\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $70\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $112\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $42\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The height drawn from an endpoint of the shorter base cuts off a segment of $10-4=6\,\mathrm{cm}$ on the longer base. The slanted side is the hypotenuse of the right triangle whose legs are the height and this segment. By the Pythagorean theorem the height is $\sqrt{100-36}=8\,\mathrm{cm}$. The area is therefore $\dfrac{(10+4)\cdot 8}{2}=56\,\mathrm{cm}^2$.
