Rods of $7$ and $11$ centimetres will form two sides of a non-degenerate triangular frame. The third rod must have a whole-number length in centimetres. How many different lengths are possible?

- $15$
- $14$
- $12$
- $13$

## Explanation

The triangle inequality requires $11-7<x<11+7$, or $4<x<18$. Equality would produce a flat frame. The integers run from $5$ through $17$ inclusive, giving $17-5+1=13$ lengths.
