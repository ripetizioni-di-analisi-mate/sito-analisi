The perimeter of a rectangle is $34\,\mathrm{cm}$ and its diagonal is $13\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. What is the area of the rectangle?

- $30\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $120\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $60\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $\dfrac{169}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

Let $a$ and $b$ be the sides. The perimeter and the Pythagorean theorem applied to the diagonal give
$$
\begin{cases}
a+b=17\\
a^2+b^2=169
\end{cases}
$$
Since $(a+b)^2=a^2+b^2+2ab$, we have $289=169+2ab$, so $ab=60$. The area is therefore $60\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. Indeed, the sides $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ satisfy both conditions.
