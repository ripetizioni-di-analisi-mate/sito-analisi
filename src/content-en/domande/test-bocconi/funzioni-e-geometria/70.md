Two points on a rotating disc are respectively $3$ and $5$ centimetres from the centre. During the same rotation the first travels along an arc of length $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. How far does the second travel?

- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7.2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Both points sweep out the same central angle. Arc length is $r\theta$ for $\theta$ in radians, so the angle is $\dfrac{12}{3}=4$. The second point travels $5\cdot4=20$ centimetres.
