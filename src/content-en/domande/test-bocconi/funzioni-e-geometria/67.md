The minor arc $AB$ of a circle measures $100^\circ$. An observer $C$ lies on this very arc, away from its endpoints. What is the size of angle $ACB$?

- $50^\circ$
- $100^\circ$
- $260^\circ$
- $130^\circ$

## Explanation

An inscribed angle intercepts the arc $AB$ that does not contain its vertex $C$. This is the major arc, measuring $360^\circ-100^\circ=260^\circ$. The angle is half that measure, or $130^\circ$.
