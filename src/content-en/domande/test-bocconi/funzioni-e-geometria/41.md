A circle has a diameter with endpoints $(0,0)$ and $(6,8)$. What is its equation?

- $(x+3)^2+(y+4)^2=25$
- $(x-3)^2+(y-4)^2=25$
- $(x-3)^2+(y-4)^2=100$
- $(x-6)^2+(y-8)^2=25$

## Explanation

The center is the midpoint $(3,4)$. The distance between the endpoints is $\sqrt{36+64}=10$, so the radius is $5$. Its square $25$ appears in the equation.
