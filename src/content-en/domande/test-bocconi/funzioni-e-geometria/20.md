What is the domain of the inverse of $f(x)=2-3x$ restricted to $[-1,4)$?

- $[-10,5)$
- $[-1,4)$
- $(-10,5]$
- $(-\infty,5]$

## Explanation

The inverse's domain is the range of the original function. The line is decreasing, so it attains $5$ at $-1$ and approaches $-10$ as $x$ approaches $4$ from the left. The value $-10$ is excluded.
