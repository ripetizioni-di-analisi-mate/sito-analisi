A probe uses the relation $s=(t-3)^2$ only for $t\le3$. Which formula recovers the time $t$ from any reading $s\ge0$?

- $t=\sqrt{s+3}$
- $t=3+\sqrt s$
- $t=3-\sqrt s$
- $t=3-s^2$

## Explanation

The relation gives $\lvert t-3\rvert=\sqrt s$. On the specified interval $t-3\le0$, so the absolute value equals $3-t$. Solving $3-t=\sqrt s$ gives $t=3-\sqrt s$.
