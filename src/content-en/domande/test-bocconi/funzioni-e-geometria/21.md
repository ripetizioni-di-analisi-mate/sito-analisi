Which formula gives the inverse of $f(x)=\dfrac{x}{x+1}$ on its natural domain?

- $1-\dfrac1y$
- $\dfrac{y}{1-y}$
- $\dfrac{y}{1+y}$
- $\dfrac{1-y}{y}$

## Explanation

Multiplying $y=\dfrac{x}{x+1}$ by $x+1$ gives $yx+y=x$. The output $y=1$ is impossible. For every other $y$, isolating $x$ gives $x=\dfrac{y}{1-y}$, which is always different from $-1$.
