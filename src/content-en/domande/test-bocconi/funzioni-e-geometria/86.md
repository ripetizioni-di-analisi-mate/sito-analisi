An angle $\theta$ has sine equal to $\dfrac35$. What is the value of $\cos(2\theta)$?

- $\dfrac{7}{25}$
- $-\dfrac{7}{25}$
- $\dfrac{24}{25}$
- $-\dfrac15$

## Explanation

The double-angle formula that contains only the sine is $\cos(2\theta)=1-2\sin^2\theta$. Substituting $\sin\theta=\dfrac35$ gives $1-2\cdot\dfrac{9}{25}=\dfrac{7}{25}$. The result does not depend on the quadrant of $\theta$, because only $\sin^2\theta$ appears in the formula.
