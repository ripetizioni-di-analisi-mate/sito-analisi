A compass measures direction clockwise in degrees from $0^\circ$ to $360^\circ$. A boat turns from a heading of $350^\circ$ to $20^\circ$ by the shortest rotation. What is the turn?

- $30^\circ$ clockwise.
- $20^\circ$ anticlockwise.
- $330^\circ$ clockwise.
- $30^\circ$ anticlockwise.

## Explanation

From $350^\circ$ to $360^\circ$ requires $10^\circ$ clockwise. Continuing past zero takes another $20^\circ$. The total is $30^\circ$, less than the $330^\circ$ needed the other way.
