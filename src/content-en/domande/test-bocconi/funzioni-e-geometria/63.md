One cable is placed along each diagonal of a convex octagonal plaza. How many cables are needed if each connection is counted once?

- $20$
- $40$
- $28$
- $16$

## Explanation

From each vertex, five vertices other than itself and its two neighbours can be reached. This gives $8\cdot5$ counts. Each diagonal is counted from both ends, giving $\dfrac{40}{2}=20$ cables.
