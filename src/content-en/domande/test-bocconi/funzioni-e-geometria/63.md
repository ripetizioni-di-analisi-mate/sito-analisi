What is the value of the base-$4$ logarithm of $32$?

- $\dfrac52$
- $8$
- $\dfrac25$
- $3$

## Explanation

Since $4=2^2$ and $32=2^5$, the change-of-base formula with base $2$ gives
$$
\log_4 32=\frac{\log_2 32}{\log_2 4}=\frac{5}{2}.
$$
Indeed $4^{\frac52}=\left(2^2\right)^{\frac52}=2^5=32$.
