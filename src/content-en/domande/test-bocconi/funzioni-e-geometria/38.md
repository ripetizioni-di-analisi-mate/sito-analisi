For what value of $k$ are the lines $2x+ky=7$ and $x-3y=1$ parallel?

- $6$
- $-3$
- $-\dfrac32$
- $-6$

## Explanation

Multiplying the second equation by $2$ gives $2x-6y=2$. The first line has the same slope when the coefficient of $y$ is $-6$. The right sides differ, so the lines are distinct.
