What is $\sin 300^\circ$?

- $-\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The angle $300^\circ=360^\circ-60^\circ$ has reference angle $60^\circ$ in the fourth quadrant. The vertical coordinate on the unit circle is negative, so the sine is $-\sin60^\circ=-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.
