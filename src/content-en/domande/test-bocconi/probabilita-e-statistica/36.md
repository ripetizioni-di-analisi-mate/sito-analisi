Two machines $A,B$ produce all copies of an item. A copy is inspected with probability $\dfrac12$ if made by $A$ and $\dfrac14$ if made by $B$. Of the inspected copies, $\dfrac23$ are from $A$. What share of all copies are made by $A$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

Among inspected copies, the ratio of $A$ to $B$ is $2$ to $1$. For an initial share $p$ for $A$, the ratio of contributions is $\dfrac{\frac p2}{\frac{1-p}4}=\dfrac{2p}{1-p}$. Setting it equal to $2$ gives $p=1-p$, so $p=\dfrac12$.
