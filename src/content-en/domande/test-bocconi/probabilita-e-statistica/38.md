Of $80$ morning orders, $25\%$ are picked up in person. Of $120$ afternoon orders, $15\%$ are picked up in person. What is the relative frequency of in-person pickups for the whole day?

- $15\%$
- $20\%$
- $25\%$
- $19\%$

## Explanation

There are $20$ in-person pickups in the morning and $18$ in the afternoon. Across $200$ orders the ratio is $\dfrac{38}{200}=19\%$. The two percentages are weighted by the order counts of their groups.
