Three sensors operate independently. Each has a $20\%$ probability of failing during a shift. What is the probability that none fails?

- $\dfrac{1}{125}$
- $\dfrac{64}{125}$
- $\dfrac{124}{125}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

One sensor works throughout the shift with probability $\dfrac45$. Independence lets us multiply the three probabilities, giving $\left(\dfrac45\right)^3=\dfrac{64}{125}$.
