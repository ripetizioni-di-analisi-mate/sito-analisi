Service $A$ handles $9$ of $10$ easy cases and $4$ of $10$ difficult cases successfully. Service $B$ handles $80$ of $100$ easy and $3$ of $10$ difficult cases successfully. Which has the higher overall success rate when both case types are pooled?

- $A$, at $\dfrac{13}{20}$.
- $B$, at $\dfrac{83}{110}$.
- The overall rates are equal.
- The data do not determine it.

## Explanation

The total for $A$ is $\dfrac{13}{20}=65\,\%$. The total for $B$ is $\dfrac{83}{110}$, above $75\,\%$. Although $A$ outperforms $B$ in each case type, the much larger share of easy cases for $B$ changes the overall comparison.
