A fair die is rolled. Let $A$ be the event “an even number comes up” and let $B$ be the event “a multiple of $3$ comes up.” Which statement is true?

- $A$ and $B$ are disjoint
- $A$ and $B$ are independent
- $P(A\cup B)=\dfrac56$
- $P(A\mid B)=\dfrac13$

## Explanation

Here $A=\{2,4,6\}$ and $B=\{3,6\}$, so $A\cap B=\{6\}$ and the two events are not disjoint. Since $P(A)P(B)=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac13=\dfrac16=P(A\cap B)$, the events are independent. Moreover, $P(A\cup B)=\dfrac12+\dfrac13-\dfrac16=\dfrac23$ and $P(A\mid B)=\dfrac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}=\dfrac16\cdot3=\dfrac12$.
