Eight compartments are lined up and numbered $1$ through $8$. Four compartments must be occupied, with at least one unoccupied compartment between successive occupied ones. How many selections are possible?

- $4$
- $6$
- $16$
- $5$

## Explanation

Four occupied compartments require three internal gaps. Place the seven necessary compartments in alternating order, occupied, empty, occupied, empty, occupied, empty, occupied. The extra empty compartment goes before, after, or in one of the three internal gaps, giving five choices.
