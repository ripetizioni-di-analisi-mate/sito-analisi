On a grid, travel from $(0,0)$ to $(3,2)$ is allowed only by unit steps right or up. The point $(1,1)$ is closed. How many shortest paths remain?

- $3$
- $4$
- $8$
- $6$

## Explanation

Without the closure there are $\binom52=10$ ways to place the two up steps among all five. For a path through $(1,1)$ there are $2$ ways to reach it and $3$ ways to continue. Removing those $2\cdot3=6$ paths leaves $4$.
