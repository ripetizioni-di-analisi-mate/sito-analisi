Events $A,B$ are independent. Given $P(A)=60\%$ and $P(B)=25\%$, what is $P(A^c\cap B)$?

- $15\%$
- $25\%$
- $10\%$
- $40\%$

## Explanation

The intersection $A\cap B$ has probability $15\%$. Within $B$, the events $A$ and $A^c$ partition all outcomes. This gives $P(A^c\cap B)=25\%-15\%=10\%$.
