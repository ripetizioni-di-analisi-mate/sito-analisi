A data set has minimum $2$ and maximum $9$. Each value $x$ is replaced by $10-3x$. What is the new range?

- $7$
- $11$
- $21$
- $31$

## Explanation

The new expression is decreasing. The original minimum gives the maximum $4$, and the original maximum gives the minimum $-17$. The range is $4-(-17)=21$.
