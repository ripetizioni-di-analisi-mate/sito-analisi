Of $48$ participants, $28$ choose track $A$ and the others choose track $B$. Thirty choose the morning session, including sixteen on track $A$. Everyone else chooses the afternoon. How many participants choose track $B$ in the afternoon?

- $12$
- $6$
- $14$
- $20$

## Explanation

Track $B$ has $48-28=20$ participants. In the morning there are $30-16=14$ participants on track $B$. This leaves $20-14=6$ in the afternoon.
