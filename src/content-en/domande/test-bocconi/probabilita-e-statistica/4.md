Six distinct photographs are arranged in a row. Photograph $A$ must appear before photograph $B$, with no adjacency requirement. How many orderings are possible?

- $120$
- $240$
- $360$
- $720$

## Explanation

Swapping $A$ and $B$ in a valid arrangement gives an arrangement with $B$ before $A$. The swap is reversible, so the $6!$ arrangements split into two equal groups. The required count is $\dfrac{6!}{2}=360$.
