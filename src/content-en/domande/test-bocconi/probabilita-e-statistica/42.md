The values $1,4,7,10$ occur $2,2,3,1$ times, respectively. What is the median of the eight observations?

- $4$
- $7$
- $6$
- $5.5$

## Explanation

The first two positions have value $1$. Positions three and four have value $4$, and positions five through seven have value $7$. The median averages positions four and five, giving $\dfrac{4+7}{2}=5.5$.
