A strip has three squares, each coloured red or blue. It can be turned around to read the colours in reverse order. Two strips count as the same design if they match directly or after one is turned around. How many distinct designs are there?

- $4$
- $8$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

With a fixed reading direction there are $2^3=8$ sequences. The four with the same colour at both ends look the same in reverse: two choices for the ends and two for the middle. The other four form two pairs of reverse sequences, giving $4+2=6$ designs.
