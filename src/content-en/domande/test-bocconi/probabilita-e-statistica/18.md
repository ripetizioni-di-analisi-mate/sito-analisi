Five distinct people stand in a line. Two of them are sisters. In how many arrangements is there at least one person between the sisters?

- $48$
- $60$
- $96$
- $72$

## Explanation

There are $5!=120$ arrangements. To count those with the sisters together, treat them as a single block with two internal orders. This excludes $4!\cdot2=48$ arrangements, leaving $72$.
