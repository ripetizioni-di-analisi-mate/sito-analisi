Events $A,B$ are independent, with $P(A)=\dfrac35$ and $P(A\cap B)=\dfrac3{20}$. Find $P(B)$.

- $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- $\dfrac{9}{100}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

Independence gives $P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)$. Dividing $\dfrac3{20}$ by $\dfrac35$ gives $\dfrac14$.
