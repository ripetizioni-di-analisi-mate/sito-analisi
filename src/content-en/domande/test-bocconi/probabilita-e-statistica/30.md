Three cards are drawn in random order without replacement from four blue and four green cards. The first card is blue and the second is unobserved. What is the probability that the third is blue?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$

## Explanation

After the first draw, three blue cards remain among seven cards. Any of those seven cards is equally likely to occupy each remaining position in the random order. Hence the third card is blue with probability $\dfrac37$.
