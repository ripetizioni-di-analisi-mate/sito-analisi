Which of the following situations has exactly $5\cdot4\cdot3=60$ possible outcomes?

- Choosing a group of three from five people, with no roles
- Forming a three-digit code from the symbols $1,2,3,4,5$, with repetition allowed
- Awarding a first, a second, and a third prize to five people, at most one prize each
- Lining up five people

## Explanation

When the prizes are awarded, order matters, because receiving the first or the second prize gives different outcomes. The first prize has five possible recipients, the second four, and the third three, so there are $5\cdot4\cdot3=60$ outcomes. By contrast, the group with no roles has $\dbinom53=10$ outcomes, the code with repetition has $5^3=125$, and the line of five people has $5!=120$.
