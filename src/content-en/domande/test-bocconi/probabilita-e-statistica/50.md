Six people can form a group with one moderator and two participants whose roles are identical. Only Ada and Luca may moderate, but they may also participate. How many groups with a designated moderator can be formed?

- $40$
- $30$
- $12$
- $20$

## Explanation

There are $2$ choices for moderator. Once that person is chosen, the two participants are an unordered pair from the remaining five, giving $\binom52=10$ ways. The total is $2\cdot10=20$ because changing the moderator changes the outcome.
