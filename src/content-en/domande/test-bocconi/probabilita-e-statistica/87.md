A box plot uses quartiles $Q_1=12$ and $Q_3=20$. It flags a value as an outlier only if its distance from the interval $[Q_1,Q_3]$ exceeds one and a half times the interquartile range. Among readings $-1,0,27,32,33$, which are flagged?

- $\{-1,0,32,33\}$
- $\{-1,33\}$
- $\{0,32\}$
- $\{27,32,33\}$

## Explanation

The interquartile range is $20-12=8$. The fences are $12-1.5\cdot8=0$ and $20+1.5\cdot8=32$. The rule uses strict inequalities: $0$ and $32$ stay within the fences, while $-1$ and $33$ lie beyond them.
