Consider the data $7,3,9,3,5,10$. Which statement is true?

- The mean is greater than the median
- The median equals the mode
- The range is $10$
- The median is $5$

## Explanation

Sorting the data gives $3,3,5,7,9,10$, so the median is the average of the third and fourth values, $\dfrac{5+7}{2}=6$. The mean is $\dfrac{37}{6}$, which is greater than $6=\dfrac{36}{6}$. The mode is $3$, so it does not equal the median, and the range is $10-3=7$.
