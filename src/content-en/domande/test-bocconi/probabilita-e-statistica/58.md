How many diagonals does a convex polygon with ten sides have?

- $45$
- $70$
- $35$
- $40$

## Explanation

Each pair of distinct vertices determines a segment, so there are $\dbinom{10}{2}=45$ segments joining two vertices. Of these segments, $10$ are sides of the polygon. The polygon therefore has $45-10=35$ diagonals.
