Three colleagues independently and uniformly choose one of the seven days of the week for a visit. What is the probability that at least two choose the same day?

- $\dfrac{19}{49}$
- $\dfrac37$
- $\dfrac17$
- $\dfrac{30}{49}$

## Explanation

Consider the complement. The first choice is unrestricted, the second must avoid one day, and the third must avoid two. The probability of all different days is $(\dfrac{6}{7})(\dfrac{5}{7})=\dfrac{30}{49}$. Subtracting from one gives $\dfrac{19}{49}$.
