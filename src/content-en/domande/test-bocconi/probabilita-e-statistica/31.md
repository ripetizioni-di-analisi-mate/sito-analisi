For two events, $P(A)=\dfrac12$, $P(B)=\dfrac25$, and $P(A\cup B)=\dfrac7{10}$. Find $P(A\mid B)$.

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}$

## Explanation

The intersection is $\dfrac12+\dfrac25-\dfrac7{10}=\dfrac15$. Since the condition is $B$, divide $\dfrac15$ by $\dfrac25$ to get $\dfrac12$.
