Four friends each choose one of three films being shown, and several friends may choose the same film. Which expression counts the possible choices of the four friends?

- $4^3$
- $4\cdot3$
- $3^4$
- $4\cdot3\cdot2$

## Explanation

An outcome assigns a film to each of the four friends. Each friend has three choices, whatever the others have chosen, because films may repeat. By the multiplication rule there are therefore $3\cdot3\cdot3\cdot3=3^4=81$ outcomes. The expression $4^3$ would instead count the assignments of a friend to each of the three films.
