A fair coin selects one of two envelopes, then one card is drawn uniformly from that envelope. The first contains one card and the second contains four. What is the probability of drawing one particular card from the second envelope?

- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

Reaching the card first requires choosing the second envelope, with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Given that choice, the required card has probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$. Their product is $\dfrac{1}{8}$. Drawing from all five cards mixed together would be a different experiment.
