Two fair dice are rolled independently. What is the probability that the sum is $2$ or $9$?

- $\dfrac{1}{18}$
- $\dfrac{5}{36}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

There are $36$ equally likely ordered pairs. Only $(1,1)$ sums to $2$. The pairs summing to $9$ are $(3,6),(4,5),(5,4),(6,3)$. The cases are disjoint, so the probability is $\dfrac{1+4}{36}$.
