Of a magazine's readers, $35\%$ have a digital subscription. Of these, $40\%$ log in at least once a week. What is the joint relative frequency of a digital subscription and weekly access among all readers?

- $5\%$
- $14\%$
- $35\%$
- $40\%$

## Explanation

The desired group is $40\%$ of the digital group, which itself is $35\%$ of the total. Multiplying gives $0.40\cdot0.35=0.14$, or $14\%$.
