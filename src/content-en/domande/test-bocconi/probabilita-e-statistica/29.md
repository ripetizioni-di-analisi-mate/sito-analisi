Two fair dice are rolled independently. Given that their sum is greater than $9$, what is the probability that they show the same number?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The sums $10,11,12$ arise from three, two, and one ordered pair, respectively. Of these six equally likely outcomes, only $(5,5)$ and $(6,6)$ are doubles. The ratio is $\dfrac26=\dfrac13$.
