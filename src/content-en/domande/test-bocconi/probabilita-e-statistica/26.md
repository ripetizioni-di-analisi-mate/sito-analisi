Two numbers are drawn independently and uniformly from $1$ to $6$, with replacement. What is the probability that their product is even?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The product is odd only when both factors are odd. Each factor has probability $\dfrac12$ of being odd, so the complement has probability $\dfrac14$. The result is $1-\dfrac14=\dfrac34$.
