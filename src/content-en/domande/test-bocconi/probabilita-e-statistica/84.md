School A has $200$ students, and $30\%$ of them get to school by bicycle. School B has $300$ students, and among them the percentage who arrive by bicycle is $20\%$. Which statement is true?

- School A has more students who cycle than school B
- School B has $10$ fewer students who cycle than school A
- In the two schools combined, $25\%$ of the students arrive by bicycle
- The two schools have the same number of students who arrive by bicycle

## Explanation

In school A there are $0.3\cdot200=60$ students who cycle. In school B there are $0.2\cdot300=60$, so the two numbers are equal even though the percentages differ. In the two schools combined, $120$ of the $500$ students arrive by bicycle, so the overall relative frequency is $\dfrac{120}{500}=24\%$. The simple average of the two percentages, $25\%$, would give the same weight to two groups of different sizes.
