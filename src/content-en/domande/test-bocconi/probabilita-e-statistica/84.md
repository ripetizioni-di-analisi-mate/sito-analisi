A questionnaire records only the ordered levels “low”, “medium” and “high”, without specifying numerical distances. Which summary uses the ordering and remains meaningful if the levels are relabelled in the same order?

- The variance of the assigned codes.
- The mean of freely assigned numerical codes.
- The numerical difference between highest and lowest.
- The category of the middle observation in an odd-sized sample.

## Explanation

The median category is obtained by ordering responses and locating the middle position. Order-preserving relabelling keeps that position and its corresponding category. Numerical means, differences and variances instead depend on the distances assigned to the codes.
