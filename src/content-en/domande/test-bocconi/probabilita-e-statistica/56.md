A request succeeds on the first submission with probability $\dfrac{3}{5}$. It is resubmitted only after a failure, and in that case it succeeds with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$. What is the probability of success within two submissions?

- $\dfrac45$
- $\dfrac7{10}$
- $\dfrac3{20}$
- $\dfrac{17}{20}$

## Explanation

Immediate success has probability $\dfrac{3}{5}$. Delayed success requires a failure and then a success, with probability $(\dfrac{2}{5})(\dfrac{1}{4})=\dfrac{1}{10}$. The two paths are disjoint and sum to $\dfrac{3}{5}+\dfrac{1}{10}=\dfrac{7}{10}$.
