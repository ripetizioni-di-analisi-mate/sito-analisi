Arrangements of all the letters of the word LIBRO are formed, meaningful or not. In how many of them do the two vowels occupy the first and last positions?

- $6$
- $24$
- $120$
- $12$

## Explanation

The vowels $I$ and $O$ occupy the two ends in $2$ different orders. The consonants $L$, $B$, and $R$, all distinct, fill the three middle positions in $3!=6$ orders. By the multiplication rule there are $2\cdot6=12$ arrangements.
