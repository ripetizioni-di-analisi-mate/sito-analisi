Four observations have descriptive variance $9$. What is their corrected sample variance, using denominator $N-1$?

- $9$
- $12$
- $\dfrac{27}{4}$
- $36$

## Explanation

The sum of squared deviations is $4\cdot9=36$. For corrected sample variance, divide the same sum by $4-1=3$, giving $12$.
