Three flags are placed along a track. Each may be red, white, or green. How many color sequences contain at most one red flag?

- $20$
- $12$
- $18$
- $26$

## Explanation

With no red flag there are $2^3=8$ sequences. With one red flag, its position has three choices, leaving $2^2$ possibilities for the other two flags. The total is $8+3\cdot4=20$.
