Two lines each make four pieces. Deviations in millimetres are $-1,-1,1,1$ for line $A$ and $-2,0,0,2$ for line $B$. Which line has the larger population variance?

- Both variances are $1$.
- Line $B$, with variance $2$.
- Line $A$, with variance $2$.
- Both variances are zero.

## Explanation

Both means are zero. For $A$ the four squared deviations total $4$, giving variance $\dfrac{4}{4}=1$. For $B$ they total $8$, giving $\dfrac{8}{4}=2$. The greater squared spread is that of $B$.
