The table shows the cumulative relative frequencies of the grades in an exam, given as whole numbers out of thirty.

| Grade | Cumulative relative frequency |
|---|---:|
| up to $21$ | $0.20$ |
| up to $24$ | $0.45$ |
| up to $27$ | $0.80$ |
| up to $30$ | $1$ |

What percentage of the students received a grade from $25$ to $27$, inclusive?

- $80\%$
- $45\%$
- $35\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

The cumulative frequency at $27$ includes all grades up to $27$, that is, $0.80$ of the students. The one at $24$ includes the grades up to $24$, that is, $0.45$. Since the grades are whole numbers, the difference $0.80-0.45=0.35$ is the frequency of grades from $25$ to $27$, that is, $35\%$.
