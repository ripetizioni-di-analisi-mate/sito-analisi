A raffle ticket costs $3$ euros. It pays $20$ euros with probability $\dfrac{1}{10}$, $4$ euros with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$, and nothing otherwise. What is the expected net gain per ticket?

- $3$ euros
- $0$ euros
- $1$ euro
- $-3$ euros

## Explanation

The expected payout is $20(\dfrac{1}{10})+4(\dfrac{1}{4})=2+1=3$ euros. The certain cost is $3$ euros for every ticket. Subtracting it from the expected payout gives an expected net gain of zero.
