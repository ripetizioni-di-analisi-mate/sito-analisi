Four bridges are open in both directions and two are open only in the outbound direction. A round trip uses one bridge on the way out and a different one on the way back. How many round trips are possible?

- $18$
- $20$
- $24$
- $30$

## Explanation

After taking a bidirectional bridge, three bridges remain for the return, giving $4\cdot3$ trips. After either one-way bridge, any bidirectional bridge can be used, giving $2\cdot4$ trips. The total is $12+8=20$.
