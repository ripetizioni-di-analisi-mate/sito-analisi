Two of five envelopes in a randomly ordered stack contain a voucher. Envelopes are opened from the top until the first voucher appears. What is the probability of opening exactly three envelopes?

- $\dfrac1{10}$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac35$

## Explanation

Two empty envelopes must be followed by one with a voucher. Without replacement the successive probabilities are $\dfrac{3}{5}$, $\dfrac{2}{4}$ and $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Their product is $(\dfrac{3}{5})(\dfrac{2}{4})(\dfrac{2}{3})=\dfrac{1}{5}$.
