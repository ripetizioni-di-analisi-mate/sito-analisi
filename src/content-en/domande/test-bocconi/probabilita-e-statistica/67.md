Two fair dice are rolled. What is the probability that the higher of the two scores is $4$?

- $\dfrac{7}{36}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

The higher score is at most $4$ when both dice show a number from $1$ to $4$, that is, in $4\cdot4=16$ ordered pairs. The higher score is at most $3$ in $3\cdot3=9$ pairs. So the higher score equals $4$ exactly in $16-9=7$ of the $36$ pairs, and the probability is $\dfrac{7}{36}$.
