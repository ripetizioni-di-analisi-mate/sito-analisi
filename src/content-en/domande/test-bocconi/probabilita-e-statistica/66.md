In a company, $60\%$ of the employees speak English, $30\%$ speak Spanish, and $20\%$ speak both languages. What percentage of the employees speak exactly one of the two languages?

- $70\%$
- $90\%$
- $50\%$
- $40\%$

## Explanation

Employees who speak only English make up $60\%-20\%=40\%$, because the $20\%$ who speak both languages must be excluded. Those who speak only Spanish make up $30\%-20\%=10\%$. The two groups are disjoint, so $40\%+10\%=50\%$ of the employees speak exactly one language.
