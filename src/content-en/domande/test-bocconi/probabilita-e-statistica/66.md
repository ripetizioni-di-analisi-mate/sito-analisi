Two sensors each send one signal at an independent uniformly chosen time during a minute. What is the probability that the signals are more than $30$ seconds apart?

- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The possible times form a square of side $60$ in the plane of the two times. Their absolute difference exceeds $30$ in two right triangles in opposite corners, each with legs $30$. The area ratio is $\dfrac{2\dfrac{30^2}{2}}{60^2}=\dfrac{1}{4}$.
