A four-character code alternates between letters and digits and may begin with either type. Four letters and three digits are available. No character may repeat. How many codes are possible?

- $72$
- $144$
- $84$
- $288$

## Explanation

The two letters occupy distinguishable positions, so they have $4\cdot3$ possible orders. The digits have $3\cdot2$. There are two alternating patterns, so the total is $2\cdot12\cdot6=144$.
