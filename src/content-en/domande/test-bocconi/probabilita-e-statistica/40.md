Twelve observations have mean $50$. A second group has mean $62$. Combining the groups gives mean $58$. How many observations are in the second group?

- $12$
- $18$
- $24$
- $36$

## Explanation

Relative to the combined mean of $58$, the first group contributes $12(50-58)=-96$. The average contribution per observation in the second group is $62-58=4$. The positive deviations must total $96$, so the second group needs $24$ observations.
