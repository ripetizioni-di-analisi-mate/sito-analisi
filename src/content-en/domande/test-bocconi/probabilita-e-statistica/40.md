A shop replaces its chronological series of daily receipts with a table listing each distinct amount and how often it occurred. Which information can be lost in this change?

- Total receipts.
- The difference between the largest and smallest receipts.
- The number of days on which receipts fell from the previous day.
- Mean daily receipts.

## Explanation

The table keeps every value and its frequency but loses the order of the days. For example, receipts of $1,2,3$ and $3,2,1$ give the same table but have zero and two decreases, respectively.
