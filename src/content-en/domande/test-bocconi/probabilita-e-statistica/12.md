One fifth of shipments are urgent. A check is applied to $\dfrac34$ of urgent shipments and $\dfrac18$ of the others. A shipment is chosen uniformly from those checked. What is the probability that it was urgent?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

The probability of urgent and checked is $\dfrac15\cdot\dfrac34=\dfrac3{20}$. The other branch contributes $\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac18=\dfrac1{10}$. Of all checked shipments, the urgent fraction is $\dfrac{3}{3+2}=\dfrac35$.
