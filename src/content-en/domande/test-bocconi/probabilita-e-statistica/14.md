A list of scores contains one $3$, five copies of $7$, and some copies of $5$. The mean is $6$. How many times does $5$ occur?

- $1$
- $3$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

With $x$ copies of $5$, the sum is $38+5x$ across $6+x$ scores. The equation $\dfrac{38+5x}{6+x}=6$ gives $38+5x=36+6x$, so $x=2$.
