The table classifies fifty course registrants.

| Channel | Attended | Absent |
|---|---:|---:|
| App | 15 | 5 |
| Website | 21 | 9 |

Among those attending, what is the relative frequency of registering through the website?

- $\dfrac{7}{10}$
- $\dfrac{7}{12}$
- $\dfrac{21}{50}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

The condition “attended” selects the first column, which contains $15+21=36$ people. Of these, $21$ registered through the website. The ratio is $\dfrac{21}{36}=\dfrac7{12}$.
