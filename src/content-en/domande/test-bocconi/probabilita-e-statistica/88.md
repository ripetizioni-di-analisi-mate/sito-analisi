What is the standard deviation of the data $2,4,4,4,5,5,7,9$?

- $4$
- $\dfrac{32}{7}$
- $\sqrt5$
- $2$

## Explanation

The sum of the data is $40$, so the mean is $\dfrac{40}{8}=5$. The squared deviations from the mean are $9,1,1,1,0,0,4,16$, with sum $32$. The descriptive variance is therefore $\dfrac{32}{8}=4$ and the standard deviation is $\sqrt4=2$.
