The final mark is the mean of the best three scores from four tests. The first three are $12$, $16$ and $18$. What is the smallest integer fourth score that produces a final mean of at least $17$?

- $19$
- $16$
- $17$
- $15$

## Explanation

A mean of $17$ requires at least $51$ points across the three retained scores. The first three sum to $46$, so the new score must replace $12$. Keeping $16$ and $18$ leaves at least $51-16-18=17$ points to earn on the fourth test.
