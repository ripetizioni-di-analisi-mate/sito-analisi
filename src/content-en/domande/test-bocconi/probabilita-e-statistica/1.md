A center offers four workshops and three time slots. Two workshops run in every slot, and the others run in the first slot only. How many distinct workshop-and-slot pairs can be booked?

- $10$
- $8$
- $12$
- $6$

## Explanation

The two workshops available in every slot yield $2\cdot3=6$ pairs. The others yield two pairs, both in the first slot. Since all the pairs are distinct, the total is $6+2=8$.
