A train is selected from three lines with probabilities $\dfrac12,\dfrac13,\dfrac16$. The probabilities of a delay on the three lines are $\dfrac15,\dfrac12,0$, respectively. What is the overall probability of a delay?

- $\dfrac{4}{15}$
- $\dfrac{7}{30}$
- $\dfrac{7}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The first contribution is $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac15=\dfrac1{10}$ and the second is $\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac16$. The third is zero. Their sum is $\dfrac1{10}+\dfrac16=\dfrac4{15}$.
