A pie chart of $140$ preferences has three sectors of $72^\circ,126^\circ,162^\circ$. By how many preferences does the largest sector exceed the second largest?

- $14$
- $10$
- $16$
- $36$

## Explanation

The difference between the angles is $36^\circ$, or $\dfrac{36}{360}=\dfrac1{10}$ of the circle. The difference in preferences is therefore $\dfrac1{10}$ of $140$, which is $14$.
