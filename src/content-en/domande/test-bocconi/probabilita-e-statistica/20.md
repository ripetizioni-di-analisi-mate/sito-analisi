A four-digit PIN uses only $0,1,2,3,4,5$. Its first and last digits match. The two middle digits differ from each other and from the first digit. How many PINs meet these constraints?

- $60$
- $150$
- $120$
- $216$

## Explanation

The first digit has six choices and determines the last. The second digit has five choices. The third has four because the first two digits are excluded. This gives $6\cdot5\cdot4=120$.
