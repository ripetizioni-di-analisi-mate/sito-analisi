During the hotter weeks of one year, fan sales and swimming-pool admissions both rise. Which conclusion is justified by this observation alone?

- Buying a fan causes people to visit a pool.
- Every pool visitor bought a fan.
- Temperature could influence both quantities.
- Opening more pools necessarily reduces fan sales.

## Explanation

The observation describes joint variation during hot weeks. Temperature is a possible common cause. The aggregate data do not track individual customers or establish a causal effect from either quantity to the other.
