The bar chart shows how many books, from one to five, the forty students of a course read in one year. What percentage of the students read at least four books?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=6 ymin=0 ymax=16 nomi="books,students" poligoni="0.7,0 1.3,0 1.3,6 0.7,6;1.7,0 2.3,0 2.3,10 1.7,10;2.7,0 3.3,0 3.3,14 2.7,14;3.7,0 4.3,0 4.3,7 3.7,7;4.7,0 5.3,0 5.3,3 4.7,3" etichette="1,6.9,6;2,10.9,10;3,14.9,14;4,7.9,7;5,3.9,3" legenda=0 titolo="Number of students by number of books read: 6 with 1 book, 10 with 2, 14 with 3, 7 with 4, 3 with 5."}

- $17.5\%$
- $25\%$
- $7.5\%$
- $60\%$

## Explanation

The students who read at least four books are those in the bars for $4$ and $5$ books, that is, $7+3=10$ students. The relative frequency is computed over all forty students, so it equals $\dfrac{10}{40}=\dfrac14$, that is, $25\%$.
