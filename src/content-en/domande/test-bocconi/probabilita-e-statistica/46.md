A single-elimination tournament has $13$ teams. Some may receive a bye. Each match eliminates exactly one team and play ends with one winner. How many matches take place?

- $6$
- $16$
- $12$
- $13$

## Explanation

Going from $13$ teams to one winner requires $12$ eliminations. Each match accounts for one elimination, while a bye accounts for none. The total is therefore $12$.
