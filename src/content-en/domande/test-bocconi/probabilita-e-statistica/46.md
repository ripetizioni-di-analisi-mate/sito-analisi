How many three-digit natural numbers are even and have three different digits?

- $256$
- $450$
- $328$
- $504$

## Explanation

Split into two cases according to the last digit. If the last digit is $0$, the first digit has nine choices and the second has eight, which gives $9\cdot8=72$ numbers.

If the last digit is $2$, $4$, $6$, or $8$, the first digit has eight choices, because it must differ from $0$ and from the last digit. The middle digit again has eight choices, so this case gives $4\cdot8\cdot8=256$ numbers. The two cases are disjoint, therefore the total is $72+256=328$.
