Five distinct tiles are placed around a circle. Arrangements differing only by rotation count as one. Reversing the reading direction generally gives a different arrangement. How many orderings are possible?

- $24$
- $12$
- $60$
- $120$

## Explanation

Always start from the same tile and read the other four clockwise. Each linear ordering of those four tiles describes one arrangement up to rotation. Hence the count is $4!=24$.
