A centre needs a single predicted duration for three cases that take $2$, $4$ and $12$ minutes. Which prediction minimises the sum of absolute errors?

- $2$ minutes
- $6$ minutes
- $12$ minutes
- $4$ minutes

## Explanation

Between $2$ and $12$, the distances to the two endpoints always add to $10$. The remaining distance to $4$ is minimised at $4$, where it is zero. Outside the interval the endpoint distances alone already exceed $10$.
