In the data $3,5,6,8,100$, the value $100$ was a transcription error and is replaced by $10$. Which statement is true?

- The mean and the median both decrease
- The median stays $6$ and the mean decreases by $90$
- The median stays $6$ and the mean decreases by $18$
- The mean stays the same and the median decreases

## Explanation

In both versions of the data, the middle value of the five sorted values is $6$, so the median stays $6$. The sum decreases by $100-10=90$ and the number of values remains $5$. Therefore the mean decreases by $\dfrac{90}{5}=18$, going from $24.4$ to $6.4$.
