Three distinct boxes are selected uniformly from five type-$A$ boxes and three type-$B$ boxes. What is the probability that all three are of the same type?

- $\dfrac{11}{56}$
- $\dfrac{5}{28}$
- $\dfrac{1}{56}$
- $\dfrac{11}{48}$

## Explanation

There are $\binom83=56$ triples in total. Three type-$A$ boxes can be chosen in $\binom53=10$ ways, or three type-$B$ boxes in $\binom33=1$ way. This gives $\dfrac{10+1}{56}$.
