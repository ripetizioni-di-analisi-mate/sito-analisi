Six local teams and four visiting teams qualify for a competition. Three teams are selected without assigned roles, exactly two of them visiting teams. How many selections are possible?

- $24$
- $48$
- $72$
- $36$

## Explanation

The visiting teams can be chosen in $\binom42=6$ ways. For each pair there are six choices for the local team. This gives $6\cdot6=36$ selections.
