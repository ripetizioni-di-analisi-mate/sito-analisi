A group of three is formed from nine people. Four of them have already worked on the project, and the new group must include at least one of those four. How many groups are possible?

- $74$
- $60$
- $70$
- $84$

## Explanation

There are $\binom93=84$ groups without restrictions. A group with no experienced member is chosen from the five other people, in $\binom53=10$ ways. The valid count is $84-10=74$.
