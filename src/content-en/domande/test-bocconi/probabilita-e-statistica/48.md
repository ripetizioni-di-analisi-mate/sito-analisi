How many integers from $1$ to $100$, inclusive, are divisible by $4$ or by $6$?

- $41$
- $37$
- $29$
- $33$

## Explanation

From $1$ to $100$ there are $25$ multiples of $4$ and $16$ multiples of $6$, since $6\cdot16=96$. An integer is divisible by both $4$ and $6$ if and only if it is a multiple of their least common multiple $12$. There are $8$ multiples of $12$ up to $100$, and the sum $25+16$ counts them twice. Therefore the required count is $25+16-8=33$.
