Five identical vouchers are shared among three distinct counters and each counter must receive at least one. How many allocations are possible?

- $6$
- $15$
- $10$
- $3$

## Explanation

After giving one voucher to each counter, two remain. They can go to the same counter in $3$ ways or to two different counters in $\binom32=3$ ways. The cases are disjoint and give $6$ allocations.
