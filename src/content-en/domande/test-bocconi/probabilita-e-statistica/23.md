There are three blue cables, three red cables, and two green cables, all distinguishable. How many groups of four cables contain all three colors?

- $45$
- $18$
- $36$
- $54$

## Explanation

One color appears twice and the others once. Repeating blue gives $\binom32\cdot3\cdot2=18$, and repeating red gives the same count. Repeating green gives $\binom22\cdot3\cdot3=9$. The total is $18+18+9=45$.
