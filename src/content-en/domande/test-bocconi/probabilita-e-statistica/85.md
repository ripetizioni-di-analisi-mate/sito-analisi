The mean of nine numbers is $12$. A tenth number is added, and the mean of the ten numbers becomes $13$. What number was added?

- $13$
- $22$
- $10$
- $25$

## Explanation

The sum of the nine numbers is $9\cdot12=108$. After the addition, the sum of the ten numbers is $10\cdot13=130$. The added number is therefore the difference $130-108=22$.
