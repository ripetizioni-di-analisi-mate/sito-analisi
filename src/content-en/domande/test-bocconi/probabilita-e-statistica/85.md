Two help desks operate for different periods. Desk $A$ handles $24$ requests in $3$ hours and desk $B$ handles $35$ in $5$ hours. Which result correctly compares their hourly productivity and gives the difference?

- $A$ exceeds $B$ by one request per hour.
- $B$ exceeds $A$ by one request per hour.
- Their hourly productivity is equal.
- $B$ exceeds $A$ by $11$ requests per hour.

## Explanation

For different exposure times, divide each count by its own operating time. The rates are $\dfrac{24}{3}=8$ and $\dfrac{35}{5}=7$ requests per hour. Their difference is $8-7=1$ in favour of $A$.
