A university has three campuses, attended by $50\%$, $30\%$, and $20\%$ of the students, respectively. Out-of-town students make up $6\%$ at the first campus, $10\%$ at the second, and $30\%$ at the third. An out-of-town student is chosen at random. What is the probability that the student attends the third campus?

- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The three branches give out-of-town students with probabilities $0.5\cdot0.06=0.03$, $0.3\cdot0.1=0.03$, and $0.2\cdot0.3=0.06$. The total probability of being out of town is therefore $0.12$. By Bayes' rule the required probability is $\dfrac{0.06}{0.12}=\dfrac12$.
