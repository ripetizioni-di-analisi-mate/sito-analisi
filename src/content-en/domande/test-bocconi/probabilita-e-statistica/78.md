Five orders with distinct positive amounts are already sorted from lowest to highest. Only the highest amount is doubled. Which measure is certain to stay the same?

- The median.
- The arithmetic mean.
- The range.
- The maximum.

## Explanation

For five orders the median is the third value. The positive maximum increases and remains last, so the third value stays the same. The total and the maximum increase, while the minimum remains fixed.
