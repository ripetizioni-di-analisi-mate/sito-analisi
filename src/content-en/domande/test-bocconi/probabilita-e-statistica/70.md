We have $360=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5$. How many positive divisors does $360$ have, including $1$ and $360$?

- $6$
- $12$
- $24$
- $9$

## Explanation

Every positive divisor of $360$ can be written in exactly one way as $2^a\cdot3^b\cdot5^c$, with $a$ from $0$ to $3$, $b$ from $0$ to $2$, and $c$ from $0$ to $1$. So a divisor is built in three stages, by choosing the three exponents. The exponent of $2$ has four choices, that of $3$ three, and that of $5$ two, because the exponent $0$ is also allowed. By the multiplication rule there are therefore $4\cdot3\cdot2=24$ divisors.
