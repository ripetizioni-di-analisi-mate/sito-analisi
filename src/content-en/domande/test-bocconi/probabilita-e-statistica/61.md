On a square grid you move from the point $(0,0)$ to the point $(5,2)$ with unit steps to the right or upward. How many different paths are there?

- $10$
- $21$
- $128$
- $42$

## Explanation

Every path consists of $7$ steps, of which $5$ go right and $2$ go up. A path is determined by choosing which two of the seven positions hold the upward steps. Therefore there are $\dbinom72=21$ paths.
