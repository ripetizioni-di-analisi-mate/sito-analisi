A program simulates $1000$ independent trials and observes $248$ successes. With no further information about the mechanism, what follows directly?

- The theoretical probability is exactly $0.248$.
- The observed relative frequency is $0.248$.
- Every block of four trials contains one success.
- The next $1000$ trials will produce $248$ successes.

## Explanation

Relative frequency is the observed count divided by the number of trials, here $\dfrac{248}{1000}=0.248$. It estimates the probability underlying the mechanism. A further simulation can have a different frequency even with the same theoretical probability.
