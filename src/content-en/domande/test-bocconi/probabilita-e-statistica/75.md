A die is loaded so that $6$ comes up with probability $\dfrac13$, while the other five faces are equally likely. The die is rolled once. What is the probability that an even number comes up?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{15}$

## Explanation

The faces $1$ to $5$ have total probability $1-\dfrac13=\dfrac23$, so each has probability $\dfrac{2}{15}$. Since the outcomes are not equally likely, the probability of the event is obtained by adding the probabilities of the favorable outcomes $2$, $4$, and $6$. Therefore the probability is
$$
\frac{2}{15}+\frac{2}{15}+\frac13=\frac{4}{15}+\frac{5}{15}=\frac{9}{15}=\frac35 .
$$
