In a histogram, the class $[0,2)$ contains $12$ observations and $[2,8)$ contains $24$. The area of each bar must equal its count. What is the ratio of the first bar's height to the second's?

- $\dfrac23$
- $3$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac32$

## Explanation

Height is found by dividing the count by the class width. The heights are $\dfrac{12}{2}=6$ and $\dfrac{24}{6}=4$. Their ratio is $\dfrac{6}{4}=\dfrac{3}{2}$, even though the second class has the larger count.
