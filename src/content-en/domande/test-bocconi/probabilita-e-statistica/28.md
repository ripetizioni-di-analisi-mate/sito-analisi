Components $A$ and $B$ cannot both fail in the same shift. Their individual failure probabilities are $0.3$ and $0.2$. What is the probability that both work throughout the shift?

- $0.56$
- $0.44$
- $0.5$
- $0.06$

## Explanation

The failure events are disjoint. At least one failure therefore has probability $0.3+0.2=0.5$. Both components work when the complementary event occurs, which has probability $0.5$.
