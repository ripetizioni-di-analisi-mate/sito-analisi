A club has fifty members. The diagram shows how many members are enrolled in swimming ($S$), tennis ($T$), and yoga ($Y$) classes, region by region. A member is chosen at random. What is the probability that the member is enrolled in exactly two classes?

::venn{tipo="tre" universo=1 ambiente="Ω" nomi="S,T,Y" elementi="a:12;b:9;c:7;ab:5;ac:4;bc:3;abc:2;u:8" titolo="Number of members in each region. There are 8 members outside the three sets."}

- $\dfrac{7}{25}$
- $\dfrac{14}{25}$
- $\dfrac{1}{25}$
- $\dfrac{6}{25}$

## Explanation

Members enrolled in exactly two classes lie in the regions shared by two sets and outside the third, which contain $5$, $4$, and $3$ members. The $2$ members in the central region take all three classes, so they are excluded. Therefore there are $5+4+3=12$ favorable cases and the probability is $\dfrac{12}{50}=\dfrac{6}{25}$.
