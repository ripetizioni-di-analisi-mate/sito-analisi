Two independent fair coins are tossed. An observer reports only that at least one is heads. Given that information, if the first coin is also revealed to be tails, what is the probability that the second is heads?

- $1$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The first piece of information excludes two tails. The second excludes all outcomes with heads on the first coin. Only tails-heads remains, so heads on the second coin is certain.
