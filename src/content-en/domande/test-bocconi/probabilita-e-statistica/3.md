A sequence of five symbols is made using $A,B,C$. Symbols may be repeated, but neighboring positions must differ. How many sequences are possible?

- $48$
- $32$
- $120$
- $243$

## Explanation

Any of the three symbols may occupy the first position. At each of the following four positions only the previous symbol is prohibited. The product is therefore $3\cdot2^4=48$.
