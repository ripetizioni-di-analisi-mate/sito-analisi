For the number of three-person committees that can be formed from ten candidates, which equality is true?

- $\dbinom{10}{3}=\dbinom{10}{4}$
- $\dbinom{10}{3}=10\cdot9\cdot8$
- $\dbinom{10}{3}=\dbinom{10}{7}$
- $\dbinom{10}{3}=2\dbinom{9}{3}$

## Explanation

Choosing the three committee members is equivalent to choosing the seven candidates left out, so $\dbinom{10}{3}=\dbinom{10}{7}$. Both numbers equal $\dfrac{10\cdot9\cdot8}{3!}=120$. The product $10\cdot9\cdot8=720$ counts committees with ordered roles, while $\dbinom{10}{4}=210$ and $2\dbinom93=168$ differ from $120$.
