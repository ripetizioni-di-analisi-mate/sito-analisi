Two independent fair coins are tossed. Let $A$ be heads on the first, $B$ heads on the second and $C$ the event that the two faces match. What is $P(A\cap B\cap C)$?

- $\dfrac12$
- $0$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac18$

## Explanation

The event $A\cap B$ is the single outcome of two heads. The faces already match in that outcome, so adding $C$ imposes no further restriction. Its probability is $(\dfrac{1}{2})^2=\dfrac{1}{4}$. Multiplying again by $P(C)$ would treat $C$ as independent of the intersection.
