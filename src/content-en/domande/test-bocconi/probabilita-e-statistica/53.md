A traveller arrives at a uniformly distributed time between 8:00 and 8:20. Buses leave at 8:05 and 8:20. What is the probability that the wait is at most three minutes?

- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac3{20}$
- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

The traveller may arrive in the three minutes before 8:05 or the three minutes before 8:20. These disjoint intervals cover $6$ of the $20$ possible minutes. The probability is $\dfrac{6}{20}=\dfrac{3}{10}$.
