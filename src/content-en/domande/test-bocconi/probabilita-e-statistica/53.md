A team of three people is formed from eight volunteers, and one of the three is appointed team leader. The other two members have no roles. How many different teams, each with its leader, can be formed?

- $56$
- $168$
- $336$
- $280$

## Explanation

The three team members, without roles, can be chosen in $\dbinom83=56$ ways. For each group of three, the leader is chosen from its members in $3$ ways. By the multiplication rule there are therefore $56\cdot3=168$ teams. The number $D_{8,3}=336$ would assign a different role to each of the three members.
