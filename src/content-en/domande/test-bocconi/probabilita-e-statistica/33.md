One quarter of orders have express delivery. A fraction $\dfrac15$ of express orders and $\dfrac35$ of other orders are late. An order is chosen uniformly from those that are late. What is the probability that it has express delivery?

- $\dfrac{1}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

Late express orders have weight $\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac15=\dfrac1{20}$. Other late orders have weight $\dfrac34\cdot\dfrac35=\dfrac9{20}$. Of ten expected late orders per twenty orders, one is express. The conditional probability is $\dfrac1{10}$.
