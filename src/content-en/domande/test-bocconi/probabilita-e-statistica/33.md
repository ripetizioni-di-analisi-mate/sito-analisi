A library receives $9$ new reviews. Each assigns one whole-number score from $1$ to $4$. What is the largest number of reviews with the same score that can be guaranteed before seeing the results?

- $2$
- $5$
- $4$
- $3$

## Explanation

At most two reviews per score would account for only $4\cdot2=8$ reviews, so at least three must share a score. A distribution of $3,2,2,2$ is possible, showing that four cannot be guaranteed.
