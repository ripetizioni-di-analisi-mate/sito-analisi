A drawing has three vertical and four horizontal lines, all distinct. How many rectangles have all four sides lying on these lines?

- $6$
- $18$
- $12$
- $24$

## Explanation

Two vertical lines determine the left and right sides in $\binom32=3$ ways. Two horizontal lines give the top and bottom in $\binom42=6$ ways. Each pairing defines one rectangle, so there are $3\cdot6=18$.
