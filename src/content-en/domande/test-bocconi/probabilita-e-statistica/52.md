Four-digit numbers with all different digits are formed from the digits $1,2,3,4,5$. How many of these numbers are greater than $3000$?

- $120$
- $48$
- $96$
- $72$

## Explanation

A four-digit number formed from these digits exceeds $3000$ if and only if its first digit is $3$, $4$, or $5$. The first digit therefore has three choices. The three remaining positions are filled with the four remaining digits, without repetition, in $4\cdot3\cdot2=24$ ways. Hence there are $3\cdot24=72$ numbers.
