All letters in $AABBC$ are rearranged. How many distinct arrangements have the two copies of $B$ in nonadjacent positions?

- $12$
- $18$
- $24$
- $30$

## Explanation

The total number of arrangements is $\dfrac{5!}{2!\,2!}=30$. Adjacent copies of $B$ form one block, so the four items, including identical copies of $A$, can be ordered in $\dfrac{4!}{2!}=12$ ways. This leaves $30-12=18$ arrangements.
