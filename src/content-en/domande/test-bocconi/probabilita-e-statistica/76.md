The mean of $25$ recorded amounts is $48$ euros. An amount of $62$ euros was mistakenly entered as $26$ euros. What is the corrected mean?

- $46.56$ euros
- $49.20$ euros
- $49.44$ euros
- $84$ euros

## Explanation

The correction increases the total by $62-26=36$ euros while leaving the number of observations unchanged. The mean therefore rises by $\dfrac{36}{25}=1.44$ euros. The corrected mean is $48+1.44=49.44$ euros.
