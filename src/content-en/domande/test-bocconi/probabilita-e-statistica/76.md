A disease affects $10\%$ of a population. A test is positive in $90\%$ of the sick and in $10\%$ of the healthy. A person from the population, chosen at random, tests positive. What is the probability that the person is sick?

- $\dfrac{9}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{9}{100}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The probability of being sick and testing positive is $0.1\cdot0.9=0.09$. The probability of being healthy and testing positive is $0.9\cdot0.1=0.09$. By Bayes' rule, the probability of being sick given a positive test is therefore $\dfrac{0.09}{0.09+0.09}=\dfrac12$.
