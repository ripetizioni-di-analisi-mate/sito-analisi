Marta scores $74$ on a test with group mean $62$ and standard deviation $6$. On another test she scores $90$, with mean $75$ and standard deviation $10$. On which test is her advantage above the mean larger in standard-deviation units?

- On the first, at $2$ standard deviations.
- On the second, at $1.5$ standard deviations.
- The standardised advantages are equal.
- On the second, at $15$ standard deviations.

## Explanation

Divide the difference from the mean by the corresponding group standard deviation. The first test gives $\dfrac{74-62}{6}=2$, while the second gives $\dfrac{90-75}{10}=1.5$. The first standardised score is higher.
