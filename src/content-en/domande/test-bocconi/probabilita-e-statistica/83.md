Two hundred students in a course are classified by year of enrollment and by place of residence.

| Year | Commuter | Resident | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| First | $45$ | $75$ | $120$ |
| Second | $35$ | $45$ | $80$ |
| Total | $80$ | $120$ | $200$ |

Which statement is true?

- More than half of the commuters are in their first year
- More than half of the first-year students are commuters
- The share of commuters is higher in the first year than in the second
- Commuters make up $45\%$ of the students

## Explanation

Of the $80$ commuters, $45$ are in their first year, and $\dfrac{45}{80}$ is greater than $\dfrac12$. Among the $120$ first-year students, by contrast, commuters make up $\dfrac{45}{120}=37.5\%$. In the second year the share of commuters is $\dfrac{35}{80}=43.75\%$, so it is higher than in the first. Commuters are $\dfrac{80}{200}=40\%$ of the total.
