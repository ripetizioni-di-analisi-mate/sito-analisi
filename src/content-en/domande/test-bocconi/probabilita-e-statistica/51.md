How many different sequences are obtained by rearranging all the letters of the word BANANA?

- $60$
- $720$
- $120$
- $360$

## Explanation

The word has six letters, with $A$ appearing three times, $N$ twice, and $B$ once. The number of permutations with indistinguishable items is therefore
$$
\frac{6!}{3!\,2!\,1!}=\frac{720}{12}=60 .
$$
