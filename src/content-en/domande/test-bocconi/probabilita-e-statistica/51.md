A final between $A$ and $B$ ends as soon as either team wins three games. Games cannot be drawn. In how many sequences of results does $A$ win the final on exactly the fourth game?

- $3$
- $6$
- $8$
- $4$

## Explanation

Game four must be won by $A$. In the first three games $A$ must have exactly two wins and $B$ one, or the final would already have ended or could not end in game four. The single $B$ win can occupy three positions.
