A component passes a test with probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Three components are tested independently. What is the probability that exactly two pass?

- $\dfrac{20}{27}$
- $\dfrac8{27}$
- $\dfrac49$
- $\dfrac4{27}$

## Explanation

A prescribed sequence of two passes and one failure has probability $(\dfrac{2}{3})^2(\dfrac{1}{3})=\dfrac{4}{27}$. The failure can occupy any of three positions. Adding those cases gives $\dfrac{12}{27}=\dfrac{4}{9}$.
