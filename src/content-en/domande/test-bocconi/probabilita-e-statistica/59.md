A set has six elements. How many of its subsets contain at least two elements?

- $57$
- $64$
- $63$
- $15$

## Explanation

A subset is built by deciding, for each of the six elements, whether to include it, so there are $2^6=64$ subsets. Those with fewer than two elements are the empty set and the $6$ one-element subsets. Hence the required number of subsets is $64-1-6=57$.
