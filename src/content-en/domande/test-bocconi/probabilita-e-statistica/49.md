A code consists of one, two, or three letters chosen from $A$, $B$, and $C$, and letters may repeat. How many different codes can be formed?

- $27$
- $39$
- $15$
- $12$

## Explanation

Each position of a code has three choices, because letters may repeat. Hence there are $3$ one-letter codes, $3^2=9$ two-letter codes, and $3^3=27$ three-letter codes. Codes of different lengths are different, so the three cases are disjoint and the total is $3+9+27=39$.
