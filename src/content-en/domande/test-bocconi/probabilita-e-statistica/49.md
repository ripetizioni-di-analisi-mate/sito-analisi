Three different name labels are returned to three corresponding cases, one label per case. In how many ways does no label end up in its own case?

- $6$
- $3$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The first label can go into either of the other two cases. Once that is chosen, the remaining assignments are forced to avoid a correct match. The only sequences of labels in the cases are $B,C,A$ and $C,A,B$.
