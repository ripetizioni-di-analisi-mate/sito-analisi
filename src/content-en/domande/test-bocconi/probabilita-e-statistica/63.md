Three representatives are drawn from a class of twelve students, and all groups of three are equally likely. What is the probability that Marta, a student in the class, is among those drawn?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

There are $\dbinom{12}{3}=220$ possible groups. A group containing Marta is obtained by choosing the other two members from her eleven classmates, in $\dbinom{11}{2}=55$ ways. Hence the probability is $\dfrac{55}{220}=\dfrac14$, that is, three places for twelve students.
