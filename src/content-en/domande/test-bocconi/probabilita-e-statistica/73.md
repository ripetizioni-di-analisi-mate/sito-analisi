Two readers separately classify the same $20$ texts as suitable or unsuitable. Each labels $10$ texts suitable. Their labels agree on $14$ texts. How many does the first reader label suitable and the second unsuitable?

- $3$
- $6$
- $7$
- $10$

## Explanation

There are $20-14=6$ disagreements. The number accepted only by the first reader must equal the number accepted only by the second: each starts with the same jointly accepted texts and ends with the same total of $10$. Each disagreement category therefore contains $3$ texts.
