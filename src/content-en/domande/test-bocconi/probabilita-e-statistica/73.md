A fair die is rolled repeatedly. What is the smallest number of rolls for which the probability of getting at least one $6$ exceeds $\dfrac12$?

- $3$
- $4$
- $6$
- $2$

## Explanation

With $n$ independent rolls the probability of getting no $6$ is $\left(\dfrac56\right)^n$, so we need the smallest $n$ with $\left(\dfrac56\right)^n<\dfrac12$. For $n=3$ this gives $\dfrac{125}{216}$, which is greater than $\dfrac{108}{216}=\dfrac12$. For $n=4$ it gives $\dfrac{625}{1296}$, which is less than $\dfrac{648}{1296}=\dfrac12$. Therefore at least $4$ rolls are needed.
