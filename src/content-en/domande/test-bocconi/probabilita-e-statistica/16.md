An archive contains $14$ documents in one folder and $11$ in another. Four documents appear in both. How many distinct documents are in the two folders?

- $17$
- $25$
- $21$
- $29$

## Explanation

The sum $14+11$ includes each common document twice. Subtracting the four extra copies gives $14+11-4=21$.
