Three people state the day of the week on which they were born. Assume that the seven days are equally likely and that the three people's days are independent. What is the probability that the three days are all different?

- $\dfrac{19}{49}$
- $\dfrac{30}{49}$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{49}$

## Explanation

There are $7^3=343$ ordered triples of days, all equally likely. There are $7\cdot6\cdot5=210$ triples with all days different, because each person must avoid the days of the previous ones. Hence the probability is $\dfrac{210}{343}=\dfrac{30}{49}$.
