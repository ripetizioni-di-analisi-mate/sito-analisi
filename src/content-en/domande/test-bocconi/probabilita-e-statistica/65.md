Eight people, including Anna and Leo, are assigned at random to four pairs, with all pairings equally likely. What is the probability that Anna and Leo are paired together?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac27$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac17$

## Explanation

With Anna fixed, each of the other seven people is equally likely to be her partner because exchanging names preserves the number of pairings. Only one of those seven possible partners is Leo. The probability is therefore $\dfrac{1}{7}$.
