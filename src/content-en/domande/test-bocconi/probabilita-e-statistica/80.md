An urn contains three red balls and two white balls. A ball is drawn, its color is noted, and it is put back into the urn together with another ball of the same color. Then a second ball is drawn. What is the probability that the second ball is red?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

If the first ball is red, an event of probability $\dfrac35$, the urn holds four red balls out of six and the second ball is red with probability $\dfrac46$. If the first ball is white, an event of probability $\dfrac25$, the urn holds three red balls out of six and the second ball is red with probability $\dfrac36$. By the law of total probability,
$$
\frac35\cdot\frac46+\frac25\cdot\frac36=\frac25+\frac15=\frac35 .
$$
