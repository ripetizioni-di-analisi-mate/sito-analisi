Eight ordered delivery times are $1,2,4,6,7,9,10,15$ days. Define the quartiles as the medians of the lower and upper groups of four observations. What is the interquartile range?

- $14$ days
- $6$ days
- $7.5$ days
- $6.5$ days

## Explanation

The two middle values in the lower half are $2$ and $4$, so $Q_1=3$. In the upper half they are $9$ and $10$, so $Q_3=9.5$. The interquartile range is $Q_3-Q_1=6.5$ days.
