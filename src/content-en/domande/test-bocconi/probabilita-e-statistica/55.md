Five people, including Anna and Bruno, stand in a line. In how many orders is Anna first or Bruno last?

- $42$
- $48$
- $36$
- $24$

## Explanation

There are $4!=24$ orders with Anna first, because the other four people can be arranged freely. Similarly, there are $24$ orders with Bruno last. The $3!=6$ orders with Anna first and Bruno last appear in both counts. Therefore the required number of orders is $24+24-6=42$.
