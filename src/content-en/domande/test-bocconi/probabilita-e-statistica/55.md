Two simulators choose a point in a disc of radius $R>0$. The first samples uniformly by area. The second samples the distance from the centre uniformly from $0$ to $R$ and chooses the angle independently. In that order, what are their probabilities of landing within half the radius?

- $\dfrac12$ and $\dfrac12$.
- $\dfrac12$ and $\dfrac14$.
- $\dfrac14$ and $\dfrac14$.
- $\dfrac14$ and $\dfrac12$.

## Explanation

The first simulator uses an area ratio: halving the radius reduces the area to one quarter. The second uses the length of the interval of radii: distances from zero to half the radius occupy half the possible interval.
