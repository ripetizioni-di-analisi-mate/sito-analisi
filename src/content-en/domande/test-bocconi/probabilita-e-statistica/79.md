A survey records one means of transport per person: $12$ bicycles, $9$ buses, $8$ trams and $14$ cars. After combining buses and trams into “public transport”, which category is the mode?

- Cars and bicycles jointly.
- Cars.
- Public transport.
- Bicycles.

## Explanation

Bus and tram were disjoint categories, so the new frequency is $9+8=17$. The other frequencies remain $12$ and $14$. Public transport is thus the most frequent category.
