Three archers each shoot one arrow, independently of one another. They hit the target with probabilities $\dfrac12$, $\dfrac13$, and $\dfrac14$. What is the probability that the target is hit at least once?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{24}$
- $\dfrac{23}{24}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The archers miss the target with probabilities $\dfrac12$, $\dfrac23$, and $\dfrac34$. Since the shots are independent, the probability that all three miss is $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac34=\dfrac14$. The event “at least one hit” is the complement, so its probability is $1-\dfrac14=\dfrac34$.
