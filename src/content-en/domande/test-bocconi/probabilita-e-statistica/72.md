Exactly $\dfrac{3}{8}$ of a group use a service, and exactly $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of those users use it daily. What is the smallest possible size of the group?

- $24$
- $8$
- $20$
- $40$

## Explanation

The total size must be $8k$ to give a whole number $3k$ of users. Then $3k$ must be divisible by $5$, so $k$ must be a multiple of $5$. The minimum $k=5$ gives $40$ members, $15$ users and $6$ daily users.
