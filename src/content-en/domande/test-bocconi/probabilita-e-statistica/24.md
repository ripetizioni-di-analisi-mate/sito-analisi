Two disjoint pairs are selected from six people. Order within each pair does not matter, and the two pairs are unlabeled. How many selections are possible?

- $30$
- $60$
- $45$
- $90$

## Explanation

Temporarily labeling the pairs gives $\binom62\binom42=15\cdot6=90$ outcomes. Swapping the labels leaves the selected pairs unchanged. Each selection is counted twice, giving $45$.
