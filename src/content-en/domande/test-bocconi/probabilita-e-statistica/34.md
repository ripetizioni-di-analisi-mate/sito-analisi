One quarter of complaints come from physical stores, and $50\%$ of them are upheld. Overall, $20\%$ of all complaints are upheld. What percentage of online complaints, which make up the rest, are upheld?

- $7.5\%$
- $12.5\%$
- $20\%$
- $10\%$

## Explanation

Upheld store complaints account for $12.5\%$ of all complaints. Upheld online complaints therefore contribute $7.5\%$ of the total. Online complaints account for $75\%$, so their rate of being upheld is $\dfrac{7.5}{75}=10\%$.
