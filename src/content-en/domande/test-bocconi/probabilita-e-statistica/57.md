A prize goes to one of $10$ people on a list. Each of the first two is three times as likely to win as each of the other eight. What is the probability that one of the first two wins?

- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac37$

## Explanation

Give weight $1$ to each of the eight ordinary entries and weight $3$ to each of the first two. The total weight is $8+2\cdot3=14$. The first two account for weight $6$, so their probability is $\dfrac{6}{14}=\dfrac{3}{7}$.
