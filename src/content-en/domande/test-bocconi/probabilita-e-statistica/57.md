How many three-digit natural numbers have strictly increasing digits from left to right, like $258$?

- $120$
- $84$
- $504$
- $720$

## Explanation

The digit $0$ cannot appear, because it would be the smallest digit and would therefore have to occupy the first position. Every group of three distinct digits chosen from $1$ to $9$ can be arranged in increasing order in exactly one way. So the numbers correspond to the groups, and there are $\dbinom93=84$ of them.
