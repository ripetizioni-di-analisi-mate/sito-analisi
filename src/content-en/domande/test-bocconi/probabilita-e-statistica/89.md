Four data sets all have mean $5$ and range $8$. Which one has the greatest standard deviation?

- $1,3,7,9$
- $1,1,9,9$
- $1,5,5,9$
- $1,2,8,9$

## Explanation

The sets have the same mean and the same number of values, so it suffices to compare the sums of squared deviations from $5$. These are $16+4+4+16=40$ for $1,3,7,9$, $16+16+16+16=64$ for $1,1,9,9$, $16+0+0+16=32$ for $1,5,5,9$, and $16+9+9+16=50$ for $1,2,8,9$. The largest sum belongs to $1,1,9,9$, because all of its values lie $4$ from the mean.
