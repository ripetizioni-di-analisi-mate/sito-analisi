A chart splits $300$ responses into three exclusive categories with $100$ responses each. Percentages in the legend are rounded to the nearest whole percent. What should the three printed labels add up to?

- $101\,\%$
- $99\,\%$
- $90\,\%$
- $100\,\%$

## Explanation

Each category makes up exactly $\dfrac{100}{300}=\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the responses, or $33.\overline3\,\%$. Each printed label therefore reads $33\,\%$. They sum to $99\,\%$, even though the categories cover all the responses.
