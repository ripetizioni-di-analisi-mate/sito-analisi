At a college, $600$ students attend daytime classes and $200$ attend evening classes. A sample of $80$ is to use the same sampling fraction in each group. How many evening students should be selected?

- $10$
- $60$
- $20$
- $40$

## Explanation

The college has $800$ students and the sample is $\dfrac{80}{800}=\dfrac{1}{10}$ of the total. The same fraction must be applied to the $200$ evening students. Hence $\dfrac{200}{10}=20$ are selected.
