A caretaker has four keys, and only one opens the door. He tries them one at a time in random order, without retrying keys already discarded. What is the probability that the door opens on exactly the third attempt?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{9}{64}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The first attempt fails with probability $\dfrac34$. After one failure three keys remain, one of them correct, so the second attempt also fails with probability $\dfrac23$. At the third attempt two keys remain, so the correct key is chosen with probability $\dfrac12$. By the multiplication rule the probability is $\dfrac34\cdot\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac14$.
