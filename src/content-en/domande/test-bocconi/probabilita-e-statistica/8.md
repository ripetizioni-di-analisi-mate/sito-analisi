For two events $A,B$, $P(A)=P(B)=45\%$. The probability that neither event occurs is $20\%$. What is $P(A\cap B)$?

- $5\%$
- $20\%$
- $10\%$
- $25\%$

## Explanation

The complement of “neither” is the union, with probability $80\%$. The union formula subtracts the intersection, so the intersection is $45\%+45\%-80\%=10\%$.
