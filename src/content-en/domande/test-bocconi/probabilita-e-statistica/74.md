No outcome makes the events $A$ and $B$ occur together, and their probabilities are $P(A)=0.3$ and $P(B)=0.4$. Which statement is true?

- $P(A\cup B)=0.58$
- $P(A\mid B)=0.3$
- $A$ and $B$ are not independent
- $P(A^c\cap B^c)=0.42$

## Explanation

The events are disjoint, so $P(A\cap B)=0$. Since $P(A)P(B)=0.12$ is not $0$, the events are not independent. Moreover, $P(A\cup B)=0.3+0.4=0.7$, so $P(A^c\cap B^c)=1-0.7=0.3$, and $P(A\mid B)=\dfrac{0}{0.4}=0$.
