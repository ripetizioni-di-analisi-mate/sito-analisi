An archive records $18$ cases completed within $2$ days, $47$ within $5$ days and $59$ within $9$ days. How many were completed after day two but by day five?

- $12$
- $29$
- $47$
- $41$

## Explanation

The counts are cumulative. The $47$ cases completed by day five include the $18$ already completed by day two. Subtracting gives $47-18=29$ cases in the requested interval.
