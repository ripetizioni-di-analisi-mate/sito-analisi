Eight parcels are grouped into two classes. Three weigh between $2$ and $4$ kilograms inclusive and the other five between $6$ and $8$ kilograms inclusive. What is the smallest mean weight consistent with this information alone?

- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $5.5\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The minimum occurs when each parcel is at the lower endpoint of its class, which is allowed here. The smallest sum is $3\cdot2+5\cdot6=36$ kilograms. Dividing by $8$ gives $4.5$ kilograms.
