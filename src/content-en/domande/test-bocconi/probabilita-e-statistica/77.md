A fair die is rolled. If it shows $1$ or $2$, a ball is drawn from an urn with one red ball and one blue ball; otherwise it is drawn from an urn with one red ball and seven blue balls. What is the probability of drawing a red ball?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{16}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$

## Explanation

The first urn is used with probability $\dfrac13$ and gives a red ball with probability $\dfrac12$. The second urn is used with probability $\dfrac23$ and gives a red ball with probability $\dfrac18$. By the law of total probability,
$$
\frac13\cdot\frac12+\frac23\cdot\frac18=\frac16+\frac1{12}=\frac14 .
$$
