A fair coin is tossed four times. Which of the following events has the highest probability?

- At least three heads
- The sequence heads, heads, tails, tails, in that order
- All four tosses give the same result
- Exactly two heads

## Explanation

There are $2^4=16$ sequences of four tosses, all equally likely. There are $\dbinom42=6$ sequences with exactly two heads. There are $\dbinom43+\dbinom44=5$ with at least three heads, $2$ with all results equal, and $1$ fixed sequence. So the most likely event is “exactly two heads,” with probability $\dfrac{6}{16}=\dfrac38$.
