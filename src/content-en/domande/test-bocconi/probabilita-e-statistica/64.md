A point is chosen uniformly on the real segment $[0,1]$. Which of the following events has probability zero despite having possible outcomes?

- The point differs from $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- The point is below $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- The point belongs to $[\dfrac{1}{4},\dfrac{1}{2}]$.
- The point is exactly $\dfrac{1}{2}$.

## Explanation

For a continuous uniform distribution the probability of an interval equals its length. The singleton containing $\dfrac{1}{2}$ has zero length while still containing that point. Half the segment has probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, excluding just one point leaves probability $1$, and the interval from $\dfrac{1}{4}$ to $\dfrac{1}{2}$ has probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$.
