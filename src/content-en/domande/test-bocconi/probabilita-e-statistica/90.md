All pieces in one box weigh $10$ grams and all pieces in another weigh $14$ grams. The boxes contain the same positive number of pieces. What is the population variance after the pieces are pooled?

- $0\,\mathrm g^2$
- $2\,\mathrm g^2$
- $16\,\mathrm g^2$
- $4\,\mathrm g^2$

## Explanation

The boxes have equal statistical weight, so the combined mean is $\dfrac{10+14}{2}=12$ grams. Each piece lies $2$ grams from the mean and has squared deviation $4$. Averaging squared deviations that all equal $4$ yields $4$ grams squared.
