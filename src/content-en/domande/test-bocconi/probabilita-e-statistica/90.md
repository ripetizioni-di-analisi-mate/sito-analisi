Two dice are rolled and the two scores are recorded without regard to which die gave them, for example “$2$ and $5$” or “$3$ and $3$.” How many different results can be recorded?

- $18$
- $36$
- $21$
- $15$

## Explanation

There are $\dbinom62=15$ results with two different scores, because it suffices to choose the two values from the six possible ones. There are $6$ results with two equal scores, one for each value. Therefore there are $15+6=21$ different results. The quotient $\dfrac{36}{2}=18$ gives a wrong count, because a result with equal scores corresponds to only one ordered pair, not to two.
