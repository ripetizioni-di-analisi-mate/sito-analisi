What is the ordered pair $(z_x,z_y)$ at the origin for $F=0$ if $F$ is given by $5 x + 2 y + e^{3 z} - 1$?

- $\left( - \dfrac{2}{3}, \  - \dfrac{5}{3}\right)$
- $\left( - \dfrac{5}{3}, \  - \dfrac{2}{3}\right)$
- $\left( -5, \  -2\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{5}{3}, \  \dfrac{2}{3}\right)$

## Explanation

The derivatives of $F$ at the origin are $\left( 5, \  2, \  3\right)$. Divide each of the first two components by the negative of the last one.
