What is the pair $(z_x,z_y)$ at $(1,1,1)$ on the level surface $8$ of $x^{2} + 2 y^{2} + 5 z^{2}$?

- $\left( \dfrac{1}{5}, \  \dfrac{2}{5}\right)$
- $\left( - \dfrac{1}{2}, \  - \dfrac{5}{2}\right)$
- $\left( - \dfrac{1}{5}, \  - \dfrac{2}{5}\right)$
- $\left( - \dfrac{2}{5}, \  - \dfrac{1}{5}\right)$

## Explanation

The gradient at the point is $\left( 2, \  4, \  10\right)$. Its third component is nonzero and determines both negative ratios.
