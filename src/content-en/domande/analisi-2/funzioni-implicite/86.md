Which affine function of $x,y$ describes the tangent plane at the origin to $F=0$ if $F$ is given by $x^{2} + 4 x + 2 y + 7 z$?

- $- 4 x - 2 y$
- $\dfrac{4 x}{7} + \dfrac{2 y}{7}$
- $- \dfrac{4 x}{7} + \dfrac{2 y}{7}$
- $- \dfrac{4 x}{7} - \dfrac{2 y}{7}$

## Explanation

The tangent plane is obtained by setting the differential of $F$ at the origin to zero. The quadratic term has zero differential there.
