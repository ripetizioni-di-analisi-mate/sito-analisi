What is the tangent line at the origin to the curve $2x - 3y + x^3 + x y^2 + y^3 = 0$?

- $y = \dfrac{3}{2}\,x$
- $y = \dfrac{2}{3}\,x$
- $y = -\dfrac{2}{3}\,x$
- $x = 0$

## Explanation

Let $F(x, y) = 2x - 3y + x^3 + x y^2 + y^3$; the origin lies on the curve. The partial derivatives of the terms of degree at least $2$ vanish at the origin, so $\nabla F(0, 0) = (2, -3)$, made of the coefficients of the first-degree terms. The tangent line is therefore
$$
2(x - 0) - 3(y - 0) = 0 ,
$$
that is, $y = \dfrac{2}{3}\,x$. At the origin, therefore, the tangent line is obtained by setting the **linear part** of the polynomial equal to zero.
