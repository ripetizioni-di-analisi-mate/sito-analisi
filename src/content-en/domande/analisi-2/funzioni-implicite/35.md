What is the tangent plane to the surface $x^2 + 2y^2 - z^2 = 2$ at the point $(1, 1, 1)$?

- $x + 2y + z = 4$
- $x + 2y - z = 0$
- $x + 2y - z = 2$
- $2x + 4y - 2z = 2$

## Explanation

Let $F(x, y, z) = x^2 + 2y^2 - z^2 - 2$, of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^3$; we have $F(1, 1, 1) = 1 + 2 - 1 - 2 = 0$. The partial derivatives are $F_x = 2x$, $F_y = 4y$ and $F_z = -2z$, so $\nabla F(1, 1, 1) = (2, 4, -2) \neq (0, 0, 0)$. The tangent plane therefore has equation
$$
2(x - 1) + 4(y - 1) - 2(z - 1) = 0 ,
$$
that is, $2x + 4y - 2z = 4$. Dividing by $2$ we obtain $x + 2y - z = 2$.
