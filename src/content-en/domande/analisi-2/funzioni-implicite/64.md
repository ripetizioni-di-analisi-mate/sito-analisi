What is $x^{\prime\prime}(0)$ for the branch $x=x(y)$ through $(0,0)$ satisfying $F=0$ with $F$ given by $3 x + 2 y^{3} + 5 y^{2}$?

- $- \dfrac{10}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{5}{3}$
- $-4$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}$

## Explanation

Solve for $x$ by dividing the polynomial in $y$ by $-3$. The second derivative at zero is twice the coefficient of $y^2$.
