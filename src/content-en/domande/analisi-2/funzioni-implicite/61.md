For the branch $y=y(x)$ through $(0,0)$, what is $y^{\prime}(0)$ when $F=0$ with $F$ given by $3 x^{2} + 2 x + y^{2} + 5 y$?

- $-2$
- $- \dfrac{2}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

Differentiating $F(x,y(x))=0$ gives $F_x+F_y y^\prime=0$. At the origin the coefficients are $2$ and $5$.
