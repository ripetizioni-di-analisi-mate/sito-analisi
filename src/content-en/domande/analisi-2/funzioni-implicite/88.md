What is $y^\prime(0)$ for the solution of the system $4y+z=\sin x,\quad y+2z=7x$?

- $- \dfrac{5}{7}$
- $\dfrac{27}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}$

## Explanation

Differentiate at zero to obtain the same linear system with right-hand side $\left( 1, \  7\right)$. Eliminating $z^\prime$ gives $- \dfrac{5}{7}$.
