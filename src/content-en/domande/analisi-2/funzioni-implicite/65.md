On the cone $x^2 + y^2 - z^2 = 0$, near the point $(3, 4, 5)$, the coordinate $z$ is a function $z = g(x, y)$ given by the implicit function theorem. What is $x\,g_x + y\,g_y$ at the point $(3, 4)$?

- $1$
- $25$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{7}{5}$

## Explanation

Let $F(x, y, z) = x^2 + y^2 - z^2$, with $F(3, 4, 5) = 0$ and $F_z(3, 4, 5) = -2z \,\big|_{(3,4,5)} = -10 \neq 0$. The partial derivatives of $g$ are $g_x = -\dfrac{2x}{-2z} = \dfrac{x}{z}$ and $g_y = \dfrac{y}{z}$, so at $(3, 4)$ they equal $\dfrac{3}{5}$ and $\dfrac{4}{5}$. Therefore
$$
3 \cdot \frac{3}{5} + 4 \cdot \frac{4}{5} = \frac{25}{5} = 5 ,
$$
which coincides with $g(3, 4) = 5$, as expected since $g$ is homogeneous of degree $1$.
