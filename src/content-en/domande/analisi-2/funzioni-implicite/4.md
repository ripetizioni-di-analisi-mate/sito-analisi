Near $(0,1)$, the curve $F(x,y)=\ln(x+y)+xy=0$ is approximated by its tangent line $y=1-2x$. What is the leading nonzero term of the residual $F(x,1-2x)$ as $x\to0$?

- $\dfrac52x^2$
- $-2x$
- $-\dfrac52x^2$
- $-\dfrac12x^2$

## Explanation

On the tangent, $F(x,1-2x)=\ln(1-x)+x-2x^2$. The expansion $\ln(1-x)=-x-\dfrac{x^2}{2}+O(x^3)$ cancels the linear term, as it must along the tangent. The two quadratic terms add to give $-\dfrac{5x^2}{2}$.
