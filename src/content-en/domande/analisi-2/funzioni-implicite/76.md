For the branch $y=y(x)$ through $(0,0)$, what is $y^{\prime}(0)$ when $F=0$ with $F$ given by $4 x^{2} + 7 x + y^{2} + 2 y$?

- $-7$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{7}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{2}{7}$

## Explanation

Differentiating $F(x,y(x))=0$ gives $F_x+F_y y^\prime=0$. At the origin the coefficients are $7$ and $2$.
