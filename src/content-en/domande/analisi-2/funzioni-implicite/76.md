What angle does the tangent plane to the sphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 2$ at the point $(1, 0, 1)$ form with the plane $z = 0$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$

## Explanation

Let $F(x, y, z) = x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - 2$, with $\nabla F(1, 0, 1) = (2, 0, 2)$, so the tangent plane has normal vector $n = (1, 0, 1)$. The angle between two planes is the angle between their normal vectors, and the plane $z = 0$ has normal vector $k = (0, 0, 1)$. Since
$$
\cos \theta = \frac{n \cdot k}{\|n\|\,\|k\|} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} ,
$$
the angle is $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
