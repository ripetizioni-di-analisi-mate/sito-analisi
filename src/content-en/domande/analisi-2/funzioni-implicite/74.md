Which of the following points lies on the tangent plane to the cone $x^2 + y^2 = 4z^2$ at the point $(2, 0, 1)$?

- $(0, 0, 0)$
- $(2, 0, 0)$
- $(0, 0, 1)$
- $(1, 1, 1)$

## Explanation

Let $F(x, y, z) = x^2 + y^2 - 4z^2$, with $F(2, 0, 1) = 0$ and $\nabla F = (2x, 2y, -8z)$, so $\nabla F(2, 0, 1) = (4, 0, -8)$. The tangent plane is $4(x - 2) - 8(z - 1) = 0$, that is, $x - 2z = 0$. The point $(0, 0, 0)$ satisfies the equation, while at $(2, 0, 0)$, $(0, 0, 1)$ and $(1, 1, 1)$ the left-hand side equals $2$, $-2$ and $-1$. The tangent plane therefore contains the **vertex** of the cone, as happens at every point of the cone other than the vertex.
