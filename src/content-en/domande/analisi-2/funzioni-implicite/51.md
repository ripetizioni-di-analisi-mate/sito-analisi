What is the ordered pair $(z_x,z_y)$ at the origin for $F=0$ if $F$ is given by $3 x + 5 y + e^{2 z} - 1$?

- $\left( \dfrac{3}{2}, \  \dfrac{5}{2}\right)$
- $\left( - \dfrac{5}{2}, \  - \dfrac{3}{2}\right)$
- $\left( -3, \  -5\right)$
- $\left( - \dfrac{3}{2}, \  - \dfrac{5}{2}\right)$

## Explanation

The derivatives of $F$ at the origin are $\left( 3, \  5, \  2\right)$. Divide each of the first two components by the negative of the last one.
