What is $y^\prime(0)$ for the positive branch of $y^2+2xy+3x=25$ through $(0,5)$?

- $- \dfrac{13}{10}$
- $\dfrac{13}{10}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{10}$
- $-1$

## Explanation

At the point, the two partial derivatives of $F$ are $\left( 13, \  10\right)$. The derivative is their negative ratio.
