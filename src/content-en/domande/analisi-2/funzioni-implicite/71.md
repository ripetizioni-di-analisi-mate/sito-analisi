Which affine function of $x,y$ describes the tangent plane at the origin to $F=0$ if $F$ is given by $x^{2} + 3 x + 5 y + 2 z$?

- $- 3 x - 5 y$
- $\dfrac{3 x}{2} + \dfrac{5 y}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{3 x}{2} + \dfrac{5 y}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{3 x}{2} - \dfrac{5 y}{2}$

## Explanation

The tangent plane is obtained by setting the differential of $F$ at the origin to zero. The quadratic term has zero differential there.
