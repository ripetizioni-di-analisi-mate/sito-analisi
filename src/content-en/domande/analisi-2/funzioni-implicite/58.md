What is $y^\prime(0)$ for the solution of $2y+z=\sin x,\quad y+3z=5x$?

- $\dfrac{9}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Differentiate at zero to obtain the same coefficient matrix with right-hand side $\left( 1, \  5\right)$. Eliminating $z^\prime$ gives $- \dfrac{2}{5}$.
