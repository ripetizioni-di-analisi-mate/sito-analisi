A line passes through the point $(1, 1, 1)$ of the ellipsoid $x^2 + 2y^2 + 3z^2 = 6$ and is orthogonal to the tangent plane at that point. What are its parametric equations?

- $(x, y, z) = (t,\ 2t,\ 3t)$, $t \in \mathbb{R}$
- $(x, y, z) = (1 + t,\ 1 + 2t,\ 1 + 3t)$, $t \in \mathbb{R}$
- $(x, y, z) = (1 + t,\ 1 + t,\ 1 + t)$, $t \in \mathbb{R}$
- $(x, y, z) = (1 + 3t,\ 1 + 2t,\ 1 + t)$, $t \in \mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

Let $F(x, y, z) = x^2 + 2y^2 + 3z^2 - 6$, with $F(1, 1, 1) = 0$ and $\nabla F = (2x, 4y, 6z)$, so $\nabla F(1, 1, 1) = (2, 4, 6)$. The gradient is orthogonal to the tangent plane, therefore the normal line passes through $(1, 1, 1)$ with the direction of $(2, 4, 6)$, or of its multiple $(1, 2, 3)$. Its parametric equations are
$$
(x, y, z) = (1 + t,\ 1 + 2t,\ 1 + 3t), \qquad t \in \mathbb{R} .
$$
The line $(t, 2t, 3t)$ has the same direction but passes through the origin, not through the point.
