For the branch $y=y(x)$ through $(0,0)$, what is $y^{\prime}(0)$ when $F=0$ with $F$ given by $2 x^{2} + 5 x + y^{2} + 3 y$?

- $- \dfrac{3}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{5}{3}$
- $-5$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$

## Explanation

Differentiating $F(x,y(x))=0$ gives $F_x+F_y y^\prime=0$. At the origin the coefficients are $5$ and $3$.
