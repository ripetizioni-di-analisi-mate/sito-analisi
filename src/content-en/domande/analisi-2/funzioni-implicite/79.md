What is $x^{\prime\prime}(0)$ for the branch $x=x(y)$ through $(0,0)$ satisfying $F=0$ with $F$ given by $4 x + 7 y^{3} + 2 y^{2}$?

- $-1$
- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-14$
- $1$

## Explanation

Solve for $x$ by dividing the polynomial in $y$ by $-4$. The second derivative at zero is twice the coefficient of $y^2$.
