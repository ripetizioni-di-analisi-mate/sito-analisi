The point $(x_0, y_0, z_0)$ lies on the ellipsoid $\dfrac{x^2}{4} + \dfrac{y^2}{9} + z^2 = 1$. What is the equation of the tangent plane to the ellipsoid at that point?

- $\dfrac{x_0\,x}{4} + \dfrac{y_0\,y}{9} + z_0\,z = 0$
- $\dfrac{x_0\,x}{2} + \dfrac{2\,y_0\,y}{9} + 2\,z_0\,z = 1$
- $\dfrac{x_0\,x}{4} + \dfrac{y_0\,y}{9} + z_0\,z = 1$
- $\dfrac{x - x_0}{4} + \dfrac{y - y_0}{9} + (z - z_0) = 0$

## Explanation

Let $F(x, y, z) = \dfrac{x^2}{4} + \dfrac{y^2}{9} + z^2 - 1$, with $\nabla F = \left(\dfrac{x}{2}, \dfrac{2y}{9}, 2z\right)$, which never vanishes on the ellipsoid. The tangent plane at $(x_0, y_0, z_0)$ is
$$
\frac{x_0}{2}(x - x_0) + \frac{2y_0}{9}(y - y_0) + 2z_0(z - z_0) = 0 .
$$
Dividing by $2$ and moving the constant terms to the right gives $\dfrac{x_0\,x}{4} + \dfrac{y_0\,y}{9} + z_0\,z = \dfrac{x_0^2}{4} + \dfrac{y_0^2}{9} + z_0^2$. Since the point lies on the ellipsoid, the right-hand side equals $1$, therefore the plane is $\dfrac{x_0\,x}{4} + \dfrac{y_0\,y}{9} + z_0\,z = 1$.
