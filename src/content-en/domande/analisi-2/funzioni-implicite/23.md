What is the tangent line to the curve $x^3 - xy + y^2 = 7$ at the point $(2, 1)$?

- $y = 1$
- $11x + 2y = 24$
- $x = 2$
- $y = 11x - 21$

## Explanation

Let $F(x, y) = x^3 - xy + y^2 - 7$, of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$; we have $F(2, 1) = 8 - 2 + 1 - 7 = 0$. The partial derivatives are $F_x = 3x^2 - y$ and $F_y = -x + 2y$, so $\nabla F(2, 1) = (11, 0) \neq (0, 0)$. The tangent line has equation $11(x - 2) + 0 \cdot (y - 1) = 0$, that is, $x = 2$. Since $F_y(2, 1) = 0$ and $F_x(2, 1) \neq 0$, the tangent line is **vertical** and near the point the curve is the graph of a function $x = \psi(y)$.
