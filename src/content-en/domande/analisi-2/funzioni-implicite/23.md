The gradient of $F$ at $P$ is $(2,-1)$. Which replacement for the equation $F=0$ leaves the tangent line obtained from the gradient unchanged at $P$, given $F(P)=0$?

- $F^2=0$
- $F^3=0$
- $3F=0$
- $F^4=0$

## Explanation

Multiplying by $3$ scales the normal by a nonzero constant. For the listed powers the gradient is $kF^{k-1}\nabla F$, which vanishes at the point and cannot determine the tangent using this method.
