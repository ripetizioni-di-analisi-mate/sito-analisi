What is the coefficient of $x^2$ on the branch $y(0)=0$ of $F=0$, with $F$ given by $2 x^{2} + 3 x y + 5 y$?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{2}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

Collecting $y$ gives $- \dfrac{2 x^{2}}{3 x + 5}$. Divide by $x^2$ and take the limit to get $- \dfrac{2}{5}$.
