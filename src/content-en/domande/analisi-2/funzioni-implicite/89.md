On $4 x^{2} + 2 y^{2} + z^{2}=1$, how many points on the $x$ axis admit a local representation $z=z(x,y)$ by the implicit function theorem?

- $2$
- $0$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

Points on the $x$ axis have $z=0$. At both points $F_z=2z=0$, so the implicit function theorem condition for $z$ fails.
