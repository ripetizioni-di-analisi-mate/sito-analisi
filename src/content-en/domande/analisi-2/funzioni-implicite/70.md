What is the slope of the tangent to the level curve at level $8$, at the point $(1,1)$, for $3 x^{2} + 5 y^{2}$?

- $0$
- $- \dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

The gradient at the point is $\left( 6, \  10\right)$. The tangent slope is the negative of the first component divided by the second.
