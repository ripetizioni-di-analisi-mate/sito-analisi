What is $y^{\prime\prime}(0)$ for the solution $y(0)=0$ to $F=0$ if $F$ is given by $3 x^{2} + 2 y^{2} + 5 y$?

- $-6$
- $- \dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{6}{5}$

## Explanation

At the origin $y^\prime(0)=0$. After a second differentiation only $F_{xx}=6$ and the term $F_y y^{\prime\prime}$ remain.
