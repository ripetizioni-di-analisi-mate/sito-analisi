A regular curve can be written both as $y=\varphi(x)$ and as $x=\psi(y)$. If $\varphi'(2)=-3$ and $\varphi(2)=1$, find $\psi'(1)$.

- $-3$
- $\dfrac13$
- $-\dfrac13$
- $3$

## Explanation

The functions are local inverses. Differentiating $\psi(\varphi(x))=x$ at $x=2$ gives $\psi'(1)(-3)=1$.
