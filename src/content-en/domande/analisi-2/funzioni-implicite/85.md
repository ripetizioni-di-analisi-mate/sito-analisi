The system
$$
\begin{cases}
u + v = x \\[4pt]
uv = y
\end{cases}
$$
is satisfied at the point $(x, y, u, v) = (3, 2, 1, 2)$ and near it defines $u$ and $v$ as functions of $(x, y)$, which are the two roots of the polynomial $t^2 - xt + y$. What is $\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}(3, 2)$?

- $-1$
- $2$
- $1$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The Jacobian determinant with respect to $(u, v)$ is $\det \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ v & u \end{pmatrix} = u - v$, which at the point equals $1 - 2 = -1 \neq 0$, so the implicit function theorem for systems applies. Differentiating the two equations with respect to $x$ gives $u_x + v_x = 1$ and $v\,u_x + u\,v_x = 0$, that is, at the point, $2u_x + v_x = 0$. Subtracting the first equation from the second gives $u_x = -1$, and hence $v_x = 2$. Therefore
$$
\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}(3, 2) = -1 .
$$
