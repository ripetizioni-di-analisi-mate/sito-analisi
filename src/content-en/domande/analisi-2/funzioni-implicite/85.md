What is the slope of the tangent to the level curve at level $6$, at the point $(1,1)$, for $4 x^{2} + 2 y^{2}$?

- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-2$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

The gradient at the point is $\left( 8, \  4\right)$. The tangent slope is the negative of the first component divided by the second.
