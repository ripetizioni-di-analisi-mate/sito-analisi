What is the coefficient of $x^2$ on the branch $y(0)=0$ of $F=0$, with $F$ given by $4 x^{2} + 2 x y + 7 y$?

- $- \dfrac{2}{7}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $- \dfrac{4}{7}$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Factoring out $y$ gives $- \dfrac{4 x^{2}}{2 x + 7}$. Divide by $x^2$ and take the limit to get $- \dfrac{4}{7}$.
