What is the pair $(z_x,z_y)$ at $(1,1,1)$ on the level surface $12$ of $x^{2} + 4 y^{2} + 7 z^{2}$?

- $\left( - \dfrac{1}{4}, \  - \dfrac{7}{4}\right)$
- $\left( - \dfrac{4}{7}, \  - \dfrac{1}{7}\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{1}{7}, \  \dfrac{4}{7}\right)$
- $\left( - \dfrac{1}{7}, \  - \dfrac{4}{7}\right)$

## Explanation

The gradient at the point is $\left( 2, \  8, \  14\right)$. Its third component is nonzero and determines both negative ratios.
