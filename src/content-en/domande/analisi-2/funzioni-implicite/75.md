What is $y^\prime(0)$ for the positive branch through $(0,2)$ of $y^2+3xy+5x=4$?

- $- \dfrac{5}{4}$
- $- \dfrac{11}{4}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{11}{4}$

## Explanation

At the point, the two partial derivatives of $F$ are $\left( 11, \  4\right)$. The derivative is the negative of the ratio of the first to the second.
