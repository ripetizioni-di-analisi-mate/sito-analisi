What is $x^{\prime\prime}(0)$ for the branch $x=x(y)$ through $(0,0)$ satisfying $F=0$ with $F$ given by $2 x + 5 y^{3} + 3 y^{2}$?

- $-3$
- $- \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $3$
- $-10$

## Explanation

Solve for $x$ by dividing the polynomial in $y$ by $-2$. The second derivative at zero is twice the coefficient of $y^2$.
