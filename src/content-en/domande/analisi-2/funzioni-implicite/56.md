Which affine function of $x,y$ describes the tangent plane at the origin to $F=0$ if $F$ is given by $x^{2} + 2 x + 3 y + 5 z$?

- $- \dfrac{2 x}{5} - \dfrac{3 y}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{2 x}{5} + \dfrac{3 y}{5}$
- $- 2 x - 3 y$
- $\dfrac{2 x}{5} + \dfrac{3 y}{5}$

## Explanation

The tangent plane is obtained by setting the differential of $F$ at the origin to zero. The quadratic term has zero differential there.
