For which values of $k$ is the line $x + y = k$ tangent to the ellipse $x^2 + 3y^2 = 12$?

- $k = \pm 2\sqrt{3}$
- $k = \pm 3$
- $k = \pm 4$
- $k = \pm 2$

## Explanation

Let $F(x, y) = x^2 + 3y^2 - 12$, with $\nabla F = (2x, 6y)$, never zero on the ellipse. The line $x + y = k$ is tangent to the ellipse at a point if and only if the gradient at that point is parallel to the normal vector $(1, 1)$ of the line, that is, $2x = 6y$. Substituting $x = 3y$ into the equation of the ellipse gives $12y^2 = 12$, so the points of tangency are $(3, 1)$ and $(-3, -1)$. Therefore $k = x + y$ equals $4$ or $-4$.
