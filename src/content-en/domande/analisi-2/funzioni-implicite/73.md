What is $y^\prime(0)$ for the solution of $3y+z=\sin x,\quad y+5z=2x$?

- $\dfrac{5}{14}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{14}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{14}$

## Explanation

Differentiate at zero to obtain the same coefficient matrix with right-hand side $\left( 1, \  2\right)$. Eliminating $z^\prime$ gives $\dfrac{3}{14}$.
