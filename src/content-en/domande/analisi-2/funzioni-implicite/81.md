What is the ordered pair $(z_x,z_y)$ at the origin for $F=0$ if $F$ is given by $2 x + 7 y + e^{4 z} - 1$?

- $\left( - \dfrac{1}{2}, \  - \dfrac{7}{4}\right)$
- $\left( - \dfrac{7}{4}, \  - \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$
- $\left( -2, \  -7\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{1}{2}, \  \dfrac{7}{4}\right)$

## Explanation

The derivatives of $F$ at the origin are $\left( 2, \  7, \  4\right)$. Divide each of the first two components by the negative of the last one.
