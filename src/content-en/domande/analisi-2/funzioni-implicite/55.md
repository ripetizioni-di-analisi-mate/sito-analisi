The tangent line to the hyperbola $xy = 4$ at the point $(1, 4)$ forms a triangle with the coordinate axes. What is its area?

- $16$
- $4$
- $8$
- $2$

## Explanation

Let $F(x, y) = xy - 4$, with $\nabla F(1, 4) = (y, x)\,\big|_{(1,4)} = (4, 1)$. The tangent line is $4(x - 1) + (y - 4) = 0$, that is, $4x + y = 8$. It meets the $x$-axis at $(2, 0)$ and the $y$-axis at $(0, 8)$, so the triangle has legs of length $2$ and $8$ and area
$$
\frac{2 \cdot 8}{2} = 8 .
$$
