What is the slope of the tangent to the level curve at level $5$, at the point $(1,1)$, for $2 x^{2} + 3 y^{2}$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{2}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The gradient at the point is $\left( 4, \  6\right)$. The tangent slope is the negative of the first component divided by the second.
