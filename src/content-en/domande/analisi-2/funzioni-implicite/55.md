What is the tangent slope at $(1,1)$ on the level curve $5$ of $2 x^{2} + 3 y^{2}$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{2}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The gradient at the point is $\left( 4, \  6\right)$. The tangent slope is the negative first component divided by the second.
