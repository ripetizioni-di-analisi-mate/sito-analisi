What is $y^{\prime\prime}(0)$ for the solution $y(0)=0$ to $F=0$ if $F$ is given by $2 x^{2} + 5 y^{2} + 3 y$?

- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{2}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{4}{3}$
- $-4$

## Explanation

At the origin $y^\prime(0)=0$. After a second differentiation only $F_{xx}=4$ and $F_y y^{\prime\prime}$ remain.
