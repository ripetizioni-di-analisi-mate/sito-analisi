What is the pair $(z_x,z_y)$ at $(1,1,1)$ on the level surface $6$ of $x^{2} + 3 y^{2} + 2 z^{2}$?

- $\left( \dfrac{1}{2}, \  \dfrac{3}{2}\right)$
- $\left( - \dfrac{1}{3}, \  - \dfrac{2}{3}\right)$
- $\left( - \dfrac{1}{2}, \  - \dfrac{3}{2}\right)$
- $\left( - \dfrac{3}{2}, \  - \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$

## Explanation

The gradient at the point is $\left( 2, \  6, \  4\right)$. Its third component is nonzero and determines both negative ratios.
