What is the coefficient of $x^2$ on the branch $y(0)=0$ of $F=0$, with $F$ given by $3 x^{2} + 5 x y + 2 y$?

- $- \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

Factoring out $y$ gives $- \dfrac{3 x^{2}}{5 x + 2}$. Divide by $x^2$ and take the limit to get $- \dfrac{3}{2}$.
