The curve $xe^y+y=1$ is parametrised using $y$ as its parameter. At which value of $y$ does inverting the parametrisation cease to give a finite derivative $\dfrac{\mathrm dy}{\mathrm dx}$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $-1$
- $2$

## Explanation

Isolate $x=(1-y)e^{-y}$. Its derivative with respect to the parameter is $(y-2)e^{-y}$. It vanishes only at $y=2$, where its reciprocal cannot be a finite derivative.
