- $(-2, 1)$ and $(2, -1)$
- $(1, -2)$ and $(-1, 2)$
- $(\sqrt{3}, 0)$ and $(-\sqrt{3}, 0)$
- $(0, \sqrt{3})$ and $(0, -\sqrt{3})$

## Explanation

Let $F(x, y) = x^2 + xy + y^2 - 3$, of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$, with $F_x = 2x + y$ and $F_y = x + 2y$. The tangent line $F_x\,(x - x_0) + F_y\,(y - y_0) = 0$ is horizontal at the points of the curve where $F_x = 0$ and $F_y \neq 0$. From the condition $2x + y = 0$ it follows that $y = -2x$, and substituting into the equation of the curve we obtain $x^2 - 2x^2 + 4x^2 = 3x^2 = 3$, so $x = \pm 1$. The points are therefore $(1, -2)$ and $(-1, 2)$, at which $F_y$ equals $-3$ and $3$ respectively, which are nonzero.
