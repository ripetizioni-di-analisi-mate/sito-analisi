What is the counterclockwise circulation of $\left( - y^{2}, \  4 x y\right)$ around $x\geq0,\ y\geq0,\ x+y\leq 2$?

- $16$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}$
- $-8$
- $8$

## Explanation

The scalar curl is $6y$. At height $y$ the triangle has width $2-y$. Integrate the product from zero to $2$.
