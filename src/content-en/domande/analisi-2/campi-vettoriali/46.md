The work of the field $F(x, y, z) = (y^2, \; z, \; x)$ along the curve $r(t) = (t, \; t^2, \; t^3)$, $t \in [0, 1]$, is computed with the definition of line integral. Which function of $t$ is integrated over $[0, 1]$?

- $3t^4 + 3t^3$
- $t^4 + t^3 + t$
- $t^6 + 5t^3$
- $t^4 + 2t^3 + 3t$

## Explanation

The work is $\displaystyle\int_0^1 F(r(t)) \cdot r'(t) \, dt$. On the curve $x = t$, $y = t^2$ and $z = t^3$, so $F(r(t)) = (t^4, \; t^3, \; t)$. The derivative of the parametrization is $r'(t) = (1, \; 2t, \; 3t^2)$. The dot product is therefore
$$
t^4 \cdot 1 + t^3 \cdot 2t + t \cdot 3t^2 = 3t^4 + 3t^3 .
$$
The function $t^4 + t^3 + t$ is obtained by adding the components of $F(r(t))$ without multiplying them by those of $r'(t)$, while $t^6 + 5t^3$ is obtained by substituting $y = t^3$ and $z = t^2$.
