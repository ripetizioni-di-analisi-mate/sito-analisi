Which potential vanishing at the origin belongs to the field $\left( 6 x y, \  3 x^{2} + 10 y\right)$?

- $3 x^{2} y + 5 y^{2} + 5 y$
- $3 x^{2} y + 5 y^{2} + 2$
- $3 x^{2} y + 5 y^{2}$
- $3 x^{2} y + 3 x^{2} + 5 y^{2}$

## Explanation

Integrate the first component in $x$ and add a function of $y$. The second component determines that function up to a constant.
