What is the work of the field $F(x, y) = (x^2, \; xy)$ along the arc of the parabola $y = x^2$ from $(0, 0)$ to $(1, 1)$?

- $\dfrac{11}{15}$
- $\dfrac{7}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $-\dfrac{11}{15}$

## Explanation

The cross derivatives are $\dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = 0$ and $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} = y$, so the field is not irrotational and the work is computed with the definition. The arc is parametrized by $r(t) = (t, t^2)$, $t \in [0, 1]$, so $r'(t) = (1, 2t)$ and $F(r(t)) = (t^2, t^3)$. The dot product equals $F(r(t)) \cdot r'(t) = t^2 + 2t^4$, therefore the work is
$$
\int_0^1 (t^2 + 2t^4) \, dt = \frac{1}{3} + \frac{2}{5} = \frac{11}{15} .
$$
