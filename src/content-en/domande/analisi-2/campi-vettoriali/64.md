What is the counterclockwise circulation of $\left( - y^{2}, \  3 x y\right)$ around $x\geq0,\ y\geq0,\ x+y\leq 5$?

- $- \dfrac{625}{6}$
- $\dfrac{625}{6}$
- $\dfrac{625}{3}$
- $\dfrac{125}{2}$

## Explanation

The scalar curl is $5y$. At height $y$ the triangle has width $5-y$. Integrate the product from zero to $5$.
