## Explanation

The field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ and the circle traversed counterclockwise is the positively oriented boundary of the disk $D$ of radius $3$, so **Green's theorem** applies. The integrand of the double integral is
$$
\frac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = 1 - (-1) = 2 .
$$
The circulation is therefore twice the area of $D$, that is, $2 \cdot 9\pi = 18\pi$.
