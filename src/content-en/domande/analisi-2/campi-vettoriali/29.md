What is the work of the field $F(x, y) = \bigl( y \cos (xy), \; x \cos (xy) \bigr)$ along any piecewise regular curve from $(0, 0)$ to $\left( 1, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)$?

- $0$
- $-1$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The function $U(x, y) = \sin (xy)$ is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ and, by the chain rule, $U_x = y \cos (xy)$ and $U_y = x \cos (xy)$. Hence $\nabla U = F$ and the field is conservative with potential $U$. By the theorem on the **work of a conservative field** the work does not depend on the curve and equals
$$
U\!\left( 1, \frac{\pi}{2} \right) - U(0, 0) = \sin \frac{\pi}{2} - \sin 0 = 1 .
$$
