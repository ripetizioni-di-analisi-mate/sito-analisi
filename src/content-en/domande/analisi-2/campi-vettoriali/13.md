The field $\nabla(xyz)$ does work $7$ from $(1,1,1)$ to a point in the plane $z=2$. Which relation must the final coordinates $x,y$ satisfy?

- $xy=3$
- $xy=4$
- $x+y=4$
- $xy=8$

## Explanation

The initial potential is $1$, so the final potential must be $1+7=8$. In the specified plane the potential is $2xy$, giving $xy=4$.
