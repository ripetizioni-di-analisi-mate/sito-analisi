## Explanation

The field $F = (yz, \, xz, \, xy)$ is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^3$, its curl is $(x - x, \; y - y, \; z - z) = (0, 0, 0)$ and $\mathbb{R}^3$ is simply connected, so the field is conservative. A potential is $U(x, y, z) = xyz$, since $U_x = yz$, $U_y = xz$ and $U_z = xy$.

The curve starts at $r(0) = (1, 1, 1)$ and ends at $r(1) = (2, 2, 2)$. By the theorem on the **work of a conservative field** the work equals
$$
U(2, 2, 2) - U(1, 1, 1) = 8 - 1 = 7 .
$$
