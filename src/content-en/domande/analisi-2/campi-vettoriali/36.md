What are the potentials on $\mathbb{R}^3$ of the field $F(x, y, z) = (y + z, \; x + z, \; x + y)$?

- $U(x, y, z) = xyz + k$
- $U(x, y, z) = xy + yz + zx + k$
- $U(x, y, z) = 2(xy + yz + zx) + k$
- $U(x, y, z) = \dfrac{(x + y + z)^2}{2} + k$

## Explanation

The curl of the field is $(1 - 1, \; 1 - 1, \; 1 - 1) = (0, 0, 0)$ and $\mathbb{R}^3$ is simply connected, so the field is **conservative**. Integrating $F_1 = y + z$ with respect to $x$ gives $U = xy + xz + c(y, z)$. Imposing $U_y = x + z$ gives $c_y(y, z) = z$, so $c(y, z) = yz + d(z)$. Finally, imposing $U_z = x + y$ gives $x + y + d'(z) = x + y$, therefore $d$ is constant and the potentials are
$$
U(x, y, z) = xy + yz + zx + k, \qquad k \in \mathbb{R} .
$$
