The field $F$ is conservative on $\mathbb{R}^2$ and a potential $U$ of it satisfies $U(0, 0) = 1$ and $U(2, 1) = 4$. What is the work of $F$ along the polygonal path that goes from $(2, 1)$ to $(0, 1)$ and then from $(0, 1)$ to $(0, 0)$?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $5$
- $0$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the **work of a conservative field** the work along a piecewise regular curve is the difference between the value of the potential at the final point and its value at the initial point. The polygonal path starts at $(2, 1)$ and ends at $(0, 0)$, so the work is $U(0, 0) - U(2, 1) = 1 - 4 = -3$. The value of $U$ at the intermediate vertex $(0, 1)$ plays no role, since it appears once with a plus sign and once with a minus sign.
