A constant force $F = (3, \; -2)$ acts on a point that moves from $(1, 1)$ to $(4, 5)$ along an arbitrary piecewise regular curve. What is the work of $F$?

- $17$
- $1$
- $-1$
- $5$

## Explanation

The constant field $F = (3, -2)$ is conservative on $\mathbb{R}^2$ with potential $U(x, y) = 3x - 2y$, since $U_x = 3$ and $U_y = -2$. By the theorem on the **work of a conservative field** the work does not depend on the curve and equals
$$
U(4, 5) - U(1, 1) = (12 - 10) - (3 - 2) = 1 .
$$
The same value is the dot product of $F$ with the displacement $(3, 4)$, that is, $9 - 8 = 1$.
