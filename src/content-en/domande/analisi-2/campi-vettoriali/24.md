Let $D = \{ (x, y) : x^2 + y^2 \leq 4, \; y \geq 0 \}$ be the upper half-disk of radius $2$. What is the circulation of the field $F(x, y) = \bigl( -y^3, \; x^3 \bigr)$ along the positively oriented boundary of $D$?

- $12\pi$
- $24\pi$
- $4\pi$
- $-12\pi$

## Explanation

The boundary of $D$ consists of the diameter on the $x$-axis and the semicircle; the field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$, so **Green's theorem** applies. The integrand is $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = 3x^2 + 3y^2$. In polar coordinates the half-disk corresponds to $0 \leq \rho \leq 2$ and $0 \leq \theta \leq \pi$, and the area element is $\rho \, d\rho \, d\theta$, therefore
$$
\iint_D 3(x^2 + y^2) \, dx \, dy = 3 \int_0^{\pi} \int_0^2 \rho^3 \, d\rho \, d\theta = 3 \cdot \pi \cdot 4 = 12\pi .
$$
The value $24\pi$ is the circulation along the boundary of the whole disk.
