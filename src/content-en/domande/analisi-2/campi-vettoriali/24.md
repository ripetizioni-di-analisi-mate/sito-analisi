What is the circulation of the field $F(x, y) = \bigl( -x^2 y, \; x y^2 \bigr)$ along the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 4$, traversed counterclockwise?

- $8\pi$
- $\dfrac{16\pi}{3}$
- $-8\pi$
- $4\pi$

## Explanation

The field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ and the circle traversed counterclockwise is the positively oriented boundary of the disk $D$ of radius $2$, so **Green's theorem** applies. The integrand of the double integral is $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = y^2 + x^2$. In polar coordinates $x^2 + y^2 = \rho^2$ and the area element is $\rho \, d\rho \, d\theta$, therefore
$$
\iint_D (x^2 + y^2) \, dx \, dy = \int_0^{2\pi} \int_0^2 \rho^3 \, d\rho \, d\theta = 2\pi \cdot 4 = 8\pi .
$$
