The area of the square $Q = [0, 2] \times [0, 2]$ is computed with the integral $\displaystyle\oint_{\partial Q} x \, dy$ along the boundary traversed counterclockwise, adding the contributions of the four sides. Which sides give a nonzero contribution?

- Only the side $x = 2$
- Only the two horizontal sides
- All four sides
- Only the sides $x = 0$ and $x = 2$

## Explanation

On the horizontal sides $y$ is constant, so $dy = 0$ and the contribution is zero. On the side $x = 0$ the integrand $x \, dy$ is zero, because $x = 0$. On the side $x = 2$, traversed upward with $r(t) = (2, t)$ and $t \in [0, 2]$, the contribution is $\displaystyle\int_0^2 2 \, dt = 4$. Therefore **only the side** $x = 2$ contributes, and its contribution equals the area of the square.
