A field $F$ is conservative on $\mathbb{R}^2$. Its work along a curve from $A$ to $B$ equals $3$ and its work along a curve from $B$ to $C$ equals $-5$. What is the work of $F$ along an arbitrary curve from $C$ to $A$?

- $-2$
- $8$
- $-8$
- $2$

## Explanation

Let $U$ be a potential of $F$. By the theorem on the **work of a conservative field** one has $U(B) - U(A) = 3$ and $U(C) - U(B) = -5$. Adding the two equalities gives $U(C) - U(A) = -2$. The work from $C$ to $A$ is $U(A) - U(C)$, so it equals $2$, whatever curve is chosen.
