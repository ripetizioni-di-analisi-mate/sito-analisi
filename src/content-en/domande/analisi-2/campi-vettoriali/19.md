What is the circulation of the field $F(x, y) = \bigl( xy^2, \; x^2 y + x \bigr)$ along the boundary of the rectangle $[0, 2] \times [0, 1]$, traversed counterclockwise?

- $4$
- $0$
- $-2$
- $2$

## Explanation

The field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ and the boundary of the rectangle $R$, traversed counterclockwise, is positively oriented, so **Green's theorem** applies. The integrand of the double integral is
$$
\frac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = (2xy + 1) - 2xy = 1 .
$$
The circulation is therefore the integral of $1$ over $R$, that is the area of the rectangle, which equals $2$.
