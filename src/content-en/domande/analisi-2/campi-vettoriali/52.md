Let $D$ be a regular domain with positively oriented boundary $\partial D$. By Green's theorem, which double integral equals $\displaystyle\oint_{\partial D} xy \, dx + (x^2 + y) \, dy$?

- $\displaystyle\iint_D 3x \, dx \, dy$
- $\displaystyle\iint_D (-x) \, dx \, dy$
- $\displaystyle\iint_D x \, dx \, dy$
- $\displaystyle\iint_D (2x - y) \, dx \, dy$

## Explanation

The field $F = (xy, \; x^2 + y)$ is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$, so **Green's theorem** applies with integrand $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y}$. One has $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} = 2x$ and $\dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = x$, therefore the integrand is $2x - x = x$. The integral $\displaystyle\iint_D 3x \, dx \, dy$ is obtained by adding the two derivatives instead of subtracting them, and $\displaystyle\iint_D (-x) \, dx \, dy$ by reversing the order of the subtraction.
