What is the potential, vanishing at the origin, of the field $\left( 4 x y, \  2 x^{2} + 6 y\right)$?

- $2 x^{2} y + 3 y^{2} + 3 y$
- $2 x^{2} y + 3 y^{2}$
- $2 x^{2} y + 2 x^{2} + 3 y^{2}$
- $2 x^{2} y + 3 y^{2} + 5$

## Explanation

Integrate the first component with respect to $x$ and add a function of $y$. The second component determines that function up to a constant.
