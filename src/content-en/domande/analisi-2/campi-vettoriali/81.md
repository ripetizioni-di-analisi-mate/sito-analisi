In space, what is the work of the field $F(x, y, z) = (z, \; x, \; y)$ along the segment from the origin to the point $(1, 2, 3)$?

- $7$
- $11$
- $\dfrac{11}{2}$
- $3$

## Explanation

The curl of the field is $(1, 1, 1)$, so the field is not conservative and the work is computed with the definition. The segment is parametrized by $r(t) = (t, \; 2t, \; 3t)$, $t \in [0, 1]$, so $r'(t) = (1, 2, 3)$ and $F(r(t)) = (3t, \; t, \; 2t)$. The dot product equals $3t + 2t + 6t = 11t$, therefore the work is
$$
\int_0^1 11t \, dt = \frac{11}{2} .
$$
The value $7$ is obtained by swapping the components, that is, by using the field $(x, y, z)$ instead of $(z, x, y)$.
