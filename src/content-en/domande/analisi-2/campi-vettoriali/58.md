The field $F$ is the gradient of the function $U(x, y) = x e^y + y^3$. What is the circulation of $F$ along the boundary of the triangle with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(3, 1)$, $(1, 4)$, traversed counterclockwise?

- $\dfrac{11}{2}$
- $0$
- $11$
- $-\dfrac{11}{2}$

## Explanation

The field $F = \nabla U$ is conservative on $\mathbb{R}^2$, since $U$ is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$. By the theorem on the **work of a conservative field** the work along a curve from the point $P$ to the point $Q$ equals $U(Q) - U(P)$. The boundary of the triangle is a closed curve, which starts from a vertex and returns to it, so the circulation equals $U(P) - U(P) = 0$. The value $\dfrac{11}{2}$ is the area of the triangle and plays no role in the computation.
