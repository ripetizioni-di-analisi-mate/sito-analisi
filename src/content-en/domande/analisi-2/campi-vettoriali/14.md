## Explanation

The field is irrotational on $A$, since both cross derivatives equal $-\dfrac{2xy}{(x^2 + y^2)^2}$. The domain $A$ is not simply connected, therefore the theorem on irrotational fields on a simply connected open set does not apply and conservativeness must be established in another way.

The function $U(x, y) = \dfrac{1}{2} \ln (x^2 + y^2)$ is of class $C^1$ on all of $A$, with $U_x = \dfrac{x}{x^2 + y^2}$ and $U_y = \dfrac{y}{x^2 + y^2}$. Hence $\nabla U = F$ at every point of $A$, and by the definition the field is **conservative** on $A$. Consequently the circulation along the unit circle is zero.
