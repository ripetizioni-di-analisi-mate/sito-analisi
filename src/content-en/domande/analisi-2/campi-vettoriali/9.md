## Explanation

The field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ and the boundary of the triangle $T$ is traversed counterclockwise, so **Green's theorem** applies. The integrand of the double integral is $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = y - 0 = y$. The triangle is the normal domain $T = \{ (x, y) : 0 \leq x \leq 2, \ 0 \leq y \leq 2 - x \}$, therefore by the reduction theorem
$$
\iint_T y \, dx \, dy = \int_0^2 \left( \int_0^{2 - x} y \, dy \right) dx = \int_0^2 \frac{(2 - x)^2}{2} \, dx = \left[ -\frac{(2 - x)^3}{6} \right]_0^2 = \frac{4}{3} .
$$
The circulation therefore equals $\dfrac{4}{3}$.
