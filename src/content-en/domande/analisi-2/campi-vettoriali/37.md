Let $D = \{ (x, y) : 1 \leq x^2 + y^2 \leq 4 \}$. What is the circulation of the field $F(x, y) = (-y, \; x)$ along the positively oriented boundary of $D$?

- $10\pi$
- $8\pi$
- $3\pi$
- $6\pi$

## Explanation

The positively oriented boundary of $D$ consists of the circle of radius $2$ traversed counterclockwise and the circle of radius $1$ traversed clockwise. The field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$, so **Green's theorem** applies to the annulus $D$, with integrand $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = 1 - (-1) = 2$. The circulation is therefore twice the area of the annulus, that is $2 \, (4\pi - \pi) = 6\pi$.
