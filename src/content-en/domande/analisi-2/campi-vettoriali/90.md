What is the circulation of $\left( 4 x + 2 y, \  2 x + 7 y\right)$ around the triangle with vertices $(0,0),(1,0),(0,7)$ counterclockwise?

- $13$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $0$
- $14$

## Explanation

The two cross partials agree at $2$. The integrand in Green’s formula is therefore zero.
