What is the work of the field $F(x, y, z) = (y, \; z, \; x)$ along the helix $r(t) = (\cos t, \; \sin t, \; t)$, $t \in [0, 2\pi]$?

- $\pi$
- $0$
- $-\pi$
- $-2\pi$

## Explanation

The curl of the field is $(-1, -1, -1)$, so the field is not conservative and the work is computed with the definition. We have $r'(t) = (-\sin t, \; \cos t, \; 1)$ and $F(r(t)) = (\sin t, \; t, \; \cos t)$, therefore the dot product is $-\sin^2 t + t \cos t + \cos t$. On the interval $[0, 2\pi]$ the integral of $\cos t$ is zero and, by integration by parts, $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi} t \cos t \, dt = \bigl[ t \sin t + \cos t \bigr]_0^{2\pi} = 0$. The integral of $-\sin^2 t$ over $[0, 2\pi]$ equals $-\pi$, so the work is $-\pi$.
