What is the circulation of the field $F(x, y) = (y^2, \; x^2)$ along the boundary, traversed counterclockwise, of the region $D = \{ (x, y) : 0 \leq x \leq 1, \; x^2 \leq y \leq x \}$?

- $-\dfrac{1}{30}$
- $\dfrac{1}{15}$
- $\dfrac{1}{30}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ and the boundary of $D$ is positively oriented, so **Green's theorem** applies with integrand $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = 2x - 2y$. The region is normal with respect to the $x$-axis, therefore by the reduction theorem
$$
\iint_D (2x - 2y) \, dx \, dy = \int_0^1 \bigl[ 2xy - y^2 \bigr]_{y = x^2}^{y = x} \, dx = \int_0^1 (x^2 - 2x^3 + x^4) \, dx .
$$
Hence the circulation equals $\dfrac{1}{3} - \dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{5} = \dfrac{1}{30}$.
