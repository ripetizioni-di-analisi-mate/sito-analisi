Let $R = [1, 3] \times [0, 2]$. What is the circulation of the field $F(x, y) = \bigl( e^y, \; x^2 \bigr)$ along the boundary of $R$ traversed counterclockwise?

- $18 - 2e^2$
- $17 - e^2$
- $14 + 2e^2$
- $16$

## Explanation

The field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ and the boundary of the rectangle is positively oriented, so by **Green's theorem** the circulation is the integral over $R$ of $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = 2x - e^y$. By the reduction theorem
$$
\iint_R 2x \, dx \, dy = 2 \int_1^3 2x \, dx = 16, \qquad \iint_R e^y \, dx \, dy = 2 \int_0^2 e^y \, dy = 2(e^2 - 1) .
$$
The circulation therefore equals $16 - 2(e^2 - 1) = 18 - 2e^2$. The value $17 - e^2$ is obtained by forgetting that the rectangle has base $2$ in the second integral.
