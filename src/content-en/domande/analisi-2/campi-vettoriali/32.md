What is the circulation of the field $F(x, y) = (y, \; 3x)$ along the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 4$, traversed clockwise?

- $-8\pi$
- $8\pi$
- $-4\pi$
- $-16\pi$

## Explanation

The field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ and the circle traversed counterclockwise is the positively oriented boundary of the disk $D$ of radius $2$. By **Green's theorem** the counterclockwise circulation is the integral over $D$ of $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = 3 - 1 = 2$, that is $2 \cdot 4\pi = 8\pi$. The clockwise direction is the opposite one and the circulation changes sign, so it equals $-8\pi$.
