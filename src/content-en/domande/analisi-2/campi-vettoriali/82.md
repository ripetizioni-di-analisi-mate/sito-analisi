The radial field $F(x, y, z) = (x^2 + y^2 + z^2) \, (x, \; y, \; z)$ is defined on $\mathbb{R}^3$. What is its curl?

- $(4yz, \; 4xz, \; 4xy)$
- $(0, \; 0, \; 0)$
- $(2yz, \; 2xz, \; 2xy)$
- $(2x, \; 2y, \; 2z)$

## Explanation

The components are $F_1 = x \rho^2$, $F_2 = y \rho^2$, $F_3 = z \rho^2$ with $\rho^2 = x^2 + y^2 + z^2$. For the first component of the curl one has $\dfrac{\partial F_3}{\partial y} = 2yz$ and $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial z} = 2yz$, so the difference is zero. In the same way $\dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial z} - \dfrac{\partial F_3}{\partial x} = 2xz - 2xz = 0$ and $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = 2xy - 2xy = 0$, therefore $\operatorname{rot} F = (0, 0, 0)$. The result is consistent with the fact that $F$ is the gradient of $U = \dfrac{\rho^4}{4}$, so it is **conservative** on $\mathbb{R}^3$. The vector $(4yz, 4xz, 4xy)$ is obtained by adding the cross derivatives instead of subtracting them.
