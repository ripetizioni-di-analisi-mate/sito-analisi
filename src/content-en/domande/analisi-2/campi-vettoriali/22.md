What is the work of the field $F(x, y) = (-y, \; x)$ along the semicircle $x^2 + y^2 = 4$, $y \geq 0$, traversed from $(2, 0)$ to $(-2, 0)$?

- $2\pi$
- $-4\pi$
- $4\pi$
- $0$

## Explanation

The cross derivatives are $\dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = -1$ and $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} = 1$, so the field is not conservative and the work is computed with the definition. The semicircle from $(2, 0)$ to $(-2, 0)$ is parametrized by $r(t) = (2\cos t, \; 2\sin t)$, $t \in [0, \pi]$, so $r'(t) = (-2\sin t, \; 2\cos t)$ and $F(r(t)) = (-2\sin t, \; 2\cos t)$. The dot product equals $4\sin^2 t + 4\cos^2 t = 4$, therefore the work is
$$
\int_0^{\pi} 4 \, dt = 4\pi .
$$
