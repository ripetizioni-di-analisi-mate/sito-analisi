What is the circulation of $\left( 3 x + 5 y, \  5 x + 2 y\right)$ along the counterclockwise perimeter of the triangle with vertices $(0,0),(1,0),(0,2)$?

- $10$
- $1$
- $0$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The two cross derivatives coincide and both equal $5$. The integrand in Green’s formula is therefore zero.
