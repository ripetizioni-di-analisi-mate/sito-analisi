Let $D = \{ (x, y) : -1 \leq x \leq 1, \; x^2 \leq y \leq 1 \}$. What is $\displaystyle\oint_{\partial D} x \, dy$ along the positively oriented boundary of $D$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The boundary consists of the arc of the parabola $y = x^2$, traversed from $(-1, 1)$ to $(1, 1)$, and of the segment $y = 1$, traversed from $(1, 1)$ to $(-1, 1)$. On the segment $y$ is constant, so $dy = 0$ and the contribution is zero. On the arc, with $r(t) = (t, t^2)$ and $t \in [-1, 1]$, one has $dy = 2t \, dt$, therefore
$$
\oint_{\partial D} x \, dy = \int_{-1}^{1} t \cdot 2t \, dt = \frac{4}{3} .
$$
The value equals the area of $D$, as the area formula predicts.
