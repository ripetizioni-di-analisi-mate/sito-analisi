What is the work of the field $F(x, y) = (-y, \; x^2)$ along the arc of the unit circle from $(1, 0)$ to $(0, 1)$, counterclockwise?

- $\dfrac{2}{3} - \dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2} + \dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The cross derivatives are $\dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = -1$ and $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} = 2x$, so the field is not conservative and the work is computed with the definition. The arc is parametrized by $r(t) = (\cos t, \; \sin t)$, $t \in \left[ 0, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right]$, so $r'(t) = (-\sin t, \; \cos t)$ and $F(r(t)) = (-\sin t, \; \cos^2 t)$. The dot product equals $\sin^2 t + \cos^3 t$, therefore the work is
$$
\int_0^{\pi/2} \sin^2 t \, dt + \int_0^{\pi/2} \cos^3 t \, dt = \frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{2}{3} .
$$
The second integral is computed by writing $\cos^3 t = (1 - \sin^2 t) \cos t$, whose antiderivative is $\sin t - \dfrac{\sin^3 t}{3}$.
