What is the area of the region enclosed by the astroid $r(t) = (\cos^3 t, \; \sin^3 t)$, $t \in [0, 2\pi]$?

- $\dfrac{3\pi}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{16}$
- $\pi$

## Explanation

The parametrization traverses the astroid counterclockwise, so the area is computed with the formula $\operatorname{area} = \dfrac{1}{2} \displaystyle\oint (x \, dy - y \, dx)$. With $x = \cos^3 t$ and $y = \sin^3 t$ we have $dx = -3\cos^2 t \sin t \, dt$ and $dy = 3\sin^2 t \cos t \, dt$, therefore
$$
x \, dy - y \, dx = 3\sin^2 t \cos^2 t \, (\cos^2 t + \sin^2 t) \, dt = \frac{3}{4} \sin^2 (2t) \, dt .
$$
Since $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi} \sin^2 (2t) \, dt = \pi$, the area equals $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{3}{4} \cdot \pi = \dfrac{3\pi}{8}$.
