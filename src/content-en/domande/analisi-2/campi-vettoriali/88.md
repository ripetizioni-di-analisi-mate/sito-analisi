The figure shows an arch of the cycloid $r(t) = (t - \sin t, \; 1 - \cos t)$, $t \in [0, 2\pi]$. What is the area of the region $D$ between the arch and the $x$-axis?

::grafico{xmin=-0.5 xmax=6.8 ymin=-0.6 ymax=2.6 uguale=1 curve="t-sin(t),1-cos(t),0,6.2832" punti="0,0,pieno;6.2832,0,pieno" etichette="5.9,-0.4,2π;2.9,0.8,D" titolo="An arch of a cycloid and the region $D$ between the arch and the $x$-axis"}

- $-3\pi$
- $3\pi$
- $2\pi$
- $4\pi$

## Explanation

We use the formula $\operatorname{area} = -\displaystyle\oint_{\partial D} y \, dx$. The positively oriented boundary traverses the segment of the $x$-axis from $(0, 0)$ to $(2\pi, 0)$, where $y = 0$ and the contribution is zero, and then the cycloid arch from $(2\pi, 0)$ to $(0, 0)$, that is, with $t$ from $2\pi$ to $0$. With $y = 1 - \cos t$ and $dx = (1 - \cos t) \, dt$ one obtains
$$
\operatorname{area} = -\int_{2\pi}^{0} (1 - \cos t)^2 \, dt = \int_0^{2\pi} (1 - 2\cos t + \cos^2 t) \, dt = 2\pi + \pi = 3\pi .
$$
The value $-3\pi$ is obtained by traversing the arch with $t$ from $0$ to $2\pi$, which is the direction opposite to that of the positively oriented boundary.
