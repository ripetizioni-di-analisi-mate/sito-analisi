The field $F(x, y) = \bigl( y^2 + 1, \; 2xy \bigr)$ is conservative on $\mathbb{R}^2$. What is the value at the point $(2, 3)$ of the potential $U$ that vanishes at the origin?

- $18$
- $20$
- $21$
- $38$

## Explanation

The work along the polygonal path that goes from $(0, 0)$ to $(x, 0)$ horizontally and then from $(x, 0)$ to $(x, y)$ vertically is a potential that vanishes at the origin, given by
$$
U(x, y) = \int_0^x F_1(t, 0) \, dt + \int_0^y F_2(x, t) \, dt .
$$
Since $F_1(t, 0) = 1$ and $F_2(x, t) = 2xt$, we obtain $U(x, y) = x + xy^2$. On $\mathbb{R}^2$, which is connected, two potentials differ by a constant, so this is the only potential that vanishes at the origin. Therefore $U(2, 3) = 2 + 18 = 20$.
