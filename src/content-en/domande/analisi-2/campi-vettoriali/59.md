What is the difference between the work of $\left( 2 y, \  3 x\right)$ along $(0,0)\to(1,0)\to(1,5)$ and along $(0,0)\to(0,5)\to(1,5)$?

- $5$
- $-5$
- $25$
- $0$

## Explanation

The first work is $15$ and the second is $10$. Their difference is the counterclockwise circulation around the rectangle.
