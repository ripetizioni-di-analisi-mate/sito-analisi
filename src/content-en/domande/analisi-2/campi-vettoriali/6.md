## Explanation

The field $F = (xy, \, x)$ is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ and the circle traversed counterclockwise is the positively oriented boundary of the disk $D$ of radius $1$, so **Green's theorem** applies. The integrand of the double integral is
$$
\frac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = 1 - x .
$$
The disk $D$ is symmetric with respect to the $y$-axis and the function $x$ takes opposite values at the points $(x, y)$ and $(-x, y)$, therefore its integral over $D$ is zero. The integral of $1$ over $D$ is the area of the disk, that is, $\pi$, so the circulation equals $\pi$.
