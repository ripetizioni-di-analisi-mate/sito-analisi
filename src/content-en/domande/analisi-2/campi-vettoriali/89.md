What is the circulation of the field $F(x, y) = (-xy, \; xy)$ along the boundary of the rectangle $R = [0, 2] \times [0, 1]$, traversed clockwise as in the figure?

::grafico{xmin=-0.6 xmax=2.7 ymin=-0.5 ymax=1.6 uguale=1 unicolore=1 area="1;0;0;2" vettori="0.7,1,1.3,1;1.3,0,0.7,0" etichette="1,0.5,R" titolo="The rectangle $R = [0, 2] \times [0, 1]$ with its boundary traversed clockwise"}

- $-3$
- $3$
- $1$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$. The boundary traversed clockwise is the *negatively* oriented boundary, so by **Green's theorem** the circulation is the opposite of the integral over $R$ of
$$
\frac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = y - (-x) = x + y .
$$
By the reduction theorem $\displaystyle\iint_R (x + y) \, dx \, dy = \int_0^2 \left( \int_0^1 (x + y) \, dy \right) dx = \int_0^2 \left( x + \frac{1}{2} \right) dx = 2 + 1 = 3$. The clockwise circulation is therefore $-3$. The value $3$ is the counterclockwise circulation.
