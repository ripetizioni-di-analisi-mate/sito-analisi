What is the difference between the work of $\left( 4 y, \  2 x\right)$ along $(0,0)\to(1,0)\to(1,7)$ and along $(0,0)\to(0,7)\to(1,7)$?

- $0$
- $-14$
- $42$
- $14$

## Explanation

The first work is $14$ and the second is $28$. Their difference is the counterclockwise circulation around the rectangle.
