A regular curve starts at $(1, 0)$ and ends at an unspecified point of the line $y = x$. What is the work of the field $F(x, y) = (2x, \; -2y)$ along the curve?

- $1$
- $0$
- $-2$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The field is the gradient of $U(x, y) = x^2 - y^2$, since $U_x = 2x$ and $U_y = -2y$, so it is conservative on $\mathbb{R}^2$. The potential equals $0$ at every point $(a, a)$ of the line $y = x$ and equals $1$ at the point $(1, 0)$. By the theorem on the **work of a conservative field** the work is therefore $U(a, a) - U(1, 0) = 0 - 1 = -1$. The value depends neither on the curve nor on the end point on the line.
