What is the potential, vanishing at the origin, of the field $\left( 8 x y, \  4 x^{2} + 4 y\right)$?

- $4 x^{2} y + 2 y^{2} + 7$
- $4 x^{2} y + 2 y^{2} + 2 y$
- $4 x^{2} y + 4 x^{2} + 2 y^{2}$
- $4 x^{2} y + 2 y^{2}$

## Explanation

Integrate the first component with respect to $x$ and add a function of $y$. The second component determines that function up to a constant.
