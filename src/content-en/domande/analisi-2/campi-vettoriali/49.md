Let $D$ be a regular domain with positively oriented boundary $\partial D$. Which of the following line integrals equals the area of $D$?

- $\displaystyle\oint_{\partial D} y \, dx$
- $\displaystyle\oint_{\partial D} x \, dy$
- $\displaystyle\oint_{\partial D} x \, dx$
- $\dfrac{1}{2} \displaystyle\oint_{\partial D} (y \, dx - x \, dy)$

## Explanation

By **Green's theorem** applied to the field $F = (0, x)$ the integrand of the double integral is $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = 1$, so $\displaystyle\oint_{\partial D} x \, dy$ is the integral of $1$ over $D$, that is, the area. For the field $(y, 0)$ the integrand equals $-1$, therefore $\displaystyle\oint_{\partial D} y \, dx$ is the opposite of the area. The field $(x, 0)$ has zero integrand and zero circulation. The last integral is the opposite of the formula $\dfrac{1}{2} \displaystyle\oint (x \, dy - y \, dx)$, so it also equals the opposite of the area.
