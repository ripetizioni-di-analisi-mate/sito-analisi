What is the counterclockwise circulation of $\left( - \dfrac{4 y}{x^{2} + y^{2}}, \  \dfrac{4 x}{x^{2} + y^{2}}\right)$ around the circle centered at the origin with radius $7$?

- $- 8 \pi$
- $56 \pi$
- $8 \pi$
- $0$

## Explanation

With $(x,y)=(7\cos t,7\sin t)$ the dot product with the tangent is $4$. The angle makes one full turn.
