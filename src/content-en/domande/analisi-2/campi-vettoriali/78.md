What is the clockwise circulation of $\left( - 4 y, \  2 x\right)$ around the circle $x^2+y^2=49$?

- $294 \pi$
- $98 \pi$
- $- 294 \pi$
- $- 42 \pi$

## Explanation

The circulation density is the constant $6$. Multiply by the disk area and reverse the sign for clockwise orientation.
