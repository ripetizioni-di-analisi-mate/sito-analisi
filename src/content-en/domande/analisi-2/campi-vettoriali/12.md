What are the potentials on $\mathbb{R}^3$ of the field $F(x, y, z) = \bigl( 2xz, \; 3y^2 z^2, \; x^2 + 2y^3 z \bigr)$?

- $U(x, y, z) = 2x^2 z + 2y^3 z^2 + k$
- $U(x, y, z) = x^2 z + 2y^3 z^2 + k$
- $U(x, y, z) = x^2 z + y^3 z^2 + x^2 + k$
- $U(x, y, z) = x^2 z + y^3 z^2 + k$

## Explanation

The components are polynomials, so the field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^3$. The curl is $\operatorname{curl} F = \bigl( 6y^2 z - 6y^2 z, \; 2x - 2x, \; 0 - 0 \bigr) = (0, 0, 0)$, so the field is irrotational on $\mathbb{R}^3$. Since $\mathbb{R}^3$ is simply connected, the theorem on irrotational fields on a simply connected open set applies and the field is **conservative**.

Integrating $F_1 = 2xz$ with respect to $x$ we obtain $U = x^2 z + c(y, z)$. Imposing $U_y = 3y^2 z^2$ we obtain $c_y(y, z) = 3y^2 z^2$, so $c(y, z) = y^3 z^2 + d(z)$. Finally, imposing $U_z = x^2 + 2y^3 z$ we obtain $x^2 + 2y^3 z + d'(z) = x^2 + 2y^3 z$, therefore $d'(z) = 0$ and $d$ is constant. The potentials are
$$
U(x, y, z) = x^2 z + y^3 z^2 + k, \qquad k \in \mathbb{R} .
$$
