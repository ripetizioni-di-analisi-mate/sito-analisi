What is the counterclockwise circulation of $\left( - \dfrac{2 y}{x^{2} + y^{2}}, \  \dfrac{2 x}{x^{2} + y^{2}}\right)$ around the circle centered at the origin with radius $5$?

- $- 4 \pi$
- $4 \pi$
- $20 \pi$
- $0$

## Explanation

With $(x,y)=(5\cos t,5\sin t)$ the dot product with the tangent vector is $2$. The angle makes one full turn.
