The field $F(x, y) = (x^2 + y, \; xy)$ acts along the vertical segment from $(2, 0)$ to $(2, 3)$. What is the work of $F$?

- $\dfrac{33}{2}$
- $9$
- $-9$
- $6$

## Explanation

The segment is parametrized by $r(t) = (2, \; t)$, $t \in [0, 3]$, so $r'(t) = (0, 1)$. Since the first component of $r'(t)$ is zero, only the second component of the field, $F_2(2, t) = 2t$, remains in the dot product. The work is therefore
$$
\int_0^3 2t \, dt = 9 .
$$
The value $\dfrac{33}{2}$ is the integral of the first component $F_1(2, t) = 4 + t$, which gives no contribution on the vertical segment.
