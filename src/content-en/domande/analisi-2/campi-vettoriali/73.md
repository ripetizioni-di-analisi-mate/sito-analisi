The plane without the points $(0, 0)$ and $(4, 0)$ is the domain of a field $F$ of class $C^1$ and irrotational. The circulation of $F$ equals $3$ along the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ and $-1$ along the circle $(x - 4)^2 + y^2 = 1$, both traversed counterclockwise. What is the circulation of $F$ along the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 36$, traversed counterclockwise?

- $4$
- $3$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

The circle of radius $6$ encloses both removed points and the two circles of radius $1$, which are disjoint. Let $D$ be the region inside the large circle and outside the two small disks; the field is of class $C^1$ on an open set containing $D$, so **Green's theorem** applies to $D$. The positively oriented boundary consists of the large circle traversed counterclockwise and the two small ones traversed clockwise. The integrand is zero, because the field is irrotational, therefore, denoting by $C$ the required circulation,
$$
C - 3 - (-1) = 0 .
$$
Hence $C = 2$, the sum of the circulations around the two removed points.
