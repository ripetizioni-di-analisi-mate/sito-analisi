A field $F$ is of class $C^1$ and irrotational on the open disk $D = \{ (x, y) : x^2 + y^2 < 4 \}$. Which conclusion is correct?

- Nothing can be concluded without computing a circulation
- $F$ is conservative on $D$, because $D$ is simply connected
- $F$ is conservative on $D$ only if it is defined on all of $\mathbb{R}^2$
- $F$ is conservative on $D$ only if it is a constant field

## Explanation

The disk is convex, hence **simply connected**. The field is of class $C^1$ and irrotational on $D$, so the hypotheses of the theorem on irrotational fields on a simply connected open set are satisfied. The theorem guarantees that $F$ **is conservative** on $D$. The behavior of the field outside the disk plays no role.
