What is the clockwise circulation of $\left( - 2 y, \  3 x\right)$ around the circle $x^2+y^2=25$?

- $125 \pi$
- $- 25 \pi$
- $0$
- $- 125 \pi$

## Explanation

The circulation density is the constant $5$. Multiply by the disk area and reverse the sign for clockwise orientation.
