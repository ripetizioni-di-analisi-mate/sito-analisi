Which of the following sets is simply connected?

- $\mathbb{R}^3 \setminus \{(0, 0, 0)\}$
- $\mathbb{R}^2 \setminus \{(0, 0)\}$
- $\{ (x, y) : 1 < x^2 + y^2 < 4 \}$
- $\mathbb{R}^3$ minus the $z$-axis

## Explanation

In $\mathbb{R}^3$ minus the origin every closed curve can be continuously deformed until it shrinks to a point by going around the origin, so the set is **simply connected**. In the plane minus the origin the unit circle goes around the removed point and cannot be shrunk to a point without crossing it, therefore the set is not simply connected. The annulus has the disk $x^2 + y^2 \leq 1$ as a hole, and the circle of radius $\dfrac{3}{2}$ centered at the origin goes around it, so the annulus is not simply connected either. In space minus the $z$-axis a circle around the axis cannot be shrunk while remaining in the set, since the removed line cannot be bypassed.
