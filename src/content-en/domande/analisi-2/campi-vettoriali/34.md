What is the circulation of the field $F(x, y) = \left( \dfrac{-y}{x^2 + y^2}, \; \dfrac{x}{x^2 + y^2} \right)$ along the circle $(x - 3)^2 + y^2 = 1$, traversed counterclockwise?

- $2\pi$
- $-2\pi$
- $0$
- $\pi$

## Explanation

The field is of class $C^1$ and irrotational on $\mathbb{R}^2 \setminus \{(0, 0)\}$, since both cross derivatives equal $\dfrac{y^2 - x^2}{(x^2 + y^2)^2}$. The half-plane $x > 0$ is convex, hence simply connected, and contains the given circle, which has center $(3, 0)$ and radius $1$. On the half-plane the field is therefore conservative, and by the characterization theorem its circulation along every closed curve in the half-plane is zero. The required circulation therefore equals $0$. The value $2\pi$ is obtained instead along the circles centered at the origin, which go around the point excluded from the domain.
