Which vector field is the gradient of the function $f(x, y) = x^2 y + \sin y$?

- $(2xy, \; x^2 - \cos y)$
- $(x^2, \; 2xy + \cos y)$
- $(2xy + \cos y, \; x^2)$
- $(2xy, \; x^2 + \cos y)$

## Explanation

The gradient of $f$ is the field $\nabla f = (f_x, f_y)$, whose first component is the partial derivative with respect to $x$ and whose second component is the partial derivative with respect to $y$. Differentiating with respect to $x$ with $y$ fixed, the term $\sin y$ is constant, so $f_x = 2xy$. Differentiating with respect to $y$ with $x$ fixed gives $f_y = x^2 + \cos y$, since the derivative of $\sin y$ is $\cos y$. The gradient is therefore the field $\nabla f = (2xy, \; x^2 + \cos y)$, which is **conservative** on $\mathbb{R}^2$ with potential $f$.
