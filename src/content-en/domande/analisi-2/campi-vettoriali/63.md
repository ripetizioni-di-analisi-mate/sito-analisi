What is the clockwise circulation of $\left( - 3 y, \  5 x\right)$ around the circle $x^2+y^2=4$?

- $- 32 \pi$
- $32 \pi$
- $- 8 \pi$
- $- 16 \pi$

## Explanation

The circulation density is the constant $8$. Multiply by the disk area and reverse the sign for clockwise orientation.
