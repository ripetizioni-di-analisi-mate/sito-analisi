What is the work of the field $F(x, y) = (2x + y, \; x + 2y)$ along the curve $r(t) = (e^t, \; t^2 + 1)$, $t \in [0, 1]$?

- $e^2 + 2e + 4$
- $e^2 + 2e + 1$
- $e^2 + 4e$
- $-e^2 - 2e - 1$

## Explanation

The field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$, the cross derivatives both equal $1$ and $\mathbb{R}^2$ is simply connected, so the field is conservative. Integrating $2x + y$ with respect to $x$ gives $U(x, y) = x^2 + xy + c(y)$, and the condition $U_y = x + 2y$ forces $c'(y) = 2y$, therefore a potential is $U(x, y) = x^2 + xy + y^2$. The curve starts at $r(0) = (1, 1)$ and ends at $r(1) = (e, 2)$. By the theorem on the **work of a conservative field** the work equals
$$
U(e, 2) - U(1, 1) = (e^2 + 2e + 4) - 3 = e^2 + 2e + 1 .
$$
