What is the circulation of the field $F(x, y) = \left( \dfrac{-y}{x^2 + y^2}, \; \dfrac{x}{x^2 + y^2} \right)$ along the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{4} + y^2 = 1$, traversed clockwise?

- $-2\pi$
- $2\pi$
- $0$
- $-4\pi$

## Explanation

Let $D$ be the region between the ellipse and the circle $x^2 + y^2 = \dfrac{1}{4}$, which lies inside the ellipse. The field is of class $C^1$ and irrotational on $\mathbb{R}^2 \setminus \{(0, 0)\}$, which contains $D$, so by **Green's theorem** the circulation along the positively oriented boundary of $D$ is zero. Therefore the counterclockwise circulation along the ellipse equals the counterclockwise circulation along the circle, which is $2\pi$ as for every circle centered at the origin. In the clockwise direction the circulation changes sign, so it equals $-2\pi$.
