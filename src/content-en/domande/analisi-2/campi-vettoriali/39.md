A field is defined on the disjoint union of two open disks. Two potentials $U,V$ agree at the centre of the first disk. What follows?

- They agree throughout the first disk and may differ by a constant on the second.
- They must agree on both disks.
- They may differ by a nonconstant linear function on the first disk.
- Their differences on the two disks must have opposite signs.

## Explanation

The gradient of $U-V$ vanishes. A function with zero gradient is constant on each disk. The given value fixes the constant only on the first component.
