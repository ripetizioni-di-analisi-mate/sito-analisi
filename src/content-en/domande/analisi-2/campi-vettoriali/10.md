## Explanation

The parametrization traverses the ellipse counterclockwise, so the area is computed with the formula $\operatorname{area} = \dfrac{1}{2} \displaystyle\oint (x \, dy - y \, dx)$. With $x = 5 \cos t$ and $y = 2 \sin t$ we have $dx = -5 \sin t \, dt$ and $dy = 2 \cos t \, dt$, therefore
$$
x \, dy - y \, dx = (10 \cos^2 t + 10 \sin^2 t) \, dt = 10 \, dt .
$$
The area is therefore
$$
\frac{1}{2} \int_0^{2\pi} 10 \, dt = 10\pi ,
$$
which coincides with $\pi a b$ for the semi-axes $a = 5$ and $b = 2$.
