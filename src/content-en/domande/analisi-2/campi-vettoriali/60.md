What is the circulation of $\left( 2 x + 3 y, \  3 x + 5 y\right)$ along the counterclockwise perimeter of the triangle with vertices $(0,0),(1,0),(0,5)$?

- $10$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $15$

## Explanation

The two cross derivatives coincide and both equal $3$. The integrand in Green’s formula is therefore zero.
