The field $F(x, y) = \bigl( 2xy^3, \; 3x^2 y^2 \bigr)$ is conservative on $\mathbb{R}^2$. Along which of the following piecewise regular curves is the work of $F$ zero?

- A curve from $(1, 1)$ to $(1, -1)$
- A curve from $(0, 0)$ to $(1, 1)$
- A curve from $(1, 2)$ to $(2, 1)$
- A curve from $(1, 1)$ to $(-1, 1)$

## Explanation

Integrating $F_1 = 2xy^3$ with respect to $x$ gives $U(x, y) = x^2 y^3 + c(y)$, and the condition $U_y = 3x^2 y^2$ forces $c'(y) = 0$, so a potential is $U(x, y) = x^2 y^3$. By the theorem on the **work of a conservative field** the work from $P$ to $Q$ equals $U(Q) - U(P)$ along any curve. The potential equals $1$ both at $(1, 1)$ and at $(-1, 1)$, therefore along a curve from $(1, 1)$ to $(-1, 1)$ the work is **zero**. In the other cases the work equals $U(1, -1) - U(1, 1) = -2$, $U(1, 1) - U(0, 0) = 1$ and $U(2, 1) - U(1, 2) = 4 - 8 = -4$.
