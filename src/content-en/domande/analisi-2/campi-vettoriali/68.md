What is the counterclockwise circulation of $\left( - \dfrac{3 y}{x^{2} + y^{2}}, \  \dfrac{3 x}{x^{2} + y^{2}}\right)$ around the circle centered at the origin with radius $2$?

- $- 6 \pi$
- $12 \pi$
- $0$
- $6 \pi$

## Explanation

With $(x,y)=(2\cos t,2\sin t)$ the dot product with the tangent is $3$. The angle makes one full turn.
