## Explanation

A conservative field of class $C^1$ is irrotational, so the equality $\dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = \dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x}$ is a *necessary* condition. For this field we have $\dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = x^2$ and $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} = y^2$, and the two functions differ, for example, at the point $(1, 0)$. The necessary condition is not satisfied, therefore the field **is not conservative** on $\mathbb{R}^2$.

The derivatives $\dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial x}$ and $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial y}$ do not appear in the irrotationality condition. The simple connectedness of $\mathbb{R}^2$ is involved only in the opposite implication, from irrotational to conservative.
