What is the work of the field $F(x, y) = \bigl( x^2 - y, \; x + y^2 \bigr)$ along the semicircle in the figure, from $A = (2, 0)$ to $B = (-2, 0)$?

::grafico{xmin=-3 xmax=3 ymin=-0.8 ymax=2.8 uguale=1 curve="2*cos(t),2*sin(t),0,3.1416" vettori="0.4,1.96,-0.4,1.96" punti="2,0,pieno,A;-2,0,pieno,B" titolo="The semicircle $x^2 + y^2 = 4$, $y \geq 0$, traversed from $A = (2, 0)$ to $B = (-2, 0)$"}

- $4\pi$
- $4\pi - \dfrac{16}{3}$
- $4\pi + \dfrac{16}{3}$
- $8\pi - \dfrac{16}{3}$

## Explanation

The semicircle is not closed; adding the segment from $B$ to $A$ on the $x$-axis gives the positively oriented boundary of the half-disk $D$ of radius $2$. By **Green's theorem** the circulation along this boundary is the integral over $D$ of $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = 1 - (-1) = 2$, that is, twice the area $2\pi$, so it equals $4\pi$. On the segment, parametrized by $r(t) = (t, 0)$ with $t \in [-2, 2]$, one has $F_1(t, 0) = t^2$, therefore its contribution is
$$
\int_{-2}^{2} t^2 \, dt = \frac{16}{3} .
$$
The work along the semicircle is the circulation minus the contribution of the segment, that is, $4\pi - \dfrac{16}{3}$.
