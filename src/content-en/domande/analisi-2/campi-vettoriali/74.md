What is the difference between the work of $\left( 3 y, \  5 x\right)$ along $(0,0)\to(1,0)\to(1,2)$ and along $(0,0)\to(0,2)\to(1,2)$?

- $16$
- $4$
- $-4$
- $0$

## Explanation

The first work is $10$ and the second is $6$. Their difference is the counterclockwise circulation around the rectangle.
