What is the difference between the work of $\left( 3 y, \  5 x\right)$ along the path $(0,0)\to(1,0)\to(1,2)$ and along the path $(0,0)\to(0,2)\to(1,2)$?

- $16$
- $4$
- $-4$
- $0$

## Explanation

The work along the first path is $10$ and along the second is $6$. Their difference is the counterclockwise circulation around the rectangle.
