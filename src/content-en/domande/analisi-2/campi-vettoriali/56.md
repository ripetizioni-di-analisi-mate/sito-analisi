The regular domain $D$ has area $5$. What is $\displaystyle\oint_{\partial D} 3x \, dy - y \, dx$ along the positively oriented boundary of $D$?

- $15$
- $10$
- $4$
- $20$

## Explanation

The integral is the circulation of the field $F = (-y, \; 3x)$, which is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$, so **Green's theorem** applies. The integrand of the double integral is
$$
\frac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = 3 - (-1) = 4 ,
$$
therefore the integral equals $4$ times the area of $D$, that is, $20$. The same value follows from the area formulas, since $\displaystyle\oint_{\partial D} 3x \, dy = 3 \cdot 5 = 15$ and $-\displaystyle\oint_{\partial D} y \, dx = 5$. The value $10$ is obtained by assigning the value $-5$ to $-\displaystyle\oint y \, dx$.
