Which of the following fields on $\mathbb{R}^3$ has curl equal to $(0, 0, 2)$ at every point?

- $(y, \; -x, \; 0)$
- $(-y, \; 0, \; 0)$
- $(x, \; y, \; 2z)$
- $(-y, \; x, \; 0)$

## Explanation

In the four fields the components do not depend on $z$ and the third component depends neither on $x$ nor on $y$, so the first two components of the curl are zero. It remains to compute the third component $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y}$. For $(-y, x, 0)$ it equals $1 - (-1) = 2$, therefore the curl is $(0, 0, 2)$. For $(y, -x, 0)$ it equals $-2$, for $(-y, 0, 0)$ it equals $1$ and for $(x, y, 2z)$ it equals $0$.
