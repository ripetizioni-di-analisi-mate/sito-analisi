## Explanation

The field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^2 \setminus \{(0, 0)\}$ and is irrotational, since both cross derivatives equal $\dfrac{y^2 - x^2}{(x^2 + y^2)^2}$. On $\mathbb{R}^2 \setminus \{(0, 0)\}$, which is not simply connected, the field is not conservative, since its circulation along the unit circle equals $2\pi$. The half-plane $x > 0$ is convex, hence simply connected, therefore on the half-plane the theorem on irrotational fields on a simply connected open set applies and the field is **conservative**.

A potential on the half-plane is $U(x, y) = \arctan \dfrac{y}{x}$, whose gradient coincides with $F$ at every point with $x > 0$. By the theorem on the **work of a conservative field** the work along every curve in the half-plane from $(1, 0)$ to $(1, 1)$ equals
$$
U(1, 1) - U(1, 0) = \arctan 1 - \arctan 0 = \frac{\pi}{4} .
$$
