What is the curl of the field $F(x, y, z) = (xy, \; yz, \; zx)$?

- $(y, \; z, \; x)$
- $(-z, \; -x, \; -y)$
- $(0, \; 0, \; 0)$
- $(-y, \; -z, \; -x)$

## Explanation

The curl is the field
$$
\operatorname{curl} F = \left( \frac{\partial F_3}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial F_2}{\partial z}, \;\; \frac{\partial F_1}{\partial z} - \frac{\partial F_3}{\partial x}, \;\; \frac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} \right) .
$$
For $F_1 = xy$, $F_2 = yz$, $F_3 = zx$ we have $\dfrac{\partial F_3}{\partial y} = 0$ and $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial z} = y$, so the first component is $-y$. In the same way $\dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial z} = 0$ and $\dfrac{\partial F_3}{\partial x} = z$, therefore the second component is $-z$, while $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} = 0$ and $\dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = x$ give the third component $-x$. The curl is $(-y, -z, -x)$, which is not zero, so the field **is not conservative** on $\mathbb{R}^3$.
