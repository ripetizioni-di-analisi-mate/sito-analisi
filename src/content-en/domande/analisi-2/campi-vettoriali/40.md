Let $\partial T$ be the boundary of the triangle $T$ with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(3, 0)$, $(0, 2)$, traversed counterclockwise. What is the value of $\displaystyle\oint_{\partial T} y \, dx$?

- $3$
- $-3$
- $6$
- $-6$

## Explanation

The boundary is positively oriented, so by the area formula $-\displaystyle\oint_{\partial T} y \, dx = \operatorname{area}(T)$. The triangle is a right triangle with legs $3$ and $2$, therefore its area is $\dfrac{3 \cdot 2}{2} = 3$ and the integral equals $-3$. The same value is obtained by integrating one side at a time. On the horizontal leg $y = 0$ and on the vertical leg $dx = 0$, so only the hypotenuse remains, parametrized by $r(t) = (3 - 3t, \; 2t)$ with $t \in [0, 1]$, on which the integral is $\displaystyle\int_0^1 2t \cdot (-3) \, dt = -3$.
