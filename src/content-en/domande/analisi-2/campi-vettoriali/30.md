What is the work of the field $F(x, y) = (xy, \; y - x)$ along the segment from $(0, 0)$ to $(1, 2)$?

- $\dfrac{7}{6}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{5}{3}$

## Explanation

The cross derivatives are $\dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} = x$ and $\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} = -1$, so the field is not conservative and the work is computed with the definition. The segment is parametrized by $r(t) = (t, 2t)$, $t \in [0, 1]$, so $r'(t) = (1, 2)$ and $F(r(t)) = (2t^2, \; t)$. The dot product equals $2t^2 \cdot 1 + t \cdot 2 = 2t^2 + 2t$, therefore the work is
$$
\int_0^1 (2t^2 + 2t) \, dt = \frac{2}{3} + 1 = \frac{5}{3} .
$$
