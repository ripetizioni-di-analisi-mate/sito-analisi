What is the area of the region enclosed by the closed curve $r(t) = (1 - t^2, \; t - t^3)$, $t \in [-1, 1]$, which is traversed counterclockwise?

- $\dfrac{16}{15}$
- $\dfrac{8}{15}$
- $-\dfrac{8}{15}$
- $\dfrac{4}{15}$

## Explanation

The curve starts at the origin and returns to it, since $r(-1) = r(1) = (0, 0)$, and it is traversed counterclockwise, so the area is computed with the formula $\operatorname{area} = \displaystyle\oint x \, dy$. With $x = 1 - t^2$ and $y = t - t^3$ we have $dy = (1 - 3t^2) \, dt$, therefore
$$
\operatorname{area} = \int_{-1}^{1} (1 - t^2)(1 - 3t^2) \, dt = \int_{-1}^{1} (1 - 4t^2 + 3t^4) \, dt = 2 - \frac{8}{3} + \frac{6}{5} = \frac{8}{15} .
$$
