## Explanation

The boundary of the quadrilateral is the closed polygonal path joining the vertices counterclockwise, so the area is $\dfrac{1}{2} \displaystyle\oint (x \, dy - y \, dx)$. On the side from the vertex $(x_i, y_i)$ to the vertex $(x_{i+1}, y_{i+1})$, parametrized by $r(t) = \bigl( x_i + t(x_{i+1} - x_i), \; y_i + t(y_{i+1} - y_i) \bigr)$ with $t \in [0, 1]$, the integral of $x \, dy - y \, dx$ equals $x_i \, y_{i+1} - x_{i+1} \, y_i$. The four sides give
$$
0 \cdot 1 - 4 \cdot 0 = 0, \qquad 4 \cdot 4 - 3 \cdot 1 = 13, \qquad 3 \cdot 3 - 1 \cdot 4 = 5, \qquad 1 \cdot 0 - 0 \cdot 3 = 0 .
$$
The area is therefore $\dfrac{1}{2} (0 + 13 + 5 + 0) = 9$.
