For the function $f(x, y) = 5 - x^2 - 4y^2$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$, which statement is true?

- The absolute minimum is $5$ and the absolute maximum does not exist
- The absolute maximum is $5$ and the absolute minimum is $0$
- The origin is a saddle point with $f(0, 0) = 5$
- The absolute maximum is $5$ and the absolute minimum does not exist

## Explanation

Since $x^2 + 4y^2 \geq 0$, we have $f(x, y) \leq 5 = f(0, 0)$ for every $(x, y)$, so the origin is an **absolute maximum point** with value $5$. Along the $x$-axis we have $f(x, 0) = 5 - x^2$, which tends to $-\infty$ as $x \to +\infty$. Therefore $f$ is not bounded below and the **absolute minimum does not exist**.
