What is the maximum of $f(x, y, z) = x^2 + y^2 + z^2$ on the curve where the cylinder $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ meets the plane $x + y + z = 1$?

- $3 + 2\sqrt{2}$
- $4$
- $2 + \sqrt{2}$
- $4 + 2\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

On the curve we have $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ and $z = 1 - x - y$, so
$$
f = 1 + (1 - x - y)^2 .
$$
With $x = \cos t$, $y = \sin t$ we have $x + y = \cos t + \sin t$, which takes all the values in $[-\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{2}]$. The quantity $(1 - x - y)^2$ is largest for $x + y = -\sqrt{2}$, where it equals $(1 + \sqrt{2})^2 = 3 + 2\sqrt{2}$. Therefore the **maximum** is $1 + 3 + 2\sqrt{2} = 4 + 2\sqrt{2}$.
