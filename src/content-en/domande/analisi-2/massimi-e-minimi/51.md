What are the critical points of $f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2 - 2\ln(xy)$, defined for $xy > 0$?

- Only the point $(1, 1)$
- The points $(1, 1)$ and $(-1, -1)$
- The points $(1, -1)$ and $(-1, 1)$
- The four points $(\pm 1, \pm 1)$

## Explanation

The domain $xy > 0$ is the union of the first and the third quadrant, without the axes. Since $\ln(xy)$ has derivative $\dfrac{1}{x}$ with respect to $x$ and $\dfrac{1}{y}$ with respect to $y$, the system for the critical points is
$$
\begin{cases}
2x - \dfrac{2}{x} = 0 \\[8pt]
2y - \dfrac{2}{y} = 0
\end{cases}
$$
which gives $x = \pm 1$ and $y = \pm 1$. Of the four points only $(1, 1)$ and $(-1, -1)$ satisfy $xy > 0$, so the critical points are **$(1, 1)$ and $(-1, -1)$**.
