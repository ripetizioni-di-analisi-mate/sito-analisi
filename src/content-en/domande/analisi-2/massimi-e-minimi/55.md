What is the value of the function $f(x, y) = x^4 + y^4 - 2(x - y)^2$ at its local minimum points?

- $-4$
- $-8$
- $-16$
- $0$

## Explanation

The system for the critical points is
$$
\begin{cases}
4x^3 - 4(x - y) = 0 \\[4pt]
4y^3 + 4(x - y) = 0
\end{cases}
$$
Adding the two equations gives $x^3 + y^3 = 0$, so $y = -x$. Substituting into the first one gives $4x^3 - 8x = 0$, that is $x = 0$ or $x = \pm\sqrt{2}$. At $(\sqrt{2}, -\sqrt{2})$ and at $(-\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{2})$ the second derivatives are $f_{xx} = 12x^2 - 4 = 20$, $f_{xy} = 4$, $f_{yy} = 20$, so $\det H = 384 > 0$ with $f_{xx} > 0$, and the two points are **local minimum points**. The value is
$$
f(\sqrt{2}, -\sqrt{2}) = 4 + 4 - 2\,(2\sqrt{2})^2 = 8 - 16 = -8 .
$$
At the origin $f_{xx} = f_{yy} = -4$ and $f_{xy} = 4$, so $\det H = 0$; moreover $f(x, x) = 2x^4 > 0$ and $f(x, 0) = x^4 - 2x^2 < 0$ near the origin, therefore the origin is a saddle point and gives no other minima.
