The function $f(x, y) = x^3 - 3xy^2$ has a critical point at the origin with zero Hessian. The lines through the origin on which $f$ vanishes divide the plane into sectors. In how many of these sectors is $f$ positive?

- $2$
- $1$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

The function factors as
$$
f(x, y) = x\,(x^2 - 3y^2) = x\,(x - \sqrt{3}\,y)(x + \sqrt{3}\,y) ,
$$
so it vanishes on the three lines $x = 0$, $y = \dfrac{x}{\sqrt{3}}$ and $y = -\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{3}}$, which divide the plane into six sectors. Crossing one of these lines changes the sign of exactly one of the three factors, therefore the sign of $f$ alternates from one sector to the next. It follows that $f$ is positive in **three** sectors and negative in the other three. In particular, in every neighborhood of the origin $f$ takes both signs, so the origin is a saddle point.
