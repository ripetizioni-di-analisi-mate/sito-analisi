What is the minimum distance from the origin of the points of the curve $xy = 4$?

- $2\sqrt{2}$
- $8$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

We minimize the square of the distance $f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2$ with the constraint $g(x, y) = xy - 4 = 0$. The gradient $\nabla g = (y, x)$ vanishes only at the origin, which does not belong to the curve, so the candidates solve the Lagrange system
$$
\begin{cases}
2x = \lambda y \\[4pt]
2y = \lambda x \\[4pt]
xy = 4
\end{cases}
$$
Multiplying the first equation by $x$ and the second by $y$ gives $2x^2 = \lambda xy = 2y^2$, so $x^2 = y^2$. Since $xy = 4 > 0$, the two coordinates have the same sign, therefore the candidates are $(2, 2)$ and $(-2, -2)$, where $x^2 + y^2 = 8$. Along the curve $x^2 + y^2$ tends to $+\infty$ as $|x| \to +\infty$ and as $x \to 0$, so the minimum exists and is attained at the candidates. The **minimum distance** is therefore $\sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2}$.
