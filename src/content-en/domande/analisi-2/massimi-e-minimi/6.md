For the function $f(x, y) = x^2 - y^2$ on the rectangle $K = [-1, 2] \times [0, 1]$, what is the absolute minimum?

- $-1$
- $0$
- $-2$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The function is continuous and $K$ is compact, so by the **extreme value theorem** the absolute minimum exists. The only critical point is $(0, 0)$, which lies on the side $y = 0$ and is therefore not interior to $K$. For every $(x, y) \in K$ we have $x^2 \geq 0$ and $y^2 \leq 1$, so $f(x, y) \geq 0 - 1 = -1$. The value $-1$ is attained at $(0, 1)$, therefore the **absolute minimum** is $-1$.
