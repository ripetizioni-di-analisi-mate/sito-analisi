When maximizing $x$ subject to $g(x,y)=x^2+y^2=4$, what is the multiplier at the maximum under $\nabla f=\lambda\nabla g$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{4}$
- $4$

## Explanation

The maximum is at $(2,0)$. The gradients at that point are $(1,0)$ and $(4,0)$.
