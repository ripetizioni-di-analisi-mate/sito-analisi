What is the minimum of $f(x, y, z) = z - 2x - 4y$ on the points of the paraboloid $z = x^2 + y^2$?

- $-5$
- $-1$
- $5$
- $-10$

## Explanation

The constraint is explicit with respect to $z$, so substituting $z = x^2 + y^2$ gives the function of two variables
$$
h(x, y) = x^2 + y^2 - 2x - 4y = (x - 1)^2 + (y - 2)^2 - 5 .
$$
Since $(x - 1)^2 \geq 0$ and $(y - 2)^2 \geq 0$, we have $h(x, y) \geq -5$ for every $(x, y)$, with equality only at $(1, 2)$. The corresponding point of the paraboloid is $(1, 2, 5)$, therefore the **minimum** of $f$ on the paraboloid is $-5$.
