A rectangular field along the straight bank of a river is to be fenced, putting the fence only on the other three sides, with $40\ \mathrm{m}$ of fence in all. What is the maximum area of the field?

- $100\ \mathrm{m}^2$
- $400\ \mathrm{m}^2$
- $\dfrac{1600}{9}\ \mathrm{m}^2$
- $200\ \mathrm{m}^2$

## Explanation

Let $x$ be the side parallel to the river and $y$ each of the two perpendicular sides, with $x, y > 0$. The constraint is $x + 2y = 40$, so $x = 40 - 2y$ with $y \in (0, 20)$, and the area is
$$
A(y) = (40 - 2y)\, y = 40y - 2y^2 .
$$
The derivative $A'(y) = 40 - 4y$ vanishes at $y = 10$, with $A$ increasing before and decreasing after, so the maximum is at $y = 10$, $x = 20$. The **maximum area** is $A(10) = 200\ \mathrm{m}^2$.
