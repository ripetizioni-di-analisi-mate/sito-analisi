Which point $P = (x, y)$ of the plane minimizes the sum of the squares of its distances from the points $(0, 0)$, $(4, 0)$ and $(2, 6)$?

- $(2, 3)$
- $(2, 2)$
- $(3, 2)$
- $(2, 0)$

## Explanation

The function to minimize is
$$
f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2 + (x - 4)^2 + y^2 + (x - 2)^2 + (y - 6)^2 .
$$
The partial derivatives are $f_x = 2x + 2(x - 4) + 2(x - 2) = 6x - 12$ and $f_y = 2y + 2y + 2(y - 6) = 6y - 12$, so the only critical point is $(2, 2)$. The function is a second-degree polynomial with $f_{xx} = f_{yy} = 6$ and $f_{xy} = 0$, and it tends to $+\infty$ at infinity, therefore $(2, 2)$ is the **absolute minimum point**. It is the centroid of the three points, whose coordinates are the averages of the coordinates.
