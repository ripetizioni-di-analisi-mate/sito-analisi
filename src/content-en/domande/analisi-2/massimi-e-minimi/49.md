What is the only critical point of $2 \left(x - 1\right)^{2} + 3 \left(y + 1\right)^{2} + 5$?

- $\left( 1, \  -1\right)$
- $\left( 0, \  0\right)$
- $\left( -1, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 1, \  1\right)$

## Explanation

Set both partial derivatives to zero. The first gives $x=1$ and the second gives $y=-1$.
