A function of class $C^2$ has the given Hessian matrix at one of its critical points. In which case is the point a local maximum point?

- $\begin{pmatrix} -2 & 3 \\ 3 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -2 & 1 \\ 1 & -3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 2 \\ 2 & -4 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

For each matrix we look at the determinant and at the top-left entry $f_{xx}$. For $\begin{pmatrix} -2 & 3 \\ 3 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$ we have $\det H = 2 - 9 = -7 < 0$, so the point is a saddle point. For $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ we have $\det H = 5 > 0$ and $f_{xx} = 2 > 0$, so the point is a local minimum point, while for $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 2 \\ 2 & -4 \end{pmatrix}$ we have $\det H = 4 - 4 = 0$ and the criterion does not decide. For $\begin{pmatrix} -2 & 1 \\ 1 & -3 \end{pmatrix}$ we have $\det H = 6 - 1 = 5 > 0$ and $f_{xx} = -2 < 0$, therefore by the **Hessian criterion** the point is a **local maximum point**.
