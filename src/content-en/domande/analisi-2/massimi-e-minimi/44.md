Among the rectangles with sides parallel to the axes and all four vertices on the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$, what is the maximum area?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

Let $(x, y)$ be the vertex in the first quadrant, with $x, y > 0$. The sides measure $2x$ and $2y$, so the area is $A = 4xy$, with the constraint $x^2 + y^2 = 1$. Setting $x = \cos t$ and $y = \sin t$ with $t \in \left(0, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$, we get $A(t) = 4\cos t \sin t = 2\sin 2t$. This function has maximum $2$ for $t = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$, that is, for $x = y = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$. The rectangle of maximum area is therefore the **square** with side length $\sqrt{2}$, and the **maximum area** is $2$.
