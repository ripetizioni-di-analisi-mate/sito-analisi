What kind of point is the origin for $3 x^{4} - 5 y^{6}$?

- Noncritical point
- Saddle point
- Strict local minimum
- Strict local maximum

## Explanation

On the $x$ axis the value is positive away from the origin. On the $y$ axis it is negative, so every neighborhood contains both signs.
