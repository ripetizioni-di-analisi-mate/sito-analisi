From a square sheet with side $12$, four equal small squares with side $x$ are cut out at the corners and the flaps are folded up, giving a box without a lid. What is the maximum volume of the box?

- $128$
- $108$
- $144$
- $256$

## Explanation

The box has a square base with side $12 - 2x$ and height $x$, with $x \in (0, 6)$, so the volume is
$$
V(x) = x\,(12 - 2x)^2 .
$$
The derivative is $V'(x) = (12 - 2x)^2 - 4x\,(12 - 2x) = (12 - 2x)(12 - 6x)$, which on $(0, 6)$ vanishes only at $x = 2$, positive before and negative after. Therefore the maximum is at $x = 2$ and the **maximum volume** is $V(2) = 2 \cdot 8^2 = 128$.
