In maximising $f(x,y)=x+y$ under $g(x,y)=x^2+y^2-2=0$, the multiplier at $(1,1)$ is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ using $\nabla f=\lambda\nabla g$. What is the multiplier if the same constraint is written $5g=0$?

- $\dfrac52$
- $\dfrac12$
- $5$
- $\dfrac1{10}$

## Explanation

The new constraint gradient is five times the original one. To preserve the same equality with $\nabla f$, the multiplier must be divided by five.
