What is the value of $f(x, y) = \sin x + \sin y + \sin(x + y)$ at its local maximum point in the open square $(0, \pi) \times (0, \pi)$?

- $\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- $3$
- $\sqrt{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The system for the critical points is $\cos x + \cos(x + y) = 0$ and $\cos y + \cos(x + y) = 0$. Subtracting gives $\cos x = \cos y$, so in the square $x = y$, and the first equation becomes $\cos x + \cos 2x = 0$, that is $2\cos^2 x + \cos x - 1 = 0$. The solutions are $\cos x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\cos x = -1$; only the first gives a point of the open square, $x = y = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$. At that point $f_{xx} = f_{yy} = -\sqrt{3}$ and $f_{xy} = -\sin\dfrac{2\pi}{3} = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, so $\det H = 3 - \dfrac{3}{4} > 0$ with $f_{xx} < 0$, and the point is a **local maximum point**. The value is
$$
f\left(\frac{\pi}{3}, \frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} .
$$
