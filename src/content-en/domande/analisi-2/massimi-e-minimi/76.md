What is the minimum of $f(x, y, z) = xy + yz + zx$ on the sphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 3$?

- $-3$
- $0$
- $-1$
- $-\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

Expanding the square of the sum gives
$$
(x + y + z)^2 = x^2 + y^2 + z^2 + 2(xy + yz + zx) .
$$
On the sphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 3$, so $(x + y + z)^2 = 3 + 2f$. Since $(x + y + z)^2 \geq 0$, it follows that $f \geq -\dfrac{3}{2}$ on the whole sphere. Equality holds at the points of the sphere with $x + y + z = 0$, for example at $\left(\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, -\sqrt{2}\right)$, therefore the **constrained minimum** is $-\dfrac{3}{2}$.
