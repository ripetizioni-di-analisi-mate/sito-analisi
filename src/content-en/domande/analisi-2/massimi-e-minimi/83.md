Among the pairs of positive numbers $x, y$ with $x + y = 9$, what is the maximum value of $x^2 y$?

- $81$
- $\dfrac{729}{8}$
- $108$
- $162$

## Explanation

Solving the constraint for $y = 9 - x$, with $x \in (0, 9)$, we study
$$
h(x) = x^2 (9 - x) = 9x^2 - x^3 .
$$
The derivative $h'(x) = 18x - 3x^2 = 3x(6 - x)$ is positive on $(0, 6)$ and negative on $(6, 9)$, so $h$ has its maximum at $x = 6$. Then $y = 3$ and the **maximum value** is $36 \cdot 3 = 108$.
