The points of the curve $x^4 + y^4 = 2$ are not all at the same distance from the origin. What is the minimum of $x^2 + y^2$ on this curve?

- $1$
- $2$
- $\sqrt[4]{2}$
- $\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The Lagrange system is
$$
\begin{cases}
2x = 4\lambda x^3 \\[4pt]
2y = 4\lambda y^3 \\[4pt]
x^4 + y^4 = 2
\end{cases}
$$
The first equation gives $x = 0$ or $x^2 = \dfrac{1}{2\lambda}$, and the same holds for $y$. If $x = 0$, the constraint gives $y^4 = 2$, so $f = y^2 = \sqrt{2}$; the same value is obtained with $y = 0$. If $x$ and $y$ are both nonzero, then $x^2 = y^2$, the constraint gives $x^4 = 1$ and we get $f = 2$. Since the constraint is compact and $\nabla g = (4x^3, 4y^3)$ vanishes only at the origin, which is not on the constraint, the **constrained minimum** is $\sqrt{2}$.
