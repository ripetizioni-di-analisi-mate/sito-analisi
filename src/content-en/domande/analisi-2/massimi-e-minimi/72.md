What is the maximum of $3x+5y+2z$ on the unit sphere?

- $38$
- $\sqrt{38}$
- $10$
- $5$

## Explanation

The dot product with a point on the sphere is at most the norm of the coefficient vector. Equality is attained by taking the unit vector obtained by normalizing $\left( 3, \  5, \  2\right)$.
