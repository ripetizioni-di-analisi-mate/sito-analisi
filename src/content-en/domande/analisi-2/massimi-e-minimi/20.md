For which values of the real parameter $k$ does the Hessian test establish that the origin is a local minimum point of $f(x, y) = x^2 + kxy + y^2$?

- $-2 < k < 2$
- $k < 2$
- $|k| > 2$
- $0 < k < 2$

## Explanation

The partial derivatives $f_x = 2x + ky$ and $f_y = kx + 2y$ vanish at the origin, so the origin is a critical point for every $k$. The second derivatives are $f_{xx} = 2$, $f_{xy} = k$ and $f_{yy} = 2$, therefore $\det H = 4 - k^2$. The criterion gives a local minimum when $\det H > 0$ and $f_{xx} > 0$. The condition $f_{xx} = 2 > 0$ is satisfied for every $k$, while $4 - k^2 > 0$ is equivalent to $k^2 < 4$. Hence the criterion establishes a **local minimum** for $-2 < k < 2$.
