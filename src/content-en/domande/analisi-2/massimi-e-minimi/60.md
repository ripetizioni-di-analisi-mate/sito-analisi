When maximizing $x$ subject to $g(x,y)=x^2+y^2=25$, what is the multiplier at the maximum point using the convention $\nabla f=\lambda\nabla g$?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{10}$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$

## Explanation

The maximum is at $(5,0)$. The gradients at that point are $(1,0)$ and $(10,0)$.
