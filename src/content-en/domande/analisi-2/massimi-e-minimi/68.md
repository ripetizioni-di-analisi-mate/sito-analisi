What minimum value does $x^2 + 2y^2$ take as the point $(x, y)$ moves along the line $x + y = 3$?

- $9$
- $3$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$

## Explanation

With $g(x, y) = x + y - 3$ the Lagrange system is
$$
\begin{cases}
2x = \lambda \\[4pt]
4y = \lambda \\[4pt]
x + y = 3
\end{cases}
$$
The first two equations give $x = 2y$, and substituting into the constraint gives $3y = 3$, so the candidate is $(2, 1)$, with $f(2, 1) = 4 + 2 = 6$. On the line $f = x^2 + 2(3 - x)^2$, which tends to $+\infty$ as $|x| \to +\infty$, therefore the minimum exists and the **constrained minimum** is $6$.
