For the problem of finding the extrema of $f(x, y) = x + y$ with the constraint $g(x, y) = x^2 + y^2 - 2 = 0$, what is the multiplier $\lambda$ at the point $(1, 1)$, with the convention $\nabla f = \lambda\, \nabla g$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $1$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

At the point $(1, 1)$ we have $\nabla f = (1, 1)$ and $\nabla g = (2x, 2y) = (2, 2)$. The equality $(1, 1) = \lambda\,(2, 2)$ is satisfied for
$$
\lambda = \frac{1}{2} .
$$
