A rectangular box has the sum of the lengths of its twelve edges equal to $24$. What is the maximum volume?

- $64$
- $8$
- $512$
- $\dfrac{512}{27}$

## Explanation

Let $x$, $y$, $z$ be the three dimensions. The sum of the twelve edges is $4(x + y + z) = 24$, so the constraint is $x + y + z = 6$ and the volume to be maximized is $V = xyz$. The Lagrange system gives $yz = \lambda$, $xz = \lambda$ and $xy = \lambda$. For $x, y, z > 0$ the first two equations give $x = y$, the second and the third give $y = z$, and the constraint gives $x = y = z = 2$. The set of points with $x, y, z \geq 0$ and $x + y + z = 6$ is compact, so by the **extreme value theorem** the volume has a maximum. Since $V = 0$ when one dimension is zero and $V > 0$ otherwise, the maximum is at the only interior candidate, and the **maximum volume** is $V = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 8$, attained by the cube.
