On the sphere $x^2+y^2+z^2=12$, impose the additional constraint $x+y+z=0$. What is the maximum of $xy$ on the resulting curve?

- $3$
- $4$
- $2$
- $6$

## Explanation

Substituting $z=-x-y$ into the first constraint gives $x^2+y^2+xy=6$. Since $x^2+y^2\ge2xy$, we obtain $xy\le2$. This value is attained at $(x,y,z)=(\sqrt2,\sqrt2,-2\sqrt2)$.
