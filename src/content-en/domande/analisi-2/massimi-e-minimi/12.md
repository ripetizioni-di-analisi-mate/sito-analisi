Among the rectangles of area $36$, what is the minimum perimeter?

- $12$
- $26$
- $36$
- $24$

## Explanation

Let $x$ and $y$ be the dimensions, with $x, y > 0$. The constraint $xy = 36$ gives $y = \dfrac{36}{x}$, so the perimeter is $P(x) = 2x + \dfrac{72}{x}$ for $x > 0$. The derivative $P'(x) = 2 - \dfrac{72}{x^2}$ vanishes for $x^2 = 36$, that is, for $x = 6$, since $x > 0$. Since $P'$ is negative for $0 < x < 6$ and positive for $x > 6$, the point $x = 6$ is an absolute minimum point on $(0, +\infty)$. The required rectangle is therefore the square of side length $6$, and the **minimum perimeter** is $P(6) = 12 + 12 = 24$.
