At which point does the graph of $f(x, y) = y\,e^{x} - e^{y}$ have a horizontal tangent plane?

- $(0, 0)$
- $(1, 1)$
- $(0, 1)$
- At no point

## Explanation

The tangent plane is horizontal at the critical points, where $\nabla f = (0, 0)$. The partial derivatives are $f_x = y\,e^{x}$ and $f_y = e^{x} - e^{y}$. Since $e^{x} > 0$, the condition $f_x = 0$ gives $y = 0$. Then $f_y = e^{x} - 1 = 0$ gives $x = 0$, so the only point with a horizontal tangent plane is $(0, 0)$.
