A rectangle has two sides on the coordinate axes, one vertex at the origin and the opposite vertex on the segment of the line $\dfrac{x}{4} + \dfrac{y}{2} = 1$ lying in the first quadrant. What is the maximum area of the rectangle?

- $2$
- $4$
- $1$
- $8$

## Explanation

Let $(x, y)$ be the vertex on the line, with $x, y > 0$. The constraint gives $y = 2 - \dfrac{x}{2}$, with $x \in (0, 4)$, so the area is
$$
A(x) = x\left(2 - \frac{x}{2}\right) = 2x - \frac{x^2}{2} .
$$
The derivative $A'(x) = 2 - x$ is positive for $x < 2$ and negative for $x > 2$, so $A$ has its maximum at $x = 2$. Then $y = 1$ and the **maximum area** is $A(2) = 2$.
