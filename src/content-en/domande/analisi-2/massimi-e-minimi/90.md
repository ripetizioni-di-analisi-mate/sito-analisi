Among the triangles with perimeter $12$, what is the maximum area? For the area use Heron's formula $A = \sqrt{p\,(p - a)(p - b)(p - c)}$, where $p$ is the semiperimeter.

- $6$
- $8$
- $2\sqrt{3}$
- $4\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

The semiperimeter is $p = 6$, so we maximize the product $(6 - a)(6 - b)(6 - c)$. Setting $u = 6 - a$, $v = 6 - b$, $w = 6 - c$, the constraint $a + b + c = 12$ becomes $u + v + w = 6$, with $u, v, w > 0$ by the triangle inequality. The maximum of $uvw$ with $u + v + w = 6$ is obtained for $u = v = w = 2$, as given by the Lagrange system $vw = uw = uv$. Then $a = b = c = 4$ and the triangle is equilateral, with
$$
A = \sqrt{6 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2} = \sqrt{48} = 4\sqrt{3} .
$$
