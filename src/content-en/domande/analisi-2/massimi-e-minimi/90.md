When maximizing $x$ subject to $g(x,y)=x^2+y^2=49$, what is the multiplier at the maximum point using the convention $\nabla f=\lambda\nabla g$?

- $- \dfrac{1}{14}$
- $14$
- $\dfrac{1}{14}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$

## Explanation

The maximum is at $(7,0)$. The gradients at that point are $(1,0)$ and $(14,0)$.
