Which point of the sphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 9$ is farthest from the point $P = (4, 4, 2)$?

- $(2, 2, 1)$
- $(-2, -2, -1)$
- $(-4, -4, -2)$
- $(-1, -2, -2)$

## Explanation

The square of the distance from $P$ is
$$
(x - 4)^2 + (y - 4)^2 + (z - 2)^2 = x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - 2(4x + 4y + 2z) + 36 ,
$$
which on the sphere equals $45 - 2(4x + 4y + 2z)$. So the farthest point is the one where $4x + 4y + 2z$ is minimum. The sphere is compact and the gradient $(2x, 2y, 2z)$ of the constraint does not vanish on it, therefore the minimum exists and satisfies $(4, 4, 2) = \lambda\,(2x, 2y, 2z)$. It follows that $(x, y, z)$ is parallel to $(2, 2, 1)$, which has norm $3$, and the candidates are $(2, 2, 1)$ and $(-2, -2, -1)$. At $(-2, -2, -1)$ we have $4x + 4y + 2z = -18$, so the farthest point is **$(-2, -2, -1)$**, at distance $\sqrt{45 + 36} = 9$ from $P$.
