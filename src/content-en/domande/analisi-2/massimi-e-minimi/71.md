The function $f(x, y) = 2x^2 + y^2 - 4x$ is restricted to the circle centered at the origin with radius $2$. What is the maximum value of the restriction?

- $8$
- $12$
- $16$
- $4$

## Explanation

On the constraint we have $y^2 = 4 - x^2$ with $x \in [-2, 2]$, so the restriction of $f$ is
$$
h(x) = 2x^2 + 4 - x^2 - 4x = (x - 2)^2 .
$$
On $[-2, 2]$ the function $h$ is decreasing, so it has its maximum at $x = -2$, with $h(-2) = 16$, and its minimum at $x = 2$, with $h(2) = 0$. The point corresponding to the maximum is $(-2, 0)$, therefore the **constrained maximum** is $16$. The same result follows from the Lagrange system, whose candidates are $(2, 0)$ and $(-2, 0)$.
