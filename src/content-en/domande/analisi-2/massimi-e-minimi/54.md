What is the Hessian matrix of $f(x, y) = x^3 y$ at the point $(1, 2)$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 6 & 3 \\ 3 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 12 & 6 \\ 6 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 12 & 3 \\ 3 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 12 & 3 \\ 3 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The first derivatives are $f_x = 3x^2 y$ and $f_y = x^3$. The second derivatives are therefore
$$
f_{xx} = 6xy, \qquad f_{xy} = f_{yx} = 3x^2, \qquad f_{yy} = 0 .
$$
At the point $(1, 2)$ we get $f_{xx} = 12$, $f_{xy} = 3$ and $f_{yy} = 0$, therefore the Hessian matrix is
$$
H_f(1, 2) = \begin{pmatrix} 12 & 3 \\ 3 & 0 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
