A closed cylindrical can must have volume $16\pi$. What is the minimum total surface area, including the two bases?

- $16\pi$
- $32\pi$
- $20\pi$
- $24\pi$

## Explanation

Let $r$ be the radius and $h$ the height; the constraint is $\pi r^2 h = 16\pi$, so $h = \dfrac{16}{r^2}$, and the surface area is
$$
S(r) = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi r h = 2\pi r^2 + \frac{32\pi}{r}, \qquad r > 0 .
$$
The derivative $S'(r) = 4\pi r - \dfrac{32\pi}{r^2}$ vanishes for $r^3 = 8$, that is $r = 2$, and it is negative before and positive after. Therefore the minimum is at $r = 2$, with $h = 4$, and the **minimum surface area** is $8\pi + 16\pi = 24\pi$.
