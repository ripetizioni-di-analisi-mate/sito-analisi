What is the maximum of $4x+2y+7z$ on the unit sphere?

- $69$
- $\sqrt{69}$
- $7$
- $13$

## Explanation

The dot product with a point on the sphere is at most the norm of the coefficient vector. Equality is attained by taking the unit vector obtained by normalizing $\left( 4, \  2, \  7\right)$.
