How many saddle points does the function $f(x, y) = x^2 + 2y^2 - x^2 y$ have?

- $0$
- $1$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

The system for the critical points is
$$
\begin{cases}
f_x = 2x(1 - y) = 0 \\[4pt]
f_y = 4y - x^2 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
If $x = 0$, the second equation gives $y = 0$; if $y = 1$, the second gives $x^2 = 4$, that is $x = \pm 2$. The critical points are therefore $(0, 0)$, $(2, 1)$ and $(-2, 1)$. The second derivatives are $f_{xx} = 2 - 2y$, $f_{xy} = -2x$, $f_{yy} = 4$. At the origin $\det H = 8 > 0$ with $f_{xx} = 2 > 0$, so the origin is a local minimum point. At $(\pm 2, 1)$ we have $f_{xx} = 0$ and $f_{xy} = \mp 4$, so $\det H = -16 < 0$, hence there are **two** saddle points.
