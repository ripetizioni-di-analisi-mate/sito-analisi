Which parametrization, with $(\varphi, \theta) \in [0, \pi] \times [0, 2\pi]$, has the sphere $x^2 + y^2 + (z - 1)^2 = 4$ as its trace?

- $\bigl( 2\sin\varphi\cos\theta,\; 2\sin\varphi\sin\theta,\; -1 + 2\cos\varphi \bigr)$
- $\bigl( 4\sin\varphi\cos\theta,\; 4\sin\varphi\sin\theta,\; 1 + 4\cos\varphi \bigr)$
- $\bigl( 2\sin\varphi\cos\theta,\; 2\sin\varphi\sin\theta,\; 1 + 2\sin\varphi \bigr)$
- $\bigl( 2\sin\varphi\cos\theta,\; 2\sin\varphi\sin\theta,\; 1 + 2\cos\varphi \bigr)$

## Explanation

The sphere has center $(0, 0, 1)$ and radius $2$, because the right-hand side is $4 = 2^2$. Its spherical coordinates are obtained by adding the center to the parametrization of the sphere of radius $2$ centered at the origin.

For the parametrization $\bigl( 2\sin\varphi\cos\theta,\; 2\sin\varphi\sin\theta,\; 1 + 2\cos\varphi \bigr)$ we have
$$
x^2 + y^2 + (z - 1)^2 = 4\sin^2\varphi + 4\cos^2\varphi = 4 ,
$$
so every point lies on the sphere, and as $\varphi$ and $\theta$ vary all of its points are obtained. The parametrization with $-1 + 2\cos\varphi$ describes the sphere with center $(0, 0, -1)$, the one with the factor $4$ the sphere of radius $4$.
