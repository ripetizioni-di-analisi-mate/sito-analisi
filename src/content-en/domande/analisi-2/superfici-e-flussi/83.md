What is the outward flux of $\left( 4 x, \  2 y, \  7 z\right)$ through the boundary of the unit ball?

- $\dfrac{52 \pi}{3}$
- $13 \pi$
- $- \dfrac{52 \pi}{3}$
- $52 \pi$

## Explanation

By Gauss’s theorem we integrate the constant divergence $13$ over the ball. Its volume is $\dfrac{4 \pi}{3}$.
