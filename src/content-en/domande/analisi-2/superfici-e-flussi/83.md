What is the outward flux of $\left( 4 x, \  2 y, \  7 z\right)$ across the unit sphere?

- $\dfrac{52 \pi}{3}$
- $13 \pi$
- $- \dfrac{52 \pi}{3}$
- $52 \pi$

## Explanation

By the divergence theorem integrate the constant divergence $13$ over the ball of volume $\dfrac{4 \pi}{3}$.
