Which vector is normal to the level surface through $(1,1,0)$ with the exact components of the gradient of $2 x^{2} + 3 y^{2} + 5 z^{2}$?

- $\left( 4, \  6, \  10\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  3, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  6, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 6, \  4, \  0\right)$

## Explanation

The gradient is perpendicular to the surface. At $(1,1,0)$ the $z$ derivative vanishes.
