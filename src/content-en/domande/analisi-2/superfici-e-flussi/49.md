For the parabolic cylinder $\mathbf{r}(u, v) = \bigl( u,\; u^2,\; v \bigr)$, with $(u, v) \in [0, 1] \times [0, 2]$, what is $\lVert \mathbf{r}_u \wedge \mathbf{r}_v \rVert$?

- $1 + 4u^2$
- $\sqrt{1 + 4u^2}$
- $\sqrt{1 + u^2}$
- $2u$

## Explanation

The partial derivative vectors are $\mathbf{r}_u = (1,\; 2u,\; 0)$ and $\mathbf{r}_v = (0,\; 0,\; 1)$, so
$$
\mathbf{r}_u \wedge \mathbf{r}_v = \bigl( 2u \cdot 1 - 0 \cdot 0,\; 0 \cdot 0 - 1 \cdot 1,\; 1 \cdot 0 - 2u \cdot 0 \bigr) = (2u,\; -1,\; 0) .
$$
Therefore the norm is $\sqrt{4u^2 + 1}$, which never vanishes. The normal vector is horizontal, since the surface contains the vertical lines obtained by fixing $u$.
