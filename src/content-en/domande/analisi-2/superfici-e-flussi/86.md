What is the circulation of $\left( - 4 y, \  2 x, \  7 z\right)$ around the disk boundary $z=0,\ x^2+y^2\leq 49$ oriented by the upward normal?

- $294 \pi$
- $- 294 \pi$
- $42 \pi$
- $- 98 \pi$

## Explanation

Stokes’ theorem uses the vertical curl component, $6$. Multiply by the disk area.
