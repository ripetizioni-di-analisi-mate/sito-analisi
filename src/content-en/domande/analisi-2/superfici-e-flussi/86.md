What is the integral of $\left( - 4 y, \  2 x, \  7 z\right)$ along the boundary of the disk $z=0,\ x^2+y^2\leq 49$ oriented by the upward normal?

- $294 \pi$
- $- 294 \pi$
- $42 \pi$
- $- 98 \pi$

## Explanation

Stokes’ theorem requires the vertical component of the curl, which equals $6$. Multiply by the disk area.
