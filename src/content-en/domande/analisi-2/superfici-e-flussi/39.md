The sphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 4$ is oriented by the outward unit normal. What is the flux through it of the field $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = \bigl( y z^2,\; x^2 z,\; x y^2 \bigr)$?

- $\dfrac{32\pi}{3}$
- $16\pi$
- $32\pi$
- $0$

## Explanation

The ball of radius $2$ is a regular domain, its boundary is the sphere oriented by the outward unit normal and the field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^3$, so the **divergence theorem** applies.

Each component of the field does not depend on the variable with respect to which it is differentiated in the divergence: $F_1$ does not depend on $x$, $F_2$ does not depend on $y$, $F_3$ does not depend on $z$. Therefore $\operatorname{div} \mathbf{F} = 0 + 0 + 0 = 0$ at every point, that is, the field is **solenoidal**.

The outward flux is the triple integral of the zero function, so it equals $0$.
