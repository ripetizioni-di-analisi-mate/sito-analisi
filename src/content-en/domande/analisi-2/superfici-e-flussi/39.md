What is the outward flux of the field $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = (x, y, z)$ through the boundary of the solid cylinder $V = \{ (x, y, z) : x^2 + y^2 \leq 4,\; 0 \leq z \leq 3 \}$?

- $12\pi$
- $36\pi$
- $24\pi$
- $48\pi$

## Explanation

The solid cylinder is a regular domain, because its boundary consists of the lateral surface and two disks, and the field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^3$. The **divergence theorem** applies, so the outward flux is the triple integral of $\operatorname{div} \mathbf{F}$ over $V$.

Since $\operatorname{div} \mathbf{F} = 1 + 1 + 1 = 3$ is constant, the flux is three times the volume of the cylinder,
$$
\Phi = 3 \cdot \pi \cdot 2^2 \cdot 3 = 36\pi .
$$
