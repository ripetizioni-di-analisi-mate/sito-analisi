What is the flux of the constant field $\mathbf{F} = (0, 0, 1)$ through the hemisphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1$ with $z \geq 0$, oriented with the unit normal pointing toward the origin?

- $\pi$
- $-\pi$
- $-2\pi$
- $2\pi$

## Explanation

On the sphere of radius $1$ the unit normal pointing toward the origin is $\mathbf{n} = -(x, y, z)$, so $\mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{n} = -z$.

In spherical coordinates $z = \cos\varphi$, $d\sigma = \sin\varphi \; d\varphi \, d\theta$, and the hemisphere corresponds to $\varphi \in \left[ 0, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right]$. The flux therefore equals
$$
\Phi = -\int_0^{2\pi} \!\! \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos\varphi \sin\varphi \; d\varphi \, d\theta = -2\pi \cdot \frac{1}{2} = -\pi .
$$
The sign is negative because the field points upward, while the chosen normal has negative third component.
