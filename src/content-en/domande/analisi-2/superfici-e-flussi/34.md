What is the flux of the field $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = (x, y, 0)$ through the hemisphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1$ with $z \geq 0$, oriented with the unit normal pointing toward the origin?

- $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{8\pi}{3}$
- $-2\pi$

## Explanation

On the sphere of radius $1$ the unit normal pointing toward the origin is $\mathbf{n} = -(x, y, z)$, so $\mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{n} = -(x^2 + y^2)$.

In spherical coordinates $x^2 + y^2 = \sin^2\varphi$, $d\sigma = \sin\varphi \; d\varphi \, d\theta$, and the hemisphere corresponds to $\varphi \in \left[ 0, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right]$. With the substitution $c = \cos\varphi$ we obtain $\displaystyle\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^3\varphi \; d\varphi = \int_0^1 (1 - c^2) \; dc = \frac{2}{3}$, therefore
$$
\Phi = -\int_0^{2\pi} \!\! \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^3\varphi \; d\varphi \, d\theta = -2\pi \cdot \frac{2}{3} = -\frac{4\pi}{3} .
$$
The sign is negative because $\mathbf{F} \cdot (x, y, z) = x^2 + y^2 \geq 0$, that is, the field points out of the sphere, while the chosen normal points inward.
