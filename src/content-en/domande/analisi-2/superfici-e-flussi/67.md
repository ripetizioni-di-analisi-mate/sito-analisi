What is the flux of the constant field $\mathbf{F} = (3,\; 1,\; 2)$ through the rectangle $\Sigma = \{ (2, y, z) : 0 \leq y \leq 1,\; 0 \leq z \leq 2 \}$, oriented by the unit normal $\mathbf{n} = (1, 0, 0)$?

- $2$
- $6$
- $4$
- $12$

## Explanation

The rectangle lies in the plane $x = 2$, so the unit normal is $(1, 0, 0)$ at every point and the area element is $d\sigma = dy \, dz$.

The dot product $\mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{n}$ is the first component of the field, that is $3$. Therefore the flux is the product of $3$ and the area of the rectangle, which is $1 \cdot 2 = 2$, so $\Phi = 6$.
