What is the divergence of the field $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = \bigl( e^{xy},\; \sin(yz),\; x^2 z^3 \bigr)$ at the point $(1, 0, 2)$?

- $15$
- $13$
- $26$
- $14$

## Explanation

By the definition of **divergence**, with the chain rule in the first two terms,
$$
\operatorname{div} \mathbf{F} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x} e^{xy} + \frac{\partial}{\partial y} \sin(yz) + \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \bigl( x^2 z^3 \bigr) = y\, e^{xy} + z \cos(yz) + 3x^2 z^2 .
$$
At the point $(1, 0, 2)$ we obtain $0 \cdot e^{0} + 2 \cos 0 + 3 \cdot 1 \cdot 4 = 0 + 2 + 12 = 14$.
