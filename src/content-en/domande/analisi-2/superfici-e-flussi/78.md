What is the cross product $r_u\times r_v$ for $\left( 4 u, \  2 v, \  7 u + 7 v\right)$?

- $\left( 14, \  28, \  -8\right)$
- $\left( -14, \  -28, \  8\right)$
- $\left( -28, \  -14, \  8\right)$
- $\left( 0, \  0, \  8\right)$

## Explanation

The derivatives are $\left( 4, \  0, \  7\right)$ and $\left( 0, \  2, \  7\right)$. Take the cross product in the stated order.
