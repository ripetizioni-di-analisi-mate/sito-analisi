Let $\boldsymbol{\omega} = (1, 2, 3)$ and let $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = \boldsymbol{\omega} \wedge (x, y, z)$ be the velocity field of a rigid rotation about the line through the origin parallel to $\boldsymbol{\omega}$. What is $\operatorname{rot} \mathbf{F}$?

- $(2,\; 4,\; 6)$
- $(1,\; 2,\; 3)$
- $(0,\; 0,\; 0)$
- $(-2,\; -4,\; -6)$

## Explanation

The cross product gives $\mathbf{F} = \bigl( 2z - 3y,\; 3x - z,\; y - 2x \bigr)$. From the definition of the **curl**,
$$
\operatorname{rot} \mathbf{F} = \left( \frac{\partial F_3}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial F_2}{\partial z},\; \frac{\partial F_1}{\partial z} - \frac{\partial F_3}{\partial x},\; \frac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} \right) = \bigl( 1 + 1,\; 2 + 2,\; 3 + 3 \bigr) = (2,\; 4,\; 6) .
$$
Hence the curl is twice $\boldsymbol{\omega}$ and is directed along the axis of rotation.
