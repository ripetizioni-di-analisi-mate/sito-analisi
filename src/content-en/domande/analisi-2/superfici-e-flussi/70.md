What is the outward flux of $F=(x,y,0)$ through the lateral surface of the cylinder with radius $3$ and height $2$?

- $12 \pi$
- $18 \pi$
- $36 \pi$
- $- 36 \pi$

## Explanation

The field is radial and has normal component $3$ on the lateral surface. Multiply by the lateral area.
