What is the outward flux of $F=(0,0,4)$ through the upper hemisphere of radius $2$?

- $16 \pi$
- $32 \pi$
- $0$
- $- 16 \pi$

## Explanation

Close the surface with the equatorial disk. The total flux is zero because the divergence vanishes. The disk has downward normal and flux $- 16 \pi$.
