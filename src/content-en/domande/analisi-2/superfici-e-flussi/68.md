What is the flux of the field $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = (0, 0, 1)$ through the graph of $f(x, y) = x^2 + \sin(xy)$ over the rectangle $[0, 1] \times [0, 2]$, oriented upward?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

By the formula for the **flux through a Cartesian surface** the integrand is $-F_1 f_x - F_2 f_y + F_3$. Since $F_1 = F_2 = 0$ and $F_3 = 1$, the integrand equals $1$ whatever $f$ is.

Hence the flux is the area of the base rectangle, $1 \cdot 2 = 2$. The same value is obtained for the flat rectangle $[0, 1] \times [0, 2]$ of the plane $z = 0$ oriented upward.
