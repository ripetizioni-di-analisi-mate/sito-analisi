What is the outward flux of $\left( 3 x, \  5 y, \  2 z\right)$ across the unit sphere?

- $40 \pi$
- $10 \pi$
- $- \dfrac{40 \pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{40 \pi}{3}$

## Explanation

By the divergence theorem integrate the constant divergence $10$ over the ball of volume $\dfrac{4 \pi}{3}$.
