## Explanation

The cube $V = [0, 1]^3$ is a regular domain, because its boundary consists of the six square faces, which are regular surfaces, oriented with the outward unit normal, and the field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^3$. The **divergence theorem** applies, so the outward flux is the triple integral of the divergence over $V$.

We have $\operatorname{div} \mathbf{F} = 2x + 2y + 2z$. Since the volume of the cube is $1$ and $\displaystyle\iiint_V x \; dx \, dy \, dz = \int_0^1 x \; dx = \frac{1}{2}$, and the same holds for $y$ and $z$, we obtain
$$
\Phi = 2 \left( \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} \right) = 3 .
$$
