## Explanation

The portion of the plane is the graph of $f(x, y) = 4 - 2x - 2y$. The condition $z \geq 0$ is equivalent to $x + y \leq 2$, so the projection onto the $xy$-plane is the triangle $T$ with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(2, 0)$, $(0, 2)$, which has area $\dfrac{2 \cdot 2}{2} = 2$.

Since $f_x = -2$ and $f_y = -2$, the integrand in the formula for the **area of a Cartesian surface** is $\sqrt{1 + 4 + 4} = 3$. The integrand is constant, therefore the integral is the product of $3$ and the area of $T$,
$$
\operatorname{Area} = \iint_T 3 \; dx \, dy = 3 \cdot 2 = 6 .
$$
