What is the curl of $\left( 3 y, \  5 z, \  2 x\right)$?

- $\left( 0, \  0, \  0\right)$
- $\left( -3, \  -5, \  -2\right)$
- $\left( -5, \  -2, \  -3\right)$
- $\left( 5, \  2, \  3\right)$

## Explanation

The three differences are $R_y-Q_z$, $P_z-R_x$, and $Q_x-P_y$. The first term in each difference is zero.
