Which of the following fields is not the curl of any field of class $C^2$ on $\mathbb{R}^3$?

- $(x,\; y,\; -2z)$
- $(y,\; z,\; x)$
- $(x^2,\; y,\; z)$
- $(yz,\; xz,\; xy)$

## Explanation

For every field $\mathbf{G}$ of class $C^2$ we have $\operatorname{div}(\operatorname{curl} \mathbf{G}) = 0$, so a field whose divergence is not identically zero is not the curl of any $C^2$ field.

The divergences of the four fields are $1 + 1 - 2 = 0$, $0$, $2x + 1 + 1 = 2x + 2$ and $0$. Only the field $(x^2, y, z)$ has divergence that is not identically zero, therefore it is the field that **is not a curl**. For the other three the necessary condition $\operatorname{div} = 0$ is satisfied.
