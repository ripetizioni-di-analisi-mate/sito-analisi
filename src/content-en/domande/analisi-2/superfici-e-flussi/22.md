What is the area of the portion of the plane $z = 2x + y$ whose projection onto the $xy$-plane is the disk $x^2 + y^2 \leq 1$?

- $\sqrt{5}\,\pi$
- $6\pi$
- $\pi$
- $\sqrt{6}\,\pi$

## Explanation

The portion of the plane is the graph of $f(x, y) = 2x + y$ over the disk $D$ of radius $1$. Since $f_x = 2$ and $f_y = 1$, the integrand of the formula for the **area of a Cartesian surface** is $\sqrt{1 + 4 + 1} = \sqrt{6}$.

The integrand is constant, so the area is the product of $\sqrt{6}$ and the area of $D$,
$$
\operatorname{Area} = \iint_D \sqrt{6} \; dx \, dy = \sqrt{6}\,\pi .
$$
