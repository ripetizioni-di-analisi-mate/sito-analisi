For the surface $\mathbf{r}(u, v) = \bigl( u^2,\; v,\; uv \bigr)$, what is the equation of the tangent plane at the point corresponding to $(u_0, v_0) = (1, 1)$?

- $x - 2y - 2z = -3$
- $2x + z = 3$
- $x + 2y - 2z = 1$
- $x + 2y - 2z = -1$

## Explanation

The point is $P_0 = \mathbf{r}(1, 1) = (1, 1, 1)$. The derivative vectors are $\mathbf{r}_u = (2u, 0, v)$ and $\mathbf{r}_v = (0, 1, u)$, so at $(1, 1)$ they equal $\mathbf{r}_u = (2, 0, 1)$ and $\mathbf{r}_v = (0, 1, 1)$.

The **normal vector** is their cross product,
$$
\mathbf{N} = \mathbf{r}_u \wedge \mathbf{r}_v = \bigl( 0 \cdot 1 - 1 \cdot 1,\; 1 \cdot 0 - 2 \cdot 1,\; 2 \cdot 1 - 0 \cdot 0 \bigr) = (-1,\; -2,\; 2) .
$$
The tangent plane passes through $P_0$ and is orthogonal to $\mathbf{N}$, therefore it has equation
$$
-(x - 1) - 2(y - 1) + 2(z - 1) = 0 ,
$$
which, multiplied by $-1$, becomes $x + 2y - 2z = 1$.
