For the surface $\mathbf{r}(u, v) = \bigl( 1 + u + v,\; 2 - u,\; 3 + 2v \bigr)$, with $(u, v) \in [0, 1] \times [0, 1]$, what is the vector $\mathbf{r}_u \wedge \mathbf{r}_v$?

- $(2,\; 2,\; -1)$
- $(-2,\; -2,\; 1)$
- $(-2,\; 2,\; 1)$
- $(-2,\; -2,\; -1)$

## Explanation

The components of $\mathbf{r}$ have degree one in $u$ and $v$, so the partial derivative vectors are constant: $\mathbf{r}_u = (1, -1, 0)$ and $\mathbf{r}_v = (1, 0, 2)$.

The cross product is computed component by component,
$$
\mathbf{r}_u \wedge \mathbf{r}_v = \bigl( (-1) \cdot 2 - 0 \cdot 0,\; 0 \cdot 1 - 1 \cdot 2,\; 1 \cdot 0 - (-1) \cdot 1 \bigr) = (-2,\; -2,\; 1) .
$$
The vector is the same at every point, because the trace is a portion of a plane. The vector $(2, 2, -1)$ is $\mathbf{r}_v \wedge \mathbf{r}_u$, which has the opposite direction.
