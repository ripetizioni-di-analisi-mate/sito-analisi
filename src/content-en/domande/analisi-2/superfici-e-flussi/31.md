What is the flux of the field $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = (0, 0, z)$ through the portion of the paraboloid $z = 1 - x^2 - y^2$ with $z \geq 0$, oriented upward?

- $\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The surface is the graph of $f(x, y) = 1 - x^2 - y^2$, and the condition $z \geq 0$ is equivalent to $x^2 + y^2 \leq 1$. By the formula for the **flux through a Cartesian surface** the integrand is $-F_1 f_x - F_2 f_y + F_3$; since $F_1 = F_2 = 0$, what remains is $F_3$ evaluated on the surface, that is, $1 - x^2 - y^2$.

In polar coordinates we obtain
$$
\Phi = \int_0^{2\pi} \!\! \int_0^1 (1 - \rho^2)\, \rho \; d\rho \, d\theta = 2\pi \left( \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{4} \right) = \frac{\pi}{2} .
$$
