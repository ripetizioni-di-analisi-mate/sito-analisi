The segment of the plane $y = 0$ with equations $x = t$, $z = 0$, with $t \in [1, 2]$, is rotated through a full turn about the $z$-axis. What is the area of the resulting surface?

- $3\pi$
- $\pi$
- $4\pi$
- $6\pi$

## Explanation

The formula for the **area of a surface of revolution** applies with $g(t) = t \geq 0$ and $h(t) = 0$, and $\sqrt{g'(t)^2 + h'(t)^2} = 1$. We obtain
$$
\operatorname{Area} = 2\pi \int_1^2 t \; dt = 2\pi \cdot \frac{4 - 1}{2} = 3\pi .
$$
The surface is the annulus of the plane $z = 0$ between the circles of radii $1$ and $2$, and indeed its area is $\pi \cdot 2^2 - \pi \cdot 1^2 = 3\pi$.
