What is the flux of the field $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = (x, y, z)$ through the portion of the paraboloid $z = 4 - x^2 - y^2$ with $z \geq 0$, oriented upward?

- $8\pi$
- $16\pi$
- $48\pi$
- $24\pi$

## Explanation

The surface $\Sigma$ is not closed; we add the disk $D$ of radius $2$ in the plane $z = 0$, oriented downward. Then $\Sigma \cup D$ is the boundary of the regular domain $V = \{ 0 \leq z \leq 4 - x^2 - y^2 \}$, oriented with the outward unit normal, and by the **divergence theorem** the total outward flux is $3 \operatorname{Vol}(V)$, since $\operatorname{div} \mathbf{F} = 3$.

The cross-section of $V$ at height $z \in [0, 4]$ is a disk of area $\pi(4 - z)$, so $\operatorname{Vol}(V) = \displaystyle\int_0^4 \pi(4 - z) \; dz = 8\pi$ and the total flux is $24\pi$.

On $D$ we have $\mathbf{n} = (0, 0, -1)$ and $\mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{n} = -z = 0$, so the flux through $D$ is zero. Therefore the flux through $\Sigma$ is $24\pi$.
