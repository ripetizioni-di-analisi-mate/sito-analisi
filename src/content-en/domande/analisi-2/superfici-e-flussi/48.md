What is the cross product $r_u\times r_v$ for $\left( 2 u, \  3 v, \  5 u + 5 v\right)$?

- $\left( -10, \  -15, \  6\right)$
- $\left( -15, \  -10, \  6\right)$
- $\left( 15, \  10, \  -6\right)$
- $\left( 0, \  0, \  6\right)$

## Explanation

The derivatives are $\left( 2, \  0, \  5\right)$ and $\left( 0, \  3, \  5\right)$. Take the cross product in the stated order.
