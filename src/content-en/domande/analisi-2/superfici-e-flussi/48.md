For the surface $\mathbf{r}(u, v) = \bigl( u,\; v^2,\; v^3 \bigr)$, with $(u, v) \in D = [-1, 1] \times [-1, 1]$, which set is formed by the points $(u, v)$ of $D$ at which $\mathbf{r}_u \wedge \mathbf{r}_v = \mathbf{0}$?

- The segment $u = 0$, $-1 \leq v \leq 1$
- Only the point $(0, 0)$
- The empty set
- The segment $v = 0$, $-1 \leq u \leq 1$

## Explanation

The partial derivative vectors are $\mathbf{r}_u = (1, 0, 0)$ and $\mathbf{r}_v = (0,\; 2v,\; 3v^2)$, so
$$
\mathbf{r}_u \wedge \mathbf{r}_v = \bigl( 0 \cdot 3v^2 - 0 \cdot 2v,\; 0 \cdot 0 - 1 \cdot 3v^2,\; 1 \cdot 2v - 0 \cdot 0 \bigr) = \bigl( 0,\; -3v^2,\; 2v \bigr) .
$$
The vector is zero if and only if $v = 0$, for every value of $u$. Hence the required set is the segment $v = 0$, $-1 \leq u \leq 1$, which contains interior points of $D$; therefore the surface **is not regular**. The image of the segment is a piece of the $x$-axis, along which the surface has an edge.
