## Explanation

The sphere has radius $R = 3$ and is parametrized with spherical coordinates, for which the area element is $d\sigma = R^2 \sin\varphi \; d\varphi \, d\theta = 9 \sin\varphi \; d\varphi \, d\theta$.

On the sphere we have $z = 3\cos\varphi$, so the condition $z \geq \dfrac{3}{2}$ is equivalent to $\cos\varphi \geq \dfrac{1}{2}$. Since $\varphi \in [0, \pi]$, this means $\varphi \in \left[ 0, \dfrac{\pi}{3} \right]$, while $\theta$ varies in $[0, 2\pi]$.

The area therefore equals
$$
\operatorname{Area} = \int_0^{2\pi} \!\! \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{3}} 9 \sin\varphi \; d\varphi \, d\theta = 18\pi \bigl[ -\cos\varphi \bigr]_0^{\frac{\pi}{3}} = 18\pi \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 9\pi .
$$
