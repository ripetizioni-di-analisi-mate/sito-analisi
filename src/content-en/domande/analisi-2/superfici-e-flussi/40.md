What is the outward flux of the field $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = (x, y, 0)$ through the boundary of the region $V = \{ (x, y, z) : x^2 + y^2 \leq z \leq 1 \}$?

- $2\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\pi$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The region $V$ lies between the paraboloid $z = x^2 + y^2$ and the plane $z = 1$, so it is a regular domain, and the field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^3$. By the **divergence theorem** the outward flux is the integral of $\operatorname{div} \mathbf{F} = 1 + 1 + 0 = 2$ over $V$, that is, twice the volume of $V$.

The cross-section of $V$ at height $z \in [0, 1]$ is the disk $x^2 + y^2 \leq z$, of area $\pi z$, therefore
$$
\operatorname{Vol}(V) = \int_0^1 \pi z \; dz = \frac{\pi}{2} , \qquad \Phi = 2 \cdot \frac{\pi}{2} = \pi .
$$
