Consider the half ball $V = \{ (x, y, z) : x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \leq 1,\; z \geq 0 \}$. What is the outward flux through $\partial V$ of the field $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = \bigl( x,\; y,\; z^2 \bigr)$?

- $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{19\pi}{12}$
- $\dfrac{11\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{8\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

The half ball is a regular domain, whose boundary consists of the hemisphere and the base disk, and the field is of class $C^1$, so the **divergence theorem** applies. We have $\operatorname{div} \mathbf{F} = 2 + 2z$.

The volume of $V$ is $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, and in spherical coordinates
$$
\iiint_V z \; dx \, dy \, dz = \int_0^{2\pi} \!\! \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \!\! \int_0^1 r\cos\varphi \cdot r^2 \sin\varphi \; dr \, d\varphi \, d\theta = 2\pi \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{4} = \frac{\pi}{4} .
$$
Therefore
$$
\Phi = 2 \cdot \frac{2\pi}{3} + 2 \cdot \frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{4\pi}{3} + \frac{\pi}{2} = \frac{11\pi}{6} .
$$
