What is the curl of the field $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = \bigl( y^2,\; z^2,\; x^2 \bigr)$?

- $(-2z,\; -2x,\; -2y)$
- $(2z,\; 2x,\; 2y)$
- $(-2y,\; -2z,\; -2x)$
- $(0,\; 0,\; 0)$

## Explanation

The components are $F_1 = y^2$, $F_2 = z^2$ and $F_3 = x^2$, and each depends on a single variable. By the definition of **curl**,
$$
\operatorname{rot} \mathbf{F} = \left( \frac{\partial F_3}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial F_2}{\partial z},\; \frac{\partial F_1}{\partial z} - \frac{\partial F_3}{\partial x},\; \frac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} \right) = \bigl( 0 - 2z,\; 0 - 2x,\; 0 - 2y \bigr) .
$$
Hence $\operatorname{rot} \mathbf{F} = (-2z,\; -2x,\; -2y)$.
