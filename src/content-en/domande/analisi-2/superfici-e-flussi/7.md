## Explanation

The lateral surface is parametrized by $\mathbf{r}(\theta, z) = (\cos\theta,\; \sin\theta,\; z)$, with $(\theta, z) \in [0, 2\pi] \times [0, 2]$. The derivative vectors are $\mathbf{r}_\theta = (-\sin\theta, \cos\theta, 0)$ and $\mathbf{r}_z = (0, 0, 1)$, so $\mathbf{r}_\theta \wedge \mathbf{r}_z = (\cos\theta, \sin\theta, 0)$, which has norm $1$.

The area element is therefore $d\sigma = d\theta \, dz$, and on the surface the function equals $y^2 = \sin^2\theta$. The integral becomes
$$
\iint_\Sigma y^2 \; d\sigma = \int_0^2 \!\! \int_0^{2\pi} \sin^2\theta \; d\theta \, dz = 2 \cdot \pi = 2\pi ,
$$
since the integral of $\sin^2\theta$ over $[0, 2\pi]$ equals $\pi$.
