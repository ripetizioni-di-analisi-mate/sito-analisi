For the field $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = \bigl( x^2 z,\; xyz,\; -z^2 y \bigr)$, what is the value of $\operatorname{curl} \mathbf{F}$ at the point $(1, 1, 1)$?

- $(-2,\; 1,\; 1)$
- $(2,\; -1,\; -1)$
- $(-1,\; 1,\; 1)$
- $(-2,\; -1,\; 1)$

## Explanation

The components of the field are $F_1 = x^2 z$, $F_2 = xyz$ and $F_3 = -z^2 y$. From the definition of **curl** we obtain
$$
\operatorname{curl} \mathbf{F} = \left( \frac{\partial F_3}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial F_2}{\partial z},\; \frac{\partial F_1}{\partial z} - \frac{\partial F_3}{\partial x},\; \frac{\partial F_2}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial F_1}{\partial y} \right) = \bigl( -z^2 - xy,\; x^2,\; yz \bigr) .
$$
In the second component $F_3$ does not depend on $x$, and in the third $F_1$ does not depend on $y$, so in both only one term remains.

At the point $(1, 1, 1)$ the curl therefore equals $(-1 - 1,\; 1,\; 1) = (-2,\; 1,\; 1)$.
