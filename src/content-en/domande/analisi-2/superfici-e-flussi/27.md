What is $\displaystyle\iint_\Sigma z \; d\sigma$, where $\Sigma$ is the hemisphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1$ with $z \geq 0$?

- $\pi$
- $2\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

In spherical coordinates with radius $1$ the area element is $d\sigma = \sin\varphi \; d\varphi \, d\theta$ and the function equals $z = \cos\varphi$. The condition $z \geq 0$ is equivalent to $\varphi \in \left[ 0, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right]$.

The integral therefore equals
$$
\iint_\Sigma z \; d\sigma = \int_0^{2\pi} \!\! \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos\varphi \sin\varphi \; d\varphi \, d\theta = 2\pi \left[ \frac{\sin^2\varphi}{2} \right]_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} = 2\pi \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \pi .
$$
