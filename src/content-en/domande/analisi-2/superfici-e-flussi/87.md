What is the curl of $\left( 4 y, \  2 z, \  7 x\right)$?

- $\left( 0, \  0, \  0\right)$
- $\left( -4, \  -2, \  -7\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  7, \  4\right)$
- $\left( -2, \  -7, \  -4\right)$

## Explanation

The three differences are $R_y-Q_z$, $P_z-R_x$, and $Q_x-P_y$. The first term in each difference is zero.
