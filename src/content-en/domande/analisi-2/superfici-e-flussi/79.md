Which vector is normal to the level surface through $(1,1,0)$ with the exact components of the gradient of $4 x^{2} + 2 y^{2} + 7 z^{2}$?

- $\left( 8, \  4, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 8, \  4, \  14\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  2, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  8, \  0\right)$

## Explanation

The gradient is perpendicular to the surface. At $(1,1,0)$ the $z$ derivative vanishes.
