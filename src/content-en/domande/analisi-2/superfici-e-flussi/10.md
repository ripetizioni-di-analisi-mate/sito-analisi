## Explanation

The disk lies in the plane $z = 3$ and is oriented upward, so the unit normal vector is $\mathbf{n} = (0, 0, 1)$ at every point and the area element is $d\sigma = dx \, dy$.

The dot product of the field and the unit normal vector is the third component of the field, $\mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{n} = x^2 + y^2$. The flux is therefore the double integral of $x^2 + y^2$ over the disk of radius $2$, which in polar coordinates, with the Jacobian $\rho$, equals
$$
\Phi = \int_0^{2\pi} \!\! \int_0^2 \rho^2 \cdot \rho \; d\rho \, d\theta = 2\pi \cdot \frac{2^4}{4} = 8\pi .
$$
The flux is positive because on the disk the third component of the field is positive at every point other than the center and the chosen normal points upward.
