Let $V = \bigl\{ (x, y, z) : \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \leq z \leq 1 \bigr\}$ be the solid cone with vertex at the origin. What is the flux of the field $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = \bigl( 0,\; 0,\; z^2 \bigr)$ out of the boundary of $V$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\pi$

## Explanation

The solid cone is a regular domain, whose boundary consists of the lateral surface of the cone and the disk of radius $1$ in the plane $z = 1$, and the field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^3$. Hence the **divergence theorem** applies, with $\operatorname{div} \mathbf{F} = 2z$.

The cross-section of $V$ at height $z \in [0, 1]$ is the disk $x^2 + y^2 \leq z^2$, of area $\pi z^2$. Therefore
$$
\Phi = \iiint_V 2z \; dx \, dy \, dz = \int_0^1 2z \cdot \pi z^2 \; dz = 2\pi \cdot \frac{1}{4} = \frac{\pi}{2} .
$$
