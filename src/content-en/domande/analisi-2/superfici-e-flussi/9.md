## Explanation

A field is **solenoidal** when its divergence is identically zero. For the given field we have
$$
\operatorname{div} \mathbf{F} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}(2xy) + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(a y^2) + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(6yz) = 2y + 2ay + 6y = (8 + 2a)\, y .
$$
This expression is zero for *every* $y$ if and only if the coefficient $8 + 2a$ is zero, so the field is solenoidal if and only if $a = -4$.
