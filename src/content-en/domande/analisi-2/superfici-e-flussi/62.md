What is the area vector with third component $1$ at $(1,1)$ for the graph of $3 x^{2} + 5 y^{2}$?

- $\left( -10, \  -6, \  1\right)$
- $\left( -3, \  -5, \  1\right)$
- $\left( -6, \  -10, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 6, \  10, \  1\right)$

## Explanation

For a graph use $(-z_x,-z_y,1)$. The two derivatives at the point are $\left( 6, \  10\right)$.
