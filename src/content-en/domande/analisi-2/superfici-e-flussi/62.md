What is the surface integral $\displaystyle\iint_T z \; d\sigma$ over the torus $T$ obtained by rotating about the $z$-axis the circle in the plane $y = 0$ with center $(2, 0, 0)$ and radius $1$, parametrized by $\mathbf{r}(u, v) = \bigl( (2 + \cos u)\cos v,\; (2 + \cos u)\sin v,\; \sin u \bigr)$, $(u, v) \in [0, 2\pi] \times [0, 2\pi]$?

- $8\pi^2$
- $4\pi^2$
- $0$
- $2\pi^2$

## Explanation

The torus is symmetric with respect to the plane $z = 0$: the points $(x, y, z)$ and $(x, y, -z)$ both lie on $T$, because they correspond to the parameters $(u, v)$ and $(2\pi - u, v)$. At the two points the function $z$ takes opposite values, so the contributions to the integral cancel in pairs and $\displaystyle\iint_T z \; d\sigma = 0$.

The same value follows from the computation. We have $\lVert \mathbf{r}_u \wedge \mathbf{r}_v \rVert = 2 + \cos u$, therefore
$$
\iint_T z \; d\sigma = \int_0^{2\pi} \!\! \int_0^{2\pi} \sin u \, (2 + \cos u) \; du \, dv = 2\pi \cdot 0 = 0 .
$$
The value $8\pi^2$ is the area of the torus.
