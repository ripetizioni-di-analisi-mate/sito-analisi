What is $\displaystyle\iint_\Sigma (x + y + z) \; d\sigma$, where $\Sigma$ is the face $\{ (1, y, z) : 0 \leq y \leq 1,\; 0 \leq z \leq 1 \}$ of the cube $[0, 1]^3$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The face is parametrized by $\mathbf{r}(y, z) = (1, y, z)$, with $(y, z) \in [0, 1]^2$. The partial derivative vectors are $\mathbf{r}_y = (0, 1, 0)$ and $\mathbf{r}_z = (0, 0, 1)$, so $\mathbf{r}_y \wedge \mathbf{r}_z = (1, 0, 0)$ and $d\sigma = dy \, dz$.

On the face we have $x = 1$, therefore
$$
\iint_\Sigma (x + y + z) \; d\sigma = \int_0^1 \!\! \int_0^1 (1 + y + z) \; dy \, dz = 1 + \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} = 2 .
$$
