What is the cross product $r_u\times r_v$ for $\left( 3 u, \  5 v, \  2 u + 2 v\right)$?

- $\left( -10, \  -6, \  15\right)$
- $\left( 10, \  6, \  -15\right)$
- $\left( 0, \  0, \  15\right)$
- $\left( -6, \  -10, \  15\right)$

## Explanation

The derivatives are $\left( 3, \  0, \  2\right)$ and $\left( 0, \  5, \  2\right)$. Take the cross product in the stated order.
