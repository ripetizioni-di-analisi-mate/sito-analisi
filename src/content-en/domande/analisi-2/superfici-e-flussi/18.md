What is the equation of the tangent plane to the sphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 9$ at the point $(1, 2, 2)$?

- $x + 2y + 2z = 3$
- $x + 2y + 2z = 9$
- $x + 2y + 2z = 0$
- $2x + 4y + 4z = 9$

## Explanation

The sphere is centered at the origin, and at each of its points the normal vector is parallel to the position vector, since in spherical coordinates $\mathbf{r}_\varphi \wedge \mathbf{r}_\theta = \sin\varphi \; \mathbf{r}$. At the point $P_0 = (1, 2, 2)$ we can therefore take $\mathbf{N} = (1, 2, 2)$.

The tangent plane passes through $P_0$ and is orthogonal to $\mathbf{N}$, so its equation is
$$
(x - 1) + 2(y - 2) + 2(z - 2) = 0 ,
$$
that is $x + 2y + 2z = 9$. The constant term is $1 + 4 + 4 = 9$, the square of the radius, because $P_0$ lies on the sphere.
