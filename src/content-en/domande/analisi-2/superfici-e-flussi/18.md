What is the equation of the tangent plane to the graph of $f(x, y) = \arctan\dfrac{y}{x}$ at the point with coordinates $(x_0, y_0) = (1, 1)$?

- $z = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{x - y}{2}$
- $z = \dfrac{y - x}{2}$
- $z = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + y - x$
- $z = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{y - x}{2}$

## Explanation

We have $f(1, 1) = \arctan 1 = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$. By the chain rule,
$$
f_x = \frac{1}{1 + \frac{y^2}{x^2}} \cdot \left( -\frac{y}{x^2} \right) = -\frac{y}{x^2 + y^2},
\qquad
f_y = \frac{1}{1 + \frac{y^2}{x^2}} \cdot \frac{1}{x} = \frac{x}{x^2 + y^2},
$$
so $f_x(1, 1) = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $f_y(1, 1) = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

The tangent plane therefore has equation
$$
z = \frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{1}{2}(x - 1) + \frac{1}{2}(y - 1) = \frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{y - x}{2} .
$$
