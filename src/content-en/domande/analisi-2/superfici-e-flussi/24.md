What is the area of the portion of the cylinder $x^2 + y^2 = 4$ lying between the planes $z = 0$ and $z = 3 + x$?

- $6\pi$
- $18\pi$
- $12\pi$
- $24\pi$

## Explanation

The surface is parametrized by $\mathbf{r}(\theta, t) = (2\cos\theta,\; 2\sin\theta,\; t)$, with $\theta \in [0, 2\pi]$ and $0 \leq t \leq 3 + 2\cos\theta$; the upper limit is always positive, because $3 + 2\cos\theta \geq 1$. We have $\mathbf{r}_\theta \wedge \mathbf{r}_t = (2\cos\theta,\; 2\sin\theta,\; 0)$, so the area element is $d\sigma = 2 \, dt \, d\theta$.

The area therefore equals
$$
\operatorname{Area} = \int_0^{2\pi} \!\! \int_0^{3 + 2\cos\theta} 2 \; dt \, d\theta = 2 \int_0^{2\pi} (3 + 2\cos\theta) \; d\theta = 2 \cdot 6\pi = 12\pi ,
$$
since the integral of $\cos\theta$ over $[0, 2\pi]$ is zero.
