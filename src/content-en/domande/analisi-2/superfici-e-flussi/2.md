- $z = x + 2y + 2$
- $z = x + 2y$
- $z = 2x + y - 2$
- $z = x + y$

## Explanation

The tangent plane to the graph of $f$ at the point with coordinates $(x_0, y_0)$ has equation $z = f(x_0, y_0) + f_x(x_0, y_0)(x - x_0) + f_y(x_0, y_0)(y - y_0)$.

For $f(x, y) = x\, e^{y}$ we have $f_x = e^{y}$ and $f_y = x\, e^{y}$, because in the derivative with respect to $y$ the factor $x$ is a constant. At the point $(2, 0)$ we therefore obtain $f(2, 0) = 2$, $f_x(2, 0) = 1$ and $f_y(2, 0) = 2$.

The tangent plane therefore has equation
$$
z = 2 + 1 \cdot (x - 2) + 2 \cdot (y - 0) = x + 2y .
$$
