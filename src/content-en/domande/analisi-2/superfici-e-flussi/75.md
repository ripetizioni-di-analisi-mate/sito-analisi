What is the outward flux of $F=(0,0,3)$ through the upper hemisphere of radius $5$?

- $0$
- $75 \pi$
- $150 \pi$
- $- 75 \pi$

## Explanation

Close the surface with the equatorial disk. The total flux is zero because the divergence vanishes. The disk has downward normal and flux $- 75 \pi$.
