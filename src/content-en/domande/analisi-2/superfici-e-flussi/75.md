For the field $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = \bigl( x - y,\; x + y,\; z \bigr)$, which pair $\bigl( \operatorname{div} \mathbf{F},\; \operatorname{rot} \mathbf{F} \bigr)$ is correct?

- $\bigl( 2,\; (0, 0, 2) \bigr)$
- $\bigl( 3,\; (0, 0, 2) \bigr)$
- $\bigl( 3,\; (0, 0, 0) \bigr)$
- $\bigl( 3,\; (0, 0, -2) \bigr)$

## Explanation

By the definition of **divergence** we have
$$
\operatorname{div} \mathbf{F} = \frac{\partial (x - y)}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial (x + y)}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial z} = 1 + 1 + 1 = 3 .
$$
In the **curl** the first and second components are zero, because $F_3 = z$ does not depend on $x$ and $y$ and the first two components do not depend on $z$. The third component is
$$
\frac{\partial (x + y)}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial (x - y)}{\partial y} = 1 - (-1) = 2 ,
$$
so $\operatorname{rot} \mathbf{F} = (0, 0, 2)$.

The field is the sum of the radial field $(x, y, z)$, which has divergence $3$ and zero curl, and of the rotation field $(-y, x, 0)$, which has zero divergence and curl $(0, 0, 2)$.
