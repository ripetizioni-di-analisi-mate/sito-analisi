What is the area of the graph of $f(x, y) = xy$ over the disk $x^2 + y^2 \leq 1$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{3} \bigl( 2\sqrt{2} - 1 \bigr)$
- $\dfrac{4\sqrt{2}\,\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3} \bigl( \sqrt{2} - 1 \bigr)$
- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3} \bigl( 2\sqrt{2} - 1 \bigr)$

## Explanation

Since $f_x = y$ and $f_y = x$, the formula for the **area of a Cartesian surface** gives the integrand $\sqrt{1 + x^2 + y^2}$, which in polar coordinates becomes $\sqrt{1 + \rho^2}$. With the Jacobian $\rho$ we obtain
$$
\operatorname{Area} = 2\pi \int_0^1 \rho \sqrt{1 + \rho^2} \; d\rho = 2\pi \left[ \frac{(1 + \rho^2)^{\frac{3}{2}}}{3} \right]_0^1 = \frac{2\pi}{3} \bigl( 2\sqrt{2} - 1 \bigr) .
$$
The integral in $\rho$ reduces to the standard integral $\displaystyle\int g^{\alpha}\, g' \; d\rho = \frac{g^{\alpha + 1}}{\alpha + 1} + c$, with $g(\rho) = 1 + \rho^2$ and $\alpha = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
