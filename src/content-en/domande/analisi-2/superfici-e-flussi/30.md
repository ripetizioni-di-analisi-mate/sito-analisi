What is the flux of the field $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = (x, y, z)$ through the hemisphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 4$ with $z \geq 0$, oriented with the unit normal pointing out of the ball?

- $8\pi$
- $16\pi$
- $32\pi$
- $\dfrac{16\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

On the sphere of radius $2$ the outward unit normal is $\mathbf{n} = \dfrac{(x, y, z)}{2}$, so
$$
\mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{n} = \frac{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}{2} = \frac{4}{2} = 2 .
$$
The dot product is constant, therefore the **flux** is the product of $2$ and the area of the hemisphere, which equals $2\pi \cdot 2^2 = 8\pi$. We obtain $\Phi = 2 \cdot 8\pi = 16\pi$.
