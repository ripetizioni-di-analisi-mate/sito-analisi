At which point of the graph of $f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 4y$ is the tangent plane horizontal?

- $(1,\; -2,\; -5)$
- $(-1,\; 2,\; 15)$
- $(1,\; -2,\; 0)$
- $(1,\; -2,\; 5)$

## Explanation

The tangent plane $z = f(x_0, y_0) + f_x(x_0, y_0)(x - x_0) + f_y(x_0, y_0)(y - y_0)$ is horizontal if and only if the coefficients of $x$ and $y$ are zero. Since $f_x = 2x - 2$ and $f_y = 2y + 4$, the point $(x_0, y_0)$ must solve
$$
\begin{cases}
2x - 2 = 0 \\
2y + 4 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
so $(x_0, y_0) = (1, -2)$. The height of the point is $f(1, -2) = 1 + 4 - 2 - 8 = -5$, therefore the point of the graph is $(1, -2, -5)$ and the tangent plane is $z = -5$.
