Let $V \subset \mathbb{R}^3$ be a regular domain. The outward flux of the field $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = (x, y, z)$ through the boundary of $V$ is $12\pi$. What is the volume of $V$?

- $12\pi$
- $36\pi$
- $6\pi$
- $4\pi$

## Explanation

The field is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}^3$, so the **divergence theorem** applies to $V$.

Since $\operatorname{div} \mathbf{F} = 1 + 1 + 1 = 3$, the outward flux is $\displaystyle\iiint_V 3 \; dx \, dy \, dz = 3 \operatorname{Vol}(V)$. From the equality $3 \operatorname{Vol}(V) = 12\pi$ we get $\operatorname{Vol}(V) = 4\pi$, whatever the shape of $V$.
