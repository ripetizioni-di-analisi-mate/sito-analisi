What is the trace of the surface $\mathbf{r}(\theta, t) = \bigl( t\cos\theta,\; t\sin\theta,\; t^2 \bigr)$, with $(\theta, t) \in [0, 2\pi] \times [0, 1]$?

- The portion of the paraboloid $z = x^2 + y^2$ with $0 \leq z \leq 1$
- The portion of the cone $z = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$ with $0 \leq z \leq 1$
- The portion of the cylinder $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ with $0 \leq z \leq 1$
- The hemisphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1$ with $z \geq 0$

## Explanation

From the first two components we obtain $x^2 + y^2 = t^2\cos^2\theta + t^2\sin^2\theta = t^2$, and the third component is $z = t^2$. So every point of the trace satisfies $z = x^2 + y^2$, that is, it lies on the paraboloid.

Since $t$ varies in $[0, 1]$, the height $z = t^2$ varies in $[0, 1]$. For each fixed $t$, as $\theta$ varies in $[0, 2\pi]$ we obtain the whole circle of radius $t$ at height $t^2$. Therefore the trace is the portion of the **paraboloid** $z = x^2 + y^2$ with $0 \leq z \leq 1$.
