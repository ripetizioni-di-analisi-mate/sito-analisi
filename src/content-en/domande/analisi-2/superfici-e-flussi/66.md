The triangle of the plane $x + y + z = 1$ with $x \geq 0$, $y \geq 0$, $z \geq 0$ is oriented upward. What is the flux of the field $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = (x, y, z)$ through it?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The triangle is the graph of $f(x, y) = 1 - x - y$ over the triangle $T$ with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(1, 0)$, $(0, 1)$, and $f_x = f_y = -1$. By the formula for the **flux through a Cartesian surface** the integrand is
$$
-F_1 f_x - F_2 f_y + F_3 = x + y + (1 - x - y) = 1 .
$$
Therefore the flux is the area of $T$, that is $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The value $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ is obtained by multiplying once more by the norm $\sqrt{3}$ of the normal vector, which does not appear in the flux formula.
