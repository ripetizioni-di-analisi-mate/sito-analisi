What is the integral of $\left( - 3 y, \  5 x, \  2 z\right)$ along the boundary of the disk $z=0,\ x^2+y^2\leq 4$ oriented by the upward normal?

- $- 32 \pi$
- $32 \pi$
- $8 \pi$
- $16 \pi$

## Explanation

Stokes’ theorem requires the vertical component of the curl, which equals $8$. Multiply by the disk area.
