What is the circulation of $\left( - 3 y, \  5 x, \  2 z\right)$ around the disk boundary $z=0,\ x^2+y^2\leq 4$ oriented by the upward normal?

- $- 32 \pi$
- $32 \pi$
- $8 \pi$
- $16 \pi$

## Explanation

Stokes’ theorem uses the vertical curl component, $8$. Multiply by the disk area.
