Let $\Sigma$ be the saddle $z = xy$ above the square $[0, 1] \times [0, 1]$, oriented upward, and let $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = (y,\; 0,\; z)$. What is $\displaystyle\iint_\Sigma \mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{n} \; d\sigma$?

- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{7}{12}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

We have $f_x = y$ and $f_y = x$, and on the graph the components of the field are $F_1 = y$, $F_2 = 0$ and $F_3 = xy$. Hence the integrand in the formula for the **flux through a Cartesian surface** is
$$
-F_1 f_x - F_2 f_y + F_3 = -y^2 + xy .
$$
Therefore
$$
\Phi = \int_0^1 \!\! \int_0^1 \bigl( xy - y^2 \bigr) \; dx \, dy = \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{3} = -\frac{1}{12} .
$$
