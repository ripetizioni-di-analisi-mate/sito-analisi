What is the outward flux of $F=(0,0,2)$ through the upper hemisphere of radius $3$?

- $- 18 \pi$
- $0$
- $18 \pi$
- $36 \pi$

## Explanation

When the surface is closed with the equatorial disk, the total flux is zero because the divergence vanishes. The disk has downward normal and flux $- 18 \pi$.
