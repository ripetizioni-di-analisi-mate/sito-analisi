What is the area of the graph of $f(x, y) = \cosh x$ over the rectangle $[0, 1] \times [0, 2]$?

- $2\cosh 1$
- $2\sinh 1$
- $\sinh 1$
- $2(\cosh 1 - 1)$

## Explanation

We have $f_x = \sinh x$ and $f_y = 0$, so the integrand in the formula for the **area of a Cartesian surface** is $\sqrt{1 + \sinh^2 x}$.

Since $1 + \sinh^2 x = \cosh^2 x$ and $\cosh x > 0$, the integrand equals $\cosh x$. The integrand does not depend on $y$, therefore
$$
\operatorname{Area} = \int_0^2 \!\! \int_0^1 \cosh x \; dx \, dy = 2 \bigl[ \sinh x \bigr]_0^1 = 2\sinh 1 .
$$
