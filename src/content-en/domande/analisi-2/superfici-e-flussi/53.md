At the point of the cone $\mathbf{r}(u, v) = \bigl( u\cos v,\; u\sin v,\; u \bigr)$ corresponding to $(u_0, v_0) = \left( 1,\; \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)$, which plane is tangent to the cone?

- $y + z = 2$
- $z = 1$
- $x + z = 1$
- $z = y$

## Explanation

The point is $P_0 = \mathbf{r}\left( 1, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right) = (0, 1, 1)$. The partial derivative vectors are $\mathbf{r}_u = (\cos v,\; \sin v,\; 1)$ and $\mathbf{r}_v = (-u\sin v,\; u\cos v,\; 0)$, which at $\left( 1, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)$ equal $\mathbf{r}_u = (0, 1, 1)$ and $\mathbf{r}_v = (-1, 0, 0)$.

Hence the normal vector is
$$
\mathbf{N} = \bigl( 1 \cdot 0 - 1 \cdot 0,\; 1 \cdot (-1) - 0 \cdot 0,\; 0 \cdot 0 - 1 \cdot (-1) \bigr) = (0,\; -1,\; 1) .
$$
Therefore the tangent plane has equation $-(y - 1) + (z - 1) = 0$, that is $z = y$. This plane passes through the vertex $(0, 0, 0)$ and contains the whole line of the cone through $P_0$.
