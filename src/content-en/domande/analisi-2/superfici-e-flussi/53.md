What is the outward flux of $\left( 2 x, \  3 y, \  5 z\right)$ through the boundary of the unit ball?

- $\dfrac{40 \pi}{3}$
- $40 \pi$
- $- \dfrac{40 \pi}{3}$
- $10 \pi$

## Explanation

By Gauss’s theorem we integrate the constant divergence $10$ over the ball. Its volume is $\dfrac{4 \pi}{3}$.
