What is the area vector with third component $1$ at $(1,1)$ for the graph of $2 x^{2} + 3 y^{2}$?

- $\left( 4, \  6, \  1\right)$
- $\left( -6, \  -4, \  1\right)$
- $\left( -4, \  -6, \  1\right)$
- $\left( -2, \  -3, \  1\right)$

## Explanation

For a graph use $(-z_x,-z_y,1)$. The two derivatives at the point are $\left( 4, \  6\right)$.
