What is the area of the lateral surface of the cone $z = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$ with $0 \leq z \leq 2$?

- $4\pi$
- $4\sqrt{2}\,\pi$
- $2\sqrt{2}\,\pi$
- $8\sqrt{2}\,\pi$

## Explanation

The surface is the graph of $f(x, y) = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$, and the condition $z \leq 2$ is equivalent to $x^2 + y^2 \leq 4$. The base domain is therefore the disk of radius $2$, which has area $4\pi$.

For $(x, y) \neq (0, 0)$ we have $f_x = \dfrac{x}{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}}$ and $f_y = \dfrac{y}{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}}$, so $f_x^2 + f_y^2 = 1$ and the integrand of the formula for the **area of a Cartesian surface** equals $\sqrt{2}$. The single point $(0, 0)$ does not change the value of the double integral, therefore
$$
\operatorname{Area} = \sqrt{2} \cdot 4\pi = 4\sqrt{2}\,\pi .
$$
