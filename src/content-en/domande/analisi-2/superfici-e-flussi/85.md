An oriented surface has two boundary curves. The curl flux through it is $7$. Circulation along the outer boundary in its induced orientation is $10$. What is the circulation along the inner boundary in its own induced orientation?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $17$
- $-17$

## Explanation

Stokes’ theorem adds the contributions of all boundary components in their respective induced orientations. Hence $10+C_{\mathrm{int}}=7$ and the inner contribution is $-3$.
