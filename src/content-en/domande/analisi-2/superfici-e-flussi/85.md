What is the circulation of the field $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = (0,\; -z,\; y)$ along the circle $y^2 + z^2 = 1$ of the plane $x = 0$, traversed counterclockwise as seen from the positive $x$-axis?

- $2\pi$
- $\pi$
- $-2\pi$
- $0$

## Explanation

As a surface with that boundary we choose the disk $D = \{ (0, y, z) : y^2 + z^2 \leq 1 \}$, oriented by $\mathbf{n} = (1, 0, 0)$; the counterclockwise direction as seen from the positive $x$-axis is the one compatible with this normal. The field is of class $C^1$, so **Stokes' theorem** applies.

The curl is
$$
\operatorname{rot} \mathbf{F} = \left( \frac{\partial y}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial (-z)}{\partial z},\; 0 - 0,\; 0 - 0 \right) = (2,\; 0,\; 0) ,
$$
so $\operatorname{rot} \mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{n} = 2$ and the circulation is twice the area of the disk, that is $2\pi$.
