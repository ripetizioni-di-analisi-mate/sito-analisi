What is the outward flux of $F=(x,y,0)$ through the lateral surface of the cylinder with radius $4$ and height $7$?

- $- 224 \pi$
- $224 \pi$
- $112 \pi$
- $56 \pi$

## Explanation

The field is radial and has normal component $4$ on the lateral surface. Multiply by the lateral area.
