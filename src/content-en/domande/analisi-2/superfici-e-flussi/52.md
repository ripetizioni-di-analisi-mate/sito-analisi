The cylinder $x^2 + y^2 = 4$ passes through the point $\bigl( \sqrt{2},\; \sqrt{2},\; 5 \bigr)$. Which plane is tangent to the cylinder at that point?

- $x + y + z = 2\sqrt{2} + 5$
- $x + y = 4$
- $z = 5$
- $x + y = 2\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

With the parametrization $\mathbf{r}(\theta, t) = (2\cos\theta,\; 2\sin\theta,\; t)$ the point corresponds to $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and $t = 5$. We have $\mathbf{r}_\theta \wedge \mathbf{r}_t = (2\cos\theta,\; 2\sin\theta,\; 0)$, which at $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$ equals $\bigl( \sqrt{2},\; \sqrt{2},\; 0 \bigr)$.

The normal vector is horizontal, so the tangent plane is vertical and its equation does not contain $z$. The plane has equation
$$
\sqrt{2}\,\bigl( x - \sqrt{2} \bigr) + \sqrt{2}\,\bigl( y - \sqrt{2} \bigr) = 0 ,
$$
which, divided by $\sqrt{2}$, becomes $x + y = 2\sqrt{2}$.
