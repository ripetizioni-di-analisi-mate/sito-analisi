What is the area of the torus obtained by rotating through a full turn about the $z$-axis the circle in the plane $y = 0$ with center $(2, 0, 0)$ and radius $1$?

- $8\pi^2$
- $4\pi^2$
- $16\pi^2$
- $8\pi$

## Explanation

The circle is parametrized by $x = g(t) = 2 + \cos t$, $z = h(t) = \sin t$, $t \in [0, 2\pi]$, and we have $g(t) \geq 1 > 0$. Since $g'(t)^2 + h'(t)^2 = \sin^2 t + \cos^2 t = 1$, the formula for the **area of a surface of revolution** gives
$$
\operatorname{Area} = 2\pi \int_0^{2\pi} (2 + \cos t) \; dt = 2\pi \cdot 4\pi = 8\pi^2 ,
$$
because the integral of $\cos t$ over $[0, 2\pi]$ is zero.
