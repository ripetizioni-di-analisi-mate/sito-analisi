What is the integral of $\left( - 2 y, \  3 x, \  5 z\right)$ along the boundary of the disk $z=0,\ x^2+y^2\leq 25$ oriented by the upward normal?

- $0$
- $- 125 \pi$
- $125 \pi$
- $25 \pi$

## Explanation

Stokes’ theorem requires the vertical component of the curl, which equals $5$. Multiply by the disk area.
