The torus is parametrized by $\mathbf{r}(u, v) = \bigl( (2 + \cos u)\cos v,\; (2 + \cos u)\sin v,\; \sin u \bigr)$, with $(u, v) \in [0, 2\pi] \times [0, 2\pi]$. What is $\mathbf{N} = \mathbf{r}_u \wedge \mathbf{r}_v$ at the point corresponding to $(u, v) = \left( \dfrac{\pi}{2},\; 0 \right)$?

- $(0,\; 0,\; -2)$
- $(0,\; 0,\; 2)$
- $(-2,\; 0,\; 0)$
- $(0,\; 0,\; -1)$

## Explanation

The point is $\mathbf{r}\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2}, 0 \right) = (2, 0, 1)$. The partial derivative vectors are
$$
\mathbf{r}_u = \bigl( -\sin u \cos v,\; -\sin u \sin v,\; \cos u \bigr), \qquad \mathbf{r}_v = \bigl( -(2 + \cos u)\sin v,\; (2 + \cos u)\cos v,\; 0 \bigr),
$$
which at $\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2}, 0 \right)$ equal $\mathbf{r}_u = (-1, 0, 0)$ and $\mathbf{r}_v = (0, 2, 0)$. Hence the cross product is
$$
\mathbf{N} = \bigl( 0 \cdot 0 - 0 \cdot 2,\; 0 \cdot 0 - (-1) \cdot 0,\; (-1) \cdot 2 - 0 \cdot 0 \bigr) = (0,\; 0,\; -2) .
$$
The point $(2, 0, 1)$ is the highest point of the torus above the positive $x$-axis, therefore the normal vector is vertical; with this parametrization it points downward, that is, toward the inside of the torus.
