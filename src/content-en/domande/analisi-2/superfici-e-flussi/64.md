Which vector is normal to the level surface through $(1,1,0)$ and equals the gradient of $3 x^{2} + 5 y^{2} + 2 z^{2}$?

- $\left( 10, \  6, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 6, \  10, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  5, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 6, \  10, \  4\right)$

## Explanation

The gradient is perpendicular to the surface. At $(1,1,0)$ the $z$ derivative vanishes.
