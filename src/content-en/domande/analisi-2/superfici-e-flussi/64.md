What is $\displaystyle\iint_\Sigma \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 + 4z}} \; d\sigma$, where $\Sigma$ is the portion of the paraboloid $z = x^2 + y^2$ with $z \leq 1$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{6} \bigl( 5\sqrt{5} - 1 \bigr)$
- $2\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\pi$

## Explanation

The surface is the graph of $f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2$ over the disk $D$ of radius $1$, and the area element is $d\sigma = \sqrt{1 + 4x^2 + 4y^2} \; dx \, dy$.

On the surface we have $z = x^2 + y^2$, so the integrand equals $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1 + 4x^2 + 4y^2}}$ and cancels with the area element. Therefore
$$
\iint_\Sigma \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 + 4z}} \; d\sigma = \iint_D dx \, dy = \pi ,
$$
that is, the area of the disk of radius $1$. The value $\dfrac{\pi}{6} \bigl( 5\sqrt{5} - 1 \bigr)$ is the area of the surface.
