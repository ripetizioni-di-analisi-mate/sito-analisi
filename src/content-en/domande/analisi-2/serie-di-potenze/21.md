What is the radius of convergence of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{n^3}{n!} \, x^n$?

- $1$
- $0$
- $e$
- $+\infty$

## Explanation

The coefficient is $a_n = \dfrac{n^3}{n!}$. Since $(n+1)! = (n+1) \, n!$, we have
$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{(n+1)^3}{(n+1)!} \cdot \frac{n!}{n^3} = \frac{(n+1)^2}{n^3} \to 0 .
$$
By the **ratio test for the radius**, with the convention for $\ell = 0$, we therefore have $R = +\infty$.
