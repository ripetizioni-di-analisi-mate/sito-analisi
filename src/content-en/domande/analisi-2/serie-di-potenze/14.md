## Explanation

The denominator is $x^4$, so the expansion of $e^{x^2}$ is needed up to the fourth order in $x$, that is, up to the second order in $x^2$. From the expansion $e^y = 1 + y + \dfrac{y^2}{2} + o(y^2)$ as $y \to 0$, with $y = x^2$ we obtain
$$
e^{x^2} = 1 + x^2 + \frac{x^4}{2} + o(x^4) .
$$
The numerator therefore equals $\dfrac{x^4}{2} + o(x^4)$, and
$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^{x^2} - 1 - x^2}{x^4} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\dfrac{x^4}{2} + o(x^4)}{x^4} = \frac{1}{2} .
$$
