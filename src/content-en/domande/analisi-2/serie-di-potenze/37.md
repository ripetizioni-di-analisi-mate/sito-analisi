For the function $f(x) = \cos(2x)$, which estimate of the derivatives holds for every $n \geq 0$ and every $x \in \mathbb{R}$?

- $|f^{(n)}(x)| \leq 2^n$
- $|f^{(n)}(x)| \leq 1$
- $|f^{(n)}(x)| \leq 2$
- $|f^{(n)}(x)| \leq 2n$

## Explanation

The $n$-th derivative of $f$ equals $\pm 2^n \cos(2x)$ or $\pm 2^n \sin(2x)$, so $|f^{(n)}(x)| \leq 2^n$ for every $n$ and every $x$.

The other estimates are false. We have $f''(0) = -4$, so the estimates with $1$ and with $2$ do not hold for $n = 2$; we have $f'''(x) = 8 \sin(2x)$ and $f'''\!\left( \dfrac{\pi}{4} \right) = 8 > 6$, so the estimate with $2n$ does not hold for $n = 3$.

The estimate $|f^{(n)}(x)| \leq 2^n$ is the condition $|f^{(n)}(x)| \leq M L^n$ of the **expandability criterion** with $M = 1$ and $L = 2$, therefore $\cos(2x)$ is expandable in a Maclaurin series on all of $\mathbb{R}$.
