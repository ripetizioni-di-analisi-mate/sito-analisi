What is the exact convergence set of $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \dfrac{\left(-1\right)^{n} 3^{- n} \left(x - 2\right)^{n}}{n}$?

- $\left[-1, 5\right]$
- $\left(-1, 5\right]$
- $\left(-1, 5\right)$
- $\left[-1, 5\right)$

## Explanation

The sign $(-1)^n$ combines with the sign of the base. On the left the signs cancel and the harmonic series remains. On the right the alternating harmonic series remains.
