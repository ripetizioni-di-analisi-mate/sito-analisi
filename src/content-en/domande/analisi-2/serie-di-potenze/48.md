The coefficients of the power series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n x^n$ form a convergent sequence, with $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} a_n = 7$. What is the radius of convergence of the series?

- $7$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $+\infty$

## Explanation

Since $a_n \to 7$, from some index on we have $6 < a_n < 8$, hence
$$
\sqrt[n]{6} < \sqrt[n]{|a_n|} < \sqrt[n]{8} .
$$
The sequences $\sqrt[n]{6}$ and $\sqrt[n]{8}$ both tend to $1$, therefore by the **squeeze theorem** for sequences $\sqrt[n]{|a_n|} \to 1$ as well.

By the **root test for the radius** we thus have $R = 1$. The value $7$ of the limit of the coefficients does not affect the radius, because the $n$-th root of every positive constant tends to $1$.
