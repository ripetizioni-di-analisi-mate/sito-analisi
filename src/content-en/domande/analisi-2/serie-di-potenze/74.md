What is the exact remainder after $\sum_{n=0}^{2}\left(\dfrac{x}{3}\right)^n$ at $x=3/2$ compared with the infinite sum?

- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The first omitted power has exponent $3$ and the ratio is $1/2$. The geometric tail is the first omitted term divided by $1-1/2$.
