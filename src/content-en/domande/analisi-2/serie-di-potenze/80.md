What is the exact convergence set of $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \dfrac{2^{- n} \left(x - 4\right)^{n}}{n}$?

- $\left[2, 6\right]$
- $\left(2, 6\right)$
- $\left(2, 6\right]$
- $\left[2, 6\right)$

## Explanation

Inside radius $2$ the series converges absolutely. The left endpoint gives the alternating harmonic series and the right endpoint gives the harmonic series.
