What is the sum of $\sum_{n=1}^\infty n \left(\dfrac{x}{5}\right)^{n}$ for $|x|<5$?

- $\dfrac{x}{\left(5 - x\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{x}{5 - x}$
- $\dfrac{25}{\left(5 - x\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{5 x}{\left(5 - x\right)^{2}}$

## Explanation

Let $s=x/5$. Differentiate the geometric series in $s$ and multiply by $s$ to obtain $s/(1-s)^2$.
