Let $P_3(x)$ be the cubic Maclaurin polynomial of $\ln(1+2x)$. For $0<x<\dfrac{1}{2}$, which error bound follows from the alternating-series estimate?

- $|\ln(1+2x)-P_3(x)|\le x^5$
- $|\ln(1+2x)-P_3(x)|=0$
- $|\ln(1+2x)-P_3(x)|\le4x^4$
- $|\ln(1+2x)-P_3(x)|\ge2x^3$

## Explanation

The magnitudes $\dfrac{(2x)^n}{n}$ decrease to zero under the stated condition. The first omitted term has magnitude $\dfrac{(2x)^4}{4}=4x^4$.
