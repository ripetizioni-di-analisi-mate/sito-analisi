What is the radius of convergence of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{n!}{n^n} \, x^n$?

- $\dfrac{1}{e}$
- $1$
- $+\infty$
- $e$

## Explanation

The coefficient is $a_n = \dfrac{n!}{n^n}$, so
$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{(n+1)!}{(n+1)^{n+1}} \cdot \frac{n^n}{n!} = \frac{n^n}{(n+1)^n} = \frac{1}{\left( 1 + \dfrac{1}{n} \right)^{\! n}} .
$$
By the standard limit for the number $e$, this ratio tends to $\dfrac{1}{e}$. By the **ratio test for the radius** we therefore have $R = e$.
