What is the set of convergence of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n^n \, x^n$?

- $\{ 0 \}$
- $(-1,\, 1)$
- $[-1,\, 1]$
- $\mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

The coefficient $a_n = n^n$ is an $n$-th power and $\sqrt[n]{a_n} = n \to +\infty$. By the **root test for the radius**, with the convention for $\ell = +\infty$, we therefore have $R = 0$.

With zero radius the series converges only at its center. Indeed, for every $x \neq 0$ the absolute value of the general term is $(n \, |x|)^n$, which tends to $+\infty$ because $n \, |x| > 2$ from some $n$ on. Therefore $E = \{ 0 \}$.
