The series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} 2^n \, x^{2n+1}$ contains only the odd powers of $x$ and converges for $|x| < \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$. What is its sum on this interval?

- $\dfrac{1}{1 - 2x^2}$
- $\dfrac{x}{1 - 2x^2}$
- $\dfrac{x}{1 - 2x}$
- $\dfrac{x}{1 + 2x^2}$

## Explanation

Factoring out $x$ we have $2^n \, x^{2n+1} = x \left( 2x^2 \right)^{n}$, so the series is $x$ times the geometric series of ratio $2x^2$.

The geometric series of ratio $2x^2$ converges if and only if $2x^2 < 1$, that is $|x| < \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$, and in this case its sum is $\dfrac{1}{1 - 2x^2}$. Therefore
$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} 2^n \, x^{2n+1} = \frac{x}{1 - 2x^2} , \qquad |x| < \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} .
$$
The sum is an odd function, as it must be for a series with only odd powers.
