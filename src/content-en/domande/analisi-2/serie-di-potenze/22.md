What is the radius of convergence of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^{3n}}{8^n}$?

- $8$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

The series contains only the powers of $x$ whose exponent is a multiple of $3$, so it is **lacunary** and is studied with a substitution. With $t = x^3$ the series becomes
$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \left( \frac{t}{8} \right)^{\! n} ,
$$
a geometric series that converges if and only if $|t| < 8$.

The condition $|x^3| < 8$ is equivalent to $|x| < 2$, so the series converges for $|x| < 2$ and does not converge for $|x| \geq 2$. The radius is therefore $R = 2$.
