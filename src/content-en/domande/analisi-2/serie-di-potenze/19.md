What is the radius of convergence of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left( 1 + \frac{1}{n} \right)^{\! n^2} x^n$?

- $e$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{e}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The coefficient $a_n = \left( 1 + \dfrac{1}{n} \right)^{\! n^2}$ is an $n$-th power, because $n^2 = n \cdot n$. We therefore have
$$
\sqrt[n]{a_n} = \left( 1 + \frac{1}{n} \right)^{\! n} \to e
$$
by the standard limit for the number $e$. By the **root test for the radius** we therefore have $R = \dfrac{1}{e}$.
