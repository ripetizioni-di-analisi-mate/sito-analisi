The function $\tan x$ is odd, so its Maclaurin expansion is $a_1 x + a_3 x^3 + o(x^3)$. What is the coefficient $a_3$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Since $\tan x \cdot \cos x = \sin x$, the product of the required expansion and that of the cosine must coincide with the expansion of the sine:
$$
\left( a_1 x + a_3 x^3 \right) \left( 1 - \frac{x^2}{2} \right) = x - \frac{x^3}{6} + o(x^3) .
$$
The coefficient of $x$ gives $a_1 = 1$. The coefficient of $x^3$ gives $a_3 - \dfrac{a_1}{2} = -\dfrac{1}{6}$, so $a_3 = \dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{1}{3}$.

By the **uniqueness of the power series expansion** the coefficients found are those of the Maclaurin series of $\tan x$.
