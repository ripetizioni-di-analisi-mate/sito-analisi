The Maclaurin expansion of $\arcsin x$ contains only odd powers. What coefficient does $x^5$ have?

- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{40}$
- $\dfrac{3}{40}$

## Explanation

For $|x| < 1$ we have $\arcsin x = \displaystyle\int_0^x \frac{dt}{\sqrt{1 - t^2}}$. From the **binomial series** with $\alpha = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ and the substitution of $x$ with $-t^2$:
$$
(1 - t^2)^{-\frac{1}{2}} = 1 + \frac{t^2}{2} + \frac{3}{8} t^4 + \cdots ,
$$
because $\dbinom{-\frac{1}{2}}{1} (-t^2) = \dfrac{t^2}{2}$ and $\dbinom{-\frac{1}{2}}{2} t^4 = \dfrac{\left( -\frac{1}{2} \right) \left( -\frac{3}{2} \right)}{2} \, t^4 = \dfrac{3}{8} \, t^4$.

By **term-by-term integration**
$$
\arcsin x = x + \frac{x^3}{6} + \frac{3}{8} \cdot \frac{x^5}{5} + \cdots ,
$$
so the coefficient of $x^5$ is $\dfrac{3}{40}$.
