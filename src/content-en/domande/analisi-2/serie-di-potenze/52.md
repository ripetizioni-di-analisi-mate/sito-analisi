What is the exact convergence set of $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \dfrac{5^{- n} \left(x - 3\right)^{n}}{n^{2}}$?

- $\left(-2, 8\right)$
- $\left[-2, 8\right]$
- $\left[-2, 8\right)$
- $\left(-2, 8\right]$

## Explanation

The radius is $5$. At both endpoints the absolute values of the terms are $1/n^2$, so both endpoints are included.
