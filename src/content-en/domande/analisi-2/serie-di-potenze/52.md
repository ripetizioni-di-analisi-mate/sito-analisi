The series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^{2n}}{4^n}$ is written in the form $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} a_k x^k$. What is the coefficient $a_6$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4096}$
- $\dfrac{1}{64}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$

## Explanation

Only the even powers $x^{2n}$ appear in the series, and the power $x^{2n}$ has coefficient $\dfrac{1}{4^n}$. The coefficient $a_k$ of the power $x^k$ is therefore obtained by setting $k = 2n$, that is $n = \dfrac{k}{2}$, when $k$ is even, while $a_k = 0$ when $k$ is odd.

For $k = 6$ we have $n = 3$, therefore $a_6 = \dfrac{1}{4^3} = \dfrac{1}{64}$.

For this series the sequence $(a_k)$ alternates zero and nonzero values, so the ratio $\dfrac{a_{k+1}}{a_k}$ is not even defined: this is why in lacunary series the radius is computed with a substitution.
