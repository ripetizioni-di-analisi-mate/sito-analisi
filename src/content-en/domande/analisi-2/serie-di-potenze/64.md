What is $\displaystyle\int_0^1 \left( \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n!} \right) dx$?

- $e - 1$
- $e$
- $\dfrac{e}{2}$
- $1 - \dfrac{1}{e}$

## Explanation

The series has radius $+\infty$, so on the interval $[0, 1]$ it converges normally and by **term-by-term integration**
$$
\int_0^1 \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n!} \, dx = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n!} \cdot \frac{1}{n+1} = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(n+1)!} .
$$
With the index $k = n + 1$ the series becomes $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{k!} = e - 1$, because the series $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{k!}$ equals $e$ and the term with $k = 0$ equals $1$.

The same value is obtained by recognizing in the series the function $e^x$, whose antiderivative gives $e^1 - e^0 = e - 1$.
