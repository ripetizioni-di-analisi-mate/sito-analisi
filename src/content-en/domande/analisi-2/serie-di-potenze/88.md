What is the sum of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(2n)!} = 1 + \frac{1}{2!} + \frac{1}{4!} + \cdots$?

- $\dfrac{e}{2}$
- $\dfrac{e - e^{-1}}{2}$
- $\cos 1$
- $\dfrac{e + e^{-1}}{2}$

## Explanation

From the exponential expansion at $x = 1$ and at $x = -1$ we have
$$
e = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n!} ,
\qquad
e^{-1} = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n}{n!} .
$$
Adding term by term, the term of index $n$ is $\dfrac{1 + (-1)^n}{n!}$, which equals $\dfrac{2}{n!}$ for even $n$ and $0$ for odd $n$.

Hence $e + e^{-1} = \displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{2}{(2k)!}$, and the required sum is $\dfrac{e + e^{-1}}{2}$. The value $\cos 1$ corresponds to the series with alternating signs $\displaystyle\sum \frac{(-1)^k}{(2k)!}$.
