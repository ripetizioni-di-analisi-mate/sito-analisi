What is the coefficient of $x^3$ in the expansion at zero of $\int_0^x \dfrac{1}{1+4t}\,dt$?

- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{16}{3}$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}$
- $16$

## Explanation

The quadratic term of the integrand is $16 t^{2}$. Integrating from zero to $x$ gives coefficient $\dfrac{16}{3}$.
