The function $f(x) = \sin x \cos x$ coincides on all of $\mathbb{R}$ with which power series?

- $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n \, 4^n \, x^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!}$
- $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n \, 2^{2n+1} \, x^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!}$
- $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n \, x^{2n+1}}{2 \, (2n+1)!}$
- $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n \, 4^n \, x^{2n}}{(2n)!}$

## Explanation

By the double-angle formula, $\sin x \cos x = \dfrac{1}{2} \sin(2x)$. Replacing $x$ with $2x$ in the expansion of the sine, valid on all of $\mathbb{R}$,
$$
\frac{1}{2} \sin(2x) = \frac{1}{2} \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n \, (2x)^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!} .
$$

Since $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2^{2n+1} = 2^{2n} = 4^n$, we obtain $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n \, 4^n \, x^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!}$, valid for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$. The first terms are $x - \dfrac{2x^3}{3} + \cdots$
