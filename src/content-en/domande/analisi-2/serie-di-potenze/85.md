What number is obtained by summing the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(\ln 3)^n}{n!}$?

- $3$
- $\ln 3$
- $e^3$
- $3e$

## Explanation

The series has the form of the exponential expansion $e^x = \displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n!}$, valid for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$, with $x = \ln 3$.

Hence the sum is $e^{\ln 3} = 3$.
