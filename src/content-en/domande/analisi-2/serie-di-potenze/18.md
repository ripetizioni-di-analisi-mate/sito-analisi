What is the radius of convergence of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(2n)!}{(n!)^2} \, x^n$?

- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The coefficient is $a_n = \dfrac{(2n)!}{(n!)^2}$. Since $(2n+2)! = (2n+2)(2n+1) \, (2n)!$ and $(n+1)! = (n+1) \, n!$, we have
$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{(2n+2)(2n+1)}{(n+1)^2} = \frac{2 \, (2n+1)}{n+1} \to 4 .
$$
By the **ratio test for the radius** the radius is the reciprocal of this limit, so $R = \dfrac{1}{4}$.
