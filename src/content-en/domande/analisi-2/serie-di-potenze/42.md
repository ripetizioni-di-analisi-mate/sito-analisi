What is the sum of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n \, \pi^{2n}}{4^n \, (2n)!}$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
- $0$
- $-1$

## Explanation

Since $\dfrac{\pi^{2n}}{4^n} = \left( \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)^{\! 2n}$, the general term is $\dfrac{(-1)^n}{(2n)!} \left( \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)^{\! 2n}$. The series is therefore the standard expansion
$$
\cos x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n \, x^{2n}}{(2n)!}
$$
evaluated at $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The expansion of the cosine holds on all of $\mathbb{R}$, therefore the sum of the series is $\cos \dfrac{\pi}{2} = 0$.
