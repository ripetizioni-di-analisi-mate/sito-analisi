What is the set of convergence of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{x^{2n}}{n \, 25^n}$?

- $(-5,\, 5)$
- $(-25,\, 25)$
- $[-5,\, 5)$
- $[-5,\, 5]$

## Explanation

The series contains only the even powers of $x$, so it is **lacunary** and is studied with a substitution. With $t = \dfrac{x^2}{25} \geq 0$ the series becomes $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{t^n}{n}$.

This series has radius $1$, because the ratio $\dfrac{n}{n+1}$ tends to $1$, and for $t = 1$ it is the harmonic series, which diverges. Since $t \geq 0$, the series in $t$ converges if and only if $0 \leq t < 1$, that is, $x^2 < 25$, that is, $|x| < 5$.

The endpoints $x = \pm 5$ both correspond to $t = 1$, so at those points the series diverges and $E = (-5,\, 5)$.
