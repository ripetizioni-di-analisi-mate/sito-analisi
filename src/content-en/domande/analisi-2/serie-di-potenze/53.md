On which interval does the lacunary series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(x - 1)^{2n}}{9^n}$ converge?

- $(-8,\, 10)$
- $[-2,\, 4]$
- $(-4,\, 2)$
- $(-2,\, 4)$

## Explanation

We set $t = \dfrac{(x - 1)^2}{9} \geq 0$, and the series becomes the geometric series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} t^n$. This converges if and only if $t < 1$, that is $(x - 1)^2 < 9$.

The condition $(x - 1)^2 < 9$ is equivalent to $|x - 1| < 3$, that is $-2 < x < 4$. At the endpoints $x = -2$ and $x = 4$ we have $t = 1$ and the general term equals $1$, so it does not tend to $0$ and the series does not converge.

Therefore the set of convergence is $(-2,\, 4)$, an interval centered at $1$ with radius $3$.
