- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{25}{16}$
- $\dfrac{5}{16}$
- $\dfrac{5}{36}$

## Explanation

For $|x| < 1$, differentiating the geometric series term by term we obtain $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n \, x^{n-1} = \frac{1}{(1 - x)^2}$, and multiplying by $x$
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n \, x^n = \frac{x}{(1 - x)^2} .
$$
The given series is the value of this series at $x = \dfrac{1}{5}$, which is interior to $(-1, 1)$, so
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{n}{5^n} = \frac{\dfrac{1}{5}}{\left( 1 - \dfrac{1}{5} \right)^{\! 2}} = \frac{\dfrac{1}{5}}{\dfrac{16}{25}} = \frac{5}{16} .
$$
