## Explanation

The coefficient is $a_n = \dfrac{1}{n^2 \, 3^n}$ and $\dfrac{|a_{n+1}|}{|a_n|} = \dfrac{1}{3} \left( \dfrac{n}{n+1} \right)^{\! 2} \to \dfrac{1}{3}$, so by the **ratio test for the radius** we have $R = 3$. Since $x + 2 = x - (-2)$, the series is centered at $x_0 = -2$, therefore the open interval of convergence is $(-2 - 3,\, -2 + 3) = (-5,\, 1)$.

At $x = 1$ and at $x = -5$ we have $|x + 2| = 3$, so the absolute value of the general term equals $\dfrac{3^n}{n^2 \, 3^n} = \dfrac{1}{n^2}$. The series $\sum \dfrac{1}{n^2}$ converges, therefore the given series **converges absolutely** at both endpoints and $E = [-5,\, 1]$.
