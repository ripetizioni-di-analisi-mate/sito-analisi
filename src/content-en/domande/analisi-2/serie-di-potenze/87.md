What is the sum of $\sum_{n=1}^\infty n \left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)^{n}$ for $|x|<2$?

- $\dfrac{2 x}{\left(2 - x\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{4}{\left(2 - x\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{x}{\left(2 - x\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{x}{2 - x}$

## Explanation

With $s=x/2$, differentiate the geometric series with respect to $s$ and multiply by $s$. This gives $s/(1-s)^2$.
