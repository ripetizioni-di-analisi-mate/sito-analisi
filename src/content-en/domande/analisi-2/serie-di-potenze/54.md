What is the coefficient of $x^3$ in the expansion at zero of $\ln{\left(2 x + 1 \right)}$?

- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{8}{3}$
- $8$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

In the series for $\ln(1+s)$, the term with index $3$ has an alternating sign and denominator $3$. Set $s=2x$.
