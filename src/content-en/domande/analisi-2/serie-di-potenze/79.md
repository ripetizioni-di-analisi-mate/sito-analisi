In the binomial series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \binom{\alpha}{n} x^n$ with $\alpha = 3$, which coefficients are zero?

- None
- Those with $n \geq 4$
- Those with $n \geq 3$
- Those with odd $n$

## Explanation

The coefficient is $\dbinom{3}{n} = \dfrac{3 \cdot (3 - 1) \cdots (3 - n + 1)}{n!}$. For $n \geq 4$ the product in the numerator contains the factor $3 - 3 = 0$, so the coefficient is zero.

For $n = 0, 1, 2, 3$ the coefficients are $1, 3, 3, 1$, all different from $0$. Therefore the series reduces to the polynomial $1 + 3x + 3x^2 + x^3 = (1 + x)^3$, that is, to Newton's binomial formula, and the expansion holds for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$.
