Two power series centred at zero both have radius 1. What is the radius of their sum if their coefficients cancel at every degree?

- $1$
- $0$
- $+\infty$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The summed series has only zero coefficients. It converges for every $x$. Its radius can therefore exceed those of the summands.
