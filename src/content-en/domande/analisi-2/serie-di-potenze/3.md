What is the set of convergence of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left( \frac{n+2}{2n+1} \right)^{\! n} x^n$?

- $(-2,\, 2)$
- $[-2,\, 2)$
- $\left( -\dfrac{1}{2},\, \dfrac{1}{2} \right)$
- $[-2,\, 2]$

## Explanation

The coefficient is an $n$-th power and $\sqrt[n]{|a_n|} = \dfrac{n+2}{2n+1} \to \dfrac{1}{2}$, so by the **root test for the radius** we have $R = 2$.

At $x = \pm 2$ the absolute value of the general term equals
$$
\left( \frac{2n+4}{2n+1} \right)^{\! n} = \left( 1 + \frac{3}{2n+1} \right)^{\! n} .
$$
Since $\dfrac{3}{2n+1} \to 0$ and $n \cdot \dfrac{3}{2n+1} \to \dfrac{3}{2}$, by the standard limit for the number $e$ this quantity tends to $e^{\frac{3}{2}} \neq 0$. The general term does not tend to $0$ at either endpoint, therefore the series does not converge at $x = \pm 2$ and $E = (-2,\, 2)$.
