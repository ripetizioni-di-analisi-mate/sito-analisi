The series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n x^n$ has radius of convergence $2$ and the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} b_n x^n$ has radius of convergence $5$. What is the radius of convergence of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} (a_n + b_n) x^n$?

- $2$
- $5$
- $7$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$

## Explanation

For $|x| < 2$ both series converge, so the sum series also converges, and it has radius at least $2$.

For $2 < |x| < 5$ the series $\sum b_n x^n$ converges and the series $\sum a_n x^n$ does not converge. If the sum series converged at such an $x$, then $\sum a_n x^n = \sum (a_n + b_n) x^n - \sum b_n x^n$ would converge too, which is false. Therefore the sum series does not converge for $2 < |x| < 5$.

Hence the radius of convergence of the sum series is exactly $2$, the smaller of the two radii.
