What is the exact convergence set of $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \dfrac{3^{- n} \left(x - 2\right)^{n}}{n}$?

- $\left(-1, 5\right]$
- $\left[-1, 5\right]$
- $\left[-1, 5\right)$
- $\left(-1, 5\right)$

## Explanation

Within the radius $3$ the series converges absolutely. The left endpoint gives the alternating harmonic series and the right endpoint gives the harmonic series.
