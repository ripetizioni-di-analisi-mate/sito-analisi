What is the exact remainder after $\sum_{n=0}^{5}\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)^n$ at $x=2/2$ compared with the infinite sum?

- $\dfrac{1}{32}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{64}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$

## Explanation

The first omitted power has exponent $6$ and the ratio is $1/2$. The geometric tail is the first omitted term divided by $1-1/2$.
