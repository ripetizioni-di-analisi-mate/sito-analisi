For $p > 0$ the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n^p}$ has radius of convergence $1$. For which values of $p$ is its set of convergence $[-1,\, 1]$?

- $p \geq 1$
- $p > 1$
- $p > 0$
- $p > 2$

## Explanation

At $x = -1$ the series is $\displaystyle\sum \frac{(-1)^n}{n^p}$. For every $p > 0$ the sequence $\dfrac{1}{n^p}$ is decreasing and tends to $0$, so by the **alternating series test** the series converges.

At $x = 1$ the series is the generalized harmonic series $\displaystyle\sum \frac{1}{n^p}$, which converges if and only if $p > 1$.

Therefore the set of convergence is $[-1,\, 1]$ if and only if $p > 1$; for $0 < p \leq 1$ it is $[-1,\, 1)$.
