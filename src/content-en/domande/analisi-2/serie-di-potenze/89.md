What is the exact remainder after $\sum_{n=0}^{7}\left(\dfrac{x}{4}\right)^n$ at $x=4/2$ compared with the infinite sum?

- $\dfrac{1}{256}$
- $\dfrac{1}{128}$
- $\dfrac{1}{64}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The first omitted power has exponent $8$ and the ratio is $1/2$. Divide the first omitted term by $1-1/2$.
