What is the coefficient of $x^3$ in the expansion at zero of $\int_0^x \dfrac{1}{1+2t}\,dt$?

- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{4}{3}$
- $4$

## Explanation

The quadratic term of the integrand is $4 t^{2}$. Integrating it from zero to $x$ gives the coefficient $\dfrac{4}{3}$.
