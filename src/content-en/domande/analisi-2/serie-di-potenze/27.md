The power series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n x^n$ has radius of convergence $4$. What is the radius of convergence of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n x^{2n}$?

- $4$
- $2$
- $16$
- $8$

## Explanation

With $t = x^2$ the second series can be written as $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n t^n$, which has radius $4$. By the **radius of convergence theorem** this series converges absolutely for $|t| < 4$ and does not converge for $|t| > 4$.

The condition $x^2 < 4$ is equivalent to $|x| < 2$, and the condition $x^2 > 4$ is equivalent to $|x| > 2$. Hence the series $\sum a_n x^{2n}$ converges absolutely for $|x| < 2$ and does not converge for $|x| > 2$, therefore its radius is $R = 2$.
