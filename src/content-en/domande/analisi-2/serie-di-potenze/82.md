What is the exact convergence set of $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \dfrac{7^{- n} \left(x - 2\right)^{n}}{n^{2}}$?

- $\left(-5, 9\right]$
- $\left[-5, 9\right]$
- $\left[-5, 9\right)$
- $\left(-5, 9\right)$

## Explanation

The radius is $7$. At both endpoints the term magnitudes are $1/n^2$, so both endpoints are included.
