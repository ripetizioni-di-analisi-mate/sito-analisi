What is the radius of convergence of the lacunary series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n! \, x^{n!} = x + 2x^2 + 6x^6 + 24x^{24} + \cdots$, in which the coefficient of the power $x^{n!}$ is $n!$?

- $0$
- $+\infty$
- $e$
- $1$

## Explanation

The sequence of coefficients contains infinitely many zeros, so we study the terms $u_n = n! \, x^{n!}$ with $x \neq 0$.

If $|x| > 1$, then $|u_n| \geq |x|^{n!} \to +\infty$. The general term does not tend to $0$, so the series does not converge.

If $0 < |x| < 1$, since $(n+1)! - n! = n \cdot n!$, we have
$$
\frac{|u_{n+1}|}{|u_n|} = (n+1) \, |x|^{n \cdot n!} \leq (n+1) \, |x|^{n} \to 0 .
$$
By the ratio test the series converges absolutely.

The series therefore converges for $|x| < 1$ and does not converge for $|x| > 1$, hence the radius is $1$. The coefficients $n!$ do not change the radius, because in the ratio the factor $|x|^{n \cdot n!}$ tends to $0$ faster than $n + 1$ tends to $+\infty$.
