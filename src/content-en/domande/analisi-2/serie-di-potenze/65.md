What is the exact convergence set of $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \dfrac{5^{- n} \left(x - 3\right)^{n}}{n}$?

- $\left(-2, 8\right)$
- $\left[-2, 8\right]$
- $\left(-2, 8\right]$
- $\left[-2, 8\right)$

## Explanation

Within the radius $5$ the series converges absolutely. The left endpoint gives the alternating harmonic series and the right endpoint gives the harmonic series.
