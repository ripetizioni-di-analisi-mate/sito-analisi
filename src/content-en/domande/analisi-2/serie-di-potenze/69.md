What is the coefficient of $x^5$ in the expansion at zero of $\ln{\left(3 x + 1 \right)}$?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{243}{5}$
- $243$
- $\dfrac{243}{5}$

## Explanation

In the series for $\ln(1+s)$, the term with index $5$ has an alternating sign and denominator $5$. Set $s=3x$.
