## Explanation

The coefficient is $a_n = \dfrac{1}{n \, 2^n}$ and $\dfrac{|a_{n+1}|}{|a_n|} = \dfrac{n}{2 \, (n+1)} \to \dfrac{1}{2}$, so by the **ratio test for the radius** we have $R = 2$. The series is centered at $x_0 = 1$, therefore the open interval of convergence is $(1 - 2,\, 1 + 2) = (-1,\, 3)$.

At $x = 3$ we have $(x - 1)^n = 2^n$, so the general term is $\dfrac{1}{n}$ and the series is the harmonic series, which diverges.

At $x = -1$ we have $(x - 1)^n = (-2)^n$, so the general term is $\dfrac{(-1)^n}{n}$. Since $\dfrac{1}{n}$ is decreasing and tends to $0$, the series converges by the **alternating series test**. Consequently $E = [-1,\, 3)$.
