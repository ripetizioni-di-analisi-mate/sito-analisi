The series with positive terms $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{n+1}{n!}$ converges by the ratio test. What is its sum?

- $e$
- $2e$
- $e + 1$
- $e^2$

## Explanation

Multiplying the exponential series by $x$ gives, for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$,
$$
x \, e^x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^{n+1}}{n!} .
$$
The series has radius $+\infty$, so by **term-by-term differentiation**
$$
(1 + x) \, e^x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(n+1) \, x^n}{n!} , \qquad x \in \mathbb{R} .
$$
The given series is the value of this series at $x = 1$, therefore its sum is $(1 + 1) \, e^1 = 2e$.
