The function $f(x) = \displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n x^n$, with positive radius of convergence, satisfies $f'(x) = 2 f(x)$ and $f(0) = 3$. What is the coefficient $a_n$?

- $\dfrac{3 \cdot 2^n}{n!}$
- $3 \cdot 2^n$
- $\dfrac{2^n}{n!}$
- $\dfrac{6^n}{n!}$

## Explanation

By **term-by-term differentiation**
$$
f'(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n \, a_n x^{n-1} = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} (n+1) \, a_{n+1} x^n .
$$
The equality $f' = 2f$ and the **uniqueness of the power series expansion** give $(n+1) \, a_{n+1} = 2 a_n$ for every $n$, that is $a_{n+1} = \dfrac{2}{n+1} \, a_n$.

Since $a_0 = f(0) = 3$, applying the relation $n$ times we obtain $a_n = \dfrac{3 \cdot 2^n}{n!}$. The function is therefore $f(x) = 3 e^{2x}$.
