What is the exact convergence set of $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \dfrac{2^{- n} \left(x - 5\right)^{n}}{n^{2}}$?

- $\left(3, 7\right]$
- $\left[3, 7\right]$
- $\left(3, 7\right)$
- $\left[3, 7\right)$

## Explanation

The radius is $2$. At both endpoints the term magnitudes are $1/n^2$, so both endpoints are included.
