What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x - \arctan x}{x^3}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $0$

## Explanation

As $x \to 0$ the numerator and the denominator tend to $0$, so the limit is in the indeterminate form $\dfrac{0}{0}$. The denominator is $x^3$, therefore the expansion of the arctangent is needed up to the third order. From the Maclaurin expansion of the arctangent we have $\arctan x = x - \dfrac{x^3}{3} + o(x^3)$, so the numerator is $\dfrac{x^3}{3} + o(x^3)$ and
$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x - \arctan x}{x^3} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\dfrac{x^3}{3} + o(x^3)}{x^3} = \frac{1}{3} .
$$
