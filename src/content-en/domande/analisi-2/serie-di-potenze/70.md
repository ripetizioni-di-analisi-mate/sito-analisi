What is the coefficient of $x^4$ in the expansion at zero of $\cos{\left(3 x^{2} \right)}$?

- $- \dfrac{9}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $-9$

## Explanation

The first term after the constant in the cosine expansion is minus one half of the square of the argument. Here the argument has degree two.
