What is the coefficient of $x^4$ in the expansion at zero of $\cos{\left(3 x^{2} \right)}$?

- $- \dfrac{9}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $-9$

## Explanation

The first term after the constant in cosine is negative half the squared argument. Here the argument is quadratic.
