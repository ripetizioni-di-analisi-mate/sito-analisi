Let $L$ be the L-shaped region given by the union of the rectangle $[0, 2] \times [0, 1]$ and the square $[0, 1] \times [1, 2]$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\iint_L x\, dx\, dy$?

::grafico{xmin=-0.4 xmax=2.6 ymin=-0.4 ymax=2.5 uguale=1 area="1;0;0;2|2;1;0;1" segmenti="0,0,2,0;2,0,2,1;2,1,1,1;1,1,1,2;1,2,0,2;0,2,0,0" etichette="1.55,0.45,L" titolo="The region $L$, union of the rectangle $[0, 2] \times [0, 1]$ and the square $[0, 1] \times [1, 2]$"}

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The rectangle and the square have no interior points in common, so by **additivity over the domain** the integral over $L$ is the sum of the two integrals. On the rectangle
$$
\iint_{[0,2] \times [0,1]} x\, dx\, dy = \int_0^2 x\, dx \cdot \int_0^1 dy = 2 ,
$$
and on the square $\displaystyle\iint_{[0,1] \times [1,2]} x\, dx\, dy = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 1 = \frac{1}{2}$. Therefore the integral equals $2 + \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{5}{2}$. The value $4$ is the integral over the whole square $[0, 2] \times [0, 2]$, which also contains the square $[1, 2] \times [1, 2]$ that is not part of $L$.
