With the change of variables $x = 2u + v$, $y = u + 3v$, which relation between the area elements is correct?

- $dx\, dy = 7\, du\, dv$
- $dx\, dy = \dfrac{1}{5}\, du\, dv$
- $dx\, dy = 5\, du\, dv$
- $dx\, dy = 6\, du\, dv$

## Explanation

The Jacobian of the transformation is the determinant of the matrix of the partial derivatives of $x$ and $y$ with respect to $u$ and $v$, so
$$
J = \det
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
1 & 3
\end{pmatrix}
= 2 \cdot 3 - 1 \cdot 1 = 5 .
$$
By the **change of variables** theorem the area element is multiplied by $|J|$, therefore $dx\, dy = 5\, du\, dv$. The factor $\dfrac{1}{5}$ is the Jacobian of the inverse transformation, which expresses $u$ and $v$ as functions of $x$ and $y$.
