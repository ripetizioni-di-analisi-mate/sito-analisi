Let $D$ be the region lying between the parabola $y = x^2$ and the line $y = 1$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\iint_D y\, dx\, dy$?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{8}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$

## Explanation

The parabola and the line intersect where $x^2 = 1$, that is, at $x = -1$ and $x = 1$, and for $-1 \leq x \leq 1$ we have $x^2 \leq 1$. Hence $D$ is the **normal domain** with respect to the $x$-axis
$$
D = \left\{ (x, y) :
\begin{cases}
-1 \leq x \leq 1 \\
x^2 \leq y \leq 1
\end{cases}
\right\} .
$$
By the reduction formula the inner integral equals $\displaystyle\int_{x^2}^{1} y\, dy = \frac{1 - x^4}{2}$, therefore
$$
\iint_D y\, dx\, dy = \int_{-1}^{1} \frac{1 - x^4}{2}\, dx = \frac{1}{2} \left( 2 - \frac{2}{5} \right) = \frac{4}{5} .
$$
