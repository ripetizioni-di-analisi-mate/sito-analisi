What is $\iint_D 3 x^{2} + 5 y^{2}\,dx\,dy$ with $D=\{x^2+y^2\leq 4\}$?

- $32 \pi$
- $12 \pi$
- $\dfrac{64 \pi}{3}$
- $64 \pi$

## Explanation

In polar coordinates the two squared components have the same angular average. Each angular integral is $\pi$ and the radial integral is $16/4$.
