## Explanation

The tetrahedron lies between the plane $z = 0$ and the plane $z = 2 - x - y$ above the triangle $0 \leq x \leq 2$, $0 \leq y \leq 2 - x$, so **integration by vertical lines** applies. Along the vertical line we obtain
$$
\int_0^{2 - x - y} z \, dz = \frac{(2 - x - y)^2}{2} .
$$
Integrating with respect to $y$ between $0$ and $2 - x$ gives $\displaystyle\int_0^{2 - x} \frac{(2 - x - y)^2}{2}\, dy = \frac{(2 - x)^3}{6}$, therefore
$$
\iiint_T z \, dx\, dy\, dz = \int_0^2 \frac{(2 - x)^3}{6}\, dx = \frac{1}{6} \cdot \frac{2^4}{4} = \frac{2}{3} .
$$
