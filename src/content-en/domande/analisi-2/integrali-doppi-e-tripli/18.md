What is the value of the double integral $\displaystyle\iint_Q \frac{x}{1 + xy}\, dx\, dy$, where $Q = [0, 1] \times [0, 1]$?

- $\ln 2$
- $2 \ln 2 - 1$
- $2 \ln 2$
- $\ln 2 - 1$

## Explanation

The function is continuous on the square, so by **Fubini's theorem** we can integrate first with respect to $y$. For fixed $x$, the inner integral reduces to the standard integral $\displaystyle\int \frac{f'(y)}{f(y)}\, dy = \ln |f(y)| + c$ with $f(y) = 1 + xy$, therefore
$$
\int_0^1 \frac{x}{1 + xy}\, dy = \bigl[ \ln (1 + xy) \bigr]_{y=0}^{y=1} = \ln (1 + x) .
$$
Integrating by parts with respect to $x$ gives
$$
\int_0^1 \ln (1 + x)\, dx = \bigl[ (1 + x) \ln (1 + x) - x \bigr]_0^1 = 2 \ln 2 - 1 .
$$
