What is $\iint_D 2 x^{2} + 3 y^{2}\,dx\,dy$ with $D=\{x^2+y^2\leq 25\}$?

- $\dfrac{625 \pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{625 \pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3125 \pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3125 \pi}{2}$

## Explanation

In polar coordinates the two squared components have the same angular average. Each angular integral is $\pi$ and the radial integral is $625/4$.
