What is $\iint_D 3 x + 5\,dx\,dy$ with $D=\{0\leq x\leq 1,\ 0\leq y\leq 2x^2\}$?

- $\dfrac{16}{3}$
- $7$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{29}{6}$

## Explanation

Integrating in $y$ multiplies the integrand by the height $2 x^{2}$. Integrate the resulting two monomials from zero to one.
