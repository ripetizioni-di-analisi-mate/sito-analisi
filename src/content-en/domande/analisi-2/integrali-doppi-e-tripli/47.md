On the square $Q=[0,1]^2$ the identity $f(x,y)+f(1-x,1-y)=6$ holds everywhere and $f$ is continuous. Find $\iint_Q f(x,y)\,dx\,dy$.

- $6$
- $\dfrac32$
- $0$
- $3$

## Explanation

A half-turn takes the square onto itself and has Jacobian determinant of absolute value one, so the two terms have the same integral. Integrating the identity gives $2I=6\cdot1$.
