What is the value of the double integral of the function $e^{x + y}$ over the rectangle $R = [0, 1] \times [0, 2]$?

- $e^3 - 1$
- $e^2 (e - 1)$
- $(e - 1)^2$
- $(e - 1)\left( e^2 - 1 \right)$

## Explanation

Since $e^{x + y} = e^x e^y$, the function is the product of a function of $x$ and a function of $y$, and the domain is a rectangle. By the theorem on **functions of separated variables** the integral is the product of two single integrals:
$$
\int_0^1 e^x \, dx \cdot \int_0^2 e^y \, dy = (e - 1)\left( e^2 - 1 \right) .
$$
The value $e^2 (e - 1)$ forgets the contribution $-1$ of the lower limit in the integral in $y$, while $(e - 1)^2$ integrates $e^y$ over $[0, 1]$ instead of $[0, 2]$.
