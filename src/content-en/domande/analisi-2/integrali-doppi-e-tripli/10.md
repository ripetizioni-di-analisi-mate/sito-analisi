## Explanation

By linearity the integral is the sum of the integrals of $x^3$, of $5y$ and of $2$. The disk $x^2 + y^2 \leq 4$ is symmetric with respect to the $y$-axis and $x^3$ is odd in $x$, so by the theorem on **odd functions on symmetric domains** the integral of $x^3$ is zero. The disk is also symmetric with respect to the $x$-axis and $5y$ is odd in $y$, therefore the integral of $5y$ is zero as well. What remains is the integral of the constant $2$, equal to $2$ times the area of the disk of radius $2$, that is, $2 \cdot 4\pi = 8\pi$.
