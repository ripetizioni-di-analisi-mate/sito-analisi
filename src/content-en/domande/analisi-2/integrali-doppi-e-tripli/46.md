The square $Q = [0, 1] \times [0, 1]$ is divided into four squares of side $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and in each of them the upper right vertex is chosen. What is the value of the Riemann sum of $f(x, y) = x + y$ built with these points?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $6$

## Explanation

The chosen points are $\left( \dfrac{1}{2}, \dfrac{1}{2} \right)$, $\left( 1, \dfrac{1}{2} \right)$, $\left( \dfrac{1}{2}, 1 \right)$ and $(1, 1)$, where $f$ takes the values $1$, $\dfrac{3}{2}$, $\dfrac{3}{2}$ and $2$. Each square has area $\dfrac{1}{4}$, so the **Riemann sum** is
$$
S = \frac{1}{4} \left( 1 + \frac{3}{2} + \frac{3}{2} + 2 \right) = \frac{1}{4} \cdot 6 = \frac{3}{2} .
$$
The value $6$ is the sum of the values of $f$ without the areas, $\dfrac{1}{2}$ is the sum built with the lower left vertices and $1$ is the double integral of $f$ over $Q$. The sum with the upper right vertices exceeds the integral, because $f$ is increasing in both variables.
