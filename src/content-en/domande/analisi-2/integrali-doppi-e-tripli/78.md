What is $\iint_D 4 x^{2} + 2 y^{2}\,dx\,dy$ with $D=\{x^2+y^2\leq 49\}$?

- $\dfrac{7203 \pi}{2}$
- $7203 \pi$
- $2401 \pi$
- $686 \pi$

## Explanation

In polar coordinates the two quadratic components have the same angular average. Each angular integral is $\pi$ and the radial integral is $2401/4$.
