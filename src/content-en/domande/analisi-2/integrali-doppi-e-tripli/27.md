Let $C$ be the annulus $1 \leq x^2 + y^2 \leq 4$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\iint_C \frac{1}{x^2 + y^2}\, dx\, dy$?

- $\pi \ln 2$
- $\pi$
- $2\pi \ln 2$
- $4\pi \ln 2$

## Explanation

The domain is an annulus centered at the origin and the function depends on $x^2 + y^2$, so we switch to **polar coordinates**. The circles $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ and $x^2 + y^2 = 4$ have radii $1$ and $2$, therefore the annulus becomes the rectangle $1 \leq \rho \leq 2$, $0 \leq \theta \leq 2\pi$. With the area element $\rho\, d\rho\, d\theta$ the integrand becomes $\dfrac{1}{\rho^2} \cdot \rho = \dfrac{1}{\rho}$, so
$$
\iint_C \frac{1}{x^2 + y^2}\, dx\, dy = \int_0^{2\pi} d\theta \cdot \int_1^2 \frac{1}{\rho}\, d\rho = 2\pi \ln 2 .
$$
