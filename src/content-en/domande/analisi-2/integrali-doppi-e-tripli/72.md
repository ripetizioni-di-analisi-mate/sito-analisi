The solid $E$ lies between the plane $z = 0$ and the plane $z = y$, above the square $[0, 1] \times [0, 1]$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\iiint_E e^{z}\, dx\, dy\, dz$?

- $e - 1$
- $e - 2$
- $e$
- $2 - e$

## Explanation

The solid lies between the plane $z = 0$ and the plane $z = y$ above the square $[0, 1] \times [0, 1]$, so by **integration by vertical lines**
$$
\iiint_E e^{z}\, dx\, dy\, dz = \iint_{[0,1] \times [0,1]} \left( \int_0^{y} e^{z}\, dz \right) dx\, dy = \int_0^1 dx \cdot \int_0^1 \left( e^{y} - 1 \right) dy .
$$
Since $\displaystyle\int_0^1 \left( e^{y} - 1 \right) dy = (e - 1) - 1$, the integral equals $e - 2$. The value $e - 1$ forgets the contribution $-1$ of the lower limit in the integral in $z$.
