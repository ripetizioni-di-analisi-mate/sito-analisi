What are the exact limits in the order $dx\,dy$ on the domain $0\leq x\leq1,\quad 3x\leq y\leq 3$?

- $0\leq y\leq 3,\ y/3\leq x\leq1$
- $0\leq y\leq 3,\ 0\leq x\leq y/3$
- $0\leq y\leq1,\ 0\leq x\leq y/3$
- $0\leq y\leq 3,\ 0\leq x\leq1$

## Explanation

The projection onto the $y$-axis is $[0,3]$. For fixed $y$ the constraint $3x\leq y$ gives the upper bound $y/3$.
