On the square $Q = [0, 1] \times [0, 1]$ consider $A = \displaystyle\iint_Q \sin(xy)\, dx\, dy$ and $B = \displaystyle\iint_Q xy\, dx\, dy = \dfrac{1}{4}$. Which relation between $A$ and $B$ is true?

- $A > B$
- $A < B$
- $A = B$
- $A < 0$

## Explanation

For $s \geq 0$ we have $\sin s \leq s$, with equality only for $s = 0$. On $Q$ we have $xy \geq 0$, so $\sin(xy) \leq xy$ and by **monotonicity** $A \leq B$. The continuous function $xy - \sin(xy)$ is positive on the square $\left[ \dfrac{1}{2}, 1 \right] \times \left[ \dfrac{1}{2}, 1 \right]$, where it has a positive minimum $c$, therefore by additivity and monotonicity $B - A \geq \dfrac{c}{4} > 0$ and hence $A < B$. Moreover $\sin(xy) \geq 0$ on $Q$, since $0 \leq xy \leq 1 < \pi$, so $A \geq 0$. The value of $A$ is about $0.24$.
