What is the triple integral of $x^2+y^2+z^2$ over the ball centered at the origin with radius $2$?

- $\dfrac{64 \pi}{5}$
- $\dfrac{128 \pi}{5}$
- $\dfrac{32 \pi}{5}$
- $\dfrac{32 \pi}{3}$

## Explanation

In spherical coordinates the integrand gives $r^2$ and the Jacobian gives another $r^2$ in addition to the sine of the angle. The angular integral is $4\pi$.
