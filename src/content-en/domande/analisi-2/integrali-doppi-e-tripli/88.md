What is the volume of the solid $E = \left\{ (x, y, z) : x^2 + y^2 \leq 1, \ x^2 + z^2 \leq 1 \right\}$, the intersection of two cylinders of radius $1$ with perpendicular axes?

- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$
- $2\pi$

## Explanation

We integrate **by slices** with respect to $x \in [-1, 1]$, cutting the solid with planes perpendicular to the $x$-axis. For fixed $x$ the conditions become $|y| \leq \sqrt{1 - x^2}$ and $|z| \leq \sqrt{1 - x^2}$, so the cross-section is a square of side $2\sqrt{1 - x^2}$ and area $4 \left( 1 - x^2 \right)$. The volume is the integral of the areas of the cross-sections:
$$
\operatorname{Vol}(E) = \int_{-1}^{1} 4 \left( 1 - x^2 \right) dx = 4 \cdot \frac{4}{3} = \frac{16}{3} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$ is the volume of the unit ball, which is contained in $E$ and whose cross-sections are the disks inscribed in those squares.
