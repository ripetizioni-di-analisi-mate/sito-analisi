Let $B$ be the ball $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \leq 1$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\iiint_B z^2\, dx\, dy\, dz$?

- $\dfrac{4\pi}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2\pi}{15}$
- $\dfrac{4\pi}{9}$
- $\dfrac{4\pi}{15}$

## Explanation

The region is the ball of radius $1$, so we switch to **spherical coordinates**, with $0 \leq r \leq 1$, $0 \leq \varphi \leq \pi$, $0 \leq \theta \leq 2\pi$. Since $z = r \cos\varphi$ and the volume element is $r^2 \sin\varphi \, dr\, d\varphi\, d\theta$, the integrand becomes $r^4 \cos^2\varphi \sin\varphi$. The integral in $\varphi$ reduces to the standard integral $\displaystyle\int t^2\, dt = \frac{t^3}{3} + c$ with $t = \cos\varphi$, so it equals $\left[ -\dfrac{\cos^3\varphi}{3} \right]_0^{\pi} = \dfrac{2}{3}$. The limits are constant and the function has separated variables, therefore
$$
\iiint_B z^2\, dx\, dy\, dz = \int_0^{2\pi} d\theta \cdot \int_0^{\pi} \cos^2\varphi \sin\varphi \, d\varphi \cdot \int_0^1 r^4 \, dr = 2\pi \cdot \frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{5} = \frac{4\pi}{15} .
$$
