What is $\iint_D 2 x + 3\,dx\,dy$ with $D=\{0\leq x\leq 1,\ 0\leq y\leq 5x^2\}$?

- $\dfrac{65}{6}$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}$
- $\dfrac{25}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

Integrating in $y$ multiplies the integrand by the height $5 x^{2}$. Integrate the resulting two monomials from zero to one.
