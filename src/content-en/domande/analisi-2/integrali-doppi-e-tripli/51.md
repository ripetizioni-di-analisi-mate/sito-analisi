What are the exact limits in the order $dx\,dy$ on $0\leq x\leq1,\quad 2x\leq y\leq 2$?

- $0\leq y\leq 2,\ 0\leq x\leq y/2$
- $0\leq y\leq1,\ 0\leq x\leq y/2$
- $0\leq y\leq 2,\ 0\leq x\leq1$
- $0\leq y\leq 2,\ y/2\leq x\leq1$

## Explanation

The projection onto the $y$ axis is $[0,2]$. For fixed $y$ the constraint $2x\leq y$ gives upper bound $y/2$.
