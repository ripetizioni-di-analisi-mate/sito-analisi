What is the value of the double integral $\displaystyle\iint_R y \cos(xy)\, dx\, dy$, where $R = [0, \pi] \times [0, 1]$?

- $2$
- $0$
- $-\dfrac{2}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{2}{\pi}$

## Explanation

The function is continuous on the rectangle, so by **Fubini's theorem** the order of integration can be chosen. For fixed $y$, an antiderivative of $y \cos(xy)$ with respect to $x$ is $\sin(xy)$, therefore we integrate first with respect to $x$ and obtain
$$
\int_0^{\pi} y \cos(xy)\, dx = \bigl[ \sin(xy) \bigr]_{x=0}^{x=\pi} = \sin(\pi y) .
$$
Integrating this result with respect to $y$ gives
$$
\int_0^1 \sin(\pi y)\, dy = \left[ -\frac{\cos(\pi y)}{\pi} \right]_0^1 = \frac{1}{\pi} + \frac{1}{\pi} = \frac{2}{\pi} .
$$
In the opposite order the inner integral in $y$ would require integration by parts.
