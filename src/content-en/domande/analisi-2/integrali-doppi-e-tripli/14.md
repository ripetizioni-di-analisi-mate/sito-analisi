- $\dfrac{64\pi}{5}$
- $\dfrac{32\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{64\pi^2}{5}$

## Explanation

The region is the ball of radius $2$ and the function is the square of the distance from the origin, so we switch to **spherical coordinates**, with $0 \leq r \leq 2$, $0 \leq \varphi \leq \pi$, $0 \leq \theta \leq 2\pi$. Since $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = r^2$ and the volume element is $r^2 \sin\varphi \, dr\, d\varphi\, d\theta$, the integrand becomes $r^4 \sin\varphi$. The limits are constant and the function has separated variables, therefore the integral is the product of three single integrals
$$
\int_0^{2\pi} d\theta \cdot \int_0^{\pi} \sin\varphi \, d\varphi \cdot \int_0^2 r^4 \, dr = 2\pi \cdot 2 \cdot \frac{32}{5} = \frac{128\pi}{5} .
$$
