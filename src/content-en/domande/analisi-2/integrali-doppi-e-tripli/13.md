## Explanation

The solid is a cylinder whose axis is the $z$-axis, so we switch to **cylindrical coordinates**, with $0 \leq \rho \leq 1$, $0 \leq \theta \leq 2\pi$, $0 \leq z \leq 2$. Since $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2} = \rho$ and the volume element is $\rho \, d\rho\, d\theta\, dz$, the integrand becomes $z \rho \cdot \rho = z \rho^2$. The limits are constant and the function has separated variables, therefore
$$
\iiint_E z \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}\, dx\, dy\, dz = \int_0^{2\pi} d\theta \cdot \int_0^1 \rho^2 \, d\rho \cdot \int_0^2 z \, dz = 2\pi \cdot \frac{1}{3} \cdot 2 = \frac{4\pi}{3} .
$$
