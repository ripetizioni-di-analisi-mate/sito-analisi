What is the area of the region between the parabola $y = x^2$ and the line $y = x + 2$?

- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}$
- $3$

## Explanation

The two curves meet where $x^2 = x + 2$, that is, at $x = -1$ and $x = 2$, and for $-1 \leq x \leq 2$ the line lies above the parabola. The region is therefore normal with respect to the $x$-axis and its area is the integral of $1$:
$$
\operatorname{Area} = \int_{-1}^{2} \left( \int_{x^2}^{x + 2} dy \right) dx = \int_{-1}^{2} \left( x + 2 - x^2 \right) dx = \frac{3}{2} + 6 - 3 = \frac{9}{2} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{15}{2}$ is the area under the line without subtracting the area under the parabola, and $\dfrac{10}{3}$ considers only the part with $x \geq 0$.
