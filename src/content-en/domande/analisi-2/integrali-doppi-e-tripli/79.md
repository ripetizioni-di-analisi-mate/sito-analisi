The solid $E$ consists of the points of the ball $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \leq 1$ lying between the cones with axis $z$ and half-opening angles $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, that is, $0 \leq r \leq 1$, $\dfrac{\pi}{4} \leq \varphi \leq \dfrac{\pi}{3}$, $0 \leq \theta \leq 2\pi$ in spherical coordinates. What is the volume of $E$?

- $\pi \left( \sqrt{2} - 1 \right)$
- $\dfrac{\pi^2}{18}$
- $\dfrac{\pi \left( \sqrt{2} - 1 \right)}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi \left( \sqrt{3} - \sqrt{2} \right)}{3}$

## Explanation

In **spherical coordinates** the volume element is $r^2 \sin\varphi\, dr\, d\varphi\, d\theta$. The limits are constant and the integrand is a product of factors in the single variables, so
$$
\operatorname{Vol}(E) = \int_0^{2\pi} d\theta \cdot \int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{3}} \sin\varphi\, d\varphi \cdot \int_0^1 r^2\, dr = 2\pi \left( \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} - \frac{1}{2} \right) \cdot \frac{1}{3} = \frac{\pi \left( \sqrt{2} - 1 \right)}{3} .
$$
The value $\pi \left( \sqrt{2} - 1 \right)$ forgets the factor $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the integral in $r$, $\dfrac{\pi^2}{18}$ is obtained without the factor $\sin\varphi$, and $\dfrac{\pi \left( \sqrt{3} - \sqrt{2} \right)}{3}$ uses $\sin\varphi$ as an antiderivative of $\sin\varphi$.
