Let $E = \left\{ (x, y, z) : 0 \leq z \leq 1 - \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \right\}$ be the homogeneous cone with base the unit disk in the plane $z = 0$ and vertex at $(0, 0, 1)$. What is the height $\bar{z}$ of its center of mass?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The cone is homogeneous, so $\bar{z}$ is the ratio between the integral of $z$ over $E$ and the volume of $E$. We integrate **by slices**: at height $z \in [0, 1]$ the cross-section is the disk of radius $1 - z$, with area $\pi (1 - z)^2$. Therefore
$$
\operatorname{Vol}(E) = \int_0^1 \pi (1 - z)^2\, dz = \frac{\pi}{3} ,
\qquad
\iiint_E z\, dx\, dy\, dz = \int_0^1 z \, \pi (1 - z)^2\, dz = \frac{\pi}{12} ,
$$
and $\bar{z} = \dfrac{\pi}{12} \cdot \dfrac{3}{\pi} = \dfrac{1}{4}$. The center of mass lies closer to the base, where the cross-sections are larger; the value $\dfrac{3}{4}$ is the height of the center of mass of a cone with its vertex at the bottom.
