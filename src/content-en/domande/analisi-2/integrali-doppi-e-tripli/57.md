What is the value of the integral of the function $|x|$ over the unit disk $D = \{ (x, y) : x^2 + y^2 \leq 1 \}$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The disk is symmetric with respect to the $y$-axis and the function $|x|$ is **even** in $x$, so the integral is twice the integral over the half-disk $D^+$ with $x \geq 0$, where $|x| = x$. In polar coordinates $D^+$ is described by $0 \leq \rho \leq 1$, $-\dfrac{\pi}{2} \leq \theta \leq \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, therefore
$$
\iint_{D^+} x\, dx\, dy = \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos\theta \, d\theta \cdot \int_0^1 \rho^2 \, d\rho = 2 \cdot \frac{1}{3} = \frac{2}{3} .
$$
The integral over $D$ is therefore $\dfrac{4}{3}$. The value $0$ treats $|x|$ as an odd function, and $\dfrac{2}{3}$ is the contribution of $D^+$ alone.
