The function $xyz$ is integrated over the box $P = [0, 1] \times [0, 2] \times [0, 3]$. What is the value of the triple integral?

- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $9$

## Explanation

The domain is a box with edges parallel to the axes and the function is the product of three factors, each in a single variable. Integrating one variable at a time, the limits are constant and each factor comes out of the integrals in the other variables, so
$$
\iiint_P xyz\, dx\, dy\, dz = \int_0^1 x\, dx \cdot \int_0^2 y\, dy \cdot \int_0^3 z\, dz = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot \frac{9}{2} = \frac{9}{2} .
$$
The value $6$ is the volume of $P$, that is, the integral of $1$.
