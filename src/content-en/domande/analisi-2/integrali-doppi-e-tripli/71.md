What is the triple integral of $x^2+y^2+z^2$ over the ball centered at the origin with radius $3$?

- $\dfrac{972 \pi}{5}$
- $\dfrac{243 \pi}{5}$
- $36 \pi$
- $\dfrac{486 \pi}{5}$

## Explanation

In spherical coordinates the integrand gives $r^2$ and the Jacobian gives another $r^2$ besides the angular sine. The angular integral is $4\pi$.
