What is the volume of the solid lying between the paraboloids $z = x^2 + y^2$ and $z = 2 - x^2 - y^2$?

- $2\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{8\pi}{3}$
- $\pi$

## Explanation

The two surfaces meet where $x^2 + y^2 = 2 - x^2 - y^2$, that is, on the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$. Inside the disk $D$ of radius $1$ we have $x^2 + y^2 \leq 2 - x^2 - y^2$, so the volume is the integral over $D$ of the **difference** $2 - 2(x^2 + y^2)$. In polar coordinates, with the area element $\rho\, d\rho\, d\theta$, we obtain
$$
\operatorname{Vol} = \int_0^{2\pi} \left( \int_0^1 \left( 2 - 2\rho^2 \right) \rho \, d\rho \right) d\theta = 2\pi \left( 1 - \frac{1}{2} \right) = \pi .
$$
