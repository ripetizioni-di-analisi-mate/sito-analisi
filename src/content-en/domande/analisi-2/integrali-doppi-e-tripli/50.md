What is the volume of the solid between the rectangle $R = [0, 2] \times [0, 3]$ in the $xy$-plane and the graph of $f(x, y) = 1 + xy$?

- $15$
- $9$
- $24$
- $6$

## Explanation

The function $f$ is positive on $R$, so the volume is the double integral of $f$ over $R$. By **linearity** the integral splits into two terms. The integral of $1$ is the area of the rectangle, that is $6$, and by separation of variables
$$
\iint_R xy \, dx\, dy = \int_0^2 x\, dx \cdot \int_0^3 y\, dy = 2 \cdot \frac{9}{2} = 9 .
$$
Therefore the volume is $6 + 9 = 15$. The value $9$ forgets the constant term, and $24$ is obtained by writing $9$ instead of $\dfrac{9}{2}$ as the integral of $y$.
