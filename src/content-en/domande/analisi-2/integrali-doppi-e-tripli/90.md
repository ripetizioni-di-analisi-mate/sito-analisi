What is the area of the half-annulus $D = \{ (x, y) : 1 \leq x^2 + y^2 \leq 4, \ x \geq 0 \}$?

- $3\pi$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$
- $\pi$

## Explanation

In **polar coordinates** the half-annulus becomes the rectangle $1 \leq \rho \leq 2$, $-\dfrac{\pi}{2} \leq \theta \leq \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, and the area is the integral of $1$:
$$
\operatorname{Area}(D) = \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \left( \int_1^2 \rho \, d\rho \right) d\theta = \pi \cdot \frac{4 - 1}{2} = \frac{3\pi}{2} .
$$
The value $3\pi$ is the area of the whole annulus, and $\pi$ is obtained by integrating without the factor $\rho$.
