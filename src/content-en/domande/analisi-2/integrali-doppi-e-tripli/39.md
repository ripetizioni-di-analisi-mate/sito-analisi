Let $E = \{ (x, y, z) : 0 \leq z \leq 1, \ |x| \leq z, \ |y| \leq z \}$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\iiint_E z\, dx\, dy\, dz$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The solid is a pyramid with apex at the origin and base the square $[-1, 1] \times [-1, 1]$ at height $z = 1$. Its cross-section at height $z \in [0, 1]$ is the square $|x| \leq z$, $|y| \leq z$, of side $2z$ and area $4z^2$. The function $z$ depends only on the height, so by **integration by slices** the double integral over each cross-section equals $z \cdot 4z^2$. Therefore
$$
\iiint_E z\, dx\, dy\, dz = \int_0^1 4z^3 \, dz = 1 .
$$
