## Explanation

With the variables $u = xy$ and $v = \dfrac{y}{x}$ the region becomes the rectangle $1 \leq u \leq 3$, $1 \leq v \leq 2$. The Jacobian of $(u, v)$ with respect to $(x, y)$ is
$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
y & x \\[4pt]
-\dfrac{y}{x^2} & \dfrac{1}{x}
\end{pmatrix}
= \frac{y}{x} + \frac{y}{x} = 2v .
$$
The Jacobians of the two transformations are reciprocal, so $dx\, dy = \dfrac{du\, dv}{2v}$. Therefore, by the **change of variables** theorem,
$$
\operatorname{Area} = \int_1^3 \left( \int_1^2 \frac{1}{2v}\, dv \right) du = 2 \cdot \frac{\ln 2}{2} = \ln 2 .
$$
