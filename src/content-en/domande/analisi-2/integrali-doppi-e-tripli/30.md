Let $P = \{ (x, y) : 0 \leq x + y \leq 1, \ 0 \leq x - y \leq 2 \}$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\iint_P (x + y)^2\, dx\, dy$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

With the variables $u = x + y$ and $v = x - y$ the parallelogram $P$ becomes the rectangle $0 \leq u \leq 1$, $0 \leq v \leq 2$ and the function becomes $u^2$. The Jacobian of $(u, v)$ with respect to $(x, y)$ is
$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 1 \\
1 & -1
\end{pmatrix}
= -2 .
$$
The Jacobians of the two transformations are reciprocal and the formula contains the absolute value, so $dx\, dy = \dfrac{1}{2}\, du\, dv$. Therefore, by **change of variables**,
$$
\iint_P (x + y)^2\, dx\, dy = \frac{1}{2} \int_0^2 \left( \int_0^1 u^2\, du \right) dv = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{3} \cdot 2 = \frac{1}{3} .
$$
