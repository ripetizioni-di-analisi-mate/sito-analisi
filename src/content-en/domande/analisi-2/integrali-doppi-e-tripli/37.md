Let $E = \{ (x, y, z) : x^2 + y^2 \leq z \leq 1 \}$, let $D$ be the disk $x^2 + y^2 \leq 1$ and let $f$ be continuous on $E$. Which formula for $\displaystyle\iiint_E f\, dx\, dy\, dz$ is correct?

- $\displaystyle\iint_D \left( \int_0^1 f\, dz \right) dx\, dy$
- $\displaystyle\iint_{[-1, 1] \times [-1, 1]} \left( \int_{x^2 + y^2}^{1} f\, dz \right) dx\, dy$
- $\displaystyle\int_0^1 \left( \iint_D f\, dx\, dy \right) dz$
- $\displaystyle\iint_D \left( \int_{x^2 + y^2}^{1} f\, dz \right) dx\, dy$

## Explanation

The solid lies between the paraboloid $z = x^2 + y^2$ and the plane $z = 1$. The two surfaces meet where $x^2 + y^2 = 1$, so the projection of $E$ onto the $xy$-plane is the disk $D$. For each $(x, y) \in D$ the vertical line enters $E$ through the paraboloid and exits through the plane $z = 1$, therefore by **integration by vertical lines** the height $z$ runs from $x^2 + y^2$ to $1$ and the double integral is computed over $D$. With $z$ between $0$ and $1$ one would integrate over the cylinder $x^2 + y^2 \leq 1$, $0 \leq z \leq 1$, which contains $E$. In integration by slices the cross-section at height $z$ is the disk $x^2 + y^2 \leq z$, which depends on $z$, so integrating over $D$ at every height gives the cylinder again.
