Let $E$ be the cone $\{ (x, y, z) : \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \leq z \leq 1 \}$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\iiint_E \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}\, dx\, dy\, dz$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The solid is a cone with axis $z$, so we switch to **cylindrical coordinates**. Since $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2} = \rho$, the cone is described by $0 \leq \theta \leq 2\pi$, $0 \leq \rho \leq 1$, $\rho \leq z \leq 1$. With the volume element $\rho\, d\rho\, d\theta\, dz$ the integrand becomes $\rho \cdot \rho = \rho^2$. By **integration by vertical lines** the integral in $z$ equals $\displaystyle\int_{\rho}^{1} \rho^2\, dz = \rho^2 (1 - \rho)$, therefore
$$
\iiint_E \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}\, dx\, dy\, dz = \int_0^{2\pi} d\theta \cdot \int_0^1 \left( \rho^2 - \rho^3 \right) d\rho = 2\pi \left( \frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{4} \right) = \frac{\pi}{6} .
$$
