Let $E$ be the cone $\{ (x, y, z) : \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \leq z \leq 2 \}$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\iiint_E z\, dx\, dy\, dz$?

- $\dfrac{8\pi}{3}$
- $2\pi$
- $4\pi$
- $8\pi$

## Explanation

The solid lies between the planes $z = 0$ and $z = 2$, and its cross-section at height $z$ is the disk $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \leq z$, of radius $z$ and area $\pi z^2$. The function $z$ depends only on the height, so by **integration by slices** the double integral over each cross-section equals $z \cdot \pi z^2$. Therefore
$$
\iiint_E z\, dx\, dy\, dz = \int_0^2 \pi z^3 \, dz = \pi \cdot \frac{2^4}{4} = 4\pi .
$$
