The function $\sqrt{1 - x^2 - y^2}$ is integrated over the unit disk $D = \{ (x, y) : x^2 + y^2 \leq 1 \}$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\iint_D \sqrt{1 - x^2 - y^2}\, dx\, dy$?

- $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi^2}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

In **polar coordinates** the function equals $\sqrt{1 - \rho^2}$ and the disk becomes the rectangle $0 \leq \rho \leq 1$, $0 \leq \theta \leq 2\pi$, so
$$
\int_0^{2\pi} \left( \int_0^1 \sqrt{1 - \rho^2}\, \rho \, d\rho \right) d\theta = 2\pi \left[ -\frac{\left( 1 - \rho^2 \right)^{\frac{3}{2}}}{3} \right]_0^1 = \frac{2\pi}{3} .
$$
The result is the volume of the half-ball of radius $1$, because the graph of $\sqrt{1 - x^2 - y^2}$ is the upper hemisphere. The value $\dfrac{\pi^2}{2}$ is obtained without the factor $\rho$, and $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$ is the volume of the whole ball.
