Let $E = \left\{ (x, y, z) : x^2 + y^2 \leq z \leq 2 - \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \right\}$, a solid between a paraboloid and a cone. What is the volume of $E$?

- $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\pi$
- $2\pi$

## Explanation

With $\rho = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$ the two surfaces are $z = \rho^2$ and $z = 2 - \rho$, which meet for $\rho = 1$, at height $z = 1$; so $z$ ranges between $0$ and $2$. We integrate **by slices**. For $0 \leq z \leq 1$ the cross-section is the disk $\rho \leq \sqrt{z}$, because $\sqrt{z} \leq 1 \leq 2 - z$; for $1 \leq z \leq 2$ the cross-section is the disk $\rho \leq 2 - z$, because $2 - z \leq 1 \leq \sqrt{z}$. Therefore
$$
\operatorname{Vol}(E) = \int_0^1 \pi z \, dz + \int_1^2 \pi (2 - z)^2\, dz = \frac{\pi}{2} + \frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{5\pi}{6} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ is the volume of the part below the plane $z = 1$ only, and $2\pi$ uses the disk of radius $\sqrt{z}$ on the whole interval $[0, 2]$.
