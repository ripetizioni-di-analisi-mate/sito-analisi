What is the x-coordinate of the center of mass of the uniform triangle $x\geq0,\ y\geq0,\ x/2+y/3\leq1$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The center of mass of a triangle is the average of its vertices. Their x-coordinates are zero, $2$, and zero.
