Let $D = \{ (x, y) : x^2 + y^2 \leq 4, \ y \geq 0 \}$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\iint_D y\, dx\, dy$?

- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

The domain is the upper half-disk of radius $2$, which in **polar coordinates** becomes the rectangle $0 \leq \rho \leq 2$, $0 \leq \theta \leq \pi$. Since $y = \rho \sin\theta$ and the area element is $\rho\, d\rho\, d\theta$, the integrand becomes $\rho^2 \sin\theta$. The limits are constant and the function has separated variables, therefore
$$
\iint_D y\, dx\, dy = \int_0^{\pi} \sin\theta \, d\theta \cdot \int_0^2 \rho^2 \, d\rho = 2 \cdot \frac{8}{3} = \frac{16}{3} .
$$
The result is positive, since the function $y$ is positive in the interior of $D$.
