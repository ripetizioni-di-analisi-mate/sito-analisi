Let $D$ be the disk $x^2 + y^2 \leq 1$. Which of the following integrals is zero?

- $\displaystyle\iint_D x^2 y^2\, dx\, dy$
- $\displaystyle\iint_D \left( x^2 + y \right) dx\, dy$
- $\displaystyle\iint_D |x|\, y^2\, dx\, dy$
- $\displaystyle\iint_D x y^2\, dx\, dy$

## Explanation

The disk is symmetric with respect to the $y$-axis and the function $x y^2$ is odd in $x$, since $(-x)\, y^2 = -x y^2$. Hence, by the theorem on **odd functions on symmetric domains**, its integral is zero. The functions $x^2 y^2$ and $|x|\, y^2$ are positive at every point of $D$ not lying on the axes, therefore their integrals are positive. In the integral of $x^2 + y$ the term $y$ has zero integral by the symmetry with respect to the $x$-axis, while the term $x^2$ has a positive integral, equal to $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
