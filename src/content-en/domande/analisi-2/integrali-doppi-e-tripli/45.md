What is the mass of the cylinder $E = \{ (x, y, z) : x^2 + y^2 \leq 1, \ 0 \leq z \leq 3 \}$ with density $\delta(x, y, z) = z \left( x^2 + y^2 \right)$?

- $3\pi$
- $\dfrac{9\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9\pi}{8}$

## Explanation

The mass is the triple integral of the density over $E$. The solid is a cylinder with axis $z$, so we switch to **cylindrical coordinates**, with $0 \leq \rho \leq 1$, $0 \leq \theta \leq 2\pi$, $0 \leq z \leq 3$. Since $x^2 + y^2 = \rho^2$ and the volume element is $\rho \, d\rho\, d\theta\, dz$, the integrand becomes $z \rho^3$. The limits are constant and the function has separated variables, therefore
$$
m = \int_0^{2\pi} d\theta \cdot \int_0^1 \rho^3 \, d\rho \cdot \int_0^3 z \, dz = 2\pi \cdot \frac{1}{4} \cdot \frac{9}{2} = \frac{9\pi}{4} .
$$
