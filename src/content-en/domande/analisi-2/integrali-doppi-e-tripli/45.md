What is the mass of the triangular lamina $T = \{ (x, y) : x \geq 0, \ y \geq 0, \ x + y \leq 1 \}$ with density $\delta(x, y) = xy$?

- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{24}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

The mass is the integral of the density over the lamina. The triangle is normal with respect to the $x$-axis, with $0 \leq x \leq 1$ and $0 \leq y \leq 1 - x$, so
$$
m = \int_0^1 \left( \int_0^{1 - x} xy \, dy \right) dx = \int_0^1 \frac{x (1 - x)^2}{2}\, dx .
$$
Since $\displaystyle\int_0^1 x (1 - x)^2\, dx = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{12}$, the mass is $m = \dfrac{1}{24}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{4}$ is the mass of the square $[0, 1] \times [0, 1]$ with the same density, and $\dfrac{1}{12}$ forgets the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of the inner integral.
