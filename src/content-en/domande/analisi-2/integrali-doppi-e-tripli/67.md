A parallelogram $P$ has a vertex at the origin and its two sides from the origin are given by the vectors $(3, 1)$ and $(1, 2)$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\iint_P x\, dx\, dy$?

- $10$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $5$

## Explanation

The vertices of $P$ are $(0, 0)$, $(3, 1)$, $(4, 3)$ and $(1, 2)$, and $P$ is the image of the square $[0, 1] \times [0, 1]$ under the transformation $x = 3u + v$, $y = u + 2v$. The Jacobian is $\det \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix} = 5$, therefore by the **change of variables theorem**
$$
\iint_P x\, dx\, dy = \int_0^1 \!\! \int_0^1 (3u + v) \cdot 5 \, du\, dv = 5 \left( \frac{3}{2} + \frac{1}{2} \right) = 10 .
$$
The value $2$ is obtained without the factor $5$, $\dfrac{2}{5}$ by dividing by the Jacobian instead of multiplying, and $5$ is the area of $P$.
