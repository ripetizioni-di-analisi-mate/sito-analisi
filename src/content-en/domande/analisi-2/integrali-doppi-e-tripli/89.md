From the ball $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \leq 4$ the part with $x^2 + y^2 < 1$ is removed, that is, a cylindrical hole of radius $1$ is drilled along the $z$-axis. What is the volume of the remaining solid?

- $2\sqrt{3}\, \pi$
- $\dfrac{32\pi}{3}$
- $4\sqrt{3}\, \pi$
- $\dfrac{28\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

In **cylindrical coordinates** the solid is described by $1 \leq \rho \leq 2$, $0 \leq \theta \leq 2\pi$ and $-\sqrt{4 - \rho^2} \leq z \leq \sqrt{4 - \rho^2}$, so the vertical segment has length $2\sqrt{4 - \rho^2}$. The volume is
$$
\int_0^{2\pi} \left( \int_1^2 2\sqrt{4 - \rho^2}\, \rho \, d\rho \right) d\theta = 2\pi \left[ -\frac{2}{3} \left( 4 - \rho^2 \right)^{\frac{3}{2}} \right]_1^2 = 2\pi \cdot \frac{2}{3} \cdot 3\sqrt{3} = 4\sqrt{3}\, \pi .
$$
The value $2\sqrt{3}\, \pi$ counts only the part with $z \geq 0$, and $\dfrac{28\pi}{3}$ removes a ball of radius $1$ from the ball instead of the cylindrical hole.
