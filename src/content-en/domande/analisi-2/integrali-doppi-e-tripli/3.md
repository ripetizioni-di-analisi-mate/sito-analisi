## Explanation

The triangle with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(1, 0)$ and $(1, 1)$ lies between the $x$-axis and the line $y = x$ for $0 \leq x \leq 1$, so it is the **normal domain** with respect to the $x$-axis
$$
T = \left\{ (x, y) :
\begin{cases}
0 \leq x \leq 1 \\
0 \leq y \leq x
\end{cases}
\right\} .
$$
By the reduction formula the inner integral in $y$ equals $\displaystyle\int_0^x x \, dy = x \cdot x = x^2$, therefore
$$
\iint_T x \, dx\, dy = \int_0^1 x^2 \, dx = \frac{1}{3} .
$$
