Let $D = \{ (x, y) : 1 \leq y \leq 2, \ 0 \leq x \leq y \}$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\iint_D e^{\frac{x}{y}}\, dx\, dy$?

- $\dfrac{e - 1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3(e - 1)}{2}$
- $e - 1$
- $3(e - 1)$

## Explanation

The domain is normal with respect to the $y$-axis, so by the **reduction formula** we integrate first with respect to $x$ between $0$ and $y$. For fixed $y$, an antiderivative of $e^{\frac{x}{y}}$ with respect to $x$ is $y\, e^{\frac{x}{y}}$, therefore
$$
\int_0^y e^{\frac{x}{y}}\, dx = \Bigl[ y\, e^{\frac{x}{y}} \Bigr]_{x=0}^{x=y} = y\, (e - 1) .
$$
Integrating with respect to $y$ gives
$$
\int_1^2 y\, (e - 1)\, dy = (e - 1) \cdot \frac{4 - 1}{2} = \frac{3(e - 1)}{2} .
$$
