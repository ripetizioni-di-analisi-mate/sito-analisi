What is the value of $\displaystyle\iint_Q |x - y|\, dx\, dy$, where $Q = [0, 1] \times [0, 1]$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The diagonal $y = x$ divides $Q$ into the triangle $T_1$, where $y \leq x$, and the triangle $T_2$, where $y \geq x$. On $T_1$ we have $|x - y| = x - y$ and on $T_2$ we have $|x - y| = y - x$, so by **additivity with respect to the domain** the integral is the sum of the two integrals. Exchanging $x$ and $y$ maps $T_1$ onto $T_2$ and leaves the function unchanged, therefore the two contributions are equal and
$$
\iint_Q |x - y|\, dx\, dy = 2 \int_0^1 \left( \int_0^x (x - y)\, dy \right) dx = 2 \int_0^1 \frac{x^2}{2}\, dx = \frac{1}{3} .
$$
