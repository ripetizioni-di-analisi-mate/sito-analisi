What are the exact limits in the order $dx\,dy$ on the domain $0\leq x\leq1,\quad 4x\leq y\leq 4$?

- $0\leq y\leq 4,\ y/4\leq x\leq1$
- $0\leq y\leq1,\ 0\leq x\leq y/4$
- $0\leq y\leq 4,\ 0\leq x\leq1$
- $0\leq y\leq 4,\ 0\leq x\leq y/4$

## Explanation

The projection onto the $y$-axis is $[0,4]$. For fixed $y$ the constraint $4x\leq y$ gives the upper bound $y/4$.
