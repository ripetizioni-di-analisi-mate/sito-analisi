The solid torus $E = \left\{ (x, y, z) : \left( \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} - 2 \right)^2 + z^2 \leq 1 \right\}$ is obtained by rotating about the $z$-axis the disk of the $xz$-plane with center $(2, 0, 0)$ and radius $1$. What is the volume of $E$?

- $2\pi^2$
- $4\pi^2$
- $6\pi^2$
- $2\pi$

## Explanation

In **cylindrical coordinates** the condition does not contain $\theta$, so $E$ is described by $0 \leq \theta \leq 2\pi$ and by the disk $C = \{ (\rho, z) : (\rho - 2)^2 + z^2 \leq 1 \}$. With the volume element $\rho\, d\rho\, d\theta\, dz$ we get
$$
\operatorname{Vol}(E) = 2\pi \iint_C \rho\, d\rho\, dz = 2\pi \left( \iint_C (\rho - 2)\, d\rho\, dz + 2 \iint_C d\rho\, dz \right) .
$$
The disk $C$ is symmetric with respect to the line $\rho = 2$ and the function $\rho - 2$ is odd with respect to that symmetry, therefore its integral is zero. The area of $C$ equals $\pi$, so $\operatorname{Vol}(E) = 2\pi \cdot 2\pi = 4\pi^2$. The value $2\pi^2$ is obtained without the factor $\rho$ of the volume element, $6\pi^2$ replaces $\rho$ with the maximum distance $3$ from the axis, and $2\pi$ forgets the factor $2\pi$ of the integral in $\theta$.
