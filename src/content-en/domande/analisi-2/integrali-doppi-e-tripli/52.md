The region $D$ lies between the $x$-axis and an arch of the sine curve, that is, $0 \leq y \leq \sin x$ for $0 \leq x \leq \pi$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\iint_D y\, dx\, dy$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $1$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

The domain is normal with respect to the $x$-axis, so by the **reduction formula** we integrate first with respect to $y$ between $0$ and $\sin x$:
$$
\int_0^{\pi} \left( \int_0^{\sin x} y \, dy \right) dx = \int_0^{\pi} \frac{\sin^2 x}{2}\, dx .
$$
Since $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} \sin^2 x \, dx = \frac{\pi}{2}$, the integral equals $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. The value $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ forgets the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $2$ is the area of $D$, that is, the integral of $1$ instead of $y$.
