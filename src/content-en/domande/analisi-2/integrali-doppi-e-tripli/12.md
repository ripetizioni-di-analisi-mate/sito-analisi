## Explanation

The solid lies between the planes $z = 0$ and $z = 4$, and its cross-section at height $z$ is the disk $x^2 + y^2 \leq z$, of radius $\sqrt{z}$ and area $\pi z$. The function $z$ depends only on the height, so by **integration by slices** the double integral over each cross-section equals $z \cdot \pi z$. Therefore
$$
\iiint_E z \, dx\, dy\, dz = \int_0^4 \pi z^2 \, dz = \pi \cdot \frac{4^3}{3} = \frac{64\pi}{3} .
$$
