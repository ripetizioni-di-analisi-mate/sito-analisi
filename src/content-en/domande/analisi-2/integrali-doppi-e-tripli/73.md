What is the x-coordinate of the center of mass of the uniform triangle $x\geq0,\ y\geq0,\ x/3+y/5\leq1$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$

## Explanation

The center of mass of a triangle is the average of its vertices. Their x-coordinates are zero, $3$, and zero.
