What is the volume of the ellipsoid $E = \left\{ (x, y, z) : x^2 + \dfrac{y^2}{4} + z^2 \leq 1 \right\}$?

- $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{16\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8\pi}{3}$
- $4\pi$

## Explanation

We integrate **by slices** with respect to $z \in [-1, 1]$. At height $z$ the cross-section is the ellipse $x^2 + \dfrac{y^2}{4} \leq 1 - z^2$, with semi-axes $\sqrt{1 - z^2}$ and $2\sqrt{1 - z^2}$, whose area is $\pi \cdot \sqrt{1 - z^2} \cdot 2\sqrt{1 - z^2} = 2\pi \left( 1 - z^2 \right)$. The volume is the integral of the areas of the cross-sections:
$$
\operatorname{Vol}(E) = \int_{-1}^{1} 2\pi \left( 1 - z^2 \right) dz = 2\pi \left( 2 - \frac{2}{3} \right) = \frac{8\pi}{3} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$ is the volume of the unit ball, whose cross-section is the same with the semi-axis $2$ replaced by $1$.
