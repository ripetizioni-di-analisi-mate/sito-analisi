Let $I = \displaystyle\iint_Q \frac{1}{1 + x + y}\, dx\, dy$, with $Q = [0, 2] \times [0, 2]$. Which bounds result from multiplying the minimum and maximum of the integrand by the area of $Q$?

- $\dfrac{1}{5} \leq I \leq 1$
- $4 \leq I \leq 20$
- $0 \leq I \leq \dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5} \leq I \leq 4$

## Explanation

For $(x, y) \in Q$ we have $1 \leq 1 + x + y \leq 5$, so $\dfrac{1}{5} \leq \dfrac{1}{1 + x + y} \leq 1$. By **monotonicity** the integral $I$ lies between the integrals over $Q$ of the constants $\dfrac{1}{5}$ and $1$, that is, between $\dfrac{1}{5}$ and $1$ multiplied by the area of $Q$. The area of $Q$ is $4$, therefore $\dfrac{4}{5} \leq I \leq 4$. The exact value is $I = 5 \ln 5 - 6 \ln 3$, about $1.46$, so the other three estimates are false.
