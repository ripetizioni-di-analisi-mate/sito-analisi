Let $E$ be the half-ball $\{ (x, y, z) : x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \leq 1, \ z \geq 0 \}$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\iiint_E z\, dx\, dy\, dz$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{8}$

## Explanation

In **spherical coordinates** the half-ball becomes the box $0 \leq r \leq 1$, $0 \leq \varphi \leq \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, $0 \leq \theta \leq 2\pi$. Since $z = r \cos\varphi$ and the volume element is $r^2 \sin\varphi \, dr\, d\varphi\, d\theta$, the integrand becomes $r^3 \cos\varphi \sin\varphi$. The integral in $\varphi$ equals $\left[ \dfrac{\sin^2\varphi}{2} \right]_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, therefore
$$
\iiint_E z\, dx\, dy\, dz = 2\pi \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \int_0^1 r^3 \, dr = 2\pi \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{4} = \frac{\pi}{4} .
$$
The result is positive, since the function $z$ is positive in the interior of the half-ball; over the whole ball the integral would be zero by the symmetry with respect to the plane $z = 0$.
