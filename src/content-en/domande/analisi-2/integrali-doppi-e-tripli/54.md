On a domain $D$ of area $4$ the continuous functions $f$ and $g$ satisfy $\displaystyle\iint_D f\, dx\, dy = 3$ and $\displaystyle\iint_D g\, dx\, dy = -2$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\iint_D (2f - 3g + 1)\, dx\, dy$?

- $13$
- $4$
- $12$
- $16$

## Explanation

By **linearity** the integral splits into the combination of the integrals:
$$
\iint_D (2f - 3g + 1)\, dx\, dy = 2 \cdot 3 - 3 \cdot (-2) + \iint_D 1 \, dx\, dy .
$$
The integral of the constant $1$ is the area of $D$, that is $4$, so the result is $6 + 6 + 4 = 16$. The value $13$ counts the constant as $1$ instead of as the area, and $4$ comes from getting the sign of the term in $g$ wrong.
