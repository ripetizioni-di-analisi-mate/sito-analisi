The parabolas $x = y^2$ and $x = 2 - y^2$, whose axis is the $x$-axis, bound a region $D$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\iint_D x\, dx\, dy$?

- $\dfrac{16}{3}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$

## Explanation

The two parabolas meet where $y^2 = 2 - y^2$, that is, for $y = \pm 1$, and for $-1 \leq y \leq 1$ we have $y^2 \leq 2 - y^2$. The domain is therefore normal with respect to the $y$-axis, with $-1 \leq y \leq 1$ and $y^2 \leq x \leq 2 - y^2$, and the inner integral is
$$
\int_{y^2}^{2 - y^2} x\, dx = \frac{\left( 2 - y^2 \right)^2 - y^4}{2} = 2 - 2y^2 .
$$
Therefore $\displaystyle\iint_D x\, dx\, dy = \int_{-1}^{1} \left( 2 - 2y^2 \right) dy = 4 - \frac{4}{3} = \frac{8}{3}$. The domain is symmetric with respect to the $x$-axis, but the function $x$ is even in $y$, so the integral is not zero.
