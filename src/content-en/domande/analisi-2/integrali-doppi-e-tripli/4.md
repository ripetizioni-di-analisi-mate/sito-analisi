- $\dfrac{9}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{7}{20}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$

## Explanation

The domain is normal with respect to the $x$-axis, with $0 \leq x \leq 1$ and $0 \leq y \leq x^2$, so by the **reduction formula** we integrate first with respect to $y$ between $0$ and $x^2$. With $x$ fixed we obtain
$$
\int_0^{x^2} (x + y)\, dy = \left[ x y + \frac{y^2}{2} \right]_0^{x^2} = x^3 + \frac{x^4}{2} .
$$
Integrating with respect to $x$ between $0$ and $1$ gives
$$
\int_0^1 \left( x^3 + \frac{x^4}{2} \right) dx = \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{10} = \frac{7}{20} .
$$
