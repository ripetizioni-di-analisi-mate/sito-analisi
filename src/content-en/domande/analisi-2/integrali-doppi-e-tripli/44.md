Which point is the centroid of the homogeneous lamina $D = \{ (x, y) : -2 \leq x \leq 2, \ 0 \leq y \leq 4 - x^2 \}$?

- $\left( 0,\ \dfrac{8}{5} \right)$
- $\left( 0,\ 2 \right)$
- $\left( 0,\ \dfrac{4}{3} \right)$
- $\left( 0,\ \dfrac{16}{5} \right)$

## Explanation

The domain is symmetric with respect to the $y$-axis and the lamina is homogeneous, so the centroid lies on the $y$-axis and $\bar{x} = 0$. The area of $D$ equals $\displaystyle\int_{-2}^{2} \left( 4 - x^2 \right) dx = 16 - \frac{16}{3} = \frac{32}{3}$. By the reduction formula we have
$$
\iint_D y\, dx\, dy = \int_{-2}^{2} \frac{(4 - x^2)^2}{2}\, dx = \frac{1}{2} \left( 64 - \frac{128}{3} + \frac{64}{5} \right) = \frac{256}{15} .
$$
Therefore $\bar{y} = \dfrac{256}{15} \cdot \dfrac{3}{32} = \dfrac{8}{5}$ and the centroid is $\left( 0, \dfrac{8}{5} \right)$.
