What is the value of the triple integral of $x^2$ over the solid cylinder $E$ with axis $z$, radius $1$ and height $2$, that is, over $x^2 + y^2 \leq 1$, $0 \leq z \leq 2$?

- $\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

In **cylindrical coordinates** the cylinder becomes $0 \leq \rho \leq 1$, $0 \leq \theta \leq 2\pi$, $0 \leq z \leq 2$, the function equals $\rho^2 \cos^2\theta$ and the volume element is $\rho\, d\rho\, d\theta\, dz$. The limits are constant and the integrand is a product of factors in the single variables, so
$$
\int_0^{2\pi} \cos^2\theta \, d\theta \cdot \int_0^1 \rho^3 \, d\rho \cdot \int_0^2 dz = \pi \cdot \frac{1}{4} \cdot 2 = \frac{\pi}{2} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ forgets the height of the cylinder, and $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ is obtained without the factor $\rho$ of the Jacobian.
