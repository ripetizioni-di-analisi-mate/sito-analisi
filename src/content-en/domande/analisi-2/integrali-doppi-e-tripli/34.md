Let $D = \{ (x, y) : |x| + |y| \leq 1 \}$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\iint_D \left( x^5 + y^3 + 4 \right) dx\, dy$?

- $4$
- $0$
- $8$
- $16$

## Explanation

The domain is the square with vertices $(\pm 1, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 1)$, symmetric with respect to both axes. By linearity the integral is the sum of the integrals of $x^5$, of $y^3$ and of $4$. The function $x^5$ is odd in $x$ and the function $y^3$ is odd in $y$, so by the theorem on **odd functions on symmetric domains** their integrals are zero. What remains is the integral of the constant $4$, equal to $4$ times the area of $D$. The square has diagonals of length $2$, therefore its area is $\dfrac{2 \cdot 2}{2} = 2$ and the integral equals $4 \cdot 2 = 8$.
