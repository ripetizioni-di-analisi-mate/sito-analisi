What is the natural domain of $f(x, y) = \dfrac{\ln(x^2 + y^2 - 1)}{\sqrt{9 - x^2 - y^2}}$?

- The points with $1 \leq x^2 + y^2 < 9$
- The points with $1 < x^2 + y^2 < 9$
- The points with $1 < x^2 + y^2 \leq 9$
- The points with $1 < x^2 + y^2 < 3$

## Explanation

The argument of the logarithm must be positive and the argument of the square root, which is in the denominator, must be strictly positive, so the domain is given by the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 + y^2 - 1 > 0 \\[4pt]
9 - x^2 - y^2 > 0
\end{cases}
$$
The first condition is equivalent to $x^2 + y^2 > 1$ and the second to $x^2 + y^2 < 9$. Therefore the domain is the **open annulus** $1 < x^2 + y^2 < 9$, lying between the circles centered at the origin with radii $1$ and $3$, both excluded.
