- The limit equals $3$
- The limit equals $-1$
- The limit equals $1$
- The limit does not exist

## Explanation

On the $x$-axis, that is, for $y = 0$ and $x \neq 0$, we have $f(x, 0) = \dfrac{3x^2}{x^2} = 3$, so the restriction to the $x$-axis tends to $3$. On the $y$-axis, that is, for $x = 0$ and $y \neq 0$, we have $f(0, y) = \dfrac{-y^2}{y^2} = -1$, so the restriction to the $y$-axis tends to $-1$. Since two restrictions produce different limits, by the **restriction theorem** the limit **does not exist**.
