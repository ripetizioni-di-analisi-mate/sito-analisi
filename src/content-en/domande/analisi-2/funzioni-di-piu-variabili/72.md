What is the maximum of $3x+5y$ on the disk $x^2+y^2\leq 4$?

- $- 2 \sqrt{34}$
- $16$
- $2 \sqrt{34}$
- $\sqrt{34}$

## Explanation

By Cauchy–Schwarz the dot product is at most radius $2$ times the norm of $\left( 3, \  5\right)$. The boundary point in that direction attains equality.
