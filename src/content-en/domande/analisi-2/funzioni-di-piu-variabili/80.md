What is the exact range of $\dfrac{4 x^{2} + 2 y^{2}}{x^{2} + y^{2}}$?

- $\left[0, 6\right]$
- $\left[2, 4\right]$
- $\left(2, 4\right)$
- $\left\{2, 4\right\}$

## Explanation

The ratio is a weighted average of $4$ and $2$. Every weight between zero and one is obtained by varying the direction of the point, including the axes.
