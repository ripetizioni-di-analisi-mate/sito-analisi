On which curve of the plane does $x^2 - y = -1$ hold, that is, what is the level curve $k = -1$ of $f(x, y) = x^2 - y$?

- The parabola $y = x^2 - 1$
- The parabola $y = x^2 + 1$
- The parabola $y = 1 - x^2$
- The circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$

## Explanation

The level curve $-1$ is the set of points with $x^2 - y = -1$. Solving for $y$ gives $y = x^2 + 1$. Therefore the level curve is the **parabola** with vertex $(0, 1)$ opening upward.
