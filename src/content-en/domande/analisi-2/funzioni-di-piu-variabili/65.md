What is the exact range of $\dfrac{3 x^{2} + 5 y^{2}}{x^{2} + y^{2}}$?

- $\left[0, 8\right]$
- $\left\{3, 5\right\}$
- $\left[3, 5\right]$
- $\left(3, 5\right)$

## Explanation

The ratio is a weighted average of $3$ and $5$. Varying the direction, including the axes, gives every weight from zero to one.
