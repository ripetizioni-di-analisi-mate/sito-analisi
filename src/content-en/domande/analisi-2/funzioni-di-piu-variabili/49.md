Let $A = \{ (x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : 1 < x^2 + y^2 \leq 4 \}$. What is the boundary $\partial A$?

- The union of the circles $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ and $x^2 + y^2 = 4$
- Only the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 4$
- Only the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$
- The set $A$ itself

## Explanation

A point of the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ has, in each of its neighborhoods, points with $x^2 + y^2 > 1$, which lie in $A$, and points with $x^2 + y^2 < 1$, which do not lie in $A$, so it is a boundary point. The same holds for a point of the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 4$. The points with $1 < x^2 + y^2 < 4$ are interior and those with $x^2 + y^2 < 1$ or $x^2 + y^2 > 4$ are exterior. Therefore $\partial A$ is the **union of the two circles**, even though only the outer one belongs to $A$.
