Let $r^2=x^2+y^2$. For which value of $a$ is $\displaystyle\lim_{(x,y)\to(0,0)}\frac{e^{r^2}-1-a r^2}{r^4}$ finite?

- $1$
- $0$
- $\dfrac12$
- $2$

## Explanation

We have $e^{r^2}=1+r^2+\dfrac{r^4}{2}+o(r^4)$. After division a term $\dfrac{1-a}{r^2}$ remains and its coefficient must vanish. For $a=1$ the limit is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
