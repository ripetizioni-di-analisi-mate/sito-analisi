For the function $f(x, y) = \dfrac{y}{x}$, what is the level curve $k = 2$?

- The line $y = 2x$
- The line $y = -2x$ minus the origin
- The line $y = \dfrac{x}{2}$ minus the origin
- The line $y = 2x$ minus the origin

## Explanation

The domain of $f$ is the set of points with $x \neq 0$. The level curve $k = 2$ is the set of points of the domain that satisfy $\dfrac{y}{x} = 2$, and for $x \neq 0$ this equation is equivalent to $y = 2x$. The line $y = 2x$ passes through the origin, which has $x$-coordinate zero and therefore does not belong to the domain. Hence the level curve is the line $y = 2x$ **minus the origin**.
