Which subset of the plane is the level curve $k = 0$ of the function $f(x, y) = x^2 - y^2$?

- Only the line $y = x$
- The hyperbola $x^2 - y^2 = 1$
- Only the point $(0, 0)$
- The union of the lines $y = x$ and $y = -x$

## Explanation

The level curve $0$ is the set of points with $x^2 - y^2 = 0$. Since $x^2 - y^2 = (x - y)(x + y)$, the product vanishes if and only if $y = x$ or $y = -x$. Therefore the level curve $0$ is the **union of the two bisectors**; for $k \neq 0$ the level curves are hyperbolas instead.
