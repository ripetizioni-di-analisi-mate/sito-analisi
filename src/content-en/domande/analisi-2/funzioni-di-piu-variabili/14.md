## Explanation

The function $f$ is a polynomial, so it is continuous on $\mathbb{R}^2$ and in particular on $K$. The set $K$ is defined by the non-strict inequality $x^2 + y^2 \leq 1$ between continuous functions, so it is closed. Moreover $K$ is contained in the disk of radius $1$, therefore it is bounded, and consequently it is compact. Both hypotheses of the **Weierstrass theorem in several variables** are satisfied, so $f$ has an **absolute maximum and minimum** on $K$.
