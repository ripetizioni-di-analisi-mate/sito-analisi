The graph of $f(x, y) = 3x - y + 2$ is a plane. What is its level curve $k = 5$?

- The line $y = 3x + 3$
- The line $y = -3x + 3$
- The line $y = 3x - 3$
- The line $y = 3x - 7$

## Explanation

The level curve $5$ is the set of points with $3x - y + 2 = 5$. Solving for $y$ gives $y = 3x + 2 - 5$, so $y = 3x - 3$. Therefore the level curve is the **line** with slope $3$ that meets the $y$-axis at $(0, -3)$.
