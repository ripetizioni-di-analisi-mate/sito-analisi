What is the value of $\displaystyle\lim_{(x, y) \to (1, 2)} \frac{x^2 + y}{xy + 1}$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

The function is a quotient of polynomials, and at the point $(1, 2)$ the denominator equals $1 \cdot 2 + 1 = 3 \neq 0$. Hence the function is **continuous** at $(1, 2)$ and the limit is computed by substitution. The numerator equals $1 + 2 = 3$, therefore the limit equals $\dfrac{3}{3} = 1$.
