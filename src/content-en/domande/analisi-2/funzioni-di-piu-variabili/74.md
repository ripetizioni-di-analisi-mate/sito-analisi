Which condition gives the exact domain of $\dfrac{\sqrt{25-x^2-y^2}}{x-2}$?

- $x^2+y^2\leq 25$
- $x^2+y^2<25,\ x\ne 2$
- $x^2+y^2\leq 25,\ x\ne 2$
- $x^2+y^2\geq 25,\ x\ne 2$

## Explanation

The square root permits zero under the radical. The denominator excludes points with $x=2$.
