Which statement about the natural domain of $f(x, y) = \sqrt{x^2 - 4}$ is true?

- It is closed and not connected
- It is open and not connected
- It is closed and connected
- It is compact

## Explanation

The square root requires $x^2 - 4 \geq 0$, that is, $x \leq -2$ or $x \geq 2$, so the domain is the union of the two half-planes $x \leq -2$ and $x \geq 2$. The domain is defined by a non-strict inequality between continuous functions, therefore it is **closed**. It contains the points $(2, n)$ for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, so it is not bounded and not compact. The x-coordinate of a continuous curve joining $(-2, 0)$ and $(2, 0)$ varies continuously from $-2$ to $2$, so the curve passes through a point with $x = 0$, which does not belong to the domain. Therefore the domain is **not connected**.
