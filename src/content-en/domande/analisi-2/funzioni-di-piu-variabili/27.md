For the function of three variables $f(x, y, z) = x^2 + y^2$, what is the level surface $k = 4$?

- The circle centered at the origin with radius $2$ in the $xy$-plane
- The cylinder with axis $z$ and radius $2$
- The sphere centered at the origin with radius $2$
- The cylinder with axis $z$ and radius $4$

## Explanation

The level surface $k = 4$ is the set of points $(x, y, z)$ in space that satisfy $x^2 + y^2 = 4$. The equation does not contain $z$, so if the point $(x_0, y_0, z_0)$ satisfies it, then all the points $(x_0, y_0, z)$ with arbitrary $z$ satisfy it as well. In the plane $z = 0$ the equation describes the circle centered at the origin with radius $\sqrt{4} = 2$. Therefore the level surface is the **cylinder** with axis $z$ and radius $2$, formed by the vertical lines through the points of that circle.
