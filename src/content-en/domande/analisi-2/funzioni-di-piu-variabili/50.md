What is the exact range of $\dfrac{2 x^{2} + 3 y^{2}}{x^{2} + y^{2}}$?

- $\left(2, 3\right)$
- $\left\{2, 3\right\}$
- $\left[0, 5\right]$
- $\left[2, 3\right]$

## Explanation

The ratio is a weighted average of $2$ and $3$. Every weight between zero and one is obtained by varying the direction of the point, including the axes.
