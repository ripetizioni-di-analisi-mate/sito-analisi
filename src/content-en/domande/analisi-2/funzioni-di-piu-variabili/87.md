What is the maximum of $4x+2y$ on the disk $x^2+y^2\leq 49$?

- $2 \sqrt{5}$
- $14 \sqrt{5}$
- $- 14 \sqrt{5}$
- $42$

## Explanation

By Cauchy–Schwarz the dot product is at most the radius $7$ times the norm of $\left( 4, \  2\right)$. The boundary point in that direction attains equality.
