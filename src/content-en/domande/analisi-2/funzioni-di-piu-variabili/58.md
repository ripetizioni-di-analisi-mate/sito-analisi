Let $f(x, y) = \sqrt{(x^2 + y^2 - 1)(4 - x^2 - y^2)}$. On which set of points is $f$ defined?

- The open annulus $1 < x^2 + y^2 < 4$
- The closed annulus $1 \leq x^2 + y^2 \leq 4$
- The closed disk $x^2 + y^2 \leq 4$
- The set of points with $x^2 + y^2 \geq 1$

## Explanation

The argument of the square root must be greater than or equal to zero. Setting $t = x^2 + y^2$, we study the sign of the product $(t - 1)(4 - t)$. The first factor is positive for $t > 1$ and the second for $t < 4$, so the product is positive for $1 < t < 4$, negative for $t < 1$ and for $t > 4$, zero for $t = 1$ and $t = 4$. Therefore the domain is the **closed annulus** $1 \leq x^2 + y^2 \leq 4$, with both circles included.
