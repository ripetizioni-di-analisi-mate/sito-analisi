For the function $f(x, y) = e^{\,xy}$, what is the level curve $k = 1$?

- The hyperbola $xy = 1$
- The union of the two coordinate axes
- The hyperbola $xy = e$
- The empty set

## Explanation

The level curve $k = 1$ is the set of points at which $e^{\,xy} = 1$. Since the exponential is injective and $e^{0} = 1$, the equation is equivalent to $xy = \ln 1 = 0$. The product $xy$ is zero if and only if $x = 0$ or $y = 0$, so the points that satisfy the equation are those of the $y$-axis and those of the $x$-axis. Therefore the level curve $k = 1$ is the **union of the two coordinate axes**.
