What is the absolute minimum of $f(x, y) = \ln(1 + x^2 + y^2)$ on the closed annulus $K = \{ (x, y) : 1 \leq x^2 + y^2 \leq 4 \}$?

- $\ln 2$
- $0$
- $\ln 5$
- $f$ has no minimum on $K$

## Explanation

The function is continuous and $K$ is closed and bounded, hence compact; therefore, by the **extreme value theorem**, the minimum exists. The function depends only on $t = x^2 + y^2$, which on $K$ ranges over $[1, 4]$, and $\ln(1 + t)$ is increasing. Hence the minimum is attained for $t = 1$, that is on the inner circle, and it equals $\ln 2$. The value $0$ would be attained only at the origin, which does not belong to $K$.
