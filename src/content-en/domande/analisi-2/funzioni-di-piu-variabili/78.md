Away from the coordinate axes the ratio $\dfrac{\sin x \, \sin y}{xy}$ is defined. What is its limit at the origin, if it exists?

- $0$
- The limit does not exist
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

The function is the product of two factors, each depending on a single variable:
$$
\frac{\sin x \, \sin y}{xy} = \frac{\sin x}{x} \cdot \frac{\sin y}{y} .
$$
By the **standard limit of the sine**, $\dfrac{\sin x}{x} \to 1$ as $x \to 0$ and $\dfrac{\sin y}{y} \to 1$ as $y \to 0$, and when $(x, y) \to (0, 0)$ both variables tend to $0$. By the theorem on the limit of a product, the limit equals $1 \cdot 1 = 1$.
