What is the distance of the point $P = (3, 4)$ from the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$, that is, the minimum distance between $P$ and the points of the circle?

- $5$
- $24$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The distance of $P$ from the origin is $\lVert P \rVert = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$. The point of the circle closest to $P$ lies on the ray starting at the origin and passing through $P$, at distance $1$ from the origin. Hence the minimum distance is the difference between the distance of $P$ from the center and the radius, that is $5 - 1 = 4$.
