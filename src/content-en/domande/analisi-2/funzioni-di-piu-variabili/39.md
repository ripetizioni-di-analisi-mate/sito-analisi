Let $a \in \mathbb{R}$ and let
$$
f(x, y) =
\begin{cases}
x^2 + y^2 & x^2 + y^2 \leq 1 \\[4pt]
a & x^2 + y^2 > 1
\end{cases}
$$
For which value of $a$ is the function $f$ continuous on $\mathbb{R}^2$?

- $a = 0$
- For no value of $a$
- $a = 1$
- $a = 2$

## Explanation

At the points with $x^2 + y^2 < 1$ the function coincides with a polynomial on an open set, and at the points with $x^2 + y^2 > 1$ it coincides with a constant, so it is continuous in both regions. It remains to check continuity on the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$, where $f$ equals $1$. Approaching a point of the circle from inside, $f$ tends to $1$; from outside it is constantly equal to $a$. Therefore the limit exists and coincides with the value of the function only if $a = 1$, and for $a = 1$ the function is **continuous** on all of $\mathbb{R}^2$.
