What is the distance between the points $P = (1, -2, 3)$ and $Q = (3, 1, -3)$ of $\mathbb{R}^3$?

- $7$
- $\sqrt{41}$
- $11$
- $49$

## Explanation

The distance between two points is the norm of their difference, so $d(P, Q) = \lVert Q - P \rVert$. The components of $Q - P$ are $3 - 1 = 2$, $1 - (-2) = 3$ and $-3 - 3 = -6$. Therefore
$$
d(P, Q) = \sqrt{2^2 + 3^2 + (-6)^2} = \sqrt{4 + 9 + 36} = \sqrt{49} = 7 .
$$
