What is the natural domain of $f(x, y) = \dfrac{\sqrt{x^2 - y}}{x - 1}$?

- The points with $y \leq x^2$ and $x \neq 1$
- The points with $y < x^2$ and $x \neq 1$
- The points with $y \geq x^2$ and $x \neq 1$
- The points with $y \leq x^2$ and $y \neq 1$

## Explanation

The argument of the square root must be greater than or equal to zero and the denominator must be nonzero, so the domain is given by the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 - y \geq 0 \\[4pt]
x - 1 \neq 0
\end{cases}
$$
The first condition is equivalent to $y \leq x^2$, therefore it describes the region below the parabola $y = x^2$, parabola included. The second condition excludes the points of the vertical line $x = 1$. The domain is therefore the set of points with $y \leq x^2$ and $x \neq 1$.
