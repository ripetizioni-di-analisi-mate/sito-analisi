For the function $f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2 + 2y$, what is the level curve $k = 3$?

- The circle centered at $(0, 1)$ with radius $2$
- The circle centered at $(0, -1)$ with radius $\sqrt{3}$
- The circle centered at $(0, -1)$ with radius $2$
- The circle centered at $(0, -1)$ with radius $4$

## Explanation

The level curve $k = 3$ is the set of points that satisfy $x^2 + y^2 + 2y = 3$. Since $y^2 + 2y = (y + 1)^2 - 1$, completing the square the equation becomes
$$
x^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 4 .
$$
This is the equation of the circle centered at $(0, -1)$ with radius $\sqrt{4} = 2$. Therefore the level curve $k = 3$ is the **circle centered at $(0, -1)$ with radius $2$**.
