Consider the quarter-plane $A = \{ (x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : x > 0, \ y \geq 0 \}$ and the point $P = (2, 0)$. Which statement is true?

- $P$ is an interior point of $A$
- $P$ is a boundary point of $A$ and does not belong to $A$
- $P$ is a boundary point of $A$ and belongs to $A$
- $P$ is an exterior point of $A$

## Explanation

The point $(2, 0)$ has $x = 2 > 0$ and $y = 0 \geq 0$, so it belongs to $A$. Every neighborhood of $(2, 0)$ contains points $(2, \varepsilon)$ with $\varepsilon > 0$, which lie in $A$, and points $(2, -\varepsilon)$, which do not lie in $A$ because their ordinate is negative. Therefore $P$ is neither interior nor exterior, so it is a **boundary point**, and it **belongs** to $A$.
