Which function has these level curves?

- $f(x, y) = x^2 - y^2$
- $f(x, y) = \dfrac{y}{x}$
- $f(x, y) = xy$
- $f(x, y) = x + y$

## Explanation

The curves in the figure are hyperbolas whose asymptotes are the coordinate axes: those with $k > 0$ lie in the first and third quadrants, the one with $k = -1$ in the second and fourth. For $f(x, y) = xy$ the level curve $k \neq 0$ is the hyperbola $y = \dfrac{k}{x}$, which lies in the first and third quadrants if $k > 0$ and in the second and fourth if $k < 0$, so it coincides with the one drawn. The level curves of $x^2 - y^2$ are hyperbolas whose asymptotes are the bisectors, those of $\dfrac{y}{x}$ are lines through the origin and those of $x + y$ are parallel lines.
