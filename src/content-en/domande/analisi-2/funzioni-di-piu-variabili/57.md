What is the maximum of $2x+3y$ on the disk $x^2+y^2\leq 25$?

- $5 \sqrt{13}$
- $- 5 \sqrt{13}$
- $25$
- $\sqrt{13}$

## Explanation

By Cauchy–Schwarz the dot product is at most the radius $5$ times the norm of $\left( 2, \  3\right)$. The boundary point in that direction attains equality.
