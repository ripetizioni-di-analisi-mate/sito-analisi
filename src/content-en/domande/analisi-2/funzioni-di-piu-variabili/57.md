What set is the natural domain of $f(x, y) = \dfrac{1}{\ln(x^2 + y^2)}$?

- The plane without the origin
- The points with $x^2 + y^2 > 1$
- The plane without the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$
- The plane without the origin and the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$

## Explanation

The conditions to be satisfied together are
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 + y^2 > 0 \\[4pt]
\ln(x^2 + y^2) \neq 0
\end{cases}
$$
The first one excludes only the origin. Since $\ln t = 0$ only for $t = 1$, the second one excludes the points with $x^2 + y^2 = 1$. Hence the domain is the **plane without the origin and the unit circle**.
