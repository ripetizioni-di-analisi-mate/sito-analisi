At which point does this function attain its absolute maximum $e^{- \left(x - 3\right)^{2} - 5 \left(y + 1\right)^{2}}$?

- $\left( -3, \  -1\right)$
- $\left( 0, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  -1\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  1\right)$

## Explanation

The exponential is increasing. Its exponent is at most zero and equals zero when both squares vanish.
