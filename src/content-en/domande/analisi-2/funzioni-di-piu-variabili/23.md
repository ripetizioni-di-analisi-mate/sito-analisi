What is the natural domain of $f(x, y) = \sqrt{y} + \ln(x - y)$?

- The points with $0 < y < x$
- The points with $0 \leq y \leq x$
- The points with $y < x$
- The points with $0 \leq y < x$

## Explanation

The square root requires $y \geq 0$ and the logarithm requires $x - y > 0$, so the domain is given by the system
$$
\begin{cases}
y \geq 0 \\[4pt]
y < x
\end{cases}
$$
Therefore the domain is the set of points with $0 \leq y < x$, that is, the region between the positive $x$-semi-axis and the bisector $y = x$ of the first quadrant. The points of the semi-axis with $x > 0$ belong to the domain, because at them $y = 0$ and $x - y = x > 0$. The points of the bisector are instead excluded, because at them $x - y = 0$.
