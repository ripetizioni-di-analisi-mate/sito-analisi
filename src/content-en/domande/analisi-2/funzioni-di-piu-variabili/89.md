What is the absolute maximum of $f(x, y) = \dfrac{x}{y}$ on the square $K = [1, 2] \times [1, 2]$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

On $K$ we have $y \geq 1$, so the denominator does not vanish and $f$ is continuous. Since $K$ is compact, by the **extreme value theorem** the maximum exists. For $(x, y) \in K$ we have $x \leq 2$ and $y \geq 1$, so $\dfrac{x}{y} \leq \dfrac{2}{1} = 2$. The value $2$ is attained at the point $(2, 1)$, which belongs to $K$, therefore the maximum equals $2$.
