## Explanation

Since $(x^2 + y^2)^2 = x^4 + 2x^2 y^2 + y^4$ and $2x^2 y^2 \geq 0$, the inequality $x^4 + y^4 \leq (x^2 + y^2)^2$ holds. Dividing by $x^2 + y^2 > 0$ we obtain
$$
0 \leq \frac{x^4 + y^4}{x^2 + y^2} \leq x^2 + y^2 .
$$
The function $x^2 + y^2$ tends to $0$ as $(x, y) \to (0, 0)$, so by the **squeeze theorem in two variables** the limit equals $0$.
