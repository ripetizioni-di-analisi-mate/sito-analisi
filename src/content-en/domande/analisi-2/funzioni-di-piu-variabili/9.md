For $r^2=x^2+y^2>0$ find the smallest constant $C$ such that $\dfrac{x^4+y^4}{r^2}\le Cr^2$ holds at every point. What is $C$?

- $\dfrac12$
- $1$
- $\dfrac14$
- $2$

## Explanation

Since $x^4+y^4\le(x^2+y^2)^2$, the value $1$ works. On the axis $y=0$ equality is attained at every nonzero point, so no smaller constant works.
