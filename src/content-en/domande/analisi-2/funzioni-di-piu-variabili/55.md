For which points $(x, y)$ does the expression $\arccos\!\left( x^2 + y^2 - 1 \right)$ make sense?

- The annulus $1 \leq x^2 + y^2 \leq 2$
- The closed disk centered at the origin with radius $2$
- The closed disk centered at the origin with radius $\sqrt{2}$
- The closed disk centered at the origin with radius $\sqrt{2}$, without the origin

## Explanation

The argument of $\arccos$ must lie between $-1$ and $1$, so
$$
-1 \leq x^2 + y^2 - 1 \leq 1 .
$$
Adding $1$ to each side gives $0 \leq x^2 + y^2 \leq 2$. The inequality $x^2 + y^2 \geq 0$ holds at every point, the origin included, so only $x^2 + y^2 \leq 2$ remains. The domain is the **closed disk** centered at the origin with radius $\sqrt{2}$.
