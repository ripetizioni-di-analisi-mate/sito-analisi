What is the natural domain of $f(x, y) = \arcsin(x + y)$?

- The strip $-1 - x < y < 1 - x$, lines excluded
- The half-plane $y \leq 1 - x$
- The strip $-1 - x \leq y \leq 1 - x$, lines included
- The strip $x - 1 \leq y \leq x + 1$, lines included

## Explanation

The arcsine is defined when its argument lies between $-1$ and $1$, endpoints included, so the domain is given by the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y \geq -1 \\[4pt]
x + y \leq 1
\end{cases}
$$
The first condition is equivalent to $y \geq -1 - x$ and the second to $y \leq 1 - x$. Therefore the domain is the **strip** between the parallel lines $y = -1 - x$ and $y = 1 - x$, lines included. The inequalities are non-strict, so the strip is a closed set.
