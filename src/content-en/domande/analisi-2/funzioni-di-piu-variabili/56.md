The logarithm $\ln\!\left( y - |x| \right)$ is defined on a region of the plane. Which one?

- The points with $y > |x|$
- The points with $y \geq |x|$
- The points with $y > x$
- The points with $|y| > |x|$

## Explanation

The argument of the logarithm must be positive, so $y - |x| > 0$, that is $y > |x|$. The graph of $y = |x|$ consists of the rays $y = x$ with $x \geq 0$ and $y = -x$ with $x \leq 0$. Therefore the domain is the region **above** this graph, boundary excluded, that is, the open angle between the two rays, contained in the half-plane $y > 0$.
