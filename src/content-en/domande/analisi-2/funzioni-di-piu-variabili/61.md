Which surface of $\mathbb{R}^3$ is formed by the points where the function of three variables $f(x, y, z) = z - x^2 - y^2$ equals $1$?

- The paraboloid $z = 1 + x^2 + y^2$
- The paraboloid $z = x^2 + y^2 - 1$
- The sphere centered at the origin with radius $1$
- The cylinder with axis $z$ and radius $1$

## Explanation

The level surface $1$ is the set of points with $z - x^2 - y^2 = 1$. Solving for $z$ gives $z = 1 + x^2 + y^2$. Therefore the level surface is the **paraboloid** with vertex $(0, 0, 1)$ and axis $z$, opening upward, obtained by shifting the paraboloid $z = x^2 + y^2$ up by $1$.
