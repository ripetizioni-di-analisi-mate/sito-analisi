- The half-plane $y > 1 - x$, line $y = 1 - x$ excluded
- The half-plane $y \geq 1 - x$, line $y = 1 - x$ included
- The half-plane $y < 1 - x$, line $y = 1 - x$ excluded
- The half-plane $y > x - 1$, line $y = x - 1$ excluded

## Explanation

The logarithm is defined when its argument is strictly positive, so the domain is given by the condition $x + y - 1 > 0$. Moving $x$ and $1$ to the right-hand side we obtain $y > 1 - x$. Therefore the domain is the half-plane lying above the line $y = 1 - x$, and the points of the line are excluded because at them the argument equals $0$. The inequality is a strict inequality between continuous functions, so the domain is an **open** set.
