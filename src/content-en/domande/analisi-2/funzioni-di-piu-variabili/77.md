The function $\dfrac{\ln(1 + 3xy)}{xy}$ is defined at the points with $xy \neq 0$ and $1 + 3xy > 0$. What is its limit at the origin?

- $1$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Setting $t = xy$, we have $t \to 0$ as $(x, y) \to (0, 0)$, with $t \neq 0$ in the domain. The expression becomes
$$
\frac{\ln(1 + 3t)}{t} = 3 \cdot \frac{\ln(1 + 3t)}{3t} .
$$
By the **standard limit of the logarithm** $\dfrac{\ln(1 + s)}{s} \to 1$ with $s = 3t \to 0$, therefore the limit equals $3$.
