- The region $y > x^2$ minus the points with $x^2 + y^2 = 4$
- The region $y \geq x^2$ minus the points with $x^2 + y^2 = 2$
- The region $y \geq x^2$ intersected with the disk $x^2 + y^2 < 4$
- The region $y \geq x^2$ minus the points with $x^2 + y^2 = 4$

## Explanation

The argument of the square root must be greater than or equal to zero and the denominator must be nonzero, so the domain is given by the system
$$
\begin{cases}
y - x^2 \geq 0 \\[4pt]
x^2 + y^2 - 4 \neq 0
\end{cases}
$$
The first condition is equivalent to $y \geq x^2$, therefore it describes the region above the parabola $y = x^2$, parabola included. The second excludes only the points of the circle centered at the origin with radius $2$, while the points inside and outside the disk remain in the domain. The domain is therefore the region $y \geq x^2$ minus the points with $x^2 + y^2 = 4$.
