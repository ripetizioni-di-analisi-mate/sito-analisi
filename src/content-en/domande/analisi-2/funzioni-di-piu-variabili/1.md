- The open disk centered at the origin with radius $3$
- The closed disk centered at the origin with radius $3$
- The closed disk centered at the origin with radius $9$
- The set of points with $x^2 + y^2 \geq 9$

## Explanation

The square root is defined when its argument is greater than or equal to zero, so the domain is given by the condition $9 - x^2 - y^2 \geq 0$. This condition is equivalent to $x^2 + y^2 \leq 9$, that is, $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \leq 3$. Therefore the domain is the **closed disk** centered at the origin with radius $\sqrt{9} = 3$, including the boundary circle. Since it is closed, because it is defined by a non-strict inequality, and it is bounded, the domain is a compact set.
