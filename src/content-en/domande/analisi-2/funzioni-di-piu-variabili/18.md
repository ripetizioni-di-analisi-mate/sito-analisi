Let $A = \{ (x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : x^2 + y^2 < 1 \} \cup \{ (2, 0) \}$. Which statement is true?

- $(2, 0)$ is an accumulation point of $A$
- $(1, 0)$ is not an accumulation point of $A$, because it does not belong to $A$
- $(2, 0)$ is an isolated point of $A$
- $(0, 0)$ is an isolated point of $A$

## Explanation

The disk centered at $(2, 0)$ with radius $\dfrac{1}{2}$ contains only points at distance at least $\dfrac{3}{2}$ from the origin, so the only point of $A$ it contains is $(2, 0)$. Therefore $(2, 0)$ belongs to $A$ and is not an accumulation point of $A$, that is, it is an **isolated point**. The point $(1, 0)$ does not belong to $A$, but every neighborhood of it contains points $(1 - t, 0)$ of the disk, with $t > 0$ small enough, so $(1, 0)$ is an accumulation point of $A$. The point $(0, 0)$ is an interior point of the disk, therefore it is also an accumulation point of $A$.
