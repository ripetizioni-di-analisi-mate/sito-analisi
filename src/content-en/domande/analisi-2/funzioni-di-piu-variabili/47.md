Which of the following points belongs to the neighborhood $B(P_0, 2)$ with $P_0 = (1, 2)$?

- $(3, 2)$
- $(1, 0)$
- $(3, 3)$
- $(2, 3)$

## Explanation

The point $P$ belongs to $B(P_0, 2)$ if its distance from $P_0$ is *strictly* less than $2$. For $(2, 3)$ the distance is $\sqrt{1^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{2} < 2$, so the point belongs to the neighborhood. The points $(3, 2)$ and $(1, 0)$ are at distance exactly $2$ from $P_0$, so they lie on the boundary circle, which is not part of the open disk. The point $(3, 3)$ is at distance $\sqrt{4 + 1} = \sqrt{5} > 2$, so it lies outside.
