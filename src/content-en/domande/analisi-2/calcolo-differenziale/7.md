For the function $f(x, y) = x^2 y$, what is the maximum value of the directional derivative at the point $(1, 2)$?

- $17$
- $5$
- $\sqrt{5}$
- $\sqrt{17}$

## Explanation

The partial derivatives are $f_x = 2xy$ and $f_y = x^2$, so $\nabla f(1, 2) = (4,\, 1)$. The function is a polynomial, therefore it is differentiable and the theorem on the **direction of steepest ascent** applies. The directional derivative is maximal along the unit vector in the direction of the gradient, where it equals the magnitude of the gradient. The maximum value is therefore $|\nabla f(1, 2)| = \sqrt{16 + 1} = \sqrt{17}$.
