The polynomial $f(x, y) = x^2 y + 3xy - y^2 + 2x$ is approximated near the origin by its second-order Taylor polynomial. Which polynomial is obtained?

- $T_2(x, y) = 2x + 3xy - y^2$
- $T_2(x, y) = 2x + 3xy - y^2 + x^2 y$
- $T_2(x, y) = 2x - y^2$
- $T_2(x, y) = 2x + 3xy$

## Explanation

For a polynomial centered at the origin the second-order Taylor polynomial collects the terms of degree at most $2$, because the difference between $f$ and those terms is a polynomial with only terms of degree at least $3$, which is $o\left( x^2 + y^2 \right)$. The term $x^2 y$ has degree $3$ and is discarded, while $2x$, $3xy$ and $-y^2$ remain. Therefore $T_2(x, y) = 2x + 3xy - y^2$.
