The temperature of a flat plate is $T(x, y) = 20 - x^2 - 2y^2$, in degrees Celsius, with $x$ and $y$ in meters. What is the rate of change of the temperature at the point $(1, 1)$ in the positive direction of the $y$-axis?

- $-2\,\mathrm{^\circ C/m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{^\circ C/m}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{^\circ C/m}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{^\circ C/m}$

## Explanation

The rate of change in the positive direction of the $y$-axis is the directional derivative along the unit vector $(0, 1)$, that is, the partial derivative $T_y$. Differentiating with respect to $y$ with $x$ held constant gives $T_y = -4y$. At the point $(1, 1)$ the rate is therefore $-4\,\mathrm{^\circ C/m}$, and the temperature decreases.
