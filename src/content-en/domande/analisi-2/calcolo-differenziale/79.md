What is the maximum directional derivative over all unit directions for $4 x + 2 y$?

- $6$
- $4$
- $20$
- $2 \sqrt{5}$

## Explanation

The gradient is $\left( 4, \  2\right)$. Its dot product with a unit vector is greatest when the unit vector is parallel to and in the same direction as the gradient.
