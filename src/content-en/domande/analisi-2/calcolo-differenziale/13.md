For the function $f(x, y) = x^3 e^{2y}$, what is the value of the mixed second derivative $f_{xy}$?

- $3x^2 e^{2y}$
- $6x^2 e^{2y}$
- $6x\, e^{2y}$
- $2x^3 e^{2y}$

## Explanation

Differentiating with respect to $x$ with $y$ held constant we obtain $f_x = 3x^2 e^{2y}$. Differentiating $f_x$ with respect to $y$, the derivative of $e^{2y}$ is $2e^{2y}$, so $f_{xy} = 6x^2 e^{2y}$. In the opposite order we have $f_y = 2x^3 e^{2y}$ and $f_{yx} = 6x^2 e^{2y}$. The two mixed derivatives coincide, in agreement with **Schwarz's theorem**, since $f$ is of class $C^2$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$.
