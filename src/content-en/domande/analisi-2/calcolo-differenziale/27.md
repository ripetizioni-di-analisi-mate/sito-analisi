A volume measurement $V(x,y)$ has differential $dV=3\,dx-4\,dy$ at the operating point. If $|dx|\le0.02$ and $|dy|\le0.01$, what is the greatest $|dV|$ predicted by the linear approximation?

- $0.02$
- $0.07$
- $0.14$
- $0.10$

## Explanation

The triangle inequality gives $|dV|\le3\cdot0.02+4\cdot0.01=0.10$. Choosing errors with opposite signs attains this bound.
