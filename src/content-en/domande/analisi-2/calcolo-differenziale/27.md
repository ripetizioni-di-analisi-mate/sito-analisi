What is the equation of the tangent plane to the graph of $f(x, y) = \sqrt{9 - x^2 - y^2}$ at the point with coordinates $(1, 2)$?

- $z = \dfrac{13 - x - 2y}{4}$
- $z = \dfrac{x + 2y - 1}{2}$
- $z = \dfrac{4 - x - 2y}{2}$
- $z = \dfrac{9 - x - 2y}{2}$

## Explanation

We have $f(1, 2) = \sqrt{9 - 1 - 4} = 2$. By the chain rule, $f_x = -\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{9 - x^2 - y^2}}$ and $f_y = -\dfrac{y}{\sqrt{9 - x^2 - y^2}}$, which are continuous in the disk $x^2 + y^2 < 9$, so $f$ is differentiable at $(1, 2)$. At the point we have $f_x(1, 2) = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $f_y(1, 2) = -1$. The tangent plane therefore has equation
$$
z = 2 - \frac{1}{2}(x - 1) - (y - 2) = \frac{9 - x - 2y}{2} .
$$
