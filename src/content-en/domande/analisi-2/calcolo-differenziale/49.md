What is the maximum directional derivative over all unit directions for $2 x + 3 y$?

- $3$
- $\sqrt{13}$
- $5$
- $13$

## Explanation

The gradient is $\left( 2, \  3\right)$. Its dot product with a unit vector is greatest when the unit vector is parallel to and in the same direction as the gradient.
