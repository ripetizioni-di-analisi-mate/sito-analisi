Which polynomial $P$ of degree at most $2$ satisfies $\dfrac{1}{1 - x - y} = P(x, y) + o\left( x^2 + y^2 \right)$ as $(x, y) \to (0, 0)$?

- $P(x, y) = 1 + x + y + x^2 + y^2$
- $P(x, y) = 1 + x + y + x^2 + 2xy + y^2$
- $P(x, y) = 1 + x + y + \dfrac{x^2 + 2xy + y^2}{2}$
- $P(x, y) = 1 - x - y + x^2 + 2xy + y^2$

## Explanation

Setting $u = x + y$, one has $\dfrac{1}{1 - x - y} = \dfrac{1}{1 - u}$, and by the one-variable Taylor formula $\dfrac{1}{1 - u} = 1 + u + u^2 + o\left( u^2 \right)$. Since $|u| \leq \sqrt{2}\, \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$, an $o\left( u^2 \right)$ is also an $o\left( x^2 + y^2 \right)$. Expanding $u^2 = x^2 + 2xy + y^2$ gives
$$
P(x, y) = 1 + x + y + x^2 + 2xy + y^2 .
$$
