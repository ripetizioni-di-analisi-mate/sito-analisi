What is the gradient at $(1,0)$ of $2 x^{2} y + 5 y + 3 e^{x - y}$?

- $\left( 3 e, \  -7 + 3 e\right)$
- $\left( 3 e, \  7 - 3 e\right)$
- $\left( 7 - 3 e, \  3 e\right)$
- $\left( 3 e, \  2 - 3 e\right)$

## Explanation

The derivative of the exponential with respect to $x$ is positive, while its derivative with respect to $y$ has the opposite sign. Add the polynomial derivatives and evaluate at $(1,0)$.
