Starting from the point $(2, 0)$ one moves in a straight line toward the origin. What is, at the start of the motion, the directional derivative of $f(x, y) = x\,e^{y}$ in that direction?

- $1$
- $-2$
- $2$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The vector from $(2, 0)$ to the origin is $(-2,\, 0)$, and its unit vector is $\mathbf{v} = (-1,\, 0)$. The partial derivatives are $f_x = e^{y}$ and $f_y = x\,e^{y}$, continuous on $\mathbb{R}^2$, so $f$ is differentiable and $\nabla f(2, 0) = (1,\, 2)$. By the **gradient formula**
$$
D_{\mathbf{v}} f(2, 0) = 1 \cdot (-1) + 2 \cdot 0 = -1 .
$$
