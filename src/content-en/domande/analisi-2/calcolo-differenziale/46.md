What is the gradient at $(1,0)$ of $2 x^{2} y + 5 y + 3 e^{x - y}$?

- $\left( 3 e, \  -7 + 3 e\right)$
- $\left( 3 e, \  7 - 3 e\right)$
- $\left( 7 - 3 e, \  3 e\right)$
- $\left( 3 e, \  2 - 3 e\right)$

## Explanation

The exponential has opposite signs in its $x$ and $y$ derivatives. Add the polynomial derivatives and evaluate at $(1,0)$.
