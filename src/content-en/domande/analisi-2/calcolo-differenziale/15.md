The plane $y = 1$ cuts the graph of $f(x, y) = 6 - x^2 - 2y^2$ along the curve $z = f(x, 1)$. What is the slope of this curve at the point with $x = 1$?

- $-4$
- $2$
- $-2$
- $3$

## Explanation

On the plane $y = 1$ the curve is the graph of the one-variable function $g(x) = f(x, 1) = 4 - x^2$. Its slope at $x = 1$ is $g'(1)$, which by definition coincides with the partial derivative $f_x(1, 1)$. Since $f_x = -2x$, the slope equals $f_x(1, 1) = -2$. The value $f(1, 1) = 3$ is the height of the point on the curve, while $f_y(1, 1) = -4$ is the slope of the section with the plane $x = 1$.
