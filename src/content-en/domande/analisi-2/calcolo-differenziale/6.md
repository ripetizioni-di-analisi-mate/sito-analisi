## Explanation

The partial derivatives are $f_x = 2x + y$ and $f_y = x$, continuous on $\mathbb{R}^2$, so $f$ is differentiable and the **gradient formula** applies. At the point $(2, 1)$ we have $\nabla f(2, 1) = (5,\, 2)$. Therefore
$$
D_{\mathbf{v}} f(2, 1) = 5 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + 2 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \frac{7\sqrt{2}}{2} .
$$
