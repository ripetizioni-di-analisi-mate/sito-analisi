For the function $f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 6y$, what is the equation of the tangent plane to the graph that is horizontal?

- $z = 23$
- $z = -13$
- $z = 3$
- $z = 39$

## Explanation

The tangent plane $z = f(x_0, y_0) + f_x(x_0, y_0)(x - x_0) + f_y(x_0, y_0)(y - y_0)$ is horizontal if and only if the gradient at the point is zero. Since $\nabla f(x, y) = (2x - 4,\; 2y + 6)$, the gradient vanishes only at $(2, -3)$. At that point $f(2, -3) = 4 + 9 - 8 - 18 = -13$, so the horizontal tangent plane has equation $z = -13$.
