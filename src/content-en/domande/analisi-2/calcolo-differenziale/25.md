What is the equation of the tangent plane to the graph of $f(x, y) = x\,e^{xy}$ at the point with coordinates $(1, 0)$?

- $z = x + y + 1$
- $z = x + y$
- $z = x$
- $z = 2x + y - 1$

## Explanation

By the product rule we have $f_x = e^{xy} + xy\,e^{xy}$, while $f_y = x^2 e^{xy}$. The partial derivatives are continuous on $\mathbb{R}^2$, so $f$ is differentiable and the tangent plane exists. At the point $(1, 0)$ we have $f(1, 0) = 1$, $f_x(1, 0) = 1$ and $f_y(1, 0) = 1$. The tangent plane therefore has equation
$$
z = 1 + (x - 1) + y = x + y .
$$
