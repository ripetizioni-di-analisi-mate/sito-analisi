For the function $f(x, y) = y^{x}$, defined for $y > 0$, what is the value of $f_x(2, 3)$?

- $9 \ln 3$
- $6$
- $9$
- $6 \ln 3$

## Explanation

With $y$ held constant, the function $x \mapsto y^{x}$ is an exponential with base $y$, so its derivative is $y^{x} \ln y$. Therefore $f_x = y^{x} \ln y$. The expression $x\,y^{x - 1}$ is the derivative with respect to $y$, in which the exponent $x$ is constant. At the point $(2, 3)$ we obtain $f_x(2, 3) = 3^2 \ln 3 = 9 \ln 3$.
