What is the tangent line at the point $(1, 1)$ to the level curve of $f(x, y) = x^2 + xy + y^2$ passing through that point?

- $y = x$
- $x + y = 2$
- $2x + y = 3$
- $x + 2y = 3$

## Explanation

The function is a polynomial, so it is differentiable, and at the points where the gradient is not zero the gradient is orthogonal to the level curve. We have $f_x = 2x + y$ and $f_y = x + 2y$, therefore $\nabla f(1, 1) = (3,\, 3)$. The tangent line passes through $(1, 1)$ and is orthogonal to $(3,\, 3)$, so it has equation $3(x - 1) + 3(y - 1) = 0$, that is, $x + y = 2$. The line $y = x$ has the direction of the gradient and is the normal line to the curve.
