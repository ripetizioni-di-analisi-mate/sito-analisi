The second-order Taylor polynomial at the origin contains the term $6xy$ and no $x^2$ or $y^2$ term. Find $v^\mathsf{T}H v$ for $v=(1,-1)$, where $H$ is the Hessian at the origin.

- $-6$
- $6$
- $0$
- $-12$

## Explanation

The quadratic term is half the Hessian form. At $v=(1,-1)$ it equals $-6$. Doubling gives $-12$.
