For the function $f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2$, at which point $(x_0, y_0)$ is the tangent plane to the graph parallel to the plane $z = 2x - 4y + 7$?

- $(1, -2)$
- $(2, -4)$
- $(-1, 2)$
- $(1, 2)$

## Explanation

The tangent plane at $(x_0, y_0)$ has equation $z = f(x_0, y_0) + f_x(x_0, y_0)(x - x_0) + f_y(x_0, y_0)(y - y_0)$, so the coefficients of $x$ and of $y$ are the partial derivatives at the point. Two planes of this kind are parallel if and only if they have the same coefficients of $x$ and of $y$. Since $f_x = 2x$ and $f_y = 2y$, the point must satisfy the system
$$
\begin{cases}
2x_0 = 2 \\
2y_0 = -4
\end{cases}
$$
whose only solution is $(x_0, y_0) = (1, -2)$.
