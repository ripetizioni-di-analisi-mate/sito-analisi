What is the expression of the second derivative $f_{yy}$ of $f(x, y) = x \sin y + y^3$?

- $x \sin y + 6y$
- $x \cos y + 3y^2$
- $-x \sin y + 6y$
- $-\sin y + 6y$

## Explanation

Differentiating with respect to $y$ with $x$ held constant gives $f_y = x \cos y + 3y^2$. Differentiating again with respect to $y$, the derivative of $\cos y$ is $-\sin y$ and that of $3y^2$ is $6y$. Therefore $f_{yy} = -x \sin y + 6y$.
