What is the gradient at $(1,0)$ of $3 x^{2} y + 2 y + 5 e^{x - y}$?

- $\left( 5 e, \  -5 + 5 e\right)$
- $\left( 5 e, \  5 - 5 e\right)$
- $\left( 5 - 5 e, \  5 e\right)$
- $\left( 5 e, \  3 - 5 e\right)$

## Explanation

The exponential has opposite signs in its $x$ and $y$ derivatives. Add the polynomial derivatives and evaluate at $(1,0)$.
