Let $f$ be differentiable on $\mathbb{R}^2$ with $\nabla f(1, 2) = (3, -1)$, and let $F(t) = f\bigl( t^2,\, 2t \bigr)$. What is the value of $F'(1)$?

- $8$
- $2$
- $4$
- $-4$

## Explanation

For $t = 1$ the point of the curve is $\bigl( 1^2,\, 2 \cdot 1 \bigr) = (1, 2)$, where the gradient of $f$ is known. The derivatives of the components are $x'(t) = 2t$ and $y'(t) = 2$, so at $t = 1$ the derivative vector is $(2,\, 2)$. By the **chain rule** we have
$$
F'(1) = \nabla f(1, 2) \cdot (2,\, 2) = 3 \cdot 2 + (-1) \cdot 2 = 4 .
$$
