For the function
$$
f(x, y) =
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{x^3 + 2y^3}{x^2 + y^2} & (x, y) \neq (0, 0) \\[10pt]
0 & (x, y) = (0, 0)
\end{cases}
$$
what is the value of $f_y(0, 0)$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- The partial derivative does not exist

## Explanation

At the point $(0, 0)$ the function is defined separately, so the partial derivative is computed from the definition. On the $y$-axis we have $f(0, t) = \dfrac{2t^3}{t^2} = 2t$ for $t \neq 0$, and the equality also holds for $t = 0$. The difference quotient is therefore
$$
\frac{f(0, t) - f(0, 0)}{t} = \frac{2t}{t} = 2 ,
$$
so $f_y(0, 0) = 2$. The same computation on the $x$-axis gives $f_x(0, 0) = 1$.
