What is the slope of the tangent plane in the direction of the $x$-axis at $(1,0)$ for $5 x^{2} + 3 x y + 2 y$?

- $5$
- $0$
- $-10$
- $10$

## Explanation

The slope in the direction of the $x$-axis is the partial derivative with respect to $x$. Evaluate $10 x + 3 y$ at $(1,0)$.
