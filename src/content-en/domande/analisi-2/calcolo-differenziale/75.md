What is $f_{xy}$ at $\left( \dfrac{\pi}{6}, \  0\right)$ if $f$ is given by $\sin{\left(3 x - 5 y \right)}$?

- $15$
- $5$
- $-15$
- $0$

## Explanation

Differentiate first with respect to $x$ and then $y$ to obtain $15 \sin{\left(3 x - 5 y \right)}$. The sine is one at the given point.
