What is the homogeneous degree-two term in the expansion at the origin of $e^{2 x + 3 y}$?

- $2 x^{2} + \dfrac{9 y^{2}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\left(2 x + 3 y\right)^{2}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\left(2 x - 3 y\right)^{2}}{2}$
- $\left(2 x + 3 y\right)^{2}$

## Explanation

The quadratic term of $e^s$ is $s^2/2$. Substitute the linear form in the exponent.
