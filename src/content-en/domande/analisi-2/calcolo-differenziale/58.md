A function $f$ satisfies
$$
\bigl| f(x, y) - 3 - 2x \bigr| \leq x^2 + y^2
$$
for every $(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2$. What is $\nabla f(0, 0)$?

- $(3,\, 2)$
- $(2,\, 1)$
- $(0,\, 0)$
- $(2,\, 0)$

## Explanation

For $(x, y) = (0, 0)$ the inequality gives $|f(0, 0) - 3| \leq 0$, so $f(0, 0) = 3$. Setting $\rho = \sqrt{h^2 + k^2}$, one has
$$
\frac{\bigl| f(h, k) - 3 - 2h - 0 \cdot k \bigr|}{\rho} \leq \frac{\rho^2}{\rho} = \rho \to 0 ,
$$
therefore $f$ is **differentiable** at $(0, 0)$ with differential $(h, k) \mapsto 2h$. Since the differential is $f_x(0,0)\, h + f_y(0,0)\, k$, one obtains $\nabla f(0, 0) = (2,\, 0)$.
