For a differentiable function, $(f_x+f_y)(P)=7$ and $(f_x-f_y)(P)=1$ are known. What is the first-order change in $f$ for a displacement $(0,h)$ from $P$?

- $4h$
- $6h$
- $7h$
- $3h$

## Explanation

Subtracting the second relation from the first gives $2f_y(P)=6$. The displacement leaves $x$ fixed, so the differential is $f_y(P)h=3h$.
