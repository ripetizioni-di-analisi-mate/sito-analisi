- $z = 4x + 4y - 10$
- $z = 4x - 2y - 4$
- $z = 4x - 4y - 2$
- $z = 4x - 4y - 4$

## Explanation

The function is a polynomial, so it is differentiable and the tangent plane exists at every point. We have $f(2, 1) = 4 - 2 = 2$; since $f_x = 2x$ and $f_y = -4y$, we have $f_x(2, 1) = 4$ and $f_y(2, 1) = -4$. The tangent plane therefore has equation
$$
z = 2 + 4(x - 2) - 4(y - 1) = 4x - 4y - 2 .
$$
