What is the mixed derivative $f_{xy}$ at $\left( \dfrac{\pi}{4}, \  0\right)$ if $f$ is given by $\sin{\left(2 x - 3 y \right)}$?

- $0$
- $-6$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

Differentiate first with respect to $x$ and then $y$ to obtain $6 \sin{\left(2 x - 3 y \right)}$. The sine is one at the given point.
