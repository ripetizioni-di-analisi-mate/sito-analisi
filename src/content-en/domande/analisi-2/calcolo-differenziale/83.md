For the function $f(x, y) = \cos(xy)$, which second-degree terms appear in the second-order Taylor polynomial centered at $(0, 0)$?

- None: the polynomial is the constant $1$
- Only the term $-xy$
- Only the term $-\dfrac{x^2 y^2}{2}$
- The terms $-\dfrac{x^2}{2}$ and $-\dfrac{y^2}{2}$

## Explanation

Setting $u = xy$, by the one-variable Taylor formula $\cos u = 1 - \dfrac{u^2}{2} + o\left( u^2 \right)$, so $\cos(xy) = 1 - \dfrac{x^2 y^2}{2} + o\left( x^2 y^2 \right)$. The term $x^2 y^2$ has degree $4$ and, since $x^2 y^2 \leq \left( x^2 + y^2 \right)^2$, it is an $o\left( x^2 + y^2 \right)$. Therefore no term of degree $1$ or $2$ appears in the second-order polynomial, and $T_2(x, y) = 1$.
