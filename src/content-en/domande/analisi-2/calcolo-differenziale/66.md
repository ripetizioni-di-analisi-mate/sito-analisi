What angle does the tangent plane to the graph of $f(x, y) = y\,e^{x}$ at the point with coordinates $(0, \sqrt{2})$ form with the $xy$-plane?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\arctan 3$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

The partial derivatives are $f_x = y\,e^{x}$ and $f_y = e^{x}$, continuous on $\mathbb{R}^2$, so $f$ is differentiable and $\nabla f(0, \sqrt{2}) = (\sqrt{2},\, 1)$. The tangent plane $z = \sqrt{2} + \sqrt{2}\,x + \left( y - \sqrt{2} \right)$ has normal vector $\mathbf{n} = (\sqrt{2},\, 1,\, -1)$, while the $xy$-plane has normal vector $(0, 0, 1)$. The angle $\theta$ between the two planes is the acute angle between the normal vectors, so
$$
\cos\theta = \frac{\left| \mathbf{n} \cdot (0, 0, 1) \right|}{|\mathbf{n}|} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2 + 1 + 1}} = \frac{1}{2} .
$$
Therefore $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$.
