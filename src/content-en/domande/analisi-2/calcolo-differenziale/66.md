What is the coefficient of $xy$ in the second-order Taylor expansion at $(0,0)$ of $\ln{\left(2 x y + 3 x + 5 y + 1 \right)}$?

- $2$
- $-15$
- $-13$
- $15$

## Explanation

The linear term in the logarithm of one plus the increment contributes $2$. The squared increment multiplied by $-1/2$ contributes $-15$.
