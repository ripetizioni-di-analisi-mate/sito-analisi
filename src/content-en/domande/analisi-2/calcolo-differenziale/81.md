What is the coefficient of $xy$ in the second-order Taylor expansion at $(0,0)$ of $\ln{\left(7 x y + 4 x + 2 y + 1 \right)}$?

- $-8$
- $8$
- $-1$
- $7$

## Explanation

The linear term in the logarithm of one plus the increment contributes $7$. The squared increment multiplied by $-1/2$ contributes $-8$.
