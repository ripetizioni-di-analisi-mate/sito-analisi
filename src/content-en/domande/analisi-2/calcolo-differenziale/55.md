For the function $f(x, y) = x^4 y^2$, what is the third derivative $f_{xxy}$ at the point $(1, 1)$, obtained by differentiating twice with respect to $x$ and then once with respect to $y$?

- $12$
- $48$
- $8$
- $24$

## Explanation

Differentiating with respect to $x$ gives $f_x = 4x^3 y^2$, and differentiating again $f_{xx} = 12x^2 y^2$. The derivative with respect to $y$ is therefore $f_{xxy} = 24x^2 y$. At the point $(1, 1)$ it therefore equals $24$.
