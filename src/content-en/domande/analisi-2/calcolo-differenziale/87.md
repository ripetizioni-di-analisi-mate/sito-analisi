What is the Jacobian determinant of the map $(x,y)\mapsto(4 x + 2 y,7 x - y)$?

- $-4$
- $10$
- $-18$
- $18$

## Explanation

The rows are $\left( 4, \  2\right)$ and $\left( 7, \  -1\right)$. Subtract the product on the secondary diagonal from the product on the main diagonal.
