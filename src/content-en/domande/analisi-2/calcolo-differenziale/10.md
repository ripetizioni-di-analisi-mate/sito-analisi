## Explanation

The partial derivatives of $f$ are $f_x = 2x$ and $f_y = 2y$, continuous on $\mathbb{R}^2$, so the **chain rule** applies. Since $x'(t) = e^{t}$ and $y'(t) = 2t$, we have
$$
F'(t) = 2x(t)\, e^{t} + 2y(t) \cdot 2t = 2e^{2t} + 4t^3 .
$$
Therefore $F'(0) = 2e^{0} + 0 = 2$.
