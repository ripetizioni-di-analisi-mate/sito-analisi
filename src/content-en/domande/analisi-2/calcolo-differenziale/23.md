Using the differential of $f(x, y) = x^2 y$ at the point $(2, 1)$, which approximate value is obtained for $f(2{,}01;\, 0{,}98)$?

- $3{,}96$
- $4{,}12$
- $3{,}92$
- $4{,}04$

## Explanation

The function is a polynomial, so it is differentiable and $f(2 + h,\, 1 + k) = f(2, 1) + f_x(2, 1)\, h + f_y(2, 1)\, k + o\!\left( \sqrt{h^2 + k^2} \right)$. We have $f(2, 1) = 4$; since $f_x = 2xy$ and $f_y = x^2$, we also have $f_x(2, 1) = 4$ and $f_y(2, 1) = 4$. With $h = 0{,}01$ and $k = -0{,}02$ we obtain
$$
f(2{,}01;\, 0{,}98) \approx 4 + 4 \cdot 0{,}01 + 4 \cdot (-0{,}02) = 3{,}96 .
$$
The exact value is $3{,}959298$, which differs from the approximation by less than one thousandth.
