A drone flies at constant altitude with velocity $(30,\, 40)\,\mathrm{km/h}$. At its current position the atmospheric pressure $p(x, y)$ has gradient $\nabla p = (-2,\, 1)\,\mathrm{hPa/km}$. How fast does the pressure measured by the drone change at that instant?

- $20\,\mathrm{hPa/h}$
- $100\,\mathrm{hPa/h}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{hPa/h}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{hPa/h}$

## Explanation

The measured pressure is $P(t) = p\bigl( x(t), y(t) \bigr)$, and the vector $\bigl( x'(t), y'(t) \bigr)$ is the velocity of the drone. By the **chain rule**
$$
P'(t) = \nabla p \cdot \bigl( x'(t), y'(t) \bigr) = (-2) \cdot 30 + 1 \cdot 40 = -20 .
$$
The units are $\mathrm{hPa/km} \cdot \mathrm{km/h} = \mathrm{hPa/h}$, so the pressure decreases by $20\,\mathrm{hPa}$ per hour.
