What is the affine approximation at $(0,0)$ of $\sqrt{2 x + 3 y + 1}$?

- $2 x + 3 y + 1$
- $- x - \dfrac{3 y}{2} + 1$
- $x + \dfrac{3 y}{2} + 1$
- $x + \dfrac{3 y}{2}$

## Explanation

The value at the origin is one. Each partial derivative is half the corresponding derivative of the radicand.
