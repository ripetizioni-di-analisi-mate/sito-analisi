A quantity is computed with the formula $f(x, y) = x y^2$, with $x > 0$ and $y > 0$. The measurements of $x$ and $y$ have relative errors of $1\%$ and $2\%$. What estimate of the maximum relative error on $f$ does the differential give?

- $3\%$
- $4\%$
- $2\%$
- $5\%$

## Explanation

The differential is $df = y^2\, dx + 2xy\, dy$. Dividing by $f = x y^2$ gives
$$
\frac{df}{f} = \frac{dx}{x} + 2\, \frac{dy}{y} .
$$
By the triangle inequality $\left| \dfrac{df}{f} \right| \leq \left| \dfrac{dx}{x} \right| + 2 \left| \dfrac{dy}{y} \right| = 1\% + 2 \cdot 2\%$. The estimate of the maximum relative error is therefore $5\%$.
