What is the homogeneous degree-two term in the expansion at the origin of $e^{3 x + 5 y}$?

- $\dfrac{\left(3 x + 5 y\right)^{2}}{2}$
- $\left(3 x + 5 y\right)^{2}$
- $\dfrac{\left(3 x - 5 y\right)^{2}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9 x^{2}}{2} + \dfrac{25 y^{2}}{2}$

## Explanation

The quadratic term of $e^s$ is $s^2/2$. Substitute the linear form in the exponent.
