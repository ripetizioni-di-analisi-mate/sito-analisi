At $(2,0)$ the gradient of $f(x,y)=xe^y+y^2$ is $(1,2)$. An instrument reports a first-order change of $0.07$ while the $x$ coordinate increases by $0.01$. Which increment of $y$ is consistent with the data to first order?

- $0.06$
- $0.04$
- $0.12$
- $0.03$

## Explanation

The gradient components give $df=dx+2\,dy$. Substituting the data yields $2\,dy=0.07-0.01=0.06$, hence $dy=0.03$.
