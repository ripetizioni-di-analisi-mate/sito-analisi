In space consider $f(x, y, z) = xyz$ and the vector $\mathbf{v} = \left( \dfrac{2}{3},\, \dfrac{1}{3},\, \dfrac{2}{3} \right)$. What is the value of $D_{\mathbf{v}} f(1, 1, 2)$?

- $8$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}$

## Explanation

The vector $\mathbf{v}$ has magnitude $\sqrt{\dfrac{4 + 1 + 4}{9}} = 1$, so it is a unit vector. The partial derivatives are $f_x = yz$, $f_y = xz$ and $f_z = xy$, therefore $\nabla f(1, 1, 2) = (2,\, 2,\, 1)$. The function is a polynomial, so it is differentiable, and by the **gradient formula**
$$
D_{\mathbf{v}} f(1, 1, 2) = 2 \cdot \frac{2}{3} + 2 \cdot \frac{1}{3} + 1 \cdot \frac{2}{3} = \frac{8}{3} .
$$
