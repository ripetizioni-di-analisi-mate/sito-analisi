What is the homogeneous degree-two term in the expansion at the origin of $e^{4 x + 2 y}$?

- $\dfrac{\left(4 x + 2 y\right)^{2}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\left(4 x - 2 y\right)^{2}}{2}$
- $\left(4 x + 2 y\right)^{2}$
- $8 x^{2} + 2 y^{2}$

## Explanation

The quadratic term of $e^s$ is $s^2/2$. Substitute the linear form in the exponent.
