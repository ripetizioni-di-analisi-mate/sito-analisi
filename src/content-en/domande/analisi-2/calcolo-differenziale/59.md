The tangent plane to the graph of $f(x, y) = x^2 + y^3$ at the point with coordinates $(2, 1)$ meets the $z$-axis at one point. What is the height of that point?

- $5$
- $6$
- $-1$
- $-6$

## Explanation

One has $f(2, 1) = 5$, $f_x = 2x$ and $f_y = 3y^2$, so $f_x(2, 1) = 4$ and $f_y(2, 1) = 3$. The tangent plane is
$$
z = 5 + 4(x - 2) + 3(y - 1) = 4x + 3y - 6 .
$$
The $z$-axis consists of the points with $x = y = 0$, therefore the intersection has height $z = -6$.
