Extend $\dfrac{4 x^{2} y}{x^{2} + y^{2}}$ to the origin with value zero. Which statement describes its behavior at the origin exactly?

- Nonzero partial derivative with respect to $y$
- Both partial derivatives zero and differentiable
- Both partial derivatives zero and not differentiable
- Nonzero partial derivative with respect to $x$

## Explanation

The function vanishes on both axes, so both partials are zero. Along $y=x$ we have $f(x,x)=2 x$, and the remainder divided by the distance to the origin does not tend to zero.
