Which equation does the function $f(x, t) = \sin(x - 3t)$ satisfy at every point $(x, t)$?

- $f_{tt} = 3\, f_{xx}$
- $f_{tt} = -9\, f_{xx}$
- $f_{xx} = 9\, f_{tt}$
- $f_{tt} = 9\, f_{xx}$

## Explanation

Differentiating twice with respect to $x$ gives $f_x = \cos(x - 3t)$ and $f_{xx} = -\sin(x - 3t)$. Differentiating twice with respect to $t$, each differentiation brings the factor $-3$, so $f_t = -3 \cos(x - 3t)$ and $f_{tt} = -9 \sin(x - 3t)$. Therefore $f_{tt} = 9\, f_{xx}$, which is the wave equation with speed $3$.
