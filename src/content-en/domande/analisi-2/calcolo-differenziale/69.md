What is the affine approximation at $(0,0)$ of $\sqrt{3 x + 5 y + 1}$?

- $3 x + 5 y + 1$
- $- \dfrac{3 x}{2} - \dfrac{5 y}{2} + 1$
- $\dfrac{3 x}{2} + \dfrac{5 y}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3 x}{2} + \dfrac{5 y}{2} + 1$

## Explanation

The value at the origin is one. Each partial derivative is half the corresponding derivative of the radicand.
