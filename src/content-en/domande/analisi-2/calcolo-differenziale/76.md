Let $f(x, y) = g(xy)$, with $g$ of class $C^2$ on $\mathbb{R}$. What is the expression of the mixed second derivative $f_{xy}$?

- $g'(xy) + xy\,g''(xy)$
- $xy\,g''(xy)$
- $g''(xy)$
- $g'(xy) + g''(xy)$

## Explanation

By the **chain rule** $f_x = g'(xy) \cdot y$, because the derivative of $xy$ with respect to $x$ is $y$. Differentiating $f_x$ with respect to $y$, one applies the product rule to the factors $y$ and $g'(xy)$. The derivative of $y$ is $1$, and by the chain rule the derivative of $g'(xy)$ with respect to $y$ is $g''(xy) \cdot x$. Therefore $f_{xy} = g'(xy) + xy\,g''(xy)$.
