What is the gradient at $(1,0)$ of $4 x^{2} y + 7 y + 2 e^{x - y}$?

- $\left( 11 - 2 e, \  2 e\right)$
- $\left( 2 e, \  4 - 2 e\right)$
- $\left( 2 e, \  11 - 2 e\right)$
- $\left( 2 e, \  -11 + 2 e\right)$

## Explanation

The exponential has opposite signs in its $x$ and $y$ derivatives. Add the polynomial derivatives and evaluate at $(1,0)$.
