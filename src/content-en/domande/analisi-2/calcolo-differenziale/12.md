- $\begin{pmatrix} 12 & 4 \\ 4 & 12 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 12 & 0 \\ 0 & 12 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 12 & -4 \\ -4 & 12 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 12 & -8 \\ -8 & 12 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The first derivatives are $f_x = 4x^3 - 4y$ and $f_y = 4y^3 - 4x$. Differentiating again we obtain $f_{xx} = 12x^2$, $f_{yy} = 12y^2$ and $f_{xy} = f_{yx} = -4$. At the point $(1, 1)$ we therefore have $f_{xx} = f_{yy} = 12$, while the mixed derivatives equal $-4$ at every point. Therefore the **Hessian matrix** at $(1, 1)$ has $12$ on the diagonal and $-4$ off the diagonal.
