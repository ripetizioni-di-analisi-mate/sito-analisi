For the function $f(x, y) = x^3 + 2xy^2$, what is the value of $x\, f_x(x, y) + y\, f_y(x, y)$ at the point $(1, 1)$?

- $3$
- $5$
- $9$
- $6$

## Explanation

The partial derivatives are $f_x = 3x^2 + 2y^2$ and $f_y = 4xy$. At the point $(1, 1)$ they equal $f_x(1, 1) = 5$ and $f_y(1, 1) = 4$. Therefore
$$
1 \cdot f_x(1, 1) + 1 \cdot f_y(1, 1) = 5 + 4 = 9 .
$$
The result is $3 f(1, 1) = 3 \cdot 3$, consistent with the fact that $f$ is a homogeneous polynomial of degree $3$.
