Which vector is perpendicular to the tangent plane to the graph of $f(x, y) = x^2 y$ at the point $(1, 2, 2)$?

- $(4,\, 1,\, -1)$
- $(4,\, 1,\, 1)$
- $(2,\, 1,\, -1)$
- $(4,\, 2,\, -1)$

## Explanation

The partial derivatives are $f_x = 2xy$ and $f_y = x^2$, so $f_x(1, 2) = 4$ and $f_y(1, 2) = 1$. The tangent plane has equation $z = 2 + 4(x - 1) + (y - 2)$, that is $4x + y - z - 4 = 0$. The coefficients of $x$, $y$ and $z$ form a vector perpendicular to the plane, therefore the required vector is $(4,\, 1,\, -1)$.
