What is $f_{xy}$ at $\left( \dfrac{\pi}{8}, \  0\right)$ if $f$ is given by $\sin{\left(4 x - 2 y \right)}$?

- $-8$
- $0$
- $2$
- $8$

## Explanation

Differentiate first with respect to $x$ and then $y$ to obtain $8 \sin{\left(2 \left(2 x - y\right) \right)}$. The sine is one at the given point.
