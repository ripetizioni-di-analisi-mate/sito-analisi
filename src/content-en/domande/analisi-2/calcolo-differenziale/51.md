What is the coefficient of $xy$ in the second-order Taylor expansion at $(0,0)$ of $\ln{\left(5 x y + 2 x + 3 y + 1 \right)}$?

- $6$
- $5$
- $-1$
- $-6$

## Explanation

The linear term in the logarithm of one plus the increment contributes $5$. The squared increment multiplied by $-1/2$ contributes $-6$.
