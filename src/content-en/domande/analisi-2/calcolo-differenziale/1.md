- $\nabla f(1, 1) = (2,\, 2)$
- $\nabla f(1, 1) = (5,\, 2)$
- $\nabla f(1, 1) = (5,\, 3)$
- $\nabla f(1, 1) = (5,\, -1)$

## Explanation

Differentiating with respect to $x$ with $y$ held constant we obtain $f_x = 2xy^3 + 3$, because $-y$ is constant with respect to $x$ and has zero derivative. Differentiating with respect to $y$ with $x$ held constant we obtain $f_y = 3x^2 y^2 - 1$, because $3x$ is constant with respect to $y$. At the point $(1, 1)$ we therefore have $f_x(1, 1) = 2 + 3 = 5$ and $f_y(1, 1) = 3 - 1 = 2$. Therefore the **gradient** is $\nabla f(1, 1) = (5,\, 2)$.
