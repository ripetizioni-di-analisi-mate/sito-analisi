A rectangle has sides $x = 4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $y = 3\,\mathrm{cm}$. Side $x$ increases by $0.1\,\mathrm{cm}$ and side $y$ decreases by $0.2\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the differential of the area $A(x, y) = xy$ evaluated at these increments?

- $0.5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $-0.5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $-0.2\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $1.1\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The partial derivatives of the area are $A_x = y$ and $A_y = x$, so at the point $(4, 3)$ they equal $A_x = 3$ and $A_y = 4$. The increments are $h = 0.1$ and $k = -0.2$. The differential is therefore
$$
A_x\, h + A_y\, k = 3 \cdot 0.1 + 4 \cdot (-0.2) = -0.5 ,
$$
that is, the area decreases by about $0.5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$.
