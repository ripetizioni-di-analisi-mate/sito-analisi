A point travels along the graph of $y = \sin x$ from the point $(0, 0)$ to the point $(\pi, 0)$ under the action of the field $F(x, y) = (y,\; 2x)$. What is the work done by the field?

- $2$
- $-2$
- $0$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The graph is parametrized by $\gamma(t) = (t,\; \sin t)$, $t \in [0, \pi]$, so $\gamma'(t) = (1,\; \cos t)$.

We have $F(\gamma(t)) = (\sin t,\; 2t)$, therefore the integrand is $\sin t + 2t\cos t$.

Integrating by parts, $\displaystyle\int_0^\pi t\cos t \, dt = \bigl[ t\sin t \bigr]_0^\pi - \int_0^\pi \sin t \, dt = -2$.

Hence the work is
$$
\int_\gamma F \cdot d\gamma = \int_0^\pi \sin t \, dt + 2\int_0^\pi t\cos t \, dt = 2 - 4 = -2 .
$$
