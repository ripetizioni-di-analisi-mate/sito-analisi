What is the unit tangent vector at $t=1$ to the curve $\left( 3 t, \  5 t^{2}, \  2 t^{3}\right)$?

- $\left( \dfrac{3}{145}, \  \dfrac{2}{29}, \  \dfrac{6}{145}\right)$
- $\left( - \dfrac{3 \sqrt{145}}{145}, \  - \dfrac{2 \sqrt{145}}{29}, \  - \dfrac{6 \sqrt{145}}{145}\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{3 \sqrt{145}}{145}, \  \dfrac{2 \sqrt{145}}{29}, \  \dfrac{6 \sqrt{145}}{145}\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  10, \  6\right)$

## Explanation

The derivative at that point is $\left( 3, \  10, \  6\right)$. Divide the vector by its norm.
