Let $\gamma$ be the circle centered at the origin with radius $2$, traversed once counterclockwise. What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_\gamma x \, dy$?

- $2\pi$
- $8\pi$
- $0$
- $4\pi$

## Explanation

We parametrize the circle by $\gamma(t) = (2\cos t,\; 2\sin t)$, $t \in [0, 2\pi]$, which traverses it counterclockwise. Along the curve $x = 2\cos t$, and $dy$ is replaced by $y'(t)\, dt = 2\cos t \, dt$.

We therefore get
$$
\int_\gamma x \, dy = \int_0^{2\pi} 4\cos^2 t \, dt = 4\pi ,
$$
since the integral of $\cos^2 t$ over one period equals $\pi$.
