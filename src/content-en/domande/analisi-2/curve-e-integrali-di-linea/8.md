- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{3}$

## Explanation

The segment from the origin to the point $(1, 1)$ is parametrized by $\gamma(t) = (t, t)$, $t \in [0, 1]$. Since $\gamma'(t) = (1, 1)$, we have $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = \sqrt{2}$, and along the curve the function equals $xy = t^2$.

By the definition of the **line integral of the first kind** we therefore obtain
$$
\int_\gamma xy \, ds = \int_0^1 t^2 \sqrt{2} \, dt = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}.
$$
