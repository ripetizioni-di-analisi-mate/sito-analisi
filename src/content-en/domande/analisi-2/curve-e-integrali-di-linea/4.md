## Explanation

By the product rule we obtain $x'(t) = -\sin t + \sin t + t\cos t = t\cos t$ and $y'(t) = \cos t - \cos t + t\sin t = t\sin t$. Hence
$$
\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = \sqrt{t^2\cos^2 t + t^2\sin^2 t} = \sqrt{t^2} = |t| ,
$$
which for $t \in [0, \pi]$ equals $t$.

By the **length formula** we therefore have
$$
L(\gamma) = \int_0^\pi t \, dt = \frac{\pi^2}{2}.
$$
