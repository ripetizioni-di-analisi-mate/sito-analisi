Along the arc of cycloid
$$
\gamma(t) = (t - \sin t,\; 1 - \cos t), \qquad t \in [0, 2\pi],
$$
the height $y$ is integrated with respect to arc length. What is $\displaystyle\int_\gamma y \, ds$?

- $\dfrac{16}{3}$
- $2\pi$
- $8$
- $\dfrac{32}{3}$

## Explanation

We have $\gamma'(t) = (1 - \cos t,\; \sin t)$, so
$$
\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert^2 = 2 - 2\cos t = 4\sin^2 \frac{t}{2} .
$$
Since $\sin \dfrac{t}{2} \geq 0$ on $[0, 2\pi]$, we have $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = 2\sin \dfrac{t}{2}$.

Along the curve $y = 1 - \cos t = 2\sin^2 \dfrac{t}{2}$, therefore, with the substitution $v = \dfrac{t}{2}$,
$$
\int_\gamma y \, ds = \int_0^{2\pi} 4\sin^3 \frac{t}{2} \, dt = 8\int_0^{\pi} \sin^3 v \, dv = 8 \cdot \frac{4}{3} = \frac{32}{3} .
$$
The integral $\displaystyle\int_0^\pi \sin^3 v \, dv$ is computed by writing $\sin^3 v = \left( 1 - \cos^2 v \right)\sin v$.
