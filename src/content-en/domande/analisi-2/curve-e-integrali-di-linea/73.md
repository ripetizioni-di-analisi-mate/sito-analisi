Which unnormalized normal vector has a positive second component and norm equal to the speed at $t=1$ for $\left( 3 t, \  5 t^{2}\right)$?

- $\left( 10, \  -3\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  10\right)$
- $\left( -5, \  3\right)$
- $\left( -10, \  3\right)$

## Explanation

The tangent vector is $\left( 3, \  10\right)$. Rotate it ninety degrees counterclockwise to obtain the required normal.
