What is the length for $0\leq t\leq 5$ of the curve $\left( 2 \cos{\left(t \right)}, \  2 \sin{\left(t \right)}, \  3 t\right)$?

- $25$
- $10$
- $\sqrt{13}$
- $5 \sqrt{13}$

## Explanation

The squared speed is $13$ because the sine and cosine contribute the sum of their squares. The speed is constant.
