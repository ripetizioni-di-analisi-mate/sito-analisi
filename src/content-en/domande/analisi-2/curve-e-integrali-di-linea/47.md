Which of the following curves is closed?

- $\gamma(t) = (t,\; \sin t)$, $t \in [0, 2\pi]$
- $\gamma(t) = (\cos t,\; t \sin t)$, $t \in [0, \pi]$
- $\gamma(t) = (\cos 2t,\; \sin t)$, $t \in [0, \pi]$
- $\gamma(t) = (\sin t,\; \cos t)$, $t \in [0, \pi]$

## Explanation

A curve $\gamma : [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}^2$ is **closed** if $\gamma(a) = \gamma(b)$, so we compare the initial point and the final point.

For $\gamma(t) = (\cos 2t,\; \sin t)$ we have $\gamma(0) = (1, 0)$ and $\gamma(\pi) = (\cos 2\pi,\; \sin \pi) = (1, 0)$, therefore the curve is closed.

The others are not. The curve $(t, \sin t)$ goes from $(0, 0)$ to $(2\pi, 0)$, the curve $(\cos t, t\sin t)$ goes from $(1, 0)$ to $(-1, 0)$ and the curve $(\sin t, \cos t)$ goes from $(0, 1)$ to $(0, -1)$.
