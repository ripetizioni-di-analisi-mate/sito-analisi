What is the unit tangent vector at $t=1$ to the curve $\left( 4 t, \  2 t^{2}, \  7 t^{3}\right)$?

- $\left( \dfrac{4 \sqrt{473}}{473}, \  \dfrac{4 \sqrt{473}}{473}, \  \dfrac{21 \sqrt{473}}{473}\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{4}{473}, \  \dfrac{4}{473}, \  \dfrac{21}{473}\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  4, \  21\right)$
- $\left( - \dfrac{4 \sqrt{473}}{473}, \  - \dfrac{4 \sqrt{473}}{473}, \  - \dfrac{21 \sqrt{473}}{473}\right)$

## Explanation

The derivative at that point is $\left( 4, \  4, \  21\right)$. Divide the vector by its norm.
