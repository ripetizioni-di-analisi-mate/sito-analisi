What is the unit tangent vector to the curve
$$
\gamma(t) = \left( 2t,\; t^2,\; \frac{t^3}{3} \right), \qquad t \in [0, 2],
$$
at the point $\gamma(1)$?

- $\left( \dfrac{2}{5},\; \dfrac{2}{5},\; \dfrac{1}{5} \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{2}{3},\; \dfrac{2}{3},\; \dfrac{1}{3} \right)$
- $(2,\; 2,\; 1)$
- $\left( \dfrac{1}{3},\; \dfrac{2}{3},\; \dfrac{2}{3} \right)$

## Explanation

Differentiating the components gives $\gamma'(t) = (2,\; 2t,\; t^2)$, so $\gamma'(1) = (2,\; 2,\; 1)$.

The norm of the tangent vector is $\lVert \gamma'(1) \rVert = \sqrt{4 + 4 + 1} = 3$.

The **unit tangent vector** is obtained by dividing the tangent vector by its norm, therefore
$$
T(1) = \frac{\gamma'(1)}{\lVert \gamma'(1) \rVert} = \left( \frac{2}{3},\; \frac{2}{3},\; \frac{1}{3} \right).
$$
