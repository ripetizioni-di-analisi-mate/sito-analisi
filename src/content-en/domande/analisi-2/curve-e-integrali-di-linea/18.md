What is the unit tangent vector to the curve $\gamma(t) = (t^2,\; t^3)$, $t \in [0, 2]$, at the point $\gamma(1)$?

- $\left( \dfrac{2}{5},\; \dfrac{3}{5} \right)$
- $(2,\; 3)$
- $\left( \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{13}},\; \dfrac{3}{\sqrt{13}} \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{3}{\sqrt{13}},\; \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{13}} \right)$

## Explanation

We have $\gamma'(t) = (2t,\; 3t^2)$, so the tangent vector at $\gamma(1) = (1, 1)$ is $\gamma'(1) = (2,\; 3)$. Its norm is $\lVert \gamma'(1) \rVert = \sqrt{4 + 9} = \sqrt{13}$.

The **unit tangent vector** is obtained by dividing the tangent vector by its norm, therefore
$$
T(1) = \frac{\gamma'(1)}{\lVert \gamma'(1) \rVert} = \left( \frac{2}{\sqrt{13}},\; \frac{3}{\sqrt{13}} \right).
$$
