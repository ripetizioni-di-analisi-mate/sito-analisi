Which point is reached after arc length $1$ from $t=0$ in the increasing-$t$ direction along $\left( 4 t, \  2 t\right)$?

- $\left( - \dfrac{2 \sqrt{5}}{5}, \  - \dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{5}\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{5}, \  \dfrac{2 \sqrt{5}}{5}\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  2\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{2 \sqrt{5}}{5}, \  \dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{5}\right)$

## Explanation

The speed is constant at $2 \sqrt{5}$. The corresponding time is its reciprocal.
