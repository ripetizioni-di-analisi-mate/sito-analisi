What is the work of the field $F=(-y,x)$ along $\left( 2 \cos{\left(t \right)}, \  2 \sin{\left(t \right)}\right)$ from $t=\pi$ to $t=0$?

- $- 8 \pi$
- $0$
- $- 4 \pi$
- $4 \pi$

## Explanation

The field’s dot product with the derivative of the parametrization is constant at $4$. The limits of integration are in decreasing order.
