What is the speed $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert$ of the spiral
$$
\gamma(t) = (t\cos t,\; t\sin t), \qquad t \in [0, 4\pi],
$$
at the instant $t = \sqrt{3}$?

- $\sqrt{3}$
- $4$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

By the product rule
$$
\gamma'(t) = (\cos t - t\sin t,\; \sin t + t\cos t).
$$

Expanding the squares, the cross terms $\mp 2t\sin t\cos t$ cancel, therefore
$$
\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert^2 = \cos^2 t + \sin^2 t + t^2 \left( \sin^2 t + \cos^2 t \right) = 1 + t^2 .
$$

Hence $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = \sqrt{1 + t^2}$ and for $t = \sqrt{3}$ the speed equals $\sqrt{4} = 2$.
