Let $\gamma$ be the polygonal path that goes from the origin to the point $(1, 0)$ and then from the point $(1, 0)$ to the point $(1, 1)$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_\gamma (x + y) \, ds$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\sqrt{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The polygonal path is piecewise regular, so the integral is the sum of the integrals over the two segments.

The first segment is parametrized by $(t,\; 0)$, $t \in [0, 1]$, where $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = 1$ and $x + y = t$; its contribution is $\displaystyle\int_0^1 t \, dt = \frac{1}{2}$. The second segment is parametrized by $(1,\; t)$, $t \in [0, 1]$, where $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = 1$ and $x + y = 1 + t$; its contribution is $\displaystyle\int_0^1 (1 + t) \, dt = \frac{3}{2}$.

Therefore $\displaystyle\int_\gamma (x + y) \, ds = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{3}{2} = 2$.
