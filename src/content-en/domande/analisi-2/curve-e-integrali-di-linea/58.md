What is the length of the polygonal line that goes from the point $(0, 0)$ to the point $(3, 4)$ and then from the point $(3, 4)$ to the point $(3, 0)$?

::grafico{xmin=-1 xmax=5 ymin=-1 ymax=5 uguale=1 linee="0,0 3,4 3,0" punti="0,0,pieno;3,4,pieno;3,0,pieno" legenda=0 titolo="The polygonal line with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(3, 4)$ and $(3, 0)$"}

- $5$
- $9$
- $7$
- $12$

## Explanation

The polygonal line is a **piecewise regular** curve, so its length is the sum of the lengths of the two segments.

The first segment, from $(0, 0)$ to $(3, 4)$, has length $\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5$.

The second segment, from $(3, 4)$ to $(3, 0)$, is vertical and has length $4$.

Therefore the length of the polygonal line is $5 + 4 = 9$.
