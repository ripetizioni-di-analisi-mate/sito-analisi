Which unnormalized normal vector has a positive second component and norm equal to the speed at $t=1$ for $\left( 2 t, \  3 t^{2}\right)$?

- $\left( 6, \  -2\right)$
- $\left( -3, \  2\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  6\right)$
- $\left( -6, \  2\right)$

## Explanation

The tangent vector is $\left( 2, \  6\right)$. Rotate it ninety degrees counterclockwise to obtain the required normal.
