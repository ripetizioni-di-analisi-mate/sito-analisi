What is the center of mass of the homogeneous wire lying along the circle with center $(2, -1)$ and radius $3$?

- $(2,\; -1)$
- $(0,\; 0)$
- $\left( 2,\; -1 + \dfrac{6}{\pi} \right)$
- $(5,\; -1)$

## Explanation

If the trace of a homogeneous wire is symmetric with respect to a line, the center of mass belongs to that line.

The circle is symmetric with respect to the line $x = 2$ and to the line $y = -1$, so the center of mass belongs to both.

Therefore the center of mass is the center $(2, -1)$, which does not belong to the wire. The same result is obtained with $\gamma(t) = (2 + 3\cos t,\; -1 + 3\sin t)$, because the integrals of $\cos t$ and of $\sin t$ over $[0, 2\pi]$ are zero.
