A regular curve $\gamma : [0, 4] \to \mathbb{R}^3$ has speed $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = 2t + 1$. What is the arc length parameter $s(2)$, measured from $\gamma(0)$?

- $5$
- $6$
- $20$
- $4$

## Explanation

The arc length parameter measured from $\gamma(0)$ is the length of the arc traversed between the instants $0$ and $t$, that is,
$$
s(t) = \int_0^t \lVert \gamma'(\tau) \rVert \, d\tau .
$$

To compute it the speed is enough, so the components of $\gamma$ are not needed. We obtain
$$
s(2) = \int_0^2 (2\tau + 1) \, d\tau = \bigl[ \tau^2 + \tau \bigr]_0^2 = 6 .
$$
The value $5$ is the speed at the instant $t = 2$, that is, $s'(2)$, and the value $20$ is the length of the whole curve.
