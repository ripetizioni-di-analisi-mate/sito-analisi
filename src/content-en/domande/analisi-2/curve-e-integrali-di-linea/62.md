What is the length for $0\leq t\leq 2$ of the curve $\left( 3 \cos{\left(t \right)}, \  3 \sin{\left(t \right)}, \  5 t\right)$?

- $2 \sqrt{34}$
- $16$
- $6$
- $\sqrt{34}$

## Explanation

The squared speed is $34$ because the sine and cosine contribute the sum of their squares. The speed is constant.
