What is $\int_\gamma (x^2+y^2)\,ds$ along $\left( 4 \cos{\left(t \right)}, \  4 \sin{\left(t \right)}\right)$ for $0\leq t\leq\pi/2$?

- $64 \pi$
- $8 \pi$
- $32 \pi$
- $128 \pi$

## Explanation

On the circle the density is $16$ and the speed is $4$. The parameter interval has length $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
