Along the vertical segment $\gamma(t) = (2,\; t)$, $t \in [0, 3]$, what is the integral $\displaystyle\int_\gamma xy \, dy$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $9$
- $18$

## Explanation

In the notation of differential forms $dy$ is replaced by $y'(t)\, dt$.

We have $x(t) = 2$, $y(t) = t$ and $y'(t) = 1$, so the integrand becomes $2t$.

Therefore
$$
\int_\gamma xy \, dy = \int_0^3 2t \, dt = 9 .
$$
On the vertical segment $dx$ is zero, because $x'(t) = 0$, while $dy$ is not.
