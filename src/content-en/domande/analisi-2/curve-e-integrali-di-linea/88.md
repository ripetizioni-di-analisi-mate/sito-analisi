Which normal vector has positive second component and the same norm as the velocity at $t=1$ for $\left( 4 t, \  2 t^{2}\right)$?

- $\left( 4, \  -4\right)$
- $\left( -4, \  4\right)$
- $\left( -2, \  4\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  4\right)$

## Explanation

The tangent vector is $\left( 4, \  4\right)$. Rotate it ninety degrees counterclockwise to obtain the required normal.
