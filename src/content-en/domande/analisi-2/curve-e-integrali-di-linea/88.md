What is the integral $\displaystyle\int_\gamma x \, dy$ along the graph of $y = e^x$, traversed from the point $(1, e)$ to the point $(0, 1)$?

- $1$
- $e - 1$
- $-1$
- $1 - e$

## Explanation

With the parametrization $\gamma(t) = (t,\; e^t)$, $t \in [0, 1]$, the graph is traversed from $(0, 1)$ to $(1, e)$, that is, in the direction opposite to the required one.

In that direction $dy = e^t \, dt$ and, integrating by parts,
$$
\int_0^1 t e^t \, dt = \bigl[ t e^t \bigr]_0^1 - \int_0^1 e^t \, dt = e - (e - 1) = 1 .
$$

The integral of the second kind changes sign on the reversed curve, therefore in the direction from $(1, e)$ to $(0, 1)$ it equals $-1$.
