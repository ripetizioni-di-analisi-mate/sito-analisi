What is the slope of the tangent line to the curve
$$
\gamma(t) = (\ln t,\; t^2), \qquad t \in [1, 3],
$$
at the point $\gamma(2)$?

- $4$
- $8$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The tangent line at $\gamma(2)$ has the direction of the vector $\gamma'(2)$.

We have $\gamma'(t) = \left( \dfrac{1}{t},\; 2t \right)$, so $\gamma'(2) = \left( \dfrac{1}{2},\; 4 \right)$.

The slope of a line with direction vector $(v_1, v_2)$, $v_1 \neq 0$, is $\dfrac{v_2}{v_1}$, therefore it equals
$$
\frac{4}{\frac{1}{2}} = 8 .
$$
