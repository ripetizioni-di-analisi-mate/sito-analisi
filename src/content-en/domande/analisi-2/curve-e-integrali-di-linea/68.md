What is the work of the field $F=(-y,x)$ along $\left( 3 \cos{\left(t \right)}, \  3 \sin{\left(t \right)}\right)$ from $t=\pi$ to $t=0$?

- $9 \pi$
- $- 18 \pi$
- $- 9 \pi$
- $0$

## Explanation

The field’s dot product with the derivative of the parametrization is constant at $9$. The limits of integration are in decreasing order.
