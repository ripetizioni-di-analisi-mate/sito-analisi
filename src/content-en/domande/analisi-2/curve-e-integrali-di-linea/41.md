What is the work of the field $F(x, y) = (x^2,\; xy)$ along the segment from the point $(1, 0)$ to the point $(0, 1)$?

- $-\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The segment oriented from the point $(1, 0)$ to the point $(0, 1)$ is parametrized by $\gamma(t) = (1 - t,\; t)$, $t \in [0, 1]$, so $\gamma'(t) = (-1,\; 1)$ and $F(\gamma(t)) = \bigl( (1 - t)^2,\; (1 - t)\,t \bigr)$.

The scalar product equals
$$
F(\gamma(t)) \cdot \gamma'(t) = -(1 - t)^2 + t\,(1 - t) .
$$
Since $\displaystyle\int_0^1 (1 - t)^2 \, dt = \frac{1}{3}$ and $\displaystyle\int_0^1 (t - t^2) \, dt = \frac{1}{6}$, the work is $-\dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{1}{6} = -\dfrac{1}{6}$.
