At which point of the curve $\gamma(t) = (t^2,\; 2t)$, $t \in [0, 3]$, is the tangent vector parallel to the vector $(1, 1)$?

- $(1,\; 1)$
- $\left( \dfrac{1}{4},\; 1 \right)$
- $(4,\; 4)$
- $(1,\; 2)$

## Explanation

We have $\gamma'(t) = (2t,\; 2)$. A vector is parallel to $(1, 1)$ when its two components are equal, so the condition is $2t = 2$, that is, $t = 1$.

The corresponding point is $\gamma(1) = (1,\; 2)$, where the tangent vector equals $(2,\; 2)$. The point $(1, 1)$ does not belong to the curve, since the first component $t^2 = 1$ requires $t = 1$ and then the second one equals $2$.
