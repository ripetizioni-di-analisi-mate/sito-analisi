What is the center of mass of the homogeneous wire lying along the semicircle centered at the origin with radius $2$ contained in the half-plane $y \geq 0$?

- $\left( 0,\; \dfrac{2}{\pi} \right)$
- $\left( 0,\; \dfrac{8}{3\pi} \right)$
- $\left( 0,\; \dfrac{4}{\pi} \right)$
- $(0,\; 1)$

## Explanation

The trace is symmetric with respect to the $y$-axis, so the center of mass belongs to the $y$-axis and $\bar{x} = 0$.

With the parametrization $\gamma(t) = (2\cos t,\; 2\sin t)$, $t \in [0, \pi]$, we have $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = 2$, therefore the length is $L = 2\pi$ and
$$
\int_\gamma y \, ds = \int_0^\pi 2\sin t \cdot 2 \, dt = 8 .
$$
The wire is homogeneous, so $\bar{y} = \dfrac{1}{L} \displaystyle\int_\gamma y \, ds = \dfrac{8}{2\pi} = \dfrac{4}{\pi}$, and the center of mass is $G = \left( 0,\; \dfrac{4}{\pi} \right)$.
