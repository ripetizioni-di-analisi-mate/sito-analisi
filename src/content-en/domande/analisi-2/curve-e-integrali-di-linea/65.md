What is $\int_\gamma (x^2+y^2)\,ds$ along $\left( 3 \cos{\left(t \right)}, \  3 \sin{\left(t \right)}\right)$ for $0\leq t\leq\pi/2$?

- $27 \pi$
- $\dfrac{27 \pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9 \pi}{2}$
- $54 \pi$

## Explanation

On the circle the density is $9$ and the speed is $3$. The parameter interval has length $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
