What is the work of $F=(-y,x)$ along $\left( 4 \cos{\left(t \right)}, \  4 \sin{\left(t \right)}\right)$ from $t=\pi$ to $t=0$?

- $16 \pi$
- $0$
- $- 16 \pi$
- $- 32 \pi$

## Explanation

The field’s dot product with the derivative of the parametrization is constant at $16$. The endpoints are in decreasing order.
