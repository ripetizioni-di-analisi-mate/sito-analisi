Which parametric equation describes the tangent line to the helix
$$
\gamma(t) = (\cos t,\; \sin t,\; t), \qquad t \in [0, 2\pi],
$$
at the point $\gamma\!\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)$?

- $r(s) = \left( -s,\; 1,\; \dfrac{\pi}{2} + s \right)$, $s \in \mathbb{R}$
- $r(s) = \left( s,\; 1,\; \dfrac{\pi}{2} + s \right)$, $s \in \mathbb{R}$
- $r(s) = \left( -s,\; 1 + s,\; \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)$, $s \in \mathbb{R}$
- $r(s) = (-s,\; 1,\; s)$, $s \in \mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

For $t = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ we have $\cos t = 0$ and $\sin t = 1$, so the point of tangency is $\gamma\!\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right) = \left( 0,\; 1,\; \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)$.

Since $\gamma'(t) = (-\sin t,\; \cos t,\; 1)$, the tangent vector at that point is $\gamma'\!\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right) = (-1,\; 0,\; 1)$. The tangent line passes through the point of tangency with this direction, therefore
$$
r(s) = \left( 0,\; 1,\; \frac{\pi}{2} \right) + s\,(-1,\; 0,\; 1) = \left( -s,\; 1,\; \frac{\pi}{2} + s \right).
$$
