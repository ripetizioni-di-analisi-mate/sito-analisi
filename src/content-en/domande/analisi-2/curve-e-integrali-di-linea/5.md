## Explanation

Differentiating the components we obtain $\gamma'(t) = \left( -2\sin t,\; 2\cos t,\; \sqrt{5} \right)$. Since $4\sin^2 t + 4\cos^2 t = 4$, we have
$$
\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = \sqrt{4\sin^2 t + 4\cos^2 t + 5} = \sqrt{9} = 3
$$
for every $t$.

By the **length formula** the length of the helix is therefore
$$
L(\gamma) = \int_0^{4\pi} 3 \, dt = 12\pi .
$$
