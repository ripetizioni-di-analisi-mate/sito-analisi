What is the trace of the curve
$$
\gamma(t) = (1 + 2\cos t,\; -1 + 2\sin t), \qquad t \in [0, 2\pi]?
$$

- The circle with center $(1, -1)$ and radius $2$
- The circle with center $(-1, 1)$ and radius $2$
- The circle with center $(1, -1)$ and radius $4$
- The circle with center $(1, -1)$ and radius $\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The components satisfy $x - 1 = 2\cos t$ and $y + 1 = 2\sin t$. Since $\cos^2 t + \sin^2 t = 1$, every point of the trace satisfies
$$
(x - 1)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 4 .
$$
This is the equation of the circle with center $(1, -1)$ and radius $\sqrt{4} = 2$. As $t$ varies in $[0, 2\pi]$ the angle makes a full turn, so the trace is **the whole circle with center $(1, -1)$ and radius $2$**.
