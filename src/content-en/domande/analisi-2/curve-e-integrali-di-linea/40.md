What is the work of the field $F(x, y, z) = (y,\; z,\; x)$ along the helix
$$
\gamma(t) = (\cos t,\; \sin t,\; t), \qquad t \in [0, 2\pi]?
$$

- $\pi$
- $0$
- $-\pi$
- $-2\pi$

## Explanation

Along the curve $F(\gamma(t)) = (\sin t,\; t,\; \cos t)$ and $\gamma'(t) = (-\sin t,\; \cos t,\; 1)$, so
$$
F(\gamma(t)) \cdot \gamma'(t) = -\sin^2 t + t\cos t + \cos t .
$$
We have $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi} \sin^2 t \, dt = \pi$ and $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi} \cos t \, dt = 0$. Integrating by parts, $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi} t\cos t \, dt = \bigl[ t\sin t + \cos t \bigr]_0^{2\pi} = 0$.

By the definition of the **line integral of the second kind** the work is therefore $-\pi + 0 + 0 = -\pi$.
