The curve
$$
\gamma(t) = (3t^2,\; 4t^2), \qquad t \in [0, 1],
$$
has a segment as its trace. How long is the path traveled?

- $5$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $25$

## Explanation

Differentiating the components gives $\gamma'(t) = (6t,\; 8t)$, so for $t \geq 0$
$$
\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = \sqrt{36t^2 + 64t^2} = 10t .
$$

By the **length formula**
$$
L(\gamma) = \int_0^1 10t \, dt = 5 .
$$

The result agrees with the trace, because the curve traverses once the segment from $(0, 0)$ to $(3, 4)$, which has length $5$. The value $10$ is the speed at the instant $t = 1$.
