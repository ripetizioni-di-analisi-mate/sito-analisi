The curve $\gamma(t)=(t^2,t^4)$, for $-1\le t\le1$, traces an arc of $y=x^2$. If $L$ is the geometric length of that arc, find $\int_{-1}^1|\gamma'(t)|\,dt$.

- $L$
- $0$
- $2L$
- $\dfrac{L}{2}$

## Explanation

The first half of the parameter interval goes from $(1,1)$ to $(0,0)$, and the second retraces the arc in reverse. Speed is nonnegative, so the two lengths add.
