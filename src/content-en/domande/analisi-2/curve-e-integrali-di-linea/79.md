A point moves in a straight line from the origin to the point $(3, 1)$ under the action of the constant force $F = (2,\; -1)$. What is the work done by the force?

- $5$
- $7$
- $5\sqrt{2}$
- $-5$

## Explanation

The segment is parametrized by $\gamma(t) = (3t,\; t)$, $t \in [0, 1]$, so $\gamma'(t) = (3, 1)$.

The dot product of the field and the tangent vector is constant and equals
$$
F \cdot \gamma'(t) = 2 \cdot 3 + (-1) \cdot 1 = 5 .
$$

Therefore the work is $\displaystyle\int_0^1 5 \, dt = 5$, that is, the dot product of the force and the displacement $(3, 1)$.
