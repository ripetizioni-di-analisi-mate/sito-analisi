Which statement is true for the curve
$$
\gamma(t) = (\cos t,\; \sin 2t), \qquad t \in [0, 2\pi]?
$$

- The curve is closed and simple
- The curve is neither closed nor simple
- The curve is closed and not simple
- The curve is not closed and is simple

## Explanation

We have $\gamma(0) = (1, 0)$ and $\gamma(2\pi) = (1, 0)$, so the final point coincides with the initial one and the curve is **closed**.

For $t = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and for $t = \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$ we get $\gamma(t) = (0, 0)$, since $\cos t = 0$ and $\sin 2t = 0$ in both cases. The two values belong to $[0, 2\pi)$, therefore $\gamma$ is not injective on $[0, 2\pi)$ and the curve is **not simple**. The trace is a figure eight that passes twice through the origin.
