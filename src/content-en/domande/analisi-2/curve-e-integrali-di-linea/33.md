What is the value of the line integral $\displaystyle\int_\gamma x \, ds$ along the arc of parabola $\gamma(t) = (t,\; t^2)$, $t \in [0, 1]$?

- $\dfrac{5\sqrt{5} - 1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{5\sqrt{5}}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5\sqrt{5} - 1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

We have $\gamma'(t) = (1,\; 2t)$, so $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = \sqrt{1 + 4t^2}$; along the curve $x = t$. By the definition of **line integral of the first kind** we get $\displaystyle\int_\gamma x \, ds = \int_0^1 t\sqrt{1 + 4t^2} \, dt$.

The integral reduces to the standard integral $\displaystyle\int t\,(1 + 4t^2)^{\frac{1}{2}} \, dt = \frac{1}{12}(1 + 4t^2)^{\frac{3}{2}} + c$, therefore
$$
\int_\gamma x \, ds = \frac{1}{12}\left( 5^{\frac{3}{2}} - 1 \right) = \frac{5\sqrt{5} - 1}{12} .
$$
