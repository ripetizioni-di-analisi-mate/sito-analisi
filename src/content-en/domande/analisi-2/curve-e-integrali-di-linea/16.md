The curve
$$
\gamma(t) = (t^2 - 1,\; t^3 - t), \qquad t \in [-2, 2],
$$
passes twice through the same point. Which one?

- $(-1,\; 0)$
- $(0,\; 0)$
- $(3,\; 6)$
- $(0,\; -1)$

## Explanation

We look for two values $t \neq u$ with $\gamma(t) = \gamma(u)$. Equating the first components gives $t^2 = u^2$, hence $u = -t$ with $t \neq 0$.

With $u = -t$, equating the second components gives $t^3 - t = -(t^3 - t)$, that is, $t^3 - t = 0$. Since $t \neq 0$, we get $t = 1$ or $t = -1$, therefore the two parameter values are $1$ and $-1$. We have $\gamma(1) = \gamma(-1) = (0, 0)$, so the point traversed twice is **the origin**.
