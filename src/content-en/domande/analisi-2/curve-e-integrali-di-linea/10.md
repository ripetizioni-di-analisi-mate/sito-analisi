## Explanation

The mass of the wire is the integral of the first kind of the density, $m = \displaystyle\int_\gamma \delta \, ds$. Since $\gamma'(t) = (3, 4)$, we have $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$; along the segment $x = 3t$, so $\delta(\gamma(t)) = 3t$.

We therefore obtain
$$
m = \int_0^1 3t \cdot 5 \, dt = 15 \int_0^1 t \, dt = \frac{15}{2}.
$$
