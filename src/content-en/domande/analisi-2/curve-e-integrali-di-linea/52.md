A point moves along the curve
$$
\gamma(t) = \left( e^t,\; e^{-t},\; \sqrt{2}\, t \right), \qquad t \in [-1, 1].
$$
What is the minimum value of its speed $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert$?

- $\sqrt{2}$
- $1$
- $2$
- $\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Differentiating the components gives $\gamma'(t) = \left( e^t,\; -e^{-t},\; \sqrt{2} \right)$.

Hence
$$
\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert^2 = e^{2t} + e^{-2t} + 2 = \left( e^t + e^{-t} \right)^2 ,
$$
therefore $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = e^t + e^{-t} = 2\cosh t$.

The function $\cosh t$ attains its minimum at $t = 0$, where it equals $1$, so the minimum speed is $2$.
