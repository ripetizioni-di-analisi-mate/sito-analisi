Along the arc of parabola
$$
\gamma(t) = (2t,\; t^2), \qquad t \in [0, 1],
$$
the linear density of a wire is $\delta(x, y) = \sqrt{1 + y}$. What is the mass of the wire?

- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}$

## Explanation

We have $\gamma'(t) = (2,\; 2t)$, so $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = 2\sqrt{1 + t^2}$.

Along the curve $y = t^2$, therefore $\delta(\gamma(t)) = \sqrt{1 + t^2}$.

The product of the two square roots is $1 + t^2$, hence
$$
m = \int_0^1 2\left( 1 + t^2 \right) dt = 2 + \frac{2}{3} = \frac{8}{3} .
$$
