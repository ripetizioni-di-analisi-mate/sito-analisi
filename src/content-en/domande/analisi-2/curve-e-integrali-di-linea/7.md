For the curve
$$
\gamma(t) = (3t,\; 4t), \qquad t \in [0, 1],
$$
what is the arc length parametrization?

- $\tilde{\gamma}(s) = \left( \dfrac{3s}{5},\; \dfrac{4s}{5} \right)$, $s \in [0, 5]$
- $\tilde{\gamma}(s) = \left( \dfrac{3s}{5},\; \dfrac{4s}{5} \right)$, $s \in [0, 1]$
- $\tilde{\gamma}(s) = (15s,\; 20s)$, $s \in \left[ 0, \dfrac{1}{5} \right]$
- $\tilde{\gamma}(s) = \left( \dfrac{3s}{7},\; \dfrac{4s}{7} \right)$, $s \in [0, 7]$

## Explanation

We have $\gamma'(t) = (3, 4)$, so $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$. The **arc length** is $s(t) = \displaystyle\int_0^t 5 \, d\tau = 5t$, which varies in $[0, 5]$ as $t$ varies in $[0, 1]$.

Inverting, we obtain $t(s) = \dfrac{s}{5}$. Substituting this expression into the components of $\gamma$ we find the arc length parametrization $\tilde{\gamma}(s) = \left( \dfrac{3s}{5},\; \dfrac{4s}{5} \right)$, with $s \in [0, 5]$.
