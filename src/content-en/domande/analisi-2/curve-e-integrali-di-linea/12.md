## Explanation

Along the curve $x = t$ and $y = t^2$, so $F(\gamma(t)) = (t^4,\; t)$. Since $\gamma'(t) = (1,\; 2t)$, the dot product equals
$$
F(\gamma(t)) \cdot \gamma'(t) = t^4 \cdot 1 + t \cdot 2t = t^4 + 2t^2 .
$$
By the definition of the **line integral of the second kind** the work is therefore
$$
\int_\gamma F \cdot d\gamma = \int_0^1 \left( t^4 + 2t^2 \right) dt = \frac{1}{5} + \frac{2}{3} = \frac{13}{15}.
$$
