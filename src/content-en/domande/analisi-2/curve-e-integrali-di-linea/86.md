At what positive value of t is the arc length function measured from t=0 equal to $4$ along $\left( 2 \cos{\left(t \right)}, \  2 \sin{\left(t \right)}, \  7 t\right)$?

- $4 \sqrt{53}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $\dfrac{4 \sqrt{53}}{53}$

## Explanation

The arc length function is the product of the parameter and the constant norm of the derivative. Divide the prescribed length by the speed.
