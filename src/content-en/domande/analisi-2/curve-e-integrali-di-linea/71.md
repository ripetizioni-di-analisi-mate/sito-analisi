At what positive value of t is the arc length function measured from t=0 equal to $3$ along $\left( 5 \cos{\left(t \right)}, \  5 \sin{\left(t \right)}, \  2 t\right)$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3 \sqrt{29}}{29}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $3 \sqrt{29}$

## Explanation

The arc length function is the product of the parameter and the constant norm of the derivative. Divide the prescribed length by the speed.
