Let $\gamma$ be the graph of the function $y = \ln x$ for $x \in \left[ 1, \sqrt{3} \right]$. What is $\displaystyle\int_\gamma x^2 \, ds$?

- $\dfrac{8 - 2\sqrt{2}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{16 - 4\sqrt{2}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3\sqrt{3} - 1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$

## Explanation

The graph is the trace of the Cartesian curve $\gamma(t) = (t,\; \ln t)$, $t \in \left[ 1, \sqrt{3} \right]$. We have $\gamma'(t) = \left( 1,\; \dfrac{1}{t} \right)$, hence for $t > 0$
$$
\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = \sqrt{1 + \frac{1}{t^2}} = \frac{\sqrt{t^2 + 1}}{t} .
$$

Along the curve $x^2 = t^2$, therefore the integrand is $t^2 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{t^2 + 1}}{t} = t\sqrt{t^2 + 1}$.

The integral reduces to the standard integral $\displaystyle\int t\,(t^2 + 1)^{\frac{1}{2}} \, dt = \frac{1}{3}(t^2 + 1)^{\frac{3}{2}} + c$, hence
$$
\int_\gamma x^2 \, ds = \frac{1}{3}\left( 4^{\frac{3}{2}} - 2^{\frac{3}{2}} \right) = \frac{8 - 2\sqrt{2}}{3} .
$$
