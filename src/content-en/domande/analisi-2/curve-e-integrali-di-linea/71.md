At what positive t is the arc length from t=0 equal to $3$ along $\left( 5 \cos{\left(t \right)}, \  5 \sin{\left(t \right)}, \  2 t\right)$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3 \sqrt{29}}{29}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $3 \sqrt{29}$

## Explanation

The arc length is the parameter times the constant speed. Divide the prescribed length by the speed.
