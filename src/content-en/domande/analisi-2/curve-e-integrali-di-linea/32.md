Let $\gamma$ be the circle centered at the origin with radius $2$, traversed once. What is the value of
$$
\int_\gamma (x^2 + y^2) \, ds \, ?
$$

- $16\pi$
- $8\pi$
- $32\pi$
- $4\pi$

## Explanation

We parametrize the circle by $\gamma(t) = (2\cos t,\; 2\sin t)$, $t \in [0, 2\pi]$. We have $\gamma'(t) = (-2\sin t,\; 2\cos t)$, so $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = 2$; along the curve $x^2 + y^2 = 4$.

By the definition of the **line integral of the first kind** we therefore get
$$
\int_\gamma (x^2 + y^2) \, ds = \int_0^{2\pi} 4 \cdot 2 \, dt = 16\pi .
$$
