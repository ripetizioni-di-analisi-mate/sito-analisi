What is the length for $0\leq t\leq 7$ of the curve $\left( 4 \cos{\left(t \right)}, \  4 \sin{\left(t \right)}, \  2 t\right)$?

- $14 \sqrt{5}$
- $28$
- $42$
- $2 \sqrt{5}$

## Explanation

The squared speed is $20$ because the sine and cosine contribute the sum of their squares. The speed is constant.
