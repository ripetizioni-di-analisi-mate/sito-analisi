For the curve
$$
\gamma(t) = (t^2 + 1,\; 2t^3), \qquad t \in [1, 2],
$$
what is the tangent vector at the point $\gamma(1)$?

- $(2,\; 2)$
- $(2,\; 3)$
- $(2,\; 6)$
- $(4,\; 24)$

## Explanation

The tangent vector at the point $\gamma(t_0)$ is $\gamma'(t_0)$, which is obtained by differentiating the components one at a time. Since the derivative of $t^2 + 1$ is $2t$ and the derivative of $2t^3$ is $6t^2$, we have $\gamma'(t) = (2t,\; 6t^2)$.

For $t \in [1, 2]$ the first component satisfies $2t \geq 2$, so $\gamma'(t)$ never vanishes and the curve is **regular**. The point $\gamma(1) = (2, 2)$ corresponds to the parameter value $t = 1$, therefore the tangent vector is $\gamma'(1) = (2,\; 6)$.
