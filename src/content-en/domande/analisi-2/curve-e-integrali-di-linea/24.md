What is the length of the arc of cycloid
$$
\gamma(t) = (t - \sin t,\; 1 - \cos t), \qquad t \in [0, 2\pi]?
$$

- $4$
- $2\pi$
- $16$
- $8$

## Explanation

We have $\gamma'(t) = (1 - \cos t,\; \sin t)$, so
$$
\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert^2 = 1 - 2\cos t + \cos^2 t + \sin^2 t = 2 - 2\cos t = 4\sin^2 \frac{t}{2} .
$$
For $t \in [0, 2\pi]$ we have $\dfrac{t}{2} \in [0, \pi]$, where the sine is nonnegative, therefore $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = 2\sin \dfrac{t}{2}$.

By the **length formula** we get
$$
L(\gamma) = \int_0^{2\pi} 2\sin \frac{t}{2} \, dt = \left[ -4\cos \frac{t}{2} \right]_0^{2\pi} = 4 + 4 = 8 .
$$
