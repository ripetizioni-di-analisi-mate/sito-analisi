What is the length of the arc of the spiral
$$
\gamma(t) = (e^t \cos t,\; e^t \sin t), \qquad t \in [0, 1]?
$$

- $\sqrt{2}\,(e - 1)$
- $e - 1$
- $\sqrt{2}\, e$
- $2\,(e - 1)$

## Explanation

By the product rule we get $\gamma'(t) = \bigl( e^t(\cos t - \sin t),\; e^t(\sin t + \cos t) \bigr)$. Since $(\cos t - \sin t)^2 + (\sin t + \cos t)^2 = 2$, we have
$$
\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = \sqrt{2 e^{2t}} = \sqrt{2}\, e^t .
$$
By the **length formula** we therefore have
$$
L(\gamma) = \int_0^1 \sqrt{2}\, e^t \, dt = \sqrt{2}\,(e - 1).
$$
