## Explanation

The circle traversed counterclockwise is parametrized by $\gamma(t) = (2\cos t,\; 2\sin t)$, $t \in [0, 2\pi]$. We have $F(\gamma(t)) = (-2\sin t,\; 2\cos t)$ and $\gamma'(t) = (-2\sin t,\; 2\cos t)$, so
$$
F(\gamma(t)) \cdot \gamma'(t) = 4\sin^2 t + 4\cos^2 t = 4 .
$$
By the definition of the **line integral of the second kind** the work is $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi} 4 \, dt = 8\pi$. The value is positive because at every point of the curve the field has the same direction and the same orientation as the velocity vector.
