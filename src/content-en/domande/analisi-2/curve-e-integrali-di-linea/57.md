Which point is reached after arc length $1$ from $t=0$ in the increasing-$t$ direction along $\left( 2 t, \  3 t\right)$?

- $\left( \dfrac{3 \sqrt{13}}{13}, \  \dfrac{2 \sqrt{13}}{13}\right)$
- $\left( - \dfrac{2 \sqrt{13}}{13}, \  - \dfrac{3 \sqrt{13}}{13}\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  3\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{2 \sqrt{13}}{13}, \  \dfrac{3 \sqrt{13}}{13}\right)$

## Explanation

The speed is constant at $\sqrt{13}$. The corresponding time is its reciprocal.
