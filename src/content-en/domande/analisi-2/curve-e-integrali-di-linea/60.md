What is $\int_\gamma (x\,dx+y\,dy)$ along $\left( 2 t, \  3 t^{2}\right)$ from $t=0$ to $t=1$?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{13}{2}$
- $\dfrac{13}{2}$
- $13$

## Explanation

The form is the differential of $(x^2+y^2)/2$. Evaluate it at the endpoints $\left( 0, \  0\right)$ and $\left( 2, \  3\right)$.
