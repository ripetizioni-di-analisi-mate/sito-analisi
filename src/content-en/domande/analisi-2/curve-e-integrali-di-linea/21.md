For which values of the real parameter $a$ is the curve
$$
\gamma(t) = (t^2 - a t,\; t^3 - 3t), \qquad t \in [-2, 2],
$$
regular?

- For $a \neq \pm 1$
- For $a \neq \pm 2$
- For $a \neq 0$
- For every $a \in \mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

The curve is of class $C^1$ and $\gamma'(t) = (2t - a,\; 3t^2 - 3)$. The second component vanishes only for $t = 1$ and for $t = -1$, both interior to $[-2, 2]$, so $\gamma'$ can vanish only at these two values.

We have $\gamma'(1) = (2 - a,\; 0)$, which is zero for $a = 2$, and $\gamma'(-1) = (-2 - a,\; 0)$, which is zero for $a = -2$. Therefore the curve is **regular for $a \neq \pm 2$**.
