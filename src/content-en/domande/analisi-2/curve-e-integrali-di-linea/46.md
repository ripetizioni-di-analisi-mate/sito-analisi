Which of the following points belongs to the trace of the curve
$$
\gamma(t) = (1 + 2t,\; 3 - t), \qquad t \in [0, 1] ?
$$

- $\left( 2,\; \dfrac{5}{2} \right)$
- $(5,\; 1)$
- $(-1,\; 4)$
- $(2,\; 2)$

## Explanation

A point $(x_0, y_0)$ belongs to the trace if there exists $t \in [0, 1]$ with $\gamma(t) = (x_0, y_0)$.

For the point $\left( 2,\; \dfrac{5}{2} \right)$ the first equation $1 + 2t = 2$ gives $t = \dfrac{1}{2}$, and indeed $3 - \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{5}{2}$. Since $\dfrac{1}{2} \in [0, 1]$, the point belongs to the trace.

The points $(5, 1)$ and $(-1, 4)$ correspond to $t = 2$ and $t = -1$, which do not belong to the parameter interval, so they lie on the line but not on the segment. The point $(2, 2)$ does not even belong to the line, because for $t = \dfrac{1}{2}$ the second coordinate equals $\dfrac{5}{2}$.
