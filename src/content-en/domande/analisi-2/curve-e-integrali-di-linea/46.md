What is the unit tangent vector at $t=1$ to the curve $\left( 2 t, \  3 t^{2}, \  5 t^{3}\right)$?

- $\left( \dfrac{2 \sqrt{265}}{265}, \  \dfrac{6 \sqrt{265}}{265}, \  \dfrac{3 \sqrt{265}}{53}\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  6, \  15\right)$
- $\left( - \dfrac{2 \sqrt{265}}{265}, \  - \dfrac{6 \sqrt{265}}{265}, \  - \dfrac{3 \sqrt{265}}{53}\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{2}{265}, \  \dfrac{6}{265}, \  \dfrac{3}{53}\right)$

## Explanation

The derivative at that point is $\left( 2, \  6, \  15\right)$. Divide the vector by its norm.
