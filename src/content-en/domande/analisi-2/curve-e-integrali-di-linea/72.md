Which point is reached after arc length $1$ from $t=0$ in the increasing-$t$ direction along $\left( 3 t, \  5 t\right)$?

- $\left( \dfrac{3 \sqrt{34}}{34}, \  \dfrac{5 \sqrt{34}}{34}\right)$
- $\left( - \dfrac{3 \sqrt{34}}{34}, \  - \dfrac{5 \sqrt{34}}{34}\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  5\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{5 \sqrt{34}}{34}, \  \dfrac{3 \sqrt{34}}{34}\right)$

## Explanation

The speed is constant at $\sqrt{34}$. The corresponding time is its reciprocal.
