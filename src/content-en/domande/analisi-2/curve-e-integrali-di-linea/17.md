Which of the following curves, all defined for $t \in [-1, 1]$, is not regular?

- $\gamma(t) = (t,\; t^3)$
- $\gamma(t) = (\sin t,\; t)$
- $\gamma(t) = (e^t,\; t^2)$
- $\gamma(t) = (t^3,\; t^2)$

## Explanation

The four curves are of class $C^1$, so regularity depends on whether $\gamma'(t)$ vanishes at the interior points of $[-1, 1]$.

For $(t,\; t^3)$ and for $(\sin t,\; t)$ one component of $\gamma'(t)$ is constantly equal to $1$, therefore $\gamma'(t)$ never vanishes. For $(e^t,\; t^2)$ the first component of $\gamma'(t)$ is $e^t > 0$, so this curve is regular as well.

For $\gamma(t) = (t^3,\; t^2)$ we have $\gamma'(t) = (3t^2,\; 2t)$, which equals $(0, 0)$ for $t = 0$, an interior point of the interval. Therefore this curve is **not regular**.
