At what positive value of t is the arc length function measured from t=0 equal to $2$ along $\left( 3 \cos{\left(t \right)}, \  3 \sin{\left(t \right)}, \  5 t\right)$?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{34}}{17}$
- $2 \sqrt{34}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The arc length function is the product of the parameter and the constant norm of the derivative. Divide the prescribed length by the speed.
