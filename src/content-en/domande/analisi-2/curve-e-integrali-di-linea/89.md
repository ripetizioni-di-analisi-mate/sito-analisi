The work of a continuous field $F$ along a curve $\gamma_1$ going from the point $A$ to the point $B$ equals $3$; along another curve $\gamma_2$, also from $A$ to $B$, it equals $1$. What is the work along the closed curve that goes from $A$ to $B$ along $\gamma_1$ and comes back from $B$ to $A$ along the trace of $\gamma_2$?

- $4$
- $0$
- $-2$
- $2$

## Explanation

The closed curve is piecewise regular and consists of $\gamma_1$ followed by the reversed curve $-\gamma_2$, so the work is the sum of the two contributions.

The work along $\gamma_1$ equals $3$. The work along $-\gamma_2$ is the opposite of the work along $\gamma_2$, therefore it equals $-1$.

The work along the closed curve hence equals $3 - 1 = 2$.
