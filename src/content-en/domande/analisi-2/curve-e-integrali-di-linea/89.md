What is the mass of the wire $\left( 4 t, \  2 t, \  7 t\right)$ for $0\leq t\leq1$ with density $\rho=x+y+z$?

- $\dfrac{13 \sqrt{69}}{2}$
- $\sqrt{69}$
- $13 \sqrt{69}$
- $\dfrac{13}{2}$

## Explanation

Along the wire the density is $13 t$. The length element is $\sqrt{69}$ times $dt$.
