What is the value of the integral
$$
\int_\gamma y \, dx - x \, dy
$$
along the semicircle $\gamma(t) = (\cos t,\; \sin t)$, $t \in [0, \pi]$?

- $\pi$
- $-\pi$
- $0$
- $-2\pi$

## Explanation

Along the curve $x = \cos t$ and $y = \sin t$, so $dx$ is replaced by $x'(t)\, dt = -\sin t \, dt$ and $dy$ by $y'(t)\, dt = \cos t \, dt$.

We get
$$
y \, dx - x \, dy = \left( -\sin^2 t - \cos^2 t \right) dt = -dt ,
$$
therefore
$$
\int_\gamma y \, dx - x \, dy = \int_0^\pi (-1) \, dt = -\pi .
$$
