A field does work $6$ on one anticlockwise traversal of a circle. A curve traverses the circle anticlockwise twice and clockwise once, then follows a segment and retraces it. What is the total work?

- $18$
- $12$
- $0$
- $6$

## Explanation

For a position-dependent field, reversing the orientation negates the work. By additivity the loops give $6+6-6=6$, and the two opposite traversals of the segment cancel.
