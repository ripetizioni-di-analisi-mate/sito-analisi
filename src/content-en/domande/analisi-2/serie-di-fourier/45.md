For the $\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x) = x$, $x \in [0, \pi)$, what is the value of the Fourier coefficient $b_1$?

- $1$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-2$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The period is $2L = \pi$, so $L = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $\dfrac{n\pi x}{L} = 2nx$. Since the integral over one period does not depend on the starting point, we can integrate over $[0, \pi]$, and
$$
b_1 = \frac{1}{L} \int_0^{\pi} x \sin(2x)\, dx = \frac{2}{\pi} \int_0^{\pi} x \sin(2x)\, dx .
$$
Integrating by parts gives
$$
\int_0^{\pi} x \sin(2x)\, dx = \left[ -\frac{x \cos(2x)}{2} \right]_0^{\pi} + \int_0^{\pi} \frac{\cos(2x)}{2}\, dx = -\frac{\pi}{2} ,
$$
therefore $b_1 = \dfrac{2}{\pi} \cdot \left( -\dfrac{\pi}{2} \right) = -1$.
