For the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x) = \pi - |x|$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi]$, what is the value of the Fourier coefficient $a_1$?

- $\dfrac{2}{\pi}$
- $-\dfrac{4}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{4}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The function is **even**, so the series contains only cosines and $a_1 = \dfrac{2}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} (\pi - x) \cos x\, dx$, since on $[0, \pi]$ we have $|x| = x$. Integrating by parts with $\pi - x$ as the factor to differentiate we obtain
$$
\int_0^{\pi} (\pi - x) \cos x\, dx = \bigl[ (\pi - x) \sin x \bigr]_0^{\pi} + \int_0^{\pi} \sin x\, dx = 0 + 2 = 2 .
$$
Therefore $a_1 = \dfrac{2}{\pi} \cdot 2 = \dfrac{4}{\pi}$.
