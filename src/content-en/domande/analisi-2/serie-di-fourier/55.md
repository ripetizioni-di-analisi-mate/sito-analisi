What is the value of the integral of the product $\cos(2x) \cos(5x)$ over the period $[0, 2\pi]$?

- $\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $2\pi$
- $0$

## Explanation

The function $\cos(2x) \cos(5x)$ is $2\pi$-periodic, and the integral over one period does not depend on the starting point. Hence the integral over $[0, 2\pi]$ coincides with the one over $[-\pi, \pi]$. By the **orthogonality of the trigonometric system** with $n = 2 \neq m = 5$ the latter is zero, therefore the integral equals $0$.
