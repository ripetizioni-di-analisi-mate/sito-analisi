The $2$-periodic extension of $f(x) = x$, $x \in [-1, 1)$, has Fourier coefficients $a_n = 0$ and $b_n = \dfrac{2\,(-1)^{n+1}}{n\pi}$. What is $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_n^2$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2\pi^2}{3}$

## Explanation

The period is $2L = 2$, so $L = 1$ and **Parseval's identity** has $\dfrac{1}{L} \displaystyle\int_{-L}^{L} f(x)^2\, dx$ on the left. Since $a_n = 0$ for every $n$, we have $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_n^2 = \int_{-1}^{1} x^2\, dx = \frac{2}{3}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{3}$ would be obtained by dividing by $2L$ instead of $L$.
