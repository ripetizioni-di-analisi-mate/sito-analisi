Which of the following functions, defined on $[-\pi, \pi)$ and extended $2\pi$-periodically, is *not* piecewise smooth?

- $\sqrt{|x|}$
- $|x|$
- $x^3$
- $e^{-x}$

## Explanation

A piecewise smooth function has a continuous derivative on finitely many open intervals, with *finite* one-sided limits of $f$ and of $f'$ at the endpoints. For $\sqrt{|x|}$ on $(0, \pi)$ we have $f'(x) = \dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}$, and $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0^+} \frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}} = +\infty$, so at $x = 0$ the right limit of $f'$ is not finite and $\sqrt{|x|}$ **is not piecewise smooth**. The functions $|x|$, $x^3$ and $e^{-x}$ have continuous derivative on $(-\pi, 0)$ and on $(0, \pi)$, with finite one-sided limits. Their extensions may have a jump at $\pm\pi$, and the definition allows finitely many jumps in one period.
