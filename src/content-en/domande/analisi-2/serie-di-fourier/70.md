For the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x)=3|x|$ on $[-\pi,\pi]$, what is the coefficient $a_1$?

- $0$
- $- \dfrac{6}{\pi}$
- $- \dfrac{12}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{12}{\pi}$

## Explanation

The integrand for the coefficient is even. Doubling the integral from zero to $\pi$, integration by parts gives a contribution of $-2$ from the integral of $x\cos x$.
