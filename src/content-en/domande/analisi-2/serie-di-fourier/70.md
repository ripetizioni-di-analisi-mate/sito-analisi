For the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x)=3|x|$ on $[-\pi,\pi]$, what is coefficient $a_1$?

- $0$
- $- \dfrac{6}{\pi}$
- $- \dfrac{12}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{12}{\pi}$

## Explanation

The coefficient integrand is even. Double the integral from zero to $\pi$, where integration by parts gives $-2$ from $x\cos x$.
