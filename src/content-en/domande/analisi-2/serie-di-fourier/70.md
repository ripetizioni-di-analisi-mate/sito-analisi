Writing $f(x) = \sin\left( x + \dfrac{\pi}{3} \right)$ as a trigonometric polynomial, which coefficient $b_1$ is obtained?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

By the addition formula for the sine
$$
\sin\left( x + \frac{\pi}{3} \right) = \sin x \cos\frac{\pi}{3} + \cos x \sin\frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{1}{2} \sin x + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \cos x .
$$
Hence $f$ is a **trigonometric polynomial** of degree $1$, and its Fourier coefficients coincide with the coefficients of this expression. Therefore $b_1 = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $a_1 = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.
