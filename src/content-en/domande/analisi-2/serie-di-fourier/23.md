For the function $f(x) = |\sin x|$, what is the value of the Fourier coefficient $a_2$?

- $\dfrac{4}{3\pi}$
- $0$
- $-\dfrac{2}{3\pi}$
- $-\dfrac{4}{3\pi}$

## Explanation

The function is **even** and on $[0, \pi]$ it coincides with $\sin x$, so $a_2 = \dfrac{2}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} \sin x \cos(2x)\, dx$. By the product-to-sum formula $\sin x \cos(2x) = \dfrac{\sin(3x) - \sin x}{2}$, and since $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} \sin(3x)\, dx = \dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} \sin x\, dx = 2$, we get
$$
\int_0^{\pi} \sin x \cos(2x)\, dx = \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{2}{3} - 2 \right) = -\frac{2}{3} .
$$
Therefore $a_2 = \dfrac{2}{\pi} \cdot \left( -\dfrac{2}{3} \right) = -\dfrac{4}{3\pi}$.
