The function $f(x) = |\sin x|$ is periodic with period $\pi$. Regarding it as a function of period $2L = \pi$, what is the coefficient $a_1$ of its Fourier series expansion?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{4}{3\pi}$
- $-\dfrac{4}{15\pi}$
- $-\dfrac{4}{3\pi}$

## Explanation

The period is $2L = \pi$, so $L = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and the cosines of the expansion are $\cos\!\left( \dfrac{n\pi x}{L} \right) = \cos(2nx)$. The function is **even** and on $\left[ 0, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right]$ it coincides with $\sin x$, therefore
$$
a_1 = \frac{2}{L} \int_0^{L} \sin x \cos(2x)\, dx = \frac{4}{\pi} \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin x \cos(2x)\, dx .
$$
By the product-to-sum formula $\sin x \cos(2x) = \dfrac{\sin(3x) - \sin x}{2}$, so the integral equals $\dfrac{1}{2} \left[ -\dfrac{\cos(3x)}{3} + \cos x \right]_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} = \dfrac{1}{2} \left( 0 - \dfrac{2}{3} \right) = -\dfrac{1}{3}$. Therefore $a_1 = -\dfrac{4}{3\pi}$, the same value as the coefficient of $\cos(2x)$ in the expansion with period $2\pi$.
