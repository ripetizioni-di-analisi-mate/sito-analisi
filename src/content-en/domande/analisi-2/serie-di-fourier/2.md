For the $2\pi$-periodic extension of
$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
0 & \text{if } -\pi \leq x < 0 \\
2 & \text{if } 0 \leq x < \pi
\end{cases}
$$
what is the value of the constant term $\dfrac{a_0}{2}$ of the Fourier series?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

The constant term of the series is the **mean** of $f$ over one period, that is, $\dfrac{a_0}{2} = \dfrac{1}{2\pi} \displaystyle\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x)\, dx$. Since $f$ equals $0$ on $[-\pi, 0)$, the integral reduces to the interval $[0, \pi)$, where $f$ equals $2$, so
$$
\frac{a_0}{2} = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_0^{\pi} 2\, dx = \frac{2\pi}{2\pi} = 1 .
$$
Therefore $a_0 = 2$ and the constant term equals $1$.
