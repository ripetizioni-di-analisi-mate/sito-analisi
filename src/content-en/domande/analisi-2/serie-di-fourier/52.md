To what value at $x=\pi$ does the Fourier series of the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x)=2 x + 5$ on $(-\pi,\pi)$ converge?

- $5$
- $0$
- $5 + 2 \pi$
- $5 - 2 \pi$

## Explanation

The one-sided limits at the jump are $5 + 2 \pi$ and $5 - 2 \pi$, since beyond $\pi$ the period restarts at $-\pi$. The series takes their mean.
