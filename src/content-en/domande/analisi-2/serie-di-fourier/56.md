What is the least positive period of $2 \cos{\left(2 x \right)} + 3 \cos{\left(4 x \right)}$?

- $\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $2 \pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The harmonics have frequencies two and four. A period must reproduce both, and the least one is the period of the frequency-two harmonic.
