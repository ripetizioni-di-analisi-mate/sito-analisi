The half-wave rectifier is the $2\pi$-periodic function that equals $\sin x$ on $[0, \pi)$ and $0$ on $[-\pi, 0)$. What is its Fourier coefficient $b_1$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{2}{\pi}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The function is zero on $[-\pi, 0)$, so $b_1 = \dfrac{1}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} \sin^2 x\, dx$. Since $\sin^2 x = \dfrac{1 - \cos(2x)}{2}$ and the integral of $\cos(2x)$ over $[0, \pi]$ is zero, we have $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} \sin^2 x\, dx = \frac{\pi}{2}$. Therefore $b_1 = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
