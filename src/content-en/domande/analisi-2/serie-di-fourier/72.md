Let $g(x) = f(x + \pi)$ be the translate of the sawtooth wave $f$, the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $x$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi)$, whose Fourier coefficients are $a_n = 0$ and $b_n = \dfrac{2\,(-1)^{n+1}}{n}$. What is the coefficient $b_3$ of $g$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

With the substitution $t = x + \pi$ we obtain
$$
b_n(g) = \frac{1}{\pi} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x + \pi) \sin(nx)\, dx = \frac{1}{\pi} \int_{0}^{2\pi} f(t) \sin(nt - n\pi)\, dt .
$$
Since $\sin(nt - n\pi) = (-1)^n \sin(nt)$ and the integral over one period does not depend on the starting point, we have $b_n(g) = (-1)^n\, b_n(f)$. For $n = 3$ it follows that $b_3(g) = -b_3(f) = -\dfrac{2}{3}$, because $b_3(f) = \dfrac{2}{3}$.
