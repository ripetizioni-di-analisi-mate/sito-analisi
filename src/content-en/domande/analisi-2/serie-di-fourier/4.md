What is the value of the Fourier coefficient $b_3$ of $f(x) = \sin^3 x$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

From the triple-angle formula $\sin(3x) = 3 \sin x - 4 \sin^3 x$ we obtain
$$
\sin^3 x = \frac{3}{4} \sin x - \frac{1}{4} \sin(3x) .
$$
So $f$ is a **trigonometric polynomial**, and by the proposition on the coefficients of a trigonometric polynomial its Fourier coefficients coincide with the coefficients of this expression. Therefore $b_1 = \dfrac{3}{4}$, $b_3 = -\dfrac{1}{4}$ and all the other coefficients are zero, without computing any integral.
