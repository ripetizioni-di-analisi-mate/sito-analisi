Let $f$ be the $2\pi$-periodic extension of
$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
x^2 & \text{se } -\pi \leq x < 0 \\
1 & \text{se } 0 \leq x < \pi
\end{cases}
$$
What is the sum of the Fourier series of $f$ at $x = \pi$?

- $\pi^2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1 + \pi^2}{2}$

## Explanation

The function is piecewise smooth, so by the **pointwise convergence theorem** the series converges at every point to the mean $\dfrac{f(x^+) + f(x^-)}{2}$. The left limit at $x = \pi$ is $f(\pi^-) = 1$, since to the left of $\pi$ the function equals $1$. By periodicity the right limit at $\pi$ coincides with the right limit at $-\pi$, so $f(\pi^+) = \displaystyle\lim_{x \to -\pi^+} x^2 = \pi^2$. Therefore the sum of the series at $x = \pi$ equals $\dfrac{1 + \pi^2}{2}$.
