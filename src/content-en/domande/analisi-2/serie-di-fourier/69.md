For the $2\pi$-periodic step with value $3$ on $(-\pi,0)$ and $5$ on $(0,\pi)$, what is $b_1$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{4}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{2}{\pi}$
- $- \dfrac{4}{\pi}$

## Explanation

The sine integrals over the two halves are negative two and two, respectively. Weight them by the step levels and divide by $\pi$.
