For a function $f$ periodic with period $6$, which cosines appear, with $n \geq 1$, in the Fourier series of $f$?

- $\cos\left( \dfrac{n\pi x}{6} \right)$
- $\cos(3n\pi x)$
- $\cos\left( \dfrac{n\pi x}{3} \right)$
- $\cos\left( \dfrac{nx}{6} \right)$

## Explanation

The period is $2L = 6$, so $L = 3$. The Fourier series with period $2L$ contains the terms $\cos\left( \dfrac{n\pi x}{L} \right)$ and $\sin\left( \dfrac{n\pi x}{L} \right)$, therefore the cosines are $\cos\left( \dfrac{n\pi x}{3} \right)$. Each of them has period $\dfrac{6}{n}$, and hence also period $6$. The function $\cos\left( \dfrac{n\pi x}{6} \right)$ with $n = 1$ has period $12$, therefore it is not $6$-periodic.
