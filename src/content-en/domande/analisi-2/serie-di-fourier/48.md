Let $f$ be the $2\pi$-periodic rectangular pulse that equals $1$ for $|x| \leq \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $0$ for $\dfrac{\pi}{2} < |x| \leq \pi$. What is the Fourier coefficient $a_1$?

- $\dfrac{1}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{2}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{4}{\pi}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The function is zero outside $\left[ -\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right]$, so the integral defining $a_1$ reduces to this interval and
$$
a_1 = \frac{1}{\pi} \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos x\, dx = \frac{1}{\pi} \bigl[ \sin x \bigr]_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} = \frac{2}{\pi} .
$$
The coefficient is positive, because $\cos x$ is positive on the interval where the pulse equals $1$.
