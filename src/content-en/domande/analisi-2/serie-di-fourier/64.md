With period $2\pi$, what is the sine coefficient at frequency 10 for $3 \sin{\left(5 x \right)} \cos{\left(5 x \right)}$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $3$
- $- \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The double-angle identity turns the product into half of $\sin(10x)$. The coefficient is half the original factor.
