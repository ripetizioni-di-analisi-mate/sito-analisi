A $2\pi$-periodic integrable function $f$ satisfies $\displaystyle\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x) \cos(3x)\, dx = 6$. What is the Fourier coefficient $a_3$?

- $6$
- $\dfrac{3}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{2}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{6}{\pi}$

## Explanation

By definition $a_3 = \dfrac{1}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x) \cos(3x)\, dx$, hence $a_3 = \dfrac{6}{\pi}$. The factor $\dfrac{1}{\pi}$ comes from orthogonality, because the integral of $\cos^2(3x)$ over $[-\pi, \pi]$ equals $\pi$. The value $\dfrac{3}{\pi}$ would be obtained by dividing by $2\pi$, as one does for the constant term $\dfrac{a_0}{2}$.
