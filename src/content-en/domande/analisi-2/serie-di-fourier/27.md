Let $f$ be the $2\pi$-periodic extension of
$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
\cos x & \text{if } -\pi \leq x < 0 \\
\sin x & \text{if } 0 \leq x < \pi
\end{cases}
$$
What is the sum of the Fourier series of $f$ at $x = \pi$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-1$
- $0$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

On each of the intervals $(-\pi, 0)$ and $(0, \pi)$ the function coincides with a differentiable function with continuous derivative, with finite one-sided limits at the endpoints, so $f$ is piecewise smooth and the **pointwise convergence theorem** applies. The left limit at $x = \pi$ is $f(\pi^-) = \sin\pi = 0$. By periodicity the right limit at $\pi$ coincides with the right limit at $-\pi$, that is, $f(\pi^+) = \cos(-\pi) = -1$. Therefore the sum of the series at $x = \pi$ equals $\dfrac{0 + (-1)}{2} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.
