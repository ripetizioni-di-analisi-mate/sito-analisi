The $2\pi$-periodic extension of
$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
-1 & \text{se } -\pi \leq x < 0 \\
1 & \text{se } 0 \leq x < \pi
\end{cases}
$$
has Fourier series $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{4}{(2k+1)\pi} \sin\bigl( (2k+1)x \bigr)$. Evaluating it at $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, what is the sum of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^k}{2k+1}$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{4}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{\pi^2}{8}$

## Explanation

The function is piecewise smooth and is continuous at $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, where it equals $1$, so by the **pointwise convergence theorem** the series at that point has sum $1$. Since $\sin\left( (2k+1) \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right) = (-1)^k$, we obtain
$$
1 = \frac{4}{\pi} \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^k}{2k+1} .
$$
Therefore the sum of the series equals $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
