Let $f$ be the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x) = x^3$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi)$. What is the sum of the Fourier series of $f$ at $x = \pi$?

- $\pi^3$
- $\dfrac{\pi^3}{2}$
- $0$
- $-\pi^3$

## Explanation

The function $x^3$ is differentiable with continuous derivative on $(-\pi, \pi)$, with finite one-sided limits at the endpoints, so the extension is piecewise smooth and the **pointwise convergence theorem** applies. The left limit at $x = \pi$ is $\pi^3$. By periodicity the right limit at $\pi$ coincides with the right limit at $-\pi$, that is, $(-\pi)^3 = -\pi^3$. Therefore the sum of the series at $x = \pi$ is the mean $\dfrac{\pi^3 + (-\pi^3)}{2} = 0$.
