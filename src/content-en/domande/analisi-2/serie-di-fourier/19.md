For the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x) = e^x$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi)$, what is the value of the Fourier coefficient $a_0$?

- $\dfrac{e^{\pi} - e^{-\pi}}{2\pi}$
- $\dfrac{e^{\pi} - e^{-\pi}}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{e^{\pi} + e^{-\pi}}{\pi}$
- $e^{\pi} - e^{-\pi}$

## Explanation

By definition $a_0 = \dfrac{1}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x)\, dx$, and on $[-\pi, \pi)$ the function coincides with $e^x$. Since an antiderivative of $e^x$ is $e^x$, we get
$$
a_0 = \frac{1}{\pi} \bigl[ e^x \bigr]_{-\pi}^{\pi} = \frac{e^{\pi} - e^{-\pi}}{\pi} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{e^{\pi} - e^{-\pi}}{2\pi}$ is the constant term $\dfrac{a_0}{2}$ of the series, that is, the mean of $f$ over one period.
