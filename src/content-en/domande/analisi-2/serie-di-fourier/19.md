Let $f$ be a $2\pi$-periodic function integrable on $[-\pi, \pi]$ and let $g(x) = f(x) + 5$. What is the difference $a_0(g) - a_0(f)$ between the coefficients $a_0$ of the two functions?

- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $10$
- $10\pi$

## Explanation

The coefficient $a_0$ is $\dfrac{1}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x)\, dx$ and depends linearly on the function. Hence $a_0(g) - a_0(f) = \dfrac{1}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} 5\, dx = \dfrac{10\pi}{\pi} = 10$. Therefore the constant term $\dfrac{a_0}{2}$ increases by $5$, like the function. For $n \geq 1$ the integrals of $5\cos(nx)$ and of $5\sin(nx)$ over $[-\pi, \pi]$ are zero, so the other coefficients of $g$ coincide with those of $f$.
