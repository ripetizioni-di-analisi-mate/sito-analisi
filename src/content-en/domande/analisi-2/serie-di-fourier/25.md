Let $f$ be the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x) = x|x|$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi)$. Which Fourier coefficients of $f$ are certainly zero?

- All the coefficients $b_n$
- Only $a_0$
- All the coefficients $a_n$, with $n \geq 0$
- The coefficients $a_n$ and $b_n$ with $n$ even

## Explanation

For $x \in (-\pi, \pi)$ we have $f(-x) = -x\,|{-x}| = -x|x| = -f(x)$, and the value at a single point does not change the integrals. So the coefficients are computed as for an **odd** function, and by the proposition on sine-only series $a_n = 0$ for every $n \geq 0$. The coefficients $b_n$ are in general different from zero, for example
$$
b_2 = \frac{2}{\pi} \int_0^{\pi} x^2 \sin(2x)\, dx = -\pi .
$$
Therefore the Fourier series of $f$ is a **sine-only** series.
