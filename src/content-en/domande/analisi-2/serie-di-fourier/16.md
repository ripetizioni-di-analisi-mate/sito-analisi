The product $f(x) = \sin x \, \bigl( 1 + 2\cos x + 2\cos(2x) \bigr)$ is a trigonometric polynomial. What is its coefficient $b_1$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $-1$
- $2$

## Explanation

We expand the product term by term. By the double-angle formula $2 \sin x \cos x = \sin(2x)$, and by the product-to-sum formula $2 \sin x \cos(2x) = \sin(3x) + \sin(-x) = \sin(3x) - \sin x$. Hence
$$
f(x) = \sin x + \sin(2x) + \sin(3x) - \sin x = \sin(2x) + \sin(3x) .
$$
The term $\sin x$ cancels with the term $-\sin x$ coming from $2 \sin x \cos(2x)$. Therefore $f$ is a **trigonometric polynomial** with $b_2 = b_3 = 1$ and $b_1 = 0$.
