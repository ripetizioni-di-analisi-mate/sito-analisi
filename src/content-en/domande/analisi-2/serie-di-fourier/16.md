What is the value of the Fourier coefficient $b_2$ of the function $f(x) = 2 \sin x \cos(3x)$?

- $1$
- $-1$
- $0$
- $-2$

## Explanation

By the product-to-sum formula $\sin\alpha \cos\beta = \dfrac{\sin(\alpha + \beta) + \sin(\alpha - \beta)}{2}$, with $\alpha = x$ and $\beta = 3x$ we have
$$
2 \sin x \cos(3x) = \sin(4x) + \sin(-2x) = \sin(4x) - \sin(2x) .
$$
So $f$ is a **trigonometric polynomial**, and its Fourier coefficients coincide with the coefficients of this expression. Therefore $b_4 = 1$, $b_2 = -1$ and all the other coefficients are zero.
