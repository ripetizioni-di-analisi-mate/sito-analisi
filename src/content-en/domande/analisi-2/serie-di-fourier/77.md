The function $p(x) = 7 - \cos x + 2\sin(3x)$ is differentiated. What Fourier coefficient $a_3$ does the derivative $p'$ have?

- $6$
- $2$
- $-6$
- $0$

## Explanation

For every $n \geq 1$ the derivative of $\cos(nx)$ is $-n \sin(nx)$ and the derivative of $\sin(nx)$ is $n \cos(nx)$, while the derivative of the constant is zero. Hence
$$
p'(x) = \sin x + 6 \cos(3x) .
$$
The derivative is again a **trigonometric polynomial**, and its Fourier coefficients coincide with the coefficients of this expression. Therefore $a_3 = 6$ and $b_1 = 1$, and all other coefficients of $p'$ are zero.
