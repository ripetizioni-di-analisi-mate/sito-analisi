Let $f$ be the function of period $4$ that coincides with $|x|$ on $[-2, 2]$. What is $a_0$ in its Fourier series expansion with period $4$?

- $2$
- $1$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{2}{\pi}$

## Explanation

The period is $2L = 4$, so $L = 2$ and $a_0 = \dfrac{1}{L} \displaystyle\int_{-L}^{L} f(x)\, dx = \frac{1}{2} \int_{-2}^{2} |x|\, dx$. The integral of $|x|$ over $[-2, 2]$ is the sum of the areas of two triangles with base $2$ and height $2$, that is $4$. Therefore $a_0 = 2$, and the constant term $\dfrac{a_0}{2} = 1$ is the mean of $f$ over one period.
