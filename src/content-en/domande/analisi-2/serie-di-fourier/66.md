What is coefficient $b_{5}$ for the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x)=3 x + 2$ on $(-\pi,\pi)$?

- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{6}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The constant times sine is odd. Integration by parts of $x\sin(5x)$ leaves the boundary contribution from the cosine term.
