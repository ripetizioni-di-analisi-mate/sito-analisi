What is the coefficient $b_{5}$ for the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x)=3 x + 2$ on $(-\pi,\pi)$?

- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{6}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The product of the constant term and the sine is odd. Integrating $x\sin(5x)$ by parts leaves the boundary values of the term containing the cosine.
