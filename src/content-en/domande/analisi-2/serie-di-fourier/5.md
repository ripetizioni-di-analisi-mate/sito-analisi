For the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x) = \dfrac{x}{2}$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi)$, what is the value of the Fourier coefficient $b_2$?

- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-1$
- $-\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The function is **odd**, so $a_n = 0$ and
$$
b_n = \frac{2}{\pi} \int_0^{\pi} \frac{x}{2} \sin(nx)\, dx = \frac{1}{\pi} \int_0^{\pi} x \sin(nx)\, dx .
$$
Integrating by parts, and since $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} \cos(nx)\, dx = 0$, we obtain
$$
\int_0^{\pi} x \sin(nx)\, dx = \left[ -\frac{x \cos(nx)}{n} \right]_0^{\pi} = -\frac{\pi\, (-1)^n}{n} ,
$$
so $b_n = \dfrac{(-1)^{n+1}}{n}$. For $n = 2$ we therefore have $b_2 = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.
