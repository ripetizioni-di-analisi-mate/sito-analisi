To what value at $x=\pi$ does the Fourier series of the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x)=3 x + 2$ on $(-\pi,\pi)$ converge?

- $2$
- $2 + 3 \pi$
- $2 - 3 \pi$
- $0$

## Explanation

The one-sided limits at the jump are $2 + 3 \pi$ and $2 - 3 \pi$, since beyond $\pi$ the period restarts at $-\pi$. The series takes their mean.
