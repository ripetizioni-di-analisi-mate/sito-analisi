What is the degree of the trigonometric polynomial $p(x) = 3 + 2\cos x - \sin(4x)$?

- $1$
- $3$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The degree of a trigonometric polynomial is the largest integer $n$ for which at least one of the coefficients $a_n$, $b_n$ is nonzero. In $p$ we find $\cos x$, with $a_1 = 2$, and $\sin(4x)$, with $b_4 = -1$, and no term of frequency greater than $4$. Therefore the degree of $p$ is $4$.
