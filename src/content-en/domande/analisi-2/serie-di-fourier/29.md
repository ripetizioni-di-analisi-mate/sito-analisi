The $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x) = \pi - |x|$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi]$, has Fourier series $\dfrac{\pi}{2} + \displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{4}{\pi (2k+1)^2} \cos\bigl( (2k+1)x \bigr)$. Evaluating it at $x = 0$, what is the sum of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(2k+1)^2}$?

- $\dfrac{\pi^2}{8}$
- $\dfrac{\pi^2}{6}$
- $\dfrac{\pi^2}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi^2}{16}$

## Explanation

The extension is continuous, because $f(-\pi) = f(\pi) = 0$, and it is piecewise smooth, so by the **pointwise convergence theorem** the series at $x = 0$ has sum $f(0) = \pi$. Since $\cos 0 = 1$, we get
$$
\pi = \frac{\pi}{2} + \frac{4}{\pi} \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(2k+1)^2} .
$$
Solving for the series gives $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(2k+1)^2} = \frac{\pi}{4} \cdot \frac{\pi}{2} = \frac{\pi^2}{8}$.
