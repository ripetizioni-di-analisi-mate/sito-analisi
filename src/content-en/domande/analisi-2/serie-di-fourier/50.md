What is $\dfrac1\pi\int_{-\pi}^\pi f(x)^2\,dx$ if $f(x)=5 \sin{\left(4 x \right)} + 2 \cos{\left(3 x \right)}$?

- $49$
- $\dfrac{29}{2}$
- $29$
- $7$

## Explanation

The two harmonics are orthogonal, and each has squared norm $\pi$ without its coefficient. The squares of the coefficients are added.
