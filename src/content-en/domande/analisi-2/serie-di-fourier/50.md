The Fourier series of the sawtooth wave, that is of the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $x$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi)$, is evaluated at $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{2}$. To which value does it converge?

- $\dfrac{5\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The extension is piecewise smooth, so the **pointwise convergence theorem** applies. The jumps are at the points $\pi + 2k\pi$, therefore the function is continuous at $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{2}$ and the series converges to $f\left( \dfrac{5\pi}{2} \right)$. By periodicity $f\left( \dfrac{5\pi}{2} \right) = f\left( \dfrac{5\pi}{2} - 2\pi \right) = f\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right) = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The value $\dfrac{5\pi}{2}$ is that of the function $x$, which outside $[-\pi, \pi)$ differs from the extension.
