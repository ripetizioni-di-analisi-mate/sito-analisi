What is the coefficient $b_{3}$ for the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x)=2 x + 5$ on $(-\pi,\pi)$?

- $0$
- $- \dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The product of the constant term and the sine is odd. Integrating $x\sin(3x)$ by parts leaves the boundary values of the term containing the cosine.
