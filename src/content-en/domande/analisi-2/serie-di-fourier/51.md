What is coefficient $b_{3}$ for the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x)=2 x + 5$ on $(-\pi,\pi)$?

- $0$
- $- \dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The constant times sine is odd. Integration by parts of $x\sin(3x)$ leaves the boundary contribution from the cosine term.
