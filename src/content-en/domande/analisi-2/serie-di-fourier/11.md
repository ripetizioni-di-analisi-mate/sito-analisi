A period-four sawtooth equal to $x$ on $[-2,2)$ is expressed using $\theta=\dfrac{\pi x}{2}$. Which angular frequency in $x$ corresponds to the third harmonic $\sin(3\theta)$?

- $3\pi$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $6\pi$

## Explanation

Substitution gives $\sin(3\theta)=\sin((\dfrac{3\pi}{2})x)$. The coefficient of $x$ is the angular frequency, three times the fundamental frequency $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
