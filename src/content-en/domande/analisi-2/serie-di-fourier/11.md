For the $4$-periodic extension of $f(x) = x$, $x \in [-2, 2)$, what is the value of the Fourier coefficient $b_2$?

- $\dfrac{2}{\pi}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{\pi}$
- $-1$
- $-\dfrac{1}{\pi}$

## Explanation

The period is $2L = 4$, so $L = 2$. The function is **odd** and the simplifications for odd functions also hold with period $2L$, therefore $a_n = 0$ and
$$
b_n = \frac{2}{L} \int_0^{L} x \sin\!\left( \frac{n\pi x}{L} \right) dx = \int_0^{2} x \sin\!\left( \frac{n\pi x}{2} \right) dx .
$$
Integrating by parts, and since the integral of $\cos\!\left( \dfrac{n\pi x}{2} \right)$ over $[0, 2]$ is zero, we obtain
$$
b_n = \left[ -\frac{2x}{n\pi} \cos\!\left( \frac{n\pi x}{2} \right) \right]_0^{2} = -\frac{4 \cos(n\pi)}{n\pi} = \frac{4\,(-1)^{n+1}}{n\pi} .
$$
For $n = 2$ it follows that $b_2 = -\dfrac{4}{2\pi} = -\dfrac{2}{\pi}$.
