For the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x) = x^2 + \sin(3x)$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi]$, what is the value of the Fourier coefficient $b_3$?

- $1$
- $0$
- $-\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{5}{9}$

## Explanation

The Fourier coefficients depend linearly on the function, so $b_3$ is the sum of the coefficients $b_3$ of $x^2$ and of $\sin(3x)$. The function $x^2$ is even, therefore its product with $\sin(3x)$ is odd and its contribution to $b_3$ is zero. The function $\sin(3x)$ is a trigonometric polynomial whose only coefficient is $b_3 = 1$. So $b_3 = 0 + 1 = 1$. The value $-\dfrac{4}{9}$ is the coefficient $a_3$ of $x^2$, which contributes only to the cosines.
