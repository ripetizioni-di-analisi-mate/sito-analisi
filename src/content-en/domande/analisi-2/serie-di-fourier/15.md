Which trigonometric polynomial coincides with the function $f(x) = \sin^4 x - \cos^4 x$?

- $\cos(2x)$
- $-2\cos(2x)$
- $-\cos(2x)$
- $1 - \cos(2x)$

## Explanation

The difference of two squares factors as $\sin^4 x - \cos^4 x = (\sin^2 x - \cos^2 x)(\sin^2 x + \cos^2 x)$. Since $\sin^2 x + \cos^2 x = 1$, we get $f(x) = \sin^2 x - \cos^2 x$. By the double-angle formula $\cos(2x) = \cos^2 x - \sin^2 x$, hence $f(x) = -\cos(2x)$. Therefore $f$ is the **trigonometric polynomial** of degree $2$ with $a_2 = -1$ and all other coefficients equal to zero.
