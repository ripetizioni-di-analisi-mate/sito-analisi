What is the value of the Fourier coefficient $a_0$ of the function $f(x) = \cos^4 x$?

- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

Applying the formula $\cos^2 t = \dfrac{1 + \cos(2t)}{2}$ twice gives
$$
\cos^4 x = \frac{\bigl( 1 + \cos(2x) \bigr)^2}{4} = \frac{1}{4} + \frac{\cos(2x)}{2} + \frac{1 + \cos(4x)}{8} = \frac{3}{8} + \frac{1}{2} \cos(2x) + \frac{1}{8} \cos(4x) .
$$
So $f$ is a **trigonometric polynomial**, and its Fourier coefficients coincide with the coefficients of this expression. The constant term of the expansion is $\dfrac{a_0}{2} = \dfrac{3}{8}$, therefore $a_0 = \dfrac{3}{4}$.
