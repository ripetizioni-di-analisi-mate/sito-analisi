Two periodic functions coincide except at one point per period, where their values are 0 and 1 respectively. Can the Fourier series of both functions converge uniformly to the corresponding function?

- Yes, because one point does not affect the sup norm.
- Yes, because the constant coefficients differ by 1.
- No, because all coefficients must vanish.
- No, because their partial sums are identical and cannot have two different uniform limits.

## Explanation

Changing an isolated value leaves the Fourier coefficient integrals unchanged. The two sequences of partial sums coincide. Uniform convergence to both functions would force them to agree even at the given point.
