What is the coefficient $b_{2}$ for the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x)=4 x + 7$ on $(-\pi,\pi)$?

- $-2$
- $0$
- $4$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The product of the constant term and the sine is odd. Integrating $x\sin(2x)$ by parts leaves the boundary values of the term containing the cosine.
