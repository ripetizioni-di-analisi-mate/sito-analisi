Let $f$ be a $2\pi$-periodic function with Fourier coefficients $a_n$, $b_n$, and let $g(x) = f(\pi x)$, which is periodic with period $2$. What are the Fourier coefficients of $g$ in the expansion with period $2$?

- $\dfrac{a_n}{\pi}$ and $\dfrac{b_n}{\pi}$
- The same $a_n$ and $b_n$ as $f$
- $\pi a_n$ and $\pi b_n$
- $\dfrac{a_n}{2}$ and $\dfrac{b_n}{2}$

## Explanation

The period of $g$ is $2L = 2$, so $L = 1$ and $a_n(g) = \displaystyle\int_{-1}^{1} f(\pi x) \cos(n\pi x)\, dx$. The **substitution** $t = \pi x$, with $dx = \dfrac{dt}{\pi}$, turns the integral into $\dfrac{1}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(t) \cos(nt)\, dt = a_n$. In the same way $b_n(g) = b_n$. Therefore $g$ has the same coefficients as $f$, and its series is obtained from that of $f$ by writing $\pi x$ in place of $x$.
