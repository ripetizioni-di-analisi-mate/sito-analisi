Let $f$ be the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x) = x^2 + cx$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi)$, with $c \in \mathbb{R}$. For which value of $c$ does the Fourier series of $f$ converge uniformly on $\mathbb{R}$?

- $c = \pi$
- $c = -\pi$
- $c = \pi^2$
- $c = 0$

## Explanation

The extension is piecewise smooth for every $c$, because on $(-\pi, \pi)$ it coincides with a polynomial. The extension is continuous at $\pm\pi$ if and only if the values at the endpoints coincide, that is, $\pi^2 - c\pi = \pi^2 + c\pi$, hence if and only if $c = 0$. For $c = 0$ the **uniform convergence theorem** applies, therefore the series converges uniformly on $\mathbb{R}$. For $c \neq 0$ the extension has a jump, and since a uniform limit of continuous functions is continuous, uniform convergence on $\mathbb{R}$ is impossible.
