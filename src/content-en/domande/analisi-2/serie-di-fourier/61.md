Let $f$ be a $2\pi$-periodic integrable function with $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi} f(x)\, dx = 5$. What is $\displaystyle\int_3^{3 + 2\pi} f(x)\, dx$?

- $2$
- $10$
- $0$
- $5$

## Explanation

The interval $[3, 3 + 2\pi]$ has length $2\pi$, that is one period of $f$. For an integrable periodic function the integral over one period does not depend on the starting point, hence $\displaystyle\int_3^{3 + 2\pi} f(x)\, dx = \int_0^{2\pi} f(x)\, dx$. Therefore the integral equals $5$.
