For the $6$-periodic extension of $f(x) = x^2$, $x \in [-3, 3]$, what is the value of the Fourier coefficient $a_1$?

- $-\dfrac{36}{\pi^2}$
- $\dfrac{36}{\pi^2}$
- $-4$
- $-\dfrac{12}{\pi^2}$

## Explanation

The period is $2L = 6$, so $L = 3$. The function is **even**, therefore $b_n = 0$ and
$$
a_1 = \frac{2}{3} \int_0^{3} x^2 \cos\!\left( \frac{\pi x}{3} \right) dx .
$$
With the substitution $t = \dfrac{\pi x}{3}$ we have $x = \dfrac{3t}{\pi}$ and $dx = \dfrac{3}{\pi}\, dt$. Since two integrations by parts give $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} t^2 \cos t\, dt = -2\pi$, it follows that
$$
a_1 = \frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{27}{\pi^3} \int_0^{\pi} t^2 \cos t\, dt = \frac{18}{\pi^3} \cdot (-2\pi) = -\frac{36}{\pi^2} .
$$
