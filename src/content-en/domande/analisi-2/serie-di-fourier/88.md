What is the coefficient $b_1$ of the Fourier series of $e^x$ on the interval $[-\pi, \pi)$, that is of its $2\pi$-periodic extension?

- $\dfrac{e^{\pi} - e^{-\pi}}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{e^{\pi} - e^{-\pi}}{2\pi}$
- $-\dfrac{e^{\pi} - e^{-\pi}}{2\pi}$
- $\dfrac{e^{\pi} - e^{-\pi}}{4\pi}$

## Explanation

By definition $b_1 = \dfrac{1}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} e^x \sin x\, dx$. Integrating by parts twice we obtain the antiderivative $\dfrac{e^x (\sin x - \cos x)}{2}$. Since $\sin(\pm\pi) = 0$ and $\cos(\pm\pi) = -1$, we have
$$
b_1 = \frac{1}{\pi} \left[ \frac{e^x (\sin x - \cos x)}{2} \right]_{-\pi}^{\pi} = \frac{1}{\pi} \left( \frac{e^{\pi}}{2} - \frac{e^{-\pi}}{2} \right) = \frac{e^{\pi} - e^{-\pi}}{2\pi} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{e^{\pi} - e^{-\pi}}{\pi}$ is obtained by forgetting the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of the antiderivative.
