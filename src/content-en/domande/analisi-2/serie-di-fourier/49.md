Which integral gives the coefficients $b_n$ of the Fourier series of $x^3$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi)$, extended with period $2\pi$?

- $\dfrac{1}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} x^3 \sin(nx)\, dx$
- $\dfrac{2}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} x^3 \cos(nx)\, dx$
- $\dfrac{2}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} x^3 \sin(nx)\, dx$
- $\dfrac{2}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} x^3 \sin(nx)\, dx$

## Explanation

For $|x| < \pi$ we have $(-x)^3 = -x^3$, and the value at the single point $x = -\pi$ does not change the integrals, so $f$ is treated as an **odd** function. The product $x^3 \sin(nx)$ is then even, and its integral over $[-\pi, \pi]$ is twice the integral over $[0, \pi]$. Therefore $b_n = \dfrac{1}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} x^3 \sin(nx)\, dx = \dfrac{2}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} x^3 \sin(nx)\, dx$, while all the $a_n$ are zero.
