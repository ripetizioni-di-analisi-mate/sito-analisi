Let $f$ be the $4$-periodic extension of $f(x) = x + x^2$, $x \in [-2, 2)$. What is the sum of the Fourier series of $f$ at $x = 2$?

- $6$
- $4$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The pointwise convergence theorem also holds with period $2L = 4$, and the extension is piecewise smooth because on $(-2, 2)$ it coincides with a polynomial. The left limit at $x = 2$ is $2 + 4 = 6$. By periodicity the right limit at $2$ coincides with the right limit at $-2$, that is, $-2 + 4 = 2$. Therefore by the **pointwise convergence theorem** the series at $x = 2$ converges to the mean $\dfrac{6 + 2}{2} = 4$.
