The function $f(x) = |\sin x|$ has Fourier coefficients $a_0 = \dfrac{4}{\pi}$, $a_{2n} = -\dfrac{4}{\pi (4n^2 - 1)}$ for $n \geq 1$, and all other coefficients equal to zero. Applying Parseval's identity, what is $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(4n^2 - 1)^2}$?

- $\dfrac{\pi^2 - 8}{8}$
- $\dfrac{\pi^2 - 8}{16}$
- $\dfrac{\pi^2 - 4}{16}$
- $\dfrac{\pi^2}{16}$

## Explanation

The function is bounded, so it is square integrable and **Parseval's identity** applies. The left-hand side equals $\dfrac{1}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin^2 x\, dx = \dfrac{\pi}{\pi} = 1$. The right-hand side equals
$$
\frac{a_0^2}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_{2n}^2 = \frac{8}{\pi^2} + \frac{16}{\pi^2} \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(4n^2 - 1)^2} .
$$
Therefore $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(4n^2 - 1)^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{16} \left( 1 - \frac{8}{\pi^2} \right) = \frac{\pi^2 - 8}{16}$.
