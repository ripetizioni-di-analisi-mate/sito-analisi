The $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x) = \pi - |x|$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi]$, has Fourier coefficients $a_0 = \pi$, $a_n = \dfrac{2 \bigl( 1 - (-1)^n \bigr)}{\pi n^2}$ for $n \geq 1$ and $b_n = 0$. Using Parseval's identity, what is the sum of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(2k+1)^4}$?

- $\dfrac{\pi^4}{90}$
- $\dfrac{\pi^4}{48}$
- $\dfrac{\pi^4}{96}$
- $\dfrac{\pi^4}{192}$

## Explanation

The coefficients $a_n$ are zero for $n$ even and equal $\dfrac{4}{\pi n^2}$ for $n$ odd. The left-hand side of **Parseval's identity** equals
$$
\frac{1}{\pi} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} (\pi - |x|)^2\, dx = \frac{2}{\pi} \int_0^{\pi} (\pi - x)^2\, dx = \frac{2}{\pi} \cdot \frac{\pi^3}{3} = \frac{2\pi^2}{3} .
$$
The right-hand side equals $\dfrac{\pi^2}{2} + \dfrac{16}{\pi^2} \displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(2k+1)^4}$, hence
$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(2k+1)^4} = \frac{\pi^2}{16} \left( \frac{2\pi^2}{3} - \frac{\pi^2}{2} \right) = \frac{\pi^2}{16} \cdot \frac{\pi^2}{6} = \frac{\pi^4}{96} .
$$
