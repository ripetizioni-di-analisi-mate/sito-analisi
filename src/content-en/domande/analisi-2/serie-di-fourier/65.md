To expand $f(x) = x$, $x \in [0, \pi]$, in a cosine-only series, one extends $f$ to $[-\pi, \pi]$ by an even function, then $2\pi$-periodically. Which function is obtained on $[-\pi, \pi]$?

- $|x|$
- $x$
- $-x$
- $\pi - |x|$

## Explanation

The extension $g$ must coincide with $x$ on $[0, \pi]$ and satisfy $g(-x) = g(x)$. For $x \in [-\pi, 0)$ we therefore have $g(x) = g(-x) = -x$, so $g(x) = |x|$ on $[-\pi, \pi]$. The function $x$ is the odd extension, which gives a sine-only series. The function $\pi - |x|$ is even, but on $[0, \pi]$ it does not coincide with $x$.
