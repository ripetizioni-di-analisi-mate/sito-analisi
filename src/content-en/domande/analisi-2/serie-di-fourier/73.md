A $2\pi$-periodic piecewise smooth function $f$ is continuous at $x = 0$ and has Fourier coefficients $a_0 = 2$ and $a_n = \dfrac{1}{n^2}$ for $n \geq 1$. What is $f(0)$?

- $2 + \dfrac{\pi^2}{6}$
- $1 + \dfrac{\pi^2}{6}$
- $\dfrac{\pi^2}{6}$
- $1 + \dfrac{\pi^2}{12}$

## Explanation

By the **pointwise convergence theorem** the Fourier series converges to $f(0)$, because $f$ is continuous at $0$. At $x = 0$ we have $\cos 0 = 1$ and $\sin 0 = 0$, so the coefficients $b_n$ play no role and
$$
f(0) = \frac{a_0}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n = 1 + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} .
$$
Since $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6}$, we obtain $f(0) = 1 + \dfrac{\pi^2}{6}$.
