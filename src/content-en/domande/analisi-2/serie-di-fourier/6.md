For the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x) = (x - 1)^2$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi)$, what is the value of the Fourier coefficient $b_1$?

- $4$
- $-2$
- $-4$
- $0$

## Explanation

Expanding the square we have $f(x) = x^2 - 2x + 1$, and the Fourier coefficients depend linearly on the function. The functions $x^2$ and $1$ are even, so their product with $\sin(nx)$ is odd and their contribution to $b_n$ is zero. For the sawtooth wave $x$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi)$, we have $b_n = \dfrac{2\,(-1)^{n+1}}{n}$, therefore for $-2x$ we obtain
$$
b_n = -2 \cdot \frac{2\,(-1)^{n+1}}{n} = \frac{4\,(-1)^n}{n} .
$$
For $n = 1$ it follows that $b_1 = -4$.
