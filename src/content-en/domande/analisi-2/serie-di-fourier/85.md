For the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x)=4|x|$ on $[-\pi,\pi]$, what is coefficient $a_1$?

- $- \dfrac{8}{\pi}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{16}{\pi}$
- $- \dfrac{16}{\pi}$

## Explanation

The coefficient integrand is even. Double the integral from zero to $\pi$, where integration by parts gives $-2$ from $x\cos x$.
