Let $f$ be the $2\pi$-periodic extension of
$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
0 & \text{se } -\pi \leq x < 0 \\
1 & \text{se } 0 \leq x < \pi
\end{cases}
$$
and let $a_n$, $b_n$ be its Fourier coefficients. What is the value of $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \bigl( a_n^2 + b_n^2 \bigr)$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The coefficient $a_0$ equals $\dfrac{1}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} 1\, dx = 1$. Since $f^2 = f$, the left-hand side of **Parseval's identity** is $\dfrac{1}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x)^2\, dx = \dfrac{1}{\pi} \cdot \pi = 1$. Hence
$$
1 = \frac{a_0^2}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \bigl( a_n^2 + b_n^2 \bigr) = \frac{1}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \bigl( a_n^2 + b_n^2 \bigr) ,
$$
therefore the required sum equals $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
