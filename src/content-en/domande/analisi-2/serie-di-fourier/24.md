For the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x) = x \sin x$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi]$, what is the value of the Fourier coefficient $a_1$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-1$
- $0$

## Explanation

The function is **even**, being the product of two odd functions, so $a_1 = \dfrac{2}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} x \sin x \cos x\, dx$. Since $\sin x \cos x = \dfrac{\sin(2x)}{2}$, we have $a_1 = \dfrac{1}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} x \sin(2x)\, dx$. Integrating by parts gives
$$
\int_0^{\pi} x \sin(2x)\, dx = \left[ -\frac{x \cos(2x)}{2} \right]_0^{\pi} + \int_0^{\pi} \frac{\cos(2x)}{2}\, dx = -\frac{\pi}{2} + 0 .
$$
Therefore $a_1 = \dfrac{1}{\pi} \cdot \left( -\dfrac{\pi}{2} \right) = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.
