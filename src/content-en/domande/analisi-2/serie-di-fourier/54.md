The trigonometric polynomial $p(x) = 2 - \cos(3x) + 4\sin x$ is written in the form $\dfrac{a_0}{2} + \displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{3} \bigl( a_n \cos(nx) + b_n \sin(nx) \bigr)$. What is $a_0$?

- $2$
- $1$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The constant term of the given form is $\dfrac{a_0}{2}$, and the constant term of $p$ is $2$. Hence $\dfrac{a_0}{2} = 2$, therefore $a_0 = 4$. The other nonzero coefficients are $a_3 = -1$ and $b_1 = 4$.
