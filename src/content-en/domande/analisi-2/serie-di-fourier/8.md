Let $f$ be a $2\pi$-periodic, odd, piecewise smooth function with right limit $f(0^+) = 3$. What is the sum of the Fourier series of $f$ at $x = 0$?

- $0$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $6$

## Explanation

Since $f$ is odd, we have $f(-x) = -f(x)$, so the left limit at $0$ is $f(0^-) = \displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0^+} f(-x) = -f(0^+) = -3$. The function is piecewise smooth, therefore the **pointwise convergence theorem** applies and at $x = 0$ the series converges to the mean $\dfrac{3 + (-3)}{2} = 0$. The result is consistent with the form of the series, because $f$ is odd, hence its series contains only sines and every term $b_n \sin(nx)$ vanishes at $x = 0$.
