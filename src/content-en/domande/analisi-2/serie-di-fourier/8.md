Let $f$ be the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x) = e^x$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi)$. What is the sum of the Fourier series of $f$ at $x = \pi$?

- $e^{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{e^{\pi} + e^{-\pi}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{e^{\pi} - e^{-\pi}}{2}$
- $e^{-\pi}$

## Explanation

The function $e^x$ is differentiable with a continuous derivative on $(-\pi, \pi)$, with finite one-sided limits at the endpoints, so the extension is piecewise smooth and the **pointwise convergence theorem** applies. The left limit at $x = \pi$ is $e^{\pi}$. By periodicity the right limit at $\pi$ coincides with the right limit at $-\pi$, that is, $e^{-\pi}$, so the extension has a jump at $\pi$. Therefore the sum of the series at $x = \pi$ is the mean of the two one-sided limits,
$$
\frac{e^{\pi} + e^{-\pi}}{2} = \cosh \pi .
$$
