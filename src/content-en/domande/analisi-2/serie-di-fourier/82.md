To what value at $x=\pi$ does the Fourier series of the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x)=4 x + 7$ on $(-\pi,\pi)$ converge?

- $7 - 4 \pi$
- $7 + 4 \pi$
- $7$
- $0$

## Explanation

The one-sided limits at the jump are $7 + 4 \pi$ and $7 - 4 \pi$, since beyond $\pi$ the period restarts at $-\pi$. The series takes their mean.
