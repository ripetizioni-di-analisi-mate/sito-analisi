Which of the following functions has Fourier coefficient $a_0 = 0$?

- $\cos^3 x$
- $\sin^2 x \cos^2 x$
- $\sin^4 x$
- $\cos^2 x$

## Explanation

By the formula $\cos^3 x = \dfrac{3\cos x + \cos(3x)}{4}$ the function $\cos^3 x$ is a trigonometric polynomial without constant term, so $a_0 = 0$. The other three functions are nonnegative and not identically zero, therefore their integral over $[-\pi, \pi]$ is positive and $a_0 > 0$. For instance $\sin^2 x \cos^2 x = \dfrac{\sin^2(2x)}{4} = \dfrac{1 - \cos(4x)}{8}$ has $a_0 = \dfrac{1}{4}$, and $\cos^2 x = \dfrac{1 + \cos(2x)}{2}$ has $a_0 = 1$.
