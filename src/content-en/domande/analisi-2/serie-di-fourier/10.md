- $\dfrac{\pi^7}{945}$
- $\dfrac{\pi^6}{1890}$
- $\dfrac{4\pi^6}{315}$
- $\dfrac{\pi^6}{945}$

## Explanation

The function is square integrable on $[-\pi, \pi]$ and its coefficients are $a_n = 0$ and $b_n = \dfrac{12\,(-1)^n}{n^3}$, so by **Parseval's identity**
$$
\frac{1}{\pi} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} (x^3 - \pi^2 x)^2\, dx = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{144}{n^6} .
$$
The integrand $x^6 - 2\pi^2 x^4 + \pi^4 x^2$ is even, therefore
$$
\frac{2}{\pi} \int_0^{\pi} \bigl( x^6 - 2\pi^2 x^4 + \pi^4 x^2 \bigr)\, dx = \frac{2}{\pi} \left( \frac{\pi^7}{7} - \frac{2\pi^7}{5} + \frac{\pi^7}{3} \right) = \frac{16\pi^6}{105} .
$$
Hence $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^6} = \frac{16\pi^6}{105 \cdot 144} = \frac{\pi^6}{945}$.
