For the $2\pi$-periodic step function with value $4$ on $(-\pi,0)$ and $2$ on $(0,\pi)$, what is $b_1$?

- $- \dfrac{2}{\pi}$
- $0$
- $- \dfrac{4}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{4}{\pi}$

## Explanation

The sine integrals over the two halves are negative two and two, respectively. Weight them by the values of the step function and divide by $\pi$.
