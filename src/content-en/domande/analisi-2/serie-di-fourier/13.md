What is the minimal period, that is, the smallest positive period, of the function $f(x) = \sin(2x) + \cos(3x)$?

- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
- $\pi$
- $2\pi$
- $6\pi$

## Explanation

The periods of $\sin(2x)$ are the positive integer multiples of $\pi$, and the periods of $\cos(3x)$ are the positive integer multiples of $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$. The smallest positive number belonging to both sets is $2\pi = 2 \cdot \pi = 3 \cdot \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, so the minimal period of the sum is $2\pi$. For example, $\pi$ is not a period, because $f(x + \pi) = \sin(2x) - \cos(3x)$, which is different from $f(x)$. The number $6\pi$ is a period of $f$, but it is a multiple of $2\pi$ and therefore it is not the minimal one.
