The inequality $2x + 6 < 0$ does not contain the variable $y$. Which half-plane does it describe in the Cartesian plane?

- The half-plane to the right of the line $x = -3$, line excluded
- The half-plane to the left of the line $x = 3$, line excluded
- The half-plane to the left of the line $x = -3$, line excluded
- The half-plane below the line $y = -3$, line excluded

## Explanation

From $2x + 6 < 0$ we obtain $2x < -6$ and then $x < -3$. The direction stays the same, because we divide by $2$, which is positive. The variable $y$ does not appear, so the boundary is the vertical line $x = -3$. The variable $x$ is isolated with the symbol $<$, so the solutions form the half-plane **to the left** of the line. The symbol is strict, so the line is excluded.
