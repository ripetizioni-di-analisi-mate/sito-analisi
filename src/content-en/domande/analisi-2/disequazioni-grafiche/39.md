The double inequality $5 \leq x^2 + y^2 + 4x \leq 21$ describes an annulus. What are the radii of the two circles that bound it?

- $\sqrt{5}$ and $\sqrt{21}$
- $3$ and $5$
- $9$ and $25$
- $1$ and $\sqrt{17}$

## Explanation

We complete the square in $x$, since $x^2 + 4x = (x + 2)^2 - 4$. Adding $4$ to the three members we obtain
$$
9 \leq (x + 2)^2 + y^2 \leq 25 .
$$
The middle expression is the square of the distance from the point $(-2, 0)$, so the two circles have center $(-2, 0)$. The radii are $\sqrt{9} = 3$ and $\sqrt{25} = 5$.
