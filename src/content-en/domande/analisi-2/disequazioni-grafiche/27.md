At which points does the boundary of the region $x \leq y^2 - 9$ meet the $y$-axis?

- $(0, 3)$ and $(0, -3)$
- $(3, 0)$ and $(-3, 0)$
- $(0, 9)$ and $(0, -9)$
- $(-9, 0)$ and $(9, 0)$

## Explanation

The boundary is the parabola $x = y^2 - 9$, in which the variable $y$ appears squared, so the axis of the parabola is **horizontal**. The points of the $y$-axis have $x = 0$, so the intersections are found by solving $y^2 - 9 = 0$, which gives $y = 3$ and $y = -3$. The parabola therefore meets the $y$-axis at the points $(0, 3)$ and $(0, -3)$. The parabola meets the $x$-axis, instead, only at the vertex $(-9, 0)$.
