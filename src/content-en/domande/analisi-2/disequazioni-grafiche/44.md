The ellipse $x^2 + 9y^2 = 36$ bounds the region $x^2 + 9y^2 \leq 36$. What are the four vertices of the ellipse?

- $(\pm 6, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 2)$
- $(\pm 1, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 3)$
- $(\pm 2, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 6)$
- $(\pm 36, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 4)$

## Explanation

Dividing both sides of the equation by $36$ gives $\dfrac{x^2}{36} + \dfrac{y^2}{4} = 1$. The equation has the form $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2} + \dfrac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$ with $a^2 = 36$ and $b^2 = 4$, so the horizontal semi-axis is $a = 6$ and the vertical semi-axis is $b = 2$. The center of the ellipse is the origin. The vertices are therefore $(\pm 6, 0)$ on the $x$-axis and $(0, \pm 2)$ on the $y$-axis.
