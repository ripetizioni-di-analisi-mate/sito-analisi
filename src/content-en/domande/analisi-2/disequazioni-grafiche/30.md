To which of the following conditions is the inequality $|y| \leq \sqrt{x + 4}$ equivalent?

- $x \geq y^2 + 4$
- $x \leq y^2 - 4$
- $x \geq y^2 - 4$
- $x \geq y^2 - 4$ and $y \geq 0$

## Explanation

Both sides are greater than or equal to zero, so squaring preserves the direction and gives $y^2 \leq x + 4$, that is, $x \geq y^2 - 4$. The existence condition of the root is $x \geq -4$, and it is already contained in $x \geq y^2 - 4$, because $y^2 - 4 \geq -4$. The region therefore consists of the points **to the right** of the parabola $x = y^2 - 4$, parabola included. Since $|y| = |-y|$, the region is symmetric with respect to the $x$-axis and also contains points with negative $y$-coordinate, such as $(0, -2)$, which the condition $y \geq 0$ would exclude.
