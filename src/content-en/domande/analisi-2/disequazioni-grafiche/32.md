The region $x \geq y^2$ consists of the points to the right of the parabola $x = y^2$, which has its vertex at the origin. The region is translated so that the vertex of the parabola is the point $(3, -2)$. Which inequality describes the translated region?

- $x \geq (y + 2)^2 + 3$
- $x \geq (y - 2)^2 + 3$
- $x \geq (y + 2)^2 - 3$
- $y \geq (x - 3)^2 - 2$

## Explanation

The parabola $x = y^2$ translated so that its vertex is the point $V(x_V, y_V)$ has equation $x - x_V = (y - y_V)^2$. With $x_V = 3$ and $y_V = -2$ we obtain $x - 3 = (y + 2)^2$, that is, $x = (y + 2)^2 + 3$. The translation preserves the position of the region with respect to the curve, so the translated region consists of the points **to the right** of the new parabola, parabola included. The inequality is therefore $x \geq (y + 2)^2 + 3$.
