The curve in the figure is a hyperbola that has the coordinate axes as asymptotes and passes through the points $(2, 2)$ and $(4, 1)$. The branches are drawn as solid lines. Which inequality describes the shaded region?

::grafico{regione="x*y>=4" xmin=-5 xmax=5 ymin=-5 ymax=5 uguale=1 punti="2,2,pieno;4,1,pieno" titolo="The shaded region"}

- $xy \leq 4$
- $xy > 4$
- $xy \geq 4$
- $xy \geq -4$

## Explanation

A hyperbola that has the axes as asymptotes has equation $xy = k$. The point $(2, 2)$ belongs to the curve, so $k = 2 \cdot 2 = 4$. The shaded region consists of the two **regions beyond the branches**, which do not contain the origin. At the test points $(3, 3)$ and $(-3, -3)$, one in each of the two regions, the product $xy$ equals $9$, which is greater than $4$, so $xy > 4$ holds beyond the branches. The branches drawn as solid lines are included, therefore the inequality is $xy \geq 4$.
