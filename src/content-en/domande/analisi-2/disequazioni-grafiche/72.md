The region described by the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 + y^2 \leq 5 \\[4pt]
xy > 2
\end{cases}
$$
consists of two parts, one in the first quadrant and one in the third quadrant, each bounded by an arc of a circle and by an arc of a hyperbola. The two curves meet at the points $(1, 2)$, $(2, 1)$, $(-1, -2)$ and $(-2, -1)$. Which statement is true?

- The arcs of the circle are included, the arcs of the hyperbola are excluded and the four intersection points are excluded
- The arcs of the circle are included, the arcs of the hyperbola are excluded and the four intersection points are included
- The arcs of the circle are excluded, the arcs of the hyperbola are included and the four intersection points are excluded
- The arcs of the circle, the arcs of the hyperbola and the four intersection points are all included

## Explanation

In a system each boundary piece follows the symbol of its own inequality. The circle $x^2 + y^2 = 5$ appears with the symbol $\leq$, so the two arcs of the circle are **included** and are drawn as solid lines. The hyperbola $xy = 2$ appears with the symbol $>$, so the two arcs of the hyperbola are **excluded** and are drawn as dashed lines. At each of the four intersection points we have $xy = 2$, therefore the inequality $xy > 2$ is false and the point does not belong to the region.
