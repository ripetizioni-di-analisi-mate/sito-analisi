In the figure the two boundary lines are parallel to the line $y = x$ and are drawn as solid lines. Which second-degree inequality describes the shaded region?

::grafico{regione="(x-y)^2<=4" xmin=-4 xmax=4 ymin=-4 ymax=4 uguale=1 titolo="The shaded region"}

- $(x - y)^2 \geq 4$
- $(x - y)^2 \leq 4$
- $(x - y)^2 \leq 2$
- $x^2 - y^2 \leq 4$

## Explanation

The region is the **strip** lying between the lines $y = x - 2$ and $y = x + 2$, drawn as solid lines and therefore included. It is described by $x - 2 \leq y \leq x + 2$, that is, by $-2 \leq x - y \leq 2$, which is equivalent to $|x - y| \leq 2$. Both sides are greater than or equal to zero, so squaring gives the equivalent inequality $(x - y)^2 \leq 4$. The inequality $(x - y)^2 \leq 2$ describes the narrower strip between $y = x - \sqrt{2}$ and $y = x + \sqrt{2}$, and $x^2 - y^2 \leq 4$ has a hyperbola as its boundary.
