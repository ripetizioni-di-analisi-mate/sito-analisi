The region $xy - 3x + y < 5$ has as boundary a hyperbola with asymptotes parallel to the coordinate axes. What are the asymptotes?

- $x = 1$ and $y = -3$
- $x = 3$ and $y = -1$
- $x = 0$ and $y = 0$
- $x = -1$ and $y = 3$

## Explanation

We subtract $3$ from both sides, so that the left-hand side can be factored by grouping.
$$
\begin{aligned}
xy - 3x + y - 3 &< 2 \\[6pt]
x(y - 3) + (y - 3) &< 2 \\[6pt]
(x + 1)(y - 3) &< 2
\end{aligned}
$$
The boundary is the hyperbola $(x - x_0)(y - y_0) = k$ with $x_0 = -1$, $y_0 = 3$ and $k = 2$. Its asymptotes are the lines $x = x_0$ and $y = y_0$, that is, $x = -1$ and $y = 3$.
