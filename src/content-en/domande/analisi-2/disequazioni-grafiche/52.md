Which lines are the asymptotes of the hyperbola that bounds the region $\dfrac{y^2}{16} - \dfrac{x^2}{4} \geq 1$?

- $y = \pm \dfrac{1}{2} x$
- $y = \pm 4x$
- $y = \pm 2x$
- $y = \pm \dfrac{1}{4} x$

## Explanation

The positive term is the one in $y^2$, so the hyperbola has its branches above and below and its equation has the form $\dfrac{y^2}{b^2} - \dfrac{x^2}{a^2} = 1$ with $b^2 = 16$ and $a^2 = 4$. Hence $b = 4$ and $a = 2$. The asymptotes are the lines $y = \pm \dfrac{b}{a} x$, that is, $y = \pm 2x$. The same lines are obtained by setting the left-hand side equal to zero, since $\dfrac{y^2}{16} = \dfrac{x^2}{4}$ is equivalent to $y^2 = 4x^2$.
