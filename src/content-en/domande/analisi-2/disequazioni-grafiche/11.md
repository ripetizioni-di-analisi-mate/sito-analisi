Which region is formed by the points of the plane whose $y$-coordinate is less than twice their $x$-coordinate?

- The half-plane above the line $y = 2x$, line excluded
- The half-plane below the line $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x$, line excluded
- The half-plane below the line $y = 2x$, line included
- The half-plane below the line $y = 2x$, line excluded

## Explanation

The $y$-coordinate of a point is $y$ and twice the $x$-coordinate is $2x$, so the condition is written $y < 2x$. The boundary is the line $y = 2x$, which passes through the origin. The variable $y$ is isolated with the symbol $<$, so by the isolated variable rule the solutions form the half-plane **below** the line. The point $(1, 0)$ lies below the line and indeed satisfies the condition, because $0 < 2$ is true. The points of the line have $y$-coordinate equal to twice the $x$-coordinate, so the line is **excluded**.
