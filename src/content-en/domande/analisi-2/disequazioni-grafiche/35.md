The two circles in the figure have center at the origin and radii $2$ and $4$. Which double inequality describes the shaded region?

::grafico{regione="x^2+y^2>=4; x^2+y^2<16" xmin=-5 xmax=5 ymin=-5 ymax=5 uguale=1 titolo="The shaded region"}

- $2 \leq x^2 + y^2 < 4$
- $4 \leq x^2 + y^2 < 16$
- $4 < x^2 + y^2 \leq 16$
- $4 \leq x^2 + y^2 \leq 16$

## Explanation

The expression $x^2 + y^2$ is the square of the distance from the origin, so the circles of radius $2$ and $4$ have equations $x^2 + y^2 = 4$ and $x^2 + y^2 = 16$. The shaded region is the **annulus** lying between the two circles. The inner circle is drawn as a solid line, so it is included and $4 \leq x^2 + y^2$ holds. The outer circle is dashed, so it is excluded and $x^2 + y^2 < 16$ holds.
