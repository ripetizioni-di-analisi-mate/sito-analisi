The annulus $2 < x^2 + y^2 \leq 8$ contains only one of the following points. Which one?

- $(1, 1)$
- $(3, 0)$
- $(2, 2)$
- $(1, 0)$

## Explanation

We compute $x^2 + y^2$ at each point and check whether the value is greater than $2$ and less than or equal to $8$. At the point $(2, 2)$ we have $x^2 + y^2 = 8$, so the point lies on the outer circle, which is included, and it **belongs** to the annulus. At the point $(1, 1)$ we have $x^2 + y^2 = 2$, so the point lies on the inner circle, which is excluded. At the points $(1, 0)$ and $(3, 0)$ we obtain the values $1$ and $9$, which do not lie between $2$ and $8$.
