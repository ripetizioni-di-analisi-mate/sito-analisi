Which region is described in polar coordinates by $0 \leq \rho \leq 3$, $\dfrac{\pi}{2} \leq \theta \leq \pi$?

- $\begin{cases} x^2 + y^2 \leq 9 \\ x \geq 0 \\ y \geq 0 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} x^2 + y^2 \leq 3 \\ x \leq 0 \\ y \geq 0 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} x^2 + y^2 \leq 9 \\ x \leq 0 \\ y \geq 0 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} x^2 + y^2 \leq 9 \\ x \leq 0 \end{cases}$

## Explanation

The number $\rho$ is the distance of the point from the origin, so $0 \leq \rho \leq 3$ is equivalent to $x^2 + y^2 \leq 9$. For $\dfrac{\pi}{2} \leq \theta \leq \pi$ we have $\cos\theta \leq 0$ and $\sin\theta \geq 0$, so $x = \rho\cos\theta \leq 0$ and $y = \rho\sin\theta \geq 0$. The region is therefore the **quarter disk** of radius $3$ contained in the second quadrant. The number $3$ is the radius, while the inequality in $x$ and $y$ contains its square.
