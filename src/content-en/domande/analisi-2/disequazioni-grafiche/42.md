The disk $x^2 + y^2 - 6x \leq 0$ is contained in one of the following half-planes. Which one?

- $x \leq 0$
- $y \geq 0$
- $x \geq 0$
- $x \geq 3$

## Explanation

We complete the square in $x$, since $x^2 - 6x = (x - 3)^2 - 9$, and the inequality becomes $(x - 3)^2 + y^2 \leq 9$. The region is the disk with center $(3, 0)$ and radius $3$. The $x$-coordinates of its points range from $3 - 3 = 0$ to $3 + 3 = 6$, so the disk is contained in the half-plane $x \geq 0$. The point $(1, -1)$ belongs to the disk, because $(1 - 3)^2 + (-1)^2 = 5$ and $5 \leq 9$. Its $x$-coordinate is positive and less than $3$ and its $y$-coordinate is negative, so it belongs to none of the other three half-planes.
