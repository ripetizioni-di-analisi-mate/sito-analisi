On which set of the plane is the function $f(x, y) = \arcsin(2x + y)$ defined?

- On the strip between the lines $y = -2x - 1$ and $y = -2x + 1$, lines excluded
- On the strip between the lines $y = -2x - 1$ and $y = -2x + 1$, lines included
- On the half-plane below the line $y = -2x + 1$, line included
- On the whole plane

## Explanation

The function is defined where the argument of the arcsine lies between $-1$ and $1$, endpoints included, so the domain is described by $-1 \leq 2x + y \leq 1$. Subtracting $2x$ from the three members we obtain $-2x - 1 \leq y \leq -2x + 1$. The region is therefore a **strip** between two parallel lines, $y = -2x - 1$ at the bottom and $y = -2x + 1$ at the top. The symbols are $\leq$, so the two lines are included.
