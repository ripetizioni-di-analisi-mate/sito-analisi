The shaded region is the angle bounded by the rays $y = x$ and $y = -x$ with $x \geq 0$, which are excluded. Which inequality describes it?

::grafico{regione="y<x; y>-x" xmin=-3 xmax=3 ymin=-3 ymax=3 uguale=1 titolo="The shaded region"}

- $|x| < y$
- $|y| < |x|$
- $|y| < x$
- $y < |x|$

## Explanation

An absolute value is greater than or equal to zero, so $|y| < x$ can be true only if $x > 0$. For $x > 0$ the inequality is equivalent to $-x < y < x$. The condition $y < x$ describes the points below the line $y = x$, and the condition $y > -x$ the points above the line $y = -x$. Their intersection is the **angle** that contains the positive $x$-semi-axis, with the two rays excluded. The inequality $|y| < |x|$ is also true at the point $(-2, 0)$, which is not shaded.
