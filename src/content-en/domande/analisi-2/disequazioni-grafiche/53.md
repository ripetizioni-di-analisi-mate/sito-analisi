The hyperbola in the figure has vertices $(0, \pm 1)$, and the two dashed lines are its asymptotes $y = \pm \dfrac{1}{2} x$. The branches are drawn as solid lines. Which inequality describes the shaded region?

::grafico{regione="y^2-x^2/4>=1" xmin=-5 xmax=5 ymin=-5 ymax=5 uguale=1 segmenti="-5,-2.5,5,2.5;-5,2.5,5,-2.5" titolo="The shaded region"}

- $y^2 - \dfrac{x^2}{4} \geq 1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{4} - y^2 \geq 1$
- $y^2 - \dfrac{x^2}{4} \leq 1$
- $\dfrac{y^2}{4} - x^2 \geq 1$

## Explanation

The branches of the hyperbola lie above and below, so in the equation the positive term is the one in $y^2$ and the form is $\dfrac{y^2}{b^2} - \dfrac{x^2}{a^2} = 1$. The vertices $(0, \pm 1)$ give $b = 1$. The asymptotes $y = \pm \dfrac{b}{a} x$ have slope $\pm \dfrac{1}{2}$, so $a = 2$ and the equation is $y^2 - \dfrac{x^2}{4} = 1$. The shaded region consists of the two **regions inside the branches**, where the left-hand side is greater than $1$. The branches drawn as solid lines are included, so the inequality is $y^2 - \dfrac{x^2}{4} \geq 1$.
