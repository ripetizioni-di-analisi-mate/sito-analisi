In the figure the boundary is a parabola with vertex $V(1, -2)$ that passes through the point $(0, -1)$. Which inequality describes the shaded region?

::grafico{regione="y>=(x-1)^2-2" xmin=-3 xmax=5 ymin=-3 ymax=5 uguale=1 punti="1,-2,pieno,V" titolo="The shaded region"}

- $y \geq (x + 1)^2 - 2$
- $y \geq (x - 1)^2 - 2$
- $y \leq (x - 1)^2 - 2$
- $y > (x - 1)^2 - 2$

## Explanation

A parabola with vertical axis and vertex $V(x_V, y_V)$ has equation $y - y_V = a(x - x_V)^2$, so here $y = a(x - 1)^2 - 2$. The passage through $(0, -1)$ gives $-1 = a - 2$, so $a = 1$. The shaded region lies **above** the parabola, so $y$ is greater than $(x - 1)^2 - 2$. The parabola is drawn as a solid line, so it is included and the symbol is $\geq$.
