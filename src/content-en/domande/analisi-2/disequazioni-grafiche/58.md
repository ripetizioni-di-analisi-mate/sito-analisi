The lines $y = \dfrac{1}{2} x$ and $y = -\dfrac{1}{2} x$ divide the plane into four angles. In the figure the two opposite angles that contain the $y$-axis are shaded, and the two lines are drawn as solid lines. Which inequality describes the shaded region?

::grafico{regione="x^2-4*y^2<=0" xmin=-4 xmax=4 ymin=-4 ymax=4 uguale=1 titolo="The shaded region"}

- $x^2 - 4y^2 \leq 0$
- $x^2 - 4y^2 \geq 0$
- $4x^2 - y^2 \leq 0$
- $x^2 - 4y^2 \leq 1$

## Explanation

The points of the lines $y = \pm \dfrac{1}{2} x$ satisfy $4y^2 = x^2$, that is, $x^2 - 4y^2 = 0$. The left-hand side factors as $(x - 2y)(x + 2y)$, so the boundary is a pair of lines. At the test points $(0, 1)$ and $(0, -1)$, one in each of the two shaded angles, we have $x^2 - 4y^2 = -4$, which is negative, so $x^2 - 4y^2 < 0$ holds in the shaded region. The lines drawn as solid lines are included, therefore the inequality is $x^2 - 4y^2 \leq 0$.
