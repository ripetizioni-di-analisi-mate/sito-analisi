Which of the following points satisfies the inequality $xy < 3$ and does not satisfy the inequality $y < \dfrac{3}{x}$?

- $(1, 1)$
- $(-1, 1)$
- $(2, 2)$
- $(-2, -2)$

## Explanation

For $x > 0$, multiplying both sides of $y < \dfrac{3}{x}$ by $x$ preserves the direction, and we obtain $xy < 3$. For $x < 0$ the direction changes and we obtain $xy > 3$. Therefore a point with negative $x$-coordinate that satisfies $xy < 3$ does not satisfy $y < \dfrac{3}{x}$. At the point $(-1, 1)$ we have $xy = -1$, so $xy < 3$ is true, while $y < \dfrac{3}{x}$ becomes $1 < -3$, which is false. The point $(1, 1)$ satisfies both inequalities. At the points $(2, 2)$ and $(-2, -2)$ we have $xy = 4$, so $xy < 3$ is false.
