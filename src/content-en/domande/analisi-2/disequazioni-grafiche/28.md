The figure shows the region $x \leq 3 - y^2$, bounded by a parabola with vertex $V(3, 0)$. Which of the following lines meets the region in a segment?

::grafico{regione="x<=3-y^2" xmin=-5 xmax=4 ymin=-4.5 ymax=4.5 uguale=1 punti="3,0,pieno,V" titolo="The region $x \leq 3 - y^2$"}

- The line $y = 1$
- The line $x = 4$
- The line $y = -2$
- The line $x = -1$

## Explanation

On the line $x = -1$ the inequality becomes $-1 \leq 3 - y^2$, that is, $y^2 \leq 4$, so the points of the line that belong to the region are those with $-2 \leq y \leq 2$. These points form the **segment** with endpoints $(-1, -2)$ and $(-1, 2)$. On the line $y = 1$ the inequality becomes $x \leq 2$ and on the line $y = -2$ it becomes $x \leq -1$, so the two horizontal lines meet the region in a ray. On the line $x = 4$ the inequality becomes $y^2 \leq -1$, which has no solutions because a square is greater than or equal to zero, so the line does not meet the region.
