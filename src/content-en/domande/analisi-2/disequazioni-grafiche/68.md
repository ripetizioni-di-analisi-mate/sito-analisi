The boundary of the shaded region is the graph of an exponential function, passes through the point $(0, 1)$ and is included. Which inequality describes the region?

::grafico{regione="y<=exp(-x)" xmin=-3 xmax=4 ymin=-3 ymax=4 uguale=1 punti="0,1,pieno" titolo="The shaded region"}

- $y \leq e^{x}$
- $y \leq e^{-x}$
- $x \leq -\ln y$
- $y \leq -e^{x}$

## Explanation

The curve passes through $(0, 1)$ and is decreasing, so among the given functions it is the graph of $y = e^{-x}$. The region lies below the graph, which is drawn as a solid line. By the **isolated variable rule** the inequality is therefore $y \leq e^{-x}$. The graph of $y = e^{x}$ passes through the same point, but it is increasing. The inequality $x \leq -\ln y$ is defined only for $y > 0$, while the shaded region also contains points with $y \leq 0$, such as $(0, -1)$.
