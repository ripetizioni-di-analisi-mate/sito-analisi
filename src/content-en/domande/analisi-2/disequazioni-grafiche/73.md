The shaded region is bounded by a parabola with vertex $V(-4, 0)$ and by a line, which meet at the points $(0, 2)$ and $(-3, -1)$. Which system describes it?

::grafico{regione="x>=y^2-4; y>x+2" xmin=-5.5 xmax=2.5 ymin=-3.5 ymax=4.5 uguale=1 punti="-4,0,pieno,V;0,2,vuoto;-3,-1,vuoto" titolo="The shaded region"}

- $\begin{cases} x \geq y^2 - 4 \\ y < x + 2 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} x > y^2 - 4 \\ y \geq x + 2 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} y \geq x^2 - 4 \\ y > x + 2 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} x \geq y^2 - 4 \\ y > x + 2 \end{cases}$

## Explanation

The parabola has a horizontal axis and vertex $(-4, 0)$, so its equation is $x = ay^2 - 4$, and the condition of passing through $(0, 2)$ gives $a = 1$. The region lies to the right of the parabola, which is drawn as a solid line, therefore the first condition is $x \geq y^2 - 4$. The line through $(0, 2)$ and $(-3, -1)$ has equation $y = x + 2$ and is dashed, so it is **excluded**. The point $(-3, 0)$ belongs to the region and has $y$-coordinate $0$, which is greater than $-3 + 2 = -1$, therefore the region lies above the line and the second condition is $y > x + 2$. The two points where the parabola meets the line are excluded.
