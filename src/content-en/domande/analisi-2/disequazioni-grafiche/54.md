The inequality $x^2 - y^2 \leq -9$ describes a region of the plane with its boundary included. Which one?

- The region between the two branches of the hyperbola with vertices $(\pm 3, 0)$
- The two regions inside the branches of the hyperbola with vertices $(\pm 3, 0)$
- The region between the two branches of the hyperbola with vertices $(0, \pm 3)$
- The two regions inside the branches of the hyperbola with vertices $(0, \pm 3)$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides by $-1$ the direction changes, and we obtain $y^2 - x^2 \geq 9$, that is,
$$
\frac{y^2}{9} - \frac{x^2}{9} \geq 1 .
$$
The positive term is the one in $y^2$, so the hyperbola has its branches above and below, with vertices $(0, \pm 3)$. At the center the left-hand side equals $0$, so the left-hand side greater than or equal to $1$ describes the two **regions inside the branches**, one above and one below. Indeed, the point $(0, 4)$, which lies above the vertex $(0, 3)$, satisfies the original inequality, because $0 - 16 \leq -9$.
