The inequality $x^2 + 9y^2 + 4x - 18y + 4 \leq 0$ describes the interior points of an ellipse, ellipse included. What are the center $C$, the horizontal semi-axis $a$ and the vertical semi-axis $b$?

- $C(2, -1)$, $a = 3$, $b = 1$
- $C(-2, 1)$, $a = 1$, $b = 3$
- $C(-2, 1)$, $a = 3$, $b = 1$
- $C(-2, 1)$, $a = 9$, $b = 1$

## Explanation

We factor out the coefficient of $y^2$ and complete the squares, since $x^2 + 4x = (x + 2)^2 - 4$ and $9(y^2 - 2y) = 9(y - 1)^2 - 9$.
$$
\begin{aligned}
(x + 2)^2 - 4 + 9(y - 1)^2 - 9 + 4 &\leq 0 \\[6pt]
(x + 2)^2 + 9(y - 1)^2 &\leq 9 \\[6pt]
\frac{(x + 2)^2}{9} + (y - 1)^2 &\leq 1
\end{aligned}
$$
The center is $C(-2, 1)$. The denominator of the term in $x$ is $a^2 = 9$ and that of the term in $y$ is $b^2 = 1$, so $a = 3$ and $b = 1$.
