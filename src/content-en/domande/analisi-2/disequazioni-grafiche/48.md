Which of the following inequalities describes the exterior points of an ellipse whose vertical semi-axis is greater than its horizontal semi-axis?

- $x^2 + 4y^2 > 8$
- $4x^2 + y^2 > 8$
- $4x^2 + y^2 < 8$
- $4x^2 - y^2 > 8$

## Explanation

The exterior points of an ellipse with center at the origin satisfy an inequality of the form $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2} + \dfrac{y^2}{b^2} > 1$, in which $a$ is the horizontal semi-axis and $b$ the vertical semi-axis. Dividing both sides of $4x^2 + y^2 > 8$ by $8$ we obtain
$$
\frac{x^2}{2} + \frac{y^2}{8} > 1 ,
$$
so $a = \sqrt{2}$ and $b = 2\sqrt{2}$, and the vertical semi-axis is the greater one. In $x^2 + 4y^2 > 8$ the denominators are exchanged, so the greater semi-axis is the horizontal one. The inequality $4x^2 + y^2 < 8$ describes the interior points. The inequality $4x^2 - y^2 > 8$ has a hyperbola as boundary, because the coefficients of $x^2$ and $y^2$ have opposite signs.
