The inequality $xy \leq 0$ has the two coordinate axes as boundary. Which set does it describe?

- The first and third quadrants, axes included
- The second and fourth quadrants, axes excluded
- The second and fourth quadrants, axes included
- The third quadrant, axes included

## Explanation

The product $xy$ is negative when $x$ and $y$ have opposite signs, that is, at the interior points of the second and fourth quadrants. The product is zero when $x = 0$ or $y = 0$, that is, at the points of the two axes. The symbol $\leq$ includes equality, so the region consists of the **second and fourth quadrants, axes included**. The boundary $xy = 0$ is the pair of lines formed by the two axes.
