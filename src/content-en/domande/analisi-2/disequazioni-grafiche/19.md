What is the vertex of the parabola that forms the boundary of the region $y < x^2 + 4x + 1$?

- $(2, 13)$
- $(-2, 1)$
- $(-4, 1)$
- $(-2, -3)$

## Explanation

The boundary is the parabola $y = x^2 + 4x + 1$, with **vertical axis** and coefficients $a = 1$, $b = 4$, $c = 1$. The $x$-coordinate of the vertex is $x_V = -\dfrac{b}{2a} = -\dfrac{4}{2} = -2$. The $y$-coordinate is obtained by substituting $x_V$ into the equation, so $y_V = (-2)^2 + 4 \cdot (-2) + 1 = 4 - 8 + 1 = -3$. The vertex is therefore $V(-2, -3)$, as can also be read off from the form $y = (x + 2)^2 - 3$.
