What is the set of all the points of the plane at which the product $y\,(x^2 + 4y^2 - 4)$ is positive?

- The points with $y > 0$ outside the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{4} + y^2 = 1$
- The points with $y > 0$ outside the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{4} + y^2 = 1$, together with the points with $y < 0$ inside the ellipse
- The points with $y > 0$ inside the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{4} + y^2 = 1$, together with the points with $y < 0$ outside the ellipse
- The points with $y \neq 0$ outside the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{4} + y^2 = 1$

## Explanation

A product is positive when the two factors have the same sign. The factor $y$ is positive above the $x$-axis and negative below it. Dividing by $4$, the condition $x^2 + 4y^2 - 4 > 0$ becomes $\dfrac{x^2}{4} + y^2 > 1$, so the second factor is positive at the points outside the ellipse with semi-axes $2$ and $1$ and negative at the points inside it. The two factors are both positive at the points with $y > 0$ outside the ellipse, and both negative at the points with $y < 0$ inside the ellipse. The solutions are the union of these two regions, with the $x$-axis and the ellipse **excluded**, because on the two curves the product is zero.
