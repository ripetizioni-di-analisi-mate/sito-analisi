The shaded region is bounded by the $x$-axis, by the curve $y = \sqrt{x}$ and by the line $y = 6 - x$. The vertical segments that cross it enter through the $x$-axis, while the exit curve changes. At which $x$-coordinate does the change occur?

::grafico{regione="y>=0; y<=sqrt(x); y<=6-x" xmin=-1 xmax=7 ymin=-3 ymax=5 uguale=1 tracce=0 titolo="The shaded region"}

- $x = 2$
- $x = 3$
- $x = 6$
- $x = 4$

## Explanation

The exit curve changes at the point where $y = \sqrt{x}$ meets the line $y = 6 - x$. Squaring both sides of $\sqrt{x} = 6 - x$ gives $x = 36 - 12x + x^2$, that is, $x^2 - 13x + 36 = 0$, which has the solutions $x = 4$ and $x = 9$. The solution $x = 9$ is not acceptable, because for $x = 9$ the right-hand side $6 - x$ is negative, so the two curves meet at $(4, 2)$. For $0 \leq x \leq 4$ the vertical segments exit through the curve $y = \sqrt{x}$, and for $4 \leq x \leq 6$ they exit through the line. The exit function $g_2$ is therefore defined piecewise, and to compute a double integral the region is split into **two normal domains**, separated by the line $x = 4$.
