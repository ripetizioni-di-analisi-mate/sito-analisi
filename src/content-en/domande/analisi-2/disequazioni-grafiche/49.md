The ellipse in the figure has center $C(-1, 2)$ and vertices $(-4, 2)$, $(2, 2)$, $(-1, 0)$, $(-1, 4)$. The boundary is drawn as a solid line. Which inequality describes the shaded region?

::grafico{regione="(x+1)^2/9+(y-2)^2/4>=1" xmin=-6 xmax=4 ymin=-3 ymax=7 uguale=1 punti="-1,2,pieno,C" titolo="The shaded region"}

- $\dfrac{(x - 1)^2}{9} + \dfrac{(y + 2)^2}{4} \geq 1$
- $\dfrac{(x + 1)^2}{4} + \dfrac{(y - 2)^2}{9} \geq 1$
- $\dfrac{(x + 1)^2}{9} + \dfrac{(y - 2)^2}{4} \geq 1$
- $\dfrac{(x + 1)^2}{9} + \dfrac{(y - 2)^2}{4} \leq 1$

## Explanation

The ellipse has center $C(-1, 2)$, so $(x + 1)^2$ and $(y - 2)^2$ appear in the numerators. The vertices $(-4, 2)$ and $(2, 2)$ are at distance $3$ from the center, so the horizontal semi-axis is $a = 3$ and the denominator of the term in $x$ is $9$. The vertices $(-1, 0)$ and $(-1, 4)$ are at distance $2$ from the center, so the vertical semi-axis is $b = 2$ and the denominator of the term in $y$ is $4$. The shaded region consists of the **exterior points**, where the left-hand side is greater than $1$. The boundary drawn as a solid line is included, so the symbol is $\geq$.
