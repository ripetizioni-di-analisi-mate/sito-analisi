Which set is described by the inequality $\dfrac{y}{x} \geq 1$?

- The points with $y \geq x$
- The points with $y \geq x$ and $x \neq 0$
- The points with $x\,(y - x) \geq 0$
- The points with $x > 0$ and $y \geq x$, together with the points with $x < 0$ and $y \leq x$

## Explanation

The quotient is defined for $x \neq 0$. For $x > 0$ both sides are multiplied by $x$ preserving the direction of the inequality, and we obtain $y \geq x$. For $x < 0$ the direction changes, and we obtain $y \leq x$. The solutions are therefore the points with $x > 0$ and $y \geq x$, together with the points with $x < 0$ and $y \leq x$. The point $(-2, -3)$ is a solution, because $\dfrac{-3}{-2} = \dfrac{3}{2}$, but it does not satisfy $y \geq x$. The point $(0, 1)$ satisfies $x\,(y - x) \geq 0$, but it is not a solution, because the quotient is not defined for $x = 0$.
