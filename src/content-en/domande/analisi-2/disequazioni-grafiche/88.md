Let $f$ be a function continuous on the whole plane and let $D$ be the set described by the system
$$
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{x^2}{9} + y^2 \leq 1 \\[8pt]
x > 0
\end{cases}
$$
Which statement is true?

- $D$ is closed and bounded, so the Weierstrass theorem applies to $f$ on $D$
- $D$ is closed and not bounded, so the Weierstrass theorem cannot be applied to $f$ on $D$
- $D$ is bounded and not closed, so the Weierstrass theorem cannot be applied to $f$ on $D$
- $D$ is neither bounded nor closed, so the Weierstrass theorem cannot be applied to $f$ on $D$

## Explanation

The Weierstrass theorem, or extreme value theorem, applies to a continuous function on a closed and bounded set. The points of $D$ are inside the ellipse with semi-axes $3$ and $1$ or belong to the ellipse, so $D$ is contained in the disk centered at the origin with radius $3$ and is **bounded**. The origin does not belong to $D$, because there the condition $x > 0$ is false. However, every disk centered at the origin contains points of $D$, because it contains the points $(x, 0)$ with $x$ positive and small enough, so the origin is a boundary point of $D$. A closed set contains all its boundary points, therefore $D$ is **not closed** and the theorem cannot be applied.
