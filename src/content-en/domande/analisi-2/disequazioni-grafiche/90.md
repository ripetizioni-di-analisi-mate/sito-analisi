Which pieces and how many vertices make up the boundary of the set described by the system?
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 + y^2 \leq 4 \\[4pt]
|y| \leq 1
\end{cases}
$$

- Two segments and two arcs of a circle, with four vertices
- Two segments and two arcs of a circle, with two vertices
- One segment and one arc of a circle, with two vertices
- Two segments, with four vertices

## Explanation

The condition $|y| \leq 1$ is equivalent to $-1 \leq y \leq 1$, so the set is the part of the disk of radius $2$ contained in the strip between the lines $y = -1$ and $y = 1$. Each line meets the circle where $x^2 + 1 = 4$, that is, for $x = \pm\sqrt{3}$, therefore the vertices are the **four** points $(\pm\sqrt{3}, 1)$ and $(\pm\sqrt{3}, -1)$. On the lines the boundary pieces are the two segments $y = 1$ and $y = -1$ with $-\sqrt{3} \leq x \leq \sqrt{3}$. On the circle the boundary pieces are the two arcs with $-1 \leq y \leq 1$, one on the right and one on the left.
