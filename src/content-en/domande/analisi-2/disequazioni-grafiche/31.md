In only one of the following inequalities is the boundary a parabola with horizontal axis that opens to the left. Which one?

- $y^2 - 2x + 6 \geq 0$
- $x^2 + 2y + 6 \geq 0$
- $y^2 + 2x + 6 \geq 0$
- $x^2 - 2y + 6 \geq 0$

## Explanation

A parabola has a horizontal axis when only the variable $y$ appears squared, so the two inequalities with $x^2$ have as boundary a parabola with vertical axis. Isolating $x$ in the equation $y^2 + 2x + 6 = 0$ gives $x = -\dfrac{1}{2} y^2 - 3$. The coefficient of $y^2$ is negative, so the parabola **opens to the left**, with vertex $(-3, 0)$. From the equation $y^2 - 2x + 6 = 0$ we obtain $x = \dfrac{1}{2} y^2 + 3$, in which the coefficient of $y^2$ is positive, so this parabola opens to the right.
