The shaded strip in the figure is bounded by two vertical lines, one solid and one dashed. Which double inequality describes it?

::grafico{regione="x>=-2; x<1" xmin=-4.5 xmax=3.5 ymin=-4 ymax=4 uguale=1 titolo="The shaded region"}

- $-2 \leq x < 1$
- $-2 < x \leq 1$
- $-2 \leq y < 1$
- $-2 \leq x \leq 1$

## Explanation

The region is the strip lying between the vertical lines $x = -2$ and $x = 1$, so the variable that appears in the middle of the double inequality is $x$. The line $x = -2$ is drawn as a solid line, so it is **included** and the symbol on its side is $\leq$. The line $x = 1$ is dashed, so it is **excluded** and the symbol on its side is $<$. The region is therefore described by $-2 \leq x < 1$.
