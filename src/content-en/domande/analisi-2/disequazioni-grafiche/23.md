Which points $(x, 0)$ of the $x$-axis belong to the region $y \geq x^2 - x - 6$?

- Those with $x \leq -2$ or $x \geq 3$
- Those with $-3 \leq x \leq 2$
- Those with $-2 < x < 3$
- Those with $-2 \leq x \leq 3$

## Explanation

The points of the $x$-axis have $y = 0$, so they belong to the region when $0 \geq x^2 - x - 6$, that is, when $x^2 - x - 6 \leq 0$. The trinomial factors as $(x + 2)(x - 3)$ and vanishes for $x = -2$ and $x = 3$. The coefficient of $x^2$ is positive, so the trinomial is less than or equal to zero **between the two roots**, that is, for $-2 \leq x \leq 3$. The endpoints are included, because the points $(-2, 0)$ and $(3, 0)$ lie on the parabola, which with the symbol $\geq$ is part of the region.
