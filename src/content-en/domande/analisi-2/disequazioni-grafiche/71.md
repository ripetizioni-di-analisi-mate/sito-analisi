Which of the following points satisfies the system?
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 + y^2 < 10 \\[4pt]
y \geq x + 2
\end{cases}
$$

- $(1, 3)$
- $(0, 1)$
- $(-1, 1)$
- $(-3, 2)$

## Explanation

The solutions of a system are the points that satisfy all the inequalities. At the point $(-1, 1)$ we have $x^2 + y^2 = 2$, which is less than $10$, and $x + 2 = 1$, so $y \geq x + 2$ is true with the equality sign. The point lies on the line, which is included, and therefore it **belongs** to the region. At the point $(1, 3)$ we have $x^2 + y^2 = 10$, so the point lies on the circle, which is excluded. At the point $(0, 1)$ the second inequality is false, because $1 < 2$. At the point $(-3, 2)$ the first inequality is false, because $9 + 4 = 13$.
