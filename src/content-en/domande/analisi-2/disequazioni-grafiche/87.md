The set $D$ in the figure is described by the system
$$
\begin{cases}
y \leq 2 - x^2 \\[4pt]
y \geq x
\end{cases}
$$
and has the vertices $A(-2, -2)$ and $B(1, 1)$. In the search for the maxima and minima of $f(x, y) = xy$ on $D$, which function of one variable is studied on the boundary piece that lies on the parabola?

::grafico{regione="y<=2-x^2; y>=x" xmin=-4 xmax=3 ymin=-4 ymax=3 uguale=1 punti="-2,-2,pieno,A;1,1,pieno,B" titolo="The set $D$ with the vertices $A$ and $B$"}

- $\varphi(x) = 2x - x^3$ with $-2 \leq x \leq 1$
- $\varphi(x) = 2x - x^3$ with $-\sqrt{2} \leq x \leq \sqrt{2}$
- $\varphi(x) = x^2$ with $-2 \leq x \leq 1$
- $\varphi(x) = 2x - x^3$ with $-1 \leq x \leq 2$

## Explanation

The boundary piece on the parabola is obtained when the first inequality becomes the equality $y = 2 - x^2$ and the second one remains. The two curves meet where $2 - x^2 = x$, that is, $x^2 + x - 2 = 0$, which has the solutions $x = -2$ and $x = 1$. The piece is therefore the arc $y = 2 - x^2$ with $-2 \leq x \leq 1$, between the vertices $A$ and $B$. Substituting $y = 2 - x^2$ into $f(x, y) = xy$ gives $\varphi(x) = x\,(2 - x^2) = 2x - x^3$. The function $x^2$ is the restriction of $f$ to the other piece, the segment $y = x$. The numbers $\pm\sqrt{2}$ are the $x$-coordinates of the points where the parabola meets the $x$-axis.
