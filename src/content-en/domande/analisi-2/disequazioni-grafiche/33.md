What is the axis of symmetry of the parabola that bounds the region $y^2 + 6y - 2x + 1 \leq 0$?

- $y = 3$
- $x = -4$
- $x = -3$
- $y = -3$

## Explanation

The variable $y$ appears squared, so the parabola has a horizontal axis and we isolate $x$. From $2x \geq y^2 + 6y + 1$, since $y^2 + 6y = (y + 3)^2 - 9$, we obtain
$$
x \geq \frac{1}{2}(y + 3)^2 - 4 .
$$
The vertex of the parabola is $(-4, -3)$. The axis of symmetry is the horizontal line through the vertex, that is, $y = -3$.
