The line $y = 2x$ and the region $y \geq x^2 + 1$ are drawn in the same Cartesian plane. How many points do they have in common?

- None
- One
- Two
- Infinitely many

## Explanation

A point of the line has coordinates $(x, 2x)$ and belongs to the region when $2x \geq x^2 + 1$. Moving all the terms to the right-hand side we obtain $x^2 - 2x + 1 \leq 0$, that is, $(x - 1)^2 \leq 0$. A square is greater than or equal to zero, so the inequality is satisfied only for $x = 1$. The line is tangent to the parabola $y = x^2 + 1$ at the point $(1, 2)$, and all its other points lie below the parabola. With the symbol $\geq$ the boundary is included, so the point $(1, 2)$ belongs to the region and is **the only** point in common.
