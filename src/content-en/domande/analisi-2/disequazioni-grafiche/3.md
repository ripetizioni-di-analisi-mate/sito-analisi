In the figure the parabola $y = x^2 - 3$ is dashed and all the other points of the plane are shaded. Which condition describes the shaded set?

::grafico{regione="y!=x^2-3" xmin=-4 xmax=4 ymin=-4 ymax=4 uguale=1 titolo="The shaded region"}

- $y > x^2 - 3$
- $y \geq x^2 - 3$
- $y \neq x^2 - 3$
- $y = x^2 - 3$

## Explanation

The shaded points lie both above and below the parabola, so the set contains both regions into which the parabola divides the plane. The parabola is dashed, so its points are excluded. The set therefore consists of **all the points of the plane except those of the curve**, and the condition that describes it is $y \neq x^2 - 3$. The condition $y > x^2 - 3$ describes only the region above the parabola, and $y = x^2 - 3$ describes only the points of the curve.
