In the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 + y^2 = 25 \\[4pt]
y \geq 3
\end{cases}
$$
the first condition is an equality constraint. What is the solution set?

- The part of the disk $x^2 + y^2 \leq 25$ that lies above the line $y = 3$, with the line included
- The arc of the circle with endpoints $(-4, 3)$ and $(4, 3)$ that passes through $(0, 5)$, with the endpoints included
- The arc of the circle with endpoints $(-4, 3)$ and $(4, 3)$ that passes through $(0, -5)$, with the endpoints included
- Only the points $(-4, 3)$ and $(4, 3)$

## Explanation

The first condition is an equality, so the solutions are points of the circle centered at the origin with radius $5$, and the set has no interior points. Among the points of the circle, those with $y$-coordinate greater than or equal to $3$ satisfy the second condition. The line $y = 3$ meets the circle where $x^2 + 9 = 25$, that is, for $x = \pm 4$, at the points $(-4, 3)$ and $(4, 3)$. The point $(0, 5)$ satisfies both conditions, while the point $(0, -5)$ does not satisfy $y \geq 3$. The set is therefore the **arc** that goes from $(-4, 3)$ to $(4, 3)$ passing through $(0, 5)$, with the endpoints included because the symbol is $\geq$.
