In the inequality $3x - 4y > 8$ the variable $y$ is isolated. Which inequality is obtained?

- $y > \dfrac{3}{4}x - 2$
- $y < \dfrac{3}{4}x - 2$
- $y < \dfrac{3}{4}x + 2$
- $y > -\dfrac{3}{4}x + 2$

## Explanation

Moving $3x$ to the right-hand side we obtain $-4y > -3x + 8$. To isolate $y$ both sides are divided by $-4$, which is a negative number, so the direction of the inequality changes.
$$
y < \frac{3}{4}x - 2
$$
By the isolated variable rule the solutions are the points **below** the line $y = \dfrac{3}{4}x - 2$, line excluded.
