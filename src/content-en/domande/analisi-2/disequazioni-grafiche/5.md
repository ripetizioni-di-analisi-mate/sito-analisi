The figure shows the curve $F(x, y) = 0$, where $F$ is a polynomial, and two points $P$ and $Q$. It is known that $F(P) = 5$ and $F(Q) = -3$. What is the solution set of the inequality $F(x, y) \leq 0$?

::grafico{regione="x^2+2*y^2=8" xmin=-4 xmax=4 ymin=-4 ymax=4 uguale=1 punti="1,1,pieno,P;3,1,pieno,Q" titolo="The curve $F(x, y) = 0$ and the points $P$ and $Q$"}

- The exterior points of the curve and the points of the curve
- The interior points of the curve and the points of the curve
- The exterior points of the curve, curve excluded
- The interior points of the curve, curve excluded

## Explanation

A polynomial is a continuous function, so by the theorem on **constant sign** $F$ has the same sign at all the points of each of the two regions into which the curve divides the plane. The point $P$ is an interior point of the curve and $F(P) = 5$, so $F$ is positive at all the interior points. The point $Q$ is an exterior point and $F(Q) = -3$, so $F$ is negative at all the exterior points. The inequality $F(x, y) \leq 0$ is therefore satisfied by the **exterior points** and, because of the symbol $\leq$, also by the points of the curve.
