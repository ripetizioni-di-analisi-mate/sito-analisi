In the figure the boundary is the parabola $y = 1 - x^2$. Which of the following functions has the shaded region as its domain?

::grafico{regione="y<1-x^2" xmin=-3.5 xmax=3.5 ymin=-5 ymax=2 uguale=1 titolo="The shaded region"}

- $f(x, y) = \sqrt{1 - x^2 - y}$
- $f(x, y) = \ln(x^2 + y - 1)$
- $f(x, y) = \ln(1 - x^2 - y)$
- $f(x, y) = \dfrac{1}{1 - x^2 - y}$

## Explanation

The shaded region lies below the parabola, which is dashed and therefore excluded, so the region is described by $y < 1 - x^2$, that is, by $1 - x^2 - y > 0$. The logarithm is defined where its argument is **greater than zero**, so the function with this domain is $f(x, y) = \ln(1 - x^2 - y)$. The root $\sqrt{1 - x^2 - y}$ is defined also where the radicand equals zero, so its domain includes the parabola. The domain of $\ln(x^2 + y - 1)$ is the region above the parabola, and the quotient $\dfrac{1}{1 - x^2 - y}$ is defined on the whole plane except the parabola.
