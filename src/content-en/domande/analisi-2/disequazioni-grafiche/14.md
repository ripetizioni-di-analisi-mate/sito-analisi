Which quadrant is entirely contained in the half-plane $x + 2y \geq -2$?

- The first quadrant
- The second quadrant
- The third quadrant
- The fourth quadrant

## Explanation

In the first quadrant we have $x \geq 0$ and $y \geq 0$, so $x + 2y \geq 0$, and $0$ is greater than $-2$. All the points of the **first quadrant** therefore satisfy the inequality. The boundary line $x + 2y = -2$ passes through $(-2, 0)$ and $(0, -1)$, so it crosses the other three quadrants, and each of them contains points that are not solutions. At the point $(-5, 1)$ of the second quadrant the left-hand side equals $-3$, at $(-2, -2)$ of the third it equals $-6$ and at $(1, -3)$ of the fourth it equals $-5$, three numbers less than $-2$.
