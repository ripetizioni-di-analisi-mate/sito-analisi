In the figure the boundary line passes through the points $(1, 0)$ and $(0, -3)$. Which inequality describes the shaded region?

::grafico{regione="3*x-y<3" xmin=-4 xmax=4 ymin=-4 ymax=4 uguale=1 titolo="The shaded region"}

- $3x - y > 3$
- $3x - y \leq 3$
- $3x - y < 3$
- $x - 3y < 3$

## Explanation

The line through $(1, 0)$ and $(0, -3)$ has slope $3$ and $y$-intercept $-3$, so its equation is $y = 3x - 3$, that is, $3x - y = 3$. The shaded region contains the origin, which is used as a **test point**. At the origin $3x - y$ equals $0$, which is less than $3$, so $3x - y < 3$ holds in the whole shaded region. The line is dashed, so it is excluded and the symbol is strict.
