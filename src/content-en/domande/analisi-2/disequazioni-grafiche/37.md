The circle in the figure has center $C(2, -3)$, passes through the point $(2, 0)$ and is drawn as a solid line. Which inequality describes the shaded region?

::grafico{regione="(x-2)^2+(y+3)^2>=9" xmin=-3 xmax=7 ymin=-8 ymax=2 uguale=1 punti="2,-3,pieno,C;2,0,pieno" titolo="The shaded region"}

- $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 3)^2 \geq 9$
- $(x + 2)^2 + (y - 3)^2 \geq 9$
- $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 3)^2 \geq 3$
- $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 3)^2 > 9$

## Explanation

The center is $C(2, -3)$, so the square of the distance of a point $(x, y)$ from the center is $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 3)^2$. The point $(2, 0)$ is at distance $3$ from the center, so the radius is $3$ and the equation of the circle is $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 3)^2 = 9$. The shaded region consists of the **exterior points**, where the square of the distance is greater than $9$. The circle drawn as a solid line is included, therefore the inequality is $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 3)^2 \geq 9$.
