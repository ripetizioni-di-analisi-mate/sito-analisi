Which set consists of the points of the plane that do not satisfy the inequality $x^2 + y^2 - 2y \geq 3$?

- The interior points of the circle with center $(0, 1)$ and radius $2$, circle included
- The interior points of the circle with center $(0, 1)$ and radius $2$, circle excluded
- The exterior points of the circle with center $(0, 1)$ and radius $2$, circle excluded
- The interior points of the circle with center $(0, 1)$ and radius $\sqrt{3}$, circle excluded

## Explanation

The points that do not satisfy $x^2 + y^2 - 2y \geq 3$ are those that satisfy $x^2 + y^2 - 2y < 3$. Completing the square in $y$ gives $x^2 + (y - 1)^2 - 1 < 3$, that is, $x^2 + (y - 1)^2 < 4$. The set therefore consists of the **interior points** of the circle with center $(0, 1)$ and radius $2$. The points of the circle satisfy the original inequality, because the symbol $\geq$ includes equality, so the circle is **excluded**.
