The shaded region is the triangle with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(0, 2)$ and $(4, 2)$, with the sides included. Which expression describes it as a normal domain with respect to the $x$-axis?

::grafico{regione="x>=0; y<=2; y>=x/2" xmin=-1.5 xmax=5.5 ymin=-2.5 ymax=4.5 uguale=1 tracce=0 punti="0,0,pieno;0,2,pieno;4,2,pieno" titolo="The shaded region"}

- $0 \leq x \leq 4$, $\ \dfrac{x}{2} \leq y \leq 2$
- $0 \leq x \leq 4$, $\ 0 \leq y \leq \dfrac{x}{2}$
- $0 \leq x \leq 4$, $\ 2x \leq y \leq 2$
- $0 \leq x \leq 2$, $\ \dfrac{x}{2} \leq y \leq 2$

## Explanation

The projection of the triangle onto the $x$-axis is the interval $[0, 4]$. The side joining $(0, 0)$ and $(4, 2)$ lies on the line $y = \dfrac{x}{2}$, and the side joining $(0, 2)$ and $(4, 2)$ lies on the line $y = 2$. Every vertical segment with $x$-coordinate between $0$ and $4$ enters the triangle through the line $y = \dfrac{x}{2}$, at the bottom, and exits through the line $y = 2$, at the top. Hence $g_1(x) = \dfrac{x}{2}$ and $g_2(x) = 2$, and the triangle is written $0 \leq x \leq 4$, $\dfrac{x}{2} \leq y \leq 2$.
