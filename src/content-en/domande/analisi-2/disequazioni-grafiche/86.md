The boundary of the set described by the system
$$
\begin{cases}
y \geq x^2 \\[4pt]
y \leq 8 - x^2
\end{cases}
$$
consists of two arcs of parabolas. At which points do the two arcs meet, that is, what are the vertices of the set?

- $(0, 0)$ and $(0, 8)$
- $(-2\sqrt{2}, 8)$ and $(2\sqrt{2}, 8)$
- $(-2, 4)$, $(2, 4)$, $(0, 0)$ and $(0, 8)$
- $(-2, 4)$ and $(2, 4)$

## Explanation

The **vertices** of a set are the points where two boundary pieces meet, so they are found by solving the system of the two equations $y = x^2$ and $y = 8 - x^2$. Equating the right-hand sides gives $x^2 = 8 - x^2$, that is, $x^2 = 4$, which has the solutions $x = -2$ and $x = 2$. In both cases $y = x^2 = 4$, therefore the vertices of the set are $(-2, 4)$ and $(2, 4)$. The points $(0, 0)$ and $(0, 8)$ are the vertices of the two parabolas. Each of them belongs to only one arc, so it is not a point where the two boundary pieces meet.
