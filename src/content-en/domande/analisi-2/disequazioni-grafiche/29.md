The domain of the function $f(x, y) = \ln(2x - y^2 + 4y)$ is bounded by a parabola with horizontal axis. Which description of the domain is correct?

- The points to the left of the parabola with vertex $(-2, 2)$, parabola excluded
- The points to the right of the parabola with vertex $(-2, 2)$, parabola excluded
- The points to the right of the parabola with vertex $(2, -2)$, parabola excluded
- The points to the right of the parabola with vertex $(-2, 2)$, parabola included

## Explanation

The function is defined where the argument of the logarithm is greater than zero, so the domain is described by $2x - y^2 + 4y > 0$. Isolating $x$ and completing the square we obtain
$$
x > \frac{1}{2}y^2 - 2y = \frac{1}{2}(y - 2)^2 - 2 .
$$
The vertex of the parabola has $y$-coordinate $y_V = 2$ and $x$-coordinate $x_V = -2$, so it is the point $(-2, 2)$. The variable $x$ is isolated with the symbol $>$, so the domain consists of the points **to the right** of the parabola, which is excluded.
