What is the domain of the function $f(x, y) = \sqrt{x - 2y + 4}$?

- The half-plane above the line $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x + 2$, line included
- The half-plane below the line $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x + 2$, line included
- The half-plane below the line $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x + 2$, line excluded
- The half-plane below the line $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x - 2$, line included

## Explanation

The function is defined where the radicand is greater than or equal to zero, so the domain is described by $x - 2y + 4 \geq 0$. Isolating the term with $y$ we obtain $-2y \geq -x - 4$. Dividing by $-2$, which is negative, the direction changes.
$$
y \leq \frac{1}{2}x + 2
$$
By the isolated variable rule the domain is the half-plane **below** the line $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x + 2$. The symbol is $\leq$, so the line is included.
