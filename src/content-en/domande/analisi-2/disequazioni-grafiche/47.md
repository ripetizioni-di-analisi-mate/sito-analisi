The projections of the region $(x - 1)^2 + 4(y + 2)^2 \leq 16$ onto the $x$-axis and onto the $y$-axis are two intervals. Which ones?

- $[-3, 5]$ on the $x$-axis and $[-4, 0]$ on the $y$-axis
- $[-1, 3]$ on the $x$-axis and $[-6, 2]$ on the $y$-axis
- $[-5, 3]$ on the $x$-axis and $[0, 4]$ on the $y$-axis
- $[-15, 17]$ on the $x$-axis and $[-6, 2]$ on the $y$-axis

## Explanation

Dividing both sides by $16$ we obtain
$$
\frac{(x - 1)^2}{16} + \frac{(y + 2)^2}{4} \leq 1 .
$$
The region consists of the interior points of the ellipse with center $(1, -2)$, horizontal semi-axis $a = 4$ and vertical semi-axis $b = 2$, ellipse included. The $x$-coordinates of its points range from $1 - 4 = -3$ to $1 + 4 = 5$, so the projection onto the $x$-axis is $[-3, 5]$. The $y$-coordinates range from $-2 - 2 = -4$ to $-2 + 2 = 0$, so the projection onto the $y$-axis is $[-4, 0]$.
