The inequality $x^2 + 2x - 4y - 7 > 0$ is rewritten with the variable $y$ isolated and the square completed. Which form is obtained?

- $y > \dfrac{1}{4}(x + 1)^2 - 2$
- $y < \dfrac{1}{4}(x - 1)^2 - 2$
- $y < \dfrac{1}{4}(x + 1)^2 - 2$
- $y < \dfrac{1}{4}(x + 1)^2 - \dfrac{7}{4}$

## Explanation

The term with $y$ is isolated and both sides are divided by $-4$, which is negative, so the direction changes.
$$
\begin{aligned}
-4y &> -x^2 - 2x + 7 \\[6pt]
y &< \frac{1}{4}\left( x^2 + 2x - 7 \right)
\end{aligned}
$$
Since $x^2 + 2x = (x + 1)^2 - 1$, we have $x^2 + 2x - 7 = (x + 1)^2 - 8$, therefore
$$
y < \frac{1}{4}(x + 1)^2 - 2 .
$$
The solutions are the points **below** the parabola with vertex $(-1, -2)$, parabola excluded.
