Let $A$ be the disk $x^2 + y^2 \leq 9$ and $B$ the region $9x^2 + y^2 \leq 9$. Which statement is true?

- $A$ is contained in $B$ and the two sets are different
- $A$ and $B$ coincide
- Neither of the two sets contains the other
- $B$ is contained in $A$ and the two sets are different

## Explanation

Dividing by $9$, the inequality of $B$ becomes $x^2 + \dfrac{y^2}{9} \leq 1$, so $B$ consists of the interior points of the ellipse with horizontal semi-axis $1$ and vertical semi-axis $3$, ellipse included. At every point of $B$ we have $x^2 + y^2 \leq 9x^2 + y^2 \leq 9$, so the point also belongs to the disk $A$. Therefore $B$ **is contained** in $A$. The point $(2, 0)$ belongs to $A$, because $4 \leq 9$, and does not belong to $B$, because $9 \cdot 4 = 36$ is greater than $9$. The two sets are therefore different.
