Which of the following inequalities is satisfied by all the points of the plane?

- $x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 8y + 17 > 0$
- $x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 8y + 16 \geq 0$
- $x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 8y + 18 \leq 0$
- $x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 8y + 18 > 0$

## Explanation

Completing the squares gives $x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 8y = (x + 1)^2 + (y - 4)^2 - 17$. The inequality $x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 8y + 18 > 0$ therefore becomes $(x + 1)^2 + (y - 4)^2 > -1$. A sum of squares is greater than or equal to zero, so this inequality is true at **all the points of the plane**, while $(x + 1)^2 + (y - 4)^2 \leq -1$ has no solutions. With the constant term $17$ we obtain $(x + 1)^2 + (y - 4)^2 > 0$, which is false at the point $(-1, 4)$. With the constant term $16$ we obtain $(x + 1)^2 + (y - 4)^2 \geq 1$, which is false at the points inside the circle with center $(-1, 4)$ and radius $1$.
