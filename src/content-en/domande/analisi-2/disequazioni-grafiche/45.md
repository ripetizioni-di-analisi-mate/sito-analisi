The region $\dfrac{x^2}{4} + \dfrac{y^2}{25} > 1$ has as boundary an ellipse with center at the origin. Which statement about the point $(2, 0)$ is true?

- It is an interior point of the ellipse and does not belong to the region
- It is an exterior point of the ellipse and belongs to the region
- It is a vertex of the ellipse and belongs to the region
- It is a vertex of the ellipse and does not belong to the region

## Explanation

The denominator of $x^2$ is $a^2 = 4$, so the horizontal semi-axis is $a = 2$ and the vertices on the $x$-axis are $(\pm 2, 0)$. At the point $(2, 0)$ the left-hand side equals $\dfrac{4}{4} + 0 = 1$, so the point lies on the ellipse. The symbol $>$ does not include equality, therefore the boundary is **excluded** and the point does not belong to the region. The region consists of the exterior points of the ellipse, where the left-hand side is greater than $1$.
