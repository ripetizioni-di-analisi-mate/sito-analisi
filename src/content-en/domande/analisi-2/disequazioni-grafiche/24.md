What is the greatest $y$-coordinate of the points of the region $y \leq -x^2 + 4x - 1$?

- $3$
- $2$
- $-1$
- It does not exist, because the region is unbounded above

## Explanation

The region consists of the points below the parabola $y = -x^2 + 4x - 1$, parabola included. The coefficient of $x^2$ is negative, so the parabola opens downward and its highest point is the **vertex**. The $x$-coordinate of the vertex is $x_V = -\dfrac{4}{2 \cdot (-1)} = 2$ and the $y$-coordinate is $y_V = -4 + 8 - 1 = 3$. Indeed $-x^2 + 4x - 1 = 3 - (x - 2)^2$, so every point of the region has $y \leq 3$. The vertex $(2, 3)$ belongs to the region, therefore the greatest $y$-coordinate is $3$.
