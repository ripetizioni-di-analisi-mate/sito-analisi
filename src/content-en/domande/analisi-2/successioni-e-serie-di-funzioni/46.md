What is the supremum of $|f_n|$ on $[0,5]$ for $f_n=\dfrac{2 x}{n + x + 3}$?

- $\dfrac{10}{n}$
- $\dfrac{2}{n + 4}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{10}{n + 8}$

## Explanation

The derivative with respect to $x$ is positive throughout the interval. The maximum is attained at the right endpoint $5$.
