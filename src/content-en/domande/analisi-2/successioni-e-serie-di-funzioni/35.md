For $x > 0$, what is the sum of the series of functions $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} e^{-n x}$?

- $\dfrac{e^{-x}}{1 - e^{-x}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{1 + e^{-x}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{1 - e^{-x}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{1 - e^{x}}$

## Explanation

We have $e^{-n x} = \left( e^{-x} \right)^n$, so the series is the geometric series with ratio $q = e^{-x}$. For $x > 0$ we have $0 < e^{-x} < 1$, therefore the series converges and its sum is
$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} q^n = \frac{1}{1 - q} = \frac{1}{1 - e^{-x}} .
$$
The first term, for $n = 0$, equals $e^0 = 1$ and is part of the sum; the series starting from $n = 1$ has sum $\dfrac{e^{-x}}{1 - e^{-x}}$.
