What is the set of pointwise convergence of the series of functions $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{e^{n x}}{n^2}$?

- $(-\infty, 0)$
- $\mathbb{R}$
- $(-\infty, 0]$
- $[0, +\infty)$

## Explanation

The terms are positive. By the **root test** we have $\sqrt[n]{\dfrac{e^{n x}}{n^2}} = \dfrac{e^x}{\left( \sqrt[n]{n} \right)^2} \to e^x$, so the series converges for $e^x < 1$, that is, for $x < 0$, and diverges for $x > 0$. For $x = 0$ we obtain the series $\sum \dfrac{1}{n^2}$, which converges. The set of convergence is therefore $(-\infty, 0]$. On this set we have $\dfrac{e^{n x}}{n^2} \leq \dfrac{1}{n^2}$, so the convergence is also **total**.
