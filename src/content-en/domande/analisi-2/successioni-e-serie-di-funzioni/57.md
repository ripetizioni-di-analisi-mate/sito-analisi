What happens at $x=5$ for $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \dfrac{(-1)^n(x/5)^n}{n}$?

- The term oscillates without tending to zero
- Diverges to $+\infty$
- Converges, but the absolute series diverges
- Converges absolutely

## Explanation

At that point the term is $(-1)^n/n$ and the alternating series test applies. The absolute values form the harmonic series.
