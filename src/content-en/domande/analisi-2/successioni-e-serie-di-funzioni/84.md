What is the supremum of $|f_n|$ on $[0,28/(5)]$ for $f_n(x)=\left(\dfrac{x}{7}\right)^{n}$?

- $\left(\dfrac{1}{5}\right)^{n}$
- $\left(\dfrac{4}{5}\right)^{n}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The function is increasing on the interval. At the right endpoint the ratio $x/7$ is $\dfrac{4}{5}$.
