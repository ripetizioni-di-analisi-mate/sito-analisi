- Converges uniformly on $\mathbb{R}$, but not totally
- Converges totally only on every bounded interval
- Converges pointwise on $\mathbb{R}$, but not uniformly
- Converges totally on $\mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

For every $x \in \mathbb{R}$ we have $|\cos(n x)| \leq 1$, so
$$
\left| \frac{\cos(n x)}{n^2 + 1} \right| \leq \frac{1}{n^2 + 1} = M_n .
$$
Since $\dfrac{1}{n^2 + 1} \leq \dfrac{1}{n^2}$ and the series $\sum \dfrac{1}{n^2}$ converges, by the **comparison test** $\sum M_n$ also converges. The bound holds with the same $M_n$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$, therefore the series **converges totally** on $\mathbb{R}$. By the Weierstrass test the series then also converges uniformly on $\mathbb{R}$.
