For the sequence of functions $f_n(x) = x^n (1 - x)$ on $[0, 1]$, which converges pointwise to $f = 0$, what is $M_n = \displaystyle\max_{x \in [0, 1]} |f_n(x)|$?

- $\dfrac{1}{n + 1}$
- $\left( \dfrac{n}{n + 1} \right)^{n}$
- $\left( \dfrac{n}{n + 1} \right)^{n + 1}$
- $\dfrac{n^n}{(n + 1)^{n + 1}}$

## Explanation

On $[0, 1]$ we have $f_n \geq 0$. The derivative is
$$
f_n'(x) = n x^{n-1} (1 - x) - x^n = x^{n-1} \bigl( n - (n + 1) x \bigr) ,
$$
so $f_n$ is increasing on $\left[ 0, \dfrac{n}{n + 1} \right]$ and decreasing on $\left[ \dfrac{n}{n + 1}, 1 \right]$. Therefore the maximum is
$$
M_n = \left( \frac{n}{n + 1} \right)^{n} \cdot \frac{1}{n + 1} = \frac{n^n}{(n + 1)^{n + 1}} .
$$
Since $\left( \dfrac{n}{n + 1} \right)^n \leq 1$, we have $M_n \leq \dfrac{1}{n + 1} \to 0$, so by the **sup criterion** the convergence is **uniform** on $[0, 1]$.
