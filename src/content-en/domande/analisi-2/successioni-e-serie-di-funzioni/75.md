What is the pointwise limit for fixed $x\in\mathbb R$ of $\left(1 + \dfrac{3 x}{n}\right)^{n}$?

- $1$
- $3 x + 1$
- $e^{3 x}$
- $e^{\dfrac{x}{3}}$

## Explanation

For fixed $x$ the base is eventually positive. The logarithm of the power tends to the linear coefficient of the increment.
