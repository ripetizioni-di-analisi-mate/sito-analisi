At which points of $\mathbb{R}$ is the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{n x}{n + 1}$ convergent?

- Only at $x = 0$
- At every $x \in \mathbb{R}$
- For $|x| < 1$
- For $|x| \leq 1$

## Explanation

For $x = 0$ all terms vanish, so the series converges. For $x \neq 0$ the general term $\dfrac{n x}{n + 1}$ tends to $x \neq 0$, so the **necessary condition** for convergence is not satisfied and the series does not converge. The set of convergence is therefore $\{0\}$.
