On $[0, 1]$ the sequence $f_n(x) = \dfrac{x^n}{n}$ converges uniformly to $0$. To which function does the sequence of derivatives $f_n'$ converge pointwise?

- To $0$ on all of $[0, 1]$
- To $1$ on all of $[0, 1]$
- To $0$ on $(0, 1]$ and to $1$ at $x = 0$
- To $0$ on $[0, 1)$ and to $1$ at $x = 1$

## Explanation

The derivative is $f_n'(x) = x^{n-1}$. For $0 \leq x < 1$ we have $x^{n-1} \to 0$, while $f_n'(1) = 1$ for every $n$, so the limit of the derivatives equals $0$ on $[0, 1)$ and $1$ at $x = 1$. The derivative of the limit $f = 0$, instead, vanishes at $x = 1$ as well. The **differentiation theorem for sequences** does not apply on $[0, 1]$, because the limit of the $f_n'$ is discontinuous and therefore the convergence of the derivatives is not uniform.
