A bound for $|u_n(x)|$ is $2^{-n}$ on $[0,1]$ and $3^{-n}$ on $[1,2]$. Which choice of $M_n$ establishes total convergence on the union without splitting the series again?

- $M_n=3^{-n}$ always suffices.
- $M_n=6^{-n}$ always suffices.
- $M_n=2^{-n}$
- $M_n=1$ gives a summable bound.

## Explanation

The larger bound holds on both intervals, namely $2^{-n}$ for $n\ge1$. Its geometric series converges, so the Weierstrass test applies on the union.
