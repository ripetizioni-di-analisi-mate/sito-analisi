What is the sum of $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \left(\dfrac{x}{3}\right)^n$ for $|x|<3$?

- $\dfrac{x}{x + 3}$
- $\dfrac{x}{3 - x}$
- $\dfrac{x}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{3 - x}$

## Explanation

The ratio is $x/3$ and equals the first term. The sum is the first term divided by one minus the ratio.
