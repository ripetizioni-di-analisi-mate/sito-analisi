What is the exact uniform bound for a single term of the series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \dfrac{\cos(nx)}{5^n}$ with base $5$?

- $\dfrac{1}{n}$
- $1$
- $5^{- n}$
- $5^{- 2 n}$

## Explanation

The cosine has absolute value at most one and attains it at zero. The supremum of the term equals the reciprocal of the power.
