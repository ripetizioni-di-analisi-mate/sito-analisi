Which convergence description for $\left(\dfrac{x}{7}\right)^{n}$ on $[0,7)$ is correct?

- On $[0,7)$ it converges pointwise to zero, with uniform error equal to one
- On $[0,7)$ it converges pointwise to one
- On $[0,7)$ it converges uniformly to zero
- On $[0,7)$ the pointwise limit exists only at zero

## Explanation

At each fixed point the ratio $x/7$ is below one. Its supremum on the interval is one even after raising it to the $n$th power.
