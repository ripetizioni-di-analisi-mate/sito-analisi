For $x \geq 0$, what is the sum of the telescoping series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left( \frac{1}{n + x} - \frac{1}{n + 1 + x} \right)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2 + x}$
- $\dfrac{1}{1 + x}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{x}$

## Explanation

In the partial sum each term $-\dfrac{1}{n + 1 + x}$ cancels with the first term of the next summand, so
$$
s_N(x) = \frac{1}{1 + x} - \frac{1}{N + 1 + x} .
$$
As $N \to +\infty$ the second term tends to $0$, so the sum equals $\dfrac{1}{1 + x}$ for every $x \geq 0$.
