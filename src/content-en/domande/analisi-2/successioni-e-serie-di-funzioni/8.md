- $e$
- $e - 1$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

The pointwise limit of $x^n e^x$ equals $0$ on $[0, 1)$ and $e$ at $x = 1$, so it is discontinuous and the convergence is not uniform on $[0, 1]$. The integral is therefore estimated with an upper bound. On $[0, 1]$ we have $0 < e^x \leq e$, so $0 \leq x^n e^x \leq e \, x^n$, and integrating we obtain
$$
0 \leq \int_0^1 x^n e^x \, dx \leq e \int_0^1 x^n \, dx = \frac{e}{n + 1} .
$$
Since $\dfrac{e}{n + 1} \to 0$, by the **squeeze theorem** the limit equals $0$.
