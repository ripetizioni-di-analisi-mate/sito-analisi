Which statement holds for $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \dfrac{x}{n^2+2}$ on $[0,5]$?

- Converges only pointwise on the stated interval
- The term fails to tend to zero at the right endpoint
- Diverges at every positive point of the interval
- Converges normally on the stated interval

## Explanation

The term magnitude is at most $5/n^2$ on the interval. The series of bounds converges.
