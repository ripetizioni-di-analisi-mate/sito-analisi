What is the pointwise limit for fixed $x\in\mathbb R$ of $\left(1 + \dfrac{2 x}{n}\right)^{n}$?

- $e^{\dfrac{x}{2}}$
- $e^{2 x}$
- $2 x + 1$
- $1$

## Explanation

For every fixed $x$ the base is positive from some index onward. The logarithm of the power tends to the linear coefficient of the increment.
