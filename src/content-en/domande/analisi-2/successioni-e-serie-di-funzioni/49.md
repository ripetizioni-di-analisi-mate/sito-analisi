The sequence $f_n(x) = \dfrac{e^x}{n}$ tends to $0$ at every point of $\mathbb{R}$. On which of the following sets is the convergence uniform?

- $\mathbb{R}$
- $(-\infty, 5]$
- $[0, +\infty)$
- $[-5, +\infty)$

## Explanation

Since $e^x$ is increasing, on $(-\infty, 5]$ we have $0 < f_n(x) \leq \dfrac{e^5}{n}$, so $\sup_{(-\infty, 5]} |f_n| = \dfrac{e^5}{n} \to 0$ and the convergence is uniform. The other three sets are unbounded on the right, and on each of them $\sup |f_n| = +\infty$ for every $n$, because $e^x \to +\infty$ as $x \to +\infty$. Therefore on $\mathbb{R}$, on $[0, +\infty)$ and on $[-5, +\infty)$ the convergence is not uniform.
