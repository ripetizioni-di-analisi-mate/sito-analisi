What is the supremum of $|f_n|$ on $[0,6/(4)]$ for $f_n(x)=\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)^{n}$?

- $\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^{n}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^{n}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The function is increasing on the interval. At the right endpoint the ratio $x/2$ is $\dfrac{3}{4}$.
