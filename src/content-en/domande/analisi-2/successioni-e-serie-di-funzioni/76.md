What is the supremum of $|f_n|$ on $[0,7]$ for $f_n=\dfrac{4 x}{n + x + 2}$?

- $\dfrac{28}{n + 9}$
- $\dfrac{4}{n + 3}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{28}{n}$

## Explanation

The derivative with respect to $x$ is positive throughout the interval. The maximum is attained at the right endpoint $7$.
