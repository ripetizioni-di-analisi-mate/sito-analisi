What is the supremum of $|f_n|$ on $[0,2]$ for $f_n=\dfrac{3 x}{n + x + 5}$?

- $\dfrac{3}{n + 6}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{6}{n}$
- $\dfrac{6}{n + 7}$

## Explanation

The derivative with respect to $x$ is positive throughout the interval. The maximum is attained at the right endpoint $2$.
