The sequence $f_n(x) = \dfrac{x - x^2}{n}$ converges pointwise to $0$ on $[0, 1]$. What is $M_n = \displaystyle\sup_{x \in [0, 1]} |f_n(x)|$?

- $\dfrac{1}{n}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2n}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4n}$
- $0$

## Explanation

On $[0, 1]$ we have $x - x^2 = x(1 - x) \geq 0$, so $|f_n(x)| = \dfrac{x - x^2}{n}$. The graph of $x - x^2$ is a downward parabola with vertex at $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$, therefore its maximum on $[0, 1]$ is $\dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{1}{4}$. Hence $M_n = \dfrac{1}{4n}$. Since $M_n \to 0$, by the **supremum criterion** the convergence is uniform on $[0, 1]$.
