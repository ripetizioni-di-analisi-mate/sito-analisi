Let $f_n$ equal $\dfrac{1}{n}$ at the single point $x=n$ and vanish elsewhere, for $x\ge1$. Does $\sum_{n\ge1}f_n$ converge uniformly, totally, both, or neither?

- It converges uniformly, but the series of sup norms diverges.
- It converges both totally and uniformly.
- It converges totally but not uniformly.
- It converges only pointwise.

## Explanation

At most one term is nonzero at any point, so the supremum of the tail after $N$ terms equals $\dfrac{1}{N+1}$ and tends to zero. Individual sup norms are $\dfrac{1}{n}$ and their sum diverges.
