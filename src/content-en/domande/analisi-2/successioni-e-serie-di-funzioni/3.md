The sequence of functions $f_n(x) = \dfrac{x}{n}$ converges pointwise to $0$ on $\mathbb{R}$. At the point $x = 5$, with tolerance $\varepsilon = \dfrac{1}{10}$, what is the smallest index $N$ such that $|f_n(5)| < \varepsilon$ for every $n \geq N$?

- $N = 51$
- $N = 11$
- $N = 500$
- $N = 6$

## Explanation

At the point $x = 5$ we have $|f_n(5)| = \dfrac{5}{n}$. The inequality $\dfrac{5}{n} < \dfrac{1}{10}$ is equivalent to $n > 50$, so the smallest index satisfying it is $N = 51$. For $n = 50$ we get $\dfrac{5}{50} = \dfrac{1}{10}$, which is not less than $\varepsilon$. Since $\dfrac{5}{n}$ decreases as $n$ grows, the inequality holds for every $n \geq 51$. At the point $x = 10$ the same computation gives $N = 101$, so in **pointwise convergence** the index $N$ also depends on the point $x$.
