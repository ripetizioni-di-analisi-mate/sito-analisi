What is the supremum of $\left|\dfrac{x^2}{n}\right|$ as $x$ ranges over the interval $[-1, 3]$?

- $\dfrac{9}{n}$
- $\dfrac{1}{n}$
- $\dfrac{3}{n}$
- $\dfrac{10}{n}$

## Explanation

On $[-1, 3]$ the function $x^2$ attains its maximum at the point farthest from the origin, that is, at $x = 3$, where it equals $9$. Hence $\sup_{[-1, 3]} |f_n| = \dfrac{9}{n}$ for $f_n(x) = \dfrac{x^2}{n}$. Since $\dfrac{9}{n} \to 0$, by the **supremum criterion** the convergence of $f_n$ to $0$ is uniform on $[-1, 3]$.
