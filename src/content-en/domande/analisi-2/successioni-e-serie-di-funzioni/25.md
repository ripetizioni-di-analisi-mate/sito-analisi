For the sequence of functions $f_n(x) = x^n \ln x$ on $(0, 1]$, which converges pointwise to $f = 0$, what is $M_n = \displaystyle\sup_{x \in (0, 1]} |f_n(x)|$?

- $\dfrac{1}{e}$
- $\dfrac{1}{n e}$
- $\dfrac{1}{n}$
- $\dfrac{e}{n}$

## Explanation

On $(0, 1]$ we have $\ln x \leq 0$, so $|f_n(x)| = -x^n \ln x$. The derivative of $g(x) = -x^n \ln x$ is
$$
g'(x) = -n x^{n-1} \ln x - x^{n-1} = -x^{n-1} (n \ln x + 1) ,
$$
which is positive for $\ln x < -\dfrac{1}{n}$ and negative for $\ln x > -\dfrac{1}{n}$. Therefore $g$ has its maximum at $x = e^{-\frac{1}{n}}$, where $x^n = e^{-1}$ and $\ln x = -\dfrac{1}{n}$, and the maximum equals
$$
M_n = -e^{-1} \cdot \left( -\frac{1}{n} \right) = \frac{1}{n e} .
$$
Since $M_n \to 0$, by the **supremum criterion** the convergence is **uniform** on $(0, 1]$.
