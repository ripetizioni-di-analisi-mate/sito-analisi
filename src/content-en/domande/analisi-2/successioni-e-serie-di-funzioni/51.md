What is the uniform limit on $\mathbb R$ of the sequence $\dfrac{\sin{\left(n x \right)}}{3 n}$?

- $0$
- $\sin{\left(x \right)}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The magnitude is at most the reciprocal of the denominator. This bound is independent of $x$ and tends to zero.
