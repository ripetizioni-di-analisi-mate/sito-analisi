What happens at $x=7$ for $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \dfrac{(-1)^n(x/7)^n}{n}$?

- Diverges to $+\infty$
- Converges, but the series of absolute values diverges
- The general term oscillates without tending to zero
- Converges absolutely

## Explanation

At that point the term is $(-1)^n/n$ and the alternating series test applies. The absolute values form the harmonic series.
