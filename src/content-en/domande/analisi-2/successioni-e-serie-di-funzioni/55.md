What is the exact uniform bound for one term in $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \dfrac{\cos(nx)}{3^n}$ with base $3$?

- $1$
- $3^{- n}$
- $3^{- 2 n}$
- $\dfrac{1}{n}$

## Explanation

Cosine has magnitude at most one and attains it at zero. Thus the term’s supremum equals the reciprocal power.
