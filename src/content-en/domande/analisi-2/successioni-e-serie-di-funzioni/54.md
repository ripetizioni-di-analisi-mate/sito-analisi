What is the supremum of $|f_n|$ on $[0,10/(3)]$ for $f_n(x)=\left(\dfrac{x}{5}\right)^{n}$?

- $\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^{n}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{n}$

## Explanation

The function is increasing on the interval. At the right endpoint the ratio $x/5$ is $\dfrac{2}{3}$.
