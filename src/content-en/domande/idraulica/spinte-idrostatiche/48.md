A flat vertical gate closes a trapezoidal channel $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ wide at the bottom and $4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ wide at the surface. The water depth is $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ as shown. What force does the water exert on the gate?

::schema{acqua="0,0 2,0 3,2 -1,2" solidi="-1.8,2.3 -1.15,2.3 0,0 0,-0.5 -1.8,-0.5;2,0 3.15,2.3 3.8,2.3 3.8,-0.5 2,-0.5;0,-0.5 2,-0.5 2,0 0,0" pelo="-1,3,2," quote="0 0,2 0,b = 2.00 m,0.85;-1 2,3 2,B = 4.00 m,-0.5;3.8 0,3.8 2,h = 2.00 m,0.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Trapezoidal channel cross section closed by the gate"}

- $58.9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $78.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $65.4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $52.3\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The gate has the shape of the wetted cross section, a trapezoid with its longer base $B = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ at the surface and shorter base $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ at the bottom. Its area is $A = \dfrac{(4.00 + 2.00) \cdot 2.00}{2} = 6.00\,\mathrm{m}^2$. For a trapezoid of height $h$ the centroid lies at a distance from base $B$ of
$$
h_G = \frac{h\,(B + 2b)}{3\,(B + b)} = \frac{2.00 \cdot 8.00}{3 \cdot 6.00} = 0.889\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
placing it nearer the wider base, where most of the area lies. Thus $S = 9.81 \cdot 0.889 \cdot 6.00 = 52.3\,\mathrm{kN}$. Taking the centroid at midheight would give $58.9\,\mathrm{kN}$. Measuring its distance from the bottom instead of the free surface would give $65.4\,\mathrm{kN}$.
