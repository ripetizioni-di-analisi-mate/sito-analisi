A boat floating in a tank carries an iron block. The block is thrown overboard and sinks. What happens to the water level in the tank?

- It rises.
- It stays the same.
- It rises or falls depending on the tank depth.
- It falls.

## Explanation

While the iron block is aboard, the floating boat displaces an additional volume $\dfrac{\rho_{\mathrm{Fe}}}{\rho}\,V$ whose weight equals the weight of the block. Here $V$ is the block volume and $\rho_{\mathrm{Fe}}$ the density of iron. Once on the bottom, it displaces only its own volume $V$. Since $\rho_{\mathrm{Fe}} > \rho$, the displaced volume decreases and the tank water level **falls**.
