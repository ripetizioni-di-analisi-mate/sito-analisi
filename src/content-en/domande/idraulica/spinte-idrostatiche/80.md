A rectangular concrete block rests on the bottom of a tank full of water and is fully submerged. Its base seals tightly against the bottom, preventing water from entering underneath. What vertical force does the water exert on the block?

- It acts upward and equals the weight of water displaced by the block.
- It acts downward and equals the weight of the water column above the top face.
- It is zero because the forces on the side faces cancel.
- It acts upward and equals the weight of the water column above the top face.

## Explanation

Archimedes’ principle requires liquid pressure around the body. Here no water reaches the base, so evaluate each face separately. The vertical sides carry horizontal forces. The top face, at depth $h$, experiences a downward force $\gamma\,h\,A$, and water exerts no pressure under the block. Its vertical force is therefore **downward**, equal to the weight of the water column above the top face. It presses the block against the bottom.
