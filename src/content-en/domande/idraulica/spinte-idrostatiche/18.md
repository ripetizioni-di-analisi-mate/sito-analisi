A square gate of side $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ is mounted in a wall inclined at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal. Its centroid is at depth $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$. What force does the water exert on the gate?

- $14.1\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $56.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $28.3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $24.0\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The gate area is $A = 1.20^2 = 1.44\,\mathrm{m}^2$ and the vertical depth of its centroid is $h_G = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$. The hydrostatic force formula is independent of inclination, giving
$$
S = \gamma\,h_G\,A = 9.81 \cdot 2.00 \cdot 1.44 = 28.3\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
normal to the gate. Using the distance $\dfrac{2.00}{\sin 30^\circ} = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ measured along the inclined plane as the vertical depth would give $56.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. Multiplying the force by $\sin 30^\circ$ would give $14.1\,\mathrm{kN}$.
