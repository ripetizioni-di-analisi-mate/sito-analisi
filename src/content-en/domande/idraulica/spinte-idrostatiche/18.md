Two liquids are separated by a fully submerged vertical flat hatch of area $0.60\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Their atmospheric free surfaces are at the same elevation, $2\,\mathrm m$ above the hatch centroid. Densities are $1000$ and $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the net thrust?

- $2.4\,\mathrm{kN}$ towards the denser liquid
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ towards the less dense liquid
- $2.4\,\mathrm{kN}$ towards the less dense liquid
- Zero

## Explanation

Atmospheric pressure cancels, and both pressures vary linearly with the same depth coordinate. Their difference at the centroid is $(1000-800)\cdot10\cdot2=4000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Multiplication by the area gives $2400\,\mathrm N$.
