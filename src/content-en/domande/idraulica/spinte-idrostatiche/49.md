A horizontal pipe of diameter $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ is closed by a vertical blind flange. At the flange the pressure head at the pipe centerline is $\dfrac{p}{\gamma} = 25.0\,\mathrm{m}$. What force does the water exert on the flange?

- $69.3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $17.3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $22.1\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $24.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The hydrostatic head plane is $25.0\,\mathrm{m}$ above the pipe axis, so the flange centroid on that axis is at depth $h_G = 25.0\,\mathrm{m}$ below the head plane. The force is therefore
$$
S = \gamma\,h_G\,\frac{\pi D^2}{4} = 9.81 \cdot 25.0 \cdot \frac{\pi \cdot 0.300^2}{4} = 17.3\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The center of pressure is only $\dfrac{D^2}{16\,h_G} = 0.000225\,\mathrm{m}$ below the center of the flange, so the force acts practically through the center. Atmospheric pressure acts on the outside face. Use gauge pressure for the net force. Absolute pressure would give $24.5\,\mathrm{kN}$.
