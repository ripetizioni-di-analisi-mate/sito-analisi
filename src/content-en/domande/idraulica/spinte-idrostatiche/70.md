A cylindrical roller gate of radius $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ rests on a channel bed. The upstream water reaches its top, $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ above the bed, and the downstream depth is $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ as shown. What is the horizontal component of the net force per meter of roller length?

::schema{acqua="-3,0 0,0 -0.5,0.134 -0.866,0.5 -1,1 -0.866,1.5 -0.5,1.866 0,2 -3,2;0,0 3,0 3,1 1,1 0.866,0.5 0.5,0.134" poligoni="1,1 0.9659,1.259 0.866,1.5 0.7071,1.707 0.5,1.866 0.2588,1.966 0,2 -0.2588,1.966 -0.5,1.866 -0.7071,1.707 -0.866,1.5 -0.9659,1.259 -1,1 -0.9659,0.7412 -0.866,0.5 -0.7071,0.2929 -0.5,0.134 -0.2588,0.03407 0,0 0.2588,0.03407 0.5,0.134 0.7071,0.2929 0.866,0.5 0.9659,0.7412" solidi="-3.4,-0.6 3.4,-0.6 3.4,0 -3.4,0" pelo="-3,-1.6,2,upstream;1.6,3,1,downstream" quote="-3 0,-3 2,2.00 m,-0.6;3 0,3 1,1.00 m,0.6" punti="0,1,O,centro" riferimento=0 titolo="Roller gate of radius $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ with water depths $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ upstream and $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ downstream"}

- $4.91\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $14.7\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $19.6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $9.81\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

The horizontal water force on each side of the cylinder equals the force on its vertical projection. The upstream projection extends $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ from the bottom to the free surface and gives force $9.81 \cdot \dfrac{2.00^2}{2} = 19.6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per meter directed downstream. The downstream projection has height $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and gives $9.81 \cdot \dfrac{1.00^2}{2} = 4.91\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ upstream. The net horizontal force is $19.6 - 4.91 = 14.7\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ downstream. Using the difference in water levels as a single water depth would give $4.91\,\mathrm{kN/m}$.
