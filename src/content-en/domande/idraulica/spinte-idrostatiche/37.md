A cylindrical sector forms the curved wall of a tank. The pivot coincides with the cylinder axis. Pressure on the surface varies with depth. What hydrostatic torque about the pivot is contributed by the cylindrical surface alone?

- Zero even when the pressure varies with depth.
- The horizontal thrust times the radius.
- The weight of overlying liquid times the radius.
- Zero only for uniform pressure over the surface.

## Explanation

Every pressure element is normal to the cylindrical surface and acts on a line intersecting the axis. Each element therefore has zero torque about that axis before integration.
