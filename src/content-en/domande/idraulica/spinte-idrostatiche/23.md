A circular gate of diameter $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ is mounted in a vertical wall with its center at depth $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and a hinge along its horizontal diameter. What moment do the pressure forces exert about the hinge?

- $0.394\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0.197\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0.592\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $5.92\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The moment of the elemental pressure forces about the centroidal axis is $M_G = \gamma \sin\alpha\,I_G$. The wall is vertical, so $\sin\alpha = 1$. For a circle of diameter $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$,
$$
I_G = \frac{\pi \cdot 0.80^4}{64} = 0.0201\,\mathrm{m}^4 ,
$$
giving $M = 9.81 \cdot 0.0201 = 0.197\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The result is independent of depth because the force grows with $y_G$ while its moment arm $\dfrac{I_G}{y_G A}$ decreases in the same proportion. Omitting $y_G$ from the denominator of the moment arm would give $0.592\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
