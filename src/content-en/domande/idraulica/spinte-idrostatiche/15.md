A vertical rectangular gate of width $1.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ has its top edge at depth $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$. What force does the water exert on it?

- $88.3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $58.9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $29.4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $44.1\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The lower edge lies at depth $1.00 + 2.00 = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$, placing the gate centroid at depth $h_G = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$. Its area is $A = 1.50 \cdot 2.00 = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}^2$, so
$$
S = \gamma\,h_G\,A = 9.81 \cdot 2.00 \cdot 3.00 = 58.9\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
Using the depth of the lower edge instead of the centroid gives $88.3\,\mathrm{kN}$. Using the upper-edge depth gives $29.4\,\mathrm{kN}$.
