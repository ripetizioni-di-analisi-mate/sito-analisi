A sensor reads gauge pressure $p_G$ at the area centroid of a fully submerged flat window. A technician proposes using $p_G A$ for the total force without knowing the inclination. Under which condition must this proposal be reconsidered?

- The window is triangular and the liquid density is uniform.
- The window spans the interface between liquids of different densities.
- The window is tilted in a homogeneous liquid.
- A uniform extra pressure is already included in the reading.

## Explanation

For an affine pressure distribution, the area mean equals the value at the centroid. Across an interface the hydrostatic gradient changes, so the contributions from the two portions must be integrated separately.
