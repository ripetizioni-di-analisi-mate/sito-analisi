A circular gate of diameter $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ is mounted in a vertical wall with its center at depth $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$. How far from the center is the center of pressure?

- $41.7\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.30\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $20.8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $27.8\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

By symmetry the center of pressure lies on the vertical diameter, so find only its distance below the centroid. A circle of diameter $D$ has $I_G = \dfrac{\pi D^4}{64} = 0.0491\,\mathrm{m}^4$ and $A = \dfrac{\pi D^2}{4} = 0.785\,\mathrm{m}^2$, giving
$$
y_C - y_G = \frac{I_G}{y_G\,A} = \frac{0.0491}{3.00 \cdot 0.785} = 0.0208\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Equivalently, the result is $\dfrac{D^2}{16\,y_G} = \dfrac{1.00}{48} = 20.8\,\mathrm{mm}$. Using the rectangular second moment $\dfrac{\ell^2}{12\,y_G}$ with $\ell = D$ would give $27.8\,\mathrm{mm}$.
