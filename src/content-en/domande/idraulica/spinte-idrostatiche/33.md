A quarter-cylindrical fillet of radius $1.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and width $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ joins a vertical wall to the bottom. The water depth is $4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ as shown. What is the horizontal component of the hydrostatic force on the fillet?

- $120\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $196\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $147\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $27.6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The vertical projection of the curved face is a rectangle of height $R = 1.50\,\mathrm{m}$, width $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$, and area $A_x = 3.75\,\mathrm{m}^2$. It extends between depths $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4.00\,\mathrm{m}$, so its centroid depth is $h_G = 3.25\,\mathrm{m}$. The horizontal force is
$$
S_x = \gamma\,h_G\,A_x = 9.81 \cdot 3.25 \cdot 3.75 = 120\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
toward the wall. Projecting the full water depth instead of just the height of the curved face would give $196\,\mathrm{kN}$.
