Two curved panels have identical projections on a vertical plane and contact the same water on the same side at the same elevations. The second bulges further, and each projects without overlap. Which force component can be compared directly from these projections?

- The component normal to the vertical plane is identical for both panels.
- The vertical component is identical.
- The magnitude of the resultant is identical.
- The resultant necessarily has the same line of action.

## Explanation

The force component normal to the plane is the integral of $p\,dA_{\rm projected}$ at identical elevations. A different curvature can change the volume of fluid above the panel and its vertical force component.
