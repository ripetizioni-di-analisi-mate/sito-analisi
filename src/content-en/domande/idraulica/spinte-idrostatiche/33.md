## Explanation

The horizontal component of the force on a curved surface equals the force on its projection on a vertical plane, because each surface element receives a horizontal force equal to the pressure times the area of its projection. The two panels have the same projection at the same depths, so they have the same **horizontal component**. The vertical component is instead $\gamma$ times the pressure volume, that is, the volume enclosed by the surface, the hydrostatic head plane and the vertical lines through the boundary, and that volume changes with the shape of the panel. Therefore the vertical components of the two panels are different, and so are the magnitude and the line of action of the resultant.
