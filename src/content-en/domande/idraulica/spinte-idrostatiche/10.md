The radial gate shown has radius $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and width $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$. Its pin $O$ is at the upstream free surface. The upstream depth is $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$, and the gate touches the bottom directly below $O$. The downstream side is dry. What is the horizontal component of the water force?

- $92.0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $171\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Use the vertical projection of the curved surface to find the horizontal force. The projection is a rectangle of height $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$, width $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$, and area $A_x = 7.50\,\mathrm{m}^2$. Its upper edge is at the free surface, so its centroid lies at depth $h_G = 1.25\,\mathrm{m}$ and
$$
S_x = \gamma\,h_G\,A_x = 9.81 \cdot 1.25 \cdot 7.50 = 92.0\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
acting downstream. The vertical component is $144\,\mathrm{kN}$, the product of $\gamma$ and the hypothetical fluid volume above the surface. The resultant has magnitude $171\,\mathrm{kN}$. Using the full water depth for the centroid would give $184\,\mathrm{kN}$.
