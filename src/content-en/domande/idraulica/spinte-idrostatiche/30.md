A fully submerged vertical gate separates two freshwater basins. Their free surfaces, both at atmospheric pressure, differ in level by $0.40\,\mathrm m$. A horizontal hinge passes through the area centroid of the wetted surface. What is the moment of the net pressure force about the hinge?

The hinge axis passes through G perpendicular to the drawing."}

- It equals the weight of fluid between the levels multiplied by the hinge depth.
- It is zero only if the two levels coincide.
- Zero for any gate shape.
- It always tends to open the lower part downstream.

## Explanation

Both pressures grow with the same depth gradient. Their difference is uniform over the gate, so its resultant passes through the area centroid and has zero moment arm.
