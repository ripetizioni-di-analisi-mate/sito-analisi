A uniform wooden cylinder of diameter $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$, height $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$, and density $500\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ floats with its axis vertical. Is this orientation stable?

- It is stable, with metacentric height $0.0563\,\mathrm{m}$.
- It is neutral because the waterplane is circular.
- It is unstable because the metacenter is $0.144\,\mathrm{m}$ below the center of gravity.
- It is stable, with metacentric height $0.256\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The draft is $d = \dfrac{500}{1000} \cdot 0.80 = 0.40\,\mathrm{m}$. The center of buoyancy lies $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ above the base, and the center of gravity lies $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ above it. The waterplane is circular with $I = \dfrac{\pi D^4}{64}$, and the displaced volume is $V_c = \dfrac{\pi D^2}{4}\,d$. The metacentric radius is therefore
$$
r = \frac{I}{V_c} = \frac{D^2}{16\,d} = \frac{0.36}{6.40} = 0.0563\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The metacenter is $0.20 + 0.056 = 0.256\,\mathrm{m}$ above the base and therefore $0.144\,\mathrm{m}$ below the center of gravity. The equilibrium is **unstable**. The value $0.0563\,\mathrm{m}$ is the metacentric radius. Compare it with the distance $\overline{CG} = 0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ to determine metacentric height.
