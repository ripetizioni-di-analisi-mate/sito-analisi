A semicircular gate of radius $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ is mounted in a vertical wall with its horizontal diameter at the free surface and its arc facing downward, as shown. What force does the water exert on it?

::schema{poligoni="-1.2,0 1.2,0 1.1276,-0.4104 0.9193,-0.7713 0.6,-1.0392 0.2084,-1.1818 -0.2084,-1.1818 -0.6,-1.0392 -0.9193,-0.7713 -1.1276,-0.4104" tratteggi="-2.6,-1.8 2.6,-1.8 2.6,0;-2.6,0 -2.6,-1.8" pelo="-2.6,2.6,0,p_g = 0" quote="0 0,1.2 0,R = 1.20 m,0.45" riferimento=0 titolo="Front view of the wall showing a semicircular gate of radius $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ with its diameter at the free surface"}

- $13.3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $26.6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $11.3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $22.6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The force is $S = \gamma\,h_G\,A$, where $h_G$ is the depth of the semicircle’s centroid. The centroid lies $\dfrac{4R}{3\pi}$ from its diameter, which is at the free surface, so
$$
h_G = \frac{4 \cdot 1.20}{3\pi} = 0.509\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The area is $A = \dfrac{\pi R^2}{2} = 2.26\,\mathrm{m}^2$, giving $S = 9.81 \cdot 0.509 \cdot 2.26 = 11.3\,\mathrm{kN}$. Putting the centroid halfway along the radius, as for a rectangle, would give $13.3\,\mathrm{kN}$. Using the area of a full circle would give $22.6\,\mathrm{kN}$.
