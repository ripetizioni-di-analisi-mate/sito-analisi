An isosceles triangular gate is mounted in a vertical wall. Its apex is at the free surface and its horizontal base is at depth $h$. What is the depth of the center of pressure?

- $\dfrac{2h}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3h}{4}$
- $\dfrac{h}{2}$
- $\dfrac{11h}{12}$

## Explanation

The triangle’s centroid lies two-thirds of its height down from the vertex, so $y_G = \dfrac{2h}{3}$. Its area is $A = \dfrac{b\,h}{2}$ and its second moment about the centroidal axis parallel to the base is $I_G = \dfrac{b\,h^3}{36}$. Since the free surface passes through the vertex, distances along the gate are measured from that point, giving
$$
\frac{I_G}{y_G A} = \frac{b\,h^3}{36} \cdot \frac{3}{2h} \cdot \frac{2}{b\,h} = \frac{h}{12} .
$$
This gives $y_C = \dfrac{2h}{3} + \dfrac{h}{12} = \dfrac{3h}{4}$. The value $\dfrac{11h}{12}$ uses the rectangular second moment of area $\dfrac{b\,h^3}{12}$, and $\dfrac{2h}{3}$ is the centroid depth.
