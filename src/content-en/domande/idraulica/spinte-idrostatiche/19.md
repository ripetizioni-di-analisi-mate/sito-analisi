Two identical rectangular gates are submerged with their centroids at the same depth. One is vertical and the other lies in a wall inclined at $60^\circ$ to the horizontal. How do the magnitudes of the hydrostatic forces compare?

- The force on the vertical gate is greater.
- The forces have equal magnitudes.
- The force on the inclined gate is greater.
- The force on the inclined gate is $87\%$ of the force on the vertical gate.

## Explanation

The force magnitude $S = \gamma h_G A$ depends only on the centroid depth and area. Both gates have the same area and centroid depth, so **the magnitudes are equal**. Tilting the gate changes the direction of the force, which remains normal to the gate. It also changes the center of pressure because its offset $\dfrac{I_G}{y_G A}$ is measured along the surface and depends on $y_G = \dfrac{h_G}{\sin \alpha}$.
