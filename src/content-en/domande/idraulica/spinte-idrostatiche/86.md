A uniform cylindrical wooden log floats with its axis horizontal. What type of equilibrium does it have with respect to rotation about its longitudinal axis?

- Neutral.
- Stable because the metacenter is above the center of gravity.
- Unstable because the center of gravity is above the center of buoyancy.
- Stable only if the wood density is less than half the water density.

## Explanation

The log has a circular cross section, so each elemental pressure force acts normal to its surface and passes through the axis. The resultant buoyancy force also passes through the axis at every angle of roll. Because the log is homogeneous, its center of gravity lies on the axis. After any rotation, weight and buoyancy still share a vertical line through the axis and produce no couple. The log is in **neutral equilibrium** at every angle. Equivalently, the metacenter $M$ coincides with $G$ on the axis and the metacentric height is zero.
