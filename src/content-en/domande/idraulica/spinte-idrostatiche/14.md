A rectangular pontoon is $10.00\,\mathrm{m}$ long, $4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ wide, and $1.50\,\mathrm{m}$ high. Its total weight is $300\,\mathrm{kN}$ and its center of gravity is $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ above the keel, as shown. What is its metacentric height for rolling about the longitudinal axis?

::schema{acqua="-4.4,0 4.4,0 4.4,2 -4.4,2" solidi="-4.8,-0.7 4.8,-0.7 4.8,0 -4.8,0" poligoni="-2,1.3 2,1.3 2,2.8 -2,2.8" punti="0,2.1,G,baricentro" pelo="4.0,4.4,2,p_g = 0" quote="-2 2.8,2 2.8,B = 4.00 m,-0.9;2 1.3,2 2.8,H = 1.50 m,1;-2 1.3,-2 2.1,0.80 m,-0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="Cross section of the pontoon of width $4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $1.50\,\mathrm{m}$, with its center of gravity $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ above the keel"}

- $1.74\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.33\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.944\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The flotation condition $\gamma V_c = P$ gives a displaced volume $V_c = \dfrac{300}{9.81} = 30.6\,\mathrm{m}^3$ and hence a draft $d = \dfrac{30.6}{10.00 \cdot 4.00} = 0.765\,\mathrm{m}$. The waterplane’s second moment of area about the longitudinal axis is $I = \dfrac{10.00 \cdot 4.00^3}{12} = 53.3\,\mathrm{m}^4$, so the metacentric radius is
$$
r = \frac{I}{V_c} = \frac{53.3}{30.6} = 1.74\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The center of buoyancy lies halfway down the submerged depth, or $0.382\,\mathrm{m}$ above the keel. The metacenter is therefore at $0.382 + 1.744 = 2.13\,\mathrm{m}$, giving a metacentric height of
$$
\overline{GM} = 2.13 - 0.80 = 1.33\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $1.74\,\mathrm{m}$ is the metacentric radius, measured upward from the center of buoyancy. Metacentric height is measured from the center of gravity.
