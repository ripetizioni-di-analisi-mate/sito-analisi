A circular gate of diameter $0.90\,\mathrm{m}$ is mounted in a wall inclined at $50^\circ$ to the horizontal. Its center is at depth $2.40\,\mathrm{m}$ as shown. What force does the water exert on the gate?

::schema{nodi="A,0.3107,0.4553;B,0.8893,1.1447" aste="A-B" acqua="-2.6,0 -0.0713,0 2.614,3.2 -2.6,3.2" solidi="-3,-0.8 4,-0.8 4,3.6 2.9496,3.6 -0.0713,0 -3,0" pelo="-2.6,2.614,3.2,p_g = 0" punti="0.6,0.8,,baricentro" quote="0.6 0.8,0.6 3.2,2.40 m,-1.7;A,B,D = 0.90 m,0.7" etichette="-0.32,0.07,α;0.3,1.06,G" riferimento=0 titolo="Circular gate of diameter $0.90\,\mathrm{m}$ in a wall inclined at $50^\circ$"}

- $15.0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $11.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $19.6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $59.9\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The gate area is $A = \dfrac{\pi \cdot 0.90^2}{4} = 0.636\,\mathrm{m}^2$ and its centroid depth is given as $h_G = 2.40\,\mathrm{m}$. The hydrostatic force formula applies at any inclination, so
$$
S = \gamma\,h_G\,A = 9.81 \cdot 2.40 \cdot 0.636 = 15.0\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
normal to the gate. The distance $y_G = \dfrac{2.40}{\sin 50^\circ} = 3.13\,\mathrm{m}$ along the gate enters the center-of-pressure calculation. Using it as the vertical centroid depth would give $19.6\,\mathrm{kN}$.
