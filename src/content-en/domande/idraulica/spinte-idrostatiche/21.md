A cable holds a fully submerged body of nonuniform density at rest in water. Through which point does the resultant force of the water act?

- Through the centroid of the body’s volume.
- Through the body’s center of mass.
- Through the centroid of the wetted surface.
- Through the centroid of the body’s horizontal projection.

## Explanation

By **Archimedes’ principle**, the buoyant force equals the weight of the displaced liquid. It depends only on the submerged volume, regardless of the material occupying it. Imagine replacing the body with the same volume of liquid. The pressure on its boundary stays the same. Since the replacement liquid is at rest under its weight and the buoyant force, the two forces share a line of action through the **centroid of the displaced volume**. Because the original body is nonuniform, its weight generally acts through a different point, its center of mass.
