The vertical rectangular gate shown is $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ wide and $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ high. It rests on the bottom and separates basins of depths $3.50\,\mathrm{m}$ upstream and $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ downstream. What is the net hydrostatic force on it?

::schema{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,2.5;D,0,1.2,-" aste="A-B" acqua="-2.6,0 0,0 0,3.5 -2.6,3.5;0,0 2.6,0 2.6,1.2 0,1.2" solidi="-3,-0.7 3,-0.7 3,0 -3,0;-0.15,2.5 0.15,2.5 0.15,3.9 -0.15,3.9" pelo="-2.6,-1.2,3.5,upstream;1.2,2.6,1.2,downstream" quote="0 0,0 2.5,2.50 m,-1.1;-2.6 0,-2.6 3.5,3.50 m,-0.7" riferimento=0 titolo="Gate of height $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ with water depths $3.50\,\mathrm{m}$ upstream and $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ downstream"}

- $110\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $124\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $106\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $96.2\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Calculate the forces on the two sides separately, using each basin’s hydrostatic head plane. The upstream water covers the full gate height $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and the centroid is at depth $3.50 - 1.25 = 2.25\,\mathrm{m}$, so
$$
S_1 = 9.81 \cdot 2.25 \cdot 5.00 = 110\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
Downstream, water covers only the lowest $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ of the gate. That part has its centroid at depth $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ and a wetted area of $2.40\,\mathrm{m}^2$, so $S_2 = 9.81 \cdot 0.60 \cdot 2.40 = 14.1\,\mathrm{kN}$. The two hydrostatic forces act in opposite directions, giving the net force as their difference,
$$
S = 110 - 14.1 = 96.2\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
acting downstream.
