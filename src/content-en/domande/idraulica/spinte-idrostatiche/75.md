A retaining wall is at impending overturning, with factor of safety $F_r = 1$. Where does the resultant of all the forces intersect the foundation plane?

- At the midpoint of the base.
- At the upstream heel.
- At the downstream toe.
- One third of the base width from the downstream toe, at the edge of the middle third.

## Explanation

At $F_r = 1$ the stabilizing and overturning moments about the downstream toe are equal. All the forces together then have zero moment about that point, so their resultant passes through the **downstream toe**. For $F_r > 1$, the point where the resultant crosses the base moves upstream. The middle-third condition requires it to lie at least $\dfrac{B}{3}$ from the downstream toe.
