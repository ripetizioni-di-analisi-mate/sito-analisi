A closed tank is full of water. A hatch covers a circular opening of diameter $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ in the horizontal lid. A piezometer connected to the tank shows water at $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ above the hatch, as illustrated. What force does the water exert on the hatch?

::schema{nodi="L1,1.4,2,-;L2,2,2,-" aste="L1-L2" acqua="0,0 4,0 4,2 3.5,2 3.5,4.5 3.3,4.5 3.3,2 0,2" solidi="-0.3,-0.3 4.3,-0.3 4.3,0 -0.3,0;-0.3,0 0,0 0,2.3 -0.3,2.3;4,0 4.3,0 4.3,2.3 4,2.3;0,2 1.4,2 1.4,2.3 0,2.3;2,2 3.3,2 3.3,2.3 2,2.3;3.5,2 4,2 4,2.3 3.5,2.3" linee="3.3,2.3 3.3,5;3.5,2.3 3.5,5" pelo="3.0,3.8,4.5," quote="2.6 2,2.6 4.5,2.50 m,0.3;1.4 2,2 2,D = 0.60 m,0.7" riferimento=0 titolo="Closed tank showing the hatch in its cover and the piezometer"}

- $6.93\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $6.93\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward
- $27.7\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $8.83\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward

## Explanation

The piezometer level marks the hydrostatic head plane. The hatch lies $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ below it and is subject to gauge pressure $p = 9.81 \cdot 2.50 = 24.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The hatch is horizontal, so that pressure is uniform and the resultant is
$$
S = p\,\frac{\pi D^2}{4} = 24.5 \cdot \frac{\pi \cdot 0.60^2}{4} = 6.93\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The water is beneath the hatch, so the force acts **upward** and tends to lift it. Using the diameter as the radius would give $27.7\,\mathrm{kN}$, while using the square area $D^2$ would give $8.83\,\mathrm{kN}$.
