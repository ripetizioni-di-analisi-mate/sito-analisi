A concrete block of volume $0.25\,\mathrm{m}^3$ and specific weight $24\,\mathrm{kN/m^3}$ is fully submerged in water and suspended from a vertical cable. What is the cable tension?

- $3.55\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6.00\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2.45\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8.45\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The block is at rest, so its vertical forces balance. Its weight $24 \cdot 0.25 = 6.00\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward, and by Archimedes’ principle the buoyant force $9.81 \cdot 0.25 = 2.45\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. The cable tension is therefore
$$
T = (24 - 9.81) \cdot 0.25 = 3.55\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
Adding the buoyancy to the weight instead of subtracting it would give $8.45\,\mathrm{kN}$.
