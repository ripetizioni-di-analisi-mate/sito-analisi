A cylindrical radial gate pivots about a pin on the cylinder axis. What moment does the hydrostatic force exert about the pin?

- It is $S\,R$, where $R$ is the gate radius.
- It increases with the square of the water level.
- It is zero only when the pin is at the free surface.
- It is zero at any water level.

## Explanation

Each elemental pressure force acts normal to the cylindrical gate surface and therefore through its axis. The resultant likewise passes through the pivot, giving **zero** moment about it at every water level. The mechanism of a radial gate needs to overcome only its weight and friction. A hinged flat gate also requires a moment that increases with water level.
