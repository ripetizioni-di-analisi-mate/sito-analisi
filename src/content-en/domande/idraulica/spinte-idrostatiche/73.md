A retaining wall of base width $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ holds water to depth $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$. Uplift varies linearly from $\gamma h$ at the upstream heel to zero at the downstream toe, as shown. What is the overturning moment per meter of wall length about the downstream toe?

::schema{nodi="T,0,0,-;P,2,0,-" solidi="0,0 2,0 2,3.6 0,3.6;-3.6,-0.9 4.4,-0.9 4.4,0 -3.6,0" acqua="-3.4,0 0,0 0,3 -3.4,3" pelo="-3.4,-0.6,3,p_g = 0" pressioni="T,P,1.0,0,U,-1" quote="T,P,B = 2.00 m,1.5;-3.4 0,-3.4 3,3.00 m,-0.7" riferimento=0 titolo="Retaining wall of base width $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ with the triangular uplift pressure diagram $U$"}

- $63.8\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$
- $44.1\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$
- $73.6\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$
- $83.4\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$

## Explanation

The horizontal force $9.81 \cdot \dfrac{3.00^2}{2} = 44.1\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ above the bottom, giving moment $44.1\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$ about the downstream toe. The uplift force $U = \dfrac{9.81 \cdot 3.00 \cdot 2.00}{2} = 29.43\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ passes through the centroid of its triangular diagram, $\dfrac{B}{3}$ from the upstream heel or $\dfrac{2B}{3} = 1.333\,\mathrm{m}$ from the downstream toe. Both tend to overturn the wall about that toe, so
$$
M_{\text{overturning}} = 44.1 + 29.43 \cdot 1.333 = 44.1 + 39.2 = 83.4\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m} .
$$
Measuring the uplift moment arm from the heel instead of the downstream toe would give $63.8\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$.
