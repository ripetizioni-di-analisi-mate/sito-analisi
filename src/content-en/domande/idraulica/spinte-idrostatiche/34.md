A flat rectangular gate of width $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and downslope length $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ is mounted on a wall inclined at $60^\circ$ to the horizontal. Its centroid is at depth $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the vertical component of the hydrostatic force?

- $85.0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $75.6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $49.1\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $98.1\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The gate area is $A = 2.00 \cdot 2.50 = 5.00\,\mathrm{m}^2$ and the resultant force $S = 9.81 \cdot 2.00 \cdot 5.00 = 98.1\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts normal to it. The normal makes the same angle $\alpha$ with the vertical as the gate makes with the horizontal. Therefore
$$
S_z = S\,\cos\alpha = 98.1 \cdot \cos 60^\circ = 49.1\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
downward. The water volume above the gate gives the same result. The gate’s horizontal projection is $A_h = 5.00 \cdot \cos 60^\circ = 2.50\,\mathrm{m}^2$, and the water volume between the gate and the head plane is $V_p = 2.50 \cdot 2.00 = 5.00\,\mathrm{m}^3$, giving $S_z = 9.81 \cdot 5.00 = 49.1\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $85.0\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the horizontal component and $98.1\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the resultant force magnitude.
