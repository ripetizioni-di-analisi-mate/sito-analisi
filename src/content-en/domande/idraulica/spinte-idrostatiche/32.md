A vertical rectangular gate of height $h$ is hinged along its upper edge at the free surface. How far from the hinge does the resultant pressure force act?

- $\dfrac{h}{2}$
- $\dfrac{h}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3h}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2h}{3}$

## Explanation

With the upper edge at the free surface, the triangular pressure diagram has its vertex at the hinge and base $\gamma h$ at the lower edge. Its force passes through the triangle’s centroid, two-thirds of the height from the vertex, at a distance from the hinge of
$$
\frac{2h}{3} .
$$
The center-of-pressure formula gives the same result. Using $y_G = \dfrac{h}{2}$, $A = b h$, and $I_G = \dfrac{b h^3}{12}$ gives $y_C = \dfrac{h}{2} + \dfrac{h}{6} = \dfrac{2h}{3}$. The distance $\dfrac{h}{3}$ is measured from the lower edge to the center of pressure.
