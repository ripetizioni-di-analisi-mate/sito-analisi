A vertical cylindrical tank of diameter $6.00\,\mathrm{m}$ is full of water. Consider a horizontal ring of shell of height $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$, centered at depth $4.00\,\mathrm{m}$, over which pressure may be treated as uniform. What tensile force acts across each of the two vertical sections cut by a diametral plane?

- $235\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $58.9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $118\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $370\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

A diametral plane cuts the ring into two semicylinders. The normal force on one half equals the pressure force on its projection onto that plane. The projection is a rectangle of width $D = 6.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$, giving
$$
S = \gamma\,h\,D \cdot 1.00 = 9.81 \cdot 4.00 \cdot 6.00 \cdot 1.00 = 235\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The tensile forces at the two cuts balance the pressure force. Each carries $T = \dfrac{S}{2} = 118\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $T = \dfrac{p\,D}{2}$ per meter of height. Using the curved semicylinder surface, of arc length $\dfrac{\pi D}{2}$, instead of its projection would give $370\,\mathrm{kN}$.
