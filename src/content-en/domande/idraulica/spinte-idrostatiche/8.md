## Explanation

The gate has height $2.80 - 0.80 = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$, centroid depth $y_G = 1.80\,\mathrm{m}$, and area $A = 3.20\,\mathrm{m}^2$, so $S = 9.81 \cdot 1.80 \cdot 3.20 = 56.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. Its centroidal second moment of area is $I_G = \dfrac{1.60 \cdot 2.00^3}{12} = 1.067\,\mathrm{m}^4$, placing the center of pressure at
$$
y_C = 1.80 + \frac{1.067}{1.80 \cdot 3.20} = 1.985\,\mathrm{m}
$$
below the surface. The hinge lies at depth $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$, giving a moment arm $1.985 - 0.800 = 1.19\,\mathrm{m}$. Moment equilibrium gives $M = 56.5 \cdot 1.19 = 67.0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Using the distance from the hinge to the centroid would give $56.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
