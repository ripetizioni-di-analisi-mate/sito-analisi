A flat vertical gate closes an opening extending from depth $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ to depth $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the shape of the pressure diagram over the gate?

- A triangle with its vertex at the top edge.
- A trapezoid.
- A rectangle.
- A downward-opening parabola.

## Explanation

The hydrostatic pressure law gives gauge pressure $p = \gamma h$, a linear function of depth. The pressure diagram therefore has a straight sloping edge. The top and bottom pressures $9.81\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and $29.4\,\mathrm{kPa}$ are both nonzero, giving a **trapezoid**. A triangular diagram occurs when the gate extends up to the free surface, where the gauge pressure is zero.
