A rectangular pontoon of length $12.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and width $5.00\,\mathrm{m}$ has draft $0.90\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its metacentric radius about the longitudinal axis?

- $13.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9.26\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.08\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.31\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The waterplane is a rectangle $12.00 \times 5.00\,\mathrm{m}$. The longitudinal axis is parallel to its long sides, giving a second moment of area about this axis of
$$
I = \frac{12.00 \cdot 5.00^3}{12} = 125\,\mathrm{m}^4 .
$$
The displaced volume is $V_c = 12.00 \cdot 5.00 \cdot 0.90 = 54.0\,\mathrm{m}^3$, so the metacentric radius is $r = \dfrac{125}{54.0} = 2.31\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $13.3\,\mathrm{m}$ uses the second moment about the transverse axis. That is the larger second moment. Stability is governed by rotation about the axis for which $I$ is smallest.
