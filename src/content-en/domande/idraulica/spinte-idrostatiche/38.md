A concrete retaining wall of specific weight $24\,\mathrm{kN/m^3}$ has a vertical upstream face, height $4.00\,\mathrm{m}$, and base width $2.40\,\mathrm{m}$. The water depth is $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and there is no uplift, as shown. How far from the upstream face does the resultant intersect the foundation plane?

::schema{solidi="-3.6,-0.9 5.4,-0.9 5.4,0 -3.6,0;0,0 2.4,0 2.4,4 0,4" acqua="-3.4,0 0,0 0,3 -3.4,3" pelo="-3.4,-0.6,3,p_g = 0" quote="0 0,2.4 0,B = 2.40 m,1.5;2.4 0,2.4 4,4.00 m,1;-3.4 0,-3.4 3,3.00 m,-0.7" riferimento=0 titolo="Retaining wall of height $4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ with base width $2.40\,\mathrm{m}$ and water depth $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$"}

- $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.49\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.39\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.01\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Consider a unit length of wall. Its weight $W = 24 \cdot 4.00 \cdot 2.40 = 230\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts at midwidth, a distance $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ from the upstream heel. The hydrostatic force is
$$
S_x = \gamma\,\frac{h^2}{2} = 9.81 \cdot \frac{3.00^2}{2} = 44.1\,\mathrm{kN/m}
$$
and acts at a height $\dfrac{h}{3} = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ above the base. The upstream face is vertical, so the water force has no vertical component. The resultant vertical load on the foundation is therefore $N = 230\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Taking moments about the upstream heel gives
$$
x_R = \frac{230 \cdot 1.20 + 44.1 \cdot 1.00}{230} = 1.39\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
giving an eccentricity $0.19\,\mathrm{m}$ from the centerline. This is less than $\dfrac{B}{6} = 0.400\,\mathrm{m}$, so the resultant lies within the middle third of the base.
