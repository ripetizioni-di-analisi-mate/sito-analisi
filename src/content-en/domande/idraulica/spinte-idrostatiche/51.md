A vertical rectangular gate of height $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ closes an opening extending upward from the bed of a channel. It pivots about a horizontal axis $O$ at height $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ above the bed. The downstream channel is dry, as shown. The gate stays shut while the resultant water force acts below the pivot and opens when it acts above. At what upstream depth $h$ does it begin to open?

::schema{nodi="A,0,0,-;B,0,1.2,-;O,0,0.5" aste="A-B" cerniere="O" acqua="-3.5,0 0,0 0,2 -3.5,2" solidi="-3.9,-0.6 3,-0.6 3,0 -3.9,0;-0.15,1.2 0.15,1.2 0.15,2.6 -0.15,2.6" pelo="-3.5,-1.5,2,p_g = 0" quote="0 0,0 1.2,1.20 m,1.5;0 0,0 0.5,0.50 m,0.65;-3.5 0,-3.5 2,h,-0.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Gate of height $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ with its axis of rotation $O$ at a distance $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ above the bottom"}

- $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.600\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.40\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Until water reaches the top edge, the pressure diagram is triangular and the resultant acts one-third of the wetted height above the bottom. It therefore stays at or below $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ above the bottom, below the axis. When $h > 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$, the gate centroid lies at depth $y_G = h - 0.60\,\mathrm{m}$. Using $\ell = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ gives the distance from the centroid down to the center of pressure as
$$
y_C - y_G = \frac{I_G}{y_G A} = \frac{\ell^2}{12\,y_G} = \frac{0.120}{h - 0.60} .
$$
The center of pressure reaches the axis when the offset is $0.60 - 0.50 = 0.10\,\mathrm{m}$, giving $h - 0.60 = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ and $h = 1.80\,\mathrm{m}$. As the water rises, the offset decreases and the center of pressure approaches the centroid. The gate therefore opens for every $h > 1.80\,\mathrm{m}$. At the opening condition, the upper edge is at depth $0.600\,\mathrm{m}$.
