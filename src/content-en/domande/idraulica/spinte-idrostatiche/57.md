A flat vertical gate separates two basins. Both water surfaces are above the top of the gate, and the upstream surface is $\Delta h$ higher than the downstream one. What is the shape of the net pressure diagram?

- A rectangle of constant pressure $\gamma\,\Delta h$.
- A trapezoid with pressure increasing downward.
- A triangle with its vertex at the top edge.
- A rectangle of constant pressure $\gamma\,\Delta h$ at the top and a trapezoid below.

## Explanation

A point at depth $y$ below the upstream surface experiences pressure $\gamma\,y$ from upstream. It is at depth $y - \Delta h$ below the downstream surface and receives pressure $\gamma\,(y - \Delta h)$ from that side. The net pressure is the difference,
$$
\gamma\,y - \gamma\,(y - \Delta h) = \gamma\,\Delta h ,
$$
which is independent of depth. The net pressure diagram is therefore rectangular, and the force $\gamma\,\Delta h\,A$ acts through the gate centroid.
