## Explanation

With the top edge at the free surface, the pressure diagram is triangular and its resultant is
$$
S = \gamma\,\frac{b\,h^2}{2} = 9.81 \cdot \frac{2.50 \cdot 3.00^2}{2} = 110\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The force passes through the triangle’s centroid, $\dfrac{h}{3} = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ above the bottom. Its moment arm about the lower hinge is therefore $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$. Taking moments about the hinge gives
$$
F \cdot 3.00 = 110 \cdot 1.00 ,
$$
and hence $F = 36.8\,\mathrm{kN}$. Placing the force at midheight would instead give $55.2\,\mathrm{kN}$.
