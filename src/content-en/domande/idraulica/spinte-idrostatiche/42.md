A prismatic wooden block of density $700\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and height $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ floats in water with its base horizontal. What is its draft?

- $0.120\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.571\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.280\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.200\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Let $A_f$ be the base area. The flotation condition $\gamma V_c = P$ becomes $\rho\,g\,A_f\,d = \rho_c\,g\,A_f\,a$, where $a$ is the block height and $d$ is its draft. The base area cancels, giving
$$
d = \frac{\rho_c}{\rho}\,a = \frac{700}{1000} \cdot 0.40 = 0.280\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The submerged fraction equals the density ratio, leaving $0.120\,\mathrm{m}$ above the surface. Inverting the ratio gives $0.571\,\mathrm{m}$, greater than the height of the block.
