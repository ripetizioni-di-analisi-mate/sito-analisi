A vertical rectangular gate is fully submerged with water on one side only. It pivots about a horizontal axis through its centroid. In what direction does it tend to rotate?

- It has no tendency to rotate because the force acts through the centroid.
- The bottom edge tends to move toward the dry side and the top edge toward the water.
- The top edge tends to move toward the dry side and the bottom edge toward the water.
- It tends to rotate only if the top edge is at the free surface.

## Explanation

The force points from the water toward the dry side and acts $\dfrac{I_G}{y_G A} > 0$ below the centroid. The pivot passes through the centroid, so the force produces a moment that pushes the lower part of the gate toward the dry side. The bottom edge therefore tends to open toward the dry side and the top edge rotates toward the water. This occurs at every depth because the pressure moment $\gamma\,I_G$ about the centroid is independent of depth.
