A vertical rectangular gate is $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$ wide and $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ high. Its top edge is at the free surface and its bottom edge is at the bed. What is the resultant hydrostatic force exerted by the water on the gate?

- $110\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $30.7\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $55.2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $27.6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Since the top edge is at the free surface, the gate centroid lies at depth $h_G = \dfrac{2.50}{2} = 1.25\,\mathrm{m}$ and its area is $A = 1.80 \cdot 2.50 = 4.50\,\mathrm{m}^2$. Applying the **hydrostatic force theorem for a plane surface** gives
$$
S = \gamma\,h_G\,A = 9.81 \cdot 1.25 \cdot 4.50 = 55.2\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The triangular pressure diagram gives the same result. Its area is $\gamma\,\dfrac{h^2}{2} = 30.7\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, and multiplying by the gate width $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$ again gives $55.2\,\mathrm{kN}$. Using the depth of the bottom edge would give $110\,\mathrm{kN}$, twice the correct force, since the pressure at the bottom is twice that at the centroid.
