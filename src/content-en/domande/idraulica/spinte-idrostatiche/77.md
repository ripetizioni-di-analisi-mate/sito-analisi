A long prismatic float has an isosceles triangular cross section with its apex down and its horizontal base at the top. The triangle has height $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$, and the material has density $640\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What is the draft measured from the apex?

- $0.384\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.480\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.246\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.120\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The float has a uniform cross section along its length, so the flotation condition $\gamma\,V_c = P$ can be expressed using cross-sectional areas. The submerged area is $\dfrac{640}{1000} = 0.64$ times the area of the full triangle. The submerged triangle is similar to the full triangle with length ratio $\dfrac{d}{a}$, giving area ratio $\left(\dfrac{d}{a}\right)^2$. Hence
$$
\left(\frac{d}{a}\right)^2 = 0.64 ,
$$
giving $d = 0.80 \cdot 0.60 = 0.480\,\mathrm{m}$. The relation $d = \dfrac{\rho_c}{\rho}\,a$, which would give $0.384\,\mathrm{m}$, applies to a body with constant horizontal cross-sectional area over its height. The height above the surface is $0.120\,\mathrm{m}$.
