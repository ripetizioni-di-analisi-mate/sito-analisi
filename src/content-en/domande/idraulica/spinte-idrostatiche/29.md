A vertical rectangular gate of width $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ is hinged at its bottom edge. A horizontal force at its top edge, at the free surface, holds it closed. What is the horizontal hinge reaction?

- $58.9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $29.4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $88.3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $118\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The triangular pressure distribution gives the force
$$
S = \gamma\,\frac{b\,h^2}{2} = 9.81 \cdot \frac{2.00 \cdot 3.00^2}{2} = 88.3\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
and acts at distance $\dfrac{h}{3} = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ from the hinge. The moment balance about the hinge gives $F \cdot 3.00 = 88.3 \cdot 1.00$ and hence the holding force $F = 29.4\,\mathrm{kN}$. The gate is at rest, so the holding force and hinge reaction must together balance the horizontal water force. Thus
$$
R = S - F = 88.3 - 29.4 = 58.9\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
