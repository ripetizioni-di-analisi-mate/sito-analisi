A tank with a horizontal rectangular base of sides $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ contains water to depth $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$. What force does the water exert on the base?

- $73.6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $92.0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $61.3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $147\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Every point on the horizontal bottom is at the same depth and therefore at the same pressure $p = \gamma h = 9.81 \cdot 2.50 = 24.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Its area is $A = 6.00\,\mathrm{m}^2$, giving
$$
S = \gamma\,h\,A = 9.81 \cdot 2.50 \cdot 6.00 = 147\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
downward. Using $\dfrac{h}{2}$ as the centroid depth would give $73.6\,\mathrm{kN}$. That centroid depth applies to a vertical wall. On the horizontal bottom, pressure is uniform and its diagram is rectangular.
