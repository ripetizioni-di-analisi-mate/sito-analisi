An S2 profile forms on a steep slope. How does the water depth change downstream?

- It increases from normal depth toward critical depth
- It decreases from critical depth toward normal depth
- It increases from critical depth toward progressively greater depths
- It remains equal to critical depth

## Explanation

On a steep bed normal depth is below critical depth. An S2 profile occupies zone 2, between $h_0$ and $h_c$. There $h > h_0$, so the friction slope is less than the bed slope and the numerator $i - J$ is positive. In addition $h < h_c$, making the flow supercritical and the denominator $1 - Fr^2$ negative. Their ratio is negative, giving $\dfrac{dh}{dx} < 0$, so depth **decreases** downstream from $h_c$ toward $h_0$. This profile occurs immediately downstream of a change from a mild to a steep slope.
