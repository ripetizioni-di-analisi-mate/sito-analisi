A horizontal rectangular channel of width $b = 5.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 8.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ at a supercritical depth of $h_1 = 0.30\,\mathrm{m}$. Which type of hydraulic jump forms?

- An undular jump
- A weak jump
- A steady jump
- An oscillating jump

## Explanation

The upstream velocity is $v_1 = \dfrac{8.00}{5.00 \cdot 0.30} = 5.33\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so
$$
Fr_1 = \frac{5.33}{\sqrt{9.81 \cdot 0.30}} = 3.11 .
$$
This lies in the interval $2.5 < Fr_1 < 4.5$ for an **oscillating jump**. The jet moves between the bed and the surface, generating waves that travel downstream. An undular jump requires $Fr_1 < 1.7$ and a weak jump $1.7 < Fr_1 < 2.5$. Neither condition holds here, and steady jumps start at $Fr_1 = 4.5$. The conjugate depth is $1.18\,\mathrm{m}$ and Smetana's formula gives a jump length of $5.26\,\mathrm{m}$.
