- $0.583$
- $0.658$
- $0.825$
- $0.412$

## Explanation

When the conduit flows half full, the flow area is a semicircle, giving $A = \dfrac{\pi D^2}{8} = 0.565\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The free surface passes through the center, so its width is $B = D = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$. The hydraulic depth is therefore $h_m = \dfrac{A}{B} = 0.471\,\mathrm{m}$ and the mean velocity is $v = \dfrac{Q}{A} = 1.41\,\mathrm{m/s}$, giving
$$
Fr = \frac{v}{\sqrt{g\,h_m}} = \frac{1.41}{\sqrt{9.81 \cdot 0.471}} = 0.658 .
$$
The flow is subcritical. The value $0.583$ uses the water depth $\dfrac{D}{2}$ in place of the hydraulic depth. The value $0.825$ uses the hydraulic radius $\dfrac{D}{4}$, and $0.412$ uses the diameter.
