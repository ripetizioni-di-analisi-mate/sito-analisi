An M1 profile on a mild slope is traced from downstream to upstream. What happens to the water depth as it approaches normal depth?

- It reaches $h_0$ at a finite distance and then crosses into zone 2
- It approaches $h_0$ asymptotically and the free surface becomes parallel to the bed
- It approaches critical depth with a vertical tangent
- It stays at the value imposed at the control section

## Explanation

The numerator $i - J$ in the profile equation vanishes at depth $h_0$ because the friction slope then equals the bed slope. As $h \to h_0$, the denominator $1 - Fr^2$ tends to a nonzero value. Hence $\dfrac{dh}{dx} \to 0$, and the water surface becomes parallel to the bed. Normal depth is therefore an asymptote reached only at infinite distance. The profile stays in its original zone. To cross into another zone it would have to pass through $h_0$, where the derivative vanishes, or through $h_c$, where the gradually varied flow assumptions fail.
