A horizontal rectangular channel of width $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 10.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. What is the momentum function in force units at the section where the depth is $h = 0.50\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $54.9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $59.8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $50.0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4.91\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The momentum function in force units is the hydrostatic force on the section plus the momentum flux through it. For a rectangular channel,
$$
S(h) = \frac{\gamma\,b\,h^2}{2} + \frac{\rho\,Q^2}{b\,h} .
$$
The first term is $\dfrac{9.81 \cdot 4.00 \cdot 0.50^2}{2} = 4.91\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the second is $\dfrac{1000 \cdot 10.0^2}{4.00 \cdot 0.50} = 50\,000\,\mathrm{N}$, or $50.0\,\mathrm{kN}$. Their sum is therefore $S = 54.9\,\mathrm{kN}$. Since $h = 0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ is below the critical depth of $0.860\,\mathrm{m}$, the momentum flux is the larger contribution. The values $4.91\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $50.0\,\mathrm{kN}$ each include only one term. The value $59.8\,\mathrm{kN}$ omits the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ in the hydrostatic force.
