A trapezoidal channel of bottom width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and side slopes $m = 2$ carries $Q = 13.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ at a depth of $h = 0.90\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the Froude number?

- $0.801$
- $1.19$
- $1.01$
- $1.90$

## Explanation

For the trapezoidal channel, the flow area is $A = (b + m\,h)\,h = (3.00 + 2 \cdot 0.90) \cdot 0.90 = 4.32\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the free-surface width is $B = b + 2\,m\,h = 6.60\,\mathrm{m}$. The hydraulic depth is therefore $h_m = \dfrac{A}{B} = 0.655\,\mathrm{m}$. The mean velocity is $v = \dfrac{Q}{A} = 3.01\,\mathrm{m/s}$, giving
$$
Fr = \frac{v}{\sqrt{g\,h_m}} = \frac{3.01}{\sqrt{9.81 \cdot 0.655}} = 1.19 .
$$
This exceeds $1$, so the flow is supercritical. The value $1.01$ uses the actual flow depth in place of the hydraulic depth. The value $0.801$ uses the bottom width to calculate hydraulic depth, and $1.90$ uses a flow area of $b\,h$, excluding the areas along the sloping banks.
