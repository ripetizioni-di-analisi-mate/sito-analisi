A symmetric triangular channel has side slopes $m = 1.5$, so each bank extends $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ horizontally per meter of vertical rise. It carries $Q = 2.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, as shown. What is the momentum function in force units at a section where the depth is $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$?

::schema{solidi="0,0 -2.4,1.6 -3.4,1.6 -3.4,-0.6 0,-0.6;0,0 2.4,1.6 3.4,1.6 3.4,-0.6 0,-0.6" acqua="0,0 1.5,1.0 -1.5,1.0" pelo="-1.5,1.5,1.0," quote="2.9 0,2.9 1.0,1.00 m,-0.6" etichette="-2.9,1.95,m = 1.5;2.9,1.95,m = 1.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Triangular section with side slope $m = 1.5$"}

- $10.0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4.91\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $16.0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $7.57\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The triangular flow area is $A = m\,h^2 = 1.50\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Its base lies at the free surface and its apex at the bed, so the centroid is one third of the depth below the surface. Thus $h_G = \dfrac{h}{3}$ and the hydrostatic force is
$$
\gamma\,h_G\,A = 9.81 \cdot \frac{1.00}{3} \cdot 1.50 = 4.91\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The momentum flux is $\dfrac{\rho\,Q^2}{A} = \dfrac{1000 \cdot 4.00}{1.50} = 2.67\,\mathrm{kN}$, giving a momentum function of $S = 4.91 + 2.67 = 7.57\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $10.0\,\mathrm{kN}$ places the centroid at middepth, as for a rectangle. The value $4.91\,\mathrm{kN}$ omits momentum flux, and $16.0\,\mathrm{kN}$ uses the rectangular-channel formula with a width equal to the free-surface width $B = 2\,m\,h = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$.
