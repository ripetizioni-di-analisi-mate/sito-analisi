A horizontal stilling basin of width $b = 6.00\,\mathrm{m}$ receives $Q = 24.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ at a depth of $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$. A row of baffle blocks on the floor sustains the jump at a tailwater depth of $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$, less than the conjugate depth of the incoming flow, as shown. Neglecting bed resistance, what horizontal force does the water exert on the blocks?

::schema{solidi="0,0 15,0 15,-0.5 0,-0.5;6.0,0 6.5,0 6.5,0.40 6.0,0.40;7.2,0 7.7,0 7.7,0.40 7.2,0.40" acqua="0,0 15,0 15,2.0 10.5,2.0 9.2,1.85 8.0,1.40 6.8,0.90 5.0,0.55 0,0.50" quote="13.4 0,13.4 2.0,2.00 m,0.6" frecce="1.6,0.25,3.0,0.25,Q" etichette="6.9,-0.85,baffle blocks;2.6,1.00,h_1 = 0.50 m;11.0,2.40,2" riferimento=0 titolo="Stilling basin with a row of baffle blocks on its floor"}

- $110\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $33.6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $144\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $76.7\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Use the water between upstream section 1 and downstream section 2 as the control volume, and take downstream as positive in the horizontal momentum balance. The weight acts vertically and bed resistance is neglected. In addition to the pressure forces at the end sections, the only external horizontal force is $R_x$, exerted by the blocks on the water. The momentum balance gives $R_x = S(h_2) - S(h_1)$. The velocities are $v_1 = 8.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2 = 2.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so
$$
S(0.50) = \frac{9.81 \cdot 6.00 \cdot 0.50^2}{2} + \frac{1000 \cdot 24.0 \cdot 8.00}{1000} = 7.36 + 192 = 199.4\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
$$
S(2.00) = 117.7 + 48.0 = 165.7\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
It follows that $R_x = -33.6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The blocks push the water upstream, so the water exerts a force of **$33.6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downstream** on the blocks. The values $110\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $144\,\mathrm{kN}$ account only for the hydrostatic forces and the momentum fluxes, respectively. The value $76.7\,\mathrm{kN}$ omits the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ in the hydrostatic forces.
