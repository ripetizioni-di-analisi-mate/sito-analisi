A rectangular channel of width $b = 5.00\,\mathrm{m}$, roughness coefficient $k_s = 55\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ and bed slope $i = 0.0006$ carries $Q = 9.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Using the direct step method with two depth increments of $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$, find the distance between the section of depth $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and the section of depth $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$.

- $57.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $37.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $65.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $118\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The direct-step method gives the distance between adjacent sections as
$$
\Delta x = \frac{E_{j+1} - E_j}{i - \bar{J}} ,
\qquad
\bar{J} = \frac{J_j + J_{j+1}}{2} .
$$
The specific energies are $E(1.00) = 1.1651\,\mathrm{m}$, $E(0.90) = 1.1039\,\mathrm{m}$ and $E(0.80) = 1.0580\,\mathrm{m}$ and the friction slopes are $0.001677$, $0.002293$ and $0.003263$. The first step gives $\Delta x_1 = 44.2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the second $\Delta x_2 = 21.1\,\mathrm{m}$, for a total distance of $65.3\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $57.3\,\mathrm{m}$ uses a single step from $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ to $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $118\,\mathrm{m}$ uses depth differences in place of specific-energy differences. The value $37.3\,\mathrm{m}$ adds the mean friction slope to the bed slope instead of subtracting it.
