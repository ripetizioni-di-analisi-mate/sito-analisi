The bed of a rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ rises in the flow direction at slope $i = -0.0015$, giving an adverse slope. The discharge is $Q = 4.50\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ and water emerges from the sluice gate shown at a depth of $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$. What water-surface profile forms downstream of the gate?

::schema{solidi="0,0.30 12,0.80 12,0.30 0,-0.20;3.85,0.76 4.15,0.76 4.15,2.90 3.85,2.90" acqua="0,0.30 3.85,0.46 3.85,2.45 0,2.45;4.15,0.47 7.0,0.59 7.0,0.89 4.15,0.77" frecce="0.8,1.70,2.4,1.70,Q" etichette="4.0,3.15,gate;6.2,1.30,h = 0.30 m;9.5,0.25,i = −0.0015" riferimento=0 titolo="Sluice gate in a rectangular channel of width $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ on an adverse slope"}

- An A2 profile with depth decreasing downstream
- An H3 profile with depth increasing downstream
- An A3 profile with depth increasing downstream
- An M3 profile with depth increasing downstream

## Explanation

The critical depth is
$$
h_c = \sqrt[3]{\frac{Q^2}{g\,b^2}} = \sqrt[3]{\frac{4.50^2}{9.81 \cdot 9}} = 0.612\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
On an adverse slope, the numerator $i - J$ of the profile equation is negative at every depth and there is no normal depth. The two remaining zones are separated by $h_c$, and the profiles are designated $A$. The depth downstream of the gate, $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$, is below $h_c$, so the flow is supercritical. The denominator $1 - Fr^2$ is negative, giving $\dfrac{dh}{dx} > 0$. This is an **A3** profile, increasing in depth downstream. An H3 profile requires a horizontal bed, and an M3 profile requires a positive bed slope below critical.
