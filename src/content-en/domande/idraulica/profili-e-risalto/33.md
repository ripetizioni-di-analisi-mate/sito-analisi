A mildly sloping channel meets a steep reach, as shown. Where is the control section, and what depth is established there?

::schema{solidi="0,1.30 6,1.15 12,0.25 12,-0.15 6,0.75 0,0.90" frecce="1.0,1.95,2.8,1.92,Q" tratteggi="6,1.15 6,2.40" etichette="2.8,0.30,i_1 < i_c;9.4,-0.20,i_2 > i_c;6.0,2.65,slope change" riferimento=0 titolo="Mildly sloping channel entering a steep reach"}

- At the slope change, where critical depth is established
- At the downstream end of the lower reach, where normal depth is established
- At the slope change, where the normal depth of the upstream reach is established
- There is no control section because the flow is uniform in both reaches

## Explanation

The upstream bed slope is less than critical, so normal depth exceeds critical depth and the approaching flow is subcritical. In the downstream reach the inequality reverses and the flow is supercritical. The transition passes through critical depth at the slope change. This section controls both profiles. Trace the upstream M2 profile upstream from $h_c$, and trace the downstream S2 profile downstream from the same depth. Uniform flow is approached only at a distance from the change of slope.
