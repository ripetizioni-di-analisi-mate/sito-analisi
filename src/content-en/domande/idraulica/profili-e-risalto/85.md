At a rectangular channel section, the water depth is $1.5$ times the critical depth. What is the Froude number?

- $0.667$
- $0.544$
- $0.444$
- $0.816$

## Explanation

In a rectangular channel the critical depth satisfies $h_c^3 = \dfrac{q^2}{g}$, where $q = \dfrac{Q}{b}$ is the discharge per unit width. The mean velocity is $v = \dfrac{q}{h}$, giving
$$
Fr^2 = \frac{v^2}{g\,h} = \frac{q^2}{g\,h^3} = \left(\frac{h_c}{h}\right)^3 .
$$
Substituting $\dfrac{h}{h_c} = 1.5$ gives
$$
Fr = \left(\frac{1}{1.5}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}} = 0.544 ,
$$
so the flow is subcritical, as expected for a depth greater than critical. The values $0.667$, $0.444$, and $0.816$ use the exponents $1$, $2$, and $\dfrac{1}{2}$, respectively, in place of $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
