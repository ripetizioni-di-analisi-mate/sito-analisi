A rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 7.50\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ and changes from a mild slope to a steep slope. What is the velocity head at the change of slope?

- $0.860\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.29\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.287\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.430\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At the change in bed slope the flow passes from subcritical to supercritical, so it passes through the critical depth
$$
h_c = \sqrt[3]{\frac{7.50^2}{9.81 \cdot 3.00^2}} = 0.860\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
In a rectangular section at critical depth, $Fr = 1$, giving $v^2 = g\,h_c$. The velocity head is therefore
$$
\frac{v^2}{2g} = \frac{h_c}{2} = 0.430\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
as confirmed by $v = \dfrac{7.50}{3.00 \cdot 0.860} = 2.91\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $0.860\,\mathrm{m}$ uses $\dfrac{v^2}{g}$. The value $1.29\,\mathrm{m}$ is the critical specific energy $\dfrac{3}{2}\,h_c$, and $0.287\,\mathrm{m}$ is one third of $h_c$.
