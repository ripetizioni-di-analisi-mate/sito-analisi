A rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$, roughness coefficient $k_s = 45\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ and bed slope $i = 0.0010$ carries $Q = 5.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. A sill at the downstream end imposes a depth of $h = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ at the last section. Which profile forms upstream of the sill?

- An M2 profile
- An S1 profile
- An M3 profile
- An M1 profile

## Explanation

The critical depth is $h_c = \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{25}{9.81 \cdot 9}} = 0.657\,\mathrm{m}$ and the critical slope is $i_c = 0.00904$. Since $i < i_c$, the bed slope is mild. The Gauckler–Strickler equation gives $h_0 = 1.44\,\mathrm{m}$, greater than $h_c$ as expected on a mild slope. The depth imposed by the weir, $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$, exceeds both $h_0$ and $h_c$, so the flow occupies zone 1 and the profile is **M1**. Both the numerator and denominator of the profile equation are positive, so depth increases downstream and approaches normal depth upstream.
