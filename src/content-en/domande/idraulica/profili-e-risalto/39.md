A steep rectangular channel with normal depth $h_0 = 0.55\,\mathrm{m}$ and critical depth $h_c = 0.86\,\mathrm{m}$ ends at a barrier that imposes a depth of $1.60\,\mathrm{m}$ at the last section. Which profile forms upstream of the barrier, and in which direction is it traced?

- An S2 profile traced downstream
- An S1 profile traced upstream
- An S3 profile traced downstream
- An M1 profile traced upstream

## Explanation

The barrier depth $1.60\,\mathrm{m}$ exceeds both normal and critical depth. The water surface is therefore in zone 1, on an **S1** profile. The friction slope there is less than the bed slope, so the numerator $i - J$ is positive. Flow above critical depth is subcritical, making the denominator $1 - Fr^2$ positive as well. It follows that $\dfrac{dh}{dx} > 0$ and the flow deepens downstream. Because disturbances in subcritical flow propagate upstream, trace the profile **upstream** from the barrier. Its upstream end meets a jump because normal flow on the steep bed is supercritical. An M1 profile would require a mild slope.
