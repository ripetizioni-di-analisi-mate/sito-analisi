A rectangular channel of width $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and roughness coefficient $k_s = 50\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ carries $Q = 10.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The upstream bed slope is $i_1 = 0.030$ and the downstream slope is $i_2 = 0.0015$, as shown. Where does the jump occur, and which profile follows it?

::schema{solidi="0,2.00 5,1.40 12,1.20 12,0.80 5,1.00 0,1.60" frecce="0.6,2.25,1.7,2.22,Q" tratteggi="5,1.40 5,2.70" etichette="2.2,1.00,i_1 = 0.030;9.0,0.45,i_2 = 0.0015;5.4,2.95,slope change" riferimento=0 titolo="Transition from a steep reach to a mildly sloping reach"}

- Downstream of the slope change, followed by an M1 profile
- At the slope change, with no profile following it
- Downstream of the slope change, followed by an M3 profile
- Upstream of the slope change, followed by an S1 profile

## Explanation

The critical depth is $h_c = 0.860\,\mathrm{m}$ and the critical slope $i_c = 0.00665$. The upstream reach is steep with $h_{01} = 0.521\,\mathrm{m}$ and the downstream reach is mild with $h_{02} = 1.450\,\mathrm{m}$. The upstream Froude number is $Fr_1 = 2.13$ and the depth conjugate to $h_{01}$ is $1.33\,\mathrm{m}$. Since $1.33\,\mathrm{m}$ is less than $h_{02} = 1.450\,\mathrm{m}$, the tailwater is deeper than needed for a jump at the slope change, forcing the jump upstream into the steep reach. The flow after the jump is subcritical and lies in zone 1 on a steep bed. It follows an **S1** profile increasing in depth downstream to $h_{02}$ at the slope change.
