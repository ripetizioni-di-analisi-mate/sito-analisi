Which gradually varied flow profiles can form in a rectangular channel with a horizontal bed?

- M1, M2 and M3
- C1 and C3
- H2 and H3
- S1, S2 and S3

## Explanation

On a horizontal bed the numerator of the profile equation is $-J$, which is negative at all depths. There is no finite normal depth because the Gauckler–Strickler equation with $i = 0$ would give zero discharge for every finite depth. Treat $h_0$ as infinite. Zone 1 disappears, leaving two zones separated by critical depth. The **H2** profile between $h_c$ and infinity decreases in depth downstream, and the **H3** profile below $h_c$ increases in depth downstream. M and S profiles require a positive bed slope respectively below and above critical, while C profiles require a bed slope exactly equal to critical.
