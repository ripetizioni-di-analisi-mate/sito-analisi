A horizontal rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 6.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The depth downstream of the sluice gate is $h_1 = 0.35\,\mathrm{m}$, as shown. What depth is conjugate to $h_1$?

::schema{solidi="0,0 14,0 14,-0.60 0,-0.60;4.85,0.55 5.15,0.55 5.15,2.70 4.85,2.70" acqua="0,0 4.85,0 4.85,2.30 0,2.30;5.15,0 14,0 14,1.10 5.15,0.35" frecce="1.0,1.05,2.6,1.05,Q" etichette="5.0,3.00,gate;7.4,0.85,h_1 = 0.35 m;2.0,-0.95,i = 0" riferimento=0 titolo="Sluice gate in a horizontal rectangular channel of width $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$"}

- $1.54\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.36\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.71\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.712\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The upstream velocity is $v_1 = \dfrac{6.00}{3.00 \cdot 0.35} = 5.71\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so
$$
Fr_1 = \frac{v_1}{\sqrt{g\,h_1}} = \frac{5.71}{\sqrt{9.81 \cdot 0.35}} = 3.08
$$
The flow is therefore supercritical. The conjugate-depth formula gives
$$
h_2 = \frac{h_1}{2}\left(\sqrt{1 + 8\,Fr_1^2} - 1\right) = \frac{0.35}{2}\left(\sqrt{1 + 8 \cdot 3.08^2} - 1\right) = 1.36\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The values $1.54\,\mathrm{m}$ and $1.71\,\mathrm{m}$ result from omitting the term $-1$ or using a plus sign for it. The value $0.712\,\mathrm{m}$ uses $Fr_1$ in place of $Fr_1^2$ under the square root.
