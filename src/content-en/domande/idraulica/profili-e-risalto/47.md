A rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 6.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ in steady, gradually varied flow. At a section where the water depth is $h = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the gauge pressure at the bed?

- $11.8\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $13.2\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $5.89\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $113\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

In gradually varied flow, the streamlines are nearly parallel to the bed, so pressure has a hydrostatic distribution across the section. Gauge pressure is zero at the free surface. At the bed, a depth $h$ below the surface, the gauge pressure is therefore
$$
p = \gamma\,h = 9.81 \cdot 1.20 = 11.8\,\mathrm{kPa} .
$$
The velocity head $\dfrac{v^2}{2g} = 0.142\,\mathrm{m}$ contributes to total head. Adding it to the depth to calculate pressure would give $13.2\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The value $5.89\,\mathrm{kPa}$ is the pressure at middepth, and $113\,\mathrm{kPa}$ is the absolute pressure obtained by adding atmospheric pressure.
