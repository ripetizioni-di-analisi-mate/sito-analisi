A rectangular channel of width $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and roughness coefficient $k_s = 55\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ carries $Q = 12.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. What is the critical slope?

- $0.00327$
- $0.00555$
- $0.00438$
- $0.00978$

## Explanation

The critical slope is the bed slope for which normal depth equals critical depth, so impose uniform flow with $h = h_c$. The critical depth is $h_c = \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{144}{9.81 \cdot 16}} = 0.972\,\mathrm{m}$, giving $A_c = 3.887\,\mathrm{m^2}$, $C_c = 5.943\,\mathrm{m}$, and $R_c = 0.654\,\mathrm{m}$. The Gauckler–Strickler equation then gives
$$
i_c = \left(\frac{Q}{k_s\,A_c\,R_c^{\frac{2}{3}}}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{12.0}{55 \cdot 3.887 \cdot 0.654^{\frac{2}{3}}}\right)^2 = 0.00555 .
$$
The value $0.00327$ follows from equating the hydraulic radius to $h_c$. The value $0.00438$ counts only one sidewall in the wetted perimeter, and $0.00978$ raises the hydraulic radius to the power $\dfrac{4}{3}$ in place of $\dfrac{2}{3}$.
