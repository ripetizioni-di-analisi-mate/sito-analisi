In a stilling basin with a horizontal bed and rectangular cross-section, the supercritical flow enters with Froude number $Fr_1 = 3.00$ and forms a hydraulic jump. What is the ratio $\dfrac{h_2}{h_1}$ between the downstream and upstream sequent depths?

- $4.27$
- $2.00$
- $7.54$
- $3.77$

## Explanation

The bed is horizontal and the section is rectangular, so the sequent-depth formula applies, derived from the equality of the specific forces of the two sections:
$$
\frac{h_2}{h_1} = \frac{1}{2}\left(\sqrt{1 + 8\,Fr_1^2} - 1\right) = \frac{1}{2}\left(\sqrt{1 + 8 \cdot 9.00} - 1\right) = \frac{1}{2}\,(8.544 - 1) = 3.77 .
$$
The downstream depth is therefore $3.77$ times the upstream depth, and the downstream flow is subcritical. The value $4.27$ comes from dropping the term $-1$, the value $7.54$ from dropping the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and the value $2.00$ from using $Fr_1$ instead of its square.
