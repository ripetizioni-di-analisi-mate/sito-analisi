A horizontal rectangular channel of width $b = 6.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 21.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The supercritical depth is $h_1 = 0.60\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the conjugate depth?

- $2.06\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.05\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.36\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.76\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The upstream mean velocity is $v_1 = \dfrac{21.0}{6.00 \cdot 0.60} = 5.83\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so
$$
Fr_1 = \frac{5.83}{\sqrt{9.81 \cdot 0.60}} = 2.40 .
$$
Since $Fr_1 > 1$, the flow is supercritical and the conjugate-depth formula applies:
$$
h_2 = \frac{0.60}{2}\left(\sqrt{1 + 8 \cdot 2.40^2} - 1\right) = 1.76\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
As a check, the momentum function in force units equals $S(h_1) = S(h_2) = 133\,\mathrm{kN}$ at both sections. The values $2.06\,\mathrm{m}$ and $2.36\,\mathrm{m}$ result from omitting the term $-1$ or reversing its sign. The value $1.05\,\mathrm{m}$ uses $Fr_1$ instead of its square.
