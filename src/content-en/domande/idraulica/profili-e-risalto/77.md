A roadside ditch of symmetric triangular section has side slopes $m = 1.0$ and carries $1.50\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. At what water depth is the Froude number equal to $1$?

- $0.856\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.745\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.612\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.386\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The critical depth satisfies $Fr = 1$, or $Fr^2 = \dfrac{Q^2 B}{g A^3} = 1$. In the triangular channel the flow area is $A = m\,h^2$ and the free-surface width is $B = 2\,m\,h$, giving
$$
\frac{Q^2 \cdot 2\,m\,h}{g\,m^3 h^6} = 1
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
h_c = \sqrt[5]{\frac{2\,Q^2}{g\,m^2}} = \sqrt[5]{\frac{2 \cdot 2.25}{9.81}} = 0.856\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $0.745\,\mathrm{m}$ takes $B = m\,h$ and therefore counts only one side. The values $0.612\,\mathrm{m}$ and $0.386\,\mathrm{m}$ use the rectangular-channel formula $\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{Q^2}{g\,b^2}}$ with $b = m$ and $b = 2\,m$, respectively. That formula does not apply to a triangle because its width changes with depth.
