A rectangular channel of width $b = 5.00\,\mathrm{m}$, roughness coefficient $k_s = 40\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ and bed slope $i = 0.015$ carries $Q = 10.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Immediately downstream of a sluice gate the water depth is $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$. Which profile forms?

- An S3 profile
- An M3 profile
- An S2 profile
- An S1 profile

## Explanation

The critical depth is $h_c = \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{100}{9.81 \cdot 25}} = 0.742\,\mathrm{m}$ and the critical slope is $i_c = 0.00958$. Since $i > i_c$, the bed slope is steep. The Gauckler–Strickler equation gives $h_0 = 0.640\,\mathrm{m}$, less than $h_c$ as expected on a steep slope. The depth immediately downstream of the gate, $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$, is below both $h_0$ and $h_c$. The flow therefore occupies zone 3 on an **S3** profile, increasing in depth downstream toward normal depth. An M3 profile would require a mild slope.
