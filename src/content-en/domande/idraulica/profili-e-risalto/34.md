A horizontal rectangular channel of width $b = 5.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 20.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ at a supercritical depth of $h_1 = 0.80\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the Froude number upstream of the jump?

- $0.637$
- $1.26$
- $1.78$
- $3.19$

## Explanation

The mean velocity is $v_1 = \dfrac{Q}{b\,h_1} = \dfrac{20.0}{5.00 \cdot 0.80} = 5.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$. For a rectangular channel, the hydraulic depth equals the water depth, giving
$$
Fr_1 = \frac{v_1}{\sqrt{g\,h_1}} = \frac{5.00}{\sqrt{9.81 \cdot 0.80}} = 1.78 .
$$
The result exceeds $1$, so the flow is supercritical, as stated. The value $3.19$ is the square of the Froude number. The value $0.637$ omits the square root in the denominator, and $1.26$ puts $2 g h_1$ in place of $g h_1$ under the square root.
