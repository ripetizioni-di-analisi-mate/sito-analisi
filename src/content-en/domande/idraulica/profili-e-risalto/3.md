A rectangular channel of width $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$, roughness coefficient $k_s = 65\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ and bed slope $i = 0.0006$ carries $Q = 2.40\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Water emerges from under the sluice gate at a depth of $h_1 = 0.18\,\mathrm{m}$, as shown. Which profile forms immediately downstream of the gate?

- An M3 profile with depth increasing downstream
- An M2 profile with depth decreasing downstream
- An S3 profile with depth increasing downstream
- An M1 profile with depth increasing downstream

## Explanation

The critical depth is $h_c = 0.528\,\mathrm{m}$ and the critical slope is $i_c = 0.00506$. Since $i < i_c$, the bed is mild and the normal depth $h_0 = 1.145\,\mathrm{m}$ exceeds $h_c$. The depth leaving the gate, $0.18\,\mathrm{m}$, is below both $h_0$ and $h_c$, placing the water surface in zone 3. The profile is **M3**. In this zone the numerator $i - J$ and denominator $1 - Fr^2$ in the profile equation are both negative, so $\dfrac{dh}{dx} > 0$ and the profile rises downstream. An S3 profile would require a steep bed.
