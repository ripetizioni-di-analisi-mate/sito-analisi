A laboratory compares two jumps in horizontal rectangular channels. The first has conjugate depths $h_1,h_2$; the second has $2h_1,2h_2$. How does the energy loss **per unit weight** across the jump change?

- It doubles.
- It remains unchanged.
- It quadruples.
- It increases eightfold.

## Explanation

The loss is $(h_2-h_1)^3/(4h_1h_2)$. Doubling both depths multiplies the numerator by eight and the denominator by four, for a net factor of two.
