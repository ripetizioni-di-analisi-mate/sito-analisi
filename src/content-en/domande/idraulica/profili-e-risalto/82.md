Which configuration gives rise to an H2 profile?

- A horizontal channel ending in a bed drop, in the reach preceding the outlet
- A horizontal channel immediately downstream of a sluice gate
- A mildly sloping channel ending in a bed drop, in the reach preceding the outlet
- A steep channel in the reach upstream of a barrier

## Explanation

An H2 profile occurs on a horizontal bed in zone 2. It lies above critical depth and decreases in depth downstream. Where a horizontal channel ends in a drop, the approaching flow is subcritical and reaches critical depth at the outlet. Immediately upstream, depth exceeds $h_c$ and decreases downstream to $h_c$, giving an **H2** profile. Downstream of a sluice gate the depth is below $h_c$ and the profile is H3. The same outlet on a mild bed gives M2, while a barrier on a steep bed gives S1.
