At a section of a rectangular channel carrying gradually varied flow, the measured values are bed slope $i = 0.0012$, friction slope $J = 0.0030$ and Froude number $Fr = 0.45$. What is $\dfrac{dh}{dx}$?

- $+0.00226$
- $-0.00180$
- $-0.00327$
- $-0.00226$

## Explanation

The gradually varied flow equation is
$$
\frac{dh}{dx} = \frac{i - J}{1 - Fr^2} ,
$$
so substituting the data gives
$$
\frac{dh}{dx} = \frac{0.0012 - 0.0030}{1 - 0.45^2} = \frac{-0.0018}{0.7975} = -0.00226 .
$$
The negative sign means the water depth decreases downstream and the free surface falls. Using only the numerator would give $-0.00180$. The value $-0.00327$ follows from writing $1 - Fr$ in place of $1 - Fr^2$ in the denominator.
