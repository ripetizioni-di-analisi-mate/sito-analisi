The direct-step method for a prismatic channel uses $\Delta x=(E_2-E_1)/(i-\bar J)$. Measurements give $E_2-E_1=0.020\,\mathrm m$, $i=0.0020$ and $\bar J=0.0018$. If only $\bar J$ is revised to $0.0019$, what estimate follows and why is it so sensitive?

- $200\,\mathrm m$, because the denominator is halved.
- $50\,\mathrm m$, because the denominator doubles.
- $100\,\mathrm m$, because the numerator is unchanged.
- $20\,\mathrm m$, because the difference in gradients is small.

## Explanation

Bed slope minus friction slope changes from $0.0002$ to $0.0001$. The distance changes from $0.020/0.0002=100$ to $0.020/0.0001=200$ metres. Subtracting close values magnifies the relative effect of the correction.
