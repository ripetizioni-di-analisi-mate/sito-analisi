Along a prismatic channel with bed slope $i = 0.0004$, an M2 profile is computed with the direct step method. At section $1$ the specific energy is $E_1 = 1.250\,\mathrm{m}$ and the friction slope is $J_1 = 0.0012$; at section $2$, with the water depth chosen for the step, one computes $E_2 = 1.210\,\mathrm{m}$ and $J_2 = 0.0016$. How far is section $2$ from section $1$?

- $40.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $22.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $50.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $33.3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The direct step method uses the mean friction slope of the step, $\bar{J} = \dfrac{J_1 + J_2}{2} = 0.0014$. The distance between the two sections is therefore
$$
\Delta x = \frac{E_2 - E_1}{i - \bar{J}} = \frac{1.210 - 1.250}{0.0004 - 0.0014} = \frac{-0.040}{-0.0010} = 40.0\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The result is positive, so section $2$ lies $40.0\,\mathrm{m}$ downstream of section $1$, as required by an M2 profile, along which the water depth decreases in the downstream direction. Using only the friction slope $J_1$ would give $50.0\,\mathrm{m}$, and using only $J_2$ would give $33.3\,\mathrm{m}$. Adding the mean friction slope to the bed slope instead of subtracting it would give a distance of $22.2\,\mathrm{m}$ with the wrong sign.
