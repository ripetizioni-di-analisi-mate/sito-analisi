A rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 2.70\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. At one section the depth is $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$. In which direction should the profile through that section be traced, and why?

- Downstream, because the flow is supercritical with $Fr = 3.48$
- Upstream, because the flow is supercritical with $Fr = 3.48$
- Downstream, because the flow is subcritical with $Fr = 0.287$
- Upstream, because the flow is subcritical with $Fr = 0.287$

## Explanation

The mean velocity is $v = \dfrac{2.70}{3.00 \cdot 1.00} = 0.900\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so
$$
Fr = \frac{v}{\sqrt{g\,h}} = \frac{0.900}{\sqrt{9.81 \cdot 1.00}} = 0.287 .
$$
Since $Fr < 1$, the flow is subcritical. Velocity is less than small-wave speed and conditions downstream determine the upstream depths. Trace the profile **upstream** from the downstream control section. The value $3.48$ is the reciprocal of the Froude number, not the Froude number itself.
