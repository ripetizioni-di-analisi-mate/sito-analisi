A rectangular channel of width $b = 4.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and roughness coefficient $k_s = 50\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ carries $Q = 9.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The upstream reach has bed slope $i_1 = 0.0008$ and the downstream reach has bed slope $i_2 = 0.025$, as shown. What is the normal depth in the downstream reach?

::schema{solidi="0,1.30 6,1.15 12,0.25 12,-0.15 6,0.75 0,0.90" frecce="1.0,1.95,2.8,1.92,Q" tratteggi="6,1.15 6,2.40" etichette="2.8,0.30,i_1 = 0.0008;9.4,-0.20,i_2 = 0.025;6.0,2.65,slope change" riferimento=0 titolo="Change of bed slope from $i_1 = 0.0008$ to $i_2 = 0.025$"}

- $1.51\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.742\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.473\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.438\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The normal depth in the downstream reach satisfies the Gauckler–Strickler equation evaluated with $i_2 = 0.025$:
$$
9.00 = 50 \cdot 4.50\,h_{02}\left(\frac{4.50\,h_{02}}{4.50 + 2 h_{02}}\right)^{\frac{2}{3}}\sqrt{0.025} ,
$$
Iteration gives $h_{02} = 0.473\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $1.51\,\mathrm{m}$ is the normal depth in the upstream reach, whose slope is one thirtieth as large. The value $0.742\,\mathrm{m}$ is the critical depth, identical in both reaches because it depends only on discharge and width. The value $0.438\,\mathrm{m}$ uses water depth in place of hydraulic radius. The true hydraulic radius is $\dfrac{A}{C} = 0.391\,\mathrm{m}$, smaller than the water depth.
