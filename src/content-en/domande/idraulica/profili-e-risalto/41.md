A horizontal rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 6.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. At what depth is the momentum function in force units a minimum?

- $0.742\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.07\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.494\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In a rectangular channel, the momentum function in force units is
$$
S(h) = \frac{\gamma\,b\,h^2}{2} + \frac{\rho\,Q^2}{b\,h} ,
$$
so setting its derivative with respect to depth equal to zero gives $\gamma\,b\,h = \dfrac{\rho\,Q^2}{b\,h^2}$, or $h^3 = \dfrac{Q^2}{g\,b^2}$. The momentum function therefore reaches its minimum at critical depth. Here,
$$
h_c = \sqrt[3]{\frac{36}{9.81 \cdot 9}} = 0.742\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Every value above the minimum corresponds to two depths, one below and one above $h_c$. These are sequent depths. The value $1.11\,\mathrm{m}$ equals $\dfrac{3}{2}\,h_c$, the minimum specific energy.
