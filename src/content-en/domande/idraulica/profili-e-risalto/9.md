A horizontal rectangular channel of width $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 13.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ at a supercritical depth of $h_1 = 0.50\,\mathrm{m}$. How much power is dissipated in the hydraulic jump?

- $171\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $83.4\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $8.50\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $334\,\mathrm{kW}$

## Explanation

The upstream velocity is $v_1 = 6.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and its Froude number is $Fr_1 = 2.93$, giving the conjugate depth
$$
h_2 = \frac{0.50}{2}\left(\sqrt{1 + 8 \cdot 2.93^2} - 1\right) = 1.84\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The head loss across the jump is then
$$
\Delta E = \frac{(h_2 - h_1)^3}{4\,h_1 h_2} = \frac{(1.84 - 0.50)^3}{4 \cdot 0.50 \cdot 1.84} = 0.654\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
and the dissipated power is $P = \gamma\,Q\,\Delta E = 9.81 \cdot 13.0 \cdot 0.654 = 83.4\,\mathrm{kW}$. The value $171\,\mathrm{kW}$ takes the depth difference $h_2 - h_1$ as the head loss. The value $334\,\mathrm{kW}$ omits the factor $4$ in the head-loss denominator, and $8.50\,\mathrm{kW}$ omits the specific weight of water.
