In which direction is a subcritical profile traced, and why?

- Downstream, because disturbances travel only in the flow direction
- Upstream, because disturbances can propagate against the flow
- Downstream, because the flow velocity exceeds the wave speed of small disturbances
- In either direction, because the flow is steady

## Explanation

Small disturbances travel at speed $\sqrt{g\,h_m}$ relative to the water, where $h_m$ is hydraulic depth. Subcritical flow satisfies $Fr < 1$, or $v < \sqrt{g\,h_m}$. A disturbance generated downstream can therefore travel upstream and carry a downstream level condition with it. A subcritical profile is traced **upstream** from its downstream control. The opposite inequality holds in supercritical flow. No disturbance travels upstream and the profile is traced downstream.
