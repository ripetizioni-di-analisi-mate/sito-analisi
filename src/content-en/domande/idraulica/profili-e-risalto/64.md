A horizontal rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 7.50\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Which pair could represent the depths upstream and downstream of a hydraulic jump?

- $0.70\,\mathrm{m}$ and $1.37\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and $1.10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and $1.37\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ and $1.37\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Each candidate pair straddles the critical depth $h_c = \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{7.50^2}{9.81 \cdot 9}} = 0.860\,\mathrm{m}$. To be conjugate, the two depths must also give the same value of the momentum function
$$
S(h) = \frac{\gamma\,b\,h^2}{2} + \frac{\rho\,Q^2}{b\,h} .
$$
The values $S(0.50) = 3.68 + 37.5 = 41.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $S(1.37) = 27.6 + 13.7 = 41.3\,\mathrm{kN}$ agree to within rounding, so **$0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and $1.37\,\mathrm{m}$** are conjugate depths. For the other pairs, $S(1.10) = 34.9\,\mathrm{kN}$ differs from $S(0.50)$, and both $S(0.70) = 34.0\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $S(0.40) = 49.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ differ from $S(1.37)$. None of those pairs has equal momentum functions.
