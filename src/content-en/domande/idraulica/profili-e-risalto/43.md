The floor of a stilling basin at the foot of an outlet lies below the downstream channel bed. What is the purpose of lowering the floor?

- To reduce the discharge from the outlet
- To increase the available downstream depth and keep the jump inside the basin
- To turn the subcritical flow in the downstream channel into supercritical flow
- To reduce the jump length to zero

## Explanation

Water reaches the toe of the spillway in supercritical flow. It must return to subcritical flow before entering the natural channel to avoid bed erosion. The jump occurs where the upstream and downstream depths are conjugate. If the downstream water level gives less than the conjugate depth $h_2$, the jump moves downstream and may leave the lined reach. Lowering the basin floor increases the depth of tailwater available above that floor and keeps the jump in the basin. The basin length includes the jump length plus a margin. An end sill helps stabilize the jump location.
