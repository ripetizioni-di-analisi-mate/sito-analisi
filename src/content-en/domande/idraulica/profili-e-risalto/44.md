A rectangular channel of width $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 12.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ at a depth of $h = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$. At what speed do small disturbances travel relative to the water?

- $2.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $11.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.85\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.43\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Small disturbances travel at speed $\sqrt{g\,h_m}$ relative to the water. In a rectangular channel, hydraulic depth equals water depth, so
$$
c = \sqrt{g\,h} = \sqrt{9.81 \cdot 1.20} = 3.43\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The mean flow velocity is $v = \dfrac{12.0}{4.00 \cdot 1.20} = 2.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$, giving $Fr = \dfrac{2.50}{3.43} = 0.729$. Because the flow is subcritical, a disturbance can travel upstream from a downstream control, and the profile is traced upstream. The value $2.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the mean flow velocity. The value $11.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ omits the square root, and $4.85\,\mathrm{m/s}$ puts $2 g h$ in place of $g h$ under the square root.
