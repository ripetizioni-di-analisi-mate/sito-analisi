A rectangular channel of width $b = 3.50\,\mathrm{m}$, roughness coefficient $k_s = 45\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ and bed slope $i = 0.0009$ carries $Q = 5.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ and ends at a bed drop. What water depth is established at the outlet section?

- $1.29\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.889\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.395\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.593\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The critical depth is
$$
h_c = \sqrt[3]{\frac{Q^2}{g\,b^2}} = \sqrt[3]{\frac{25}{9.81 \cdot 12.25}} = 0.593\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
while the Gauckler–Strickler equation gives $h_0 = 1.29\,\mathrm{m}$ and the critical slope is $i_c = 0.00851$. Since $i < i_c$, the bed is mild and the upstream flow is subcritical. At the bed drop the flow changes from subcritical to supercritical, establishing critical depth $0.593\,\mathrm{m}$ at the outlet. This is the boundary condition for the upstream M2 profile. The value $0.889\,\mathrm{m}$ is $\dfrac{3}{2}\,h_c$, the minimum specific energy, rather than a water depth.
