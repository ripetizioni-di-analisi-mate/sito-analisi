A very long, steep rectangular channel ends in a bed drop. What water depth is observed at the last section immediately before the drop?

- Critical depth, as at every outlet over a bed drop
- The depth conjugate to normal depth
- The channel normal depth, which is less than critical depth
- A depth between $h_0$ and $h_c$, reached along an S2 profile

## Explanation

Normal flow on a steep bed is supercritical. Disturbances cannot travel upstream against it, so a downstream bed drop has no effect on the preceding sections. The channel is long enough for uniform flow to develop, and the water reaches the end at **normal depth** $h_0 < h_c$. The outlet would establish critical depth only if the approaching flow were subcritical, as on a mild slope, because only then could it act as a control section. An S2 profile would start at a critical section, which is absent here. Conjugate depths do not enter because flow remains supercritical and no jump forms.
