At the foot of an outlet, water enters a rectangular stilling basin of width $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ with discharge $Q = 14.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ and depth $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$. The downstream channel has water depth $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$, as shown. What is the minimum distance the basin floor must lie below the downstream bed to keep the jump in the basin? Neglect the change in entrance depth caused by lowering the floor.

::schema{solidi="0,0.60 3,0.60 3,0 13,0 13,0.60 17,0.60 17,-0.70 0,-0.70" acqua="0,0.60 3,0.60 3,0 13,0 13,0.60 17,0.60 17,2.40 13,2.40 11.4,2.34 9.8,2.15 8.2,1.75 6.6,1.20 5.0,1.00 3,1.00 0,1.00" frecce="0.6,0.80,1.8,0.80,Q" quote="16.4 0.60,16.4 2.40,1.80 m,-0.6" etichette="1.6,1.75,h_1 = 0.40 m;13.45,0.28,d;8.0,2.70,stilling basin" riferimento=0 titolo="Stilling basin with its floor $d$ below the downstream channel bed"}

- $1.91\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.23\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.51\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.31\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The inlet velocity is $v_1 = \dfrac{14.0}{4.00 \cdot 0.40} = 8.75\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the Froude number is $Fr_1 = \dfrac{8.75}{\sqrt{9.81 \cdot 0.40}} = 4.42$. The jump therefore requires a downstream depth of
$$
h_2 = \frac{0.40}{2}\left(\sqrt{1 + 8 \cdot 4.42^2} - 1\right) = 2.31\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The tailwater depth is $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$, less than $h_2$, so the jump would leave the basin unless its floor were lowered. Lowering the floor by $d$ increases the available tailwater depth by $d$ at the same free-surface elevation. The required condition is $1.80 + d \geq 2.31$, giving $d \geq 0.51\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $1.91\,\mathrm{m}$ is the difference between the sequent depths. The value $1.23\,\mathrm{m}$ is the difference between the downstream sequent depth and critical depth, and $2.31\,\mathrm{m}$ is the downstream sequent depth itself.
