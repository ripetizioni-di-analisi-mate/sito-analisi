A rectangular channel of width $b = 2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 4.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. What is the critical depth?

- $0.867\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.511\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.639\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.959\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At a fixed discharge, the derivative of specific energy with respect to depth vanishes at critical depth. For a rectangular channel this gives
$$
h_c = \sqrt[3]{\frac{Q^2}{g\,b^2}} = \sqrt[3]{\frac{16}{9.81 \cdot 6.25}} = 0.639\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The Froude number confirms the result. At $h = 0.639\,\mathrm{m}$ and $v = 2.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$, its value is $Fr = \dfrac{2.50}{\sqrt{9.81 \cdot 0.639}} = 1.00$. The value $0.867\,\mathrm{m}$ follows from writing $b$ in place of $b^2$ in the denominator. The value $0.511\,\mathrm{m}$ uses a square root in place of a cube root. The value $0.959\,\mathrm{m}$ is $\dfrac{3}{2}\,h_c$, the minimum specific energy.
