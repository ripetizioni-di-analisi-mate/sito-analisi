A rectangular channel of width $b = 2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and roughness coefficient $k_s = 60\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ carries $Q = 3.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. What is the friction slope at the section where the water depth is $h = 0.80\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $0.00163$
- $0.00101$
- $0.000842$
- $0.00122$

## Explanation

The flow area is $A = 2.50 \cdot 0.80 = 2.00\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the wetted perimeter is $C = b + 2h = 4.10\,\mathrm{m}$. The hydraulic radius is therefore $R = \dfrac{A}{C} = 0.488\,\mathrm{m}$ and the mean velocity is $v = \dfrac{Q}{A} = 1.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$. For gradually varied flow, the Gauckler–Strickler equation evaluated at the local depth gives the friction slope as
$$
J = \frac{v^2}{k_s^2\,R^{\frac{4}{3}}} = \frac{1.50^2}{60^2 \cdot 0.488^{\frac{4}{3}}} = 0.00163 .
$$
The value $0.000842$ sets the hydraulic radius equal to the water depth. The value $0.00122$ counts only one sidewall in the wetted perimeter, and $0.00101$ raises the hydraulic radius to the power $\dfrac{2}{3}$ in place of $\dfrac{4}{3}$.
