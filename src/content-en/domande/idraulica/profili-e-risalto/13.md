A horizontal trapezoidal channel has bottom width $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and side slopes $m = 1.5$. The discharge is $Q = 7.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ and the supercritical depth is $h_1 = 0.45\,\mathrm{m}$, as shown. What is the conjugate depth?

::schema{solidi="-6,-0.8 6,-0.8 6,0 -6,0;-6,0 -1.0,0 -4.3,2.2 -6,2.2;6,0 1.0,0 4.3,2.2 6,2.2" acqua="-1.0,0 1.0,0 1.675,0.45 -1.675,0.45" pelo="-1.675,1.675,0.45," quote="-1.0 -0.8,1.0 -0.8,2.00 m,0.9" etichette="0,1.05,h_1 = 0.45 m;-5.2,1.45,m = 1.5;5.2,1.45,m = 1.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Trapezoidal section with bottom width $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and side slope $m = 1.5$"}

- $1.76\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.44\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.14\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.862\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In the trapezoidal section the flow area is $A = (b + m\,h)\,h$, giving $A_1 = 1.204\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $v_1 = 5.82\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The momentum function in force units equals the hydrostatic force on the section plus the momentum flux through it:
$$
S(h) = \gamma\left(\frac{b\,h^2}{2} + \frac{m\,h^3}{3}\right) + \frac{\rho\,Q^2}{A(h)} ,
$$
At the upstream section this is $S(h_1) = 43.1\,\mathrm{kN}$. The momentum function is conserved across the jump, so the conjugate depth is the root of $S(h_2) = S(h_1)$ greater than the critical depth $h_c = 0.862\,\mathrm{m}$. Iteration gives $h_2 = 1.44\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $2.14\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from the rectangular conjugate-depth formula using the bottom width, while $1.76\,\mathrm{m}$ uses the same formula with the Froude number of the trapezoidal section. That formula applies only to a rectangular section.
