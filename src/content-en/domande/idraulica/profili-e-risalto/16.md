A rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 7.50\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ at a depth of $h = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$. The bed elevation at this section is $z = 12.50\,\mathrm{m}$ above the horizontal datum. What is the total head there?

- $13.82\,\mathrm{m}$
- $13.50\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14.14\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12.82\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The mean velocity is
$$
v = \frac{Q}{b\,h} = \frac{7.50}{3.00 \cdot 1.00} = 2.50\,\mathrm{m/s} ,
$$
so the velocity head is $\dfrac{v^2}{2g} = \dfrac{2.50^2}{2 \cdot 9.81} = 0.319\,\mathrm{m}$. For open-channel flow with a kinetic energy correction factor of $\alpha = 1$, total head is $H = z + h + \dfrac{v^2}{2g}$. Hence
$$
H = 12.50 + 1.00 + 0.319 = 13.82\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $13.50\,\mathrm{m}$ is the piezometric head, equal to the free-surface elevation. It omits velocity head. The value $14.14\,\mathrm{m}$ follows from using $\dfrac{v^2}{g}$ in place of $\dfrac{v^2}{2g}$, and $12.82\,\mathrm{m}$ results from omitting water depth.
