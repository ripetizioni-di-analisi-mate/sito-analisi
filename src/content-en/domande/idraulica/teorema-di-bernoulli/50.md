At a nozzle section the centerline is $1.50\,\mathrm{m}$ above the datum, gauge pressure is $40\,\mathrm{kPa}$, and speed is $8.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the mechanical energy per unit volume of fluid there?

- $72.0\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $54.7\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $118.7\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $86.7\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Multiplying total head by $\gamma$ gives the energy per unit volume $p + \gamma z + \dfrac{1}{2}\rho v^2$. Each term has units of pressure. The two terms to be added to the pressure are
$$
\gamma z = 9.81 \cdot 1.50 = 14.7\,\mathrm{kPa} ,
\qquad
\frac{1}{2}\rho v^2 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 1000 \cdot 8.00^2 = 32\,000\,\mathrm{Pa} = 32.0\,\mathrm{kPa} ,
$$
so the energy per unit volume is $40 + 14.7 + 32.0 = 86.7\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The same value follows by multiplying the total head $H = 1.50 + 4.08 + 3.26 = 8.84\,\mathrm{m}$ by $\gamma$. The dynamic pressure includes the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so using $\rho v^2$ would give $118.7\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
