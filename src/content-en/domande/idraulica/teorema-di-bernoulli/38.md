An orifice meter is calibrated using an independent flowmeter. At the same geometry and differential head, the ideal model predicts $40\,\mathrm{L/s}$ while the measurement reads $26\,\mathrm{L/s}$. If differential head quadruples with the coefficient unchanged, which pair gives the discharge coefficient and the new actual flow rate?

- $0.65$ and $104\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $0.65$ and $52\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $1.54$ and $52\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $0.65$ and $26\,\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

Calibration gives $C=Q_{\rm actual}/Q_{\rm ideal}=26/40$. With unchanged geometry $Q$ is proportional to $\sqrt{\Delta h}$; quadrupling the head reading doubles the measured $26\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
