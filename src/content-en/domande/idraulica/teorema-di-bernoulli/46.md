A mechanical pressure gauge is mounted $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$ above the pipe centerline and connected by a water-filled tube. It reads gauge pressure $95\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The centerline is $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ above the datum. What is the piezometric head at this section?

::schema{nodi="M,0.4,2,-;N,7,2,-" condotte="M-N,0.6" linee="4,2.3 4,3.55;0,0 10,0" cerchi="4,3.8,0.25" tratteggi="7.2,2 9.4,2;4.3,3.8 8.2,3.8" quote="9.2 0,9.2 2,2.00 m,0;8 2,8 3.8,1.80 m,0" etichette="2,0.35,datum;2.4,4.2,pressure gauge" riferimento=0 titolo="Mechanical pressure gauge installed above the pipe centerline"}

- $11.68\,\mathrm{m}$
- $13.48\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9.88\,\mathrm{m}$
- $23.81\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The water in the connecting tube is at rest. By the **hydrostatic pressure law**, $z + \dfrac{p}{\gamma}$ has the same value at the gauge connection and at the pipe centerline. The gauge is at elevation $2.00 + 1.80 = 3.80\,\mathrm{m}$ and its pressure head is $\dfrac{95}{9.81} = 9.68\,\mathrm{m}$, so
$$
h = 3.80 + 9.68 = 13.48\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The centerline is $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$ lower and the pressure there is higher. Adding its pressure head $9.68 + 1.80 = 11.48\,\mathrm{m}$ to its elevation again gives $13.48\,\mathrm{m}$. Assigning the gauge reading directly to the centerline would give $11.68\,\mathrm{m}$ and neglect the water column in the connecting tube.
