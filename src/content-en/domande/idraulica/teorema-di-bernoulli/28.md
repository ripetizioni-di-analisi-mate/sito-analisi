The mean speed in laminar flow in a circular pipe is $0.40\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the kinetic energy correction factor is $\alpha = 2$. What kinetic term enters the total head of the flow?

- $0.00815\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.0326\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.0652\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.0163\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For one-dimensional flow, the velocity term in the total head is $\alpha\,\dfrac{v^2}{2g}$, where $v$ is the mean velocity and the **kinetic energy correction factor** accounts for the velocity distribution across the section. Substitution gives
$$
\alpha\,\frac{v^2}{2g} = 2 \cdot \frac{0.40^2}{2 \cdot 9.81} = 2 \cdot 0.00815 = 0.0163\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
For the laminar flow considered here the factor is exactly $2$. Assuming $\alpha = 1$ would therefore halve the term and give $0.00815\,\mathrm{m}$. In turbulent pipe flow, the factor is between $1.03$ and $1.10$, so $\alpha = 1$ is usually a good approximation.
