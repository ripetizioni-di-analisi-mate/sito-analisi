A Pitot tube in a rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and water depth $h = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ shows a liquid level $0.18\,\mathrm{m}$ above the free surface. Assuming uniform velocity, what is the Froude number and flow regime?

- $Fr = 1.83$, supercritical flow
- $Fr = 0.387$, subcritical flow
- $Fr = 0.160$, subcritical flow
- $Fr = 0.548$, subcritical flow

## Explanation

The rise in the tube above the free surface equals the velocity head. The **Pitot tube** relation gives $v = \sqrt{2 g \cdot 0.18} = 1.88\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In a rectangular section the hydraulic depth $h_m = \dfrac{A}{B} = \dfrac{3.00 \cdot 1.20}{3.00} = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ equals the water depth. The Froude number is therefore
$$
Fr = \frac{v}{\sqrt{g\,h_m}} = \frac{1.88}{\sqrt{9.81 \cdot 1.20}} = 0.548 .
$$
Since $Fr < 1$, the flow is **subcritical**.
