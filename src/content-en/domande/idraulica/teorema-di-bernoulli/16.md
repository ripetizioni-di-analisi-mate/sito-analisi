At a pipe section the centerline is at elevation $z = 3.20\,\mathrm{m}$ and the gauge pressure is $p = 85\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the piezometric head?

- $8.66\,\mathrm{m}$
- $22.19\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11.86\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Dividing the pressure by the specific weight of water gives the pressure head,
$$
\frac{p}{\gamma} = \frac{85}{9.81} = 8.66\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Adding the elevation gives the piezometric head,
$$
h = z + \frac{p}{\gamma} = 3.20 + 8.66 = 11.86\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The given pressure is a gauge pressure, so divide it directly by $\gamma$. Adding atmospheric pressure first would give $22.19\,\mathrm{m}$, the piezometric head expressed in terms of absolute pressure.
