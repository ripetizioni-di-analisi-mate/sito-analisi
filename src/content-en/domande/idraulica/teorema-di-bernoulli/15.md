A pipe of diameter $D = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries discharge $Q = 50\,\mathrm{L/s}$. What is the velocity head?

- $0.258\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.27\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.129\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.0081\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The cross-sectional area is $A = \dfrac{\pi D^2}{4} = \dfrac{\pi \cdot 0.200^2}{4} = 0.0314\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so the mean velocity is
$$
v = \frac{Q}{A} = \frac{0.050}{0.0314} = 1.59\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Dividing the square of this velocity by twice the acceleration due to gravity gives the velocity head,
$$
\frac{v^2}{2g} = \frac{1.59^2}{2 \cdot 9.81} = 0.129\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Omitting the factor $2$ from the denominator gives $\dfrac{v^2}{g}$ and doubles the result to $0.258\,\mathrm{m}$.
