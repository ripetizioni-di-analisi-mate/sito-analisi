Water leaves an open constant-level tank through a short converging nozzle of diameter $D = 80\,\mathrm{mm}$ whose axis is $3.20\,\mathrm{m}$ below the free surface. Neglecting head loss, what is the discharge?

- $39.8\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $28.2\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $159\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $316\,\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

The reservoir is open and its level stays constant, so the free surface is at zero gauge pressure and has negligible speed. The jet at the outlet is also at zero gauge pressure. By **Bernoulli’s equation**, total head is the same at both points. The elevation difference between the free surface and nozzle centerline therefore equals the outlet velocity head, giving
$$
v = \sqrt{2 g h} = \sqrt{2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 3.20} = 7.92\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The nozzle outlet area is $A = \dfrac{\pi \cdot 0.080^2}{4} = 0.00503\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so the discharge is
$$
Q = v A = 7.92 \cdot 0.00503 = 0.0398\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 39.8\,\mathrm{L/s} .
$$
