A pipe section has total head $H = 24.00\,\mathrm{m}$, centerline elevation $z = 5.00\,\mathrm{m}$, and velocity head $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the pressure head?

- $19.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $19.40\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Total head is the sum of elevation, pressure head, and velocity head. Subtracting the other two terms gives the pressure head,
$$
\frac{p}{\gamma} = H - z - \frac{v^2}{2g} = 24.00 - 5.00 - 0.40 = 18.60\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The piezometric head at this section is therefore $h = 5.00 + 18.60 = 23.60\,\mathrm{m}$, the total head less the velocity head. Adding the velocity head instead would give $19.40\,\mathrm{m}$.
