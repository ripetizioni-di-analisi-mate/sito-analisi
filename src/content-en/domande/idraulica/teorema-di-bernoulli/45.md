A pumping system must be checked to ensure that absolute pressure stays above the vapor pressure of water. Which section should be checked?

- The pump inlet above the free surface of the supply reservoir
- The pump discharge section, where pressure is highest
- The outlet into the downstream reservoir
- The pipe section at the lowest elevation in the system

## Explanation

**Cavitation** begins where absolute pressure falls to the vapor pressure. Look for the section where the hydraulic grade line is lowest relative to the pipe centerline. In the suction line of a pump above the free surface, the elevation rise, velocity head, and head loss all contribute to the distance that the hydraulic grade line lies below the centerline at the pump inlet. The inlet therefore has the lowest pressure in this system. At the discharge section, the pump has already added head to the fluid and the pressure is high. At the low points of this system, piezometric head exceeds centerline elevation, so gauge pressure is positive and cavitation does not occur.
