A constant-level reservoir supplies a service connection at elevation $15.00\,\mathrm{m}$. At design discharge the required gauge pressure is at least $150\,\mathrm{kPa}$, the pipe speed is $1.20\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and total head loss is $4.50\,\mathrm{m}$. What minimum reservoir surface elevation is required?

- $34.9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $25.9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $19.6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Gauge pressure at the reservoir free surface is zero, and velocity there is negligible. The reservoir total head therefore equals the free-surface elevation $z_s$. Applying **Bernoulli’s equation with head loss** between the surface and the delivery point gives
$$
z_s = 15.00 + \frac{150}{9.81} + \frac{1.20^2}{2 \cdot 9.81} + 4.50
= 15.00 + 15.29 + 0.07 + 4.50 = 34.9\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The reservoir must supply the head required at the delivery point plus the head dissipated in the pipe, so the loss is added. Subtracting it would give a reservoir elevation of $25.9\,\mathrm{m}$ and a delivery pressure of only $62\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at the design discharge.
