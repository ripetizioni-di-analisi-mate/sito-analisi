A Venturi meter in a horizontal pipe of diameter $D_1 = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ has throat diameter $D_2 = 150\,\mathrm{mm}$. Piezometers connected upstream and at the throat show a level difference $\Delta h = 1.10\,\mathrm{m}$. Neglecting head loss, what is the discharge?

::schema{acqua="0,0.6 3,0.6 4.4,0.3 6,0.3 7.4,0.6 11,0.6 11,-0.6 7.4,-0.6 6,-0.3 4.4,-0.3 3,-0.6 0,-0.6" linee="0,0.6 3,0.6 4.4,0.3 6,0.3 7.4,0.6 11,0.6;0,-0.6 3,-0.6 4.4,-0.3 6,-0.3 7.4,-0.6 11,-0.6;1.5,0.6 1.5,4 2.1,4 2.1,0.6;5,0.3 5,3.3 5.6,3.3 5.6,0.3" tratteggi="0,0 11,0;2.1,4 7.2,4;5.6,3.3 7.2,3.3" frecce="0.3,0,1.2,0,Q" etichette="1.8,-0.95,1;5.3,-0.95,2;1.8,-1.5,D_1 = 300 mm;5.3,-1.5,D_2 = 150 mm;8.8,3.5,Δh = 1.10 m" quote="6.9 3.3,6.9 4,,0.45" riferimento=0 titolo="Venturi meter with two piezometers and $\Delta h = 1.10\,\mathrm{m}$"}

- $84.8\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $82.1\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $60.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $339\,\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

The areas are $A_1 = 0.0707\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $A_2 = 0.0177\,\mathrm{m^2}$, giving $0.250$ for the ratio $\dfrac{A_2}{A_1}$. The **Venturi meter** equation therefore gives
$$
Q = \frac{A_2}{\sqrt{1 - \left(\dfrac{A_2}{A_1}\right)^2}}\;\sqrt{2 g\,\Delta h}
= \frac{0.0177}{\sqrt{1 - 0.0625}} \cdot \sqrt{2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 1.10} = 0.0848\,\mathrm{m^3/s} ,
$$
or $84.8\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The factor $\sqrt{1 - 0.0625} = 0.968$ accounts for the upstream velocity head. Omitting it gives $82.1\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and underestimates the discharge.
