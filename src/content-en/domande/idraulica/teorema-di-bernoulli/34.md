A Pitot tube in a channel shows a liquid level $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$ above the piezometer level at the same section. What is the flow speed at its opening?

- $2.21\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.57\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.91\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.13\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

A piezometer measures piezometric head, while a Pitot tube measures total head. The difference between their liquid levels therefore equals the velocity head,
$$
\Delta = \frac{v^2}{2g} = 0.25\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Solving for velocity gives the **Pitot tube** relation
$$
v = \sqrt{2 g\,\Delta} = \sqrt{2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.25} = 2.21\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $2 g\,\Delta = 4.91$ is the square of the velocity, so its square root must be taken.
