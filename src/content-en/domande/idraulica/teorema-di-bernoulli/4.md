## Explanation

The **continuity equation** gives speeds $v_1 = \dfrac{0.100}{0.0707} = 1.41\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2 = \dfrac{0.100}{0.0314} = 3.18\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so the velocity heads are $0.102\,\mathrm{m}$ and $0.516\,\mathrm{m}$. Applying **Bernoulli’s equation** between the two sections gives
$$
\frac{p_2}{\gamma} = z_1 - z_2 + \frac{p_1}{\gamma} + \frac{v_1^2}{2g} - \frac{v_2^2}{2g}
= -8.00 + 40.77 + 0.102 - 0.516 = 32.36\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Multiplying by specific weight gives $p_2 = 32.36 \cdot 9.81 = 317\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The elevation rises by $8.00\,\mathrm{m}$. This rise alone accounts for a pressure drop of $78.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The smaller diameter increases the velocity head and accounts for a further drop of $4\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
