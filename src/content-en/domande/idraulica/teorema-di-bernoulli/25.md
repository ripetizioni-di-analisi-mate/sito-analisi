At a pipe section, the centerline is at elevation $12.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and the hydraulic grade line at $9.50\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the gauge pressure?

- $-24.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $24.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $76.8\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $118\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The elevation of the hydraulic grade line is the local piezometric head $h = z + \dfrac{p}{\gamma}$. The pressure head is therefore the elevation of that line relative to the pipe centerline,
$$
\frac{p}{\gamma} = h - z = 9.50 - 12.00 = -2.50\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Multiplying by the specific weight gives $p = -2.50 \cdot 9.81 = -24.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Since the hydraulic grade line lies below the centerline, the gauge pressure is negative and this section operates below atmospheric pressure.
