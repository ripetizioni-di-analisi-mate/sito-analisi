A Pitot-static tube in a pipe of diameter $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ is connected to a differential mercury manometer showing a level difference $\Delta = 28\,\mathrm{mm}$. Mercury has specific gravity $13.6$. Assuming uniform velocity across the section, what is the flow speed?

- $0.741\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.73\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.63\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.86\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The **differential manometer** measures the difference between total head at the forward-facing port and piezometric head at the side ports.
$$
\Delta h = \Delta\left(\frac{\gamma_m}{\gamma} - 1\right) = 0.028 \cdot 12.6 = 0.353\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
This difference is the velocity head of the stream. The **Pitot tube** relation therefore gives
$$
v = \sqrt{2 g\,\Delta h} = \sqrt{2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.353} = 2.63\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The manometer reading must first be converted to meters of water. Substituting the reading directly would give $0.741\,\mathrm{m/s}$, only about one quarter of the actual speed.
