At a pipe section the centerline elevation is $8.00\,\mathrm{m}$, a piezometer reads elevation $13.60\,\mathrm{m}$, and a Pitot tube reads elevation $13.95\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the gauge pressure?

- $58.4\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $133\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $156\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $54.9\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The piezometer measures piezometric head, so $h = 13.60\,\mathrm{m}$. Subtracting the pipe centerline elevation gives the pressure head,
$$
\frac{p}{\gamma} = h - z = 13.60 - 8.00 = 5.60\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Multiplying by the specific weight gives $p = 5.60 \cdot 9.81 = 54.9\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The Pitot tube reads total head, which also includes the velocity head $\dfrac{v^2}{2g} = 13.95 - 13.60 = 0.35\,\mathrm{m}$. Using its liquid level to calculate static pressure would therefore give $58.4\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
