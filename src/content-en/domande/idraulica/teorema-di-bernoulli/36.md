A Pitot-static probe has its stagnation and static ports at the same height in water. An ideal differential gauge reads $2\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Static pressure is increased everywhere by $30\,\mathrm{kPa}$ without changing the local speed. What will it read?

- $32\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $28\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Stagnation pressure is $p+\rho v^2/2$. The gauge reads the difference from $p$. The common pressure rise cancels between the ports, leaving the same dynamic pressure.
