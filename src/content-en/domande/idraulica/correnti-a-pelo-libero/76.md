Two sections of a rectangular channel are $200\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. At the upstream section the bed elevation is $12.00\,\mathrm{m}$, water depth $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ and mean velocity $1.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At the downstream section the corresponding values are $11.90\,\mathrm{m}$, $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and $1.80\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the head loss between the sections?

- $0.300\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.100\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.250\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.199\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The total head at a section is $H = z_f + h + \dfrac{v^2}{2g}$, so
$$
H_1 = 12.00 + 1.20 + \frac{1.50^2}{2 \cdot 9.81} = 13.315\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
H_2 = 11.90 + 1.00 + \frac{1.80^2}{2 \cdot 9.81} = 13.065\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
and the head loss is $\Delta H = H_1 - H_2 = 0.250\,\mathrm{m}$. Since depth changes between the sections, the flow is nonuniform. The head loss therefore differs from the drop in bed elevation, $0.100\,\mathrm{m}$, which would be the loss in uniform flow. The value $0.300\,\mathrm{m}$ omits the velocity heads, while $0.199\,\mathrm{m}$ calculates them as $\dfrac{v^2}{g}$.
