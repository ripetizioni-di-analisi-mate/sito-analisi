At a fixed discharge per unit width in a rectangular channel, the minimum specific energy is $0.90\,\mathrm m$. A student tries to find alternate depths at specific energy $0.85\,\mathrm m$. How many physically admissible positive depths exist?

- One, necessarily critical
- Two
- Three
- None

## Explanation

The specified minimum of the specific-energy curve occurs at critical depth. A horizontal line below this minimum cannot intersect the curve at any positive depth.
