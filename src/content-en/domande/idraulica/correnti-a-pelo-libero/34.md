A horizontal rectangular throat must carry $Q=6.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The available specific energy is $E=1.50\,\mathrm m$ and losses are negligible. Using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the minimum width that allows passage without raising the upstream level?

- $0.95\,\mathrm m$
- $1.90\,\mathrm m$
- $3.00\,\mathrm m$
- $2.45\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

At the limiting width flow in the throat is critical, with $h_c=2E/3=1.00\,\mathrm m$. The maximum discharge per unit width is $q_c=\sqrt{g h_c^3}=\sqrt{10}\,\mathrm{m^2/s}$, giving $b_{\min}=Q/q_c\simeq1.90\,\mathrm m$.
