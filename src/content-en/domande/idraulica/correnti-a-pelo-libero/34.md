A rectangular channel of width $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a discharge of $Q = 4.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. What is the critical depth?

- $0.408\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.742\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.18\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In a rectangular section the critical depth depends only on the discharge per unit width, which is
$$
q = \frac{Q}{b} = \frac{4.00}{2.00} = 2.00\,\mathrm{m^2/s} .
$$
This gives
$$
h_c = \sqrt[3]{\frac{q^2}{g}} = \sqrt[3]{\frac{4.00}{9.81}} = \sqrt[3]{0.408} = 0.742\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $1.11\,\mathrm{m}$ is the minimum specific energy $\dfrac{3}{2}\,h_c$. The value $1.18\,\mathrm{m}$ would result from using total discharge in place of discharge per unit width.
