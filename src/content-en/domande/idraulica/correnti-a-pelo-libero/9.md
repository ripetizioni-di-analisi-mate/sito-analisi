A trapezoidal channel of bottom width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and side slope $m = 1$ carries a discharge of $Q = 10.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. What is the critical depth?

- $0.819\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.934\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.04\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.878\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Critical flow occurs at $Fr = 1$, which can be written for any cross section as $Q^2 B = g\,A^3$. For a trapezoidal section of side slope $m = 1$, $A(h) = (3.00 + h)\,h$ and $B(h) = 3.00 + 2h$. The critical-flow condition therefore becomes
$$
100 \cdot (3.00 + 2h_c) = 9.81\,\big[(3.00 + h_c)\,h_c\big]^3
$$
Solve iteratively. At $h = 0.900\,\mathrm{m}$ the right-hand side is $424$ and the left-hand side is $480$, so increase the depth. At $h = 0.934\,\mathrm{m}$ both sides agree. Thus $h_c = 0.934\,\mathrm{m}$, giving $A_c = 3.67\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $v_c = 2.72\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The rectangular-section formula $h_c = \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{Q^2}{g\,b^2}}$ would give $1.04\,\mathrm{m}$ and does not apply to this channel.
