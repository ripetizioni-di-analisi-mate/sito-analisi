A trapezoidal channel with side slope $m = 1$ has the most efficient hydraulic section and a water depth of $h = 1.50\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the bottom width?

- $0.621\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7.24\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For a trapezoidal section with a fixed side slope, minimizing the wetted perimeter gives
$$
b = 2h\left(\sqrt{1 + m^2} - m\right) .
$$
With $m = 1$ this gives $\sqrt{2} - 1 = 0.4142$, so
$$
b = 2 \cdot 1.50 \cdot 0.4142 = 1.24\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The bed is narrower than in the most efficient rectangular section, whose width at the same water depth is $2h = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$. The hydraulic radius is again $R = \dfrac{h}{2} = 0.750\,\mathrm{m}$, as in the rectangular case.
