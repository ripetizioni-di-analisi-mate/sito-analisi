A rectangular channel has the most efficient hydraulic section and a water depth of $h = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the hydraulic radius?

- $0.400\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.800\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.600\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For the most efficient rectangular section the bed width is $b = 2h$, so
$$
A = b\,h = 2h^2 ,
\qquad
C = b + 2h = 4h ,
$$
which gives
$$
R = \frac{A}{C} = \frac{2h^2}{4h} = \frac{h}{2} = \frac{1.20}{2} = 0.600\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The hydraulic radius also equals half the water depth in the most efficient trapezoidal section for a given side slope.
