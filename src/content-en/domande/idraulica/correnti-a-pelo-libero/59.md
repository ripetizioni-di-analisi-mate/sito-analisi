A channel carries uniform flow at a mean velocity of $v = 1.40\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and its Chézy coefficient is $\chi = 45.0\,\mathrm{m^{1/2}/s}$. What is the mean boundary shear stress?

- $0.968\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $9.50\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $305\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $4.75\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

In uniform flow the mean wall shear stress is $\tau_0 = \gamma\,R\,i$ and the energy gradient equals the bed slope. Rearranging the Chézy equation $v = \chi\sqrt{R\,i}$ gives $R\,i = \dfrac{v^2}{\chi^2}$. The product $R\,i$ can therefore be calculated without knowing the hydraulic radius and slope separately:
$$
\tau_0 = \gamma\,\frac{v^2}{\chi^2} = 9810 \cdot \frac{1.40^2}{45.0^2} = 9.50\,\mathrm{Pa} .
$$
The value $0.968\,\mathrm{Pa}$ uses density in place of specific weight. The value $305\,\mathrm{Pa}$ uses $\dfrac{v}{\chi}$ without squaring the terms. The value $4.75\,\mathrm{Pa}$ introduces a factor of $\dfrac{1}{2}$ as in the velocity head, although that factor does not appear in the Chézy equation.
