At a rectangular channel section, the water depth is $h = 0.500\,\mathrm{m}$ and the energy grade line lies $0.450\,\mathrm{m}$ above the free surface. What is the Froude number, and what is the flow regime?

- $Fr = 0.949$, subcritical flow
- $Fr = 1.80$, supercritical flow
- $Fr = 0.900$, subcritical flow
- $Fr = 1.34$, supercritical flow

## Explanation

The energy grade line lies one velocity head above the free surface, so $\dfrac{v^2}{2g} = 0.450\,\mathrm{m}$. In a rectangular section hydraulic depth equals water depth, giving
$$
Fr^2 = \frac{v^2}{g\,h} = \frac{2}{h}\,\frac{v^2}{2g} = \frac{2 \cdot 0.450}{0.500} = 1.80
$$
and $Fr = 1.34$. The Froude number exceeds $1$, so the flow is **supercritical**. The value $1.80$ is the square of the Froude number. The values $0.900$ and $0.949$ omit the factor $2$ relating $v^2$ to velocity head.
