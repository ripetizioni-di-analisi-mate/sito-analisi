For a rectangular channel with water depth $h = 1.50\,\mathrm{m}$, the wide-channel approximation $R = h$ is acceptable if the true hydraulic radius falls below $h$ by at most $10\%$ of $h$. What is the minimum channel width?

- $30.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $27.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $13.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For a rectangular section $R = \dfrac{b\,h}{b + 2h}$, so the condition $R \ge 0.9\,h$ is equivalent to
$$
\frac{b}{b + 2h} \ge 0.9 ,
$$
or $b \ge 0.9\,b + 1.8\,h$, giving $b \ge 18\,h = 27.0\,\mathrm{m}$. For a maximum error of $10\%$, the very-wide-channel approximation therefore requires a width of at least eighteen times the water depth. For a channel a few meters wide the hydraulic radius is appreciably smaller than $h$. The value $30.0\,\mathrm{m}$ measures the error relative to $R$ in place of $h$, and $13.5\,\mathrm{m}$ includes only one sidewall in the wetted perimeter.
