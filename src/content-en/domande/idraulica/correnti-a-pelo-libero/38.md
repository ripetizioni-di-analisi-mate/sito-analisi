A rectangular channel of width $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a discharge of $Q = 6.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ at a depth of $h = 0.500\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the flow regime?

- Subcritical, with $Fr = 0.738$
- Supercritical, with $Fr = 1.35$
- Subcritical, with $Fr = 0.958$
- Supercritical, with $Fr = 1.83$

## Explanation

The mean velocity is $v = \dfrac{6.00}{4.00 \cdot 0.500} = 3.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Since the section is rectangular, hydraulic depth equals water depth. Thus
$$
Fr = \frac{v}{\sqrt{g\,h}} = \frac{3.00}{\sqrt{9.81 \cdot 0.500}} = \frac{3.00}{2.215} = 1.35 ,
$$
the Froude number exceeds $1$ and the flow is **supercritical**. As a check, $q = 1.50\,\mathrm{m^2/s}$ gives $h_c = \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{2.25}{9.81}} = 0.612\,\mathrm{m}$. The water depth is below $h_c$, as expected for supercritical flow.
