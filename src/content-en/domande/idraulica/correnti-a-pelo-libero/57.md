A channel carries uniform flow on a bed slope of $i = 0.0004$ with mean velocity $v = 2.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Two cross sections are $L = 2.50\,\mathrm{km}$ apart. What is the head loss between them?

- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.204\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In uniform flow the energy gradient equals the bed slope, $J = i$, so the head loss over a reach of length $L$ is
$$
\Delta H = J\,L = i\,L = 0.0004 \cdot 2500 = 1.00\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The velocity heads at the two sections are equal and cancel in the energy balance. The total head therefore decreases by the same amount as the bed elevation. Equal velocities make the energy grade line parallel to the sloping bed, so the head loss is nonzero. Adding the velocity head $0.204\,\mathrm{m}$ to the loss would give $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$.
