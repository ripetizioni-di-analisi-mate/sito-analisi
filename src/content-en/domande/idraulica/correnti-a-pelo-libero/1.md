Water flows to a depth of $h = 1.10\,\mathrm{m}$ in a rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the hydraulic radius of the cross section?

- $0.402\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.550\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.635\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.10\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The wetted area of the rectangular cross section is $A = b\,h = 3.00 \cdot 1.10 = 3.30\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The wetted perimeter is the length of the boundary where water touches a solid surface. It includes the bed and both sidewalls, and excludes the free surface:
$$
C = b + 2h = 3.00 + 2 \cdot 1.10 = 5.20\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The hydraulic radius is the ratio of wetted area to wetted perimeter, so
$$
R = \frac{A}{C} = \frac{3.30}{5.20} = 0.635\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
