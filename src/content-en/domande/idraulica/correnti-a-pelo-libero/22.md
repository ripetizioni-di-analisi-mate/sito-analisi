In uniform flow a compound section is divided into the main channel and a floodplain. Their conveyances $K_i=k_{s,i}A_iR_i^{2/3}$, already evaluated by the subsection method, are $45$ and $15\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. With common slope $i=0.0016$, what total discharge is estimated?

- $1.2\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $0.096\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$

## Explanation

With common slope each subsection carries $Q_i=K_i\sqrt i$. Their sum is $(45+15)\cdot0.04=2.4\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.
