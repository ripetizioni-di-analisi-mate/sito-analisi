A rectangular channel of width $b = 1.50\,\mathrm{m}$, bed slope $i = 0.002$ and roughness coefficient $k_s = 50\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ carries uniform flow to a depth of $h_0 = 0.600\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the mean velocity?

- $0.801\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.07\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.29\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.59\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The cross-sectional dimensions give $A = 1.50 \cdot 0.600 = 0.900\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $C = 1.50 + 2 \cdot 0.600 = 2.70\,\mathrm{m}$, hence $R = 0.333\,\mathrm{m}$. In uniform flow the energy gradient equals the bed slope, so use $i$ in place of $J$ in the Gauckler–Strickler equation:
$$
v = k_s\,R^{\frac{2}{3}}\,i^{\frac{1}{2}} = 50 \cdot 0.333^{\frac{2}{3}} \cdot 0.002^{\frac{1}{2}} = 50 \cdot 0.4807 \cdot 0.04472 = 1.07\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The hydraulic radius is raised to the power $\dfrac{2}{3}$. The exponent $\dfrac{1}{2}$ belongs to the Chézy equation, which uses the coefficient $\chi$ in place of $k_s$.
