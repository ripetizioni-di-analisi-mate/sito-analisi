An earthen trapezoidal channel has bottom width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$, side slope $m = 2$ and design depth $h_0 = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$. The soil resists erosion as long as the mean boundary shear stress does not exceed $\tau_{\mathrm{allow}} = 3.50\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Assuming uniform flow, what is the greatest permissible bed slope?

- $3.57 \cdot 10^{-4}$
- $1.03 \cdot 10^{-3}$
- $5.33 \cdot 10^{-4}$
- $7.14 \cdot 10^{-5}$

## Explanation

In uniform flow the mean wall shear stress is $\tau_0 = \gamma\,R\,i$, so the condition $\tau_0 \le \tau_{\mathrm{allow}}$ is equivalent to $i \le \dfrac{\tau_{\mathrm{allow}}}{\gamma\,R}$. The cross-sectional properties are $A = (3.00 + 2 \cdot 1.00) \cdot 1.00 = 5.00\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $C = 3.00 + 2 \cdot 1.00\sqrt{5} = 7.47\,\mathrm{m}$, giving $R = 0.669\,\mathrm{m}$ and
$$
i_{\max} = \frac{3.50}{9810 \cdot 0.669} = 5.33 \cdot 10^{-4} .
$$
Using $R = h_0$ would give $3.57 \cdot 10^{-4}$. Including the free surface in the wetted perimeter would give $1.03 \cdot 10^{-3}$. The value $7.14 \cdot 10^{-5}$ uses flow area in place of hydraulic radius.
