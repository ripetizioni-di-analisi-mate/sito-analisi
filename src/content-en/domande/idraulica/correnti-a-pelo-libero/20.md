Water flows uniformly in a straight channel of constant cross section. How are the bed, free surface and energy grade line oriented relative to one another?

- They are three parallel lines
- The free surface coincides with the energy grade line
- The energy grade line is horizontal
- The free surface is less steep than the bed

## Explanation

In uniform flow the depth is constant, so the free surface stays a distance $h$ above and parallel to the bed. The flow area also stays constant, so continuity gives constant mean velocity $v = \dfrac{Q}{A}$ and velocity head $\dfrac{v^2}{2g}$. Thus the energy grade line is parallel to the water surface as well. The bed, water surface and energy grade line are **three parallel lines**, and the friction slope equals the bed slope, $J = i$. The energy grade line lies one velocity head above the free surface.
