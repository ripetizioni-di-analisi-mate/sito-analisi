A rectangular channel of width $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a discharge of $Q = 8.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ at a depth of $h = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the specific energy?

- $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.41\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.82\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Specific energy is the total head measured from the channel bed, equal to the water depth plus the velocity head. The mean velocity is
$$
v = \frac{Q}{b\,h} = \frac{8.00}{4.00 \cdot 1.00} = 2.00\,\mathrm{m/s} ,
$$
so the velocity head is $\dfrac{v^2}{2g} = \dfrac{4.00}{19.62} = 0.204\,\mathrm{m}$ and
$$
E = h + \frac{v^2}{2g} = 1.00 + 0.204 = 1.20\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The velocity head has $2g$ in the denominator. The value $1.41\,\mathrm{m}$ would result from using twice the actual velocity head.
