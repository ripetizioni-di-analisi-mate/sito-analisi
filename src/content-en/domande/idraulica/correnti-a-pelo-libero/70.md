A concrete flume has a symmetric triangular section with side slopes $m = 2$ and carries a discharge of $Q = 0.500\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. What is the critical depth?

- $0.364\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.418\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.480\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.117\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For a triangular section $A = m\,h^2$ and $B = 2\,m\,h$, so the critical-flow condition $Q^2 B = g A^3$ becomes
$$
Q^2 \cdot 2\,m\,h_c = g\,m^3 h_c^6 ,
$$
giving
$$
h_c = \left(\frac{2\,Q^2}{g\,m^2}\right)^{\frac{1}{5}} = \left(\frac{2 \cdot 0.250}{9.81 \cdot 4}\right)^{\frac{1}{5}} = 0.418\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Omitting the factor $2$ in the free-surface width would give $0.364\,\mathrm{m}$. Using $m$ in place of $m^2$ in the denominator would give $0.480\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $0.117\,\mathrm{m}$ applies the rectangular-channel formula with an assumed width of $2m$.
