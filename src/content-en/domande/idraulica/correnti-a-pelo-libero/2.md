A trapezoidal channel has bottom width $b = 2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and side slope $m = 2$, so each bank extends $2\,\mathrm{m}$ horizontally for every meter of vertical rise. The section shown has water depth $h = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its hydraulic depth?

::schema{nodi="S,-2.4,1.2;T,4.9,1.2" nomi=0 acqua="0,0 2.5,0 4.9,1.2 -2.4,1.2" solidi="-5,1.7 -3.4,1.7 0,0 2.5,0 5.9,1.7 7.5,1.7 7.5,-0.6 -5,-0.6" pelo="-2.4,4.9,1.2,p_a = 0" quote="2 0,2 1.2,1.20 m,0;0 -0.6,2.5 -0.6,2.50 m,0.45;S,T,B,-0.45" riferimento=0 titolo="Trapezoidal channel with $b = 2.50\,\mathrm{m}$, side slope $m = 2$ and $h = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$"}

- $0.805\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.747\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.35\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Hydraulic depth is the flow area divided by the top width, so both are needed. The trapezoidal flow area is
$$
A = (b + m\,h)\,h = (2.50 + 2 \cdot 1.20) \cdot 1.20 = 5.88\,\mathrm{m^2} ,
$$
and the top width is $B = b + 2m\,h = 2.50 + 4 \cdot 1.20 = 7.30\,\mathrm{m}$ because each bank extends horizontally by $m\,h$. Hence
$$
h_m = \frac{A}{B} = \frac{5.88}{7.30} = 0.805\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The inclined sides make the top wider than the bed, so hydraulic depth is smaller than water depth.
