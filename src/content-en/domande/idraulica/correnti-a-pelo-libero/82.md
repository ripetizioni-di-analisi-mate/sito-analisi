A rectangular channel has a specified width, bed slope and roughness coefficient $k_s$. Which statement about its critical slope is true?

- It depends on discharge, so the same channel can have a mild bed for one discharge and a steep bed for another
- It depends only on width and $k_s$, so the bed classification is fixed
- It equals the bed slope because uniform flow requires $J = i$
- It depends only on bed slope because critical depth is independent of roughness

## Explanation

The critical slope is $i_c = \dfrac{Q^2}{k_s^2\,A_c^2\,R_c^{\frac{4}{3}}}$, where $A_c$ and $R_c$ are evaluated at critical depth $h_c = \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{Q^2}{g\,b^2}}$, which increases with discharge. Thus $i_c$ depends on discharge, and changing discharge can change how the bed slope compares with $i_c$. Substitution of $Q^2 = g\,b^2 h_c^3$ gives $i_c = \dfrac{g\,h_c}{k_s^2\,R_c^{\frac{4}{3}}}$. For a wide channel, where $R_c \simeq h_c$, this becomes $i_c \simeq \dfrac{g}{k_s^2\,h_c^{\frac{1}{3}}}$ and decreases as discharge increases. The bed slope is a geometric property of the channel. The critical slope $i_c$ is the slope at which uniform flow would be critical.
