Of all rectangular sections with a given flow area, bed slope and roughness coefficient, one carries the greatest discharge in uniform flow. How are its bottom width and water depth related?

- $b = 2h$
- $b = h$
- $b = \dfrac{h}{2}$
- $b = 4h$

## Explanation

Substitution of $R = \dfrac{A}{C}$ in the Gauckler–Strickler equation gives $Q = k_s\,A^{\frac{5}{3}}\,C^{-\frac{2}{3}}\,i^{\frac{1}{2}}$. For a fixed flow area, discharge is greatest when the wetted perimeter is smallest. For a rectangular section $b = \dfrac{A}{h}$, giving
$$
C(h) = \frac{A}{h} + 2h ,
\qquad
C'(h) = -\frac{A}{h^2} + 2 ,
$$
and the derivative vanishes at $h = \sqrt{\dfrac{A}{2}}$, or $A = 2h^2$. This gives $b = \dfrac{A}{h} = 2h$ and a hydraulic radius of $R = \dfrac{2h^2}{4h} = \dfrac{h}{2}$. This design rule for lined rectangular channels minimizes the excavation area for a given discharge.
