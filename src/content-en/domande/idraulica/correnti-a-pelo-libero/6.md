A rectangular channel of width $b = 2.50\,\mathrm{m}$, bed slope $i = 0.0008$ and roughness coefficient $k_s = 70\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ must carry a discharge of $Q = 3.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ in uniform flow. What is the normal depth?

- $0.924\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.740\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.852\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.13\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Normal depth satisfies $Q(h_0) = Q$, or
$$
70 \cdot 2.50\,h_0 \left(\frac{2.50\,h_0}{2.50 + 2h_0}\right)^{\frac{2}{3}} \cdot 0.0283 = 3.00 ,
$$
This equation has no closed-form solution here. For an initial estimate, use the wide-channel approximation $R \simeq h$, giving $h = 0.740\,\mathrm{m}$. The true hydraulic radius is smaller than the water depth, so the true normal depth is greater than this estimate. Iteration gives $h_0 = 0.924\,\mathrm{m}$. Direct substitution gives $A = 2.31\,\mathrm{m^2}$, $R = 0.531\,\mathrm{m}$ and $Q = 3.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, confirming the depth.
