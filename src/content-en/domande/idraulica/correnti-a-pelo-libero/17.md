A trapezoidal channel has bottom width $b$, side slope $m > 0$ and water depth $h$. How does its hydraulic depth $h_m = \dfrac{A}{B}$ compare with its water depth?

- $h_m < h$
- $h_m = h$
- $h_m > h$
- $h_m = \dfrac{h}{2}$

## Explanation

For the trapezoidal section, $A = (b + m\,h)\,h$ and $B = b + 2m\,h$, giving
$$
h_m = \frac{(b + m\,h)\,h}{b + 2m\,h} .
$$
Dividing the inequality $h_m < h$ by $h > 0$ and multiplying by $b + 2m\,h > 0$ gives $b + m\,h < b + 2m\,h$, or $0 < m\,h$. This holds because $m$ and $h$ are positive. Thus the hydraulic depth of a trapezoidal section is always less than the water depth. The two depths coincide only in the rectangular case, where $m = 0$ and $B = b$.
