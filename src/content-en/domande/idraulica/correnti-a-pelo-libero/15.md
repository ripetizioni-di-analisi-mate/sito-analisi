The pressure distribution at an open-channel cross section is hydrostatic. Which line coincides with the free surface?

- The energy grade line
- The channel bed
- The hydrostatic head plane
- The hydraulic grade line

## Explanation

Gauge pressure is zero at the free surface because it is exposed to the atmosphere. With a hydrostatic pressure distribution across the section, a point at depth $a$ below the surface has pressure $p = \gamma\,a$ and elevation $z = z_f + h - a$. Its piezometric head is therefore
$$
z + \frac{p}{\gamma} = z_f + h - a + a = z_f + h ,
$$
which is the free-surface elevation. Every point across the section has this same piezometric head, so the **hydraulic grade line** coincides with the free surface. The energy grade line lies above it by the velocity head $\dfrac{v^2}{2g}$.
