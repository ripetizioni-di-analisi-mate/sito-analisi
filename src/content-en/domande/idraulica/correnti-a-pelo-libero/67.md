Four channel sections have the same flow area $A = 2.00\,\mathrm{m^2}$, bed slope and roughness coefficient $k_s$. Which carries the greatest uniform-flow discharge?

- The rectangular section with $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and $h = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- The rectangular section with $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and $h = 0.500\,\mathrm{m}$
- The trapezoidal section with $b = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$, side slope $m = 1$ and $h = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- The trapezoidal section with $b = 1.24\,\mathrm{m}$, banks inclined at $60^\circ$ to the horizontal and $h = 1.07\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For the same area, slope and $k_s$, the discharge $Q = k_s\,A^{\frac{5}{3}}\,C^{-\frac{2}{3}}\,i^{\frac{1}{2}}$ increases as wetted perimeter decreases. The wetted perimeters of the first three sections are
$$
C_1 = 2.00 + 2 \cdot 1.00 = 4.00\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
C_2 = 4.00 + 2 \cdot 0.500 = 5.00\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
C_3 = 1.00 + 2\sqrt{2} = 3.83\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
In the last section each bank has length $\dfrac{h}{\sin 60^\circ} = 1.24\,\mathrm{m}$, giving $C_4 = 3 \cdot 1.24 = 3.72\,\mathrm{m}$, the smallest of the four perimeters. This section is half a regular hexagon and is the most efficient of all trapezoidal sections. The rectangular section with $b = 2h$ is the most efficient rectangle, but has a larger wetted perimeter.
