In a stream reach carrying uniform flow, a survey gives flow area $6.00\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and wetted perimeter $18.0\,\mathrm{m}$. The bed slope is $i = 0.0012$, and previous measurements give a Chézy coefficient of $\chi = 50\,\mathrm{m^{1/2}/s}$. What is the mean velocity?

- $3.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.577\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.833\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Hydraulic radius equals flow area divided by wetted perimeter, $R = \dfrac{6.00}{18.0} = 0.333\,\mathrm{m}$. In uniform flow the energy gradient equals the bed slope, $J = i$, so the Chézy equation gives
$$
v = \chi\sqrt{R\,i} = 50\sqrt{0.333 \cdot 0.0012} = 50\sqrt{0.000400} = 50 \cdot 0.0200 = 1.00\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $3.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$ uses the inverse ratio $\dfrac{C}{A}$ for hydraulic radius. The value $0.833\,\mathrm{m/s}$ uses $R^{\frac{2}{3}}$ in place of $\sqrt{R}$, mixing the Chézy and Gauckler–Strickler equations. The value $0.577\,\mathrm{m/s}$ multiplies by $R$ in place of $\sqrt{R}$.
