A trapezoidal channel with bottom width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$, side slope $m = 2$ and water depth $h = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 7.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. What is the mean velocity?

- $1.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.75\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.33\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.40\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

By continuity the mean velocity equals the discharge divided by the flow area. For the trapezoid, add the area of the rectangle of width $b$ to those of the two side triangles:
$$
A = (b + m\,h)\,h = (3.00 + 2 \cdot 1.00) \cdot 1.00 = 5.00\,\mathrm{m^2} .
$$
This gives
$$
v = \frac{Q}{A} = \frac{7.00}{5.00} = 1.40\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The same area follows from the mean of the two parallel sides of the trapezoid, since $\dfrac{b + B}{2} = b + m\,h = 5.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and the depth is $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$.
