A rectangular channel of width $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$, roughness coefficient $k_s = 75\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ and bed slope $i = 0.006$ carries a discharge of $Q = 10.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. What is the critical slope?

- $0.00183$
- $0.00211$
- $0.00295$
- $0.00600$

## Explanation

The discharge per unit width is $q = 2.50\,\mathrm{m^2/s}$, so the critical depth is $h_c = \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{q^2}{g}} = 0.860\,\mathrm{m}$. At critical depth the section properties are
$$
A_c = 3.442\,\mathrm{m^2} ,
\qquad
C_c = 5.721\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
R_c = 0.6016\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
v_c = 2.905\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Critical slope is the bed slope that produces uniform flow at critical depth. Evaluate the Gauckler–Strickler equation at that depth:
$$
i_c = \frac{v_c^2}{k_s^2\,R_c^{\frac{4}{3}}} = \frac{8.439}{5625 \cdot 0.5081} = 0.00295 .
$$
Since the bed slope $i = 0.006$ exceeds $i_c$, the bed is steep.
