A rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and bed slope $i = 0.002$ carries uniform flow to a depth of $h = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the mean boundary shear stress?

- $7.36\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $9.81\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $11.8\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $19.6\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

In uniform flow the downslope component of the weight balances the resistance at the wall, so $\tau_0 = \gamma\,R\,i$. The hydraulic radius of the section is
$$
R = \frac{A}{C} = \frac{3.00 \cdot 1.00}{3.00 + 2 \cdot 1.00} = \frac{3.00}{5.00} = 0.600\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
giving
$$
\tau_0 = 9810 \cdot 0.600 \cdot 0.002 = 11.8\,\mathrm{Pa} .
$$
The wetted perimeter includes both sidewalls as well as the bed, making the hydraulic radius smaller than the water depth.
