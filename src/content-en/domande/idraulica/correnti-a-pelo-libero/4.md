A rectangular channel of width $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$, bed slope $i = 0.0015$ and roughness coefficient $k_s = 65\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ carries uniform flow at a depth of $h_0 = 0.900\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the discharge?

- $2.08\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $3.12\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $4.22\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $2.75\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$

## Explanation

The section geometry gives $A = 2.00 \cdot 0.900 = 1.80\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $C = 2.00 + 2 \cdot 0.900 = 3.80\,\mathrm{m}$, hence $R = 0.474\,\mathrm{m}$. In uniform flow the friction slope equals the bed slope, so the Gauckler–Strickler equation uses $i$ in place of $J$:
$$
v = k_s\,R^{\frac{2}{3}}\,i^{\frac{1}{2}} = 65 \cdot 0.474^{\frac{2}{3}} \cdot 0.0015^{\frac{1}{2}} = 65 \cdot 0.608 \cdot 0.0387 = 1.53\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Continuity gives the discharge:
$$
Q = A\,v = 1.80 \cdot 1.53 = 2.75\,\mathrm{m^3/s} .
$$
The wetted perimeter includes both sidewalls as well as the bed, making the hydraulic radius smaller than the water depth.
