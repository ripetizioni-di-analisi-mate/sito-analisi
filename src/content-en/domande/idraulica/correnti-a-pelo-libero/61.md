A drainage ditch has a symmetric triangular cross section with side slopes $m = 1.5$ as shown, bed slope $i = 0.002$ and roughness coefficient $k_s = 40\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$. What is the normal depth when the ditch carries $0.300\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$?

::schema{nomi=0 acqua="-0.9,0.6 0,0 0.9,0.6" solidi="-1.5,1.0 0,0 1.5,1.0 1.5,-0.3 -1.5,-0.3" pelo="-0.9,0.9,0.6" quote="0 0,0 0.6,h_0,0" etichette="1.3,1.2,m = 1.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Triangular drainage ditch with side slope $m = 1.5$"}

- $0.440\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.844\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.381\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.548\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In a triangular section the geometric properties are powers of $h$ multiplied by constants:
$$
A = m\,h^2 ,
\qquad
C = 2h\sqrt{1 + m^2} ,
\qquad
R = \frac{m\,h}{2\sqrt{1 + m^2}} = 0.416\,h .
$$
Substitution in the Gauckler–Strickler equation gives
$$
Q = k_s\,m\,h^2\,(0.416\,h)^{\frac{2}{3}}\,i^{\frac{1}{2}} = 40 \cdot 1.5 \cdot 0.557 \cdot 0.0447\,h^{\frac{8}{3}} = 1.495\,h^{\frac{8}{3}} ,
$$
so normal depth follows directly:
$$
h_0 = \left(\frac{0.300}{1.495}\right)^{\frac{3}{8}} = 0.548\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Using $R = h$ would give $0.440\,\mathrm{m}$, although the hydraulic radius in a triangular channel is much smaller than the water depth. The value $0.381\,\mathrm{m}$ uses the exponent $\dfrac{3}{5}$ for a very wide rectangular channel.
