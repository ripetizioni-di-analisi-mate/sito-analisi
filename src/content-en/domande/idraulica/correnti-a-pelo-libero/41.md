The boundary of a channel has Gauckler–Strickler coefficient $k_s = 70\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$, and the hydraulic radius is $R = 0.640\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the Chézy coefficient?

- $52.0\,\mathrm{m^{1/2}/s}$
- $56.0\,\mathrm{m^{1/2}/s}$
- $65.0\,\mathrm{m^{1/2}/s}$
- $70.0\,\mathrm{m^{1/2}/s}$

## Explanation

The Chézy equation gives the mean velocity as $v = \chi\sqrt{R\,J}$. Setting $\chi = k_s\,R^{\frac{1}{6}}$ gives the Gauckler–Strickler equation. Substituting the data gives
$$
\chi = 70 \cdot 0.640^{\frac{1}{6}} = 70 \cdot 0.9283 = 65.0\,\mathrm{m^{1/2}/s} .
$$
The Chézy coefficient depends on both the boundary and the cross section, whereas $k_s$ depends only on the boundary. The exponent $\dfrac{1}{6}$ converts the Chézy equation to the uniform-flow power law $v = k_s\,R^{\frac{2}{3}}\,i^{\frac{1}{2}}$ because $\dfrac{1}{6} + \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{2}{3}$.
