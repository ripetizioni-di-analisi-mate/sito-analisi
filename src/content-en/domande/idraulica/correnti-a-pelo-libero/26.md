A rectangular channel of width $b = 6.00\,\mathrm{m}$, bed slope $i = 0.001$ and roughness coefficient $k_s = 70\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ must carry $Q = 9.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. What initial estimate of normal depth does the wide-channel approximation give if the hydraulic radius is set equal to the water depth?

- $0.523\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.792\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.678\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.26\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

With $R \simeq h$ the Gauckler–Strickler equation becomes $Q = k_s\,b\,h\,h^{\frac{2}{3}}\,i^{\frac{1}{2}}$, or $Q = k_s\,b\,h^{\frac{5}{3}}\,i^{\frac{1}{2}}$, giving an explicit expression for depth:
$$
h = \left(\frac{Q}{k_s\,b\,i^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right)^{\frac{3}{5}} .
$$
The ratio $\dfrac{Q}{k_s\,b\,i^{\frac{1}{2}}}$ is
$$
\frac{9.00}{70 \cdot 6.00 \cdot 0.03162} = 0.678 ,
$$
so $h = 0.678^{\frac{3}{5}} = 0.792\,\mathrm{m}$. The estimate is low because the actual hydraulic radius is smaller than the water depth. A greater depth is therefore needed to carry the specified discharge.
