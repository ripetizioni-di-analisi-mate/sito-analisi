A rectangular channel of width $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a discharge of $Q = 5.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ at a depth of $h = 0.800\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the Froude number?

- $1.12$
- $0.398$
- $0.789$
- $1.24$

## Explanation

For a rectangular section, hydraulic depth equals water depth because $A = b\,h$ and $B = b$. The Froude number is therefore $Fr = \dfrac{v}{\sqrt{g\,h}}$. The mean velocity is
$$
v = \frac{5.00}{2.00 \cdot 0.800} = 3.125\,\mathrm{m/s} ,
$$
and the square root in the denominator is $\sqrt{9.81 \cdot 0.800} = 2.802\,\mathrm{m/s}$. This gives
$$
Fr = \frac{3.125}{2.802} = 1.12 ,
$$
so the flow is supercritical. The product under the square root is $g\,h$. The product $2g\,h$ belongs to the velocity-head formula.
