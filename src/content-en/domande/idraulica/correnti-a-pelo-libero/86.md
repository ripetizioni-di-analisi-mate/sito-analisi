In a rectangular channel, the critical depth for the discharge is $h_c = 0.900\,\mathrm{m}$ and the water depth at a section is $h = 0.600\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the Froude number there?

- $1.84$
- $1.50$
- $2.25$
- $1.22$

## Explanation

In a rectangular channel hydraulic depth equals water depth, so $Fr^2 = \dfrac{v^2}{g\,h} = \dfrac{q^2}{g\,h^3}$, with $q$ denoting discharge per unit width. The critical-depth formula gives $q^2 = g\,h_c^3$, hence
$$
Fr^2 = \frac{g\,h_c^3}{g\,h^3} = \left(\frac{h_c}{h}\right)^3 ,
\qquad
Fr = \left(\frac{0.900}{0.600}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}} = 1.5^{\frac{3}{2}} = 1.84 ,
$$
and the flow is supercritical, as expected because $h < h_c$. The value $1.50$ sets the Froude number equal to the ratio $\dfrac{h_c}{h}$. The value $1.22$ takes the square root of the ratio and $2.25$ takes its square.
