A circular sewer of diameter $D = 0.800\,\mathrm{m}$ and bed slope $i = 0.004$ has Manning coefficient $n = 0.013\,\mathrm{s/m^{1/3}}$. What is its uniform-flow discharge when flowing full?

- $1.33\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $0.836\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $3.35\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $0.527\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$

## Explanation

The Gauckler–Strickler coefficient is $k_s = \dfrac{1}{n} = 76.9\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$. For the full pipe the area is $A = \dfrac{\pi D^2}{4} = 0.503\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the hydraulic radius is $R = \dfrac{D}{4} = 0.200\,\mathrm{m}$, so
$$
Q = k_s\,A\,R^{\frac{2}{3}}\,i^{\frac{1}{2}} = 76.9 \cdot 0.503 \cdot 0.342 \cdot 0.0632 = 0.836\,\mathrm{m^3/s} ,
$$
with mean velocity $1.66\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Using the geometric radius $\dfrac{D}{2}$ in place of the hydraulic radius would give $1.33\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, while using $\dfrac{D}{8}$ would give $0.527\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The value $3.35\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ uses the area $\pi D^2$, omitting the factor $\dfrac{1}{4}$.
