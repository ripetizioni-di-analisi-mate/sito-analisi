A roadside ditch has a symmetric triangular cross section with side slopes $m = 3$, corresponding to $1$ horizontally for $3$ vertically. The water depth is $h = 0.120\,\mathrm{m}$, as shown. What is the hydraulic depth?

::schema{nomi=0 acqua="-0.36,0.12 0,0 0.36,0.12" solidi="-0.6,0.2 0,0 0.6,0.2 0.6,-0.1 -0.6,-0.1" pelo="-0.36,0.36,0.12" quote="0 0,0 0.12,0.12 m,0" etichette="0.5,0.26,m = 3" riferimento=0 titolo="Triangular ditch with side slope $m = 3$ and water depth $h = 0.120\,\mathrm{m}$"}

- $0.120\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.0400\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.0600\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.0300\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Each bank extends $m\,h$ horizontally, giving a free-surface width of $B = 2\,m\,h = 0.720\,\mathrm{m}$. The flow area is that of a triangle of base $B$ and height $h$, namely $A = \dfrac{B\,h}{2} = m\,h^2 = 0.0432\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Thus
$$
h_m = \frac{A}{B} = \frac{m\,h^2}{2\,m\,h} = \frac{h}{2} = 0.0600\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
and in a triangular channel the hydraulic depth is half the water depth for any side slope. The value $0.0300\,\mathrm{m}$ counts only one of the two triangles on either side of the centerline. The value $0.0400\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance from the free surface to the centroid of the section.
