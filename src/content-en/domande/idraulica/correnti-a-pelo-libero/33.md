A trapezoidal channel with bottom width $b = 1.50\,\mathrm{m}$, side slope $m = 2$ and water depth $h = 0.800\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 6.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. What is the Froude number?

- $0.467$
- $0.864$
- $1.06$
- $3.16$

## Explanation

The Froude number uses the hydraulic depth $h_m = \dfrac{A}{B}$, which in this nonrectangular section is less than the water depth. The cross-sectional properties are
$$
A = (1.50 + 2 \cdot 0.800) \cdot 0.800 = 2.48\,\mathrm{m^2} ,
\qquad
B = 1.50 + 4 \cdot 0.800 = 4.70\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
h_m = 0.528\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
so the mean velocity is $v = \dfrac{6.00}{2.48} = 2.42\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and
$$
Fr = \frac{v}{\sqrt{g\,h_m}} = \frac{2.42}{\sqrt{9.81 \cdot 0.528}} = \frac{2.42}{2.275} = 1.06 ,
$$
and the flow is supercritical. Using water depth in place of hydraulic depth would give $0.864$ and misclassify the flow.
