A rectangular channel of width $b = 5.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a discharge of $Q = 12.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. What is the minimum specific energy?

- $0.837\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.67\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.67\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.26\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The discharge per unit width is $q = \dfrac{Q}{b} = \dfrac{12.0}{5.00} = 2.40\,\mathrm{m^2/s}$, so the critical depth in the rectangular channel is
$$
h_c = \sqrt[3]{\frac{q^2}{g}} = \sqrt[3]{\frac{5.76}{9.81}} = 0.837\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Specific energy is a minimum at critical flow. At that point the velocity head is half the water depth, so
$$
E_{\min} = \frac{3}{2}\,h_c = \frac{3}{2} \cdot 0.837 = 1.26\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
