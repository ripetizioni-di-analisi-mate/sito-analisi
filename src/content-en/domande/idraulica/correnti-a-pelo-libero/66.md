A lined rectangular channel is designed with bottom width twice its water depth, $b = 2h$. For a given discharge, bed slope and roughness coefficient $k_s$, which quantity does this choice minimize among all rectangular sections?

- The total construction cost, including the lining
- The mean flow velocity
- The flow area, which equals the excavation volume per meter of channel
- The bank height

## Explanation

The Gauckler–Strickler equation, written as $Q = k_s\,A^{\frac{5}{3}}\,C^{-\frac{2}{3}}\,i^{\frac{1}{2}}$, shows that a smaller wetted perimeter permits a smaller flow area for a fixed discharge. The rectangular section with $b = 2h$ has the smallest wetted perimeter for its area, and hence the **smallest flow area** for a given discharge. With minimum area and fixed discharge, the velocity $v = \dfrac{Q}{A}$ is the highest among rectangular sections. Construction cost also depends on lining and wall height. A wider section has lower walls, so the most hydraulically efficient section may cost more than another design.
