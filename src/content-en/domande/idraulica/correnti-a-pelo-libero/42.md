On a steep bed, the bed slope exceeds the critical slope. What is the uniform-flow regime?

- Subcritical, because $h_0 > h_c$
- Supercritical, because $h_0 < h_c$
- Supercritical, because $h_0 > h_c$
- Critical, because $h_0 = h_c$

## Explanation

The critical slope makes normal depth equal to critical depth. The rating curve $Q(h)$ increases with depth. On a steeper bed the same discharge can pass through a smaller area and at a smaller depth. Thus $i > i_c$ implies $h_0 < h_c$, and a depth below critical identifies **supercritical** flow. Since critical depth depends on discharge, a given channel can be mild at one discharge and steep at another.
