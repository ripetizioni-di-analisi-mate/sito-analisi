The graph gives the specific-energy curve for a rectangular channel at a fixed discharge. Point $P$ represents subcritical flow. Which point represents its alternate depth?

::grafico{curve="t+0.203874/t^2,t,0.25,2.35" xmin=0 xmax=2.6 ymin=0 ymax=2.4 nomi="E,h" punti="1.6,1.5107,pieno,P;2.2,0.3302,pieno,A;1.6,0.4147,pieno,B;1.1123,0.7415,pieno,C;2.2,2.1561,pieno,D" titolo="Specific-energy curve $E(h)$ for a fixed discharge in a rectangular channel"}

- Point $A$
- Point $B$
- Point $C$
- Point $D$

## Explanation

Alternate depths carry the same discharge at the same specific energy, one on each branch of the curve. To find the alternate depth for $P$, follow the vertical through $P$ at the same horizontal coordinate $E$ to the lower, supercritical branch. This gives point $B$. Point $A$ is also on the supercritical branch but has higher specific energy than $P$. Point $C$ is at the minimum of the curve, the critical state where the alternate depths coincide. Point $D$ represents subcritical flow at a greater specific energy.
