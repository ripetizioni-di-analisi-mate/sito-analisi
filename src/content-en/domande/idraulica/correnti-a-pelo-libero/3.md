A circular drain of diameter $D = 0.800\,\mathrm{m}$ carries water to a depth of $h = 0.300\,\mathrm{m}$, as shown in the cross section. What is the hydraulic radius?

- $0.200\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.163\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.0941\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.400\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The central angle subtended by the free surface follows from $\cos\dfrac{\theta}{2} = 1 - \dfrac{2h}{D} = 1 - 0.750 = 0.250$, giving $\theta = 2.636\,\mathrm{rad}$. Substitute into the formulas for a partly full circular section:
$$
A = \frac{D^2}{8}\,(\theta - \sin\theta) = \frac{0.640}{8}\,(2.636 - 0.484) = 0.172\,\mathrm{m^2} ,
\qquad
C = \frac{D\,\theta}{2} = 1.054\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
Here the wetted perimeter is only the wall arc in contact with water. Therefore
$$
R = \frac{A}{C} = \frac{0.172}{1.054} = 0.163\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{D}{4} = 0.200\,\mathrm{m}$ is the hydraulic radius of a full circular pipe, for which the entire circumference is wetted.
