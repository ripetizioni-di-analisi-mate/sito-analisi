A trapezoidal channel carries $Q$ at critical depth. Let $A$ denote the flow area, $B$ the top width, $b$ the bottom width and $h$ the water depth. Which relation holds at this section?

- $Q^2 B = g\,A^3$
- $Q^2 = g\,b^2 h^3$
- $Q^2 = g\,A^2 h$
- $Q^2 A = g\,B^3$

## Explanation

Critical flow occurs at the minimum of specific energy, where $\dfrac{dE}{dh} = 1 - \dfrac{Q^2 B}{g A^3} = 0$. For any section $Q^2 B = g A^3$, or $Fr = 1$ using the hydraulic depth $h_m = \dfrac{A}{B}$. The equality $Q^2 = g\,b^2 h^3$ expresses the condition for a rectangular section, where $A = b\,h$ and $B = b$. It fails in the trapezoid because $B$ is greater than $b$. The equality $Q^2 = g A^2 h$ corresponds to $Fr = 1$ calculated using water depth in place of hydraulic depth. The last expression is dimensionally inconsistent: the left side has units of $\mathrm{m^8/s^2}$ and the right side has units of $\mathrm{m^4/s^2}$.
