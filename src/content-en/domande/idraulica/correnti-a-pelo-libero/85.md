The bed slope of a rectangular channel equals the critical slope for its discharge. The normal depth is $h_0 = 0.800\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the specific energy?

- $0.800\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.533\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

When bed slope equals critical slope, normal depth equals critical depth, so $h_0 = h_c$ and the flow is critical at every section. In a rectangular section at critical flow, the velocity head is half the water depth, giving
$$
E = h_c + \frac{h_c}{2} = \frac{3}{2}\,h_c = \frac{3}{2} \cdot 0.800 = 1.20\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $1.60\,\mathrm{m}$ sets the velocity head equal to the water depth, using $\dfrac{v^2}{g}$ in place of $\dfrac{v^2}{2g}$. The value $0.533\,\mathrm{m}$ inverts the relation $E_{\min} = \dfrac{3}{2}\,h_c$.
