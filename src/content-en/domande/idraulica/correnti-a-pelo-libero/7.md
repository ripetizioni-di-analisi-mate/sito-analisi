A trapezoidal channel has bottom width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$, side slope $m = 1$, bed slope $i = 0.0006$ and roughness coefficient $k_s = 50\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$. What is the normal depth for a discharge of $Q = 6.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$?

- $1.21\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.29\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.07\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For the trapezoidal section with side slope $m = 1$, the geometric relations are $A(h) = (3.00 + h)\,h$ and $C(h) = 3.00 + 2h\sqrt{2}$. The uniform-flow equation is therefore
$$
50\,A(h)\left(\frac{A(h)}{C(h)}\right)^{\frac{2}{3}} \cdot 0.0245 = 6.00
$$
Solve by iteration. At $h = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ the calculated discharge is $5.27\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, below the required value, so increase the depth. At $h = 1.29\,\mathrm{m}$ the discharge is $6.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Thus $h_0 = 1.29\,\mathrm{m}$, giving $A = 5.54\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $v = 1.08\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
