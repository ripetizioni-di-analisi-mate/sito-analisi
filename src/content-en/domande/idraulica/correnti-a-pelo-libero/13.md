A lined rectangular channel must carry $Q = 3.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ in uniform flow on a bed slope of $i = 0.0012$ with roughness coefficient $k_s = 60\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$. The design uses the most efficient hydraulic section. What is the water depth?

- $0.885\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.05\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.51\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.10\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For the most efficient rectangular section, $b = 2h$ and $R = \dfrac{h}{2}$. The flow area is therefore $A = 2h^2$, and the Gauckler–Strickler equation contains only one unknown, the water depth:
$$
60 \cdot 2h^2 \left(\frac{h}{2}\right)^{\frac{2}{3}} \cdot 0.0346 = 3.00 .
$$
Collecting the constants gives $h^{\frac{8}{3}} = 1.146$, so
$$
h = 1.146^{\frac{3}{8}} = 1.05\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The bottom width is then $b = 2h = 2.10\,\mathrm{m}$ and the mean velocity is $v = 1.35\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
