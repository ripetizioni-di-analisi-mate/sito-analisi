At a channel section, the bed lies $0.500\,\mathrm{m}$ above the elevation datum, the water depth is $h = 0.800\,\mathrm{m}$ and the mean velocity is $v = 2.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the total head relative to that datum?

- $1.62\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.94\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Total head in open-channel flow equals bed elevation plus specific energy, $H = z_f + E$. The specific energy is
$$
E = h + \frac{v^2}{2g} = 0.800 + \frac{2.50^2}{2 \cdot 9.81} = 0.800 + 0.319 = 1.12\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
so $H = 0.500 + 1.12 = 1.62\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $1.12\,\mathrm{m}$ is specific energy, the head measured from the bed. The value $1.30\,\mathrm{m}$ is the free-surface elevation, or piezometric head. The value $1.94\,\mathrm{m}$ uses $\dfrac{v^2}{g}$ in place of $\dfrac{v^2}{2g}$.
