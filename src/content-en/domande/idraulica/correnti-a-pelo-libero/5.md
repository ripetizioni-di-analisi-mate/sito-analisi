An earthen trapezoidal channel has bottom width $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$, side slope $m = 1.5$, bed slope $i = 0.0005$ and roughness coefficient $k_s = 40\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$. Its normal depth is $h_0 = 1.50\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the discharge?

- $10.2\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $8.37\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $3.98\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $8.95\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$

## Explanation

The flow area is $A = (4.00 + 1.5 \cdot 1.50) \cdot 1.50 = 9.38\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The wetted perimeter includes the bed and both sloping banks, each of length $h\sqrt{1 + m^2}$, so
$$
C = b + 2h\sqrt{1 + m^2} = 4.00 + 2 \cdot 1.50 \cdot 1.803 = 9.41\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
R = \frac{9.38}{9.41} = 0.996\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The Gauckler–Strickler equation for uniform flow then gives
$$
Q = k_s\,A\,R^{\frac{2}{3}}\,i^{\frac{1}{2}} = 40 \cdot 9.38 \cdot 0.996^{\frac{2}{3}} \cdot 0.02236 = 8.37\,\mathrm{m^3/s} ,
$$
with mean velocity $v = 0.892\,\mathrm{m/s}$, a typical value for an earthen channel of this slope.
