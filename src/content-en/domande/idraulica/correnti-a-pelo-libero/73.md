A circular drain of diameter $D = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ runs half full with the water surface level with the center. If the discharge is $Q = 0.600\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, what is the Froude number?

- $0.690$
- $0.488$
- $0.778$
- $0.389$

## Explanation

In a half-full conduit the flow area is half the circular area and the free-surface width equals the diameter, so
$$
A = \frac{\pi D^2}{8} = 0.393\,\mathrm{m^2} ,
\qquad
B = D = 1.00\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
h_m = \frac{A}{B} = 0.393\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The mean velocity is $v = \dfrac{0.600}{0.393} = 1.53\,\mathrm{m/s}$, giving
$$
Fr = \frac{v}{\sqrt{g\,h_m}} = \frac{1.53}{\sqrt{9.81 \cdot 0.393}} = \frac{1.53}{1.96} = 0.778 ,
$$
and the flow is subcritical. Using the water depth $0.500\,\mathrm{m}$ in place of the hydraulic depth would give $0.690$, and using the diameter would give $0.488$. The value $0.389$ uses the entire circular area.
