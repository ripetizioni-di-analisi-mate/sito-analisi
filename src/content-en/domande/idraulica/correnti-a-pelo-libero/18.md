A circular drain of diameter $D = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ carries water to a depth of $h = 0.900\,\mathrm{m}$. What angle does the water surface subtend at the center?

- $2.09\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $4.19\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $4.71\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $3.14\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

Find the central angle from $\cos\dfrac{\theta}{2} = 1 - \dfrac{2h}{D}$:
$$
\cos\frac{\theta}{2} = 1 - \frac{2 \cdot 0.900}{1.20} = 1 - 1.50 = -0.500 .
$$
The cosine is negative, so the half-angle is greater than a right angle and equals $\dfrac{\theta}{2} = 2.09\,\mathrm{rad}$. Thus
$$
\theta = 4.19\,\mathrm{rad} = 240^\circ .
$$
This agrees with the degree of filling. Since $h > \dfrac{D}{2}$, the angle must exceed $\pi$.
