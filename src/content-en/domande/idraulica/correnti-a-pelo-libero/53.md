A river reach of length $L = 1.00\,\mathrm{km}$ carries uniform flow. The flow area is $A = 40.0\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the bed slope is $i = 0.0004$. What is the magnitude of the total streamwise force exerted by the channel boundary on the water in the reach?

- $157\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $16.0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $3.92 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

In uniform flow, the end sections have the same area and velocity, so the net change in momentum flux is zero. Their pressure forces have equal magnitudes and opposite directions. The channel boundary force therefore balances the component of weight along the flow, which for a small slope is $\gamma\,A\,L\,i$:
$$
F = \gamma\,A\,L\,i = 9.81 \cdot 40.0 \cdot 1000 \cdot 0.0004 = 157\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
The force acts upstream. Its calculation does not require the wetted perimeter because the total force is $\tau_0\,C\,L$ and $\tau_0 = \gamma\,R\,i = \gamma\,\dfrac{A}{C}\,i$. The value $16.0\,\mathrm{kN}$ uses density in place of specific weight, and $3.92 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the weight of all the water in the reach. A zero result neglects the streamwise weight component, which the boundary force balances in uniform flow.
