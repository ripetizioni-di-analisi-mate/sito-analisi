A rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a discharge of $Q = 6.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ at a depth of $h = 1.40\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the alternate depth?

- $0.437\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.742\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.468\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.104\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The mean velocity is $v = \dfrac{6.00}{3.00 \cdot 1.40} = 1.43\,\mathrm{m/s}$, giving a specific energy of
$$
E = h + \frac{v^2}{2g} = 1.40 + 0.104 = 1.504\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The alternate depth is the other positive solution of $E(h) = E$. In the rectangular channel with $q = 2.00\,\mathrm{m^2/s}$, the equation becomes
$$
h^3 - 1.504\,h^2 + \frac{q^2}{2g} = h^3 - 1.504\,h^2 + 0.204 = 0 .
$$
The positive roots are $1.40\,\mathrm{m}$, the given depth, and $0.437\,\mathrm{m}$. Since $h_c = 0.742\,\mathrm{m}$, the alternate depth is less than critical depth and corresponds to supercritical flow.
