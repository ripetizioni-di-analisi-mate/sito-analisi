Air flows steadily through a duct of varying area, with density increasing downstream. Which quantity has the same value at every section?

- Volumetric flow rate $A\,v$
- Mass flow rate $\rho\,A\,v$
- Mean speed $v$
- The product $\rho\,v$

## Explanation

The flow is steady and the walls are impermeable, so the mass between any two sections is constant. The mass entering per unit time therefore equals the mass leaving,
$$
\rho_1 A_1 v_1 = \rho_2 A_2 v_2 .
$$
The conserved quantity is the mass flow rate $Q_m = \rho\,A\,v$. The volumetric flow rate $A\,v$ changes from section to section because a kilogram of air occupies a smaller volume where density is higher. The product $\rho\,v$ also changes because the area varies, and the mean velocity depends on both area and density.
