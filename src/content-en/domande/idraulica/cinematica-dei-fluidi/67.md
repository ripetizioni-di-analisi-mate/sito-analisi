A system supplies $20\,000$ people with an average of $250$ liters per person per day. The entire average daily flow passes through a supply pipe of diameter $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the mean speed?

- $0.819\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.205\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.27\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8.19\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The volume supplied in one day is $20\,000 \cdot 250\,\mathrm{L} = 5.00 \cdot 10^{6}\,\mathrm{L}$, or $5000\,\mathrm{m^3}$. A day contains $86\,400\,\mathrm{s}$, so the mean discharge is
$$
Q = \frac{5000}{86\,400}\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 0.0579\,\mathrm{m^3/s} ,
$$
equivalent to $57.9\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The pipe area is $A = \dfrac{\pi \cdot 0.300^2}{4} = 0.0707\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so
$$
v = \frac{Q}{A} = \frac{0.0579}{0.0707}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 0.819\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Using $\pi D^2$ for the area gives $0.205\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Substituting the radius for the diameter in $\dfrac{\pi D^2}{4}$ gives $3.27\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Writing the discharge as $0.579\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ introduces a factor of $10$ in the conversion from liters and gives $8.19\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
