A rectangular channel of width $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$, depth $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$, and mean speed $0.800\,\mathrm{m/s}$ receives a tributary carrying $1.20\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Below the confluence the width is $4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and depth $1.50\,\mathrm{m}$. For steady flow, what is the downstream mean speed?

- $0.680\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.480\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.13\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.800\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The main channel carries $Q_1 = 3.00 \cdot 1.20 \cdot 0.800 = 2.88\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ upstream of the confluence. Two streams enter the junction and one leaves, so continuity gives the downstream discharge as
$$
Q_3 = Q_1 + Q_2 = 2.88 + 1.20\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 4.08\,\mathrm{m^3/s} .
$$
The downstream area is $4.00 \cdot 1.50 = 6.00\,\mathrm{m^2}$, giving $v_3 = \dfrac{4.08}{6.00} = 0.680\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Omitting the tributary would give $0.480\,\mathrm{m/s}$, while dividing $Q_3$ by the upstream area $3.60\,\mathrm{m^2}$ would give $1.13\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Discharges add at the confluence, while mean velocity changes with the flow area.
