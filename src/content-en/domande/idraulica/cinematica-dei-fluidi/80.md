For which of the following flows does using $\alpha = 1$ in the velocity head introduce the largest error?

- Water in a main of diameter $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ with $Re = 4 \cdot 10^5$
- Water in a wide, regular rectangular channel
- Oil in a tube of diameter $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ with $Re = 800$
- Water in a penstock with $Re = 2 \cdot 10^6$

## Explanation

For $Re = 800$ the oil flow is laminar because $Re < 2000$, so the pipe velocity profile is parabolic and the correction factor is $\alpha = 2$. Using $\alpha = 1$ would therefore give only half the actual velocity head. The other three flows are turbulent and have flatter profiles. In pipes, measured values of $\alpha$ range from $1.03$ to $1.10$. The factor is of the same order in a wide, regular channel. The approximation gives an error of only a few percent in those cases.
