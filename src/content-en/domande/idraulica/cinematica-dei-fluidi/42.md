A measurement in a circular water pipe gives $Re = 2600$. Which statement about the flow regime is correct?

- Stable laminar flow, because $Re$ is less than $4000$
- Transitional flow, because $Re$ lies between $2000$ and $4000$
- Turbulent flow, because $Re$ is greater than $2000$
- Laminar flow, because $Re$ is of order $10^3$

## Explanation

Measurements in circular pipes identify three ranges. Flow is stably laminar for $Re < 2000$, transitional for $2000 < Re < 4000$, and turbulent for $Re > 4000$. The value $2600$ lies in the second range. Flow can switch between regimes there and is sensitive to inlet disturbances and vibrations. The order of magnitude of $Re$ alone cannot determine the regime, because the laminar-to-transitional threshold is a critical value rather than a power of ten. Under carefully controlled laboratory conditions, laminar flow has persisted to values of $Re$ on the order of $10^4$. The three ranges therefore serve as practical guidelines.
