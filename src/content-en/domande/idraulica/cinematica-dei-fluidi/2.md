A pipe must carry discharge $Q = 200\,\mathrm{L/s}$ at a mean speed no greater than $1.80\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which standard diameter should be selected from $250$, $300$, $400$, and $500\,\mathrm{mm}$?

- $250\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $300\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $400\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $500\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The velocity limit sets a minimum area. From $v = \dfrac{Q}{A}$,
$$
A \geq \frac{Q}{v_{\max}} = \frac{0.200}{1.80}\,\mathrm{m^2} = 0.111\,\mathrm{m^2} .
$$
The area relation $A = \dfrac{\pi D^2}{4}$ then gives the minimum diameter
$$
D \geq \sqrt{\frac{4\,Q}{\pi\,v_{\max}}} = \sqrt{\frac{4 \cdot 0.200}{\pi \cdot 1.80}}\,\mathrm{m} = 0.376\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The first standard diameter to meet this requirement is $400\,\mathrm{mm}$. It gives an actual velocity $v = \dfrac{0.200}{0.126} = 1.59\,\mathrm{m/s}$ below the limit. Diameter $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ would give velocity $2.83\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and would exceed the limit.
