Fluid travels at speed $1.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$ over half a section and $3.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$ over the other half. What is the correction factor $\beta$?

- $1.00$
- $1.25$
- $1.75$
- $1.50$

## Explanation

The total discharge is the sum of the contributions from the two halves, so the mean velocity is
$$
v = \frac{1}{A}\left(1.00 \cdot \frac{A}{2} + 3.00 \cdot \frac{A}{2}\right) = 2.00\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The definition of $\beta$ uses the integral of velocity squared. For two equal areas this integral is
$$
\frac{1}{A}\int_A u^2\,dA = \frac{1.00^2 + 3.00^2}{2}\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2} = 5.00\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2} .
$$
Dividing by $v^2 = 4.00\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$ gives $\beta = \dfrac{5}{4} = 1.25$. Repeating the calculation with velocity cubed gives $\alpha = \dfrac{14}{8} = 1.75$ and confirms $\alpha > \beta > 1$.
