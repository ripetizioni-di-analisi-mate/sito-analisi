A rigid pipe of constant diameter carries incompressible liquid. A downstream valve is slowly closed, reducing the discharge with time. Which statement about the mean speed at the pipe sections is true during closure?

- It stays the same over time because the diameter is constant
- It varies from section to section because the flow is unsteady
- It decreases first near the valve and later at more distant sections
- At each instant it is the same at every section, and it decreases with time

## Explanation

The liquid is incompressible and the pipe is rigid, so the volume of liquid between any two sections is constant. At each instant, the volume entering upstream equals the volume leaving downstream, and discharge is the same at all sections. The sections also have the same area, so mean velocity is independent of position and the flow is **uniform**. Discharge decreases during valve closure, so the flow is also **unsteady**. A delay between sections near the valve and those farther away would require fluid compressibility or pipe deformation, both excluded by the assumptions.
