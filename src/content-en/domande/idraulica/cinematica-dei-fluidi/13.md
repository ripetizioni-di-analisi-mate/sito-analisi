The air duct shown changes from diameter $D_1 = 400\,\mathrm{mm}$ to $D_2 = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$. At the first section the density is $\rho_1 = 1.25\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and mean speed is $v_1 = 10.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At the second the density is $\rho_2 = 1.80\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What is the mean speed there?

::schema{nodi="A,0,0,-;B,3,0,-;C,3.8,0,-;D,7.2,0,-" condotte="A-B,1.2,D_1 = 400 mm;C-D,0.9,D_2 = 300 mm" linee="3,0.6 3.8,0.45;3,-0.6 3.8,-0.45" frecce="1.2,0,2.4,0,v_1;4.9,0,6.1,0,v_2" etichette="1.0,-0.95,ρ_1 = 1.25 kg/m³;5.6,-0.9,ρ_2 = 1.80 kg/m³" riferimento=0 titolo="Air duct with $v_1 = 10.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at the first cross section"}

- $6.94\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $17.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $25.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Density changes between sections. Mass flow rate is conserved, while volumetric flow rate changes:
$$
\rho_1 A_1 v_1 = \rho_2 A_2 v_2 .
$$
The sections have areas $A_1 = 0.126\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $A_2 = 0.0707\,\mathrm{m^2}$, giving a mass flow rate $Q_m = 1.25 \cdot 0.126 \cdot 10.0 = 1.57\,\mathrm{kg/s}$. Hence
$$
v_2 = \frac{Q_m}{\rho_2 A_2} = \frac{1.57}{1.80 \cdot 0.0707}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 12.3\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The volumetric flow rates are $1.26$ and $0.873\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Treating the air as incompressible would give $17.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
