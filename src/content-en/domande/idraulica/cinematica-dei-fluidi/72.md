Groundwater enters a well through a cylindrical vertical screen of diameter $D = 0.300\,\mathrm{m}$ and screened length $L = 10.0\,\mathrm{m}$. The well withdrawal is $Q = 30\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and inflow is uniform over the screened lateral surface. What is the mean approach speed across the screen?

- $6.37\,\mathrm{mm/s}$
- $424\,\mathrm{mm/s}$
- $3.18\,\mathrm{mm/s}$
- $10.0\,\mathrm{mm/s}$

## Explanation

Water enters the pipe radially through the cylindrical side surface, which is perpendicular to the incoming velocity and has area
$$
A = \pi D L = \pi \cdot 0.300 \cdot 10.0\,\mathrm{m^2} = 9.42\,\mathrm{m^2} .
$$
The entire extracted discharge crosses this surface. Thus
$$
v = \frac{Q}{A} = \frac{0.0300}{9.42}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 3.18 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 3.18\,\mathrm{mm/s} .
$$
Using the horizontal pipe section of area $\dfrac{\pi D^2}{4}$ gives $424\,\mathrm{mm/s}$, the upward velocity inside the pipe. The radial inlet velocity is measured across a different surface. Using the radius in place of the diameter gives $6.37\,\mathrm{mm/s}$. Using area $D L$ without the factor $\pi$ gives $10.0\,\mathrm{mm/s}$.
