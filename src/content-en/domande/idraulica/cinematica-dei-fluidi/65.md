Water flows at discharge $Q = 30\,\mathrm{L/s}$ through the annulus between two coaxial pipes. The outer pipe has inside diameter $D = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ and the inner pipe has outside diameter $d = 100\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the mean speed in the annulus?

- $0.955\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.27\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.82\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.318\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The flow cross section is the annulus between the two circles. Its area equals the difference between their areas,
$$
A = \frac{\pi\,(D^2 - d^2)}{4} = \frac{\pi\,(0.200^2 - 0.100^2)}{4}\,\mathrm{m^2} = 0.0236\,\mathrm{m^2} .
$$
The mean velocity is therefore
$$
v = \frac{Q}{A} = \frac{0.0300}{0.0236}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1.27\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Using the outer circle alone gives $0.955\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the velocity for the same flow without the inner pipe. Using a circle of diameter $D - d$ gives $3.82\,\mathrm{m/s}$, but that circle has a different area from the annulus.
