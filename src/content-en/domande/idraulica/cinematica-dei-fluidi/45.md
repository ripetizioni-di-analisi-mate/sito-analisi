The plane velocity field $\vec{v} = (k\,x^2,\;c\,x\,y)$ with $k = 0.200\,\mathrm{m^{-1}s^{-1}}$ describes an incompressible fluid. What is the value of the constant $c$?

- $-0.400\,\mathrm{m^{-1}s^{-1}}$
- $-0.200\,\mathrm{m^{-1}s^{-1}}$
- $0.200\,\mathrm{m^{-1}s^{-1}}$
- $0.400\,\mathrm{m^{-1}s^{-1}}$

## Explanation

Since the fluid is incompressible, $\operatorname{div}\vec{v} = 0$ at every point. Evaluating the two partial derivatives gives
$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(k\,x^2\right) + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(c\,x\,y\right) = 2k\,x + c\,x = (2k + c)\,x .
$$
This expression must vanish for every value of $x$, so its coefficient must be zero and $c = -2k$. Substituting the given value gives
$$
c = -2 \cdot 0.200\,\mathrm{m^{-1}s^{-1}} = -0.400\,\mathrm{m^{-1}s^{-1}} .
$$
Continuity requires the negative sign. The horizontal component increases in the $x$ direction, and the vertical component must offset this increase.
