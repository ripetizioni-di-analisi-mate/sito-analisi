The plane motion of an incompressible fluid has velocity field $\vec{v} = \big(a\,(x + y),\; a\,(x - y)\big)$, with $a = 0.200\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, coordinates in meters and velocities in $\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the slope $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ of the streamline at the point $P = (1.00;\,3.00)$?

- $-2.00$
- $-0.500$
- $0.500$
- $-0.447$

## Explanation

At the point $P$ the velocity components are $v_x = 0.200 \cdot (1.00 + 3.00) = 0.800\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_y = 0.200 \cdot (1.00 - 3.00) = -0.400\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The streamline is tangent to the velocity at each of its points, so its slope is the ratio of the $y$ component to the $x$ component:
$$
\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{v_y}{v_x} = \frac{-0.400}{0.800} = -0.500 .
$$
The component $v_x$ is positive and $v_y$ is negative, so at $P$ the fluid moves to the right and downward. The value $-2.00$ comes from the inverted ratio $\dfrac{v_x}{v_y}$, the value $0.500$ from dropping the sign of $v_y$, and the value $-0.447$ from the ratio $\dfrac{v_y}{|\vec{v}|}$, which is the sine of the angle between the velocity and the $x$ axis.
