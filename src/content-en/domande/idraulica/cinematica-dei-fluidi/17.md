In a steady planar flow the velocity field is $u=ay$, $v=0$, with constant $a>0$. How does a short vertical material line joining two particles at different positive values of $y$ initially evolve?

- It translates vertically oriented without deformation.
- Its top tilts to the right because the upper end moves faster.
- The lower end rises to meet the upper end.
- It rotates as a rigid body with constant length for all time.

## Explanation

Each particle keeps its height because $v=0$. The horizontal velocity difference is $a\Delta y$, positive for the upper end, so their horizontal separation grows as $a\Delta y\,t$.
