The rectangular channel shown has width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and carries constant discharge $Q = 4.50\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The depth is $h_1 = 1.50\,\mathrm{m}$ at section $1$ and $h_2 = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ at section $2$ farther downstream. Which statement is correct?

::schema{acqua="0,0 8,0 8,2.5 6,2.5 2,3.75 0,3.75" solidi="-1.2,-0.4 9.2,-0.4 9.2,0 -1.2,0" tratteggi="1,0 1,4.2;7,0 7,3" quote="0 0,0 3.75,h_1 = 1.50 m,-0.5;8 0,8 2.5,h_2 = 1.00 m,0.5" frecce="3.3,1.3,4.7,1.3,Q" etichette="1,4.5,1;7,3.3,2" riferimento=0 titolo="Longitudinal channel profile between sections $1$ and $2$"}

- $v_2 = 1.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the flow is steady and nonuniform
- $v_2 = 1.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the flow is steady and uniform
- $v_2 = 0.667\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the flow is steady and nonuniform
- $v_2 = 1.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the flow is unsteady and nonuniform

## Explanation

The discharge is independent of time, so the flow is steady and continuity gives equal discharges at the two sections. The flow areas are $A_1 = b\,h_1 = 4.50\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $A_2 = b\,h_2 = 3.00\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so
$$
v_1 = \frac{Q}{A_1} = \frac{4.50}{4.50}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1.00\,\mathrm{m/s} , \qquad v_2 = \frac{Q}{A_2} = \frac{4.50}{3.00}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1.50\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Both flow area and velocity vary along the channel. The flow is therefore **steady and nonuniform**. The value $0.667\,\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from multiplying $v_1$ by $\dfrac{h_2}{h_1}$, which treats velocity as proportional to depth. At constant discharge, velocity is inversely proportional to area.
