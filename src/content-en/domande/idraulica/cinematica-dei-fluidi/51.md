Incompressible plane flow has the velocity field $\vec{v} = (u_0,\; k\,x)$ with $u_0 = 2.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $k = 0.500\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, and coordinates in meters. What is the equation of the streamline through the origin?

- $y = \dfrac{x^2}{8}$
- $y = \dfrac{x^2}{4}$
- $y = 2x^2$
- $y = \dfrac{x}{4}$

## Explanation

Streamlines are tangent to the velocity, so they satisfy
$$
\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{v_y}{v_x} = \frac{k\,x}{u_0} = \frac{x}{4} ,
$$
with $x$ and $y$ in meters. Integration gives $y = \dfrac{x^2}{8} + c$, and the condition that the curve passes through the origin gives $c = 0$. The required streamline is therefore the parabola $y = \dfrac{x^2}{8}$. Omitting the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ when integrating $x$ gives $\dfrac{x^2}{4}$. Reversing the ratio of $u_0$ to $k$ gives $2x^2$. The line $y = \dfrac{x}{4}$ assumes constant slope, whereas the slope increases with $x$.
