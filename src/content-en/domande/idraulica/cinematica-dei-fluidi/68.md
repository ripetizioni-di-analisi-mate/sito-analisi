The trapezoidal channel shown has bottom width $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$, side slope $1:1$, depth $h = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$, and mean speed $v = 0.900\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the discharge?

::schema{acqua="0,0 2,0 3,1 -1,1" solidi="-2.2,1.4 -1.4,1.4 0,0 2,0 3.4,1.4 4.2,1.4 4.2,-1 -2.2,-1" pelo="-1,3,1" quote="0 0,2 0,b = 2.00 m,0.5;0 0,0 1,h,0" etichette="-1.3,0.35,1:1" riferimento=0 titolo="Trapezoidal cross section with side slope $1:1$"}

- $1.80\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $3.60\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $2.70\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $2.25\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$

## Explanation

With a side slope of $1:1$, each bank extends horizontally by the same distance that it rises vertically. The water-surface width is therefore $B = b + 2h = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$. The flow section is a trapezoid with parallel sides $b$ and $B$ and depth $h$, giving
$$
A = \frac{b + B}{2}\,h = (b + h)\,h = 3.00 \cdot 1.00\,\mathrm{m^2} = 3.00\,\mathrm{m^2} .
$$
The discharge is $Q = A\,v = 3.00 \cdot 0.900 = 2.70\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The rectangle $b\,h$ gives $1.80\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ and the rectangle $B\,h$ gives $3.60\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Including only one of the two triangular side areas gives $2.25\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.
