In turbulent flow through a circular pipe of radius $r_0$, speed at distance $r$ from the axis is approximated by
$$
u = u_{\max}\left(1 - \frac{r}{r_0}\right)^{\frac{1}{7}} ,
$$
where $u_{\max} = 2.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the centerline speed. What is the mean speed?

- $1.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.63\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.817\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Mean velocity is discharge divided by area. Using the annular area element $dA = 2\pi r\,dr$ gives
$$
v = \frac{1}{\pi r_0^2}\int_0^{r_0} u\,2\pi r\,dr .
$$
Make the substitution $s = 1 - \dfrac{r}{r_0}$, so that $r = r_0\,(1 - s)$ and $dr = -r_0\,ds$. This gives
$$
v = 2\,u_{\max}\int_0^1 s^{\frac{1}{7}}\,(1 - s)\,ds = 2\,u_{\max}\left(\frac{7}{8} - \frac{7}{15}\right) = \frac{49}{60}\,u_{\max} .
$$
Hence $v = \dfrac{49}{60} \cdot 2.00 = 1.63\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The ratio $\dfrac{v}{u_{\max}} = 0.817$ is much closer to $1$ than the laminar value $\dfrac{1}{2}$ because the turbulent velocity profile is flatter. Omitting the factor $2$ from the area element would give $0.817\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
