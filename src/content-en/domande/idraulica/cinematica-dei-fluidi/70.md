A pipe of diameter $D = 100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and length $300\,\mathrm{m}$ receives discharge $Q_0 = 12.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$ at its upstream end and delivers water uniformly at a rate $0.030\,\mathrm{L/s}$ per meter along its length, as shown. What is the mean speed at section $S$, a distance $200\,\mathrm{m}$ from the inlet?

::schema{nodi="A,0,0,-;B,9,0,-" condotte="A-B,0.5,D = 100 mm" frecce="-2,0,-0.3,0;1,-0.35,1,-1;2,-0.35,2,-1;3,-0.35,3,-1;4,-0.35,4,-1;5,-0.35,5,-1;6.5,-0.35,6.5,-1;7.5,-0.35,7.5,-1;8.5,-0.35,8.5,-1" tratteggi="6,-0.6 6,0.6" etichette="-1.2,0.45,Q_0 = 12.0 L/s;6.3,0.75,S;4.5,-1.55,0.030 L/s per m" quote="0 0,6 0,200 m,-1.4;0 0,9 0,300 m,-2.1" riferimento=0 titolo="Pipe with uniformly distributed outflow along its length"}

- $1.53\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.382\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.955\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.764\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Between the inlet and section $S$, the pipe delivers $0.030 \cdot 200 = 6.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$ along its length. Continuity over this reach gives the discharge passing through $S$ as the inlet discharge less the amount delivered, or $Q = 12.0 - 6.0 = 6.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The section has area $A = \dfrac{\pi \cdot 0.100^2}{4} = 7.85 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so
$$
v = \frac{Q}{A} = \frac{6.0 \cdot 10^{-3}}{7.85 \cdot 10^{-3}}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 0.764\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The discharge decreases linearly along the pipe, from $12.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$ at the inlet to $3.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$ at the end. The value $0.955\,\mathrm{m/s}$ uses the average discharge $7.5\,\mathrm{L/s}$ over the whole pipe, instead of the discharge at section $S$.
