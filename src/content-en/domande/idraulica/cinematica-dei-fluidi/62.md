A roof of horizontal projected area $400\,\mathrm{m^2}$ receives rainfall of constant intensity $60\,\mathrm{mm/h}$. All runoff enters a vertical downspout of diameter $D = 100\,\mathrm{mm}$. If it flows full, what is the mean water speed?

- $8.49\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.212\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.40\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.849\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Rainfall intensity is the depth of water falling per unit time, so
$$
60\,\mathrm{mm/h} = \frac{0.060\,\mathrm{m}}{3600\,\mathrm{s}} = 1.67 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Multiplying by the horizontal plan area of the roof gives a collected discharge of $Q = 400 \cdot 1.67 \cdot 10^{-5} = 6.67 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, or $6.67\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The downspout has area $A = \dfrac{\pi \cdot 0.100^2}{4} = 7.85 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so
$$
v = \frac{Q}{A} = \frac{6.67 \cdot 10^{-3}}{7.85 \cdot 10^{-3}}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 0.849\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $8.49\,\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from an incorrect factor $10$ in converting millimeters. The values $0.212$ and $3.40\,\mathrm{m/s}$ result from calculating the area as $\pi D^2$ or substituting the radius for the diameter in $\dfrac{\pi D^2}{4}$.
