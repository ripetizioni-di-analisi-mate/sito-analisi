An air duct of diameter $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries mass flow rate $Q_m = 0.500\,\mathrm{kg/s}$. What is the mean speed at a section where the air density is $\rho = 1.20\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$?

- $1.47\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.63\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.07\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.90\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The density at the section is known, so the volumetric flow rate follows from the mass flow rate using $Q = \dfrac{Q_m}{\rho}$, giving
$$
Q = \frac{0.500}{1.20}\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 0.417\,\mathrm{m^3/s} .
$$
The section has area $A = \dfrac{\pi \cdot 0.300^2}{4} = 0.0707\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so
$$
v = \frac{Q}{A} = \frac{0.417}{0.0707}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 5.90\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Substituting back gives $\rho\,A\,v = 1.20 \cdot 0.0707 \cdot 5.90 = 0.500\,\mathrm{kg/s}$. Dividing mass flow rate by area without accounting for density would give $7.07\,\mathrm{m/s}$, which has the wrong units for velocity.
