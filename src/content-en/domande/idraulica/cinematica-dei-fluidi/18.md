A cylindrical constant-diameter pipe is supplied by a constant-level reservoir. How is the flow classified far from the entrance and bends?

- Unsteady and uniform
- Steady and nonuniform
- Steady and uniform
- Unsteady and nonuniform

## Explanation

The reservoir level is constant, so velocity at a fixed section does not change with time. The derivative $\dfrac{\partial \vec{v}}{\partial t}$ is zero and the flow is steady. The pipe diameter is constant, and the reach is far from the inlet and any bends. The transverse sections therefore have the same area and the same velocity distribution. The streamwise derivative $\dfrac{\partial \vec{v}}{\partial s}$ is also zero, so the flow is uniform. Under these conditions the streamlines are straight and parallel to the pipe axis.
