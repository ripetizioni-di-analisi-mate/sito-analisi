A circular pipe must carry discharge $Q = 90\,\mathrm{L/s}$ at mean speed $v = 1.20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What diameter is required?

- $0.0955\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.155\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.309\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.274\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Solving $Q = A\,v$ gives the required area,
$$
A = \frac{Q}{v} = \frac{0.0900}{1.20}\,\mathrm{m^2} = 0.0750\,\mathrm{m^2} ,
$$
and the area formula for a circle gives the diameter,
$$
D = \sqrt{\frac{4A}{\pi}} = \sqrt{\frac{4 \cdot 0.0750}{\pi}}\,\mathrm{m} = 0.309\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Substitution provides a check. With $D = 0.309\,\mathrm{m}$, the area is $A = 0.0750\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the discharge is $Q = 0.0900\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Omitting the factor $4$ under the square root would give the radius $0.155\,\mathrm{m}$, half the required diameter.
