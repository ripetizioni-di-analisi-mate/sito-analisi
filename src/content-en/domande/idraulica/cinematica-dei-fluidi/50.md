In an unsteady flow, which procedure makes the streamlines at a particular instant $t$ visible?

- Inject dye from a fixed needle and photograph the dye filament at time $t$
- Seed the fluid with floating particles and take a short-exposure photograph at time $t$
- Track one particle with a camera throughout the motion
- Seed the fluid with floating particles and take a long-exposure photograph

## Explanation

A streamline at time $t$ is tangent everywhere to the velocity at that instant. During a short exposure each particle travels only a very short distance in the direction of its velocity at $t$. The resulting streaks show local streamline tangents from which the streamlines can be drawn. Dye from a fixed needle forms a streakline containing particles that passed the needle at different times. A camera following one particle records its pathline. In unsteady flow these curves generally differ from the streamlines because the field changes as the particles move. For the same reason a long exposure records portions of pathlines.
