An incompressible fluid in plane flow has the velocity field
$$
\vec{v} = (a\,y,\;a\,x) ,
$$
with $a = 0.500\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, coordinates in meters, and velocities in $\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the speed at $P = (3.00;\,1.00)$?

- $1.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.58\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.16\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Substitute the coordinates of $P$ into the velocity field to find its components:
$$
v_x = a\,y_P = 0.500 \cdot 1.00\,\mathrm{m/s} = 0.500\,\mathrm{m/s} ,
\qquad
v_y = a\,x_P = 0.500 \cdot 3.00\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1.50\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The speed is the square root of the sum of the squared components, giving
$$
|\vec{v}| = \sqrt{0.500^2 + 1.50^2}\,\mathrm{m/s} = \sqrt{2.50}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1.58\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The magnitude exceeds the larger component and is smaller than the sum of the two positive components, in agreement with the triangle inequality.
