A full pipe carries water along the $x$-axis. Axial velocity is $+2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ over three quarters of the area, while recirculation gives $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on the remaining quarter. What mean axial velocity gives the net discharge?

- $1.75\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Discharge integrates the signed normal velocity over the section. Its area-weighted mean is $(3/4)2+(1/4)(-1)=1.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
