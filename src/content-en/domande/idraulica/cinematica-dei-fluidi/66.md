A circular sewer of diameter $D = 400\,\mathrm{mm}$ runs half full, with water in the lower semicircle at mean speed $v = 1.20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the discharge?

- $151\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $37.7\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $302\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $75.4\,\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

The flow section is a semicircle of diameter $D$. Its area is half the area of the full circle,
$$
A = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{\pi D^2}{4} = \frac{\pi \cdot 0.400^2}{8}\,\mathrm{m^2} = 0.0628\,\mathrm{m^2} .
$$
The discharge is $Q = A\,v = 0.0628 \cdot 1.20 = 0.0754\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, or $75.4\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The value $151\,\mathrm{L/s}$ corresponds to a full pipe and $37.7\,\mathrm{L/s}$ to a quarter circle. The value $302\,\mathrm{L/s}$ results from using $\dfrac{\pi D^2}{2}$ as the area of the semicircle.
