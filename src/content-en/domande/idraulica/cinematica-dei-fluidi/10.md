A rectangular channel of width $b = 2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and depth $h = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ is divided into five layers of thickness $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$. Speeds measured at their midpoints from bed to surface are $0.60$, $0.85$, $1.00$, $1.10$, and $1.15\,\mathrm{m/s}$, as shown. What is the cross-sectional mean speed?

::schema{acqua="0,0 3.5,0 3.5,1 0,1" solidi="-0.25,-0.35 3.75,-0.35 3.75,0 -0.25,0" pelo="0,3.5,1" tratteggi="0,0.2 0.45,0.2;0,0.4 0.45,0.4;0,0.6 0.45,0.6;0,0.8 0.45,0.8" frecce="0.5,0.1,1.46,0.1,0.60 m/s;0.5,0.3,1.86,0.3,0.85 m/s;0.5,0.5,2.10,0.5,1.00 m/s;0.5,0.7,2.26,0.7,1.10 m/s;0.5,0.9,2.34,0.9,1.15 m/s" quote="0 0,0 1,h = 1.00 m,-0.35" riferimento=0 titolo="Velocities measured at the centers of five layers of thickness $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$"}

- $0.875\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.940\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.15\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Each layer has height $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ and width $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$, so all five have the same area $\Delta A = 0.500\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Adding the contributions from the five layers gives
$$
Q = \Delta A \sum_{i=1}^{5} u_i = 0.500 \cdot 4.70\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 2.35\,\mathrm{m^3/s} .
$$
The total area is $A = 5\,\Delta A = 2.50\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so the mean velocity equals the arithmetic mean of the five readings:
$$
v = \frac{Q}{A} = \frac{0.60 + 0.85 + 1.00 + 1.10 + 1.15}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 0.940\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
As a check, the result lies between the smallest and largest measured velocities. The middle-layer velocity $1.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the average of the two extreme readings $0.875\,\mathrm{m/s}$ both omit some measurements.
