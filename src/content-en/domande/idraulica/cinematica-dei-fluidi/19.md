Which of the following flows is steady?

- A pipe fed by a constant-level reservoir with its gate valve held fixed
- A tank draining through a bottom orifice
- A flood wave passing along a river
- Closing a gate valve in a pipe

## Explanation

Flow is steady when velocity at every fixed point is independent of time, so $\dfrac{\partial \vec{v}}{\partial t} = \vec{0}$. A pipe fed by a constant-level reservoir with a fixed gate-valve opening carries a constant discharge. The velocity measured at a given section is therefore constant in time and satisfies this condition. As a tank drains, its water level falls and its discharge decreases. During a flood wave, depth and velocity vary at each section. Moving a gate valve changes the resistance to flow. In each of these three cases, velocity at a fixed section depends on time, so the flow is unsteady.
