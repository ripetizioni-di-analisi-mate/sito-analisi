A full rectangular duct of sides $0.500\,\mathrm{m}$ and $0.300\,\mathrm{m}$ carries water of kinematic viscosity $\nu = 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2/s}$ at mean speed $1.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the Reynolds number?

- $9.38 \cdot 10^4$
- $1.88 \cdot 10^5$
- $5.00 \cdot 10^5$
- $3.75 \cdot 10^5$

## Explanation

Since the section is noncircular, use the hydraulic diameter $D_e = 4R$ as the characteristic length, where $R$ is the hydraulic radius. The cross-sectional area is $A = 0.500 \cdot 0.300 = 0.150\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the wetted perimeter is $C = 2\,(0.500 + 0.300) = 1.60\,\mathrm{m}$, so
$$
R = \frac{A}{C} = \frac{0.150}{1.60}\,\mathrm{m} = 0.0938\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
D_e = 4R = 0.375\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
It follows that
$$
Re = \frac{v\,D_e}{\nu} = \frac{1.00 \cdot 0.375}{10^{-6}} = 3.75 \cdot 10^5 .
$$
Using the hydraulic radius without the factor $4$ would give $9.38 \cdot 10^4$, one-quarter of the correct value.
