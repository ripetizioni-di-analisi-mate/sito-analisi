In the rectangular channel shown, width is $b = 2.50\,\mathrm{m}$, depth is $h = 0.90\,\mathrm{m}$, and the vertical velocity profile is
$$
u = u_{\max}\left(\frac{2y}{h} - \frac{y^2}{h^2}\right) ,
$$
where $y$ is distance above the bed and $u_{\max} = 1.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is surface speed. What is the discharge?

## Explanation

Velocity depends only on distance from the bed. Average the profile over the depth to obtain the mean:
$$
v = \frac{1}{h}\int_0^h u_{\max}\left(\frac{2y}{h} - \frac{y^2}{h^2}\right) dy = u_{\max}\left(1 - \frac{1}{3}\right) = \frac{2}{3}\,u_{\max} .
$$
Thus $v = \dfrac{2}{3} \cdot 1.50 = 1.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The wetted cross-sectional area is $A = b\,h = 2.50 \cdot 0.90 = 2.25\,\mathrm{m^2}$, giving a discharge of
$$
Q = A\,v = 2.25 \cdot 1.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 2.25\,\mathrm{m^3/s} .
$$
The mean velocity is lower than the surface velocity, as expected because the no-slip condition gives zero velocity at the bed.
