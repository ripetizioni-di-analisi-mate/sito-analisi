The pipe shown has three sections in series of diameters $D_1 = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$, $D_2 = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$, and $D_3 = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$, carrying discharge $Q = 80\,\mathrm{L/s}$. What is the mean speed in the section of diameter $200\,\mathrm{mm}$?

::schema{nodi="A,0,0,-;B,2.6,0,-;C,3.2,0,-;D,5.4,0,-;E,6.0,0,-;F,8.6,0,-" condotte="A-B,1.0,D_1 = 250 mm;C-D,0.8,D_2 = 200 mm;E-F,1.2,D_3 = 300 mm" linee="2.6,0.5 3.2,0.4;2.6,-0.5 3.2,-0.4;5.4,0.4 6.0,0.6;5.4,-0.4 6.0,-0.6" frecce="0.6,0,1.6,0,Q = 80 L/s" riferimento=0 titolo="Three pipe sections carrying the same flow rate in series"}

- $0.637\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.63\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.55\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The three pipe sections form one streamtube, so each carries the same discharge $Q = 0.0800\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The middle section has area
$$
A_2 = \frac{\pi D_2^2}{4} = \frac{\pi \cdot 0.200^2}{4}\,\mathrm{m^2} = 0.0314\,\mathrm{m^2} ,
$$
giving a mean velocity there of
$$
v_2 = \frac{Q}{A_2} = \frac{0.0800}{0.0314}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2.55\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The section of diameter $200\,\mathrm{mm}$ is the narrowest and has the highest mean velocity. The velocities at diameters $250$ and $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ are $1.63$ and $1.13\,\mathrm{m/s}$, respectively.
