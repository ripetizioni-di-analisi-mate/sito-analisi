A straight pipe is supplied by a constant-level reservoir and its discharge is independent of time. Which statement about particle pathlines is correct?

- They change shape with time because each particle changes speed as it moves
- They differ from streamlines because velocity depends on position
- They coincide with streamlines only in the middle of the section
- They coincide with streamlines everywhere

## Explanation

The reservoir level and discharge are constant, so velocity at each fixed point is independent of time and the flow is steady. Since the field $\vec{v}$ is independent of $t$, the streamlines remain fixed. A particle on a streamline has a velocity tangent to the curve and continues to move tangentially as it travels along it. Its pathline therefore coincides with the streamline. In unsteady flow the two families of curves generally differ because the streamlines can change shape while a particle moves.
