A rectangular open channel has width $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$, depth $h = 0.500\,\mathrm{m}$, and mean speed $v = 0.300\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The kinematic viscosity is $\nu = 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2/s}$. What is the Reynolds number based on the hydraulic diameter $D_e = 4R$?

- $4.00 \cdot 10^5$
- $2.40 \cdot 10^5$
- $1.00 \cdot 10^5$
- $1.50 \cdot 10^5$

## Explanation

Only the bed and the two banks are in contact with the water. The free surface is excluded from the wetted perimeter, so $C = b + 2h = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$. The flow area is $A = b\,h = 1.00\,\mathrm{m^2}$, giving hydraulic radius $R = \dfrac{A}{C} = 0.333\,\mathrm{m}$ and hydraulic diameter $D_e = 4R = 1.33\,\mathrm{m}$. The Reynolds number is
$$
Re = \frac{v\,D_e}{\nu} = \frac{0.300 \cdot 1.33}{10^{-6}} = 4.00 \cdot 10^5 .
$$
Including the free surface as though the channel were a closed duct of side lengths $b$ and $h$ would give $R = 0.200\,\mathrm{m}$ and $Re = 2.40 \cdot 10^5$. Using $R$ instead of $4R$ gives $1.00 \cdot 10^5$, and using water depth as the characteristic length gives $1.50 \cdot 10^5$.
