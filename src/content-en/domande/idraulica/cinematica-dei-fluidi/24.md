The mean speed in a pipe of diameter $D = 350\,\mathrm{mm}$ is $v = 1.60\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the discharge?

- $38.5\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $196\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $616\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $154\,\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

The area of the circular section is
$$
A = \frac{\pi D^2}{4} = \frac{\pi \cdot 0.350^2}{4}\,\mathrm{m^2} = 0.0962\,\mathrm{m^2} .
$$
The section is perpendicular to the streamlines, so discharge equals area times mean velocity,
$$
Q = A\,v = 0.0962 \cdot 1.60\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 0.154\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 154\,\mathrm{L/s} .
$$
The unit conversion uses $1\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 1000\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Using $\pi D^2$ for the area would substitute the diameter for the radius and quadruple the result, giving $616\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
