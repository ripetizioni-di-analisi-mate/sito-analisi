A pipe supplies discharge $Q = 180\,\mathrm{L/s}$ to a junction feeding three outlets of diameters $D_1 = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$, $D_2 = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$, and $D_3 = 150\,\mathrm{mm}$, as shown. All three have the same mean speed. What is that speed?

::schema{nomi=0 nodi="N,0,0;A,-3.4,0;B,3.2,2.0;C,3.6,0;D,3.2,-2.0" condotte="A-N,0.9;N-B,0.75,D_1 = 250 mm;N-C,0.6,D_2 = 200 mm;N-D,0.45,D_3 = 150 mm" frecce="-3.0,0,-1.8,0,Q = 180 L/s" riferimento=0 titolo="Flow dividing among three pipes with the same mean velocity"}

- $1.83\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.458\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.22\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.67\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Each branch carries $Q_i = A_i v$ at the same velocity $v$, so the junction balance gives $Q = v\,(A_1 + A_2 + A_3)$. The three areas are $0.0491$, $0.0314$, and $0.0177\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and their sum is
$$
A_1 + A_2 + A_3 = \frac{\pi}{4}\left(0.250^2 + 0.200^2 + 0.150^2\right)\,\mathrm{m^2} = 0.0982\,\mathrm{m^2} .
$$
This gives
$$
v = \frac{Q}{A_1 + A_2 + A_3} = \frac{0.180}{0.0982}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1.83\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The three branch discharges are $90.0$, $57.6$, and $32.4\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Their sum $180\,\mathrm{L/s}$ checks the calculation.
