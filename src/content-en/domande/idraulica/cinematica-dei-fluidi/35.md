At a section with a nonuniform velocity profile, which relation holds between correction factors $\alpha$ and $\beta$?

- $\alpha = \beta = 1$
- $\beta > \alpha > 1$
- $\alpha > \beta > 1$
- $\alpha > 1 > \beta$

## Explanation

The two correction factors are defined by
$$
\alpha = \frac{1}{A\,v^3}\int_A u^3\,dA ,
\qquad
\beta = \frac{1}{A\,v^2}\int_A u^2\,dA ,
$$
where $u$ is local velocity and $v$ is mean velocity. For a nonuniform profile, the mean of either power of velocity exceeds the corresponding power of the mean velocity. Both integrals therefore exceed their uniform-flow values, so $\alpha > 1$ and $\beta > 1$. The power-mean inequality applied to orders $2$ and $3$ gives the larger mean for the higher order, hence $\alpha > \beta$. In laminar flow through a circular pipe, $\alpha = 2$ and $\beta = \dfrac{4}{3}$, consistent with $\alpha > \beta > 1$.
