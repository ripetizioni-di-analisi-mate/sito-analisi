A long pipeline starts at reservoir elevation $58\,\mathrm{m}$ and reaches a crest at distance $800\,\mathrm{m}$ from the entrance and centerline elevation $55\,\mathrm{m}$, as shown. Gauge pressure at the crest must be at least $-6\,\mathrm{m}$ of water. What is the maximum permissible energy gradient?

- $0.00375$
- $0.0113$
- $0.00600$
- $-0.00375$

## Explanation

The gauge pressure head at the summit is $\dfrac{p}{\gamma} = z_1 - J x_c - z_c$, so the condition $\dfrac{p}{\gamma} \geq -6\,\mathrm{m}$ becomes
$$
58 - 800\,J - 55 \geq -6 .
$$
Solving for the gradient gives $800\,J \leq 3 + 6$ and hence
$$
J \leq \frac{9}{800} = 0.0113 .
$$
A steeper energy gradient would lower the summit pressure below the permitted minimum. The discharge must therefore be limited, with a gate valve downstream of the summit dissipating the head left after pipe friction. The value $0.00375$ sets the summit gauge pressure to zero and neglects the permitted negative pressure.
