At different speeds of the same pump, the same installation reaches two operating points $(Q,H)=(0.10,20)$ and $(0.20,26)$, in $\mathrm{m^3/s}$ and metres. The difference between reservoir levels stays fixed, and the system curve is $H=\Delta z+rQ^2$. What elevation difference follows without knowing the pump curves?

- $14\,\mathrm m$
- $18\,\mathrm m$
- $20\,\mathrm m$
- $6\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Subtracting the operating-point equations gives $6=r(0.20^2-0.10^2)$, so $r=200\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$. The discharge-independent term is $\Delta z=20-200(0.10)^2=18\,\mathrm m$.
