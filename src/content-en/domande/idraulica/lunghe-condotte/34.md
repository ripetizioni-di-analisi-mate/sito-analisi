The hydraulic grade line lies below the centerline over part of a long pipeline. What does this indicate about pressure in that part?

- The gauge pressure is positive
- The gauge pressure is negative
- The gauge pressure is zero
- The absolute pressure is zero

## Explanation

The piezometric head is $h = z + \dfrac{p}{\gamma}$. The pressure head $\dfrac{p}{\gamma} = h - z$ is the signed vertical distance from the pipe centerline to the hydraulic grade line. Where the grade line falls below the centerline, $h < z$ and gauge pressure is **negative**. Absolute pressure remains positive. For liquid water at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ it must stay above the vapor pressure $2.34\,\mathrm{kPa}$, equivalent to a gauge pressure head of $-10.09\,\mathrm{m}$ of water.
