A pump with characteristic curve $H_p = 28 - 900\,Q^2$, with $Q$ in $\mathrm{m^3/s}$ and $H_p$ in meters, delivers water to a reservoir whose free surface is $32\,\mathrm{m}$ above the supply reservoir, through a pipe of resistance $r = 1500\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$. Which statement is correct?

- The pump delivers the discharge at which its head falls to zero
- The pump delivers a small discharge given by the intersection of the two curves
- The pump delivers the discharge at which the pipe head loss equals $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- The pump delivers no water because its head is less than the static lift at every discharge

## Explanation

An operating point with positive discharge requires the shutoff head to exceed the static lift, $H_0 > \Delta z_g$. Here $H_0 = 28\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\Delta z_g = 32\,\mathrm{m}$, so that condition is not satisfied. For every $Q \geq 0$, the pump supplies at most $28\,\mathrm{m}$, whereas the system requires at least $32\,\mathrm{m}$. The two curves therefore have no intersection. Indeed, $28 - 900\,Q^2 = 32 + 1500\,Q^2$ gives $Q^2 = -\dfrac{4}{2400}$, which has no real solution for the discharge. The pump **cannot deliver water** to the higher reservoir in this system.
