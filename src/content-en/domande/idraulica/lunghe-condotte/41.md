A pump is installed in a pipe connecting two constant-level reservoirs, with the delivery reservoir higher than the supply reservoir. What does the pump head represent?

- The static lift plus the head loss in the pipe
- Only the elevation difference between the two free surfaces
- Only the head loss in the pipe
- The maximum pressure in the discharge main

## Explanation

The pump supplies $H_p$ of energy per unit weight, so the total head increases by $H_p$ across the pump. The energy balance between the free surfaces is $H_1 + H_p = H_2 + \Delta H$. Both free surfaces have zero gauge pressure and negligible velocity, giving
$$
H_p = (z_2 - z_1) + \Delta H ,
$$
so the pump head equals **the static lift plus the head loss**. The static lift alone would suffice if the pipe dissipated no energy, as in ideal flow.
