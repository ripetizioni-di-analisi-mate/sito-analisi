A pipeline of length $3000\,\mathrm{m}$ connects reservoir surfaces at elevations $50\,\mathrm{m}$ and $10\,\mathrm{m}$. From the entrance to distance $1500\,\mathrm{m}$ its centerline is horizontal at elevation $36\,\mathrm{m}$. It then descends linearly to elevation $4\,\mathrm{m}$ at distance $2100\,\mathrm{m}$, and continues horizontally to the downstream reservoir, as shown. Over what length is the gauge pressure negative?

::schema{acqua="-1.8,5 0,5 0,3.2 -1.8,3.2;12,1 13.8,1 13.8,0 12,0" solidi="-1.8,2.9 0,2.9 0,3.2 -1.8,3.2;12,-0.3 13.8,-0.3 13.8,0 12,0" nodi="A,0,3.6,-;B,6,3.6,-;C,8.4,0.4,-;U,12,0.4,-" condotte="A-B-C-U,0.14" pelo="-1.8,0,5,50.0 m;12,13.8,1,10.0 m" etichette="3,4.05,centerline 36.0 m;9.6,0.95,centerline 4.0 m" quote="0 -0.7,6 -0.7,1500 m,0.2;6 -0.7,8.4 -0.7,600 m,0.2;8.4 -0.7,12 -0.7,900 m,0.2" titolo="Profile of a pipeline of length $3000\,\mathrm{m}$ with a long horizontal section"}

- $1050\,\mathrm{m}$
- $600\,\mathrm{m}$
- $450\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The energy gradient is $J = \dfrac{50 - 10}{3000} = 0.0133$ and the piezometric head is $h(x) = 50 - 0.0133\,x$. Along the horizontal reach the grade line meets the pipe centerline where $50 - 0.0133\,x = 36$, at $x = 1050\,\mathrm{m}$. Beyond this point gauge pressure is negative and at $1500\,\mathrm{m}$ it is $\dfrac{p}{\gamma} = 30 - 36 = -6\,\mathrm{m}$. In the descending reach the centerline elevation is $z(x) = 36 - 0.0533\,(x - 1500)$, so the condition $h(x) = z(x)$ gives
$$
50 - 0.0133\,x = 36 - 0.0533\,(x - 1500) , \qquad x = 1650\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The gauge pressure is therefore negative between $1050$ and $1650\,\mathrm{m}$, a distance of $600\,\mathrm{m}$. Ending the interval where the horizontal section ends would give $450\,\mathrm{m}$. Comparing the pipe centerline with the upstream reservoir surface in place of the hydraulic grade line would give $0\,\mathrm{m}$.
