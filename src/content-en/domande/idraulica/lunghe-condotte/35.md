A long pipeline starts at a constant-level reservoir and discharges freely to the atmosphere. At what elevation does the hydraulic grade line end at the outlet?

- At the upstream reservoir surface
- At a height $\dfrac{v^2}{2g}$ above the outlet centerline
- At the elevation of the pipe centerline at the outlet
- At ground level below the outlet

## Explanation

At a free outlet the jet leaves the pipe at atmospheric pressure. Gauge pressure is zero, so the piezometric head $h = z$ equals the outlet centerline elevation. The hydraulic grade line therefore falls from the upstream reservoir surface to the **pipe centerline at the outlet** at the energy gradient. If the pipe discharges into another reservoir instead, the grade line ends at the downstream free surface, where gauge pressure is zero.
