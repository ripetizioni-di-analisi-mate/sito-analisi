The Darcy design formula for a long pipe at fixed discharge and friction factor gives $\Delta H\propto D^{-5}$. An internal lining reduces the clear diameter from $250$ to $240\,\mathrm{mm}$. The original head loss was $20\,\mathrm m$ and the available head is $22\,\mathrm m$. Is the original discharge still achievable?

- Yes, it would require about $20.8\,\mathrm m$.
- Yes, it would require about $16.3\,\mathrm m$.
- Yes, specifying the discharge makes the loss independent of diameter.
- No, it would require about $24.5\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

At the original discharge the loss becomes $20(250/240)^5\simeq24.5\,\mathrm m$. The reduced clear diameter therefore demands more head than is available.
