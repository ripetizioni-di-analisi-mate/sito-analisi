A long uniform pipe loses $1\,\mathrm m$ of head at flow rate $Q$ and $4\,\mathrm m$ at $2Q$. Assuming $\Delta H=KQ^n$ throughout the range, what loss is expected at $1.5Q$?

- $2.50\,\mathrm m$
- $2.25\,\mathrm m$
- $1.50\,\mathrm m$
- $3\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The measurement ratio gives $2^n=4$, so $n=2$. At the intermediate flow the loss is $1.5^2\cdot1=2.25\,\mathrm m$ because the loss law is quadratic.
