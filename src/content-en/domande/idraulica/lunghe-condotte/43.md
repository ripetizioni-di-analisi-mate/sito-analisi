A gate valve on a pump discharge line is partially closed, increasing the pipe system’s hydraulic resistance. How do discharge and head at the operating point change?

- Discharge increases and pump head decreases
- Both discharge and pump head decrease
- Discharge decreases and pump head increases
- Discharge stays the same and pump head increases

## Explanation

The system curve is $H_c(Q) = \Delta z_g + r Q^2$. Increasing $r$ therefore makes the curve steeper for the same static lift. The pump curve slopes downward and remains unchanged, so at the new intersection the **discharge decreases**. The pump develops a higher head at this lower discharge, so the **pump head increases**. The valve dissipates the extra head while the static lift remains the same, and the operating point moves toward the shutoff head.
