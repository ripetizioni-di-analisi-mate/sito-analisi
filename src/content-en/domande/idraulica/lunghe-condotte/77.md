A pump lifts water from a reservoir to a higher reservoir along a long pipeline. It is some distance from the pipe entrance. Which description of the hydraulic grade line is correct?

- It rises at a constant slope from the source reservoir surface to the receiving reservoir surface
- It is horizontal at the receiving reservoir water level
- It rises by $H_p$ through the pump and then runs horizontally to the receiving reservoir
- From the source reservoir it falls at the energy gradient to the pump, rises by $H_p$ through the pump, then falls at the energy gradient to the receiving reservoir

## Explanation

From the source reservoir to the pump the flow loses head to pipe friction, so the hydraulic grade line falls from the free surface at the energy gradient. The pump adds energy per unit weight $H_p$, producing a vertical rise of $H_p$ in the grade line. Friction then makes the grade line fall along the discharge main until it reaches the receiving reservoir surface. A gradual rise in this pipe would imply an increase in head along the flow direction without a machine to supply it.
