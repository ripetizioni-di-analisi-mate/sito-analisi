A full circular pipe of diameter $D = 400\,\mathrm{mm}$ and roughness coefficient $k_s = 90\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ carries flow at mean velocity $v = 1.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the energy gradient from Gauckler–Strickler?

- $0.00152$
- $0.00383$
- $0.000603$
- $0.345$

## Explanation

For a full circular pipe, the hydraulic radius is
$$
R = \frac{A}{C} = \frac{D}{4} = 0.100\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Solving the Gauckler–Strickler equation, $v = k_s R^{\frac{2}{3}} J^{\frac{1}{2}}$, for the energy gradient gives
$$
J = \frac{v^2}{k_s^2\,R^{\frac{4}{3}}} = \frac{1.2^2}{90^2 \cdot 0.100^{\frac{4}{3}}} = \frac{1.44}{8100 \cdot 0.04642} = 3.83 \cdot 10^{-3} .
$$
Substitution into the original velocity equation confirms the result, since $90 \cdot 0.100^{\frac{2}{3}} \cdot \sqrt{3.83 \cdot 10^{-3}} = 1.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $0.00152$ follows from taking $R = \dfrac{D}{2}$, which confuses the hydraulic radius with the pipe radius.
