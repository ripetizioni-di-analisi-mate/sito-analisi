A steel pipe with $D = 400\,\mathrm{mm}$ has roughness coefficient $k_s = 80\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$. Which Darcy–Weisbach friction factor $\lambda$ gives the same energy gradient as Gauckler–Strickler at the same velocity?

- $0.0105$
- $0.00660$
- $0.0166$
- $0.0264$

## Explanation

The Darcy–Weisbach equation gives $J = \dfrac{\lambda}{D}\,\dfrac{v^2}{2g}$, and the Gauckler–Strickler equation gives $J = \dfrac{v^2}{k_s^2 R^{\frac{4}{3}}}$ when solved for the energy gradient. Equating the two expressions gives
$$
\lambda = \frac{2 g D}{k_s^2\,R^{\frac{4}{3}}} .
$$
For the full circular pipe, the hydraulic radius is $R = \dfrac{D}{4} = 0.1\,\mathrm{m}$, so $R^{\frac{4}{3}} = 0.0464$ and
$$
\lambda = \frac{2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.4}{80^2 \cdot 0.0464} = 0.0264 .
$$
The value $0.0105$ follows from equating the hydraulic radius to the pipe radius $\dfrac{D}{2}$.
