During a test, a pump delivers head $58\,\mathrm{m}$ at shutoff and head $42\,\mathrm{m}$ at discharge $80\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Fitting the curve $H_p = H_0 - a\,Q^2$, with $Q$ in $\mathrm{m^3/s}$, what is coefficient $a$?

- $2500\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$
- $0.00250\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$
- $6560\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$
- $200\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$

## Explanation

The discharge at shutoff is zero, so $H_0 = 58\,\mathrm{m}$. At the second test point the discharge is $Q = 0.080\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The condition $42 = 58 - a \cdot 0.080^2$ therefore gives
$$
a = \frac{58 - 42}{0.080^2} = \frac{16}{0.0064} = 2500\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5} .
$$
Leaving the discharge in liters per second would give $0.00250\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$. The value $6560\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$ follows from dividing the measured head $42\,\mathrm{m}$ by the squared discharge instead of using the difference between the two measured heads.
