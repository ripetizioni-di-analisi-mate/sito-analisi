A long pipeline starts from a constant-level reservoir and its profile rises and falls several times. Which section is the critical point, where gauge pressure is lowest?

- At the section where $z + J x$ is greatest
- At the section where the elevation $z$ is greatest
- At the section farthest from the entrance
- At the midpoint of the pipe

## Explanation

Piezometric head decreases linearly with distance from the inlet according to $h(x) = z_1 - J x$, so the gauge pressure head at distance $x$ is
$$
\frac{p(x)}{\gamma} = z_1 - \big(z(x) + J\,x\big) .
$$
Since $z_1$ is fixed, pressure is lowest where the sum of the centerline elevation and the accumulated head loss, $z + J x$, reaches its **maximum**. The critical point is thus the point highest relative to the hydraulic grade line. A lower section much farther from the inlet can have a lower pressure than the summit because $J x$ is larger there.
