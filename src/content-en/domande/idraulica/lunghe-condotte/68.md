A pipeline between two reservoirs passes over a crest where the gauge pressure falls below its permissible value with the pipe unrestricted. The discharge is to be reduced with a gate valve to raise pressure at the crest. Where should the valve be placed?

- Immediately after the entrance, upstream of the summit
- At the summit
- Downstream of the summit
- At any section, because the discharge is the same throughout the pipe

## Explanation

Reducing discharge reduces the energy gradient, so the hydraulic grade line falls less along the pipe. The valve adds a vertical drop in the grade line only downstream of its location. If the valve is **downstream of the summit**, the summit head is $z_1 - J x_c$ with the reduced gradient and the summit pressure rises. With the valve upstream of the summit, its head loss would already have been deducted there. At the same discharge the summit pressure would be lower.
