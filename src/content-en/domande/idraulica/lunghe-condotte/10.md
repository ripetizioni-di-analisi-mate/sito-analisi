A pump delivers $Q = 55\,\mathrm{L/s}$ through a static lift of $36\,\mathrm{m}$ and through the discharge pipe shown, of length $1300\,\mathrm{m}$ with $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda = 0.023$. What pump head is required?

::schema{nodi="A,1.6,1,-;P,3.6,1,-;C,7.2,4,-;U,10.4,4,-" acqua="0,1.4 1.6,1.4 1.6,-0.4 0,-0.4;10.4,5.2 12,5.2 12,3.6 10.4,3.6" solidi="0,-0.7 1.6,-0.7 1.6,-0.4 0,-0.4;10.4,3.3 12,3.3 12,3.6 10.4,3.6" condotte="A-P-C-U,0.16" pompa="3.6,1,P" pelo="0,1.6,1.4,upstream;10.4,12,5.2,downstream" tratteggi="1.6,1.4 13,1.4;10.4,5.2 13,5.2" quote="13 1.4,13 5.2,36.0 m" etichette="8.6,3.1,D=250 mm;8.6,2.5,L=1300 m" titolo="Pumping system with $Q = 55\,\mathrm{L/s}$"}

- $36.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $28.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $43.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $51.3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The mean velocity is $v = \dfrac{0.055}{0.0491} = 1.12\,\mathrm{m/s}$, giving the following energy gradient in the discharge main:
$$
J = 0.023 \cdot \frac{1}{0.250} \cdot \frac{1.12^2}{2 \cdot 9.81} = 5.89 \cdot 10^{-3}
$$
The total head loss is $\Delta H = 5.89 \cdot 10^{-3} \cdot 1300 = 7.65\,\mathrm{m}$. The pump must supply the static lift plus this loss, so
$$
H_p = (z_2 - z_1) + \Delta H = 36 + 7.65 = 43.7\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The hydraulic output power is $P_u = \gamma Q H_p = 9.81 \cdot 0.055 \cdot 43.7 = 23.6\,\mathrm{kW}$. The value $36.0\,\mathrm{m}$ accounts only for static lift and would be the required head if there were no losses.
