A penstock of resistance $r = 250\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$ supplies a turbine under a gross head of $90\,\mathrm{m}$ between reservoir surfaces, giving a net head $H_t = 90 - r Q^2$ at the turbine. At what discharge is the power $\gamma\,Q\,H_t$ delivered to the turbine greatest?

- $0.600\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $0.424\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $0.346\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $0.120\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$

## Explanation

The hydraulic power delivered to the turbine is $P(Q) = \gamma\,Q\,(90 - 250\,Q^2) = \gamma\,(90\,Q - 250\,Q^3)$. Its derivative
$$
P'(Q) = \gamma\,(90 - 750\,Q^2)
$$
vanishes when $Q^2 = \dfrac{90}{750} = 0.12$. The derivative is positive below this discharge and negative above it, so the maximum occurs at $Q = \sqrt{0.12} = 0.346\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The head loss at this operating point is $250 \cdot 0.12 = 30\,\mathrm{m}$, one third of the gross head. The value $0.600\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ is the discharge at which friction consumes the entire gross head and turbine power falls to zero. The value $0.120\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ omits the square root.
