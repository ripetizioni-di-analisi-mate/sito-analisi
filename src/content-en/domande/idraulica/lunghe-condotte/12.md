A pump has characteristic curve $H_p = 62 - 1100\,Q^2$, with $Q$ in $\mathrm{m^3/s}$ and $H_p$ in meters. It lifts water through a static head of $38\,\mathrm{m}$ and a pipe of hydraulic resistance $r = 2902\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$. What is the discharge at the operating point?

- $148\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $90.9\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $124\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $77.4\,\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

At the operating point the head supplied by the pump equals the head required by the system, so set
$$
62 - 1100\,Q^2 = 38 + 2902\,Q^2 .
$$
Collecting the terms in $Q^2$ gives
$$
Q^2 = \frac{62 - 38}{1100 + 2902} = \frac{24}{4002} = 5.997 \cdot 10^{-3} ,
$$
Thus $Q = 0.0774\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, or $77.4\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The operating head is $H = 38 + 2902 \cdot 5.997 \cdot 10^{-3} = 55.4\,\mathrm{m}$, which agrees with the value on the pump curve, $62 - 1100 \cdot 5.997 \cdot 10^{-3} = 55.4\,\mathrm{m}$. The velocity is $1.58\,\mathrm{m/s}$, within the usual range for a discharge main.
