A sharp-crested rectangular weir of width $b = 0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ is installed in a channel of width $1.50\,\mathrm{m}$, so the nappe contracts on both sides. The upstream head is $h = 0.20\,\mathrm{m}$. What effective width is used in the Francis formula?

- $0.560\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.600\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.640\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.520\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The weir does not span the whole channel. The nappe contracts at both ends, making the effective discharge width less than the crest width. The Francis correction gives the effective width $b - 0.2\,h$. With $b = 0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ and $h = 0.20\,\mathrm{m}$, this yields $0.60 - 0.04 = 0.560\,\mathrm{m}$. Alternatively, the full crest width can be used with a lower discharge coefficient taken from experimental tables.
