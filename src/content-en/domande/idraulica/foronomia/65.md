A sluice gate raised $a = 0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ passes a measured free discharge $Q = 2.80\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ in a rectangular channel of width $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$. The upstream depth is $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and the jet contraction coefficient is $C_c = 0.61$. What head loss occurs between the approach section and the vena contracta?

- $0.078\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.347\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.103\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At the upstream section and the vena contracta the streamlines are straight and parallel. The pressure distribution is hydrostatic, so the piezometric head above the bed equals the water depth. Upstream the velocity is $v_1 = \dfrac{2.80}{2.00 \cdot 2.00} = 0.700\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the total head is $H_1 = 2.00 + 0.025 = 2.025\,\mathrm{m}$. At the vena contracta the depth is $a_c = C_c\,a = 0.244\,\mathrm{m}$ and the velocity is $v_c = \dfrac{2.80}{2.00 \cdot 0.244} = 5.74\,\mathrm{m/s}$, giving $H_2 = 0.244 + 1.678 = 1.922\,\mathrm{m}$. The head loss is therefore $\Delta H = H_1 - H_2 = 0.103\,\mathrm{m}$, approximately $5\%$ of the upstream head. Neglecting the upstream velocity head would give $0.078\,\mathrm{m}$.
