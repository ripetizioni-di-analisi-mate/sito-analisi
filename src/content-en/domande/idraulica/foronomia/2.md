## Explanation

The air above the water is pressurized. The effective head at the orifice is the air gauge pressure expressed as head of water plus the difference in elevation between the surface and the orifice. Since $\dfrac{p_0}{\gamma} = \dfrac{35}{9.81} = 3.57\,\mathrm{m}$, this gives $h = 3.57 + 1.80 = 5.37\,\mathrm{m}$. The area is $A = \dfrac{\pi D^2}{4} = 2.83 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $\sqrt{2 g h} = 10.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$, giving $Q = 0.60 \cdot 2.83 \cdot 10^{-3} \cdot 10.3 = 0.0174\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, or $17.4\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Ignoring air pressure would give $10.1\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
