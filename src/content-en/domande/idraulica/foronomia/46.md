A rectangular orifice of width $200\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ is cut below the free surface in a thin vertical tank wall. Approximately how far from the wall is the vena contracta?

- $100\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $80\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Streamlines approach the opening at an angle to its axis, so the jet continues to contract beyond the wall until it reaches the vena contracta. Experiments place this section about half the *smallest* orifice dimension downstream of the wall. Here the smallest dimension is the height $80\,\mathrm{mm}$, so the vena contracta is approximately $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the wall. Half the width would give $100\,\mathrm{mm}$.
