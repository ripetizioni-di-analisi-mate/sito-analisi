## Explanation

The sharp-crested weir has an aerated nappe, so Bazin’s formula $Q = \mu\,b\,h\,\sqrt{2 g h}$ applies. With $h = 0.32\,\mathrm{m}$, the coefficient is $\sqrt{2 g h} = 2.51\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Substitution gives $Q = 0.42 \cdot 1.50 \cdot 0.32 \cdot 2.51 = 0.505\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The factor $h$ before the square root comes from integrating over the nappe depth. Omitting it would give $1.58\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.
