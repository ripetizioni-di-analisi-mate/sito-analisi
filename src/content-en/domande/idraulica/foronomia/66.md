In a rectangular channel a sluice gate is raised $a = 0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ above the bed with upstream depth $2.20\,\mathrm{m}$ and free outflow. Its contraction coefficient is $C_c = 0.61$. What is the gauge pressure on the bed at the vena contracta?

- $2.39\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $3.92\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $21.6\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

At the vena contracta the jet depth is $a_c = C_c\,a = 0.61 \cdot 0.40 = 0.244\,\mathrm{m}$ and the streamlines are straight and parallel, giving a hydrostatic pressure distribution. The free surface has zero gauge pressure, so the bed pressure is $p = \gamma\,a_c = 9.81 \cdot 0.244 = 2.39\,\mathrm{kPa}$. In a free jet from a wall orifice, the entire jet boundary is exposed to the atmosphere and gauge pressure is effectively zero across the vena contracta. Here the jet flows along the channel bed. Using the gate opening $a$ instead of the contracted depth would give $3.92\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
