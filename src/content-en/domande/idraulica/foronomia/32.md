A tank of constant plan area drains completely through a bottom orifice with no inflow. Its initial discharge is $Q_0 = 24.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$. What is the average discharge over the entire draining time?

- $17.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $12.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $8.00\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $24.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

The average discharge is the drained volume divided by the elapsed time, $Q_m = \dfrac{\Omega\left(h_0 - h_1\right)}{t}$. Substituting the draining-time formula and using $h_0 - h_1 = \left(\sqrt{h_0} - \sqrt{h_1}\right)\left(\sqrt{h_0} + \sqrt{h_1}\right)$ gives $Q_m = \dfrac{Q_0 + Q_1}{2}$, the arithmetic mean of the initial and final discharges. For complete drainage the final head is zero, so $Q_1 = 0$ and $Q_m = \dfrac{Q_0}{2} = 12.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The draining time is therefore twice the time required to discharge the same volume at the initial flow rate.
