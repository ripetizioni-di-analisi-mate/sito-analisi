An inverted conical tank has walls inclined at $45^\circ$, so the free-surface radius equals its height above the apex, as shown. An orifice of diameter $D = 50\,\mathrm{mm}$ with $\mu = 0.60$ is located at the apex. There is no inflow and the initial water level is $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ above the opening. How long does it take to empty?

::schema{solidi="-2.75,2.4 -2.45,2.4 -0.06,0 -0.06,-0.3;0.06,-0.3 0.06,0 2.45,2.4 2.75,2.4" acqua="-2,2 2,2 0.06,0.06 0.06,0 -0.06,0 -0.06,0.06;-0.06,0 0.06,0 0.04,-0.9 -0.04,-0.9" pelo="-2,2,2," tratteggi="0,0 0,2.6" quote="2.4 0,2.4 2,2.00 m,0.6;0 2,2 2,r = h,0.4" riferimento=0 titolo="Conical tank with its apex downward and an orifice at the apex. The radius of the free surface equals its height above the orifice"}

- $6811\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2270\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1362\,\mathrm{s}$
- $681\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

When the head is $h$, the free-surface area is $\Omega(h) = \pi h^2$. During time $dt$, the decrease in stored volume $\Omega(h)\,(-dh)$ equals the volume discharged $\mu A\sqrt{2gh}\;dt$. Separating variables and integrating from $0$ to $h_0$ gives
$$
t = \frac{\pi}{\mu A \sqrt{2g}}\int_0^{h_0} h^{\frac{3}{2}}\,dh = \frac{2\pi\,h_0^{\frac{5}{2}}}{5\,\mu A \sqrt{2g}} .
$$
With $\mu A\sqrt{2g} = 5.22 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{5/2}/s}$ and $h_0^{\frac{5}{2}} = 5.66\,\mathrm{m^{5/2}}$, the result is $t = 1362\,\mathrm{s}$, or approximately $23\,\mathrm{min}$. Using the constant-area tank formula with the initial surface area $12.6\,\mathrm{m^2}$ would give $6811\,\mathrm{s}$. In the conical tank, the surface area decreases as the tank drains.
