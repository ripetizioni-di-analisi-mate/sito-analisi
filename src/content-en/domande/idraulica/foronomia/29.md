At the vena contracta downstream of a free-flowing sluice gate in a rectangular channel, the water depth is $a_c = 0.28\,\mathrm{m}$ and the mean speed $v_c = 6.64\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the Froude number, and what is the flow regime?

- $Fr = 0.250$, subcritical flow
- $Fr = 2.83$, supercritical flow
- $Fr = 4.01$, supercritical flow
- $Fr = 0.353$, subcritical flow

## Explanation

In a rectangular channel the top width equals the bed width, so the hydraulic depth equals the flow depth $Fr = \dfrac{v}{\sqrt{g\,h}}$. We obtain $\sqrt{g\,a_c} = \sqrt{9.81 \cdot 0.28} = 1.66\,\mathrm{m/s}$, giving $Fr = \dfrac{6.64}{1.66} = 4.01$. Since $Fr > 1$, the flow is **supercritical** and a hydraulic jump connects it to the downstream flow. The quantity under the square root is $g\,h$ because $\sqrt{g\,h}$ is the speed of small surface disturbances. Using $\sqrt{2 g\,h}$ would give $2.83$.
