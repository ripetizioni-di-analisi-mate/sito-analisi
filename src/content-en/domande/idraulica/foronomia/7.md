## Explanation

The orifice is submerged on both sides, so the driving head is the difference between the two free-surface elevations. Its area is $A = \ell^2 = 0.0225\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $\Delta h = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ gives $\sqrt{2 g\,\Delta h} = 4.85\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The discharge is $Q = \mu\,A\,\sqrt{2 g\,\Delta h} = 0.60 \cdot 0.0225 \cdot 4.85 = 0.0655\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, or $65.5\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Treating the side of the square as a circle diameter would give $51.4\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
