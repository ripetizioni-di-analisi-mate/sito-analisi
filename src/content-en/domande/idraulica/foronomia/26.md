A vertical sluice gate in a rectangular channel of width $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ passes a free discharge $Q = 2.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The upstream depth is $h = 2.20\,\mathrm{m}$ above the bed and the discharge coefficient is $\mu = 0.58$. How far is the gate raised?

- $0.152\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.262\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.371\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.525\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For free discharge, $Q = \mu\,b\,a\,\sqrt{2 g h}$. Solving for the gate opening gives $a = \dfrac{Q}{\mu\,b\,\sqrt{2 g h}}$. With $h = 2.20\,\mathrm{m}$, we obtain $\sqrt{2 g h} = 6.57\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and hence $a = \dfrac{2.00}{0.58 \cdot 2.00 \cdot 6.57} = 0.262\,\mathrm{m}$. The ratio $\dfrac{a}{h} = 0.119$ is less than $\dfrac{1}{3}$, so the assumed coefficient is consistent with the experimental range. Omitting the discharge coefficient would give $0.152\,\mathrm{m}$.
