A submerged orifice connects two tanks with constant surface elevations $5.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and $3.20\,\mathrm{m}$ above its center. Which head enters the square root in the discharge formula?

- $5.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The orifice is submerged on both sides. The pressure at the vena contracta therefore equals the hydrostatic pressure in the surrounding downstream liquid, which is nearly at rest near the orifice. Applying Bernoulli’s equation from the upstream free surface to the vena contracta gives the difference in piezometric heads, equal to the difference between the free-surface elevations. Thus the head under the square root is $\Delta h = 5.00 - 3.20 = 1.80\,\mathrm{m}$ and the discharge is $Q = \mu\,A\,\sqrt{2 g\,\Delta h}$.
