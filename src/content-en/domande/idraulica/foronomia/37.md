A designer is deciding whether a thin-plate weir has a V-notch or a rectangular notch. The vertex or crest in either case would be at the same elevation. With constant discharge coefficient, increasing head by a factor of $4$ increases flow by a factor of $32$. Which law fits the observations?

- The rectangular-notch law with $Q\propto h^{3/2}$.
- Both, because flow is proportional to wetted area.
- The V-notch law with $Q\propto h^{5/2}$.
- The small fixed-area orifice law with $Q\propto h^{1/2}$.

## Explanation

From $4^n=32$ one gets $n=5/2$. Widening of the V-notch with height contributes one more power of $h$ than a rectangular crest.
