Two closed water vessels communicate through a fully submerged orifice. The free surface in A is $0.60\,\mathrm m$ above that in B. Gas pressure above B exceeds that above A by $8.0\,\mathrm{kPa}$. With $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, which way does the water initially flow when the orifice opens?

The water surface is higher in A and the gas pressure is higher in B."}

- From B to A, because the additional pressure head in B exceeds the elevation difference.
- From A to B, because A has the higher surface.
- There is no flow because both sides are submerged.
- The direction depends only on the orifice discharge coefficient.

## Explanation

The gas adds $8000/(\rho g)\simeq0.815\,\mathrm m$ of head on side B. After subtracting A's $0.60\,\mathrm m$ elevation advantage, about $0.215\,\mathrm m$ remains in B's favour.
