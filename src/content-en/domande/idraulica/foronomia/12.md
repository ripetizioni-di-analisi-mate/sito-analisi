A sharp-crested Thomson V-notch weir of included angle $\theta = 90^\circ$ and discharge coefficient $\mu = 0.60$ measures discharge $Q = 32\,\mathrm{L/s}$. What head is measured above the notch vertex?

- $0.0799\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.150\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.171\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.220\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The triangular weir satisfies $Q = \dfrac{8}{15}\,\mu\,\tan\dfrac{\theta}{2}\,\sqrt{2g}\;h^{\frac{5}{2}}$. Substituting $\theta = 90^\circ$ gives $\tan\dfrac{\theta}{2} = 1$. The numerical coefficient is $\dfrac{8}{15} \cdot 0.60 \cdot \sqrt{2g} = 1.417$, giving $Q = 1.417\,h^{\frac{5}{2}}$ with $Q$ in $\mathrm{m^3/s}$ and $h$ in meters. Inverting this relation gives $h = \left(\dfrac{0.032}{1.417}\right)^{\frac{2}{5}} = 0.220\,\mathrm{m}$. Exponent $\dfrac{2}{3}$ belongs to a rectangular weir and would give $0.0799\,\mathrm{m}$.
