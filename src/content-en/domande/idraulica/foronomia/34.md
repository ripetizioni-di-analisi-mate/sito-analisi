Two tanks of constant plan areas $\Omega_1 = 16.0\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $\Omega_2 = 8.00\,\mathrm{m^2}$ communicate through a submerged orifice. Their initial level difference is $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$. How far does the surface in the first tank fall before the levels equalize?

- $0.600\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.900\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Let $V$ be the volume transferred. The first tank’s level falls by $\dfrac{V}{\Omega_1}$ and the second tank’s level rises by $\dfrac{V}{\Omega_2}$. Their sum is the decrease in the elevation difference, $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$. Equation $V\left(\dfrac{1}{16.0} + \dfrac{1}{8.00}\right) = 1.80$ gives $V = \dfrac{\Omega_1\,\Omega_2}{\Omega_1 + \Omega_2} \cdot 1.80 = 9.60\,\mathrm{m^3}$. The first level thus falls by $\dfrac{9.60}{16.0} = 0.600\,\mathrm{m}$ and the second rises by $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$. Their sum is indeed $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$.
