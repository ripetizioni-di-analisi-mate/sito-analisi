A circular orifice of diameter $D = 80\,\mathrm{mm}$ in a thin vertical wall produces a jet with vena contracta diameter $D_c = 62\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the contraction coefficient?

- $0.601$
- $0.775$
- $0.225$
- $1.66$

## Explanation

The contraction coefficient is the ratio of the jet area at the vena contracta to the orifice area, so $C_c = \dfrac{A_c}{A} = \left(\dfrac{D_c}{D}\right)^2$. Substitution gives $C_c = \left(\dfrac{62}{80}\right)^2 = 0.775^2 = 0.601$. This is close to $0.6$, the typical experimental value for a sharp-edged circular orifice. The diameter ratio alone is $0.775$. The area ratio uses the squares of the diameters.
