A circular orifice of diameter $D = 80\,\mathrm{mm}$ is cut in a wall. What is the greatest wall thickness for which it is classified as a thin-wall orifice?

- $160\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $80\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

A thin wall is less than about half as thick as the smallest dimension of the opening. The liquid then touches only the upstream edge, and the jet contracts freely beyond it. For a circular orifice the smallest dimension is its diameter, giving a limiting thickness of $\dfrac{D}{2} = 40\,\mathrm{mm}$. In a thicker wall the jet reattaches to the bore, and a different discharge coefficient applies.
