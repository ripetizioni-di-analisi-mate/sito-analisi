The anticipated head over a sharp-crested rectangular weir is about $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$. At what minimum distance upstream of the crest should the head be measured to avoid drawdown effects?

- $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$
- At the crest itself

## Explanation

The water surface draws down as the flow approaches the crest, so a reading taken too close to the crest underestimates the head. The discharge error is amplified. With $Q = k\,h^{\frac{3}{2}}$, the relative discharge error is $\dfrac{3}{2}$ times the relative head error. Measure the head at least four head lengths upstream of the crest, before drawdown is appreciable. For a head of about $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$, the minimum distance is $4 \cdot 0.25 = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$.
