A nozzle discharges a free jet at flow rate $Q = 40.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$ with uniform exit speed $v = 28.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the jet power?

- $7.84\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $15.7\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $11.0\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $31.4\,\mathrm{kW}$

## Explanation

At the outlet the gauge pressure is zero and the section lies at the datum elevation, so the total jet head equals the velocity head $\dfrac{v^2}{2g}$. Power is the weight flow rate multiplied by the total head, giving $P = \gamma\,Q\,\dfrac{v^2}{2g} = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\rho\,Q\,v^2$. Substitution yields $P = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 1000 \cdot 0.0400 \cdot 28.0^2 = 15.7\,\mathrm{kW}$. Using $\dfrac{v^2}{g}$ instead of velocity head would give $31.4\,\mathrm{kW}$.
