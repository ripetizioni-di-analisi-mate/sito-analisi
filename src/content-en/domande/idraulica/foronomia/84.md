The tailwater rises sufficiently above the crest to submerge a full-width broad-crested weir. Which statement is correct?

- Discharge still depends only on upstream level, with $\mu = 0.385$
- Discharge also depends on tailwater level, and the crest is no longer a control section
- Flow on the crest remains critical, so discharge is unchanged
- The weir passes more water because water also flows below the tailwater surface

## Explanation

When the tailwater rises sufficiently above the crest, the weir is drowned. Flow over the crest then remains subcritical and its depth is controlled by the downstream level. The free-flow relation between discharge and upstream level relied on the crest condition $h_c = \dfrac{2}{3}\,H$. Once that condition is lost, discharge depends **on the downstream level as well**. The weir serves as a control section and flowmeter while tailwater remains low enough to avoid drowning it.
