To derive the sharp-crested rectangular weir equation, sum the contributions of horizontal nappe strips, each with coefficient $\mu' = 0.63$. What is the coefficient $\mu$ in the discharge formula $Q = \mu\,b\,h\sqrt{2gh}$?

- $0.63$
- $0.945$
- $0.315$
- $0.42$

## Explanation

Integrating the discharge contribution $\mu'\,b\,\sqrt{2gy}\;dy$ of a strip at depth $y$ over the nappe depth gives $Q = \dfrac{2}{3}\,\mu'\,b\,\sqrt{2g}\;h^{\frac{3}{2}}$. Since $\sqrt{2g}\;h^{\frac{3}{2}} = h\sqrt{2gh}$, comparison with the form $Q = \mu\,b\,h\sqrt{2gh}$ gives $\mu = \dfrac{2}{3}\,\mu'$. With $\mu' = 0.63$ the result is $\mu = 0.42$, the value commonly used in Bazin’s equation. Multiplying by $\dfrac{3}{2}$ instead of $\dfrac{2}{3}$ would give $0.945$, close to the coefficient for a rounded-entry orifice.
