A tank of constant plan area $\Omega = 15.0\,\mathrm{m^2}$ receives a constant inflow $Q_e = 25\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and discharges to the atmosphere through a circular bottom orifice of diameter $D = 100\,\mathrm{mm}$ with $\mu = 0.60$. At a given instant the head over the opening is $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$. Which statement about the change in level is correct?

- The level falls toward the equilibrium head $1.43\,\mathrm{m}$
- The level rises because the tank receives an inflow
- The level stays at $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ because the tank receives an inflow
- The level falls until the tank empties

## Explanation

At head $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$, the orifice discharge $Q = \mu A\sqrt{2gh} = 0.60 \cdot 7.85 \cdot 10^{-3} \cdot 7.00 = 33.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$ exceeds the inflow, so the stored volume decreases and the water level **falls**. As the level falls, the head and outflow decrease until the outflow equals the inflow. The equilibrium head follows from $\mu A\sqrt{2gh_e} = Q_e$, giving
$$
h_e = \frac{1}{2g}\left(\frac{Q_e}{\mu A}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{19.62}\left(\frac{0.025}{4.71 \cdot 10^{-3}}\right)^2\,\mathrm{m} = 1.43\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The level approaches this value without passing below it. Below $h_e$ the inflow would exceed the outflow and the level would rise.
