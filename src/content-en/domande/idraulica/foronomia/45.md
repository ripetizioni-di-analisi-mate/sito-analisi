A contoured nozzle of velocity coefficient $C_v = 0.96$ and contraction coefficient $C_c = 1$ receives discharge $Q$ at total head $H$ above the outlet axis. What fraction of the available power $\gamma\,Q\,H$ appears in the jet?

- $88\%$
- $96\%$
- $92\%$
- $98\%$

## Explanation

The jet velocity is $v = C_v\sqrt{2 g H}$, so the jet velocity head is $\dfrac{v^2}{2g} = C_v^2\,H$. The jet power is therefore $P = \gamma\,Q\,C_v^2\,H$, and its ratio to the available power $\gamma\,Q\,H$ is $C_v^2$. With $C_v = 0.96$, this gives $C_v^2 = 0.922$, or $92\%$. The remaining power is dissipated in the nozzle.
