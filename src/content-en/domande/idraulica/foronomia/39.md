Two side-by-side V-notch weirs have their vertices at the same elevation, the same head and the same discharge coefficient. The first has $\tan(\theta_1/2)=1$, the second $\tan(\theta_2/2)=1/2$. What fraction of the total discharge passes through the first?

- $1/2$
- $2/3$
- $1/3$
- $4/5$

## Explanation

A V-notch rating contains the product $\tan(\theta/2)h^{5/2}$. Equal remaining factors give $Q_1=2Q_2$, and $Q_1/(Q_1+Q_2)=2/3$.
