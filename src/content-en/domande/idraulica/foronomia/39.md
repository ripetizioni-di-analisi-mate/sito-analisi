A sharp-crested V-notch weir has its vertex at the bottom, a vertical axis, included angle $\theta = 60^\circ$, and discharge coefficient $\mu = 0.60$. What discharge corresponds to head $h = 0.25\,\mathrm{m}$ above the vertex?

- $12.8\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $25.6\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $44.3\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $76.7\,\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

Thomson’s formula is $Q = \dfrac{8}{15}\,\mu\,\tan\dfrac{\theta}{2}\,\sqrt{2g}\;h^{\frac{5}{2}}$, where $\dfrac{\theta}{2}$ is the half-angle of the notch. With $\theta = 60^\circ$, we obtain $\tan 30^\circ = 0.577$ and the numerical coefficient $\dfrac{8}{15} \cdot 0.60 \cdot 0.577 \cdot \sqrt{2g} = 0.818$. Since $0.25^{\frac{5}{2}} = 0.03125$, the result is $Q = 0.818 \cdot 0.03125 = 0.0256\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, or $25.6\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Using $\tan 60^\circ$ would give $76.7\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
