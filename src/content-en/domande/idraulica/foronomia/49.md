A constant-level tank discharges to the atmosphere through a circular thin-wall orifice of diameter $D = 20\,\mathrm{mm}$ with head $h = 0.15\,\mathrm{m}$ at its center. Taking $C_c = 0.62$ and $C_v = 0.97$, what is the Reynolds number at the vena contracta?

- $3.33 \cdot 10^{4}$
- $1.31 \cdot 10^{4}$
- $2.62 \cdot 10^{4}$
- $1.85 \cdot 10^{4}$

## Explanation

At the vena contracta the velocity is $v_c = C_v\sqrt{2gh} = 0.97 \cdot 1.72 = 1.66\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The contraction coefficient gives the jet diameter through $C_c = \left(\dfrac{D_c}{D}\right)^2$, so $D_c = D\sqrt{C_c} = 15.7\,\mathrm{mm}$. This gives $Re = \dfrac{v_c\,D_c}{\nu} = \dfrac{1.66 \cdot 0.0157}{10^{-6}} = 2.62 \cdot 10^{4}$. The value exceeds $10^4$, so the jet is turbulent and the experimental coefficients for sharp-edged orifices still apply at this low head. Using the orifice diameter in place of the jet diameter would give $3.33 \cdot 10^{4}$.
