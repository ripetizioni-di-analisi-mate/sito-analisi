A large tank discharges to atmosphere through two small openings with the same discharge coefficient. The openings lie at depths $h$ and $4h$. What ratio of the deeper to the shallower opening area gives equal volume flow rates?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Ideal speed is proportional to the square root of depth and is twice as high at the deeper hole. In $C_d A_1\sqrt{2gh}=C_d A_2\sqrt{2g(4h)}$, this requires $A_2=A_1/2$.
