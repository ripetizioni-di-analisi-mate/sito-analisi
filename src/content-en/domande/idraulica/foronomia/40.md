The measured head above a sharp-crested rectangular weir is $h = 0.25\,\mathrm{m}$, with a reading error of $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. What relative discharge error does this imply?

- $0.53\%$
- $0.80\%$
- $2.0\%$
- $1.2\%$

## Explanation

The rectangular-weir equation takes the form $Q = k\,h^{n}$ with $n = \dfrac{3}{2}$. Differentiation gives $\dfrac{dQ}{Q} = n\,\dfrac{dh}{h}$. The relative error in the head is $\dfrac{0.002}{0.25} = 0.80\%$, giving a relative discharge error of $\dfrac{3}{2} \cdot 0.80\% = 1.2\%$. The exponent $\dfrac{5}{2}$ applies to a V-notch weir and would give $2.0\%$ for the same head and reading error.
