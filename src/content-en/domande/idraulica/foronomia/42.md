A sharp-edged broad-crested weir of width $b = 2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and crest length $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries discharge $Q = 1.50\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ in a rectangular channel, as shown. Approach velocity is negligible and its discharge coefficient is $\mu = 0.385$. How far above the crest is the upstream surface?

::schema{solidi="0,-0.35 7.4,-0.35 7.4,0 0,0;3,0 5,0 5,1.2 3,1.2" acqua="0,0 3,0 3,1.8 0,1.8;3,1.2 5,1.2 5,1.5 3,1.8;5,1.5 5.4,1.15 5.75,0.3 5.85,0 5.35,0 5.3,0.45 5.15,0.9 5,1.2" pelo="0,3,1.8,;3,5,1.5," tratteggi="2.2,1.2 3,1.2" quote="3 0,5 0,2.00 m,0.6" titolo="Broad-crested sill of width $b = 2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and crest length $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ with sharp crest edges"}

- $0.498\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.332\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.470\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.659\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For a broad-crested weir, $Q = \mu\,b\,H\,\sqrt{2 g H} = \mu\,b\,\sqrt{2g}\;H^{\frac{3}{2}}$, giving $H^{\frac{3}{2}} = \dfrac{Q}{\mu\,b\,\sqrt{2g}}$. Substitution gives $H^{\frac{3}{2}} = \dfrac{1.50}{0.385 \cdot 2.50 \cdot 4.43} = 0.352$. Raising this to the power $\dfrac{2}{3}$ gives $H = 0.498\,\mathrm{m}$. The crest length in the flow direction exceeds three times this head, so the nappe reattaches and the broad-crested formula applies. As a check, the critical depth is $h_c = \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{Q^2}{g\,b^2}} = 0.332\,\mathrm{m}$ and satisfies $H = \dfrac{3}{2}\,h_c$, as required by conservation of specific energy between the upstream section and the critical section on the crest.
