A fire hose ends in a contoured nozzle with $C_v = 0.96$ and $C_c = 1$. Total head immediately upstream is $H = 30.0\,\mathrm{m}$ relative to the exit axis. The jet leaves at angle $45^\circ$ above the horizontal. Neglecting air resistance, at what horizontal distance does the jet return to nozzle elevation?

- $55.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $27.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $39.1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $111\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The nozzle exit speed is $v = C_v\sqrt{2gH} = 0.96 \cdot 24.3 = 23.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Beyond the nozzle, gravity acts alone and the jet follows a parabola. A particle launched at speed $v$ and angle $\alpha$ returns to its starting elevation at a horizontal range $\dfrac{v^2\sin 2\alpha}{g}$. With $\alpha = 45^\circ$ we obtain $\sin 2\alpha = 1$, giving the range $\dfrac{v^2}{g} = 2\,C_v^2\,H = 2 \cdot 0.9216 \cdot 30.0 = 55.3\,\mathrm{m}$. Using the velocity head $\dfrac{v^2}{2g}$ in place of $\dfrac{v^2}{g}$ would give $27.6\,\mathrm{m}$.
