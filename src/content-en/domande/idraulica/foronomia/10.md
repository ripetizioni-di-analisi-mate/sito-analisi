Two tanks of constant plan areas $\Omega_1 = 20.0\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $\Omega_2 = 12.0\,\mathrm{m^2}$ communicate through a submerged orifice, as shown. The initial difference in surface levels is $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$. What volume passes from the first tank to the second before their levels become equal?

- $18.75\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $30.0\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $50.0\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $80.0\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

Let $V$ be the volume transferred. The first tank level falls by $\dfrac{V}{\Omega_1}$ and the second rises by $\dfrac{V}{\Omega_2}$, so their level difference decreases by the sum of those changes. At equilibrium the levels coincide. Their changes must therefore add to $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$, giving $V\left(\dfrac{1}{20.0} + \dfrac{1}{12.0}\right) = 2.50$ and hence $V = \dfrac{\Omega_1\,\Omega_2}{\Omega_1 + \Omega_2} \cdot 2.50 = 7.50 \cdot 2.50 = 18.75\,\mathrm{m^3}$. As a check, the first level falls by $0.938\,\mathrm{m}$ and the second rises by $1.56\,\mathrm{m}$, which add to $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$.
