The head above the vertex of a sharp-crested V-notch weir doubles, with discharge coefficient and included angle unchanged. By what factor does the discharge increase?

- $2$
- $2.83$
- $4$
- $5.66$

## Explanation

For a V-notch weir, $Q = \dfrac{8}{15}\,\mu\,\tan\dfrac{\theta}{2}\,\sqrt{2g}\;h^{\frac{5}{2}}$. With the discharge coefficient and notch angle held fixed, discharge varies as the head raised to the power $\dfrac{5}{2}$. Doubling the head therefore multiplies the discharge by $2^{\frac{5}{2}} = 5.66$. The exponent $\dfrac{5}{2}$ arises because the triangular opening widens with elevation. For a rectangular weir the exponent is $\dfrac{3}{2}$, giving a factor of $2^{\frac{3}{2}} = 2.83$.
