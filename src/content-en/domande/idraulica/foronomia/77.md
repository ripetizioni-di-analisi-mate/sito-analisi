A Thomson V-notch weir of included angle $\theta = 90^\circ$ must operate up to a maximum head $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ above its vertex. How wide is the nappe at the free surface at maximum head?

- $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.42\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.85\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At elevation $h$ above the vertex, each side of the notch is $h\tan\dfrac{\theta}{2}$ from the centerline, so the opening width is $2\,h\tan\dfrac{\theta}{2}$. With $\theta = 90^\circ$ we obtain $\tan 45^\circ = 1$, giving a width of $2 \cdot 0.30 = 0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ at the free surface. At depth $y$, this same width is $2\,(h - y)\tan\dfrac{\theta}{2}$, the expression integrated to obtain Thomson’s formula. Using $\sin 45^\circ$ in place of the tangent would give $0.42\,\mathrm{m}$, and counting just one half-width would give $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$.
