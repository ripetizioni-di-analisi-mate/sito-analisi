## Explanation

In the draining equation, measure head above the orifice centroid, which is $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ above the bottom. The initial and final heads are $h_0 = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and $h_1 = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$. The tank plan area is $\Omega = \dfrac{\pi \cdot 4.00^2}{4} = 12.6\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $\mu\,A\,\sqrt{2g} = 0.0212\,\mathrm{m^{5/2}/s}$. This gives $t = \dfrac{2\,\Omega\left(\sqrt{h_0} - \sqrt{h_1}\right)}{\mu\,A\,\sqrt{2g}} = \dfrac{2 \cdot 12.6 \cdot \left(1.732 - 1\right)}{0.0212} = 867\,\mathrm{s}$, or about $14.4\,\mathrm{min}$. Measuring both levels from the bottom would give $749\,\mathrm{s}$.
