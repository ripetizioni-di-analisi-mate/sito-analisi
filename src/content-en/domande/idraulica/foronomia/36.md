A rectangular sharp-crested weir with a horizontal crest of width $b = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ and a ventilated nappe must carry discharge $Q = 0.250\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Given discharge coefficient $\mu = 0.42$, what head is measured upstream?

- $0.112\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.335\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.417\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.232\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Bazin’s formula can be written as $Q = \mu\,b\,\sqrt{2g}\;h^{\frac{3}{2}}$, so $h^{\frac{3}{2}} = \dfrac{Q}{\mu\,b\,\sqrt{2g}}$. Substitution gives $h^{\frac{3}{2}} = \dfrac{0.250}{0.42 \cdot 1.20 \cdot 4.43} = 0.112$. Raising this value to the power $\dfrac{2}{3}$ yields $h = 0.112^{\frac{2}{3}} = 0.232\,\mathrm{m}$. Direct substitution verifies $Q = 0.42 \cdot 1.20 \cdot 0.232 \cdot \sqrt{2 g \cdot 0.232} = 0.250\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The exponent $\dfrac{2}{5}$ applies to a V-notch weir and would give $0.417\,\mathrm{m}$.
