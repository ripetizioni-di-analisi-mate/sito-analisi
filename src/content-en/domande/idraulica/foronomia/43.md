The upstream water surface is $H = 0.75\,\mathrm{m}$ above a horizontal broad-crested weir, with negligible approach velocity. What is the critical depth on the crest?

- $0.375\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.750\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.500\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Head losses and the upstream velocity head are negligible, so the specific energy referred to the crest is conserved and equals $E = H$. Flow changes from subcritical to supercritical over the crest, passing through critical depth. At critical depth the specific energy is a minimum, $E_{\min} = \dfrac{3}{2}h_c$. From $\dfrac{3}{2}h_c = H$ we obtain $h_c = \dfrac{2}{3} \cdot 0.75 = 0.500\,\mathrm{m}$, and the velocity over the crest is $\sqrt{g\,h_c} = 2.21\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
