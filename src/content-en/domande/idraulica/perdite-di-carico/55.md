A full square conduit of side $a = 0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ carries water in uniform flow with energy gradient $J = 0.0060$. What is the mean shear stress exerted by the wall on the water?

- $11.8\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $5.89\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $23.5\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $0.600\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

For uniform flow in a conduit of any cross-sectional shape, the mean wall shear stress is $\tau_0 = \gamma R J$, with hydraulic radius $R = \dfrac{A}{C}$. For a square conduit flowing full, the area is $a^2$ and the wetted perimeter is $4a$, so
$$
R = \frac{a^2}{4a} = \frac{a}{4} = 0.10\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The mean wall shear stress is therefore $\tau_0 = 9810 \cdot 0.10 \cdot 0.0060 = 5.89\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Taking $R = \dfrac{a}{2}$ as if it were a geometric radius would double the result. Substituting density for specific weight would give $0.600\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
