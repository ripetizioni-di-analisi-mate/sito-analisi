A pipe has two fittings. The first has a loss coefficient $K_1=2$ based on speed $v$. The second has $K_2=1$ based on its local speed $2v$. Which single coefficient based on $v$ reproduces the sum of the losses?

- $3$
- $4$
- $6$
- $10$

## Explanation

The losses add as $2v^2/(2g)+(2v)^2/(2g)$. Expressing both relative to the same velocity head gives $K_{\rm eq}=2+4=6$.
