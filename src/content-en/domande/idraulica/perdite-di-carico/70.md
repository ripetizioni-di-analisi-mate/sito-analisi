Water flows through a hydraulically smooth pipe at Reynolds number $Re = 1.0 \cdot 10^5$. Which friction factor satisfies Prandtl’s smooth-pipe relation?

- $0.0250$
- $0.000640$
- $0.0120$
- $0.0180$

## Explanation

Prandtl’s law for smooth pipes is $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{\lambda}} = 2\,\log_{10}\!\left( Re\,\sqrt{\lambda} \right) - 0.8$. A candidate friction factor must give the same value on both sides. For $\lambda = 0.0180$, the two sides are
$$
\frac{1}{\sqrt{0.0180}} = 7.45 ,
\qquad
2\,\log_{10}\!\left( 10^5 \cdot 0.134 \right) - 0.8 = 2 \cdot 4.13 - 0.8 = 7.46 ,
$$
so they agree to the accuracy of the calculation, giving $\lambda = 0.0180$. For $\lambda = 0.0250$, the two sides are $6.32$ and $7.60$. For $\lambda = 0.0120$, they are $9.13$ and $7.28$. Neither of these candidates satisfies the equation. The value $0.000640$ comes from $\dfrac{64}{Re}$, which applies only to laminar flow.
