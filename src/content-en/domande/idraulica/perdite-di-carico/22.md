Water and oil flow in geometrically similar circular pipes. The oil pipe has twice the diameter and the oil has ten times the kinematic viscosity. What ratio of oil speed to water speed gives the same Reynolds number?

- $20$
- $\dfrac15$
- $5$
- $\dfrac1{20}$

## Explanation

Equal Reynolds numbers require $v_{\rm oil}D_{\rm oil}/\nu_{\rm oil}=v_{\rm water}D_{\rm water}/\nu_{\rm water}$. The speed must therefore be multiplied by $10/2=5$ to preserve the relative influence of inertia and viscosity.
