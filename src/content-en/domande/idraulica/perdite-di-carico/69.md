Flow in a circular pipe of radius $r_0$ is laminar. What fraction of the discharge passes through the central disk of radius $\dfrac{r_0}{2}$?

- $25.0\,\%$
- $56.3\,\%$
- $43.8\,\%$
- $50.0\,\%$

## Explanation

The laminar velocity profile is $v(r) = c\,(r_0^2 - r^2)$, with constant $c$. The discharge through a central circle is found by integrating velocity over concentric rings of area $2\pi r\,dr$. The resulting fraction is
$$
\frac{Q\!\left( \frac{r_0}{2} \right)}{Q} = \frac{\displaystyle\int_0^{\frac{r_0}{2}} (r_0^2 - r^2)\,r\,dr}{\displaystyle\int_0^{r_0} (r_0^2 - r^2)\,r\,dr} = \frac{\dfrac{r_0^4}{8} - \dfrac{r_0^4}{64}}{\dfrac{r_0^4}{4}} = \frac{7}{16} .
$$
The central circle therefore carries $43.8\,\%$ of the discharge through only $25\,\%$ of the area because the velocities are highest near the centerline. A uniform velocity profile would give $25\,\%$. The outer annulus carries the remaining $56.3\,\%$ of the discharge.
