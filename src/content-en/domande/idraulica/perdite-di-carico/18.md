Water flows uniformly through a full circular pipe of diameter $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and length $L = 500\,\mathrm{m}$ with energy gradient $J = 0.0090$. What total tangential force does the water exert on the pipe wall?

- $0.993\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $1.56\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6.24\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $3.12\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

For a circular pipe flowing full, the hydraulic radius is $R = \dfrac{D}{4} = 0.0750\,\mathrm{m}$. The relation between **wall shear stress and the energy gradient** therefore gives
$$
\tau_0 = \gamma\,R\,J = 9810 \cdot 0.0750 \cdot 0.0090 = 6.62\,\mathrm{Pa} .
$$
The shear stress is uniform around the pipe circumference. The total force is therefore the shear stress times the wetted wall area, $\pi D L = 471\,\mathrm{m^2}$, giving
$$
F = \tau_0\,\pi\,D\,L = 6.62 \cdot 471 = 3.12\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The head loss gives the same result, since $\Delta H = J L = 4.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\gamma\,A\,\Delta H = 9810 \cdot 0.0707 \cdot 4.50 = 3.12\,\mathrm{kN}$. Using the pipe radius in place of the hydraulic radius would double the force to $6.24\,\mathrm{kN}$.
