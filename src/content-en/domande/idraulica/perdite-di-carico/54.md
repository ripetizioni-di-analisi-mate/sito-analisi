The figure shows a long pipeline between two reservoirs and its hydraulic grade line. At which of the centerline points $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$ is gauge pressure negative?

::schema{nodi="P,1,4,-;A,3,1;B,5.5,3.6;C,8,5.6;D,10.5,3.2;U,13,1.5,-" condotte="P-A-B-C-D-U,0.3" acqua="-2,2.5 1,2.5 1,7 -2,7;13,0.8 16,0.8 16,3 13,3" pelo="-2,1,7,;13,16,3," linee="-2,7.4 -2,2.5 1,2.5 1,7.4;13,3.4 13,0.8 16,0.8 16,3.4" piezometrica="1,7 13,3" etichette="3.4,7.0,hydraulic grade line" riferimento=0 titolo="Profile and hydraulic grade line of a long pipe between two reservoirs"}

- At point $A$
- At point $B$
- At point $C$
- At point $D$

## Explanation

The piezometric head at any section is $h = z + \dfrac{p}{\gamma}$. The pressure head, $\dfrac{p}{\gamma} = h - z$, is therefore the signed vertical distance from the pipe centerline to the hydraulic grade line. Gauge pressure is positive where the hydraulic grade line is above the centerline and negative where it is below it. At $A$, $B$, and $D$, the hydraulic grade line lies above the pipe, so gauge pressure is positive. It is highest at the lowest point, $A$. At the summit, $C$, the pipe centerline rises above the hydraulic grade line, so the gauge pressure at $C$ is **negative**.
