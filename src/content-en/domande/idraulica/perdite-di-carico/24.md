In a pipe of diameter $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ carrying $Q = 80\,\mathrm{L/s}$ of water, the measured energy gradient is $J = 0.0105$. What is the friction factor?

- $0.00485$
- $0.0194$
- $0.00970$
- $0.0776$

## Explanation

The mean velocity is $v = \dfrac{0.080}{0.0491} = 1.63\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so the velocity head is $\dfrac{v^2}{2g} = 0.135\,\mathrm{m}$. Solving the **Darcy–Weisbach** equation, $J = \lambda\dfrac{1}{D}\dfrac{v^2}{2g}$, for the Darcy friction factor gives
$$
\lambda = \frac{J\,D}{\dfrac{v^2}{2g}} = \frac{0.0105 \cdot 0.25}{0.135} = 0.0194 .
$$
The result lies within the usual range for pipes in service, between $0.01$ and $0.08$. Substituting the hydraulic radius $\dfrac{D}{4}$ for the diameter would give $0.00485$.
