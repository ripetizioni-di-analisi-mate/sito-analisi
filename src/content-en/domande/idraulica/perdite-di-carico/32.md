A concrete pipe of diameter $D = 400\,\mathrm{mm}$ and absolute roughness $\varepsilon = 2.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ operates in fully rough turbulent flow. What is the friction factor?

- $0.174$
- $0.00572$
- $0.0472$
- $0.0303$

## Explanation

The **Prandtl–Nikuradse** relation applies in the fully rough turbulent regime and is independent of the Reynolds number. The argument of the logarithm is
$$
\frac{3.71\,D}{\varepsilon} = \frac{3.71 \cdot 400}{2.0} = 742 ,
$$
and the base-ten logarithm of $742$ is $2.870$, so
$$
\frac{1}{\sqrt{\lambda}} = 2 \cdot 2.870 = 5.74
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\lambda = \frac{1}{5.74^2} = 0.0303 .
$$
This value lies within the usual range and is independent of discharge. Omitting the square would give $0.174$. Using the natural logarithm in place of the base-ten logarithm would give $0.00572$.
