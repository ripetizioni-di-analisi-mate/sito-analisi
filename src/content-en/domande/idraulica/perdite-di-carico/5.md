## Explanation

The pipe area is $A = \dfrac{\pi \cdot 0.125^2}{4} = 0.01227\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so the mean velocity is $v = \dfrac{0.022}{0.01227} = 1.79\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the velocity head is
$$
\frac{v^2}{2g} = \frac{1.79^2}{19.62} = 0.164\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
All the minor losses use the same reference velocity, so their coefficients can be added:
$$
\sum_i \xi_i = 0.5 + 0.3 + 0.3 + 0.3 + 1 = 2.40 .
$$
Since $\Delta H_{\mathrm{loc}} = \left( \sum_i \xi_i \right)\dfrac{v^2}{2g}$, the combined loss is $2.40 \cdot 0.164 = 0.393\,\mathrm{m}$. Omitting the exit, where the entire velocity head is dissipated, would give $0.229\,\mathrm{m}$.
