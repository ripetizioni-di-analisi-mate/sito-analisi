A steel pipe must carry $Q = 60\,\mathrm{L/s}$ of water with an energy gradient no greater than $J_{\max} = 0.004$. At diameter $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$, the friction factor is $\lambda = 0.0176$. Which of the standard diameters $200$, $250$, $300$, and $400\,\mathrm{mm}$ is the smallest that meets the gradient limit?

- $200\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $300\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $250\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $400\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

For $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ the mean velocity is $v = \dfrac{0.060}{0.0491} = 1.22\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the velocity head is $0.0762\,\mathrm{m}$ and the energy gradient is
$$
J = \lambda\,\frac{1}{D}\,\frac{v^2}{2g} = \frac{0.0176}{0.25} \cdot 0.0762 = 0.00536 .
$$
This exceeds $J_{\max}$, ruling out diameter $250\,\mathrm{mm}$ and, even more so, $200\,\mathrm{mm}$. At fixed discharge and friction factor the energy gradient varies as $D^{-5}$ because $v$ varies as $D^{-2}$. Thus, on increasing the diameter to $300\,\mathrm{mm}$, the energy gradient falls to
$$
0.00536 \cdot \left( \frac{250}{300} \right)^5 = 0.00215 ,
$$
which is less than $J_{\max}$. Select **$300\,\mathrm{mm}$**.
