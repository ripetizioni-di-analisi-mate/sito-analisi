A cast-iron pipe of diameter $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ has absolute roughness $\varepsilon = 0.50\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is its relative roughness?

- $0.00200$
- $0.00400$
- $0.000500$
- $2.00$

## Explanation

Relative roughness is the ratio of the mean height of the wall roughness elements to the pipe diameter. The two lengths must therefore be expressed in the same units, giving
$$
\frac{\varepsilon}{D} = \frac{0.50\,\mathrm{mm}}{250\,\mathrm{mm}} = 0.00200 .
$$
This ratio is dimensionless. Together with the Reynolds number, it determines the Darcy friction factor. Using the roughness in millimeters and the diameter in meters would give $2.00$, a thousand times the correct value.
