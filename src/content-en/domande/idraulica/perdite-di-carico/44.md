A gate valve with $\xi = 4.0$ is installed in a pipe of diameter $D = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ and friction factor $\lambda = 0.025$. What is its equivalent length?

- $0.500\,\mathrm{m}$
- $32.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.0200\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The equivalent length is the length of straight pipe of the same diameter that produces the same head loss as the fitting. Equating the two losses gives $\xi\dfrac{v^2}{2g} = \lambda\dfrac{L_{\mathrm{eq}}}{D}\dfrac{v^2}{2g}$. The velocity head cancels, leaving
$$
L_{\mathrm{eq}} = \xi\,\frac{D}{\lambda} = 4.0 \cdot \frac{0.200}{0.025} = 32.0\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
or $160$ pipe diameters. Interchanging the diameter and the friction factor would give $0.500\,\mathrm{m}$. Using the radius in place of the diameter would give $16.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
