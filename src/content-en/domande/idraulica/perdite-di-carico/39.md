A full circular pipe of diameter $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ has Chézy coefficient $\chi = 60\,\mathrm{m^{1/2}/s}$. What is its Gauckler–Strickler roughness coefficient?

- $39.0\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$
- $73.3\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$
- $82.3\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$
- $92.4\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$

## Explanation

The Gauckler–Strickler relation $\chi = k_s R^{\frac{1}{6}}$ expresses the Chézy coefficient as a function of the hydraulic radius. Thus
$$
k_s = \frac{\chi}{R^{\frac{1}{6}}} .
$$
For the full circular pipe, the hydraulic radius is $R = \dfrac{D}{4} = 0.0750\,\mathrm{m}$ and $R^{\frac{1}{6}} = 0.649$, giving
$$
k_s = \frac{60}{0.649} = 92.4\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s} ,
$$
a typical value for new cast iron. Multiplying in place of dividing would give $39.0\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$. Using the pipe radius in place of the hydraulic radius would give $82.3\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$.
