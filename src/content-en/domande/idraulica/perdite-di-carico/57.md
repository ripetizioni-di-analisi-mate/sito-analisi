Water flows uniformly in a full circular pipe of diameter $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ with energy gradient $J = 0.0040$. What is the friction velocity $u_* = \sqrt{\dfrac{\tau_0}{\rho}}$?

- $0.0542\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.0767\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.0173\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.00294\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The energy gradient gives the wall shear stress through $\tau_0 = \gamma R J$. For a circular pipe flowing full, $R = \dfrac{D}{4} = 0.075\,\mathrm{m}$. Thus
$$
\tau_0 = 9810 \cdot 0.075 \cdot 0.0040 = 2.94\,\mathrm{Pa} ,
$$
and the friction velocity is $u_* = \sqrt{\dfrac{2.94}{1000}} = 0.0542\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The ratio $\dfrac{\tau_0}{\rho}$ has the dimensions of velocity squared, so the square root is required. Dividing by $\gamma$ in place of $\rho$ would give the erroneous result $0.0173\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
