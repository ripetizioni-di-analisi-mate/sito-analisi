A pipe has friction factor $\lambda = 0.0240$. What is its corresponding Chézy coefficient?

- $28.6\,\mathrm{m^{1/2}/s}$
- $57.2\,\mathrm{m^{1/2}/s}$
- $40.4\,\mathrm{m^{1/2}/s}$
- $3270\,\mathrm{m^{1/2}/s}$

## Explanation

The Chézy and Darcy–Weisbach equations describe the same energy gradient. Combining $v = \chi\sqrt{R J}$ with $J = \lambda\dfrac{1}{4R}\dfrac{v^2}{2g}$, where $D = 4R$, gives the relation
$$
\chi = \sqrt{\frac{8g}{\lambda}} .
$$
Substituting the given values yields
$$
\chi = \sqrt{\frac{8 \cdot 9.81}{0.0240}} = \sqrt{3270} = 57.2\,\mathrm{m^{1/2}/s} ,
$$
a value typical of a pipe in good condition. Omitting the square root would give $3270$, which is the square of the Chézy coefficient.
