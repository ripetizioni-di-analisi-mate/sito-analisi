A straight pipe of diameter $D = 80\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries $Q = 3.8\,\mathrm{L/s}$ of oil with $\rho = 900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $\nu = 6 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^2/s}$. What is the centerline velocity?

- $0.378\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.51\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.756\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.13\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The area is $A = 5.03 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, giving a mean velocity of $v = \dfrac{0.0038}{5.03 \cdot 10^{-3}} = 0.756\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and a Reynolds number of
$$
Re = \frac{0.756 \cdot 0.080}{6 \cdot 10^{-5}} = 1008 .
$$
Since $Re < 2000$, the flow is **laminar** and the velocity profile is parabolic. For Hagen–Poiseuille flow the mean velocity is half the centerline velocity, so
$$
v_{\max} = 2\,v = 1.51\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
