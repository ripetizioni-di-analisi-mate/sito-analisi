A conical reducer connects a pipe of diameter $D_1 = 150\,\mathrm{mm}$ to one of diameter $D_2 = 100\,\mathrm{mm}$. Its catalog gives loss coefficient $\xi_2 = 0.08$ based on downstream velocity. What is the coefficient $\xi_1$ for the same fitting based on upstream velocity?

- $0.180$
- $0.0158$
- $0.120$
- $0.405$

## Explanation

The reducer produces a single head loss, so the upstream and downstream reference velocities must give the same loss. Thus
$$
\xi_1\,\frac{v_1^2}{2g} = \xi_2\,\frac{v_2^2}{2g}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\xi_1 = \xi_2\left( \frac{v_2}{v_1} \right)^2 .
$$
Continuity gives $\dfrac{v_2}{v_1} = \dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = \left( \dfrac{D_1}{D_2} \right)^2 = 2.25$, so $\xi_1 = 0.08 \cdot 2.25^2 = 0.405$. The coefficient based on the lower velocity is larger because it multiplies a smaller velocity head. Using the diameter ratio in place of the area ratio would give $0.180$. Inverting the velocity ratio would give $0.0158$.
