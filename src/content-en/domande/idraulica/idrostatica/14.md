A cylindrical vessel of radius $R = 0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ rotates about its vertical axis at $n = 100\,\mathrm{rpm}$. The water inside rotates as a rigid body. What is the elevation difference between the rim and the vertex of the free surface?

- $0.0127\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.503\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.252\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.01\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Convert revolutions per minute to angular velocity:
$$
\omega = \frac{2\pi \cdot 100}{60} = 10.47\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
The free surface is a paraboloid of revolution. The rim level exceeds the level on the axis by $\dfrac{\omega^2 R^2}{2 g}$, giving
$$
\Delta z = \frac{10.47^2 \cdot 0.30^2}{2 \cdot 9.81} = 0.503\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The rim rises $0.252\,\mathrm{m}$ above the rest level and the vertex falls by the same amount. The elevation difference between them is twice either change.
