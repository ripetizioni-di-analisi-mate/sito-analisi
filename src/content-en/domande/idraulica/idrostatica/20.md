One vessel contains glycerin at rest, another water at rest. In which liquid is the shear stress between adjacent fluid layers greater?

- Glycerin, because its dynamic viscosity is higher
- Water, because its density is lower
- Glycerin, because its density is higher
- The shear stress is zero in both liquids

## Explanation

By **Newton’s law of viscosity**, the shear stress is $\tau = \mu\,\dfrac{dv}{dy}$. A fluid at rest has zero velocity everywhere, so the velocity gradient and the shear stress both vanish, whatever the viscosity. The two fluids therefore behave alike at rest. Viscosity distinguishes their behavior in motion, where it produces head losses. This is why hydrostatic results apply to real fluids as well.
