A submersible is at depth $120\,\mathrm{m}$ in fresh water. The air inside is at atmospheric pressure. Taking pressure as uniform over the window, what net force acts on a circular window of diameter $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $47.1\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $148\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $37.0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $3.77\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The cabin air is at atmospheric pressure, which also acts on the lake surface. The net force on the porthole therefore depends on gauge pressure. At depth $120\,\mathrm{m}$ the hydrostatic pressure law gives
$$
p = 9.81 \cdot 120 = 1177\,\mathrm{kPa} .
$$
Since the porthole diameter is small relative to its depth, the pressure can be treated as uniform. Multiplying pressure by area gives
$$
F = p\,\frac{\pi D^2}{4} = 1177 \cdot \frac{\pi \cdot 0.20^2}{4} = 37.0\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
Omitting the factor $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ would give $47.1\,\mathrm{kN}$, and using the diameter as the radius would give $148\,\mathrm{kN}$.
