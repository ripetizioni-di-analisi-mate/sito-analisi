The water-filled siphon shown is at rest because its downstream valve is closed. The reservoir surface is at elevation $50.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and the siphon crest $C$ at elevation $56.50\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the gauge pressure at the crest?

- $-63.8\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $63.8\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $37.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $-6.50\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The siphon and reservoir contain one connected body of water at rest. The hydrostatic head plane therefore coincides with the reservoir free surface at elevation $50.00\,\mathrm{m}$. The siphon crest is $6.50\,\mathrm{m}$ above that plane, so its gauge pressure is negative and equals
$$
p_C = \gamma\,(h - z_C) = 9.81 \cdot (50.00 - 56.50) = -63.8\,\mathrm{kPa} .
$$
The value $37.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$ is the corresponding absolute pressure, $101.3 - 63.8$. The suction head $6.50\,\mathrm{m}$ is below the approximate limit of ten meters of water, so the water at the crest stays liquid.
