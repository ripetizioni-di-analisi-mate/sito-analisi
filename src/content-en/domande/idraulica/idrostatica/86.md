A cart carrying a partly full open water tank slides without friction down a plane inclined at $25^\circ$ to the horizontal. With the water at rest relative to the tank, how is the free surface oriented?

- Horizontal, as in a stationary cart
- Parallel to the incline, at $25^\circ$ to the horizontal
- At $22.9^\circ$ to the horizontal
- Perpendicular to the incline

## Explanation

The frictionless cart accelerates along the incline with $a = g\sin 25^\circ$. In its frame the effective body force per unit mass combines gravity and the inertial force $-\vec{a}$. The downhill component of gravity $g\sin 25^\circ$ and the inertial force cancel, leaving only the gravity component normal to the incline, $g\cos 25^\circ$. By the **differential equation of hydrostatic equilibrium**, constant-pressure surfaces are normal to this effective force. The free surface is therefore parallel to the incline. The value $22.9^\circ$ comes from applying the relation $\tan\theta = \dfrac{a}{g}$ for horizontal acceleration to acceleration along the incline.
