A flat slider of area $A = 0.25\,\mathrm{m^2}$ travels at constant speed $V = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ over an oil film of thickness $s = 1.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ and dynamic viscosity $\mu = 0.12\,\mathrm{Pa\,s}$. What tangential force maintains its motion?

- $0.0600\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $240\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Across the thin gap, velocity varies linearly, so the velocity gradient is constant and equals $\dfrac{V}{s}$. By **Newton’s law of viscosity**, the shear stress is
$$
\tau = \mu\,\frac{V}{s} = 0.12 \cdot \frac{2.0}{0.0010} = 240\,\mathrm{Pa} ,
$$
and the shear force is the stress multiplied by the contact area,
$$
F = \tau\,A = 240 \cdot 0.25 = 60.0\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $240\,\mathrm{Pa}$ is a shear stress. Multiplying it by the area gives the force.
