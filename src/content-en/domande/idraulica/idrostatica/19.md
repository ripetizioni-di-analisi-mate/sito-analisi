Two Newtonian fluid layers, each of thickness $h$, lie between flat plates. The lower layer has viscosity $2\mu$ and the upper layer $\mu$. The lower plate is fixed and the upper plate moves at speed $U$. In steady laminar flow with no pressure gradient or slip, what is the interface speed?

- $U/2$
- $2U/3$
- $U/3$
- $U/4$

## Explanation

Shear stress is continuous across the interface. Writing $u$ for the interface speed gives $2\mu u/h=\mu(U-u)/h$, hence $3u=U$.
