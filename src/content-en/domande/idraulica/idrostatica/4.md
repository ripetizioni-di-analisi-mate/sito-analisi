A closed pipe contains water at rest. Points $A$ and $B$ are at elevations $48.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and $61.00\,\mathrm{m}$ above a horizontal datum, respectively. The gauge pressure at $A$ is $220\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the gauge pressure at $B$?

- $122.6\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $342.6\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $97.4\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $220\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The water at rest forms a single connected body. Under the **hydrostatic pressure law**, the piezometric head $h = z + \dfrac{p}{\gamma}$ is the same at $A$ and $B$. Evaluating it at $A$ gives
$$
h = 48.50 + \frac{220}{9.81} = 48.50 + 22.43 = 70.93\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Point $B$ has elevation $61.00\,\mathrm{m}$, so
$$
p_B = \gamma\,(h - z_B) = 9.81 \cdot (70.93 - 61.00) = 97.4\,\mathrm{kPa} .
$$
Point $B$ lies $12.50\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$, so its pressure is lower by $9.81 \cdot 12.50 = 123\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
