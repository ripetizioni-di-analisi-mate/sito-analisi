An open tank contains still water with its free surface at elevation $25.00\,\mathrm{m}$ above a horizontal datum. If the datum is lowered by $10.00\,\mathrm{m}$, what happens to the piezometric head and the pressure at each point in the water?

- Piezometric head stays the same and pressure increases by $98.1\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- Piezometric head increases by $10.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and pressure stays the same
- Piezometric head decreases by $10.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and pressure stays the same
- Piezometric head increases by $10.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and pressure increases by $98.1\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The **piezometric head** is $h = z + \dfrac{p}{\gamma}$, where the point elevation $z$ is measured from the chosen datum. Lowering the datum by $10.00\,\mathrm{m}$ increases every elevation by $10.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and changes the piezometric head from $25.00\,\mathrm{m}$ to $35.00\,\mathrm{m}$. The depth of each point below the free surface stays the same, so all pressures stay the same. The numerical values of elevations and heads depend on the datum, while the physical pressure is independent of this choice.
