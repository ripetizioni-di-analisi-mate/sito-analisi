A piezometer is connected to a pipe with centerline elevation $12.40\,\mathrm{m}$. Water in the piezometer rises to elevation $27.90\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the gauge pressure at the pipe centerline?

- $152\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $274\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $122\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $15.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The piezometer is open to the atmosphere, so gauge pressure at its meniscus is zero. The water level has elevation $h = 27.90\,\mathrm{m}$, the piezometric head at the pressure tap. The pressure follows from $p = \gamma\,(h - z)$, giving
$$
p = 9.81 \cdot (27.90 - 12.40) = 9.81 \cdot 15.50 = 152\,\mathrm{kPa} .
$$
Use the vertical distance between the meniscus and the pressure tap. Using the meniscus elevation alone would give $274\,\mathrm{kPa}$, the pressure for a tap located on the datum.
