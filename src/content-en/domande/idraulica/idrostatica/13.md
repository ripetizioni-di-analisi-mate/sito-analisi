A tank truck has a rectangular tank of length $7.00\,\mathrm{m}$ containing water to a mean depth of $1.40\,\mathrm{m}$. It accelerates horizontally at $a = 1.80\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the difference between the water levels at the two ends?

- $1.28\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.642\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.57\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.183\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In the moving tanker’s frame, the liquid is at rest under gravity and an inertial force. Its free surface therefore tilts through an angle of
$$
\tan\theta = \frac{a}{g} = \frac{1.80}{9.81} = 0.183 .
$$
Multiply the slope of the free surface by the tank length to find the level difference between the end walls:
$$
\Delta z = 0.183 \cdot 7.00 = 1.28\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Relative to its rest level, the surface falls $0.642\,\mathrm{m}$ at the front and rises the same amount at the rear. The difference between the end levels is twice that amount.
