A tank open to the atmosphere contains water to a depth of $6.20\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the gauge pressure at a point $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ below the free surface?

- $41.2\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $19.6\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $60.8\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $120.9\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The tank is open, so the gauge pressure is zero at the free surface and the plane of zero gauge pressure coincides with that surface. By the **hydrostatic pressure law (Stevin’s law)**, the gauge pressure at a point is $p = \gamma\,h$, where $h$ is its depth below that plane. Here the depth is $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$, so
$$
p = 9.81 \cdot 2.00 = 19.6\,\mathrm{kPa} .
$$
Depth is measured downward from the free surface. The value $41.2\,\mathrm{kPa}$ would correspond to a point $4.20\,\mathrm{m}$ below that surface.
