A piezometer is connected to a pipe whose centerline is at elevation $102.30\,\mathrm{m}$ and gauge pressure $186\,\mathrm{kPa}$. To what elevation does water rise in the tube?

- $19.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $83.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $288.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $121.3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The **piezometer** is open to the atmosphere, so water rises to the hydrostatic head plane, at an elevation equal to the piezometric head at the tap. The pressure head is
$$
\frac{p}{\gamma} = \frac{186}{9.81} = 18.96\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
so the water reaches elevation
$$
h = 102.30 + 18.96 = 121.3\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Divide pressure by the specific weight of water to convert it to a water-column height. The pressure $186$ in kilopascals cannot be added directly to an elevation in meters.
