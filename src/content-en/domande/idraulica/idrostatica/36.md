When calculating the net force on a tank wall, gauge pressure is used. Why does atmospheric pressure drop out?

- Because atmospheric pressure is negligible compared with the other pressures involved
- Because atmospheric pressure acts only on the free surface
- Because atmospheric pressure also acts on the outside face and its two contributions cancel
- Because atmospheric pressure is not transmitted through the liquid

## Explanation

Atmospheric pressure acts on the free surface and is transmitted throughout the liquid, contributing to the pressure at every point on the inside face of the wall. The same pressure acts on the outside face. These contributions cancel in the net force, leaving only the gauge-pressure contribution. Atmospheric pressure $101.3\,\mathrm{kPa}$ is substantial, equivalent to a water column of $10.33\,\mathrm{m}$. Absolute pressure is needed in calculations such as cavitation checks.
