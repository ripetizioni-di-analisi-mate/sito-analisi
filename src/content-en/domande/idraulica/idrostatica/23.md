At a point in a liquid at rest, the pressure measured on a horizontal surface element is $48\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the pressure at the same point on an element inclined at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal?

- $24.0\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $41.6\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $48.0\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $55.4\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The **isotropy of pressure** means that pressure at a point in a fluid at rest has the same value on every surface passing through that point, regardless of orientation. The fluid is at rest here, so the pressure on the inclined surface is still $48.0\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Tilting the surface changes the direction of the compressive force, which always acts normal to it. On an element of area $\Delta A$ the force magnitude is $48\,\Delta A$ for every orientation.
