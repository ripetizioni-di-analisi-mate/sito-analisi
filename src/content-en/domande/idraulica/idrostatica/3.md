A closed tank holds water at rest below an air cushion. Two pressure taps in the same water are $0.80\,\mathrm m$ apart vertically. Air pressure is slowly raised by $20\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at fixed water levels. By how much does the difference between the two final readings change?

- $0\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $7.85\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

At the end of the operation both pressures have risen by $20\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Their difference is still $\rho g\Delta z$, set by the height difference and water density.
