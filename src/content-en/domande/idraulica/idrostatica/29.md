A pipe with a closed gate valve connects two open reservoirs. The upstream and downstream free surfaces are at elevations $86.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and $62.00\,\mathrm{m}$, and the valve is at elevation $58.00\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the gauge pressure on the downstream face of the valve?

- $235\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $275\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $314\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $39.2\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

With the gate closed, each tank contains a separate body of water and has its own hydrostatic head plane at its free surface. The downstream gate face is in contact with the downstream water. Its depth below the free surface at elevation $62.00\,\mathrm{m}$ is $4.00\,\mathrm{m}$, giving
$$
p = 9.81 \cdot 4.00 = 39.2\,\mathrm{kPa} .
$$
The upstream face is $28.00\,\mathrm{m}$ below the upstream free surface, so its pressure is $275\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
