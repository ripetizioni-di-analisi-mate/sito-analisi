The absolute pressure at a point in a pipe is $62\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Given $p_{\mathrm{atm}} = 101.3\,\mathrm{kPa}$, what is the gauge pressure there?

- $39.3\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $-39.3\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $163.3\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $-62.0\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Gauge pressure is measured relative to atmospheric pressure, so $p_{\mathrm{rel}} = p_{\mathrm{abs}} - p_{\mathrm{atm}}$. Substitution gives
$$
p_{\mathrm{rel}} = 62 - 101.3 = -39.3\,\mathrm{kPa} .
$$
The absolute pressure is below atmospheric pressure, so the gauge pressure is negative. The pressure deficit corresponds to $\dfrac{-39.3}{9.81} = -4.01\,\mathrm{m}$ of water head.
