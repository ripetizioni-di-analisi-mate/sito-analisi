A container holds $1.80\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of mercury with specific gravity $\delta = 13.6$. What is the mass of the mercury?

- $1800\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2495\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $24\,480\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $13\,600\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Specific gravity is the ratio of the fluid density to the density of water, so the density of mercury is $\rho = 13.6 \cdot 1000 = 13\,600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Mass equals density times volume, giving
$$
m = \rho\,V = 13\,600 \cdot 1.80 = 24\,480\,\mathrm{kg} .
$$
The weight of this volume is $\gamma\,V = 133.4 \cdot 1.80 = 240\,\mathrm{kN}$. It is the mass multiplied by $g$.
