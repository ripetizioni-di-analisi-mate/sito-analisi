A closed tank holds water to a depth of $2.80\,\mathrm{m}$, with air at gauge pressure $p_0 = 40\,\mathrm{kPa}$ above it. What is the gauge pressure at the bottom?

- $27.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $40.0\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $67.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The air pressure is transmitted throughout the liquid. By the **hydrostatic pressure law**, pressure at depth $h$ below the free surface is $p = p_0 + \gamma\,h$. The bottom is $2.80\,\mathrm{m}$ below the surface, giving
$$
p = 40 + 9.81 \cdot 2.80 = 40 + 27.5 = 67.5\,\mathrm{kPa} .
$$
The water column alone contributes $27.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and the air alone contributes $40.0\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Both act at the bottom.
