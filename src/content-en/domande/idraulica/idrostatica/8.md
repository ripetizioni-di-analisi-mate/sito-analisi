## Explanation

Water wets glass and the contact angle is zero. Surface tension therefore supplies an upward vertical force $\sigma\,\pi D$ around the contact line. The weight of the raised water column balances this force, giving **Jurin’s law**:
$$
h = \frac{4\,\sigma\cos\theta}{\gamma\,D} = \frac{4 \cdot 0.0728}{9810 \cdot 0.00080} = 0.0371\,\mathrm{m} = 37.1\,\mathrm{mm} .
$$
The denominator uses the tube diameter. Substituting its radius would double the result to $74.2\,\mathrm{mm}$.
