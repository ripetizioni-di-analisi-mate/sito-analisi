A pressurized circular pipe of length $1000\,\mathrm{m}$ and diameter $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ runs full and has roughness coefficient $k_s = 90\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$. What is its hydraulic resistance according to the Gauckler–Strickler equation?

- $123\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$
- $310\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$
- $781\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$
- $523\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$

## Explanation

The pipe flows full, so its entire circumference is wetted and the hydraulic radius is
$$
R = \frac{A}{C} = \frac{\dfrac{\pi D^2}{4}}{\pi D} = \frac{D}{4} .
$$
Substituting $R = \dfrac{D}{4}$ and $v = \dfrac{4Q}{\pi D^2}$ into the **Gauckler–Strickler** equation $v = k_s R^{\frac{2}{3}} J^{\frac{1}{2}}$ gives the resistance
$$
r = \frac{10.29\,L}{k_s^2\,D^{\frac{16}{3}}} .
$$
Substituting the given values gives
$$
r = \frac{10.29 \cdot 1000}{90^2 \cdot 0.300^{\frac{16}{3}}} = \frac{10290}{8100 \cdot 1.627 \cdot 10^{-3}} = 781\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5} .
$$
Using the geometric radius $\dfrac{D}{2}$ as the hydraulic radius would give $310\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$. Using the exponent $5$ in place of $\dfrac{16}{3}$ gives $523\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$.
