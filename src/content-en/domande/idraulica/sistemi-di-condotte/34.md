Three reservoirs with free surfaces at $z_A = 80.00\,\mathrm{m}$, $z_B = 62.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and $z_C = 50.00\,\mathrm{m}$ are connected to junction $N$, as shown. The piezometric head at the junction is $h_N = 66.00\,\mathrm{m}$. How is the flow distributed among the three branches?

::schema{nodi="N,18,6,-;A,3.5,16;B,13,14;C,26,2,-" etichette="17.2,5.2,N;26.8,1.2,C" condotte="A-N,0.55,;B-N,0.45,;N-C,0.65," acqua="0,13 3.5,13 3.5,20 0,20;10,14 13,14 13,17 10,17;26,-1 30,-1 30,5 26,5" solidi="0,12 3.5,12 3.5,13 0,13;10,13 13,13 13,14 10,14;26,-2 30,-2 30,-1 26,-1" pelo="0,3.5,20,80.00 m;10,13,17,62.00 m;26,30,5,50.00 m" titolo="Three reservoirs connected to junction $N$"}

- Reservoirs $A$ and $B$ both supply reservoir $C$
- Reservoir $C$ supplies reservoirs $A$ and $B$
- There is no flow in branch $B$
- Reservoir $A$ supplies reservoirs $B$ and $C$

## Explanation

Water flows from higher to lower head in each branch. Comparing each reservoir's water level with the junction head therefore determines the flow direction.

Since $z_A = 80.00\,\mathrm{m} > h_N$, branch $A$ carries flow toward the junction. Since $h_N = 66.00\,\mathrm{m} > z_B$ and $h_N > z_C$, branches $B$ and $C$ carry flow from the junction to their reservoirs.

Thus **reservoir $A$ supplies both $B$ and $C$**, and continuity at the junction gives $Q_A = Q_B + Q_C$.
