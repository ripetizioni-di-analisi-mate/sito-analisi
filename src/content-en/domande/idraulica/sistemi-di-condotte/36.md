A junction has piezometric head $h_N = 78.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and connects to a reservoir with free surface at $95.00\,\mathrm{m}$ through a branch of resistance $r_A = 680\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$. What is the discharge in this branch?

- $25.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $158\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $374\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $112\,\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

The head loss in the branch equals the difference between the reservoir water level and the junction head,
$$
\Delta H = 95.00 - 78.00 = 17.00\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The reservoir head exceeds the junction head, so water flows toward the junction. From $\Delta H = r_A Q_A^2$ we obtain
$$
Q_A = \sqrt{\frac{17.00}{680}} = \sqrt{0.0250} = 0.158\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 158\,\mathrm{L/s} .
$$
The value $25.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$ is the ratio $\dfrac{\Delta H}{r_A}$ before taking the square root. The value $374\,\mathrm{L/s}$ would result from taking the reservoir water-surface elevation as the head loss without subtracting the junction head.
