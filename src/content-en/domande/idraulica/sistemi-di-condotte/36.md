A junction with no external demand connects three reservoirs. A trial junction head gives inflows of $8$ and $5\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and an outflow of $10\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Which way should the junction head be adjusted to reduce the imbalance, with fixed reservoir levels and monotonic resistance laws?

- It should be decreased.
- It should be increased.
- It is already the correct head.
- It must equal the arithmetic mean of the three reservoir levels.

## Explanation

The trial balance has $8+5-10=3\,\mathrm{L/s}$ excess inflow. Raising the junction head reduces the driving heads on the incoming branches and increases it on the outgoing branch, reducing the residual.
