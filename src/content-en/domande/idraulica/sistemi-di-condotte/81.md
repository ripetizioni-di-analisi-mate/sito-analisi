A square loop is traversed clockwise. The flows in branches $1\!-\!2$ ($r = 400\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$, $30\,\mathrm{L/s}$) and $2\!-\!3$ ($r = 900\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$, $20\,\mathrm{L/s}$) follow that direction, while those in $4\!-\!3$ ($r = 3600\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$, $10\,\mathrm{L/s}$) and $1\!-\!4$ ($r = 100\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$, $60\,\mathrm{L/s}$) run opposite to it. The distribution satisfies nodal continuity. Which statement is true?

- The distribution is not the solution because the signed head losses around the loop sum to $1.44\,\mathrm{m}$
- The distribution is not the solution because the Hardy Cross correction is $-10.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- The distribution is not the solution because the branches carrying flow against the traversal direction carry more water than those carrying it in that direction
- The distribution solves the network

## Explanation

The head losses in the four branches are
$$
400 \cdot 0.030^2 = 0.36\,\mathrm{m} ,
\quad
900 \cdot 0.020^2 = 0.36\,\mathrm{m} ,
\quad
3600 \cdot 0.010^2 = 0.36\,\mathrm{m} ,
\quad
100 \cdot 0.060^2 = 0.36\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Where the flow follows the loop direction the branch loss enters with a positive sign, and where it opposes that direction the loss enters with a negative sign. Thus
$$
\sum_i r_i\,Q_i\,|Q_i| = 0.36 + 0.36 - 0.36 - 0.36 = 0 .
$$
Both the loop equation and the junction equations are satisfied, so **the distribution is the solution** and the Hardy Cross correction is zero.

The value $1.44\,\mathrm{m}$ is the sum of the unsigned losses. Using it in the numerator of the Hardy Cross formula gives the spurious correction $-10.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
