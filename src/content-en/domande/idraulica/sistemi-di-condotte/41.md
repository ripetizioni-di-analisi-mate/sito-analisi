A looped network has $10$ branches and $8$ nodes. How many independent loop equations are needed to determine all discharges?

- $2$
- $4$
- $3$
- $7$

## Explanation

The unknowns are the $10$ branch discharges. There are $8 - 1 = 7$ independent junction equations. Summing all the junction equations simply recovers the overall balance between inflows and withdrawals and supplies no additional information.

The required number of loop equations is therefore
$$
m = l - (n - 1) = 10 - 8 + 1 = 3 ,
$$
This equals the number of elementary loops in the diagram, each of which encloses no other loop.

The $7$ junction equations and $3$ loop equations give ten equations for ten unknowns. The loop equations are quadratic in the branch discharges, so the system is solved iteratively.
