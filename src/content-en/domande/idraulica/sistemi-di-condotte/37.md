Three reservoirs with free surfaces at $90.00\,\mathrm{m}$, $72.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and $55.00\,\mathrm{m}$ are connected to a junction with zero withdrawal. Whatever the branch resistances, which interval must contain the piezometric head at the junction?

- Between $55.00$ and $90.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- Between $72.00$ and $90.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- Between $55.00$ and $72.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- Exactly $72.00\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Write the sum of the discharges directed toward the junction as a function of its head $h$,
$$
\Phi(h) = \sum_{i} \operatorname{sgn}(z_i - h)\,\sqrt{\frac{|z_i - h|}{r_i}} ,
$$
This function is continuous and strictly decreasing. The junction head satisfies $\Phi(h_N) = 0$.

At $h = 55.00\,\mathrm{m}$, all terms are nonnegative and two are positive, so $\Phi > 0$. At $h = 90.00\,\mathrm{m}$, the same argument gives $\Phi < 0$. By the **intermediate value theorem** and strict monotonicity, there is exactly one $h_N$ between the lowest and highest reservoir levels.

The junction head can lie above or below the intermediate reservoir level, depending on the branch resistances.
