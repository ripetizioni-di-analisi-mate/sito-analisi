A pump is installed in the branch connecting nodes $M$ and $V$. Node $M$ has piezometric head $h_M = 48.00\,\mathrm{m}$. The branch has resistance $r = 1500\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$ and carries $60\,\mathrm{L/s}$ from $M$ to $V$. The pump adds head $H_p = 25.00\,\mathrm{m}$ in the flow direction. What is the piezometric head at node $V$?

- $42.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $73.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $67.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $78.40\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The pump raises the total head by $H_p$ between its inlet and outlet, while the branch resistance produces a head loss of $r\,Q^2$. The branch energy balance is therefore
$$
h_M + H_p = h_V + r\,Q^2 .
$$
The head loss is $\Delta H = 1500 \cdot 0.060^2 = 5.40\,\mathrm{m}$, so
$$
h_V = 48.00 + 25.00 - 5.40 = 67.60\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The downstream junction has a greater head than the upstream junction because the pump head exceeds the branch head loss. Without the pump, the head at $V$ would fall to $42.60\,\mathrm{m}$.
