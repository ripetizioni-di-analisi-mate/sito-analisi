A pipeline of length $2000\,\mathrm{m}$ must carry $100\,\mathrm{L/s}$ with a head loss of $25.00\,\mathrm{m}$. It consists of a section of diameter $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ followed by a section of diameter $250\,\mathrm{mm}$, both with $\lambda = 0.022$. How long must the section of diameter $250\,\mathrm{mm}$ be for the head loss to equal the specified value?

- $1098\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{m}$
- $902\,\mathrm{m}$
- $539\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The hydraulic gradient in a pipe carrying discharge $Q$ is $J = \dfrac{8\,\lambda\,Q^2}{g\,\pi^2 D^5}$. The two diameters therefore give
$$
J_{300} = \frac{8 \cdot 0.022 \cdot 0.100^2}{9.81\,\pi^2\,0.300^5} = 0.00748 ,
\qquad
J_{250} = \frac{8 \cdot 0.022 \cdot 0.100^2}{9.81\,\pi^2\,0.250^5} = 0.01861 .
$$
Using diameter $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ throughout would give a head loss of $14.96\,\mathrm{m}$, below the required value. Using only diameter $250\,\mathrm{mm}$ would give $37.23\,\mathrm{m}$, above it. Let $x$ be the length of the section with diameter $250\,\mathrm{mm}$. The two conditions to satisfy are
$$
\begin{cases}
x + L_{300} = 2000 \\[4pt]
0.01861\,x + 0.00748\,L_{300} = 25.00
\end{cases}
$$
Substituting $L_{300} = 2000 - x$ into the second equation gives
$$
x = \frac{25.00 - 14.96}{0.01861 - 0.00748} = 902\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $1098\,\mathrm{m}$ is the length of the section of diameter $300\,\mathrm{mm}$. The value $539\,\mathrm{m}$ results from dividing the excess head loss by $J_{250}$ alone.
