Three reservoirs with water surfaces at $z_A = 65.00\,\mathrm{m}$, $z_B = 50.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and $z_C = 40.00\,\mathrm{m}$ are connected to node $N$ by pipes $A\!-\!N$ ($400\,\mathrm{m}$, $350\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.020$), $B\!-\!N$ ($1000\,\mathrm{m}$, $250\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.022$) and $C\!-\!N$ ($1500\,\mathrm{m}$, $250\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.022$). In which direction does the water flow in branch $B$?

- From the node toward reservoir $B$
- From reservoir $B$ toward the node
- The discharge in branch $B$ is zero
- The direction depends on the resistance of branch $B$

## Explanation

The resistances are $r_A = 126\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$ and $r_C = 2792\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$.

Test the head $h_N = z_B = 50.00\,\mathrm{m}$, at which no water flows through branch $B$. The other two branch discharges are
$$
Q_A = \sqrt{\frac{65 - 50}{126}} = 0.345\,\mathrm{m^3/s} ,
\qquad
Q_C = \sqrt{\frac{50 - 40}{2792}} = 0.0598\,\mathrm{m^3/s} .
$$
More water arrives from reservoir $A$ than branch $C$ can carry away at that head. The nodal head therefore exceeds $z_B$, and the node supplies reservoir $B$. Water flows **from the node toward reservoir** $B$ and continuity gives $Q_A = Q_B + Q_C$.
