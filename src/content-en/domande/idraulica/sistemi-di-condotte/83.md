The first Hardy Cross iteration for a clockwise loop traversal gives $\sum_i r_i\,Q_i\,|Q_i| = +2.30\,\mathrm{m}$. Which statement is true?

- The clockwise discharges are too small and the correction is positive
- The clockwise discharges are too large and the correction is negative
- The clockwise discharges are too large and the correction is positive
- The distribution already solves the network because the sum is positive

## Explanation

A positive sum around the clockwise loop means the losses of the branches flowing clockwise exceed those of the opposing branches. The clockwise discharges are therefore too large relative to the opposing discharges. The loop equation requires a zero sum, so the current distribution is not yet the solution.

The denominator $2\sum_i r_i |Q_i|$ in the correction formula is positive, so
$$
\Delta Q = -\,\frac{2.30}{2\sum_i r_i |Q_i|} < 0 .
$$
Adding this negative correction to every signed branch discharge **reduces the clockwise flows and increases the magnitudes of the opposing flows**, bringing the loss sum closer to zero.
