Three reservoirs with water surfaces at $z_A = 90.00\,\mathrm{m}$, $z_B = 70.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and $z_C = 45.00\,\mathrm{m}$ are connected to node $N$ by pipes $A\!-\!N$ ($1000\,\mathrm{m}$, $300\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.022$), $B\!-\!N$ ($600\,\mathrm{m}$, $200\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.023$) and $N\!-\!C$ ($1300\,\mathrm{m}$, $350\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.021$), as shown. What is the piezometric head at the node?

- $70.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $68.33\,\mathrm{m}$
- $65.35\,\mathrm{m}$
- $61.41\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The branch resistances are $r_A = 748\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$, $r_B = 3563\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$ and $r_C = 429\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$.

At the trial head $h_N = z_B = 70.00\,\mathrm{m}$ there is no flow in branch $B$, giving
$$
\sqrt{\frac{90 - 70}{748}} = 0.164\,\mathrm{m^3/s} ,
\qquad
\sqrt{\frac{70 - 45}{429}} = 0.241\,\mathrm{m^3/s} ,
$$
Branch $C$ then needs more water than arrives from $A$. It follows that $h_N < z_B$ and reservoir $B$ also supplies the node.

Continuity at the junction therefore becomes
$$
\sqrt{\frac{90 - h_N}{748}} + \sqrt{\frac{70 - h_N}{3563}} = \sqrt{\frac{h_N - 45}{429}} ,
$$
Solving for $h_N$ by iteration gives $h_N = 65.35\,\mathrm{m}$. Neglecting branch $B$ would give $61.41\,\mathrm{m}$ and underestimate the nodal head.
