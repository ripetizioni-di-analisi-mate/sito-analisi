A branch is common to two loops in a network. Which corrections are applied to its discharge at each Hardy Cross iteration?

- The corrections from both loops, each with its appropriate sign
- Only the correction for the first loop traversed
- The average of the two loop corrections
- No correction, because the two cancel

## Explanation

Add the loop correction to the signed discharge in every branch of that loop. At each junction the correction enters once with a positive sign and once with a negative sign, leaving the junction equation satisfied.

The shared branch belongs to both loops and therefore receives **a correction from each loop**, with the sign set by the direction chosen for that loop.

The two corrections are calculated from different loops and generally have different magnitudes. They therefore do not cancel, and the discharge in the shared branch changes from one iteration to the next.
