In a branched network, node $A$ has piezometric head $h_A = 121.00\,\mathrm{m}$. A branch from $A$, of length $900\,\mathrm{m}$ and with $\lambda = 0.022$, must carry $90\,\mathrm{L/s}$ to terminal node $B$ at elevation $95.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and maintain a pressure of at least $20.00\,\mathrm{m}$ of water. If the standard diameter $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ is used, what is the pressure at $B$?

- $20.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $26.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12.43\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20.55\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The branch of diameter $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ has resistance
$$
r = \frac{8 \cdot 0.022 \cdot 900}{9.81\,\pi^2\,0.300^5} = 673.3\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5} ,
$$
so the actual head loss is $\Delta H = 673.3 \cdot 0.090^2 = 5.45\,\mathrm{m}$.

The terminal piezometric head is $h_B = 121.00 - 5.45 = 115.55\,\mathrm{m}$, giving
$$
\frac{p_B}{\gamma} = 115.55 - 95.00 = 20.55\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
This exceeds the required $20.00\,\mathrm{m}$, so the selected diameter **meets** the design requirement. With diameter $250\,\mathrm{mm}$ the loss would rise to $13.57\,\mathrm{m}$ and the pressure would drop to $12.43\,\mathrm{m}$.
