A circular pipe of length $800\,\mathrm{m}$, diameter $250\,\mathrm{mm}$ and Darcy friction factor $\lambda = 0.023$ carries steady flow. What is its hydraulic resistance?

- $389\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$
- $1557\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$
- $3114\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$
- $778\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$

## Explanation

Hydraulic resistance is the coefficient $r$ in the relation $\Delta H = r\,Q^2$. Substituting $v = \dfrac{4Q}{\pi D^2}$ into the **Darcy–Weisbach** equation gives
$$
r = \frac{8\,\lambda\,L}{g\,\pi^2 D^5} .
$$
Substituting the given values gives
$$
r = \frac{8 \cdot 0.023 \cdot 800}{9.81 \cdot \pi^2 \cdot 0.250^5} = \frac{147.2}{0.0946} = 1557\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5} .
$$
The value $389\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$ results from using $D^4$ in place of $D^5$ in the denominator. The value $3114\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$ results from writing the velocity head as $\dfrac{v^2}{g}$ instead of $\dfrac{v^2}{2g}$.
