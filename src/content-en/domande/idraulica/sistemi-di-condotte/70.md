In the network shown, $Q$ enters at node $1$ and leaves at node $3$. In the solved network, branch flows run from $1$ to $2$, $2$ to $3$, $1$ to $4$, $4$ to $3$ and $2$ to $4$, as indicated by the arrows. Which ordering of the four piezometric heads is correct?

::schema{nodi="1,2,8,-;2,13,14;3,24,8,-;4,13,2" condotte="1-2,0.4,;2-3,0.4,;3-4,0.4,;4-1,0.4,;2-4,0.4," frecce="-3,8,1.1,8,;24.9,8,28,8,Q;5.95,11.33,8.05,12.47,;17.95,12.47,20.05,11.33,;5.95,4.67,8.05,3.53,;17.95,3.53,20.05,4.67,;14,9.2,14,6.8," etichette="-1.2,9.2,Q;2,6.6,1;24,6.6,3" titolo="Network showing diagonal branch $2\!-\!4$ and flow directions"}

- $h_1 > h_4 > h_2 > h_3$
- $h_1 > h_2 > h_3 > h_4$
- $h_2 > h_1 > h_4 > h_3$
- $h_1 > h_2 > h_4 > h_3$

## Explanation

In each branch the head loss $r\,Q^2$ is positive in the flow direction. Water therefore flows from the junction with the higher head to the one with the lower head.

Flow in branch $1\!-\!2$ goes from $1$ to $2$, giving $h_1 > h_2$. Flow in the diagonal branch goes from $2$ to $4$, giving $h_2 > h_4$. Flow in branch $4\!-\!3$ goes from $4$ to $3$, giving $h_4 > h_3$. It follows that
$$
h_1 > h_2 > h_4 > h_3 ,
$$
This agrees with the flow directions in branches $2\!-\!3$ and $1\!-\!4$, which require $h_2 > h_3$ and $h_1 > h_4$. The head is highest at the supply junction and lowest at junction $3$, where all the connected branches carry flow inward.

The ordering $h_1 > h_4 > h_2 > h_3$ would require flow in the diagonal branch from $4$ to $2$. The ordering $h_1 > h_2 > h_3 > h_4$ would require flow in branch $4\!-\!3$ from $3$ to $4$, and $h_2 > h_1 > h_4 > h_3$ would require flow in branch $1\!-\!2$ from $2$ to $1$.
