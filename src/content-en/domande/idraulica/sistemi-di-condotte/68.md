Reservoirs $A$ and $B$, with free surfaces at $70.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and $64.00\,\mathrm{m}$, connect to node $N$ at elevation $40.00\,\mathrm{m}$, where a customer withdraws $E$, as shown. Branch $A\!-\!N$ has resistance $r_A = 600\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$ and branch $B\!-\!N$ has resistance $r_B = 900\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$. At what value of $E$ does reservoir $B$ stop receiving water from the node?

::schema{nodi="A,3.5,16;N,16,4;B,28.5,13" condotte="A-N,0.5,r_A = 600;N-B,0.5,r_B = 900" acqua="0,13 3.5,13 3.5,20 0,20;28.5,11 32,11 32,17 28.5,17" solidi="0,12 3.5,12 3.5,13 0,13;28.5,10 32,10 32,11 28.5,11" pelo="0,3.5,20,70.00 m;28.5,32,17,64.00 m" frecce="16,3.3,16,0.4,E" etichette="21.5,2.2,z_N = 40.00 m" titolo="Demand at node $N$ supplied by two reservoirs"}

- $100\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $10.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $224\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $63.2\,\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

At small $E$, reservoir $A$ supplies both the demand and reservoir $B$, and the junction head exceeds $64.00\,\mathrm{m}$. As $E$ increases, the junction head falls. Reservoir $B$ stops receiving flow when the junction head reaches its water-surface elevation, $h_N = 64.00\,\mathrm{m}$.

At this point there is no flow in branch $B$, so the entire demand is supplied by $A$,
$$
E = Q_A = \sqrt{\frac{70.00 - 64.00}{600}} = 0.100\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 100\,\mathrm{L/s} .
$$
For $E > 100\,\mathrm{L/s}$, reservoir $B$ also supplies the junction.

The value $10.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$ is the ratio $\dfrac{6.00}{600}$ before taking the square root. The value $224\,\mathrm{L/s}$ uses the junction elevation in place of its piezometric head. The value $63.2\,\mathrm{L/s}$ is the discharge from $A$ to $B$ with $E = 0$, when the two branches act in series.
