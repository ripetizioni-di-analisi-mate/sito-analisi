A pump conveys $90\,\mathrm{L/s}$ between reservoirs with an elevation difference of $\Delta z = 45.00\,\mathrm{m}$. The pipeline has two sections in series. The first has length $800\,\mathrm{m}$, diameter $250\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda_1 = 0.021$. The second has length $1200\,\mathrm{m}$, diameter $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda_2 = 0.022$. The overall pumping efficiency is $\eta = 0.74$. What input power is required?

- $53.7\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $56.3\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $76.1\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $41.7\,\mathrm{kW}$

## Explanation

The two pipe resistances are $r_1 = 1421\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$ and $r_2 = 898\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$, so the head losses are $\Delta H_1 = 1421 \cdot 0.090^2 = 11.51\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\Delta H_2 = 898 \cdot 0.090^2 = 7.27\,\mathrm{m}$.

The pump must supply the static lift plus all head losses, giving
$$
H_p = 45.00 + 11.51 + 7.27 = 63.78\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The required input power is
$$
P = \frac{\gamma\,Q\,H_p}{\eta} = \frac{9.81 \cdot 0.090 \cdot 63.78}{0.74} = 76.1\,\mathrm{kW} .
$$
The value $56.3\,\mathrm{kW}$ is the power transferred to the fluid, before division by pump efficiency. The value $53.7\,\mathrm{kW}$ accounts only for the static lift.
