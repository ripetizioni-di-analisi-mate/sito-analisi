A branch in a distribution tree has length $800\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\lambda = 0.022$. It must carry $70\,\mathrm{L/s}$ with head loss no greater than $5.00\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the minimum branch diameter?

- $194\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $270\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $300\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $135\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The design requirement is $r\,Q^2 \leq \Delta H_{\mathrm{avail}}$. Substituting the expression for resistance gives
$$
\frac{8\,\lambda\,L\,Q^2}{g\,\pi^2 D^5} \leq \Delta H_{\mathrm{avail}} ,
\qquad
D \geq \left(\frac{8\,\lambda\,L\,Q^2}{g\,\pi^2\,\Delta H_{\mathrm{avail}}}\right)^{\frac{1}{5}} .
$$
Substituting the given values gives
$$
D \geq \left(\frac{8 \cdot 0.022 \cdot 800 \cdot 0.070^2}{9.81 \cdot \pi^2 \cdot 5.00}\right)^{\frac{1}{5}} = 0.270\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
or $270\,\mathrm{mm}$. Select the commercially available diameter $300\,\mathrm{mm}$. Its velocity of $v = \dfrac{4 \cdot 0.070}{\pi \cdot 0.300^2} = 0.99\,\mathrm{m/s}$ lies within the usual operating range.
