In a branched network, node $M$ has piezometric head $h_M = 130.00\,\mathrm{m}$. A pipe leads from $M$ to terminal node $V$ at elevation $102.00\,\mathrm{m}$, where the pressure must be at least $25.00\,\mathrm{m}$ of water. What head loss is available for the pipe?

- $28.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $25.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $53.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The required piezometric head at the terminal junction is its elevation plus the minimum pressure head, giving $102.00 + 25.00 = 127.00\,\mathrm{m}$.

Subtracting this value from the upstream junction head gives the head available to overcome losses in the branch,
$$
\Delta H_{\mathrm{avail}} = h_M - z_V - \frac{p_{\min}}{\gamma} = 130.00 - 102.00 - 25.00 = 3.00\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $28.00\,\mathrm{m}$ is simply the elevation difference between the junctions. It would be the available head loss if zero pressure head were acceptable at $V$.
