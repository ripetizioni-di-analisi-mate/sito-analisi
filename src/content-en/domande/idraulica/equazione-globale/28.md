A horizontal pressure-pipe elbow turns the flow through angle $\theta$. In what direction does the fluid force on the elbow act?

- In the direction of the inlet velocity
- Along the bisector of the two section planes, outward from the bend
- Along the bisector of the two section planes, inward toward the bend
- Normal to the outlet velocity

## Explanation

Let $K = \rho Q v + p A$. The force on the elbow has component $K\,(1 - \cos\theta)$ along the inlet velocity and component $-K\sin\theta$ normal to it. The transverse-to-axial component ratio is $-\dfrac{1}{\tan\frac{\theta}{2}}$, so the force makes angle $90^\circ - \dfrac{\theta}{2}$ with the inlet axis and bisects the angle between the two end-section planes. The elbow pushes the water inward to turn it, so the water pushes the elbow outward. Orient the thrust block to resist the force in that direction.
