A fitting connects a horizontal inlet to an upward-sloping outlet in a different vertical plane. The two velocity vectors and the vertical direction are not coplanar. How many scalar momentum equations result, and how many unknown wall-force components do they contain?

- Two equations with two unknown components
- One equation along the inlet pipe axis
- Three equations with the three components of $\vec{R}$
- Three equations with only one unknown component

## Explanation

The integral momentum equation is a vector equation. The number of independent component equations depends on the number of dimensions spanned by its vectors. In a planar problem the momentum fluxes, pressure forces, and weight lie in a single plane, so two components suffice. Here the two velocities and the weight are not coplanar. Resolving along three axes gives three scalar equations for the wall-force components $R_x$, $R_y$, and $R_z$. These determine the three unknowns.
