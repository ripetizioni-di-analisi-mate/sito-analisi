A pipe elbow lies in a horizontal plane. When the momentum equation is applied to the control volume between its inlet and outlet, what balances the weight of the fluid?

- The vertical component of the wall force on the fluid
- The pressure forces on the two flow sections
- The change in momentum flux between inlet and outlet
- No force, because weight is absent from the horizontal component equations

## Explanation

The inlet and outlet velocities are horizontal and their flow sections lie in vertical planes. The pressure forces $\vec{\Pi}$ and the momentum fluxes therefore have no vertical component. The vertical momentum balance reduces to $0 = -G + R_z$, so $R_z = G$. The vertical wall force balances the fluid weight, which the elbow in turn transmits to its supports. Weight acts even in a horizontal elbow. It drops out of the horizontal equations because it is perpendicular to the plane of flow.
