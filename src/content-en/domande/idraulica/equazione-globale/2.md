A horizontal pipe elbow of diameter $D = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ turns the flow through angle $\theta = 45^\circ$. The discharge is $Q = 60\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and the gauge pressure at both sections is $p = 250\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Find the magnitude of the fluid force on the elbow.

- $5.63\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $11.3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $15.9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6.10\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The area is $A = 0.0314\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the mean velocity is $v = \dfrac{Q}{A} = 1.91\,\mathrm{m/s}$. This gives $\rho Q v = 115\,\mathrm{N}$ and $p A = 7.85\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $K = 7.97\,\mathrm{kN}$. The bend turns through $\theta = 45^\circ$, so the force formula uses the sine of half that angle:
$$
F = 2 K \sin\frac{\theta}{2} = 2 \cdot 7.97 \cdot \sin 22.5^\circ = 6.10\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
Using $\sin\theta$ instead of $\sin\dfrac{\theta}{2}$ would give $11.3\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $15.9\,\mathrm{kN}$ is twice $K$, the force magnitude for a deflection of $180^\circ$.
