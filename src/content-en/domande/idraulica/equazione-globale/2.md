A horizontal bend of uniform diameter turns the flow through $45^\circ$. Gauge pressure is equal and positive at both sections. At fixed discharge both pressures are raised by the same amount. How does this affect the horizontal projection of the fluid force on the bend?

- It is unchanged because the pressure difference is still zero.
- Its magnitude decreases without a change of direction.
- It rotates by $45^\circ$ without changing magnitude.
- Its magnitude increases and its direction remains unchanged.

## Explanation

With inlet and outlet direction vectors $\mathbf e_1,\mathbf e_2$, the horizontal force projection is $(pA+\rho Qv)(\mathbf e_1-\mathbf e_2)$. The section normals differ, so a common increase in pressure adds force along the same direction.
