During start-up a pipe bend accumulates fluid momentum in the $x$-direction at a rate of $18\,\mathrm{N}$. At the instant observed the outgoing minus incoming $x$-momentum flux is $-6\,\mathrm{N}$. The resultant pressure force in $x$ at the control sections is $+5\,\mathrm{N}$ and the weight component in $x$ is negligible. What $x$-force does the wall exert on the fluid?

- $-11\,\mathrm N$
- $+17\,\mathrm N$
- $+7\,\mathrm N$
- $+23\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

For a fixed control volume, $F_{\rm wall}+5=18-6$. Boundary flux and internal storage are separate contributions, giving a wall force of $7\,\mathrm N$.
