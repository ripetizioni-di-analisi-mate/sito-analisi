A horizontal pipe elbow of diameter $D = 150\,\mathrm{mm}$ turns the flow through angle $\theta = 60^\circ$. The discharge is $Q = 40\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and the gauge pressure at both sections is $p = 200\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Find the magnitude of the fluid force on the elbow.

- $1.81\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $7.25\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $3.62\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6.28\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The cross-sectional area is $A = 0.0177\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the mean velocity is $v = \dfrac{Q}{A} = 2.26\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Thus $\rho Q v = 90.5\,\mathrm{N}$ and $p A = 3.53\,\mathrm{kN}$, giving $K = 3.62\,\mathrm{kN}$. The deflection angle is $\theta = 60^\circ$, so
$$
F = 2 K \sin\frac{\theta}{2} = 2 \cdot 3.62 \cdot \sin 30^\circ = 3.62\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
directed outward along the bisector of the angle between the two end sections. For a deflection of $60^\circ$, twice the sine of the half-angle is $1$, so the force magnitude equals $K$. The value $6.28\,\mathrm{kN}$ follows from using $\sin\theta$ in place of $\sin\dfrac{\theta}{2}$.
