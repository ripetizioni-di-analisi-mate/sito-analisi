A nozzle and a vertical plate are mounted on a cart free to move horizontally. Water enters the nozzle from above through a vertical hose. A horizontal jet of discharge $Q$ and speed $v$ strikes the plate normally and divides into sheets flowing along it. Neglecting air resistance, what horizontal force holds the cart stationary?

- $0$
- $\rho Q v$ in the jet direction
- $\rho Q v$ opposite to the jet
- $2 \rho Q v$ in the jet direction

## Explanation

Choose all of the water carried by the cart as the control volume, from the flexible hose inlet to the sheets leaving the plate. At the inlet both the velocity and the pressure force $\vec{\Pi}$ are vertical, so the momentum flux and pressure force have zero horizontal components. The sheets leave parallel to the vertical plate at zero gauge pressure, so the outlet terms also have zero horizontal components. The horizontal momentum balance therefore gives $R_x = 0$. The cart stays at rest without an external horizontal force. Considered separately, the nozzle experiences a backward force $\rho Q v$ from the water and the plate experiences a force $\rho Q v$ in the jet direction. These forces cancel on the cart.
