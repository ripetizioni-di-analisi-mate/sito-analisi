The crest of a siphon is a $180^\circ$ bend in a vertical plane. Flow rises through the left limb and descends through the right, as shown. The diameter is $D = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$, the discharge $Q = 50\,\mathrm{L/s}$, and the gauge pressure $p = -50\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at sections $1$ and $2$, which are at the same elevation. The control volume contains $W = 0.060\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of water. What are the magnitude and direction of the vertical force the water exerts on the bend?

::schema{nodi="A,0,0,-;B,0,2.6,-;C,2,2.6,-;D,2,0,-" condotte="A-B-C-D,0.5" tratteggi="-0.4,1 0.4,1;1.6,1 2.4,1" frecce="-0.8,0.2,-0.8,1,Q;2.8,1,2.8,0.2,Q" etichette="-0.65,1.3,1;2.65,1.3,2;1,3.3,D = 200 mm" riferimento=0 titolo="Crest of a siphon in a vertical plane"}

- $2.71\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $3.57\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward
- $2.98\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward
- $2.39\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward

## Explanation

The area is $A = 0.0314\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the speed, $v = 1.59\,\mathrm{m/s}$, is the same at both sections. With $z$ positive upward, the vertical velocity is $+v$ at section $1$ and $-v$ at section $2$, so the net vertical momentum flux is $-2\rho Q v$. The inward normals of both sections point upward, so each pressure force contributes $pA$ with the signed gauge pressure. The vertical momentum balance gives
$$
-2\,\rho\,Q\,v = 2\,p\,A - \gamma\,W + R_z .
$$
The force on the bend is therefore
$$
F_z = -R_z = 2\,\rho\,Q\,v + 2\,p\,A - \gamma\,W = 0.159 - 3.142 - 0.589 = -3.57\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
or $3.57\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Because the gauge pressure is negative, both pressure forces act outward from the control volume and contribute to the downward force on the bend along with the fluid weight. The value $2.71\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward uses the absolute value of gauge pressure. The value $2.98\,\mathrm{kN}$ omits the weight, and $2.39\,\mathrm{kN}$ gives it the wrong sign.
