A fixed vane intercepts a fraction $f=0.40$ of a steady jet of total mass flow $\dot m$. The intercepted part reverses direction at unchanged speed $v$; the remainder continues undisturbed. What is the ratio of the force on the vane to $\dot m v$?

- $2$
- $0.40$
- $0.80$
- $1.20$

## Explanation

The bypass flow contributes equally to incoming and outgoing flux and cancels. The intercepted flow $f\dot m$ loses longitudinal momentum at a rate $2f\dot m v$.
