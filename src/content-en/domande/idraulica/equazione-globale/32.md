A horizontal $90^\circ$ pipe elbow of diameter $D = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries discharge $Q = 90\,\mathrm{L/s}$ with gauge pressure $p = 350\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at both sections. What is the component of the force on the elbow along the inlet velocity?

- $11.3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $7.96\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $15.9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The area is $A = 0.0314\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the mean velocity is $v = \dfrac{Q}{A} = 2.86\,\mathrm{m/s}$, giving $\rho Q v = 258\,\mathrm{N}$ and $p A = 11.00\,\mathrm{kN}$. Their sum is $K = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$. The magnitudes of the force components on the elbow are $K\,(1 - \cos\theta)$ parallel to the inlet velocity and $K\sin\theta$ perpendicular to it. For $\theta = 90^\circ$ both therefore equal $K = 11.3\,\mathrm{kN}$. The resultant has magnitude $K\sqrt{2} = 15.9\,\mathrm{kN}$ and makes an angle of $45^\circ$ with the inlet axis. The value $22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ is twice $K$, corresponding to a $180^\circ$ bend.
