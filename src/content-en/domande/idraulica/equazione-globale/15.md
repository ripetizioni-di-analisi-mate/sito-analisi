To find the force of the fluid on an elbow in a pressure pipe using the integral momentum equation, where should the two flow sections of the control volume be placed?

- In straight reaches, one upstream and one downstream of the elbow
- At the start and end of the bend, where the streamlines are curved
- At any two sections provided their areas are equal
- On the elbow wall where the force is required

## Explanation

In the integral momentum equation the pressure force at a flow section is $\vec{\Pi} = p_G A\,\hat{n}$, because pressure is distributed hydrostatically across that section. This is the case where streamlines are straight and parallel, so the inlet and outlet sections are taken in the straight runs upstream and downstream of the fitting. Within the bend, the streamlines curve and the pressure distribution is nonhydrostatic. Selecting a section there would leave the pressure force unknown. No discharge crosses the wall, so it is not a flow section. The force that the wall exerts on the fluid is included in $\vec{R}$.
