A water jet of diameter $d = 45\,\mathrm{mm}$ and speed $v = 22\,\mathrm{m/s}$ enters a fixed vane and leaves at angle $\theta = 135^\circ$ to the inlet direction. Neglecting friction, what is the magnitude of the jet force on the vane?

- $770\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1089\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1422\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1540\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The jet area $A = 1.590 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and discharge $Q = A v = 35.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$ give a momentum flux $\rho Q v = 770\,\mathrm{N}$. With negligible friction, the inlet and outlet speeds have the same magnitude. The resulting force magnitude is
$$
F = 2\,\rho\,Q\,v\,\sin\frac{\theta}{2} = 2 \cdot 770 \cdot \sin 67.5^\circ = 1422\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $1540\,\mathrm{N}$, twice the momentum flux, requires a full reversal through $\theta = 180^\circ$. The value $770\,\mathrm{N}$ applies to a flat plate normal to the jet axis.
