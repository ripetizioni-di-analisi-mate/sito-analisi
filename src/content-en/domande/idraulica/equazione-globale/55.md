A rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and bed slope $i = 0.001$ carries uniform flow of depth $h = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$. Apply the integral momentum equation to a reach of length $L$ in the flow direction. What is the mean shear stress $\tau_0$ exerted by the wetted boundary on the flow?

- $9.81\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $7.36\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $5.89\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $4.91\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

In uniform flow the two sections have the same water depth and speed. The section pressure forces cancel, and the net momentum flux is zero. The remaining streamwise forces are the component of weight, approximately $\gamma A L\,i$ for a gentle slope, and the resisting force along the wetted boundary, $\tau_0\,C\,L$. The momentum balance gives $\gamma A L\,i = \tau_0\,C\,L$, or $\tau_0 = \gamma R\,i$ using the hydraulic radius
$$
R = \frac{A}{C} = \frac{3.00 \cdot 1.00}{3.00 + 2 \cdot 1.00} = 0.600\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
so $\tau_0 = 9810 \cdot 0.600 \cdot 0.001 = 5.89\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The value $9.81\,\mathrm{Pa}$ uses the water depth in place of the hydraulic radius, while $7.36\,\mathrm{Pa}$ includes only one sidewall in the wetted perimeter.
