At a circular pipe section of diameter $D = 400\,\mathrm{mm}$, the gauge pressure at the centroid is $p_G = 250\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and atmospheric pressure is $101\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the net pressure force on the section?

- $44.1\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $62.8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $126\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $31.4\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The streamlines at the section are straight and parallel, so the pressure distribution is hydrostatic and the force is $\Pi = p_G A$, where $A = \dfrac{\pi D^2}{4} = 0.1257\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Using gauge pressure in the integral momentum equation gives
$$
\Pi = 250 \cdot 0.1257 = 31.4\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The value $44.1\,\mathrm{kN}$ uses the absolute pressure $351\,\mathrm{kPa}$. That approach would require including atmospheric pressure on the entire exterior of the fitting as well. The value $126\,\mathrm{kN}$ uses the area $\pi D^2$, substituting the diameter for the radius.
