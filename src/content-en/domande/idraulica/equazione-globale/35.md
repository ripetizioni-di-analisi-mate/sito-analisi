A diffuser is installed in a horizontal pipe carrying a specified discharge. In what direction does the fluid force on the diffuser act?

- Downstream, as in a reducer
- Normal to the pipe axis
- Upstream, because $p_2 A_2$ exceeds $p_1 A_1$
- Zero, because the two section pressure forces balance

## Explanation

A diffuser widens downstream, so the mean velocity decreases and, by Bernoulli’s equation, the pressure rises. Both factors in $p A$ therefore increase. The downstream term $p_2 A_2$ exceeds the upstream term $p_1 A_1$ by more than the smaller momentum-flux contribution can offset. The resultant force on the diffuser acts upstream. A reducer, whose area decreases, gives a force in the opposite direction. For a diffuser expanding from $200$ to $350\,\mathrm{mm}$ with $Q = 100\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and $p_1 = 160\,\mathrm{kPa}$, the force is $10.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upstream.
