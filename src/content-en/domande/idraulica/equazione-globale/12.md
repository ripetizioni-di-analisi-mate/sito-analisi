A horizontal diffuser increases the pipe diameter from $D_1 = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ to $D_2 = 350\,\mathrm{mm}$ and carries discharge $Q = 100\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The inlet and outlet gauge pressures are $p_1 = 160\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and $p_2 = 163.8\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the magnitude of the fluid force on the diffuser?

::schema{acqua="0,0.95 2.6,0.95 3.8,0.5 7,0.5 7,2.1 3.8,2.1 2.6,1.65 0,1.65" linee="0,0.95 2.6,0.95 3.8,0.5 7,0.5;0,1.65 2.6,1.65 3.8,2.1 7,2.1" frecce="0.5,1.3,1.6,1.3,Q" tratteggi="1.6,0.7 1.6,1.9;5.6,0.25 5.6,2.35" etichette="1.6,2.2,1;5.6,2.65,2;0.3,1.9,D_1 = 200 mm;5.2,0.0,D_2 = 350 mm" riferimento=0 titolo="Diffuser expanding from $200$ to $350\,\mathrm{mm}$"}

- $5.03\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $15.8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $20.8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The two areas $A_1 = 0.0314\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $A_2 = 0.0962\,\mathrm{m^2}$ give velocities $v_1 = 3.18\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2 = 1.04\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and pressure forces $p_1 A_1 = 5.03\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $p_2 A_2 = 15.76\,\mathrm{kN}$. Taking the pipe axis as positive downstream, the integral momentum equation gives
$$
F = p_2 A_2 - p_1 A_1 + \rho\,Q\,(v_2 - v_1) = 15.76 - 5.03 - 0.21 = 10.5\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The diffuser increases both area and pressure, so the downstream contribution exceeds the upstream contribution. The force acts upstream, opposite to the force on a reducer. The values $5.03\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $15.8\,\mathrm{kN}$ are the individual section pressure forces, and $20.8\,\mathrm{kN}$ is their sum.
