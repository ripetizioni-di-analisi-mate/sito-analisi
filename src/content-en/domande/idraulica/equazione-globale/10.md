A vertical sluice gate spans a rectangular channel of width $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and horizontal bed. Water depths are $h_1 = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ upstream and $h_2 = 0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ downstream. Neglecting losses, what is the discharge?

::schema{acqua="0,0 3,0 3,3 0,3;3,0 3.3,0 3.3,0.6 3,0.6;3.3,0 8,0 8,0.6 3.3,0.6" solidi="0,-0.45 8,-0.45 8,0 0,0;3,0.6 3.3,0.6 3.3,4 3,4" pelo="0,3,3,p_a;3.3,8,0.6" quote="0 0,0 3,h_1 = 3.00 m,-1.0" frecce="0.6,1.4,1.8,1.4,Q" etichette="2,3.4,1;5.2,1.6,2;7.3,1.1,h_2 = 0.60 m;5,2.8,b = 4.00 m" riferimento=0 titolo="Gate in a channel of width $4.00\,\mathrm{m}$"}

- $4.12\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $16.8\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $16.5\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $18.4\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$

## Explanation

With losses neglected, total head is conserved from upstream to downstream. Expressing the velocities as $v = \dfrac{Q}{b h}$ gives Bernoulli’s equation in the form
$$
h_1 + \frac{Q^2}{2 g b^2 h_1^2} = h_2 + \frac{Q^2}{2 g b^2 h_2^2} .
$$
Solving for discharge gives $Q = 16.8\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, with velocities $v_1 = 1.40\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2 = 7.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting the upstream velocity head, equivalent to setting $Q = b h_2 \sqrt{2 g (h_1 - h_2)}$, gives $16.5\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The value $18.4\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ comes from formula $Q = b h_2 \sqrt{2 g h_1}$, which would use the full upstream water depth as the head at the opening.
