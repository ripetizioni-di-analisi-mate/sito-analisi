In a horizontal tee, inlet $1$ has diameter $D_1 = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ and carries discharge $Q_1 = 120\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The straight outlet has diameter $D_2 = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ and discharge $Q_2 = 70\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The side branch turns through $90^\circ$ and has diameter $D_3 = 150\,\mathrm{mm}$ and discharge $Q_3 = 50\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The gauge pressures are $p_1 = 220\,\mathrm{kPa}$, $p_2 = 220.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$, and $p_3 = 219.0\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the magnitude of the force the fluid exerts on the tee?

::schema{nodi="A,0,0,-;B,3,0,-;C,6,0,-;D,3,2.6,-" condotte="A-C,0.6;B-D,0.45" frecce="0.3,0,1.3,0,Q_1;4.4,0,5.4,0,Q_2;3,1.5,3,2.4,Q_3" tratteggi="1.2,-0.55 1.2,0.55;4.8,-0.55 4.8,0.55;2.6,1.9 3.4,1.9" etichette="1.05,0.85,1;5.2,0.85,2;3.5,1.55,3;0.3,-0.85,D_1 = 250 mm;4.6,-0.85,D_2 = 200 mm;4.3,2.3,D_3 = 150 mm" riferimento=0 titolo="Plan view of a pipe tee"}

- $4.01\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8.02\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $5.67\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10.8\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The velocities are $v_1 = 2.44\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $v_2 = 2.23\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and $v_3 = 2.83\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Each section pressure force points into the control volume. Take $x$ along the straight branch and $y$ along the side branch. The two momentum components give
$$
\begin{cases}
\rho\,(Q_2 v_2 - Q_1 v_1) = p_1 A_1 - p_2 A_2 + R_x \\[4pt]
\rho\,Q_3 v_3 = - p_3 A_3 + R_y
\end{cases}
$$
so each component of the force on the junction has magnitude $4.01\,\mathrm{kN}$. The first points with the incoming flow and the second points opposite the side-branch flow. Their resultant has magnitude $F = \sqrt{4.01^2 + 4.01^2} = 5.67\,\mathrm{kN}$. The arithmetic sum $8.02\,\mathrm{kN}$ does not give the vector magnitude.
