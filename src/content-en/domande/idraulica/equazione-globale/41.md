A steady atmospheric water jet strikes a flat, fixed, smooth plate. A sensor on the plate detects a nonzero mean tangential force. Which feature should be checked first in a model that attributes the entire force to pressure?

- The reference elevation in the energy balance.
- The total water mass in the supply tank.
- The absolute pressure scale assigned to all atmospheric surfaces.
- Sensor alignment relative to the surface and any actual friction.

## Explanation

Pressure forces act normally at every point on a flat plate. A measured tangential component therefore calls for a check of the measurement direction or a physical source of tangential stress.
