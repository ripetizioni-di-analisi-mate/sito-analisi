A horizontal $90^\circ$ elbow of diameter $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries discharge $Q = 100\,\mathrm{L/s}$ at gauge pressure $p = 400\,\mathrm{kPa}$ in both sections. It is anchored by a concrete block resting on the ground with friction coefficient $f = 0.50$. Neglect the lateral soil resistance and the weights of the elbow and its water. What minimum block weight prevents sliding?

- $80.4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $40.2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $56.8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $20.1\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The area is $A = 0.0707\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the mean velocity is $v = 1.41\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so $K = \rho Q v + p A = 0.14 + 28.27 = 28.42\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the fluid exerts a horizontal force of $F = 2 K \sin 45^\circ = 40.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the elbow. The block remains at rest if the maximum friction force at the ground, $f$ times the block weight, can balance this horizontal force. The condition is $f\,W \ge F$, giving
$$
W \ge \frac{F}{f} = \frac{40.2}{0.50} = 80.4\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The value $40.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the fluid force alone. The value $56.8\,\mathrm{kN}$ divides only one force component by $f$, while $20.1\,\mathrm{kN}$ multiplies by $f$ instead of dividing.
