A horizontal pipe offset has two opposed $45^\circ$ elbows, so the inlet and outlet velocities have the same direction, as shown. The diameter is $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$, the discharge $Q = 100\,\mathrm{L/s}$, and the gauge pressure $200\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at every section. What is the resultant fluid force on the entire offset?

::schema{nodi="A,0,0,-;B,2.2,0,-;C,3.6,1.4,-;D,5.8,1.4,-" condotte="A-B-C-D,0.5" tratteggi="0.8,-0.5 0.8,0.5;5,0.9 5,1.9" frecce="0.1,0,0.7,0,Q" etichette="0.8,0.8,1;5,2.2,2;1.1,-0.8,D = 250 mm;3.3,0.2,45°" riferimento=0 titolo="Plan view of an offset pipe"}

- $7.67\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $15.3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10.0\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Choose the fluid between the inlet and outlet of the entire assembly as the control volume. The diameter is constant, and the inlet and outlet velocities have the same magnitude and direction, so the net momentum flux is zero. The sections also have the same area and pressure, so their inward pressure forces are equal and opposite. The momentum balance gives $\vec{R} = 0$, a zero net force on the assembly. Each elbow nevertheless experiences a force of $2 K \sin 22.5^\circ = 7.67\,\mathrm{kN}$, with $K = \rho Q v + pA = 10.0\,\mathrm{kN}$. These equal, opposite forces act along different lines and form a couple that the anchors must resist.
