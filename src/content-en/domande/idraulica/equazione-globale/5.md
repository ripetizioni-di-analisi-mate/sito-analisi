A nozzle on a horizontal pipe of diameter $D_1 = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ discharges to the atmosphere through an opening of diameter $d_2 = 70\,\mathrm{mm}$. Upstream gauge pressure is $p_1 = 180\,\mathrm{kPa}$, and Bernoulli’s equation gives discharge $Q = 73.6\,\mathrm{L/s}$. What force does the fluid exert on the nozzle?

::schema{acqua="0,0.75 4,0.75 4.6,1.05 7.6,1.05 7.6,1.55 4.6,1.55 4,1.85 0,1.85" linee="0,0.75 4,0.75 4.6,1.05 6.4,1.05;0,1.85 4,1.85 4.6,1.55 6.4,1.55" frecce="0.5,1.3,1.6,1.3,Q" tratteggi="2.4,0.5 2.4,2.1;6.4,0.8 6.4,1.8" etichette="2.4,2.4,1;6.4,2.1,2;0.3,2.1,D_1 = 200 mm;5.4,0.5,d_2 = 70 mm;0.5,2.7,p_1 = 180 kPa" riferimento=0 titolo="Nozzle on a pipe of diameter $200\,\mathrm{mm}$"}

- $1.41\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4.42\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6.89\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $5.65\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The inlet and outlet velocities are $v_1 = \dfrac{Q}{A_1} = 2.34\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2 = \dfrac{Q}{A_2} = 19.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The outlet is open to the atmosphere, so its gauge pressure and pressure force are zero, leaving
$$
F = p_1 A_1 - \rho\,Q\,(v_2 - v_1) = 5.65 - 1.24 = 4.42\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
directed downstream. The value $5.65\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the upstream pressure force alone. Adding the change in momentum flux instead of subtracting it would give $6.89\,\mathrm{kN}$.
