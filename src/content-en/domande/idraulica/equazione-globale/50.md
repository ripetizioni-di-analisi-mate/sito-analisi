Approximate the velocity profile in a rectangular channel by two equal-depth layers. The local speed is $1.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the upper half and $0.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the lower half. What is the momentum correction factor $\beta$?

- $1.00$
- $1.25$
- $1.75$
- $2.25$

## Explanation

The two layers have equal areas $\dfrac{A}{2}$, so the mean velocity is $v = \dfrac{1.2 + 0.4}{2} = 0.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In the definition of $\beta$, split the integral into separate contributions from the two layers, giving
$$
\beta = \frac{1}{v^2 A}\int_A u^2\,dA = \frac{0.5 \cdot 1.2^2 + 0.5 \cdot 0.4^2}{0.8^2} = \frac{0.80}{0.64} = 1.25 .
$$
The value $1.00$ applies only to a uniform velocity distribution. The value $1.75$ is the kinetic energy correction factor $\alpha$, calculated using the cubes of the local velocities. The value $2.25$ squares the ratio of maximum to mean speed and neglects the slower layer.
