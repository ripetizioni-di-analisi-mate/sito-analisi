Two pipe elbows have the same diameter, discharge, and section pressures. The first turns the flow through $90^\circ$ and the second through $45^\circ$. What is the ratio of the force on the first elbow to that on the second?

- $1.41$
- $2.00$
- $1.85$
- $4.00$

## Explanation

With the same diameter, discharge, and pressure, $K = \rho Q v + p A$ is the same for both elbows. The force therefore depends on the deflection angle alone through $F = 2 K \sin\dfrac{\theta}{2}$. The ratio of the force magnitudes is
$$
\frac{F_{90}}{F_{45}} = \frac{\sin 45^\circ}{\sin 22.5^\circ} = \frac{0.707}{0.383} = 1.85 .
$$
The value $2.00$ would require force to be directly proportional to the angle. The sine of the half-angle increases more slowly, so doubling the deflection angle produces less than twice the force on the elbow.
