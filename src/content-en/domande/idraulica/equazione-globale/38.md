A fitting divides the incoming discharge $Q_1$ into two branch discharges $Q_2$ and $Q_3$. How many pressure forces at flow sections appear in the integral momentum equation, and how are they directed?

- Two, because the two branch sections combine into one
- Three, one at each section, all pointing into the control volume
- Three, one at each section, all pointing out of the control volume
- One, at the inlet only

## Explanation

The control surface has three flow sections, one at the inlet and one in each outlet branch. The momentum equation therefore contains a pressure force for each section. Each force is due to pressure exerted by the surrounding fluid and is written along the inward normal to the control volume. The outgoing momentum flux is the sum of the two branch contributions, and continuity at the junction gives $Q_1 = Q_2 + Q_3$. The same form of the momentum equation applies to any number of inlet and outlet sections.
