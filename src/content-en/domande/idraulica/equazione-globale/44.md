A rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and horizontal bed carries discharge $Q = 8.40\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. A vertical flat gate changes the depth from $h_1 = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ upstream to $h_2 = 0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ downstream. Neglecting bed resistance, what force does the flow exert on the gate?

::schema{acqua="0,0 3,0 3,2 0,2;3,0 3.3,0 3.3,0.5 3,0.5;3.3,0 8,0 8,0.5 3.3,0.5" solidi="0,-0.4 8,-0.4 8,0 0,0;3,0.5 3.3,0.5 3.3,3 3,3" pelo="0,3,2,p_a;3.3,8,0.5" quote="0 0,0 2,h_1 = 2.00 m,-0.9" frecce="0.6,1,1.8,1,Q" etichette="2,2.4,1;5.2,1.3,2;7.2,0.9,h_2 = 0.50 m;5,2.2,b = 3.00 m" riferimento=0 titolo="Gate in a channel of width $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$"}

- $55.2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $19.9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $58.9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $90.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The mean velocities are $v_1 = \dfrac{Q}{b h_1} = 1.40\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2 = \dfrac{Q}{b h_2} = 5.60\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The two sections are vertical and pressure is distributed hydrostatically, so their pressure forces have magnitudes $\dfrac{\gamma b h_1^2}{2} = 58.9\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $\dfrac{\gamma b h_2^2}{2} = 3.68\,\mathrm{kN}$. The bed is horizontal and its resistance is neglected. The streamwise momentum equation therefore gives the water force on the gate as
$$
S = \frac{\gamma b h_1^2}{2} - \frac{\gamma b h_2^2}{2} - \rho\,Q\,(v_2 - v_1) = 58.9 - 3.68 - 35.3 = 19.9\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
directed downstream. The value $55.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the difference in hydrostatic forces alone. It is nearly three times the actual gate force because part of the upstream pressure force accelerates the flow.
