A pipe bend of angle $90^\circ$ and diameter $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ lies in a horizontal plane and carries a discharge of $Q = 80\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The gauge pressure is $p = 300\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at both the inlet and the outlet. What is the magnitude of the force that the flow exerts on the bend?

::schema{nodi="A,0,0,-;B,3.2,0,-;C,3.2,3.2,-" condotte="A-B-C,0.55" tratteggi="0.9,-0.6 0.9,0.6;2.65,2.2 3.75,2.2" frecce="0.1,0,0.9,0,Q" etichette="0.72,0.9,1;3.95,2.2,2;0.2,-0.95,D = 250 mm" riferimento=0 titolo="Plan view of an $90^\circ$ elbow"}

- $14.9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $21.0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $29.7\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $83.4\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The cross-sectional area is $A = \dfrac{\pi D^2}{4} = 0.0491\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so the mean velocity is $v = \dfrac{Q}{A} = 1.63\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Since the diameter is constant and the pressures are equal, the two contributions to the momentum balance combine into $K = \rho Q v + p A = 0.130 + 14.73 = 14.86\,\mathrm{kN}$. The expression for the force on a bend then gives
$$
F = 2 K \sin\frac{\theta}{2} = 2 \cdot 14.86 \cdot \sin 45^\circ = 21.0\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
directed outward along the bisector of the bend. The two components each have magnitude $14.86\,\mathrm{kN}$. Thus $14.9\,\mathrm{kN}$ gives the magnitude of just one component of the resultant force.
