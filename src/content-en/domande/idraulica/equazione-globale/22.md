In a straight horizontal pipe, a control volume extends from upstream section $1$ to downstream section $2$. Gauge pressures are positive at both sections. With axis $x$ positive downstream, what are the signs of the two pressure-force components?

- Both positive
- Both negative
- $\Pi_{1x}$ positive and $\Pi_{2x}$ negative
- $\Pi_{1x}$ negative and $\Pi_{2x}$ positive

## Explanation

Section pressure forces point along the inward normals to the control volume. At the inlet, this normal is in the direction of velocity, making $\Pi_{1x} = p_1 A_1$ positive. At the outlet, it points against the velocity, making $\Pi_{2x} = -p_2 A_2$ negative. The upstream and downstream fluids therefore press on the control volume from opposite sides. For negative gauge pressures, the force directions reverse because $p$ changes sign while the unit normal stays fixed.
