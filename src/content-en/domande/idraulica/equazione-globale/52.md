In a rectangular channel of width $b$, depth $h$, discharge $Q$, and mean speed $v$, the Froude number is $Fr = \dfrac{v}{\sqrt{g h}}$. What is the ratio of momentum flux $\rho Q v$ to the hydrostatic force $\dfrac{\gamma b h^2}{2}$ on the section?

- $2\,Fr^2$
- $Fr^2$
- $\dfrac{Fr^2}{2}$
- $2\,Fr$

## Explanation

The discharge is $Q = b h v$, so the momentum flux is $\rho Q v = \rho b h v^2$. Since $\gamma = \rho g$, the ratio of momentum flux to hydrostatic force becomes
$$
\frac{2\,\rho\,b\,h\,v^2}{\rho\,g\,b\,h^2} = \frac{2 v^2}{g h} = 2\,Fr^2 .
$$
In subcritical flow at $Fr = 0.3$, the momentum flux is less than one fifth of the hydrostatic force. In supercritical flow at $Fr = 3$ it is eighteen times the hydrostatic force. This explains why $\rho Q v$ dominates at the vena contracta downstream of a gate, while the hydrostatic force dominates upstream.
