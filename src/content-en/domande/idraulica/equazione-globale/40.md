A water jet of diameter $d = 60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and speed $v = 12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ strikes a fixed, smooth, flat plate at angle $\alpha = 30^\circ$ to its plane. Neglecting friction, what force does the jet exert on the plate?

- $204\,\mathrm{N}$
- $353\,\mathrm{N}$
- $407\,\mathrm{N}$
- $102\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The jet area is $A = 2.827 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, giving a discharge of $Q = A v = 33.9\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and a momentum flux of $\rho Q v = 407\,\mathrm{N}$. The smooth plate exerts no tangential force, so the force is normal to the plate and depends on the normal component of the jet velocity, $v\sin\alpha$. Thus
$$
F = \rho\,Q\,v\,\sin\alpha = 407 \cdot \sin 30^\circ = 204\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $407\,\mathrm{N}$ applies to a plate perpendicular to the jet, with $\alpha = 90^\circ$. The value $353\,\mathrm{N}$ uses $\cos\alpha$ in place of $\sin\alpha$ and hence uses the component parallel to the plate, which produces no tangential force on a smooth surface.
