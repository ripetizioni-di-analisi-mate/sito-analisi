A water jet of diameter $d = 40\,\mathrm{mm}$ leaves a nozzle at speed $v = 18\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and strikes a stationary flat plate perpendicular to the jet axis. What force does the jet exert on the plate?

- $407\,\mathrm{N}$
- $204\,\mathrm{N}$
- $814\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1630\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The jet has area $A = \dfrac{\pi d^2}{4} = 1.257 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and discharge $Q = A v = 22.6\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Gauge pressure and hence section pressure force are zero throughout the free jet. The outgoing sheets travel parallel to the plate and carry no momentum component along the incoming jet axis. The momentum balance along that axis gives
$$
S = \rho\,Q\,v = 1000 \cdot 0.0226 \cdot 18 = 407\,\mathrm{N} ,
$$
in the direction of the jet velocity. Using $\dfrac{1}{2}\rho v^2 A$, the dynamic pressure, in place of the momentum flux would give $204\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $814\,\mathrm{N}$ applies to a vane deflecting the jet through $180^\circ$.
