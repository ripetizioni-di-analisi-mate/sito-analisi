A horizontal $180^\circ$ bend of diameter $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries discharge $Q = 140\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Upstream and downstream gauge pressures are $p_1 = 180\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and $p_2 = 179.2\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the magnitude of the force the flow exerts on the bend?

::schema{nodi="A,0,0,-;B,3,0,-;C,3,1.9,-;D,0,1.9,-" condotte="A-B-C-D,0.55" frecce="0.1,0,0.9,0,Q" tratteggi="0.4,-0.55 0.4,2.45" etichette="0.25,0.6,1;0.25,1.4,2;1.6,0.95,D = 300 mm" riferimento=0 titolo="Plan view of an $180^\circ$ bend"}

- $25.9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0.61\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12.7\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $18.4\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The area is $A = 0.0707\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the speed is $v = \dfrac{Q}{A} = 1.98\,\mathrm{m/s}$, giving $\rho Q v = 277\,\mathrm{N}$ at each section. The inlet and outlet are parallel and face opposite ways, so their inward normals point in the same direction. The two pressure forces therefore add:
$$
F = 2\,\rho\,Q\,v + (p_1 + p_2)\,A = 0.55 + 25.39 = 25.9\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
in the direction of the inlet velocity. Subtracting the two pressure forces, as if they opposed each other, would give $0.61\,\mathrm{kN}$. Using an elbow angle of $\theta = 90^\circ$ in the force formula would give $18.4\,\mathrm{kN}$.
