In a horizontal rectangular channel, a flat gate reduces the water depth downstream. Choose a control volume between an upstream and a downstream section, bounded by the bed, gate, and free surface. In a balance using gauge pressures, which part of the control surface exerts no force on the fluid?

- The downstream section
- The channel bed
- The free surface
- The gate

## Explanation

Atmospheric pressure is the only pressure acting on the free surface. Its gauge value is zero, so the air contributes no force in a balance written using gauge pressures. The downstream section has a hydrostatic pressure distribution and exerts a pressure force $\dfrac{\gamma b h_2^2}{2}$ directed upstream. The bed supports the fluid weight, so its vertical force enters the vertical momentum equation. The gate resists the flow, and the force it exerts on the water is the horizontal component of $\vec{R}$ sought here.
