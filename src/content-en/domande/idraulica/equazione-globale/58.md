A horizontal $90^\circ$ elbow of constant diameter receives flow along the $x$-axis and discharges along the positive $y$-axis. Let $Q$ be the discharge, $v$ the speed, $A$ the area, and $p$ the outlet pressure. Which equation gives the $y$ component of the momentum balance?

- $\rho Q v = p A + R_y$
- $\rho Q v = -p A + R_y$
- $-\rho Q v = -p A + R_y$
- $0 = p A - p A + R_y$

## Explanation

The inlet velocity is along $x$ and the outlet velocity along $y$. The $y$ component of the net momentum flux is therefore $\rho Q\,(v - 0) = \rho Q v$. The inlet pressure force acts along $x$ and has no component along $y$. The outlet pressure force points into the control volume, opposite the outgoing velocity, giving a component of $-pA$. Weight is perpendicular to the plane of the elbow. The component equation is therefore $\rho Q v = -pA + R_y$, which gives $R_y = \rho Q v + p A$.
