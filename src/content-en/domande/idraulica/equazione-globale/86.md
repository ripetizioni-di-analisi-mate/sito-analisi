A trapezoidal channel has bottom width $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and side slope $45^\circ$, as shown. At a section where the flow is one-dimensional, the depth is $h = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$. What pressure force on this section enters the momentum equation?

::schema{acqua="0,0 2,0 3,1 -1,1" solidi="-1.6,-0.4 0,-0.4 0,0 -1.2,1.2 -1.6,1.2;0,-0.4 2,-0.4 2,0 0,0;2,-0.4 3.6,-0.4 3.6,1.2 3.2,1.2 2,0" pelo="-1,3,1" quote="0 0,2 0,b = 2.00 m,0.75;4.0 1,4.0 0,h = 1.00 m,-0.4" etichette="2.9,0.25,45°" riferimento=0 titolo="Trapezoidal channel cross section"}

- $9.81\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $14.7\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $19.6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $13.1\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Straight, parallel streamlines give a hydrostatic pressure distribution over the section. The pressure force is $\Pi = \gamma\,h_G\,A$, where $h_G$ is the depth of the flow-area centroid below the free surface. The top width is $B = b + 2h = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and the area is $A = \dfrac{(b + B)\,h}{2} = 3.00\,\mathrm{m^2}$. For the trapezoid the centroid depth is
$$
h_G = \frac{h}{3}\,\frac{B + 2b}{B + b} = \frac{1.00}{3} \cdot \frac{4.00 + 4.00}{6.00} = 0.444\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
so $\Pi = 9.81 \cdot 0.444 \cdot 3.00 = 13.1\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $14.7\,\mathrm{kN}$ places the centroid at middepth, as for a rectangle. The values $9.81\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $19.6\,\mathrm{kN}$ use a rectangular section with the bottom width and the top width, respectively.
