The integral momentum equation for a control volume gives $R_x = -8.40\,\mathrm{kN}$ along the chosen $x$-axis. What follows from this result?

- The walls exert a force $8.40\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the fluid in the positive $x$ direction
- The fluid exerts a force $8.40\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the walls in the positive $x$ direction
- The fluid exerts a force $8.40\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the walls in the negative $x$ direction
- Outgoing momentum flux exceeds incoming momentum flux by $8.40\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

In the integral momentum equation, $\vec{R}$ is the resultant force of the walls on the fluid. Its component is negative, so the walls push the fluid in the negative $x$ direction with a force of $8.40\,\mathrm{kN}$. By Newton’s third law, the force that the fluid exerts on the walls is $-\vec{R}$. Its magnitude is therefore $8.40\,\mathrm{kN}$ and it acts in the positive $x$ direction. The anchors must balance this force on the fitting.
