When the integral momentum equation is used to find the fluid force on a flat sluice gate in a horizontal rectangular channel, where should the downstream flow section be placed?

- At the opening under the lower edge of the gate
- At the vena contracta, where the pathlines become straight and parallel again
- Far downstream, where flow has reached normal depth
- At any section, because pressure over a horizontal bed is always hydrostatic

## Explanation

The downstream pressure force is $\dfrac{\gamma b h_2^2}{2}$ only when the pressure distribution is hydrostatic, as in a section with straight, parallel streamlines. Immediately under the gate edge the jet is still contracting and its streamlines are curved. The pressure distribution there is nonhydrostatic, so that formula does not apply. Slightly farther downstream the jet reaches the vena contracta, where the streamlines are again straight and parallel. This is the appropriate downstream section. A section much farther away would introduce appreciable bed resistance, which the gate-force formula neglects.
