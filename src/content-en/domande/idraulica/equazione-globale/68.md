A symmetrical horizontal wye receives discharge $Q_1 = 120\,\mathrm{L/s}$ through a pipe of diameter $D_1 = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ and divides it equally between two branches of diameter $D_2 = 150\,\mathrm{mm}$, inclined at $30^\circ$ on opposite sides of the inlet axis, as shown. The inlet gauge pressure is $p_1 = 200\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Neglecting losses, what force does the fluid exert on the wye?

::schema{nodi="A,0,0,-;B,3,0,-;C,5.6,1.5,-;E,5.6,-1.5,-" condotte="A-B,0.6;B-C,0.38;B-E,0.38" tratteggi="1.6,-0.55 1.6,0.55" frecce="0.1,0,0.8,0,Q_1" etichette="1.6,0.85,1;5.95,1.75,2;5.95,-1.75,3;0.5,-0.85,D_1 = 250 mm;3.6,1.6,D_2 = 150 mm;4.4,0,30°" riferimento=0 titolo="Plan view of a symmetrical Y-junction"}

- $3.72\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2.73\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6.42\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6.92\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The inlet pipe velocity is $v_1 = 2.44\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The velocity in each branch is $v_2 = 3.40\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at a discharge of $60\,\mathrm{L/s}$, and Bernoulli’s equation gives $p_2 = p_1 - \dfrac{\rho\,(v_2^2 - v_1^2)}{2} = 197.2\,\mathrm{kPa}$ in the branches. By symmetry, the branch components perpendicular to the inlet axis cancel, so the resultant acts along that axis. Projecting onto it gives a contribution $p_2 A_2 + \rho Q_2 v_2 = 3.49 + 0.20 = 3.69\,\mathrm{kN}$ times $\cos 30^\circ$ from each branch, hence
$$
F = p_1 A_1 + \rho Q_1 v_1 - 2 \cos 30^\circ\,(p_2 A_2 + \rho Q_2 v_2) = 10.11 - 6.39 = 3.72\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
in the direction of the incoming flow. The value $2.73\,\mathrm{kN}$ treats the branches as parallel to the inlet axis, omitting the cosine. Using the sine in place of the cosine would give $6.42\,\mathrm{kN}$.
