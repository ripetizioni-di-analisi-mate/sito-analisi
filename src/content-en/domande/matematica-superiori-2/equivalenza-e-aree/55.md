A triangle has base $26$ cm and area $78$ cm². What is the corresponding altitude in centimeters?

- $3$
- $12$
- $6$
- $13$

## Explanation

The area of the triangle is half the product of the base and altitude. Double the area and divide by the base. This gives $6$.
