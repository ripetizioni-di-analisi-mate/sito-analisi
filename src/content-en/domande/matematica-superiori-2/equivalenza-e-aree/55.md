In the coordinate plane, $A=(0;0)$ and $B=(6;0)$. Which lines contain all possible vertices $C$ of triangles $ABC$ with area $24$?

- $y=8$
- $x=8$ or $x=-8$
- $y=8$ or $y=-8$
- $y=4$ or $y=-4$

## Explanation

Base $AB$ lies on the horizontal axis and has length $6$. Area $24$ requires altitude $2\cdot24/6=8$. The distance from $C$ to the axis is the absolute value of its vertical coordinate, so that coordinate can be $8$ or $-8$. The horizontal coordinate can vary since the altitude foot can lie outside $AB$. Both sides of the base line give the same positive area.
