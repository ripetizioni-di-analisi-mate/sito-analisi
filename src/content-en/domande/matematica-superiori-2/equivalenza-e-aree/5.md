A rhombus has diagonals of lengths $48\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $36\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its area?

- $865$ cm²
- $863$ cm²
- $1728$ cm²
- $864$ cm²

## Explanation

The diagonals of a rhombus are perpendicular and bisect each other. The four resulting right triangles have a total area equal to half the product of the diagonals. This formula is therefore applied.

$$
A=\frac{Dd}2=\frac{48\cdot36}2=864
$$
