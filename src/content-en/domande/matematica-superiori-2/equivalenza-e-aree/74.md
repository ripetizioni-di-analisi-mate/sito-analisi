A square has side length $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. Cutting a right triangle with legs of $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ from one corner leaves a pentagon. What is its area?

- $84\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $92\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $96\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $108\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

Both perpendicular legs measure $4$. The cut removes half of a square with side length $4$. The original area was $100$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A&=10^2-\frac{4^2}2=92\,\mathrm{cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$$
