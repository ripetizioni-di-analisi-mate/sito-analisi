A regular hexagon has side length $8$. Which apothem should be used for its area?

- $4\sqrt3$
- $8$
- $4$
- $8\sqrt3$

## Explanation

The centre and two consecutive vertices form an equilateral triangle of side $8$. The perpendicular from the centre splits that side into lengths $4$ and gives a right triangle with hypotenuse $8$. The apothem is $\sqrt{8^2-4^2}=\sqrt{48}=4\sqrt3$. The radius $8$ reaches a vertex, whereas the apothem is the perpendicular distance to a side line.
