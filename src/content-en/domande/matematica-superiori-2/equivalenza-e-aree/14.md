A regular hexagon has side length $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ and apothem $7 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find its area.

- $294 \sqrt{3}$ cm²
- $1 + 294 \sqrt{3}$ cm²
- $-1 + 294 \sqrt{3}$ cm²
- $588 \sqrt{3}$ cm²

## Explanation

The polygon splits into six triangles with altitude equal to the apothem. The sum of the bases is the perimeter. The total area is therefore half the product of the perimeter and the apothem.

$$
P=6\cdot14=84
$$

$$
A=\frac{P a}2=\frac{84\cdot(7 \sqrt{3})}2=294 \sqrt{3}
$$
