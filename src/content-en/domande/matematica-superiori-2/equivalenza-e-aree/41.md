Two figures with equal area must have

- $\text{The same perimeter}$
- $\text{The same shape}$
- $\text{The same sides}$
- $\text{The same area}$

## Explanation

Equality of area defines this relationship. The perimeter and shape can differ. Rectangles with equal products of their sides illustrate this distinction.
