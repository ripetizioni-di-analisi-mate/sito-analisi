In triangle $ABC$, $M$ is the midpoint of $AB$. The area of $AMC$ is $24$ and $AB=12$. What is the altitude from $C$?

- $4$
- $8$
- $2$
- $12$

## Explanation

Triangle $AMC$ has base $AM=6$ and the same altitude as $ABC$. Hence $6h/2=24$, giving $h=8$. Another way starts by doubling the area of $AMC$. Since $MBC$ has the same base and height, the area of $ABC$ is $48$, and $2\cdot48/12$ again gives $8$. The midpoint halves the base and area while the altitude stays the same.
