A triangle has base $22$ cm and area $55$ cm². What is the corresponding altitude in centimeters?

- $11$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $10$

## Explanation

The area of the triangle is half the product of the base and altitude. Double the area and divide by the base. This gives $5$.
