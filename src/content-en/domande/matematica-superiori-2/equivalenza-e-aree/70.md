A square with side length $13$ cm is divided by a diagonal. What is the area of one of the triangles in square centimeters?

- $\dfrac{169}{2}$
- $13$
- $169$
- $26$

## Explanation

The diagonal splits the square into congruent triangles. Each occupies half the square of the side length. This gives $\dfrac{169}{2}$.
