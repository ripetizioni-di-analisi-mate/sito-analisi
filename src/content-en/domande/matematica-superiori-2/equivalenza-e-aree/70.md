A diagonal of a rectangle forms a triangle of area $25$. The longer side is $5$ more than the shorter one. What is the longer side length?

- $10$
- $5$
- $15$
- $25$

## Explanation

The diagonal gives two triangles of area $25$, so the rectangle’s area is $50$. Letting $x$ be the shorter side gives $x(x+5)=50$, or $(x-5)(x+10)=0$. Only $5$ is positive, so the longer side is $5+5=10$. Check that $5\cdot10=50$ and that the two side lengths have the required difference.
