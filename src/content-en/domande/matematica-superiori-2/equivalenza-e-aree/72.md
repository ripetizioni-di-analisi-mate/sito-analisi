A rectangle measures $7$ cm by $4$ cm. A square of side length $3$ cm is removed from a corner. What is the remaining area in square centimeters?

- $4$
- $19$
- $28$
- $37$

## Explanation

Multiply the sides to find the rectangle’s area. Subtract the square of the side length of the removed piece. This gives $19$.
