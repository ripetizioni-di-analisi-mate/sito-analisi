A $4\times3$ rectangle is removed from a corner of a $10\times8$ rectangle. What percentage of the original area is removed?

- $12\%$
- $15\%$
- $25\%$
- $85\%$

## Explanation

The whole area is $10\cdot8=80$, and the removed area is $4\cdot3=12$. The fraction removed is $12/80=15/100$, or $15\%$. The remaining area is $68$, which is $85\%$ of the original. Use the whole rectangle’s area as denominator because the percentage refers to it. The number $12$ alone is an area, not a percentage.
