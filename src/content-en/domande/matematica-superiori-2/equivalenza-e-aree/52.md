A triangle of area $45\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ has an altitude of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the base corresponding to that altitude.

- $\frac{15}{2}$
- $30$
- $45$
- $15$

## Explanation

Two copies of the triangle would have area $90\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ and form a parallelogram of the same height. Dividing that area by $6$ gives a base length of $15\,\mathrm{cm}$. Doubling removes the factor of one half in the triangle formula. Check that $15\cdot6$ is $90$ and that halving it gives the assigned area.
