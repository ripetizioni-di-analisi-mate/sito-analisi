A triangle has base $14$ cm and area $21$ cm². What is the corresponding altitude in centimeters?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $7$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

The area of the triangle is half the product of the base and altitude. Double the area and divide by the base. This gives $3$.
