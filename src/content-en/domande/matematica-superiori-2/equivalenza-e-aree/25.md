A rectangle has perimeter $30\,\mathrm{cm}$, and its base is twice its height. What is its area?

- $75\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $100\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $112\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $50\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

Half the perimeter equals the base plus the height. If $h$ is the height, the sum is $2h+h$, which equals $15$. Use the two dimensions to find the area.

$$
\begin{aligned}
3h&=15 \\[5pt]
\mathcal A&=10\cdot5=50\,\mathrm{cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$$
