Which formula gives the area of a triangle?

- $bh$
- $\dfrac{bh}{2}$
- $2bh$
- $\dfrac{b+h}{2}$

## Explanation

Two congruent triangles make up a parallelogram with base $b$ and height $h$. One triangle has half the area of the parallelogram. Its area is half the product of the base and height.
