A field consists of a rectangle $9$ m by $5$ m and a triangle attached along a side of length $9$ m, with altitude $8$ m. What is the total area in m²?

- $81$
- $9$
- $117$
- $45$

## Explanation

The rectangle contributes the product of its dimensions. The triangle shares the same base and contributes half the product of its base and altitude. This gives $81$.
