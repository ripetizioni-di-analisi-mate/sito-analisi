Rectangles $8\times3$ and $5\times4$ are joined along a segment of length $3$, with no other overlap. What is the perimeter of their union?

- $34$
- $40$
- $37$
- $44$

## Explanation

The initial perimeters are $22$ and $18$. The shared segment is counted once in each, but becomes internal when the figures are joined. Subtract its length twice to obtain $22+18-2\cdot3=34$. The two areas can be added directly because the shared part is only a segment with no area. Perimeter and area treat a shared boundary differently.
