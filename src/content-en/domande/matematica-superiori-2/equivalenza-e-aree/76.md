A regular hexagon has perimeter $24$ cm and apothem $2\sqrt3$ cm. What is its area in square centimetres?

- $24 \sqrt{3}$
- $48 \sqrt{3}$
- $24$
- $12 \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Splitting the polygon into triangles with altitude equal to the apothem gives half the product of the perimeter and apothem. This gives $24 \sqrt{3}$.
