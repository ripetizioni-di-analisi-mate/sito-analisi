A triangle and a parallelogram with equal areas both have base $9$. The parallelogram has height $8$. What is the triangle’s altitude?

- $8$
- $16$
- $4$
- $18$

## Explanation

The parallelogram has area $9\cdot8=72$. With the same base and altitude $8$, the triangle would have half that area because its formula includes a factor of $1/2$. It therefore needs twice the altitude, or $16$. Substituting gives $9\cdot16/2=72$. The shared base also lets you cancel $9$ directly in the area equality.
