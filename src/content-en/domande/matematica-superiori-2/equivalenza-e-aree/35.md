A parallelogram has base $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ and corresponding height $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find its area.

- $181$ cm²
- $180$ cm²
- $179$ cm²
- $360$ cm²

## Explanation

Use the perpendicular height to the base. A parallelogram has the same area as a rectangle with the same base and height. The slanted side does not appear in the area formula.

$$
A=bh=15\cdot12=180
$$
