The base of a rectangle of area $5$ cm² is doubled and its height is halved. What is the new area in square centimetres?

- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $20$
- $10$

## Explanation

In the product of the two dimensions, the factors two and one half cancel. The area remains the same. This gives $5$.
