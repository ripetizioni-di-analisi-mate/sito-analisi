The diagonals of a rhombus meet at $O$. Given $AO=4$ and $BD=10$, what is the area of the rhombus?

- $20$
- $80$
- $10$
- $40$

## Explanation

Segment $AO$ is half of $AC$ because the rhombus diagonals bisect each other. Thus $AC=8$, while the given $BD=10$ is already a whole diagonal. Half their product is $8\cdot10/2=40$. Each of four triangles with legs $4$ and $5$ has area ten, giving the same total. Distinguish a whole diagonal from one of its halves before calculating.
