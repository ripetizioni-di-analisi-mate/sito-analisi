A diagonal of a trapezoid divides it into triangles of areas $18\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ and $30\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. What is the ratio of the shorter base to the longer base?

- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{9}{25}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

Each base of the trapezoid is the base of one triangle. Their altitudes equal the distance between the bases. The common factor of half the altitude therefore cancels in the ratio of their areas.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac bB&=\frac{18}{30}=\frac35
\end{aligned}
$$
