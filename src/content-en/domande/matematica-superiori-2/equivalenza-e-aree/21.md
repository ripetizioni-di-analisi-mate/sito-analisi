In a convex quadrilateral the diagonals are perpendicular. They split into lengths $4$ and $3$ on the first, $5$ and $1$ on the second. What is the area?

- $42$
- $10.5$
- $24$
- $21$

## Explanation

The whole first diagonal measures $7$. It serves as the common base of the triangles on its two sides, with altitudes $5$ and $1$ because the second diagonal is perpendicular. Their areas add to $7(5+1)/2=21$. The diagonals do not bisect each other here, but the altitudes still form the second diagonal. Halve the product of the whole diagonals once.
