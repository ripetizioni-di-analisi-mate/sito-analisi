A regular hexagon has perimeter $72$ cm and apothem $6\sqrt3$ cm. What is its area in square centimeters?

- $108 \sqrt{3}$
- $216 \sqrt{3}$
- $216$
- $432 \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Splitting the polygon into triangles with altitude equal to the apothem gives half the product of the perimeter and apothem. This gives $216 \sqrt{3}$.
