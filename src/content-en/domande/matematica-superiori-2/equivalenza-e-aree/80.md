An interior point of a convex polygon is joined to the vertices. Which fact lets you calculate area as $Pa/2$ using altitude $a$ in every triangle?

- The point is at distance $a$ from every vertex.
- The point is at distance $a$ from every side line.
- All the diagonals of the polygon are congruent.
- Each side has length $a$.

## Explanation

Each triangle uses a side of the polygon as its base and the perpendicular distance from the interior point to that side line as altitude. If all these distances are $a$, factor $a/2$ out of the sum of the products, leaving perimeter $P$. Distances to vertices would instead be sides of the triangles. In general, a segment to a vertex is not an altitude.
