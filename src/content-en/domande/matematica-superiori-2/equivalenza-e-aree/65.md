Two rhombi have diagonals of lengths $6,8$ and $4,12$. Which relationship holds?

- They have equal areas and are not congruent.
- They are congruent because both pairs of diagonals are perpendicular.
- The second has twice the area.
- The first has larger area because its shorter diagonal is longer.

## Explanation

The products $6\cdot8$ and $4\cdot12$ both equal $48$, so both areas are $24$. Their pairs of diagonal lengths differ. Congruence would preserve all distances between vertices, including the two diagonals. Different combinations of diagonal lengths therefore produce the same area in one more elongated shape and one less elongated shape.
