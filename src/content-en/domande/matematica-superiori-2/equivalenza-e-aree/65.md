A rhombus has area $78$ cm² and one diagonal of length $12$ cm. How long is the other diagonal in centimeters?

- $13$
- $26$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{13}{2}$

## Explanation

The area is half the product of the diagonals. Divide twice the area by the given diagonal. This gives $13$.
