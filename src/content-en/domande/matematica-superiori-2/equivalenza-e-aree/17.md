Rectangle $A$ has sides of $4$ and $8$ centimetres. Rectangle $B$ has perimeter $24\,\mathrm{cm}$ and one side of $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. How do their areas compare?

- The two areas are equal.
- The area of $A$ exceeds that of $B$ by $3\,\mathrm{cm}^2$.
- The area of $B$ is twice that of $A$.
- The area of $B$ exceeds that of $A$ by $3\,\mathrm{cm}^2$.

## Explanation

Half the perimeter of $B$ is the sum of its two distinct side lengths. The missing side measures $12-5=7$. The two areas are the products $4\cdot8$ and $5\cdot7$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A_A&=32\,\mathrm{cm}^2 \\[5pt]
\mathcal A_B&=35\,\mathrm{cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$$
