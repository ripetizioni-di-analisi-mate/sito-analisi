A field consists of a rectangle $13$ m by $7$ m and a triangle attached along a side of length $13$ m, with altitude $12$ m. What is the total area in m²?

- $91$
- $169$
- $247$
- $13$

## Explanation

The rectangle contributes the product of its dimensions. The triangle shares the same base and contributes half the product of its base and altitude. This gives $169$.
