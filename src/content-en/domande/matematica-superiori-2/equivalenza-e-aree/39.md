A building face is a $12\times4$ rectangle with a triangle above it of base $12$ and altitude $3$. What fraction of the total area is the triangle?

- $\frac38$
- $\frac3{11}$
- $\frac37$
- $\frac23$

## Explanation

The rectangle’s area is $48$ and the triangle’s is $12\cdot3/2=18$. The requested ratio compares the triangle to the whole face, of area $66$. Simplify $18/66=3/11$. The shared edge separates the pieces but takes up no area and is not subtracted. Using $18/48$ would compare the two pieces without using the whole area as denominator.
