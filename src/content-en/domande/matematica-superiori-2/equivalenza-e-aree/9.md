A rectangular sheet measures $80\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $1.5\,\mathrm m$. What is its area in square metres?

- $12$
- $120$
- $12000$
- $\frac65$

## Explanation

First express both sides in the same unit. Eighty centimetres equal $0.8$ metres, so the product in square metres is $0.8\cdot1.5=1.2$. Converting the other side to centimetres would give $80\cdot150=12000\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. To convert that area, divide by $100^2$, since a square metre contains one hundred rows of one hundred one-centimetre squares.
