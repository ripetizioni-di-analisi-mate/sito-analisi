A trapezoid has area $56$ cm² and height $8$ cm. One base measures $9$ cm. How long is the other base in centimeters?

- $4$
- $10$
- $5$
- $14$

## Explanation

Double the area and divide by the height to find the sum of the bases. Subtract the known base. This gives $5$.
