A trapezoid’s bases are in ratio $2:3$ and differ by $4$. Its area is $70$. What is its height?

- $14$
- $10$
- $7$
- $5$

## Explanation

Write the bases as $2t$ and $3t$. Their difference gives $t=4$, so their lengths are $8$ and $12$. Their mean is $10$. Dividing the area by that mean gives height $70/10=7$. The check has two parts: first the base lengths must have the stated ratio and difference, and then their mean times the height must give the area.
