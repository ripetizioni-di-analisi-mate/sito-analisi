A rhombus has side length $13\,\mathrm{cm}$ and one diagonal of length $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its area?

- $60\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $120\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $130\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $240\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The diagonals meet perpendicularly at their midpoints. Half of the known diagonal measures $5$, so the hypotenuse of length $13$ gives the other half-diagonal. Use both full diagonals for the area.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac d2&=\sqrt{13^2-5^2}=12 \\[5pt]
\mathcal A&=\frac{10\cdot24}2=120\,\mathrm{cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$$
