How many square centimeters are in $1\,\mathrm m^2$?

- $10000$
- $100$
- $1000$
- $10$

## Explanation

One meter equals one hundred centimeters. A square meter is a square with both sides one hundred centimeters long. Its area is therefore ten thousand square centimeters.
