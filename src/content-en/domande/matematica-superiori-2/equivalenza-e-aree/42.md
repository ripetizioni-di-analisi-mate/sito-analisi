How many square centimetres are in $1\,\mathrm m^2$?

- $10000$
- $100$
- $1000$
- $10$

## Explanation

One metre equals one hundred centimetres. A square metre is a square with both sides one hundred centimetres long. Its area is therefore ten thousand square centimetres.
