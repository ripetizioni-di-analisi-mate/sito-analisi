A rectangular floor measures $7$ m by $4$ m. How many tiles of area $0.25$ m² are needed, ignoring cuts and waste?

- $56$
- $7$
- $28$
- $112$

## Explanation

The floor area is the product of its dimensions. Each tile covers a quarter of a square meter, so multiply the area by four. This gives $112$.
