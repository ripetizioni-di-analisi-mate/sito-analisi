A rectangle has area $48\,\mathrm{m}^2$ and one side of $6\,\mathrm m$. What is its perimeter?

- $14\,\mathrm m$
- $48\,\mathrm m$
- $16\,\mathrm m$
- $28\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

This is an inverse problem. From $A=bh$ the other side is $h=\dfrac{A}{b}=\dfrac{48}{6}=8\,\mathrm m$. The perimeter is the sum of the four sides, so it equals $2\cdot(6+8)=28\,\mathrm m$. The value $14\,\mathrm m$ is the semiperimeter.
