A parallelogram has area $21$ cm² and base $7$ cm. What is the corresponding height in centimeters?

- $3$
- $7$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $6$

## Explanation

The area is the product of the base and its perpendicular height. Divide the area by the given base. This gives $3$.
