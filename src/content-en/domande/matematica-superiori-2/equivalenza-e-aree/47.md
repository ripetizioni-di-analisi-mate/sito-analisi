A parallelogram has area $48$ and base $8$. Which information is guaranteed?

- The height is $6$, while the perimeter is undetermined.
- The height is $6$ and the perimeter must be $28$.
- The slanted side is $6$ and the height is undetermined.
- The perimeter is $28$, while the height is undetermined.

## Explanation

Dividing area by base gives height $48/8=6$. The slanted side need not equal that perpendicular distance. A rectangle of width $6$, for example, has perimeter $28$. Shifting one base horizontally by $8$ instead gives a parallelogram with slanted side $10$ and perimeter $36$. The height and hence the area stay the same while the boundary changes.
