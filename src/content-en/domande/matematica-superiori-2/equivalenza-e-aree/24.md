Among rectangles with perimeter $30$, which pair of side lengths gives the largest area?

- $5,10$
- $6,9$
- $\frac{15}{2},\frac{15}{2}$
- $3,12$

## Explanation

The consecutive sides must sum to $15$, so write them as $15/2-u$ and $15/2+u$. Their product is $(15/2)^2-u^2$. Subtracting the smallest possible square gives the largest area, which occurs at $u=0$. The equal lengths make a square of side $15/2$. Every other compatible pair has a positive difference and reduces the area.
