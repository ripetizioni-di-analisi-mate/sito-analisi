The base of a rectangle of area $13$ cm² is doubled and its height is halved. What is the new area in square centimeters?

- $\dfrac{13}{2}$
- $52$
- $13$
- $26$

## Explanation

In the product of the two dimensions, the factors two and one half cancel. The area remains the same. This gives $13$.
