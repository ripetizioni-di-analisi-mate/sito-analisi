What is the area of a rectangle with base $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $7\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $64$ cm²
- $62$ cm²
- $126$ cm²
- $63$ cm²

## Explanation

The area of the rectangle is the product of its base and height. The lengths use the same unit, so they can be multiplied directly. The result is expressed in square units.

$$
A=bh=9\cdot7=63
$$
