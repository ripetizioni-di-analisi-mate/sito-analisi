Find the area of a rectangle with base $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $7\,\mathrm{cm}$.

- $64$ cm²
- $62$ cm²
- $126$ cm²
- $63$ cm²

## Explanation

The area of the rectangle is the product of its base and height. The lengths use the same unit, so multiply them directly. Express the result in square units.

$$
A=bh=9\cdot7=63
$$
