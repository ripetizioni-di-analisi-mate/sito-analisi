A triangle has area $144\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ and base $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the length of the corresponding altitude?

- $37$ cm
- $35$ cm
- $36$ cm
- $72$ cm

## Explanation

The starting point is the triangle area formula. Multiplying the area by two and dividing by the base isolates the altitude. Dividing an area by a length gives a length.

$$
A=\frac{bh}2\implies h=\frac{2A}b
$$

$$
h=\frac{2\cdot144}{8}=36
$$
