A triangle has area $144\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ and base $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the corresponding altitude.

- $37$ cm
- $35$ cm
- $36$ cm
- $72$ cm

## Explanation

Start from the triangle area formula. To isolate the altitude, double the area and divide by the base. Dividing an area by a length gives a length.

$$
A=\frac{bh}2\implies h=\frac{2A}b
$$

$$
h=\frac{2\cdot144}{8}=36
$$
