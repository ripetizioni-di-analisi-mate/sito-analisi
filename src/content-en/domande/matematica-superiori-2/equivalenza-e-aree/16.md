Two triangles have equal area. Their chosen bases measure $12$ and $10$, and the altitude to the first base is $5$. What is the altitude to the second base?

- $\frac{25}{6}$
- $3$
- $6$
- $12$

## Explanation

Twice the area is the same for the two triangles, giving $12\cdot5=10h$ and $h=6$. Because the second base is shorter, the altitude must increase to keep the product constant. The altitude ratio is the reciprocal of the base ratio. Using both pairs in the triangle formula gives area $30$ in each case.
