To cover $18\,\mathrm{m}^2$, the planned purchase must provide at least $8\%$ extra tile area for waste. Each tile covers $0.4\,\mathrm{m}^2$. What is the smallest purchase meeting that area target?

- $48$
- $45$
- $49$
- $53$

## Explanation

The area target is $18\cdot1.08=19.44\,\mathrm{m}^2$. Dividing by one tile’s area gives $48.6$. Round up because $48$ tiles would provide only $19.2\,\mathrm{m}^2$. With $49$ tiles, the area is $19.6\,\mathrm{m}^2$. Comparing both whole-number counts around the quotient checks that $49$ is the smallest sufficient purchase.
