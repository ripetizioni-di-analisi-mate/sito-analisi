A rectangular floor measures $13$ m by $7$ m. How many tiles of area $0.25$ m² are needed, ignoring cuts and waste?

- $91$
- $\dfrac{91}{4}$
- $364$
- $182$

## Explanation

The floor area is the product of its dimensions. Each tile covers a quarter of a square meter, so multiply the area by four. This gives $364$.
