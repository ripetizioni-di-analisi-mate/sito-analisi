A rhombus has diagonals of $8$ cm and $18$ cm and has the same area as a square. What is the side of the square?

- $12$ cm
- $13$ cm
- $3\sqrt2$ cm
- $6\sqrt2$ cm

## Explanation

The area of the rhombus is half the product of the diagonals, that is $\dfrac{8\cdot18}{2}=72\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. The square has the same area as the rhombus, so its area is also $72\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. The side $l$ of the square therefore satisfies $l^2=72$, hence

$$
l=\sqrt{72}=6\sqrt2\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$

The answer $12$ cm comes from using the product of the diagonals without halving it, while $3\sqrt2$ cm comes from half the product of the half-diagonals.
