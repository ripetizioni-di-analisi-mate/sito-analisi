A triangle is divided into three triangles with the same vertex and consecutive bases of $2$, $3$ and $5$ cm on its original base. The total area is $40$ cm². What is the area of the middle triangle?

- $15$ cm²
- $8$ cm²
- $20$ cm²
- $12$ cm²

## Explanation

The triangles share an altitude, so their areas are proportional to their bases. The middle triangle has area $\dfrac3{10}\cdot40=12$ cm².
