A rhombus has diagonals $6$ and $8$. What is its area?

- $48$
- $14$
- $12$
- $24$

## Explanation

The diagonals are perpendicular. The area is half their product, giving $\dfrac{6\cdot8}{2}=24$. The result uses the corresponding square unit.
