A triangle has base $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ and corresponding altitude $9\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its area?

- $63$ cm²
- $64$ cm²
- $62$ cm²
- $126$ cm²

## Explanation

The given altitude corresponds to the specified base, so their product is used. The triangle covers half the area of a parallelogram with the same base and height. The product is therefore divided by two.

$$
A=\frac{bh}2=\frac{14\cdot9}2=63
$$
