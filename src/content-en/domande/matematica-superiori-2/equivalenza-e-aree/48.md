The consecutive sides of a parallelogram measure $8$ and $10$. The height to the side of length $8$ is $5$. What is the height to the side of length $10$?

- $4$
- $\frac{25}{4}$
- $5$
- $8$

## Explanation

The first pair of measurements gives area $8\cdot5=40$. Changing the base changes the direction in which the height is measured, but the product stays $40$. Thus $10h=40$ and $h=4$. The second base is longer, so its corresponding height must be shorter. Matching the two products checks which height goes with each base.
