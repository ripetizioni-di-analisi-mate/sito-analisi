A parallelogram has area $36$ cm² and base $9$ cm. What is the corresponding height in centimeters?

- $4$
- $9$
- $2$
- $8$

## Explanation

The area is the product of the base and its perpendicular height. Divide the area by the given base. This gives $4$.
