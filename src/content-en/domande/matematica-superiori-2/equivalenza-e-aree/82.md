In an obtuse triangle $ABC$ the base $AB$ is $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. The foot $H$ of the altitude from $C$ lies on the extension of $AB$, $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ from $B$, and $CH=5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the area of the triangle?

- $15\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The altitude to $AB$ is the distance of $C$ from the line $AB$, that is $CH=5\,\mathrm{cm}$, even though $H$ lies outside the segment. The base is still $AB=6\,\mathrm{cm}$, because the segment $AH$, of length $8\,\mathrm{cm}$, is not a side of the triangle. Hence the area is $\dfrac{6\cdot5}{2}=15\,\mathrm{cm}^2$.
