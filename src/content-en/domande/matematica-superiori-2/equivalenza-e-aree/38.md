A right triangle with legs of $6\,\mathrm m$ and $4\,\mathrm m$ is left uncultivated inside a rectangular field $10\,\mathrm m$ by $8\,\mathrm m$. What area remains?

- $68\,\mathrm m^2$
- $56\,\mathrm m^2$
- $92\,\mathrm m^2$
- $74\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

The legs are perpendicular, so the uncultivated area is half the product of their lengths. The field area is the product of the rectangle’s dimensions. Subtract the uncultivated part.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A&=10\cdot8-\frac{6\cdot4}2=68\,\mathrm m^2
\end{aligned}
$$
