A square with side length $5$ cm is divided by a diagonal. What is the area of one of the triangles in square centimeters?

- $\dfrac{25}{2}$
- $10$
- $25$
- $5$

## Explanation

The diagonal splits the square into congruent triangles. Each occupies half the square of the side length. This gives $\dfrac{25}{2}$.
