A cut parallel to the base of a triangle is made halfway up its altitude, removing the small triangle containing the opposite vertex. What fraction of the original area remains in the piece containing the whole base?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

The removed triangle is similar to the original with both its base and altitude halved. Its area is therefore $\dfrac14$ of the original. The remaining trapezium occupies $1-\dfrac14=\dfrac34$.
