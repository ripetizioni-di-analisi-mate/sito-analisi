A triangle has base $10$ cm and area $10$ cm². What is the corresponding altitude in centimetres?

- $1$
- $5$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The area of the triangle is half the product of the base and altitude. Double the area and divide by the base. This gives $2$.
