A right triangle has legs of $6$ cm and $8$ cm and hypotenuse of $10$ cm. What is the altitude to the hypotenuse?

- $7$ cm
- $\dfrac{12}{5}$ cm
- $\dfrac{48}{5}$ cm
- $\dfrac{24}{5}$ cm

## Explanation

In a right triangle each leg is the altitude to the other leg, so the area can be computed from the legs and equals $\dfrac{6\cdot8}{2}=24\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. The same area is obtained by taking the hypotenuse as base and the altitude $h$ to the hypotenuse as height, therefore $\dfrac{10\,h}{2}=24$. Hence

$$
h=\frac{2\cdot24}{10}=\frac{24}{5}\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$

The answer $\dfrac{12}{5}$ cm comes from dividing the area by the base without doubling it.
