In triangle $ABC$, $AB=10$ and the foot $H$ of the altitude from $C$ lies beyond $B$. Given $BH=4$ and $BC=5$, what is the area of $ABC$?

- $15$
- $20$
- $21$
- $25$

## Explanation

Segment $BC$ is the hypotenuse of exterior right triangle $BHC$. The perpendicular leg is $\sqrt{5^2-4^2}=3$, giving the required area $10\cdot3/2=15$. The exterior foot changes the diagram while still measuring the distance to the same base line. As a check, the larger triangle $AHC$ has area $21$ and the exterior triangle $BHC$ has area $6$.
