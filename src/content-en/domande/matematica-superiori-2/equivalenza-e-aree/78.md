A regular hexagon has perimeter $48$ cm and apothem $4\sqrt3$ cm. What is its area in square centimeters?

- $96$
- $192 \sqrt{3}$
- $48 \sqrt{3}$
- $96 \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Splitting the polygon into triangles with altitude equal to the apothem gives half the product of the perimeter and apothem. This gives $96 \sqrt{3}$.
