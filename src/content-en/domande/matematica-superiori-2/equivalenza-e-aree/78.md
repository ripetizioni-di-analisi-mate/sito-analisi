A square and an equilateral triangle both have perimeter $24$. Their apothems are $3$ and $4\sqrt3/3$. Which has larger area?

- The triangle, because its apothem contains a radical.
- They have equal areas because their perimeters match.
- The triangle, because it has fewer sides.
- The square, because its apothem is larger.

## Explanation

Both shapes have half-perimeter $12$, so compare their apothems. We have $3>4\sqrt3/3$: squaring compares $9$ with $16/3$. Their areas are $36$ and $16\sqrt3$, with the first larger. Equal perimeters give only the common factor in the formulas. Equal areas would also require the distances from the centre to the sides to match.
