A parallelogram has area $55$ cm² and base $11$ cm. What is the corresponding height in centimeters?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$
- $11$
- $10$

## Explanation

The area is the product of the base and its perpendicular height. Divide the area by the given base. This gives $5$.
