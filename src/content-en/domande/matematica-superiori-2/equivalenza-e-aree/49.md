A parallelogram has height $x$, base $x+4$, and area $60$. What is its height?

- $10$
- $6$
- $-10$
- $15$

## Explanation

The area gives $x(x+4)=60$. Moving terms and factoring yields $(x-6)(x+10)=0$. Of the two solutions, $-10$ gives a negative height, while $6$ is valid. The corresponding base is $10$, and the product checks the area $60$. The value $10$ is the base $x+4$ in the original setup, rather than the height.
