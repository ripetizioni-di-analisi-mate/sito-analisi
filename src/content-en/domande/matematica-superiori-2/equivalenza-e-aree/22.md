A convex polygon, possibly irregular, is circumscribed about a circle of radius $3.5$. Its perimeter is $40$. What is its area?

- $70$
- $140$
- $80$
- $35$

## Explanation

Join the centre to the vertices to obtain triangles based on the sides. Tangency makes each altitude the distance from the centre to a side, which is the radius $3.5$. The bases add to the perimeter, giving area $40\cdot3.5/2=70$. The sides do not have to match in length because the common factor being extracted is the altitude.
