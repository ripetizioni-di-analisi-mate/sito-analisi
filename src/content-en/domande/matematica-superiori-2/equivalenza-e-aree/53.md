Triangle $ABC$ has area $20$ and base $AB=8$. The foot of the altitude from $C$ is beyond $A$, at distance $12$ from $A$. What is the length of $AC$?

- $13$
- $5$
- $12$
- $17$

## Explanation

The area and base give altitude $2\cdot20/8=5$. The exterior foot $H$ forms right triangle $AHC$, with legs $12$ along the base line and $5$ perpendicular to it. Side $AC$ is the hypotenuse, so it measures $\sqrt{12^2+5^2}=13$. Adding $12$ and $5$ would measure a two-segment path, longer than the requested side. The Pythagorean triple $5,12,13$ checks the result.
