A triangle has base $18$ cm and area $36$ cm². What is the corresponding altitude in centimeters?

- $4$
- $8$
- $9$
- $2$

## Explanation

The area of the triangle is half the product of the base and altitude. Double the area and divide by the base. This gives $4$.
