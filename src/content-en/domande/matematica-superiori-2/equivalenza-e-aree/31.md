A rectangular corner piece of width $3$ and height $5$ is removed from a $12\times8$ rectangle. Which sum of two rectangle areas measures the remaining shape?

- $9\cdot8+3\cdot5$
- $9\cdot8+3\cdot3$
- $12\cdot8+3\cdot5$
- $9\cdot5+3\cdot3$

## Explanation

A vertical cut leaves one rectangle with full height $8$ and width $12-3=9$. In the neighbouring strip, width $3$ remains with height $8-5=3$. The disjoint pieces give $72+9=81$. Check by subtracting the cutout directly: $12\cdot8-3\cdot5=81$. Using height $5$ for the second area would count the piece removed and omit the one still there.
