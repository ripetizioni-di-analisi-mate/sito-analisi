A rectangle measuring $11$ cm by $9$ cm is removed from the inside of a rectangle measuring $15$ cm by $13$ cm. What area remains?

- $97$ cm²
- $96$ cm²
- $95$ cm²
- $192$ cm²

## Explanation

The removed rectangle is completely inside the figure. Subtract its area from the area of the outer rectangle. Both products are measured in square centimeters.

$$
A=(15)(13)-(11)(9)=96
$$
