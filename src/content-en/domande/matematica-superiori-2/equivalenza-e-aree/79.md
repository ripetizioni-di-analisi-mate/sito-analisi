A regular hexagon has side length $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its area?

- $12 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $24 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $48 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The radii to the vertices divide the hexagon into six equilateral triangles. The apothem is the altitude of an equilateral triangle of side length $4$, so it is $2\sqrt3$. Multiplying it by half the perimeter gives the area.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A&=\frac{(6\cdot4)(2\sqrt3)}2=24\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$$
