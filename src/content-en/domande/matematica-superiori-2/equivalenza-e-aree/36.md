A trapezoid has height $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. A rectangle with the same height and base of $11\,\mathrm{cm}$ has the same area. What is the sum of the trapezoid’s bases?

- $11\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $55\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $22\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Equate the formulas and divide by the common height. The rectangle’s base is half the sum of the trapezoid’s bases.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{(B+b)\cdot5}2&=11\cdot5 \\[5pt]
B+b&=22\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
