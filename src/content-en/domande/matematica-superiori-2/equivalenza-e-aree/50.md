A parallelogram has area $78$ cm² and base $13$ cm. What is the corresponding height in centimeters?

- $6$
- $13$
- $12$
- $3$

## Explanation

The area is the product of the base and its perpendicular height. Divide the area by the given base. This gives $6$.
