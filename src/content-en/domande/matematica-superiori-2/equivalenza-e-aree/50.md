A parallelogram is claimed to have base $11$, perimeter $38$, and height $9$ to that base. Which check shows that it cannot exist?

- Its slanted side would be $8$, shorter than the height.
- Its slanted side would be $19$, which is a semiperimeter.
- A parallelogram’s base must equal its height.
- The height must exceed the base.

## Explanation

The perimeter gives the sum of the base and slanted side, $38/2=19$, so the slanted side would be $19-11=8$. That side joins points on the parallel lines, while the height is the shortest distance between them. The height cannot exceed the slanted side. The stated $9>8$ violates this condition even though the perimeter alone is possible.
