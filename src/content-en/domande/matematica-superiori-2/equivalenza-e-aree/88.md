A rectangular floor measures $9$ m by $5$ m. How many tiles of area $0.25$ m² are needed, ignoring cuts and waste?

- $180$
- $90$
- $\dfrac{45}{4}$
- $45$

## Explanation

The floor area is the product of its dimensions. Each tile covers a quarter of a square meter, so multiply the area by four. This gives $180$.
