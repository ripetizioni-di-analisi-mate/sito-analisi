The shorter base of a trapezoid with bases $8$ and $4$ and height $3$ slides along its line while the shape stays convex. How does the area change?

- It increases because one leg lengthens.
- It halves because the shorter base is half the longer one.
- It stays at $18$.
- It changes with the new diagonal length.

## Explanation

Sliding the shorter base along its line preserves both parallel segment lengths. It also preserves the perpendicular distance between the lines, which is the height. The formula uses only those three measurements and still gives $(8+4)\cdot3/2=18$. A diagonal may change length, but its two triangles retain the same bases and altitude. The area therefore stays the same.
