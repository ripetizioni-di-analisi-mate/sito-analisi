A trapezoid has area $85$ cm² and height $10$ cm. One base measures $11$ cm. How long is the other base in centimeters?

- $12$
- $17$
- $6$
- $5$

## Explanation

Double the area and divide by the height to find the sum of the bases. Subtract the known base. This gives $6$.
