A rhombus has area $10$ cm² and one diagonal of length $4$ cm. How long is the other diagonal in centimetres?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $2$
- $5$
- $10$

## Explanation

The area is half the product of the diagonals. Divide twice the area by the given diagonal. This gives $5$.
