The base of a rectangle of area $7$ cm² is doubled and its height is halved. What is the new area in square centimeters?

- $28$
- $7$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $14$

## Explanation

In the product of the two dimensions, the factors two and one half cancel. The area remains the same. This gives $7$.
