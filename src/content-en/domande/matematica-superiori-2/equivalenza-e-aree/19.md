Which pair of rectangles has the same perimeter and different areas?

- $2\times6\ \text{ / }\ 3\times4$
- $4\times8\ \text{ / }\ 5\times7$
- $4\times8\ \text{ / }\ 8\times4$
- $4\times8\ \text{ / }\ 5\times6$

## Explanation

The consecutive sides of $4\times8$ and $5\times7$ both add to $12$, giving perimeter $24$. Their products are $32$ and $35$. A pair with the same area, such as $2\times6$ and $3\times4$, shows a different relationship. Reversing the order of the side lengths preserves both perimeter and area because it just turns the same rectangle.
