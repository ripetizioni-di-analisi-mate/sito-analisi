A field consists of a rectangle $7$ m by $4$ m and a triangle attached along a side of length $7$ m, with altitude $6$ m. What is the total area in m²?

- $7$
- $70$
- $28$
- $49$

## Explanation

The rectangle contributes the product of its dimensions. The triangle shares the same base and contributes half the product of its base and altitude. This gives $49$.
