Rectangles with side lengths $5,4$ and $6,3$ overlap in a $2\times3$ rectangle. What is the area covered by at least one of them?

- $38$
- $26$
- $6$
- $32$

## Explanation

The individual areas are $20$ and $18$. Their sum counts the six square units of overlap twice. Subtract one copy to obtain $20+18-6=32$. Another approach starts with the exclusive parts, of areas $14$ and $12$, and adds the shared $6$ once. Subtracting both copies would instead answer the question about the region covered by exactly one rectangle.
