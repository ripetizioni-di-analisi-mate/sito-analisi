What is the area of a parallelogram with base $11\,\mathrm{cm}$ and corresponding height $8\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $89$ cm²
- $88$ cm²
- $87$ cm²
- $176$ cm²

## Explanation

The height perpendicular to the base is used. A parallelogram has the same area as a rectangle with the same base and height. The slanted side does not appear in the area formula.

$$
A=bh=11\cdot8=88
$$
