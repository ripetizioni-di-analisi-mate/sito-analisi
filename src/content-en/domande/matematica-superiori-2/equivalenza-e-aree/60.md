A trapezoid has bases of $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $120\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $50\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is its area in square centimetres?

- $500$
- $100$
- $50$
- $32$

## Explanation

First convert the lengths: $120\,\mathrm{mm}=12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $50\,\mathrm{mm}=5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The trapezoid has mean base $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and area $10\cdot5=50\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. In millimetres the area would be $5000\,\mathrm{mm}^2$, which must be divided by $10^2$ to return to square centimetres. Converting the dimensions before multiplying avoids adding numbers in different units.
