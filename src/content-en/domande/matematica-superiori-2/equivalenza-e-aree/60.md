A trapezoid has area $120$ cm² and height $12$ cm. One base measures $13$ cm. How long is the other base in centimeters?

- $6$
- $14$
- $7$
- $20$

## Explanation

Double the area and divide by the height to find the sum of the bases. Subtract the known base. This gives $7$.
