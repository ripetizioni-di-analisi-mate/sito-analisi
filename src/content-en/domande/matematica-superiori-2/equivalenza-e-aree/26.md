Two triangles share a base segment and have opposite vertices on one line parallel to the base. What is guaranteed?

- They have equal area.
- They are congruent.
- They have the same perimeter.
- Their angles are all the same.

## Explanation

Every point on the parallel line has the same perpendicular distance from the base line. Thus the triangles have the same base and altitude, and their areas agree. Moving the vertex along the parallel can change the slanted side lengths and the angles. For example, the altitude can move from an interior foot to one beyond the base without altering the area.
