A trapezoid is claimed to have height $4$, area $34$, and shorter base $9$. Why are these data incompatible?

- The missing base would be $17$, equal to the sum of the bases.
- A trapezoid’s height must exceed both bases.
- The missing base would be $8$, shorter than $9$.
- A trapezoid’s area must always be odd.

## Explanation

The bases would have to sum to $2\cdot34/4=17$. A base of length $9$ leaves $17-9=8$ for the other. Both lengths are positive, but the claim that $9$ is the shorter one fails. If both were at least $9$, the area would be at least $9\cdot4=36$. Checking the formula and the ordering identifies the inconsistent claim.
