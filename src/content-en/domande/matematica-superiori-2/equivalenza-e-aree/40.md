Triangles $ABC$ and $ABD$ share base $AB$. Points $C$ and $D$ lie on the same line parallel to $AB$. How are their areas related?

- They depend on the distance between $C$ and $D$.
- They are equal only if both triangles are right.
- They are equal.
- They are equal only if $C$ and $D$ coincide.

## Explanation

Each altitude equals the distance between the two parallel lines. Both triangles therefore have the same base and the same altitude.
