Rectangle $ABCD$ has sides $AB=8$ cm and $AD=6$ cm. Its diagonal $AC$ divides it into two triangles of equal area. When the sheet is folded along $AC$, does $B$ land exactly on $D$?

- Yes. Equal areas guarantee this overlap.
- Yes. Every diagonal of a rectangle bisects its corner angles.
- No. The distances $AB$ and $AD$ differ.
- No. One triangle has a larger area.

## Explanation

Folding fixes $A$ and preserves distances. The image of $B$ remains $8$ cm from $A$, while $D$ is only $6$ cm away. Each triangle still has area $24$ square centimetres.
