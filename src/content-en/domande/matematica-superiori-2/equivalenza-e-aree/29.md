A rectangle with sides $4$ and $2$ has diagonals of length $\sqrt{20}$. Why does half their product give $10$ instead of the area $8$?

- The diagonals do not bisect each other.
- The rectangle’s diagonals are not congruent.
- The formula applies only to regular polygons.
- The diagonals are not perpendicular.

## Explanation

Using half the product requires one diagonal to be perpendicular to the other. Then the halves or parts of one diagonal are altitudes of triangles based on the other. Here the diagonals bisect each other and are equal but meet at an angle other than $90^\circ$. Their parts are therefore not altitudes for that calculation. The rectangle’s perpendicular dimensions are $4$ and $2$.
