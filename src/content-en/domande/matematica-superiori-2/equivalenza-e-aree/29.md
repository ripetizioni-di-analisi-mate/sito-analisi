A rhombus has diagonals of lengths $72\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $54\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find its area.

- $1945$ cm²
- $1943$ cm²
- $3888$ cm²
- $1944$ cm²

## Explanation

The diagonals of a rhombus are perpendicular and bisect each other. The four resulting right triangles have a total area equal to half the product of the diagonals. Apply this formula.

$$
A=\frac{Dd}2=\frac{72\cdot54}2=1944
$$
