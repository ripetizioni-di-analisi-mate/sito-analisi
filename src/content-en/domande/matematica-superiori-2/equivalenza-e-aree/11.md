A parallelogram with base $8$, slanted side $10$, and height $6$ is rearranged into a rectangle with base $8$. Which comparison is correct?

- The area remains $48$ and the perimeter decreases by $4$.
- The area remains $48$ and the perimeter decreases by $8$.
- The area decreases and the perimeter stays at $36$.
- Both area and perimeter are unchanged.

## Explanation

Rearranging pieces without overlap preserves the area, $8\cdot6=48$. The parallelogram boundary measures $2(8+10)=36$, and the rectangle’s is $2(8+6)=28$. Two sides of length $10$ give way to two sides of length $6$. The total decrease is $2(10-6)=8$. The cut preserves the amount of surface while changing its outer boundary.
