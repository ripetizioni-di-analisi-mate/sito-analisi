A rectangular floor measures $5$ m by $3$ m. How many tiles of area $0.25$ m² are needed, ignoring cuts and waste?

- $15$
- $30$
- $60$
- $\dfrac{15}{4}$

## Explanation

The floor area is the product of its dimensions. Each tile covers a quarter of a square metre, so multiply the area by four. This gives $60$.
