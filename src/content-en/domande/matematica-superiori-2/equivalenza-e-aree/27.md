Two parallelograms have the same base of $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the same height of $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. Their slanted sides are $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What property do they share?

- They are congruent.
- They have the same area.
- They have the same perimeter.
- All their sides are congruent.

## Explanation

The product of base and height is the same in both cases. The slanted sides are different, so the lengths of the boundaries differ.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A_1&=\mathcal A_2=8\cdot3=24\,\mathrm{cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$$
