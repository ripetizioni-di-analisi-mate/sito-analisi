A floor $7\,\mathrm m\times4.5\,\mathrm m$ is covered with square tiles of side $50\,\mathrm{cm}$, without cuts. How many tiles are needed?

- $63$
- $31$
- $144$
- $126$

## Explanation

A tile side is half a metre. Fourteen tiles fit along the seven-metre side and nine along the four-and-a-half-metre side. The total is $14\cdot9=126$. Dividing the floor’s $31.5$ square metres by each tile’s $0.25$ gives the same count. The whole-number counts of rows and columns confirm that this layout needs no cuts.
