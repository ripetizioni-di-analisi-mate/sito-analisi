A rectangular floor measures $11$ m by $6$ m. How many tiles of area $0.25$ m² are needed, ignoring cuts and waste?

- $132$
- $66$
- $\dfrac{33}{2}$
- $264$

## Explanation

The floor area is the product of its dimensions. Each tile covers a quarter of a square meter, so multiply the area by four. This gives $264$.
