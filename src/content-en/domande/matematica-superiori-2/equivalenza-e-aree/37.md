A rhombus has diagonals of lengths $6$ and $8$. What is its height corresponding to one of its sides?

- $5$
- $6$
- $\frac{12}{5}$
- $\frac{24}{5}$

## Explanation

The perpendicular diagonals bisect each other, making a side the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs $3$ and $4$. Its length is $5$. Half the product of the diagonals gives area $24$. The same area is the side times its height, so the height is $24/5$. A distance of $12/5$ is from the centre to one side line, only half the height.
