Two triangles have bases of equal length and areas $18$ and $27$. The altitude to the first base is $4$. What is the second altitude?

- $\frac83$
- $6$
- $9$
- $8$

## Explanation

The bases match, so the factor of half the base is the same in both formulas. The altitude ratio is therefore the area ratio $27/18=3/2$. The second altitude is $4\cdot3/2=6$. Check by recovering the base from the first triangle, $2\cdot18/4=9$, and then getting $9\cdot6/2=27$. Only one dimension in the product changes here.
