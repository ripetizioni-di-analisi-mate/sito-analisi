The diagonal of a square measures $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the area of the square?

- $64\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $8 \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $32\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The diagonals of the square are perpendicular and have the same length. The square is a rhombus, so its area is half the product of the diagonals.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A&=\frac{8\cdot8}{2}=32\,\mathrm{cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$$
