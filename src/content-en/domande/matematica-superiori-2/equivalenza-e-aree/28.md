Two trapezoids of height $6$ have bases $11,5$ and $9,7$. How do their areas compare?

- The first is $48$ and the second $54$.
- The first is $33$ and the second $27$.
- Both are $48$.
- Both are $96$.

## Explanation

The sum of the bases is $16$ in each case. Read the area formula as height times the average of the two bases, here $8$. Both areas are $6\cdot8=48$. A diagonal gives a check: the triangle areas are $33$ and $15$ in the first trapezoid, and $27$ and $21$ in the second. Using only the longer base would omit one triangle.
