A trapezoid has area $16$ cm² and height $4$ cm. One base measures $5$ cm. How long is the other base in centimetres?

- $2$
- $3$
- $8$
- $6$

## Explanation

Double the area and divide by the height to find the sum of the bases. Subtract the known base. This gives $3$.
