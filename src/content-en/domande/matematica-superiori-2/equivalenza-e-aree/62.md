A rhombus has area $21$ cm² and one diagonal of length $6$ cm. How long is the other diagonal in centimeters?

- $7$
- $3$
- $14$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$

## Explanation

The area is half the product of the diagonals. Divide twice the area by the given diagonal. This gives $7$.
