A rhombus has area $36$ and diagonals in the ratio $1:2$. What is the longer diagonal?

- $12$
- $6$
- $18$
- $6\sqrt2$

## Explanation

If the shorter diagonal is $d$, the longer is $2d$. The formula gives $d\cdot2d/2=36$, or $d^2=36$. A length uses the positive root $d=6$, so the longer diagonal has length $12$. The product $6\cdot12$ is $72$, twice the area. The value $6$ is the unit used in the ratio of the diagonals, not the longer length requested.
