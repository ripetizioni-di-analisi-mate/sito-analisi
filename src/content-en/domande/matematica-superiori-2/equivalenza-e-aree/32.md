The base of a triangle is tripled. What change to the corresponding altitude keeps its area the same?

- Triple the altitude.
- Halve the altitude.
- Reduce the altitude to one third.
- Keep the altitude unchanged.

## Explanation

The area depends on the product of the two lengths. The reciprocal factor on the altitude offsets the scale factor on the base.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{(3b)(\dfrac h3)}2&=\frac{bh}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$
