A rhombus has area $36$ cm² and one diagonal of length $8$ cm. How long is the other diagonal in centimeters?

- $4$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $9$
- $18$

## Explanation

The area is half the product of the diagonals. Divide twice the area by the given diagonal. This gives $9$.
