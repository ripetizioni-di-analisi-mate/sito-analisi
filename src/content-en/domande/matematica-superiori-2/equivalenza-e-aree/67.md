A square with side length $7$ cm is divided by a diagonal. What is the area of one of the triangles in square centimeters?

- $49$
- $\dfrac{49}{2}$
- $7$
- $14$

## Explanation

The diagonal splits the square into congruent triangles. Each occupies half the square of the side length. This gives $\dfrac{49}{2}$.
