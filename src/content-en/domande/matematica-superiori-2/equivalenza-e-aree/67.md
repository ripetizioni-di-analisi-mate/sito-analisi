A diagonal divides a square into triangles of area $50$ each. What is the square’s side length?

- $5\sqrt2$
- $10$
- $20$
- $50$

## Explanation

The two triangles fill the square without overlap, so the square’s area is $100$. A square’s dimensions are equal and their product is the side length squared. Its positive root is $10$. Check with the right triangle formula: each piece has legs equal to the square’s sides and area $10\cdot10/2=50$. Using one triangle’s area alone would give the side of a smaller square.
