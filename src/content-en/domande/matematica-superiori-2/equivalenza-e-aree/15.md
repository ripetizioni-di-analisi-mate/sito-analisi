A rectangle measuring $9$ cm by $7$ cm is removed from the inside of a rectangle measuring $13$ cm by $11$ cm. What area remains?

- $81$ cm²
- $80$ cm²
- $79$ cm²
- $160$ cm²

## Explanation

The removed rectangle is completely inside the figure. Subtract its area from the area of the outer rectangle. Both products are measured in square centimeters.

$$
A=(13)(11)-(9)(7)=80
$$
