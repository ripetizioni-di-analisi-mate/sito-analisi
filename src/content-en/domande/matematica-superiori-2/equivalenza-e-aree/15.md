A $3\times2$ rectangle is removed from an $8\times5$ sheet. Compare an interior hole with a corner cutout. By how much do the total boundary lengths differ?

- $5$
- $10$
- $6$
- $0$

## Explanation

An interior hole adds the cut rectangle’s boundary, of length $2(3+2)=10$, to the outer boundary. A corner cutout instead replaces two outer edges with two of the same lengths. Its boundary stays at $26$. The remaining areas match because six square units are removed in both cases. The hole’s boundary accounts for the full difference.
