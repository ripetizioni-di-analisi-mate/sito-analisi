A rectangle measuring $8$ cm by $6$ cm is removed from the inside of a rectangle measuring $12$ cm by $10$ cm. What area remains?

- $73$ cm²
- $72$ cm²
- $71$ cm²
- $144$ cm²

## Explanation

The removed rectangle is completely inside the figure. Subtract its area from the area of the outer rectangle. Both products are measured in square centimeters.

$$
A=(12)(10)-(8)(6)=72
$$
