A rectangle measures $13$ cm by $7$ cm. A square of side length $6$ cm is removed from a corner. What is the remaining area in square centimeters?

- $91$
- $7$
- $55$
- $127$

## Explanation

Multiply the sides to find the rectangle’s area. Subtract the square of the side length of the removed piece. This gives $55$.
