A rectangular cutout $2$ units wide and $3$ units deep is removed from the edge of a $10\times8$ rectangular sheet. It can be at a corner or entirely between the endpoints of a side. How much longer is the boundary in the second case?

- $3$
- $2$
- $6$
- $10$

## Explanation

A corner cutout removes edges of lengths $2$ and $3$, replacing them with two edges of the same lengths. Its boundary length is therefore unchanged. A cutout in the middle of a side replaces a segment of length $2$ with a matching back edge plus two sides of depth $3$. This adds $2\cdot3=6$ units, while both cuts remove the same area.
