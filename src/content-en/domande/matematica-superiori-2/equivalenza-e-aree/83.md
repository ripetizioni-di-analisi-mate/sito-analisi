In triangle $ABC$ of area $50$, point $M$ on $AB$ gives $AM:MB=2:3$. What is the area of $AMC$?

- $30$
- $\frac{100}{3}$
- $25$
- $20$

## Explanation

Triangles $AMC$ and $MBC$ share the altitude from $C$ to line $AB$. Their areas are proportional to their bases in ratio $2:3$. The whole area contains five parts, of which two belong to $AMC$. This gives $50\cdot2/5=20$. The ratio $2/3$ compares the two parts, while multiplying the whole area uses $2/5$.
