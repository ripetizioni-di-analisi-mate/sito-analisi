A rectangle measures $9$ cm by $5$ cm. A square of side length $4$ cm is removed from a corner. What is the remaining area in square centimeters?

- $61$
- $5$
- $45$
- $29$

## Explanation

Multiply the sides to find the rectangle’s area. Subtract the square of the side length of the removed piece. This gives $29$.
