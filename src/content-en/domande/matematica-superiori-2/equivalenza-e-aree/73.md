A rectangular hole is cut inside a $12\times9$ sheet. The remaining area is $96$, and one side of the hole is $3$. What is the length of the other side?

- $12$
- $32$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

The full sheet has area $12\cdot9=108$. The hole occupies the difference $108-96=12$. Divide its area by the known side $3$ to find the other length $4$. Both hole dimensions are smaller than the outer ones, so the opening can fit inside the sheet. Check that subtracting $3\cdot4$ from $108$ leaves $96$.
