Two triangles of equal area have bases in the ratio $3:5$ from first to second. What is the ratio of the second altitude to the first?

- $\frac53$
- $\frac9{25}$
- $\frac35$
- $1$

## Explanation

The areas match, so the base-altitude products match. As the base increases from the first triangle to the second, the altitude must decrease. From $b_1h_1=b_2h_2$ we get $h_2/h_1=b_1/b_2=3/5$. For instance, bases $3$ and $5$ with altitudes $10$ and $6$ both give product $30$. The reciprocal would compare the altitudes in reverse order.
