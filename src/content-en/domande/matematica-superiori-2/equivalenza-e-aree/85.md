Two triangles have the same base of $13$ cm. The second altitude is twice the first. How are their areas related?

- The second is four times the first.
- The second is half the first.
- The second is twice the first.
- The areas are equal.

## Explanation

The area is directly proportional to the altitude when the base is fixed. Doubling the altitude doubles the area.
