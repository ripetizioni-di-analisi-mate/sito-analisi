A square with side length $9$ cm is divided by a diagonal. What is the area of one of the triangles in square centimeters?

- $81$
- $18$
- $\dfrac{81}{2}$
- $9$

## Explanation

The diagonal splits the square into congruent triangles. Each occupies half the square of the side length. This gives $\dfrac{81}{2}$.
