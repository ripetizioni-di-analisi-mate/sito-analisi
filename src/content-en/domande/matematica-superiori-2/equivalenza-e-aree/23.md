A rectangle measures $2\,\mathrm m$ by $75\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its area in square centimeters?

- $150\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $15000\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $1500\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $150000\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

Convert meters to centimeters before multiplying. The product of two lengths in centimeters is in square centimeters.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A&=200\,\mathrm{cm}\cdot75\,\mathrm{cm}=15000\,\mathrm{cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$$
