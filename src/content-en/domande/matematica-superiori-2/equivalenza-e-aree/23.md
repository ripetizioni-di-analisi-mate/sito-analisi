A rectangle measures $2\,\mathrm m$ by $75\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its area in square centimetres?

- $150\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $15000\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $1500\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $150000\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The two measurements are in different units, so first the side of $2\,\mathrm m$ is converted to centimetres, giving $200\,\mathrm{cm}$. The product of two lengths in centimetres is an area in square centimetres, therefore

$$
\mathcal A=200\,\mathrm{cm}\cdot75\,\mathrm{cm}=15000\,\mathrm{cm}^2 .
$$

The same value is obtained working in metres, because $2\cdot0.75=1.5\,\mathrm m^2$ and $1\,\mathrm m^2=10000\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. The answer $150\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ comes from multiplying $2$ by $75$ without converting.
