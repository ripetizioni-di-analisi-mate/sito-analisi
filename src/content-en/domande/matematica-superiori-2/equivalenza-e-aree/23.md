A wall measures $3$ m by $2$ m. Tiles measuring $30$ cm by $20$ cm cover it without cutting. How many tiles are needed with the $30$ cm side horizontal along the $3$ m width?

- $10$
- $100$
- $60$
- $1000$

## Explanation

The wall measures $300$ cm by $200$ cm. There are $300:30=10$ columns and $200:20=10$ rows, for $100$ tiles.
