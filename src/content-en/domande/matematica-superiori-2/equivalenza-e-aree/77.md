A regular hexagon has perimeter $36$ cm and apothem $3\sqrt3$ cm. What is its area in square centimeters?

- $54 \sqrt{3}$
- $54$
- $27 \sqrt{3}$
- $108 \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Splitting the polygon into triangles with altitude equal to the apothem gives half the product of the perimeter and apothem. This gives $54 \sqrt{3}$.
