A regular polygon has area $54\sqrt3$, apothem $3\sqrt3$, and side length $6$. How many sides does it have?

- $6$
- $3$
- $12$
- $9$

## Explanation

Solve the area formula for the perimeter: $P=2A/a=108\sqrt3/(3\sqrt3)=36$. Every side contributes length $6$, so there are $36/6=6$ sides. The apothem is the shared altitude of the triangles from the centre, and the perimeter is the sum of their bases. Check that six triangles of base $6$ and altitude $3\sqrt3$ have total area $54\sqrt3$.
