Which length is the height of a parallelogram?

- $\text{Always the slanted side}$
- $\text{The longer diagonal}$
- $\text{The perpendicular distance between the bases}$
- $\text{Half the perimeter}$

## Explanation

The height is the distance between the parallel lines containing the bases. It is therefore perpendicular to them. The side is a height only in the rectangular case.
