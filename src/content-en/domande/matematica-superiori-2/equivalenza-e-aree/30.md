A field consists of a rectangle $5$ m by $3$ m and a triangle attached along a side of length $5$ m, with altitude $4$ m. What is the total area in m²?

- $25$
- $15$
- $35$
- $5$

## Explanation

The rectangle contributes the product of its dimensions. The triangle shares the same base and contributes half the product of its base and altitude. This gives $25$.
