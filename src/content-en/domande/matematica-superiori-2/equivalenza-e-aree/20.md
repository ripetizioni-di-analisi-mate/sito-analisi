A square hole is cut in a rectangle measuring $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. The remaining area is $47\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. What is the side length of the hole?

- $25\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The area of the hole is the whole area minus the remaining part. The hole is a square, so its area is the square of its side length. Take the positive square root.

$$
\begin{aligned}
l^2&=9\cdot8-47=25 \\[5pt]
l&=5\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
