A parallelogram has area $10$ cm² and base $5$ cm. What is the corresponding height in centimeters?

- $5$
- $4$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

The area is the product of the base and its perpendicular height. Divide the area by the given base. This gives $2$.
