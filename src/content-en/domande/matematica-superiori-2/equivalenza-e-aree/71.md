A rectangle measures $5$ cm by $3$ cm. A square of side length $2$ cm is removed from a corner. What is the remaining area in square centimeters?

- $19$
- $15$
- $3$
- $11$

## Explanation

Multiply the sides to find the rectangle’s area. Subtract the square of the side length of the removed piece. This gives $11$.
