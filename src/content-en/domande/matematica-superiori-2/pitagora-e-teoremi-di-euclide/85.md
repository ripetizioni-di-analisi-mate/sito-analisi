An isosceles triangle has a base of $36$ cm and equal sides of $30$ cm. What is the altitude to the base, in centimeters?

- $18$
- $48$
- $24$
- $12$

## Explanation

The altitude bisects the base. In the resulting right triangle, the equal side is the hypotenuse, and half the base is a leg. This gives $24$.

$$
h=\sqrt{30^2-\left(\frac{36}2\right)^2}=24
$$
