An isosceles triangle has equal sides of $5$ cm and area $12$ cm$^2$. Which base lengths are possible?

- Only $6$ cm
- Only $8$ cm
- $6$ cm or $8$ cm
- $3$ cm or $4$ cm

## Explanation

Let $s$ be the half-base and $h$ the altitude. The area gives $sh=12$, and the Pythagorean theorem gives $s^2+h^2=25$. Thus $(s+h)^2=49$, so $s+h=7$. The positive numbers with sum $7$ and product $12$ are $3$ and $4$. Either can be the half-base, which is then doubled for the whole triangle. Both constructions work because the half-base is shorter than an equal side.
