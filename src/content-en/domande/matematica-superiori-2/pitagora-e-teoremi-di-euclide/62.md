A right triangle has an angle of $30^\circ$ and a hypotenuse of $14$ cm. What is the length of the leg opposite that angle, in centimeters?

- $7 \sqrt{3}$
- $14$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $7$

## Explanation

A right triangle with a thirty-degree angle is half of an equilateral triangle. The opposite leg is half the hypotenuse. This gives $7$.

$$
l=\frac{14}2=7
$$
