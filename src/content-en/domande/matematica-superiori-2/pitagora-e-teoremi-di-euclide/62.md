$ABC$ is right at $A$, and $AB$ measures half of $BC$. Which angle measures $30^\circ$?

- $\widehat{ABC}$
- $\widehat{BAC}$
- None of them
- $\widehat{ACB}$

## Explanation

With hypotenuse $BC$, leg $AB$ is the shorter leg and equals half the hypotenuse. The third side from the Pythagorean theorem equals the altitude of an equilateral triangle of side length $BC$. By SSS the angles match those of that half-equilateral triangle. The shorter leg is opposite the $30^\circ$ angle. The angle opposite $AB$ has its vertex at $C$.
