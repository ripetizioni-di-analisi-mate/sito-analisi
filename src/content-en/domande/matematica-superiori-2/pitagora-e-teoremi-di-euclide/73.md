The altitude to the hypotenuse is $24$ cm, and one projection measures $16$ cm. How many centimeters long is the other projection?

- $24$
- $36$
- $144$
- $16$

## Explanation

By Euclid’s second theorem, the product of the projections equals the square of the altitude. Divide that square by the known projection. This gives $36$.

$$
q=\frac{24^2}{16}=36
$$
