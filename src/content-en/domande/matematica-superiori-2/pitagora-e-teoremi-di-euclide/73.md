The altitude to the hypotenuse measures $12$ cm. One projection measures $0.09$ m. How long is the other in centimetres?

- $4$ cm
- $16$ cm
- $144$ cm
- $1600$ cm

## Explanation

First convert the given projection to centimetres, giving $9$ cm. Divide $12^2=144$ by $9$ to get $16$ cm. The square of the altitude is in square centimetres and the divisor in centimetres, leaving centimetres. The product of the projections returns $144$ cm$^2$.
