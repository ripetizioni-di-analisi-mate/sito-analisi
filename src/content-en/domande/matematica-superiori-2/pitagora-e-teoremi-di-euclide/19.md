A right triangle has a hypotenuse of $30$ cm and an altitude to the hypotenuse of $12$ cm. What is its area in square centimeters?

- $126$
- $360$
- $180$
- $90$

## Explanation

The hypotenuse can be used as the base. The given altitude is perpendicular to it, so take half their product. This gives $180$.

$$
\mathcal A=\frac{ch}{2}=\frac{30\cdot12}2=180
$$
