A right triangle has a hypotenuse of $13$ cm. Which area is impossible?

- $40$ cm$^2$
- $35$ cm$^2$
- $43$ cm$^2$
- $30$ cm$^2$

## Explanation

The altitude to the hypotenuse cannot exceed half of $13$ cm. Area is half the product of hypotenuse and altitude, so the maximum is $\dfrac{13^2}{4}=\dfrac{169}{4}$ cm$^2$. This lies between $42$ and $43$ cm$^2$. With the hypotenuse fixed, smaller areas are possible by choosing a smaller positive altitude.
