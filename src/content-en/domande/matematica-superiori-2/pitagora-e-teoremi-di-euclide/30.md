In a right triangle, the projections onto the hypotenuse measure $8$ and $18$ cm. What is the ratio of the longer leg to the shorter leg?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$

## Explanation

The squares of the legs are proportional to their corresponding projections because the hypotenuse is the same. The ratio of the legs is the square root of the ratio of the projections. This gives $\dfrac{3}{2}$.

$$
\sqrt{\frac{18}{8}}=\dfrac{3}{2}
$$
