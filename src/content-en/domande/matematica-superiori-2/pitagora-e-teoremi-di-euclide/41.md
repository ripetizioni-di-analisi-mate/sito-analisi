Which side is the hypotenuse of a right triangle?

- $\text{The side opposite the right angle}$
- $\text{The shortest side}$
- $\text{Any leg}$
- $\text{The altitude to the base}$

## Explanation

The hypotenuse is opposite the right angle and is the longest side. The two sides that form the right angle are the legs. This distinction determines where to use addition or subtraction in the Pythagorean equation.
