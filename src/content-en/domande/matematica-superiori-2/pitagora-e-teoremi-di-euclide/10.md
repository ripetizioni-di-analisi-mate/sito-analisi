Which check rules out a right triangle with hypotenuse $\sqrt{50}$ cm and a leg of $8$ cm?

- $50<64$, so the unknown leg would be shorter than the other leg
- $50<64$, so the hypotenuse would be shorter than the leg
- The hypotenuse is written as a square root, and the leg as an integer
- The difference of squares is even

## Explanation

The hypotenuse is the greatest side of a right triangle. Comparing the squares suffices to compare positive lengths. Here $50<8^2=64$, so the data make the leg longer. The same issue appears in the formula for the other leg, which would require a squared length equal to $50-64=-14$.
