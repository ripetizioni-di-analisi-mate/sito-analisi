A right triangle has a hypotenuse of $35$ cm and one leg of $21$ cm. Find the other leg.

- $29$ cm
- $28$ cm
- $27$ cm
- $56$ cm

## Explanation

Isolate the square of the unknown leg in the Pythagorean equation. The hypotenuse is the longest side, so the difference of the squares is positive. The square root gives the length of the other leg.

$$
b=\sqrt{35^2-21^2}=\sqrt{784}=28
$$
