In the coordinate plane, what is the distance between $A=(-2,1)$ and $B=(2,4)$?

::grafico{uguale=1 punti="-2,1,pieno,A;2,4,pieno,B" linee="-2,1 2,1 2,4 -2,1;1.75,1 1.75,1.25 2,1.25" xmin=-3 xmax=3.5 ymin=-0.5 ymax=5 titolo="Distance between two points in the plane"}

- $7$
- $5$
- $1$
- $\sqrt{13}$

## Explanation

The horizontal displacement is $2-(-2)=4$ and the vertical displacement is $4-1=3$. These are the legs because the horizontal and vertical directions are perpendicular. The hypotenuse is the distance between the points.

$$
\begin{aligned}
AB&=\sqrt{4^2+3^2}=5
\end{aligned}
$$
