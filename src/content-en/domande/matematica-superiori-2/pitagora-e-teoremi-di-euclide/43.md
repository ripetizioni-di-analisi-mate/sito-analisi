If the projections of the legs measure $p$ and $q$, how long is the hypotenuse?

- $pq$
- $\sqrt{pq}$
- $p+q$
- $p-q$

## Explanation

The foot of the altitude lies on the hypotenuse and splits it into two segments of lengths $p$ and $q$. Their sum equals the length of the full segment. Their product gives the square of the altitude.
