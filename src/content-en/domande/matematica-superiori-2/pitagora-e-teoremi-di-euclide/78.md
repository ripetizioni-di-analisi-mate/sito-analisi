The projections of the legs onto the hypotenuse measure $4$ cm and $9$ cm. What is the length of the hypotenuse, in centimeters?

- $13$
- $36$
- $5$
- $\sqrt{97}$

## Explanation

The foot of the altitude divides the hypotenuse into two segments that are the projections. Add their lengths. This gives $13$.

$$
c=4+9=13
$$
