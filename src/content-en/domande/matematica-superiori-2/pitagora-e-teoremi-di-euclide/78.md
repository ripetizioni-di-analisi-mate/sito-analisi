The two projections add to $17$ cm and differ by $5$ cm. How long is the altitude to the hypotenuse?

- $\sqrt{66}$ cm
- $66$ cm
- $6$ cm
- $11$ cm

## Explanation

First recover the two lengths. The greater is half of $17+5$, or $11$ cm. The smaller is half of $17-5$, or $6$ cm. Their product is $66$ cm$^2$, and the second theorem takes its square root to obtain the altitude. The altitude must lie between the two calculated lengths.
