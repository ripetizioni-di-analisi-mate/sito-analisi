The projections of the legs onto the hypotenuse differ by $5\,\mathrm{cm}$, and the altitude to the hypotenuse is $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. How long is the hypotenuse?

- $11\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $17\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $13\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Euclid’s second theorem gives $pq=36$. The product and the difference let us find the square of the sum before finding $p$ and $q$ individually. The sum is the hypotenuse and is positive.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(p+q)^2&=(p-q)^2+4pq \\[5pt]
&=25+144=169 \\[5pt]
c&=13\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
