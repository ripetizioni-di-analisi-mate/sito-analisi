In right triangle $ABC$, angle $B$ is $30^\circ$. The leg opposite $B$ measures $7\,\mathrm{cm}$. How long is the leg adjacent to $B$?

- $7 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7 \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{7 \sqrt{3}}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The leg opposite $30^\circ$ is half the hypotenuse, so the hypotenuse measures $14$. The adjacent leg follows from the difference of the squares of $14$ and $7$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
b&=\sqrt{14^2-7^2}=\sqrt{49\cdot3}=7\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
