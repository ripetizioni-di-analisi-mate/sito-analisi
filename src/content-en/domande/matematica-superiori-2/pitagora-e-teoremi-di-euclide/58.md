A square has a diagonal of $9\sqrt2$ cm. How many centimeters long is its side?

- $18$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $9 \sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The diagonal is the hypotenuse of a triangle whose legs are the two sides of the square. Its length equals the side length multiplied by the square root of two. This gives $9$.

$$
l=\frac{9\sqrt2}{\sqrt2}=9
$$
