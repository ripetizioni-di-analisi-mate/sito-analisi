Among rectangles with prescribed diagonal $d$, which has the largest area?

- The one with one side twice the other
- The one with two equal sides
- They all have equal area
- The one with one side of length $d$

## Explanation

For sides $a,b$, we have $d^2=a^2+b^2=2ab+(a-b)^2$. The area $ab$ is therefore largest when the square of the difference is zero, exactly when the sides are equal. A side of length $d$ would make the other zero and give no positive-area rectangle. With a fixed diagonal, the area depends on both side lengths.
