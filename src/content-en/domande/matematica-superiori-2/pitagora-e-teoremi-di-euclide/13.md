$ABC$ is right at $A$. Which product equals $AB^2$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 xmin=-0.66 xmax=5.7 ymin=-0.7 ymax=3.15 linee="0,0 5,0 1.8,2.4 0,0;1.8,2.4 1.8,0;1.8,0.2 2,0.2 2,0" punti="0,0;5,0;1.8,2.4;1.8,0" etichette="-0.16,-0.35,B;5.19,-0.35,C;1.8,2.8,A;1.8,-0.4,H" titolo="$H$ is the foot of the altitude from $A$"}

- $BC\cdot BH$
- $BC\cdot HC$
- $BH\cdot HC$
- $AH\cdot BC$

## Explanation

The first theorem multiplies the hypotenuse by the projection of the selected leg. Leg $AB$ has $B$ on the projection line, while its endpoint $A$ projects to $H$. Thus the projection is $BH$. Segment $HC$ is used for the leg opposite $B$, and the product of the two segments instead gives $AH^2$.
