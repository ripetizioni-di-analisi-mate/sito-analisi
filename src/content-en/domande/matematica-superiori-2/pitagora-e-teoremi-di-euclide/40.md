An isosceles triangle has equal sides of $8$ cm. Which base length is impossible?

- $15$ cm
- $12$ cm
- $8$ cm
- $16$ cm

## Explanation

Half the base is a leg in the right triangle with hypotenuse $8$ cm. It must therefore be shorter than $8$ cm. A base of $16$ cm would make that half equal to the hypotenuse and leave zero altitude. The triangle inequality gives the same limit, since the base has to be shorter than the sum of the equal sides.
