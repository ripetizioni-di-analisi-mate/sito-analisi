The projections of the legs onto the hypotenuse measure $2$ cm and $5$ cm. What is the length of the hypotenuse, in centimetres?

- $7$
- $10$
- $\sqrt{29}$
- $3$

## Explanation

The foot of the altitude divides the hypotenuse into two segments that are the projections. Add their lengths. This gives $7$.

$$
c=2+5=7
$$
