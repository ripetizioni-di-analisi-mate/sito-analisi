A $5$ m ladder reaches a point $4$ m up a vertical wall. Keeping the top at that point, move the foot one metre farther from the wall. How long must the new ladder be?

- $4\sqrt2$ m
- $6$ m
- $\sqrt{17}$ m
- $5\sqrt2$ m

## Explanation

The initial horizontal distance is $\sqrt{25-16}=3$ m, so the new distance is $4$ m. The new hypotenuse is $\sqrt{4^2+4^2}=4\sqrt2$ m.
