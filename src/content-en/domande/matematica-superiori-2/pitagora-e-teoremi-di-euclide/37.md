The square of leg $AB$ is $45\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. The foot $H$ of the altitude splits the hypotenuse into $BH=5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $HC=4\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is $AC^2$?

- $36\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $45\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $81\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The projection of leg $AC$ is $HC$, so Euclid’s first theorem uses $4$. The hypotenuse is the sum $5+4=9$. The product $9\cdot4$ gives $AC^2$. The Pythagorean theorem confirms the value because $9^2-45=36$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
AC^2&=(5+4)\cdot4=36\,\mathrm{cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$$
