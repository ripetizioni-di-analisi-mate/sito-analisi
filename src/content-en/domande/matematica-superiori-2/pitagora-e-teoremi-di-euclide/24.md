A right triangle has area $90$ cm$^2$ and altitude to the hypotenuse $5$ cm. What is the length of the hypotenuse?

- $18$ cm
- $45$ cm
- $25$ cm
- $36$ cm

## Explanation

Use the side corresponding to the known altitude as the base. Twice the area is $180$ cm$^2$ and equals the product of the hypotenuse and its altitude. Dividing by $5$ cm gives $36$ cm. Dividing square units by linear units gives the required length. Multiplying again and halving recovers the original area.
