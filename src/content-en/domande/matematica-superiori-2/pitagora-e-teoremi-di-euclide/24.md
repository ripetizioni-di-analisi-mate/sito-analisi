A right triangle has a hypotenuse of $60$ cm and an altitude to the hypotenuse of $24$ cm. What is its area in square centimeters?

- $360$
- $1440$
- $504$
- $720$

## Explanation

The hypotenuse can be used as the base. The given altitude is perpendicular to it, so take half their product. This gives $720$.

$$
\mathcal A=\frac{ch}{2}=\frac{60\cdot24}2=720
$$
