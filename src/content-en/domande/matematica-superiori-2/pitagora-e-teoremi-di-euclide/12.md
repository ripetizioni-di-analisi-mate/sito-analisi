An equilateral triangle has side $l$ and altitude $h$. Which relation follows from the right triangle formed by drawing the altitude?

- $h^2=l^2+\left(\dfrac l2\right)^2$
- $h^2=l^2-l^2$
- $h^2=\left(\dfrac l2\right)^2-l^2$
- $h^2=l^2-\left(\dfrac l2\right)^2$

## Explanation

The altitude is also a median, so the leg along the base has length $\dfrac l2$. The other leg is $h$, and the hypotenuse is an equilateral side of length $l$. Subtract the square of the known leg from the square of the hypotenuse. This leaves a positive value, three quarters of the square of the side.
