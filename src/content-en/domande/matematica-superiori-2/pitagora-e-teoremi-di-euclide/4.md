Find the altitude of an equilateral triangle with side length $12$ cm.

- $1 + 6 \sqrt{3}$ cm
- $-1 + 6 \sqrt{3}$ cm
- $12 \sqrt{3}$ cm
- $6 \sqrt{3}$ cm

## Explanation

The altitude of an equilateral triangle bisects the base. The resulting right triangle has a hypotenuse equal to the side length and a leg equal to half the base. The difference of the squares is the square of the altitude.

$$
h=\sqrt{12^2-6^2}=\sqrt{108}=6 \sqrt{3}
$$
