A right triangle has hypotenuse $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the greatest possible altitude to it?

- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\sqrt{10}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

If $p$ and $q$ are the projections, then $p+q=10$. Their product is at most $25$ because $4pq=(p+q)^2-(p-q)^2$. Equality holds when $p=q=5$. By Euclid’s second theorem, the maximum altitude is the square root of the maximum product.

$$
\begin{aligned}
4h^2&=4pq=100-(p-q)^2\le100 \\[5pt]
h&\le5\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
