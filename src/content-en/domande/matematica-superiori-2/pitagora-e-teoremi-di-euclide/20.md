On each side of a right triangle, draw a semicircle with that side as diameter. The two semicircles on the legs have areas $6$ and $10$ square centimetres. What is the area of the semicircle on the hypotenuse?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $\sqrt{136}\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

A semicircle of diameter $s$ has area $\pi s^2/8$. Multiplying Pythagoras’ identity by the common factor $\pi/8$ shows that the required area is the sum of the other two.
