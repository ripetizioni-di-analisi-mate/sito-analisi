The altitude to the hypotenuse is $36$ cm, and one projection measures $24$ cm. How many centimeters long is the other projection?

- $216$
- $36$
- $24$
- $54$

## Explanation

By Euclid’s second theorem, the product of the projections equals the square of the altitude. Divide that square by the known projection. This gives $54$.

$$
q=\frac{36^2}{24}=54
$$
