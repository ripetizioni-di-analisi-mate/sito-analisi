Given the area $\mathcal A$ and hypotenuse $c$ of a right triangle, which expression gives the altitude to the hypotenuse?

- $2\mathcal A c$
- $\dfrac{c}{2\mathcal A}$
- $\dfrac{\mathcal A^2}{c}$
- $\dfrac{2\mathcal A}{c}$

## Explanation

With the hypotenuse as the base, the area is $\mathcal A=\dfrac{ch}{2}$. Multiply by two and divide by $c$ to isolate $h$. The units agree because the result divides an area by a length. The product $2\mathcal A c$ would have cubic units. Reversing the ratio gives the reciprocal of a length, which cannot be an altitude.
