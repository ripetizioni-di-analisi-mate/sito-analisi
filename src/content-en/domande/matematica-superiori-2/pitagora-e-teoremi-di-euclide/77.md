The projections of the legs onto the hypotenuse measure $3$ cm and $7$ cm. What is the length of the hypotenuse, in centimeters?

- $21$
- $\sqrt{58}$
- $4$
- $10$

## Explanation

The foot of the altitude divides the hypotenuse into two segments that are the projections. Add their lengths. This gives $10$.

$$
c=3+7=10
$$
