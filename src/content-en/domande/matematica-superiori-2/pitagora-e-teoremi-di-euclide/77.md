In a second construction, both projections onto the hypotenuse are doubled. How does the right triangle's area change?

- It becomes twice the original area
- It becomes eight times the original area
- It stays the same
- It becomes four times the original area

## Explanation

The sum of the projections, the hypotenuse, doubles. Their product is multiplied by four, so the second theorem makes the altitude double after taking the root. Area is half the product of hypotenuse and altitude, so it becomes four times as large. Adding the scale factors would not describe the change in a product.
