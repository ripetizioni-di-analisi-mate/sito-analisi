The two projections of a right triangle satisfy $0<q<p$. Which comparison with the altitude $h$ always holds?

- $h<q<p$
- $q<p<h$
- $h=p+q$
- $q<h<p$

## Explanation

Since $q<p$ and the factors are positive, $q^2<pq<p^2$. The middle term is $h^2$ by the second theorem. Taking positive square roots keeps the order and places the altitude between the projections. Their sum is the whole hypotenuse. Equality with a projection is possible only in the equal-projection case.
