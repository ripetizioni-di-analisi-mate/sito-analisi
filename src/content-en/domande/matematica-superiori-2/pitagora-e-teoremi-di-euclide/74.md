The altitude to the hypotenuse is $30$ cm, and one projection measures $20$ cm. How many centimeters long is the other projection?

- $180$
- $20$
- $30$
- $45$

## Explanation

By Euclid’s second theorem, the product of the projections equals the square of the altitude. Divide that square by the known projection. This gives $45$.

$$
q=\frac{30^2}{20}=45
$$
