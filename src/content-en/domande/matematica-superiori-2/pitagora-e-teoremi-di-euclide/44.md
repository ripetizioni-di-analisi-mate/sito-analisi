Which formula expresses Euclid’s second theorem?

- $h=p+q$
- $h^2=p^2+q^2$
- $h=pq$
- $h^2=pq$

## Explanation

The second theorem applies to the altitude to the hypotenuse. Its square is the product of the segments into which it splits the hypotenuse. The positive square root of that product gives the length of the altitude.
