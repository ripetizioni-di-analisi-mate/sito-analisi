An isosceles triangle has equal sides of $30$ cm and a base of $36$ cm. What is its area?

- $433$ cm²
- $431$ cm²
- $864$ cm²
- $432$ cm²

## Explanation

The altitude to the base splits the isosceles triangle into two congruent right triangles. The horizontal leg is half the base. The altitude is found with the Pythagorean theorem, and the area is half the product of the base and the altitude.

$$
h=\sqrt{30^2-\left(\frac{36}{2}\right)^2}=24
$$

$$
A=\frac{36\cdot24}2=432
$$
