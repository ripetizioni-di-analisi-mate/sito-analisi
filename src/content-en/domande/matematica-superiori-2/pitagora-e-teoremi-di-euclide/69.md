One leg measures $15$ cm and the hypotenuse measures $25$ cm. What is the length of the projection of that leg onto the hypotenuse, in centimeters?

- $15$
- $16$
- $9$
- $25$

## Explanation

By Euclid’s first theorem, the square of the leg equals the product of the hypotenuse and the projection. Divide the square of the leg by the hypotenuse. This gives $9$.

$$
p=\frac{15^2}{25}=9
$$
