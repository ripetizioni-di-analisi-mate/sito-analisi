In the figure $AB=15$ cm and $BH=9$ cm. What is $BC$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 xmin=-0.95 xmax=25.83 ymin=-1.35 ymax=13.57 linee="0,0 25,0 9,12 0,0;9,12 9,0;9,0.58 9.58,0.58 9.58,0" punti="0,0;25,0;9,12;9,0" etichette="-0.31,-0.63,B;25.28,-0.62,C;9,12.75,A;9,-0.66,H;4.5,-0.65,9;3.71,6.41,15" titolo="$ABC$ is right at $A$ and $AH$ is the altitude to $BC$"}

- $16$ cm
- $24$ cm
- $25$ cm
- $135$ cm

## Explanation

The given leg's projection is known. The first theorem gives $AB^2=BC\cdot BH$, so $BC=\dfrac{225}{9}=25$ cm. The value $16$ cm is the remaining part $HC$, found by subtracting the known projection from the hypotenuse. The result $25$ cm is longer than the $15$ cm leg and contains both parts formed by the altitude.
