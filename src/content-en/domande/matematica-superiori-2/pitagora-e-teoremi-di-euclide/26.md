Three segments measure $4$, $7$, and $11$ centimetres. What figure do they form when their endpoints are joined?

- A right triangle.
- A segment, because the three points are collinear.
- An acute triangle.
- An obtuse triangle.

## Explanation

The sum of the two shorter lengths equals the longest length. The strict triangle inequality therefore fails. The two shorter segments can join only along the longest segment.

$$
\begin{aligned}
4+7&=11
\end{aligned}
$$
