An equilateral triangle has area $16\sqrt3$ cm$^2$. What is its altitude?

- $8\sqrt3$ cm
- $8$ cm
- $2\sqrt3$ cm
- $4\sqrt3$ cm

## Explanation

The area of an equilateral triangle with side $l$ is $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}l^2$. Equating it to the given value cancels $\sqrt3$ and gives $l^2=64$. The side is therefore $8$ cm. The altitude is the side times $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$. The answer $8$ cm would give only the side length. The altitude must be shorter than the side, which is the hypotenuse of the half-triangle.
