A right triangle has an angle of $30^\circ$ and a hypotenuse of $18$ cm. What is the length of the leg opposite that angle, in centimeters?

- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $9$
- $9 \sqrt{3}$
- $18$

## Explanation

A right triangle with a thirty-degree angle is half of an equilateral triangle. The opposite leg is half the hypotenuse. This gives $9$.

$$
l=\frac{18}2=9
$$
