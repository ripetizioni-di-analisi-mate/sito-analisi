In a right triangle with acute angles of $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$, the leg opposite $60^\circ$ measures $6\sqrt3$ cm. What is the hypotenuse?

- $12\sqrt3$ cm
- $12$ cm
- $6$ cm
- $18$ cm

## Explanation

The triangle is similar to half an equilateral triangle, with the hypotenuse corresponding to an equilateral side. The leg opposite $60^\circ$ matches its altitude, which is $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ of the hypotenuse. Dividing $6\sqrt3$ by that factor gives $12$ cm. Doubling the given leg would use the relation for the leg opposite $30^\circ$.
