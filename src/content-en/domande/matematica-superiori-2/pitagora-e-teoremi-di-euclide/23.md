The legs of a right triangle measure $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $15\,\mathrm{cm}$. How long is the altitude to the hypotenuse?

- $\dfrac{60}{17}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{17}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{120}{17}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{120}{23}\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The Pythagorean theorem gives the hypotenuse $17$. The area is half the product of the legs, or half the product of the hypotenuse and its altitude. Equating the two expressions for the same area gives the requested length.

$$
\begin{aligned}
c&=\sqrt{8^2+15^2}=17 \\[5pt]
\frac{17h}{2}&=\frac{8\cdot15}{2} \\[5pt]
h&=\frac{120}{17}\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
