Four rectangles have a perimeter of $40$ cm. Which pair of sides, in centimetres, gives the shortest diagonal?

- $(8,12)$
- $(7,13)$
- $(10,10)$
- $(6,14)$

## Explanation

All the side pairs add to $20$ cm. Write the sides as $10-d$ and $10+d$. The square of the diagonal is then $200+2d^2$, since the mixed terms cancel. The shortest diagonal occurs with $d=0$. Rectangles with unequal sides contribute an additional positive quantity.
