Four rectangles have a perimeter of $40$ cm. Which pair of sides, in centimetres, gives the shortest diagonal?

- $(8;12)$
- $(7;13)$
- $(10;10)$
- $(6;14)$

## Explanation

All the side pairs add to $20$ cm. Denoting by $s$ half the difference of the sides, the sides can be written as $10-s$ and $10+s$. The square of the diagonal is then $200+2s^2$, since the mixed terms cancel. The shortest diagonal occurs with $s=0$. Rectangles with unequal sides contribute an additional positive quantity.
