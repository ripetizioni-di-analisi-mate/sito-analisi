A right triangle has a hypotenuse of $20$ cm and an altitude to the hypotenuse of $8$ cm. What is its area in square centimeters?

- $160$
- $80$
- $56$
- $40$

## Explanation

The hypotenuse can be used as the base. The given altitude is perpendicular to it, so take half their product. This gives $80$.

$$
\mathcal A=\frac{ch}{2}=\frac{20\cdot8}2=80
$$
