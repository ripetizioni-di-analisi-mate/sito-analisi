A rectangle has a diagonal of $10$ cm and a side of $8$ cm. What is its area in square centimetres?

- $48$
- $64$
- $80$
- $24$

## Explanation

The Pythagorean theorem gives the unknown side as the square root of the difference of the squares of the diagonal and the known side. Then multiply the two side lengths. This gives $48$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
b&=\sqrt{10^2-8^2}=6 \\[5pt]
\mathcal A&=8\cdot6=48
\end{aligned}
$$
