An isosceles trapezoid has bases of $16$ cm and $6$ cm and legs of $13$ cm. What is its height?

- $\sqrt{69}$ cm
- $8$ cm
- $12$ cm
- $4\sqrt3$ cm

## Explanation

The heights are drawn from the endpoints of the shorter base. The trapezoid is isosceles, so they cut off two equal segments on the longer base, each of length $\dfrac{16-6}{2}=5$ cm. Each leg is therefore the hypotenuse of a right triangle with one leg of $5$ cm and the other equal to the height $h$. By the Pythagorean theorem

$$
h=\sqrt{13^2-5^2}=\sqrt{144}=12\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$

The answer $\sqrt{69}$ cm comes from using the whole difference of the bases, $10$ cm, as a leg, while $8$ cm comes from subtracting the lengths instead of their squares.
