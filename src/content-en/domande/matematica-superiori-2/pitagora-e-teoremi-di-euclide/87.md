A rectangle has diagonal $d$, sides $a,b$ and area $S$. Which equality gives its perimeter $P$ without finding the sides separately?

- $P=2\sqrt{d^2-2S}$
- $P=\sqrt{d^2+S}$
- $P=2\sqrt{d^2+2S}$
- $P=2d$

## Explanation

We have $(a+b)^2=a^2+b^2+2ab=d^2+2S$. Both sides are positive and the perimeter is $2(a+b)$.
