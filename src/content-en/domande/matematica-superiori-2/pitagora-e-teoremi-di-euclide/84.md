In triangle $ABC$, $H$ is the foot of the altitude from $A$ and lies inside $BC$. Which fact guarantees that both $BH$ and $HC$ equal half of $BC$?

- $AB=BC$
- $AC=BC$
- $AB=AC$
- $\widehat B=45^\circ$

## Explanation

If the two sides from $A$ have equal length, the triangle is isosceles with base $BC$. The altitude from $A$ is also a median and therefore reaches the midpoint of that base. This is what justifies halving the base. Equal sides meeting at another vertex give a different base for applying the isosceles properties.
