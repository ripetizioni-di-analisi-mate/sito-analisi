An isosceles triangle has a base of $30$ cm and equal sides of $25$ cm. What is the altitude to the base, in centimeters?

- $40$
- $15$
- $20$
- $10$

## Explanation

The altitude bisects the base. In the resulting right triangle, the equal side is the hypotenuse, and half the base is a leg. This gives $20$.

$$
h=\sqrt{25^2-\left(\frac{30}2\right)^2}=20
$$
