The legs of a right triangle are in the ratio $3:4$. What is the ratio of their projections onto the hypotenuse in the same order?

- $9:16$
- $3:4$
- $16:9$
- $4:3$

## Explanation

For each leg, divide its square by the same hypotenuse. That divisor cancels in the projection ratio. This leaves the ratio of the squares of the legs, $3^2:4^2=9:16$. The order matters because the greater leg must have the greater projection. The ratio $4:3$ reverses the order and uses the linear lengths.
