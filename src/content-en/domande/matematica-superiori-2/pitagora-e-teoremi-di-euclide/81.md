An isosceles triangle has a base of $12$ cm and equal sides of $10$ cm. What is the altitude to the base, in centimeters?

- $8$
- $4$
- $6$
- $16$

## Explanation

The altitude bisects the base. In the resulting right triangle, the equal side is the hypotenuse, and half the base is a leg. This gives $8$.

$$
h=\sqrt{10^2-\left(\frac{12}2\right)^2}=8
$$
