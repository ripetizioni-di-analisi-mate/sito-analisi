A rectangle is enlarged by multiplying both side lengths by $5$. By what factor does the square of its diagonal change?

- $5$
- $10$
- $25$
- $125$

## Explanation

The squared diagonal is the sum of the squares of the sides. Multiplying each side by $5$ multiplies each square by $25$, so their sum changes by that factor too. The diagonal length itself is multiplied by $5$. The question asks about the square, so use the factor for the two areas.
