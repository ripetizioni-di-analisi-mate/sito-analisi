A triangle has sides of $12$, $16$, and $20$ cm. Which side is opposite the right angle?

- The side of length $16$ cm.
- The triangle has no right angle.
- The side of length $20$ cm.
- The side of length $12$ cm.

## Explanation

The square of the longest side is the sum of the squares of the other two. By the converse of the Pythagorean theorem, it is the hypotenuse.

$$
12^2+16^2=400=20^2
$$
