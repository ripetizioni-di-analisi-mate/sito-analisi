Which triple of lengths must be discarded before using the converse, because it cannot form a triangle?

- $(4,5,6)$
- $(5,12,13)$
- $(2,3,5)$
- $(6,6,7)$

## Explanation

To form a triangle, the greatest side must be strictly shorter than the sum of the other two. At $2+3=5$ the three vertices would lie on a line. The other triples pass this check, even though not all give a right angle. The two tests first determine whether the figure exists and then classify it.
