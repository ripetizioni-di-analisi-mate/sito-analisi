Two routes from $A$ to the opposite corner $B$ of a rectangle each follow two edges through one other corner. The edges are $9$ and $12$ m long. How many metres are saved by taking the diagonal?

- $6$
- $3$
- $15$
- $21$

## Explanation

The edge route measures $21$ m. Pythagoras gives a diagonal of $\sqrt{81+144}=15$ m, saving $6$ m.
