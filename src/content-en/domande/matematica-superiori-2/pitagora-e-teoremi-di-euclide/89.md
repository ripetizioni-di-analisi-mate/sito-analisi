A rectangle has a diagonal of $1$ m and one side of $60$ cm. What is its area in square centimetres?

- $4800$
- $48$
- $480$
- $6000$

## Explanation

First convert the diagonal to $100$ cm. The missing side is a leg and measures $\sqrt{100^2-60^2}=80$ cm. The area is $60\cdot80=4800$ cm$^2$. Working in metres instead would give $0.48$ m$^2$, the same area. Converting square units uses the square of the length conversion factor.
