To go from a vertex $A$ to the opposite vertex $B$ of a rectangle with sides $9$ m and $12$ m, one can follow the boundary, passing through another vertex, or the diagonal. How many metres are saved by following the diagonal?

- $6$
- $3$
- $15$
- $21$

## Explanation

The path along the boundary passes through an intermediate vertex and follows two consecutive sides, so it measures $9+12=21$ m. The two consecutive sides are perpendicular, therefore the diagonal $AB$ is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs of $9$ m and $12$ m. By the Pythagorean theorem

$$
AB=\sqrt{9^2+12^2}=\sqrt{225}=15\,\mathrm m .
$$

The saving is hence $21-15=6$ m. The answers $15$ and $21$ are the lengths of the two paths, not their difference.
