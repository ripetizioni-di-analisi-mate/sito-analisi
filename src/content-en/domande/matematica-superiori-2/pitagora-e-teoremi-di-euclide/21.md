In $ABC$ right at $A$, $BC=15$ cm and $AB=9$ cm. What is the length along the hypotenuse from $B$ to the foot $H$ of the altitude?

- $\dfrac{27}{5}$ cm
- $\dfrac{48}{5}$ cm
- $6$ cm
- $9$ cm

## Explanation

The segment starting at $B$ is the projection of $AB$. The first theorem gives $BH=\dfrac{AB^2}{BC}=\dfrac{81}{15}=\dfrac{27}{5}$ cm. The segment is shorter than the leg because the squared leg is divided by a length greater than that leg. The rest of the hypotenuse measures $\dfrac{48}{5}$ cm and would be the projection of $AC$.
