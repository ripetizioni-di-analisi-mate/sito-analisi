Point $H$ is inside $BC$, with $BH=6$ cm and $HC=24$ cm. Choose $A$ on the perpendicular to $BC$ through $H$. What length of $AH$ makes the angle at $A$ right?

- $12$ cm
- $15$ cm
- $18$ cm
- $30$ cm

## Explanation

The required altitude has square equal to the product $6\cdot24=144$. The converse of the second theorem guarantees that $12$ cm gives a right angle. The squares of the two sides meeting at $A$ add to $6^2+24^2+2\cdot144=900$, which is the square of $BC=30$ cm. The interior foot makes the two base segments add.
