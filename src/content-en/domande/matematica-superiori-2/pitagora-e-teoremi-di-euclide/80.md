The projections of the legs onto the hypotenuse measure $6$ cm and $13$ cm. What is the length of the hypotenuse, in centimeters?

- $19$
- $7$
- $78$
- $\sqrt{205}$

## Explanation

The foot of the altitude divides the hypotenuse into two segments that are the projections. Add their lengths. This gives $19$.

$$
c=6+13=19
$$
