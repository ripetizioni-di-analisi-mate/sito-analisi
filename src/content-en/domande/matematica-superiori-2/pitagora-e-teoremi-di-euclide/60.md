In an isosceles right triangle, what fraction of the hypotenuse is each leg?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$
- $\sqrt2$

## Explanation

Write $l$ for the common leg. The hypotenuse squared is $2l^2$, so the hypotenuse measures $l\sqrt2$. Divide the leg by that length to get $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$. The hypotenuse is longer, so the fraction must be below one. The fraction $\dfrac12$ describes the leg opposite $30^\circ$ in a different family of triangles.
