A square has a diagonal of $13\sqrt2$ cm. How many centimeters long is its side?

- $26$
- $13 \sqrt{2}$
- $13$
- $\dfrac{13}{2}$

## Explanation

The diagonal is the hypotenuse of a triangle whose legs are the two sides of the square. Its length equals the side length multiplied by the square root of two. This gives $13$.

$$
l=\frac{13\sqrt2}{\sqrt2}=13
$$
