In a right triangle the names of the two acute vertices $B,C$ are swapped. What happens to projections $p,q$ and altitude $h$?

- $p,q$ swap and $h$ stays the same
- Both projections are halved
- $h$ and $p$ swap
- The sum of the projections doubles

## Explanation

Swapping the names interchanges $BH$ and $CH$, hence the two leg projections. The foot and the right-angle vertex remain the same points, so length $AH$ stays the same. The second theorem confirms this because the product of the projections is unchanged by reversing their order.
