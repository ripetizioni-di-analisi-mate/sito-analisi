The hypotenuse of a right triangle measures $225$ cm. The projection of one leg onto it measures $81$ cm. Find that leg.

- $135$ cm
- $136$ cm
- $134$ cm
- $270$ cm

## Explanation

Euclid’s first theorem relates the square of the leg to the product of the hypotenuse and the projection of that leg. Use the two given lengths. The leg is the positive square root of the product.

$$
a^2=cp=225\cdot81=18225
$$

$$
a=135
$$
