A rectangle has sides of $6$ cm and $12$ cm. Find its diagonal.

- $1 + 6 \sqrt{5}$ cm
- $-1 + 6 \sqrt{5}$ cm
- $6 \sqrt{5}$ cm
- $12 \sqrt{5}$ cm

## Explanation

The diagonal divides the rectangle into two right triangles. The sides of the rectangle are the legs and the diagonal is the hypotenuse. Apply the Pythagorean theorem and simplify the radical.

$$
d=\sqrt{6^2+12^2}=\sqrt{180}=6 \sqrt{5}
$$
