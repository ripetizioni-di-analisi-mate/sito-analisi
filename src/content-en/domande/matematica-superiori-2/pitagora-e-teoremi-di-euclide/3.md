A rectangle has sides of $6$ cm and $12$ cm. What is the length of its diagonal?

- $1 + 6 \sqrt{5}$ cm
- $-1 + 6 \sqrt{5}$ cm
- $6 \sqrt{5}$ cm
- $12 \sqrt{5}$ cm

## Explanation

The diagonal divides the rectangle into two right triangles. The sides of the rectangle are the legs and the diagonal is the hypotenuse. The Pythagorean theorem is applied and the radical is simplified.

$$
d=\sqrt{6^2+12^2}=\sqrt{180}=6 \sqrt{5}
$$
