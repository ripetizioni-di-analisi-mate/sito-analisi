The altitude to the hypotenuse of a right triangle splits it into segments measuring $24$ cm and $54$ cm. What is the length of the altitude?

- $37$ cm
- $36$ cm
- $35$ cm
- $72$ cm

## Explanation

The two segments are the projections of the legs onto the hypotenuse. Euclid’s second theorem states that their product is the square of the corresponding altitude. The positive square root is therefore taken.

$$
h^2=pq=24\cdot54=1296
$$

$$
h=36
$$
