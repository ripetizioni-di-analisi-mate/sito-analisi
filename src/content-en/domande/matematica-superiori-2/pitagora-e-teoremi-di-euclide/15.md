In $ABC$ right at $A$, the foot $H$ of the altitude divides the hypotenuse into $BH=3$ cm and $HC=12$ cm. What is the area of triangle $ABH$ alone?

- $36$ cm$^2$
- $45$ cm$^2$
- $9$ cm$^2$
- $18$ cm$^2$

## Explanation

The second theorem gives $AH=\sqrt{3\cdot12}=6$ cm. For the triangle with vertices $A,B,H$, the base is $BH$. Its area is half of $3\cdot6$, or $9$ cm$^2$. Using $HC$ gives the area of the other part, while using the full hypotenuse gives the area of $ABC$. Both parts share the same altitude. The smaller triangles' areas are in the ratio of their bases, here $1:4$.
