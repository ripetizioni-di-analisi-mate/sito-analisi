One leg measures $12$ cm and the hypotenuse measures $20$ cm. What is the length of the projection of that leg onto the hypotenuse, in centimeters?

- $20$
- $12$
- $\dfrac{36}{5}$
- $\dfrac{64}{5}$

## Explanation

By Euclid’s first theorem, the square of the leg equals the product of the hypotenuse and the projection. Divide the square of the leg by the hypotenuse. This gives $\dfrac{36}{5}$.

$$
p=\frac{12^2}{20}=\dfrac{36}{5}
$$
