In a right triangle with positive legs, $p$ is the projection of leg $a$ onto hypotenuse $c$. Which ordering always holds?

- $0<a<p<c$
- $0<p<c<a$
- $0<p<a<c$
- $0<a<c<p$

## Explanation

We have $a<c$ because $c$ is the hypotenuse. The first theorem gives $p=\dfrac{a^2}{c}$. Writing it as $a\left(\dfrac ac\right)$ shows that $p$ is positive and smaller than $a$, since $\dfrac ac$ lies between zero and one. The inequality compares the projection with its own leg. The other projection may be longer or shorter than the one considered here.
