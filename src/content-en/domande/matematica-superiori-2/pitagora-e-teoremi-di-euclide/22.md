The hypotenuse measures $10$ cm and its altitude measures $4$ cm. Which pair of projections in centimetres is compatible?

- $(4,6)$
- $(2,8)$
- $(5,5)$
- $(1,9)$

## Explanation

The projections must add to the hypotenuse length, and their product must equal the square of the altitude. All pairs add to $10$, but only $2\cdot8$ gives $16$. The equal pair would give an altitude of $5$ cm. Choosing the projections therefore uses both the sum condition and the product condition.
