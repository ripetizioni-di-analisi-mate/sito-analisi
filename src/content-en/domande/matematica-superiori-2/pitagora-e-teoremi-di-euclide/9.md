A right triangle has legs of $21$ cm and $28$ cm. Find the hypotenuse.

- $35$ cm
- $36$ cm
- $34$ cm
- $70$ cm

## Explanation

The given sides are the legs, so add their squares. The Pythagorean theorem gives the square of the hypotenuse. Take the positive square root because the result is a length.

$$
c=\sqrt{21^2+28^2}=\sqrt{1225}=35
$$
