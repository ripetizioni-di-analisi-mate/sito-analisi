Which equality between squares of sides is valid for the triangle shown?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 xmin=-0.62 xmax=5.7 ymin=-0.65 ymax=3.25 linee="0,0 5,0 1.8,2.4 0,0;1.644,2.192 1.852,2.036 2.008,2.244" punti="0,0;5,0;1.8,2.4" etichette="-0.13,-0.36,A;5.17,-0.36,B;1.8,2.82,C" titolo="The mark at $C$ shows the right angle"}

- $AB^2=AC^2+BC^2$
- $AC^2=AB^2+BC^2$
- $BC^2=AB^2+AC^2$
- $AB^2=AC^2-BC^2$

## Explanation

The right angle is at $C$, so its sides are $AC$ and $BC$. The opposite side is $AB$, even though it is horizontal in the figure. Its square equals the sum of the squares of the legs. Rotating the drawing leaves the role of each side unchanged because the angles do not change.
