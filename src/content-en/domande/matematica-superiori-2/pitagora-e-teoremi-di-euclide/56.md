A square has a diagonal of $5\sqrt2$ cm. How many centimetres long is its side?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $10$
- $5$
- $5 \sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The diagonal is the hypotenuse of a triangle whose legs are the two sides of the square. Its length equals the side length multiplied by the square root of two. This gives $5$.

$$
l=\frac{5\sqrt2}{\sqrt2}=5
$$
