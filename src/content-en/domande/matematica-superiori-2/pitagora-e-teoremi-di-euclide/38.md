The proposed values for a triangle right at $A$ with altitude foot $H$ are $BC=25$ cm, $BH=9$ cm, and $AB=20$ cm. Which statement is correct?

- The data work because $20<25$
- The data are inconsistent because $AB$ would have to measure $15$ cm
- The data work because $BH$ is shorter than $AB$
- The data are inconsistent because $AB$ would have to measure $9$ cm

## Explanation

The projection of $AB$ is $BH$, so its square would have to be $25\cdot9=225$. The positive root is $15$. The length $20$ would fit the other leg because the remaining segment measures $16$ cm and $25\cdot16=400$. Comparing the lengths alone is a necessary check but does not match each leg to its projection.
