One leg measures $18$ cm and the hypotenuse measures $30$ cm. What is the length of the projection of that leg onto the hypotenuse, in centimeters?

- $18$
- $\dfrac{96}{5}$
- $30$
- $\dfrac{54}{5}$

## Explanation

By Euclid’s first theorem, the square of the leg equals the product of the hypotenuse and the projection. Divide the square of the leg by the hypotenuse. This gives $\dfrac{54}{5}$.

$$
p=\frac{18^2}{30}=\dfrac{54}{5}
$$
