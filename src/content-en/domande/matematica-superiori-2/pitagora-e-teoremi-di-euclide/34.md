In a right triangle, the longer leg is twice the shorter one. What is the ratio of the longer projection to the shorter projection?

- $2$
- $4$
- $8$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

By Euclid’s first theorem, each projection equals the square of the corresponding leg divided by the hypotenuse. The hypotenuse cancels in the ratio. The ratio of the squares of the legs remains, which is $2^2=4$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{q}{p}&=\frac{\dfrac{b^2}{c}}{\dfrac{a^2}{c}}=\left(\frac{b}{a}\right)^2=4
\end{aligned}
$$
