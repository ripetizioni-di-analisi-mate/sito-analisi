In a right triangle, the altitude divides the hypotenuse into lengths of $9$ and $16$ cm. In the **smaller** of the resulting triangles, draw a new altitude to its own hypotenuse. How long is this new altitude?

- $12$ cm
- $\dfrac{36}{5}$ cm
- $\dfrac{48}{5}$ cm
- $\dfrac{27}{5}$ cm

## Explanation

The original altitude is $\sqrt{9\cdot16}=12$. The smaller triangle has legs $9$ and $12$ and hypotenuse $15$. Equating the two expressions for its area gives $15h=9\cdot12$.
