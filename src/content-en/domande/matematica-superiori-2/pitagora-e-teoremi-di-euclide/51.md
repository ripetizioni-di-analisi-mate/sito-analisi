A triangle has sides of $6$, $8$, and $10$ cm. Which side is opposite the right angle?

- The side of length $6$ cm.
- The side of length $10$ cm.
- The triangle has no right angle.
- The side of length $8$ cm.

## Explanation

The square of the longest side is the sum of the squares of the other two. By the converse of the Pythagorean theorem, it is the hypotenuse.

$$
6^2+8^2=100=10^2
$$
