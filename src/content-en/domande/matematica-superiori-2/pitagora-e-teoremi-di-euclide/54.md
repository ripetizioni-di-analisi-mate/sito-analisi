A triangle has sides of $6$, $8$, and $11$ centimeters. What follows from comparing $6^2+8^2$ with $11^2$?

- The angle opposite the $11$-centimeter side is right.
- The angle opposite the $8$-centimeter side is right.
- The three sides do not form a triangle.
- The triangle has no right angle.

## Explanation

Since $6+8>11$, the lengths can form a triangle. If this were a right triangle, the side of length $11$ would be the hypotenuse. Its square is $121$, whereas the sum of the squares of the other two sides is $100$. The equality required by the Pythagorean theorem therefore fails.
