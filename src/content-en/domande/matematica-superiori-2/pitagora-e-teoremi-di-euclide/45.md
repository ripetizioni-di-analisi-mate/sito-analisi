What is the diagonal of a square with side length $3$?

- $3\sqrt2$
- $6$
- $3\sqrt3$
- $9$

## Explanation

The diagonal is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with both legs equal to three. Its square is eighteen. The positive square root is $3\sqrt2$.

$$
d=\sqrt{3^2+3^2}=3 \sqrt{2}
$$
