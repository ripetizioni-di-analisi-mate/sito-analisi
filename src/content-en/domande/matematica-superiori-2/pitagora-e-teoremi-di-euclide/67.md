In a right triangle, leg $a$ measures $\dfrac23$ of the hypotenuse. What fraction of the hypotenuse is the projection of $a$?

- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac59$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac49$

## Explanation

The first theorem gives $\dfrac pc=\dfrac{a^2}{c^2}$, the square of $\dfrac ac$. Square the whole given fraction to get $\left(\dfrac23\right)^2=\dfrac49$. The remaining projection takes the other five ninths of the hypotenuse. The resulting fraction is smaller than the leg's fraction, as it must be when a fraction less than one is squared.
