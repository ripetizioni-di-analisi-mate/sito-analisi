One leg measures $9$ cm and the hypotenuse measures $15$ cm. What is the length of the projection of that leg onto the hypotenuse, in centimeters?

- $15$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{48}{5}$
- $\dfrac{27}{5}$

## Explanation

By Euclid’s first theorem, the square of the leg equals the product of the hypotenuse and the projection. Divide the square of the leg by the hypotenuse. This gives $\dfrac{27}{5}$.

$$
p=\frac{9^2}{15}=\dfrac{27}{5}
$$
