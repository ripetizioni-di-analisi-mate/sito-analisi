An isosceles triangle has a base of $18$ cm and equal sides of $15$ cm. What is the altitude to the base, in centimeters?

- $6$
- $12$
- $24$
- $9$

## Explanation

The altitude bisects the base. In the resulting right triangle, the equal side is the hypotenuse, and half the base is a leg. This gives $12$.

$$
h=\sqrt{15^2-\left(\frac{18}2\right)^2}=12
$$
