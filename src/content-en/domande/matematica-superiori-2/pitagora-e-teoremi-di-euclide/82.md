An isosceles triangle has equal sides of $13$ cm and an altitude to the base of $12$ cm. What is its area?

- $30$ cm$^2$
- $60$ cm$^2$
- $78$ cm$^2$
- $120$ cm$^2$

## Explanation

The altitude divides the base into two equal parts. In either half of the isosceles triangle, the leg along the base measures $\sqrt{13^2-12^2}=5$ cm. The full base is $10$ cm, making its area half of $10\cdot12$, namely $60$ cm$^2$. The result $30$ cm$^2$ would be the area of just one of the two right triangles.
