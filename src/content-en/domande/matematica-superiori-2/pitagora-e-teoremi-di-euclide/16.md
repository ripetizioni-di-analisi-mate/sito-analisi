$AB=AC$ in an isosceles triangle. Which segment divides base $BC$ into halves and lets you use the Pythagorean theorem in each half?

- The altitude from $B$ to $AC$
- Side $AB$
- The angle bisector at $C$
- The altitude from $A$ to $BC$

## Explanation

The altitude starts at the vertex opposite the base. It is perpendicular to $BC$ and, because the triangle is isosceles about that base, meets it at the midpoint. This gives two right triangles with a leg equal to half of $BC$. A segment from a different vertex refers to a different side or angle.
