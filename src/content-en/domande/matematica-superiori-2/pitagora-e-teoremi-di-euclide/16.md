An isosceles triangle has equal sides of $35$ cm and a base of $42$ cm. Find its area.

- $589$ cm²
- $587$ cm²
- $1176$ cm²
- $588$ cm²

## Explanation

The altitude to the base splits the isosceles triangle into two congruent right triangles. The horizontal leg is half the base. Find the altitude with the Pythagorean theorem, then take half the product of the base and altitude.

$$
h=\sqrt{35^2-\left(\frac{42}{2}\right)^2}=28
$$

$$
A=\frac{42\cdot28}2=588
$$
