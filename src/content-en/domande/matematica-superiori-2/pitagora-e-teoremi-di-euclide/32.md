An isosceles triangle has area $30\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ and altitude to its base $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. How long is each equal side?

- $13\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\sqrt{34}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\sqrt{11}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\sqrt{61}\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The area formula gives the full base. The altitude bisects the base, so each equal side is the hypotenuse of a triangle with legs $6$ and $5$. Add the squares of those two lengths.

$$
\begin{aligned}
b&=\frac{2\cdot30}{5}=12 \\[5pt]
l&=\sqrt{(\frac{12}{2})^2+5^2}=\sqrt{61}\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
