A rectangle has side lengths in ratio $2:3$ and diagonal $2\sqrt{13}$ cm. What is its area?

- $24$ cm$^2$
- $12$ cm$^2$
- $48$ cm$^2$
- $52$ cm$^2$

## Explanation

Write the sides as $2k$ and $3k$. By the Pythagorean theorem the diagonal squared is $13k^2$, and the given diagonal gives $52$ cm$^2$. Thus $k^2=4$ and $k=2$ cm. The sides are $4$ cm and $6$ cm, giving area $24$ cm$^2$. The area can also be calculated directly as $6k^2$. The factor $k$ has units of length because the numbers in the ratio have no units.
