Two sides of a right triangle measure $6$ cm and $8$ cm. What lengths can the third side have?

- Only $10$ cm
- Only $2\sqrt7$ cm
- $10$ cm or $2\sqrt7$ cm
- $14$ cm or $2$ cm

## Explanation

If both given lengths are legs, the third side has square $6^2+8^2=100$. If one is the hypotenuse, it must be $8$ cm and the third side has square $8^2-6^2=28$. The positive roots give valid configurations in both cases. A supposed hypotenuse of $6$ cm would violate the order of lengths in a right triangle.
