A triangle has sides of $18$, $24$, and $30$ cm. Which side is opposite the right angle?

- The side of length $18$ cm.
- The side of length $24$ cm.
- The side of length $30$ cm.
- The triangle has no right angle.

## Explanation

The square of the longest side is the sum of the squares of the other two. By the converse of the Pythagorean theorem, it is the hypotenuse.

$$
18^2+24^2=900=30^2
$$
