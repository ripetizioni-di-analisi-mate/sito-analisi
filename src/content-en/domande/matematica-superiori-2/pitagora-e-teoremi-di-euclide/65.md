A leg opposite $30^\circ$ belongs to a right triangle with hypotenuse $c$. What is its projection onto the hypotenuse?

- $\dfrac c2$
- $\dfrac{3c}{4}$
- $\dfrac c4$
- $\dfrac{c^2}{4}$

## Explanation

The leg opposite the $30^\circ$ angle measures $\dfrac c2$. By the first theorem, its projection is the square of the leg divided by the hypotenuse. The square is $\dfrac{c^2}{4}$, and dividing by $c$ leaves $\dfrac c4$. The other projection is the rest of the hypotenuse, or $\dfrac{3c}{4}$. The projection, like the leg, is measured in units of length.
