A right triangle has an angle of $30^\circ$ and a hypotenuse of $26$ cm. What is the length of the leg opposite that angle, in centimeters?

- $13 \sqrt{3}$
- $\dfrac{13}{2}$
- $13$
- $26$

## Explanation

A right triangle with a thirty-degree angle is half of an equilateral triangle. The opposite leg is half the hypotenuse. This gives $13$.

$$
l=\frac{26}2=13
$$
