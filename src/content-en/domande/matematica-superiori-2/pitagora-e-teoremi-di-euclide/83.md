An isosceles triangle has a base of $24$ cm and equal sides of $20$ cm. What is the altitude to the base, in centimeters?

- $12$
- $32$
- $8$
- $16$

## Explanation

The altitude bisects the base. In the resulting right triangle, the equal side is the hypotenuse, and half the base is a leg. This gives $16$.

$$
h=\sqrt{20^2-\left(\frac{24}2\right)^2}=16
$$
