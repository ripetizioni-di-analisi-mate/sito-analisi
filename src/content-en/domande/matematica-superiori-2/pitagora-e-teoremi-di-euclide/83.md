An isosceles triangle has perimeter $36$ cm and equal sides of $10$ cm. What is its area?

- $24$ cm$^2$
- $80$ cm$^2$
- $96$ cm$^2$
- $48$ cm$^2$

## Explanation

Subtracting $10$ cm twice from the perimeter gives the base of $16$ cm. The altitude meets the base at the midpoint and measures $\sqrt{10^2-8^2}=6$ cm. The area is $\dfrac{16\cdot6}{2}=48$ cm$^2$. The perimeter is used to recover the base length, and the area comes from multiplying it by the altitude.
