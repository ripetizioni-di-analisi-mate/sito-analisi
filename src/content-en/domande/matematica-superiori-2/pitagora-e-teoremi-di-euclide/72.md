The altitude to the hypotenuse is $18$ cm, and one projection measures $12$ cm. How many centimeters long is the other projection?

- $108$
- $27$
- $18$
- $12$

## Explanation

By Euclid’s second theorem, the product of the projections equals the square of the altitude. Divide that square by the known projection. This gives $27$.

$$
q=\frac{18^2}{12}=27
$$
