A square and an equilateral triangle have the same side length $l>0$. What is the ratio of the square's diagonal to the triangle's altitude?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{\sqrt3}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2\sqrt2}$
- $\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{\sqrt2}$
- $\dfrac{2\sqrt2}{\sqrt3}$

## Explanation

The diagonal is $l\sqrt2$, and the altitude is $\dfrac{l\sqrt3}{2}$. The common side cancels in the ratio, and the divisor two moves to the numerator. The ratio must exceed one because the diagonal exceeds the side while the altitude is shorter than the side. Its reciprocal compares the measures in the opposite order.
