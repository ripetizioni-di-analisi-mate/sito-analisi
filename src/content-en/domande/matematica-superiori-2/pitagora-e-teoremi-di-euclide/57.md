A square has diagonal $14$ cm. What is its area?

- $98$ cm$^2$
- $196$ cm$^2$
- $49$ cm$^2$
- $7$ cm$^2$

## Explanation

The two adjacent sides have the same square, so the diagonal squared is twice the square of a side. The square's area is that side squared, half the square of $14$ cm. The result is $98$ cm$^2$. There is no need to find the side length first, which would be $7\sqrt2$ cm.
