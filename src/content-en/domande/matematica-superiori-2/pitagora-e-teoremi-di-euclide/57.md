A square has a diagonal of $7\sqrt2$ cm. How many centimeters long is its side?

- $7$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $14$
- $7 \sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The diagonal is the hypotenuse of a triangle whose legs are the two sides of the square. Its length equals the side length multiplied by the square root of two. This gives $7$.

$$
l=\frac{7\sqrt2}{\sqrt2}=7
$$
