A right triangle has legs of $18$ cm and $24$ cm. What is the length of the hypotenuse?

- $30$ cm
- $31$ cm
- $29$ cm
- $60$ cm

## Explanation

The given sides are the legs, so their squares are added. The Pythagorean theorem gives the square of the hypotenuse. The positive square root is taken because the result is a length.

$$
c=\sqrt{18^2+24^2}=\sqrt{900}=30
$$
