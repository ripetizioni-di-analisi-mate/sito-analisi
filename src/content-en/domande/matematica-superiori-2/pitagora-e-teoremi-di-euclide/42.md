Which three lengths form a right triangle?

- $5,\ 6,\ 7$
- $5,\ 12,\ 13$
- $4,\ 5,\ 6$
- $6,\ 7,\ 8$

## Explanation

The longest side measures thirteen. Its square equals $5^2+12^2=169$. By the converse of the Pythagorean theorem, this makes the triangle right.
