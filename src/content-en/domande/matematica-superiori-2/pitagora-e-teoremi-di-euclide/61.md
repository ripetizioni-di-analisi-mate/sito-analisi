A right triangle has an angle of $30^\circ$ and a hypotenuse of $10$ cm. What is the length of the leg opposite that angle, in centimetres?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$
- $5 \sqrt{3}$
- $10$

## Explanation

A right triangle with a thirty-degree angle is half of an equilateral triangle. The opposite leg is half the hypotenuse. This gives $5$.

$$
l=\frac{10}2=5
$$
