A rectangle has area $48$ cm$^2$ and diagonal $10$ cm. What is its perimeter?

- $14$ cm
- $20$ cm
- $28$ cm
- $48$ cm

## Explanation

The sides $a$ and $b$ satisfy $ab=48$ and $a^2+b^2=100$. Find their sum without finding the sides using $(a+b)^2=a^2+b^2+2ab=196$. The positive sum is $14$ cm. The perimeter contains two copies of the sum, so it measures $28$ cm. Sides of $6$ and $8$ cm verify both given values. Take the positive square root, since both side lengths are positive.
