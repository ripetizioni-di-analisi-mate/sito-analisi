A triangle has sides of $5$, $12$, and $13$ cm. Which reasoning proves it is right?

- Use $5+12>13$ to obtain a right angle
- Start from the idea that the longest side is always a hypotenuse
- Compare only the two shorter sides
- Check $13^2=5^2+12^2$ and apply the converse

## Explanation

The converse starts with three side lengths and checks the square of the greatest one. Here $169=25+144$, so the angle opposite the $13$ cm side is right. The inequality between the lengths checks whether a triangle exists. The word hypotenuse refers to the side opposite a right angle, which must first be established by the equality.
