Which value is impossible for the altitude to a hypotenuse of length $12$ cm?

- $6$ cm
- $7$ cm
- $5$ cm
- $4$ cm

## Explanation

The projections add to $12$ cm, and the altitude squared equals their product. With that sum the product is greatest when both measure $6$ cm, since $p(12-p)=36-(p-6)^2$. The altitude is therefore at most $6$ cm. Smaller positive values are obtained by moving the foot off the symmetry axis toward either endpoint.
