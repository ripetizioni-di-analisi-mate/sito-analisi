In a right triangle, the hypotenuse measures $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and its altitude is $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. What are the projections of the legs?

- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The two projections must add up to $10$ because they make up the hypotenuse. Their product must be $16$ by Euclid’s second theorem. The pair $2$ and $8$ satisfies both conditions.

$$
\begin{aligned}
p+q&=10 \\[5pt]
pq&=4^2=16
\end{aligned}
$$
