From a point $P$ on a semicircle with diameter $AB=13$ cm the perpendicular $PH$ to the diameter is drawn, with $AH=4$ cm. What is the length of $PH$?

- $2\sqrt{13}$ cm
- $36$ cm
- $6$ cm
- $3\sqrt{13}$ cm

## Explanation

The angle $\widehat{APB}$ subtends a semicircle, so it is a right angle and triangle $APB$ is right-angled at $P$. The segment $PH$ is the altitude to the hypotenuse $AB$, and $AH$ and $HB$ are the projections of the legs, with $HB=13-4=9$ cm. By Euclid’s second theorem

$$
PH^2=AH\cdot HB=4\cdot9=36,
$$

so $PH=6$ cm. The answer $36$ cm is the value of $PH^2$, without the square root. The answers $2\sqrt{13}$ cm and $3\sqrt{13}$ cm are the lengths of the chords $PA$ and $PB$, given by Euclid’s first theorem.
