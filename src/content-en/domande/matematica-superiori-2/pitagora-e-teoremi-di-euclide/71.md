The altitude to the hypotenuse is $12$ cm, and one projection measures $8$ cm. How many centimetres long is the other projection?

- $8$
- $12$
- $72$
- $18$

## Explanation

By Euclid’s second theorem, the product of the projections equals the square of the altitude. Divide that square by the known projection. This gives $18$.

$$
q=\frac{12^2}{8}=18
$$
