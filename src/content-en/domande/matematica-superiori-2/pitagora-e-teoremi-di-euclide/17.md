A right triangle has a leg of $5$ cm, and the hypotenuse is $1$ cm longer than the other leg. What is the length of the hypotenuse?

- $13$ cm
- $25$ cm
- $\sqrt{26}$ cm
- $6$ cm

## Explanation

Let $x$ be the other leg, so the hypotenuse measures $x+1$. By the Pythagorean theorem $(x+1)^2=x^2+5^2$. Expanding the square gives $x^2+2x+1=x^2+25$, therefore $2x=24$ and $x=12$. The hypotenuse hence measures $12+1=13$ cm. The answer $25$ cm comes from obtaining $x=24$ from $2x=24$ without dividing by $2$, while $\sqrt{26}$ cm comes from taking $1$ cm as the length of the other leg.
