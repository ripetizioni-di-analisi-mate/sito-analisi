One leg measures $6$ cm and the hypotenuse measures $10$ cm. What is the length of the projection of that leg onto the hypotenuse, in centimetres?

- $6$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{32}{5}$
- $\dfrac{18}{5}$

## Explanation

By Euclid’s first theorem, the square of the leg equals the product of the hypotenuse and the projection. Divide the square of the leg by the hypotenuse. This gives $\dfrac{18}{5}$.

$$
p=\frac{6^2}{10}=\dfrac{18}{5}
$$
