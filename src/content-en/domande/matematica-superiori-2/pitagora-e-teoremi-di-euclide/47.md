A rectangular panel is $0.75$ m by $100$ cm. What is the direct distance between opposite corners in metres?

- $1.75$ m
- $1.25$ m
- $0.25$ m
- $1$ m

## Explanation

Express the second side as $1$ m. The diagonal is the hypotenuse of the triangle formed by two adjacent sides. Its square is $0.75^2+1^2=1.5625$, so its length is $1.25$ m. The value $1.75$ m describes the route along two consecutive borders, whose total exceeds the direct segment.
