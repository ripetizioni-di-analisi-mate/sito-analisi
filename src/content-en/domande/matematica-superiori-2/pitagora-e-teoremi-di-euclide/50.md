How far apart are $P=(-3,4)$ and $Q=(5,-2)$?

::grafico{xmin=-4.2 xmax=5.9 ymin=-3.15 ymax=5.2 punti="-3,4,pieno,P;5,-2,pieno,Q;5,4" segmenti="-3,4,5,4;5,4,5,-2" linee="-3,4 5,-2" titolo="The dashed segments are horizontal and vertical"}

- $10$
- $14$
- $2\sqrt2$
- $2\sqrt7$

## Explanation

The horizontal difference is $5-(-3)=8$. The vertical difference is $-2-4=-6$, giving a vertical leg of length $6$. Thus $\sqrt{8^2+6^2}=10$. Adding the absolute values gives the route along the two dashed segments. The direct segment uses the squares of the differences, even when one difference is negative.
