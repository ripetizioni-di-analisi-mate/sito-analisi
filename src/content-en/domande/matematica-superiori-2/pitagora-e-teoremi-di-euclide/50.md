A ladder of length $30$ m leans against a vertical wall. Its foot is $18$ m from the wall. How high does it reach, in meters?

- $24$
- $96$
- $48$
- $12$

## Explanation

The ladder is the hypotenuse of the right triangle. Subtract the square of the horizontal distance from the square of the ladder’s length and take the square root. This gives $24$.

$$
h=\sqrt{30^2-18^2}=24
$$
