The diagram shows $P=(-3,4)$, $Q=(5,-2)$ and $C=(5,4)$. Where is the centre of the circle through all three?

::grafico{xmin=-4.2 xmax=5.9 ymin=-3.15 ymax=5.2 punti="-3,4,pieno,P;5,-2,pieno,Q;5,4,pieno,C" segmenti="-3,4,5,4;5,4,5,-2" linee="-3,4 5,-2" titolo="Right angle at C with P and Q on its two arms"}

- $(1,1)$
- $(5,4)$
- $(4,-3)$
- $(-3,-2)$

## Explanation

Segments $PC$ and $CQ$ are horizontal and vertical and hence make a right angle at $C$. The hypotenuse $PQ$ is a diameter of the circumcircle. Its midpoint is $(1,1)$.
