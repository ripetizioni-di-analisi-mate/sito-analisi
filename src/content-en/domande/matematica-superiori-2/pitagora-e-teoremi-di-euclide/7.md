In a right triangle, the projections of the legs onto the hypotenuse measure $54$ cm and $96$ cm. What is the area of the triangle?

- $5401$ cm²
- $5399$ cm²
- $5400$ cm²
- $10800$ cm²

## Explanation

The sum of the projections gives the full hypotenuse. By Euclid’s second theorem, their product is the square of the altitude. The hypotenuse is then used as the base in the area formula.

$$
c=54+96=150
$$

$$
h=\sqrt{54\cdot96}=72
$$

$$
A=\frac{ch}2=\frac{150\cdot72}2=5400
$$
