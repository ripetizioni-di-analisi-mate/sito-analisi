The diagonal of a square equals the side of an equilateral triangle. If the square has side length $6\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the area of the equilateral triangle?

- $9 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $18 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $36 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $18 \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The square has diagonal $6\sqrt2$, which is also the side of the triangle. The area formula for an equilateral triangle uses the square of its side. The square of $6\sqrt2$ is $72$, so a quarter of it is $18$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
l&=6\sqrt2 \\[5pt]
\mathcal A&=\frac{\sqrt3}{4}(6\sqrt2)^2=18\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$$
