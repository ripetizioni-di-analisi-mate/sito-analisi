A series has interquartile range $3$. Every value is transformed to $y=3x-1$. Under the nearest-rank convention, what is the new interquartile range?

- $9$
- $8$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

The transformation is increasing, so the same transformed observations occupy the quartile ranks. Each quartile becomes three times its original value minus one. The two terms of minus one cancel in the difference, leaving three times the original distance. The result is $3\cdot3=9$.
