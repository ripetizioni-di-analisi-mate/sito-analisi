What is the median of $9$, $4$, $7$, and $3$?

- $\dfrac{11}{2}$
- $4$
- $7$
- $\dfrac{23}{4}$

## Explanation

After sorting, the middle two values are $4$ and $7$. With four data points, the median is the average of these two values. The result is $\dfrac{11}{2}$.
