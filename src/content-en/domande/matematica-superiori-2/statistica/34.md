The original data are $6,6,6$. Just one of the three values is doubled. What is the descriptive variance now?

- $8$
- $0$
- $4$
- $12$

## Explanation

The new list is $6,6,12$ with mean $8$. The squared deviations are $4,4,16$. Their mean is $\dfrac{24}{3}=8$. The rule that multiplies variance by the square of the scale factor applies when scaling every observation. Here only one value changes, so the deviations have to be recalculated.
