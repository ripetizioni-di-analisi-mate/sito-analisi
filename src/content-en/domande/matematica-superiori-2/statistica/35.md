The sorted observations are $0,2,3,5,9,14$. Using nearest ranks, what is $Q_3-Q_1$?

- $5$
- $7$
- $9$
- $14$

## Explanation

With six observations calculate $\dfrac{6}{4}=1.5$ and $\dfrac{3\cdot6}{4}=4.5$. Rounding up gives ranks $2$ and $5$. The values at those positions are $2$ and $9$, giving interquartile range $7$. The value $14$ instead measures the distance between the first and last observations.
