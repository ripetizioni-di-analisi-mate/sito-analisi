The value $6$ occurs $2$ times and the value $11$ occurs $3$ times. What is the mean of the data?

- $9$
- $10$
- $8$
- $18$

## Explanation

Multiply each value by its absolute frequency. Divide by the total number of observations, counting repeated values as separate observations. This gives the weighted mean.

$$
\bar x=\frac{2\cdot6+3\cdot11}{2+3}=9
$$

The higher value contributes more because it represents three observations out of five. The result should therefore be closer to $11$ than to $6$.
