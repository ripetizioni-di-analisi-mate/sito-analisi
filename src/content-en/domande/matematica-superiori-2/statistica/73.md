Of $80$ invitees, $75\%$ respond. Of the respondents, $40\%$ are in favour. What are the minimum and maximum possible counts in favour among all invitees, with no other information?

- $24,\quad32$
- $24,\quad44$
- $32,\quad52$
- $40,\quad60$

## Explanation

There are $80\cdot0.75=60$ respondents. Of them $60\cdot0.40=24$ are in favour. The twenty people who did not answer can add anywhere from zero to twenty favourable responses, giving extremes of $24$ and $44$. The $40\%$ uses only respondents as its denominator and cannot be extended to all invitees with certainty.
