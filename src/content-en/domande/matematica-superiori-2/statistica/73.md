A class has $36$ students, and $25\%$ come to school by bicycle. How many students is that?

- $36$
- $9$
- $18$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$

## Explanation

Twenty-five percent is one quarter of the total. Divide the class size by four to obtain $9$.
