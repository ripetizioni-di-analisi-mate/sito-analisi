The values $4$, $7$, and $10$ have mean $7$. What is their descriptive variance, using three as the divisor?

- $2$
- $6$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

The deviations from the mean are negative $3$, zero, and $3$. Add their squares and divide by three to obtain $6$.

The middle deviation is zero because the second observation equals the mean. It still counts among the three observations to describe, along with the two extremes that give equal squared deviations.
