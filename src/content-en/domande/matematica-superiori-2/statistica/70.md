Five nonnegative observations have a sum of squares of $90$ and descriptive variance $2$. What is their mean?

- $16$
- $4$
- $\sqrt{20}$
- $\sqrt{88}$

## Explanation

The mean of the squares is $\dfrac{90}{5}=18$. Subtracting the square of the mean gives variance $2$. Thus $\bar x^2=18-2=16$. The mean must be nonnegative like the data, so the positive root gives $4$. The value $16$ is the square of the mean.
