A class has $52$ students, and $25\%$ come to school by bicycle. How many students is that?

- $26$
- $13$
- $\dfrac{13}{4}$
- $52$

## Explanation

Twenty-five percent is one quarter of the total. Divide the class size by four to obtain $13$.
