One person supplies three measurements $5,5,5$, and another supplies one measurement $9$. First find the mean for each person, then give the two people equal weight. What is the result?

- $6$
- $7$
- $5$
- $8$

## Explanation

The first person’s mean is $5$ and the second’s is $9$. Giving each person one contribution yields $\dfrac{5+9}{2}=7$. The mean of all individual measurements would be $\dfrac{5+5+5+9}{4}=6$ because it would give the first person three times the weight. The choice depends on the unit the question asks us to describe.
