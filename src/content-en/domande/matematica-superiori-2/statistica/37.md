The values $1$, $4$, and $6$ have frequencies $3$, $5$, and $5$. Remove one observation of $6$ and add one of $1$. What is the new mode?

- $6$
- $1,6$
- $4,6$
- $4$

## Explanation

Before the change, $4$ and $6$ share the maximum count of five. The counts change from $3,5,5$ to $4,5,4$. Only $4$ still occurs five times. The other two values now tie with one another, but their counts are smaller, so neither is a mode.
