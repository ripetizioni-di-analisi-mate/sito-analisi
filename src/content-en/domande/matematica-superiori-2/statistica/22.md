One group of $24$ people gives $6$ yes responses. A disjoint group of $36$ people has the same relative frequency. How many yes responses do the groups give in total?

- $15$
- $12$
- $9$
- $18$

## Explanation

In the first group the proportion is $\dfrac6{24}=\dfrac14$. In the second it is still a quarter, giving $\dfrac{36}{4}=9$ yes responses. Since no person is in both groups, add the two counts to get $6+9=15$. This is one quarter of all sixty people observed.
