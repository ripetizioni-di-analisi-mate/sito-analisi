Out of $80$ responses, one category is reported as $14\%$, rounded to the nearest whole percent. Which count is compatible?

- $11$
- $12$
- $14$
- $10$

## Explanation

Each response out of eighty contributes $\dfrac{100}{80}=1.25$ percentage points. Eleven responses give $13.75\%$, which rounds to $14\%$. Ten would give $12.5\%$, and twelve would give $15\%$. Those proportions are too far away, and fourteen responses would give an even higher proportion.
