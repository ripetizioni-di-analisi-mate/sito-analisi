A class has $28$ students, and $25\%$ come to school by bicycle. How many students is that?

- $7$
- $\dfrac{7}{4}$
- $14$
- $28$

## Explanation

Twenty-five percent is one quarter of the total. Divide the class size by four to obtain $7$.
