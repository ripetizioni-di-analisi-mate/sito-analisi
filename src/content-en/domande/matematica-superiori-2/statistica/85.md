A grouped table has two observations in $[0,6)$ and two in $[6,10)$. What is the approximate descriptive variance using the class midpoints?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{25}{4}$
- $25$
- $\dfrac{25}{3}$

## Explanation

The midpoints are $3$ and $8$, each with weight two. The representative list is $3,3,8,8$. It has mean $\dfrac{11}{2}$, and every deviation has absolute value $\dfrac{5}{2}$. The mean of the squares is $\dfrac{25}{4}$. It remains an estimate because the table does not show the actual deviations within classes.
