What is the median of $7$, $3$, $5$, and $2$?

- $4$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{17}{4}$
- $5$

## Explanation

After sorting, the middle two values are $3$ and $5$. With four data points, the median is the average of these two values. The result is $4$.
