The value $2$ occurs three times, $6$ twice, and $8$ four times. What is the median?

- $2$
- $6$
- $8$
- $5$

## Explanation

There are nine observations, so the central position is the fifth. The value $2$ occupies the first three positions. The value $6$ occupies the fourth and fifth.

At value $6$, the cumulative count reaches five for the first time. The fifth observation therefore belongs to that group even when several observations have the same value.
