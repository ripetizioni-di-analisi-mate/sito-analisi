Lengths in group A have variance $9\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. Lengths in group B have standard deviation $0.04\,\mathrm m$. Which group has the higher standard deviation once the units match?

- B, because $4\,\mathrm{cm}>3\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- A, because $9\,\mathrm{cm}>4\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- A, because $3\,\mathrm{cm}>0.04\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- The standard deviations are equal.

## Explanation

The variance of A is in square centimetres. Taking the square root gives $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. B’s standard deviation is already a length and just needs to be written in centimetres. Multiplying $0.04$ by $100$ gives $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. Comparing the two distances in the same unit gives B.
