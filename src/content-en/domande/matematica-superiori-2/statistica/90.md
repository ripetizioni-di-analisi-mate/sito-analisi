A data set has standard deviation $6$. What is the new standard deviation if every value is tripled?

- $18$
- $54$
- $6$
- $2$

## Explanation

The mean triples, so every deviation from the mean also triples. The standard deviation is multiplied by the same factor. The result is $18$.
