Three of the deviations from the mean of four observations are $-5$, $-2$, and $4$. What is the fourth deviation?

- $-3$
- $11$
- $0$
- $3$

## Explanation

Signed deviations add to zero because the mean is subtracted once per observation. The three given deviations sum to $-5-2+4=-3$. The fourth must be $3$ to balance them. Squaring would remove the signs instead, which would prepare a variance calculation.
