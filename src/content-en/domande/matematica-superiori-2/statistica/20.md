The values $5$, $9$, and $13$ have mean $9$. What is their descriptive variance, using three as the divisor?

- $\dfrac{16}{3}$
- $16$
- $\dfrac{32}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$

## Explanation

The deviations from the mean are negative $4$, zero, and $4$. Add their squares and divide by three to obtain $\dfrac{32}{3}$.
