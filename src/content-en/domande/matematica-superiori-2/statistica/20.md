Group $A$ contains $4,4$, and group $B$ contains $8,8$. Both group variances are zero. What is the descriptive variance of the four observations combined?

- $0$
- $2$
- $4$
- $16$

## Explanation

The combined mean is $6$, so each observation is two units from the new centre. All four squared deviations equal four, giving a mean of four. The zero variances in the original groups describe deviations from their separate centres. The combined calculation uses the overall mean.
