Series $A=(1;1;5;5)$ and $B=(1;3;3;5)$ have the same mean. What is the ratio $\dfrac{\sigma_A^2}{\sigma_B^2}$ of their descriptive variances?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The common centre is $3$. All four observations in $A$ are two units from it. In $B$ only two observations are that far away, while the others equal the mean. Both variances divide the squared-deviation sum by four. The sum for $A$ is twice as large, so the ratio is $2$.
