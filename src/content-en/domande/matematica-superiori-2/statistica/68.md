The list $2,4,6,8$ changes to $2,2,8,8$. What happens to the mean and descriptive variance?

- Mean and variance both stay at $5$.
- The mean increases from $5$ to $9$ and the variance stays at $5$.
- The mean stays at $5$ and the variance increases from $5$ to $9$.
- The mean stays at $5$ and the variance decreases.

## Explanation

Replacing $4$ with $2$ subtracts two units and replacing $6$ with $8$ adds them back. The mean stays $5$. In the first list the squared deviations add to $9+1+1+9=20$. In the second all four observations are three units from the mean and the sum rises to $36$. The variances are $\dfrac{20}{4}=5$ and $\dfrac{36}{4}=9$.
