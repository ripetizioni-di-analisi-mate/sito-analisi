Can descriptive variance be negative?

- $\text{Yes, if the mean is negative}$
- $\text{No}$
- $\text{Yes, if all the values are negative}$
- $\text{Yes, with an even number of observations}$

## Explanation

The variance is the mean of squared deviations. Every square is nonnegative, and the number of data values is positive. Their mean is therefore nonnegative.
