A class has $20$ students, and $25\%$ come to school by bicycle. How many students is that?

- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $5$
- $20$
- $10$

## Explanation

Twenty-five percent is one quarter of the total. Divide the class size by four to obtain $5$.
