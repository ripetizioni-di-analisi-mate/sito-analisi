In a calibration $y=ax+7$, set $a=0$. What holds for the transformed values if there is at least one observation?

- The mean and standard deviation both equal $7$.
- The mean is $7$ and the standard deviation is zero.
- The mean is zero and the standard deviation is $7$.
- The mean is $7$ and the standard deviation stays at its original value for every series.

## Explanation

When $a$ is zero, the formula assigns $7$ to every observation. Identical values have that common value as their mean. Every deviation from the new mean is zero. The standard-deviation formula gives the same result, because the factor $|a|$ is zero.
