A list of $7$ values has median $12$. Add $3$ to each observation. What is the new median?

- $15$
- $12$
- $9$
- $36$

## Explanation

Adding the same constant preserves the order. The fourth sorted observation, which gave the original median, increases by $3$. The median is therefore $15$.
