The values $0$, $3$, and $6$ occur $1$, $4$, and $1$ times respectively. What is their descriptive variance?

- $6$
- $9$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{18}{5}$

## Explanation

The values at the extremes balance around $3$, giving mean $3$. The nonzero squared deviations are $9$ and $9$, while four others are zero. The divisor is six because observations equal to the mean count too. The descriptive variance is therefore $\dfrac{18}{6}=3$.
