The values $7$, $13$, and $19$ have mean $13$. What is their descriptive variance, using three as the divisor?

- $36$
- $12$
- $24$
- $4$

## Explanation

The deviations from the mean are negative $6$, zero, and $6$. Add their squares and divide by three to obtain $24$.
