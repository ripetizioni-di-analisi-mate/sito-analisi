Find the median of the sorted values $6,\,7,\,10,\,13$.

- $\dfrac{19}{2}$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}$
- $\dfrac{17}{2}$
- $17$

## Explanation

With an even number of observations, the median is the average of the two middle values, here the second and third. Averaging all the values would give the arithmetic mean.

$$
M_e=\frac{7+10}2=\dfrac{17}{2}
$$

The midpoint can be absent from the list. Here it lies strictly between consecutive observations, because the two middle values are distinct.
