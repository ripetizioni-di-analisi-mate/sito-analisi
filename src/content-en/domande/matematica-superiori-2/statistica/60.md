Which list has a unique mode of $2$ and a median of $5$?

- $2,2,2,5,9$
- $2,5,5,7,9$
- $2,2,5,7,9$
- $2,2,5,9,9$

## Explanation

Each list has five observations, so the median is the third. In $2,2,5,7,9$ the third is $5$ and only $2$ occurs twice. In the list with three copies of $2$, the median would be $2$. In the list with two copies of $9$, that value also has frequency two, so $2$ is not the only mode.
