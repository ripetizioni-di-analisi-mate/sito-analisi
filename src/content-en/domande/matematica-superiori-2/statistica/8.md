What is the descriptive standard deviation of the two values $6$ and $10$?

- $3$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Find the mean, then compute the variance using the number of observations as the divisor. The standard deviation is the nonnegative square root of the variance. It has the same units as the original values.

$$
\bar x=8
$$

$$
\sigma^2=\frac{(-2)^2+2^2}{2}=4
$$

$$
\sigma=\sqrt4=2
$$

With two values, the opposite deviations have the same length. The standard deviation equals the distance of either observation from the mean.
