A series has mean $3$ and descriptive variance $5$. What is the mean of the squared observations?

- $14$
- $9$
- $25$
- $8$

## Explanation

Variance is the mean of the squared observations minus the square of the mean. Here the square of the mean is $3^2=9$. Recover the mean of the squares by adding $9$ to variance $5$, giving $14$. The two quantities differ because one calculation squares each observation separately.
