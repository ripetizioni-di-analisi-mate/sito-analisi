What do the relative frequencies of all categories sum to?

- $1$
- $100$
- $0$
- $\text{The number of categories}$

## Explanation

Each relative frequency divides a count by the total. The counts add to that total, so the overall ratio is one. The percentage frequencies add to one hundred percent.
