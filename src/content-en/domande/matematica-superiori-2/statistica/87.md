A data set has standard deviation $3$. What is the new standard deviation if every value is tripled?

- $1$
- $27$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

The mean triples, so every deviation from the mean also triples. The standard deviation is multiplied by the same factor. The result is $9$.
