Two groups have the same mean. The first contains $4$ observations with population variance $9$, the second $12$ observations with population variance $1$. What is the population standard deviation of all $16$ observations?

- $\sqrt5$
- $2$
- $3$
- $\sqrt3$

## Explanation

The common mean is also the pooled mean. The sum of squared deviations is $4\cdot9+12\cdot1=48$. Dividing by $16$ gives variance $3$, and taking its square root gives the standard deviation.
