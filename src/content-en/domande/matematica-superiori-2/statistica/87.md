Find the descriptive standard deviation of the values $2,\,4,\,6,\,8$.

- $5$
- $2$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{20}{3}}$
- $\sqrt5$

## Explanation

The mean is $\bar x=\dfrac{2+4+6+8}{4}=5$, so the deviations from the mean are $-3$, $-1$, $1$ and $3$. The descriptive variance divides the sum of the squared deviations by the number of values.

$$
\sigma^2=\frac{9+1+1+9}{4}=5
$$

The standard deviation is the nonnegative square root of the variance, so $\sigma=\sqrt5$. The value $5$ is the variance, while $\sqrt{\dfrac{20}{3}}$ would come from dividing by $3$ instead of $4$.
