Find the median of $13,\,6,\,9,\,7,\,15$.

- $10$
- $9$
- $8$
- $18$

## Explanation

To find the median, first sort the observations. There are an odd number of observations, so take the middle value. Here the third sorted observation leaves two values on either side.

$$
6<7<9<13<15
$$

$$
M_e=9
$$

Increasing the last observation would leave it in fifth place and leave the centre unchanged. The calculation uses the sorted positions.
