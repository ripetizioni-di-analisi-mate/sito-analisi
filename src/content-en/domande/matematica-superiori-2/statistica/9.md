The mean of $5$ data values is $12$. Remove the value $20$. What is the mean of the four remaining values?

- $8$
- $12$
- $15$
- $10$

## Explanation

The initial sum is $5\cdot12=60$. Removing $20$ leaves a sum of $40$. Four values remain, so their mean is $10$.
