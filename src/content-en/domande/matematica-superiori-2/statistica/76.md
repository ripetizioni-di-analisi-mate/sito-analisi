In a pie chart, one category contains $2$ out of $8$ observations. What is the angle of the sector in degrees?

- $90$
- $45$
- $25$
- $180$

## Explanation

The sector occupies the same fraction of a full revolution as the category does of the total. One quarter of three hundred sixty degrees is ninety. The result is $90$.
