For $1,2,2,2,5,7,7,9$, use nearest ranks. What proportions are strictly below $Q_1$ and at most $Q_1$, respectively?

- $\dfrac{2}{8},\quad \dfrac{2}{8}$
- $\dfrac{2}{8},\quad \dfrac{4}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8},\quad \dfrac{4}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8},\quad \dfrac{2}{8}$

## Explanation

The first quartile is at the second position, so it equals $2$. Only $1$ is strictly smaller than $2$. The inclusive inequality also counts all three copies of $2$, giving four observations. With repeated values, the quartile rank is not necessarily the last position occupied by its value.
