A chart compares $20$ and $30$ enrolments using squares with side lengths $2$ and $3$. What are the ratios of counts and of areas, taking the second over the first in both cases?

- $\dfrac{3}{2},\quad\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2},\quad\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4},\quad\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3},\quad\dfrac{4}{9}$

## Explanation

The counts have ratio $\dfrac{30}{20}=\dfrac{3}{2}$. The areas of the squares are $2^2=4$ and $3^2=9$. So the second area is $\dfrac{9}{4}$ of the first. Scaling both sides of a figure scales its area by the factor squared, amplifying the visible gap here.
