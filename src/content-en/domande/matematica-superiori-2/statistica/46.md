There are $2$ ratings equal to $3$ and $5$ ratings equal to $5$. What is the mean?

- $\dfrac{31}{5}$
- $5$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{31}{7}$

## Explanation

Each value contributes as many times as its frequency. Add the products and divide by the number of ratings to obtain $\dfrac{31}{7}$.
