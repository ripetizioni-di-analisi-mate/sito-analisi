Three disjoint categories cover all responses. The first two represent $45\%$ and $35\%$. What is the angle of the third sector?

- $80^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $108^\circ$
- $72^\circ$

## Explanation

The first two proportions sum to eighty percent. The third therefore represents $20\%$, one fifth of the total. One fifth of a turn is $\dfrac{360^\circ}{5}=72^\circ$. Alternatively, the first two sectors have angles $162^\circ$ and $126^\circ$, which together are $72^\circ$ short of $360^\circ$.
