The lengths $2,2,3,4,4$ are recorded in centimetres. Replace one measurement of $4$ by one of $3$. What are the new modes?

- $2,3$
- $2,4$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The replacement reduces the count at $4$ by one and increases the count at $3$ by one. The list becomes $2,2,3,3,4$. Both $2$ and $3$ now occur twice, and $4$ occurs once. There are still two modes, but one of the two most frequent values has changed.
