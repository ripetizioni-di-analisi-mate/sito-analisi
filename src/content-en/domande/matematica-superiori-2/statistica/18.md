Which data set has greater descriptive variance, $A=(1,4,7)$ or $B=(3,4,5)$?

- $A$
- $B$
- $\sigma_A^2=\sigma_B^2=0$
- $\sigma_A^2=\sigma_B^2>0$

## Explanation

Both data sets have mean $4$. The extremes of A are $3$ units from the mean and the extremes of B are $1$ unit away. The sets have the same size, so the larger squared deviations in A produce greater variance.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_A^2&=\frac{9+0+9}{3}=6,\\
\sigma_B^2&=\frac{1+0+1}{3}=\frac23.
\end{aligned}
$$

Tripling deviations multiplies each squared deviation by nine. The ratio of the variances captures exactly that effect.
