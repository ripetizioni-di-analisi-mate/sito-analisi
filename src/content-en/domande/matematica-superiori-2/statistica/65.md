A series has range $10$. What must hold for the deviations from the mean?

- All deviations have absolute value at least $5$.
- All deviations have absolute value at most $5$.
- The maximum’s deviation always has absolute value $10$.
- At least one deviation has absolute value at least $5$.

## Explanation

The mean lies between the extremes. The distance from the minimum to the mean, plus the distance from the mean to the maximum, is $10$. If both distances were below $5$, their sum would be below ten. So at least one distance is at least five. Many other values can still lie near the mean.
