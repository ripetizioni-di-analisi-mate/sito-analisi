A chart shows counts $40$ and $50$. The vertical axis starts at $b<40$. What value of $b$ makes the second visible height three times the first?

- $30$
- $35$
- $25$
- $37$

## Explanation

The visible heights are $40-b$ and $50-b$. To make the second triple the first, set $50-b=3(40-b)$. Expanding gives $2b=70$, hence $b=35$. The heights check out as $5$ and $15$. The ratio of the full counts remains $\dfrac{50}{40}=\dfrac{5}{4}$.
