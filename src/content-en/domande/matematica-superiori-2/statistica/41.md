Which measure depends on the positions of the data in increasing order?

- $\text{The arithmetic mean}$
- $\text{The total absolute frequency}$
- $\text{The sum of the values}$
- $\text{The median}$

## Explanation

The median is determined by the middle position in the sorted list. A value at the middle of the unsorted list does not necessarily have that role. The mean can be calculated in any order.
