In a group of $40$ students, $35\%$ choose guitar. How many students choose it?

- $14$
- $16$
- $26$
- $35$

## Explanation

The proportion represents thirty-five people out of every hundred. Multiplying the total $40$ by $\dfrac{35}{100}$ gives $14$ people.
