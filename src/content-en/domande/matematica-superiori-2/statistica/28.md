A sorted list is $1,3,8,10$. Add one value below $1$ and one above $10$. What is the new median?

- $3$
- $8$
- $\dfrac{11}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$

## Explanation

With six observations use the third and fourth positions. The new small value shifts all previous values one place, and the new large value goes at the end. So the third and fourth values are still the old middle values $3$ and $8$. Their mean is still $\dfrac{11}{2}$.
