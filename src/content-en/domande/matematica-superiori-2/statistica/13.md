The frequencies of $1$, $2$, and $3$ are $4$, $4$, and $2$, respectively. Add one more observation of value $2$. What is the mode?

- $1$
- $3$
- $\{1;2\}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The count for $2$ rises from four to five. The other counts stay at four and two. The new highest frequency is five and belongs only to $2$.
