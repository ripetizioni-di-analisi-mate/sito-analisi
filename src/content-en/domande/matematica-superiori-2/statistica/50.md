There are $6$ ratings equal to $7$ and $13$ ratings equal to $9$. What is the mean?

- $\dfrac{159}{19}$
- $\dfrac{159}{13}$
- $8$
- $9$

## Explanation

Each value contributes as many times as its frequency. Add the products and divide by the number of ratings to obtain $\dfrac{159}{19}$.
