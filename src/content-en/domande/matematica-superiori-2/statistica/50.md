A grouped table contains three values in $[0,4)$ and two in $[4,8)$. What is the mean estimated from the class midpoints?

- $\dfrac{18}{5}$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{22}{5}$
- $\dfrac{18}{2}$

## Explanation

The midpoints are $2$ and $6$. Weight them by three and two, since they represent three and two observations. The estimated sum is $3\cdot2+2\cdot6=18$, giving estimated mean $\dfrac{18}{5}$. The table gives the weights exactly, but the individual observations may differ from the midpoints.
