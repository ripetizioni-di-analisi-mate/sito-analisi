A series has standard deviation $3$. Every value $x$ is replaced by $5-2x$. What is the new standard deviation?

- $-6$
- $12$
- $6$
- $11$

## Explanation

The term $5$ occurs in both the new values and new mean and cancels in the deviations. The factor $-2$ reverses signs and doubles distances from the centre, so it quadruples their squares. Taking the square root then doubles the standard deviation, giving $6$.
