A series contains only the values $2$ and $8$. Its mean is $6$. What proportion of observations equals $8$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

If the share at $8$ is $p$, the share at $2$ is $1-p$. The weighted mean is $2(1-p)+8p=2+6p$. Making it $6$ requires $6p=4$, so $p=\dfrac{2}{3}$. The higher value has more weight, as indicated by the mean lying nearer to $8$ than to $2$.
