There are $3$ ratings equal to $4$ and $7$ ratings equal to $6$. What is the mean?

- $6$
- $\dfrac{27}{5}$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{54}{7}$

## Explanation

Each value contributes as many times as its frequency. Add the products and divide by the number of ratings to obtain $\dfrac{27}{5}$.
