In a histogram, the bar areas equal the absolute frequencies. Class $[0,2)$ has height $6$ and class $[2,6)$ has height $4$. How many observations fall in the second class?

::grafico{poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,6 0,6;2,0 6,0 6,4 2,4" xmin=-0.6 xmax=7 ymin=0 ymax=7 nomi="x,d" titolo="The bar areas equal the absolute frequencies"}

- $4$
- $12$
- $24$
- $16$

## Explanation

The second bar has width $6-2=4$ and height $4$. Their product gives area $16$, so the second class contains $16$ observations.
