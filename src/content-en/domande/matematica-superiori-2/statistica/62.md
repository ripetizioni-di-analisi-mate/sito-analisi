A nonempty series has range zero. What can be said about its mean and descriptive variance?

- Both the mean and the variance must be zero.
- The variance can be positive if the common value is positive.
- The data can contain distinct values and still have variance zero.
- The mean is the common value of all observations and the variance is zero.

## Explanation

Zero range means maximum and minimum coincide. Every observation lies between them and must equal that common value. The mean equals the same value and every deviation from it is zero. The common value can be different from zero without changing any of those deviations.
