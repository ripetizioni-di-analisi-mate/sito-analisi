Find the descriptive variance of $9,\,11,\,13$ using the number of data values as the divisor.

- $\dfrac{11}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}$

## Explanation

The mean is the middle value here. Subtract the mean from each observation, square the deviations, and average them. This is descriptive variance, so the divisor is the number of observations.

$$
\bar x=11
$$

$$
\sigma^2=\frac{(-2)^2+0^2+2^2}{3}=\frac83
$$
