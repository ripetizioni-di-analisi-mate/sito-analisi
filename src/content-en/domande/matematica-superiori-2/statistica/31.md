Four observations have mean $5$. Three are $5$, $6$, and $7$. Which pair gives the fourth value and the descriptive variance?

- $2,\quad 3$
- $2,\quad \dfrac{7}{2}$
- $4,\quad \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $5,\quad \dfrac{5}{4}$

## Explanation

The sum must be $4\cdot5=20$ and the three known observations contribute $18$. The fourth is therefore $2$. The deviations from the mean are $0$, $1$, $2$, and $-3$. Their squares add to $0+1+4+9=14$. Dividing by four gives $\dfrac{7}{2}$.
