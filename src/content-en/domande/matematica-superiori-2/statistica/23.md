A data set has descriptive variance $9$. Add $5$ to each value, then multiply by $2$. What is the new variance?

- $18$
- $36$
- $14$
- $28$

## Explanation

Adding $5$ also raises the mean by $5$, so the deviations stay the same. Multiplying by $2$ doubles the deviations. Their squares become four times as large, so the variance is $36$.
