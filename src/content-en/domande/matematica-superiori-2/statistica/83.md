Two bars in a relative frequency chart show $70\%$ for group A and $65\%$ for B. What can be concluded without knowing the group sizes?

- A has a higher proportion, while the absolute counts could rank either way.
- A always has a higher absolute count.
- B always has a higher absolute count.
- The two absolute counts must be equal.

## Explanation

The percentages compare each count with its own group. If both groups had $20$ people, the counts would be $14$ and $13$. If B instead had $40$ people, it would preserve its proportion with $26$, more than $14$. The percentage bars stay in the same order, but the absolute counts can reverse.
