A class has $44$ students, and $25\%$ come to school by bicycle. How many students is that?

- $11$
- $\dfrac{11}{4}$
- $22$
- $44$

## Explanation

Twenty-five percent is one quarter of the total. Divide the class size by four to obtain $11$.
