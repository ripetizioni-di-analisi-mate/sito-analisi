In an easy group, method A gives $9$ successes out of $10$, and B gives $80$ out of $100$. In a hard group, A gives $30$ out of $100$, and B gives $2$ out of $10$. Each trial belongs to just one group. Which method has the higher success proportion when both groups are combined?

- B, with $82$ successes out of $110$.
- A, with $39$ successes out of $110$.
- The combined proportions are equal.
- A, with $60\%$ from the mean of its two group proportions.

## Explanation

For A add $9+30=39$ successes, and for B add $80+2=82$. Both methods have $110$ trials, so B has the higher combined proportion. A has a higher proportion within each group, but for B the easy group has more weight in the total. The combined proportions also reflect that different composition.
