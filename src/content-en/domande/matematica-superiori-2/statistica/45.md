In a histogram with unequal class widths, what quantity must be proportional to the frequency?

- $\text{Always the height of the bar}$
- $\text{The perimeter of the bar}$
- $\text{Only the width of the bar}$
- $\text{The area of the bar}$

## Explanation

A histogram represents frequencies by areas. When class widths differ, the bar height is the frequency density. The product of width and height then preserves the correct comparison of frequencies.
