What is the median of $13$, $6$, $11$, and $5$?

- $11$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{17}{2}$
- $\dfrac{35}{4}$

## Explanation

After sorting, the middle two values are $6$ and $11$. With four data points, the median is the average of these two values. The result is $\dfrac{17}{2}$.
