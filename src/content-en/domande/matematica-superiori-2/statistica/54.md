A list of values has median $4$. The sign of every value is reversed. What is the new median?

- $4$
- $0$
- $-4$
- It depends on the number of observations.

## Explanation

Changing the signs reverses the order. With an odd count, the middle observation remains in the middle and changes sign. With an even count, the two middle observations swap places and become their negatives. Their mean also changes sign. Both cases give a median of $-4$.
