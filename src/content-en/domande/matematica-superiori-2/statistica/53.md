What is the median of $11$, $5$, $9$, and $4$?

- $5$
- $\dfrac{29}{4}$
- $9$
- $7$

## Explanation

After sorting, the middle two values are $5$ and $9$. With four data points, the median is the average of these two values. The result is $7$.
