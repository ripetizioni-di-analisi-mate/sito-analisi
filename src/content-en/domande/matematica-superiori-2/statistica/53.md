Four observations fall in $[0,4)$ and four in $[4,10)$. What can be established about the median’s class?

- It is always in the first class.
- It is always in the second class.
- It must equal $4$.
- Depending on the observations, it can fall in the first class or the second.

## Explanation

The middle values are at positions four and five. The fourth is in the first class and the fifth in the second. Four values of $1$ and four of $5$ give median $3$. Four values of $3$ and four of $9$ give median $6$. Both distributions fit the stated counts.
