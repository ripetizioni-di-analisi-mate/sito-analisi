Four measurements have mean $7$ and descriptive variance $4$. A fifth measurement equal to $7$ is added. What is the new descriptive variance?

- $4$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}$
- $\dfrac{16}{5}$

## Explanation

Adding a value equal to the mean preserves the centre. The original deviations stay the same and the new deviation is zero. The sum of squares was $4\cdot4=16$ and is unchanged. There are now five observations, so the variance is $\dfrac{16}{5}$. The new value reduces the relative weight of the nonzero deviations.
