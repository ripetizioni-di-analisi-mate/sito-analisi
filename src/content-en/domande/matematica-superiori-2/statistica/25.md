Find the mean of $9,\,11,\,13,\,15$.

- $13$
- $11$
- $24$
- $12$

## Explanation

Add all the values and divide by the number of observations. Each value carries the same weight because it occurs once. The result lies between the minimum and maximum.

$$
\bar x=\frac{9+11+13+15}4=12
$$
