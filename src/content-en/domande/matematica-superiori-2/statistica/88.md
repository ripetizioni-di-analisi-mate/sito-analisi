A data set has standard deviation $4$. What is the new standard deviation if every value is tripled?

- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $36$
- $12$
- $4$

## Explanation

The mean triples, so every deviation from the mean also triples. The standard deviation is multiplied by the same factor. The result is $12$.
