A series changes from standard deviation $3$ to standard deviation $12$ by applying $y=ax+b$ to all values. Which relation for $a$ must hold?

- $a=4$
- $a=-4$
- $|a|=4$
- $|a|=16$

## Explanation

The standard deviation is multiplied by $|a|$ because it measures distances from the mean. The ratio of the new standard deviation to the original one is $\dfrac{12}{3}=4$. This determines the absolute value. A positive factor of four and a negative factor of minus four both give the same spread.
