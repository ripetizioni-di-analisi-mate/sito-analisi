A series contains exactly two observations with mean $8$ and descriptive standard deviation $3$. What are the observations?

- $8,11$
- $2,14$
- $5,11$
- $6,10$

## Explanation

With two observations the deviations from the mean are opposites. If one is $d$, the other is $-d$, so the mean of their squares is $d^2$ and the standard deviation is $|d|$. Each datum must be three units from $8$. Subtracting and adding three gives $5$ and $11$.
