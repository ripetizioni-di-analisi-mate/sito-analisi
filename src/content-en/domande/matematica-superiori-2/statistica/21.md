A table gives counts for several numerical values. A second table triples every count while keeping the same values. How do the mean and descriptive variance change?

- Both triple.
- The mean stays the same and the variance triples.
- The mean triples and the variance stays the same.
- Both stay the same.

## Explanation

Tripling all counts preserves the relative frequencies. For the mean, it triples both the weighted sum and the total count, leaving their ratio fixed. The deviations from that mean are therefore the same. The numerator and denominator of the variance both triple as well, so the variance is unchanged.
