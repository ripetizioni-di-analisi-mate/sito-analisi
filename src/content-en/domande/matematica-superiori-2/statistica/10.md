A group of $3$ students has mean $8$. A group of $5$ students has mean $12$. What is the combined mean?

- $10.5$
- $10$
- $11$
- $12$

## Explanation

The group totals are $3\cdot8=24$ and $5\cdot12=60$. Their combined sum is $84$. Dividing by all eight students gives $10.5$.
