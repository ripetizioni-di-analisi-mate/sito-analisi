What happens to the standard deviation if $10$ is added to each value?

- $\text{It increases by }10$
- $\text{It is multiplied by }10$
- $\text{It stays the same}$
- $\text{It becomes zero}$

## Explanation

The mean also increases by ten. Every deviation from the mean stays the same because the increase cancels in the subtraction. The variance and standard deviation stay the same.
