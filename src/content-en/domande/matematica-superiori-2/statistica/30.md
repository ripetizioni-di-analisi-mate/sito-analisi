In $4$, $6$, $7$, $9$, and $10$, replace $10$ with $25$. By how much does the range increase?

- $15$
- $21$
- $6$
- $25$

## Explanation

The minimum remains $4$. The range goes from $10-4=6$ to $25-4=21$. It increases by $21-6=15$.

We can also compare the two maxima directly. The replacement sets a new maximum and leaves the minimum in place. Subtracting the same minimum from both ranges gives the same increase as the maximum.
