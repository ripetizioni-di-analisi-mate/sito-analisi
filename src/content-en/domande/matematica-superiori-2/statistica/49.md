The graph shows frequencies $2$, $5$, and $3$ for values $1$, $2$, and $3$, respectively. What is the mean of the ten observations?

::grafico{poligoni="0.7,0 1.3,0 1.3,2 0.7,2;1.7,0 2.3,0 2.3,5 1.7,5;2.7,0 3.3,0 3.3,3 2.7,3" xmin=0 xmax=4 ymin=0 ymax=6 nomi="x,n" titolo="Absolute frequencies"}

- $2$
- $3.3$
- $1.9$
- $2.1$

## Explanation

Each height shows how often the value under that bar occurs. The sum is $2\cdot1+5\cdot2+3\cdot3=21$. With ten observations, the mean is $2.1$.
