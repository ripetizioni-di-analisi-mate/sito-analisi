In a group of $20$ students, $8$ choose an activity. What is the percentage frequency?

- $40$ %
- $41$ %
- $39$ %
- $80$ %

## Explanation

The relative frequency is the ratio of the number choosing the activity to the size of the whole group. To express it as a percentage, multiply by one hundred. The resulting number is between zero and one hundred.

$$
f=\frac{8}{20}
$$

$$
f_\%=100\cdot\frac{8}{20}=40\%
$$
