In two disjoint groups of a survey, categories $A,B,C$ receive $4,3,0$ and $0,3,4$ votes, respectively. What is the mode of the combined groups?

- $A$
- $C$
- $B$
- $A,C$

## Explanation

Combining the groups gives four votes for $A$, six for $B$, and four for $C$. So $B$ has the highest overall count. In the first group $A$ led and in the second $C$ led, but votes for $B$ were spread across both groups. To combine groups we need their counts, since the separate modes do not contain them.
