Among $12$ observations, the values $1$, $2$, and $3$ have frequencies $5$, $3$, and $4$, respectively. What is the relative frequency of the mode?

- $\dfrac1{12}$
- $\dfrac5{12}$
- $\dfrac4{12}$
- $\dfrac35$

## Explanation

The mode is $1$ because it has the largest absolute frequency, $5$. The relative frequency divides that count by the total $12$.
