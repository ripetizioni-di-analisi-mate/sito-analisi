What is the descriptive variance of $6,\,8,\,10$, computed with the number of data values as the divisor?

- $\dfrac{11}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}$

## Explanation

The mean is the middle value here. Subtract the mean from each observation, square the deviations, and average them. This is descriptive variance, so the divisor is the number of observations.

$$
\bar x=8
$$

$$
\sigma^2=\frac{(-2)^2+0^2+2^2}{3}=\frac83
$$

The zero deviation contributes nothing to the numerator, but it counts in the denominator. The observation equal to the mean is part of the series being described.
