Find the median of $16,\,9,\,12,\,10,\,18$.

- $13$
- $12$
- $11$
- $24$

## Explanation

To find the median, first sort the observations. There are an odd number of observations, so take the middle value. Here the third sorted observation leaves two values on either side.

$$
9<10<12<16<18
$$

$$
M_e=12
$$
