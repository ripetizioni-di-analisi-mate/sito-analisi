Seven sorted observations are $2,3,5,6,8,10,13$. Remove the two smallest. What is the median of the remaining values?

- $6$
- $8$
- $5$
- $7$

## Explanation

Five observations remain, so the central position is third in the new list. The new list starts at the old third value, $5$. The new third position is therefore the original fifth, which is $8$. The old median $6$ is still present, but it is no longer in the middle.
