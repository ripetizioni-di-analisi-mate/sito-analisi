For six observations, the sum of the differences $x_i-7$ is $-6$. What is the mean?

- $7$
- $6$
- $8$
- $1$

## Explanation

We subtracted $7$ six times. Adding $42$ back to $-6$ recovers the sum $36$. Dividing by six gives mean $6$. Alternatively, the mean difference from $7$ is $-\dfrac{6}{6}=-1$, so the mean of the observations is one unit below $7$.
