Ten measurements have mean $5$ and descriptive variance $9$. What must follow?

- All observations are outside $[4,6]$.
- At least one observation is outside $[4,6]$.
- The range is $9$.
- The maximum is $14$.

## Explanation

If every measurement were between $4$ and $6$, its distance from mean $5$ would be at most one. Every square would be at most one, so their mean could not be nine. Therefore at least one measurement lies outside the interval. The variance does not fix the maximum or the exact range.
