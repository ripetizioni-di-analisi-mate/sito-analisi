The cumulative counts at $2$, $4$, and $9$ are $3$, $7$, and $10$. What are $Q_1$ and $Q_3$ under the nearest-rank convention?

- $3,\quad8$
- $2,\quad4$
- $4,\quad9$
- $2,\quad9$

## Explanation

The total is ten and the outer quartile ranks are $3$ and $8$. The value $2$ takes up the first three positions, and $4$ goes up to the seventh. At rank eight the block of $9$s begins. The quartiles are therefore $2$ and $9$. The numbers $3$ and $8$ are their positions.
