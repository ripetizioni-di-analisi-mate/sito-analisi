What is the median of $15$, $7$, $13$, and $6$?

- $7$
- $13$
- $\dfrac{41}{4}$
- $10$

## Explanation

After sorting, the middle two values are $7$ and $13$. With four data points, the median is the average of these two values. The result is $10$.
