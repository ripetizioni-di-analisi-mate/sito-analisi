The mean of $6$ values is $9$. A seventh value is added, and the new mean is $10$. What is the new value?

- $10$
- $19$
- $16$
- $7$

## Explanation

The original sum is $54$ and the new sum is $70$. The added value is the difference between the sums, $16$.
