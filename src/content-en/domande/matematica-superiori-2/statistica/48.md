Ten measurements have mean $11$. A group of four measurements with mean $8$ is removed. What is the remaining mean?

- $7.8$
- $11$
- $13$
- $19$

## Explanation

The ten measurements sum to $110$. The removed group contributes $4\cdot8=32$, leaving a total of $78$ for the other six. Dividing by six gives $13$. The remaining mean is higher because the removed group’s mean was below the overall mean.
