There are $4$ ratings equal to $5$ and $9$ ratings equal to $7$. What is the mean?

- $6$
- $\dfrac{83}{9}$
- $\dfrac{83}{13}$
- $7$

## Explanation

Each value contributes as many times as its frequency. Add the products and divide by the number of ratings to obtain $\dfrac{83}{13}$.
