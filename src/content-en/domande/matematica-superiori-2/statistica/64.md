For the four ordered observations $0,2,5,20$, one program takes $Q_1$ and $Q_3$ at ranks $\lceil n/4\rceil$ and $\lceil3n/4\rceil$. Another takes the medians of the lower and upper halves. Which interquartile ranges do the two programs report, in order?

- $5$ and $5$
- $11.5$ and $5$
- $5$ and $11.5$
- $5$ and $12.5$

## Explanation

The first method takes the first and third observations, giving $5-0=5$. The medians of the halves are $1$ and $12.5$, whose difference is $11.5$. With four observations the choice of convention matters.
