A data set has standard deviation $2$. What is the new standard deviation if every value is tripled?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $2$
- $18$
- $6$

## Explanation

The mean triples, so every deviation from the mean also triples. The standard deviation is multiplied by the same factor. The result is $6$.
