The sorted values $1$, $3$, $x$, and $10$ have median $6$, with $3\le x\le10$. What is $x$?

- $6$
- $3$
- $9$
- $10$

## Explanation

The middle values are $3$ and $x$. Their average is $6$, so their sum is $12$. Thus $x=9$, which preserves the required order.
