Find the descriptive standard deviation of the two values $10$ and $14$.

- $3$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Find the mean, then compute the variance using the number of observations as the divisor. The standard deviation is the nonnegative square root of the variance. It has the same units as the original values.

$$
\bar x=12
$$

$$
\sigma^2=\frac{(-2)^2+2^2}{2}=4
$$

$$
\sigma=\sqrt4=2
$$
