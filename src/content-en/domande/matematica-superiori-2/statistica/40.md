Two groups of times are measured in the same unit. Their standard deviations are $2$ and $6$. What is the ratio of the second variance to the first?

- $3$
- $4$
- $9$
- $36$

## Explanation

To obtain the variances, square both standard deviations. The variances are $4$ and $36$, giving ratio $\dfrac{36}{4}=9$. The units are the same in both groups and cancel in the comparison. The ratio three is for the standard deviations before squaring.
