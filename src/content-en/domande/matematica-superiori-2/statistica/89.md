A series of $n$ observations has mean $m$ and sum of squared deviations from the mean $24$. What is the sum of squared deviations from $m+1$?

- $24+1$
- $24-n$
- $24+n$
- $24n$

## Explanation

Expanding $(x-m-1)^2$ gives $(x-m)^2$, the term $-2(x-m)$ and $1$. The deviations $x-m$ sum to zero, while the $n$ ones add $n$.
