A data set has standard deviation $5$. Transform every value $x$ into $3-\dfrac{x}{2}$. What is the new standard deviation?

- $-\dfrac52$
- $5$
- $\dfrac52$
- $10$

## Explanation

The shift by $3$ leaves the deviations from the mean unchanged. The factor $-\dfrac12$ halves the magnitude of each deviation. The standard deviation is therefore multiplied by $\dfrac12$.
