The standard deviations of A and B are $2$ and $4$ in the same unit. To correct the two origins, add $12$ to each value in A and subtract $6$ from each value in B. What is the new ratio of B’s standard deviation to A’s?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $3$
- $0$

## Explanation

Adding a constant within a series moves that series’ mean by the same amount. The corrections cancel in the deviations, separately in group A and in group B. Their standard deviations stay at $2$ and $4$, so the requested ratio is $\dfrac{4}{2}=2$.
