You multiply $x^2+2x+5=0$ by $x-1$ without imposing any restriction. What happens to the real solution set?

- It gains the new solution $1$
- It stays empty
- It gains the new solution $-1$
- It becomes all of $\mathbb R$

## Explanation

The original polynomial is $(x+1)^2+4$, which is always positive. The original equation has no solutions. The product $(x-1)(x^2+2x+5)$ vanishes at $x=1$ because of its first factor, and the second factor gives no further zeroes. The new value does not solve the original equation: at $1$ the polynomial is $8$. Multiplication can be reversed only where the factor is nonzero. On the domain $x\ne1$ the equations remain equivalent.
