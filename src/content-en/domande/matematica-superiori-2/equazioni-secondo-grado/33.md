How far apart are the horizontal coordinates where $y=-2x^2+4x+6$ meets the horizontal axis?

- $-4$
- $8$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The discriminant is $4^2-4(-2)\cdot6=64$. Subtract the roots from the formula and take the absolute difference to get $\dfrac{\sqrt{64}}{|{-2}|}=4$. The absolute value keeps the distance positive when the leading coefficient is negative. The factorisation $-2(x+1)(x-3)$ checks this: the zeroes are $-1$ and $3$, and the distance is $3-(-1)=4$.
