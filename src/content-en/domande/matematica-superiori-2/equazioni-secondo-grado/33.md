A monic quadratic has two roots with sum $10$ and distance $2$. What is the smaller root?

- $8$
- $5$
- $4$
- $3$

## Explanation

The midpoint of the roots is $10/2=5$. Each root is half the total distance, or $1$, from that midpoint. The smaller root is $5-1=4$.
