The parabola $y=(x-3)(x-7)$ meets the $x$-axis at two points. How far apart are they?

- $2$
- $10$
- $4$
- $21$

## Explanation

The x-coordinates of the intersections are the zeros of the product. Both points have y-coordinate zero, so subtract their x-coordinates to find the distance. This gives $4$.
