A rectangle has area $55$ cm², and its longer side is $6$ cm longer than its shorter side. How many centimeters long is the shorter side?

- $-11$
- $6$
- $5$
- $11$

## Explanation

If the shorter side is $x$, then $x(x+6)=55$. The roots are $5$ and $-11$, and only the positive one can represent a length. The result is $5$.
