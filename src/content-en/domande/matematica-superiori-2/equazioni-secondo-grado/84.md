A model gives height $h(t)=-t^2+5t$ in metres after launch from the ground, with $t$ in seconds. When does the object return to the ground?

- $0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $25\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Set $h(t)=0$, or $t(5-t)=0$. The root $t=0$ is the launch. Returning requires the positive value $t=5$. For $0<t<5$, both factors are positive and the height is above zero. The time $2.5$ is halfway between the zeroes and gives the vertex of the parabola. It is the time of maximum height, rather than of the return.
