Solve $\dfrac{x^2-9}{x+3}=0$.

- $\{3\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\{-3\}$
- $\{-3,3\}$

## Explanation

The numerator is zero at $3$ and $-3$. The second value also makes the denominator zero and lies outside the domain.
