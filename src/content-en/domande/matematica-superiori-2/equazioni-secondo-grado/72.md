The graph of $y=\dfrac{x^2-16}{x-4}$ follows a line with one missing point. Which point is missing?

- $(4;8)$
- $(4;0)$
- $(-4;0)$
- $(-4;8)$

## Explanation

The horizontal coordinate $4$ is excluded because it makes the denominator zero. Factoring the numerator as $(x-4)(x+4)$ shows that the line is $y=x+4$ at all other values. The line would have vertical coordinate $8$ at $x=4$, so $(4;8)$ is missing. The point $(-4;0)$ is present because the numerator is zero while the denominator is nonzero.
