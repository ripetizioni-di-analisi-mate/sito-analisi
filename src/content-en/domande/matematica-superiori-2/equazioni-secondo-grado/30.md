The parabola $y=(x-2)(x-5)$ meets the $x$-axis at two points. How far apart are they?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $10$
- $7$
- $3$

## Explanation

The x-coordinates of the intersections are the zeros of the product. Both points have y-coordinate zero, so subtract their x-coordinates to find the distance. This gives $3$.
