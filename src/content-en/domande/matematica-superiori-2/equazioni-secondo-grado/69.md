What is the sum of the roots of $5x^2-55x-6=0$?

- $11$
- $\dfrac{11}{5}$
- $55$
- $-11$

## Explanation

The discriminant is positive because the leading coefficient and the constant term have a negative product. The sum is the negative of the linear coefficient divided by the leading coefficient. This gives $11$.
