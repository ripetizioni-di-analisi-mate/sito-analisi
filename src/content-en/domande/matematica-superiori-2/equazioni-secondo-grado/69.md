The polynomials $P(x)=x^2+ax+6$ and $Q(x)=x^2+bx+6$ share a root $r\ne0$. What follows about their linear coefficients?

- $a=b$
- $a=-b$ necessarily
- $ab=6$
- $a+b=6$

## Explanation

Both expressions vanish at their common root. Subtracting gives $(a-b)r=0$. Since $r\ne0$, we need $a-b=0$, so the two polynomials coincide.
