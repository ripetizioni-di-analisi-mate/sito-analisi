The roots of $2x^2-5x+1=0$ are nonzero. Which equation has their reciprocals as roots?

- $y^2+5y+2=0$
- $2y^2+5y+1=0$
- $y^2-5y+2=0$
- $2y^2-5y+1=0$

## Explanation

Set $x=1/y$. Multiplying $2/y^2-5/y+1=0$ by $y^2$ gives $2-5y+y^2=0$. Its solutions do not include $y=0$.
