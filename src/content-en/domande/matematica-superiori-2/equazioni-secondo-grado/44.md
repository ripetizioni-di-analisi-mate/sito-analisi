What domain condition does the denominator of $\dfrac{x^2-1}{x+1}=0$ impose?

- $x\ne1$
- $x\ne-1$
- $x>0$
- $x\ge-1$

## Explanation

The denominator must be nonzero. Solving $x+1=0$ gives the excluded value. The exclusion remains even if the numerator contains the same factor.
