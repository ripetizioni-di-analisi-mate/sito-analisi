A downward-opening parabola has two zeroes $r_1<r_2$. What sign does the polynomial have between them?

- Strictly negative
- Zero throughout the interval
- Strictly positive
- Negative up to the midpoint and positive afterwards

## Explanation

The factorisation is $a(x-r_1)(x-r_2)$ with $a<0$. For $r_1<x<r_2$, the first linear factor is positive and the second negative. Their product is negative, so multiplying by $a$ makes it positive. The curve lies above the axis between the roots. It is zero at the endpoints, while outside the interval the factors have the same sign and the polynomial is negative.
