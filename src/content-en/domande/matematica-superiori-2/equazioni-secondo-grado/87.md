A printed graph shows the points $(0;2)$, $(1;1)$ and $(2;2)$ on a parabola. Which proposed equation fits all three?

- $y=(x-1)^2+2$
- $y=-(x-1)^2+1$
- $y=(x-1)^2+1$
- $y=(x+1)^2+1$

## Explanation

The inputs $0$ and $2$ give the same height and are symmetric about $1$. At $x=1$ the height must be $1$. The formula $(x-1)^2+1$ gives $2,1,2$ in order.
