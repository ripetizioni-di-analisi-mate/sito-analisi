Which equation has two irrational real roots?

- $x^2-5x+6=0$
- $x^2-4x+4=0$
- $x^2-x-1=0$
- $x^2+x+1=0$

## Explanation

For $x^2-x-1$, the discriminant is $1+4=5$. It is positive and not a perfect square, so the roots $\dfrac{1\pm\sqrt5}{2}$ are irrational. For $x^2-5x+6$, the discriminant is $1$, a square, and the roots are integers. The other two polynomials have discriminants $0$ and $-3$, respectively: one has a double root and the other has no real roots.
