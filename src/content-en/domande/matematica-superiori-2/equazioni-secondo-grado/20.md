For which $k$ are $4$ and $9$ the roots of $x^2-kx+36=0$?

- $36$
- $13$
- $-13$
- $5$

## Explanation

The product of the roots already matches the constant term. By Viète's formulas, their sum must equal $k$. This gives $13$.
