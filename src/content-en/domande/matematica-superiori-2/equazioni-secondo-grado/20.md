Which monic equation has roots with sum $7$ and product $12$?

- $x^2+7x+12=0$
- $x^2-7x+12=0$
- $x^2-12x+7=0$
- $x^2-7x-12=0$

## Explanation

In a monic polynomial the sum of the roots is the negative of the linear coefficient, and the product is the constant. We need $-7$ and $12$. Such roots exist because the discriminant is $49-48=1$. We can also find $3$ and $4$ directly and check that $(x-3)(x-4)=x^2-7x+12$. The minus sign before the sum comes from expanding the factors.
