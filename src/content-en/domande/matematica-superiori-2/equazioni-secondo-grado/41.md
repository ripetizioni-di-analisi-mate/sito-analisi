A parabola meets the horizontal axis at two distinct points. Only the constant term $c$ is changed. Which feature of the graph is certain to remain the same?

- The product of the roots
- The number of real roots
- The horizontal coordinate of the vertex
- The vertical coordinate of the vertex

## Explanation

The vertex has horizontal coordinate $-b/(2a)$, independent of $c$. Changing $c$ shifts the parabola vertically and can change both the zeros and the vertex height.
