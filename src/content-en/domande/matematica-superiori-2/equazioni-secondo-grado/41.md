How many distinct real roots does a quadratic equation with $\Delta=0$ have?

- $0$
- $2$
- $1$
- $\text{Infinitely many}$

## Explanation

The term with the square root of the discriminant vanishes. Both signs in the formula give the same value. There is one distinct root, with multiplicity two.
