What standard form results from $(x-1)(x+3)=2x+1$?

- $x^2-4=0$
- $x^2+2x-4=0$
- $x^2-2=0$
- $x^2+4=0$

## Explanation

The product on the left is $x^2+2x-3$. Subtracting $2x+1$ from both sides cancels the linear terms and leaves $x^2-4=0$.
