For which value of $k$ does $x^2-3x+k=0$ have two positive roots, one twice the other?

- $3$
- $4$
- $\dfrac92$
- $2$

## Explanation

The roots are $t$ and $2t$. Their sum is $3$ by Viète's formulas, so $3t=3$ and $t=1$. Their product is $1\cdot2=2$, giving $k=2$.
