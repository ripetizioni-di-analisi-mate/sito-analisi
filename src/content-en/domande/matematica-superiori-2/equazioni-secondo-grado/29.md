Complete the square to see why $2x^2-8x+11=0$ has no real roots. Which form shows this?

- $2(x-2)^2-3=0$
- $2(x-4)^2+3=0$
- $2(x-2)^2+3=0$
- $2(x-2)^2+11=0$

## Explanation

Expanding $2(x-2)^2$ gives $2x^2-8x+8$. Add $3$ to obtain the constant term $11$. The expression $2(x-2)^2+3$ is at least $3$ and cannot be zero.
