Which form, obtained by completing the square, shows that the equation $2x^2-8x+11=0$ has no real roots?

- $2(x-2)^2-3=0$
- $2(x-4)^2+3=0$
- $2(x-2)^2+3=0$
- $2(x-2)^2+11=0$

## Explanation

Expanding $2(x-2)^2$ gives $2x^2-8x+8$. Therefore $3$ must be added to obtain the constant term $11$. The expression $2(x-2)^2+3$ is at least $3$ and cannot be zero.
