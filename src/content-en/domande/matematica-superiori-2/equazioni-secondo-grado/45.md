What is the discriminant of $3x^2+2x-1=0$?

- $-8$
- $4$
- $16$
- $8$

## Explanation

Use $b^2-4ac$ with $a=3$, $b=2$, and $c=-1$. The second contribution is positive because it contains two negative signs. The result is $4+12=16$.
