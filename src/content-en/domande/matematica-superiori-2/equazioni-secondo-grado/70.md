Which polynomial has roots $-1$ and $3$ and a coefficient of $-2$ for $x^2$?

- $-2x^2-4x+6$
- $x^2-2x-3$
- $-2x^2+4x+6$
- $-2x^2+4x-6$

## Explanation

The factors corresponding to the roots are $x+1$ and $x-3$. The required coefficient gives the product $-2(x+1)(x-3)$, which expands to $-2x^2+4x+6$.
