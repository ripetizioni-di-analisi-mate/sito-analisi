What can you deduce from a root sum of $0$ and a root product of $-16$?

- The roots are $-8$ and $8$
- The roots are $-4$ and $4$
- One root is zero and the other is $-16$
- No real roots have these values

## Explanation

A zero sum means one number is the opposite of the other. If one is $u$, their product is $u(-u)=-u^2=-16$. Thus $u^2=16$, giving the pair $-4$, $4$. The monic equation $t^2-16=0$ gives the same result. A negative product is possible for opposite roots, while a positive product would be incompatible with a zero sum.
