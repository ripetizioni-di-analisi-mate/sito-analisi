For which $k$ are $6$ and $13$ the roots of $x^2-kx+78=0$?

- $-19$
- $19$
- $7$
- $78$

## Explanation

The product of the roots already matches the constant term. By Viète's formulas, their sum must equal $k$. This gives $19$.
