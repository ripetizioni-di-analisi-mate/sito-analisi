What are the roots of $2x^2+x-4=0$?

- $\dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{33}}4$
- $\dfrac{1\pm\sqrt{33}}4$
- $\dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{33}}2$
- $\dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{17}}4$

## Explanation

The coefficients are $a=2$, $b=1$, and $c=-4$. The discriminant is $1^2-4\cdot2\cdot(-4)=33$. The denominator in the formula is $2a=4$, so the result is $\dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{33}}4$.
