The parabola $y=(x-1)^2-4$ meets the $x$-axis at two points. What is the x-coordinate of their midpoint?

- $1$
- $-1$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

Setting $y=0$ gives $(x-1)^2=4$, so $x=1\pm2$. The two x-coordinates are $-1$ and $3$. Their average is $\dfrac{-1+3}{2}=1$.
