What are the solutions of the equation $x^{2} - 49=0$?

- $\{7\}$
- $\{-7\}$
- $\{-7;\,7\}$
- $\{-49;\,49\}$

## Explanation

The square of the unknown is isolated. The right side is positive, so there are two opposite roots. Both are solutions because their squares are equal.

$$
x^2=49
$$

$$
x=\pm\sqrt{49}=\pm7
$$
