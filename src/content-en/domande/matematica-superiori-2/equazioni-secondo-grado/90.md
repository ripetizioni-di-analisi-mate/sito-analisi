In which interval does the positive root of $x^2+2x-5=0$ lie?

- $(0,1)$
- $(2,3)$
- $(1,2)$
- $(3,4)$

## Explanation

Completing the square gives $(x+1)^2=6$. The positive root is $-1+\sqrt6$. Since $4<6<9$, it follows that $2<\sqrt6<3$. Subtracting one gives $1<-1+\sqrt6<2$.
