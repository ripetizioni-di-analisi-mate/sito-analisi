To approximate the positive root of $x^2-2=0$, we know it lies between $1$ and $2$. Trying the midpoint $3/2$ gives $(3/2)^2-2>0$. Which interval should be retained?

- $(2,5/2)$
- $(1/2,1)$
- $(1,3/2)$
- $(3/2,2)$

## Explanation

For $x>0$ the square increases. The value of $x^2-2$ is negative at $1$ and positive at $3/2$. The crossing of zero lies in the left half of the interval.
