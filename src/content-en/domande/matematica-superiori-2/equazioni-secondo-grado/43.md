Which root would be lost by dividing $x^2-5x=0$ by $x$?

- $0$
- $5$
- $-5$
- $1$

## Explanation

Zero satisfies the original equation, but division by $x$ would exclude it. Factoring as $x(x-5)=0$ preserves both roots. The solutions are zero and five.
