The two real roots are $0$ and a positive number. If the leading coefficient is negative, what signs must the linear coefficient $b$ and constant term $c$ have?

- $b>0,\ c=0$
- $b<0,\ c=0$
- $b<0,\ c<0$
- $b=0,\ c>0$

## Explanation

Substituting $x=0$ into the equation forces $c=0$. The other root is $-b/a$. With $a<0$ this root is positive precisely when $b>0$.
