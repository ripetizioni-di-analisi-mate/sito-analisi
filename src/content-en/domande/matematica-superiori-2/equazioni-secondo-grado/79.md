For which value of $k$ does the equation $x^2+12x+k=0$ have exactly one distinct real solution?

- $-36$
- $12$
- $6$
- $36$

## Explanation

One distinct root corresponds to a zero discriminant. Set $144-4k$ equal to zero to obtain $36$.
