For which $k$ does $x^2+kx+4=0$ have a negative double root?

- $-4$
- $0$
- $16$
- $4$

## Explanation

The discriminant $k^2-16$ is zero for $k=4$ and $k=-4$. The double root is $-\dfrac k2$, which is negative only in the first case. Indeed, $k=4$ gives $(x+2)^2$, and $k=-4$ gives $(x-2)^2$. A zero discriminant guarantees contact with the axis, but the required sign depends on the horizontal position of the vertex.
