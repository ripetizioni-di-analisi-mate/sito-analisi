For which $k$ are $2$ and $5$ the roots of $x^2-kx+10=0$?

- $3$
- $10$
- $-7$
- $7$

## Explanation

The product of the roots already matches the constant term. By Viète's formulas, their sum must equal $k$. This gives $7$.
