What is the sum of the roots of $2x^2-7x+3=0$?

- $-\dfrac72$
- $\dfrac32$
- $7$
- $\dfrac72$

## Explanation

Viète's formula gives $x_1+x_2=-\dfrac ba$. Here $a=2$ and $b=-7$, so the sum is seven halves. The sign of the linear coefficient is reversed.
