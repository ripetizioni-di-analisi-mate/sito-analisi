For which real parameters $k$ does $x^2+k^2=0$ have at least one real solution?

- $k=0$
- $k<0$
- $k\le0$
- $k\in\mathbb R$

## Explanation

Both terms are squares, so both are nonnegative. Their sum can be zero only if both terms are zero. Thus we need $k=0$ and $x=0$. Choosing a negative parameter does not help: its square is still positive. For example, at $k=-2$ the second term is $4$ and the equation has no real solutions.
