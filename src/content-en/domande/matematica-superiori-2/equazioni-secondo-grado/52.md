Solve $x^2+7=0$ over $\mathbb{R}$.

- $\varnothing$
- $\{0\}$
- $\{\sqrt{7}\}$
- $\{- \sqrt{7},\sqrt{7}\}$

## Explanation

The square of every real number is nonnegative. Adding $7>0$ always gives a positive value.
