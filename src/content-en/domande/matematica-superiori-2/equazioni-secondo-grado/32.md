For which $k\in\mathbb R$ does $(k-1)x^2+4x+1=0$ have exactly one real solution?

- $k=5$
- $k=1$ or $k=5$
- $k=1$
- $k=4$ or $k=5$

## Explanation

For $k=1$, the equation becomes $4x+1=0$ and has one solution. For $k\ne1$, the discriminant is $20-4k$, so the double root occurs at $k=5$.
