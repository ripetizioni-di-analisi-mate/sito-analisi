For $x^2+2x+4=0$, which observation about the vertex lets you count the roots without using the quadratic formula?

- The maximum is $3$, so there are two zeros
- The vertex is at $x=-1$, so $-1$ is a root
- The minimum is $3$, so the horizontal axis is never reached
- The vertical intercept is $4$, so $4$ is a root

## Explanation

Rewrite $x^2+2x+4=(x+1)^2+3$. The square is at least zero, so the trinomial reaches its minimum of $3$ at $x=-1$. There are no real roots.
