For $6x^2-5x-1=0$ you obtain $\Delta=49$. Which expression applies the denominator to the entire numerator in the quadratic formula?

- $5\pm\dfrac7{12}$
- $\dfrac5{12}\pm7$
- $\dfrac{5\pm49}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5\pm7}{12}$

## Explanation

The entire numerator is $-b\pm\sqrt{\Delta}=5\pm7$, all divided by $2a=12$. The roots are therefore $1$ and $-1/6$.
