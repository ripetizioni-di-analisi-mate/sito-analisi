Let $r,s$ be the roots of $3x^2-2x-1=0$. Which equation has roots $-r,-s$?

- $3x^2+2x-1=0$
- $3x^2-2x+1=0$
- $3x^2+x-2=0$
- $-3x^2+2x+1=0$

## Explanation

To obtain the opposite roots, substitute $-x$ for $x$. The quadratic and constant terms stay the same, while the linear term changes sign.
