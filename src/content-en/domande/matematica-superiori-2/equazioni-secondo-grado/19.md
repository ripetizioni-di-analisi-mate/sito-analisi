For which $k$ are $3$ and $7$ the roots of $x^2-kx+21=0$?

- $10$
- $21$
- $-10$
- $4$

## Explanation

The product of the roots already matches the constant term. By Viète's formulas, their sum must equal $k$. This gives $10$.
