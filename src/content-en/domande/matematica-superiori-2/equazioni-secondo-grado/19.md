The roots of $3x^2+bx+c=0$ are $-1$ and $4$. What is the value of $b$?

- $-9$
- $-3$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

The sum of the roots is $-1+4=3$. Viète’s relation makes this sum $-\dfrac b3$, so $b=-9$. The leading coefficient matters: using just the negative of the sum would give the coefficient of the monic polynomial. Expanding $3(x+1)(x-4)=3x^2-9x-12$ checks the required coefficient and the constant, which is three times the product of the roots.
