One root of $-3x^2+6x=0$ is $0$. Which of Viète’s relations finds the other root without division by zero?

- The product, which equals $2$
- The sum, which equals $2$
- The sum, which equals $-2$
- The product, which determines both roots because it equals $0$

## Explanation

The sum is $-\dfrac6{-3}=2$. With one root zero, the other must equal the whole sum, namely $2$. The product is zero regardless of the value of the other root, so it cannot determine that root. Factoring as $-3x(x-2)$ checks the two zeroes. The minus sign before $\dfrac ba$ matters even with a negative leading coefficient.
