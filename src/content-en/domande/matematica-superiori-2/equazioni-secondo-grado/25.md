Which solution of the equation $x^2=20$ belongs to the interval $[-5,0]$?

- $\sqrt{20}$
- $0$
- $-\sqrt{20}$
- $-10$

## Explanation

The solutions of the equation are $x=\sqrt{20}$ and $x=-\sqrt{20}$. The interval $[-5,0]$ contains only numbers less than or equal to zero, so $\sqrt{20}$ does not belong to it. Since $16<20<25$, we have $4<\sqrt{20}<5$, hence $-5<-\sqrt{20}<-4$ and the solution $-\sqrt{20}$ belongs to the interval. The number $0$ belongs to the interval but is not a solution, because $0^2=0$ is different from $20$.
