Solve $3x^2-18=0$ over $\mathbb R$.

- $\{\sqrt6\}$
- $\{-6;6\}$
- $\{-\sqrt6;\sqrt6\}$
- $\{-\sqrt{18};\sqrt{18}\}$

## Explanation

Dividing by $3$ gives $x^2=6$. Since $\sqrt{x^2}=|x|$, the two values are at distance $\sqrt6$ from zero. The roots are $-\sqrt6$ and $\sqrt6$.
