Which of the following equations has $0$ as its only real solution?

- $6x^2-42x=0$
- $6x^2-42=0$
- $6x^2=0$
- $6x^2+42=0$

## Explanation

The equation $6x^2=0$ gives $x^2=0$ and has only the zero root. Factoring $6x^2-42x=0$ also gives $x=7$. The other two polynomials have nonzero constant terms, so $0$ is not a root of either.
