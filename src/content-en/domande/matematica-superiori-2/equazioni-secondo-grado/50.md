Two parabolas with the same axis are $y=(x-2)^2$ and $y=(x-2)^2+1$. At how many points do they meet?

- $2$
- $1$
- $0$
- Infinitely many

## Explanation

At any given horizontal coordinate the second height exceeds the first by $1$. Equating them and subtracting $(x-2)^2$ leaves the impossible equality $0=1$.
