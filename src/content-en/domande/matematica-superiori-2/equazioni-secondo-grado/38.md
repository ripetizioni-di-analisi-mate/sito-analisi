The parabola $y=(x-5)(x-11)$ meets the $x$-axis at two points. How far apart are they?

- $16$
- $3$
- $55$
- $6$

## Explanation

The x-coordinates of the intersections are the zeros of the product. Both points have y-coordinate zero, so subtract their x-coordinates to find the distance. This gives $6$.
