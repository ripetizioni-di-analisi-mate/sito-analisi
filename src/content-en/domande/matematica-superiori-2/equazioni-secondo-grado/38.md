Polynomial $f$ has zeroes $-1$ and $4$. What are the zeroes of $g(x)=f(x-2)$?

- $-3$ and $2$
- $-1$ and $4$
- $-2$ and $8$
- $1$ and $6$

## Explanation

The new polynomial vanishes when its argument $x-2$ equals one of the old horizontal coordinates. The equation $x-2=-1$ gives $x=1$, and $x-2=4$ gives $x=6$. For instance, $g(6)=f(4)=0$. The graph moved two units to the right. The zeroes are still five units apart because the same number was added to both coordinates.
