A rectangle has perimeter $26\,\mathrm{cm}$ and area $40\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. How long is the shorter side?

- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The half-perimeter is $13$, the sum of adjacent sides. The area gives their product, $40$. Solve $t^2-13t+40=(t-5)(t-8)=0$. Both values are positive and are needed for the same rectangle: they are its two side lengths. Choose $5$ because it is smaller. The pair $4$ and $10$ would have the right product but sum to $14$.
