A rectangle has area $21$ cm², and its longer side is $4$ cm longer than its shorter side. How many centimeters long is the shorter side?

- $3$
- $4$
- $-7$
- $7$

## Explanation

If the shorter side is $x$, then $x(x+4)=21$. The roots are $3$ and $-7$, and only the positive one can represent a length. The result is $3$.
