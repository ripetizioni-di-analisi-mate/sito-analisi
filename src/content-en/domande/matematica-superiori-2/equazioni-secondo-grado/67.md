What is the sum of the **squares** of the roots of $2x^2-8x-6=0$?

- $16$
- $10$
- $22$
- $4$

## Explanation

The sum of the roots is $4$ and their product is $-3$. Subtract twice the product from the square of their sum: $x_1^2+x_2^2=4^2-2(-3)=22$. The value $16$ is the square of the sum, which includes the cross term. The roots are indeed real, since the discriminant is $64+48>0$. There is no need to calculate each root.
