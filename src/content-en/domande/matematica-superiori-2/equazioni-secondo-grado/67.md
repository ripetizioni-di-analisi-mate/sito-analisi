Two real roots always sum to $6$, but they may move closer together or farther apart. What is the minimum sum of their squares, allowing a double root?

- $9$
- $36$
- $18$
- $12$

## Explanation

Write the roots as $3-d$ and $3+d$. Then $(3-d)^2+(3+d)^2=18+2d^2$. The minimum $18$ occurs at $d=0$, when the two roots coincide at $3$.
