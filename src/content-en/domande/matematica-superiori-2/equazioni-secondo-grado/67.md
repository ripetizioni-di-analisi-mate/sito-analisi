What is the sum of the roots of $3x^2-21x-4=0$?

- $21$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $7$
- $-7$

## Explanation

The discriminant is positive because the leading coefficient and the constant term have a negative product. The sum is the negative of the linear coefficient divided by the leading coefficient. This gives $7$.
