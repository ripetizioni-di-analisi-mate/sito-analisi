A water jet follows the model $h(t)=4t-t^2$. A camera records two different times when $h=3$. How much time elapses between the two shots?

- $4$ time units
- $1$ time unit
- $3$ time units
- $2$ time units

## Explanation

Setting $4t-t^2=3$ gives $(t-1)(t-3)=0$. The recording times are $1$ and $3$. The elapsed time is their difference, $3-1=2$.
