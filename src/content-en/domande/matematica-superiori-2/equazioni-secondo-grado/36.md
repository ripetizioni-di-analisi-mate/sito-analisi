The polynomial $ax^2+bx+12$ has a double root at $x=2$. What are $a$ and $b$?

- $a=1,\quad b=-4$
- $a=3,\quad b=-12$
- $a=3,\quad b=12$
- $a=12,\quad b=-48$

## Explanation

With double root $2$, the polynomial is $a(x-2)^2$. Its constant is $4a$, so $4a=12$ gives $a=3$. The linear term in the expansion is $-4ax$, giving $b=-12$. Substituting $2$ into $3x^2-12x+12$ gives zero. The squared form also guarantees multiplicity two and fixes the sign of the linear coefficient.
