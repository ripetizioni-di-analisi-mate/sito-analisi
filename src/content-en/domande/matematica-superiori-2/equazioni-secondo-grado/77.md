For which $k$ does $-2x^2+6x+k=0$ have two distinct positive roots?

- $-\dfrac92<k<0$
- $0<k<\dfrac92$
- $k>-\dfrac92$
- $-\dfrac92\le k\le0$

## Explanation

The discriminant $36+8k$ is positive for $k>-\dfrac92$. The product $-\dfrac k2$ must be positive, so $k<0$. With those conditions the roots are real, distinct, and have the same sign. Their sum is $3$, so they are positive. Both endpoints are excluded: at $k=-\dfrac92$ the roots coincide, while at $k=0$ one root is zero.
