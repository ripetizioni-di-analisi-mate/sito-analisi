For which value of $k$ does the equation $x^2+8x+k=0$ have exactly one distinct real solution?

- $16$
- $8$
- $4$
- $-16$

## Explanation

One distinct root corresponds to a zero discriminant. Set $64-4k$ equal to zero to obtain $16$.
