The proposed roots of $2x^2-5x+3=0$ are $2$ and $\dfrac12$. Which check reveals a mistake?

- The sum is $\dfrac52$, but it should be $5$
- Both roots are positive, but their product should be negative
- A fractional root is impossible with integer coefficients
- The proposed product is $1$, but it should be $\dfrac32$

## Explanation

The proposed sum $2+\dfrac12=\dfrac52$ matches the required sum. The product $2\cdot\dfrac12=1$ differs from $\dfrac32$. This discrepancy is enough to reject the pair. The correct factorisation is $(x-1)(2x-3)$, with roots $1$ and $\dfrac32$. Checking only one relation is insufficient when both roots need verification.
