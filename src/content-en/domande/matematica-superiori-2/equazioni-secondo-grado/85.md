A square with side $4$ cm is surrounded by a frame of uniform width $w$. If the frame alone covers $20$ cm², which equation models the problem?

::grafico{linee="0,0 6,0 6,6 0,6 0,0;1,1 5,1 5,5 1,5 1,1" assi=0 uguale=1 xmin=-1 xmax=7 ymin=-1 ymax=7 etichette="3,0.55,w;3,1.45,4" titolo="The frame surrounds the inner square"}

- $(4+w)^2=20,\ w>0$
- $4w^2=20,\ w>0$
- $(4+2w)^2=20,\ w>0$
- $(4+2w)^2-16=20,\ w>0$

## Explanation

The outer side increases by $w$ on both sides of the square. Subtract the inner area $16$ from the outer area $(4+2w)^2$. The positive width given by the equation is $w=1$ cm.
