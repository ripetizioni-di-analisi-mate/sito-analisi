A rectangle has area $45\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ and perimeter $28\,\mathrm{cm}$. What are its side lengths?

- $3$ and $15$ cm
- $4$ and $10$ cm
- $6$ and $8$ cm
- $5$ and $9$ cm

## Explanation

The side lengths have sum $14$ and product $45$. They are therefore the roots of $t^2-14t+45=0$. The factorization $(t-5)(t-9)$ gives $5$ and $9$ centimeters.
