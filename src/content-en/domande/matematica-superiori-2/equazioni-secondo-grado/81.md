A rectangle has area $10$ cm², and its longer side is $3$ cm longer than its shorter side. How many centimetres long is the shorter side?

- $2$
- $3$
- $-5$
- $5$

## Explanation

If the shorter side is $x$, then $x(x+3)=10$. The roots are $2$ and $-5$, and only the positive one can represent a length. The result is $2$.
