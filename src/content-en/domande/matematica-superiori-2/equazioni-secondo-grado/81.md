At a meeting each participant shakes hands once with every other participant. There are $21$ handshakes. How many participants were there?

- $7$
- $21$
- $6$
- $14$

## Explanation

With $n$ people the individual counts add to $n(n-1)$, counting every handshake twice. Thus $n(n-1)/2=21$. The roots are $7$ and $-6$, and only $7$ can count people.
