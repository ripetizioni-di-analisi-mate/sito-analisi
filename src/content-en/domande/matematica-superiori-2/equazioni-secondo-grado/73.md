Solve $\dfrac{x^2-16}{x+4}=0$.

- $\{-4,4\}$
- $\{4\}$
- $\{-4\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The numerator is zero at $4$ and $-4$. The second value also makes the denominator zero and lies outside the domain.
