Using the quadratic formula, what solutions are obtained for the equation $x^{2} - 16 x + 63=0$?

- $\{7;\,9\}$
- $\{-9;\,-7\}$
- $\{7\}$
- $\{7;\,10\}$

## Explanation

The coefficients are read from the standard form. The discriminant is positive, so there are two distinct real roots. The two signs in the formula give the two solutions.

$$
\Delta=(-16)^2-4\cdot63=4
$$

$$
x=\frac{16\pm\sqrt{4}}2
$$

$$
x_1=7,\qquad x_2=9
$$
