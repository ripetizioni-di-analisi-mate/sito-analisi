Which interval contains the positive root of $4x^2-7=0$?

- $(0,1)$
- $(\tfrac32,2)$
- $(1,\tfrac32)$
- $(2,3)$

## Explanation

The square of the positive root is $\dfrac74$. The square of $1$ is $1$, and the square of $\dfrac32$ is $\dfrac94$, so $1<\dfrac74<\dfrac94$. On the positive half of the line, larger numbers have larger squares. Thus $\dfrac{\sqrt7}{2}$ is between $1$ and $\dfrac32$. Comparing squares avoids a decimal approximation of the root.
