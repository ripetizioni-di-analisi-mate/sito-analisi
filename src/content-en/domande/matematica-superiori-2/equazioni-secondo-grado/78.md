For which value of $k$ does the equation $x^2+10x+k=0$ have exactly one distinct real solution?

- $10$
- $5$
- $-25$
- $25$

## Explanation

One distinct root corresponds to a zero discriminant. Set $100-4k$ equal to zero to obtain $25$.
