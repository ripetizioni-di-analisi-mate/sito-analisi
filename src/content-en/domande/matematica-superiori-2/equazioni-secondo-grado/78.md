For which $k$ does $kx^2-4x+4=0$ have exactly one distinct real solution?

- Only $k=1$
- Only $k=0$
- $k=1$ or $k=-1$
- $k=0$ or $k=1$

## Explanation

At $k=0$ the degree drops to one. This leaves $-4x+4=0$, with solution $1$. At other values it remains a quadratic. Its discriminant $16-16k$ vanishes at $k=1$, giving $(x-2)^2=0$ with double root $2$. Both parameters give one distinct solution, but only the second gives a quadratic with a double root.
