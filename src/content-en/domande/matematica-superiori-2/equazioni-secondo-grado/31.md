A rectangle has perimeter $24\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which listed area is impossible?

- $40\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $36\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $35\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $32\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The adjacent sides have sum $12$. A product $A$ is possible only if $t^2-12t+A=0$ has real roots. Its discriminant is $144-4A$, which is negative for $A>36$. Thus area $40$ is impossible. The bound $36$ is the square of side $6$. The other two areas come from sides $5$ and $7$, or $4$ and $8$, respectively.
