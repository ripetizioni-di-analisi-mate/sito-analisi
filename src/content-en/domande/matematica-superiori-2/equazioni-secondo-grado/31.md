A rectangle has area $130\,\mathrm{cm}^2$, and its base is $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ longer than its height. What is its height?

- $10$ cm
- $11$ cm
- $9$ cm
- $20$ cm

## Explanation

Let $h$ be the height and $h+3$ the base. Their product is the area and gives a quadratic equation. The negative root is excluded because a length is positive.

$$
h(h+3)=130
$$

$$
(h-10)(h+13)=0
$$

$$
h=10\quad(h>0)
$$
