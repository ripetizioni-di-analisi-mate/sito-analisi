Solve $x^{2} - 14 x + 49=0$.

- $\{-7\}$
- $\{7\}$
- $\{-7;\,7\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The discriminant is zero, so the two roots given by the formula coincide. The trinomial is the square of a binomial. The solution set contains one distinct value.

$$
\Delta=196-4\cdot49=0
$$

$$
(x-7)^2=0\iff x=7
$$
