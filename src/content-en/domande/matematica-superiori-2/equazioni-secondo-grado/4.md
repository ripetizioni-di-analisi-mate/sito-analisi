The parabola $y=x^2-4x+4$ touches the horizontal axis at $T$. Which pair gives the coordinates of this point of tangency?

::grafico{f="(x-2)^2" xmin=-0.6 xmax=4.6 ymin=-0.9 ymax=4.7 uguale=1 titolo="The graph of the given quadratic"}

- $(2,4)$
- $(2,0)$
- $(0,2)$
- $(-2,0)$

## Explanation

The trinomial is $(x-2)^2$. Its minimum is zero when $x=2$. The vertex is therefore $(2,0)$, and no other point of the axis lies on the parabola.
