Zero is an obvious root of $x^2-2x=0$. After the equation is written as $x(x-2)=0$, which rule identifies the other root too?

- A product is zero only if both factors are zero
- The sum of the factors must equal their product
- Opposite factors always have product zero
- A product is zero if at least one factor is zero

## Explanation

The two cases are $x=0$ or $x-2=0$. The second factor gives the other root $2$. The two factors do not have to vanish at the same point.
