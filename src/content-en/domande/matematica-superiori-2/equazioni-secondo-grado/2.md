Solve $x^{2} - 7 x=0$.

- $\{7\}$
- $\{0\}$
- $\{-7;\,0\}$
- $\{0;\,7\}$

## Explanation

Factor out $x$. A product is zero when at least one factor is zero. Factoring preserves the zero root, which dividing by $x$ would lose.

$$
x(x-7)=0
$$

$$
x=0\quad\text{or}\quad x=7
$$
