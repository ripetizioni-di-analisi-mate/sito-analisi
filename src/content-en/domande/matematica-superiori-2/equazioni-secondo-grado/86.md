The two solutions of a quadratic equation, rounded to two decimal places, are both equal to $1.41$. Can one conclude that the equation has a double solution?

- No, because two distinct solutions can have the same rounding to two decimal places
- Yes, because the two rounded values are positive
- No, because a double solution is always an integer
- Yes, because two numbers with the same rounding to two decimal places are equal

## Explanation

The numbers $1.411$ and $1.412$ are distinct, yet rounded to two decimal places they are both equal to $1.41$. Therefore equal rounded values do not guarantee that the two solutions coincide. A quadratic equation has a double solution if and only if its discriminant is zero, so the question is settled by computing $\Delta$ with the exact coefficients.
