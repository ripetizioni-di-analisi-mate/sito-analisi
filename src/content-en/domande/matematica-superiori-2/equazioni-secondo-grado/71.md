For $x\ne0$, consider $x-\dfrac{2}{x}=1$. Given that $x=2$ is a solution, which other value satisfies the original equation?

- $1$
- $0$
- $-1$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Multiplying by $x$ gives $x^2-x-2=(x-2)(x+1)=0$. The other candidate is $-1$, which lies in the domain and gives $-1-2/(-1)=1$.
