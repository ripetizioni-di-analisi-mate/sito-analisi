Solve $\dfrac{x^2-4}{x+2}=0$.

- $\varnothing$
- $\{-2\}$
- $\{2\}$
- $\{-2,2\}$

## Explanation

The numerator is zero at $2$ and $-2$. The second value also makes the denominator zero and lies outside the domain.
