Solve $x^{2} - 64=0$.

- $\{8\}$
- $\{-8\}$
- $\{-8;\,8\}$
- $\{-64;\,64\}$

## Explanation

Isolate the square of the unknown. The right side is positive, so there are two opposite roots. Both are solutions because their squares are equal.

$$
x^2=64
$$

$$
x=\pm\sqrt{64}=\pm8
$$
