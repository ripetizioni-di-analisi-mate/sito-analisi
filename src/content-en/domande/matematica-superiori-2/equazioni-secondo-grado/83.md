A rectangle has area $36$ cm², and its longer side is $5$ cm longer than its shorter side. How many centimeters long is the shorter side?

- $9$
- $4$
- $-9$
- $5$

## Explanation

If the shorter side is $x$, then $x(x+5)=36$. The roots are $4$ and $-9$, and only the positive one can represent a length. The result is $4$.
