A rectangle has width three times its height and area $48\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. What must its perimeter be?

- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $32\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $28\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

With height $h>0$, the width is $3h$. The area gives $3h^2=48$, so $h^2=16$ and $h=4$. The width is $12$, and the perimeter is $2(4+12)=32$ centimetres. The value $16$ counts just one of each side. To check both constraints, use $12=3\cdot4$ and $12\cdot4=48$, with both lengths positive.
