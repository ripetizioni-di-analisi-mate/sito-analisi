Jo divides $5x^2+15x=0$ by $x$ and finds $x=-3$. What is needed to complete the solution?

- Add $x=3$ by symmetry
- Discard $x=-3$ because it is negative
- List $-3$ as a double root
- Check $x=0$, which is a second root

## Explanation

Division by $x$ is possible only when $x\ne0$. Check the excluded case: at $x=0$ both original terms vanish. Factoring as $5x(x+3)=0$ handles the two cases together. The distinct roots are $0$ and $-3$. The linear term means the polynomial is not symmetric about the vertical axis.
