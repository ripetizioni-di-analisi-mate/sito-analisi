Where is the graph of $y=-4(x-2)^2-7$ relative to the horizontal axis?

- Always above, with minimum vertical coordinate $7$
- It touches the axis at $x=2$
- Always below, with maximum vertical coordinate $-7$
- It crosses the axis twice

## Explanation

Since $(x-2)^2\ge0$, multiplying by $-4$ gives a value at most zero. Subtracting $7$ gives $y\le-7$, with equality at $x=2$. That point is the maximum of the parabola and still lies below the axis. Elsewhere the square is strictly positive and the vertical coordinate is smaller. The curve never meets the horizontal axis.
