The roots of $2x^2+bx+c=0$ are $4$ and $-2$. Which pair $(b,c)$ completes the equation?

- $(-4,-16)$
- $(-2,-8)$
- $(4,-16)$
- $(-4,16)$

## Explanation

The sum is $4+(-2)=2$ and the product is $4\cdot(-2)=-8$. Thus $-\dfrac b2=2$ and $\dfrac c2=-8$, giving $b=-4$ and $c=-16$. The pair $(-2,-8)$ would fit the monic equation and ignore the prescribed coefficient. Expanding $2(x-4)(x+2)$ confirms both required coefficients and retains the prescribed roots. The product is negative because exactly one of the roots is negative.
