You have found two distinct roots, $2$ and $-1$. Which additional information determines a **unique** quadratic polynomial with these roots?

- The value of the polynomial at $x=0$
- The position of the axis of symmetry
- The distance between the roots
- The sum of the roots

## Explanation

Every possible polynomial has the form $a(x-2)(x+1)$ with $a\ne0$. Its value at $0$ is $-2a$, which fixes $a$. The other data depend only on the roots that are already known.
