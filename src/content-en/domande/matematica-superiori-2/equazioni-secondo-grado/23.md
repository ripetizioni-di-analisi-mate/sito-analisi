For which $k$ are $5$ and $11$ the roots of $x^2-kx+55=0$?

- $16$
- $55$
- $-16$
- $6$

## Explanation

The product of the roots already matches the constant term. By Viète's formulas, their sum must equal $k$. This gives $16$.
