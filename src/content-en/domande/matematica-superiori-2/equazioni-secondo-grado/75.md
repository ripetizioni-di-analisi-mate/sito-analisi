What is the solution set of $\dfrac{x^2-4}{x-2}=x+2$?

- $\mathbb R$
- $\{2\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{2\}$

## Explanation

Exclude $x=2$ because it makes the denominator zero. For every other value, the numerator is $(x-2)(x+2)$ and the factor $x-2$ can be cancelled. The equation becomes $x+2=x+2$ on the original domain.
