A student applies Viète's formula to the numerator of $\dfrac{x^2-8x+12}{x-2}=0$ and says the sum of the solutions is $8$. Which sum should be recorded after checking the domain?

- $12$
- $2$
- $8$
- $6$

## Explanation

The numerator has roots $2$ and $6$ and their sum is indeed $8$. However, $x=2$ makes the denominator zero. The fractional equation has just the solution $6$, so its solution sum is $6$.
