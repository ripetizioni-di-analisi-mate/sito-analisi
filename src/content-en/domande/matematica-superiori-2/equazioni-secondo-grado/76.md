For which value of $k$ does the equation $x^2+6x+k=0$ have exactly one distinct real solution?

- $6$
- $-9$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

One distinct root corresponds to a zero discriminant. Set $36-4k$ equal to zero to obtain $9$.
