For the equation $x^2-6x+k=0$, $k$ increases from $8$ to $10$. What happens to the initial two intersections with the horizontal axis during this change?

- They stay fixed and only the vertex moves
- There are still two at $k=10$
- They move steadily apart
- They approach, coincide at $k=9$, then disappear

## Explanation

The form $(x-3)^2=9-k$ gives the positions $3\pm\sqrt{9-k}$ for $k\le9$. The distance between them shrinks to zero. For $k>9$, the square would have to be negative.
