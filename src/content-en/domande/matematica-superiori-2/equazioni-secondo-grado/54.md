Solve $x^2+11=0$ over $\mathbb{R}$.

- $\{- \sqrt{11},\sqrt{11}\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\{0\}$
- $\{\sqrt{11}\}$

## Explanation

The square of every real number is nonnegative. Adding $11>0$ always gives a positive value.
