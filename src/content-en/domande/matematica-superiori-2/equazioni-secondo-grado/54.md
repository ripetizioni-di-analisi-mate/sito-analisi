By how many units must $y=x^2-4x+7$ be lowered for the parabola to touch the horizontal axis?

- $4$
- $3$
- $7$
- $12$

## Explanation

The completed square is $(x-2)^2+3$, so the vertex has vertical coordinate $3$. Lowering the graph by three units subtracts $3$ from every height and leaves $(x-2)^2$. The vertex lands on the axis, giving a double root. A smaller shift leaves the minimum positive. A larger shift puts the vertex below the axis and gives two intersections.
