A model is $k(x^2-1)=0$. A reliable measurement shows that $x=2$ satisfies the equation. What can you conclude about the other real inputs?

- No other input satisfies it
- They all satisfy the equation
- Only $-2$ satisfies it besides $2$
- Only $1$ and $-1$ are allowed besides $2$

## Explanation

The measurement forces $k(4-1)=0$, so $k=0$. The model then reads $0=0$ for every $x$, although for a nonzero parameter the only roots would be $\pm1$.
