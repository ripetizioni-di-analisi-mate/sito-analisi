For which $k$ does $k(x^2-1)=0$ have exactly two real solutions?

- $k=0$
- $k\ne0$
- $k>0$
- $k\ge0$

## Explanation

For $k\ne0$, dividing by the parameter leaves $x^2=1$, with solutions $-1$ and $1$. A negative $k$ is allowed too: division by a negative number preserves equality and does not change the roots. At $k=0$ we get the identity $0=0$. The degree disappears in that case and every real number is a solution.
