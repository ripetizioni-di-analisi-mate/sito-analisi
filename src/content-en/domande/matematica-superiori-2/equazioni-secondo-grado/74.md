Two equations each set a fraction with numerator $N(x)=x(x-4)$ equal to zero. Which pair of denominators produces **different solution sets**?

- $x-1$ and $x-2$
- $x-4$ and $x+4$
- $1$ and $2$
- $x+1$ and $x+2$

## Explanation

The candidates are $0$ and $4$. The denominator $x-4$ excludes $4$, whereas $x+4$ allows both. The other denominator pairs retain both candidates.
