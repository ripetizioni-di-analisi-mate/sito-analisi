Solve $\dfrac{x^2-25}{x+5}=0$.

- $\varnothing$
- $\{5\}$
- $\{-5,5\}$
- $\{-5\}$

## Explanation

The numerator is zero at $5$ and $-5$. The second value also makes the denominator zero and lies outside the domain.
