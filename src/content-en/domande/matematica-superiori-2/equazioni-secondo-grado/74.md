When solving $\dfrac{x+3}{x-1}=\dfrac4{x(x-1)}$, what solution set remains after checking the denominators?

- $\{1\}$
- $\{-4\}$
- $\{-4,1\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The denominators exclude $0$ and $1$. Multiplying by $x(x-1)$ gives $x^2+3x-4=0$, or $(x+4)(x-1)=0$. Candidate $1$ makes a denominator zero. Candidate $-4$ is allowed and gives $\dfrac15$ on both sides. Clearing denominators preserves equivalence in the domain and does not let us restore a previously excluded value.
