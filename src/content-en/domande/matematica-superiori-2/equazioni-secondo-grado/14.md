The equations $\dfrac{x^2-8x+15}{x-3}=k$ and $x-5=k$ have the same solutions for every value of $k$ except one. Which one?

- $3$
- $5$
- $0$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The numerator factors as $(x-3)(x-5)$. The simplified equation gives $x=k+5$. The solution sets differ only when this is the excluded value $3$, namely when $k=-2$.
