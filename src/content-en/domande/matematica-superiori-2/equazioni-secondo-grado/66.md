What is the sum of the roots of $2x^2-10x-3=0$?

- $-5$
- $10$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

The discriminant is positive because the leading coefficient and the constant term have a negative product. The sum is the negative of the linear coefficient divided by the leading coefficient. This gives $5$.
