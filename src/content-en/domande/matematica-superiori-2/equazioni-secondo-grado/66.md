The roots of $2x^2-6x+1=0$ are $r,s$. Without solving the equation, what is the arithmetic mean of $2r+1$ and $2s+1$?

- $2$
- $7$
- $4$
- $3$

## Explanation

The sum of the roots is $r+s=-b/a=3$. The requested mean is $(2r+1+2s+1)/2=r+s+1=4$.
