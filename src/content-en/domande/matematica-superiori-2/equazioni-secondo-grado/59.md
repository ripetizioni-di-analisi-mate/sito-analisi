A monic quadratic has discriminant $16$ and root sum $6$. What is the constant term?

- $6$
- $5$
- $9$
- $13$

## Explanation

The sum of the roots gives $b=-6$. With leading coefficient one, $\Delta=b^2-4c=36-4c$. Equating this to $16$ gives $4c=20$ and $c=5$. We can also recover the roots: their midpoint is $3$ and they are $4$ apart, so they are $1$ and $5$. Their product confirms the constant and their sum recovers the given data.
