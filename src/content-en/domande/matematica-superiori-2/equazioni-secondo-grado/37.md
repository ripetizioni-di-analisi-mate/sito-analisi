Which polynomial is strictly positive for every real $x$?

- $-x^2-1$
- $x^2-1$
- $2x^2+2x+3$
- $(x-1)^2$

## Explanation

For $2x^2+2x+3$ the discriminant is $4-24=-20$. The parabola never meets the axis and opens upwards, so it stays above it. In $-x^2-1$ the discriminant is also negative, but the leading coefficient is negative and the sign is opposite. The square $(x-1)^2$ does not work because it equals zero at $x=1$, and the question requires strict positivity.
