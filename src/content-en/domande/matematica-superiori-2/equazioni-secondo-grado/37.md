How many real roots does $x^2+2x+12=0$ have?

- $1$
- $-1$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

The discriminant is negative. The square root of a negative number is not real, so the formula gives no real roots. The equation has no solution in the real numbers.

$$
\Delta=2^2-4\cdot12=-44<0
$$
