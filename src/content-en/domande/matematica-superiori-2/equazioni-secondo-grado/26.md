Someone expects two roots after expanding $(x+1)^2=x^2+3$. What kind of equation remains after collecting like terms?

- An identity for every $x$
- An equation with no solution
- A quadratic equation with roots $\pm1$
- A linear equation with solution $1$

## Explanation

The $x^2$ terms cancel. This leaves $2x+1=3$ and hence $x=1$. The degree is determined after simplifying the difference between the two sides.
