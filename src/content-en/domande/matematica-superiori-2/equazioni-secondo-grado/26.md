What are the solutions of $3x^2+2x=0$?

- $\left\{-\dfrac23\right\}$
- $\left\{0;\dfrac23\right\}$
- $\left\{0;-\dfrac32\right\}$
- $\left\{0;-\dfrac23\right\}$

## Explanation

The left side is $x(3x+2)$. The product is zero if $x=0$ or $3x+2=0$, which gives $x=-\dfrac23$.
