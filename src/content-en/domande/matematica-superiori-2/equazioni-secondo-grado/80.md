The positive number $r$ is a root of $x^2-kx-1=0$. Under which condition on $r$ is the parameter $k$ positive?

- $r=1$
- $r>1$
- For every $r>0$
- $0<r<1$

## Explanation

Substituting and solving for the parameter gives $k=(r^2-1)/r$. The denominator is positive, so we need $r^2>1$. For positive $r$ this is equivalent to $r>1$.
