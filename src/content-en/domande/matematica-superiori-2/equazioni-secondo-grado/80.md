For which $k$ does $x^2-4x+k=0$ have two distinct positive roots?

- $0\le k\le4$
- $0<k<4$
- $k<4$
- $k>0$

## Explanation

Two distinct real roots require $\Delta=16-4k>0$, or $k<4$. The sum is $4$ and the product is $k$, so the roots are both positive if their product is strictly positive. This gives $0<k<4$.
