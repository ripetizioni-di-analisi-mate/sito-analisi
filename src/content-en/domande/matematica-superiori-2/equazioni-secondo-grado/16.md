What value of $k$ gives $-2x^2+12x+k=0$ a double root?

- $18$
- $-18$
- $-36$
- $36$

## Explanation

The discriminant is $12^2-4(-2)k=144+8k$. Setting it to zero gives $8k=-144$, hence $k=-18$. At that value the polynomial is $-2(x-3)^2$ and the double root is $3$. The vertex gives the same result: the parabola opens downwards and touches the horizontal axis exactly when its maximum is zero.
