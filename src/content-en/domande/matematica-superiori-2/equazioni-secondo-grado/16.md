We want $x=1$ always to be a root of $x^2+kx+c=0$ as $k$ varies. How should $c$ be chosen?

- $c=k-1$
- $c=-1-k$
- $c=1+k$
- $c=-k$

## Explanation

Substituting the required value gives $1+k+c=0$. Solving for $c$ gives the rule $c=-1-k$, including when the two roots coincide.
