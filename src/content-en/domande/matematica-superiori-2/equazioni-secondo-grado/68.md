What is the sum of the roots of $4x^2-36x-5=0$?

- $9$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $-9$
- $36$

## Explanation

The discriminant is positive because the leading coefficient and the constant term have a negative product. The sum is the negative of the linear coefficient divided by the leading coefficient. This gives $9$.
