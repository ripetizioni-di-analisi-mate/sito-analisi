For which value of $k$ does the equation $x^2-14x+k=0$ have a double root?

- $50$
- $49$
- $48$
- $98$

## Explanation

A double root requires a zero discriminant. Write the discriminant in terms of the parameter and solve the resulting equation. The leading coefficient is one, so the equation remains quadratic.

$$
\Delta=196-4k
$$

$$
\Delta=0\iff k=49
$$
