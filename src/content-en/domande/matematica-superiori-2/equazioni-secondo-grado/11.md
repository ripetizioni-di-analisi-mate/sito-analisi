What coefficients $(a,b,c)$ result when all terms are moved to the left in $(2x-1)(x+3)=x^2+7$?

- $(1,5,-10)$
- $(2,5,-10)$
- $(1,7,-10)$
- $(1,5,4)$

## Explanation

The product on the left is $2x^2+6x-x-3=2x^2+5x-3$. Subtracting the entire right side subtracts both $x^2$ and $7$. This leaves $x^2+5x-10$, with quadratic coefficient $1$. A quick check sets $x=0$: the difference between the original sides is $-3-7=-10$, the constant we found.
