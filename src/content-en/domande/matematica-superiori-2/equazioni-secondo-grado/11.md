When finding the roots of $x^2-8x+15=0$, a classmate writes $x=(-8\pm\sqrt{64-60})/2$. Which part needs correcting?

- The first $-8$ must be $8$
- The $60$ must become $15$
- The $64$ must be $-64$
- The denominator must be $-2$

## Explanation

The formula contains $-b$. Here $b=-8$, so $-b=8$. The discriminant is already correct, and the two roots are $(8\pm2)/2$, or $3$ and $5$.
