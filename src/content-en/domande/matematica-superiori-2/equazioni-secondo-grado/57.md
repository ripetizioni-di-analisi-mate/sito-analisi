A quadratic equation has discriminant $9$. All coefficients are multiplied by $-2$. What is the new discriminant?

- $-18$
- $-36$
- $9$
- $36$

## Explanation

Substituting $-2a$, $-2b$, and $-2c$ into the formula makes the squared term $4b^2$ and the product of the first and last coefficients $4ac$. Thus the new value is $4(b^2-4ac)=36$. The numerical discriminant changes, but its sign and the roots stay the same. The new leading coefficient has twice the magnitude, offsetting the doubled square root in the formula for the distance between roots.
