Which factorisation keeps all the solutions of $(x+2)^2=4(x+2)$?

- $(x+2)(x-2)=0$
- $(x+2)(x+4)=0$
- $x^2+4=0$
- $(x-2)^2=0$

## Explanation

Subtract $4(x+2)$ and factor out the whole group to obtain $(x+2)[(x+2)-4]=0$. The second factor is $x-2$. The roots are $-2$ and $2$: the first makes the shared group zero, and the second makes it $4$. Dividing both sides by $x+2$ would be possible only when that factor is nonzero and would leave the first case unchecked.
