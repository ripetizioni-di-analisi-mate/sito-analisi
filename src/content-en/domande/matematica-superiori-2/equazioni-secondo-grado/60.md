Calculate the discriminant of $2x^2-3x-2=0$.

- $-7$
- $1$
- $9$
- $25$

## Explanation

The constant term is negative, so $-4ac=-4\cdot2\cdot(-2)=16$. The square of the linear coefficient is $(-3)^2=9$. Adding gives $\Delta=25$.
