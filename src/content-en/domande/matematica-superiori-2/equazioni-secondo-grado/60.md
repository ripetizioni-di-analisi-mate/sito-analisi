For a quadratic with integer coefficients and $a\ne0$, which information about its discriminant guarantees two **distinct rational** roots?

- It is even
- It is positive
- It is zero
- It is the square of a nonzero integer

## Explanation

In the quadratic formula the only potentially irrational term is $\sqrt{\Delta}$. If $\Delta$ is a positive integer square, both numerators are different integers and the denominator $2a$ is a nonzero integer.
