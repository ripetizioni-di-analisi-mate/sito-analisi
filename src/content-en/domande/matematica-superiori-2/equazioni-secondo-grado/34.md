A parabola has axis of symmetry $x=2$ and a zero at $x=-3$. What is the other zero?

- $-7$
- $3$
- $5$
- $7$

## Explanation

The point at $-3$ is five units to the left of axis $x=2$. Its reflection is five units to the right, at $2+5=7$. It has the same vertical coordinate, zero. The midpoint $\dfrac{-3+7}{2}=2$ checks the symmetry. The number $5$ measures the distance from the axis; add the position of the axis to obtain the requested coordinate.
