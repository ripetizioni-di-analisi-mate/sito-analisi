The parabola $y=(x-4)(x-9)$ meets the $x$-axis at two points. How far apart are they?

- $36$
- $13$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$

## Explanation

The x-coordinates of the intersections are the zeros of the product. Both points have y-coordinate zero, so subtract their x-coordinates to find the distance. This gives $5$.
