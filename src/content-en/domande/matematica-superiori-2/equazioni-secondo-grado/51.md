Two people propose $n=5$ and $n=-6$ for the number of rows in a mosaic of $30$ tiles, with $n+1$ tiles per row. Which check distinguishes the two proposals?

- Both can count rows
- Both satisfy $n(n+1)=30$, but only $5$ can count rows
- Neither gives a product of $30$
- Only $-6$ satisfies the product equation

## Explanation

The products are $5\cdot6=30$ and $(-6)(-5)=30$. The model requires a positive integer number of rows and tiles per row, leaving $n=5$.
