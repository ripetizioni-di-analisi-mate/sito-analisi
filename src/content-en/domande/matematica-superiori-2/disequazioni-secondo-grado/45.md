Which interval represents $-1<x\le3$?

- $[-1,3]$
- $(-1,3)$
- $[-1,3)$
- $(-1,3]$

## Explanation

The comparison on the left is strict, so negative one is excluded. Equality is allowed on the right, so three is included. Use a parenthesis and a square bracket.
