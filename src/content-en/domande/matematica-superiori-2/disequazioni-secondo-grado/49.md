For which values of $x$ is the trinomial $-x^2+2x+8$ nonnegative?

::grafico{f="-x^2+2*x+8" area="0;-x^2+2*x+8;-2;4" punti="-2,0,pieno;4,0,pieno" xmin=-4 xmax=6 ymin=-8 ymax=10 titolo="The part of the parabola at nonnegative height"}

- $[-2,4]$
- $(-\infty,-2]\cup[4,+\infty)$
- $[-4,2]$
- $(-2,4)$

## Explanation

The question asks to solve $-x^2+2x+8\ge0$. Multiplying both sides by $-1$ reverses the inequality, so it is equivalent to $x^2-2x-8\le0$. The numbers $-2$ and $4$ have sum $2$ and product $-8$, hence $x^2-2x-8=(x+2)(x-4)$ and the roots are $-2$ and $4$. Since the coefficient of $x^2$ in $x^2-2x-8$ is positive, the trinomial is negative between the roots. The inequality allows equality, so the roots are included and the solutions form the interval $[-2,4]$.
