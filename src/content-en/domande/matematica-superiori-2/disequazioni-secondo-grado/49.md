Solve $(x-2)(x+4)\le0$.

::grafico{f="(x-2)*(x+4)" area="0;(x-2)*(x+4);-4;2" punti="-4,0,pieno;2,0,pieno" xmin=-6 xmax=4 ymin=-11 ymax=9 titolo="The arc at nonpositive height"}

- $[-4,2]$
- $[-2,4]$
- $(-4,2)$
- $(-\infty,-4]\cup[2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The roots are $-4$ and $2$. Between them, $x-2$ is negative and $x+4$ is positive. Equality includes both roots of the trinomial, which are the points where the parabola crosses the $x$-axis.
