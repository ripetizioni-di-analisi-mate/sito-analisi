Solve $\dfrac{(x-3)(x+7)}{x-3}>0$.

- $(-7,3)$
- $(-7,\infty)$
- $(3,\infty)$
- $(-7,3)\cup(3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The domain excludes $3$. For all other values, the fraction simplifies to $x+7$, which must be positive.
