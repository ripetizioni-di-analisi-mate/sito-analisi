Compare the area of a square of side $x$ with that of a rectangle with sides $x-1$ and $x+1$, for $x>1$. When does the square have greater area?

- Only if $1<x<2$
- For no $x>1$
- Only if $x>2$
- For every $x>1$

## Explanation

The rectangle has area $(x-1)(x+1)=x^2-1$. Subtracting it from $x^2$ always leaves $1$, regardless of the chosen side.
