Solve $-(x-2)(x+5)>0$.

- $[-5,2]$
- $(-2,5)$
- $(-\infty,-5)\cup(2,+\infty)$
- $(-5,2)$

## Explanation

The minus sign reverses the sign of the product. A negative product is required, which occurs strictly between $-5$ and $2$.
