The difference between two consecutive squares must exceed the smaller square. For which integers $n\ge0$ does $(n+1)^2-n^2>n^2$ hold?

- $\{1,2,3\}$
- $\{0,1\}$
- All nonnegative integers
- $\{0,1,2\}$

## Explanation

The inequality becomes $n^2-2n-1<0$, or $(n-1)^2<2$. For integer $n$ the square can only be $0$ or $1$. This gives $0,1,2$.
