Solve $(x-2)^2(x+1)\le0$.

- $(-\infty,-1]$
- $(-\infty,2]$
- $[-1,2]$
- $(-\infty,-1]\cup\{2\}$

## Explanation

Away from its root $2$, the square is positive and the sign agrees with that of $x+1$. This gives all values up to and including $-1$. Include $2$ as well because it makes the product zero.
