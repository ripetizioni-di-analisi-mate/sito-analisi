Compare the sum and product of the integer solutions of $(x-1)(x-5)<0$. What is their absolute difference?

- $15$
- $9$
- $6$
- $24$

## Explanation

The integer solutions are $2,3,4$. Their sum is $9$ and their product is $24$, so their absolute difference is $15$.
