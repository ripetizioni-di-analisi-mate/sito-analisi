Solve the system $\begin{cases}x^2\leq 25\\x>2\end{cases}$.

- $(2,5]$
- $(2,5)$
- $[2,5]$
- $(2,\infty)$

## Explanation

The first condition gives the closed interval from $-5$ to $5$. The second keeps only points strictly greater than $2$.
