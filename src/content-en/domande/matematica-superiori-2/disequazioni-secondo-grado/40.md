For which values of $k$ does $k-(x-3)^2>0$ have no solutions?

- $k<0$
- $k\ge0$
- $k\le0$
- $k=0$

## Explanation

The expression has maximum $k$, reached when the square is zero, at $x=3$. If $k\le0$, even the maximum is at most zero and the strict sign excludes every value. For $k>0$, checking $x=3$ already gives one solution. The case $k=0$ is among the cases with no solution because the vertex gives equality.
