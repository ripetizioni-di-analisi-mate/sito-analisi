How are the solution sets of the inequalities in a system combined?

- $\text{Always take the union}$
- $\text{Take the intersection}$
- $\text{Add the endpoints}$
- $\text{Choose the larger set}$

## Explanation

All conditions in the system must hold at once. The remaining values belong to every solution set. This operation is the intersection.
