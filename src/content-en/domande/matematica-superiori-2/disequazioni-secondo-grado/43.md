One constraint is $0\le x\le5$. Which second condition removes exactly the values between $1$ and $4$ while keeping both $1$ and $4$?

- $(x+1)(x+4)\ge0$
- $(x-1)(x-4)\ge0$
- $(x-1)(x-4)\le0$
- $(x-1)(x-4)>0$

## Explanation

The product is negative between its zeros and positive outside. The symbol $\ge$ also includes the zeros. Intersecting with the first constraint leaves $[0,1]\cup[4,5]$.
