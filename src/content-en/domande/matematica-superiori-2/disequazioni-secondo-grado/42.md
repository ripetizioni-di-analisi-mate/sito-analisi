What is the solution of $(x-2)^2<0$?

- $\varnothing$
- $\{2\}$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-\infty,2)$

## Explanation

The square of a real number is always nonnegative. Thus no value can make it strictly negative. The solution set is empty.
