Someone squares $x-3<2$ to get $(x-3)^2<4$. Which condition, added to the original inequality, makes the two solution sets equal?

- $x-3>-2$
- $x-3<-2$
- $x-3\ge0$
- $x-3\ne0$

## Explanation

The square is less than $4$ exactly when $-2<x-3<2$. The upper bound was already imposed. The extra condition supplies the strict lower bound.
