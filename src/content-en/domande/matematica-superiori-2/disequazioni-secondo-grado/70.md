Solve $\dfrac{x+6}{x-13}\leq0$.

- $[-6,13]$
- $[-6,13)$
- $(-\infty,-6)\cup(13,+\infty)$
- $(-6,13)$

## Explanation

The quotient is negative between the roots. Include the zero of the numerator and exclude the value that makes the denominator zero.
