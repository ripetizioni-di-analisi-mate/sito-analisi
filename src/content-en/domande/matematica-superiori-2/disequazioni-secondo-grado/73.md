Solve the system $\begin{cases}x^2\leq 81\\x>4\end{cases}$.

- $(4,\infty)$
- $(4,9]$
- $[4,9]$
- $(4,9)$

## Explanation

The first condition gives the closed interval from $-9$ to $9$. The second keeps only points strictly greater than $4$.
