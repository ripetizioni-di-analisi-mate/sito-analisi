Find the domain of $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{(x-1)(4-x)}}$.

- $[1,4]$
- $(1,4)$
- $(-\infty,1)\cup(4,+\infty)$
- $[1,4)$

## Explanation

The root is in the denominator, so its radicand must be strictly positive. Both factors are positive together only for $1<x<4$. The endpoints make the denominator zero.
