For which $x$ is $x^2+2x\le x^2+6$?

- $(-\infty,3]$
- $(-\infty,3)$
- $[3,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$

## Explanation

The term $x^2$ is the same on both sides, so subtracting it preserves the sign. This leaves the linear inequality $2x\le6$. Division by $2$ gives $x\le3$. At $x=3$ both sides equal $15$, so that value is included. At $x=4$ they are $24$ and $22$, which fail the comparison. Determine the degree after taking the difference of the two sides.
