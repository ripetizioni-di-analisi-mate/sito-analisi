Which real numbers satisfy at least one of $(x+1)^2\le0$ and $(x-2)^2\le0$?

- $[-1,2]$
- $\{-1\}$
- $\{-1,2\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

Every square is nonnegative. The first comparison therefore requires the first square to equal zero, which happens only at $-1$. The second requires the second square to vanish, which happens only at $2$. Either condition is sufficient, so take both points and leave out the numbers between them. At zero both squares are positive.
