Sara solves $\dfrac{(x-2)(x+1)}{x-2}\ge0$ and shades every point from $-1$ onwards. What single change should she make to the drawing?

- Leave an open circle at $-1$
- Add all points less than $-1$
- Remove the entire interval $(2,+\infty)$
- Leave an open circle at $2$

## Explanation

Cancellation gives $x+1\ge0$ only where $x\ne2$. The point $-1$ belongs to the domain and makes the numerator zero. Its filled dot therefore stays.
