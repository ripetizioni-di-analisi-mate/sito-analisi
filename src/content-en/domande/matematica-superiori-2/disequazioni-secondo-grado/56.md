A polynomial has a zero at $x=2$ and is positive immediately to either side. If the zero can have multiplicity one, two or three, which must it have?

- $3$
- $1$
- $2$
- All three are possible

## Explanation

The remaining factors have constant sign near the zero. An odd power of $x-2$ changes sign there and an even power keeps its sign. Only $2$ is even among the choices.
