Solve $x^2+6x+9>0$.

- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-3\}$
- $\mathbb R$
- $\{-3\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{3\}$

## Explanation

The trinomial is the square of $x+3$. The square is zero at $x=-3$ and positive on both sides. The strict comparison excludes only $-3$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^2+6x+9&=(x+3)^2.
\end{aligned}
$$
