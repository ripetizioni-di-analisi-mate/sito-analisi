Solve the system $\begin{cases}x^2\leq 49\\x>3\end{cases}$.

- $(3,7)$
- $[3,7]$
- $(3,7]$
- $(3,\infty)$

## Explanation

The first condition gives the closed interval from $-7$ to $7$. The second keeps only points strictly greater than $3$.
