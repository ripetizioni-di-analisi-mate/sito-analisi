To solve $\dfrac{P(x)}{x^2+1}\le0$ over $\mathbb R$, which step is equivalent for every polynomial $P$?

- Require $P(x)=0$
- Solve $P(x)\le0$ over all of $\mathbb R$
- Solve $P(x)\ge0$ over all of $\mathbb R$
- Exclude $x=0$ and solve $P(x)<0$

## Explanation

The denominator $x^2+1$ is at least $1$. Multiplying both sides by it preserves the inequality for every real number, including values where $P(x)$ is zero.
