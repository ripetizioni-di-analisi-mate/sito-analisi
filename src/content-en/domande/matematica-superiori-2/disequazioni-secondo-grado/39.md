Solve $\dfrac{(x-6)(x+13)}{x-6}>0$.

- $(-13,\infty)$
- $(-13,6)\cup(6,+\infty)$
- $(-13,6)$
- $(6,\infty)$

## Explanation

The domain excludes $6$. For all other values, the fraction simplifies to $x+13$, which must be positive.
