What is the solution set of $\dfrac{x-2}{(x-2)(x-1)}\ge0$?

- $(1,+\infty)$
- $(1,2)\cup(2,+\infty)$
- $[2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,1)$

## Explanation

The values $1$ and $2$ make the denominator zero and remain excluded. For all other values the factor $x-2$ cancels and the quotient becomes $\dfrac1{x-1}$. Its numerator $1$ is positive, so the quotient is positive when $x>1$ and never equals zero. Remove $2$ from that half-line because cancellation is valid only in the original domain.
