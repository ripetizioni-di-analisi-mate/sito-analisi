Solve $-(x-4)^2\ge0$.

- $\{4\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-\infty,4]$

## Explanation

The opposite of a square is always nonpositive. To satisfy the given inequality as well, it must be zero. This occurs only when $x=4$.
