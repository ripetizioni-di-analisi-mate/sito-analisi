For $P(x)=x(6-x)$, which formula directly shows that the height of the graph never exceeds $9$?

- $P(x)=9-(x-3)^2$
- $P(x)=9+(x-3)^2$
- $P(x)=6-(x-3)^2$
- $P(x)=(x-3)^2-9$

## Explanation

Expanding $9-(x-3)^2$ gives $6x-x^2$. The subtracted square is nonnegative and vanishes at $x=3$, where the maximum is attained.
