Solve $\begin{cases}x^2>9\\x^2<16\end{cases}$.

- $[-4,-3]\cup[3,4]$
- $(-4,4)$
- $(3,4)$
- $(-4,-3)\cup(3,4)$

## Explanation

The first condition requires a distance from zero greater than $3$. The second requires it to be less than $4$. The intersection consists of two symmetric open intervals.
