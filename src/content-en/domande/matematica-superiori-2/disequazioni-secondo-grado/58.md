Which points of the real number line are closer to $-1$ than to $3$?

- $(-\infty,1]$
- $(-\infty,1)$
- $(1,+\infty)$
- $(-1,3)$

## Explanation

Distances are nonnegative, so their squares can be compared. Write $(x+1)^2<(x-3)^2$. Expanding cancels the $x^2$ terms and leaves $8x<8$, giving $x<1$. The point $1$ is the midpoint, two units from either point. Since the distance must be strictly smaller, that midpoint is excluded from the solution.
