For which $x$ is $\sqrt{(5-x)(x+2)}$ defined?

- $[-2,5]$
- $(-2,5)$
- $[2,5]$
- $(-\infty,-2]\cup[5,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The radicand is a product of two factors that are both nonnegative between the roots. At the endpoints the square root is zero.
