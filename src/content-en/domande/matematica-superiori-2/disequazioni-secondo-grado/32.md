For what value of $k$ does the parabola $y=(x+2)^2-k$ have exactly one point on the horizontal axis?

- $2$
- $-2$
- $0$
- $4$

## Explanation

The points on the axis satisfy $(x+2)^2=k$. If $k<0$, a square cannot reach that value. If $k>0$, the bases $\sqrt{k}$ and $-\sqrt{k}$ give two distinct horizontal coordinates. At $k=0$ those possibilities coincide and the only point is $(-2,0)$. The parabola touches the axis there without crossing it, because the root is double.
