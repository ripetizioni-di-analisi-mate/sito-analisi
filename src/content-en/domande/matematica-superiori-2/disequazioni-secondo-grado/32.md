A quadratic has values $P(-2)=3$, $P(0)=-1$ and $P(4)=2$. Where must its two zeros lie?

- One in $(-\infty,-2)$ and one in $(4,+\infty)$
- Both in $(0,4)$
- One in $(-2,0)$ and one in $(0,4)$
- Both in $(-2,0)$

## Explanation

The parabola moves from above the axis to below it between $-2$ and $0$, and back above it between $0$ and $4$. It meets the axis at least once in each interval. A quadratic has at most two zeros.
