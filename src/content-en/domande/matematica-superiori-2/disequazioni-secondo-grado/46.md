The solution of $P(x)<0$ is $(-2,5)$. On which interval is $P(-x)<0$?

- $(-5,-2)$
- $(-5,2)$
- $(2,5)$
- $(-2,5)$

## Explanation

The argument $-x$ must lie between $-2$ and $5$. Multiplying $-2<-x<5$ by $-1$ reverses both inequalities and gives $-5<x<2$.
