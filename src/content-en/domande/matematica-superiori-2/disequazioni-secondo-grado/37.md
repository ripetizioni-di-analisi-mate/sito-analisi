Which set satisfies both $\dfrac{x-3}{(x+1)^2}>0$ and $x+1<0$?

- $(-\infty,-1)$
- $(3,+\infty)$
- $(-1,3)$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The denominator is positive for every $x\ne-1$, so the quotient is positive only where $x-3>0$, namely $x>3$. The other condition asks for $x<-1$. The two intervals share no values. For example, at $x=-2$ the denominator is $1$ and the numerator is $-5$. The second condition holds but the quotient is negative.
