Solve $\dfrac{x-10}{x-12}\ge0$.

- $(-\infty,10]\cup[12,+\infty)$
- $[10,12)$
- $(-\infty,10)\cup(12,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,10]\cup(12,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Identify the zeros of the numerator and denominator separately. The quotient is positive when the two factors have the same sign. Include the zero of the numerator, and exclude every zero of the denominator.

$$
x\ne12
$$

$$
\frac{x-10}{x-12}\ge0\iff x\le10\;\text{or}\;x>12
$$
