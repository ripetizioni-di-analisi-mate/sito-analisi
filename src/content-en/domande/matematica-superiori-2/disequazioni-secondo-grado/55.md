Solve $-(x-6)(x+13)>0$.

- $(-\infty,-13)\cup(6,+\infty)$
- $(-6,13)$
- $(-13,6)$
- $[-13,6]$

## Explanation

The minus sign reverses the sign of the product. A negative product is required, which occurs strictly between $-13$ and $6$.
