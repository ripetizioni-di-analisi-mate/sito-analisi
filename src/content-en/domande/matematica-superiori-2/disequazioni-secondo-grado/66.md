A sign chart for $\dfrac{x+1}{x-2}$ shows a change of sign at both $-1$ and $2$. What determines which endpoints get filled dots in the solution of $\dfrac{x+1}{x-2}\le0$?

- Every sign change requires an open dot
- The fraction is zero at $-1$ and undefined at $2$
- Only the rightmost sign change is included
- Both sign changes are always included

## Explanation

An endpoint is included if it is in the domain and satisfies the inequality. The numerator vanishes at $-1$, which is allowed by $\le0$. The denominator vanishes at $2$, so the result is $[-1,2)$.
