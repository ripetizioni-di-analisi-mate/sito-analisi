A quadratic $P$ is negative at $x=2$. Define $Q(x)=(x-2)^2P(x)$. How does the solution set change from $P(x)<0$ to $Q(x)<0$?

- Only $x=2$ is lost
- Only $x=2$ is added
- The entire interval to the left of $2$ is lost
- It stays identical

## Explanation

For $x\ne2$ the new factor is strictly positive, so it preserves the sign of $P$. At $2$, however, $Q(2)=0$ and the strict inequality fails even though $P(2)<0$.
