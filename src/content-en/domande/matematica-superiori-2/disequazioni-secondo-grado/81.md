Solve $(x-2)^2(x-5)<0$.

- $(-\infty,2)\cup(2,5)$
- $(-\infty,2)\cup(5,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,5)$
- $(2,5)$

## Explanation

The square is positive except at $2$. A negative product requires $x<5$, and $2$ must be excluded because it makes the product zero.
