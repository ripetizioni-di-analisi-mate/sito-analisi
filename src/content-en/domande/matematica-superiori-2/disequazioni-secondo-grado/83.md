At which zero does $P(x)=(x-1)^3(x+2)^2$ change sign?

- Only at $1$
- Only at $-2$
- At both zeros
- At neither zero

## Explanation

The factor $(x-1)^3$ has the sign of $x-1$, so it changes sign at $1$. The factor $(x+2)^2$ is positive on both sides of $-2$, so its zero does not reverse the sign. On either side of $-2$, as long as $x<1$, the cubic factor is negative and the product stays negative.
