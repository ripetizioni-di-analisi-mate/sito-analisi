Solve $(x-4)^2(x-9)<0$.

- $(-\infty,4)\cup(4,9)$
- $(-\infty,4)\cup(9,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,9)$
- $(4,9)$

## Explanation

The square is positive except at $4$. A negative product requires $x<9$, and $4$ must be excluded because it makes the product zero.
