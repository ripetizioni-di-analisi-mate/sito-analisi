What is the real domain of $\sqrt{\dfrac{x+1}{x-2}}$?

- $(-\infty,-1)\cup[2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-1]\cup(2,+\infty)$
- $[-1,2)$
- $(-\infty,-1]\cup[2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The denominator excludes $2$ and the square root requires a nonnegative quotient. Below $-1$ both factors are negative, and above $2$ both are positive. Between the two boundaries their signs differ. At $-1$ the quotient is zero and the root is defined. At $2$ the radicand itself is undefined, so the point is excluded.
