What is the domain of $\sqrt{\dfrac{x^2-9}{x}}$?

- $[-3,0]\cup[3,+\infty)$
- $[-3,0)\cup[3,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-3]\cup(0,3]$
- $[3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The square root is defined when the radicand is greater than or equal to zero, and the fraction is defined for $x\ne0$. The numerator $x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3)$ is negative between $-3$ and $3$ and positive outside, while the denominator $x$ is negative for $x<0$ and positive for $x>0$. From the sign table the quotient is positive on $(-3,0)$ and on $(3,+\infty)$, negative on $(-\infty,-3)$ and on $(0,3)$. The quotient is zero at $-3$ and at $3$, so these two points belong to the domain, while $0$ is excluded because it makes the denominator zero. The domain is therefore $[-3,0)\cup[3,+\infty)$.
