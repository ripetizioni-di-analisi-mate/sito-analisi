Find the domain of $\sqrt{x^{2} - 20 x + 99}$.

- $[9,11]$
- $(-\infty,9]\cup[11,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,9)\cup(11,+\infty)$
- $(9,11)$

## Explanation

A radical with even index requires a nonnegative radicand. The trinomial has a positive leading coefficient, so it is nonnegative outside the roots. The roots are included because the square root of zero is defined.

$$
x^{2} - 20 x + 99\ge0
$$

$$
x\le9\quad\text{or}\quad x\ge11
$$
