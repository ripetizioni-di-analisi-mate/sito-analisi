A sensor accepts a reading $x$ when $x^2-6x+8\le0$. The display shows $3$ with an error of at most $\delta$ in either direction. What is the greatest $\delta$ guaranteeing that every possible true reading is accepted?

- $2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $3$

## Explanation

The inequality $(x-2)(x-4)\le0$ gives $[2,4]$. An interval centred at $3$ stays entirely within this set as long as its radius is at most $1$.
