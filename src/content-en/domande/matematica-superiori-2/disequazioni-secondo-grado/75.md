Solve the system $\begin{cases}x^2\leq 169\\x>6\end{cases}$.

- $(6,13]$
- $(6,13)$
- $[6,13]$
- $(6,\infty)$

## Explanation

The first condition gives the closed interval from $-13$ to $13$. The second keeps only points strictly greater than $6$.
