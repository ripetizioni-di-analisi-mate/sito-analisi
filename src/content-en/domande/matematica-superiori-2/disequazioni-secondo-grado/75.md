Which real numbers satisfy both $x^2-9\le0$ and $x^2-x-2\ge0$?

- $[-3,-1]\cup[2,3]$
- $[-1,2]$
- $[-3,3]$
- $(-3,-1)\cup(2,3)$

## Explanation

The first inequality gives $[-3,3]$. The second factors as $(x+1)(x-2)\ge0$, true for $x\le-1$ or $x\ge2$. Intersect both branches with the first interval to get $[-3,-1]$ and $[2,3]$. All endpoints remain included because both comparisons allow equality. The point zero satisfies the first comparison and fails the second.
