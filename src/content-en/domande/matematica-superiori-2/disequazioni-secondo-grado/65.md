For which real values of $k$ does $x^2+2kx+4>0$ hold for every real $x$?

- $[-2,2]$
- $(-2,2)$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(2,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$

## Explanation

The quadratic is $(x+k)^2+4-k^2$, so its minimum is $4-k^2$, attained at $x=-k$. It must be strictly positive, giving $k^2<4$ and $-2<k<2$. At $k=-2$ and $k=2$ the quadratic is a perfect square and equals zero at its vertex. That input prevents strict positivity for all real numbers. This is why both boundaries on the parameter are strict.
