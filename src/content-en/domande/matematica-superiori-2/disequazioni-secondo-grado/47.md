For which values of $x$ is $(x+4)^2(x-2)$ strictly positive?

- $(-4,2)$
- $(2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-4)\cup(2,+\infty)$
- $[2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The squared factor is positive except at $x=-4$, so at all other points the product takes the sign of $x-2$. Positivity requires $x>2$. The double root $-4$ is already outside the result. At $2$ the product is zero, excluded by the strict comparison. For example, at $x=-5$ the product is still negative.
