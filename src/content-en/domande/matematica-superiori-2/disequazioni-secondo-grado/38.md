Paolo claims that $\frac{1}{x-2}<\frac{1}{x-3}$ whenever both sides are defined, because $x-2>x-3$. On which interval is his conclusion false?

- $(2,3)$
- $(-\infty,2)$
- $(3,+\infty)$
- On none of the three intervals

## Explanation

Between $2$ and $3$ the left side is positive and the right side negative. On the other two intervals the denominators have the same sign, so the difference of the reciprocals is $-1/[(x-2)(x-3)]<0$.
