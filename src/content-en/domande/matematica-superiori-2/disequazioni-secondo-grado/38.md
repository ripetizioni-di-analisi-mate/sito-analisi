Solve $\dfrac{x-1}{(x-3)^2}>0$.

- $(1,3)\cup(3,+\infty)$
- $(1,+\infty)$
- $(3,+\infty)$
- $[1,3)\cup(3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The denominator is positive wherever the quotient is defined, so require $x>1$. Remove $3$ because it makes the denominator zero. Exclude $1$ because the inequality is strict.
