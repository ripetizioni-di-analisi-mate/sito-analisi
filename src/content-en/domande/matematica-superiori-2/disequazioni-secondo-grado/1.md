Solve $x^{2} - 14 x + 48\le0$.

- $(-\infty,6)\cup(8,+\infty)$
- $(6,8)$
- $(-\infty,8]$
- $[6,8]$

## Explanation

The trinomial has a positive leading coefficient and two distinct roots. Between the roots, the factors have opposite signs, so the product is negative. The roots are included because the inequality allows equality.

$$
x^{2} - 14 x + 48=(x-6)(x-8)
$$

$$
(x-6)(x-8)\le0\iff 6\le x\le8
$$
