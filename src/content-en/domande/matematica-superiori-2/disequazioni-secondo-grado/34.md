Solve $\dfrac{(x-4)(x+9)}{x-4}>0$.

- $(-9,4)\cup(4,+\infty)$
- $(-9,\infty)$
- $(-9,4)$
- $(4,\infty)$

## Explanation

The domain excludes $4$. For all other values, the fraction simplifies to $x+9$, which must be positive.
