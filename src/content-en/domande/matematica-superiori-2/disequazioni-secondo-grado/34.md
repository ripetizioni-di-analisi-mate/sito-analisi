For $f(x)=\dfrac{(x-1)(x+3)}{x-1}$, where is $f$ zero and where is it undefined, in that order?

- $-3,\ 1$
- $1,\ -3$
- $-3,\ -1$
- $3,\ 1$

## Explanation

At $x=1$ the denominator vanishes and the original expression is undefined. Away from $1$ the common factor cancels and the value is $x+3$. That sum is zero at $-3$, which belongs to the domain. Substituting $-3$ directly gives a zero numerator and denominator $-4$. The two horizontal coordinates therefore have different roles.
