What are the solutions of the inequality $2x^2\le x+3$?

- $(-1,\dfrac32)$
- $[1,\dfrac32]$
- $(-\infty,-1]\cup[\dfrac32,+\infty)$
- $[-1,\dfrac32]$

## Explanation

Subtracting $x+3$ gives a trinomial that factors as $(x+1)(2x-3)$. The product is negative between the roots and zero at the endpoints. Therefore, the solution interval is closed.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x^2-x-3&=(x+1)(2x-3),\\
S&=\left[-1,\frac32\right].
\end{aligned}
$$
