The graph of a quadratic passes through $(-3,0)$, $(2,0)$, and $(0,-12)$. What is the coefficient of $x^2$?

- $-2$
- $6$
- $-12$
- $2$

## Explanation

The intersections with the horizontal axis give the roots $-3$ and $2$. The factored form is therefore $a(x+3)(x-2)$. At horizontal coordinate zero this equals $-6a$. The given height is $-12$, so $a=2$. The sign agrees with the position of the point because zero lies between the roots and the height there is negative.
