Solve $x^2>36$.

- $(-\infty,-6]\cup[6,+\infty)$
- $(-6,6)$
- $(6,\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-6)\cup(6,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Compare the absolute value of $x$ with $6$. The required values are at distance strictly greater than $6$ from the origin.
