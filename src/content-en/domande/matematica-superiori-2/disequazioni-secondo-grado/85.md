Solve $(x-6)^2(x-13)<0$.

- $(6,13)$
- $(-\infty,6)\cup(13,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,13)$
- $(-\infty,6)\cup(6,13)$

## Explanation

The square is positive except at $6$. A negative product requires $x<13$, and $6$ must be excluded because it makes the product zero.
