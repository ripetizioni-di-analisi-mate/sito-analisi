On $I=(0,1)$ you have established that $A(x)>0$. Which information about $B$ is sufficient to decide where $A(x)B(x)\le0$ in $I$?

- The maximum of $|B(x)|$ on $I$, if it exists
- How many zeros $B$ has in $I$, without knowing its sign
- Only the value of $B(1/2)$
- Where $B(x)\le0$ in $I$

## Explanation

Dividing by the positive factor $A(x)$ preserves the inequality and gives $B(x)\le0$. An isolated value or just the number of zeros does not describe the sign throughout the interval.
