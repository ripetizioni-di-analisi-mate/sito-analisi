What is the solution set of $(x-1)^2(x+2)^3\le0$?

- $(-\infty,-2]$
- $[-2,1]$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup\{1\}$
- $(-\infty,-2]\cup\{1\}$

## Explanation

Away from the zeros the square is positive, so the sign is the sign of $x+2$ because its exponent is odd. For $x<-2$ the product is negative. It vanishes at $-2$ and $1$, and the non-strict comparison includes both. The point $1$ is isolated because the product is positive at every other point above $-2$.
