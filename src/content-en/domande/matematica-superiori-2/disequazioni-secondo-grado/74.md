Solve the system $\begin{cases}x^2\leq 121\\x>5\end{cases}$.

- $(5,11)$
- $(5,\infty)$
- $[5,11]$
- $(5,11]$

## Explanation

The first condition gives the closed interval from $-11$ to $11$. The second keeps only points strictly greater than $5$.
