For which nonnegative real numbers does $4\le x^2<9$ hold?

- $(-3,-2]\cup[2,3)$
- $(2,3]$
- $[4,9)$
- $[2,3)$

## Explanation

Since $x\ge0$, its square increases with $x$, and $x^2\ge4$ requires $x\ge2$. The comparison $x^2<9$ requires $x<3$. At the boundary $2$, the square equals the allowed lower value $4$. At $3$ it equals $9$, which is excluded. The nonnegative domain also removes the entire negative branch that would occur without that constraint.
