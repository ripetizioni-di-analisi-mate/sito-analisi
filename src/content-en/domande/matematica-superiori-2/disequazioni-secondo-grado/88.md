Solve $x^2>25$.

- $(-\infty,-5]\cup[5,+\infty)$
- $(-5,5)$
- $(5,\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-5)\cup(5,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Compare the absolute value of $x$ with $5$. The required values are at distance strictly greater than $5$ from the origin.
