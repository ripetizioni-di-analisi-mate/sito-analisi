For which values of $x$ is $(2x-1)^2$ at most $9$?

- $[-3,3]$
- $(-1,2)$
- $[-2,4]$
- $[-1,2]$

## Explanation

The base $2x-1$ must be between $-3$ and $3$, inclusive. Adding $1$ to all parts gives $-2\le2x\le4$, and dividing by $2$ gives $-1\le x\le2$. Substituting the endpoints gives bases $-3$ and $3$. Both square to the allowed value $9$. A base beyond either boundary would give a larger square. Each boundary can be checked separately by substitution in the original inequality.
