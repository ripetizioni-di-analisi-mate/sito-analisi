For a quadratic $P$, the solution of $P(x)>0$ is $(-\infty,-3)\cup(3,+\infty)$. Which **coefficient** of $P$ must be zero?

- No coefficient must be zero
- The coefficient of $x$
- The constant term
- The coefficient of $x^2$

## Explanation

The endpoints $-3$ and $3$ are the two zeros and $P(x)=a(x+3)(x-3)=ax^2-9a$, with $a>0$. The symmetric factors eliminate the linear term.
