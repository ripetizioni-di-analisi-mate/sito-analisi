Solve $x^2>9$.

- $(-\infty,-3]\cup[3,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-3)\cup(3,+\infty)$
- $(3,\infty)$
- $(-3,3)$

## Explanation

Compare the absolute value of $x$ with $3$. The required values are at distance strictly greater than $3$ from the origin.
