Solve $(x-3)^2(x-7)<0$.

- $(-\infty,3)\cup(3,7)$
- $(-\infty,3)\cup(7,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,7)$
- $(3,7)$

## Explanation

The square is positive except at $3$. A negative product requires $x<7$, and $3$ must be excluded because it makes the product zero.
