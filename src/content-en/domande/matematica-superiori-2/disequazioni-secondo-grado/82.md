In the sign chart for $(x-1)^2(x+2)$, the interval $x>1$ is already marked positive. Moving left, what signs occur on the three intervals in right-to-left order?

- $+,\,+,\,-$
- $+,\,+,\,+$
- $+,\,-,\,-$
- $+,\,-,\,+$

## Explanation

At $1$ the squared factor vanishes without changing sign. At $-2$ the simple factor changes sign. From the right the sequence is therefore positive, positive, negative.
