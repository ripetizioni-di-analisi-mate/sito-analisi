It is known that $N(x)/D(x)$ is negative at $x=1$. Both expressions are defined and $D(1)\ne0$. Which sign information alone is sufficient to conclude that $N(1)>0$?

- $D(1)<0$
- $D(1)>0$
- $D(1)\ne1$
- $N(1)\ne0$

## Explanation

A negative ratio has numerator and denominator of opposite signs. A negative denominator therefore forces a positive numerator. Knowing only that a value is nonzero does not determine its sign.
