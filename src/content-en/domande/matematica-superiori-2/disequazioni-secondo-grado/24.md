Among positive integers $x$, what is the greatest value satisfying $x^2\leq 168$?

- $168$
- $14$
- $12$
- $13$

## Explanation

The square of $13$ exceeds the bound. The preceding integer is positive and its square is within the bound. This gives $12$.
