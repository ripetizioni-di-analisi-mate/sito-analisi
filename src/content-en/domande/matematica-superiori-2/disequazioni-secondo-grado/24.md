How many integers from $0$ through $6$ satisfy $(x-1)^2\ge4$?

- $3$
- $5$
- $4$
- $7$

## Explanation

The square is at least $4$ when its base is at most $-2$ or at least $2$. Thus $x\le-1$ or $x\ge3$. The lower bound of zero removes the negative branch. This leaves $3,4,5,6$. The number $3$ gives a square of $4$ and is included, while $2$ gives a square of $1$.
