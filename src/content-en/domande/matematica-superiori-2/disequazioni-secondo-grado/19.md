Among positive integers $x$, what is the greatest value satisfying $x^2\leq 48$?

- $7$
- $6$
- $48$
- $8$

## Explanation

The square of $7$ exceeds the bound. The preceding integer is positive and its square is within the bound. This gives $6$.
