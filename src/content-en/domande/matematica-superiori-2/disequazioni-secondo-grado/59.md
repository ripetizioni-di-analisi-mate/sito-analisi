What is the solution set of $(x-1)^2+(x+1)^2\le0$?

- $\varnothing$
- $\{-1,1\}$
- $\{0\}$
- $[-1,1]$

## Explanation

Both terms are nonnegative, so their sum could be nonpositive only if both were zero. The first vanishes only at $1$, and the second vanishes only at $-1$. No value satisfies both equalities. Expanding confirms the result, since the sum equals $2x^2+2$, which is at least $2$ for every real number.
