For which real $x$ does the point $(x;x^2)$ belong to the closed rectangle $0\le X\le2$, $0\le Y\le1$?

::grafico{f="x^2" xmin=-1.4 xmax=2.6 ymin=-0.75 ymax=2.9 linee="0,0 2,0 2,1 0,1 0,0" titolo="The allowed rectangle and the parabola"}

- $[0,1]$
- $[0,2]$
- $\{0,1\}$
- $[-1,1]$

## Explanation

The horizontal coordinate requires $0\le x\le2$. The vertical coordinate gives $x^2\le1$, or $-1\le x\le1$. Their common values form $[0,1]$.
