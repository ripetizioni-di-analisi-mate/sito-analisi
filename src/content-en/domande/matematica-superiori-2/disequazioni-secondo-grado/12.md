A student starts looking for roots to solve $(x-2)^2<-1$. What is the quickest comparison to determine whether any solutions exist?

- The left side has maximum $0$, which is less than $1$
- The two sides coincide at $x=-1$
- The left side has minimum $0$, which exceeds $-1$
- The left side changes sign at $2$

## Explanation

For every real number, $(x-2)^2\ge0>-1$. This comparison even holds at the minimum and proves that no number satisfies the requirement.
