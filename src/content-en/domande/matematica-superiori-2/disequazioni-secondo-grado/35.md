For which values of $x$ does the graph of $y=-(x-1)(x-5)$ lie strictly above the line $y=3$?

- $(1,5)$
- $(2,4)$
- $[2,4]$
- $(-\infty,2)\cup(4,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Subtract the height $3$ and study $-x^2+6x-8=-(x-2)(x-4)$. This quadratic is positive when the linear factors have opposite signs, between $2$ and $4$. At both endpoints the graph meets $y=3$, so the strict comparison excludes them. The value $x=3$ gives height $4$, confirming that the interior is allowed. Comparing with the line changes the zeros of the difference from the roots of the parabola.
