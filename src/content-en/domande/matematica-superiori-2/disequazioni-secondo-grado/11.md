Solve $4-2x^2\ge0$.

- $(-\sqrt2,\sqrt2)$
- $[-\sqrt2,\sqrt2]$
- $[0,\sqrt2]$
- $[-2,2]$

## Explanation

The condition is equivalent to $x^2\le2$, so the distance of $x$ from zero is at most $\sqrt2$. Equality allows both endpoints.

$$
\begin{aligned}
4-2x^2\ge0&\iff x^2\le2.
\end{aligned}
$$
