The solution of $4-2x^2\ge0$ is an interval symmetric about $0$. Which property explains the symmetry without finding the endpoints?

- Its constant term is positive
- Replacing $x$ by $-x$ leaves its value unchanged
- Its value at $0$ is zero
- Its leading coefficient is negative

## Explanation

Since $(-x)^2=x^2$, the left side has the same value at each pair of opposite points. Either both points satisfy the inequality or neither does.
