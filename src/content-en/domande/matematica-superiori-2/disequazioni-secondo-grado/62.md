You want a formula $\sqrt{P(x)}$ defined at every real number except $0$. Can $P$ be a quadratic polynomial?

- Yes, take $P(x)=-x^2$
- Yes, take $P(x)=x^2$
- No, because every quadratic is positive at $0$
- No, a polynomial negative at $0$ also stays negative sufficiently close to $0$

## Explanation

Excluding $0$ requires $P(0)<0$. The parabola then stays below the axis for inputs sufficiently close to zero, so a whole interval would be excluded. If $P(0)=0$, the square root is defined.
