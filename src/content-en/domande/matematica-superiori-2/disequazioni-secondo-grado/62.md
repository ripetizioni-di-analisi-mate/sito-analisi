What is the real domain of $\sqrt{-x^2+2x-2}$?

- $\{1\}$
- $\mathbb R$
- $[-1,1]$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

Completing the square gives the radicand $-(x-1)^2-1$. Its maximum is $-1$, reached at the vertex with horizontal coordinate $1$. Thus the radicand is strictly negative for every real value and the real square root is undefined. The point $1$ is excluded along with the other values, since even the maximum falls short of zero.
