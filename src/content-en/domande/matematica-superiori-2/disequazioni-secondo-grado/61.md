An arch has height $h(x)=4-(x-2)^2$ metres above the floor for $0\le x\le4$. How wide is the region with clearance for a panel $3$ metres tall, allowing contact?

::grafico{curve="t,4-(t-2)^2,0,4" f="3" xmin=-0.7 xmax=4.7 ymin=-0.6 ymax=5 uguale=1 legenda="y=3;y=4-(x-2)^2" titolo="Arch profile and panel height"}

- $2$ m
- $4$ m
- $1$ m
- $3$ m

## Explanation

The clearance condition is $h(x)\ge3$, or $(x-2)^2\le1$. The panel fits from $x=1$ to $x=3$. The width is the difference between these coordinates, $2$ metres.
