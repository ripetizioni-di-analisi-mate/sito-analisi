What is the length of the interval that solves $(x+1)^2<9$?

- $3$
- $9$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

The base $x+1$ must lie between $-3$ and $3$ because its square must be below $9$. Subtracting $1$ gives $-4<x<2$. The interval length is the difference of its endpoints, $2-(-4)=6$. Excluding the endpoints leaves the distance between them unchanged, which is the quantity the question asks for. The centre is halfway between the boundaries, three units from each.
