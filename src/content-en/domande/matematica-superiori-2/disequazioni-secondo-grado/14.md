In the system $x^2-4\ge0$, $-3<x\le2$, why is $2$ a solution even though points immediately to its left are excluded?

- At $2$ the first left side vanishes, but just to its left it is negative
- The polynomial is undefined on $(0,2)$
- Every system has at least one isolated solution
- The constraint $x\le2$ excludes every value below $2$

## Explanation

For $0<x<2$, we have $x^2<4$, so the first constraint fails. At $x=2$ it reads $0\ge0$, and the second constraint holds too. The full solution is $(-3,-2]\cup\{2\}$.
