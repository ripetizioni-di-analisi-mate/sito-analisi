Solve $\begin{cases}x^2-4\ge0\\-3<x\le2\end{cases}$.

- $(-3,-2]\cup\{2\}$
- $(-3,-2]$
- $[-3,-2]\cup\{2\}$
- $[-2,2]$

## Explanation

The first inequality allows $x\le-2$ or $x\ge2$. Intersecting with $(-3,2]$ keeps the part $(-3,-2]$ and the single point $2$ from the right-hand interval.

$$
\begin{aligned}
S&=\bigl((-\infty,-2]\cup[2,+\infty)\bigr)\cap(-3,2],\\
&=(-3,-2]\cup\{2\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
