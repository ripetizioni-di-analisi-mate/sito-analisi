Solve $(x+4)(x-9)\geq0$.

- $(-4,9)$
- $[-4,9]$
- $(-\infty,-4]\cup[9,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-4)\cup(9,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The factors have the same sign outside the two roots. The roots are included because the inequality also allows the value zero.
