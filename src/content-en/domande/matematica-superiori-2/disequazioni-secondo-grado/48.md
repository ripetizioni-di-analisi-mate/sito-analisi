Let $P(x)=x^2-6x+5$. Which comparison holds for every real $x$?

- $P(x)>-4$
- $P(x)\ge-3$
- $P(x)\ge-4$
- $P(x)\le-4$

## Explanation

Completing the square gives $P(x)=(x-3)^2-4$. Thus $-4$ is the minimum and is attained at $x=3$. The strict comparison $P(x)>-4$ fails precisely there, as does $P(x)\ge-3$. To rule out $P(x)\le-4$, choose $x=4$, which gives $-3$. The non-strict lower bound given by the minimum holds everywhere. This is the lower bound attained by the parabola.
