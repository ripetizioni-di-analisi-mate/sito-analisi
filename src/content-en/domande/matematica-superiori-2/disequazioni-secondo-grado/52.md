Which of the given values satisfies $-(x-2)(x+3)\ge0$?

- $-4$
- $3$
- $-3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The roots are $-3$ and $2$. The negative coefficient makes the expression nonnegative between the roots, including both. The only choice in $[-3,2]$ is $-3$, where the factor $x+3$ vanishes. The number $-4$ is to the left of the allowed interval, while $3$ and $4$ are to its right and produce negative values.
