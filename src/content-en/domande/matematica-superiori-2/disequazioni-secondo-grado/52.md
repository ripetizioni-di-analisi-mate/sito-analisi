Solve $-(x-3)(x+7)>0$.

- $(-3,7)$
- $(-\infty,-7)\cup(3,+\infty)$
- $(-7,3)$
- $[-7,3]$

## Explanation

The minus sign reverses the sign of the product. A negative product is required, which occurs strictly between $-7$ and $3$.
