The real solution of a constraint is $\sqrt{10}<x<\sqrt{15}$. How many possible values are there if $x$ counts boxes and must be an integer?

- $5$
- $0$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

Since $3^2<10<15<4^2$, both endpoints are between $3$ and $4$. The interval contains no integer.
