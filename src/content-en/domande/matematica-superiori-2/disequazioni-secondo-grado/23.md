Find the greatest positive integer satisfying $x(x+1)\le30$.

- $6$
- $5$
- $29$
- $4$

## Explanation

Subtracting $30$ and factoring gives $(x+6)(x-5)\le0$. The solution interval runs from $-6$ to $5$. The greatest positive integer in that interval is $5$, which gives exactly $30$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^2+x-30&=(x+6)(x-5).
\end{aligned}
$$
