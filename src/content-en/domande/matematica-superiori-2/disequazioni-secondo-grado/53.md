Solve $-(x-4)(x+9)>0$.

- $(-4,9)$
- $(-9,4)$
- $(-\infty,-9)\cup(4,+\infty)$
- $[-9,4]$

## Explanation

The minus sign reverses the sign of the product. A negative product is required, which occurs strictly between $-9$ and $4$.
