Let $t=2x$. For which values of $x$ is the product $-(t-3)(t+4)$ positive?

- $(-4,3)$
- $\left(-2,\dfrac32\right)$
- $\left[-2,\dfrac32\right]$
- $\left(-\infty,-2\right)\cup\left(\dfrac32,+\infty\right)$

## Explanation

As a function of $t$, the expression is positive between the roots $-4$ and $3$ because the minus sign reverses the product of the linear factors. This gives $-4<t<3$. Since $t=2x$, divide both bounds by $2$, preserving both signs. The new endpoints are $-2$ and $\dfrac32$, both excluded because they give zero.
