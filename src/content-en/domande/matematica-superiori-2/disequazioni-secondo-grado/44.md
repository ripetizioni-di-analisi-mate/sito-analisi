If $\Delta<0$ and $a<0$, what is the sign of $ax^2+bx+c$?

- $\text{Always positive}$
- $\text{Zero at two values}$
- $\text{Always negative}$
- $\text{It changes sign twice}$

## Explanation

A trinomial with no real roots has the same sign as its leading coefficient. That coefficient is negative here, so the trinomial is negative for every real number.
