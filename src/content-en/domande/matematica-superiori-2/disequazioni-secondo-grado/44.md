A quadratic polynomial has negative discriminant and satisfies $P(2)=-7$. What follows even for values of $x$ far from $2$?

- $P$ has a double root at $2$
- The maximum of $P$ is exactly $-7$
- $P(x)<0$ for every real $x$
- $P(x)<0$ only for $x>2$

## Explanation

A parabola with negative discriminant never meets the axis and therefore has constant sign. The value at $2$ determines that sign without locating the vertex.
