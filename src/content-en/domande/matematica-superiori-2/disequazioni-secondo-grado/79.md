A calculation rounds $\sqrt5$ to $2.24$ and uses this to find the domain of $\sqrt{5-x^2}$. Which check decides whether $x=2.24$ actually belongs to the domain?

- $224^2>5\cdot100^2$, so it is excluded
- $224<500$, so it is included
- Square roots include every rounded endpoint
- A positive rounded endpoint is enough

## Explanation

The condition is $x^2\le5$. At $x=224/100$ we have $224^2=50176>50000$. The radicand is negative even though the value is close to the endpoint.
