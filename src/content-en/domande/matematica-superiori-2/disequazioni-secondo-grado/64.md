At which horizontal coordinates does the graph of $y=(x+2)^2+5$ have height at least $6$?

- $[-3,-1]$
- $(-\infty,-3]\cup[-1,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-3)\cup(-1,+\infty)$
- $[-2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Subtracting $5$ gives $(x+2)^2\ge1$. This happens when the base is at most $-1$ or at least $1$, so $x\le-3$ or $x\ge-1$. At $-3$ and $-1$ the graph reaches exactly height $6$, which is included. The vertex at $-2$ has height $5$ and is excluded along with nearby points. The part at or above the required height therefore occupies both outer branches.
