Which $x$ satisfy the system $\begin{cases}x^{2} - 14 x + 48\le0\\x>7\end{cases}$?

- $(7,8]$
- $[7,8]$
- $[6,8]$
- $(7,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The first inequality gives the closed interval between the roots. The second requires values greater than the midpoint. The system requires the intersection, so only the common part remains, with the left endpoint excluded and the right endpoint included.

$$
6\le x\le8
$$

$$
[6,8]\cap(7,+\infty)=(7,8]
$$
