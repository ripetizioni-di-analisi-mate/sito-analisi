What is the solution set of $\dfrac3{x-2}\ge1$?

- $(-\infty,2)\cup[5,+\infty)$
- $[2,5]$
- $(2,5)$
- $(2,5]$

## Explanation

Exclude $x=2$ and subtract $1$ to get $\dfrac{5-x}{x-2}\ge0$. Between $2$ and $5$, numerator and denominator are both positive. Below $2$ the denominator is negative, and above $5$ the numerator is negative. The point $5$ gives $3:(5-2)=1$ in the original comparison and is included. The domain excludes $2$. The original ratio at $x=0$ is negative, which checks the excluded left branch.
