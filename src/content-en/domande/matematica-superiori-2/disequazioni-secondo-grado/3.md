Anna rewrites $-x^2+4x-3\ge0$ as $x^2-4x+3\ge0$. Which value shows that her step changes the solution set?

- $x=3$
- $x=2$
- No real value
- $x=1$

## Explanation

At $x=2$ the original left side is $1$, whereas the rewritten one is $-1$. Multiplying by $-1$ reverses the inequality. Both expressions vanish at $1$ and $3$.
