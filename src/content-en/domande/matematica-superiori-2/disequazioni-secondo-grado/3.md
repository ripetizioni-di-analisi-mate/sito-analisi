Solve $- x^{2} + 14 x - 48\ge0$.

- $(-\infty,6)\cup(8,+\infty)$
- $[6,8]$
- $(6,8)$
- $\mathbb R$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides by negative one reverses the inequality. The new trinomial has a positive leading coefficient. It is nonpositive on the closed interval between the roots.

$$
x^{2} - 14 x + 48\le0
$$

$$
6\le x\le8
$$
