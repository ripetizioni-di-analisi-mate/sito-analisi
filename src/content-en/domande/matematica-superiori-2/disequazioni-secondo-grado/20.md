Among positive integers $x$, what is the greatest value satisfying $x^2\leq 80$?

- $80$
- $10$
- $8$
- $9$

## Explanation

The square of $9$ exceeds the bound. The preceding integer is positive and its square is within the bound. This gives $8$.
