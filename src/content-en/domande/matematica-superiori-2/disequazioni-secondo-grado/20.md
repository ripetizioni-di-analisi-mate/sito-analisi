A rectangle has positive whole-number side lengths $n$ and $n+2$ metres. How many values of $n$ give an area strictly less than $80\,\mathrm{m}^2$?

- $8$
- $9$
- $7$
- $10$

## Explanation

The area condition is $n(n+2)<80$, or $(n+10)(n-8)<0$. The first factor is positive for every positive integer $n$, so the condition reduces to $n<8$. The allowed values run from one to seven. At eight metres the area would be $8\cdot10=80$ square metres, exactly the excluded boundary. The geometric domain also excludes zero and negative numbers.
