What is the smallest integer $k$ for which the inequality $x^2-2x\le k$ is true for every integer $x$ with $0\le x\le3$?

- $9$
- $-1$
- $0$
- $3$

## Explanation

For $x=0$, $x=1$, $x=2$ and $x=3$ the left-hand side $x^2-2x$ equals $0$, $-1$, $0$ and $3$ respectively. The inequality must be true for all four values, so $k$ must be greater than or equal to the largest of them, that is $k\geq3$. Therefore the smallest integer that meets the requirement is $k=3$.
