A sensor accepts only the integer readings $x=0,1,2,3$. What is the smallest integer $k$ for which all the allowed readings satisfy $x^2-2x\le k$?

- $9$
- $-1$
- $0$
- $3$

## Explanation

The four readings give $0,-1,0,3$. The threshold must reach the largest of these values, and $k=3$ is sufficient.
