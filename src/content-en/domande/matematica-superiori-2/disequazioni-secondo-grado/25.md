For which real values of $k$ does $x^2+k\ge0$ hold for every real $x$?

- $k>0$
- $k\le0$
- $k=0$
- $k\ge0$

## Explanation

The value $x=0$ gives $k$, so $k$ must be nonnegative. When $k\ge0$, the square $x^2$ is also nonnegative for every real input, and the sum satisfies the comparison. The case $k=0$ is allowed because equality is included. The vertex gives the minimum, so checking it is enough to cover all other points.
