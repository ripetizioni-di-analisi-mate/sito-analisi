Luca thinks $\sqrt{(x-1)(x-4)}$ and $\sqrt{x-1}\sqrt{x-4}$ are equivalent wherever the first expression exists. Which value disproves this claim over the reals?

- $x=4$
- $x=6$
- $x=5$
- $x=0$

## Explanation

At $x=0$ the product inside the first radical is $4$. Inside the separate radicals the values are $-1$ and $-4$, neither of which has a real square root.
