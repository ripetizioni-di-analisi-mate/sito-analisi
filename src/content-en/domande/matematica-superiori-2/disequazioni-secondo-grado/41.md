A workshop accepts a length $L$ if $(L-20)^2\le\frac{1}{16}$, with $L$ measured in centimetres. What is the largest permitted deviation from $20$ cm?

- $\frac1{16}$ cm
- $\frac12$ cm
- $4$ cm
- $\frac14$ cm

## Explanation

The deviation is $|L-20|$. Taking square roots gives $|L-20|\le1/4$. Both endpoints attain that deviation.
