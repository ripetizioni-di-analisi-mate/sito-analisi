Which values are always excluded from a rational inequality?

- $\text{The zeros of the numerator}$
- $\text{All negative numbers}$
- $\text{All integers}$
- $\text{The zeros of the denominator}$

## Explanation

The fraction is defined only when the denominator is nonzero. Zeros of the numerator can be included when equality is allowed and the denominator is nonzero. Establish the domain before checking signs.
