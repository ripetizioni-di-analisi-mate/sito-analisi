For which values of $c$ is $x^2-2x+c$ strictly positive for every real $x$?

- $c>1$
- $c\ge 1$
- $c>0$
- $c<1$

## Explanation

The trinomial is $(x-1)^2+c-1$. Since the square is smallest at $x=1$, the minimum value of the trinomial is $c-1$. Strict positivity at that point requires $c>1$.

$$
x^2-2x+c=(x-1)^2+c-1.
$$
