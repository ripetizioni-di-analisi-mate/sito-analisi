Solve $-x^2-6<0$.

- $\varnothing$
- $(0,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-\infty,0)$

## Explanation

The square is nonnegative and the added number is strictly positive. Their sum is positive for every real number. Reversing the sign gives a negative number in every case.

$$
x^2+6>0\quad\text{for every }x\in\mathbb R
$$
