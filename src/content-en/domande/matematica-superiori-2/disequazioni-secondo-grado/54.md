Solve $-(x-5)(x+11)>0$.

- $(-\infty,-11)\cup(5,+\infty)$
- $(-5,11)$
- $(-11,5)$
- $[-11,5]$

## Explanation

The minus sign reverses the sign of the product. A negative product is required, which occurs strictly between $-11$ and $5$.
