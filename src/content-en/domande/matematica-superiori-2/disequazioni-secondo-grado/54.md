A rectangle has perimeter $22\,\mathrm m$ and whole-number side lengths. What are the possible lengths of its shorter side if the area is at least $24\,\mathrm{m}^2$?

- $\{3,4,5,6,7,8\}$
- $\{4,5\}$
- $\{3,4,5\}$
- $\{1,2,3\}$

## Explanation

If the shorter side is $x$, the other is $11-x$. The area requires $x(11-x)\ge24$, or $(x-3)(x-8)\le0$. This allows $3\le x\le8$. Since $x$ is the shorter side, also require $x\le11-x$, giving $x\le5.5$. The remaining whole numbers are $3,4,5$. The value $3$ is included because it gives exactly $24\,\mathrm{m}^2$. For example, sides $2$ and $9$ meet the perimeter requirement and give only $18\,\mathrm{m}^2$.
