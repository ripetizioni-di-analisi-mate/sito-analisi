Solve $x^{2} - 14 x + 48>0$.

- $(-\infty,6)\cup(8,+\infty)$
- $[6,8]$
- $(-\infty,6]\cup[8,+\infty)$
- $(6,8)$

## Explanation

Below the smaller root both factors are negative. Above the larger root both are positive. The product is therefore positive on the outer intervals. The roots are excluded because the required inequality is strict.

$$
x^{2} - 14 x + 48=(x-6)(x-8)
$$

$$
x<6\quad\text{or}\quad x>8
$$
