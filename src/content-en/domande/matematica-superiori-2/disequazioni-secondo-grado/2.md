You know that $x^2-4x+3\le 0$ holds exactly on $[1,3]$. Which operation on the number line gives the solution of $x^2-4x+3>0$?

- Take the complement of $[1,3]$ in $\mathbb R$
- Keep only $1$ and $3$
- Add the endpoints to the complement of $[1,3]$
- Remove only the endpoints from $[1,3]$

## Explanation

Every real number satisfies exactly one of $P(x)\le0$ and $P(x)>0$. This leaves all points to the left of $1$ or to the right of $3$, excluding both endpoints.
