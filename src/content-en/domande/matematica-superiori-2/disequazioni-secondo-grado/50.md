What is the solution set of $x^2\ge1$ with the constraint $-2\le x\le1$?

- $[-2,-1]\cup\{1\}$
- $[-2,-1]$
- $[-2,-1)\cup\{1\}$
- $[-2,1]$

## Explanation

The condition on the square gives $x\le-1$ or $x\ge1$. The bound $-2\le x\le1$ keeps the stretch from $-2$ to $-1$ and, on the positive branch, just the point $1$. There the square equals exactly $1$, which is allowed. A value between $-1$ and $1$, such as zero, fails the first condition.
