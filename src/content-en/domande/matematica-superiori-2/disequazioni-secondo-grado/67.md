Which real numbers satisfy $\dfrac{x^2+1}{(x-3)^2}>0$?

- $\mathbb R$
- $(3,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{3\}$
- $(-\infty,3)$

## Explanation

The numerator is at least $1$, so it is positive for every real input. The denominator is positive everywhere except at $3$, where it is zero. The quotient is therefore positive on both sides. Only $3$ is excluded, because the fraction is undefined there. The sign stays the same on both approaches to the excluded point.
