Solve $(x-9)^2>0$.

- $\mathbb R$
- $(9,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{9\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

A square is always nonnegative, and is zero exactly when its base is zero. Strict positivity excludes that single value. Every other real number gives a positive square.

$$
(x-9)^2=0\iff x=9
$$

$$
(x-9)^2>0\iff x\ne9
$$
