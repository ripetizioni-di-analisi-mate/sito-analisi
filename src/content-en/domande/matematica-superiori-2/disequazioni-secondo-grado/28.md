What is the solution set of $-(x+4)^2\ge0$?

- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-4\}$
- $\mathbb R$
- $\{-4\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The expression is the negative of a square, so it is always at most zero. It can meet the requested comparison only when it equals zero. This requires $x+4=0$, or $x=-4$. The graph touches the axis there and stays below it on both sides. The solution therefore consists of a single value.
