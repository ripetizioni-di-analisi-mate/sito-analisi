A classmate takes the union of the solutions of $x-1>0$ and $x-4>0$ to solve $(x-1)(x-4)>0$. Which value accepted by the union disproves this method?

- $5$
- $-1$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

At $x=2$ the first factor is positive and the second is negative, so the product is $-2$. A positive product requires two factors with the same sign.
