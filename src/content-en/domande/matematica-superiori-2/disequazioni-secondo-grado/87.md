Solve $x^2>16$.

- $(-4,4)$
- $(4,\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-4)\cup(4,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-4]\cup[4,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Compare the absolute value of $x$ with $4$. The required values are at distance strictly greater than $4$ from the origin.
