For which value of $k$ does $(x-1)^2+k\le0$ have $x=1$ as its only solution?

- $-1$
- $1$
- $0$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The minimum of the square is zero and is attained at $1$. With $k>0$ the minimum is positive and there are no solutions. With $k<0$, points near $1$ also satisfy the inequality. Only $k=0$ leaves a single solution.
