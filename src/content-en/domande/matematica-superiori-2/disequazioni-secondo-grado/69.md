A quadratic with positive leading coefficient has zeros $r$ and $r+4$. For which values of $r$ is the polynomial strictly negative at $x=0$?

- $0<r<4$
- $r<-4$
- $-4<r<0$
- $-4\le r\le0$

## Explanation

The quadratic is negative between its roots. For the coordinate $0$ to lie between them, we need $r<0<r+4$, or $-4<r<0$. At either endpoint the polynomial value would be zero.
