An urn contains $2$ red balls and $5$ blue balls. A ball is drawn and replaced. What is the probability of two blue balls in two independent draws?

- $\dfrac{4}{49}$
- $\dfrac{10}{21}$
- $\dfrac{25}{49}$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}$

## Explanation

With replacement, the probability of blue stays the same on the second draw. Multiply the two equal probabilities to obtain $\dfrac{25}{49}$.
