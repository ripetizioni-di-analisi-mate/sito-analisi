Two coins have heads probabilities $\dfrac23$ and $\dfrac14$ and are tossed independently. What is the probability of heads on the first coin only?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{7}{12}$

## Explanation

You need heads on the first coin and tails on the second. Tails on the second has probability $\dfrac34$. The product along that path is $\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac34=\dfrac12$. The path with tails on the first coin and heads on the second is excluded because the question specifies which coin must show heads.
