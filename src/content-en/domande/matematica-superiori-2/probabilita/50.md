A bag contains $6$ red balls, $13$ blue balls, and $7$ green balls. What is the probability that a ball drawn uniformly is blue or green?

- $\dfrac{20}{19}$
- $\dfrac{3}{13}$
- $\dfrac{10}{13}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The two colors give mutually exclusive events. Add the favorable outcomes and divide by the total number of equally likely balls to obtain $\dfrac{10}{13}$.
