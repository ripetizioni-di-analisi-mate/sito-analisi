A bag contains five red balls and four blue ones. Draw two balls without replacement. Before seeing the first draw, what is the probability that the second is red?

- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{18}$
- $\dfrac{5}{9}$

## Explanation

Both possible first colours are considered. The $RR$ path has probability $\dfrac59\cdot\dfrac48$, and the $BR$ path has probability $\dfrac49\cdot\dfrac58$. Their sum is $\dfrac{40}{72}=\dfrac59$. The two branches have different conditional probabilities, while the second draw on its own retains the initial proportion of reds.
