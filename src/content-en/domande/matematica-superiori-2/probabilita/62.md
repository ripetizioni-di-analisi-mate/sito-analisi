An urn contains $3$ red balls and $7$ blue balls. A ball is drawn and replaced. What is the probability of two blue balls in two independent draws?

- $\dfrac{7}{10}$
- $\dfrac{7}{15}$
- $\dfrac{9}{100}$
- $\dfrac{49}{100}$

## Explanation

With replacement, the probability of blue stays the same on the second draw. Multiply the two equal probabilities to obtain $\dfrac{49}{100}$.
