An urn contains $8$ red balls and $10$ blue balls. Two uniform draws are made, replacing each ball in the urn. What is the probability of two red balls?

- $\dfrac{65}{81}$
- $\dfrac{8}{81}$
- $\dfrac{32}{81}$
- $\dfrac{16}{81}$

## Explanation

Replacement restores the original contents. The probability of red is the same on the second draw and the draws are independent. Multiply the two probabilities.

$$
P(RR)=\frac{8}{18}\cdot\frac{8}{18}=\dfrac{16}{81}
$$
