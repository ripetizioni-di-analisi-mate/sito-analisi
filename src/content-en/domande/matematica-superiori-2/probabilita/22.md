A bag contains $8$ green tiles and some yellow ones. Each tile is equally likely. How many yellow tiles make the probability of yellow $\dfrac35$?

- $5$
- $8$
- $20$
- $12$

## Explanation

Green is the complement of yellow, so its probability is $\dfrac25$. Eight tiles make up two fifths of the total. One fifth corresponds to four tiles, the total is twenty, and twelve are yellow. Checking the result gives $\dfrac{12}{20}=\dfrac35$.
