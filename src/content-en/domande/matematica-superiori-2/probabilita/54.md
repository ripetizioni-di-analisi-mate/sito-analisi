For a fair die, $A$ means “even score” and $B$ means “score of at least $4$.” What does comparing the product with the intersection show?

- They are dependent because $\dfrac13\ne\dfrac14$.
- They are independent because $P(A)=P(B)$.
- They are mutually exclusive because $P(A\cap B)=0$.
- They are independent because the intersection has probability $\dfrac14$.

## Explanation

Each event has three outcomes, so the product of their probabilities is $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac14$. The intersection contains $4$ and $6$, giving $\dfrac26=\dfrac13$. The unequal values rule out independence. The positive intersection also shows that the two events can occur on the same roll.
