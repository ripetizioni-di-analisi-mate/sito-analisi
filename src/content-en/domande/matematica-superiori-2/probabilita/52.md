For two events, $P(A)=\dfrac35$, $P(B)=\dfrac14$, and $P(A\cap B)=\dfrac3{20}$. Which conclusion is correct?

- They are mutually exclusive.
- They are independent and can occur together.
- They are dependent because they overlap.
- They are independent and cover the whole sample space.

## Explanation

The product $\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac14$ equals $\dfrac3{20}$ and matches the probability of the intersection. This is the condition for independence. The intersection has positive probability, so the events can both occur. Their union has probability $\dfrac35+\dfrac14-\dfrac3{20}=\dfrac7{10}$, leaving part of the sample space outside.
