An urn contains $4$ red balls and $9$ blue balls. A ball is drawn and replaced. What is the probability of two blue balls in two independent draws?

- $\dfrac{16}{169}$
- $\dfrac{81}{169}$
- $\dfrac{9}{13}$
- $\dfrac{6}{13}$

## Explanation

With replacement, the probability of blue stays the same on the second draw. Multiply the two equal probabilities to obtain $\dfrac{81}{169}$.
