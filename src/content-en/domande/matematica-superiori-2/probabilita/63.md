A bag contains five yellow balls and five black balls. How much greater is the probability of two yellows with replacement than without replacement?

- $\dfrac{1}{18}$
- $\dfrac{1}{36}$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

With replacement the probability is $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac14$. Without replacement a yellow leaves four yellows out of nine, so the probability is $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac49=\dfrac29$. With common denominator thirty-six the difference is $\dfrac9{36}-\dfrac8{36}=\dfrac1{36}$. Removing a yellow cuts its proportion for the second draw.
