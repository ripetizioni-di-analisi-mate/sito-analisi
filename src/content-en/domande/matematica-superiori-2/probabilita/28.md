Two choices are made. What is the complement of “red is chosen on both occasions”?

- Both choices give a colour other than red.
- At least one choice gives a colour other than red.
- Exactly one choice gives red.
- The first choice gives red.

## Explanation

To negate a conjunction, at least one of its two conditions must fail. Red followed by a different colour is in the complement, as is a different colour followed by red. Two nonred choices also lie in the complement. Only the path with two reds belongs to the original event.
