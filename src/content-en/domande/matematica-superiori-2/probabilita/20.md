In a class of $36$ students, $18$ play sports, $9$ study music, and $4$ do both. What is the probability that a uniformly selected student does at least one of the two activities?

- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{23}{36}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{19}{36}$

## Explanation

Adding the group sizes counts students who do both activities twice. Subtract the intersection once and divide by the total to obtain $\dfrac{23}{36}$.
