For a fair die, event $A$ means “even score” and event $B$ means “score at least $5$.” Which outcome lies in $B$ and outside $A$?

- $6$
- $5$
- $4$
- $3$

## Explanation

The outcomes in $B$ are $5$ and $6$. Of these, $6$ is even and also lies in $A$. This leaves $5$, which satisfies only the second condition. The value $3$ is outside both, while $4$ is outside $B$ and inside $A$.
