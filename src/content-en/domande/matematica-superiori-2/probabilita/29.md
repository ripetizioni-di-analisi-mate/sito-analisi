A coin gives heads with probability $\dfrac14$. In three independent tosses, what is the probability of at least one head?

- $\dfrac{27}{64}$
- $\dfrac{1}{64}$
- $\dfrac{37}{64}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The probability of tails on one toss is $\dfrac34$. Never seeing heads means three tails in a row, with probability $\left(\dfrac34\right)^3=\dfrac{27}{64}$. The requested event includes every other path, so its probability is $\dfrac{37}{64}$. Adding three heads probabilities would count some sequences more than once.
