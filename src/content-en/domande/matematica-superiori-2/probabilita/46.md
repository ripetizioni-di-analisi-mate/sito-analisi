A bag contains $2$ red balls, $5$ blue balls, and $3$ green balls. What is the probability that a ball drawn uniformly is blue or green?

- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{8}{7}$

## Explanation

The two colors give mutually exclusive events. Add the favorable outcomes and divide by the total number of equally likely balls to obtain $\dfrac{4}{5}$.
