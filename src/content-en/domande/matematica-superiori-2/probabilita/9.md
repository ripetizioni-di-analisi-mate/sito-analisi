A box contains $12$ red or blue tokens. A token selected uniformly is red with probability $\dfrac13$. How many tokens are blue?

- $4$
- $9$
- $8$
- $3$

## Explanation

The red tokens are one third of the total, namely $4$. The remaining tokens are all blue, so there are $12-4=8$.

As a check, the $8$ blue tokens make up $\dfrac{8}{12}=\dfrac23$ of the total. Their share plus the one third of red tokens accounts for every token.
