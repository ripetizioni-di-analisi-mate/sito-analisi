An event occurs $13$ times in $29$ trials. What is the observed relative frequency?

- $\dfrac{16}{29}$
- $\dfrac{13}{29}$
- $\dfrac{13}{58}$
- $\dfrac{26}{29}$

## Explanation

Relative frequency is the ratio of the observed number of occurrences to the total number of trials. It describes the collected data and may differ from the theoretical probability.

$$
f=\frac{13}{29}=\dfrac{13}{29}
$$
