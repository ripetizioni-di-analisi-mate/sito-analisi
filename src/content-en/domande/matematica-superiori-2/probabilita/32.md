Of $60$ tiles, $26$ show a circle, $28$ a square, and $10$ both. If one tile is selected uniformly, what is the probability of exactly one of the two symbols?

- $\dfrac{11}{15}$
- $\dfrac{17}{30}$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The ten overlapping tiles are removed from each group. This leaves $16$ with only a circle and $18$ with only a square. These groups are now disjoint and contain a total of $34$ out of $60$ tiles. The union contains $44$, including the ten tiles with both symbols.
