Draw two tiles without replacement from three red tiles, two blue tiles, and one green tile. What is the probability of drawing green exactly once?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{11}{36}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$

## Explanation

Green can come first with probability $\dfrac16$, in which case the second colour is certainly different. If the first tile is a different colour, which has probability $\dfrac56$, green remains among five tiles and the second path has probability $\dfrac56\cdot\dfrac15=\dfrac16$. Adding the two paths gives $\dfrac13$.
