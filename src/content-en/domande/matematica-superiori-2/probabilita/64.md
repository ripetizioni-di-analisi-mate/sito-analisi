An urn contains $5$ red balls and $11$ blue balls. A ball is drawn and replaced. What is the probability of two blue balls in two independent draws?

- $\dfrac{11}{24}$
- $\dfrac{121}{256}$
- $\dfrac{11}{16}$
- $\dfrac{25}{256}$

## Explanation

With replacement, the probability of blue stays the same on the second draw. Multiply the two equal probabilities to obtain $\dfrac{121}{256}$.
