An urn contains $1$ red token and $2$ blue tokens. Two uniform draws are made with replacement. What is the probability of getting different colors?

- $\dfrac29$
- $\dfrac59$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac49$

## Explanation

Red followed by blue has probability $\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac23=\dfrac29$. With replacement, the path blue followed by red has the same product. Add the two disjoint-path probabilities.
