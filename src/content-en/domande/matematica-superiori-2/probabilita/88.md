You know that $P(A)=\dfrac7{10}$ and $P(B)=\dfrac35$. What is the smallest possible value of $P(A\cap B)$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

The union has probability at most one. Since the two probabilities sum to $\dfrac{13}{10}$, the intersection must be at least $\dfrac3{10}$. That minimum is possible. With that overlap, the $A$-only and $B$-only regions have probabilities $\dfrac4{10}$ and $\dfrac3{10}$, and together with the overlap they cover the sample space.
