Draw two balls without replacement from a bag with four red balls and three blue ones. What is the probability that they have the same color?

- $\dfrac{2}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{25}{49}$

## Explanation

The same color can mean two reds or two blues. Their probabilities are $\dfrac47\cdot\dfrac36=\dfrac27$ and $\dfrac37\cdot\dfrac26=\dfrac17$. The denominator on the second draw is six and the count of the color just drawn drops by one. Adding the two paths gives $\dfrac37$.
