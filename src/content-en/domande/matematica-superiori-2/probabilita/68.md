In a finite model, $P(A)>0$ and $P(B)=0$. What value does the conditional probability formula give for $P(A\mid B)$?

- The value is undefined.
- $0$
- $1$
- $P(A)$

## Explanation

The intersection has probability zero because it lies inside $B$. Thus the ratio defining $P(A\mid B)$ would have zero in both numerator and denominator. An event of probability zero cannot serve as the reference set for that ratio. The formula explicitly requires $P(B)>0$.
