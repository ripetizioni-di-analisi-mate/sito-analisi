An urn contains $4$ red balls and $9$ blue balls. Two balls are drawn without replacement. What is the probability of drawing red followed by blue?

- $\dfrac{3}{13}$
- $\dfrac{6}{13}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{36}{169}$

## Explanation

The probability on the first draw uses all the balls. After a red ball is removed, the blue count stays the same and the total decreases by one. The result is $\dfrac{3}{13}$.
