After $50$ trials there are $27$ successes. Ten more trials are added. How many successes are needed in the new block to reach a cumulative frequency of at least $60\%$?

- $6$
- $9$
- $3$
- $10$

## Explanation

There will be $60$ trials at the end. Taking $60\%$ of $60$ gives $36$ successes, so $36-27=9$ more are needed. Eight additional successes would give $35$, below the threshold. Nine gives exactly $\dfrac{36}{60}$, so nine is the required minimum.
