In $130$ trials, an event fails to occur in $60$ cases. What is its relative frequency of occurrence?

- $\dfrac{13}{7}$
- $\dfrac{7}{13}$
- $\dfrac{6}{13}$
- $\dfrac{6}{7}$

## Explanation

Subtract the trials where the event did not occur from the total. Divide the difference by the total number of trials to obtain $\dfrac{7}{13}$.
