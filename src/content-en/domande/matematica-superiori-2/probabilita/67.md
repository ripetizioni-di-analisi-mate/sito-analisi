A participant is chosen uniformly. Two thirds of participants use the online materials. Three fifths of those online users have taken a practice test. What is the probability the selected participant meets both conditions?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{15}$

## Explanation

The first proportion is measured across all participants. The second is restricted to online users. Three fifths of two thirds of the full group is $\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac23=\dfrac25$. For example, out of fifteen participants in these proportions, ten use the online materials and six of those ten have taken a practice test.
