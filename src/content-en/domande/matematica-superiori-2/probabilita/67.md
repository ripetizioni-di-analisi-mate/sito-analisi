An urn contains $3$ red balls and $7$ blue balls. Two balls are drawn without replacement. What is the probability of drawing red followed by blue?

- $\dfrac{7}{15}$
- $\dfrac{7}{30}$
- $\dfrac{21}{100}$
- $\dfrac{7}{9}$

## Explanation

The probability on the first draw uses all the balls. After a red ball is removed, the blue count stays the same and the total decreases by one. The result is $\dfrac{7}{30}$.
