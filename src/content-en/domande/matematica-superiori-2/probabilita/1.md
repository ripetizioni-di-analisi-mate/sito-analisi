An urn contains $6$ red balls and $9$ blue balls that feel identical. One ball is drawn uniformly. What is the probability that it is red?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$

## Explanation

Each ball is equally likely to be drawn. The red balls are the favourable outcomes, and all the balls make up the possible outcomes. The probability is the ratio of these counts.

$$
P(R)=\frac{6}{15}=\dfrac{2}{5}
$$

The probability of blue is $\dfrac9{15}$. Added to the probability of red, it gives one because every ball has one of the two colours.
