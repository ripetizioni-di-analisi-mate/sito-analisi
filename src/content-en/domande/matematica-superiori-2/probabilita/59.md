Two fair dice are rolled independently. What is the probability of getting equal faces or a sum of $3$?

- $\dfrac29$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac1{18}$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

Equal faces give six pairs. The sum $3$ gives $(1,2)$ and $(2,1)$. The two events are mutually exclusive because equal faces give an even sum. The result is $\dfrac{6+2}{36}=\dfrac29$.

With no overlap, there are no common pairs to subtract. The total increases by two from one double to the next, so it always stays even.
