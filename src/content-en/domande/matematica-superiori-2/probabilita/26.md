For a weighted six-sided die, the only known probability is $P(\{6\})=\dfrac13$. What can be determined with certainty?

- $P(\{1\})=\dfrac{2}{15}$
- $P(\{2,4,6\})=\dfrac12$
- $P(\{3,6\})=\dfrac23$
- $P(\{1,2,3,4,5\})=\dfrac23$.

## Explanation

The other five scores make up the complement of rolling a six. Their combined probability is $1-\dfrac13=\dfrac23$. The allocation of this probability among the five scores is unknown, so the probability of one of them or of a proper subset cannot be determined.
