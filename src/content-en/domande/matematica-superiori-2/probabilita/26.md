An urn contains $2$ red balls and $13$ blue balls. Each ball is equally likely to be selected. What is the probability of drawing a ball that is not red?

- $\dfrac{2}{15}$
- $\dfrac{13}{30}$
- $\dfrac{14}{15}$
- $\dfrac{13}{15}$

## Explanation

The desired event is the complement of drawing a red ball. Subtract the probability of red from one. Since there are only two colors, counting the blue balls directly gives the same result.

$$
P(\overline R)=1-\frac{2}{15}=\frac{13}{15}=\dfrac{13}{15}
$$
