Two fair dice are rolled independently. What is the probability that the first score is strictly greater than the second?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{7}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The six tied pairs do not satisfy the strict inequality. Of the thirty remaining pairs, each with the first value greater corresponds to one with the first value smaller by reversing the order. Each group has fifteen outcomes. The denominator is still $36$ because all pairs are possible.
