An urn contains $6$ blue and $4$ red tokens. Two blue tokens are removed, and one token is then drawn uniformly. What is the probability of red?

- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

After removal, four blue and four red tokens remain. Four of the eight outcomes are favorable, so the probability is $\dfrac12$.

Changing the total from ten to eight increases the probability of red because all four favorable tokens remain available. Before removing the blue tokens, the ratio was $\dfrac4{10}$.
