Two distinguishable fair dice are rolled independently. What is the probability that their sum is $8$?

- $\dfrac{31}{36}$
- $\dfrac{5}{36}$
- $\dfrac{5}{72}$
- $\dfrac{5}{18}$

## Explanation

The elementary outcomes are equally likely ordered pairs. Count the pairs that produce the requested sum, since different sums can arise from different numbers of pairs. Divide the number of favorable pairs by thirty-six.

$$
\#\Omega=6\cdot6=36
$$

$$
\#E=5
$$

$$
P(E)=\frac{5}{36}=\dfrac{5}{36}
$$
