Roll a fair die. If it shows $1$, roll it again and keep the **second** result; otherwise keep the first. What is the probability that the result kept is $6$?

- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac7{36}$
- $\dfrac{11}{36}$
- $\dfrac1{36}$

## Explanation

The favourable cases are an immediate six or a one followed by a six. These disjoint events have probabilities $\dfrac16$ and $\dfrac1{36}$.
