A fair die is rolled. If it shows $1$, it is rolled a second time and the second result is kept; otherwise the first result is kept. What is the probability that the result kept is $6$?

- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac7{36}$
- $\dfrac{11}{36}$
- $\dfrac1{36}$

## Explanation

The result kept is $6$ in two cases: a $6$ on the first roll, or a $1$ on the first roll and a $6$ on the second. The first case has probability $\dfrac16$. The two rolls are independent, so the second case has probability $\dfrac16\cdot\dfrac16=\dfrac1{36}$. The two cases are mutually exclusive, therefore their probabilities add up and

$$
P=\frac16+\frac1{36}=\frac{7}{36}.
$$

The answer $\dfrac{11}{36}$ is the probability of at least one $6$ in two rolls that are always made, which also counts sequences in which the second roll does not take place.
