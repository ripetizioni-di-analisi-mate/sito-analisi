Two fair dice are rolled independently. What is the probability that the sum is $4$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{36}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

A sum of four comes from $(1,3)$, $(2,2)$, and $(3,1)$. Order distinguishes the first and last pairs, while reversing the dice leaves the middle pair unchanged. There are three favourable pairs out of thirty-six, giving $\dfrac{3}{36}=\dfrac1{12}$.
