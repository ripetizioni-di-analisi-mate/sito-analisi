An urn contains $2$ red balls and $10$ blue balls. Each ball is equally likely to be selected. What is the probability of drawing a ball that is not red?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{11}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$

## Explanation

The desired event is the complement of drawing a red ball. Subtract the probability of red from one. Since there are only two colors, counting the blue balls directly gives the same result.

$$
P(\overline R)=1-\frac{2}{12}=\frac{10}{12}=\dfrac{5}{6}
$$
