A fair coin has shown tails on eight independent tosses in a row. What is the probability of heads on the ninth toss, given that it is independent of the earlier tosses?

- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{8}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{256}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The first eight tails are observations from completed trials. Independence means knowing those observations leaves the chance of heads on the ninth toss at $\dfrac12$. The number $\dfrac1{256}$ is the probability of seeing eight consecutive tails when computed before all eight tosses.
