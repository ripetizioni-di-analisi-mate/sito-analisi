A fair die is rolled. Let $A$ be the event that the result is even and $B$ the event that the result is less than $4$. What is $P(A\cap B)$?

- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac56$

## Explanation

Of $2$, $4$, and $6$, only $2$ is less than $4$. The intersection thus contains one of the six equally likely outcomes and has probability $\dfrac16$.
