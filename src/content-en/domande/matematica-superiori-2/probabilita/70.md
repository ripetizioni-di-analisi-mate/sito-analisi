An urn contains $6$ red balls and $13$ blue balls. Two balls are drawn without replacement. What is the probability of drawing red followed by blue?

- $\dfrac{13}{57}$
- $\dfrac{13}{18}$
- $\dfrac{26}{57}$
- $\dfrac{78}{361}$

## Explanation

The probability on the first draw uses all the balls. After a red ball is removed, the blue count stays the same and the total decreases by one. The result is $\dfrac{13}{57}$.
