Roll two fair dice independently. Given that their sum is greater than $9$, what is the probability that the first die shows an odd number?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{18}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The condition keeps six pairs, three with sum $10$, two with sum $11$, and one with sum $12$. Only $(5,5)$ and $(5,6)$ have an odd first coordinate. Since the six remaining pairs came from equally likely outcomes, the requested probability is $\dfrac26$. The original thirty-six outcomes are no longer the denominator.
