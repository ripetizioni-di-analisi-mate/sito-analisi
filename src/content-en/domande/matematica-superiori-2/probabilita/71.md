A fair coin is tossed until the first head appears. It is known that the game ended within the third toss. What is the probability that it ended exactly at the second toss?

- $\dfrac27$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac37$

## Explanation

The game ends at the second toss if a tail is followed by a head, and the tosses are independent, so this event has probability $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac14$. The game does not end within the third toss only if three tails appear, which has probability $\dfrac18$, therefore it ends within the third toss with probability $1-\dfrac18=\dfrac78$. Ending at the second toss implies ending within the third, so the intersection of the two events is the first event. By the definition of conditional probability

$$
P=\frac{\frac14}{\frac78}=\frac14\cdot\frac87=\frac27 .
$$

The answer $\dfrac14$ is the probability without the condition.
