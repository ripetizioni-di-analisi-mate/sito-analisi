A fair coin is tossed twice independently. What is the probability of at least one head?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The complementary event is getting only tails. Its probability is a power of one half. Subtract that probability from one to obtain $\dfrac{3}{4}$.

Three sequences are favorable. Two have one head in different positions and the third has two heads. The event does not specify where the first head must occur.
