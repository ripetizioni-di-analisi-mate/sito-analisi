A fair coin is tossed until heads first appears. You learn that the game ended by the third toss. What is the probability that it ended on the second toss?

- $\dfrac27$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac37$

## Explanation

Ending on the second toss requires tails followed by heads, with probability $\dfrac14$. Ending by the third toss excludes only three consecutive tails, giving probability $\dfrac78$. The conditional probability is $\dfrac14\div\dfrac78=\dfrac27$.
