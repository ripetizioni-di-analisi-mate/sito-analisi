A fair coin is tossed $2$ times independently. What is the probability of at least one head?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The complementary event is getting only tails. Its probability is a power of one half. Subtract that probability from one to obtain $\dfrac{3}{4}$.
