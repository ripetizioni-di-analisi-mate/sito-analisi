A fair coin is tossed $5$ times independently. What is the probability of at least one head?

- $\dfrac{1}{32}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{31}{32}$

## Explanation

The complementary event is getting only tails. Its probability is a power of one half. Subtract that probability from one to obtain $\dfrac{31}{32}$.
