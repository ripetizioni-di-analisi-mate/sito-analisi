A fair die and a fair coin are used independently. What is the probability of getting $3$ on the die and heads on the coin?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The die has one favourable outcome out of six, and the coin one out of two. Since the results are independent, the probabilities are multiplied, giving $\dfrac{1}{12}$.

The outcomes can also be listed as pairs. Each die score combines with two coin outcomes. Among these twelve pairs, only one contains the score three and heads in the specified positions.
