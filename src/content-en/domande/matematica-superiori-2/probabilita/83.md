In $90$ trials, an event fails to occur in $40$ cases. What is its relative frequency of occurrence?

- $\dfrac{9}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$

## Explanation

Subtract the trials where the event did not occur from the total. Divide the difference by the total number of trials to obtain $\dfrac{5}{9}$.
