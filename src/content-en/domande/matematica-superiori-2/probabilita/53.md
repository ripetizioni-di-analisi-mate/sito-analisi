Events $A$ and $B$ are independent. If $P(A)=\dfrac45$ and $P(A\cap B)=\dfrac15$, what is $P(B)$?

- $\dfrac{4}{25}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

Independence gives $\dfrac45 P(B)=\dfrac15$. Dividing by $\dfrac45$ multiplies $\dfrac15$ by $\dfrac54$, giving $\dfrac14$. Substituting the result back into the equation confirms that the joint probability is $\dfrac15$. The division is possible because $P(A)$ is positive.
