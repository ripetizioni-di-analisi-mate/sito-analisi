A bag contains $3$ red balls, $7$ blue balls, and $4$ green balls. What is the probability that a ball drawn uniformly is blue or green?

- $\dfrac{11}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{11}{14}$
- $\dfrac{3}{14}$

## Explanation

The two colors give mutually exclusive events. Add the favorable outcomes and divide by the total number of equally likely balls to obtain $\dfrac{11}{14}$.
