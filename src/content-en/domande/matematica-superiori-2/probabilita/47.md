A fair coin and a fair die are used independently. What is the probability of heads and a score strictly greater than $4$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$

## Explanation

The favorable outcomes are two pairs, one with heads and $5$ and one with heads and $6$. There are $2\cdot6=12$ equally likely pairs altogether, giving $\dfrac2{12}$. Multiplying the probabilities gives the same result because heads has probability $\dfrac12$ and a score greater than $4$ has probability $\dfrac26$.
