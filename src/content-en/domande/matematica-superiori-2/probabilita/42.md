If $P(E)=\dfrac25$, what is $P(\overline E)$?

- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac35$

## Explanation

An event and its complement are disjoint and cover all outcomes. Their probabilities therefore add to one. Subtracting two fifths from one gives three fifths.

Picture a model with five equally likely outcomes, two inside $E$ and three outside. The two groups together contain all five outcomes and have complementary probabilities.
