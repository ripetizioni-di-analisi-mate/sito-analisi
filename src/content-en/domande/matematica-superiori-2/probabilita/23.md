In a group of $24$ students, $11$ study French, $10$ study English, and $6$ study neither. How many study both?

- $3$
- $5$
- $6$
- $18$

## Explanation

There are $24-6=18$ students who study at least one language. The sum of the two counts is $21$, so their intersection contains $21-18=3$ students.

Removing the shared three leaves eight in French only and seven in English only. The total $8+3+7+6=24$ reconstructs the whole group.
