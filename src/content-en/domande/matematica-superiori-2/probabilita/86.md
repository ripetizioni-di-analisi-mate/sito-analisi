A fair die is rolled. How are the events “rolling $2$” and “rolling a number other than $2$” related?

- Both are impossible.
- They are the same event.
- They can occur together.
- They are complementary.

## Explanation

Every outcome belongs to exactly one of the two events. Their union is the entire outcome space, and their intersection is empty.

The two conditions are complements. The first event contains one outcome and the second contains five. Their probabilities are $\dfrac16$ and $\dfrac56$, which add to one.
