A number from $1$ to $18$ is selected uniformly. Given that it is a multiple of $3$, what is the probability that it is greater than $10$?

- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac49$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The given information restricts the outcomes to $3$, $6$, $9$, $12$, $15$, and $18$. Three of them are greater than $10$. The ratio is $\dfrac36=\dfrac12$.
