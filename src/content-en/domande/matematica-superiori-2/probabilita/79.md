Two fair coins are tossed independently. Given that at least one head appeared, what is the probability that two heads appeared?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac13$

## Explanation

The equally likely outcomes are the ordered pairs $HH$, $HT$, $TH$ and $TT$. The information rules out only $TT$, so three equally likely outcomes remain. Among them only $HH$ has two heads, therefore the conditional probability is $\dfrac13$. The answer $\dfrac12$ comes from treating the two cases “one head” and “two heads” as equally likely, while $\dfrac23$ is the probability of exactly one head under the same condition.
