A box holds two counters, each either red or blue. One randomly chosen counter is revealed to be red. Can the probability that the other is red be calculated from this information alone?

- Yes, it is always $\dfrac12$
- Yes, it is always $1$
- Yes, it is always $0$
- No, the way the colours were chosen is needed

## Explanation

If the box is always prepared with two reds, the other counter is certainly red. If it always holds one of each colour, the other is blue. Both preparations allow the observation.
