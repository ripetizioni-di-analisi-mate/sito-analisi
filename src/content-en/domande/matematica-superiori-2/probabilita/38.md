A coin with probability of heads $\dfrac23$ is tossed twice independently. What is the probability of exactly one head?

- $\dfrac29$
- $\dfrac89$
- $\dfrac19$
- $\dfrac49$

## Explanation

Each order has probability $\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac13=\dfrac29$. Heads then tails and tails then heads are distinct outcomes, so the two probabilities add.
