Draw two balls without replacement from four red and three blue balls. You know the second ball is blue. What is the probability that the first was red?

- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $\dfrac{2}{7}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

Label the balls. With a blue second draw there are three choices for the second ball and six for the first, giving eighteen pairs. To have a red first draw, each of the three blue choices leaves four possibilities for the first, giving twelve favourable pairs. The ratio is $\dfrac{12}{18}=\dfrac23$. The conditioned position can be the last draw.
