A fair coin is tossed $6$ times independently. What is the probability of at least one head?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{63}{64}$
- $\dfrac{1}{64}$

## Explanation

The complementary event is getting only tails. Its probability is a power of one half. Subtract that probability from one to obtain $\dfrac{63}{64}$.
