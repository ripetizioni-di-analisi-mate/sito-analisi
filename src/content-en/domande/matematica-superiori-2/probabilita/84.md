After $20$ trials there are $14$ successes. Trial twenty-one fails. By how much does the relative frequency of success decrease?

- $\dfrac{1}{21}$
- $\dfrac{1}{20}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{30}$

## Explanation

The numerator remains $14$, while the total rises from $20$ to $21$. The frequency drops from $\dfrac7{10}$ to $\dfrac23$. On a common denominator of thirty, the decrease is $\dfrac{21}{30}-\dfrac{20}{30}=\dfrac1{30}$. The new ratio $\dfrac23$ is the latest frequency, while the question asks for the difference between the two ratios.
