In $110$ trials, an event fails to occur in $50$ cases. What is its relative frequency of occurrence?

- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{5}{11}$
- $\dfrac{11}{6}$
- $\dfrac{6}{11}$

## Explanation

Subtract the trials where the event did not occur from the total. Divide the difference by the total number of trials to obtain $\dfrac{6}{11}$.
