An urn contains $8$ red balls and $3$ blue balls. Two balls are drawn uniformly without replacement. What is the probability that both are red?

- $\dfrac{28}{55}$
- $\dfrac{27}{55}$
- $\dfrac{14}{55}$
- $\dfrac{57}{110}$

## Explanation

After a red ball is drawn, one fewer red ball and one fewer ball in total remain. The second probability must therefore use the updated counts. Their product accounts for the order and dependence of the two draws.

$$
P(RR)=\frac{8}{11}\cdot\frac{7}{10}=\dfrac{28}{55}
$$
