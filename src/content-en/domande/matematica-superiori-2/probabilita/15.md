A trial consists of drawing a tile numbered from $1$ to $4$ and tossing a coin. Which event includes the outcome $(3,H)$, where $H$ means heads?

- An odd number and heads.
- An even number or tails.
- A number of at least four.
- An odd number and tails.

## Explanation

The two coordinates are read separately. The first is the drawn number, $3$, which is odd. The second is heads. The outcome therefore satisfies both parts of the event. That event also contains $(1,H)$ because $1$ is odd as well and has the same second coordinate.
