Choose one card uniformly from $40$. Which value of $k$ makes characteristics $A$ and $B$ independent?

| Cards | $B$ | Outside $B$ | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| $A$ | $k$ | $20-k$ | 20 |
| Outside $A$ | $12-k$ | $8+k$ | 20 |
| Total | 12 | 28 | 40 |

- $5$
- $6$
- $10$
- $12$

## Explanation

The margins give $P(A)=\dfrac{20}{40}$ and $P(B)=\dfrac{12}{40}$. Their product is $\dfrac3{20}$, which equals $\dfrac6{40}$. The overlap cell must therefore contain six cards. At that value, half of the twelve cards in $B$ lie in $A$, the same proportion as in the whole sample.
