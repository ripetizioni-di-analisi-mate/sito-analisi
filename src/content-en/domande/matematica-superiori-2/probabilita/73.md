A fair coin is tossed $4$ times independently. What is the probability of at least one head?

- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{15}{16}$

## Explanation

The complementary event is getting only tails. Its probability is a power of one half. Subtract that probability from one to obtain $\dfrac{15}{16}$.
