Two labs perform the same check. Lab $A$ receives two thirds of the samples and half of its samples test positive. At lab $B$ a quarter of its samples test positive. Given that a uniformly selected sample tested positive, what is the probability it came from $A$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$

## Explanation

Out of all samples, the positive ones at $A$ have probability $\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac13$. The positives at $B$ have probability $\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac14=\dfrac1{12}$. Knowing a sample tested positive restricts the reference group to the overall positive share $\dfrac5{12}$. The proportion from $A$ is $\dfrac{\frac13}{\frac5{12}}=\dfrac45$.
