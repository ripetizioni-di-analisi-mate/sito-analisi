In a group of $40$ trials the success frequency is $50\%$. In another $10$ trials the success frequency is $80\%$. What is the overall frequency?

- $65\%$
- $58\%$
- $62\%$
- $56\%$

## Explanation

The first group has $20$ successes and the second has $8$. There are $50$ trials altogether, so calculate $\dfrac{28}{50}=0.56$. The two frequencies carry different weights because the group of forty trials is four times as large. That makes the result closer to $50\%$.
