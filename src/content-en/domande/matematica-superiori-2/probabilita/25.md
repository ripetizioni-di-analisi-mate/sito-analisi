An urn contains $9$ red balls and $12$ blue balls that feel identical. One ball is drawn uniformly. What is the probability that it is red?

- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $\dfrac{3}{14}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{6}{7}$

## Explanation

Each ball is equally likely to be drawn. The red balls are the favorable outcomes, and all the balls make up the possible outcomes. The probability is the ratio of these counts.

$$
P(R)=\frac{9}{21}=\dfrac{3}{7}
$$
