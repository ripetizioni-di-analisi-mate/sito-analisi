Choose an integer uniformly from $1$ to $24$. What is the probability that it is a multiple of both $4$ and $6$?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$

## Explanation

Both conditions must hold. The least common multiple of $4$ and $6$ is $12$, so the favorable outcomes are $12$ and $24$. Divide by all twenty-four original outcomes to get $\dfrac2{24}$. Include $24$, since it is an endpoint of the stated range.
