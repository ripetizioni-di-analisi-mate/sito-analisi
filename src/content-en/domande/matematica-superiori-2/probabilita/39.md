Two fair dice are rolled independently. What is the probability that the scores differ by exactly two, in either order?

- $\dfrac{2}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{5}{18}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

With the first score smaller, there are four pairs, from $(1;3)$ to $(4;6)$. Each gives a second pair by reversing the order, so there are eight favourable outcomes. No pair equals its reverse because the scores differ. Out of $36$ equally likely outcomes the probability is $\dfrac8{36}=\dfrac29$.
