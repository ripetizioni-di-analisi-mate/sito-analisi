Events $A$ and $B$ are independent, with $P(A)=\dfrac25$ and $P(B)=\dfrac14$. What is the probability that neither occurs?

- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{9}{20}$
- $\dfrac{7}{20}$
- $\dfrac{11}{20}$

## Explanation

First calculate $P(A\cap B)=\dfrac25\cdot\dfrac14=\dfrac1{10}$. The union gives $\dfrac25+\dfrac14-\dfrac1{10}=\dfrac{11}{20}$. Neither occurring is the complement of the union, so its probability is $\dfrac9{20}$. Multiplying the two complementary probabilities gives the same result.
