An integer from $1$ to $78$ is selected uniformly. What is the probability that it is a multiple of $3$ and not of $2$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Every block of six consecutive integers contains two multiples of three. Exactly one is odd, giving $\dfrac{1}{6}$.
