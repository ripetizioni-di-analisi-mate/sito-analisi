A card numbered from $1$ to $15$ is drawn uniformly. What is the probability of getting an even number?

- $\dfrac{7}{15}$
- $\dfrac{8}{15}$
- $\dfrac{7}{30}$
- $\dfrac{14}{15}$

## Explanation

The cards are equally likely. Count the even numbers in the stated range, including either endpoint when it is even. Divide by the total number of cards.

$$
\#E=7,\qquad\#\Omega=15
$$

$$
P(E)=\frac{7}{15}=\dfrac{7}{15}
$$
