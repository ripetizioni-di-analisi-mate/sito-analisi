Of $25$ singers, $12$ study piano, $10$ study guitar, and $4$ study both. What is the probability that a uniformly selected singer studies exactly one instrument?

- $\dfrac{18}{25}$
- $\dfrac{22}{25}$
- $\dfrac{14}{25}$
- $\dfrac4{25}$

## Explanation

There are $12-4=8$ piano-only students and $10-4=6$ guitar-only students. The resulting groups are disjoint, so there are $8+6=14$ favourable outcomes out of $25$.

Adding the two totals and subtracting one copy of the overlap would give the union. Here we subtract both copies of the four students who study both instruments.
