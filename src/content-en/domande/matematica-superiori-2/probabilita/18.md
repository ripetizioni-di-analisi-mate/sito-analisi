Of $30$ people, $12$ have subscription A, $16$ have subscription B, and $5$ have both. What is the probability that a uniformly selected person has neither?

- $\dfrac{23}{30}$
- $\dfrac2{30}$
- $\dfrac5{30}$
- $\dfrac7{30}$

## Explanation

The union has $12+16-5=23$ people, with the five people in both groups counted once. The complement has $30-23=7$ people.

The four regions contain $7$ subscribers to A alone, $11$ to B alone, $5$ to both, and $7$ to neither. Divide the last count by all thirty people.
