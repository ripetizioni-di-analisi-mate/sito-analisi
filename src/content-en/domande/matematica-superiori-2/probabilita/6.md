An urn contains $6$ red balls and $8$ blue balls. Two uniform draws are made, replacing each ball in the urn. What is the probability of two red balls?

- $\dfrac{40}{49}$
- $\dfrac{9}{98}$
- $\dfrac{18}{49}$
- $\dfrac{9}{49}$

## Explanation

Replacement restores the original contents. The probability of red is the same on the second draw and the draws are independent. Multiply the two probabilities.

$$
P(RR)=\frac{6}{14}\cdot\frac{6}{14}=\dfrac{9}{49}
$$
