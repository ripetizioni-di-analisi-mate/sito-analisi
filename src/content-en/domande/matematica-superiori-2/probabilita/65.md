Choose one activity at random from the table. Given that it was done in the afternoon, what is the probability it was successful?

| Time | Successful | Unsuccessful | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Morning | 16 | 4 | 20 |
| Afternoon | 8 | 12 | 20 |
| Total | 24 | 16 | 40 |

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

The condition fixes the afternoon row, so use only its twenty activities. Eight were successful, giving the ratio $\dfrac8{20}=\dfrac25$. Dividing by $24$ would answer a different question, namely the share of successful activities that took place in the afternoon.
