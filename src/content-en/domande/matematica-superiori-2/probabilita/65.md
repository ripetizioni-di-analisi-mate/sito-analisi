An urn contains $6$ red balls and $13$ blue balls. A ball is drawn and replaced. What is the probability of two blue balls in two independent draws?

- $\dfrac{26}{57}$
- $\dfrac{36}{361}$
- $\dfrac{169}{361}$
- $\dfrac{13}{19}$

## Explanation

With replacement, the probability of blue stays the same on the second draw. Multiply the two equal probabilities to obtain $\dfrac{169}{361}$.
