A device selects a point uniformly along a spinner’s circumference. The blue sector covers $150^\circ$ and the green one $60^\circ$. The rest is red. What is the probability of red?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{7}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The probability is the fraction of the turn covered by the sector. Blue and green together occupy $210^\circ$, leaving $360^\circ-210^\circ=150^\circ$ for red. The ratio is $\dfrac{150}{360}=\dfrac5{12}$. Red has the same probability as blue because the two sectors have equal angles.
