In a class of $52$ students, $26$ play sports, $13$ study music, and $6$ do both. What is the probability that a uniformly selected student does at least one of the two activities?

- $\dfrac{3}{26}$
- $\dfrac{33}{52}$
- $\dfrac{27}{52}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

Adding the group sizes counts students who do both activities twice. Subtract the intersection once and divide by the total to obtain $\dfrac{33}{52}$.
