A wheel is divided into three circular sectors: the blue sector has an angle of $150^\circ$, the green one $60^\circ$ and the rest is red. The wheel is spun in front of a fixed pointer and, when the wheel stops, every point of the rim has the same probability of being under the pointer. What is the probability that the pointer indicates the red sector?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{7}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

Every point of the rim has the same probability, so the probability of a sector is the fraction of a full turn that it occupies. Blue and green together occupy $210^\circ$, hence red gets $360^\circ-210^\circ=150^\circ$. The probability of red is therefore $\dfrac{150}{360}=\dfrac{5}{12}$. Red has the same probability as blue because the two sectors have the same angle.
