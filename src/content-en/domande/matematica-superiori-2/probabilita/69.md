An urn contains $2$ red balls and $3$ blue balls. Two uniform draws are made without replacement. What is the probability of two balls of the same color?

- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac1{10}$

## Explanation

Add the probabilities of the paths with two red balls and two blue balls. For the second draw, update the counts using the color drawn first.

$$
\begin{aligned}
P(RR)+P(BB)&=\frac25\cdot\frac14+\frac35\cdot\frac24=\frac25.
\end{aligned}
$$
