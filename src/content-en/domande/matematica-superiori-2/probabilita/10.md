A fair coin is tossed three times independently. What is the probability of at least one tail?

- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac78$

## Explanation

Three heads is the only complementary outcome. Independence gives it probability $\left(\dfrac12\right)^3=\dfrac18$. The requested probability is therefore $\dfrac78$.

There are eight ordered sequences. All three sequences with just one tail, one for each position, belong to the event. The sequence with three tails is also included.
