Two events satisfy $B\subseteq A$, with $P(A)=\dfrac58$ and $P(B)=\dfrac14$. What is the probability of $A\setminus B$?

- $\dfrac{7}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$

## Explanation

Because $B$ is contained in $A$, the event $A$ splits into two disjoint regions, $B$ and $A\setminus B$. Subtracting the probability of the first region from the total gives $\dfrac58-\dfrac28=\dfrac38$. Adding it back to $P(B)$ recovers the total probability of $A$.
