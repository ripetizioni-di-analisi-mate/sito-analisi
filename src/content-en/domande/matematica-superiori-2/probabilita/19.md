In a class of $28$ students, $14$ play sports, $7$ study music, and $3$ do both. What is the probability that a uniformly selected student does at least one of the two activities?

- $\dfrac{9}{14}$
- $\dfrac{15}{28}$
- $\dfrac{3}{28}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

Adding the group sizes counts students who do both activities twice. Subtract the intersection once and divide by the total to obtain $\dfrac{9}{14}$.
