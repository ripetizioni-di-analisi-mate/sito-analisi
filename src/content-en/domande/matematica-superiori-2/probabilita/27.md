A card numbered from $1$ to $21$ is drawn uniformly. What is the probability of getting an even number?

- $\dfrac{10}{21}$
- $\dfrac{11}{21}$
- $\dfrac{5}{21}$
- $\dfrac{20}{21}$

## Explanation

The cards are equally likely. Count the even numbers in the stated range, including either endpoint when it is even. Divide by the total number of cards.

$$
\#E=10,\qquad\#\Omega=21
$$

$$
P(E)=\frac{10}{21}=\dfrac{10}{21}
$$
