A data collection system makes two independent readings. The success probabilities are $\dfrac25$ and $\dfrac34$. What is the probability of at least one success?

- $\dfrac{17}{20}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{11}{20}$

## Explanation

The complement is that both readings fail. The two failure probabilities are $\dfrac35$ and $\dfrac14$. Independence gives their product, $\dfrac3{20}$. Subtracting it from one gives $\dfrac{17}{20}$. The case with two successes belongs to the requested event.
