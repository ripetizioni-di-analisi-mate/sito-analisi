In three coin tosses only the number of heads is recorded. Which of the listed counts groups together exactly three ordered outcomes?

- $0$
- $2$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

Two heads leave exactly one tail. It can be first, second, or third, giving three distinct outcomes. The count zero requires three tails, and the count three requires three heads. Each of those has one ordered outcome. Four heads are impossible in three tosses.
