Two fair dice are rolled independently. What is the probability that the sum is $8$?

- $\dfrac{5}{36}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{36}$

## Explanation

For a sum from four to eight, count the ordered pairs of allowed values. Divide the number of favorable pairs by thirty-six to obtain $\dfrac{5}{36}$.
