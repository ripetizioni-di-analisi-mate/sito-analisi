A bag has four blue balls and some red balls. After drawing and keeping a blue ball, the chance of drawing a second blue is $\dfrac13$. How many balls were originally in the bag?

- $10$
- $9$
- $12$
- $13$

## Explanation

Three blue balls remain after the first draw. If they are one third of the remaining balls, the new total is nine. There was one more ball before the first draw, so the original total was ten. With this total the conditional probability is indeed $\dfrac39=\dfrac13$.
