A bag contains $4$ red balls, $9$ blue balls, and $5$ green balls. What is the probability that a ball drawn uniformly is blue or green?

- $\dfrac{7}{9}$
- $\dfrac{14}{13}$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The two colors give mutually exclusive events. Add the favorable outcomes and divide by the total number of equally likely balls to obtain $\dfrac{7}{9}$.
