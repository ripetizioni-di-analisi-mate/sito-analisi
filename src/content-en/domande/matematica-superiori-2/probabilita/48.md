Route $A$ is selected with probability $\dfrac23$, and route $B$ otherwise. The chance of success on the chosen route is $\dfrac35$ on $A$ and $\dfrac15$ on $B$. What is the overall probability of success?

- $\dfrac{7}{15}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

Success can come through the $A$ branch or the $B$ branch. The contributions are $\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac35=\dfrac25$ and $\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac15=\dfrac1{15}$. The initial choices are mutually exclusive, so the contributions add to $\dfrac7{15}$. Each success rate must be weighted by the probability of taking its route.
