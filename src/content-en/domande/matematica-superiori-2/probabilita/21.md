Six equally likely tickets display $1,1,2,3,3,3$. What is the probability of reading the most frequent number?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The elementary outcomes are the individual tickets. The most frequent number is $3$, which appears on three of the six tickets. Its probability is $\dfrac36=\dfrac12$. The three distinct numbers have different weights because they are not written on equal numbers of tickets.
