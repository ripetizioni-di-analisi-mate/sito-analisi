A bag contains $3$ red balls and $4$ blue ones. Draw two balls uniformly without replacement. Which calculation gives the probability of at least one blue?

- $1-\dfrac37\cdot\dfrac26$
- $1-\left(\dfrac37\right)^2$
- $1-\dfrac47\cdot\dfrac36$
- $\dfrac47\cdot\dfrac36+\dfrac37\cdot\dfrac46$

## Explanation

The excluded event is drawing two reds. The first draw has three reds out of seven. After a red, two of the six remaining balls are red, so multiply $\dfrac37$ and $\dfrac26$. Drawing without replacement explains why the second factor changes. Subtract the product from one to include all paths with at least one blue.
