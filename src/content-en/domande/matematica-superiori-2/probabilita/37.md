The outcomes of a fair die are split into three pairwise mutually exclusive events $A$, $B$, and $C$. If $P(A)=\dfrac12$ and $P(B)=\dfrac13$, what is $P(C)$?

- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The events cover the whole sample space, so their probabilities add to one. Event $A$ contains three of the six outcomes, and $B$ contains two. This leaves one outcome for $C$, with probability $\dfrac16$. Mutual exclusivity means the counts can be subtracted without adjusting for overlaps.
