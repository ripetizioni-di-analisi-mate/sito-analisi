An integer from $1$ to $16$ is selected uniformly. What is the probability that it is a multiple of $2$ or of $3$?

- $\dfrac{5}{16}$
- $\dfrac{11}{32}$
- $\dfrac{11}{16}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

Multiples of six belong to both events. Adding the two counts would include them twice, so subtract the intersection once. Divide the count of the union by the total number of available integers.

$$
\#(A\cup B)=8+5-2=11
$$

$$
P(A\cup B)=\frac{11}{16}=\dfrac{11}{16}
$$
