Choose an integer uniformly from $1$ to $12$. Let $A$ mean “multiple of $6$” and $B$ mean “even.” What are $P(B\mid A)$ and $P(A\mid B)$, in that order?

- $\left(\dfrac13;1\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac16;\dfrac12\right)$
- $\left(1;\dfrac16\right)$
- $\left(1;\dfrac13\right)$

## Explanation

The multiples of $6$ are $6$ and $12$, both even, so given $A$ the probability of $B$ is one. Given $B$, look at all six even numbers, which still include those same two multiples. The result is $\dfrac26=\dfrac13$. The reference group for the ratio has changed.
