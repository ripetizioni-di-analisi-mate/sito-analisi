In $75$ trials the exact frequency of an event is $36\%$. How many more times did it occur than a count of $25$ successes?

- $11$
- $27$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The $36\%$ is the share of successes in seventy-five trials. Multiplying $\dfrac{36}{100}$ by $75$ gives $27$ successes. The question compares counts, so the difference is $27-25=2$. Comparing the frequencies instead of the counts would give a difference of $\dfrac2{75}$.
