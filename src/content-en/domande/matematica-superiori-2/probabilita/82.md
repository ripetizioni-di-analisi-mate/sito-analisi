In $70$ trials, an event fails to occur in $30$ cases. What is its relative frequency of occurrence?

- $\dfrac{7}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$

## Explanation

Subtract the trials where the event did not occur from the total. Divide the difference by the total number of trials to obtain $\dfrac{4}{7}$.
