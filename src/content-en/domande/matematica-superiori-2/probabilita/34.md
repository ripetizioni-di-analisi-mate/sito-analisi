A fair die and a fair coin are used independently. What is the probability of getting $4$ on the die and heads on the coin?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$

## Explanation

The die has one favorable outcome out of six, and the coin one out of two. Since the results are independent, multiply the probabilities to obtain $\dfrac{1}{12}$.
