An urn contains $2$ red balls and $5$ blue balls. Two balls are drawn without replacement. What is the probability of drawing red followed by blue?

- $\dfrac{10}{49}$
- $\dfrac{10}{21}$
- $\dfrac{5}{21}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$

## Explanation

The probability on the first draw uses all the balls. After a red ball is removed, the blue count stays the same and the total decreases by one. The result is $\dfrac{5}{21}$.

The product is $\dfrac27\cdot\dfrac56$. The blue-then-red path would have the same weight, but is the opposite order and stays out of this calculation.
