An urn contains $2$ red balls and $5$ blue balls. The first ball drawn is returned only if it is red. What is the probability that the second draw is red? Each draw is uniform among the balls present.

- $\dfrac27$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac{47}{147}$
- $\dfrac{5}{21}$

## Explanation

After a red first draw the next red probability is $\dfrac27$. After a blue first draw two of the six remaining balls are red. Weighting the cases gives $\dfrac27\cdot\dfrac27+\dfrac57\cdot\dfrac26=\dfrac{47}{147}$.
