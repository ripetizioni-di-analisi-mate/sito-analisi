An urn contains $3$ red balls and $2$ blue balls. Three balls are drawn one after another, without replacement. What is the probability of drawing, in this order, a red ball, a blue ball and a red ball?

- $\dfrac{18}{125}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac35$

## Explanation

At the first draw there are $3$ red balls out of $5$, so the probability of red is $\dfrac35$. After a red ball, $2$ red and $2$ blue balls remain, therefore the probability of blue is $\dfrac24$. After a red and a blue ball, $2$ red balls and $1$ blue ball remain, so the probability of red is $\dfrac23$. The probability of the sequence is the product of the probabilities along the path of the tree, therefore

$$
P=\frac35\cdot\frac24\cdot\frac23=\frac{12}{60}=\frac15 .
$$

The answer $\dfrac{18}{125}$ would be obtained by putting the ball back in the urn each time.
