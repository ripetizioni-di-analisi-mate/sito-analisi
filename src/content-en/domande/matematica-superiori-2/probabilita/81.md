In $50$ trials, an event fails to occur in $20$ cases. What is its relative frequency of occurrence?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

Subtract the trials where the event did not occur from the total. Divide the difference by the total number of trials to obtain $\dfrac{3}{5}$.

The observed frequency of the complementary event is $\dfrac{20}{50}$. The two frequencies add to one because each of the fifty trials belongs to exactly one category.
