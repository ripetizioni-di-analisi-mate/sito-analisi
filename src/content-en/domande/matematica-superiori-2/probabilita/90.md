In two independent rolls of a fair die, what is the probability of seeing a $6$ exactly once?

- $\dfrac{11}{36}$
- $\dfrac{5}{36}$
- $\dfrac{5}{18}$
- $\dfrac{1}{36}$

## Explanation

There are two paths. The six may come first and be followed by another score, or it may come second after another score. Each path has probability $\dfrac16\cdot\dfrac56=\dfrac5{36}$. Adding them gives $\dfrac{10}{36}=\dfrac5{18}$. Two sixes are excluded because the six would appear twice.
