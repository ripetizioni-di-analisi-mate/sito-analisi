A fair die is rolled. How are the events “rolling $6$” and “rolling a number other than $6$” related?

- They are the same event.
- Both are impossible.
- They are complementary.
- They can occur together.

## Explanation

Every outcome belongs to exactly one of the two events. Their union is the entire outcome space, and their intersection is empty.
