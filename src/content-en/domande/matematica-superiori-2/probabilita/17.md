A spinner has sectors $A,B,C$. Spin it twice and record the order. Which set represents “sector $A$ at least once and sector $C$ never”?

- $\{AB,BA\}$
- $\{AA,AB,AC,BA,CA\}$
- $\{AA,AB,BA\}$
- $\{AA,BB\}$

## Explanation

The second condition leaves only sectors $A$ and $B$, so start with $AA,AB,BA,BB$. The first condition requires at least one $A$ and removes $BB$. The outcome $AA$ is allowed because it contains two $A$s. List both $AB$ and $BA$ because the trial records the order.
