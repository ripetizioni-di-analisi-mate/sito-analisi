An integer from $1$ to $30$ is selected uniformly. What is the probability that it is a multiple of $3$ and not of $2$?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

Every block of six consecutive integers contains two multiples of three. Exactly one is odd, giving $\dfrac{1}{6}$.

The favourable scores are $3$, $9$, $15$, $21$, and $27$. The number $30$ is possible but even, so it counts in the denominator without being favourable.
