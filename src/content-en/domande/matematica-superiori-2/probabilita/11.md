An integer from $10$ to $18$ inclusive is selected uniformly. What is the probability of a multiple of $3$?

- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac29$
- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac49$

## Explanation

The eligible multiples of $3$ are $12$, $15$, and $18$. There are $18-10+1=9$ integers between the inclusive endpoints. The ratio is $\dfrac39=\dfrac13$.

The first multiple comes two numbers after the lower endpoint. The last multiple equals the upper endpoint, so it is included. The ratio counts the tickets, each once.
