In $20$ tosses of a coin assumed fair by the model, you observe $7$ heads. Which pair gives the frequency first and the theoretical probability second?

- $\left(\dfrac12;\dfrac7{20}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac7{20};\dfrac7{20}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{13}{20};\dfrac12\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac7{20};\dfrac12\right)$

## Explanation

The observed proportion comes from the data and is $\dfrac7{20}$. The fair coin model assigns probability $\dfrac12$ to heads on each toss. The two values have different roles. Evaluating the assumption would require comparing the expected variability in the data with what was actually observed.
