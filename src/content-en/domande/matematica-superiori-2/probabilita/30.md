A fair die and a fair coin are used independently. What is the probability of getting heads or an even number, including the outcome when both occur?

- $\dfrac12$
- $1$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

The complementary event requires tails and an odd number. Each has probability $\dfrac12$, and the two trials are independent. Thus the complement has probability $\dfrac14$ and the requested event has probability $\dfrac34$.
