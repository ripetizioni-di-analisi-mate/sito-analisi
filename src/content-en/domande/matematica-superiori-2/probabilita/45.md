After a draw without replacement, how should the probability on the second draw be calculated?

- $\text{Always use the initial fraction}$
- $\text{Double the initial probability}$
- $\text{Update the contents of the urn}$
- $\text{Set the probability equal to one}$

## Explanation

The drawn ball is no longer available. The total number and the count of the drawn color change. The second probability must use these updated counts.
