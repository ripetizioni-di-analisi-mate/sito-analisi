Draw twice uniformly from a bag without replacement. How do you find the probability of a given color on the second draw, knowing the color of the first?

- $\text{Always use the initial fraction}$
- $\text{Double the initial probability}$
- $\text{Update the contents of the urn}$
- $\text{Set the probability equal to one}$

## Explanation

The drawn ball is no longer available. The total number and the count of the drawn color change. The second probability must use these updated counts.

For example, observing a blue draw from three blues and two reds leaves two blues out of four. The probability of blue on the second draw is $\dfrac24$ on that branch.
