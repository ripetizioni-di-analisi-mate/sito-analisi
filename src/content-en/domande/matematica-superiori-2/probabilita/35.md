A card numbered from $1$ to $23$ is drawn uniformly. What is the probability of getting an even number?

- $\dfrac{11}{23}$
- $\dfrac{12}{23}$
- $\dfrac{11}{46}$
- $\dfrac{22}{23}$

## Explanation

The cards are equally likely. Count the even numbers in the stated range, including either endpoint when it is even. Divide by the total number of cards.

$$
\#E=11,\qquad\#\Omega=23
$$

$$
P(E)=\frac{11}{23}=\dfrac{11}{23}
$$
