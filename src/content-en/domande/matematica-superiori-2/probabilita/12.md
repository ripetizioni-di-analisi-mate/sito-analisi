Two fair dice are rolled independently. Given that the first shows $5$, what is the probability that the sum is greater than $8$?

- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

Given the first result, the second die has six equally likely outcomes. A $4$, $5$, or $6$ makes the sum exceed $8$. The probability is $\dfrac36=\dfrac12$.

A second score of $3$ would make the sum exactly $8$. The strict inequality excludes it. With the first score already known, the denominator is no longer $36$.
