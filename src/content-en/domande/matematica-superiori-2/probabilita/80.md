After $25$ trials there are $16$ successes. After $65$ trials the cumulative number of successes is $34$. What is the relative frequency in trials $26$ through $65$ alone?

- $\dfrac{34}{65}$
- $\dfrac{9}{20}$
- $\dfrac{16}{25}$
- $\dfrac{18}{65}$

## Explanation

The totals after trial sixty-five already include the first block. To keep only the second, both the success counts and the total trials are subtracted. This gives $34-16=18$ successes out of $65-25=40$ trials. The ratio $\dfrac{18}{40}=\dfrac9{20}$ refers exactly to the requested block.
