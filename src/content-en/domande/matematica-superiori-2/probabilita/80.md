A card numbered from $1$ to $52$ is drawn uniformly. Given that its number is even, what is the probability that the number is a multiple of $4$?

- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The given information restricts the outcomes to even integers. Every other even integer is a multiple of four. This gives $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
