What is the distance from $(3;4)$ to the horizontal line $y=7$?

- $3$
- $4$
- $11$
- $7$

## Explanation

The shortest segment is vertical and perpendicular to the line. Its length is the absolute difference between the y-coordinates, which gives $3$.
