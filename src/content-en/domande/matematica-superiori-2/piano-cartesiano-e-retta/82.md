Which points in the plane are equally distant from **both coordinate axes**?

- The points on the two lines $y=x$ and $y=-x$
- All points with $x+y=1$
- Only the origin
- Only the line $y=x$

## Explanation

The distances to the axes are $|y|$ and $|x|$ respectively. Equating them gives $|y|=|x|$, allowing coordinates that are either equal or opposites.
