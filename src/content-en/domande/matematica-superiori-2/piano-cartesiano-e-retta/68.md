How are $y=4x+9$ and $y=4x+5$ positioned relative to each other?

- Perpendicular.
- Coincident.
- Intersecting at a single point.
- Distinct and parallel.

## Explanation

The slopes are equal, so the lines have the same direction. Their different intercepts make them distinct.
