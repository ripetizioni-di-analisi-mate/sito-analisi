How are the lines $4x+6y=12$ and $2x+3y=8$ related?

- Coincident
- Perpendicular
- Intersecting without being perpendicular
- Distinct and parallel

## Explanation

Dividing the first equation by two gives $2x+3y=6$. The left-hand sides are now identical, so the directions agree. A common point would require the same value $2x+3y$ to equal both $6$ and $8$, which is impossible. The lines are distinct and parallel. The variable coefficients are proportional, while the right-hand sides also matter for coincidence.
