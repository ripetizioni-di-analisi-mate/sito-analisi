What is the intersection point of $y=4x+5$ and $y=9$?

- $\left( 1, \  5\right)$
- $\left( 1, \  9\right)$
- $\left( -1, \  9\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{9}{4}, \  9\right)$

## Explanation

At the intersection the y-coordinate is $9$. Set the other expression equal to that value and solve for the x-coordinate.
