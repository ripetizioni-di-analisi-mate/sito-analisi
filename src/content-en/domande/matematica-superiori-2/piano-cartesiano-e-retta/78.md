Where do $x=2$ and $3x-4y=10$ intersect?

- $(-1;2)$
- $(2;-1)$
- $(2;1)$
- $(2;4)$

## Explanation

The vertical line sets $x=2$. Substituting in the second equation gives $6-4y=10$, so $-4y=4$ and $y=-1$. The point $(2;-1)$ satisfies both equations. Swapping the coordinates would fail the vertical equation. Also, height $1$ gives $6-4=2$ in the second expression, so the sign of the height matters for reaching the required value.
