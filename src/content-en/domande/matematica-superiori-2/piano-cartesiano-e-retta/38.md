For which value of $a$ is the line through $A(0;1)$ and $B(4;a)$ perpendicular to $y=2x+3$?

- $3$
- $-2$
- $-1$
- $9$

## Explanation

The perpendicular line must have slope $-\dfrac12$. Over four horizontal units, the vertical change is $-2$. Adding this change to the $y$-coordinate of $A$ gives $a=-1$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{a-1}{4}&=-\frac12,\\
        a-1&=-2,\\
        a&=-1.
\end{aligned}
$$
