A line is perpendicular to $y=-4x+9$. What is its slope?

- $4$
- $- \dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The product of the slopes of two nonvertical perpendicular lines is negative one. Take the negative reciprocal of the given slope. The result is $\dfrac{1}{4}$.
