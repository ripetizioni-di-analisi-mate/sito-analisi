You want to reach the line $x+y=6$ from the origin along the shortest segment. At which point does the route end?

- $(2,4)$
- $(0,6)$
- $(3,3)$
- $(6,0)$

## Explanation

The shortest segment lies on the perpendicular. The given line has slope $-1$, so the perpendicular through the origin is $y=x$. Solving $2x=6$ gives $(3,3)$.
