Which line goes through $(2;-1)$ and is perpendicular to $y=-\dfrac14x+5$?

- $y=4x+7$
- $y=-4x+7$
- $y=4x-9$
- $y=\dfrac14x-\dfrac32$

## Explanation

The product of the slopes must equal $-1$. Given $m_1=-\dfrac14$, the second slope is $4$. Passing through $(2;-1)$ gives $-1=4\cdot2+q$, so $q=-9$. This satisfies both requirements. The equation $y=4x+7$ has the correct direction but gives $15$ at $x=2$, rather than the required height. The choice must satisfy both direction and position.
