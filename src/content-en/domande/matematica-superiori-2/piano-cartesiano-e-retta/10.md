The midpoint of $AB$ is $M(1;3)$, and $A(-2;5)$. What are the coordinates of $B$?

- $(3;-2)$
- $(0;4)$
- $(4;1)$
- $(-4;7)$

## Explanation

From $A$ to $M$, add $3$ to the $x$-coordinate and subtract $2$ from the $y$-coordinate. The same displacement from $M$ reaches $B(4;1)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x_B&=2\cdot1-(-2)=4,\\
        y_B&=2\cdot3-5=1.
\end{aligned}
$$
