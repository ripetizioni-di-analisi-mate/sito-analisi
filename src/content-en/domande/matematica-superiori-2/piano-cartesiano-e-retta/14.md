What is the equation of the line perpendicular to $y=4$ through $P(-3;2)$?

- $x=2$
- $y=2$
- $x=-3$
- $y=-3$

## Explanation

The line $y=4$ is horizontal, so the perpendicular line is vertical. Its constant $x$-coordinate is the $x$-coordinate of $P$, which is $-3$.
