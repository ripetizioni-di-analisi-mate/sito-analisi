The points $A(1,1)$ and $B(1,5)$ are compared with the line $2x-y+1=0$. Which signs of the left side show that segment $AB$ crosses the line?

- Zero at both
- Positive at $A$, negative at $B$
- Positive at both
- Negative at $A$, positive at $B$

## Explanation

At $A$ the expression is $2-1+1=2$, and at $B$ it is $2-5+1=-2$. Along the vertical segment it passes through zero at $(1,3)$, between the endpoints.
