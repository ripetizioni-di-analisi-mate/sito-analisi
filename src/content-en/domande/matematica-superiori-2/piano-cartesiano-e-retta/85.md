For which values of $k$ is $(3;-1)$ at distance $2$ from the line $y=k$?

- $\{-1,1\}$
- $\{-3,1\}$
- $\{-2,2\}$
- $\{-3,3\}$

## Explanation

Distance from a horizontal line is the absolute difference in height. Require $|-1-k|=2$, or $|k+1|=2$. The two possibilities $k+1=2$ and $k+1=-2$ give $1$ and $-3$. The lines are two units above and two units below the point. Its horizontal coordinate $3$ stays the same at the perpendicular foot in both cases.
