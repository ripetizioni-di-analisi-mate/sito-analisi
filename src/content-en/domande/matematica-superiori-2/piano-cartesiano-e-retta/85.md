What is the distance from $(6,7)$ to the horizontal line $y=13$?

- $20$
- $6$
- $7$
- $13$

## Explanation

The shortest segment is vertical and perpendicular to the line. Its length is the absolute difference between the y-coordinates, which gives $6$.
