What is the y-intercept of $y=4x-3$?

- $3$
- $4$
- $-4$
- $-3$

## Explanation

Set $x=0$ to find the point on the vertical axis. This gives $y=-3$, so the y-intercept is negative three. It equals the constant term in slope-intercept form.
