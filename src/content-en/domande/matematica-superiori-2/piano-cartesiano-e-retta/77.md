What is the intersection point of $y=3x+4$ and $y=7$?

- $\left( 1, \  4\right)$
- $\left( -1, \  7\right)$
- $\left( 1, \  7\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{7}{3}, \  7\right)$

## Explanation

At the intersection the y-coordinate is $7$. Set the other expression equal to that value and solve for the x-coordinate.
