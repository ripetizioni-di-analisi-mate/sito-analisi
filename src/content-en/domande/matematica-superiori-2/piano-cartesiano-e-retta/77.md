The lines $r$ and $s$ are given by $x+y=5$ and $2x-y=1$. Which third line must pass through their common point, as seen by adding the equations term by term?

- $3x=4$
- $x+2y=6$
- $3x=6$
- $3y=6$

## Explanation

Both equalities hold at the common point. Adding them eliminates $y$ and gives $3x=6$. The common point therefore lies on the vertical line $x=2$ as well.
