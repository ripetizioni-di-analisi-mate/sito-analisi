The point $(2,13)$ lies on the line $y=6x+k$. What is the y-intercept $k$?

- $\dfrac{13}{6}$
- $25$
- $13$
- $1$

## Explanation

Substitution of the coordinates gives $13=12+k$. Subtract the contribution of the $x$ term to isolate the constant, giving $1$.
