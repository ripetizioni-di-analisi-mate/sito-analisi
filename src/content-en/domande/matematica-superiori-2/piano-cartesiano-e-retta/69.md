For which value of $b$ do the lines $y=(b-2)x+5$ and $y=2x+5$ coincide?

- $4$
- $2$
- $0$
- $7$

## Explanation

Both intercepts are $5$, so the lines already share the same point on the $y$-axis. They also need the same slope to coincide. The equation $b-2=2$ gives $b=4$.
