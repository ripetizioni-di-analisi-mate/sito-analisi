All lines in the family $y=k(x-2)+1$ go through the same point. Which point is it, and how can it be recognised?

- $(2,1)$, because the term containing $k$ vanishes
- $(1,2)$, because $k$ is the slope
- $(2,0)$, because $x-2$ vanishes
- $(0,1)$, because each formula ends in $1$

## Explanation

Setting $x=2$ gives $y=k\cdot0+1=1$, independently of $k$. At any other horizontal coordinate the height changes as $k$ varies.
