The segment from $A(0,0)$ to $B(6,4)$ contains points with integer coordinates. Which one lies **strictly between** the endpoints?

- $(2,2)$
- $(3,2)$
- $(9,6)$
- $(3,3)$

## Explanation

The line has equation $y=2x/3$. The point $(3,2)$ satisfies it and has horizontal coordinate between $0$ and $6$. The point $(9,6)$ is on the line too, but it lies beyond $B$.
