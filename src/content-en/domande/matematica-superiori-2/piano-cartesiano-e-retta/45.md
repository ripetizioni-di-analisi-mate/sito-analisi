What is the distance between $(1;2)$ and $(1;-3)$?

- $-5$
- $5$
- $1$
- $\sqrt{13}$

## Explanation

The points have the same x-coordinate, so the segment is vertical. Its length is the absolute difference between the y-coordinates, which gives $|2-(-3)|=5$.
