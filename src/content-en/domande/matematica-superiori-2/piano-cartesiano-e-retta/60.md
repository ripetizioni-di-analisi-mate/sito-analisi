What is the slope of the line $6x+13y=7$?

- $- \dfrac{6}{13}$
- $- \dfrac{13}{6}$
- $\dfrac{7}{13}$
- $\dfrac{6}{13}$

## Explanation

To isolate $y$, move the $x$ term to the other side and divide the equation by $13$. The slope is the coefficient of $x$, which gives $- \dfrac{6}{13}$.
