A displacement along a line is $(3;-2)$. Which of the following displacements is possible along a parallel line?

- $(-6;4)$
- $(-6;-4)$
- $(4;-6)$
- $(-3;4)$

## Explanation

The original displacement has slope $-\dfrac23$. Along a parallel line the ratio of vertical to horizontal change must stay the same. Multiplying both components by $-2$ gives $(-6;4)$, whose ratio is $\dfrac4{-6}=-\dfrac23$. The sign of the multiplier indicates travel in the opposite direction along the parallel, over twice the distance.
