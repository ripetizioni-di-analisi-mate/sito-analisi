A trail follows the line $2x+y=0$. Apart from the origin, which quadrants does it pass through?

- The second and fourth
- All four
- The first and third
- The first and fourth

## Explanation

The line satisfies $y=-2x$. At $x<0$ the height is positive, placing the point in the second quadrant. At $x>0$ the height is negative, placing it in the fourth.
