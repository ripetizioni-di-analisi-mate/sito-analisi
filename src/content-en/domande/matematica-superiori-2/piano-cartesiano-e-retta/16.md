What is the distance from $P(4;-2)$ to the line $3x+4y-4=0$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac45$
- $\dfrac85$
- $4$

## Explanation

Substitution gives $3\cdot4+4(-2)-4=12-8-4=0$. This means $P$ lies on the line, so its distance is zero. In the formula, the numerator is the absolute value of that calculation, and the denominator is $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$. Dividing zero by five confirms the result. The closest point on the line is $P$ itself.
