Find the distance from $O(0;0)$ to the line $3x+4y-7=0$.

- $\dfrac{7}{5}$
- $\dfrac{12}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{14}{5}$

## Explanation

To find the distance to a line, evaluate the left-hand side at the point and take its absolute value. Divide by the length of the vector of coefficients. The absolute value ensures a nonnegative distance.

$$
d=\frac{|3\cdot0+4\cdot0-7|}{\sqrt{3^2+4^2}}=\frac{7}5
$$
