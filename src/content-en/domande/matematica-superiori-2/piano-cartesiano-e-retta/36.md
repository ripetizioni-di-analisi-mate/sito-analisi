A line meets the axes at $(6,0)$ and $(0,3)$. What are the coordinates of the point on it whose horizontal coordinate is twice its vertical coordinate?

- $\left(3,\dfrac32\right)$
- $(4,2)$
- $(2,1)$
- $\left(\dfrac32,3\right)$

## Explanation

The line has equation $x+2y=6$. Using $x=2y$ gives $4y=6$, hence $y=\dfrac32$ and $x=3$.
