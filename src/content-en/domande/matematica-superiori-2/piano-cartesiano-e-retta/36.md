A line of slope $-2$ meets the horizontal axis at $(3;0)$. What is its intercept on the vertical axis?

- $6$
- $-6$
- $3$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The point $(3;0)$ satisfies $y=-2x+q$, so $0=-2\cdot3+q$ and $q=6$. Another method starts at $(3;0)$ and moves three units left to the vertical axis. With slope $-2$, the height then increases by six units. The point on the vertical axis is $(0;6)$, and its height is the requested intercept. The given slope and the computed intercept determine a unique slope-intercept equation.
