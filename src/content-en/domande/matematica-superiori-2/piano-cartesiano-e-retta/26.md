For what value of $t$ does the midpoint of $A(-2;t)$ and $B(6;4)$ lie on the horizontal axis?

- $4$
- $-2$
- $-4$
- $0$

## Explanation

On the horizontal axis the vertical coordinate must be zero. At the midpoint it is $\dfrac{t+4}{2}$, so set $t+4=0$, giving $t=-4$. The horizontal coordinate is already the average of $-2$ and $6$, which is $2$. With that value the midpoint is $(2;0)$. The endpoints have opposite heights and are each four units from the axis.
