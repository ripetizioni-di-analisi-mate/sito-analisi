On graph paper, one square represents $2$ horizontal units and $5$ vertical units. A line rises one square for each square to the right. What is its slope in axis units?

- $\dfrac25$
- $3$
- $\dfrac52$
- $1$

## Explanation

The ratio uses actual coordinates. The vertical change is $5$ and the horizontal change is $2$, giving $m=5/2$. Counting only squares would ignore the different scales.
