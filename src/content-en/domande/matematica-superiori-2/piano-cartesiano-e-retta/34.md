The point $(2,9)$ lies on the line $y=4x+k$. What is the y-intercept $k$?

- $9$
- $17$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$

## Explanation

Substitution of the coordinates gives $9=8+k$. Subtract the contribution of the $x$ term to isolate the constant, giving $1$.
