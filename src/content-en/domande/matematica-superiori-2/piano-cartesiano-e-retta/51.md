The points $A(0,0)$, $B(4,0)$ and $C(5,3)$ are three consecutive vertices of a parallelogram $ABCD$. What is the fourth vertex $D$?

::grafico{punti="0,0,pieno,A;4,0,pieno,B;5,3,pieno,C" linee="0,0 4,0 5,3" xmin=-1 xmax=6.4 ymin=-0.9 ymax=4.6 uguale=1 titolo="The three vertices in order along the boundary"}

- $(9,3)$
- $(1,3)$
- $(1,-3)$
- $(5,-3)$

## Explanation

Opposite sides $BC$ and $AD$, followed in those directions, have the same displacement. From $B$ to $C$ it is $(1,3)$. Applying that displacement to $A$ gives $D(1,3)$.
