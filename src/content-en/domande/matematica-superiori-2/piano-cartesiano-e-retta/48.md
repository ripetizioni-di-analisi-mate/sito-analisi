A point $P$ has integer coordinates and is at distance $1$ from the origin. How many different points are possible?

- $8$
- $4$
- $2$
- Infinitely many

## Explanation

The coordinates must satisfy $x^2+y^2=1$. For integers one square must be $1$ and the other $0$. The points are $(1,0),(-1,0),(0,1),(0,-1)$.
