What is the distance between $A=(4,9)$ and $B=(19,29)$?

- $35$
- $25$
- $5$
- $125$

## Explanation

The coordinate differences are $15$ and $20$. By the Pythagorean theorem the distance is the square root of the sum of their squares, which gives $25$.
