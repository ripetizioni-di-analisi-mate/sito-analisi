Point $B(5;t)$ is $5$ units from $A(2;1)$. What are the possible values of $t$?

- $\{-4,4\}$
- $\{-3,5\}$
- $\{5\}$
- $\{-2,4\}$

## Explanation

The horizontal displacement is $5-2=3$. The distance gives $3^2+(t-1)^2=25$, so $(t-1)^2=16$. The height of $B$ can be four units above that of $A$, at $5$, or four below, at $-3$. Both give the same distance because the squared vertical displacement is $16$ either way. Its sign determines only which side contains $B$.
