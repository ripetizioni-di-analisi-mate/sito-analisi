What is the intersection point of $y=2x+3$ and $y=5$?

- $\left( 1; \  3\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{5}{2}; \  5\right)$
- $\left( 1; \  5\right)$
- $\left( -1; \  5\right)$

## Explanation

At the intersection the y-coordinate is $5$. Set the other expression equal to that value and solve for the x-coordinate.
