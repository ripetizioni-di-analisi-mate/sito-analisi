A straight road is shifted $3$ units upwards. Its original equation is $2x-y+4=0$. What is its equation after the shift?

- $5x-y+4=0$
- $2x-4y+4=0$
- $2x-y+7=0$
- $2x-y+1=0$

## Explanation

The explicit form is $y=2x+4$. At a fixed horizontal coordinate every height increases by $3$, giving $y=2x+7$. The slope stays the same.
