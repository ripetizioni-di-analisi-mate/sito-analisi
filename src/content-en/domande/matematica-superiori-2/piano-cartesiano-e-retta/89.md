What condition makes $A(a;2)$ and $B(3;b)$ distinct points on a vertical line?

- $a=3,\ b=2$
- $a=3,\ b\ne2$
- $a\ne3,\ b=2$
- $a\ne3,\ b\ne2$

## Explanation

For a vertical line the horizontal coordinates must agree, so $a=3$. To keep the points distinct, the heights must then differ, giving $b\ne2$. If $b=2$ as well, both data describe $(3;2)$ and more than one line goes through that single point. With $a\ne3$, different horizontal coordinates would prevent a vertical line from containing both points.
