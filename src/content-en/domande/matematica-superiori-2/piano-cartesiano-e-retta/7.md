What is the intersection point of the lines $y=x+6$ and $y=-x+18$?

- $\left(12;\,6\right)$
- $\left(-6;\,12\right)$
- $\left(6;\,6\right)$
- $\left(6;\,12\right)$

## Explanation

The two expressions for $y$ agree at the intersection. Their right-hand sides are therefore set equal, which gives the x-coordinate. Substitution into either equation gives the y-coordinate, which also satisfies the other equation.

$$
x+6=-x+18
$$

$$
2x=12
$$

$$
x=6,\qquad y=12
$$
