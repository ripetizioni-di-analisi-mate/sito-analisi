Which of the listed lines is decreasing?

- $-2x+3y=6$
- $y=2$
- $x=2$
- $2x+3y=6$

## Explanation

The equation $2x+3y=6$ becomes $y=-\dfrac23x+2$. Its slope is negative, so the height falls as the horizontal coordinate grows. The equation with $-2x$ on the left instead has a positive slope. The line $y=2$ is horizontal because its height is constant. The line $x=2$ is vertical and its slope is undefined.
