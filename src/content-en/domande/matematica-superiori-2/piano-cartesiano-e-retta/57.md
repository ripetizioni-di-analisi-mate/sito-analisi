What is the slope of the line $3x+7y=4$?

- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $- \dfrac{7}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{7}$

## Explanation

To isolate $y$, move the $x$ term to the other side and divide the equation by $7$. The slope is the coefficient of $x$, which gives $- \dfrac{3}{7}$.
