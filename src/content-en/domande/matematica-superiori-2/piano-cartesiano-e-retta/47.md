What is the distance between $A=(3,7)$ and $B=(15,23)$?

- $20$
- $100$
- $4$
- $28$

## Explanation

The coordinate differences are $12$ and $16$. By the Pythagorean theorem the distance is the square root of the sum of their squares, which gives $20$.
