A point in the second quadrant is $3$ units from the vertical axis and $4$ units from the horizontal axis. What are its coordinates?

- $(-3;4)$
- $(3;4)$
- $(-4;3)$
- $(-3;-4)$

## Explanation

Distance from the vertical axis is $|x|$, so the absolute horizontal coordinate is $3$. Distance from the horizontal axis is $|y|$, which equals $4$. The second quadrant lies left and up from the origin, so $x<0$ and $y>0$. This gives $(-3;4)$. The distances fix the magnitudes and the quadrant fixes the signs.
