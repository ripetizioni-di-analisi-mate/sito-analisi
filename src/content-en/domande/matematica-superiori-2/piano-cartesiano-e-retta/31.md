For which values of $k$ do $y=x+3$ and $y=kx+5$ have an intersection with positive horizontal coordinate?

- $k>1$
- $k\le1$
- $k\ne1$
- $k<1$

## Explanation

Equating the heights gives $(1-k)x=2$. At $k=1$ this becomes $0=2$, so the lines are distinct and parallel. For $k\ne1$ the numerator of $x=\dfrac2{1-k}$ is positive. The coordinate therefore has the sign of the denominator and is positive exactly when $k<1$. For example, $k=0$ gives $x=2$ and height $5$ on both lines.
