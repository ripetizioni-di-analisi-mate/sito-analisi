Find the intersection of $y=x+9$ and $y=-x+27$.

- $\left(18;\,9\right)$
- $\left(-9;\,18\right)$
- $\left(9;\,9\right)$
- $\left(9;\,18\right)$

## Explanation

The two expressions for $y$ agree at the intersection. Set their right-hand sides equal and solve for the x-coordinate. Substitution into either equation gives the y-coordinate, which also satisfies the other equation.

$$
x+9=-x+27
$$

$$
2x=18
$$

$$
x=9,\qquad y=18
$$
