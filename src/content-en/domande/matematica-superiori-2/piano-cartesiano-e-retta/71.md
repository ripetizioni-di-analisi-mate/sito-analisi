A line goes through $A(0,0)$ and $B(3,1)$. Which proposed vector gives a displacement along a line perpendicular to $AB$?

- $(1,-3)$
- $(3,-1)$
- $(1,3)$
- $(-3,-1)$

## Explanation

The displacement from $A$ to $B$ has slope $1/3$. The displacement $(1,-3)$ has slope $-3$, and the two slopes have product $-1$.
