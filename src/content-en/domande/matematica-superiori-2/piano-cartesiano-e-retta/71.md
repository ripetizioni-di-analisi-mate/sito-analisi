A line is perpendicular to $y=-2x+5$. What is its slope?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The product of the slopes of two nonvertical perpendicular lines is negative one. Take the negative reciprocal of the given slope. The result is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
