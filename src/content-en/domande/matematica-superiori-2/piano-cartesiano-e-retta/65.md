At what x-coordinate does $y=6x-13$ intersect the $x$-axis?

- $\dfrac{13}{6}$
- $- \dfrac{13}{6}$
- $78$
- $-13$

## Explanation

On the $x$-axis the y-coordinate is zero. Solving $6x-13=0$ gives the x-coordinate at the intersection. The result is $\dfrac{13}{6}$.
