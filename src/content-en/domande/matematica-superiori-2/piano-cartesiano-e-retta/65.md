Which equation represents the line that meets the axes at $(3;0)$ and $(0;-4)$?

- $4x-3y-12=0$
- $-4x-3y-12=0$
- $4x+3y-12=0$
- $3x-4y-12=0$

## Explanation

The vertical intercept is $-4$. From $(0;-4)$ to $(3;0)$ the height increases by $4$ and the horizontal coordinate by $3$, so the slope is $\dfrac43$. From $y=\dfrac43x-4$ we get $4x-3y-12=0$. As a check, $4x$ is $12$ at the first point and $-3y$ is $12$ at the second. Each cancels the constant term. The two intercepts are distinct points, so they determine a unique line.
