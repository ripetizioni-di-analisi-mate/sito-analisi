What is the equation of the vertical line through $(3;2)$?

- $y=2$
- $x=3$
- $y=3x+2$
- $y=3$

## Explanation

On a vertical line, the x-coordinate is constant and the y-coordinate is unrestricted. The given point has x-coordinate three, so the equation is $x=3$.
