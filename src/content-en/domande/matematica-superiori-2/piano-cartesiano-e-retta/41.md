The equality $2(x+y)=2x+2y$ is expanded. Which set of points in the plane satisfies it?

- One vertical line
- The whole plane
- No points
- Only the two angle bisectors

## Explanation

All terms cancel, leaving $0=0$ for every pair $(x,y)$. To describe a single line in the form $ax+by+c=0$, at least one of $a$ and $b$ must be nonzero.
