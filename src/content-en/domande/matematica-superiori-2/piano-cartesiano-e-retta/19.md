A pricing plan is described by $C(n)=7+3n$ euros. How much does the cost increase from $4$ to $6$ visits?

- $3$
- $12$
- $13$
- $6$

## Explanation

The fixed fee is unchanged. The cost increases by $3$ euros per visit, so multiply by the two additional visits to obtain $6$.
