Two plans cost $6+2n$ and $13+n$ euros for $n$ positive whole hours. From how many hours is the second plan strictly cheaper?

- $7$
- $6$
- $13$
- $8$

## Explanation

The required comparison is $13+n<6+2n$, giving $7<n$. The first allowed integer is $8$. At $n=7$ both plans cost $20$ euros, so strict inequality does not yet hold. At $n=8$ they cost $22$ and $21$ euros, respectively. The second cost minus the first is $7-n$, which keeps falling as the hours increase.
