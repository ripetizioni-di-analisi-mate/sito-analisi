For what value of $k$ do $(k-1)x-y+4=0$ and $2x-y+4=0$ represent the same line?

- $1$
- $2$
- $-1$
- $3$

## Explanation

Solving for $y$ shows both lines have intercept $4$. For them to coincide the slopes must also agree, so $k-1=2$ and $k=3$. Substituting $3$ makes the equations identical. Any other value would still give a shared point $(0;4)$ but different directions. One common point is therefore insufficient for coincidence.
