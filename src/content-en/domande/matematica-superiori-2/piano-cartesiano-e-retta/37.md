A route from $(0,0)$ to $(3,4)$ uses only streets parallel to the axes and never backtracks. By how much does its length exceed the direct segment?

::grafico{punti="0,0,pieno,A;3,4,pieno,B" linee="0,0 3,4;0,0 3,0 3,4" xmin=-1 xmax=4.5 ymin=-1 ymax=5.4 uguale=1 titolo="A route along the streets and the direct segment"}

- $1$
- $2$
- $5$
- $7$

## Explanation

The street route covers $3+4=7$ units. The direct segment is the hypotenuse of a right triangle and measures $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$. The difference is $2$.
