Which value of $a$ makes $y=(a+1)x+1$ perpendicular to $y=-2x+3$?

- $-\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac12$
- $-1$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The slopes are $a+1$ and $-2$. Their product must be $-1$, so $-2(a+1)=-1$ and $a+1=\dfrac12$. This gives $a=-\dfrac12$. The value $\dfrac12$ is the required slope. Substituting the parameter makes the coefficient of $x$ equal to $1-\dfrac12=\dfrac12$, whose product with $-2$ is $-1$. The intercepts do not affect direction. The direction depends only on the coefficients multiplying the horizontal coordinate.
