A line is perpendicular to $y=-6x+13$. What is its slope?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $-6$
- $6$
- $- \dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The product of the slopes of two nonvertical perpendicular lines is negative one. Take the negative reciprocal of the given slope. The result is $\dfrac{1}{6}$.
