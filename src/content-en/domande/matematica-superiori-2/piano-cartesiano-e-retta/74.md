What displacement results from rotating $(3;-2)$ by $90^\circ$ anticlockwise?

- $(-2;-3)$
- $(2;3)$
- $(3;2)$
- $(-3;-2)$

## Explanation

An anticlockwise rotation takes displacement $(u;v)$ to $(-v;u)$. The three units right become three units up. The two units down become two units right. This gives $(2;3)$. Its length is the same as the original displacement. The slopes $-\dfrac23$ and $\dfrac32$ have product $-1$, as expected for a quarter-turn.
