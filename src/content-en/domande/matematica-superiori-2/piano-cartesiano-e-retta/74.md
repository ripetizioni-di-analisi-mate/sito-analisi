A line is perpendicular to $y=-5x+11$. What is its slope?

- $5$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $-5$
- $- \dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

The product of the slopes of two nonvertical perpendicular lines is negative one. Take the negative reciprocal of the given slope. The result is $\dfrac{1}{5}$.
