An activity requires at least two entries. The total cost for $n$ entries is $7+3n$ euros and the budget is $20$ euros. Which entry counts are allowed?

- $\{2,3,4\}$
- $\{1,2,3,4\}$
- $\{2,3,4,5\}$
- $\{3,4\}$

## Explanation

The budget requires $7+3n\le20$, or $n\le\dfrac{13}{3}$. The entry count is a whole number and must be at least two. This leaves $2$, $3$, and $4$. Four entries cost $19$ euros, while five would cost $22$ euros. One entry would fit the budget but falls short of the activity’s minimum.
