A pricing plan is described by $C(n)=13+6n$ euros. How much does the cost increase from $7$ to $9$ visits?

- $12$
- $25$
- $42$
- $6$

## Explanation

The fixed fee is unchanged. The cost increases by $6$ euros per visit, so multiply by the two additional visits to obtain $12$.
