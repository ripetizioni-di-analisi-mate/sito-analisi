A pricing plan is described by $C(n)=5+2n$ euros. How much does the cost increase from $3$ to $5$ visits?

- $6$
- $2$
- $4$
- $9$

## Explanation

The fixed fee is unchanged. The cost increases by $2$ euros per visit, so multiply by the two additional visits to obtain $4$.
