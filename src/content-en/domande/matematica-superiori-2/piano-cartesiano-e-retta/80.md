What is the area of the triangle bounded by the axes and $3x+2y=12$?

- $24$
- $12$
- $10$
- $6$

## Explanation

Set $y=0$ on the horizontal axis to obtain $x=4$. Set $x=0$ on the vertical axis to obtain $y=6$. Together with the origin, $(4;0)$ and $(0;6)$ form a right triangle. Its legs are the two axis segments, so the area is $\dfrac{4\cdot6}{2}=12$. The full product would give the area of the surrounding rectangle, of which the triangle is half.
