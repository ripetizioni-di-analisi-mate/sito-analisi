A triangle has a fixed base from $(0,0)$ to $(6,0)$. Its third vertex can move in the half-plane $y>0$ while keeping the area equal to $9$. Which line describes all its positions?

- $y=x+3$
- $y=3$
- $x=3$
- $y=9$

## Explanation

With the base on the horizontal axis, the height is the vertical coordinate $y$ because $y>0$. The area is $6y/2=3y$. Requiring $3y=9$ fixes $y=3$ and leaves the horizontal coordinate free.
