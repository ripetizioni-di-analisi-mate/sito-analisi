What is the intersection point of $y=6x+7$ and $y=13$?

- $\left( -1, \  13\right)$
- $\left( 1, \  13\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{13}{6}, \  13\right)$
- $\left( 1, \  7\right)$

## Explanation

At the intersection the y-coordinate is $13$. Set the other expression equal to that value and solve for the x-coordinate.
