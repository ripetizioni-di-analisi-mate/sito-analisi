Find the distance from $O(0;0)$ to the line $3x+4y-6=0$.

- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $\dfrac{11}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{12}{5}$

## Explanation

The distance from a line $ax+by+c=0$ is the absolute value of the left-hand side evaluated at the point, divided by $\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$. Here $a=3$ and $b=4$, so the denominator is $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$. The absolute value ensures a nonnegative distance.

$$
d=\frac{|3\cdot0+4\cdot0-6|}{\sqrt{3^2+4^2}}=\frac{6}5
$$
