The line $y=6x+q$ passes through $P(2;25)$. Find $q$.

- $13$
- $14$
- $12$
- $26$

## Explanation

A point lies on a line when its coordinates satisfy the equation. Substitute the x- and y-coordinates and solve the resulting linear equation for the intercept.

$$
25=6\cdot2+q
$$

$$
q=25-12=13
$$
