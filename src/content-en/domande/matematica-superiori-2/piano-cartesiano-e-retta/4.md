The line $y=6x+q$ passes through $P(2;25)$. What is the value of $q$?

- $13$
- $14$
- $12$
- $26$

## Explanation

A point lies on a line when its coordinates satisfy the equation. The x- and y-coordinates are therefore substituted, and the resulting linear equation gives the intercept.

$$
25=6\cdot2+q
$$

$$
q=25-12=13
$$
