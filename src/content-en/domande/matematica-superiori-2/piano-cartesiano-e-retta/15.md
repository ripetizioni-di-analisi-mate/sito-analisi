One segment follows $y=x+2$ for $0\le x\le4$. Another follows $y=8-x$ for $4\le x\le6$. What is their intersection?

- $(3;5)$
- $(4;6)$
- $(4;4)$
- No point

## Explanation

The full lines meet where $x+2=8-x$, namely at $(3;5)$. The second segment allows horizontal coordinates from $4$ to $6$, which excludes that point. The only coordinate allowed by both segments is $4$. There their heights are $6$ and $4$, which differ. Finding the intersection of the lines must therefore be followed by checking each segment’s domain.
