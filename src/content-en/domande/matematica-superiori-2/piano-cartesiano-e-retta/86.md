The coordinates of every point on the line $y=2x+4$ are swapped. Which equation describes the resulting image?

- $y=-2x+4$
- $y=2x-4$
- $y=(x+4)/2$
- $y=(x-4)/2$

## Explanation

The new horizontal coordinate is the old vertical one and the new vertical coordinate is the old horizontal one. The equation becomes $x=2y+4$, giving $y=(x-4)/2$.
