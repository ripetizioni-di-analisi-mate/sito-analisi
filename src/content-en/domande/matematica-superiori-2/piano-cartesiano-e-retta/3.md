Find the slope of the line through $A(6;1)$ and $B(8;13)$.

- $7$
- $5$
- $12$
- $6$

## Explanation

The x-coordinates differ, so the line is nonvertical and has a defined slope. Divide the change in y by the change in x, using the same endpoint order in both differences.

$$
m=\frac{13-1}{8-6}=\frac{12}2=6
$$
