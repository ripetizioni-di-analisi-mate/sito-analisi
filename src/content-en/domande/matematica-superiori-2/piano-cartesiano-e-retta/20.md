A pricing plan is described by $C(n)=9+4n$ euros. How much does the cost increase from $5$ to $7$ visits?

- $8$
- $17$
- $4$
- $20$

## Explanation

The fixed fee is unchanged. The cost increases by $4$ euros per visit, so multiply by the two additional visits to obtain $8$.
