The cost $C(n)$ is a fixed fee plus a constant price per entry. Two entries cost $9$ euros and five cost $15$ euros. Which formula models the cost?

- $C(n)=2n+5$
- $C(n)=5n+2$
- $C(n)=2n+9$
- $C(n)=2n$

## Explanation

From two to five entries the cost rises by $15-9=6$ euros. The three additional entries therefore give slope $6:3=2$ euros per entry. Two entries cost $4$ euros beyond the fixed fee, making the fee $9-4=5$ euros. The model $2n+5$ gives $15$ for five entries, confirming the second observation as well.
