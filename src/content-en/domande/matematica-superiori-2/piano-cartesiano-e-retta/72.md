A drawing tool has one edge along $y=2x$ and another along $y=-2x$. A student thinks they meet at a right angle because their slopes are opposites. Which check settles the question?

- The angle is right because both lines pass through the origin
- The lines are parallel because the slope magnitudes agree
- Their slopes sum to zero, so the angle is right
- Their slopes have product $-4$, so the angle is not right

## Explanation

Two nonvertical lines are perpendicular when $m_1m_2=-1$. Here $2(-2)=-4$. A perpendicular to a line of slope $2$ would have slope $-1/2$.
