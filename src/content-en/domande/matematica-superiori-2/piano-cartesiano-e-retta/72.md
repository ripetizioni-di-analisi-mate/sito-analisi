A line is perpendicular to $y=-3x+7$. What is its slope?

- $3$
- $- \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $-3$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The product of the slopes of two nonvertical perpendicular lines is negative one. Take the negative reciprocal of the given slope. The result is $\dfrac{1}{3}$.
