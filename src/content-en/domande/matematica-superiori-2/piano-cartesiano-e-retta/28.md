Which point lies on the extension of the line from $A(1,2)$ towards $B(3,5)$, beyond $B$ and as far from $B$ as $A$ is?

- $(5,8)$
- $(2,3)$
- $(5,7)$
- $(4,7)$

## Explanation

From $A$ to $B$, the horizontal coordinate increases by $2$ and the vertical coordinate by $3$. Repeating that displacement from $B$ stays on the line, travels the same distance and reaches $(5,8)$.
