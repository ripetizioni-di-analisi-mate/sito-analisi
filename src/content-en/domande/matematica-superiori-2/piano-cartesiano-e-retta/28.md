Which equation represents the line through $A(-2;3)$ and $B(0;-1)$?

- $y=-2x-1$
- $y=2x-1$
- $y=-2x+1$
- $y=-\dfrac12x-1$

## Explanation

Point $B$ has horizontal coordinate zero, so the intercept is $-1$. Moving from $A$ to $B$ goes two units right and four down. Their ratio is $\dfrac{-4}{2}=-2$. The equation is therefore $y=-2x-1$. At $x=-2$ it gives $4-1=3$, the height of $A$. The two distinct points determine exactly one line. The negative sign agrees with the drop in height when moving to the right.
