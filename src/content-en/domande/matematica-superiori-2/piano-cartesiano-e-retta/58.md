What is the slope of the line $4x+9y=5$?

- $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $- \dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $- \dfrac{4}{9}$

## Explanation

To isolate $y$, move the $x$ term to the other side and divide the equation by $9$. The slope is the coefficient of $x$, which gives $- \dfrac{4}{9}$.
