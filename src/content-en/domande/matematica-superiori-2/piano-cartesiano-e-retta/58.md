For what value of $k$ does $kx+2y=4$ represent a horizontal line?

- $2$
- $-2$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $y$ is $2$, so solving for the height gives $y=-\dfrac k2x+2$. A horizontal line requires slope zero. Thus $-\dfrac k2=0$ and $k=0$. The equation then becomes $y=2$, satisfied at every horizontal coordinate. The constant term sets the fixed height and stays unchanged when the coefficient of $x$ vanishes.
