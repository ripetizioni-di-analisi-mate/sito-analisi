What is the slope of a line parallel to $y=9x-3$?

- $10$
- $9$
- $8$
- $18$

## Explanation

Two nonvertical lines have the same direction when their slopes are equal. The constant term changes a line’s position while preserving its direction. The required slope therefore equals the coefficient of $x$.

$$
m_1=m_2=9
$$
