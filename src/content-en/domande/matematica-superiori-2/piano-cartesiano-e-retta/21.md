For what value of $k$ are $2x-3y+5=0$ and $kx+6y-2=0$ distinct parallel lines?

- $4$
- $-4$
- $-2$
- $2$

## Explanation

The first line has slope $\dfrac23$ and intercept $\dfrac53$. The second has slope $-\dfrac k6$ and intercept $\dfrac13$. Equating slopes gives $-k=4$, so $k=-4$. The intercepts remain different, so the lines are distinct and parallel. The coefficients can also be compared by multiplying the first equation by $-2$. This gives $-4x+6y-10=0$, with a different constant term from $-2$.
