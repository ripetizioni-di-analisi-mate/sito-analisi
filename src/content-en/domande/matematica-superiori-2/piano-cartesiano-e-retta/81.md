What is the distance from $(2,3)$ to the horizontal line $y=5$?

- $5$
- $2$
- $3$
- $8$

## Explanation

The shortest segment is vertical and perpendicular to the line. Its length is the absolute difference between the y-coordinates, which gives $2$.
