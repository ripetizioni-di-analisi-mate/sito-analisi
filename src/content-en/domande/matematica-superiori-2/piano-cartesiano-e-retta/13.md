Which equation represents the same line as $2x-3y+6=0$?

- $-4x+6y+12=0$
- $-4x+6y-12=0$
- $4x-6y+6=0$
- $2x+3y+6=0$

## Explanation

Multiplying every term of the equation by $-2$ preserves the solution set. This gives $-4x+6y-12=0$. Changing only the constant term can instead produce a distinct parallel line.
