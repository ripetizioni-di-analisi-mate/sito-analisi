The point $B(5;t)$ has positive $y$-coordinate and is at distance $5$ from $A(2;0)$. What is $t$?

- $3$
- $4$
- $5$
- $16$

## Explanation

The horizontal displacement is $3$. The squared vertical displacement is therefore $25-9=16$. The positivity condition selects $t=4$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(5-2)^2+t^2&=25,\\
        t^2&=16,\\
        t&=4\qquad(t>0).
\end{aligned}
$$
