The line through $A(2;4)$ and $B(t;-2)$ has slope $-3$. What is $t$?

- $0$
- $2$
- $8$
- $4$

## Explanation

From $A$ to $B$, the vertical change is $-2-4=-6$. The ratio $\dfrac{-6}{t-2}$ must equal $-3$, so the horizontal change is $2$. This gives $t=2+2=4$. The value $t=2$ would put the points on the same vertical line and the ratio would be undefined. Substituting $4$ gives exactly the required slope. The slope is negative because the two coordinate changes have opposite signs.
