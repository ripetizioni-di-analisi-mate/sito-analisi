A table lists heights $3,7,12$ at horizontal coordinates $0,2,4$. Can all three points come from a single nonvertical line?

- No, because the first height would have to be zero
- Yes, because the horizontal intervals are equal
- Yes, because the heights increase
- No, equal horizontal steps give different rises

## Explanation

The two slopes are $(7-3)/2=2$ and $(12-7)/2=5/2$. A single nonvertical line must have constant slope, even if it does not pass through the origin.
