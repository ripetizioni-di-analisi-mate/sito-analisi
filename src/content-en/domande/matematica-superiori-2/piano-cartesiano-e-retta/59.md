For which value of $k$ is $kx-4y+6=0$ parallel to the line through $A(1;3)$ and $B(5;1)$?

- $2$
- $-2$
- $-8$
- $8$

## Explanation

The line through the two points has slope $-\dfrac12$. Isolating $y$ in the equation with the parameter gives slope $\dfrac{k}{4}$. Equating the slopes gives $k=-2$. The intercepts differ, so the lines are distinct.

$$
\begin{aligned}
m_{AB}&=\frac{1-3}{5-1}=-\frac12,\\
        \frac{k}{4}&=-\frac12,\qquad k=-2.
\end{aligned}
$$
