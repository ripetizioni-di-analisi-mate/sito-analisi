A triangle has vertices $A(0,0)$, $B(8,0)$ and $C(3,5)$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $AB$. What is the ratio of the areas of triangles $AMC$ and $MBC$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac45$
- $\dfrac35$
- $2$

## Explanation

The horizontal bases $AM$ and $MB$ both measure $4$. Their common height is $5$, the vertical coordinate of $C$. Each area is $4\cdot5/2=10$, so their ratio is $1$.
