For what value of $k$ does the midpoint of $A(k;0)$ and $B(2;8)$ lie on $y=3x+1$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The height of the midpoint is already fixed at $\dfrac{0+8}{2}=4$. On the line, height $4$ means $3x+1=4$, giving $x=1$. Thus $\dfrac{k+2}{2}=1$, so $k=0$. With that value the midpoint is $(1;4)$. Substitution gives $3\cdot1+1=4$, confirming that the point obtained by averaging satisfies the constraint. The nonzero coefficient $3$ makes the horizontal coordinate for the fixed height unique.
