Which equation represents the same line as $3x-2y+6=0$?

- $6x-4y+6=0$
- $-6x+4y-12=0$
- $3x+2y+6=0$
- $6x-4y-12=0$

## Explanation

Multiplying every term of $3x-2y+6=0$ by $-2$ gives $-6x+4y-12=0$. The factor is nonzero, so dividing by it recovers the original equation. Rescaling only the terms with $x$ and $y$ changes the intercept and gives a different line. The point $(0;3)$ provides a check because it satisfies both equivalent equations.
