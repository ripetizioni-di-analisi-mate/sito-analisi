A printer omitted a coefficient in the line $6x+ay=12$. You know the point $(1,2)$ is on the line. What is the missing coefficient?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $2$
- $6$

## Explanation

The point's coordinates must satisfy the equation. Substitution gives $6+2a=12$, so $a=3$. The coefficient multiplies the vertical coordinate $2$.
