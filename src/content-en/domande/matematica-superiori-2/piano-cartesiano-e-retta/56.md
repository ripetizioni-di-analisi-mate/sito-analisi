In the equation $ax+by+c=0$, the coefficient $b$ is nonzero. Which expression gives the vertical intercept even when $a=0$?

- $-c/b$
- $a/b$
- $-c/a$
- $-b/a$

## Explanation

On the vertical axis, set $x=0$. This leaves $by+c=0$, so $y=-c/b$. The formula does not divide by $a$ and covers horizontal lines.
