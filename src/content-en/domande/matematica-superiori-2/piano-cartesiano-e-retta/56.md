What is the slope of the line $2x+5y=3$?

- $- \dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

To isolate $y$, move the $x$ term to the other side and divide the equation by $5$. The slope is the coefficient of $x$, which gives $- \dfrac{2}{5}$.
