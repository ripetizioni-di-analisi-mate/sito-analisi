At what x-coordinate does $y=2x-5$ intersect the $x$-axis?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{5}{2}$
- $-5$
- $10$

## Explanation

On the $x$-axis the y-coordinate is zero. Solving $2x-5=0$ gives the x-coordinate at the intersection. The result is $\dfrac{5}{2}$.
