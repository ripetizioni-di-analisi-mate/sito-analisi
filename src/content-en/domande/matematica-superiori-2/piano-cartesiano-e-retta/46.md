On a map, one coordinate unit represents $2$ km. Two shelters are at $(1,1)$ and $(4,5)$. Which calculation gives their straight-line distance in kilometres?

- $2(3+4)$
- $2\sqrt{3^2+4^2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3^2+4^2}}2$
- $\sqrt{2(3^2+4^2)}$

## Explanation

The coordinate differences are $3$ and $4$, so the map distance is $5$ units. The scale factor multiplies a length, giving $2\cdot5=10$ km.
