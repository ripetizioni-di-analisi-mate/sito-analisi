What is the distance between $A=(2;5)$ and $B=(11;17)$?

- $21$
- $15$
- $3$
- $75$

## Explanation

The coordinate differences are $9$ and $12$. By the Pythagorean theorem the distance is the square root of the sum of their squares, which gives $15$.
