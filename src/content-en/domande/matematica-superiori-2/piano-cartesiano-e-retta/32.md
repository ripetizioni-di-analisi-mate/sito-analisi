A point $P$ is off the line $ax+by+c=0$. If the equation is multiplied by $-2$, by what factor does the computed distance from $P$ change?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The numerator in the distance formula is an absolute value, so it is multiplied by $|-2|=2$. The denominator is also multiplied by $2$, because the squared coefficients under the root are multiplied by $4$. The factors cancel and the distance stays the same. Multiplying the equation by a nonzero number describes the same line.
