To draw a perpendicular to $y=x+2$, Marta starts from a point and moves $3$ units to the right. How must the vertical coordinate change to stay on the perpendicular?

- Stay the same
- Increase by $3$
- Decrease by $3$
- Decrease by $1/3$

## Explanation

The given line has slope $1$. Its perpendicular has slope $-1$, so $\Delta y=-\Delta x$. A horizontal move of $3$ therefore requires a drop of $3$.
