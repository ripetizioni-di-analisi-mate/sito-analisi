What is the distance from $(5,6)$ to the horizontal line $y=11$?

- $11$
- $17$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

The shortest segment is vertical and perpendicular to the line. Its length is the absolute difference between the y-coordinates, which gives $5$.
