A railway follows $y=2x+1$. A parallel service track goes through $(0,-3)$. What is the **vertical** separation between them at the same horizontal coordinate $x=10$?

- $24$
- $20$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The service track is $y=2x-3$. Subtracting the heights at the same horizontal coordinate cancels the $2x$ terms. This leaves $1-(-3)=4$ for every $x$.
