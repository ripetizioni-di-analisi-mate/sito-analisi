What is the area of the triangle bounded by the coordinate axes and $2x+3y=12$?

- $24$
- $12$
- $10$
- $6$

## Explanation

On the $x$-axis, $y=0$, so the horizontal intercept is $6$. On the $y$-axis, $x=0$, so the vertical intercept is $4$. The triangle is right-angled and its area is half the product of the two legs.

$$
A=\frac{6\cdot4}{2}=12.
$$
