Point $B$ is the reflection of $A(-4;3)$ across the vertical axis. What are $B$ and the distance $AB$?

- $B(-4;-3),\ AB=6$
- $B(4;3),\ AB=8$
- $B(4;-3),\ AB=10$
- $B(4;3),\ AB=4$

## Explanation

Reflection keeps height $3$ and changes the sign of the horizontal coordinate, giving $B(4;3)$. The segment is horizontal and has length $|4-(-4)|=8$. Each point is four units from the vertical axis. The axis passes through midpoint $(0;3)$, so both four-unit pieces contribute to the distance between the points.
