Find the distance between $A(8;1)$ and $B(11;5)$.

- $6$
- $5$
- $4$
- $10$

## Explanation

Find the differences between corresponding coordinates. Their squares are the areas of the squares on the legs of a right triangle. The Pythagorean theorem gives the resulting nonnegative distance.

$$
AB=\sqrt{(11-8)^2+(5-1)^2}
$$

$$
AB=\sqrt{9+16}=5
$$
