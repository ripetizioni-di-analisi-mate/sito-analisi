A map's origin moves from $(0,0)$ to the old point $(2,-1)$, with no rotation or change of scale. What are the new coordinates of the old point $(5,4)$?

- $(3,3)$
- $(7,3)$
- $(7,5)$
- $(3,5)$

## Explanation

Measure the displacement from the new origin to the point. The differences are $5-2=3$ horizontally and $4-(-1)=5$ vertically.
