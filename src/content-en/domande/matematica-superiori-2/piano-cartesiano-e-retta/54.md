Two points $A$ and $B$ have horizontal coordinates $-4$ and $6$ and opposite vertical coordinates. Where is their midpoint?

- $(0;1)$
- $(0;0)$
- $(2;0)$
- $(1;0)$

## Explanation

Opposite heights have sum zero, so their average is zero. The horizontal coordinates have sum $-4+6=2$, so their average is $1$. The midpoint is $(1;0)$. It lies on the horizontal axis and is five units horizontally from either endpoint. The sum of the horizontal coordinates is still halved even when the vertical coordinates cancel.
