Find the midpoint of $A(-6;2)$ and $B(8;12)$.

- $\left(7;\,1\right)$
- $\left(2;\,14\right)$
- $\left(1;\,7\right)$
- $\left(-1;\,7\right)$

## Explanation

The midpoint’s x-coordinate is the average of the endpoints’ x-coordinates. The same holds for the y-coordinate. Calculate the two averages separately and preserve the coordinate order.

$$
M=\left(\frac{-6+8}2;\frac{2+12}2\right)=\left(1;\,7\right)
$$
