From $A(-1;3)$ to $B$ the horizontal coordinate increases by $8$ and the height falls by $4$. Which point is reached one quarter of the way from $A$ to $B$?

- $(3;1)$
- $(7;-1)$
- $(1;2)$
- $(-3;4)$

## Explanation

Travelling one quarter of the segment takes a quarter of each displacement. The horizontal coordinate increases by $2$ and the height drops by $1$. Adding these changes to the starting coordinates gives $(1;2)$. The point $(3;1)$ would be the midpoint because it uses half the changes. The endpoint $B$ instead uses both full displacements.
