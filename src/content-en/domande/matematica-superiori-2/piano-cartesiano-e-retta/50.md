What is the distance between $A=(6,13)$ and $B=(27,41)$?

- $175$
- $7$
- $35$
- $49$

## Explanation

The coordinate differences are $21$ and $28$. By the Pythagorean theorem the distance is the square root of the sum of their squares, which gives $35$.
