What is the area of the triangle with vertices $A(1;2)$, $B(7;2)$, and $C(4;-1)$?

- $18$
- $6$
- $9$
- $3$

## Explanation

The points $A$ and $B$ both have height $2$, so the base is horizontal and measures $7-1=6$. The height is the vertical distance from $C$ to $y=2$, or $|-1-2|=3$. Half the product gives area $\dfrac{6\cdot3}{2}=9$. The negative coordinate of $C$ means the vertex is below the base and contributes through the absolute distance.
