How are two nonvertical lines with slopes $2$ and $-\dfrac12$ positioned relative to each other?

- $\text{Perpendicular}$
- $\text{Parallel}$
- $\text{Coincident}$
- $\text{Both horizontal}$

## Explanation

The product of the two slopes is negative one, which is the condition for perpendicularity for nonvertical lines. The slopes differ, so the lines are not parallel.
