For what value of $t$ does the line through $A(-1;4)$ and $B(3;t)$ have slope $-\dfrac12$?

- $6$
- $-2$
- $-4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The $x$-coordinate increases by $4$. Since the slope is $-\dfrac12$, the $y$-coordinate decreases by $2$. Starting from $4$ therefore gives $2$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{t-4}{3-(-1)}&=-\frac12,\\
        t-4&=-2,\\
        t&=2.
\end{aligned}
$$
