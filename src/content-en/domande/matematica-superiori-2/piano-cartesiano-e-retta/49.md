Which point on the line $x=2$ is equidistant from $A(0;0)$ and $B(4;2)$?

- $(2;0)$
- $(2;1)$
- $(2;2)$
- $(2;-1)$

## Explanation

For $P(2;y)$ the horizontal differences have the same absolute value, so they cancel when the squared distances are set equal. It follows that $y$ is halfway between $0$ and $2$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
4+y^2&=4+(y-2)^2,\\
        4y&=4,\qquad y=1.
\end{aligned}
$$
