A tank contains $18$ litres after $2$ minutes and $30$ litres after $5$ minutes. If the inflow is constant, how many litres did it contain initially?

- $12$
- $14$
- $10$
- $26$

## Explanation

The volume increases by $12$ litres in $3$ minutes, so $4$ litres enter each minute. In the first two minutes, $8$ litres entered the tank. Subtracting them from the measured $18$ litres gives the initial volume.

$$
\begin{aligned}
m&=\frac{30-18}{5-2}=4,\\
        q&=18-2\cdot4=10\,\mathrm{L}.
\end{aligned}
$$
