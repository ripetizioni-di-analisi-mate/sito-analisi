A tank drains from $30$ L to $18$ L in $4$ minutes at a constant rate. If time on the graph is changed from minutes to seconds while volume stays in litres, what is the slope?

- $-3\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $-180\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $-\dfrac1{20}\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $\dfrac1{20}\,\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

The change is $18-30=-12$ litres in $4\cdot60=240$ seconds. The ratio is $-12/240=-1/20$ litres per second. The negative sign indicates draining.
