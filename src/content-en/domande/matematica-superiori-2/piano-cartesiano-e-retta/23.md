A pricing plan is described by $C(n)=11+5n$ euros. How much does the cost increase from $6$ to $8$ visits?

- $21$
- $30$
- $5$
- $10$

## Explanation

The fixed fee is unchanged. The cost increases by $5$ euros per visit, so multiply by the two additional visits to obtain $10$.
