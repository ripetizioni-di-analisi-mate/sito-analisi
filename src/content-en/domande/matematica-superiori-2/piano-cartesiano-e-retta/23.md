Volume $V(t)=2+4t$ is in litres and time $t$ is in minutes. Which value with its unit gives the increase in volume per minute?

- $2\,\mathrm{L/min}$
- $4\,\mathrm{min/L}$
- $6\,\mathrm{L/min}$
- $4\,\mathrm{L/min}$

## Explanation

Between two times one minute apart, the term $4t$ increases by $4$ litres. The initial $2$ litres appear in both readings and cancel in the difference. The increase is therefore $4$ litres per minute. The volume of $6$ litres after one minute includes the two starting litres, which do not contribute to the change.
