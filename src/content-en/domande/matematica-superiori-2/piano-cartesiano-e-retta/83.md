Which line contains all points at the same distance from $y=1$ and $y=7$?

- $y=3$
- $x=4$
- $y=4$
- $y=8$

## Explanation

The distances from the horizontal lines are $|y-1|$ and $|y-7|$. For equality the height must be halfway between $1$ and $7$, at $4$. The horizontal coordinate does not affect either distance and can have any value, so the set is $y=4$. Every point on it is three units from each of the original lines, measured perpendicularly.
