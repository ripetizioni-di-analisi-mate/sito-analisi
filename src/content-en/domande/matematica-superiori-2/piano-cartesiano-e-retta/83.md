What is the distance from $(4,5)$ to the horizontal line $y=9$?

- $9$
- $5$
- $4$
- $14$

## Explanation

The shortest segment is vertical and perpendicular to the line. Its length is the absolute difference between the y-coordinates, which gives $4$.
