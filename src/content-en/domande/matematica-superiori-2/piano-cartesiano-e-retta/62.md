At what x-coordinate does $y=3x-7$ intersect the $x$-axis?

- $- \dfrac{7}{3}$
- $21$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $-7$

## Explanation

On the $x$-axis the y-coordinate is zero. Solving $3x-7=0$ gives the x-coordinate at the intersection. The result is $\dfrac{7}{3}$.
