The line $y=2x-4$ is drawn in the square $0\le x\le3$, $0\le y\le3$. Which two points are on both the line and the boundary of the square?

::grafico{f="2*x-4" linee="0,0 3,0 3,3 0,3 0,0" xmin=-0.6 xmax=4.1 ymin=-0.7 ymax=4.3 uguale=1 titolo="The square boundary and the line"}

- $(0,0)$ and $(3,3)$
- $(2,0)$ and $(7/2,3)$
- $(2,0)$ and $(3,2)$
- $(0,-4)$ and $(2,0)$

## Explanation

On the bottom side set $y=0$ to obtain $x=2$. On the right side set $x=3$ to obtain $y=2$. The intersections with the lines containing the other sides lie outside the square.
