Which nonvertical line passes through $P(-2;3)$ and has $y$-intercept $-1$?

- $y=-2x-1$
- $y=2x-1$
- $y=-2x+1$
- $y=-x-1$

## Explanation

The intercept fixes the point $(0;-1)$. From $P$ to this point the $x$-coordinate increases by $2$ and the $y$-coordinate decreases by $4$, so the slope is $-2$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
m&=\frac{-1-3}{0-(-2)}=-2,\\
        y&=-2x-1.
\end{aligned}
$$
