Which vertical line is the same distance from $A(1;2)$ and $B(7;5)$?

- $y=\dfrac72$
- $x=3$
- $x=7$
- $x=4$

## Explanation

For a vertical line $x=k$, distances are measured horizontally. Here they are $|k-1|$ and $|k-7|$. Equality places the line halfway between the horizontal coordinates, at $\dfrac{1+7}{2}=4$. The line $x=4$ is three units from both points. The heights $2$ and $5$ locate the two perpendicular feet without changing the lengths of those segments.
