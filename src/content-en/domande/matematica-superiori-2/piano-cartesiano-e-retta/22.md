What is the slope of a line perpendicular to $y=8x+2$?

- $\dfrac{7}{8}$
- $- \dfrac{9}{8}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{8}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

Both lines are nonvertical and the given slope is nonzero. Perpendicularity requires the product of the slopes to be negative one, so the new slope is the negative reciprocal of the given one.

$$
m_1m_2=-1
$$

$$
m_2=-\frac1{8}
$$
