Which line goes through $(2;5)$ and is perpendicular to $y=-3$?

- $y=5$
- $y=2$
- $x=2$
- $x=-3$

## Explanation

The given line is horizontal because its height stays at $-3$. A perpendicular is vertical and has a fixed horizontal coordinate. To include $(2;5)$ that coordinate must be $2$, giving $x=2$. The height $5$ locates the specified point on the new line. The intersection of the two lines instead has height $-3$ and horizontal coordinate $2$.
