On a floor plan the wall follows $x=2$ and the corridor follows $y=-3$. Which pair of coordinates identifies the doorway at their crossing?

- $(-2,-3)$
- $(2,3)$
- $(2,-3)$
- $(-3,2)$

## Explanation

The first coordinate is $2$ everywhere on the wall. The second coordinate is $-3$ everywhere in the corridor. The doorway must satisfy both conditions.
