The midpoint of $AB$ is $M=(3,7)$, and $A=(0,4)$. What are the coordinates of $B$?

- $\left( 6, \  3\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  3\right)$
- $\left( 6, \  18\right)$
- $\left( 6, \  10\right)$

## Explanation

Each coordinate of $M$ is the average of the corresponding coordinates of the endpoints. Double the midpoint coordinate and subtract the coordinate of $A$.
