The midpoint $M$ of a nonzero segment $AB$ has the same height as $A$. What can be concluded about the segment?

- The segment is vertical
- Its slope is $1$
- Its slope is $-1$
- The segment is horizontal

## Explanation

The relation $\dfrac{y_A+y_B}{2}=y_M=y_A$ gives $y_B=y_A$. All points on the segment therefore have the same height. Since the endpoints are distinct, their horizontal coordinates must differ. The direction is horizontal and the slope is zero. The displacement from $A$ to the midpoint has no vertical component, and the second half repeats that displacement.
