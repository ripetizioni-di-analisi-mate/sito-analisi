Which vertical line goes through the midpoint of $A(-2;3)$ and $B(6;-1)$?

- $y=1$
- $x=1$
- $x=4$
- $x=2$

## Explanation

The midpoint has horizontal coordinate $\dfrac{-2+6}{2}=2$ and height $\dfrac{3-1}{2}=1$. A vertical line keeps every point’s horizontal coordinate fixed, giving $x=2$. The average height locates the midpoint on that line, and other points on it can have other heights. The equation $y=1$ also contains the midpoint but is horizontal.
