A hiker is at kilometre $2$ at $9$ a.m. and walks at $3$ km/h. A second hiker starts from kilometre $0$ at $10$ a.m. and walks in the same direction at $5$ km/h. When does the second catch the first?

- At $11{:}00$ a.m.
- At $1{:}00$ p.m.
- At $12{:}30$ p.m.
- At $11{:}30$ a.m.

## Explanation

At $10$ a.m. the first hiker is already at kilometre $5$. From the second hiker's start the gap closes at $5-3=2$ km per hour. Closing $5$ km takes $5/2$ hours, until $12{:}30$ p.m.
