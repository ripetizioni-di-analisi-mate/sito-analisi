Plan A costs $4+3n$ euros and plan B costs $10+n$ euros. For which integer numbers of visits $n\ge0$ does plan A cost strictly less?

- $\{0;1;2;3\}$
- $\{3;4;5\}$
- $\{0;1;2\}$
- $\{1;2\}$

## Explanation

The comparison requires a strict inequality. The plans cost the same at $n=3$. Since $n$ is a nonnegative integer, the numbers of visits for which $n<3$ are $0$, $1$, and $2$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
4+3n&<10+n,\\
        2n&<6,\\
        n&<3.
\end{aligned}
$$
