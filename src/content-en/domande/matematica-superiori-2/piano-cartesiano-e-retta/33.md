The distance from $P(k;1)$ to the vertical line $x=2$ is $3$. For which values of $k$ does this hold?

- $\{1;5\}$
- $\{-1;5\}$
- $\{-5;1\}$
- $\{5\}$

## Explanation

The horizontal distance is $|k-2|$. A point at distance $3$ may lie three units to the left or three units to the right of the line. The possible $x$-coordinates are $-1$ and $5$.

$$
|k-2|=3\quad\Longrightarrow\quad k=2\pm3.
$$
