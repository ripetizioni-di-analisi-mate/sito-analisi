On a line, moving three units to the left raises the height by six units. What is the slope?

- $2$
- $-\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac12$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The horizontal change is $-3$ and the vertical change is $6$. The slope is the vertical change divided by the horizontal change, so $\dfrac6{-3}=-2$. Traversing the line in the other direction means moving three right and six down, with the same ratio $-2$. The negative sign indicates that the height falls to the right.
