At what x-coordinate does $y=4x-9$ intersect the $x$-axis?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $- \dfrac{9}{4}$
- $36$
- $-9$

## Explanation

On the $x$-axis the y-coordinate is zero. Solving $4x-9=0$ gives the x-coordinate at the intersection. The result is $\dfrac{9}{4}$.
