Where does $2x-3y+6=0$ meet the horizontal and vertical axes, in that order?

- $(-3;0),\ (0;2)$
- $(3;0),\ (0;2)$
- $(-3;0),\ (0;-2)$
- $(0;-3),\ (2;0)$

## Explanation

On the horizontal axis set $y=0$. The equation becomes $2x+6=0$, so the point is $(-3;0)$. On the vertical axis set $x=0$, giving $-3y+6=0$ and the point $(0;2)$. In each pair, the coordinate fixed by the axis must be zero. Substituting both points confirms that each makes the left-hand side zero.
