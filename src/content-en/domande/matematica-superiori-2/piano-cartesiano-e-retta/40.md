What is the distance between the lines $3x+4y-1=0$ and $6x+8y-12=0$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{11}{5}$
- $\dfrac65$

## Explanation

Divide the second equation by $2$ to get $3x+4y-6=0$. The coefficients of the variables now agree. A point on the first line has $3x+4y=1$, so the second expression gives $1-6=-5$. The distance is $\dfrac{|-5|}{\sqrt{3^2+4^2}}=1$. This holds for every point on the first line because $3x+4y$ is constant there.
