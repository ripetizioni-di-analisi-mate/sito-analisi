Where does the line $y=-2$ meet the axes?

- Only $(-2;0)$ on the horizontal axis
- Both $(-2;0)$ and $(0;-2)$
- No point on either axis
- Only $(0;-2)$ on the vertical axis

## Explanation

The line fixes height at $-2$ for every horizontal coordinate. The horizontal axis requires height zero, so both conditions cannot hold together. The vertical axis requires horizontal coordinate zero, which is allowed. Thus the only intersection is $(0;-2)$. The line is parallel to the horizontal axis, with a constant separation of two units.
