At what x-coordinate does $y=5x-11$ intersect the $x$-axis?

- $-11$
- $55$
- $- \dfrac{11}{5}$
- $\dfrac{11}{5}$

## Explanation

On the $x$-axis the y-coordinate is zero. Solving $5x-11=0$ gives the x-coordinate at the intersection. The result is $\dfrac{11}{5}$.
