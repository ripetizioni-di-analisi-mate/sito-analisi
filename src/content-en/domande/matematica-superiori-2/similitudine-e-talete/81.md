A drawing at scale $1:400$ is reprinted with all lengths on the sheet halved. What is the scale of the new drawing?

- $1:200$
- $1:800$
- $1:400$
- $1:1600$

## Explanation

At scale $1:400$ one centimetre of the drawing represents $400$ real centimetres. After the reprint that same segment measures half a centimetre and still represents $400$ real centimetres. Hence one centimetre of the new drawing represents $2\cdot400=800$ real centimetres, and the new scale is $1:800$. The scale $1:200$ would come from doubling the lengths on the sheet, while $1:1600$ comes from squaring the factor $2$ as for areas.
