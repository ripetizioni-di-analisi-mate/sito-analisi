A drawing at scale $1:400$ is reprinted with all paper lengths halved. What is its new scale?

- $1:200$
- $1:800$
- $1:400$
- $1:1600$

## Explanation

A real length of $400$ units now takes up half a unit on paper. One unit in the reprint therefore represents $800$ actual units.
