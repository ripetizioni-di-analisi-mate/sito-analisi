A real segment $100$ m long is drawn at scale $1:2000$. How many centimeters long is it on the map?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $5$
- $500$
- $50$

## Explanation

Convert the real length from meters to centimeters and divide by the denominator of the scale. The factor $2000$ cancels. This gives $5$.
