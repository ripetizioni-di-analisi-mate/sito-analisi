Right triangles $ABC\sim DEF$ have right angles at $A,D$ and perimeter ratio $P_{DEF}/P_{ABC}=2/3$. The altitudes to their hypotenuses meet them at $H$ and $K$. If $BH=9$, what is $EK$?

- $\frac{27}{2}$
- $4$
- $9$
- $6$

## Explanation

The perimeter ratio gives the length scale factor. The altitudes from $A$ and $D$ correspond, so their feet $H$ and $K$ also match. Segment $BH$ maps to $EK$, of length $9\cdot2/3=6$. The squared ratio for projections applies to two legs within one triangle. Here we compare corresponding projections in two similar triangles.
