Two similar figures have areas of $6$ cm² and $150$ cm². The corresponding side in the first measures $11$ cm. How many centimeters long is it in the second?

- $\dfrac{11}{5}$
- $275$
- $11$
- $55$

## Explanation

The area ratio is the square of the ratio of side lengths. Take the square root and multiply by the original length. This gives $55$.
