In triangle $ABC$, segment $DE$ joins sides $AB$ and $AC$ and is parallel to $BC$. The area of $ADE$ is $9/16$ of the area of $ABC$. If $BC=16$, find $DE$.

- $9$
- $16$
- $12$
- $\frac{64}{3}$

## Explanation

The parallel to the base makes the two triangles similar. The value $9/16$ compares areas, so the length scale factor is the positive square root $3/4$. Segment $DE$ corresponds to $BC$, giving $DE=(3/4)16=12$. Check that squaring $12/16$ gives $9/16$. Using the area fraction as a length scale factor would produce a segment that is too short.
