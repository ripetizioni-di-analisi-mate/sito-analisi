In triangle $ABC$, $DE$ is parallel to $BC$, with $D$ on $AB$ and $E$ on $AC$. If $AD=5$ cm, $AB=10$ cm, and $DE=11$ cm, how many centimeters long is $BC$?

- $11$
- $\dfrac{11}{2}$
- $22$
- $44$

## Explanation

The triangles are similar because their corresponding angles are equal. The ratio of their bases equals the ratio of $AD$ to $AB$. This gives $22$.
