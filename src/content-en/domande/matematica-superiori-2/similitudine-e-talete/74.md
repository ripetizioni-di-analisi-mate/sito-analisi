In triangle $ABC$, segment $DE$ joins sides $AB$ and $AC$ and is parallel to $BC$. The area of $ADE$ is $\dfrac{9}{16}$ of the area of $ABC$. If $BC=16$, find $DE$.

- $9$
- $16$
- $12$
- $\frac{64}{3}$

## Explanation

The parallel to the base makes the two triangles similar. The value $\dfrac{9}{16}$ compares areas, so the length scale factor is the positive square root $\dfrac{3}{4}$. Segment $DE$ corresponds to $BC$, giving $DE=\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot 16=12$. Check that squaring $\dfrac{12}{16}$ gives $\dfrac{9}{16}$. Using the area fraction as a length scale factor would produce a segment that is too short.
