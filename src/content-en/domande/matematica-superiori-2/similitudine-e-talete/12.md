A rectangle initially has base $3$ and height $2$. Its base is doubled and its height is tripled. By what factor does its area change?

- $6$
- $4$
- $9$
- $5$

## Explanation

The initial area is $bh$ and the final area is $(2b)(3h)=6bh$. Multiply the two dimension factors. There is no common length scale factor here because one direction doubles and the other triples. The side ratios differ, so the rule of squaring a common scale factor does not apply. With base $3$ and height $2$, the area goes from $6$ to $36$.
