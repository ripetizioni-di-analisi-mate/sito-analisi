A figure has area $5$ cm². A similar figure has a length scale factor of $8$ relative to the first. Find the new area.

- $320$ cm²
- $321$ cm²
- $319$ cm²
- $640$ cm²

## Explanation

The base and altitude are both multiplied by the length scale factor. Their product is therefore multiplied by the square of that factor. The area follows the same rule.

$$
A_2=k^2A_1=8^2\cdot5=320
$$
