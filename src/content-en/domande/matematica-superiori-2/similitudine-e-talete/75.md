A line parallel to one side cuts a triangle into a similar triangle with length factor $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and a trapezoid of area $50$. What is the area of the whole triangle?

- $75$
- $90$
- $112.5$
- $150$

## Explanation

The smaller triangle occupies $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^2=\dfrac{4}{9}$ of the whole area. The trapezoid occupies the complement, $\dfrac{5}{9}$. Hence $\dfrac{5}{9}A=50$ and the whole area is $50\cdot\dfrac{9}{5}=90$. Check that the smaller area is $40$ and adding the trapezoid’s area $50$ restores $90$. The given area belongs to the remainder and so uses the complement of the squared ratio.
