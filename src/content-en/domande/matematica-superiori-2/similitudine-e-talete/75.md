A line parallel to one side cuts a triangle into a similar triangle with length factor $2/3$ and a trapezoid of area $50$. What is the area of the whole triangle?

- $75$
- $90$
- $112.5$
- $150$

## Explanation

The smaller triangle occupies $(2/3)^2=4/9$ of the whole area. The trapezoid occupies the complement, $5/9$. Hence $(5/9)A=50$ and the whole area is $50\cdot9/5=90$. Check that the smaller area is $40$ and adding the trapezoid’s area $50$ restores $90$. The given area belongs to the remainder and so uses the complement of the squared ratio.
