Two similar figures have area ratio $81$. The shorter altitude measures $4$ cm. How many centimeters long is the longer altitude?

- $13$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $36$
- $324$

## Explanation

Corresponding altitudes are lengths. Their ratio is the square root of the area ratio. This gives $36$.
