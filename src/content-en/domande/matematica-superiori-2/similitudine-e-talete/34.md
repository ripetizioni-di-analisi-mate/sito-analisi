Two similar obtuse triangles have scale factor $\dfrac{2}{3}$ from the first to the second. An altitude of the first to an extended base has length $6$. What is the corresponding altitude of the second?

- $9$
- $\frac83$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The altitude is the perpendicular distance from the vertex to the line containing the base. Similarity maps the line and vertex to their counterparts, preserves the perpendicular direction, and multiplies every distance by the same factor. This holds even when the foot lies on an extension. Thus the new altitude is $6\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}=4$. Squaring the factor would use an area ratio in place of a length ratio.
