In $ABC$, angles $A$ and $B$ are $42^\circ$ and $66^\circ$. In $DEF$, angles $E$ and $F$ are $42^\circ$ and $72^\circ$. Which ordered similarity statement is correct?

- $ABC\sim DEF$
- $ABC\sim EFD$
- $ABC\sim FED$
- $ABC\sim EDF$

## Explanation

The interior angles add to $180^\circ$. The first triangle therefore has $C=72^\circ$, and the second has $D=66^\circ$. Equal angles match $A$ with $E$, $B$ with $D$, and $C$ with $F$. Two pairs suffice for the similarity criterion, but checking the third also helps write the vertices and sides in the correct order.
