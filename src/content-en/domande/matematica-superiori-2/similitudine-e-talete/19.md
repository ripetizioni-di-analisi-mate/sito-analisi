Two triangles have corresponding angles of $33^\circ$ and $54^\circ$. What can be concluded?

- They must have the same perimeter.
- They must be right triangles.
- They must be congruent.
- They are similar.

## Explanation

The third angle is determined because the angles add up to one hundred eighty degrees. The criterion of two equal angles guarantees similarity.
