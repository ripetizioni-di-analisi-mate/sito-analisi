A plan at scale $1:250$ shows a corridor as $5.6\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. What is the actual length in metres?

- $14$
- $140$
- $1.4$
- $1400$

## Explanation

One centimetre on the plan stands for $250$ real centimetres, or $2.5$ metres. Multiplying the plan measurement by this correspondence gives $5.6\cdot2.5=14$ metres. Alternatively, multiplying by $250$ gives $1400$ centimetres, which must then be divided by one hundred. The scale compares lengths in the same unit. Converting to metres is a separate step.
