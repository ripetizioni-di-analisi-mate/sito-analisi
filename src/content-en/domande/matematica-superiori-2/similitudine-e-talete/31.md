In $ABC$, $D$ is inside $AB$ and $E$ inside $AC$. If $AD=6$, $DB=3$, and $AE=8$, which length of $EC$ makes $DE$ parallel to $BC$?

- $3$
- $6$
- $12$
- $4$

## Explanation

The first division gives the ratio between its parts, $\dfrac{AD}{DB}=\dfrac{6}{3}=2$. The other side needs the same ratio $\dfrac{AE}{EC}=2$, so $EC=\dfrac{8}{2}=4$. That length gives equal fractions of the whole sides: $\dfrac{6}{6+3}=\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\dfrac{8}{8+4}=\dfrac{2}{3}$. The converse gives parallelism because both points lie on their sides at the same fraction of the way from $A$.
