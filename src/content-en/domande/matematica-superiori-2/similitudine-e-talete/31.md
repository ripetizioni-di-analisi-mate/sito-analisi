In $ABC$, $D$ is inside $AB$ and $E$ inside $AC$. If $AD=6$, $DB=3$, and $AE=8$, which length of $EC$ makes $DE$ parallel to $BC$?

- $3$
- $6$
- $12$
- $4$

## Explanation

The first division gives the ratio between its parts, $AD/DB=6/3=2$. The other side needs the same ratio $AE/EC=2$, so $EC=8/2=4$. That length gives equal fractions of the whole sides: $6/(6+3)=2/3$ and $8/(8+4)=2/3$. The converse gives parallelism because both points lie on their sides at the same fraction of the way from $A$.
