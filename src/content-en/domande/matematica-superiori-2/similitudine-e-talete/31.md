In triangle $ABC$, $D$ lies on $AB$, $E$ lies on $AC$, and $DE\parallel BC$. Given $\dfrac{AD}{AB}=\dfrac13$ and $BC=33$ cm, find $DE$.

- $12$ cm
- $10$ cm
- $22$ cm
- $11$ cm

## Explanation

The parallel sides give congruent corresponding angles, so the triangles are similar. The ratio of the shorter side to the longer side is one third. The segment parallel to the base is therefore also one third of the base.

$$
\triangle ADE\sim\triangle ABC
$$

$$
\frac{DE}{BC}=\frac{AD}{AB}=\frac13
$$

$$
DE=\frac{33}3=11
$$
