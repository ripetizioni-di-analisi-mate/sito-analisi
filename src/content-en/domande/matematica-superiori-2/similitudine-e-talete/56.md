A similarity changes a figure’s perimeter from $5$ cm to $10$ cm. What is the ratio of the new area to the original area?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $4$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The ratio of perimeters equals the ratio of lengths. For the areas, square that ratio. This gives $4$.
