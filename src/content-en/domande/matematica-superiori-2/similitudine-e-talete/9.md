An enlargement takes a side from length $10$ to length $25$. Which factor takes the enlarged figure back to the original?

- $\frac52$
- $\frac25$
- $\frac35$
- $\frac14$

## Explanation

To return to the original figure, the starting length for the new step is $25$ and the destination length is $10$. The ratio of destination to starting length is $\dfrac{10}{25}=\dfrac{2}{5}$. It is the reciprocal of the enlargement factor $\dfrac{5}{2}$. The two factors multiply to one, and the return factor is less than one, as expected for a reduction.
