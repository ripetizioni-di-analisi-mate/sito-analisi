In a photograph of a façade, a $4$ m horizontal segment appears $2$ cm long and a $6$ m vertical segment appears $4$ cm long. What follows about the horizontal and vertical scales?

- Both are $1:200$
- Both are $1:150$
- They differ, $1:2$ and $1:3$
- They differ, $1:200$ and $1:150$

## Explanation

In centimetres the ratios are $2:400$ and $4:600$. Their reduced forms differ, so a single similarity does not describe the photograph.
