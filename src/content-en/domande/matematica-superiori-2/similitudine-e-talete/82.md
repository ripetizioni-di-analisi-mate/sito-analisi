A real segment $210$ m long is drawn at scale $1:3000$. How many centimetres long is it on the map?

- $70$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$
- $700$
- $7$

## Explanation

Convert the real length from metres to centimetres and divide by the denominator of the scale. The factor $3000$ cancels. This gives $7$.
