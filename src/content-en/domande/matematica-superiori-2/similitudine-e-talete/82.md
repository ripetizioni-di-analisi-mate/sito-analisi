In a drawing of a façade, a horizontal segment that is $4$ m long in reality measures $2$ cm, and a vertical segment $6$ m long measures $4$ cm. Is the drawing a scale reproduction of the façade?

- Yes, at scale $1:200$
- Yes, at scale $1:150$
- No, because the two ratios are $1:2$ and $2:3$
- No, because the two ratios are $1:200$ and $1:150$

## Explanation

A scale drawing reduces all lengths by the same ratio, and the ratios are compared with the lengths expressed in the same unit. The real horizontal segment measures $400$ cm, so its ratio is $2:400=1:200$. The real vertical segment measures $600$ cm, so its ratio is $4:600=1:150$. Since the two ratios are different, the drawing is not a scale reproduction of the façade. The ratios $1:2$ and $2:3$ come from comparing centimetres and metres without converting them.
