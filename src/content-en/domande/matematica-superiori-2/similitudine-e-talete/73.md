In triangle $ABC$, $DE$ is parallel to $BC$, with $D$ on $AB$ and $E$ on $AC$. If $AD=4$ cm, $AB=8$ cm, and $DE=9$ cm, how many centimeters long is $BC$?

- $36$
- $18$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$

## Explanation

The triangles are similar because their corresponding angles are equal. The ratio of their bases equals the ratio of $AD$ to $AB$. This gives $18$.
