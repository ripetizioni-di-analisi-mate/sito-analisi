In $ABC$, $D\in AB$, $E\in AC$, $AD=3$, $AB=8$, $AE=6$, and $AC=15$. What follows about whether $DE$ and $BC$ are parallel?

- They are parallel because $AE$ is twice $AD$.
- They are not parallel because $AD/AB\ne AE/AC$.
- They are parallel because $D$ and $E$ are interior.
- An angle measurement is needed to decide.

## Explanation

Parallelism would require $AD/AB=AE/AC$. Cross multiplication gives $3\cdot15=45$ and $6\cdot8=48$, so the fractions differ. This is enough to rule out parallelism. The fact that $AE$ is twice $AD$ would matter only if $AC$ were also twice $AB$. Here the parts occupy different fractions of their whole sides.
