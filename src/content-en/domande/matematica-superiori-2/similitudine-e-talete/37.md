In triangle $ABC$ with a right angle at $A$, the altitude to $BC$ meets it at $H$. Given $BH/CH=25/49$, find $AB/AC$.

- $\frac{25}{49}$
- $\frac57$
- $\frac75$
- $\frac{49}{25}$

## Explanation

Similarity of the triangles formed by the altitude gives $AB^2=BH\cdot BC$ and $AC^2=CH\cdot BC$. Dividing cancels the common factor $BC$, making the projection ratio the square of $AB/AC$. The positive square root of $25/49$ is $5/7$. Since $BH$ is the shorter projection, its corresponding leg must also be shorter. This checks the direction of the ratio.
