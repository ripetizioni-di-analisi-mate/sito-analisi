In triangle $ABC$ with a right angle at $A$, the altitude to $BC$ meets it at $H$. Given $\dfrac{BH}{CH}=\dfrac{25}{49}$, find $\dfrac{AB}{AC}$.

- $\frac{25}{49}$
- $\frac57$
- $\frac75$
- $\frac{49}{25}$

## Explanation

Similarity of the triangles formed by the altitude gives $AB^2=BH\cdot BC$ and $AC^2=CH\cdot BC$. Dividing cancels the common factor $BC$, making the projection ratio the square of $\dfrac{AB}{AC}$. The positive square root of $\dfrac{25}{49}$ is $\dfrac{5}{7}$. Since $BH$ is the shorter projection, its corresponding leg must also be shorter. This checks the direction of the ratio.
