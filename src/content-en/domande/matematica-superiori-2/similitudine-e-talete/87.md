A pole and a tree stand upright on level ground. The pole’s shadow is measured in the morning and the tree’s at noon. What information is needed to use a direct proportion between heights and shadow lengths?

- Equal trunk widths
- The same inclination of sunlight at both measurements
- Identical shadow colours
- Equal distances between the bases and between the tops

## Explanation

Both triangles are right-angled. Similarity also requires equal angles between sunlight and the ground, which different times do not guarantee.
