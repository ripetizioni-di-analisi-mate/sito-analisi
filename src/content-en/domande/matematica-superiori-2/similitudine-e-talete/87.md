At the same instant, a pole $3$ m tall casts a $4$ m shadow. A tree casts a shadow $28$ m long. How many meters tall is the tree?

- $7$
- $21$
- $28$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$

## Explanation

The parallel rays form similar triangles with the verticals. The tree’s shadow is $7$ times as long as the pole’s shadow, so the same is true of their heights. This gives $21$.
