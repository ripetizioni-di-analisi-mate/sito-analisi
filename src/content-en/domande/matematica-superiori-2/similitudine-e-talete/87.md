A mirror lies flat on the ground, $6$ m from the base of a tree. A person whose eyes are $1.5$ m above the ground walks away from the mirror on the side opposite the tree and sees the top of the tree reflected in the mirror when standing $2$ m from it. By the law of reflection, the incoming ray and the reflected ray make equal angles with the ground. How tall is the tree?

- $8$ m
- $4.5$ m
- $6$ m
- $3$ m

## Explanation

The tree and the person are vertical, so each of them forms with the ground and the light ray a right triangle with a vertex at the mirror. The angles of the two triangles at the mirror are equal by the law of reflection, so the triangles are similar by the first similarity criterion. It follows that the height $h$ of the tree is to $6$ m as the eye height is to $2$ m, that is $\dfrac{h}{6}=\dfrac{1.5}{2}$. Hence $h=\dfrac{6\cdot1.5}{2}=4.5$ m. The value $8$ m comes from the proportion with the legs swapped, $\dfrac{h}{6}=\dfrac{2}{1.5}$.
