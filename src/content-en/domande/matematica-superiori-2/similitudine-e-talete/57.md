A similarity changes a figure’s perimeter from $7$ cm to $21$ cm. What is the ratio of the new area to the original area?

- $9$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $3$

## Explanation

The ratio of perimeters equals the ratio of lengths. For the areas, square that ratio. This gives $9$.
