A figure similar to an original has perimeter $60$ instead of $45$. What is its area divided by the original area?

- $\frac{16}{9}$
- $\frac43$
- $\frac9{16}$
- $\frac32$

## Explanation

Perimeter measures total length, so the ratio $60/45=4/3$ gives the length factor. Area includes the change in two dimensions, so the factor becomes $(4/3)^2=16/9$. This ratio is above one and greater than the length factor, as expected when squaring a number above one. Its reciprocal would describe the return to the original figure.
