A triangle has sides of lengths $4$ and $6$ with a $40^\circ$ included angle. In the other triangle the sides forming the $40^\circ$ angle measure $6$ and $9$. What follows?

- The triangles are congruent.
- The triangles are similar with scale factor $2$ from the first to the second.
- The length of the third side is needed to decide.
- The triangles are similar with scale factor $\dfrac32$ from the first to the second.

## Explanation

The ratios of both pairs of sides are $\dfrac32$. The equal angles are between those sides, so the two-sides-and-included-angle criterion applies.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac64&=\frac96=\frac32
\end{aligned}
$$
