Which pair of rectangles shows that equal areas do not guarantee similarity?

- Sides $2,8$ and $4,16$.
- Sides $2,8$ and $4,4$.
- Sides $2,8$ and $3,4$.
- Sides $2,8$ and $8,2$.

## Explanation

The rectangles with sides $2,8$ and $4,4$ both have area $16$. One is a square, while the other has a ratio of long side to short side of $4$. The rectangles are therefore not similar. Merely swapping the two measurements instead gives the same rectangle rotated. The counterexample must preserve the area while failing to have a common scale factor between the sides.
