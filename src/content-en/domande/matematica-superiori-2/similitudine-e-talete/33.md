The area ratio of two similar triangles, larger to smaller, is $\dfrac{49}{16}$. An altitude of the smaller is $8$. What is the corresponding altitude in the larger?

- $28$
- $14$
- $24$
- $\frac{49}{2}$

## Explanation

Recover the length factor by taking the positive square root of $\dfrac{49}{16}$, giving $\dfrac{7}{4}$. The altitude to the corresponding base is a length, so the new measurement is $8\cdot\dfrac{7}{4}=14$. It is greater than $8$ as expected. Check in reverse: the altitude ratio is $\dfrac{14}{8}=\dfrac{7}{4}$, and its square recovers the area ratio.
