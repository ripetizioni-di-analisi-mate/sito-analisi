Two similar figures have area ratio $49$. The shorter altitude measures $3$ cm. How many centimeters long is the longer altitude?

- $147$
- $21$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$

## Explanation

Corresponding altitudes are lengths. Their ratio is the square root of the area ratio. This gives $21$.
