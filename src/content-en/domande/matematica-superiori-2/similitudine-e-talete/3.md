A polygon has perimeter $12$ cm. A similar figure has scale factor $7$ relative to the original. What is the perimeter of the similar figure?

- $85$ cm
- $83$ cm
- $168$ cm
- $84$ cm

## Explanation

Each side is multiplied by the scale factor. Consequently, their sum, the perimeter, is multiplied by the same factor. Perimeter scales linearly.

$$
P_2=kP_1=7\cdot12=84
$$
