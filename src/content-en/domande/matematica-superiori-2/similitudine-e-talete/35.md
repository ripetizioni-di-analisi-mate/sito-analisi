A polygon has perimeter $12$ cm. A similar figure has scale factor $11$ relative to the original. Find the new perimeter.

- $133$ cm
- $131$ cm
- $264$ cm
- $132$ cm

## Explanation

Each side is multiplied by the scale factor. Consequently, their sum, the perimeter, is multiplied by the same factor. Perimeter scales linearly.

$$
P_2=kP_1=11\cdot12=132
$$
