Two similar figures have perimeters $30$ and $45$. One side of the second measures $18$. What is the corresponding side in the first?

- $27$
- $18$
- $15$
- $12$

## Explanation

To go from the second figure back to the first, take the perimeter ratio in that order, $\dfrac{30}{45}=\dfrac{2}{3}$. Corresponding lengths use the same factor, so the requested side is $18\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}=12$. The perimeter works because it sums sides all transformed in the same way. The factor $\dfrac{45}{30}$ would describe enlargement from the first figure to the second.
