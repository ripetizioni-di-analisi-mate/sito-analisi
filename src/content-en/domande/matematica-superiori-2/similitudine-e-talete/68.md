Three parallel lines cross two transversals. The consecutive segments on the first transversal measure $4$ and $9$ cm. On the second transversal, the segment corresponding to $4$ measures $12$ cm. How many centimeters long is the other segment?

- $81$
- $9$
- $27$
- $3$

## Explanation

By the intercept theorem, corresponding segments have the same ratio. The first segment triples, so the second triples as well. This gives $27$.
