Three horizontal parallels are crossed by the same oblique transversal. Between the first two it moves $3$ horizontally and $4$ vertically. Between the last two it rises by $6$. How far does it move horizontally there?

- $8$
- $9$
- $\frac92$
- $2$

## Explanation

In both strips, the horizontal and vertical displacements are legs of a right triangle. The transversal makes the same acute angle with each parallel, so the triangles are similar. The vertical leg goes from $4$ to $6$, with factor $\dfrac{3}{2}$. The horizontal leg grows in the same ratio and equals $3\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}=\dfrac{9}{2}$. The proportion compares the projections of the displacement.
