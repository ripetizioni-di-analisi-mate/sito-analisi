Two square tiles have side lengths $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $9\,\mathrm{cm}$. How many small tiles have the same total area as four large tiles?

- $6$
- $4$
- $12$
- $9$

## Explanation

The ratio of the sides from the small tile to the large tile is $\dfrac32$. The area ratio is $\dfrac94$. In terms of area alone, four large tiles therefore cover the same area as nine small tiles.

$$
\begin{aligned}
4\left(\frac96\right)^2&=4\cdot\frac94=9
\end{aligned}
$$
