One figure is an enlargement of another. A side goes from $2$ cm to $10$ cm. What factor multiplies the original lengths?

- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $10$
- $5$

## Explanation

The requested scale factor is the new length divided by the original length. The factor $2$ cancels. This gives $5$.
