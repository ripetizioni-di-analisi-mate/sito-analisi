A real distance of $45\,\mathrm m$ is drawn at scale $1:1500$. What is its length on the map in centimetres?

- $3$
- $30$
- $0.3$
- $300$

## Explanation

A scale compares lengths in the same unit. Converting $45$ metres gives $4500$ centimetres. To go from reality to the map, divide by $1500$ to get $3$ centimetres. The reverse check multiplies the map length by the denominator of the scale, returning $4500$ centimetres, or $45$ metres. A drawing at this scale reduces every length.
