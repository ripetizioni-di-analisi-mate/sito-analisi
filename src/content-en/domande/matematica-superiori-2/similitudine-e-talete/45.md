In the triangle similarity criterion with two proportional sides, which angle must be congruent?

- $\text{Any angle}$
- $\text{The angle between those sides}$
- $\text{Always the right angle}$
- $\text{Only the largest angle}$

## Explanation

The given sides must form the angle being compared. Equality of that angle and proportionality of the sides guarantee the same shape. An angle outside the pair of sides generally does not give the same criterion.
