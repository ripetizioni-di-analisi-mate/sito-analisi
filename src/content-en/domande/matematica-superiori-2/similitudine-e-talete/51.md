Two similar triangles have corresponding sides of $2$ and $6$ cm. A median in the smaller triangle measures $3$ cm. How many centimeters long is its counterpart in the larger triangle?

- $1$
- $27$
- $9$
- $3$

## Explanation

A similarity multiplies all corresponding lengths by the same factor. The ratio of the sides gives a factor of three. This gives $9$.
