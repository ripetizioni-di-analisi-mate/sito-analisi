A map has scale $1:6000$. How many metres in reality correspond to $3$ cm on the map?

- $180$ m
- $181$ m
- $179$ m
- $360$ m

## Explanation

A scale expresses the ratio of lengths in the same unit. Multiply the centimetres on the map by the denominator of the scale. Then divide the resulting centimetres by one hundred to convert to metres.

$$
d=18000\,\mathrm{cm}
$$

$$
d=\frac{18000}{100}\,\mathrm{m}=180\,\mathrm{m}
$$
