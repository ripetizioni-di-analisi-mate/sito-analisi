A map has scale $1:6000$. How many meters in reality correspond to $3$ cm on the map?

- $180$ m
- $181$ m
- $179$ m
- $360$ m

## Explanation

A scale expresses the ratio of lengths in the same unit. Multiply the centimeters on the map by the denominator of the scale. Then divide the resulting centimeters by one hundred to convert to meters.

$$
d=18000\,\mathrm{cm}
$$

$$
d=\frac{18000}{100}\,\mathrm{m}=180\,\mathrm{m}
$$
