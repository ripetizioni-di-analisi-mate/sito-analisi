Every length of a polygon grows by $10\%$ under a similarity transformation. How do its perimeter and area change?

- Both grow by $10\%$.
- The perimeter grows by $10\%$ and the area by $20\%$.
- The perimeter grows by $10\%$ and the area by $21\%$.
- Both grow by $21\%$.

## Explanation

Each side is multiplied by $\dfrac{11}{10}$, so their sum also grows by $10\%$. Area is multiplied by $\left(\dfrac{11}{10}\right)^2=\dfrac{121}{100}$. The increase is $21\%$ because $121\%$ includes the starting area. A square of side $10$ becomes one of side $11$: the difference between $121$ and $100$ shows the effect of changing both dimensions.
