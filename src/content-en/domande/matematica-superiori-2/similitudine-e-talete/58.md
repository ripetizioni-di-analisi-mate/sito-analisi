A similarity changes a figure’s perimeter from $9$ cm to $36$ cm. What is the ratio of the new area to the original area?

- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $16$
- $8$

## Explanation

The ratio of perimeters equals the ratio of lengths. For the areas, square that ratio. This gives $16$.
