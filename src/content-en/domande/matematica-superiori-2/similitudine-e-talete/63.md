In right triangle $ABC$ with right angle at $A$, $AB:AC=2:3$ and $BC=65$. The altitude from $A$ meets $BC$ at $H$. What is the length of $BH$?

- $26$
- $20$
- $45$
- $13$

## Explanation

Each squared leg equals the hypotenuse times its corresponding projection. The hypotenuse is common, so $BH:CH=AB^2:AC^2=4:9$. The projections add to $65$, so each of the thirteen parts is $5$. Projection $BH$ takes four parts and has length $20$. Dividing in the original linear ratio $2:3$ would confuse the legs with their projections.
