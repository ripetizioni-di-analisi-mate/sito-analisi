Two similar figures have areas of $5$ cm² and $80$ cm². The corresponding side in the first measures $9$ cm. How many centimeters long is it in the second?

- $144$
- $36$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $9$

## Explanation

The area ratio is the square of the ratio of side lengths. Take the square root and multiply by the original length. This gives $36$.
