Three parallel lines cross two transversals. The consecutive segments on the first transversal measure $9$ and $18$ cm. The segment corresponding to the first measures $27$ cm on the second transversal. Find the other segment.

- $55$ cm
- $53$ cm
- $54$ cm
- $108$ cm

## Explanation

The intercept theorem guarantees that corresponding segments on the two transversals are proportional. Write each ratio in the same order. Equating the cross products isolates the unknown segment.

$$
\frac{9}{18}=\frac{27}x
$$

$$
x=\frac{18\cdot27}{9}=54
$$
