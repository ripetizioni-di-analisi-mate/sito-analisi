Which assumption allows the intercept theorem to be used for three distinct lines crossing two transversals?

- The two transversals are perpendicular to each other.
- The two drawn transversals have equal lengths.
- The three lines being crossed are parallel to one another.
- The two segments on one transversal have different lengths.

## Explanation

The theorem compares strips bounded by the same parallel lines. Tilting a transversal changes lengths along that line by one factor across every strip. The ratios within each transversal are therefore preserved. If the three crossed lines are not parallel, the separation between two of them can change from one intersection to another, so the theorem’s condition is absent.
