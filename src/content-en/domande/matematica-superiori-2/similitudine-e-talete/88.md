On level ground, a $2\,\mathrm m$ pole casts a $3\,\mathrm m$ shadow. At the same instant, how long is the shadow of another vertical pole that measures $8\,\mathrm m$?

- $\dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm m$
- $12\,\mathrm m$
- $8\,\mathrm m$
- $9\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The sun’s rays are parallel and both poles are vertical. The triangles are therefore similar. The second pole is four times as tall as the first, so the same factor multiplies its shadow length.

$$
\begin{aligned}
l&=3\cdot\frac82=12\,\mathrm m
\end{aligned}
$$
