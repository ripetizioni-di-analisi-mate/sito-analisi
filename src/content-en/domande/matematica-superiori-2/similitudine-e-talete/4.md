A figure has area $5$ cm². A similar figure has a length scale factor of $7$ relative to the first. Find the new area.

- $245$ cm²
- $246$ cm²
- $244$ cm²
- $490$ cm²

## Explanation

The base and altitude are both multiplied by the length scale factor. Their product is therefore multiplied by the square of that factor. The area follows the same rule.

$$
A_2=k^2A_1=7^2\cdot5=245
$$
