After a similarity transformation, a figure’s area is $144\%$ of the original. By what percentage do corresponding lengths increase?

- $44\%$
- $20\%$
- $12\%$
- $120\%$

## Explanation

The area ratio is $144/100$, so the length factor is the positive root $12/10=1.2$. Lengths become $120\%$ of their originals, an increase of $20\%$. A square provides a check: a side of $10$ and area of $100$ become a side of $12$ and area of $144$. The final percentage includes the original amount, while the increase counts only the extra portion.
