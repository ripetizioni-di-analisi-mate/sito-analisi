One figure is an enlargement of another. A side goes from $4$ cm to $36$ cm. What factor multiplies the original lengths?

- $9$
- $36$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

The requested scale factor is the new length divided by the original length. The factor $4$ cancels. This gives $9$.
