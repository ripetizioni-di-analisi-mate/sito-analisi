Two rectangles have the same perimeter of $30$. The shorter side of the first measures $4$, and that of the second measures $5$. What follows?

- They are not similar.
- They are congruent.
- They are similar with scale factor $5/4$.
- They are similar because all their angles are right.

## Explanation

If the rectangles were similar, their equal perimeters would give scale factor one. The shorter side would keep its length, but it goes from $4$ to $5$. Also, half the perimeter is $15$, so the longer sides measure $11$ and $10$. One ratio is greater than one and the other less than one. Matching right angles alone cannot resolve that mismatch.
