Three parallel lines cross two transversals. The consecutive segments on the first transversal measure $3$ and $7$ cm. On the second transversal, the segment corresponding to $3$ measures $9$ cm. How many centimeters long is the other segment?

- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $63$
- $7$
- $21$

## Explanation

By the intercept theorem, corresponding segments have the same ratio. The first segment triples, so the second triples as well. This gives $21$.
