Three parallel lines meet one transversal at ordered points $A,B,C$ and the other at corresponding points $D,E,F$. Which proportion compares the first segment on each with its whole length?

- $\frac{AB}{BC}=\frac{DE}{DF}$
- $\frac{AB}{AC}=\frac{EF}{DF}$
- $\frac{AC}{AB}=\frac{DE}{DF}$
- $\frac{AB}{AC}=\frac{DE}{DF}$

## Explanation

The first pieces are bounded by the first two parallel lines, giving $AB$ and $DE$. The totals run from the first to the last parallel, giving $AC$ and $DF$. Thus compare $AB/AC$ with $DE/DF$. A fraction such as $AB/BC$ instead compares two pieces and can be equated to $DE/EF$. Keep both the comparison type and its direction consistent.
