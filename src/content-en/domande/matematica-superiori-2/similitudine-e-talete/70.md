Three parallels cut the first transversal into two segments in the ratio $3:4$. On the second, the corresponding lengths are $2x-2$ and $x+4$. What is $x$?

- $2$
- $5$
- $4$
- $3$

## Explanation

The intercept theorem preserves the ratio of the two strips in the same order. Set $\dfrac{2x-2}{x+4}=\dfrac{3}{4}$, giving $8x-8=3x+12$ and $x=4$. At this value the lengths are $6$ and $8$, both positive, and their ratio reduces to $\dfrac{3}{4}$. Check the lengths after solving the equation because the segments must have valid measures.
