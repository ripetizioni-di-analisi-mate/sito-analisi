Three parallel lines cross two transversals. The consecutive segments on the first transversal measure $6$ and $13$ cm. On the second transversal, the segment corresponding to $6$ measures $18$ cm. How many centimeters long is the other segment?

- $117$
- $\dfrac{13}{3}$
- $39$
- $13$

## Explanation

By the intercept theorem, corresponding segments have the same ratio. The first segment triples, so the second triples as well. This gives $39$.
