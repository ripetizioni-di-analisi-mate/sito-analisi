Three parallel lines meet two transversals at ordered points $A,B,C$ and $A\prime,B\prime,C\prime$. If $AB=6$, $AC=15$, and $A\prime C\prime=20$, find $A\prime B\prime$.

- $12$
- $10$
- $8$
- $9$

## Explanation

The first segment $AB$ is $6/15=2/5$ of the full length $AC$. By the intercept theorem, its corresponding segment is the same fraction of the total on the second transversal. Thus $A\prime B\prime=(2/5)20=8$. The remainder there has length $12$, and the part-to-part ratio $8/12$ agrees with $6/(15-6)=6/9$. Part-to-whole and part-to-part are two different comparisons.
