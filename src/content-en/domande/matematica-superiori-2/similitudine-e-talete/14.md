Three parallel lines cross two transversals. The consecutive segments on the first transversal measure $8$ and $16$ cm. The segment corresponding to the first measures $24$ cm on the second transversal. Find the other segment.

- $49$ cm
- $47$ cm
- $48$ cm
- $96$ cm

## Explanation

The intercept theorem guarantees that corresponding segments on the two transversals are proportional. Write each ratio in the same order. Equating the cross products isolates the unknown segment.

$$
\frac{8}{16}=\frac{24}x
$$

$$
x=\frac{16\cdot24}{8}=48
$$
