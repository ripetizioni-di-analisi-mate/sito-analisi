At scale $1:100$, what does a segment of $2$ cm represent?

- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $20\,\mathrm m$
- $200\,\mathrm m$
- $0.2\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Multiply the two centimeters by one hundred. The actual length is two hundred centimeters. Dividing by one hundred converts that to two meters.
