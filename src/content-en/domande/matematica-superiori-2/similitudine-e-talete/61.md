Two similar figures have areas of $3$ cm² and $12$ cm². The corresponding side in the first measures $5$ cm. How many centimetres long is it in the second?

- $20$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$

## Explanation

The area ratio is the square of the ratio of side lengths. Take the square root and multiply by the original length. This gives $10$.
