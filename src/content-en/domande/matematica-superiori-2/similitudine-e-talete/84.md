Twenty real metres correspond to $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ on a plan. What is the scale?

- $1:50$
- $1:5$
- $1:8000$
- $1:500$

## Explanation

First both lengths are expressed in centimetres. Twenty metres is $2000\,\mathrm{cm}$. The ratio of plan length to real length is four two-thousandths. Reducing it to a unit numerator gives the scale.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac4{2000}&=\frac1{500}
\end{aligned}
$$
