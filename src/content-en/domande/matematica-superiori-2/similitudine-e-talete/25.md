A print file was reduced to $\dfrac23$ of its original dimensions. What factor restores this copy to the original dimensions?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $3$

## Explanation

The second transformation must undo the effect of the first on lengths. The product of the two scale factors must therefore be one.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac23 k&=1 \\[5pt]
k&=\frac32
\end{aligned}
$$
