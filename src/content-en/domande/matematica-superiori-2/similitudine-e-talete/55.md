Two similar triangles have corresponding sides of $6$ and $18$ cm. A median in the smaller triangle measures $7$ cm. How many centimeters long is its counterpart in the larger triangle?

- $21$
- $7$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $63$

## Explanation

A similarity multiplies all corresponding lengths by the same factor. The ratio of the sides gives a factor of three. This gives $21$.
