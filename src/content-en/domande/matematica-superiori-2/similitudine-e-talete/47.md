One figure is an enlargement of another. A side goes from $3$ cm to $21$ cm. What factor multiplies the original lengths?

- $7$
- $3$
- $21$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$

## Explanation

The requested scale factor is the new length divided by the original length. The factor $3$ cancels. This gives $7$.
