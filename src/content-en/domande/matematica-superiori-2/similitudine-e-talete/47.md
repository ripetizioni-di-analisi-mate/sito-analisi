A figure is enlarged by factor $3/2$ and then reduced by factor $2/5$. What is the overall factor?

- $\frac35$
- $\frac53$
- $\frac43$
- $\frac{19}{10}$

## Explanation

The second factor applies to the enlarged lengths, so the two factors multiply. This gives $(3/2)(2/5)=3/5$. An initial side of length $10$ would become $15$ after the first transformation and $6$ after the second. The final ratio $6/10$ confirms that the resulting figure is smaller than the original even though the first step enlarged it.
