For $ABC\sim DEF$, suppose $AB/DE=2/5$ and $EF=20$. What is the length of $BC$?

- $50$
- $12$
- $8$
- $40$

## Explanation

Side $BC$ corresponds to $EF$, so the ratios in the same direction give $BC/EF=AB/DE=2/5$. To obtain the length in the first triangle, multiply $20$ by $2/5$, giving $8$. The given fraction is a reduction from the second triangle to the first. Its reciprocal would describe the opposite direction and give a larger length in place of the one requested.
