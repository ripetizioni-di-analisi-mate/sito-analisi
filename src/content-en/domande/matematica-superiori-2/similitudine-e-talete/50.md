One figure is an enlargement of another. A side goes from $6$ cm to $78$ cm. What factor multiplies the original lengths?

- $\dfrac{1}{13}$
- $78$
- $13$
- $6$

## Explanation

The requested scale factor is the new length divided by the original length. The factor $6$ cancels. This gives $13$.
