A drawing is enlarged by $25\%$ and then reduced by $20\%$ relative to the enlarged copy. What is the overall scale factor?

- $\dfrac{21}{20}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$

## Explanation

The first step multiplies lengths by $\dfrac54$. The second multiplies the lengths in the copy by $\dfrac45$. The factors are reciprocals and their product is one.

$$
\begin{aligned}
k&=\frac54\cdot\frac45=1
\end{aligned}
$$
