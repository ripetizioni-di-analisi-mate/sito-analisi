Two similar triangles have corresponding sides of $3$ and $9$ cm. A median in the smaller triangle measures $4$ cm. How many centimeters long is its counterpart in the larger triangle?

- $12$
- $36$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $4$

## Explanation

A similarity multiplies all corresponding lengths by the same factor. The ratio of the sides gives a factor of three. This gives $12$.
