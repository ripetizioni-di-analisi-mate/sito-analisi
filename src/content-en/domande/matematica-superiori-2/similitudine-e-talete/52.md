Two similar triangles have corresponding medians of lengths $8$ and $10$. The triangle with median $10$ has perimeter $45$. What is the other perimeter?

- $36$
- $\frac{225}{4}$
- $40$
- $35$

## Explanation

Going from the triangle with median $10$ to the one with median $8$ uses factor $8/10=4/5$. The median is a corresponding length, just like each side contributing to the perimeter. Multiply $45$ by $4/5$ to obtain $36$. The smaller result matches the direction of the reduction, and the reverse ratio $45/36$ matches the ratio of the medians.
