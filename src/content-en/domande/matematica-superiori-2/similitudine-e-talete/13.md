The ratio of the area of the second figure to that of the first is $64$. What is the ratio of their corresponding lengths?

- $9$
- $8$
- $7$
- $16$

## Explanation

For similar figures, the area ratio is the square of the length ratio. To find the length ratio, take the square root. Use the positive value because the ratio compares lengths.

$$
k^2=64
$$

$$
k=\sqrt{64}=8
$$
