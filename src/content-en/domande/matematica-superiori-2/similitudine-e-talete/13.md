The second of two similar figures has $\dfrac{9}{16}$ of the area of the first. By what percentage does a corresponding side decrease?

- $43.75\%$
- $25\%$
- $75\%$
- $56.25\%$

## Explanation

The length factor is the positive square root of the area ratio, so $\sqrt{\frac{9}{16}}=\dfrac{3}{4}$. The final side retains three quarters of the original length and loses the remaining quarter, or $25\%$. A square checks this: its side goes from $4$ to $3$ and its area from $16$ to $9$. Subtracting the area ratio from one would give the area loss instead.
