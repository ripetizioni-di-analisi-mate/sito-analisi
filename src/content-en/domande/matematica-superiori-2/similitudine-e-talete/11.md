A polygon has perimeter $12$ cm. A similar figure has scale factor $8$ relative to the original. Find the new perimeter.

- $97$ cm
- $95$ cm
- $192$ cm
- $96$ cm

## Explanation

Each side is multiplied by the scale factor. Consequently, their sum, the perimeter, is multiplied by the same factor. Perimeter scales linearly.

$$
P_2=kP_1=8\cdot12=96
$$
