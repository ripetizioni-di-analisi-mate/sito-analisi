A triangle has side lengths in the ratio $2:3:4$ and perimeter $54\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the middle side length?

- $12$
- $24$
- $27$
- $18$

## Explanation

The sides are $2t,3t,4t$ in a common unit $t$. Their sum gives $9t=54$, so $t=6$. The middle side occupies three of the nine parts and is $18\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. The other lengths are $12$ and $24$. The ratio and perimeter both check, and the shorter two lengths also add to more than the longest.
