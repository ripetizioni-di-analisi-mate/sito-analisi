On a plan at scale $1:200$, a rectangular room occupies $6\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. What is its actual area in square meters?

- $24\,\mathrm m^2$
- $\dfrac{3}{25}\,\mathrm m^2$
- $12\,\mathrm m^2$
- $240\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

Each centimeter on the plan corresponds to two actual meters. A square one centimeter wide and high therefore corresponds to four square meters. Multiply the area on the plan by four.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A&=6\cdot(2\,\mathrm m)^2=24\,\mathrm m^2
\end{aligned}
$$
