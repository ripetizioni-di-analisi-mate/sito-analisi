Two triangles have sides of lengths $3$, $4$, $5$ and $6$, $8$, $11$, respectively. Are they similar?

- No, the three ratios of corresponding sides are different.
- Yes, the two shorter sides are in a ratio of two.
- Yes, both have three sides of different lengths.
- No, because they have different perimeters.

## Explanation

If the triangles were similar, the longest side in one would match the longest side in the other. The first two ratios are $2$, but the ratio of the longest sides is $\dfrac{11}{5}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac63&=\frac84=2 \\[5pt]
\frac{11}5&\ne2
\end{aligned}
$$
