A region occupies $8\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ on a plan at scale $1:300$. What is its actual area in square metres?

- $24$
- $240$
- $720000$
- $72$

## Explanation

One centimetre on the plan equals $300$ real centimetres, or $3$ metres. A square on the plan with one-centimetre sides represents a real square with three-metre sides and area nine square metres. Multiplying $8$ by $9$ gives $72\,\mathrm{m}^2$. In square centimetres the calculation gives $8\cdot300^2=720000$, which becomes square metres after dividing by $100^2$.
