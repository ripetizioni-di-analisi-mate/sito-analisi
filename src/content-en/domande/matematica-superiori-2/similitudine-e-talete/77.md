A plan at scale $1:3000$ shows a segment $7$ cm long. How many meters long is the real segment?

- $21000$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $21$
- $210$

## Explanation

Multiply by the denominator of the scale to get the real length in centimeters. Divide by one hundred to convert to meters. This gives $210$.
