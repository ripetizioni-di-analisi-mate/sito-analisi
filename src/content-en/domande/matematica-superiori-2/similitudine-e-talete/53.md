Two similar triangles have corresponding sides of $4$ and $12$ cm. A median in the smaller triangle measures $5$ cm. How many centimeters long is its counterpart in the larger triangle?

- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $5$
- $15$
- $45$

## Explanation

A similarity multiplies all corresponding lengths by the same factor. The ratio of the sides gives a factor of three. This gives $15$.
