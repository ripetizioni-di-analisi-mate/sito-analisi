Given $ABC\sim DEF$, let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$ and $N$ the midpoint of $EF$. Which segment is the median corresponding to $AM$?

- $EN$
- $FN$
- $DN$
- $DE$

## Explanation

Median $AM$ goes from vertex $A$ to the midpoint of the opposite side. Vertex $A$ matches $D$, and side $BC$ matches $EF$. Point $M$ maps to midpoint $N$ because both halves scale by the same factor. The corresponding median is therefore $DN$. Segments $EN$ and $FN$ are halves of a side and do not start at the opposite vertex.
