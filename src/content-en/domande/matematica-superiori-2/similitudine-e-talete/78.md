The same avenue measures $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ on a plan at scale $1:100$. How many centimetres long is it on a plan at scale $1:400$?

- $16$
- $4$
- $\frac14$
- $1$

## Explanation

The real avenue measures $4\cdot100=400$ centimetres. On the second plan divide this by $400$, leaving one centimetre. You can also compare the two reductions directly: moving from scale $1:100$ to $1:400$ multiplies lengths by $\dfrac{\frac{1}{400}}{\frac{1}{100}}=\dfrac{1}{4}$. A larger denominator represents the same real distance with a shorter drawn length.
