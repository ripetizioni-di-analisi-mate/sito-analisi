A plan at scale $1:4000$ shows a segment $9$ cm long. How many meters long is the real segment?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $36$
- $36000$
- $360$

## Explanation

Multiply by the denominator of the scale to get the real length in centimeters. Divide by one hundred to convert to meters. This gives $360$.
