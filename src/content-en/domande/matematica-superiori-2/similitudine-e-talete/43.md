In the correspondence $ABC\sim DEF$, which side corresponds to $AC$?

- $DE$
- $EF$
- $AB$
- $DF$

## Explanation

The order of the vertices gives $A\leftrightarrow D$ and $C\leftrightarrow F$. The side joining those vertices therefore corresponds to $DF$. Any comparison of lengths must respect that correspondence.
