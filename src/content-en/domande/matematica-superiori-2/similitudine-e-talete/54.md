A median divides a side of length $12$ into halves. In the similar triangle obtained with scale factor $\dfrac{3}{4}$, what is the length of one half of the corresponding side?

- $9$
- $\frac92$
- $6$
- $8$

## Explanation

Similarity multiplies both halves by the same factor, so it sends a midpoint to the corresponding midpoint. The initial half has length $6$, which becomes $6\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}=\dfrac{9}{2}$. Alternatively, scale the whole side and then halve it: $12\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}=9$ and the requested half is $\dfrac{9}{2}$. The two procedures agree because both operations act on the length.
