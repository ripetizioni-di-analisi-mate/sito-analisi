Two similar triangles have corresponding sides of $5$ and $15$ cm. A median in the smaller triangle measures $6$ cm. How many centimeters long is its counterpart in the larger triangle?

- $6$
- $18$
- $2$
- $54$

## Explanation

A similarity multiplies all corresponding lengths by the same factor. The ratio of the sides gives a factor of three. This gives $18$.
