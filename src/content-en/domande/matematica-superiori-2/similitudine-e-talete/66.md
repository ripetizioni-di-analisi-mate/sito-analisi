In triangle $ABC$, points $D$ on $AB$ and $E$ on $AC$ satisfy $AD:DB=2:3$ and $AE:EC=4:5$. With $D$ fixed, which way must $E$ move to make $DE\parallel BC$?

- Towards $A$
- Towards $C$
- It is already parallel
- Moving $D$ is essential

## Explanation

The fixed fraction on $AB$ is $\dfrac25$, while on $AC$ it is $\dfrac49>\dfrac25$. Shortening $AE$ makes the fractions agree.
