Three parallel lines cross two transversals. The consecutive segments on the first transversal measure $2$ and $5$ cm. On the second transversal, the segment corresponding to $2$ measures $6$ cm. How many centimetres long is the other segment?

- $15$
- $45$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $5$

## Explanation

By the intercept theorem, corresponding segments have the same ratio. The first segment triples, so the second triples as well. This gives $15$.
