Three parallel lines cross two transversals. The consecutive segments on the first transversal measure $7$ and $14$ cm. The segment corresponding to the first measures $21$ cm on the second transversal. How long is the segment corresponding to the second?

- $43$ cm
- $41$ cm
- $42$ cm
- $84$ cm

## Explanation

The intercept theorem guarantees that corresponding segments on the two transversals are proportional. Write each ratio in the same order. Equating the cross products isolates the unknown segment.

$$
\frac{7}{14}=\frac{21}x
$$

$$
x=\frac{14\cdot21}{7}=42
$$
