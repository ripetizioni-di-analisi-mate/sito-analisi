A real segment $360$ m long is drawn at scale $1:4000$. How many centimeters long is it on the map?

- $900$
- $90$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}$
- $9$

## Explanation

Convert the real length from meters to centimeters and divide by the denominator of the scale. The factor $4000$ cancels. This gives $9$.
