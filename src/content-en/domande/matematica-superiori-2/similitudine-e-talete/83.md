A drawing at scale $1:4000$ shows a $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ segment. Which calculation correctly verifies that its real length is $120\,\mathrm m$?

- $\frac{120\,\mathrm m}{3\,\mathrm m}=4000$
- $\frac{120\,\mathrm{cm}}{3\,\mathrm{cm}}=4000$
- $\frac{3\,\mathrm{cm}}{12000\,\mathrm{cm}}=4000$
- $\frac{12000\,\mathrm{cm}}{3\,\mathrm{cm}}=4000$

## Explanation

To check the factor from drawing to reality, use the actual length in the numerator and the drawn length in the denominator in the same unit. Since $120\,\mathrm m=12000\,\mathrm{cm}$, division by $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ gives $4000$. Inverting the ratio would give $1/4000$. Using $3$ metres in the denominator would change the measured length on the drawing, which is in centimetres.
