A similarity changes a figure’s perimeter from $13$ cm to $78$ cm. What is the ratio of the new area to the original area?

- $6$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $36$
- $12$

## Explanation

The ratio of perimeters equals the ratio of lengths. For the areas, square that ratio. This gives $36$.
