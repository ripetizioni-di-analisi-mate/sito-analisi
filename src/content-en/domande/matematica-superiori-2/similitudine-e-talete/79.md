The same path appears on maps at scales $1:1000$ and $1:2500$. What is the ratio of the first drawn length to the second?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{25}{4}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The same real length is divided by $1000$ on the first map and by $2500$ on the second. The real length cancels in the quotient of the drawn lengths.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{l_1}{l_2}&=\frac{2500}{1000}=\frac52
\end{aligned}
$$
