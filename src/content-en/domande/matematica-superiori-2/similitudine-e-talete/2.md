Two similar triangles have corresponding sides of lengths $7$ and $21$ cm. Another side of the first triangle measures $10$ cm. Find the length of the corresponding side in the second.

- $31$ cm
- $29$ cm
- $30$ cm
- $60$ cm

## Explanation

Corresponding sides all have the same ratio. The first pair gives a factor of three. Multiply the other side by that factor as well.

$$
k=\frac{21}{7}=3
$$

$$
l_2=3\cdot10=30
$$
