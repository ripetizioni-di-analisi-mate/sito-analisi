Two similar figures have area ratio $25$. The shorter altitude measures $2$ cm. How many centimetres long is the longer altitude?

- $7$
- $50$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

Corresponding altitudes are lengths. Their ratio is the square root of the area ratio. This gives $10$.
