A plan at scale $1:6000$ shows a segment $13$ cm long. How many meters long is the real segment?

- $78$
- $780$
- $\dfrac{13}{6}$
- $78000$

## Explanation

Multiply by the denominator of the scale to get the real length in centimeters. Divide by one hundred to convert to meters. This gives $780$.
