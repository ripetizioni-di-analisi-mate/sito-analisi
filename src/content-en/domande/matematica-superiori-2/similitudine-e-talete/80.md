A real distance of $180\,\mathrm m$ is represented by $7.2\,\mathrm{cm}$ on a plan. What scale was used?

- $1:25$
- $1:2500$
- $1:250$
- $1:25000$

## Explanation

To find the denominator, both lengths are expressed in centimetres. The actual distance is $18000\,\mathrm{cm}$, so the real-to-plan factor is $\dfrac{18000}{7.2}=2500$. The scale is written $1:2500$ because it gives the plan-to-reality ratio. This can be checked by multiplying $7.2$ by $2500$, then converting the resulting centimetres to metres. Without a common unit the calculation would give a different ratio.
