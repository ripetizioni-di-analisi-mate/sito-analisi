A plan at scale $1:2000$ shows a segment $5$ cm long. How many metres long is the real segment?

- $10000$
- $10$
- $100$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

Multiply by the denominator of the scale to get the real length in centimetres. Divide by one hundred to convert to metres. This gives $100$.
