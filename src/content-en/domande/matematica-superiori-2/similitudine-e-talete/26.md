Two similar triangles have corresponding sides of lengths $10$ and $30$ cm. Another side of the first triangle measures $13$ cm. Find the length of the corresponding side in the second.

- $40$ cm
- $38$ cm
- $39$ cm
- $78$ cm

## Explanation

Corresponding sides all have the same ratio. The first pair gives a factor of three. Multiply the other side by that factor as well.

$$
k=\frac{30}{10}=3
$$

$$
l_2=3\cdot13=39
$$
