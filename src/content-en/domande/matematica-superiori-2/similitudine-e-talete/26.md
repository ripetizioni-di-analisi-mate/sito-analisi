One triangle has sides $4,5,7$. Another has corresponding first two sides of lengths $6$ and $7.5$. Which third length guarantees SSS similarity?

- $9$
- $14$
- $\frac{21}{2}$
- $18$

## Explanation

Both known pairs give factor $\dfrac{3}{2}$. The three-side criterion requires the third ratio to equal the same value, so the missing length is $7\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}=\dfrac{21}{2}$. No angle measurement is needed once all three proportions have been checked. The resulting length can form a triangle, since the shorter two sides of the second triangle sum to more than its longest side.
