Suppose $\dfrac{AB}{DE}=\dfrac{BC}{EF}$. Which angle equality lets you conclude $ABC\sim DEF$ by the SAS similarity criterion?

- $\angle ABC=\angle DEF$
- $\angle BAC=\angle EDF$
- $\angle BCA=\angle EFD$
- $\angle BAC=\angle DEF$

## Explanation

Find the vertex shared by the two sides whose ratios are given. In the first triangle $AB$ and $BC$ meet at $B$, and in the second $DE$ and $EF$ meet at $E$. The included angles are therefore $ABC$ and $DEF$. Equal angles at other vertices can leave more than one possible shape. For SAS, the key position is where the given sides meet.
