The statement $ABC\sim QRP$ matches vertices in the stated order. Given $AB=4$, $QR=10$, and $BC=6$, what is the length of $RP$?

- $12$
- $10$
- $15$
- $\frac{12}{5}$

## Explanation

In the written order, $A$ matches $Q$, $B$ matches $R$, and $C$ matches $P$. Thus $AB$ corresponds to $QR$, while $BC$ corresponds to $RP$. The first pair gives factor $10/4=5/2$, so the second gives $6\cdot5/2=15$. The alphabetical order of the second triangle’s labels does not determine the matching. The reverse calculation checks: $15\cdot2/5=6$.
