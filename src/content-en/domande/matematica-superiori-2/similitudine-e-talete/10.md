Two similar triangles have corresponding sides of lengths $8$ and $24$ cm. Another side of the first triangle measures $11$ cm. Find the length of the corresponding side in the second.

- $34$ cm
- $32$ cm
- $33$ cm
- $66$ cm

## Explanation

Corresponding sides all have the same ratio. The first pair gives a factor of three. Multiply the other side by that factor as well.

$$
k=\frac{24}{8}=3
$$

$$
l_2=3\cdot11=33
$$
