In triangle $ABC$, $D$ lies on side $AB$, $E$ on side $AC$, and $DE\parallel BC$. Given $AD=4$, $DB=8$, and $AC=18$, find $EC$.

- $6$
- $9$
- $8$
- $12$

## Explanation

The whole side $AB$ has length $4+8=12$. Similarity of the nested triangles gives $\dfrac{AE}{AC}=\dfrac{AD}{AB}=\dfrac{1}{3}$. So $AE=6$ and the part remaining to $C$ is $18-6=12$. Segment $EC$ is the part outside the smaller triangle. Its ratio to the whole side also matches the corresponding remainder: $\dfrac{EC}{AC}=\dfrac{DB}{AB}=\dfrac{2}{3}$.
