Which transformation preserves similarity for every rectangle?

- Multiply both sides by $\dfrac45$.
- Add $1$ to both sides.
- Halve only the longer side.
- Double one side and triple the other.

## Explanation

The corresponding angles in rectangles are all right angles. Similarity also requires the corresponding sides to have the same ratio. Applying the same factor to both sides guarantees this property.
