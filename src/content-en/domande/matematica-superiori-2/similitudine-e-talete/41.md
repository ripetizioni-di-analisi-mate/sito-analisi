Two triangles with two pairs of congruent corresponding angles are

- $\text{Always congruent}$
- $\text{Similar}$
- $\text{Always equilateral}$
- $\text{Always right triangles}$

## Explanation

The two-angle criterion guarantees similarity. The sizes may differ because the criterion determines the shape. Congruence would also require equal sizes.
