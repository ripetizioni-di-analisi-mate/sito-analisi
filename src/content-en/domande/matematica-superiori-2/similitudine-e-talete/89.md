Which setup justifies using similar triangles for the shadows of two objects?

- Vertical objects on the same level ground, with shadows measured at different times and no further information.
- One object is tilted and the shadows are measured at the same time.
- Vertical objects on the same level ground, with shadows measured at the same time.
- The two objects have the same height and their shadows fall on ground with different slopes.

## Explanation

Verticals on the same level ground give two right angles. At the same time, parallel rays also make two equal acute angles with the ground. The angle-angle criterion therefore applies. Changing the time can change the direction of the rays, and tilting the object can change its base angle. Those setups would require another check.
