At the same instant, a pole $5$ m tall casts a $6$ m shadow. A tree casts a shadow $66$ m long. How many meters tall is the tree?

- $\dfrac{11}{5}$
- $66$
- $55$
- $11$

## Explanation

The parallel rays form similar triangles with the verticals. The tree’s shadow is $11$ times as long as the pole’s shadow, so the same is true of their heights. This gives $55$.
