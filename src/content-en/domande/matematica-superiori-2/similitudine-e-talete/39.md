Two similar figures have areas $64$ and $100$. The smaller has perimeter $28$. What is the perimeter of the larger?

- $\frac{175}{4}$
- $56$
- $\frac{112}{5}$
- $35$

## Explanation

The area ratio is $\dfrac{100}{64}=\dfrac{25}{16}$. Its positive square root gives the length factor $\dfrac{5}{4}$. Since the perimeter sums corresponding lengths, it has the same factor, so $28\cdot\dfrac{5}{4}=35$. Check that the perimeter grows by one quarter and that squaring the ratio $\dfrac{35}{28}$ gives exactly $\dfrac{100}{64}$.
