Two similar figures have area ratio $169$. The shorter altitude measures $6$ cm. How many centimeters long is the longer altitude?

- $1014$
- $\dfrac{6}{13}$
- $19$
- $78$

## Explanation

Corresponding altitudes are lengths. Their ratio is the square root of the area ratio. This gives $78$.
