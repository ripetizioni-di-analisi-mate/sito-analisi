Two similar figures have areas $64$ and $100$. The smaller has perimeter $28$. What is the perimeter of the larger?

- $\frac{175}{4}$
- $56$
- $\frac{112}{5}$
- $35$

## Explanation

The area ratio is $100/64=25/16$. Its positive square root gives the length factor $5/4$. Since the perimeter sums corresponding lengths, it has the same factor, so $28\cdot5/4=35$. Check that the perimeter grows by one quarter and that squaring the ratio $35/28$ gives exactly $100/64$.
