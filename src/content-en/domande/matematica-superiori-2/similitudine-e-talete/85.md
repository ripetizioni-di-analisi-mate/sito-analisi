The same field is drawn at scales $1:1000$ and $1:2000$. What is the ratio of its drawn area on the second map to its drawn area on the first?

- $\frac14$
- $\frac12$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

On the second map each drawn length is half its length on the first, because the scale denominator doubles. The drawing-to-drawing length factor is therefore $1/2$, and the area factor is $(1/2)^2=1/4$. The real field’s area remains unchanged: the comparison is between areas on paper. The reciprocal $4$ would be their ratio in the opposite order.
