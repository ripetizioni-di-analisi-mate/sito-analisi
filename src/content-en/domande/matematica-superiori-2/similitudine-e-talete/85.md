A real segment $780$ m long is drawn at scale $1:6000$. How many centimeters long is it on the map?

- $13$
- $130$
- $\dfrac{13}{10}$
- $1300$

## Explanation

Convert the real length from meters to centimeters and divide by the denominator of the scale. The factor $6000$ cancels. This gives $13$.
