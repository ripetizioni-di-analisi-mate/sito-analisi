In triangle $ABC$, $D$ lies inside $AB$, $E$ inside $AC$, and $DE\parallel BC$. Which equality identifies the corresponding angles along transversal $AB$?

- $\angle ADE=\angle ABC$
- $\angle AED=\angle ABC$
- $\angle ADE=\angle BAC$
- $\angle DAE=\angle ACB$

## Explanation

Line $AB$ cuts $DE$ at $D$ and $BC$ at $B$. The smaller triangle’s angle at $D$ uses ray $DA$ and the parallel $DE$. The angle at $B$ uses $BA$ and $BC$ in corresponding positions, so their measures agree. The other interior vertex $E$ matches vertex $C$ along transversal $AC$, a different pair.
