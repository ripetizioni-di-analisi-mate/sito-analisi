At the same instant, a pole $6$ m tall casts a $7$ m shadow. A tree casts a shadow $91$ m long. How many meters tall is the tree?

- $78$
- $91$
- $13$
- $\dfrac{13}{6}$

## Explanation

The parallel rays form similar triangles with the verticals. The tree’s shadow is $13$ times as long as the pole’s shadow, so the same is true of their heights. This gives $78$.
