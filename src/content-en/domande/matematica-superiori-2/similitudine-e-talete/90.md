A vertical pole and a vertical tree stand on the same level ground. At one time their shadows are $2$ and $8$ metres long. Later the pole’s shadow is $3$ metres long. What is the tree’s shadow length then in metres?

- $12$
- $9$
- $24$
- $\frac{16}{3}$

## Explanation

At the first time the shadows are corresponding sides of the similar triangles. The ratio $\dfrac{8}{2}=4$ matches the height ratio. At the later time the rays have a different inclination, but similarity still holds between the two objects at that new time on level ground. The heights have stayed the same, so the ratio of the new shadows is still $4$. Hence $3\cdot4=12$.
