The larger of two similar figures has area $9/4$ of the smaller’s. One of its sides measures $15$. How long is the corresponding shorter side?

- $\frac{45}{2}$
- $\frac{20}{3}$
- $5$
- $10$

## Explanation

The positive root of $9/4$ gives factor $3/2$ from the smaller to the larger figure. The requested direction is the reverse, so multiply $15$ by $2/3$ to obtain $10$. The two operations have different roles: the root changes the type of ratio, while the reciprocal changes its direction. Check by squaring $15/10$ to get $9/4$.
