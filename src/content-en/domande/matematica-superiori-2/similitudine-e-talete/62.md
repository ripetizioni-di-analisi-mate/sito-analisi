Two similar figures have areas of $4$ cm² and $36$ cm². The corresponding side in the first measures $7$ cm. How many centimeters long is it in the second?

- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $63$
- $7$
- $21$

## Explanation

The area ratio is the square of the ratio of side lengths. Take the square root and multiply by the original length. This gives $21$.
