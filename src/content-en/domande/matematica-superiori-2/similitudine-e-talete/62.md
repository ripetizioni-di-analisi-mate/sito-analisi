The larger of two similar figures has area $\dfrac{9}{4}$ of the smaller’s. One of its sides measures $15$. How long is the corresponding shorter side?

- $\frac{45}{2}$
- $\frac{20}{3}$
- $5$
- $10$

## Explanation

The positive root of $\dfrac{9}{4}$ gives factor $\dfrac{3}{2}$ from the smaller to the larger figure. The requested direction is the reverse, so multiply $15$ by $\dfrac{2}{3}$ to obtain $10$. The two operations have different roles: the root changes the type of ratio, while the reciprocal changes its direction. Check by squaring $\dfrac{15}{10}$ to get $\dfrac{9}{4}$.
