At the same instant, a pole $2$ m tall casts a $3$ m shadow. A tree casts a shadow $15$ m long. How many metres tall is the tree?

- $5$
- $15$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

The parallel rays form similar triangles with the verticals. The tree’s shadow is $5$ times as long as the pole’s shadow, so the same is true of their heights. This gives $10$.
