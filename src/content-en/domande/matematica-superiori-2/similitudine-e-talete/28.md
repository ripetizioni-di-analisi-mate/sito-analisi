A figure has area $5$ cm². A similar figure has a length scale factor of $10$ relative to the first. Find the new area.

- $500$ cm²
- $501$ cm²
- $499$ cm²
- $1000$ cm²

## Explanation

The base and altitude are both multiplied by the length scale factor. Their product is therefore multiplied by the square of that factor. The area follows the same rule.

$$
A_2=k^2A_1=10^2\cdot5=500
$$
