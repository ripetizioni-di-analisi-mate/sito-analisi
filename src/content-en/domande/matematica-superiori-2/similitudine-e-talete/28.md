A line parallel to the base cuts off a triangle whose length scale factor from the original is $2/5$. What fraction of the original area remains in the trapezoid?

- $\frac{21}{25}$
- $\frac{4}{25}$
- $\frac35$
- $\frac9{25}$

## Explanation

The length factor relates the two similar triangles. The smaller triangle occupies $(2/5)^2=4/25$ of the original area. The trapezoid is the remainder, so its fraction is $1-4/25=21/25$. For a check, start with total area $25$: removing the triangle of area $4$ leaves $21$. Take the complement after finding the area fraction.
