Two polygons have perimeter ratio $3$ and area ratio $4$, both from the first to the second. What follows?

- They cannot be similar.
- They are similar with scale factor $3$.
- They are similar with scale factor $2$.
- They are similar with scale factor $\dfrac{4}{3}$.

## Explanation

If the figures were similar, the perimeter ratio would require length factor $3$. The area ratio would then be $3^2=9$, while the given ratio is $4$. Alternatively, the area ratio would require length factor $2$, incompatible with the perimeter ratio. This inconsistency rules out similarity. Agreement of these ratios would be necessary, but the shape would still need checking.
