Two similar figures have areas of $7$ cm² and $252$ cm². The corresponding side in the first measures $13$ cm. How many centimeters long is it in the second?

- $78$
- $13$
- $\dfrac{13}{6}$
- $468$

## Explanation

The area ratio is the square of the ratio of side lengths. Take the square root and multiply by the original length. This gives $78$.
