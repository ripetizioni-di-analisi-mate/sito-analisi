Two similar hexagons have perimeters $36\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $45\,\mathrm{cm}$. A diagonal in the first measures $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. How long is it in the second?

- $\dfrac{32}{5}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $17\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{25}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The perimeter ratio equals the ratio of side lengths. Diagonals between corresponding vertices are lengths as well, so they are multiplied by the same ratio.

$$
\begin{aligned}
k&=\frac{45}{36}=\frac54 \\[5pt]
d_2&=8\cdot\frac54=10\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
