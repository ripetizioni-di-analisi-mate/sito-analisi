The perimeter ratio of two similar figures, from the first to the second, is $\dfrac23$. By what fraction of the original area does the area decrease?

- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The perimeter ratio is the length scale factor. The second area is therefore four ninths of the first. The decrease is the difference from the whole original area.

$$
\begin{aligned}
1-\left(\frac23\right)^2&=1-\frac49=\frac59
\end{aligned}
$$
