In triangle $ABC$ of area $64\,\mathrm{cm}^2$, $D$ and $E$ are the midpoints of $AB$ and $AC$. What is the area of quadrilateral $DBCE$?

- $48\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $32\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $56\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

Triangles $ADE$ and $ABC$ share the angle at $A$, and the adjacent sides have ratio $\dfrac12$. Thus they are similar. The smaller occupies one quarter of the larger area, leaving three quarters for the quadrilateral.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A_{DBCE}&=64-64\left(\frac12\right)^2=48\,\mathrm{cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$$
