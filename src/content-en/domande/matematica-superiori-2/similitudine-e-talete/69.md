Three parallel lines mark two consecutive segments on each transversal. On the first, the lengths are $6$ and $9$ centimetres. On the second, the full segment is $25\,\mathrm{cm}$. How long is the segment corresponding to the first $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ piece?

- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{50}{3}\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The two full segments lie in the same strip between the outer parallel lines. Compare part and whole in the same order. The requested segment is six fifteenths of the second full length.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{x}{25}&=\frac{6}{6+9} \\[5pt]
x&=10\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
