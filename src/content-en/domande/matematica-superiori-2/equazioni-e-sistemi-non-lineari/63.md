A spreadsheet replaces $\sqrt{a^2x}$ by $a\sqrt x$ for every real parameter $a$ and every $x\ge0$. What correction makes the replacement valid in all cases?

- Use $\sqrt{a}\sqrt x$
- Use $-a\sqrt x$
- Use $|a|\sqrt x$
- Use $a^2\sqrt x$

## Explanation

Both $a^2$ and $x$ are nonnegative, so their square roots can be taken separately. We have $\sqrt{a^2}=|a|$. For example, when $a=-2$ and $x=1$ the original square root is $2$.
