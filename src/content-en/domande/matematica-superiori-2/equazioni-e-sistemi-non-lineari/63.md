Which expression is equal to $\sqrt{a^2x}$ for every real $a$ and every $x\ge0$?

- $a\sqrt x$
- $-a\sqrt x$
- $|a|\sqrt x$
- $a^2\sqrt x$

## Explanation

The factors $a^2$ and $x$ are non-negative, so $\sqrt{a^2x}=\sqrt{a^2}\,\sqrt x$. For every real $a$ we have $\sqrt{a^2}=|a|$, therefore $\sqrt{a^2x}=|a|\sqrt x$. With $a=-2$ and $x=1$, for example, $\sqrt{a^2x}=2$ and $a\sqrt x=-2$, so the equality $\sqrt{a^2x}=a\sqrt x$ holds for every $x\geq0$ only when $a\geq0$.
