Which set of values satisfies $\sqrt{4x}=2\sqrt x$ over the reals?

- $\mathbb R$
- $(0,+\infty)$
- $[0,+\infty)$
- $\{0\}$

## Explanation

Both radicals require $x\ge0$. Throughout the domain the two sides are nonnegative and have square $4x$. So they agree at every allowed point. Zero is included: at $x=0$ both sides equal zero. Squaring produces an identity, but it cannot extend the domain to negative values, where the original radicals are undefined.
