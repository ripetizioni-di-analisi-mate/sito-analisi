How many common points do $y=x^2$ and $y=|x|$ have?

- $3$
- $2$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

Let $u=|x|$. Then $x^2=u^2$ and the equality becomes $u^2=u$. Factoring as $u(u-1)=0$ gives $u=0$ and $u=1$. The first gives only $(0,0)$, while the second gives $(-1,1)$ and $(1,1)$. There are three intersections. Counting only the values of $u$ would miss that a positive absolute value corresponds to two opposite horizontal coordinates.
