How many points do $y=x^2$ and $y=36$ have in common?

- $2$
- $1$
- $0$
- $4$

## Explanation

The x-coordinates must satisfy $x^2=36$. Since the right side is positive, there are two distinct x-coordinates. This gives $2$.
