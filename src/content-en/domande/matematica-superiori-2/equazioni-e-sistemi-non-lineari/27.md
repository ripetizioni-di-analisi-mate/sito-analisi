What expression results from expanding $(\sqrt x+2)^2$ for $x\ge0$?

- $x+4$
- $x+2\sqrt x+4$
- $x+4\sqrt x+4$
- $x+4\sqrt x+2$

## Explanation

Add twice the product $2\cdot\sqrt x\cdot2=4\sqrt x$ to the squares of $\sqrt x$ and $2$. This gives $x+4\sqrt x+4$.
