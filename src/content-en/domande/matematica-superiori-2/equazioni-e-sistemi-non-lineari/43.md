The points $0$ and $4$ are marked on the number line. For which positions $x$ is the sum of the distances to those points exactly $4$?

- $\{0,4\}$
- $\mathbb R$
- $[0,4]$
- $\{2\}$

## Explanation

Between the points the distances are $x$ and $4-x$, whose sum is $4$. Moving outside the segment adds a stretch traversed both ways, so the sum becomes greater than $4$.
