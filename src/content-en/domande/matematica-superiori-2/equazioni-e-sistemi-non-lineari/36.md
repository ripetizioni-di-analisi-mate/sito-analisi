We want to choose $c$ so that $\sqrt{x+c}=\sqrt{5-x}$ has at least one real solution. Which parameter values allow this?

- $c\le-5$
- $c>-5$
- Every real $c$
- $c\ge-5$

## Explanation

Equal radicands require $x=(5-c)/2$. Substitution gives the common radicand $(5+c)/2$, which must be nonnegative. At the endpoint $c=-5$ both square roots are zero.
