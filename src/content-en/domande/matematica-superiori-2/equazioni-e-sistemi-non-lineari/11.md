Lea turns $\sqrt{x+3}=4$ into $x+3=16$. Why is reversing this step valid here?

- A square root can mean either $4$ or $-4$ interchangeably
- Every squared equation is equivalent to its original equation
- The square root of $16$ is exactly the nonnegative value $4$
- All real $x$ are in the domain

## Explanation

If $x+3=16$, the radicand is automatically admissible and its principal square root is $4$. The sign of the right side therefore lets us reverse the step.
