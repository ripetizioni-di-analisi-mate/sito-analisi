Before squaring $\sqrt{3-2x}=2-x$, which set makes the radical defined and the right side nonnegative?

- $x\le2$
- $\dfrac32\le x\le2$
- $x\le\dfrac32$
- $x\ge\dfrac32$

## Explanation

The nonnegative radicand requires $x\le\dfrac32$. The nonnegative right side requires $x\le2$, already true at every value in the first set. So squaring is valid for $x\le\dfrac32$. The restrictions retain the endpoint where the radicand is zero and the right side is still positive. The quadratic form reduces to $(x-1)^2=0$, and candidate $1$ is allowed.
