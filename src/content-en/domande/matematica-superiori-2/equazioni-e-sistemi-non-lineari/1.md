Which real numbers solve $x^{4} - 113 x^{2} + 3136=0$?

- $\{-8;\,-7;\,7;\,8\}$
- $\{7;\,8\}$
- $\{-7;\,7\}$
- $\{-8;\,8\}$

## Explanation

The equation contains only even powers and is biquadratic. Setting $t=x^2$ gives a quadratic equation with two positive roots. Each gives two opposite values of $x$.

$$
t=x^2\ge0
$$

$$
(t-49)(t-64)=0
$$

$$
x^2=49\quad\text{or}\quad x^2=64
$$
