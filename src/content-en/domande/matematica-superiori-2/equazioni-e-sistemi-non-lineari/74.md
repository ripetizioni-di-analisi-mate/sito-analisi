Which data pair $(s;p)$ makes $x+y=s$, $xy=p$ a system with exactly one real solution pair?

- $(8;15)$
- $(8;16)$
- $(8;17)$
- $(8;0)$

## Explanation

The equation $t^2-st+p=0$ needs a double root, since with distinct roots the swapped order is another solution. Here the discriminant is $64-4p$, zero at $p=16$. The only pair is $(4;4)$. Smaller products give two ordered pairs. Product $17$ makes the discriminant negative and gives no real pair.
