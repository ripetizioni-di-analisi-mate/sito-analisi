The model $y=\dfrac{x(x-2)}{x-2}$ is defined for $x\ne2$. We want to fill the gap with a point on the same straight line as the rest of the graph. Which height should we choose?

- $2$
- $1$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

At every allowed input the quotient simplifies to $y=x$. The line contains $(2;2)$, so assigning height $2$ fills the graph's gap, even though the original fraction remains undefined at $2$.
