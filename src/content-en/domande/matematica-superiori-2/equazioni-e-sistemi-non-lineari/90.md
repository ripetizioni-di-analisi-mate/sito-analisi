Solve $\dfrac{x^3-4x}{x^2-4}=0$.

- $\{0\}$
- $\{-2;0;2\}$
- $\{-2;2\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The domain excludes both $-2$ and $2$. For every other value, cancel the factors $x-2$ and $x+2$. The fraction then equals $x$, so the only solution is $0$.
