In the system $\begin{cases}x+y=16\\xy=55\end{cases}$, what is the value of $x^2+y^2$?

- $256$
- $36$
- $55$
- $146$

## Explanation

Expanding the square of the sum gives $x^2+2xy+y^2$. Subtract twice the product from the square of the sum to obtain $146$.
