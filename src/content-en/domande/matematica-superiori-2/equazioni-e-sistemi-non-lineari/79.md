The quantities $a,b$ have sum $6$ and product $7$. Both are increased by $1$. What is the product after the change?

- $16$
- $8$
- $13$
- $14$

## Explanation

Expanding gives $(a+1)(b+1)=ab+(a+b)+1$. The three contributions are $7$, $6$ and $1$. There is no need to find $a$ and $b$ separately.
