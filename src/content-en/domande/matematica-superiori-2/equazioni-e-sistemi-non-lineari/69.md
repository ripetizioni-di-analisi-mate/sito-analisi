We know that $x=3$ solves $|2x-1|=k$. What is the other solution?

- $-3$
- $2$
- $-2$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Substituting $3$ gives $k=|6-1|=5$. The other value must make the argument $-5$, so $2x-1=-5$ gives $x=-2$. The two solutions are symmetric about $\dfrac12$, where the argument vanishes. Their midpoint is indeed $\dfrac{-2+3}{2}=\dfrac12$. Reversing the sign of $3$ would give symmetry about the origin, which is not the required centre.
