How many distinct real solutions does $x^4+3x^2=0$ have?

- One.
- None.
- Two.
- Four.

## Explanation

The substitution $t=x^2$ gives $t(t+3)=0$. The root $-3$ is not the square of a real number. The root $t=0$ gives only $x=0$.
