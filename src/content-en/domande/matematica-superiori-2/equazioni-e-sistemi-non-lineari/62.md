When can $\sqrt{x+3}=-\sqrt{x+3}$ hold?

- $x=-3$
- $x\ge-3$
- $x=3$
- Never

## Explanation

For $x\ge-3$, the left side is nonnegative and the right side is nonpositive. They can agree only when both equal zero. This happens when $x+3=0$, so $x=-3$. Their squares agree throughout the domain because squaring removes the minus sign. The sign condition recovers the only equality between the original sides.
