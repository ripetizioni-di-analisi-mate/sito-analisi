Solve $x(x^2-25)=0$.

- $\{-5,5\}$
- $\{-5,0,5\}$
- $\{0,25\}$
- $\{0,5\}$

## Explanation

The product is zero if the first factor is zero or if $x^2=25$. The second equation has two opposite solutions.
