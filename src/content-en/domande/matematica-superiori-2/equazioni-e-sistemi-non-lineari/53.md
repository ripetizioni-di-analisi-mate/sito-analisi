Where is $P(x)=x(x-2)^2$ strictly positive?

- $(0,+\infty)$
- $(0,2)\cup(2,+\infty)$
- $[0,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,0)\cup(2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The square is positive everywhere except at $2$. Wherever it is positive, the product has the sign of $x$. Thus we need $x>0$ and $x\ne2$, giving the two intervals. The point $2$ is a double zero: the sign stays positive on both sides, but the point itself fails strict positivity. Zero is excluded too.
