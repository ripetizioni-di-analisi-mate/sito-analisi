Which identity expresses $x^2+y^2$ using the sum and product?

- $(x+y)^2+2xy$
- $(x-y)^2-2xy$
- $x+y-2xy$
- $(x+y)^2-2xy$

## Explanation

Expanding the square of the sum gives $x^2+2xy+y^2$. Subtracting twice the product leaves the sum of squares. The identity holds for all real numbers.
