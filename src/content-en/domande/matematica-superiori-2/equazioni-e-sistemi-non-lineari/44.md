Two real numbers $x,y$ have product $-6$. Without knowing their sum, which additional information determines **which** number is negative?

- $xy\ne0$
- $x\ne y$
- $x^2+y^2>0$
- $x<y$

## Explanation

Since the product is negative, exactly one number is negative. Knowing $x<y$ identifies $x$ as the negative one. The other statements remain true when the numbers are swapped.
