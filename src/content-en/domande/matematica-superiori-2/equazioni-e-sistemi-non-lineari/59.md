Which value satisfies $\sqrt{5x+11}=6$?

- $-5$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{47}{5}$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The right side is positive. Squaring gives a linear equation, and the solution makes the radicand equal to $36$. The result is $5$.
