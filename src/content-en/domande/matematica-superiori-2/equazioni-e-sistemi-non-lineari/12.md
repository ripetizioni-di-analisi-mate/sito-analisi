Squaring $\sqrt{x+12}=x$ gives $-3$ and $4$. Which check shows that only one is a solution?

- $-3$ is outside the radical’s domain
- $4$ makes the right side negative
- $4$ is not a root of the transformed equation
- $-3$ has the wrong sign for the right side

## Explanation

At $x=-3$ the radicand equals $9$, so the radical is defined. Its value is $3$, while the right side is $-3$. The squares agree but the numbers differ. At $x=4$, we have $\sqrt{16}=4$ with the correct sign. Before squaring, check the sign of the side opposite the radical as well as its domain.
