Solve $\sqrt{x+72}=x$.

- $\{-8;\,9\}$
- $\{-8\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\{9\}$

## Explanation

The left side is nonnegative, so $x$ must also be nonnegative. Squaring gives two candidates, but the negative one violates this condition. The positive root belongs to the domain and satisfies the original equation.

$$
x\ge0
$$

$$
x+72=x^2
$$

$$
(x-9)(x+8)=0
$$

$$
x=9\quad\text{is allowed}
$$
