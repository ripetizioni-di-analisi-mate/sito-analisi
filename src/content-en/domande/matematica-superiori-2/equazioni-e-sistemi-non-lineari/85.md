The paths $y=x^2$ and $y=2-x^2$ meet twice. What do their equations show about the positions of the two crossings?

- Both with positive horizontal coordinate
- Same horizontal coordinate and opposite heights
- Same height and opposite horizontal coordinates
- Both on the horizontal axis

## Explanation

Equating the heights gives $2x^2=2$, so $x=-1$ or $x=1$. In both cases $y=1$. This symmetry arises because both equations contain $x$ only through $x^2$.
