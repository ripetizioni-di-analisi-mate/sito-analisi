How many points do the line $x+y=4$ and the circle $x^2+y^2=8$ have in common?

- None.
- Two.
- One.
- Four.

## Explanation

Substituting $y=4-x$ into the circle equation gives $x^2+(4-x)^2=8$, equivalent to $2(x-2)^2=0$. The only pair is $(2;2)$, the point of tangency.
