In which equation is squaring both sides reversible **throughout the domain of the radicals**?

- $\sqrt{x-2}=x-4$
- $\sqrt{x-2}=-1$
- $\sqrt{x-2}=\sqrt{5-x}$
- $\sqrt{x-2}=2-x$

## Explanation

In the equation with two radicals, both sides are nonnegative throughout the common domain $[2,5]$. Squaring therefore preserves equality in both directions. In the other forms the first radical's domain is $x\ge2$, which does not guarantee the sign of the right side. In each of the other forms, $x=3$ gives a radical of $1$ and a right side of $-1$. Their squares agree at that point while the original sides differ.
