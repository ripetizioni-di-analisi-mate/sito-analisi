For which $x$ does $\sqrt{x+5}=\sqrt{11-x}$ hold?

- $8$
- $-3$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

Set the radicands equal. At the value obtained, both radicands are half the positive sum $16$. This gives $3$.
