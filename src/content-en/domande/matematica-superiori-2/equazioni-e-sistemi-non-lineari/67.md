For which $k$ does $|x-5|=k$ have exactly one real solution?

- $k>0$
- $k<0$
- $k=0$
- $k=5$

## Explanation

For $k>0$, two distinct coordinates are distance $k$ from $5$: one on each side. For $k<0$, a distance cannot equal the required value. At $k=0$ both constructions coincide at the centre $5$, the only solution. For instance, choosing $k=5$ gives $0$ and $10$, which are two distinct points.
