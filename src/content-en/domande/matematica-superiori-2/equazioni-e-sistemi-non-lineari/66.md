The graph of $y=|x-1|+2$ has two branches. Which horizontal line meets them at two distinct points?

- $y=2$
- $y=0$
- $y=1$
- $y=3$

## Explanation

The vertex has height $2$. At height $3$, the equation $|x-1|=1$ gives the two positions $0$ and $2$. Height $2$ meets only the vertex and lower lines miss the graph.
