Which form represents the quotient $\dfrac{x^3-4x}{x+2}$ and preserves its exact domain?

- $x(x-2)\quad(x\in\mathbb R)$
- $x(x+2)\quad(x\ne-2)$
- $x(x-2)\quad(x\ne2)$
- $x(x-2)\quad(x\ne-2)$

## Explanation

The numerator is $x(x-2)(x+2)$ and the denominator is $x+2$. Cancel the shared factor only for $x\ne-2$. On its own the simplified expression would equal $8$ at that point, where the original fraction is undefined. Conversely, $2$ is allowed and the quotient equals zero. The numerator zeroes are not all excluded values.
