For $x\ge0$ the two operations “add $1$ and then take the square root” and “take the square root and then add $1$” are compared. When do they give the same result?

- For every $x\ge0$
- Only for $x=1$
- For no $x\ge0$
- Only for $x=0$

## Explanation

The two operations give the same result when $\sqrt{x+1}=\sqrt{x}+1$, with $x\ge0$. Both sides are nonnegative, so the equation is equivalent to the one obtained by squaring them:

$$
x+1=x+2\sqrt{x}+1 .
$$

Subtracting $x+1$ from both sides gives $2\sqrt{x}=0$, therefore $\sqrt x=0$ and $x=0$. For $x=0$ both operations give $1$, so the equality holds only for $x=0$.
