A real pair $(x,y)$ satisfies $x+y=6$ and $x^2+y^2=20$. What is $(x-y)^2$ without solving separately for $x$ and $y$?

- $16$
- $8$
- $36$
- $4$

## Explanation

The squares of the sum and difference add up to $2(x^2+y^2)=40$. Since $(x+y)^2=36$, the squared difference is $40-36=4$.
