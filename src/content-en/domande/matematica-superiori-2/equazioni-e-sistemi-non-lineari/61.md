For $x\ge0$, compare “add $1$ and then take the square root” with “take the square root and then add $1$”. When do they give the same result?

- For every $x\ge0$
- Only for $x=1$
- For no $x\ge0$
- Only for $x=0$

## Explanation

The equality is $\sqrt{x+1}=\sqrt{x}+1$. Both sides are nonnegative. Squaring gives $x+1=x+2\sqrt{x}+1$, so $\sqrt x=0$. Checking $0$ gives $1=1$.
