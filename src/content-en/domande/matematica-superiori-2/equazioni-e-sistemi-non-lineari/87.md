What is the real solution set of $\dfrac{x^3-9x}{x^2-9}=x$?

- $\mathbb R$
- $\{-3,3\}$
- $\{0\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-3,3\}$

## Explanation

The denominator vanishes at $-3$ and $3$, so exclude both. At all other points the numerator is $x(x^2-9)$ and the common factor can be cancelled. This leaves $x=x$, an identity on the domain. Zero is allowed along with every number other than the two exclusions. At the excluded points the quotient would still have form $0:0$.
