The coefficient $a$ of the biquadratic $P(x)=x^4+ax^2$ has been lost, but its table still shows $P(3)=0$. What value can be recovered for $P(1)$?

- $0$
- $10$
- $-8$
- $-9$

## Explanation

The known value gives $81+9a=0$, hence $a=-9$. Substituting $1$ gives $P(1)=1-9=-8$. The fourth power differs from the square when computing $P(3)$.
