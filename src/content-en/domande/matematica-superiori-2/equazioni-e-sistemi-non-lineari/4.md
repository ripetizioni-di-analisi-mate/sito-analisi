To find the nonnegative integer solution of $\sqrt{x+6}=x$ without the quadratic formula, the equation is rewritten as $x(x-1)=6$. Which reading of the factors gives the solution?

- The factors are $3$ and $2$, with $x=2$
- The factors are $6$ and $1$, with $x=6$
- The factors are $-2$ and $-3$, with $x=-2$
- The factors are $3$ and $2$, with $x=3$

## Explanation

The factors $x$ and $x-1$ are consecutive integers, with the first larger. Among nonnegative integers, $6$ is obtained as $3\cdot2$. Checking gives $\sqrt{3+6}=3$.
