If $x+y=5$ and $x^2+y^2=17$, what is $xy$?

- $8$
- $21$
- $4$
- $12$

## Explanation

The square of the sum is $25$ and includes the term $2xy$. Subtracting the sum of squares leaves $2xy=25-17=8$, so $xy=4$. The value $8$ counts the product twice. As a check, construct $t^2-5t+4=0$, whose roots are $1$ and $4$. These numbers sum to $5$, and their squares sum to $17$.
