Which candidate solves $\sqrt{x+4}=2-x$?

- $5$
- $-4$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

The square root requires $x\ge-4$, and the right side requires $x\le2$. Equating the squares gives $x(x-5)=0$. Only $0$ is in the allowed interval, and both sides then give $2$.
