You want $x=4$ to solve $\sqrt{x+a}=x-1$. Which value of $a$ makes the original equation true?

- $9$
- $-1$
- $12$
- $5$

## Explanation

At the required point the right side is $3$. Thus $4+a=3^2$, giving $a=5$. Substitution yields $\sqrt9=3$.
