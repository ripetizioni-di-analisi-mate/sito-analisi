Which value satisfies $\sqrt{4x+9}=5$?

- $\dfrac{17}{2}$
- $-4$
- $-1$
- $4$

## Explanation

The right side is positive. Squaring gives a linear equation, and the solution makes the radicand equal to $25$. The result is $4$.
