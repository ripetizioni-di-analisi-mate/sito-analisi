Which solution is left for $\dfrac{x^2-25}{x-5}=0$?

- $\varnothing$
- $\{-5,5\}$
- $\{-5\}$
- $\{5\}$

## Explanation

The numerator is zero at two points. The denominator excludes the positive one, so the opposite value remains.
