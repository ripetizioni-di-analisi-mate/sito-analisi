For $x\ne0$, the equality $x+\frac4x=5$ can be turned into a polynomial equation. Which transformation preserves exactly the solutions?

- $x+4=5x,\ x\ne0$
- $x^2+4=5,\ x\ne0$
- $x^2-5x+4=0,\ x\ne0$
- $x^2-5x-4=0,\ x\ne0$

## Explanation

Multiplying every term by $x$ gives $x^2+4=5x$. This multiplication is reversible because $x\ne0$. Both roots $1$ and $4$ are admissible.
