A biquadratic contains only the powers $x^4,x^2$ and a constant term. If $r$ is a nonzero real root, what must also be true?

- $-r$ is a second root
- $r^2$ is a root
- $1/r$ is a root
- $0$ is a root

## Explanation

Replacing $r$ by $-r$ leaves its square and fourth power unchanged. The polynomial therefore has the same value zero, and the two roots are distinct because $r\ne0$.
