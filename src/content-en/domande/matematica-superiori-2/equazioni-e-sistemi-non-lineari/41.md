When substituting $t=x^2$ into a biquadratic equation over the reals, which condition must be kept?

- $t\ge0$
- $t>0$
- $t\le0$
- $t\ne0$

## Explanation

The square of a real number is nonnegative and can be zero. A negative root of the equation in $t$ gives no real value of $x$. The correct condition therefore includes zero.
