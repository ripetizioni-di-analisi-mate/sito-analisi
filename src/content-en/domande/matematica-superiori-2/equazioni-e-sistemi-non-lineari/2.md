The polynomial $x^3-4x$ is written as a product, and one of its three factors is $x$. Which pair completes the factorisation into linear factors?

- $x-4,\ x+4$
- $x-2,\ x+2$
- $x-1,\ x+4$
- $x-2,\ x-2$

## Explanation

Factoring out $x$ leaves $x^2-4$. The difference of squares factors as $(x-2)(x+2)$. The product then shows the three roots $0,-2,2$.
