Substituting $t=x^2$ into $x^4+5x^2+4=0$ gives two real roots in $t$. How many real solutions are there in $x$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The auxiliary equation factors as $(t+1)(t+4)=0$. Its roots are $-1$ and $-4$, and neither can be the square of a real number. So counting the roots in $t$ does not determine the original solution count. A direct check notes that $x^4$ and $5x^2$ are nonnegative, while the constant $4$ makes their sum strictly positive.
