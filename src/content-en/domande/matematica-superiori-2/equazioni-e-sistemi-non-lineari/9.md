For $x^4-25x^2+144=0$, Lea obtains $(x^2-9)(x^2-16)=0$ and lists only $x=3,4$. What exactly is missing in her reasoning?

- $-3$ and $-4$ also have the required squares
- $9$ and $16$ are also values of $x$
- Both factors must vanish at the same point
- The product always vanishes at $0$ as well

## Explanation

The product vanishes when $x^2=9$ or $x^2=16$. Each equality has two opposite solutions, giving the complete list $-4,-3,3,4$.
