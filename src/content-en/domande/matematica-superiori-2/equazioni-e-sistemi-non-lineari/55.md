The expressions $x^2(x+1)$ and $x(x+1)$ give the same value. Which solution list includes the cases where their common factor vanishes?

- $\{0,1\}$
- $\{1\}$
- $\{-1,0,1\}$
- $\{-1,1\}$

## Explanation

Subtracting the sides gives $x(x+1)(x-1)=0$. Cancelling $x(x+1)$ straight away would lose $0$ and $-1$. All three values satisfy the original equality.
