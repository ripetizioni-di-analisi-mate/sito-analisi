Which factorization solves $x^3-x^2-6x=0$ using the zero-product property?

- $x(x+3)(x-2)=0$
- $(x-3)(x+2)=0$
- $x(x-3)(x+2)=0$
- $x(x-3)(x-2)=0$

## Explanation

Factoring out $x$ leaves $x^2-x-6$. The numbers $-3$ and $2$ have sum $-1$ and product $-6$, so the trinomial is $(x-3)(x+2)$. The factor $x$ remains in the product.
