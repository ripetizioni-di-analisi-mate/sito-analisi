Solve $x^4-97x^2+1296=0$.

- $\{16,81\}$
- $\{-9,-4,4,9\}$
- $\{4,9\}$
- $\{-4,4\}$

## Explanation

Setting $t=x^2$ gives a trinomial with roots $16$ and $81$. Each gives two opposite values of $x$.
