The system $y=x^2$, $y=2x$ has two solutions. If the model requires $y>0$, which point remains?

- $(0;0)$
- $(2;4)$
- $(4;2)$
- $(-2;4)$

## Explanation

Equating the heights gives $x(x-2)=0$. The points are $(0;0)$ and $(2;4)$. The condition on the height excludes the origin.
