Solve $x^{3} - 100 x=0$.

- $\{-10;\,10\}$
- $\{-10;\,0;\,10\}$
- $\{0;\,10\}$
- $\{0;\,100\}$

## Explanation

Factor out $x$ and factor the difference of squares. The product is zero when at least one of the three factors is zero. There are three distinct real roots.

$$
x(x^2-100)=0
$$

$$
x(x-10)(x+10)=0
$$
