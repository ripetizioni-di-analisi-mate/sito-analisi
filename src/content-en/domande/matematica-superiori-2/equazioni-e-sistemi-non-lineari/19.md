For which $x$ does $\sqrt{x+3}=\sqrt{7-x}$ hold?

- $4$
- $5$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Set the radicands equal. At the value obtained, both radicands are half the positive sum $10$. This gives $2$.
