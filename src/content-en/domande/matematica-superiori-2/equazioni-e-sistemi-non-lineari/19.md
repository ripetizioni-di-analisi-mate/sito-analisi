What are the domain $D$ and solution set $S$ of $\sqrt{x-3}=\sqrt{9-2x}$?

- $D=[3,+\infty),\quad S=\{4\}$
- $D=(-\infty,\tfrac92],\quad S=\{4\}$
- $D=[3,\tfrac92],\quad S=\{4\}$
- $D=[3,\tfrac92],\quad S=\{3\}$

## Explanation

The first radicand requires $x\ge3$ and the second $x\le\dfrac92$. Both hold in the closed interval. Since the roots are nonnegative, equality is equivalent to $x-3=9-2x$, giving $x=4$. The value is inside the domain and makes both radicals equal to one. The endpoints belong to the domain because a zero radicand is allowed.
