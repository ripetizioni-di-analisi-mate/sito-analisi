Which value satisfies $\sqrt{7-3x}=2$?

- $1$
- $-1$
- $\dfrac53$
- $3$

## Explanation

The right side is positive. Squaring gives $7-3x=4$, leading to $-3x=-3$ and $x=1$. Substitution confirms $\sqrt4=2$.
