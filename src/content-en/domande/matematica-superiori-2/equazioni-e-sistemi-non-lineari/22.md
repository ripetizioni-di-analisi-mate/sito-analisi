Which ordered pair satisfies $x+y=7$, $xy=10$, and the restriction $x<y$?

- $(5;2)$
- $(2;5)$
- $(1;6)$
- $(3;4)$

## Explanation

The unknowns are the roots of $t^2-7t+10=0$, namely $2$ and $5$. Without a restriction the equations accept either order. The condition $x<y$ assigns the smaller value to the first coordinate, leaving $(2;5)$. The sum and product are $2+5=7$ and $2\cdot5=10$. The other increasing pairs have the right sum but the wrong products.
