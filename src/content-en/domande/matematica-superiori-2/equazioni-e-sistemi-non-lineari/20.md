For which $x$ does $\sqrt{x+4}=\sqrt{9-x}$ hold?

- $\dfrac{13}{2}$
- $5$
- $- \dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

Set the radicands equal. At the value obtained, both radicands are half the positive sum $13$. This gives $\dfrac{5}{2}$.
