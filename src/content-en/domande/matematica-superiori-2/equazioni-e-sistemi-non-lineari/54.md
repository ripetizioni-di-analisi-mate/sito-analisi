How many common points do the curves $y=x^3-x$ and $y=x$ have?

- $1$
- $3$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

The vertical coordinates must agree, so $x^3-x=x$, or $x(x^2-2)=0$. This gives three distinct horizontal coordinates: $0$ and $\pm\sqrt2$. Each vertical coordinate equals its horizontal one because one graph is the line $y=x$. There are three points. Counting zeroes of the original cubic would instead count its intersections with the horizontal axis, a different line.
