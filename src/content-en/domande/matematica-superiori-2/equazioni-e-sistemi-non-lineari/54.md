Solve $x(x^2-36)=0$.

- $\{-6,6\}$
- $\{-6,0,6\}$
- $\{0,36\}$
- $\{0,6\}$

## Explanation

The product is zero if the first factor is zero or if $x^2=36$. The second equation has two opposite solutions.
