What are the solutions of $|2x-1|=x+2$?

- $\left\{-\dfrac13;3\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac13;3\right\}$
- $\left\{-\dfrac13\right\}$
- $\{3\}$

## Explanation

The condition $x\ge-2$ is required. For $x\ge\dfrac12$, solve $2x-1=x+2$ to obtain $3$. For $-2\le x<\dfrac12$, solve $1-2x=x+2$ to obtain $-\dfrac13$. Each value belongs to the branch used to find it.
