How many points do $y=x^2$ and $y=16$ have in common?

- $0$
- $4$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The x-coordinates must satisfy $x^2=16$. Since the right side is positive, there are two distinct x-coordinates. This gives $2$.
