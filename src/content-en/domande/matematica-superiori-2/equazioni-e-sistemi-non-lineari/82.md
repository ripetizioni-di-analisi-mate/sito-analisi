For which $m$ is the line $y=4x+m$ tangent to the parabola $y=x^2$?

- $4$
- $-2$
- $-4$
- $0$

## Explanation

Equating the vertical coordinates gives $x^2-4x=m$. Completing the square gives $(x-2)^2=m+4$. Exactly one horizontal coordinate requires a zero right side, so $m=-4$. The common point is $(2;4)$, which satisfies both relations. A greater value of $m$ gives two solutions of the squared equation, while a smaller value gives none.
