A pair of real numbers is supposed to have sum $5$ and product $7$. Which check shows that no such real pair exists?

- The product exceeds the sum
- $5+7$ is even
- $(x-y)^2=5^2-4\cdot7=-3$
- $5$ and $7$ are both odd

## Explanation

The identity $(x-y)^2=(x+y)^2-4xy$ would force a square to equal $-3$. This is impossible for real numbers. Comparing the sum and the product alone is insufficient.
