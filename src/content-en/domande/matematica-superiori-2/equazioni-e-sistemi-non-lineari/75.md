Which pair solves the system $\begin{cases}x+y=6\\xy=9\end{cases}$?

- $(3;3)$
- $(2;4)$
- $(1;5)$
- $(0;6)$

## Explanation

The two unknowns are the roots of $t^2-6t+9=(t-3)^2$. Both equal $3$. The resulting pair has sum $6$ and product $9$.
