The system contains $y=x^2$ and $2y=2x^2$. How many real ordered pairs are in its solution set?

- Infinitely many, one for every real $x$
- One, because the curves are tangent
- None, because the coefficients differ
- Two, because there are two equations

## Explanation

Dividing the second equation by $2$ gives the first. Both describe the same complete parabola, and every pair $(t;t^2)$ satisfies them.
