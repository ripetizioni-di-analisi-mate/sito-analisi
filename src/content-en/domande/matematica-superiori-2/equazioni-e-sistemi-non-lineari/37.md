On a straight road, two stops are at coordinates $-1$ and $3$. A third stop must be **twice as far** from $-1$ as from $3$ and lie between them. Where should it go?

- $x=\dfrac53$
- $x=1$
- $x=7$
- $x=\dfrac13$

## Explanation

Between the stops the distances are $x+1$ and $3-x$. Setting $x+1=2(3-x)$ gives $3x=5$. The point divides the stretch of length $4$ into lengths $8/3$ and $4/3$.
