Solve $|x-3|=|x+1|$.

- $x=1$
- $x=2$
- $x=-1$
- $x=3$

## Explanation

The absolute values are distances from $3$ and $-1$. The point equidistant from those numbers is their midpoint, with coordinate $\dfrac{3+(-1)}2=1$.
