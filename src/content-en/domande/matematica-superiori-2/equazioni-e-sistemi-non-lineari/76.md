In the system $\begin{cases}x+y=7\\xy=10\end{cases}$, what is the value of $x^2+y^2$?

- $10$
- $29$
- $9$
- $49$

## Explanation

Expanding the square of the sum gives $x^2+2xy+y^2$. Subtract twice the product from the square of the sum to obtain $29$.
