For two nonzero numbers, $x+y=8$ and $xy=5$ are known. Without finding the individual numbers, what is $\dfrac1x+\dfrac1y$?

- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac85$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac58$

## Explanation

Using the common denominator $xy$ gives $(x+y)/(xy)$. Both required values are already in the data, giving $8/5$.
