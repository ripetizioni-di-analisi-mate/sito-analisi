Which sign condition is necessary for $\sqrt{A(x)}=B(x)$?

- $B(x)<0$
- $B(x)\ge0$
- $B(x)\ne0$
- $B(x)\le0$

## Explanation

The principal square root is nonnegative. The side equal to the square root must satisfy the same sign condition. The domain condition on the radicand still applies.
