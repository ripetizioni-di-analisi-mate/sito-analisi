Which points are common to the vertical line $x=1$ and the circle $x^2+y^2=10$?

- $\{(1;-3),(1;3)\}$
- $\{(-3;1),(3;1)\}$
- $\{(1;3)\}$
- $\{(1;9)\}$

## Explanation

The linear relation fixes $x$ directly. Substituting $1$ into the circle gives $1+y^2=10$, so $y=-3$ or $y=3$. The pairs share their first coordinate because both must lie on the vertical line. Swapping them would preserve the circle equation but lose the restriction $x=1$. Both heights check as $1+9=10$.
