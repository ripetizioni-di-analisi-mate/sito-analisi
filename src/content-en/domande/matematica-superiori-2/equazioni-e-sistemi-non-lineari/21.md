What is the solution set of $|x+2|=|3x-2|$?

- $\{0,2\}$
- $\{-2,0\}$
- $\{2,4\}$
- $\{2\}$

## Explanation

The arguments can agree or be opposites. In the first case, $x+2=3x-2$ gives $x=2$. In the second, $x+2=2-3x$ gives $x=0$. The checks give absolute values $4$ and $2$, respectively, on both sides. Both cases are allowed because the equation compares the absolute values. Factoring the difference of their squares gives the same two cases.
