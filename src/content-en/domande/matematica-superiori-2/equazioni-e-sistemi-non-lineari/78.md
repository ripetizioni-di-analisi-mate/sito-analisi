If $x^2+y^2=26$ and $xy=5$, what values can $x+y$ take?

- Only $6$
- $-4$ and $4$
- Only $36$
- $-6$ and $6$

## Explanation

Expanding the square gives $(x+y)^2=26+2\cdot5=36$. The sum can therefore be $6$ or $-6$. Both cases occur: $(1,5)$ and $(-1,-5)$ have the same product and sum of squares. Reversing both signs keeps the two given quantities fixed but changes the sign of the sum. Taking only the positive square root would lose the second case.
