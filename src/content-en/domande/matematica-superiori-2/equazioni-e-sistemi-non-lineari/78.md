In the system $\begin{cases}x+y=13\\xy=36\end{cases}$, what is the value of $x^2+y^2$?

- $169$
- $36$
- $25$
- $97$

## Explanation

Expanding the square of the sum gives $x^2+2xy+y^2$. Subtract twice the product from the square of the sum to obtain $97$.
