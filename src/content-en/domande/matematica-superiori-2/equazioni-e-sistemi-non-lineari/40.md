Can $\dfrac{x^2-1}{x-a}=0$ have exactly two distinct real solutions? Which proposed choice of $a$ allows it?

- $a=1$
- $a=-1$
- No choice can allow it
- $a=0$

## Explanation

The numerator vanishes at $1$ and $-1$. Both survive if the denominator excludes a different value. With $a=0$ it excludes only $0$.
