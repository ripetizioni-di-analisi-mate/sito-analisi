Which value makes the numerator of $\dfrac{x^3-9x}{x+3}$ zero but lies outside the domain?

- $0$
- $3$
- $9$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The numerator is $x(x-3)(x+3)$. For $x=-3$, the denominator $x+3$ is also zero, so that value must be excluded.
