Solve $x^4-29x^2+100=0$.

- $\{4,25\}$
- $\{-2,2\}$
- $\{2,5\}$
- $\{-5,-2,2,5\}$

## Explanation

Setting $t=x^2$ gives a trinomial with roots $4$ and $25$. Each gives two opposite values of $x$.
