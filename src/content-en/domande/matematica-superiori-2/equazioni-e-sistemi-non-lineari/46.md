Which equation in $x$ reduces to $u^2-3u-4=0$ under the substitution $u=(x-1)^2$?

- $(x-1)^2-3(x-1)-4=0$
- $x^4-3x^2-4=0$
- $(x-1)^4-3(x-1)-4=0$
- $(x-1)^4-3(x-1)^2-4=0$

## Explanation

The term $u^2$ becomes $(x-1)^4$, and $-3u$ becomes $-3(x-1)^2$. When solving for $u$, only roots with $u\ge0$ can lead to real $x$.
