For $\dfrac{x^3-4x}{x-2}=4x$, what set remains after checking the domain?

- $\{0,2\}$
- $\{0\}$
- $\{-2,0\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

For $x\ne2$, cancel $x-2$ to obtain $x(x+2)=4x$. Factoring then gives $x(x-2)=0$, with candidates $0$ and $2$. The second is excluded even though the reduced sides agree there. At $0$, the original fraction is zero because the denominator equals $-2$. The original domain determines which root of the reduced form is actually valid.
