In $(x^2-1)^2=4$, let $z=x^2-1$. Which value of $z$ solves $z^2=4$ but must be discarded when returning to real $x$?

- Neither of them
- $-2$
- Both $-2$ and $2$
- $2$

## Explanation

Since $x^2\ge0$, we have $z\ge-1$. The value $z=-2$ would require $x^2=-1$. In contrast, $z=2$ gives $x=\pm\sqrt3$.
