Which pairs solve $x-y=2$ and $x^2+y^2=20$?

- $\{(4,2),(2,4)\}$
- $\{(-4,-2),(-2,-4)\}$
- $\{(4,2),(-2,-4)\}$
- $\{(4,-2),(-4,2)\}$

## Explanation

The first relation gives $y=x-2$. Substitution into the second gives $x^2+(x-2)^2=20$, or $x^2-2x-8=0$. The horizontal coordinates are $4$ and $-2$, with corresponding vertical coordinates $2$ and $-4$. Swapping coordinates preserves the sum of squares but reverses the sign of the difference. The swap is therefore not a symmetry of this solution set.
