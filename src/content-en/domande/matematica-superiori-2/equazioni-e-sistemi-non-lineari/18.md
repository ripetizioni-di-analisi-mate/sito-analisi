Two containers hold volumes $x-1$ and $5-x$, both nonnegative. We want to compare $\sqrt{x-1}$ and $\sqrt{5-x}$. For which values of $x$ is the comparison defined?

- $(-\infty,1]\cup[5,+\infty)$
- $[1,5]$
- $(1,5)$
- $\{3\}$

## Explanation

We need both $x\ge1$ and $x\le5$. The values $1$ and $5$ are allowed because zero has a square root. Requiring the two quantities to be equal would be an additional condition.
