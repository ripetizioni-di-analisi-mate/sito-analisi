For which $x$ does $\sqrt{x+2}=\sqrt{5-x}$ hold?

- $- \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$

## Explanation

Set the radicands equal. At the value obtained, both radicands are half the positive sum $7$. This gives $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
