The graph of $y=\sqrt{x}$ is compared with the line $y=-1$. How far must the line be shifted upwards to make first contact with the graph?

::grafico{f="sqrt(x);-1" xmin=-1.1 xmax=5 ymin=-1.8 ymax=3.5 uguale=1 titolo="The line in its starting position"}

- By $1$, meeting at $(1,0)$
- By $1$, meeting at $(0,0)$
- By $2$, first meeting at $(1,1)$
- By $0$, because the graphs already meet

## Explanation

The values of $\sqrt{x}$ start at zero, which occurs only at $x=0$. The line must rise from $-1$ to $0$ before it can meet the graph.
