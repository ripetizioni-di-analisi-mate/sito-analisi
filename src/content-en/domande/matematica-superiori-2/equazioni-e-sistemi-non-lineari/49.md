Solve $x^4-146x^2+3025=0$.

- $\{5,11\}$
- $\{25,121\}$
- $\{-11,-5,5,11\}$
- $\{-5,5\}$

## Explanation

Setting $t=x^2$ gives a trinomial with roots $25$ and $121$. Each gives two opposite values of $x$.
