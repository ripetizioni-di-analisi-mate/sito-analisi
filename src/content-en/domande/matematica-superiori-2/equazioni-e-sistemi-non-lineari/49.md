For which $k$ does $x^4-5x^2+k=0$ have four distinct real roots?

- $k<\dfrac{25}{4}$
- $0\le k\le\dfrac{25}{4}$
- $0<k<\dfrac{25}{4}$
- $k>\dfrac{25}{4}$

## Explanation

The equation in $t$ must have two distinct positive roots. Its discriminant $25-4k$ and product $k$ must therefore both be positive. Since the sum is $5$, these conditions make both roots positive. At $k=0$, an auxiliary value is zero and gives just one coordinate. At $k=\dfrac{25}{4}$ the two auxiliary values coincide.
