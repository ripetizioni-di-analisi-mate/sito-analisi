What is the real solution of $\sqrt{x+4}=\sqrt x+1$?

- $\dfrac32$
- $3$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac94$

## Explanation

On the domain $x\ge0$, both sides are positive. Squaring gives $x+4=x+2\sqrt x+1$. From $\sqrt x=\dfrac32$ we obtain $x=\dfrac94$, which satisfies the original equation.
