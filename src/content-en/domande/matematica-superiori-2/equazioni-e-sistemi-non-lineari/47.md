Solve $x^4-58x^2+441=0$.

- $\{-3,3\}$
- $\{3,7\}$
- $\{9,49\}$
- $\{-7,-3,3,7\}$

## Explanation

Setting $t=x^2$ gives a trinomial with roots $9$ and $49$. Each gives two opposite values of $x$.
