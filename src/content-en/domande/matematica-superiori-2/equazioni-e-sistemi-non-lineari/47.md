How should the real roots of $(x^2-2)^2=0$ be described?

- One double root
- Four distinct roots
- Two distinct roots, both simple
- Two distinct roots, each double

## Explanation

The only auxiliary value is $t=2$, but it is positive and therefore gives two values of $x$. Factoring gives $(x-\sqrt2)^2(x+\sqrt2)^2$. The two roots are opposites, and each occurs in a squared factor. There are two distinct values, while the multiplicities sum to four. A double value of $t$ does not give just one horizontal coordinate.
