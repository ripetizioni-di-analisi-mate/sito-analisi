Solve $\sqrt{x+1}=x-1$.

- $\{0;3\}$
- $\{3\}$
- $\{0\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The right side requires $x\ge1$. Squaring gives $x+1=(x-1)^2$, or $x(x-3)=0$. Only $3$ meets the condition and satisfies $\sqrt4=3-1$.
