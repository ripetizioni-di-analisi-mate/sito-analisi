We know that $x=2$ is a root of $P(x)=x^3+ax+2$. What must the remainder be when $P(x)$ is divided by $x-2$?

- $2$
- $0$
- $a$
- $8$

## Explanation

Write $P(x)=(x-2)Q(x)+r$ and substitute $x=2$ to get $P(2)=r$. Since $2$ is a root, this value is zero and the division is exact.
