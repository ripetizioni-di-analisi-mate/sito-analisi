A rectangle has area $12$ and its sides differ by $1$. If $u$ represents the shorter side, which model also respects the meaning of the unknowns?

- $u+(u+1)=12,\quad u>0$
- $u(u+1)=12,\quad u>0$
- $u(u+1)=12,\quad u<0$
- $u(u-1)=12,\quad u>0$

## Explanation

The longer side is $u+1$ and the area is the product of the sides. The equation gives $u=3$ or $u=-4$. Only $3$ is a length, so the sides are $3$ and $4$.
