If $x+y=-7$, $xy=10$, and $x<y$, what is $y-x$?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $9$
- $7$

## Explanation

The squared difference is $(y-x)^2=(x+y)^2-4xy=49-40=9$. Since $x<y$, the requested difference is positive, so it equals $3$. Without the ordering restriction, swapping the coordinates would give both signs. The auxiliary quadratic checks this: its roots are $-5$ and $-2$, and the difference between the larger and smaller is three.
