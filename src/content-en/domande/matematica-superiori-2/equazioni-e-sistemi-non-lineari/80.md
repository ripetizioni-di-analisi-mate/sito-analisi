What value of $xy$ follows from $x+y=7$ and $x^2+y^2=29$?

- $10$
- $20$
- $22$
- $39$

## Explanation

Since $(x+y)^2=x^2+y^2+2xy$, we have $49=29+2xy$. Dividing $49-29$ by $2$ gives $xy=10$.
