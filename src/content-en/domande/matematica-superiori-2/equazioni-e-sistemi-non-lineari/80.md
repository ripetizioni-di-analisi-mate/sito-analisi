In the system $xy=0$, $x^2+y^2=9$, how many real ordered pairs belong both to the circle and to the union of the two axes?

- $4$
- $2$
- $5$
- $3$

## Explanation

The zero product requires $x=0$ or $y=0$. The vertical axis gives $(0,\pm3)$ and the horizontal axis gives $(\pm3,0)$. The origin does not satisfy the circle equation and never appears.
