Solve $x(x^2-9)=0$.

- $\{-3,0,3\}$
- $\{0,3\}$
- $\{0,9\}$
- $\{-3,3\}$

## Explanation

The product is zero if the first factor is zero or if $x^2=9$. The second equation has two opposite solutions.
