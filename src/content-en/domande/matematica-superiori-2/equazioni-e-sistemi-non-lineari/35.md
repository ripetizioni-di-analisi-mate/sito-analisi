Solve $\sqrt{x+11}=12$.

- $\{1\}$
- $\{155\}$
- $\{133\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The square root equals a positive number. On the domain, squaring both sides does not introduce a sign conflict. The value obtained also satisfies the original equation.

$$
x\ge-11
$$

$$
x+11=144
$$

$$
x=133
$$
