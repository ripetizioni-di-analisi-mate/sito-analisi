When both sides of $\sqrt{x+17}=\sqrt x+1$ are squared, which extra term appears with $x$ and $1$ on the right?

- $\sqrt x$
- $2x$
- $2\sqrt x$
- $2$

## Explanation

The square of $\sqrt x+1$ includes twice the product $2\cdot\sqrt x\cdot1=2\sqrt x$. The equation becomes $x+17=x+2\sqrt x+1$. This term gives $\sqrt x=8$, hence $x=64$. The check gives $\sqrt{81}=9$ and $\sqrt{64}+1=9$. Omitting the cross term would create a contradiction that is absent from the original equation.
