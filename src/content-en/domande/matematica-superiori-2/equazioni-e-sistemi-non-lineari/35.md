For $x\ge0$, which expression equals $\sqrt{x+1}-\sqrt x$ and immediately shows that it is positive?

- $\dfrac{2x+1}{\sqrt{x+1}+\sqrt x}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x+1}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x+1}+\sqrt x}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x+1}-\sqrt x}$

## Explanation

Multiplying the difference by the sum of the radicals gives $(x+1)-x=1$. The sum is positive throughout the domain, so division by it is allowed.
