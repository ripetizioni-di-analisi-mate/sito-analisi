When $x+y=4$ and $x,y$ are real, what is the set of all possible values of the product $p=xy$?

- $0\le p\le4$
- $p\le4$
- $p\ge4$
- $p<4$

## Explanation

The auxiliary equation is $t^2-4t+p=0$, with discriminant $16-4p$. Two real values, possibly equal, require $p\le4$. Every product meeting this condition comes from the roots of that equation. The boundary occurs when $x=y=2$. Negative products are possible because the unknowns need not be positive. For example, $x=-1$ and $y=5$ give $p=-5$.
