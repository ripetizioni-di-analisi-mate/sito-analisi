Two numbers have sum $7$ and product $12$. Which equation in one unknown has exactly those two numbers as its roots?

- $t^2-12t+7=0$
- $t^2-7t+12=0$
- $t^2-7t-12=0$
- $t^2+7t+12=0$

## Explanation

If the first number is $t$, the second is $7-t$. Their product gives $t(7-t)=12$, which rearranges to the stated equation and factors as $(t-3)(t-4)=0$.
