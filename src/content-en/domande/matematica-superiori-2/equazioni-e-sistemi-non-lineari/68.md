Which branch of $|x-1|=x+3$ gives an allowed solution?

- The branch $x\ge1$ gives $x=1$
- The branch $x<-3$ gives $x=-4$
- Both branches give a solution
- The branch $-3\le x<1$ gives $x=-1$

## Explanation

The right side requires $x\ge-3$. For $x\ge1$, removing the absolute value gives $x-1=x+3$, which never holds. For $-3\le x<1$, we get $1-x=x+3$, so $x=-1$. The value is on its branch, and substitution gives $|-2|=2$. The boundary $x=1$ is not a solution because the left side is zero and the right side is four.
