How many points do the graph of $y=\dfrac{x^2-4}{x-2}$ and the line $y=4$ have in common?

- $1$
- $0$
- $2$
- Infinitely many

## Explanation

The quotient equals $x+2$ when $x\ne2$. The full line $y=x+2$ would meet $y=4$ at $(2,4)$. The original fraction has a missing point there because the denominator is zero. No other horizontal coordinate satisfies $x+2=4$. Simplification gives the shape of the graph as long as the exclusion at the cancelled factor is retained.
