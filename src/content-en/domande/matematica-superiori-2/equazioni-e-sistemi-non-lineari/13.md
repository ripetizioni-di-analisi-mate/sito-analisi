Solve $|x-8|=9$.

- $\{-1;\,17\}$
- $\{17\}$
- $\{-1\}$
- $\{-9;\,9\}$

## Explanation

The absolute value measures distance from the given number. A positive distance gives one point on each side of that number. This leads to two linear equations.

$$
x-8=9\quad\text{or}\quad x-8=-9
$$

$$
x=17\quad\text{or}\quad x=-1
$$
