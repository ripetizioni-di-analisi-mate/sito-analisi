What is the solution set of $|x-3|=x-3$?

- $[3,+\infty)$
- $(3,+\infty)$
- $\{3\}$
- $(-\infty,3]$

## Explanation

The right side must be nonnegative, so $x\ge3$. Throughout that interval, the argument $x-3$ is already nonnegative and the absolute value leaves it unchanged. The endpoint $3$ is included because both sides equal zero. Below $3$, the right side is negative and cannot equal the absolute value. One whole branch satisfies the equation.
