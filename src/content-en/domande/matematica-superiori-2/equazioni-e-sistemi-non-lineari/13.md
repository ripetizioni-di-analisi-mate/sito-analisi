How are the solutions of $|x-3|=4$ represented on the number line?

- Two points at distance $4$ from $3$, one on either side
- All points at distance at most $4$ from $3$
- A single point at distance $3$ from $4$
- Every point outside the interval $[-1,7]$

## Explanation

The absolute value measures distance from $3$. Equality requires the exact distance, so the only points are $3-4=-1$ and $3+4=7$.
