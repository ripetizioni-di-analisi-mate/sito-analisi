Which pairs satisfy $x+y=4$ and $xy=0$?

- $\{(0,4),(4,0)\}$
- $\{(0,4)\}$
- $\{(4,0)\}$
- $\{(2,2)\}$

## Explanation

A zero product requires at least one coordinate to be zero. If it is $x$, the sum gives $y=4$. If it is $y$, the sum gives $x=4$. Both orders satisfy both equations, so there are two distinct pairs. The pair $(2,2)$ has the right sum but product $4$. Dividing by a coordinate without checking when it is zero would lose a solution.
