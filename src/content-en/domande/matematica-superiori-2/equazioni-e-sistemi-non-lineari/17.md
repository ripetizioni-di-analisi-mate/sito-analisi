What are the real solutions of $x^4+x^2-6=0$?

- $\{-\sqrt2;\sqrt2\}$
- $\{-\sqrt2;0;\sqrt2\}$
- $\{-2;2\}$
- $\{\sqrt2\}$

## Explanation

Setting $t=x^2$ gives $(t+3)(t-2)=0$. Exclude $t=-3$ because $t$ is a square. From $t=2$, the solutions are $x=-\sqrt2$ and $x=\sqrt2$.
