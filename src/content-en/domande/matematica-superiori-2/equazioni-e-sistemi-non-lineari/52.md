Which monic polynomial of degree three has zeroes $-1$, $0$, and $2$?

- $x^3+x^2-2x$
- $x^3-x^2-2x$
- $x^3-x^2+2x$
- $x^3-2x^2-x$

## Explanation

The three roots require factors $x+1$, $x$, and $x-2$. Their product already has leading coefficient one. Expanding $(x+1)(x-2)$ gives $x^2-x-2$, and the factor $x$ gives the required form. The root zero ensures there is no constant term. Checking the other two factors also determines the signs of the remaining coefficients.
