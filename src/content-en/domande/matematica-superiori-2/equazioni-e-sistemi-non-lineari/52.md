Solve $x(x^2-16)=0$.

- $\{-4,4\}$
- $\{-4,0,4\}$
- $\{0,4\}$
- $\{0,16\}$

## Explanation

The product is zero if the first factor is zero or if $x^2=16$. The second equation has two opposite solutions.
