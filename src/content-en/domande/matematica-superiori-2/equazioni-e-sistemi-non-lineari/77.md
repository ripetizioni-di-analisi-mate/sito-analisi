In the system $\begin{cases}x+y=10\\xy=21\end{cases}$, what is the value of $x^2+y^2$?

- $58$
- $16$
- $100$
- $21$

## Explanation

Expanding the square of the sum gives $x^2+2xy+y^2$. Subtract twice the product from the square of the sum to obtain $58$.
