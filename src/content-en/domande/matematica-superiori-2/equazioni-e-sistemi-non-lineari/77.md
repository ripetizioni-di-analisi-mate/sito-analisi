Given $x+y=5$ and $xy=6$, what is $x^3+y^3$?

- $35$
- $125$
- $95$
- $107$

## Explanation

Expanding the cube of the sum gives $x^3+y^3=(x+y)^3-3xy(x+y)$. This yields $125-3\cdot6\cdot5=35$. The value $125$ includes the cross terms that must be subtracted. Alternatively, the auxiliary quadratic gives $2$ and $3$. Their cubes are $8$ and $27$, with sum $35$ regardless of the order in which the roots are assigned to $x$ and $y$.
