A square has area $A$ and perimeter $4\sqrt A$. If its positive perimeter doubles, by what factor does its area change?

- $\sqrt2$
- $8$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

To double $4\sqrt A$, its side $\sqrt A$ must double. Squaring then gives four times the original area.
