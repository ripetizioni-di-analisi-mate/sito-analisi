For $x^3-9x=0$, someone proposes dividing both sides by $x$ immediately. Which solution would be lost and why?

- None, because the right side is zero
- $0$, because division excludes it
- $-3$, because a divisor is always positive
- $3$, because its square is $9$

## Explanation

The value $0$ satisfies the original equation. Factoring as $x(x^2-9)=0$ keeps the case $x=0$ alongside the two solutions $x=\pm3$.
