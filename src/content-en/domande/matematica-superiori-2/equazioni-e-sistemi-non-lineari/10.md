How many **distinct** real roots does $x^2(x-1)(x+2)=0$ have?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

The factor $x^2$ gives only the value $0$, repeated twice in the factorisation. The other factors vanish at $1$ and $-2$. This gives three distinct values. Here degree four is the sum of the multiplicities, $2+1+1=4$. The question counts the coordinates where the polynomial vanishes, so zero is counted only once.
