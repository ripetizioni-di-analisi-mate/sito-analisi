Which value satisfies $\sqrt[3]{x+3}=-2$ over the reals?

- $1$
- $5$
- $-5$
- $-11$

## Explanation

A real cube root can be negative. Cubing is reversible over the reals and gives $x+3=(-2)^3=-8$, so $x=-11$. Substitution returns $\sqrt[3]{-8}=-2$. The minus sign remains because exponent three is odd. Applying square-root restrictions here would wrongly exclude both the negative radicand and the negative right side.
