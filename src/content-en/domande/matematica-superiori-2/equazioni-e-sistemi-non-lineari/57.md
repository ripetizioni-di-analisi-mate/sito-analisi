Which value satisfies $\sqrt{3x+7}=4$?

- $\dfrac{23}{3}$
- $-1$
- $-3$
- $3$

## Explanation

The right side is positive. Squaring gives a linear equation, and the solution makes the radicand equal to $16$. The result is $3$.
