How many real solutions does $\bigl||x|-2\bigr|=1$ have?

- None.
- One.
- Two.
- Four.

## Explanation

Setting $t=|x|$ gives $|t-2|=1$, so $t=1$ or $t=3$. For each positive value of $t$ there are two opposite values of $x$, giving four in total.
