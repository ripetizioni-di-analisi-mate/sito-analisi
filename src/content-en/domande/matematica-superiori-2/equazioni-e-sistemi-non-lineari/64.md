How many solutions does $\sqrt{x+2}=-|x-1|$ have?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- Infinitely many

## Explanation

On the left the radical is nonnegative, and on the right the negative absolute value is nonpositive. Equality therefore requires both sides to vanish. The radical is zero only at $x=-2$, while the absolute value is zero only at $x=1$. There is no common value. The incompatibility can be seen before squaring, avoiding candidates that would make only the magnitudes equal.
