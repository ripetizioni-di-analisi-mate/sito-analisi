Which system describes the common points of the circles $x^2+y^2=4$ and $(x-2)^2+y^2=4$ after subtracting their equations?

- $x=1,\ x^2+y^2=4$
- $x^2+y^2=0,\ x=1$
- $x=2,\ y^2=4$
- $y=1,\ x^2+y^2=4$

## Explanation

Subtracting cancels $x^2$ and $y^2$, leaving $-4x+4=0$. Keeping either original equation enforces membership of the circles. The resulting points are $(1,\pm\sqrt3)$.
