Collect like terms in $9\sqrt2-\sqrt8$.

- $1 + 7 \sqrt{2}$
- $-1 + 7 \sqrt{2}$
- $7 \sqrt{2}$
- $14 \sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

First simplify the square root of eight. The resulting radicals have the same index and radicand, so subtract their coefficients. The factor of the square root of two stays the same.

$$
\sqrt8=2\sqrt2
$$

$$
9\sqrt2-2\sqrt2=7\sqrt2
$$
