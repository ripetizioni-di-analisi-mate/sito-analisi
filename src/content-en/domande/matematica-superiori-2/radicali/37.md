A square of side $\sqrt A$ is enlarged so that its side grows by $1$ and its area by $7$. What was its initial area $A$?

- $3$
- $9$
- $6$
- $36$

## Explanation

The area increases by $(\sqrt A+1)^2-A=2\sqrt A+1$. Setting this equal to $7$ gives $\sqrt A=3$, and hence $A=9$.
