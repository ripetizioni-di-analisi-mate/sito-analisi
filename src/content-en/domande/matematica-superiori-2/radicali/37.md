How do you rationalise $\dfrac1{2+\sqrt3}$?

- $2+\sqrt3$
- $2-\sqrt3$
- $\sqrt3-2$
- $\dfrac{2-\sqrt3}{7}$

## Explanation

The product of the denominator and $2-\sqrt3$ is $2^2-(\sqrt3)^2=4-3=1$. The reciprocal of $2+\sqrt3$ is therefore $2-\sqrt3$.
