A square has side $\sqrt A$. If the side increases by $1$, the area increases by $7$. What is the initial area $A$?

- $3$
- $9$
- $6$
- $36$

## Explanation

The enlarged square has side $\sqrt A+1$, so its area is $(\sqrt A+1)^2=A+2\sqrt A+1$, because $(\sqrt A)^2=A$. The increase in area is therefore $2\sqrt A+1$. Since the increase is $7$, we have $2\sqrt A+1=7$ and hence $\sqrt A=3$. So the initial area is $A=3^2=9$. The value $3$ is the length of the initial side.
