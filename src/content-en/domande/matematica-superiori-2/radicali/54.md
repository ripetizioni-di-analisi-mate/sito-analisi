What is the value of $11\sqrt2-\sqrt{50}$?

- $6 \sqrt{2}$
- $12$
- $6$
- $16 \sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The second radical is $5\sqrt2$ because $5$ is positive. Subtract the coefficients of the like radicals to obtain $6 \sqrt{2}$.
