Which of the following intervals contains $\sqrt7+\sqrt2$?

- $(4,5)$
- $(3,4)$
- $(5,6)$
- $(2,3)$

## Explanation

Since $\sqrt7<3$ and $\sqrt2<2$, the sum is less than $5$. Its square is $9+2\sqrt{14}$. Now $(2\sqrt{14})^2=56>49=7^2$, and both values being compared are positive, so $2\sqrt{14}>7$. The square of the sum is greater than $16$. As the sum is positive, it is greater than $4$.
