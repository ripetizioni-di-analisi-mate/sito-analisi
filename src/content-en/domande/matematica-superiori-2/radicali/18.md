Which of the following numbers is irrational?

- $\sqrt{4}$
- $2+\sqrt{9}$
- $\sqrt{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

The number $5$ lies between the consecutive perfect squares $4$ and $9$, so it is not a perfect square and $\sqrt5$ is irrational. The other numbers are rational, because $\sqrt4=2$ and $2+\sqrt9=5$.
