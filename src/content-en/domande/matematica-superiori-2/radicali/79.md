For which values of $x$ does $\sqrt[6]{x^2}$ equal $\sqrt[3]x$?

- $(0,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-\infty,0]$
- $[0,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The sixth root is $\sqrt[3]{|x|}$ because that quantity is nonnegative and its sixth power is $|x|^2=x^2$. For $x\ge0$, we have $|x|=x$, so the two roots agree. For $x<0$, the first root is positive and the second is negative. Substituting $x=-8$, for instance, gives $2$ and $-2$. At $x=0$ both radicands are zero, so the endpoint is included.
