What is the value of $\sqrt{(x-5)^2}$ when $x<5$?

- $x-5$
- $-x-5$
- $x+5$
- $5-x$

## Explanation

The square root of the square is the absolute value. On this interval, $x-5$ is negative, so take its opposite.
