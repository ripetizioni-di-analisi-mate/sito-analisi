What is the real domain of $\dfrac1{\sqrt{x-4}}$?

- $[4,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,4]$
- $(4,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$

## Explanation

The square root requires a nonnegative radicand, and the denominator cannot be zero. The two conditions together give $x-4>0$. Therefore, $x>4$.
