Which positive integer $k$ makes the fraction $\dfrac{k}{5}$ lie strictly between $\sqrt5$ and $\sqrt6$?

- $11$
- $13$
- $12$
- No positive integer

## Explanation

The condition is $\sqrt5<\dfrac k5<\sqrt6$, with all terms positive. Squaring preserves the order of positive numbers, so the condition is equivalent to $5<\dfrac{k^2}{25}<6$. Multiplying by $25$ gives $125<k^2<150$. Since $11^2=121$, $12^2=144$ and $13^2=169$, the only positive integer satisfying the condition is $k=12$.
