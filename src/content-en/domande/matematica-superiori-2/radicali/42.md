We seek a fraction $\dfrac{k}{5}$ with positive integer $k$ that lies strictly between $\sqrt5$ and $\sqrt6$. Which numerator works without rounding the roots?

- $11$
- $13$
- $12$
- $25$

## Explanation

All the terms are positive, so squaring and multiplying by $25$ gives $125<k^2<150$. The only integer square in this interval is $144=12^2$, between $11^2=121$ and $13^2=169$.
