Let $u=\sqrt5-\sqrt4$ and $v=\sqrt{50}-\sqrt{49}$. Which relation is true?

- $u<v$
- $u=v$
- $u>v$
- $u<0$

## Explanation

Both differences are positive. Multiplying each by its corresponding sum gives one, because the radicands differ by one in each case. Thus $u=\dfrac1{\sqrt5+\sqrt4}$ and $v=\dfrac1{\sqrt{50}+\sqrt{49}}$. The denominator of $v$ is greater, since both its roots exceed their counterparts in the denominator of $u$. Therefore $u>v$.
