Rationalize $\dfrac1{\sqrt{5}+\sqrt{4}}$.

- $2 + \sqrt{5}$
- $2 - \sqrt{5}$
- $-2 + \sqrt{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

Multiply the numerator and denominator by the conjugate. The denominator becomes the difference of $5$ and $4$, which is one. The result is $-2 + \sqrt{5}$.
