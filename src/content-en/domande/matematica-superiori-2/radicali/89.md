Which relation holds between $a^{\frac13}$ and $a^{\frac23}$ for every $0<a<1$?

- $a^{\frac13}<a^{\frac23}$
- $a^{\frac13}=a^{\frac23}$
- $a^{\frac23}<0$
- $a^{\frac13}>a^{\frac23}$

## Explanation

Let $t=\sqrt[3]a$. Since $0<a<1$, the value of $t$ is also between zero and one. The first power is $t$ and the second is $t^2$. Multiplying the positive number $t$ by itself makes it smaller, so $t^2<t$. For example, $a=\dfrac18$ gives $\dfrac12$ and $\dfrac14$, in that order. The interval for $t$ also follows by comparing the cubes of the endpoints, which are still zero and one.
