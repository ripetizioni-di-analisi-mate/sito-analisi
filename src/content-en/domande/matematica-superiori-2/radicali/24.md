Which of the following numbers is irrational for $u=6$?

- $\sqrt{37}$
- $\dfrac{1}{13}$
- $\sqrt{36}$
- $6+\sqrt{49}$

## Explanation

The square of $u$ is a perfect square, and the next integer lies between consecutive squares. Its square root cannot be rational.
