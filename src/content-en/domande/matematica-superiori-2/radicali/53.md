A rectangle has sides of $3$ cm and $6$ cm. What is the exact length of its diagonal in centimetres?

- $9$
- $3\sqrt5$
- $3\sqrt3$
- $3\sqrt7$

## Explanation

The diagonal is the hypotenuse of the right triangle formed by the two sides. Its length squared is $3^2+6^2=9+36=45$. Take the nonnegative root because the answer is a length. Since $45=9\cdot5$, taking out the factor $3$ gives $3\sqrt5$. Its square gives $45$, as required by the relation between the three lengths.
