What is the value of $9\sqrt2-\sqrt{32}$?

- $5$
- $5 \sqrt{2}$
- $10$
- $13 \sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The second radical is $4\sqrt2$ because $4$ is positive. Subtract the coefficients of the like radicals to obtain $5 \sqrt{2}$.
