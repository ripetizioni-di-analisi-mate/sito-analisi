Which expression with a rational denominator equals $\dfrac{7}{\sqrt{3}}$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{7}$
- $\dfrac{7 \sqrt{3}}{3}$
- $7 \sqrt{3}$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$

## Explanation

Multiply the numerator and denominator by $\sqrt{3}$. The denominator becomes $3$, and the result is $\dfrac{7 \sqrt{3}}{3}$.
