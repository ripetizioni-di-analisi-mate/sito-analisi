What is the simplified form of $\sqrt{192}$?

- $4 \sqrt{3}$
- $16 \sqrt{3}$
- $8 \sqrt{6}$
- $8 \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Write the radicand as the product of $64$ and $3$. Take the positive square root of the square factor, giving $8 \sqrt{3}$.
