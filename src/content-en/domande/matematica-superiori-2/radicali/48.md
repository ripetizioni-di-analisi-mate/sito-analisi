Which input allowed by $\sqrt{x^2(x-1)}$ is excluded from the form $|x|\sqrt{x-1}$?

- $1$
- $-1$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

In the original radicand, the squared factor is zero at $x=0$. The product is zero even though the other factor is $-1$, so the original root exists. In the rewritten form, $\sqrt{x-1}$ has to be evaluated first, and it is not real at zero. For $x\ge1$, both forms exist and give the same value.
