What is the value of $5\sqrt2-\sqrt{8}$?

- $3$
- $6$
- $7 \sqrt{2}$
- $3 \sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The second radical is $2\sqrt2$ because $2$ is positive. Subtract the coefficients of the like radicals to obtain $3 \sqrt{2}$.
