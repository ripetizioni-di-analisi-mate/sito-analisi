For $a\ge0$, what is the value of $\sqrt{9a}+\sqrt{4a}-\sqrt a$?

- $6\sqrt a$
- $\sqrt{12a}$
- $4a$
- $4\sqrt a$

## Explanation

For $a\ge0$ all the radicands are non-negative, so the product rule applies and gives $\sqrt{9a}=\sqrt9\cdot\sqrt a=3\sqrt a$ and $\sqrt{4a}=\sqrt4\cdot\sqrt a=2\sqrt a$. The three terms are then like radicals with radicand $a$, therefore their coefficients are added. This gives $(3+2-1)\sqrt a=4\sqrt a$. The value $\sqrt{12a}$ comes instead from adding the radicands under a single root.
