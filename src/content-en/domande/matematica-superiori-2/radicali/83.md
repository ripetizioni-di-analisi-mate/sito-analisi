Which equation describes the graph of $y=\sqrt[3]{(x-2)^3}$ over all of $\mathbb R$?

- $y=x-2$
- $y=2-x$
- $y=|x-2|$
- $y=x+2$

## Explanation

Cubing and taking a cube root are inverse operations over the reals. Setting $u=x-2$, the cube root of $u^3$ gives $u$ even when $u$ is negative. The graph is the line $y=x-2$, which passes through $(2,0)$. At $x=0$, for example, the radicand is $-8$ and the output is $-2$, while $|x-2|$ would give $2$. At $x=3$ the output is $1$, consistent with the positive slope.
