Which expression is a sum of two irrational terms with a rational result?

- $\sqrt2+(5-\sqrt2)$
- $\sqrt2+\sqrt2$
- $\sqrt2+5$
- $\sqrt9+\sqrt{16}$

## Explanation

The sum $\sqrt2+(5-\sqrt2)$ is $5$. The first term is irrational. The term $5-\sqrt2$ is irrational as well, because subtracting it from $5$ gives $\sqrt2$. If the second term were rational, that subtraction would give a rational number. The sum $\sqrt9+\sqrt{16}=7$ is rational, and so are its two terms, $3$ and $4$.
