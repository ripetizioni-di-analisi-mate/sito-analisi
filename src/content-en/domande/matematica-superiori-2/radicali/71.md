What is the real domain of $f(x)=\sqrt{5-2x}$?

- $(-\infty,\dfrac{2}{5}]$
- $(-\infty,\dfrac{5}{2})$
- $(-\infty,\dfrac{5}{2}]$
- $[\dfrac{5}{2},\infty)$

## Explanation

The radicand must be at least zero. Move the term in $x$ to the right side and divide by $2>0$.
