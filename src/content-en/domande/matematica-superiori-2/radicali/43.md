For which integers $n$ does $\sqrt[3]{n^3+1}=n+1$ hold?

- Only $n=0$
- Every integer
- No integer
- $n=-1$ or $n=0$

## Explanation

Cubing preserves equivalence for both signs. Subtracting $n^3+1$ from the expansion of $(n+1)^3$ leaves $3n(n+1)=0$.
