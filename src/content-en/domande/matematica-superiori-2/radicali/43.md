What is the value of $\sqrt[3]{-64}$?

- $4$
- $-8$
- $\text{No real value}$
- $-4$

## Explanation

An odd index allows negative radicands. Since $(-4)^3=-64$, the requested value is $-4$. Taking the cube root preserves the sign.
