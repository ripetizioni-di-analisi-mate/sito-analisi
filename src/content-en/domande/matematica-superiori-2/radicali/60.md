Calculate $\dfrac{\sqrt{507}}{\sqrt3}$.

- $13$
- $169$
- $\dfrac{13}{3}$
- $39$

## Explanation

The roots are positive, so their quotient is the square root of the quotient of the radicands. This gives the square root of $169$, which is $13$.
