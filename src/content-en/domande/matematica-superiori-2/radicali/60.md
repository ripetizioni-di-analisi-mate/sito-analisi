In the quotient $\dfrac{\sqrt{-x}}{\sqrt{x+2}}$, which values of $x$ meet both restrictions?

- $(-2,0]$
- $[-2,0]$
- $(-2,0)$
- $[0,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The numerator gives $-x\ge0$, or $x\le0$. The denominator gives $x+2>0$, or $x>-2$. The intersection is $(-2,0]$. At $0$ the numerator vanishes and the denominator is $\sqrt2$, so that endpoint is included. At $-2$ the denominator is zero, so the other endpoint is excluded. At an interior input such as $x=-1$, both roots equal one.
