What is the value of $\sqrt[3]{x^3}-\sqrt{x^2}$ if $x=-4$?

- $-8$
- $8$
- $0$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The first root is $x=-4$ because its index is odd. The second is $|x|=4$. Their difference is therefore $-4-4=-8$.
