What is the domain of $\dfrac1{\sqrt{(x+1)(x-2)}}$?

- $(-\infty,-1]\cup[2,+\infty)$
- $(-1,2)$
- $(-\infty,-1)\cup(2,+\infty)$
- $(2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The product under the root must be strictly positive, because the root is the entire denominator. Both factors are negative to the left of $-1$ and both are positive to the right of $2$. Between the zeros the factors have opposite signs. At $-1$ and $2$ the product is zero, so both endpoints are excluded.
