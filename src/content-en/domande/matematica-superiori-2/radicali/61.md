A real number $r$ satisfies $1.4<r<1.5$. Which of the following numbers can be the value of $r^2$?

- $2.9$
- $2$
- $2.25$
- $1.45$

## Explanation

The numbers $1.4$, $r$ and $1.5$ are positive, and squaring preserves the order of positive numbers. Therefore $1.4^2<r^2<1.5^2$, that is $1.96<r^2<2.25$. Among the values offered only $2$ lies in this interval, and indeed $r=\sqrt2$ lies between $1.4$ and $1.5$. The value $2.25$ is excluded because the inequality $r<1.5$ is strict, while $2.9$ comes from doubling $r$ instead of squaring it.
