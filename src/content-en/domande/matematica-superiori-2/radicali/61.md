Which expression with a rational denominator equals $\dfrac{5}{\sqrt{2}}$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5 \sqrt{2}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5 \sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

Multiply the numerator and denominator by $\sqrt{2}$. The denominator becomes $2$, and the result is $\dfrac{5 \sqrt{2}}{2}$.
