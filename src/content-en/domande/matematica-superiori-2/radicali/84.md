Which radical remains after simplifying $\sqrt[3]{54}+\sqrt[3]{-16}$?

- $\sqrt[3]2$
- $5\sqrt[3]2$
- $-\sqrt[3]2$
- $\sqrt[3]{38}$

## Explanation

The radicand $54$ is $3^3\cdot2$, so its cube root is $3\sqrt[3]2$. The radicand $-16$ is $(-2)^3\cdot2$, so its cube root is $-2\sqrt[3]2$. The radicals are now like terms. Adding their coefficients gives $(3-2)\sqrt[3]2=\sqrt[3]2$. The cube root of $38$ would come from combining the radicands in a sum.
