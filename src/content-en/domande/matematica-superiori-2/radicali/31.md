What is the set of all inputs at which $\sqrt{(x-4)^2}=2$?

- $\{2\}$
- $\{6\}$
- $[2,6]$
- $\{2,6\}$

## Explanation

The radical is $|x-4|$, the distance from $x$ to $4$. That distance is two both two units to the left of $4$ and two units to its right. The inputs are therefore $2$ and $6$. At every input inside $(2,6)$ the distance is strictly less than two, while outside it is greater.
