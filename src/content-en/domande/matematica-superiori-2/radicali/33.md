What is the domain of $f(x)=\dfrac1{\sqrt{x+4}}$?

- $(-\infty,-4)$
- $(-4,\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-4]$
- $[-4,\infty)$

## Explanation

The radicand must be positive because its square root is in the denominator. The value $-4$ is excluded because it makes the denominator zero.
