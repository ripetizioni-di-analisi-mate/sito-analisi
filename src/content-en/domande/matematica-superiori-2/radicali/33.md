What is the domain of $\dfrac1{\sqrt{x+2}-1}$, whose denominator is a difference?

- $(-2,+\infty)\setminus\{-1\}$
- $[-2,+\infty)\setminus\{-1\}$
- $[-2,+\infty)$
- $(-1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The root requires $x+2\ge0$, so start with $x\ge-2$. The denominator is zero when $\sqrt{x+2}=1$, which means $x+2=1$ and $x=-1$. That input is excluded. At $x=-2$, the root is zero, but the difference in the denominator is $-1$. That endpoint is allowed and the quotient is defined.
