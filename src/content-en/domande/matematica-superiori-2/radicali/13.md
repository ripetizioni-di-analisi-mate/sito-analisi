Rationalize $\dfrac1{\sqrt{9}-\sqrt{8}}$.

- $3 - 2 \sqrt{2}$
- $2 \sqrt{2} + 3$
- $-3 - 2 \sqrt{2}$
- $\sqrt{17}$

## Explanation

The denominator is a difference of radicals. Multiply by its conjugate and use the product of a sum and a difference. The radicands differ by one, so the denominator simplifies.

$$
\frac{\sqrt{9}+\sqrt{8}}{(9)-8}=\sqrt{9}+\sqrt{8}
$$
