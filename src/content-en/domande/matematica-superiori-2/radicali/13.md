What is the product of $2\sqrt2-\sqrt5$ and its conjugate?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $-1$
- $13$

## Explanation

The conjugate is $2\sqrt2+\sqrt5$. The product gives the difference between the squares of the two terms. The first square includes the coefficient $2$, so $(2\sqrt2)^2=4\cdot2=8$. The second square is $5$. The difference is $8-5=3$. It is positive because $2\sqrt2$ is greater than $\sqrt5$, as their squares confirm.
