When is the reciprocal $\dfrac1{\sqrt[3]{x-2}}$ strictly negative?

- $x<2$
- $x>2$
- $x\le2$
- $x<0$

## Explanation

The cube root has the same sign as its radicand. For $x<2$, the radicand is negative, so both the denominator and the reciprocal are negative. Division is impossible at $x=2$. For $x>2$, the denominator and the reciprocal are both positive. For example, at $x=1$ the denominator is $-1$, so the fraction equals $-1$.
