What is the domain of $\dfrac{\sqrt{x-2}}{\sqrt{6-x}}$?

- $(2,6)$
- $[2,6]$
- $[2,6)$
- $(2,6]$

## Explanation

The numerator requires $x-2\ge0$, and the denominator requires $6-x>0$. The two conditions hold when $x\ge2$ and $x<6$, so the domain is $[2,6)$.
