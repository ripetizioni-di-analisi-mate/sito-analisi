Which ordered pair gives the values of $\dfrac{\sqrt{x^2}}x$, first for $x<0$ and then for $x>0$?

- $(1;-1)$
- $(1;1)$
- $(-1;-1)$
- $(-1;1)$

## Explanation

The numerator is $|x|$ and the denominator excludes zero. For $x<0$, we have $|x|=-x$, so the ratio is $-1$. For $x>0$, we have $|x|=x$ and the ratio is $1$. For example, $x=-2$ gives $\dfrac2{-2}=-1$. The negative sign comes from the denominator, while the numerator is always nonnegative. At the opposite input $x=2$, the numerator remains $2$ and the ratio becomes $1$.
