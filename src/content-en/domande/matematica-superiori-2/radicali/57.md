Calculate $\dfrac{\sqrt{147}}{\sqrt3}$.

- $21$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $49$
- $7$

## Explanation

The roots are positive, so their quotient is the square root of the quotient of the radicands. This gives the square root of $49$, which is $7$.
