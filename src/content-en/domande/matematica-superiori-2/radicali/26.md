Which expression results from collecting like terms in $\sqrt{27}-2\sqrt{12}+\sqrt{75}$?

- $6\sqrt3$
- $8\sqrt3$
- $4\sqrt3$
- $4\sqrt2$

## Explanation

The radicals become $3\sqrt3$, $2\sqrt3$, and $5\sqrt3$. The second is multiplied by $-2$, giving $(3-4+5)\sqrt3=4\sqrt3$.
