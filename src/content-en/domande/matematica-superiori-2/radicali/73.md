What is the domain of $\sqrt{\dfrac{(x+2)^2}{x-1}}$, taking account of the zero of the numerator?

- $(1,+\infty)$
- $[-2,1)$
- $\{-2\}\cup(1,+\infty)$
- $\{-2\}\cup[1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

For $x>1$ the denominator is positive and the numerator is nonnegative. For $x<1$ the denominator is negative, so the quotient is negative except at $x=-2$. There the numerator is zero, the denominator is $-3$ and the root is zero. The domain includes that isolated point and the interval to the right of $1$, which is excluded.
