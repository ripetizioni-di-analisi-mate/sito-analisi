What is the real domain of $f(x)=\sqrt{9-4x}$?

- $(-\infty,\dfrac{9}{4})$
- $(-\infty,\dfrac{4}{9}]$
- $(-\infty,\dfrac{9}{4}]$
- $[\dfrac{9}{4},\infty)$

## Explanation

The radicand must be at least zero. Move the term in $x$ to the right side and divide by $4>0$.
