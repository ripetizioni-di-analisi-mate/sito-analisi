What is the value of $\sqrt{(x-3)^2}$ when $x<3$?

- $x-3$
- $-x-3$
- $3-x$
- $x+3$

## Explanation

The square root of the square is the absolute value. On this interval, $x-3$ is negative, so take its opposite.
