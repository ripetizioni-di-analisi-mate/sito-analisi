Which form with a single square root represents the number $-2\sqrt3$?

- $\sqrt{12}$
- $-\sqrt6$
- $-\sqrt{12}$
- $\sqrt{-12}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of $-2\sqrt3$ is $2\sqrt3$. Squaring it gives $4\cdot3=12$, so its magnitude is $\sqrt{12}$. The original number is negative and equals the opposite of that root. The minus sign stays outside because a real square root is always nonnegative, and the radicand $-12$ is not allowed.
