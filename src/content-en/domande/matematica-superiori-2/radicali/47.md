What is the simplified form of $\sqrt{108}$?

- $3 \sqrt{3}$
- $12 \sqrt{3}$
- $6 \sqrt{3}$
- $6 \sqrt{6}$

## Explanation

Write the radicand as the product of $36$ and $3$. Take the positive square root of the square factor, giving $6 \sqrt{3}$.
