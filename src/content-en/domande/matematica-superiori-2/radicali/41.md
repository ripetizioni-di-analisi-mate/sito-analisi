Which identity holds for every $x\in\mathbb R$?

- $\sqrt{x^2}=x$
- $\sqrt{x^2}=|x|$
- $\sqrt{x^2}=-x$
- $\sqrt{x^2}=x^2$

## Explanation

The principal root is nonnegative. The absolute value equals $x$ when $x$ is nonnegative and $-x$ when it is negative. The identity covers both cases.
