What is the simplified form of $\sqrt{300}$?

- $20 \sqrt{3}$
- $5 \sqrt{3}$
- $10 \sqrt{3}$
- $10 \sqrt{6}$

## Explanation

Write the radicand as the product of $100$ and $3$. Take the positive square root of the square factor, giving $10 \sqrt{3}$.
