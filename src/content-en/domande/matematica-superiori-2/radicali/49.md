For which real values of $x$ are $\sqrt{x^2}$ and $(\sqrt x)^2$ both defined and equal?

- $\mathbb R$
- $(0,+\infty)$
- $[0,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,0]$

## Explanation

In the first expression, squaring comes first, so the radicand is nonnegative for every real input. The result is $|x|$. In the second, the root $\sqrt x$ comes first and requires $x\ge0$. Squaring that root gives $x$. On that whole interval, $|x|=x$, including $x=0$, where both expressions give zero.
