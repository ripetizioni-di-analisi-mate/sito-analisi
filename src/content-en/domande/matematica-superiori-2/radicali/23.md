Which quotient of two irrational roots has a rational value?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{18}}{\sqrt3}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{20}}{\sqrt2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{12}}{\sqrt6}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{18}}{\sqrt8}$

## Explanation

The quotient $\dfrac{\sqrt{18}}{\sqrt8}$ becomes $\dfrac{3\sqrt2}{2\sqrt2}$. The factor $\sqrt2$ is nonzero and cancels, so the value is $\dfrac32$. The other quotients simplify to $\sqrt6$, $\sqrt{10}$ and $\sqrt2$. Those radicands are integers that are not perfect squares, so their square roots are irrational. Multiplying $\dfrac32$ by $\sqrt8$ also recovers the numerator $3\sqrt2$.
