What is the value of $7\sqrt2-\sqrt{18}$?

- $4$
- $8$
- $10 \sqrt{2}$
- $4 \sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The second radical is $3\sqrt2$ because $3$ is positive. Subtract the coefficients of the like radicals to obtain $4 \sqrt{2}$.
