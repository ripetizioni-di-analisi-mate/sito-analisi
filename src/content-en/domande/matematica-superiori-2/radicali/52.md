What is the value of $(\sqrt6+\sqrt2)^2+(\sqrt6-\sqrt2)^2$?

- $8$
- $16+4\sqrt{12}$
- $16-4\sqrt{12}$
- $16$

## Explanation

Each square contains the sum $6+2=8$ of the squares of the two terms. The square of the sum contributes the mixed term $2\sqrt{12}$, and the square of the difference contributes its opposite. These two mixed terms cancel in the sum, leaving two copies of $8$. The total is therefore $16$.
