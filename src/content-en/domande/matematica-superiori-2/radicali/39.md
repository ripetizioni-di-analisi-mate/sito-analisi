Which values of $x$ make both roots in $\sqrt{2-\sqrt x}$ real?

- $[0,2]$
- $(0,4)$
- $(-\infty,4]$
- $[0,4]$

## Explanation

The inner root requires $x\ge0$. The outer root also requires $2-\sqrt x\ge0$. On the first domain, the root and the number $2$ are nonnegative, so $\sqrt x\le2$ is equivalent to $x\le4$. This gives $[0,4]$. The inner root can be zero at the left endpoint, and the outer root can be zero at the right endpoint.
