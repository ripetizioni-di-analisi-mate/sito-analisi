What is the domain of $f(x)=\dfrac1{\sqrt{x+7}}$?

- $[-7,\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-7)$
- $(-\infty,-7]$
- $(-7,\infty)$

## Explanation

The radicand must be positive because its square root is in the denominator. The value $-7$ is excluded because it makes the denominator zero.
