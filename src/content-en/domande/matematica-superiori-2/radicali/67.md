Rationalize $\dfrac1{\sqrt{4}+\sqrt{3}}$.

- $2 - \sqrt{3}$
- $-2 + \sqrt{3}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{7}}{7}$
- $\sqrt{3} + 2$

## Explanation

Multiply the numerator and denominator by the conjugate. The denominator becomes the difference of $4$ and $3$, which is one. The result is $2 - \sqrt{3}$.
