Which fraction equals $\sqrt{x+1}-\sqrt x$ for every $x\ge0$?

- $\dfrac1{\sqrt{x+1}+\sqrt x}$
- $\dfrac1{\sqrt{x+1}-\sqrt x}$
- $\dfrac1{\sqrt{x+1}}$
- $\dfrac1{\sqrt{2x+1}}$

## Explanation

The product of the difference and its conjugate sum is $(x+1)-x=1$. The sum $\sqrt{x+1}+\sqrt x$ is positive for every $x\ge0$, so it can be divided out. The difference is exactly the reciprocal of the sum. At $x=0$ the sum equals one and the original difference also equals one.
