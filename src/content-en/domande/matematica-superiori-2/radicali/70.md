What is the value of $(\sqrt5-\sqrt2)^2$?

- $3$
- $7-\sqrt{10}$
- $7+2\sqrt{10}$
- $7-2\sqrt{10}$

## Explanation

Squaring the difference gives the squares $5$ and $2$ and subtracts twice the product. Since $\sqrt5\sqrt2=\sqrt{10}$, the result is $7-2\sqrt{10}$.
