Which expression with a rational denominator equals $\dfrac{9}{\sqrt{4}}$?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $18$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$

## Explanation

Multiply the numerator and denominator by $\sqrt{4}$. The denominator becomes $4$, and the result is $\dfrac{9}{2}$.
