Which fraction with a rational denominator equals $\dfrac1{\sqrt[3]{-2}}$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt[3]4}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt[3]4}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt[3]2}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt[3]4}{4}$

## Explanation

Multiplying numerator and denominator by $\sqrt[3]4$ gives $\sqrt[3]{(-2)\cdot4}=\sqrt[3]{-8}=-2$ in the denominator. The factor is positive and nonzero, so the quotient keeps its value. The minus sign can be placed in front of the fraction. A positive answer would have the wrong sign for the reciprocal of the cube root of $-2$.
