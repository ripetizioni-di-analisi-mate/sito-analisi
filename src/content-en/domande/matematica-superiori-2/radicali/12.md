Rationalize $\dfrac{7}{\sqrt3}$.

- $\dfrac{7 \sqrt{3}}{3}$
- $1 + \dfrac{7 \sqrt{3}}{3}$
- $-1 + \dfrac{7 \sqrt{3}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{14 \sqrt{3}}{3}$

## Explanation

Multiply the numerator and denominator by the radical in the denominator. The factor introduced equals one, so the value of the fraction stays the same. The new denominator is rational.

$$
\frac{7}{\sqrt3}\cdot\frac{\sqrt3}{\sqrt3}=\frac{7\sqrt3}3
$$
