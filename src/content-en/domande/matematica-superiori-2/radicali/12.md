What factor $\sqrt[4]{2^j}$, with the smallest positive integer $j$, makes the product with $\sqrt[4]8$ rational?

- $\sqrt[4]2$
- $\sqrt[4]4$
- $\sqrt[4]8$
- $\sqrt[4]{16}$

## Explanation

The radicand $8$ contains three factors of $2$. Multiplying the fourth roots adds $j$ to the exponent three. For the result to be rational, the exponent of $2$ must become a multiple of four. The smallest such exponent is four, obtained at $j=1$. The product of the roots is then $\sqrt[4]{16}=2$.
