What is the sum of the powers $4^{-\frac12}+4^{\frac12}$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac32$
- $4$
- $\dfrac52$

## Explanation

The power $4^{\frac12}$ is the square root of $4$, namely $2$. The negative exponent takes the reciprocal of that power, so $4^{-\frac12}=\dfrac12$. Adding $2$ and $\dfrac12$ gives $\dfrac52$. The opposite exponents would give $1$ in the product, because exponents add in a product. The expression here adds the two values themselves.
