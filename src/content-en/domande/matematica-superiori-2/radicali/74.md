For which real values of the parameter $k$ is $x\mapsto\sqrt{x^2+k}$ defined for every real $x$?

- $k>0$
- $k\le0$
- $k\ge0$
- $k\in\mathbb R$

## Explanation

The minimum of $x^2+k$ is attained at $x=0$ and equals $k$, since $x^2$ is always nonnegative. If $k\ge0$, the radicand is nonnegative for every $x$. If $k<0$, it is already negative at zero. The case $k=0$ is included because the radical becomes $|x|$, which is zero at the origin.
