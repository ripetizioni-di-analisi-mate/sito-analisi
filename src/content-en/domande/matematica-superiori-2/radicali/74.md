What is the real domain of $f(x)=\sqrt{11-5x}$?

- $(-\infty,\dfrac{5}{11}]$
- $[\dfrac{11}{5},\infty)$
- $(-\infty,\dfrac{11}{5}]$
- $(-\infty,\dfrac{11}{5})$

## Explanation

The radicand must be at least zero. Move the term in $x$ to the right side and divide by $5>0$.
