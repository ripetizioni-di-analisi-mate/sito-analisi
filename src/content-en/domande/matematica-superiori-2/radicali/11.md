What is the common domain of $\sqrt{x-1}\sqrt{x+2}$ and $\sqrt{(x-1)(x+2)}$?

- $(-\infty,-2]\cup[1,+\infty)$
- $(-2,+\infty)$
- $(1,+\infty)$
- $[1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

In the first expression, the conditions $x-1\ge0$ and $x+2\ge0$ must both hold, giving $x\ge1$. That also makes the product in the second root nonnegative. For $x<-2$, the product of the radicands is positive, while the two separate roots are undefined over the reals. The endpoint $1$ is included in both domains.
