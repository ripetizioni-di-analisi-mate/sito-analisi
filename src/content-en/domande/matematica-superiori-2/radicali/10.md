Let $a,b\ge0$. What condition is necessary and sufficient for $\sqrt{a+b}=\sqrt a+\sqrt b$?

- Both must be zero.
- The two radicands must be equal.
- At least one of $a$ and $b$ is zero.
- Both must be positive.

## Explanation

Both sides are nonnegative, so their squares can be compared. The left square is $a+b$, while the right square is $a+b+2\sqrt a\sqrt b$. They are equal exactly when the product of the roots is zero. It is enough for one of the roots to vanish. For example, at $a=0$ and $b=9$, both sides equal $3$.
