What is the domain of $f(x)=\dfrac1{\sqrt{x+3}}$?

- $(-\infty,-3]$
- $[-3,\infty)$
- $(-3,\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-3)$

## Explanation

The radicand must be positive because its square root is in the denominator. The value $-3$ is excluded because it makes the denominator zero.
