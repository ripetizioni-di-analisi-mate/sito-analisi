Rationalize $\dfrac1{\sqrt{6}+\sqrt{5}}$.

- $- \sqrt{6} + \sqrt{5}$
- $- \sqrt{5} + \sqrt{6}$
- $\sqrt{5} + \sqrt{6}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{11}}{11}$

## Explanation

Multiply the numerator and denominator by the conjugate. The denominator becomes the difference of $6$ and $5$, which is one. The result is $- \sqrt{5} + \sqrt{6}$.
