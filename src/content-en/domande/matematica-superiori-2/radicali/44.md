What is the value of $\sqrt9+\sqrt{16}$?

- $7$
- $5$
- $25$
- $\sqrt7$

## Explanation

The principal roots are three and four. Calculate them separately and then add to get seven. The square root of the sum of the radicands would give a different expression.
