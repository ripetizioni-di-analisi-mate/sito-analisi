For which values of $x$ does $\sqrt{x-1}\sqrt{3-x}=\sqrt{(x-1)(3-x)}$ hold as an equality of real radicals?

- $\mathbb R$
- $(1,3)$
- $[1,3]$
- $(-\infty,1]\cup[3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The product on the left requires both radicands to be nonnegative, so $x\ge1$ and $x\le3$. At the endpoints, one radicand is zero and the equality still holds.
