What is the value of the nested power $\left(8^{\frac23}\right)^{\frac12}$?

- $4$
- $16$
- $8$
- $2$

## Explanation

The original base is positive. The inner value is obtained by squaring the cube root of $8$, giving $2^2=4$. The outside power takes the square root of $4$, which is $2$. Multiplying the exponents gives the same result, since $\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac13$ and the cube root of $8$ is $2$.
