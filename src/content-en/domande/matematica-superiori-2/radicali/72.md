What is the domain of $\sqrt{\dfrac{(x-1)^2}{x+2}}$?

- $[-2,+\infty)$
- $(-2,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-2\}$
- $(-2,+\infty)\setminus\{1\}$

## Explanation

The denominator is positive for $x>-2$, so the quotient is nonnegative there. The numerator is zero at $x=1$, which is already in the domain and gives root zero. For $x<-2$, the numerator is positive and the denominator negative, so the root is not real. The input $-2$ is excluded because it makes the denominator zero.
