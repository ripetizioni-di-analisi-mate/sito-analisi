What is the real domain of $f(x)=\sqrt{7-3x}$?

- $(-\infty,\dfrac{7}{3})$
- $(-\infty,\dfrac{7}{3}]$
- $[\dfrac{7}{3},\infty)$
- $(-\infty,\dfrac{3}{7}]$

## Explanation

The radicand must be at least zero. Move the term in $x$ to the right side and divide by $3>0$.
