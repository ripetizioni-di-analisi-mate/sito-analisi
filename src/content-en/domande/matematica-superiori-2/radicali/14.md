For which real values is $\sqrt{x-8}$ defined?

- $(8,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,8]$
- $[8,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$

## Explanation

The index is even, so the radicand must be nonnegative. Equality is allowed because the square root of zero is defined. Solving the linear inequality gives the domain.

$$
x-8\ge0\iff x\ge8
$$
