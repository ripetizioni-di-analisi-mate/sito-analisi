Which real pair $(a,b)$ makes $\sqrt{ab}$ real while both separate roots $\sqrt a$ and $\sqrt b$ are nonreal?

- $(2,8)$
- $(0,4)$
- $(-2,8)$
- $(-2,-8)$

## Explanation

For neither separate root to be real, we need $a<0$ and $b<0$. Two negative numbers have a positive product, so the root of that product is real. With $a=-2$ and $b=-8$, the product is $16$ and its root is $4$. The rule for multiplying square roots requires both separate radicands to be nonnegative.
