What is the smallest positive integer $m$ for which the quotient $\dfrac{m\sqrt{12}}{\sqrt{75}}$ is an integer?

- $2$
- $5$
- $3$
- $15$

## Explanation

Taking out the square factors gives $\sqrt{12}=\sqrt{4\cdot3}=2\sqrt3$ and $\sqrt{75}=\sqrt{25\cdot3}=5\sqrt3$. Therefore the quotient equals $\dfrac{2m\sqrt3}{5\sqrt3}=\dfrac{2m}{5}$. Since $2$ and $5$ are coprime, $\dfrac{2m}{5}$ is an integer if and only if $m$ is a multiple of $5$. Hence the smallest value is $m=5$, for which the quotient equals $2$.
