Which number is irrational?

- $\sqrt{16}$
- $\sqrt3$
- $\dfrac25$
- $0.125$

## Explanation

The square root of three is not a ratio of two integers. The square root of sixteen is four, and the remaining numbers are a fraction and a terminating decimal. Only the square root of three is irrational.
