What is the smallest positive integer $m$ for which $m\sqrt{12}$ is an integer multiple of $\sqrt{75}$?

- $2$
- $5$
- $3$
- $15$

## Explanation

Since $\sqrt{12}=2\sqrt3$ and $\sqrt{75}=5\sqrt3$, their scaled ratio is $\dfrac{2m}{5}$. The first positive integer $m$ making it integral is $5$.
