What number is $27^{-\frac23}$ equal to?

- $9$
- $-\dfrac19$
- $\dfrac19$
- $\dfrac16$

## Explanation

The cube root of $27$ is $3$. The negative exponent requires the reciprocal of the square, so $27^{-\frac23}=3^{-2}=\dfrac19$.
