The expression $f(x)=\dfrac1{\sqrt x+2}$ is rewritten as $\dfrac{\sqrt x-2}{x-4}$. Which pair $(x,f(x))$ is missing from the new formula?

- $(4,0)$
- $\left(4,\dfrac14\right)$
- $\left(0,\dfrac12\right)$
- $\left(2,\dfrac14\right)$

## Explanation

The original denominator is positive for every $x\ge0$. At $x=4$, the root is $2$, so the original value is $\dfrac14$. The conjugate $\sqrt x-2$ vanishes at that input. The new denominator $x-4$ is zero, so the rewritten form omits the corresponding pair. At $x=0$, both formulas are still defined.
