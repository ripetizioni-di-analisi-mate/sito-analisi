Which set is the domain of $\dfrac{\sqrt{x-3}}{\sqrt{3-x}}$?

- $\{3\}$
- $[3,+\infty)$
- $\varnothing$
- $(-\infty,3)$

## Explanation

The numerator exists for $x\ge3$. The root in the denominator must exist and be nonzero, so $3-x>0$, or $x<3$. Both conditions are required and have no common input. At $3$, the numerator is defined, but division by the zero root in the denominator is still impossible.
