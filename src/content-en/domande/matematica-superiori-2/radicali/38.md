What is the domain of $f(x)=\dfrac1{\sqrt{x+6}}$?

- $(-\infty,-6)$
- $[-6,\infty)$
- $(-6,\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-6]$

## Explanation

The radicand must be positive because its square root is in the denominator. The value $-6$ is excluded because it makes the denominator zero.
