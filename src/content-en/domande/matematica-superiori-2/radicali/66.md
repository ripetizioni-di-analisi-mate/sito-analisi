Both $\sqrt{7+4\sqrt3}$ and $\sqrt{7-4\sqrt3}$ are positive. What is their sum?

- $2\sqrt3$
- $\sqrt{14}$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The radicands are $(2+\sqrt3)^2$ and $(2-\sqrt3)^2$. Since $2>\sqrt3$, their principal roots are $2+\sqrt3$ and $2-\sqrt3$, which add to $4$.
