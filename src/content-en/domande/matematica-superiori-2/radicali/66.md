Rationalize $\dfrac1{\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{2}}$.

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$
- $- \sqrt{3} + \sqrt{2}$
- $\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3}$
- $- \sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Multiply the numerator and denominator by the conjugate. The denominator becomes the difference of $3$ and $2$, which is one. The result is $- \sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3}$.
