Rationalise $\dfrac1{\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{2}}$.

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$
- $\sqrt{2}-\sqrt{3}$
- $\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3}$
- $\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The numerator and denominator are multiplied by the conjugate. The denominator becomes the difference of $3$ and $2$, which is one. The result is $\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2}$.
