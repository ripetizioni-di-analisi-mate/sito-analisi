Calculate $\dfrac{\sqrt{75}}{\sqrt3}$.

- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $15$
- $25$

## Explanation

The roots are positive, so their quotient is the square root of the quotient of the radicands. This gives the square root of $25$, which is $5$.
