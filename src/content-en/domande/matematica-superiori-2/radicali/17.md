For $x\in\mathbb R$, which expression equals $\sqrt{18x^2}$?

- $3x\sqrt2$
- $3|x|\sqrt2$
- $9|x|\sqrt2$
- $3|x|\sqrt6$

## Explanation

Writing $18x^2=(3x)^2\cdot2$ lets us take out $|3x|=3|x|$. The absolute value makes the result nonnegative for every $x$.
