In this order, what are the values of $\sqrt{(-8)^2}$ and $-\sqrt{8^2}$?

- $(-8;-8)$
- $(8;8)$
- $(-8;8)$
- $(8;-8)$

## Explanation

In the first expression the minus sign belongs to the base of the power. Squaring gives $64$, whose principal square root is $8$. In the second expression the minus sign is in front of the whole radical. The square again gives $64$ and the root gives $8$. Taking the opposite of that value gives $-8$.
