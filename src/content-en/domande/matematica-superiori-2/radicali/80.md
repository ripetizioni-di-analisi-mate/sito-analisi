Which expression is equal to $\sqrt{x^2-6x+9}+x$ for $x<3$?

- $3$
- $2x-3$
- $2x+3$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The radicand is the square of a binomial, because $x^2-6x+9=(x-3)^2$. Since the square root of a square is the absolute value of the base, we have $\sqrt{x^2-6x+9}=|x-3|$. For $x<3$ the base $x-3$ is negative, so $|x-3|=3-x$. Adding $x$ therefore gives $3-x+x=3$.
