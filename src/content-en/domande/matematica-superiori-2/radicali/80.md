Simplify $\sqrt{(x+1)^2}+\sqrt{(x-1)^2}$ for $-1<x<1$.

- $2$
- $2x$
- $-2x$
- $0$

## Explanation

On the given interval, $x+1>0$ and $x-1<0$. The two roots become their respective absolute values $x+1$ and $1-x$, which add to $2$.
