What factor multiplies $\sqrt{12}$ to give $6$?

- $\sqrt2$
- $2\sqrt3$
- $\sqrt3$
- $3$

## Explanation

Since $6=\sqrt{36}$ and $12\cdot3=36$, the second radicand must be $3$. Checking gives $\sqrt{12}\cdot\sqrt3=\sqrt{36}=6$.
