Which expression with a rational denominator equals $\dfrac{11}{\sqrt{5}}$?

- $\dfrac{11}{5}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{11}$
- $11 \sqrt{5}$
- $\dfrac{11 \sqrt{5}}{5}$

## Explanation

Multiply the numerator and denominator by $\sqrt{5}$. The denominator becomes $5$, and the result is $\dfrac{11 \sqrt{5}}{5}$.
