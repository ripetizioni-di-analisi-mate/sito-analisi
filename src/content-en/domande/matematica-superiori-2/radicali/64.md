After reducing the index of $\sqrt[4]9$, what form results from rationalizing $\dfrac1{\sqrt[4]9}$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{9}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt[4]3}{3}$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}$

## Explanation

The number $\sqrt3$ is nonnegative and its fourth power is $3^2=9$. Thus $\sqrt[4]9=\sqrt3$. Multiplying numerator and denominator by $\sqrt3$ gives $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}$. The answer can be checked by multiplying it by the original denominator. Since that denominator equals $\sqrt3$, their product is one.
