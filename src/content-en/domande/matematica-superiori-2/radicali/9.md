What simplified form results from taking a fourth-power factor out of $\sqrt[4]{192}$?

- $\sqrt[4]{12}$
- $2\sqrt[4]{12}$
- $4\sqrt[4]{12}$
- $2\sqrt{12}$

## Explanation

The radicand contains the factor $16=2^4$, since $192=16\cdot12$. Taking the fourth-power factor out puts $2$ outside and leaves $12$ under the root. The index stays four. Raising $2\sqrt[4]{12}$ to the fourth power gives $2^4\cdot12=192$. The answer is positive, so this check identifies the principal root. The remaining factor $12=2^2\cdot3$ contains no further integer fourth power to take out.
