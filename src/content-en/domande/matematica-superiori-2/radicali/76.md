What is the value of $\sqrt{(x-2)^2}$ when $x<2$?

- $x-2$
- $2-x$
- $x+2$
- $-x-2$

## Explanation

The square root of the square is the absolute value. On this interval, $x-2$ is negative, so take its opposite.
