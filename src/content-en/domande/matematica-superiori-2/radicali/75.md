What is the real domain of $\sqrt{\dfrac{x-1}{x+2}}$?

- $(-\infty,-2)\cup[1,+\infty)$
- $[-2,1]$
- $(-\infty,-2]\cup[1,+\infty)$
- $(-2,1]$

## Explanation

The quotient is positive for $x<-2$ or $x>1$, when the numerator and denominator have the same sign. It is zero at $x=1$. The fraction is undefined at $x=-2$.
