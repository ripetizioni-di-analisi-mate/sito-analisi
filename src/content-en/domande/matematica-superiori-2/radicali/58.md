Calculate $\dfrac{\sqrt{243}}{\sqrt3}$.

- $9$
- $3$
- $27$
- $81$

## Explanation

The roots are positive, so their quotient is the square root of the quotient of the radicands. This gives the square root of $81$, which is $9$.
