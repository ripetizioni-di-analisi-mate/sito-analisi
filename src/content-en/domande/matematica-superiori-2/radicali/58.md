What is the value of $\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{-54}}{\sqrt[3]2}$, taking account of the numerator’s sign?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $-27$
- $27$

## Explanation

The denominator is nonzero. The odd index allows radicands with opposite signs to be combined. The quotient becomes $\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{-54}{2}}=\sqrt[3]{-27}=-3$. The cube of $-3$ is $-27$. Its sign is predictable before doing the calculation, since the original numerator is negative and the original denominator is positive.
