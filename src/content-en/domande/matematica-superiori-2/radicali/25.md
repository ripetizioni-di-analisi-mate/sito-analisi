Which expression with a smaller root index equals $\sqrt[6]{2^8}$?

- $\sqrt[3]4$
- $2\sqrt[3]2$
- $2\sqrt2$
- $4\sqrt[3]2$

## Explanation

The sixth root of $2^8$ is $2\sqrt[6]{2^2}$, since one factor of $2$ can be taken out of a group of six. The number $\sqrt[3]2$ is positive and has sixth power $2^2$, so it replaces $\sqrt[6]{2^2}$. As a check, raising $2\sqrt[3]2$ to the sixth power gives $2^6\cdot2^2=2^8$, the original radicand. The same two steps appear in the rational exponent $\dfrac86=1+\dfrac13$.
