Simplify $\sqrt{972}$.

- $1 + 18 \sqrt{3}$
- $18 \sqrt{3}$
- $-1 + 18 \sqrt{3}$
- $36 \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

The radicand contains a perfect-square factor. The square root of the product is the product of the square roots because both factors are nonnegative. Take the square factor out and leave the remaining factor under the radical sign.

$$
\sqrt{972}=\sqrt{324\cdot3}=18\sqrt3
$$
