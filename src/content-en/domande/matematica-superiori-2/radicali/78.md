What is the solution set of $|x+1|+|x-1|=2$?

- $(-1,1)$
- $\{-1,1\}$
- $\mathbb R$
- $[-1,1]$

## Explanation

The first absolute value is the distance from $-1$ and the second is the distance from $1$. Between the endpoints, the distances add to the length of the segment, which is two. This also holds at the endpoints, where one distance is zero. Outside, the sum is greater. For example, $x=2$ gives $3+1=4$.
