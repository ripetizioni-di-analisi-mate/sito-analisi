What is the value of $\sqrt{(x-4)^2}$ when $x<4$?

- $x-4$
- $x+4$
- $-x-4$
- $4-x$

## Explanation

The square root of the square is the absolute value. On this interval, $x-4$ is negative, so take its opposite.
