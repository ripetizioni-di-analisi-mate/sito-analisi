To rationalize $\dfrac1{\sqrt[3]{x^2}}$ for every $x\ne0$, multiply numerator and denominator by $\sqrt[3]{x^j}$. What is the smallest positive integer $j$ that works?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

After multiplication, the exponent under the root in the denominator is $2+j$. The first multiple of three above two is three, giving $j=1$. The denominator becomes $\sqrt[3]{x^3}=x$ even for negative values of the variable. The choice $j=4$ also produces a multiple of three, but uses a larger exponent than necessary.
