Which radical can you multiply by $\sqrt[3]{9}$ to get the rational number $3$?

- $\sqrt[3]{9}$
- $\sqrt[3]{3}$
- $\sqrt3$
- $\sqrt[3]{27}$

## Explanation

The number $9$ is $3^2$. To reach the radicand $27=3^3$, a factor of $3$ is needed. The requested radical is therefore $\sqrt[3]{3}$.
