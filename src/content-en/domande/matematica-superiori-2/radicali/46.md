Which expression with a single simplified radical is equal to $\sqrt{\dfrac{45}{4}}$?

- $\dfrac{3\sqrt5}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3\sqrt5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9\sqrt5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5\sqrt3}{2}$

## Explanation

The radicand is the quotient of two positive numbers, so the root of the quotient is the quotient of the roots and $\sqrt{\dfrac{45}{4}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{45}}{\sqrt4}$. The denominator is $\sqrt4=2$. Since $45=3^2\cdot5$, the factor $3$ can be taken out of the numerator, which gives $\sqrt{45}=3\sqrt5$. Therefore $\sqrt{\dfrac{45}{4}}=\dfrac{3\sqrt5}{2}$.
