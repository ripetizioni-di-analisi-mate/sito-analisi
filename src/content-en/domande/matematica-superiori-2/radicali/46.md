What is the simplified form of $\sqrt{48}$?

- $8 \sqrt{3}$
- $4 \sqrt{3}$
- $4 \sqrt{6}$
- $2 \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Write the radicand as the product of $16$ and $3$. Take the positive square root of the square factor, giving $4 \sqrt{3}$.
