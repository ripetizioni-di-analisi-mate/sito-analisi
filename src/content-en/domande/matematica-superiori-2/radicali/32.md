How do $\sqrt[3]{-9}$ and $-2$ compare?

- $\sqrt[3]{-9}<-2$
- $\sqrt[3]{-9}>-2$
- $\sqrt[3]{-9}=-2$
- The root is not real.

## Explanation

A cube root is real even for a negative radicand. The cube of $-2$ is $-8$, while the cube of $\sqrt[3]{-9}$ is $-9$. Since $-9<-8$ and cubing preserves order over the reals, the first base is less than $-2$. Its distance from zero is therefore greater than two.
