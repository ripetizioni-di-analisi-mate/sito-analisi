Calculate $\sqrt[3]{-1331}$.

- $-11$
- $-10$
- $-12$
- $-22$

## Explanation

The index is odd, so negative radicands are allowed. The cube of the requested number must equal the radicand. The cube root keeps the negative sign.

$$
(-11)^3=-1331\implies\sqrt[3]{-1331}=-11
$$
