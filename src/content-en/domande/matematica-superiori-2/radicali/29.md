Which fraction with a rational denominator is equivalent to $\dfrac1{\sqrt2-3}$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt2+3}{7}$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt2+3}{7}$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt2-3}{7}$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt2+3}{5}$

## Explanation

The product of $\sqrt2-3$ and $\sqrt2+3$ is $2-9=-7$. Multiplying the numerator and denominator by the conjugate gives $-\dfrac{\sqrt2+3}{7}$.
