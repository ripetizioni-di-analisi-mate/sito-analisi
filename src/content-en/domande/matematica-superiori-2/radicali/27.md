Let $a=\sqrt7+\sqrt3$ and $b=\sqrt7-\sqrt3$. Which relation expresses the reciprocal of $a$ in terms of $b$?

- $a^{-1}=4b$
- $a^{-1}=-\dfrac b4$
- $a^{-1}=\dfrac4b$
- $a^{-1}=\dfrac b4$

## Explanation

The expressions are conjugates and their product is $7-3=4$. Thus $a\cdot\dfrac b4$ equals one. Since $a$ is a sum of positive roots, it has a reciprocal, namely $\dfrac b4$. The value of $b$ is positive as well, since $7>3$, so its sign agrees with that of the required reciprocal.
