An inscribed angle intercepts an arc of measure $60^\circ$. What is the angle in degrees?

- $120$
- $15$
- $30$
- $60$

## Explanation

The inscribed angle measures half its intercepted arc. Divide the given arc measure by two. This gives $30$.
