$C$ and $D$ lie in the interiors of the two arcs with endpoints $A,B$, one on each arc. What relation always holds between $\widehat{ACB}$ and $\widehat{ADB}$?

- They are complementary
- They are always congruent
- They are supplementary
- One is always twice the other

## Explanation

Each angle intercepts the arc that does not contain its vertex. The two arcs are different and together form the entire circle. Their measures add to $360^\circ$. The inscribed angles are half those measures, so they add to $180^\circ$. They are also equal in the special case where $AB$ is a diameter.
