Two chords are both $3$ cm from their respective centres. The circle radii are $5$ cm and $10$ cm. Which comparison is correct?

- The chord in the first circle is longer
- The chord in the second circle is longer
- The chords have equal lengths
- The first chord is a diameter

## Explanation

Compare the squares of the half-chords, $5^2-3^2=16$ and $10^2-3^2=91$. The second is greater, so its square root and the full chord are greater too. Chords equidistant from a centre have equal lengths when the same radius is fixed. Here the radius changes.
