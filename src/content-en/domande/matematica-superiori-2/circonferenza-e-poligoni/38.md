In a regular polygon, each interior angle is five times its adjacent exterior angle. How many sides does it have?

- $5$
- $10$
- $6$
- $12$

## Explanation

An interior angle and its adjacent exterior angle add up to $180^\circ$. If the exterior angle is $\beta$, their sum is $6\beta$. After finding the exterior angle, divide a full turn by it.

$$
\begin{aligned}
6\beta&=180^\circ \\[5pt]
N&=\frac{360^\circ}{30^\circ}=12
\end{aligned}
$$
