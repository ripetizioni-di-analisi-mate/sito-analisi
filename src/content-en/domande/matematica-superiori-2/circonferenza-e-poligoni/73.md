What is the measure in degrees of each exterior angle of a regular polygon with $8$ sides?

- $135$
- $45$
- $90$
- $\dfrac{45}{2}$

## Explanation

The exterior angles, one at each vertex and taken in the same direction, add up to three hundred sixty degrees. For a regular polygon, divide by the number of sides. This gives $45$.
