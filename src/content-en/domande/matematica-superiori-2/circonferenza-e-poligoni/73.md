In a regular polygon, the interior angle and its adjacent exterior angle are in the ratio $5:2$. How many sides does the polygon have?

- $5$
- $7$
- $10$
- $14$

## Explanation

The two angles form a linear pair and add to $180^\circ$. The ratio has seven parts in all, and the exterior takes two of them, so it measures $\dfrac27\cdot180^\circ=\dfrac{360^\circ}{7}$. A full turn contains seven equal exterior angles. Here the number of ratio parts matches the side count because the exterior takes two parts of the straight angle.
