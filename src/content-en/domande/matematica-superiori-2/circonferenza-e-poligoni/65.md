Two concentric circles have radii of $5$ cm and $7$ cm. Point $P$ is $6$ cm from the centre. How many tangents pass through $P$ to the first and second circle, respectively?

- $(2,0)$
- $(0,2)$
- $(2,2)$
- $(1,1)$

## Explanation

For the first circle, $P$ is exterior because $6>5$, so there are two tangents. For the second it is interior because $6<7$, so there are none. Each comparison uses the radius of that circle. The centres and the distance of $P$ are the same, while its position relative to the boundary changes.
