Two tangents are drawn from a point outside a circle. One tangent segment measures $13$ cm. What is the sum of the lengths of the two segments in centimeters?

- $26$
- $\dfrac{13}{2}$
- $13$
- $52$

## Explanation

Tangent segments drawn from the same external point are congruent. Add two copies of the given length. This gives $26$.
