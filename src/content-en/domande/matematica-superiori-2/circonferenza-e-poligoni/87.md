A disc of radius $R$ is cut from a square of side $2R$. What fraction of the square remains in the corners?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $1-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $1-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{4-\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The remaining area is $4R^2-\pi R^2$. Dividing by the original area $4R^2$ gives $1-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
