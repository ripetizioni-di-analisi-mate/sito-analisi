Quadrilateral $ABCD$ is circumscribed about a circle. If $AB=6\,\mathrm{cm}$, $BC=8\,\mathrm{cm}$, and $CD=7\,\mathrm{cm}$, how long is $DA$?

- $7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $13\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Two tangent segments of the same length extend from each vertex. Each sum of opposite sides contains one segment from every vertex, so the sums are equal. Subtract $BC$ to find the requested side.

$$
\begin{aligned}
DA&=AB+CD-BC=6+7-8=5\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
