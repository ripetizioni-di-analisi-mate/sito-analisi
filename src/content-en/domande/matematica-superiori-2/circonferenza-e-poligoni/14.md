An arc subtends a central angle of $90^\circ$ in a circle with radius $14\,\mathrm{cm}$. How long is the arc?

- $1 + 7 \pi$ cm
- $-1 + 7 \pi$ cm
- $14 \pi$ cm
- $7 \pi$ cm

## Explanation

Ninety degrees is a quarter of a full turn. The arc length is therefore a quarter of the circumference. This proportion relates lengths and central angles.

$$
\ell=\frac{90}{360}\cdot2\pi\cdot14=7\pi
$$
