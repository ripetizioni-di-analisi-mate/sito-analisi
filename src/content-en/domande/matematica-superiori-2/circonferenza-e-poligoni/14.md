An arc is exactly $\dfrac38$ of its circle's circumference. What is its measure in degrees?

- $45^\circ$
- $120^\circ$
- $225^\circ$
- $135^\circ$

## Explanation

A fraction of the total circumference corresponds to the same fraction of a full turn. Divide $360^\circ$ into eight parts of $45^\circ$ and take three, giving $135^\circ$. The radius is unnecessary because the data give a ratio of lengths. The remaining arc occupies the missing five eighths and measures $225^\circ$.
