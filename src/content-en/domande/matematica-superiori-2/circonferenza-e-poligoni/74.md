What is the measure in degrees of each exterior angle of a regular polygon with $9$ sides?

- $140$
- $20$
- $40$
- $72$

## Explanation

The exterior angles, one at each vertex and taken in the same direction, add up to three hundred sixty degrees. For a regular polygon, divide by the number of sides. This gives $40$.
