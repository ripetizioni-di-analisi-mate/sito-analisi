A sector has radius $r$ and central angle $0<\alpha<180^\circ$. Halve the radius and double the angle. What is the ratio of the new area to the original area?

- $\dfrac12$
- $1$
- $2$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

The angle enters the area formula to the first power, so doubling it gives a factor of two. The radius is squared, so halving it gives a factor of $\dfrac14$. Multiplying the effects gives $\dfrac12$. The assumption on the initial angle keeps the doubled angle below a full turn.
