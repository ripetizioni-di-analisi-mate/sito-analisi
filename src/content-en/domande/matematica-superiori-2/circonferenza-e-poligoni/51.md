In the convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, inscribed in a circle, the inscribed angles $\widehat{BAC}$ and $\widehat{BDC}$ subtend the same arc. What are the endpoints of that arc?

- $A$ and $C$
- $B$ and $C$
- $B$ and $D$
- $A$ and $D$

## Explanation

The angle $\widehat{BAC}$ has its vertex at $A$ and its arms meet the circle again at $B$ and at $C$, so it subtends the arc with endpoints $B$ and $C$ that does not contain $A$. The arms of $\widehat{BDC}$ also pass through $B$ and $C$. The quadrilateral is convex, therefore the vertices follow one another on the circle in the order $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$, and neither $A$ nor $D$ lies on the arc between $B$ and $C$. The two angles therefore subtend the arc with endpoints $B$ and $C$, and for this reason they are equal, because each is half of the same central angle $\widehat{BOC}$.
