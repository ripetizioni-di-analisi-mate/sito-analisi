In cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$, the diagonals meet at $P$. Which chord is subtended by both $\angle BAC$ and $\angle BDC$?

- $AC$
- $BC$
- $BD$
- $AD$

## Explanation

The sides of both angles meet the circle at the same points $B$ and $C$. Thus both angles subtend chord $BC$.
