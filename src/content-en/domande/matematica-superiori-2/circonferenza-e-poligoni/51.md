A quadrilateral is inscribed in a circle. One interior angle measures $80^\circ$. What is the measure of the opposite angle in degrees?

- $80$
- $100$
- $10$
- $280$

## Explanation

Opposite angles in a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary. Subtract the given angle from one hundred eighty degrees. This gives $100$.
