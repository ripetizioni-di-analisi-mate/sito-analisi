A circle has radius $3$ cm. Which length cannot be the length of one of its arcs?

- $5\pi$ cm
- $3\pi$ cm
- $7\pi$ cm
- $2\pi$ cm

## Explanation

The full circumference is $6\pi$ cm. An arc cannot be longer than that, so $7\pi$ cm is impossible. An arc of $5\pi$ cm is larger than a semicircle, but it is still only part of the full turn. A semicircle measures $3\pi$ cm, and an arc of $2\pi$ cm spans one third of the turn.
