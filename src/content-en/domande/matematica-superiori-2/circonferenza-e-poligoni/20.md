An annulus has area $24\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ and inner radius $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its outer radius?

- $2 \sqrt{6}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The area of the annulus is the difference of the areas of the concentric disks. After dividing by $\pi$, add the square of the inner radius. The positive square root gives the outer radius.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2-5^2&=24 \\[5pt]
R&=\sqrt{49}=7\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
