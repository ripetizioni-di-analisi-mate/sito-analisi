What is the measure in degrees of each exterior angle of a regular polygon with $6$ sides?

- $60$
- $120$
- $180$
- $30$

## Explanation

The exterior angles, one at each vertex and taken in the same direction, add up to three hundred sixty degrees. For a regular polygon, divide by the number of sides. This gives $60$.
