Chord $AB$ is at a positive distance from the centre. $C$ is on the circle and is distinct from $A$ and $B$. Can $\widehat{ACB}$ be right?

- No, because a right angle would intercept a diameter.
- Yes, if $C$ is on the major arc.
- Yes, if $C$ is on the minor arc.
- Yes, for every point on the circle other than $A,B$.

## Explanation

A right inscribed angle intercepts a $180^\circ$ arc. The chord joining its endpoints therefore runs through the centre and has distance zero. This contradicts the stated positive distance. For a chord that does not pass through the centre, one arc is smaller and the other larger than a semicircle.
