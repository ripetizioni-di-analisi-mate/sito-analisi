Triangle $ABC$ is inscribed in a circle. Side $AB$ is a diameter, and $\angle CAB=32^\circ$. What is $\angle ABC$?

- $58^\circ$
- $32^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $148^\circ$

## Explanation

The angle at $C$ intercepts the diameter, so it is a right angle. Subtract $32^\circ$ from $90^\circ$ to find the other acute angle.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\angle ABC&=180^\circ-90^\circ-32^\circ=58^\circ
\end{aligned}
$$
