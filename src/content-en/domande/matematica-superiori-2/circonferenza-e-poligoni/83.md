An arc of $75^\circ$ measures $5\pi$ cm. What is the full circumference?

- $24\pi$ cm
- $12\pi$ cm
- $60\pi$ cm
- $5\pi$ cm

## Explanation

The arc takes the fraction $\dfrac{75}{360}=\dfrac5{24}$ of a full turn. If five parts of the circumference measure $5\pi$ cm, each part measures $\pi$ cm and the twenty-four parts measure $24\pi$ cm. As a check, dividing that circumference by $2\pi$ gives the radius of $12$ cm.
