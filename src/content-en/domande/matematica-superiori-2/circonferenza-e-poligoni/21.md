Two wheels of diameters $30$ cm and $60$ cm roll without slipping. The first makes $8$ turns. How many turns of the second cover the same distance?

- $8$
- $16$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

Each turn without slipping advances a wheel by one circumference. The second diameter is twice the first, so its distance per turn is doubled. The lengths per turn are $30\pi$ cm and $60\pi$ cm. The first travels $8\cdot30\pi=240\pi$ cm. Divide that same distance by $60\pi$ cm to obtain four turns.
