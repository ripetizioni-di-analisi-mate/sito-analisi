$A,B,C$ are three distinct points on one circle. Angle $\widehat{ACB}$ is right. Which conclusion is correct?

- $AC$ is a diameter
- $BC$ is a diameter
- $AB$ is a diameter
- $AB$ is a radius

## Explanation

Vertex $C$ lies on the circle. Doubling its angle gives $180^\circ$, the measure of the intercepted arc from $A$ to $B$. The endpoints are therefore opposite relative to the centre. The segment joining them passes through the centre and measures two radii. In the inscribed right triangle this segment is also the hypotenuse.
