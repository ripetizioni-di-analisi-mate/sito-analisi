A triangle is inscribed in a circle with one side along a diameter of $7$ cm. What is the angle opposite the diameter?

- $180^\circ$.
- $60^\circ$.
- $90^\circ$.
- $45^\circ$.

## Explanation

The arc opposite the angle measures one hundred eighty degrees. The inscribed angle intercepting that arc has half its measure.
