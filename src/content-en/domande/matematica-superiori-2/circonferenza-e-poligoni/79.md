Two arcs have the same length in circles with radii of $6$ and $9$ centimetres. The first central angle is $90^\circ$. What is the measure of the second?

- $135^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $30^\circ$

## Explanation

Arc length is proportional to the product of the radius and central angle. Because the lengths are equal, set the two products equal. The larger radius requires the smaller angle.

$$
\begin{aligned}
6\cdot90^\circ&=9\beta \\[5pt]
\beta&=60^\circ
\end{aligned}
$$
