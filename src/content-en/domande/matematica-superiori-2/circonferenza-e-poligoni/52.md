A quadrilateral is inscribed in a circle. One interior angle measures $90^\circ$. What is the measure of the opposite angle in degrees?

- $90$
- $270$
- $0$
- $-90$

## Explanation

Opposite angles in a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary. Subtract the given angle from one hundred eighty degrees. This gives $90$.
