Which four measures in degrees, read in the order $A,B,C,D$, satisfy both opposite-angle relations for an inscribed quadrilateral?

- $(96;116;84;64)$
- $(96;84;116;64)$
- $(96;116;64;84)$
- $(96;64;116;84)$

## Explanation

Opposite vertices are two positions apart in the stated order. So add the first measure to the third and the second to the fourth. In the required arrangement this gives $96^\circ+84^\circ=180^\circ$ and $116^\circ+64^\circ=180^\circ$. The other orders change those pairings even though the sum of all four measures stays correct.
