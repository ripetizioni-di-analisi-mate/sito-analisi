What is the perimeter of a semicircular sector of radius $8$ cm?

- $8\pi$ cm
- $(8\pi+8)$ cm
- $(8\pi+16)$ cm
- $16\pi$ cm

## Explanation

The curved boundary is a semicircle, so it measures half of $16\pi$ cm, namely $8\pi$ cm. The straight boundary is the diameter, made up of two opposite radii with total length $16$ cm. The perimeter follows both parts. Because the two radii are collinear, the boundary shows one straight segment whose length is their sum.
