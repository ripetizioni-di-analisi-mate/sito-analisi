A sector with radius $13$ cm has a central angle of $90^\circ$. What is its area in square centimeters?

- $169 \pi$
- $\dfrac{13 \pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{169 \pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{169 \pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The sector occupies a quarter of the disk. Divide the area of the disk, pi times the square of the radius, by four. This gives $\dfrac{169 \pi}{4}$.
