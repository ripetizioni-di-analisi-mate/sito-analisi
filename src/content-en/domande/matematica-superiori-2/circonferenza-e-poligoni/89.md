Two concentric disks have radii $r$ and $3r$. What fraction of the larger disk’s area lies inside the smaller disk?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{8}{9}$

## Explanation

The areas are $\pi r^2$ and $\pi(3r)^2$, respectively. The factor $\pi r^2$ cancels in the ratio. The result is one ninth because the factor three applies to the radius and is squared.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\pi r^2}{\pi(3r)^2}&=\frac19
\end{aligned}
$$
