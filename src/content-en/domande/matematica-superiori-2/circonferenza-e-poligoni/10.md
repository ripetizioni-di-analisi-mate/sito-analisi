In the figure, which angle should be halved to obtain $\widehat{ACB}$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 xmin=-4.7 xmax=4.7 ymin=-4.7 ymax=4.7 curve="4*cos(t),4*sin(t),0,6.28318530718" linee="-4,0 2.000000,-3.464102 0,0 2.000000,3.464102 -4,0" punti="-4,0;2.000000,-3.464102;2.000000,3.464102;0,0" etichette="-4.28,-0.26,C;2.14,-3.89,A;2.12,3.9,B;-0.27,-0.35,O" titolo="$C$ lies on the major arc $AB$"}

- The central angle for major arc $AB$
- $\widehat{CAO}$
- $\widehat{OBC}$
- The central angle for minor arc $AB$

## Explanation

The angle to halve has its vertex at $O$ and intercepts the same arc as the angle at $C$. Of the two arcs $AB$, choose the one that does not contain $C$. In the figure it is the minor arc, bounded by the radii drawn toward $A$ and $B$. The angles at vertices $A$ and $B$ are not central angles.
