An inscribed angle measures $45^\circ$. What is the measure of the central angle that intercepts the same arc?

- $91$ degrees
- $89$ degrees
- $180$ degrees
- $90$ degrees

## Explanation

The central angle is twice an inscribed angle intercepting the same arc. Double the given measure. Both measures remain in degrees.

$$
\beta=2\cdot45^\circ=90^\circ
$$
