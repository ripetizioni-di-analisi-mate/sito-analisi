A chord cuts off a minor arc of $110^\circ$. What is the inscribed angle whose vertex lies on that minor arc?

- $55^\circ$
- $110^\circ$
- $125^\circ$
- $70^\circ$

## Explanation

The vertex lies on the minor arc, so the angle intercepts the major arc. The major arc completes the full turn and measures $250^\circ$. The inscribed angle is half that measure.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\beta&=\frac{360^\circ-110^\circ}{2}=125^\circ
\end{aligned}
$$
