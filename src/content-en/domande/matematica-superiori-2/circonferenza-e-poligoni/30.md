A regular polygon has an exterior angle of $45^\circ$. How many sides does it have?

- $4$
- $6$
- $16$
- $8$

## Explanation

The exterior angles add up to three hundred sixty degrees. Dividing that sum by one exterior angle gives the number of sides. This gives $8$.
