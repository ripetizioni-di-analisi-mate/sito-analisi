In a circle of radius $8$ cm, which statement describes the longest chord?

- It passes through the centre and measures $8$ cm
- It passes through the centre and measures $16$ cm
- It is $8$ cm from the centre and measures $16$ cm
- It is $8$ cm from the centre and measures $8$ cm

## Explanation

The half-chord is $\sqrt{r^2-d^2}$ where $d$ is the distance from the centre. It is largest at the smallest value of $d$, zero, when the half-chord equals the radius. This gives a diameter of $16$ cm. At a distance equal to the radius, the line would be tangent with only one contact point.
