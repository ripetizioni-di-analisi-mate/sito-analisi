A chord is at distance $21\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center of a circle with radius $35\,\mathrm{cm}$. How long is the chord?

- $57$ cm
- $56$ cm
- $55$ cm
- $112$ cm

## Explanation

The perpendicular from the center to a chord bisects it. The radius, the distance to the chord, and the half-chord form a right triangle. Use the Pythagorean theorem, then double the half-chord.

$$
\frac{AB}2=\sqrt{1225-441}=28
$$

$$
AB=2\cdot28=56
$$
