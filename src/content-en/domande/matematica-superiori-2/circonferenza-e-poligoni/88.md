A disk has circumference $18\pi$ cm. What is its area?

- $324\pi$ cm$^2$
- $81\pi$ cm$^2$
- $18\pi$ cm$^2$
- $9\pi$ cm$^2$

## Explanation

The boundary measure is a length. Dividing $18\pi$ cm by $2\pi$ gives the radius of $9$ cm. Squaring it and multiplying by $\pi$ yields $81\pi$ cm$^2$. Using $18$ directly as the radius would give four times the area, because $18$ is the diameter. The units also change when finding the area.
