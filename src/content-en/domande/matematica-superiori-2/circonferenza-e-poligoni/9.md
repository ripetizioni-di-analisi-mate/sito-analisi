A central angle measures $90^\circ$. What is the measure of an inscribed angle that intercepts the same arc?

- $46$ degrees
- $44$ degrees
- $45$ degrees
- $90$ degrees

## Explanation

The central angle and the inscribed angle intercept the same arc. The inscribed angle is half the central angle. They must intercept the same arc because the other arc would give a different angle.

$$
\alpha=\frac{90^\circ}2=45^\circ
$$
