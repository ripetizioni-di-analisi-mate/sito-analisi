A circle has centre $O$ and radius $6$ cm. For which listed distance does the point belong both to the circle and to the disk?

- $5$ cm
- $0$ cm
- $6$ cm
- $7$ cm

## Explanation

The circle is the boundary formed by points at exactly the radius from the centre. The disk contains that boundary and all points at smaller distances. A point at distance $6$ cm therefore belongs to both. The point at distance $5$ cm and the centre are inside the disk, while a distance of $7$ cm gives a point outside it.
