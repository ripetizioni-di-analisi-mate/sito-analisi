Two chords of the same circle are at distances of $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $7\,\mathrm{cm}$ from its centre. How can their lengths be ordered without knowing the radius?

- The second chord is longer.
- The first chord is longer.
- The two chords have equal lengths.
- The comparison requires knowing the radius.

## Explanation

The square of a half-chord is the difference between the squares of the radius and the distance. The radius is the same for both, so the shorter distance gives the longer half-chord.
