A regular polygon has an exterior angle of $36^\circ$. How many sides does it have?

- $7$
- $20$
- $10$
- $6$

## Explanation

The exterior angles add up to three hundred sixty degrees. Dividing that sum by one exterior angle gives the number of sides. This gives $10$.
