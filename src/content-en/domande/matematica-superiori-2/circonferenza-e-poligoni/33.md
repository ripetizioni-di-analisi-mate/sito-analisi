In a regular octagon, the exterior angle and the central angle between consecutive vertices both measure $45^\circ$. Which statement distinguishes them?

- Both have their vertex at the centre
- They form a linear pair with a shared side
- The exterior angle has its vertex on the boundary and the other at the centre
- They are interior angles of two triangles with a vertex at the centre

## Explanation

The exterior angle describes the turn at a vertex of the polygon and is formed by extending a side. The central angle is between radii to consecutive vertices. Dividing a full turn into eight parts gives the same measure for the two constructions. Equality of measures leaves their vertices distinct.
