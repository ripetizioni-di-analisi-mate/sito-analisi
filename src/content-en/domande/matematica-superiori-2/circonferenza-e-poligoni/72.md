A regular hexagon has perimeter $36$ cm and area $54\sqrt3$ cm$^2$. What apothem follows from the area?

- $\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}$ cm
- $3\sqrt3$ cm
- $6$ cm
- $6\sqrt3$ cm

## Explanation

The area of the regular polygon is half the product of its perimeter and apothem. Isolating the apothem gives $2\cdot54\sqrt3/36=3\sqrt3$ cm. Dividing by the perimeter alone would give half that value. As a check, the apothem is smaller than the circumradius, which equals the hexagon's side length of $6$ cm.
