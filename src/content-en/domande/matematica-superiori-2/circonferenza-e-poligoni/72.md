What is the measure in degrees of each exterior angle of a regular polygon with $7$ sides?

- $\dfrac{180}{7}$
- $\dfrac{360}{7}$
- $\dfrac{900}{7}$
- $120$

## Explanation

The exterior angles, one at each vertex and taken in the same direction, add up to three hundred sixty degrees. For a regular polygon, divide by the number of sides. This gives $\dfrac{360}{7}$.
