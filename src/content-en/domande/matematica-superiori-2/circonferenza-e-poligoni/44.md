One angle in a cyclic quadrilateral measures $110^\circ$. What is the measure of the opposite angle?

- $110^\circ$
- $70^\circ$
- $55^\circ$
- $80^\circ$

## Explanation

Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary. Their sum is one hundred eighty degrees. Subtracting one hundred ten gives seventy degrees.
