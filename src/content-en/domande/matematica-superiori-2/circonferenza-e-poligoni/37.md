A wheel travels $15\pi$ m without slipping in exactly five turns. What is its radius?

- $3$ m
- $5$ m
- $\dfrac32$ m
- $\dfrac{15}{2}$ m

## Explanation

One turn travels one circumference. Divide $15\pi$ m by five to obtain $3\pi$ m. Since this length is $2\pi r$, the radius is $\dfrac32$ m. The value $3$ m would be the diameter. Substituting the radius recovers the total distance after multiplying the circumference by the number of turns.
