On circles of radii $5$ cm and $8$ cm, choose arcs with the same central angle. What is the ratio of their lengths in the same order as the radii?

- $25:64$
- $8:5$
- $1:1$
- $5:8$

## Explanation

The two arcs take the same fraction of their respective circumferences. In the ratio of lengths, both that fraction and the common factor $2\pi$ cancel. This leaves the radii in the ratio $5:8$. The longer arc belongs to the larger circle even though the central angles cover the same part of a full turn.
