Two tangents are drawn from a point outside a circle. One tangent segment measures $9$ cm. What is the sum of the lengths of the two segments in centimeters?

- $18$
- $9$
- $36$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$

## Explanation

Tangent segments drawn from the same external point are congruent. Add two copies of the given length. This gives $18$.
