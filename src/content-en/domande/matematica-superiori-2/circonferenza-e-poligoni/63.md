A circumscribed quadrilateral has consecutive sides of $7$, $11$, and $9$ cm. What is its perimeter?

- $32$ cm
- $27$ cm
- $36$ cm
- $18$ cm

## Explanation

The first and third sides are opposite and add to $16$ cm. Since all sides are tangent, the other opposite pair has the same sum, because each pair counts one tangent segment from every vertex. The perimeter is therefore twice $16$ cm. The missing side measures $16-11=5$ cm.
