A tangent is drawn from $P$ to a circle with centre $O$ and radius $18\,\mathrm{cm}$. If $OP=30\,\mathrm{cm}$, how long is tangent segment $PT$?

- $24$ cm
- $25$ cm
- $23$ cm
- $48$ cm

## Explanation

The radius to the point of tangency is perpendicular to the tangent. The centre, the external point, and the point of contact therefore form a right triangle. The distance from the centre is the hypotenuse, so the Pythagorean theorem applies.

$$
OT\perp PT
$$

$$
PT=\sqrt{OP^2-OT^2}=\sqrt{900-324}=24
$$
