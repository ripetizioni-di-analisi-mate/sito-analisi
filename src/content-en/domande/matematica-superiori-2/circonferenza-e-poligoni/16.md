A disk has diameter $12$ cm. Which of the proposed results is its correct area?

- $144\pi$ cm$^2$
- $36\pi$ cm$^2$
- $12\pi$ cm$^2$
- $24\pi$ cm$^2$

## Explanation

The diameter consists of two radii, so each radius measures $6$ cm. Square the radius and multiply by $\pi$ to get area $36\pi$ cm$^2$. Using the diameter in place of the radius gives four times the area. The value $12\pi$ instead gives the circumference, measured in centimetres.
