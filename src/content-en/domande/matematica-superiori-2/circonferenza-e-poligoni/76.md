A sector with radius $5$ cm has a central angle of $90^\circ$. What is its area in square centimeters?

- $25 \pi$
- $\dfrac{25 \pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5 \pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{25 \pi}{4}$

## Explanation

The sector occupies a quarter of the disk. Divide the area of the disk, pi times the square of the radius, by four. This gives $\dfrac{25 \pi}{4}$.
