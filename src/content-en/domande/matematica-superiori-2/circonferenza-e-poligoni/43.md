What is the apothem of a regular polygon?

- $\text{The distance from the centre to one side}$
- $\text{The diameter of the circumscribed circle}$
- $\text{The sum of the side lengths}$
- $\text{The distance between two consecutive vertices}$

## Explanation

The apothem is the perpendicular segment from the centre to a side. It is also the radius of the inscribed circle. It measures the distance from the centre to a side.
