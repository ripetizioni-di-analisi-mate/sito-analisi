The radius of a disk is doubled. How do its circumference and area change?

- Both double
- Both quadruple
- The circumference doubles and the area quadruples
- The circumference quadruples and the area doubles

## Explanation

The circumference formula uses the first power of $r$. Substituting $2r$ therefore doubles the length. The area formula uses $r^2$, and $(2r)^2=4r^2$. The factor $\pi$ is unchanged by the substitution. The two changes follow from the powers to which the radius is raised.
