The vertices of two inscribed angles lie on the same arc $AB$, and both angles have sides through $A$ and $B$. The first measures $38^\circ$. What is the measure of the second?

- $76^\circ$
- $142^\circ$
- $38^\circ$
- $19^\circ$

## Explanation

The two vertices lie on the same arc. Thus the angles both intercept the other arc cut off by chord $AB$. Their measures are equal because both are half the same arc.
