At which vertices are the right angles of triangles $OTP$ and $OSP$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 xmin=-3.65 xmax=5.7 ymin=-3.55 ymax=3.65 curve="3*cos(t),3*sin(t),0,6.28318530718" linee="0,0 1.8,2.4 5,0 1.8,-2.4 0,0" segmenti="0,0,5,0" punti="0,0;1.8,2.4;5,0;1.8,-2.4" etichette="-0.29,-0.38,O;1.98,2.76,T;1.99,-2.81,S;5.3,-0.31,P" titolo="The two lines through $P$ are tangent at the points $T,S$"}

- Both at $P$
- Both at $O$
- At $T$ and $P$
- At $T$ and $S$

## Explanation

The radius to a point of tangency is perpendicular to the tangent at that point. Thus $OT$ meets $PT$ at a right angle, as does $OS$ with $PS$. The vertices are the contact points. Segment $OP$ is opposite the right angle in both triangles, making it their shared hypotenuse.
