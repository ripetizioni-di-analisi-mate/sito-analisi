Two tangents are drawn from a point outside a circle. One tangent segment measures $7$ cm. What is the sum of the lengths of the two segments in centimeters?

- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $28$
- $7$
- $14$

## Explanation

Tangent segments drawn from the same external point are congruent. Add two copies of the given length. This gives $14$.
