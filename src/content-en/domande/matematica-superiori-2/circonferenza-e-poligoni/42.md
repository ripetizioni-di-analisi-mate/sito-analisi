How are the tangent line and the radius to the point of tangency related?

- $\text{Parallel}$
- $\text{Coincident}$
- $\text{Always at an angle of }45^\circ$
- $\text{Perpendicular}$

## Explanation

The radius joins the center to the point of contact. The tangent forms a right angle with the radius there. This property gives a right triangle in problems involving tangents.
