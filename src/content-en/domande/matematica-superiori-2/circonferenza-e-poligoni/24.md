An annulus has radii $4$ cm and $9$ cm. How long is its entire boundary, including the inner part?

- $18\pi$ cm
- $26\pi$ cm
- $10\pi$ cm
- $65\pi$ cm

## Explanation

The outer boundary has circumference $18\pi$ cm. The inner boundary has circumference $8\pi$ cm. The total path along both contains both lengths, for $26\pi$ cm. Finding the surface area would instead involve subtracting the areas of two disks. The question asks for a length, measured in centimetres.
