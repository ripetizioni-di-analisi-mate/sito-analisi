An annulus has radii $13$ cm and $6$ cm. What is its area in square centimeters?

- $14 \pi$
- $133 \pi$
- $49 \pi$
- $205 \pi$

## Explanation

Subtract the area of the inner disk from the area of the outer disk. Use the difference of the squares of the radii. This gives $133 \pi$.
