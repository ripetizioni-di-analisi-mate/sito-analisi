An inscribed angle intercepts an arc of measure $80^\circ$. What is the angle in degrees?

- $20$
- $80$
- $40$
- $160$

## Explanation

The inscribed angle measures half its intercepted arc. Divide the given arc measure by two. This gives $40$.
