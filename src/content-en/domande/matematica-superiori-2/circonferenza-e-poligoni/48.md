A student claims that $\widehat{ACB}=90^\circ$ because $AB$ is a diameter. What hypothesis of the theorem is missing in the figure?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 xmin=-4.7 xmax=4.7 ymin=-4.7 ymax=4.7 curve="4*cos(t),4*sin(t),0,6.28318530718" linee="-4,0 4,0;-4,0 0,2.15 4,0" punti="-4,0;4,0;0,0;0,2.15" etichette="-4.27,-0.28,A;4.28,-0.28,B;0,-0.37,O;0,2.53,C" titolo="Point $C$ is inside the disk"}

- $O$ must lie on segment $AB$
- $AB$ must be longer than the diameter
- $C$ must lie on the circle
- $AC$ and $AB$ must have equal lengths

## Explanation

The diameter determines a semicircle, but the right-angle theorem requires an inscribed angle. Its vertex must lie on the boundary, distinct from the two endpoints of the diameter. The point $C$ in the figure is inside the disk. The fact that $O$ lies on the segment is already guaranteed by the diameter definition and cannot replace the missing hypothesis.
