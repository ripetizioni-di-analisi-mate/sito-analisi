Find the area of a circle with radius $12\,\mathrm{cm}$.

- $24 \pi$ cm²
- $144 \pi$ cm²
- $72 \pi$ cm²
- $288 \pi$ cm²

## Explanation

The area of a circle is pi times the square of the radius. Square the radius before multiplying. The resulting measure is an area.

$$
A=\pi r^2=\pi\cdot12^2=144\pi
$$
