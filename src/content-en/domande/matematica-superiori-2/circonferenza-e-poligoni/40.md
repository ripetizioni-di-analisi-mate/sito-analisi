Two disks have areas $16\pi$ cm$^2$ and $81\pi$ cm$^2$. What is the ratio of their radii in that order?

- $16:81$
- $4:9$
- $9:4$
- $8:81$

## Explanation

Dividing each area by $\pi$ gives the square of its radius. The positive radii are therefore $4$ cm and $9$ cm. The area ratio is the square of the required ratio. Taking only the area coefficients would compare surfaces and miss the step from squared quantities back to lengths.
