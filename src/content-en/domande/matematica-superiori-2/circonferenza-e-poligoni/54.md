Two opposite angles in a cyclic quadrilateral have ratio $2:3$. What is the measure of the smaller angle?

- $60^\circ$
- $72^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $108^\circ$

## Explanation

The opposite angles are supplementary. They can be written as two and three times the same quantity. The five parts together measure $180^\circ$, so one part is $36^\circ$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
5x&=180^\circ \\[5pt]
2x&=72^\circ
\end{aligned}
$$
