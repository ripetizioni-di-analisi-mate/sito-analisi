A convex kite $ABCD$ is inscribed in a circle, with $AB=AD$ and $CB=CD$. What are the angles at $B$ and $D$?

- Both $60^\circ$
- Both $90^\circ$
- One $60^\circ$ and the other $120^\circ$
- Both $45^\circ$

## Explanation

Triangles $ABC$ and $ADC$ are congruent by three sides, so the angles at $B,D$ are equal. They are opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral and sum to $180^\circ$, making each $90^\circ$.
