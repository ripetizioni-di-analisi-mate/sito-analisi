Two opposite angles of a convex quadrilateral inscribed in a circle are in the ratio $2:3$. What is the measure of the smaller one?

- $60^\circ$
- $72^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $108^\circ$

## Explanation

The quadrilateral is inscribed, so the two opposite angles are supplementary and add up to $180^\circ$. Since they are in the ratio $2:3$, they can be written as $2x$ and $3x$, where $x$ is the size of one part. Then

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x+3x&=180^\circ \\[5pt]
x&=36^\circ
\end{aligned}
$$

The smaller angle therefore measures $2x=72^\circ$, while $3x=108^\circ$ is the larger angle.
