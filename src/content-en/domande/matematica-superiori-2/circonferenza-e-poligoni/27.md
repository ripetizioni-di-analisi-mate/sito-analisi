A tangent from $P$ touches a circle with center $O$ at $T$. If $OP=17\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $PT=15\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the radius?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $32\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\sqrt{514}\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Radius $OT$ is perpendicular to $PT$, so $OP$ is the hypotenuse. Subtract the square of the known leg from the square of the hypotenuse and take the square root.

$$
\begin{aligned}
r^2&=17^2-15^2=64 \\[5pt]
r&=8\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
