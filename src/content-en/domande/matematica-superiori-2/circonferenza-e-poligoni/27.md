From a point $P$ outside a circle with centre $O$ the two tangents are drawn, touching the circle at $S$ and at $T$. The angle $\widehat{SPT}$ measures $50^\circ$. What is the measure of the central angle $\widehat{SOT}$ subtending the minor arc $ST$?

- $130^\circ$
- $50^\circ$
- $40^\circ$
- $65^\circ$

## Explanation

The radius at the point of contact is perpendicular to the tangent, so the angles $\widehat{OSP}$ and $\widehat{OTP}$ are right angles. Quadrilateral $OSPT$ therefore has two angles of $90^\circ$ and the angle of $50^\circ$ at $P$. The interior angles of a convex quadrilateral add up to $(4-2)\cdot180^\circ=360^\circ$, hence

$$
\widehat{SOT}=360^\circ-90^\circ-90^\circ-50^\circ=130^\circ .
$$

The answer $65^\circ$ is half of this value, that is the measure of an inscribed angle subtending the minor arc $ST$.
