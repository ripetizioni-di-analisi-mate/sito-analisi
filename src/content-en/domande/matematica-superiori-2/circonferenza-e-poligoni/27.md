Two externally tangent circles have radii $3$ and $5$ cm. How far is their tangency point from the midpoint of the segment joining their centres?

- $1$ cm
- $2$ cm
- $4$ cm
- $3$ cm

## Explanation

The centres are $8$ cm apart. The midpoint is $4$ cm from the first centre and the tangency point is $3$ cm away, so they are $1$ cm apart.
