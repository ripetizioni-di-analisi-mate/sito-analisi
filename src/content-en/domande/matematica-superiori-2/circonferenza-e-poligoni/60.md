A right triangle has legs of $9$ cm and $12$ cm. What is the radius of the circle through the three vertices?

- $\dfrac{15}{2}$ cm
- $15$ cm
- $6$ cm
- $\dfrac{21}{2}$ cm

## Explanation

The Pythagorean theorem gives hypotenuse $\sqrt{9^2+12^2}=15$ cm. Since all three vertices lie on one circle, the side opposite the right angle is the diameter. That length equals two radii, so take half of it. The centre lies at the midpoint of the hypotenuse.
