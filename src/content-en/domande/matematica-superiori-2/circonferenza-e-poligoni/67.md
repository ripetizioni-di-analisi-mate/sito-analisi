In a circle of radius $10$ cm, a chord is as long as the radius. How far is it from the centre?

- $0$ cm
- $5$ cm
- $5\sqrt3$ cm
- $10\sqrt3$ cm

## Explanation

The perpendicular distance is the leg of the triangle whose other leg is the half-chord of $5$ cm. The hypotenuse is the $10$ cm radius. Thus the square of the distance is $100-25=75$, giving distance $5\sqrt3$ cm. Using the whole chord would incorrectly give zero, the diameter case.
