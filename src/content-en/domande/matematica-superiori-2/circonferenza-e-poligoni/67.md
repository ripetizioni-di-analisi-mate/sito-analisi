A chord is $24$ cm long and the radius is $15$ cm. How far is the chord from the center in centimeters?

- $3$
- $12$
- $9$
- $15$

## Explanation

The perpendicular from the center bisects the chord. With the radius and half-chord, use the Pythagorean theorem to find the other leg. This gives $9$.
