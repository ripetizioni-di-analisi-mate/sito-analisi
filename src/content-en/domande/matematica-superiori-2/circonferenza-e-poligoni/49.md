Two inscribed angles both measure $68^\circ$ and lie on circles of different radii. What follows?

- The intercepted arcs have equal length
- One of the two chords must be a diameter
- Both intercepted arcs are larger than a semicircle
- The intercepted arcs have equal angular measure

## Explanation

In both cases the intercepted arc measures twice its inscribed angle, or $136^\circ$. This measure gives the same fraction of a full turn. Arc length also depends on the radius, so different radii give different lengths. Since $136^\circ$ is below $180^\circ$, both arcs are smaller than a semicircle.
