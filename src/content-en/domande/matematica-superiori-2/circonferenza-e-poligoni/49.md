An inscribed angle intercepts an arc of measure $100^\circ$. What is the angle in degrees?

- $200$
- $25$
- $100$
- $50$

## Explanation

The inscribed angle measures half its intercepted arc. Divide the given arc measure by two. This gives $50$.
