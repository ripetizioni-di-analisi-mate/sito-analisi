Which measure can be the interior angle of a regular polygon?

- $140^\circ$
- $142^\circ$
- $146^\circ$
- $158^\circ$

## Explanation

For each measure subtract it from $180^\circ$ to find the exterior turn. A full turn of $360^\circ$ has to contain an integer number of these turns. With $140^\circ$ the remainder is $40^\circ$, giving a ratio of $9$. The other candidates leave $38^\circ$, $34^\circ$, and $22^\circ$, which do not divide a full turn into an integer number of parts.
