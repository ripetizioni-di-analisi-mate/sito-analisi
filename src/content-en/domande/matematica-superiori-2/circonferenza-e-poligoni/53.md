In convex cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$, $\widehat{BAD}=100^\circ$. The arc $BC$ that avoids $A,D$ measures $76^\circ$. What is the measure of arc $CD$ that avoids $A,B$?

- $24^\circ$
- $124^\circ$
- $48^\circ$
- $200^\circ$

## Explanation

The angle at $A$ intercepts the arc from $B$ to $D$ through $C$. The whole arc measures twice $100^\circ$, or $200^\circ$. Removing the $76^\circ$ part $BC$ leaves arc $CD$ of $124^\circ$. Subtract the two measures only after expressing both as arc measures.
