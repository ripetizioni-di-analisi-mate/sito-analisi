A quadrilateral is inscribed in a circle. One interior angle measures $120^\circ$. What is the measure of the opposite angle in degrees?

- $60$
- $240$
- $-30$
- $120$

## Explanation

Opposite angles in a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary. Subtract the given angle from one hundred eighty degrees. This gives $60$.
