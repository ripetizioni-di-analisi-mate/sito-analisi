The interior angles of a regular polygon add up to $1440^\circ$. What is the measure of each of its exterior angles?

- $36^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $144^\circ$
- $40^\circ$

## Explanation

The interior angles of a convex polygon with $n$ sides add up to $(n-2)\cdot180^\circ$, so $(n-2)\cdot180^\circ=1440^\circ$ and $n-2=8$. The polygon therefore has $n=10$ sides. The exterior angles of a regular polygon are all equal and add up to $360^\circ$, hence each one measures

$$
\frac{360^\circ}{10}=36^\circ .
$$

The answer $45^\circ$ comes from taking $8$ as the number of sides without adding $2$, while $144^\circ$ is the measure of each interior angle.
