A regular polygon has an exterior angle of $\dfrac{45}{2}^\circ$. How many sides does it have?

- $16$
- $10$
- $12$
- $32$

## Explanation

The exterior angles add up to three hundred sixty degrees. Dividing that sum by one exterior angle gives the number of sides. This gives $16$.
