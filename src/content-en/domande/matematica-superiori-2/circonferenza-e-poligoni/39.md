The number of sides of a regular polygon doubles from $5$ to $10$. By how many degrees does each interior angle increase?

- $36^\circ$
- $18^\circ$
- $72^\circ$
- $108^\circ$

## Explanation

The exterior angles decrease from $360^\circ:5=72^\circ$ to $36^\circ$. Their supplementary interior angles therefore increase by the difference, $36^\circ$.
