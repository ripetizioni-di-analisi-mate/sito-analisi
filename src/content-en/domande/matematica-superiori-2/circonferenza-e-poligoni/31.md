What is the measure of each interior angle of a regular polygon with $12$ sides?

- $150$ degrees
- $151$ degrees
- $149$ degrees
- $300$ degrees

## Explanation

A convex polygon can be divided into two fewer triangles than its number of sides. This determines the sum of the interior angles. In a regular polygon, the angles are equal, so divide the sum by the number of angles.

$$
\Sigma=(12-2)\cdot180^\circ
$$

$$
\alpha=\frac{(12-2)\cdot180^\circ}{12}=150^\circ
$$
