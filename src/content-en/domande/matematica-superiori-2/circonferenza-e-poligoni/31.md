A convex quadrilateral has all sides congruent and an interior angle of $75^\circ$. Which conclusion is justified?

- It is a rhombus and the data rule out regularity
- It is regular because the sides are congruent
- It is regular because $75^\circ$ is acute
- The data are impossible for a rhombus

## Explanation

Four equal sides make the quadrilateral a rhombus. Regularity also requires four equal angles. They add to $360^\circ$, so each would have to measure $90^\circ$. The given angle is $75^\circ$, so the condition fails. A rhombus can have two angles of $75^\circ$ and two of $105^\circ$.
