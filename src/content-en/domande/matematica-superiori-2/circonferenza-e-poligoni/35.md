A tangent segment $PT$ from $P$ is $24$ cm long. The distance $OP$ is $25$ cm. What is the radius?

- $7$ cm
- $1$ cm
- $49$ cm
- $14$ cm

## Explanation

The radius $OT$ is perpendicular to the tangent segment at $T$. The right triangle therefore has hypotenuse $OP$ and known leg $PT$. The square of the radius is $25^2-24^2=(25-24)(25+24)=49$. Take the positive root, $7$ cm. It is less than $OP$, as required for an exterior point.
