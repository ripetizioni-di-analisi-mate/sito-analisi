A tangent is drawn from $P$ to a circle with center $O$ and radius $30\,\mathrm{cm}$. If $OP=50\,\mathrm{cm}$, how long is tangent segment $PT$?

- $40$ cm
- $41$ cm
- $39$ cm
- $80$ cm

## Explanation

The radius to the point of tangency is perpendicular to the tangent. The center, the external point, and the point of contact therefore form a right triangle. The distance from the center is the hypotenuse, so the Pythagorean theorem applies.

$$
OT\perp PT
$$

$$
PT=\sqrt{OP^2-OT^2}=\sqrt{2500-900}=40
$$
