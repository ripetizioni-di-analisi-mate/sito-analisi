Two tangents are drawn from a point outside a circle. One tangent segment measures $5$ cm. What is the sum of the lengths of the two segments in centimetres?

- $20$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$
- $10$

## Explanation

Tangent segments drawn from the same external point are congruent. Add two copies of the given length. This gives $10$.
