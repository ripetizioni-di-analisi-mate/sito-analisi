If the radius is doubled, by what factor is the area of the circle multiplied?

- $2$
- $8$
- $4$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Area is proportional to the square of the radius. The new radius is twice the original radius, so its square is four times as large. The area is also multiplied by four.
