An inscribed angle intercepts an arc of measure $120^\circ$. What is the angle in degrees?

- $120$
- $30$
- $240$
- $60$

## Explanation

The inscribed angle measures half its intercepted arc. Divide the given arc measure by two. This gives $60$.
