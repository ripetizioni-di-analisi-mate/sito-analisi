From three points in the interior of the same major arc $AB$, draw chords to $A$ and $B$. What arc property explains why the three angles are congruent?

- The three vertices are equally spaced from one another
- The three points are collinear
- All the drawn chords are equal in length
- All intercept minor arc $AB$

## Explanation

For each vertex on the major arc, its chords to $A$ and $B$ bound an angle intercepting the minor arc. Each measure is therefore half of the same arc measure. Moving the vertex along the major arc can change individual chord lengths. The required angle relation remains the one with the opposite arc.
