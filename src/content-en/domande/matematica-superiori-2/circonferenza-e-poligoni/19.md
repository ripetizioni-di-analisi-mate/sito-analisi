An annulus has radii $7$ cm and $3$ cm. What is its area in square centimeters?

- $40 \pi$
- $8 \pi$
- $58 \pi$
- $16 \pi$

## Explanation

Subtract the area of the inner disk from the area of the outer disk. Use the difference of the squares of the radii. This gives $40 \pi$.
