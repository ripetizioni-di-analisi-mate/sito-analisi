Two annuli have pairs of inner and outer radii $(3,5)$ cm and $(4,6)$ cm. By how much does the second area exceed the first?

- $4\pi$ cm$^2$
- $0$ cm$^2$
- $2\pi$ cm$^2$
- $11\pi$ cm$^2$

## Explanation

Both radial widths measure $2$ cm. The areas depend on the differences of squares, $25-9=16$ and $36-16=20$. The second exceeds the first by $4\pi$ cm$^2$. At fixed width, the outer square grows relative to the inner one because the sum of the radii increases in the product $(R-r)(R+r)$.
