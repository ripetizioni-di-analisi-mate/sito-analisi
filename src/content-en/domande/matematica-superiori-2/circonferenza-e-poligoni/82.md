In a circle of radius $9$ cm the inscribed angle $\widehat{ACB}$ measures $40^\circ$. How long is the arc $AB$ that it subtends?

- $2\pi$ cm
- $4\pi$ cm
- $8\pi$ cm
- $\pi$ cm

## Explanation

The central angle subtending the same arc is twice the inscribed angle, so the arc measures $80^\circ$. The arc is therefore the fraction $\dfrac{80^\circ}{360^\circ}=\dfrac29$ of the circumference, whose length is $2\pi\cdot9=18\pi$ cm. Hence

$$
\ell=\frac29\cdot18\pi=4\pi\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$

The answer $2\pi$ cm comes from using $40^\circ$ as the measure of the arc, without doubling it.
