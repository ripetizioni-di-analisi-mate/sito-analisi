An arc is $7\pi$ cm long in a circle of radius $7$ cm. What is the central angle in degrees?

- $90$
- $180$
- $360$
- $60$

## Explanation

The full circumference measures $14\pi$ cm. The arc is half that length and corresponds to half a full turn. This gives $180$.
