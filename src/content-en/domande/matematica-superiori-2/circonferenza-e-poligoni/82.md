A wheel of radius $20$ cm rolls without slipping. How many revolutions has it completed after travelling $\pi$ metres?

- $5$
- $\dfrac52$
- $\dfrac54$
- $\dfrac25$

## Explanation

One revolution covers $40\pi$ cm, or $0.4\pi$ m. The number of revolutions is $\dfrac{\pi}{0.4\pi}=\dfrac52$.
