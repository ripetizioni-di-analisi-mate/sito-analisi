A sector of radius $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ has a central angle of $90^\circ$. What is its perimeter?

- $4 \pi\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4 \pi + 16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8 + 4 \pi\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16 \pi\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The arc is a quarter of the circumference, so its length is $4\pi$. Two radii complete the boundary of the sector, adding $16$ centimeters to the curved part.

$$
\begin{aligned}
p&=\frac14(2\pi\cdot8)+2\cdot8=4\pi+16
\end{aligned}
$$
