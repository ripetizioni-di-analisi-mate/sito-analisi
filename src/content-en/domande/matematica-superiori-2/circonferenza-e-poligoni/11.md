Point $P$ is $4$ cm from the centre of a circle of radius $5$ cm. How many lines through $P$ are tangent to the circle?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- Infinitely many

## Explanation

Point $P$ is inside because its distance from the centre is less than the radius. Any line through that point has distance from the centre at most $4$ cm. A tangent would require a distance of $5$ cm. Every line through $P$ is therefore a secant and meets the circle at two points.
