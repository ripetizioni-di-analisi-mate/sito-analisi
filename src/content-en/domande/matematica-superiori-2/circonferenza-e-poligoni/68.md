A chord is $32$ cm long and the radius is $20$ cm. How far is the chord from the center in centimeters?

- $4$
- $20$
- $12$
- $16$

## Explanation

The perpendicular from the center bisects the chord. With the radius and half-chord, use the Pythagorean theorem to find the other leg. This gives $12$.
