What is the measure of an inscribed angle that intercepts a diameter?

- $180^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $360^\circ$

## Explanation

The corresponding central angle is a straight angle. An inscribed angle on the same arc has half its measure. The result is ninety degrees.
