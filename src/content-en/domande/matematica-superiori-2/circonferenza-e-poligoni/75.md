Which segment in the figure is the apothem of the regular hexagon?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 xmin=-4.65 xmax=4.65 ymin=-4.62 ymax=4.62 curve="4*cos(t),4*sin(t),0,6.28318530718" linee="4.000000,0.000000 2.000000,3.464102 -2.000000,3.464102 -4.000000,0.000000 -2.000000,-3.464102 2.000000,-3.464102 4.000000,0.000000;0,0 4,0;0,0 2,3.464102;0,0 3,1.732051" punti="0,0;4,0;2,3.464102;3,1.732051" etichette="-0.27,-0.35,O;4.32,-0.29,A;2.16,3.82,B;3.21,1.98,H" titolo="$H$ is the foot of the perpendicular on side $AB$"}

- $OA$
- $OB$
- $AB$
- $OH$

## Explanation

The apothem measures the perpendicular distance from the centre to a side. In the figure $H$ is the foot on $AB$, so the required segment is $OH$. Segments $OA$ and $OB$ reach vertices and are radii of the circumcircle. Segment $AB$ is the polygon side and the base of the central triangle.
