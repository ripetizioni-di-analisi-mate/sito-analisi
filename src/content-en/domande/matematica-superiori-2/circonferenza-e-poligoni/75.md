What is the measure in degrees of each exterior angle of a regular polygon with $10$ sides?

- $60$
- $144$
- $18$
- $36$

## Explanation

The exterior angles, one at each vertex and taken in the same direction, add up to three hundred sixty degrees. For a regular polygon, divide by the number of sides. This gives $36$.
