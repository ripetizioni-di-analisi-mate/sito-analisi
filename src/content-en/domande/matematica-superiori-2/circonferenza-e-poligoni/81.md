An arc is $5\pi$ cm long in a circle of radius $5$ cm. What is the central angle in degrees?

- $180$
- $60$
- $90$
- $360$

## Explanation

The full circumference measures $10\pi$ cm. The arc is half that length and corresponds to half a full turn. This gives $180$.
