A sector has radius $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and an arc length of $4\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the sector’s area?

- $12 \pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $4 \pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $18 \pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $24 \pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The arc is one third of the circumference, since $\dfrac{4\pi}{12\pi}=\dfrac13$. The sector also occupies one third of the disk, whose area is $36\pi$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A&=\frac13\cdot36\pi=12\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$$
