An inscribed angle measures $55^\circ$. What is the measure of the central angle that intercepts the same arc?

- $111$ degrees
- $109$ degrees
- $220$ degrees
- $110$ degrees

## Explanation

The central angle is twice an inscribed angle intercepting the same arc. Double the given measure. Both measures remain in degrees.

$$
\beta=2\cdot55^\circ=110^\circ
$$
