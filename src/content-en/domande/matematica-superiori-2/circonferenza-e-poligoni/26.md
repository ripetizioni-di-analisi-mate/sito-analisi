The inscribed angle $\widehat{ACB}$ measures $112^\circ$. What is the measure of minor arc $AB$?

- $224^\circ$
- $112^\circ$
- $56^\circ$
- $136^\circ$

## Explanation

Doubling the inscribed angle gives the arc that avoids the vertex, of measure $224^\circ$. Since it exceeds a semicircle, this is the major arc. Subtract from $360^\circ$ to obtain the minor arc, $136^\circ$. The given angle intercepts the major arc, so the first doubling only answers the question about the intercepted arc.
