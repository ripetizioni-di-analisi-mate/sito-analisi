A chord $16\,\mathrm{cm}$ long is $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center. What is the diameter?

- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4 \sqrt{73}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $28\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The perpendicular from the center reaches the midpoint of the chord. The half-chord and the perpendicular are the legs, so their relation to the hypotenuse gives the radius. Double that value for the diameter.

$$
\begin{aligned}
r&=\sqrt{8^2+6^2}=10 \\[5pt]
D&=2r=20\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
