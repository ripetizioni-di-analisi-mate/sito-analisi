An inscribed angle is between $39.5^\circ$ and $40.5^\circ$, endpoints included. How far from $80^\circ$ could the central angle subtending the same arc be, at most?

- $0.5^\circ$
- $2^\circ$
- $40^\circ$
- $1^\circ$

## Explanation

Doubling each endpoint gives the interval from $79^\circ$ to $81^\circ$. Both endpoints are $1^\circ$ from the central estimate.
