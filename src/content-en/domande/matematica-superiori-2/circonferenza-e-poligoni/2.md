Points $A$, $B$ and $C$ lie on a circle with centre $O$, and the inscribed angle $\widehat{ACB}$ measures $35^\circ$. What is the measure of the angle $\widehat{OAB}$ of triangle $OAB$?

- $35^\circ$
- $70^\circ$
- $72.5^\circ$
- $55^\circ$

## Explanation

The central angle $\widehat{AOB}$ subtends the same arc as $\widehat{ACB}$, so it is twice as large, that is $70^\circ$. Triangle $OAB$ is isosceles, because $OA$ and $OB$ are radii, therefore the base angles $\widehat{OAB}$ and $\widehat{OBA}$ are equal. The angles of a triangle add up to $180^\circ$, hence

$$
\widehat{OAB}=\frac{180^\circ-70^\circ}{2}=55^\circ .
$$

The answer $72.5^\circ$ comes from subtracting the inscribed angle from $180^\circ$ instead of the central angle.
