An inscribed angle measures $40^\circ$. What is the measure of the central angle that intercepts the same arc?

- $81$ degrees
- $79$ degrees
- $160$ degrees
- $80$ degrees

## Explanation

The central angle is twice an inscribed angle intercepting the same arc. Double the given measure. Both measures remain in degrees.

$$
\beta=2\cdot40^\circ=80^\circ
$$
