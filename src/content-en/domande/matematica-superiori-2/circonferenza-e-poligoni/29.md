A circle of radius $7$ cm is translated from centre $O$ to centre $O'$ without changing the radius. What follows about its circumference?

- It increases by $OO'$
- It halves
- It stays $14\pi$ cm
- It stays $49\pi$ cm

## Explanation

Translation moves the points while preserving distances, and so leaves the radius unchanged. The circumference formula depends only on that radius and gives $2\pi\cdot7=14\pi$ cm. The distance between the centres describes how far the figure moved. The value $49\pi$ comes from the area, which uses square units.
