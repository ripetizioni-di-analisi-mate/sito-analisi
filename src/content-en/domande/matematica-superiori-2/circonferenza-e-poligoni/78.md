A sector has area $30\pi$ cm$^2$ and arc length $10\pi$ cm. What is its radius?

- $3$ cm
- $6$ cm
- $12$ cm
- $20$ cm

## Explanation

Eliminating the fraction of a turn between the arc length and sector area formulas gives $A=\dfrac{r\ell}{2}$. Thus $r=2A/\ell=6$ cm. An area divided by a length has units of length. The given arc is shorter than the full circumference of $12\pi$ cm, so the radius is compatible with a sector.
