A sector with radius $9$ cm has a central angle of $90^\circ$. What is its area in square centimeters?

- $\dfrac{81 \pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{81 \pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{9 \pi}{2}$
- $81 \pi$

## Explanation

The sector occupies a quarter of the disk. Divide the area of the disk, pi times the square of the radius, by four. This gives $\dfrac{81 \pi}{4}$.
