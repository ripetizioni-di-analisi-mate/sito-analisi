A chord of length $24$ cm is drawn in each of two circles of radii $13$ cm and $15$ cm. What are its distances from the two centres in that order?

- $9$ cm and $5$ cm
- Both $5$ cm
- Both $9$ cm
- $5$ cm and $9$ cm

## Explanation

The half-chord is $12$ cm in both cases. The right triangles have hypotenuses of $13$ cm and $15$ cm. The square of the perpendicular leg is $169-144=25$ in the first, and $225-144=81$ in the second. Equal chord lengths do not imply equal distances when the radii differ.
