A chord is $48$ cm long and the radius is $30$ cm. How far is the chord from the center in centimeters?

- $30$
- $6$
- $24$
- $18$

## Explanation

The perpendicular from the center bisects the chord. With the radius and half-chord, use the Pythagorean theorem to find the other leg. This gives $18$.
