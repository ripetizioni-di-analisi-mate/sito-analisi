A regular polygon has an exterior angle of $30^\circ$. How many sides does it have?

- $24$
- $-12$
- $8$
- $12$

## Explanation

The exterior angles add up to three hundred sixty degrees. Dividing that sum by one exterior angle gives the number of sides. This gives $12$.
