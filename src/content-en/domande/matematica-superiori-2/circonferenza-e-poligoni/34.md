In a regular polygon, three consecutive central sectors together cover $108^\circ$. How many sides does the polygon have?

- $3$
- $30$
- $12$
- $10$

## Explanation

The central sector between consecutive vertices is one third of the given measure, or $36^\circ$. A full turn contains $360^\circ/36^\circ=10$ such sectors, one per side. Dividing directly by $108^\circ$ would count groups of three instead of the individual sectors matching the sides. Regularity guarantees all three measures are equal.
