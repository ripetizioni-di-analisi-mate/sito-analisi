An annulus has radii $5$ cm and $2$ cm. What is its area in square centimetres?

- $9 \pi$
- $6 \pi$
- $29 \pi$
- $21 \pi$

## Explanation

Subtract the area of the inner disk from the area of the outer disk. Use the difference of the squares of the radii. This gives $21 \pi$.
