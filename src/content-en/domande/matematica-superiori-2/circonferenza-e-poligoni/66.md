A chord is $16$ cm long and the radius is $10$ cm. How far is the chord from the center in centimeters?

- $2$
- $8$
- $10$
- $6$

## Explanation

The perpendicular from the center bisects the chord. With the radius and half-chord, use the Pythagorean theorem to find the other leg. This gives $6$.
