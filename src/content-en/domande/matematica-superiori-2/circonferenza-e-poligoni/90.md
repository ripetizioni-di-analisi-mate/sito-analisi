A square of side $6$ cm is inscribed in a circle. What is the area of the circle?

- $9\pi$ cm$^2$
- $36\pi$ cm$^2$
- $72\pi$ cm$^2$
- $18\pi$ cm$^2$

## Explanation

The vertices of the square lie on the circle and the angle at each vertex is a right angle, so each diagonal of the square is a diameter. By the Pythagorean theorem the diagonal measures $\sqrt{6^2+6^2}=6\sqrt2$ cm, therefore the radius is $3\sqrt2$ cm. The area of the circle is hence

$$
\pi\,\bigl(3\sqrt2\bigr)^2=18\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2 .
$$

The answer $9\pi$ cm$^2$ comes from taking half the side as the radius, which is the radius of the circle inscribed in the square.
