A circle has a chord $AB$ of length $16$ cm, at a distance of $6$ cm from its centre. How far from the centre must another chord of the same length lie?

- $8$ cm
- $10$ cm
- $12$ cm
- $6$ cm

## Explanation

Perpendiculars from the centre bisect chords. Both right triangles have the same radius and half-chord, so Pythagoras gives the same distance from the centre.
