A disk has area $100\pi$ cm$^2$. What is its diameter in decimetres?

- $1$ dm
- $20$ dm
- $\dfrac15$ dm
- $2$ dm

## Explanation

Divide by $\pi$ and take the positive square root to find radius $10$ cm. The diameter is therefore $20$ cm, or $2$ dm. You can convert the area first, giving $\pi$ dm$^2$, because one square decimetre equals a hundred square centimetres. In those units the radius is $1$ dm and the diameter twice that.
