For which $k$ are the rows $3x-6y=9$ and $x-2y=k$ equivalent, so the system has infinitely many solutions?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $9$
- $0$

## Explanation

The first row’s coefficients are three times the coefficients in the second row. For the lines to coincide, the same factor must multiply the whole equation. So the constants must satisfy $3k=9$, giving $k=3$. For any other $k$, elimination gives a contradiction that no pair can satisfy.
