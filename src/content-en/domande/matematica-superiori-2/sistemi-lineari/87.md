Two groups cover $34$ km in total. Their distances become equal after the first transfers $3$ km of its route to the second. What was the first group’s original distance?

- $17$ km
- $14$ km
- $23$ km
- $20$ km

## Explanation

Let $x,y$ be the original distances. The sum remains thirty-four. After the transfer $x-3=y+3$, so $x-y=6$. Adding the two conditions gives $2x=40$. The first group originally had twenty kilometers and the second fourteen. After the transfer both cover seventeen kilometers.
