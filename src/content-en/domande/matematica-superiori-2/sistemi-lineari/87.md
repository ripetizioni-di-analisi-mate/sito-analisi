Using $x+y+z=14$ and $x+y=10$, find the value of $z$.

- $3$
- $14$
- $7$
- $4$

## Explanation

Subtract the second equation from the first. The terms in $x$ and $y$ cancel, leaving only the requested unknown. This gives $4$.
