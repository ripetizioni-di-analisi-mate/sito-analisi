A group buys $12$ tickets at $5$ or $3$ euros each. Which of the following totals is possible?

- $46$ euros
- $43$ euros
- $35$ euros
- $61$ euros

## Explanation

If $i$ is the number of tickets costing $5$ euros, the total cost is $5i+3(12-i)=36+2i$, with $i$ an integer between $0$ and $12$. For $i=5$, the cost is $46$ euros. The cost is even and ranges from $36$ to $60$ euros.
