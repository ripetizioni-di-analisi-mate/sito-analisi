Which pair satisfies both $y=6x-1$ and $x+y=90$?

- $\left( 12, \  77\right)$
- $\left( 13, \  77\right)$
- $\left( 13, \  79\right)$
- $\left( 6, \  13\right)$

## Explanation

Substituting the first expression for $y$ into the second equation gives an equation with coefficient $7$ for $x$. Substitute the value obtained back into the first equation. The result is $(6+1)x=91$, so $x=13$. It follows that $y=6\cdot 13-1=77$.
