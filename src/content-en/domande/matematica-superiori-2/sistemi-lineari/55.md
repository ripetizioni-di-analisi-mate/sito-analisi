Using only reversible operations, both equations of a system in $x,y$ reduce to $0=0$. What is its solution set?

- $\varnothing$
- $\mathbb R^2$
- $\{(0;0)\}$
- $\{(t;t):t\in\mathbb R\}$

## Explanation

Every pair satisfies both reduced equalities. Because the steps are all reversible, every pair also satisfies the original system. Two identities impose no relation between $x$ and $y$. This distinguishes the case where both coordinates are free from the case where the solution set is a line.
