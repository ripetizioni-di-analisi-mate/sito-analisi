Starting from $\dfrac{x-1}{3}+\dfrac{y}{2}=3$, what equation results from multiplying both sides by $6$ and collecting terms?

- $2x+3y=18$
- $2x+3y=16$
- $2x+3y=20$
- $3x+2y=21$

## Explanation

The factors $\dfrac63=2$ and $\dfrac62=3$ give $2(x-1)+3y=18$. Since $2(x-1)=2x-2$, add $2$ to both sides to obtain $2x+3y=20$.

To reverse the step, subtract two from the constant and recover $2(x-1)+3y$ on the left. Then divide the whole equation by six. The reverse step shows that both rows have the same solutions.
