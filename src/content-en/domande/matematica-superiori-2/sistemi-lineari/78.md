A total of $25$ tickets were sold. A full-price ticket costs $7$ euros and a reduced-price ticket costs $4$ euros. Revenue is $136$ euros. Which system uses $x$ for full-price tickets and $y$ for reduced-price tickets?

- $\begin{cases}x+y=136\\7x+4y=25\end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases}x+y=25\\7x+4y=136\end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases}x+y=25\\4x+7y=136\end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases}7x+4y=25\\x-y=136\end{cases}$

## Explanation

Ticket counts add the variables directly. Revenue multiplies each quantity by its price, so $7x$ and $4y$ are amounts in euros. Substituting $y=25-x$ gives $3x=36$. The values $x=12$ and $y=13$ give $25$ tickets and $136$ euros as required.
