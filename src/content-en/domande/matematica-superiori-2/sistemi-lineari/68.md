The first two rows are $x+y=5$ and $x-y=1$. Which option can be added as a third row without changing the system?

- $3x+y=11$
- $3x+y=10$
- $3x-y=13$
- $x+3y=7$

## Explanation

The first row is doubled and the second is added. The left side is $3x+y$ and the right side is $2\cdot5+1=11$. Any solution of the first two rows satisfies the third constructed this way. As a check, the only original pair is $(3;2)$, giving $3\cdot3+2=11$.
