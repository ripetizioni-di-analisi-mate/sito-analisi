How are the lines $x+4y=9$ and $2x+8y=19$ related?

- Parallel and distinct.
- Perpendicular.
- Coincident.
- Intersecting at exactly one point.

## Explanation

The coefficients of $x$ and $y$ are proportional, but the constant term is not doubled. The lines are therefore parallel and distinct.
