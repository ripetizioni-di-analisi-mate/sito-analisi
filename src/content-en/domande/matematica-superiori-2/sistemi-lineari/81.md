A group buys $7$ tickets. Full-price tickets cost $5$ euros and discounted tickets cost $3$ euros. The group spends $25$ euros. How many tickets are full-price?

- $4$
- $7$
- $2$
- $5$

## Explanation

If all tickets were discounted, they would cost $21$ euros. Each full-price ticket adds two euros, so divide the difference in cost by two. This gives $2$.
