The point $(2,4)$ is a solution of the system given by $x+y=6$ and $kx+y=9$. What is $k$?

- $\dfrac{13}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$

## Explanation

The first equation is already satisfied. Substitute the coordinates into the second equation and isolate $k$ in the equation $2k+4=9$. This gives $\dfrac{5}{2}$.
