For $\dfrac x2-\dfrac y4=1$ and $x+y=7$, what is the horizontal coordinate of the solution?

- $\dfrac73$
- $\dfrac{11}{3}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}$
- $4$

## Explanation

Multiplying the first row by four gives $2x-y=4$. Adding this to the second eliminates $y$ and leaves $3x=11$. The vertical coordinate is $7-\dfrac{11}{3}=\dfrac{10}{3}$. The question asks for the first coordinate, and the two coordinates add to seven as required.
