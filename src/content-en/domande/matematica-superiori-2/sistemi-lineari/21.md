Start with $3x+2y=7$ and $5x+4y=13$. Subtract twice the first row from the second. What equation remains?

- $-x=-1$
- $-x=6$
- $x=-1$
- $11x=27$

## Explanation

Twice the first equation has terms $6x+4y$ and constant $14$. Subtraction yields $(5-6)x+(4-4)y=13-14$, hence $-x=-1$. The right side must undergo the same operation as the coefficients. Keeping the first row then gives $x=1$ and $y=2$.
