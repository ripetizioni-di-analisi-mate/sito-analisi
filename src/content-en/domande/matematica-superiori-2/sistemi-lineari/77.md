Keep $x+y+z=6$. Remove the $x$ term from the second row $2x-y+z=4$ and the third row $x+3y-z=2$. Which rows result from subtracting the needed multiples of the first?

- $\begin{cases}-3y-z=-8\\2y-2z=-4\end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases}-3y-z=-2\\2y-2z=-4\end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases}-3y-z=-8\\2y=-4\end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases}-y-z=-8\\2y-2z=-4\end{cases}$

## Explanation

The second row has coefficient two on $x$, so subtract two copies of the first. Its constant becomes $4-12=-8$. Subtract one copy from the third row, whose constant becomes $2-6=-4$. The coefficient of $z$ in the third row becomes $-1-1=-2$. Keeping the first row makes both steps reversible.
