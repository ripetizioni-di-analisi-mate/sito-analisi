What is the determinant of the coefficient matrix of the system $\begin{cases}2x+y=5\\x+3y=6\end{cases}$?

- $7$
- $5$
- $10$
- $-5$

## Explanation

Subtract the product of the entries on the other diagonal from the product of the entries on the main diagonal. The constant terms do not enter this determinant. This gives $5$.

The main diagonal uses $2\cdot3$ and the other $1\cdot1$. Changing the constants could change the solution pair but would leave the requested determinant unchanged.
