How are the lines $x+2y=5$ and $2x+4y=11$ related?

- Perpendicular.
- Parallel and distinct.
- Coincident.
- Intersecting at exactly one point.

## Explanation

The coefficients of $x$ and $y$ are proportional, but the constant term is not doubled. The lines are therefore parallel and distinct.

Isolating $y$ in both equations, the slope is $-\dfrac12$ in each case, but the vertical intercepts are $\dfrac52$ and $\dfrac{11}{4}$. No point can lie on both.
