In the two equations $2x+y+z=10$ and $2x+y-z=4$, which value of $z$ is already forced?

- $6$
- $7$
- $3$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The groups $2x+y$ match. Subtracting the second equation from the first eliminates both $x$ and $y$. The left side becomes $z-(-z)=2z$ and the right side is $10-4=6$. Thus $z=3$. The condition $2x+y=7$ remains, so the other two coordinates can vary subject to that equation.
