Do the lines $-2x+4y=6$ and $x-2y=-1$ have a common point?

- Exactly one, because the constants differ.
- None, because the first row gives $x-2y=-3$.
- Infinitely many, because the coefficients are proportional.
- Exactly one, at the origin.

## Explanation

The first equation is divided by $-2$. Its left-side coefficients become those of the second, but its constant becomes $-3$. The expression $x-2y$ cannot be negative three and negative one at the same time. On a graph the lines have the same slope and different intercepts.
