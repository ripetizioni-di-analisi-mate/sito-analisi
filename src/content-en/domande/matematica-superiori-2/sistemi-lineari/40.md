Given the equations $x+y+z=7$ and $2x+2y+2z=14$, what new information does the second provide?

- $x=2$.
- $y=z$.
- The system has no solution.
- None, because it is twice the first.

## Explanation

Multiplying the first equation by $2$ gives exactly the left and right sides of the second. Every triple that satisfies the first therefore satisfies the second.

For example $(7;0;0)$ and $(3;1;3)$ already satisfy both. Choose the first two coordinates freely and recover the third from $z=7-x-y$. The second equation does not reduce those possibilities.
