The three digits of a number add up to $13$. The hundreds digit is twice the units digit, and the tens digit exceeds the units digit by $1$. What is the number?

- $346$
- $634$
- $463$
- $643$

## Explanation

Let $a$, $b$ and $c$ be the hundreds, tens and units digits. The conditions to be satisfied together form the system
$$
\begin{cases}a+b+c=13\\a=2c\\b=c+1\end{cases}
$$
Substituting the last two equations into the first gives $2c+(c+1)+c=13$, that is $4c=12$, so $c=3$. Then $a=2\cdot3=6$ and $b=3+1=4$, so the number is $643$.
