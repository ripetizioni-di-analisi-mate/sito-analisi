Two sensors record $x+y$ and $2x+y$. Both readings rise by $2$ on the next measurement. Which changes in $x$ and $y$ are consistent with the readings?

- $x$ increased by $2$ and $y$ unchanged
- Both increased by $1$
- $x$ decreased by $2$ and $y$ increased by $4$
- $x$ unchanged and $y$ increased by $2$

## Explanation

Subtracting the first reading from the second gives $x$. The equal increases cancel in this difference, so $x$ stays fixed. The first reading then forces $y$ to rise by $2$.
