Using $x+y+z=10$ and $x+y=7$, find the value of $z$.

- $5$
- $2$
- $10$
- $3$

## Explanation

Subtract the second equation from the first. The terms in $x$ and $y$ cancel, leaving only the requested unknown. This gives $3$.
