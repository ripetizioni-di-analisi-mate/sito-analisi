From $4x+4y+2z=34$ and $x+y=7$, which value of $z$ is forced?

- $5$
- $2$
- $10$
- $3$

## Explanation

Multiply the second equation by four to get $4x+4y=28$. Subtracting it from the first gives $2z=6$, so $z=3$. The same conclusion follows by treating $4(x+y)$ as twenty-eight. The sum of $x$ and $y$ is fixed, but their individual values are not needed.
