Solve $\begin{cases}\dfrac x2+\dfrac y3=19\\\dfrac x2-\dfrac y3=-7\end{cases}$.

- $\left( 6, \  13\right)$
- $\left( 18, \  26\right)$
- $\left( 26, \  18\right)$
- $\left( 12, \  39\right)$

## Explanation

Adding gives $x=12$. Subtracting the second equation from the first gives $\dfrac{2y}{3}=26$.
