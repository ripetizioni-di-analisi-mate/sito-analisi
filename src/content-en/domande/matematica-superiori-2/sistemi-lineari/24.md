Which operation can change the solution set of a linear system?

- Swap the two equations.
- Multiply a whole row by negative two.
- Subtract the first row from the second while keeping the first.
- Multiply all terms of one row by zero.

## Explanation

Multiplying by zero turns any row into $0=0$, true for every pair. Division by zero cannot recover the lost information. A swap is reversed by another swap. Multiplying by negative two and replacing one row by subtraction also have inverse operations.
