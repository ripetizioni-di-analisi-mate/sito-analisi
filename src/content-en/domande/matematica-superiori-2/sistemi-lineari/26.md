What is $y$ if $7x+2y=1$ and $-7x+4y=11$?

- $3$
- $2$
- $-2$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$

## Explanation

The two rows already have opposite coefficients on $x$, so adding them leaves $6y=12$. Thus $y=2$ before $x$ is known. Substitution into the first equation gives $x=-\dfrac37$. This checks the second row because $-7x=3$ and $4y=8$, adding to eleven.
