When does the system $\begin{cases}kx+2y=8\\3x+y=4\end{cases}$ have infinitely many solutions?

- $k=3$
- $k=-6$
- $k=0$
- $k=6$

## Explanation

Subtracting twice the second equation from the first gives $(k-6)x=0$. When $k=6$, this becomes $0=0$, and only one linear condition in two unknowns remains, so there are infinitely many solutions.
