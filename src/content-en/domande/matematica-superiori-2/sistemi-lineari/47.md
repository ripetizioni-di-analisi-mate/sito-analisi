Solve $\begin{cases}x+y=10\\x-y=-4\end{cases}$.

- $\left( 10, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 7, \  3\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  7\right)$
- $\left( -3, \  7\right)$

## Explanation

Adding the equations eliminates $y$ and gives $2x=6$. The first equation then gives $y$. Substituting $x=3$ into the sum relation $3+y=10$ gives $y=7$.
