For $2x+3y=1$ and $5x+y=9$, what is $D_y$ in Cramer’s rule?

- $-13$
- $-26$
- $13$
- $23$

## Explanation

To calculate $D_y$, keep the column of $x$ coefficients, $2$ and $5$. Replace the $y$ column with the constants $1$ and $9$. This gives $2\cdot9-1\cdot5=13$. Dividing by $D=2\cdot1-3\cdot5=-13$ gives $y=-1$.
