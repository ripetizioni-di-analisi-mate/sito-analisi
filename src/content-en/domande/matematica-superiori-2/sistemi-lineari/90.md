What value of $y$ is obtained from $x+y+z=9$, $x+y-z=3$, and $x-y=2$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Adding the first two equations gives $2x+2y=12$, so $x+y=6$. Subtracting $x-y=2$ gives $2y=4$, so $y=2$.

With that value, $x+y=6$ gives $x=4$. The first equation gives $z=3$, and the second checks the triple through $4+2-3=3$. All three conditions are recovered.
