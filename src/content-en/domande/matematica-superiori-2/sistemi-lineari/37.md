Which of the following pairs solves $\begin{cases}2x-y=4\\4x-2y=8\end{cases}$?

- $(3;2)$
- $(4;2)$
- $(2;4)$
- $(0;4)$

## Explanation

The second equation is twice the first, so checking $2x-y=4$ is sufficient. The pair $(3;2)$ gives $2\cdot3-2=4$.
