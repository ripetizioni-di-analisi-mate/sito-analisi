For the system $\dfrac{x-4}{x-4}+y=3$, $x+y=7$, what is the solution in the original domain?

- $(4;3)$
- $(2;5)$
- $(5;2)$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The denominator excludes $x=4$. On the domain the fraction equals one, so the first equation gives $y=2$. The second fixes $x=5$, which is allowed. Checking the original form gives $\dfrac11$ for the fraction, three for the first left side, and seven for the sum of the coordinates.
