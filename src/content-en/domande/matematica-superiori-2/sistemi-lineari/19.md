Solve $\begin{cases}\dfrac x2+\dfrac y3=10\\\dfrac x2-\dfrac y3=-4\end{cases}$.

- $\left( 9, \  14\right)$
- $\left( 14, \  9\right)$
- $\left( 6, \  21\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  7\right)$

## Explanation

Adding gives $x=6$. Subtracting the second equation from the first gives $\dfrac{2y}{3}=14$.
