How many solutions does $\begin{cases}x+3y=7\\2x+6y=14\end{cases}$ have?

- None.
- One.
- Infinitely many.
- Two.

## Explanation

The second equation is exactly twice the first. Both equations describe the same line, which contains infinitely many points.
