In the system $4x+3y=11$, $x-5y=2$, which relation directly isolates one variable without introducing fractions?

- $x=2-5y$
- $x=2+5y$
- $y=2+5x$
- $4x=11+3y$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $x$ in the second equation is one. Add $5y$ to both sides to get $x=2+5y$. This expression can replace $x$ directly in the first row. The choice avoids a denominator while you set up the equation in one variable.
