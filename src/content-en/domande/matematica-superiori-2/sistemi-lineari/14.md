In the system $4x+3y=11$, $x-5y=2$, which relation directly isolates one variable without introducing fractions?

- $x=2-5y$
- $x=2+5y$
- $y=2+5x$
- $4x=11+3y$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $x$ in the second equation is one. Adding $5y$ to both sides gives $x=2+5y$. This expression can replace $x$ directly in the first row. This choice avoids a denominator while the equation in one variable is set up.
