Two notebooks and one pen cost $11$ euros. One notebook and three pens cost $13$ euros. How much does a pen cost?

- $4$ euros
- $2$ euros
- $3$ euros
- $5$ euros

## Explanation

Let $q$ be the price of the notebook and $p$ the price of the pen. The equations are $2q+p=11$ and $q+3p=13$. Subtracting the first equation from twice the second gives $5p=26-11=15$. The pen therefore costs $3$ euros.
