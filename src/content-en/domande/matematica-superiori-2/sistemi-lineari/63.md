Solve the system $\dfrac{x^2-1}{x-1}+y=4$, $x-y=-1$ with its original conditions.

- $\{(1;2)\}$
- $\{(2;1)\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\{(0;1)\}$

## Explanation

Before canceling, exclude horizontal coordinate $1$. Factor $x^2-1=(x-1)(x+1)$ to get $x+y=3$. Adding $x-y=-1$ gives $x=1$, hence $y=2$. This is the reduced system’s only candidate, but it makes the original denominator zero. The requested solution set is empty.
