Using Cramer’s rule you know $D=6$ and $D_x=-3$. You are asked for both coordinates. What additional numerical datum is sufficient?

- Only the sign of $D_y$.
- Only knowing that $D_x$ is nonzero.
- The number of equations in the system.
- The determinant $D_y$.

## Explanation

The horizontal coordinate is already fixed by $\dfrac{D_x}{D}=-\dfrac12$. The vertical coordinate uses the same denominator and the numerator $D_y$. Its sign alone tells which side of the horizontal axis the point lies on, but leaves the distance undetermined. The numerical value of $D_y$ gives the missing coordinate.
