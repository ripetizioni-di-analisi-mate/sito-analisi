Using $x+y+z=22$ and $x+y=16$, find the value of $z$.

- $11$
- $22$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

Subtract the second equation from the first. The terms in $x$ and $y$ cancel, leaving only the requested unknown. This gives $6$.
