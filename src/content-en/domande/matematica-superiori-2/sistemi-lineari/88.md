One line passes through $(0;4)$ and $(4;0)$. The other passes through $(0;0)$ and $(2;4)$. What is the intersection?

- $\left(\dfrac43;\dfrac83\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac83;\dfrac43\right)$
- $(2;2)$
- $(0;4)$

## Explanation

The first line is $y=4-x$ because the vertical coordinate falls by one when the horizontal coordinate rises by one. The second goes through the origin and rises by four over two horizontal units, so $y=2x$. Equating the vertical coordinates gives $3x=4$ and then $y=\dfrac83$. Both formulas give that output at the same input.
