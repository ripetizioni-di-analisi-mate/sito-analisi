Using $x+y+z=18$ and $x+y=13$, find the value of $z$.

- $5$
- $4$
- $18$
- $9$

## Explanation

Subtract the second equation from the first. The terms in $x$ and $y$ cancel, leaving only the requested unknown. This gives $5$.
