Keep $2x+3y=7$ and add twice this equation to the row $-4x+y=1$. What is the new second equation?

- $7y=15$
- $7y=8$
- $4y=15$
- $-8x-5y=-13$

## Explanation

Doubling the retained row gives $4x+6y=14$. Add it to the second row. The coefficients on $x$ cancel, leaving $y+6y=1+14$. Keep the first row so that the second can be reconstructed if needed. Subtracting the same doubled row from the new second recovers the original one.
