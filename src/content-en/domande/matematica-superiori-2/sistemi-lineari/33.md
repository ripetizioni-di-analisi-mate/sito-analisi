The point $(2,3)$ is a solution of the system given by $x+y=5$ and $kx+y=7$. What is $k$?

- $2$
- $-2$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

The first equation is already satisfied. Substitute the coordinates into the second equation and isolate $k$ in the equation $2k+3=7$. This gives $2$.
