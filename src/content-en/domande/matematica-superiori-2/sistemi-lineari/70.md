Where do the lines given by $x=2$ and $y=3x-1$ meet?

- $(5;2)$
- $(2;7)$
- $(1;2)$
- $(2;5)$

## Explanation

Every point on the first line has x-coordinate $2$. At the intersection, substitute $x=2$ into the other equation to obtain $y=3\cdot2-1=5$.
