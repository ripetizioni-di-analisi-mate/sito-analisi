For which value of $k$ does the system $\begin{cases}kx+y=1\\7x+y=2\end{cases}$ have no solution?

- $8$
- $7$
- $6$
- $14$

## Explanation

When the determinant is nonzero, the system has a unique solution. When it is zero, the equations have the same left side and different constant terms. The system therefore has no solution for that value.

$$
D=k-7
$$

$$
D=0\iff k=7
$$

$$
1=2\quad\text{is impossible}
$$

Substitute the singular value $k=7$ into the original rows. Both constrain the same expression $7x+y$, but one sets it to one and the other sets it to two.
