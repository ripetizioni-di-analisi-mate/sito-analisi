The point $(2,6)$ is a solution of the system given by $x+y=8$ and $kx+y=13$. What is $k$?

- $7$
- $- \dfrac{7}{2}$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $\dfrac{19}{2}$

## Explanation

The first equation is already satisfied. Substitute the coordinates into the second equation and isolate $k$ in the equation $2k+6=13$. This gives $\dfrac{7}{2}$.
