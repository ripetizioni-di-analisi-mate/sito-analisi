What is the coefficient determinant of the system formed by $3x-2y=11$ and $-x+4y=2$?

- $14$
- $12$
- $10$
- $-10$

## Explanation

The coefficients are written in the order $x,y$ for both rows. The main diagonal gives $3\cdot4=12$. The other gives $(-2)(-1)=2$, a positive product. Their difference is $12-2=10$. Since the determinant is nonzero, Cramer’s rule applies, and it guarantees exactly one solution.
