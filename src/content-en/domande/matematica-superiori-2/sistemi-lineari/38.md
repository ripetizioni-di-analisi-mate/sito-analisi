The point $(1;2)$ lies on the line $kx-y=1$. For that value of $k$, where does the line meet the $x$-axis?

- $(3;0)$
- $\left(\dfrac13;0\right)$
- $(1;0)$
- $\left(-\dfrac13;0\right)$

## Explanation

Substituting the coordinates of $(1;2)$ gives $k-2=1$, so $k=3$. On the $x$-axis, $y=0$. The equation becomes $3x=1$, so the point is $\left(\dfrac13;0\right)$.
