Three bags are weighed in pairs. The results are $A+B=13$, $B+C=15$, $C+A=14$, in hectograms. What do all three weigh together?

- $42$ hectograms
- $14$ hectograms
- $28$ hectograms
- $21$ hectograms

## Explanation

Adding the readings counts each bag twice, so the total is $(13+15+14):2=21$ hectograms.
