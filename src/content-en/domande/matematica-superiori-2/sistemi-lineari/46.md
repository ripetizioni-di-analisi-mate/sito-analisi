Three bags $A$, $B$ and $C$ are weighed two at a time. The weighings give $A+B=13$ hg, $B+C=15$ hg and $C+A=14$ hg. How much do the three bags weigh together?

- $42$ hg
- $14$ hg
- $28$ hg
- $21$ hg

## Explanation

Adding the three equations side by side gives $2A+2B+2C=13+15+14=42$, because each bag appears in two weighings. Dividing by $2$ gives $A+B+C=\dfrac{42}{2}=21$. Hence the three bags together weigh $21$ hg. The value $42$ hg is the sum of the three weighings, in which each bag is counted twice, while $14$ hg is the mean of the three weighings.
