Solve $\begin{cases}x+y=7\\x-y=-3\end{cases}$.

- $\left( -2, \  5\right)$
- $\left( 5, \  2\right)$
- $\left( 7, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  5\right)$

## Explanation

Adding the equations eliminates $y$ and gives $2x=4$. The first equation then gives $y$. Substituting $x=2$ into the sum relation $2+y=7$ gives $y=5$.

For $(2;5)$, the second row checks as $2-5=-3$. A pair that adds to seven can still fail the second relation.
