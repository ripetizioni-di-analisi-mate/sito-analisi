The point $(2,2)$ is a solution of the system given by $x+y=4$ and $kx+y=5$. What is $k$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$

## Explanation

The first equation is already satisfied. Substitute the coordinates into the second equation and isolate $k$ in the equation $2k+2=5$. This gives $\dfrac{3}{2}$.

Inserting $k=\dfrac32$ into the second row gives $3+2=5$. With a different parameter the pair stays on the first line, but the second passes elsewhere.
