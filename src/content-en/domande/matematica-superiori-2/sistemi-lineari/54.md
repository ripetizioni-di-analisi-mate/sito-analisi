The equations $0x+0y=0$ and $2x-y=5$ form a system. Which set contains exactly its solutions?

- $\mathbb R^2$
- $\{(t;2t-5):t\in\mathbb R\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\{(5;0)\}$

## Explanation

The first equation is an identity for every pair. The second gives $y=2x-5$. Choose any $x=t$ to get $(t;2t-5)$. The pair $(0;-5)$ is one example. Some pairs in the plane are excluded because the second equation still imposes a line.
