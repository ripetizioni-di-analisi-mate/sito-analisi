Which pair satisfies both $y=5x-1$ and $x+y=65$?

- $\left( 11, \  56\right)$
- $\left( 11, \  54\right)$
- $\left( 10, \  54\right)$
- $\left( 5, \  11\right)$

## Explanation

Substituting the first expression for $y$ into the second equation gives an equation with coefficient $6$ for $x$. Substitute the value obtained back into the first equation. The result is $(5+1)x=66$, so $x=11$. It follows that $y=5\cdot 11-1=54$.
