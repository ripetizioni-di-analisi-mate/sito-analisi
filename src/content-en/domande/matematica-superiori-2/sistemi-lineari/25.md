For the system $2x-y=4$, $x+2y=3$, a program rounds $x$ to $2$ and claims that adjusting $y$ will suffice. Which check decides whether the rounded value can give an exact solution?

- With $x=2$ the rows require $y=0$ and $y=\dfrac12$, which are incompatible.
- It suffices to use $y=0$ from the first row.
- The mean of the two values of $y$ satisfies both rows.
- Every system with integer coefficients has integer solutions.

## Explanation

A solution must satisfy both equations with the same $y$. At $x=2$ the first becomes $4-y=4$ and the second $2+2y=3$. Their requirements differ.
