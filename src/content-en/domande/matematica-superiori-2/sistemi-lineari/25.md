Which pair solves $\begin{cases}2x-y=4\\x+2y=3\end{cases}$?

- $\left(\dfrac{11}5;\dfrac25\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac25;\dfrac{11}5\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{11}5;-\dfrac25\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac95;\dfrac25\right)$

## Explanation

The first equation gives $y=2x-4$. The second becomes $x+2(2x-4)=3$, so $5x=11$. This gives $x=\dfrac{11}5$, followed by $y=2\cdot\dfrac{11}5-4=\dfrac25$.

The first equation checks as $\dfrac{22}5-\dfrac25=4$, and the second as $\dfrac{11}5+\dfrac45=3$. Both equalities hold exactly even though the coordinates are fractional.
