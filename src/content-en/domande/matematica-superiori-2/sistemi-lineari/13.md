Substitute $y=2x-1$ into $x+y=5$. Which pair of equations preserves all the constraints?

- $\begin{cases}y=2x-1\\3x=6\end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases}y=2x-1\\x=6\end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases}y=2x-1\\3x=4\end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases}y=2x+1\\3x=6\end{cases}$

## Explanation

The substitution gives $x+2x-1=5$, so $3x=6$. Keep $y=2x-1$ to recover the vertical coordinate. Using $x=2$ gives $y=3$, with sum five in the original equation. Keeping only the new condition on $x$ would leave the second coordinate free.
