In the system $\begin{cases}3x+2y=5\\5x-3y=2\end{cases}$, what multipliers let you eliminate $y$ by adding the equations?

- Multiply the first by $2$ and the second by $3$.
- Multiply the first by $3$ and the second by $2$.
- Multiply the first by $5$ and the second by $3$.
- Multiply the first by $3$ and the second by $-2$.

## Explanation

Multiplying the first equation by $3$ gives the term $6y$. Multiplying the second by $2$ gives $-6y$. The two terms cancel when the equations are added.
