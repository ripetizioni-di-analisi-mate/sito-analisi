The system contains $x+y=2$, $2x+2y=4$, and $x-y=6$. Which effective conditions determine the pair?

- The first and second. The third already follows from them.
- The second alone, since its coefficients are larger.
- All three are necessary for a unique pair.
- The first and third. The second follows from them.

## Explanation

Doubling the first equation produces the second, so it adds no constraint. Adding the first and third gives $2x=8$, hence $x=4$ and $y=-2$. The pair satisfies the second as well. Keeping only the first two would leave an entire line, because both describe the same set.
