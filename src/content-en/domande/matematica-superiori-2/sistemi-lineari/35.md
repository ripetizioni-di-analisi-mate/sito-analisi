In the system $\begin{cases}\dfrac x3+\dfrac y2=5\\[3pt]\dfrac x3-\dfrac y2=-1\end{cases}$, what equation results from adding the equations?

- $\dfrac{x}{3}=4$
- $\dfrac{2x}{3}=6$
- $\dfrac{2x}{3}=4$
- $\dfrac{y}{2}=4$

## Explanation

When the equations are added, the two terms in $y$ have opposite coefficients. The terms in $x$ give $\dfrac x3+\dfrac x3=\dfrac{2x}{3}$, and the right side is $5+(-1)=4$.
