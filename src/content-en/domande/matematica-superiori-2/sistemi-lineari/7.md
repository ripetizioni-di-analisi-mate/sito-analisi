There are 23 students in a group. There are 5 more girls than boys. How many boys are there?

- $10$ boys
- $8$ boys
- $9$ boys
- $18$ boys

## Explanation

Let $r$ be the number of girls and $b$ the number of boys. The sum gives the total and the difference expresses the comparison. Subtracting the equations gives twice the number of boys.

$$
\begin{cases}r+b=23\\r-b=5\end{cases}
$$

$$
2b=23-5=18
$$

$$
b=9
$$

Nine boys means fourteen girls. The counts add to twenty-three, and fourteen minus nine equals five. The solution is also valid as a count of people.
