Which pair satisfies $x+y=7$ and $2x-y=2$ together?

- $(3;4)$
- $(4;3)$
- $(5;2)$
- $(2;3)$

## Explanation

The first equation requires a sum of seven. The pairs $(3;4)$, $(4;3)$, and $(5;2)$ pass that check. The second equation requires twice the first coordinate minus the second to equal two. For $(3;4)$ this is $2\cdot3-4=2$, while $(4;3)$ gives five and $(5;2)$ gives eight. A system requires both checks.
