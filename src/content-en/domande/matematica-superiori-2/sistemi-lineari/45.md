In the elimination method, an equation is multiplied by $3$. What happens to its constant term?

- $\text{Is multiplied by }3$
- $\text{Stays the same}$
- $\text{Is divided by }3$
- $\text{It always becomes zero}$

## Explanation

Multiply both sides of the equation. The resulting equation is equivalent because the multiplier is nonzero. Multiplying only the coefficients would change the solutions.
