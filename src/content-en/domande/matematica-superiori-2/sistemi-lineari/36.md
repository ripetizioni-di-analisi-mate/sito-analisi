In a system with a unique solution, multiply the first equation by $-2$ while keeping the second. Which effect on the solution is guaranteed?

- The two coordinates are swapped.
- Both coordinates change sign.
- The first coordinate becomes zero.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

The factor $-2$ multiplies the entire first row, including the constant. That row keeps exactly the same points because division by $-2$ reverses the step. In Cramer’s rule, $D$, $D_x$, and $D_y$ also get the same factor, so the ratio for each coordinate stays unchanged.
