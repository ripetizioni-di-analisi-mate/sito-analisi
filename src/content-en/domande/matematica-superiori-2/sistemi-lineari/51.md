A pair satisfies $2x+y=7$. The missing equation must make $(2,3)$ the unique solution. Which equation should be selected?

- $4x+2y=14$
- $x-y=-1$
- $x-y=1$
- $2x+y=8$

## Explanation

The point satisfies $x-y=-1$ and the two lines have different slopes. An equation proportional to the first would leave infinitely many points.
