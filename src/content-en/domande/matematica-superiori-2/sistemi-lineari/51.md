Which pair satisfies both $y=2x-1$ and $x+y=14$?

- $\left( 2, \  5\right)$
- $\left( 5, \  9\right)$
- $\left( 5, \  11\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  9\right)$

## Explanation

Substituting the first expression for $y$ into the second equation gives an equation in $x$ alone. Substitute the value obtained back into the first equation. The result is $(2+1)x=15$, so $x=5$. It follows that $y=2\cdot 5-1=9$.
