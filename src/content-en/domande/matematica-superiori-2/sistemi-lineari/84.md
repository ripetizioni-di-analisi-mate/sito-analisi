A group buys $16$ tickets. Full-price tickets cost $5$ euros and discounted tickets cost $3$ euros. The group spends $58$ euros. How many tickets are full-price?

- $10$
- $16$
- $5$
- $11$

## Explanation

If all tickets were discounted, they would cost $48$ euros. Each full-price ticket adds two euros, so divide the difference in cost by two. This gives $5$.
