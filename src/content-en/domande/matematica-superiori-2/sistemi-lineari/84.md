Two notebooks and one pen cost $7$ euros. Four notebooks and two pens cost $14$ euros. Which additional purchase would allow you to determine the individual prices?

- Six notebooks and three pens for $21$ euros.
- Eight notebooks and four pens for $28$ euros.
- Two notebooks and two pens for $10$ euros.
- Another two notebooks and one pen for $7$ euros.

## Explanation

The second purchase is exactly twice the first and adds no constraint. A different ratio of items is needed. Subtracting $2q+p=7$ from $2q+2p=10$ gives $p=3$. The first equation then gives $q=2$. The other purchases scale up the quantities in the first without determining the two prices separately.
