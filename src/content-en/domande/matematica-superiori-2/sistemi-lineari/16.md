In the system $\begin{cases}x+y+z=24\\x+y=15\\x-y=-1\end{cases}$, find $z$.

- $10$
- $8$
- $18$
- $9$

## Explanation

The second equation already gives the sum of $x$ and $y$. Subtracting it from the first isolates $z$. The last two equations have a nonzero determinant, so they are consistent and the value obtained is part of the solution of the system.

$$
z=24-(15)=9
$$
