The two equations give $y=4x-1$ and $y=x+5$. Which equality applies the comparison method?

- $4x+1=x+5$
- $4x-1=5-x$
- $4x-1=0$
- $4x-1=x+5$

## Explanation

Both expressions represent the same vertical coordinate at a solution, even though the lines have different slopes. Equating them gives $3x=6$, hence $x=2$. At that input the two values are $4\cdot2-1=7$ and $2+5=7$. This confirms that the point lies on both lines.
