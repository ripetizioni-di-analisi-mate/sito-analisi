The system $\begin{cases}ax+2y=1\\3x-y=4\end{cases}$ has a unique solution. What condition does $a$ satisfy?

- $a\ne -6$
- $a\ne 6$
- $a=-6$
- $a=6$

## Explanation

The determinant is $D=a(-1)-2\cdot3=-a-6$. The solution is unique when $D\ne0$, which holds for every real value of $a$ except $-6$.
