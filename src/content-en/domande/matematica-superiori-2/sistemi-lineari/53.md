You know that $D=-4$, $D_x=6$, and $D_y=-2$. Which pair follows from Cramer’s rule?

- $\left(\dfrac32;-\dfrac12\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac12;-\dfrac32\right)$
- $\left(-\dfrac23;2\right)$
- $\left(-\dfrac32;\dfrac12\right)$

## Explanation

Both coordinates use the same denominator. The horizontal coordinate uses numerator six and is negative, giving $-\dfrac32$. The vertical coordinate is positive because both signs are negative, giving $\dfrac12$. Multiplying each coordinate by $-4$ recovers its numerator, six for the first and negative two for the second.
