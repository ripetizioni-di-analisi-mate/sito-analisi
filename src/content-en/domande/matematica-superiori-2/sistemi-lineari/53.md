Which pair satisfies both $y=4x-1$ and $x+y=44$?

- $\left( 4, \  9\right)$
- $\left( 9, \  37\right)$
- $\left( 8, \  35\right)$
- $\left( 9, \  35\right)$

## Explanation

Substituting the first expression for $y$ into the second equation gives an equation with coefficient $5$ for $x$. Substitute the value obtained back into the first equation. The result is $(4+1)x=45$, so $x=9$. It follows that $y=4\cdot 9-1=35$.
