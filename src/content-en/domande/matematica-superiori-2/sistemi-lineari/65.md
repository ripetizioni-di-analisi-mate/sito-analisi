What formula describes all the solutions of $\begin{cases}x-2y=4\\2x-4y=8\end{cases}$, with $t\in\mathbb R$?

- $(2t+4;t)$
- $(2t-4;t)$
- $(t;2t+4)$
- $(4;t)$

## Explanation

The second equation repeats the first multiplied by $2$. Assigning the arbitrary value $t$ to $y$ leaves $x=2t+4$ in the first equation. Every solution therefore has the form $(2t+4;t)$.
