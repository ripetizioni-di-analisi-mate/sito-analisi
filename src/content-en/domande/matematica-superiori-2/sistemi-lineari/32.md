What solution triple is obtained from $x+y+z=12$, $x+2y+z=16$, and $2x+2y+z=21$?

- $(4;5;3)$
- $(5;3;4)$
- $(9;4;-1)$
- $(5;4;3)$

## Explanation

Subtracting the first equation from the second gives $y=16-12=4$. Subtracting the second from the third gives $x=21-16=5$. Substitution into the first leaves $z=12-5-4=3$.
