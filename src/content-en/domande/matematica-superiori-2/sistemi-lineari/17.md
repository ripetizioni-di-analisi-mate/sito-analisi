In $2x+ky=4$, the parameter $k$ can be any real number. Which formula isolates one variable for every value of the parameter?

- $x=\dfrac{4-ky}{2}$
- $y=\dfrac{4-2x}{k}$
- $x=\dfrac{4+ky}{2}$
- $y=\dfrac{4-kx}{2}$

## Explanation

The coefficient two is always nonzero. Subtracting $ky$ and dividing by two gives the formula for $x$ without any restriction on the parameter. The formula for $y$ with denominator $k$ needs $k\ne0$. At $k=0$ the equation fixes $x=2$ and leaves $y$ free.
