What is the geometric interpretation of a system in the plane with two genuine linear equations and infinitely many solutions?

- $\text{Two distinct parallel lines}$
- $\text{Two perpendicular lines}$
- $\text{Two coincident lines}$
- $\text{Two distinct intersecting lines}$

## Explanation

A system with infinitely many solutions has infinitely many solution pairs. Two distinct lines can have at most one point in common. The two lines must therefore coincide and share all their points.
