Solve $\begin{cases}3x+2y=33\\x-2y=-5\end{cases}$ by elimination.

- $\left(6;\,7\right)$
- $\left(7;\,6\right)$
- $\left(-7;\,6\right)$
- $\left(7;\,7\right)$

## Explanation

The coefficients of $y$ are opposites, so adding the equations eliminates $y$. After dividing by the coefficient of $x$, use the first equation to find $y$. Elimination preserves the solutions of the system.

$$
4x=33+(-5)=28
$$

$$
x=7,\qquad 2y=33-3\cdot7=12
$$

$$
y=6
$$

For the check, $(7;6)$ gives $21+12=33$ and $7-12=-5$. The second check uses the negative constant in the original row.
