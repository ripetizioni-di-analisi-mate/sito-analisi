The system requires $x+y+z=6$, $x+y=4$, and $z=3$. How is it classified?

- Uniquely solvable with $z=3$.
- Infinitely many solutions with $z=3$.
- Inconsistent, because the first two equations force $z=2$.
- Infinitely many solutions with any $z$.

## Explanation

Subtract the equation containing only $x+y$ from the first equation. This gives $z=6-4=2$. The third equation instead requires three. No triple can satisfy both values. Before imposing the third equation, $x$ and $y$ could vary along the line with sum four.
