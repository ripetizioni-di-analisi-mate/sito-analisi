For which value of $k$ does the system $\begin{cases}kx+y=9\\4x+y=5\end{cases}$ have no solution?

- $9$
- $5$
- $4$
- $-4$

## Explanation

Subtracting the equations gives $(k-4)x=4$. The right side is nonzero, so the system has no solution when the coefficient is zero. This gives $4$.
