Solve $\begin{cases}\dfrac x2+\dfrac y3=13\\\dfrac x2-\dfrac y3=-5\end{cases}$.

- $\left( 12, \  18\right)$
- $\left( 18, \  12\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  9\right)$
- $\left( 8, \  27\right)$

## Explanation

Adding gives $x=8$. Subtracting the second equation from the first gives $\dfrac{2y}{3}=18$.
