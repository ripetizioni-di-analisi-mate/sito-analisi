A rectangle has perimeter $28$ cm. The base is two centimeters less than three times the height. How long is the base?

- $4$ cm
- $10$ cm
- $12$ cm
- $8$ cm

## Explanation

Let $b$ be the base and $h$ the height. The perimeter gives $b+h=14$. The other condition is $b=3h-2$. Substituting yields $4h-2=14$, so $h=4$. The base is $10$. Checking the perimeter gives $2(10+4)=28$, and the base also satisfies the other condition.
