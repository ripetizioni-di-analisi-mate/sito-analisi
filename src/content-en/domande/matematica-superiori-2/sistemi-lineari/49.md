Solve $\begin{cases}x+y=16\\x-y=-6\end{cases}$.

- $\left( 16, \  0\right)$
- $\left( -5, \  11\right)$
- $\left( 11, \  5\right)$
- $\left( 5, \  11\right)$

## Explanation

Adding the equations eliminates $y$ and gives $2x=10$. The first equation then gives $y$. Substituting $x=5$ into the sum relation $5+y=16$ gives $y=11$.
