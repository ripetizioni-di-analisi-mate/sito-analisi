Two lines give a system with $D\ne0$, $D_x=0$, and $D_y\ne0$. Where does their intersection lie?

- On the horizontal axis away from the origin.
- At the origin.
- Off both axes.
- On the vertical axis away from the origin.

## Explanation

The nonzero determinant gives a single point. Its horizontal coordinate is $\dfrac0D=0$, while its vertical coordinate $\dfrac{D_y}{D}$ is nonzero. A zero horizontal coordinate puts the point on the vertical axis. The origin would require the vertical coordinate to be zero as well.
