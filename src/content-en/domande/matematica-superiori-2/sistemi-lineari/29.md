A device generates all pairs $(x,y)=(2t+1,t-1)$ for real $t$. It needs a check of the form $ax+by=4$ that **every** generated pair passes. Which coefficients work?

- $a=\dfrac43,\ b=-\dfrac83$
- $a=2,\ b=-4$
- $a=1,\ b=2$
- $a=0,\ b=-4$

## Explanation

Substitution gives $(2a+b)t+a-b=4$. For this to hold for every $t$, the coefficients must satisfy $2a+b=0$ and $a-b=4$. Solving gives $3a=4$ and then $b=-2a$.
