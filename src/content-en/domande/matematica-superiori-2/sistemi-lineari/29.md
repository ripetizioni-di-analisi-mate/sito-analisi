For which value of $a$ does the system $\begin{cases}x-2y=3\\-2x+4y=a\end{cases}$ have infinitely many solutions?

- $a=-6$
- $a=6$
- $a=3$
- $a=-2$

## Explanation

The coefficients of the second equation are those of the first multiplied by $-2$. The equations describe the same line only if the constant term is also multiplied by $-2$, so $a=-2\cdot3=-6$.
