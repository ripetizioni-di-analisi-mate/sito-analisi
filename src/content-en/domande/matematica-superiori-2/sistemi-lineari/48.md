Solve $\begin{cases}x+y=13\\x-y=-5\end{cases}$.

- $\left( 4, \  9\right)$
- $\left( -4, \  9\right)$
- $\left( 9, \  4\right)$
- $\left( 13, \  0\right)$

## Explanation

Adding the equations eliminates $y$ and gives $2x=8$. The first equation then gives $y$. Substituting $x=4$ into the sum relation $4+y=13$ gives $y=9$.
