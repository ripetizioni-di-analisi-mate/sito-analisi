In a system with $D\ne0$, you swap the two equations. How do Cramer’s determinants and the solution change?

- Each determinant is replaced by its negative and the solution stays the same.
- Only the denominator changes sign, and both coordinates change.
- The two numerator determinants swap, and the coordinates swap.
- The coefficient determinant becomes zero and the coordinates are lost.

## Explanation

The swap exchanges the rows in every determinant. It reverses the difference of diagonal products, so $D$, $D_x$, and $D_y$ all become their negatives. The ratios keep their values because the minus signs cancel in each numerator and denominator. The conditions in the system are still the same.
