What must a pair satisfy to be a solution of a system?

- $\text{All the equations}$
- $\text{At least one equation}$
- $\text{Only the first equation}$
- $\text{Only the last equation}$

## Explanation

The brace requires all the conditions to hold together. A pair that makes only one equation true lies on its line and may not lie on the other. To solve the system, the pair must satisfy every equation.
