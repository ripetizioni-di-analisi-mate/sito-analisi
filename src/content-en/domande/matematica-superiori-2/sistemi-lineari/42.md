If the determinant of the coefficient matrix of a system of two linear equations is nonzero, how many solutions does the system have?

- $\text{None}$
- $\text{One}$
- $\text{Infinitely many}$
- $\text{Exactly two}$

## Explanation

Cramer's rule applies because the denominator $D$ is nonzero. It gives exactly one value of $x$ and one value of $y$. The system therefore has a unique solution.
