Solve $\begin{cases}\dfrac x2+\dfrac y3=16\\\dfrac x2-\dfrac y3=-6\end{cases}$.

- $\left( 5, \  11\right)$
- $\left( 15, \  22\right)$
- $\left( 10, \  33\right)$
- $\left( 22, \  15\right)$

## Explanation

Adding gives $x=10$. Subtracting the second equation from the first gives $\dfrac{2y}{3}=22$.
