Which equation is equivalent to $\dfrac x2+\dfrac y3=4$ after clearing denominators with the least common multiple?

- $3x+2y=4$
- $2x+3y=24$
- $3x+2y=24$
- $3x+y=24$

## Explanation

The least common multiple of two and three is six. Every term of the equation is multiplied by six, including the constant. The first coefficient becomes three, the second two, and the constant twenty-four. Dividing by six recovers the original row, so the points satisfying it are unchanged.
