You know that $x+y=4$ and $y+z=5$. For which $k$ does adding $x+2y+z=k$ keep all the previous solutions?

- $1$
- $4$
- $9$
- $20$

## Explanation

The sum of the two rows has exactly $x+2y+z$ on the left and $4+5=9$ on the right. The third condition follows from the first two at that value of $k$. Any other value would require a different constant for the same left side. The original solutions are $(4-t;t;5-t)$ with $t$ free.
