For which value of $k$ does the system $\begin{cases}kx+y=11\\5x+y=6\end{cases}$ have no solution?

- $-5$
- $6$
- $5$
- $11$

## Explanation

Subtracting the equations gives $(k-5)x=5$. The right side is nonzero, so the system has no solution when the coefficient is zero. This gives $5$.
