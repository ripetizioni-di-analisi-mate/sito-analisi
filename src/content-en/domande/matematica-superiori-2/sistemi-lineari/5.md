How many solutions does $\begin{cases}x+2y=8\\2x+4y=17\end{cases}$ have?

- $\text{No solution}$
- $\text{A unique solution}$
- $\text{Infinitely many solutions}$
- $\text{Two solutions}$

## Explanation

Multiplying the first equation by two makes its left side match that of the second equation. The constant terms differ, so no pair can satisfy both. The system has no solution.

$$
2x+4y=16
$$

$$
16=17\quad\text{is impossible}
$$

Subtracting twice the first equation from the second directly gives $0=1$. This contradiction appears without isolating a variable or computing a determinant.
