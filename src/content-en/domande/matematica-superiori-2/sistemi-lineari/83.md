Two drinks contain $20\%$ juice and $50\%$ juice. Mix them to obtain $30$ liters containing $40\%$ juice. How many liters of the second drink are needed?

- $10$ liters
- $12$ liters
- $20$ liters
- $18$ liters

## Explanation

Let $x,y$ be the liters of the two drinks. The total requires $x+y=30$, and the juice must amount to $12$ liters. Thus $\dfrac15x+\dfrac12y=12$. Substituting $x=30-y$ gives $\dfrac3{10}y=6$, so $y=20$. Ten liters of the first give two liters of juice, and twenty of the second give ten.
