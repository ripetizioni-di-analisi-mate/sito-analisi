A group buys $13$ tickets. Full-price tickets cost $5$ euros and discounted tickets cost $3$ euros. The group spends $47$ euros. How many tickets are full-price?

- $8$
- $9$
- $4$
- $13$

## Explanation

If all tickets were discounted, they would cost $39$ euros. Each full-price ticket adds two euros, so divide the difference in cost by two. This gives $4$.
