Two drinks contain $20\%$ juice and $50\%$ juice. Mix them to obtain $30$ litres containing $40\%$ juice. How many litres of the second drink are needed?

- $10$ litres
- $12$ litres
- $20$ litres
- $18$ litres

## Explanation

Let $x,y$ be the litres of the two drinks. The total requires $x+y=30$, and the juice must amount to $12$ litres. Thus $\dfrac15x+\dfrac12y=12$. Substituting $x=30-y$ gives $\dfrac3{10}y=6$, so $y=20$. Ten litres of the first give two litres of juice, and twenty of the second give ten.
