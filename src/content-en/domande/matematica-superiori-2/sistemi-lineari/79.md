Someone reports collecting $93$ euros by selling $13$ tickets priced at $6$ or $8$ euros. What does the counting system show?

- The system has a whole-number solution with 7 tickets at 8 euros.
- The equations have a real solution, but the number of 8-euro tickets is not a whole number.
- The equations have no solution even over the real numbers.
- Every split of 13 tickets produces the reported revenue.

## Explanation

If all tickets cost six euros, the total would be $78$. Each eight-euro ticket raises revenue by two euros. Reaching $93$ requires $2x=15$, giving $x=\dfrac{15}{2}$. This is a positive real solution, but the ticket count must be a whole number. The data do not describe a possible sale.
