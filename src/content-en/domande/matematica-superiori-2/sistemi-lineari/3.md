What is the solution of the system $\begin{cases}\dfrac x2+\dfrac y3=13\\[3pt]\dfrac x2-\dfrac y3=-1\end{cases}$?

- $\left(6;\,7\right)$
- $\left(21;\,12\right)$
- $\left(12;\,21\right)$
- $\left(12;\,-21\right)$

## Explanation

The fractions have nonzero numerical denominators, so there are no values to exclude. The sum eliminates $y$, while the difference eliminates $x$. The two operations give the unknowns directly.

$$
x=13+(-1)=12
$$

$$
\frac{2y}3=13-(-1)=14
$$

$$
y=21
$$

At $x=12$ and $y=21$ the two terms equal $6$ and $7$. The first row adds them to thirteen and the second subtracts them to get negative one.
