A group buys $10$ tickets. Full-price tickets cost $5$ euros and discounted tickets cost $3$ euros. The group spends $36$ euros. How many tickets are full-price?

- $7$
- $10$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

If all tickets were discounted, they would cost $30$ euros. Each full-price ticket adds two euros, so divide the difference in cost by two. This gives $3$.
