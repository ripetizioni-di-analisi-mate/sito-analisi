Two plans cost $8+3n$ and $2+5n$ euros for $n$ uses. They tie at $n=3$. Which plan costs less for $n>3$?

- The second, with cost $2+5n$.
- The costs stay equal.
- The cheaper plan alternates at even and odd counts.
- The first, with cost $8+3n$.

## Explanation

The second cost is subtracted from the first. The difference is $6-2n=2(3-n)$, which is negative for $n>3$. The comparison can also start from the tie. After the third use, each additional use costs three euros in the first plan and five in the second. At four uses the costs are twenty and twenty-two euros.
