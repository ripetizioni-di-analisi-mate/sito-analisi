For which values of $k$ is $(0;4)$ a solution of the system $kx+y=4$, $2x+y=4$?

- $k=2$
- $k\in\mathbb R$
- $k\ne2$
- $k=0$

## Explanation

Substitute the horizontal coordinate zero in both equations. The terms $kx$ and $2x$ are zero for every $k$, and $y=4$ satisfies both. At $k=2$ the equations coincide and additional pairs solve the system. The question asks whether $(0;4)$ is a solution, without restricting the total number of solutions.
