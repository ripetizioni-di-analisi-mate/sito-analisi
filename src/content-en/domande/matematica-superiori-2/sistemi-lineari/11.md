You want $(3;-2)$ to solve $2x+y=4$ and $x-4y=k$. What value must you use for $k$?

- $-5$
- $5$
- $11$
- $-11$

## Explanation

The condition independent of $k$ is satisfied, because $2\cdot3-2=4$. In the second equation, substituting $y=-2$ turns $-4y$ into $8$. The left side becomes $3+8=11$, so the constant must be eleven. Different values of $k$ would move the second line away from the required point.
