What is the solution of the system $\begin{cases}2x+y=19\\x-y=-1\end{cases}$?

- $\left(6;\,7\right)$
- $\left(7;\,6\right)$
- $\left(6;\,-7\right)$
- $\left(7;\,7\right)$

## Explanation

The second equation gives $y$ in terms of $x$. Substituting into the first equation gives a single equation in $x$. The value obtained is then substituted into the expression for $y$, and the resulting pair satisfies both equations.

$$
y=x+1
$$

$$
2x+x+1=19\iff 3x=18
$$

$$
x=6,\qquad y=6+1=7
$$

The pair $(6;7)$ gives $12+7=19$ in the first row and $6-7=-1$ in the second. The sign of the difference also checks the order of the coordinates.
