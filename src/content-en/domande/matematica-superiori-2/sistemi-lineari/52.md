Which pair satisfies both $y=3x-1$ and $x+y=27$?

- $\left( 7, \  22\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  7\right)$
- $\left( 6, \  20\right)$
- $\left( 7, \  20\right)$

## Explanation

Substituting the first expression for $y$ into the second equation gives an equation with coefficient $4$ for $x$. Substitute the value obtained back into the first equation. The result is $(3+1)x=28$, so $x=7$. It follows that $y=3\cdot 7-1=20$.
