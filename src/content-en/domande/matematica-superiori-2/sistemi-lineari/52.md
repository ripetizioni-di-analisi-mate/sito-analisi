Cramer’s elimination gives $D=0$ and $D_x=-3$. What follows?

- The system has infinitely many solutions.
- The solution has horizontal coordinate zero.
- The system has a solution for every vertical coordinate.
- The system has no solution.

## Explanation

Even when the quotient is undefined, elimination still gives the necessary relation $Dx=D_x$. Here it would require $0=-3$, which no pair satisfies. A zero coefficient determinant alone would be insufficient to decide the number of solutions, but the nonzero numerator exposes a contradiction.
