In the system $\begin{cases}3x+2y=7\\2x+3y=3\end{cases}$, what is the determinant $D_y$ in Cramer's rule?

- $5$
- $15$
- $-1$
- $-5$

## Explanation

To form $D_y$, keep the first column and replace the second with the column of constant terms. The resulting calculation is $D_y=\begin{vmatrix}3&7\\2&3\end{vmatrix}=3\cdot3-7\cdot2=-5$.
