Solve $\begin{cases}\dfrac x2+\dfrac y3=7\\\dfrac x2-\dfrac y3=-3\end{cases}$.

- $\left( 2, \  5\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  15\right)$
- $\left( 10, \  6\right)$
- $\left( 6, \  10\right)$

## Explanation

Adding gives $x=4$. Subtracting the second equation from the first gives $\dfrac{2y}{3}=10$.
