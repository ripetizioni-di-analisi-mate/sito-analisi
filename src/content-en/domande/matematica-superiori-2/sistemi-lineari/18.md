Solve $\begin{cases}\dfrac x2+\dfrac y3=7\\\dfrac x2-\dfrac y3=-3\end{cases}$.

- $\left( 2; \  5\right)$
- $\left( 4; \  15\right)$
- $\left( 10; \  6\right)$
- $\left( 6; \  10\right)$

## Explanation

Adding gives $x=4$. Subtracting the second equation from the first gives $\dfrac{2y}{3}=10$.

This gives $y=15$. In the original fractions, $\dfrac42=2$ and $\dfrac{15}3=5$. Their sum is seven and their difference is negative three. Both conditions determine the two coordinates.
