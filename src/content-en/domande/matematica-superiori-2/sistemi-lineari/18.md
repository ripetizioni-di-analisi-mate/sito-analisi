A system has rows $R_1:2x+y=8$ and $R_2:x-y=1$. Row $R_1$ is retained together with a second row derived from them. Which choice loses information?

- $R_1+R_2$
- $2R_1$
- $R_2-R_1$
- $3R_2$

## Explanation

Keeping $R_1$ and $2R_1$ leaves only one independent condition. In every other case $R_2$ can be recovered from the new row and the retained one.
