Substitute $y=5-x$ into $3x-2y=7$. After expanding and rearranging, which equation do you get?

- $x=17$
- $5x=-3$
- $5x=17$
- $3x=17$

## Explanation

The factor $-2$ multiplies the entire expression $5-x$, giving $3x-10+2x=7$. The terms in $x$ are combined and ten is added to both sides. Writing $-2x$ in place of $+2x$ would give a relation different from the original equation.
