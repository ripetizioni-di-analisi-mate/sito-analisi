For which value of $k$ does the system $\begin{cases}kx+y=7\\3x+y=4\end{cases}$ have no solution?

- $3$
- $4$
- $7$
- $-3$

## Explanation

Subtracting the equations gives $(k-3)x=3$. The right side is nonzero, so the system has no solution when the coefficient is zero. This gives $3$.
