One line is vertical with equation $x=3$. The other is $y=2x-1$. What are the coordinates of their intersection?

- $(5;3)$
- $(3;3)$
- $(0;-1)$
- $(3;5)$

## Explanation

The first line fixes the horizontal coordinate at three while allowing any vertical coordinate. At that same input, the second gives $2\cdot3-1=5$. The only common pair is therefore $(3;5)$. The point $(0;-1)$ lies on the sloping line, but its horizontal coordinate differs from three, so it fails the first equation.
