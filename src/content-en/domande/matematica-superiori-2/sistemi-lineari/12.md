Solve $\begin{cases}2x+3y=39\\x+2y=23\end{cases}$ using Cramer's rule.

- $\left(7;\,9\right)$
- $\left(-9;\,7\right)$
- $\left(9;\,8\right)$
- $\left(9;\,7\right)$

## Explanation

The determinant of the coefficient matrix is nonzero, so the system has a unique solution. For the determinant associated with $x$, replace the first column with the constant terms. For the determinant associated with $y$, replace the second column. Divide each determinant by $D$.

$$
D=2\cdot2-3\cdot1=1
$$

$$
D_x=39\cdot2-3\cdot23=9
$$

$$
D_y=2\cdot23-39=7
$$

$$
x=\frac{D_x}D=9,\qquad y=\frac{D_y}D=7
$$
