What is the determinant of the coefficient matrix of the system $\begin{cases}4x+y=9\\x+5y=10\end{cases}$?

- $19$
- $9$
- $21$
- $-19$

## Explanation

Subtract the product of the entries on the other diagonal from the product of the entries on the main diagonal. The constant terms do not enter this determinant. This gives $19$.
