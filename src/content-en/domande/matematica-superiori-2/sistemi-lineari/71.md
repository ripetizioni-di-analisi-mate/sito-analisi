For which value of $k$ does the system $\begin{cases}kx+y=5\\2x+y=3\end{cases}$ have no solution?

- $3$
- $2$
- $5$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Subtracting the equations gives $(k-2)x=2$. The right side is nonzero, so the system has no solution when the coefficient is zero. This gives $2$.
