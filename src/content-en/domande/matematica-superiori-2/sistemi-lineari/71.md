Which triple solves $x+z=5$, $y-z=1$, and $x+y+z=8$?

- $(3;2;3)$
- $(3;3;2)$
- $(2;4;3)$
- $(2;3;3)$

## Explanation

Adding the first two rows gives $x+y=6$. Comparing this with the third gives $z=2$. The first then gives $x=3$ and the second gives $y=3$. The three checks are $3+2=5$, $3-2=1$, and $3+3+2=8$. The triple is ordered as $x,y,z$.
