The graph of $f$ is above the horizontal axis for $-3<x<1$, meets it at $-3$ and $1$, and lies below it elsewhere. What is the domain of $\sqrt{f(x)}$?

- $(-3,1)$
- $(-\infty,-3]\cup[1,+\infty)$
- $[-3,1]$
- $[0,1]$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-3,1\}$

## Explanation

The square root requires $f(x)\ge0$. The graph shows that this holds between the two zeros and at the zeros themselves. Hence the domain is $[-3,1]$. The endpoints are included because the square root of zero is defined; portions below the axis instead correspond to negative radicands.
