Which positive pair shows that $\ln(u+v)=\ln u+\ln v$ is not an identity?

- $u=2$, $v=2$
- $u=3$, $v=\dfrac32$
- $u=4$, $v=\dfrac43$
- $u=5$, $v=\dfrac54$
- $u=1$, $v=1$

## Explanation

With $u=v=1$, the left side equals $\ln2$, while the right side is $\ln1+\ln1=0$. The results differ, so this pair is a counterexample. For the other proposed pairs, $u+v=uv$, so the equality holds in those particular cases despite not being a general identity.
