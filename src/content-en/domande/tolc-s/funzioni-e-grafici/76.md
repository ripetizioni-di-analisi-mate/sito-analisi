For which $k$ does $|x-1|=k-2$ have exactly two distinct real solutions?

- $k>2$
- $k\ge2$
- $k<2$
- $k=2$
- $k>0$

## Explanation

Absolute value represents the distance of $x$ from $1$. A positive distance identifies two points, one on each side; a zero distance identifies only $1$, and a negative distance is impossible. Thus $k-2>0$, namely $k>2$. In that case the solutions are $1\pm(k-2)$.
