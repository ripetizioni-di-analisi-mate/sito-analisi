For which valid real base $a$ is $\log_a 16=2$?

- $-4$
- $8$
- $2$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

The definition turns the statement into $a^2=16$. The algebraic roots are $4$ and $-4$, but a real logarithm’s base must be positive and different from $1$. Only $a=4$ remains. Substitution verifies $4^2=16$, as required.
