A table gives $f(0)=3$, $f(1)=6$, $f(2)=12$ and $f(3)=24$. Which proposed model reproduces all four data points?

- $f(x)=3+3x$
- $f(x)=3x^2$
- $f(x)=3\cdot2^x$
- $f(x)=2\cdot3^x$
- $f(x)=3\cdot x^2+3$

## Explanation

The initial value is $3$, and the ratio of consecutive values is always $2$. The exponential model $3\cdot2^x$ therefore gives $3,6,12,24$ at the assigned inputs. A constant increment does not describe the table: the differences are $3,6,12$. The question selects among the listed models, without claiming uniqueness among all possible functions.
