An initial quantity of $80$ halves at each step. After how many whole steps does it first become strictly less than $10$?

- $4$
- $3$
- $2$
- $5$
- $8$

## Explanation

The values after one, two and three steps are $40$, $20$ and $10$. After three steps the threshold is reached, but the condition requires a strictly smaller value. At the fourth step the value becomes $5$, so the smallest whole number of steps is $4$.
