What is the domain of $g(x)=\dfrac{\ln x}{\sqrt{6-x}}$?

- $[0,6]$
- $(0,6]$
- $(-\infty,6)$
- $(0,+\infty)$
- $(0,6)$

## Explanation

The logarithm requires $x>0$. The square root is in the denominator, so it must be defined and nonzero: this imposes $6-x>0$, namely $x<6$. The simultaneous conditions give $0<x<6$. Neither endpoint is allowed, for different reasons.
