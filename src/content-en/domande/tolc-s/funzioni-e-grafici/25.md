It is known that $f(2)=-3$ and $g(2)=4$. Which point must belong to the graph of $h=f+g$?

- $(4,1)$
- $(2,-12)$
- $(2,-7)$
- $(1,2)$
- $(2,1)$

## Explanation

The sum of functions is calculated at the same input. Thus $h(2)=f(2)+g(2)=-3+4=1$, and the required point is $(2,1)$. Adding the horizontal coordinates would not match the definition. The product $-12$ would instead be the value of the function $fg$ at that input.
