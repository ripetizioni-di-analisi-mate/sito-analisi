Let $f(x)=\sqrt{x+1}$ and $g(x)=\ln(4-x)$. What is the domain of $f+g$?

- $[-1,4)$
- $[-1,4]$
- $(-1,4)$
- $(-\infty,4)$
- $[-1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

To add two values, both must be defined at the same input. The square root requires $x\ge-1$, while the logarithm requires $x<4$. Their intersection is $[-1,4)$. The first endpoint is included because the square root of zero exists; the second is excluded because the logarithm of zero does not.
