What is the complete solution set of $\log_2(x+1)\ge3$?

- $(-1,7]$
- $[7,+\infty)$
- $(7,+\infty)$
- $[2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,7]$

## Explanation

The domain requires $x>-1$. The base-$2$ logarithm is increasing and $3=\log_2 8$, so the inequality is equivalent to $x+1\ge8$. Hence $x\ge7$, which already satisfies the domain. The point $7$ is included because it produces the permitted equality.
