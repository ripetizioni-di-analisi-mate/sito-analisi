If $f(x)=x^2$ and $g(x)=x+1$, what is $(f\circ g)(2)$?

- $5$
- $9$
- $6$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

The composition $f\circ g$ applies $g$ first. Thus $g(2)=2+1=3$, followed by $f(3)=3^2=9$. The value $5$ instead comes from the reversed order, because $g(f(2))=g(4)=5$. Specifying the order distinguishes two operations that generally give different results.
