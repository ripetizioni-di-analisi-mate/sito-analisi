What is the range of $f(x)=\sqrt{9-x^2}$ on its natural domain?

- $[-3,3]$
- $[0,3]$
- $[0,9]$
- $(0,3)$
- $[3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The domain is $[-3,3]$, where $0\le9-x^2\le9$. The square root therefore takes values between $0$ and $3$. Both are attained: $f(\pm3)=0$ and $f(0)=3$. For each $y\in[0,3]$, choosing $x=\sqrt{9-y^2}$ gives $f(x)=y$, so the entire interval is the range.
