What is the real domain of $\ln(x^2-4)$?

- $(-2,2)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-2,2\}$
- $[2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(2,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$

## Explanation

The logarithm argument must be strictly positive, so $x^2-4>0$. Since $x^2-4=(x-2)(x+2)$, the product is positive outside the roots. This gives $x<-2$ or $x>2$. Both endpoints are excluded because the logarithm of zero is undefined.
