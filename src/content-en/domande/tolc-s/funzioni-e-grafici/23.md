Which value solves $\log_2(x^2)=4$ subject to $x<0$?

- $4$
- $-2$
- $-4$
- $-16$
- $2$

## Explanation

The logarithm requires $x^2>0$, so $x\ne0$. Its definition gives $x^2=2^4=16$, hence $x=4$ or $x=-4$. The additional constraint $x<0$ selects only $-4$. Substituting it gives argument $16$ and logarithm $4$.
