For $f(x)=\ln(x-1)$, which line is the vertical asymptote, and on which side is the function defined?

::grafico{f="log(x-1)" xmin=-1 xmax=6 ymin=-4 ymax=3 asintoti="x=1"}

- $x=-1$, for $x>-1$
- $x=1$, for $x>1$
- $y=1$, for every $x$
- $x=1$, for $x<1$
- $y=0$, for $x>0$

## Explanation

The domain requires $x-1>0$, namely $x>1$. As $x$ approaches $1$ from the right, the positive argument approaches zero and the logarithm decreases without bound. The asymptote is therefore the vertical line $x=1$, with the graph defined on its right-hand side.
