Let $f(x)=x^2$ and $g(x)=x+1$. For which $x$ is $(f\circ g)(x)=(g\circ f)(x)$?

- For every $x\in\mathbb R$.
- Only $x=0$.
- Only $x=1$.
- For $x=0$ and $x=1$.
- For no real number.

## Explanation

The two compositions are $(x+1)^2$ and $x^2+1$. Equality becomes $x^2+2x+1=x^2+1$, so $2x=0$ and $x=0$. The composed functions are different even though they take the same value at one input. Agreement at one point does not mean agreement on the whole domain.
