For which horizontal coordinates does the graph of $y=\dfrac1{4x-3}$ lie above the horizontal axis?

- $\left(-\infty,\dfrac34\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac34,+\infty\right)$
- $\left[\dfrac34,+\infty\right)$
- $\left(0,\dfrac34\right)$
- $(0,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The numerator is positive, so the ratio is positive exactly when its denominator is positive. Solving $4x-3>0$ gives $x>\dfrac34$. The endpoint is excluded because it makes the denominator zero. Lying above the axis means having a positive vertical coordinate, independently of the input itself.
