Which geometric relation connects $y=2^x$ and $y=\left(\dfrac12\right)^x$?

- They are symmetric about the horizontal axis.
- They are vertical translates by one unit.
- They coincide at every point.
- They are symmetric about the vertical axis.
- They are symmetric about the line $y=x$.

## Explanation

We have $\left(\dfrac12\right)^x=2^{-x}$. Replacing $x$ by $-x$ in a function’s formula reflects every point across the vertical axis, preserving the vertical coordinate and changing the sign of the horizontal coordinate. Both graphs pass through $(0,1)$ and remain above the horizontal axis.
