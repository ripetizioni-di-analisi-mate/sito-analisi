A function $f$ has exactly the zeros $-2$ and $5$. What are the zeros of $g(x)=[f(x)]^2$ on the same domain?

- $\{4,25\}$
- $\{-2,5\}$
- $\{-5,2\}$
- $\{-2,2,-5,5\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The square of a real number is zero exactly when that number is zero. Thus $[f(x)]^2=0$ is equivalent to $f(x)=0$, and the inputs making the function zero remain $-2$ and $5$. The function values are squared, not the horizontal coordinates of its zeros.
