An odd function is defined at $x=0$. Which value is required for $f(0)$?

- $1$
- $0$
- $-1$
- Any positive number.
- Any real number.

## Explanation

Oddness requires $f(-x)=-f(x)$. At $x=0$, since $-0=0$, this gives $f(0)=-f(0)$ and thus $2f(0)=0$. The value must be $0$. The assumption that zero belongs to the domain is needed to apply this equality at that point.
