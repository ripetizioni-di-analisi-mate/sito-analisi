Let $g(x)=x^2$ and $f(u)=2u+5$. What is the complete set of $x$ such that $f(g(x))=23$?

- $\{3\}$
- $\{-9,9\}$
- $\{-3,3\}$
- $\{9\}$
- $\{-2,2\}$

## Explanation

Substituting $g(x)$ into the outer function gives $f(g(x))=2x^2+5$. The equation becomes $2x^2+5=23$, hence $2x^2=18$ and $x^2=9$. Both $-3$ and $3$ are allowed and produce $23$. Taking only the positive root would lose a solution.
