What is the domain of $f(x)=\log_2\left(\dfrac{x-4}{x+2}\right)$?

- $(-2,4)$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(4,+\infty)$
- $[-2,4]$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-2\}$
- $(4,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The ratio must be positive. For $x<-2$, numerator and denominator are both negative; for $-2<x<4$, they have opposite signs; for $x>4$, both are positive. Thus the two outer intervals are taken. The point $-2$ makes the denominator zero, while $4$ gives zero argument: both are excluded.
