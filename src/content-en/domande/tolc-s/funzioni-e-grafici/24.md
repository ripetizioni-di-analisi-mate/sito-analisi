At which point does the graph $y=(x-2)^2-7$ meet the vertical axis?

- $(2,-7)$
- $(-3,0)$
- $(0,-7)$
- $(0,-3)$
- $(0,11)$

## Explanation

On the vertical axis, the horizontal coordinate is $0$. Therefore $f(0)=(-2)^2-7=4-7=-3$, giving $(0,-3)$. The point $(2,-7)$ is instead the parabola’s vertex. Swapping the coordinates would produce a point on the horizontal axis, different from the requested intercept.
