For which value of $k$ do the line $y=2x+k$ and the parabola $y=x^2$ have exactly one shared point?

- $-1$
- $1$
- $0$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The intersection equation is $x^2-2x=k$, hence $(x-1)^2=k+1$. A square equal to zero gives one solution; a positive right side gives two, and a negative one gives none. Therefore $k+1=0$, namely $k=-1$.
