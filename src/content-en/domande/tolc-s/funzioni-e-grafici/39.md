What is the complete solution set of $2^x>3^x$?

- $x>0$
- $x\ne0$
- $x\le0$
- $x<0$
- $x>1$

## Explanation

The powers are positive, so divide by $3^x$ to obtain $\left(\dfrac23\right)^x>1$. The base is below one and $1=\left(\dfrac23\right)^0$, so decreasing behavior gives $x<0$. At $x=0$, both original sides equal $1$, and equality is not allowed.
