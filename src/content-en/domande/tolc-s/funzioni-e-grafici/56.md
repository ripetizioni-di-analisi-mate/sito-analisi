How many points do $y=x^2+3$ and $y=2x$ share?

- None.
- One.
- Two.
- Three.
- Infinitely many.

## Explanation

The intersection equation is $x^2+3=2x$, equivalent to $x^2-2x+3=0$. Completing the square gives $(x-1)^2+2=0$. The left side is at least $2$ for every real number and therefore cannot vanish. The parabola remains above the line, so there are no shared points.
