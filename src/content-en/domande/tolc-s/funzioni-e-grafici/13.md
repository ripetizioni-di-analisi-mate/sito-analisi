What is the solution of $3^x-3^{x-1}=18$?

- $x=2$
- $x=4$
- $x=3$
- $x=6$
- $x=1$

## Explanation

Since $3^x=3\cdot3^{x-1}$, factor out $3^{x-1}$ to obtain $(3-1)3^{x-1}=18$. Thus $3^{x-1}=9=3^2$, giving $x-1=2$ and $x=3$. Checking gives $27-9=18$. The difference of powers requires factoring, rather than subtracting the exponents.
