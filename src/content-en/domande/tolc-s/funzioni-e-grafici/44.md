The graph of $f$ is reflected across the horizontal axis and then translated upward by $3$ units. What is the new function?

- $g(x)=-f(x+3)$
- $g(x)=-(f(x)+3)$
- $g(x)=f(-x)+3$
- $g(x)=-f(x)+3$
- $g(x)=3f(-x)$

## Explanation

Reflection across the horizontal axis preserves the horizontal coordinate and changes the sign of the vertical coordinate, producing $-f(x)$. The subsequent translation adds $3$ to that value, so $g(x)=-f(x)+3$. The formula $-(f(x)+3)$ would correspond to the reversed order, translating before reflecting.
