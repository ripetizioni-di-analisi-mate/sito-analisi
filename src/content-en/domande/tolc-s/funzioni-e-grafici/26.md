A piecewise-linear graph joins $(0,1)$, $(2,5)$ and $(6,1)$. What are all the horizontal coordinates where the vertical coordinate is $3$?

::grafico{linee="0,1 2,5 6,1" punti="0,1;2,5;6,1" segmenti="0,3,6,3" xmin=-1 xmax=7 ymin=-1 ymax=6}

- $1$ and $4$
- $1$ and $5$
- $2$ and $4$
- $3$ and $5$
- $0$ and $6$

## Explanation

The vertical coordinate $3$ lies halfway between $1$ and $5$. Since both pieces are line segments, it is attained at their midpoints. On the first segment the midpoint’s horizontal coordinate is $\dfrac{0+2}{2}=1$; on the second it is $\dfrac{2+6}{2}=4$. These are the two intersections with $y=3$.
