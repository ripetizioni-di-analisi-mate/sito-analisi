The function $f$ is strictly increasing on $\mathbb R$ and $f(2)=0$. Which relation is guaranteed?

- $f(1)>0>f(3)$
- $f(1)=f(3)$
- $f(0)>0$
- $f(3)=1$
- $f(1)<0<f(3)$

## Explanation

The inputs satisfy $1<2<3$. Strict increase preserves these comparisons between their images, so $f(1)<f(2)<f(3)$. Substituting $f(2)=0$ gives $f(1)<0<f(3)$. Their signs can be determined, but not exact numerical values of the other images.
