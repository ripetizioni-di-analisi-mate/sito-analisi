The graph shows distance traveled $d$ as a function of time $t$. During which interval is the cyclist stationary?

::grafico{linee="0,0 2,3 4,3 5,5" punti="0,0;2,3;4,3;5,5" xmin=-1 xmax=6 ymin=-1 ymax=6 nomi="t,d"}

- $[0,2]$
- $[2,4]$
- $[4,5]$
- $[0,4]$
- $[2,5]$

## Explanation

When the cyclist is stationary, distance traveled does not increase, so the graph is horizontal. The segment from $(2,3)$ to $(4,3)$ has constant vertical coordinate and corresponds to $[2,4]$. The other two segments are increasing and describe distance increasing over time.
