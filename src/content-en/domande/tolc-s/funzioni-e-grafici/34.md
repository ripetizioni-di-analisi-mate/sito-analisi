What is the range of $f(x)=2^x-4$ on $\mathbb R$?

- $[-4,+\infty)$
- $(0,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-4,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-4)$

## Explanation

The exponential $2^x$ takes exactly the strictly positive values. Subtracting $4$ turns these into exactly the values greater than $-4$. The endpoint is not included because $2^x$ is never zero. The line $y=-4$ is a horizontal asymptote, not an attained level.
