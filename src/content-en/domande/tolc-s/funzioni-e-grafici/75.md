What is the minimum of $f(x)=(x-2)^2+1$ on the domain $[3,5]$?

- $1$
- $3$
- $5$
- $10$
- $2$

## Explanation

The vertex at horizontal coordinate $2$ is outside the assigned domain. On $[3,5]$, the number $x-2$ is positive and increases with $x$, so its square increases too. The minimum is attained at the left endpoint $x=3$ and equals $(3-2)^2+1=2$. The parabola’s global minimum $1$ is unavailable on this domain.
