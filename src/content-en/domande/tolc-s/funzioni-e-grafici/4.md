Which value solves $\log_3(x-1)=2$?

- $x=7$
- $x=9$
- $x=3$
- $x=10$
- $x=8$

## Explanation

The domain requires $x-1>0$, namely $x>1$. The logarithm definition turns the equation into $x-1=3^2=9$. Adding $1$ gives $x=10$. This value belongs to the domain and produces the argument $9$, whose base-$3$ logarithm is indeed $2$.
