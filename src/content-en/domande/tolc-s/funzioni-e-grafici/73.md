What is the range of $f(x)=2x-1$ with domain $[1,4)$?

- $[1,7]$
- $(1,7)$
- $[1,7)$
- $[1,4)$
- $(-\infty,7)$

## Explanation

The function is strictly increasing because its slope is $2>0$. At the included endpoint $x=1$, it takes the value $1$. As $x$ approaches $4$ from the left, its value approaches $7$ but does not attain it because $4$ is excluded. The range is therefore $[1,7)$.
