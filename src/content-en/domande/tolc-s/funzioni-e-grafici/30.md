Which points are shared by the graphs $y=x^2+1$ and $y=4x-2$?

- $(1,0)$ and $(3,0)$
- $(2,1)$ and $(10,3)$
- $(1,3)$ and $(2,10)$
- $(0,1)$ and $(0,-2)$
- $(1,2)$ and $(3,10)$

## Explanation

Equate the vertical coordinates: $x^2+1=4x-2$. Moving everything to the left gives $(x-1)(x-3)=0$, so the horizontal coordinates are $1$ and $3$. Substituting in $y=4x-2$ gives $2$ and $10$. The shared points are therefore $(1,2)$ and $(3,10)$.
