What is the largest interval on which $f(x)=2(x-4)^2+1$ is strictly increasing?

- $\mathbb R$
- $[4,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,4]$
- $[1,+\infty)$
- $[5,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The parabola opens upward and its vertex has horizontal coordinate $4$. For $x\ge4$, increasing $x$ also increases the nonnegative number $x-4$ and hence its square. The function is strictly increasing on $[4,+\infty)$. The interval $[5,+\infty)$ has the property but is not the largest one requested.
