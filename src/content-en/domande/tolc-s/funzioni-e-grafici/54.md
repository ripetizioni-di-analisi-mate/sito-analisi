A line passes through $(0,3)$ and $(3,0)$. Which function does it represent?

- $y=x-3$
- $y=3x$
- $y=-3x+3$
- $y=3-x$
- $y=\dfrac x3+3$

## Explanation

The vertical intercept is $3$. The slope is $m=\dfrac{0-3}{3-0}=-1$. In the form $y=mx+q$, this gives $y=-x+3$. Checking the point $(3,0)$ yields $-3+3=0$, so both assigned points are respected.
