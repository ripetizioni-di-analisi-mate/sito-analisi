For $x>0$, which model keeps the product $xy=12$ constant?

- $y=12x$
- $y=\dfrac{12}{x}$
- $y=\dfrac{x}{12}$
- $y=12-x$
- $y=x+12$

## Explanation

Since $x>0$, divide $xy=12$ by $x$ to obtain $y=\dfrac{12}{x}$. This is inverse proportionality: doubling $x$ halves $y$. The other models keep a ratio or a sum constant, or preserve none of the requested quantities.
