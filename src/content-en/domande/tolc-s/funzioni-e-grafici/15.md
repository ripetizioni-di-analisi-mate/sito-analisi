It is known that $5^x=75$. What is $5^{x-2}$?

- $15$
- $73$
- $1875$
- $\dfrac{75}{2}$
- $3$

## Explanation

The power rule gives $5^{x-2}=\dfrac{5^x}{5^2}$. The value $5^x$ is already known, so substitute directly to obtain $\dfrac{75}{25}=3$. There is no need to find $x$. Subtracting $2$ from the exponent divides the power by $25$, whereas subtracting $2$ from its value is a different operation.
