A function $f$ is nondecreasing on an interval. For $a<b$ in that interval, which relation is guaranteed?

- $f(a)>f(b)$
- $f(a)>0$
- $f(b)=0$
- $a>0$
- $f(a)\le f(b)$

## Explanation

Nondecreasing behavior means that increasing the input does not decrease the function value. By definition, $a<b$ gives $f(a)\le f(b)$. Equality is possible, for example for a constant function. This property compares two images and does not determine whether they are positive, negative or zero.
