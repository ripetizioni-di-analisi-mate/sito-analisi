On a logarithmic-scale axis the number $c>0$ is represented by the point with abscissa $\log_{10}c$. Which number lies at the midpoint between the points representing $2$ and $18$?

- $10$
- $6$
- $8$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

The abscissa of the midpoint is the mean of the abscissas of the endpoints, so $\log_{10}c=\dfrac{\log_{10}2+\log_{10}18}{2}$. By the product rule for logarithms, $\log_{10}2+\log_{10}18=\log_{10}36$, hence $\log_{10}c=\dfrac12\log_{10}36=\log_{10}6$. Since the logarithm is an injective function, we obtain $c=6$. The value $10$ is the arithmetic mean of $2$ and $18$, which corresponds to the midpoint on a linear-scale axis.
