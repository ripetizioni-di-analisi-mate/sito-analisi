On a chart, the horizontal position of a positive concentration $c$ is $\log_{10}c$. Which concentration belongs halfway between the marks for $2$ and $18$?

- $10$
- $6$
- $8$
- $4$
- $9$

## Explanation

At the midpoint the logarithmic distances are equal. Thus $\log_{10}(c/2)=\log_{10}(18/c)$, giving $c^2=36$ and $c=6$. Both steps multiply the concentration by three.
