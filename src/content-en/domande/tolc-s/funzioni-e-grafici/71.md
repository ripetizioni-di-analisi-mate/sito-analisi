An experiment records the readings $\ln x=-1$ and $\ln y=1$. Which algebraic relation between $x$ and $y$ is guaranteed without decimal approximations?

- $xy=1$
- $x+y=0$
- $y-x=2$
- $x=y$
- $xy=-1$

## Explanation

The readings sum to zero. Since $x,y>0$, $\ln(xy)=\ln x+\ln y=0$. The only input with natural logarithm zero is $1$.
