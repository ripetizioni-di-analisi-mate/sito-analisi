What is the complete solution set of $\ln x\le0$?

- $(0,1]$
- $[0,1]$
- $(0,1)$
- $(-\infty,1]$
- $[1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The domain requires $x>0$. The natural logarithm is strictly increasing and $\ln1=0$, so nonpositive values correspond to arguments no greater than $1$. Combining the conditions gives $0<x\le1$. Zero is outside the domain, while one is included because equality is allowed.
