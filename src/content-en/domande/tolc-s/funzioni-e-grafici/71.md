For which real numbers $x$ is the natural logarithm $\ln x$ defined and does it satisfy $\ln x\le0$?

- $(0,1]$
- $[0,1]$
- $(0,1)$
- $(-\infty,1]$
- $[1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The domain requires $x>0$. The natural logarithm is strictly increasing and $\ln1=0$, so nonpositive values correspond to arguments no greater than $1$. Combining the two conditions gives $0<x\le1$, that is, the interval $(0,1]$. Zero does not belong to the domain, while $1$ is a solution because $\ln1=0$ satisfies the non-strict inequality.
