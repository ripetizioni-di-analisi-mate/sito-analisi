The range of a function $f$ is $[-1,2]$. What is the range of $g(x)=3-2f(x)$ on the same domain?

- $[1,7]$
- $[-5,1]$
- $[-1,5]$
- $[2,5]$
- $[-1,2]$

## Explanation

Transform every value $y\in[-1,2]$ using $3-2y$. The negative coefficient reverses order: the maximum $y=2$ produces the minimum $3-4=-1$, while the minimum $y=-1$ produces the maximum $3+2=5$. Every intermediate value is obtained, so the range is $[-1,5]$.
