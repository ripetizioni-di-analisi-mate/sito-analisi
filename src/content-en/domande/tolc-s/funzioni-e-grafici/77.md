The function $f$ is invertible and $f^{-1}(9)=4$. Which point must belong to the graph of $g(x)=f(x)+1$?

- $(9,5)$
- $(4,10)$
- $(4,8)$
- $(10,4)$
- $(5,9)$

## Explanation

The inverse statement means that $4$ is the input sent to $9$ by $f$, so $f(4)=9$. The function $g$ adds $1$ to the image without changing the input. Thus $g(4)=10$ and the required point is $(4,10)$.
