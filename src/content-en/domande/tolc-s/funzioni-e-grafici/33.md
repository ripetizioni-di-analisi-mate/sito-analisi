What is $\log_{\frac12}8$?

- $3$
- $-4$
- $-3$
- $4$
- $\dfrac13$

## Explanation

We seek the exponent $c$ such that $\left(\dfrac12\right)^c=8$. Since $\left(\dfrac12\right)^c=2^{-c}$ and $8=2^3$, we get $-c=3$, hence $c=-3$. The result is negative because, with a base less than $1$, powers with a positive exponent are less than $1$, while $8$ is greater than $1$.
