A signal has positive intensity $x$. A base $b$ must be chosen so that $\log_b x$ strictly decreases as $x$ increases and is also defined for $0<x<1$. Which proposed base meets both conditions?

- $1$
- $-\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac32$
- $0$

## Explanation

A real logarithm is defined for every positive input when its base is positive and different from $1$. It is strictly decreasing precisely for $0<b<1$, and only $2/3$ lies in this interval.
