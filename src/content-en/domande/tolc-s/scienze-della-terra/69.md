Moving eastwards from a trench, hypocentres become progressively deeper and outline a dipping zone. Which orientation of the subducting slab is compatible?

- The slab descends westwards
- The slab stays horizontal at constant depth
- The slab rises eastwards towards the surface
- The slab descends eastwards
- The data establish only a divergent boundary

## Explanation

The earthquake zone traces the geometry of the descending slab. Depth increases with eastward distance, so the dip is eastwards. The profile provides a direction, not by itself a movement speed.
