At a ridge, each side moves away from the axis at $2\,\mathrm{cm/year}$. With constant speeds, how far apart are two points initially at the axis after one million years?

- $20\,\mathrm{km}$
- $4\,\mathrm{km}$
- $400\,\mathrm{km}$
- $2\,\mathrm{km}$
- $40\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

The points move in opposite directions, so their separation speed is $2+2=4\,\mathrm{cm/year}$. Over $10^6$ years the distance is $4\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{cm}=40\,\mathrm{km}$. Using the speed of only one side would give half the required separation.
