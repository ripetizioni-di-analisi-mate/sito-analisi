P and S waves leave the hypocentre simultaneously and follow the same path through an ordinary homogeneous solid. Which arrives first, and why?

- P, because it has greater speed
- S, because it has greater speed
- They always arrive together because they start together
- P, because it travels a shorter distance
- S, because transverse motion always requires less time

## Explanation

For the same distance $d$, travel time is $t=\dfrac dv$. Since $v_P>v_S$ in the stated medium, $t_P<t_S$. Simultaneous departure does not imply simultaneous arrival. S waves travel through solids but not liquids.
