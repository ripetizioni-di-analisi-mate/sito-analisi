An island chain has an active volcano in the east and progressively older islands towards the west. Assuming a fixed source, in which direction has the plate moved over it?

- Westwards
- Eastwards
- Vertically with no horizontal component
- Necessarily northwards
- It has never moved

## Explanation

The active volcano marks the source’s current position. Volcanoes formed earlier have been carried westwards with the plate, producing increasing ages in that direction. The inference uses the explicit assumption that the source remains fixed.
