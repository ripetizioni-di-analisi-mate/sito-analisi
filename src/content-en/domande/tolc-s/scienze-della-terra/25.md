Which pairing describes the prevailing physical states of Earth’s two core regions under current conditions?

- Solid outer and inner core
- Solid outer core, liquid inner core
- Liquid outer and inner core
- Gaseous outer core, solid inner core
- Liquid outer core, solid inner core

## Explanation

The outer core is liquid, whereas the inner core is solid. Temperature rises towards the centre, but pressure also rises and changes melting conditions. Physical state therefore cannot be inferred from temperature alone.
