In a simplified model, dry air contains $78\%$ nitrogen and $21\%$ oxygen. What total percentage remains for other gases?

- $1\%$
- $21\%$
- $22\%$
- $79\%$
- $99\%$

## Explanation

Component percentages must sum to $100\%$. Nitrogen and oxygen occupy $78+21=99\%$, leaving $1\%$. The model concerns dry air: variable water vapour is excluded from the stated composition.
