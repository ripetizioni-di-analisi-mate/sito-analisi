A permeable area is paved. With rainfall, slope, and drainage network unchanged, which change is generally expected?

- More infiltration through asphalt and less runoff
- Greater groundwater recharge caused by greater impermeability
- Unchanged runoff because the rainfall amount is unchanged
- Less infiltration and more rapid surface runoff
- A slower flood response because water must pass through more soil

## Explanation

Asphalt restricts water entry into the ground. A larger fraction of rainfall can therefore rapidly reach ditches and channels, increasing flood response. The actual effect also depends on drainage; the question holds the other conditions constant.
