An observer at a temperate Northern Hemisphere latitude identifies Polaris. The horizontal direction towards it approximately indicates which cardinal direction?

- North
- South
- East
- West
- Always the sunset point

## Explanation

Polaris lies close to the direction of the north celestial pole. Its projection onto the horizon therefore gives an approximate north reference. Sunset position changes through the year and does not generally coincide with this direction.
