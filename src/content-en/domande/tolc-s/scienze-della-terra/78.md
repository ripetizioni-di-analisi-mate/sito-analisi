The mantle transmits S waves but can deform over millions of years. Which statement reconciles these observations?

- It must be wholly liquid at every depth
- A solid can never deform
- It is mostly solid and its behaviour depends on timescale
- S waves travel only through gases
- Deformation necessarily implies complete melting

## Explanation

Over short times the mantle responds as a solid able to sustain shear deformation. Over long times it can deform slowly under persistent stress. Flow on geological timescales therefore does not imply a wholly liquid state.
