Tilted layers are truncated by an erosion surface and covered by undeformed horizontal layers. Which minimum sequence explains the unconformity?

- Older deposition, erosion, renewed deposition, joint tilting of both sequences
- Older deposition, renewed deposition, joint tilting, final erosion
- Simultaneous deposition of all layers with identical tilt
- Older deposition, tilting, erosion, renewed deposition
- Older deposition, renewed deposition, erosion without deformation

## Explanation

The lower layers had to exist before they were tilted and eroded. The upper layers were deposited after the surface they cover formed. Their horizontality distinguishes this later stage from the deformation affecting the older sequence.
