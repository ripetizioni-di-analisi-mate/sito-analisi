Two samples have allowed age intervals of 98–102 and 100–104 million years respectively. From the intervals alone, which conclusion is correct?

- The first is certainly younger in every allowed case
- The second is certainly younger in every allowed case
- Their ages are certainly identical
- The relative order is uncertain because the intervals overlap
- The ages certainly differ by two million years

## Explanation

Ages of 99 and 103 million years are possible, but so are 102 and 100 million years. These examples give opposite orders while respecting the intervals. A difference between central values is therefore insufficient to establish a certain order.
