Two surfaces each receive $100$ units of solar energy. Snow reflects $80\%$, while dark ground reflects $20\%$. Neglecting transmission, how much more energy does the dark ground absorb?

- 20 units
- 80 units
- 100 units
- 0 units
- 60 units

## Explanation

Snow absorbs $100-80=20$ units; dark ground absorbs $100-20=80$. The difference is $80-20=60$ units. Albedo concerns the reflected fraction, so higher albedo means lower absorption under the stated conditions.
