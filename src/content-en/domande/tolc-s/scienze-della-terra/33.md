An aquifer initially stores 30 million cubic metres. Each year it receives 4 million and loses 6 million to pumping; there are no other fluxes. With these constant values, how much does it store after three years?

- 28 million cubic metres
- 36 million cubic metres
- 24 million cubic metres
- 12 million cubic metres
- 42 million cubic metres

## Explanation

The annual balance is $4-6=-2$ million cubic metres. Over three years storage changes by $3(-2)=-6$ million, falling to $30-6=24$ million cubic metres. Subtracting only pumping would ignore the stated recharge.
