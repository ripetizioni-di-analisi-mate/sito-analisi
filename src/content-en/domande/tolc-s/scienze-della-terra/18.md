A valley has a broad floor and steep sides, a U-shaped cross-section, and striated rock surfaces. Which agent most probably shaped it?

- A river incising only a narrow V-shaped channel
- Wind accumulating sand dunes
- A moving glacier
- Dissolution producing underground cavities
- Sedimentation building a delta

## Explanation

Moving ice erodes the valley floor and sides and carries debris that can cut striations. A U-shaped section together with striated surfaces therefore supports a glacial origin. The shape does not prove that ice is still present today: it may be inherited from an earlier period.
