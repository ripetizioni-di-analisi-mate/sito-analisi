Lava cools so quickly that it forms mainly glass. Which interpretation is correct?

- Enormous crystals must have formed
- Atoms had insufficient time to organise into extensive crystals
- The material was never molten
- Glass is always a sedimentary rock
- Rapid solidification requires regional metamorphism

## Explanation

Crystal growth requires atomic organisation and time. Very rapid cooling may prevent it, preserving a glassy structure. The origin remains associated with a melt; lack of crystals does not automatically make the material sedimentary.
