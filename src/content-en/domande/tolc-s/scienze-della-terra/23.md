A conglomerate contains rounded granite fragments. Which time relation is required between those fragments and conglomerate deposition?

- The granite must be younger than the conglomerate
- Both must have formed at the same instant
- The granite in the fragments existed before deposition
- The granite formed by melting the already deposited conglomerate
- No relative time relation can be inferred

## Explanation

To be eroded, rounded, and included, the granite had to exist already. The fragment is therefore older than deposition of its host rock. This principle gives a relative order without automatically providing a numerical age.
