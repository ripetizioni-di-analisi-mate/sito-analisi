The same earthquake causes very different damage in two cities. Which statement correctly distinguishes magnitude and intensity?

- Magnitude is defined exclusively by damage to one building
- Intensity and magnitude are exact synonyms
- Intensity must be identical at every location
- The source has a magnitude on the chosen scale; intensity may vary between cities
- Different damage necessarily proves two separate earthquakes

## Explanation

Magnitude describes the source using a defined instrumental criterion. Intensity concerns local effects influenced by distance, ground characteristics, and building vulnerability. Different effects do not require different earthquakes.
