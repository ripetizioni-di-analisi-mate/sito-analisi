S waves do not pass directly through the outer core, whereas P waves can propagate within it. Which property is supported by these observations?

- The outer core is empty
- The outer core behaves as a liquid
- All of Earth is liquid
- S waves do not exist in solids
- The outer core is identical to the rigid crust

## Explanation

S waves require shear resistance, which a liquid does not sustain like a solid. Compressional P waves can propagate through liquids. The combined observations concern the outer core and do not imply that the whole mantle is molten.
