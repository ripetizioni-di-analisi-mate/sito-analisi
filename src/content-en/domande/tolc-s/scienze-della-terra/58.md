During an eruption, an ash cloud enters winds blowing steadily from west to east. In an initial assessment, other conditions equal, which sector should be considered more affected by ash transport?

- The western sector, because every particle always moves upwind
- Only the crater, because wind cannot transport ash
- The sector east of the volcano, downwind
- Only lower valleys, regardless of wind
- Necessarily all directions equally

## Explanation

Suspended particles can travel in the wind direction, hence eastwards. Fallout also depends on grain size, cloud height, and wind changes; the question holds these comparable. Ash distribution may differ from that of flows channelled by topography.
