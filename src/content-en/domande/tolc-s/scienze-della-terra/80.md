Ignoring local complications, at which lunar phases does Sun-Earth-Moon alignment favour a larger tidal range?

- First and last quarter only
- Only during a visible eclipse
- Every phase must have exactly the same range
- Only at the summer solstice
- New and full moon

## Explanation

At new and full moon, solar and lunar tidal effects tend to reinforce through alignment geometry. At quarter phases their combination tends to reduce tidal range. The approximate alignment for spring tides does not require the exact alignment of an eclipse.
