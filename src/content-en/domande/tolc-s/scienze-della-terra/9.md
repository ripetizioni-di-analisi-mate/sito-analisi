Lake water becomes vapour, forms droplets in a cloud, and then falls as rain. Which sequence describes these three steps?

- Condensation, infiltration, evaporation
- Evaporation, precipitation, condensation
- Infiltration, melting, runoff
- Evaporation, condensation, precipitation
- Sublimation, freezing, infiltration

## Explanation

The transition from liquid to vapour is evaporation. Droplet formation from vapour is condensation; their fall from the cloud is precipitation. Infiltration may follow arrival at the ground, but it is not one of the three stated steps.
