A closed rigid $1\,\mathrm{m^3}$ container holds $18\,\mathrm{g}$ of water vapour. At the final temperature, the maximum equilibrium vapour amount in that volume is $15\,\mathrm{g}$. Neglecting other transformations, how much water condenses?

- $15\,\mathrm{g}$
- $18\,\mathrm{g}$
- $3\,\mathrm{g}$
- $33\,\mathrm{g}$
- $0\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

In the closed volume, total water mass remains $18\,\mathrm{g}$. At the final temperature, $15\,\mathrm{g}$ can remain as equilibrium vapour. The excess becomes liquid: $18-15=3\,\mathrm{g}$. Not all water condenses because some remains in the gas phase.
