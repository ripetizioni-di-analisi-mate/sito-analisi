A sample lies below the crust-mantle boundary but within the rigid portion moving with a plate. Which regions does it belong to?

- Crust and lithosphere
- Mantle and outer core
- Crust and asthenosphere
- Mantle and lithosphere
- Inner core and lithosphere

## Explanation

Its position below the compositional boundary places it in the mantle. Its rigid behaviour and membership of the plate place it in the lithosphere. The classifications use different criteria: the lithosphere includes part of the mantle as well as the crust.
