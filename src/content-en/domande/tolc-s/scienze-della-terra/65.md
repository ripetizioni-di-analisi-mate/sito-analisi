A fault cuts layers A and B but ends beneath layer C, which was deposited later and remains undeformed. Which order is compatible?

- Faulting, deposition of A and B, deposition of C
- Deposition of C, faulting, deposition of A and B
- Simultaneous deposition of all layers after faulting
- The fault must be younger than C
- Deposition of A and B, faulting, deposition of C

## Explanation

The fault postdates the rocks it cuts, so it follows A and B. C covers the structure without being affected and is stated to be later: its deposition follows the described movement. The data do not generally exclude future movement, but order the events shown.
