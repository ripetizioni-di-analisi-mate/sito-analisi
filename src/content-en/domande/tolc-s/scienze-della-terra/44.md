Water enters a crack, repeatedly freezes, and helps separate fragments that remain in place. Which description is most precise?

- Marine deposition
- Rock melting
- River transport already demonstrated
- Mechanical weathering without transport necessarily occurring
- Mineral precipitation from solution

## Explanation

Ice can widen cracks and fragment rock without requiring chemical transformation. Because the pieces remain in place, the text documents weathering but not transport. Erosion and transport could follow, but the data do not establish them.
