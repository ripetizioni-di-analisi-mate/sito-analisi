In a homogeneous medium, P and S waves emitted simultaneously travel the same distance at speeds of $6$ and $3\,\mathrm{km/s}$ respectively. S waves arrive $10\,\mathrm{s}$ after P waves. What is the source distance along the path?

- $30\,\mathrm{km}$
- $90\,\mathrm{km}$
- $60\,\mathrm{km}$
- $6\,\mathrm{km}$
- $120\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

Using the stated units, $t_S=\dfrac d3$ and $t_P=\dfrac d6$. Their difference satisfies $\dfrac d3-\dfrac d6=\dfrac d6=10$, so $d=60\,\mathrm{km}$. The delay is not the total travel time of either individual wave.
