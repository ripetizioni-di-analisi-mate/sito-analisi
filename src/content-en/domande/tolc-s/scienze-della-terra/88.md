Two continental masses converge after an ocean closes. Which deformation is consistent with formation of a major mountain range?

- Mandatory stretching with uniform thinning
- Crust creation through separation of the continental masses
- Horizontal shortening and crustal thickening
- Lateral sliding alone without shortening
- Systematic thinning caused by convergence alone

## Explanation

Continental convergence compresses and shortens crust, producing folds and thrusts that can thicken it. Mountain uplift is connected with this deformation. This differs from the spreading and crust-formation model of a ridge.
