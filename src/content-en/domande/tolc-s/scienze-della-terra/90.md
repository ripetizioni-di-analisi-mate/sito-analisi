An uninhabited volcanic island has a high eruption probability during the study interval. Which statement correctly distinguishes hazard and human exposure?

- No inhabitants means eruptions are impossible
- Hazard and exposure are always identical
- Eruption probability depends only on building numbers
- Low exposure proves the volcano is extinct
- It can have high hazard but low human exposure

## Explanation

Phenomenon probability describes hazard; the presence of people describes part of exposure. The components can vary independently. An uninhabited island can erupt while on-site human exposure remains low in the stated context.
