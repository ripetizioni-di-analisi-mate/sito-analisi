A municipality moves a warehouse out of a flood-prone zone and strengthens a school that remains in the same zone. Which components does it mainly change, respectively?

- Warehouse vulnerability and rainfall hazard
- Flood hazard in both cases
- Earthquake magnitude and rainfall duration
- Rainfall probability and evaporation
- Warehouse exposure and school vulnerability

## Explanation

Moving the warehouse removes assets from the affected area, reducing exposure. Strengthening the school reduces its susceptibility to damage, or vulnerability. Neither measure alone prevents the flood from occurring.
