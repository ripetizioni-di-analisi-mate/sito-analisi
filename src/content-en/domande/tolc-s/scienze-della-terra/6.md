At a convergent boundary, an earthquake zone descends from a trench beneath the upper plate. Which material is mainly descending?

- Oceanic lithosphere
- Ocean water only
- Surface sediment alone without crust
- Liquid outer core
- New crust rising from a ridge

## Explanation

Subduction involves oceanic lithosphere: crust and rigid uppermost mantle. The dipping earthquake zone follows the descending plate. Water and sediment may be involved, but they do not alone constitute the subducting slab.
