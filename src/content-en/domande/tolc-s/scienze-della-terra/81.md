A sedimentary rock now at an elevation of 2000 metres contains marine fossils in life position. Which reconstruction is compatible without automatically assigning that ancient elevation to the sea?

- Deposition in a marine environment followed by rock uplift
- Deposition in an exclusively freshwater lake, demonstrated by marine fossils in life position
- Marine deposition at the present elevation with no later change in sea level or land elevation
- Fossil formation by recrystallisation during uplift
- Solidification of lava, demonstrated by the fossils

## Explanation

The fossils document a marine environment at deposition. Present elevation belongs to a later time and may result from tectonic movement. The observation therefore separates original environment from present position without by itself determining a precise ancient sea level.
