Two magmas have the same initial gas content and comparable conditions, but one is more viscous. Which effect may favour an explosive eruption of the more viscous magma?

- Gas escape always becomes faster as viscosity increases
- Greater resistance to gas escape and possible pressure buildup
- Bubbles must disappear solely because of viscosity
- No relationship between gases and fragmentation
- Every viscous magma automatically becomes a quiet lava flow

## Explanation

Viscous magma can hinder bubble separation and escape. Gas pressure may then contribute to explosive fragmentation. This is a tendency under comparable conditions, not a rule describing every eruption with one parameter.
