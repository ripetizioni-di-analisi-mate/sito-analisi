A buried rock develops new minerals as pressure and temperature rise, while remaining solid. Which process is documented?

- Lava crystallisation
- River deposition
- Metamorphism
- Complete melting followed by cooling
- Cementation of newly deposited sand

## Explanation

New minerals form in response to pressure and temperature conditions without overall melting. This is metamorphism. Heat alone does not make a rock igneous: a melt must subsequently solidify.
