When climbing a mountain, atmospheric pressure generally decreases. Which explanation is appropriate?

- The weight of overlying air per unit area decreases
- The weight of the overlying air column necessarily increases
- Pressure becomes zero at every elevation above sea level
- Pressure is determined only by oxygen percentage
- Pressure must remain constant while gravity is present

## Explanation

Atmospheric pressure is related to the weight of the overlying air column. At greater altitude, some atmosphere lies below the observer, leaving less weight above. Gravity need not disappear and composition need not change radically.
