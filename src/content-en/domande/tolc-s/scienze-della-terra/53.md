A geologist compares ripples preserved in ancient sandstone with those formed by currents today. Which principle is being used without assuming all rates have always remained constant?

- An unchanging Earth surface
- All past climates must have been identical
- Actualism
- Catastrophic events are impossible
- Every rock must have the same age

## Explanation

Observable processes help interpret ancient structures when conditions are comparable. This is actualism. The principle concerns understanding mechanisms, not requiring constant rates or excluding sudden events.
