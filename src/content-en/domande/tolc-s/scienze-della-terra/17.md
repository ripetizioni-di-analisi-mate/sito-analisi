In a schist, platy minerals are aligned along preferred surfaces. Which condition can explain this metamorphic foliation?

- Rapid cooling forming only glass
- Solid-state deformation under directed stress
- Deposition of sand layers without metamorphism
- Pore cementation without deformation or recrystallisation
- Surface fragmentation caused only by freezing

## Explanation

Directed stress can promote recrystallisation and alignment of minerals during metamorphism. Foliation records this organisation, although not every metamorphic rock is foliated. It is neither a glassy texture nor simply a salt deposit.
