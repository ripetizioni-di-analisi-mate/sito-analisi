During a lunar eclipse, the Moon enters Earth’s shadow. What is the order of the three bodies along the alignment?

- Sun, Moon, Earth
- Earth, Sun, Moon
- Moon, Sun, Earth
- Sun, Earth, Moon
- Sun, Earth, and Moon forming a right angle

## Explanation

To cast its shadow on the Moon, Earth must lie between the Sun and Moon. Starting from the Sun, the order is Sun, Earth, Moon. The Moon between the Sun and Earth instead gives the geometry of a solar eclipse.
