Vegetation is removed from a slope, leaving exposed soil. Other conditions equal, which effect can increase surface erosion?

- Less protection from raindrops and less root binding of soil
- Less direct exposure of soil to raindrop impact
- Greater cohesion caused by removal of the root system
- No runoff because every rainfall event must infiltrate completely
- Reduced runoff speed caused by loss of vegetation obstacles

## Explanation

Canopy and vegetation cover reduce raindrop impact; roots help hold soil. Removing them can make particles easier to detach and transport. This links the biosphere, water, and solid surface: loss of cover does not imply that all rain infiltrates.
