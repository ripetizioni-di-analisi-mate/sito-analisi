A river enters a lake and slows. For grains of equal density but different sizes, which deposit distribution is most plausible?

- Coarse grains near the mouth and fines carried farther
- Fine grains always before coarse grains
- All grains remain suspended indefinitely
- Coarse grains immediately turn into clay
- Deposition occurs only if all water evaporates

## Explanation

Slowing reduces the current’s ability to carry and suspend larger grains. These tend to settle near the inlet; fine particles can remain suspended farther away. Mechanical deposition requires neither evaporation nor chemical transformation of grains.
