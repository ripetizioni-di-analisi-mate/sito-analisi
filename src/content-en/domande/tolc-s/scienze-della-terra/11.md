In a vertical cross-section, the surface is $z=0$ and the hypocentre is $H=(18,-12)\,\mathrm{km}$. What are the epicentre’s coordinates in the same section?

- $(18,0)\,\mathrm{km}$
- $(0,-12)\,\mathrm{km}$
- $(18,12)\,\mathrm{km}$
- $(0,0)\,\mathrm{km}$
- $(-18,0)\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

The epicentre lies vertically above the hypocentre at the surface. It therefore keeps horizontal coordinate $18$ and has elevation $z=0$: $E=(18,0)\,\mathrm{km}$. The point $(18,12)$ is above the surface and does not meet the definition.
