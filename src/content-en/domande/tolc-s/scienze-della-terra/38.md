Which order lists geological time units from broadest to shortest?

- Age, epoch, period, era, eon
- Era, eon, epoch, period, age
- Eon, era, period, epoch, age
- Period, era, eon, age, epoch
- Eon, period, era, age, epoch

## Explanation

An eon contains eras; an era contains periods; a period contains epochs, subdivided into ages. This is a hierarchy of time divisions. It does not imply that all eras or all periods have equal duration.
