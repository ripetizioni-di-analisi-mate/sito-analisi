Over long periods, continents receive rainfall and return water to the sea through rivers. Which transfer connects ocean evaporation with continental rainfall?

- Atmospheric moisture transport by winds
- River runoff from the continent towards the ocean
- Infiltration of rainfall into continental soil
- Continental evapotranspiration alone without any oceanic contribution
- Evaporation and precipitation both occurring exclusively over the ocean

## Explanation

Evaporation transfers water from the ocean to the atmosphere; winds can move vapour and clouds towards land. Precipitation and runoff complete return pathways. Each molecule need not immediately follow the same path or pass through the deep interior.
