A volcanic rock contains large crystals within a mass of very small crystals. Which history is compatible with this porphyritic texture?

- A single instantaneous cooling event without any crystal growth
- Slow early growth of some crystals, followed by faster cooling of the remaining material
- Salt deposition alone from evaporating water
- Sand compaction without melt
- Crystallisation of every mineral only after complete glassy solidification

## Explanation

The large crystals had time to grow while melt remained. A later faster stage limits crystal size in the remaining groundmass. The size contrast therefore suggests a multistage history rather than necessarily one cooling rate.
