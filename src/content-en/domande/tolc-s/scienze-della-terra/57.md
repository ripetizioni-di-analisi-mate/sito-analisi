Wind transports sand into mobile ridges with a steep downwind slope. Which landform is described?

- Talus cones dominated by gravitational falls
- Dunes
- River deltas
- Glacial moraines
- Marine abrasion terraces

## Explanation

Wind transport and accumulation of sand form dunes. The downwind slope can grow through deposition and small grain avalanches. The agent and material distinguish this landform from tectonic or glacial structures.
