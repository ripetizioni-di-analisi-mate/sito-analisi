Along an ocean-continent boundary, a trench is observed offshore and a volcanic chain on the continent. Which model explains both structures together?

- Symmetrical spreading from a ridge on the continent
- Subduction of oceanic lithosphere beneath the continent
- Transform sliding alone without subduction
- River erosion of a passive margin without subduction
- Cooling and subsidence of a passive margin without convergence

## Explanation

Bending and descent of the oceanic plate explain the trench. Fluids released at depth promote partial melting in the overlying mantle, feeding the volcanic arc. The model connects two observations; a purely transform boundary does not normally produce this association.
