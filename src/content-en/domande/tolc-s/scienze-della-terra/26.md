A city records an unusually cold week during a period of rising average temperature over many decades. Which conclusion is justified?

- A cold weather episode can coexist with a warming climate trend
- The week alone proves the multidecadal average is false
- Climate always equals one day’s weather
- All locations must have the same temperature each week
- An increasing average excludes all variability

## Explanation

Weather concerns short-term conditions, while climate describes distributions and statistics over long periods. An average can rise while fluctuations and cold episodes remain. Testing the trend requires an adequate record, not selection of a single week.
