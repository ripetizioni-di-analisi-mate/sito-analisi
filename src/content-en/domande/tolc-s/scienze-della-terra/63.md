Granite is weathered; fragments are transported, deposited as sand, and cemented. How is the new product classified?

- Necessarily igneous rock because the source was igneous
- Liquid magma
- Sedimentary rock, such as sandstone
- Metamorphic rock merely because quartz is present
- A pure mineral of necessarily unique composition

## Explanation

Final classification depends on the latest formation process. Transport, deposition, and cementation of sand produce sedimentary rock. Granite provenance does not automatically preserve an igneous classification after destruction and reorganisation of the material.
