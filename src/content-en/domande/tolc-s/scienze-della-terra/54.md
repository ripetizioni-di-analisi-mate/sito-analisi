At an equinox, on an unobstructed horizon at a temperate latitude, an observer faces sunrise. Which cardinal direction is approximately on the observer’s left?

- South
- East
- West
- North
- The zenith

## Explanation

At an equinox the Sun rises approximately due east. Facing east places north on the left and south on the right; west is behind. The zenith is vertically overhead rather than a cardinal direction on the horizon.
