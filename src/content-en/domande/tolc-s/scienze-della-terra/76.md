Mirror sequences of normal- and reversed-polarity basalt occur on either side of a ridge. Which process explains both the record and its symmetry?

- Cooling at the ridge during periods of different field polarity and movement away on both sides
- Magnetisation caused only by present seawater
- All basalts solidified at the same instant
- The magnetic field has never reversed
- Oceanic crust forms only at trenches

## Explanation

Magnetic minerals record field direction during cooling. Continuing crust formation and outward movement preserve the polarity sequence on both sides. Symmetry connects a time record with seafloor motion.
