Water evaporates from a closed basin while dissolved salts remain. Once saturation is reached, which process can form a chemical sedimentary rock?

- Salt melting merely because water decreases
- Mandatory formation of granite
- Regional metamorphism without changes in pressure or temperature
- Salt precipitation forming evaporites
- All salts turning into vapour with the water

## Explanation

Evaporation reduces solvent volume and raises solute concentration. At saturation, some salts can precipitate and accumulate. The origin is chemical sedimentary; magma melting and cooling are unnecessary.
