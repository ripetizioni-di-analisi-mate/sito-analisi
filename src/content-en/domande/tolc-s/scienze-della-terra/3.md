To define a light-year, the speed of light in a vacuum is multiplied by the duration of one year. Which quantity results?

- A duration
- An acceleration
- A distance
- A frequency
- A speed

## Explanation

Speed has dimensions of length divided by time. Multiplying by a time cancels that dimension: $d=ct$. A light-year therefore measures distance, even though its name contains a word for a time interval.
