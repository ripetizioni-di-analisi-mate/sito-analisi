Two drops fall on opposite sides of a ridge separating two drainage basins and flow over the surface. What is expected?

- They must always reach the same river before any other outlet
- They must first infiltrate despite the stated surface runoff
- Destination cannot depend on topography
- They may reach different outlets because the ridge is a drainage divide
- A drainage divide always coincides with the main river bed

## Explanation

A drainage divide is the topographic boundary between areas directing runoff to different outlets. Drops on opposite slopes may therefore follow different drainage networks. The question specifies surface flow, avoiding confusion with more complex underground pathways.
