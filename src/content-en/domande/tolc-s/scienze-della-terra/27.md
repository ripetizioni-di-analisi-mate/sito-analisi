Which situation is especially favourable for generating a tectonic tsunami?

- Purely horizontal fault motion on a flat seafloor without displacing water
- Rapid vertical displacement of a large seafloor area
- A small deep earthquake without appreciable seafloor deformation
- A slow uniform seasonal sea-level change
- Simple propagation of S waves through seawater

## Explanation

Extensive vertical seafloor displacement disturbs the overlying water column and may generate tsunami waves. Displacement geometry, size, and speed matter. An earthquake alone is insufficient, and the phenomenon differs from wind-generated waves.
