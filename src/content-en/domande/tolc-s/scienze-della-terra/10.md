A thunderstorm develops in the atmospheric layer nearest the surface, which contains abundant water vapour. Which layer is mainly involved?

- Stratosphere
- Mesosphere
- Thermosphere
- Exosphere
- Troposphere

## Explanation

The troposphere is the lowest layer and contains much of the atmospheric water vapour. Most clouds and weather develop there. The stratosphere lies above it and contains the ozone layer; the other layers are higher still.
