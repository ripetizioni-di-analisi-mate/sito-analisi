A fossil-bearing layer that has not been overturned lies above ash deposited 120 million years ago and below ash deposited 100 million years ago. The ash ages are reliable. Which age is compatible with the layer?

- 130 million years
- 90 million years
- 20 million years
- 110 million years
- 220 million years

## Explanation

The layer was deposited after the lower ash, so it is younger than 120 million years, and before the upper ash, so it is older than 100 million years. The compatible age is 110 million years. The 20-million-year difference is an interval, not the layer’s age.
