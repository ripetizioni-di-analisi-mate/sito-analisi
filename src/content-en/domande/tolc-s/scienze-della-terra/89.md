Which radiative interaction is central to the atmospheric greenhouse effect?

- Ultraviolet absorption by ozone as the sole mechanism
- Reflection of solar radiation by clouds as the sole mechanism
- Retention of warm air solely by preventing convective motion
- Absorption and re-emission of some infrared radiation emitted by Earth
- Increased albedo as the only greenhouse-gas mechanism

## Explanation

The heated surface emits infrared radiation. Greenhouse gases absorb some wavelength bands and re-emit energy in different directions, changing exchanges and equilibrium temperature. They do not create energy or make the atmosphere opaque at every wavelength.
