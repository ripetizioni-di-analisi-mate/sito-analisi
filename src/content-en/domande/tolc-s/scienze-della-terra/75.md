Which reservoir contains most of Earth’s total water, including both saltwater and freshwater?

- Rivers
- Freshwater lakes
- Atmospheric vapour
- Living organisms
- Oceans

## Explanation

Oceans are by far the largest reservoir of Earth’s total water. Rivers, lakes, atmosphere, and organisms matter for fluxes but hold much smaller fractions. The answer would differ if the question concerned freshwater storage only.
