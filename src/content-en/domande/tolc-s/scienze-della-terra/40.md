After crystallisation, a mineral loses some parent isotope through fluid circulation. A closed-system model with known initial amount is nevertheless used, attributing all loss to decay. How is the apparent age affected?

- It must be zero
- It certainly equals the true age
- It is always negative
- The remaining amount appears greater than the initial amount
- It is too old

## Explanation

The model interprets a smaller remaining amount as more elapsed time. Physical parent loss adds to radioactive decay, so calculated time is overestimated. This conclusion depends on the stated model; other open isotopic systems can behave differently.
