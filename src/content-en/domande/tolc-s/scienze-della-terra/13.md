A closed system initially contains $80$ units of a parent isotope. Its half-life is $10$ million years. How many parent units remain after $20$ million years?

- 40
- 0
- 20
- 60
- 10

## Explanation

Two half-lives have passed: $\dfrac{20}{10}=2$. After the first, $\dfrac{80}{2}=40$ remain; after the second, $\dfrac{40}{2}=20$. Equivalently, $80\left(\dfrac12\right)^2=20$. Half the initial amount is not subtracted twice: each half-life acts on the amount still present.
