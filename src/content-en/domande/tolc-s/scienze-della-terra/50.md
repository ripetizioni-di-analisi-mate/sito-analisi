A plant uses water heated at depth by Earth’s heat. Which energy source does it mainly exploit?

- Wind
- Tidal
- Solar photovoltaic
- Chemical energy from directly burning the fluid
- Geothermal

## Explanation

The stated source is Earth’s internal heat, so the energy is geothermal. Water is the heat-carrying fluid, not the primary heat source. Use must still consider local replenishment of fluids and heat.
