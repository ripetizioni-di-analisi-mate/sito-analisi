Land fossils of the same species and age occur on continents now separated by a wide ocean. In Wegener’s reasoning, which interpretation does this support?

- Continental immobility demonstrated by fossil similarity alone
- A former connection between the continents
- Formation of the terrestrial fossils on the present ocean floor
- A drift speed calculable from species identity alone
- Determination of the exact drift mechanism from the fossils alone

## Explanation

Former land continuity provides a coherent explanation for the distribution of terrestrial organisms. The observation is evidence to compare with rocks, structures, and other findings. Alone it measures neither movement speed nor the mechanism causing it.
