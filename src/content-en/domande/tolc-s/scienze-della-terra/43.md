A mineral scratches gypsum (Mohs hardness 2) without being scratched by it; calcite (hardness 3) scratches the mineral, but the mineral cannot scratch calcite. Assuming reliable tests, what follows?

- Its hardness is exactly 5
- Its hardness is below 2
- Its hardness lies between 2 and 3
- Its hardness is above 3
- It must be twice as resistant to breaking as gypsum

## Explanation

The reciprocal tests show that the sample is harder than gypsum and softer than calcite. This gives an interval between 2 and 3 without an exact value. The scale measures relative scratch resistance, not resistance to breaking.
