Water containing a soluble contaminant infiltrates and reaches an aquifer without retention or degradation of the contaminant. Which conclusion is correct?

- Every infiltration event automatically guarantees drinking water
- Recharge can increase stored water while worsening its quality
- Contaminant mass cannot increase as long as water volume increases
- Increasing volume necessarily makes contaminant concentration zero
- High permeability always prevents solute transport

## Explanation

Quantity and quality are distinct aspects. Infiltration adds water but, under the stated conditions, also transfers contaminant into the aquifer. The subsurface is not a perfect filter in every situation; the text explicitly excludes removal or degradation.
