In a simplified model, one event type has an annual probability of $2\%$ and would cause a loss of $300\,000$ euros. Otherwise the loss is zero. What is the expected annual loss?

- $150\,000$ euros
- $300\,000$ euros
- $60\,000$ euros
- $6\,000$ euros
- $600$ euros

## Explanation

The theoretical average weights each loss by its probability: $0.02\cdot300\,000+0.98\cdot0=6\,000$ euros. This is not a certain loss each year. An actual year may have zero loss or, in the model, a loss of $300\,000$ euros.
