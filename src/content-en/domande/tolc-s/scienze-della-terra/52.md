A very hot, fast mixture of gas, ash, and fragments moves down a volcano. Which phenomenon best describes the observation?

- A flow consisting only of liquid lava
- Pyroclastic density current
- A cold mudflow rich in liquid water
- An eruption cloud rising vertically above the crater
- Emission of gases alone from a fumarole

## Explanation

Hot gas and fragments moving down slopes characterise a pyroclastic density current. A lava flow instead consists mainly of molten material and behaves differently. Identifying the eruptive product helps identify the hazard type.
