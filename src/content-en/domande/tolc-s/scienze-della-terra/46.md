Along a profile across an active ridge, which basalt-age pattern is most consistent with regular spreading on both sides?

- Minimum age at the axis, increasing away on both sides
- Maximum age at the axis and zero everywhere at the edges
- Identical age for every sample regardless of distance
- Age always increasing from left to right, even across the axis
- Ages determined only by water depth, independently of spreading

## Explanation

New basalt forms near the axis and is progressively carried away. More distant rocks have had more time to travel and are on average older. Symmetry is expected in the stated regular-spreading model, without requiring every real ridge to be perfectly symmetrical.
