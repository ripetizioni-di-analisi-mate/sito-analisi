Which process currently produces part of Earth’s internal heat through changes in atomic nuclei?

- Conduction alone, which transfers heat rather than providing that source
- Convection alone, which redistributes heat
- Solar-radiation absorption in surface layers
- Gravitational sedimentation of grains alone
- Radioactive decay of isotopes in rocks

## Explanation

Decay of unstable isotopes releases energy contributing to radiogenic heat. Residual heat from Earth’s formation and differentiation is another source. Solar heating instead mainly concerns surface processes.
