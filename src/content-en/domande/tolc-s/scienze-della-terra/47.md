One natural sample has an ordered crystal lattice and composition within defined limits; another is an aggregate of quartz, feldspars, and micas. Which distinction is correct?

- Both must be pure chemical elements
- The first can be a mineral; the second is a rock
- Every aggregate is a single mineral
- A mineral cannot have an ordered lattice
- A rock must contain only one chemical element

## Explanation

Lattice and composition are diagnostic mineral characteristics. A natural association of several minerals constitutes a rock, as in the stated aggregate. A mineral need not be a pure element: many contain multiple elements.
