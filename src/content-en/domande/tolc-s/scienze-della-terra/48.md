At temperate latitudes, which astronomical event corresponds to approximately equal day and night lengths?

- Only the summer solstice
- Only the winter solstice
- An equinox
- Every new moon
- Every perihelion passage

## Explanation

At equinoxes, illumination gives approximately equal daylight and night lengths. Solstices instead mark the annual extremes of daylight duration. Exact observed lengths also depend on refraction and how sunrise and sunset are defined.
