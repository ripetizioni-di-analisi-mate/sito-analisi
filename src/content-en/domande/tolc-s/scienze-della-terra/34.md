In a model, light takes exactly 8 minutes to travel from the Sun to Earth. A light signal leaves the Sun at 12\:00 according to synchronised clocks. When is it received on Earth?

- At 12\:00
- At 11\:52
- At 12\:16
- At 12\:08
- One year later

## Explanation

Reception occurs after the propagation time: 12\:00 plus 8 minutes gives 12\:08. The observer receives information about an event that has already occurred. The stated 8 minutes describe one-way travel and must not be doubled.
