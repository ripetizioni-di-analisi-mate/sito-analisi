A large ice sheet melts and the ground beneath slowly rises through isostatic readjustment. Which explanation is consistent?

- Erosion alone necessarily increases ice-sheet weight
- Crust rises because the melting ice sheet becomes heavier
- The response requires a mantle unable to deform
- Uplift proves the mantle is wholly liquid
- Removing the load allows slow readjustment of deep materials

## Explanation

The ice imposed a load on the lithosphere. Removing it allows slow readjustment, consistent with mantle deformation over long timescales. The process does not require a wholly liquid mantle: a solid can deform slowly under stress.
