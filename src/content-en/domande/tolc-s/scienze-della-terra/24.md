After prolonged rain, pore-water pressure rises within a slope. Which consequence can favour sliding?

- Friction must rise because every pore fills
- Total slope weight must decrease
- An increase in effective contact pressure between grains
- Reduced effective resistance along sliding surfaces
- Pore pressure must decrease

## Explanation

Pore water can reduce the effective pressure holding materials in contact along potential sliding surfaces. Resistance may decrease while gravity continues to act. Rainfall is a possible trigger, not proof that every slope will fail.
