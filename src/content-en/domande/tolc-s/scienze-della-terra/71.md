One rock has many pores, but almost all are isolated. Another has fewer, well-connected pores. Which conclusion avoids confusing porosity and permeability?

- The first can have high porosity but low permeability
- The first must be more permeable
- The two properties are always numerically identical
- A permeable rock cannot contain water
- Pore connectivity never affects flow

## Explanation

Porosity measures the fraction of volume occupied by voids; permeability also depends on connectivity and geometry. Isolated pores contribute to void volume but not to continuous flow paths. The data therefore do not imply that greater porosity necessarily means greater permeability.
