Weakly acidic water passes through limestone and progressively dissolves part of it, forming cavities. How is the rock-changing process classified?

- Mechanical fragmentation alone without reactions
- Chemical weathering
- Magmatic melting
- Snow compaction
- Crystallisation from a melt

## Explanation

Dissolution transfers rock components into solution through chemical interactions. It is therefore chemical weathering, important in karst landscapes. It differs from simple fragmentation with essentially unchanged composition.
