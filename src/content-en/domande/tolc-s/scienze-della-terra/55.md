Why is oil considered nonrenewable on human societal timescales?

- Every material of organic origin is necessarily nonrenewable
- Carbon released by burning cannot participate in other natural cycles
- Renewable only means recyclable after combustion
- Renewability depends only on engine efficiency
- Geological formation is much slower than consumption

## Explanation

Renewability requires comparing replenishment and use timescales. Oil forms through geological processes that are very slow compared with extraction and consumption. Natural origin therefore does not imply sufficiently rapid replenishment.
