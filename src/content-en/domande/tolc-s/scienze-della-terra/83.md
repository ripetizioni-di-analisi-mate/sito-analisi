For correlating distant layers in time, which fossil organism is generally most useful as an index fossil?

- A local species lasting hundreds of millions of years
- Any organism without knowing its time range
- A geographically widespread species with a short time range
- A species present only in one undated sample
- A species existing throughout Earth’s entire history

## Explanation

A short range restricts the possible layer age; wide distribution permits regional comparisons. Both characteristics are useful. A long-lived species may inform about environment but constrains time less precisely.
