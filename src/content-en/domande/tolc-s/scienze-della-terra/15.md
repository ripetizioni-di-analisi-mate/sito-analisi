An identified deposit contains useful material extractable with available technology and economically viable under the other applicable criteria. What is this exploitable portion called?

- Total resource, including every nonexploitable portion
- Ore grade
- Annual production already extracted
- Recovery-process efficiency
- Reserve

## Explanation

A reserve is the identified part of a resource meeting the adopted exploitation criteria. Mineral presence alone is insufficient: technical and economic feasibility and other relevant criteria matter. A reserve is material still available, not production already extracted.
