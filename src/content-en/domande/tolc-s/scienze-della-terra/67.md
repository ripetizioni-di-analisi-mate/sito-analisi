Hot magma rises through a conduit and carries energy from depth towards the surface. Which term describes heat transport associated with the moving magma?

- Conduction alone in stationary material
- Advection
- Radiation through a vacuum
- Molecular diffusion alone without magma transport
- Local radiogenic production alone without transport

## Explanation

Energy is carried with a moving mass of material: this is advection. Conduction into the walls and other exchanges may also occur. The question identifies the contribution specifically associated with rising magma.
