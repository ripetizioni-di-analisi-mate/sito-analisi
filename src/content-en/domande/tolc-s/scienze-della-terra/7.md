A rock forms by solidification of a melt, without sediment deposition. Which genetic group does it belong to?

- Clastic sedimentary
- Igneous
- Evaporitic sedimentary
- Contact metamorphic
- Regional metamorphic

## Explanation

The decisive criterion is solidification of a melt, which defines an igneous origin. Cooling rate affects texture without changing this genetic group. Metamorphism instead transforms a pre-existing rock mainly in the solid state.
