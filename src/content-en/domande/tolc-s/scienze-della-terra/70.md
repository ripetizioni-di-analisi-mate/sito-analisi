Two plates slide horizontally past one another along an active boundary segment without systematic creation or consumption of lithosphere. Which boundary is described?

- Divergent ridge
- Convergent subduction
- Frontal continental collision
- A boundary defined only by the coastline
- Transform

## Explanation

Relative sideways sliding identifies a transform boundary. It may generate earthquakes without producing new crust like a ridge or consuming lithosphere like subduction. A coastline does not necessarily define a plate boundary.
