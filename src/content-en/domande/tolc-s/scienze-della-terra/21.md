$200\,\mathrm{t}$ of ore with a metal grade of $3\%$ is extracted. Processing recovers $50\%$ of the contained metal. How much metal is obtained?

- $3\,\mathrm{t}$
- $6\,\mathrm{t}$
- $100\,\mathrm{t}$
- $30\,\mathrm{t}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{t}$

## Explanation

The contained metal is $200\cdot0.03=6\,\mathrm{t}$. Recovery applies to these $6\,\mathrm{t}$, not to all extracted mass: $6\cdot0.50=3\,\mathrm{t}$. The result distinguishes deposit grade from processing efficiency.
