During photosynthesis, a land plant absorbs carbon dioxide from the air and incorporates its carbon into organic matter. Which transfer between spheres is described?

- From atmosphere to biosphere
- From biosphere to atmosphere
- From hydrosphere to lithosphere
- From lithosphere to atmosphere
- No matter transfer

## Explanation

Carbon initially present in atmospheric carbon dioxide enters the plant’s organic molecules. This is matter transfer into the biosphere, accompanied by energy transformations. Photosynthesis does not create carbon atoms from nothing.
