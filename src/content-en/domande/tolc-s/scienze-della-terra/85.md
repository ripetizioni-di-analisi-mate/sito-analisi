Around an igneous intrusion lies a zone of rocks recrystallised in the solid state, with stronger effects near the contact. Which process is most directly indicated?

- Wind deposition
- Glacial erosion
- Crystallisation of the entire zone from a single melt
- Compaction of newly deposited sediment without heating
- Contact metamorphism

## Explanation

The intrusion transfers heat to surrounding rocks and can change their minerals without completely melting them. This altered zone is a contact metamorphic aureole. The text distinguishes recrystallised host rocks from the igneous body.
