A stationary rock wall transfers heat from its warm face to its cool face without bulk material movement. Which mechanism describes transfer within the wall?

- Magma advection
- River transport
- Convection of the entire rock
- Mechanical erosion
- Conduction

## Explanation

The decisive condition is the absence of bulk matter movement through the wall. Energy transfers by conduction. Advection and convection instead involve material transport; erosion and river transport describe other processes.
