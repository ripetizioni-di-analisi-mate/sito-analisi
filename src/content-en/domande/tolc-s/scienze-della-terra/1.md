A location alternates between daylight and night in a complete cycle lasting about one day. Which motion directly explains this alternation?

- Earth’s rotation about its axis
- Earth’s revolution around the Sun
- Precession of Earth’s axis
- The Moon’s revolution around Earth
- The drift of lithospheric plates

## Explanation

As Earth rotates, the location alternately faces the Sun and points away from it. The daily period identifies rotation; revolution around the Sun takes about one year. Lunar and plate motions do not determine this local alternation.
