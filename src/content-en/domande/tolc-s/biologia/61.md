An illuminated leaf exports sucrose to a growing root. Which description of the connection is correct?

- Phloem carries sugars from source to sink
- Xylem carries only sucrose in every plant
- The root must photosynthesize to receive sugars
- Sucrose moves only as vapor through stomata
- Transport uses plant neurons

## Explanation

The producing leaf acts as a source, while the root using or storing sugars is a sink. Phloem connects these functions and transports organic compounds. Direction is defined by the source-sink relationship, not a universal upward or downward rule. Xylem mainly transports water and minerals.
