In animal oogenesis, meiotic divisions distribute cytoplasm very unequally. Which consequence distinguishes this case from four equivalent products?

- Every product must remain diploid
- One large egg cell and small polar bodies can arise from the same meiosis
- DNA replication occurs after each division
- Homologous chromosomes never separate
- Four eggs identical in size are produced

## Explanation

Meiosis concerns chromosome segregation, but cytoplasm partitioning can be asymmetric. In oogenesis most cytoplasm is retained in the cell destined to become the egg; other products are polar bodies. Four equivalent functional gametes therefore cannot be generalized to every meiosis. Reduced ploidy is compatible with different cell sizes.
