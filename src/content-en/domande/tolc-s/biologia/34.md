In a double-stranded DNA molecule, adenine makes up $30\%$ of all bases. What percentage is cytosine?

- $30\%$
- $40\%$
- $15\%$
- $20\%$
- $70\%$

## Explanation

In the duplex each A pairs with T, so T is also $30\%$. This leaves $100-30-30=40$ percentage points for G and C. Since G and C occur in equal amounts, each is $\dfrac{40}{2}=20\%$. The reasoning uses both complementary pairings and applies to the whole double-stranded DNA; it cannot automatically be transferred to one strand.
