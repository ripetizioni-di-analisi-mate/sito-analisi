In an oxygenic photosynthesis experiment, oxygen atoms in water molecules are labeled. In which gaseous product is this label directly found?

- In atmospheric nitrogen fixed
- In methane produced by the chloroplast
- In the hydrogen of every sugar
- In the molecular oxygen released
- In carbon dioxide absorbed before the experiment

## Explanation

Light reactions oxidize water and release molecular oxygen. Labeling oxygen atoms in water therefore allows them to be tracked into the oxygen gas produced. The carbon in sugars instead comes from carbon dioxide. The overall photosynthesis balance alone does not show this atomic origin: the tracer distinguishes possible sources.
