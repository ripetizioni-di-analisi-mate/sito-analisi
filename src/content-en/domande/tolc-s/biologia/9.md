During prophase I, a segment of a maternally derived chromatid is exchanged with the corresponding segment of a chromatid of the paternal homolog. How is the event described?

- An obligatory exchange between nonhomologous chromosomes
- Replication of the whole genome
- Translation of two mRNAs
- Crossing-over between nonsister chromatids of homologs
- Separation of sister chromatids

## Explanation

Homologs carry the same loci, although they may have different alleles. Crossing-over involves nonsister chromatids of the two homologs and generates new combinations of alleles along the chromosome. It is neither DNA replication nor copy separation. The corresponding segments and prophase I identify regular meiotic recombination rather than an exchange between nonhomologous chromosomes.
