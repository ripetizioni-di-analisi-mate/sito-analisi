The scientific names are *Quercus robur* and *Quercus ilex*. Which taxonomic level do they certainly share?

- Species
- Subspecies
- Local population
- Genus
- The same individual

## Explanation

The first term of a binomial name indicates the genus, here Quercus; the second distinguishes the species within the genus. Different full names indicate different species. Nomenclature does not establish whether individuals live in the same area or belong to one population, which by definition concerns a single species.
