An ion is accumulated in a cell against its electrochemical gradient. Which energy requirement is necessary?

- No energy source because all transport is passive
- Disappearance of the plasma membrane
- Conversion of the ion into water
- Obligatory passage through a vesicle
- An energy input, direct or coupled to another process

## Explanation

Moving an ion against its electrochemical gradient requires energy. Transport may use ATP directly or exploit, for example, a favorable flow of another solute. It is therefore incorrect to require direct ATP consumption by the same transporter in every case. Facilitated diffusion instead follows the gradient and cannot explain accumulation against it.
