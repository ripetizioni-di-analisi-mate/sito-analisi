Which set of observations supports the endosymbiotic origin of mitochondria?

- Complete absence of membranes and DNA
- Their own DNA and ribosomes with features of bacterial origin
- Production exclusively from isolated free ribosomes
- Occurrence only outside eukaryotic cells
- Identity with the cell nucleus

## Explanation

Mitochondria retain genetic material and protein synthesis machinery with features traceable to bacteria. Together with other molecular and structural evidence, this is consistent with descent from an organism incorporated into an ancestral cell. It does not show that they are autonomous organisms today: many functions depend on nuclear genes and the host cell.
