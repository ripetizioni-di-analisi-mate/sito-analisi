Which property of water contributes to dissolving many ionic salts?

- All water molecules lack partial charges
- Water converts every ion into a neutral atom
- Water destroys ion nuclei
- Every salt is entirely converted into glucose
- Polarity allows stabilizing interactions with ions

## Explanation

Partial charges in water interact with solute ions: the region near oxygen orients favorably toward cations and the hydrogen region toward anions. Hydration interactions help stabilize separated ions. Dissolving a salt does not require transforming elements or neutralizing every ion; actual solubility also depends on the salt lattice.
