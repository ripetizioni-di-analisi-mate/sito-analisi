A protein destined for secretion follows the classical pathway. After synthesis associated with rough endoplasmic reticulum, which route is consistent?

- Golgi apparatus, secretory vesicles, plasma membrane
- Nucleolus, chromosome, cellulose wall
- Mitochondrial matrix, chloroplast, nucleus
- DNA, tRNA, free amino acids
- Centriole, spindle, chromatids

## Explanation

The protein enters the endomembrane system, passes from the reticulum to the Golgi where it may be modified and sorted, and is then carried in vesicles. Vesicle fusion with the membrane enables secretion by exocytosis. This route concerns a specific cellular destination; not every protein synthesized in the cell must follow it.
