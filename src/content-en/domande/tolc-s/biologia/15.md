All individuals of one frog species present in a pond during the same period are counted. Which ecological level is surveyed?

- The entire community
- The entire ecosystem
- A single organism
- All abiotic factors
- A population

## Explanation

A population comprises individuals of the same species living in a defined area. The survey meets the species, spatial and temporal criteria. The community would also include other populations, such as algae, insects and bacteria; the ecosystem would additionally include water, light and other physical components. Counting many frogs therefore does not survey the whole pond.
