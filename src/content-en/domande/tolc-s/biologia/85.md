A vessel carries oxygen-poor blood from the heart to the lungs. How is it classified by flow direction?

- Vein because the blood is oxygen-poor
- Capillary because it starts at the heart
- Vein because it reaches the lungs
- Lymphatic vessel by definition
- Artery

## Explanation

Arteries carry blood away from the heart toward other regions, regardless of oxygen content. The described vessel is therefore a pulmonary artery. Veins return blood to the heart; pulmonary veins normally carry oxygen-rich blood. Using oxygenation as the sole criterion would confuse pulmonary and systemic circuits.
