What is the strand complementary to 5′–AGTC–3′, also written in the 5′→3′ direction?

- 5′–TCAG–3′
- 5′–GACU–3′
- 5′–CTGA–3′
- 5′–GACT–3′
- 5′–AGTC–3′

## Explanation

Antiparallel pairing gives 3′–TCAG–5′ opposite 5′–AGTC–3′. To write the new strand in the requested direction, read it from the other end: 5′–GACT–3′. Writing TCAG while keeping the 5′ label at the start ignores antiparallelism. Uracil does not occur because both strands are DNA.
