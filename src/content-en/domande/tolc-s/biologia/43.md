In the standard genetic code, GAA and GAG both specify glutamate. A substitution changes GAA to GAG in a coding region, with no other changes. How is it classified with respect to the amino acid sequence?

- Nonsense
- Frameshifting insertion
- Synonymous
- Deletion of the entire gene
- Chromosome duplication

## Explanation

The final nucleotide changes, but the resulting codon specifies the same amino acid. The substitution is therefore synonymous with respect to protein sequence. This illustrates degeneracy of the genetic code. No stop codon is introduced and nucleotide number does not change, so it is neither a nonsense mutation nor a frameshifting insertion.
