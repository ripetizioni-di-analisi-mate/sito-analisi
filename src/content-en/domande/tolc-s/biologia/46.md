Two bird species eat the same seeds, which are available in limited amounts. The presence of one reduces food available to the other. Which interaction is supported?

- Interspecific competition
- Only intraspecific competition
- Obligate mutualism
- Predation by one species on the other
- Commensalism without an effect on the second species

## Explanation

The species share a limited resource, and each can reduce its availability to the other. This is interspecific competition because different species are involved. Using the same resource would not necessarily imply competition if it were unlimited; the statement supplies the required limitation. No killing or consumption of individuals of the other species is described.
