The initial rate of the same enzyme is compared at two pH values, keeping temperature, enzyme amount and substrate concentration equal. Rates differ. Which conclusion is supported?

- Temperature must have changed
- Enzyme amount must differ
- pH can influence enzyme activity under the tested conditions
- pH transforms the substrate’s chemical elements
- Every enzyme has the same optimal pH

## Explanation

The comparison controls the other stated variables and changes pH. A rate difference is therefore consistent with pH affecting charges and conformations involved in catalysis. Two measurements do not necessarily identify the full optimum or permit generalization to every enzyme. There is no need to invent changes in quantities stated to be equal.
