In an isolated pathway $A\to B$, an enzyme is completely inhibited. Uncatalyzed conversion is negligible in the model. $A$ continues to arrive and has no other consumption pathway. What happens initially?

- A accumulates and new B production stops
- A decreases because it is converted faster
- B is produced without using A
- A automatically becomes DNA
- Amounts necessarily remain identical

## Explanation

The block prevents the catalyzed conversion from A to B. Since A continues entering but is not consumed elsewhere, its balance is positive and it accumulates. Neglecting uncatalyzed conversion as the model requires, new B production through this pathway stops; this does not require B already present to become zero immediately. The outcome depends on the explicit conditions concerning the pathway and other possible uses.
