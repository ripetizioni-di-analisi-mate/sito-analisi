Near a root tip, a zone of small cells with numerous mitotic figures is observed. Which process directly produces new cells in this zone?

- Only water uptake without divisions
- Photosynthesis in cells lacking chloroplasts
- Gamete formation through meiosis
- Conversion of xylem into nervous tissue
- Proliferation in the apical meristem

## Explanation

Mitotic figures indicate ongoing cell divisions. In apical meristems, mitosis increases cell number, contributing to primary root growth. Subsequent cell elongation increases cell size but does not replace production of new cells. The vegetative context does not describe gamete formation or photosynthesis.
