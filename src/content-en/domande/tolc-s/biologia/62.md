In a community model wolves prey on deer and deer consume young plants. If wolves decline and no other changes occur, which indirect effect is plausible?

- Fewer deer because of increased predation
- More deer and greater pressure on young plants
- Immediate disappearance of every producer by definition
- Conversion of deer into producers
- Certain plant increase because wolves do not eat plants

## Explanation

Reducing the predator can reduce deer mortality and increase their abundance in the model. More herbivores can consume more young plants: the vegetation effect is indirect. The prediction depends on the absence of other changes, such as food shortage or other predators. Wolves not eating plants does not remove the connection through herbivores.
