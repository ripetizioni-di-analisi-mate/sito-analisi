A linear polypeptide chain contains $51$ amino acids and no bond between its ends. How many peptide bonds must be hydrolyzed to separate all the amino acids?

- $51$
- $52$
- $50$
- $25$
- $102$

## Explanation

In a linear chain the first amino acid connects to the second, the second to the third, and so on. Between $n$ units there are $n-1$ connections: here $51-1=50$. Each hydrolysis breaks one of these bonds. The statement excludes a cyclic structure, which would also have a bond between the ends. The result counts bonds, not amino acids.
