An $AaBb$ individual produces gametes; the two loci assort independently. What is the probability of an $Ab$ gamete?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac1{16}$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

The probability of receiving $A$ at the first locus is $\dfrac12$, and that of receiving $b$ at the second is $\dfrac12$. Independence permits multiplication: $P(Ab)=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac14$. This describes a gamete with one allele per locus, not a diploid offspring genotype. Without independent assortment the product would not automatically be valid.
