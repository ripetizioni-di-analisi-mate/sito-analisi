A plant cell absorbs water in a hypotonic environment and becomes turgid without bursting. Which structure mainly provides mechanical resistance to expansion?

- Ribosome
- Nucleolus
- Cell wall
- Golgi apparatus
- Chromatin

## Explanation

Water initially enters by osmosis and cell contents exert outward pressure. The cell wall resists expansion and contributes to turgor. The membrane maintains selective exchange but alone does not provide the same rigid support. The nucleus, ribosomes and Golgi are not the external mechanical covering described.
