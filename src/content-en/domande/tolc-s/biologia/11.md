A sample contains elongated cells with organized contractile proteins that develop force and shorten when activated. Which tissue is mainly described?

- Muscle
- Lining epithelium
- Cartilaginous connective tissue
- Nervous
- Bone connective tissue

## Explanation

Developing force through interactions of contractile proteins is the specialization of muscle tissue. Muscle cells can shorten and transmit tension. Nervous tissue often controls activation, but signal transmission is not the production of the described force. Epithelia and connective tissues mainly provide lining, support and connection.
