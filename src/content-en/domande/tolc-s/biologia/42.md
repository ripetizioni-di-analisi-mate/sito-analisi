From the cross $Aa\times Aa$, under complete dominance, a dominant-phenotype offspring is selected at random. What is the probability that it is $AA$?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

The unconditional genotype probabilities are $P(AA)=\dfrac14$, $P(Aa)=\dfrac12$, $P(aa)=\dfrac14$. The dominant phenotype excludes $aa$ and has probability $\dfrac34$. We must divide by this restricted set: $P(AA\mid\text{dominant})=\dfrac14\div\dfrac34=\dfrac13$. Answering one quarter would ignore the known phenotype information.
