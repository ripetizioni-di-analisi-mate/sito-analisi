A double helix contains two labeled strands. It replicates twice in unlabeled medium by semiconservative replication. What fraction of the four final double helices still contains an original labeled strand?

- $\dfrac14$
- $1$
- $0$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

After the first replication there are two duplexes, each with one original labeled strand and one new strand. In the second replication every strand acts as a template: four duplexes form, but there are still only two original strands. They occupy two separate duplexes, so the requested fraction is $\dfrac24=\dfrac12$. Labeling is not copied into new strands.
