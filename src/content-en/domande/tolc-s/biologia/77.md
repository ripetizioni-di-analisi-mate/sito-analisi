A neuron and a muscle cell from the same individual normally have the same nuclear genetic information but produce different amounts of many proteins. Which explanation is correct?

- Every muscle cell must have lost all neural genes
- The two cell types express genes differently
- The neuron contains no DNA
- Proteins are produced independently of genetic information
- Muscle DNA uses uracil instead of thymine

## Explanation

Cell specialization largely depends on which genes are expressed and how their activity is regulated. Cells with the same genome can therefore have different proteins and functions. There is no need to assume generalized loss of unused genes. Differential expression concerns use of information, not replacement of DNA bases by RNA bases.
