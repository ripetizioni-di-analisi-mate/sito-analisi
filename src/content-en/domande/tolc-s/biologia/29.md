Which description of cholesterol is correct?

- It is a polysaccharide in plant cell walls
- It is the monomer of all proteins
- It is a nitrogenous base exclusive to RNA
- It is a ring-shaped lipid found in animal membranes and a precursor of steroid hormones
- It consists of glycerol and three fatty acids

## Explanation

Cholesterol is a sterol, a lipid with a characteristic ring structure. It contributes to the properties of animal membranes and is a precursor of molecules such as steroid hormones. It must not be identified as a triglyceride, whose structure is glycerol linked to three fatty acids. The term lipid therefore includes structurally different molecules.
