In cultured cells, labeled amino acids are tracked as they are incorporated into new polypeptide chains. Which structure directly catalyzes this assembly?

- Ribosome
- Lysosome
- Cell wall
- Central vacuole
- Centriole

## Explanation

Amino acids are joined by peptide bonds during translation. The ribosome reads messenger RNA and catalyzes chain elongation with the help of tRNAs. Rough endoplasmic reticulum can host ribosomes, but the structure that directly performs assembly remains the ribosome. Lysosomes and vacuoles mainly have other functions; walls and centrioles do not translate mRNA.
