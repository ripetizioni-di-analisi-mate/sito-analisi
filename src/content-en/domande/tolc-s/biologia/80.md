During a dry day a plant closes its stomata. Which pair of direct consequences is plausible?

- Greater CO₂ entry and always zero water loss
- Elimination of mitochondria and increased chromosomes
- Obligatory arrest of every cellular reaction
- Conversion of xylem into phloem
- Less water vapor loss and less CO₂ entry

## Explanation

Stomata are openings for gas exchange. Closing them limits water vapor loss but also reduces a route for carbon dioxide entry. Controlling water loss can therefore limit photosynthesis. It does not imply that every water exchange becomes zero or that all metabolic processes stop immediately.
