An enzyme uses one DNA strand as a template and produces a chain containing uracil. Which process is taking place?

- Translation
- DNA replication
- Protein hydrolysis
- Centrosome duplication
- Transcription

## Explanation

The uracil-containing product is RNA, and its synthesis on a DNA template is called transcription. In replication the product is DNA; in translation the ribosome reads RNA to produce a polypeptide. Identifying both template and product therefore distinguishes the process, rather than considering only the initial presence of DNA.
