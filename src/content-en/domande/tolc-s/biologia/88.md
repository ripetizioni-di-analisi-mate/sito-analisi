Over an interval producers fix $1200$ energy units as gross primary production and use $450$ in respiration. What is net primary production?

- $1650$
- $1200$
- $750$
- $450$
- $270$

## Explanation

Net primary production is gross production minus the portion used by producers in respiration: $1200-450=750$. It represents energy retained in new biomass and potentially available for growth and other trophic levels. Adding respiration would count it as new production, while using all gross production would ignore producers’ metabolic consumption.
