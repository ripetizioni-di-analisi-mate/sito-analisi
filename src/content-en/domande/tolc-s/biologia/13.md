A root cell has a cellulose wall, a nucleus and mitochondria, but no chloroplasts. Which interpretation is correct?

- It must be prokaryotic
- It cannot produce ATP
- It can be a normal nonphotosynthetic plant cell
- It must be an animal cell
- The wall replaces the nucleus

## Explanation

Not every plant cell performs photosynthesis. Cells in many root tissues receive organic compounds produced elsewhere and use them in cellular respiration. The nucleus indicates eukaryotic organization; a cellulose wall and root context are consistent with a plant cell. Absence of chloroplasts does not imply absence of mitochondria or inability to obtain ATP.
