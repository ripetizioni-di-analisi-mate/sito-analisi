An insect obtains nectar from a flower and, during visits, increases the plant’s pollination success. If both effects are beneficial, which interaction is described?

- Parasitism
- Mutualism
- Competition
- Predation
- Commensalism with no effect on the plant

## Explanation

Nectar is a resource for the insect, and pollen transfer promotes plant reproduction. Since the statement specifies a benefit to both, the classification is mutualism. Commensalism would involve a benefit to one partner without an appreciable effect on the other; parasitism would harm one. Mere proximity would not be sufficient without these effects.
