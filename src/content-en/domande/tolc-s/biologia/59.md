Which sequence does blood normally follow from the right ventricle before entering systemic circulation?

- Liver, right ventricle, lungs
- Right atrium, aorta, lungs
- Left ventricle, intestine, right atrium
- Lungs, left atrium, left ventricle
- Kidneys, right atrium, aorta

## Explanation

The right ventricle sends blood to the lungs through pulmonary arteries. After exchange, blood returns through pulmonary veins to the left atrium and passes into the left ventricle, which sends it into the aorta. This links pulmonary exchange to tissue distribution. The order of chambers and regions is essential for following the route.
