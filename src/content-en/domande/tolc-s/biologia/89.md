Community X has three species with 10 individuals each; Y has the same three species with 28, 1 and 1 individuals. Which comparison is correct?

- X has more species because it is more even
- Y has only one species because one dominates
- X has more individuals in total
- Equal species richness and different abundance distributions
- Richness counts only the most abundant species

## Explanation

Both communities contain three species and thirty individuals. Richness, defined as the number of species, is therefore equal. Distribution differs: X is even, while Y is numerically dominated by one species. Richness, total individual count and evenness must not be confused, even though all contribute to describing biodiversity.
