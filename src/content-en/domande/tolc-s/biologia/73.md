A plant receives strong light but very little carbon dioxide. Increasing light further does not increase sugar production. Which explanation is most consistent?

- Light has become a mineral nutrient
- Photosynthesis never uses carbon dioxide
- Carbon availability may be the limiting factor
- DNA has disappeared from all cells
- The plant can no longer respire by definition

## Explanation

Synthesizing organic substances requires carbon to fix. If carbon dioxide is very scarce, more light does not solve that shortage, and the rate can remain limited by CO₂ availability. The result illustrates interaction among required resources. It does not establish that light is always irrelevant or that respiration, a distinct process, has stopped.
