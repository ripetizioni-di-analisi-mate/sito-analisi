With other conditions unchanged, a large decrease in hemoglobin mainly reduces which blood function?

- Ability to replicate all plasma chromosomes
- Bile production
- Starch synthesis
- Chloroplast formation
- Oxygen-carrying capacity

## Explanation

Most oxygen carried by human blood is bound to hemoglobin in red blood cells; a small fraction is dissolved in plasma. Decreasing hemoglobin therefore reduces total carrying capacity even without an initial ventilation change. Platelets and other proteins have different roles: blood does not perform photosynthesis or produce bile.
