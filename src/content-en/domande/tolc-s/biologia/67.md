In a species, $RR$ produces red flowers, $BB$ white flowers and $RB$ uniformly pink flowers. Which allelic relationship is described by the heterozygote’s intermediate phenotype?

- Complete dominance of R
- Incomplete dominance
- Complete dominance of B
- Absence of allele segregation
- Exclusively environmental inheritance

## Explanation

The heterozygote has an intermediate phenotype relative to both homozygotes: this is incomplete dominance. Under complete dominance it would have one homozygote’s phenotype. Alleles can still segregate regularly into gametes; how genotype is expressed does not abolish transmission rules. The statement directly links genotype and color, so it does not support a purely environmental cause.
