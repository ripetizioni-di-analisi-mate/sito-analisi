A cell completes mitosis normally, but cytokinesis is blocked. Which immediate result is compatible with the experiment?

- Four haploid cells
- One cell with two daughter nuclei
- One cell without DNA
- Two cells without cytoplasm
- One nucleus that has not separated chromosomes

## Explanation

Mitosis distributes chromosomes into daughter nuclei; cytokinesis physically separates the cytoplasm. If the first process finishes and the second fails, both nuclei can remain in the same cell volume. Haploid cells do not automatically result because regular mitosis preserves nuclear ploidy. Distinguishing nuclear from cell division predicts the outcome.
