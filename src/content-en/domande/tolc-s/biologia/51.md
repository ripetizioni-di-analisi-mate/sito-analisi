A tissue forms a continuous layer of closely joined cells lining the intestinal lumen. Which category describes it?

- Epithelial
- Bone
- Cartilage
- Skeletal muscle
- Nervous

## Explanation

Epithelia line surfaces and cavities and can regulate exchange across the cellular barrier. A continuous layer separating the intestinal lumen from underlying tissues belongs to this category. The intestine also contains muscle, connective tissue and nerves, but the question identifies its specific lining rather than treating the whole organ as one tissue.
