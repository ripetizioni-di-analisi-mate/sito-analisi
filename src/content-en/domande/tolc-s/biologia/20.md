In a phospholipid bilayer with water on both sides, which arrangement is energetically favorable?

- Nonpolar tails are fully exposed to water on both sides
- All heads are only on the outer side
- All tails are only on the cytoplasmic side
- Phospholipids are converted into nucleotides
- Polar heads face water and nonpolar tails face inward

## Explanation

Polar heads interact with water and occupy both surfaces. Nonpolar tails associate in the internal region, reducing contact with water. This organization follows from the amphipathic character of phospholipids and creates a selective barrier. It does not require all heads to be on one side or a chemical conversion into other biomolecules.
