An animal muscle cell mobilizes a branched reserve of glucose units. Which molecule is described?

- Cellulose
- Glycogen
- Cholesterol
- DNA
- Keratin

## Explanation

Glycogen is a branched polysaccharide storing glucose units and is present in muscle. Its breakdown supplies substrates for energy metabolism. Cellulose instead has a structural role in plant walls; cholesterol, DNA and keratin belong to lipids, nucleic acids and proteins respectively. Branching alone is insufficient: composition and location in an animal also matter.
