Starch and cellulose both contain glucose, but human digestive enzymes break down starch and not cellulose. Which explanation connects structure and function?

- Cellulose contains only amino acids
- Bonds between units have different configurations and enzymes are specific
- The glucose in starch is a nucleotide
- Enzymes recognize only the substance’s color
- Cellulose contains no chemical bonds

## Explanation

A polymer’s function also depends on how its monomers are connected. Starch and cellulose have glycosidic bonds with different configurations; human digestive enzymes do not have the same ability to hydrolyze them. Knowing that both contain glucose is therefore insufficient to predict equal digestibility. Enzyme specificity concerns substrate structure.
