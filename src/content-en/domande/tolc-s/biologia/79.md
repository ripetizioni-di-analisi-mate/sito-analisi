A species disappears from one lake but continues living and reproducing in many other lakes. Which conclusion is correct?

- The species became globally extinct
- All its individuals became another species
- No community can have changed
- A local population extinction occurred
- Local disappearance proves that variation was absent

## Explanation

Disappearance concerns the lake considered, while the species persists elsewhere. This is local extinction, distinct from loss of every individual of the species. The lake community can change even though the event is not global. Without cause data, disappearance cannot necessarily be attributed to absence of variation or transformation into another species.
