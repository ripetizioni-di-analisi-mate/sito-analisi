A population has $240$ individuals. In one year there are $40$ births, $25$ deaths, $10$ immigrants and $5$ emigrants. What is the final population size?

- $250$
- $280$
- $270$
- $255$
- $260$

## Explanation

Births and immigration add individuals; deaths and emigration remove them. The balance is $N_f=240+40-25+10-5=260$. Natural change is $40-25=15$ and migration contributes $10-5=5$: total growth is therefore $20$. Counting only births and deaths would omit some data because the population is not closed.
