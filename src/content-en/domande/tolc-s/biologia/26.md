A DNA nucleotide is separated into its three component types. Which group is correct?

- Deoxyribose, phosphate and a nitrogenous base
- Ribose, glycerol and an amino acid
- Glucose, phosphate and a fatty acid
- Glycerol and three fatty acids
- Deoxyribose and two amino acids

## Explanation

A nucleotide contains a pentose sugar, a phosphate group and a nitrogenous base. In DNA the pentose is deoxyribose; in RNA it is ribose. Glycerol plus three fatty acids instead identifies a triglyceride. A base alone is not a complete nucleotide: the sugar and phosphate are also required.
