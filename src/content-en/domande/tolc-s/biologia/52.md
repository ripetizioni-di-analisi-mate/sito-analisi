Cardiac cells contract continuously and contain many mitochondria. Which functional connection explains this abundance?

- Replacement of contractile filaments with mitochondrial DNA
- High ATP demand supported by aerobic respiration
- Photosynthetic glucose production in the heart
- Absence of energy consumption
- Elimination of the need for oxygen

## Explanation

Contraction and restoration of ion gradients require ATP. Many mitochondria support a high aerobic metabolic capacity, consistent with continuous cardiac work. The organelle does not replace contractile proteins and does not perform photosynthesis. Mitochondrial abundance is linked to using oxygen in aerobic respiration, rather than eliminating its need.
