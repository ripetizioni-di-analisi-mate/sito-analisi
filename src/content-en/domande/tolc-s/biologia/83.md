Photosynthesis in aquatic plants is compared at two light intensities. To isolate the effect of light, which variable should be kept equal in both trials?

- The light intensity constituting the treatment
- Oxygen produced, imposed before measurement
- Water temperature
- The final result, made identical by definition
- Bubble count, experimentally fixed at the same value

## Explanation

Light is the variable intended to change, whereas temperature can influence enzymatic processes and should be controlled. Oxygen produced or bubbles can measure the response, rather than being values imposed equally before comparison. Species, biomass and CO₂ availability should also be comparable to attribute a difference to the light treatment.
