Two genetically identical plants grown under different light conditions develop differently shaped leaves. Which conclusion is justified without further data?

- The environment can contribute to the phenotype difference
- The plants must belong to different species
- The difference proves a new inherited mutation
- Genes have no role in development
- Leaves must be gametes

## Explanation

The same genotype can produce different phenotypes under different environmental conditions. The comparison therefore supports an environmental contribution, often called phenotypic plasticity. It does not establish that the DNA sequence changed or that the difference is transmitted independently of environment. Recognizing an environmental component does not eliminate genes’ role in development.
