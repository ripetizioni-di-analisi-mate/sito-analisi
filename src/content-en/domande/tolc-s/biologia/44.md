A bat’s forelimb and a whale’s front flipper share a bone pattern inherited from a common ancestor despite different functions. Which concept is illustrated?

- Commensalism
- Identity of species
- Absence of evolution
- Homology
- Independent origin without a common ancestor

## Explanation

Homology concerns a structure inherited from a common origin. The same basic pattern can be modified for flight, swimming or other functions. Different functions therefore do not remove homology. A functional similarity arising independently would be a different situation; here the statement explicitly specifies historical continuity of the bone pattern.
