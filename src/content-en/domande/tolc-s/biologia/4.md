A nucleic acid contains ribose and the base uracil. Which identification is consistent with these observations?

- Ordinary nuclear DNA
- A triglyceride
- Cellulose
- RNA
- A polypeptide chain

## Explanation

Ribose and uracil are characteristic components of RNA. In ordinary DNA the sugar is deoxyribose and thymine is used instead of uracil. Phosphate alone would not distinguish the two nucleic acids, whereas the pair of features given does. Lipids, polysaccharides and proteins do not have a chain built from these nucleotides.
