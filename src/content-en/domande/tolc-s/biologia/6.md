Two parents have genotype $Aa$ at an autosomal locus. Under Mendelian segregation and random fertilization, what is the probability that one offspring is $aa$?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac34$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

Each parent produces $a$ gametes with probability $\dfrac12$. Producing $aa$ requires receiving $a$ from both; the events are independent in the model: $P(aa)=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac14$. Dominance is not involved in calculating genotype. A probability of one quarter does not require every group of four offspring to contain exactly one $aa$ individual.
