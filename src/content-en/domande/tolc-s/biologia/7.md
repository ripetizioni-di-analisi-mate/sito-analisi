During interphase, the amount of DNA in one nucleus gradually doubles while the cell does not divide. Which phase describes the observation?

- Mitotic anaphase
- S phase
- Cytokinesis
- Telophase
- Meiosis II

## Explanation

S phase is the DNA synthesis phase: chromosomes are replicated and each acquires two sister chromatids. The DNA increase precedes mitosis and does not yet separate daughter cells. In anaphase, copies already produced separate; during cytokinesis, the cytoplasm divides. Replication must therefore not be placed within the division itself.
