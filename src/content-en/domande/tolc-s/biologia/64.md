An animal cell is placed in a hypertonic solution containing a solute that cannot cross the membrane. At equal initial pressure, which initial change is expected?

- Water entry and increased volume
- Water loss and increased volume
- Obligatory solute entry in an equal amount
- Water loss and reduced volume
- Immediate doubling of chromosome number

## Explanation

The external solution has a higher effective concentration of impermeant solute. Water crosses the membrane with net outward flow, reducing cell volume. Specifying that the solute cannot cross makes the tonicity prediction applicable. The process concerns water and volume; it does not immediately change chromosome number or identity.
