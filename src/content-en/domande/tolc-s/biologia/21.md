Glucose is present in the glomerular filtrate of a healthy person but is normally almost absent from final urine. Which step explains the difference?

- Glucose reabsorption from tubules into blood
- Glucose production in the bladder
- Destruction of all filtrate in the glomerulus
- Glucose passage from blood to urine in the ureter
- Conversion of glucose into red blood cells

## Explanation

The initial filtrate contains small molecules from plasma, including glucose. During passage through the tubules, glucose is normally almost completely recovered into blood. The initial filtrate therefore differs in composition from final urine. The bladder stores urine that has already formed; it is not the location where filtered nutrients are selectively recovered.
