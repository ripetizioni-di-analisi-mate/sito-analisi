A particle contains genetic material and a capsid but has no ribosomes and multiplies only using a host cell. Which interpretation is consistent?

- It is compatible with a virus
- It must be an autonomous bacterial cell
- It is a fourth cellular domain
- It is a free mitochondrion with complete autonomy
- It is a unicellular animal with every organelle

## Explanation

Genetic material, a capsid and host-cell dependence are compatible with a virus. Lack of its own ribosomes explains the need to use protein synthesis in the infected cell. Having nucleic acids is not sufficient to be a cell. Viruses do not fit alongside the three cellular domains as a fourth equivalent domain.
