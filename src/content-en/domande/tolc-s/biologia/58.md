Oxygen, more concentrated outside a cell, passes directly through membrane lipids inward. No transporters are involved. Which process is described?

- Channel-mediated facilitated diffusion
- Primary active transport
- Simple diffusion
- Osmosis
- Endocytosis

## Explanation

Movement follows the concentration gradient and occurs directly through the bilayer: this is simple diffusion. Facilitated diffusion would require a transport protein; active transport would require energy coupling to move against a gradient. Osmosis concerns water, and endocytosis involves vesicles, neither of which is described.
