A membrane vesicle contains hydrolytic enzymes and maintains an acidic interior to break down cellular material. Which animal organelle is described?

- Lysosome
- Ribosome
- Centriole
- Nucleolus
- Chloroplast

## Explanation

Lysosomes are intracellular digestion compartments and maintain conditions suitable for acid hydrolases. Their membrane separates these processes from the cytosol. A ribosome assembles proteins and is not a membrane-bounded vesicle; centrioles and nucleoli have other functions. Chloroplasts are not organelles of animal cells.
