Two plant groups occupy the same area but flower in different weeks, greatly reducing crosses between groups. Which barrier is described?

- Geographic isolation on different continents
- Sterility of hybrids already born
- Zygote death after fertilization
- Temporal reproductive isolation
- Absence of any barrier to crossing

## Explanation

Failure of reproductive encounters depends on flowering time: this is temporal isolation, a barrier acting before zygote formation. Occupying the same area rules out attributing this case to geographic separation. No hybrid viability or fertility data are supplied, so a postfertilization barrier is not established. Reduced gene flow can promote divergence.
