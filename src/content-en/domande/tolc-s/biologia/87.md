In typical reproductive development of an angiosperm after fertilization, which association between initial structure and product is correct?

- Ovule → adult root; ovary → pollen
- Ovule → seed; ovary → fruit
- Ovule → stoma; ovary → mitochondrion
- Petal → chromosome; root → seed
- Xylem → embryo; phloem → pollen

## Explanation

The fertilized ovule develops into the seed, containing the embryo and associated structures. The ovary contributes to fruit formation; some fruits also involve other floral tissues. The association thus connects two structural levels of the flower to two distinct products. Pollen, stomata and conducting tissues do not replace this relationship.
