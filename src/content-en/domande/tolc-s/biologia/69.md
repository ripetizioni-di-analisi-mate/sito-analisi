Fungi and bacteria break down dead leaves and release mineral nutrients into soil. Which ecological role do they play here?

- Producers creating energy from nothing
- Consumers preventing all recycling
- Organisms turning every atom into light
- Decomposers contributing to matter recycling
- Predators of minerals

## Explanation

Breaking down organic residues makes some elements available again in forms organisms can use. This is the decomposer role in matter cycles. They obtain energy from organic substances and dissipate some as heat; energy is not completely recycled like elements in their cycles. Matter and energy therefore require different descriptions.
