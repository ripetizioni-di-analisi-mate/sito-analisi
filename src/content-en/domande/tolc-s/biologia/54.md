A healthy plant with available organic reserves is placed in darkness briefly. Which exchange can continue through aerobic respiration?

- Oxygen production powered by absent light
- Photon absorption in complete darkness
- Glucose production without any energy source
- Oxygen consumption and carbon dioxide release
- Conversion of mitochondria into chloroplasts

## Explanation

In darkness there is no light energy to support photosynthetic reactions, but the plant can break down organic substances already available. Aerobic respiration uses oxygen and produces carbon dioxide. The statement limits observation to a short interval and specifies reserves, so it does not require predicting prolonged energy shortage. Plants perform both processes.
