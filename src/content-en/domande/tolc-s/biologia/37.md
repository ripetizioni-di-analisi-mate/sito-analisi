A tick feeds on a mammal’s blood, obtaining resources and imposing a cost on the host without killing it during the meal. Which relationship is described?

- Mutualism
- Parasitism
- Commensalism
- Intraspecific competition
- Primary production

## Explanation

The tick benefits from host resources and the mammal is harmed: this is parasitism. The host remaining alive during feeding is consistent with the parasite retaining its resource. In mutualism both would benefit; in commensalism there would be no appreciable cost to the host.
