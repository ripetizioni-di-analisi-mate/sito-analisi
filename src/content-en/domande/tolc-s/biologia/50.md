Some individuals immigrate into a population, reproduce with residents and introduce an allele previously absent there. Which process is directly described?

- Drift due exclusively to random mortality
- Formation of a new allele without migration
- Global extinction
- DNA replication as the only cause
- Gene flow

## Explanation

The allele moves between populations through individuals that contribute to reproduction: this is gene flow. Immigration alone would not guarantee this effect if immigrants left no descendants. The allele need not have just arisen by mutation: it was absent from the receiving population, not necessarily from the whole species.
