After a meal, blood glucose rises; a hormonal response promotes glucose uptake and storage in some tissues, reducing the initial rise. Which regulatory principle is illustrated?

- Obligatory amplification of the disturbance
- Absence of homeostatic control
- Production of oxygen from glucose
- Negative feedback
- Transmission of alleles to gametes

## Explanation

The response opposes the initial deviation: increased blood glucose activates processes tending to lower it. This relationship identifies negative feedback. Negative describes the direction of control, not a harmful effect. The case does not require perfectly constant blood glucose or uptake in every cell type to depend on the same hormone.
