A disease increases the thickness of the barrier between alveolar air and blood, initially leaving other conditions unchanged. Which process is directly hindered?

- Starch digestion in the stomach
- Urine formation in the bladder
- Gamete production in alveoli
- DNA transcription in mature red blood cells
- Gas diffusion between alveoli and capillaries

## Explanation

Pulmonary exchange requires oxygen and carbon dioxide to cross the alveolar-capillary barrier. At equal surface area and gradient, greater thickness increases the diffusion path and reduces exchange efficiency. Ventilation brings air to the alveoli but does not replace passage across the barrier. The other listed functions do not explain the direct effect of the described change.
