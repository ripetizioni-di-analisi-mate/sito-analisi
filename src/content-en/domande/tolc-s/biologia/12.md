A water-soluble dye is absorbed by roots with water and rapidly reaches the stem and leaf veins. In which conducting tissue should the dye mainly be sought?

- Phloem
- Xylem
- Leaf epidermis
- Apical meristem
- Nervous tissue

## Explanation

Xylem is the main pathway carrying water and minerals from roots to aerial parts. A tracer following that water is therefore expected in its conducting elements. Phloem mainly distributes organic solutes from sources to sinks; the epidermis covers organs and the meristem produces new cells. Plants do not have nervous tissue.
