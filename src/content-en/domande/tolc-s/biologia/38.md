A mitotic cell shows duplicated chromosomes aligned at the equatorial plane; sister chromatids have not separated. Which phase is observed?

- Anaphase
- Telophase
- Metaphase
- G1 phase
- Completed cytokinesis

## Explanation

Alignment of chromosomes at the equatorial plane characterizes metaphase. Sister chromatids remaining joined excludes anaphase, when copies separate and move to the poles. Daughter nuclei form in telophase. G1 precedes replication and does not show the duplicated chromosomes arranged on the spindle described here.
