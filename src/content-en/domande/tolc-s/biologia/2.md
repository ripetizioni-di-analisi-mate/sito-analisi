A micrograph shows DNA enclosed by a double membrane with pores. Which conclusion is justified?

- The cell must be animal
- The cell has eukaryotic organization
- The cell must be photosynthetic
- The cell lacks ribosomes
- The cell is a typical bacterium

## Explanation

A nucleus bounded by a membrane envelope is characteristic of eukaryotic organization. This observation does not distinguish an animal cell from a plant, fungal or other eukaryotic cell. It does not establish the presence of chloroplasts. Eukaryotes also contain ribosomes: the nucleus separates part of the genetic material from the cytoplasm without eliminating protein synthesis.
