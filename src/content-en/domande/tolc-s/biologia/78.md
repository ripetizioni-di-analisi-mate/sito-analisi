A heated protein loses activity through a conformational change, without alterations to covalent bonds. Which structural level is certainly preserved?

- The same tertiary structure
- Every interaction between chains
- The primary amino acid sequence
- The original function
- The complete three-dimensional shape

## Explanation

Primary structure is the order of amino acids linked by peptide bonds. If these remain intact and no other sequence changes occur, primary structure is preserved. Heating can instead alter interactions responsible for folding and cause activity loss. Denaturation does not necessarily mean complete hydrolysis into free amino acids.
