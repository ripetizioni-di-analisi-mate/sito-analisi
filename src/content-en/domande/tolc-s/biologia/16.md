An organism builds its organic compounds using carbon dioxide as its carbon source and light as its energy source. Which role does it normally have in a food web?

- Autotrophic producer
- Obligate primary consumer
- Secondary predator
- Obligate animal parasite
- Consumer using organic matter as its only carbon source

## Explanation

Building organic matter from inorganic carbon defines autotrophy. Using light makes the organism photoautotrophic, and it makes new organic matter available to the food web. Absorbing water and minerals does not make it a consumer: the criterion here is the carbon source. A herbivore instead obtains organic carbon by eating producers.
