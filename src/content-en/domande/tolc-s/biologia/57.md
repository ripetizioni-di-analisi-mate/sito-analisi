Which function of a tRNA anticodon links genetic information to protein synthesis?

- Duplicating all DNA by itself
- Pairing with an mRNA codon during translation
- Forming a nuclear membrane
- Removing phosphates from chromosomes
- Converting every amino acid into glucose

## Explanation

The anticodon is a tRNA sequence that recognizes an mRNA codon through complementarity. The tRNA, charged with the appropriate amino acid, allows the ribosome to connect nucleotide sequence to protein sequence. The anticodon is not a genome replication site and is not converted into protein: it participates in recognition during translation.
