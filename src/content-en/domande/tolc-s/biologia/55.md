In a model producers make $10000$ energy units available; herbivores receive $20\%$, and carnivores receive $10\%$ of herbivore energy. How many units reach carnivores?

- $1000$
- $2000$
- $3000$
- $20$
- $200$

## Explanation

Efficiencies apply successively to different quantities. Herbivores receive $10000\cdot0.20=2000$ units; carnivores receive $2000\cdot0.10=200$. Overall efficiency is therefore $0.20\cdot0.10=0.02$, or $2\%$. Adding percentages would incorrectly treat successive transfers as shares of the same initial base.
