A drug prevents formation of spindle microtubules in a cell about to divide. Which process is directly impaired?

- Base pairing during protein digestion
- Glucose production by the cell wall
- Regular chromosome distribution to daughter nuclei
- Conversion of phospholipids into chromosomes
- Obligatory formation of new alleles in every gene

## Explanation

The mitotic spindle consists of microtubules and participates in chromosome organization and separation. Preventing its formation compromises regular distribution to the poles and therefore to daughter nuclei. Microtubules are cytoskeletal components, not nucleic acids. The block does not automatically produce the same mutation in every gene: it primarily affects the segregation system.
