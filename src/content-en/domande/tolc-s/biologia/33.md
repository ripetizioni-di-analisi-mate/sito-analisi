At a chemical synapse a drug blocks postsynaptic receptors, but neurotransmitter is still released. Which step is directly hindered?

- Replication of presynaptic chromosomes
- Entry of red blood cells into the synapse
- Recognition of neurotransmitter by the target cell
- Passage of a nucleus between cells
- Formation of a cellulose wall

## Explanation

The chemical signal requires released neurotransmitter to cross the cleft and interact with receptors in the postsynaptic membrane. If receptors are blocked, release can remain normal while the target response is reduced or prevented. The mechanism separates signal production from reception: observing release does not establish that communication is complete.
