A unicellular organism belongs to Archaea. In the three-domain model, which statement is consistent with this classification?

- It must be an animal
- It is a virus included in Eukarya
- It must have chloroplasts
- It has prokaryotic organization and belongs to a domain distinct from Bacteria
- Archaea and Bacteria are names for the same species

## Explanation

The model distinguishes Bacteria, Archaea and Eukarya. Archaea have prokaryotic organization, but this does not place them in Bacteria: the two groups differ in important molecular characteristics. Unicellular does not automatically mean bacterial and does not identify a virus. Chloroplasts and animal organization belong to eukaryotic lineages.
