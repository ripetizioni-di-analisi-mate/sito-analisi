A storm leaves a few survivors chosen randomly with respect to genotype. An allele’s frequency changes greatly among survivors. Which mechanism directly explains the change?

- Certain selection in favor of that allele
- Translation of DNA
- An obligatory increase in variation
- The same mutation in every survivor
- Genetic drift through a bottleneck

## Explanation

The surviving sample is small and random with respect to genotype. Frequencies can therefore change through sampling without an allele-associated reproductive advantage: this is genetic drift, here linked to a bottleneck. The statement rules out directly attributing survival to genotype. Random reduction does not guarantee increased diversity and can eliminate variants.
