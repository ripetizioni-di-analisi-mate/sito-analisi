In meiosis one homologous pair fails to separate in anaphase I and moves entirely to one pole. Meiosis II is regular. For that pair, what outcome is expected among the four products?

- Two with one extra chromosome and two with one missing
- All with the normal number
- One normal and three with altered numbers
- Four identical diploid cells
- All without any chromosomes

## Explanation

The first division sends both homologs to one cell and neither to the other. The second regularly separates the chromatids present but cannot correct homolog distribution. The two products of the first cell have one extra copy, and the two of the other lack one. The defect concerns that pair; any other pairs segregate normally.
