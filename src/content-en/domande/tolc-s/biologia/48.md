A diploid cell has $2n=8$. Just after S phase, before mitosis, how many chromosomes and chromatids does it contain?

- $16$ chromosomes and $16$ chromatids
- $4$ chromosomes and $8$ chromatids
- $8$ chromosomes and $16$ chromatids
- $8$ chromosomes and $8$ chromatids
- $16$ chromosomes and $32$ chromatids

## Explanation

S phase replicates each of the eight chromosomes, forming two still-joined sister chromatids. Chromosome number remains $8$, while chromatids number $2\cdot8=16$. Copies have not yet separated. Doubling DNA does not make the cell tetraploid: it duplicates each chromosome rather than adding new homologous sets.
