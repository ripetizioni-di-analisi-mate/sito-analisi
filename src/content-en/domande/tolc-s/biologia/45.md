A herbivore incorporates carbon by eating grass. Which process directly returns some of that carbon to the environment as carbon dioxide?

- Water osmosis
- Diffusion of atmospheric nitrogen
- DNA replication without breakdown
- Intestinal mineral absorption
- Cellular respiration of organic substances

## Explanation

In cellular respiration, carbon in organic molecules can be oxidized to carbon dioxide. The herbivore then releases CO₂ through respiratory exchange. Osmosis concerns water, while absorption and replication do not directly explain this conversion to inorganic carbon. Carbon may also remain in biomass or residues: the question asks for the specific route to CO₂.
