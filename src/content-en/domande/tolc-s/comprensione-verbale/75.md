A reading room questionnaire asks, “Is the light sufficient?” One group says yes while looking at screens, another says no while reading printed sheets. The manager wants to compare the answers before changing the lamps.

Which specification would make a new question more informative?

- Ask everyone to rate the room as a whole without mentioning how they read
- Specify only how long each group spent in the room
- Combine ratings without distinguishing the medium in use
- Ask only whether lamps are installed in the room
- Specify the activity for which the lighting is judged sufficient

## Explanation

“Sufficient” requires a task as its reference. Screens and printed text give different reference points and may make both answers understandable.
