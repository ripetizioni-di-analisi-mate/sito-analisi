In an interview with a potter, the typesetter reserves quotation marks for recorded words. The paragraph reads: The drought slowed the work. “We had to wait for the water cart,” the potter recalled.

Which part may the reader attribute to the potter’s exact wording?

- The whole paragraph indiscriminately
- The word “recalled” that follows the quotation
- The full sentence about the effects of the drought
- The first-person account of waiting for the cart
- The words used to introduce the quotation and summarise the effects of drought

## Explanation

Only the words about the cart are enclosed in quotation marks. The opening sentence interprets the situation, even though it may fit the recollection.
