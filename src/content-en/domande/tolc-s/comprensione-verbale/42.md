In an exhibition a chart breaks library loans down by age group. The label says, “Each loan counts once, even when one reader takes several books.” The youngest group has the tallest bar.

To move from “most loans” to “most distinct borrowers”, what information is missing?

- How many different titles were borrowed by the youngest group
- How loans are distributed across individual people in each group
- The number of residents in each age group
- The number of days the library lent books to each age group
- Which categories of books produce the most loans

## Explanation

One person may contribute many loans. Counting borrowers requires linking loans to people and removing repeat appearances within each age group.
