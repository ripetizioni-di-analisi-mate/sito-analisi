“The cat is back,” Lina says as soon as she hears scratching at the door. The narration continues, “She opened it and found a branch dragged by the wind.”

At what level does the cat’s return belong in the passage?

- To what the narrator verifies when the door opens
- To a promise the narrator makes to the reader
- To a later event already narrated
- To what Lina believes at that moment
- To a fact confirmed by the branch

## Explanation

The return appears in Lina’s direct speech. The branch at the door gives another explanation for the noise and does not confirm the cat’s arrival.
