In the town’s weather key a “frost day” means “a day with at least one reading below zero”. On Tuesday the temperature dipped below zero for just one reading and was positive at the others. A chronicle says the town froze for twenty-four hours.

Which correction preserves the classification without lengthening the event?

- All of Tuesday’s readings must be corrected to negative values
- Tuesday must be excluded from frost days because its average may be positive
- Frost lasted at least until the final positive reading
- Tuesday qualifies as a frost day because one reading fell below zero
- The category applies only if frost recurs on Wednesday

## Explanation

The definition uses “at least one” and does not require twenty-four hours’ duration. The other positive readings rule out the chronicle’s account of continuous frost.
