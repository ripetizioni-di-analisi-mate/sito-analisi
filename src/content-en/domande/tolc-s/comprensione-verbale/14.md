A survey caption shows blue arrows “from springs towards the collection point”. A reader takes the longest arrow to indicate the greatest flow. The key assigns arrow length only to distance on the map.

Which feature of the figure would tell where a spring drains?

- The length of the spring’s route without regard to direction
- The side of the map from which the shortest arrows start
- The spring’s proximity to one collection point even if its arrow indicates another
- The location at which the tip of its arrow points
- The length of the arrow taken as a volume

## Explanation

“Towards the collection point” gives direction its meaning. Length represents distance, while flow is not encoded.
