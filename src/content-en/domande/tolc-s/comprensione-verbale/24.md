A subtitler consults both the script and a recording of a talk. The script says “Tuesday”, while the recording says “Thursday”. The project instructions ask for subtitles of what the audience actually heard.

Which action follows the project’s criterion?

- Drop both days as unrecordable words
- Merge the two into “midweek”
- Use “Tuesday” because the written version predates the talk
- Use “Thursday” in the subtitle at that point
- Write “Tuesday” in the first subtitle and “Thursday” in the next

## Explanation

The aim is to reproduce the words heard, and the recording documents “Thursday”. The script may explain the discrepancy but represents what was planned.
