An index labels a day “dry” if less than one millimetre of water reaches the collector. A diary for the same day records “drizzle in the morning”. The measurement was half a millimetre.

What allows the two descriptions to be reconciled?

- The diary entry necessarily describes precipitation after the measurement
- The diary uses “dry” in a technical sense
- Half a millimetre exceeds the chosen threshold
- The index always distinguishes rain and drizzle by name
- “Dry” in the index is a threshold category that includes small amounts of precipitation

## Explanation

Half a millimetre meets the “less than one millimetre” condition. The conventional category can therefore coexist with the observed drizzle.
