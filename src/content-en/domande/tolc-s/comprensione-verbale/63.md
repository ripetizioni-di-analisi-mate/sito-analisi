A shop offers free delivery for spending “over thirty euros, calculated after discounts”. Ada’s basket lists at thirty-two euros and totals exactly thirty after discount.

Which word decides the case once the discount has been applied?

- “Discounts”, taken to mean discount items are left out of the calculation
- “Thirty”, taken as a threshold that includes the equal value
- “Over”, which excludes equality with thirty
- “Calculated”, taken as an instruction to use the difference between the list and discounted prices
- “After”, taken as permission to choose between discounted total and list price

## Explanation

The correct base is the discounted total, namely thirty. “Over” requires a strictly larger value, so an exact total of thirty does not pass the threshold.
