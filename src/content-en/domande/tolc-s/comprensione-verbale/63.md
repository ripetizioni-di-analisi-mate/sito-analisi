A transport service offers two monthly fare options. Under Flex there is no fixed charge and each journey costs two euros. Under Plan there is a twenty-euro monthly charge plus one euro per journey. There are no other charges or discounts; every journey considered is eligible under both options.

| Fare | Monthly charge | Cost per journey |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Flex | 0 euros | 2 euros |
| Plan | 20 euros | 1 euro |

An advertising brochure calls Plan “the cheapest choice for everyone.” A consultant observes that value depends on the number of journeys made that month: the lower price per journey is insufficient to compare total spending. For someone whose travel varies, she also recommends comparing several plausible scenarios rather than treating one forecast as certain.

Which case is enough to refute “Plan is cheaper for everyone”?

- Someone making thirty journeys: Flex costs 60 euros and Plan 50
- Someone making forty journeys: Flex costs 80 euros and Plan 60
- Someone making five journeys: Flex costs 10 euros and Plan 25
- Someone making fifty journeys: Flex costs 100 euros and Plan 70
- Someone making one hundred journeys: Flex costs 200 euros and Plan 120

## Explanation

Refuting “for everyone” requires only one user for whom Plan is not cheaper. With five journeys, Plan costs twenty-five euros against ten. In the other proposed cases Plan is cheaper, so those cases alone do not refute the advertisement.
