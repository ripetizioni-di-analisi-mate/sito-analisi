A notebook documents tests of a thin layer of paint on glass. Under the laboratory lighting a colour shift appears after three weeks. The planned display will instead use wooden supports and layers of unknown thickness.

Which statement belongs on the sheet without extending the result beyond the test?

- A colour shift was observed after three weeks in the thin layer on glass under the test lighting
- A layer of any thickness shifts after exactly three weeks
- On wood the paint will necessarily shift sooner than on glass
- The laboratory light is the only possible cause of the shift
- Changing the support rules out a shift in the display

## Explanation

The notebook specifies support, thickness and lighting for the observation. At least two conditions change in the new display, and their effect is undocumented here.
