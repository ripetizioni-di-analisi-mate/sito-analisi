The teaching garden gate says, “Entry is free during visits. Fruit picking remains subject to the supervisor’s permission.” A child enters with the group and wants to pick a pear.

What does “remains” signal in the second clause?

- The supervisor has already authorised every child
- Free entry leaves the fruit-picking requirement in force
- Permission applies only when the gate is closed
- Permission to pick was already included in admission
- Picking becomes compulsory after the visit

## Explanation

The verb preserves the condition on picking even when entry is free. Entering and picking are actions governed separately.
