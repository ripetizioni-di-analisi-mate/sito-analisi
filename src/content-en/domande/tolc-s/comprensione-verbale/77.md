A catalogue preface says that entries give the maker’s name when documented. The first edition named a maker for a vase. The second says “proposed attribution” and cites the same letter from which the hypothesis arose.

Which aspect of the entry has explicitly changed?

- The identity of the letter cited
- The degree of certainty with which the name is presented
- The vase has been dropped from the catalogue
- The maker’s identity has been definitively disproved
- A signature has been discovered on the vase

## Explanation

“Proposed attribution” presents as a hypothesis what formerly appeared as an unqualified name. The letter remains the same and the passage announces no new finds.
