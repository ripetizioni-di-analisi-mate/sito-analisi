An observation group’s rule says, “Every report must give at least two distinguishing features. Even a fleeting sighting is accepted.” A participant saw an animal for a long time but remembers only its colour.

Which reading of the criterion explains why duration does not solve the problem?

- A long sighting always counts as two features
- The duration may be short, but two features are still required
- Colour suffices for long sightings
- Only fleeting sightings are accepted
- The two features may come from different animals as long as they are seen on the same outing

## Explanation

“Every report” includes long observations as well. Time elapsed does not add a second feature to the remembered colour.
