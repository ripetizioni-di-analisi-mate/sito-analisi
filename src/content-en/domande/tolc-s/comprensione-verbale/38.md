Scholarships are awarded in two stages. The office checks that the form is complete. A committee then ranks eligible applications on merit. One applicant receives the message “form complete”.

Which sentence correctly describes her status at that moment?

- The committee has already assessed her merit
- The office has awarded her the scholarship
- She has passed the formal check, with no rank communicated
- She has tied for first place
- She has been excluded from the ranking

## Explanation

The office assesses completeness in the first stage. Its message contains no judgement by the committee responsible for ranking.
