In a photographic index “panorama” describes the breadth of the view and “night shot” the time of exposure. The ridge entry bears both terms. A pupil thinks the second is a correction of the first.

How should the entry be interpreted?

- The shot can be a broad view taken at night
- “Panorama” here means the hour before sunset
- The view was initially mistaken for a daytime image
- The two words must refer to different photographs
- A night photograph must necessarily have a narrow field

## Explanation

Breadth and time are independent properties expressed by two index terms. Using both describes two aspects of the same photograph.
