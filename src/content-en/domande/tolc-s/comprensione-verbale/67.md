The microscope guide says, “First centre the specimen at low magnification. Only then switch to the next setting.” Luca increases magnification first and loses the specimen from view.

Which event does “then” refer back to?

- A first increase in magnification without centring
- Completion of centring at low magnification
- Disappearance of the specimen
- The end of the entire observation
- Preparation of a second specimen

## Explanation

“Then” refers to the action just prescribed and introduces what may follow it. Luca reverses exactly the order the text makes explicit.
