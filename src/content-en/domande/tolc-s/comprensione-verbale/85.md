A journal uses “never recorded on the island” for a phenomenon absent from its own archive, begun in 1998. A reader interprets “never” as “at no point in the history of the island”.

Which qualification makes explicit the documentable meaning of “never”?

- It was absent from every possible account before 1998
- It can never appear in the island archive in future
- It appears in no contemporary archive, on the island or elsewhere
- 1998 was when the phenomenon was first observed on the island
- It does not appear in that journal’s records from 1998 onwards

## Explanation

The start year limits the period and “own archive” limits the source. Absence from that material does not describe the island’s entire history.
