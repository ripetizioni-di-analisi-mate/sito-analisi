A leaflet promises a “step-free” route. A visitor confirms that she encountered no steps but reports that the narrow entrance passage kept her companion from walking alongside. The museum considers the report without deleting the original description.

Why does the report not contradict the leaflet’s description?

- It confirms that everyone can pass through the entrance side by side
- It turns “step-free” into an explicit promise of width
- It proves that the visitor followed a different route
- It concerns usable width, while the description covers steps alone
- It shows that the route changes only when companions are present

## Explanation

The visitor expressly confirms the absence of steps. Her difficulty concerns a different characteristic, whether two people can walk side by side.
