A group forecasts flowering “between weeks two and four if conditions remain similar to those used in the model”. In week one the site is moved to a room with different conditions.

What is the most precise consequence of the move for the original forecast?

- Flowering will certainly occur before week two
- The condition on which the forecast interval depended must be reconsidered
- Flowering will certainly occur after week four
- The outcome under the old conditions applies without review
- The interval automatically narrows

## Explanation

The period was stated under an explicit condition. The change requires checking its validity in the new room and does not by itself indicate the direction of any shift in flowering.
