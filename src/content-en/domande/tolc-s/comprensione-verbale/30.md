In a letter to his brother Marco calls the old cinema “our living room”. He then recalls the Sunday they could not enter because tickets were sold out.

How should “our living room” be understood?

- As proof that admission was reserved for them
- As the official name printed on the ticket
- As a claim to private ownership of the cinema
- As an indication that the brothers lived in the ticket office
- As an image of the brothers’ familiarity with the place

## Explanation

Sold-out tickets could keep them out: “our” does not guarantee ownership or exclusive access. “Living room” gives an affectionate description of a familiar place.
