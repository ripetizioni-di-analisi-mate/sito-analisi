An association prepares two versions of a storm recording. Its sound archive keeps the full duration and the noise made when the microphone moved. For a lesson on the distance of the storm it wants listeners to compare the flash, marked by a synchronised click, and the first thunder. The technician may shorten the recording provided the evidence needed for the comparison is preserved.

Which edit serves the purpose of the lesson?

- Cut noise after the thunder while retaining the interval from the click
- Remove the silence between click and thunder to shorten the sample
- Retain only the loudest thunder even if it is not the first
- Move the click onto the first thunder to highlight both
- Remove the click and give only the total recording duration

## Explanation

Comparing flash and first thunder requires both markers and the time between them. The later noise follows the end of that interval and can be cut without changing it.
