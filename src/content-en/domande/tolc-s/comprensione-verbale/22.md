In a cave, a sign reading “Water reached the red mark here” is placed by a notch. The log says the notch was cut after a single flood, the largest observed by the keepers during that decade.

Which addition to the caption would avoid suggesting an all-time record?

- “according to all traces present”
- “among floods observed by the keepers in that decade”
- “in every rainy season”
- “in every account of local floods, including earlier ones”
- “since the cave originated”

## Explanation

The log’s superlative is limited by the observers and the period. The notch records the level of one event within that series.
