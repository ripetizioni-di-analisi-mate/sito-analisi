A box of tree labels contains a March invoice and a drawing of the rows dated May. The gardener’s notebook says the labels were kept in storage pending delivery of posts. A historian wants the date when the labels were actually put on display for the first time.

Which further record bears most directly on the event sought?

- The work log dating the first installation of labels along the rows
- The first estimate for the design of the labels
- An inventory of posts still to be bought
- A preparatory sketch showing how the rows will look
- A duplicate invoice confirming the date already shown on that document

## Explanation

The invoice and drawing may predate use on site, and the notebook records time in storage. First installation connects a date to the physical appearance of the labels.
