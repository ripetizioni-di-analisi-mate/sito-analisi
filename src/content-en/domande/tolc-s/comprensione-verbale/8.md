The authors collected questionnaires completed voluntarily by commuters. The choice among car, bicycle and other means of transport also took place before the survey.

Which two separate decisions rested with commuters?

- Which transport to record for themselves and which population to include in the report
- Whether to cycle and whether to present the group difference as an effect
- How to travel and whether to take part
- Which results to publish and how to calculate averages
- Whether to respond and how the researchers would group the results

## Explanation

“Voluntarily” refers to answering the questionnaire. Choosing transport was a second act, already completed when researchers collected the responses.
