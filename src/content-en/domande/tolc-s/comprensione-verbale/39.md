A meeting sheet has three fields: “version submitted”, “proposed correction”, “approved text”. The second reads “replace ‘all’ with ‘some’”. The third is still blank. The key says even rejected proposals remain on record.

What evidence would establish whether the replacement made it into the text?

- The fact that someone wrote the proposal
- A recording confirming the correction was discussed
- A dated copy of the proposal containing the same suggested wording
- The wording approved or a decision adopting the correction
- The presence of “all” in the first version

## Explanation

The second field records proposals of either outcome. The suggestion’s presence shows it was raised, while acceptance requires a later decision.
