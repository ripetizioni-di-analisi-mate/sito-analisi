Some evening library visitors read alone and others work together. The report proposes observing activities and, separately, comparing the cost of the additional hours with visits.

Which pairing connects each investigation to its question?

- Observed activities: how long the building stays open. Costs and visits: which tables should be used
- Observed activities: how many users are newly registered. Costs and visits: which books are read
- Observed activities: how much staff cost. Costs and visits: who works in groups
- Observed activities: which hours residents will vote for. Costs and visits: how long reading lasts
- Observed activities: how the room is used. Costs and visits: what it costs to provide those visits

## Explanation

Observing activities distinguishes group work from reading. Comparing spending with visit numbers relates the resources used to take-up of evening opening.
