“If counting continues at the same rate, the register will be complete on Friday,” says the coordinator. On Thursday a colleague writes in the log, “Friday is a mandatory deadline.” The coordinator’s text reports no deadline imposed by anyone else.

How should Friday be classified in the coordinator’s statement?

- As a date postponed by a formal decision
- As a date to meet regardless of rate
- As the day on which the work has already been completed
- As the designated start day for counting
- As a forecast date conditional on a certain rate continuing

## Explanation

“If … continues” ties Friday to a working condition. A mandatory deadline would apply even if the pace changed and would require information the statement does not supply.
