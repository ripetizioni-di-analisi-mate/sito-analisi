An observatory receives small meteorite collections from several schools. This year it schedules arrivals on different days. A circular says there is only one table for transcribing original labels and that opening two collections together has already led to labels being switched. Public visiting hours remain as before.

Which problem is the new schedule directly designed to address?

- The risk of losing the correct match between specimens and labels during transcription
- A need to reduce the total collections accepted
- The impossibility of reading any original label
- Differences in scientific preparation between participating schools
- Lack of room for the public during visiting hours

## Explanation

The single table brings materials close together, and a switch has already occurred with two collections open. Spacing out arrivals prevents that precise overlap.
