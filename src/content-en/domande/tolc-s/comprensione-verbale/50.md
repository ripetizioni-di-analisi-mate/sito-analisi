Butterflies are counted on the same trail every year. This year half the trail was closed and the published total covers only the accessible half. The total is lower than last year’s.

Which comparison would use a consistent observation area?

- Assign zero individuals to the closed stretch in both years
- Always double last year’s total
- Compare last year’s whole trail with this year’s total after counting the accessible stretch twice
- Compare this year’s half with last year’s whole trail
- Compare the accessible stretch with that same stretch last year

## Explanation

The closure changes the area visited. Restricting both series to the same stretch removes that difference without assuming how many butterflies occupied the unvisited stretch.
