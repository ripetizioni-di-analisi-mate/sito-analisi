On a park website the headline announces, “The owl returns.” The report says the species was photographed again after three years without images. During those three years the cameras had covered only part of the area.

Which wording distinguishes renewed documentation from an actual return?

- “The owl never left the photographed site”
- “New owl photograph after three years without images”
- “The owl has colonised the park for the first time”
- “The species was absent from the entire park for three years”
- “Camera coverage was complete for three years”

## Explanation

The new image is documented. Partial coverage during the previous three years leaves open both continued presence outside the cameras’ reach and absence.
