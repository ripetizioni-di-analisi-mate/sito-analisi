For three months the library stayed open until ten. The announcement of higher evening entries is followed by management’s sentence, “Before making the choice permanent, we will compare visits and costs.”

Minutes say “The evening extension has been definitively approved.” Which feature of the passage requires correction of that entry?

- Management supplied the sentence
- Evening entries were counted
- Closing time was specified precisely
- The permanent decision depends on a comparison that still lies ahead
- The trial lasted exactly three months

## Explanation

“Before” places the comparison between the completed trial and the final decision. Trial duration alone would not say whether the final choice had already been made.
