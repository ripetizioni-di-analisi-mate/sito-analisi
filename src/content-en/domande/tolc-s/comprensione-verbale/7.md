The commuter form has two boxes, usual transport and reported well-being. The report proposes adding prior health and occupation, which might already distinguish the groups at the outset.

How should the statement about occupation be classified in the current report?

- A criterion used to allocate transport at random
- Hypothesis about a characteristic to be recorded next
- A result measured by the second box
- A measure already used to make groups comparable before data collection
- Description of a change already trialled

## Explanation

“Proposes adding” places the measurement in the future and “might” signals a possibility. Neither existing box records occupation.
