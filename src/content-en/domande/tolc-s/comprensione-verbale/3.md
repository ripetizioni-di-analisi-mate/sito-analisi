The library records entries in evening and morning time bands. Of the former the report says that attendance increased. Of the latter it notes that “entries remained steady”.

What information does “steady” add in context?

- The count was identical in every single minute
- No visitor changed time bands
- The morning total did not change appreciably from the comparison period
- The morning and evening bands have the same number of visits
- Precisely the same people entered every morning

## Explanation

“Steady” describes the trend over time of one series, the morning one. Comparing the levels of the two time bands would also require their respective totals.
