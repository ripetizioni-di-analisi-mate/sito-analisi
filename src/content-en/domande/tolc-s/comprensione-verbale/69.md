An identification key says, “If the leaf has lobes, go to step 4. If the margin is entire, go to step 7.” In the illustration the whole edge is hidden by a fold in the sheet on which the leaf is mounted.

How should the key be used with this illustration?

- Conclude that the species is missing from the key
- Go to step 7 because no lobes are visible
- Follow step 7 because the edge of the fold looks continuous in the picture
- Defer the choice until the edge can be observed
- Go to step 4 because a fold is equivalent to a lobe

## Explanation

Both branches require observing the edge. A hidden edge leaves its character unknown and does not establish an entire margin.
