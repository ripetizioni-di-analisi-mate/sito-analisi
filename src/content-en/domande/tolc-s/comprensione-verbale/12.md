In a birdwatcher’s notebook a star beside a bird’s name means “identified by sound”. Pages for foggy days have more stars than other pages. The notebook contains no count of individual birds.

In what sense could fog explain the difference between the pages?

- It assigns a star to each individual present
- It may change how the birds present are identified
- It necessarily makes every call louder
- A star indicates that an individual’s call was counted only once
- It proves that more species arrive in fog

## Explanation

The star classifies the kind of evidence used. When visibility is poor, calls may become the available clue even with no increase in bird numbers.
