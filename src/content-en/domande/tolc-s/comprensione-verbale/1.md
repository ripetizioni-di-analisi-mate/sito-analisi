“During the three-month trial the evening counter clicked more often,” writes the library. “Some of those who enter are people who step out briefly and then return.” The report gives users no individual identifier.

Which event would show how entries can rise while the number of visitors stays the same?

- The same people return more often in the evening
- Readers change tables more often while remaining inside
- The number of distinct readers rises while return visits decline
- Many new readers arrive and enter once
- Those already inside stay longer without passing the counter again

## Explanation

Each return triggers the counter again while the number of people stays fixed. Lengthening a visit alone does not generate another entry.
