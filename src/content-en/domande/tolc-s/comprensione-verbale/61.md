Every sheet in a collection carries a catalogue number. A guide claims, “All odd-numbered sheets are handwritten.” Five entries, each with number and technique, are described to check the claim.

Which entry alone would disprove the claim?

- Number 17, printed sheet
- Number 20, handwritten sheet
- Number 15, handwritten sheet
- Number 12, printed sheet
- Number 22, technique still to be identified

## Explanation

A counterexample must belong to the stated group, odd numbers, and lack the required feature, handwriting. Printed item 17 meets both conditions.
