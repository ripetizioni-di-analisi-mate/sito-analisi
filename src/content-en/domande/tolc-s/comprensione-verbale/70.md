A sampling sheet says to label the jar “before separating it from the sheet giving the collection site”. Two identical jars are moved without labels and the two sheets remain on the table.

Which information has become uncertain even though both places remain legible?

- The total number of jars moved
- The spelling of the place names
- The presence of two sheets on the table
- The existence of the two sites named on the sheets
- The match between each jar and its site

## Explanation

The sheets retain the two names and the jars retain the samples. Separation before labelling breaks the one-to-one link between the two sets.
