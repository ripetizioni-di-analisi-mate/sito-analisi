To count rings in a tree section two observers inspect the same photo. They work separately before comparing results. The protocol asks them to keep both initial counts even after reaching a final agreement.

Which question do the initial counts allow the team to answer?

- Which observer knew the tree’s true age?
- Who correctly identified every individual ring in the final count?
- How many rings were invisible to both observers?
- How closely did the readings agree before discussion?
- How old was the tree with certainty before the last ring?

## Explanation

The final value may be the same even when the initial counts were far apart. Keeping the initial figures makes independent agreement between readers assessable.
