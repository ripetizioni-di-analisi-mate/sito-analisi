An astronomy bulletin lists occultations forecast for the night. A separate field, completed by the observer after the session, reads “seen”. One entry has a printed forecast time but a blank observation field. The bulletin says missing entries may result from failure to return the form.

Which status can be assigned to that entry with certainty?

- The occultation was forecast, with no sighting recorded in the field
- The form was certainly completed and then lost
- The observer searched for the event and failed to see it
- The printed time is the actual sighting time
- The event did not occur at the forecast time

## Explanation

The time belongs to the forecast list. An empty field means no confirmation appears there, but the possibility of a missing return prevents treating it as a certain failure to observe.
