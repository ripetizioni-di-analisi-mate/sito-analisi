Museum admission covers the permanent gallery. A temporary exhibition ticket “is added to admission and includes the audio guide”. A party buys only standard admission.

Which ticket is the subject of “includes” in the quoted sentence?

- Any museum admission
- The temporary exhibition ticket
- Standard admission when used by a group
- The audio guide purchased separately before the visit
- The ticket for the permanent gallery alone, to which the audio guide fee should be added

## Explanation

“Is added” and “includes” share the subject “a temporary exhibition ticket”. The purchase described concerns only the other ticket.
