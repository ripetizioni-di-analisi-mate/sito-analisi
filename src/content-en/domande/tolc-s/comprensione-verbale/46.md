A museum compares the proportion of legible photos in two series digitised with different programs. The first series comes from well-preserved plates and the second from already cracked plates. The report gives a higher proportion for the first series.

Which conclusion would require a test that the comparison does not provide?

- That the first program caused the higher legibility
- That the original plates were in different condition
- That the series were digitised with different programs
- That the comparison concerns images obtained from plates
- That the observed proportion was higher for the first series

## Explanation

Software changes together with the plates’ condition. Processing the same plates with both programs would enable a comparison that does not also attribute the initial condition difference to the program.
