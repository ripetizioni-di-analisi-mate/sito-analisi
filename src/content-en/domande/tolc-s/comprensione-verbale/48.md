In a survey 40% of “participants” say they have read a guide. A note specifies, “People who skipped the question are excluded from the denominator; ‘participants’ here means those who answered this item.”

If one hundred people opened the questionnaire but only fifty answered the item, how many reported readings does the percentage represent?

- Ten
- Eighty
- Twenty
- Forty
- Fifty

## Explanation

The note restricts the base to fifty usable answers. Forty per cent of fifty is twenty. A base of one hundred would include those who skipped the item.
