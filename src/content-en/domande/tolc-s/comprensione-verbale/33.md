A sensor manual distinguishes “out of service” from “waiting”. In the first state recording should be stopped. In the second the device awaits a synchronisation signal. The screen shows “waiting”.

Which shift note reports the state shown without presuming its cause?

- “Recording must be stopped because of a fault”
- “The device is irreparably broken”
- “The device is awaiting synchronisation”
- “Synchronisation succeeded”
- “The signal has been received and rejected”

## Explanation

The manual assigns the message one precise function, waiting for the signal. It does not say whether the signal is delayed, absent or still due.
