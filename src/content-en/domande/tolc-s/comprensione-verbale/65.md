A planetarium poster says “one accompanying adult free per group of at least ten pupils”. Ten pupils and two adults arrive under a single group booking.

Which part of the poster limits the extent of the concession?

- “At least ten” excludes the group because it has exactly ten
- A single booking makes admission free for everyone
- “Per group” turns each pupil into a separate group
- “Free” automatically extends the concession to both adults
- “One accompanying adult” limits free entry to one of the two

## Explanation

Ten pupils meet the “at least” threshold. There is one group booking, and the poster assigns that group one free adult place.
