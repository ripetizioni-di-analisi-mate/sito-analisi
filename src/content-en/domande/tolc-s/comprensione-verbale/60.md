“There is a nest above every roof,” writes a reporter. The volunteer who accompanied him replies, “You asked me to show you only the houses where I had found a nest.”

Why could the route not test the general claim?

- The reporter needed to visit each selected roof twice
- The reporter visited equal numbers of houses with and without nests
- A nest on one roof shows that roof can never be an exception at any time
- The nests had already been observed, so they could no longer be present when the reporter visited
- Selection excluded houses without a known nest from the outset

## Explanation

The route was constructed from previously observed nest presence. Possible counterexamples could never have entered the visit.
