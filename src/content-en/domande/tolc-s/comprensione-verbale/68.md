In a greenhouse a yellow light indicates that an inspection is due. A card on the door says, “Mark ‘done’ after inspecting the tanks.” The light comes on, but the attendant is called elsewhere before entering.

What can she truthfully note at handover?

- The light cancels the need for inspection
- The inspection is complete because the scheduled time has arrived
- The inspection has been signalled and still needs to be done
- The tanks certainly have a fault
- The light means another attendant has finished the inspection

## Explanation

The light gives a time signal. “Done” requires an actual inspection, and the attendant was called away before carrying one out.
