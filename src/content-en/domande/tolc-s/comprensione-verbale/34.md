At an exhibition meeting the captions are approved “subject to correction of the scientific names”. The curator reads the first half of the sentence and sends them to print before the corrections.

What is the effect of the overlooked phrase?

- It requires another meeting even after correction
- It identifies who voted against
- It permanently invalidates approval of every caption
- It makes an amendment necessary for the approval to take full effect
- It allows the names to remain unchanged

## Explanation

“Subject to” ties assent to a specific requirement, correcting the names. The wording does not explicitly require another vote.
