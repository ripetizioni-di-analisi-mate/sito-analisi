At a public reading the start signal is a gong stroke. The running order says to start without repeating it if it is audible at the back. If it is inaudible, the assistant raises a card and the gong is repeated. From the back the assistant records “heard clearly”.

Which continuation follows the applicable branch of the instructions?

- Begin the reading without requesting another stroke
- Raise the card to confirm the sound can be heard
- Repeat the gong only after the reading has begun
- Wait until the audience raises the card
- Always sound the gong twice before each reading

## Explanation

The assistant’s note satisfies the first condition, audibility at the back. The card belongs exclusively to the other branch, where the signal was not heard.
