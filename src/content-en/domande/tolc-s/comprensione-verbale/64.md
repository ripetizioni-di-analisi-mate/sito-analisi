A club catalogue describes a “fee per household” and a “personal excursion ticket”. Two sisters belong to the same household and both join the excursion.

Which count respects the two units used in the catalogue?

- Two fees and one ticket
- One fee and one ticket
- Two fees and two tickets
- One fee and two tickets
- Only two tickets, because belonging to a household cancels the fee

## Explanation

“Per household” counts the shared family once. “Personal” requires one ticket for each of the two participants.
