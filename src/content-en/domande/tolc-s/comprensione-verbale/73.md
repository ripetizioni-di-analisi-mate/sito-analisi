Residents want to identify a path at dusk. A designer proposes larger signs. During the trial people recognise the route only when a pale border is added. Small signs with the border are recognised too.

What conclusion about size is supported by the described trial?

- The large signs were unreadable even when bordered
- Small signs without borders worked better than large ones
- Large size was not necessary for recognition in the cases tested
- Size never matters at any distance
- The border had an effect only on the large signs

## Explanation

Recognition using small bordered signs supplies a case in which large size is absent but the task succeeds. The passage confines the conclusion to the dusk trial.
