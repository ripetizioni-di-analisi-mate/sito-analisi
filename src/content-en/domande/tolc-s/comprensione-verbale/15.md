A field bulletin uses bold type for samples examined twice. One bold entry is marked “uncertain result”. The introduction says a repeat can also confirm a reading difficulty.

Which conclusion respects both annotations?

- Bold means the second examination still has to be scheduled
- Bold type guarantees a definitive identification
- The second reading necessarily reversed the first
- “Uncertain” cancels the information about repetition
- The examination was repeated and identification remains uncertain

## Explanation

The typographical convention concerns how many readings took place. The annotation concerns their outcome. A persistent difficulty is compatible with two readings.
