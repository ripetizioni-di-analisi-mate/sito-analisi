A notice reports two contributions: a technician said the bridge can be inspected from below. A councillor said she would propose the inspection to the council. The headline merges the two voices into “Technician decides on inspection”.

Which elements does the headline confuse?

- The name of the council and the day set for its decision
- The result of a completed inspection and the publication date
- Two people’s agreement on a decision already put to a vote
- The kind of bridge and the point from which the technician deems it observable
- Who makes the proposal and the distinction between technical feasibility and an adopted decision

## Explanation

The technician gives a feasibility judgement. The councillor promises a proposal, and neither statement establishes that the council has decided.
