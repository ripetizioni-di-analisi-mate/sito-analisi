A summary shows “three out of four” beside “reviews”. Its key says a file may go through review several times and the numerator counts reviews completed within the week, not distinct files. All four review instances fall in the same period.

Which set does the denominator four refer to?

- The review instances recorded in the period
- Distinct files certainly examined only once
- Reviewers involved in the procedure
- Weeks in which at least one review arrived
- Only the reviews not completed on time

## Explanation

The numerator three selects timely completions from the four recorded instances. Since a file can return, four instances do not determine the number of distinct files.
