Two teams count tracks along a bank. The first erases each track after recording it. The second leaves it and revisits the same stretch in the afternoon. By evening the second team has many more entries.

What should be known before interpreting the difference as more animal traffic?

- Whether observers used separate rows for morning and afternoon without identifying individual tracks
- Whether previously seen tracks were counted again on the second visit
- Whether both available counts have already been expressed as daily totals
- Whether both teams added up their entries that evening
- Whether the second team’s total includes its morning and afternoon visits arranged by time

## Explanation

Leaving tracks intact and returning makes it possible to log the same mark twice. The higher entry count could therefore result from the counting method.
