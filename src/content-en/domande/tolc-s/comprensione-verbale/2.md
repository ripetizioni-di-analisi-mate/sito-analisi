Management knows the number of evening entries. It wants to know whether the new hours encourage individual reading. Two occupied tables could hold readers or working groups equally well.

Which objection does the example of the two tables answer?

- “The counter records how many times someone crosses the entrance”
- “Counting occupied seats would suffice to identify the activity”
- “Not every visitor stays for the same amount of time”
- “The library is also open in the evening”
- “People reading alone can occupy an entire desk”

## Explanation

With occupancy held constant, the passage supplies two possible activities. That counters the suggestion that occupancy uniquely identifies what visitors are doing.
