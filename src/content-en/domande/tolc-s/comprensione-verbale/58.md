The statement “Not all seeds in the batch germinated” is summarised as “No seeds germinated”. The notebook gives an example sufficient to reject the latter: one seed in the batch with a visible emerging root.

Which rewriting preserves the meaning of the first sentence?

- All seeds germinated on different days
- At least half the batch failed to germinate
- At least one seed in the batch failed to germinate
- Exactly one seed in the batch germinated
- Only seeds individually observed in the notebook germinated

## Explanation

Negating “all” requires at least one exception without specifying how many. The seed with an emerging root also shows why “none” would make a different claim.
