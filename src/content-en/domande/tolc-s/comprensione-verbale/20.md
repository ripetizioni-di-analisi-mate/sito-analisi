A depot list has a blank line between two instruments. The compiler says she leaves a line blank when a name is illegible. Elsewhere she writes “empty shelf” in full when she finds no objects.

What distinguishes the blank line from the written phrase?

- The former certifies a theft, the latter a return
- Both indicate an unreadable name
- Both indicate that no object was present
- The latter proves the shelf was always empty
- The former marks a transcription limit, the latter an observation about the shelf

## Explanation

The convention associates blank space with reading a name. To communicate the physical absence of objects, the compiler uses explicit words.
