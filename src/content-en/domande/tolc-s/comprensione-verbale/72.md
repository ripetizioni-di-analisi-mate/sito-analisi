A museum asks five volunteers to try a new audio guide. All start it unaided. One volunteer notes, however, that when two works are close the guide sometimes describes the neighbouring one. The test sheet records only “start successful”.

Which check should be added to investigate the problem that arose during the visit?

- Record whether volunteers needed help switching on the device
- Compare the work in front of the visitor with the description actually played
- Ask which title visitors prefer among the tracks heard near the two works
- Time how long the guide takes to switch on at the entrance
- Measure the audio volume at the beginning of the visit

## Explanation

The guide started even for the volunteer who reports a swapped description. The sheet records the first event but does not check track selection near adjacent works.
