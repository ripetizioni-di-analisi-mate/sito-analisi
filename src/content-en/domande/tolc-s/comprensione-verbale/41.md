A census sheet publishes nests in three sectors. Its “verified” row contains eight nests in the Lake sector and six in the Wood sector. Four reports from the Meadow sector await checking. A note requires that reports stay outside the verified total until a visit confirms them.

Which total can carry the label “verified” today?

- Fourteen, adding only the first two sectors
- Eighteen, including every report received
- Ten, subtracting the four reports from the fourteen nests
- No total, because one sector still awaits checking
- Four, counting only the sector awaiting checks

## Explanation

Eight plus six gives fourteen observations already confirmed. The four Meadow reports belong to another, provisional category and do not prevent reporting the number verified so far.
