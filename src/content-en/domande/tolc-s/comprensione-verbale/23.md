A herbarium curator describes a sheet: “Specimen collected in the valley. The flower drawn in the margin comes from a second individual.” The drawing and the pressed specimen appear to have different petals.

Which assumption would create an apparent contradiction that the note resolves?

- That placing them on one sheet might invite comparison
- That the margin may hold drawings
- That the drawing depicts the very flower of the mounted specimen
- That two individuals may be represented on one sheet
- That the specimen’s origin is recorded

## Explanation

The note assigns the drawing to a second individual. This removes the premise that every drawn petal must match one on the mounted specimen.
