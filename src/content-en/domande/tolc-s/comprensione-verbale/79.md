An online paper corrects a lecture time and keeps an “updated at 4 pm” note beside the story. One reader printed the page at 2 pm. Another opened it at 5 pm. Their copies show different times for the same lecture.

Which information explains the difference without positing two lectures?

- The 2 pm printout timestamp coincides with the start time of the lecture
- The newspaper’s print edition might give a different time from the online edition
- The second reader did not print the page
- The two consultations fall on opposite sides of the recorded correction
- The two readers may have different clocks

## Explanation

The 2 pm printout predates the 4 pm update and preserves the version visible then. The 5 pm consultation accesses the corrected version.
