A student wants to find out whether a covering slows heat loss. She fills identical containers with water at the same temperature and covers only one. Before measuring, she puts the covered one by an open window and the other in the middle of the room.

Which of the choices described creates a second possible explanation for the result?

- Giving the water the same initial temperature
- Using the covering as the object under test
- Placing the containers in different locations
- The intention to measure heat loss
- Using identical containers

## Explanation

The open window introduces an environmental difference aligned with covered versus uncovered status. Any observed gap would not isolate the effect of the covering alone.
