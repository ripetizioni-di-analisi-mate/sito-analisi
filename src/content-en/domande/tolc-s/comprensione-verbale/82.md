A guide defines an “observed species” as one seen during the outing and a “reported species” as one mentioned by a source that may predate the outing. A list reads “reported in the keeper’s notebook, observed today”.

What does this entry show about the two categories?

- The earlier report is cancelled by today’s observation
- They can apply to the same species on different evidence
- The keeper must have taken part in today’s outing
- Every report is necessarily an observation on the outing
- A species seen today cannot appear in an earlier notebook

## Explanation

The keeper’s notebook is a source, so the species meets the definition of “reported”. Today’s sighting took place during the outing, so the same species also meets the definition of “observed”. The two definitions describe different kinds of evidence and neither excludes the other, so the two categories can apply to the same species.
