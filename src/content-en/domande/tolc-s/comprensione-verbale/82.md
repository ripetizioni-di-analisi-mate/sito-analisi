A nature guide explains a layout choice. “I left a column blank next to each description so that people who revisit the trail can add their own dates. In the trial copy I had filled in mine, and readers mistook them for the only suitable weeks.” Why does the blank column better serve the guide’s purpose?

- It hides the dates because the author considers them inaccurate
- It invites personal records without making the author’s dates seem prescriptive
- It means that no species has yet been observed
- It assigns every reader the same week for a visit
- It replaces each species description with a blank space

## Explanation

Readers treated the author’s example dates in the trial copy as calendar restrictions. The blank column lets readers record their own observations and removes the source of that misunderstanding.
