Among adults who answered the survey, cyclists report greater well-being on average. A reader asks whether, given this result, some cyclists could have given their well-being a low score.

Which explanation answers the reader while preserving the meaning of the comparison?

- A comparison of averages permits low individual values in the higher-average group
- The questionnaire automatically excludes low scores
- The first observation concerns adults and the last only minors
- The cyclists’ average must equal every individual score
- An average uses only the highest score

## Explanation

“On average” concerns the scores taken together. Some low values can be offset by higher ones within the same group.
