An editorial team corrects a statement about waste collection: “Every building received a container” becomes “Every building that requested one received a container.” The note does not list buildings that made no request.

What does the new version assert about buildings that did not apply?

- It establishes that none received a container
- It asserts that they applied after delivery
- It does not specify whether they received a container
- It identifies them as the only recipients
- It establishes that all received one

## Explanation

The relative clause “that requested one” restricts the set over which “every” applies. The statement guarantees the outcome for applicants and leaves the others open.
