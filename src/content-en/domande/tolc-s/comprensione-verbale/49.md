An exhibition arranges specimens from smallest to largest without using equal size intervals. The physical gap from the third label to the fourth equals the gap from the first to the second.

What can a visitor infer from the exhibition order?

- That size increases by a constant amount between neighbours
- That label positions measure specimen size to scale
- That the fourth is twice as large as the second
- That the fourth specimen is at least as large as the third
- That the first three are identical in size

## Explanation

The display preserves rank. The gap between labels concerns physical layout and does not quantify differences in size.
