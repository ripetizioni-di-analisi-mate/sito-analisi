On the eve of a journey a diarist writes, “A year from now I shall laugh at this heavy suitcase.” Twelve months later she records only that the suitcase is still in the attic.

What does the future-tense sentence establish?

- That the suitcase had always stayed in the attic
- How the diarist then imagined her later reaction
- That she actually laughed a year later
- That the second entry corrects the weight of the case
- That the trip lasted a year

## Explanation

“I shall laugh” expresses a prediction made before departure. The later entry concerns the suitcase’s location and does not report the predicted reaction.
