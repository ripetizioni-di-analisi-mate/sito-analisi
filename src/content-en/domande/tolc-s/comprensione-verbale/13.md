The garden manual distinguishes “The path is passable” from “The path has been travelled”. The first follows from a surface inspection. The second requires a documented passage by someone.

Which change introduces an additional fact?

- From “documented passage” to “attested passage”
- From “is traversable” to “one can pass”
- From “can be travelled” to “someone has travelled it”
- From “is passable” to “the surface permits passage”
- From “has been travelled” to “someone has passed along it”

## Explanation

An inspection can establish that the surface permits travel without recording a traveller. Moving from possibility to event requires additional evidence.
