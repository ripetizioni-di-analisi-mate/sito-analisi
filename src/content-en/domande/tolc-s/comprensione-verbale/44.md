On a station display solid dots mark temperatures actually measured, while the line joining them is drawn by the software. There are dots at 10 and 12, and only the line at 11.

What does a value read at 11 on that graph represent?

- The maximum temperature of the day
- The sum of the two recorded temperatures
- A third measurement taken by the station at 11
- A value obtained from the graphical link between two measurements
- The instrument’s precision limit

## Explanation

Dots distinguish actual readings. At 11 one reads the segment drawn between two dots, which does not document an instrument reading at that instant.
