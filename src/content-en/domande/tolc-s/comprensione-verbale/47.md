A nursery records “seedlings delivered” rather than “seedlings surviving”. A report uses the register to claim that every recipient balcony stayed in bloom all summer.

Which later observation directly addresses the missing step?

- Ask recipients whether they had grown flowers the previous summer
- Checking flowering over the summer on the recipient balconies
- Counting the trays again as they leave the nursery
- Check whether every initial delivery contained the planned number of seedlings
- Photograph only the newly delivered plants before they go on the balconies

## Explanation

Delivery precedes the outcome attributed to the whole summer. Repeated observation at the recipient sites addresses the duration and actual presence of flowers.
