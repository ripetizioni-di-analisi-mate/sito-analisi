A notice reads, “Students may enrol and teachers may accompany them.” An internal translation renders the second part as “teachers must accompany them”.

Which consequence does the translation introduce?

- It removes the students’ permission to enrol from the first clause
- It extends enrolment to every teacher
- It bars teachers from accompanying students
- It makes optional what had been compulsory for teachers
- It turns permission granted to teachers into an obligation

## Explanation

“May” allows either accompanying or choosing not to accompany. “Must” excludes the second possibility and changes the notice’s scope.
