An astronomer indexes a notebook. She writes, “For some spots the draughtsperson recorded only their shape, without stating their position on the disc. Therefore those entries were not used for the position map.” The shape descriptions remain in the catalogue transcript.

Which relationship does “therefore” introduce?

- A missing coordinate motivates an editorial choice limited to the position map
- An independent copying error is added to the first observation
- The shape of the spots yields the missing coordinate
- Transcription in the catalogue is opposed by a ban on describing shapes
- The decision not to use the entries caused the lack of coordinates in the original notebook

## Explanation

The connective moves from a missing clue to its consequence for a specific output. The shapes’ presence in the catalogue rules out rejection of the entries for every purpose.
