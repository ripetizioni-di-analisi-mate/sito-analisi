The invitation to describe travel and well-being was addressed to adult commuters. Only some invitees responded. The report gives the averages of their answers.

A subheading says “Here is how all invitees feel.” Which phrase correctly narrows the population?

- on-site workers
- the recipients of the invitation
- the city’s cyclists
- the residents
- the respondents

## Explanation

Recipients include people who left the questionnaire unanswered. Only respondents supplied the well-being statements used in the averages.
