An altarpiece appears in an inventory compiled in 1708. The compiler uses the phrase “already in the chapel”, without giving the year it was made. The altarpiece bears no other date.

For the altarpiece’s presence in the chapel, what does 1708 provide?

- The exact year the artist began work
- A date by which its presence is documented
- The year when it left the chapel
- The exact year of delivery
- The length of its stay

## Explanation

“Already” indicates that the work was there when the inventory was made. It could have arrived earlier, and the compiler does not claim to have witnessed its arrival.
