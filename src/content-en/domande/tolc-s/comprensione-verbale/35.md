Two labels accompany a specimen tube: “Comparison sample” states its intended role, and “not examined” its current status. Someone writes in the report, “As a comparison, its value is already known.”

Which piece of information undermines the move from role to measured value?

- The report was written after labelling
- A comparison with other samples is planned
- The tube has two labels instead of one
- The tube is intended for storage
- The status label says the examination has yet to take place

## Explanation

Stating the tube’s purpose supplies no examination result. “Not examined” prevents the labels from establishing a value already measured on that sample.
