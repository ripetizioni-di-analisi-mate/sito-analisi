A story opens with the sound of a glass falling. A character behind the wall takes it for a broken window. The reader has already been shown the glass falling from the edge of the table.

What is the effect of this unequal distribution of information?

- The narrator establishes the broken window as a fact
- The reader is forced to make the same mistake
- The reader can recognise the mistake before the character does
- The character knows the cause better than the reader
- The two explanations are presented as equally confirmed

## Explanation

The character hears a sound from the next room. The reader has also seen the fall and can check the character’s hypothesis against it.
