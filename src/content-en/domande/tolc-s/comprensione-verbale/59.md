A press note says, “According to preliminary calculations, the new tank could reduce waste.” During editing only “could reduce” is changed to “reduces”. The tank has not yet entered service.

Which qualification is lost in the revision?

- The existence of preliminary calculations as the stated source
- Identification of the tank as new
- Reference to the tank rather than the entire plant
- The possible and still predictive nature of the benefit
- The general topic of waste

## Explanation

“Could” signals that the saving is a prediction. The simple present presents it as an established property, although no observation in service is available yet.
