A laboratory sheet distinguishes an “original specimen” from a “working copy”, prepared from the original and intended for annotations. A researcher finds writing only on the copy and immediately attributes it to the collector of the original.

What check would address the doubt over authorship of the writing?

- Check only that the original specimen still exists
- Count how many working copies the laboratory produced
- Look for when and by whom the working copy was annotated
- Identify who prepared the copying medium without checking who wrote on it
- Confirm the copy came from that original without examining its annotations

## Explanation

The copy may receive later writing precisely because of its function. Its derivation from the original does not automatically attribute additions found only on the copy to the collector.
