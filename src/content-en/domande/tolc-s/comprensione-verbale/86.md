Before a site visit a report puts a wall’s length “between 12 and 14 metres” using the available plan. It calls this a plausible estimate to be checked and says only the survey will supply the measurement for cutting the panel.

What can be entered on the work sheet before the survey?

- The interval as a preliminary estimate, leaving the cutting dimension pending
- Any value in the interval as an equally correct cutting dimension
- Twelve metres as a minimum certified by an already completed survey
- Fourteen metres as the length certainly measured on site
- Thirteen metres as the exact measurement because it is the midpoint

## Explanation

The plan provides an estimate while the cutting sheet requires an actual measurement. Neither the midpoint nor an endpoint becomes a certified measurement just by belonging to the interval.
