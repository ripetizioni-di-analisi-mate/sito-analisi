In the final chapter of a story a child seated at a courtyard window remembers a party without music. A postman recalls a quartet playing at the same time under the street-facing arcade. The narrator then shows the long enclosed corridor separating courtyard and street on a plan.

What does the plan invite the reader to do?

- Assess what each character could perceive from their own location
- Use the plan to establish what pieces the quartet played
- Take failure to hear the music as proof the quartet played only after the party
- Accept the postman as narrator of every scene merely because he names the quartet
- Move one of the two accounts to another day

## Explanation

The plan introduces physical separation between the listening positions. Before discarding either account, the reader can ask whether the music would have been equally audible from both.
