A map key divides depths into “under 5 metres”, “5 to under 10”, and “10 or more”. Two wells are both coloured for the middle band.

Which statement is guaranteed for both wells?

- They necessarily have the same depth
- Both are strictly deeper than 5 metres
- Their depth difference is necessarily 5 metres
- One reaches exactly 10 metres
- Both reach at least 5 metres and neither reaches 10

## Explanation

The band includes the lower boundary and excludes the upper one. A shared colour identifies an interval, leaving different values possible within it.
