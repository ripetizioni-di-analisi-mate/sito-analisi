A median divides a triangle of area $36\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ into two triangles. What is the area of each?

- $12\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $36\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The median ends at the midpoint of the opposite side. The resulting triangles therefore have equal bases on the same line and the same altitude from their shared vertex. Their areas are equal and sum to $36\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, so each is $18\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Their other sides need not be equal.
