A panel has area $2500\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. What is the same area in square meters?

- $25\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac14\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac52\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac1{40}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac1{400}\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

One meter contains $100$ centimeters, so one square meter contains $100^2=10000$ square centimeters. The given area equals $\dfrac{2500}{10000}=\dfrac14\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The area conversion factor is the square of the length conversion factor, which explains the divisor $10000$.
