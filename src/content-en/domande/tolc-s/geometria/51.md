An object of volume $80\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ is fully submerged in a cylindrical tank with base area $40\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, without overflow. How much does the water rise?

- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $120\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The submerged object occupies volume previously available to water. The same $80\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ appears in the extra layer above the initial level. Since the cross section is constant, $40\Delta h=80$, giving $\Delta h=2\,\mathrm{cm}$. The object’s shape does not change the result as long as it is fully submerged.
