What is the area of a disk with radius $3\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $6\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $9\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $3\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $18\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $27\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The disk area is $A=\pi r^2$. The given length is the radius, so $A=\pi\cdot3^2=9\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Squaring the length produces square centimeters. The value $6\pi$ comes from $2\pi r$, which instead calculates the circumference and would have units of centimeters.
