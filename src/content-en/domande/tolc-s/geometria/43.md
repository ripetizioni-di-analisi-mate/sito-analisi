How many points do the circle $x^2+y^2=16$ and line $y=4$ have in common?

::grafico{f="4" curve="4*cos(t),4*sin(t),0,6.283185307179586" xmin=-5 xmax=5 ymin=-5 ymax=5 uguale=1 legenda=0 titolo="The line y = 4 and the circle of radius 4"}

- $0$
- $2$
- $1$
- $4$
- Infinitely many

## Explanation

Substituting $y=4$ into the circle equation gives $x^2+16=16$, so $x^2=0$ and $x=0$. The only point is $(0,4)$. The line is tangent because its distance from the center is $4$, equal to the radius. The equation’s double root does not represent two distinct points.
