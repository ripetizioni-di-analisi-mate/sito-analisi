A rectangle with sides $6$ and $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ is inscribed in a circle. What is the radius?

- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The rectangle’s diagonals meet at their common midpoint, which is equidistant from all four vertices and is therefore the circle’s center. A diagonal measures $\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and is a diameter. The radius is half of it, $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. Half the longer side would not reach the vertices.
