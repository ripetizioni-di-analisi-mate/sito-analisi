A $6\,\mathrm{m}$ by $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$ floor is exactly covered by square tiles with side $30\,\mathrm{cm}$. How many tiles are needed?

- $30$
- $90$
- $300$
- $150$
- $900$

## Explanation

Each tile side is $0.3\,\mathrm{m}$. The two floor dimensions fit $6:0.3=20$ and $4.5:0.3=15$ tiles. The grid therefore contains $20\cdot15=300$ pieces. Both quotients are integers, so the specified covering is possible without cuts or additional waste.
