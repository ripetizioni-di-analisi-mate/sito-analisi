A triangle is reflected in a line. Which statement is always true?

- Every vertex stays fixed
- The vertex orientation stays the same
- The image triangle is always disjoint from the original
- Its area and side lengths stay the same
- Its area doubles

## Explanation

Reflection is an isometry, so it preserves all distances. Corresponding sides have equal lengths and the triangles are congruent, hence their areas agree. Reflection does reverse orientation. Only points on the reflection axis stay fixed, and the figures may overlap depending on their position.
