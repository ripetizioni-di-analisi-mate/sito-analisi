In an isosceles triangle, the angle opposite the base is $38^\circ$. What is each base angle?

- $76^\circ$
- $142^\circ$
- $38^\circ$
- $71^\circ$
- $52^\circ$

## Explanation

The base angles are equal because they are opposite equal sides. Subtracting the vertex angle from the total leaves $180^\circ-38^\circ=142^\circ$, to be divided into two equal parts. Each base angle therefore measures $71^\circ$. The check $71^\circ+71^\circ+38^\circ=180^\circ$ confirms the sum.
