The interior angles of a triangle are in the ratio $2:3:4$. What is the largest angle?

- $40^\circ$
- $80^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $100^\circ$

## Explanation

Write the angles as $2t,3t,4t$. Their sum is $180^\circ$, so $9t=180^\circ$ and $t=20^\circ$. The largest angle is $4t=80^\circ$. The ratio assigns parts of the total; the numbers $2,3,4$ are not already degree measures. The other angles are $40^\circ$ and $60^\circ$.
