A triangle’s median is $21\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. How far is the centroid from the vertex where the median starts?

- $7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{21}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $21\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The centroid divides each median in the ratio $2:1$ starting from the vertex. The full median contains three equal parts, each $21:3=7\,\mathrm{cm}$. The vertex-to-centroid distance contains two parts and is $14\,\mathrm{cm}$. The remaining segment from centroid to side midpoint is instead $7\,\mathrm{cm}$.
