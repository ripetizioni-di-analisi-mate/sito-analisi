A right prism of height $7\,\mathrm{cm}$ has a right-triangular base with legs $3$ and $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its lateral surface area?

- $42\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $49\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $96\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $21\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $84\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The base hypotenuse is $5$, so the base perimeter is $3+4+5=12\,\mathrm{cm}$. The three lateral faces are rectangles of height $7$ whose widths sum to $12$, hence the lateral area is $12\cdot7=84\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Multiplying the triangular area by the height would instead give the volume.
