What is the smallest positive rotation angle about the center that maps a regular pentagon onto itself?

- $72^\circ$
- $36^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $108^\circ$
- $180^\circ$

## Explanation

The five vertices are equally spaced in angle around the center. Moving from one vertex to the next requires $\dfrac{360^\circ}{5}=72^\circ$ and sends every vertex to a vertex, preserving the sides. No smaller positive rotation sends a vertex to another. The interior angle $108^\circ$ instead describes a boundary angle.
