A point is reflected first in $x=0$ and then in $x=3$. What is the combined transformation?

- Translation 3 units to the right
- Translation 6 units to the left
- Rotation of 180 degrees about the origin
- Reflection in x = 3
- Translation 6 units to the right

## Explanation

The first reflection sends $(x,y)$ to $(-x,y)$. Reflecting a horizontal coordinate $u$ in $x=3$ changes it to $6-u$, so the second operation gives $(6+x,y)$. The result is a horizontal translation $6$ units right, twice the distance between the two parallel reflection axes.
