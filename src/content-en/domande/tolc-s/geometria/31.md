From a $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ rectangle, a $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ rectangle is cut at one corner, with sides parallel to those of the sheet. What is the remaining figure’s perimeter?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 10,0 10,4 6,4 6,6 0,6" nodi="A,0,0;B,10,0;C,10,4;D,6,4;E,6,6;F,0,6" quote="A,B,10 cm;F,A,6 cm;D,C,4 cm;E,D,2 cm" titolo="The boundary follows the remaining region"}

- $32\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $44\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The original perimeter is $2(10+6)=32\,\mathrm{cm}$. The cut removes two outer segments of lengths $4$ and $2$, but creates two inner edges of the same lengths. The total change is therefore $-4-2+4+2=0$, and the perimeter remains $32\,\mathrm{cm}$. The area decrease does not imply a boundary decrease in this case.
