A right triangle has legs of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the hypotenuse length?

- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\sqrt{14}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $48\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Since the given sides are perpendicular, apply the Pythagorean theorem to the hypotenuse $c$. This gives $c^2=6^2+8^2=36+64=100$, hence $c=10\,\mathrm{cm}$. Choose the positive root because $c$ is a length. Adding the legs directly would give the path along two sides, rather than the segment joining their endpoints.
