The circle $(x-1)^2+(y-2)^2=25$ intersects the line $x=4$. What are the vertical coordinates of the intersection points?

- $-4\ \text{and}\ 4$
- $-3\ \text{and}\ 7$
- $-2\ \text{and}\ 6$
- $0\ \text{and}\ 4$
- $-6\ \text{and}\ 2$

## Explanation

Substituting $x=4$, the horizontal term is $(4-1)^2=9$. This leaves $(y-2)^2=25-9=16$, so $y-2=\pm4$. The vertical coordinates are $y=-2$ and $y=6$, symmetric about the center’s vertical coordinate $2$. Writing only $\pm4$ would omit the circle’s vertical translation.
