A parallelogram has adjacent sides of $10$ and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ with included angle $30^\circ$. What is its area?

- $60\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $30\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $60\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

Taking side $10$ as the base, the height is the perpendicular component of side $6$. Thus $h=6\sin30^\circ=6\cdot\dfrac12=3\,\mathrm{cm}$. The area is $A=bh=10\cdot3=30\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Multiplying the two side lengths without the sine would apply only if the included angle were a right angle.
