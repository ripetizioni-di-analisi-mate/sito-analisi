An open-top cylindrical container has radius $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. Ignoring thickness, what material area forms its wall and bottom?

- $66\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $57\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $48\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $24\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $81\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The lateral wall unfolds into a rectangle of base $2\pi r$ and height $h$, so its area is $2\pi\cdot3\cdot8=48\pi$. The bottom adds one base area, $\pi\cdot3^2=9\pi$. The total is $57\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Counting two bases would include a lid that is not present.
