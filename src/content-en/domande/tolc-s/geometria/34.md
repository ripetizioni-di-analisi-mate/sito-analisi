In triangle $ABC$, point $D$ on $BC$ satisfies $BD:DC=2:3$. If triangle $ABD$ has area $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, what is the area of $ADC$?

- $45\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $50\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $\dfrac{40}{3}\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The two triangles have the same altitude to line $BC$. In the area formula, this height is a common factor, so the area ratio equals the base ratio, $2:3$. Thus the area of $ADC$ is $20\cdot\dfrac32=30\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The ratio is not squared because no similarity was specified.
