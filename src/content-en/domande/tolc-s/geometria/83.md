What is the area of the region described by $-1\le x\le3$ and $2\le y\le5$?

- $15$
- $8$
- $12$
- $7$
- $20$

## Explanation

The conditions bound a rectangle. Its width is the difference between extreme horizontal coordinates, $3-(-1)=4$, and its height is $5-2=3$. Its area is therefore $4\cdot3=12$. Its position relative to the origin does not change the product; coordinate differences are used, rather than just positive endpoints.
