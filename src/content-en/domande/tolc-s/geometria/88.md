Which point belongs to the region $(x-1)^2+(y+2)^2<4$?

- $(3,-2)$
- $(1,0)$
- $(2,-2)$
- $(-2,-2)$
- $(1,1)$

## Explanation

The region is the interior of the disk centered at $(1,-2)$ with radius $2$, excluding its circle boundary. For $(2,-2)$, the left side is $1^2+0^2=1<4$. Points $(3,-2)$ and $(1,0)$ instead give $4$ and lie on the excluded boundary; the other two give $9$ and lie outside.
