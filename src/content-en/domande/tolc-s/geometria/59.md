Two distinct lines in space are both perpendicular to the same plane. Which relationship must hold?

- They intersect
- They are skew
- They are perpendicular to each other
- They are parallel
- They lie in the plane

## Explanation

The direction perpendicular to a plane is its normal direction. Both lines therefore share that normal direction. Since they are distinct, they are parallel. Their positions may differ, but their directions do not; skew lines would instead have different directions and would not lie in one plane.
