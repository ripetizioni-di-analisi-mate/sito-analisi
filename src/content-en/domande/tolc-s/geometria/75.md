A dilation centered at $C=(1,2)$ with factor $2$ transforms $P=(3,1)$. What is the image?

- $(6,2)$
- $(4,-2)$
- $(3,4)$
- $(4,0)$
- $(5,0)$

## Explanation

The vector from the center to the point is $P-C=(2,-1)$. Dilation doubles this vector to $(4,-2)$, which must then be added to the center: $P_1=C+2(P-C)=(1,2)+(4,-2)=(5,0)$. Doubling $P$’s coordinates directly would instead use the origin as the center.
