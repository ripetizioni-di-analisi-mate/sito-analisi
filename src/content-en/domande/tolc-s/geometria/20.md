A right triangle has hypotenuse $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the radius of its circumcircle?

- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{10}{\sqrt2}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac52\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

A right inscribed angle subtends a semicircle. Hence the hypotenuse of a right triangle is a diameter of its circumcircle. The center is the midpoint of the hypotenuse, and the radius is half the diameter, namely $10:2=5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The two leg lengths are not needed separately.
