A tank has internal dimensions $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $25\,\mathrm{cm}$. How many liters does it hold when full?

- $30\,\mathrm{L}$
- $3\,\mathrm{L}$
- $300\,\mathrm{L}$
- $30000\,\mathrm{L}$
- $12\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The internal volume is $40\cdot30\cdot25=30000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Since one liter contains $1000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, the capacity is $\dfrac{30000}{1000}=30\,\mathrm{L}$. All dimensions are internal, so wall thickness need not be subtracted. Dividing only by $100$ would apply a length conversion to a volume.
