Which point satisfies $x\ge-2$, $y<4$, and $x+y=1$ simultaneously?

- $(-3,4)$
- $(0,4)$
- $(2,1)$
- $(-2,4)$
- $(-2,3)$

## Explanation

For $(-2,3)$, the first condition holds with equality, the second holds because $3<4$, and the third gives $-2+3=1$. Thus the point belongs to the intersection of all three conditions. Every other option violates at least one requirement: a coordinate outside a half-plane or a sum different from $1$ excludes it.
