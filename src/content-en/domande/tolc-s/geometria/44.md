What is the area of the part of the disk $x^2+y^2\le16$ in the first quadrant, including the axes?

- $16\pi$
- $8\pi$
- $2\pi$
- $4\pi$
- $4$

## Explanation

The disk is centered at the origin and has radius $4$. The two axes divide it into four congruent sectors, each with central angle $90^\circ$. The required part therefore has area $\dfrac14\pi\cdot4^2=4\pi$. Including the axes adds only boundary segments and does not change the region’s area.
