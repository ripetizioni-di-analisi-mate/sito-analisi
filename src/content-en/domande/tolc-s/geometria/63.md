For which value of $k$ do the lines $2x-y=3$ and $4x-2y=k$ coincide?

- $3$
- $0$
- $6$
- $-6$
- $\dfrac32$

## Explanation

Multiplying the entire first equation by $2$ gives $4x-2y=6$. To describe the same points, the second equation must therefore have $k=6$. The variable coefficients are already proportional; if the constant term did not have the same ratio, the lines would be distinct and parallel.
