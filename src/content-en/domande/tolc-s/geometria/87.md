The region $x\ge0$, $y\ge0$, $x+y\le6$ is a triangle. What is its perimeter?

- $18$
- $12+6\sqrt2$
- $6+6\sqrt2$
- $12$
- $18\sqrt2$

## Explanation

The vertices are the origin and the intercepts of $x+y=6$, namely $(6,0)$ and $(0,6)$. Both legs are $6$, and the hypotenuse is $\sqrt{6^2+6^2}=6\sqrt2$. Adding the three sides gives $12+6\sqrt2$. The inequality selects the triangle’s interior together with its boundary.
