Which line is the locus of points equidistant from $A=(1,2)$ and $B=(5,2)$?

- $y=2$
- $x=2$
- $x=3$
- $y=3$
- $x+y=5$

## Explanation

Segment $AB$ is horizontal and has midpoint $M=(3,2)$. Its perpendicular bisector is therefore the vertical line through $M$, namely $x=3$. Every point on this line has horizontal offsets from $A$ and $B$ of equal absolute value and a common vertical offset, so its distances to them agree.
