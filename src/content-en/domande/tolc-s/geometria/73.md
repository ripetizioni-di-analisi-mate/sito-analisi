Point $P=(4,-3)$ is rotated through $180^\circ$ about $C=(1,-1)$. What are the image coordinates?

- $(-4,3)$
- $(2,-1)$
- $(-2,1)$
- $(-3,2)$
- $(6,-5)$

## Explanation

In a $180^\circ$ rotation, the center is the midpoint between a point and its image. Thus $P_1=2C-P=(2-4,-2-(-3))=(-2,1)$. The transformation $(-x,-y)$ would apply to rotation about the origin; this different center requires considering displacement from $C$.
