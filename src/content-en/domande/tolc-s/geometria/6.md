What is the slope of a line perpendicular to $y=2x+1$?

- $-\dfrac12$
- $2$
- $-2$
- $\dfrac12$
- $0$

## Explanation

The given line has slope $2$. For two perpendicular nonvertical lines, the product of their slopes is $-1$, so $2m=-1$ and $m=-\dfrac12$. The constant term $1$ determines the line’s position but not its direction. Merely changing the sign of the slope would not satisfy the condition.
