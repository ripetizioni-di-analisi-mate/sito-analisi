One vertex of a triangle is moved $6$ units right while the other two stay fixed. How does the centroid change?

- It moves 6 units to the right
- It stays fixed
- It moves 3 units to the right
- It moves 2 units to the right
- It moves 2 units upward

## Explanation

The centroid’s horizontal coordinate is the mean of the three vertex coordinates. Increasing just one of them by $6$ increases the sum by $6$ and the mean by $\dfrac63=2$. The vertices’ vertical coordinates do not change, so neither does the centroid’s. Its displacement is therefore $2$ units right.
