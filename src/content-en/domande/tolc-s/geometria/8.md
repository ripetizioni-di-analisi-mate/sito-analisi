Point $P=(-2,5)$ is rotated $90^\circ$ counterclockwise about the origin. What is the image?

- $(5,2)$
- $(2,-5)$
- $(-5,-2)$
- $(5,-2)$
- $(-2,-5)$

## Explanation

A counterclockwise $90^\circ$ rotation sends the horizontal unit direction to the vertical direction and the vertical direction to the negative horizontal direction. Thus the coordinate rule is $(x,y)\mapsto(-y,x)$. Applied to $(-2,5)$, it gives $(-5,-2)$. The distance from the origin remains $\sqrt{29}$, as a rotation preserves lengths.
