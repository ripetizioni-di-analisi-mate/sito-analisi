Which real set is described by $(x-1)^2+(y+2)^2=-4$?

- The empty set
- A circle of radius 2
- Only the point (1, -2)
- A line
- The entire plane

## Explanation

For real coordinates, each square is nonnegative, so their sum is at least zero. It therefore cannot equal $-4$. The equation has no real points and describes the empty set. A right side of zero would give only the center; a circle of radius $2$ would require a right side of $4$.
