Two similar triangles have corresponding side lengths in the ratio $3$. What is their area ratio, in the same order?

- $3$
- $6$
- $27$
- $9$
- $\sqrt3$

## Explanation

In the formula $A=\dfrac{bh}{2}$, both base and height are multiplied by the similarity factor $3$. The new area is therefore $\dfrac{(3b)(3h)}{2}=9A$. The area ratio is the square of the length ratio; the cube $27$ would instead concern volumes of similar solids.
