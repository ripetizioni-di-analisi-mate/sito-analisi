A translation sends $A=(-1,4)$ to $A_1=(3,2)$. Where does it send $B=(2,-3)$?

- $(6,-5)$
- $(4,-1)$
- $(-2,-1)$
- $(6,-1)$
- $(3,2)$

## Explanation

The translation vector is the final point minus the initial point: $A_1-A=(4,-2)$. The same vector applies to every point, so $B_1=B+(4,-2)=(6,-5)$. The transformation preserves the direction and length of every segment; in particular, vector $A_1B_1$ equals $AB$.
