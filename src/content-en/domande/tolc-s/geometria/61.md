What is the distance between the parallel lines $3x+4y-5=0$ and $3x+4y+10=0$?

- $3$
- $15$
- $5$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac{15}{7}$

## Explanation

The equations have the same coefficients of $x$ and $y$. The distance is the absolute difference of their constant terms divided by the normal vector’s length: $d=\dfrac{|10-(-5)|}{\sqrt{3^2+4^2}}=\dfrac{15}{5}=3$. Using only the difference $15$ would omit normalization of the perpendicular direction.
