A circle centered at $(2,-3)$ with radius $5$ is translated $4$ units right and then reflected in the $x$-axis. What is its final center?

- $(6,3)$
- $(6,-3)$
- $(-6,3)$
- $(2,7)$
- $(-2,3)$

## Explanation

Translation to the right adds $4$ to the horizontal coordinate and leaves the vertical coordinate unchanged, so the center becomes $(6,-3)$. Reflection in the $x$-axis then changes the sign of the vertical coordinate, giving $(6,3)$. Both transformations preserve distances, so the radius remains $5$ and does not enter the center calculation.
