What is the locus of points equidistant from the parallel lines $y=-2$ and $y=4$?

- $y=2$
- $x=1$
- $y=0$
- $y=6$
- $y=1$

## Explanation

The distances from $(x,y)$ to the two lines are $|y+2|$ and $|y-4|$. Equating their squares gives $(y+2)^2=(y-4)^2$, hence $12y=12$ and $y=1$. The horizontal coordinate remains free. The locus is therefore the intermediate parallel line, at distance $3$ from each given line.
