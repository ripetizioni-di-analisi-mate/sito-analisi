Which point always lies inside every nondegenerate triangle and is equidistant from the lines containing its three sides?

- The incenter
- The centroid
- The circumcenter
- The orthocenter
- The midpoint of the longest side

## Explanation

A point on an internal angle bisector is equidistant from the lines forming the angle. The intersection of the three internal bisectors is therefore equidistant from the triangle’s three side lines and lies inside it. This is the incenter, the center of the incircle. The circumcenter is instead defined by equal distances from the vertices.
