A floor is tiled by congruent regular hexagons without gaps or overlaps. How many hexagons meet at each interior vertex?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

Each interior angle of a regular hexagon measures $\dfrac{(6-2)180^\circ}{6}=120^\circ$. The full turn around an interior vertex must be $360^\circ$. Therefore $360:120=3$ hexagons are needed. Tiling without gaps constrains the angle sum, rather than the polygon’s number of sides.
