In a disk of radius $4\,\mathrm{cm}$, two perpendicular radii bound a sector. Remove the triangle formed by the radii and the chord joining their endpoints. What area remains?

- $(4\pi-8)\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $(4\pi-4)\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $(8\pi-8)\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $4\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $(16\pi-8)\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The sector is a quarter disk and has area $\dfrac14\pi\cdot4^2=4\pi$. The removed triangle is right-angled with both legs equal to the radius, so its area is $\dfrac{4\cdot4}{2}=8$. The remaining area is $(4\pi-8)\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, the difference between the sector and its inscribed triangle.
