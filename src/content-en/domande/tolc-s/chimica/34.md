During hydrocarbon combustion, which product may become more abundant when available oxygen is insufficient for complete combustion?

- Molecular oxygen produced from carbon
- Hydrogen chloride in the absence of chlorine
- Molecular nitrogen produced from hydrogen
- Carbon monoxide
- Sodium metal

## Explanation

In complete combustion, carbon forms carbon dioxide. With less oxygen, CO may form, in which carbon is less oxidized, and solid carbon may also appear. The other proposed products would require absent elements or an interpretation incompatible with the reaction. Element conservation still applies when combustion is incomplete.
