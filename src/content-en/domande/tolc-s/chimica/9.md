Which description correctly represents pure solid sodium chloride?

- Isolated NaCl molecules joined by hydrogen bonds
- Neutral sodium and chlorine atoms with no interactions
- A lattice containing only electrons
- A lattice of sodium and chloride ions
- A mixture of sodium metal and chlorine gas

## Explanation

The formula $\mathrm{NaCl}$ states the one-to-one ratio between $\mathrm{Na^+}$ and $\mathrm{Cl^-}$ in the lattice. Electrostatic attraction acts among many neighboring ions, so the crystal is not made of isolated molecules. Forming the compound produces properties different from those of the two starting elemental substances.
