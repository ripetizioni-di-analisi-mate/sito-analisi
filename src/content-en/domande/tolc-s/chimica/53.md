For the same amount of substance, which interpretation generally describes the entropy increase associated with going from liquid to gas?

- Atomic number increases
- Molecules necessarily lose all electrons
- The accessible microscopic configurations of particles increase
- Mass vanishes
- Internal energy must always be zero

## Explanation

In a gas, particles can occupy a much larger volume than in a liquid and access more configurations consistent with the macroscopic state. This generally corresponds to greater entropy. Entropy is neither proton count nor a measure of lost mass. Chemical composition can remain unchanged during the phase change.
