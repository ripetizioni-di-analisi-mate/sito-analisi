Why can an aqueous electrolyte solution conduct current?

- All water molecules become metals
- Nuclear protons move freely through the solution
- The solute contains no electrons
- Only neutral particles carry current
- It contains mobile positively and negatively charged ions

## Explanation

An electric field moves cations and anions in opposite directions, transferring charge. The solution can remain globally neutral because positive and negative charges balance overall. Conductivity requires mobile carriers, whereas the presence of dissolved neutral molecules does not guarantee the same current transport.
