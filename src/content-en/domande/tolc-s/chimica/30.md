An atom loses an electron without changing its nucleus. Which property certainly remains unchanged?

- Its total electric charge
- Its number of electrons
- Its electrical neutrality
- Its outer-electron distribution
- Its atomic number

## Explanation

Atomic number counts protons, which remain in the nucleus and do not participate in electron loss. The atom therefore keeps its element identity while becoming a cation. Electron count, charge and electron distribution change. A change in proton number would instead be a nuclear transformation.
