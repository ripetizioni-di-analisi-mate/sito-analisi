In the change $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}\to Fe^{3+}+e^-}$, what happens to iron?

- It is reduced by gaining an electron
- It is oxidized by losing an electron
- It is oxidized by gaining a proton
- It is reduced by losing a proton
- It remains in the same oxidation state

## Explanation

The electron is among the products, so it is lost by the initial species. Loss of electrons is oxidation, and the oxidation number changes from $+2$ to $+3$. The nucleus remains an iron nucleus: the process requires neither gain nor loss of protons. Total charge is also conserved because $+3-1=+2$.
