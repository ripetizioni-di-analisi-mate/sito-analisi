A sufficiently dilute solution has $[\mathrm{H_3O^+}]=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{mol/L}$. What is its approximate pH?

- $3$
- $-3$
- $11$
- $7$
- $10^{-3}$

## Explanation

In the dilute model, pH is minus the decimal logarithm of the numerical concentration expressed in $\mathrm{mol/L}$. Since $\log_{10}(10^{-3})=-3$, pH is $-(-3)=3$. pH is dimensionless; $10^{-3}$ represents the numerical concentration, not its negative logarithm.
