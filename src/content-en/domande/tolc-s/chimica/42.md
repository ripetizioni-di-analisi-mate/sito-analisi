An element has isotopes with approximate masses $20\,\mathrm u$ and $22\,\mathrm u$, with abundances $75\%$ and $25\%$. What is the approximate average mass?

- $21\,\mathrm u$
- $20.5\,\mathrm u$
- $21.5\,\mathrm u$
- $42\,\mathrm u$
- $22\,\mathrm u$

## Explanation

Abundances weight the contributions to the mean: $20\cdot0.75+22\cdot0.25=15+5.5=20.5\,\mathrm u$. The simple mean $21\,\mathrm u$ would assume equal abundances. The correct result lies between the masses and is closer to that of the isotope present at seventy-five percent.
