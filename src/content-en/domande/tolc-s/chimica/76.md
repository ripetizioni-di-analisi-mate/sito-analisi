An ideal gas in a rigid container goes from $300\,\mathrm K$ to $450\,\mathrm K$ without gas loss. By what factor does pressure change?

- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac23$
- $150$
- $\dfrac12$
- $2$

## Explanation

From $pV=nRT$, with $n$ and $V$ constant, pressure is proportional to absolute temperature. Therefore $\dfrac{p_2}{p_1}=\dfrac{450}{300}=\dfrac32$. Temperature is already in kelvin, so the ratio applies directly. A ratio of Celsius temperatures would not have the same physical meaning.
