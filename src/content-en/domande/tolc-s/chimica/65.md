To balance $\mathrm{H_2+O_2\to H_2O}$, a student replaces the product with $\mathrm{H_2O_2}$. Why does this change fail to solve the assigned problem?

- The subscript two is forbidden in all formulas
- Hydrogen atom count can never be conserved
- A reaction may have only one reactant
- Oxygen cannot form compounds
- It changes the product's identity instead of only coefficients

## Explanation

$\mathrm{H_2O}$ and $\mathrm{H_2O_2}$ are different substances, water and hydrogen peroxide. Balancing must retain the assigned species and change how many of each participate. For water, the result is $2\mathrm{H_2}+\mathrm{O_2}\to2\mathrm{H_2O}$. A different formula may conserve atoms in another equation but describes another transformation.
