Which description is correct for a buffer containing appreciable amounts of a weak acid and its conjugate base?

- It always maintains pH $7$
- It limits pH changes from small acid or base additions within a finite capacity
- It neutralizes unlimited acid amounts
- It prevents every chemical reaction
- It necessarily contains no ions

## Explanation

The conjugate base can consume a small acid addition; the weak acid can consume a small base addition. Available amounts are finite, so buffering has a limit. pH depends on the pair and component proportions; it need not be seven and does not remain perfectly constant after every addition.
