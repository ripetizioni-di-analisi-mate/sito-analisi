Which arrangement of two electrons in the same orbital obeys the Pauli principle?

- Parallel spins
- Opposite spins
- Three electrons with different spins
- Positively charged electrons
- Two protons instead of electrons

## Explanation

Two electrons in the same orbital share their spatial quantum numbers. To avoid having all four quantum numbers identical, they must differ in spin quantum number. An orbital therefore holds at most two electrons with opposite spins. The principle limits electron occupancy; it does not change particle charge or identity.
