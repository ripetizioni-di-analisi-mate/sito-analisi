A volume is reported as $(10.0\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{mL}$. What is the percentage relative uncertainty with respect to the central value?

- $0.2\%$
- $20\%$
- $5\%$
- $50\%$
- $2\%$

## Explanation

The absolute uncertainty is $0.2\,\mathrm{mL}$. Divide it by the central value and express the ratio as a percentage: $\dfrac{0.2}{10.0}\cdot100\%=2\%$. Volume units cancel in the ratio. The $0.2$ after the plus-or-minus sign is an absolute uncertainty, not an already calculated percentage.
