A hydrocarbon has formula $\mathrm{C_4H_8}$. Which conclusion is justified by the formula alone?

- It is certainly a saturated acyclic alkane
- It certainly contains a triple bond
- It may be an acyclic alkene or a cycloalkane
- It is certainly aromatic
- It necessarily contains oxygen

## Explanation

The formula $\mathrm{C_nH_{2n}}$ is compatible with either an open chain containing one double bond or a saturated structure containing one ring. A molecular formula counts atoms but does not specify connectivity. An acyclic alkane with four carbons would instead have ten hydrogens, namely $\mathrm{C_4H_{10}}$.
