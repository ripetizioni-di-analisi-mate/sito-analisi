What is the empirical formula of the neutral compound formed by $\mathrm{Al^{3+}}$ and $\mathrm{O^{2-}}$?

- $\mathrm{Al_3O_2}$
- $\mathrm{Al_2O_3}$
- $\mathrm{AlO}$
- $\mathrm{AlO_3}$
- $\mathrm{Al_2O}$

## Explanation

Neutrality requires equal magnitudes of positive and negative charge. The smallest common total is six: two aluminum ions give $2\cdot3=6$ and three oxygen ions give $3(-2)=-6$. Thus the smallest ratio is $2:3$. Subscripts are not simply the charges written on the same element.
