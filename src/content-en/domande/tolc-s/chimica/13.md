In $\mathrm{Zn+Cu^{2+}\to Zn^{2+}+Cu}$, which initial species is the oxidizing agent?

- $\mathrm{Zn}$
- $\mathrm{Cu}$
- $\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}$
- $\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}$
- The solvent alone, regardless of the reaction

## Explanation

Copper(II) gains two electrons and becomes metallic copper. The species gaining electrons is the oxidizing agent and undergoes reduction. Zinc performs the complementary process: it loses electrons and is oxidized. The agent's name describes its effect on the other species, not the process it undergoes itself.
