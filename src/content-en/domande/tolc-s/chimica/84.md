In water, ammonium reacts according to $\mathrm{NH_4^++H_2O\rightleftharpoons NH_3+H_3O^+}$. Which effect does this reaction describe?

- A tendency to produce hydroxide directly as the only ionic product
- Reduction of nitrogen to metallic nitrogen
- Removal of all ions
- A tendency to acidify the solution
- Mandatory formation of a neutral solution

## Explanation

Ammonium donates a proton to water, generating hydronium. This contribution increases solution acidity. An initial solute being a salt therefore does not guarantee neutral pH: some ions react with water. The equation is proton transfer without a change in nitrogen oxidation number.
