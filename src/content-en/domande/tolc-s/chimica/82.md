The forward reaction of an equilibrium is exothermic. On raising temperature, with other conditions equal, which tendency is observed?

- The exothermic forward direction is always favored
- The endothermic reverse direction is favored
- All reactions cease
- The constant necessarily stays identical
- Direction depends only on the catalyst

## Explanation

Raising temperature favors the direction that absorbs heat. If the forward direction releases heat, the reverse is endothermic and is favored, shifting toward the forward reaction's reactants. Temperature also changes the equilibrium constant here. A catalyst instead does not select a new equilibrium at the same temperature.
