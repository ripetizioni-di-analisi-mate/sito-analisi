For $\mathrm{N_2O_4(g)\rightleftharpoons2NO_2(g)}$ at equilibrium, volume is reduced at constant temperature. Which direction is favored during readjustment?

- Toward $\mathrm{N_2O_4}$
- Toward $\mathrm{NO_2}$
- Neither, because pressure does not change
- Toward the side with more total atoms
- The equilibrium constant must double

## Explanation

Compression initially increases all concentrations. In $Q_c=\dfrac{[\mathrm{NO_2}]^2}{[\mathrm{N_2O_4}]}$, the numerator grows with the square of the factor and the denominator with the first power, so $Q_c$ exceeds $K_c$. More $\mathrm{N_2O_4}$ forms, the side with fewer gas moles. At constant temperature, $K_c$ remains unchanged.
