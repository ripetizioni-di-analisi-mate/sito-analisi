Which condition characterizes dynamic chemical equilibrium in a closed system at constant temperature?

- All species have equal concentrations
- All molecules stop reacting
- Every reactant is completely exhausted
- The reverse rate is zero and the forward rate positive
- Forward and reverse rates are equal

## Explanation

Both changes continue microscopically but produce opposite effects of equal magnitude. Concentrations therefore remain constant over time, even though they may differ greatly among species. Equilibrium is dynamic precisely because reactions need not stop. Equality concerns rates, not the amounts present.
