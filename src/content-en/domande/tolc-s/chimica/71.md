For an electron with principal quantum number $n=2$, which value of quantum number $l$ is allowed?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $-1$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The subshell quantum number is an integer satisfying $0\leq l\leq n-1$. With $n=2$, only $l=0$ and $l=1$ are possible. Only one of these appears among the choices. The value $\dfrac12$ concerns the electron spin quantum number, not $l$.
