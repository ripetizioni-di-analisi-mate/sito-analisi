An effervescent tablet reacts in an open beaker, and the mass of the beaker and contents decreases. Which explanation is consistent with conservation of mass?

- Some matter leaves as gas
- Some atoms are destroyed by the reaction
- Neutrons become massless
- All water turns into nuclear energy
- The produced gas has no mass

## Explanation

The weighed system is open and loses gas that disperses into the surroundings. The remaining material's mass decreases, while total mass, including escaped gas, is conserved. The law applies to the balance of all matter involved. Gas has mass even if it does not remain visible as a separate phase in the beaker.
