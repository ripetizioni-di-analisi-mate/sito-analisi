Which formula can belong to an acyclic alkyne with four carbons and one triple bond, with no other unsaturation?

- $\mathrm{C_4H_{10}}$
- $\mathrm{C_4H_8}$
- $\mathrm{C_4H_6}$
- $\mathrm{C_4H_{12}}$
- $\mathrm{C_4H_4}$

## Explanation

For an open chain with one triple bond, the general formula is $\mathrm{C_nH_{2n-2}}$. With $n=4$, there are $2\cdot4-2=6$ hydrogens. Specifying an open chain, one triple bond and no other unsaturation excludes additional rings or multiple bonds that would change the count. The formula alone does not distinguish possible isomers.
