An exothermic reaction occurs at constant pressure with expansion work only. What is the sign of $\Delta H$ for the reacting system?

- Negative, because the system releases heat
- Positive, because the system releases heat
- Always zero
- It depends only on atom count
- It always matches the sign of electric charge

## Explanation

Under the stated conditions, heat exchanged at constant pressure equals the system's enthalpy change. An exothermic reaction transfers heat to the surroundings, so for the system $q_p=\Delta H<0$. The sign refers to the reacting system; the surroundings instead receive the released heat.
