Which formula represents methane, the alkane with one carbon atom?

- $\mathrm{CO_2}$
- $\mathrm{C_2H_6}$
- $\mathrm{CH_4}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3OH}$
- $\mathrm{C_2H_4}$

## Explanation

A saturated acyclic alkane has formula $\mathrm{C_nH_{2n+2}}$. Setting $n=1$ gives $\mathrm{CH_4}$, with four single carbon–hydrogen bonds. $\mathrm{CH_3OH}$ contains oxygen and is an alcohol; $\mathrm{C_2H_6}$ is an alkane but has two carbon atoms. Both the class and the carbon count must be satisfied.
