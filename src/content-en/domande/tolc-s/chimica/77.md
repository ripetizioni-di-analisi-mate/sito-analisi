An ideal mixture contains one mole of gas A and three moles of gas B at a total pressure of $8\,\mathrm{bar}$. What is A's partial pressure?

- $6\,\mathrm{bar}$
- $2\,\mathrm{bar}$
- $8\,\mathrm{bar}$
- $\dfrac83\,\mathrm{bar}$
- $32\,\mathrm{bar}$

## Explanation

A's mole fraction is $x_A=\dfrac{1}{1+3}=\dfrac14$. Dalton's law gives $p_A=x_Ap=\dfrac14\cdot8=2\,\mathrm{bar}$. The remaining partial pressure is six bar, and the sum returns eight bar. The mole-fraction denominator includes all moles present, not only those of the other component.
