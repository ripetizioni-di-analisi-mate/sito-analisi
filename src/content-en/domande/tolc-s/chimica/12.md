Which procedure separates insoluble sand grains from water without requiring a chemical transformation?

- Neutralization
- Filtration with suitable pores
- Complete dissolution of the sand
- Electrolysis of water
- Addition of more solvent without separation

## Explanation

The grains are large enough to be retained by the filter while water passes through its pores. This uses a physical difference and preserves the substances. An ordinary filter would not separate an already dissolved salt because its ions pass with the solvent. The choice depends on the component's state.
