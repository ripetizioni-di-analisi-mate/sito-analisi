A system absorbs $80\,\mathrm J$ of heat and does $30\,\mathrm J$ of work on its surroundings. Using $\Delta U=q+w$, where $w$ is work on the system, what is $\Delta U$?

- $+110\,\mathrm J$
- $-50\,\mathrm J$
- $+50\,\mathrm J$
- $-110\,\mathrm J$
- $+30\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Heat enters the system, so $q=+80\,\mathrm J$. Work is done by the system on the surroundings, so work on the system is $w=-30\,\mathrm J$. The balance gives $\Delta U=80-30=50\,\mathrm J$. The sign convention is explicit because changing the definition of $w$ changes the equation's form.
