A commercial product states concentration, a hazard pictogram and directions for use. Which statement is correct?

- The pictogram automatically determines molarity
- Concentration makes instructions unnecessary
- They provide complementary information and the pictogram alone does not specify a safe dose
- Any household product may be mixed with others
- The same liquid color guarantees the same risk

## Explanation

Concentration describes quantitative composition; the pictogram identifies a hazard category; directions state conditions of use and precautions. None of these alone replaces the others. In particular, a symbol does not define a universally safe dose, and mixing products requires explicit compatible manufacturer instructions.
