To prepare $250\,\mathrm{mL}$ of solution at a specified concentration, which volume must be set after dissolving the solute?

- The initial volume of solvent alone
- The solid solute's volume
- The sum of separately measured volumes even if mixing causes contraction
- The volume of evaporated solvent
- The final volume of the whole solution

## Explanation

Molarity uses moles of solute divided by the resulting solution volume. After dissolution, the liquid is therefore taken to the specified final volume. Measuring $250\,\mathrm{mL}$ of solvent first does not guarantee that the solution has that volume, because dissolution may change it. The procedure must match the quantity defined in the denominator.
