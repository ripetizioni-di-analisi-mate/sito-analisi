An indicator is yellow below pH $4$ and blue only above pH $6$, with intermediate colors between them. A solution appears blue. Which conclusion is justified?

- Its pH is above $6$, without establishing an exact value
- Its pH is exactly $7$
- Its pH is exactly $14$
- The solution is certainly basic at every temperature
- Its hydronium concentration is zero

## Explanation

Blue identifies only the region above the stated transition range. Values such as $6.5$, $7$ or $9$ may give the same color under the described conditions. The indicator does not measure exact pH, and blue in this example does not even guarantee pH above seven. The information depends on the specified thresholds.
