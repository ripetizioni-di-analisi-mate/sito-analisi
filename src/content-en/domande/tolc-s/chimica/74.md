In the Lewis structure of $\mathrm{NH_3}$, nitrogen has three bonds and one lone pair. What is the geometry of the nuclei according to VSEPR?

- Trigonal planar
- Linear
- Square planar
- Trigonal pyramidal
- Octahedral

## Explanation

The four electron regions adopt an approximately tetrahedral arrangement. One region is a lone pair, however, so only three positions contain hydrogen nuclei. The nuclear geometry is trigonal pyramidal. Confusing electron geometry with molecular geometry would treat a position without a nucleus as a molecular vertex.
