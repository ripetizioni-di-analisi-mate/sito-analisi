Given $E^\circ(\mathrm{Cu^{2+}/Cu})=+0.34\,\mathrm V$ and $E^\circ(\mathrm{Zn^{2+}/Zn})=-0.76\,\mathrm V$, what is $E^\circ_{\mathrm{cell}}$ for $\mathrm{Zn+Cu^{2+}\to Zn^{2+}+Cu}$, using cathode reduction potential minus anode reduction potential?

- $-1.10\,\mathrm V$
- $+1.10\,\mathrm V$
- $-0.42\,\mathrm V$
- $+0.42\,\mathrm V$
- $+2.20\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

Copper(II) is reduced at the cathode, while zinc is oxidized at the anode. Using both potentials written as reductions, $E^\circ_{\mathrm{cell}}=0.34-(-0.76)=1.10\,\mathrm V$. The positive sign indicates the thermodynamically favored direction under standard conditions. Potential is not multiplied by the number of transferred electrons.
