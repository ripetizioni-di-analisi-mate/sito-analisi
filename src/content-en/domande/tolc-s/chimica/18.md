Which compound has the functional group of an alcohol?

- $\mathrm{CH_3CHO}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3COCH_3}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2OH}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3COOH}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3OCH_3}$

## Explanation

Ethanol has an OH group attached to a saturated carbon, identifying the alcohol function. Ethanoic acid's COOH group also contains OH, but connects it to a carbonyl and defines a different function. Classifying a compound therefore requires reading local connections rather than merely finding one symbol in the formula.
