A compound contains $12\,\mathrm g$ of carbon and $4\,\mathrm g$ of hydrogen. With atomic molar masses $12$ and $1\,\mathrm{g/mol}$, what is the empirical formula?

- $\mathrm{CH_4}$
- $\mathrm{C_3H}$
- $\mathrm{C_4H}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3}$
- $\mathrm{C_{12}H_4}$

## Explanation

Formulas express atom ratios, so masses must be converted into moles. Carbon is $\dfrac{12}{12}=1\,\mathrm{mol}$ and hydrogen is $\dfrac41=4\,\mathrm{mol}$. The atomic ratio is $1:4$, already in smallest integers, giving $\mathrm{CH_4}$. Directly using $12:4$ would confuse mass and atomic ratios.
