Which substance consists of molecules containing only one chemical element?

- Water $\mathrm{H_2O}$
- Carbon dioxide $\mathrm{CO_2}$
- Oxygen $\mathrm{O_2}$
- Ammonia $\mathrm{NH_3}$
- Sodium chloride $\mathrm{NaCl}$

## Explanation

An oxygen molecule contains two atoms, but both belong to the same element. Water, carbon dioxide and ammonia contain different elements. Sodium chloride is also an ionic lattice rather than a substance made of isolated molecules. Element count comes from the distinct symbols in the formula.
