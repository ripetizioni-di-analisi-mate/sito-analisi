Ethanol $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2OH}$ and dimethyl ether $\mathrm{CH_3OCH_3}$ both have formula $\mathrm{C_2H_6O}$. Which relationship describes them?

- They are isotopes
- They are the same rotated compound
- They are oppositely charged ions
- They are structural isomers
- They differ only in neutron number

## Explanation

The formulas count the same atoms but connect them differently. In ethanol, oxygen is bonded to one carbon and one hydrogen; in dimethyl ether it is bonded to two carbons. Different connectivity defines structural isomerism and changes the functional group. Rotating the whole molecule instead preserves every connection.
