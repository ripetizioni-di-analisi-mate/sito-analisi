Which component is most abundant by molecular count in dry air under usual conditions near ground level?

- Molecular oxygen
- Carbon dioxide
- Molecular hydrogen
- Ozone
- Molecular nitrogen

## Explanation

Dry air contains approximately $78\%$ nitrogen and $21\%$ oxygen by volume. In the ideal-gas model, under the same conditions, volume fractions equal mole fractions. Therefore $\mathrm{N_2}$ predominates. Air remains a mixture: a dominant component does not make it a pure substance.
