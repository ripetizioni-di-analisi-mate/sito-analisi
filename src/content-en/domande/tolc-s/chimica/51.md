What is the general trend in atomic radius from sodium to chlorine across the third period?

- It tends to decrease
- It tends to increase because a new shell is added at every element
- It stays identical for all elements
- It doubles at each step
- It becomes zero for chlorine

## Explanation

Across a period, outer electrons are added to the same principal shell while nuclear charge increases. Effective attraction on outer electrons therefore tends to increase and radius to decrease. Adding a new principal shell instead characterizes moving to a new period. This is a general trend.
