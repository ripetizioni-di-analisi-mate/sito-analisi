Which change involving water is physical?

- Melting of ice
- Electrolysis of water
- Reaction with sodium metal
- Decomposition into hydrogen and oxygen
- Formation of water from hydrogen and oxygen

## Explanation

Ice melts by changing the arrangement of molecules, which remain $\mathrm{H_2O}$. The substance's composition is preserved, so the change is physical. The other alternatives describe reactions that produce or consume water. A change of physical state must be distinguished from a change in substances' chemical identity.
