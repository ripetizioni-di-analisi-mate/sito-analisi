A label states ethanol at $12\%$ by volume. Which interpretation is correct according to the definition of volume percentage?

- $12\,\mathrm{mL}$ of ethanol per $100\,\mathrm{mL}$ of solution at reference conditions
- $12\,\mathrm g$ of ethanol per $100\,\mathrm g$ of solution
- $12\,\mathrm{mol}$ per liter
- $12\,\mathrm{mL}$ of ethanol per $100\,\mathrm{mL}$ of water alone
- Exactly $12\%$ of molecules are certainly ethanol

## Explanation

Volume percentage compares a component volume with final solution volume under the defined measurement conditions. It is not automatically a mass or mole percentage. Mixing ethanol and water may also change total volume: one hundred milliliters of solution does not mean one hundred initial milliliters of water.
