Two electrons occupy an initially empty p subshell in the ground state. Which arrangement follows Hund's rule?

- The same orbital with parallel spins
- The same orbital before occupying the others
- Two different orbitals with parallel spins
- Necessarily one s orbital and one p orbital
- Three orbitals with one electron each

## Explanation

A p subshell contains three degenerate orbitals. Electrons initially occupy them separately with parallel spins before pairing. With two electrons, two different orbitals are therefore occupied and one remains empty. Pairing both electrons immediately would not describe the ground-state distribution under Hund's rule.
