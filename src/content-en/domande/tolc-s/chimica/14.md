At constant temperature, a catalyst speeds up a reversible reaction. Which explanation is correct?

- It necessarily increases the equilibrium constant
- It changes the total mass of the elements
- It always makes the enthalpy change negative
- It provides a pathway with a lower activation barrier in both directions
- It completely prevents the reverse reaction

## Explanation

A catalyst changes the kinetic pathway and allows equilibrium to be reached more quickly. Both forward and reverse reactions are accelerated. Initial and final thermodynamic states remain the same, so the equilibrium constant at the given temperature is unchanged. A greater rate does not imply a greater equilibrium yield.
