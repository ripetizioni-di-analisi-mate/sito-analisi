A dilute solution of a strong monoprotic acid has pH $2$. Its volume is increased tenfold with water. Neglecting water autoionization, what pH is obtained?

- $20$
- $3$
- $0.2$
- $1$
- $7$

## Explanation

The initial pH corresponds to $10^{-2}\,\mathrm{mol/L}$ hydronium. Volume increases tenfold at constant amount, so concentration becomes $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{mol/L}$ and pH is $3$. A tenfold dilution raises pH by one unit under these conditions, rather than multiplying the pH value by ten.
