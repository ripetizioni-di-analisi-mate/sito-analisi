Two dilute solutions have pH values $3$ and $5$. How many times greater is hydronium concentration in the first?

- $2$
- $10$
- $\dfrac53$
- $100$
- $1000$

## Explanation

The concentrations are approximately $10^{-3}$ and $10^{-5}\,\mathrm{mol/L}$. Their ratio is $\dfrac{10^{-3}}{10^{-5}}=10^2=100$. A difference of two pH units corresponds to two factors of ten. Lower pH identifies higher hydronium concentration because the definition includes a minus sign.
