In ethyne, $\mathrm{HC\equiv CH}$, each carbon has two bonding regions and linear geometry. Which hybridization describes this model?

- $sp^2$
- $sp^3$
- $sp^3d$
- $sp^3d^2$
- $sp$

## Explanation

The two bonding regions correspond, in the hybridization model, to two linearly oriented $sp$ orbitals. The triple bond counts as one region for geometry despite containing several bonding components. The $sp^2$ and $sp^3$ configurations describe three and four regions, respectively, with different geometries.
