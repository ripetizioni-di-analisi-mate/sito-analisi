Which pair of neutral atoms represents two isotopes of the same element? Each number pair states protons and neutrons.

- $(6,6)$ and $(8,6)$
- $(6,6)$ and $(6,8)$
- $(6,8)$ and $(7,7)$
- $(6,6)$ and $(7,6)$
- $(8,8)$ and $(7,9)$

## Explanation

Element identity is fixed by the protons. The two atoms with six protons are both carbon; with six and eight neutrons, their mass numbers are $12$ and $14$. In the other pairs the proton number changes, so the element changes, even where the sum of protons and neutrons is the same.
