At a temperature other than $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, which condition defines a neutral aqueous solution?

- pH must be exactly $7$ at every temperature
- Hydronium ions are completely absent
- $[\mathrm{H_3O^+}]=[\mathrm{OH^-}]$
- Hydroxide ions are completely absent
- The solution contains no charged particles

## Explanation

Neutrality requires equal hydronium and hydroxide concentrations. Water's ion product depends on temperature, so the common concentration and neutral pH may change. A value of about seven is associated with $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ in the dilute model; it is not the general definition of neutrality.
