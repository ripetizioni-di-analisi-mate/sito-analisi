Which emission can contribute to acid rain by becoming acidic species in the atmosphere?

- Helium
- Argon
- Molecular nitrogen treated as inert under the stated conditions
- Sulfur dioxide
- Pure water vapor as the only emitted substance

## Explanation

Sulfur dioxide can oxidize and form acidic species in the presence of water, contributing to precipitation acidification. The listed noble gases do not undergo analogous changes under usual atmospheric conditions. This mechanism differs from the absorption of infrared radiation characteristic of greenhouse gases.
