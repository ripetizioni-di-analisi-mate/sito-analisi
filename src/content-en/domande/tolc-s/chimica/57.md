In $\mathrm{CH_3COCH_3}$, the carbon of the C=O group is bonded to two carbons. Which class does the compound belong to?

- Aldehydes
- Ketones
- Alcohols
- Ethers
- Carboxylic acids

## Explanation

A carbonyl between two carbon groups characterizes a ketone; here the compound is propanone. In an aldehyde the carbonyl is bonded to hydrogen, whereas in a carboxylic acid it is also bonded to OH. The function is identified from the carbonyl carbon's bonds, not oxygen's presence alone.
