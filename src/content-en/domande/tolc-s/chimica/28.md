What is the molar mass of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ using $M(\mathrm C)=12\,\mathrm{g/mol}$ and $M(\mathrm O)=16\,\mathrm{g/mol}$?

- $28\,\mathrm{g/mol}$
- $32\,\mathrm{g/mol}$
- $44\,\mathrm{g/mol}$
- $56\,\mathrm{g/mol}$
- $192\,\mathrm{g/mol}$

## Explanation

One mole of molecules contains one mole of carbon atoms and two moles of oxygen atoms. The total mass is therefore $12+2\cdot16=44\,\mathrm{g/mol}$. The value $28$ would count only one oxygen and would correspond to carbon monoxide. Subscripts multiply the mass contribution of their element.
