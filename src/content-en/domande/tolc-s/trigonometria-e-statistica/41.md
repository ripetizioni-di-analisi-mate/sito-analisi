A triangle has side lengths $6,8,11$. How is its largest angle classified?

- Obtuse
- Right
- Acute
- Straight
- The sides do not determine this

## Explanation

The largest angle is opposite the side of length $11$. By the cosine rule, its cosine has the sign of $6^2+8^2-11^2=100-121=-21$. The denominator $2\cdot6\cdot8$ is positive, so the cosine is negative. An interior angle with negative cosine is obtuse.
