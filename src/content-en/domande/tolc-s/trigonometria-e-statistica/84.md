For $10$ observations, $\sum x_i=30$ and $\sum x_i^2=110$. What is the descriptive variance using divisor $10$?

- $11$
- $8$
- $9$
- $2$
- $\dfrac29$

## Explanation

The mean is $\bar x=\dfrac{30}{10}=3$. Variance is the mean of the squares minus the square of the mean, hence $\sigma^2=\dfrac{110}{10}-3^2=11-9=2$. Dividing the sum of squares by $10$ gives only the first of these two terms.
