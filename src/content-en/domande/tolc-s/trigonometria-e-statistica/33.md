How many pairs can be chosen from $8$ people if one specified person must belong to the pair?

- $8$
- $14$
- $7$
- $28$
- $21$

## Explanation

One member is already fixed by the condition. Only the companion remains to be chosen from the other $7$ people, so there are $7$ pairs. No division by two is needed: each choice of companion already produces one distinct pair without double counting.
