A committee consists of $2$ of $5$ students and $2$ of $4$ teachers. How many committees are possible?

- $40$
- $20$
- $120$
- $60$
- $126$

## Explanation

The student pair can be chosen in $\binom52=10$ ways and the teacher pair in $\binom42=6$ ways. Each student pair can be combined with every teacher pair, giving $10\cdot6=60$ committees. No internal roles are assigned.
