Two samples of the same size both have mean $10$ but have different population variances, $2$ and $8$. What is the population variance of the pooled sample?

- $10$
- $3$
- $5$
- $4$
- $\sqrt5$

## Explanation

The pooled mean is also $10$, so all the deviations were already taken from the correct mean. If each sample has $n$ values, the sum of squared deviations is $2n+8n$ and its divisor is $2n$.
