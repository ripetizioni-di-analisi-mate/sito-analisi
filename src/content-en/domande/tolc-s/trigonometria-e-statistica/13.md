If $\sin\alpha=\dfrac35$ and $\dfrac\pi2<\alpha<\pi$, what is $\cos\alpha$?

- $\dfrac45$
- $-\dfrac25$
- $-\dfrac45$
- $\dfrac{16}{25}$
- $-\dfrac35$

## Explanation

The fundamental identity gives $\cos^2\alpha=1-\dfrac9{25}=\dfrac{16}{25}$, so the absolute value of cosine is $\dfrac45$. The specified interval is the second quadrant, where cosine is negative. Hence $\cos\alpha=-\dfrac45$. The quadrant resolves the ambiguity between the two square roots.
