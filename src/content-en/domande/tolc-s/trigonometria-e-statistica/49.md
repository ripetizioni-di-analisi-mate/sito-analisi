The values $0,1,2,3$ have respective frequencies $4,7,5,3$. What is the mode?

- $7$
- $2$
- $4$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

The largest frequency is $7$, associated with the value $1$. The mode is the value occurring most often, so it is $1$, not the number of its occurrences. The other frequencies are smaller, so there is a unique mode.
