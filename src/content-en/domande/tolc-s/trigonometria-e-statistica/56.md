Every observation in a dataset is written twice without changing its value. How do the mean and descriptive variance, divided by the number of observations, change?

- Both are unchanged
- Both double
- The mean is unchanged and the variance doubles
- The mean doubles and the variance is unchanged
- The mean is unchanged and the variance is halved

## Explanation

The sum and number of observations both double, so their ratio, the mean, is unchanged. Each squared deviation occurs twice, but the variance divisor also doubles. Thus descriptive variance remains the same. Repeating an observation differs from multiplying its value by two.
