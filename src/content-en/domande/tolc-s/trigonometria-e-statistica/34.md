At which of the following angles is tangent undefined?

- $0$
- $\dfrac\pi4$
- $\pi$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

Tangent is the ratio $\dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}$, so it is undefined where cosine is zero. Among the listed angles, this occurs at $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$. The others have nonzero cosine and therefore give a defined ratio.
