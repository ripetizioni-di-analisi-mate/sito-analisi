Ten observations belong to classes $[0,10)$ and $[10,20)$ with frequencies $4$ and $6$. Which statement about the median must be true?

- It equals exactly $15$
- It belongs to $[0,10)$
- It belongs to $[10,20)$
- It equals exactly $10$
- It is at least $20$

## Explanation

The median is the mean of the fifth and sixth sorted observations. The first four lie in the first class, so the fifth and sixth both lie in $[10,20)$. Their mean also belongs to that interval. Without the individual values, no exact number can be assigned.
