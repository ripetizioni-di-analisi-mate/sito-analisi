$3$ people are chosen from $7$. Two specified people cannot both be selected. How many groups are allowed?

- $35$
- $25$
- $30$
- $20$
- $10$

## Explanation

Without restrictions there are $\binom73=35$ groups. A forbidden group contains both specified people and one third person chosen from the other five, giving $5$ forbidden groups. Subtraction gives $35-5=30$. Groups containing only one of the specified people are allowed.
