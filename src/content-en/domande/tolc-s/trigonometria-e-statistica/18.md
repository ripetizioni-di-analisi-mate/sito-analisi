Which point on the unit circle corresponds to the angle $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$?

- $\left(-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2},-\dfrac12\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac12,\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)$
- $\left(-\dfrac12,-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)$
- $\left(-\dfrac12,\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac12,-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)$

## Explanation

The coordinates are cosine followed by sine. The angle $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}=\pi+\dfrac\pi3$ lies in the third quadrant, so both coordinates are negative. Their absolute values are $\dfrac12$ for cosine and $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ for sine.
