Which expression equals $\sin(2x)$ for every real $x$?

- $2\sin x$
- $\sin^2x$
- $\cos^2x-\sin^2x$
- $2\sin x\cos x$
- $2\cos x$

## Explanation

The addition formula gives $\sin(x+x)=\sin x\cos x+\cos x\sin x$. The two terms are equal, so the result is $2\sin x\cos x$. The expression $\cos^2x-\sin^2x$ instead represents the cosine of the double angle.
