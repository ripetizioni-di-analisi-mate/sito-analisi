Nine observations have arithmetic mean $7$. What is the sum $\sum_{i=1}^9(x_i-7)$?

- $0$
- $7$
- $63$
- $9$
- It cannot be determined

## Explanation

The observations sum to $9\cdot7=63$. The sum of deviations subtracts $7$ for each of the nine observations, giving $63-9\cdot7=0$. This cancellation concerns unsquared deviations; their squares, used in variance, do not cancel in the same way.
