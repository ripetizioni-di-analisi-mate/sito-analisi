How many distinct arrangements, including meaningless ones, can be made using all the letters of SASSO?

- $20$
- $120$
- $60$
- $30$
- $10$

## Explanation

Five letters would have $5!=120$ orders if all were distinguishable. However, the three S letters are identical: their $3!=6$ permutations do not create different strings. Dividing by $3!$ gives $\dfrac{5!}{3!}=20$. A and O each appear only once.
