Of $80$ inspected items, $12$ are defective. What percentage are defective?

- $12\%$
- $\dfrac{20}{3}\%$
- $80\%$
- $68\%$
- $15\%$

## Explanation

The relative frequency is the number of defective items divided by the total: $\dfrac{12}{80}=\dfrac3{20}=0.15$. Multiplying by $100$ gives $15\%$. The number $12$ is an absolute frequency, which does not equal the percentage when the total is not $100$.
