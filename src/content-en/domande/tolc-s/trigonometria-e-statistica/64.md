From a horizontal distance of $12\,\mathrm m$ from a tower base, an observer whose eyes are $1.5\,\mathrm m$ above the ground sees the top at an elevation of $45^\circ$. The ground is level. How tall is the tower?

- $12\,\mathrm m$
- $10.5\,\mathrm m$
- $12\sqrt2\,\mathrm m$
- $13.5\,\mathrm m$
- $24\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The vertical leg of the triangle is the difference between the tower height and the observer’s eye height. Since $\tan45^\circ=1$, this difference is $12\cdot1=12\,\mathrm m$. The total height is therefore $12+1.5=13.5\,\mathrm m$.
