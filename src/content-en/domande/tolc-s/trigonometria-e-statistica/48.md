An acute angle $\alpha$ satisfies $\tan\alpha=\dfrac34$. What is $\sin\alpha$?

- $\dfrac45$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac43$
- $\dfrac54$

## Explanation

The ratio of the opposite to the adjacent leg is $3:4$. We may therefore consider a triangle with legs $3$ and $4$, whose hypotenuse is $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$. Sine is the opposite leg divided by the hypotenuse, hence $\dfrac35$.
