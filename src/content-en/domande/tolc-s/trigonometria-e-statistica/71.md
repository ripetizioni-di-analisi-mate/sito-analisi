Object lengths are measured in centimeters. What are the respective units of variance and standard deviation?

- $\mathrm{cm}^2$ and $\mathrm{cm}$
- $\mathrm{cm}$ and $\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $\mathrm{cm}$ and $\mathrm{cm}$
- $\mathrm{cm}^2$ and $\mathrm{cm}^2$
- Both are dimensionless

## Explanation

Deviations from the mean are lengths, so they are measured in centimeters. Their squares are measured in square centimeters, which remain the units of variance. Standard deviation is the square root of variance and therefore returns to centimeters.
