What is the arithmetic mean of $2,4,6,8$?

- $5$
- $4$
- $6$
- $20$
- $\dfrac{20}{3}$

## Explanation

The sum of the four observations is $2+4+6+8=20$. Divide this sum by the number of observations to obtain the mean: $\bar x=\dfrac{20}{4}=5$. The divisor counts all observations, not the gaps between them.
