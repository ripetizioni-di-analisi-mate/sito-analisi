Batch A contains $12$ defective items out of $80$, and batch B contains $15$ out of $120$. Which comparison is correct?

- B has a higher defective percentage than A
- The defective percentages are equal
- A has more defective items in absolute terms
- The percentages cannot be compared
- A has a higher defective percentage than B

## Explanation

Batch A has a defective share of $\dfrac{12}{80}=15\%$, while batch B has $\dfrac{15}{120}=12.5\%$. The percentage is therefore higher in A, although B contains more defective items in absolute terms. The comparison must account for the different totals.
