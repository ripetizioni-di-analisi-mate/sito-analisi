Which equality is correct?

- $\sin20^\circ=\sin70^\circ$
- $\sin20^\circ=-\cos70^\circ$
- $\sin20^\circ=\cos70^\circ$
- $\cos20^\circ=-\sin70^\circ$
- $\tan20^\circ=\tan70^\circ$

## Explanation

The angles $20^\circ$ and $70^\circ$ sum to $90^\circ$, so they are complementary. In a right triangle, the leg opposite one is adjacent to the other, while the hypotenuse is unchanged. Hence $\sin20^\circ=\cos70^\circ$. Both values are positive.
