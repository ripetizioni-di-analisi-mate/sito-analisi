The maximum temperatures on four days are $16$, $20$, $24$ and $20$ degrees Celsius. What is the descriptive standard deviation, in degrees Celsius, with divisor $N=4$ in the variance?

- $8$
- $2\sqrt2$
- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{4\sqrt6}{3}$

## Explanation

The mean is $\dfrac{16+20+24+20}{4}=20$, so the deviations from the mean are $-4$, $0$, $4$ and $0$. The sum of the squared deviations is $16+0+16+0=32$, hence the variance with divisor $4$ equals $\dfrac{32}{4}=8$. The standard deviation is the square root of the variance, so it equals $\sqrt8=2\sqrt2$ degrees Celsius. The value $8$ is the variance, which is measured in degrees Celsius squared, while $\dfrac{4\sqrt6}{3}$ is obtained by using the divisor $N-1=3$.
