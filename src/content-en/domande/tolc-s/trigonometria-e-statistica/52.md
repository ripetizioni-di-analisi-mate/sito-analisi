A table records $4,4,4,8$ grams. A technician corrects only the last reading, changing it from $8$ to $4$ grams. By how many square grams does the population variance with divisor $4$ fall?

- $4$
- $3$
- $1$
- $9$
- $12$

## Explanation

Initially the mean is $5$ and the sum of squared deviations is $1+1+1+9=12$, so the variance is $3$. After the correction all four values coincide and the variance is zero.
