Which statement correctly describes $h(x)=\tan x\cos x$?

- $h(x)=\sin x$ for every real $x$, without exclusions
- $h(x)=\sin x$ only on the domain $\cos x\ne0$
- $h(x)=\cos x$ on its original domain
- $h(x)=1$ on its original domain
- $h(x)=\sin x\cos^2x$ on its original domain

## Explanation

Replacing tangent by $\dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}$ simplifies the product to $\sin x$ when $\cos x\ne0$. At excluded points, the original tangent factor is undefined. The simplified formula admits an extension, but the domain of the specified function remains the original one.
