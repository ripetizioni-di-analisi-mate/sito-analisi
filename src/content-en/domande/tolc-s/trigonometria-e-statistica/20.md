How many four-digit codes can be formed using only $1,2,3$, with repetition allowed?

- $12$
- $24$
- $27$
- $64$
- $81$

## Explanation

Each of the four positions independently allows three digits. The product rule gives $3\cdot3\cdot3\cdot3=3^4=81$. Repetition is allowed, so the number of choices does not decrease after choosing a digit. Order matters because different positions produce different codes.
