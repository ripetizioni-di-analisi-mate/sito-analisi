What is the maximum value of $f(x)=2-3\cos x$ on $\mathbb R$?

::grafico{f="2-3*cos(x)" xmin=-1 xmax=7 ymin=-2 ymax=6 punti="3.1415926536,5,pieno" titolo="The maximum is the highest level reached by the curve."}

- $3$
- $2$
- $5$
- $-1$
- $1$

## Explanation

Since $-1\le\cos x\le1$, the product $-3\cos x$ ranges from $-3$ to $3$. Adding $2$ gives the interval $[-1,5]$. The maximum $5$ is attained when $\cos x=-1$, for example at $x=\pi$.
