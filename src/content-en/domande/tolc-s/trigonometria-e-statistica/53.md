What is the complete solution set of $\sin x\ge\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$ in $[0,\pi]$?

- $\left[0,\dfrac\pi4\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac{3\pi}{4},\pi\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac\pi4,\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\right]$
- $\left(\dfrac\pi4,\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\right)$
- $\left[0,\dfrac\pi4\right]\cup\left[\dfrac{3\pi}{4},\pi\right]$

## Explanation

The threshold is reached at $\dfrac\pi4$ and $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$. Between these angles, the unit-circle height is at least $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$; outside them it is smaller. Both endpoints are included because equality is allowed.
