A cup contains two ice-cream scoops chosen from three flavors. The flavors may be identical, and scoop order does not matter. How many different cups can be made?

- $9$
- $3$
- $12$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

There are three cups with two scoops of the same flavor, one per flavor. Cups with different flavors correspond to the $\binom32=3$ flavor pairs. These cases are disjoint, so the total is $3+3=6$. Counting $3^2$ would incorrectly distinguish scoop order.
