What is $\sin\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$
- $-\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac12$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The angle $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}=2\pi-\dfrac\pi4$ lies in the fourth quadrant, where sine is negative. Its reference angle is $\dfrac\pi4$, whose sine is $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$. Applying the quadrant sign gives $-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$.
