How many nonempty subsets does a four-element set have?

- $16$
- $8$
- $4$
- $15$
- $24$

## Explanation

For each of the four elements, choose whether to include it, giving $2^4=16$ subsets in total. Exactly one choice excludes all elements and produces the empty set. Removing it leaves $16-1=15$ nonempty subsets.
