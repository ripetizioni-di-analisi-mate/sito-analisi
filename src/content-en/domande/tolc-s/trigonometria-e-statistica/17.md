A mark on the unit circle has coordinates $(\cos x,\sin x)$. The mark is rotated anticlockwise through $\pi/2$. Which expression gives its new vertical coordinate?

- $-\cos x$
- $\cos x$
- $\sin x$
- $-\sin x$
- $\cos(2x)$

## Explanation

An anticlockwise quarter-turn takes $(u,v)$ to $(-v,u)$. Its new vertical coordinate is the original horizontal coordinate, $\cos x$.
