What is the solution set of $\cos x=-\dfrac12$ in $[0,2\pi)$?

- $\left\{\dfrac\pi3,\dfrac{5\pi}{3}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{2\pi}{3},\dfrac{4\pi}{3}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{5\pi}{6},\dfrac{7\pi}{6}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac\pi6,\dfrac{11\pi}{6}\right\}$

## Explanation

Cosine is negative in the second and third quadrants. Absolute value $\dfrac12$ corresponds to reference angle $\dfrac\pi3$. The required angles are $\pi-\dfrac\pi3=\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ and $\pi+\dfrac\pi3=\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$, both within the interval.
