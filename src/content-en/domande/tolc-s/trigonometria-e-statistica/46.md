What is the median of $4,7,2,13,6,8$?

- $6.5$
- $6$
- $7$
- $7.5$
- $\dfrac{20}{3}$

## Explanation

Sorting gives $2,4,6,7,8,13$. There are six observations, so the central values are the third and fourth, namely $6$ and $7$. Their mean is the median: $\dfrac{6+7}{2}=6.5$. The original ordering does not determine the central positions.
