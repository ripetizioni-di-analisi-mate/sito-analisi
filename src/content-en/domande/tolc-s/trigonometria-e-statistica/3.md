What is the solution set of $\sin x=\dfrac12$ in $[0,2\pi)$?

- $\left\{\dfrac\pi6\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac\pi6,\dfrac{7\pi}{6}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac\pi6,\dfrac{5\pi}{6}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac\pi3,\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{7\pi}{6},\dfrac{11\pi}{6}\right\}$

## Explanation

Sine is the vertical coordinate on the unit circle. The height $\dfrac12$ occurs in the first quadrant at $\dfrac\pi6$ and in the second at its supplement $\pi-\dfrac\pi6=\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$. Sine is negative in the lower half-plane, so there are no further solutions in the interval.
