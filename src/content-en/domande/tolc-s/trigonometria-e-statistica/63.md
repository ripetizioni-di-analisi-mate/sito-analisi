A device used to report only whether $\sin t$ was positive, negative or zero. It is modified to record $|\sin t|$ instead. Which pair of times gives the same recorded magnitude but used to have opposite sign readings?

- $t=\pi/6,\ 5\pi/6$
- $t=0,\ \pi$
- $t=\pi/6,\ 7\pi/6$
- $t=\pi/2,\ \pi/6$
- $t=7\pi/6,\ 11\pi/6$

## Explanation

Sine is $1/2$ at $\pi/6$ and $-1/2$ at $7\pi/6$. Absolute value retains the magnitude and discards the sign. Pairs in which the sines are both positive, both negative or both zero do not meet the requirement.
