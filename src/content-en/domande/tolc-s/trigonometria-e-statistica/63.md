What is the fundamental period of $f(x)=\tan(3x)$?

- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
- $3\pi$
- $\dfrac\pi3$
- $\pi$
- $\dfrac\pi6$

## Explanation

Tangent has fundamental period $\pi$. For $\tan(3x)$ to repeat its values and domain, the smallest positive argument increase is $3T=\pi$. Thus $T=\dfrac\pi3$. Starting with $2\pi$ would use sine’s period instead of tangent’s.
