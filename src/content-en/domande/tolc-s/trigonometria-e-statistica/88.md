How many integers from $1$ through $20$, inclusive, are multiples of $2$ or $5$?

- $14$
- $10$
- $12$
- $2$
- $16$

## Explanation

There are $10$ multiples of $2$ and $4$ multiples of $5$. The numbers $10$ and $20$ belong to both groups and have been counted twice. Subtract one copy of each to obtain $10+4-2=12$.
