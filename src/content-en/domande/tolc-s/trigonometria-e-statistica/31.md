Seven observations have mean $10$. What value must be added so that the mean of all eight becomes $12$?

- $26$
- $12$
- $14$
- $24$
- $2$

## Explanation

The original sum is $7\cdot10=70$. The required sum after the addition is $8\cdot12=96$. Thus the new value must be $96-70=26$. The mean rises by $2$, but the added value must also account for the divisor increasing from $7$ to $8$.
