The values $1,2,5$ occur $2,4,4$ times respectively. What is the median of the ten observations?

- $\dfrac83$
- $\dfrac72$
- $4$
- $5$
- $2$

## Explanation

With ten observations, the median is the mean of the fifth and sixth sorted values. The first two positions contain $1$, and positions three through six contain $2$. Both central values are therefore $2$, so the median is also $2$.
