A group of $20$ students has mean $24$ and a group of $10$ has mean $27$. What is the mean for all $30$ students?

- $25.5$
- $25$
- $26$
- $24$
- $51$

## Explanation

The two group sums are $20\cdot24=480$ and $10\cdot27=270$. Their combined sum is therefore $750$, giving a mean of $\dfrac{750}{30}=25$. Averaging the two means directly would give equal weight to groups of different sizes.
