If $\sin\alpha>0$ and $\cos\alpha<0$, in which quadrant does the terminal side of $\alpha$ lie?

- The first
- The third
- The fourth
- The second
- On the vertical axis

## Explanation

Negative cosine requires a negative horizontal coordinate, while positive sine requires a positive vertical coordinate. This combination identifies the second quadrant. Both inequalities are strict, so neither coordinate is zero and the point is not on an axis.
