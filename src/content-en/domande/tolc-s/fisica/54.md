A ray crosses a glass slab with parallel faces and air on both sides, emerging from the second face. How do the incident and emerging directions compare?

- They are always perpendicular
- They always form 45 degrees
- The ray always emerges along the normal
- They are parallel
- The ray returns toward the source

## Explanation

The normals to the two faces are parallel. Applying Snell’s law on entry and exit cancels the glass index and gives equal external angles. The directions are therefore parallel, although the ray may be displaced sideways from the continuation of the incident ray.
