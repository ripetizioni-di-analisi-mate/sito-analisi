An excited nucleus emits a gamma photon. How do $Z$ and $A$ change in the gamma process alone?

- Both remain unchanged
- Both decrease by one
- $Z$ increases by one
- $A$ decreases by four
- $Z$ decreases by two

## Explanation

A gamma photon carries energy while no protons or neutrons are expelled. The nucleus moves to a lower energy level while retaining atomic number and mass number. Gamma emission is therefore distinct from alpha and beta decays, which change nuclear composition.
