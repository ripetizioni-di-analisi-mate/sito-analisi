A sound source approaches a stationary observer at a speed below the speed of sound. How does perceived frequency compare with emitted frequency?

- It is greater
- It is lower
- It is always equal
- It becomes zero
- It depends only on sound loudness

## Explanation

During approach, each new wavefront is emitted closer to the observer. Wavefronts are closer together in space and arrive at shorter intervals, so perceived frequency increases. The Doppler effect concerns frequency; intensity may change for a separate reason related to distance.
