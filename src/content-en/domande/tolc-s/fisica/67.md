Two coherent waves on the same string have equal frequency and amplitude $A>0$. At one point their disturbances are always in opposite phase. In the linear-superposition model, what is the resulting amplitude at that point?

- $2A$
- $0$
- $A$
- $\dfrac A2$
- $4A$

## Explanation

With a suitable time origin, the contributions are $y_1=A\sin(\omega t)$ and $y_2=-A\sin(\omega t)$. Their sum is zero at every instant, so the resulting amplitude at the point is zero. This is destructive interference; adding amplitude magnitudes would ignore relative phase.
