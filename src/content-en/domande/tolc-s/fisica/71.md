In a conductor at electrostatic equilibrium, what is the electric field inside the conducting material?

- It is zero
- It always points north
- It is greater than the external field
- It is proportional to conductor mass
- It is infinite

## Explanation

Free charges redistribute until the field in the material is zero. If a field remained, they would experience a force and keep moving, contradicting electrostatic equilibrium. The statement concerns the conducting material’s interior, not every possible cavity.
