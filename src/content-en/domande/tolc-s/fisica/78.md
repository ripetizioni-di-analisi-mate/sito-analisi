A sample initially contains $800$ radioactive nuclei. On average, how many original nuclei remain after three half-lives?

- $400$
- $200$
- $100$
- $267$
- $0$

## Explanation

Each half-life halves the nuclei still present. The average sequence is $800\to400\to200\to100$, so $\dfrac18$ of the initial number remains. Decay is exponential: the absolute numerical decrease becomes smaller each interval rather than staying constant.
