Heat is supplied to an equilibrium mixture of water and ice at atmospheric pressure. While both phases remain present, what happens?

- Ice stays unchanged and temperature rises
- Ice decreases and temperature stays near zero degrees Celsius
- Ice increases and temperature falls
- All the water freezes immediately
- Temperature rises without melting

## Explanation

At fixed pressure, equilibrium melting occurs at the phase-change temperature. Supplied heat is used to turn ice into water, according to $Q=mL$. Temperature can rise after the last remaining ice has melted.
