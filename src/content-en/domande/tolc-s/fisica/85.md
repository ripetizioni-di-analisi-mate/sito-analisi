An oscillator has position $x(t)=4\cos(2\pi t)$ in centimeters, with $t$ in seconds. What are amplitude and period?

- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $2\pi\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $0.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $1\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Amplitude is the positive coefficient before the cosine, hence $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. Period is found by requiring a $2\pi$ increase in the argument: $2\pi T=2\pi$, so $T=1\,\mathrm{s}$. The distance between extremes is $8\,\mathrm{cm}$, twice the amplitude.
