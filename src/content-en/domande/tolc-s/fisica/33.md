A capacitor of capacitance $5\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ is connected to $12\,\mathrm{V}$. What is the magnitude of charge on one plate?

- $30\,\mu\mathrm{C}$
- $120\,\mu\mathrm{C}$
- $60\,\mu\mathrm{C}$
- $17\,\mu\mathrm{C}$
- $0.060\,\mu\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

Capacitance is the ratio of one plate’s charge to voltage, so $Q=CV$. This gives $5\cdot12=60\,\mu\mathrm{C}$. The plates carry opposite charges of equal magnitude; the requested value concerns one plate, not the sum of their magnitudes.
