What is the speed of light in a transparent medium of index $n=1.5$? Take $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $4.5\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.5\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.5\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Index is defined by $n=\dfrac cv$, so the required speed is $v=\dfrac cn$. Substitution gives $\dfrac{3\cdot10^8}{1.5}=2\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. An index greater than one corresponds to a speed below that in vacuum; multiplying by the index would give the opposite behavior.
