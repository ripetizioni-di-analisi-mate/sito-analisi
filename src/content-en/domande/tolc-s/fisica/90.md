Which description characterizes laminar flow in a pipe?

- Irregular eddies at all scales
- Necessarily zero velocity
- Density that must continually increase
- Necessarily increasing flow rate
- Ordered layers with limited transverse mixing

## Explanation

In laminar flow, fluid layers move in an orderly way with limited transverse mixing. Turbulent flow instead has fluctuations and irregular eddies. The distinction concerns the organization of motion, not a requirement that flow rate or density change over time.
