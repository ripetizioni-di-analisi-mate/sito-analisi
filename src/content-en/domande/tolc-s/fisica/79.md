A nucleus with $Z=84$ and $A=210$ emits an alpha particle. What are the daughter nucleus’s values?

- $Z=83$, $A=210$
- $Z=86$, $A=214$
- $Z=84$, $A=206$
- $Z=82$, $A=206$
- $Z=82$, $A=208$

## Explanation

An alpha particle contains two protons and two neutrons. The nucleus therefore loses two units of atomic number and four of mass number. This gives $Z=84-2=82$ and $A=210-4=206$, consistent with conservation of charge and total nucleon number.
