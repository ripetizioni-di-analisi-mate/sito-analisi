A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart moving at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ sticks to a stationary $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart. With negligible external impulse, what common velocity do they reach?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Initial momentum is $2\cdot6=12\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$. After the carts stick, total mass is $6\,\mathrm{kg}$, so $6v_f=12$ and $v_f=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The collision is inelastic: momentum is conserved, while some kinetic energy changes form.
