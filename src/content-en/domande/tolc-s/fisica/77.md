An atom moves from energy level $-2\,\mathrm{eV}$ to $-5\,\mathrm{eV}$ in a radiative transition. What happens?

- It absorbs a 3 eV photon
- It emits a 3 eV photon
- It emits a 7 eV photon
- It absorbs a 7 eV photon
- It exchanges no energy

## Explanation

The final level is lower, so the atom loses energy by emitting a photon. Photon energy is $E_i-E_f=(-2)-(-5)=3\,\mathrm{eV}$. Negative level energies reflect the reference choice and do not imply negative photon energy.
