An ohmic resistor of $6\,\Omega$ has $12\,\mathrm{V}$ across it. What current flows through it?

- $6\,\mathrm{A}$
- $18\,\mathrm{A}$
- $2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $72\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

Ohmic behavior allows $V=RI$ to be used. Dividing voltage by resistance gives $I=\dfrac{12}{6}=2\,\mathrm{A}$. The product $VR$ does not represent a current; the units also confirm that volts divided by ohms give amperes.
