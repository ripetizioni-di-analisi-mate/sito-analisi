The net force does $-40\,\mathrm{J}$ of work on an object with initial kinetic energy $70\,\mathrm{J}$. What is its final kinetic energy?

- $110\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $70\,\mathrm{J}$
- $30\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work-energy theorem states $W_{\mathrm{tot}}=K_f-K_i$. Solving for the final value gives $K_f=K_i+W_{\mathrm{tot}}=70-40=30\,\mathrm{J}$. Negative work means a reduction in speed, while final kinetic energy remains positive.
