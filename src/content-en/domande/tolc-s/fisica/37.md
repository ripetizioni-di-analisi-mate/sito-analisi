Two divers are at the same depth in the same still lake. Water density is uniform. One is near shore and the other offshore. How do their pressures compare?

- It is greater near shore
- They are equal
- It is greater offshore
- The near-shore pressure is zero
- They depend on horizontal separation

## Explanation

The hydrostatic law is $p=p_0+\rho gh$. Both points have the same surface pressure, density, gravity and depth. Their pressures are therefore equal. Distance from shore and the bottom’s shape do not enter this balance for a liquid at rest.
