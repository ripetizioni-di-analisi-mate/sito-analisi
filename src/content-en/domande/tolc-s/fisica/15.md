A robot moves $6\,\mathrm{m}$ east and then $8\,\mathrm{m}$ north. What is the magnitude of its total displacement?

- $14\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $48\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The east and north components are perpendicular. Displacement directly joins the starting and ending points, so its magnitude is $\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=10\,\mathrm{m}$. The sum $6+8=14\,\mathrm{m}$ instead measures the distance traveled along the two segments of the route.
