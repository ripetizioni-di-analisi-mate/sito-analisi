An ideal blackbody is brought to a higher absolute temperature. How do the peak wavelength of its thermal spectrum and the total power emitted per unit surface area change?

- The peak wavelength increases and emitted power increases
- The peak wavelength decreases and emitted power increases
- Both remain unchanged
- The peak wavelength decreases and emitted power decreases
- The peak wavelength increases and emitted power decreases

## Explanation

Wien’s law gives $\lambda_{\max}T=b$: as $T$ rises, the peak wavelength decreases. The Stefan-Boltzmann law gives $P/A=\sigma T^4$, so emitted power per unit surface area increases. These are two properties of the same spectrum: peak position and total emission. A blackbody emits across a continuous range of wavelengths, not only at the peak.
