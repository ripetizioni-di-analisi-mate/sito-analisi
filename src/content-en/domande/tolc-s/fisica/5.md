In water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, what pressure increase corresponds to descending $2\,\mathrm{m}$? Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $2000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $200\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $5000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $20000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

By the hydrostatic pressure law, the pressure difference between two levels is $\Delta p=\rho g\Delta h$. The data give $1000\cdot10\cdot2=20000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The question asks for the increase, so surface pressure is not added. It would enter the calculation of absolute pressure at the final point.
