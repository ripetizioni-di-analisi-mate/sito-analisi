A force meter supports a stationary object and reads $18\,\mathrm{N}$ in air and $13\,\mathrm{N}$ when immersed in water without touching the bottom. Neglecting air buoyancy, what is water buoyancy?

- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $31\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $13\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

In air the reading equals weight. In water, vertical equilibrium is $T+F_A=P$, so $F_A=18-13=5\,\mathrm{N}$. The body’s weight remains unchanged; the force meter supplies less force because the fluid provides part of the support.
