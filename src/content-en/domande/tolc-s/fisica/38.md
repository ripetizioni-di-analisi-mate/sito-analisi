A charge $q=-2\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ is in a $3000\,\mathrm{N/C}$ electric field pointing east. What is the electric force?

- $6\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N}$ eastward
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$ westward
- $6\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N}$ westward
- $1500\,\mathrm{N}$ eastward
- Zero

## Explanation

The force is $\vec F=q\vec E$. Its magnitude is $|q|E=2\cdot10^{-6}\cdot3000=6\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N}$. Since the charge is negative, force points opposite to the field, hence westward. The charge sign determines direction, not a negative magnitude.
