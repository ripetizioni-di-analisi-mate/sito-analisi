An object of mass $12\,\mathrm{kg}$ is taken to the Moon, where $g$ is about one sixth of its Earth value. How do mass and weight change?

- Mass and weight both become one sixth
- Mass stays 12 kg and weight becomes one sixth
- Mass and weight both stay unchanged
- Mass becomes six times greater
- Weight becomes six times greater

## Explanation

Mass measures the body’s inertia and remains $12\,\mathrm{kg}$. Weight is the gravitational force $P=mg$, so it follows the change in $g$ and becomes one sixth. A scale measuring force therefore requires calibration consistent with local gravitational acceleration.
