A normal force of $200\,\mathrm{N}$ acts uniformly over $0.02\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What is average pressure?

- $4\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $400\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $10000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $200\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Average pressure is normal force divided by area, $p=\dfrac{F_\perp}{A}$. This gives $\dfrac{200}{0.02}=10000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The small area concentrates force: the product $FA$ would have different dimensions and would not describe pressure.
