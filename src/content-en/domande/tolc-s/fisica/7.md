A gas absorbs $500\,\mathrm{J}$ of heat and does $200\,\mathrm{J}$ of work on its surroundings. What is its internal-energy change?

- $+700\,\mathrm{J}$
- $+300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-700\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Absorbed heat enters the system, whereas work done transfers energy to the surroundings. With this convention, the first law is $\Delta U=Q-W$. Thus $\Delta U=500-200=300\,\mathrm{J}$. Adding both magnitudes would incorrectly treat the work delivered as energy received.
