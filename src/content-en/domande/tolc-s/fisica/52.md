Two resistors of $6\,\Omega$ and $3\,\Omega$ are in parallel across $6\,\mathrm{V}$. What total current does the source supply?

- $1\,\mathrm{A}$
- $3\,\mathrm{A}$
- $2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $\dfrac23\,\mathrm{A}$
- $9\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

Each branch has voltage $6\,\mathrm{V}$. Currents are $I_1=\dfrac66=1\,\mathrm{A}$ and $I_2=\dfrac63=2\,\mathrm{A}$. The source supplies their sum, $3\,\mathrm{A}$. Adding resistances would be correct for series, whereas these two branches share voltage.
