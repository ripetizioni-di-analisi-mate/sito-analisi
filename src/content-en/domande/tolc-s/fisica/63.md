A body of mass $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ travels around a circle of radius $4\,\mathrm{m}$ at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the radial net-force magnitude?

- $3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $48\,\mathrm{N}$
- $72\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Circular motion requires centripetal acceleration $a_c=\dfrac{v^2}{r}=\dfrac{36}{4}=9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The radial net force is therefore $F_r=ma_c=2\cdot9=18\,\mathrm{N}$, toward the center. This is the required net force, regardless of which interaction supplies it.
