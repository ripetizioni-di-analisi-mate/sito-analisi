A ray enters a higher-index glass obliquely from air. How does its angle to the normal change?

- It increases
- It always becomes zero
- It decreases
- It always becomes a right angle
- It stays the same

## Explanation

Snell’s law requires $n_1\sin\theta_1=n_2\sin\theta_2$. Since $n_2>n_1$, it follows that $\sin\theta_2<\sin\theta_1$. For angles between zero and ninety degrees, sine is increasing, so the angle decreases as well. The ray bends toward the normal without necessarily coinciding with it.
