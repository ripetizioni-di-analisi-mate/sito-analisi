Two photons in vacuum have wavelengths $\lambda$ and $3\lambda$. What is the ratio of the first photon’s energy to the second’s?

- $3$
- $\dfrac13$
- $9$
- $\dfrac19$
- $1$

## Explanation

For a photon, $E=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$. The first has a wavelength three times shorter, so its energy is three times greater. Constants $h$ and $c$ cancel in the ratio, leaving $\dfrac{E_1}{E_2}=\dfrac{3\lambda}{\lambda}=3$.
