A charge moves perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field. Only magnetic force acts. How does kinetic energy change?

- It increases continuously
- It decreases continuously
- It immediately becomes zero
- It oscillates between zero and its initial value
- It stays constant

## Explanation

Magnetic force is always perpendicular to velocity, so its work is zero. By the work-energy theorem, $\Delta K=0$. Velocity changes direction while its magnitude remains constant; under the stated conditions the motion is uniform circular motion.
