Water, treated as incompressible, flows steadily through a pipe without leaks or branches. At cross-sectional area $6\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ its average speed is $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is it at area $3\,\mathrm{cm^2}$?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

For an incompressible fluid, the same flow rate crosses both sections. Continuity gives $A_1v_1=A_2v_2$, so $v_2=\dfrac{6\cdot2}{3}=4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Areas may remain in square centimeters because they occur in a ratio and their units cancel.
