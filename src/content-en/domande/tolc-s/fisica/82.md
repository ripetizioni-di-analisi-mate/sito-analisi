In the single-photon photoelectric effect, frequency is below the material’s threshold. What happens if only intensity is increased?

- Each photon gains more energy
- No photoelectrons are emitted in this model
- Frequency increases automatically
- The work function becomes zero
- Electrons emerge with twice the energy

## Explanation

A photon has energy $hf$, fixed by frequency. Below threshold, this energy is insufficient to release an electron in a single-photon process. Increasing intensity increases incident photon number but not each photon’s energy, so the threshold is not overcome.
