A square plate has side length $30\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its area in square meters?

- $9\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.9\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $900\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.0009\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.09\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The side is $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$, so the area is $(0.30)^2=0.09\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Alternatively, convert $900\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ by multiplying by $10^{-4}$. The factor $10^{-2}$ applies to a length and must be squared when converting an area.
