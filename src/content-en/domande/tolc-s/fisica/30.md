A horizontal weightless lever carries a vertical $60\,\mathrm{N}$ load $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the pivot. What perpendicular force, applied on the other side $0.6\,\mathrm{m}$ away, keeps it in equilibrium?

- $180\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

In equilibrium, torques about the pivot must have equal magnitudes and opposite senses. Thus $60\cdot0.2=F\cdot0.6$, giving $F=20\,\mathrm{N}$. The force’s lever arm is three times the load’s, so a force one third of the load is sufficient.
