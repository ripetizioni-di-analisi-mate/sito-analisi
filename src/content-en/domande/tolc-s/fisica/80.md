In beta-minus decay, how do atomic number $Z$ and mass number $A$ change?

- $Z$ decreases by one, $A$ stays unchanged
- $Z$ and $A$ increase by one
- $Z$ stays unchanged, $A$ decreases by one
- $Z$ decreases by two, $A$ by four
- $Z$ increases by one, $A$ stays unchanged

## Explanation

A neutron becomes a proton while emitting an electron and an antineutrino. The proton number increases by one, so $Z$ increases. The nucleon is transformed rather than removed: the sum of protons and neutrons, namely $A$, remains unchanged.
