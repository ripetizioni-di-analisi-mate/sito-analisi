Two resistors of $2\,\Omega$ and $4\,\Omega$ are in series with an ideal $12\,\mathrm{V}$ source. What voltage is across the $4\,\Omega$ resistor?

- $12\,\mathrm{V}$
- $8\,\mathrm{V}$
- $2\,\mathrm{V}$
- $4\,\mathrm{V}$
- $6\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

In series, total resistance is $2+4=6\,\Omega$, so the common current is $\dfrac{12}{6}=2\,\mathrm{A}$. The requested resistor has $V=RI=4\cdot2=8\,\mathrm{V}$. The remaining $4\,\mathrm{V}$ is across the other resistor, and the sum reproduces the source voltage.
