A fixed amount of ideal gas expands at constant temperature from $2\,\mathrm{L}$ to $4\,\mathrm{L}$. How does pressure change?

- It halves
- It doubles
- It stays constant
- It quadruples
- It becomes one quarter

## Explanation

Amount of gas and temperature stay constant, so $pV=nRT$ gives $p_1V_1=p_2V_2$. The ratio is $\dfrac{p_2}{p_1}=\dfrac{2}{4}=\dfrac12$. Volume and pressure are inversely proportional only under these constraints; an expansion at constant pressure would instead require a temperature change.
