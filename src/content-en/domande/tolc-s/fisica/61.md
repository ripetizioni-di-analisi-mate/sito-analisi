A velocity-time graph is a straight line through $(0\,\mathrm{s},18\,\mathrm{m/s})$ and $(4\,\mathrm{s},6\,\mathrm{m/s})$. What is the acceleration?

- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Acceleration is the slope of a velocity-time graph. Calculate $a=\dfrac{6-18}{4-0}=-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The negative sign indicates velocity changing toward lower values; over the stated interval velocity remains positive, so the body slows without reversing direction.
