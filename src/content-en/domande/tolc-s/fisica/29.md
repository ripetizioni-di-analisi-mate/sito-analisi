A person stands $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from a plane mirror. How far is the person from their virtual image?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

A plane mirror forms its image as far behind the plane as the object is in front. The image is therefore $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ behind the mirror. The person-image distance is the sum of the two segments, $1.5+1.5=3\,\mathrm{m}$.
