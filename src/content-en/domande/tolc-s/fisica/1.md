How many meters per second correspond to a speed of $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$?

- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $72\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $25.92\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $200\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

One kilometer contains $1000\,\mathrm{m}$ and one hour contains $3600\,\mathrm{s}$. The conversion is therefore $72\cdot\dfrac{1000}{3600}=20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Numerator and denominator are converted together: dividing only by the number of seconds in an hour would leave length expressed in kilometers.
