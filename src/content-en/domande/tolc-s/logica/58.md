A strip repeats the block $1,1,2,3$. What is the sum of its first $10$ values?

- $14$
- $17$
- $16$
- $20$
- $21$

## Explanation

Each block has four values summing to $1+1+2+3=7$. Ten values contain two complete blocks, occupying eight positions, followed by the first two values of the next block. The required sum is therefore $2\cdot7+1+1=16$. The remainder uses the opening positions of the new block.
