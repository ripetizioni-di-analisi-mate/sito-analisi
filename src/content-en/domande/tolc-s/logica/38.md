All $8$ members of workshop A also belong to workshop B, which has $19$ members. How many belong to B but not A?

- $8$
- $19$
- $11$
- $27$
- $0$

## Explanation

Since $A\subseteq B$, A’s eight members are already included among B’s nineteen. The part of B outside A therefore contains $19-8=11$ people. Here inclusion determines the intersection, which equals A; no additional information about the two groups is needed.
