Three meetings occupy the intervals 9–11, 10–12 and 11–13. A room can host the next meeting at the instant the previous one ends. What is the minimum number of rooms needed?

- $1$
- $3$
- $4$
- $2$
- The data do not determine a minimum

## Explanation

Between 10 and 11, the first two meetings overlap, so one room is insufficient. Two suffice by assigning the first and third to the same room, changing over at 11, and the second to another room. This construction attains the lower bound, so the minimum is two.
