Activity A lasts $2$ hours. After A, B and C can start in parallel, lasting $3$ and $5$ hours respectively. D lasts $1$ hour and starts only after both B and C finish. What is the minimum total duration?

- $8\,\mathrm{h}$
- $6\,\mathrm{h}$
- $10\,\mathrm{h}$
- $11\,\mathrm{h}$
- $7\,\mathrm{h}$

## Explanation

A finishes at hour $2$. Starting both following activities immediately, B finishes at hour $5$ and C at hour $7$. D must wait for the later finish and therefore ends at hour $8$. The A-C-D path requires $2+5+1=8$ hours, and no permitted overlap can shorten it further.
