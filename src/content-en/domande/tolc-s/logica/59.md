A sequence starts at $a_0=9$. Each term is obtained from the previous one by adding $3$ if the previous term is odd and by dividing it by $2$ if it is even. What is $a_5$?

- $6$
- $12$
- $9$
- $3$
- $18$

## Explanation

The rule is applied each time to the term just obtained. Since $9$ is odd, $a_1=12$; since $12$ is even, $a_2=6$, and in the same way $a_3=3$. From here the terms alternate, so $a_4=6$ and $a_5=3$. The value $6$ is the fifth number in the list $9,12,6,3,6$, which also contains $a_0$, so it corresponds to $a_4$.
