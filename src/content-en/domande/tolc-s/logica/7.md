What is the negation of “Marco studies and Lucia works”? The word “or” is inclusive.

- Marco does not study and Lucia does not work
- Marco does not study or Lucia does not work
- Marco studies or Lucia works
- Marco studies and Lucia does not work
- Marco does not study and Lucia works

## Explanation

A conjunction is true only when both parts are true. Its negation therefore allows all cases in which at least one part is false. By De Morgan’s law, $\neg(P\land Q)\iff\neg P\lor\neg Q$. Requiring both to be false would exclude two of the three cases that negate the original sentence.
