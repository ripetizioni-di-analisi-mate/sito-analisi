A group has $20$ people. It is reported that $14$ swim, $12$ run and $3$ do both. What is the correct assessment?

- The data are incompatible: the union would contain 23 people
- The data are compatible and nobody is outside
- The data are compatible and 3 people are outside
- The data are compatible and 6 only swim
- The data require everyone to run

## Explanation

Inclusion and exclusion would give $14+12-3=23$ people with at least one activity. A subset of a $20$-person group cannot contain $23$ people. The issue is therefore not to choose a negative outside count: at least one reported value must be wrong.
