A counter starts at $5$ at step zero and adds $7$ at each step. What is the first value strictly greater than $80$?

- $75$
- $80$
- $82$
- $81$
- $89$

## Explanation

At step $n$, the value is $5+7n$. We require $5+7n>80$, namely $7n>75$. The first suitable integer is $n=11$, producing $82$. The preceding step gives $5+70=75$, still below the threshold. This check confirms that $82$ is the first exceedance.
