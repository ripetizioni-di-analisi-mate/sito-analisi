In the arithmetic progression with first term $a_0=5$ and common difference $7$, what is the first term strictly greater than $80$?

- $75$
- $80$
- $82$
- $81$
- $89$

## Explanation

The term with index $n$ is $a_n=5+7n$. The condition $5+7n>80$ is equivalent to $7n>75$, that is $n>\dfrac{75}{7}$. Since $\dfrac{75}{7}$ lies between $10$ and $11$, the first index that satisfies it is $n=11$, and so the required term is $a_{11}=5+77=82$. The previous term is $a_{10}=75$, which is still less than $80$.
