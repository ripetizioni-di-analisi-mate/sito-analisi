If the elevator is under maintenance, the external stairs are open. If the external stairs are open, the lobby is supervised. The lobby is not supervised. Which pair is certain?

- Stairs open and elevator under maintenance
- Stairs closed and elevator under maintenance
- Stairs open and elevator not under maintenance
- Elevator faulty and lobby supervised
- Stairs closed and elevator not under maintenance

## Explanation

The second implication, together with an unsupervised lobby, rules out open stairs. Applying the contrapositive to the first implication as well, closed stairs rule out elevator maintenance. Both negations therefore follow from the data. “Not under maintenance” cannot be replaced by “working”, because the text does not establish that equivalence.
