A sequence starts with $2,3$, and every later term is the sum of the preceding two. What is the seventh term?

- $21$
- $34$
- $55$
- $29$
- $32$

## Explanation

The two initial conditions successively give $5$, $8$, $13$, $21$ and $34$. These are the third through seventh terms. In particular, the seventh is $13+21=34$. Each update uses the latest two values obtained; repeatedly adding the first two would follow a different procedure.
