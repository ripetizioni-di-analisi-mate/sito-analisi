Every student consults at least one tutor, but it need not be the same tutor for everyone. Which further conclusion is not guaranteed, assuming students exist?

- Every student consults a tutor
- At least one tutor exists
- At least one student consults a tutor
- No student has consulted no tutor
- There is one tutor consulted by every student

## Explanation

Two students may consult two different tutors, one each, with no common tutor. This situation satisfies the premise and refutes the proposed conclusion. The other statements follow from the premise and the existence of students. The error moves the tutor’s existence before “all students”, requiring one shared person.
