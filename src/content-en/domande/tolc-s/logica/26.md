The counter opens on at least one of Monday and Tuesday. If it opens on Tuesday, it also opens on Monday. On which day is it certainly open?

- Monday
- Tuesday
- Both days
- Neither day
- Wednesday

## Explanation

There are two possible Tuesday states. If the counter opens on Tuesday, the second rule requires it to open on Monday. If it stays closed on Tuesday, the first rule still requires Monday. Since these cases exhaust the possibilities, it is always open on Monday. Tuesday may be open or closed.
