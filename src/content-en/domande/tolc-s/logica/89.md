The register accepts integers that are even and leave remainder $1$ when divided by $3$. Which proposed value is accepted?

- $12$
- $15$
- $19$
- $16$
- $20$

## Explanation

The number $16$ is even and can be written $16=3\cdot5+1$, so it satisfies both requirements. Numbers $12$ and $15$ have remainder zero; $20$ has remainder two. Number $19$ has remainder one but is odd. The filter requires the conjunction of both properties, not satisfaction of just one.
