If it rains, the field is wet. The field is not wet. What necessarily follows?

- It is raining
- It is not raining
- The field is covered
- It has stopped raining
- The ground absorbs all the rain

## Explanation

If it were raining, the first premise would require a wet field, contradicting the second. Rain is therefore ruled out. This uses the **contrapositive**: rain $\Rightarrow$ wet field gives field not wet $\Rightarrow$ no rain. The text provides no information about covers, earlier rain or absorption.
