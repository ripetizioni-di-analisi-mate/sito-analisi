How many truth assignments to $P,Q,R$ satisfy both $P\Rightarrow Q$ and $Q\Rightarrow R$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $3$
- $6$
- $8$

## Explanation

If $P$ is true, both implications force $Q$ and $R$ true, giving one case. If $P$ is false and $Q$ true, $R$ must be true, giving a second case. If both $P$ and $Q$ are false, $R$ is unrestricted and gives two further cases. There are four assignments in total.
