What is the negation of “Every student has read at least one assigned text”?

- Every student has left one text unread
- At least one student has read none of the assigned texts
- No text has been read by everyone
- At least one student has read every text
- All students have read only one text

## Explanation

The sentence requires at least one read text to exist for each student. Its negation requires a student for whom that existence fails. That particular student has read no assigned text. Thus the negation changes “every student” to “at least one student” and “at least one text” to “no text”.
