A lamp is initially off. Each press of its switch reverses its state. It is pressed $17$ times and then $8$ more times. What is its final state?

- Off
- It depends on the interval between presses
- On only after the seventeenth press
- Undetermined without knowing its power
- On

## Explanation

Two consecutive presses return the lamp to its initial state. The total is $17+8=25$, an odd number, so twenty-four presses can be paired, leaving one. That remaining press changes the initial off state to on. Timing and power do not enter the stated rule.
