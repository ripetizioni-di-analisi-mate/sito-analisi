The parcel is in depot A or depot B, with inclusive “or”. It is not in depot A. What follows?

- The parcel is not in depot B
- The parcel is in both
- No parcel exists
- The parcel is in depot B
- The first premise is false

## Explanation

The disjunction requires at least one location among A and B. Once A is ruled out, B is the only way to satisfy the premise. Formally, $P\lor Q$ and $\neg P$ imply $Q$. The inclusive reading does not alter this deduction: being in both locations is already excluded by absence from A.
