“The visit takes place unless the museum is closed” means that an open museum guarantees the visit. Which situation violates this guarantee?

- Museum closed and visit canceled
- Museum closed and visit held elsewhere
- Museum open and visit canceled
- Museum open and visit held
- Visit canceled, with no information about whether the museum is open

## Explanation

The meaning specified in the text is museum open $\Rightarrow$ visit held. The guarantee is violated when the museum is open and the visit is canceled. Cases of closure are not governed by this implication alone: a visit elsewhere is compatible, although it is not guaranteed. Knowing only that the visit is canceled does not establish a violation without knowing that the museum is open.
