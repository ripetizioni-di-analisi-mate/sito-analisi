“The visit takes place unless the museum is closed” means that an open museum guarantees the visit. Which situation violates this guarantee?

- Museum closed and visit canceled
- Museum closed and visit held elsewhere
- Museum open and visit canceled
- Museum open and visit held
- Visit canceled, with no information about whether the museum is open

## Explanation

The meaning specified in the text is museum open $\Rightarrow$ visit held. An implication is false only when the antecedent is true and the consequent is false, so the guarantee is violated when the museum is open and the visit is canceled. The implication says nothing about the cases in which the museum is closed, so a visit held elsewhere does not violate it, although it is not guaranteed. Knowing only that the visit is canceled does not establish a violation without knowing whether the museum is open.
