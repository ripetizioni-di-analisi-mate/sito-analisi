Museum visits are either guided or self-guided, never both. Management proposes the rule “A guided visit takes place only with a reservation.” Which second rule would ensure that reservations and guided visits always go together?

- There is at least one guided visit
- No guided visit is self-guided
- Every reserved visit is guided
- Every self-guided visit is available in the morning
- Some reserved visits are guided

## Explanation

The first rule says “guided implies reserved.” Equivalence also requires “reserved implies guided” for all visits. One example of a reserved guided visit would not impose that condition.
