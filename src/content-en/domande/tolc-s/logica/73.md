Which pair of integers refutes “If the product of two integers is even, both integers are even”?

- $2$ and $4$
- $3$ and $5$
- $2$ and $3$
- $4$ and $6$
- $8$ and $10$

## Explanation

A counterexample must make the antecedent true and the consequent false. The product $2\cdot3=6$ is even, but $3$ is odd, so the two factors are not both even. The pair $3,5$ instead has an odd product and does not test the statement’s antecedent.
