What is the negation of “If the caretaker opens the gate, the indicator goes off”?

- The caretaker does not open the gate
- The indicator goes off and the gate stays closed
- If the indicator goes off, the caretaker opens the gate
- The caretaker opens the gate and the indicator does not go off
- The caretaker does not open the gate or the indicator goes off

## Explanation

To refute a conditional promise, its initial condition must occur and its promised outcome must fail. Writing $P$ for opening and $Q$ for switching off gives $\neg(P\Rightarrow Q)\iff P\land\neg Q$. Failure to open alone does not violate the rule, because in that case the rule does not require the indicator to go off.
