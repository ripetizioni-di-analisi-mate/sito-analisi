A pair of numbers starts at $(2,7)$. Each move adds $1$ to one number and subtracts $1$ from the other. Which pair is impossible to reach?

- $(5,5)$
- $(3,6)$
- $(0,9)$
- $(7,2)$
- $(4,5)$

## Explanation

Each move preserves the sum because the increase and decrease cancel. The initial sum is $2+7=9$, whereas $5+5=10$. Thus $(5,5)$ is ruled out. All the other pairs have sum $9$ and are obtained by transferring the appropriate number of units between components.
