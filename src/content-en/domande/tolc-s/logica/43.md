Four activities A, B, C and D occupy four slots. B is immediately after A, C precedes A and D finishes. What is the order?

- A, B, C, D
- C, B, A, D
- C, A, B, D
- A, C, B, D
- C, A, D, B

## Explanation

D occupies the fourth slot. The AB block requires two consecutive slots before D and must leave C before A. It therefore cannot begin in the first slot and must occupy the second and third. C remains first. This gives CABD, satisfying all three constraints.
