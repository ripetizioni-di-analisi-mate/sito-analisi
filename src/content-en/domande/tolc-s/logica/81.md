Four counters A, B, C and D are open or closed. Exactly two are open. A and B have the same state, and C is open. Which pair is closed?

- A and B
- A and C
- B and C
- C and D
- A and D

## Explanation

A and B are either both open or both closed. If both were open, C would bring the open total to at least three, contradicting the limit of two. They must therefore be closed. To reach exactly two open counters, D must be open together with C. The final configuration is unique.
