What is the negation of “All students have a badge”?

- At least one student does not have a badge
- No student has a badge
- Exactly one student has a badge
- At least one student has a badge
- All students have two badges

## Explanation

The original sentence asserts a property of every student. Making it false requires only one **counterexample**, namely a student without a badge. The other students may or may not have one. Saying that nobody has a badge would exclude situations in which only one student lacks it, so it would be too strong a negation.
