When the pass is valid and the room is free, entry is allowed. The pass is valid, but entry is not allowed. What follows?

- The room is not free
- The pass is invalid
- The room is free
- Entry is allowed
- The pass expired yesterday

## Explanation

If the room were free, that fact together with the valid pass would satisfy both sufficient conditions for entry. Allowed entry would follow, contrary to the given fact. The room must therefore not be free. This reasoning uses the known validity of the pass; without it, forbidden entry would rule out only the conjunction of requirements.
