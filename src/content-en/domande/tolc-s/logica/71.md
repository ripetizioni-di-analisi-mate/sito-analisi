“Every subscriber will receive the notice because the system sends it to every address in the register.” Which premise connects the system’s operation to the conclusion?

- Every subscriber has a valid address in the register to which the notice is delivered
- The register contains at least one address
- The notice is short
- The system uses a green background
- Some subscribers have two addresses

## Explanation

The sending rule concerns addresses in the register, while the conclusion concerns all subscribers. Every person must be connected to a valid listed address reached by the notice. Merely having some addresses does not guarantee full coverage. The chosen premise fills exactly this gap and includes the distinction between sending and receipt.
