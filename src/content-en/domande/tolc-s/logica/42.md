Three activities A, B and C occupy three distinct slots. The only constraint is that A precedes C. How many orders are possible?

- $1$
- $3$
- $2$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The possible orders are ABC, ACB and BAC. In each, A precedes C, while B can be placed before them, between them or after them. The other three of the six total orders reverse A and C. The constraint does not require A and C to be consecutive.
