All restored artifacts are photographed. Which information is compatible with this premise without being required by it?

- A restored artifact without a photograph exists
- Restored artifacts exist and none of them is photographed
- No photographed artifact can be restored, and a restored one exists
- Some restored artifacts fail to satisfy the rule
- Photographed artifacts that have not been restored exist

## Explanation

The inclusion restored $\subseteq$ photographed leaves the part of photographed artifacts outside the restored set unrestricted. That part may contain artifacts or be empty. The existence of photographed artifacts that have not been restored is therefore possible but not necessary. The other options, when asserting a restored artifact without a photograph or requiring one, violate the premise.
