A bag contains $4$ red tokens and $6$ blue ones. What is the smallest number of draws without replacement guaranteeing at least two red tokens?

- $2$
- $6$
- $8$
- $7$
- $9$

## Explanation

The worst case is drawing all six blue tokens first. After seven draws there might therefore be only one red token, so seven are insufficient. At the eighth draw, at most six tokens are blue, hence at least two drawn tokens are red. The worst case establishes both sufficiency and minimality of eight.
