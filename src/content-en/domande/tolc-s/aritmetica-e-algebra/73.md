You want to dilute $15$ mL of concentrate in a ratio of $1:4$ concentrate to water. Your cylinder holds at most $70$ mL. How many mL of concentrate can you use at most, preserving the exact ratio and preparing the mixture all at once?

- $12$
- $15$
- $14$
- $17.5$
- $10$

## Explanation

For $v$ mL of concentrate the water volume must be $4v$, giving $5v$ in all. Capacity requires $5v\le70$, so $v\le14$. That amount is available and, with $56$ mL of water, fills the cylinder.
