What is the sum of the roots of $3x^2+5x-2=0$?

- $\dfrac53$
- $\dfrac23$
- $-\dfrac23$
- $-5$
- $-\dfrac53$

## Explanation

The discriminant is $25+24=49$, so the roots are real. Expanding $a(x-x_1)(x-x_2)$ shows that the coefficient of $x$ is $-a(x_1+x_2)$. Hence $x_1+x_2=-\dfrac ba=-\dfrac53$. Finding the individual roots is unnecessary to determine the requested sum.
