How far from zero is the number $\sqrt{49}-\sqrt[3]{512}$?

- $15$
- $7$
- $1$
- $-1$
- $8$

## Explanation

The roots are $7$ and $8$, because $7^2=49$ and $8^3=512$. The given number is therefore $7-8=-1$. Its distance from zero is its absolute value, $|-1|=1$. The algebraic value retains the negative sign, whereas distance is nonnegative.
