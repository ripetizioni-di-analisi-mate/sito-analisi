Someone solves $x(x-4)=3(x-4)$ by immediately dividing by $x-4$. Which change preserves every solution?

- Reverse the sign of the right side before dividing
- Replace $x-4$ by $1$ in all cases
- Cancel $x$ inside the binomial $x-4$
- Immediately impose $x\ne4$ because $x-4$ appears in the equation
- Check the case $x=4$ first and divide in the case $x\ne4$

## Explanation

At $x=4$ both sides vanish, so $4$ is a solution. Only in the case $x\ne4$ may one divide and obtain $x=3$. Together the two branches give $\{3,4\}$.
