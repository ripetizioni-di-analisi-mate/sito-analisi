What is the degree of the nonzero polynomial $(x^2+1)(x^2-1)-x^4+5x$?

- $3$
- $0$
- $2$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

The initial product is a difference of squares and equals $x^4-1$. Thus the whole expression becomes $x^4-1-x^4+5x=5x-1$. The highest-degree term with nonzero coefficient is $5x$, so the degree is one. Degree is determined after like terms have been combined.
