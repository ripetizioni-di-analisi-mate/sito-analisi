A lab records two successive real temperature changes $a$ and $b$. Which condition characterizes exactly when the sum of the magnitudes of the changes equals the magnitude of their total?

- $a+b=0$
- $a=b$
- $a+b\ge0$
- $ab\ge0$
- $ab<0$

## Explanation

Compare $|a|+|b|$ and $|a+b|$. Since both are nonnegative, their squares can be compared. Equality reduces to $2|ab|=2ab$, or $ab\ge0$: the changes are in the same direction or at least one is zero.
