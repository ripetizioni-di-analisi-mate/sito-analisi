The number of registrations rises from $160$ to $200$. What is the percentage increase?

- $40\%$
- $20\%$
- $125\%$
- $16\%$
- $25\%$

## Explanation

The absolute increase is $200-160=40$ registrations. The percentage is measured relative to the initial value, so the ratio is $\dfrac{40}{160}=\dfrac14$. Multiplying by $100\%$ gives $25\%$. Dividing by $200$ would instead measure the increase as a share of the final total, which is a different quantity.
