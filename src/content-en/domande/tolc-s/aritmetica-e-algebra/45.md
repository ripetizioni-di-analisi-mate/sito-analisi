A club advertises an increase in membership “of at least $20\%$”. It started with $43$ members. What is the least whole number of new members that makes the claim true if nobody leaves?

- $8$
- $10$
- $12$
- $52$
- $9$

## Explanation

The new-member count must satisfy $n/43\ge0.20$, or $n\ge8.6$. The smallest admissible integer is $9$. Rounding down would miss the advertised threshold.
