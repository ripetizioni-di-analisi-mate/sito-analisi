Which number is equally distant from $-\dfrac72$ and $\dfrac52$ on the real number line?

- $\dfrac12$
- $0$
- $-1$
- $-\dfrac12$
- $3$

## Explanation

The point equidistant from two distinct points on the line is their midpoint. Its coordinate is their average: $\dfrac{-\frac72+\frac52}{2}=\dfrac{-1}{2}=-\dfrac12$. Both distances are $3$, because $-\dfrac12-(-\dfrac72)=3$ and $\dfrac52-(-\dfrac12)=3$.
