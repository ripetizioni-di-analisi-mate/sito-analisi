An unknown positive divisor $d$ leaves remainder $2$ when both $38$ and $62$ are divided by it. What is the largest possible $d$?

- $6$
- $2$
- $24$
- $12$
- $36$

## Explanation

The divisor must divide both $38-2=36$ and $62-2=60$. Their greatest common divisor is $12$. Since $2<12$, the remainder is allowed, and $38=3\cdot12+2$, $62=5\cdot12+2$ verify it.
