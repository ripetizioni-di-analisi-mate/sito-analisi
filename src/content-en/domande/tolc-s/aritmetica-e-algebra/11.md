Which is the largest of $\dfrac78$, $\dfrac{11}{12}$, $\dfrac9{10}$, $\dfrac{13}{15}$ and $\dfrac56$?

- $\dfrac{11}{12}$
- $\dfrac78$
- $\dfrac9{10}$
- $\dfrac{13}{15}$
- $\dfrac56$

## Explanation

All the fractions are less than one. Their distances from one are, in order, $\dfrac18$, $\dfrac1{12}$, $\dfrac1{10}$, $\dfrac2{15}$ and $\dfrac16$. The smallest distance is $\dfrac1{12}$: in particular, $\dfrac1{12}<\dfrac2{15}$ because $15<24$. Therefore the fraction closest to one from below, and hence the largest, is $\dfrac{11}{12}$.
