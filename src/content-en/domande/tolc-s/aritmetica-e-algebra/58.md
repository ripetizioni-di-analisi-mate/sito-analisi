What is the coefficient of $x$ in $(2x-3)(x+4)$?

- $2$
- $-3$
- $5$
- $-12$
- $8$

## Explanation

The first-degree contributions come from $2x\cdot4=8x$ and $(-3)\cdot x=-3x$. Their sum is $5x$, so the requested coefficient is $5$. The other products give $2x^2$ and $-12$, which do not contribute to the first-degree term. A full expansion can therefore be avoided.
