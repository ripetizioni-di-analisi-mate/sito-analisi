A direct measurement gives $x>0$. A second observation says $x^2<2x$. Which conclusion, together with the first measurement, describes exactly the possible values?

- $x<1$
- $x<2$
- $x<\sqrt2$
- $x>2$
- $x\le2$

## Explanation

The first measurement allows division by $x$ without reversing the inequality, giving $x<2$. Conversely, every $0<x<2$ satisfies the original inequality. The value $2$ would produce equality.
