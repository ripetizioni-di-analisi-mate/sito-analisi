A model needs the same $x$ to make both $x^2+2$ and $x^2+x+1$ vanish. Which candidate is forced by their difference, before the final check?

- $x=1$
- $x=-2$
- $x=2$
- $x=0$
- $x=-1$

## Explanation

If both vanish, their difference must vanish too. Subtracting the first from the second gives $x-1=0$, so $x=1$. This candidate fails the final check because the first polynomial equals $3$ at $1$.
