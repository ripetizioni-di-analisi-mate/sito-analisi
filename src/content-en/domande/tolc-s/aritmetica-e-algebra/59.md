A positive integer has the form $2^a3^b$, where $a,b$ are nonnegative integers. If its square is divisible by $72=2^3\cdot3^2$, which divisor must the original number have?

- $18$
- $36$
- $72$
- $12$
- $24$

## Explanation

The exponents in the square are $2a$ and $2b$. We need $2a\ge3$ and $2b\ge2$. Since the exponents are integers, $a\ge2$ and $b\ge1$, making $2^2\cdot3=12$ a divisor. The number $12$ itself shows that no further factors are required.
