What is $\left(\dfrac35\right)^{-2}$?

- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac{25}{9}$
- $\dfrac9{25}$
- $\dfrac53$
- $-\dfrac9{25}$

## Explanation

A negative exponent indicates the reciprocal of the positive power. Thus $\left(\dfrac35\right)^{-2}=\left(\dfrac53\right)^2=\dfrac{25}{9}$. The sign remains positive: the minus belongs to the exponent and does not make the base negative. Since the original base lies between zero and one, the reciprocal of its square exceeds one.
