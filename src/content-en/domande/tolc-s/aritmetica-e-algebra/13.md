A laboratory orders three positive rational readings $a<b<c$. Which ratio must be greater than $1$ whatever the readings?

- $\dfrac{a+b}{b+c}$
- $\dfrac{a}{c}$
- $\dfrac{b+c}{a+b}$
- $\dfrac{b^2}{ac}$
- $\dfrac{b}{a+c}$

## Explanation

The denominators are positive. Since $c>a$, we have $b+c>a+b$, making the first ratio greater than $1$. In contrast, $\dfrac{b^2}{ac}$ can be $1$, for example when $a=1,b=2,c=4$, and the remaining ratios are below $1$.
