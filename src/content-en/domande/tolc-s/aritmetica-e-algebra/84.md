An experiment produces a signal $h(t)=t(6-t)$ for $0\le t\le6$. Without finding a maximum, which pair of times must give the same signal by symmetry of the formula?

- $4$ and $6$
- $0$ and $5$
- $1$ and $3$
- $1$ and $5$
- $2$ and $3$

## Explanation

Replacing $t$ by $6-t$ swaps the factors and preserves their product. The listed pair of distinct times summing to $6$ is $1,5$, and both give signal $5$.
