Which integer is obtained by truncating $-3.8+1.1$ toward zero?

- $-4$
- $2$
- $-3$
- $-2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The sum equals $-2.7$. Truncating toward zero means retaining the sign and removing the decimal part, so the result is $-2$. The number $-3$ would instead be the nearest integer. For negative numbers, truncation and rounding down do not generally coincide.
