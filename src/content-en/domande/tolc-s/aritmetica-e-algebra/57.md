Two doses, in micrograms, are $3\cdot10^k$ and $6\cdot10^{k-2}$. What percentage of the larger dose is the smaller one, for any integer $k$?

- $20\%$
- $2\%$
- $50\%$
- $200\%$
- $0.2\%$

## Explanation

The second-to-first dose ratio is $(\dfrac{6}{3})10^{k-2-k}=2\cdot10^{-2}=0.02$. It is below $1$, so the second is the smaller dose and equals $2\%$ of the first.
