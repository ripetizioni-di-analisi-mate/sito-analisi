What is $\left(2-\dfrac34\right)\div\dfrac56$?

- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac{25}{24}$
- $\dfrac52$
- $\dfrac56$
- $\dfrac78$

## Explanation

The subtraction is performed before the division: $2-\dfrac34=\dfrac84-\dfrac34=\dfrac54$. Dividing by $\dfrac56$ is equivalent to multiplying by $\dfrac65$. Thus $\dfrac54\cdot\dfrac65=\dfrac64=\dfrac32$. The divisor is positive and less than one, so the quotient exceeds the dividend $\dfrac54$.
