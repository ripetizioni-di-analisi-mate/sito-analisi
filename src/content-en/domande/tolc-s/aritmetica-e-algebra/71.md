Two readings in an archive differ by $0.006$ and the smaller one is recorded as $4.8\cdot10^{-3}$. Which interval contains the larger reading?

- $[10^{-2},\,2\cdot10^{-2})$
- $[0,\,10^{-3})$
- $[10^{-3},\,5\cdot10^{-3})$
- $[2\cdot10^{-2},\,10^{-1})$
- $[5\cdot10^{-3},\,10^{-2})$

## Explanation

In the same units, $0.006=6\cdot10^{-3}$. The sum is $(4.8+6)10^{-3}=1.08\cdot10^{-2}$, between $10^{-2}$ inclusive and $2\cdot10^{-2}$ exclusive.
