Two positive numbers differ by $0.006$ and the smaller one is $4.8\cdot 10^{-3}$. Which interval contains the larger one?

- $[10^{-2},\,2\cdot 10^{-2})$
- $[0,\,10^{-3})$
- $[10^{-3},\,5\cdot 10^{-3})$
- $[2\cdot 10^{-2},\,10^{-1})$
- $[5\cdot 10^{-3},\,10^{-2})$

## Explanation

The larger number is the sum of the smaller one and the difference. Since $0.006=6\cdot 10^{-3}$, we have $4.8\cdot 10^{-3}+6\cdot 10^{-3}=10.8\cdot 10^{-3}=1.08\cdot 10^{-2}$. This value is greater than or equal to $10^{-2}$ and less than $2\cdot 10^{-2}$, so the larger number lies in the interval $[10^{-2},\,2\cdot 10^{-2})$.
