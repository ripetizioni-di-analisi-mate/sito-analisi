A sensor transforms a reading $t$ according to $P(t)=t^2+1$. The same transformation is applied three times, using each output as the next input. What is the degree of the final polynomial in $t$?

- $3$
- $8$
- $9$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The first output has degree $2$. Squaring a nonconstant polynomial doubles its degree, and adding $1$ does not affect its leading term. The degrees after the three stages are $2,4,8$.
