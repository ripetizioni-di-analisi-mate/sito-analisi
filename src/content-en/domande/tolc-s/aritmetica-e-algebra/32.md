What is the quotient when $x^3-8$ is divided by $x-2$?

- $x^2-2x+4$
- $x^2+2x+4$
- $x^2+4$
- $x^2+4x+2$
- $x^2+2x-4$

## Explanation

The difference of cubes satisfies $a^3-b^3=(a-b)(a^2+ab+b^2)$. Setting $a=x$ and $b=2$ gives $x^3-8=(x-2)(x^2+2x+4)$. The quotient is therefore $x^2+2x+4$ and the remainder is zero. In the product, the second- and first-degree terms cancel.
