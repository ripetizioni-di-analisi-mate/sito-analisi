Let $P(t)=t^2+1$. What is the degree of the polynomial $P\big(P(P(t))\big)$?

- $3$
- $8$
- $9$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The polynomial $P(t)$ has degree $2$. Substituting a nonconstant polynomial $Q(t)$ into $P$ gives $Q(t)^2+1$, whose degree is twice that of $Q(t)$, because squaring doubles the degree and adding $1$ does not change the leading term. Hence $P(P(t))$ has degree $4$ and $P\big(P(P(t))\big)$ has degree $8$.
