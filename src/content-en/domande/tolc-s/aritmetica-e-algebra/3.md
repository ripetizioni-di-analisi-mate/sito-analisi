For $x\ne0$, what is $\dfrac{x^5x^{-2}}x$?

- $x$
- $1$
- $x^2$
- $x^3$
- $x^4$

## Explanation

When powers with the same base are multiplied, their exponents are added, so $x^5x^{-2}=x^3$. Dividing by $x=x^1$ subtracts one from the exponent and gives $x^{3-1}=x^2$. The condition $x\ne0$ is required by both the negative power and the denominator, and remains valid after simplification.
