For $x\ne0$, compare $F(x)=\dfrac{x^5x^{-2}}{x}$ and $F(-x)$. Which relation always holds?

- $F(-x)=-F(x)$
- $F(-x)=F(x)=0$
- $F(-x)=F(x)>0$
- $F(-x)<0<F(x)$
- $F(-x)=\dfrac{1}{F(x)}$

## Explanation

For $x\ne0$, the power laws give $F(x)=x^{5-2-1}=x^2$. The square of a nonzero number is positive and stays the same if the sign of the base changes.
