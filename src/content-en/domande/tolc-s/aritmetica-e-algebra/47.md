A report says that the proportion of suitable samples rose from $40\%$ to $50\%$. Which pair gives the increase in percentage points and the increase relative to the initial proportion?

- $10$ percentage points and $20\%$
- $10$ percentage points and $25\%$
- $50$ percentage points and $125\%$
- $10$ percentage points and $10\%$
- $25$ percentage points and $10\%$

## Explanation

Subtracting the proportions as percentages gives $50-40=10$ percentage points. Dividing the difference by the initial proportion gives $\dfrac{10}{40}=\dfrac{1}{4}$, or $25\%$.
