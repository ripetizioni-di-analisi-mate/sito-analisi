A sample is measured weekly. A fraction $q$ of the preceding amount is said to remain each week, where $0<q<1$. Which single observation would disprove this model, starting from a positive amount?

- One quarter remains after two weeks
- The first measurement exceeds $1$
- Less than half remains after one week
- The measurement after three weeks is below that after two
- Exactly zero remains after three weeks

## Explanation

For initial amount $M>0$, after $n$ weeks the amount is $Mq^n$, strictly positive for every finite $n$. The other observations can occur, for example with $q<\dfrac{1}{2}$ or $q=\dfrac{1}{2}$, depending on the observation.
