A lab index equals $\dfrac{A}{B-C}$. Keeping $A=1$, you want the index to be negative with absolute value greater than $1$. What is the exact set of admissible values of $B-C$?

- $(-1,0)$
- $(1,+\infty)$
- $[-1,0]$
- $(-\infty,-1)$
- $(0,1)$

## Explanation

Writing $d=B-C$, a negative index requires $d<0$. Also, $|\dfrac{1}{d}|>1$ is equivalent to $0<|d|<1$. Combining the two gives $-1<d<0$.
