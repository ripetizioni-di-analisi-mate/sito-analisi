Three samples are labelled with pH intervals $[5,7)$, $(6,8]$ and $[7,9]$. Which pair of intervals shares no value?

- The first and the second
- The second and the third
- All pairs
- No pair
- The first and the third

## Explanation

The first interval contains only values strictly below $7$, while the third starts at and includes $7$. The first two both contain $6.5$, for example, and the last two both contain $7$.
