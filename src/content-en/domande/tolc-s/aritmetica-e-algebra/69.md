A measurement is defined by $w=\dfrac{\sqrt{x-1}}{x-4}$. Which set consists exactly of the values of $x$ for which $w$ is defined and equals zero?

- $\varnothing$
- $[1,4)$
- $\{4\}$
- $\{1\}$
- $\{1,4\}$

## Explanation

The radical requires $x\ge1$ and the denominator requires $x\ne4$. A defined quotient equals zero when its numerator vanishes: $\sqrt{x-1}=0$ gives $x=1$, which passes both checks.
