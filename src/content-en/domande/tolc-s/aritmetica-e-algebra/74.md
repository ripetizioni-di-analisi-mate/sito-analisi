Identical packages are made from $84$ notebooks and $126$ pens, using everything with no leftovers. What is the maximum number of packages?

- $63$
- $14$
- $28$
- $42$
- $21$

## Explanation

The package count must divide both $84$ and $126$, since each package must contain the same whole-number quantity of notebooks as every other package, and likewise for pens. The maximum is therefore their greatest common divisor. The factorizations $84=2^2\cdot3\cdot7$ and $126=2\cdot3^2\cdot7$ give $2\cdot3\cdot7=42$. Each package contains two notebooks and three pens.
