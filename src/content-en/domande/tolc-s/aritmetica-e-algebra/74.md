A study compares $a=2^{30}$ and $b=3^{20}$ without computing all their digits. Which argument correctly decides their order?

- $2\cdot30=3\cdot20$, so $a=b$
- $30>20$, so $a>b$
- $2^{10}>3^{10}$, so $a>b$
- $8<9$, so $8^{10}<9^{10}$ and $a<b$
- $2<3$, so $a<b$ for any pair of positive exponents

## Explanation

The exponents have a common factor of $10$. Rewrite the powers as $(2^3)^{10}=8^{10}$ and $(3^2)^{10}=9^{10}$. Taking tenth powers preserves order between positive bases.
