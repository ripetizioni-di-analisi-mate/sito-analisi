Which statement is true?

- $\sqrt5>3$
- $\sqrt5>\sqrt[3]8$
- $\sqrt5=\sqrt[3]8$
- $\sqrt[3]8<1$
- $\sqrt5<\sqrt[3]8$

## Explanation

The cube root of $8$ is $2$, because $2^3=8$. Also, $2^2=4<5<9=3^2$, so $2<\sqrt5<3$. Thus the square root of $5$ is greater than the cube root of $8$, but less than $3$. These bounds allow comparison without decimal approximations.
