Two real values $a$ and $b$ are both increased by the same constant $c$. Which expression remains unchanged for every choice of $a,b,c$?

- $a+b$
- $(a-b)^2$
- $ab$
- $(a+b)^2$
- $a^2-b^2$

## Explanation

The new difference is $(a+c)-(b+c)=a-b$, so its square is unchanged too. For example, taking $a=1,b=2,c=1$ changes every other expression listed.
