To adjust a measurement $\dfrac{u}{v}$, a program adds the same positive constant $c$ to numerator and denominator. If $0<u<v$, where does the adjusted reading lie?

- Always equal to $\dfrac{u}{v}$
- Strictly above $1$
- Strictly below $\dfrac{u}{v}$
- Strictly between $\dfrac{u}{v}$ and $1$
- Always equal to $1$

## Explanation

The numerator $u+c$ remains below the denominator $v+c$, so the adjusted reading is less than $1$. Subtracting $\dfrac{u}{v}$ yields $\dfrac{c(v-u)}{v(v+c)}>0$, showing that it also exceeds the original reading.
