A student writes $\dfrac23+\dfrac14=\dfrac37$. Which correction justifies the exact result?

- Multiply the numerators to obtain $\dfrac16$
- Add only the numerators to obtain $1$
- Invert the second fraction to obtain $\dfrac{14}{3}$
- Express the terms in twelfths to obtain $\dfrac{11}{12}$
- Use denominator $12$ and retain numerator $3$

## Explanation

The denominators describe parts of different sizes, so they are not added. One changes to a common fractional unit: $\dfrac23=\dfrac8{12}$ and $\dfrac14=\dfrac3{12}$. The numerators are then added, giving $\dfrac{11}{12}$. The fraction $\dfrac37$ would even be smaller than the first term, despite adding a positive quantity.
