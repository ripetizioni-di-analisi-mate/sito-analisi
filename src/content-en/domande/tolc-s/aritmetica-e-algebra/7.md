A sample satisfies $x+y=7$ and $2x-y=5$. Which combination of the two observations gives a relation involving only $y$ directly?

- Three times the second minus the first
- Twice the first equation minus the second
- The first plus twice the second
- The sum of the two equations
- The second minus the first

## Explanation

The coefficient of $x$ in the second equation is twice that in the first. Therefore $2(x+y)-(2x-y)=14-5$ eliminates $x$ and yields $3y=9$.
