In the system $\begin{cases}x+y=7,\\2x-y=5,\end{cases}$ what is $x$?

- $6$
- $4$
- $3$
- $2$
- $5$

## Explanation

The two equations must hold simultaneously. Adding corresponding sides cancels $y$ and $-y$, leaving $3x=12$, hence $x=4$. The first equation then gives $y=3$. The pair also satisfies the second equation because $2\cdot4-3=5$; the requested value is therefore $4$.
