In a box, $30\%$ of the items are bolts and the rest are nuts. There are $56$ nuts. How many items are in the box?

- $84$
- $80$
- $168$
- $72$
- $186$

## Explanation

The nuts represent the complementary share, namely $70\%$. If $N$ is the total count, the equation is $0.70N=56$. Dividing by $0.70$ gives $N=80$. There are then $24$ bolts, which are $30\%$ of $80$, while $24+56=80$ verifies the total.
