For every $c>0$, the two solutions of $|2x-1|=c$ have the same midpoint. What is this point, including when $c=5$?

- $-\dfrac12$
- $0$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac c2$
- $1$

## Explanation

Since $|2x-1|=2|x-\dfrac{1}{2}|$, the condition describes the two points at distance $\dfrac{c}{2}$ from $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Their midpoint is therefore $\dfrac{1}{2}$ for any $c>0$.
