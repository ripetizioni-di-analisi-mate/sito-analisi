Which real numbers are at a distance from $3$ of less than $2$?

- $(1,5)$
- $[1,5]$
- $(-5,-1)$
- $(-\infty,1)\cup(5,+\infty)$
- $(-2,2)$

## Explanation

The distance from $3$ is $|x-3|$, so $|x-3|<2$ is required. This is equivalent to $-2<x-3<2$. Adding $3$ throughout gives $1<x<5$. The endpoints have distance exactly $2$ and are excluded because the requirement says “less than”.
