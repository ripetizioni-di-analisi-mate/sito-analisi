What is the remainder when $2^{10}$ is divided by $5$?

- $1$
- $4$
- $3$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

The power $2^4=16$ leaves remainder $1$ when divided by $5$. Writing $2^{10}=(2^4)^2\cdot2^2$, the product has the same remainder as $1^2\cdot4$, namely $4$. A direct check gives $2^{10}=1024=5\cdot204+4$. The resulting remainder lies between zero and four.
