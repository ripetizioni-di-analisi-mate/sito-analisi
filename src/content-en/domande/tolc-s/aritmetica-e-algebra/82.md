The measurements $3.040$ m and $3.04$ m are reported with all their displayed significant figures. What does the final zero present only in the first notation convey?

- The first measurement has only one significant figure
- The numerical values agree, but the first measurement is reported to the nearest millimetre
- The second measurement is rounded to the nearest metre
- The first measurement is one millimetre larger
- The first measurement is ten times more accurate in every possible experimental sense

## Explanation

A trailing decimal zero indicates the place of the last reported digit. In $3.040$ it is $0.001$ m, or a millimetre; in $3.04$ it is a centimetre. The decimal values are equal, and notation alone does not establish the actual measurement errors.
