If $2<a<3$ and $4<b<5$, which interval contains exactly all possible values of $ab$?

- $[8,15]$
- $(6,8)$
- $(8,15)$
- $(8,10)$
- $(12,15)$

## Explanation

Both quantities are positive, so the product increases with either factor. Thus $ab>2\cdot4=8$ and $ab<3\cdot5=15$. The bounds are not attained. Varying $a=2+t$ and $b=4+t$ together, with $0<t<1$, makes the product $8+6t+t^2$ cover the whole interval $(8,15)$ without gaps.
