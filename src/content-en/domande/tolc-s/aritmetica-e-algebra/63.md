What is the simplified form of $(x-2)(x+2)-(x-1)^2$?

- $-2x-5$
- $2x-4$
- $2x-5$
- $x^2+2x-5$
- $2x-3$

## Explanation

The first product is $x^2-4$, and the square is $x^2-2x+1$. Subtracting the second polynomial changes every sign in it: $x^2-4-x^2+2x-1=2x-5$. The quadratic terms cancel, while the constant terms add to $-5$.
