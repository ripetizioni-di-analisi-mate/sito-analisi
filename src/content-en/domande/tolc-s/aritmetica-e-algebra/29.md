What is the complete solution set of $\sqrt{x+2}<3$?

- $[-2,7]$
- $(-\infty,7)$
- $[0,7)$
- $[-2,7)$
- $(-2,7)$

## Explanation

The square root requires $x+2\ge0$, or $x\ge-2$. On this domain, both sides are nonnegative, so the comparison is equivalent to $x+2<9$, or $x<7$. Intersecting the conditions gives $[-2,7)$. The first endpoint gives a zero square root; the second gives equality and remains excluded.
