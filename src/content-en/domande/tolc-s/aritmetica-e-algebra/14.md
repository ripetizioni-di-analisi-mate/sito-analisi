Two consecutive positive integers have product $m$. Which property must always hold?

- $2m-1$ is a perfect square
- $m$ is divisible by $3$
- $m$ is divisible by $4$
- $4m+1$ is a perfect square
- $m+1$ is a perfect square

## Explanation

Write $m=n(n+1)$. Then $4m+1=4n^2+4n+1=(2n+1)^2$. The pair $1,2$, giving $m=2$, already disproves every other property listed.
