The roots of $x^2-5x+6=0$ are $2$ and $3$. If you record $t=x-2$ instead, which monic quadratic has the two recorded values as roots?

- $t^2-5t+6$
- $t^2+t$
- $t^2-9t+20$
- $t^2-t$
- $t^2-4$

## Explanation

The shift sends $2$ to $0$ and $3$ to $1$. The monic polynomial with those roots is $(t-0)(t-1)=t^2-t$. Substitution of $x=t+2$ in the original polynomial gives the same result.
