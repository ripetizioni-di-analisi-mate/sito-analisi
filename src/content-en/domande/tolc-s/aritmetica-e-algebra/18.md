Two measurements satisfy $a+b=10$ and $a^2+b^2=58$. What is $(a-b)^2$, without having to determine which measurement is larger?

- $42$
- $4$
- $16$
- $32$
- $84$

## Explanation

Adding the identities for $(a+b)^2$ and $(a-b)^2$ eliminates the cross term and gives $2(a^2+b^2)$. Thus $(a-b)^2=2\cdot58-100=16$.
