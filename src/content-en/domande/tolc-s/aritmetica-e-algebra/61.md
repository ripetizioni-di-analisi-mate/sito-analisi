Position $s$, measured in metres, is described by $s=at^2+bt+c$, where time $t$ is in seconds. Which units must $a$ and $b$ have, respectively, for the sum to make sense?

- $\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\mathrm{m/s}$ and $\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\mathrm m$ and $\mathrm m$
- $\mathrm{m\cdot s^2}$ and $\mathrm{m\cdot s}$
- $\mathrm{m^2/s}$ and $\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Every term must have the unit of position. For $at^2$ to have units of metres, the units of $a$ must cancel two powers of seconds. In $bt$ one power must cancel. This gives metres per second squared and metres per second, respectively.
