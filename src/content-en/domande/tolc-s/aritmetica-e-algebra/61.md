For which value of $k$ is the polynomial $x^2+kx+12$ divisible by $x+3$?

- $7$
- $-7$
- $4$
- $3$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The factor $x+3$ vanishes at $x=-3$. To be divisible by this factor, the polynomial must also vanish at that point. Thus $9-3k+12=0$, giving $3k=21$ and $k=7$. Indeed, $x^2+7x+12=(x+3)(x+4)$, with zero remainder.
