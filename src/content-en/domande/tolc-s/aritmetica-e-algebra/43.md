What is the remainder when $P(x)=x^3-2x+5$ is divided by $x-2$?

- $7$
- $13$
- $9$
- $-1$
- $5$

## Explanation

The division can be written as $P(x)=(x-2)Q(x)+r$, where the remainder $r$ is constant. Setting $x=2$ makes the product involving $Q$ vanish, leaving $r=P(2)$. Thus $P(2)=2^3-2\cdot2+5=8-4+5=9$. This evaluation gives the remainder without performing the full division.
