What is the solution set of $\dfrac{x^2-1}{x-1}=x+1$?

- $\mathbb R$
- $\{1\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{1\}$
- $\{-1\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The denominator requires $x\ne1$. For every admissible value, $x^2-1=(x-1)(x+1)$, and the fraction simplifies to $x+1$. The equation is therefore true for every real number in its domain, namely $\mathbb R\setminus\{1\}$. The value $1$ remains excluded even though the simplified sides look equal.
