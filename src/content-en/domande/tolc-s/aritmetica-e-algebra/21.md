What is the set of real numbers satisfying both $(x-1)(x+3)\le0$ and $x>0$?

- $(0,1]$
- $[-3,1]$
- $(1,+\infty)$
- $[0,1]$
- $(0,1)$

## Explanation

The product is nonpositive between its zeros $-3$ and $1$, including the endpoints, so the first condition gives $[-3,1]$. The second requires positive values only. Intersecting the sets leaves $(0,1]$. Zero is excluded by $x>0$, whereas one makes the product zero and is positive.
