A note claims that $\dfrac{x-1}{x+2}>0$ always requires $x-1>0$. Which value disproves the claim?

- $-2$
- $1$
- $-1$
- $2$
- $-3$

## Explanation

At $x=-3$ the numerator and denominator are $-4$ and $-1$: the ratio is positive although the numerator is negative. Multiplying the inequality by $x+2$ without checking its sign loses precisely such cases.
