An analysis gives $E=\dfrac{a-b}{a+b}$ with $a,b>0$. The two samples are swapped and all masses are simultaneously converted from grams to milligrams. What is the new index?

- $-1000E$
- $-E$
- $E$
- $\dfrac{1}{E}$
- $1000E$

## Explanation

The unit conversion multiplies both numerator and denominator by $1000$, which cancels. Swapping the samples changes $a-b$ to $b-a$ while preserving $a+b$, so only the sign of the index changes.
