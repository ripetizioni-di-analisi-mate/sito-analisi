A $200$ g solution contains $12$ g of salt. Water evaporates without loss of salt until the solution weighs $150$ g. Which quantity stays constant during this process?

- The salt mass fraction
- The product of the salt mass fraction and the total mass
- The difference between total mass and salt mass
- The mass of water
- The ratio of the mass of water to that of salt

## Explanation

If $f$ is the mass fraction and $M$ the total mass, then $fM$ is the mass of salt. This remains $12$ g. The fraction changes from $\dfrac{12}{200}$ to $\dfrac{12}{150}$ as the water mass decreases.
