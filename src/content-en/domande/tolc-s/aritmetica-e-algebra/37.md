What is $(-2)^4-(-2^4)$?

- $0$
- $32$
- $-32$
- $16$
- $-16$

## Explanation

In the first term, the base is $-2$, so the even exponent gives $(-2)^4=16$. In $-2^4$, however, one first calculates $2^4=16$ and then applies the minus sign, obtaining $-16$. The required difference is therefore $16-(-16)=32$. The two notations have different bases.
