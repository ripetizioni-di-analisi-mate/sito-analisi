A lamp emits light for $\dfrac23$ of a second during each pulse and is off for $\dfrac56$ of a second before the next pulse. What fraction of the time is it on over a whole number of complete cycles?

- $\dfrac49$
- $\dfrac59$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac45$

## Explanation

A cycle lasts $\dfrac23+\dfrac56=\dfrac32$ seconds. The on fraction is the pulse duration divided by the cycle duration: $\dfrac{2}{3}\div\dfrac{3}{2}=\dfrac{4}{9}$. Repeating the cycle scales both durations equally.
