What is $16^{\frac34}$?

- $64$
- $8$
- $12$
- $16$
- $4$

## Explanation

The denominator $4$ of the exponent indicates a fourth root, while the numerator $3$ indicates the power. Since $16=2^4$, $16^{\frac34}=(2^4)^{\frac34}=2^3=8$. Equivalently, one first calculates $\sqrt[4]{16}=2$ and then cubes it. The positive base makes these steps admissible.
