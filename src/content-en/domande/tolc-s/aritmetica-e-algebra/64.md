A device can store only fractions with positive denominator at most $4$ in lowest terms. Which fraction can it store strictly between $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{3}{4}$?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac45$

## Explanation

In twelfths, $\dfrac{6}{12}<\dfrac{8}{12}<\dfrac{9}{12}$, so $\dfrac{1}{2}<\dfrac{2}{3}<\dfrac{3}{4}$, and its reduced denominator is $3$. Both endpoints are excluded. Although $\dfrac{3}{5}$ is inside the interval, its denominator $5$ exceeds the limit.
