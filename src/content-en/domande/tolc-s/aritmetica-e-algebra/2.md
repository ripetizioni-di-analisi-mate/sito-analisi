Five instruments cost $80$ euros each before a $20\%$ increase followed by a $20\%$ reduction. By how many euros is the bill larger if each final price is rounded to the nearest euro before adding, compared with rounding the total only once?

- $5$
- $1$
- $0.20$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

Each price becomes $80\cdot1.20\cdot0.80=76.80$ euros. Five rounded prices total $5\cdot77=385$ euros. The exact total is $5\cdot76.80=384$, already a whole number. The difference is one euro.
