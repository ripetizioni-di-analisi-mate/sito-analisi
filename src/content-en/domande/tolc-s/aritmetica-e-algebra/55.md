A tank initially one-third full receives $18$ litres, bringing it to five-sixths of its capacity. How many litres did it contain at the start?

- $18$
- $6$
- $36$
- $30$
- $12$

## Explanation

The added $18$ litres occupy $\dfrac{5}{6}-\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{1}{2}$ of the capacity. The tank therefore holds $36$ litres. It began with one third of that, or $12$ litres.
