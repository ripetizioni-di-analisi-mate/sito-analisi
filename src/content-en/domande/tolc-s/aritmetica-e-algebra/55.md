A rectangle has perimeter $46\,\mathrm{cm}$, and its longer side exceeds its shorter side by $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the shorter side?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The semiperimeter is $46\div2=23\,\mathrm{cm}$ and equals the sum of the two different sides. If the shorter side is $x$, the longer is $x+5$. The equation $x+x+5=23$ gives $2x=18$, hence $x=9$. The other side is $14$, and $2(9+14)=46$ confirms the perimeter.
