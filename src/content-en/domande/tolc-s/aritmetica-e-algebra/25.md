A spreadsheet replaced $x^2+x-20$ with $(x+5)(x-4)$. Knowing both are monic quadratics, which check alone would suffice to prove that they are identical?

- Finding one common root
- Checking that the two parabolas have the same axis of symmetry
- Getting the same value at $x=0$
- Checking that they have the same sign at $x=1$
- Getting equal values at two distinct points

## Explanation

The difference of two monic quadratics has degree at most one. If it vanishes at two distinct points, it must be zero everywhere. A single agreement still allows a nonzero linear difference.
