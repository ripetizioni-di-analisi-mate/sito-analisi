Which product equals $x^2+x-20$ for every real $x$?

- $(x+5)(x+4)$
- $(x-10)(x+2)$
- $(x+10)(x-2)$
- $(x-5)(x+4)$
- $(x+5)(x-4)$

## Explanation

In the expansion of $(x+u)(x+v)$, the coefficient of $x$ is $u+v$ and the constant term is $uv$. Thus two numbers with sum $1$ and product $-20$ are needed. The pair $5,-4$ satisfies both conditions. Indeed, $(x+5)(x-4)=x^2-4x+5x-20=x^2+x-20$.
