We want to check that the polynomials $x^2+x-20$ and $(x+5)(x-4)$ agree for every $x$. Both have degree $2$ and coefficient of $x^2$ equal to $1$. Which check alone is enough to conclude this?

- Finding one common root
- Checking that the two parabolas have the same axis of symmetry
- Getting the same value at $x=0$
- Checking that they have the same sign at $x=1$
- Getting equal values at two distinct points

## Explanation

In the difference of the two polynomials the $x^2$ terms cancel, so the difference is a polynomial of degree at most $1$. A nonzero polynomial of degree at most $1$ has at most one root, therefore a difference that vanishes at two distinct points is the zero polynomial. Hence, if the two polynomials take equal values at two distinct points, they agree for every $x$. A single equal value, or a single common root, still allows a nonzero first-degree difference.
