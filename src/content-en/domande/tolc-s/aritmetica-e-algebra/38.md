Five readings have arithmetic mean $12$. One reading of $18$ is removed because it was flagged as invalid. What is the mean of the four remaining readings?

- $8.4$
- $10$
- $10.5$
- $11$
- $12$

## Explanation

The original total was $5\cdot12=60$. After removing $18$, the four remaining readings total $42$, giving mean $\dfrac{42}{4}=10.5$.
