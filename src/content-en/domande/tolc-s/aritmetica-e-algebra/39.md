For $a<0$, which expression equals $\sqrt{9a^2}$?

- $9a$
- $3a$
- $-9a$
- $-3a$
- $3a^2$

## Explanation

The principal square root is nonnegative. Thus $\sqrt{9a^2}=3\sqrt{a^2}=3|a|$. Since $a<0$ is given, $|a|=-a$ and hence $3|a|=-3a$. This quantity is positive, consistently with the sign of the square root. Writing $3a$ would instead give a negative value.
