What is $\sqrt{72}-\sqrt{18}$?

- $3\sqrt2$
- $\sqrt2$
- $9\sqrt2$
- $3\sqrt6$
- $3$

## Explanation

The radicands factor as $72=36\cdot2$ and $18=9\cdot2$. Hence $\sqrt{72}=6\sqrt2$ and $\sqrt{18}=3\sqrt2$. Both quantities are multiples of the same root, so their difference is $(6-3)\sqrt2=3\sqrt2$. Subtracting the radicands directly would not be a square-root property.
