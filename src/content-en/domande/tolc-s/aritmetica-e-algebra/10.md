A detector accepts an integer code from $-3$ to $4$ inclusive if $-3x+6\le0$. How many codes are rejected?

- $3$
- $2$
- $4$
- $6$
- $5$

## Explanation

The rule accepts $x\ge2$ because division by a negative coefficient reverses the inequality. The five integers in the domain below $2$ are rejected: $-3,-2,-1,0,1$.
