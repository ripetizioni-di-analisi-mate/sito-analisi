How many integers from $-3$ to $4$ inclusive do *not* satisfy the inequality $-3x+6\le 0$?

- $3$
- $2$
- $4$
- $6$
- $5$

## Explanation

Moving $6$ to the right-hand side gives $-3x\le -6$. Dividing by $-3$, which is negative, reverses the inequality, so the solutions are $x\ge 2$. Among the integers from $-3$ to $4$, the inequality is satisfied by $2$, $3$ and $4$. Therefore it is not satisfied by the five integers $-3$, $-2$, $-1$, $0$, $1$.
