Which expression equals $|x+4|$ for every $x<-4$?

- $-x+4$
- $x+4$
- $-x-4$
- $-x$
- $x-4$

## Explanation

The condition $x<-4$ implies $x+4<0$. The absolute value of a negative number is its opposite, so $|x+4|=-(x+4)=-x-4$. The minus sign must be distributed to both terms. For example, at $x=-5$, both the absolute value and the resulting expression equal $1$.
