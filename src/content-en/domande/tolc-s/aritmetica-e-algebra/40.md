A counter expresses a duration as $\dfrac{11}{12}$ of an hour. Its base-ten decimal expansion repeats. Why can this duration nevertheless be written as a whole number of minutes?

- Because $10$ divides $60$
- Because $11$ is prime
- Because $12$ is even
- Because every fraction below $1$ gives whole minutes
- Because $60$ is a multiple of the denominator $12$

## Explanation

Converting hours to minutes multiplies the fraction by $60$. Since $\dfrac{60}{12}=5$, the denominator cancels completely, yielding $55$ minutes. Decimal expansion in hours and unit conversion use different factors.
