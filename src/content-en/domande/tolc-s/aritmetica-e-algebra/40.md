The fraction $\dfrac{11}{12}$ has a repeating decimal expansion, yet $\dfrac{11}{12}$ of an hour is a whole number of minutes. Why?

- Because $10$ divides $60$
- Because $11$ is prime
- Because $12$ is even
- Because every fraction below $1$ gives whole minutes
- Because $60$ is a multiple of the denominator $12$

## Explanation

Converting hours to minutes means multiplying by $60$, so $\dfrac{11}{12}$ of an hour is $\dfrac{11\cdot 60}{12}$ minutes. Since $60$ is a multiple of $12$, we simplify $\dfrac{60}{12}=5$ and obtain $11\cdot 5=55$ minutes. The decimal expansion of $\dfrac{11}{12}$ repeats because the denominator contains the factor $3$, which does not divide $10$; this factor does divide $60$.
