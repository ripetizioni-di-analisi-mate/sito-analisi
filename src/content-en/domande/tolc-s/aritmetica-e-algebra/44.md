A quantity grows by the same factor each half-day. After one full day it has increased by $21\%$. What is the percentage increase per half-day?

- $1.21\%$
- $21\%$
- $10.5\%$
- $10\%$
- $11\%$

## Explanation

The daily factor is $1.21$, the product of two equal positive factors. Each is $\sqrt{1.21}=1.10$, which represents a $10\%$ increase per half-day.
