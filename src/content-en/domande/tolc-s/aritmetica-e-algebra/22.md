A lab assistant must distribute $4$ litres among tubes holding at most $0.15$ litres each, without wasting liquid. At least how many tubes are needed if the last one may be partly filled?

- $26$
- $27$
- $28$
- $60$
- $25$

## Explanation

The quotient is $\dfrac{4}{0.15}=\dfrac{80}{3}=26+\dfrac{2}{3}$. Twenty-six tubes hold only $3.90$ litres. One further tube is needed for the remaining $0.10$ litre.
