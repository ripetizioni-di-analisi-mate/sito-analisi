A table reports $\dfrac{x^2-9}{x-3}$ at values approaching $3$ from both sides. What number should be entered in the $x=3$ row for the table to follow the same linear rule there too?

- $9$
- $3$
- $-6$
- $6$
- $0$

## Explanation

For every $x\ne3$, divide $(x-3)(x+3)$ by $x-3$. The table thus follows $x+3$, which gives $6$ in the missing row. This completes the table; direct substitution in the original quotient would give $\dfrac{0}{0}$.
