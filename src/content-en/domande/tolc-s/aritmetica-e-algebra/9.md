For $x\ne3$, which expression equals $\dfrac{x^2-9}{x-3}$?

- $x^2+3$
- $1$
- $x-3$
- $x+3$
- $x^2-3$

## Explanation

The numerator is a difference of squares, so $x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3)$. Since $x\ne3$, the factor $x-3$ is nonzero and can be canceled against the denominator. This leaves $x+3$. The equality holds throughout the original domain, which continues to exclude $3$.
