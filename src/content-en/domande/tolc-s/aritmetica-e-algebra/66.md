What is the real domain of $\sqrt{x+2}+\sqrt{5-x}$?

- $[-2,5]$
- $(-2,5)$
- $[-5,2]$
- $(-\infty,5]$
- $[-2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Both roots must exist at the same point. The first requires $x+2\ge0$, or $x\ge-2$; the second requires $5-x\ge0$, or $x\le5$. Their intersection is $[-2,5]$. The endpoints are allowed because a square root may have zero radicand and neither root is in a denominator.
