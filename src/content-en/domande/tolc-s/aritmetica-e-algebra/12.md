Two salt solutions, at $4\%$ and $10\%$ by mass, are mixed to make an $8\%$ solution. What must be the ratio of the mass of the $4\%$ solution to that of the $10\%$ solution, in this order?

- $2:1$
- $1:2$
- $3:2$
- $2:5$
- $1:1$

## Explanation

For masses $a,b$, conservation of salt gives $4a+10b=8(a+b)$, using the same percentage units throughout. Thus $2b=4a$, giving $a:b=1:2$.
