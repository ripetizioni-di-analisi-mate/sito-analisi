A nonnegative integer $c$ shifts the interval of possible readings from $[-1,1]$ to $[c-1,c+1]$. What is the smallest $c$ for which *every* shifted reading satisfies $|2x+1|\ge5$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $0$
- $3$

## Explanation

The condition accepts $x\le-3$ or $x\ge2$. Since $c\ge0$, the shifted interval starts at or above $-1$ and cannot lie in the left branch. Its entirety lies in the right branch precisely when $c-1\ge2$. The first eligible integer is $3$.
