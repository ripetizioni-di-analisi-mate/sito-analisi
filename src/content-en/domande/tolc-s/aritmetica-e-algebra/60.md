What is the complete solution set of $|2x+1|\ge5$?

- $(-3,2)$
- $[-3,2]$
- $(-\infty,-2]\cup[3,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-3)\cup(2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-3]\cup[2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The absolute value is at least $5$ when the argument is at most $-5$ or at least $5$. Thus one solves $2x+1\le-5$ and $2x+1\ge5$, obtaining $x\le-3$ or $x\ge2$. The endpoints are included because they produce an absolute value of exactly $5$.
