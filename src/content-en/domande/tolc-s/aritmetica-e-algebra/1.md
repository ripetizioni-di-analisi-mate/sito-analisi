Without first reducing $\dfrac34+\dfrac56$ to a single fraction, how can you see by how much it exceeds $\dfrac32$?

- Replacing $\dfrac56$ by $\dfrac34$ loses $\dfrac1{12}$
- Replacing $\dfrac56$ by $\dfrac34$ loses $\dfrac16$
- Both addends exceed $\dfrac34$ by $\dfrac1{12}$
- The sum is exactly $\dfrac32$
- The sum is $\dfrac1{12}$ below $\dfrac32$

## Explanation

Write the reference as $\dfrac32=\dfrac34+\dfrac34$. The first term is common to both sums, so their difference comes from the second: $\dfrac56-\dfrac34=\dfrac{10-9}{12}=\dfrac{1}{12}$.
