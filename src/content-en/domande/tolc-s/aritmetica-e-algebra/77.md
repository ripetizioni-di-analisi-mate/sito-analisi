A tally of two sample types records total $x+y=37$ and difference $x-y=8$, with $x,y$ nonnegative integers. Which check immediately reveals an inconsistency?

- The total must always be even
- Total and difference must have the same parity
- The total must be twice the difference
- The difference must be a multiple of the total
- The difference must always be odd

## Explanation

Subtracting the difference from the total gives $2y$, an even number. Here $37-8=29$ is odd. Thus the two records cannot come from two integer counts.
