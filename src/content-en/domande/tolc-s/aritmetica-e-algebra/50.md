A box has red and blue tokens in the ratio $2:3$. Four red tokens are removed and the ratio becomes $1:2$. How many blue tokens are there?

- $12$
- $32$
- $8$
- $16$
- $24$

## Explanation

Initially the quantities are $2k$ and $3k$. Removing four reds leaves $2k-4$ reds and the same $3k$ blues. The new ratio requires $2(2k-4)=3k$, so $k=8$ and there are $24$ blue tokens.
