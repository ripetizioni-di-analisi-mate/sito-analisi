For which value of $k$ does the system $\begin{cases}x+2y=3,\\2x+4y=k\end{cases}$ have infinitely many solutions?

- $3$
- $0$
- $2$
- $12$
- $6$

## Explanation

The left-hand side of the second equation is twice that of the first. The equations coincide only if the constant term is also doubled, namely if $k=2\cdot3=6$. In that case, every pair on the line $x+2y=3$ satisfies both. For any other $k$, the system has no solutions.
