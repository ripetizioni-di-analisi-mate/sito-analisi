A probe reports $a=0.00081$ to two significant figures. Which power of ten is closest to $\sqrt a$ on a logarithmic scale?

- $10^{-3}$
- $10^{-4}$
- $10^0$
- $10^{-1}$
- $10^{-2}$

## Explanation

We have $\sqrt a=\sqrt{8.1}\cdot10^{-2}$. The geometric midpoint of $10^{-2}$ and $10^{-1}$ is $\sqrt{10}\cdot10^{-2}$. Since $\sqrt{8.1}<\sqrt{10}$, the closest power is $10^{-2}$.
