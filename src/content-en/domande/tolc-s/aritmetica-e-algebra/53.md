What is the complete solution set of $\dfrac{(x-2)^2}{x+1}>0$?

- $(-1,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-1)$
- $(-1,2)\cup(2,+\infty)$
- $(-1,2)$
- $(2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The numerator is positive for $x\ne2$ and vanishes at $2$ without changing sign. Where it is positive, the ratio has the sign of the denominator $x+1$, so $x>-1$ is required. The strict inequality also excludes $x=2$. The domain already excludes $-1$, leaving the two stated intervals.
