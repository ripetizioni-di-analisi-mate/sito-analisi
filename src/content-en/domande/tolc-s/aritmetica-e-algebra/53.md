Given that $\sqrt2$ is irrational, which of the following numbers is certainly irrational?

- $(1+\sqrt2)(1-\sqrt2)$
- $(\sqrt2)^2$
- $3-2\sqrt2$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{\sqrt8}$
- $\sqrt{18}-3\sqrt2$

## Explanation

If $3-2\sqrt2$ were rational, subtracting it from $3$ and dividing by $2$ would make $\sqrt2$ rational. The remaining values simplify to $2$, $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $-1$ and $0$ respectively.
