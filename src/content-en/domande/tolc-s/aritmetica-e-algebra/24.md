One rock has mass $10$ g and volume $4\,\mathrm{cm}^3$. A second has mass $9$ g and volume $6\,\mathrm{cm}^3$. Ignoring any gaps between samples, what is their combined density?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $1.9\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The combined mass is $19$ g and the combined volume is $10\,\mathrm{cm}^3$. Their ratio is $\dfrac{19}{10}=1.9$ grams per cubic centimetre. The arithmetic mean of the two densities would give equal weight to unequal volumes.
