What is $199\cdot201$?

- $40000$
- $40001$
- $39800$
- $39999$
- $39998$

## Explanation

The two factors are symmetric around $200$, so they can be written as $200-1$ and $200+1$. The difference of squares gives $(200-1)(200+1)=200^2-1^2=40000-1=39999$. The identity reduces the product to a simple square and a subtraction, avoiding full multiplication.
