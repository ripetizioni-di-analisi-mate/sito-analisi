What is the complete solution set of $\dfrac1{x-2}\ge1$?

- $(2,3)$
- $(-\infty,2)$
- $[2,3]$
- $(3,+\infty)$
- $(2,3]$

## Explanation

The domain excludes $x=2$. Moving everything to the left gives $\dfrac1{x-2}-1=\dfrac{3-x}{x-2}\ge0$. For $x<2$, the ratio is negative; for $2<x<3$, it is positive; for $x>3$, it is negative. The point $3$ makes the numerator zero and is allowed, so the solution is $(2,3]$.
