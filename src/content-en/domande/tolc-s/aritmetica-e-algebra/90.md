The threshold of an instrument reading $\dfrac1{x-2}$ changes from “at least $1$” to “at least $2$”. How does the width of the interval of real inputs reaching or exceeding the threshold change?

- The interval becomes empty
- It shrinks by a factor of $4$
- It doubles
- It stays the same
- It is halved

## Explanation

A positive threshold requires $x>2$. For a threshold $s>0$ the condition is then equivalent to $0<x-2\le\dfrac{1}{s}$, so the interval has width $\dfrac{1}{s}$. Raising $s$ from $1$ to $2$ changes the width from $1$ to $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
