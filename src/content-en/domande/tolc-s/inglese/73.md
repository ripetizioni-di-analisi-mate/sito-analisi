Complete: “The student ___ notebook was found in the hall has collected it.”

- who
- which
- whose
- whom
- where

## Explanation

The notebook belongs to the student, so the relative clause expresses possession. *Whose* precedes the possessed noun *notebook*. *Who* and *whom* refer to a person as subject or object, but do not directly connect the possessor to the noun in this structure.
