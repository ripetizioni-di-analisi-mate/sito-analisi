Complete: “This is the village ___ my grandparents lived.”

- where
- whose
- who
- what
- whom

## Explanation

The relative clause describes the place where the grandparents lived. *Where* functions as *in which* here. *Who* and *whom* refer to people, while *whose* would require a possessed noun; having *village* as antecedent does not license any relative word.
