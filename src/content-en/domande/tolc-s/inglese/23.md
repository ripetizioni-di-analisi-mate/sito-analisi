Complete: “You should ___ a short break.”

- to take
- taking
- take
- takes
- took

## Explanation

*Should* is a modal and directly takes the base verb, giving *should take*. No *to* is inserted, no *-s* ending is added, and the past form is not used. The advice concerns a break to take, not an already completed action.
