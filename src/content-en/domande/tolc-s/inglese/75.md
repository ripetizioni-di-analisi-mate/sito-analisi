Complete: “___ the heavy rain, the match continued.”

- Although
- Even though
- So that
- Because it
- Despite

## Explanation

*The heavy rain* is a noun phrase, so it can be introduced by *despite*. *Although* and *even though* would require a clause, for example *it was raining*. The selected construction indicates that the match continued despite the rain.
