Which sentence means that printing the form is optional?

- You mustn't print the form.
- You must print the form.
- You don't have to print the form.
- You have to print the form.
- You are forbidden to print the form.

## Explanation

*Don't have to* denies obligation: one can choose not to print. *Mustn't* and *are forbidden to* instead express prohibition, while *must* and *have to* require printing. All are grammatical sentences, but only one preserves the meaning of optional.
