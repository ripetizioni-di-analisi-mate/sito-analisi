What does “The shelf is too high for me to reach” mean?

- I can reach the shelf comfortably.
- The shelf is lower than every other shelf.
- I must move the shelf tomorrow.
- I have already reached the shelf.
- I cannot reach the shelf because of its height.

## Explanation

*Too high ... to reach* indicates that the height exceeds what allows the speaker to reach the shelf. *For me* identifies the person affected by the limit. The sentence does not compare all other shelves or require a future move.
