Complete: “The audience is still waiting. The film hasn't started ___.”

- yet
- ago
- yesterday
- during
- for

## Explanation

In a negative present perfect sentence, final *yet* indicates that the expected event has not occurred up to now. The audience is still waiting, consistent with this meaning. *Ago* requires a preceding duration; *for* and *during* would need a following complement. *Yesterday* does not normally combine with this present perfect.
