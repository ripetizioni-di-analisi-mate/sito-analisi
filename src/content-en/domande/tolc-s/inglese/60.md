What does “We stopped walking to drink some water” mean?

- We stopped drinking and continued walking.
- We drank without interrupting our walk.
- We decided never to walk again.
- We were unable to find any water.
- We paused our walk in order to drink.

## Explanation

*Stopped walking* identifies the interrupted activity. The infinitive *to drink* expresses the purpose of the pause: drinking water. The sentence does not assert a permanent end to walking or a lack of water; it distinguishes the paused action from the action motivating the pause.
