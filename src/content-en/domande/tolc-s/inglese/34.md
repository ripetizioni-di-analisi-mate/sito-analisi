Complete to indicate that Leo accidentally injured his own hand: “Leo cut ___ while cooking.”

- herself
- his
- he
- himself
- themselves

## Explanation

The person performing the action is also the person affected, so a reflexive is needed. With Leo, identified as masculine singular, *himself* is used. *His* expresses possession and *he* is a subject; the other reflexive forms do not match the stated person.
