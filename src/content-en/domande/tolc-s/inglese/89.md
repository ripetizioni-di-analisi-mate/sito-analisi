Complete: “The project was a great ___.”

- successful
- successfully
- succeed
- success
- succeeding

## Explanation

The article *a* and adjective *great* introduce a singular noun here: *success*. *Successful* is an adjective, *successfully* an adverb and *succeed* a verb. Syntactic position determines which member of the word family completes the sentence.
