Complete: “Have you ___ tried climbing indoors?” “Yes, twice.”

- ever
- every
- ago
- yesterday
- for

## Explanation

*Ever* asks whether the experience has occurred at least once up to the present. *Twice* answers by counting two occasions. *Every* would require a noun; *ago* a duration; *yesterday* locates an event in a finished past time and does not complete this present perfect question. *For* cannot introduce participle *tried* in this position.
