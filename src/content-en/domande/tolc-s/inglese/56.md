Complete to say that the talk caused boredom and the narrator experienced it: “The talk did not hold my attention. It was ___, and I felt ___.”

- boring / bored
- bored / boring
- boring / boring
- bored / bored
- boredom / boring

## Explanation

The talk causes the emotion, so it is *boring*. The listener experiences the emotion and feels *bored*. The distinction between *-ing* and *-ed* here concerns the cause and experience of the emotion; *boredom* is a noun, not the required adjective.
