You did not understand the teacher's last sentence. Which request politely asks the teacher to explain it again?

- May I lend you my coat?
- Could you explain that again, please?
- Would you like another cup of tea?
- Can you close the door behind you?
- Could you tell me your address?

## Explanation

The situation calls for another explanation of the sentence just spoken. *Could you explain that again, please?* makes precisely that request politely. The other utterances are grammatical, but request actions or information unrelated to the comprehension difficulty.
