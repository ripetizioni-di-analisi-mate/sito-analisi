Complete with the superlative of *good*: “Of all the students in the group, Maya achieved the highest score. She had the ___ result.”

- best
- better
- good
- well
- more good

## Explanation

The result is compared with those of the whole group and is the best. The irregular superlative of *good* is *best*. *Better* is comparative; *well* is normally an adverb in this use and does not express the required highest degree before *result*.
