Complete: “Luca is back home now. He has ___ to London twice.”

- was
- went
- been
- being
- go

## Explanation

The present perfect requires *has* followed by a past participle. The participle of *be* is *been*, while *was* is a past simple form. *Been to London twice* describes two visits; *went* instead belongs to the *go* paradigm and is not a past participle.
