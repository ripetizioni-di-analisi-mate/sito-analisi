Complete: “I have finished this exercise. Please give me ___ one to practise.”

- others
- other
- each of
- another
- all

## Explanation

The speaker asks for one additional singular exercise: *another one*. *Other* alone does not normally introduce this singular countable item; *others* is already a plural form without a following noun. *Each of* would require a defined group, and *all* does not agree with *one*.
