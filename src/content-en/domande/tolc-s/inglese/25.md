Complete with a conjunction expressing contrast: “I was tired, ___ I finished the exercise.”

- because
- so
- until
- if
- but

## Explanation

The instruction specifies contrast: completion occurs despite tiredness. *But* links the two independent clauses with this meaning. *Because* would give a cause, *so* a consequence, while *until* and *if* would introduce different relationships.
