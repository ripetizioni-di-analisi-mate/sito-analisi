Complete: “This exercise is ___ than the previous one.”

- easiest
- easy
- more easier
- the easiest
- easier

## Explanation

*Than* introduces the second term of comparison. The comparative of *easy* is *easier*, changing *y* to *i*. *More easier* would add two comparative markers; *easiest* is a superlative and does not complete the given structure.
