Complete the dialogue: “___ did you leave early?” “Because I was feeling ill.”

- Where
- Whose
- How many
- Why
- Which

## Explanation

The answer introduced by *because* supplies the reason for leaving early. The question must therefore begin with *why*. *Where* would ask for a place, *whose* for a possessor and *how many* for a number: none matches the information supplied.
