Complete to express the prohibition: “The sign says ‘No photography’. You ___ take photos here.”

- don't have to
- mustn't
- should
- might
- would like to

## Explanation

*No photography* prohibits taking photos. *Mustn't* communicates that prohibition. *Don't have to* would make photos optional without forbidding them; *should* would give contrary advice, while the other expressions do not convey the sign's rule.
