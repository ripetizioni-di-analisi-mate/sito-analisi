Which word replaces *hand in* in “Please hand in your report before noon” without changing the meaning?

- rewrite
- discuss
- borrow
- hide
- submit

## Explanation

*Hand in* means deliver an assignment to its intended recipient; *submit* expresses the same action. *Before noon* sets a deadline, but does not require rewriting or discussing the text. The task is to deliver it before noon.
