What does *reliable* mean in “We need a reliable source for these figures”?

- Available only online.
- Able to be trusted.
- Using only large numbers.
- Recently opened.
- Difficult to understand.

## Explanation

A *reliable* source is trustworthy: the data it provides deserves confidence. The word does not specify publication medium, the source's age or the size of the numbers. Such characteristics might be present, but are not the adjective's meaning.
