Complete the prediction based on the visible clouds: “Look at those dark clouds. It ___ rain.”

- used to
- is going to
- managed to
- had to
- has been able to

## Explanation

*Is going to* introduces a prediction supported by present evidence, the dark clouds. With *it*, the auxiliary is *is*. The other options describe past habits, necessities or abilities and do not express this prediction in the given context.
