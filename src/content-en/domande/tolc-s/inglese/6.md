Complete the question asking about a person's preference: “___ you like coffee?”

- Do
- Does
- Are
- Is
- Has

## Explanation

*Like*, in the required sense of enjoying something, is an ordinary present simple verb. With *you*, the question requires *do* before the subject, followed by base-form *like*. *Does* is used with third-person singular subjects; *are* and *is* do not replace this auxiliary before *like*.
