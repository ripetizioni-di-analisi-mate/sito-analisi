Report Maya's words “I am tired,” spoken yesterday, using the usual tense backshift: “Maya said that she ___ tired.”

- is
- am
- was
- were
- be

## Explanation

The instruction specifies tense backshift in the past report. Present *am* becomes *was* with the new subject *she*. *Is* can occur in other reports without backshift, but does not satisfy this instruction; *were* does not agree with this singular subject here.
