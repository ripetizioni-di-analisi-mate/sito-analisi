Choose the sentence that speaks about books in general without identifying a particular group.

- The books on this shelf are expensive.
- These books are expensive.
- Books can be expensive.
- Those two books are expensive.
- My books were expensive.

## Explanation

The article-free plural *books* can refer to the general category. The other sentences delimit a group through location, a demonstrative, a number or a possessive. Cost does not change the article's function; the scope of the reference does.
