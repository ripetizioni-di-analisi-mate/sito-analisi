Read: “No cash payments after six. Card payments are accepted until closing.” Which payment is allowed after six and before closing?

- Paying only with coins.
- Paying only with banknotes.
- Paying in cash if the amount is small.
- No payment at all.
- Paying by card.

## Explanation

The restriction after six concerns *cash*, including coins and banknotes. The second sentence confirms that cards remain accepted until closing. No exception for small amounts or suspension of all payments is stated.
