Complete: “This article is different ___ the one we read last week.”

- from
- in
- at
- of
- by

## Explanation

*Different from* introduces the comparison target: here the earlier article. The other prepositions do not construct this relationship in the sentence. The sentence does not identify the respect in which the texts differ, a use that might allow *different in* followed by another expression.
