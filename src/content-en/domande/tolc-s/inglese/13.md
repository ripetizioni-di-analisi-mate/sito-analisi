Complete: “Send the form ___ Friday; we will keep your place ___ Monday.”

- until / by
- during / since
- by / until
- since / during
- while / for

## Explanation

*By Friday* sets the deadline for sending. *Until Monday* indicates how long the place will be held. Sending is an action to complete, while holding the place is a continuing situation: exchanging the prepositions does not preserve these meanings.
