Complete both expressions: “We have lived here ___ April, so we have been here ___ three months.”

- for / since
- during / since
- since / since
- since / for
- for / during

## Explanation

*April* identifies the starting point and requires *since*. *Three months* measures duration and requires *for*. The situation continues into the present, as the present perfect shows. The two prepositions are not interchangeable because they introduce different kinds of time reference.
