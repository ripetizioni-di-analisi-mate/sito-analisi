Complete: “At the moment the accident happened yesterday, I ___ for the bus.”

- am waiting
- was waiting
- waits
- will wait
- have been waited

## Explanation

Waiting is presented as ongoing at the precise moment of the past accident. The past continuous with *I* is *was waiting*. Present or future forms do not respect the reference to yesterday; *have been waited* does not construct intransitive *wait* here.
