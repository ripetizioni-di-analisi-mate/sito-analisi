Complete: “Both lamps stop working when their batteries are removed. Therefore ___ lamp works without batteries.”

- each
- every
- neither
- both
- either

## Explanation

The information excludes operation for both lamps. *Neither lamp* means not either of the two and combines with singular *lamp*. *Each* and *every* would attribute operation to each one; *both* would require a plural and give the opposite meaning.
