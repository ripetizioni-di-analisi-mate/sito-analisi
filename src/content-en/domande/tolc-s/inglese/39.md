Complete in the passive: “All the windows ___ by the caretaker yesterday.”

- are cleaning
- was cleaned
- did cleaned
- were cleaned
- have clean

## Explanation

The windows receive the action and are plural; *yesterday* places the event in the past. The passive therefore requires *were* and participle *cleaned*. *Was cleaned* has the wrong number, while the other sequences do not form the requested past passive.
