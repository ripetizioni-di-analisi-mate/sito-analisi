A fair six-sided die numbered from $1$ to $6$ is rolled once. What is the probability of an even number?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac23$
- $1$

## Explanation

The die is fair, so its six faces are equally likely. The required event contains $2$, $4$, and $6$, giving three favorable outcomes out of six possible outcomes. The ratio is $\dfrac36=\dfrac12$. The probability concerns the group of even numbers, not an individual face, which would have probability $\dfrac16$.
