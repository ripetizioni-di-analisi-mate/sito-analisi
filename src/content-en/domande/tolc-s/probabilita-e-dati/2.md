Two fair coins are tossed independently. What is the probability of at least one head?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac12$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

The complement of “at least one head” is “no heads,” meaning two tails. By independence, this event has probability $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac14$. Subtracting from one gives $\dfrac34$. This includes both sequences with exactly one head and the sequence with two heads.
