A fair coin or a coin with heads on both sides is chosen with equal probability. The same coin is tossed twice; fair-coin tosses are independent. Given that the first toss is heads, what is the probability of heads on the second?

- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac56$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac23$
- $1$

## Explanation

The first-head probability is $\dfrac12\dfrac12+\dfrac12=\dfrac34$. The probability of two heads is $\dfrac12\dfrac14+\dfrac12=\dfrac58$. Thus $P(H_2\mid H_1)=\dfrac{5/8}{3/4}=\dfrac56$. The observed head makes the double-headed coin more plausible.
