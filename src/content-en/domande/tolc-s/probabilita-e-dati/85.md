In the list $2,2,4,5,5,5,8,8$, one $5$ is replaced by a $2$. What is the mode of the new list?

- $5$
- $8$
- $4$
- There is no mode
- $2$

## Explanation

After replacement, two occurs three times, five twice, eight twice, and four once. The maximum frequency is three and is attained only by two, which is therefore the unique mode. The central position of the data is not needed to determine this measure.
