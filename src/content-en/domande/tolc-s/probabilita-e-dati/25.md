In a group of $30$ people, the percentage supporting a proposal is published as $33\%$, rounded to the nearest integer. How many people support it?

- $9$
- $11$
- $12$
- $15$
- $10$

## Explanation

Ten people out of thirty correspond to $\dfrac{10}{30}\cdot100\%=33.\overline3\%$, which rounds to $33\%$. Nine people would give $30\%$, and eleven would give about $37\%$ after rounding. The product $30\cdot0.33=9.9$ uses an already rounded percentage and is not the exact count.
