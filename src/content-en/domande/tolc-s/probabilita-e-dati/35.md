Among $30$ students, $17$ take chemistry, $14$ take biology, and $6$ take both. How many take neither activity?

- $1$
- $6$
- $11$
- $25$
- $5$

## Explanation

The number taking at least one activity is $17+14-6=25$. Subtracting the six taking both removes double counting. The complement within the thirty students contains $30-25=5$ people. The direct sum $17+14$ is not the correct size of the union.
