A menu offers two first courses and four main courses. One may order a single dish or one first course and one main course. How many different orders are possible?

- $14$
- $6$
- $8$
- $12$
- $24$

## Explanation

Single-dish orders number $2+4=6$. Two-dish orders require two successive choices and number $2\cdot4=8$. The two order types are disjoint because they contain different numbers of dishes. Their counts are therefore added: $6+8=14$.
