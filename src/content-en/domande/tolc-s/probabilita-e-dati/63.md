How many sequences of three digits, each $0$ or $1$, contain no two consecutive $1$s? Leading zeros are allowed.

- $4$
- $6$
- $5$
- $7$
- $8$

## Explanation

The allowed sequences are $000,001,010,100,101$, giving five. From the eight total sequences, exclude $011,110,111$. The sequence $111$ violates the restriction in two places but must be excluded only once. Explicit listing avoids this double counting in the complement.
