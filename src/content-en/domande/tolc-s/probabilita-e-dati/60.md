The graph shows the number of absences $x$ and the corresponding number of students $n$. What is the relative frequency of exactly one absence?

::grafico{poligoni="-0.3,0 0.3,0 0.3,5 -0.3,5;0.7,0 1.3,0 1.3,8 0.7,8;1.7,0 2.3,0 2.3,4 1.7,4;2.7,0 3.3,0 3.3,3 2.7,3" xmin=-0.7 xmax=3.7 ymin=-0.5 ymax=9 nomi="x,n" titolo="Zero to three absences, with frequencies five, eight, four, and three"}

- $\dfrac8{15}$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac12$
- $8$
- $\dfrac25$

## Explanation

The bar at one absence has height eight, representing eight students. The total is obtained by adding all the heights: $5+8+4+3=20$. The relative frequency is $\dfrac8{20}=\dfrac25$. The bar height is an absolute frequency and must be divided by the total.
