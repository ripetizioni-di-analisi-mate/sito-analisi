An integer from $1$ to $30$, inclusive, is selected uniformly. What is the probability that it is a multiple of $4$ or $6$?

- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac7{30}$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac1{15}$

## Explanation

There are seven multiples of four and five multiples of six. Common multiples are multiples of twelve, namely $12$ and $24$, so there are two. The favorable outcomes total $7+5-2=10$, and the probability is $\dfrac{10}{30}=\dfrac13$. The intersection is subtracted to avoid counting the same integers twice.
