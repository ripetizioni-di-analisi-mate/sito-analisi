The ratings are “low, low, medium, high, high”, ordered low < medium < high. Without assigning numerical scores, which measures are meaningful?

- Only the arithmetic mean
- The arithmetic mean and variance of the labels
- No measure because the categories are words
- Median and mode, but not the arithmetic mean of the labels
- The arithmetic mean is necessarily medium

## Explanation

The ordering identifies the median, “medium”. Frequencies identify two modes, “low” and “high”. An arithmetic mean requires numerical values with quantitative meaning: arbitrary category codes do not automatically define distances between the ratings.
