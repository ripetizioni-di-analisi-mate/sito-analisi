For a die roll, $A=\{1,2\}$ and $B=\{5,6\}$. Which statement correctly describes the two events?

- They are disjoint but not complements
- They are complements
- They have a nonempty intersection
- $A\cup B=\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$
- $A\subseteq B$

## Explanation

No result belongs to both sets, so they are disjoint. Their union is $\{1,2,5,6\}$, leaving out three and four. To be complements, they would have to be disjoint and cover the entire sample space. Lack of overlap alone does not satisfy this second condition.
