In a finite space of equally likely outcomes, $P(B)>0$ and $P(A\mid B)=1$. Which relation is necessary?

- $A\subseteq B$
- $A\cap B=\varnothing$
- $B\subseteq A$
- $P(A)=1$
- $P(B\mid A)=1$

## Explanation

The conditional probability is $\dfrac{|A\cap B|}{|B|}=1$, so the intersection contains every outcome in B. Hence $B\subseteq A$. A may also contain outcomes outside B and need not equal the whole space. The finite equally likely model gives every outcome positive probability.
