A biased die shows $6$ with probability $\dfrac13$. The other five faces all have the same probability. What is the probability of an odd number?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac2{15}$
- $\dfrac25$

## Explanation

The five faces other than six share the remaining probability $1-\dfrac13=\dfrac23$. Each therefore has probability $\dfrac23\div5=\dfrac2{15}$. Three faces are odd, so their total probability is $3\cdot\dfrac2{15}=\dfrac25$. The ratio three out of six does not apply to this biased die.
