Only $P(A)=P(B)=\dfrac12$ is known. What can be concluded about the independence of $A$ and $B$?

- The information is insufficient
- They are certainly independent
- They are certainly disjoint
- They are certainly the same event
- They are certainly complements

## Explanation

To check independence, one must know whether $P(A\cap B)=\dfrac14$. The individual probabilities do not determine the intersection. Outcomes of independent coins provide an independent example; choosing $B=A$ instead gives intersection probability $\dfrac12$. Both models satisfy the given information but have different relationships.
