In a database, codes $1,2,3$ denote three plant species. What can be said about the arithmetic mean of these codes?

- It is always the most frequent species
- It is always the central species
- It turns species into a continuous quantitative variable
- It measures no species quantity: it depends on the arbitrary coding
- It is unchanged under every reassignment of codes

## Explanation

The numbers are labels for categories, not measurements. Changing a species’ code may change the numerical mean without changing any observed plant. Frequencies and the mode can still be determined, and they do not depend on the numerical values chosen as labels.
