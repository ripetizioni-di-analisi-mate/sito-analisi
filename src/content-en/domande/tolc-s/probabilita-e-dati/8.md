A fair die is rolled once. What is the probability of getting $1$ or $2$?

- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac1{36}$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

In a single roll, the events “one” and “two” cannot occur together, so they are disjoint. Their probabilities are added without subtracting an intersection: $\dfrac16+\dfrac16=\dfrac13$. The product $\dfrac1{36}$ would concern a different experiment structure, with two results required successively.
