A fair die is rolled once. A win occurs on $1$ or $2$. An app has six identical prizes to assign to the die faces. The winning faces must give the same number of prizes, the other faces give none, and the average payout must be one prize per roll. How many prizes should each winning face give?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The two winning faces are disjoint and have total probability $2/6=1/3$. If each pays $v$ prizes, the average payout is $v/3$. One prize on average therefore requires $v=3$.
