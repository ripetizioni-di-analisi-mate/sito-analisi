For a fair die roll, $A$ is “the result is $1$” and $B$ is “the result is greater than $4$.” Why are $A$ and $B$ not independent?

- $P(A\cap B)=0$, whereas $P(A)P(B)>0$
- $P(A)=P(B)$
- $P(A\cup B)=1$
- Every pair of distinct events is dependent
- $P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)$

## Explanation

The events are disjoint: a face cannot simultaneously be one and greater than four. Thus $P(A\cap B)=0$. However, $P(A)P(B)=\dfrac16\cdot\dfrac26=\dfrac1{18}>0$. The independence condition is not satisfied; knowing that one occurred completely rules out the other event.
