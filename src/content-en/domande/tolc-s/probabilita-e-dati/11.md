An urn contains three red and seven blue balls. Two balls are drawn uniformly without replacement. Without knowing the first color, what is the probability that the second is red?

- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac29$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac7{10}$
- $\dfrac9{100}$

## Explanation

Add the red-red and blue-red paths: $\dfrac3{10}\dfrac29+\dfrac7{10}\dfrac39=\dfrac{27}{90}=\dfrac3{10}$. The unconditional probability for the second draw equals the initial one. The value $\dfrac29$ would apply if the first ball were known to be red.
