Three fair coins are tossed independently. Given that exactly two heads occurred, what is the probability that the first coin shows tails?

- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

Let H denote heads and T tails. The condition leaves only $HHT,HTH,THH$, all with equal weights. Only $THH$ has tails in the first position, so the probability is $\dfrac13$. The original independence of the tosses does not remove the restriction introduced by knowing the total number of heads.
