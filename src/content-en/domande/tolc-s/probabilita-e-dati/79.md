Two disjoint events have probabilities $\dfrac14$ and $\dfrac38$. What is the probability that neither occurs?

- $\dfrac58$
- $\dfrac3{32}$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac{15}{32}$

## Explanation

Because the events are disjoint, their union has probability $\dfrac14+\dfrac38=\dfrac58$. The event “neither” is the complement of the union, so it has probability $1-\dfrac58=\dfrac38$. Multiplying the complements would require independence, which does not hold here because both disjoint events have positive probability.
