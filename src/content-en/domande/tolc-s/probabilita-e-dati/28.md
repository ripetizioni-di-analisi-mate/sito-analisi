A school has $120$ morning students and $80$ afternoon students. A survey is answered by $25\%$ of the first group and $50\%$ of the second. Compared with its share of all students, how is the afternoon group represented among respondents?

- Underrepresented
- Represented in the same proportion
- Overrepresented
- Absent
- Represented by all its students

## Explanation

Afternoon students make up $\dfrac{80}{200}=\dfrac25$ of the school. Thirty morning and forty afternoon students respond, so afternoon students make up $\dfrac{40}{70}=\dfrac47$ of respondents. Since $\dfrac47>\dfrac25$, the group has greater weight in the sample than in the population. The comparison uses two different reference groups.
