A trial has success probability $\dfrac12$, and repetitions are independent. What is the smallest number of trials making the probability of at least one success at least $95\%$?

- $3$
- $4$
- $5$
- $6$
- $10$

## Explanation

After $n$ trials, the probability of no success is $2^{-n}$, so $1-2^{-n}\ge\dfrac{19}{20}$ is required. Equivalently, $2^n\ge20$. Four trials give $2^4=16$, which is insufficient; five give $2^5=32$, which suffices. Probability increases with the trial count, so five is minimal.
