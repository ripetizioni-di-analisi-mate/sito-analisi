An urn contains three red balls and two blue balls. Two independent uniform draws are made, replacing the ball and mixing after the first draw. What is the probability of two red balls?

- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac65$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac9{25}$
- $\dfrac35$

## Explanation

Replacement restores the three red balls among five before the second draw. Each draw therefore has probability $\dfrac35$ of red. Independence allows multiplication: $\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac35=\dfrac9{25}$. Adding the probabilities would not describe the requirement that both draws produce red.
