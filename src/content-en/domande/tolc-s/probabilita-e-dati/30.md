Among $50$ files, $20$ are urgent, $15$ are closed, and $6$ are both urgent and closed. A non-urgent file is selected uniformly. What is the probability that it is closed?

- $\dfrac3{25}$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac9{50}$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac3{10}$

## Explanation

There are $50-20=30$ non-urgent files. Of the fifteen closed files, six are urgent, so $15-6=9$ are closed and non-urgent. The required ratio is $\dfrac9{30}=\dfrac3{10}$. The denominator must count only the files eligible for selection, not all fifty files.
