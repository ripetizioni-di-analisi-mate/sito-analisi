One of two urns is chosen with equal probability. The first contains three red balls and one blue; the second contains one red and three blue. A ball is drawn uniformly and observed to be red. What is the probability that the first urn was chosen?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac18$

## Explanation

The first-urn-and-red path has probability $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac34=\dfrac38$. The second-urn-and-red path has probability $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac14=\dfrac18$. Among all red cases, the share from the first urn is $\dfrac{\frac38}{\frac38+\frac18}=\dfrac34$. The observation changes the initial probability of one half.
