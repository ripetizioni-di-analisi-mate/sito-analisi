Two independent fair dice are rolled. What is the probability that they show different numbers?

- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac{25}{36}$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac56$

## Explanation

There are thirty-six equally likely ordered pairs. Six pairs match, from $(1,1)$ to $(6,6)$. The complement contains $36-6=30$ pairs, with probability $\dfrac{30}{36}=\dfrac56$. Equivalently, whatever the first result, five of the six faces of the second die are different.
