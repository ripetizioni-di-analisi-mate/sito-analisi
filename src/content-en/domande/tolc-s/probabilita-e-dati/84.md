All values in a nonempty numerical list lie between $10$ and $20$, inclusive. Which value is impossible for the mean?

- $10$
- $12$
- $15$
- $22$
- $20$

## Explanation

If there are $N$ values, their sum is at least $10N$ and at most $20N$. Dividing by $N>0$ places the mean between ten and twenty. Twenty-two is therefore impossible. Every other proposed value is attainable, for example with a list whose values all equal that value.
