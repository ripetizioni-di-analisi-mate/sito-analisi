Two independent fair dice are rolled. What is the probability that their sum is $4$?

- $\dfrac1{11}$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac1{18}$
- $\dfrac1{12}$
- $\dfrac19$

## Explanation

There are thirty-six equally likely ordered pairs. A sum of four occurs with $(1,3)$, $(2,2)$, and $(3,1)$, giving three pairs. The probability is $\dfrac3{36}=\dfrac1{12}$. The eleven possible sums from two to twelve are not equally likely, so eleven cannot be used as the denominator.
