Two events satisfy $P(A)=\dfrac12$, $P(B)=\dfrac13$, and $P(A\cap B)=\dfrac16$. Which statement is correct?

- They are disjoint
- They are independent and can occur together
- They are complements
- They are the same event
- $P(A\mid B)=1$

## Explanation

The product of the probabilities is $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac13=\dfrac16$, equal to the intersection probability. This equality is the independence condition. The intersection has positive probability, so the events can occur together and are not disjoint. Also, $P(A\mid B)=\dfrac12$, not one.
