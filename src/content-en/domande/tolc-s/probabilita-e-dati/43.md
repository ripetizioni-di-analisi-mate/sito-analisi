Three people independently and uniformly choose one of four rooms. What is the probability that at least two choose the same room?

- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac58$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac1{16}$

## Explanation

There are $4^3=64$ equally likely assignments. Those using three different rooms number $4\cdot3\cdot2=24$. The requested event is the complement, with probability $1-\dfrac{24}{64}=\dfrac58$. “At least two” includes all three choosing the same room.
