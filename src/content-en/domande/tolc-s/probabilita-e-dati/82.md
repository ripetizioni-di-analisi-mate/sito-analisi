A bar chart of four disjoint categories gives absolute frequencies $3,6,4,2$ and includes all observations. How many observations are there?

- $4$
- $15$
- $6$
- $12$
- $24$

## Explanation

Absolute frequencies count observations, so the total is their sum: $3+6+4+2=15$. The categories are disjoint and exhaustive, so each observation is counted exactly once. Four is the number of categories; six is the maximum frequency, not the size of the whole sample.
