A survey classifies $80$ responses into the disjoint categories A, B, and C. The relative frequency of A is $\dfrac38$, and B has $18$ responses. How many responses belong to C?

- $30$
- $42$
- $32$
- $50$
- $62$

## Explanation

The absolute frequency of A is obtained by multiplying its relative frequency by the total: $80\cdot\dfrac38=30$. The three categories are disjoint and exhaust the responses, so C contains $80-30-18=32$ responses. This subtraction would not be justified if the categories could overlap.
