A spinner gives red with probability $\dfrac13$ on each spin, independently of the other spins. What is the probability that the first red occurs on the third spin?

- $\dfrac1{27}$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac{19}{27}$
- $\dfrac4{27}$
- $\dfrac29$

## Explanation

The first red on the third spin requires two non-red results followed by red. The non-red probability is $\dfrac23$, so independence gives the path probability $\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac13=\dfrac4{27}$. No other orders are counted because the position of the first red is fixed.
