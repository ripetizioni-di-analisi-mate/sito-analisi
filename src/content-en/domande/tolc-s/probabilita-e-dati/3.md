An urn contains three red balls and two blue balls. Two balls are drawn successively, each uniformly from those present, without replacement. What is the probability that both are red?

- $\dfrac9{25}$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The first ball is red with probability $\dfrac35$. After a first red ball, two red balls remain among four balls, so the conditional probability that the second is red is $\dfrac24$. Multiplying along this path gives $\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac24=\dfrac3{10}$. The second denominator decreases because there is no replacement.
