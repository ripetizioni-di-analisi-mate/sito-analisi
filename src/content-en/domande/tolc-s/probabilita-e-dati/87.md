It is known that $0<P(B)<1$ and $P(A\mid B)=P(A\mid B^c)=\dfrac25$. Which statement necessarily follows?

- $P(A)=\dfrac45$
- $P(A)=\dfrac25$ and A, B are independent
- A and B are disjoint
- $P(B)=\dfrac12$
- $P(A)$ is undetermined

## Explanation

Writing $p=P(B)$, total probability gives $P(A)=\dfrac25p+\dfrac25(1-p)=\dfrac25$. Conditioning on B therefore leaves A’s probability unchanged. Since $P(B)>0$, this establishes independence. The exact value of $p$ is neither needed nor determined.
