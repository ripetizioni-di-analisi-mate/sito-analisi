Two sensors detect an object independently, with probabilities $\dfrac45$ and $\dfrac34$, respectively. What is the probability that neither detects it?

- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac9{20}$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac{19}{20}$
- $\dfrac1{20}$

## Explanation

The failure-to-detect probabilities are $1-\dfrac45=\dfrac15$ and $1-\dfrac34=\dfrac14$. Independence also holds for the complements, so the probability of two failures to detect is $\dfrac15\cdot\dfrac14=\dfrac1{20}$. Subtracting the product of detection probabilities from one would instead give “not both detect.”
