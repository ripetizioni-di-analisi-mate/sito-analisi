There are three red tokens and one blue token. First a color is chosen with equal probabilities, then a token of that color is selected uniformly. What is the probability of selecting the blue token?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac16$
- $1$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The procedure chooses blue with probability $\dfrac12$. Once blue is chosen, its only token is selected with probability one. The product is therefore $\dfrac12$. The four tokens are not equally likely: each red token has probability $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac13=\dfrac16$, whereas the blue token has weight one half.
