An item is selected uniformly from all items in the table. What is the probability that it was produced in the afternoon and is defective?

| Shift | Conforming | Defective |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Morning | 36 | 4 |
| Afternoon | 28 | 12 |

- $\dfrac3{20}$
- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Selection is from all items, so the denominator is $36+4+28+12=80$. The afternoon-and-defective cell contains twelve items. The joint probability is $\dfrac{12}{80}=\dfrac3{20}$. Dividing by forty would instead calculate the defect probability given that the shift is afternoon.
