Two observations are $1$ and $3$, with mean $2$. What is the mean of their squares?

- $4$
- $2$
- $10$
- $5$
- $8$

## Explanation

The squared observations are $1$ and $9$, so their mean is $\dfrac{1+9}{2}=5$. Squaring the original mean would give $2^2=4$, a different result. For a nonaffine transformation, the mean of transformed values is generally not the transformation of the original mean.
