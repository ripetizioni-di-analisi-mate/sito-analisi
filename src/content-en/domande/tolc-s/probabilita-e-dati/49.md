A factory produces $60\%$ of its items on line A and $40\%$ on B. Of A’s items, $5\%$ are defective; of B’s, $10\%$ are defective. An item is selected uniformly from all production. What is the probability that it is defective?

- $7.5\%$
- $15\%$
- $3\%$
- $7\%$
- $4\%$

## Explanation

A defect can come from A or B, two disjoint sources. A contributes $0.60\cdot0.05=0.03$; B contributes $0.40\cdot0.10=0.04$. The total probability is $0.03+0.04=0.07=7\%$. Defect rates must be weighted by the shares of production.
