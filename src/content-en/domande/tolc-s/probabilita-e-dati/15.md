Three objects are selected without order from three red and three blue objects, all distinct. How many groups contain exactly two red objects?

- $3$
- $6$
- $18$
- $20$
- $9$

## Explanation

Two of the three red objects can be selected in $\binom32=3$ ways, and one of the three blue objects in $\binom31=3$ ways. Each red pair combines with each blue choice, so multiplication gives $3\cdot3=9$. The twenty total groups also include color compositions not requested.
