Two independent uniform draws with replacement are made from an urn containing two white and three black balls. What is the probability that the balls have the same color?

- $\dfrac{13}{25}$
- $\dfrac6{25}$
- $\dfrac{12}{25}$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac35$

## Explanation

The same color occurs with two white balls or two black balls, which are disjoint paths. Their probabilities are $\left(\dfrac25\right)^2=\dfrac4{25}$ and $\left(\dfrac35\right)^2=\dfrac9{25}$. Adding gives $\dfrac{13}{25}$. The white-black path does not satisfy the requirement and is not included.
