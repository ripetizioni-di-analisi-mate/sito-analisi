A test for an infection is carried out on $100$ people; the table shows the test result and whether the infection is present. One of these people is chosen at random. Which ratio gives the probability that the test is positive **given that the person has the infection**?

| Test | Infection present | Infection absent |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Positive | 12 | 8 |
| Negative | 3 | 77 |

- $\dfrac{12}{12+8}$
- $\dfrac{12}{12+3}$
- $\dfrac{12}{100}$
- $\dfrac{8}{8+77}$
- $\dfrac{3}{3+77}$

## Explanation

The known information restricts the choice to the people with the infection, namely the first column of the table. There are $12+3=15$ such people and twelve of them test positive, so the probability is $\dfrac{12}{12+3}$. The denominator $12+8$ instead counts the people who test positive, so it would give the probability of infection given a positive test.
