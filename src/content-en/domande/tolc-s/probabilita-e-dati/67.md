A detector signals a feature that is present or absent in samples. Which ratio gives the probability of a positive signal **given that the feature is present**, when a sample is selected uniformly?

| Signal | Feature present | Feature absent |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Positive | 12 | 8 |
| Negative | 3 | 77 |

- $\dfrac{12}{12+8}$
- $\dfrac{12}{12+3}$
- $\dfrac{12}{100}$
- $\dfrac8{8+77}$
- $\dfrac3{3+77}$

## Explanation

The known information restricts selection to samples with the feature present, namely the first column. There are $12+3=15$ such samples, and twelve have a positive signal. The ratio is therefore $\dfrac{12}{12+3}$. The denominator $12+8$ would reverse the condition, selecting among samples with a positive signal.
