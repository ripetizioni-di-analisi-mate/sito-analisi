In a histogram, the classes $[0,2)$ and $[2,5)$ have heights $6$ and $4$, respectively. Frequencies are proportional to areas. What is the ratio of the first class frequency to the second?

- $\dfrac32$
- $1$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac94$
- $\dfrac49$

## Explanation

The first class has width two and area $2\cdot6=12$. The second has width three and area $3\cdot4=12$. The frequency ratio is the area ratio, so it equals $\dfrac{12}{12}=1$. The ratio of heights is insufficient when the classes have different widths.
