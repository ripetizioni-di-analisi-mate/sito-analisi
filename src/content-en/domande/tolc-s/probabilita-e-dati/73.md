An urn contains four red balls and two blue balls. Two balls are drawn uniformly without replacement. What is the probability of different colors?

- $\dfrac4{15}$
- $\dfrac49$
- $\dfrac8{15}$
- $\dfrac7{15}$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

The valid paths are red-blue and blue-red. Their probabilities are $\dfrac46\cdot\dfrac25=\dfrac4{15}$ and $\dfrac26\cdot\dfrac45=\dfrac4{15}$. They are disjoint, so adding gives $\dfrac8{15}$. Counting only one order would halve the result; using six in the second denominator would ignore the lack of replacement.
