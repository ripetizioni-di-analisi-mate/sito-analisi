The distances traveled by ten people are given in the table. What is the mean distance?

| Distance in km | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| People | 3 | 4 | 3 |

- $\dfrac35\,\mathrm{km}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{km}$
- $6\,\mathrm{km}$
- $20\,\mathrm{km}$
- $2\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

Each distance contributes as many times as the number of people who traveled it. The sum is $1\cdot3+2\cdot4+3\cdot3=20\,\mathrm{km}$. Dividing by $3+4+3=10$ people gives $2\,\mathrm{km}$. Neither the total distance nor the number of distinct values is the requested mean.
