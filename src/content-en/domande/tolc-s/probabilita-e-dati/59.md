A numerical list has minimum $3$ and maximum $9$. Every value is multiplied by $2$. What is the range of the new list?

- $6$
- $18$
- $24$
- $12$
- $3$

## Explanation

Multiplying by a positive number preserves order: the new minimum is $2\cdot3=6$, and the new maximum is $2\cdot9=18$. The range is their difference, $18-6=12$. It is also twice the original range $9-3=6$, because all distances are scaled.
