What is the median of the data $1,2,3,4,100$?

- $3$
- $22$
- $4$
- $2$
- $50.5$

## Explanation

The five values are already in increasing order. For an odd number of values, the median is the central value, in position $\dfrac{5+1}{2}=3$, so it is $3$. The extreme value $100$ affects the mean, which is $\dfrac{110}{5}=22$, but does not change the central position in the list.
