The figure shades the parts belonging to exactly one of the two events, leaving the intersection unshaded. Which event is represented?

::venn{tipo="due" zone="a,b" titolo="The two regions outside the intersection are highlighted"}

- $A\cap B$
- $(A\cap B^c)\cup(A^c\cap B)$
- $A\cup B$
- $(A\cup B)^c$
- $A\cap B^c$

## Explanation

The A-only region is $A\cap B^c$, and the B-only region is $A^c\cap B$. The figure contains both, so their union is taken. The simple union $A\cup B$ would also include the common part, which is unshaded here. The complement of the union would instead lie outside both circles.
