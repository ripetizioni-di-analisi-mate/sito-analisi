It is known that $P(A)=\dfrac35$ and $P(B)=\dfrac7{10}$. What is the smallest possible value of $P(A\cap B)$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac1{10}$
- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac7{10}$

## Explanation

The union probability cannot exceed one. From $P(A\cup B)=\dfrac35+\dfrac7{10}-P(A\cap B)$ it follows that $P(A\cap B)\ge\dfrac3{10}$. The bound is attainable with three tenths in A only, four tenths in B only, and three tenths in the intersection. These disjoint parts sum to one.
