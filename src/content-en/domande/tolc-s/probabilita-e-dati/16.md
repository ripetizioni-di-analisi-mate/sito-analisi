A coin gives heads H or tails T. The probability of H is $\dfrac23$ on independent tosses. Which specific three-toss sequence is most likely?

- HHH
- HTH
- HTT
- THT
- TTT

## Explanation

A sequence with $h$ heads has probability $\left(\dfrac23\right)^h\left(\dfrac13\right)^{3-h}=\dfrac{2^h}{27}$. No counting coefficient is added for one specific sequence. This value increases with $h$, so HHH, with three heads, has the largest probability $\dfrac8{27}$.
