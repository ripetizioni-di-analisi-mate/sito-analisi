In a questionnaire allowing multiple responses, $100$ people indicate a total of $130$ preferences among several activities. Which conclusion is correct?

- At least one person selected more than one activity
- The count must be wrong
- Every person selected exactly two activities
- The categories must be disjoint
- The percentage of people interested in each individual activity exceeds 100%

## Explanation

If each person selected at most one activity, there would be at most one hundred preferences. A total of one hundred thirty therefore implies that at least one person gave multiple responses. It does not determine how many people did so. Percentages can sum to more than one hundred because the categories overlap.
