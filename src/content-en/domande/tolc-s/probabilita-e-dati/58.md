The table gives books read in one month. What fraction of the people read at least two books?

| Books | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| People | 2 | 6 | 8 | 4 |

- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac45$

## Explanation

“At least two” includes both two and three books. The relevant people total $8+4=12$, while the overall total is $2+6+8+4=20$. The required fraction is $\dfrac{12}{20}=\dfrac35$. Counting only the eight people who read two books would turn the inclusive threshold into “exactly two.”
