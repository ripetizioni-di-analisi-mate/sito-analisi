An urn contains three red and four blue balls. Two balls are drawn uniformly without replacement. Given that the second is blue, what is the probability that the first is also blue?

- $\dfrac47$
- $\dfrac37$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac27$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

The probability of two blue balls is $\dfrac47\cdot\dfrac36=\dfrac27$. By symmetry of the positions, the probability that the second is blue is $\dfrac47$. The conditional ratio is therefore $\dfrac{\frac27}{\frac47}=\dfrac12$. Knowing that one position contains a blue ball leaves three blue balls among the other six.
