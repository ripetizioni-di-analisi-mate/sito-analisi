The share enrolled in an activity rises from $20\%$ to $25\%$. By how many percentage points does it increase?

- $25$
- $5$
- $20$
- $1.25$
- $45$

## Explanation

Percentage points measure the difference between two percentages: $25-20=5$. The relative increase over the initial share would instead be $\dfrac{25-20}{20}=\dfrac14$, or $25\%$. The question asks for the first measure, expressed in percentage points, not the ratio to the initial value.
