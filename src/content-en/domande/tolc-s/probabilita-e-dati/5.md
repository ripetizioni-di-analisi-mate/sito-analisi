A fair six-sided die is rolled. Given that the result is even, what is the probability that it is $6$?

- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac23$
- $1$
- $\dfrac13$

## Explanation

The information “even” restricts the possible outcomes to $\{2,4,6\}$. These three outcomes retain equal weights because the die faces are equally likely. Only one satisfies the requirement, so the conditional probability is $\dfrac13$. Using six in the denominator would ignore the information already available.
