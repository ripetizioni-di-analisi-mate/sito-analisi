Which variable is continuous quantitative in the usual measurement model?

- The number of leaves on a plant
- The number of seeds in a fruit
- The species of a plant
- The color of a flower
- The mass of a sample

## Explanation

Mass is a measurement that can take any value in an interval in the model, even though the instrument records it with finite precision. Leaves and seeds are counted, giving discrete quantitative variables. Species and color instead identify qualitative categories rather than measured quantities.
