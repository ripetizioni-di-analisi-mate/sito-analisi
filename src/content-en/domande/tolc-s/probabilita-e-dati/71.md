The table gives successes and attempts for two methods on easy and hard trials. Which statement is correct?

| Method | Easy trials | Hard trials |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| A | 9 successes out of 10 | 1 success out of 10 |
| B | 72 successes out of 90 | 0 successes out of 1 |

- A has a higher rate in each category, but B has the higher overall rate
- A has the higher overall rate
- B has a higher rate in each category
- The overall rates are equal
- Overall rates are obtained by adding the two category percentages

## Explanation

On easy trials, A has $\dfrac9{10}>\dfrac{72}{90}$; on hard trials, it has $\dfrac1{10}>0$. Overall, however, A has $\dfrac{10}{20}=\dfrac12$, while B has $\dfrac{72}{91}>\dfrac12$. B attempts almost exclusively easy trials. Different trial compositions can reverse the aggregate comparison without contradicting the within-category comparisons.
