Let $B$ be an event with $P(B)>0$. What is $P(B\mid B)$ always equal to?

- $1$
- $0$
- $\dfrac12$
- $P(B)^2$
- $1-P(B)$

## Explanation

Knowing that B occurred makes B certain in the new reference space. By definition, $P(B\mid B)=\dfrac{P(B\cap B)}{P(B)}=\dfrac{P(B)}{P(B)}=1$. The condition $P(B)>0$ ensures that the ratio is defined. No independence assumption is necessary.
