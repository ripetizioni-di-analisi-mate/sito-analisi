A chart compares the values $90$ and $100$, but its vertical axis starts at $80$. Which statement correctly distinguishes data from visible heights?

- The second value is twice the first
- The two values are equal
- Visible heights are in the ratio 1 to 2, while the data are in the ratio 9 to 10
- The difference between the values is 20
- The axis origin never affects ratios between visible heights

## Explanation

The bar portions above the chosen baseline have lengths proportional to $90-80=10$ and $100-80=20$, so one appears twice the other. The original values instead have ratio $\dfrac{90}{100}=\dfrac9{10}$. Subtracting the same amount preserves differences but not ratios, which is why the axis origin must be checked.
