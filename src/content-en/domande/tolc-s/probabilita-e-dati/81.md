A fair coin is tossed independently each time. The first ten tosses produced seven heads. What is the probability of heads on the eleventh toss?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac7{10}$
- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac3{11}$
- $\dfrac7{11}$

## Explanation

Fairness assigns probability one half to heads on each toss. Independence ensures that earlier results do not change this probability. The value $\dfrac7{10}$ is the relative frequency observed in the first ten tosses; it does not replace the theoretical probability of the next trial in the stated model.
