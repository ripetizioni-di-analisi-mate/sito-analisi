One hundred people are invited to answer yes or no. Sixty respond: $45$ yes and $15$ no. Without assumptions about the $40$ nonrespondents, what are the smallest and largest possible favorable percentages among all invitees?

- $75\%$ and $75\%$
- $45\%$ and $60\%$
- $15\%$ and $75\%$
- $0\%$ and $100\%$
- $45\%$ and $85\%$

## Explanation

The $45$ favorable responses are known. If every nonrespondent were opposed, the share would be $\dfrac{45}{100}=45\%$; if all were favorable, it would be $\dfrac{45+40}{100}=85\%$. The $75\%$ describes respondents only and does not determine the invitee share.
