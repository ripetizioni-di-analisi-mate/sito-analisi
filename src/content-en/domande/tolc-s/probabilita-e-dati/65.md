Four independent fair coins are tossed. What is the probability of exactly three heads?

- $\dfrac1{16}$
- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

Three heads imply exactly one tail. The tail can occupy any of the four positions, giving four favorable sequences. Each sequence has probability $\left(\dfrac12\right)^4=\dfrac1{16}$. Adding the four probabilities gives $\dfrac4{16}=\dfrac14$.
