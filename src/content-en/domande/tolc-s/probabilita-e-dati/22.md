It is known that $P(A)=\dfrac12$, $P(B)=\dfrac25$, and $P(A\cup B)=\dfrac7{10}$. What is $P(A\cap B)$?

- $\dfrac9{10}$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac1{10}$
- $\dfrac7{20}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The union formula gives $P(A\cap B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cup B)$. Substitution yields $\dfrac5{10}+\dfrac4{10}-\dfrac7{10}=\dfrac2{10}=\dfrac15$. The intersection is precisely the part counted twice in the sum of the individual probabilities. The result does not exceed either probability, as required.
