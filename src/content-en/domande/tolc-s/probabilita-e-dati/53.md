Seven ordered values have median $10$. One value smaller than all the originals and one larger than all the originals are added. What is the new median?

- $5$
- $20$
- $10$
- The mean of the two new values
- It cannot be determined

## Explanation

With seven values, the median is the fourth value. Inserting a new minimum shifts that value from fourth to fifth position; adding a new maximum does not change its position further. The new list has nine values, whose central position is fifth. The median therefore remains ten.
