The four values are ordered as $2,7,x,15$, with $7\le x\le15$. If the median is $9$, what is $x$?

- $9$
- $7$
- $13$
- $11$
- $15$

## Explanation

The two central values are seven and $x$, so the median satisfies $\dfrac{7+x}{2}=9$. Multiplying by two gives $7+x=18$, hence $x=11$. This value satisfies the ordering condition $7\le x\le15$, so the two values used are indeed central.
