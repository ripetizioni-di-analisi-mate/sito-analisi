To travel to school, Luca chooses the bus with probability $\dfrac25$ and the train otherwise. The delay probability is $\dfrac14$ by bus and $\dfrac16$ by train. What is the probability that he chooses the train and arrives late?

- $\dfrac1{10}$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac1{15}$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac{23}{30}$

## Explanation

The probability of choosing the train is $1-\dfrac25=\dfrac35$. The delay probability by train is already conditional on that choice. Thus the two factors along the path are multiplied: $\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac16=\dfrac1{10}$. The bus delay probability is not part of the requested event.
