A person is selected uniformly from those present in the table. What is the probability that the person belongs to course B?

| Course | Present | Absent |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| A | 12 | 8 |
| B | 9 | 11 |

- $\dfrac9{20}$
- $\dfrac37$
- $\dfrac9{40}$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac{11}{19}$

## Explanation

The selection concerns only those present, totaling $12+9=21$. Nine of them belong to B. The ratio is therefore $\dfrac9{21}=\dfrac37$. Dividing by twenty would answer a question about attendance within course B; dividing by forty would instead use all enrolled students.
