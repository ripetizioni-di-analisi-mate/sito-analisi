Three events A, B, C are disjoint and cover all outcomes. Proposed probabilities are $0.4$, $0.5$, and $0.3$. Why is the assignment invalid?

- One probability is negative
- Each probability must equal one third
- The probabilities sum to $1.2$ rather than $1$
- Probabilities must be integers
- Disjoint events cannot have positive probability

## Explanation

For a partition of the sample space, the probabilities must sum to $P(\Omega)=1$. Here $0.4+0.5+0.3=1.2$, so this condition fails. Having every number between zero and one is necessary but insufficient: the total must also be consistent.
