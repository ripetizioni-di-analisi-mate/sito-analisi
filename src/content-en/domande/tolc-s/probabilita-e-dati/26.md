Two groups contain three observations each and have medians $2$ and $8$ respectively. What is the median of the six combined observations?

- It is not determined by the given information
- $5$
- $2$
- $8$
- $10$

## Explanation

The groups $0,2,3$ and $7,8,9$ give combined median $\dfrac{3+7}{2}=5$. The groups $0,2,7$ and $8,8,9$ keep the same original medians but give combined median $\dfrac{7+8}{2}=7.5$. Different results with the same summaries establish that the answer is undetermined.
