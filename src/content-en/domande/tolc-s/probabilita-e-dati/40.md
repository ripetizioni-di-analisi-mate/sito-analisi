A list of three real numbers has mean $6$. With no further information, which statement is necessarily true?

- The median is 6
- All the numbers are positive
- One of the numbers is 6
- The maximum is 12
- The sum of the three numbers is 18

## Explanation

The sum is the mean multiplied by the number of values: $3\cdot6=18$. The other information does not follow from the mean alone. For example, the list $0,8,10$ has mean six, median eight, no value six, and maximum ten. Negative values are also possible, as in the list $-2,9,11$.
