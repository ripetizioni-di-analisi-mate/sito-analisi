Three fair coins are tossed independently. What is the probability that the number of heads is odd?

- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

An odd number of heads can be one or three. There are three sequences with one head and one with three heads, totaling four out of eight equally likely sequences. The probability is $\dfrac48=\dfrac12$. Zero heads is even and is not included.
