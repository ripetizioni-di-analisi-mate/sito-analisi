Let $A\subseteq B$ and $P(A)=\dfrac7{10}$. Which value can $P(B)$ take?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac45$
- $\dfrac23$
- $-\dfrac1{10}$
- $\dfrac{11}{10}$

## Explanation

Since B contains A, $P(B)\ge P(A)=\dfrac7{10}$. Every probability must also belong to $[0,1]$. Only $\dfrac45$ satisfies both restrictions. It can be realized by adding to A a disjoint region with probability $\dfrac1{10}$.
