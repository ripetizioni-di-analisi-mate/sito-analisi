Two independent fair dice are rolled, distinguishing the first from the second. Given that the first shows $2$, what is the probability that the sum is $7$?

- $\dfrac1{36}$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac5{36}$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

With the first die fixed at two, the sum is seven only if the second shows five. By independence, knowing the first result does not change the six probabilities for the second die. The required probability is therefore $\dfrac16$. The denominator is not thirty-six because the first outcome is already known.
