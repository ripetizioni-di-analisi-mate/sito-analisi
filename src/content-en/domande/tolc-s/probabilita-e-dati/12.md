Among ten distinct tickets, one is special. A pair of tickets is selected uniformly without replacement. What is the probability that the pair contains the special ticket?

- $\dfrac1{10}$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac19$
- $\dfrac2{45}$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

There are $\binom{10}{2}=45$ possible pairs. A favorable pair contains the special ticket and any one of the other nine, so there are nine favorable pairs. The probability is $\dfrac9{45}=\dfrac15$. The special ticket can occupy either of the two positions in an equivalent ordered draw.
