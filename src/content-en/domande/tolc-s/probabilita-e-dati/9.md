An event has probability $0.15$. What is the probability that it does not occur?

- $0.15$
- $1.15$
- $0.75$
- $0.85$
- $0.95$

## Explanation

The event and its complement are disjoint and together cover every possible outcome. Their probabilities must therefore sum to one. The required probability is $1-0.15=0.85$. The result is greater than one half, consistently with the original event being less likely than its complement.
