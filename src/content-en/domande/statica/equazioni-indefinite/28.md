A cantilever is fixed at $x = 0$ and is free and unloaded in $x = L$; on it acts a distributed load $q(x)$. Which conditions determine the two constants obtained by integrating $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -q$ and $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T$?

- $M(0) = 0$ and $M(L) = 0$
- $T(L) = 0$ and $M(L) = 0$
- $T(0) = 0$ and $M(0) = 0$
- $T(0) = 0$ and $M(L) = 0$

## Explanation

At the unloaded free end no forces or couples act, so the shear and the moment are zero, $T(L) = 0$ and $M(L) = 0$. Fixed support transmits any shear and any moment, so in $x = 0$ it doesn't give conditions. The $T(0)$ and $M(0)$ values are obtained at the end and give the fixed support reactions. Conditions $M(0) = 0$ and $M(L) = 0$ are those of the simply supported beam at the ends.
