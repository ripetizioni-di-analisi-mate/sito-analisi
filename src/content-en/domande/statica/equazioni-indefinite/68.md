A simply supported beam of span $L$ carries the triangular load $q(x) = \dfrac{q_0\,x}{L}$, zero in the $A$ ($x = 0$) mount and equal to $q_0$ in the $B$ mount. Which statement about the diagrams is true?

- The shear diagram has a horizontal tangent at $B$
- The shear diagram has a horizontal tangent at $A$
- The moment diagram has a horizontal tangent at $A$
- The shear is zero at $A$

## Explanation

The slope of the shear diagram is $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -q$, and in $A$ the load is zero, so the shear diagram has horizontal tangent in $A$. In $B$ the same slope equals $-q_0$, which is different from zero. The moment slope in $A$ is $T(0^+) = V_A = \dfrac{q_0 L}{6}$, different from zero, so the moment has in $A$ tangent inclined. For the same reason the $A$ shear equals $\dfrac{q_0 L}{6}$.
