The beam shown has a pin at $A$ ($x = 0$) and a roller support at $B$, span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the linear load $q(x) = 6 - 2x$ ($\mathrm{kN/m}$, with $x$ in meters), which equals $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ down in $A$, vanishes at midspan and equals $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ up in $B$. What is the bending moment at midspan?

- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The load has zero resultant, and its clockwise moment about $A$ is $\displaystyle\int_0^6 (6 - 2x)\,x\,dx = 108 - 144 = -36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $6\,V_B + 36 = 0$, then $V_B = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, and the vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward. On the left half the load is triangular, from $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $0$, with the resultant $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ applied $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, i.e. to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ by the midspan. Therefore $M(3) = 6 \cdot 3 - 9 \cdot 2 = 0$. The value $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ neglects the load of the left half.
