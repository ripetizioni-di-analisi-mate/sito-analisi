A simply supported beam carries a uniform load on the left half and no load on the right half. What is the shape of the bending moment diagram over the right half?

- A straight line segment
- Part of a parabola
- A constant segment
- Part of a cubic curve

## Explanation

On the right half $q = 0$, then for the equation $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -q$ the shear is constant. For the equation $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T$ the moment is then a first degree polynomial, and the diagram is a segment of line that vanishes to the right-hand support. The moment is not constant, because on the right half the shear equals $-V_B$, other than zero. The parabola arch is the shape of the diagram on the loaded half.
