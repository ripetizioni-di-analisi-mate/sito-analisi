A beam has a pin at $A$ ($x = 0$) and a vertical reaction roller support at $B$ ($x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$), and only carries the horizontal force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, directed to the right and applied on the axis at section $C$ ($x = 1\,\mathrm{m}$). What is the axial force in segment $AC$?

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, in compression
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension

## Explanation

Roller support at $B$ does not transmit horizontal forces, so at the end $B$ it equals $N = 0$. In the segment $CB$, no axial loads act, therefore $N = 0$ throughout the segment. For the $N(C^+) - N(C^-) = -F_x = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$ jump rule, then $N(C^-) = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, positive, i.e. tension, throughout the section $AC$. The force is held back by the hinge $A$, which exerts the reaction $H_A = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left.
