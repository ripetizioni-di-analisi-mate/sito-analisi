The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries only the couple $\mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise, applied in the $A$ support. What is the bending moment at midspan?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="A,1,ℳ" quote="A,B,4 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at support $A$"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

At the end left, with a counterclockwise couple applied, the boundary condition is $M(0^+) = -\mathcal{M} = -8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$; in the $B$ support, no couples, equals $M(4) = 0$. The beam is unloaded, so the moment is linear and passes through the two values, $M(x) = -8 + 2x$. Therefore at midspan $M(2) = -4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and the fibers in tension are the upper ones. The constant $T = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ shear gives the reaction $V_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward.
