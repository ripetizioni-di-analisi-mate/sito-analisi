In a segment of beam with no concentrated forces or couples and no distributed couples the shear is negative and grows proceeding to the right. Which statement is true?

- The moment increases and the load acts upward
- The moment decreases and the load acts downward
- The moment increases and the load acts downward
- The moment decreases and the load acts upward

## Explanation

Since $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T < 0$, the moment decreases by proceeding to the right. Since $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -q$ and the shear grows, you have $-q > 0$, i.e. $q < 0$. The load is positive downward, so in the segment it is directed upward, like ground pressure on a foundation beam.
