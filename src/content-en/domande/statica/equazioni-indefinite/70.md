The beam shown is fixed at $A$ ($x = 0$), has a roller support at $B$ ($x = 8\,\mathrm{m}$) and carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. The beam is once statically indeterminate; with a condition on the deformation the reaction of the roller support was obtained, $V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. What is the maximum positive bending moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.7,0.7,q" quote="A,B,8 m,1" titolo="Beam fixed at $A$ and simply supported at $B$ under uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

In roller support of $M(8) = 0$ and $T(8^-) = -V_B = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$ ends. By integrating $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -2$ you get $T(x) = -6 + 2\,(8 - x) = 10 - 2x$, which you undo in $x = 5\,\mathrm{m}$. Since $q > 0$, at this point the moment has a relative maximum, and for the integral form
$$
M(5) = M(8) - \int_5^8 (10 - 2x)\,dx = 0 - (-9) = 9\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
At the fixed end the moment equals $M(0) = -16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, negative, so the positive maximum is $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, i.e. $\dfrac{9\,qL^2}{128}$. The value $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$ of the simply supported beam, and the value $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ neglects the load between the section and the roller support.
