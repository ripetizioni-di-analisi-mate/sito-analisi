The beam shown has the free end $A$ ($x = 0$), a pin at $B$ ($x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$) and a roller support at $C$ ($x = 6\,\mathrm{m}$), and carries the uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ only on the $AB$ overhang. What is the bending moment at the midpoint of span $BC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="B,cerniera;C,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,2 m,1;B,C,4 m,1" titolo="Beam with a loaded overhang and an unloaded span"}

- $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The $AB$ overhang has the free end unloaded in $A$, then $T(0) = 0$ and $M(0) = 0$; By integrating $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -4$ and $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T$ you get $M(x) = -2x^2$, so $M(B) = -8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. On the span $BC$ the load is zero, so the shear is constant and the moment is linear, from $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $B$ to $0$ in the roller support at $C$. At midspan, $x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, the moment is the average of the two values, $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension.
