The beam in the figure has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ and span $8\,\mathrm{m}$. It carries the uniformly distributed load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the maximum bending moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.8,0.8,q" quote="A,B,8 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam under uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $96\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Integrating $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -3$ and $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T$ gives $M(x) = c_2 + c_1 x - \dfrac{3}{2}\,x^2$. The boundary conditions $M(0) = 0$ and $M(8) = 0$ give $c_2 = 0$ and $c_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, so $T(x) = 12 - 3x$. The shear force vanishes at $x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$. Since $q > 0$, the bending moment reaches its maximum there:
$$
M(4) = 12 \cdot 4 - \frac{3}{2} \cdot 16 = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m} = \frac{qL^2}{8} .
$$
The value $48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ corresponds to $\dfrac{qL^2}{4}$, and $96\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ corresponds to $\dfrac{qL^2}{2}$.
