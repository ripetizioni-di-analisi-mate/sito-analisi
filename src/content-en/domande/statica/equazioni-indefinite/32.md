The beam shown has a pin at $A$ ($x = 0$) and a roller support at $B$ ($x = 6\,\mathrm{m}$) and carries the uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$; in $A$ the counterclockwise $\mathcal{M}_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ couple is applied, in $B$ the clockwise $\mathcal{M}_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ couple. How far from $A$ is the first section at which the bending moment vanishes?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" coppie="A,1,ℳ_A;B,-1,ℳ_B" quote="A,B,6 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and two couples of magnitude $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at the supports"}

- $3 - \sqrt{6} \approx 0.55\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3 - \sqrt{3} \approx 1.27\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The counterclockwise couple to the left end gives $M(0) = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, the clockwise couple to the right end gives $M(6) = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Integrating $\dfrac{d^2M}{dx^2} = -4$ gives $M(x) = -2x^2 + c_1 x + c_2$, and the two conditions give $c_2 = -12$ and $c_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$. Therefore
$$
M(x) = 12x - 2x^2 - 12 ,
$$
which vanishes by $x^2 - 6x + 6 = 0$, i.e. in $x = 3 \pm \sqrt{3}$. The closest section to $A$ is therefore $x = 3 - \sqrt{3} \approx 1.27\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $3 - \sqrt{6}$ is obtained by writing the moment of load as $q\,x\,(L - x)$, without the $\dfrac{1}{2}$ factor.
