In a simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$, with a pin at $A$ ($x = 0$) and roller support at $B$, the bending moment is
$$
M(x) = \frac{x\,(16 - x^2)}{2} \quad (\mathrm{kN\,m}) .
$$
What load $q(x)$, expressed in $\mathrm{kN/m}$, acts on the beam?

- $q(x) = -3x$
- $q(x) = 3x$
- $q(x) = 6x$
- $q(x) = 8 - \dfrac{3}{2}\,x^2$

## Explanation

The moment is $M(x) = 8x - \dfrac{x^3}{2}$, so the shear is $T(x) = \dfrac{dM}{dx} = 8 - \dfrac{3}{2}\,x^2$. From the equation $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -q$ follows $q(x) = 3x$, positive, i.e. downward-directed. The load is therefore triangular, zero in $A$ and equal to $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in $B$. The $-3x$ function is the second derivative of the moment, taken without the minus sign of the equation $\dfrac{d^2M}{dx^2} = -q$, and $8 - \dfrac{3}{2}\,x^2$ is the shear.
