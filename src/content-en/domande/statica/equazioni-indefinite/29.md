A Gerber beam is fixed at $A$ ($x = 0$), has a unloaded internal pin at $C$ ($x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$) and a roller support at $B$ ($x = 7\,\mathrm{m}$), and carries a uniform load over the entire length. What condition does the internal hinge impose when integrating the differential equilibrium equations?

- $T(3) = 0$
- The shear jumps at $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $M(3) = 0$
- $M(3) = 0$ and $T(3) = 0$

## Explanation

The internal hinge does not transmit moment, so in its section the bending moment is zero, $M(3) = 0$. The hinge transmits forces, so the shear at $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ is generally different from zero. No concentrated forces are applied in the hinge, so the shear is also continuous there. Along with the $M(7) = 0$ condition in roller support, the $M(3) = 0$ condition determines the two integration constants.
