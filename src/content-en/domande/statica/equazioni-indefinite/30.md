A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ carries two concentrated forces, in $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, and no distributed load. How many integration constants arise when the differential equilibrium equations are integrated piecewise?

- $6$
- $2$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The concentrated forces divide the beam into three sections, $[0, 2]$, $[2, 4]$ and $[4, 6]$. In each segment the integration of $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -q$ and $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T$ introduces two constants, so the constants are $3 \cdot 2 = 6$. The conditions are also six, i.e. $M(0) = 0$, $M(6) = 0$ and, under each force, the continuity of the moment and the leap of the shear. The number of conditions is therefore equal to the number of constants, as in each statically determinate beam.
