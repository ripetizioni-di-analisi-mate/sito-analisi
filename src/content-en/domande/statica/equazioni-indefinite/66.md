The beam shown is pinned at $A$ and supported by a roller at $B$ over a span of $5\,\mathrm{m}$. Directly beneath section $C$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, a short cantilever bracket $CE$ of length $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ is welded to the beam. A vertical load is suspended from its end $E$ and has magnitude $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$. What are the jumps at $C$ in shear force and bending moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0;E,2,-0.5" aste="A-B;C-E" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="E,0,-1,P" quote="A,C,2 m,1.2;C,B,3 m,1.2;C,E,0.5 m,0.4" titolo="Simply supported beam with load $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ suspended from bracket $CE$"}

- $\Delta T = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $\Delta M = -5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $\Delta T = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $\Delta M = 5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $\Delta T = 0$ and $\Delta M = -5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $\Delta T = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $\Delta M = 0$

## Explanation

The load at $E$ is carried in the section $C$ of the axle with its transport couple. The line of action of $P$ is vertical and passes through $C$, so the moment arm about $C$ is zero and the transport couple is zero. Therefore in $C$ only the force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. For the rule of jumps the shear jumps by $-P = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the moment is continuous, $\Delta M = 0$, with a corner. The value $\pm 5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the product of $P$ for the length of the cantilever, which would be the transport couple of a horizontal force.
