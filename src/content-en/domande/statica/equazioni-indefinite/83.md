In a beam segment with no concentrated forces or couples and no distributed couples, the bending moment diagram has an inflection point in $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. Which statement is true?

- The shear is zero at $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$
- The moment is zero at $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$
- The shear jumps at $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$
- The distributed load is zero at $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In the segment is $\dfrac{d^2M}{dx^2} = -q$. At an inflection point the second derivative of $M$ changes sign and, being continuous, vanishes, then $q(2) = 0$. The load changes sign to $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, so the concavity of the diagram reverses. The zero shear instead identifies a horizontal tangent point of the moment, and in a segment without concentrated forces or couples the shear is continuous.
