A simply supported beam carries a uniform load down, and the bending moment diagram is drawn on the side of the fibers in tension. What is the shape of the plotted curve?

- A parabolic arc concave downward, in the direction of the load
- Two straight segments meeting at a corner at midspan
- A parabolic arc concave upward, against the load
- A cubic curve with the point farthest from the axis near a support

## Explanation

With uniform load the moment is a quadratic polynomial, and since $\dfrac{d^2M}{dx^2} = -q < 0$ the $M$ function is concave. The moment is positive and it puts the lower fibers in tension, so the diagram is drawn below the axis, with positive values down. Drawn in this way, the curve has the concavity facing upward, i.e. against the load, with the lowest point at midspan. The two segments with the corner are the diagram of a force concentrated at midspan.
