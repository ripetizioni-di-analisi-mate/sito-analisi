The Gerber beam shown is fixed at $A$, it has an internal pin at $C$ $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and a roller support at $B$ $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$; carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire length. What is the reaction moment $M_A$ at the fixed support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,C,2 m,1;C,B,3 m,1" titolo="Gerber beam under uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

The internal hinge $C$ does not transmit moment, so the segment $CB$ behaves like a simply supported beam in $C$ and $B$, of span $3\,\mathrm{m}$. The force that the segment $CB$ transmits in $C$ is therefore $\dfrac{2 \cdot 3}{2} = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is directed downward on the segment $AC$. On the segment $AC$ moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $M_A - 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 1 - 3 \cdot 2 = 0$, then $M_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise. The differential equilibrium equations give the same value, because $M(x) = -10 + 7x - x^2$ satisfies $M(2) = 0$ and $M(5) = 0$ and equals $-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at the fixed end.
