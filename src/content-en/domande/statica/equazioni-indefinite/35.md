The cantilever shown is fixed at $A$ ($x = 0$), is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ long and only carries the uniform distributed couple $m = 2\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$ counterclockwise. What is the bending moment at the fixed end?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" coppie="0.75 0,1;1.5 0,1,m;2.25 0,1" quote="A,B,3 m,0.6" titolo="Cantilever under distributed counterclockwise couple $m = 2\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$"}

- $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The free end $B$ is unloaded, so $T(3) = 0$ and $M(3) = 0$. There are no transverse loads, so the shear is constant and zero on the whole cantilever. The third differential equilibrium equation then gives $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T - m = -2$, so the moment is linear and $M(x) = 2\,(3 - x)$. At the fixed end $M(0) = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, positive, i.e. with the lower fibers in tension. The value $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by the equation $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T$, which neglects the distributed couple.
