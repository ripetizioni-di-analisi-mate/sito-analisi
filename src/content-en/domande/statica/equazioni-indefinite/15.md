On a horizontal beam acts a distributed $p = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ axial load, directed to the right and applied on the top edge, at a distance of $e = 0.25\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis. When the load is transferred to the axis, what distributed couple $m$ must be added?

- $1\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$, counterclockwise
- $0.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$, clockwise
- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$, clockwise
- $1\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

A rightward force $p\,dx$ applied a distance $e$ above the axis has moment $-p\,e\,dx$ about the point on the axis directly below it, because it tends to rotate the beam clockwise. Carrying the force on the axis then adds the $m\,dx = -p\,e\,dx$ transport couple. Therefore $m = -4 \cdot 0.25 = -1\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$, i.e. a clockwise distributed couple of $1\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$. The value $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$ is obtained by dividing $p$ by $e$ instead of multiplying them.
