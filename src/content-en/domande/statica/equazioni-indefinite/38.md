A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, with $A$ hinge and $B$ roller support, carries the load $q(x) = 5\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{6}$ ($\mathrm{kN/m}$, with $x$ in meters measured by $A$). What is the value of the upward reaction $V_A$?

- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $\dfrac{60}{\pi} \approx 19.10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $\dfrac{30}{\pi} \approx 9.55\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $\dfrac{180}{\pi^2} \approx 18.24\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

By integrating $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -q$ you get $T(x) = \dfrac{30}{\pi}\cos\dfrac{\pi x}{6} + c_1$, and by integrating $M(x) = \dfrac{180}{\pi^2}\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{6} + c_1 x + c_2$ again. Conditions $M(0) = 0$ and $M(6) = 0$ give $c_2 = 0$ and $c_1 = 0$. Therefore $V_A = T(0) = \dfrac{30}{\pi} \approx 9.55\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $\dfrac{60}{\pi}\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the resultant of the load, which by symmetry divides in half between the two supports, and $\dfrac{180}{\pi^2}$ is the maximum moment at $\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
