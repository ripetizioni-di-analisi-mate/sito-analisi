In a beam segment with no concentrated forces or couples, a uniform downward load acts, and the shear goes from $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left end of the segment to $-6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right end. Which statement about the bending moment in the segment is true?

- The moment has a local minimum where the shear vanishes
- The moment varies linearly and decreases
- The moment has a corner where the shear vanishes
- The moment has a local maximum where the shear vanishes

## Explanation

With the uniform load the shear is linear, so it vanishes at a point inside the segment, and for the equation $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T$ at that point the moment has horizontal tangent. To the left of point $T > 0$ and the moment grows, to the right $T < 0$ and the moment decreases, therefore the point is of relative maximum. The same result follows from $\dfrac{d^2M}{dx^2} = -q < 0$. A corner of the moment appears under a concentrated force, where the shear has a jump.
