The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries a triangular load that equals $q_0 = 12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in $A$ and vanishes in $B$. At which section is the bending moment greatest?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.9,0,q_0" quote="A,B,6 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam under a triangular load of intensity $q_0 = 12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$"}

- $x = 6 - 2\sqrt{3} \approx 2.54\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = 2\sqrt{3} \approx 3.46\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The load is $q(x) = 12 - 2x$, so by integrating $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -q$ and $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T$ with $M(0) = 0$ you get $M(x) = c_1 x - 6x^2 + \dfrac{x^3}{3}$. The $M(6) = 0$ condition gives $6c_1 - 216 + 72 = 0$, therefore $c_1 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $T(x) = 24 - 12x + x^2$. The shear vanishes in the root of the equation $x^2 - 12x + 24 = 0$ between $0$ and $6$, i.e. in $x = 6 - 2\sqrt{3} \approx 2.54\,\mathrm{m}$, where the moment equals $16\sqrt{3} \approx 27.71\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The section is moved to $A$, the most loaded extreme. The $x = 2\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ position applies to the triangular load growing towards $B$.
