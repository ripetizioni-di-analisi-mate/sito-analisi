The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and only carries the downward force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $C$, $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. What is the maximum bending moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,1,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1,F" quote="A,C,1 m,0.8;C,B,3 m,0.8" titolo="Simply supported beam with force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $V_A = \dfrac{12 \cdot 3}{4} = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward, and the vertical force equilibrium gives $V_B = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$. In the sections $AC$ and $CB$ the distributed load is zero, so the shear is constant, $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $-3\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the moment is linear. The shear changes sign with an $C$ jump, so the maximum is below the force and equals $M(1) = 9 \cdot 1 = 9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, i.e. $\dfrac{F\,a\,b}{L}$ with $a = 1\,\mathrm{m}$ and $b = 3\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{FL}{4}$, which applies to the force at midspan.
