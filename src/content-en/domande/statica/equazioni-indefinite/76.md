In a beam the shear is $T(x)=6-kx^2$, in kN with $x$ in metres, and $M'=T$. A smooth interior maximum of the moment is observed at $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$. What is $k$?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$

## Explanation

At a smooth maximum the derivative of the moment vanishes, so $6-4k=0$. With $k=1.5\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$, shear changes from positive to negative at the stated point, as required.
