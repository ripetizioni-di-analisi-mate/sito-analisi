The cantilever shown is fixed at $B$ ($x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$) and is free at the end $A$ ($x = 0$), where the force $F = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the shear force in the cantilever?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="B,incastro,0" forze="A,0,-0.9,F" quote="A,B,3 m,0.6" titolo="Cantilever fixed at $B$ with force $F = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ at its free end $A$"}

- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-15\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

At the free left end, the downward force $F$ imposes the boundary condition $T(0^+) = -F = -5\,\mathrm{kN}$. There is no distributed load, so $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -q$ makes the shear constant at $-5\,\mathrm{kN}$ in every section. The location of the free end matters for the sign. At a free right end, the same downward force would give $T = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The number $15$ is the magnitude of the moment at the fixed support, $M(3) = -15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
