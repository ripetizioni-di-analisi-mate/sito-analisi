The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and downward force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $C$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. What is the shear force immediately to the right of $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" forze="C,0,-1.6,F" quote="A,C,4 m,1;C,B,2 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam under uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-14\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $6\,V_A = 12 \cdot 3 + 12 \cdot 2$, then $V_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward. In the segment $AC$ the shear is $T(x) = 10 - 2x$, so to the left of the force is $T(4^-) = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$. Under the downward force $F$ the shear jumps by $-F$, then $T(4^+) = 2 - 12 = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $-14\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the shear at $B$, equal to $-V_B$.
