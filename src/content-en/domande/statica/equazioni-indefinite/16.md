At a section of a horizontal beam the shear force is $T = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$, using the convention that positive shear tends to rotate a small beam element clockwise. What force does the part of the beam to the left of the section exert on the part to its right?

- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left

## Explanation

The left side acts on the right side through the left side of the latter. On the left face of a beam segment the positive shear is directed upward, on the right face downward, and the two forces form a clockwise couple. Since $T = 5\,\mathrm{kN} > 0$, the left side exerts an upward force of $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the right side. The downward force is that which the right side exerts on the left side, by the principle of action and reaction. The horizontal forces exchanged in the section form the axial force, which is not specified here.
