The cantilever shown is fixed at $A$, has length $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries a uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the reaction moment $M_A$ at the fixed end?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2.5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.3,0.3,q" quote="A,B,2.5 m,0.5" titolo="Cantilever under uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $12.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $6.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $12.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The free end $B$ is unloaded, so $T(2.5) = 0$ and $M(2.5) = 0$. Integrating $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -4$ and $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T$ using these end conditions gives $T(x) = 4\,(2.5 - x)$ and $M(x) = -2\,(2.5 - x)^2$. At the fixed end the bending moment $M(0) = -12.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ puts the upper fibers in tension. The reaction couple causes the moment to jump from $0$ to $-M_A$, so $M_A = 12.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts counterclockwise. Taking moments about $A$ confirms this value because the resultant load $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ has a moment arm of $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$.
