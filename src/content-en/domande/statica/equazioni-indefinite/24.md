A simply supported beam in $A$ and $B$ carries only a concentrated counterclockwise couple applied at midspan. Which statement about the shear and bending moment diagrams is true?

- The shear jumps at midspan and the moment is continuous
- The shear is constant along the beam and the moment jumps at midspan
- Both shear and moment jump at midspan
- The shear is zero and the moment is constant along the beam

## Explanation

There are no distributed loads, so for the equation $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -q$ the shear is constant in each segment. Under a concentrated couple the shear is continuous, so it has the same $V_A$ value along the entire beam. The moment instead jumps $-\mathcal{M}$ below the couple, and in the two segments it is linear with the same slope. Reactions are different from zero, because the applied couple is balanced by the couple formed by $V_A$ and $V_B$, equal and opposite.
