At section $C$ of the beam shown an $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ form force is applied, directed downward and rightwards, whose line of action forms with the beam axis an angle $\alpha$ with $\cos\alpha = \dfrac{4}{5}$. Let $\Delta N = N(C^+) - N(C^-)$ and $\Delta T = T(C^+) - T(C^-)$ denote the jumps from immediately left of $C$ to immediately right of it. What are their values?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,1.2,-0.9,F" quote="A,C,3 m,1;C,B,3 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with inclined force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

- $\Delta N = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $\Delta T = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $\Delta N = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $\Delta T = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $\Delta N = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $\Delta T = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $\Delta N = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $\Delta T = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Because $\cos\alpha = \dfrac{4}{5}$, you have $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{3}{5}$. The components of the force are then $F_x = 10 \cdot \dfrac{4}{5} = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the right, and $F_y = 10 \cdot \dfrac{3}{5} = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, down. For the $\Delta N = -F_x$ and $\Delta T = -F_y$ jump rule, with $F_x$ positive to the right and $F_y$ positive downward. Therefore $\Delta N = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $\Delta T = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The bending moment is continuous in $C$, because the line of action of the force passes through the axis of the beam.
