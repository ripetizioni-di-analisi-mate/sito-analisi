The cantilever shown is fixed at $A$ and has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Its free end $B$ carries a downward force $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. What is the support moment $M_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0,-0.9,F" coppie="B,1,ℳ" quote="A,B,3 m,0.6" titolo="Cantilever with $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $B$"}

- $21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

In the cantilever there are no distributed loads, so the shear is constant and the moment linear. To the right end, with the downward force and counterclockwise couple, they equal $T(3^-) = F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M(3^-) = \mathcal{M} = 9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Therefore $M(x) = 9 - 4\,(3 - x) = 4x - 3$, and at the fixed end $M(0) = -3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Because under the reactive couple the moment jumps from $0$ to $-M_A$, you have $M_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise. The value $21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ adds the moments of $F$ and $\mathcal{M}$, while around $A$ the force has clockwise moment and the couple is counterclockwise.
