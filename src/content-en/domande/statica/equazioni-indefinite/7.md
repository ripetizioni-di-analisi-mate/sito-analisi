The cantilever shown is fixed at $A$ and has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Its trapezoidal load varies from $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at the fixed end to $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at the free end $B$. What is the support moment $M_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.2,q" quote="A,B,3 m,0.6" etichette="-0.15,0.78,6 kN/m;3.15,0.38,2 kN/m" titolo="Cantilever under a trapezoidal load varying from $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The load is $q(x) = 6 - \dfrac{4}{3}\,x$, resulting in $\dfrac{6 + 2}{2} \cdot 3 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$. Its moment about $A$ is
$$
\int_0^3 \left(6 - \frac{4}{3}\,x\right) x\,dx = 27 - 12 = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
then the resultant is applied to $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$ by fixed support, on the side of the major side of the trapezoid. Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $M_A - 15 = 0$, therefore $M_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise. The value $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by applying the resultant half way through the cantilever, the value $21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ by applying it to $1.75\,\mathrm{m}$ from fixed support.
