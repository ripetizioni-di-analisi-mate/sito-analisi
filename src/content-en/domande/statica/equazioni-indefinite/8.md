A simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$, with $A$ hinge and $B$ roller support, carries the load $q(x) = 3\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{4}$ ($\mathrm{kN/m}$, with $x$ in meters measured by $A$). What is the maximum bending moment?

- $\dfrac{12}{\pi} \approx 3.82\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $\dfrac{48}{\pi^2} \approx 4.86\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $\dfrac{24}{\pi^2} \approx 2.43\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

By integrating $\dfrac{d^2M}{dx^2} = -3\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{4}$ twice you get $M(x) = \dfrac{48}{\pi^2}\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{4} + c_1 x + c_2$. Conditions $M(0) = 0$ and $M(4) = 0$ give $c_2 = 0$ and $c_1 = 0$, so the moment has the same shape as the load. The maximum is at midspan, where $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{2} = 1$, and equals $\dfrac{48}{\pi^2} \approx 4.86\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{q_0 L^2}{8}$ of the uniform load $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, while $\dfrac{12}{\pi}$ is the value of the reactions in $\mathrm{kN}$.
