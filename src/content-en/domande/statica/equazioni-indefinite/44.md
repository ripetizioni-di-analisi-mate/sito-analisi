In a simply supported beam in $A$ ($x = 0$) and $B$ ($x = 6\,\mathrm{m}$), without distributed loads and without couples, the shear equals $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ between $0$ and $2\,\mathrm{m}$, $1\,\mathrm{kN}$ between $2$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $-5\,\mathrm{kN}$ between $4$ and $6\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the maximum bending moment?

- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

In $A$ the moment is zero, and the change in moment between two sections is the area of the shear diagram between them. Therefore $M(2) = 4 \cdot 2 = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $M(4) = 8 + 1 \cdot 2 = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. After $x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ the shear is negative and the moment decreases to $M(6) = 10 - 5 \cdot 2 = 0$, as support requires. The maximum is then $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, in $x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, where the shear changes sign with a jump.
