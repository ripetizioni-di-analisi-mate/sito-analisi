The beam shown has a pin at $A$ ($x = 0$), a roller support at $B$ ($x = 5\,\mathrm{m}$) and an overhang to the unloaded free end $C$ ($x = 7\,\mathrm{m}$), and carries a uniform load over the entire length. By integrating the differential equilibrium equations on the segments $AB$ and $BC$ four constants appear. Which conditions determine them?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,5 m,1;B,C,2 m,1" titolo="Overhanging beam under uniform load $q$"}

- $M(0) = 0$, $M(5) = 0$, $T(7) = 0$, and $M(7) = 0$
- $M(0) = 0$, $T(7) = 0$, $M(7) = 0$, and continuity of $M$ at $B$
- $M(0) = 0$, $T(7) = 0$, $M(7) = 0$, and continuity of $T$ at $B$
- $T(0) = 0$, $M(0) = 0$, $T(7) = 0$, and $M(7) = 0$

## Explanation

The hinge $A$ is an end support that transmits no couple, so $M(0) = 0$, while the $A$ shear is the unknown reaction. The unloaded free end gives $T(7) = 0$ and $M(7) = 0$. In $B$ the roller support reaction is an unknown concentrated force, so the shear has an unknown jump and does not provide conditions; The moment is continuous. The four conditions determine the four constants, and eventually the jump of the shear at $B$ gives $V_B$. The moment on the $B$ intermediate support is different from zero, because the loaded overhang puts the upper fibers in tension.
