On an interval of a beam, $M(x)=a+bx-cx^4$, with $c>0$, $M'=T$ and $T'=-q$. Which load function must act on that interval?

- $q(x)=4cx^3$
- $q(x)=12cx^2$
- $q(x)=-12cx^2$
- $q(x)=b-4cx^3$

## Explanation

The first derivative gives the shear $T=b-4cx^3$. Its derivative is $T'=-12cx^2$, and the prescribed convention gives $q=-T'=12cx^2$. The constant and linear terms of the moment depend on the boundary conditions.
