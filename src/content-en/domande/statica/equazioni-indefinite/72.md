The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries a trapezoidal load that equals $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in $A$ and $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in $B$. What is the bending moment at midspan?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.3,0.9,q" quote="A,B,6 m,1" etichette="-0.3,0.55,2 kN/m;6.4,1.15,6 kN/m" titolo="Simply supported beam with a trapezoidal load varying from $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $27\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The differential equilibrium equations are linear in loads, so the trapezoidal load breaks down into the uniform load of $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and the zero triangular load at $A$ and equals $q_0 = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in $B$. The uniform load gives at midspan $\dfrac{2 \cdot 6^2}{8} = 9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. For the triangular load $M(x) = \dfrac{q_0 L\,x}{6} - \dfrac{q_0\,x^3}{6L}$, which in $x = \dfrac{L}{2}$ equals $\dfrac{q_0 L^2}{16} = \dfrac{4 \cdot 36}{16} = 9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. So the moment at midspan is $9 + 9 = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ neglects the triangular part, and the value $21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses the moment $\dfrac{q_0 L^2}{12}$ of the symmetrical triangular load for the triangular part.
