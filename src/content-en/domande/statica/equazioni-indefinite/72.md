A simply supported beam of span $L = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, with the pin at $A$ ($x = 0$) and the roller at $B$, carries the parabolic load $q(x) = \dfrac{3x^2}{4}$ ($\mathrm{kN/m}$, with $x$ in metres), zero at $A$ and equal to $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. At which section is the bending moment maximum?

- $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = \sqrt[3]{16} \approx 2.52\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = \sqrt[3]{32} \approx 3.17\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The load has resultant $\displaystyle\int_0^4 \frac{3x^2}{4}\,dx = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$, acting at $\dfrac{3}{4}L = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, so moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $V_A = \dfrac{16 \cdot 1}{4} = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$. Integrating $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -\dfrac{3x^2}{4}$ with $T(0) = V_A$ gives $T(x) = 4 - \dfrac{x^3}{4}$. The shear force decreases from $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $A$ to $-12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$ and vanishes only once, therefore the moment is maximum at the section where $x^3 = 16$, that is at $x = \sqrt[3]{16} \approx 2.52\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $3\,\mathrm{m}$ is the position of the resultant, and $\sqrt[3]{32}\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by splitting the resultant equally between the two supports, that is with $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$.
