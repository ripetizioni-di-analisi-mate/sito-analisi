The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, is clamped at $A$ ($x = 0$) and free and unloaded at $B$. It carries the triangular load $q(x) = 2x$ ($\mathrm{kN/m}$, with $x$ in metres), zero at the clamp and equal to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at the free end. Which expression of the bending moment, in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$, is obtained by integrating the differential equilibrium equations?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0,0.8,q" quote="A,B,3 m,0.6" titolo="Cantilever with a triangular load peaking at the free end"}

- $M(x) = 9x - \dfrac{x^3}{3}$
- $M(x) = 18 - 9x + \dfrac{x^3}{3}$
- $M(x) = -\dfrac{x^3}{3}$
- $M(x) = -18 + 9x - \dfrac{x^3}{3}$

## Explanation

The equation $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -q = -2x$ gives $T(x) = c_1 - x^2$. The end $B$ is free and unloaded, so $T(3) = 0$ and $c_1 = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$. From $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T$ it follows that $M(x) = c_2 + 9x - \dfrac{x^3}{3}$, and the condition $M(3) = 0$ gives $c_2 = -18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Therefore $M(x) = -18 + 9x - \dfrac{x^3}{3}$, which at the clamp equals $-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibres in tension. The expression $-\dfrac{x^3}{3}$ imposes the conditions at the clamp instead of the free end, $9x - \dfrac{x^3}{3}$ uses $M(0) = 0$ as at a support and $18 - 9x + \dfrac{x^3}{3}$ comes from $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = +q$.
