The beam shown is pinned at $A$ and supported by a roller at $B$. Its span is $6\,\mathrm{m}$. A uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts only on the central segment $CD$, which has length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the maximum bending moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="C,D,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,C,2 m,1;C,D,2 m,1;D,B,2 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the central segment"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

By $V_A = V_B = \dfrac{4 \cdot 2}{2} = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ symmetry, upward. In the section $AC$ the shear equals $4\,\mathrm{kN}$, and in the loaded section $T(x) = 4 - 4\,(x - 2)$, which vanishes at midspan, $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$, where the moment is maximum. The balance of the left side gives $M(3) = 4 \cdot 3 - 4 \cdot 1 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ concentrates all the load at midspan, and the value $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment at the beginning of the loaded segment.
