A downward transverse load varies linearly from $x=0$ to $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$. Shear readings at $x=0,1,2\,\mathrm{m}$ are $10$, $8$ and $2\,\mathrm{kN}$, respectively. Given $T'=-q$, what is the load intensity at $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

The shear drops give mean loads of $2$ and $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the two one-metre intervals. For a linear load these are its midpoint values. Its slope is $4\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$, so it increases from $6$ to $8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the final half metre.
