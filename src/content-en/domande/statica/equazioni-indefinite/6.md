The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $5\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise, applied in the $B$ support. What is the maximum positive bending moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" coppie="B,-1,ℳ" quote="A,B,5 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $B$"}

- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

A clockwise couple is applied at the right end, i.e. $\mathcal{M} = -10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ with the counterclockwise direction taken as positive, so the condition in $B$ is $M(5^-) = -10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$; $A$ is $M(0) = 0$. Integrating $\dfrac{d^2M}{dx^2} = -4$ with $M(0) = 0$ gives $M(x) = c_1 x - 2x^2$, and the condition in $B$ gives $5c_1 - 50 = -10$, therefore $c_1 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$. The $T(x) = 8 - 4x$ shear vanishes in $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, where $M(2) = 16 - 8 = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $12.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$ and neglects the couple, the value $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained with the couple taken counterclockwise.
