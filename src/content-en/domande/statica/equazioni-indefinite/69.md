The vertical retaining wall shown has height $3\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at its base $A$, and holds back water up to its free top edge $B$. Per meter of wall width, the horizontal water load has a triangular distribution, increasing from zero at $B$ to $q_0 = 30\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$. What is the magnitude of the bending moment at the base?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro" carichi="A,B,0.9,0,q_0,0" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.6" titolo="Retaining wall fixed at the base under triangular water pressure of intensity $q_0 = 30\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$"}

- $90\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $135\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $22.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Treat the wall as a vertical cantilever with an unloaded free end at $B$. Both the shear and the moment therefore vanish at $B$. The resultant thrust is $\dfrac{30 \cdot 3}{2} = 45\,\mathrm{kN}$ and acts through the centroid of the triangular load diagram, one-third of the wall height above the base, or $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Its moment about $A$ gives the base moment, $45 \cdot 1 = 45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, equivalently $\dfrac{q_0 h^2}{6}$. Integrating $\dfrac{d^2M}{ds^2} = -q$ twice from $B$ gives the same result. The answer $90\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ places the resultant two-thirds of the height above the base. The answer $135\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses the uniform-load expression $\dfrac{q_0 h^2}{2}$.
