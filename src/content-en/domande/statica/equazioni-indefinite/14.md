The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $8\,\mathrm{m}$; carries the uniform $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load on the section $AC$, along $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and the force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting downward at $D$, $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. What is the maximum bending moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;D,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.7,0.7,q" forze="D,0,-1.6,F" quote="A,C,4 m,1;C,D,2 m,1;D,B,2 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $AC$ and force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $D$"}

- $18.375\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The resultant load, $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, is applied at $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, and moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $8\,V_A = 12 \cdot 6 + 6 \cdot 2$; then $V_A = 10.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward. In the segment $AC$ the shear is $T(x) = 10.5 - 3x$, which vanishes in $x = 3.5\,\mathrm{m}$, inside the segment. Therefore the maximum moment is $M(3.5) = 10.5 \cdot 3.5 - 1.5 \cdot 3.5^2 = 18.375\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. In the segments $CD$ and $DB$ the shear is negative and the moment decreases, so $M(4) = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $M(6) = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ are less than the maximum.
