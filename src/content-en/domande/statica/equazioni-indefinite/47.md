On a horizontal beam section, the $q$ transverse load, the $p$ axial load and the $m$ distributed couple, all of which are constant and non-zero, act. If the distributed couple is removed while $q$ and $p$ stay the same, which differential equilibrium equations change?

- Only the shear equation, $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -q$
- Both the shear and moment equations
- Only the moment equation, $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T - m$
- Neither, because the distributed couple has zero resultant force

## Explanation

The couple $m\,dx$ acting on the beam element results in zero, so it does not appear in the translation equations, from which $\dfrac{dN}{dx} = -p$ and $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -q$ are derived. Its moment appears instead in the spin equation of the beam element, which gives $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T - m$. Therefore, by removing the distributed couple, it changes only the equation of the moment, which becomes $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T$. Axial force and shear remain the same.
