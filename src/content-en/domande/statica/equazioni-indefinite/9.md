The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller support at $B$ $8\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and an overhang of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the free end $C$; carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire length. What is the maximum bending moment in span $AB$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0;C,10,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.9,0.9,q" quote="A,B,8 m,1;B,C,2 m,1" titolo="Overhanging beam under uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $\dfrac{225}{16} \approx 14.06\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $\dfrac{289}{16} \approx 18.06\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The resultant load, $20\,\mathrm{kN}$, is applied at $x = 5\,\mathrm{m}$, and moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $8\,V_A = 20 \cdot 3$; then $V_A = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward. In the span the shear is $T(x) = 7.5 - 2x$, which vanishes in $x = 3.75\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore the maximum span moment is $M(3.75) = 7.5 \cdot 3.75 - 3.75^2 = \dfrac{225}{16} \approx 14.06\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$ of the span only and neglects the overhang, which reduces $V_A$. The value $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the absolute value of the moment on the $B$ mount.
