The cantilever shown is fixed at $A$, is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long and only carries the downward force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $C$, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$; end $B$ is free. Which statement about the internal forces and moments in segment $CB$ is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="C,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,C,2 m,0.6;C,B,2 m,0.6" titolo="Cantilever with force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

- The shear and bending moment are both zero
- The shear is $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the bending moment is zero
- The shear is zero and the bending moment is $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- The shear is $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the bending moment varies linearly from $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $C$ to zero at $B$

## Explanation

The free end $B$ is unloaded, so $T(B) = 0$ and $M(B) = 0$. In the section $CB$ there are no loads, so the shear is constant and equal to the value in $B$, i.e. zero. Since $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T = 0$, the moment is also constant and equal to the value in $B$, i.e. zero. The $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ shear and the moment, which at the fixed end equals $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, are different from zero only in the section $AC$.
