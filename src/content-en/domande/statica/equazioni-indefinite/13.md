The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and the uniform distributed couple $m = 2\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$ clockwise. At which section is the bending moment greatest?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.45,0.45,q" coppie="1 0,-1;2 0,-1,m;3 0,-1" quote="A,B,4 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and distributed clockwise couple $m = 2\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$"}

- $x = \dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = \dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = \dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distributed couple is clockwise, so $m = -2\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$, and the third differential equilibrium equation is $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T - m$. Moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $-4\,V_A + 12 \cdot 2 - 2 \cdot 4 = 0$, where $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the total moment of the distributed couple, therefore $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward, and $T(x) = 4 - 3x$. The moment is stationary where $T = m$, i.e. where $4 - 3x = -2$, then in $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, and there is $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The section $x = \dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$, in which the shear vanishes, would give the maximum only without distributed couples.
