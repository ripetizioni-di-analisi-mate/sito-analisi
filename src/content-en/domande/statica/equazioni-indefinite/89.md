The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller support at $B$ $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and an overhang unloaded of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the free end $C$, where the $\mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise couple is applied. What is reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="C,1,ℳ" quote="A,B,4 m,1;B,C,2 m,1" titolo="Overhanging beam with counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at the free end"}

- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward

## Explanation

To the right free end, where the counterclockwise couple acts, $T(6^-) = 0$ and $M(6^-) = \mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ equal. The overhang is unloaded, so on $BC$ the shear is zero and the moment equals $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ up to $B$, where it is continuous. On the span $AB$ the moment is linear, from $0$ in $A$ to $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $B$, so the shear equals $\dfrac{8}{4} = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$. The reaction is $V_A = T(0^+) = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward. The value $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$ divides the couple by the total length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ instead of the distance between the supports.
