In a simply supported beam in $A$ ($x = 0$) and $B$, with no couples applied in the supports, the shear in the first segment, free of concentrated forces or couples, is $T(x) = 9 - 3x$ ($\mathrm{kN}$). What is the bending moment at $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ within this segment?

- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

In $A$ the mount has no couples applied, so $M(0) = 0$. By the integral form of differential equilibrium equations the difference $M(2) - M(0)$ is the area of the shear diagram between $0$ and $2\,\mathrm{m}$. The diagram is a trapezoid with bases $T(0) = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $T(2) = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ and height $2\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore $M(2) = \dfrac{9 + 3}{2} \cdot 2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ multiplies by $2\,\mathrm{m}$ the shear into $A$, as if the shear were constant.
