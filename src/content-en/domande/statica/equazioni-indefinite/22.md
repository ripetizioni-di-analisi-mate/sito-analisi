The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and brings only the couple $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise, applied at $C$, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. What are the bending moments immediately to the left and right of $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="C,1,ℳ" quote="A,C,2 m,1;C,B,4 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $C$"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $-6\,V_A + 12 = 0$, then $V_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward. At section $AC$ the shear equals $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the moment is $M(x) = 2x$, so $M(2^-) = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Under a counterclockwise couple the moment jumps by $-\mathcal{M}$, then $M(2^+) = 4 - 12 = -8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The shear is continuous in $C$, and the moment at $B$ confirms the result, because $M(6) = -8 + 2 \cdot 4 = 0$.
