The beam shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, has a roller support with vertical reaction at $A$ ($x = 0$) and at the end $B$ ($x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$) a parallel-link support that prevents horizontal translation and rotation and leaves the vertical translation free. It carries the uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the bending moment at $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,doppio-pendolo,0" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m,1" titolo="Beam with a roller at $A$, a parallel-link support at $B$, and uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Parallel-link support exerts a horizontal force and a couple, but no vertical force. For the jump rule, with zero values out of the beam, you then have $T(4^-) = 0$, while the moment at $B$ coincides with the unknown reactive couple. The roller support does not transmit moment, therefore $M(0) = 0$. Integrating $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -3$ with $T(4) = 0$ results in $T(x) = 12 - 3x$, and integrating $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T$ with $M(0) = 0$ results in $M(x) = 12x - \dfrac{3x^2}{2}$. Therefore $M(4) = 48 - 24 = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, positive, with the lower fibers in tension. The value $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$ of the simply supported beam at the ends, and the value $48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ neglects the load in the equation of the moment.
