A simply supported beam of span $L$ carries a distributed load $q(x)$, with $x$ measured by the $A$ mount. For which of the following loads is the bending moment proportional to the load at every section, so that $M(x) = k\,q(x)$ for a constant $k$?

- $q(x) = q_0$
- $q(x) = q_0\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{L}$
- $q(x) = \dfrac{q_0\,x}{L}$
- $q(x) = \dfrac{4q_0\,x\,(L - x)}{L^2}$

## Explanation

The supports give $M(0) = 0$ and $M(L) = 0$, so a proportional load at the moment must cancel in both supports. The uniform load and triangular load do not meet this condition. For parabolic load the moment, obtained by integrating twice, is a fourth degree polynomial, therefore it is proportional to a quadratic polynomial only if it is zero. By integrating the sine load twice with the same conditions you get $M(x) = \dfrac{q_0 L^2}{\pi^2}\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{L}$, then $M = k\,q$ with $k = \dfrac{L^2}{\pi^2}$.
