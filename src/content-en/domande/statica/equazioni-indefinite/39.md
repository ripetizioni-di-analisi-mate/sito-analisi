The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and only carries the uniform distributed couple $m = 3\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$ counterclockwise. What is the bending moment at midspan?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="1 0,1;2 0,1,m;3 0,1" quote="A,B,4 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam under distributed counterclockwise couple $m = 3\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The distributed couple produces a total counterclockwise moment of $3 \cdot 4 = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $-4\,V_A + 12 = 0$, hence $V_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. No transverse load acts on the span, so the shear is constant at $T = V_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$. With a distributed couple, the third differential equilibrium equation is $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T - m = 3 - 3 = 0$, so the bending moment is also constant. Since $M(0) = 0$, it vanishes along the whole beam and equals $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at midspan. The answer $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ follows from using $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T$, which omits the distributed couple.
