The beam shown has two $A$ and $B$ supports distant $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and two equal $a$ length overhangs, and carries a uniform $q$ load over the entire length. For what value of $a$ do the support moment and midspan moment have the same magnitude?

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,1.4,0;B,5.4,0;D,6.8,0" aste="C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="C,D,0.6,0.6,q" quote="C,A,a,1;A,B,4 m,1;B,D,a,1" titolo="Beam with two equal overhangs under uniform load $q$"}

- $a = 2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $a = 1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $a = \sqrt{2} \approx 1.41\,\mathrm{m}$
- $a = 2\sqrt{2} \approx 2.83\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Each overhang is a cantilever with the free end unloaded, so on the support the moment equals $-\dfrac{q a^2}{2}$. In span is $\dfrac{d^2M}{dx^2} = -q$, with $M = -\dfrac{q a^2}{2}$ in both supports, therefore, with $x$ measured by $A$, $M(x) = \dfrac{q\,x\,(4 - x)}{2} - \dfrac{q a^2}{2}$. At midspan $M(2) = 2q - \dfrac{q a^2}{2}$. The equality of absolute values requires $2q - \dfrac{q a^2}{2} = \dfrac{q a^2}{2}$, i.e. $a^2 = 2$, then $a = \sqrt{2} \approx 1.41\,\mathrm{m}$. With $a = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ the moment at midspan is zero.
