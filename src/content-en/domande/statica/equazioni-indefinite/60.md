A beam of span $L$ is fixed at $x = 0$, has a roller support at $x = L$ and carries a uniform $q$ load. By integrating $\dfrac{d^2M}{dx^2} = -q$ two constants appear. How many conditions on $T$ and $M$ do the two supports supply?

- Only one, $M(L) = 0$, because the beam is statically indeterminate
- Two, $M(0) = 0$ and $M(L) = 0$
- Two, $T(0) = 0$ and $M(L) = 0$
- Two, $M(L) = 0$ and $T(L) = 0$

## Explanation

Fixed support transmits any shear and moment, so in $x = 0$ it does not give conditions on $T$ and $M$. The roller support does not transmit moment, so it gives $M(L) = 0$, while the shear at $L$ coincides with the unknown reaction. So there remains only one condition for two constants. The beam has four scalar constraints, three of fixed support and one of roller support, and three degrees of freedom, so it is once statically indeterminate. The moment is determined only by adding a condition on the deformation of the beam, which the equilibrium does not provide.
