In a segment of beam with no concentrated forces or couples a uniform load acts. The bending moment equals $0$ in $x = 0$, $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the load?

- $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, upward
- $1\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, downward
- $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, downward
- $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, downward

## Explanation

With a uniform load it equals $\dfrac{d^2M}{dx^2} = -q$, so in the section $M(x) = c_2 + c_1 x - \dfrac{q\,x^2}{2}$. The $M(0) = 0$ condition gives $c_2 = 0$, and the other two conditions damage
$$
\begin{cases}
2c_1 - 2q = 10 \\
4c_1 - 8q = 12
\end{cases}
$$
Subtracting from the second equation twice as much as the first results in $-4q = -8$, therefore $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, positive, i.e. downward, and $c_1 = 7\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $1\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ is obtained by writing the term $-q\,x^2$ without the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
