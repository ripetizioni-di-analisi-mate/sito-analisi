The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$, separated by $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Its two overhangs each have length $1\,\mathrm{m}$ and end at $C$ and $D$. At $C$ and $D$, identical downward forces $F = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ are the only loads. What is the bending moment in span $AB$?

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,1,0;B,5,0;D,6,0" aste="C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.2,F;D,0,-1.2,F" quote="C,A,1 m,1;A,B,4 m,1;B,D,1 m,1" titolo="Beam with two overhangs and end forces $F = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, constant
- $5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, constant
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, constant

## Explanation

By symmetry the reactions equal $V_A = V_B = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward. On the $CA$ overhang the shear equals $-5\,\mathrm{kN}$, and in $A$ jumps by $V_A = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$, so in the span the shear is zero. Since $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T = 0$, in the span the moment is constant. On the $A$ support the moment is that of the overhang, $M = -F \cdot 1 = -5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, so the moment equals $-5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on the whole span, with the upper fibers in tension. The span is therefore in pure bending.
