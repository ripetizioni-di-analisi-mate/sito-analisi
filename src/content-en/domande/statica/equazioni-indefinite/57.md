The cantilever shown is fixed at $A$ and is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ long. In $C$, at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, a vertical $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$-high cantilever is welded, at the top of which $E$ acts the horizontal $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ force to the right. What is the bending moment in segment $AC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,3,0;E,2,0.5" aste="A-B;C-E" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="E,0.8,0,F" quote="A,C,2 m,0.6;C,B,1 m,0.6;E,C,0.5 m,0.4" titolo="Cantilever with horizontal force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the top of bracket $CE$"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The force at $E$ is equivalent to the same force applied on the axis at $C$ together with a transfer couple of moment $-6 \cdot 0.5 = -3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, that is, $3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise. The section $CB$ is unloaded and end $B$ is free, so in $CB$ the shear and moment are zero. Under the $\mathcal{M} = -3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise couple the moment jumps to $-\mathcal{M} = 3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, so $M(C^-) = M(C^+) - 3 = -3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. In the section $AC$ the shear is zero, so the moment is constant and equals $-3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension. The value $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses as an arm $1\,\mathrm{m}$, the distance of $C$ from the free end.
