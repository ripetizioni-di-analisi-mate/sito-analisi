The beam shown has a pin at $A$ ($x = 0$), a roller support at $B$ ($x = 6\,\mathrm{m}$) and an overhang to the free end $C$ ($x = 8\,\mathrm{m}$). It carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the span $AB$ and the downward force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $C$. Along which segment are the upper fibers in tension?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" forze="C,0,-1.8,F" quote="A,B,6 m,1;B,C,2 m,1" titolo="Overhanging beam with $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the span and $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

- Only on overhang $BC$
- Between $A$ and $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$
- Along the entire beam
- Between $x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and end $C$

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $6\,V_A = 2 \cdot 6 \cdot 3 - 6 \cdot 2 = 24$, then $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward. In the span the shear is $T(x) = 4 - 2x$ and, since $M(0) = 0$, the moment is
$$
M(x) = 4x - x^2 = x\,(4 - x) .
$$
The moment is positive for $0 < x < 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and is negative for $4\,\mathrm{m} < x \le 6\,\mathrm{m}$, where it equals $M(6) = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. On the overhang the moment is $M(x) = -6\,(8 - x)$, negative at end $C$, where it vanishes. The upper fibers are in tension where $M < 0$, so in the segment between $x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $C$. In $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ the shear vanishes and the moment has the maximum positive, $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
