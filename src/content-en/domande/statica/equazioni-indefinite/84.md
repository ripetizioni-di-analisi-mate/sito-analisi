A beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ ($x = 0$), no couples applied, and a roller support at $B$. The shear diagram, shown, is the line $T(x) = 7 - 2x$ ($\mathrm{kN}$), without jumps between $A$ and $B$. What couple is applied at support $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,6,0,7-2*s" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $12.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $18.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

In $A$ there are no couples, so $M(0) = 0$. For the integral form $M(6^-) = \displaystyle\int_0^6 (7 - 2x)\,dx = 42 - 36 = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, which is the positive area $12.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ minus the negative area $6.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. In right-hand support with an applied couple equals $M(L^-) = \mathcal{M}$, therefore $\mathcal{M} = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, positive, i.e. counterclockwise. The value $12.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ only considers the positive part of the shear diagram, and the value $18.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ adds the two areas without a sign.
