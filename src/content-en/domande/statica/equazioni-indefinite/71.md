A cantilever is fixed at $A$ ($x = 0$), is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ long and carries the parabolic load $q(x) = x^2$ ($\mathrm{kN/m}$, with $x$ in meters), zero at the fixed end and equal to $9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in the free end $B$. What is the shear force at the midspan section, $x = 1.5\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $1.125\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6.75\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $7.875\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

At the unloaded free end the shear is zero, $T(3) = 0$. For the integral form $T(1.5) - T(3) = \displaystyle\int_{1.5}^{3} x^2\,dx$, then
$$
T(1.5) = \frac{27 - 3.375}{3} = 7.875\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The shear in the section is therefore the resultant of the load between the section and the free end. The value $1.125\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the resultant of the load between fixed support and section, and the value $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ is half of the total $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ result.
