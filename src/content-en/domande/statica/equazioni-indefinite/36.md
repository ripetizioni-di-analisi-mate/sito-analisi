The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries a trapezoidal load that equals $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in $A$ and $8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in $B$. What is reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.3,1.2,q" quote="A,B,6 m,1" etichette="-0.3,0.55,2 kN/m;6.4,1.45,8 kN/m" titolo="Simply supported beam with a trapezoidal load varying from $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward

## Explanation

The load is the sum of a uniform load of $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and a triangular load that is zero at $A$ and equal to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in $B$. The uniform load gives $A$ the reaction $\dfrac{2 \cdot 6}{2} = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The triangular load has resultant $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ being applied to two thirds of the span, which splits off with the $1 : 2$ ratio, then gives $A$ the reaction $6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The differential equilibrium equations are linear in loads, so reactions add up and $V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward. The value $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the reaction $V_B$, the value $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ is half of the total $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ resultant.
