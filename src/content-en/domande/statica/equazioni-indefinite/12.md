The beam shown has a roller support with vertical reaction at $A$ and a pin at $B$, span $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the distributed axial load $p = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ directed to the right. What is the horizontal reaction at $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera" frecce="0.1,0.4,0.5,0.4;0.9,0.4,1.3,0.4;1.7,0.4,2.1,0.4;2.5,0.4,2.9,0.4;3.3,0.4,3.7,0.4" etichette="0.3,0.7,p" quote="A,B,4 m,1" titolo="Beam with distributed axial load $p = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to the right"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left

## Explanation

The roller support at $A$ has vertical reaction, so at the end $A$ it equals $N(0) = 0$. Integrating $\dfrac{dN}{dx} = -3$ results in $N(x) = -3x$, and in $B$ the axial force equals $N(4^-) = -12\,\mathrm{kN}$, compression. Beyond end $B$ the axial force is zero, and for the rule of $0 - N(4^-) = -H_B$ jumps; therefore $H_B = -12\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. The horizontal translation of the beam confirms the result, because the resultant of the axial load is $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and the hinge is the only constraint that transmits horizontal forces.
