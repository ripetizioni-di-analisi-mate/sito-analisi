The cantilever shown is fixed at $A$ ($x = 0$) and has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Its distributed axial load $p(x) = 2x$ ($\mathrm{kN/m}$, with $x$ in meters) acts to the right and increases from zero at the fixed end to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at the free end $B$. What is the axial force at the fixed end?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" frecce="0.9,0.35,1.1,0.35;1.6,0.35,2,0.35;2.3,0.35,2.9,0.35" etichette="2.6,0.65,p(x)" quote="A,B,3 m,0.6" titolo="Cantilever with axial load $p(x) = 2x$ to the right"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$, in compression
- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension

## Explanation

The free end $B$ is unloaded, so $N(3) = 0$. By the integral form of the equation $\dfrac{dN}{dx} = -p$ equals
$$
N(0) = N(3) + \int_0^3 2x\,dx = 0 + 9 = 9\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The axial force is positive, so it is tension: the load pulls the cantilever to the right and the fixed support holds it from the left. The value $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by multiplying the maximum value of the load by the length, as if the load were uniform.
