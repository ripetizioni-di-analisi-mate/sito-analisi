The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the uniform load $q_1 = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the left half and the uniform load $q_2 = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the right half. At which section is the bending moment greatest?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.4,0.4,q_1;C,B,0.8,0.8,q_2" quote="A,C,3 m,1;C,B,3 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with $q_1 = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $AC$ and $q_2 = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $CB$"}

- $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = 3.75\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = 4.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = 3.375\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The two distributed loads have resultants $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $x = 1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $x = 4.5\,\mathrm{m}$. Moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $6\,V_A = 6 \cdot 4.5 + 12 \cdot 1.5 = 45$, hence $V_A = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. On the left half, $T(x) = 7.5 - 2x$. At $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ the shear is still positive at $1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. On the right half, $T(x) = 1.5 - 4\,(x - 3)$. This expression reaches zero at $x = 3 + \dfrac{1.5}{4} = 3.375\,\mathrm{m}$, where the bending moment is largest. The answer $3.75\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from setting $7.5 - 2x$ equal to zero beyond midspan, where the load intensity has already changed to $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$.
