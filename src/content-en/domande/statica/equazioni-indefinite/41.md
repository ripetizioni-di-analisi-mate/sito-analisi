In an inner section of a beam the distributed load down switches from $2$ to $5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. No forces or concentrated couples are applied in the section. What happens to the shear force $T$ and the moment $M$?

- $T$ is continuous with a change in slope. $M$ is continuous with a continuous tangent.
- $T$ jumps by $3\,\mathrm{kN}$. $M$ is continuous.
- $T$ is continuous. $M$ jumps.
- Both $T$ and $M$ jump.

## Explanation

The $q$ jump changes the value of $T'=-q$. Integrating on a narrowing range to the section, the variation of $T$ tends to zero. Since $M'=T$, the tangent of the $M$ diagram remains continuous.
