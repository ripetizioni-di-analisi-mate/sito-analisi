The Gerber beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller support at $B$ $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, an internal pin at $C$ $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and a roller support at $D$ $8\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$; carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire length. What is the bending moment at support $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,5,0;D,8,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" cerniere="C" carichi="A,D,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,4 m,1;B,C,1 m,1;C,D,3 m,1" titolo="Gerber beam under uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $-1\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Segment $CD$ has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$. The internal hinge imposes $M(C) = 0$ and the roller imposes $M(D) = 0$, so the segment acts as a simply supported beam and its shear at $C$ is $\dfrac{q \cdot 3}{2} = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$. The hinge transmits that shear to overhang $BC$, giving $T(5) = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$. Since $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -2$, it follows that $T(x) = 3 + 2\,(5 - x)$. The integral form gives $M(5) - M(4) = \displaystyle\int_4^5 T\,dx = 3 + 1 = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Since $M(5) = 0$, the moment at the support is $M(B) = -4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, putting the top fibers in tension. The value $-1\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ neglects the force transmitted through the hinge, while $-3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ neglects the load on overhang $BC$.
