The beam shown has a pin at $A$ ($x = 0$) and a roller support at $B$ ($x = 5\,\mathrm{m}$). In $C$ ($x = 1\,\mathrm{m}$) the force $F_1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward, in $D$ ($x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$) the force $F_2 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. What is the bending moment at $D$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,1,0;D,3,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.2,F_1;D,0,1.2,F_2" quote="A,C,1 m,1;C,D,2 m,1;D,B,2 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with two equal and opposite forces, $F_1 = F_2 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $5\,V_A - 10 \cdot 4 + 10 \cdot 2 = 0$, then $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; because the two forces are balanced, $V_B = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. There are no distributed loads, so the shear is constant at times and equals $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ on $AC$ and $4 - 10 = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$ on $CD$. For the integral form the moment is the area of the shear diagram starting with $M(0) = 0$, then $M(1) = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and
$$
M(3) = 4 - 6 \cdot 2 = -8\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The value $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by adding only the area of the shear diagram on the segment $CD$, without the $M(1) = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ contribution of the segment $AC$.
