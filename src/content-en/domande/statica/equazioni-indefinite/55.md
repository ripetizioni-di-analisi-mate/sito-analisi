The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries only two couples of $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, a counterclockwise in $C$, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and a clockwise in $D$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. What is the bending moment at midspan?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="C,1,ℳ;D,-1,ℳ" quote="A,C,2 m,1;C,D,2 m,1;D,B,2 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with two opposing couples of magnitude $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$"}

- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The two applied couples cancel, and no external forces load the beam. Taking moments about $A$ gives $6\,V_B = 0$, so both support reactions vanish. The shear is zero throughout, and the bending moment is constant between applied couples. Starting at $A$, the moment remains zero up to $C$. The counterclockwise couple there causes a jump of $-\mathcal{M} = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The moment is then $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ throughout segment $CD$, including midspan, with the upper fibers in tension. At $D$ the clockwise couple causes a jump of $+6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, bringing the moment back to zero as required at support $B$.
