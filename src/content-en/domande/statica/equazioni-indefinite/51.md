In a section of a horizontal beam the shear equals $T = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and acts a uniform distributed couple $m = 2\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$ counterclockwise. At that section, what is the slope $\dfrac{dM}{dx}$ of the bending moment diagram?

- $7\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

In the presence of a distributed couple the third differential equilibrium equation is $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T - m$, with $m$ positive counterclockwise. Here $m = 2\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$, then $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = 5 - 2 = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained with $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T$, which is only valid without distributed couples. The value $7\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained with the inverted $m$ sign.
