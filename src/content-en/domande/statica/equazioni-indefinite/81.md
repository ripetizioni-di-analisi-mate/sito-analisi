A simply supported beam of span $5\,\mathrm{m}$ carries the uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. To trace the parabola of the bending moment, the tangents to the diagram are drawn in the two supports, which meet on the vertical line through the midspan. What is the value at midspan on the tangent drawn at $A$, that is, the ordinate where the two tangents intersect?

- $25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $50\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The shear equals the slope of the bending-moment diagram, so the tangent at $A$ has slope $T(0^+) = V_A = \dfrac{qL}{2} = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$. Since $M(0) = 0$, the tangent at $A$ has equation $10\,x$. At midspan, $x = 2.5\,\mathrm{m}$, it reaches $25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. By symmetry the tangent at $B$ passes through the same point. The height of their intersection is therefore $\dfrac{qL^2}{4}$, twice the maximum bending moment $\dfrac{qL^2}{8} = 12.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at the vertex of the parabola. The answer $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the numerical value of the slope.
