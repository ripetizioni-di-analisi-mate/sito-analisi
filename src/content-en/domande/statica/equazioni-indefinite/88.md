The beam shown has a pin at $A$ ($x = 0$), a roller support at $B$ ($x = 6\,\mathrm{m}$) and an overhang to the $C$ ($x = 8\,\mathrm{m}$) free end, and carries the uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire length. The largest value of $|T|$ along the beam is needed for shear check. Where does it occur, and what is its value?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.7,0.7,q" quote="A,B,6 m,1;B,C,2 m,1" titolo="Overhanging beam under uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$, at support $A$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, immediately to the right of $B$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, immediately to the left of $B$
- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$, at support $B$

## Explanation

The resultant load is $24\,\mathrm{kN}$, applied at $x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, so moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $6\,V_A = 24 \cdot 2$, i.e. $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the vertical force equilibrium gives $V_B = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$. On the span $T(x) = 8 - 3x$, which is linear and decreasing, therefore its extreme values are $T(0^+) = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $T(6^-) = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$. On the $T(x) = 3\,(8 - x)$ overhang, which equals at most $T(6^+) = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The maximum of $|T|$ is therefore $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, immediately to the left of $B$. The value $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ is $\dfrac{qL}{2}$ of the span without overhang, and the value $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the reaction $V_B$, i.e. the leap of the shear into $B$.
