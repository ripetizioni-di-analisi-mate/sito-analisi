An $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long foundation beam, with both free ends, receives the load of the column $P = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan, downward, and is supported by the ground with a uniform upward-directed pressure, which balances $P$. What is the bending moment at midspan?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" carichi="A,B,0.45,0.45,r,90" forze="C,0,-1.2,P" quote="A,C,2 m,1.1;C,B,2 m,1.1" titolo="Foundation beam under column load $P = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and uniform soil pressure $r$"}

- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The ground pressure balances the load of the column, so its resultant value is $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and per unit length is $\dfrac{24}{4} = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, upward, i.e. $q = -6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. To the left free end $T(0) = 0$ and $M(0) = 0$. Since $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -q = 6$, between the extreme and the column the shear is $T(x) = 6x$, and integrating $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T$ you get $M(x) = 3x^2$. Therefore at midspan $M(2) = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, positive, so the lower fibers are in tension. The value $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{PL}{4}$, i.e. the moment of a simply supported beam at the ends, in which reactions are concentrated.
