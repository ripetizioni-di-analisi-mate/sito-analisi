In a beam the only interior zero-shear point has $M=7\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. A concentrated couple at $C$ gives $M(C^-)=4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $M(C^+)=9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The end moments vanish. On either side of $C$ the moment is differentiable, with continuous shear as its derivative. What is the maximum positive moment among the limiting sections?

- $7\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, at the zero-shear point
- $5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, equal to the jump
- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, just right of $C$
- $6.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, the mean of the values at $C$

## Explanation

The search must include the ends of the smooth intervals and both sections adjacent to the couple. The candidate values are $0$, $7$, $4$ and $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The largest is the right-hand limiting value.
