A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, with the pin at $A$ ($x = 0$) and the roller at $B$, is acted on by the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the whole span and by a counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ applied at section $C$, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. What is the maximum bending moment, and at which section is it found?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" coppie="C,1,ℳ" quote="A,C,2 m,1;C,B,4 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with a uniform load and a counterclockwise couple at $C$"}

- $\dfrac{81}{16} \approx 5.06\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, at $x = 3.75\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, at midspan
- $11\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, immediately to the left of $C$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, immediately to the right of $C$

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium about $B$, with counterclockwise positive, gives $-6\,V_A + 12 \cdot 3 + 9 = 0$, so $V_A = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, upwards. The couple produces no jump in the shear force, therefore $T(x) = 7.5 - 2x$ along the whole beam, and the shear vanishes at $x = 3.75\,\mathrm{m}$, to the right of $C$. In segment $AC$ the shear is positive, so the moment increases up to $M(2^-) = 7.5 \cdot 2 - 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 1 = 11\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Under the counterclockwise couple the moment jumps by $-\mathcal{M}$, so $M(2^+) = 2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and in segment $CB$ it rises only up to the relative maximum $M(3.75) = \dfrac{81}{16} \approx 5.06\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The maximum is therefore $11\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, **immediately to the left of $C$**. The value $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by taking the jump with the opposite sign, and the value $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$ and neglects the couple.
