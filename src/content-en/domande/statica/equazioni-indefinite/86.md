The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and the couple $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise, at midspan. What is the maximum bending moment, and where does it occur?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" coppie="C,-1,ℳ" quote="A,C,3 m,1;C,B,3 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at midspan"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, at $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, at midspan
- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, immediately to the right of midspan
- $3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, immediately to the left of midspan

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $-6\,V_A + 12 \cdot 3 - 12 = 0$, then $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward, and the shear is $T(x) = 4 - 2x$ over the whole beam, because the couple does not produce shear jumps. The shear vanishes in $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, where the moment has the maximum relative $M(2) = 8 - 4 = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. At midspan $M(3^-) = 12 - 9 = 3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and under the clockwise couple the moment jumps by $-\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, therefore $M(3^+) = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Beyond the $T < 0$ midspan and the moment decreases to $M(6) = 0$. The maximum is then $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, at the end of the right segment, immediately to the right of the couple.
