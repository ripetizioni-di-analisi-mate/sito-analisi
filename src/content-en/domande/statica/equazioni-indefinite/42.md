The cantilever shown is fixed at $A$ ($x = 0$), is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ long and in the free end $B$ carries the force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$, directed downward and to the right, whose line of action forms with the axis of the cantilever an angle $\alpha$ with $\cos\alpha = \dfrac{3}{5}$. What are the axial force and bending moment at the fixed end?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0.6,-0.8,F" quote="A,B,2 m,0.6" titolo="Cantilever with inclined force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the free end"}

- $N = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $N = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M = -16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $N = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M = -16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $N = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M = -20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Resolving $F$ gives an axial component of $10 \cdot \dfrac{3}{5} = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and a transverse component of $10 \cdot \dfrac{4}{5} = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. The boundary conditions at the free end $B$ are therefore $N(2) = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, $T(2) = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M(2) = 0$. With no distributed loads on the cantilever, $N$ and $T$ are constant. The axial force at the fixed end is thus also $N = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension. Since $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, the bending moment varies linearly and is given by
$$
M(0) = M(2) - 8 \cdot 2 = -16\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
with the upper fibers in tension. The value $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained using all the force $F$ as if it were perpendicular to the axis.
