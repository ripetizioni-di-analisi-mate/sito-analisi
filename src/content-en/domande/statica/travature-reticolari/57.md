On the joint $G = (6, 4)$ of the top chord of the Pratt truss shown, supported with a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $E$, an oblique force acts: its horizontal component equals $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and vertical $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Panels and height measure $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the value of reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="G,1.2,-1.6,F" quote="A,B,3 m,1.3;B,C,3 m,1.3;C,D,3 m,1.3;D,E,3 m,1.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Pratt truss with inclined force $F$, of components $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $20\,\mathrm{kN}$, acting at $G$"}

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward

## Explanation

Take moments about $E$. Reactions $V_E$ and $H_A$ drop out because their lines of action pass through $E$. About $E$, the vertical load component has moment arm $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and counterclockwise moment $20 \cdot 6 = 120\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The horizontal component has moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$, the elevation of $G$, and clockwise moment $15 \cdot 4 = 60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Thus
$$
-12\,V_A + 20 \cdot 6 - 15 \cdot 4 = 0 ,
$$
giving $V_A = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ **upward**. Omitting the moment of the horizontal component, which acts at elevation $4\,\mathrm{m}$ above the bottom chord, would give $10\,\mathrm{kN}$.
