## Explanation

The structure has $n = 6$ joints, $a = 8$ members, and $v = 3$ external scalar restraints. Since $2n = 12$ and $a + v = 11$, the count gives $2n - (a + v) = l - i = 1$ and there is at least one mechanism. Triangles $ACE$ and $DBF$ are rigid, but the central panel $CDFE$ has no diagonal and forms a four-bar linkage. Rotation of its verticals is the only mechanism, so $l = 1$ and $i = 0$. Equal vertical loads at $C$ and $D$ do zero net virtual work in this mechanism and can be carried in equilibrium. A single load at $C$ cannot. Adding a diagonal to the central panel makes the truss statically determinate.
