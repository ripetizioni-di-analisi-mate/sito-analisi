The Warren truss shown has base triangles $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $2\,\mathrm{m}$, pin at $A$ and roller support on horizontal plane in $D$. An $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward force acts directly at joint $D$, above the roller support. Which statement is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,1.5,2;G,4.5,2;H,7.5,2" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;E-G;G-H;A-E;E-B;B-G;G-C;C-H;H-D" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="D,0,-1.3,P" quote="A,B,3 m,1.2;B,C,3 m,1.2;C,D,3 m,1.2;9 0,9 2,2 m,0.9" titolo="Warren truss with force $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ applied at the roller joint"}

- $V_A = V_D = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$, and members $CD$ and $HD$ are in compression
- Members $CD$ and $HD$ carry zero force, and all the other members are in tension
- All members carry zero force and $V_D = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$
- Member $HD$ is in compression with $30\,\mathrm{kN}$, and all the other members carry zero force

## Explanation

Taking moments about $A$ gives $9\,V_D - 30 \cdot 9 = 0$ and hence $V_D = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$. Force equilibrium gives $V_A = 0$ and $H_A = 0$. The roller reaction at joint $D$ therefore balances the load directly. Zero member forces satisfy equilibrium at every joint. No external force acts at any joint other than $D$, and at $D$ the applied load cancels the reaction. Since the truss is statically determinate, this solution is unique. **Every member carries zero force** because the load passes directly into the support.
