In the Howe shown the internal diagonals $BG$ and $DG$ go up towards the midspan; the panels are $3\,\mathrm{m}$ wide, the height is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and the supports are a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $E$. If the only load is a downward force $P = 32\,\mathrm{kN}$ at joint $H$, what force does diagonal $DG$ carry?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;B-G;D-G" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="H,0,-1.5,P" quote="A,B,3 m,1.3;B,C,3 m,1.3;C,D,3 m,1.3;D,E,3 m,1.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Howe truss with only force $P = 32\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $H$"}

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)

## Explanation

Taking moments about $E$ gives $-12\,V_A + 32 \cdot 3 = 0$ and hence $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$. A vertical section between $C$ and $D$ cuts $GH$, $DG$, and $CD$. The only vertical force to the left of the section is $V_A$, giving $V_s = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$. Diagonal $DG$ runs from $G = (6, 4)$ to $D = (9, 0)$ and slopes down from left to right. Vertical equilibrium of the section gives
$$
N_{DG} = \frac{V_s}{\sin\alpha} = \frac{8}{\frac{4}{5}} = 10\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The diagonal is **in tension**. Under symmetric downward loading a Howe truss’s diagonals carry compression, but the sign follows the panel shear $V_s$. Here it is positive because the load lies to the right of the cut.
