The truss shown has two panels measuring $3\,\mathrm{m}$ by $4\,\mathrm{m}$, the $AD$, $BE$, $CG$ verticals and the diagonals $DB$ and $BG$, which form a V with the vertex in $B$; it is supported by a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $C$. At joints $D$, $E$, $G$ the forces $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ act downward. What is the force in diagonal $DB$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,0,4;E,3,4;G,6,4" aste="A-B;B-C;D-E;E-G;A-D;B-E;C-G;D-B;B-G" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" forze="D,0,-1.4,P_1;E,0,-1.4,P_2;G,0,-1.4,P_1" quote="A,B,3 m,1.1;B,C,3 m,1.1;6 0,6 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="V-shaped truss with $P_1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $D$ and $G$ and $P_2 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $E$"}

- $12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $16.67\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)

## Explanation

By symmetry, $V_A = V_C = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$. A vertical section through the first panel cuts $DE$, $DB$, and $AB$. The two parallel chords are horizontal, so take vertical equilibrium of the left-hand part containing joints $A$ and $D$. Diagonal $DB$ slopes down to the right with $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{4}{5}$, giving
$$
20 - 10 - \frac{4}{5}\,N_{DB} = 0 .
$$
This gives $N_{DB} = 12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the diagonal is **in tension**. The value $16.67\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by dividing by $\dfrac{3}{5}$ instead of $\sin\alpha$.
