Each of the three vertical loads on the symmetric Howe truss shown is $20\,\mathrm{kN}$. Additional downward loads of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ are applied at both $F$ and $H$. How does the force in diagonal $BG$ of the panel between $B$ and $C$ change?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;B-G;D-G" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="F,0,-1.5,P;G,0,-1.5,P;H,0,-1.5,P" quote="A,B,3 m,1.3;B,C,3 m,1.3;C,D,3 m,1.3;D,E,3 m,1.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Howe truss under three forces $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- It is unchanged.
- Compression increases by $6\,\mathrm{kN}$.
- Compression increases by $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$.
- The member force becomes zero.

## Explanation

By symmetry each reaction increases by $6\,\mathrm{kN}$. To the left of the section, the downward load at $F$ also increases by $6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The panel shear and the vertical component of the diagonal force are therefore unchanged.
