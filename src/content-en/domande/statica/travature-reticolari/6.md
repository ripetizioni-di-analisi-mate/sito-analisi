In the Pratt truss in the figure, supported by a pin at $A$ and a roller at $E$, two downward forces of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ act at joints $F$ and $H$ of the top chord, while the central joint $G$ is unloaded. Among the inclined members $AF$, $FC$, $HC$ and $EH$, which are zero-force members?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="F,0,-1.5,20 kN;H,0,-1.5,20 kN" quote="A,B,3 m,1.3;B,C,3 m,1.3;C,D,3 m,1.3;D,E,3 m,1.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Pratt truss with two forces of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $F$ and $H$"}

- Diagonals $FC$ and $HC$
- End members $AF$ and $EH$
- Diagonal $FC$ only
- None of the four

## Explanation

By symmetry, $V_A = V_E = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$. The chords are parallel, so the force in a diagonal follows from vertical force equilibrium of the part to the left of a vertical section through its panel. In the panel between $B$ and $C$, the forces to the left of the section are $V_A$ upward and the $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ force at $F$ downward, so the sum of the vertical forces is zero and $N_{FC} = 0$. In the panel between $C$ and $D$, the same two forces act to the left of the section, so $N_{HC} = 0$ as well. At joint $A$ the chord $AB$ and member $AF$ meet, with $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{4}{5}$, so vertical equilibrium of the joint gives $20 + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AF} = 0$, that is, $N_{AF} = -25\,\mathrm{kN}$, and by symmetry $EH$ is also in compression. The inclined zero-force members are therefore diagonals $FC$ and $HC$.
