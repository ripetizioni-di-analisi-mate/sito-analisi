You want to know how much load the triangular roof truss can carry shown, with $AB$ base of $8\,\mathrm{m}$, $C$ full to $3\,\mathrm{m}$ height, pin at $A$ and roller support at $B$. Each member in compression can resist at most $30\,\mathrm{kN}$, and each member in tension at most $60\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the largest vertical force $P$ that can be applied at the ridge?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0;C,4,3" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.4,P" quote="A,B,8 m,1.1;8 0,8 3,3 m,0.9" titolo="Triangular truss with force $P$ at vertex $C$"}

- $36\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $90\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $45\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $50\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

By symmetry, $V_A = V_B = \dfrac{P}{2}$. At joint $A$, member $AC$ has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and unit vector $\left(\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$. Vertical equilibrium gives $N_{AC} = -\dfrac{5P}{6}$ and horizontal equilibrium gives $N_{AB} = \dfrac{2P}{3}$. By symmetry, $N_{BC} = N_{AC}$. The struts carry compression and the tie carries tension, so both of the following limits must hold,
$$
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{5P}{6} \leq 30 \\[10pt]
\dfrac{2P}{3} \leq 60
\end{cases}
$$
giving $P \leq 36\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $P \leq 90\,\mathrm{kN}$. The struts in compression govern the maximum load $P = 36\,\mathrm{kN}$. The larger value $90\,\mathrm{kN}$ satisfies the tie limit alone.
