The Pratt truss shown is a lower-way deck of span $L$, divided into four equal panels, and $h$ height, with a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $E$. The scaffold rests on the bottom chord and carries the uniform load $q$ down, which is transmitted to the joints $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$, $E$ restarting each segment halfway between its two joints. What is the force in top chord $FG$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" carichi="A,E,0.5,0.5," etichette="1.5,0.85,q" quote="A,E,L,1.3;12 0,12 4,h,0.9" titolo="Through-truss bridge with uniform load $q$ on the bottom chord"}

- $N_{FG} = -\dfrac{qL^2}{8h}$
- $N_{FG} = -\dfrac{3qL^2}{16h}$
- $N_{FG} = \dfrac{qL^2}{8h}$
- $N_{FG} = -\dfrac{qL^2}{4h}$

## Explanation

Each bottom-chord segment has length $\dfrac{L}{4}$ and carries load $\dfrac{qL}{4}$. Interior joints $B$, $C$, and $D$ therefore receive $\dfrac{qL}{4}$ each, while end joints $A$ and $E$ receive $\dfrac{qL}{8}$ each. By symmetry, $V_A = V_E = \dfrac{qL}{2}$. A vertical section between $F$ and $G$ cuts $FG$, $FC$, and $BC$. The moment center for $FG$ is joint $C$ at horizontal coordinate $\dfrac{L}{2}$. About $C$, the support reaction and the loads at joints $A$ and $B$ give the following clockwise moment on the left-hand free body,
$$
M_C = \frac{qL}{2} \cdot \frac{L}{2} - \frac{qL}{8} \cdot \frac{L}{2} - \frac{qL}{4} \cdot \frac{L}{4} = \frac{qL^2}{8} ,
$$
equal to the midspan moment of a simply supported beam under uniform load. Moment equilibrium of the section then gives $N_{FG} = -\dfrac{M_C}{h} = -\dfrac{qL^2}{8h}$, so the top chord is **in compression**. Omitting the load $\dfrac{qL}{8}$ at support joint $A$ would give $-\dfrac{3qL^2}{16h}$.
