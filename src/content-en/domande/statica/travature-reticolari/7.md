The Warren truss shown has basic triangles $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $2\,\mathrm{m}$, pin at $A$ and roller support at $D$. At joints $E$, $G$, $H$, three forces of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ act downward. What is the force in bottom chord $BC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,1.5,2;G,4.5,2;H,7.5,2" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;E-G;G-H;A-E;E-B;B-G;G-C;C-H;H-D" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="E,0,-1.3,P;G,0,-1.3,P;H,0,-1.3,P" quote="A,B,3 m,1.2;B,C,3 m,1.2;C,D,3 m,1.2;9 0,9 2,2 m,0.9" titolo="Warren truss under three forces $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $40.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)

## Explanation

By symmetry, $V_A = V_D = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$. A vertical section between $B$ and $G$ cuts members $EG$, $BG$, and $BC$. To isolate the force in $BC$ by the method of sections, take moments about the intersection of $EG$ and $BG$, joint $G$, which lies $h = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ above the chord. The forces to the left of the cut are $V_A$ and the load at $E$. Their total clockwise moment about $G$ is $M_G = 18 \cdot 4.5 - 12 \cdot 3 = 45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The chord-force equation gives
$$
N_{BC} = \frac{M_G}{h} = \frac{45}{2}\,\mathrm{kN} = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
so the bottom chord is **in tension**. Omitting the load at $E$ would give $40.5\,\mathrm{kN}$.
