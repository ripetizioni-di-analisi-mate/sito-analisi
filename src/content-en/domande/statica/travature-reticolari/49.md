In a truss the joint $A$ it is supported by a hinge and only the member $AB$ competes with it, with $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (3, 4)$ in meters; in $A$ do not act loads. From the global equilibrium, $H_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ was obtained to the right and $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. What is the force in member $AB$?

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $14\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)

## Explanation

Member $AB$ has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, so its unit vector from $A$ to $B$ is $\left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$. The member force $N_{AB}\left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ and support reaction $(6, 8)$ act at the joint. Its horizontal and vertical force balances are
$$
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AB} + 6 = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AB} + 8 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
Both equations give $N_{AB} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$, confirming that the support reaction acts along the member. The member is **in compression**. It pushes the joint toward the pin, which reacts toward $B$. The value $14\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the arithmetic sum of the reaction components. Their vector magnitude is $\sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$.
