The cantilever truss shown is attached to the wall with a hinge $A = (0, 0)$ and a roller support at $B = (0, 3)$ that moves along the vertical wall. The other joints are $C = (4, 3)$, $D = (4, 0)$, $E = (8, 0)$ and the $AB$, $BC$, $CE$, $AD$, $DE$, $CD$, $AC$ members. In $D$ a force $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts and in $E$ a force $24\,\mathrm{kN}$, both downward. What is the force in member $BC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3;D,4,0;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-E;A-D;D-E;C-D;A-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera,180;B,carrello,180" forze="4 -1.3,0,-1.3,;E,0,-1.6,P_2" etichette="4.25,-1.1,P_1" quote="A,D,4 m,1.9;D,E,4 m,1.9;0 0,0 3,3 m,-1.5" titolo="Cantilever truss with $P_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ suspended at $D$ and $P_2 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $E$"}

- $80\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $64\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $80\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $60\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)

## Explanation

The roller at $B$ provides the horizontal reaction $H_B$. Taking moments of the whole truss about $A$, reaction $H_B$ has moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and the two loads have moment arms $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $8\,\mathrm{m}$, giving
$$
-3\,H_B - 12 \cdot 4 - 24 \cdot 8 = 0 ,
$$
Thus $H_B = -80\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $80\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. At joint $B$, member $AB$ is vertical, so horizontal equilibrium gives $N_{BC} + H_B = 0$. This yields $N_{BC} = 80\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the member is **in tension**. Omitting the load at $D$ would give $64\,\mathrm{kN}$. Using the moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$ in place of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ would give $60\,\mathrm{kN}$.
