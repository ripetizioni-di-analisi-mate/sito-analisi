The truss shown has the lower horizontal chord $ABCG$, with joints each $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and the upper straight chord $DEFH$, with $D = (0, 2)$, $E = (4, 3)$, $F = (8, 4)$ and $H = (12, 5)$ in meters; the verticals are $AD$, $BE$, $CF$, $GH$ and the diagonals $AE$, $EC$, $FG$. It is supported by a pin at $A$ and a horizontal-level roller support at $G$. At joint $B$ hangs the downward force $P = 36\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the force in diagonal $EC$?

## Explanation

Taking moments about $G$ gives $12\,V_A - 36 \cdot 8 = 0$ and hence $V_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$. A vertical section between $B$ and $C$ cuts $EF$, $EC$, and $BC$. The line $y = 2 + \dfrac{x}{4}$ of the top chord intersects the bottom-chord line at $O = (-8, 0)$, the moment center for the diagonal. The force in $EC$ acts on the left-hand part at $E$ along unit vector $\left(\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$ and has moment $-\dfrac{48}{5}\,N_{EC}$ about $O$. Taking moments about $O$ therefore gives
$$
24 \cdot 8 - 36 \cdot 12 - \frac{48}{5}\,N_{EC} = 0 .
$$
This gives $N_{EC} = -25\,\mathrm{kN}$, placing the diagonal **in compression**. The value $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ follows from $N_d = \dfrac{V_s}{\sin\alpha}$, which requires parallel chords.
