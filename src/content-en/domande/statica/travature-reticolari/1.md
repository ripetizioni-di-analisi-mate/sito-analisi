The truss shown has joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (6, 0)$ and $C = (3, 4)$, with coordinates in meters. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A horizontal force $P = 48\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right at $C$. What is the axial force in member $AC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,1.5,0,P" quote="A,B,6 m,1.1;6 0,6 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Triangular truss with horizontal force $P = 48\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

- $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $32\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $53.33\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)

## Explanation

Taking moments about $A$ gives $6\,V_B - 48 \cdot 4 = 0$, so $V_B = 32\,\mathrm{kN}$. Only $V_A$ and $V_B$ enter the vertical force balance, which gives $V_A = -32\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $32\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Horizontal member $AB$ and member $AC$ meet at joint $A$. The latter has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and unit direction vector $\left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$. Only $N_{AC}$ contributes a member force to the vertical equilibrium equation at this joint:
$$
\frac{4}{5}\,N_{AC} - 32 = 0 ,
$$
so $N_{AC} = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the member is **in tension**. The value $53.33\,\mathrm{kN}$ uses the horizontal direction cosine $\dfrac{3}{5}$ in place of the vertical one.
