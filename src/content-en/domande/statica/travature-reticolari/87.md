The triangular truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (6, 0)$ and $C = (3, 4)$ in meters, a pin at $A$ and a roller support on a horizontal plane in $B$. At joint $A$, a horizontal force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the left and at joint $B$, a horizontal force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Which statement is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="-1.6 0,-1.6,0,;7.6 0,1.6,0," etichette="-1.1,0.35,F;7.1,0.35,F" quote="A,B,6 m,1.6;6 0,6 4,4 m,1.6" titolo="Triangular truss with two opposing forces $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting along line $AB$"}

- The reactions are $H_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A = V_B = 0$, and all members carry zero force
- Member $AB$ is in compression with $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, and all the other members carry zero force
- Members $AC$ and $BC$ are in tension and member $AB$ carries zero force
- The reactions are zero, member $AB$ is in tension with $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, and members $AC$ and $BC$ carry zero force

## Explanation

The two forces act in opposite directions along the same line $AB$. Their resultant and their moment about any point are zero. Equilibrium of the whole truss therefore gives $H_A = V_A = V_B = 0$. Only two noncollinear members meet at unloaded joint $C$, so $AC$ and $BC$ carry zero force. At joint $B$, member $AB$ and the rightward force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ remain. Horizontal equilibrium gives $N_{AB} = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$. Member $AB$ is **in tension** because the two end forces pull its ends apart.
