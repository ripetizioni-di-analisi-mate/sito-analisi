The cantilever truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (0, 3)$ and $C = (4, 3)$ in meters and the members $AB$, $BC$, $AC$. It is attached to the wall with a hinge $B$ and a roller support at $A$ that moves along the vertical wall. In $C$, the force $P = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the horizontal reaction $H_A$ at the roller?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3" aste="A-B;B-C;A-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="B,cerniera,180;A,carrello,180" forze="C,0,-1.2,P" quote="B,C,4 m,-0.9;0 0,0 3,3 m,-1.5" titolo="Cantilever truss with a wall roller at $A$"}

- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right

## Explanation

Take moments of the entire cantilever about pin $B$ to eliminate both pin reactions. Assuming reaction $H_A$ acts to the right, its point of application is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ below $B$, and its counterclockwise moment is $3\,H_A$. Force $P$ has moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and acts clockwise, giving
$$
3\,H_A - 15 \cdot 4 = 0 .
$$
Thus $H_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Member $AC$ is in compression and pushes against the wall. Interchanging the moment arms $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ would give $11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$.
