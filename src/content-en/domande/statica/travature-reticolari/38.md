The cantilever truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $D = (0, 3)$, $B = (4, 0)$, $E = (4, 3)$ and $C = (8, 0)$ in meters and the members $AD$, $AB$, $BC$, $DE$, $EC$, $BE$, $DB$. It is attached to the wall with a hinge $A$ and a roller support at $D$ that moves along the vertical wall. In $E$, the force $P = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the force in diagonal $DB$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,3;B,4,0;E,4,3;C,8,0" aste="A-D;A-B;B-C;D-E;E-C;B-E;D-B" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera,180;D,carrello,180" forze="E,0,-1.4,P" quote="A,B,4 m,1.1;B,C,4 m,1.1;0 0,0 3,3 m,-1.5" titolo="Cantilever truss with force $P = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $E$"}

- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)

## Explanation

A vertical section between the wall and joints $B$ and $E$ cuts $DE$, $DB$, and $AB$. Because $DE$ and $AB$ are parallel, the moment center for $DB$ is at infinity. Instead, use vertical equilibrium of the right-hand part consisting of joints $B$, $E$, and $C$. Assuming tension, $DB$ exerts a force on that part at $B$ toward $D$ with unit vector $\left(-\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$. Thus
$$
\frac{3}{5}\,N_{DB} - 18 = 0 .
$$
Therefore $N_{DB} = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the diagonal is **in tension**. The value $22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained using the direction cosine $\dfrac{4}{5}$ instead of $\dfrac{3}{5}$.
