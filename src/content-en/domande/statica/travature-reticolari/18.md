The truss shown is formed by the four $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, $DA$ members, with $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)$, $C = (4, 3)$ and $D = (0, 3)$ in meters, without diagonals. She it is supported by a pin at $A$ and a pin at $B$. How is the truss classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,4,3;D,0,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-A" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" quote="A,B,4 m,1.1;4 0,4 3,3 m,0.9" titolo="Four-member truss with pin supports at $A$ and $B$"}

- Statically determinate
- Unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$
- Statically indeterminate, with $i = 1$
- Kinematically unstable, with $l = 1$ and $i = 0$

## Explanation

There are $n = 4$ joints, $a = 4$ members, and $v = 2 + 2 = 4$ external scalar restraints, giving $2n = a + v = 8$ and $l - i = 0$. Joints $A$ and $B$ are fixed, so the member $AB$ connecting them is redundant and $i = 1$. Members $AD$, $DC$, and $CB$ form a four-bar linkage with $AB$. Joint $D$ can rotate about $A$ and joint $C$ about $B$, while $DC$ translates. Hence $l = 1$, consistent with $l - i = 0$, and the truss is **unstable and statically indeterminate**.
