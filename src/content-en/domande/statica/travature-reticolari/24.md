The Pratt truss shown has four $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ height panels, $A$ hinge and $E$ roller support, with the $FC$ and $HC$ internal diagonals. A line dividing the truss into two parts cuts three members. Which set of three cut members forms a Ritter section?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" quote="A,B,3 m,1.3;B,C,3 m,1.3;C,D,3 m,1.3;D,E,3 m,1.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Four-panel Pratt truss"}

- $FG$, $FC$, and $BC$
- $FG$, $GH$, and $CG$
- $AB$, $BC$, and $BF$
- $FC$, $HC$, and $CG$

## Explanation

A Ritter section cuts three members whose lines of action are neither concurrent nor all parallel. Members $FG$ and $BC$ are horizontal and parallel. The inclined member $FC$ intersects their lines at two distinct points $F$ and $C$, so $FG$, $FC$, and $BC$ form a valid section. Members $FG$, $GH$, and $CG$ all meet at joint $G$. Cutting them isolates only joint $G$. Likewise, $AB$, $BC$, and $BF$ meet at $B$, while $FC$, $HC$, and $CG$ meet at $C$. When the three lines are concurrent, taking moments about their intersection eliminates all three axial forces, so it cannot isolate any one of them.
