The through-truss bridge shown is a Pratt truss of four $3\,\mathrm{m}$ panels and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ height, with a pin at $A$ and roller support at $E$. A load $P$ down crosses the bridge and acts, in turn, in one of the $B$, $C$, $D$ joints of the bottom chord. At which joint should the load be placed to maximize the tensile force in diagonal $FC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" quote="A,B,3 m,1.3;B,C,3 m,1.3;C,D,3 m,1.3;D,E,3 m,1.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Through-truss bridge with Pratt trusses"}

- At joint $B$
- At joint $D$
- At joint $C$
- At any of the three joints because the tensile force is the same

## Explanation

A vertical section through the second panel cuts $FG$, $FC$, and $BC$. Diagonal $FC$ slopes down from left to right, so vertical equilibrium gives $N_{FC} = \dfrac{V_s}{\sin\alpha} = \dfrac{5}{4}\,V_s$. Its tension is greatest when $V_s$ is greatest. With the load at $B$, $V_A = \dfrac{3P}{4}$. The load lies to the left of the section, so $V_s = \dfrac{3P}{4} - P = -\dfrac{P}{4}$ and the diagonal is in compression. A load at $C$ gives $V_s = V_A = \dfrac{P}{2}$ and hence $N_{FC} = \dfrac{5P}{8}$. A load at $D$ gives $V_s = V_A = \dfrac{P}{4}$ and hence $N_{FC} = \dfrac{5P}{16}$. Maximum tension therefore occurs with the load at joint **$C$**, the first joint to the right of the cut.
