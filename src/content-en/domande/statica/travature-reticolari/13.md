The triangular truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (6, 0)$ and $C = (3, 4)$ in meters. In $A$ there is a hinge and in $B$ a roller support that moves along a vertical plane. How is the truss classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" forze="C,0,-1.3,P" quote="A,B,6 m,1.1;6 0,6 4,4 m,1.2" titolo="Triangular truss with a roller at $B$ on a vertical surface"}

- Statically determinate
- Unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$
- Kinematically unstable, with $l = 1$ and $i = 0$
- Statically indeterminate, with $i = 1$

## Explanation

There are $n = 3$ joints, $a = 3$ members, and $v = 3$ scalar restraints, giving $2n = a + v = 6$ and $l - i = 0$. The roller on the vertical surface has a horizontal reaction whose line of action, $AB$, passes through $A$. Both pin reactions also pass through $A$. All three lines of action therefore intersect at $A$, leaving the rigid triangle free to rotate about $A$. Thus $l = 1$. The count $l - i = 0$ gives $i = 1$, so the truss is **unstable and statically indeterminate**. The count alone could incorrectly suggest static determinacy.
