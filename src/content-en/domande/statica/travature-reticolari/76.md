The K-truss shown has two $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$-wide and $4\,\mathrm{m}$-high panels. The joints $K = (1.5;\ 2)$ and $J = (3;\ 2)$ split the verticals $BE$ and $CF$ in half, and in each panel the two diagonals start from the middle joint of the right vertical: $KD$ and $KA$ in the first, $JE$ and $JB$ in the second. The truss it is supported by a pin at $A$ and a horizontal roller support at $C$, and in $E$ the force $P = 32\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the force in diagonal $KD$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;B,1.5,0;K,1.5,2;E,1.5,4;C,3,0;J,3,2;F,3,4" aste="A-B;B-C;D-E;E-F;A-D;E-K;K-B;F-J;J-C;K-D;K-A;J-E;J-B" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" forze="E,0,-1.2,P" quote="A,B,1.5 m,1.1;B,C,1.5 m,1.1;3 0,3 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="K-truss with force $P = 32\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $E$"}

- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)

## Explanation

Taking moments about $C$ gives $-3\,V_A + 32 \cdot 1.5 = 0$ and hence $V_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$. The diagonals have length $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$. From $K$ their unit vectors are $\left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ toward $D$ and $\left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ toward $A$. At joint $K$ the vertical-member segments have no horizontal component, so horizontal equilibrium involves only the diagonals and gives $N_{KA} = -N_{KD}$. A vertical cut in the first panel crosses $DE$, $KD$, $KA$, and $AB$. Vertical equilibrium of the left-hand part, containing joints $A$ and $D$, eliminates the chord forces and gives
$$
16 - \frac{4}{5}\,N_{KD} + \frac{4}{5}\,N_{KA} = 16 - \frac{8}{5}\,N_{KD} = 0 .
$$
This gives $N_{KD} = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in **tension**, and $N_{KA} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$. In this K-truss the two diagonals share the panel force $V_s = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ equally. Assigning the entire $V_s$ to one diagonal, as in a single-diagonal panel, would give $20\,\mathrm{kN}$.
