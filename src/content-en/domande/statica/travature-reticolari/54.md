The truss shown is a three-hinged trussed arch: the triangle $ADC$ and triangle $CEB$ are connected only at joint $C$, with $A = (0, 0)$, $D = (3, 0)$, $C = (4, 3)$, $E = (5, 0)$ and $B = (8, 0)$ in meters, and are supported by the pin supports $A$ and $B$. In $D$, the force $P = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the horizontal reaction $H_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,3,0;C,4,3;E,5,0;B,8,0" aste="A-D;D-C;A-C;C-E;E-B;C-B" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="D,0,-1.3,P" quote="A,D,3 m,1.2;D,E,2 m,1.2;E,B,3 m,1.2;8 0,8 3,3 m,0.9" titolo="Three-hinged trussed arch with force $P = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $D$"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right

## Explanation

There are four unknown reactions. In addition to equilibrium of the entire truss, take moments of the right-hand part $CEB$ about internal hinge $C$. No external load acts on this part, and the force at $C$ has zero moment about $C$. The reaction at $B$ remains. About $C$ its component $V_B$ has moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and its component $H_B$ has moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$. The resulting equations are
$$
\begin{cases}
8\,V_B - 24 \cdot 3 = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_B + 3\,H_B = 0 \\[4pt]
H_A + H_B = 0
\end{cases}
$$
These equations express moment equilibrium of the truss about $A$, moment equilibrium of the right-hand part about $C$, and horizontal equilibrium of the truss. They give $V_B = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$, $H_B = -12\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $H_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$. Pin $A$ therefore pushes the arch **to the right** with force $12\,\mathrm{kN}$. The thrust $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ would occur if the load were applied at crown $C$.
