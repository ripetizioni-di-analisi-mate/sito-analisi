At joint $K = (0, 0)$ of a truss the horizontal member $KA$, with $A = (-4, 0)$, the member $KB$, with $B = (3, 4)$, and the vertical member $KC$, with $C = (0, -3)$, in meters. In $K$, the force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Which set of three member forces $(N_{KA}, N_{KB}, N_{KC})$, positive in tension, satisfies equilibrium at joint $K$?

::struttura{nodi="K,0,0;A,-4,0;B,3,4;C,0,-3" aste="K-A;K-B;K-C" reticolare=1 forze="K,0,-1.2,P" titolo="Joint $K$ with three members and force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $(6,\ 10,\ 2)\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $(8,\ 10,\ -2)\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $(6,\ 10,\ -2)\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $(-6,\ 10,\ -2)\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

From joint $K$, the unit direction vectors are $(-1, 0)$ for member $KA$, $\left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ for $KB$, and $(0, -1)$ for $KC$. The horizontal and vertical equilibrium equations at the joint are
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{KA} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{KB} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{KB} - N_{KC} - 10 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
For the set $(6, 10, -2)$, the first equation gives $-6 + 6 = 0$ and the second $8 + 2 - 10 = 0$, satisfying both. With $(6, 10, 2)$ the second equation gives $-4$. With $(8, 10, -2)$ the first gives $-2$, and with $(-6, 10, -2)$ the first gives $12$. The other sets therefore fail equilibrium. This joint alone has three unknowns and only two equations. Equilibrium at other joints is needed to find the forces, after which this joint provides a check.
