The truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (6, 0)$, $E = (12, 0)$, $C = (3, 4)$ and $D = (9, 4)$ in meters and the members $AB$, $BE$, $CD$, $AC$, $CB$, $BD$, $DE$; it is supported by a pin at $A$ and a horizontal roller support at $E$. In $C$, the force $P = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the force in top chord $CD$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;E,12,0;C,3,4;D,9,4" aste="A-B;B-E;C-D;A-C;C-B;B-D;D-E" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.6,P" quote="A,B,6 m,1.1;B,E,6 m,1.1;12 0,12 4,4 m,1.3" titolo="Truss with force $P = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)

## Explanation

Taking moments about $A$ gives $12\,V_E - 40 \cdot 3 = 0$ and hence $V_E = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$. At joint $E$, member $ED$ has unit vector $\left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$. Vertical equilibrium $\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{DE} + 10 = 0$ gives $N_{DE} = -12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. At joint $D$, which has no external load, vertical equilibrium $-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{BD} - \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{DE} = 0$ gives $N_{BD} = 12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, and horizontal equilibrium gives
$$
-N_{CD} - \frac{3}{5}\,N_{BD} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{DE} = 0 .
$$
Therefore $N_{CD} = -7.5 - 7.5 = -15\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the top chord is **in compression**. The value $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by forgetting the contribution of one of the two diagonals.
