A truss of three panel $3\,\mathrm{m}$ side panels has the inclined end members of $45^\circ$ and only one internal diagonal, $EC$, also at $45^\circ$. The joints are $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (3, 0)$, $C = (6, 0)$, $D = (9, 0)$, $E = (3, 3)$ and $F = (6, 3)$ in meters; the hinge is in $A$ and the roller support on a horizontal plane in $D$. In the top joint $F$ the force $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is $N_{EC}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,3,3;F,6,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;E-F;A-E;F-D;B-E;C-F;E-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="F,0,-1.3,P" quote="A,B,3 m,1.1;B,C,3 m,1.1;C,D,3 m,1.1;9 0,9 3,3 m,0.9" titolo="Truss with diagonals at $45^\circ$ and force $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $F$"}

- $10\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $10\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $20\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)

## Explanation

Taking moments about $D$ gives $9\,V_A - 30 \cdot 3 = 0$, so $V_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. Vertical equilibrium gives $V_D = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$. A vertical section through the second panel cuts $EF$, $EC$, and $BC$. Since $EF$ and $BC$ are parallel and horizontal, vertical equilibrium of the left-hand section isolates the diagonal force. The only external force on the left is $V_A$, giving $V_s = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$. Diagonal $EC$ slopes down to the right and $\sin 45^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$, so
$$
N_{EC} = \frac{V_s}{\sin 45^\circ} = \frac{10}{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} = 10\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 14.1\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
and the diagonal is in tension. Omitting division by $\sin 45^\circ$ would give $10\,\mathrm{kN}$. Using $V_D$ in place of $V_A$ would give $20\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN}$.
