The truss shown has joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)$, and $C = (4, 3)$, with coordinates in meters. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A downward force $P = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. Which members carry zero force?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,4,3" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.3,P" quote="A,B,4 m,1.1;4 0,4 3,3 m,0.9" titolo="Triangular truss with force $P$ on the vertical through the roller"}

- Members $AB$ and $AC$
- Only member $AB$
- Members $AB$ and $BC$
- No member

## Explanation

At joint $B$ the roller reaction and member $BC$ are vertical. Only member $AB$ contributes to horizontal equilibrium, so $N_{AB} = 0$. At joint $C$ the load $P$ and member $BC$ are vertical. The horizontal equilibrium equation therefore contains only the component $-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AC}$ of member $AC$, giving $N_{AC} = 0$. Members $AB$ and $AC$ are **zero-force members**, as the zero-force member rule predicts when a load at a joint acts along one of two members. Member $BC$ carries the entire load in compression, with $N_{BC} = -24\,\mathrm{kN}$.
