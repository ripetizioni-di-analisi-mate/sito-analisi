The Pratt truss shown has four $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ height panels, a pin at $A$, a horizontal-level roller support at $E$ and three equal forces downward at joints $F$, $G$, $H$. They swap constraints, putting roller support at $A$ and pin at $E$, without changing loads. Which statement is true?

- The bottom chord goes into compression because the pin has moved to the opposite end
- The chord forces change and the diagonal forces stay the same
- The member forces stay the same because the horizontal reaction is zero and the vertical reactions are the same in both cases
- The truss becomes unstable because the roller at $A$ allows vertical translation

## Explanation

With the pin at $A$, horizontal equilibrium gives $H_A = 0$ because all applied loads are vertical. Moving the pin to $E$ similarly gives $H_E = 0$. The vertical reactions follow from moments about the supports, independent of which support is the pin. Joints $A$ and $E$ therefore receive the same vertical forces $V_A$ and $V_E$ in both arrangements. The joint equations and member forces are identical. The roller at $A$ restrains vertical translation and the truss remains statically determinate. With horizontal loads, moving the pin would change where those loads are resisted and could change the member forces.
