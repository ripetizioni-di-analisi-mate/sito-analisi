The truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (6, 0)$ and $C = (2, 3)$ in meters, a pin at $A$ and a roller support on a horizontal plane in $B$. In $C$, the force $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the vertical reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,2,3" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.4,P" quote="A,2 0,2 m,1.1;2 0,B,4 m,1.1;6 0,6 3,3 m,0.9" titolo="Triangular truss with force $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

The triangular truss is rigid, so its support reactions follow from equilibrium of the whole truss. Taking moments about $B$ eliminates reaction $V_B$. Reaction $V_A$ has moment arm $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and load $P$, whose line of action passes through $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, has moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Thus
$$
-6\,V_A + 30 \cdot 4 = 0 .
$$
This gives $V_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. Vertical equilibrium then gives $V_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$. Interchanging the reaction moment arms would give $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ for $V_A$.
