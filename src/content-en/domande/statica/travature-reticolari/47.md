In the bottom chord of a truss the member $BC$, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, is connected to the joints $B$ and $C$ with hinges. In the middle of the member, then out of the joints, is hung a hoist that transmits the force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. What is the maximum bending moment in member $BC$ due to this force?

::struttura{nodi="B,0,0;C,4,0" aste="B-C" reticolare=1 forze="2 0,0,-1.1,P" quote="B,C,4 m,0.5" titolo="Member $BC$ with force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ applied at its midpoint"}

- $0$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The force is applied between joints, so member $BC$ is not a two-force member. With pins at its ends it acts as a simply supported beam of span $L = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ carrying a midspan point load. Each end carries $\dfrac{P}{2} = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the midspan bending moment is
$$
M = \frac{P}{2} \cdot \frac{L}{2} = \frac{PL}{4} = \frac{12 \cdot 4}{4} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The member also carries the truss axial force, found by applying the equivalent end loads $\dfrac{P}{2}$ at joints $B$ and $C$. The value $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ corresponds to $\dfrac{PL}{8}$, the formula for a uniformly distributed load of total magnitude $P$.
