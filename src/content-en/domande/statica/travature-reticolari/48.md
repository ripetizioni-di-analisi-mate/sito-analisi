The cover shown is a Pratt truss of four $3\,\mathrm{m}$ panels and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ height, with a pin at $A$ and a bilateral roller support at $E$. The wind depression on the cover results in three forces of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ **upward** at joints $F$, $G$, $H$. Which statement about the member forces is true?

- The interior diagonals $FC$ and $HC$ are in compression, and the bottom chord is in compression
- The interior diagonals $FC$ and $HC$ are in tension because the interior diagonals of a Pratt truss are in tension
- The interior diagonals $FC$ and $HC$ are in compression, and the bottom chord is in tension
- The member forces have the same signs as when downward forces act at the same joints

## Explanation

By symmetry, $V_A = V_E = -15\,\mathrm{kN}$, so both support reactions act downward. For a cut in the second panel, the vertical forces to the left sum to $V_s = -15 + 10 = -5\,\mathrm{kN}$. Diagonal $FC$ slopes down from left to right. Vertical equilibrium of the section therefore gives
$$
N_{FC} = \frac{V_s}{\sin\alpha} = \frac{-5}{\frac{4}{5}} = -6.25\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
and the diagonal is **in compression**. By symmetry the same holds for $HC$. The moment center for bottom chord $BC$ is $F$. The external forces on the left give clockwise moment $M_F = -15 \cdot 3 = -45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about $F$, so $N_{BC} = \dfrac{M_F}{h} = -11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the bottom chord is also **in compression**. Since member forces vary linearly with the applied loads, reversing every load reverses every member-force sign.
