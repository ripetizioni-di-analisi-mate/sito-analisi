You want to calculate the axial forces of the cantilever shown by the method of joints, without first deriving the reactions of the hinge $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (0, 3)$ roller support fixed to the wall. The $E = (6, 3)$ tip brings the load $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ down; the other joints are $C = (3, 3)$ and $D = (3, 0)$. Which joint should be analyzed first?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,3,3;D,3,0;E,6,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-E;A-D;D-E;C-D;A-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera,180;B,carrello,180" forze="E,0,-1.4,P" quote="A,D,3 m,1.1;0 3,0 0,3 m,1.4" titolo="Cantilever truss with force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $E$"}

- At joint $E$
- At joint $D$
- At joint $C$
- At joint $B$

## Explanation

The method of joints requires a joint with at most two unknowns at each step. Only the two noncollinear members $CE$ and $DE$ meet at $E$, where the known load is $P$. Its two equilibrium equations immediately give $N_{CE}$ and $N_{DE}$. Joint $D$ has three unknowns, $AD$, $DE$, and $CD$. Joint $C$ has four. At joint $B$, the unknown roller reaction is additional to the member forces $AB$ and $BC$. After solving joint $E$, only $AD$ and $CD$ remain unknown at joint $D$. Continue toward the wall, determining the support reactions last.
