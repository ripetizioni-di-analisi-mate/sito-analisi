At a joint with no applied load two collinear horizontal members meet a third vertical member. The joint is also a support that exerts an upward $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ reaction. The third member extends upward from the joint. What member force is required by equilibrium?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0,-;C,2,0;B,4,0,-;D,2,2" aste="A-C;C-B;C-D" cerniere="C" reazioni="C,0,1.2,5 kN" titolo="Support joint with two collinear members"}

- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ tension
- Zero, because the other two members are collinear.
- An indeterminate force without the member lengths.
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ compression

## Explanation

The support reaction enters joint equilibrium. The vertical member must exert $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward on the joint, pushing it away from the upper end. It is therefore in compression.
