The truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (8, 0)$, $C = (4, 3)$ and $D = (12, 3)$ in meters and the members $AB$, $AC$, $BC$, $CD$, $BD$; it is supported by a pin at $A$ and a horizontal roller support at $B$. In $C$, the force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Which members carry zero force?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0;C,4,3;D,12,3" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C;C-D;B-D" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.6,P" quote="A,B,8 m,1.1;12 0,12 3,3 m,1.4" titolo="Truss with two members meeting at joint $D$"}

- No member
- Only member $CD$
- Members $BC$ and $BD$
- Members $CD$ and $BD$

## Explanation

Joint $D$ has no applied load or support reaction. Its only two members, horizontal $CD$ and inclined $BD$, are noncollinear. Projecting equilibrium perpendicular to $CD$ leaves only $BD$ with a nonzero coefficient, so $N_{BD} = 0$. Applying the same argument to the other member gives $N_{CD} = 0$. Thus **$CD$ and $BD$** are zero-force members, and triangle $ABC$ carries the load at $C$.
