The truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)$, $C = (4, 3)$ and $D = (0, 3)$ in meters; the two diagonals of the rectangle are connected at their meeting point, the joint $O = (2;\ 1.5)$. The members are $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $AO$, $OC$, $BO$, $OD$, and the chord $CD$ is missing. The truss it is supported by a pin at $A$ and a horizontal-plane roller support at $B$, and in $D$ the horizontal force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is the force in member $BO$?

- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $0$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)

## Explanation

At joint $D$, vertical member $AD$ meets member $DO$ of length $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and unit vector $\left(\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$ from $D$ toward $O$. The force $AD$ does not enter horizontal equilibrium, so $\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{OD} + 12 = 0$ and $N_{OD} = -15\,\mathrm{kN}$. Unloaded joint $O$ has two pairs of collinear members, $AO$ with $OC$ and $BO$ with $OD$. Project equilibrium perpendicular to $AC$ to eliminate $AO$ and $OC$. The forces in $BO$ and $OD$ have opposite unit directions, giving $(N_{OD} - N_{BO})\,c = 0$ with $c \neq 0$ because $BD$ is not parallel to $AC$. This yields $N_{BO} = N_{OD} = -15\,\mathrm{kN}$. Member $BO$ is **in compression**, just as if diagonal $BD$ were a single member.
