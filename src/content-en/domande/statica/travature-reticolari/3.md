## Explanation

The truss has $n = 8$ joints, $a = 12$ members, and $v = 2 + 1 = 3$ external restraints. The count gives $2n - (a + v) = 16 - 15 = 1$. Since $2n - (a + v) = l - i$, it follows that $l \geq 1$. Panel $CDHG$ has no diagonal. It can deform as a four-bar linkage when its members rotate about their joints. The other panels are triangulated and no member is redundant, so there is exactly one mechanism. Thus $l = 1$ and $i = 0$, and the truss is **unstable**.
