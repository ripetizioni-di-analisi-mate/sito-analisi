The truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)$, $C = (1, 4)$ and $D = (3, 4)$ in meters, the inclined verticals $AC$ and $BD$, which converge upward, the crossbeam $CD$, the base $AB$ and the diagonal $AD$. It rests on a pin at $A$ and on a horizontal-level roller support at $B$. At joint $C$ the wind exerts the horizontal force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right. What is the force in diagonal $AD$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,1,4;D,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-D;C-D;A-D" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,1.3,0,F" tratteggi="-0.6,2 4.6,2" etichette="-0.9,2.2,s" quote="A,B,4 m,1;C,D,2 m,-0.8;4.6 0,4.6 4,4 m,0" titolo="Tower truss with converging legs, force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$, and section $s$"}

- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)

## Explanation

Horizontal section $s$ cuts the two tower legs $AC$ and $BD$ and diagonal $AD$. The leg centerlines $x = \dfrac{y}{4}$ and $x = 4 - \dfrac{y}{4}$ meet at $O = (2, 8)$, outside the tower. This is the moment center for the diagonal. Take moments of the upper free body about $O$ and assume the diagonal is in tension. Its force acts at $D$ toward $A$, with unit vector $\left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$. The force $F$ has counterclockwise moment $12 \cdot (8 - 4) = 48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about $O$, and the moment of the force at $D$ is
$$
1 \cdot \left(-\frac{4}{5}\,N_{AD}\right) - (-4) \cdot \left(-\frac{3}{5}\,N_{AD}\right) = -\frac{16}{5}\,N_{AD} .
$$
Thus $48 - \dfrac{16}{5}\,N_{AD} = 0$, giving $N_{AD} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a tensile force in the diagonal. Treating the legs as parallel when balancing horizontal forces would give $20\,\mathrm{kN}$. Dividing the moment of $F$ by the tower height rather than the perpendicular moment arm of the diagonal would give $12\,\mathrm{kN}$.
