The trussed tower shown has $AB$ base wide $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and two $4\,\mathrm{m}$ high segments, with the $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (3, 0)$, $C = (0, 4)$, $D = (3, 4)$, $E = (0, 8)$, $F = (3, 8)$ joints in meters. It is supported by a pin at $A$ and a horizontal-level roller support at $B$. At the top, at joint $E$, the wind exerts horizontal force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right. What is the vertical reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,0,4;D,3,4;E,0,8;F,3,8" aste="A-B;A-C;B-D;C-D;A-D;C-E;D-F;E-F;C-F" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="E,1.5,0,F" quote="A,B,3 m,1.1;3 0,3 4,4 m,0.9;3 4,3 8,4 m,0.9" titolo="Trussed tower with wind force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the top"}

- $32\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $32\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward

## Explanation

The horizontal wind force acts at elevation $8\,\mathrm{m}$. Its moment about $A$ is clockwise, with moment arm $8\,\mathrm{m}$. Taking moments about $A$ and balancing vertical forces gives
$$
\begin{cases}
3\,V_B - 12 \cdot 8 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B = 0
\end{cases}
$$
The equations yield $V_B = 32\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A = -32\,\mathrm{kN}$, so the reaction at $A$ is $32\,\mathrm{kN}$ **downward**. The wind tends to overturn the tower about $B$, and pin $A$ holds it down. Using the first-panel height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ instead of the full tower height would give $16\,\mathrm{kN}$.
