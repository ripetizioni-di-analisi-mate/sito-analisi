The tower shown has width $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $8\,\mathrm{m}$. It consists of two four-sided panels, each of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, braced by diagonal $AD$ in the lower panel and $CF$ in the upper panel. The tower has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$. A rightward wind load $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts only at joint $C$ at midheight. Which members carry zero force?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,0,4;D,3,4;E,0,8;F,3,8" aste="A-B;A-C;B-D;C-D;A-D;C-E;D-F;E-F;C-F" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,1.5,0,F" quote="A,B,3 m,1.1;3 0,3 4,4 m,0.9;3 4,3 8,4 m,0.9" titolo="Trussed tower with force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at joint $C$"}

- Members $AB$, $AC$, $CE$, $CF$, $DF$, and $EF$
- Members $CE$, $CF$, $DF$, and $EF$
- Members $CE$ and $EF$
- No member

## Explanation

Only the noncollinear members $CE$ and $EF$ meet at unloaded joint $E$, so both carry zero force. This leaves only noncollinear members $DF$ and $CF$ at joint $F$, and both also carry zero force. At joint $C$ the remaining forces are in vertical member $AC$, in horizontal member $CD$, and the applied horizontal load $F$. Vertical equilibrium gives $N_{AC} = 0$ and horizontal equilibrium gives $N_{CD} = -12\,\mathrm{kN}$. Finally, at joint $B$ both member $BD$ and the roller reaction are vertical, so horizontal equilibrium gives $N_{AB} = 0$. There are **six** zero-force members. Only $CD$, $AD$, and $BD$ carry force.
