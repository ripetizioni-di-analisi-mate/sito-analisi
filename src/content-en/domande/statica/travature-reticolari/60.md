The roof truss shown has the tie $AC$ of span $8\,\mathrm{m}$, divided in half by the joint $B$, the $AD$ and $DC$ struts with the $D = (4, 3)$ ridge and the $BD$ king post; is backed with a pin at $A$ and a horizontal roller support at $C$. At joint $B$ of the tie hangs the downward force $P = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the force in strut $AD$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,4,3" aste="A-B;B-C;A-D;D-C;B-D" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" forze="4 -1.4,0,-1.4," etichette="4.3,-1.1,P" quote="A,B,4 m,1.9;B,C,4 m,1.9;8 0,8 3,3 m,0.9" titolo="Roof truss with king post $BD$ and force $P = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ suspended at $B$"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)

## Explanation

By symmetry, $V_A = V_C = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $H_A = 0$. At joint $A$, tie $AB$ has unit vector $(1, 0)$. Strut $AD$ has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and unit vector $\left(\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$. The horizontal and vertical equilibrium equations are
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{AB} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AD} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AD} + 9 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
These give $N_{AD} = -15\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{AB} = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$. The strut is **in compression**. The king post carries tension $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ by equilibrium of joint $B$ and transfers the load to the ridge. The struts then carry it to the supports. The value $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the force in the tie.
