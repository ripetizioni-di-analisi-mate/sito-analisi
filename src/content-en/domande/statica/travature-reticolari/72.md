On the Pratt truss shown, with four $3\,\mathrm{m}$ panels, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ height, $A$ hinge and $E$ roller support, the wind exerts only the horizontal $W = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ force to the right at joint $F$ of the top chord. What is the force in bottom chord $BC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="F,1.5,0,W" quote="A,B,3 m,1.3;B,C,3 m,1.3;C,D,3 m,1.3;D,E,3 m,1.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Pratt truss with horizontal force $W = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $F$"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)

## Explanation

Horizontal equilibrium of the whole truss gives $H_A = -12\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. Taking moments about $A$ gives $12\,V_E - 12 \cdot 4 = 0$, hence $V_E = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the downward reaction $V_A = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$. A vertical section between $B$ and $C$ cuts $FG$, $FC$, and $BC$, and the moment center for $BC$ is $F$. About $F$ on the left-hand free body, force $W$ has zero moment. Reaction $H_A$ has moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and acts clockwise. Reaction $V_A$ has moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and acts counterclockwise. The assumed tensile force in $BC$ acts to the right at $B$ and has counterclockwise moment $4\,N_{BC}$. Moment equilibrium about $F$ gives
$$
-12 \cdot 4 + 4 \cdot 3 + 4\,N_{BC} = 0 ,
$$
The result is $N_{BC} = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ in **tension**. Neglecting the moment of horizontal reaction $H_A$ would give $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression. The horizontal reaction is present because the applied load is inclined.
