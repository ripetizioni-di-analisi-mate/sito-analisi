The truss shown is an equilateral triangle on the $4\,\mathrm{m}$ side, with the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)$ and $C = \left(2,\ 2\sqrt{3}\right)$ in meters, a pin at $A$ and a roller support on a horizontal plane in $B$. In $C$, the force $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the force in member $AB$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,2,3.464" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.2,P" quote="A,B,4 m,1.1" titolo="Equilateral triangular truss with force $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $5\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $5\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $15\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)

## Explanation

By symmetry, $H_A = 0$ and $V_A = V_B = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$. At joint $A$, member $AB$ has unit vector $(1, 0)$, and member $AC$ is inclined at $60^\circ$ with unit vector $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}, \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$. Vertical equilibrium at joint $A$ gives $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,N_{AC} + 15 = 0$ and hence $N_{AC} = -10\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$. Horizontal equilibrium gives $N_{AB} + \dfrac{1}{2}\,N_{AC} = 0$, so
$$
N_{AB} = 5\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 8.66\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
and chord $AB$ is in tension. Interchanging the sine and cosine of $60^\circ$ would give $15\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$.
