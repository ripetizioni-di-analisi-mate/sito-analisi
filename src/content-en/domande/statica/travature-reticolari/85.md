The Warren truss shown, with base triangles $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $2\,\mathrm{m}$, supports the scaffolding of a bridge and it is supported by a pin at $A$ and a horizontal roller support at $D$. The braking of a vehicle transmits the horizontal force $F = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right to the joint $C$ of the bottom chord. What is the force in chord $BC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,1.5,2;G,4.5,2;H,7.5,2" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;E-G;G-H;A-E;E-B;B-G;G-C;C-H;H-D" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="C,1.4,0,F" quote="A,B,3 m,1.2;B,C,3 m,1.2;C,D,3 m,1.2;9 0,9 2,2 m,0.9" titolo="Warren truss with braking force $F = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ at joint $C$"}

- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $0$

## Explanation

The line of action of $F$ follows the bottom chord through $A$. Taking moments about $A$ therefore gives $V_D = 0$, hence $V_A = 0$ and $H_A = -15\,\mathrm{kN}$. Consider $N_{AB} = N_{BC} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ with every other member force zero. At joint $C$ the tension in $BC$ balances $F$. At $B$ the two collinear tensile forces cancel, and at $A$ the tension in $AB$ balances $H_A$. All other joints are force-free. This satisfies every joint equation, and static determinacy makes the solution unique. Thus $N_{BC} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ in **tension**, with the load transmitted along the bottom chord to the pin.
