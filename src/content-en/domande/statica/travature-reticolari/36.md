In the Pratt truss shown, with four $3\,\mathrm{m}$ panels, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ height, $A$ hinge and $E$ roller support, two load cases were calculated. With only the force of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ down at joint $G$ the diagonal $FC$ has force $12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension); with only the downward force $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at joint $F$ has $6.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ force (compression). What is the force in $FC$ when the two loads act together?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="F,0,-1.5,P;G,0,-1.5,P" quote="A,B,3 m,1.3;B,C,3 m,1.3;C,D,3 m,1.3;D,E,3 m,1.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Pratt truss with two forces of magnitude $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $F$ and $G$"}

- $18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $6.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $6.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)

## Explanation

The truss equilibrium equations are linear in member forces, reactions, and applied loads. The member force under two simultaneous loads therefore equals the algebraic sum of the forces induced by each load separately. With tension taken as positive,
$$
N_{FC} = 12.5 + (-6.25) = 6.25\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
so the diagonal carries $6.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension. To check, cut through the second panel. With both loads $V_A = 25\,\mathrm{kN}$ applied, the vertical forces to the left of the section sum to $V_s = 25 - 20 = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$, giving $N_{FC} = \dfrac{5}{\frac{4}{5}} = 6.25\,\mathrm{kN}$. Adding the magnitudes and ignoring their signs would give $18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$.
