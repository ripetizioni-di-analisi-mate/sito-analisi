The triangular truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (8, 0)$ and $C = (4, 3)$ in meters, a pin at $A$ and $B$ a roller support on inclined plane, with reaction $R_B$ directed as the $\left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ unit vector. In $C$, a force $F = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts directed along the line $CB$, towards $B$. What is the force in member $AB$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0;C,4,3" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,306.87" forze="C,1.2,-0.9,F" quote="A,B,8 m,1.3;0 0,0 3,3 m,-0.9" titolo="Triangular truss with force $F$ acting along $CB$ and an inclined roller support at $B$"}

- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $7\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $0$
- $7\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)

## Explanation

At joint $C$, force $F$ acts along member $CB$. Treat it as an additional collinear force along $CB$. The zero-force member rule then gives $N_{AC} = 0$ and $N_{BC} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$. At joint $B$, member $BC$ has unit direction vector $\left(-\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$, and vertical equilibrium $\dfrac{3}{5}(-20) + \dfrac{4}{5}\,R_B = 0$ gives $R_B = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$. Horizontal equilibrium at joint $B$ gives
$$
-N_{AB} - \frac{4}{5}(-20) - \frac{3}{5} \cdot 15 = 0 ,
$$
so $N_{AB} = 16 - 9 = 7\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the member is **in tension**. Omitting the horizontal component of the inclined reaction $R_B$ would give $16\,\mathrm{kN}$.
