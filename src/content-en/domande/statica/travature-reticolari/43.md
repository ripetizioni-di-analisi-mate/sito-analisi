The triangular truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (6, 0)$ and $C = (3, 4)$ in meters and the members $AB$, $AC$, $BC$. It is supported by a pin at $A$ and, at joint $B$, with a roller moving along a vertical wall, so the reaction at $B$ is horizontal. Which statement is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" quote="A,B,6 m,1.1;-0.8 0,-0.8 4,4 m,0" titolo="Triangular truss with a roller at $B$ against a vertical wall"}

- The truss is unstable because the line of action of the roller reaction passes through pin $A$
- The truss is statically determinate because it is a simple truss with three external restraints
- The truss is unstable because $2n > a + v$
- The truss is statically determinate because the roller prevents horizontal displacement of $B$

## Explanation

The truss has $n = 3$ joints, $a = 3$ members, and $v = 3$ external scalar restraints, so $2n = a + v = 6$. The triangle is a rigid simple truss. Both pin reaction components pass through $A$. The horizontal roller reaction acts along line $y = 0$, which also passes through $A$. The three reaction lines are therefore concurrent at $A$ and fail to restrain rotation. The triangle can rotate infinitesimally about $A$ while $B$ slides vertically along the wall. The truss is unstable with $l = 1$ and, since $l - i = 2n - (a + v) = 0$, it is also statically indeterminate with $i = 1$. For example, a vertical force at $C$ has a nonzero moment about $A$ that no support reaction can balance.
