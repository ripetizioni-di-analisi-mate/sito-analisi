The Pratt truss shown has four $3\,\mathrm{m}$ panels and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ height, pin at $A$, roller support at $E$ and three $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ forces down at joints $F$, $G$, $H$. Line $s$ divides the truss into the left side, with the joints $A$, $B$, $C$, $F$, and the right side, cutting off the four members $FG$, $CG$, $HC$ and $CD$. Which member force can be found from a single equilibrium equation for the left portion?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="F,0,-1.5,P;G,0,-1.5,P;H,0,-1.5,P" tratteggi="5,5.4 5,2 6.8,2 7.2,-0.9" etichette="5.15,5.3,s" quote="A,B,3 m,1.3;B,C,3 m,1.3;C,D,3 m,1.3;D,E,3 m,1.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Line $s$ cutting members $FG$, $CG$, $HC$, and $CD$"}

- $N_{CG}$
- $N_{HC}$
- $N_{CD}$
- $N_{FG}$

## Explanation

Although the section cuts four members, three of them, $CG$, $HC$, and $CD$, meet at joint $C$. Their moment arms are zero when moments of the left-hand part are taken about $C$. This isolates $N_{FG}$, just as in Ritter’s method. Using $V_A = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ and taking counterclockwise moments as positive gives
$$
-30 \cdot 6 + 20 \cdot 3 - 4\,N_{FG} = 0 ,
$$
This gives $N_{FG} = -30\,\mathrm{kN}$. The two force balances on the cut part relate the other three unknown member forces, but cannot determine all three by themselves.
