The cantilever truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (3, 0)$, $C = (6, 0)$ and $D = (3, 4)$ in meters and the members $AB$, $BC$, $BD$, $CD$, $AD$. It is fixed to the wall with a hinge $A$ and supported at $D$ by a two-force link, the reaction $R_D$ of which has the direction of the $\left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ unit vector. In $B$ a force $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts and in $C$ a force $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts, downward. What is the reaction $R_D$ of the link?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,3,4" aste="A-B;B-C;B-D;C-D;A-D" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera,180;D,pendolo,126.87" forze="3 -1.3,0,-1.3,;C,0,-1.6,P_2" etichette="3.25,-1.1,P_1" quote="A,B,3 m,1.9;B,C,3 m,1.9;6 0,6 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Cantilever truss with an inclined link at $D$, $P_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $P_2 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $75\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $45\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $37.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Take moments of the whole truss about pin $A$ to eliminate $H_A$ and $V_A$. Reaction $R_D$, applied at $D = (3, 4)$, has components $-\dfrac{3}{5}\,R_D$ and $\dfrac{4}{5}\,R_D$ and produces a counterclockwise moment $3 \cdot \dfrac{4}{5}\,R_D + 4 \cdot \dfrac{3}{5}\,R_D = \dfrac{24}{5}\,R_D$ about $A$. The applied loads have a clockwise moment $12 \cdot 3 + 24 \cdot 6 = 180\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, so
$$
\frac{24}{5}\,R_D - 180 = 0 ,
$$
giving $R_D = 37.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $75\,\mathrm{kN}$ accounts only for the vertical reaction component and omits the moment of the horizontal component.
