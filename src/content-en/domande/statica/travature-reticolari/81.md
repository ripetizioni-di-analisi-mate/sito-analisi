The truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (6, 0)$ and $C = (3, 4)$ in meters and it is supported by three two-force links: two vertical in $A$ and $B$ and one horizontal in $C$, connected to a wall on the right. In $C$ the force $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward and in $B$ the horizontal $Q = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ force acts left. What is the reaction $V_B$ of the link at $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,pendolo;B,pendolo;C,pendolo,0" forze="C,0,-1.4,P;B,-1.4,0,Q" quote="A,B,6 m,1.6;0 0,0 4,4 m,-0.9" titolo="Triangular truss on three links, with $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$ and $Q = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$"}

- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $31\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $23\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $7\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

The lines of action of the three two-force support links are neither concurrent nor all parallel, so the truss is statically determinate. The horizontal link reaction $R_C$ and applied force $Q$ are the only horizontal forces. Horizontal equilibrium gives $R_C = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right. Taking moments about $A$ eliminates $Q$ because its line of action passes through $A$, while $R_C$ acts at elevation $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and gives a clockwise moment. Thus
$$
6\,V_B - 30 \cdot 3 - 12 \cdot 4 = 0 .
$$
This gives $V_B = 23\,\mathrm{kN}$ **upward**. Vertical equilibrium then gives $V_A = 7\,\mathrm{kN}$. Neglecting the moment of the horizontal reaction at $C$ would give $15\,\mathrm{kN}$.
