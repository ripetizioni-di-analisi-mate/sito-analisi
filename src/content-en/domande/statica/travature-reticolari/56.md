The Pratt truss shown has four $3\,\mathrm{m}$ panels and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ height, pin at $A$ and roller support at $E$. At joint $G$, a horizontal force of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right and at joint $C$, on the same vertical, a horizontal force of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the left. What is reaction $V_E$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="G,1.5,0,F;C,-1.5,0,F" quote="A,B,3 m,2.3;B,C,3 m,2.3;C,D,3 m,2.3;D,E,3 m,2.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Pratt truss with two opposing horizontal forces $F = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $G$ and $C$"}

- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $0$
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

The two forces have zero resultant, so $H_A = 0$. Their parallel lines of action are separated by $4\,\mathrm{m}$, giving a couple with moment $-15 \cdot 4 = -60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, or $60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise, about any point. Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives
$$
12\,V_E - 15 \cdot 4 = 0 ,
$$
Thus the reaction is $V_E = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ **upward**. Vertical equilibrium gives $V_A = -5\,\mathrm{kN}$ for the other reaction, directed downward. The value $0$ accounts for the zero resultant of the loads but omits their couple.
