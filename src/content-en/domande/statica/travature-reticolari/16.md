In a truss with loads applied only in joints, the member $AB$ has $N_{AB} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$ force. What are the internal forces and moments at the midpoint of the member?

- $N = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$, $T = 0$, $M = 0$
- $N = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$, $T = 0$, $M = 0$
- $N = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$, $T = 0$, $M = 0$
- $N = 0$, $T = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M = 0$

## Explanation

Ideal truss members are straight, carry no intermediate loads, and connect through pins that transmit no couples. Each member is therefore a two-force member. The end forces are equal, opposite, and collinear with its axis, so every section carries the same axial force. At midspan $N = N_{AB} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$, with zero shear and bending moment. The value $-10\,\mathrm{kN}$ would divide the axial force between two halves even though the full force is transmitted through every section.
