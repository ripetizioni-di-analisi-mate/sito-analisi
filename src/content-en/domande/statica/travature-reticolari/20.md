A truss has $7$ joints and is bound to the outside with a hinge and roller support. How many members must it have to satisfy condition $2n = a + v$?

- $12$
- $10$
- $14$
- $11$

## Explanation

The pin supplies $2$ scalar restraints and the roller $1$, giving $v = 3$. With $n = 7$, the count $2n = a + v$ becomes $14 = a + 3$, hence
$$
a = 2n - v = 14 - 3 = 11 .
$$
Counting the pin as only one restraint would give $12$. Ignoring the supports would give $14$. The result $11$ is also the number of members in a simple truss with $7$ joints, for which $a = 2n - 3$.
