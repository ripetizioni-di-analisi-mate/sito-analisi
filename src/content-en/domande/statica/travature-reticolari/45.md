The Pratt truss shown has four $3\,\mathrm{m}$ panels and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ height, pin at $A$, roller support at $E$ and three equal forces of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ down at joints $F$, $G$, $H$. The truss and loads are symmetrical compared to the vertical through $C$ and $G$. Which statement about the member forces is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="F,0,-1.5,P;G,0,-1.5,P;H,0,-1.5,P" quote="A,B,3 m,1.3;B,C,3 m,1.3;C,D,3 m,1.3;D,E,3 m,1.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Pratt truss under three forces $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $N_{FC} = -N_{HC}$, because the two diagonals slope in opposite directions
- $N_{BF} = N_{CG}$, because all the verticals carry the same load
- $N_{FC} = N_{HC}$ and $N_{AB} = N_{DE}$
- $N_{FG} = N_{BC}$, because the chords in the same panel carry the same force

## Explanation

The structure and loads are symmetric, so the support reactions are equal, $V_A = V_E = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$. Members mirrored about the vertical line through $C$ and $G$ have equal axial forces with the same sign. Diagonals $FC$ and $HC$ are mirror images, giving $N_{FC} = N_{HC} = 12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. Chords $AB$ and $DE$ are also mirror images, giving $N_{AB} = N_{DE} = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The verticals carry different forces because $BF$ is a zero-force member while $N_{CG} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$. In the second panel the two chord forces are $N_{FG} = -30\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{BC} = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. They differ because the external forces to the left of the cut have different moments about their respective centers, $C$ for $FG$ and $F$ for $BC$.
