The Warren truss shown has three spans of $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ height, with the joints $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ on the bottom chord and $E$, $G$, $H$ on the upper one; it is supported by a pin at $A$ and a horizontal roller support at $D$. At the joints $B$ and $C$ are hung two forces of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Which diagonals carry zero force?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,12,0;E,2,3;G,6,3;H,10,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;E-G;G-H;A-E;E-B;B-G;G-C;C-H;H-D" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="4 -1.4,0,-1.4,;8 -1.4,0,-1.4," etichette="4.3,-1.1,P;8.3,-1.1,P" quote="A,B,4 m,1.9;B,C,4 m,1.9;C,D,4 m,1.9;12 0,12 3,3 m,0.9" titolo="Warren truss with two forces $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ suspended at $B$ and $C$"}

- No diagonal
- Diagonals $BG$ and $GC$
- Diagonals $EB$ and $CH$
- Diagonals $AE$ and $HD$

## Explanation

By symmetry, $V_A = V_D = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$. A vertical section between $x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ cuts top chord $EG$, diagonal $BG$, and bottom chord $BC$. The vertical forces to the left of the cut sum to $V_s = 20 - 20 = 0$. The parallel chords are horizontal, so their forces do not enter vertical equilibrium. The section gives $N_{BG} \sin\alpha = 0$, making $BG$ a **zero-force member**. A section between $x = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x = 8\,\mathrm{m}$ similarly shows that $GC$ carries zero force. In the end panels $V_s = \pm 20\,\mathrm{kN}$, so diagonals $AE$, $EB$, $CH$, and $HD$ do carry force.
