The truss shown has four panels of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ height with the Pratt scheme, but is supported with a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $D$, so that the $DEH$ panel forms one overhang. At the $E$ extreme of the overhang the force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the force in top chord $GH$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="E,0,-1.5,P" quote="A,B,3 m,1.3;B,C,3 m,1.3;C,D,3 m,1.3;D,E,3 m,1.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Truss with overhang $DEH$ and force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $E$"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)

## Explanation

Taking moments about $A$ gives $9\,V_D - 12 \cdot 12 = 0$, hence $V_D = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, so the reaction at $A$ acts downward. A vertical section between $C$ and $D$ cuts $GH$, diagonal $HC$, and $CD$. The moment center for $GH$ is joint $C$. The forces on the left produce clockwise moment $M_C = V_A \cdot 6 = -24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about $C$, so the chord-force equation gives
$$
N_{GH} = -\frac{M_C}{h} = -\frac{-24}{4} = 6\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The top chord is **in tension**. Near the support of the overhang, the bending moment is negative and puts the top fibers in tension, as in a cantilever. Using $D$ instead of $C$ as the moment center would give $9\,\mathrm{kN}$.
