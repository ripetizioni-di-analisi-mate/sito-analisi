To calculate the force in the vertical $DF$ of the tower shown, wide $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and formed by two $4\,\mathrm{m}$ high segments, the tower is cut with the horizontal section $s$ at $6\,\mathrm{m}$ elevation. At the top, at joint $E$, the wind exerts $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right; at the base are a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$. What is $N_{DF}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,0,4;D,3,4;E,0,8;F,3,8" aste="A-B;A-C;B-D;C-D;A-D;C-E;D-F;E-F;C-F" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="E,1.5,0,F" tratteggi="-1,6 3.4,6" etichette="-1.2,6.3,s" quote="A,B,3 m,1.1;3 0,3 4,4 m,0.9;3 4,3 8,4 m,0.9" titolo="Trussed tower with force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the top and section $s$"}

- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $32\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)

## Explanation

The section cuts $CE$, $CF$, and $DF$. Isolate the upper part, which contains joints $E$ and $F$ and no supports. The lines of action of $CE$ and $CF$ pass through $C$, making $C$ the moment center for $DF$. About $C$, the wind has moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and acts clockwise. Assuming tension, $DF$ acts on the upper part at $F$ toward $D$, downward, with moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and a clockwise moment. Taking moments about $C$ gives
$$
-12 \cdot 4 - 3\,N_{DF} = 0 ,
$$
This gives $N_{DF} = -16\,\mathrm{kN}$. The vertical on the leeward side is **in compression**. Using the entire tower height $8\,\mathrm{m}$ as the moment arm would give $32\,\mathrm{kN}$. That would use $A$ as the moment center, which is inappropriate for the section $s$.
