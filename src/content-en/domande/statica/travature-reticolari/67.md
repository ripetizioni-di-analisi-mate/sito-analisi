Only one force acts on the Pratt shown: $P = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ down in the center joint $G$ of the top chord. The truss has four panels of $3\,\mathrm{m}$, height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, pin at $A$ and roller support at $E$. Which members have zero axial force?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="G,0,-1.5,P" quote="A,B,3 m,1.3;B,C,3 m,1.3;C,D,3 m,1.3;D,E,3 m,1.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Pratt truss with only force $P = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $G$"}

- Verticals $BF$, $DH$, and $CG$
- Only verticals $BF$ and $DH$
- Verticals $BF$ and $DH$ and diagonals $FC$ and $HC$
- No member

## Explanation

At each unloaded joint $B$ and $D$, two collinear bottom-chord members meet a vertical. Verticals $BF$ and $DH$ therefore **carry zero force**. At joint $G$, members $FG$ and $GH$ are collinear and the applied load acts along vertical $CG$. Thus $N_{CG} = -24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the vertical is in compression. Disregarding $BF$ at joint $F$ leaves three noncollinear members $AF$, $FG$, and $FC$, so the zero-force member rule does not apply there. In the second panel $V_s = V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the force in diagonal $FC$, which slopes down to the right, is given by $N_{FC} = \dfrac{12}{\frac{4}{5}} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$.
