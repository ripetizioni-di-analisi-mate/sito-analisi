The truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (6, 0)$, $E = (12, 0)$, $C = (3, 4)$ and $D = (9, 4)$ in meters and the members $AB$, $BE$, $CD$, $AC$, $CB$, $BD$, $DE$; it is supported by a pin at $A$ and a horizontal roller support at $E$. Which statement is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;E,12,0;C,3,4;D,9,4" aste="A-B;B-E;C-D;A-C;C-B;B-D;D-E" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m,1.1;B,E,6 m,1.1;12 0,12 4,4 m,1.3" titolo="Truss with five joints and seven members"}

- The truss is statically determinate because it is a simple truss with three properly arranged external restraints
- The truss is unstable because $2n > a + v$
- The truss is statically indeterminate because $2n < a + v$
- The truss is statically indeterminate because member $CD$ is redundant

## Explanation

There are $n = 5$ joints, $a = 7$ members, and $v = 3$ scalar restraints. Thus $2n = a + v = 10$, satisfying the necessary count. Starting with triangle $ABC$, add joint $D$ with members $CD$ and $BD$, then joint $E$ with members $BE$ and $DE$. Each added joint has two noncollinear members, so the truss is a rigid **simple truss**. The pin at $A$ and roller at $E$ supply three reactions whose lines of action are neither concurrent nor all parallel. The simple truss is therefore **statically determinate**. Removing $CD$ would leave joint $D$ connected to the triangle by a single member, making the truss unstable.
