The joint $C = (3, 0)$ shown is hung on two fixed hooks, $A = (0, 4)$ and $B = (7, 3)$ in meters, by means of the members $CA$ and $CB$, connected with hinges. In $C$, the force $P = 50\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the force in member $CA$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,4;B,7,3;C,3,0" aste="C-A;C-B" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera,90;B,cerniera,90" forze="C,0,-1.4,P" riferimento=0 titolo="Joint $C$ suspended from members $CA$ and $CB$ and carrying force $P = 50\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $50\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)

## Explanation

From joint $C$ the unit vectors are $\left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ toward $A$ and $\left(\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$ toward $B$. Their dot product $-\dfrac{12}{25} + \dfrac{12}{25}$ is zero, so the members are perpendicular. Project joint equilibrium along $CA$. Member $CB$ then drops out, and force $P$ contributes $-\dfrac{4}{5}\,P$. The equation is
$$
N_{CA} - \frac{4}{5} \cdot 50 = 0 ,
$$
This gives $N_{CA} = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$, so the member is **in tension**. Projecting along $CB$ instead gives $N_{CB} = \dfrac{3}{5} \cdot 50 = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$, the value obtained by interchanging the direction cosines.
