The Pratt truss shown has four $3\,\mathrm{m}$ panels and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ height, pin at $A$ and roller support at $E$. At joints $B$, $C$, $D$ of the bottom chord are hung three forces of $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. What is the force in diagonal $FC$?

- $18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)

## Explanation

By symmetry, $V_A = V_E = 45\,\mathrm{kN}$. A vertical section between $B$ and $C$ cuts members $FG$, $FC$, and $BC$. The parallel chords are horizontal, so vertical equilibrium of the left-hand part, containing joints $A$, $B$, and $F$, isolates the diagonal force. Diagonal $FC$ slopes down to the right with $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{4}{5}$. Assuming tension, its vertical force component on the left-hand part is $-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{FC}$, giving
$$
45 - 30 - \frac{4}{5}\,N_{FC} = 0 .
$$
Therefore $N_{FC} = 18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the diagonal is **in tension**. The value $25\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by dividing by $\dfrac{3}{5}$ instead of $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{4}{5}$.
