The trussed beam shown consists of top chord $AB$ of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$ with midpoint joint $C$, vertical $CD$, and ties $AD$ and $DB$. The coordinates $D = (4, -3)$ are in meters. The beam is pinned at $A$ and supported by a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. At $C$ a force $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the axial force in tie $AD$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,8,0;D,4,-3" aste="A-C;C-B;C-D;A-D;D-B" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.4,P" quote="A,C,4 m,-1.1;C,B,4 m,-1.1;8 0,8 -3,3 m,-0.9" titolo="Trussed beam with force $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

- $25\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $25\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)

## Explanation

By symmetry, $V_A = V_B = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $H_A = 0$. At joint $A$, chord $AC$ has unit vector $(1, 0)$, and tie $AD$ has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and unit vector $\left(\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$. The horizontal and vertical equilibrium equations are
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{AC} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AD} = 0 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AD} + 15 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
The vertical equation gives $N_{AD} = 25\,\mathrm{kN}$, placing the tie **in tension**. The horizontal equation gives $N_{AC} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$, so the top chord is in compression. Using the cosine $\dfrac{4}{5}$ in place of the sine $\dfrac{3}{5}$ in the vertical equation would give $20\,\mathrm{kN}$.
