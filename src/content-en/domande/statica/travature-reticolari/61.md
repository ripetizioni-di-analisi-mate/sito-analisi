Two symmetric tension members support a $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ vertical load at their common lower joint. Each member makes the same angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal and can carry at most $10\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the smallest permissible angle?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,2;C,2,0;B,4,2" catene="A-C;C-B" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera,90;B,cerniera,90" tratteggi="0.4,0 3.6,0" forze="C,0,-1.2,12 kN" titolo="Tie rods in a symmetric arrangement"}

- $\arccos(0.6)$
- $\arcsin(0.3)$
- $\arcsin(0.6)$
- $60^\circ$

## Explanation

The two vertical components balance the load, so $2N\sin\alpha=12$. The limit $N\le10$ requires $\sin\alpha\ge0.6$. For an angle between zero and $90^\circ$, the minimum is $\arcsin(0.6)$, approximately $36.9^\circ$.
