The truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)$, $C = (8, 0)$, $D = (4, 3)$ and $E = (8, 3)$ in meters and the members $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $DE$, $CE$, $DB$, $DC$; it is supported by a pin at $A$ and a horizontal roller support at $C$. In $D$, the force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Which members carry zero force?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,4,3;E,8,3" aste="A-B;B-C;A-D;D-E;C-E;D-B;D-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" forze="D,0,-1.4,P" quote="A,B,4 m,1.1;B,C,4 m,1.1;8 0,8 3,3 m,1.3" titolo="Truss with force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $D$"}

- Only members $DE$ and $CE$
- Members $DB$, $DE$, and $CE$
- Only member $DB$
- Members $DB$ and $DC$

## Explanation

Only two noncollinear members $DE$ and $CE$ meet at unloaded joint $E$, so both are zero-force members. At unloaded joint $B$, collinear members $AB$ and $BC$ meet $DB$. Thus $DB$ carries zero force and $N_{AB} = N_{BC}$. The zero-force members are **$DB$, $DE$, and $CE$**. The load at $D$ reaches the supports through $AD$ and $DC$.
