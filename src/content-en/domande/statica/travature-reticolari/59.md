A slab is hung from the bottom chord of the Howe truss shown and unloaded $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ down into each of the $B$, $C$, $D$ joints. The truss has panels of $3\,\mathrm{m}$, height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, pin at $A$ and roller support at $E$. What force does vertical $BF$ carry if no load hangs from it?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;B-G;D-G" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="3 -1.6,0,-1.6,;6 -1.6,0,-1.6,;9 -1.6,0,-1.6," etichette="3.3,-1.3,P;6.3,-1.3,P;9.3,-1.3,P" quote="A,B,3 m,2.3;B,C,3 m,2.3;C,D,3 m,2.3;D,E,3 m,2.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Howe truss with three forces $P = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ suspended from the bottom chord"}

- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension)
- $0$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression)

## Explanation

By symmetry, $V_A = V_E = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $H_A = 0$. At joint $A$, chord $AB$ has unit vector $(1, 0)$ and member $AF$ has unit vector $\left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$. Joint equilibrium gives
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{AB} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AF} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AF} + 24 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
This gives $N_{AF} = -30\,\mathrm{kN}$. At unloaded joint $F$, members $AF$ and $FG$ meet vertical $BF$. Chord $FG$ has no vertical component. Member $FA$ contributes $-\dfrac{4}{5}$ vertically and member $FB$ contributes $-1$. Thus $-\dfrac{4}{5}(-30) - N_{BF} = 0$, or $N_{BF} = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$. The vertical is **in tension** and transfers the vertical component of the compressive force in $AF$ upward.
