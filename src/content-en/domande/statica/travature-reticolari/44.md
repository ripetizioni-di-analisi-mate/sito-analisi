The Warren truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (8, 0)$, $C = (16, 0)$, $D = (4, 3)$, $E = (12, 3)$ in meters and the members $AB$, $BC$, $DE$, $AD$, $DB$, $BE$, $EC$; it is supported by a pin at $A$ and a horizontal roller support at $C$. At joint $B$ hangs the downward force $P = 60\,\mathrm{kN}$. Which statement about diagonals $AD$ and $DB$ is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0;C,16,0;D,4,3;E,12,3" aste="A-B;B-C;D-E;A-D;D-B;B-E;E-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" forze="8 -2.2,0,-2.2," etichette="8.5,-1.8,P" quote="A,B,8 m,2.6;B,C,8 m,2.6;16 0,16 3,3 m,1" titolo="Warren truss with force $P = 60\,\mathrm{kN}$ suspended at $B$"}

- Both diagonals are in compression
- $AD$ is in compression and $DB$ is in tension
- $AD$ is in tension and $DB$ is in compression
- $AD$ is in compression and $DB$ carries zero force

## Explanation

By symmetry, $V_A = V_C = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$. At joint $A$, diagonal $AD$ has unit vector $\left(\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$. Vertical equilibrium $\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AD} + 30 = 0$ gives $N_{AD} = -50\,\mathrm{kN}$, placing $AD$ in compression. A vertical section between $D$ and $B$ cuts $DE$, $DB$, and $AB$. To the left of the cut, $V_s = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$. Diagonal $DB$ slopes down to the right with $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{3}{5}$, so $N_{DB} = \dfrac{V_s}{\sin\alpha} = 50\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $DB$ is in tension. From the support toward midspan the diagonals **alternate between compression and tension**, as in a Warren truss under these loads.
