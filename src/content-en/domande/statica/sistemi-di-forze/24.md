On a body act the force $F_1 = 7\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, the force $F_2 = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and the force $F_3 = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $13\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10.44\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $7\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The resultant is the sum of the vectors, which is calculated component by component:
$$
R_x = 7 - 3 = 4\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
R_y = 3\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
So its intensity is $R = \sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $13\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the sum of the intensities, which coincides with the intensity of the resultant only for forces with the same direction and the same direction. The value $10.44\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by also taking $F_2$ to the right.
