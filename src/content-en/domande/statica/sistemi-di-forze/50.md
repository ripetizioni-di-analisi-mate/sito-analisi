A vertical load of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, directed downward, acts on an inclined rod of $30^\circ$ on the horizontal. What is the component of the load perpendicular to the member axis?

- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8.66\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $11.55\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

A reference is used with an axis along the rod and one perpendicular to the rod. The axis of the rod forms $30^\circ$ with the horizontal, then the perpendicular to the rod forms $30^\circ$ with the vertical. The load is vertical, so its component perpendicular to the rod is
$$
10\cos 30^\circ = 8.66\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
and the component along the rod is $10\sin 30^\circ = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ is then the axial component, and $11.55\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by dividing by the cosine instead of multiplying.
