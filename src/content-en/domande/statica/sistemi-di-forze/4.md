- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $-1\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

For the **change of reference point formula**
$$
M_P = M_O + (x_O - x_P)\,R_y - (y_O - y_P)\,R_x .
$$
With $x_O - x_P = -2\,\mathrm{m}$, $y_O - y_P = -1\,\mathrm{m}$, $R_x = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $R_y = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$ you get
$$
M_P = 10 + (-2) \cdot (-4) - (-1) \cdot 3 = 10 + 8 + 3 = 21\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The value $-1\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by using the vector $\overrightarrow{OP}$ instead of $\overrightarrow{PO}$, i.e. by the opposite sign transport term.
