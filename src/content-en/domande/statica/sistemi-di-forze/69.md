The supporting wall in the figure has $2\,\mathrm{m}$ wide base, from foot $O$ to point $D$. On the wall act the $W = 100\,\mathrm{kN}$ weight, vertical, with $1\,\mathrm{m}$ line of action from $O$, and the $S = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ ground thrust, horizontal and directed to the left, applied on the right wall to $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from the base. How far from $O$ does the line of action of the resultant intersect the base?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,4.5 0,4.5" nodi="O,0,0;D,2,0" forze="1 2.25,0,-1.2,W;2 1.5,-1.2,0,S" quote="O,D,2 m,0.8;D,2 1.5,1.5 m,1.6" titolo="Retaining wall under weight $W$ and earth thrust $S$"}

- $0.55\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.45\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.78\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

You put the origin at $O$. The resultant has $R_x = -30\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $R_y = -100\,\mathrm{kN}$ components, and the moment about $O$ is
$$
M_O = 1 \cdot (-100) - 1.5 \cdot (-30) = -100 + 45 = -55\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The line of action of the resultant has equation $x R_y - y R_x = M_O$, i.e. $-100x + 30y = -55$. On the basis you have $y = 0$, so
$$
x = \frac{-55}{-100} = 0.55\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The leftward thrust shifts the resultant to the $O$ foot relative to the weight-only line of action. The value $1.45\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained with the moment sign of the inverted thrust, and $0.78\,\mathrm{m}$ by applying the thrust at half its elevation.
