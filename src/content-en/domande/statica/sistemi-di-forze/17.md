In the cantilever $AB$ in the figure, $3\,\mathrm{m}$ long, the force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the end $B$, directed downwards and to the right and tilted by $30^\circ$ below the horizontal. What is the moment of the force about $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,1.04,-0.6,F" quote="A,B,3 m,0.8" titolo="Cantilever under inclined force $F$ at end $B$"}

- $-25.98\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $-15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $-30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The components of the force are $F_x = 10\cos 30^\circ = 8.66\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $F_y = -10\sin 30^\circ = -5\,\mathrm{kN}$. by **Varignon's theorem** the moment of force is the sum of the moments of the two components applied at $B$. The horizontal component has line of action along the cantilever axis, which passes through $A$, so it has zero moment. The vertical component has $3\,\mathrm{m}$ arm and is directed downward to the right of $A$, therefore
$$
M_A = 3 \cdot (-5) = -15\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
clockwise. The value $-25.98\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by using the cosine instead of the sine, and $-30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ by multiplying the intensity by the length of the cantilever.
