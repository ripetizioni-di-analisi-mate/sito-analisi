Member $AB$ ranges from $A = (0, 0)$ to $B = (4, 3)$, with coordinates in meters. At $B$, a force $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ is directed along the axis of the rod, from $A$ to $B$. Which system applied at $A$ is equivalent to the force?

- The same force at $A$ and a couple of magnitude $50\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- The same force at $A$, with no couple
- The same force applied at $A$ and a clockwise couple of $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- The same force at $A$ and a counterclockwise couple of magnitude $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The force has the direction of $\overrightarrow{AB}$, so its line of action is the line $AB$, which passes through $A$. For the **scroll** the force can be moved to $A$ along its own line of action without adding anything. The same result follows from the transport couple: the force has $(8, 6)\,\mathrm{kN}$ components, and its moment about $A$ is
$$
\mathcal{M}_t = 4 \cdot 6 - 3 \cdot 8 = 0 .
$$
$24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ values are the moments of only one of the two components, which cancel each other out, and $50\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the product of the force for the length of the rod.
