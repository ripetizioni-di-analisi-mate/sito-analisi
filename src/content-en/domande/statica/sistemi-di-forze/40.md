The force $\vec{F} = (6, 8)\,\mathrm{kN}$ is applied at point $P = (2, 1)$, with coordinates in meters. It replaces with its horizontal component of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, applied at the origin $O$, and its vertical component of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$, applied in the point $(2, 0)$. What couple must be added to make the new system equivalent to force $\vec{F}$?

- No couple
- A counterclockwise couple of magnitude $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- A clockwise couple of $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- A counterclockwise couple of magnitude $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

By **Varignon's theorem** the force $\vec{F}$ is equivalent to its two components applied at $P$. The line of action of the vertical component is the line $x = 2$, which passes through $(2, 0)$, so for the **scroll** the vertical component moves to $(2, 0)$ without couple. The line of action of the horizontal component is the line $y = 1$, which does not pass through $O$, so its displacement at $O$ requires the transport couple, equal to its moment about $O$:
$$
\mathcal{M}_t = -\,1 \cdot 6 = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
It therefore needs a clockwise couple of $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment of $\vec{F}$ about $O$; the new pairless system has $2 \cdot 8 = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ moment about $O$, and the $10 - 16 = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ difference is the couple moment to add.
