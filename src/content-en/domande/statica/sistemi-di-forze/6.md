Beam $AB$ has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and carries a uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over its entire length and a downward force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$. How far from $A$ does the line of action of the resultant pass?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="B,0,-1.3,F" quote="A,B,5 m,0.8" titolo="Uniform load $q$ and point force $F$ at end $B$"}

- $3.75\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.33\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.67\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The uniform load has resultant $4 \cdot 5 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ down, applied in the middle, a distance $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The two forces are parallel, so the line of action of the resultant passes through the **center of parallel forces**:
$$
x_C = \frac{20 \cdot 2.5 + 10 \cdot 5}{20 + 10} = \frac{100}{30} = \frac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{m} = 3.33\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $3.75\,\mathrm{m}$ is the average of the two axes without the weights, and $1.67\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance of the line of action from $B$.
