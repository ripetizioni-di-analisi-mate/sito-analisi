The plate shown carries a downward force $F_1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $(1, 0)$, a rightward force $F_2 = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $(0, 2)$, and a clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Coordinates are in meters. What is the equation of the line of action of the resultant, with $x$ and $y$ in meters?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,2 0,2" nodi="O,0,0;P,1,0,-;Q,0,2,-" forze="1 -1.2,0,-1.2,F_1;Q,1,0,F_2" coppie="2 1,-1,ℳ" quote="O,1 0,1 m,1.5;O,Q,2 m,-0.8" titolo="Plate under forces $F_1$ and $F_2$ and clockwise couple $\mathcal{M}$"}

- $2x + y = 4$
- $x + 2y = 5.2$
- $2x + y = 5.2$
- $2x + y = 2.8$

## Explanation

The resultant is $\vec{R} = (5, -10)\,\mathrm{kN}$. The moment about the origin adds the moments of the two forces and that of the clockwise couple:
$$
M_O = 1 \cdot (-10) - 2 \cdot 5 - 6 = -26\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
For **reduction of a planar force system** the line of action of the resultant has equation $x\,R_y - y\,R_x = M_O$, i.e. $-10x - 5y = -26$. Divide by $-5$ you get $2x + y = 5.2$. Without the couple you would find $2x + y = 4$, and with the couple taking counterclockwise $2x + y = 2.8$.
