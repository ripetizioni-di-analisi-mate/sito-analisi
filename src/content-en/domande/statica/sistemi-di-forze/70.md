A force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ down is applied at point $P = (3, 0)$, with coordinates in meters. What is the set of points about which the moment of the force equals $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$?

- The line $x = 1$
- The line $x = 5$
- Only the point $(1, 0)$
- The circle with center $P$ and radius $2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For the $Q = (x_Q, y_Q)$ reference point the force, with $F_x = 0$ and $F_y = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$, has moment
$$
M_Q = (3 - x_Q) \cdot (-10) - (0 - y_Q) \cdot 0 = -10\,(3 - x_Q) .
$$
The moment does not depend on $y_Q$, and equals $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ if and only if $3 - x_Q = 2$, i.e. $x_Q = 1$. Therefore the reference points sought form the line $x = 1$, parallel to the line of action and at a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from it, on the left side, where a load down has clockwise moment. On the line $x = 5$ the moment equals $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and the radius $2\,\mathrm{m}$ circumference contains points with distances other than the line of action.
