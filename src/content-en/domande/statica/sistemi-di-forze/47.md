One hook is supported by two cables, each stretched with a force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$. The two cables rise from the hook from opposite sides and both form an angle of $30^\circ$ with the vertical. What is the resultant of the two cable forces acting on the hook?

- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $8.66\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $17.32\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

Each force makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the vertical, giving an upward component of $10\cos 30^\circ = 8.66\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a horizontal component of magnitude $10\sin 30^\circ = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The two ropes lie on opposite sides of the vertical, so the horizontal components cancel. The resultant points upward and has magnitude
$$
R_y = 2 \cdot 10\cos 30^\circ = 17.32\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
Upwards. The value $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the sum of the intensities, which coincides with the resultant only for parallel cables; the value $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by using the sine instead of the cosine.
