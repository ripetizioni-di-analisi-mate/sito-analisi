Two horizontal forces act to the right on vertical member $AB$ of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The first, $F_1 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, acts a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ above the base $A$. The second, $F_2 = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, acts at the top point $B$. At what height above the base does the line of action of the resultant pass?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4" aste="A-B" forze="0 1,1.2,0,F_1;B,1.2,0,F_2" quote="A,0 1,1 m,0.8;0 1,B,3 m,0.8" titolo="Column under two horizontal forces"}

- $2.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The two forces are parallel and act in the same direction, so the resultant equals $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and its line of action passes through the **center of parallel forces**. The vertical coordinate of the center is
$$
y_C = \frac{6 \cdot 1 + 4 \cdot 4}{10} = \frac{22}{10} = 2.2\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The center is closer to the force majeure, which acts at $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from the base. The value $2.8\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by exchanging the two intensities, and $1.8\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance of the line of action from the top.
