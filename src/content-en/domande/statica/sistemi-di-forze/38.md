A platform rests on two scales $3\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. After a crate is added, the left reading increases by $80\,\mathrm{N}$ and the right reading by $40\,\mathrm{N}$. How far from the left scale is the line of action of the crate’s weight?

- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Subtracting the empty readings removes the platform’s weight. The crate weighs $80+40=120\,\mathrm{N}$. Its moment about the left scale gives $120x=40\cdot3$, so $x=1\,\mathrm{m}$.
