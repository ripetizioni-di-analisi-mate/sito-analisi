On the triangle in the figure, with $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (3, 0)$ and $C = (0, 4)$ in meters, three forces act directed along the sides: at $A$ the force $F_1 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ directed towards $B$, at $B$ the force $F_2 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ directed towards $C$ and at $C$ the force $F_3 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ directed towards $A$. Which system is equivalent to the three forces?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 0,4" nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,0,4" forze="A,1.2,0,F_1;B,-0.72,0.96,F_2;C,0,-1.2,F_3" titolo="Three forces directed along the sides of triangle $ABC$"}

- To a clockwise couple of $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- A counterclockwise couple of magnitude $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- A counterclockwise couple of magnitude $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- The zero force system

## Explanation

The $BC$ side is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long and has the direction of $(-3, 4)$, so $F_2$ has $(-6, 8)\,\mathrm{kN}$ components; The other two forces are $(6, 0)\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $(0, -8)\,\mathrm{kN}$. The resultant is
$$
\vec{R} = (6 - 6 + 0,\; 0 + 8 - 8) = (0, 0) .
$$
It is calculated the moment about $A$: $F_1$ and $F_3$ have lines of action for $A$, so null moment, and $F_2$, applied at $(3, 0)$, has $3 \cdot 8 - 0 \cdot (-6) = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ moment. The resultant is zero and the moment is not, so the system is equivalent to an **counterclockwise couple** of $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The null system would be obtained with three concurrent forces, while here the lines of action form a triangle.
