The vertical wall $AB$ shown has base $A$ and height $3\,\mathrm{m}$. It retains water on its right up to its top $B$. Per meter of wall width, the water exerts a horizontal load that increases linearly from $0$ at $B$ to $30\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$. What is the magnitude of the moment about the base $A$ per meter of width?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro" carichi="A,B,1.2,0,q,180" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Water pressure on a wall, varying from $30\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at the base to $0$ at the top"}

- $45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $67.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $90\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $135\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The load is triangular on the length of $3\,\mathrm{m}$, so its resultant is the area of the diagram,
$$
Q = \frac{30 \cdot 3}{2} = 45\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
horizontal. The line of action passes through the triangle's centroid, one third of the height from the side where the load is maximum, i.e. to $1\,\mathrm{m}$ above the foot $A$. The moment arm about $A$ is therefore $1\,\mathrm{m}$ and the moment equals $45 \cdot 1 = 45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ per meter wide. The value $67.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ applies the resultant mid-height, and $90\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ applies it to a third of the top.
