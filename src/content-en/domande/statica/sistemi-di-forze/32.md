An $W = 600\,\mathrm{N}$ weight gate is supported by two hinges placed on the same vertical, at $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from each other, and its centroid is located at $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ horizontally from the vertical of the hinges. The upper hinge exerts only a horizontal force on the gate. What is the magnitude of this force if the gate is in equilibrium?

- $750\,\mathrm{N}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N}$
- $480\,\mathrm{N}$
- $240\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The weight, horizontal $H$ force of the top hinge and lower hinge force act on the gate. The system is balanced, so the resultant moment about the lower hinge is zero; about that point the force of the lower hinge has zero moment. The weight has moment arm $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the force $H$ has moment arm $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$, so
$$
H \cdot 1.5 = 600 \cdot 1.2 , \qquad H = 480\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $750\,\mathrm{N}$ is obtained by exchanging the two arms, and $240\,\mathrm{N}$ dividing in half between the two hinges.
