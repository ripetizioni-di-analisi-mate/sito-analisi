A machine applies a downward $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ force and a counterclockwise $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ couple at its mounting point. The only guide available for a replacement force extends from $x=0$ to $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of the mount. Where can a single force reproduce the system?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;B,3.5,0" aste="O-B" forze="O,0,-1.3,6 kN" coppie="O,1,9 kN m" quote="O,B,2 m,0.8" titolo="Machine mount and available segment for the replacement force"}

- At $x=1.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- At $x=0$
- Nowhere on the guide
- At $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The replacement force must act downward. Its moment about the mount is $-6x$. Producing $+9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ would require $x=-1.5\,\mathrm{m}$, to the left of the mount and outside the guide.
