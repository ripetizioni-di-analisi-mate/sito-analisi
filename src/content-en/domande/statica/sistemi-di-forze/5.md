- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, a distance $2.8\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, a distance $3.6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, a distance $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$

## Explanation

The couple has zero resultant, so the resultant of the system is $R_y = -6 - 9 = -15\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ down. The moment about $A$ adds the moments of the two forces and the moment of the couple:
$$
M_A = 1 \cdot (-6) + 4 \cdot (-9) + 12 = -30\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The loads are vertical, so the line of action of the resultant is the horizontal coordinate $x = \dfrac{M_A}{R_y} = \dfrac{-30}{-15} = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ vertical. Without the couple you would find $2.8\,\mathrm{m}$, and with the couple taken clockwise you would find $3.6\,\mathrm{m}$.
