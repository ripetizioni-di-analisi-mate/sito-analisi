A force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ has the line of action of equation $3x + 4y = 12$, with $x$ and $y$ in meters, and is directed from point $(0, 3)$ towards point $(4, 0)$. What is the moment of the force about the origin $O$?

- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $-30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $-40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

The arm is the distance of $O$ from the line of action. By the formula of the distance of a point from a line
$$
b = \frac{|3 \cdot 0 + 4 \cdot 0 - 12|}{\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2}} = \frac{12}{5} = 2.4\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
then $|M_O| = 10 \cdot 2.4 = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. For the sign the force at point $(0, 3)$ of the line is applied, where it has components $F_x = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $F_y = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$; then $M_O = 0 \cdot (-6) - 3 \cdot 8 = -24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise. The $-30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $-40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ values use as a moment arm the distances of $O$ from the intersections of the line with the axes, which are not perpendicular to the force.
