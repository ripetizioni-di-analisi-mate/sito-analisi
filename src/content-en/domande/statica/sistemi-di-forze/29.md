Two downward loads act at the ends of a $4\,\mathrm{m}$ beam. Their sum is $16\,\mathrm{kN}$. A portion of $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ is transferred from the left load to the right load. How far does their centre move?

- $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ left
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$ right
- It stays in place
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ right

## Explanation

The transfer preserves the resultant and increases its clockwise moment about the left end by $2\cdot4=8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The total force must therefore move $\dfrac{8}{16}=0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right.
