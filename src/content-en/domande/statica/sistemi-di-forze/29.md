Beam $AB$ has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and carries a uniform load $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on segment $AC$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and a uniform load $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on segment $CB$. At what distance from $A$ does the line of action of the resultant pass?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" carichi="A,C,0.3,0.3,q_1;C,B,0.9,0.9,q_2" quote="A,C,3 m,0.8;C,B,3 m,0.8" titolo="Stepped load of intensity $q_1 = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $AC$ and $q_2 = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $CB$"}

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.75\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Each segment carries a uniform load, so it replaces with its resultant at the midpoint of the segment: $2 \cdot 3 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ a distance $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and $6 \cdot 3 = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ a distance $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. For the **center of parallel forces**
$$
x_Q = \frac{6 \cdot 1.5 + 18 \cdot 4.5}{24} = \frac{90}{24} = 3.75\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $2.25\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance from $B$, and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ is achieved by putting the result in the middle.
