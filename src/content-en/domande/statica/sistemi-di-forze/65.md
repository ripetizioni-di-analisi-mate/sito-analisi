On the beam $AB$, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, a concentrated load of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ acts. The load is replaced with a uniform load of $5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ down, spread over only one section of the beam, so that the two systems are equivalent. Over which segment must the uniform load be applied?

- From $0$ to $4\,\mathrm{m}$, measured from $A$
- From $2$ to $4\,\mathrm{m}$, measured from $A$
- From $1$ to $5\,\mathrm{m}$, measured from $A$
- From $2$ to $6\,\mathrm{m}$, measured from $A$

## Explanation

Two systems are equivalent if they have the same resultant and the same moment about a reference point. The uniform load spread over a segment of length $\ell$ has resultant $5\,\ell$, applied in the middle of the segment. The resultant must equal $20\,\mathrm{kN}$, so
$$
5\,\ell = 20
\quad \Longrightarrow \quad
\ell = 4\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
In addition the resultant must stand on the line of action of the concentrated load, because only thus has the same moment about $A$; therefore the segment has the midpoint at $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and ranges from $1$ a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The segment from $0$ to $4\,\mathrm{m}$ has the correct resultant at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and the segment from $2$ to $4\,\mathrm{m}$ has the correct midpoint and the resultant $10\,\mathrm{kN}$.
