A force with components $F_x = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $F_y = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $P = (4, 1)$, whose coordinates are in meters. What is the perpendicular distance from the origin $O$ to the line of action?

- $1.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The moment of force about $O$ is
$$
M_O = x_P\,F_y - y_P\,F_x = 4 \cdot 6 - 1 \cdot (-8) = 32\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The force has $F = \sqrt{8^2 + 6^2} = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ intensity. For the **Theorem on the moment and the moment arm**, it is $|M_O| = F\,b$, so $b = \dfrac{32}{10} = 3.2\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $1.6\,\mathrm{m}$ arises from the neglected $F_x$ sign, which gives $M_O = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, while $4.12\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance of $O$ from $P$, which is not perpendicular to the line of action.
