An $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ force acts to the right along a line between $0.4$ and $0.6\,\mathrm{m}$ above the moment centre. With counterclockwise positive, which interval contains the possible moments?

- $[3.2;4.8]\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $[-0.6;-0.4]\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $[-2.4;-1.6]\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $[-4.8;-3.2]\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

At height $h>0$ the moment is $-8h$ because the force tends to rotate the body clockwise. The most negative value corresponds to the greatest height. The endpoints are $-4.8$ and $-3.2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
