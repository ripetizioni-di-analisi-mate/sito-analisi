A force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ directed to the right is applied at point $P = (2, 0)$, in meters. What is its moment about point $O' = (5, 4)$?

- $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The line of action of the force is the $x$ axis, which is horizontal, so the moment arm is the vertical distance of $O'$ from the axis, $b = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, and the magnitude of the moment is $6 \cdot 4 = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The force acts below the pole and points to the right, so it tends to rotate the body counterclockwise about $O'$. In components,
$$
M_{O'} = (x_P - x_{O'})\,F_y - (y_P - y_{O'})\,F_x = (2 - 5) \cdot 0 - (0 - 4) \cdot 6 = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The value $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses the horizontal distance as the moment arm, and $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses the distance $O'P = 5\,\mathrm{m}$, which is not perpendicular to the line of action.
