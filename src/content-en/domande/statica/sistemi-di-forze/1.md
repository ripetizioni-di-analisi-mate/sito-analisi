A downward load of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at point $P = (2, 0)$ on a horizontal beam. The $x$-axis runs along the beam, with origin $O$ at its left end. Coordinates are in meters. What is the moment of the load about point $Q = (5, 0)$?

- $-45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $75\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $-30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

The load is vertical, so its moment arm about $Q$ is the horizontal distance from $Q$ to the line of action, $b = 5 - 2 = 3\,\mathrm{m}$. The magnitude of the moment is therefore $15 \cdot 3 = 45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The load acts downward to the left of $Q$, so it tends to rotate the beam counterclockwise about $Q$. The component formula gives the same result:
$$
M_Q = (x_P - x_Q)\,F_y - (y_P - y_Q)\,F_x = (2 - 5) \cdot (-15) = 45\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The value $75\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses the horizontal coordinate of $Q$ as the moment arm. The value $-30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment about $O$.
