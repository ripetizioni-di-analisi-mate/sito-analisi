The force $\vec{F} = (0,\, -5)\,\mathrm{kN}$ is applied at point $P = (2, 0)$, with the coordinates in meters, in the reference with the axis $x$ to the right, the axis $y$ upward and the axis $z$ leaving the sheet. What is the moment vector $\vec{M}_O$ of the force about the origin $O$?

- $\vec{M}_O = 10\,\hat{k}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, out of the page
- $\vec{M}_O = -10\,\hat{\jmath}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, in the plane of the page
- $\vec{M}_O = 10\,\hat{\imath}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, in the plane of the page
- $\vec{M}_O = -10\,\hat{k}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, into the page

## Explanation

The moment about $O$ is the vector product $\vec{M}_O = \overrightarrow{OP} \times \vec{F}$, with $\overrightarrow{OP} = 2\,\hat{\imath}$ and $\vec{F} = -5\,\hat{\jmath}$. The two vectors are in the plane $xy$, so their vector product is perpendicular to the plane, i.e. parallel to $\hat{k}$. For vector product in components
$$
\vec{M}_O = \big( 2 \cdot (-5) - 0 \cdot 0 \big)\,\hat{k} = -10\,\hat{k}\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The component along $z$ is negative, so the moment vector points into the page and the force tends to rotate the body clockwise about $O$. The vector $10\,\hat{k}$ is obtained by calculating $\vec{F} \times \overrightarrow{OP}$, with the factors in the wrong order.
