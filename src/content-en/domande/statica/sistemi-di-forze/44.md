Inclined member $AB$ has its lower end $A$ at the origin and its upper end at $B = (3, 4)$, with coordinates in meters. Its length is therefore $5\,\mathrm{m}$. It carries a vertical snow load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per unit length of the horizontal projection. What is the moment of the load about $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,4" aste="A-B" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,3 0,3 m,0.7;3 0,B,4 m,0.7" titolo="Inclined member under snow load $q$"}

- $-22.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $-27\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $-13.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $-37.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

The load is given per unit length of the horizontal projection, which equals $3\,\mathrm{m}$, so the resultant is $Q = 3 \cdot 3 = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$, vertical and downward. The resultant passes through the midpoint of the rod, at horizontal coordinate $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$, so the arm about $A$ is $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and
$$
M_A = -9 \cdot 1.5 = -13.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
clockwise. The value $-22.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by referring the load to the length of the rod, $Q = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $-37.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ also using half the length of the rod as an arm.
