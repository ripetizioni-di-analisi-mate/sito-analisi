A plane system of forces has resultant moment $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about each of the three points $A$, $B$ and $C$, which are not aligned. What can be concluded about this system?

- The resultant is zero and the system is equivalent to a couple
- The resultant is nonzero and parallel to line $AB$
- The system is in equilibrium
- The resultant is nonzero and passes through the centroid of triangle $ABC$

## Explanation

For the **change of reference point formula** it equals $\vec{M}_B = \vec{M}_A + \overrightarrow{BA} \times \vec{R}$. Since $M_B = M_A$, the $\overrightarrow{BA} \times \vec{R}$ product is zero, so $\vec{R}$ is zero or parallel to the line $AB$. With the same reasoning on the $A$ and $C$ reference points, the resultant is either null or parallel to the line $AC$. The $AB$ and $AC$ lines are not parallel, because the three points are not aligned, therefore $\vec{R} = \vec{0}$. The moment equals $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, which is different from zero, so the system is equivalent to a moment $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ couple and is not balanced.
