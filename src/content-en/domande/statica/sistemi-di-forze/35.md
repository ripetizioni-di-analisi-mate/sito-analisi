On the plate in the figure act the force $F_1 = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right at point $(0, 1)$ and the force $F_2 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ down at point $(3, 4)$, with the coordinates in meters. Through which of the following points does the line of action of the resultant pass?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,5 0,5" nodi="O,0,0" forze="0 1,1.2,0,F_1;3 4,0,-1.2,F_2" punti="0,1;3,4" titolo="Plate under forces $F_1$ and $F_2$"}

- $(3, 1)$
- $(0, 4)$
- $(3, 4)$
- $(0, 1)$

## Explanation

The line of action of $F_1$ is the line $y = 1$ and that of $F_2$ is the line $x = 3$, which meet at point $K = (3, 1)$. by **Varignon's theorem** the system is equivalent to the resultant applied at $K$. The calculation confirms this. With $\vec{R} = (4, -6)\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M_O = 3 \cdot (-6) - 1 \cdot 4 = -22\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, the line of action of the resultant has equation $-6x - 4y = -22$, i.e. $3x + 2y = 11$, and $K$ satisfies it. The point $(0, 4)$ is the meeting of the exchanged lines, $x = 0$ and $y = 4$, and does not satisfy the equation.
