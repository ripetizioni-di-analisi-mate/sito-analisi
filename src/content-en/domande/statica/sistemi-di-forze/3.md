An upward force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $A = (0, 0)$ and a downward force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $B = (3, 0)$, with coordinates in meters. What is their combined moment about $P = (5, 2)$?

- $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $-40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $-56\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

The two forces are opposite and have parallel and distinct lines of action, $x = 0$ and $x = 3$, so they form a moment **couple** arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$. The moment of a couple is the same as at each reference point and has magnitude $8 \cdot 3 = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The upward force is on the left and the downward force on the right, so the couple is clockwise. The calculation with respect to $P$ confirms this:
$$
M_P = (0 - 5) \cdot 8 + (3 - 5) \cdot (-8) = -40 + 16 = -24\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The value $-40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment of force alone at $A$.
