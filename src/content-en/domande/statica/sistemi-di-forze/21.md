The structure in the figure is formed by the column $AB$, of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$, and the crossbeam $BC$, of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. At $C$ the load $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downwards and at $B$ the force $H = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is the resultant moment of the two forces about $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,2,3" aste="A-B;B-C" forze="C,0,-1,P;B,1,0,H" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.8;B,C,2 m,-0.7" titolo="Column and crossbeam under load $P$ and force $H$"}

- $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $-32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

The load $P$ is vertical and its line of action is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$; is directed down to the right of $A$, so its moment is $-10 \cdot 2 = -20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The force $H$ is horizontal and its line of action stands $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$; it acts to the right, so its moment is also clockwise, with magnitude $-4 \cdot 3 = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The resultant moment is
$$
M_A = 2 \cdot (-10) - 3 \cdot 4 = -32\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The value $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by giving $H$ a counterclockwise moment.
