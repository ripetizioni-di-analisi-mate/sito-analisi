The block in the figure, weighing $W = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, has wide base $1\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $2\,\mathrm{m}$, with the centroid on the middle vertical of the base. It rests on the floor and is pushed to the right by a horizontal $F$ force applied to $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ in height. For what value of $F$ is the resultant moment of $F$ and the weight about the lower right edge $S$ zero?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 1,0 1,2 0,2" nodi="S,1,0" forze="0 1.5,0.8,0,F;0.5 1,0,-0.8,W" quote="0 0,0 1.5,1.5 m,-0.5;0 0,1 0,1 m,0.6" titolo="Block pushed by horizontal force $F$"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

About the $S$ edge the weight has $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ arm, the horizontal distance between $S$ and the centroid vertical, and tends to rotate the block counterclockwise. The force $F$ has a moment arm of $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$, its height on $S$, and tends to make it rotate in clockwise. The resultant moment is zero if
$$
F \cdot 1.5 = 12 \cdot 0.5 ,
$$
then $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$. For larger values the moment about $S$ is clockwise and the block tends to tip about $S$. The value $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ uses the entire base as the weight arm, and $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ uses the block height as the arm $F$.
