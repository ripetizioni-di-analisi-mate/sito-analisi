An intensity force $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ is applied at point $P = (3, 4)$, with coordinates in meters, and its direction can be chosen. In which direction must the force act to maximize the absolute value of its moment about the origin $O$?

- Parallel to vector $(3, 4)$
- Vertical
- Horizontal
- Parallel to vector $(4, -3)$

## Explanation

The moment has absolute value $|M_O| = F b$, where the moment $b$ arm is the distance of $O$ from the line of action. The line of action passes through $P$, so its distance from $O$ does not exceed the length of $OP = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5\,\mathrm{m}$, and only reaches it when the line is perpendicular to $OP$. The vector $(4, -3)$ is perpendicular to $\overrightarrow{OP} = (3, 4)$, because $3 \cdot 4 + 4 \cdot (-3) = 0$; Therefore with this direction the moment equals $10 \cdot 5 = 50\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in magnitude, the maximum possible. With the direction $(3, 4)$ the line of action passes through $O$ and the moment is zero, while the vertical and horizontal force have $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ arms.
