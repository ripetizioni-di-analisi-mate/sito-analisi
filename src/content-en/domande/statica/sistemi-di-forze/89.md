A reinforced concrete beam has rectangular section of $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ for $50\,\mathrm{cm}$, and the reinforced concrete has specific weight $25\,\mathrm{kN/m^3}$. What is the distributed load from the beam's self-weight per unit length?

- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $3.75\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $37.5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

A section of beam along $1\,\mathrm{m}$ has volume equal to the cross-sectional area for $1\,\mathrm{m}$. The cross-sectional area is $0.3 \cdot 0.5 = 0.15\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so the segment has $0.15\,\mathrm{m^3}$ volume and weighs $25 \cdot 0.15\,\mathrm{kN}$. Therefore the self-weight is a uniform load of
$$
q = 25 \cdot 0.15 = 3.75\,\mathrm{kN/m} .
$$
The value $7.5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ uses only the base of the section, $12.5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ only the height, and $37.5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ misses the area conversion by a factor of ten.
