Two loading systems $A$ and $B$ on a rigid planar platform do equal work under each of two independent virtual translations. In a counterclockwise virtual rotation of $2\,\mathrm{mrad}$ about $O$, system $B$ does $0.06\,\mathrm{J}$ less work than $A$. What single couple must be added to $B$ to make it equivalent to $A$?

- $30\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ counterclockwise.
- $30\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ clockwise.
- $0.12\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ counterclockwise.
- $3\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ clockwise.

## Explanation

The two translation tests establish equality of both resultant force components. The remaining difference is a moment. The additional work in the positive rotation is $M\cdot0.002=0.06\,\mathrm{J}$, so the required couple is $+30\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
