Cantilever $AB$ in the figure is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ long, is fixed at $A$ and carries a uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over its entire length. The load is replaced by an equivalent system consisting of a force applied at $A$ and a couple. What is the couple?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,3 m,0.8" titolo="Cantilever under uniform load $q$"}

- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

The uniform load is equivalent to its resultant $Q = qL = 4 \cdot 3 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, applied at the midpoint of the cantilever, $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Moving the resultant to $A$ adds the transfer couple, equal to the moment of the resultant about $A$:
$$
\mathcal{M}_t = -12 \cdot 1.5 = -18\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
which is **clockwise**. The equivalent system is therefore the downward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $A$ together with the clockwise couple of $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by applying the resultant at the free end.
