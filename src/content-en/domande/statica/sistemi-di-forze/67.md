The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and fixed at $A$, carries the uniform $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load over the entire length. The load is replaced by an equivalent system consisting of a force applied at $A$ and a couple. What is the couple?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,3 m,0.8" titolo="Cantilever under uniform load $q$"}

- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

The uniform load is equivalent to its the resultant $Q = qL = 4 \cdot 3 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, applied in the middle of the cantilever, a distance $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Carrying the resultant at $A$ adds the transport couple, equal to the moment of the resultant about $A$:
$$
\mathcal{M}_t = -12 \cdot 1.5 = -18\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
in direction **clockwise**. The equivalent system is then the force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ down at $A$ with the clockwise couple of $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ applies the resultant in the free end.
