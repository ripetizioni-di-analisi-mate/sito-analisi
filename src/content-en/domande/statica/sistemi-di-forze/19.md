A force with components $F_x = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $F_y = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $P = (1, 1)$, whose coordinates are in meters. About which of the following points is the moment of the force zero?

- $(5, 4)$
- $(4, 1)$
- $(4, 5)$
- $(1, 5)$

## Explanation

For the **Theorem on the moment and the moment arm** the moment of a force is zero if and only if the reference point lies on the line of action. The line of action passes through $P$ and is parallel to the vector $(3, 4)$, so its points are $(1 + 3t,\ 1 + 4t)$; for $t = 1$ you get $(4, 5)$. About $(5, 4)$ the moment equals instead
$$
M = (1 - 5) \cdot 4 - (1 - 4) \cdot 3 = -16 + 9 = -7\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
and compared to $(4, 1)$ and $(1, 5)$ it equals $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
