On the beam $AB$, three forces act to form a balanced system: a vertical downward load at a point $C$ inside the beam, a vertical force at $B$ and a force applied at $A$. In which direction does the force applied at $A$ act?

- Directed from $A$ toward $C$
- Horizontal
- Inclined at an angle that depends on the position of $C$
- Vertical

## Explanation

The system is balanced, so its resultant is zero. The load $C$ and $B$ force are vertical and have zero horizontal component, so the force $A$ also has zero horizontal component and is **vertical**. The three-force theorem does not apply in the form of the meeting point, because the lines of action of the load and force at $B$ are parallel and do not meet; the three lines of action are then all parallel. The moment equation about $B$ then gives the intensity of the force at $A$.
