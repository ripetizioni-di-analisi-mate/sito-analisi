Two sensors measure the moments of a planar force system about $A=(0,0)$ and $B=(2,0)$ in metres. They read $9$ and $3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, respectively, counterclockwise positive. What is the vertical component of the resultant?

- $+3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $+6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Moving the moment centre from $A$ to $B$ reduces the moment by $2R_y$. Thus $3=9-2R_y$ and $R_y=+3\,\mathrm{kN}$. Taking the difference between the readings cancels any applied couples.
