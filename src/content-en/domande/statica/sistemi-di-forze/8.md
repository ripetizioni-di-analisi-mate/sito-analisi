Beam $AB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a trapezoidal load of intensity $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$ and $10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. How far from $A$ does the line of action of the resultant pass?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" carichi="A,B,0.4,1,q" quote="A,B,6 m,0.8" titolo="Trapezoidal load varying from $4$ to $10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $3.43\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.57\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The load breaks down into a uniform load of $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and a triangular load ranging from $0$ at $A$ to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. The first has resultant $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, the second has resultant $\dfrac{6 \cdot 6}{2} = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The total result is $42\,\mathrm{kN}$ and passes through the center of the two partial forces:
$$
x_Q = \frac{24 \cdot 3 + 18 \cdot 4}{42} = \frac{144}{42} = \frac{24}{7}\,\mathrm{m} = 3.43\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $2.57\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by exchanging the values of the load in the two extremes.
