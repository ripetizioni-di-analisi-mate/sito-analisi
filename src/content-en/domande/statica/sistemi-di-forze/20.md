Two forces of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ intensity are applied at the same point as a body. What angle must the forces make with each other for their resultant also to have magnitude $10\,\mathrm{kN}$?

- $120^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $150^\circ$

## Explanation

You take the axis $x$ along the first force and indicate with $\vartheta$ the angle between the two directions. Then $\vec{R} = (10 + 10\cos\vartheta,\; 10\sin\vartheta)$, then
$$
R^2 = (10 + 10\cos\vartheta)^2 + (10\sin\vartheta)^2 = 200\,(1 + \cos\vartheta) .
$$
The $R^2 = 100$ condition gives $\cos\vartheta = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, therefore $\vartheta = 120^\circ$. With $60^\circ$ the resultant is $17.32\,\mathrm{kN}$, with $90^\circ$ is $14.14\,\mathrm{kN}$ and with $150^\circ$ is $5.18\,\mathrm{kN}$.
