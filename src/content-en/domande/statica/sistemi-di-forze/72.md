On a slab, seen in the plan in the figure, are placed three machines weighing $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at point $P_1 = (0, 0)$, $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at point $P_2 = (4, 0)$ and $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at point $P_3 = (2, 3)$, with coordinates in meters. Through which point in the plan view does the line of action of the resultant of the three weights pass?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,3 0,3" punti="0,0,P_1;4,0,P_2;2,3,P_3" titolo="Floor plan showing the support points of the three machines"}

- $(2;\; 1)$
- $(2.5;\; 0.75)$
- $(2;\; 0.75)$
- $(2.5;\; 1)$

## Explanation

The three weights are parallel and concord forces, so the resultant, of $40\,\mathrm{kN}$, has the vertical line of action for the center of parallel forces. Its coordinates in plan are the averages of the coordinates of the points, weighed with weights:
$$
x_C = \frac{10 \cdot 0 + 20 \cdot 4 + 10 \cdot 2}{40} = 2.5\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
y_C = \frac{10 \cdot 0 + 20 \cdot 0 + 10 \cdot 3}{40} = 0.75\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The point $(2;\; 1)$ is the average of the three points without the weights, i.e. the centroid of the triangle $P_1 P_2 P_3$, which coincides with the center only if the three weights are equal.
