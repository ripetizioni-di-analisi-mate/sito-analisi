A homogeneous rectangular $ABCD$ plate, with $AB = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $AD = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, is hung with a thread fixed in the $A$ vertex and remains stationary. Only the weight, applied in the centroid, and the force of the wire, applied at $A$, work on the plate. What angle does side $AB$ make with the vertical at equilibrium?

- $0^\circ$
- $63.43^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $26.57^\circ$

## Explanation

On the slab only two forces act, and two forces form a balanced system if and only if they are opposite and have the same line of action. The weight is vertical and its line of action passes through the centroid $G$, center of the rectangle; the thread force has line of action for $A$. Therefore in equilibrium the line $AG$ is vertical. With $A$ in the origin, $B = (4, 0)$ and $D = (0, 2)$ in the plate reference, the centroid is $G = (2, 1)$, so the angle $\varphi$ between $AB$ and $AG$ satisfies
$$
\tan\varphi = \frac{1}{2}
\quad \Longrightarrow \quad
\varphi = 26.57^\circ .
$$
The $AB$ side then forms $26.57^\circ$ with the vertical. The value $63.43^\circ$ is the angle that forms the $AD$ side with the vertical.
