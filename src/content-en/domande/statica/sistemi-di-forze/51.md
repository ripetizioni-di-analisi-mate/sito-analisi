A key holds a die. The hand applies a force of $400\,\mathrm{N}$ at the point of the handle to $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$ from the center of the nut, and the force forms an angle of $60^\circ$ with the axis of the handle. What is the magnitude of the moment of the force about the center of the nut?

- $86.6\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $115.5\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The moment has $F b$ intensity, where $b$ is the distance of the center of the die from the line of action of the force. The line of action passes through the point of application, to $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$ from the center, and forms the angle of $60^\circ$ with the segment joining it in the center. Therefore the arm is
$$
b = 0.25 \sin 60^\circ = 0.2165\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
and the moment equals $400 \cdot 0.2165 = 86.6\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. The value $100\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ is the moment of a force perpendicular to the handle, which is the maximum possible, and $50\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ is obtained with the cosine instead of the sine.
