A force has $F_x = -9\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $F_y = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ components. What are its magnitude $F$ and its angle $\alpha$, measured counterclockwise from the $x$ axis?

- $F = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$, $\alpha = -53.13^\circ$
- $F = 21\,\mathrm{kN}$, $\alpha = 126.87^\circ$
- $F = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$, $\alpha = 126.87^\circ$
- $F = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$, $\alpha = 143.13^\circ$

## Explanation

The intensity is $F = \sqrt{9^2 + 12^2} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$. The angle satisfies $\cos\alpha = -\dfrac{9}{15} = -0.6$ and $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{12}{15} = 0.8$, then falls into the second quadrant. Because $\arctan\dfrac{12}{9} = 53.13^\circ$, you have $\alpha = 180^\circ - 53.13^\circ = 126.87^\circ$. The value $-53.13^\circ$ is that of $\arctan\dfrac{F_y}{F_x}$ and corresponds to the opposite-direction force; $21\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the sum of component modules.
