The cover flap in the figure is schematized with member $AB$, from $A = (0, 0)$ to $B = (4, 3)$ in meters, thus $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long. The wind exerts a uniform $p = 1.5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ pressure on the upper face, perpendicular to the rod and directed towards the rod. What is the moment of the wind load about $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,3" aste="A-B" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,p,306.87" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9" titolo="Sloping roof member under wind pressure $p$ normal to the member"}

- $-15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $-37.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $18.75\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $-18.75\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

The load is uniform along the rod, so the resulting value is $p\,\ell = 1.5 \cdot 5 = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, it is perpendicular to the rod and passes through the midpoint. The line of action is therefore the perpendicular to the rod at the midpoint, and its distance from $A$ is half the length of the rod, $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$. The pressure on the upper face pushes the rod to the right and down, so it tends to make it rotates about $A$ in clockwise and
$$
M_A = -7.5 \cdot 2.5 = -18.75\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The value $-15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses the horizontal distance of the midpoint as its arm, which applies to a vertical load, and $-37.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ applies the resultant at $B$.
