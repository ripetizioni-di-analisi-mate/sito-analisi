On a square $3\,\mathrm{m}$ side plate you want to apply a moment $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ couple with two opposite forces applied in two opposite $A$ and $C$ vertices, of direction to choose. What is the minimum magnitude of the two forces?

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $5.66\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2.83\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The moment of the couple is $F d$, where $d$ is the distance between the lines of action. For a given moment, the force is therefore smallest when $d$ is greatest. The two lines pass through $A$ and $C$, so their distance does not exceed the length of the diagonal $AC = 3\sqrt{2} = 4.24\,\mathrm{m}$, and reaches it when the forces are perpendicular to $AC$. Then
$$
F = \frac{12}{3\sqrt{2}} = 2\sqrt{2} = 2.83\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
With forces parallel to one side the moment arm is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and serve $4\,\mathrm{kN}$; the value $5.66\,\mathrm{kN}$ uses half of the diagonal as its arm.
