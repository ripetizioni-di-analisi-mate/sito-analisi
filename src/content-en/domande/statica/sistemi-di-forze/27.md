An upward force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $(0, 0)$ and a downward force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $(0, 3)$, with coordinates in meters. Which system is equivalent to the two forces?

- A counterclockwise couple of magnitude $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- To a clockwise couple of $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- The zero force system
- A downward force of magnitude $8\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The two forces are vertical and their application points are both on the line $x = 0$, so the two forces have the same line of action. They are opposites, so the resultant is zero. The moment about the origin is zero for both, because their lines of action pass through the origin. The system has zero result and moment, so it is **balanced** and is equivalent to the null system. $3\,\mathrm{m}$ between application points are measured along the line of action and are not the moment arm of a couple, which is the distance between two distinct lines of action.
