- $\mathcal{M}_t = 32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $\mathcal{M}_t = 0$
- $\mathcal{M}_t = -16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $\mathcal{M}_t = -32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

The transport couple is the moment of the original force about the point of arrival $A$. The line of action of $H$ is horizontal and passes through $B$, so the arm about $A$ is the height of the column, $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The force acts above $A$ and points to the right, so it tends to rotate the column clockwise about $A$, and
$$
\mathcal{M}_t = -(y_B - y_A)\,H = -4 \cdot 8 = -32\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The equivalent system is the force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right applied at $A$ along with this couple. The value $\mathcal{M}_t = 0$ would equal a scroll along the line of action, which does not pass through $A$.
