The plate shown has points $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)$, and $C = (0, 3)$, with coordinates in meters. A force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right at $C$. A vertical force applied at $B$ and a force applied at $A$ form a balanced system with $F$. What is the magnitude of the force applied at $A$?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,3 0,3" nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,0,3" forze="C,1.3,0,F" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;A,C,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Plate under force $F$ at $C$"}

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $17.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The system is balanced, so the moment about $A$ is zero. The force at $A$ has zero moment, so $-3 \cdot 10 + 4\,V_B = 0$ and the force at $B$ equals $V_B = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, upwards. The two translation equations then give for the force at $A$ the components $-10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $-7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the intensity
$$
F_A = \sqrt{10^2 + 7.5^2} = 12.5\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The line of action of this force passes through $(4, 3)$, the meeting point of the lines of action of $F$ and the force at $B$, as the theorem on the three forces in equilibrium establishes. The value $17.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the sum of the components.
