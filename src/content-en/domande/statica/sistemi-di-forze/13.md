Beam $AB$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and carries a downward force $P_1 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $A$, an upward force $P_2 = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ at a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and a downward force $P_3 = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$. How far from $A$ is the center of the parallel forces?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" forze="A,0,-1.4,P_1;2 0,0,0.6,P_2;B,0,-1.1,P_3" quote="A,2 0,2 m,1;2 0,B,2 m,1" titolo="Three vertical forces, one acting upward"}

- $1.75\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.33\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.67\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

You take positive forces down: $F_1 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$, $F_2 = -5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $F_3 = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$. The resultant equals $20 - 5 + 15 = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ down. For the **center of parallel forces**
$$
x_C = \frac{20 \cdot 0 - 5 \cdot 2 + 15 \cdot 4}{30} = \frac{50}{30} = \frac{5}{3}\,\mathrm{m} = 1.67\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $1.75\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by also taking $P_2$ down, i.e. with resultant $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ and numerator $70\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
