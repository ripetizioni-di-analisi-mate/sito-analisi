Four forces of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ are applied at different points, with coordinates in meters. Which of the four forces produces a counterclockwise moment about the origin $O$?

- The rightward force at $(0, 2)$
- The downward force at $(2, 0)$
- The upward force at $(-2, 0)$
- The leftward force at $(0, 2)$

## Explanation

The moment about $O$ of the force applied at $(x, y)$ is $M_O = x F_y - y F_x$, positive counterclockwise. The force to the left in $(0, 2)$ has $F_x = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$, so
$$
M_O = 0 - 2 \cdot (-6) = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
counterclockwise: it passes over $O$ and pushes it to the left. The force to the right in $(0, 2)$ has $M_O = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The downward force at $(2, 0)$ has $M_O = 2 \cdot (-6) = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and the upward force at $(-2, 0)$ has $M_O = -2 \cdot 6 = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$; So these three have clockwise moment.
