At one point in a body two forces of intensity $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the angle between their directions can be chosen. Which of the following values is impossible for the magnitude of the resultant?

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The resultant is the sum of the two vectors, so the vectors $\vec{F}_1$, $\vec{F}_2$ and $\vec{R}$ form a triangle, which is degenerate when the forces are parallel. For triangular inequality
$$
|F_2 - F_1| \leq R \leq F_1 + F_2 ,
\qquad \text{that is} \qquad
4\,\mathrm{kN} \leq R \leq 16\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The value $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained with the opposite forces, the value $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ with the forces acting in the same direction, and the value $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ with an intermediate angle, because $R^2 = 36 + 100 + 120\cos\theta$ equals $100$ for $\cos\theta = -0.3$. Therefore the only impossible value is $3\,\mathrm{kN}$, less than the difference of the two intensities.
