On the beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, the $\mathcal{M} = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise couple in the middle and the downward load $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ act. What is the resultant moment about $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" coppie="3 0,1,ℳ" forze="4 0,0,-1.4,F" quote="A,3 0,3 m,0.8;3 0,4 0,1 m,0.8;4 0,B,2 m,0.8" titolo="Beam under couple $\mathcal{M}$ and load $F$"}

- $0$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The load $F$ is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ away from $B$ and is directed down to the left of $B$, so its moment about $B$ is counterclockwise and equals $10 \cdot 2 = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The couple contribute to the resultant moment with their own moment, $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, whatever the reference point. Therefore
$$
M_B = 20 + 20 = 40\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The value $0$ is obtained by taking the clockwise couple, and $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ by forgetting the couple.
