A loaded wheelbarrow weighs $600\,\mathrm{N}$, with the weight applied to $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ horizontally from the wheel axis. The handles grip $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ horizontally from the axle of the wheel, on the same side of the load. What vertical force must be applied to the handles to hold the raised wheelbarrow in equilibrium?

- $200\,\mathrm{N}$ upward
- $1800\,\mathrm{N}$ upward
- $400\,\mathrm{N}$ upward
- $300\,\mathrm{N}$ upward

## Explanation

On the wheelbarrow act the weight, the force of the hands and the force of the wheel, applied in the axis of the wheel. The system is balanced, so its moment about the wheel axis is zero, and the wheel force does not appear because it has zero moment arm. Pointing with $F$ the force of the hands, upwards,
$$
1.5\,F - 0.5 \cdot 600 = 0
\quad \Longrightarrow \quad
F = \frac{300}{1.5} = 200\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The wheel carries the remaining $400\,\mathrm{N}$, as it gives the balance to the vertical translation. The value $1800\,\mathrm{N}$ is obtained by exchanging the two arms.
