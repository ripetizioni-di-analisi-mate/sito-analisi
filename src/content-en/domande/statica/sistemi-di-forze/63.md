The beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, carries a uniform load of $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ directed upwards on the $AC$ half and a uniform load of $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ directed downwards on the $CB$ half. Which system is equivalent to the load?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" carichi="A,C,0.6,0.6,q,90;C,B,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,C,3 m,1.2;C,B,3 m,1.2" titolo="Load of intensity $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acting upward on $AC$ and downward on $CB$"}

- A counterclockwise couple of magnitude $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- To a clockwise couple of $72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- To a clockwise couple of $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- To a clockwise couple of $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The load on $AC$ has resultant $4 \cdot 3 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upwards, applied a distance $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$; the load on $CB$ has resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ down, applied a distance $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The resulting two are opposite and stand on verticals distant $3\,\mathrm{m}$, so they form a couple. Its moment, calculated against $A$, is
$$
M_A = 1.5 \cdot 12 + 4.5 \cdot (-12) = -36\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
therefore the load is equivalent to an **Clockwise couple** of $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses the entire span as an arm instead of the distance between the two resultants.
