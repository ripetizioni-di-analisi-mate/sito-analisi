A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a uniform $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load over all the span. The load replaces itself with its $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ resultant, applied in the middle. Which statement is true?

- The support reactions stay the same and the midspan bending moment changes
- The support reactions and the midspan bending moment stay the same
- The support reactions change and the midspan bending moment stays the same
- The support reactions and the midspan bending moment change

## Explanation

The load and its resultant are equivalent systems, so the equilibrium equations of the entire beam give the same reactions, $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in each support. The bending moment in the middle is instead obtained from the balance of the left half of the beam, which contains only half of the loaded segment. With the distributed load on that half act the reaction and $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ load with $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ arm, therefore $M = 12 \cdot 3 - 12 \cdot 1.5 = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. With the force concentrated in the middle only the reaction acts, and $M = 12 \cdot 3 = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
