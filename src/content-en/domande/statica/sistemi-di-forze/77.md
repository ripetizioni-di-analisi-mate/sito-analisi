The L-shaped member shown consists of a horizontal segment $AB$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and a vertical segment $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Its weight per unit length is $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ throughout. What is the horizontal distance from $A$ to the line of action of the total weight?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,4,2" aste="A-B;B-C" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,C,2 m,0.8" titolo="L-shaped member under uniform self-weight"}

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.67\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.33\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The weight of each segment is a uniform load, equivalent to its resultant applied at the midpoint of the segment. The section $AB$ weighs $3 \cdot 4 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, with line of action at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$; the section $BC$ weighs $3 \cdot 2 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, with vertical line of action for $B$, a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The two weights are parallel forces acting in the same direction, so the overall weight of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ passes through
$$
x_G = \frac{12 \cdot 2 + 6 \cdot 4}{18} = \frac{48}{18} = 2.67\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $3\,\mathrm{m}$ is the average of the two positions without the weights, and $3.33\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by exchanging the two weights.
