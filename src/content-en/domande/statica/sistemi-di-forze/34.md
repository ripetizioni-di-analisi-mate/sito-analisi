On the beam $AB$ shown, of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, the force $F_1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ down at $A$, the force $F_2 = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and the force $F_3 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ up at $B$ act. Which system is it equivalent to?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" forze="A,0,-1.4,F_1;2 0,0,0.8,F_2;B,0,1,F_3" quote="A,2 0,2 m,-0.8;2 0,B,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Beam under three vertical forces"}

- To a clockwise couple of $38\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- The zero force system
- A counterclockwise couple of magnitude $22\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- A counterclockwise couple of magnitude $38\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The resultant is $R_y = -10 + 4 + 6 = 0$. The moment about $A$ is
$$
M_A = 2 \cdot 4 + 5 \cdot 6 = 38\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
because force $F_1$ acts at $A$ and has zero moment. The system has zero resultant and a nonzero moment. By **reduction of a planar force system**, it is therefore equivalent to a counterclockwise couple of $38\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, whose moment is the same about every reference point. The value $22\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ results from taking the moment of $F_2$ with the opposite sign.
