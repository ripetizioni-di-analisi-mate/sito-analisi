Two forces parallel to the vector $(3, -4)$ and with its direction, of intensity $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $15\,\mathrm{kN}$, are applied at points $(0, 0)$ and $(4, 0)$ respectively, with coordinates in meters. What is the equation of the line of action of the resultant, with $x$ and $y$ in meters?

- $4x + 3y = 4$
- $3x - 4y = 9$
- $4x + 3y = 12$
- $4x + 3y = 8$

## Explanation

The two forces are parallel and concord, so the resultant, of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$, has their direction and passes through the center of parallel forces, which does not depend on the direction:
$$
x_C = \frac{5 \cdot 0 + 15 \cdot 4}{20} = 3\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
y_C = 0 .
$$
The line of action passes through $(3, 0)$ and is parallel to $(3, -4)$, so it is perpendicular to the vector $(4, 3)$ and has equation $4x + 3y = 4 \cdot 3$, i.e. $4x + 3y = 12$. The line $4x + 3y = 4$ passes through the point at $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from the origin, which is obtained by exchanging the two intensities, and the line $3x - 4y = 9$ passes through the center but is perpendicular to the forces.
