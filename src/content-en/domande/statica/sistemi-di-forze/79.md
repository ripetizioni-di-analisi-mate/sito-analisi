Member $AB$ ranges from $A = (0, 0)$ to $B = (4, 3)$, with the coordinates in meters, and is subject to only two forces, one applied at $A$ and one applied at $B$, which form a balanced system. What is the line of action of the force applied at $B$?

- The vertical line through $B$
- The line through $B$ perpendicular to $AB$
- The line $AB$
- The horizontal line through $B$

## Explanation

Two forces form a balanced system if and only if they are opposite and have the same line of action. The force at $A$ has a line of action that passes through $A$, and the force at $B$ has a line of action that passes through $B$. The two lines coincide, so the common line passes through $A$ and $B$, and is the line $AB$. A rod loaded only in the two extremes therefore transmits only direct forces along its own axis; is the behavior of pin-ended links and two-force links, which is found in truss constraints and beams.
