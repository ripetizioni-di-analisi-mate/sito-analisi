An $0.3\,\mathrm{m}$-ray pulley is wrapped by a cable whose two segments descend vertically to both sides. The right section pulls down with $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the left with $5\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the resultant moment of the two forces about the center of the pulley?

- $0.9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $3.9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $0.9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $1.8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

Each section of cable is tangent to the pulley, so its line of action is as far from the center as the radius, $0.3\,\mathrm{m}$. The right tension is down to the right of the center, so it has clockwise moment $-8 \cdot 0.3 = -2.4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The left tension is down to the left of the center, so it has counterclockwise moment $5 \cdot 0.3 = 1.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The resultant moment is therefore
$$
M = -2.4 + 1.5 = -0.9\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
clockwise. The value $3.9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ adds the two unmarked moments, and $1.8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses the diameter as a moment arm.
