On the beam section between extreme $A$ and point $D$, at $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, two distributed loads are compared: a uniform load of $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and a triangular load that equals $0$ at $A$ and $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $D$. Which statement is true?

- The two loads have equal resultants with different lines of action
- The two loads have equal resultants with the same line of action
- The triangular load has twice the resultant of the uniform load
- The two loads have different resultants with the same line of action

## Explanation

The resultant is the area of the load diagram. For the uniform load it is $6 \cdot 4 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$, and for the triangular load it is $\dfrac{12 \cdot 4}{2} = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$, so the resulting two are equal. The line of action passes through the centroid of the diagram, which for uniform load stands at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and for triangular load at $\dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The two loads therefore have different moments than the $A$, $-48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $-64\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and are not equivalent.
