A force with $F_x = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $F_y = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$ components is applied at point $P = (3, 2)$, in meters, and transports parallel to itself at the origin $O$. What couple must be added when the force is moved?

- $\mathcal{M}_t = -36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $\mathcal{M}_t = -24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $\mathcal{M}_t = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $\mathcal{M}_t = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The transport couple is the moment of the original force about the $O$ arrival point:
$$
\mathcal{M}_t = x_P\,F_y - y_P\,F_x = 3 \cdot (-8) - 2 \cdot 6 = -24 - 12 = -36\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
Both components make a clockwise contribution. The vertical component is directed downwards to the right of $O$, and the horizontal component is directed to the right above $O$. The value $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ takes into calculation the vertical component only, and $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ takes the contribution of the horizontal component with the opposite sign.
