On the beam $AB$, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a counterclockwise couple of $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ with two vertical forces is wanted, one at the left end $A$ and one at the right end $B$. Which forces must be applied?

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward at $A$ and $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at $B$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at $A$ and $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward at $B$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at $A$ and $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward at $B$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at $A$ and $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward at $B$

## Explanation

The two forces must have result nothing, so they are opposite, of $F$ intensity, on verticals distant $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The couple moment equals $F d$, therefore
$$
4F = 24
\quad \Longrightarrow \quad
F = 6\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
For the direction we calculate the moment about $A$: the force at $A$ has zero moment and an upward force at $B$, to the right of $A$, has $4 \cdot 6 = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ moment, counterclockwise. The required couple is then formed from $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ down at $A$ and $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ up at $B$; with the directions exchanged the couple is clockwise.
