In the cantilever in the figure the load $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, vertical and directed downward, acts at node $B$, where the horizontal rod $AB$ and the strut $CB$, tilted by $30^\circ$ on the horizontal meet. The load breaks down into two direct components like the two rods. What is the magnitude of the component along the strut?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,2;B,3.46,2;C,0,0" aste="A-B;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;C,cerniera,180" forze="B,0,-1,P" etichette="0.95,0.22,30°" titolo="Bracket with horizontal member $AB$ and strut $CB$ inclined at $30^\circ$"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $13.86\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10.39\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The direct component such as the horizontal rod $AB$ has no vertical component. Therefore all vertical component of the load, $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, belongs to the direct component as the strut. This component forms the angle of $30^\circ$ with the horizontal, so its vertical part is its intensity $F_p$ multiplied by $\sin 30^\circ$, and
$$
F_p \sin 30^\circ = 12
\quad \Longrightarrow \quad
F_p = \frac{12}{0.5} = 24\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The component along $AB$ is therefore $24\cos 30^\circ = 20.78\,\mathrm{kN}$ and balances the horizontal part of the force along the strut. Multiplying by the sine instead of dividing gives $6\,\mathrm{kN}$. Dividing by the cosine gives $13.86\,\mathrm{kN}$.
