On a body act a force of $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right at point $(0, 0)$ and a force of $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left at point $(2, 4)$, in meters. What couple must be added to the two forces to produce equilibrium?

- A counterclockwise couple of magnitude $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- A clockwise couple of $22.36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- A clockwise couple of $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- A clockwise couple of $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The two forces are opposite, with horizontal lines of action $y = 0$ and $y = 4$, so they form a moment arm couple $4\,\mathrm{m}$. About the origin only the second force counts, and the moment of the two forces is
$$
M_O = -4 \cdot (-5) = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
counterclockwise. The system is balanced if the total moment is zero, so the added couple must have $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ moment, i.e. be clockwise of $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $22.36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses the distance $\sqrt{20}\,\mathrm{m}$ between the application points as its arm, and $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ their horizontal distance.
