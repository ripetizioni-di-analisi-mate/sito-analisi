On one plate act the $\mathcal{M}_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise couple, the $\mathcal{M}_2 = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise couple and two forces of $3\,\mathrm{kN}$, one upwards at point $(0, 0)$ and one downwards at point $(2, 0)$, with coordinates in meters. Which system is it equivalent to?

- A counterclockwise couple of magnitude $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- A counterclockwise couple of magnitude $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- A counterclockwise couple of magnitude $14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- A downward force of magnitude $6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The two forces are opposite and have parallel lines of action distant $2\,\mathrm{m}$, so they form a couple. The upward force is on the left and the downward force is on the right, so the couple is clockwise and its moment is $-3 \cdot 2 = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The resultant of the whole system is zero and the resultant moment, equal for each reference point, is
$$
M = 12 - 4 - 6 = 2\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The system is thus equivalent to a counterclockwise couple of $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ neglects the couple of forces, and $14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ attributes the counterclockwise.
