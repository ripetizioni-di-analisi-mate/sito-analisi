The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at the right end $B$ and carries the uniform $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load over the entire length. What is the shear force at the fixed end?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="B,incastro,0" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Cantilever fixed at $B$ under a uniform load"}

- $T = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $T = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $T = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $T = -9\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The portion to the left of the section $x$ of the coordinate is used, measured by $A$, which contains only the load of the section of length $x$, resulting in $2x$ down. With the left side the shear is the sum of the vertical components taken positive upward, then $T(x) = -2x$. At the fixed support we therefore have $T = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$, and a small beam element tends to rotate counterclockwise. With fixed support on the left and free end on the right the same load would give $T = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to fixed support; The sign depends on which extreme it is supported by. The value $-9$ is that of the moment at the fixed end, $-\dfrac{qL^2}{2} = -9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and not of the shear.
