Column $AB$ has height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and is fixed at its base $A$, as shown. Wind is modeled as a uniform horizontal load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acting to the right over the full height. Follow the column from $A$ toward $B$, taking the fibers on its right side, dashed in the figure, as the reference fibers. What is the shear force at the base?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q,0" tratteggi="0.1,0 0.1,4" quote="A,B,4 m,-1.3" titolo="Fixed-base column with a uniform horizontal load; dashed lines indicate the reference fibers"}

- $T = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $T = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $T = 0$
- $T = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The load has resultant $2 \cdot 4 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, so the horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. In the basic section the part preceding the section contains only the fixed support reactions; $V_A$ is directed along the axis and $M_A$ is a couple, so only $H_A$ contributes to the shear. Applying the section rules while moving upward from the base of the column gives $T = -R^{\mathrm{left}}_x$, hence $T = -(-8) = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$. The same sign is read by rotating the drawing clockwise until the column is horizontal and traveled from left to right: the dotted fibers go down and the column becomes a fixed cantilever to the left with a uniform load down, which at the fixed end has $T = qL = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$. The shear decreases linearly to zero in $B$, so the value $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the mid-height one.
