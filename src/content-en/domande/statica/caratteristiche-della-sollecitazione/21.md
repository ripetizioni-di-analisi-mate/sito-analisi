Of a simply supported beam $AB$, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, the diagram of the shear shown is known: $7\,\mathrm{kN}$ in the segment $AC$, $1\,\mathrm{kN}$ in the segment $CD$ and $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in the segment $DB$, with $AC = CD = DB = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. At which section is the bending moment greatest, and what is its value?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,7;2,0,4,0,1;4,0,6,0,-8" segni=1 quote="A,C,2 m,0.9;C,D,2 m,0.9;D,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

- At $C$, with $14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- At midspan, with $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- At $D$, with $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- At $D$, with $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

In $A$ the moment is zero, because $A$ is an extreme with a hinge and no couples act on it. Because $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T$, in each segment the moment varies by an amount equal to the area of the shear diagram. So $M_C = 7 \cdot 2 = 14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $M_D = 14 + 1 \cdot 2 = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$; finally $M_B = 16 - 8 \cdot 2 = 0$, as it must be on roller support. The shear changes sign to $D$, so the moment is maximum at $D$ and equals $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension. The value $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is only the area of the segment $CD$.
