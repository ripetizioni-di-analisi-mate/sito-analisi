The beam $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, has in $A$ a roller support on a horizontal surface and in $B$ a parallel-link support with the horizontal links, which prevents horizontal translation and rotation and leaves the vertical translation free. The uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts along the entire beam. What is the bending moment at section $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,doppio-pendolo,0" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Beam with a roller at $A$ and a parallel-link support at $B$"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension
- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension
- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension
- $0$

## Explanation

Parallel-link support with horizontal links exhibits a horizontal reaction and moment, but no vertical reaction. Therefore the vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = qL = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward: all the load drops into the roller support. With the left part of an $x$ horizontal coordinate section you have
$$
M(x) = 8x - \frac{2x^2}{2} = 8x - x^2 ,
$$
then $M(4) = 32 - 16 = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, positive, with the lower fibers in tension. The $T(x) = 8 - 2x$ shear vanishes itself out in $B$, consistent with parallel-link support, which does not transmit vertical forces. The value $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$, the maximum moment of a simply supported beam at the ends.
