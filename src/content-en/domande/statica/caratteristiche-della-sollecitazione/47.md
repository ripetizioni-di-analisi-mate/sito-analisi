The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at $A$ and carries the downward force $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in the free end $B$. What forces and moments does the right portion $SB$ exert on the left portion $AS$ across section $S$, a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0,-0.8,F" punti="1,0,S" quote="A,1 0,1 m;1 0,B,2 m" titolo="Cantilever showing force $F$ at the free end and section $S$"}

- A downward force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a clockwise couple of $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- An upward force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a counterclockwise couple of $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- A downward force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a counterclockwise couple of $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- A downward force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a clockwise couple of $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

With the right side, which contains only $F$, the practical rules give $T = -R^{\mathrm{right}}_y = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M = M^{\mathrm{right}}_S = -4 \cdot 2 = -8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, because $F$ has a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and clockwise moment frame about $S$. On the right side of the left side the positive shear is directed downward and the positive moment is counterclockwise. Since $T > 0$, the right side exerts a force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ **downward**; because $M < 0$, exerts a couple **clockwise** of $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The left-hand check confirms the result: fixed support gives $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ up and $M_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise, and the moment $V_A$ about $S$ is $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise, so it serves a clockwise couple of $12 - 4 = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment at the fixed end section.
