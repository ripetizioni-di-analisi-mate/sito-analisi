Column $AB$ has height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and is fixed at its base $A$, as shown. At the top, a horizontal cantilever bracket $BC$ of length $1\,\mathrm{m}$ is rigidly connected to the column. At the bracket tip $C$ a vertical force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the bending moment at the base of the column?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,1,4" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="C,0,-1,F" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,C,1 m,-0.5" titolo="Column with a loaded bracket at the top"}

- $40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the fibers on the left in tension
- $0$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the fibers on the right in tension
- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the fibers on the left in tension

## Explanation

The part following the section is used, i.e. the column above it with the cantilever, which does not contain the fixed support reactions. Only $F$ acts on it, the line of action of which is vertical and distant from $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis of the column. About any section of the column the force therefore has a moment arm of $1\,\mathrm{m}$ and $10 \cdot 1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise moment, which does not depend on the height of the section. By running the column from $A$ to $B$ with the reference fibers on the right, the $M = M^{\mathrm{right}}_S$ rule gives $M = -10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in each section, including base, so the fibers on the left are in tension, on the opposite side to the cantilever. The column is also in compression with $N = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by using the height of the column as a moment arm, which is parallel to the force.
