The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at $A$. It carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire length and in the free end $B$ the force $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ directed upward. What is the maximum positive bending moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="B,0,1,F" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Cantilever with a uniform load and an upward force at the free end"}

- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The portion to the right of the section $x$ of the coordinate is used, which contains $F$ and the load of the section of length $u = 4 - x$. Practical rules give
$$
T(x) = 2u - 4 ,
\qquad
M(x) = 4u - u^2 ,
$$
because $F$ has counterclockwise moment $4u$ and the load has resultant $2u$ with a moment arm of $\dfrac{u}{2}$. The shear vanishes by $u = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, i.e. at midspan, where the moment has the maximum $M = 8 - 4 = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension. All fixed support $M = 16 - 16 = 0$. The value $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ neglects the distributed load on the segment $CB$, the value $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment of force alone about fixed support.
