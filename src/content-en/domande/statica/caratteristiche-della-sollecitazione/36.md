Column $AB$ has height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and is fixed at its base $A$, as shown. At its top $B$ a horizontal force $P = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is the bending moment at the base, and which fibers are in tension?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="B,1,0,P" quote="A,B,3 m,0.8" titolo="Column fixed at its base with a horizontal force at its top"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the fibers on the right in tension
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the fibers on the left in tension
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the fibers on the left in tension
- $0$

## Explanation

Follow the column from $A$ toward $B$, so that the reference fibers are on the right. The only force beyond the base section is $P$, whose moment about $A$ is clockwise and equals $4 \cdot 3 = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Applying the rule $M = M^{\mathrm{right}}_S$ gives $M = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The negative sign places the fibers opposite the reference side, on the left, in tension. The column bends like a cantilever, with tension on the side opposite the direction of the load. Halving the moment arm gives $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
