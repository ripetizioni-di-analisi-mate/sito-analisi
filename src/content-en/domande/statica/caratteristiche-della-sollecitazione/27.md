At section $C$ of a horizontal beam the two segment are connected by an internal two-force link, that is, by a single pin-ended link, arranged along the axis of the beam. The beam is loaded in any way and is in equilibrium. Which internal forces or moments must vanish at $C$?

- Only $M$
- Only $T$
- $T$ and $M$
- $N$ and $M$

## Explanation

The pin-ended link is a unloaded member connected by two hinges, so it only transmits a direct force along its axis. In this case the axis of the pin-ended link coincides with the axis of the beam, so the force exchanged between the segment in $C$ is directed along the axis and passes through the centroid of the section. This force has no orthogonal component to the axis, therefore $T = 0$; its moment about the centroid is zero, so $M = 0$. The axial force can be different from zero: a horizontal force applied to one log is transmitted to the other through the pin-ended link.
