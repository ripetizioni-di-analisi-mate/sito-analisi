The beam $CD$ shown, of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, rests on a hinge $A$ and $B$ roller support, with $CA = BD = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. The $F = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ forces down act at the ends $C$ and $D$ and the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on the segment $AB$. What is the bending moment at midspan?

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,2,0;B,6,0;D,8,0" aste="C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="C,0,-1.5,F;D,0,-1.5,F" quote="C,A,2 m;A,B,4 m;B,D,2 m" titolo="Beam with two overhangs, forces at both ends, and a uniform load between the supports"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

By $V_A = V_B = \dfrac{5 + 8 + 5}{2} = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ symmetry. The midspan is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$ and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$; to the left of the section act $F$ in $C$, $V_A$ and the load on the segment of $2\,\mathrm{m}$, resulting in $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from the section. Practical rules give
$$
M = -5 \cdot 4 + 9 \cdot 2 - 4 \cdot 1 = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
so even at midspan are in tension the upper fibers. The value $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$ of the span without the overhangs, the value $-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment on the supports and the value $-2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained without the distributed load in the moment equation.
