The $AB$ inclined cantilever shown is fixed at $A$; the free end $B$ is located $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$ and brings the vertical downward force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$. You run the member from $A$ to $B$. What is the shear force in the member?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,217" forze="B,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.8;4 0,B,3 m,0.8" titolo="Inclined cantilever with a vertical force at the free end"}

- $T = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $T = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $T = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $T = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The member forms with the axis $x$ the angle $\alpha$ with $\cos\alpha = \dfrac{4}{5}$ and $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{3}{5}$. The part following a section contains only $F$, so the part preceding it is subject to external forces of opposite result, i.e. $R^{\mathrm{left}}_x = 0$ and $R^{\mathrm{left}}_y = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$. By the section rules of $T = -R^{\mathrm{left}}_x \sin\alpha + R^{\mathrm{left}}_y \cos\alpha = 10 \cdot \dfrac{4}{5} = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ inclined members, constant over the whole member. The component of $F$ along the axis gives the compressive axial force $N = -10 \cdot \dfrac{3}{5} = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ is that of a horizontal cantilever, which does not take into calculation the inclination.
