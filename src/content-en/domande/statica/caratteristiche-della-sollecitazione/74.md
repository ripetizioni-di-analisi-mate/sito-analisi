Two straight $AC$ and $CB$ members, hinged to each other in $C$ and to the ground in $A$ and $B$, form the asymmetric arch shown. Compared to $A$, point $C$ is located $3\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ above, and point $B$ is located $7\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $1\,\mathrm{m}$ on the top. In $C$ the vertical force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the reaction at pin $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,4;B,7,1" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" forze="C,0,-1.3,F" quote="A,3 0,3 m,0.6;3 0,C,4 m,0.5" titolo="Asymmetric three-hinged arch with force $F$ at $C$"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, along line $BC$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$, along line $BC$
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$, vertical
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, vertical

## Explanation

Each member is connected by two hinges and does not carry loads along the axis, so it is only subject to a constant axial force and the reaction at $B$ has the direction of $CB$. The directions of the members are given by the $\dfrac{1}{5}(3, 4)$ for $AC$ and $\dfrac{1}{5}(4, -3)$ for $CB$, which are orthogonal. Thus force $F$ applied at $C$ resolves into two components along the members, each carried to the ground by the corresponding member. The component $F$ along the $C$ to $B$ direction equals $(0, -10) \cdot \dfrac{1}{5}(4, -3) = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, so the member $CB$ is in compression with $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the hinge $B$ reacts with $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ along $BC$, towards $C$. The $CA$ long component equals $8\,\mathrm{kN}$, which is the reaction of the hinge $A$.
