The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at $A$ and carries the force $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward and a couple $\mathcal{M}$ in the free end $B$. What couple $\mathcal{M}$ makes the bending moment zero at the fixed end?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0,-0.8,F" coppie="B,1,ℳ" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Cantilever with force $F$ and couple $\mathcal{M}$ at the free end"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The portion to the right of the section $x$ of the coordinate is used, which contains $F$ and the couple, with $\mathcal{M}$ positive if counterclockwise. The force has clockwise moment $4\,(3 - x)$ about the section, so for rule $M = M^{\mathrm{right}}_S$
$$
M(x) = \mathcal{M} - 4\,(3 - x) .
$$
At the fixed end $M(0) = \mathcal{M} - 12$, which vanishes for $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, positive, i.e. **counterclockwise**. With this couple you have $M(x) = 4x$: the moment is zero at the fixed end and grows up to $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $B$, with the lower fibers in tension. A couple clockwise would add up to the moment of the force and give $M(0) = -24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
