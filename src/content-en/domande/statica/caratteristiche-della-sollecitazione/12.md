The L-shaped cantilever shown consists of column $AB$ of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at its base $A$ and beam $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$, joined rigidly to the column. At the free end $C$ a horizontal force $P = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right and a vertical force $F = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Taking the fibers on the inside of the L as the reference fibers, what is the bending moment at joint $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,2,4" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="C,0,-0.8,F;2.8 4,0.8,0," etichette="2.7,4.3,P" tratteggi="0.1,0 0.1,3.9 2,3.9" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.7;B,C,2 m,0.6" titolo="L-shaped cantilever with forces $P$ and $F$ at its free end"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the inner fibers in tension
- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the outer fibers in tension
- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the outer fibers in tension
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the outer fibers in tension

## Explanation

In crossbeam, the part following the section, which contains only the forces in $C$, agrees. About $B$ the force $P$ has zero moment arm, because its line of action is the axis of the crossbeam; $F$ force has a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $2 \cdot 2 = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise moment. For the $M = M^{\mathrm{right}}_S$ rule we therefore have $M_B = -4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and therefore the upper fibers of the crossbeam, i.e. the outer ones of the L, are in tension. For continuity in the rigid joint the same value is at the top of the column. The value $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment at the fixed end, where $P$ also has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and the value $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by giving $P$ the moment $4\,\mathrm{m}$ arm and the opposite direction.
