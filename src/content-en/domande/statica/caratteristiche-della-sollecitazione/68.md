In the Gerber beam shown, cantilever $AC$ is fixed at $A$ and has length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Beam $CD$ connects through internal hinge $C$ and rests on roller $B$, with $CB = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ to the roller and overhang length $BD = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. The only load is a downward force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at free end $D$. What force does hinge $C$ transmit to cantilever $AC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0;D,7,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" forze="D,0,-1.4,F" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,3 m;B,D,2 m" titolo="Gerber beam with force $F$ on the overhang of the suspended beam"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

Taking moments for segment $CD$ about hinge $C$ gives $3\,V_B - 6 \cdot 5 = 0$, so $V_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. Vertical force equilibrium of the segment gives the hinge force $Y_C = 6 - 10 = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. The cantilever holds down end $C$, which tends to lift. By action and reaction, segment $CD$ exerts a force $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ **upward** on cantilever $AC$ at its end $C$. The cantilever therefore bends upward. At the fixed support the moment is $M_A = 4 \cdot 2 = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension. The value $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the reaction at roller $B$.
