Beam $AB$ in the figure has span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$. Two forces act downward, $F_1 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$ and $F_2 = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $D$, with $AC = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $AD = 4\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the bending moment at section $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.5,F_1;D,0,-1,F_2" quote="A,C,2 m;C,D,2 m;D,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under forces $F_1$ and $F_2$"}

- $M_C = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $M_C = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $M_C = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $M_C = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Taking moments about $B$ gives $-6\,V_A + 6 \cdot 4 + 3 \cdot 2 = 0$, so $V_A = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. The only force to the left of section $C$ is $V_A$. It has moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and produces a clockwise moment about $C$. The sign convention therefore gives the bending moment
$$
M_C = 5 \cdot 2 = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
which is positive and puts the bottom fibers in tension. Interchanging the reactions gives $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the product $F_1 \cdot AC$, whereas $F_1$ has zero moment arm about $C$.
