A simply supported beam $AB$ of span $5\,\mathrm{m}$ is loaded only on segment $DB$, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ long and adjacent to support $B$, by a uniform load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the maximum bending moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,3,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="D,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,D,3 m;D,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with a uniform load on segment $DB$"}

- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $5.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6.4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The resultant of the load is $5 \cdot 2 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and acts $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, so moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $V_A = \dfrac{10 \cdot 1}{5} = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ and vertical equilibrium gives $V_B = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, both upwards. In the unloaded segment $AD$ the shear force is $2\,\mathrm{kN}$, therefore the moment increases up to $M_D = 2 \cdot 3 = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. In the loaded segment the shear is $T(x) = 2 - 5\,(x - 3)$, which vanishes at $x = 3.4\,\mathrm{m}$, so the moment is maximum at this section and equals
$$
M_{\max} = 2 \cdot 3.4 - \frac{5 \cdot 0.4^2}{2} = 6.8 - 0.4 = 6.4\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The value $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by replacing the load with its concentrated resultant, the value $5.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment at the section where the resultant of the load acts, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and the value $2.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{q\,c^2}{8}$ with the loaded length alone, $c = 2\,\mathrm{m}$.
