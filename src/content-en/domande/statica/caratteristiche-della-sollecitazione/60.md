The beam $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and supported at both ends, carries a symmetrical triangular load that equals zero in $A$ and $B$ and $q_0 = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in the $C$ midspan. What is the maximum bending moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0,0.7,q_0;C,B,0.7,0," quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under a symmetric triangular load"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The load has resultant $\dfrac{6 \cdot 4}{2} = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is symmetrical, so $V_A = V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the moment is maximum at midspan. The left part of the midspan carries the triangle from $0$ to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the section $AC$, resulting in $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ being applied to a third of $AC$ by $C$, i.e. to $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ by the midspan. Therefore
$$
M_{\max} = 6 \cdot 2 - 6 \cdot \frac{2}{3} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
which coincides with $\dfrac{q_0 L^2}{12}$. The value $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{q_0 L^2}{8}$, which applies to the uniform $q_0$ load over all span. The value $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by applying the resultant at the midpoint of $AC$, the value $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ by applying it to one third of $AC$ from $A$.
