The three-hinged frame shown is pinned at its bases $A$ and $B$. Columns $AD$ and $BE$ have height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and beam $DE$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. At $D$ a horizontal force $P = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is the horizontal reaction component $H_A$ at $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;C,2,4;E,4,4;B,4,0" aste="A-D;D-E;E-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" forze="D,1.2,0,P" tratteggi="0.1,0 0.1,3.9 3.9,3.9 3.9,0" quote="A,D,4 m,-0.7;D,E,4 m,-0.7" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with horizontal force $P$ at $D$"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left

## Explanation

The rotation of the entire portal frame around $A$ gives $4\,V_B - 6 \cdot 4 = 0$, then $V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The right side, from the hinge $C$ to the $B$ foot, carries no loads, so the moment of reactions in $B$ about $C$ is zero, i.e. $2\,V_B + 4\,H_B = 0$, and therefore $H_B = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$. The horizontal translation finally gives
$$
H_A = -6 - H_B = -3\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
i.e. $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. The value $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by entrusting all the force $P$ to the hinge $A$, and the value $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right has the opposite direction to that of $H_A$.
