The inclined member $AB$ shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. The roller lies $6\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right and $8\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$, giving the member a length of $10\,\mathrm{m}$. At its midpoint $D$ a vertical force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the maximum bending moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,3,4;B,6,8" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="D,0,-1.8,F" quote="A,6 0,6 m,1;6 0,B,8 m,1" titolo="Simply supported inclined member with force $F$ at its midpoint"}

- $25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The roller support gives a vertical reaction and the force is vertical, then $H_A = 0$, and by $V_A = V_B = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ symmetry upward. The moment is maximum under the force, in $D$, where the mark changes the shear. With the part that precedes $D$, which contains only $V_A$, the arm of $V_A$ about $D$ is the horizontal distance between the two vertical lines, i.e. $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore $M_D = 5 \cdot 3 = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, which is $\dfrac{F\,l}{4}$ with the horizontal projection $l = 6\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained using the member length as the arm, the value $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ using the vertical projection.
