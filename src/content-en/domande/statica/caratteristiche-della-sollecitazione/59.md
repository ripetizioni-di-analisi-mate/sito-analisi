The cantilever $AB$, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at $A$ and carries the uniform $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load over the entire length. Which statement about the bending moment diagram at the free end $B$ is true?

- $M_B = 0$, and the diagram has a corner at $B$
- $M_B = 0$, and the diagram has a horizontal tangent at $B$
- $M_B = 0$, and at $B$ the diagram has slope $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $M_B = -9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension

## Explanation

With the portion to the right of the section $x$, which contains the load of the segment of length $3 - x$, the practical rules give
$$
T(x) = 2\,(3 - x) ,
\qquad
M(x) = -(3 - x)^2 .
$$
In $B$ you have $M = 0$, because to the right of $B$ no forces act, and also $T = 0$. The slope of the $M$ diagram is the shear, so in $B$ the diagram has **horizontal tangent**. The value $-9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment at the fixed end, where the shear equals $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the slope is maximum. A corner would require a force concentrated at $B$.
