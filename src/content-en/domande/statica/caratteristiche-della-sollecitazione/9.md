The Gerber beam shown is formed by the beam $AC$, with a pin at $A$, a roller support at $B$ and the $BC$ overhang, and the beam $CD$, connected with the internal hinge $C$ and resting on the roller support at $D$. Distances are $AB = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, $BC = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, $CD = 4\,\mathrm{m}$; the downward force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts in $E$, midway through $CD$. What is reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;E,8,0;D,10,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" cerniere="C" forze="E,0,-2,F" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,2 m;C,E,2 m;E,D,2 m" titolo="Gerber beam with force $F$ on suspended beam $CD$"}

- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward

## Explanation

Suspended beam $CD$ is supported at $C$ and $D$ and carries the force at midspan. Thus $V_D = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, and hinge $C$ exerts the upward force $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the beam. By the principle of action and reaction the beam $CD$ pushes the $C$ extreme of the beam $AC$ with $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Moment equilibrium of beam $AC$ about $B$ gives
$$
-4\,V_A - 4 \cdot 2 = 0 ,
$$
therefore $V_A = -2\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ down. The value $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ is achieved by loading the hinge with the entire $F$ force, the value $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ using the moment $AC = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ arm instead of $BC$.
