The beam $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and supported at both ends, carries the uniform $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load over all the span. What is the bending moment at a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.4,0.4,q" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

By $V_A = \dfrac{qL}{2} = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ symmetry. To the left of the section act $V_A$, with $1\,\mathrm{m}$ arm, and the load on the segment of $1\,\mathrm{m}$, with the resultant $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ applied in the middle of the segment, i.e. to $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from the section. Practical rules give
$$
M = 12 \cdot 1 - 6 \cdot 0.5 = 9\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The value $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained without the load, the value $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ with the result applied to $1\,\mathrm{m}$ by the section instead of in the middle of the segment.
