Over the first $2\,\mathrm{m}$ of a span, the shear decreases linearly from $8$ to $0\,\mathrm{kN}$. Over the remainder it is negative. If the initial moment is $M=-3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, what is the maximum signed moment on the span, given $M'=T$?

- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $13\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The moment rises until the shear changes sign. The positive accumulated area is triangular, $\dfrac{2\cdot8}{2}=8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Adding the initial moment gives $-3+8=5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
