The beam $AB$ shown, of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$ and supported at both ends, carries the uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ only on the center section $CD$, with $AC = DB = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the maximum bending moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="C,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,C,2 m;C,D,4 m;D,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with a uniform load on the middle segment"}

- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The load has resultant $3 \cdot 4 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is symmetrical, hence $V_A = V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$. By symmetry the shear vanishes at midspan, where the moment is maximum. With the left side of the midspan, which contains $V_A$ with $4\,\mathrm{m}$ arm and the load of the section from $C$ at midspan, of the resultant $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $1\,\mathrm{m}$ arm, you get
$$
M_{\max} = 6 \cdot 4 - 6 \cdot 1 = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The value $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$ with the load extended to all span, the value $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment at $C$ and the value $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{q\,c^2}{8}$ with only the $c = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ loaded length.
