A simply supported beam $AB$ of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ is acted on by a uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, extending over the whole span, and by a downward force $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan $C$. What is the maximum bending moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="C,0,-1.6,F" quote="A,C,3 m;C,B,3 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with a uniform load and a force $F$ at midspan"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The load and the force are symmetric about midspan, so $V_A = V_B = \dfrac{2 \cdot 6 + 4}{2} = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, upwards. In segment $AC$ the shear force is $T(x) = 8 - 2x$, which just to the left of $C$ equals $2\,\mathrm{kN}$; under the force the shear has a jump of $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$, so just to the right of $C$ it equals $-2\,\mathrm{kN}$. The shear changes sign at $C$ without vanishing inside the two segments, therefore the moment is maximum under the force. The part to the left of $C$ contains $V_A$, with lever arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$, and the load of resultant $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, with lever arm $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$, and gives
$$
M_{\max} = 8 \cdot 3 - 6 \cdot 1.5 = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The same value is the sum of $\dfrac{qL^2}{8} = 9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $\dfrac{FL}{4} = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ neglects the force, the value $21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses $\dfrac{FL}{2}$ instead of $\dfrac{FL}{4}$ and the value $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses $\dfrac{qL^2}{4}$ instead of $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$.
