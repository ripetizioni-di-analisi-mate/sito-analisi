The structure shown is formed by two L-shaped bodies, $ADC$ and $CEB$, connected by the internal hinge $C$ and hinged to the ground in $A$ and $B$. The columns $AD$ and $BE$ are $4\,\mathrm{m}$ high and the horizontal $DC$ and $EC$ sections are $2\,\mathrm{m}$ long. Only the section $DC$ carries the uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, while the body $CEB$ is unloaded. Taking the inner fibers as the reference fibers, what is the bending moment at joint $E$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;C,2,4;E,4,4;B,4,0" aste="A-D;D-E;E-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" carichi="D,C,0.5,0.5,q" tratteggi="0.1,0 0.1,3.9 3.9,3.9 3.9,0" quote="A,D,4 m,-0.8;D,C,2 m,-1;C,E,2 m,-1" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with a uniform load on half the crossbeam; dashed lines indicate the reference fibers"}

- $0$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the inner fibers in tension
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the outer fibers in tension
- $3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the outer fibers in tension

## Explanation

The right side $CEB$ is unloaded and connected only by the $C$ and $B$ hinges, so the reaction at $B$ has the direction of the line $CB$: for every $2\,\mathrm{m}$ of ascent it moves $1\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left, therefore $H_B = -\dfrac{V_B}{2}$. The load has resultant $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and the rotation of the portal frame around $A$ gives $4\,V_B - 6 \cdot 1 = 0$; then $V_B = 1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $H_B = -0.75\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the left. The joint $E$ uses the part that follows the section, i.e. the column $EB$ with the reaction at $B$. About $E$, $V_B$ has zero moment arm and $H_B$ has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$ with $0.75 \cdot 4 = 3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise moment; for the $M = M^{\mathrm{right}}_S$ rule we therefore have $M_E = -3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the outer fibers in tension.
