The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and fixed at $A$, carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire length and in the free end $B$ the force $F = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ of components $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. What is the reaction moment $M_A$ at the fixed support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.4,0.4,q" forze="B,0.6,-0.8,F" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Cantilever with a uniform load and an inclined force at the tip"}

- $M_A = 21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_A = 21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The resultant of the uniform load is $2 \cdot 3 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is applied at midspan, $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The horizontal component of $F$ has the line of action on the beam axis, which passes through $A$, so it has zero moment about $A$; the vertical component has $3\,\mathrm{m}$ arm. Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives
$$
M_A - 6 \cdot 1.5 - 4 \cdot 3 = 0 ,
$$
Therefore $M_A = 21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise. The value $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained without the distributed load, the value $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ using the entire $F$ force with the moment $3\,\mathrm{m}$ arm.
