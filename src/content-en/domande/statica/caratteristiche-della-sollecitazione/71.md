The $AB$ ramp shown is an inclined straight member, with a pin at $A$ and a roller support on a horizontal surface in $B$, which is located $4\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$. It carries a vertical load down by $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ for each horizontal projection meter. What is the axial force at section $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.4,0.4,q" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.8;4 0,B,3 m,0.8" titolo="Ramp under a vertical load distributed over its horizontal projection"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $2.4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $3.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $2.4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension

## Explanation

The total load is $2 \cdot 4 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and passes through the coordinate $2\,\mathrm{m}$ point. The roller support gives a vertical reaction and the loads are vertical, therefore $H_A = 0$, and by symmetry of the load $V_A = V_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The member forms with the horizontal the angle $\alpha$ with $\cos\alpha = \dfrac{4}{5}$ and $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{3}{5}$. At section $A$, only $V_A$ acts, which has the component $V_A \sin\alpha = 2.4\,\mathrm{kN}$ along the axis, directed towards $B$, and the component $V_A \cos\alpha = 3.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ orthogonal to the axis. By the section rules of $N = -V_A \sin\alpha = -2.4\,\mathrm{kN}$ inclined members, i.e. $2.4\,\mathrm{kN}$ of **compression**, while $3.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the shear.
