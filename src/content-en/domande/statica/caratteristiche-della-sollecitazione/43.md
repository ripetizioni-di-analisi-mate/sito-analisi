The Gerber beam shown is formed by the beam $AC$, with a hinge $A$ and $B$ roller support, and the beam $CD$, connected with the $C$ internal hinge and resting on the roller support at $D$; $AB = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, $BC = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $CD = 4\,\mathrm{m}$. The only load is the clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ applied in the $E$ midspan of $CD$. What is the bending moment at section $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;E,8,0;D,10,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" cerniere="C" coppie="E,-1,ℳ" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,2 m;C,E,2 m;E,D,2 m" titolo="Gerber beam with couple $\mathcal{M}$ on suspended beam $CD$"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension
- $0$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension

## Explanation

The hinge $C$ does not transmit couples, so the couple applied to $CD$ is balanced by the vertical forces in $C$ and $D$. The rotation of $CD$ around $D$ gives $-4\,Y_C - 12 = 0$, so the hinge exerts the force $Y_C = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$ on $CD$, i.e. $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ down, and the roller support at $D$ gives $V_D = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ up. By the principle of action and reaction the beam $CD$ exerts on $AC$, in $C$, a force of $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. At section $B$ the right part $BC$ contains only this force, which has moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and counterclockwise moment about $B$, so for rule $M = M^{\mathrm{right}}_S$ you have $M_B = 3 \cdot 2 = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension. The value $0$ is obtained by assuming that the couple remains all on the beam $CD$, the value $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ assuming that it arrives unchanged in $B$.
