The beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and supported at both ends, carries the uniform $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load over all the span. It runs from $B$ towards $A$, with the reference fibers to the right of the travel direction, that is, the upper fibers, dotted shown. With this choice, what is the bending moment at midspan?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" tratteggi="0,0.12 6,0.12" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Simply supported beam traversed from $B$ toward $A$; dashed lines indicate the reference fibers"}

- $M = 9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $M = 0$
- $M = -18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $M = -9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The load is directed downward, so at midspan are in tension the lower fibers, whatever the direction of travel; the absolute value of the moment is $\dfrac{qL^2}{8} = \dfrac{2 \cdot 36}{8} = 9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The sign depends on the reference fibers: $M$ is positive when it tends the dotted fibers. By running the beam from $B$ to $A$, the fibers to the right of the travel direction are the upper ones, which at midspan are in compression. Therefore with this choice $M = -9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The diagram, drawn from the side of the fibers in tension, remains the same: only the sign attributed to the values changes.
