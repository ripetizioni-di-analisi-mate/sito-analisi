The beam $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and is supported at $B$ by a two-force inclined link of $45^\circ$, whose pin-ended link drops from $B$ to the left. At midspan, the vertical force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the axial force in the beam?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,pendolo,225" forze="C,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Beam with a pin at $A$ and an inclined link at $B$"}

- $0$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension

## Explanation

The two-force link reaction is directed along the pin-ended link, so it has equal horizontal and vertical components in magnitude. The horizontal component passes through $A$, so moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $4\,V_B - 8 \cdot 2 = 0$ and $V_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The pin-ended link pushes $B$ up and right, then $H_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and, for horizontal translation, $H_A = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. To the left part of each section the only horizontal force is $H_A$, then $N = -H_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, of **tension**: the hinge pulls $A$ to the left and the two-force link pushes $B$ to the right. With a horizontal-plane roller support at $B$ the axial force would be null.
