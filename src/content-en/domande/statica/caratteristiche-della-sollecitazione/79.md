Beam $AB$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a pin at $A$, and a roller at $B$. At midspan $C$, a vertical cantilever bracket $CE$ of height $1\,\mathrm{m}$ is welded to the beam. At the bracket tip $E$, a horizontal force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is the bending moment in the beam immediately to the right of $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0;E,2,1" aste="A-B;C-E" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="E,1.2,0,F" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m;C,E,1 m,-0.5" titolo="Simply supported beam with a horizontal force applied to a bracket"}

- $-3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The force $F$ has a moment arm of $1\,\mathrm{m}$ about $A$ and $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise moment, so moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $4\,V_B - 6 = 0$ and $V_B = 1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; therefore $V_A = -1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward. Immediately to the right of $C$ the right part contains only $V_B$, with a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and counterclockwise moment, then $M = 1.5 \cdot 2 = 3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension. Immediately to the left of $C$ the left side contains $V_A$ and gives $M = -3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The jump of $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is due to the clockwise transport couple $F \cdot 1 = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, which appears bringing $F$ back to the beam axis in $C$.
