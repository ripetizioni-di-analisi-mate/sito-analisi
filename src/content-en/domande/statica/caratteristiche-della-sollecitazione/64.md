The beam $AC$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The uniform $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load acts on the segment $AB$ and the downward force $F = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts in the free end $C$ of the overhang. What is the shear force immediately to the left of $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="C,0,-1,F" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,2 m" titolo="Overhanging beam with a uniform load in the span and a force at the free end"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-8.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-5.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $4\,V_B - 8 \cdot 2 - 3 \cdot 6 = 0$ and hence $V_B = 8.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. Vertical force equilibrium then gives $V_A = 11 - 8.5 = 2.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. Both reactions act upward. Immediately to the left of $B$, the left-hand free body carries $V_A$ and the entire load on $AB$, whose downward resultant is $8\,\mathrm{kN}$. The shear is therefore $T = 2.5 - 8 = -5.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. Crossing $B$ adds the reaction $V_B$ and raises the shear to $3\,\mathrm{kN}$. It stays at this value throughout the overhang and equals $F$. The jump at $B$ is $8.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, the roller reaction.
