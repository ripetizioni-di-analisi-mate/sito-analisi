In the Gerber beam shown, cantilever $AC$ is fixed at $A$ and has length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Beam $CB$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, connects to the cantilever through internal hinge $C$, and rests on roller $B$. The only load is a downward force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $D$, a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Which statement about beam $CB$ is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,1,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" forze="D,0,-1.3,F" quote="A,D,1 m;D,C,1 m;C,B,4 m" titolo="Gerber beam with force $F$ on cantilever $AC$"}

- Both shear and bending moment are zero at every section of $CB$
- Roller $B$ carries $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the moment on $CB$ is linear
- The shear on $CB$ is $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the moment is zero
- The moment on $CB$ reaches its maximum $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at midspan

## Explanation

On the segment $CB$, only the force of the hinge $C$ and the roller support at $B$ reaction act, because it does not carry loads. A body in equilibrium under only two forces has opposed and directed them along the line joining the application points, i.e. along the horizontal axis $CB$. The reaction of the roller support is vertical, so both forces are zero: $V_B = 0$. Hence $T = 0$ and $M = 0$ at every section of $CB$, and the fixed support carries the entire load. A force applied to the supporting segment does not involve the suspended segment.
