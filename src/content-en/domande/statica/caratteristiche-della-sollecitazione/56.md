The horizontal $AB$ cantilever, $3\,\mathrm{m}$ long and fixed at $A$, carries a uniform $p = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ axial load over the entire length, i.e. a distributed force directed along the axis, from $A$ to $B$. No other loads act. What is the axial force at midspan?

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension

## Explanation

The right part of the midspan section is used, which does not contain the fixed support reactions. On it acts the axial load of the segment of length $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$, resulting in $2 \cdot 1.5 = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ directed towards $B$, that is to the right. By the section rules $N = R^{\mathrm{right}}_x = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$, positive, then the section is **in tension**: the load pulls the right side away from the section. In an axis $x$ section, $N(x) = 2\,(3 - x)$ is the same, so the axial force linearly decreases from $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to fixed support at zero in the free end. The value $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ is that of the fixed support section, where all the load is collected.
