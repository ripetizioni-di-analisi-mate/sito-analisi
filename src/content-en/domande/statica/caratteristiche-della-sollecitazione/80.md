The beam $AB$ shown, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long, has a hinge $A$ and is supported at $B$ by the $BD$ vertical member, $3\,\mathrm{m}$ long, connected with a hinge to the beam $B$ and a hinge to the ground in $D$. The downward force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at midspan $C$ of the beam. What are the internal forces and moments in member $BD$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0;D,4,-3" aste="A-B;B-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,cerniera" cerniere="B" forze="C,0,-1.3,F" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m;B,D,3 m,-0.6" titolo="Beam supported by a vertical member pinned at both ends"}

- $N = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension, $T = 0$, $M = 0$
- $N = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression, $T = 0$, $M = 0$
- $N = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression, $T = 0$, $M = 0$
- $N = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression and $M$ varying linearly to $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $D$

## Explanation

The member $BD$ is straight, connected by two hinges to its extremes and free of loads, so the two forces that charge it are directed along its axis. Therefore in each section of the $T = 0$ and $M = 0$ member, and the axial force is constant. On the beam the member exerts a $R_B$ vertical force at $B$, and moment equilibrium of the beam about $A$ gives $4\,R_B - 8 \cdot 2 = 0$, then $R_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. By action and reaction the beam pushes the member down with $4\,\mathrm{kN}$, while the ground pushes it up: the member is **in compression** with $N = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$. The member acts like the two-force link of a support.
