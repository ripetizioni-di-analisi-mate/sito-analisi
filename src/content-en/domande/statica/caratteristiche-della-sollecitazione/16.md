Column $AB$ in the figure is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ tall and fixed at its base $A$. Two horizontal brackets $BC$ and $BD$, each of length $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$, extend to the left and right from a rigid joint at the top of the column. Equal downward forces $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ act at the tips $C$ and $D$. Find the axial force and bending moment in the column.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,-1.5,4;D,1.5,4" aste="A-B;C-D" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="C,0,-1,F;D,0,-1,F" quote="C,B,1.5 m;B,D,1.5 m;A,B,4 m,-1.1" titolo="Column with two symmetrically placed loaded brackets"}

- $N = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression, $M = 0$
- $N = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression, $M = 9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $N = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression, $M = 0$
- $N = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression, $M = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

In one section of the column the part above is used, on which only the two $F$ forces act. About the section, the force at $D$ produces clockwise moment $6 \cdot 1.5 = 9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and the force at $C$ produces counterclockwise moment $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Their total moment is therefore zero, giving $M = 0$ at every section of the column. The two forces has resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ down, directed along the axis of the column, so the shear is zero and the axial force equals $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ compression. The value $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment of a single cantilever, as if the other one were unloaded.
