The three-hinged arch shown is formed by the straight $AC$ and $CB$ members, with the $A$ and $B$ hinge on the ground and the internal hinge $C$. Compared to $A$, point $C$ is located $3\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above, and point $B$ is located $6\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right at the same elevation. At the midpoint $D$ of the member $AC$ the vertical force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. The member is traversed from $A$ to $C$, with the reference fibers within the arch. What is the bending moment at $D$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,1.5,1.5;C,3,3;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" forze="D,0,-1,F" tratteggi="3,0 3,3" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9;3 0,C,3 m,0.5" titolo="Three-hinged arch with force $F$ at the midpoint of $AC$"}

- $2.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the outer fibers in tension
- $6.75\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the inner fibers in tension
- $0$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the inner fibers in tension

## Explanation

The member $CB$ is unloaded and connected by two hinges, so the reaction at $B$ is directed along the line $BC$, inclined by $45^\circ$, and has equal components in magnitude: $V_B$ upward and $H_B$ to the left. Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $6\,V_B - 6 \cdot 1.5 = 0$, so $V_B = 1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $H_B = -1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The translations then give $V_A = 4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ up and $H_A = 1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right. About $D$, located a distance $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of and $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$, force $V_A$ produces clockwise moment $4.5 \cdot 1.5 = 6.75\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and force $H_A$ produces counterclockwise moment $1.5 \cdot 1.5 = 2.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. So $M_D = 6.75 - 2.25 = 4.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, positive, with the inner fibers in tension. The value $6.75\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ neglects the $H_A$ thrust.
