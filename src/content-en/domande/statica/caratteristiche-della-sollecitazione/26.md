The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at $A$ and carries a triangular load that equals zero in $A$ and $q_0 = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in the free end $B$. What is the shear force at the midspan section $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0,0.7,q_0" punti="2,0,S" quote="A,2 0,2 m;2 0,B,2 m" titolo="Cantilever with a triangular load increasing toward the free end"}

- $T = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $T = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $T = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $T = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The load grows linearly from $0$ to $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $4\,\mathrm{m}$, so at $x$ distance from $A$ it is $q(x) = x$, in $\mathrm{kN/m}$, and in $S$ it is $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. The right side of $S$ is used, which contains no reaction: on it the load is a trapezoid with bases $2$ and $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and height $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Its resultant value is $\dfrac{2 + 4}{2} \cdot 2 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ down. By the section rules $T = -R^{\mathrm{right}}_y = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ is half of the total load, which would be obtained with a uniform load.
