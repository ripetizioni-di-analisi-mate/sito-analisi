The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at the right end $B$ and carries a triangular load that equals zero in the free end $A$ and $q_0 = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in $B$. What is the bending moment at the fixed section $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="B,incastro,0" carichi="A,B,0,0.6,q_0" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Cantilever fixed at $B$ under a triangular load"}

- $M_B = -9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $M_B = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $M_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $M_B = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The portion to the left of the section is used, which contains only the load. The resultant triangle equals $\dfrac{4 \cdot 3}{2} = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is applied in the centroid of the diagram, to one third of the base from the side of $q_0$, i.e. $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$. About $B$ this force, directed down and placed to the left, has counterclockwise moment $6 \cdot 1 = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and for rule $M = -M^{\mathrm{left}}_S$ you get $M_B = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension. The value $-9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ corresponds to the result applied in the middle of the cantilever, the value $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ to the result applied to a third of the base from the $A$ side.
