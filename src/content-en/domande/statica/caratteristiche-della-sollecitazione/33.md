The Gerber beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller support at $B$, an internal pin at $C$ and a roller support at $D$, with $AB = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, $BC = 1\,\mathrm{m}$ and $CD = 4\,\mathrm{m}$. The uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ only acts on the section $CD$. How does the shear force change as we cross support $B$ from left to right?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,5,0;D,9,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" cerniere="C" carichi="C,D,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,1 m;C,D,4 m" titolo="Gerber beam with a uniform load on suspended beam $CD$"}

- It increases by $5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- It increases by $4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- It decreases by $5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- It increases by $1\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Suspended beam $CD$ is supported at $C$ and $D$ and carries $2 \cdot 4 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$. By symmetry it therefore exerts a downward force $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the supporting beam at $C$. For the $AC$ supporting segment moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $4\,V_B - 4 \cdot 5 = 0$, therefore $V_B = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $V_A = -1\,\mathrm{kN}$. In $B$ the concentrated force $V_B$ acts, directed upward, then by the theorem on the shear jump the diagram rises by $5\,\mathrm{kN}$, from $-1\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $4\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the force transmitted by the hinge, which is the shear immediately to the right of $B$ and not the jump.
