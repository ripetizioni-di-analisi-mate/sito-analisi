The beam $AD$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. It brings the downward force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $C$, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and the uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the $BD$ overhang. How far from $A$, between $C$ and $B$, does the bending moment vanish?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0;D,6,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="B,D,0.5,0.5,q" forze="C,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m;B,D,2 m" titolo="Overhanging beam with a force in the span and a uniform load on the overhang"}

- $x = \dfrac{34}{11}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 3.09\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = \dfrac{32}{11}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 2.91\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = \dfrac{56}{23}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 2.43\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = \dfrac{21}{8}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 2.63\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The resultant load on the overhang is $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is applied $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, so moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $4\,V_B - 8 \cdot 2 - 6 \cdot 5 = 0$, i.e. $V_B = 11.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = 2.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. Between $C$ and $B$ the forces to the left of the section are $V_A$ and $F$, therefore
$$
M(x) = 2.5\,x - 8\,(x - 2) = 16 - 5.5\,x .
$$
The moment vanishes for $x = \dfrac{16}{5.5} = \dfrac{32}{11} \approx 2.91\,\mathrm{m}$, between $M_C = 5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $M_B = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $\dfrac{34}{11}\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by measuring from $B$, instead of from $C$, the segment $\dfrac{10}{11}\,\mathrm{m}$ that belongs to the value $5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in the proportion between $5$ and $6$.
