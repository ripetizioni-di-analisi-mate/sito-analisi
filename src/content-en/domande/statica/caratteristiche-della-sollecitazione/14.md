The straight $AB$ member shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ on a vertical surface, which gives a horizontal reaction; $B$ is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$. At the midpoint $D$ of the member acts the vertical force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. What is the axial force in segment $AD$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,1.5,2;B,3,4" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" forze="D,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,3 0,3 m,0.7;3 0,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Inclined member with force $F$ at its midpoint"}

- $N = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $N = -10.25\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $N = -9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $N = -2.25\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$; moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $-4\,H_B - 10 \cdot 1.5 = 0$, then $H_B = -3.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ and, for horizontal translation, $H_A = 3.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right. The member forms with the horizontal the angle $\alpha$ with $\cos\alpha = \dfrac{3}{5}$ and $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{4}{5}$. At section $AD$ the part preceding the section contains only reactions in $A$, so
$$
N = -\big( 3.75 \cdot 0.6 + 10 \cdot 0.8 \big) = -10.25\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
that is a compression of $10.25\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained without $H_A$, the value $-9\,\mathrm{kN}$ by exchanging sine and cosine, and the value $-2.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the axial force in the segment $DB$.
