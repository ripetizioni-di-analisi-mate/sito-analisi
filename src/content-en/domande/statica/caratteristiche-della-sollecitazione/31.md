In the Gerber beam shown, cantilever $AC$ is fixed at $A$ and has length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Beam $CB$ has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, connects to the cantilever through internal hinge $C$, and rests on roller $B$. A downward force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $E$, the midpoint of $CB$. What is the bending moment at the fixed end?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;E,3.5,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" forze="E,0,-1.3,F" quote="A,C,2 m;C,E,1.5 m;E,B,1.5 m" titolo="Gerber beam with force $F$ on suspended beam $CB$"}

- $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The beam $CB$ is supported at $C$ and $B$ and carries the force at midspan, then $V_B = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. To the right of the fixed support section, $F$, $3.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and $V_B$, $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ act. Their moment about $A$ is $-6 \cdot 3.5 + 3 \cdot 5 = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, so for the $M = M^{\mathrm{right}}_S$ rule
$$
M = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
with the upper fibers in tension. The same value is obtained from the cantilever $AC$ loaded in $C$ with the force of $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ transmitted by the hinge. The value $-21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained without the reaction $V_B$, the value $-9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ multiplying $F$ by the distance from $C$.
