The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at $A$ and carries the clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in the $C$ midspan, without any other loads. Which description of the bending moment diagram is correct?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" coppie="C,-1,ℳ" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Cantilever with clockwise couple $\mathcal{M}$ at midspan"}

- Constant $M = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on $AC$ with the upper fibers in tension, and $M = 0$ on $CB$
- Constant $M = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on $AC$ with the lower fibers in tension, and $M = 0$ on $CB$
- Constant $M = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ along the entire cantilever
- $M$ varies linearly from $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $A$ to $0$ at $C$, and $M = 0$ on $CB$

## Explanation

The right part of each section is used, which does not contain the fixed support reactions. For a section of the segment $CB$ the right part is unloaded, then $M = 0$ and $T = 0$. For a section of the segment $AC$ the right part contains the couple, which has $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ moment about any reference point; for the $M = M^{\mathrm{right}}_S$ rule you therefore have $M = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, constant, with the upper fibers in tension. The diagram is constant because the couple has no result and the shear remains zero on the whole cantilever. In $C$ the moment jumps by $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, as the moment jump theorem establishes.
