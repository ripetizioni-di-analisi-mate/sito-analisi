The three-hinged frame shown is pinned at its bases $A$ and $B$. Columns $AD$ and $BE$ have height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and beam $DE$ has length $8\,\mathrm{m}$ and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. The beam carries a uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Taking the inner fibers as the reference fibers, what is the bending moment at joint $D$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;C,4,4;E,8,4;B,8,0" aste="A-D;D-E;E-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" carichi="D,E,0.6,0.6,q" tratteggi="0.12,0 0.12,3.88 7.88,3.88 7.88,0" quote="A,D,4 m,-0.8;D,E,8 m,-1.3" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with a uniform load on the crossbeam; dashed lines indicate the reference fibers"}

- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the inner fibers in tension
- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the outer fibers in tension
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the outer fibers in tension
- $0$

## Explanation

By $V_A = V_B = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ symmetry. The left side, from the foot $A$ to the hinge $C$, carries $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ with the resultant $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$, and the zero moment at $C$ gives $-4\,V_A + 4\,H_A + 8 \cdot 2 = 0$; so the thrust equals $H_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the right. In column $AD$, routed from $A$ to $D$, only $H_A$ has arm about the section, equal to its $s$ share, and rule $M = -M^{\mathrm{left}}_S$ gives $M = -4s$. In $D$ the moment is therefore $-16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, i.e. $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ with the outer fibers in tension. The value $0$ is that of a portal frame with a roller support in place of one of the grounded hinges, which pushed zero.
