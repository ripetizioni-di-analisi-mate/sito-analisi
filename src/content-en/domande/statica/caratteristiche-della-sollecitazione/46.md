The beam $AB$ shown, of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ on a horizontal surface. The only load is the horizontal force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, applied at $B$. Which internal forces or moments are nonzero at midspan?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,-1.3,0,F" quote="A,B,5 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with horizontal force $F$ at $B$"}

- Only $N$, which carries $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- Only $N$, equal to $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $N = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- None, because hinge $A$ carries the force

## Explanation

The roller support at $B$ only gives a vertical reaction, so the force $F$ is balanced by the hinge $A$, which exerts $H_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right. Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $V_B = 0$, because $F$ acts along the axis of the beam and has zero moment arm; Therefore also $V_A = 0$. To the left part of any section only $H_A$ acts, directed along the axis, then $T = 0$ and $M = 0$. By the section rules $N = -H_A = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. the beam is **in compression**: the two extremes are pushed towards each other. The force passes from the hinge through the whole beam, so the axial force is different from zero in each section between $A$ and $B$.
