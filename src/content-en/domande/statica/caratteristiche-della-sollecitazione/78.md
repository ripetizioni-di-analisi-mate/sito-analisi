Between two supports a beam’s shear diagram reads $+3\,\mathrm{kN}$ before $C$, $-1\,\mathrm{kN}$ between $C$ and $D$, and $+3\,\mathrm{kN}$ after $D$. The open intervals are unloaded. With downward forces producing downward jumps, which point loads does the diagram imply?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" diagramma="0,0,2,0,3;2,0,4,0,-1;4,0,6,0,3" scala=0.34 numeri=1 titolo="Shear-force diagram, values in kN"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at $C$, $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward at $D$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward at $C$, $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at $D$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at both
- Two $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ couples

## Explanation

A point load is measured by the change in shear across its point of application. The jumps are $-1-3=-4\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$ and $3-(-1)=+4\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $D$. Their signs determine the directions.
