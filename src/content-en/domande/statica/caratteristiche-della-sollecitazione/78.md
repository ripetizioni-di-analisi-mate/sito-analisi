The beam $AB$ shown, of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$. It carries a downward force $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and an equal upward force at $D$, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$. What is the bending moment at midspan?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.3,F;D,0,1.3,F" quote="A,C,2 m;C,D,4 m;D,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with two opposing forces arranged antisymmetrically"}

- $0$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $-8\,V_A + 4 \cdot 6 - 4 \cdot 2 = 0$, so $V_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; vertical equilibrium gives $V_B = -2\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ down. At midspan the left part contains $V_A$, with a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and the force at $C$, with a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$. By the practical rules
$$
M = 2 \cdot 4 - 4 \cdot 2 = 0 .
$$
The load is antisymmetric about the midspan, and so is the diagram of the moment: $M_C = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ with the lower fibers in tension, $M_D = -4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ with the upper fibers in tension, and the zero value at midspan, the centre of antisymmetry.
