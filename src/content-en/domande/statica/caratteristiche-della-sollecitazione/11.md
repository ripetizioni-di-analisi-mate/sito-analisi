In the frame shown, columns $AB$ and $DC$ have height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and beam $BC$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The frame has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $D$. It carries a uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the beam and a horizontal force $P = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right at $B$. Taking the inner fibers as the reference fibers, what is the bending moment at joint $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3;D,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" carichi="B,C,0.4,0.4,q" forze="B,1,0,P" tratteggi="0.12,0 0.12,2.88 3.88,2.88 3.88,0" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.8;B,C,4 m,-1.1" titolo="Portal frame under a uniform load and a horizontal force; dashed lines indicate the reference fibers"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the outer fibers in tension
- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the inner fibers in tension
- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the inner fibers in tension
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the inner fibers in tension

## Explanation

The roller support at $D$ gives a vertical reaction, so the horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A + 4 = 0$ and $H_A = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. In column $AB$, path from $A$ to $B$, the part preceding a section contains only reactions in $A$; $V_A$ acts along the axis, while $H_A$ has arm equal to the $s$ dimension of the section. Therefore $M = 4s$, positive, and in $B$, at the $3\,\mathrm{m}$, $M_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ elevation with the inner fibers in tension. For moment continuity in the hard joint the same value is at the beginning of the crossbeam. The value $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses the length of the crossbeam as its arm instead of the height of the column, and the value $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$ of the crossbeam.
