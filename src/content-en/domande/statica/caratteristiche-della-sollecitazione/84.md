The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at $A$. It brings the downward force $F_1 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ into the midspan $C$ and the upward force $F_2 = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ into the free end $B$. What is the bending moment at section $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="C,0,-1.3,F_1;B,0,0.8,F_2" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Cantilever with two forces acting in opposite directions"}

- $M_C = -4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $M_C = 0$
- $M_C = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $M_C = -8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The right part of section $C$ is used, which does not contain fixed support and only contains $F_2$; $F_1$ force, applied at $C$, has zero moment arm. The force $F_2$ is directed upward and stands $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $C$, so it has $2 \cdot 2 = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise moment. For the $M = M^{\mathrm{right}}_S$ rule we therefore have $M_C = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension. All fixed support instead $M_A = 2 \cdot 4 - 6 \cdot 2 = -4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension. The moment then changes sign along the segment $AC$, where the shear equals $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the diagram is linear.
