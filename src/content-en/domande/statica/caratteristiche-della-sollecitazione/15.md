In a section $S$ of a horizontal beam the left side exerts on the right side a vertical force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ down and a clockwise couple of $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Using the sign convention for a horizontal beam, what are the shear force and bending moment at $S$?

- $T = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $T = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $T = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $T = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The actions described act on the left side of the right side. On this face the positive shear is directed upward and the positive moment is clockwise moment. The force is directed downward, hence $T = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$; the couple is clockwise, then $M = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension. By the principle of action and reaction on the right side of the left side act an upward force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a counterclockwise couple, which with the positive directions of that face give the same values as $T$ and $M$.
