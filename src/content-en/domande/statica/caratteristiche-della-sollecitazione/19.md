The beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and supported at both ends, brings two equal $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ forces down into $C$ and $D$, to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Which statement about segment $CD$ is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.4,F;D,0,-1.4,F" quote="A,C,2 m;C,D,2 m;D,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with two equal forces placed symmetrically"}

- Both shear and moment are zero
- The shear is zero and the moment is constant at $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- The shear is $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the moment increases linearly
- The shear is zero and the moment reaches its maximum $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at midspan

## Explanation

By $V_A = V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ symmetry. In $C$ the shear drops by $F$, from $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to zero, and remains zero until $D$, where it drops to $-6\,\mathrm{kN}$. In the segment $CD$ the shear is zero, so the moment diagram has zero slope and the moment is constant. Its value is $M_C = 6 \cdot 2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension. The value $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment at midspan of a force $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ applied alone at midspan of the beam.
