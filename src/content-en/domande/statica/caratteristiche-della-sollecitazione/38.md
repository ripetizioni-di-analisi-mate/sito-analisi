The three-hinged arch shown is formed by the straight $AC$ and $CB$ members, with the $A$ and $B$ hinge on the ground and the internal hinge $C$ in crown; $AB = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ and $C$ is located $4\,\mathrm{m}$ above the midpoint of $AB$. In $C$ the vertical force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the axial force in member $AC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,4;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" forze="C,0,-1.2,F" tratteggi="3,0 3,4" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9;3 0,C,4 m,0.5" titolo="Three-hinged arch with a force at the crown"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression

## Explanation

Each member is connected to the rest by two hinges and does not carry loads between the two hinges, so it is subject only to two opposite forces directed along the axis; in each of its sections $T = 0$, $M = 0$ and $N$ is constant. The member $AC$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long and forms with the horizontal the angle $\alpha$ with $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{4}{5}$. By symmetry the two members have the same force, and the balance at the vertical translation of the joint $C$ gives
$$
2\,|N| \cdot \frac{4}{5} = 8 ,
$$
then $|N| = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The members support the joint by pushing it up, so they are in compression and $N = -5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the thrust, horizontal component of the force, and the value $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the vertical component of it.
