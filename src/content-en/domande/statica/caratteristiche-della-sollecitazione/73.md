The portal frame shown has the columns $AB$ and $DC$ $3\,\mathrm{m}$ high and the crossbeam $BC$ along $4\,\mathrm{m}$. It is supported by a roller support on a horizontal surface in $A$ and a pin at $D$, and carries the horizontal force $P = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right at joint $B$. In which member is the bending moment zero at every section?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3;D,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,carrello;D,cerniera" forze="B,1.2,0,P" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.8;B,C,4 m,-1" titolo="Portal frame with a roller at $A$, a pin at $D$, and a horizontal force at $B$"}

- In crossbeam $BC$
- In column $DC$
- In neither member
- In column $AB$

## Explanation

The roller support at $A$ only gives the vertical reaction $V_A$, so the entire $P$ force is balanced by the hinge $D$, with $H_D = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$. Moment equilibrium about $D$ gives $-4\,V_A - 4 \cdot 3 = 0$, therefore $V_A = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. In column $AB$ the part preceding the section contains only $V_A$, directed along the axis of the column, then $M = 0$ and $T = 0$ and the column is in tension with $N = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$. The force $P$ is applied to the joint and does not act on any section of the column. In crossbeam, $V_A$ has a horizontal moment arm and the moment reaches $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $C$, with the outer fibers in tension out, then decreases along $DC$ to zero in the hinge $D$.
