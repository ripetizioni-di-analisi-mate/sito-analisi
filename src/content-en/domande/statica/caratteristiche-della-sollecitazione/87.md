The structure shown is formed by the horizontal beam $AB$, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and the vertical column $BC$, of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$, which descends from $B$ and is connected to the beam with a rigid joint. The structure has a pin at $A$ and in $C$ a roller support on a vertical surface, which only exerts a horizontal reaction. In the $D$ midspan of the beam the force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the bending moment in the beam at section $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,3;D,2,3;B,4,3;C,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello,0" forze="D,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,D,2 m;D,B,2 m;B,C,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Beam with a vertical column and a roller on a vertical surface"}

- $0$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension

## Explanation

The rotation around $A$ involves only $F$ and the horizontal reaction $H_C$, which has $3\,\mathrm{m}$ arm because $C$ stands $3\,\mathrm{m}$ below $A$; Therefore $3\,H_C - 6 \cdot 2 = 0$ and $H_C = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right. At section $B$ of the beam, the following part is used, formed by the column, on which only $H_C$ acts. About $B$ the reaction $H_C$ has a moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4 \cdot 3 = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise moment, so for the $M = M^{\mathrm{right}}_S$ rule you have $M_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, positive, with the lower fibers in tension. Check with the left side, which contains $V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $F$, gives the same value, $6 \cdot 4 - 6 \cdot 2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$; the value $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment of the $V_A$ only. With a horizontal plane roller support at $C$ the reaction would be vertical, directed along the axis of the column, and the moment at $B$ would be zero.
