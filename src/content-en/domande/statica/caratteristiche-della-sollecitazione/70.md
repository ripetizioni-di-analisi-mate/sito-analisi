Wind acts on the left column $AB$ of a frame of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and width $4\,\mathrm{m}$ as a uniform horizontal load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to the right. The frame is pinned at $A$ and supported by a roller on a horizontal surface at $D$. Taking the inner fibers as the reference fibers, what is the bending moment at midheight of column $AB$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q,0" tratteggi="0.12,0 0.12,3.88 3.88,3.88 3.88,0" quote="A,B,4 m,-1.3;B,C,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Portal frame with a uniform horizontal load on one column; dashed lines indicate the reference fibers"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the inner fibers in tension
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the inner fibers in tension
- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the inner fibers in tension
- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the inner fibers in tension

## Explanation

The load has resultant $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, so the hinge gives $H_A = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left; $V_A$ acts along the axis of the column and does not count for the moment being. The column part is used under section $S$, at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. About $S$ the reaction $H_A$ has a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise moment, while the segment load, resulting in $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $1\,\mathrm{m}$ under $S$, has $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise moment. For the $M = -M^{\mathrm{left}}_S$ rule you then have $M = 16 - 4 = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, positive, with the inner fibers in tension. The moment grows to $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at joint $B$, so the mid-height value is not half that at the top: the diagram in the column is a parabola.
