In the beam shown, cantilever $AC$ is fixed at $A$ and has length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Beam $CB$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, connects at $C$ through a parallel-link connection with horizontal links, and rests on roller $B$. The connection transmits axial force and bending moment and allows relative vertical movement without transmitting shear. A downward force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $E$, the midpoint of $CB$. What is the bending moment at the fixed end?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;E,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" interni="C,doppio-pendolo,0" forze="E,0,-1.4,F" quote="A,C,2 m;C,E,2 m;E,B,2 m" titolo="Beam with an internal parallel-link connection at $C$"}

- $0$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension

## Explanation

The internal parallel-link connection does not transmit vertical forces, so the vertical translation of the segment $CB$ gives $V_B = F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The moment at $C$ is calculated with the right side, which contains $V_B$, with a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and counterclockwise moment, and $F$, with a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and clockwise moment: $M_C = 6 \cdot 4 - 6 \cdot 2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. In the cantilever $AC$ no loads act and the shear at $C$ is zero, so the shear remains zero and the moment remains constant. The fixed support is then $M_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, positive, with the lower fibers in tension. With an internal pin at $C$ the moment at $C$ would be zero and the cantilever would carry the vertical force instead.
