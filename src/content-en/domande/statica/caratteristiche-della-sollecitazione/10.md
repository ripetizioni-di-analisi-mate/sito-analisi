The Gerber beam shown consists of cantilever $AC$, fixed at $A$ and of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$, and beam $CD$, connected by internal hinge $C$, supported by roller $B$, and extending to overhang $BD$. The dimensions are $CB = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $BD = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. A uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the entire beam. What is the maximum positive bending moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0;D,8,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" carichi="A,D,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,4 m;B,D,2 m" titolo="Gerber beam under a uniform load over its entire length"}

- $2.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The segment $CD$ carries $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, resulting in $1\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$; moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $4\,Y_C = 12 \cdot 1$, so the hinge transmits $Y_C = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$. To the right of $C$ the shear is therefore $T = 3 - 2s$, with $s$ measured by $C$, and vanishes for $s = 1.5\,\mathrm{m}$, i.e. $3.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Since $M_C = 0$, the moment in that section equals
$$
M_{\max} = 3 \cdot 1.5 - \frac{2 \cdot 1.5^2}{2} = 2.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The value $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$ of the span $CB$ without the overhang, the value $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment at midspan of $CB$, where the shear equals $-1\,\mathrm{kN}$.
