The Gerber beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller support at $B$, an internal pin at $C$ and a roller support at $D$, with $AB = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, $BC = 1\,\mathrm{m}$ and $CD = 3\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire length. At which of the points $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$ is the bending moment zero?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,5,0;D,8,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" cerniere="C" carichi="A,D,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,1 m;C,D,3 m" titolo="Gerber beam under a uniform load over its entire length"}

- Only at $A$ and $D$
- At $A$, $C$, and $D$
- At $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$
- Only at $C$

## Explanation

In $A$ and $D$ the beam ends on a hinge and a roller support, which do not exhibit couples, and at those points do not act concentrated couples, so the moment is zero. In the internal hinge $C$ the two segment only exchange forces, therefore $M_C = 0$. In $B$ the moment is different from zero. The segment $CD$, supported at $C$ and $D$, loads the hinge $C$ at $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ down, and with the load of the overhang, resulting in $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$, you get
$$
M_B = -2 \cdot 0.5 - 3 \cdot 1 = -4\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
