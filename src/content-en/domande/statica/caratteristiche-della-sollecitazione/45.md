In a section $S$ of a horizontal beam the actions that the right side exerts on the left side are reduced to only one horizontal force of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, with the line of action $0.1\,\mathrm{m}$ below the centroid of the section. What are the axial force and bending moment at $S$?

- $N = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M = -2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $N = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M = 2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $N = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M = 0$
- $N = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M = 2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The characteristics of the stress are the components of the internal actions reduced to the centroid of the section. The force acts on the right face of the left side, where the positive axial force is directed to the right, then $N = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$, tension, and $T = 0$. Carrying the force in the centroid adds the transport couple, equal to the moment of force about the centroid. A force to the right applied $0.1\,\mathrm{m}$ under the centroid has counterclockwise moment $20 \cdot 0.1 = 2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. On the right face the positive moment is counterclockwise, therefore $M = 2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension, because tension is applied under the centroid.
