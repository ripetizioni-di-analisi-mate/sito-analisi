The beam $CD$ shown rests on a hinge $A$ and $B$ roller support, with $AB = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, and has two equal $CA$ and $BD$ $a$ length overhangs. The uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts along the entire beam. For what overhang length $a$ is the bending moment zero at the midpoint of $AB$?

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,2,0;B,6,0;D,8,0" aste="C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="C,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="C,A,a;A,B,4 m;B,D,a" titolo="Beam with two equal overhangs under a uniform load"}

- $a = 2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $a = 1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $a = 4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $a = 2\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

By symmetry, $V_A = V_B = \dfrac{q\,(4 + 2a)}{2} = q\,(2 + a)$. The free body to the left of midspan carries $V_A$ with moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$. The distributed load on the length $a + 2$ has resultant $q\,(a + 2)$ and moment arm $\dfrac{a + 2}{2}$. The midspan bending moment is therefore
$$
M = 2q\,(a + 2) - \frac{q\,(a + 2)^2}{2} = \frac{q\,(a + 2)(2 - a)}{2} = \frac{q\,(4 - a^2)}{2} .
$$
The moment then vanishes for $a = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, i.e. with overhangs half the span. The result does not depend on the value of $q$: the negative moment given by the overhangs, $\dfrac{q a^2}{2}$, compensates exactly the moment $\dfrac{q \cdot 4^2}{8}$ of the span.
