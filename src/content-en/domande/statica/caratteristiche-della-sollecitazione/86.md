Which of the following beams has constant shear over its entire length?

- A simply supported beam with a concentrated couple at midspan
- A simply supported beam with a concentrated force at midspan
- A cantilever with a uniform load over its entire length
- A simply supported beam with equal point loads at the third points

## Explanation

The shear changes only where transverse forces act: it jumps under concentrated forces and varies linearly under uniform loads. In the simply supported beam with a couple the reactions are two equal and opposite vertical forces, of magnitude $\dfrac{\mathcal{M}}{L}$, and the couple has no result, so it does not produce shear jumps. Therefore the shear equals $\dfrac{\mathcal{M}}{L}$ in each section, and only the moment has a jump at midspan. The force at midspan blows the shear, the uniform load makes it linear, and the two forces in the third produce two jumps with the zero shear at midspan segment.
