Only a vertical column and a horizontal beam meet at a rigid joint with no external point load. The column exerts $7\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right on the joint. What is the magnitude of the beam’s axial force next to the joint?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,-2,-;J,0,0;B,3,0,-" aste="A-J;J-B" forze="J,0,1.1,7 kN;J,1,0,2 kN" titolo="Joint between column and beam"}

- $7\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $\sqrt{53}\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The beam must exert the opposite components to the column forces on the joint. Its axis is horizontal, so its axial component has magnitude $2\,\mathrm{kN}$. The vertical component corresponds to shear.
