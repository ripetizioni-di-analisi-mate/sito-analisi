In the frame shown, columns $AB$ and $DC$ have height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and beam $BC$ has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The frame has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $D$. The beam carries a uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What are the axial forces in column $AB$ and beam $BC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,6,3;D,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" carichi="B,C,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.8;B,C,6 m,-1.2" titolo="Portal frame with a uniform load on the crossbeam"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression at $AB$, and $0$ at $BC$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression at both $AB$ and $BC$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression at $AB$, and $0$ at $BC$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension at $AB$, $0$ at $BC$

## Explanation

The load is vertical and the roller support at $D$ gives a vertical reaction, so the horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A = 0$. The total load equals $2 \cdot 6 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is symmetrical, therefore $V_A = V_D = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. Moving up column $AB$ from its base, only $V_A$ acts on the portion before the section. It acts along the axis toward the section, giving $N = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression. In crossbeam the axial force is the horizontal component of the forces preceding the section; the only horizontal force possible is $H_A$, which is zero, so $N = 0$ in $BC$. The value $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the total load, which is divided between the two columns.
