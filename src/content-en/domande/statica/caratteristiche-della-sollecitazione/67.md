The beam $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$. In the $C$ midspan, an $CE$ vertical member is welded, $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ long, which leans downward and carries the force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at the end $E$. What are the internal forces and moments in member $CE$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0;E,2,-1.5" aste="A-B;C-E" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="E,0,-1,F" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m;C,E,1.5 m,0.5" titolo="Simply supported beam with a member suspended at midspan"}

- $N = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression, $T = 0$, $M = 0$
- $N = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension, $T = 0$, $M = 0$
- $N = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension, $T = 0$, $M = 0$
- $N = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension and $M = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $C$

## Explanation

It is considered a section of the member $CE$ and the part that goes from the section at end $E$, on which only the force $F$ acts. The force $F$ is directed along the axis of the member, so it has orthogonal component zero and zero moment about the section; therefore $T = 0$ and $M = 0$ in each section of the member. The force pulls the bottom segment down, i.e. pulls it away from the section, so the member is **in tension** with $N = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$. The member transmits the force to the beam in $C$, which works as a simply supported beam with the force $F$ at midspan. The value $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the product of $F$ for the length of the member, which is not a moment arm, because the force acts along the axis.
