The beam $AC$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and brings the downward force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ into the free end $C$ of the overhang. What is the bending moment at the midpoint of segment $AB$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.4,F" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,2 m" titolo="Beam loaded at the free end of its overhang"}

- $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $-4\,V_A - 6 \cdot 2 = 0$, then $V_A = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. To the left of $AB$'s midspan only $V_A$ acts, with $2\,\mathrm{m}$ arm, therefore
$$
M = -3 \cdot 2 = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
with the upper fibers in tension. In the segment $AB$ the moment varies linearly from zero in $A$ to $M_B = -6 \cdot 2 = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and the midspan value is half of $M_B$. The value $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment on the $B$ mount, and the value $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained with $V_A$ directed upward.
