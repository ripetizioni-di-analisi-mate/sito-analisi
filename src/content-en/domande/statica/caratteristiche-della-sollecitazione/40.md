The beam $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ and carries at midspan $C$ an inclined force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ components to the right and $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. What is the axial force in segment $AC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0.8,-0.6,F" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with an inclined force at midspan"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $0$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension

## Explanation

The roller support at $B$ only gives a vertical reaction, so the horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A + 4 = 0$ and $H_A = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. At section $AC$ the only horizontal force to the left of the section is $H_A$, therefore $N = -H_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$. The force is tension, because the force at $C$ pulls the beam to the right and the hinge $A$ holds it back. At section $CB$ to the right of the section no horizontal forces act, hence $N = 0$.
