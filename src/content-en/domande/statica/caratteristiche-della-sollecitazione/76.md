The three-hinged frame shown is pinned at its bases $A$ and $B$. Columns $AD$ and $BE$ have height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and beam $DE$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. The only load is a downward force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ applied at joint $D$ above column $AD$. Which statement is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;C,2,4;E,4,4;B,4,0" aste="A-D;D-E;E-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" forze="D,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,D,4 m,-0.8;D,E,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with force $F$ above column $AD$"}

- The horizontal reactions are $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the moment at $D$ is $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- The crossbeam carries a compressive force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the columns each carry $4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- The moment is zero at every section and column $AD$ carries a compressive force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- The moment is zero along the crossbeam and equals $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at the top of $BE$

## Explanation

The right side $CEB$ carries no loads and is connected to the rest only by the $C$ and $B$ hinges, so it is subject to only two forces, opposite and directed along the line $CB$. The reaction at $B$ therefore has the direction of $CB$. The force $F$ acts along the vertical through $A$, so it has zero moment about $A$, and the rotation of the entire portal frame around $A$ reduces at the moment of reaction at $B$. The line $CB$ does not pass through $A$, so the reaction at $B$ is zero and the force $F$ drops all in the hinge $A$, with $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $H_A = 0$. It follows that the moment is zero in each section and that column $AD$ is only in compression, with $N = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$.
