The beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ and carries only one load, applied at section $C$, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The moment diagram is the one drawn: a segment from $0$ in $A$ to $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $C$ and a segment from $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $C$ to $0$ in $B$, with the lower fibers in tension. What load acts at $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,4*s;2,0,6,0,8-2*s" verso=-1 quote="A,C,2 m,0.9;C,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

- A downward force of $2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- A downward force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- A downward force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- A clockwise couple of $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The diagram of $M$ is continuous in $C$, so in $C$ it does not act a couple, which would produce a jump. The slope of the diagram is the shear, therefore $T = \dfrac{8}{2} = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in the segment $AC$ and $T = \dfrac{0 - 8}{4} = -2\,\mathrm{kN}$ in the segment $CB$. For the shear jump theorem, the $-2 - 4 = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$ jump is the vertical component of the force applied at $C$. In $C$, a force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ **downward** then acts. Check with reactions gives $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$, which add up to just $6\,\mathrm{kN}$.
