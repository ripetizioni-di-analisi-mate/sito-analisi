The L-shaped cantilever shown consists of column $AB$ of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at its base $A$ and beam $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$, joined rigidly to the column. The only load is a clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ applied at joint $B$. Taking the fibers on the inside of the L as the reference fibers, which description of the bending moment diagram is correct?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,2,3" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro" coppie="B,-1,ℳ" tratteggi="0.1,0 0.1,2.9 2,2.9" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.7;B,C,2 m,0.6" titolo="L-shaped cantilever with couple $\mathcal{M}$ at the joint; dashed lines indicate the reference fibers"}

- In the column, constant $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ with the inner fibers in tension; in the crossbeam, $M = 0$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is constant in both members, with the outer fibers in tension
- In the column, constant $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ with the outer fibers in tension; in the crossbeam, $M = 0$
- In the column, linear from $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $A$ to $0$ at $B$; in the crossbeam, $M = 0$

## Explanation

You run through the structure from $A$ to $C$ and use the part that follows the section, which does not contain fixed support. For a crossbeam section the following part is unloaded, then $M = 0$ and $T = 0$. For a section of the column the following part contains the couple, the moment of which is $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about any reference point; for rule $M = M^{\mathrm{right}}_S$ the moment therefore applies $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in each section of the column, with the outer fibers in tension. The column has no transverse forces, so the diagram is constant. The transition from $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ to $0$ in the joint is the $-\mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ jump predicted by the rigid joint theorem.
