The beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and supported at both ends, carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over all span and the clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at the end $B$. What is the maximum bending moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" coppie="B,-1,ℳ" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with a uniform load and a clockwise couple at $B$"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $-6\,V_A + 12 \cdot 3 - 6 = 0$, then $V_A = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$. With the left side $T(x) = 5 - 2x$ and $M(x) = 5x - x^2$, because the couple at $B$ is located to the right of each inner section. The shear vanishes in $x = 2.5\,\mathrm{m}$, where the moment has the maximum
$$
M_{\max} = 5 \cdot 2.5 - (2.5)^2 = 6.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The value $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$ without the couple, and the value $12.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained with the couple taken counterclockwise, which gives $V_A = 7\,\mathrm{kN}$.
