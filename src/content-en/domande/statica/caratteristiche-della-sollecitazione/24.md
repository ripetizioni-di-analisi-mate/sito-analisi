The beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and supported at both ends, carries a triangular load that equals zero in $A$ and $q_0 = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in $B$. What is the bending moment at midspan?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0,0.8,q_0" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under a triangular load"}

- $6.75\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $5.625\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The resultant load is $\dfrac{3 \cdot 6}{2} = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is applied $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, so moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $V_A = \dfrac{9 \cdot 2}{6} = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$. To the left of the midspan the load is a base triangle $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $1.5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, with resultant $2.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ applied to one third of the base by the section, i.e. to $1\,\mathrm{m}$. Practical rules give
$$
M = 3 \cdot 3 - 2.25 \cdot 1 = 6.75\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The value $5.625\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by applying the partial result in the middle of the segment, the value $4.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ by applying it to a third of the base on the $A$ side, and the value $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ without the load.
