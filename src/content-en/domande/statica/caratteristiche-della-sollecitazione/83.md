In the frame shown, columns $AB$ and $DC$ have height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and beam $BC$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Beyond joint $C$, the beam continues as overhang $CE$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. The frame has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $D$. A downward force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the overhang tip $E$. What is the bending moment in beam $BC$ immediately to the left of joint $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;E,6,4;D,4,0" aste="A-B;B-E;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="E,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,C,4 m,-0.8;C,E,2 m,-0.8" titolo="Portal frame with a crossbeam overhang loaded at its tip"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension
- $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension
- $0$

## Explanation

The roller support gives a vertical reaction and the load is vertical, so $H_A = 0$. Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $4\,V_D - 6 \cdot 6 = 0$, therefore $V_D = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and, for vertical translation, $V_A = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Immediately to the left of $C$ the part that precedes the section, formed by the column $AB$ and the crossbeam section, contains only $V_A$, which has horizontal arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$ about the section. The force $V_A$ is directed downward and stands to the left of the section, so it has counterclockwise moment $3 \cdot 4 = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and the $M = -M^{\mathrm{left}}_S$ rule gives $M = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension. The overhang has in $C$ the same moment, $-6 \cdot 2 = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, because the column $DC$ is loaded by the $V_D$ alone along its axis and has no moment; at joint $C$ the moment of the crossbeam then passes in full to the overhang. The value $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment of $F$ about $A$.
