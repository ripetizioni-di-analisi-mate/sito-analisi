In the frame shown, columns $AB$ and $DC$ have height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and beam $BC$ has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$. The frame has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $D$. At joint $C$ a horizontal force $P = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is the axial force in column $AB$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4;D,3,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="3.9 4,0.9,0," etichette="3.7,4.35,P" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,C,3 m,-0.9" titolo="Portal frame with horizontal force $P$ at joint $C$"}

- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $0$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension

## Explanation

Roller support at $D$ gives a vertical reaction, so horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$. Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $3\,V_D - 6 \cdot 4 = 0$, then $V_D = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, and the vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Moving up column $AB$ from its base, only the reactions at $A$ act on the portion before an arbitrary section. The section rules therefore give $N = -R^{\mathrm{left}}_y$. Therefore
$$
N = -V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
That is, tension, because the hinge $A$ holds down the column foot. The value $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the shear in the column, $T = -H_A$, and the value $0$ is obtained by neglecting the vertical reaction produced by the horizontal force.
