The self-weight of the $AB$ inclined member shown, $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long, equals $w = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ down for each meter of member length and is the only load. The member has a pin at $A$ and a roller support on a horizontal surface in $B$, which is located $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$. What is the maximum bending moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.4,0.4,w" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.8;4 0,B,3 m,0.8" titolo="Simply supported inclined member under its own weight $w$"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The total weight is $2 \cdot 5 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$; because the loads and reaction of the roller support are vertical, $H_A = 0$ and by $V_A = V_B = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ symmetry upward. The weight, referring to the $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long horizontal projection, equals $\dfrac{10}{4} = 2.5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. In the horizontal $x$ section, the preceding part contains $V_A$, with $x$ arm, and the load $2.5\,x$, with $\dfrac{x}{2}$ arm, therefore $M(x) = 5x - 1.25\,x^2$. The moment is maximum where its derivative vanishes, i.e. in $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, and equals $M = 10 - 5 = 5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained using $w = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ as the load per horizontal projection meter, the value $6.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ using the member length as the span.
