The beam $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ and carries at the end $A$ the $\mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise couple. What is the bending moment immediately to the right of $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="A,1,ℳ" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with couple $\mathcal{M}$ at $A$"}

- $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $-4\,V_A + 8 = 0$, then $V_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and, for vertical translation, $V_B = -2\,\mathrm{kN}$. To the left of a section immediately to the right of $A$ act $V_A$, with one moment arm tending to zero, and the couple $\mathcal{M}$. The couple moment equals $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise about any reference point, therefore $M = -M^{\mathrm{left}}_S = -8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension. Proceeding towards $B$ the moment equals $M(x) = 2x - 8$ and vanishes in $B$; the value $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is that at midspan.
