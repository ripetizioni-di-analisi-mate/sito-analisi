Beam $AB$ of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$ is simply supported at its ends. Uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on segment $AC$, where $AC = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, and downward force $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $D$, a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. What is the maximum bending moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;D,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.6,0.6,q" forze="D,0,-1.6,F" quote="A,C,4 m;C,D,2 m;D,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with a uniform load on $AC$ and force $F$ at $D$"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The resultant of the distributed load is $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and acts a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Taking moments about $B$ gives $-8\,V_A + 8 \cdot 6 + 4 \cdot 2 = 0$ and hence $V_A = 7\,\mathrm{kN}$. On segment $AC$, the shear $T(x) = 7 - 2x$ vanishes at $x = 3.5\,\mathrm{m}$. On segments $CD$ and $DB$ the shear equals $-1\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $-5\,\mathrm{kN}$, respectively, so the bending moment decreases. It therefore reaches its maximum at $x = 3.5\,\mathrm{m}$, where
$$
M_{\max} = 7 \cdot 3.5 - (3.5)^2 = \frac{49}{4} = 12.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The value $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment at $C$, where the distributed load ends. Interchanging the two reactions gives $6.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ by taking $V_A = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$.
