The horizontal $AB$ beam shown, of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin support at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ that moves along a vertical plane. Which statement is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" quote="A,B,5 m,-0.6" titolo="Beam with a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a vertical surface at $B$"}

- The beam can rotate about $A$
- The beam remains stationary
- The beam can translate vertically
- The beam can rotate about $B$

## Explanation

The pin support prevents any displacement of $A$, so, if the beam rotates, its center is $A$. The roller support at $B$ moves along a vertical surface, so $B$ moves vertically and the center lies on the normal plane for $B$, i.e. on the horizontal through $B$. This line coincides with the beam axis and passes through $A$, so point $A$ meets both conditions. The beam **can rotates about $A$**, and in a rotation $\varphi$ the point $B$ has $u_B = 0$ and $v_B = \varphi \cdot 5\,\mathrm{m}$, a shift compatible with roller support.
