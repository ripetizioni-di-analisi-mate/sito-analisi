The horizontal $AB$ beam shown, of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, has at $A$ and $B$ two roller supports that travel on vertical surfaces. Which statement about the displacements of the beam is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,180;B,carrello,0" quote="A,B,5 m,0.6" titolo="Beam with two rollers on vertical surfaces"}

- The beam remains stationary
- The beam has one degree of freedom and rotates about the midpoint of $AB$
- The beam has two degrees of freedom and can translate vertically and rotate about points on its axis
- The beam has one degree of freedom and translates vertically

## Explanation

Each roller support only allows its point vertical shifts, so it imposes $u_A = 0$ and $u_B = 0$. The beam is horizontal, therefore, by the theorem on the displacements of two points along the conjunct, $u_A = u_B$ in each rigid-body displacement, and the two conditions are equivalent to only one. Vertical displacement of $A$ and rotation remain free, so the beam has **two degrees of freedom**. In terms of centers, the normals at the sliding planes for $A$ and $B$ coincide with the beam axis, so the center can be any point of the axis or its point at infinity, which corresponds to the vertical translation.
