The beam $AB$ shown, of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, has at $A$ a parallel-link support with the vertical links and at $B$ a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. What displacements do the two restraints allow the beam to undergo?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo;B,carrello" quote="A,B,5 m,-0.6" titolo="Beam with a vertical parallel-link support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$"}

- None, because the two supports hold the beam stationary
- Horizontal translations
- Rotations about $B$
- Vertical translations

## Explanation

Parallel-link support with vertical links prevents the beam from rotating and moving vertically, so the center is the point at infinity in the vertical direction and the beam can at most move horizontally. The roller support at $B$ allows the point $B$ horizontal shifts, so the center also lies on the vertical line for $B$. This line has the direction of the center at infinity, so the two conditions are compatible. The beam **can move horizontally**, with all points moved the same amount, and the two constraints together are not enough to hold it still.
