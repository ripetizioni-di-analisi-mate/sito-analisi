The vertical pillar $AB$, with $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (0, 6)$ in meters, rotates by $\varphi = 0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$, counterclockwise, around the center $C = (3, 2)\,\mathrm{m}$. At what height is the horizontal displacement of the column zero?

- $y = 2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $y = 3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $y = 0$, at the base of the column
- Nowhere, because the center does not lie on the column

## Explanation

By the theorem on vertical and horizontal displacements of a rigid body, the horizontal displacement of a point depends only on its vertical coordinate:
$$
u = -\varphi\,(y - y_C) = -0.001\,(y - 2\,\mathrm{m}) .
$$
So $u = 0$ for $y = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, i.e. on the horizontal of the center, even if $C$ does not belong to the pillar. The point $(0, 2)\,\mathrm{m}$ has an $v = \varphi\,(0 - 3\,\mathrm{m}) = -3\,\mathrm{mm}$ vertical displacement, however. At the base you have $u_A = 2\,\mathrm{mm}$ and at the top $u_B = -4\,\mathrm{mm}$.
