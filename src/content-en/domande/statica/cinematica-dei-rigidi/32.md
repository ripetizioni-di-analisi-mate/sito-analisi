Two planar rigid bodies are connected by an internal parallel-link connection with the vertical links. Which statement is true?

- $\varphi_1 = \varphi_2$, and $C_{12}$ is the point at infinity in the horizontal direction
- $\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1$, and $C_{12}$ lies in the parallel-link connection
- The rotations are independent and $C_{12}$ lies in the parallel-link connection
- $\varphi_1 = \varphi_2$, and $C_{12}$ is the point at infinity in the vertical direction

## Explanation

Internal parallel-link connection prevents the relative rotation of the two bodies, hence $\varphi_1 = \varphi_2$. The relative displacement of the body $2$ with respect to the body $1$ is therefore a translation, perpendicular to the pin-ended links, that is, horizontal. The center of a translation is the point at infinity in the direction perpendicular to the displacements, so $C_{12}$ is the **Point at infinity in the vertical direction**, which is the direction of the pin-ended links.
