The beam shown is formed by the bodies $1 = AB$ and $2 = BC$, connected by the internal hinge $B$, with two pin supports at $A$ and $C$; The three points are on the same horizontal. Which statement is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,cerniera" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m" titolo="Two-body beam with two pin supports and an internal hinge, all collinear"}

- The beam can move, with $\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1$
- The beam remains stationary because it has two pin supports
- The beam can move, with $\varphi_2 = \varphi_1$
- Only body $1$ can move, rotating about $A$

## Explanation

Hinges give $C_1 = A$, $C_2 = C$ and $C_{12} = B$. The three centers stand on the same line, so the **first theorem of kinematic chains** is also satisfied with non-zero rotations and the beam can move. With the origin at $A$, the relationship between the rotations gives $\varphi_1 \cdot 4\,\mathrm{m} = \varphi_2\,(4 - 8)\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore $\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1$. In this shift the hinge $B$ moves vertically by $v_B = 4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$, while $A$ and $C$ remain stationary.
