The structure in the figure is formed by the rod $AP$ (body $1$), with a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$ and the extreme $P = (3, 3)$, and the rod $DQ$ (body $2$), with a pin support at $D = (8, 0)$ and the extreme $Q = (5, 1)$, in meters. The two rods are connected by the pin-ended link $PQ$, hinged at $P$ and $Q$. Where is the relative center $C_{12}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;P,3,3;Q,5,1;D,8,0" aste="A-P;D-Q" catene="P-Q" cerniere="P;Q" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,cerniera" titolo="Two members on pin supports connected by link $PQ$"}

- $C_{12} = (6, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $C_{12} = (4, 2)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $C_{12} = (3, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$
- At infinity in direction $(1, -1)$

## Explanation

The pin-ended link is an internal two-force link, so $C_{12}$ lies on the line of the pin-ended link, which passes through $P = (3, 3)$ and $Q = (5, 1)$ and has equation $y = 6 - x$. Pin supports give $C_1 = A$ and $C_2 = D$, and for the **first theorem of kinematic chains** $C_{12}$ it also lies on the line $AD$, i.e. $y = 0$. The two lines meet in $C_{12} = (6, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$. Since $C_{12}$ is internal to the segment $C_1 C_2$, the two bodies rotate in opposite directions, and the $\varphi_1 \cdot 6\,\mathrm{m} = \varphi_2\,(6 - 8)\,\mathrm{m}$ relationship gives $\varphi_2 = -3\varphi_1$.
