A flat rigid plate is bound only by a parallel-link support with pin-ended links parallel to the direction $(1, 1)$. Which infinitesimal rigid-body displacements can the plate undergo?

- Translations in direction $(1, 1)$
- Translations in direction $(1, -1)$
- Rotations about the attachment point of the parallel-link support
- Translations in any direction in the plane

## Explanation

Parallel-link support prevents plate rotation and translation in the direction of pin-ended links, i.e. along $(1, 1)$. Because rotation is prevented, the plate can only move, and its center of rotation is a point at infinity. The translation allowed is the one perpendicular to the pin-ended links, so the plate translates in the direction $(1, -1)$. The center is therefore the point at infinity of direction $(1, 1)$, perpendicular to the displacement, and the plate has one degree of freedom.
