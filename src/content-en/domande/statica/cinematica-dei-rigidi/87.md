The four-bar linkage in the figure has the pin supports $A = (0, 0)$ and $D = (6, 0)$ and the internal hinges $B = (4, 3)$ and $C = (2, 3)$, in meters; the $AB$ ($1$ body) and $DC$ ($3$ body) columns cross each other and the crossbeam is $BC$ ($2$ body). What is the displacement of midpoint $M = (3, 3)$ of the crossbeam for $\varphi_1 = 0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,3;C,2,3;D,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B;C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,cerniera" punti="3,3,M" quote="A,D,6 m,0.8" titolo="Four-bar linkage with crossed side members"}

- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- $0.75\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left
- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ horizontally to the left
- $12\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left

## Explanation

Hinges give $C_1 = A$, $C_3 = D$, $C_{12} = B$ and $C_{23} = C$. For the **first theorem of kinematic chains** $C_2$ it lies on line $AB$, $y = \dfrac{3}{4}x$, and line $DC$, $y = -\dfrac{3}{4}(x - 6)$; then $C_2 = (3;\ 2.25)\,\mathrm{m}$, the point where the columns cross. The relationship between $1$ and $2$ bodies gives $\varphi_1\,(4, 3) = \varphi_2\,(1;\ 0.75)$, therefore $\varphi_2 = 4\varphi_1 = 0.004\,\mathrm{rad}$. The point $M$ lies on the vertical line through $C_2$, $0.75\,\mathrm{m}$ higher, then $v_M = 0$ and
$$
u_M = -\varphi_2 \cdot 750\,\mathrm{mm} = -3\,\mathrm{mm} .
$$
The midpoint of the crossbeam therefore moves $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left, horizontally.
