Two planar bodies meet at $C$ in an ideal horizontal guide that permits sliding along the guide. The point of body 1 at $C$ moves by $(3,4)\,\mathrm{mm}$. Which displacement component of body 2 at $C$ is fixed by the bilateral no-separation condition alone?

- The horizontal component, equal to $3\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- The vertical component, equal to $4\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- Both, equal to $(3,4)\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- The vertical component, equal to zero.

## Explanation

Contact requires zero relative displacement normal to the guide. Its normal is vertical, so both points have a vertical displacement of $4\,\mathrm{mm}$. Sliding allows their horizontal components to differ.
