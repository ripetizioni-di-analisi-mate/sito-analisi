Three $1$, $2$ and $3$ rigid bodies are all connected by the same internal hinge at point $K$. Which relative centers coincide with $K$?

- $C_{12}$, $C_{13}$, and $C_{23}$
- Only $C_{12}$ and $C_{23}$
- Only $C_{13}$
- None, because the hinge connects more than two bodies

## Explanation

The point $K$ belongs to all three bodies, and the hinge requires that it have the same displacement in each of them. The relative center of two bodies is the point that has the same displacement in the two bodies, so $K$ is the relative center of each couple. Therefore $C_{12} = C_{13} = C_{23} = K$. The alignment condition of the second kinematic chain theorem is then verified, because three coincident points are aligned.
