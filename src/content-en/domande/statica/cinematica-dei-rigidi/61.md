A planar rigid body is bound only by a roller support at $A$, which moves on a horizontal plane. Where can the centers of rotation lie for infinitesimal rigid-body displacements compatible with this restraint?

- Only at point $A$
- At any point in the plane
- On the horizontal line through $A$
- On the vertical line through $A$, including its point at infinity

## Explanation

The roller support only allows horizontal shifts at point $A$. For the rotation center existence theorem the displacement of $A$ is perpendicular to the segment that connects $A$ with the center, so the center lies on the vertical line by $A$. Each point of this line is allowed: with the center at $A$ the body rotates and $A$ remains stationary, with the center in another point proper to the vertical $A$ moves horizontally, and with the center in the point at infinity in the vertical direction the body moves horizontally. The body therefore has two degrees of freedom, horizontal displacement of $A$ and rotation.
