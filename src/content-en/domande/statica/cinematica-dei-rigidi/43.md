The beam shown consists of body $1 = DB$, pinned at $A$, and body $2 = BE$, joined to the first at internal hinge $B$ and supported at $C$ by a roller on a horizontal surface. At which points is the vertical displacement zero for $\varphi_1 \neq 0$?

::struttura{nodi="D,0,0;A,1,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;E,8,0" aste="D-B;B-E" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="D,A,1 m;A,B,3 m;B,C,2 m;C,E,2 m" titolo="Two-body beam with two overhangs"}

- Only at $A$
- At $A$, $B$, and $C$
- Only at $A$ and $C$
- At $D$ and $E$

## Explanation

Pin support gives $C_1 = A$ and internal hinge gives $C_{12} = B$; center $C_2$ lies on the vertical for roller support and on the line $AB$, then $C_2 = C$. With the origin at $D$, the **first theorem of kinematic chains** gives $\varphi_1 \cdot 3\,\mathrm{m} = \varphi_2 \cdot (-2\,\mathrm{m})$, then $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{3}{2}\varphi_1 \neq 0$. The vertical displacement equals $v = \varphi_1\,(x - 1)$ on the body $1$ and $v = \varphi_2\,(x - 6)$ on the body $2$, so it cancels only on the verticals of the centers, at $A$ and at $C$. The hinge $B$ has $v_B = 3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$, other than zero.
