A flat rigid plate contains the points $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (4, 0)$, in meters. Which pair of restraints prevents all motion of the plate?

- A pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$ on a vertical surface
- Rollers at $A$ and $B$, both on horizontal surfaces
- A pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$ on a horizontal surface
- A roller at $A$ on a horizontal surface and a roller at $B$ on a vertical surface

## Explanation

A roller support requires the center of rotation to be on the normal to the support surface at the bound point, and a pin support dictates that the center is that point. With the hinge $A$ and $B$ roller Support on a horizontal plane the center should be $A$ and stand on the vertical line for $B$; because $A$ does not stand on that line, the **Stay still** plate. With the roller $B$ support on a vertical plane the normal is the horizontal line for $B$, which passes through $A$, so the plate can rotates about $A$. With two roller supports on horizontal planes the normals are vertical and parallel, so the plate moves horizontally. With the roller $A$ support on a horizontal plane and the roller $B$ support on a vertical plane normals meet at $A$, so the plate can rotates about $A$.
