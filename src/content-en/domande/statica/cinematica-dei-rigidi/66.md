The beam shown is formed by the body $1 = AB$, resting at $A = (0, 0)$ on a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane, and the body $2 = BD$, connected to the first by the internal hinge $B = (4, 0)$ and supported by two-force links: at $E = (6, 0)$ a pin-ended vertical link and at $D = (8, 0)$, in meters, a pin-ended link parallel to the direction $(1, 1)$. What is the displacement of $A$ for $\varphi_1 = 0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;E,6,0;D,8,0" aste="A-B;B-D" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,carrello;E,pendolo;D,pendolo,225" quote="A,B,4 m;B,E,2 m;E,D,2 m" titolo="Two-body beam with no pin supports"}

- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left
- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right

## Explanation

Each two-force link allows its point only perpendicular shifts to the pin-ended link, so $C_2$ lies on the line $x = 6$ of the pin-ended link at $E$ and on the line $y = x - 8$ of the pin-ended link at $D$; therefore $C_2 = (6, -2)\,\mathrm{m}$. The internal hinge gives $C_{12} = B$. The roller support at $A$ gives the line $x = 0$ for $C_1$, and for the **first theorem of kinematic chains** $C_1$ it also lies on the line $C_2 B$, of equation $y = 4 - x$; then $C_1 = (0, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$. The point $A$ stands $4\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C_1$, so
$$
u_A = -\varphi_1\,(0 - 4)\,\mathrm{m} = 0.001 \cdot 4000\,\mathrm{mm} = 4\,\mathrm{mm} ,
$$
with $v_A = 0$, as the roller support requires. The point $A$ then moves $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right. As a control, the $\varphi_1\,(4, -4) = \varphi_2\,(-2, 2)$ relationship gives $\varphi_2 = -2\varphi_1$, and the two bodies give $B$ the same $(4, 4)\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$ shift.
