Two planar rigid bodies are connected by an internal two-force link, that is, by a pin-ended link with the hinges at point $P = (2, 0)$ of body $1$ and point $Q = (2, 3)$ of body $2$, in meters. Where is the relative center of rotation $C_{12}$?

- At midpoint $(2;\ 1.5)$ of the link
- At a point on line $x = 2$ along the link
- At a point on line $y = 0$ perpendicular to the link at $P$
- At infinity in the horizontal direction

## Explanation

The pin-ended link is rigid and has the ends hinged to the two bodies, so the distance between $P$ and $Q$ remains constant. In the relative displacement of the body $2$ with respect to the body $1$ the point $P$ is stationary, therefore, by the theorem on displacements along the conjunct, the point $Q$ moves perpendicular to the pin-ended link. The center $C_{12}$ of this relative displacement therefore lies on the line through $Q$ perpendicular to the displacement, i.e. on the **pin-ended link line**, $x = 2$. The position of $C_{12}$ on this line depends on the other constraints of the chain.
