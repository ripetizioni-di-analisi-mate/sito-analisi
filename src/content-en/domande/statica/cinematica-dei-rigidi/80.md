The frame in the figure consists of three bodies: the $AB$ ($1$ body) column, with a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$; the crossbeam $BC$ (body $2$), connected to the column by the internal hinge $B = (0, 4)$ and resting at $E = (3, 4)$ on a roller support that moves along a plane parallel to the direction $(1, 1)$; the $CD$ inclined column (body $3$), connected to the crossbeam by the internal hinge $C = (6, 4)$ and supported at $D = (8, 0)$, in meters, on a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. What is the displacement of $D$ for $\varphi_1 = 0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;E,3,4;C,6,4;D,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B;C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello,315;D,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,E,3 m,-0.7;E,C,3 m,-0.7;A,D,8 m,0.8" titolo="Three-body frame with an inclined roller on its crossbeam"}

- $12\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left
- $16\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- $12\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right

## Explanation

Hinges give $C_1 = A$, $C_{12} = B$ and $C_{23} = C$. The normal on the plane of the roller support at $E$ has the direction $(1, -1)$ and is the line $y = 7 - x$; for the **first theorem of kinematic chains** $C_2$ also lies on the line $AB$, $x = 0$, then $C_2 = (0, 7)\,\mathrm{m}$. The $\varphi_1\,(0, 4) = \varphi_2\,(0, -3)$ report gives $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{4}{3}\varphi_1$. The center $C_3$ lies on the vertical through $D$, $x = 8$, and on the line $C_2 C$, of equation $y = 7 - \dfrac{x}{2}$, therefore $C_3 = (8, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$. The $\varphi_2\,(6, -3) = \varphi_3\,(-2, 1)$ report gives $\varphi_3 = -3\varphi_2 = 4\varphi_1$. The point $D$ stands $3\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C_3$, so
$$
u_D = -\varphi_3\,(0 - 3)\,\mathrm{m} = 0.004 \cdot 3000\,\mathrm{mm} = 12\,\mathrm{mm} ,
$$
and $D$ moves $12\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right.
