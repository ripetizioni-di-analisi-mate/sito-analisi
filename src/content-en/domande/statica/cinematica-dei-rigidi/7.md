The arch shown has two members $AC$ and $CB$ joined by internal hinge $C = (4, 4)$. There is a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$ and a roller at $B = (10, 0)$ on a surface inclined at $45^\circ$, parallel to direction $(1, 1)$. What is the displacement of $B$ for $\varphi_1 = 0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,4;B,10,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,315" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.8;4 0,B,6 m,0.8" titolo="Two-member arch with a roller on an inclined surface at $B$"}

- $\vec{s}_B = (20, 20)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\vec{s}_B = (5, 5)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\vec{s}_B = (-5, -5)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\vec{s}_B = (-20, -20)\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The pin support gives $C_1 = A$ and the internal hinge gives $C_{12} = C$. The roller $B$ support runs in the direction $(1, 1)$, so $C_2$ lies on the normal plane for $B$, the line $y = 10 - x$; for the **first theorem of kinematic chains** it also lies on the line $AC$, $y = x$, so $C_2 = (5, 5)\,\mathrm{m}$. $\varphi_1\,(4, 4) = \varphi_2\,(4 - 5,\ 4 - 5)$ is obtained from $\varphi_2 = -4\varphi_1 = -0.004\,\mathrm{rad}$. The displacement of $B$ has $u_B = -\varphi_2\,(0 - 5)\,\mathrm{m} = -20\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $v_B = \varphi_2\,(10 - 5)\,\mathrm{m} = -20\,\mathrm{mm}$ components. The point $B$ then descends down the support surface to the left.
