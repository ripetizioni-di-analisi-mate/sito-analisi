The portal frame in the figure is formed by the body $1 = ABC$, with a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$, and the column $CD$ (body $2$), connected to the body $1$ by the internal hinge $C = (6, 4)$ and resting at $D = (6, 0)$ on a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. If column $CD$ rotates through $\varphi_2 = 0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$, what is the rotation $\varphi_1$ of body $1$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,6,4;D,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,C,6 m,-0.7" titolo="Portal frame with an internal hinge at the top of column $CD$"}

- $\varphi_1 = 0$
- $\varphi_1 = -0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\varphi_1 = 0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\varphi_1 = -0.003\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The pin support gives $C_1 = A$ and the internal hinge gives $C_{12} = C$. The center $C_2$ lies on the vertical for the roller support, $x = 6$, and, for the **first theorem of kinematic chains**, on the line $AC$, of equation $y = \dfrac{2}{3}x$; then $C_2 = (6, 4) = C$. In the $\varphi_1\,\overrightarrow{C_1 C_{12}} = \varphi_2\,\overrightarrow{C_2 C_{12}}$ relationship the second member is zero, because $C_2 = C_{12}$, therefore $\varphi_1 = 0$ whatever $\varphi_2$ is. The body $1$, which has fixed $A$, then remains stationary, and the column $CD$ rotates about the hinge $C$. The roller at $D$, which lies on the vertical through $C$, moves horizontally by $u_D = -\varphi_2\,(0 - 4)\,\mathrm{m} = 8\,\mathrm{mm}$, as the constraint allows.
