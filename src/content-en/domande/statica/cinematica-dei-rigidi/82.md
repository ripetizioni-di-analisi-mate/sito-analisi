The structure in the figure is formed by the body $1 = ABC$, L-shaped, resting at $A = (0, 0)$ on a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane, and by the beam $CD$ (body $2$), connected to the body $1$ by the internal hinge $C = (3, 3)$ and with a pin support at $D = (7, 3)$; The L vertex is $B = (0, 3)$. What is the displacement of $A$ if beam $CD$ rotates clockwise through $\varphi_2 = -0.003\,\mathrm{rad}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,3,3;D,7,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,carrello;D,cerniera" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.8;B,C,3 m,-0.7;C,D,4 m,-0.7" titolo="L-shaped body with a roller at $A$, connected to beam $CD$"}

- $12\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left
- $12\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- $9\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- $16\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right

## Explanation

The pin support gives $C_2 = D$ and the internal hinge gives $C_{12} = C$. The center $C_1$ lies on the vertical for the roller support, $x = 0$, and, for the **first theorem of kinematic chains**, on the line $DC$, $y = 3$; then $C_1 = (0, 3) = B$. The relationship between the rotations gives $\varphi_1\,(3, 0) = \varphi_2\,(3 - 7,\ 0) = \varphi_2\,(-4, 0)$, therefore $\varphi_1 = -\dfrac{4}{3}\varphi_2 = 0.004\,\mathrm{rad}$. The point $A$ stands $3\,\mathrm{m}$ below the center $B$, so
$$
u_A = -\varphi_1\,(0 - 3)\,\mathrm{m} = 0.004 \cdot 3000\,\mathrm{mm} = 12\,\mathrm{mm} ,
$$
with $v_A = 0$. The point $A$ moves $12\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right, along the plane of the roller support.
