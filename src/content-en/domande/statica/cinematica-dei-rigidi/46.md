Three rigid flat rods, lacking external supports, are connected to two to two by three internal hinges and form the triangle in the figure. How many degrees of freedom does the system have?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,2,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-A" cerniere="A;B;C" titolo="Triangle of three pin-connected members with no external supports"}

- $3$
- $6$
- $0$
- $9$

## Explanation

Three unconnected planar rigid bodies have $3 \cdot 3 = 9$ degrees of freedom. Each internal hinge connects two rods and requires that its point have the same displacement as the point of the two rods, i.e. two conditions. The three hinges then impose $3 \cdot 2 = 6$ conditions, and the system has $9 - 6 = 3$ degrees of freedom. The result corresponds to the fact that the lengths of the three sides fix the shape of the triangle, so the three rods move like a single flat free rigid body.
