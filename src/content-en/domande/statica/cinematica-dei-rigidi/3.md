Member $AB$ of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ has a roller at $A = (3, 0)$ on a horizontal floor and another at $B = (0, 4)$ against a vertical wall. Point $B$ moves down by $6\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the displacement of $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,3,0;B,0,4" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello,180" quote="0 0,A,3 m,0.6;0 0,B,4 m,-0.6" titolo="Member with one roller on the floor and another against the wall"}

- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- $4.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right

## Explanation

The roller support at $A$ allows horizontal shifts, so the center lies on the vertical through $A$, $x = 3$. The roller support at $B$ allows vertical shifts, so the center also lies on the horizontal through $B$, $y = 4$. The center of rotation is therefore $C = (3, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$. From $v_B = \varphi\,(x_B - x_C) = -3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi = -6\,\mathrm{mm}$ we get $\varphi = 0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$, in counterclockwise. Then $u_A = -\varphi\,(y_A - y_C) = 0.002 \cdot 4000\,\mathrm{mm} = 8\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $v_A = 0$, then $A$ moves $8\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right.
