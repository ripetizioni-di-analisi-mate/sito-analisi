The beam shown consists of body $1 = AB$, pinned at $A$, and body $2 = BD$, joined to the first at internal hinge $B$ and supported at $C$ by a roller on a horizontal surface. What is the vertical displacement of end $D$ in terms of $\varphi_1$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,9,0;D,11,0" aste="A-B;B-D" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m;B,C,3 m;C,D,2 m" titolo="Two-body beam with an overhang"}

- $v_D = 4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$
- $v_D = -2\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$
- $v_D = -4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$
- $v_D = -1\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$

## Explanation

Place the origin at $A$. The pin support gives $C_1 = A$ and the internal hinge gives $C_{12} = B$. The center $C_2$ lies on the vertical for the roller support and, for the **first theorem of kinematic chains**, on the line $AB$, then $C_2 = C$. The relationship between the rotations gives $\varphi_1 \cdot 6\,\mathrm{m} = \varphi_2\,(6 - 9)\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore $\varphi_2 = -2\varphi_1$. The $CD$ overhang is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ long, so $v_D = \varphi_2 \cdot 2\,\mathrm{m} = -4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$. For $\varphi_1 > 0$, the hinge $B$ rises and the $D$ extreme falls.
