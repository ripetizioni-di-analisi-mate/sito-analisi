The rectangular plate in the figure has the vertices $A = (0, 0)$, $D = (3, 0)$, $B = (3, 4)$ and $E = (0, 4)$, in meters. At $A$ it is supported on a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane, at $B$ it is supported by a two-force link whose pin-ended link has the direction of the diagonal $AB$. The $\varphi = 0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$ wheel plate counterclockwise. What is the displacement of point $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,3,0;B,3,4;E,0,4" aste="A-D;D-B;B-E;E-A" vincoli="A,carrello;B,pendolo,53.13" tratteggi="0,0 3,4" quote="A,D,3 m,0.8;E,A,4 m,0.8" titolo="Plate with a roller at $A$ and a link at $B$ parallel to the diagonal"}

- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left
- It remains stationary
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right

## Explanation

The roller support only allows $A$ horizontal shifts, so the center lies on the vertical line for $A$, $x = 0$. The two-force link only allows $B$ to move perpendicular to the pin-ended link, so the center lies on the line of the pin-ended link, i.e. on the line $AB$ of equation $y = \dfrac{4}{3}x$. The two lines meet at $A$, so the center of rotation is $C = A$ and the point $A$ **Stay still**, although the roller support would allow it to scroll. The point $B$ moves by
$$
\vec{s}_B = \varphi\,\hat{k} \times \overrightarrow{AB} = 0.002 \cdot (-4000,\ 3000)\,\mathrm{mm} = (-8, 6)\,\mathrm{mm} ,
$$
perpendicular to the pin-ended link, as the two-force link allows.
