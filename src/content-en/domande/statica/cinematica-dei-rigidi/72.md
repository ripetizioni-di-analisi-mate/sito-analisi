The structure in the figure is formed by the $AO$, $BO$ and $OD$ rods, all connected by the same hinge at $O = (4, 3)$; $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (8, 0)$ are pin supports, and at $D = (8, 3)$, in meters, member $OD$ is constrained by a roller support that moves along a vertical plane. Which statement is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0;O,4,3;D,8,3" aste="A-O;B-O;O-D" cerniere="O" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera;D,carrello,0" quote="A,B,8 m,0.8;O,D,4 m,-0.7" titolo="Three members connected by hinge $O$"}

- The structure remains stationary
- All three members rotate and point $O$ moves vertically
- Members $AO$ and $BO$ remain stationary and member $OD$ rotates about $O$
- Members $AO$ and $BO$ remain stationary and member $OD$ translates vertically

## Explanation

The $AO$ and $BO$ rods have the $C_1 = A$ and $C_2 = B$ centers and the relative $C_{12} = O$ center. The three points are not aligned, therefore, for the first theorem of kinematic chains, $\varphi_1 = \varphi_2 = 0$, and the two rods, which have a fixed point, remain stationary. Therefore the point $O$ is stationary, and member $OD$ can only rotates about $O$. The roller $D$ support requires the center of member $OD$ to be on the normal to the support surface, the horizontal line $y = 3$, which passes through $O$. The constraint is therefore compatible with rotation about $O$, in which $D$ moves vertically, and the $OD$ **rotates about $O$** rod. A translation of member $OD$ would also move $O$, which is stationary.
