The horizontal beam shown consists of body $1 = AB$, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and pinned to the ground at $A$, and body $2 = BC$, of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and supported at $C$ by a roller on a horizontal surface. The bodies meet at $B$ through an internal parallel-link connection with horizontal links. What is the jump $v_B^{(2)} - v_B^{(1)}$ in vertical displacement at $B$, expressed in terms of $\varphi_1$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C" interni="B,doppio-pendolo,0" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="A,B,3 m;B,C,5 m" titolo="Two-body beam joined by an internal parallel-link connection with horizontal links"}

- $8\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$
- $0$
- $-8\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$

## Explanation

The internal pair of parallel links prevents relative rotation, so $\varphi_2 = \varphi_1$ and the relative center $C_{12}$ is the point at infinity in the horizontal direction. Center $C_2$ lies on the vertical through the roller. By the **three-center theorem**, it must also lie on the horizontal through $C_1 = A$. Their intersection gives $C_2 = C$. Taking $A$ as the origin gives $v_B^{(1)} = \varphi_1 \cdot 3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $v_B^{(2)} = \varphi_2\,(3 - 8)\,\mathrm{m} = -5\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$. The jump is therefore $-5\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 - 3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 = -8\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$. Because the internal links allow the two member ends to slide vertically relative to each other, the vertical displacement diagram has a jump at $B$.
