The three flat rigid rods in the figure, without external supports, have one extreme in common at point $O$, where they are all connected by the same hinge. How many degrees of freedom does the system have?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,-3,1;B,3,1;C,0,-3" aste="O-A;O-B;O-C" cerniere="O" titolo="Three members sharing a hinge at $O$, with no external supports"}

- $7$
- $5$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

The three unrelated members have $3 \cdot 3 = 9$ degrees of freedom. The hinge dictates that $O$ has the same displacement as the point of the first and second rods and as the point of the first and third, so the conditions are $2 \cdot 2 = 4$: a hinge connecting $k$ bodies equals simple $k - 1$ hinges. Therefore the system has $9 - 4 = 5$ degrees of freedom. The displacements are in fact identified by the two components of $O$ displacement and the three rotations $\varphi_1$, $\varphi_2$, $\varphi_3$, which are independent.
