The frame in the figure is formed by the $AB$ ($1$ body), with a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$, and the crossbeam $BD$ ($2$ body), connected to the column by the internal hinge $B = (0, 4)$ and resting at $D = (4, 4)$ on a roller support that moves along a plane parallel to the direction $(1, -1)$. Which statement is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;D,4,4" aste="A-B;B-D" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello,225" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,D,4 m,-0.7" titolo="Frame with a roller on a surface inclined at $45^\circ$ at point $D$"}

- The frame remains stationary
- Both bodies rotate with $\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1$
- The crossbeam translates parallel to the roller's support surface
- The two bodies rotate together about $A$, with $\varphi_2 = \varphi_1$

## Explanation

The pin support gives $C_1 = A$ and the internal hinge gives $C_{12} = B$. The normal to the support surface for $D$ has the direction $(1, 1)$, so it is the line $y = x$, and $C_2$ lies on this line; for the **first theorem of kinematic chains** also lies on the $AB$, $x = 0$ line. The two lines meet at $A$, therefore $C_2 = A = C_1$. The relationship between the rotations becomes $\varphi_1\,\overrightarrow{AB} = \varphi_2\,\overrightarrow{AB}$, then $\varphi_2 = \varphi_1$, and the two bodies rotate together around $A$ as a single rigid body. The displacement of $D$ is perpendicular to $\overrightarrow{AD} = (4, 4)$, i.e. parallel to $(1, -1)$, and is allowed by roller support.
