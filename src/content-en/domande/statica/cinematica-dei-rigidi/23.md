A planar rigid body rotates $\varphi = -0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$, clockwise, around the center $C = (2, 1)\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the displacement of point $P = (5, -3)\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $\vec{s}_P = (8, 6)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\vec{s}_P = (-6, -8)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\vec{s}_P = (8, -6)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\vec{s}_P = (-8, -6)\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

For the rotation center existence theorem the displacement of $P$ is $u_P = -\varphi\,(y_P - y_C)$, $v_P = \varphi\,(x_P - x_C)$, with $x_P - x_C = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $y_P - y_C = -4\,\mathrm{m}$. So
$$
u_P = 0.002 \cdot (-4000)\,\mathrm{mm} = -8\,\mathrm{mm} ,
\qquad
v_P = -0.002 \cdot 3000\,\mathrm{mm} = -6\,\mathrm{mm} .
$$
As a control, the displacement has magnitude $10\,\mathrm{mm} = 0.002 \cdot 5000\,\mathrm{mm} = |\varphi|\,\overline{CP}$ and is perpendicular to $\overrightarrow{CP} = (3, -4)$, because $(-8) \cdot 3 + (-6) \cdot (-4) = 0$.
