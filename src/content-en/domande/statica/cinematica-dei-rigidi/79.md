The beam shown is formed by the body $1 = AB$, which at $A$ has a parallel-link support with the horizontal links, and the body $2 = BE$, connected to the first by the internal hinge $B$ and resting at $D$ on a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane; $E$ is the extreme of overhang. The body $1$ moves upwards of $8\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the displacement of $E$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;D,7,0;E,9,0" aste="A-B;B-E" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,180;D,carrello" quote="A,B,3 m;B,D,4 m;D,E,2 m" titolo="Two-body beam with horizontal parallel links at $A$"}

- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ upward
- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward
- $16\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward

## Explanation

Parallel-link support with the horizontal links prevents the body $1$ from rotating and moving horizontally, so $C_1$ is the point at infinity in the horizontal direction and the body $1$ moves vertically. The internal hinge gives $C_{12} = B$. The center $C_2$ lies on the vertical for the roller support and, for the **first theorem of kinematic chains**, on the line through $B$ with the horizontal direction of $C_1$, i.e. on the axis; then $C_2 = D$. With the origin at $A$, the displacement of $B$ as the point of the body $2$ is $v_B = \varphi_2\,(3 - 7)\,\mathrm{m} = 8\,\mathrm{mm}$, therefore $\varphi_2 = -0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$. Then $v_E = \varphi_2\,(9 - 7)\,\mathrm{m} = -4\,\mathrm{mm}$, i.e. $E$ drops by $4\,\mathrm{mm}$.
