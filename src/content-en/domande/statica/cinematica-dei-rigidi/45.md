A chain is formed by three rigid bodies bound to the ground. How many centers of rotation does the kinematic chain have, counting both absolute and relative centers?

- $6$
- $3$
- $9$
- $4$

## Explanation

Each body has an absolute center, i.e. the center of its rotation about the ground, so the absolute centers are $C_1$, $C_2$ and $C_3$. Each pair of bodies has a relative center, and the distinct pairs of three bodies are three, so the relative centers are $C_{12}$, $C_{13}$ and $C_{23}$. Therefore the centers are in all $6$. Considering the soil as a fourth body, the number coincides with the distinct couples between four bodies, $\dfrac{4 \cdot 3}{2} = 6$.
