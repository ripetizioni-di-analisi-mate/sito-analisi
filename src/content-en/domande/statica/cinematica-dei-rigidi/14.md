The beam shown consists of body $1 = AB$, pinned at $A$, and body $2 = BC$, joined to the first at internal hinge $B$ and supported at $C$ by a roller on a horizontal surface. At point $D$, the midpoint of $BC$, force $F$ acts in the direction and sense specified by unit vector $\left( \dfrac{3}{5},\ -\dfrac{4}{5} \right)$. For $\varphi_1 = 0.005\,\mathrm{rad}$, what is the component of the displacement of $D$ in the direction of $F$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;D,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" forze="D,0.9,-1.2,F" quote="A,B,4 m;B,D,2 m;D,C,2 m" titolo="Two-body beam under an inclined force at $D$"}

- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Pin support gives $C_1 = A$ and internal hinge gives $C_{12} = B$; center $C_2$ lies on the vertical for roller support and on the line $AB$, then $C_2 = C$. With the origin at $A$, the relationship between the rotations gives $\varphi_1 \cdot 4\,\mathrm{m} = \varphi_2\,(4 - 8)\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore $\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1 = -0.005\,\mathrm{rad}$. The point $D = (6, 0)$ has $u_D = 0$ and $v_D = \varphi_2\,(6 - 8)\,\mathrm{m} = 10\,\mathrm{mm}$, i.e. moves up by $10\,\mathrm{mm}$. The component in the direction of $F$ is the dot product of $\vec{s}_D$ with the unit vector, $10\,\mathrm{mm} \cdot \left( -\dfrac{4}{5} \right) = -8\,\mathrm{mm}$. The minus sign indicates that $D$ moves $8\,\mathrm{mm}$ in the opposite direction to the force.
