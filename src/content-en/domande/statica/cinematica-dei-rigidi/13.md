The frame shown consists of body $1 = ABD$, pinned to the ground at $A = (0, 0)$, and body $2 = DEF$, joined to the first at internal hinge $D = (4, 4)$ and restrained at $F = (6, 2)$ by a roller on a vertical surface. What is the vertical displacement of $F$ in terms of $\varphi_1$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;D,4,4;E,6,4;F,6,2" aste="A-B;B-D;D-E;E-F" cerniere="D" vincoli="A,cerniera;F,carrello,0" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,D,4 m,-0.7;D,E,2 m,-0.7;E,F,2 m,-0.8" titolo="Frame with a roller against a vertical surface at $F$"}

- $v_F = 8\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$
- $v_F = -8\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$
- $v_F = 4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$
- $v_F = 2\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$

## Explanation

The pin support gives $C_1 = A$ and the internal hinge gives $C_{12} = D$. The roller $F$ support moves along a vertical surface, so $F$ moves vertically and $C_2$ lies on the horizontal through $F$, $y = 2$; for the **first theorem of kinematic chains** also lies on the $AD$, $y = x$ line. Therefore $C_2 = (2, 2)\,\mathrm{m}$. From $\varphi_1\,(4, 4) = \varphi_2\,(4 - 2,\ 4 - 2)$ you get $\varphi_2 = 2\varphi_1$, with the same direction because $C_{12}$ is external to the segment $C_1 C_2$. Then $v_F = \varphi_2\,(x_F - x_{C_2}) = 2\varphi_1 \cdot 4\,\mathrm{m} = 8\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$.
