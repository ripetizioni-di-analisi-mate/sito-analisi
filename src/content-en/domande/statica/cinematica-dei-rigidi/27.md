In a chain of three rigid bodies the relative centers are $C_{12} = (0, 2)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $C_{23} = (4, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$. For another constraint the relative center $C_{13}$ lies on the line $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. Where is $C_{13}$?

- $C_{13} = (2, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $C_{13} = (2, 2)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $C_{13} = (2, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $C_{13} = (2, 6)\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For the **Second theorem of kinematic chains**, the relative centers $C_{12}$, $C_{23}$ and $C_{13}$ are aligned. The line through $C_{12} = (0, 2)$ and $C_{23} = (4, 4)$ has slope $\dfrac{4 - 2}{4 - 0} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, so it has equation $y = 2 + \dfrac{x}{2}$. Since $C_{13}$ also lies on line $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, its vertical coordinate is $y = 2 + 1 = 3\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore $C_{13} = (2, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$.
