The beam shown consists of body $1 = DB$, pinned at $A$, and body $2 = BE$, joined to the first at internal hinge $B$ and supported at $C$ by a roller on a horizontal surface. What is the vertical displacement of end $E$ in terms of $\varphi_1$?

::struttura{nodi="D,0,0;A,2,0;B,5,0;C,8,0;E,10,0" aste="D-B;B-E" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="D,A,2 m;A,B,3 m;B,C,3 m;C,E,2 m" titolo="Two-body beam with two overhangs"}

- $v_E = -2\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$
- $v_E = 2\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$
- $v_E = -5\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$
- $v_E = -8\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$

## Explanation

With the origin at $D$, the pin support gives $C_1 = A = (2, 0)$ and the internal hinge gives $C_{12} = B = (5, 0)$. The center $C_2$ lies on the vertical for the roller support and on the line $AB$, then $C_2 = C = (8, 0)$. From the **first theorem of kinematic chains** you have $\varphi_1\,(5 - 2)\,\mathrm{m} = \varphi_2\,(5 - 8)\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore $\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1$. On the body $2$ the vertical displacement is $v = \varphi_2\,(x - 8)$, so at $E = (10, 0)$ it is $v_E = -\varphi_1 \cdot 2\,\mathrm{m}$. The arm is the distance of $E$ from the center $C_2$, i.e. $2\,\mathrm{m}$.
