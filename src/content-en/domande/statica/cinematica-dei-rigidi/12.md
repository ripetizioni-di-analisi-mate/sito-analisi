In the beam shown the bodies $DB$ and $BE$ are joined by the internal hinge $B$; the first rotates about the fixed pin $A$, the second rests at $C$ on a roller sliding on a horizontal plane. A diagram of vertical displacements, positive upwards, assigns to the ends the values $v_D = 2\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $v_E = -2\,\mathrm{mm}$. Which statement about this diagram is true?

::struttura{nodi="D,0,0;A,2,0;B,5,0;C,8,0;E,10,0" aste="D-B;B-E" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="D,A,2 m;A,B,3 m;B,C,3 m;C,E,2 m" titolo="Two-body beam with two overhangs"}

- It is not compatible, because it gives $B$ two different vertical displacements
- It is compatible, because the two bodies rotate by the same angle
- It is compatible, with the hinge $B$ fixed
- It is compatible, with the hinge $B$ moving $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ down

## Explanation

The body $DB$ rotates about $A$, so its diagram is linear and zero at $A$. Since $D$ and $B$ lie on opposite sides of $A$, at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $3\,\mathrm{m}$, the value $v_D = 2\,\mathrm{mm}$ gives $v_B = -\dfrac{3}{2} \cdot 2 = -3\,\mathrm{mm}$. The body $BE$ rotates about $C$, because its centre lies on the line $AB$ and on the vertical through the roller; since $B$ and $E$ lie on opposite sides of $C$, at $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $2\,\mathrm{m}$, the value $v_E = -2\,\mathrm{mm}$ gives $v_B = 3\,\mathrm{mm}$. The internal hinge requires $B$ to have the same displacement as a point of both bodies, therefore the diagram is **not compatible**. The two bodies rotate by the same angle, $-0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$, whereas the hinge requires $\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1$, that is $v_E = v_D$.
