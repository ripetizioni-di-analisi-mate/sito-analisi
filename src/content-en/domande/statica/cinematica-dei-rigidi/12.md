In the beam hinged at $B$, segments $AB$ and $BC$ are each $3\,\mathrm{m}$ long, and overhangs $DA$ and $CE$ are each $2\,\mathrm{m}$ long. Point $A$ is fixed and $C$ can slide horizontally. During an infinitesimal motion, two sensors read $v_D=+2\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $v_E=-2\,\mathrm{mm}$. What does the displacement diagram tell us?

::struttura{nodi="D,0,0;A,2,0;B,5,0;C,8,0;E,10,0" aste="D-B;B-E" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="D,A,2 m;A,B,3 m;B,C,3 m;C,E,2 m" titolo="Two-body beam with two overhangs"}

- The readings violate vertical continuity at $B$.
- The readings describe two compatible rotations.
- The reading at $D$ must always be zero.
- The reading at $E$ alone proves that $C$ lifts.

## Explanation

On the first member the diagram is zero at $A$, so the reading at $D$ gives $v_B=-3\,\mathrm{mm}$. On the second it is zero at $C$, and the reading at $E$ gives $v_B=+3\,\mathrm{mm}$. An internal hinge permits different rotations but has a single vertical displacement.
