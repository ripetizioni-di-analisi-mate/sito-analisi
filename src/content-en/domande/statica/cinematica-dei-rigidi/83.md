The horizontal beam in the figure is formed by the body $1 = AB$, with a pin support at $A$, and the body $2 = BC$, connected to the first by the internal hinge $B$; at $C$ there is a roller support that moves along a vertical plane. How many degrees of freedom does the beam have?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,7,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello,0" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,3 m" titolo="Two-body beam with a roller on a vertical surface at $C$"}

- $1$
- $0$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The body $1$ rotates about $A$ and lies on the horizontal axis, so its points have $u = 0$, and in particular $u_B = 0$. The body $2$ is on the same horizontal as $B$, therefore, by the theorem about the displacements of two points along the conjunct, all its points also have $u = 0$. Roller support imposes $u_C = 0$, a condition already met, so it does not take away degrees of freedom. $\varphi_1$ and $\varphi_2$ remain free, and the beam has **two degrees of freedom**. The $3 \cdot 2 - 2 - 2 - 1 = 1$ count gives a lower value because the condition imposed by the roller support depends on those of the hinges: the normal to the support surface for $C$ coincides with the line $AB$ of the $C_1$ and $C_{12}$ centers.
