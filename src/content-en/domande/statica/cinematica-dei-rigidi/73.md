The beam shown is formed by the body $1 = AB$, with a pin support at $A$, and the body $2 = BD$, connected to the first by the internal hinge $B$ and resting at $C$ on a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane; $D$ is the extreme of overhang. Which scalar restraint at $D$ would prevent all motion of the beam?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,8,0" aste="A-B;B-D" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,2 m;C,D,2 m" titolo="Two-body beam with overhang $CD$"}

- A roller on a horizontal surface
- A roller on a vertical surface
- A two-force link with a horizontal axis
- No single scalar restraint suffices. A pin support is required

## Explanation

Without the constraint at $D$ the beam is a one-degree chain of freedom with $C_1 = A$, $C_{12} = B$ and $C_2 = C$, because the center of the body $2$ lies on the vertical for the roller support and on the line $AB$. An $D$ constraint requires the center of the body $2$ to stand on a line for $D$: the normal to the support surface for a roller support, the pin-ended link line for a two-force link. The vertical-level roller support and the two-force link to horizontal links give the beam axis, which passes through $C$, so they allow the body $2$ to rotates about $C$. The **roller support on a horizontal plane** gives the vertical through $D$, which does not pass through $C$; therefore the body $2$ has no eligible centers and remains stationary. Then $B$ is stationary, and even the body $1$, which has $A$ and $B$ stops, remains stationary.
