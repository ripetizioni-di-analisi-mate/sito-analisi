Two links of a four-bar linkage are pinned to the ground. At the instant considered, their centre lines meet at a finite point $K$ outside the connecting coupler. What happens to the coupler during a nonzero infinitesimal motion?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1,2;C,3,2;D,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B;C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,cerniera" tratteggi="1,2 2,4;3,2 2,4" punti="2,4,K,centro" titolo="Extensions of the links pinned to ground"}

- It translates because the distance between its ends is fixed.
- It instantaneously rotates about $K$.
- It rotates about its midpoint.
- It remains fixed because there are two ground pins.

## Explanation

Each end moves perpendicular to its ground-pinned link. The lines through the ends normal to their velocities therefore coincide with the link centre lines and meet at $K$, the instantaneous centre of the coupler.
