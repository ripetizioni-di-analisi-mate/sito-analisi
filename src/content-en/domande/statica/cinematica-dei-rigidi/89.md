The beam shown consists of three aligned bodies, with abscissae in metres. Body $1 = AB$ has the fixed pin at $A$ ($x = 0$); body $2 = BC$ is connected to the first by the internal hinge $B$ ($x = 3$) and rests on a roller at $E$ ($x = 5$); body $3 = CD$ is connected to the second at $C$ ($x = 7$) by an internal double link with horizontal links and rests on a roller at $D$ ($x = 10$). Both rollers slide on horizontal planes. What is the rotation $\varphi_3$ of body $3$ in terms of $\varphi_1$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;E,5,0;C,7,0;D,10,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B" interni="C,doppio-pendolo,0" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello;D,carrello" quote="A,B,3 m;B,E,2 m;E,C,2 m;C,D,3 m" titolo="Three-body beam with an internal double link at $C$"}

- $\varphi_3 = \dfrac{3}{2}\,\varphi_1$
- $\varphi_3 = -\dfrac{3}{2}\,\varphi_1$
- $\varphi_3 = 0$
- $\varphi_3 = -\dfrac{2}{3}\,\varphi_1$

## Explanation

The fixed pin gives $C_1 = A$ and the internal hinge gives $C_{12} = B$. By the **first theorem of kinematic chains** $C_2$ lies on the line $AB$, and it also lies on the vertical through the roller $E$, so $C_2 = E$. The vertical displacement of $B$ is the same on both bodies, therefore $3\,\varphi_1 = (3 - 5)\,\varphi_2$ and $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{3}{2}\,\varphi_1$. The internal double link prevents the relative rotation of bodies $2$ and $3$, so $\varphi_3 = \varphi_2 = -\dfrac{3}{2}\,\varphi_1$; body $3$ rotates about the roller $D$ and at $C$ it slides vertically relative to body $2$. The value $\varphi_3 = 0$ is obtained by treating the double link as an internal rigid joint, and the value $-\dfrac{2}{3}\,\varphi_1$ inverts the ratio of the lever arms.
