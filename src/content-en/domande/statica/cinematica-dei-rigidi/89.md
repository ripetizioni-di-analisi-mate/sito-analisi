The four-bar linkage in the figure has the pin supports $A = (0, 0)$ and $D = (4, 0)$ and the internal hinges $B = (1, 3)$ and $C = (5, 3)$, in meters, so the columns $AB$ and $DC$ are parallel and equal. What is the displacement of crossbeam $BC$ for $\varphi_1 = 0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1,3;C,5,3;D,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B;C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,cerniera" quote="A,D,4 m,0.8;B,C,4 m,-0.7" titolo="Parallelogram four-bar linkage"}

- It translates by $(-6, 0)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- It translates by $(-6, 2)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- It translates by $(6, -2)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- It translates by $(-2, 6)\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Hinges give $C_1 = A$, $C_3 = D$, $C_{12} = B$ and $C_{23} = C$. For the **first theorem of kinematic chains** $C_2$ it lies on the $AB$ and $DC$ lines, which are parallel, so $C_2$ is the point at infinity of their $(1, 3)$ direction and the crossbeam moves. The displacement is that of $B$ as the point of the column $AB$:
$$
u_B = -\varphi_1 \cdot 3\,\mathrm{m} = -6\,\mathrm{mm} ,
\qquad
v_B = \varphi_1 \cdot 1\,\mathrm{m} = 2\,\mathrm{mm} .
$$
The crossbeam therefore translates $(-6, 2)\,\mathrm{mm}$, perpendicular to the columns, which are inclined, and thus with a vertical component.
