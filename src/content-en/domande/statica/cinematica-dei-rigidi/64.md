The vertical pillar in the figure is formed by the body $1 = AB$, with a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$, and the body $2 = BC$, connected to the first by the internal hinge $B = (0, 3)$ and bound at $C = (0, 5)$, in meters, by a roller support that moves along a vertical plane. What is the horizontal displacement of point $D = (0, 4)$ on body $2$ in terms of $\varphi_1$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;D,0,4;C,0,5" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello,0" quote="A,B,3 m,0.8;B,D,1 m,0.8;D,C,1 m,0.8" titolo="Two-body column with a roller on a vertical surface at $C$"}

- $u_D = \dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$
- $u_D = -\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$
- $u_D = -4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$
- $u_D = -3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$

## Explanation

The pin support gives $C_1 = A$ and the internal hinge gives $C_{12} = B$. The roller support allows $C$ only vertical shifts, so $C_2$ lies on the horizontal $y = 5$ line; for the **first theorem of kinematic chains** it also lies on the line $AB$, $x = 0$, so $C_2 = C$. The relationship between the rotations gives $\varphi_1 \cdot 3\,\mathrm{m} = \varphi_2\,(3 - 5)\,\mathrm{m}$, then $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{3}{2}\varphi_1$. By the theorem on the horizontal displacements of a body, on the segment $BC$ it is $u = -\varphi_2\,(y - 5)$, and in the point $D$
$$
u_D = -\varphi_2\,(4 - 5)\,\mathrm{m} = \varphi_2 \cdot 1\,\mathrm{m} = -\frac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 .
$$
The $u(y)$ diagram is continuous in the hinge, where the two bodies give the same value $u_B = -3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$, and cancels at $A$ and $C$.
