The horizontal beam in the figure is formed by the body $1 = AB$, with a pin support at $A$, and the body $2 = BC$, with a roller support at $C$ that moves along a horizontal plane. The two bodies are connected at $B$ by an internal parallel-link connection with the vertical links. Which statement is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C" interni="B,doppio-pendolo,90" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m" titolo="Two-body beam joined by an internal parallel-link connection with vertical links"}

- Body $1$ remains stationary and body $2$ translates horizontally
- Both bodies rotate with $\varphi_2 = \varphi_1 \neq 0$
- The beam remains stationary
- Body $1$ remains stationary and body $2$ translates vertically

## Explanation

Internal parallel-link connection prevents relative rotation, hence $\varphi_2 = \varphi_1$, and only allows relative translation perpendicular to pin-ended links, i.e. horizontal. With the origin at $A$, the body $1$ rotates about $A$ and the body $2$ has $1$ body displacement plus a horizontal translation, so at $C = (8, 0)$ it has $v_C = \varphi_1 \cdot 8\,\mathrm{m}$. The roller support imposes $v_C = 0$, then $\varphi_1 = \varphi_2 = 0$ and the body $1$, which has the fixed $A$ point, **Stay still**. The body $2$ can still move horizontally, a shift allowed by both parallel-link support and roller support.
