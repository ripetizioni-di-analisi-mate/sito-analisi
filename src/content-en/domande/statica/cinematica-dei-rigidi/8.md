The frame shown consists of column $AB$ (body $1$), pinned to the ground at $A$, and body $2 = BCD$, joined to the column at internal hinge $B$ and restrained at $D$ by a parallel-link support with vertical links. What is the displacement of body $2$ for $\varphi_1 = -0.003\,\mathrm{rad}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,5,4;D,5,1" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,doppio-pendolo" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,C,5 m,-0.7;C,D,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Frame with a vertical parallel-link support at $D$"}

- It translates horizontally by $12\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left
- It translates horizontally by $12\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- It translates horizontally by $15\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- It translates vertically by $12\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward

## Explanation

Parallel-link support at vertical links prevents the body $2$ from rotating and moving vertically, so $C_2$ is the point at infinity in the vertical direction and the body $2$ can only move horizontally. The pin support gives $C_1 = A$ and the internal hinge gives $C_{12} = B$. The two centers stand on the vertical through $A$, which has the direction of $C_2$, so the **first theorem of kinematic chains** is satisfied and the chain moves. The body $2$ moves with the displacement of $B$ calculated as the point of the column, $u_B = -\varphi_1 \cdot 4\,\mathrm{m} = 0.003 \cdot 4000\,\mathrm{mm} = 12\,\mathrm{mm}$, with $v_B = 0$. The body $2$ then shifts $12\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right.
