The portal frame in the figure consists of the straight $AB$ (body $1$) and $CD$ (body $3$) and the inclined crossbeam $BC$ (body $2$), with $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (0, 3)$, $C = (5, 5)$, $D = (5, 0)$ in meters; $A$ and $D$ are pin supports, $B$ and $C$ internal hinges. What is the displacement of hinge $C$ for $\varphi_1 = 0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,5,5;D,5,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B;C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,cerniera" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.8;D,C,5 m,0.8;A,D,5 m,0.8" titolo="Portal frame with an inclined crossbeam"}

- $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- It remains stationary

## Explanation

Hinges give $C_1 = A$, $C_3 = D$, $C_{12} = B$ and $C_{23} = C$. For the **first theorem of kinematic chains** $C_2$ it lies on line $AB$, $x = 0$, and line $DC$, $x = 5$; the two lines are parallel, so $C_2$ is the point at infinity in the vertical direction and the crossbeam moves horizontally. All points in the crossbeam therefore have the displacement of $B$, calculated as the point in the $AB$: $u_B = -\varphi_1 \cdot 3\,\mathrm{m} = -6\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $v_B = 0$. The hinge $C$ then moves $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left, although the crossbeam is tilted. The column $CD$, higher, rotates by $\varphi_3 = \dfrac{6\,\mathrm{mm}}{5000\,\mathrm{mm}} = 0.0012\,\mathrm{rad}$.
