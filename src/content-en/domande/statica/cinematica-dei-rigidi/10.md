The portal frame shown consists of bodies $1 = AB$, $2 = BC$, and $3 = CD$. Points $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (1, 4)$, $C = (5, 4)$, and $D = (6, 0)$ have coordinates in meters. Points $A$ and $D$ are pin supports, while $B$ and $C$ are internal hinges. What is the rotation $\varphi_2$ of the crossbeam in terms of $\varphi_1$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1,4;C,5,4;D,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B;C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,cerniera" quote="A,D,6 m,0.8;B,C,4 m,-0.7" titolo="Four-bar linkage with inclined side members"}

- $\varphi_2 = -2\varphi_1$
- $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{1}{2}\varphi_1$
- $\varphi_2 = \dfrac{1}{2}\varphi_1$
- $\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1$

## Explanation

Pin supports give $C_1 = A$ and $C_3 = D$, internal hinges give $C_{12} = B$ and $C_{23} = C$. For the **first theorem of kinematic chains** $C_2$ it lies on the $AB$, $y = 4x$, and the $DC$, $y = 24 - 4x$, then $C_2 = (3, 12)\,\mathrm{m}$. The relationship between the rotations of $1$ and $2$ bodies gives $\varphi_1\,(1, 4) = \varphi_2\,(1 - 3,\ 4 - 12) = \varphi_2\,(-2, -8)$. Therefore $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{1}{2}\varphi_1$, and the crossbeam rotates in the opposite direction to the column $AB$. The center $C_{12} = B$ is internal to the segment $C_1 C_2$, consistent with the opposite directions of the two rotations.
