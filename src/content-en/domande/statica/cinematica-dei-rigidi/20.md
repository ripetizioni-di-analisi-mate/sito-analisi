A horizontal beam $AB$ has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, with $A$ to the left of $B$. It undergoes an infinitesimal rigid displacement for which $v_A = 2\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $v_B = 7\,\mathrm{mm}$. Through what angle does the beam rotate?

- $0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$ clockwise
- $0.0014\,\mathrm{rad}$ counterclockwise
- $0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$ counterclockwise
- $0.0018\,\mathrm{rad}$ counterclockwise

## Explanation

With $O = A$, the formula $v = v_A + \varphi\,(x - x_A)$ applied to $B$ gives $v_B - v_A = \varphi \cdot 5\,\mathrm{m}$. So
$$
\varphi = \frac{(7 - 2)\,\mathrm{mm}}{5000\,\mathrm{mm}} = 0.001\,\mathrm{rad} .
$$
The value is positive, so the rotation is counterclockwise, and in fact the right end $B$ rises more than the left end $A$. Rotation depends on the difference in vertical displacements of the two ends.
