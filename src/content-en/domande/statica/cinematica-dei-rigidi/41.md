The beam shown is formed by the body $1 = AC$, with a pin support at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ that moves along a horizontal plane, and by the body $2 = CD$, connected to the first by the internal hinge $C$ and free of other constraints. Which description of the vertical displacement diagram is correct?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,9,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,2 m;C,D,3 m" titolo="Beam with an overhang joined by an internal hinge at $C$"}

- Linear on $AC$, with zero value at $A$, and linear on $CD$
- Zero along the entire beam
- Zero on $AC$ and constant and nonzero on $CD$
- Zero on $AC$ and linear on $CD$, with zero value at $C$

## Explanation

For the body $1$ the center should be $A$, for the pin support, and stand on the vertical through $B$, for the roller support. The vertical through $B$ does not pass through $A$, so the body $1$ remains stationary and $v = 0$ on $AC$. The point $C$ also belongs to the body $1$ and is stationary, so the body $2$ can only rotates about $C$, with $C_2 = C$. On the body $2$ the diagram is then the segment $v = \varphi_2\,(x - x_C)$, null at $C$ and of slope $\varphi_2$, and the diagram is continuous in the internal hinge.
