The arch in the figure is formed by the $AC$ and $CB$ rods, connected by the internal hinge $C = (3, 3)$; at $A = (0, 0)$ there is a pin support and at $B = (6, 0)$ a parallel-link support with vertical links. Which statement is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,3;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,doppio-pendolo" quote="A,3 0,3 m,0.8;3 0,B,3 m,0.8" titolo="Arch with a pin support at $A$ and a vertical parallel-link support at $B$"}

- Member $CB$ translates horizontally and member $AC$ rotates about $A$
- Both members rotate with $\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1$
- The arch remains stationary
- Member $AC$ remains stationary and member $CB$ translates horizontally

## Explanation

Parallel-link support with vertical links prevents member $CB$ from rotating and moving vertically, so $C_2$ is the point at infinity in the vertical direction. The pin support gives $C_1 = A$ and the internal hinge gives $C_{12} = C$. For the **first theorem of kinematic chains**, with non-zero rotations the line through $C_1$ and $C_{12}$ would have the vertical direction of $C_2$; $AC$ is $y = x$, so $\varphi_1 = 0$. Then member $AC$, which has fixed $A$, stays still, and the point $C$ is stationary. Member $CB$ can only move and has the point $C$ still, so it also stays still, and the arch **Stay still**.
