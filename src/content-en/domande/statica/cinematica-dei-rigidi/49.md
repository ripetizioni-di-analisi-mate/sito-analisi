A flat rigid plate rotates $\theta = 0.02\,\mathrm{rad}$, counterclockwise, around the origin $O = (0, 0)$. According to the formulas of rigid-body displacements infinitesimals the point $P = (2, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ has horizontal displacement null. What is its exact horizontal displacement, rounded to the nearest tenth of a millimeter?

- $0$
- $-0.8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-40\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-0.4\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

In the exact $P' = R(\theta)\,P$ rigid-body displacement, then the horizontal displacement of $P$ is $u = x_P\,(\cos\theta - 1)$. For the Taylor $\cos\theta - 1 = -\dfrac{\theta^2}{2} + o(\theta^2)$ formula, therefore
$$
u \approx -\frac{\theta^2}{2}\,x_P = -\frac{0.0004}{2} \cdot 2000\,\mathrm{mm} = -0.4\,\mathrm{mm} .
$$
The value is of the second order in $\theta$, and for this reason the formula infinitesimal $u = -\varphi\,(y - y_O)$, which neglects terms beyond the first order, gives $u = 0$. The vertical displacement equals $x_P \sin\theta \approx 40\,\mathrm{mm}$, a hundred times larger.
