Two points on a rigid plate are $A=(0,0)$ and $B=(3,4)$ in metres. In an infinitesimal motion the measured relative displacement is $\mathbf u_B-\mathbf u_A=(4,-3)\,\mathrm{mm}$. Which property follows from these readings?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,4" linee="0,0 3,4" tratteggi="0,0 3,0 3,4" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,4 m" titolo="Initial positions of the two plate points"}

- The plate translates without rotating.
- Point $A$ is the centre of rotation.
- Distance $AB$ is unchanged to first order.
- The segment extends by $5\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

The first-order change in length is the projection of relative displacement along $AB$. The dot product $3\cdot4+4(-3)$ vanishes. The readings therefore permit rotation without extension of the segment.
