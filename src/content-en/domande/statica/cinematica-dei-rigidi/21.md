A vertical column $AB$, with $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (0, 4)$ in metres, undergoes an infinitesimal rigid-body displacement with $u_A = 1\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $u_B = -7\,\mathrm{mm}$. Through what angle does the column rotate?

- $0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$ clockwise
- $0.0015\,\mathrm{rad}$ counterclockwise
- $0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$ counterclockwise
- $0.0015\,\mathrm{rad}$ clockwise

## Explanation

With $O = A$, the formula $u = u_A - \varphi\,(y - y_A)$ applied to $B$ gives $u_B - u_A = -\varphi \cdot 4\,\mathrm{m}$. So
$$
\varphi = -\frac{(-7 - 1)\,\mathrm{mm}}{4000\,\mathrm{mm}} = 0.002\,\mathrm{rad} ,
$$
counterclockwise. The minus sign in the formula for $u$ reflects the fact that in a counterclockwise rotation the higher points move to the left relative to the lower ones, as the top $B$ of the column does.
