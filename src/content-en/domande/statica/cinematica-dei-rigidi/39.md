The structure in the figure is formed by the body $1 = AB$, with a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$, and the body $2 = BE$, connected to the first by the internal hinge $B = (4, 0)$ and bound at $E = (4, 3)$ by a roller support that moves along a vertical plane. What is the displacement of body $2$ for $\varphi_1 = 0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;E,4,3" aste="A-B;B-E" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello,0" quote="A,B,4 m;B,E,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Two-body structure with a roller on a vertical surface at $E$"}

- It translates vertically by $8\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward
- It translates vertically by $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ upward
- It rotates about $E$
- It translates vertically by $8\,\mathrm{mm}$ upward

## Explanation

The pin support gives $C_1 = A$ and the internal hinge gives $C_{12} = B$. The roller $E$ support allows vertical shifts, so $C_2$ lies on the horizontal through $E$, $y = 3$; for the **first theorem of kinematic chains** also lies on the $AB$, $y = 0$ line. The two lines are parallel, so $C_2$ is the point at infinity in the horizontal direction and the body $2$ moves vertically. The displacement is that of $B$ as the point of the body $1$, i.e. $u_B = 0$ and $v_B = \varphi_1 \cdot 4\,\mathrm{m} = 8\,\mathrm{mm}$, so the body $2$ moves up by $8\,\mathrm{mm}$.
