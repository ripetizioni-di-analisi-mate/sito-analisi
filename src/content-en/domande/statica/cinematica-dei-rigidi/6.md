The frame shown consists of body $1 = ABC$, pinned to the ground at $A$, and body $2 = CD$, joined to the first at internal hinge $C$. A two-force link at $D$ slopes downward to the right at $45^\circ$. What is the ratio $\dfrac{\varphi_2}{\varphi_1}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,8,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,pendolo,315" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,C,4 m,-0.7;C,D,4 m,-0.7" titolo="Frame with an inclined link at $D$"}

- $-2$
- $2$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-1$

## Explanation

Place the origin at $A$, then $C = (4, 4)$ and $D = (8, 4)$. The pin support gives $C_1 = A$ and the internal hinge gives $C_{12} = C$. The two-force link at $D$ allows perpendicular shifts to the pin-ended link, so $C_2$ lies on the line of the pin-ended link, $y = 12 - x$; for the **first theorem of kinematic chains** also lies on the $AC$, $y = x$ line. Therefore $C_2 = (6, 6)\,\mathrm{m}$. The $\varphi_1\,\overrightarrow{C_1 C_{12}} = \varphi_2\,\overrightarrow{C_2 C_{12}}$ relationship becomes $\varphi_1\,(4, 4) = \varphi_2\,(-2, -2)$, then $\dfrac{\varphi_2}{\varphi_1} = -2$. The minus sign corresponds to $C_{12}$ inside the segment $C_1 C_2$, with the two bodies rotating in opposite directions.
