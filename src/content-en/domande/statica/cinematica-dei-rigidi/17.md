A planar rigid body undergoes an infinitesimal rigid-body displacement where the point $O = (1, 1)\,\mathrm{m}$ moves by $u_O = 2\,\mathrm{mm}$, $v_O = 1\,\mathrm{mm}$ and the body rotates by $\varphi = -0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$, towards clockwise. What is the displacement of point $P = (4, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $\vec{s}_P = (0, 4)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\vec{s}_P = (4, -2)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\vec{s}_P = (5, -1)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\vec{s}_P = (5, -3)\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The formulas of the rigid-body displacements are infinitesimal $u = u_O - \varphi\,(y - y_O)$ and $v = v_O + \varphi\,(x - x_O)$, with the coordinate differences measured from $O$. For point $P$ you have $x_P - x_O = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $y_P - y_O = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. So
$$
u_P = 2\,\mathrm{mm} + 0.001 \cdot 2000\,\mathrm{mm} = 4\,\mathrm{mm} ,
\qquad
v_P = 1\,\mathrm{mm} - 0.001 \cdot 3000\,\mathrm{mm} = -2\,\mathrm{mm} .
$$
The horizontal displacement depends on the difference of the orderly and the vertical one on the difference of the horizontal coordinates.
