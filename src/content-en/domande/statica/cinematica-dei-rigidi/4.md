Beam $AB$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ has a roller on a horizontal surface at $A$ and a two-force link at $B$ sloping downward to the left at $45^\circ$. What is the displacement of $B$ for a counterclockwise rotation $\varphi = 0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,pendolo,225" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.6" titolo="Beam with a roller at $A$ and a two-force link inclined at $45^\circ$ at point $B$"}

- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right and $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ upward
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ upward
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left and $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ upward
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left and $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward

## Explanation

Place the origin at $A$. The roller $A$ support only allows horizontal shifts, so the center lies on the vertical through $A$, $x = 0$. The two-force link at $B = (4, 0)$ only allows perpendicular shifts to the pin-ended link, so the center lies on the line of the pin-ended link, $y = x - 4$. The two lines meet in $C = (0, -4)\,\mathrm{m}$. With the rotation center formulas you get $u_B = -\varphi\,(y_B - y_C) = -0.001 \cdot 4000\,\mathrm{mm} = -4\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $v_B = \varphi\,(x_B - x_C) = 0.001 \cdot 4000\,\mathrm{mm} = 4\,\mathrm{mm}$. The $(-4, 4)\,\mathrm{mm}$ shift is perpendicular to the pin-ended link, as the two-force link requires.
