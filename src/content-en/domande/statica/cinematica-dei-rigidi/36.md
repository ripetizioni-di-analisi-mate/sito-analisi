The horizontal $AB$ beam in the figure, with $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (6, 0)$ in meters, is suspended at two-force links: at $A$ the pin-ended link is inclined of $45^\circ$ upwards to the right, at $B$ it is inclined of $45^\circ$ upwards to the left. The beam rotates $\varphi = 0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$ counterclockwise. What is the displacement of midpoint $M = (3, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ of the beam?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;M,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,pendolo,45;B,pendolo,135" quote="A,M,3 m;M,B,3 m" titolo="Beam suspended from two inclined links"}

- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ upward
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- Zero, because $M$ is the center of rotation

## Explanation

A two-force link requires the center of rotation to stand on the line of its pin-ended link. The pin-ended link at $A$ lies on the line $y = x$ and the one at $B$ on the line $y = 6 - x$, so the center is their point of intersection, $C = (3, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$. The center is on the vertical line through $M$, so
$$
u_M = -\varphi\,(y_M - y_C) = -0.002 \cdot (0 - 3\,\mathrm{m}) = 6\,\mathrm{mm} ,
\qquad
v_M = \varphi\,(x_M - x_C) = 0 .
$$
The midpoint then moves **$6\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right**, horizontally, because the segment $CM$ is vertical.
