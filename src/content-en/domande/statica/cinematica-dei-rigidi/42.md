The points $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)$ and $D = (2, 0)$, in meters, belong to a flat rigid plate. Which set of data uniquely determines an infinitesimal rigid-body displacement of the plate?

- $u_A$, $u_B$, and $v_A$
- $u_A$, $v_A$, and $v_B$
- $v_A$ and $v_B$
- $u_B$, $v_B$, and $u_D$

## Explanation

An infinitesimal rigid-body displacement depends on three independent parameters, for example $u_A$, $v_A$ and $\varphi$. The points $A$, $B$ and $D$ stand on the same horizontal line, so, by the theorem of the displacements of two points along the conjunct, we have $u_A = u_B = u_D$. Therefore in the groups $u_A$, $u_B$, $v_A$ and $u_B$, $v_B$, $u_D$ two data coincide and the rotation remains undetermined. Group $v_A$, $v_B$ contains only two data and leaves the horizontal displacement undetermined. With $u_A$, $v_A$ and $v_B$ you get $\varphi = \dfrac{v_B - v_A}{4\,\mathrm{m}}$, so the shift is **identified**.
