The square $ABDE$ plate in the figure, with $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (3, 0)$, $D = (3, 3)$, $E = (0, 3)$ in meters, has a pin support at $A$ and at $D$ a roller support that moves along a plane parallel to the direction $(1, -1)$. What is the displacement of $D$ when the plate rotates through $\varphi = -0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;D,3,3;E,0,3" aste="A-B;B-D;D-E;E-A" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello,45" quote="A,B,3 m,0.8;E,A,3 m,0.8" titolo="Square plate with a pin support at $A$ and a roller on an inclined surface at $D$"}

- The plate remains stationary, so $D$ does not move
- $(-6, 6)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(6, 6)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(6, -6)\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The pin support prevents any displacement of $A$, so, if the plate rotates, the center is $A$. The roller support allows $D$ to move in the direction $(1, -1)$, so the center lies on the normal plane for $D$, the line $y = x$. This line passes through $A$, so the two conditions are compatible and the plate can rotates about $A$. The displacement of $D$ is
$$
u_D = -\varphi \cdot 3\,\mathrm{m} = 6\,\mathrm{mm} ,
\qquad
v_D = \varphi \cdot 3\,\mathrm{m} = -6\,\mathrm{mm} ,
$$
i.e. $(6, -6)\,\mathrm{mm}$, parallel to the support surface.
