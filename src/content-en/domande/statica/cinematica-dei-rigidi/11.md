The beam shown consists of bodies $1 = AB$, $2 = BC$, and $3 = CD$, joined by internal hinges $B$ and $C$. It has a pin support at $A$ and rollers on horizontal surfaces at $E$ and $D$. What is the vertical displacement of hinge $C$ in terms of $\varphi_1$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;E,5,0;C,6,0;D,9,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B;C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello;D,carrello" quote="A,B,3 m;B,E,2 m;E,C,1 m;C,D,3 m" titolo="Three-body beam"}

- $v_C = \dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$
- $v_C = -\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$
- $v_C = -\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$
- $v_C = -\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$

## Explanation

With the origin at $A$, the centers are $C_1 = A$ and $C_{12} = B = (3, 0)$. The center $C_2$ lies on the vertical for the roller at $E$ and, for the **first theorem of kinematic chains**, on the line $AB$, then $C_2 = E = (5, 0)$. The relationship between the rotations gives $\varphi_1 \cdot 3\,\mathrm{m} = \varphi_2\,(3 - 5)\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{3}{2}\varphi_1$. The hinge $C = (6, 0)$ belongs to the body $2$, which rotates about $E$, then $v_C = \varphi_2\,(6 - 5)\,\mathrm{m} = -\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$. The arm is the distance of $C$ from the center $E$, i.e. $1\,\mathrm{m}$.
