A planar rigid body has the fixed $A$ point, i.e. with zero displacement in every allowed displacement. Which infinitesimal rigid-body displacements can the body undergo?

- Rotations about $A$ and translations, with three degrees of freedom
- Translations only, with two degrees of freedom
- Rotations about $A$ and vertical translations, with two degrees of freedom
- Rotations about $A$ only, with one degree of freedom

## Explanation

For the rigid-body displacements infinitesimal theorem with $O = A$, the displacement of a point $P$ is $\vec{s}_P = \vec{s}_A + \varphi\,\hat{k} \times \overrightarrow{AP}$. Since $\vec{s}_A = \vec{0}$, $\vec{s}_P = \varphi\,\hat{k} \times \overrightarrow{AP}$ remains, which depends on the $\varphi$ parameter alone. The possible displacements are therefore the rotations around $A$, and the body has **one degree of freedom**. A translation would also move $A$, so it is excluded.
