The scissor mechanism in the figure is formed by the $AB$ (body $1$) and $DE$ (body $2$) rods, with $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 4)$, $D = (4, 0)$ and $E = (0, 4)$ in meters, connected by the internal hinge $O = (2, 2)$ at the point where they cross. At $A$ there is a pin support and at $D$ a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. What is the displacement of end $E$ for $\varphi_1 = 0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,4;D,4,0;E,0,4;O,2,2" aste="A-O;O-B;D-O;O-E" cerniere="O" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" quote="A,D,4 m;D,B,4 m,-0.9" titolo="Scissor mechanism"}

- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ upward
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left
- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$ upward

## Explanation

The pin support gives $C_1 = A$ and the internal hinge gives $C_{12} = O$. $D$ roller support requires $C_2$ to stand on the vertical $x = 4$; for the **first theorem of kinematic chains** $C_2$ also lies on the line $AO$, $y = x$, therefore $C_2 = (4, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$. The displacement of $O$ calculated with the two bodies is the same, therefore $\varphi_1\,\overrightarrow{AO} = \varphi_2\,\overrightarrow{C_2 O}$, i.e. $\varphi_1\,(2, 2) = \varphi_2\,(-2, -2)$, from which $\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1 = -0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$. Point $E$ has the same vertical coordinate as $C_2$, so $u_E = 0$ and
$$
v_E = \varphi_2\,(x_E - x_{C_2}) = -0.001 \cdot (0 - 4\,\mathrm{m}) = 4\,\mathrm{mm} .
$$
The extreme $E$ then moves by **$4\,\mathrm{mm}$ upward**.
