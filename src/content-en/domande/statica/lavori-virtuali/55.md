In the mechanism in the figure the $AB$ and $BC$ sections are connected by the internal hinge $B$ and the $X_B$, $Y_B$ forces are exchanged at $B$: the force $(X_B, Y_B)$ acts on $BC$ and the force $(-X_B, -Y_B)$ on $AB$. In a virtual displacement the two segments rotate by different angles and $B$ moves by $(\delta u_B, \delta v_B)$. What is the total virtual work of the two forces?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Mechanism with internal hinge $B$"}

- $0$
- $Y_B\,\delta v_B$
- $2\,Y_B\,\delta v_B$
- $X_B\,\delta u_B + Y_B\,\delta v_B$

## Explanation

The internal hinge imposes the same displacement at point $B$ as point of the two segments, even when their rotations are different. The two forces are applied at points that move in the same way and are equal and opposite, so the overall work is
$$
X_B\,\delta u_B + Y_B\,\delta v_B - X_B\,\delta u_B - Y_B\,\delta v_B = 0 .
$$
This is why the principle of virtual work is written for the entire structure without disassembling it, and the forces in the internal hinges do not appear. The value $X_B\,\delta u_B + Y_B\,\delta v_B$ is the work of the only force acting on $BC$.
