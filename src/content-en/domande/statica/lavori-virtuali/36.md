The cantilever shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at $A$ and carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire length. Using virtual work, what is the bending moment at midpoint $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.4,0.4,q" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Cantilever under uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the top fibers in tension
- $-16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the top fibers in tension
- $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the top fibers in tension
- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension

## Explanation

You insert a hinge at $S$. Segment $AS$ is fixed and remains stationary, $\delta\varphi_s = 0$, while segment $SB$ rotates about $S$ through angle $\delta\varphi_d$; then $\Delta\varphi = -\delta\varphi_d$. The load on $AS$ does not do any work, while the load on $SB$ has has resultant $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $S$, which moves by $\delta v = \delta\varphi_d$. Therefore $\delta L = -4\,\delta\varphi_d$ and
$$
M_S = -\frac{\delta L}{\Delta\varphi} = -\frac{-4\,\delta\varphi_d}{-\delta\varphi_d} = -4\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
with the upper fibers stretched. The value $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained with the resultant at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $S$, and $-16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is now fixed support.
