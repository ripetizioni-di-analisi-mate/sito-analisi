The beam $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, is hinged to the wall at $A = (0, 0)$ and supported at $B = (4, 0)$ by the $BD$ tie rod, fixed to the wall at $D = (0, 3)$ (coordinates in meters). At $B$, the force $F = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Using virtual work, what is the axial force in the tie rod?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;D,0,3" aste="A-B" catene="B-D" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;D,cerniera,180" forze="B,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;A,D,3 m,-0.9" titolo="Beam pinned to a wall and supported by tie rod $BD$"}

- $25\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $25\,\mathrm{kN}$, in compression

## Explanation

Remove the tie rod and apply its force at $B$. The force $N$ points from $B$ toward $D$ when the rod is in tension. Since $BD = 5\,\mathrm{m}$, the unit vector in that direction is $\left( -\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5} \right)$. The beam rotates about $A$ through $\delta\varphi$, so $B$ rises by $4\,\delta\varphi$ without moving horizontally. The work of $N$ is therefore $\dfrac{3}{5}\,N \cdot 4\,\delta\varphi$, and the work of $F$ is $-15 \cdot 4\,\delta\varphi$. Thus $\dfrac{12}{5}\,N - 60 = 0$, giving $N = 25\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension. Using the horizontal component of the unit vector in place of its vertical component would give $18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$.
