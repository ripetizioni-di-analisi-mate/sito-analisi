The structure in the figure is formed by the cantilever $AB$, fixed at $A$, and the segment $BC$, connected to the cantilever at $B$ by an internal parallel-link connection with the horizontal links and resting at $C$ on a roller support; $AB = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $BC = 3\,\mathrm{m}$. At $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$ the force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Using virtual work, what is the roller reaction $R_C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;E,3,0;C,5,0" aste="A-B;B-C" interni="B,doppio-pendolo,0" vincoli="A,incastro,180;C,carrello" forze="E,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,B,2 m,0.9;B,E,1 m,0.9;E,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Cantilever and segment $BC$ joined by an internal parallel-link connection at $B$"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $0$

## Explanation

Remove the roller at $C$. The cantilever $AB$ is fixed and stays still; parallel-link support prevents relative rotation and horizontal relative sliding, so the segment $BC$ does not rotate and does not transpose horizontally, and can only transpose $\delta v$ vertically. All points of $BC$ then move by $\delta v$, and the principle of virtual work gives $R_C\,\delta v - 12\,\delta v = 0$, i.e. $R_C = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, upwards, whatever the position of $F$ on the segment $BC$. Parallel-link support with horizontal links does not transmit vertical forces, so all the segment $BC$ load goes to roller support. The value $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by treating the link at $B$ as a hinge, and $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ with the same error and the arms exchanged.
