At an ideal hinge of a planar mechanism, body 1 exerts an upward vertical force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ on body 2. The hinge undergoes an upward virtual displacement of $3\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the total virtual work of both hinge forces for the entire mechanism?

- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Hinge compatibility gives both bodies the same virtual displacement at their shared point. Action and reaction give force–displacement products of $+12\,\mathrm{J}$ and $-12\,\mathrm{J}$. Their sum vanishes.
