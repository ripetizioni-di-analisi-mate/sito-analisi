The structure in the figure is formed by the section $AB$, with a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$, and the section $BC$, connected to $AB$ by the internal hinge $B = (4, 0)$ and resting at $C = (7, 0)$ on a roller support (coordinates in meters). In the hinge $B$, the force $P = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. The segment $AB$ rotates $\delta\varphi_1$, positive counterclockwise. What is the virtual work of $P$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,7,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" forze="B,0,-1.4,P" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,3 m,0.9" titolo="Mechanism under force $P = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at internal hinge $B$"}

- $-48\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$
- $0$
- $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$
- $-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$

## Explanation

The point $B$ has the same displacement as the point of the two segments, so $P$'s work does not depend on the segment to which the force is assigned. As the point of $AB$, which rotates about $A$, $B$ rises by $\delta v_B = 4\,\delta\varphi_1$, therefore $\delta L = -6 \cdot 4\,\delta\varphi_1 = -24\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$. As a control, the segment $BC$ rotates about $C$ with $4\,\delta\varphi_1 = (4 - 7)\,\delta\varphi_2$, then $\delta\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{4}{3}\,\delta\varphi_1$ and $\delta v_B = -3\,\delta\varphi_2 = 4\,\delta\varphi_1$. Counting force on both segments would get $-48$; the value $-18$ arises from the wrong $\delta\varphi_2 = -\delta\varphi_1$ hypothesis.
