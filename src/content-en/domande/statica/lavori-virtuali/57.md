In the mechanism shown the segments $AB$ and $BC$, aligned on the $x$ axis, are joined by the internal hinge $B = (3, 0)$; $A = (0, 0)$ is a fixed pin and at $C = (5, 0)$ the segment $BC$ rests on a roller sliding on a horizontal plane (coordinates in metres). At the hinge $B$ the horizontal force $H = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is its virtual work for a rotation $\delta\varphi_1$ of segment $AB$, positive counterclockwise?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,5,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" forze="B,1.3,0,H" quote="A,B,3 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Mechanism with the horizontal force $H$ at the hinge $B$"}

- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$
- $-15\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$
- $0$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$

## Explanation

The segment $AB$ rotates about $A$, so the displacement of $B$ is perpendicular to the segment $AB$. The segment $AB$ is horizontal, therefore $B$ moves only vertically, by $\delta v_B = 3\,\delta\varphi_1$, and $\delta u_B = 0$. The same result is obtained by regarding $B$ as a point of segment $BC$, which rotates about the roller $C$, also on the $x$ axis. The force $H$ is horizontal, so its work is $H\,\delta u_B = 0$ for every value of $\delta\varphi_1$. The values $\pm 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$ are obtained by multiplying $H$ by the vertical displacement of $B$, which is perpendicular to the force, and the value $-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$ uses the lever arm $BC$.
