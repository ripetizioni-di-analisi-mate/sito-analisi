The Gerber beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$, an internal hinge $C$, and a roller at $D$. Their respective horizontal coordinates are $0$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$, $5\,\mathrm{m}$, and $9\,\mathrm{m}$. A downward force $F = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $x = 7\,\mathrm{m}$. Using virtual work, what is the vertical reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,5,0;D,9,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" forze="7 0,0,-1.6,F" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,1 m,0.9;C,7 0,2 m,0.9;7 0,D,2 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with $F = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ on segment $CD$"}

- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward

## Explanation

It replaces the pin at $A$ with a roller on a vertical surface. Segment $AC$ rotates about $B$ through angle $\delta\varphi_1$, and segment $CD$ rotates about $D$ through angle $\delta\varphi_2$; the displacement of $C$ gives $(5 - 4)\,\delta\varphi_1 = (5 - 9)\,\delta\varphi_2$, then $\delta\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{1}{4}\,\delta\varphi_1$. Then $\delta v_A = -4\,\delta\varphi_1$, and the point of application of $F$ moves by $\delta v = (7 - 9)\,\delta\varphi_2 = 0.5\,\delta\varphi_1$. The principle of virtual work gives
$$
V_A \cdot (-4\,\delta\varphi_1) - 16 \cdot 0.5\,\delta\varphi_1 = 0 ,
\qquad
V_A = -2\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
i.e. $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ down. The value $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the force that the segment $CD$ transmits to the segment $AC$ at $C$.
