## Explanation

At $S$, introduce a parallel-link connection with its links parallel to the axis. Segment $AS$ rotates about $A$ and segment $SB$ about $B$, both through angle $\delta\varphi$. Then $\delta v_s = 3\,\delta\varphi$, $\delta v_d = -3\,\delta\varphi$ and $\Delta v = -6\,\delta\varphi$. The clockwise couple equals $-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in the counterclockwise convention, lies on $AS$ and does work $-18\,\delta\varphi$; the force $F$ lies on $SB$, where $\delta v = (4 - 6)\,\delta\varphi = -2\,\delta\varphi$, and does the $24\,\delta\varphi$ work. Therefore
$$
\delta L = -18\,\delta\varphi + 24\,\delta\varphi = 6\,\delta\varphi ,
\qquad
T_S = -\frac{6\,\delta\varphi}{-6\,\delta\varphi} = 1\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The value $7\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained with the direction of the exchanged couple, and $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ neglecting the couple.
