The structure in the figure is formed by the section $AB$, with a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$, and by the section $BD$, connected to $AB$ by the internal hinge $B = (3, 0)$ and resting at $C = (5, 0)$ on a roller support; the overhang arrives at $D = (7, 0)$ (coordinates in meters). At $D$, the counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts. The segment $AB$ rotates $\delta\varphi_1$, positive counterclockwise. What is the virtual work of the couple?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,5,0;D,7,0" aste="A-B;B-D" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" coppie="D,1,ℳ" quote="A,B,3 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,D,2 m,0.9" titolo="Mechanism under counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $D$"}

- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$
- $-\dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$

## Explanation

The segment $BD$ rotates about the roller at $C$, and the point $B$ has the same vertical displacement on the two segments: $3\,\delta\varphi_1 = (3 - 5)\,\delta\varphi_2$, then $\delta\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{3}{2}\,\delta\varphi_1$. The couple is applied to the segment $BD$, so its work is $\mathcal{M}\,\delta\varphi_2$, whatever the point of the segment it acts in. Then $\delta L = 8 \cdot \left( -\dfrac{3}{2}\,\delta\varphi_1 \right) = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$. The value $8\,\delta\varphi_1$ uses the rotation of the segment $AB$, and $-\dfrac{16}{3}\,\delta\varphi_1$ uses the ratio between the upside-down arms.
