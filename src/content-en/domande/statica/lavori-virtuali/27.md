The beam shown has the pin supports $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (8, 0)$ and the internal hinge $C = (4, 0)$ (coordinates in meters). Which statement about its virtual displacements is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,8,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" quote="A,C,4 m,0.9;C,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with pin supports $A$ and $B$ and internal hinge $C$"}

- It permits a nonzero displacement in which hinge $C$ moves vertically
- It permits only zero displacement because the six scalar restraints equal the six degrees of freedom
- It permits a nonzero displacement in which hinge $C$ moves horizontally
- It permits only zero displacement because each segment has a pin support

## Explanation

The centers of the two segments are the pin supports, $C_1 = A$ and $C_2 = B$, and the relative center is the internal hinge, $C_{12} = C$. The three points stand on the line $y = 0$, so the alignment condition of the first kinematic chain theorem is satisfied and the structure is a mechanism. The point $C$ moves vertically by $\delta v_C = 4\,\delta\varphi_1 = (4 - 8)\,\delta\varphi_2$, and horizontally by $\delta u_C = 0$, because it stands at the elevation of the centers. The $3n = s = 6$ count is only a necessary condition of static determinacy, and here constraints are ill-arranged.
