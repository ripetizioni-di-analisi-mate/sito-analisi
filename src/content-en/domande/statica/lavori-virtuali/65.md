The Gerber beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$, an internal hinge $C$, and a roller at $D$. Their respective horizontal coordinates are $0$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$, $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and $10\,\mathrm{m}$. Remove the roller at $B$ and consider a nonzero virtual displacement of the resulting mechanism. At which point does the vertical displacement have the largest absolute value?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,D,4 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with the roller at $B$ removed"}

- At point $B$
- At the midpoint of $CD$
- At the midpoint of $AC$
- At hinge $C$

## Explanation

After the roller support is removed, the segment $AC$ rotates about $A$ and the segment $CD$ around $D$. The vertical displacement diagram then consists of two segments, which cancel out below $A$ and below $D$ and meet in the hinge $C$. On $AC$ you have $|\delta v| = |\delta\varphi_1|\,x$, which grows to $6\,|\delta\varphi_1|$ at $C$; $CD$ has $|\delta v| = |\delta\varphi_2|\,(10 - x)$, which decreases from $C$ to $D$. Therefore the maximum is in the **hinge $C$**. At $B$ the displacement equals $4\,|\delta\varphi_1|$, two thirds of that of $C$.
