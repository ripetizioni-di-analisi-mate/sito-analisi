The portal frame in the figure has a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$, the high right $4\,\mathrm{m}$, the internal hinge $C = (2, 4)$ on the crossbeam and at $B = (6, 0)$ a roller support that moves along a vertical plane, with the horizontal reaction (coordinates in meters). The body $ADC$ rotates $\delta\varphi_1$, positive counterclockwise. What is the vertical displacement of $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;C,2,4;E,6,4;B,6,0" aste="A-D;D-C;C-E;E-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" quote="D,C,2 m,-0.6;C,E,4 m,-0.6;E,B,4 m,-0.7" titolo="Portal frame with internal hinge $C$ and a roller against a vertical surface at $B$"}

- $\delta v_B = -6\,\delta\varphi_1$
- $\delta v_B = 6\,\delta\varphi_1$
- $\delta v_B = 0$
- $\delta v_B = 3\,\delta\varphi_1$

## Explanation

The roller support only leaves $B$ with vertical displacement, so the center $C_2$ of the body $CEB$ lies on the horizontal through $B$, the line $y = 0$. For the first theorem of the kinematic chains $C_2$ also lies on the line through $C_1 = A$ and $C_{12} = C$, of equation $y = 2x$. The two lines meet at $A$, so the two bodies rotate about the same point. The hinge $C$ has the same displacement on the two bodies, so $\delta\varphi_2 = \delta\varphi_1$ and the portal frame rotates like a single body around $A$. Then $\delta v_B = \delta\varphi_1\,(6 - 0) = 6\,\delta\varphi_1$. The value $-6\,\delta\varphi_1$ is obtained with $\delta\varphi_2 = -\delta\varphi_1$, which does not respect the hinge $C$.
