The $AB$ inclined beam in the figure goes from the hinge $A = (0, 0)$ to the point $B = (3, 4)$, where it rests on a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane (coordinates in meters). At the midpoint $E = (1.5;\ 2)$ acts the force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ downwards. Using virtual work, what is the axial force at section $S = (2.25;\ 3)$ between $E$ and $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;E,1.5,2;S,2.25,3;B,3,4" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="E,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,3 0,3 m,0.6;3 0,B,4 m,0.6" titolo="Inclined beam with $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at its midpoint"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$, in compression
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension

## Explanation

Introduce an axial sliding connection at $S$. Both segments rotate through $\delta\varphi$ about $A$, while segment $SB$ also slides along the axis of $\delta w_d$ in the direction of unit vector $\left( \dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5} \right)$ from $A$ toward $B$. The displacement of $B$ is therefore $(-4\,\delta\varphi, 3\,\delta\varphi) + \delta w_d\,\left( \dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5} \right)$. Roller support prevents vertical displacement of $B$, so $3\,\delta\varphi + \dfrac{4}{5}\,\delta w_d = 0$ and $\delta w_d = -\dfrac{15}{4}\,\delta\varphi$. The force $F$ acts on the segment $AS$, at the horizontal coordinate $1.5$ point, and does work $-15\,\delta\varphi$. Since $\Delta w = -\delta w_d = \dfrac{15}{4}\,\delta\varphi$, the principle gives $N \cdot \dfrac{15}{4}\,\delta\varphi - 15\,\delta\varphi = 0$, that is, $N = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension. With the balance of the segment $SB$ you find the same value: the reaction of the roller support, $R_B = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upwards, has $5 \cdot \dfrac{4}{5} = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ component along the axis, while $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained using the component $\dfrac{3}{5}$.
