The cantilever shown has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and is fixed at $A$. A downward force $F = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and a counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at free end $B$. Using virtual work, what is the fixed-end moment $M_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="C,0,-1,F" coppie="B,1,ℳ" quote="A,C,2 m,0.8;C,B,1 m,0.8" titolo="Cantilever with $F = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ and counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $B$"}

- $40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

Replace the fixed support with a pin. The cantilever rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi$, the displacement conjugate to $M_A$. The point of application of $F$ moves by $\delta v = 2\,\delta\varphi$, and the couple does work $\mathcal{M}\,\delta\varphi$, which does not depend on where it is applied. Therefore
$$
M_A\,\delta\varphi - 15 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi + 10\,\delta\varphi = 0 ,
$$
from which $M_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise. The value $40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained with the wrong sign of the couple's work, and $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ neglecting the couple.
