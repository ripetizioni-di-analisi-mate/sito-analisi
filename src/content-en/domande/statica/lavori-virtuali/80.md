The simply supported beam in the figure has $AB = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ span, a hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, and brings the force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ down to point $S$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Using virtual work, what is the shear force immediately to the right of $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="S,0,-1.6,F" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam with $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $S$"}

- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

It fits at $S$ a parallel-link support with the horizontal links; both segments rotate through the same angle $\delta\varphi$, with $AS$ rotating about $A$ and $SB$ about $B$. So $\delta v_s = 2\,\delta\varphi$, $\delta v_d = (2 - 6)\,\delta\varphi = -4\,\delta\varphi$ and $\Delta v = -6\,\delta\varphi$. For the section immediately to the right of $S$ the force belongs to the left section, it rises by $2\,\delta\varphi$ and does the $-24\,\delta\varphi$ work. Then $T \cdot (-6\,\delta\varphi) - 24\,\delta\varphi = 0$, that is $T = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$. Assigning the force to the right segment you get $T = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, the shear force immediately to the left of $S$; The $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ jump between the two values is the force $F$.
