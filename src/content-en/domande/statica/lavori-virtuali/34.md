The Gerber beam shown consists of segment $AB$, fixed at $A$, and segment $BC$, joined to $AB$ at internal hinge $B$ and supported by a roller at $C$. The dimensions are $AB = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $BC = 3\,\mathrm{m}$. At $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ the force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Using virtual work, what is the roller reaction $R_C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;C,carrello" forze="1.5 0,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,1.5 0,1.5 m,0.9;1.5 0,B,1.5 m,0.9;B,C,3 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam under force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ on segment $AB$"}

- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $2.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $0$

## Explanation

Remove the roller. Segment $AB$ remains stationary because its fixed support prevents any displacement. Segment $BC$ rotates about hinge $B$ through angle $\delta\varphi$. The point of application of $F$ lies on $AB$ and does not move, so the work of the loads is $\delta L = 0$. The principle of virtual work gives $R_C\,\delta v_C = 0$ with $\delta v_C = 3\,\delta\varphi \neq 0$, therefore $R_C = 0$. The load applied to the load $AB$-bearing segment does not reach the $BC$ carried segment.
