The body $L$ in the figure is formed by the column $AD$, with $A = (0, 0)$ and $D = (0, 3)$, and the arm $DE$, with $E = (4, 3)$ (coordinates in meters). At $A$ there is a roller support that moves along a horizontal surface, at $E$ a two-force link with the pin-ended direct link as the vector $(1, -1)$. Where is the center of rotation $C$ for its virtual displacements?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,3;E,4,3" aste="A-D;D-E" vincoli="A,carrello;E,pendolo,315" quote="A,D,3 m,-0.8;D,E,4 m,-0.6" titolo="Body shaped like $L$ with a roller at $A$ and a two-force link inclined at $45^\circ$ at point $E$"}

- $C = (0, 7)$
- $C = (0, -1)$
- $C = (7, 0)$
- $C = (0, 3)$

## Explanation

The roller support leaves $A$ with only horizontal displacement, so the center lies on the vertical through $A$, the line $x = 0$. The two-force link prevents $E$ from moving along the pin-ended link, so $E$ moves perpendicular to the pin-ended link and the center lies on the line of the pin-ended link. This line passes through $E = (4, 3)$ with direction $(1, -1)$, so it has equation $y = 7 - x$. The two lines meet at $C = (0, 7)$. The point $(0, -1)$ is obtained by using the pin-ended link perpendicular to the line instead of the pin-ended link, and $(7, 0)$ by using the horizontal through $A$ instead of the vertical.
