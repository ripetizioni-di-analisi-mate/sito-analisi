The beam $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, has at $A$ a parallel-link support with the horizontal links, which prevents horizontal translation and rotation, and at $B$ a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. The $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ uniform load acts on the entire beam. Using virtual work, what is the bending moment at midpoint $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with a parallel-link support at $A$ and a roller at $B$, with $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension

## Explanation

You insert a hinge at $S$. The parallel-link connection prevents rotation of $AS$, so $AS$ translates vertically, while segment $SB$ rotates about $B$ through angle $\delta\varphi_d$; therefore $\Delta\varphi = 0 - \delta\varphi_d = -\delta\varphi_d$. The point $S$ moves by $(2 - 4)\,\delta\varphi_d = -2\,\delta\varphi_d$, and occasionally translates the entire $AS$ segment. The load on $AS$, resulting in $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, does work $12\,\delta\varphi_d$; the one on $SB$, resulting in $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 3$, moves $-\delta\varphi_d$ and does the $6\,\delta\varphi_d$ work. Then $M \cdot (-\delta\varphi_d) + 18\,\delta\varphi_d = 0$, i.e. $M = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers stretched. With the balance of the segment $SB$ you find $M = R_B \cdot 2 - 6 \cdot 1 = 24 - 6$, with $R_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$; $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$ of the simply supported beam.
