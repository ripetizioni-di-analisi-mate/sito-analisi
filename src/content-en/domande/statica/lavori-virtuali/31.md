The simply supported beam in the figure has $AB = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ span, a hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, and carries a triangular load that decreases from $q_0 = 9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$ to $0$ at $B$. Using virtual work, what is reaction $R_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,1.2,0,q_0" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam with a triangular load varying from $q_0 = 9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $0$"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $27\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward

## Explanation

Remove the roller. The beam rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi$, with $\delta v_B = 6\,\delta\varphi$. The triangular load has resultant equal to the area of the diagram, $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 9 \cdot 6 = 27\,\mathrm{kN}$, applied in the triangle centroid, to one third of the span from the side of the maximum value, i.e. in $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, where $\delta v = 2\,\delta\varphi$. Therefore
$$
R_B \cdot 6\,\delta\varphi - 27 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi = 0 ,
\qquad
R_B = 9\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
Upwards. The value $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained with the resultant at two thirds of the span from $A$, the value $13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ with the resultant in the middle.
