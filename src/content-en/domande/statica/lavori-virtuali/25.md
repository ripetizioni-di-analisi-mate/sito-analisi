The $AB$ rigid bodies, with $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (4, 0)$, and $DE$, with $D = (6, 2)$ and $E = (10, 2)$, are connected by two pin-ended links hinged at the ends: the first goes from $B$ to $D$, the second from $G = (3, 0)$ to $H = (7, 2)$ (coordinates in meters). Where is the relative center of rotation $C_{12}$ of the two bodies?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;G,3,0;B,4,0;D,6,2;H,7,2;E,10,2" aste="A-B;D-E;B-D;G-H" cerniere="B;G;D;H" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;D,E,4 m,-0.7" titolo="Two bodies joined by crossed links $BD$ and $GH$"}

- $C_{12} = (4, 0)$
- The point at infinity in the horizontal direction
- $C_{12} = (6, 2)$
- $C_{12} = (5, 1)$

## Explanation

A pin-ended link prevents the relative displacement of its extremes along its own axis, so the relative center of the two bodies lies on the axis of each pin-ended link. The axis of the pin-ended link $BD$ passes through $(4, 0)$ and $(6, 2)$ and is the line $y = x - 4$. The axis of the pin-ended link $GH$ passes through $(3, 0)$ and $(7, 2)$ and is the line $y = \dfrac{x - 3}{2}$. Equalizing the two expressions results in $2x - 8 = x - 3$, i.e. $x = 5$ and $y = 1$. Therefore $C_{12} = (5, 1)$, the point where the two pin-ended links cross. The point at infinity in the horizontal direction is the relative center of an internal parallel-link connection with the horizontal links.
