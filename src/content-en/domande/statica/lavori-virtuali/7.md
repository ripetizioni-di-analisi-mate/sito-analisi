The three-hinged portal frame shown has fixed pins $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (6, 0)$, columns of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$, and internal hinge $C = (3, 3)$ at the midpoint of the crossbeam (coordinates in meters). At $D = (0, 3)$, horizontal force $P = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Using virtual work, what is the horizontal reaction $H_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,3;C,3,3;E,6,3;B,6,0" aste="A-D;D-C;C-E;E-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="D,1.4,0,P" quote="D,C,3 m,-0.6;C,E,3 m,-0.6;E,B,3 m,0.7" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame under horizontal force $P = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $D$"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the left
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the right
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the left
- $0$

## Explanation

It replaces the pin at $B$ with a roller on a horizontal surface. The body on the left rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi_1$; the center $C_2$ of the right body lies on the vertical through $B$ and on the line $AC$, of equation $y = x$, then $C_2 = (6, 6)$. The point $C$ moves $(-3\,\delta\varphi_1, 3\,\delta\varphi_1)$ as the point of the first body and $(3\,\delta\varphi_2, -3\,\delta\varphi_2)$ as the point of the second, therefore $\delta\varphi_2 = -\delta\varphi_1$ and $\delta u_B = -\delta\varphi_2\,(0 - 6) = -6\,\delta\varphi_1$. The point $D$ moves by $\delta u_D = -3\,\delta\varphi_1$, and the principle of virtual work gives
$$
H_B \cdot (-6\,\delta\varphi_1) + 8 \cdot (-3\,\delta\varphi_1) = 0 ,
\qquad
H_B = -4\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
i.e. $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left.
