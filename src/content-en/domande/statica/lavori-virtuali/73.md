The three-hinged portal frame in the figure has the pin support $A = (0, 0)$ at the base of the column $AD$, with $D = (0, 4)$, the pin support $B = (6, 2)$ at the base of the column $EB$, with $E = (6, 4)$, and the internal hinge $C = (3, 4)$ at the center of the crossbeam (coordinates in meters). At the midpoint $G$ of the segment $CE$ the force $F = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downwards. Using virtual work, what is the vertical component $V_B$ of the reaction at $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;C,3,4;G,4.5,4;E,6,4;B,6,2" aste="A-D;D-C;C-E;E-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="G,0,-1.4,F" quote="A,D,4 m,0.9;D,C,3 m,-0.6;C,E,3 m,-0.6;B,E,2 m,0.8" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with bases at different elevations and $F = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward

## Explanation

It replaces the pin at $B$ with a roller support that leaves the vertical displacement free, so the center $C_2$ of the body $CEB$ lies on the horizontal through $B$, the line $y = 2$. For the first theorem of the kinematic chains $C_2$ also lies on the line through $C_1 = A$ and $C_{12} = C$, of equation $y = \dfrac{4}{3}\,x$, therefore $C_2 = \left( \dfrac{3}{2}, 2 \right)$. The vertical displacement of $C$ equals $3\,\delta\varphi_1$ on the body $ADC$ and $\left( 3 - \dfrac{3}{2} \right) \delta\varphi_2$ on the body $CEB$, then $\delta\varphi_2 = 2\,\delta\varphi_1$. Then $B$ gets up by $\left( 6 - \dfrac{3}{2} \right) \delta\varphi_2 = 9\,\delta\varphi_1$ and $G$ by $\left( \dfrac{9}{2} - \dfrac{3}{2} \right) \delta\varphi_2 = 6\,\delta\varphi_1$. The principle gives $9\,V_B\,\delta\varphi_1 - 18 \cdot 6\,\delta\varphi_1 = 0$, i.e. $V_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, upwards. The value $13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained from rotation about $A$ neglecting the thrust, which with $B$ higher than $A$ has non-zero moment.
