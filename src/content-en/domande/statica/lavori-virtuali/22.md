A free plane rigid body is subject to only two forces of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ equal and opposite, with parallel lines of action at $3\,\mathrm{m}$ distance. For which infinitesimal rigid-body displacements is the virtual work of the two forces zero?

- For every rigid-body displacement
- For translations only
- For rotations about the midpoint between the two lines of action only
- For no nonzero rigid-body displacement

## Explanation

By the theorem on virtual work on a rigid body $\delta L = R_x\,\delta u_O + R_y\,\delta v_O + M_O\,\delta\varphi$. The two forces form a couple, which has zero resultant and moment of magnitude $4 \cdot 3 = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about any reference point. So $\delta L = M_O\,\delta\varphi$ with $|M_O| = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and the work is zero if and only if $\delta\varphi = 0$, i.e. **for translation only**. Since the work is not null for each rigid-body displacement, by the principle of virtual work for the free rigid body the two forces do not form a balanced system.
