Beam $AB$ shown has a roller at $A = (0, 0)$ on a horizontal surface and a roller at $B = (6, 0)$ whose reaction is in the direction of unit vector $\left( -\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5} \right)$ (coordinates in meters). Where is the center of rotation $C$ for its virtual displacements?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello,306.87" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with a roller on a horizontal surface at $A$ and a roller on an incline at $B$"}

- $C = (0, -4.5)$
- $C = (6, 8)$
- $C = (0, 8)$
- $C = (0, 6)$

## Explanation

The displacement of $A$ is horizontal, so the center $C$ lies on the vertical line through $A$, the line $x = 0$. The displacement of $B$ is parallel to the support surface, i.e. perpendicular to the reaction, therefore $C$ lies on the line through $B$ directed as the reaction, formed by the points $(6 - 3t, 4t)$. This line meets $x = 0$ by $t = 2$, then $C = (0, 8)$. The point $(0, -4.5)$ is obtained with the line through $B$ parallel to the support surface.
