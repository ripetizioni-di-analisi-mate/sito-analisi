To find the shear force at section $S$ of a horizontal beam by virtual work, which release must be introduced at $S$?

- A hinge
- A parallel-link connection with links perpendicular to the beam axis
- A parallel-link connection with links parallel to the beam axis
- A two-force link parallel to the beam axis

## Explanation

The shear force does work for the relative sliding of the two segments perpendicular to the axis. The disconnect leaves only this movement free and transmits $N$ and $M$. The **parallel-link support with pin-ended links parallel to the axis** prevents relative rotation and axial sliding and leaves perpendicular sliding free. In virtual work, the term $T\,\Delta v$ appears only. Hinge serves for $M$, parallel-link support with pin-ended links perpendicular to the axis serves for $N$, and a two-force link would leave two relative movements free.
