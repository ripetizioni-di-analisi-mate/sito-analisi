The L-shaped beam in the figure is formed by the column $AD$, with $A = (0, 0)$ and $D = (0, 3)$, and the crossbeam $DE$, with $E = (4, 3)$ (coordinates in meters). At $A$ there is a hinge, at $E$ a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. At $D$ the horizontal force $P = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Using virtual work, what is the roller reaction $R_E$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,3;E,4,3" aste="A-D;D-E" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="D,1.2,0,P" quote="A,D,3 m,0.8;D,E,4 m,-0.6" titolo="L-shaped beam under horizontal force $P = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $D$"}

- $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward
- $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward

## Explanation

Remove the roller. The body rotates about hinge $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi$. The point $E = (4, 3)$ moves vertically by $\delta v_E = 4\,\delta\varphi$ and the point $D = (0, 3)$ horizontally by $\delta u_D = -3\,\delta\varphi$. The force $P$ does the $6 \cdot (-3\,\delta\varphi)$ work, so
$$
R_E \cdot 4\,\delta\varphi - 18\,\delta\varphi = 0 ,
\qquad
R_E = 4.5\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
Upwards. The value $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by exchanging the arms of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$.
