The structure in the figure is formed by the section $AB$, with a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$, and the section $BC$, connected to $AB$ by the internal hinge $B = (4, 0)$. At $C = (6, 0)$ there is a parallel-link support with the horizontal links, which prevents the section $BC$ from horizontal translation and rotation (coordinates in meters). The segment $AB$ rotates $\delta\varphi_1$, positive counterclockwise. What is the vertical displacement of $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,doppio-pendolo,180" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Structure with internal hinge $B$ and a parallel-link support at $C$"}

- $\delta v_C = 4\,\delta\varphi_1$
- $\delta v_C = 6\,\delta\varphi_1$
- $\delta v_C = 0$
- $\delta v_C = -4\,\delta\varphi_1$

## Explanation

Parallel-link support prevents the section $BC$ from rotating and moving horizontally, so $BC$ can only move vertically, and its $C_2$ center is the point at infinity of horizontal lines. The $C_1 = A$, $C_{12} = B$ and $C_2$ centers therefore all stand on the horizontal line $y = 0$, so the structure is unstable even though it has $6$ scalar constraints for $6$ degrees of freedom. As the point of $AB$, which rotates about $A$, the hinge $B$ moves by $\delta u_B = 0$ and $\delta v_B = 4\,\delta\varphi_1$. The hinge imposes the same displacement at point $B$ as the segment $BC$, so the whole $BC$ segment moves upwards of $4\,\delta\varphi_1$ and $\delta v_C = 4\,\delta\varphi_1$. The value $6\,\delta\varphi_1$ is obtained by rotating the whole structure around $A$, and the value $0$ attributing to parallel-link support also the block of vertical displacement.
