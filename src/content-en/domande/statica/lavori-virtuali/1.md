The beam in the figure has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ with $AB = 5\,\mathrm{m}$, and an overhang $BC = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. A uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts along $AB$, and a force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward at $C$. Using the principle of virtual work, find the vertical reaction $V_A$ at the pin.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="C,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam with $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $AB$ and $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

- $19\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $11\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $11\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward

## Explanation

Replace the pin at $A$ with a roller that can slide vertically. The beam then rotates about $B$ through $\delta\varphi$, with $\delta v_A = -5\,\delta\varphi$ and $\delta v_C = 2\,\delta\varphi$. The resultant $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ of the uniform load acts at $x = 2.5\,\mathrm{m}$, where $\delta v = -2.5\,\delta\varphi$. The principle of virtual work gives
$$
V_A \cdot (-5\,\delta\varphi) + 30 \cdot 2.5\,\delta\varphi - 10 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi = 0 ,
$$
so $V_A = \dfrac{75 - 20}{5} = 11\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. The value $19\,\mathrm{kN}$ comes from using the wrong sign for the work done by $F$. During rotation about $B$, point $C$ moves in the direction opposite to $A$ and to the load on $AB$.
