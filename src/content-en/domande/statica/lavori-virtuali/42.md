The portal frame in the figure has a pin at $A = (0, 0)$, the $4\,\mathrm{m}$ high bars, the crossbeam $DE$ with $D = (0, 4)$ and $E = (4, 4)$ and at $B = (4, 0)$ a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane (coordinates in meters). At $D$ the horizontal force $F = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Using virtual work, what is the bending moment at midpoint $S$ of the crossbeam?

- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension
- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the top fibers in tension
- $5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension
- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension

## Explanation

You insert a hinge at $S$. Body $ADS$ rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi_s$; The center of the body $SEB$ is on the vertical through $B$ and the straight for $A$ and $S$, of equation $y = 2x$, then in $(4, 8)$. The point $S$ has the same displacement on the two bodies, $\delta v_S = 2\,\delta\varphi_s = (2 - 4)\,\delta\varphi_d$, therefore $\delta\varphi_d = -\delta\varphi_s$ and $\Delta\varphi = 2\,\delta\varphi_s$. The point $D$ moves horizontally by $\delta u_D = -4\,\delta\varphi_s$, then $\delta L = 5 \cdot (-4\,\delta\varphi_s) = -20\,\delta\varphi_s$ and
$$
M = -\frac{-20\,\delta\varphi_s}{2\,\delta\varphi_s} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The moment is positive, so the lower fibers of the crossbeam are in tension. The value $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained using $\delta\varphi_s$ instead of the relative rotation $\Delta\varphi$.
