The cantilever truss in the figure has the fixed $A = (0, 0)$ and $D = (0, 2)$ hinges on the wall and the $B = (2, 0)$ and $C = (2, 2)$ nodes, connected by the $AB$, $BC$, $CD$ rods and the diagonal $AC$ (coordinates in meters). In the $C$ node, the force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Using virtual work, what is the axial force in diagonal $AC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,2,2;D,0,2" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;A-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera,180;D,cerniera,180" forze="C,0,-1.2,P" quote="A,B,2 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.7" titolo="Cantilever truss with $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at joint $C$"}

- $10\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN}$, in compression
- $10\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, in compression
- $5\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN}$, in compression

## Explanation

Remove diagonal $AC$ and apply at $C$ the tensile force $N$ directed toward $A$. At $A$ the opposite force does no work because $A$ is fixed. The remaining members $AB$, $BC$, and $CD$ form a four-bar linkage. Member $AB$ rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi$, member $CD$ rotates about $D$, and joints $B$ and $C$ move vertically. Vertical member $BC$ retains its length, so $B$ and $C$ have the same displacement $\delta v = 2\,\delta\varphi$ and member $BC$ translates. The force at $C$ has vertical component $-\dfrac{N}{\sqrt{2}}$, so the principle of virtual work gives
$$
-\frac{N}{\sqrt{2}} \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi - 10 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi = 0 ,
\qquad
N = -10\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The force is negative, so the diagonal is compressed with $10\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the only vertical component of the force, and the value $5\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by multiplying by $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ instead of dividing.
