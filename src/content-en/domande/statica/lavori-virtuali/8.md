## Explanation

You insert an axial parallel-link support into a section $S$ of the column $EB$. The roller prevents vertical displacement at $B$, so segment $SB$ has no vertical displacement. The rest of the portal frame rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi$. The top face of $S$ moves vertically by $5\,\delta\varphi$, so the column lengthens a distance $S$ from $5\,\delta\varphi$. The resultant load, $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 2.5\,\mathrm{m}$, moves $\delta v = 2.5\,\delta\varphi$ and point $D$ of $\delta u_D = -3\,\delta\varphi$, then
$$
\delta L = -20 \cdot 2.5\,\delta\varphi + 5 \cdot (-3\,\delta\varphi) = -65\,\delta\varphi ,
\qquad
N_{EB} = \frac{\delta L}{5\,\delta\varphi} = -13\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
of compression. The value $-10\,\mathrm{kN}$ is achieved by neglecting the work of the force $F$.
