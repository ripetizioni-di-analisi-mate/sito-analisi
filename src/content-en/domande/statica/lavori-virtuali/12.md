## Explanation

The structure has two bodies and five scalar constraints, and is a mechanism with $C_1 = A$, $C_{12} = B$ and $C_2 = C$. The displacement of $B$ is the same on the two segments, $4\,\delta\varphi_1 = (4 - 6)\,\delta\varphi_2$, then $\delta\varphi_2 = -2\,\delta\varphi_1$. Then the point of application of $F_1$ moves by $2\,\delta\varphi_1$ and the point $D$ by $\delta v_D = (9 - 6)\,\delta\varphi_2 = -6\,\delta\varphi_1$. With $F_D$ positive down the principle of virtual work gives
$$
-9 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi_1 - F_D \cdot (-6\,\delta\varphi_1) = 0 ,
\qquad
F_D = 3\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
Downward. The value $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained with $\delta\varphi_2 = -\delta\varphi_1$, and $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the force that the segment $AB$ transmits at $B$.
