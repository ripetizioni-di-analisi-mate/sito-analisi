The Gerber beam in the figure has a pin at $A$, a roller support at $B$, the internal hinge $C$ and a roller support at $D$. To find only the vertical component $Y_C$ of the force transmitted at $C$ by virtual work, which internal connection must replace hinge $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,D,4 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with internal hinge $C$"}

- An internal parallel-link connection with horizontal links
- An internal two-force link with a vertical axis
- An internal two-force link with a horizontal axis
- An internal parallel-link connection with vertical links

## Explanation

The internal hinge prevents the relative horizontal and vertical displacement of the two sections, and transmits $X_C$ and $Y_C$. Because you only work $Y_C$, you remove the only constraint on the relative vertical displacement and keep the horizontal one. The internal two-force link with the horizontal pin-ended link prevents relative sliding along the pin-ended link and leaves vertical sliding and relative rotation free, so it only transmits $X_C$. Parallel-link support with the horizontal links would also prevent relative rotation, i.e. it would add a constraint that the hinge does not have, and the two-force link with the vertical pin-ended link would retain just $Y_C$.
