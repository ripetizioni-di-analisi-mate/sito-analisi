The virtual shifts of the horizontal beam in the figure are rotations $\delta\varphi$ around the center $C = (0, 6)$ (coordinates in meters). What is the magnitude of the displacement of its point $P = (8, 0)$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;P,8,0" aste="A-P" punti="0,6,C,centro" tratteggi="0,6 8,0" quote="A,P,8 m,0.9" titolo="Beam rotating about center $C = (0, 6)$"}

- $8\,|\delta\varphi|$
- $6\,|\delta\varphi|$
- $10\,|\delta\varphi|$
- $14\,|\delta\varphi|$

## Explanation

With the rotation formulas around $C$ you have $\delta u_P = -\delta\varphi\,(0 - 6) = 6\,\delta\varphi$ and $\delta v_P = \delta\varphi\,(8 - 0) = 8\,\delta\varphi$. The magnitude is therefore $\sqrt{6^2 + 8^2}\,|\delta\varphi| = 10\,|\delta\varphi|$, i.e. the distance $CP$ multiplied by the absolute value of the rotation. The displacement is perpendicular to the segment $CP$, because the vector $(6, 8)$ has produced zero scalar with $(8, -6)$. The value $8\,|\delta\varphi|$ is the only vertical component, and $14\,|\delta\varphi|$ is the sum of the two components.
