The beam $AB$ in the figure, with $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (6, 0)$ (coordinates in meters), has at $A$ a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane and at $B$ a roller support on an inclined plane of $45^\circ$; its virtual shifts are $\delta\varphi$ rotations around the center $C = (0, 6)$. They act the force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ down at $E = (3, 0)$ and the force $H = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ left at $B$. What is the virtual work of the loads?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;E,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello,315" forze="E,0,-1.5,P;B,-1.5,0,H" punti="0,6,C,centro" quote="A,E,3 m,0.9;E,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with center of rotation $C = (0, 6)$, $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $H = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $-30\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$
- $54\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$
- $-54\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$

## Explanation

For a rotation around $C = (0, 6)$ the beam points move by $\delta u = -\delta\varphi\,(y - 6)$ and $\delta v = \delta\varphi\,x$. On the beam $y = 0$, then $\delta u = 6\,\delta\varphi$, the point $E$ moves by $(6\,\delta\varphi, 3\,\delta\varphi)$ and the point $B$ by $(6\,\delta\varphi, 6\,\delta\varphi)$. The force $P$ does work $-10 \cdot 3\,\delta\varphi$ and the force $H$, with horizontal component $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$, does work $-4 \cdot 6\,\delta\varphi$. Therefore
$$
\delta L = -30\,\delta\varphi - 24\,\delta\varphi = -54\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi ,
$$
equal to the moment of loads about $C$ multiplied by $\delta\varphi$. The value $-6$ is obtained by the sign of $\delta u$ exchanged.
