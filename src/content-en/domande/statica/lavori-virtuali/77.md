Beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A = (0, 0)$ and a roller at $B = (6, 0)$ on an inclined surface. The roller reaction acts along unit vector $\left( -\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5} \right)$. At $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ the force $F = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Using virtual work, what is the axial force at section $S$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;E,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,323.13" forze="E,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,E,2 m,0.9;E,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with an inclined roller at $B$ and $F = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $-16\,\mathrm{kN}$, in compression
- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN}$, in compression
- $0$

## Explanation

Introduce an axial sliding connection at $S$. The two segments rotate through the same angle $\delta\varphi$ about $A$, and segment $SB$ also slides along the axis of $\delta w_d$. The displacement of $B$ is then $(\delta w_d, 6\,\delta\varphi)$, and the roller support requires it to be perpendicular to the reaction: $-\dfrac{4}{5}\,\delta w_d + \dfrac{3}{5} \cdot 6\,\delta\varphi = 0$, then $\delta w_d = \dfrac{9}{2}\,\delta\varphi$. The point of application of $F$ is raised by $4\,\delta\varphi$, so $F$ does the $-72\,\delta\varphi$ work; because the segment $AS$ does not travel you have $\Delta w = -\delta w_d$. Then $N \cdot \left( -\dfrac{9}{2}\,\delta\varphi \right) - 72\,\delta\varphi = 0$, i.e. $N = -16\,\mathrm{kN}$, compression. The segment $SB$ is compressed by the horizontal component of the reaction at $B$, which equals $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left; its vertical component equals $12\,\mathrm{kN}$.
