Beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, rests at $A$ and $B$ on rollers that travel on parallel inclined surfaces in the direction of unit vector $\left( \dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5} \right)$. At the midpoint, the $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ weight acts. Let $\delta s$ denote the virtual displacement of $A$ along its support surface, positive in the direction of the unit vector. What is the virtual work of the weight?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;M,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,306.87;B,carrello,306.87" forze="M,0,-1.5,P" quote="A,M,3 m,0.9;M,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Beam supported by rollers on two parallel inclined surfaces"}

- $-6\,\mathrm{kN} \cdot \delta s$
- $-8\,\mathrm{kN} \cdot \delta s$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kN} \cdot \delta s$
- $0$

## Explanation

Each roller support leaves at its point only the parallel displacement to the plane, so the center of rotation lies on the normal to the plane conducted by $A$ and that conducted by $B$. The two normals are parallel and distinct, so the center is their point at infinity and the virtual displacement is a translation. Each point on the beam then moves as $A$, of $\delta s\,\left( \dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5} \right)$, and the midpoint rises by $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\delta s$. The weight is directed downward, so it does the $-10 \cdot \dfrac{3}{5}\,\delta s = -6\,\mathrm{kN} \cdot \delta s$ work. The value $-8$ uses the horizontal component of the unit vector. The value $-10$ treats the displacement as vertical, and $0$ treats it as horizontal.
