The simply supported beam in the figure has $AB = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ span, a hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $B$. At $A$, the $\mathcal{M}_1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise couple acts, at $B$, the $\mathcal{M}_2 = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise couple. Using virtual work, what is the bending moment at midpoint $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="A,1,ℳ_1;B,-1,ℳ_2" quote="A,S,3 m,0.9;S,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam under opposite couples at its ends"}

- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension
- $-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the top fibers in tension
- $0$
- $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the top fibers in tension

## Explanation

Introduce a hinge at $S$. Segment $AS$ rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi_s$, and segment $SB$ rotates about $B$ through angle $\delta\varphi_d$, with $3\,\delta\varphi_s = -3\,\delta\varphi_d$, so $\Delta\varphi = \delta\varphi_s - \delta\varphi_d = 2\,\delta\varphi_s$. The counterclockwise couple at $A$ does the $10\,\delta\varphi_s$ work, the clockwise couple at $B$ does the $-10\,\delta\varphi_d = 10\,\delta\varphi_s$ work. Then $M \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi_s + 20\,\delta\varphi_s = 0$, i.e. $M = -10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers stretched. The two couples balance each other, so the reactions are zero and the moment equals $-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in each section of the beam.
