An internal hinge is inserted at a section to determine a bending moment. The virtual rotations just to its left and right are both $0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$ counterclockwise. Can this motion determine the internal moment from virtual work?

- Yes, using the sum of the rotations.
- No, the relative rotation is zero.
- Yes, using the product of the rotations.
- Yes, because the two faces carry equal moments in the same direction.

## Explanation

The face moments are opposite, so their work is proportional to the difference in rotations. Here that difference vanishes and the unknown drops out of the equation. A motion with nonzero relative rotation is needed.
