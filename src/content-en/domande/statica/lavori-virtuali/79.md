A triangular load, zero at the pin $A$ and equal to $q_0 = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at the roller $B$, acts on a simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$. Using the principle of virtual work, what bending moment is obtained at midspan $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0,0.9,q_0" quote="A,S,3 m,0.9;S,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam with a triangular load increasing towards $B$"}

- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, lower fibres in tension
- $13.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, lower fibres in tension
- $27\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, lower fibres in tension
- $-13.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, upper fibres in tension

## Explanation

A hinge is inserted at $S$; segment $AS$ rotates about $A$ by $\delta\varphi_s$ and segment $SB$ about $B$ by $\delta\varphi_d$, with $3\,\delta\varphi_s = -3\,\delta\varphi_d$, so the relative rotation is $\Delta\varphi = 2\,\delta\varphi_s$. The vertical displacement is $x\,\delta\varphi_s$ on $AS$ and $(6 - x)\,\delta\varphi_s$ on $SB$, while the load is $q(x) = x$ ($\mathrm{kN/m}$, with $x$ in metres from $A$). The load is directed downwards, therefore its work is
$$
\delta L = -\delta\varphi_s \left( \int_0^3 x^2\,dx + \int_3^6 x\,(6 - x)\,dx \right) = -(9 + 18)\,\delta\varphi_s = -27\,\delta\varphi_s .
$$
The principle of virtual work gives $M \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi_s - 27\,\delta\varphi_s = 0$, so $M = 13.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the **lower fibres in tension**. The value $27\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ ignores that the relative rotation is $2\,\delta\varphi_s$, and the value $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the contribution of the reaction $V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ alone, without the load to the left of $S$.
