The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at $A$. The uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on the entire length, and in the free end $B$ the counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Using virtual work, what is the vertical reaction $V_A$ at the fixed support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.4,0.4,q" coppie="B,1,ℳ" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Cantilever with $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $B$"}

- $14.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward

## Explanation

It replaces fixed support with a parallel-link support with horizontal links, which prevents horizontal translation and rotation and leaves $A$'s vertical displacement free. The cantilever can then only translate vertically of $\delta v$, equal for all its points, and the displacement conjugated to $V_A$ is $\delta v_A = \delta v$. The load has resultant $3 \cdot 4 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ down and does the $-12\,\delta v$ work. The couple does the $\mathcal{M}\,\delta\varphi$ work, which is zero because the cantilever does not rotate. Then $V_A\,\delta v - 12\,\delta v = 0$, i.e. $V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, upwards. The value $14.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by adding the $\dfrac{\mathcal{M}}{L}$ ratio to the load, as if the couple were doing work in the translation.
