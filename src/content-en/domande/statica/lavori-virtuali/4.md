## Explanation

At the midpoint $S$, a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, introduce a parallel-link connection with its links parallel to the axis. Segment $AS$ rotates about $A$ and segment $SB$ about $B$, both through angle $\delta\varphi$. Then $\delta v_s = 5\,\delta\varphi$ and $\delta v_d = (5 - 10)\,\delta\varphi = -5\,\delta\varphi$, then $\Delta v = \delta v_d - \delta v_s = -10\,\delta\varphi$. The force $F_1$ is on $AS$, where $\delta v = 3\,\delta\varphi$, and the force $F_2$ is on $SB$, where $\delta v = (7 - 10)\,\delta\varphi = -3\,\delta\varphi$. Therefore
$$
\delta L = -20 \cdot 3\,\delta\varphi + 10 \cdot 3\,\delta\varphi = -30\,\delta\varphi ,
\qquad
T = -\frac{\delta L}{\Delta v} = -\frac{-30\,\delta\varphi}{-10\,\delta\varphi} = -3\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The value $17\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the reaction $V_A$, i.e. the shear force to the left of $F_1$.
