The beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, rotates about the hinge $A$ of an $\delta\varphi$ virtual rotation, positive counterclockwise. A $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ acts the clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. What is the virtual work of the couple?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera" coppie="4 0,-1,ℳ" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.9;4 0,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Beam rotating about $A$ under clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$"}

- $-60\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$
- $-15\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$
- $0$

## Explanation

The virtual work of a couple applied to an $\delta\varphi$ rotating body is $\mathcal{M}\,\delta\varphi$, with $\mathcal{M}$ positive counterclockwise. The couple is clockwise, so in the convention it equals $-15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and its work is $-15\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$. The work depends only on the rotation of the body to which the couple is applied, so the distance of $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ does not enter the calculation. The value $-60$ is obtained by multiplying the couple by that distance, as is done by a force.
