The structure in the figure is formed by the section $AB$, with a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$, and the section $BC$, connected to $AB$ by the internal hinge $B = (4, 0)$ and resting at $C = (6, 0)$ on a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane (coordinates in meters). Over the entire length $AC$ the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts. The segment $AB$ rotates $\delta\varphi_1$, positive counterclockwise. What is the virtual work of the load?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Mechanism under uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on both segments"}

- $-36\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$
- $-16\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$
- $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$

## Explanation

The segment $BC$ rotates about $C$, and the point $B$ has the same displacement on the two segments: $4\,\delta\varphi_1 = (4 - 6)\,\delta\varphi_2$, then $\delta\varphi_2 = -2\,\delta\varphi_1$. The two sections have different displacements, so the load is divided into two parts, one by one. On $AB$ the resultant is $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 2$, which rises by $2\,\delta\varphi_1$; on $BC$ is $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 5$, which rises by $\delta\varphi_2\,(5 - 6) = 2\,\delta\varphi_1$. Then $\delta L = -8 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi_1 - 4 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi_1 = -24\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$. The overall result of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 3$, treated as if the beam were a single body rotating around $A$, would give $-36$; the only load on $AB$ gives $-16$.
