The frame in the figure is formed by the $AD$ and $BE$ columns and the crossbeam $DE$, with $A = (0, 0)$, $D = (0, 4)$, $E = (4, 4)$ and $B = (4, 0)$ (coordinates in meters). At $A$ and $B$ there are pin supports, at $D$ and at $E$ internal hinges. At point $G = (0, 2)$, the horizontal force $Q = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What horizontal force $F$ at $E$ holds the structure in equilibrium?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;G,0,2;D,0,4;E,4,4;B,4,0" aste="A-D;D-E;E-B" cerniere="D;E" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="G,1.2,0,Q" quote="A,G,2 m,0.8;G,D,2 m,0.8;D,E,4 m,-0.6" titolo="Four-hinge frame under force $Q = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $G$"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the right
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the left
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the left
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the left

## Explanation

Column $AD$ rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi_1$, and column $BE$ rotates about $B$ through angle $\delta\varphi_3$. Points $D$ and $E$ therefore move only horizontally, by $-4\,\delta\varphi_1$ and $-4\,\delta\varphi_3$, respectively. The crossbeam is rigid and its extremes do not move vertically, so it moves horizontally and $\delta\varphi_3 = \delta\varphi_1$. The point $G$ moves by $\delta u_G = -2\,\delta\varphi_1$ and the point $E$ by $\delta u_E = -4\,\delta\varphi_1$. The principle of virtual work gives
$$
6 \cdot (-2\,\delta\varphi_1) + F \cdot (-4\,\delta\varphi_1) = 0 ,
\qquad
F = -3\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
i.e. $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. The value $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by neglecting the difference between the $G$ and $E$ elevations.
