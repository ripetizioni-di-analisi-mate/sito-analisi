The simply supported beam in the figure has $AB = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ span, a hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $B$; at the midpoint $S$ the counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts. Using virtual work, what is the bending moment immediately to the right of $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="S,1,ℳ" quote="A,S,3 m,0.9;S,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam under counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $S$"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the top fibers in tension
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the top fibers in tension
- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension

## Explanation

You insert a hinge at $S$; The $AS$ and $SB$ sections rotates about $A$ and $B$, with $3\,\delta\varphi_s = -3\,\delta\varphi_d$, then $\Delta\varphi = 2\,\delta\varphi_s$. For the section immediately to the right of $S$ the couple acts on the left section and does the $12\,\delta\varphi_s$ work. Therefore
$$
M = -\frac{12\,\delta\varphi_s}{2\,\delta\varphi_s} = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
with the upper fibers stretched. Immediately to the left of $S$ the couple acts on the right segment, does the $12\,\delta\varphi_d = -12\,\delta\varphi_s$ work and the moment equals $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, so the diagram has at $S$ an $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ jump.
