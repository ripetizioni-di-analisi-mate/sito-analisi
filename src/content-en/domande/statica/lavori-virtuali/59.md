On a rigid body free plane acts a system of forces and couples. Its virtual work is zero for each translation and equals $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$ for the rotation $\delta\varphi$ around the origin $O$, positive counterclockwise. What is the system equivalent to?

- A force of magnitude $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ with a line of action through $O$
- A counterclockwise couple of magnitude $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- To a clockwise couple of $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- A force system in equilibrium

## Explanation

By the theorem on virtual work on a rigid body $\delta L = R_x\,\delta u_O + R_y\,\delta v_O + M_O\,\delta\varphi$. The work is zero for each translation, so $R_x = R_y = 0$. For rotation around $O$ the work equals $M_O\,\delta\varphi = 12\,\delta\varphi$, therefore $M_O = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, positive and therefore counterclockwise. A system with resultant null is equivalent to a couple, the moment of which is the same as at each reference point. The system is not balanced, because its work is not null for every rigid-body displacement.
