On a flat rigid plate acts a system of forces and couples with resultant components $R_x = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $R_y = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and with resultant moment about the origin $O$ equal to $M_O = -10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, i.e. clockwise moment. The $\delta\varphi$ wheel plate, positive counterclockwise, around the center $C = (2, 1)$ (coordinates in meters). What is the virtual work of the force system?

- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$
- $-26\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$
- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$

## Explanation

In rotation about $C$ the point $O$ moves by $\delta u_O = -\delta\varphi\,(0 - 1) = \delta\varphi$ and $\delta v_O = \delta\varphi\,(0 - 2) = -2\,\delta\varphi$. By the theorem on virtual work on a rigid body
$$
\delta L = R_x\,\delta u_O + R_y\,\delta v_O + M_O\,\delta\varphi = 4\,\delta\varphi + 12\,\delta\varphi - 10\,\delta\varphi = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi .
$$
The same value is the moment of the system about the center, $M_C = M_O + (x_O - x_C)\,R_y - (y_O - y_C)\,R_x = -10 + 12 + 4 = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, multiplied by $\delta\varphi$. The value $-10$ neglects the displacement of $O$, the value $16$ neglects the moment $M_O$, and $-26$ is obtained with the reverse moment transport sign.
