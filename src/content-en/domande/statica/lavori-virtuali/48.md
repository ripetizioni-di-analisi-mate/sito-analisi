The beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$; $B$'s roller support has been removed, and $M$ is the midpoint. Which three vertical displacements, expressed in terms of the infinitesimal parameter $\delta\varphi$, form a virtual displacement compatible with the hinge?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;M,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera" quote="A,M,3 m,0.9;M,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Beam pinned at $A$ with its roller at $B$ removed"}

- $\delta v_A = 0$, $\delta v_M = 6\,\delta\varphi$, $\delta v_B = 6\,\delta\varphi$
- $\delta v_A = 2\,\delta\varphi$, $\delta v_M = 4\,\delta\varphi$, $\delta v_B = 6\,\delta\varphi$
- $\delta v_A = 0$, $\delta v_M = -3\,\delta\varphi$, $\delta v_B = 6\,\delta\varphi$
- $\delta v_A = 0$, $\delta v_M = 3\,\delta\varphi$, $\delta v_B = 6\,\delta\varphi$

## Explanation

A virtual beam displacement is rigid, so the vertical displacement varies linearly along the axis: $\delta v(x) = \delta v_A + \delta\varphi\,x$, with $\delta\varphi$ rotating the beam. The hinge $A$ imposes $\delta v_A = 0$, therefore $\delta v(x) = \delta\varphi\,x$, and then $\delta v_M = 3\,\delta\varphi$ and $\delta v_B = 6\,\delta\varphi$. The back with $\delta v_A = 2\,\delta\varphi$ is rigid but shifts the point of the hinge. The triples with $\delta v_M = 6\,\delta\varphi$ and $\delta v_M = -3\,\delta\varphi$ do not vary linearly along the axis, so they would deform the beam.
