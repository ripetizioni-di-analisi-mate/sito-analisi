The beam shown rests at $A$ and $B$ on two roller supports that travel with friction on a horizontal plane: in addition to the vertical reactions $V_A$ and $V_B$, the roller supports exert the horizontal forces of friction $T_A$ and $T_B$, positive to the right. For the virtual horizontal translation $\delta u$ of the beam, what work do the support reactions do?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello" reazioni="A,0,1.2,V_A;A,1.2,0,T_A;B,0,1.2,V_B;B,1.2,0,T_B" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9" titolo="Beam on two rollers with friction"}

- $(T_A + T_B)\,\delta u$
- $0$, because the support reactions do no work
- $(V_A + V_B)\,\delta u$
- $(T_A - T_B)\,\delta u$

## Explanation

In the horizontal translation each point of the beam moves $\delta u$ horizontally and $0$ vertically, so the displacement respects the two roller supports. The $V_A$ and $V_B$ reactions are perpendicular to the displacement, so they do no work. The friction forces are parallel to the displacement and perform the $T_A\,\delta u + T_B\,\delta u$ work. The null-work theorem of reactions requires smooth constraints, and a roller support with friction is not smooth; for this the friction forces remain in the virtual work equation.
