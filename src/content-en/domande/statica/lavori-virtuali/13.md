The beam shown has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$. At $A$ a parallel-link support with horizontal links restrains horizontal translation and rotation. At $B$ a roller moves along a horizontal surface. A uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the entire beam. Using virtual work, what is the reaction moment $M_A$ at the parallel-link support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with a parallel-link support at $A$ and a roller at $B$, with $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $50\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $50\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $100\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $0$

## Explanation

To isolate $M_A$ you leave the rotation free at $A$, so it remains a roller support that moves vertically, and the point $A$ moves only vertically. The center of rotation therefore lies on the horizontal through $A$ and the vertical through $B$, at their intersection $B$. The beam rotates about $B$ through angle $\delta\varphi$, the displacement conjugate to $M_A$. The resultant load, $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 2.5\,\mathrm{m}$, moves by $\delta v = (2.5 - 5)\,\delta\varphi = -2.5\,\delta\varphi$. Therefore
$$
M_A\,\delta\varphi + 20 \cdot 2.5\,\delta\varphi = 0 ,
\qquad
M_A = -50\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
i.e. $50\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in clockwise. The value $100\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment of $R_B = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ about $A$, without the contribution of the load.
