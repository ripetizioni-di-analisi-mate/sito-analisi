The angular lever in the figure rotates about the hinge $O = (0, 0)$ and is formed by the horizontal $OA$ arm, with $A = (3, 0)$, and the vertical $OB$ arm, with $B = (0, 2)$ (coordinates in meters). At $A$, the force $P = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What horizontal force at $B$ holds the lever in equilibrium?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,3,0;B,0,2" aste="O-A;O-B" vincoli="O,cerniera" forze="A,0,-1.2,P" quote="O,A,3 m,0.9;O,B,2 m,-0.8" titolo="Bell crank under force $P = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $A$"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the right
- $\dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the left
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the left
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the left

## Explanation

The lever has $3$ degrees of freedom, and the pin at $O$ removes $2$ of them. The resulting mechanism rotates about $O$ through angle $\delta\varphi$. The point $A$ rises by $3\,\delta\varphi$ and the point $B$ moves horizontally by $-2\,\delta\varphi$. With $H$ positive to the right the principle of virtual work gives $-8 \cdot 3\,\delta\varphi + H \cdot (-2\,\delta\varphi) = 0$, i.e. $H = -12\,\mathrm{kN}$: the force equals $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. The value $\dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by exchanging the two arms.
