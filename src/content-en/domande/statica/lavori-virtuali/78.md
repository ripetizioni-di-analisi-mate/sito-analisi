The three-hinged arch in the figure is formed by the straight $AC$ and $CB$ rods, with the pin support $A = (0, 0)$, the internal hinge $C = (3, 2)$ and at $B = (6, 0)$ a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane; tie $AB$ connects the two springings (coordinates in meters). In the hinge $C$, the force $F = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Using virtual work, what is the axial force in the tie?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,2;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" catene="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.4,F" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9;3 0,C,2 m,0.5" titolo="Three-hinged tied arch with $F = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ at its crown"}

- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, in compression

## Explanation

Remove the tie and apply its forces $N$ at the springings, taking tension as positive; at $B$ the force equals $N$ and is directed towards $A$. Without the tie the arch is a mechanism with $C_1 = A$ and $C_{12} = C$, and $C_2$ lies on the vertical through $B$ and on the line $AC$, of equation $y = \dfrac{2}{3}\,x$, then $C_2 = (6, 4)$. The horizontal displacement of $C$ equals $-2\,\delta\varphi_1$ on the $AC$ and $-(2 - 4)\,\delta\varphi_2$ body on the body $CB$, therefore $\delta\varphi_2 = -\delta\varphi_1$ and $\delta u_B = -\delta\varphi_2\,(0 - 4) = -4\,\delta\varphi_1$. The point $C$ rises by $3\,\delta\varphi_1$, so the principle of virtual work gives $-N \cdot (-4\,\delta\varphi_1) - 16 \cdot 3\,\delta\varphi_1 = 0$, i.e. $N = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, traction. The value coincides with $\dfrac{FL}{4f}$, where $L = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ is the span and $f = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ the arrow; $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ corresponds to $\dfrac{FL}{2f}$.
