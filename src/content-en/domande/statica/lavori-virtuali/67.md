The $AB$ rigid ladder in the figure has its foot on a hinge $A = (0, 0)$ and the extreme $B = (4, 3)$ leaning against a smooth vertical wall, schematized as a roller support that moves vertically (coordinates in meters). At the midpoint of the ladder, the $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ weight acts. Using virtual work, what is the wall reaction?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;M,2,1.5;B,4,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" forze="M,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,4 0,4 m,-0.6;4 0,B,3 m,-0.6" titolo="Ladder pinned at the base with smooth contact against the wall at $B$"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the left
- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the left
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the left
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the right

## Explanation

Remove the support at $B$. The ladder rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi$, and $B$ moves horizontally by $\delta u_B = -\delta\varphi \cdot 3 = -3\,\delta\varphi$. The wall reaction is horizontal; positive grip to the left, does the $R_B \cdot 3\,\delta\varphi$ work. The midpoint with horizontal coordinate $2$, so it rises by $2\,\delta\varphi$ and the weight does the $-12 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi$ work. Then $3\,R_B\,\delta\varphi - 24\,\delta\varphi = 0$, i.e. $R_B = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the left. Dividing by the horizontal coordinate of $B$ instead of its elevation would get $6\,\mathrm{kN}$.
