The body $L$ in the figure is formed by the column $AD$, with $A = (0, 0)$ and $D = (0, 4)$, and the arm $DE$, with $E = (3, 4)$ (coordinates in meters). The body rotates about the hinge $A$ of an $\delta\varphi$ virtual rotation, positive counterclockwise. What is the horizontal displacement $\delta u_E$ of point $E$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;E,3,4" aste="A-D;D-E" vincoli="A,cerniera" quote="A,D,4 m,-0.8;D,E,3 m,-0.6" titolo="Body shaped like $L$ with a hinge at $A$"}

- $\delta u_E = -4\,\delta\varphi$
- $\delta u_E = 4\,\delta\varphi$
- $\delta u_E = -3\,\delta\varphi$
- $\delta u_E = 3\,\delta\varphi$

## Explanation

The center of rotation is $A$, so the points of the body move by $\delta u = -\delta\varphi\,(y - y_A)$ and $\delta v = \delta\varphi\,(x - x_A)$. The point $E$ has $y = 4$, therefore $\delta u_E = -4\,\delta\varphi$, and for $\delta\varphi > 0$ the point $E$ moves to the left. The horizontal displacement depends only on the point's share relative to the center. The value $3\,\delta\varphi$ is the vertical displacement $\delta v_E$.
