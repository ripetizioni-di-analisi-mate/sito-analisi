## Explanation

You insert a hinge in the midpoint $S$, at $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $D$. Segment $DS$ rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi_s$, and segment $SE$ rotates about $B$ through angle $\delta\varphi_d$; the displacement of $S$ gives $3\,\delta\varphi_s = -3\,\delta\varphi_d$, then $\Delta\varphi = 2\,\delta\varphi_s$. The ends move by $\delta v_D = (0 - 2)\,\delta\varphi_s = -2\,\delta\varphi_s$ and $\delta v_E = (10 - 8)\,\delta\varphi_d = -2\,\delta\varphi_s$, while the resulting two of the load, $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 3.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x = 6.5\,\mathrm{m}$, both move by $1.5\,\delta\varphi_s$. Therefore
$$
\delta L = 10 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi_s + 10 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi_s - 6 \cdot 1.5\,\delta\varphi_s - 6 \cdot 1.5\,\delta\varphi_s = 22\,\delta\varphi_s ,
\qquad
M = -\frac{22\,\delta\varphi_s}{2\,\delta\varphi_s} = -11\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
with the upper fibers stretched. The value $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment on the supports, without the contribution of the distributed load.
