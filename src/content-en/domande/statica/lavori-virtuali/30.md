The simply supported beam in the figure has $AB = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ span, a hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $B$. A $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ acts the $\mathcal{M} = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise couple. Using virtual work, what is the roller reaction $R_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="2 0,1,ℳ" quote="A,2 0,2 m,0.9;2 0,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam under counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward

## Explanation

Remove the roller. The beam rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi$, with $\delta v_B = 6\,\delta\varphi$. The couple does work $\mathcal{M}\,\delta\varphi = 18\,\delta\varphi$, whatever the point of the beam where it is applied. The principle of virtual work gives
$$
R_B \cdot 6\,\delta\varphi + 18\,\delta\varphi = 0 ,
\qquad
R_B = -3\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
i.e. $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ down. The value $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by multiplying the couple by the distance of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, as if it were a force.
