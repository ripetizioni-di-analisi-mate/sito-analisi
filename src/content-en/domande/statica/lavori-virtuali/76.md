The Gerber beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$, an internal hinge $C$, and a roller at $D$. Their respective horizontal coordinates are $0$, $5\,\mathrm{m}$, $7\,\mathrm{m}$, and $11\,\mathrm{m}$. A uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on segment $CD$. Using virtual work, what is the shear force at section $S$ with horizontal coordinate $6\,\mathrm{m}$ on overhang $BC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;S,6,0;C,7,0;D,11,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" carichi="C,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9;B,S,1 m,0.9;S,C,1 m,0.9;C,D,4 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on segment $CD$"}

- $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

It fits into $S$ a parallel-link support with the horizontal links. The segment $AS$ has the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, so it stays still; the segment $SC$ has the same rotation, nothing, and it translates vertically as $\delta v_d$, therefore $\Delta v = \delta v_d$. The section $CD$ rotates about $D$ and at $C$ moves by $\delta v_d$, so the center of the load, in $x = 9$, halfway between $C$ and $D$, moves by $\dfrac{\delta v_d}{2}$. The load has resultant $8\,\mathrm{kN}$, therefore $T\,\delta v_d - 8 \cdot \dfrac{\delta v_d}{2} = 0$, i.e. $T = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$. By giving the $CD$ all-round the $\delta v_d$ translation you would get $8\,\mathrm{kN}$.
