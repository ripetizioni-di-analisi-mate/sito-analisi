The $AB$ and $BC$ sections of the beam shown are connected at $B$ by an internal parallel-link connection with the horizontal links, which prevents relative rotation and horizontal relative sliding. Where is the relative center of rotation $C_{12}$ of the two segments?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C" interni="B,doppio-pendolo,0" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,4 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with an internal parallel-link connection at $B$"}

- At the point at infinity of horizontal lines
- At the point at infinity of vertical lines
- At point $B$
- It does not exist because the two segments have no relative motion

## Explanation

Parallel-link support prevents relative rotation, so the motion of the segment $BC$ about the segment $AB$ is a translation. Horizontal links only prevent relative displacement along their direction, so the permitted relative translation is vertical. A vertical translation is a rotation around a center at infinity in the direction perpendicular to the displacement, that is, in the horizontal direction. So $C_{12}$ is the **point at infinity of horizontal lines**, and in the first kinematic chain theorem the line passing through $C_1$ and $C_2$ must be horizontal.
