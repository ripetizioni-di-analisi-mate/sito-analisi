The portal frame in the figure has a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$, the internal hinge $C = (2, 4)$ on the crossbeam and at $B = (6, 0)$ a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane (coordinates in meters). The body 1 is the $ADC$ part and the body 2 is the $CEB$ part. Where is the absolute center of rotation $C_2$ of body 2?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;C,2,4;E,6,4;B,6,0" aste="A-D;D-C;C-E;E-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="D,C,2 m,-0.6;C,E,4 m,-0.6;E,B,4 m,-0.7" titolo="Portal frame with internal hinge $C$ and a roller at $B$"}

- $C_2 = (6, 6)$
- $C_2 = (6, 12)$
- $C_2 = (6, 4)$
- $C_2 = (6, 0)$

## Explanation

The body 1 rotates about $A$, then $C_1 = A$, and the relative center $C_{12}$ is the hinge $C$. The roller support only leaves $B$ with horizontal displacement, so $C_2$ lies on the vertical through $B$, the line $x = 6$. For the first theorem of the kinematic chains $C_1$, $C_2$ and $C_{12}$ are aligned, so $C_2$ also lies on the line through $A$ and $C$, of equation $y = 2x$. The intersection of the two lines is $C_2 = (6, 12)$. The point $(6, 6)$ is obtained using the bisector $y = x$ instead of the line $AC$.
