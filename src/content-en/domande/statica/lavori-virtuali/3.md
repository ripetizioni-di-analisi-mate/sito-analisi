- $54\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension
- $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension

## Explanation

Introduce a hinge at $S$. Segment $AS$ rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi_s$, and segment $SB$ rotates about $B$ through angle $\delta\varphi_d$. The point $S$ has the same displacement on the two segments, $3\,\delta\varphi_s = -3\,\delta\varphi_d$, then $\Delta\varphi = \delta\varphi_s - \delta\varphi_d = 2\,\delta\varphi_s$. On $AS$ the load ranges from $0$ to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and has resultant $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, where $\delta v = 2\,\delta\varphi_s$. On $SB$ the load is divided into a rectangle of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 4.5\,\mathrm{m}$, where $\delta v = 1.5\,\delta\varphi_s$, and a triangle of $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 5\,\mathrm{m}$, where $\delta v = \delta\varphi_s$. Therefore
$$
\delta L = -9 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi_s - 18 \cdot 1.5\,\delta\varphi_s - 9 \cdot 1\,\delta\varphi_s = -54\,\delta\varphi_s ,
\qquad
M_S = -\frac{\delta L}{\Delta\varphi} = 27\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
with the lower fibers stretched. The value $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by replacing the entire load with the resultant of $36\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, although the load extends over two sections that move differently.
