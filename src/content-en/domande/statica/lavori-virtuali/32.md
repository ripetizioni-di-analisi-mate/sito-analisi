A planar rigid body is bound, among other things, by a two-force link at point $P = (2, 3)$, with the pin-ended link directed as the $\vec{n} = \left( \dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5} \right)$ unit vector (coordinates in meters). The virtual displacement of the body is described by the vector $\vec{q} = (\delta u_O, \delta v_O, \delta\varphi)$, formed by the components of the displacement of the origin $O$ and the rotation, positive counterclockwise. The two-force link imposes the $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{q} = 0$ compatibility condition, where $\vec{a}$ is the corresponding line of the constraint matrix. What is $\vec{a}$?

- $\vec{a} = \left( \dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{17}{5} \right)$
- $\vec{a} = \left( \dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{1}{5} \right)$
- $\vec{a} = \left( \dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{1}{5} \right)$
- $\vec{a} = \left( \dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{1}{5} \right)$

## Explanation

The point $P$ moves by $\delta u_P = \delta u_O - 3\,\delta\varphi$ and $\delta v_P = \delta v_O + 2\,\delta\varphi$. The two-force link prevents $P$ from moving along the pin-ended link, so the move is compatible if and only if $\vec{n} \cdot \delta\vec{s}_P = 0$, i.e.
$$
\frac{3}{5}\,(\delta u_O - 3\,\delta\varphi) + \frac{4}{5}\,(\delta v_O + 2\,\delta\varphi) = \frac{3}{5}\,\delta u_O + \frac{4}{5}\,\delta v_O - \frac{1}{5}\,\delta\varphi = 0 .
$$
Therefore $\vec{a} = \left( \dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{1}{5} \right)$. The third component is the moment about $O$ of the direct unit force as $\vec{n}$ and applied at $P$, $2 \cdot \dfrac{4}{5} - 3 \cdot \dfrac{3}{5} = -\dfrac{1}{5}$; for this the reaction $r\,\vec{n}$ of the two-force link resulted and moment given by $r\,\vec{a}$. The third $\dfrac{1}{5}$ component is obtained by the sign of inverted rotation, $\dfrac{17}{5}$ by adding the two products instead of subtracting them, and the vector $\left( \dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{1}{5} \right)$ by exchanging the components of $\vec{n}$.
