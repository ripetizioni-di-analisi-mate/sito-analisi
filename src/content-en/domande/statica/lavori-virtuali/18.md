The beam $AB$ in the figure rotates about the hinge $A = (0, 0)$ of an $\delta\varphi$ virtual rotation, positive counterclockwise. At point $P = (4, 0)$, a force of $F_x = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $F_y = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$ components (coordinates in meters) acts. What is the virtual work of the force?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;P,4,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera" forze="P,0.9,-1.2,F" quote="A,P,4 m,0.9" titolo="Beam rotating about $A$ under force $F$ with components $F_x = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $F_y = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $-40\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$
- $-32\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$
- $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$

## Explanation

The point $P$ is at the elevation of the center $A$, then $\delta u_P = -\delta\varphi\,(0 - 0) = 0$ and $\delta v_P = 4\,\delta\varphi$. Virtual work is
$$
\delta L = F_x\,\delta u_P + F_y\,\delta v_P = 6 \cdot 0 + (-8) \cdot 4\,\delta\varphi = -32\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi .
$$
The horizontal component does not do work, because $P$ moves vertically. The value $-40\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$ is obtained by multiplying the $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ intensity of the force by $4\,\delta\varphi$.
