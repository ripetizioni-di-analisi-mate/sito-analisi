The portal frame in the figure has a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$, the high right $3\,\mathrm{m}$, the internal hinge $C = (3, 3)$ in the center of the crossbeam and at $B = (6, 0)$ a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane (coordinates in meters). At $G = (2, 3)$ the force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward, at $E = (6, 3)$ the force $H = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. The body $ADC$ rotates $\delta\varphi_1$, positive counterclockwise. What is the total virtual work of the two loads?

- $-42\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$
- $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$
- $-60\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$

## Explanation

Body $ADC$ rotates about $A$. The roller allows point $B$ to move only horizontally, so the center $C_2$ of body $CEB$ lies on the vertical through $B$. It must also lie on line $AC$, with equation $y = x$. Their intersection gives $C_2 = (6, 6)$. The displacement of joint $C$ is $(-3\,\delta\varphi_1, 3\,\delta\varphi_1)$ when calculated from the first body and $(3\,\delta\varphi_2, -3\,\delta\varphi_2)$ when calculated from the second, giving $\delta\varphi_2 = -\delta\varphi_1$. Point $G$ rises by $2\,\delta\varphi_1$, so force $F$ does work $-24\,\delta\varphi_1$. Point $E$ moves horizontally by $-\delta\varphi_2\,(3 - 6) = -3\,\delta\varphi_1$, so force $H$ does work $-18\,\delta\varphi_1$. The total virtual work is $-42\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi_1$. The answer $-6$ reverses the sign of $\delta u_E$. The answer $-24$ assumes that point $E$ has no horizontal displacement.
