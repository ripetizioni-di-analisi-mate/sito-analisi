The simply supported beam in the figure has $AB = 8\,\mathrm{m}$ span, a hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, and brings the force $F = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ down to point $S$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Using virtual work, what is the bending moment at $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="S,0,-1.8,F" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,B,6 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam with $F = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $S$"}

- $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension
- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the top fibers in tension

## Explanation

Introduce a hinge at $S$. Segment $AS$ rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi_s$, and segment $SB$ rotates about $B$ through angle $\delta\varphi_d$. The displacement of $S$ is the same on the two segments, $2\,\delta\varphi_s = (2 - 8)\,\delta\varphi_d$, then $\delta\varphi_d = -\dfrac{1}{3}\,\delta\varphi_s$ and $\Delta\varphi = \dfrac{4}{3}\,\delta\varphi_s$. The force is applied in the hinge, which moves by $2\,\delta\varphi_s$, so $\delta L = -32\,\delta\varphi_s$ and
$$
M_S = -\frac{\delta L}{\Delta\varphi} = \frac{32\,\delta\varphi_s}{\frac{4}{3}\,\delta\varphi_s} = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
putting the bottom fibers in tension. The value $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is $\dfrac{FL}{4}$, the midspan moment produced by a point load at midspan.
