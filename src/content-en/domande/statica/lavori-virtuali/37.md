The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller support at $B$ with $AB = 5\,\mathrm{m}$ and the overhang $BC = 1\,\mathrm{m}$; at $C$ the force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downwards. Using virtual work, what is the shear force at section $S$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,5,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.4,F" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,B,3 m,0.9;B,C,1 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam with $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

At $S$, introduce a parallel-link connection with its links parallel to the beam axis. Segment $AS$ rotates about $A$ and segment $SC$ about $B$, both through angle $\delta\varphi$. Thus $\delta v_s = 2\,\delta\varphi$, $\delta v_d = (2 - 5)\,\delta\varphi = -3\,\delta\varphi$, and $\Delta v = -5\,\delta\varphi$. Point $C$ moves by $\delta v_C = (6 - 5)\,\delta\varphi = \delta\varphi$, so $\delta L = -10\,\delta\varphi$ and
$$
T_S = -\frac{\delta L}{\Delta v} = -\frac{-10\,\delta\varphi}{-5\,\delta\varphi} = -2\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The value $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the reaction of the roller support at $B$, which acts on the segment to the right of $S$.
