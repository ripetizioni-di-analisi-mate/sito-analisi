The cantilever $L$ in the figure fixed at $A = (0, 0)$ and is formed by the column $AD$, with $D = (0, 3)$, and the arm $DE$, with $E = (4, 3)$ (coordinates in meters). At $E$ the horizontal force $P = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and the vertical force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward act. Using virtual work, what is the fixed-end moment $M_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,3;E,4,3" aste="A-D;D-E" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="E,1.2,0,P;E,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,D,3 m,-0.8;D,E,4 m,-0.6" titolo="Cantilever shaped like $L$, with $P = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $E$"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $39\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $39\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

Replace the fixed support with a pin. The cantilever rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi$, and the work of $M_A$ is $M_A\,\delta\varphi$. The point $E$ moves by $\delta u_E = -3\,\delta\varphi$ and $\delta v_E = 4\,\delta\varphi$. Then
$$
M_A\,\delta\varphi + 5 \cdot (-3\,\delta\varphi) - 6 \cdot 4\,\delta\varphi = 0 ,
\qquad
M_A = 39\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
counterclockwise. Both forces tend to rotate the cantilever clockwise about $A$, so their contributions add; the value $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ arises from a wrong sign in $P$'s work.
