The beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ and brings the force $F$ a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. To calculate $R_B$ with the principle of virtual work you remove the roller support and rotate the beam around $A$ of $\delta\varphi$. Which support reactions do work during this displacement?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="2 0,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,2 0,2 m,0.9;2 0,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with a pin at $A$, roller at $B$, and force $F$"}

- Only $R_B$
- $H_A$, $V_A$, and $R_B$
- No reaction
- Only $V_A$ and $R_B$

## Explanation

The pin at $A$ is a smooth, bilateral constraint that remains in place, and its point is still. By the theorem on the work of reactions of smooth constraints, $H_A$ and $V_A$ then perform null work. The reaction $R_B$ is applied as a load at point $B$, which moves by $\delta v_B = 6\,\delta\varphi$, so it does the $R_B \cdot 6\,\delta\varphi$ work. This is why the virtual work equation contains **$R_B$ only** as an unknown.
