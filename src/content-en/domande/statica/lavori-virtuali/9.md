The beam shown has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a pin at $A$, and a roller at $B$ on an inclined surface. Its reaction points in the direction of unit vector $\left( -\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5} \right)$. A downward force $F = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at midspan. Using virtual work, what is the magnitude of the roller reaction $R_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,306.87" forze="C,0,-1,F" quote="A,C,2 m,0.8;C,B,2 m,0.8" titolo="Beam with an inclined roller at $B$ and $F = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan"}

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $\dfrac{50}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Remove the roller. The beam rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi$, and point $B$ moves by $(0, 4\,\delta\varphi)$. The conjugate displacement of $R_B$ is the component of this displacement along the reaction,
$$
\delta_{R_B} = 0 \cdot \left( -\frac{3}{5} \right) + 4\,\delta\varphi \cdot \frac{4}{5} = \frac{16}{5}\,\delta\varphi .
$$
The force $F$ does the $-20 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi = -40\,\delta\varphi$ work, then $R_B \cdot \dfrac{16}{5}\,\delta\varphi - 40\,\delta\varphi = 0$ and $R_B = 12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the only vertical component of the reaction, and $\dfrac{50}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by projecting the displacement with $\dfrac{3}{5}$ instead of $\dfrac{4}{5}$.
