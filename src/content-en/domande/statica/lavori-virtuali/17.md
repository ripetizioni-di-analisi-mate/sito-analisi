The beam $AB$ shown, of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ that moves along a horizontal plane. Which statement about its virtual displacements is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$"}

- It permits rotation about $A$
- It permits horizontal translation
- It permits rotation about $B$
- It permits only zero virtual displacement

## Explanation

The hinge imposes $\delta u_A = \delta v_A = 0$, so the only possible rigid-body displacement is a rotation $\delta\varphi$ around $A$. This rotation moves $B$ vertically by $\delta v_B = 5\,\delta\varphi$, and the roller support imposes $\delta v_B = 0$, so $\delta\varphi = 0$. The beam is statically determinate and admits **only zero virtual displacement**. Rotation around $A$ only becomes compatible after removing the roller support, as is done for calculating $R_B$.
