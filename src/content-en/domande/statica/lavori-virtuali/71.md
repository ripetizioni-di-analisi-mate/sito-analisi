The $AB$ inclined beam in the figure goes from the hinge $A = (0, 0)$ to the point $B = (4, 3)$, where it rests on a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane (coordinates in meters). It carries a uniform vertical load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per unit length of the horizontal projection. Using virtual work, what is the bending moment at midpoint $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,1.5;B,4,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.6;4 0,B,3 m,0.6" titolo="Inclined beam with $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ applied per unit horizontal projection"}

- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the top fibers in tension

## Explanation

Introduce a hinge at $S = (2;\ 1.5)$. Segment $AS$ rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi_s$. The roller support leaves $B$ with only the horizontal displacement, so the center of the segment $SB$ lies on the vertical through $B$; also lies on the line through $A$ and $S$, which passes through $B$, so the center is $B$. The vertical displacement of $S$ equals $2\,\delta\varphi_s$ on the first segment and $(2 - 4)\,\delta\varphi_d$ on the second, then $\delta\varphi_d = -\delta\varphi_s$ and $\Delta\varphi = 2\,\delta\varphi_s$. The load on $AS$ has resultant $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 1$, which rises by $\delta\varphi_s$; the one on $SB$ has resultant $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 3$, which rises by $\delta\varphi_d\,(3 - 4) = \delta\varphi_s$. Then $\delta L = -12\,\delta\varphi_s$ and
$$
M = -\frac{-12\,\delta\varphi_s}{2\,\delta\varphi_s} = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
with the lower fibers stretched. The value coincides with $\dfrac{q a^2}{8}$ of the horizontal beam of span equal to the $a = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ projection; referring to $q$ to the length of the beam, $5\,\mathrm{m}$, you would get $7.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
