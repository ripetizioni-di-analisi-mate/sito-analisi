The structure in the figure is formed by the section $AB$, with a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$, and the section $BC$, connected to $AB$ at $B = (4, 0)$ by an internal parallel-link connection with the horizontal links and resting at $C = (8, 0)$ on a roller support (coordinates in meters). At $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ the force $F_1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What vertical force $F_2$ at point $P$ with horizontal coordinate $7\,\mathrm{m}$ holds the structure in equilibrium?

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward

## Explanation

The structure has $6$ degrees of freedom and $2 + 2 + 1 = 5$ scalar constraints, and virtual displacement is thus constructed. Segment $AB$ rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi$; parallel-link support prevents relative rotation, so $BC$ also rotates $\delta\varphi$, and does so around $C$, because roller support blocks its vertical displacement. The point of application of $F_1$ rises by $2\,\delta\varphi$ and the point $P$ moves by $(7 - 8)\,\delta\varphi = -\delta\varphi$. With $F_2$ positive down the principle of virtual work gives $-10 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi + F_2\,\delta\varphi = 0$, i.e. $F_2 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$, down.
