- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the top fibers in tension
- $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the top fibers in tension
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the top fibers in tension

## Explanation

You insert a hinge at $B$, above the roller support. The section $AB$ has the pin at $A$ and the point $B$ stationary vertically, so it stays stationary and $\delta\varphi_s = 0$. Segment $BC$ rotates about $B$ through angle $\delta\varphi_d$, and segment $CD$ rotates about $D$ through angle $\delta\varphi_3$, with $2\,\delta\varphi_d = (6 - 10)\,\delta\varphi_3$, that is, $\delta\varphi_3 = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,\delta\varphi_d$. The resultant load, $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 8\,\mathrm{m}$, moves by $\delta v = (8 - 10)\,\delta\varphi_3 = \delta\varphi_d$, then $\delta L = -12\,\delta\varphi_d$. With $\Delta\varphi = \delta\varphi_s - \delta\varphi_d = -\delta\varphi_d$ you get
$$
M_B = -\frac{\delta L}{\Delta\varphi} = -\frac{-12\,\delta\varphi_d}{-\delta\varphi_d} = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
with the upper fibers stretched. The value $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by transferring the entire load of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $C$.
