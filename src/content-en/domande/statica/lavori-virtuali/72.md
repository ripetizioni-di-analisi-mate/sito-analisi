To find a vertical reaction by virtual work, the corresponding restraint is removed. In the resulting mechanism the reaction point can move only horizontally. Can the motion be normalised by prescribing a unit vertical displacement there?

- Yes, by multiplying rotation by the span.
- Yes, by choosing one millimetre as the unit.
- Yes, because the numerical value of the reaction is arbitrary.
- No, every admissible motion has zero vertical projection at that point.

## Explanation

Normalisation changes the scale of an admissible displacement. Multiplying a horizontal motion by a constant still gives zero vertical projection. The vertical reaction therefore does no work in that motion.
