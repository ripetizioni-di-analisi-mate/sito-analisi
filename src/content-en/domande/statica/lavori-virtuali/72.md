The Gerber beam in the figure consists of three segments: $AC$, with a hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $B$; $CE$, connected to $AC$ by the internal hinge $C$ and resting on a roller support at $D$; $EG$, connected to $CE$ by the internal hinge $E$ and resting on a roller support at $G$. The axes are $A = 0$, $B = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, $C = 6\,\mathrm{m}$, $D = 10\,\mathrm{m}$, $E = 12\,\mathrm{m}$, $G = 16\,\mathrm{m}$. Midway through the segment $EG$, the force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Using virtual work, what is reaction $R_D$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0;E,12,0;G,16,0" aste="A-C;C-E;E-G" cerniere="C;E" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello;G,carrello" forze="14 0,0,-1.8,F" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,D,4 m,0.9;D,E,2 m,0.9;E,14 0,2 m,0.9;14 0,G,2 m,0.9" titolo="Three-segment Gerber beam with $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ on segment $EG$"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward

## Explanation

Remove the roller at $D$. The segment $AC$ has the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, so it stays still; segment $CE$ rotates about $C$ through angle $\delta\varphi_2$, and segment $EG$ rotates about $G$ through angle $\delta\varphi_3$. The point $E$ has the same displacement on the two segments: $(12 - 6)\,\delta\varphi_2 = (12 - 16)\,\delta\varphi_3$, then $\delta\varphi_3 = -\dfrac{3}{2}\,\delta\varphi_2$. The point of application of $F$ moves by $\delta\varphi_3\,(14 - 16) = 3\,\delta\varphi_2$, and $D$ by $4\,\delta\varphi_2$. Then $R_D \cdot 4\,\delta\varphi_2 - 12 \cdot 3\,\delta\varphi_2 = 0$, i.e. $R_D = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$, upwards. The value $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the force that the segment $EG$ transmits at $E$, without the contribution of the $DE$ overhang.
