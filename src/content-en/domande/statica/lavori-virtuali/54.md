Column $AB$ has height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and a pin at its base $A = (0, 0)$, as shown. It undergoes a virtual rotation about $A$ through an angle $\delta\varphi$, positive counterclockwise. A uniform horizontal load $p = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts to the right over its full height. What virtual work does the load do?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,p,0" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Column rotating about $A$ under horizontal load $p = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $-32\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$
- $-16\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$
- $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$
- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$

## Explanation

In rotation about $A$ the point of the column at elevation $y$ moves horizontally by $\delta u = -\delta\varphi\,y$. The resultant load is $Q = 2 \cdot 4 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the right, applied mid-height, where $\delta u = -2\,\delta\varphi$. Therefore $\delta L = 8 \cdot (-2\,\delta\varphi) = -16\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \cdot \delta\varphi$. The negative sign corresponds to the fact that for $\delta\varphi > 0$ the column tilts to the left, against the load. Applying the resultant at the top would get $-32$, and forgetting the $\delta u$ sign would get $16$.
