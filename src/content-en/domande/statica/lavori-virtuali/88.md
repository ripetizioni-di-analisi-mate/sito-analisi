The structure in the figure is formed by the section $AB$, with a pin support at $A$, and by the section $BD$, connected to $AB$ by the internal hinge $B$ and resting on a roller support at $C$; the horizontal coordinates are $A = 0$, $B = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, $C = 6\,\mathrm{m}$, $D = 9\,\mathrm{m}$. At which point can a vertical force be applied while keeping the structure in equilibrium?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,6,0;D,9,0" aste="A-B;B-D" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="A,B,2 m,0.9;B,C,4 m,0.9;C,D,3 m,0.9" titolo="Structure with internal hinge $B$ and a roller at $C$"}

- At hinge $B$
- At point $C$ on the roller
- At end $D$
- At the midpoint of $AB$

## Explanation

The structure has $n = 2$ bodies and $s = 2 + 2 + 1 = 5$ scalar constraints, and the $C_1 = A$, $C_{12} = B$ and $C_2 = C$ centers are aligned, so it is a mechanism. The point $B$ has the same displacement on the two segments, $2\,\delta\varphi_1 = (2 - 6)\,\delta\varphi_2$, therefore $\delta\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,\delta\varphi_1$. Vertical shifts equal $\delta v_B = 2\,\delta\varphi_1$, $\delta v_D = 3\,\delta\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{3}{2}\,\delta\varphi_1$ and $\delta\varphi_1$ at the midpoint of $AB$, and are zero only at $A$ and $C$. A vertical force at $C$ then performs null work, and by the theorem on the equilibrium of unstable structures the structure is in equilibrium; the force is absorbed by the roller support. In the other three points the force does a different work from zero.
