The horizontal $AB$ beam shown, of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, is constrained at $A$ by a parallel-link support with the vertical links, which prevents vertical displacement and rotation and leaves horizontal sliding free, and at $B$ by a roller support that moves along a vertical plane, with the horizontal reaction. The $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ uniform load acts on the entire beam. Using virtual work, what is the shear force at section $S$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo;B,carrello,0" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with a vertical parallel-link support at $A$ and a roller on a vertical surface at $B$, with $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $-6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

At $S$, introduce a parallel-link connection with its links parallel to the axis. Both segments must have the same rotation and the same horizontal displacement at $S$. The support at $A$ prevents rotation and vertical displacement of segment $AS$, leaving only horizontal sliding. The roller at $B$ prevents horizontal displacement of segment $SB$, so $AS$ cannot move either and remains stationary. Segment $SB$ translates vertically by $\delta v$ without rotating, so $\Delta v = \delta v_d - \delta v_s = \delta v$. Only the load on $SB$ does work. Its resultant is $3 \cdot 3 = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the principle of virtual work gives
$$
T\,\delta v - 9\,\delta v = 0 ,
\qquad
T = 9\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The value $-6\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by sliding the segment $AS$ vertically instead of $SB$, the value $1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ by treating the beam as supported.
