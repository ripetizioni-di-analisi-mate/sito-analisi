The structure in the figure is formed by the section $AB$, with a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$, and the section $BC$, connected to $AB$ by the internal hinge $B = (2, 0)$ and resting at $C = (7, 0)$ on a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane (coordinates in meters). In a virtual displacement the segment $AB$ rotates $\delta\varphi_1$ and the segment $BC$ of $\delta\varphi_2$. What relation connects the two rotations?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,7,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="A,B,2 m,0.9;B,C,5 m,0.9" titolo="Mechanism formed by segments $AB$ and $BC$"}

- $\delta\varphi_2 = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\delta\varphi_1$
- $\delta\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{5}{2}\,\delta\varphi_1$
- $\delta\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{2}{5}\,\delta\varphi_1$
- $\delta\varphi_2 = -\delta\varphi_1$

## Explanation

The segment $AB$ rotates about $A$, then $C_1 = A$. The center $C_2$ lies on the vertical for the roller support and, for the first theorem of kinematic chains, on the line through $A$ and $B$, therefore $C_2 = C$. The point $B$ has the same vertical displacement on the two segments,
$$
2\,\delta\varphi_1 = (2 - 7)\,\delta\varphi_2 ,
\qquad
\delta\varphi_2 = -\frac{2}{5}\,\delta\varphi_1 .
$$
The two rotations have opposite direction because $B$ stands between the two centers. The value $-\dfrac{5}{2}\,\delta\varphi_1$ is obtained by exchanging the distances of $B$ from the two centers.
