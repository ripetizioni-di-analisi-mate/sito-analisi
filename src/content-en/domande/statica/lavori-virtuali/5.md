The Gerber beam shown consists of segment $AB$, fixed at $A$, and segment $BC$, joined to $AB$ at internal hinge $B$ and supported by a roller at $C$. The dimensions are $AB = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $BC = 4\,\mathrm{m}$. A uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on $BC$. Using virtual work, what is the fixed-end moment $M_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,7,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;C,carrello" carichi="B,C,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,3 m,0.9;B,C,4 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on segment $BC$"}

- $48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $56\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

Replace the fixed support with a pin. Segment $AB$ rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi_1$, and segment $BC$ rotates about $C$ through angle $\delta\varphi_2$, because center $C_2$ lies both on the vertical through the roller and on the line through $A$ and $B$. The displacement of $B$ is the same on the two segments, $3\,\delta\varphi_1 = (3 - 7)\,\delta\varphi_2$, then $\delta\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{3}{4}\,\delta\varphi_1$. The resultant load, $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 5\,\mathrm{m}$, moves by $\delta v = (5 - 7)\,\delta\varphi_2 = 1.5\,\delta\varphi_1$. Therefore
$$
M_A\,\delta\varphi_1 - 16 \cdot 1.5\,\delta\varphi_1 = 0 ,
\qquad
M_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
counterclockwise. The value $48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by transferring the entire load of $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $B$ instead of half.
