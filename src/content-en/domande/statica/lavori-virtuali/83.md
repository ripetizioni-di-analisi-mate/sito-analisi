The Gerber beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$, an internal hinge $C$, and a roller at $D$. Their respective horizontal coordinates are $0$, $3\,\mathrm{m}$, $5\,\mathrm{m}$, and $9\,\mathrm{m}$. At $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ the force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Using virtual work, what is the bending moment at section $S$ with horizontal coordinate $7\,\mathrm{m}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,5,0;S,7,0;D,9,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" forze="1.5 0,0,-1.6,F" quote="A,1.5 0,1.5 m,0.9;1.5 0,B,1.5 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,S,2 m,0.9;S,D,2 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ on segment $AC$"}

- $5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension
- $0$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the top fibers in tension
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension

## Explanation

With the hinge $S$ the segment $AC$ still has the hinge $A$ and the roller $B$ support, so it stays still. Only the $CS$ and $SD$ segments, which rotates about $C$ and $D$, move, and the force $F$, applied to the stationary segment, does no work. Therefore $M\,\Delta\varphi = 0$ with $\Delta\varphi \neq 0$, and therefore $M = 0$. Equilibrium of suspended beam $CD$ gives the same result. It is unloaded, so the force it receives at $C$ and the reaction at $D$ are zero.
