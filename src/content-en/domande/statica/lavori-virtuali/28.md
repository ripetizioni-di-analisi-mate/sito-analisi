The beam $CD$ shown rests on a pin at $A$ and on a roller at $B$; the abscissae are $C = 0$, $A = 1\,\mathrm{m}$, $B = 5\,\mathrm{m}$ and $D = 7\,\mathrm{m}$. At the end $C$ the downward force $F_1 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts, and at the end $D$ the downward force $F_2 = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$. Using the principle of virtual work, what is the reaction $R_B$ of the roller?

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,1,0;B,5,0;D,7,0" aste="C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.4,F_1;D,0,-1.4,F_2" quote="C,A,1 m,0.9;A,B,4 m,0.9;B,D,2 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with two overhangs loaded at the ends"}

- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, upwards
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, upwards
- $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, upwards
- $5.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, upwards

## Explanation

The roller is removed and $R_B$ is applied as a load. The beam rotates about the pin $A$ by $\delta\varphi$, positive counterclockwise, and the point of abscissa $x$ moves vertically by $\delta v = (x - 1)\,\delta\varphi$, so $\delta v_B = 4\,\delta\varphi$, $\delta v_C = -\delta\varphi$ and $\delta v_D = 6\,\delta\varphi$. The principle of virtual work gives
$$
R_B \cdot 4\,\delta\varphi - 6 \cdot (-\delta\varphi) - 4 \cdot 6\,\delta\varphi = 0 ,
$$
therefore $4\,R_B = 24 - 6 = 18$ and $R_B = 4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, upwards. When $B$ rises the overhang $CA$ goes down, so the work of $F_1$ is positive and $F_1$ reduces the reaction of the roller. The value $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained with the wrong sign of this work, the value $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ neglects $F_1$ and the value $5.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the reaction of the pin $A$.
