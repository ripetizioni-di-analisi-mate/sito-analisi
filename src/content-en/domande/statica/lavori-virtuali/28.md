After removing the vertical restraint at $B$, a virtual motion is chosen with $\delta v_B=-2\,\mathrm{mm}$, upward positive. The work of all prescribed loads on this motion is $+18\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the original vertical reaction $R_B$, upward positive?

- $-9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $+36\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $+9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Reapply the unknown reaction to the released mechanism. Its total work is $18+R_B(-0.002)=0$ in joules when $R_B$ is in newtons. This yields $R_B=9000\,\mathrm{N}$ upward.
