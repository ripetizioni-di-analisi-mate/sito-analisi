The Gerber beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$, an internal hinge $C$, and a roller at $D$. Their respective horizontal coordinates are $0$, $5\,\mathrm{m}$, $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and $10\,\mathrm{m}$. A downward force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $x = 9\,\mathrm{m}$. Using virtual work, what vertical force does segment $AC$ exert on segment $CD$ at hinge $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,6,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" forze="9 0,0,-1.6,F" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9;B,C,1 m,0.9;C,9 0,3 m,0.9;9 0,D,1 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ on segment $CD$"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward

## Explanation

It replaces the hinge $C$ with a two-force horizontal link, which transmits only the horizontal force, and applies the vertical $Y_C$ force as load, upwards on the segment $CD$ and downwards on the segment $AC$. The segment $AC$, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, remains stationary; in segment $CD$, point $C$ can move only vertically and point $D$ only horizontally. The segment therefore rotates about $D$ through angle $\delta\varphi$. Then $\delta v_C = (6 - 10)\,\delta\varphi = -4\,\delta\varphi$ and the point of application of $F$ moves by $(9 - 10)\,\delta\varphi = -\delta\varphi$. The principle of virtual work gives
$$
Y_C \cdot (-4\,\delta\varphi) - 12 \cdot (-\delta\varphi) = 0 ,
\qquad
Y_C = 3\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
then the segment $AC$ exerts an upward force of $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ on $CD$. The value $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the reaction of the roller support at $D$.
