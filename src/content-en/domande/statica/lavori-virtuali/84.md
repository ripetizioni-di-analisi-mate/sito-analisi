The simply supported beam in the figure has $AB = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ span, a hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ that moves along a horizontal plane. A uniform $p = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ axial load acts on the entire beam, directed to the right. Using virtual work, what is the axial force at section $S$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" frecce="0.3,0.35,1.0,0.35,;1.3,0.35,2.0,0.35,;2.3,0.35,3.0,0.35,;3.3,0.35,4.0,0.35,;4.3,0.35,5.0,0.35,;5.0,0.35,5.7,0.35," etichette="3,0.75,p" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam under axial load $p = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, in compression
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$, in compression
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension

## Explanation

You insert an axial parallel-link support into $S$, which gives the two segments the same rotation. A rotation around $A$ would move $B$ vertically, and the roller support prevents it, so the segment $AS$ remains stationary and the segment $SB$ moves horizontally of $\delta w_d$, with $\Delta w = -\delta w_d$. The load on $SB$ has resultant $2 \cdot 4 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and does work $8\,\delta w_d$; the load on $AS$ does not do work. Then $N \cdot (-\delta w_d) + 8\,\delta w_d = 0$, i.e. $N = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, traction. The roller support does not react horizontally, so the segment $SB$ is pulled to the right from its load and held only by the segment $AS$.
