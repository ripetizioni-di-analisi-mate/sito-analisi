The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at $A$ and carries a triangular load that decreases from $q_0 = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$ to $0$ at $B$. Using virtual work, what is the shear force at midpoint $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,1,0,q_0" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Cantilever with a triangular load varying from $q_0 = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $0$"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

You insert at $S$ a parallel-link support with pin-ended links parallel to the axis. The section $AS$ is fixed and stays still; the segment $SB$ has the same rotation as $AS$, i.e. nothing, and it transposes vertically as $\delta v_d$, then $\Delta v = \delta v_d$. The load on $SB$ is the triangle from $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $0$, resulting in $\dfrac{3 \cdot 2}{2} = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$, and does work $-3\,\delta v_d$; the load on $AS$ does not do work. Then $T\,\delta v_d - 3\,\delta v_d = 0$, that is $T = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the resultant of the load on the segment $AS$, which with the stationary segment does no work.
