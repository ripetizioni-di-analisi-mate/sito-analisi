In the structure shown the members $AC$ and $BC$, hinged to the ground in $A$ and $B$, and the beam $CD$, resting on a roller support at $D$, are three bodies connected by the only hinge $C$. The beam $CD$, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, carries the uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the axial force $N_{AC}$ in member $AC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,-3,0;B,3,0;C,0,4;D,6,4" aste="A-C;B-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera;D,carrello" carichi="C,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,0 0,3 m,0.8;0 0,B,3 m,0.8;C,D,6 m,-1.1;A,-3 4,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Beam $CD$ and members $AC$ and $BC$ connected by hinge $C$ under load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $N_{AC} = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $N_{AC} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $N_{AC} = -7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $N_{AC} = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The beam $CD$ has only vertical loads and the roller support at $D$ gives a vertical reaction, so in $C$ it receives a vertical force: by symmetry of the load it equals $\dfrac{4 \cdot 6}{2} = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the beam presses on the pin with $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ down. The members $AC$ and $BC$ carry no loads and are hinged to the extremes, so they only transmit forces along their own axis. The two members are symmetrical and form an $\dfrac{4}{5}$ sine angle with the horizontal, so they have the same $N$ axial force. The vertical force equilibrium of the pin gives
$$
-2 \cdot \frac{4}{5}\,N - 12 = 0 ,
$$
therefore $N_{AC} = -7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. the member is **in compression**. The value $-10\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by projecting with the cosine instead of with the sine.
