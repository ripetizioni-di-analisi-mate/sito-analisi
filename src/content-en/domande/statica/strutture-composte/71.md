The portal frame shown is formed by the body $ABD$, fixed to the ground at $A$, and the column $DE$, connected to the body $ABD$ by the internal hinge $D$ and bound in $E$ by a roller support on a horizontal surface. The roller support at $E$ is located on the $D$ vertical. How is the structure classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;D,6,4;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-D;D-E" cerniere="D" vincoli="A,incastro;E,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,D,6 m,-0.8" titolo="Portal frame with a fixed support at $A$, an internal hinge at $D$, and a roller at $E$"}

- Kinematically unstable because hinge $D$ lies on the normal to the sliding plane of roller $E$
- Statically determinate because $s = 3n = 6$
- Statically determinate because column $DE$ is restrained by hinge $D$ and roller $E$
- Kinematically unstable because $s < 3n$

## Explanation

Scalar constraints are $s = 3 + 2 + 1 = 6 = 3n$, so counting does not rule out static determinacy. The body $ABD$ is fixed by fixed support, so the column $DE$ can only rotate around $D$. In this rotation the point $E$, which is on the vertical line through $D$, moves horizontally, and the horizontal plane roller support allows just this shift. So the column has a rigid motion compatible with constraints and the structure is **unstable**, although $s = 3n$.
