The parabolic three-hinged arch shown has the springings $A$ and $B$ at the same elevation, the span $L = 8\,\mathrm{m}$, the $f = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ rise and the crown $C$ at midspan. Only half $AC$ carries the load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, uniform on the horizontal projection. What is the thrust $H$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,2;B,8,0" aste="0 0.0-1 0.875;1 0.875-2 1.5;2 1.5-3 1.875;3 1.875-4 2.0;4 2.0-5 1.875;5 1.875-6 1.5;6 1.5-7 0.875;7 0.875-8 0.0" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" carichi="0 2.6,4 2.6,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,8 m,0.8;4 0,C,2 m,-0.6" titolo="Three-hinged parabolic arch with load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on half $AC$"}

- $H = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $H = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $H = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $H = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

In the equivalent simply supported beam the load resultant is $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ and acts at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, then $V_B = \dfrac{16 \cdot 2}{8}\,\mathrm{kN} = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$. To the right of the crown there are no loads, so the moment at midspan is calculated with only the right forces: $M_C^0 = V_B \cdot 4 = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The push is
$$
H = \frac{M_C^0}{f} = \frac{16}{2}\,\mathrm{kN} = 8\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The value $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the push with the load on all the span, the value $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by calculating the moment at $C$ with the $V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ alone and forgetting the load of the half $AC$.
