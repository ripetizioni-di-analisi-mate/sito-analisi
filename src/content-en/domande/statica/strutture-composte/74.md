To calculate the $H_A$, $V_A$, $H_E$, $V_E$ external reactions of a three-hinged portal frame with the $A$ and $E$ pin supports and the $C$ internal hinge, the rotation of the entire structure around $A$, the rotation of the entire structure around $E$ and the horizontal translation of the entire structure were written. Which fourth equation is independent of the three already written?

- Moment equilibrium of the entire structure about $C$
- Vertical force equilibrium of the entire structure
- Moment equilibrium of part $CDE$ alone about $C$
- Moment equilibrium of the entire structure about joint $B$

## Explanation

The equations of the whole structure are only three independent, because a planar rigid body has three degrees of freedom. Therefore, any other equation of the whole structure, whether of rotation around a new reference point or of vertical translation, is a combination of the three already written. Moment equilibrium of only the $CDE$ part around $C$ concerns a different body and contains only $H_E$, $V_E$ and $CDE$ loads. So **auxiliary equation** in $C$ is the fourth independent equation.
