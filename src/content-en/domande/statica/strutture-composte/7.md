The three-hinged arch shown is formed by the straight $AC$ and $CB$ members, with the springings at the same elevation, the span $10\,\mathrm{m}$ and the crown $C$ at midspan, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ above the springings. At the midpoint of the member $CB$, the vertical force $P = 32\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the thrust $H$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,5,4;B,10,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="7.5 2,0,-1.6,P" quote="A,5 0,5 m,0.8;5 0,B,5 m,0.8;5 0,C,4 m,-0.6" titolo="Three-hinged arch with force $P = 32\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the midpoint of $CB$"}

- $H = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $H = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $H = 6.4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $H = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

In the equivalent simply supported beam, force $P$ acts $7.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$, giving $V_A = \dfrac{32 \cdot 2.5}{10}\,\mathrm{kN} = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$. There are no applied loads to the left of the crown hinge, so $M_C^0 = V_A \cdot 5 = 40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The horizontal thrust is therefore
$$
H = \frac{M_C^0}{f} = \frac{40}{4}\,\mathrm{kN} = 10\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The value $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by calculating the moment at $C$ with the $V_B = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ alone and forgetting $P$, the value $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ dividing by $f$ the moment of $P$ about $C$.
