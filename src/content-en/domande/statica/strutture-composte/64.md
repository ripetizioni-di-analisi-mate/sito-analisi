The three-hinged portal frame shown has the $4\,\mathrm{m}$ high bars and the $BD$ long crossbeam $6\,\mathrm{m}$, with the internal hinge $C$ in half; the crossbeam continues past the column $DE$ with the $DF$ overhang, along $2\,\mathrm{m}$. At the end $F$ the vertical force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the horizontal reaction $H_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4;D,6,4;F,8,4;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-F;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" forze="F,0,-1.4,P" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,C,3 m,-1.1;C,D,3 m,-1.1;D,F,2 m,-1.1" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with overhang $DF$ and force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $H_A = 0$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium of the structure about $A$ gives $6\,V_E - 12 \cdot 8 = 0$, then $V_E = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$. The auxiliary equation for the $CDEF$ part around $C$, with $E$ at $3\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C$ and the force $P$ at $5\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right, is
$$
3\,V_E + 4\,H_E - 12 \cdot 5 = 0 ,
$$
therefore $H_E = \dfrac{60 - 48}{4} = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the right. Horizontal force equilibrium of the structure gives $H_A = -H_E = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**. The springings pull the portal frame outwards, as opposed to what happens with a load on the crossbeam between the columns.
