The three-hinged portal frame in the figure has columns $4\,\mathrm{m}$ tall and crossbeam $BD$ of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, with the internal hinge $C$ at its midpoint; the crossbeam extends beyond column $DE$ with the overhang $DF$, of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. The external hinges $A$ and $E$ are at the same level. A downward vertical force $P = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the end $F$. What are the reactions $V_A$ and $H_A$ at support $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,8,4;F,10,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-F;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" forze="F,0,-1.4,P" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,C,4 m,-1.1;C,D,4 m,-1.1;D,F,2 m,-1.1" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with overhang $DF$ and force $P = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $H_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, $H_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $V_A = 0$, $H_A = 0$
- $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, $H_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right

## Explanation

The external hinges are at the same level, so moment equilibrium of the whole structure about $A$ contains only $V_E$ and gives $8\,V_E - 16 \cdot 10 = 0$, that is, $V_E = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$. Vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = 16 - 20 = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, so the reaction at $A$ is $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. The auxiliary equation for part $CDEF$ about $C$, with $E$ located $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C$ and force $P$ $6\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right, is
$$
4\,V_E + 4\,H_E - 16 \cdot 6 = 0 ,
$$
so $H_E = \dfrac{96 - 80}{4} = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the right. Horizontal force equilibrium of the whole structure gives $H_A = -H_E = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, that is, $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**. With the load on the overhang the horizontal reactions point outward from the frame, the opposite of what happens with a load on the crossbeam between the columns.
