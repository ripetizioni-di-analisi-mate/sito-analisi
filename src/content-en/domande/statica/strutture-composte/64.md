In a planar three-hinged portal, $A$ and $B$ are the feet and $C$ is the internal hinge. A student has used the three global equilibrium equations and proposes the moment equation about $C$ for the entire portal as a fourth equation. Which equation provides independent information instead?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,2.6,-;C,2,2.6;E,4,2.6,-;B,4,0" aste="A-D;D-C;C-E;E-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" titolo="Frame hinged at A, B and C"}

- The sum of moments about the midpoint of $AB$ for the entire portal.
- The sum of moments about $C$ for just one half-frame.
- The total global force projected along $AC$.
- The same global moment equation multiplied by two.

## Explanation

Changing the pole for the whole body combines global equations already used. Isolating one half-frame exploits the hinge, which transmits forces through $C$ and no moment. Balancing that half alone about $C$ expresses the internal condition.
