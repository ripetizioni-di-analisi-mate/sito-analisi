The portal frame shown has columns of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a crossbeam $BD$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and pin supports at $A$ and $E$. Internal hinge $C$ lies on column $DE$ a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ above $E$. It divides the frame into body $ABDC$ and column segment $CE$. The crossbeam carries a uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the horizontal reaction $H_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;D,6,4;C,6,2;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-D;D-C;C-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" carichi="B,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,D,6 m,0.8;D,C,2 m,-0.6;C,E,2 m,-0.6" titolo="Portal frame with internal hinge $C$ on column $DE$ and load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $6.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $H_A = 0$
- $13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left

## Explanation

The section $CE$ carries no loads and is only connected to the rest of the structure by the $C$ and $E$ hinges. For the **the theorem of the unloaded member hinged at the two extremes** the forces it receives are directed along the line $CE$, which is vertical. Therefore the reaction at $E$ is vertical and $H_E = 0$. The load is vertical, so horizontal force equilibrium of the structure gives $H_A + H_E = 0$, and then **$H_A = 0$**. The structure behaves like a unsymmetrical frame, and the load $q$ goes to the two supports without thrust: $V_A = V_E = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$. The $6.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ values are obtained from the $\dfrac{qL^2}{8h}$ formula of the portal frame with the hinge in the middle crossbeam, with $h = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and with $h = 2\,\mathrm{m}$; Here the formula does not apply.
