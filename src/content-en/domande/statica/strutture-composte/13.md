The Gerber beam shown has the segment $AC$ bound by a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, with the $BC$ overhang, and the segment $CD$ connected in $C$ by an internal hinge and resting on a roller support at $D$. In the hinge $C$, the vertical force $P = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward, applied to the extreme of the $BC$ overhang; the section $CD$ carries the uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the vertical reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,9,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.4,P" carichi="C,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,C,2 m,0.8;C,D,3 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam with force $P = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$ and load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $CD$"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $V_A = 0$

## Explanation

The $CD$ suspended segment has the resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in half, so $V_D = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the hinge carries $6\,\mathrm{kN}$. On the overhang they therefore act in $C$, at $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, the force $P$ and the force of the hinge, in all $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ down. Moment equilibrium of segment $AC$ about $A$ gives $4\,V_B - 24 \cdot 6 = 0$, i.e. $V_B = 36\,\mathrm{kN}$. The vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = 24 - 36 = -12\,\mathrm{kN}$, so the reaction at $A$ equals $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is **Directed Down**. The value $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by forgetting the force of the hinge.
