The beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, is pinned to the wall at $A$ and is supported by the $ED$ strut, pinned to the wall at $E$, $3\,\mathrm{m}$ below $A$, and to the beam $D$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The beam carries the uniform load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the axial force $N_{ED}$ in the strut?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,3;D,4,3;B,6,3;E,0,0" aste="A-B;E-D" cerniere="D" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;E,cerniera,180" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,D,4 m,-0.8;D,B,2 m,-0.8;E,A,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Beam pinned to the wall and supported by strut $ED$, carrying load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $N_{ED} = -30\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $N_{ED} = -37.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $N_{ED} = -22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $N_{ED} = 37.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The strut is unloaded and hinged at both ends, so it transmits a direct force along $ED$ to the beam. The strut rises by $3\,\mathrm{m}$ on $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long, so an $S$ intensity compression gives $D$ the vertical $\dfrac{3}{5}\,S$ component upward. The horizontal component has zero moment arm about $A$, so moment equilibrium of the beam about $A$ gives
$$
4 \cdot \frac{3}{5}\,S - 30 \cdot 3 = 0 ,
$$
from which $S = 37.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The strut is **in compression**, therefore $N_{ED} = -37.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ values are the horizontal and vertical components of the force in the strut.
