The beam shown consists of a segment $AC$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at $A$ and a segment $CB$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ supported by a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A parallel-link connection at $C$ joins the segments. The links slope upward to the right at an angle $45°$. At the midpoint of segment $CB$ a vertical force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward, and at $B$ a horizontal force $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is the reaction $V_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" interni="C,doppio-pendolo,45" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="5.5 0,0,-1.5,P;8.4 0,1.3,0,F" quote="A,C,4 m,0.8;C,5.5 0,1.5 m,0.8;5.5 0,B,1.5 m,0.8" titolo="Beam with an internal parallel-link connection whose links are inclined at $45°$, force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$, and force $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$"}

- $V_B = 14\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_B = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Parallel-link support transmits a force parallel to pin-ended links and a couple, and no force in the direction perpendicular to pin-ended links. For the auxiliary parallel-link support equation, written for the section $CB$, the sum of the components of the external forces along the direction $(1,\,-1)$, perpendicular to the pin-ended links, is zero:
$$
F - V_B + P = 0 .
$$
Therefore $V_B = 4 + 10 = 14\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward. The value $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ is what you would have with the horizontal links, when the force $F$ does not enter the vertical equation; the value $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ is what you would have with a pin at $C$.
