The three-hinged frame shown has columns of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Beam $BD$ has length $8\,\mathrm{m}$ and an internal hinge $C$ a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $D$. At a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$, a downward force $P = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts on the beam. What is the horizontal reaction $H_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,6,3;D,8,3;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" forze="2 3,0,-1.4,P" quote="A,B,3 m,0.6;B,2 3,2 m,0.6;2 3,C,4 m,0.6;C,D,2 m,0.6" titolo="Portal frame with hinge $C$ a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $D$, and force $P = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium of the structure about $A$ gives $8\,V_E - 24 \cdot 2 = 0$, then $V_E = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The $CDE$ part is unloaded and the hinge $E$ is located $2\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ under $C$, so the auxiliary equation around $C$ is
$$
2\,V_E + 3\,H_E = 0 ,
$$
from which $H_E = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$. Horizontal force equilibrium of the structure gives $H_A = -H_E = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the right. The value $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ is achieved by placing the hinge $C$ at midspan.
