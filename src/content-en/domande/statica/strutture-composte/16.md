The Gerber beam shown consists of a cantilever $AC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at $A$ and a segment $CB$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Hinge $C$ connects the segment to the cantilever, and a roller supports it at $B$. The midpoint of segment $CB$ carries a downward force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$. Which set of three reactions is correct?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="4 0,0,-1.5,P" quote="A,C,2 m,0.8;C,4 0,2 m,0.8;4 0,B,2 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam with force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the midpoint of segment $CB$"}

- $V_A = 0$, $V_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise
- $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_B = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M_A = 0$
- $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise
- $V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise

## Explanation

All four sets of reactions satisfy equilibrium of the entire structure: $V_A + V_B = 12$ and, taking moments about $A$, $M_A + 6\,V_B - 4 \cdot 12 = 0$. The global equations are three and the unknown reactions $H_A$, $V_A$, $V_B$, $M_A$ are four, so they are not enough to choose the third. We need the auxiliary equation in the hinge $C$, written for the segment $CB$:
$$
4\,V_B - 2 \cdot 12 = 0 ,
$$
from which $V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The vertical equation then gives $V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the moment equation gives $M_A = 48 - 36 = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise. The correct set of reactions is therefore **$V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise**. With $V_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ the first member of the auxiliary equation is $24$, $8$ and $-8$ instead.
