The three-hinged arch shown is formed by the straight $AC$ and $CB$ members, with the springings at the same elevation, the span $8\,\mathrm{m}$ and the crown $C$ at midspan, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ above the springings. The $\mathcal{M} = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise couple is applied to the member $AC$. What is the horizontal reaction $H_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,4;B,8,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" coppie="C,1,M" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.8;4 0,B,4 m,0.8;B,8 4,4 m,0.8" titolo="Three-hinged arch with couple $\mathcal{M} = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ applied to member $AC$ at the crown"}

- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $H_A = 0$

## Explanation

The springings are at the same elevation, so in the rotation of the whole structure around $A$ only $V_B$ and the couple appear:
$$
8\,V_B + 16 = 0 ,
$$
therefore $V_B = -2\,\mathrm{kN}$. The couple is applied to the member $AC$, so the member $CB$ is unloaded. The point $B$ is located $4\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C$, so the auxiliary equation for the $CB$ part around $C$ is $4\,V_B + 4\,H_B = 0$, hence $H_B = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$. Horizontal force equilibrium of the structure gives $H_A = -H_B = -2\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**. If the couple were applied to the member $CB$, the auxiliary equation would also contain $\mathcal{M}$ and you would get $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right.
