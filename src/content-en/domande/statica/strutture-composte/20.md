The three-hinged portal frame shown has the $4\,\mathrm{m}$ high bars, the $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long crossbeam and the $C$ internal pin at midspan; the section $CD$ carries the uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Taking counterclockwise moments as positive and reactions $H_E$ and $V_E$ as positive along the coordinate axes, which auxiliary equation sets the moment at $C$ to zero for part $CDE$ (lengths in $\mathrm{m}$, forces in $\mathrm{kN}$)?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4;D,6,4;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" carichi="C,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m,0.6;B,C,3 m,0.6;C,D,3 m,0.6" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on segment $CD$"}

- $3\,V_E - 4\,H_E - 18 = 0$
- $3\,V_E + 4\,H_E + 18 = 0$
- $3\,V_E + 4\,H_E - 18 = 0$
- $3\,V_E + 4\,H_E - 36 = 0$

## Explanation

The hinge $E$ is located $3\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C$. The reaction $V_E$, up and to the right of the reference point, has $3\,V_E$ counterclockwise moment; the reaction $H_E$, to the right and below the reference point, also has counterclockwise moment, $4\,H_E$. Load $CD$ has a downward resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting a distance $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $C$, so its moment is clockwise with magnitude $-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Therefore the equation is $3\,V_E + 4\,H_E - 18 = 0$. The term $-36$ arises from applying the resultant at $D$ instead of in the middle of the segment.
