The three-hinged frame shown has columns of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a beam of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. At joint $D$, at the top of column $DE$, a vertical force $P$ acts downward. Which statement about the reactions is correct?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,8,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" forze="D,0,-1.5,P" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,C,4 m,-0.8;C,D,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with vertical force $P$ at joint $D$"}

- The reactions at $A$ are zero and $V_E = P$, $H_E = 0$
- $V_A = V_E = \dfrac{P}{2}$ and $H_A = -H_E \neq 0$
- $V_E = P$ and $H_A = \dfrac{P}{2}$, to the right
- $V_A = 0$ and $H_E = P$, to the left

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium of the structure about $A$ gives $8\,V_E - 8\,P = 0$, then $V_E = P$ and $V_A = 0$. The auxiliary equation for the $CDE$ part around $C$, with $E$ and $D$ both at $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $C$, is
$$
4\,V_E + 4\,H_E - 4\,P = 0 ,
$$
Therefore $H_E = 0$ and $H_A = 0$. Reactions in $A$ are all zero: the force, applied on the line of action of the column $DE$, drops directly to the hinge $E$.
