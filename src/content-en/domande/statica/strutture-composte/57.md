The beam shown is formed by the sections $AC$ and $CB$, connected by the internal hinge $C$. In $A$ there is a parallel-link support with the horizontal links, which prevents horizontal translation and rotation of the segment $AC$; in $B$ there is a hinge. How is the structure classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,8,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,180;B,cerniera" quote="A,C,4 m,0.8;C,B,4 m,0.8" titolo="Beam with a parallel-link support at $A$, an internal hinge at $C$, and a pin at $B$"}

- Statically determinate because $s = 3n = 6$
- Statically indeterminate with degree of indeterminacy $1$
- Kinematically unstable because $s < 3n$
- Kinematically unstable because $B$, $C$, and the center at infinity of segment $AC$ are collinear

## Explanation

Scalar constraints are $s = 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 = 3n$, so counting does not rule out static determinacy. Parallel-link support leaves only vertical translation to the segment $AC$, so its $C_1$ rotation center is the misplaced point of the horizontal direction. The center of section $CB$ is $C_2 = B$ and the relative center is $C_{12} = C$. The line $BC$ is horizontal and contains the point at infinity in the horizontal direction, so the three centers are aligned and by the kinematic ties theorem the structure is **unstable**. In the $AC$ mechanism it moves vertically while $CB$ rotates around $B$.
