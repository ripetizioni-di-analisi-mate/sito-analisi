The Gerber beam shown consists of the $AC$, $CE$ and $EF$ sections, connected by the internal hinges $C$ and $E$, and is supported by a roller support at $A$, a roller support at $D$ and fixed support at $F$. On the section $AC$, at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, the vertical downward force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts. What is the vertical reaction $V_F$ at the fixed support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;D,7,0;E,9,0;F,11,0" aste="A-C;C-E;E-F" cerniere="C;E" vincoli="A,carrello;D,carrello;F,incastro,0" forze="2 0,0,-1.6,P" quote="A,2 0,2 m,0.8;2 0,C,2 m,0.8;C,D,3 m,0.8;D,E,2 m,0.8;E,F,2 m,0.8" titolo="Three-segment Gerber beam with force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ on segment $AC$"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

Segment $AC$ is suspended and carries the force at its midpoint. Hinge $C$ therefore supports it with force $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, and segment $CE$ receives a downward force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$. The section $CE$ has the $CD$ overhang to the left of the roller support at $D$, and moment equilibrium about $E$ gives
$$
6 \cdot 5 - 2\,V_D = 0 ,
$$
therefore $V_D = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$. The vertical translation of $CE$ then gives $-6 + 15 + Y_E = 0$, i.e. $Y_E = -9\,\mathrm{kN}$: the cantilever $EF$ holds $CE$ downward and receives a force $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting upward at $E$. So $V_F = -9\,\mathrm{kN}$, that is $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ **downward**, and $M_F = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
