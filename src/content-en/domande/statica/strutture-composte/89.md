The three-hinged arch shown consists of straight members $AC$ and $CB$, each of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$. The springings are at the same elevation and the span is $8\,\mathrm{m}$. The crown $C$ is at midspan, $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above the springings. The members carry their own weight, a vertical load $g = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per unit length along each member. What is the horizontal thrust $H$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,3;B,8,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" carichi="A,C,0.4,0.4,g;C,B,0.4,0.4,g" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.8;4 0,B,4 m,0.8;4 0,C,3 m,-0.6" titolo="Three-hinged arch under self-weight $g = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ distributed along its members"}

- $H = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $H = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $H = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $H = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The load is distributed along the member, so the resultant on each half equals $3 \cdot 5 = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ and passes through the midpoint of the member, to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the crown horizontally. By $V_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ symmetry. The auxiliary equation for the $AC$ part around $C$ gives
$$
-15 \cdot 4 + 15 \cdot 2 + 3\,H = 0 ,
$$
therefore $H = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by calculating the load on the horizontal projection, $3 \cdot 4 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ by half, instead of on the length of the member.
