The three-hinged arch shown is formed by the straight $AC$ and $CB$ members, with the springings at the same elevation, the span $8\,\mathrm{m}$ and the crown $C$ at midspan, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ above the springings. The wind acts on the member $AC$ with a horizontal load of $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ for each meter in height, directed to the right. What is the horizontal reaction $H_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,4;B,8,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" carichi="A,C,0.6,0.6,p,0" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.8;4 0,B,4 m,0.8;B,8 4,4 m,0.8" titolo="Three-hinged arch with wind loading on member $AC$, of intensity $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ per meter of height"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left

## Explanation

The wind load is distributed on the $4\,\mathrm{m}$ height of the member $AC$, so its resultant is $3 \cdot 4 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, horizontal, applied to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ elevation. The springings are at the same elevation, so moment equilibrium of the structure about $A$ gives $8\,V_B - 12 \cdot 2 = 0$, i.e. $V_B = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$. The $CB$ half is unloaded and $B$ is located $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C$, so the auxiliary equation around $C$ is
$$
4\,V_B + 4\,H_B = 0 ,
$$
from which $H_B = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$. Horizontal force equilibrium of the structure gives $H_A = -12 - H_B = -9\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**. The springing $A$, on the wind side, absorbs the higher share of the load.
