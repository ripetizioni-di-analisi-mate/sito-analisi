In the Gerber beam shown, cantilever $AC$ has length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and is fixed at $A$. Segment $CB$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, is hinged to the cantilever at $C$, and rests on a roller at $B$. A clockwise couple acts at the midpoint of segment $CB$ and has magnitude $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. What is the reaction $V_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" coppie="4 0,-1,M" quote="A,C,2 m,0.8;C,4 0,2 m,0.8;4 0,B,2 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam with clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on segment $CB$"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

Segment $CB$ is suspended. Analyze it first by taking moments about hinge $C$. The clockwise moment of a couple is the same as at any reference point, so the clockwise couple enters the equation with $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, whatever the point where it is applied:
$$
4\,V_B - 12 = 0 .
$$
Therefore $V_B = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$, **upward**. The value $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by dividing the couple by the distance of $B$ from $A$, the value $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ by dividing it by the distance of $B$ from the point of application of the couple.
