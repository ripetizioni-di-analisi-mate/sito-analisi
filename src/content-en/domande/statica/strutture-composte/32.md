The frame shown is formed by the body $ABC$, hinged to the ground in $A$, and the column $CD$, hinged to the body $ABC$ in $C$ and to the ground in $D$. The columns are $4\,\mathrm{m}$ high and the crossbeam $BC$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long. The only load is a uniform horizontal load of $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to the right, applied to all the column $CD$. What is the horizontal reaction $H_D$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,5,4;D,5,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,cerniera" carichi="D,C,0.5,0.5,2 kN/m,0" quote="A,B,4 m,0.6;B,C,5 m,-0.8" titolo="Frame with a horizontal load of $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on column $CD$"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $H_D = 0$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left

## Explanation

The column $CD$ carries a load, so the forces in its hinges are not directed along the axis and the reaction at $D$ also has a horizontal component. The load of the column has resultant $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, applied mid-height, i.e. $2\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C$. In moment equilibrium of the column about hinge $C$ the force transmitted by the body $ABC$ has zero moment arm, therefore
$$
4\,H_D + 8 \cdot 2 = 0 ,
$$
and $H_D = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, that is $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**. The value $H_D = 0$ is obtained by treating the load column as a two-force link, the value $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ attributing to the hinge $D$ all the load of the right.
