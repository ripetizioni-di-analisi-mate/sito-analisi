The Gerber beam shown is formed by the $AC$, $CE$ and $EF$ sections, connected by the internal hinges $C$ and $E$. It is bound to the ground by a roller support at $A$, a roller support at $D$ and a fixed support at $F$. In what order should the reactions of the three segments be calculated?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;D,7,0;E,9,0;F,11,0" aste="A-C;C-E;E-F" cerniere="C;E" vincoli="A,carrello;D,carrello;F,incastro,0" quote="A,C,4 m,0.8;C,D,3 m,0.8;D,E,2 m,0.8;E,F,2 m,0.8" titolo="Three-segment Gerber beam fixed at the right end"}

- $EF$, then $CE$, then $AC$
- $CE$, then $AC$, then $EF$
- $AC$, then $EF$, then $CE$
- $AC$, then $CE$, then $EF$

## Explanation

The fixed support makes segment $EF$ statically determinate on its own, so $EF$ is the supporting segment. The only external support of segment $CE$ is roller $D$. It rests on $EF$ through hinge $E$ and is therefore suspended from $EF$. Segment $AC$ has only roller $A$ and rests on $CE$ through hinge $C$. It is therefore suspended from $CE$. For the **order of calculation** of the Gerber beams we start from the segment that does not support any other segment and we go back to the supporting: first $AC$, then $CE$ with the force transmitted in $C$, finally $EF$ with the force transmitted in $E$.
