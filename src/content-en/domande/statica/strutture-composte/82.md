The three-hinged frame shown has columns of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$, a beam of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. Column $DE$ carries a uniform horizontal load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to the right. What is the vertical reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,3,3;D,6,3;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" carichi="E,D,0.5,0.5,q,0" quote="A,B,3 m,0.8;B,C,3 m,-0.8;C,D,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with horizontal load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on column $DE$"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward

## Explanation

The resultant of the load is $4 \cdot 3 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the right, and is applied in the middle of the column $DE$, at $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ elevation. The pin supports are at the same elevation, so in the rotation of the whole structure around $A$ the horizontal reactions have zero arm:
$$
6\,V_E - 12 \cdot 1.5 = 0 .
$$
So $V_E = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ **downward**. The value $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by applying the resultant to the dimension of the crossbeam.
