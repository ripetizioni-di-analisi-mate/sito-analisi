The structure shown is formed by the straight members $AC$ and $CB$, hinged to the ground in $A$ and $B$ at the same elevation and connected by the hinge $C$, which is located at midspan and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ *below* the springings. The span is $8\,\mathrm{m}$. In the hinge $C$, the vertical force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward, applied to the member $AC$. What is the horizontal reaction $H_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,2;C,4,0;B,8,2" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;B,cerniera,0" forze="C,0,-1.4,P" tratteggi="0,2 8,2" quote="A,4 2,4 m,-0.8;4 2,B,4 m,-0.8;C,4 2,2 m,0.8" titolo="Two hinged members with hinge $C$ below the line joining the springings and force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left

## Explanation

The force is half span, and moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $8\,V_B - 12 \cdot 4 = 0$, then $V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The member $CB$ is unloaded and the point $B$ is located $4\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ above $C$, so the auxiliary equation around $C$ is
$$
4\,V_B - 2\,H_B = 0 ,
$$
from which $H_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, right. The horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A = -12\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**. With the hinge under the springings the members are in tension and the springings pull outwards, while in an arch with the crown at the top they thrust inwards.
