The gable frame shown is pinned at $A$ and $E$, at the same elevation and a distance $8\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. The columns have height $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The two rafters $BC$ and $CD$ rise to internal hinge $C$ at midspan, $6\,\mathrm{m}$ above the external hinges. At joint $B$ a horizontal force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is the horizontal reaction $H_E$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,6;D,8,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" forze="B,1.4,0,F" tratteggi="4,0 4,6" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;A,4 0,4 m,0.8;4 0,E,4 m,0.8;E,8 6,6 m,0.8" titolo="Gabled portal frame with force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at joint $B$"}

- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right

## Explanation

The outer hinges are at the same elevation, so the rotation of the whole structure around $A$ contains only $V_E$ and the force $F$, which has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$:
$$
8\,V_E - 12 \cdot 4 = 0 ,
$$
therefore $V_E = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The point $E$ is located $4\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $6\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C$, so the auxiliary equation for the $CDE$ part around $C$ is $4\,V_E + 6\,H_E = 0$. $H_E = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. **$4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left**, follows. The arm $H_E$ about $C$ is the crown elevation, $6\,\mathrm{m}$; with the height of the columns, $4\,\mathrm{m}$, you would get the wrong value of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$.
