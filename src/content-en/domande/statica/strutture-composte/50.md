The Gerber beam shown is formed by three segments. The center section $CE$ is supported by a pin at $B$ and a roller support at $D$ and protrudes with two overhangs, $CB$ and $DE$; the section $AC$ is supported on a roller support at $A$ and connected to $CE$ by the hinge $C$; the section $EF$ is connected to $CE$ by the hinge $E$ and leaned on a roller support at $F$. The segment $AC$ carries the uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and at the midpoint of segment $EF$ acts the force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. What is reaction $V_D$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,5,0;D,9,0;E,11,0;F,14,0" aste="A-C;C-E;E-F" cerniere="C;E" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera;D,carrello;F,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.5,0.5,q" forze="12.5 0,0,-1.4,P" quote="A,C,3 m,0.8;C,B,2 m,0.8;B,D,4 m,0.8;D,E,2 m,0.8;E,F,3 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam with supporting segment $CE$ and its two overhangs, $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

Segments $AC$ and $EF$ are suspended and should be analyzed first as simply supported beams. The segment $AC$ has the resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in half, so it transmits to the segment $CE$ a force $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting downward at $C$; the segment $EF$ has the force $P$ in half, so it transmits $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ down into $E$. On the $CE$-supporting segment the force at $C$ stands $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$ and the force at $E$ stands $6\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $B$, so moment equilibrium about $B$ gives
$$
6 \cdot 2 - 6 \cdot 6 + 4\,V_D = 0 .
$$
So $V_D = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, up. The value $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ neglects the force transmitted on the left overhang, which lifts the beam to the side of $D$.
