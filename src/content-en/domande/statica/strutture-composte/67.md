The three-hinged portal frame shown has the $3\,\mathrm{m}$ high bars, the $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long crossbeam and the $C$ internal pin at midspan; Only the section $BC$ carries the uniform $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load. One student got $V_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_E = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$, both up, $H_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ right and $H_E = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ right. One of the four values is wrong. Which reaction is incorrect, and what is its correct value?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,3,3;D,6,3;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" carichi="B,C,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,3 m,0.8;B,C,3 m,-1.1;C,D,3 m,-1.1" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on segment $BC$"}

- $H_E$, equal to $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $H_A$, equal to $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $V_E$, equal to $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A$, equal to $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

Horizontal translation of the whole structure requires $H_A + H_E = 0$, while student values give $3 + 3 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$; So one of the two horizontal reactions is wrong. Vertical reactions pass verification instead, because $9 + 3 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the resultant of the load and moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $6 \cdot 3 - 12 \cdot 1.5 = 0$. For the $ABC$ part, with $A$ at $3\,\mathrm{m}$ on the left and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C$ and the resultant at $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ on the left of $C$, the auxiliary equation around $C$ gives
$$
-3\,V_A + 3\,H_A + 12 \cdot 1.5 = -27 + 9 + 18 = 0 ,
$$
then the value of $H_A$ is correct. For the $CDE$ unloaded part the auxiliary equation is $3\,V_E + 3\,H_E = 0$, so $H_E = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$: the wrong value is $H_E$, which equals $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**.
