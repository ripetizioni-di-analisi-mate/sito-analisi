The beam shown has two segments, $AC$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $CD$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, connected by an internal hinge $C$. The supports are a pin at $A$, a roller at $D$, and a roller directly below hinge $C$. Segment $AC$ carries a uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. On segment $CD$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$, a vertical force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the reaction $V_C$ at roller $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello;D,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.5,0.5,q" forze="6 0,0,-1.5,P" quote="A,C,4 m,0.8;C,6 0,2 m,0.8;6 0,D,4 m,0.8" titolo="Beam with internal hinge $C$ above the intermediate roller"}

- $V_C = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_C = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_C = 14\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_C = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The scalar constraints are $s = 2 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 6 = 3n$. The hinge $C$ transmits no moments and its pivot is supported by roller support, so each segment is calculated as a simply supported beam in its two extremes. The segment $AC$ has the resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in half, so it presses on the pin $C$ at $6\,\mathrm{kN}$. For the section $CD$, called $Y$ the force with which the pin supports it, moment equilibrium about $D$ gives $-6\,Y + 12 \cdot 4 = 0$, then $Y = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$. The roller support supports the pin against both forces, therefore $V_C = 6 + 8 = 14\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward. The $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ values take into calculation only one of the two segments.
