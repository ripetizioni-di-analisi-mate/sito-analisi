The Gerber beam in the figure consists of cantilever $AC$, fixed at $A$, and span $CB$, connected to the cantilever by hinge $C$ and supported by a roller at $B$. A uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the entire beam. What is the reaction moment $M_A$ at the fixed support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,8,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,C,3 m,0.8;C,B,5 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam under uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $M_A = 78\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

Analyze the suspended span $CB$ first. It behaves as a beam simply supported at $C$ and $B$, with resultant load $q \cdot 5\,\mathrm{m} = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan. By symmetry the hinge carries $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, so the cantilever is subject to a downward force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$. It also carries its own distributed load, with resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Taking moments on the cantilever about $A$ gives
$$
M_A - 12 \cdot 1.5 - 10 \cdot 3 = 0 ,
$$
so $M_A = 48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is positive and therefore **counterclockwise**. The value $78\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ transfers the entire load on span $CB$ to the cantilever. The value $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ omits the hinge force.
