The three-hinged arch shown is formed by the straight $AC$ and $CB$ members, with the span $10\,\mathrm{m}$ and the crown $C$ midspan, $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ above the springings. The springing $A$ is a hinge, the springing $B$ is a roller support on a horizontal surface, and the springings are connected by the tie $AB$. The arch carries the load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, uniform on the horizontal projection, and in the crown the vertical force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, applied to the member $AC$. What is the axial force $N$ in the tie?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,5,2.5;B,10,0" aste="A-C;C-B;A-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="0 3,10 3,0.6,0.6,q" forze="C,0,-1.4,P" quote="A,5 0,5 m,0.8;5 0,B,5 m,0.8;5 0,C,2.5 m,-0.6" titolo="Tied arch under load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the crown"}

- $N = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $N = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $N = 60\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $N = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The loads are vertical and symmetrical, so $H_A = 0$ and $V_A = V_B = \dfrac{40 + 10}{2}\,\mathrm{kN} = 25\,\mathrm{kN}$. In the auxiliary equation for the $CB$ part around $C$ $V_B$ appears, at $5\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $C$, the force $N$ of the tie, which pulls $B$ left to $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ under $C$, and the resultant $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ of the load $CB$:
$$
5 \cdot 25 - 2.5\,N - 20 \cdot 2.5 = 0 .
$$
Therefore $N = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the tie is **in tension**. The value $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by forgetting the force $P$ in the calculation of $V_B$, the value $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ dividing by twice the rise.
