A three-hinged portal frame is disassembled in the two bodies $ABC$ and $CDE$, connected by the internal hinge $C$. The three equilibrium equations are written for each of the two bodies and also the three equilibrium equations of the whole structure. How many of these equations are independent?

- $9$
- $6$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The disassembly gives three equations for each body, i.e. $3n = 6$ independent equations in the six unknown $H_A$, $V_A$, $H_E$, $V_E$, $X_C$, $Y_C$. In the hinge the forces exchanged are opposite and applied in the same point, so adding the equations of the two bodies the internal forces are eliminated. Therefore each equation of the whole structure is the sum of the corresponding equations of the two bodies and does not add information. Independent equations remain **six**.
