In the three-hinged frame shown, vertical column $AB$ has height $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Beam $BD$ has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. Inclined column $DE$ slopes down to hinge $E$, a distance $8\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $A$ at the same elevation. The beam carries a uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the vertical reaction $V_E$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4;D,6,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" carichi="B,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,C,3 m,0.8;C,D,3 m,0.8;A,6 0,6 m,0.8;6 0,E,2 m,0.8" titolo="Portal frame with inclined column $DE$ and load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the crossbeam"}

- $V_E = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_E = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_E = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_E = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The $A$ and $E$ hinges are at the same elevation, so horizontal reactions have zero arm about $A$. Therefore the rotation of the whole structure around $A$ contains only $V_E$ and the load, as for a simply supported beam of span equal to the distance $AE = 8\,\mathrm{m}$. The resultant of the load is $4 \cdot 6 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is applied $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$:
$$
8\,V_E - 24 \cdot 3 = 0 ,
$$
then **$V_E = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$**, upward, and $V_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by taking the length of the crossbeam, $6\,\mathrm{m}$, as span, instead of the distance between the pin supports.
