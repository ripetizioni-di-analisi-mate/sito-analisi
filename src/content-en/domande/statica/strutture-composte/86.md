The portal frame shown has the $4\,\mathrm{m}$ high bars and the $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long crossbeam, with the internal hinge $C$ in half. The body $ABC$ is hinged to the ground in $A$; the body $CDE$ is bound in $E$ by a parallel-link support with the vertical links, which prevents vertical translation and rotation. The entire crossbeam carries the uniform $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load. What is the reaction moment $M_E$ at the parallel-link connection?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4;D,6,4;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,doppio-pendolo,270" carichi="B,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,C,3 m,-1.1;C,D,3 m,-1.1" titolo="Portal frame with a pin at $A$, an internal hinge at $C$, and a parallel-link support at $E$"}

- $M_E = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_E = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_E = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_E = 54\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

Parallel-link support does not give horizontal reactions and the load is vertical, so the hinge $A$ has no horizontal reaction and the body $ABC$ behaves like a simply supported beam in $A$ and $C$. Its load is the resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in the middle of $BC$, so the hinge $C$ supports $ABC$ with $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the body $CDE$ receives at $C$ a force $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. The body $CDE$ then acts $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ with $3\,\mathrm{m}$ arm and the resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ with $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ arm, both to the left of $E$. Moment equilibrium about $E$ gives
$$
M_E + 6 \cdot 3 + 12 \cdot 1.5 = 0 ,
$$
then $M_E = -36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, i.e. $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in **clockwise**. The value $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ forgets the force transmitted by the hinge $C$.
