The beam shown is formed by the section $AC$, fixed at $A$, and the section $CB$, resting on a roller support at $B$; the two segments are connected in $C$ by an internal parallel-link connection with pin-ended links parallel to the axis. The vertical forces $P_1 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $P_2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, directed downward, act at the midpoints of the two segments. What is the reaction moment $M_A$ at the fixed support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" interni="C,doppio-pendolo,0" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="1.5 0,0,-1.3,P_1;4.5 0,0,-1.3,P_2" quote="A,C,3 m,0.8;C,B,3 m,0.8" titolo="Beam with an internal parallel-link connection at $C$ and forces $P_1 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $P_2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $M_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

Parallel-link support with horizontal links does not transmit vertical forces, so vertical translation of the segment $CB$ gives $V_B = P_2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$. The rotation of $CB$ around $C$ gives $3 \cdot 12 - 12 \cdot 1.5 + \mathcal{M}_C = 0$, so on the segment $CB$ the couple $\mathcal{M}_C = -18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts, clockwise, and on the segment $AC$ the opposite couple, counterclockwise. The rotation of $AC$ around $A$ gives
$$
M_A - 8 \cdot 1.5 + 18 = 0 ,
$$
then $M_A = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, i.e. $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in direction **clockwise**. The value $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by treating the parallel-link support as a hinge, the value $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ forgetting the transmitted couple.
