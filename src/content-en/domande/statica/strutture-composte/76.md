In the Gerber beam shown, segment $AC$ has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$, and an overhang $BC$ of length $b$. Segment $CD$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, connects to the overhang through an internal hinge at $C$, and rests on a roller at $D$. Span $AB$ has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and carries a downward force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan. Segment $CD$ carries a uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. For what overhang length $b$ is reaction $V_A$ zero?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0;D,12,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" forze="3 0,0,-1.5,P" carichi="C,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,3 0,3 m,0.8;3 0,B,3 m,0.8;B,C,b,0.8;C,D,4 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam with overhang $BC$ of unknown length $b$"}

- $b = 1.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $b = 3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $b = 6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $b = 2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Segment $CD$ is suspended and resultant $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at its midpoint. It therefore transmits a downward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to overhang tip $C$. The rotation of the section $AC$ around $B$, with lengths in meters, gives
$$
-6\,V_A + 12 \cdot 3 - 12\,b = 0 ,
$$
therefore $V_A = (6 - 2\,b)\,\mathrm{kN}$. The reaction vanishes by $b = 3\,\mathrm{m}$, because then the moment about $B$ of the force transmitted in $C$ is equal to and opposite to that of $P$. The value $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by loading the overhang with all the resultant of $24\,\mathrm{kN}$.
