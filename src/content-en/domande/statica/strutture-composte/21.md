The three-hinged frame shown has columns of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a beam of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. At midheight of column $AB$, a horizontal force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is the horizontal reaction $H_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4;D,6,4;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" forze="0 2,1.4,0,F" quote="A,0 2,2 m,0.6;0 2,B,2 m,0.6;B,C,3 m,-0.8;C,D,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ halfway up column $AB$"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right

## Explanation

The force $F$ is applied to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ of elevation, so moment equilibrium of the structure about $A$ gives $6\,V_E - 12 \cdot 2 = 0$, i.e. $V_E = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$. The $CDE$ part is unloaded, so the auxiliary equation around $C$ is $3\,V_E + 4\,H_E = 0$ and gives $H_E = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$. The horizontal translation of the whole structure is
$$
H_A + 12 + H_E = 0 ,
$$
then $H_A = -9\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**. The value $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by dividing $F$ in half between the two pin supports, the value $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ exchanging $H_A$ for $H_E$.
