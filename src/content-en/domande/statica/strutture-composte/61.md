The frame shown consists of three bodies. Column $AB$ and beam segment $BC$ form one body, fixed to the ground at $A$. Beam $CD$ connects to the other bodies through internal hinges $C$ and $D$. Body $DEF$ is pinned to the ground at $F$. Only beam $CD$, of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$, carries the uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the horizontal reaction $H_F$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,2,4;D,4,4;E,6,4;F,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;E-F" cerniere="C;D" vincoli="A,incastro;F,cerniera" carichi="C,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="B,C,2 m,-1.1;C,D,2 m,-1.1;D,E,2 m,-1.1;E,F,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Three-body frame with crossbeam $CD$ under load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $H_F = 0$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left

## Explanation

The body $DEF$ is unloaded and connected to the rest only by the $D$ and $F$ hinges, so for the unloaded member theorem it receives direct forces along the line $DF$, which drops by $4\,\mathrm{m}$ every $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right. On the crossbeam therefore acts in $D$ a force of $(2t,\ -4t)$ components. The rotation of the crossbeam around $C$ gives
$$
-12 \cdot 1 - 2 \cdot 4t = 0 ,
$$
then $t = -1.5$ and the force at $D$ on the crossbeam equals $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The body $DEF$ receives the opposite force at $D$, and the reaction at $F$ balances it: $H_F = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**, and $V_F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$.
