The frame shown consists of body $ABC$, fixed to the ground at $A$, and body $CDE$, supported at $E$ by a roller on a horizontal surface. An internal hinge $C$ connects the bodies. The columns have height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and the beam has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ with $C$ at its midpoint. Segment $CD$ carries a uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the support moment $M_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4;D,6,4;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro;E,carrello" carichi="C,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,C,3 m,-1.1;C,D,3 m,-1.1" titolo="Portal frame with a fixed support at $A$, an internal hinge at $C$, and a roller at $E$"}

- $M_A = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 54\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_A = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The body $CDE$ has the hinge $C$ and $E$ roller Support, which gives a vertical reaction. The auxiliary equation around $C$ for part $CDE$ contains the resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$ and $V_E$ to $3\,\mathrm{m}$:
$$
3\,V_E - 12 \cdot 1.5 = 0 ,
$$
then $V_E = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$. Moment equilibrium of the structure about $A$ gives $M_A + 6 \cdot 6 - 12 \cdot 4.5 = 0$, therefore $M_A = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, **counterclockwise**. The value $54\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ neglects the roller support reaction.
