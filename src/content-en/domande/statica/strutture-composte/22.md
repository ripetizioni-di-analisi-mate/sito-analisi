The Gerber beam shown has the cantilever $AC$, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and fixed at $A$, and the section $CD$, connected to the cantilever by the hinge $C$ and resting on a roller support at $B$, with the $BD$ overhang. At the free end $D$ acts the force $F = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. What is the vertical reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0;D,8,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="D,0,-1.6,F" quote="A,C,3 m,0.8;C,B,3 m,0.8;B,D,2 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam with force $F = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the overhang tip"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward

## Explanation

The $CD$ suspended segment is calculated first. Moment equilibrium about $C$ gives $3\,V_B - 9 \cdot 5 = 0$, then $V_B = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the vertical force equilibrium gives $Y_C + 15 - 9 = 0$, i.e. $Y_C = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$. Therefore the cantilever pulls down the segment $CD$ with $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, and by the principle of action and reaction the segment $CD$ pulls up the cantilever with $6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The vertical translation of the cantilever gives $V_A + 6 = 0$, so the reaction at $A$ equals $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is **Directed Down**. The value $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward arises from the reverse-taken direction of the force of the hinge.
