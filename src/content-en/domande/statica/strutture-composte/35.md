The structure shown is formed by the bodies $ABC$ and $CDE$, hinged to the ground in $A$ and $E$ and connected to each other by the internal hinge $C$; the three $A$, $C$, $E$ hinges are located on the same horizontal line. How is the structure classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,3;C,4,0;D,6,3;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" titolo="Two bodies joined by hinge $C$, collinear with $A$ and $E$"}

- Statically determinate because $s = 3n = 6$
- Statically indeterminate with degree of indeterminacy $1$
- Kinematically unstable because $s < 3n$
- Kinematically unstable because hinges $A$, $C$, and $E$ are collinear

## Explanation

The bodies are $n = 2$ and the scalar constraints are $s = 2 + 2 + 2 = 6$, so $s = 3n$. This condition is *necessary* for static determinacy, but not sufficient, because constraints can be poorly arranged. The center of absolute rotation of the first body is $A$, that of the second is $E$ and the relative center is $C$; the three points are aligned, so by the kinematic ties theorem the structure admits a mechanism in which $C$ moves vertically. So the structure is **unstable**.
