The three-hinged frame shown has columns of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a beam of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. At joint $D$ a horizontal force $F = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What are the components $X_C$ and $Y_C$ of the force that body $ABC$ exerts on body $CDE$ at $C$, taking each component as positive along its coordinate axis?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,8,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" forze="9.6 4,1.6,0,F" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,C,4 m,-0.8;C,D,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with force $F = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ at joint $D$"}

- $X_C = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, $Y_C = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $X_C = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$, $Y_C = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $X_C = -16\,\mathrm{kN}$, $Y_C = 0$
- $X_C = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$, $Y_C = 0$

## Explanation

The body $ABC$ is unloaded and is connected to the rest only by the $A$ and $C$ hinges, so for the **the unloaded member theorem** the force that exchanges in $C$ is directed along the line $AC$. This line rises by $4\,\mathrm{m}$ for every horizontal distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$, so the two components have the same magnitude. Moment equilibrium of the structure about $A$ gives $8\,V_E - 16 \cdot 4 = 0$, i.e. $V_E = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$. The vertical translation of the body $CDE$ gives $Y_C + V_E = 0$, then $Y_C = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $X_C = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$. The $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ components are those of the force that $CDE$ exerts on $ABC$.
