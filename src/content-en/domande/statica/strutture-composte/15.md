The $AC$, $BC$ and $CD$ members are three rigid bodies connected to each other by the hinge $C$ alone. How many scalar unknowns describe the forces transmitted through hinge $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,3;D,3,6" aste="A-C;B-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera;D,carrello,180" titolo="Three members connected at hinge $C$"}

- $2$
- $6$
- $4$
- $3$

## Explanation

A hinge connecting $k$ bodies equals simple $k - 1$ hinges and introduces $2(k - 1)$ unknowns. Here $k = 3$, so the unknowns are $2 \cdot 2 = 4$. You get to the same number by counting the forces on the three bodies: they have in all six components, but their sum is zero, because the pin of the hinge is in equilibrium, so the independent components are four. The value $2$ corresponds to a hinge between only two bodies.
