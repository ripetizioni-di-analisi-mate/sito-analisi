The Gerber beam shown is formed by the section $AC$, bound by a hinge $A$ and a roller support at $B$, the section $CD$, connected to the other two by the $C$ and $D$ internal hinges, and the section $DF$, resting on the $E$ and $F$ roller supports. Only the section $CD$ carries the uniform $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load. What is reaction $V_B$?

- $V_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_B = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_B = 4.8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_B = 16.8\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Segment $CD$ is suspended at both ends. Analyze it as a simply supported beam with supports at $C$ and $D$. Resultant $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at midspan, so each hinge carries $12\,\mathrm{kN}$. On the segment $AC$, therefore, $C$ acts a force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, at $7\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The rotation of $AC$ around $A$ gives
$$
5\,V_B - 12 \cdot 7 = 0 ,
$$
then $V_B = 16.8\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward. The value $4.8\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the intensity of $V_A$, which is directed downward.
