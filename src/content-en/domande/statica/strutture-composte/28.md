The beam shown consists of a cantilever $AC$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at $A$ and a segment $CD$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ pinned to the ground at $D$. A vertical link connects the two segments at $C$. Segment $CD$ carries a uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the support moment $M_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;D,7,0" aste="A-C;C-D" interni="C,pendolo,90" vincoli="A,incastro,180;D,cerniera" carichi="C,D,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,C,3 m,0.8;C,D,4 m,0.8" titolo="Beam with a vertical internal link at $C$ and load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $CD$"}

- $M_A = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_A = 72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The vertical pin-ended link only transmits a vertical force, so the segment $CD$ behaves like a simply supported beam in $C$ and hinged at $D$. The resultant $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ lies on the middle of $CD$, so the pin-ended link carries $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and pushes the cantilever down with $12\,\mathrm{kN}$. The rotation of the cantilever around $A$ gives
$$
M_A - 12 \cdot 3 = 0 ,
$$
then $M_A = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, **counterclockwise**. The value $72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by transmitting to the cantilever all the load of the segment $CD$.
