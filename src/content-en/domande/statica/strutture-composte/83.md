In the structure shown, the upper beam $AB$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, is pinned to the wall at $A$, and is supported at $B$ by a vertical link $BG$ connecting it to the lower beam $CD$. The lower beam has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, a pin at $C$, and a roller at $D$. Point $G$ is a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$. A downward force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the midpoint of the upper beam. What is the reaction $V_D$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,2;B,4,2;C,0,0;G,4,0;D,5,0" aste="A-B;C-D;B-G" cerniere="B;G" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;C,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="2 2,0,-1.2,P" quote="C,G,4 m,0.8;G,D,1 m,0.8" titolo="Two beams, one above the other, connected by link $BG$ and loaded by force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $V_D = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_D = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_D = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_D = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The pin-ended link only transmits a vertical force, so the upper beam is calculated as a simply supported beam in $A$ and $B$. The force $P$ is in half, so the pin-ended link supports the upper beam with $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is in compression. The lower beam then receives at $G$ a force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ down, at $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$, and moment equilibrium about $C$ gives
$$
5\,V_D - 10 \cdot 4 = 0 .
$$
So $V_D = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, up. The value $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained using the arm $1\,\mathrm{m}$ measured by $D$.
