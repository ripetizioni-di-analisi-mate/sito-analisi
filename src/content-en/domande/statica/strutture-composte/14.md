The frame shown has three bodies. Column $AB$ has height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and is fixed to the ground at $A$. Beam $BC$ has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and is pinned at $B$ to column $AB$ and at $C$ to column $CD$. Column $CD$ has height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and is pinned to the ground at $D$. Column $AB$ carries a uniform horizontal load $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to the right, while the beam carries a uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the support moment $M_A$ at the fixed base?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,6,4;D,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B;C" vincoli="A,incastro;D,cerniera" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,2 kN/m,0;B,C,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,C,6 m,-1" titolo="Three-body frame with internal hinges $B$ and $C$, horizontal load $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $AB$, and load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the crossbeam"}

- $M_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_A = 0$

## Explanation

The column $CD$ is unloaded and hinged at the two ends, so it transmits a vertical force to the crossbeam. Only vertical forces act on the crossbeam, and in $B$ the column $AB$ receives a vertical force of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, which has zero moment arm about $A$. The horizontal load of the column has has resultant $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, so the rotation of the column $AB$ around $A$ gives
$$
M_A - 8 \cdot 2 = 0 ,
$$
That is $M_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, **counterclockwise**. The value $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by applying the resultant at $B$ instead of in the middle of the column.
