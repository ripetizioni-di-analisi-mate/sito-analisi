The frame shown is formed by the body $ABC$, hinged to the ground in $A$, and the column $CD$, hinged at $C$ and $D$. The columns are $5\,\mathrm{m}$ high and the crossbeam $BC$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long. At joint $B$, the horizontal force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right; on the crossbeam, at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$, the vertical downward force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts. What is the axial force $N_{CD}$ in column $CD$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,5;C,5,5;D,5,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,cerniera" forze="B,1.4,0,F;2 5,0,-1.4,P" quote="A,B,5 m,0.8;B,2 5,2 m,-0.8;2 5,C,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Unsymmetrical frame with forces $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $N_{CD} = -18\,\mathrm{kN}$, compression
- $N_{CD} = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $N_{CD} = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$, compression
- $N_{CD} = -22\,\mathrm{kN}$, compression

## Explanation

The column $CD$ is unloaded and hinged at the two extremes, so it behaves like a vertical pin-ended link and the reaction at $D$ is the only $V_D$. Rotating the whole frame around $A$ gives
$$
5\,V_D - 10 \cdot 5 - 20 \cdot 2 = 0 ,
$$
Therefore $V_D = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward. The ground pushes the column upward in $D$ and the crossbeam pushes it downward in $C$, so the column is **in compression** and $N_{CD} = -18\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by forgetting the force $F$, the value $-22\,\mathrm{kN}$ by measuring the moment $P$ arm from $C$ instead of $A$.
