The three-hinged arch shown is formed by the straight $AC$ and $CB$ members, symmetrical compared to the vertical for the crown $C$, with the springings at the same elevation. At the middle points of the two members two equal vertical forces $P$ act, directed downward. In what direction does the force transmitted between the two halves at crown $C$ act?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,3;B,8,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="2 1.5,0,-1.4,P;6 1.5,0,-1.4,P" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.8;4 0,B,4 m,0.8;4 0,C,3 m,-0.6" titolo="Symmetric three-hinged arch with two equal forces $P$"}

- Vertical
- Along line $AC$
- Horizontal
- The force at the crown is zero

## Explanation

The structure and load are symmetrical, so the vertical reactions are the same and moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $V_B = P$, so also $V_A = P$. On the $CB$ half, the load $P$, the reaction $V_B = P$ and the force of the crown, of vertical $Y_C$ component, act. The equilibrium at the vertical translation of $CB$ gives $Y_C + P - P = 0$, therefore $Y_C = 0$. The horizontal component is the thrust, different from zero, so the force in the crown is **horizontal**.
