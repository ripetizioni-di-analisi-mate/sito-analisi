The frame shown has two columns of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed to the ground at $A$ and $E$. Beam $BD$ has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$. It is connected to column $AB$ by hinge $B$ and supported on column $DE$ by a vertical link at $D$. The beam carries a uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and a horizontal force $F = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right. What is the support moment $M_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;D,6,4;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-D;D-E" cerniere="B" interni="D,pendolo,90" vincoli="A,incastro;E,incastro" carichi="B,D,0.5,0.5,q" forze="3 4,1.5,0,F" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,D,6 m,-1.1" titolo="Crossbeam supported by two fixed-base columns, with load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and force $F = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $M_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_A = 40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The internal two-force vertical link in $D$ only transmits one vertical force, so the horizontal $F$ force switches all to the hinge $B$. Therefore the column $AB$ receives at $B$ a horizontal force of $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and a vertical force of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, the latter with zero moment arm about $A$. The rotation of the column $AB$ around $A$ gives
$$
M_A - 5 \cdot 4 = 0 ,
$$
then $M_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, **counterclockwise**. The column $DE$ receives only the vertical force of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$, and its fixed support has zero moment.
