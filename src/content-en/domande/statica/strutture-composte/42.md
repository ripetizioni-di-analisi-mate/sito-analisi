The three-hinged frame shown has columns of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a beam of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. A counterclockwise couple acts at hinge $C$ and has magnitude $\mathcal{M} = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Which statement about the support reactions is correct?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,8,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" coppie="C,1,M" quote="A,B,4 m,0.6;B,C,4 m,-0.8;C,D,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with couple $\mathcal{M} = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $C$"}

- None of the reactions depend on which body receives the couple
- $H_A$ and $H_E$ depend on which body receives the couple, while $V_A$ and $V_E$ are independent of that choice
- $V_A$ and $V_E$ depend on which body receives the couple, while $H_A$ and $H_E$ are independent of that choice
- All four reactions depend on which body receives the couple

## Explanation

The hinge does not transmit couples, so the couple acts on only one of the two bodies. The equations of the whole structure contain the couple in each case, so moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $8\,V_E + 16 = 0$, i.e. $V_E = -2\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$, whatever the loaded body is. The auxiliary equation for $CDE$ contains the couple only if it is applied to $CDE$:
$$
4\,V_E + 4\,H_E = 0 \quad \text{or} \quad 4\,V_E + 4\,H_E + 16 = 0 .
$$
Therefore $H_E$ equals $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ in the first case and $-2\,\mathrm{kN}$ in the second, and with it changes $H_A = -H_E$.
