The three-hinged arch shown is formed by the straight $AC$ and $CB$ members. Compared to the springing $A$, the crown $C$ is located $4\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ higher, the springing $B$ is located $10\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ higher. The member $AC$ carries the load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, uniform on the horizontal projection. What is the horizontal reaction $H_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,4;B,10,2" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" carichi="0 4.6,4 4.6,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.8;4 0,10 0,6 m,0.8;10 0,B,2 m,0.8;A,0 4,4 m,-0.8" tratteggi="0,0 10,0" titolo="Arch with springings at different elevations and load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ distributed over the projection of $AC$"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $36\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $14.4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right

## Explanation

Springings are at different elevations, so the moment equation around $A$ also contains $H_B$, with the arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$. The resultant of the load is $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and acts at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The rotation of the whole arch around $A$ and the auxiliary equation for the $CB$ part around $C$ give
$$
\begin{cases}
10\,V_B - 2\,H_B - 24 \cdot 2 = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_B + 2\,H_B = 0
\end{cases}
$$
Adding the two equations gives $16\,V_B = 48$, then $V_B = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $H_B = -9\,\mathrm{kN}$. Therefore the horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$, **to the right**. The value $14.4\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by neglecting the term in $H_B$ in the rotation around $A$, i.e. the difference in level between springings.
