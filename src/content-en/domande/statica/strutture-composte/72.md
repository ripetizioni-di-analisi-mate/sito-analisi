The beam shown consists of a segment $AC$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ pinned to the ground at $A$ and a segment $CB$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. An internal hinge $C$ connects the segments. At $B$ a parallel-link support with vertical links restrains vertical translation and rotation of segment $CB$. Segment $CB$ carries a uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the moment reaction $M_B$ at the parallel-link support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,doppio-pendolo,270" carichi="C,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,C,3 m,0.8;C,B,4 m,0.8" titolo="Beam with a pin at $A$, an internal hinge at $C$, and a parallel-link support at $B$"}

- $M_B = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_B = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_B = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_B = 32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

The segment $AC$ is unloaded and is hinged at $A$ and $C$, so for the unloaded member theorem it only transmits in $C$ a direct force along $AC$, i.e. horizontal. The segment $CB$ has no horizontal loads, so this force is zero, and in $C$ no force passes. All of the load $CB$, resulting in $2 \cdot 4 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$, then goes to parallel-link support. $CB$ moment equilibrium about $B$ gives
$$
M_B + 8 \cdot 2 = 0 ,
$$
therefore $M_B = -16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, i.e. $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in **clockwise**, and $V_B = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$. The segment $CB$ behaves like a fixed cantilever in $B$.
