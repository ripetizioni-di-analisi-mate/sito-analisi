The Gerber beam shown has the section $AB$ fixed at $A$, the section $BD$ connected to $AB$ by the hinge $B$ and resting on a $C$ roller support, and the section $DE$ connected to $BD$ by the hinge $D$ and resting on a $E$ roller support. The whole beam carries the uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the reaction $V_C$ at roller $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,6,0;D,8,0;E,11,0" aste="A-B;B-D;D-E" cerniere="B;D" vincoli="A,incastro,180;C,carrello;E,carrello" carichi="A,E,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,2 m,0.8;B,C,4 m,0.8;C,D,2 m,0.8;D,E,3 m,0.8" titolo="Three-segment Gerber beam under uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $V_C = 27\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_C = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_C = 36\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_C = 21\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The segment $DE$ carries no further segments and is calculated first: its load has resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in half, so the hinge $D$ carries $6\,\mathrm{kN}$. On the section $BD$ then act the resultant $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ of its load, at $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$, and the force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ down in $D$, at $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$. Moment equilibrium of $BD$ about hinge $B$ gives
$$
4\,V_C - 24 \cdot 3 - 6 \cdot 6 = 0 ,
$$
Therefore $V_C = 27\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward. The value $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by forgetting the force at $D$, the value $36\,\mathrm{kN}$ transmitting in $D$ all the load of the segment $DE$.
