The three-hinged frame shown has columns of height $5\,\mathrm{m}$, a beam of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. The beam carries a uniform load $q = 12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the horizontal reaction $H_A$ at hinge $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,5;C,4,5;D,8,5;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" carichi="B,D,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,5 m,-0.8;B,C,4 m,-1.1;C,D,4 m,-1.1" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with load $q = 12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the crossbeam"}

- $19.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $38.4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $19.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $9.6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right

## Explanation

The pin supports are at the same elevation, so the vertical reactions are those of the simply supported beam: $V_A = V_E = \dfrac{12 \cdot 8}{2}\,\mathrm{kN} = 48\,\mathrm{kN}$. In the **auxiliary equation** for the $CDE$ part around $C$, the hinge $E$ is located $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right and $5\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C$, and the load of the right half has resultant $48\,\mathrm{kN}$ $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$:
$$
4\,V_E + 5\,H_E - 48 \cdot 2 = 0 .
$$
Therefore $H_E = -19.2\,\mathrm{kN}$, and horizontal force equilibrium of the structure gives $H_A = -H_E = 19.2\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. **to the right**. The same value is obtained from $\dfrac{qL^2}{8h}$; the value $38.4\,\mathrm{kN}$ arises from using $\dfrac{qL^2}{4}$ instead of $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$.
