The knee-length beam shown is formed by the horizontal cantilever $AC$, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and fixed at $A$, and by the inclined segment $CB$, connected to the cantilever by the hinge $C$. End $B$ is located $4\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above $C$ and rests on a roller on a horizontal surface. At the midpoint of $CB$ the vertical force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the reaction moment $M_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,7,3" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="5 1.5,0,-1.5,P" quote="A,C,3 m,0.8;C,5 0,2 m,0.8;5 0,7 0,2 m,0.8;7 0,B,3 m,0.6" titolo="Bent beam with inclined segment $CB$ and force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $M_A = 30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_A = 50\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

Segment $CB$ has only hinge $C$ and roller $B$. It is therefore suspended and should be analyzed first. The roller support has the horizontal support surface, so its $V_B$ reaction is vertical. In the rotation of $CB$ around $C$ count the horizontal distances, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ for $V_B$ and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ for $P$:
$$
4\,V_B - 10 \cdot 2 = 0 ,
$$
then $V_B = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The translation of $CB$ then gives $C$ a force of $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward on the inclined beam and no horizontal force. On the cantilever therefore acts in $C$ the force of $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ down, with a moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ about $A$, and you get **$M_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise**. The value $50\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is achieved by neglecting roller support at $B$, as if the cantilever carried the force $P$ alone.
