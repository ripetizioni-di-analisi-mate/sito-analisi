In a Gerber beam the cantilever $AC$, fixed at $A$, is connected by the internal hinge $C$ to the section $CB$. It isolates the cantilever, drawing its free-body diagram with fixed support reactions and forces exchanged in the hinge. How many scalar unknowns appear on the diagram?

- $5$
- $3$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

Fixed support prevents the two translation and rotation, so it exhibits three reactions, $H_A$, $V_A$ and $M_A$. The internal hinge prevents the relative displacement of the two connected points and leaves the relative rotation free, therefore it transmits a force of $X_C$, $Y_C$ components and no couple. The cantilever's free-body diagram therefore contains $3 + 2 = 5$ unknowns. The three equations of the cantilever alone are not enough, and the two extra unknowns are found with the equilibrium of the segment $CB$.
