In the Gerber beam shown, segment $AC$ has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$, and an overhang $BC$. Segment $CD$ connects to it at internal hinge $C$ and rests on a roller at $D$. Downward forces $P_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $P_2 = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ act at the positions shown. What is the reaction $V_B$ at roller $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0;D,11,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" forze="2 0,0,-1.6,P_1;9 0,0,-1.6,P_2" quote="A,2 0,2 m,0.8;2 0,B,3 m,0.8;B,C,2 m,0.8;C,9 0,2 m,0.8;9 0,D,2 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam under forces $P_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $P_2 = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $V_B = 4.8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_B = 27.2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_B = 12.8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_B = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The suspended span $CD$ carries force $P_2$ halfway between $C$ and $D$. Thus $V_D = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the hinge carries $8\,\mathrm{kN}$. On segment $AC$, the hinge at $C$ exerts a downward force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at a distance $7\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Taking moments for segment $AC$ about $A$ gives
$$
5\,V_B - 12 \cdot 2 - 8 \cdot 7 = 0 ,
$$
so $V_B = \dfrac{80}{5}\,\mathrm{kN} = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. Neglecting the hinge force gives $4.8\,\mathrm{kN}$. Assuming the entire force $P_2$ is transmitted at $C$ gives $27.2\,\mathrm{kN}$. Using a moment arm of $5\,\mathrm{m}$ for the force at $C$ gives $12.8\,\mathrm{kN}$.
