The structure shown is formed by the three bodies $AB$, $BC$ and $CD$: the column $AB$ is hinged to the ground in $A$, the column $CD$ is hinged to the ground in $D$, and the crossbeam $BC$ is connected to the columns by the internal hinges $B$ and $C$. How is the structure classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,5,4;D,5,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B;C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,cerniera" titolo="Three bodies connected by internal hinges $B$ and $C$"}

- Kinematically unstable, with $3n = 9$ and $s = 8$
- Statically determinate because each body has two hinges
- Statically indeterminate, with $s = 10$
- Statically determinate, with $3n = s = 8$

## Explanation

Bodies are $n = 3$, so degrees of freedom are $3n = 9$. The $A$ and $D$ pin supports and the $B$ and $C$ internal hinges are all double constraints, therefore $s = 2 \cdot 4 = 8$. Because $s < 3n$, the structure is **unstable** with at least one degree of kinematic instability. The mechanism is that of the four-bar linkage, in which the columns rotate around $A$ and $D$ and the crossbeam traverses horizontally.
