The three-hinged frame shown has columns that flare outward toward the base. Hinges $A$ and $E$ are at the same elevation and are spaced $8\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. Joints $B$ and $D$ are $4\,\mathrm{m}$ higher and $1\,\mathrm{m}$ farther inward than $A$ and $E$, respectively. Beam $BD$ has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, an internal hinge $C$ at midspan, and a uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the horizontal reaction $H_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1,4;C,4,4;D,7,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" carichi="B,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="B,C,3 m,0.8;C,D,3 m,0.8;A,1 0,1 m,-0.8;1 0,7 0,6 m,-0.8;7 0,E,1 m,-0.8;E,8 4,4 m,0.8" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with inclined columns and load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the crossbeam"}

- $6.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right

## Explanation

The load equals $6 \cdot 6 = 36\,\mathrm{kN}$ in all and is symmetrical, therefore $V_A = V_E = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$. On the body $ABC$ acts the loading part that is on the section $BC$, i.e. $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ applied in the middle of $BC$, $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $C$. The point $A$ is located $4\,\mathrm{m}$ on the left and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C$, so the auxiliary equation for the part $ABC$ around $C$ is
$$
-4\,V_A + 4\,H_A + 18 \cdot 1.5 = 0 .
$$
With $V_A = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$, you get $4\,H_A = 72 - 27 = 45$, then **$H_A = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right**. The load only covers $6$ of the $8\,\mathrm{m}$ of span, so the formula $\dfrac{qL^2}{8h}$ does not apply: with $L = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ it would give the wrong value $6.75\,\mathrm{kN}$.
