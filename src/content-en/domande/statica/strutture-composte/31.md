The frame shown is formed by the body $ABC$, hinged to the ground in $A$, and the inclined member $CD$, hinged to the body $ABC$ in $C$ and to the ground in $D$. The only load is the horizontal force $F$ at joint $B$. In what direction does the reaction at pin $D$ act?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,cerniera" forze="B,1.4,0,F" quote="A,B,4 m,0.6;B,C,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Frame with inclined member $CD$ pinned at both ends"}

- Vertically
- Horizontally
- Along line $BD$
- Along line $CD$

## Explanation

The member $CD$ is unloaded and is only connected to the rest of the structure by the $C$ and $D$ hinges. For the **the unloaded member theorem** the forces it receives at $C$ and $D$ are opposite and directed along the line $CD$. The reaction at $D$ is the force that the soil exerts on the member, so it is directed along $CD$. The member drops by $4\,\mathrm{m}$ each $2\,\mathrm{m}$ horizontally, so the vertical component of the reaction is double that of the horizontal. The reaction would be vertical if the member was vertical.
