The beam shown consists of a segment $AC$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at $A$ and a segment $CB$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ supported at $B$ by a roller against a vertical surface. A parallel-link connection with vertical links joins the segments at $C$. A downward force acts at the midpoint of segment $CB$ and has magnitude $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the support moment $M_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" interni="C,doppio-pendolo,90" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello,0" forze="5.5 0,0,-1.3,P" quote="A,C,4 m,0.8;C,5.5 0,1.5 m,0.8;5.5 0,B,1.5 m,0.8" titolo="Beam with an internal parallel-link connection with vertical links and force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $M_A = 40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 55\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 27.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The roller support at $B$ only gives a horizontal reaction, which for the horizontal equilibrium of $CB$ is zero. Parallel-link support at vertical links transmits a vertical force and a couple, so all the load of the segment $CB$ switches to the segment $AC$: the vertical force equals $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the couple, from the rotation of $CB$ around $C$, equals $10 \cdot 1.5 = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. You get to the same result with the rotation of the whole beam around $A$, in which the roller support does not appear because its reaction is zero:
$$
M_A - 10 \cdot 5.5 = 0 .
$$
Therefore $M_A = 55\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, **counterclockwise**. The value $40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ forgets the couple transmitted by parallel-link support.
