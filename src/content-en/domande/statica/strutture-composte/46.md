The Gerber beam shown consists of a cantilever $AC$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at $A$ and a segment $CB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$. Hinge $C$ connects the segment to the cantilever, and a roller supports it at $B$. Segment $CB$ carries a triangular load that increases from zero at $C$ to $q_0 = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. What vertical force $Y_C$, positive upward, does the cantilever exert on segment $CB$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,9,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="C,B,0,0.9,q_0" quote="A,C,3 m,0.8;C,B,6 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam with a triangular load on segment $CB$, of intensity $q_0 = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$"}

- $Y_C = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $Y_C = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $Y_C = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $Y_C = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Segment $CB$ is suspended, so analyze it first as a beam simply supported at $C$ and $B$. The resultant triangular load is the area of the diagram, $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot 6 = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$, and passes through the triangle centroid, $\dfrac{2}{3} \cdot 6 = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$. Moment equilibrium of segment $CB$ about $B$ gives
$$
-6\,Y_C + 18 \cdot 2 = 0 ,
$$
Therefore $Y_C = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward. The value $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by putting the resultant at $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the span from $C$, i.e. the part where the load is zero.
