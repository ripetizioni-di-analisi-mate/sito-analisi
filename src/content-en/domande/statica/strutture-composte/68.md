The frame shown consists of body $ABC$ fixed to the ground at $A$ and body $CDE$ pinned to the ground at $E$. A horizontal link connects the two bodies at $C$ at the elevation of the beam. The columns have height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and the beam has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ with $C$ at its midpoint. Segment $CD$ carries a uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the horizontal reaction $H_E$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4;D,6,4;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" interni="C,pendolo,0" vincoli="A,incastro;E,cerniera" carichi="C,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,C,3 m,-1.1;C,D,3 m,-1.1" titolo="Portal frame with a fixed support at $A$, a horizontal internal link at $C$, and a pin at $E$"}

- $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $H_E = 0$

## Explanation

The internal two-force link only transmits a horizontal force, the line of action of which is the line of the crossbeam. For the **auxiliary equation of the two-force link** is zero the sum of the moments of the external forces of the $CDE$ part about any point of this line, and you choose the joint $D$, which lies on the vertical line through $E$. About $D$ the reaction $V_E$ has zero moment arm, $H_E$ is applied $4\,\mathrm{m}$ lower and the resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ stands $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left, therefore
$$
4\,H_E + 12 \cdot 1.5 = 0 .
$$
So $H_E = -4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**. The value $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by applying the resultant at $C$, $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $D$.
