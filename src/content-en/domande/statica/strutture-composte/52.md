The Gerber beam shown consists of a cantilever $AC$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at $A$ and a segment $CB$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Hinge $C$ connects the segment to the cantilever, and a roller supports it at $B$. At $C$ a counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts on the cantilever. What is the support moment $M_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" coppie="C,1,M" quote="A,C,3 m,0.8;C,B,4 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam with couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ applied to the cantilever at $C$"}

- $M_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_A = 5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

The couple is applied to the cantilever, so the segment $CB$ is unloaded. Moment equilibrium of $CB$ around $C$ gives $4\,V_B = 0$, therefore $V_B = 0$ and the hinge does not transmit forces. On the cantilever then act only the couple and the reactions of fixed support, and moment equilibrium gives
$$
M_A + 10 = 0 .
$$
So $M_A = -10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, i.e. $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in **clockwise**, opposite the applied couple.
