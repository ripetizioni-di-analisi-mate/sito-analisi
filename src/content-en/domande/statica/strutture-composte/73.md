The frame shown has external hinges $A$ and $E$ at the same elevation, columns of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and a beam of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$. An internal connection with two parallel vertical links joins the two bodies at midspan. The beam carries a uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the horizontal reaction $H_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,8,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" interni="C,doppio-pendolo,90" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" carichi="B,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,C,4 m,-1.1;C,D,4 m,-1.1" titolo="Portal frame with an internal parallel-link connection with vertical links and load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $H_A = 0$

## Explanation

Parallel-link support at vertical links prevents relative vertical translation and relative rotation, then transmits a vertical force and a couple and no horizontal force. For the **auxiliary equation** of parallel-link support, the sum of horizontal forces on the $CDE$ part is zero. The load is vertical, therefore $H_E = 0$, and horizontal force equilibrium of the structure gives $H_A = -H_E = 0$. The portal frame has no thrust; the value $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ is that of the portal frame with the pin at $C$.
