The three-hinged frame shown has columns of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a beam of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. At hinge $C$ a downward force $P = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ is applied to body $ABC$. What vertical force does body $ABC$ exert on body $CDE$ at $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,8,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" forze="C,0,-1.5,P" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,C,4 m,-0.8;C,D,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with force $P = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$ applied to body $ABC$"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- No vertical force
- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward

## Explanation

Vertical reactions do not depend on the body receiving $P$, and moment equilibrium of the structure about $A$ gives $8\,V_E - 24 \cdot 4 = 0$, then $V_E = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The body $CDE$ is unloaded, so only $V_E$ and the force $Y_C$ it receives from $ABC$ act on its vertical translation:
$$
Y_C + 12 = 0 .
$$
So $Y_C = -12\,\mathrm{kN}$: Body $ABC$ pulls body $CDE$ down with $12\,\mathrm{kN}$. If $P$ were applied to $CDE$, the same equation would give $Y_C - 24 + 12 = 0$, i.e. $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
