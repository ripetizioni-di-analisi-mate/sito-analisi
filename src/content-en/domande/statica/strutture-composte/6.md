A parabolic three-hinged arch has the springings $A$ and $B$ at the same elevation, the span $L = 20\,\mathrm{m}$, the $f = 5\,\mathrm{m}$ rise and the crown $C$ at midspan. Carry the load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, uniform on the horizontal projection. What is the thrust $H$?

- $H = 60\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $H = 120\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $H = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $H = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The thrust is the bending moment at midspan of the equivalent simply supported beam divided by the rise, $H = \dfrac{M_C^0}{f}$. With the uniform load and the midspan crown you have $M_C^0 = \dfrac{qL^2}{8} = \dfrac{6 \cdot 400}{8}\,\mathrm{kN\,m} = 300\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Therefore
$$
H = \frac{300}{5}\,\mathrm{kN} = 60\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The value $120\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained with $\dfrac{qL^2}{4}$ instead of $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$, the value $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ using half the span instead of span.
