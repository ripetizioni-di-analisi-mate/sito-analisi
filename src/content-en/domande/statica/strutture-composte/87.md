The beam shown consists of a segment $AC$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at $A$ and a segment $CB$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ pinned to the ground at $B$. An inclined link joins the segments at $C$ and transmits only a force along the line through $C$ that slopes upward to the right at an angle $45°$. A downward force acts at the midpoint of segment $CB$ and has magnitude $P = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the horizontal reaction $H_B$ at hinge $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" interni="C,pendolo,45" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,cerniera" forze="5 0,0,-1.5,P" quote="A,C,3 m,0.8;C,5 0,2 m,0.8;5 0,B,2 m,0.8" titolo="Beam with an internal link inclined at $45°$ at point $C$, and force $P = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $H_B = 0$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left

## Explanation

The internal two-force link transmits to the segment $CB$ a direct force along the line at $45°$, then with equal horizontal and vertical components, which are indicated with $t$. In the rotation of the section $CB$ about hinge $B$ the horizontal component has zero moment arm, the vertical component has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and the force $P$ has a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$:
$$
-4\,t + 8 \cdot 2 = 0 ,
$$
therefore $t = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the horizontal component equals $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right. The horizontal translation of $CB$ then gives $H_B + 4 = 0$, i.e. $H_B = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$: the reaction of the hinge $B$ equals $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**. With an internal two-force vertical link you would have $H_B = 0$.
