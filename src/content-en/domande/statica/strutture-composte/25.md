The Gerber beam shown has the segment $AC$ bound by a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, with the $BC$ overhang, and the segment $CD$ connected in $C$ by an internal hinge and resting on a roller support at $D$. The only load is the uniform $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load on the $BC$ overhang. What are reaction $V_D$ and the vertical force $Y_C$ transmitted through the hinge?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" carichi="B,C,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,C,2 m,0.8;C,D,4 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam with load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ applied only to overhang $BC$"}

- $V_D = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, $Y_C = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_D = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$, $Y_C = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_D = 0$, $Y_C = 0$
- $V_D = 0$, $Y_C = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Segment $AC$ is the supporting segment because pin $A$ and roller $B$ make it statically determinate on its own; segment $CD$ is suspended. For the order of calculation we study first the suspended segment. On $CD$ they do not act loaded, so the only forces are $Y_C$ and $V_D$. The rotation of $CD$ around $C$ gives $4\,V_D = 0$, i.e. $V_D = 0$, and the vertical force equilibrium then gives $Y_C = 0$. Therefore **$V_D = 0$ and $Y_C = 0$**: the load applied to the supporting segment is supported only by $A$ and $B$ constraints, as in a simply supported beam with overhang.
