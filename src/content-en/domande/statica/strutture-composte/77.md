The three-hinged arch shown is formed by the members $AC$ and $CB$, connected by the hinge $C$. The springing $A$ is a hinge and the springing $B$ is a roller support on a horizontal surface; springings are not linked by a tie. How is the structure classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,3;B,8,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,8 m,0.8" titolo="Untied arch with a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$"}

- Statically determinate
- Statically indeterminate with degree of indeterminacy $1$
- Statically determinate because hinges $A$ and $C$ and roller $B$ are not collinear
- Kinematically unstable because $s = 5 < 3n = 6$

## Explanation

The bodies are $n = 2$, with $3n = 6$ degrees of freedom. The $A$ and $C$ hinge give two scalar constraints each and the roller support only one, therefore $s = 5$. Since $s < 3n$, the structure is **unstable**: the two members can open, with $B$ flowing horizontally. A tie between springings adds the constraint that is missing and makes the arch statically determinate.
