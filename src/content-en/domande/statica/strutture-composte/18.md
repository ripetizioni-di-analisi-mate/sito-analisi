The three-hinged frame shown has columns of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a beam of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. Segment $BC$ carries a uniform load $q = 8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the magnitude of the force transmitted by hinge $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4;D,6,4;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" carichi="B,C,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m,0.6;B,C,3 m,-1.1;C,D,3 m,-1.1" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with load $q = 8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on segment $BC$"}

- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The body $CDE$ is unloaded and is hinged at $C$ and $E$, so the force it receives at $C$ is directed along the line $CE$, which drops by $4\,\mathrm{m}$ each $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Moment equilibrium of the structure about $A$ gives $6\,V_E - 24 \cdot 1.5 = 0$, i.e. $V_E = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the vertical translation of $CDE$ says that the force at $C$ has vertical component of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$. Since the force is parallel to $CE$, the horizontal component equals $6 \cdot \dfrac{3}{4}\,\mathrm{kN} = 4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. Therefore the intensity is
$$
\sqrt{4.5^2 + 6^2}\,\mathrm{kN} = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ values are the only components.
