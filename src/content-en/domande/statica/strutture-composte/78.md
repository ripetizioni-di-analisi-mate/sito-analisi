The shelter shown is formed by the column $AE$, of height $7\,\mathrm{m}$ and fixed to the ground at $A$, and the beam $BD$, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, hinged to the column in $B$, at $4\,\mathrm{m}$ in height. The $D$ end of the beam is supported by the tie $DE$, which connects it to the $E$ summit of the column. The beam carries the uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the force in the tie rod?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;E,0,7;D,4,4" aste="A-E;B-D" catene="D-E" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,incastro" carichi="B,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,E,3 m,0.8;B,D,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Canopy with beam $BD$ supported by tie rod $DE$ under load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The tie acts as a two-force link and transmits only a force along $DE$. The link rises $3\,\mathrm{m}$ for every $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left and has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$. In tension it carries $N$ and pulls on $D$ with an upward component $\dfrac{3}{5}\,N$. Taking moments about the beam hinge $B$, where the resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ has moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$, gives
$$
4 \cdot \frac{3}{5}\,N - 12 \cdot 2 = 0 .
$$
Therefore $N = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$, of **tension**. The $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ values are the vertical and horizontal components of the force in the tie.
