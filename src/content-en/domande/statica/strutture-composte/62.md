The frame shown consists of three bodies: the column $AB$ with the section $BC$, fixed to the ground at $A$; the crossbeam $CD$; the body $DEF$, hinged to the ground in $F$. The bodies are connected by the internal hinges $C$ and $D$. In addition to the three equilibrium equations for the entire structure, how many auxiliary equations are needed to find just the external reactions?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,2,4;D,4,4;E,6,4;F,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;E-F" cerniere="C;D" vincoli="A,incastro;F,cerniera" titolo="Three-body frame with internal hinges $C$ and $D$"}

- One
- Three
- Two
- None

## Explanation

The external reactions are three at the fixed end, $H_A$, $V_A$, $M_A$, and two in hinge, $H_F$, $V_F$, i.e. five. The equations of the whole structure are three, so there are two missing. Each internal hinge divides the frame into two parts connected only by it, therefore for each the **auxiliary equation** of zero moment is valid. The two auxiliary equations in $C$ and $D$ complete the system in the five external reactions.
