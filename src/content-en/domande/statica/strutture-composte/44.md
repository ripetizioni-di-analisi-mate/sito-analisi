In a three-hinged portal frame the body $ABC$ balance gives, by the force that the body $CDE$ exerts on $ABC$ in the hinge $C$, the $X_C = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $Y_C = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$ components, positive according to the axes. What force acts at $C$ on body $CDE$?

- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

On $ABC$ the force at $C$ has $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. For the **principle of action and reaction** the body $ABC$ exerts on the $CDE$, at the same point, the opposite force, of components $-5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $3\,\mathrm{kN}$. So on $CDE$ they act $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
