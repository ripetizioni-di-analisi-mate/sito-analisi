The beam shown is formed by the section $AC$, fixed at $A$, and the section $CB$, bound in $B$ by a roller support on a vertical surface; the two segments are connected in $C$ by an internal parallel-link connection with the vertical links. What forces and couples do the two segments transmit through the parallel-link connection?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" interni="C,doppio-pendolo,90" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello,0" titolo="Beam with an internal parallel-link connection with vertical links at $C$"}

- A horizontal force and a couple
- A vertical force and a couple
- A vertical force and a horizontal force
- A couple only

## Explanation

Parallel-link support prevents relative translation in the direction of pin-ended links and relative rotation, and leaves the relative translation in the perpendicular direction free. Here pin-ended links are vertical, so the two segments cannot move relative to each other vertically or rotate, but they can sliding motion horizontally. Therefore the constraint transmits an **vertical force and a couple**, and no horizontal force. The horizontal reaction of the segment $CB$ is given by the roller support at $B$.
