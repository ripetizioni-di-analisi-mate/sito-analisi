In the structure shown the column $AC$, of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed to the ground at $A$. The beam $DB$, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, is a single rigid body: it is connected to the column by the hinge $C$, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $D$, and rests on a roller at $B$. At the midpoint of segment $CB$ the vertical force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward, and at the end $D$ the horizontal force $F = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is the reaction moment $M_A$ at the fixed support?

::struttura{nodi="D,0,4;C,2,4;B,6,4;A,2,0" aste="D-C;C-B;A-C" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro;B,carrello" forze="4 4,0,-1.5,P;D,1.3,0,F" quote="D,C,2 m,0.8;C,4 4,2 m,0.8;4 4,B,2 m,0.8;A,C,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Beam $DB$ supported on column $AC$ by hinge $C$, with forces $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $F = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $M_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 0$

## Explanation

The beam $DB$ is first studied. The roller support at $B$ gives a vertical reaction, so moment equilibrium of the beam about $C$ gives $4\,V_B - 12 \cdot 2 = 0$, i.e. $V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$. Vertical force equilibrium of the beam gives an upward force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ that the column exerts on the beam in $C$, and the horizontal force equilibrium gives a leftward force of $3\,\mathrm{kN}$. By the principle of action and reaction the column receives at $C$ a force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward and one of $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right. The vertical force acts along the axis of the column and has zero moment arm about $A$, while the horizontal force has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore moment equilibrium of the column about $A$ gives $M_A - 4 \cdot 3 = 0$, i.e. **$M_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise**.
