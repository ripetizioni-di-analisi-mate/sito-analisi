The three-hinged arch shown is formed by the straight $AC$ and $CB$ members, with the springings at the same elevation, the span $8\,\mathrm{m}$ and the crown $C$ at midspan, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ above the springings. At the midpoint of the member $AC$, the vertical force $P = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the magnitude of the reaction at springing $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,4;B,8,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="2 2,0,-1.6,P" quote="A,2 0,2 m,0.8;2 0,4 0,2 m,0.8;4 0,B,4 m,0.8;4 0,C,4 m,0.6" titolo="Three-hinged arch with force $P = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ on half $AC$"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

In the equivalent simply supported beam the force is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$, then $V_B = \dfrac{16 \cdot 2}{8}\,\mathrm{kN} = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$. The $CB$ half is unloaded, so the reaction at $B$ is directed along the line $BC$, which rises by $4\,\mathrm{m}$ each $4\,\mathrm{m}$. It follows that the horizontal component has the same magnitude as the vertical one, $4\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the intensity of the reaction is
$$
\sqrt{4^2 + 4^2}\,\mathrm{kN} = 4\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The value $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the only vertical component.
