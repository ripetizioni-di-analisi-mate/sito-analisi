The three-hinged arch in the figure consists of straight members $AC$ and $CB$, with the supports at the same level and a span of $12\,\mathrm{m}$; the crown $C$ lies $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $A$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ above the supports. The arch carries a load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, uniform over the horizontal projection of the whole span. What is the thrust $H$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,4;B,12,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" carichi="0 4.6,12 4.6,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.8;4 0,B,8 m,0.8;4 0,C,4 m,-0.6" titolo="Three-hinged arch with an off-center crown and load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the whole span"}

- $H = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $H = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $H = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $H = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

In the equivalent simply supported beam, with span $12\,\mathrm{m}$, the load has resultant $2 \cdot 12 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan, so $V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$. To the left of the crown section, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, act $V_A$ and the load on that $4\,\mathrm{m}$ segment, whose resultant is $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the section, so
$$
M_C^0 = 12 \cdot 4 - 8 \cdot 2 = 32\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The thrust is then
$$
H = \frac{M_C^0}{f} = \frac{32}{4}\,\mathrm{kN} = 8\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The value $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by using the midspan moment $\dfrac{qL^2}{8} = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, as if the crown were at midspan, and $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ by forgetting the load to the left of the crown.
