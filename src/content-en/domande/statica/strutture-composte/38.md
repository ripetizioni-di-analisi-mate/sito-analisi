A three-hinged arch is drawn with all three hinges on the same horizontal line. At the internal hinge a vertical load would produce a $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ moment in the equivalent simply supported beam. Can any finite horizontal thrust maintain equilibrium in this configuration?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;P,1,1.3,-;C,3,0;Q,5,1.3,-;B,6,0" aste="A-P;P-C;C-Q;Q-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" tratteggi="0,0 6,0" forze="C,0,-1.6,P" titolo="Three collinear hinges on a two-part rigid arch"}

- Yes, a thrust of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ suffices.
- No, the thrust has zero lever arm about the hinge.
- Yes, the internal hinge transmits the remaining moment.
- Yes, the thrust is zero because the loads are vertical.

## Explanation

The hinge requires $M_C=M_\mathrm{beam}-Hf=0$, where $f$ is its height above the springing line. With $f=0$, the contribution $Hf$ vanishes for every finite thrust. The $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ moment remains incompatible with the hinge.
