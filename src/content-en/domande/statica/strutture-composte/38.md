The three-hinged arch shown is formed by the straight $AC$ and $CB$ members, with the springings at the same elevation and the span $10\,\mathrm{m}$; the crown $C$ is located $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $A$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ above springings. At $7\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ the vertical downward force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts on the member $CB$. What is the thrust $H$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,4;B,10,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="7 2,0,-1.6,P" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.8;4 0,7 0,3 m,0.8;7 0,B,3 m,0.8;4 0,C,4 m,-0.6" titolo="Three-hinged arch with an off-center crown and force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $H = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $H = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $H = 14\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $H = 21\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

In the equivalent simply supported beam the force is $7\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$, then $V_A = \dfrac{20 \cdot 3}{10}\,\mathrm{kN} = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$. To the left of the crown only $V_A$ acts, so the moment in the section $x_C = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ is $M_C^0 = 6 \cdot 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m} = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The push is
$$
H = \frac{M_C^0}{f} = \frac{24}{4}\,\mathrm{kN} = 6\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The value $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by putting the crown at midspan, the value $21\,\mathrm{kN}$ by calculating the moment at $C$ with the $V_B = 14\,\mathrm{kN}$ alone and forgetting $P$.
