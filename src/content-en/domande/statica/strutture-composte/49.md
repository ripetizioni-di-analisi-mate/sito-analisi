The Gerber beam shown consists of segment $AC$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ supported by a roller at $A$ and cantilever $CD$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at $D$. Hinge $C$ connects them. Segment $AC$ carries a uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the support moment $M_D$ at the fixed end?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;D,6,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,carrello;D,incastro,0" carichi="A,C,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,C,4 m,0.8;C,D,2 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam with a fixed support at $D$ and load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $AC$"}

- $M_D = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_D = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_D = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_D = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

Segment $AC$ is suspended and can be analyzed as a beam simply supported at $A$ and $C$, so the hinge exerts an upward force $\dfrac{qL}{2} = \dfrac{3 \cdot 4}{2} = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ on it. On the cantilever $CD$, therefore, $C$ acts the force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $D$. Moment equilibrium of the cantilever around $D$ gives
$$
M_D + 6 \cdot 2 = 0 ,
$$
then $M_D = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, i.e. $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in **clockwise**. The direction is opposite to that of a fixed cantilever to the left, because the force that descends to $C$ is now to the left of the fixed support.
