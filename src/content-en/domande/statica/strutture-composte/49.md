A suspended span of a Gerber beam has a hinge at $C$ and a roller at $D$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. A downward $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ load acts at midspan. Which external couple on this span makes the vertical force transmitted to the cantilever at $C$ zero, with $D$ to the right of $C$?

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;P,2,0,-;D,4,0" aste="C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="D,carrello" forze="P,0,-1.3,8 kN" quote="C,P,2 m;P,D,2 m" titolo="Suspended span CD and midspan load"}

- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise
- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise
- $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise
- No couple can change the transmitted force.

## Explanation

With zero reaction at $C$, support $D$ carries the entire $8\,\mathrm{kN}$. About $C$, the forces give $8\cdot4-8\cdot2=16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise. A clockwise couple of equal magnitude is required.
