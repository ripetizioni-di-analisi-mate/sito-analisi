In the three-hinged frame shown, hinge $A$ is at the base of column $AB$, whose height is $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Hinge $E$ is at the base of column $DE$, whose height is $2\,\mathrm{m}$. This second hinge is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$. Beam $BD$ has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, an internal hinge $C$ at midspan, and a uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the reaction $V_E$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4,-;D,6,4;E,6,2" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" carichi="B,D,0.5,0.5,q" etichette="3,3.55,C" quote="A,B,4 m,0.6;B,C,3 m,-1.1;C,D,3 m,-1.1;D,E,2 m,0.6" titolo="Portal frame with pinned supports at different elevations and load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $V_E = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_E = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_E = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $V_E = 14\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The hinge $E$ is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ higher than $A$, so $H_E$ also appears in the rotation around $A$. Compared to $C$ the hinge $E$ is located $3\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ lower. The rotation of the whole structure around $A$ and the auxiliary equation for $CDE$ around $C$ give
$$
\begin{cases}
6\,V_E - 2\,H_E - 24 \cdot 3 = 0 \\[4pt]
3\,V_E + 2\,H_E - 12 \cdot 1.5 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
When you add up you get $9\,V_E = 90$, then $V_E = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward. The value $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by neglecting the difference in level, the value $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ by mistaking the sign of the term in $H_E$ in the auxiliary equation.
