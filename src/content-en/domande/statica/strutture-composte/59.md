The book ladder shown is formed by the members $AC$ and $CB$, connected by the hinge $C$; the $A$ mount is a hinge, the $B$ mount is a roller support on a horizontal surface. The two members are connected at mid-height by a horizontal tie. In the hinge $C$, the vertical force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward, applied to the member $AC$. What is the force in the tie?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,4;B,4,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" catene="1 2-3 2" forze="C,0,-1.4,P" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,4 4,4 m,0.8" titolo="Stepladder with a tie at midheight and force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, in compression

## Explanation

The roller support at $B$ only gives a vertical reaction and the load is vertical, so $H_A = 0$; moment equilibrium of the structure about $A$ gives $4\,V_B - 20 \cdot 2 = 0$, i.e. $V_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$. It isolates the member $CB$, on which $V_B$ acts, the tie at the $2\,\mathrm{m}$ elevation point and the force of the hinge $C$. With $N$ positive in tension the tie pulls the member to the left, and moment equilibrium about $C$ gives
$$
2 \cdot 10 - 2\,N = 0 .
$$
Therefore $N = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the tie is **in tension**. With the tie at the base the moment arm would be $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and the force would equal $5\,\mathrm{kN}$.
