A straight-axle Gerber beam is formed by four sections connected by three internal hinges. How many independent external restraints does the beam need for static determinacy?

- $6$
- $5$
- $8$
- $12$

## Explanation

The four segments have $3n = 12$ degrees of freedom in all, so a statically determinate structure has $s = 12$ scalar constraints. Each internal hinge connects two segments and is a double constraint, so the three hinges give $2 \cdot 3 = 6$ scalar constraints. External supports must therefore give $12 - 6 = 6$ scalar constraints, i.e. $n + 2$, one more than that of a simple beam for each hinge. Counting is necessary; The arrangement of constraints must then be checked separately.
