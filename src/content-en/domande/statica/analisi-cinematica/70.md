The horizontal $AB$ beam shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, is hinged to the wall at $A = (0, 0)$ and is supported at $B = (4, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ by an $BD$ tie rod, hinged to the wall at $D = (0, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$. The tie rod is a unloaded rod with two hinges, i.e. a two-force link. How is the beam classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;D,0,3" aste="A-B" catene="B-D" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;D,cerniera,180" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;A,D,3 m,0.7"}

- Unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$
- Statically determinate, with $l = i = 0$
- Statically indeterminate, with $l = 0$ and $i = 1$
- Unstable, with $l = 1$ and $i = 0$

## Explanation

The beam is a body with the pin at $A$ ($2$ scalar constraints) and the two-force link $BD$ ($1$), then $s = 3 = 3n$.

The two-force link requires the center of rotation to stand on its axis, the line through $B$ and $D$; The hinge requires the center to be $A$. The line $BD$ has equation $y = 3 - \dfrac{3}{4}\,x$ and in $x = 0$ it equals $3 \neq 0$, so it does not pass by $A$.

The body should therefore have two distinct centers, and is stationary: $l = 0$ and $i = 0$. The beam would be unstable only with the tie rod aligned with the beam, i.e. with $D$ on the line $AB$.
