The beam $AB$ in the figure is fixed at $A$ and resting at $B$ on a roller support that moves over a horizontal plane, so it is statically indeterminate with $i = 1$. Remove only one of the four scalar restraints. Removing which restraint makes the beam unstable?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" quote="A,B,5 m"}

- The roller at $B$
- The fixed-support restraint that prevents vertical displacement of $A$
- The fixed-support restraint that prevents horizontal displacement of $A$
- The fixed-support restraint that prevents rotation

## Explanation

In any case, three scalar constraints remain, so the beam becomes unstable only if the three remaining constraints are ill-arranged.

Without the roller support, fixed support remains, which prevents any movement. Without the constraint to rotation they remain a hinge at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, well arranged. Without the vertical constraint, a parallel-link support remains with the horizontal links at $A$ and the roller support with the vertical reaction, not parallel to the pin-ended links; This provision is also statically determinate.

Without the horizontal constraint, however, the three remaining constraints (vertical displacement of $A$, rotation, vertical displacement of $B$) leave the horizontal translation free. Therefore the beam becomes unstable, with $l = i = 1$.
