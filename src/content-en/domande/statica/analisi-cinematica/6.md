- Statically determinate, with $l = i = 0$
- Unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$
- Unstable, with $l = 1$ and $i = 0$
- Statically indeterminate, with $l = 0$ and $i = 1$

## Explanation

The portal frame is a three-hinged arch, with $n = 2$ and $s = 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 = 3n$. For the three-hinges arch theorem it is statically determinate if and only if $A$, $B$ and $C$ are not collinear. With $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (6, 1)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $C = (2, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$ you have
$$
(x_C - x_A)(y_B - y_A) - (y_C - y_A)(x_B - x_A) = 2 \cdot 1 - 4 \cdot 6 = -22\,\mathrm{m^2} \neq 0 ,
$$
then the three points are not aligned and the portal frame is **statically determinate**. The difference in elevation between the feet does not change the conclusion, because the theorem only concerns the position of the three hinges.
