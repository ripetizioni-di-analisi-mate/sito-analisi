The beam shown is formed by section $AB$, framed at $A = (0, 0)$, and section $BC$, with $B = (3, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $C = (7, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$. The two segments are connected at $B$ by an internal two-force link with the horizontal axis, and at $C$ there is a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. Where is the center of rotation of segment $BC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,7,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;C,carrello" interni="B,pendolo,0" quote="A,B,3 m;B,C,4 m"}

- At $B = (3, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$
- At $C = (7, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$
- At infinity in the vertical direction
- Segment $BC$ is stationary

## Explanation

The section $AB$ is fixed and therefore stationary, so the internal two-force link acts on $BC$ as a two-force external link with the horizontal axis for $B$, i.e. the beam axis.

The center of $BC$ must be on this axis, the line $y = 0$, and on the line of action of the roller support reaction, the vertical $x = 7$. The two lines meet at $C$.

Then $BC$ rotates about $C$ and the point $B$ moves vertically, perpendicular to the axis of the two-force link. The beam has $n = 2$, $s = 3 + 1 + 1 = 5$, $l = 1$ and $i = 0$.
