A flat structure is formed by $n = 3$ rigid bodies, connected to each other and to the ground by constraints for a total of $s = 8$ scalar constraints. Which statement must be true?

- The structure is statically determinate if the restraints are properly arranged
- The structure has exactly $l = 1$
- The structure is unstable, with $l \ge 1$
- The structure is statically indeterminate, with $i \ge 1$

## Explanation

The degrees of freedom are $3n = 9$, hence $3n - s = 1$. From the $3n - s = l - i$ relationship follows $l = 1 + i$, and since $i \ge 0$ you have $l \ge 1$. Therefore the structure is **unstable** whatever the arrangement of constraints, and cannot be statically determinate nor statically indeterminate. The exact value of $l$ depends on the arrangement of constraints, and with poorly arranged constraints it can be $i = 1$ and $l = 2$.
