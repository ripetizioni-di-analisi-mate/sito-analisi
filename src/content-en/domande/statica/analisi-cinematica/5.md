The Gerber beam shown is fixed at $A$, has internal hinges at $C$ and $D$, and rests on horizontal rollers at $B$ and $E$. Which statement is correct?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,5,0;D,7,0;E,10,0" aste="A-C;C-B;B-D;D-E" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello;E,carrello" cerniere="C;D" quote="A,C,3 m;C,B,2 m;B,D,2 m;D,E,3 m"}

- It is unstable because segment $DE$ has only one roller
- It is statically determinate, and the segments are found to be stationary in the order $AC$, $CD$, $DE$
- It is statically determinate, and the segments are found to be stationary in the order $DE$, $CD$, $AC$
- It is statically indeterminate, with $i = 1$

## Explanation

You have $n = 3$ and $s = 3 + 2 + 1 + 2 + 1 = 9 = 3n$. The section $AC$ is fixed, so it is stationary, and the hinge $C$ is a pin support for the section $CD$. The center of $CD$ should then be $C$ and stand on the vertical through $B$, which does not pass through $C$, so $CD$ is also stationary. For the same reason the section $DE$ has the $D$ centers and the vertical through $E$, distinct, and is stationary. So $l = i = 0$ and the beam is **statically determinate**. The order starts from fixed support, because the segment $DE$ is bound to the ground by roller support alone at $E$ and becomes stationary only after the segment $CD$.
