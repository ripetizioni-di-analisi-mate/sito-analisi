In the analysis of a portal frame formed by two bodies, connected to each other and to the ground with six scalar constraints throughout, the center method provides a position of the centers of rotation compatible with all constraints and theorems of the kinematic chains. What can be concluded about the portal frame?

- The structure is unstable, with $l \ge 1$ and $i = 0$
- The structure is unstable and statically indeterminate, with $i = l \ge 1$
- The structure is statically determinate because $s = 3n$
- The structure is statically indeterminate, with $l = 0$ and $i \ge 1$

## Explanation

A constraint-compatible center layout corresponds to a non-zero shift, so $l \ge 1$ and the portal frame is unstable.

The bodies are $n = 2$ and the scalar constraints $s = 6 = 3n$, so the $3n - s = l - i$ relationship gives $i = l$. So also $i \ge 1$, and the portal frame is unstable and statically indeterminate: the constraints are poorly arranged, as in the portal frame with the three hinges collinear.
