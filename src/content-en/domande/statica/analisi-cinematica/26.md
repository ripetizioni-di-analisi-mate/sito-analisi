The horizontal $AB$ beam shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin support at $A$ and at $B$ a roller support whose support surface is tilted by $30^\circ$ on the horizontal and descends to the right. The unit vector along the roller reaction is therefore $\hat n = \left(\dfrac{1}{2}, \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$. With reference point $A$, parameters $u_A$, $v_A$, and $\varphi$, and lengths in meters, which row of the kinematic matrix corresponds to the roller?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,240" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9"}

- $\left(\dfrac{1}{2},\ \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\ 2\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\ \dfrac{1}{2},\ 2\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{2},\ \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\ 2\sqrt{3}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{2},\ \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\ -2\sqrt{3}\right)$

## Explanation

The point $B = (4, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ moves by $u_B = u_A - \varphi\,(y_B - y_A) = u_A$ and $v_B = v_A + \varphi\,(x_B - x_A) = v_A + 4\varphi$. The roller support imposes $n_x u_B + n_y v_B = 0$, i.e.
$$
\frac{1}{2}\,u_A + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,(v_A + 4\varphi) = 0 .
$$
Therefore the coefficient of $\varphi$ is $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \cdot 4 = 2\sqrt{3}$, and the row is $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}, \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, 2\sqrt{3}\right)$. The coefficient $2$ is obtained by multiplying the distance $AB$ by $n_x$ instead of $n_y$.
