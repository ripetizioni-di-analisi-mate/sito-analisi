The triangular frame in the figure, with the $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (6, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $C = (3, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$ vertices, has the three rigid joints and therefore forms a closed loop. It is supported at $A$ and $B$ on two roller supports that travel on horizontal surfaces. What are $l$ and $i$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-A" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9"}

- $l = 1$, $i = 0$
- $l = 0$, $i = 2$
- $l = 1$, $i = 1$
- $l = 1$, $i = 3$

## Explanation

It opens the mesh with a cut: it remains an open body, $n = 1$, and the cut corresponds to a rigid connection, that is to say to scalar constraints $3$. With the two roller supports you then have $s = 1 + 1 + 3 = 5$.

The reactions of the two roller supports are vertical and parallel, so the frame can move horizontally; the three constraints of the rigid connection do not prevent any displacement, because the two cut faces belong to the same body. Therefore $l = 1$.

From the $3n - s = l - i$ relationship you get $i = l - 3n + s = 1 - 3 + 5 = 3$. The three static indeterminacy are internal to the closed loop, while the external supports are insufficient.
