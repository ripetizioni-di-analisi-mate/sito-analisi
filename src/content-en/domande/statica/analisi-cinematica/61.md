The triangular plate in the figure has the vertices $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $P = (3, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$. At $A$ there is a pin support and at $B$ a roller support that moves along a vertical plane, so the plate is unstable and rotates about $A$. For an infinitesimal rotation $\varphi$, positive counterclockwise, what are the displacement components $(u_P, v_P)$ of $P$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;P,3,4" aste="A-B;B-P;P-A" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9"}

- $(4\varphi,\ -3\varphi)$
- $(3\varphi,\ 4\varphi)$
- $(-3\varphi,\ 4\varphi)$
- $(-4\varphi,\ 3\varphi)$

## Explanation

The reaction of the roller support is horizontal and its line of action, the axis $x$, passes through $A$, so the center of rotation is $A$.

For a rotation $\varphi$ around $A$ you have $u_P = -\varphi\,(y_P - y_A)$ and $v_P = \varphi\,(x_P - x_A)$. Therefore
$$
u_P = -4\varphi , \qquad v_P = 3\varphi .
$$
The displacement is perpendicular to $AP$, as is the case with the scalar product $(-4)(3) + (3)(4) = 0$.
