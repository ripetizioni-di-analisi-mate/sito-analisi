The frame in the figure consists of the column $AB$, with $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (0, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$, and the crossbeam $BC$, with $C = (4, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$, joined at $B$ by a rigid joint. At $A$ there is a pin support and at $C$ a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. With reference point $A$, parameters $u_A$, $v_A$, and $\varphi$, lengths in meters, and rows ordered as $u_A = 0$, $v_A = 0$, and the roller condition, what is the determinant of the kinematic matrix?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.7;B,C,4 m,-0.7"}

- $3$
- $5$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

The roller support prevents vertical displacement of $C$, which equals $v_C = v_A + \varphi\,(x_C - x_A) = v_A + 4\varphi$. So the kinematic matrix is
$$
\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 4 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
which is lower triangular and has determinant $1 \cdot 1 \cdot 4 = 4$. The determinant is different from zero and $s = 3n$, so the frame is statically determinate. The value $3$ is obtained using the $y_C$ dimension instead of the horizontal coordinate $x_C$, which is the arm of the vertical reaction about $A$.
