The frame in the figure consists of the crossbeam $AB$, with $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (4, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$, and the column $BC$, with $C = (4, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$, joined at $B$ by a rigid joint. At $A$ there is a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane, at $C$ a roller support that moves along a vertical plane and at $B$ a two-force link whose axis forms with the horizontal the angle $\beta$; in the figure the axis is vertical. For what orientation of the link axis is the frame unstable?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,4,3" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,carrello;B,pendolo;C,carrello,0" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.7;B,C,3 m,0.9"}

- $\beta \approx 36.87^\circ$, with the axis rising to the left
- $\beta \approx 53.13^\circ$, with the axis rising to the left
- $\beta \approx 36.87^\circ$, with the axis rising to the right
- $\beta = 45^\circ$, with the axis rising to the left

## Explanation

The frame is a body with three scalar constraints, so it is unstable when the three lines of action pass through the same point. The roller support reaction at $A$ is vertical and has the line $x = 0$, while the roller support reaction at $C$ is horizontal and has the line $y = 3\,\mathrm{m}$. These two lines meet at point $(0, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore the frame is unstable when the axis of the two-force link passes through $B = (4, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $(0, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$, i.e. when it goes up to the left with $\tan\beta = \dfrac{3}{4}$. You get $\beta \approx 36.87^\circ$, and the frame can rotates about the point $(0, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$.
