The beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, has at $A$ a parallel-link support with the horizontal links and at $B$ a pin support. What are the degree of kinematic instability $l$ and the degree of static indeterminacy $i$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,180;B,cerniera" quote="A,B,6 m"}

- $l = 1$, $i = 0$
- $l = 0$, $i = 2$
- $l = 1$, $i = 2$
- $l = 0$, $i = 1$

## Explanation

The beam is a body with $s = 2 + 2 = 4$, hence $3n - s = -1$. The hinge $B$ requires that an eventual center of rotation be $B$. Parallel-link support with horizontal links requires the center to be the point at infinity in the horizontal direction, i.e. that the beam moves vertically. The two centers are distinct, so the beam is firm and $l = 0$. The $3n - s = l - i$ report follows $i = 1$.
