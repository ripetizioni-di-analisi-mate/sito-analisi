The beam shown is formed by the $AC$ and $CD$ sections, connected by the internal hinge $C = (4, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$, with $A = (0, 0)$ and $D = (10, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$. For the section $AC$, the $A$ reference point and $u_1$, $v_1$, $\varphi_1$ parameters are used; for the section $CD$ the $D$ reference point and the $u_2$, $v_2$, $\varphi_2$ parameters. What equation does the internal hinge impose on the vertical displacement component of $C$, with lengths in meters?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" quote="A,C,4 m;C,D,6 m"}

- $v_1 + 4\,\varphi_1 = v_2 + 6\,\varphi_2$
- $v_1 + 4\,\varphi_1 = v_2 - 4\,\varphi_2$
- $v_1 + 4\,\varphi_1 = v_2 - 6\,\varphi_2$
- $v_1 - 4\,\varphi_1 = v_2 - 6\,\varphi_2$

## Explanation

The internal hinge dictates that $C$ has the same shift as the point of the two segments. The vertical displacement of a point $P$ of the body $j$ is $v_P = v_j + \varphi_j\,(x_P - x_{O_j})$.

As point of $AC$, with the reference point at $A$, you have $v_C = v_1 + \varphi_1\,(4 - 0) = v_1 + 4\,\varphi_1$. As point of $CD$, with the reference point at $D$, you have $v_C = v_2 + \varphi_2\,(4 - 10) = v_2 - 6\,\varphi_2$.

Equating the two expressions results in $v_1 + 4\,\varphi_1 = v_2 - 6\,\varphi_2$. The $x_C - x_D$ sign is negative because $C$ stands to the left of the $D$ reference point.
