The horizontal $AB$ beam shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, has two pin supports at $A$ and $B$, so it is statically indeterminate with $i = 1$. Which system of reactions is in equilibrium in the absence of applied loads?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" quote="A,B,6 m"}

- $V_A = Y$, $V_B = -Y$, and $H_A = H_B = 0$, with arbitrary $Y$
- $H_A = X$, $H_B = -X$, and $V_A = V_B = 0$, with arbitrary $X$
- $H_A = H_B = X$ and $V_A = V_B = 0$, with arbitrary $X$
- $V_A = V_B = Y$ and $H_A = H_B = 0$, with arbitrary $Y$

## Explanation

The loadless equilibrium equations are $H_A + H_B = 0$, $V_A + V_B = 0$ and, for rotation around $A$, $6\,V_B = 0$.

The third is $V_B = 0$ and then $V_A = 0$. From the first one you get $H_B = -H_A$, with any $H_A = X$. Self-balanced reactions are therefore two equal and opposite forces directed along $AB$, and the free parameter $X$ corresponds to the only static indeterminacy.

Two equal and opposite vertical forces at $A$ and $B$ instead form a moment $6\,Y$ couple, which is not zero.
