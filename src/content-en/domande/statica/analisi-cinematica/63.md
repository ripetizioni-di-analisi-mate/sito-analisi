The structure in the figure is formed by the four rods $AB$, $BC$, $CD$ and $DA$, with $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$, $C = (4, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $D = (0, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$, connected to each other by hinges in the vertices. The $A$ vertex is a pin support to which the $AB$ and $DA$ rods are connected; the $B$ vertex rests on a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. What are $l$ and $i$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,4,3;D,0,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-A" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" cerniere="B;C;D" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,3 m"}

- $l = 1$, $i = 0$
- $l = 0$, $i = 0$
- $l = 0$, $i = 1$
- $l = 1$, $i = 1$

## Explanation

The rods are $n = 4$ bodies, hence $3n = 12$. The pin support at $A$ connects two bodies to the ground and equals $4$, the internal hinges at $B$, $C$ and $D$ equal $2$ each and the roller support equals $1$. Therefore $s = 4 + 6 + 1 = 11$ and $3n - s = 1$, then $l \ge 1$.

Member $AB$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ with the vertical reaction, which does not pass through $A$, so it is stationary. The $DA$ and $BC$ rods then rotates about $A$ and $B$. Because they are parallel, the $AD$ and $BC$ lines meet at the point at infinity in the vertical direction, which is the center of $CD$; Therefore $CD$ moves horizontally and the quadrilateral deforms into a parallelogram.

The displacements depend on the parameter of rotation of $DA$, then $l = 1$ and, from the relation $3n - s = l - i$, $i = 0$. A mesh of four hinged rods is deformable, while a triangle of three hinged rods is not.
