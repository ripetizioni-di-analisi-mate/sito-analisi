In the structure in the figure the pillar $AD$, with $A = (0, 0)$ and $D = (0, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin support at $A$ and is connected at $D$ by an internal hinge to the body $DEB$, formed by the crossbeam $DE$ and the pillar $EB$ joined at $E = (6, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$ by a rigid node. At the base $B = (6, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ there is a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. The structure is unstable. What displacement does body $DEB$ undergo?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;E,6,4;B,6,0" aste="A-D;D-E;E-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" cerniere="D" quote="D,E,6 m,-0.7;B,E,4 m"}

- A rotation about point $D$
- A rotation about point $B$
- A horizontal translation
- A rotation about $A$ together with column $AD$

## Explanation

Pillar $AD$ rotates about $A$, so the centers are $C_1 = A$ for the pillar and $C_{12} = D$ for the internal hinge. For the first theorem of kinematic chains the center $C_2$ of the body $DEB$ is aligned with $A$ and $D$, so it lies on the line $x = 0$.

The roller support at $B$ has the vertical reaction, so $C_2$ also lies on the line $x = 6\,\mathrm{m}$. The two lines are parallel, therefore $C_2$ is their point at infinity, in the vertical direction, and the body $DEB$ moves perpendicular to it, that is, horizontally.

The shift is compatible with constraints: the point $D$ of the pillar, rotating around $A$, moves horizontally, and the point $B$ moves over the plane of the roller support. A rotation around $D$ or $B$ would move $B$ or $D$ in an unadmitted direction, respectively, and a rotation around $A$ would move $B$ vertically.
