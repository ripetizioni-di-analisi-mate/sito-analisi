In the structure in the figure the cantilever $AC$ and $BD$ are framed at $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (8, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$, with $C = (3, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $D = (5, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$. The $C$ and $D$ free extremes are linked by a pin-ended link, i.e. an internal two-force link. What are the degree of kinematic instability $l$ and the degree of static indeterminacy $i$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;D,5,0;B,8,0" aste="A-C;D-B" catene="C-D" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,incastro,0" quote="A,C,3 m;C,D,2 m;D,B,3 m"}

- $l = 0$, $i = 0$
- $l = 0$, $i = 2$
- $l = 0$, $i = 1$
- $l = 1$, $i = 0$

## Explanation

The cantilever are $n = 2$ bodies, hence $3n = 6$. The two fixed supports equal $3$ each and the pin-ended link is an internal two-force link of multiplicity $1$, therefore $s = 3 + 3 + 1 = 7$.

Each cantilever is fixed and therefore still, so $l = 0$. From the $3n - s = l - i$ relationship you get $i = s - 3n = 1$.

The loadless equilibrium reaction system is formed by the stress in the pin-ended link, which acts with opposite directions on the two cantilever, and the reactions of the two fixed supports that balance it. The value $i = 2$ is obtained by counting the pin-ended link as an internal hinge.
