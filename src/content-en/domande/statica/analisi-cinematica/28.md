A flat structure is formed by $n = 3$ rigid bodies with simple $s = 10$ constraints, and its kinematic matrix has $r = 8$ rank. What are the degree of kinematic instability $l$ and the degree of static indeterminacy $i$?

- $l = 0$, $i = 1$
- $l = 2$, $i = 1$
- $l = 1$, $i = 0$
- $l = 1$, $i = 2$

## Explanation

The kinematic matrix has $3n = 9$ columns and $s = 10$ rows. The degree of kinematic instability is the space size of $A\,\mathbf{q} = \mathbf{0}$ solutions, hence $l = 3n - r = 9 - 8 = 1$. The degree of static indeterminacy is the space size of $A^{T}\mathbf{R} = \mathbf{0}$ solutions, hence $i = s - r = 10 - 8 = 2$. The $3n - s = l - i$ relationship is verified, because $9 - 10 = 1 - 2$. The couple $l = 0$, $i = 1$ is obtained by counting only, assuming the constraints well arranged, while the rank $r = 8 < 9$ indicates that they are ill arranged.
