The structure in the figure is formed by the $AC$ and $BC$ rods, hinged to the ground at $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (4, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$, and the horizontal $CD$ rod, with $C = (2, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $D = (6, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$. The three rods are connected at $C$ by a single hinge and at $D$ there is a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. How is the structure classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,2,3;D,6,3" aste="A-C;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera;D,carrello" cerniere="C" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;C,D,4 m,-0.7"}

- Unstable, with $l = 1$ and $i = 0$
- Statically determinate, with $l = i = 0$
- Statically indeterminate, with $l = 0$ and $i = 1$
- Unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$

## Explanation

The bodies are $n = 3$. The pin supports equal $2 + 2$, the hinge $C$ connects three bodies and equals $2\,(3 - 1) = 4$, the roller support equals $1$; then $s = 9 = 3n$.

The $AC$ and $BC$ rods form a three-hinged arch with the $A$, $B$ and $C$ not collinear, so they are stationary. The point $C$ is then stationary and, for member $CD$, the pin at $C$ behaves like a pin support. The center of $CD$ should be $C$ and stand on the vertical through $D$, which does not pass by $C$; So $CD$ is also stationary.

This follows $l = 0$ and, from the $3n - s = l - i$ report, $i = 0$.
