Beam $AB$ has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, a parallel-link support with vertical links at $A$, and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Which displacement is compatible with these restraints?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo;B,carrello" quote="A,B,5 m"}

- Rotation about $A$
- Vertical translation
- None, because the beam is statically determinate
- Horizontal translation

## Explanation

Parallel-link support with vertical links prevents vertical rotation and translation and leaves horizontal translation free. So the beam can only move horizontally, with the center at the point at infinity in the vertical direction. $B$ roller support only prevents $B$ from moving vertically, so it is compatible with the **horizontal translation**. The constraints are poorly arranged, because the roller support reaction is parallel to the pin-ended links, and the beam has $s = 3 = 3n$ with $l = i = 1$.
