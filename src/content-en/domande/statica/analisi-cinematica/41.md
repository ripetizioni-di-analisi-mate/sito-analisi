The beam shown is formed by the cantilever $AC$, fixed at $A$, and the segment $CD$, connected to the cantilever by the internal hinge $C$. At $D$ there is a roller support that moves along a vertical plane. How is the beam classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;D,7,0" aste="A-C;C-D" vincoli="A,incastro,180;D,carrello,0" cerniere="C" quote="A,C,3 m;C,D,4 m"}

- Statically determinate, with $l = i = 0$
- Unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$
- Unstable, with $l = 1$ and $i = 0$
- Statically indeterminate, with $l = 0$ and $i = 1$

## Explanation

You have $n = 2$ and $s = 3 + 2 + 1 = 6 = 3n$. The cantilever $AC$ is fixed, so it is stationary, and the hinge $C$ is for the segment $CD$ a pin support. The roller support at $D$ on a vertical plane has the horizontal reaction, whose line of action is the axis of the beam and passes through $C$. Therefore the segment $CD$ can rotates about $C$, while $D$ moves vertically along the support surface, and $l = 1$. From the $3n - s = l - i$ relationship follows $i = 1$, so the beam is **unstable and statically indeterminate**.
