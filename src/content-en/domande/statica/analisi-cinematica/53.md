The beam $AB$ shown, of length $10\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin support at $A$, a roller support in the midpoint $D$ and a roller support at $B$, both on horizontal planes. What are the degree of kinematic instability $l$ and the degree of static indeterminacy $i$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,5,0;B,10,0" aste="A-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello;B,carrello" quote="A,D,5 m;D,B,5 m"}

- $l = 1$, $i = 0$
- $l = 1$, $i = 1$
- $l = 0$, $i = 0$
- $l = 0$, $i = 1$

## Explanation

The beam is a single body, so $3n = 3$, and the number of scalar constraints is $s = 2 + 1 + 1 = 4$.

The pin at $A$ and the roller at $B$ alone already hold the beam fixed, because the centre of rotation would have to be $A$ and also lie on the vertical through $B$, which does not pass through $A$. So $l = 0$.

From the relation $3n - s = l - i$ we get $i = s - 3n = 1$, so the continuous beam on three supports is **statically indeterminate** to degree one.
