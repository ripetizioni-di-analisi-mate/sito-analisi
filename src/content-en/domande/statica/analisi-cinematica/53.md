A horizontal rigid beam has vertical supports at $A$, $D$ and $B$, with $D$ halfway between the other two. Given prescribed support movements $v_A=2\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $v_B=8\,\mathrm{mm}$, which movement at $D$ is compatible with small rigid-body motion?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;D,carrello;B,carrello" quote="A,D,L;D,B,L" titolo="Prescribed vertical end displacements"}

- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

For a horizontal rigid beam the vertical displacement is affine in the horizontal coordinate. At the midpoint it must therefore be $\dfrac{2+8}{2}=5\,\mathrm{mm}$. A different value would require deformation.
