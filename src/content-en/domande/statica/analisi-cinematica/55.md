The kinematic matrix of a flat structure, written with three displacement parameters for each body, has $7$ rows and $6$ columns. How many rigid bodies $n$ and scalar restraints $s$ does the structure have?

- $n = 2$, $s = 7$
- $n = 6$, $s = 7$
- $n = 2$, $s = 6$
- $n = 7$, $s = 2$

## Explanation

Each scalar constraint gives an equation, i.e. a line of the kinematic matrix, so the lines are $s = 7$.

Each body has three parameters $u_j$, $v_j$, $\varphi_j$, i.e. three columns, so the columns are $3n = 6$ and $n = 2$. Because $s > 3n$, the structure has $i \ge 1$.
