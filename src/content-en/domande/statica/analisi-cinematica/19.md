Two beam sections are connected by an internal two-force link with the vertical axis, hinged to point $P_1$ of the first section and point $P_2$ of the second. Which relative displacement does the connection prevent?

- The relative rotation of the two segments
- The vertical component of the displacement of $P_2$ relative to $P_1$
- The horizontal component of the displacement of $P_2$ relative to $P_1$
- Every displacement of $P_2$ relative to $P_1$

## Explanation

The internal two-force link is a pin-ended unloaded link that keeps the distance between $P_1$ and $P_2$ constant. For infinitesimal displacements this condition only prevents the relative displacement component along the pin-ended link axis. The axis is vertical, so the constraint prevents **vertical component** from moving $P_2$ over $P_1$ and has $1$ multiplicity. Relative rotation and horizontal relative sliding remain free. Internal parallel-link connection with vertical links would also prevent relative rotation.
