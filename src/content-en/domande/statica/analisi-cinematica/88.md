The portal frame in the figure is formed by the bodies $ADC$ and $CEB$, connected in the middle of the crossbeam, in $C = (3, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$, by an internal parallel-link connection with the horizontal links. The $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (6, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ feet are pin supports. How is the portal frame classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;C,3,4;E,6,4;B,6,0" aste="A-D;D-C;C-E;E-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" interni="C,doppio-pendolo,0" quote="D,C,3 m,-0.7;C,E,3 m,-0.7;B,E,4 m"}

- Statically determinate, with $l = i = 0$
- Unstable, with $l = 1$ and $i = 0$
- Unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$
- Statically indeterminate, with $l = 0$ and $i = 1$

## Explanation

You have $n = 2$ and $s = 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 = 3n$, so $l = i$.

The absolute centers are $C_1 = A$ and $C_2 = B$. The internal parallel-link connection with the horizontal links requires the relative center $C_{12}$ to be the point at infinity in the horizontal direction. The line $AB$ is horizontal and contains that point at infinity, so the three centers are aligned and the first theorem of kinematic chains is satisfied.

In fact, with $\varphi_1 = \varphi_2 = \varphi$, the two bodies rotate about $A$ and $B$ and the point $C$ has the same horizontal displacement $-4\varphi$ as the point of the two bodies, while the relative vertical scroll is left free by pin-ended links. Therefore $l = 1$ and $i = 1$.
