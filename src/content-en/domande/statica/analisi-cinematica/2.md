- Statically determinate, with $l = i = 0$
- Unstable, with $l = 1$ and $i = 0$
- Unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$
- Statically indeterminate, with $l = 0$ and $i = 1$

## Explanation

The beam is a body with $s = 2 + 1 = 3 = 3n$, so the count is satisfied and the arrangement of constraints remains to be controlled. The roller support reaction is perpendicular to the support surface, so it forms $30^\circ$ with the vertical and its line of action passes through $B$. In a possible shift the center of rotation should coincide with $A$ and be on this line, which does not pass by $A$. So the beam is stationary and $l = 0$. With the reference point at $A$ the determinant of the kinematic matrix equals $6\cos 30^\circ = 3\sqrt{3} \neq 0$, and the beam is **statically determinate**.
