## Explanation

Cutting the mesh in the hinge $E$ remains an open body with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, the vertical reaction of which does not pass through $A$; So the body is stationary and $l = 0$. The hinge $E$ that closes the closed loop equals $2$ scalar constraints. These constraints join two cut faces of the same rigid body and do not prevent extra shifts, so each increases the degree of static indeterminacy and $i = 2$ by one. The value $i = 3$ corresponds to the closed loop with all rigid joints, in which there is a rigid connection instead of the hinge.
