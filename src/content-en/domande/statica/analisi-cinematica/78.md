The Gerber beam in the figure is formed by the $AC$, $CE$ and $EF$ sections, connected by the internal hinges $C$ and $E$. The segment $CE$ has a pin support at $B$ and a roller support at $D$; at $A$ and $F$ there are roller supports; all roller supports travel on horizontal surfaces. Which segment is the supporting beam, meaning the segment that can be shown to be stationary first?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0;D,8,0;E,10,0;F,13,0" aste="A-C;C-B;B-D;D-E;E-F" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera;D,carrello;F,carrello" cerniere="C;E" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,3 m;B,D,3 m;D,E,2 m;E,F,3 m"}

- Segment $AC$
- The middle segment $CE$
- Segment $EF$
- No segment, because the beam is unstable

## Explanation

You have $n = 3$ and $s = 1 + 2 + 2 + 1 + 2 + 1 = 9 = 3n$.

The section $CE$ is bound to the ground by a hinge at $B$ and a roller support at $D$ with the vertical reaction, which does not pass through $B$; so it is stationary on its own and is the supporting beam.

The segment $AC$ then has a pin support at $C$ and a roller support at $A$, well arranged, and is stationary; The same passes through $EF$, with the pin at $E$ and the roller support at $F$. The $AC$ and $EF$ sections are girders carried, and the girder is statically determinate.
