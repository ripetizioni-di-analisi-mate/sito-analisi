The beam shown is formed by the $AB$ and $BC$ sections, connected at $B$ by an internal hinge. The internal hinge is resting on a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane; at $A$ there is a pin support and at $C$ a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. What are $s$ and $l$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,9,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;C,carrello" cerniere="B" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,5 m"}

- $s = 7$, $l = 0$
- $s = 5$, $l = 1$
- $s = 6$, $l = 1$
- $s = 6$, $l = 0$

## Explanation

The segments are $n = 2$ bodies, hence $3n = 6$. The pin support equals $2$, the internal hinge $2$ and each roller support $1$. The roller support at $B$ prevents vertical displacement of the point $B$, which is common to the two segments, so it is only counted once, and $s = 2 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 6$.

The segment $AB$ has the pin at $A$ and, at point $B$, the vertical reaction of the roller support, which does not pass through $A$, so it is stationary. The point $B$ is then stationary and the internal hinge is, for the section $BC$, a pin support. The vertical reaction of the roller support at $C$ does not pass through $B$, so $BC$ is also stationary.

So $l = 0$ and, from the relation $3n - s = l - i$, $i = 0$, so the beam is **statically determinate**. The value $s = 7$ is obtained by counting the roller support at $B$ once for each segment.
