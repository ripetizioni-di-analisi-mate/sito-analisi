The beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, has at $A$ and $B$ two parallel-link supports, both with vertical links. What are $l$ and $i$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo;B,doppio-pendolo" quote="A,B,6 m"}

- $l = 0$, $i = 1$
- $l = 1$, $i = 0$
- $l = 1$, $i = 2$
- $l = 0$, $i = 2$

## Explanation

The beam is a body with two parallel-link supports, hence $3n = 3$ and $s = 4$.

Each parallel-link support prevents rotation and vertical displacement and leaves the horizontal translation free. Therefore the beam can move horizontally and $l = 1$.

From the $3n - s = l - i$ relationship you get $i = l - 3n + s = 1 - 3 + 4 = 2$. The two $\varphi = 0$ conditions coincide, and the two conditions on the vertical displacement with $\varphi = 0$ also coincide: two lines of the kinematic matrix are repeated.
