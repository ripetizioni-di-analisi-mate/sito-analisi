A beam has at the end $A$ a parallel-link support with the vertical links. What reactions does the support exert on the beam?

- A horizontal force and a moment
- A vertical force and a horizontal force
- A vertical force and a moment
- A single vertical force

## Explanation

A smooth constraint exerts a reaction component for each displacement component it prevents. Parallel-link support with vertical links prevents rotation and translation parallel to pin-ended links, i.e. vertical, and leaves horizontal translation free. Therefore it exerts a moment and a vertical force, which together equals a vertical force with any line of action. Horizontal displacement is free, so the constraint does not exert a horizontal force.
