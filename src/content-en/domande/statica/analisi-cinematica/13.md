- Statically determinate, with $l = i = 0$
- Unstable, with $l = 1$ and $i = 0$
- Unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$
- Statically indeterminate, with $l = 0$ and $i = 1$

## Explanation

The structure has $n = 2$ and $s = 2 + 1 + 2 + 1 = 6 = 3n$, counting the pin at $A$, the roller support at $B$, the internal hinge $C$ and the tie, which is an internal two-force link. If the two rods had a relative displacement, the relative center $C_{12}$ should be the hinge $C$ and stand on the axis of the tie, i.e. on the line $AB$. The line $AB$ does not pass through $C$, so the two rods have no relative displacement and form a single rigid body. This body has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ with the vertical reaction, which does not pass through $A$, so it is stationary. So $l = i = 0$ and the structure is **statically determinate**.
