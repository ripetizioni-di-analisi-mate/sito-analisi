The linkage shown consists of three members $AC$, $CD$, and $DE$, each of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, connected by internal hinges $C$ and $D$. End $A$ is pinned to the ground and end $E$ is free. What is the degree of kinematic instability $l$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;D,6,0;E,9,0" aste="A-C;C-D;D-E" vincoli="A,cerniera" cerniere="C;D" quote="A,C,3 m;C,D,3 m;D,E,3 m"}

- $l = 1$
- $l = 2$
- $l = 5$
- $l = 3$

## Explanation

The rods are $n = 3$ bodies, so $3n = 9$, and the scalar constraints are $s = 2 + 2 + 2 = 6$. Therefore $l - i = 3$.

The three $\varphi_1$, $\varphi_2$, $\varphi_3$ rotations can be independently assigned: $AC$ rotates about $A$, $CD$ rotates about $C$ about $AC$ and $DE$ rotates about $D$ about $CD$. The shifts therefore depend on three parameters and no system of reactions is in equilibrium without loads.

Therefore $l = 3$ and $i = 0$.
