The two-bay frame shown has columns $AD$, $BE$, and $CF$, each of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and a continuous beam $DEF$. All beam-to-column joints are rigid. The three bases $A$, $B$, and $C$ are pinned. What is the degree of static indeterminacy $i$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;E,5,4;B,5,0;F,10,4;C,10,0" aste="A-D;D-E;E-F;B-E;C-F" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera;C,cerniera" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9;B,C,5 m,0.9;C,F,4 m"}

- $i = 1$
- $i = 3$
- $i = 2$
- $i = 6$

## Explanation

The nodes are rigid and there are no internal constraints, so the columns and crossbeam form one body, $n = 1$. The three pin supports equal $2$ each, so $s = 6$.

Two pin supports at distinct points already make the body stationary, because the center should coincide with both. So $l = 0$.

From the $3n - s = l - i$ relationship you get $i = s - 3n = 6 - 3 = 3$. The value $i = 6$ is obtained by confusing $s$ with $i$.
