The beam $AB$ in the figure has at $A$ a parallel-link support with the two parallel pin-ended links, tilted by $45^\circ$ on the horizontal and rising to the right, and no other constraint. Which displacement of the beam is compatible with this restraint?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,225" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9"}

- A translation parallel to the links, directed upward to the right at angle $45^\circ$
- A translation perpendicular to the links, directed upward to the left at angle $45^\circ$
- A rotation about $A$
- A horizontal translation

## Explanation

Parallel-link support prevents rotation and translation parallel to pin-ended links, while leaving the translation perpendicular to pin-ended links free.

The links lie along unit vector $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(1, 1)$, so the permitted displacement is in the direction of $(-1, 1)$, inclined at $45^\circ$ and rising to the left. In fact, with the reference point at $A$, the constraint conditions are $\varphi = 0$ and $u_A + v_A = 0$, which admit the $(u_A, v_A, \varphi) = (-1, 1, 0)$ solution.

A horizontal translation has a component along the pin-ended links, so the constraint prevents it.
