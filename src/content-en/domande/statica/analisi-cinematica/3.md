- Statically determinate, with $l = i = 0$
- Unstable, with $l = 1$ and $i = 0$
- Unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$
- Statically indeterminate, with $l = 0$ and $i = 1$

## Explanation

Joint $B$ is rigid, so the frame acts as one body and the restraint count gives $s = 2 + 1 = 3 = 3n$. The pin at $A$ fixes the center of rotation at $A$. The link at $C$ requires the center to lie on its axis, the line $CA$. Point $A$ satisfies both conditions, so the frame can rotate about $A$. During this rotation, point $C$ moves perpendicular to $CA$ and hence to the link axis. The frame therefore has $l = 1$. Substituting in $3n - s = l - i$ gives $i = 1$, so the frame has **both a mechanism and a redundant restraint**.
