The frame enclosed in the figure is a rectangle of $5\,\mathrm{m}$ for $3\,\mathrm{m}$ with all rigid joints and is fixed to the ground in the $A$ vertex. What is the degree of static indeterminacy?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,5,3;D,0,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-A" vincoli="A,incastro" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9;B,C,3 m"}

- $i = 0$
- $i = 6$
- $i = 3$
- $i = 1$

## Explanation

It opens the mesh with a cut in any section, and remains an open body fixed at $A$, which is stationary; then $l = 0$, and the external supports, which are $3$, do not give static indeterminacy. The cut removes a rigid connection, i.e. $3$ scalar constraints that join two cut faces of the same rigid body. These constraints do not prevent extra shifts, so each increases the degree of static indeterminacy and $i = 3$ by one. The value $i = 0$ is achieved by neglecting the rigid connection that closes the mesh.
