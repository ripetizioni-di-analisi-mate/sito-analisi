A closed rectangular plane frame has rigid joints and one corner fixed to the ground. One rigid joint, away from the fixed corner, is replaced by an internal hinge. By how much does the degree of static indeterminacy change if the frame remains stable?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,2.5;C,4,2.5;D,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-A" vincoli="A,incastro,270" cerniere="C" titolo="Frame with a hinge added at corner C"}

- It decreases by two.
- It increases by one.
- It decreases by one.
- It remains unchanged.

## Explanation

A rigid joint enforces continuity of both translations and of rotation. A hinge retains the two translation conditions and releases only rotation. As the mechanism count remains zero, one redundancy is removed.
