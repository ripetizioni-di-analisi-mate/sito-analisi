The closed frame shown is a $5\,\mathrm{m}$ by $3\,\mathrm{m}$ rectangle with all joints rigid and is fixed to the ground at vertex $A$. What is its degree of static indeterminacy?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,5,3;D,0,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-A" vincoli="A,incastro" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9;B,C,3 m"}

- $i = 0$
- $i = 6$
- $i = 3$
- $i = 1$

## Explanation

The loop is opened with a cut at any section, and what remains is an open body clamped at $A$, which is fixed; so $l = 0$, and the external constraints, which are $3$, give no static indeterminacy. The cut removes an internal rigid joint, that is $3$ scalar constraints joining two faces of the same rigid body. These constraints prevent no further displacement, so each of them raises the degree of static indeterminacy by one and $i = 3$. The value $i = 0$ is obtained by neglecting the internal rigid connection that closes the loop.
