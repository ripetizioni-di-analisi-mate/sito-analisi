The frame in the figure is formed by the rectangle $ABCD$, of $8\,\mathrm{m}$ by $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and the central column $EF$, which divides it into two closed meshes; All nodes are rigid. At $A$ there is a pin support and at $B$ a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. What is the degree of static indeterminacy $i$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;E,4,0;B,8,0;C,8,4;F,4,4;D,0,4" aste="A-E;E-B;B-C;C-F;F-D;D-A;E-F" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,E,4 m,0.9;E,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,4 m"}

- $i = 3$
- $i = 6$
- $i = 9$
- $i = 1$

## Explanation

Each mesh closed to rigid joints opens with a cut, which removes a rigid connection of $3$ scalar constraints. With two cuts, one per closed loop, remains an open body, $n = 1$.

The open body has the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$ with the vertical reaction, which does not pass through $A$, so it is stationary and $l = 0$. External supports are $3 = 3n$ and do not give static indeterminacy.

The two internal fixed supports join cut faces of the same rigid body and do not prevent extra displacements, so each of their simple $6$ constraints increases the degree of static indeterminacy by one, and $i = 6$. The value $i = 3$ is obtained by counting only one mesh, and the value $i = 9$ by counting one cut for each of the three columns.
