## Explanation

The structure has $n = 3$ bodies. The three pin supports equal $2$ each and the hinge $C$, which connects three bodies, equals $2\,(3 - 1) = 4$, so $s = 6 + 4 = 10$ and $3n - s = -1$. The $ADC$ and $CEB$ bodies form a three-hinged arch with $A$, $C$ and $B$ not collinear, so they are stationary. The $FC$ pillar then has the distinct $F$ and $C$ centers, and is stationary. So $l = 0$ and $i = 1$. Counting the hinge $C$ as a simple hinge would get $s = 8$ and $l = 1$, and counting $2 \cdot 3 = 6$ would get $i = 3$.
