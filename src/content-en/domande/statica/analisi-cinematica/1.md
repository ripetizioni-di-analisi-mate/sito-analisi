- $l = 0$, $i = 2$
- $l = 2$, $i = 0$
- $l = 0$, $i = 3$

## Explanation

The beam is a single rigid body, so $n = 1$ and it has $3n = 3$ degrees of freedom. The fixed support imposes $3$ scalar constraints and the pin imposes $2$. Thus $s = 5$ and $3n - s = -2$. The fixed support alone prevents all rigid-body motion, so $l = 0$. The relation $3n - s = l - i$ then gives $i = 2$. The beam is **statically indeterminate** to the second degree. The pair $l = 2$, $i = 0$ results from reversing the sign of $3n - s$.
