## Explanation

The arch has $n = 2$ and $s = 6 = 3n$, and for the three-hinges arch theorem it is unstable if and only if $A$, $B$ and $C$ are collinear. The line through $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (10, 2)\,\mathrm{m}$ has equation $y = \dfrac{x}{5}$. The point $C = (5\,\mathrm{m},\ h)$ lies on this line if $h = \dfrac{5}{5}\,\mathrm{m} = 1\,\mathrm{m}$. For $h = 1\,\mathrm{m}$ the arch is therefore **unstable**, with $l = i = 1$. The value $h = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ puts $C$ at the same elevation as $B$, and in that case the three points are not aligned.
