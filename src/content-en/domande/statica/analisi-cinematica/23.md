The beam $AC$ in the figure is resting on three roller supports that travel on horizontal surfaces, at $A$, at $B$ and at $C$, with $AB = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $BC = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, so it has degree of static indeterminacy $i = 1$. Which system of vertical reactions $R_A$, $R_B$, and $R_C$ is in equilibrium in the absence of applied loads?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello;C,carrello" quote="A,B,2 m;B,C,4 m"}

- $R_A = Y$, $R_B = -2Y$, and $R_C = Y$, with arbitrary $Y$
- $R_A = 2Y$, $R_B = -3Y$, and $R_C = Y$, with arbitrary $Y$
- $R_A = Y$, $R_B = -3Y$, and $R_C = 2Y$, with arbitrary $Y$
- $R_A = 3Y$, $R_B = -4Y$, and $R_C = Y$, with arbitrary $Y$

## Explanation

The reactions are vertical, so the horizontal translation equation is satisfied on its own. Without loads remain the vertical translation and rotation around $A$, with the lengths in meters:
$$
\begin{cases}
R_A + R_B + R_C = 0 \\
2\,R_B + 6\,R_C = 0
\end{cases}
$$
Place $R_C = Y$, the second equation gives $R_B = -3Y$ and the first gives $R_A = 2Y$.

The self-balanced reactions are then $R_A = 2Y$, $R_B = -3Y$, $R_C = Y$, and the free parameter $Y$ corresponds to the only static indeterminacy. The three reactions $Y$, $-2Y$, and $Y$ have zero resultant, but their moment about $A$ is $-4Y + 6Y = 2Y \neq 0$; You'd get it with intermediate roller support in the middle.
