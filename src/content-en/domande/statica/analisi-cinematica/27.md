The beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, is supported on three roller supports, at $A$, in the midpoint $D$ and at $B$, all on horizontal planes. What is the rank $r$ of its kinematic matrix?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-D;D-B" vincoli="A,carrello;D,carrello;B,carrello" quote="A,D,3 m;D,B,3 m"}

- $r = 3$
- $r = 2$
- $r = 1$
- $r = 0$

## Explanation

With the $A$ reference point and $u_A$, $v_A$, $\varphi$, the three roller supports impose $v_A = 0$, $v_A + 3\varphi = 0$ and $v_A + 6\varphi = 0$, so the kinematic matrix is
$$
\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & 6 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The first column is zero, because no roller support prevents horizontal shifts, so the determinant is zero and $r < 3$. The first two lines are independent, so $r = 2$. $l = 3 - 2 = 1$ and $i = 3 - 2 = 1$ follow, though $s = 3n$.
