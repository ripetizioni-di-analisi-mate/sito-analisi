The parallelogram articulated in the figure is formed by the $AC$, $CD$ and $DB$ rods, with the pin supports at $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (4, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ and the internal hinges in $C = (1, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $D = (5, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$. A two-force link is added to the midpoint $M = (3, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$ of the $CD$ member. For what orientation of the link axis does the structure remain unstable?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,1,3;D,5,3;B,4,0" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C;D" punti="3,3,M,pieno" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9"}

- Parallel to members $AC$ and $DB$
- Vertical
- Horizontal
- Perpendicular to members $AC$ and $DB$

## Explanation

Without the two-force link the structure has $n = 3$ and $s = 8$ and is a mechanism with $l = 1$. The $AC$ and $DB$ rods are parallel, so the $AC$ and $BD$ lines meet at their point at infinity, which is the center $C_2$ of $CD$. Therefore $CD$ moves perpendicular to the $AC$ and $DB$ rods, in the direction of $(3, -1)$.

The two-force link at $M$ requires that $C_2$ stand on its axis. The mechanism remains possible only if the axis passes through the point at infinity of the rods, i.e. if it is parallel to $AC$ and $DB$; in this case the displacement of $M$ is perpendicular to the axis of the two-force link, and the structure is unstable and statically indeterminate with $l = i = 1$.

With an axis in any other direction the two-force link prevents the translation of $CD$, and the structure is statically determinate.
