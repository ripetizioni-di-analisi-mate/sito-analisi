The beam $AB$ shown, of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$ and in $B = (5, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ a roller support that moves along a vertical plane. With the $A$ reference point and $(u_A, v_A, \varphi)$ parameters, the kinematic matrix is
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Which vector $\mathbf{q}$ solves $A\,\mathbf{q} = \mathbf{0}$ and therefore describes a possible displacement?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" quote="A,B,5 m"}

- $\mathbf{q} = (1,\ 0,\ 0)$, a horizontal translation
- $\mathbf{q} = (0,\ 1,\ 0)$, a vertical translation
- $\mathbf{q} = (0,\ 0,\ 1)$, a rotation about $A$
- $\mathbf{q} = \left(0,\ 1,\ -\dfrac{1}{5}\right)$, a rotation about $B$

## Explanation

The first and third lines of $A$ are equal, so the rank is $r = 2$ and the solutions form a subspace of $3 - 2 = 1$ size.

The equations are $u_A = 0$, $v_A = 0$ and again $u_A = 0$. The parameter $\varphi$ does not appear in any equation, so it is free and the solutions are the multiples of $(0, 0, 1)$: the beam rotates about $A$. In fact, the horizontal reaction of the roller support has the line of action for $A$.

The vector $\left(0, 1, -\dfrac{1}{5}\right)$ does not satisfy the equation $v_A = 0$.
