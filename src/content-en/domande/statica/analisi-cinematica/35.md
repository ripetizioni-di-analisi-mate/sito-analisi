The beam shown is formed by the $AC$ and $CD$ sections, connected by the internal hinge $C = (4, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$, with a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$ and a roller support at $D = (10, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ that moves along a horizontal plane. The beam is unstable. If segment $AC$ rotates through $\varphi_1$, what is the rotation $\varphi_2$ of segment $CD$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" cerniere="C" quote="A,C,4 m;C,D,6 m"}

- $\varphi_2 = \dfrac{2}{3}\,\varphi_1$
- $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{2}{3}\,\varphi_1$
- $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{3}{2}\,\varphi_1$
- $\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1$

## Explanation

The segment $AC$ rotates about the pin support, then $C_1 = A$. The center $C_2$ lies on the vertical through $D$ and, for the first theorem of kinematic chains, on the line through $C_1 = A$ and $C_{12} = C$, i.e. on the axis of the beam; then $C_2 = D$. The vertical displacement of the hinge $C$ equals $\varphi_1 \cdot 4\,\mathrm{m}$ as the point of $AC$ and $\varphi_2\,(x_C - x_D) = -6\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_2$ as the point of $CD$. By matching the two values you get $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{4}{6}\,\varphi_1 = -\dfrac{2}{3}\,\varphi_1$. The minus sign indicates that the two segments rotate in opposite directions, because $C$ stands between the two centers.
