The portal frame in the figure, with the rigid $D = (0, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $E = (6, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$ nodes, has a pin support at the $A = (0, 0)$ foot and a roller support at the $B = (6, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ foot that moves along a horizontal plane. A diagonal counterwind, schematized as a two-force link, connects the $D$ and $B$ nodes. What are $l$ and $i$?

## Explanation

Columns and crossbeams, joined by rigid joints, form one body. The diagonal connects two points of the same body, so it is an internal two-force link of multiplicity $1$, and $s = 2 + 1 + 1 = 4$.

The hinge $A$ and $B$ roller support make the portal frame firm, because the vertical through $B$ does not pass through $A$. Therefore $l = 0$.

The relation $3n - s = l - i$ gives $i = 1$. The rigidity of the body already fixes the distance between $D$ and $B$. Adding the link therefore leaves the possible motion unchanged and introduces one redundant restraint, just as reconnecting a cut that closes a loop does.
