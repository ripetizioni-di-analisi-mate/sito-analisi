The structure in the figure is formed by member $AP$, hinged to the ground at $A = (0, 0)$, and member $BQ$, hinged to the ground in $B = (8, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$, with $P = (3, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $Q = (5, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$. The two rods are only connected by the horizontal pin-ended link $PQ$. What are $l$ and $i$?

## Explanation

The rods are $n = 2$ bodies, hence $3n = 6$. The pin supports equal $2 + 2$ and the pin-ended link is an internal two-force link of multiplicity $1$, therefore $s = 5$ and $3n - s = 1$.

Member $AP$ rotates about $A$ through angle $\varphi_1$, and member $BQ$ rotates about $B$ through angle $\varphi_2$. The pin-ended link requires that $P$ and $Q$ have the same horizontal displacement component: $u_P = -4\varphi_1$ and $u_Q = -4\varphi_2$, then $\varphi_2 = \varphi_1$.

The displacements therefore depend only on the parameter $\varphi_1$, then $l = 1$ and, from the relation $3n - s = l - i$, $i = 0$. With an internal hinge instead of the pin-ended link the structure would be a three-hinged arch.
