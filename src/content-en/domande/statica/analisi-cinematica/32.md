The beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, is supported on three roller supports, at $A$, in the midpoint $D$ and at $B$. The three sliding planes are parallel and tilted by $30^\circ$ on the horizontal. Which displacement is compatible with the restraints?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-D;D-B" vincoli="A,carrello,240;D,carrello,240;B,carrello,240" quote="A,D,3 m,0.9;D,B,3 m,0.9"}

- None, because $s = 3n$
- A horizontal translation
- A rotation about $D$
- A translation parallel to the support surfaces

## Explanation

The reactions of the three roller supports are perpendicular to the sliding planes, so they have parallel lines of action, all of which pass through the same point at infinity. By the theorem on the body with three scalar constraints the beam is therefore unstable, and its center of rotation is that point at infinity. Displacement is a translation perpendicular to reactions, i.e. an **translation parallel to the sliding planes**. A horizontal translation would have a component along the reactions, equal to half the shift, so it is not compatible with roller supports. The beam has $l = 1$ and, since $s = 3n$, also $i = 1$.
