The horizontal $AB$ beam shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin support at $A$ and at $B$ a roller support that moves along a vertical plane, so it is unstable. Which applied load on the beam can be balanced by the support reactions?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9"}

- A vertical force at midspan
- A horizontal force at midspan
- A couple at midspan
- A force inclined at $45^\circ$ and applied at $B$

## Explanation

The $H_A$ and $V_A$ reactions of the hinge pass through $A$, and the horizontal $R_B$ reaction of the roller support has the line of action along the beam axis, which also passes through $A$. So no reaction has moment about $A$.

Therefore the rotation equation around $A$ is only satisfied if the load has zero moment about $A$, i.e. if its line of action passes through $A$. The horizontal force in the middle acts along the axis of the beam, so it can be balanced by $H_A$ and $R_B$.

The vertical force in the middle has a moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$, the couple has no zero moment about each point and the inclined force at $B$ has a vertical component with a moment arm of $6\,\mathrm{m}$; none of these loads can be balanced.
