The beam shown is formed by the cantilever $AB$, fixed at $A$, and the segment $BC$, connected at $B$ by an internal parallel-link connection with the horizontal links. Once cantilever $AB$ is known to be stationary, what external restraint on segment $BC$ is equivalent to the connection at $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,7,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180" interni="B,doppio-pendolo,0" quote="A,B,3 m;B,C,4 m"}

- A pin support
- An external parallel-link support with horizontal links
- A fixed support
- An external parallel-link support with vertical links

## Explanation

The cantilever $AB$ is fixed, so it is stationary and acts like the ground for the bodies connected to it.

Internal parallel-link connection prevents relative rotation and relative sliding parallel to pin-ended links. With $AB$ stationary, these conditions become $\varphi_2 = 0$ and horizontal displacement of $B$ null, i.e. the conditions of an external parallel-link support with the horizontal links.

The segment $BC$ can then translate vertically: without another constraint the beam is unstable.
