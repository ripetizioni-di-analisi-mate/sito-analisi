The columns $AD$ and $BE$ shown have height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and are fixed at their bases $A$ and $B$. Beam $DE$ has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$. A hinge connects it to column $AD$ at $D$, and a vertical link connects it to column $BE$ at $E$. How is the structure classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;E,6,4;B,6,0" aste="A-D;D-E;E-B" vincoli="A,incastro;B,incastro" cerniere="D" interni="E,pendolo,90" quote="D,E,6 m,-0.7;B,E,4 m"}

- Statically determinate, with $l = i = 0$
- Statically indeterminate, with $l = 0$ and $i = 1$
- Statically indeterminate, with $l = 0$ and $i = 3$
- Unstable, with $l = 1$ and $i = 0$

## Explanation

The bodies are the two pillars and the crossbeam, hence $n = 3$. The scalar constraints are $s = 3 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 9 = 3n$.

The two pillars are fixed and therefore stationary. They then act like the ground for the crossbeam, which is bound by a pin support at $D$ and a two-force vertical link at $E$. The vertical through $E$ does not pass through $D$, so the crossbeam is also stationary.

So $l = 0$ and, from the $3n - s = l - i$ report, $i = 0$. The value $i = 3$ is obtained by thinking of the portal frame as a single body with two fixed supports.
