Two rigid bodies are connected by an internal parallel-link connection with the horizontal links and have a relative non-zero displacement. Where is the relative center of rotation $C_{12}$?

- At the midpoint between the two links
- At infinity in the vertical direction
- At a finite point on the axis of one of the two links
- At infinity in the horizontal direction

## Explanation

Internal parallel-link connection prevents relative rotation and relative displacement parallel to pin-ended links, i.e. horizontal. So the relative displacement is a vertical translation. The center of a translation is the point at infinity in the direction perpendicular to the displacements, therefore $C_{12}$ is the **Misplaced point of horizontal direction**, that is, of the direction of the pin-ended links. The point at infinity in the vertical direction would correspond to a horizontal relative translation, which pin-ended links prevent.
