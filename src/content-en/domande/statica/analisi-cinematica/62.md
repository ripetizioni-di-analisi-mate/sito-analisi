The rectangular plate in the figure, with the vertices $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$, $C = (4, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $D = (0, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$, is bound by three two-force links: at $A$ with the vertical axis, at $B$ with the horizontal axis, at $C$ with the direct axis along the diagonal $AC$. How is the plate classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,4,3;D,0,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-A" vincoli="A,pendolo;B,pendolo,0;C,pendolo,36.87" tratteggi="0,0 4,3" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,3 m"}

- Statically determinate, with $l = i = 0$
- Unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$
- Unstable, with $l = 1$ and $i = 0$
- Statically indeterminate, with $l = 0$ and $i = 1$

## Explanation

The plate is a body with three scalar constraints, so it is statically determinate if and only if the three lines of action do not pass by the same point.

The axis of the two-force link at $A$ is the line $x = 0$ and that of the two-force link at $B$ is the line $y = 0$; The two lines meet at $A$. The axis of the two-force link at $C$ is the line $AC$, which also passes through $A$. The three lines are therefore concurrent at $A$ and the plate can rotates about $A$.

Therefore $l = 1$ and, since $s = 3n$, $i = l = 1$.
