The $AB$ and $AC$ rods in the figure are both connected to the same pin support $A$ and have no other connection to each other. The horizontal $AB$ rod is placed at $B$ on a roller support that moves over a horizontal plane; the vertical rod $AC$ is placed at $C$ on a roller support that moves along a vertical plane. Which statement is correct?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,0,4" aste="A-B;A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;C,carrello,180" cerniere="A" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9;A,C,4 m"}

- $s = 6$, and the structure is statically determinate
- $s = 4$, and the structure is unstable, with $l = 2$
- $s = 6$, and the structure is unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$
- $s = 8$, and the structure is statically indeterminate, with $i = 2$

## Explanation

The structure has $n = 2$ bodies. A pin support to which two bodies are connected has multiplicity $2 \cdot 2 = 4$, so with the two roller supports $s = 4 + 1 + 1 = 6 = 3n$. Member $AB$ has the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$ with the vertical reaction, which does not pass through $A$, so it is stationary. Member $AC$ has the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $C$ with the horizontal reaction, which does not pass through $A$, so it is also stationary. So $l = i = 0$ and the structure is **statically determinate**. The value $s = 4$ is obtained by counting the hinge $A$ only once.
