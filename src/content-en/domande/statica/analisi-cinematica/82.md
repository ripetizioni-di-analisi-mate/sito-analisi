The $AB$ ladder in the figure rests with the extreme $A = (3, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ on the horizontal floor and the extreme $B = (0, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$ on the vertical wall. The two supports are smooth, so they equal roller supports that travel on the floor and wall. Where is the center of rotation of the ladder?

::struttura{nodi="A,3,0;B,0,4" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello,180" linee="0,0 4,0;0,0 0,5" quote="A,0 0,3 m,-0.7;0 0,B,4 m,-0.7"}

- At $(0, 0)$, where the floor meets the wall
- At $(3, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$
- At $\left(\dfrac{3}{2}, 2\right)\mathrm{m}$, the midpoint of the ladder
- At infinity in the direction of $AB$

## Explanation

Each roller support requires the center to stand on the line of action of its reaction, normal to the support surface.

The floor reaction at $A$ is vertical, with the line of action $x = 3$. The wall reaction at $B$ is horizontal, with the line of action $y = 4$.

The center is therefore the intersection of the two lines, the point $(3, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$. The ladder has $s = 2 < 3n$ and is unstable with $l = 1$: the ends travel along floor and wall, perpendicular to the line joining the center.
