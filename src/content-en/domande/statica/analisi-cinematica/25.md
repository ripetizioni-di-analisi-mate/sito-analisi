The beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, is supported on two roller supports at $A$ and $B$ that travel on horizontal surfaces. What are $l$ and $i$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m"}

- $l = 1$, $i = 0$
- $l = 0$, $i = 0$
- $l = 2$, $i = 0$
- $l = 1$, $i = 1$

## Explanation

The beam is a body with $s = 2$, then $3n - s = 1$ and $l \ge 1$. The two roller supports require that a possible rotation center be on the vertical through $A$ and on the vertical through $B$. These two lines have in common only the point at infinity in the vertical direction, so the only possible shift is horizontal translation. This shift depends on only one parameter, therefore $l = 1$. The $3n - s = l - i$ report follows $i = 0$.
