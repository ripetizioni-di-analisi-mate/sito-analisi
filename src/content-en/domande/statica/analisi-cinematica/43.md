The Gerber beam in the figure is formed by the cantilever $AC$, fixed at $A$, and the segment $CE$, connected to the cantilever by the internal hinge $C$ and supported at $D$ and $E$ on two roller supports that travel on horizontal surfaces. How many unknown external and internal reaction components occur in the equilibrium equations of the two segments?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;D,7,0;E,10,0" aste="A-C;C-E" vincoli="A,incastro,180;D,carrello;E,carrello" cerniere="C" quote="A,C,4 m;C,D,3 m;D,E,3 m"}

- $7$
- $5$
- $6$
- $9$

## Explanation

Each scalar constraint exhibits a reaction component, so the unknowns are as many as the scalar constraints. Fixed support gives $H_A$, $V_A$ and $M_A$, the two roller supports give $R_D$ and $R_E$, and the internal hinge gives the transmitted forces $X_C$ and $Y_C$. Therefore the unknowns are $3 + 1 + 1 + 2 = 7$, while the equilibrium equations of the two segments are $3n = 6$. The cantilever is stationary and the section $CE$ has the $C$ centers and the vertical through $D$, distinct, so $l = 0$ and the beam is statically indeterminate with $i = 7 - 6 = 1$. The value $5$ only counts external reactions.
