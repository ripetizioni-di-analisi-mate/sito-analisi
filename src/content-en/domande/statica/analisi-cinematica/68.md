Two rigid bodies are connected by an internal two-force link, i.e. a pin-ended unloaded link hinged to point $P_1$ of the first body and point $P_2$ of the second, with the tilted axis of $45^\circ$ on the horizontal. What forces or moments do the two bodies transmit through the link?

- A force in any direction, acting equally and oppositely on the two bodies
- A force directed along the link axis and a moment
- A force along the link axis, acting equally and oppositely on the two bodies
- A force perpendicular to the link axis, acting equally and oppositely on the two bodies

## Explanation

The internal two-force link only prevents the relative displacement component of $P_2$ from $P_1$ along the pin-ended link axis, so it has multiplicity $1$. A constraint exerts a reaction component for each displacement component that prevents, directed as the displacement prevented, so the force exchanged has the direction of the axis.

The pin-ended link is unloaded and hinged at the two extremes, so it is in equilibrium under the two-hinge forces alone, which must have the same line of action, the axis of the pin-ended link. Hinges let the rotations go free, so the two-force link does not transmit moments. By the principle of action and reaction force acts on the two bodies with opposite directions.

Any directional force is that exchanged by an internal hinge, of multiplicity $2$.
