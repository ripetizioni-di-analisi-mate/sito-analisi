The beam $AB$ shown, of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin support at $A$ and at $B$ a roller support whose support surface is tilted by $20^\circ$ on the horizontal and descends to the right. What is the line of action of the roller reaction?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,250" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9"}

- The line through $B$ normal to the support surface, inclined at $20^\circ$ to the vertical
- The line through $B$ parallel to the support surface, inclined at $20^\circ$ to the horizontal
- The vertical line through $B$, regardless of the inclination of the surface
- The line through $B$ normal to the support surface, inclined at $20^\circ$ to the horizontal

## Explanation

Roller support only prevents normal $B$ from moving to the support surface, so it only reacts one way, directed according to normal and with the line of action for $B$.

The plane forms $20^\circ$ with the horizontal, therefore its normal form $20^\circ$ with the vertical, i.e. $70^\circ$ with the horizontal. The reaction is vertical only when the support surface is horizontal.
