The portal frame in the figure has the rigid $D$ and $E$ nodes, an internal hinge $C$ in the middle of the crossbeam, a fixed support at the base $A$ and a pin support at the base $B$. How many bodies $n$ and scalar restraints $s$ are there?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;C,3,4;E,6,4;B,6,0" aste="A-D;D-C;C-E;E-B" vincoli="A,incastro;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" quote="D,C,3 m,-0.7;C,E,3 m,-0.7;B,E,4 m"}

- $n = 1$, $s = 5$
- $n = 3$, $s = 7$
- $n = 2$, $s = 8$
- $n = 2$, $s = 7$

## Explanation

The rods joined by the rigid joints belong to the same body, while the internal hinge $C$ separates two bodies. The portal frame is then formed by the bodies $ADC$ and $CEB$, and $n = 2$. The scalar constraints are $3$ for fixed support, $2$ for pin support and $2$ for internal hinge, therefore $s = 7$. The body $ADC$ is fixed and the body $CEB$ has the distinct $C$ and $B$ centers, so both are stationary and, because $3n - s = -1$, the portal frame is statically indeterminate with $i = 1$. The $n = 1$, $s = 5$ couple is achieved by neglecting the internal hinge.
