The beam shown fixed at $A$ and is formed by the segments $AB$, $BC$ and $CD$. At $B$ there is an internal parallel-link connection with vertical links, at $C$ an internal two-force link with the vertical axis, at $D$ a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. What are the number of scalar restraints $s$ and the degree of kinematic instability $l$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,incastro,180;D,carrello" interni="B,doppio-pendolo,90;C,pendolo,90" quote="A,B,3 m;B,C,3 m;C,D,3 m"}

- $s = 8$, $l = 1$
- $s = 9$, $l = 0$
- $s = 7$, $l = 1$
- $s = 7$, $l = 2$

## Explanation

The bodies are $n = 3$, hence $3n = 9$. Fixed support equals $3$, internal parallel-link connection $2$, internal two-force link $1$ and roller support $1$, therefore $s = 7$.

The section $AB$ is stationary, because it is fixed. The internal parallel-link connection leaves the segment $BC$ the only perpendicular translation to the pin-ended links, i.e. horizontal, and no constraint of $BC$ prevents it. The internal two-force vertical link at $C$ leaves the relative horizontal displacement of $CD$, and the roller support at $D$ does not prevent it. There are therefore two independent horizontal translations, and $l = 2$.

From the $3n - s = l - i$ report we get $i = l - 2 = 0$. The value $s = 8$ is obtained by counting the internal two-force link as a hinge.
