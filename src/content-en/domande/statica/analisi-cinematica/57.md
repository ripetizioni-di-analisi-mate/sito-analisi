The beam $AB$ shown, of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin support at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ that moves along a horizontal plane. With the reference point at $A$, the $(u_A, v_A, \varphi)$ parameters and the rows in the order $u_A = 0$, $v_A = 0$, $v_B = 0$, the kinematic matrix is
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 5 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The equilibrium equations, in the order horizontal translation, vertical translation and rotation around $A$, are written as a matrix for the vector $(H_A, V_A, R_B)$ plus the terms of the loads. Which matrix multiplies the reaction vector?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,5 m"}

- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & -5 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 5 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

For the relation between static and kinematics, the matrix of reaction coefficients in equilibrium equations is the $A^{T}$ transpose of the kinematic matrix.

It occurs directly. Only $H_A$ appears in the horizontal translation; $V_A$ and $R_B$ appear in the vertical translation; In rotation about $A$ only $R_B$ appears, with the moment $5\,\mathrm{m}$ arm and the positive sign, because the vertical reaction upwards at $B$ has counterclockwise moment about $A$. The rows are therefore $(1, 0, 0)$, $(0, 1, 1)$ and $(0, 0, 5)$, i.e. the columns of $A$.
