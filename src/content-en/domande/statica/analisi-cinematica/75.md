In a frame the crossbeam $DE$ is continuous, without hinges, and in its point $C$ is connected with a hinge at the head of the $FC$ pillar. How many scalar restraints does the connection at $C$ impose?

- $2$
- $4$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

The crossbeam $DE$ is continuous, so its $DC$ and $CE$ segments belong to the same body. In the $C$ node the hinge therefore connects $k = 2$ bodies, the crossbeam and the pillar.

A hinge between $k$ bodies equals $2\,(k - 1)$ scalar constraints, so the link at $C$ equals $2$. The value $4$ is obtained by counting three bodies, as if the crossbeam were interrupted at $C$ by a hinge.
