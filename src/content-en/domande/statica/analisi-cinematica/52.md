The beam shown is formed by the $AC$ and $CB$ sections, connected by the internal hinge $C$. The section $AC$ is fixed at $A$ and the section $CB$ has no other constraints. What are the degree of kinematic instability $l$ and the degree of static indeterminacy $i$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,5,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" cerniere="C" quote="A,C,3 m;C,B,2 m"}

- $l = 0$, $i = 0$
- $l = 0$, $i = 1$
- $l = 1$, $i = 0$
- $l = 2$, $i = 0$

## Explanation

The bodies are $n = 2$, so $3n = 6$, and the scalar constraints are $s = 3 + 2 = 5$. Therefore $3n - s = 1$ and the beam is unstable with $l \ge 1$.

The section $AC$ is stationary, because it is fixed, so the point $C$ is stationary. The segment $CB$ can only rotates about $C$, and its displacement depends on the $\varphi_2$ parameter alone. Therefore $l = 1$ and, from the report $3n - s = l - i$, $i = 0$.
