- Inclined at approximately $53.13^\circ$, rising to the right
- Inclined at approximately $53.13^\circ$, falling to the right
- Inclined at approximately $36.87^\circ$, rising to the right
- Inclined at approximately $36.87^\circ$, falling to the right

## Explanation

The beam is a body with $s = 3 = 3n$, and is unstable when the line of action of the roller support reaction passes through the hinge $A$, i.e. when the normal to the support surface at $B$ is the line $AB$. The line $AB$ has $\dfrac{3}{4}$ slope, so the support surface, perpendicular to $AB$, has $-\dfrac{4}{3}$ slope. Therefore the plane descends to the right and forms with the horizontal the angle $\alpha$ with $\tan\alpha = \dfrac{4}{3}$, i.e. $\alpha \approx 53.13^\circ$. The inclined plane of $36.87^\circ$ rising to the right is parallel to $AB$, and its normal for $B$ does not pass through $A$.
