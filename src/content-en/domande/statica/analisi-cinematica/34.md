A continuous straight beam rests on five supports: a pin support at the left end and four roller supports on horizontal planes. How many internal hinges must be inserted to satisfy $s = 3n$?

- $3$
- $4$
- $5$
- $2$

## Explanation

Without internal hinges the beam is one body with $s = 2 + 4 = 6$, hence $3n - s = -3$. Each internal hinge adds a body, i.e. $3$ degrees of freedom, and $2$ scalar constraints, therefore increases $3n - s$ by $1$. To get to $3n - s = 0$ you need $3$ internal hinges, in accordance with the rule of the $k - 2$ hinges for a beam on $k = 5$ supports. The number of hinges gives only the necessary condition, and their position must allow the beam to be analysed from a stationary bearing part.
