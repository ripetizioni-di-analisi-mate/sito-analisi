The arch shown consists of members $AC$ and $CB$ joined at internal hinge $C = (4, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$. At $A = (0, 0)$ there is a pin support and in $B = (8, 2)\,\mathrm{m}$ a parallel-link support with the horizontal links. How is the arch classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,4;B,8,2" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,doppio-pendolo,0" cerniere="C" quote="A,8 0,8 m,0.9"}

- Unstable, with $l = 1$ and $i = 0$
- Unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$
- Statically indeterminate, with $l = 0$ and $i = 1$
- Statically determinate, with $l = i = 0$

## Explanation

You have $n = 2$ and $s = 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 = 3n$, so just verify that $l = 0$.

The parallel-link support with the horizontal connecting rods imposes on $CB$ the center at the horizontal $X_\infty$ point at infinity, while $C_1 = A$ and $C_{12} = C$. If both rods moved, the three centers would be aligned, i.e. the line $AC$ would be horizontal; Instead, $AC$ has $1$ slope.

If only one rod moved, the other would be stationary, the point $C$ would be stationary and the moving rod would have two distinct centers. Therefore $l = 0$ and $i = 0$. With $C$ at the same elevation as $A$ the arch would be unstable instead.
