The beam shown consists of segments $AC$ and $CB$, each of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, joined at internal hinge $C$. It has a parallel-link support with horizontal links at $A$ and a pin support at $B$. The beam is unstable. If segment $AC$ translates upward by $v$, what is the rotation $\varphi_2$ of segment $CB$, taking counterclockwise as positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,8,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,180;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" quote="A,C,4 m;C,B,4 m"}

- $\varphi_2 = \dfrac{v}{4\,\mathrm{m}}$
- $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{v}{4\,\mathrm{m}}$
- $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{v}{8\,\mathrm{m}}$
- $\varphi_2 = 0$

## Explanation

Parallel-link support with horizontal links prevents rotation and horizontal translation of the segment $AC$, so $AC$ can only move vertically and the hinge $C$ goes up by $v$. The segment $CB$ rotates about the pin support $B$, so the vertical displacement of $C$ as the point of $CB$ equals
$$
v_C = \varphi_2\,(x_C - x_B) = -4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_2 .
$$
By imposing $v_C = v$ you get $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{v}{4\,\mathrm{m}}$, i.e. a clockwise rotation. In fact, a point to the left of the center of rotation rises when the rotation is clockwise. The value $-\dfrac{v}{8\,\mathrm{m}}$ is obtained using the distance of $B$ from $A$ instead of the distance of $B$ from $C$.
