## Explanation

The portal frame has rigid joints, so it is one body and $3n = 3$. Fixed support has $3$ multiplicity and roller support has $1$ multiplicity, so $s = 4$ and $3n - s = -1$. Fixed support alone prevents any movement of the portal frame, hence $l = 0$. From the $3n - s = l - i$ relationship follows $i = 1$, and the portal frame is **statically indeterminate** once.
