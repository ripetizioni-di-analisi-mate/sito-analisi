A plane rigid-jointed portal forms one rigid body. Its foot $A$ is fixed. At $B$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right at the same elevation, a roller prevents vertical movement. Which single change removes the redundancy while retaining both supports?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;P,0,2.6,-;Q,4,2.6,-;B,4,0" aste="A-P;P-Q;Q-B" vincoli="A,incastro,270;B,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m,1" titolo="Rigid frame with a clamp and a roller"}

- Add a horizontal restraint at $B$.
- Replace the fixed support at $A$ with a single vertical restraint.
- Replace the fixed support at $A$ with a pin.
- Replace the roller at $B$ with a fixed support.

## Explanation

A pin at $A$ restrains both translations. A rotation about $A$ would move $B$ vertically, which the roller prevents. The three scalar restraints are independent, so the portal is statically determinate.
