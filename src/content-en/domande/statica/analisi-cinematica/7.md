## Explanation

The portal frame has rigid joints, so it is a single body and $3n = 3$. The fixed support has multiplicity $3$ and the roller has multiplicity $1$, so $s = 4$ and $3n - s = -1$. The fixed support alone prevents any displacement of the frame, hence $l = 0$. From the relation $3n - s = l - i$ it follows that $i = 1$, and the portal frame is **statically indeterminate** to degree one.
