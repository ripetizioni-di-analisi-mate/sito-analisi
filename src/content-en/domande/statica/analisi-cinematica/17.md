Horizontal beam $AB$ has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and a pin support at $A$. Which support at $B$ would make the beam statically determinate?

- A roller on a vertical surface
- A horizontal two-force link
- A two-force link inclined at $45^\circ$
- A pin support

## Explanation

With the pin at $A$ the beam is statically determinate if at $B$ there is a scalar constraint whose reaction has a line of action that does not pass through $A$. The roller support on a vertical plane and the two-force horizontal link have the horizontal reaction, directed along the axis of the beam, which passes through $A$. With these constraints the beam can rotates about $A$ and is unstable and statically indeterminate. A pin support at $B$ leads to $s = 4$, and the beam is statically indeterminate with $i = 1$. $45^\circ$'s tilted two-force link has the line of action passing through $B$ and not $A$, so the beam is **statically determinate**.
