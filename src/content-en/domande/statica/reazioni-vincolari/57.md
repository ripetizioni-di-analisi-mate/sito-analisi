Beam $AB$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long. At $A$, a parallel-link support with horizontal links restrains horizontal translation and rotation. At $B$, a roller rests on a horizontal surface. A uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the entire beam. What is the reaction moment $M_A$ at the parallel-link support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Beam with a horizontal parallel-link support at $A$ and a roller at $B$"}

- $M_A = 0$
- $M_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

The support with two parallel horizontal links provides horizontal force $H_A$ and couple $M_A$, with no vertical force. Vertical force equilibrium therefore contains only $V_B$ and gives $V_B = 3 \cdot 4 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$. The roller carries the entire vertical load. Taking moments about $A$ gives
$$
M_A + 12 \cdot 4 - 12 \cdot 2 = 0 ,
$$
where $12 \cdot 2$ is the moment of the load resultant. The reaction moment is $M_A = -24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, or $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise. Treating the parallel-link support as a pin would give $0$ because a pin would require $V_A = V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$.
