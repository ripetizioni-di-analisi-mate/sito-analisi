Beam $AB$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$ and carries an inclined force $F$. The horizontal force balance gives $H_A$. Taking moments about $A$ gives $V_B$, and taking moments about $B$ gives $V_A$. Which other equilibrium equation can be used to check these reactions?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="2 0,0.9,-1.2,F" quote="A,2 0,2 m;2 0,B,3 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under an inclined force"}

- Rotation about $A$
- Vertical translation
- Horizontal translation
- Rotation about $B$

## Explanation

Check the reactions with an equilibrium equation that contains them and was not used to find them. Vertical force equilibrium involves $V_A$ and $V_B$, obtained from two different equations. The correct values give $0$, while a nonzero residual reveals an error. The other three equations were used in the calculation. Substituting their own solutions back into them cannot expose an incorrect sign or moment arm in their construction.
