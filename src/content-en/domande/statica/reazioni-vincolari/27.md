Beam $AB$ is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long and has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. The vertical load varies linearly along the beam, from $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ downward at $A$ through zero at midspan to $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ upward at $B$. What is the reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;M,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,M,0.7,0,3 kN/m;M,B,0,0.7,3 kN/m,90" quote="A,M,3 m,0.9;M,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam with a load that reverses direction at midspan"}

- $V_A = 0$
- $V_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $V_A = 2.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

The triangular load on each half has a resultant of magnitude $\dfrac{3 \cdot 3}{2} = 4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward on half $AM$ and upward on half $MB$. Each acts through its triangle’s centroid, $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from the end where the intensity is $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Their positions are therefore $1\,\mathrm{m}$ and $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Together they have zero resultant force and a counterclockwise moment about $A$ of $4.5 \cdot 5 - 4.5 \cdot 1 = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, forming a couple. Taking moments about $A$ gives $6\,V_B + 18 = 0$, hence $V_B = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$. Vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = -V_B = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The value $0$ overlooks the reactions needed to balance a couple with zero resultant force. The value $2.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ places each triangular resultant at the midpoint of its half.
