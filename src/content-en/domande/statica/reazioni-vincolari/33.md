Cantilever $AB$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long, fixed at its right end $B$, and free at its left end $A$. A uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the full length. At $A$, a couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts counterclockwise. What is the reaction moment $M_B$ at the fixed support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="B,incastro,0" carichi="A,B,0.4,0.4,q" coppie="A,1,ℳ" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Right-fixed cantilever under a uniform load and a counterclockwise couple"}

- $M_B = 26\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_B = 26\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_B = 42\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

The load resultant $2 \cdot 4 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward at a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$. Its moment about $B$ is therefore $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise. The applied couple is also counterclockwise. Taking moments about $B$ gives
$$
M_B + 8 \cdot 2 + 10 = 0 ,
$$
Thus $M_B = -26\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, or $26\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise. Applying the load resultant at $42\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ instead of at midspan would give $A$.
