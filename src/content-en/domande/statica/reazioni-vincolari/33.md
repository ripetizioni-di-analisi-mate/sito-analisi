Cantilever $AB$ in the figure is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long and fixed at its left end $A$. A counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at the free end $B$; a downward vertical force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ also acts on the cantilever, at distance $x$ from $A$. For which value of $x$ is the reaction moment $M_A$ of the fixed support zero?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;P,2.2,0,-;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="P,0,-1.3,F" coppie="B,1,ℳ" quote="A,P,x,0.8;A,B,5 m,1.6" titolo="Cantilever with couple $\mathcal{M}$ at $B$ and force $F$ at distance $x$ from $A$"}

- $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = 5\,\mathrm{m}$, that is, at $B$
- For no value of $x$ between $0$ and $5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

A couple has the same moment about every pole, so it enters moment equilibrium about $A$ as $+18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, wherever it is applied. Force $F$ points downward and lies to the right of $A$, so its moment about $A$ is clockwise and equals $-6x$. Reactions $H_A$ and $V_A$ pass through $A$, so moment equilibrium about $A$ gives
$$
M_A + 18 - 6x = 0 .
$$
Setting $M_A = 0$ gives $6x = 18$, that is, $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$. The vertical reaction is still $V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, because the couple has zero resultant. The value $2\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance of the force from $B$, and the answer "for no value" comes from taking the moment of $F$ as counterclockwise.
