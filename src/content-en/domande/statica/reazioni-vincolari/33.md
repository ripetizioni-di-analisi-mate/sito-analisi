A horizontal cantilever is fixed on the left. A counterclockwise $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ couple and a downward $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ force act on it. Where must the force act for the reaction moment to vanish, and what vertical reaction remains?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;P,3.2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="P,0,-1.3,6 kN" coppie="B,1" etichette="5,0.75,18 kN m" quote="A,P,x,0.8" titolo="Unknown lever arm of the force and applied couple"}

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from the support, with $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- At the support, with zero reaction
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from the support, with zero reaction
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the support, with $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

The total external moment about the support must vanish, so $18-6x=0$ gives $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$. A couple has no resultant force, so vertical equilibrium still requires a $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward reaction.
