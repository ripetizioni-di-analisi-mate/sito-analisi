Model the wheelbarrow as the rigid body shown. The wheel contacts the ground at $A$, providing only a vertical reaction, as a roller on a horizontal surface would. The load weighs $1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and acts along a vertical line $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The handles are lifted at $B$, a horizontal distance $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, by a vertical force $F$. Neglect the weight of the wheelbarrow. What value of $F$ holds it in equilibrium?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1.5,0.5" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello" forze="0.5 0.1667,0,-0.6,1.5 kN;B,0,0.6,F" quote="A,0.5 0,0.5 m,0.9;A,1.5 0,1.5 m,1.3" titolo="Wheelbarrow with wheel at $A$, load $1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, and hand force $F$ at $B$"}

- $F = 0.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $F = 1\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $F = 0.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $F = 4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

The wheel reaction acts through $A$, so taking moments about $A$ leaves only $F$ as an unknown. The load’s weight has moment arm $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and acts clockwise. The upward force $F$ has moment arm $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and acts counterclockwise. Thus
$$
1.5\,F - 1.5 \cdot 0.5 = 0 ,
$$
giving $F = 0.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. Vertical force equilibrium then gives the wheel reaction $V_A = 1.5 - 0.5 = 1\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $0.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ divides the weight equally between the wheel and the hands. The value $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ interchanges the moment arms of the weight and $F$.
