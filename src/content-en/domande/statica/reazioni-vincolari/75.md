A T-shaped frame consists of a column $AE$ of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at its base $A$ and a crossbeam $CD$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ welded at its midpoint to the top $E$ of the column. At the left end $C$, a vertical force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. At the right end $D$, a vertical force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ also acts downward. What is the reaction moment $M_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;E,0,4;C,-2,4;D,2,4" aste="A-E;C-D" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="C,0,-1.4,10 kN;D,0,-1,4 kN" quote="C,E,2 m,-1.6;E,D,2 m,-1.6;A,E,4 m,-0.9" titolo="T-shaped structure fixed at its base"}

- $M_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_A = 28\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_A = 0$

## Explanation

The fixed-support forces act at $A$, so taking moments about $A$ leaves only $M_A$ as an unknown. The downward force at $C$ acts $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of the vertical through $A$, producing a counterclockwise moment of $+20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The force at $D$ acts $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right and produces a clockwise moment of $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Because the forces are vertical, the column height does not enter the calculation. Thus
$$
M_A + 20 - 8 = 0 ,
$$
giving $M_A = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, or $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise. Treating the two moments as acting in the same direction would give $28\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
