Beam $CB$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, with $CA = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $AB = 4\,\mathrm{m}$. Segment $CA$ overhangs the pin and has its free end at $C$. At $C$, a vertical force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the reaction $V_B$?

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,2,0;B,6,0" aste="C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.2,F" quote="C,A,2 m;A,B,4 m" titolo="Beam with a left overhang loaded at free end $C$"}

- $V_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = \dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $V_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward

## Explanation

Taking moments about $A$ leaves $V_B$ as the only unknown. Force $F$ acts downward at a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $A$ and produces a counterclockwise moment $8 \cdot 2 = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Thus
$$
4\,V_B + 16 = 0
$$
giving $V_B = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. The overhang load tends to rotate the beam about $A$ and lift it at $B$, so the roller must hold it down. Vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = 8 + 4 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
