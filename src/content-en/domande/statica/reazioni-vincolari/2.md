Cantilever $AB$ is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ long, is fixed at $A$, and carries a uniform load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. At the free end $B$, a horizontal force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the left and a couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts clockwise. What is the reaction moment $M_A$ at the fixed support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.3,0.3,q" forze="B,-0.8,0,F" coppie="B,-1,ℳ" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Cantilever under a uniform load, axial force $F$, and clockwise couple $\mathcal{M}$"}

- $M_A = 12.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 32.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 55\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The resultant uniform load is $5 \cdot 3 = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is applied a distance $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The force $F$ acts along the axis of the cantilever, which passes through $A$, so it has no moment about $A$. The clockwise couple enters the equation with the value $-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, whatever the reference point. Taking moments about $A$ gives
$$
M_A - 15 \cdot 1.5 - 10 = 0 ,
$$
Thus $M_A = 32.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is positive and acts counterclockwise. Reversing the sign of the applied couple gives $12.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, while omitting it gives $22.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
