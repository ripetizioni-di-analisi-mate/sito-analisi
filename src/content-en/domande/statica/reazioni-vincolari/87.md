Beam $AB$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long and pinned at $A$. At $B$, a two-force link inclined at $45^\circ$ to the horizontal extends downward and to the left from $B$. The only load is a horizontal force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting to the right at $B$. What is the link reaction $R_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,pendolo,225" forze="B,1.2,0,6 kN" quote="A,B,5 m" titolo="Beam with a pin and an inclined link, loaded along its axis"}

- $R_B = 0$
- $R_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $R_B = 6\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 8.49\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $R_B = 3\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 4.24\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The load acts along the beam axis through $A$ and therefore has zero moment about $A$. Taking moments about $A$ leaves only the vertical component of $R_B$, with moment arm $5\,\mathrm{m}$, giving
$$
5 \cdot \frac{R_B}{\sqrt{2}} = 0 ,
$$
Thus $R_B = 0$ and the link carries no load. Horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$, so the pin carries the entire applied load. The value $6\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN}$ assigns the horizontal force to the link, although the pin balances it alone.
