An L-shaped member consists of a vertical segment $AC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and a horizontal segment $CB$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, welded together at $C$. Its base $A$ is pinned. Its tip $B$ rests on a roller on a surface inclined at $45^\circ$ to the horizontal. The roller reaction $R_B$ points upward and to the left. The horizontal segment carries a distributed load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the horizontal pin reaction $H_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,2;B,4,2" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,315" carichi="C,B,0.4,0.4,q" quote="A,C,2 m,-0.8;C,B,4 m,-1" titolo="L-shaped beam with a roller on a surface inclined at $45^\circ$"}

- $H_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $H_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $H_A = 0$
- $H_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right

## Explanation

The roller reaction has components $c = \dfrac{R_B}{\sqrt{2}}$ to the left and $c$ upward. About $A$, the vertical component acts at a horizontal distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and produces a counterclockwise moment $4\,c$. The leftward horizontal component acts at elevation $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and also produces a counterclockwise moment $2\,c$. The load resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ has moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Taking moments about $A$ gives
$$
4\,c + 2\,c - 12 \cdot 2 = 0 ,
$$
Thus $c = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, and horizontal force equilibrium $H_A - c = 0$ gives $H_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right. The value $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ omits the moment of the horizontal component of $R_B$.
