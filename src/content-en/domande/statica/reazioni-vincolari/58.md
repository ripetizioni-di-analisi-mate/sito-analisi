Beam $AB$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long. At $A$, a parallel-link support with vertical links restrains vertical translation and rotation. At $B$, a roller rests on a horizontal surface. How is the structure classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo;B,carrello" quote="A,B,5 m" titolo="Beam with a vertical parallel-link support at $A$ and a roller at $B$"}

- Statically determinate because there are three scalar restraints
- Statically indeterminate because two restraints prevent vertical translation
- Unstable because no restraint prevents horizontal translation
- Unstable because no restraint prevents rotation

## Explanation

The support with two parallel vertical links provides a vertical force $V_A$ and a couple $M_A$, while the roller on a horizontal surface provides a vertical force $V_B$. None contributes a horizontal force. Horizontal equilibrium thus contains no reaction unknown and requires the applied loads to have zero net horizontal component. The supports cannot balance an arbitrary horizontal force. The structure is **unstable** despite its three scalar restraints: the equilibrium matrix has a zero row and rank less than $3$. The parallel links do restrain rotation.
