Beam $AB$ is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long and has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. It carries a trapezoidal load that increases linearly from $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$ to $5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. What is the reaction $V_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.4,1,q" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with a trapezoidal load varying from $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $V_B = 10.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

Split the load diagram into a rectangle of height $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, with resultant $2 \cdot 6 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and a triangle with maximum intensity $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$, with resultant $\dfrac{3 \cdot 6}{2} = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Taking moments about $A$ gives
$$
6\,V_B - 12 \cdot 3 - 9 \cdot 4 = 0 ,
$$
Thus $V_B = \dfrac{72}{6} = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. Placing the entire resultant $21\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan would give $10.5\,\mathrm{kN}$.
