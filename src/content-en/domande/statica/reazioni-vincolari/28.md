A simply supported beam carries a trapezoidal load increasing from left to right. The vertical reactions are $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $A$ and $21\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$. The full load diagram is mirrored about midspan while the beam stays in place. What is the new difference $V_B-V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.4,1.2,q" titolo="Trapezoidal load before reflection"}

- $+6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-36\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The resultant retains its magnitude and its position is mirrored. Moment equilibrium therefore interchanges the vertical reactions. The new difference is $15-21=-6\,\mathrm{kN}$.
