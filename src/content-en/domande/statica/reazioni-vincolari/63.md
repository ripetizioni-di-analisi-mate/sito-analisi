A horizontal cantilever of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at end $A$. A downward load varies along the beam as a half sine wave, $q(x) = 5\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{3}\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, where $x$ is the distance from $A$ in meters. The load is zero at both ends and reaches $5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at midspan. What is the reaction moment $M_A$?

::grafico{f="(x>=0 && x<=3) ? 5*sin(pi*x/3) : NaN" xmin=-0.3 xmax=3.6 ymin=-0.8 ymax=6.2 nomi="x,q" titolo="Load intensity $q(x) = 5\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{3}$ along the cantilever"}

- $M_A = \dfrac{45}{\pi}\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \approx 14.3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = \dfrac{90}{\pi}\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \approx 28.6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = \dfrac{30}{\pi}\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \approx 9.55\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The resultant of the load is the area below the diagram:
$$
Q = \int_0^3 5\sin\frac{\pi x}{3}\,dx = 5 \cdot \frac{3}{\pi}\Big[-\cos\frac{\pi x}{3}\Big]_0^3 = \frac{30}{\pi}\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
Since $q(3 - x) = q(x)$, the load diagram is symmetric about the midpoint of the cantilever. Its resultant acts $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Taking moments about $A$ leaves only $M_A$ and gives $M_A - \dfrac{30}{\pi} \cdot 1.5 = 0$, hence $M_A = \dfrac{45}{\pi}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise. The value $22.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ corresponds to a uniform load of $5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. The value $\dfrac{90}{\pi}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ places the resultant at the free end.
