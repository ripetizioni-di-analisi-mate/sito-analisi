Straight member $AB$ extends from $A = (0, 0)$ to $B = (3\,\mathrm{m}, 4\,\mathrm{m})$ and has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$. It has a pin at $A$. At $B$, a roller bears on a surface parallel to the member. The reaction $R_B$ is therefore perpendicular to the member and points upward and to the left. The only load is the member's self-weight, $g = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per unit length along the member. What is $R_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,4" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,323.13" carichi="A,B,0.4,0.4,g" quote="A,3 0,3 m,0.7;3 0,B,4 m,0.7" titolo="Inclined member with a roller on a surface parallel to its axis"}

- $R_B = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $R_B = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $R_B = \dfrac{25}{3}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 8.33\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $R_B = 1.8\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The pin reactions pass through $A$, so taking moments about $A$ leaves only $R_B$. The line of action of $R_B$ is perpendicular to $AB$ at point $B$. Its distance from $A$ is therefore the member length, $5\,\mathrm{m}$, and its moment is counterclockwise. The self-weight has a vertical resultant $2 \cdot 5 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ through the midpoint $(1.5\,\mathrm{m}, 2\,\mathrm{m})$, with moment arm $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and clockwise moment. Thus
$$
5\,R_B - 10 \cdot 1.5 = 0 ,
$$
giving $R_B = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ treats $R_B$ as a vertical force with moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $\dfrac{25}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$ includes only the moment of the vertical component of $R_B$. The value $1.8\,\mathrm{kN}$ calculates the load resultant using the horizontal projection of the member.
