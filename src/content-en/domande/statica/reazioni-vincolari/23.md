Beam $AB$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long and pinned at $A$. At $B$, a roller rests on a surface inclined at $45^\circ$ to the horizontal. The roller reaction $R_B$ points upward and to the left. The unknown reactions are $H_A$, $V_A$, and $R_B$. About which point should moments be taken so that $H_A$ is the only unknown in the equation?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,315" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Beam with a pin at $A$ and a roller on a surface inclined at $45^\circ$ at point $B$"}

- About $A$
- About $B$
- About the point $4\,\mathrm{m}$ below $A$
- About the point $4\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$

## Explanation

A reaction has zero moment about any point on its line of action. Reaction $V_A$ acts vertically through $A$. Reaction $R_B$ passes through $B$ at an angle of $45^\circ$, rising toward the left. Its line of action, that of $R_B$, crosses the vertical through $A$ at a point $4\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$, since a horizontal distance of $4\,\mathrm{m}$ corresponds to a rise of $4\,\mathrm{m}$. About this point $V_A$ and $R_B$ have zero moment, while $H_A$ acts horizontally at the elevation of $A$ with moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The only unknown left is $H_A$. Taking moments about $A$ instead isolates $R_B$, and taking moments about $B$ isolates $V_A$.
