Beam $AB$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long and pinned at $A$. At $B$, a roller rests on a surface inclined at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal, with $\tan\alpha = \dfrac{3}{4}$. The reaction $R_B$ is normal to the surface and points upward and to the left. Arrange the unknown reactions in the vector $\mathbf{r} = (H_A, V_A, R_B)$. Write the horizontal force balance, the vertical force balance, and the moment balance about $A$, in that order, as $A\,\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{b}$, with the applied loads in $\mathbf{b}$. Which matrix represents $A$ when lengths are expressed in meters?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,306.87" quote="A,B,5 m" titolo="Beam with a pin at $A$ and a roller on an incline at $B$"}

- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0.6 \\ 0 & 1 & 0.8 \\ 0 & 0 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & -0.8 \\ 0 & 1 & 0.6 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & -0.6 \\ 0 & 1 & 0.8 \\ 0 & 0 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & -0.6 \\ 0 & 1 & 0.8 \\ 0 & 0 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

Since $\tan\alpha = \dfrac{3}{4}$, the direction ratios are $\sin\alpha = 0.6$ and $\cos\alpha = 0.8$. Reaction $R_B$ is inclined at $\alpha$ to the vertical, so its horizontal component is $-0.6\,R_B$, directed left, and its vertical component is $0.8\,R_B$. Horizontal and vertical force equilibrium therefore give rows $(1, 0, -0.6)$ and $(0, 1, 0.8)$. Taking moments about $A$ eliminates the pin reactions. The horizontal component of $R_B$ acts along the beam axis through $A$, while its vertical component has moment arm $5\,\mathrm{m}$. The coefficient of $R_B$ is therefore $0.8 \cdot 5 = 4$. The matrix determinant is $4 \neq 0$, so the beam is statically determinate. The matrix with $5$ in its last row applies the moment arm to the full reaction $R_B$ instead of its vertical component.
