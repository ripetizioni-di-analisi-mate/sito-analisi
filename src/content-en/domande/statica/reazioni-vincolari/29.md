Beam $AB$ is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long and has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. At point $C$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, a vertical force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. To replace $F$ with an equal force at midspan while preserving the support reactions, what couple must be added?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.3,F" quote="A,C,2 m;C,3 0,1 m;3 0,B,3 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under force $F$ at $C$"}

- A clockwise couple of $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- A counterclockwise couple of magnitude $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- A counterclockwise couple of magnitude $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- No couple, because the force has the same magnitude and direction

## Explanation

The reactions stay the same if the replacement load has the same resultant force and the same moment about one point, say $A$. The force at $C$ produces a clockwise moment of $10 \cdot 2 = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about $A$. Moving it to midspan gives a clockwise moment of $10 \cdot 3 = 30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The added couple $\mathcal{M}$ must therefore satisfy $-30 + \mathcal{M} = -20$, which gives $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise. Its magnitude is the force times the distance through which its point of application moves, $10 \cdot 1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. It offsets the extra moment from the longer lever arm.
