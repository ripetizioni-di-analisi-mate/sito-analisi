Beam $AB$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long and has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward and to the right at midspan. Its horizontal and vertical components have magnitudes $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $8\,\mathrm{kN}$, respectively. What is the magnitude of the resultant pin reaction $R_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="2 0,0.75,-1,F" quote="A,2 0,2 m;2 0,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under inclined force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan"}

- $R_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $R_A = 2\sqrt{13}\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $R_A = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $R_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A + 6 = 0$, hence $H_A = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. Taking moments about $A$ gives $4\,V_B - 8 \cdot 2 = 0$, or $V_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, and vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = 8 - 4 = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$. The pin reaction has components $H_A$ and $V_A$, so its magnitude is
$$
R_A = \sqrt{H_A^2 + V_A^2} = \sqrt{36 + 16}\,\mathrm{kN} = 2\sqrt{13}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 7.21\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The value $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ splits force $F$ equally between the supports. Only the pin can carry the horizontal component.
