A gate is modeled as a rigid rectangular plate $3\,\mathrm{m}$ wide and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ tall, attached to a vertical post at two points. The lower support at $A$ is a pin. The upper support at $B$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$, exerts only a horizontal reaction $H_B$. The gate weighs $4\,\mathrm{kN}$, and its weight acts at the center of the plate. What is the reaction $H_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,2;D,3,2;E,3,0" aste="A-B;B-D;D-E;E-A" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;B,carrello,180" forze="1.5 1,0,-1.2,4 kN" quote="A,E,3 m,0.8;E,D,2 m,0.8" titolo="Gate hung from a post, with a hinge at $A$ and a horizontal reaction at $B$"}

- $H_B = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$, directed toward the post
- $H_B = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$, directed away from the post
- $H_B = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$, directed toward the post
- $H_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, directed toward the post

## Explanation

The pin reactions pass through $A$, so taking moments about $A$ leaves only $H_B$ as an unknown. The weight has moment arm $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$, the horizontal distance from the center to the gatepost, and produces a clockwise moment. Reaction $H_B$ acts at the elevation of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and has moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$. If taken to the right, away from the post, it also produces a clockwise moment. Thus
$$
-2\,H_B - 4 \cdot 1.5 = 0 ,
$$
and $H_B = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$. Thus the upper support pulls the gate toward the gatepost with force $3\,\mathrm{kN}$, while the lower hinge pushes it outward with force $H_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by taking the center's elevation, $1\,\mathrm{m}$, as the weight arm, instead of the horizontal distance $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
