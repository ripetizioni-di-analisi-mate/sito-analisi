Beam $AB$ is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long and rests on two rollers on horizontal surfaces, one at $A$ and the other at $B$. Which of the following loads, acting alone, can the support reactions balance?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Beam supported on two rollers"}

- A horizontal force of magnitude $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan
- A force of magnitude $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ inclined at $45^\circ$ at midspan
- A horizontal force of $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a clockwise couple of $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- A clockwise couple of $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The rollers provide only vertical reactions $V_A$ and $V_B$, so horizontal equilibrium requires zero horizontal resultant of the applied loads. An unbalanced horizontal or inclined force cannot be supported, and the structure is unstable. The clockwise couple has no resultant force. Taking moments about $A$ gives $6\,V_B - 12 = 0$, hence $V_B = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. Vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = -2\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. The two reactions form a counterclockwise couple balancing the applied one.
