Square plate $ABCD$ has sides of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and is initially at rest, free to move in its plane. Four forces act on it. At $A$, a vertical force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. At $C$, a vertical force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. At $B$, a horizontal force of $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. At $D$, a horizontal force of $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the left. Which statement about the plate is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,2,2;D,0,2" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-A" forze="A,0,0.8,4 kN;C,0,-0.8,4 kN;B,0.8,0,3 kN;D,-0.8,0,3 kN" quote="A,B,2 m,0.8;B,C,2 m,0.8" titolo="Free square plate under four forces"}

- It is in equilibrium because the resultant force is zero
- The system is out of equilibrium because it is equivalent to a clockwise couple of $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- The system is out of equilibrium because it is equivalent to a clockwise couple of $14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- It is not in equilibrium. The system is equivalent to a counterclockwise couple of magnitude $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The two vertical forces cancel and so do the two horizontal forces, giving zero resultant force. Equilibrium also requires zero resultant moment. Take moments about $A$. The force at $C$ has moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and produces the clockwise moment $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The force at $D$ has moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and produces the counterclockwise moment $+6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The forces at $A$ and $B$ act through $A$. Thus
$$
\sum M_A = -8 + 6 = -2\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
The system is equivalent to a clockwise couple of $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, which tends to rotate the plate. The value $14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counts the two couples as acting in the same direction even though their moments oppose one another.
