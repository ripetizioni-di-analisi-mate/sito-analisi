Beam $AB$ is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long and has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. At a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, a vertical bracket of height $1\,\mathrm{m}$ is welded to the beam. At the top $E$ of the bracket, a force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward and to the right, with a horizontal component of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a vertical component of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the reaction $V_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,2,0,-;E,2,1;B,6,0" aste="A-B;D-E" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="E,0.9,-1.2,F" quote="A,D,2 m,-0.8;D,B,4 m,-0.8;D,E,1 m,-0.6" titolo="Simply supported beam under force $F$ applied to a bracket"}

- $V_B = \dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = \dfrac{5}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = \dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = \dfrac{11}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

The bracket is welded to the beam, so the beam and bracket are one rigid body. About $A$ the vertical component of $F$ has $2\,\mathrm{m}$ arm and the horizontal component has $1\,\mathrm{m}$ arm, the elevation of $E$; both have clockwise moment. Taking moments about $A$ gives
$$
6\,V_B - 8 \cdot 2 - 6 \cdot 1 = 0 ,
$$
Thus $V_B = \dfrac{22}{6} = \dfrac{11}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. The value $\dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$ applies the force at beam level, omitting the bracket height. The value $\dfrac{5}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$ assigns the horizontal component a counterclockwise moment.
