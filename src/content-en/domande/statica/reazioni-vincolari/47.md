The vertical column $AB$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ tall, fixed at its base $A$, and free at the top. A uniform horizontal wind load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts to the right over the full height. What is the reaction moment $M_A$ at the fixed support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q,0" quote="A,B,4 m,-1.2" titolo="Column fixed at its base under uniform wind load $q$"}

- $M_A = 32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The wind load has resultant $q \cdot 4 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting horizontally to the right through the column midpoint, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. It therefore produces a clockwise moment about $A$ with moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$. The fixed-support forces act at $A$. Taking moments about $A$ therefore leaves only the reaction moment $M_A$,
$$
M_A - 8 \cdot 2 = 0 .
$$
giving $M_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise. Placing the resultant at the top of the column instead of at midheight would give $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
