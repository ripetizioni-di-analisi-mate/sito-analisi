Beam $AB$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long and has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A downward force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at midspan. Point $P$ lies on the beam axis, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $A$. Take upward reactions and counterclockwise moments as positive. With moments expressed in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$, which equation gives moment equilibrium about $P$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="2 0,0,-1.2,F" punti="-2,0,P" quote="-2 0,A,2 m;A,2 0,2 m;2 0,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam and a moment reference point $P$ outside the beam"}

- $2\,V_A + 6\,V_B - 24 = 0$
- $-2\,V_A + 6\,V_B - 48 = 0$
- $2\,V_A + 6\,V_B + 48 = 0$
- $2\,V_A + 6\,V_B - 48 = 0$

## Explanation

Moments may be taken about any point in the plane, including a point outside the beam. About $P$ the upward reactions $V_A$ and $V_B$ act to the right of $P$ and produce counterclockwise moments with arms $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $6\,\mathrm{m}$. Force $F$ acts downward at a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $P$ and produces a clockwise moment $12 \cdot 4 = 48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Reaction $H_A$ acts along the beam axis through $P$ and contributes no moment. The equation is $2\,V_A + 6\,V_B - 48 = 0$, satisfied by $V_A = V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The term $-24$ uses the moment arm of $F$ measured from $A$ instead of $P$.
