Gable frame $ACB$ consists of two rafters welded together at the ridge $C$. Its joints are at $A = (0, 0)$, $C = (4\,\mathrm{m}, 3\,\mathrm{m})$, and $B = (8\,\mathrm{m}, 0)$. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. At the ridge $C$, a vertical force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward and a horizontal force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is the reaction $V_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,3;B,8,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.4,10 kN;C,1.2,0,4 kN" quote="A,B,8 m,0.9;4 0,C,3 m,0.6" titolo="Gable frame under two forces at its ridge"}

- $V_B = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 3.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 6.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 7\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

The two sloping roof members are welded, so the frame acts as one rigid body. Take moments about $A$. The vertical force has moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$, the horizontal distance from $C$ to $A$. The horizontal force has moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$, the elevation of $C$. Both moments are clockwise. Thus
$$
8\,V_B - 10 \cdot 4 - 4 \cdot 3 = 0 ,
$$
giving $V_B = \dfrac{52}{8} = 6.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The value $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ neglects the horizontal force. The value $3.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ assigns it a counterclockwise moment, while $7\,\mathrm{kN}$ uses the horizontal distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ as its moment arm.
