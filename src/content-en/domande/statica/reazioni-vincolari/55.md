Beam $AB$ is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long and has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Its only loads are two forces along its axis. At point $C$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, a force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. At point $D$, a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, a force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the left. What is the reaction $H_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,5,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0.6,0,8 kN;D,-0.6,0,8 kN" quote="A,C,2 m,0.8;C,D,3 m,0.8;D,B,1 m,0.8" titolo="Simply supported beam under two opposite axial forces"}

- $H_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $H_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $H_A = 0$
- $H_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right

## Explanation

The roller support only exerts a vertical reaction, so horizontal force balance contains only $H_A$:
$$
H_A + 8 - 8 = 0 ,
$$
Thus $H_A = 0$. The two forces act in opposite directions along the beam axis. Their resultant force and moment about any point are both zero. The moment equations therefore also give $V_A = V_B = 0$. The loads compress segment $CD$ without producing any support reactions. The value $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left considers only the force at $C$.
