Beam $AC$ is pinned at $A$ and supported by a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The overhang $BC$ has length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Segment $AB$ carries a uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. At $C$, a vertical force $F$ acts downward. For what value of $F$ is the reaction at $A$ zero?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" forze="C,0,-1.8,F" quote="A,B,6 m;B,C,2 m" titolo="For the overhanging beam, what value of $F$ makes the reaction at $A$ zero?"}

- $F = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $F = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $F = 36\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium about $B$ contains only $V_A$. The resultant load, $4 \cdot 6 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$, is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$, and the force $F$ is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $B$, so
$$
-6\,V_A + 24 \cdot 3 - 2\,F = 0 ,
\qquad
V_A = 12 - \frac{F}{3} .
$$
The reaction vanishes when $F = 36\,\mathrm{kN}$. Vertical force equilibrium then gives $V_B = 24 + 36 = 60\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ results from measuring the moment arm of $F$ from $A$ instead of $B$.
