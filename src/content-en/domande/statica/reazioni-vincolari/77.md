Beam $AB$ is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long. At $A$, a parallel-link support with vertical links restrains vertical translation and rotation. At $B$, a roller against a vertical wall provides a horizontal reaction. The first $4\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$ carries a uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. The parallel-link support exerts a vertical force $V_A$ and a moment $M_A$. What is the line of action of the single force equivalent to this force and moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo;B,carrello,0" carichi="A,4 0,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,4 0,4 m;4 0,B,2 m" titolo="Beam with a parallel-link support at $A$, a roller against a wall at $B$, and a load on part of the beam"}

- The vertical line a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$
- The vertical line through $A$
- The vertical line through the midpoint of the beam
- The beam axis

## Explanation

The roller reaction is horizontal and all applied loads are vertical, so vertical force equilibrium contains only $V_A$ and gives $V_A = 2 \cdot 4 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. Taking moments about $A$ gives $M_A - 8 \cdot 2 = 0$, hence $M_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise. An upward force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ with counterclockwise moment $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about $A$ acts $\dfrac{16}{8} = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $A$. The resultant reaction of the parallel-link support is therefore vertical through the midpoint of the loaded segment and opposes the load resultant. Indeed, horizontal equilibrium gives $H_B = 0$, so the parallel-link support balances the load by itself.
