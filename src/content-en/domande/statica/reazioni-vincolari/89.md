Beam $AC$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, as shown. The overhang $BC$ has length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. At $C$ a vertical force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. A student obtains $V_B = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, both upward. Which equilibrium equation do these values satisfy despite the error?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,2 m" titolo="Overhanging beam loaded at end $C$"}

- Vertical translation
- Rotation about $B$
- Rotation about $A$
- Rotation about $C$

## Explanation

The correct reactions are $V_B = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, so the student has reversed only $V_A$. Reaction $V_A$ acts through $A$ and disappears from the moment equation about $A$. With the student’s values that equation still gives $4 \cdot 18 - 12 \cdot 6 = 0$ and fails to reveal the error. The other balances contain $V_A$ and fail. Vertical force equilibrium gives $6 + 18 - 12 = 12$, the moment sum about $B$ gives $-4 \cdot 6 - 12 \cdot 2 = -48$, and the moment sum about $C$ gives $-6 \cdot 6 - 2 \cdot 18 = -72$. A check can detect an incorrect reaction only if that reaction appears in the equation.
