A simply supported beam spans $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and carries a uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the first $4\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from the pin at $A$. A student calculates equal reactions, $V_A = V_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$. What error leads to this result?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,4 0,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,4 0,4 m;4 0,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with uniform load over the first $4\,\mathrm{m}$"}

- The resultant was placed at the right end of the loaded segment
- The resultant was placed at the midpoint of the entire beam instead of the midpoint of the loaded segment
- The resultant was calculated as though the load covered the entire span
- The moment of $V_B$ about $A$ was given the wrong sign

## Explanation

The resultant load $2 \cdot 4 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the midpoint of the loaded length, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Taking moments about $A$ gives $6\,V_B - 8 \cdot 2 = 0$, so the correct reactions are $V_B = \dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A = \dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$. The student’s reactions add up to $8\,\mathrm{kN}$, the correct total, but their equal values imply that the resultant was placed at midspan, $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Placing it at the end of the loaded length, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, would give $V_B = \dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$. Extending the load over the whole span would make the reactions sum to $12\,\mathrm{kN}$.
