A rectangular frame, open at the bottom, is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ wide and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ tall. Its bases $A$ and $B$ rest on rollers on horizontal surfaces. A horizontal link at the upper right corner $D$ restrains horizontal motion. The crossbeam carries a distributed load of $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. At the upper left corner $C$, a horizontal force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is the reaction $V_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,3;D,4,3;B,4,0" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello;D,pendolo,0" carichi="C,D,0.4,0.4,q" forze="C,1.2,0,F" quote="A,C,3 m,-1;C,D,4 m,-0.9" titolo="Portal frame on two rollers with a horizontal restraining link at $D$"}

- $V_B = 8.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 0.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $V_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

Both rollers supply vertical reactions, so the link reaction $H_D$ is the only unknown in horizontal equilibrium, giving $H_D = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The link force and applied force $F$ act at the elevation of $3\,\mathrm{m}$, with equal magnitudes and opposite directions. Their moments about $A$ therefore cancel. Taking moments about $A$ gives
$$
4\,V_B - 6 \cdot 3 + 6 \cdot 3 - 8 \cdot 2 = 0 ,
$$
where $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the resultant load acting at the crossbeam midpoint. Thus $V_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. Counting the moment of $8.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ while omitting that of the link reaction would give $F$.
