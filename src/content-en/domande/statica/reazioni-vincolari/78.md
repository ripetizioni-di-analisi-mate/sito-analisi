Beam $AC$ is pinned at $A$ and supported by a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The overhang $BC$ has length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. The only load is a downward force $F$ applied at a distance $x$ from $A$, where $0 \leq x \leq 6\,\mathrm{m}$. For which load positions does the reaction $V_A$ point downward?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="5 0,0,-1.3,F" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,2 m" titolo="Overhanging beam under force $F$ at a variable position"}

- For $0 \leq x < 4\,\mathrm{m}$, with the force between the supports
- For $4\,\mathrm{m} < x \leq 6\,\mathrm{m}$, with the force on the overhang
- For $2\,\mathrm{m} < x \leq 6\,\mathrm{m}$, with the force beyond the midpoint of the span
- For no position, because $V_A$ always acts upward

## Explanation

Both pin reaction components pass through $A$. Taking moments about $A$ gives $4\,V_B - F x = 0$, or $V_B = \dfrac{F x}{4}$. Vertical force equilibrium then gives
$$
V_A = F - \frac{F x}{4} = F\,\frac{4 - x}{4} .
$$
Since $F > 0$, reaction $V_A$ is negative and acts downward if and only if $x > 4\,\mathrm{m}$. The force on the overhang tends to rotate the beam about $B$ and lift it at $A$, so the pin must hold the beam down. For $x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ the applied force acts directly at the roller and $V_A = 0$.
