Roof member $AB$ slopes upward from $A = (0, 0)$ to $B = (8\,\mathrm{m}, 6\,\mathrm{m})$. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Its self-weight is $g = 1.5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per unit length along the member. The snow load is $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per unit of horizontal length. The wind load is $p = 1\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per unit length along the member and acts perpendicular to it, downward and to the right. What is the reaction $V_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,6" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,p,306.87" quote="A,8 0,8 m,-0.6;8 0,B,6 m,-0.6" titolo="Sloping roof member with the wind load $p$ shown"}

- $V_B = 23.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 19.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 21.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 15.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

The member length is $\sqrt{8^2 + 6^2} = 10\,\mathrm{m}$ and its horizontal projection is $8\,\mathrm{m}$. The resultants of the self-weight and snow load are $1.5 \cdot 10 = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $2 \cdot 8 = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$. Both act vertically through the midpoint, a horizontal distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The wind resultant $1 \cdot 10 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts perpendicular to the member at its midpoint, giving a clockwise moment about $A$ with moment arm $5\,\mathrm{m}$, half the member length. Taking moments about $A$ gives
$$
8\,V_B - 15 \cdot 4 - 16 \cdot 4 - 10 \cdot 5 = 0 ,
$$
Thus $V_B = \dfrac{174}{8} = 21.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. Applying the snow load to the actual member length instead of its horizontal projection would give $23.75\,\mathrm{kN}$.
