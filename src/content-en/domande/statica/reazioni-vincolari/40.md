A rigid triangular plate has vertices $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (6\,\mathrm{m}, 0)$, and $C = (6\,\mathrm{m}, 3\,\mathrm{m})$. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. The plate weighs $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, and its weight acts at the centroid of the triangle. At vertex $C$, a horizontal force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is the reaction $V_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,6,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-A" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="4 1,0,-1.2,12 kN;C,1.3,0,6 kN" punti="4,1,G,baricentro" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9;B,C,3 m,-1.1" titolo="Triangular plate with its weight at centroid $G$ and a horizontal force at $C$"}

- $V_B = 11\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

The triangular plate’s centroid is the mean of its vertex coordinates, $G = \left(\dfrac{0 + 6 + 6}{3}, \dfrac{0 + 0 + 3}{3}\right) = (4\,\mathrm{m}, 1\,\mathrm{m})$. Its weight has moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$ about $A$ and produces clockwise moment $12 \cdot 4 = 48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The force at $C$ acts horizontally to the right at elevation $3\,\mathrm{m}$, producing clockwise moment $6 \cdot 3 = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The pin reactions pass through $A$, so taking moments about $A$ leaves only $V_B$,
$$
6\,V_B - 48 - 18 = 0 ,
$$
giving $V_B = 11\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The value $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ places the weight at the midpoint of the base. The value $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ neglects the moment of the horizontal force.
