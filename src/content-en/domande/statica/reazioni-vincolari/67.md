Straight member $AB$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long and fixed at $A$. It slopes upward to the right at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal, with its free end at $B$. At $B$, a vertical force $F = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the reaction moment $M_A$ at the fixed support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3.46,2" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.6" etichette="0.9,0.18,30°" titolo="Member inclined at $30^\circ$ and fixed at $A$"}

- $M_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 10\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \approx 17.3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 10\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \approx 17.3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise

## Explanation

The moment arm of a vertical force about $A$ is the horizontal distance from $A$ to its line of action. Point $B$ lies a horizontal distance $4\cos 30^\circ = 2\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, so force $F$ produces a clockwise moment of $5 \cdot 2\sqrt{3} = 10\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about $A$. Taking moments about $A$ leaves only $M_A$ as an unknown,
$$
M_A - 10\sqrt{3} = 0 ,
$$
giving $M_A = 10\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \approx 17.3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise. The value $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses the member length as the moment arm, while $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses the elevation $4\sin 30^\circ$.
