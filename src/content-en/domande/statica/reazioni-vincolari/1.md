Beam $AB$ in the figure has span $5\,\mathrm{m}$, a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the entire span. A vertical force $F = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward at a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. What is the vertical reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="2 0,0,-1.3,F" quote="A,2 0,2 m;2 0,B,3 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and force $F = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $V_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 19\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 29\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

The uniform load has resultant $Q = 4 \cdot 5 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan, $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$. The line of action of reaction $V_B$ passes through $B$, and reaction $H_A$ acts along the beam axis, which passes through $B$. Thus $V_A$ is the only unknown in the moment balance about $B$. Force $F$ acts at a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$, so
$$
-5\,V_A + 20 \cdot 2.5 + 15 \cdot 3 = 0 ,
$$
and $V_A = \dfrac{95}{5} = 19\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. The value $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ is reaction $V_B$, found from vertical force equilibrium.
