The vertical prop $AB$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ tall and pinned at its base $A$. At its top $B$, a roller against a vertical wall provides a horizontal reaction $H_B$. At a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$, a horizontal force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is the reaction $H_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" forze="0 1,1.2,0,F" quote="A,0 1,1 m,-0.8;0 1,B,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Strut with a pin at its base and a roller against a wall at its top"}

- $H_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $H_B = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $H_B = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $H_B = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left

## Explanation

The pin reactions pass through $A$, so taking moments about $A$ isolates $H_B$. Reaction $H_B$ acts horizontally at the elevation of $4\,\mathrm{m}$, with moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$. If it acts to the right, its moment is clockwise. Force $F$ has moment arm $1\,\mathrm{m}$ and also produces a clockwise moment. Thus
$$
-4\,H_B - 8 \cdot 1 = 0 ,
$$
giving $H_B = -2\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. Horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A = -8 - H_B = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the reaction at the pin, the support closer to the applied force.
