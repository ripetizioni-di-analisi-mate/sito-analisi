A beam of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Only three forces act on it. Two are the support reactions. The third acts at midspan at $45^\circ$ to the horizontal, downward and to the right, and has horizontal and vertical components of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ each. What is the line of action of the resultant pin reaction $\vec{R}_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="3 0,1,-1,F" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,3 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under a midspan force inclined at $45^\circ$"}

- The line through $A$ parallel to the applied force
- The line through $A$ and point $K$, which lies $3\,\mathrm{m}$ below $B$
- The beam axis through $A$ and $B$
- The line through $A$ and point $K$, which lies $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above $B$

## Explanation

Only three forces act on the beam: the applied force, the vertical roller reaction, and the pin reaction. The first two lines of action intersect at $K$. For their moments about that point to balance, $\vec{R}_A$ must also pass through $K$. The applied force acts through the midpoint, $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$, and its line of action drops $1\,\mathrm{m}$ for every meter to the right. It meets the vertical through $B$ at point $K$, which lies $3\,\mathrm{m}$ below $B$. Taking moments about $A$ gives $6\,V_B - 6 \cdot 3 = 0$, or $V_B = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$, so $V_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $H_A = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The line of action of reaction $(-6, 3)$ drops $3\,\mathrm{m}$ for every $6\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right and therefore passes through $K$.
