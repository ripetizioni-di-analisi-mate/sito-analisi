Beam $AB$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long, fixed at $A$, and supported by a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the full span. The beam is statically indeterminate. A deflection analysis gives the roller reaction as $V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. What is the corresponding reaction moment $M_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Beam with a fixed support and an end support under a uniform load"}

- $M_A = 32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

With $V_B$ given, equilibrium determines the three remaining unknowns $H_A$, $V_A$, and $M_A$. The moment balance about $A$ contains $M_A$, the counterclockwise moment of $V_B$ with moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and the clockwise moment of load resultant $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ with moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Thus
$$
M_A + 6 \cdot 4 - 16 \cdot 2 = 0 .
$$
The result is $M_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise, and vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = 16 - 6 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the fixed-end moment without the roller, as for a cantilever. The value $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment of $V_B$ alone.
