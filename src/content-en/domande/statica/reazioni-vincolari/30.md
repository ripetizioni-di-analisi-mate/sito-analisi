The vertical retaining wall $AB$ is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ tall and pinned at its base $A$. Its top $B$ is braced by a strut $BK$ inclined at $45^\circ$ to the horizontal. The strut is pinned at $B$ and at the ground anchor $K$, a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $A$. Water on the left reaches the top of the wall. It exerts a horizontal load to the right that increases linearly from zero at $B$ to $30\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$. What is the axial force in the strut?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,pendolo,315" carichi="A,B,1,0,q,0" punti="3,0,K" tratteggi="0,3 3,0" quote="A,B,3 m,-1.5;A,3 0,3 m,0.9" titolo="Bulkhead under water pressure, with a pin at its base and an inclined strut at its top"}

- $15\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 21.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $15\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 21.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $\dfrac{45\sqrt{2}}{2}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 31.8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression

## Explanation

The water exerts a horizontal resultant $\dfrac{30 \cdot 3}{2} = 45\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, one third of the water depth above the base, or $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. At $B$ the strut exerts a force $N$ along $BK$. If the strut is in compression, its components on the wall are $\dfrac{N}{\sqrt{2}}$ to the left and $\dfrac{N}{\sqrt{2}}$ upward. The pin reaction components pass through $A$, and the vertical component of the strut force also passes through $A$. Taking moments about $A$ gives
$$
3 \cdot \frac{N}{\sqrt{2}} - 45 \cdot 1 = 0 .
$$
The result $N = 15\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 21.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ is positive, confirming that the strut is in compression. The value $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ is only its horizontal component, while $\dfrac{45\sqrt{2}}{2}\,\mathrm{kN}$ places the hydrostatic resultant at midheight.
