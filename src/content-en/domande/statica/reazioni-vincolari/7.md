Cantilever $AB$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long and fixed at $A$. It carries a trapezoidal load that increases linearly from $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$ to $9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at the free end $B$. What is the reaction moment $M_A$ at the fixed support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.3,0.9,q" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Cantilever under a trapezoidal load varying from $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $M_A = 56\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

Split the load diagram into a rectangle of height $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and a triangle whose intensity reaches $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. The rectangular part has a resultant $3 \cdot 4 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and the triangular part has a resultant $\dfrac{6 \cdot 4}{2} = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting $\dfrac{2}{3} \cdot 4 = \dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The support forces act at $A$, so taking moments about $A$ leaves only the reaction moment $M_A$ and gives
$$
M_A - 12 \cdot 2 - 12 \cdot \frac{8}{3} = 0 ,
$$
Thus $M_A = 24 + 32 = 56\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts counterclockwise. Placing the entire resultant $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan would give $48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
