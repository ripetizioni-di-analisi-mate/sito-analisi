A simply supported beam spans $6\,\mathrm{m}$, with a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$. The distributed load changes at midspan. Its intensity is $q_1 = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ from $A$ to midspan and $q_2 = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ from midspan to $B$. What is the reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,3 0,0.4,0.4,q_1;3 0,B,0.8,0.8,q_2" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,3 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under a stepped load"}

- $V_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 10.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

Split the load diagram into two rectangles. The first has resultant $2 \cdot 3 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, or $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$. The second has resultant $4 \cdot 3 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$. Taking moments about $B$ leaves only $V_A$ as an unknown,
$$
-6\,V_A + 6 \cdot 4.5 + 12 \cdot 1.5 = 0 ,
$$
giving $V_A = \dfrac{45}{6} = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $V_B = 18 - 7.5 = 10.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. Placing the total load $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan would give $9\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $10.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the reaction at $B$.
