Beam $AB$ is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long and rests on two rollers on horizontal surfaces, one at $A$ and the other at $B$. At a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, a vertical force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. At a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, a horizontal force $H$ acts. For which values of $H$ is the beam in equilibrium, and what are the corresponding support reactions?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello" forze="2 0,0,-1.5,F;4 0,1.3,0,H" quote="A,2 0,2 m;2 0,4 0,2 m;4 0,B,2 m" titolo="Beam supported on two rollers"}

- Only for $H = 0$, with $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- For every value of $H$, with $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- Only for $H = 0$, with $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- For no value of $H$

## Explanation

The two rollers provide only vertical reactions $V_A$ and $V_B$. With no horizontal support reaction, horizontal force equilibrium requires $H = 0$. The structure is **unstable** and can be in equilibrium only for this value. When $H = 0$, taking moments about $A$ gives $6\,V_B - 12 \cdot 2 = 0$, or $V_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, and vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = 12 - 4 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$. Both reactions act upward.
