At $A$ a beam support provides only a vertical force and a moment. At $B$ a bilateral roller reaction is normal to its guide and inclined at $45^\circ$ to the horizontal. All external loads are vertical. What follows directly from horizontal equilibrium?

- Only the vertical roller component is zero.
- The roller reaction is zero.
- The moment at $A$ is zero.
- Every load is shared equally.

## Explanation

The only horizontal term in equilibrium is $R_B\cos45^\circ$. The cosine is nonzero, so $R_B=0$. The vertical force and moment at $A$ still depend on the applied loads.
