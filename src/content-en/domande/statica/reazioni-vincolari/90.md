Beam $AB$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long. At $A$, a parallel-link support with vertical links restrains vertical translation and rotation. At $B$, a roller rests on a surface inclined at $45^\circ$ to the horizontal. The roller reaction points upward and to the left. A downward force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at midspan. What is the reaction moment $M_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo;B,carrello,315" forze="2 0,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,2 0,2 m;2 0,B,2 m" titolo="Beam with a vertical parallel-link support at $A$ and an inclined roller at $B$"}

- $M_A = 0$
- $M_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_A = 40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The support with two parallel vertical links supplies a vertical force $V_A$ and a couple $M_A$, with no horizontal force. Only the horizontal component of $R_B$ enters horizontal force equilibrium and must therefore vanish. Thus $R_B = 0$ and the roller carries no load. The parallel-link support carries the whole load, with $V_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$. Taking moments about $A$ gives
$$
M_A - 10 \cdot 2 = 0 ,
$$
so $M_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise. The value $0$ treats the inclined roller as though it carried half the load. The value $40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ places the applied force at $B$.
