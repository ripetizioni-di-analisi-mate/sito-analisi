Beam $AB$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long. At $A$, a parallel-link support with vertical links exerts a vertical force $V_A$ and a moment $M_A$. At $B$, a roller against a vertical wall exerts a horizontal force $H_B$. A uniform load $q$ acts over the entire beam. Which equilibrium equation has $V_A$ as its only unknown?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo;B,carrello,0" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,5 m" titolo="Beam with a vertical parallel-link support at $A$ and a roller against a vertical wall at $B$"}

- Rotation about $A$
- Rotation about $B$
- Vertical translation
- Horizontal translation

## Explanation

Vertical force equilibrium contains only vertical force components. Since $M_A$ is a couple and $H_B$ is horizontal, the only unknown in that equation is $V_A$. Taking moments about $A$ isolates $M_A$ because $V_A$ passes through $A$ and $H_B$ acts along the beam axis. Taking moments about $B$ involves both $V_A$ and $M_A$, while horizontal force equilibrium involves only $H_B$.
