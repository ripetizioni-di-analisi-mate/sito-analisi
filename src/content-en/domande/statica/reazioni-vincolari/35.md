Beam $AB$ is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long and has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A secondary beam $CD$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ rests without friction on beam $AB$ at points $C$ and $D$, located $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, respectively. A downward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts on the secondary beam at a distance $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$. What is the reaction $V_B$ on beam $AB$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,2,0.6;D,4,0.6;C0,2,0,-;D0,4,0,-" aste="A-B;C-D" catene="C-C0;D-D0" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="2.5 0.6,0,-1.2,12 kN" quote="A,2 0,2 m,0.9;2 0,4 0,2 m,0.9;4 0,B,2 m,0.9;C,2.5 0.6,0.5 m,-0.5" titolo="Beam $AB$ carrying secondary beam $CD$, which rests on supports at $C$ and $D$"}

- $V_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 7\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

The secondary beam rests on supports at $C$ and $D$. Taking moments about $C$ gives $2\,R_D - 12 \cdot 0.5 = 0$, or $R_D = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$, and vertical force equilibrium gives $R_C = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$. By action and reaction, beam $AB$ receives a downward force at $C$ of $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ and another at $D$ of $3\,\mathrm{kN}$. Taking moments about $A$ gives
$$
6\,V_B - 9 \cdot 2 - 3 \cdot 4 = 0 ,
$$
giving $V_B = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The same result follows by applying the original force $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ directly to beam $AB$ at a distance $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, since the two transmitted forces have the same resultant and moment as the original force. The value $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ divides the force equally between $C$ and $D$, while $7\,\mathrm{kN}$ interchanges the two transmitted forces.
