The L-shaped beam $ACB$ consists of a column $AC$ of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and a crossbeam $CB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, welded together at $C$. It has a pin at its base $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. The crossbeam carries a uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. At midheight of the column, a horizontal force $F = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is the reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,4;B,6,4" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="C,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="0 2,1.4,0,F" quote="A,0 2,2 m,-1;0 2,C,2 m,-1;C,B,6 m,-1.1" titolo="L-shaped beam with a horizontal force at the midpoint of its vertical member"}

- $V_A = 0$
- $V_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

The crossbeam load has a resultant $2 \cdot 6 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting a horizontal distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Force $F$ acts horizontally at elevation $2\,\mathrm{m}$, so its moment about $A$ is clockwise with moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Taking moments about $A$ gives
$$
6\,V_B - 12 \cdot 3 - 9 \cdot 2 = 0 ,
$$
Thus $V_B = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$. Vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = 12 - 9 = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. Using the full column height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ as the moment arm of $F$ would give $V_A = 0$.
