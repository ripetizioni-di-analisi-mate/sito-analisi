Beam $AB$ is $8\,\mathrm{m}$ long and pinned at $A$. At $B$, a roller rests on a surface inclined at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal. The roller reaction points upward and to the left. A uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the entire beam. What is the horizontal pin reaction $H_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,300" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,8 m" titolo="Beam with a roller on a surface inclined at $30^\circ$"}

- $H_A = 0$
- $H_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $H_A = 12\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 20.78\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $H_A = 4\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 6.93\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right

## Explanation

Reaction $R_B$ is perpendicular to the support surface and makes an angle $30^\circ$ with the vertical. Its components are $R_B \cos 30^\circ$ vertically and $R_B \sin 30^\circ$ horizontally to the left. About $A$ the horizontal component produces no moment because it acts along the beam axis. The load resultant $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$, giving
$$
8\,R_B \cos 30^\circ - 24 \cdot 4 = 0 .
$$
It follows that $R_B \cos 30^\circ = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $R_B = \dfrac{24}{\sqrt{3}} = 8\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$. Horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A = R_B \sin 30^\circ = 4\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right. Although every applied load is vertical, the pin provides the horizontal force needed to balance the horizontal component of $R_B$.
