A sign is modeled as a rigid horizontal beam $AB$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Its weight $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at its midpoint. Two cables, $AD$ and $BD$, suspend the sign from a fixed hook $D$ located $2\,\mathrm{m}$ above the midpoint of $AB$. Each cable is therefore inclined at $45^\circ$ to the horizontal. What is the tension in each cable?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;D,2,2" aste="A-B" catene="A-D;B-D" vincoli="D,cerniera,90" forze="2 0,0,-1,6 kN" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,4 2,2 m,0.6" titolo="Sign suspended from a hook by two cables"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $6\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 8.49\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $3\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 4.24\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension

## Explanation

Each cable carries no load between its ends and pulls the beam along its own axis toward $D$, with tension $T_A$ or $T_B$. With only two scalar restraints, the structure is unstable. This load can be balanced because the weight also acts through $D$. All the forces have zero moment about $D$. Horizontal force equilibrium gives $T_A\cos 45^\circ - T_B\cos 45^\circ = 0$, hence $T_A = T_B = T$. Vertical force equilibrium gives
$$
2\,T\sin 45^\circ - 6 = 0 ,
$$
Thus $T = 3\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 4.24\,\mathrm{kN}$, in tension. The value $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ divides the weight equally without resolving the inclined cable forces. The value $6\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN}$ assigns all the weight to one cable.
