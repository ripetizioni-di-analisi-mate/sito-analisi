Beam $CD$ is $8\,\mathrm{m}$ long. It has a pin at $A$, a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from end $C$, and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$. Overhangs $CA$ and $BD$ have lengths $1\,\mathrm{m}$ and $3\,\mathrm{m}$, respectively. The entire beam, from $C$ to $D$, carries a uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,1,0;B,5,0;D,8,0" aste="C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="C,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="C,A,1 m;A,B,4 m;B,D,3 m" titolo="Beam with two overhangs under a uniform load along its entire length"}

- $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 0$

## Explanation

The resultant $Q = 2 \cdot 8 = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the midpoint of the beam, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$ and hence $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from pin $A$. Both pin reaction components pass through $A$. Taking moments about $A$ thus leaves only $V_B$ as an unknown,
$$
4\,V_B - 16 \cdot 3 = 0 ,
$$
giving $V_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. Vertical force equilibrium then gives $V_A = 16 - 12 = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The overhangs have different lengths, so the resultant lies off center between the supports and the reactions differ. The value $0$ uses the moment arm measured from end $C$ instead of the pin, giving $4\,V_B - 16 \cdot 4 = 0$.
