Ramp $AB$ extends from $A = (0, 0)$ to $B = (4\,\mathrm{m}, 3\,\mathrm{m})$. It has a pin at $A$ and, at $B$, a roller against a vertical wall. The roller reaction $H_B$ is horizontal. A downward force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the midpoint of the ramp. What is the horizontal pin reaction $H_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" forze="2 1.5,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.7;A,0 3,3 m,-0.7" titolo="Ramp with a pin at its base and a roller against a vertical wall"}

- $H_A = 0$
- $H_A = \dfrac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 6.67\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $H_A = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $H_A = \dfrac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 6.67\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right

## Explanation

The line of action of $V_A$ is vertical through $A$, and that of $H_B$ is horizontal through $B$. They meet at $D = (0, 3)$, so taking moments about $D$ leaves only $H_A$ as an unknown. About $D$, reaction $H_A$ has moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and force $F$ has moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Hence
$$
3\,H_A - 10 \cdot 2 = 0
$$
giving $H_A = \dfrac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$. Its positive sign means the reaction acts to the right. Horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_B = -H_A$, so the wall pushes the ramp to the left. The value $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ uses the horizontal distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ as the moment arm instead of the height $3\,\mathrm{m}$.
