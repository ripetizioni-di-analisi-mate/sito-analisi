Beam $AB$ is pinned at $A$ and supported at $B$ by a two-force link. The link is pinned at both ends, carries no intermediate loads, and makes an angle of $45^\circ$ with the horizontal. What reaction does the link exert on the beam?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,pendolo,225" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,5 m" titolo="Beam with a pin at $A$ and an inclined two-force link at $B$"}

- A force directed along the link axis
- A vertical force
- A force perpendicular to the link axis
- A force through $B$ in any direction

## Explanation

The link is pinned at both ends and carries no intermediate load. It is therefore in equilibrium under just the two pin forces. These forces must be collinear and act along the line joining the pins. The link restrains motion of $B$ only along its axis and supplies one unknown reaction of magnitude $R_B$ inclined at $45^\circ$. A reaction through $B$ with arbitrary direction would require a pin with two independent reaction components.
