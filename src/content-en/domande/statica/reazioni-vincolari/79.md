Beam $AB$ is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long and has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Which of the following loads, acting alone, produces equal vertical reactions, $V_A = V_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Simply supported beam $AB$"}

- A triangular load increasing from $0$ at $A$ to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$
- A uniform load of intensity $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over half $AM$ only, with $M$ the midpoint
- A symmetric triangular load that is zero at $A$ and $B$ and reaches its maximum at midspan
- A concentrated couple of magnitude $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at midspan

## Explanation

Taking moments about $A$ gives $6\,V_B = Q\,x_Q$, where $Q$ is the load resultant and $x_Q$ is the distance from $A$ to its line of action. Vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A + V_B = Q$. Thus $V_A = V_B = \dfrac{Q}{2}$ if and only if $x_Q = 3\,\mathrm{m}$, meaning that the resultant acts at midspan. The symmetric triangular load satisfies this condition because its diagram has its centroid at midspan. The increasing triangular load has its resultant $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, while the load on half $AM$ has its resultant at $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$. A couple has zero resultant force and gives $V_A = -V_B$, with magnitude $V_B = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$.
