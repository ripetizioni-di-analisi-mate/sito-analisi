Beam $AB$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long. At $A$, a parallel-link support with vertical links restrains vertical translation and rotation. At $B$, a roller bears against a vertical wall. A force acts at a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, downward and to the right, with a horizontal component of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a vertical component of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the reaction moment $M_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo;B,carrello,0" forze="3 0,0.75,-1,F" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,2 m" titolo="Beam with a parallel-link support at $A$ and a roller against a vertical wall at $B$"}

- $M_A = 40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The support with two parallel vertical links exerts a vertical force $V_A$ and a couple $M_A$. The roller against the vertical wall exerts a horizontal reaction $H_B$ along the beam axis. About $A$ the horizontal component of the applied force also acts along the beam and has zero moment arm, while the vertical component of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ has moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Taking moments about $A$ gives
$$
M_A - 8 \cdot 3 = 0 ,
$$
Thus $M_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts counterclockwise. The value $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses the lever arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$ with the full magnitude $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ of the inclined force.
