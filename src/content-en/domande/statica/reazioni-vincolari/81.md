Beam $AB$ is $8\,\mathrm{m}$ long, fixed at $A$, and supported by a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A vertical two-force link supports the beam at midspan $C$. How many unknown support reactions are there, and what is the degree of static indeterminacy?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;C,pendolo,270;B,carrello" quote="A,C,4 m,1.3;C,B,4 m,1.3" titolo="Beam fixed at $A$ with a link at $C$ and a roller at $B$"}

- $4$ unknowns, statically indeterminate to degree $1$
- $5$ unknowns, statically indeterminate to degree $2$
- $5$ unknowns, statically indeterminate to degree $3$
- $3$ unknowns, statically determinate

## Explanation

The fixed support supplies three reactions $H_A$, $V_A$, and $M_A$. The vertical link and the roller each provide one vertical reaction. This gives $s = 5$ unknowns, compared with $3$ equilibrium equations for a planar rigid body. The fixed support alone restrains all rigid-body motion, so the matrix has rank $3$ and the solutions depend on $5 - 3 = 2$ free parameters. The beam is therefore **statically indeterminate to degree $2$**. Equilibrium alone cannot determine two reactions, for example those of the link and roller.
