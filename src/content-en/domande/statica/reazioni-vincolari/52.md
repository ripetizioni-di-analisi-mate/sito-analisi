Beam $AB$ is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long and supported by three two-force links. At $A$, a link slopes downward to the left at $45^\circ$. At $B$, a second link slopes downward to the right at $45^\circ$. The third link is vertical at $C$, a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. At a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, a vertical force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the reaction $R_C$ of the vertical link?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,pendolo,225;B,pendolo,315;C,pendolo,270" forze="4 0,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,4 0,4 m,-1.3;4 0,C,1 m,-1.3;C,B,1 m,-1.3" titolo="Beam supported by three two-force links"}

- $R_C = 9.6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $R_C = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $R_C = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $R_C = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

The link reactions at $A$ and $B$ act along lines $y = x$ and $y = 6 - x$, which meet at $K = (3\,\mathrm{m}, 3\,\mathrm{m})$. Taking moments about $K$ therefore isolates $R_C$. Reaction $R_C$ acts vertically through $x = 5\,\mathrm{m}$ with moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and, if upward, produces a counterclockwise moment. Force $F$ acts vertically through $x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ with moment arm $1\,\mathrm{m}$ and produces a clockwise moment. Thus
$$
2\,R_C - 12 \cdot 1 = 0 ,
$$
giving $R_C = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The value $9.6\,\mathrm{kN}$ comes from taking moments about $A$ as though there were a pin at $A$ and omitting the link at $B$.
