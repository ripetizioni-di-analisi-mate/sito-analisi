A balcony slab $AB$ of length $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed to a wall at $A$. A railing $BE$ of height $1\,\mathrm{m}$ is welded to the free end. The slab carries a uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. At the top $E$ of the railing, a horizontal force of $1\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts outward, to the right. What is the reaction moment $M_A$ at the fixed support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1.5,0;E,1.5,1" aste="A-B;B-E" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.25,0.25,q" forze="E,0.5,0,1 kN" quote="A,B,1.5 m,0.5;B,E,1 m,0.5" titolo="Cantilever balcony with a parapet"}

- $M_A = 4.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 3.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 5.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The slab and parapet are welded together and form one rigid body fixed at $A$. Taking moments about $A$ leaves only $M_A$ as an unknown. The load resultant $4 \cdot 1.5 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts $0.75\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and produces a clockwise moment. The horizontal thrust acts to the right at elevation $1\,\mathrm{m}$ above the slab axis, with moment arm $1\,\mathrm{m}$. Its moment is also clockwise. Thus
$$
M_A - 6 \cdot 0.75 - 1 \cdot 1 = 0 ,
$$
giving $M_A = 5.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise. The value $4.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ neglects the horizontal thrust on the parapet, while $3.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ assigns it a counterclockwise moment.
