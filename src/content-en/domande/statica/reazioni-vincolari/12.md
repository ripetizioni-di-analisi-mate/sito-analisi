A portal frame has a roller on the left and a pin on the right, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. Its vertical loads are held fixed. Adding a rightward $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ force increases the vertical reaction at the right foot by $6\,\mathrm{kN}$. At what height above the feet does the force act?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,2.7,-;D,4,2.7,-;B,4,0" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera" forze="C,1.2,0,8 kN" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;A,C,h,-0.7" titolo="Frame and added horizontal force"}

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The vertical reaction changes are opposite because the added force is horizontal. They form a couple of $6\cdot4=24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. This balances the force moment $8h$, giving $h=3\,\mathrm{m}$.
