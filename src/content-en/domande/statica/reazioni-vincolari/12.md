Portal frame $ACDB$ has two columns, $AC$ and $BD$, each $5\,\mathrm{m}$ tall. Crossbeam $CD$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and is welded to both columns. The supports are a roller on a horizontal surface at $A$ and a pin at $B$. The crossbeam carries a uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. At joint $C$, a horizontal force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is the roller reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,5;D,4,5;B,4,0" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera" carichi="C,D,0.5,0.5,q" forze="C,1.3,0,F" quote="A,C,5 m,-1.1;C,D,4 m,-1.1" titolo="Portal frame with a roller at $A$ and a pin at $B$"}

- $V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward

## Explanation

Both pin reaction components pass through $B$, so taking moments about $B$ leaves only $V_A$ as an unknown. About $B$, reaction $V_A$ has moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and the load resultant $3 \cdot 4 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ has moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Force $F$ acts horizontally at elevation $5\,\mathrm{m}$, so its moment arm is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and its moment is clockwise. The equation is
$$
-4\,V_A + 12 \cdot 2 - 8 \cdot 5 = 0 ,
$$
which gives $V_A = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. The horizontal force produces a larger moment than the distributed load, so the roller must hold the frame down at $A$. Omitting force $F$ would give $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
