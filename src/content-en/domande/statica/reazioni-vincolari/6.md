Beam $AC$ is pinned at $A$ and supported by a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The overhang $BC$ has length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Segment $AB$ carries a triangular load that increases linearly from $0$ at $A$ to $q_0 = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. At the free end $C$, a vertical force $F = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0,0.8,q_0" forze="C,0,-1.6,F" quote="A,B,6 m;B,C,2 m" titolo="Overhanging beam with a triangular load on the span"}

- $V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

The resultant of the triangular load is $\dfrac{6 \cdot 6}{2} = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$. It acts at the centroid of the load diagram, one third of the base length from the end where the intensity is $q_0$, or $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$. Force $F$ acts $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $B$. Taking moments about $B$ leaves only $V_A$ as an unknown and gives
$$
-6\,V_A + 18 \cdot 2 - 9 \cdot 2 = 0 ,
$$
Thus $V_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. Placing the resultant at midspan would give $6\,\mathrm{kN}$.
