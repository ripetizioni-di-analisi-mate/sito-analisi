Beam $AB$ is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long and has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. At a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, a vertical force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. A student obtains $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, both upward. Using these reactions, what is the sum $\sum M_B$ of the moments about $B$, taking counterclockwise as positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="2 0,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,2 0,2 m;2 0,B,4 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

- $\sum M_B = 0$
- $\sum M_B = -24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $\sum M_B = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $\sum M_B = 72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

About $B$, reaction $V_A$ acts upward a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$ and has a clockwise moment. Force $F$ acts downward a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$ and has a counterclockwise moment. Reactions $V_B$ and $H_A$ act through $B$ or along the beam axis and therefore produce no moment. Thus
$$
\sum M_B = -6 \cdot 4 + 12 \cdot 4 = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The sum is nonzero, so the proposed reactions fail the check. The student has interchanged them. Using the correct values $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ gives $-6 \cdot 8 + 12 \cdot 4 = 0$.
