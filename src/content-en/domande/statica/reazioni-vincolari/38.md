An L-shaped cantilever consists of a column $AC$ of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at its base $A$ and a horizontal arm $CB$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ welded to the column at $C$. At the free end $B$, a force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts along the line from $B$ toward $A$. What is the reaction moment $M_A$ at the fixed support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,3;B,4,3" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="B,-1,-0.75,F" quote="A,C,3 m,-0.9;C,B,4 m,-0.8" titolo="L-shaped cantilever fixed at $A$, with force $F$ directed from $B$ toward $A$"}

- $M_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 0$
- $M_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $M_A = 48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

Force $F$ acts along line $AB$, which passes through $A$, so the moment of $F$ about $A$ is zero. Reactions $H_A$ and $V_A$ also pass through $A$. Taking moments about $A$ therefore leaves only $M_A$ and gives $M_A = 0$. Resolving $F$ into components gives $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. The vertical component, with moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$, produces clockwise moment $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The horizontal component, with moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$, produces counterclockwise moment $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. These moments cancel. The value $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise includes only the contribution of the vertical component to the required reaction moment, while $48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ adds the two component moments as though they acted in the same direction.
