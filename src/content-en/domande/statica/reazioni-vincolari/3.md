Beam $AC$ is pinned at $A$ and supported by a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The overhang $BC$ has length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Segment $AB$ carries a uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. At the free end $C$, a vertical force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward and a couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts clockwise. What is the reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" forze="C,0,-1.5,F" coppie="C,-1,ℳ" quote="A,B,5 m;B,C,2 m" titolo="Overhanging beam with a distributed load on the span and a force and couple at the free end"}

- $V_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 13\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 11\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 19\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

The span load has a resultant of $6 \cdot 5 = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$. Taking moments about $B$ leaves $V_A$ as the only unknown. Force $F$ acts $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $B$ and produces a clockwise moment. The applied couple contributes $-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, giving
$$
-5\,V_A + 30 \cdot 2.5 - 10 \cdot 2 - 10 = 0 ,
$$
Thus $V_A = \dfrac{45}{5} = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. The force and couple at $C$ tend to rotate the beam about $B$ and lift end $A$, reducing the reaction at $A$. Reversing the sign of the couple would give $13\,\mathrm{kN}$.
