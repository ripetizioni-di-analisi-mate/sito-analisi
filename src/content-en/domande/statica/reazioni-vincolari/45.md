Consider the following four support arrangements for a horizontal beam $AB$. Which arrangement makes the beam statically determinate?

- A pin at $A$ and a roller against a vertical wall at $B$
- Rollers on horizontal surfaces at $A$ and $B$
- A pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$
- A fixed support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$

## Explanation

A pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$ provide the three reactions $H_A$, $V_A$, and $V_B$. Equilibrium determines them for any loading. Horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A$, taking moments about $A$ gives $V_B$, and vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A$. A roller against a vertical wall at $B$ would instead provide a horizontal reaction along the beam axis through $A$. All three reactions would then pass through $A$, leaving no unknown reaction in the moment equation about $A$ and making the beam unstable. Two rollers on horizontal surfaces provide only vertical reactions and leave horizontal translation unrestrained. A fixed support and a roller provide four unknown reactions, making the beam statically indeterminate.
