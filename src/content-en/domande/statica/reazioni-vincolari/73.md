Beam $AB$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long and has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A uniform axial load $p = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts to the right along the entire beam. What is the reaction $H_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="0.9 0.25,0.5,0,;1.9 0.25,0.5,0,;2.9 0.25,0.5,0,;3.9 0.25,0.5,0,;4.9 0.25,0.5,0," etichette="2.5,0.55,p" quote="A,B,5 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under uniform axial load $p$"}

- $H_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $H_A = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $H_A = 0$
- $H_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right

## Explanation

The resultant axial load $p \cdot 5 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts horizontally to the right along the beam axis. A roller on a horizontal surface supplies only a vertical reaction. Horizontal equilibrium therefore contains just $H_A$ and gives $H_A + 10 = 0$. Thus $H_A = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. Since the load acts through both $A$ and $B$, neither support carries a vertical reaction. The value $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ splits the axial load between the supports as though the roller also restrained horizontal motion.
