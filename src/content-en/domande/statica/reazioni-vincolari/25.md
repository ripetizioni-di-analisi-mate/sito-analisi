Beam $AB$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long and has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Its only load is a counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ applied at a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. What is the reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="2 0,1,ℳ" quote="A,2 0,2 m;2 0,B,3 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$"}

- $V_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $V_A = 0$
- $V_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

A couple has zero resultant force and the same moment about every point. Taking moments about $A$ gives $5\,V_B + 15 = 0$, hence $V_B = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = -V_B = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The reactions form a clockwise couple with arm $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and moment $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, balancing the applied couple. The value $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ treats the couple as a force of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.
