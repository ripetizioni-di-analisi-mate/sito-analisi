Beam $AB$ is supported at $A$ by a parallel-link support with horizontal links and at $B$ by a roller on a horizontal surface. What reactions does the parallel-link support exert on the beam?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,5 m" titolo="Beam with a horizontal parallel-link support at $A$ and a roller at $B$"}

- A vertical force and a couple
- A horizontal force and a vertical force
- A horizontal force and a couple
- A horizontal force, a vertical force, and a couple

## Explanation

The support with two parallel horizontal links prevents horizontal movement of $A$ and rotation of the beam, while allowing vertical movement. A support supplies a force component in each direction of restrained translation and a couple if it restrains rotation. The horizontal force $H_A$ and couple $M_A$ at $A$ are the two unknowns at this support. Together with the roller reaction $V_B$, there are three unknowns for three equilibrium equations.
