Beam $ACB$ consists of a horizontal segment $AC$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and an inclined segment $CB$ welded to it at $C$. Point $B$ lies $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above $C$. The beam has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. At $C$, a vertical force $F_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Segment $CB$ carries a vertical load $g = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per unit length measured along the member. At $B$, a horizontal force $F_2 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the left. What is the reaction $V_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,8,3" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="C,B,0.4,0.4,g" forze="C,0,-1.4,F_1;B,-1.2,0,F_2" quote="A,C,4 m;C,8 0,4 m;8 0,B,3 m,0.7" titolo="Bent beam with a horizontal segment and an inclined segment"}

- $V_B = 15.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 9.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

Member $CB$ has length $\sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = 5\,\mathrm{m}$. The load $g$ therefore has a vertical resultant $2 \cdot 5 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting at the midpoint of $CB$, a horizontal distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Force $F_2$ acts horizontally to the left at elevation $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Its moment about $A$ is counterclockwise with moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Taking moments about $A$ gives
$$
8\,V_B - 12 \cdot 4 - 10 \cdot 6 + 6 \cdot 3 = 0 ,
$$
Thus $V_B = \dfrac{90}{8} = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. Applying $g$ to the horizontal projection of $CB$, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, would give $9.75\,\mathrm{kN}$.
