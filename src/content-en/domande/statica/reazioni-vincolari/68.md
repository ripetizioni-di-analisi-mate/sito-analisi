Frame $ACDB$ consists of a column $AC$ of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at its base $A$, a crossbeam $CD$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and a column $DB$ of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ with its free end $B$ at ground level. The members are welded together at $C$ and $D$. At $B$, a horizontal force of $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is the reaction moment $M_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,3;D,4,3;B,4,0" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="B,1.2,0,5 kN" quote="A,C,3 m,-1;C,D,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Frame fixed at $A$ with free end $B$"}

- $M_A = 0$
- $M_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 35\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The frame is a single rigid body fixed at $A$. Taking moments about $A$ leaves only $M_A$ as an unknown. The applied force is horizontal and acts at the elevation of $B$, the same as $A$. Its line of action passes through $A$ and its moment arm about $A$ is zero. Taking moments about $A$ therefore gives $M_A = 0$, while horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A = -5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The shape of the frame between $A$ and $B$ does not affect the moment, which depends only on the force’s line of action. The value $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses the column height as the moment arm, and $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses the crossbeam length.
