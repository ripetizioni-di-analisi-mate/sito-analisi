A plane frame is fixed at $A$. Its free end $B$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $A$ at the same elevation. A $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ force can rotate at $B$. At which angles measured from the positive horizontal is the reaction moment $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise, with $0^\circ\leq\theta\leq180^\circ$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;P,0,2,-;Q,4,2,-;B,4,0" aste="A-P;P-Q;Q-B" vincoli="A,incastro,270" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Position of the free end relative to the clamp"}

- $30^\circ,\ 150^\circ$
- $60^\circ,\ 120^\circ$
- $0^\circ,\ 180^\circ$
- $45^\circ,\ 135^\circ$

## Explanation

The reaction moment balances the moment of the force. With counterclockwise positive, $M_A=-4\cdot5\sin\theta$. Setting $M_A=-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ gives $\sin\theta=\dfrac12$, which has two solutions in the stated interval.
