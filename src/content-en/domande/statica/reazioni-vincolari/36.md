Beam $AB$ is $8\,\mathrm{m}$ long and rests on three rollers on horizontal surfaces, one at $A$, one at midspan $C$, and one at $B$. How is the structure classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;C,carrello;B,carrello" quote="A,C,4 m;C,B,4 m" titolo="Beam resting on three rollers"}

- Unstable, with the reactions under vertical loads depending on one parameter
- Statically determinate because there are three scalar restraints
- Unstable, with uniquely determined reactions under vertical loads
- Statically indeterminate to degree $1$ and in equilibrium under every load

## Explanation

All three reactions $V_A$, $V_C$, and $V_B$ are vertical. The horizontal force equation therefore has no unknowns, and its row in the equilibrium matrix is zero. The rank is less than $3$ and the structure is **unstable** because the supports cannot balance a horizontal force. For vertical loads, vertical force equilibrium and one moment equation remain for three unknowns. Their rank is $2$, so the reactions exist but depend on $3 - 2 = 1$ free parameter. Three scalar restraints make a planar structure statically determinate only when the equilibrium matrix has rank $3$.
