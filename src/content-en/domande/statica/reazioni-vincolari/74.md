Arch $AB$ is a single rigid semicircular member with center $O = (2\,\mathrm{m}, 0)$, radius $2\,\mathrm{m}$, and endpoints $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (4\,\mathrm{m}, 0)$. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. At point $P$ on the arch, where radius $OP$ makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with radius $OA$, a vertical force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. What is the reaction $V_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;P1,0.27,1,-;P,1,1.73;C,2,2,-;P3,3,1.73,-;P4,3.73,1,-;B,4,0" aste="A-P1;P1-P;P-C;C-P3;P3-P4;P4-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="P,0,-1.2,F" punti="2,0,O" tratteggi="2,0 0,0;2,0 1,1.73" etichette="1.55,0.3,60°" quote="A,B,4 m,0.7" titolo="Semicircular arch under a vertical force at point $P$"}

- $V_B = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 2\pi\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 6.28\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 3\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 5.20\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $V_B = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

The arch is a single rigid body. The three equilibrium equations determine the reactions, and its curvature matters only in locating the forces. Point $P$ has coordinates $x_P = 2 - 2\cos 60^\circ = 1\,\mathrm{m}$ and $y_P = 2\sin 60^\circ = \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{m}$. The applied force is vertical, so its moment arm about $A$ is the horizontal distance $x_P = 1\,\mathrm{m}$. The pin reactions pass through $A$, and taking moments about $A$ gives
$$
4\,V_B - 12 \cdot 1 = 0 ,
$$
giving $V_B = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. Vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $2\pi\,\mathrm{kN}$ uses the length of arc $AP$, namely $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$, as the moment arm, while $3\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$ uses the elevation $y_P$.
