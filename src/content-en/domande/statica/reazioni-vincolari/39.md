Curved member $AB$ follows a quarter circle of radius $2\,\mathrm{m}$ centered at $O = (2\,\mathrm{m}, 0)$. The member is vertical at its fixed end $A = (0, 0)$ and curves to its free end $B = (2\,\mathrm{m}, 2\,\mathrm{m})$. At $B$, a vertical force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward and a horizontal force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. What is the reaction moment $M_A$ at the fixed support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;P1,0.27,1,-;P2,1,1.73,-;B,2,2" aste="A-P1;P1-P2;P2-B" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="B,0,-1.2,6 kN;B,1.2,0,4 kN" punti="2,0,O" tratteggi="2,0 0,0;2,0 2,2" quote="A,2 0,2 m,0.6" titolo="Quarter-circular member fixed at $A$, with two forces at its free end"}

- $M_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $M_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The moment of a force about $A$ depends on its line of action, so the curved shape of the member does not enter the moment balance about $A$. For the vertical force at $B$ the moment arm is the horizontal distance from $B$ to $A$, or $2\,\mathrm{m}$, giving clockwise moment $6 \cdot 2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The horizontal force acts to the right at elevation $2\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$, giving moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and clockwise moment $4 \cdot 2 = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Taking moments about $A$ therefore gives
$$
M_A - 12 - 8 = 0 ,
$$
giving $M_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise. Assigning opposite signs to the two applied moments would give $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
