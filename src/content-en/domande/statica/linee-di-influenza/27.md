To trace with Müller–Breslau theorem the influence line for the bending moment at section $S$ of a simply supported beam of span $8\,\mathrm{m}$, with $S$ to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the hinge $A$, a hinge is inserted in $S$ and the relative rotation $\varphi_1 - \varphi_2 = 1$ is imposed. What is the rotation $\varphi_1$ of segment $AS$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The segment $AS$ rotates around $A$ of $\varphi_1$ and the segment $SB$ rotates around $B$ of $\varphi_2$. The two segment have the same displacement at $S$ and the relative rotation is one, so they must equal together
$$
\begin{cases}
2\,\varphi_1 = -6\,\varphi_2 \\
\varphi_1 - \varphi_2 = 1
\end{cases}
$$
From the first equation $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{\varphi_1}{3}$, and substituting in the second one you get $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\varphi_1 = 1$, therefore $\varphi_1 = \dfrac{3}{4}$ and $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{1}{4}$. The displacement of $S$ is $2 \cdot \dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$, equal to the $\dfrac{ab}{L}$ vertex of the moment's influence lines.
