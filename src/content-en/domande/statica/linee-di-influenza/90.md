In the beam shown the load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ extends from the hinge $A$ to the free end $C$. Using the area under its influence line, what is the bending moment over roller $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam under uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The section of $B$ is the extreme of the overhang, so by the theorem on the section on one overhang the influence line for the moment is zero on $AB$ and equals $-(\xi - 5)$ on $BC$, up to $-2\,\mathrm{m}$ in $C$. Its area is $\mathcal{A} = -\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = -2\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Therefore $M_B = 6 \cdot (-2) = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension. The load on the span does not contribute, and the value $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by applying the resultant of the overhang, $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, at the end $C$ instead of in the middle of the overhang.
