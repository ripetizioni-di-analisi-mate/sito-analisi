In the Gerber beam shown the $AC$ supporting segment has a pin at $A$, the roller support at $B$ and the overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$; suspended beam $CD$ is connected at internal hinge $C$ and supported by a roller at $D$. Which statement about the influence line for the bending moment at section $C$ is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0;D,11,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,D,4 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with internal hinge $C$"}

- It is zero only when the load is on the supporting span
- It is zero for every load position
- It reaches its minimum $-2\,\mathrm{m}$ with the load at $C$
- It is a triangle with its peak at $C$

## Explanation

The internal hinge does not transmit moment between the two bodies it connects. Therefore the bending moment at section $C$ is zero for any load, and its influence line is identically zero. The same result occurs with Müller–Breslau theorem: the release for the moment at $C$ is a hinge, which already exists, so no mechanism is born and the displacements are all zero. The ordinate $-2\,\mathrm{m}$ with the load at $C$ belongs to the current influence line in $B$, above the roller support.
