A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$. It is considered the section $S$ immediately to the right of $A$, at a distance from the small support at will. For $0 < \xi \le 6\,\mathrm{m}$, what expression describes the influence line for shear at $S$?

- $\dfrac{6 - \xi}{6}$
- $-\dfrac{\xi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{\xi}{6}$
- $1$

## Explanation

For the distance $a$ section from $A$ the shearing influence line is $-\dfrac{\xi}{6}$ for $\xi < a$ and $\dfrac{6 - \xi}{6}$ for $\xi > a$. At the $a$ to zero tendency the left branch is reduced to one point, and each $\xi > 0$ position of the load falls on the right branch. Therefore the line is $\dfrac{6 - \xi}{6}$ and coincides with the influence lines of $V_A$. The same result follows from the balance of the segment to the left of $S$: only $V_A$ acts on it, and since on the segment to the left the positive shear acts downward you have $T = V_A$.
