In the Gerber beam shown, supporting beam $AC$ has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$; suspended beam $CD$, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, is connected at $C$ and supported by a roller at $D$. Both the $Y_C$ vertical force and the suspended segment transmits to the supporting segment in the hinge $C$, positive downward. What is the influence-line ordinate for $Y_C$ when the unit load is a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" forze="7 0,0,-2,1" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,7 0,1 m,0.9;7 0,D,3 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with a unit load on the suspended beam, a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from hinge $C$"}

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $1$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The load acts on the suspended segment, therefore the equilibrium of $CD$ is written. On it act the load, the reaction of the roller support at $D$ and the force that the supporting exerts in $C$, equal to $Y_C$ and directed upward by the principle of action and reaction. The equilibrium at moment equilibrium about $D$ gives $Y_C \cdot 4 - 1 \cdot 3 = 0$, then $Y_C = \dfrac{3}{4}$. With the load on the supporting beam, the suspended beam is unloaded and $Y_C = 0$. The influence line is therefore zero over $AC$ and rises linearly from $0$ under $D$ to $1$ under $C$. The value $\dfrac{1}{4}$ is the reaction of the roller support at $D$, and $1$ is the ordinate with the load in the hinge.
