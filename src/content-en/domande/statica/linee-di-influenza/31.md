In the Gerber beam shown, supporting beam $AC$ has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$; suspended beam $CD$, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, is connected at $C$ and supported by a roller at $D$. What is the influence-line ordinate for $V_B$ when the unit load is a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" forze="8 0,0,-2,1" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,8 0,2 m,0.9;8 0,D,2 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with a unit load on suspended beam $CD$"}

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

On the supporting beam the influence line for $V_B$ is $\dfrac{\xi}{4}$, whose value at $C$ is $\dfrac{6}{4} = \dfrac{3}{2}$. For the Gerber beam influence lines theorem, on the suspended segment the line is the segment that joins this ordinate with zero under the roller support at $D$. The load is in the middle of $CD$, so the ordinate equals $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{3}{2} = \dfrac{3}{4}$. With equilibrium, the beam $CD$ transmits to the supporting in $C$ an $\dfrac{1}{2}$ downward force, and moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $V_B \cdot 4 = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 6$, i.e. $V_B = \dfrac{3}{4}$. The value $2$ is obtained by extending $\dfrac{\xi}{4}$ onto the suspended segment as if it were the same body.
