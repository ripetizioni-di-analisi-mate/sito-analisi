The cantilever shown, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at $A$ and free at the end $B$. What is the influence-line ordinate for the bending moment at section $S$, a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, when the unit load is a distance $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,1,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="2.5 0,0,-0.9,1" quote="A,S,1 m,0.7;S,2.5 0,1.5 m,0.7;2.5 0,B,0.5 m,0.7" titolo="Cantilever showing section $S$ and the unit load"}

- $-2.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-0.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-1.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The segment $SB$ is free and carries the load, so its balance is written. The load is $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $S$, and the balance at moment equilibrium about $S$ gives $M + 1 \cdot 1.5 = 0$, then $M = -1.5\,\mathrm{m}$. The negative sign indicates that the upper fibers are in tension, as in each section of a cantilever loaded downward. The value $-2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ measures the arm from fixed support instead of the section, and $-0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ measures the distance of the load from the free end.
