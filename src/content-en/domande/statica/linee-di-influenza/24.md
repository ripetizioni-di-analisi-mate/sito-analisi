Over one interval the influence line for the bending moment at $S$ is straight, with ordinates $-1\,\mathrm{m}$ at $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $-2\,\mathrm{m}$ at $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$. A vertical moving load of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ travels $0.4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right within that interval. What is the signed change in moment at $S$?

- $+1.6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-3.2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-1.6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The influence-line slope is $\dfrac{-2-(-1)}{6-4}=-0.5$. The load movement changes the ordinate by $-0.5\cdot0.4=-0.2\,\mathrm{m}$. The moment changes by $8(-0.2)=-1.6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
