The portal frame shown has columns $AB$ and $DC$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ high, and the beam $BC$, $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long, with rigid joints; there is a pin at $A$ and a roller sliding on a horizontal plane at $D$. A vertical unit load, directed downwards, travels along the beam. What is the ordinate of the influence line for the bending moment at the midspan $S$ of the beam, with the load at $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;S,3,4;C,6,4;D,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="S,0,-1.2,1" quote="B,C,6 m,-1.6;A,B,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Portal frame with the unit load on the beam"}

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The load is vertical and the roller at $D$ gives a vertical reaction, so horizontal equilibrium gives $H_A = 0$ for every position of the load. With the load at $S$ symmetry gives $V_A = V_D = \dfrac{1}{2}$, upwards. The part of the frame to the left of $S$ comprises the column $AB$ and half of the beam, and the only force acting on it is $V_A$, whose line of action is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $S$; therefore $M_S = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 3 = 1.5\,\mathrm{m}$, with the lower fibres in tension. The influence line in the beam thus coincides with that of the simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, because the columns receive no horizontal forces. The value $3\,\mathrm{m}$ ignores that the load is shared between the two supports, the value $2\,\mathrm{m}$ takes as lever arm of $V_A$ the column height instead of half the span, and $0$ is the moment at the joint $B$, where the reaction $V_A$ has zero lever arm.
