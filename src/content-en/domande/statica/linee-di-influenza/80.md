The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Which statement about the sum $\eta_{V_A}(\xi) + \eta_{V_B}(\xi)$ of the influence lines for the two vertical reactions is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9;B,C,3 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$, and overhang $BC$"}

- It equals $1$ only when the load lies between $A$ and $B$
- It equals $\dfrac{8}{5}$ when the load is at $C$
- It equals $1$ for all load positions, including the overhang
- It equals $\dfrac{\xi}{5}$

## Explanation

The vertical forces acting on the beam are the unit load and the reactions $V_A$ and $V_B$, so the balance at the vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A + V_B = 1$ for each $\xi$ position of the load. Therefore $\eta_{V_A}(\xi) + \eta_{V_B}(\xi) = 1$ along the entire beam, including overhang. With the load $C$ you have $\eta_{V_A} = \dfrac{5 - 8}{5} = -\dfrac{3}{5}$ and $\eta_{V_B} = \dfrac{8}{5}$, the sum of which is $1$. The value $\dfrac{8}{5}$ is the only ordinate $V_B$ in $C$, which exceeds $1$ because $V_A$ is negative, and $\dfrac{\xi}{5}$ is the influence lines of $V_B$.
