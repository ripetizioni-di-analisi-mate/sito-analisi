On a simply supported beam of span $8\,\mathrm{m}$, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, it acts at $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ a force $F = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ facing downward and inclined by $30^\circ$ on the horizontal. Using the influence line, what is the bending moment at section $S$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="4 0,1.56,-0.9,F" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,4 0,2 m,0.9;4 0,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam with force $F$ at angle $30^\circ$ to the horizontal"}

- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $10\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The influence lines refers to a vertical load, therefore the force is broken down. The vertical component equals $20 \sin 30^\circ = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward. The horizontal component acts along the axis of the beam and is balanced by the hinge $A$, so it does not give moment at $S$. The point of application is to the right of $S$, where $\eta_M = \dfrac{a}{L}\,(L - \xi) = \dfrac{2}{8} \cdot 4 = 1\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore $M_S = 10 \cdot 1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $10\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained with the horizontal component instead of the vertical one, and $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ using the $\dfrac{ab}{L} = \dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ vertex instead of the ordinate one at the point of application.
