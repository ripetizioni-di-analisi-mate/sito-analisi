In the beam shown the constraint in $B$ is an inclined two-force link of $45^\circ$ on the horizontal instead of a vertical roller support. How does this choice affect the influence line for the bending moment at $S$, and what is the ordinate at its vertex?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,3,0;B,9,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,pendolo,225" quote="A,S,3 m,0.9;S,B,6 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with a pin at $A$, an inclined link at $B$, and section $S$"}

- No effect. The peak ordinate is $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- Multiply the ordinates by $\sqrt{2}$. The peak ordinate is $2\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- Divide the ordinates by $\sqrt{2}$. The peak ordinate is $\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- No effect. The peak ordinate is $1\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The horizontal components of the reactions act along the axis of the beam, so they do not appear in the rotation equations around points of the axis. Therefore the equilibrium at moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $V_B = \dfrac{\xi}{9}$, as in the simply supported beam. The moment at $S$ is also derived from moment equilibrium of a segment around $S$, to which only the load and vertical components of the reactions contribute. The influence lines therefore coincides with that of the beam with vertical roller support, with the $\dfrac{ab}{L} = \dfrac{3 \cdot 6}{9} = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ vertex below $S$. The $\sqrt{2}$ factor appears if the entire reaction of the two-force link $R_B = \sqrt{2}\,V_B$ is used instead of its vertical component, and the $1\,\mathrm{m}$ vertex is obtained with the formula $\dfrac{ab}{L}$ halved.
