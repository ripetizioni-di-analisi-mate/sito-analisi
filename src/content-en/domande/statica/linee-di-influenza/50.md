The beam shown, of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$, has in $A$ a parallel-link support with the horizontal links and in $B$ a roller support. To trace with Müller–Breslau theorem the reaction moment $M_A$ influence lines, positive counterclockwise, the parallel-link support is replaced with a two-force horizontal link. About which point does the beam rotate, and what is $\eta_{M_A}$ when the unit load is a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,180;B,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with a parallel-link support at $A$ and a roller at $B$"}

- About $A$, with $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- About $B$, with $-3\,\mathrm{m}$
- About $B$, with $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- About $B$, with $-1\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The two-force horizontal link in $A$ only prevents horizontal displacement, so the center of rotation of the beam lies on the horizontal line for $A$. The roller support at $B$ prevents vertical shifting, so the center also lies on the vertical through $B$, and therefore coincides with $B$. The conjugate displacement of $M_A$ is the rotation of the beam, which is equal to $1$ counterclockwise; then the vertical displacement of the coordinate $\xi$ point is $v(\xi) = 1 \cdot (\xi - 4)$. For Müller–Breslau theorem $\eta_{M_A}(1) = 1 - 4 = -3\,\mathrm{m}$, so the reaction moment is clockwise moment. Moment equilibrium about $A$, with $V_B = 1$, confirms the value: $M_A + 1 \cdot 4 - 1 \cdot 1 = 0$.
