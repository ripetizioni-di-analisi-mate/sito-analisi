In the Gerber beam shown the uniform load $q = 10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ occupies only the suspended segment $CD$. What reaction develops at roller $B$ of the supporting beam?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,6,0;D,9,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" carichi="C,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9;B,C,1 m,0.9;C,D,3 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with uniform load $q = 10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the suspended beam"}

- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward

## Explanation

On the supporting beam the influence line for $V_B$ is $\dfrac{\xi}{5}$, whose value at $C$ is $\dfrac{6}{5}$. For the Gerber beam influence lines theorem, on the suspended segment the line is the segment ranging from $\dfrac{6}{5}$ in $C$ to $0$ in $D$, so its area is $\mathcal{A} = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 3 \cdot \dfrac{6}{5} = \dfrac{9}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore $V_B = 10 \cdot \dfrac{9}{5} = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward. By equilibrium, the suspended beam transmits half the load, $15\,\mathrm{kN}$, at $C$. Taking moments about $A$ for the supporting beam gives $V_B \cdot 5 = 15 \cdot 6$, the same result. The value $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ is achieved by neglecting that the force at $C$ has a greater moment arm than that of the roller support at $B$.
