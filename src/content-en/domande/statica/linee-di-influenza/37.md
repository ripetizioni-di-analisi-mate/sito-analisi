On the simply supported beam shown, of span $5\,\mathrm{m}$, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, an $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ counterclockwise couple of $A$ acts. What is reaction $V_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="2 0,1,ℳ" quote="A,2 0,2 m,0.9;2 0,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam under counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M}$"}

- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward

## Explanation

$V_B$'s influence line is $\dfrac{\xi}{5}$, with $\dfrac{1}{5}$ slope at each point. A counterclockwise couple contributes $-\mathcal{M}\,\eta'$, then $V_B = -10 \cdot \dfrac{1}{5} = -2\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ down. Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives the same value, because $V_B \cdot 5 + 10 = 0$. The result does not depend on the position of the couple. The $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ values are obtained by multiplying the couple by the ordinate $\dfrac{2}{5}$ and $\dfrac{3}{5}$ instead of the slope.
