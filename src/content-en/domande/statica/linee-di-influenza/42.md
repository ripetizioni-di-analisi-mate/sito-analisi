A simply supported beam of span $8\,\mathrm{m}$, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, can carry a uniform $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load of variable length. What is the maximum shear force at section $S$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $27\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The influence line for shear in $S$ is negative between $A$ and $S$, where it is $-\dfrac{\xi}{8}$, and positive between $S$ and $B$, where it is $\dfrac{8 - \xi}{8}$ and starts from $\dfrac{3}{4}$. The maximum is with the load on the section $SB$ only, with positive area
$$
\mathcal{A}^+ = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot \frac{3}{4} = \frac{9}{4}\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Therefore $T_{S,\max} = 6 \cdot \dfrac{9}{4} = 13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. With the load on all the span you add the negative area $-\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$, and the shear down to $12\,\mathrm{kN}$.
