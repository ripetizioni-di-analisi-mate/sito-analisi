The straight, inclined beam $AB$ shown is pinned at $A$ and supported at $B$ by a roller on a horizontal surface. The horizontal projection of $AB$ is $8\,\mathrm{m}$ and the elevation difference is $6\,\mathrm{m}$, giving a beam length of $10\,\mathrm{m}$. Section $S$ is a distance $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, measured along the beam. A vertical unit load travels along the beam. What is the influence-line ordinate for bending moment at $S$ when the load is a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, measured along the beam?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,1.5;B,8,6" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="4 3,0,-1.8,1" quote="A,S,2.5 m,-0.7;S,4 3,2.5 m,-0.7;A,8 0,8 m,1;8 0,B,6 m,1" titolo="Inclined beam with a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$, and a vertical unit load"}

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Roller support reactions and load are vertical, so the balance at horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A = 0$. The arms of the vertical forces are horizontal distances: the load has $4\,\mathrm{m}$ coordinate, because $5 \cdot \dfrac{8}{10} = 4$, and the section $S$ has $2\,\mathrm{m}$ coordinate. The equilibrium at moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $V_A \cdot 8 - 1 \cdot 4 = 0$, then $V_A = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The load lies to the right of $S$, so on the segment $AS$ only $V_A$ acts, and moment equilibrium about $S$ gives $M_S = V_A \cdot 2 = 1\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $\dfrac{5}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by taking as an arm the distance $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ measured along the axis, $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ is the reaction $V_A$ without the arm, and $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ is the $\dfrac{ab}{L}$ vertex of the line, which is with the load at $S$.
