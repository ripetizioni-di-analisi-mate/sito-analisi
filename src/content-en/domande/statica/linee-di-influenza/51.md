In the Gerber beam shown the $AC$ supporting segment has a pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$; suspended beam $CD$ is connected at $C$ and supported by a roller at $D$. At which points does the influence line for $V_A$ have a change in slope?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,D,3 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with supporting beam $AC$ and suspended beam $CD$"}

- At $B$ and $C$
- Only at $B$
- Only at $C$
- Nowhere

## Explanation

By the theorem on the influence lines of statically determinate beams, the line is linear in each interval in which the load remains on the same body. Between $A$ and $C$ the load is always on the supporting segment, so $\eta_{V_A}(\xi) = \dfrac{5 - \xi}{5}$ is a single segment, even under the roller support at $B$, and in $C$ it is $-\dfrac{2}{5}$. On the suspended segment the line is the segment ranging from $-\dfrac{2}{5}$ in $C$ to $0$ in $D$, with $\dfrac{2}{15}$ slope, different from the $-\dfrac{1}{5}$ slope of the previous section. Therefore the only corner is the internal hinge $C$.
