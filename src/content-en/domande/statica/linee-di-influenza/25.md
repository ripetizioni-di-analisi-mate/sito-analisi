An $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long cantilever is fixed at $A$, left, and free at the right end; section $S$ is at $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. What is the shear force at $S$ when the unit load is a distance $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

- $1$
- $-1$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The segment to the right of $S$ is free and, with the load between $A$ and $S$, carries no force. The balance of this segment then gives $T = 0$ and $M = 0$ in $S$. The value $1$ is with the load to the right of $S$, because then the balance at the vertical translation of the free segment gives $T - 1 = 0$. The $S$ shear line is therefore $0$ between fixed support and $S$ and $1$ between $S$ and the free end.
