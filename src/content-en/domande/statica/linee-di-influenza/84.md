The beam shown has a pin at $A$, the roller support at $B$ and the overhang $BC$; section $S$ is in the span and section $D$ is on the overhang. Which of the following influence lines has a change in slope at an interior point of overhang $BC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,6,0;D,7,0;C,9,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,B,4 m,0.9;B,D,1 m,0.9;D,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam showing section $S$ in the span and section $D$ on the overhang"}

- The influence line for $V_A$
- The influence line for bending moment at $S$
- The influence line for $V_B$
- The influence line for bending moment at $D$

## Explanation

Overhang and span form one body, so by the theorem on the influence lines of statically determinate beams the lines of $V_A$ and $V_B$ are a single segment from $A$ to $C$. The moment line in $S$ has the corner below $S$, which is in the span, and its right branch continues in a straight line over the whole overhang. For the overhang section theorem, the moment $D$ line is $0$ for $\xi < 7\,\mathrm{m}$ and $-(\xi - 7)$ for $\xi > 7\,\mathrm{m}$. The two slopes, $0$ and $-1$, are different, so this line has a corner in $D$, inside the overhang.
