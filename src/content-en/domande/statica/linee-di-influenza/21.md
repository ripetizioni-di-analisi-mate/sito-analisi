A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, has the $S$ to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ section from $A$. The right branch of the moment's influence lines in $S$ extends to the vertical by $A$. What is the ordinate at that point?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The right branch is $\eta_M(\xi) = \dfrac{a}{L}\,(L - \xi)$, with $a = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $L = 6\,\mathrm{m}$. For $\xi = 0$ it equals $\dfrac{a}{L} \cdot L = a = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. On this property is based the graphic construction of the line. $a$ is reported on the vertical through $A$ and $b = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ on the vertical through $B$, each point is joined with the opposite support, and the two segments meet on the vertical through $S$ in the $\dfrac{ab}{L} = \dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ vertex.
