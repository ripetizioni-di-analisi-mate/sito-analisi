The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $8\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the influence-line ordinate for the bending moment at section $S$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, when the unit load is at $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,8,0;C,10,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-2,1" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,B,6 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam showing section $S$ in the span and a unit load at $C$"}

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The load on the overhang is located on the same rigid body as the load in span, so the right branch $\eta_M(\xi) = \dfrac{a}{L}\,(L - \xi) = \dfrac{1}{4}\,(8 - \xi)$ also applies to $\xi > 8\,\mathrm{m}$. In $C$ ($\xi = 10\,\mathrm{m}$) the ordinate is $\dfrac{1}{4} \cdot (-2) = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$. The same result follows from balance. Moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $V_A = \dfrac{8 - 10}{8} = -\dfrac{1}{4}$, and the segment $AS$ gives $M = V_A \cdot 2 = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $0$ neglects the effect of the load on the overhang, which lifts the beam into $A$ and puts the upper fibers in tension into $S$.
