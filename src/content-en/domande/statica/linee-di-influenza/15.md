On a simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ the influence line for the bending moment at midspan is drawn, with the unit load adimensional. In what units are its ordinates expressed?

- $\mathrm{m}$
- $\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $\mathrm{kN}$
- They are dimensionless

## Explanation

A moment is the product of a force for a moment arm. The unit load is non-dimensional, so the moment it produces has the dimensions of only the moment arm, that is, of a length. For this beam the vertex of the line, below the midspan, equals $\dfrac{L}{4} = 1.5\,\mathrm{m}$. Multiplying an ordinate for a real load at $\mathrm{kN}$ gives you a moment at $\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The influence lines of reactions and cuts have instead ordinate that they are pure numbers.
