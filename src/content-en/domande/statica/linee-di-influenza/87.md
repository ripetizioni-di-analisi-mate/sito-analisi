A simply supported beam of span $8\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$. The influence lines of the bending moment are considered in the different $S$ sections of the beam. Which one has the greatest ordinate at $\xi = 3\,\mathrm{m}$?

- The one for the midspan section
- The one for the section $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$
- The one for the section $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$
- They all have the same ordinate

## Explanation

For the distance $a$ section from $A$, the ordinate in $\xi = 3$ is $\dfrac{a}{8} \cdot (8 - 3) = \dfrac{5}{8}\,a$ if $a \le 3$ and $\dfrac{8 - a}{8} \cdot 3$ if $a \ge 3$. The first value grows with $a$ and the second decreases with $a$, so the maximum is for $a = 3\,\mathrm{m}$, where it equals $\dfrac{15}{8}\,\mathrm{m}$. At midspan the ordinate is $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ and in the section $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ it is $\dfrac{9}{8}\,\mathrm{m}$. The largest ordinate therefore belongs to the section where the load is located.
