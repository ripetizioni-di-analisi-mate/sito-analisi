On the simply supported beam shown, of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, the $P_1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $1\,\mathrm{m}$ force from the hinge $A$ and the force $P_2 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan act, both downward. What is the bending moment at the midspan section $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="1 0,0,-0.9,P_1;S,0,-1.6,P_2" quote="A,1 0,1 m,0.9;1 0,S,2 m,0.9;S,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam under two forces"}

- $45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $35\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The influence line of the moment at midspan is the triangle with the branches $\dfrac{\xi}{2}$ and $\dfrac{6 - \xi}{2}$ and the vertex $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$. The ordinate under forces are $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ for $P_1$ and $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ for $P_2$, so
$$
M_S = 10 \cdot \frac{1}{2} + 20 \cdot \frac{3}{2} = 5 + 30 = 35\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
With balance you have $V_A = 10 \cdot \dfrac{5}{6} + 20 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{55}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the segment $AS$ gives $M_S = \dfrac{55}{3} \cdot 3 - 10 \cdot 2 = 35\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses the vertex ordinate for both forces.
