A simply supported beam of span $10\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$. What is the maximum influence-line ordinate for the bending moment at section $S$, a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

With $a = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $b = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ the current influence line in $S$ is the triangle
$$
\eta_M(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{3}{5}\,\xi & \text{if } 0 \le \xi \le 4 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{2}{5}\,(10 - \xi) & \text{if } 4 \le \xi \le 10
\end{cases}
$$
The vertex lies below the section and equals $\dfrac{ab}{L} = \dfrac{4 \cdot 6}{10} = 2.4\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ is $\dfrac{L}{4}$, i.e. the vertex for the section at midspan. The $1.6\,\mathrm{m}$ and $3.6\,\mathrm{m}$ values are obtained with $\dfrac{a^2}{L}$ and $\dfrac{b^2}{L}$.
