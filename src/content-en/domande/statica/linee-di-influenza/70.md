On which part of the Gerber beam shown does a downward force tend to lift the beam off pin $A$, producing a downward reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,D,4 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with an overhanging supporting beam and a suspended beam"}

- Only from $B$ to $C$
- Only from $C$ to $D$
- From $B$ to $D$
- On no segment

## Explanation

On the supporting segment $V_A$'s influence line is $\dfrac{4 - \xi}{4}$, positive between $A$ and $B$ and negative on the $BC$ overhang, where it reaches $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ in $C$. For the Gerber beam influence lines theorem, on the suspended segment the line is the segment that goes from $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ in $C$ to $0$ in $D$, so it is negative on all $CD$ except in $D$. Therefore a load at any point between $B$ and $D$ produces $V_A < 0$, i.e. a reaction down. The load on the suspended segment comes to the supporting as a downward force at $C$, which lies on the overhang.
