A simply supported beam of span $5\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$. What is the ordinate of the influence line for reaction $V_A$ when the unit load is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

With the load at $\xi$, taking moments about $B$ gives $V_A \cdot 5 - 1 \cdot (5 - \xi) = 0$. Hence
$$
\eta_{V_A}(\xi) = \frac{5 - \xi}{5} .
$$
At $\xi = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ the ordinate is $\dfrac{3}{5}$. The value $\dfrac{2}{5}$ is the ordinate of the influence line for $\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \dfrac{\xi}{5}$ at the same point. The two ordinates add to $1$, as required by vertical force equilibrium.
