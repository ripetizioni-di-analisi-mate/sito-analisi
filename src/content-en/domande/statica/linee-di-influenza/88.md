An influence line is drawn for a support reaction on the supporting part of a Gerber beam. As a unit vertical load approaches the hinge connecting the supporting and suspended spans, the drawing gives ordinate $0.6$ from one side and $0.2$ from the other. Which check detects the error?

- In the two limits the same load acts at the same joint.
- Every reaction ordinate must vanish at every hinge.
- Every influence line has a unit jump at every hinge.
- The two values need only add to one.

## Explanation

Moving the load to the joint from either side gives the same external loading. In a statically determinate Gerber beam the reaction must therefore have the same limiting value. The slope can change across the hinge.
