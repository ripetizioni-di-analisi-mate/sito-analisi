The Gerber beam shown consists of three bodies: the beam $AC_1$, with the roller support at $A$; the $C_1C_2$ supporting segment, with the pin at $B$ and the roller support at $D$; the beam $C_2E$, with roller support at $E$. The internal hinges are $C_1$ and $C_2$. What is the influence-line ordinate for $V_D$ when the unit load is at the midpoint of $C_2E$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C_1,2,0;B,3,0;D,7,0;C_2,8,0;E,10,0" aste="A-C_1;C_1-C_2;C_2-E" cerniere="C_1;C_2" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera;D,carrello;E,carrello" forze="9 0,0,-1.5,1" quote="A,C_1,2 m,0.9;C_1,B,1 m,0.9;B,D,4 m,0.9;D,C_2,1 m,0.9;C_2,E,2 m,0.9" titolo="Three-body Gerber beam with a unit load on beam $C_2E$"}

- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The load at midspan of $C_2E$ weighs on the suspended segment $C_2E$, which transmits to the supporting in $C_2$ an $\dfrac{1}{2}$ downward force. On the supporting this force acts at $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from the hinge $B$, while the roller support $D$ is distant from $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$. Moment equilibrium of the supporting around $B$ gives $V_D \cdot 4 - \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 5 = 0$, then $V_D = \dfrac{5}{8}$. To the same result he brings the theorem on the influence lines of the Gerber beams, because the line equals $\dfrac{5}{4}$ in $C_2$ and drops linearly to $0$ in $E$. The value $\dfrac{5}{4}$ is the ordinate at $C_2$, and $\dfrac{3}{2}$ is obtained by extending the line of the supporting on the beam $C_2E$.
