The figure shows the influence lines of a beam size with the pin at $A$, the roller support at $B$ $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and the overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$: the line equals $0$ under $A$, $1$ under $B$ and $\dfrac{4}{3}$ in $C$. Which quantity does it represent?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,6,0,(1/6)*s;6,0,8,0,1+(1/6)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for a response quantity of the overhanging beam"}

- Reaction $V_A$
- The bending moment at $B$
- Reaction $V_B$
- The shear immediately to the right of $B$

## Explanation

The line is a single segment from $A$ to $C$, of equation $\dfrac{\xi}{6}$, with no jumps or corners, so magnitude is a reaction. With the load above $B$ the line equals $1$, i.e. all the load goes to the $B$ support, and with the load above $A$ it equals $0$: this is therefore the $V_B$, which with the load at $C$ equals $\dfrac{8}{6} = \dfrac{4}{3}$. The line $V_A$ drops from $1$ under $A$ to $0$ under $B$. The current line in $B$ is zero on $AB$ and negative on the overhang, and that of the shear right away of $B$ is zero on $AB$ and equals $1$ on the overhang.
