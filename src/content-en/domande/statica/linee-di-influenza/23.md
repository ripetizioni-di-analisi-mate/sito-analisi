Müller–Breslau theorem traces the influence line for the shear at section $S$ of a cantilever $AB$, fixed at $A$ on the left and free in $B$. Which instantaneous motion of the mechanism is used?

- Segment $AS$ remains stationary and segment $SB$ translates upward by $1$
- Segment $SB$ remains stationary and segment $AS$ translates downward by $1$
- Segment $AS$ remains stationary and segment $SB$ rotates about $S$ with $\varphi = 1$
- The two segments translate by $\dfrac{1}{2}$ in opposite directions

## Explanation

For shear, a parallel-link support is inserted in $S$ with pin-ended links parallel to the axis, which leaves only the relative vertical sliding of the two segments free and prevents its relative rotation. The segment $AS$ is fixed at $A$, so it stays still. The segment $SB$ is free, so it only moves by a vertical translation. The conjugate displacement is $v_d - v_s = 1$ with $v_s = 0$, so $SB$ moves $1$ upward. The influence line is therefore $0$ with the load between $A$ and $S$ and $1$ with the load between $S$ and $B$, as the theorem on the section of a cantilever gives.
