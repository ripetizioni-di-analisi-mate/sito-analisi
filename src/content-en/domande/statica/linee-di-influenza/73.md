The only action on the beam shown is the $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise couple, applied to the right of section $S$. What bending moment does it produce at $S$, as determined from the slope of the influence line?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="4 0,1,ℳ" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,4 0,2 m,0.9;4 0,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam with counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M}$ to the right of section $S$"}

- $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The couple acts to the right of $S$, where the influence line of the moment at $S$ is the $\dfrac{a}{L}\,(L - \xi) = \dfrac{1}{3}\,(6 - \xi)$ branch, of $-\dfrac{1}{3}$ slope. By the theorem on the value of a magnitude with the influence lines, a counterclockwise couple contributes $-\mathcal{M}\,\eta_M'$, then $M_S = -12 \cdot \left(-\dfrac{1}{3}\right) = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension. The balance at moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $V_A \cdot 6 = 12$, i.e. $V_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, and the balance of the segment $AS$ gives $M_S = 2 \cdot 2 = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ again. The value $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained using the $\dfrac{2}{3}$ slope of the left branch, which does not concern the position of the couple.
