The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the influence-line ordinate for the bending moment at section $D$, a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$, when the unit load is at $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;D,6,0;C,7,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9;B,D,1 m,0.9;D,C,1 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam showing section $D$"}

- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The segment $DC$, to the right of the section, is free and carries only the load, so its balance is written. The load at $C$ is $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $D$, and the balance at moment equilibrium about $D$ gives $M + 1 \cdot 1 = 0$, then $M = -1\,\mathrm{m}$. The negative sign indicates that the upper fibers are in tension, as in each section of an overhang loaded down. The value $-2\,\mathrm{m}$ uses the distance of the load from $B$, which would give the moment in the section on the support.
