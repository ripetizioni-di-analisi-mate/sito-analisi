The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $8\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. A moving force $P = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a uniform load $q = 10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ of variable length can act. What is the minimum bending moment at the midspan section $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,4,0;B,8,0;C,10,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,S,4 m,0.9;S,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam showing midspan section $S$"}

- $-40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-100\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-50\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The moment influence lines in $S$ equals $\dfrac{\xi}{2}$ between $A$ and $S$ and $\dfrac{8 - \xi}{2}$ between $S$ and $C$, so it is negative only on the overhang, where it drops to $-1\,\mathrm{m}$ in $C$. The minimum value is therefore with the force at $C$ and with the uniform load on the overhang only. The negative area is the triangle $-\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot 1 = -1\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so
$$
M_{S,\min} = 40 \cdot (-1) + 10 \cdot (-1) = -50\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The value $-60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by calculating the area on the overhang as a rectangle of $1\,\mathrm{m}$ height instead of a triangle.
