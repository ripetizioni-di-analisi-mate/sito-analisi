An $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long cantilever is fixed at $A$, left, and free at the right end; the reaction moment $M_A$ is positive counterclockwise. A uniform $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load along $2\,\mathrm{m}$ passes through the cantilever. What is the maximum value of $M_A$?

- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The influence line for the reaction moment is $\eta_{M_A}(\xi) = \xi$, increasing over the whole cantilever, so the ordinate at the ends of the load are never equal. With the load on the segment $[z, z + 2]$, the value $M_A(z) = 5 \displaystyle\int_z^{z + 2} \xi\,d\xi$ grows with $z$, so the maximum is with the load juxtaposed to the free end, on the $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to $4\,\mathrm{m}$ segment:
$$
M_{A,\max} = 5 \int_2^4 \xi\,d\xi = 5 \cdot \frac{16 - 4}{2} = 30\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
$$
counterclockwise. The value $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ corresponds to the load juxtaposed with fixed support, and $40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ to the product of the resultant $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ for the entire length of the cantilever.
