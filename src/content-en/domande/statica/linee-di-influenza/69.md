The section $S$ is located in the middle of the fixed Gerber beam cantilever shown, and the unit load acts at midspan $CB$, which rests on the roller support at $B$. At this load position, what is the influence-line ordinate for shear at $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,1,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="4 0,0,-1.5,1" quote="A,S,1 m,0.9;S,C,1 m,0.9;C,4 0,2 m,0.9;4 0,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam showing the supporting cantilever, section $S$, and a unit load"}

- $1$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The load is at the midpoint of suspended beam $CB$, so the roller reaction is $V_B = \dfrac{1}{2}$. It is considered the part of the structure to the right of $S$, formed by the section $SC$ of the cantilever and the suspended segment: on it act the unit load, the reaction $V_B$ and the shear at $S$. On the right segment the positive shear acts upward, so the balance at the vertical force equilibrium gives $T + \dfrac{1}{2} - 1 = 0$, i.e. $\eta_T = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The value $1$ is the ordinate with the load between $S$ and $C$, and is obtained here by neglecting the share of the load that the suspended segment unloaded on the roller support at $B$.
