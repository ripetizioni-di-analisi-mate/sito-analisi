A load of $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ covers the entire beam shown, including the $BC$ overhang. Using the signed areas under the influence line, what is the shear force at section $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$, overhang $BC$, and load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over its entire length"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The $S$ shear influence line is $-\dfrac{\xi}{6}$ between $A$ and $S$ and $\dfrac{6 - \xi}{6}$ from $S$ to $C$. The marked area equals $-\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{3} = -\dfrac{1}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ on the $AS$, $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 4 \cdot \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ on the $SB$ and $-\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{3} = -\dfrac{1}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ on the $BC$ overhang. The total area is therefore $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$, and $T_S = 3 \cdot \dfrac{2}{3} = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$. With balance, the resultant $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ applied at $x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ gives $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the segment $AS$ gives $T_S = 8 - 3 \cdot 2 = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by neglecting the overhang, and $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ by adding only the positive area.
