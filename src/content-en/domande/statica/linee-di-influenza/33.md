In the Gerber beam shown, supporting beam $AC$ has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $1\,\mathrm{m}$; suspended beam $CD$, of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$, is connected at $C$ and supported by a roller at $D$. What is the influence-line ordinate for the bending moment at section $S$, the midpoint of $AB$, when the unit load is at the midpoint of $CD$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2.5,0;B,5,0;C,6,0;D,8,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" forze="7 0,0,-1.8,1" quote="A,S,2.5 m,0.9;S,B,2.5 m,0.9;B,C,1 m,0.9;C,7 0,1 m,0.9;7 0,D,1 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam showing section $S$ in the span and a unit load on the suspended beam"}

- $-\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

On the supporting beam, the right-hand branch of the influence line for bending moment at $S$ is $\dfrac{a}{L}\,(L - \xi) = \dfrac{1}{2}\,(5 - \xi)$. It extends over the overhang and reaches $-\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ at $C$. On the suspended span the influence line runs straight from $-\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ at $C$ to $0$ at roller $D$. Halfway along $CD$ its ordinate is therefore $-\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $-\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ is the ordinate at $C$. With the load halfway along $CD$, the suspended beam transfers only half the load to the supporting beam.
