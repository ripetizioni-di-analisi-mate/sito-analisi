A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$. What are the influence-line ordinates for shear at section $S$, a distance $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, immediately to the left and right of $S$?

- $-\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{4}$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and $-\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

If the load is to the right of $S$, only $V_A = \dfrac{6 - \xi}{6}$ and internal actions act on the segment $AS$, then $T = V_A$. If the load is to the left of $S$, only $V_B = \dfrac{\xi}{6}$ and internal actions act on the segment $SB$; the positive shear on the right segment is directed upward, then $T = -V_B$. Therefore
$$
\eta_T(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac{\xi}{6} & \text{if } 0 \le \xi < 1.5 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{6 - \xi}{6} & \text{if } 1.5 < \xi \le 6
\end{cases}
$$
In $S$ the line goes from $-\dfrac{1.5}{6} = -\dfrac{1}{4}$ to $\dfrac{4.5}{6} = \dfrac{3}{4}$, with an $1$ amplitude jump upward.
