Müller–Breslau theorem looks for the influence line for the shear at section $S$ of a simply supported beam with horizontal axis. What release is introduced at $S$?

- A parallel-link connection with the links parallel to the beam axis
- A hinge
- A parallel-link connection with the links perpendicular to the beam axis
- A single link parallel to the beam axis

## Explanation

The disconnect only removes the internal constraint that transmits the shear and leaves those that transmit the bending moment and the axial force. Parallel-link support with pin-ended links parallel to the axis prevents relative rotation and axial sliding, and leaves only the relative vertical sliding motion of the two segments free. The conjugate displacement is therefore $\Delta v = v_d - v_s$, and in the mechanism the two segment remain parallel. Hinge corresponds to the bending moment, and parallel-link support with pin-ended links perpendicular to the axis to the axial force.
