The beam shown, of span $5\,\mathrm{m}$, has in $A$ a parallel-link support with the horizontal links, which prevents horizontal translation and rotation, and in $B$ a roller support. What is the influence-line ordinate for the bending moment at section $S$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, when the unit load is a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,180;B,carrello" forze="1 0,0,-1.3,1" quote="A,1 0,1 m,0.9;1 0,S,1 m,0.9;S,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with a parallel-link support at $A$, a roller at $B$, and a unit load to the left of $S$"}

- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The internal pair of horizontal links transmits no vertical reaction, so vertical equilibrium gives $V_B = 1$ wherever the load is placed. The load lies to the left of $S$, leaving segment $SB$ subject only to reaction $V_B$ and the internal forces and moment at $S$. Taking moments for segment $SB$ about $S$ gives $M = V_B \cdot 3 = 3\,\mathrm{m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension. The value $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$ applies to a simply supported beam, for which the roller reaction is only $\dfrac{\xi}{5}$.
