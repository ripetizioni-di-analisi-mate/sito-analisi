Shown a beam of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ is bound in $A$ by a two-force horizontal link and in $B$ by a parallel-link support to vertical links, which prevents vertical translation and rotation. The unit load is located at the end $A$. What is the bending moment at the midspan section $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2.5,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,pendolo,180;B,doppio-pendolo,270" forze="A,0,-1.3,1" quote="A,S,2.5 m,0.9;S,B,2.5 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with a horizontal link at $A$, a parallel-link support with vertical links at $B$, and a unit load at $A$"}

- $0$
- $-2.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The horizontal two-force link in $A$ only exerts a horizontal reaction, which acts along the axis of the beam and about $S$ has zero moment arm. The segment $AS$ therefore carries only the unit load, applied $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $S$. Moment equilibrium of segment $AS$ about $S$ gives $M + 1 \cdot 2.5 = 0$, therefore $\eta_M = -2.5\,\mathrm{m}$, with the upper fibers in tension. The beam then behaves like a fixed cantilever in $B$ and free in $A$, and the line equals $\xi - 2.5$ to the left of $S$ and $0$ to the right. The value $0$ is that of the simply supported beam in $A$, and $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$ is the $\dfrac{ab}{L}$ vertex of the same simply supported beam.
