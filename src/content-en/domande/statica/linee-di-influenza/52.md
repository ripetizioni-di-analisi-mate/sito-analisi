To which of the following beams with a horizontal axis and frictionless bilateral supports does the influence-line theorem for statically determinate beams fail to apply?

- A beam fixed at $A$ and supported on a roller at $B$
- A beam with a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$, and two overhangs
- A Gerber beam consisting of a fixed cantilever and a suspended span resting on a roller
- A beam with a parallel-link support having horizontal links at $A$ and a roller at $B$

## Explanation

The theorem requires that the beam be statically determinate. The pinned and supported beam is a single rigid body with simple $3 + 1 = 4$ constraints, so it is once statically indeterminate, and the hypothesis is not met. The beam with two overhangs has $2 + 1 = 3$ scalar constraints on one body, and the Gerber beam has $3 + 2 + 1 = 6$ on two bodies. The beam with parallel-link support and roller support has $2 + 1 = 3$ scalar constraints well arranged, because parallel-link support prevents horizontal translation and rotation and the roller support vertical translation. These three beams are statically determinate, so the theorem applies.
