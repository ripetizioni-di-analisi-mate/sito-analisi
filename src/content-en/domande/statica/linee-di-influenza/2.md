The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and an overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the influence-line ordinate for $V_A$ when the unit load is at $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam"}

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $0$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

Taking moments about $B$ with the load at $\xi$ gives $V_A \cdot 6 - 1 \cdot (6 - \xi) = 0$, so $\eta_{V_A}(\xi) = \dfrac{6 - \xi}{6}$. The span and overhang belong to the same rigid body, so the expression applies throughout $0 \le \xi \le 8\,\mathrm{m}$. At $C$ ($\xi = 8\,\mathrm{m}$), the ordinate is $\dfrac{6 - 8}{6} = -\dfrac{1}{3}$. The negative sign indicates a downward reaction. Dividing the length of the overhang by the total beam length instead of the span $AB$ would give $-\dfrac{1}{4}$.
