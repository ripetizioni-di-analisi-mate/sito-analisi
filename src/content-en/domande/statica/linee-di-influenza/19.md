The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $EA$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $A$; section $D$ is located on the overhang, at $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. What is the shear force at $D$ when the unit load is at the free end $E$?

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;D,1,0;A,2,0;B,8,0" aste="E-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="E,0,-1.5,1" quote="E,D,1 m,0.9;D,A,1 m,0.9;A,B,6 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with an overhang on the left, section $D$, and a unit load at the free end $E$"}

- $-1$
- $1$
- $0$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The segment $ED$, to the left of $D$, has the free end, so only the load and internal actions in $D$ act on it. On the left segment the positive shear acts downward. The balance at the vertical translation of the segment $ED$ gives $-1 - T_D = 0$, then $T_D = -1$. The same value is found with the load at any point between $E$ and $D$, while with the load to the right of $D$ the segment $ED$ is unloaded and $T_D = 0$. The value $1$ is that of a section on a right overhang, where the free segment stands to the right of the section.
