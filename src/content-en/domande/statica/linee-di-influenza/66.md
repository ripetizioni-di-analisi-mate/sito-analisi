In the beam shown the cantilever $AC$, of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at $A$; in $C$ an internal parallel-link connection with the horizontal links connects it to the beam $CB$, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, supported with a roller support at $B$. What is the influence-line ordinate for $V_B$ when the unit load is a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" interni="C,doppio-pendolo,0" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="3 0,0,-1.5,1" quote="A,C,2 m,0.9;C,3 0,1 m,0.9;3 0,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Cantilever and beam joined by an internal parallel-link connection at $C$"}

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Apply the Müller–Breslau principle by removing the roller at $B$ and imposing an upward displacement of $1$ at $B$. Cantilever $AC$ remains fixed. The internal pair of parallel links prevents relative rotation and horizontal slip while allowing vertical slip, so beam $CB$ rises by $1$ without rotating. The influence-line ordinate is therefore $0$ along $AC$ and $1$ throughout $CB$. Vertical force equilibrium of beam $CB$ gives the same result because the horizontal links transmit no vertical force. The value $\dfrac{1}{4}$ would apply if there were a hinge at $C$.
