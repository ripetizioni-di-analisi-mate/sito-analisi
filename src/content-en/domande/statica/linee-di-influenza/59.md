An $3\,\mathrm{m}$ long cantilever, fixed at $A$, supports a beam of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ in the $C$, resting on a roller support at $B$, as shown. The unit load is in the middle of $CB$. What is the bending moment at section $S$ of the cantilever, a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,1,0;C,3,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="5 0,0,-1.5,1" quote="A,S,1 m,0.9;S,C,2 m,0.9;C,5 0,2 m,0.9;5 0,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam showing section $S$ on the cantilever and a unit load on the suspended beam"}

- $-2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0$
- $-1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The load at midspan of the load $CB$ beam splits equally between the roller support at $B$ and the hinge $C$, so the suspended segment transmits a downward force $\dfrac{1}{2}$ to the cantilever in $C$. The segment $SC$ of the cantilever carries only this force, applied $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $S$. Moment equilibrium of segment $SC$ about $S$ therefore gives $M = -\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2 = -1\,\mathrm{m}$, with the upper fibers in tension. The same value follows from the Gerber beam influence lines theorem, because on the suspended segment the line goes linearly from $-2\,\mathrm{m}$ in $C$ to $0$ in $B$. The value $-2\,\mathrm{m}$ is the ordinate with the load at $C$, and $-4\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by extending the line $-(\xi - 1)$ of the cantilever on the suspended segment, as if the two bodies were one.
