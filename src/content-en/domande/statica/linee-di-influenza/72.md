On a simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, a triangular distributed load down, zero in $A$ and of $q_0 = 9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ intensity in $B$ acts. Using the influence line, what is reaction $V_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0,0.9,q_0" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam under a triangular load varying from $0$ at $A$ to $q_0 = 9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$"}

- $27\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The load is $q(\xi) = \dfrac{9}{6}\,\xi = \dfrac{3}{2}\,\xi$ and the $V_B$'s influence line is $\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \dfrac{\xi}{6}$. By the theorem on the value of a magnitude with the influence lines
$$
V_B = \int_0^6 \frac{3}{2}\,\xi \cdot \frac{\xi}{6}\,d\xi = \frac{1}{4} \int_0^6 \xi^2\,d\xi = \frac{1}{4} \cdot 72 = 18\,\mathrm{kN} ,
$$
upward. The equilibrium confirms the value: the resultant $27\,\mathrm{kN}$ switches $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, then $V_B \cdot 6 = 27 \cdot 4$. The value $13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by applying the resultant to half span, and $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the reaction $V_A$.
