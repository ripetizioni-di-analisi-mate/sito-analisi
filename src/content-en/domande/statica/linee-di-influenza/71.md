Where on the Gerber beam shown must a downward moving force act to maximize the reaction at roller $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0;D,12,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,D,4 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with an overhanging supporting beam $AC$ and a suspended beam $CD$"}

- At hinge $C$
- At roller $B$
- At the midpoint of suspended span $CD$
- At roller $D$

## Explanation

On the supporting beam the influence line for $V_B$ is $\dfrac{\xi}{6}$. Its ordinate is $1$ at $B$ and continues to increase along the overhang, reaching $\dfrac{8}{6} = \dfrac{4}{3}$ at $C$. On the suspended span of a Gerber beam the influence line is straight, dropping from $\dfrac{4}{3}$ at $C$ to $0$ at $D$. Its maximum ordinate is therefore $\dfrac{4}{3}$, at hinge $C$. A point load gives the largest reaction when it acts at this maximum, so its critical position is $C$. Directly over $B$ the reaction is only $P$, and halfway along $CD$ it is $\dfrac{2}{3}\,P$.
