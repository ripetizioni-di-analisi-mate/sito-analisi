For a vertical reaction the permanent load contributes $+14\,\mathrm{kN}$. The signed area of all negative parts of its influence line is $-3\,\mathrm{m}$. A moving load of $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ can occupy only the unfavourable portions if desired. What is the minimum total reaction?

- $-12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $+2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $+26\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

To minimise the reaction, only the negative-ordinate portions are loaded. The moving load contributes $4(-3)=-12\,\mathrm{kN}$. The permanent weight stays in place, giving a total minimum of $14-12=2\,\mathrm{kN}$.
