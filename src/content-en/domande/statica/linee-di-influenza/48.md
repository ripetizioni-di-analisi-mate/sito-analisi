In the Gerber beam shown, suspended beam $AC$ rests on a roller at $A$ and is connected at $C$ to supporting beam $CD$, which has a pin at $B$ and a roller at $D$; $C$ is located at $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, $B$ to $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and $D$ to $9\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the influence-line ordinate for $V_D$ when the unit load is at the midpoint of $AC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,5,0;D,9,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="1.5 0,0,-1.6,1" quote="A,C,3 m,0.9;C,B,2 m,0.9;B,D,4 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with suspended beam $AC$ on the left and a unit load at the midpoint of $AC$"}

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $0$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

With the load a distance $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, suspended beam $AC$ behaves as a simply supported beam of span $3\,\mathrm{m}$. At $C$ it therefore transmits a downward force $\dfrac{1}{2}$ to the supporting beam. On the supporting this force acts at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of the hinge $B$, while the roller support $D$ is at $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $B$. Moment equilibrium of the supporting around $B$ gives $V_D \cdot 4 + \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2 = 0$, therefore $V_D = -\dfrac{1}{4}$: the reaction is directed downward. The value $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ is the ordinate at $C$. The value $0$ results from neglecting the force transmitted by the suspended beam to the supporting beam.
