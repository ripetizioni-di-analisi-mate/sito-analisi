In the Gerber beam shown the cantilever $AC$, of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at $A$; the beam $CB$, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, is connected in $C$ and supported with a roller support at $B$. What is the influence-line ordinate for the reaction moment $M_A$, positive counterclockwise, when the unit load is a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" quote="A,C,2 m,0.9;C,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with a supporting cantilever"}

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The load acts on suspended beam $CB$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$ and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$. Moment equilibrium of beam $CB$ about $B$ gives the force at $C$, which equals $\dfrac{2}{4} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ upward on the suspended segment. The cantilever then receives at $C$ a downward force $\dfrac{1}{2}$, at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from fixed support, therefore $M_A = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2 = 1\,\mathrm{m}$. On the suspended segment the influence line is in fact the segment $\eta_{M_A}(\xi) = \dfrac{6 - \xi}{2}$, from $2\,\mathrm{m}$ in $C$ to $0$ in $B$. The value $4\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by treating the entire beam as a cantilever, with $\eta_{M_A} = \xi$.
