A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, has the $S$ to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ section from $A$. Which of the following influence lines has a discontinuity when the load is at $S$?

- The influence line for bending moment at $S$
- The influence line for $V_A$
- The influence line for shear at $S$
- The influence line for $V_B$

## Explanation

The beam is one rigid body, so the reactions are linearly dependent on $\xi$ over all span and their lines are continuous segments. The $S$ shear, obtained from the balance of the left segment, contains the term $-1$ of the load only when the load lies to the left of $S$. Therefore his line in $S$ jumps by $1$, from $-\dfrac{1}{3}$ to $\dfrac{2}{3}$. The moment at $S$ instead contains the term $-(2 - \xi)$, which vanishes with the load at $S$, so its line is continuous and has in $S$ only a corner.
