A two-axle trolley has a wheelbase of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and applies a load of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ per axle as it crosses a beam of span $8\,\mathrm{m}$. The beam is pinned at $A$ and rests on a roller at $B$. Considering all positions of the trolley, what is the maximum shear force at section $S$, located $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The influence line for shear at $S$ has ordinate $-\dfrac{\xi}{8}$ to the left of $S$ and $\dfrac{8 - \xi}{8}$ to the right. Its maximum ordinate is $\dfrac{3}{4}$, immediately to the right of $S$. The shear due to the two axle loads varies piecewise linearly as the trolley moves. Its maximum therefore occurs when one load reaches the peak of the influence line. With the left axle just to the right of $S$, the other axle is at $\xi = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, where the ordinate is $\dfrac{1}{2}$. This gives $T = 10 \cdot \dfrac{3}{4} + 10 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. With the right axle just to the right of $S$, the other is at $A$, where the ordinate is zero, giving $T = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. Thus the maximum is $12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ would require both axles to occupy the position with ordinate $\dfrac{3}{4}$.
