The roller support at $B$ of a beam of length $10\,\mathrm{m}$, hinged at $A$, must be sized for a load of $5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ distributed over a length of $4\,\mathrm{m}$, which can be arranged anywhere on the beam. What maximum reaction must the roller support resist?

- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The line $\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \dfrac{\xi}{10}$ of influence is growing over the entire beam, so the ordinate at the ends of the load are never equal and the value $V_B(z) = 5 \displaystyle\int_z^{z + 4} \frac{\xi}{10}\,d\xi$ grows with $z$. The maximum is therefore with the load juxtaposed to $B$, on the segment from $6\,\mathrm{m}$ to $10\,\mathrm{m}$:
$$
V_{B,\max} = \frac{5}{10} \int_6^{10} \xi\,d\xi = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{100 - 36}{2} = 16\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The value $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the entire resultant of the load, $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained with the load centered at midspan, and $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ with the load juxtaposed to $A$.
