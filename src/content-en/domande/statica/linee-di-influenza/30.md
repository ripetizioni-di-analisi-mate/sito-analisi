For the influence line for the shear at section $S$ of a simply supported beam of span $5\,\mathrm{m}$, with $S$ to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the hinge $A$, a parallel-link support with the horizontal links is inserted in $S$ and $v_d - v_s = 1$ is imposed. What rotation do the two segments have in common?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$, counterclockwise
- $-\dfrac{1}{5}$, clockwise
- $-\dfrac{2}{5}$, clockwise
- $-\dfrac{3}{5}$, clockwise

## Explanation

Parallel-link support prevents relative rotation, so the two segment rotate the same angle $\varphi$, the segment $AS$ around $A$ and the segment $SB$ around $B$. In $S$ you have $v_s = 2\,\varphi$ and $v_d = (2 - 5)\,\varphi = -3\,\varphi$. The $v_d - v_s = 1$ condition gives $-5\,\varphi = 1$, then $\varphi = -\dfrac{1}{5}$, clockwise. The ordinate of the line in $S$ are $v_s = -\dfrac{2}{5}$ and $v_d = \dfrac{3}{5}$, i.e. $-\dfrac{a}{L}$ and $\dfrac{b}{L}$.
