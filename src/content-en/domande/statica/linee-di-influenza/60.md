In the beam shown the section $S$ is taken in the span, close to the roller support at $B$. When the unit load is at the overhang tip $C$, what is the shear force at $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.4,1" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" etichette="4.75,0.35,S" titolo="Overhanging beam showing section $S$ immediately to the left of $B$ and a unit load at $C$"}

- $0$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

The load at $C$ is located to the right of $S$, so on the segment to the left of $S$ only the reaction $V_A$ acts. Since on the left segment the positive shear acts downward, the balance at vertical force equilibrium gives $T = V_A$. The balance at the beam moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $V_A \cdot 5 + 1 \cdot 2 = 0$, then $V_A = -\dfrac{2}{5}$ and $\eta_T = -\dfrac{2}{5}$. The ordinate $1$ belongs to the section immediately to the right of $B$, on the overhang, where the shear directly balances the load.
