In the Gerber beam shown, supporting beam $AC$ has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$; suspended beam $CD$, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, rests on a roller at $D$. A uniform $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load along $4\,\mathrm{m}$ passes through the beam. What is the maximum value of reaction $V_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,D,4 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with an overhanging supporting beam $AC$ and a suspended beam $CD$"}

- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $37.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

On the $\eta_{V_B} = \dfrac{\xi}{4}$ supporting segment, which is $\dfrac{3}{2}$ in $C$; on the suspended segment the line is the segment $\dfrac{3}{8}\,(10 - \xi)$, which vanishes in $D$. The line is a base $10\,\mathrm{m}$ triangle with the vertex in $C$, so the long load $4\,\mathrm{m}$ gives the maximum at the vertex, in the position where the ordinate at the ends are equal. With the load on $[z, z + 4]$ the $\dfrac{z}{4} = \dfrac{3}{8}\,(6 - z)$ condition gives $z = 3.6\,\mathrm{m}$, and the ordinate at the ends equal $0.9$. The area on the loaded segment is
$$
\mathcal{A} = \frac{0.9 + 1.5}{2} \cdot 2.4 + \frac{1.5 + 0.9}{2} \cdot 1.6 = 4.8\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
therefore $V_{B,\max} = 5 \cdot 4.8 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by multiplying the entire load by the maximum ordinate $\dfrac{3}{2}$, and $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ by putting the load all on the suspended segment.
