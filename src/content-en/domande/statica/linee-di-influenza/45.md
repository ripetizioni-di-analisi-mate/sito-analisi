A simply supported beam of span $8\,\mathrm m$ carries two axles of equal load, distant $2\,\mathrm m$. The right reaction influence lines ordinate $\xi/8$. Where should the left axle be placed to maximize the right support reaction with both axles on the beam?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="3 0,0,-1.4,P;5 0,0,-1.4,P" quote="A,B,8 m"}

- $\xi=4\,\mathrm m$
- $\xi=6\,\mathrm m$
- $\xi=2\,\mathrm m$
- $\xi=0\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

When the left axis is in $\xi$, the other is in $\xi+2$. The sum of the ordinate grows with $\xi$. The last permissible position has the right axis to the right support, so the left one is at $6\,\mathrm m$ from the left.
