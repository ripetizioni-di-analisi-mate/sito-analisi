Section $S$ of the Gerber beam shown is at the midpoint of suspended beam $CD$. What is the highest ordinate of the influence line for the bending moment at $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,6,0;S,8,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9;B,C,1 m,0.9;C,S,2 m,0.9;S,D,2 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam showing section $S$ on the suspended beam"}

- $\dfrac{8}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Section $S$ lies on the suspended beam. By the influence-line theorem for Gerber beams, its influence line therefore vanishes along the entire supporting beam. With Müller–Breslau's theorem a hinge is inserted into $S$: the supporting remains stationary, the segment $CS$ rotates about $C$ and the segment $SD$ around $D$. The beam $CD$ therefore behaves like a simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ with the section at midspan, and the maximum ordinate is $\dfrac{2 \cdot 2}{4} = 1\,\mathrm{m}$, with the load at $S$. The value $\dfrac{8}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by applying the formula $\dfrac{ab}{L}$ at the full length from $A$ to $D$.
