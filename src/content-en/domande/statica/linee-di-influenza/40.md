The beam shown has a pin at $A$, the roller support at $B$ $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and the overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$; section $S$ is at $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. On the beam passes a force $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. What is the minimum shear force at $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,1,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,S,1 m,0.9;S,B,5 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam showing section $S$ near pin $A$"}

- $-10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $25\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

To the left of $S$ the influence line for the shear is $-\dfrac{\xi}{6}$, with the lowest value $-\dfrac{1}{6}$ immediately to the left of $S$. To the right of $S$ the line is $\dfrac{6 - \xi}{6}$, which continues on the overhang because the load stays on the same body, and in $C$ it is $-\dfrac{2}{6} = -\dfrac{1}{3}$. Since $-\dfrac{1}{3} < -\dfrac{1}{6}$, the minimum of the line is in $C$. Therefore the minimum shear is with force at $C$ and equals $30 \cdot \left(-\dfrac{1}{3}\right) = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $-5\,\mathrm{kN}$ corresponds to the force immediately to the left of $S$, and $25\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the maximum, with the force immediately to the right of $S$.
