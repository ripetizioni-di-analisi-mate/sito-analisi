A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, with the reaction of the vertical roller support. What is the influence line for the axial force at section $S$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

- It is zero for every load position
- It coincides with the influence line for shear at $S$
- It equals $1$ with the load to the right of $S$ and $0$ with the load to the left
- It equals $-\dfrac{\xi}{6}$ with the load to the left of $S$ and $0$ with the load to the right

## Explanation

The unit load is vertical and the roller support reaction is vertical, so the balance at horizontal force equilibrium of the beam gives $H_A = 0$. On the segment $AS$ the only horizontal forces are $H_A$ and the axial force at $S$, therefore $N_S = -H_A = 0$ for each position of the load. The influence lines of $N$ is therefore identically zero. The axial force becomes different from zero when a reaction has a component along the axis, as in the beam with a two-force link inclined.
