The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. It carries uniform load $q = 10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over its entire length. What is reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam under uniform load $q = 10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $45\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward

## Explanation

$V_A$'s influence line is $\eta_{V_A}(\xi) = \dfrac{4 - \xi}{4}$ over the whole beam, positive over span and negative overhang. The area on the span is $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 4 \cdot 1 = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the area on the overhang is $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot \left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right) = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$, so the overall area equals $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore $V_A = q\,\mathcal{A} = 10 \cdot \dfrac{3}{2} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$, with positive sign, i.e. upward. The value $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ neglects the negative contribution of the load on the overhang, and $45\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the reaction $V_B$.
