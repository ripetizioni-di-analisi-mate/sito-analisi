An $3\,\mathrm{m}$ long cantilever is fixed at $A$, left, and free at the right end; section $S$ is at $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. On the cantilever can act a moving downward force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a crowd $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ occupying any segments. What is the minimum bending moment at $S$?

- $-36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-28\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

For a section of a cantilever, the moment influence line at $S$ is $0$ between $A$ and $S$ and $-(\xi - 1)$ between $S$ and the free end, where it reaches its minimum $-2\,\mathrm{m}$. The line is nonpositive throughout. The most negative moment occurs with the point force at the free end and the crowd load covering the entire segment to the right of $S$. The signed area is $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 2\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Hence $M_S = 10 \cdot (-2) + 4 \cdot (-2) = -28\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension. Omitting the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ in the area of the triangle gives $-36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Measuring the distances from the fixed support instead of $S$ gives $-48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
