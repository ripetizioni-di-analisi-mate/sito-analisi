The beam shown, of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and in $B$ a two-force inclined link of $45^\circ$ on the horizontal. The horizontal $H_A$ reaction is positive to the right. What is the influence-line ordinate for $H_A$ when the unit load is at midspan?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,pendolo,225" forze="3 0,0,-1.5,1" quote="A,3 0,3 m,0.9;3 0,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with a pin at $A$, an inclined link at $B$, and a unit load at midspan"}

- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$

## Explanation

The reaction $R_B$ of the two-force link has the direction of the pin-ended link, inclined by $45^\circ$, so its horizontal and vertical components are equal: $H_B = V_B$. The horizontal components of the reactions act along the axis of the beam, so the equilibrium at moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $V_B \cdot 6 - 1 \cdot \xi = 0$, i.e. $V_B = \dfrac{\xi}{6}$ upward, and consequently $H_B = \dfrac{\xi}{6}$ to the right. The balance at the horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A + H_B = 0$, then $\eta_{H_A}(\xi) = -\dfrac{\xi}{6}$. At midspan, the ordinate is $-\dfrac{3}{6} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$: the reaction $H_A$ is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ to the left. The value $0$ is that of the beam with vertical roller support at $B$, and $-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ is the ordinate of the $R_B$'s changed influence lines of sign.
