On the beam shown, with the pin at $A$, the roller support at $B$ and the overhang $BC$, can act a uniform load of variable length. Which segment does the load cover when the shear at section $S$ is greatest?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam showing section $S$ in the span"}

- From $S$ to $C$
- From $S$ to $B$
- From $A$ to $S$
- From $A$ to $B$

## Explanation

The $S$ shear influence lines equals $-\dfrac{\xi}{6}$ between $A$ and $S$ and $\dfrac{6 - \xi}{6}$ from $S$ onwards. The right branch is positive between $S$ and $B$ and becomes negative on the overhang, where it drops to $-\dfrac{1}{3}$ in $C$. For the variable length load theorem, the maximum value is with the load on all and only the segments where the line is positive, then from $S$ to $B$. The load on the overhang or the section $AS$ would add negative contributions.
