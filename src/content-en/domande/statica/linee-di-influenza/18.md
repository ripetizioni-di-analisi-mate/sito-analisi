On a simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, a distributed load down acts that decreases linearly from $9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in $A$ to $0$ in $B$, i.e. $q(\xi) = 9 - \dfrac{3}{2}\,\xi$. Using the influence line, what is the bending moment at section $S$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.9,0,q" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam under a triangular load varying from $9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$ to $0$ at $B$"}

- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $27\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The moment influence lines in $S$ has the branches $\eta_M = \dfrac{2}{3}\,\xi$ for $0 \le \xi \le 2$ and $\eta_M = \dfrac{1}{3}\,(6 - \xi)$ for $2 \le \xi \le 6$. By the theorem on the value of a magnitude with the influence lines the moment is the integral of $q(\xi)\,\eta_M(\xi)$, which must be broken into $S$:
$$
M_S = \int_0^2 \left(9 - \frac{3}{2}\,\xi\right) \frac{2}{3}\,\xi \, d\xi + \int_2^6 \left(9 - \frac{3}{2}\,\xi\right) \frac{6 - \xi}{3} \, d\xi = \frac{28}{3} + \frac{32}{3} = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
With the balance you have $V_A = 27 \cdot \dfrac{4}{6} = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the load on the section $AS$ has $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ moment about $S$, then $M_S = 18 \cdot 2 - 16 = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained by multiplying the resultant $27\,\mathrm{kN}$ by the ordinate at its point of application, which coincides with $S$: the process does not apply because the influence lines changes slope under the load.
