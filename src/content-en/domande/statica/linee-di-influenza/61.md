The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$, and two overhangs $EA$ and $BF$, each of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Span $AB$ has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and section $S$ is at midspan. On which portions of the beam does a downward point load produce a negative bending moment at $S$?

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;A,2,0;S,5,0;B,8,0;F,10,0" aste="E-F" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="E,A,2 m,0.9;A,S,3 m,0.9;S,B,3 m,0.9;B,F,2 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with two overhangs showing midspan section $S$"}

- Only on overhang $BF$
- On overhangs $EA$ and $BF$
- On span $AB$
- On no segment

## Explanation

With $\xi$ measured by $E$, the moment influence lines in $S$ has the $\dfrac{1}{2}\,(\xi - 2)$ branches to the left of $S$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}\,(8 - \xi)$ branches to the right of $S$, because $a = b = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ and span equals $6\,\mathrm{m}$. Overhangs and span form a single body, so by the theorem on the influence lines of statically determinate beams the two branches continue in a straight line overhangs. The left branch is negative for $\xi < 2$, i.e. on the $EA$ overhang, and the right branch is negative for $\xi > 8$, i.e. on the $BF$ overhang; in $E$ and $F$ the ordinate equals $-1\,\mathrm{m}$. Between $A$ and $B$ the line is positive, so a load in span puts the lower fibers in tension in $S$.
