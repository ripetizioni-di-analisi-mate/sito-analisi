On a simply supported beam of span $12\,\mathrm{m}$, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, a uniform $q = 10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load along $6\,\mathrm{m}$ passes. What is the maximum bending moment at section $S$, a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

- $115\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $120\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $160\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $80\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The moment influence lines in $S$ has the $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\xi$ and $\dfrac{12 - \xi}{3}$ branches and the $\dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ vertex. The moment is maximum when you ordinate them at the ends of the load are equal. With the $z$ to $z + 6$ load the condition is $\dfrac{2}{3}\,z = \dfrac{12 - z - 6}{3}$, so $z = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the extreme ordinate equal $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$. The area on the segment from $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to $8\,\mathrm{m}$ is
$$
\mathcal{A} = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{4}{3} + \frac{8}{3}\right) \cdot 2 + \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{8}{3} + \frac{4}{3}\right) \cdot 4 = 4 + 8 = 12\,\mathrm{m^2} ,
$$
therefore $M_{S,\max} = 10 \cdot 12 = 120\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. With the load centered on $S$, from $1\,\mathrm{m}$ to $7\,\mathrm{m}$, you get $115\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
