A simply supported beam of span $8\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$. At $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ an upward-directed $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ force acts. Using the influence line, what is reaction $V_B$?

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward

## Explanation

$V_B$'s influence line is $\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \dfrac{\xi}{8}$, which in $\xi = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ is $\dfrac{1}{4}$. The unit load of the definition is directed downward, so a force $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward is equivalent to an $-12\,\mathrm{kN}$ intensity downward force. Therefore $V_B = -12 \cdot \dfrac{1}{4} = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ down. Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives the same value, because $V_B \cdot 8 + 12 \cdot 2 = 0$. $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ values are obtained with the ordinate $\dfrac{3}{4}$, which belongs to the line $V_A$ of influence.
