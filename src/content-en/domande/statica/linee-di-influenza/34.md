In the Gerber beam shown the $AC$ supporting segment has a pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$; suspended beam $CD$ is connected at $C$ and supported by a roller at $D$, and section $S$ lies at the midpoint of $CD$. What is the bending moment at $S$ when the unit load is anywhere in span $AB$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;S,8,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,S,2 m,0.9;S,D,2 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam showing supporting beam $AC$, suspended beam $CD$, and section $S$"}

- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

With the load on the supporting beam, suspended beam $CD$ carries no load. Only the force at $C$ and the reaction at $D$, both vertical, act on it, and moment equilibrium around $D$ and around $C$ shows that they are both zero. The moment at $S$ is therefore zero. Indeed, by the Gerber beam theorem, the influence line for any response of the suspended beam is zero over the entire supporting beam. On the beam $CD$ the moment line in $S$ is instead the triangle with the $\dfrac{2 \cdot 2}{4} = 1\,\mathrm{m}$ vertex.
