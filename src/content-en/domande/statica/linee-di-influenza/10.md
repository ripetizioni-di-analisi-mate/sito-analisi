The simply supported beam shown, of span $8\,\mathrm{m}$, carries downward forces $P_1 = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$, $P_2 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $P_3 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at distances $1\,\mathrm{m}$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from pin $A$. What is the shear force at section $S$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="1 0,0,-1.4,P_1;4 0,0,-2,P_2;6 0,0,-1,P_3" quote="A,1 0,1 m,0.9;1 0,S,1 m,0.9;S,4 0,2 m,0.9;4 0,6 0,2 m,0.9;6 0,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam under three forces"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $28\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The shear line in $S$ equals $-\dfrac{\xi}{8}$ to the left of $S$ and $\dfrac{8 - \xi}{8}$ to the right. The force $P_1$ is to the left of $S$, with ordinate $-\dfrac{1}{8}$, while the $P_2$ and $P_3$ are to the right, with ordinate $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}$. By adding the products of forces to the ordinate you get
$$
T_S = 16 \cdot \left(-\frac{1}{8}\right) + 24 \cdot \frac{1}{2} + 8 \cdot \frac{1}{4} = -2 + 12 + 2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The value $28\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained by using the right branch of the line through $P_1$, i.e. neglecting the $1$ jump at $S$.
