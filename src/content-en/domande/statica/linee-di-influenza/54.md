In the Gerber beam shown the $AC$ supporting segment has a pin at $A$, the roller support at $B$ and the $BC$ overhang; suspended beam $CD$ is connected at $C$ and supported by a roller at $D$. In the middle of $CD$ only one $\mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise couple acts. What value of reaction $V_B$ follows from the slope of the influence line?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" coppie="8 0,1,ℳ" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,8 0,2 m,0.9;8 0,D,2 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M}$ on the suspended beam"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward
- $0$

## Explanation

On the supporting span, the influence line for $V_B$ is $\dfrac{\xi}{4}$, which equals $\dfrac{6}{4} = \dfrac{3}{2}$ at $C$. By the influence-line theorem for Gerber beams, the line on the suspended span runs from $\dfrac{3}{2}$ at $C$ to $0$ at $D$, so its slope is $-\dfrac{3}{8}$. The influence-line rule for an applied couple gives a contribution $-\mathcal{M}\,\eta_{V_B}'$ for the counterclockwise couple. Thus $V_B = -8 \cdot \left(-\dfrac{3}{8}\right) = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. Taking moments for the suspended span about $D$ shows that it transmits a downward force of $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the supporting span at $C$. Taking moments for the supporting span about $A$ gives $V_B \cdot 4 = 2 \cdot 6$, confirming the result. The value $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ uses the ordinate at $C$ in place of the slope. The value $0$ neglects the vertical force transmitted between the spans.
