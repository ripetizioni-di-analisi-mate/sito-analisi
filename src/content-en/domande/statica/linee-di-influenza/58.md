The beam shown has a pin at $A$, the roller support at $B$ and the overhang $BC$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$; section $D$ is on the overhang, at $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$. They act the uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire beam and force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at midspan. Using the influence line, what is the shear force at $D$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;D,7,0;C,9,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.5,0.5,q" forze="3 0,0,-1.6,P" quote="A,3 0,3 m,0.9;3 0,B,3 m,0.9;B,D,1 m,0.9;D,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam showing section $D$ on the overhang, a uniform load, and a force in the span"}

- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

For the overhang section theorem, the shear line in $D$ is $0$ for $\xi < 7\,\mathrm{m}$ and $1$ for $\xi > 7\,\mathrm{m}$. The force $P$ acts in span, where the ordinate is zero, so it does not contribute. The uniform load contributes with $q\,\mathcal{A}$, and the area of the line is different from zero only on the segment from $D$ to $C$, where $1 \cdot 2 = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ is valid. Therefore $T_D = 4 \cdot 2 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$. The same value follows from the balance to the vertical translation of the segment $DC$, which carries $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ load. The value $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ considers all overhang from $B$, and $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ adds the force $P$, which acts on the span.
