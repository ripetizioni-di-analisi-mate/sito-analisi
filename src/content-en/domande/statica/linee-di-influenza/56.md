On a simply supported beam of span $8\,\mathrm{m}$, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, a force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ passes down. Over all beam sections and all positions of the force, what is the maximum bending moment?

- $80\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $160\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

For the distance $a$ section from $A$, with $b = 8 - a$, the current influence line has the maximum ordinate $\dfrac{ab}{8}$ below the section, so the maximum moment in that section is $P\,\dfrac{a\,(8 - a)}{8}$. The $a\,(8 - a)$ product is maximum for $a = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, where it equals $16$. Therefore the absolute maximum is at midspan, with the force at midspan, and equals $20 \cdot \dfrac{16}{8} = 40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, i.e. $\dfrac{PL}{4}$. $80\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ corresponds to $\dfrac{PL}{2}$, and $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ to $\dfrac{PL}{8}$.
