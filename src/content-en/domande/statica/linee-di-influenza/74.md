On a simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, with the hinge $A$ and $B$ roller support, a uniform $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load occupies the section from $A$ $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, straddling the section $S$ distant $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Using the area under the influence line on the loaded segment, what is the bending moment at $S$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,4 0,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,4 0,2 m,0.9;4 0,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam with uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ extending across section $S$"}

- $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The moment influence lines in $S$ has the $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\xi$ branches for $\xi \le 2$ and $\dfrac{6 - \xi}{3}$ branches for $\xi \ge 2$, with the $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ vertex under $S$. The load covers the vertex, so the area is calculated separately on the two branches. On the $0$ to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ segment the area is the triangle $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot \dfrac{4}{3} = \dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, and on the $2$ to $4\,\mathrm{m}$ segment is the trapezoid $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \left(\dfrac{4}{3} + \dfrac{2}{3}\right) \cdot 2 = 2\,\mathrm{m^2}$. So $M_S = 6 \cdot \left(\dfrac{4}{3} + 2\right) = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension. The equilibrium confirms the value, because $V_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M_S = 16 \cdot 2 - 12 \cdot 1 = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The value $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ multiplies the resultant $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ by the ordinate at its point of application, a process valid only if the line is a single segment on the loaded section.
