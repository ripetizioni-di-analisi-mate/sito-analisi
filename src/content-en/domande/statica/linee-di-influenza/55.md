A simply supported beam of span $8\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$. Two numbers are considered: the $\xi = 5\,\mathrm{m}$ ordinate of the shear line in the section $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and the shear in the section $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ produced by the unit load stationary at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. What are their values, in order?

- $-\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$ and $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $-\dfrac{5}{8}$ and $-\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$ and $-\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

In the influence lines the section is fixed, at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and the load is located at $\xi = 5\,\mathrm{m}$, to the right of the section. By the theorem on the influence lines of $M$ and $T$ in the simply supported beam the ordinate is therefore $\dfrac{8 - 5}{8} = \dfrac{3}{8}$. In the second case the load is stationary in $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, so $V_A = \dfrac{6}{8} = \dfrac{3}{4}$, and the section $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ has the load to its left. On the segment to the left of this section the positive shear acts downward, so the balance at vertical force equilibrium gives $T = \dfrac{3}{4} - 1 = -\dfrac{1}{4}$. The two numbers are different because the influence lines fixes the section and causes the position of the load to vary, while the shear diagram fixes the load and causes the section to vary.
