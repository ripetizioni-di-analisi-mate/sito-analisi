The structure shown is formed by a fixed cantilever in $A$ and a simply supported beam on a roller support at $B$, joined in $C$ by an internal parallel-link connection to horizontal links. The unit load acts on the beam $CB$, at $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$. At this load position, what is the influence-line ordinate for the fixed-end reaction moment $M_A$, positive counterclockwise?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" interni="C,doppio-pendolo,0" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="3 0,0,-1.5,1" quote="A,C,2 m,0.9;C,3 0,1 m,0.9;3 0,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Cantilever and beam joined by an internal parallel-link connection at $C$, with a unit load on $CB$"}

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The internal parallel-link connection does not transmit vertical forces, so the equilibrium at the vertical translation of the beam $CB$ gives $V_B = 1$. Parallel-link support, on the other hand, transmits a moment. Said $M_C$ the moment it exerts on the beam $CB$, moment equilibrium of $CB$ around $B$ gives $M_C + 1 \cdot 3 = 0$, therefore $M_C = -3\,\mathrm{m}$. On the cantilever acts the opposite moment, of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ counterclockwise, then moment equilibrium of the cantilever around $A$ gives $M_A + 3 = 0$ and $\eta_{M_A} = -3\,\mathrm{m}$. With Müller–Breslau theorem the same value is obtained, because the cantilever rotates around $A$ with $1$ rotation, the parallel-link support imposes the same rotation on the beam $CB$, which rotates around $B$, and the coordinate $3\,\mathrm{m}$ point moves by $1 \cdot (3 - 6) = -3\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $0$ overlooks the moment transmitted by parallel-link support, and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ extends the line $\xi$ of the cantilever on the beam $CB$.
