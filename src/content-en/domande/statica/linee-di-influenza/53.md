The beam shown, of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, has in $A$ a parallel-link support with the horizontal links and in $B$ a roller support. How does the shear at section $S$, a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, change as the unit load crosses the section? Indicate the ordinate of the influence lines immediately before and immediately after $S$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,180;B,carrello" quote="A,S,4 m,0.9;S,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Beam showing a parallel-link support at $A$, a roller at $B$, and section $S$"}

- $-\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $0$ and $1$
- $1$ and $0$
- $-1$ and $0$

## Explanation

Parallel-link support does not perform vertical reaction, so $V_B = 1$ for each load position. It is considered the segment $SB$, on which the positive shear acts upward. With the load to the left of $S$ the balance to vertical force equilibrium gives $T + 1 = 0$, then $T = -1$; with the load to the right of $S$ gives $T + 1 - 1 = 0$, then $T = 0$. The line therefore goes from $-1$ to $0$, with the $1$ jump upward predicted by the theorem on statically determinate beam influence lines. The ordinate $-\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\dfrac{1}{3}$ are those of the simply supported beam.
