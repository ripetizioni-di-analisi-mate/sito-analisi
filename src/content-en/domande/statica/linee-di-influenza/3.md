An $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ long cantilever is fixed at $A$, left, and free at the right end; the reaction moment $M_A$ is positive counterclockwise. What value of $M_A$ is produced by a downward force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the free end?

- $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise
- $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

With the unit load at $\xi$ the balance at moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $M_A - 1 \cdot \xi = 0$, then $\eta_{M_A}(\xi) = \xi$, in meters. At the free end the ordinate equals $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore
$$
M_A = P\,\eta_{M_A}(2.5) = 12 \cdot 2.5 = 30\,\mathrm{kN\,m} .
$$
The sign is positive, so the reaction moment is counterclockwise and opposes the clockwise rotation that the force tends to imprint on the cantilever. The value $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ uses the ordinate $\eta_{V_A} = 1$ of the vertical reaction instead of the moment.
