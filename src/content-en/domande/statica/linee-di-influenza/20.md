The three-hinged arch shown has the external $A$ and $B$ hinges at the same elevation, at $8\,\mathrm{m}$ distance, and the $C$ keyed pin at midspan, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ higher; the two bodies $AC$ and $CB$ are straight. A vertical unit load runs through the arch, and $\xi$ is its horizontal coordinate measured by $A$. What is the influence-line ordinate for the thrust $H$, the horizontal component of the reaction at $B$, when the load is at $\xi = 2\,\mathrm{m}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,2;B,8,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="2 1,0,-1.5,1" tratteggi="4,0 4,2" quote="A,2 0,2 m,0.9;A,B,8 m,1.6;4 0,C,2 m,-0.5" titolo="Three-hinged arch with a unit load acting a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$"}

- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium of the entire arch around $A$ gives $V_B \cdot 8 - 1 \cdot 2 = 0$, then $V_B = \dfrac{1}{4}$. The load acts on the body $AC$, so only the reaction at $B$ and the force of the hinge $C$ act on the body $CB$. Moment equilibrium of $CB$ around $C$ gives $V_B \cdot 4 - H \cdot 2 = 0$, then $H = \dfrac{1}{2}$, directed inwards of the arch. For $0 \le \xi \le 4\,\mathrm{m}$ the thrust influence line is then $\eta_H = \dfrac{\xi}{4}$, which reaches maximum $1$ with the load in crown. The value $\dfrac{1}{4}$ is the vertical reaction $V_B$, and $\dfrac{1}{8}$ is obtained by exchanging the rise and half the span in the ratio.
