A small bridge of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, pinned at $A$ and supported by a roller at $B$, carries a vehicle applying a point load $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a crowd load $5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ that may occupy any combination of segments. What is the minimum possible bending moment at the section a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$?

- $-\dfrac{40}{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The moment influence lines in $S$ equals $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\xi$ for $\xi \le 2$ and $\dfrac{1}{3}\,(6 - \xi)$ for $\xi \ge 2$, so its ordinate are positive or zero along the entire beam. By the theorem on concentrated load and the distributed load of variable length, the minimum is with the force at the minimum point of the line and with the distributed load on the only segments where the line is negative. The minimum of the line is $0$, below $A$ and below $B$, and there are no segments with negative ordinate, so the distributed load should not be applied. The minimum value is therefore $M_{S,\min} = 10 \cdot 0 = 0$. The value $-\dfrac{40}{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the maximum produced by the force alone, changed by sign.
