The beam shown, of length $9\,\mathrm{m}$, has $A$ to $1\,\mathrm{m}$ roller support from end $E$ and $B$ to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ hinge from end $F$. What is the influence-line ordinate for $V_A$ when the unit load is at $F$?

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;A,1,0;B,7,0;F,9,0" aste="E-F" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera" quote="E,A,1 m,0.9;A,B,6 m,0.9;B,F,2 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with two overhangs"}

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{9}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

Müller–Breslau theorem removes roller support at $A$, and the beam becomes a mechanism that revolves about hinge $B$. The point $A$ stands $6\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$, so the unit upward shift of $A$ corresponds to the rotation $\varphi = -\dfrac{1}{6}$. With $\xi$ measured by $E$ you get
$$
\eta_{V_A}(\xi) = v(\xi) = -\frac{1}{6}\,(\xi - 7) = \frac{7 - \xi}{6} .
$$
In $F$ ($\xi = 9\,\mathrm{m}$) the ordinate equals $-\dfrac{2}{6} = -\dfrac{1}{3}$, so a load at $F$ produces a reaction $V_A$ directed downward. The value $-\dfrac{1}{6}$ is the ordinate of $\eta_{V_B}$ in $E$.
