On a simply supported beam of span $10\,\mathrm{m}$, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, a load train of two forces passes down, $P_1 = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the left and $P_2 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the right, at a fixed distance of $2\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the maximum bending moment at midspan?

- $95\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $125\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $105\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $100\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The influence line of the moment at midspan is a triangle with the $\dfrac{L}{4} = 2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ vertex and the $\dfrac{\xi}{2}$ and $\dfrac{10 - \xi}{2}$ branches. The maximum is with either force on the vertex, so the two positions are compared. With $P_1$ at midspan, $P_2$ is at $7\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ with ordinate $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$, and $M = 30 \cdot 2.5 + 20 \cdot 1.5 = 105\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. With $P_2$ at midspan, $P_1$ is at $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ with ordinate $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$, and $M = 30 \cdot 1.5 + 20 \cdot 2.5 = 95\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The maximum value is therefore $105\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the force majeure on the vertex.
