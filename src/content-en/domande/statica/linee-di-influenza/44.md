The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$. It may carry a uniform load $q = 10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ of variable length. What is the maximum value of reaction $V_A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,9,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9;B,C,3 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam"}

- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $37.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $60\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

$V_A$'s influence line is $\dfrac{6 - \xi}{6}$, positive on the span $AB$, where it drops from $1$ to $0$, and negative on the overhang, where it reaches $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ in $C$. The maximum is with the load on the span only, with positive area $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot 1 = 3\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore $V_{A,\max} = 10 \cdot 3 = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$. The $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 3 \cdot \left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right) = -\dfrac{3}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$ negative area is added with the load along the entire beam, and $V_A$ drops to $22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $37.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ adds the area of the overhang with the wrong sign.
