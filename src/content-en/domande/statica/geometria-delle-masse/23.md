The section in the figure is a rhombus with the horizontal diagonal of $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the vertical diagonal of $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its second moment of area about the horizontal diagonal?

::struttura{poligoni="-6,0 0,-4 6,0 0,4" tratteggi="-7.5,0 7.3,0" etichette="7.7,0.35,x" quote="-6 -4.8,6 -4.8,12 cm,0;9 -4,9 4,8 cm,0" riferimento=0 titolo="Rhombus with diagonals $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $8\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $42.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $128\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $512\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $288\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The horizontal diagonal divides the rhombus into two equal triangles, each with the base of $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ on the diagonal and the height of $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. The second moment of area of a triangle about the line that contains the base equals $\dfrac{b h^3}{12}$, and the second moment of area is additive, so
$$
I_x = 2 \cdot \frac{12 \cdot 4^3}{12}\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 128\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $42.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ uses the formula $\dfrac{b h^3}{36}$, valid for the centroidal axis of the triangle, which does not coincide with the base. The value $512\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the moment of the rectangle of $12 \times 8\,\mathrm{cm}$ circumscribed to the rhombus, and $288\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the moment about the vertical diagonal.
