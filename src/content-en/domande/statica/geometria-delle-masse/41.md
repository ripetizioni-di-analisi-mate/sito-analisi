The L-section in the figure has the two equal flanges, $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ long and $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ thick. How are its principal centroidal axes oriented relative to centroidal axes $x_G$ and $y_G$ parallel to the flanges?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 8,0 8,2 2,2 2,8 0,8" frecce="0,0,10,0,x;0,0,0,10,y" quote="0 0,8 0,8 cm,0.9;0 0,0 8,8 cm,-0.9;8 0,8 2,2 cm,0.8;0 8,2 8,2 cm,-0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="L-section with equal legs of length $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- Inclined at $45^\circ$, along the angle bisector between the legs and the line perpendicular to it
- Parallel to the legs, coinciding with $x_G$ and $y_G$
- Inclined at $30^\circ$ and $120^\circ$ to $x_G$
- Inclined at an angle that depends on the thickness of the legs

## Explanation

The bisector of the angle between the flanges is section symmetry axis, then passes through $G$. For the **product of inertia and symmetry theorem** the product of inertia about the bisector and its perpendicular for $G$ is zero, therefore these two axes are the principal axes, inclined of $45^\circ$ about $x_G$. With formulas you get to the same result. Symmetry exchanges the roles of $x$ and $y$, then $I_{x_G} = I_{y_G}$; instead $I_{x_G y_G} \neq 0$, because the area lies mainly in the second and fourth quadrants of the centroidal axes. With $I_{x_G} = I_{y_G}$ and $I_{x_G y_G} \neq 0$ the principal axes are bisectors, for each flange thickness.
