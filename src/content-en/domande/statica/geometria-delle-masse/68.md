About the $x_G$ and $y_G$ centroidal axes a section has $I_{x_G} = 500\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{y_G} = 300\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. About the center $\xi$ axis rotated by $30^\circ$ counterclockwise we measure $I_\xi = 420\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. What is the second moment of area about centroidal axis $\eta$ perpendicular to $\xi$?

- $420\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $300\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $380\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $80\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

Adding up the axis rotation formulas for $I_\xi$ and $I_\eta$ the terms in $\cos 2\alpha$ and $\sin 2\alpha$ cancel out, then $I_\xi + I_\eta = I_{x_G} + I_{y_G}$. The sum is the polar moment about $G$, which does not depend on the rotation of the axes. Therefore
$$
I_\eta = 500 + 300 - 420 = 380\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The data does not require knowing the product of inertia $I_{x_G y_G}$. The value $80\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the difference $I_{x_G} - I_\xi$.
