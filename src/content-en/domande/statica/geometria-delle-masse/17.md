A section has area $A = 40\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and first moment of area about the axis $x$ equal to $S_x = 200\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. What is its first moment of area about line $y = 8\,\mathrm{cm}$, taking upward distances as positive?

- $120\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $-120\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $520\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $-320\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

For the definition of centroid $y_G = \dfrac{S_x}{A} = \dfrac{200}{40}\,\mathrm{cm} = 5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The centroid is below the line, so its distance with sign from the line is $d_G = 5 - 8 = -3\,\mathrm{cm}$. By the theorem on the first moment of area about a line
$$
S_r = A\,d_G = 40 \cdot (-3)\,\mathrm{cm^3} = -120\,\mathrm{cm^3} .
$$
The value $120\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ has the wrong sign, $520\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ is achieved by adding $A \cdot 8$ to $S_x$ instead of subtracting it, and $-320\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ neglects $S_x$.
