A section has area $30\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Its central inertia ellipse has the semi-axis of $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ along the main axis $\xi$ and the semi-axis of $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ along the main axis $\eta$. What is the second moment of area about the line through the centroid inclined at $60^\circ$ to axis $\xi$?

- $210\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $390\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $120\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $480\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The semi-direct axis along $\xi$ is the radius of gyration about the axis $\eta$, and the semi-direct axis along $\eta$ is that about $\xi$. So $\rho_\eta = 4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $\rho_\xi = 2\,\mathrm{cm}$, that is $I_\eta = 30 \cdot 16 = 480\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_\xi = 30 \cdot 4 = 120\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. The $\xi$ and $\eta$ axes are main, therefore $I_{\xi\eta} = 0$, and the rotation formula of the axes with $\alpha = 60^\circ$ gives
$$
I_r = I_\xi \cos^2 60^\circ + I_\eta \sin^2 60^\circ = 120 \cdot \frac{1}{4} + 480 \cdot \frac{3}{4} = 390\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $210\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is obtained by associating each semi-axis at the moment about the axis along which it is directed; $120\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $480\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ are the principal moments.
