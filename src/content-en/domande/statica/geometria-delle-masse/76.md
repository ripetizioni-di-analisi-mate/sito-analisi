The section in the figure is bounded by the axis $x$, the line $x = 4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the $y = 2\sqrt{x}$ curve (lengths in $\mathrm{cm}$). What is the horizontal coordinate $x_G$ of the centroid?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4.00,4.00 3.75,3.87 3.50,3.74 3.25,3.61 3.00,3.46 2.75,3.32 2.50,3.16 2.25,3.00 2.00,2.83 1.75,2.65 1.50,2.45 1.25,2.24 1.00,2.00 0.75,1.73 0.50,1.41 0.25,1.00 0.00,0.00" frecce="0,0,5.5,0,x;0,0,0,5.5,y" quote="0 0,4 0,4 cm,0.8;4 0,4 4,4 cm,0.9" etichette="1.2,3.3,y = 2√x" riferimento=0 titolo="Region under curve $y = 2\sqrt{x}$ for $0 \leq x \leq 4\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $x_G = 2.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $x_G = 2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $x_G = 3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $x_G = 1.6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Divide the section into vertical strips $dx$ wide. The strip at horizontal coordinate $x$ has $2\sqrt{x}$ height, so area $dA = 2\sqrt{x}\,dx$. Therefore
$$
A = \int_0^4 2\sqrt{x}\,dx = \frac{4}{3}\,x^{\frac{3}{2}} \Big|_0^4 = \frac{32}{3}\,\mathrm{cm^2} ,
\qquad
S_y = \int_0^4 x \cdot 2\sqrt{x}\,dx = \frac{4}{5}\,x^{\frac{5}{2}} \Big|_0^4 = \frac{128}{5}\,\mathrm{cm^3} .
$$
For the definition of centroid $x_G = \dfrac{S_y}{A} = \dfrac{128}{5} \cdot \dfrac{3}{32}\,\mathrm{cm} = \dfrac{12}{5}\,\mathrm{cm} = 2.4\,\mathrm{cm}$. The centroid stands to the right of the middle of the base because the section is higher towards $x = 4\,\mathrm{cm}$.
