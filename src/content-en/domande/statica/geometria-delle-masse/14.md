The section shown is bounded by the $x$ axis and the parabola $y = 4 - \dfrac{x^2}{4}$, where $-4 \leq x \leq 4$ and lengths are in $\mathrm{cm}$. How far is the centroid from the base?

## Explanation

The axis $y$ is an axis of symmetry, hence $x_G = 0$. The area is
$$
A = \int_{-4}^{4} \left( 4 - \frac{x^2}{4} \right) dx = \frac{64}{3}\,\mathrm{cm^2} .
$$
The vertical strip at the horizontal coordinate $x$ is a rectangle of $f(x) = 4 - \dfrac{x^2}{4}$ height and $dx$ width, with the centroid at mid-height, so it contributes to $S_x$ with $\dfrac{f(x)^2}{2}\,dx$. Therefore
$$
S_x = \int_{-4}^{4} \frac{1}{2} \left( 4 - \frac{x^2}{4} \right)^2 dx = \frac{512}{15}\,\mathrm{cm^3} ,
\qquad
y_G = \frac{S_x}{A} = \frac{512}{15} \cdot \frac{3}{64}\,\mathrm{cm} = 1.6\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The value $1.33\,\mathrm{cm}$ is a third of the height, as for the triangle, and $2.4\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the distance of $G$ from the vertex of the parabola.
