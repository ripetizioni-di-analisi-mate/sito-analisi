The triangle in the figure has the vertices at points $(0, 0)$, $(9, 0)$ and $(6, 6)$, with the coordinates in $\mathrm{cm}$. What are the coordinates of the centroid?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 9,0 6,6" frecce="0,0,10.5,0,x;0,0,0,7.5,y" tratteggi="6,0 6,6" quote="0 0,6 0,6 cm,0.8;6 0,9 0,3 cm,0.8;9 0,9 6,6 cm,1" riferimento=0 titolo="Triangle with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(9, 0)$, and $(6, 6)$"}

- $G = (5;\ 2)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $G = (4.5;\ 3)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $G = (5;\ 3)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $G = (6;\ 2)\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The side on the axis $x$ has the corresponding altitude of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$, and the centroid is one third of the height on that side, hence $y_G = 2\,\mathrm{cm}$. For the horizontal coordinate, the triangle with the line $x = 6$ is divided into two rectangle triangles of height $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. The first has the horizontal leg from $0$ to $6$, area $18\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid to one third of the leg from the vertical side, i.e. in $x = 4$; the second has the leg from $6$ to $9$, area $9\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid in $x = 7$. Therefore
$$
x_G = \frac{18 \cdot 4 + 9 \cdot 7}{27}\,\mathrm{cm} = \frac{135}{27}\,\mathrm{cm} = 5\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The result coincides with the average of the vertices coordinates, $\left( \dfrac{0 + 9 + 6}{3}, \dfrac{0 + 0 + 6}{3} \right)$, because the centroid is the meeting point of the medians.
