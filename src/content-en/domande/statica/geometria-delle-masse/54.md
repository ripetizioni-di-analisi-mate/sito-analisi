The section in the figure is a square on the $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ side with the edge at the top right blunt: the right triangle is removed with the legs of $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ along the two sides. What are the coordinates of the centroid relative to the axes shown?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,3 3,6 0,6" tratteggi="3,6 6,6 6,3" frecce="0,0,7.8,0,x;0,0,0,7.8,y" quote="0 0,6 0,6 cm,0.8;0 0,0 6,6 cm,-0.8;3 6,6 6,3 cm,-0.8;6 3,6 6,3 cm,0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="Square of side $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ with a beveled corner. The removed triangle is shown dashed"}

- $x_G = y_G = 3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $x_G = y_G = 2.33\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $x_G = y_G = 2.71\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $x_G = y_G = 3.22\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The bisector of the first quadrant is an axis of symmetry, hence $x_G = y_G$. The section is the full square, with area $36\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid $(3, 3)$, minus the removed triangle, with area $\dfrac{3 \cdot 3}{2} = 4.5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The centroid of the triangle is $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ from each of the two legs, which stand on the $x = 6$ and $y = 6$ lines, so it is the point $(5, 5)$. Treating the triangle as a part of negative area
$$
x_G = \frac{36 \cdot 3 - 4.5 \cdot 5}{36 - 4.5}\,\mathrm{cm} = \frac{85.5}{31.5}\,\mathrm{cm} = \frac{19}{7}\,\mathrm{cm} = 2.71\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The value $3.22\,\mathrm{cm}$ is obtained by adding the triangle instead of subtracting it.
