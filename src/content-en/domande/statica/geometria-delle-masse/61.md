- $875.63\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $142.25\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $254.47\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

About the diameter the semicircle has moment $\dfrac{\pi r^4}{8} = 162\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 508.94\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, half that of the whole circle. The diameter is not a centroidal axis, because the centroid is $d = \dfrac{4r}{3\pi} = \dfrac{8}{\pi}\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the diameter, and the area is $A = 18\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. For the **parallel-axis theorem** the parallel-axis term is subtracted, because it goes from the non-centroidal axis to the centroidal one:
$$
I_{x_G} = 162\pi - 18\pi \cdot \frac{64}{\pi^2} = 162\pi - \frac{1152}{\pi} = 508.94 - 366.69 = 142.25\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $875.63\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is obtained by adding up the parallel-axis term instead of subtracting it.
