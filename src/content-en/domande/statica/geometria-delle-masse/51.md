A steel beam has the I-section shown, with overall height $20\,\mathrm{cm}$, flanges measuring $10 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$, and web thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. A cover plate measuring $10 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ is welded to the upper flange. How far is the centroid of the reinforced section from its bottom edge?

::struttura{poligoni="-5,0 5,0 5,2 1,2 1,18 5,18 5,20 -5,20 -5,18 -1,18 -1,2 -5,2;-5,20 5,20 5,22 -5,22" frecce="-6.5,0,7,0,x;0,-0.8,0,24,y" quote="-5 0,5 0,10 cm,1.2;5 0,5 20,20 cm,1.4;5 20,5 22,2 cm,0.9;-5 0,-5 2,2 cm,-0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="I-section with a reinforcing plate of dimensions $10 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ on its upper flange"}

- $y_G = 15.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $y_G = 12.39\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $y_G = 13.67\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $y_G = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The double-T profile has two axes of symmetry, so its centroid is the center, at elevation $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. Its area is $2 \cdot 20 + 2 \cdot 16 = 72\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The plate has area $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid at elevation $21\,\mathrm{cm}$. For the centroid formula of a composite figure, with the profile treated as one part,
$$
y_G = \frac{72 \cdot 10 + 20 \cdot 21}{72 + 20}\,\mathrm{cm} = \frac{1140}{92}\,\mathrm{cm} = 12.39\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The value $15.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the average of the two heights without the weight of the areas; the value $13.67\,\mathrm{cm}$ is obtained by forgetting the area of the web.
