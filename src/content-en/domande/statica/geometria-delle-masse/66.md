A column has the section in the figure, formed by two equal plates of $2 \times 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ arranged vertically, symmetrical with respect to the axis $y_G$, with $d$ wheelbases between their centroids. For what value of $d$ are the centroidal second moments of area $I_{x_G}$ and $I_{y_G}$ equal?

::struttura{poligoni="-3.83,-5 -1.83,-5 -1.83,5 -3.83,5;1.83,-5 3.83,-5 3.83,5 1.83,5" frecce="-6,0,6.5,0,x_G;0,-6.3,0,5.8,y_G" quote="-2.83 5,2.83 5,d,-1.9;3.83 -5,3.83 5,10 cm,1;1.83 -5,3.83 -5,2 cm,0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="Two plates of dimensions $2 \times 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ with center-to-center spacing $d$"}

- $d = 4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $d = 5.66\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $d = 8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $d = 11.31\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Compared to $x_G$ both plates have the centroid on the axis, therefore $I_{x_G} = 2 \cdot \dfrac{2 \cdot 10^3}{12} = 333.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, independent of $d$. About $y_G$ each plate has its own $\dfrac{10 \cdot 2^3}{12} = 6.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and centroid moment at $\dfrac{d}{2}$ from the axis, then for the **parallel-axis theorem** $I_{y_G} = 2\left( 6.67 + 20 \cdot \dfrac{d^2}{4} \right) = 13.33 + 10\,d^2$. Equating the Two Moments
$$
13.33 + 10\,d^2 = 333.33 ,
\qquad
d^2 = 32 ,
\qquad
d = 5.66\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The value $11.31\,\mathrm{cm}$ is double, i.e. the distance of each centroid from the axis exchanged with the wheelbase.
