The isosceles triangle in the figure has the base of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the height of $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its second moment of area about its vertical axis of symmetry $y$?

::struttura{poligoni="-3,0 3,0 0,8" frecce="-4.5,0,4.5,0,x;0,-0.8,0,9.8,y" quote="-3 0,3 0,6 cm,1;3 0,3 8,8 cm,1.3" riferimento=0 titolo="Isosceles triangle of base $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $8\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $144\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $36\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $72\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $85.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The axis of symmetry divides the triangle into two equal rectangles, each with a leg of $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ on the axis $y$ and the other of $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. For each of them the axis $y$ contains one side, the leg of $8\,\mathrm{cm}$, and the distance of the opposite vertex from that side is $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. Therefore the formula of the moment about the base gives, with base $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $3\,\mathrm{cm}$, $\dfrac{8 \cdot 3^3}{12} = 18\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. For Additiveness
$$
I_y = 2 \cdot 18 = 36\,\mathrm{cm^4} ,
$$
which coincides with $\dfrac{h b^3}{48}$. The value $144\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the moment $\dfrac{h b^3}{12}$ of the rectangle of $6 \times 8\,\mathrm{cm}$ about the same axis.
