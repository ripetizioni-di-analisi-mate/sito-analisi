The rectangle in the figure is $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ wide and $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ high, and its base stands $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the axis $x$. What is the second moment of area $I_x$?

::struttura{poligoni="0,2 6,2 6,6 0,6" frecce="-1,0,8,0,x" quote="0 6,6 6,6 cm,-0.8;6 2,6 6,4 cm,0.9;7 0,7 2,2 cm,0.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangle of dimensions $6 \times 4\,\mathrm{cm}$ with its base a distance $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ from axis $x$"}

- $128\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $224\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $416\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $384\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The centroid of the rectangle is at elevation $y_G = 2 + 2 = 4\,\mathrm{cm}$, and the moment about the horizontal centroidal axis is $I_{x_G} = \dfrac{6 \cdot 4^3}{12} = 32\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. For the **parallel-axis theorem**, with the $A = 24\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ area,
$$
I_x = I_{x_G} + A\,y_G^2 = 32 + 24 \cdot 4^2 = 416\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $128\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is obtained using the distance $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ of the base instead of that of the centroid, $224\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ carrying on the axis $x$ the moment about the base, which is not centroidal, and $384\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ forgetting $I_{x_G}$.
