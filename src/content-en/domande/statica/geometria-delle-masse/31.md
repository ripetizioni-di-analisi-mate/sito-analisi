A triangle has the base $b$ on the axis $x$ and the vertex opposite the $h > 0$ elevation. Divide the triangle into horizontal strips of $dy$ height. Which integral gives the second moment of area $I_x$ of the triangle about axis $x$?

- $\displaystyle\int_0^h y\,b\left(1 - \frac{y}{h}\right) dy$
- $\displaystyle\int_0^h y^2\,b\,\frac{y}{h}\,dy$
- $\displaystyle\int_0^h y^2\,b\left(1 - \frac{y}{h}\right) dy$
- $\displaystyle\int_0^h y^2\,b\,dy$

## Explanation

The second moment of area about the axis $x$ is $I_x = \displaystyle\int_A y^2\,dA$. All strip points at $y$ elevation have the same $y$ distance from the axis $x$, and the strip area is $dA = b(y)\,dy$. For the similarity of triangles the width of the strip decreases linearly from the base to the vertex, then $b(y) = b\left(1 - \dfrac{y}{h}\right)$. Therefore
$$
I_x = \int_0^h y^2\,b\left(1 - \frac{y}{h}\right) dy = \frac{b h^3}{12} .
$$
The integral with $y$ in the first degree is the first moment of area $S_x$; the one with the $b\,\dfrac{y}{h}$ width describes a triangle with the vertex on the axis $x$, and the one with the constant $b$ width describes a rectangle.
