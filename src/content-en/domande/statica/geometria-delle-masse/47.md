The T-section in the figure has the flange of $8 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the web of $2 \times 8\,\mathrm{cm}$, and its centroid is $6.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the bottom edge. What is the first moment of area about centroidal axis $x_G$ of the portion of the section above $x_G$?

::struttura{poligoni="-1,0 1,0 1,8 4,8 4,10 -4,10 -4,8 -1,8" tratteggi="-5.5,6.5 5.5,6.5" etichette="5.9,6.8,x_G" quote="-4 10,4 10,8 cm,-1.6;-1 0,1 0,2 cm,1;4 0,4 10,10 cm,1.3;-4 8,-4 10,2 cm,-0.8;-5.5 0,-5.5 6.5,6.5 cm,0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="T-section with centroidal axis $x_G$ a distance $6.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ above its bottom edge"}

- $42.25\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $26.25\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $2.25\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $0\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

The part above $x_G$ is formed by the flange and the section of web between the $6.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ altitudes. The flange has $16\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ area and its centroid is $9 - 6.5 = 2.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ from $x_G$, so it contributes with $16 \cdot 2.5 = 40\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. The web segment is a rectangle of $2 \times 1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ with the centroid at $0.75\,\mathrm{cm}$ from $x_G$, so it contributes with $3 \cdot 0.75 = 2.25\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. For the additiveness of the first moment of area
$$
S = 40 + 2.25 = 42.25\,\mathrm{cm^3} .
$$
As a control, the part below $x_G$ is the web rectangle of $2 \times 6.5\,\mathrm{cm}$, with first moment of area $13 \cdot (-3.25) = -42.25\,\mathrm{cm^3}$; the sum is zero, as it must be with respect to a centroidal axis. The value $26.25\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ is obtained by measuring the distance of the flange from its bottom edge instead of its centroid.
