The right triangle shown has horizontal leg $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and vertical leg $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ along the axes. What is its product of inertia $I_{xy}$ about axes $x$ and $y$?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 12,0 0,6" frecce="0,0,13.5,0,x;0,0,0,7.5,y" quote="0 0,12 0,12 cm,0.9;0 0,0 6,6 cm,-0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="Right triangle with legs of lengths $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ along the axes"}

- $-72\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $72\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $1296\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $216\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The triangle has area $36\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid $G = \left( \dfrac{12}{3}, \dfrac{6}{3} \right) = (4, 2)\,\mathrm{cm}$. Compared to centroidal axes the right-angled right triangle at the bottom left has $I_{x_G y_G} = -\dfrac{b^2 h^2}{72} = -\dfrac{144 \cdot 36}{72} = -72\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. For the **parallel-axis theorem**
$$
I_{xy} = I_{x_G y_G} + A\,x_G\,y_G = -72 + 36 \cdot 4 \cdot 2 = 216\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $-72\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the product of inertia centroidal, without the transport, and $1296\,\mathrm{cm^4} = \dfrac{b^2 h^2}{4}$ is that of the rectangle of sides $12$ and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$.
