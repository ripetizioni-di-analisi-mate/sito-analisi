The T-section shown has a flange of dimensions $8 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a web of dimensions $2 \times 10\,\mathrm{cm}$. About which of the following horizontal lines is its second moment of area smallest?

::struttura{poligoni="-1,0 1,0 1,10 4,10 4,12 -4,12 -4,10 -1,10" quote="-4 12,4 12,8 cm,-0.8;-1 0,1 0,2 cm,0.9;4 0,4 12,12 cm,1.2;-4 10,-4 12,2 cm,-0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="T-section with flange dimensions $8 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and web dimensions $2 \times 10\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- The line at midheight of the section
- The line along the bottom edge
- The line through the centroid
- The line separating the web from the flange

## Explanation

For the **parallel-axis theorem** the moment about a horizontal line at distance $d$ from the centroid is $I_{x_G} + A\,d^2$, so it is minimum for $d = 0$, i.e. for the line for the centroid. The T centroid stands at the elevation
$$
y_G = \frac{20 \cdot 5 + 16 \cdot 11}{36}\,\mathrm{cm} = 7.67\,\mathrm{cm} ,
$$
because the web has $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ area with centroid to $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the flange has $16\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ area with centroid to $11\,\mathrm{cm}$. The mid-height line is at elevation $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the one between web and flange at elevation $10\,\mathrm{cm}$, so neither of them passes through $G$.
