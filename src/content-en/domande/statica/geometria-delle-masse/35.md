A symmetric section consists of a web and two identical flanges. Its total horizontal second moment is $780\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, with $180\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ coming from the web about the same axis. Each flange has area $12\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and its centroid lies $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis. What is the centroidal second moment of one flange?

- $192\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $300\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $108\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $408\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

Each flange contributes $\dfrac{780-180}{2}=300\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ about the common axis. Subtracting the parallel-axis term $12\cdot4^2=192\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ gives the value about its own centroidal axis, $108\,\mathrm{cm^4}$.
