The cross section in the figure is formed by a vertical rectangle of $2 \times 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and two horizontal arms of $4 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ attached at mid-height. What is the second moment of area $I_{x_G}$ about the horizontal centroidal axis?

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-5 1,-5 1,-1 5,-1 5,1 1,1 1,5 -1,5 -1,1 -5,1 -5,-1 -1,-1" quote="-1 5,1 5,2 cm,-0.8;-1 -5,-1 5,10 cm,-4.8;1 1,5 1,4 cm,-0.8;5 -1,5 1,2 cm,0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="Cruciform section with vertical rectangle $2 \times 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and arms of dimensions $4 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $166.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $188\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $172\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $344\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The cross has two axes of symmetry, so $G$ is the center, and the axis $x_G$ passes through the centroids of all three parts. There are no parallel-axis terms, so
$$
I_{x_G} = \frac{2 \cdot 10^3}{12} + 2 \cdot \frac{4 \cdot 2^3}{12} = 166.67 + 5.33 = 172\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $166.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ neglects the arms, $188\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is obtained by exchanging base and height of the arms, and $344\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the polar moment $I_{x_G} + I_{y_G}$.
