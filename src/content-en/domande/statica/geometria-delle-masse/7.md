The U section shown consists of a horizontal base of $12 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and two vertical flanges of $2 \times 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ placed on top of its ends, so it is $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ high; the centroid lies $4.75\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the bottom edge. What is the moment of inertia $I_{x_G}$ about the horizontal centroidal axis?

::struttura{poligoni="-6,0 6,0 6,12 4,12 4,2 -4,2 -4,12 -6,12" frecce="-7.5,0,7.5,0,x;0,-0.8,0,13.4,y" quote="-6 0,6 0,12 cm,1.2;6 0,6 12,12 cm,1.4;-6 12,-4 12,2 cm,-0.8;-6 0,-6 2,2 cm,-1;4 2,4 12,10 cm,-0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="U section $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ high"}

- $881.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $341.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $540\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $981.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The base has area $24\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid at height $1\,\mathrm{cm}$, and each flange has area $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid at height $2 + 5 = 7\,\mathrm{cm}$. The distances from the axis $x_G$ are therefore $3.75\,\mathrm{cm}$ for the base and $2.25\,\mathrm{cm}$ for the flanges. By the **parallel-axis theorem** applied to each rectangle
$$
\begin{aligned}
I_{x_G} &= \left( \frac{12 \cdot 2^3}{12} + 24 \cdot 3.75^2 \right) + 2 \left( \frac{2 \cdot 10^3}{12} + 20 \cdot 2.25^2 \right) \\[6pt]
&= 345.5 + 535.83 = 881.33\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
\end{aligned}
$$
The value $341.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ contains only the moments about the parts' own axes, $540\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ only the parallel-axis terms, and $981.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ measures the distances from mid-height instead of from the centroid.
