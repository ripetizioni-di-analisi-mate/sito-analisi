The centroid of the I-section shown lies $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the bottom edge. The bottom and top flanges have areas of $32$ and $16\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, with centroids at heights of $1$ and $23\,\mathrm{cm}$, respectively. Which flange contributes the larger parallel-axis term to the horizontal second moment, and by how much?

- Top, by $112\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- Bottom, by $112\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- Top, by $88\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- Bottom, by $288\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The offset appears squared in $Ad^2$. The bottom flange contributes $32\cdot9^2=2592\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. The top flange contributes $16\cdot13^2=2704\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, a difference of $112\,\mathrm{cm^4}$.
