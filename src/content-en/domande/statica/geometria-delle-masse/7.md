The asymmetric I-section shown has a bottom flange measuring $16 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$, a web measuring $2 \times 20\,\mathrm{cm}$, and a top flange measuring $8 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Its centroid lies $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the bottom edge. What is the second moment of area $I_{x_G}$ about the horizontal centroidal axis?

::struttura{poligoni="-8,0 8,0 8,2 1,2 1,22 4,22 4,24 -4,24 -4,22 -1,22 -1,2 -8,2" frecce="-9.5,0,9.5,0,x;0,-0.8,0,25.5,y" quote="-8 0,8 0,16 cm,1.2;-4 24,4 24,8 cm,-3.2;8 0,8 24,24 cm,1.6;1 2,1 22,20 cm,3;-8 0,-8 2,2 cm,-1;-4 22,-4 24,2 cm,-1" riferimento=0 titolo="Asymmetric I-section of height $24\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $6805.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $5472\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $7157.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $1349.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The bottom flange, web and top flange centroids are at $1$, $12$ and $23\,\mathrm{cm}$ altitudes, so they are $-9$, $2$ and $13\,\mathrm{cm}$ away from the axis $x_G$. For the **parallel-axis theorem** applied to each rectangle
$$
\begin{aligned}
I_{x_G} &= \left( \frac{16 \cdot 2^3}{12} + 32 \cdot 9^2 \right) + \left( \frac{2 \cdot 20^3}{12} + 40 \cdot 2^2 \right) + \left( \frac{8 \cdot 2^3}{12} + 16 \cdot 13^2 \right) \\[6pt]
&= 2602.67 + 1493.33 + 2709.33 = 6805.33\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
\end{aligned}
$$
The value $5472\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is obtained by forgetting the proper moment of the web, $7157.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ by measuring the distances from mid-height instead of $G$, and $1349.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ contains only the proper moments.
