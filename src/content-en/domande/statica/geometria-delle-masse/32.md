The tube in the figure has the outer diameter of $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the inner diameter of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$, and the line $t$ is tangent to the outer circumference. What is the second moment of area of the tube section about $t$?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,5;0,0,3,foro" tratteggi="-7,-5 7,-5" etichette="7.4,-4.7,t" punti="0,0,G,baricentro" quote="-5 0,5 0,10 cm,-6.2;-3 0,3 0,6 cm,-1.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Tube section with diameters $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and line $t$ tangent to the outside surface"}

- $1683.89\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $427.26\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $1231.50\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $2390.75\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The annulus with $R = 5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $r = 3\,\mathrm{cm}$ rays has the second moment of area about a diameter
$$
I_{x_G} = \frac{\pi\left(R^4 - r^4\right)}{4} = \frac{\pi\,(625 - 81)}{4}\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 136\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}
$$
and area $A = \pi\left(R^2 - r^2\right) = 16\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The line $t$ is parallel to the horizontal diameter and is $R = 5\,\mathrm{cm}$ away from the centroid, so for the **parallel-axis theorem**
$$
I_t = 136\pi + 16\pi \cdot 5^2 = 536\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 1683.89\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $427.26\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the $I_{x_G}$, $1231.50\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ alone uses the mean radius of $4\,\mathrm{cm}$, and $2390.75\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is obtained by removing the moment of the hole from the moment of the solid circle about $t$ about its diameter, without the parallel-axis term.
