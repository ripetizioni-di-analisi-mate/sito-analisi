A rectangle wide $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ along $x$ and tall $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ along $y$ has a vertex in the origin and sides on the positive axes. What is its polar moment of area $I_p$ about the origin?

- $67.5\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $270\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $54\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $216\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The polar moment about the origin is the sum of the second moments of area about the two axes, $I_p = I_x + I_y$. The $x$ and $y$ axes contain two sides of the rectangle, so for each equals the formula of the moment about the base, $\dfrac{b h^3}{3}$:
$$
I_x = \frac{6 \cdot 3^3}{3} = 54\,\mathrm{cm^4} ,
\qquad
I_y = \frac{3 \cdot 6^3}{3} = 216\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
Therefore $I_p = 54 + 216 = 270\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. The value $67.5\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the polar moment about the centroid, $\dfrac{A\,(b^2 + h^2)}{12}$.
