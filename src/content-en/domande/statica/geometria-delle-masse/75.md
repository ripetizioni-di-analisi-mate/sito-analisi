The I-section shown has overall height $16\,\mathrm{cm}$, flanges measuring $8 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$, and web thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Its centroidal second moments of area are $I_{x_G} = 1866.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{y_G} = 178.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. What are the direction and length of the semimajor axis of the centroidal ellipse of inertia?

::struttura{poligoni="-4,-8 4,-8 4,-6 1,-6 1,6 4,6 4,8 -4,8 -4,6 -1,6 -1,-6 -4,-6" frecce="-4.5,0,6,0,x_G;0,-9,0,9.8,y_G" quote="-4 -8,4 -8,8 cm,1;-4 8,-4 -8,16 cm,2.2;-4 6,-4 8,2 cm,-0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="I-section of height $16\,\mathrm{cm}$ with flanges of dimensions $8 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- Along $x_G$, along $5.77\,\mathrm{cm}$
- Along $y_G$, along $1.79\,\mathrm{cm}$
- Along $y_G$, along $5.77\,\mathrm{cm}$
- Along $x_G$, along $1.79\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The section has two axes of symmetry, so $x_G$ and $y_G$ are the principal axes. The area is $2 \cdot 16 + 12 \cdot 2 = 56\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, so
$$
\rho_{x_G} = \sqrt{\frac{1866.67}{56}}\,\mathrm{cm} = 5.77\,\mathrm{cm} ,
\qquad
\rho_{y_G} = \sqrt{\frac{178.67}{56}}\,\mathrm{cm} = 1.79\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
In the central ellipse, the semi-direct axis as $y_G$ equals $\rho_{x_G}$ and the direct axis as $x_G$ equals $\rho_{y_G}$. Therefore the semimajor axis is **$y_G$ long** and measures $5.77\,\mathrm{cm}$, and the ellipse is stretched in the direction in which the section takes the area away from the axis $x_G$.
