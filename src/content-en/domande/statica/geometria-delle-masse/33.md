The L-section shown has a horizontal leg of length $16\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a vertical leg of height $14\,\mathrm{cm}$, both of thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Its centroid is $G = (5, 4)\,\mathrm{cm}$ relative to the axes shown. What is the product of inertia $I_{x_G y_G}$?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 16,0 16,2 2,2 2,14 0,14" frecce="0,0,18,0,x;0,0,0,16,y" quote="0 0,16 0,16 cm,0.9;0 0,0 14,14 cm,-0.9;16 0,16 2,2 cm,0.8;0 14,2 14,2 cm,-0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="L-section with leg lengths $16\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ and thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $-672\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $672\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $0\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $448\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

Divide the section into the horizontal flange of $16 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$, with area $32\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid $(8, 1)$, and into the vertical part of $2 \times 12\,\mathrm{cm}$, with area $24\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid $(1, 8)$. About $G$ the two centroids have $(3, -3)$ and $(-4, 4)$ coordinates, and each rectangle has zero product of inertia about its own centroidal axes. For the **parallel-axis theorem**
$$
I_{x_G y_G} = 32 \cdot 3 \cdot (-3) + 24 \cdot (-4) \cdot 4 = -288 - 384 = -672\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The sign is negative because the area lies mainly in the second and fourth quadrants of the centroidal axes. The value $448\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the product of inertia about the $x$ and $y$ axes along the outer edges.
