The channel section shown has overall height $16\,\mathrm{cm}$. The web and flanges are $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ thick, and each flange has overall width $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the second moment of area $I_{x_G}$ about the horizontal centroidal axis?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,2 2,2 2,14 6,14 6,16 0,16" frecce="0,0,8,0,x;0,0,0,18,y" quote="0 0,6 0,6 cm,1;0 0,0 16,16 cm,-1;0 16,2 16,2 cm,-0.9;6 0,6 2,2 cm,0.8;6 14,6 16,2 cm,0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="Channel section of height $16\,\mathrm{cm}$ with web and flange thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $128\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $1472\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $1184\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $2048\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The horizontal line at mid-height is an axis of symmetry, so the axis $x_G$ stands at elevation $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. The section is the rectangle full of $6 \times 16\,\mathrm{cm}$ minus the void of $4 \times 12\,\mathrm{cm}$ between the flanges, and the centroid of both lies on the axis $x_G$. Therefore there are no parallel-axis terms and
$$
I_{x_G} = \frac{6 \cdot 16^3}{12} - \frac{4 \cdot 12^3}{12} = 2048 - 576 = 1472\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $1184\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is obtained by giving the cutout the $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ width instead of $4\,\mathrm{cm}$, $2048\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ neglecting the cutout, and $128\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the moment about the vertical $y_G$ axis.
