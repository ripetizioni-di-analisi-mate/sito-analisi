A triangle has the base of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ on the axis $x$ and the height of $9\,\mathrm{cm}$, with the vertex opposite the base above the axis. What is its first moment of area $S_x$ about the $x$ axis?

- $243\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $162\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $81\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $121.5\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

The centroid of a triangle is one third of the height relative to that side on each side, then $y_G = \dfrac{9}{3} = 3\,\mathrm{cm}$. The triangle area is $A = \dfrac{6 \cdot 9}{2} = 27\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. For the definition of centroid $S_x = A\,y_G$, therefore
$$
S_x = 27 \cdot 3 = 81\,\mathrm{cm^3} ,
$$
which coincides with $\dfrac{b h^2}{6}$. The value $243\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ is $\dfrac{b h^2}{2}$, i.e. the first moment of area of the rectangle with the same base and height; the value $162\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ is obtained by placing the centroid two thirds of the height from the base.
