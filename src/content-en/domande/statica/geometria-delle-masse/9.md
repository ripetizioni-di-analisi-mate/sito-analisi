A tube has outside diameter $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and inside diameter $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the radius of gyration of the section about a diameter?

- $5.10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2.24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3.61\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7.21\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The section is a annulus of $R = 6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $r = 4\,\mathrm{cm}$ rays. The area is $A = \pi(R^2 - r^2) = 20\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, and the second moment of area about a diameter is $I = \dfrac{\pi(R^4 - r^4)}{4} = \dfrac{\pi\,(1296 - 256)}{4} = 260\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. For the definition of radius of gyration
$$
\rho = \sqrt{\frac{I}{A}} = \sqrt{\frac{260\pi}{20\pi}}\,\mathrm{cm} = \sqrt{13}\,\mathrm{cm} = 3.61\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The value $5.10\,\mathrm{cm}$ is obtained with the polar moment instead of the one about the diameter, $7.21\,\mathrm{cm}$ using the diameters instead of the rays, and $2.24\,\mathrm{cm}$ with $\sqrt{\dfrac{R^2 - r^2}{4}}$ instead of $\sqrt{\dfrac{R^2 + r^2}{4}}$.
