A semicircle of radius $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ has the diameter on the axis $y$, the center in the origin and stands all on the side of positive $x$. What is its first moment of area $S_y$ about the $y$ axis?

- $0\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $9\pi\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $21.21\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

The centroid of a semicircle lies on the axis of symmetry, at the distance $\dfrac{4r}{3\pi}$ from the diameter. Here the symmetry axis is the axis $x$ and the diameter lies on the axis $y$, hence $x_G = \dfrac{4 \cdot 3}{3\pi} = \dfrac{4}{\pi}\,\mathrm{cm}$. The area is $A = \dfrac{\pi \cdot 3^2}{2} = \dfrac{9\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. For the definition of centroid $S_y = A\,x_G$, therefore
$$
S_y = \frac{9\pi}{2} \cdot \frac{4}{\pi} = 18\,\mathrm{cm^3} ,
$$
which coincides with $\dfrac{2r^3}{3}$. The value $0\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ is the first moment of area about the $x$ symmetry axis, and the value $21.21\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ is obtained by placing the centroid in the middle of the radius.
