The square in the figure has the side of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$, a vertex in the origin and the sides on the positive axes. The line $r$ has equation $y = -x$ and passes through the origin. What is the first moment of area of the square about $r$, taking distances as positive on the side containing the square?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,6 0,6" frecce="-2.5,0,8,0,x;0,-2.5,0,8,y" tratteggi="-2.5,2.5 2.5,-2.5" etichette="2.8,-2.9,r" quote="0 6,6 6,6 cm,-0.8;6 0,6 6,6 cm,0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="Square of side $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and line $r$ with equation $y = -x$"}

- $108\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $216\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $305.47\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $152.74\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

For the first moment of area theorem about a straight line, $S_r = A\,d_G$, where $d_G$ is the centroid sign distance from $r$. The centroid of the square is $G = (3, 3)\,\mathrm{cm}$, and the distance of a point $(x_0, y_0)$ from the line $x + y = 0$ is $\dfrac{|x_0 + y_0|}{\sqrt{2}}$, so
$$
d_G = \frac{3 + 3}{\sqrt{2}}\,\mathrm{cm} = 3\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{cm} = 4.24\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
Therefore $S_r = 36 \cdot 3\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{cm^3} = 108\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{cm^3} = 152.74\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. The value $108\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ uses the distance of $G$ from one of the axes, $216\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ the length of the side and $305.47\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ the length of the diagonal instead of the distance $d_G$.
