A flat figure is all in the fourth quadrant, i.e. in the region with $x > 0$ and $y < 0$. Which statement about its moments of area about axes $x$ and $y$ is true?

- $S_x > 0$, $S_y < 0$, and $I_{xy} < 0$
- $S_x < 0$, $S_y > 0$, and $I_{xy} > 0$
- $S_x < 0$, $S_y > 0$, and $I_{xy} < 0$
- $S_x < 0$, $S_y > 0$, and $I_x < 0$

## Explanation

In each element of the figure $y < 0$ and $x > 0$. Therefore $S_x = \displaystyle\int_A y\,dA$ is an integral of a negative function and is negative, while $S_y = \displaystyle\int_A x\,dA$ is positive. The product $x y$ is negative in each element, so $I_{xy} = \displaystyle\int_A x y\,dA$ is negative too. The second moment of area $I_x = \displaystyle\int_A y^2\,dA$ integrates a square and is always positive, whatever the position of the figure.
