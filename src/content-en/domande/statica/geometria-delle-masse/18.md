For which of the following sections can the centroid be located without calculation at the intersection of two axes of symmetry?

- A T-section
- An I-section with equal flanges
- An L-section with equal legs
- A channel section

## Explanation

The I-section with equal flanges is symmetrical with respect to the axis of the web and with respect to the horizontal line at mid-height. For the **centroid theorem of a symmetrical figure** the centroid is the intersection point of the two axes, that is, the center of the section. The T-section and the C-section have only one axis of symmetry, so a centroid coordinate must be calculated. The L-section with equal flanges has as its only axis of symmetry the bisector of the angle between the flanges; the centroid lies on that bisector, and its position along it must be calculated.
