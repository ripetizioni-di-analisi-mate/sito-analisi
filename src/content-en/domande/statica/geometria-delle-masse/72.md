The L-section of a compressed rod has area $40\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and, about the centroidal axes parallel to the flanges, $I_{x_G} = 533.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, $I_{y_G} = 333.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{x_G y_G} = -240\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. What is the minimum radius of gyration of the section, used in the member slenderness ratio?

- $2.89\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3.65\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2.08\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4.16\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The minimum radius of gyration corresponds to the minimum second moment of area between all centroidal axes, i.e. at the main moment $I_2$. With $C = \dfrac{533.33 + 333.33}{2} = 433.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $D = \dfrac{533.33 - 333.33}{2} = 100\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ the formula of the principal moments gives
$$
I_2 = 433.33 - \sqrt{100^2 + 240^2} = 433.33 - 260 = 173.33\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
Therefore $\rho_{\min} = \sqrt{\dfrac{173.33}{40}}\,\mathrm{cm} = 2.08\,\mathrm{cm}$. The value $2.89\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the radius of gyration about $y_G$, lower among those than the parallel axes to the flanges but greater than the minimum, because the axis $y_G$ is not main.
