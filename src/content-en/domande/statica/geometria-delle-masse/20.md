The section in the figure is formed by a square on the $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ side and an isosceles triangle resting on its upper side, with base $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. How far is the centroid from the bottom edge?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,6 3,18 0,6" tratteggi="0,6 6,6" quote="0 0,6 0,6 cm,0.9;6 0,6 6,6 cm,0.9;6 6,6 18,12 cm,0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="Square of side $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ with an isosceles triangle of base $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $12\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $y_G = 7.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $y_G = 6.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $y_G = 8.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $y_G = 7.67\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The vertical axis for the vertex of the triangle is an axis of symmetry, so the centroid lies on it. The square has area $36\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid at elevation $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. The triangle has area $\dfrac{6 \cdot 12}{2} = 36\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, and its centroid is one third of the height from its base, i.e. it stands at elevation $6 + 4 = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$. Therefore
$$
y_G = \frac{36 \cdot 3 + 36 \cdot 10}{36 + 36}\,\mathrm{cm} = \frac{468}{72}\,\mathrm{cm} = 6.5\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The value $7.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ is obtained by placing the triangle centroid at half its height, $8.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ by placing it two thirds of the height from the base, and $7.67\,\mathrm{cm}$ by giving the triangle the area $b h$.
