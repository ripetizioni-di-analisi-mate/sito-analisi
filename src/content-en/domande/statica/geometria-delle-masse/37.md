About the $x_G$ and $y_G$ centroidal axes a section has $I_{x_G} = 500\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, $I_{y_G} = 300\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{x_G y_G} = 120\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. What is the second moment of area about centroidal axis $\xi$ rotated counterclockwise by $45^\circ$ from $x_G$?

- $520\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $400\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $280\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $385.86\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

For $\alpha = 45^\circ$ you have $2\alpha = 90^\circ$, then $\cos 2\alpha = 0$ and $\sin 2\alpha = 1$. For axis rotation formula
$$
I_\xi = \frac{500 + 300}{2} + \frac{500 - 300}{2} \cdot 0 - 120 \cdot 1 = 280\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $520\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is obtained with the wrong sign in front of the product of inertia, $400\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ neglecting the product of inertia, and $385.86\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ using $\alpha$ instead of $2\alpha$.
