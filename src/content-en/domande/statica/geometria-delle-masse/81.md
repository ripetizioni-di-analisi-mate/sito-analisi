Two identical plates, each of area $3\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, are arranged symmetrically about the horizontal axis. Their centroids are moved away from the axis from $2$ to $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ without rotating the plates. By how much does the section’s second moment about that axis increase?

- $126\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $54\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $63\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $150\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

Symmetry keeps the centroid fixed and each plate retains its own centroidal second moment. Only the two parallel-axis terms change. Their total increase is $2\cdot3(5^2-2^2)=126\,\mathrm{cm^4}$.
