The section in the figure is a rectangle $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ wide and $16\,\mathrm{cm}$ high with a circular hole of radius $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ in the centre. What is the second moment of area $I_{x_G}$ about the horizontal centroidal axis?

::struttura{poligoni="-6,-8 6,-8 6,8 -6,8" cerchi="0,0,5,foro" frecce="-6.4,0,8,0,x_G;0,-9,0,9.8,y_G" quote="-6 -8,6 -8,12 cm,1;-6 8,-6 -8,16 cm,1;0 0,3.54 3.54,r = 5 cm,0.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangle of dimensions $12 \times 16\,\mathrm{cm}$ with a central hole of radius $5\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $3605.13\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $4096\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $3114.25\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $1813.13\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The section has two axes of symmetry, so the centroid is the centre of the rectangle, which is also the centre of the hole. The solid rectangle has $I_{x_G} = \dfrac{12 \cdot 16^3}{12} = 4096\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. The hole has its centre on the axis $x_G$, so its moment about a diameter is subtracted, with no parallel-axis term:
$$
I_{x_G} = 4096 - \frac{\pi \cdot 5^4}{4} = 4096 - 490.87 = 3605.13\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $3114.25\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ subtracts the polar moment of the hole, $\dfrac{\pi r^4}{2}$, instead of the moment about the diameter; the value $1813.13\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the moment about the vertical $y_G$ axis.
