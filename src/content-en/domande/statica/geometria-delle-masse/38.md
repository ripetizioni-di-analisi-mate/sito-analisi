Which of the following sets of three values $(I_{x_G},\ I_{y_G},\ I_{x_G y_G})$, referred to perpendicular centroidal axes, is impossible for any section?

- $(400;\ 100;\ 250)\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $(400;\ 100;\ 150)\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $(400;\ 100;\ -180)\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $(200;\ 200;\ 190)\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The minimum principal moment $I_2$ is a second moment of area about an axis, so it is positive. By the formula of the principal moments $I_2 = C - \sqrt{D^2 + I_{x_G y_G}^2}$, with $C = \dfrac{I_{x_G} + I_{y_G}}{2}$ and $D = \dfrac{I_{x_G} - I_{y_G}}{2}$. Therefore $I_2 > 0$ is equivalent to $C^2 > D^2 + I_{x_G y_G}^2$, i.e.
$$
I_{x_G}\,I_{y_G} > I_{x_G y_G}^2 .
$$
For the third $(400;\ 100;\ 250)$ you have $I_{x_G}\,I_{y_G} = 40\,000$ and $I_{x_G y_G}^2 = 62\,500$, so the condition is not met and $I_2 = 250 - 291.55 < 0$. For the other three backs the condition is met: $40\,000 > 22\,500$, $40\,000 > 32\,400$ and $40\,000 > 36\,100$.
