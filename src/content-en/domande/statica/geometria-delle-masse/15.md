The rectangle in the figure is wide $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and occupies the strip between the $y = 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $y = 8\,\mathrm{cm}$ lines. What is its first moment of area $S_x$ about the $x$ axis?

::struttura{poligoni="0,2 4,2 4,8 0,8" frecce="0,0,6,0,x;0,0,0,9.5,y" quote="0 2,4 2,4 cm,0.9;4 2,4 8,6 cm,0.9;0 0,0 2,2 cm,-0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangle of width $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ between lines $y = 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $y = 8\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $120\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $72\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $128\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $192\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

The rectangle has area $A = 4 \cdot 6 = 24\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid at elevation $y_G = \dfrac{2 + 8}{2} = 5\,\mathrm{cm}$. For the definition of centroid $S_x = A\,y_G$, then
$$
S_x = 24 \cdot 5\,\mathrm{cm^3} = 120\,\mathrm{cm^3} .
$$
The same value is obtained with the horizontal strips, $S_x = \displaystyle\int_2^8 4y\,dy = 2\,(64 - 4) = 120\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. The value $72\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ is obtained with the formula $\dfrac{b h^2}{2}$, which applies to the rectangle with the base on the axis $x$, and $128\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ integrating from $0$ to $8$.
