A section has the principal moments of inertia centroidal $I_1 = 800\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_2 = 200\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. As the pair of perpendicular centroidal axes rotates, what is the largest product of inertia $I_{\xi\eta}$ and for which axes does it occur?

- $600\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, for axes inclined at $45^\circ$ to the principal axes
- $300\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, for axes inclined at $45^\circ$ to the principal axes
- $500\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, for axes inclined at $45^\circ$ to the principal axes
- $300\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, for the principal axes

## Explanation

In Mohr's circle the principal moments are the intersections with the horizontal axis, so the center has $\dfrac{800 + 200}{2} = 500\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ horizontal coordinate and the radius is $\dfrac{800 - 200}{2} = 300\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. The order of a point in the circle is the product of inertia, and its maximum value is the radius, reached at the highest point. That point is $90^\circ$ on the circle from the point $(I_1, 0)$, and because the angles on the circle are double those between the axes, the corresponding pair of axes is rotated by $45^\circ$ about the principal axes. Therefore the maximum is **$300\,\mathrm{cm^4}$** and you have for the inclined axes of $45^\circ$; for the principal axes the product of inertia is zero.
