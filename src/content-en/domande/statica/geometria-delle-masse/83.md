An isosceles right triangle has the legs of $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its second moment of area about the line containing the hypotenuse?

- $1728\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $576\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $864\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $288\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

Take the hypotenuse as the base: it is $12\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$ long, and the corresponding altitude, i.e. the vertex distance of the right angle from the hypotenuse, is half the hypotenuse, $6\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$. The formula of the second moment of area of a triangle about the base gives
$$
I = \frac{b h^3}{12} = \frac{12\sqrt{2} \cdot \left( 6\sqrt{2} \right)^3}{12} = \sqrt{2} \cdot 432\sqrt{2} = 864\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The base and height should be taken both with respect to the hypotenuse. The value $1728\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is obtained by using the legs as the base and height, i.e. the moment about a leg.
