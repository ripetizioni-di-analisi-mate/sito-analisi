## Explanation

The vertical axis for the center of the semicircle is an axis of symmetry, so the centroid lies on it. The rectangle has area $200\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid at elevation $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The removed semicircle has area $\dfrac{25\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{cm^2} = 39.27\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, and its centroid is $\dfrac{4r}{3\pi} = \dfrac{20}{3\pi}\,\mathrm{cm} = 2.12\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the diameter, which is on the underside. With the semicircle as part of negative area
$$
y_G = \frac{200 \cdot 5 - 39.27 \cdot 2.12}{200 - 39.27}\,\mathrm{cm} = \frac{916.67}{160.73}\,\mathrm{cm} = 5.70\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The value $6.86\,\mathrm{cm}$ is obtained with the whole circle area, $4.53\,\mathrm{cm}$ by adding the semicircle instead of removing it.
