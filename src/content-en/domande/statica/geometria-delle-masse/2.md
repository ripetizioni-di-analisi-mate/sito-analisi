- $G = (3.5;\ 2.5)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $G = (4;\ 5)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $G = (2.5;\ 3.5)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $G = (2.33;\ 3.22)\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Divide the section into two rectangles without overlapping. The horizontal flange of $8 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ has area $16\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid $(4, 1)$; the part of the vertical flange above it, of $2 \times 8\,\mathrm{cm}$, has area $16\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid $(1, 6)$. For the centroid formula of a composite figure
$$
x_G = \frac{16 \cdot 4 + 16 \cdot 1}{32}\,\mathrm{cm} = 2.5\,\mathrm{cm} ,
\qquad
y_G = \frac{16 \cdot 1 + 16 \cdot 6}{32}\,\mathrm{cm} = 3.5\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The point $(2.33;\ 3.22)\,\mathrm{cm}$ is obtained by adding the horizontal flange and the entire vertical flange of $2 \times 10\,\mathrm{cm}$, which count twice the corner square; $(4;\ 5)\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the center of the rectangle that contains the section.
