A circular section has $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ radius. What is its polar moment of area about a point $P$ on the circumference?

- $8\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $24\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $20\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $16\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

You take by $P$ the tangent axis to the circumference and the perpendicular axis, which passes through the center. The polar moment about $P$ is the sum of the moments about these two axes. The axis for the center is a diameter, with $\dfrac{\pi r^4}{4} = 4\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ moment; the tangent is $r$ from the center, so for the **parallel-axis theorem** it has $4\pi + 4\pi \cdot 2^2 = 20\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ moment. Therefore
$$
I_{p,P} = 4\pi + 20\pi = 24\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4} ,
$$
which coincides with $\dfrac{\pi r^4}{2} + A\,r^2$. The value $8\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the polar moment about the center, and $20\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the moment about the tangent alone.
