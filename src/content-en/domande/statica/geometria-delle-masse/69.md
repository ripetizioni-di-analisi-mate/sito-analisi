About the $x_G$ and $y_G$ centroidal axes a section has $I_{x_G} = 500\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, $I_{y_G} = 300\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{x_G y_G} = 100\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. For which pair of perpendicular centroidal axes $\xi$ and $\eta$ are the second moments of area $I_\xi$ and $I_\eta$ equal?

- $\xi$ rotated $22.5^\circ$ clockwise from $x_G$
- $\xi$ rotated counterclockwise by $45^\circ$ from $x_G$
- $\xi$ rotated counterclockwise by $67.5^\circ$ from $x_G$
- $\xi$ rotated counterclockwise by $22.5^\circ$ from $x_G$

## Explanation

From axis rotation formulas
$$
I_\xi - I_\eta = (I_{x_G} - I_{y_G})\cos 2\alpha - 2 I_{x_G y_G}\sin 2\alpha = 200\cos 2\alpha - 200\sin 2\alpha .
$$
The difference is zero if $\tan 2\alpha = 1$, i.e. for $2\alpha = 45^\circ$ or $2\alpha = 225^\circ$, and then for $\alpha = 22.5^\circ$ or $\alpha = 112.5^\circ$; the two solutions describe the same pair of axes with the names exchanged. Therefore the couple you are looking for has $\xi$ rotated by $22.5^\circ$ counterclockwise. The principal axes, with $\tan 2\alpha_0 = -\dfrac{2 \cdot 100}{200} = -1$, are rotated by $22.5^\circ$ clockwise direction, so the couple with equal moments is tilted by $45^\circ$ about the principal axes.
