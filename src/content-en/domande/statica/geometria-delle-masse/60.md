A semicircle has $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ radius. What is its second moment of area about the line containing the diameter?

- $64\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $16\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $128\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $32\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The solid circle of radius $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ has second moment of area about a diameter $\dfrac{\pi r^4}{4} = 64\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. The diameter divides it into two semicircles symmetrical to the straight, and each element of one semicircle has the symmetrical in the other at the same distance from the straight. Therefore the two semicircles make the same contribution, and for the additiveness each equals half of the total:
$$
I = \frac{\pi r^4}{8} = 32\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $128\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the polar moment of the entire circle about the center.
