The main centroidal axes of a section have the second moments of area $I_1 = 900\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_2 = 100\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. Which of the following pairs could represent the second moment of area $I_\xi$ and the product of inertia $I_{\xi\eta}$ for perpendicular centroidal axes $\xi$ and $\eta$?

- $I_\xi = 740\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{\xi\eta} = 320\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $I_\xi = 740\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{\xi\eta} = 400\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $I_\xi = 950\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{\xi\eta} = 150\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $I_\xi = 500\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{\xi\eta} = 450\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

As the torque of centroidal axes changes, point $(I_\xi, I_{\xi\eta})$ describes Mohr's circle of inertia, which cuts the horizontal axis at the principal moments. Therefore the center with horizontal coordinate $\dfrac{900 + 100}{2} = 500\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and the radius equals $\dfrac{900 - 100}{2} = 400\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, and a couple is possible if and only if
$$
(I_\xi - 500)^2 + I_{\xi\eta}^2 = 400^2 = 160\,000 .
$$
For $I_\xi = 740\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{\xi\eta} = 320\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ you have $240^2 + 320^2 = 57\,600 + 102\,400 = 160\,000$, so the point is on the circle. With $I_{\xi\eta} = 400\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, the sum is $217\,600$, and the point $(500;\ 450)$ is $450\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ from the center, more than the radius. The value $I_\xi = 950\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ exceeds the maximum principal moment $I_1$, so no centroidal axis admits it.
