Two equal C-profiles, $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ high, with the thick $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ web and $4 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ flanges, are paired back as in the figure, with the webs in contact along the axis $y$. What is the second moment of area of the combined section about axis $y$?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,2 2,2 2,8 6,8 6,10 0,10;0,0 -6,0 -6,2 -2,2 -2,8 -6,8 -6,10 0,10" frecce="-7.5,5,8,5,x;0,-0.8,0,11.8,y" quote="0 0,2 0,2 cm,0.9;2 0,6 0,4 cm,0.9;6 0,6 10,10 cm,1;6 8,6 10,2 cm,-1.2" riferimento=0 titolo="Two channels placed back to back along axis $y$"}

- $608\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $304\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $216\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $2808\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The axis $y$ is the symmetry axis of the paired section, so the two profiles make the same contribution and just calculate the moment of a profile about your back. The web has the back as the side, so its moment is $\dfrac{10 \cdot 2^3}{3} = 26.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. Each flange has the centroid at $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ from its back, and for the **parallel-axis theorem** its moment is $\dfrac{2 \cdot 4^3}{12} + 8 \cdot 4^2 = 138.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. A profile therefore has $26.67 + 2 \cdot 138.67 = 304\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, and for the additiveness
$$
I_y = 2 \cdot 304 = 608\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $216\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ adds the moments of the two profiles about their centroidal axes, without the parallel-axis term.
