## Explanation

The semi-crown is the $6\,\mathrm{cm}$-radius semi-circle minus the $3\,\mathrm{cm}$-radius semi-circle, with the same diameter. The first moment of area of a semicircle about the diameter is $\dfrac{2r^3}{3}$, then $S_x = \dfrac{2 \cdot 216}{3} - \dfrac{2 \cdot 27}{3} = 144 - 18 = 126\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. The area is $A = \dfrac{\pi\,(36 - 9)}{2} = 13.5\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2} = 42.41\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Therefore
$$
y_G = \frac{S_x}{A} = \frac{126}{13.5\pi}\,\mathrm{cm} = \frac{28}{3\pi}\,\mathrm{cm} = 2.97\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The value $2.55\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the position of the centroid of the semicircle full of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ radius, and the value $3.82\,\mathrm{cm}$ is obtained by adding the two first moments of area instead of subtracting them.
