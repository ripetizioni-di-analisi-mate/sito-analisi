A triangle has the base of $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the height of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its radius of gyration about the line containing the base?

- $1.41\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1.73\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3.46\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2.45\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The second moment of area of the triangle about the base is $\dfrac{b h^3}{12} = \dfrac{8 \cdot 6^3}{12} = 144\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and the area is $\dfrac{8 \cdot 6}{2} = 24\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. For the definition of radius of gyration
$$
\rho = \sqrt{\frac{144}{24}}\,\mathrm{cm} = \sqrt{6}\,\mathrm{cm} = 2.45\,\mathrm{cm} ,
$$
which coincides with $\dfrac{h}{\sqrt{6}}$ and does not depend on the base. The value $1.41\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the radius of gyration about the centroidal axis parallel to the base, $\dfrac{h}{\sqrt{18}}$; the value $3.46\,\mathrm{cm}$ is that of the rectangle with respect to the base, $\dfrac{h}{\sqrt{3}}$.
