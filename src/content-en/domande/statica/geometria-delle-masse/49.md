The section in the figure is an isosceles trapezoid with the longer base of $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ on the $x$ axis, a shorter base of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a height of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. How far is the centroid from the longer base?

::struttura{poligoni="-6,0 6,0 3,6 -3,6" frecce="-7.5,0,8,0,x" quote="-6 0,6 0,12 cm,1;-3 6,3 6,6 cm,-0.9;6 0,6 6,6 cm,1.4" riferimento=0 titolo="Isosceles trapezoid with bases $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $6\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $y_G = 3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $y_G = 2.67\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $y_G = 3.33\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $y_G = 2\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The trapezoid is divided into a central rectangle of $6 \times 6\,\mathrm{cm}$, with area $36\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid at height $3\,\mathrm{cm}$, and into two right triangles with legs of $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. Each triangle has area $9\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid at one third of the height, i.e. at height $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. By the centroid formula for a composite figure
$$
y_G = \frac{36 \cdot 3 + 2 \cdot 9 \cdot 2}{36 + 18}\,\mathrm{cm} = \frac{144}{54}\,\mathrm{cm} = \frac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{cm} = 2.67\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The centroid lies below mid-height because the widest part of the trapezoid is at the bottom. The value $3.33\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the distance of the centroid from the shorter base.
