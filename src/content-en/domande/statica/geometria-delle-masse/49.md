A section consists of two plates of equal area with centroid heights of $2$ and $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. The first plate is widened until its area doubles while its centroid height stays the same. At what new height must the centre of the second plate be placed to preserve the original overall centroid height?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $11\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Initially the centroid height is the mean, $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. After widening, the total area is three times the original area of one plate. If $y$ is the required height, $2\cdot2+y=3\cdot5$, giving $y=11\,\mathrm{cm}$.
