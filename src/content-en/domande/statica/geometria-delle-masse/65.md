The section in the figure is a circle of radius $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ with a circular hole of radius $2\,\mathrm{cm}$, whose center lies on the axis $x$ to $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center of the large circle. What is the second moment of area of the section about axis $y$ through the center of the larger circle?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,6;3,0,2,foro" frecce="-7.5,0,8,0,x;0,-7.5,0,8,y" punti="3,0" quote="0 0,3 0,3 cm,-2.6;-6 0,0 0,R = 6 cm,0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="Circle of radius $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ with a hole of radius $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ whose center is offset by $3\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $320\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $356\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $284\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $324\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The solid circle has moment about the diameter $y$ equal to $\dfrac{\pi \cdot 6^4}{4} = 324\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. The hole has area $4\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, moment about its vertical diameter $\dfrac{\pi \cdot 2^4}{4} = 4\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and center to $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis $y$. For the **parallel-axis theorem** the moment of the hole about the axis $y$ is $4\pi + 4\pi \cdot 9 = 40\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, and the hole subtracts:
$$
I_y = 324\pi - 40\pi = 284\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $320\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ subtracts the moment of the hole without the transport moment, which would only be correct about the axis $x$, passing through the center of the hole.
