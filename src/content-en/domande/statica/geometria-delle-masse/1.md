- $y_G = 4.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the bottom edge
- $y_G = 5.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the bottom edge
- $y_G = 9\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the bottom edge
- $y_G = 12.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the bottom edge

## Explanation

The section is symmetric about its vertical axis, so the centroid lies on that axis. Only $y_G$ remains to be found. Take the $x$-axis along the bottom edge. The flange has area $32\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and its centroid is at elevation $1\,\mathrm{cm}$. The web starts at elevation $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Its area is $32\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and its centroid is at elevation $2 + 8 = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$. The centroid formula for a composite section gives
$$
y_G = \frac{32 \cdot 1 + 32 \cdot 10}{32 + 32}\,\mathrm{cm} = \frac{352}{64}\,\mathrm{cm} = 5.5\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The value $4.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ places the web centroid at elevation $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and omits the flange thickness. The value $12.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the distance from $G$ to the top edge.
