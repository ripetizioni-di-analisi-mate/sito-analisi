The channel section shown has overall height $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. Its web and flanges have thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$, and the overall width of each flange, including the web thickness, is $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. How far is the centroid from the back of the web?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 10,0 10,2 2,2 2,8 10,8 10,10 0,10" frecce="0,0,12,0,x;0,0,0,12,y" quote="0 10,10 10,10 cm,-0.9;0 0,0 10,10 cm,-0.9;10 0,10 2,2 cm,0.8;10 8,10 10,2 cm,0.8;0 5,2 5,2 cm,0" riferimento=0 titolo="Channel section of height $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ with flanges of width $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $x_G = 3.67\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $x_G = 5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $x_G = 6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $x_G = 4.08\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The axis $y = 5\,\mathrm{cm}$ is symmetry axis, so just calculate $x_G$, with the axis $y$ on the back. Divide the section into the web of $2 \times 10\,\mathrm{cm}$, with area $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid to $x = 1\,\mathrm{cm}$, and the two flanges of $8 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ that remain out of the web, each with area $16\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid to $x = 2 + 4 = 6\,\mathrm{cm}$. For the centroid formula of a composite figure
$$
x_G = \frac{20 \cdot 1 + 2 \cdot 16 \cdot 6}{20 + 2 \cdot 16}\,\mathrm{cm} = \frac{212}{52}\,\mathrm{cm} = 4.08\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The value $3.67\,\mathrm{cm}$ is obtained by counting the flanges for the entire width of $10\,\mathrm{cm}$, that is, by adding the two corner squares twice; $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ is half the width and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the horizontal coordinate of the centroid of the flanges alone.
