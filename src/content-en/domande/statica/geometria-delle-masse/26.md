The sheet metal in the figure is a rectangle $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ wide and $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ high, with two circular holes of $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ radius for bolts. About the center of the rectangle the centers of the holes stand at points $(-3, 3)\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $(3, -3)\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the second moment of area of the perforated plate about its horizontal centroidal axis $x_G$?

::struttura{poligoni="-6,-5 6,-5 6,5 -6,5" cerchi="-3,3,1.5,foro;3,-3,1.5,foro" frecce="-6.3,0,8,0,x_G;0,-6,0,6.8,y_G" quote="-6 -5,6 -5,12 cm,0.9;-6 -5,-6 5,10 cm,-1" riferimento=0 titolo="Plate of dimensions $12 \times 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ with two holes of radius $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $992.05\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $864.81\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $1135.19\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $737.58\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The two holes are symmetrical with respect to the center of the rectangle, so the centroid of the perforated sheet remains in the center and the axis $x_G$ is the horizontal axis for the center. The solid rectangle has $I_{x_G} = \dfrac{12 \cdot 10^3}{12} = 1000\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. Each hole has area $2.25\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and center to $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis $x_G$, so for the **parallel-axis theorem** its moment about $x_G$ equals
$$
\frac{\pi \cdot 1.5^4}{4} + 2.25\pi \cdot 3^2 = 1.27\pi + 20.25\pi = 21.52\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 67.59\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
Treat the holes as negative areas, giving $I_{x_G} = 1000 - 2 \cdot 67.59 = 864.81\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. The value $992.05\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ omits the parallel-axis contribution of the holes. The value $1135.19\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ adds their contributions instead of subtracting them, and $737.58\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ uses the distance of their centers from the centroid, $3\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$, in place of their perpendicular distance to axis $x_G$.
