The T-section shown has a web of dimensions $2 \times 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a flange of dimensions $10 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Its centroid is $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the bottom edge. What is its second moment of area $I_{x_G}$ about its horizontal centroidal axis?

::struttura{poligoni="-1,0 1,0 1,10 5,10 5,12 -5,12 -5,10 -1,10" frecce="-6.5,0,6.5,0,x;0,-0.8,0,13.4,y" quote="-5 12,5 12,10 cm,-2.7;-1 0,1 0,2 cm,1;5 0,5 12,12 cm,1.2;-5 10,-5 12,2 cm,-0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="T-section with web dimensions $2 \times 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and flange dimensions $10 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $533.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $173.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $360\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $3093.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The web has area $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, centroid at elevation $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and moment proper $\dfrac{2 \cdot 10^3}{12} = 166.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. The flange has area $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, centroid at elevation $11\,\mathrm{cm}$ and moment own $\dfrac{10 \cdot 2^3}{12} = 6.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. Both centroids distance $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis $x_G$, so for the **parallel-axis theorem**
$$
I_{x_G} = (166.67 + 20 \cdot 3^2) + (6.67 + 20 \cdot 3^2) = 533.33\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $173.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ contains only the proper moments, $360\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ only the parallel-axis terms, and $3093.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the second moment of area about the bottom edge.
