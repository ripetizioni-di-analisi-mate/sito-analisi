For which of the following sections does Mohr's circle for the centroidal moments of area reduce to a single point?

- The L-section with two equal legs of length $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- The rectangle of width $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- The semicircle of radius $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- The square of side $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ with a central circular hole of radius $3\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The radius of Mohr's circle is $\sqrt{\left( \dfrac{I_{x_G} - I_{y_G}}{2} \right)^2 + I_{x_G y_G}^2}$, so the circle shrinks to a point if and only if $I_{x_G} = I_{y_G}$ and $I_{x_G y_G} = 0$. The square with the central hole has two axes of symmetry parallel to the sides, therefore $I_{x_G y_G} = 0$. Also a rotation of $90^\circ$ around the center brings the section itself and swaps the $x_G$ and $y_G$ axes, then
$$
I_{x_G} = I_{y_G} = \frac{12^4}{12} - \frac{\pi \cdot 3^4}{4} = 1728 - 63.62 = 1664.38\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The L-section with the same flanges has $I_{x_G} = I_{y_G}$, but its product of inertia about the axes parallel to the flanges equals $-82.29\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. The rectangle has $I_{x_G} = 864\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{y_G} = 216\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, and the semicircle has $142.25\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ relative to the axis parallel to the diameter and $508.94\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ relative to the symmetry axis.
