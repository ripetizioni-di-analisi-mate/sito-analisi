The section in the figure is bounded by the axis $x$, the line $x = 3\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the $y = x^2$ parabola (lengths in $\mathrm{cm}$). What is its second moment of area $I_y$ about axis $y$?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3.00,9.00 2.75,7.56 2.50,6.25 2.25,5.06 2.00,4.00 1.75,3.06 1.50,2.25 1.25,1.56 1.00,1.00 0.75,0.56 0.50,0.25 0.25,0.06 0.00,0.00" frecce="0,0,4.5,0,x;0,0,0,10.5,y" quote="0 0,3 0,3 cm,0.8" etichette="1.2,4.6,y = x²" riferimento=0 titolo="Region under the parabola $y = x^2$ for $0 \leq x \leq 3\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $20.25\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $104.14\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $48.6\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $81\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The moment $I_y = \displaystyle\int_A x^2\,dA$ depends only on the distance $x$ from the axis $y$, then divides the section into vertical strips $dx$ width. The strip at horizontal coordinate $x$ has $x^2$ height, so $dA = x^2\,dx$ and all its points are $x$ from the axis $y$. So
$$
I_y = \int_0^3 x^2 \cdot x^2\,dx = \frac{x^5}{5} \Big|_0^3 = \frac{243}{5}\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 48.6\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $20.25\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the first moment of area $S_y = \displaystyle\int_0^3 x \cdot x^2\,dx$, and the value $104.14\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the second moment of area $I_x$ about the axis $x$. The value $81\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the moment of the rectangle of $3 \times 9\,\mathrm{cm}$ that contains the section.
