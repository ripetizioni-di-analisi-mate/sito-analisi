The T-section shown has a flange measuring $8 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a web of thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $h$. For what value of $h$ does the centroid lie exactly at the junction of the web and flange?

::struttura{poligoni="-1,0 1,0 1,4 4,4 4,6 -4,6 -4,4 -1,4" tratteggi="-5.5,4 5.5,4" quote="-4 6,4 6,8 cm,-1;4 4,4 6,2 cm,0.9;1 0,1 4,h,1;-1 0,1 0,2 cm,0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="T-section with web height $h$ and the web–flange junction shown dashed"}

- $h = 2.83\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $h = 8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $h = 5.66\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $h = 4\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The centroid lies on the junction line if and only if the first moment of area of the section about the line is zero. The flange has area $16\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the junction, so it contributes with $16\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. The web has area $2h$ and centroid $\dfrac{h}{2}$ under the junction, so it contributes with $-2h \cdot \dfrac{h}{2} = -h^2$. Therefore the condition is
$$
16 - h^2 = 0 ,
\qquad
h = 4\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The value $5.66\,\mathrm{cm}$ is obtained by placing the center of the flange at $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the junction instead of at the middle of the thickness.
