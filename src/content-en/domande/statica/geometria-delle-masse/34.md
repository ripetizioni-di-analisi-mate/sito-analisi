The I section shown has two equal flanges of $12 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a web of $2 \times 10\,\mathrm{cm}$, so it is $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ high. What is the moment of inertia $I_{y_G}$ about the vertical axis of symmetry?

::struttura{poligoni="-6,0 6,0 6,2 1,2 1,12 6,12 6,14 -6,14 -6,12 -1,12 -1,2 -6,2" tratteggi="0,-1 0,14.8" etichette="0.6,-1.3,y_G" quote="-6 14,6 14,12 cm,-1.2;6 0,6 14,14 cm,1.2;-6 12,-6 14,2 cm,-0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="I section with $12 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ flanges and a $2 \times 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ web"}

- $182.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $582.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $2310.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $1910.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The axis $y_G$ is an axis of symmetry and passes through the centroids of the two flanges and of the web, so there are no parallel-axis terms. For a rectangle of width $b$, measured horizontally, and height $h$ the moment about the vertical centroidal axis is $\dfrac{h\,b^3}{12}$, therefore
$$
I_{y_G} = 2 \cdot \frac{2 \cdot 12^3}{12} + \frac{10 \cdot 2^3}{12} = 576 + 6.67 = 582.67\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The flanges give almost the whole value, because their width appears cubed, and the value $1910.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the moment of inertia about the horizontal axis $x_G$. The value $182.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ cubes the vertical dimensions, as for the horizontal axis, and $2310.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ uses for the flanges the formula $\dfrac{h\,b^3}{3}$, which holds about one side.
