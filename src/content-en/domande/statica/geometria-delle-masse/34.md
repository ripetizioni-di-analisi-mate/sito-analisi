The T-section shown has a flange of dimensions $12 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a web of dimensions $2 \times 8\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its second moment of area $I_{y_G}$ about its vertical axis of symmetry?

::struttura{poligoni="-1,0 1,0 1,8 6,8 6,10 -6,10 -6,8 -1,8" tratteggi="0,-0.5 0,10.8" etichette="0.5,11.1,y_G" quote="-6 10,6 10,12 cm,-2.2;-1 0,1 0,2 cm,0.9;6 0,6 10,10 cm,1.2;-6 8,-6 10,2 cm,-0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="T-section with flange dimensions $12 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and web dimensions $2 \times 8\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $93.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $293.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $1152\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $288\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The axis $y_G$ is the axis of symmetry and passes through the centroids of the flange and web, so there are no parallel-axis terms. Compared to the vertical axis in the formula of the rectangle appears the cube of the width, therefore
$$
I_{y_G} = \frac{2 \cdot 12^3}{12} + \frac{8 \cdot 2^3}{12} = 288 + 5.33 = 293.33\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $93.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is obtained by elevating the heights to the cube, that is, with the proper moments about horizontal axes; $288\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ neglects the web, and $1152\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the moment of the flange over its short side.
