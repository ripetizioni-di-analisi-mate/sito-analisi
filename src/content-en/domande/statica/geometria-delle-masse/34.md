A student models a solid T-section by adding two overlapping rectangles. The central square of side $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ is then counted twice. The chosen vertical axis passes through the centre of that square. What correction should be made to the computed second moment?

::struttura{poligoni="-1,0 1,0 1,5 -1,5;-3,3 3,3 3,5 -3,5" tratteggi="0,-0.4 0,5.5" quote="-1 3,1 3,2 cm,0.7" titolo="Overlapping rectangles in the T-section"}

- $+\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $-\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $-\dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

Subtract one copy of the area counted twice. The axis passes through the square’s centroid, so the correction is $-\dfrac{2^4}{12}=-\dfrac43\,\mathrm{cm^4}$.
