The box section shown has outside dimensions $20 \times 30\,\mathrm{cm}$ (width by height) and wall thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the second moment of area $I_{y_G}$ about the vertical centroidal axis?

::struttura{poligoni="-10,-15 10,-15 10,15 -10,15" fori="-8,-13 8,-13 8,13 -8,13" frecce="-11,0,12.5,0,x_G;0,-15.6,0,17.5,y_G" quote="-10 -15,10 -15,20 cm,1.3;-10 -15,-10 15,30 cm,-1.6;-10 15,-8 15,2 cm,-0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="Box section of outside dimensions $20 \times 30\,\mathrm{cm}$ and wall thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $21\,565.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $6392\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $20\,000\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $11\,125.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The section has two axes of symmetry, so $G$ is the center and the axis $y_G$ passes through the centroids of the outer rectangle and void. The cutout measures $16 \times 26\,\mathrm{cm}$, because each dimension removes two walls from $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Compared to the vertical axis in the formula of the rectangle appears the cube of the width, therefore
$$
I_{y_G} = \frac{30 \cdot 20^3}{12} - \frac{26 \cdot 16^3}{12} = 20\,000 - 8874.67 = 11\,125.33\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $21\,565.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is $I_{x_G}$, which is obtained by elevating the heights to the cube; $6392\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is obtained by removing the thickness only once, that is, with the cutout of $18 \times 28\,\mathrm{cm}$.
