The rectangle in the figure is $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ wide along $x$ and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ high along $y$. What is its second moment of area about the diagonal from the lower left vertex to the upper right?

::struttura{poligoni="-4,-3 4,-3 4,3 -4,3" tratteggi="-5,-3.75 5,3.75" frecce="-4.4,0,6,0,x_G;0,-3.6,0,4.8,y_G" quote="-4 -3,4 -3,8 cm,0.9;-4 3,-4 -3,6 cm,1" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangle of dimensions $8 \times 6\,\mathrm{cm}$ with its diagonal shown dashed"}

- $184.32\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $144\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $256\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $400\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The diagonal passes through the centroid and is angled $\alpha$ with $\tan\alpha = \dfrac{6}{8}$, then $\cos 2\alpha = \dfrac{64 - 36}{100} = \dfrac{7}{25}$. Compared to the centroidal axes parallel to the $I_{x_G} = \dfrac{8 \cdot 6^3}{12} = 144\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, $I_{y_G} = \dfrac{6 \cdot 8^3}{12} = 256\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{x_G y_G} = 0$ sides. For axis rotation formula
$$
I_\xi = \frac{144 + 256}{2} + \frac{144 - 256}{2} \cdot \frac{7}{25} = 200 - 15.68 = 184.32\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The result lies between the two principal moments $144\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $256\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, as for each oblique centroidal axis; the value $400\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the polar moment centroidal.
