An $30\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ area section has the centroid at the $y_G = 4\,\mathrm{cm}$ elevation and second moment of area $I_x = 800\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ about the axis $x$. What is the second moment of area about line $y = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $1400\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $3800\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $1880\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $320\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The parallel-axis theorem connects each of the two lines to the parallel centroidal axis, then passes through $x_G$. From the axis $x$ you get $I_{x_G} = I_x - A\,y_G^2 = 800 - 30 \cdot 16 = 320\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. The line $y = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ is $10 - 4 = 6\,\mathrm{cm}$ from $G$, so
$$
I_r = I_{x_G} + A \cdot 6^2 = 320 + 1080 = 1400\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $3800\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is obtained by carrying directly $I_x$ of the distance $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ between the two lines, $1880\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ adding $A \cdot 6^2$ to $I_x$, and $320\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the centroidal moment.
