The Z-section shown has overall height $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. The web and flanges have thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$, and each flange has overall width $10\,\mathrm{cm}$, including the web thickness. What is the product of inertia $I_{xy}$ about the axes $x$ and $y$ shown, with their origin at the center of the section?

::struttura{poligoni="-9,-5 1,-5 1,3 9,3 9,5 -1,5 -1,-3 -9,-3" frecce="-11,0,11,0,x;0,-6.5,0,7,y" quote="-1 5,9 5,10 cm,-1;-9 -5,1 -5,10 cm,1;9 -5,9 5,10 cm,3.5;9 3,9 5,2 cm,0.8;-1 0,1 0,2 cm,1.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Z-section of height $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ with flange length $10\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $-640\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $0\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $320\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $640\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

Each point $(x, y)$ of the section has the corresponding $(-x, -y)$, so the first moments of area about the axes in the figure are zero and the centroid is the origin. The web has the centroid in the origin and product of inertia just null. The bottom flange, of $10 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$, has area $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid $(-4, -4)$; the top flange has area $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid $(4, 4)$; even flanges have product of inertia just null. For the **parallel-axis theorem**
$$
I_{xy} = 20 \cdot (-4) \cdot (-4) + 20 \cdot 4 \cdot 4 = 640\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The Z-section has no symmetry axes, so the product of inertia is different from zero; is positive because the area of flanges lies in the first and third quadrants. The value $320\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ contains only one flange.
