Compared to centroidal axes a section has $I_{x_G} = 800\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, $I_{y_G} = 500\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{x_G y_G} = 150\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. Through what angle is the principal axis for the maximum moment rotated from $x_G$?

- $22.5^\circ$ counterclockwise
- $45^\circ$ clockwise
- $67.5^\circ$ counterclockwise
- $22.5^\circ$ clockwise

## Explanation

For the principal axis direction formula
$$
\tan 2\alpha_0 = -\frac{2 \cdot 150}{800 - 500} = -1 ,
$$
then $2\alpha_0 = -45^\circ$ and $\alpha_0 = -22.5^\circ$; the other main axis is rotated by $-22.5^\circ + 90^\circ = 67.5^\circ$. With $\cos 2\alpha_0 = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ and $\sin 2\alpha_0 = -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ the rotation formula gives $I_\xi = 650 + 150 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + 150 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = 862.13\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, which is the maximum $650 + \sqrt{150^2 + 150^2}$ moment. Therefore the maximum moment axis is rotated by $22.5^\circ$ in **clockwise**, and the rotated axis of $67.5^\circ$ counterclockwise is that of the minimum moment.
