The T-section in the figure has the flange of $8 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the web of $2 \times 8\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the second moment of area of the section about line $s$ separating the web from the flange?

::struttura{poligoni="-1,0 1,0 1,8 4,8 4,10 -4,10 -4,8 -1,8" tratteggi="-5.5,8 5.5,8" etichette="5.9,8.3,s" quote="-4 10,4 10,8 cm,-0.9;-1 0,1 0,2 cm,0.9;4 8,4 10,2 cm,0.9;1 0,1 8,8 cm,3.4" riferimento=0 titolo="T-section with line $s$ separating web and flange"}

- $90.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $290.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $362.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $682.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

The line $s$ contains the lower side of the flange and the upper side of the web, so for each of the two rectangles is the line of one side. The second moment of area of a rectangle about the line of a side equals $\dfrac{b h^3}{3}$, where $h$ is the side perpendicular to the line, and the second moment of area is additive. Therefore
$$
I_s = \frac{8 \cdot 2^3}{3} + \frac{2 \cdot 8^3}{3} = \frac{64}{3} + \frac{1024}{3} = \frac{1088}{3}\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 362.67\,\mathrm{cm^4} ,
$$
without needing to find the centroid. The same value is obtained with the parallel-axis theorem from $I_{x_G} = 290.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, with the area of $32\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and the distance of $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ between $G$ and $s$. The value $90.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ uses $\dfrac{b h^3}{12}$, which is valid for the centroidal axes of the parts, and $682.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the moment about the top edge.
