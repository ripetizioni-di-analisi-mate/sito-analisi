In Mohr's circle of inertia of a section, with the second moment of area in horizontal coordinate and the product of inertia in order, the angle in the middle between the point $X$, which corresponds to the axis $x_G$, and the point $(I_1, 0)$ equals $60^\circ$. Through what angle must axis $x_G$ be rotated to obtain the principal axis for the maximum moment?

- $30^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $120^\circ$
- $15^\circ$

## Explanation

For Mohr's circle theorem, when the axes rotate by an angle $\alpha$ the corresponding point rotates on the $2\alpha$ circle, in the same direction. The point $(I_1, 0)$ corresponds to the axis of the maximum moment, and to go from $X$ to that point you travel an angle to the center of $60^\circ$. Then $2\alpha = 60^\circ$, and the axis $x_G$ must be rotated by $\alpha = 30^\circ$. The value $60^\circ$ is obtained by forgetting that on the circle the angles are double, and $120^\circ$ doubling the angle instead of halving it.
