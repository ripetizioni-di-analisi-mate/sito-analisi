The isosceles triangle in the figure has base $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its second moment of area about line $r$ through the opposite vertex and parallel to the base?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 3,6" tratteggi="-1.5,6 7.5,6" etichette="7.9,6.3,r" quote="0 0,6 0,6 cm,0.9;6 0,6 6,6 cm,0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="Isosceles triangle of base $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ with line $r$ through the vertex"}

- $108\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $756\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $36\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $324\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

Compared to the centroidal axis parallel to the base the triangle has $I = \dfrac{b h^3}{36} = \dfrac{6 \cdot 216}{36} = 36\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, and its area is $18\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The centroid is $\dfrac{h}{3} = 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ away from the base, so it is $\dfrac{2h}{3} = 4\,\mathrm{cm}$ away from the line $r$. For the **parallel-axis theorem**
$$
I_r = 36 + 18 \cdot 4^2 = 324\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $108\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is obtained with the distance $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ of the centroid from the base, and coincides with the moment about the base; $756\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is achieved by transporting the moment about the base, which is not centroidal.
