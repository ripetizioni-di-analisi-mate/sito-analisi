The right triangle in the figure has the legs of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$, the horizontal one at the bottom and the vertical one at the right, with the right angle at the bottom right. What is the product of inertia $I_{x_G y_G}$ about centroidal axes parallel to the legs?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,6" punti="4,2,G,baricentro" tratteggi="-0.8,2 6.8,2;4,-0.8 4,7" etichette="7.1,2.3,x_G;4.4,7.3,y_G" quote="0 0,6 0,6 cm,0.9;6 0,6 6,6 cm,2.4" riferimento=0 titolo="Right triangle with its right angle at the lower right"}

- $-18\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $162\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $0\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

For the right-angled right triangle at the bottom left, $I_{x_G y_G} = -\dfrac{b^2 h^2}{72} = -\dfrac{36 \cdot 36}{72} = -18\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is. The triangle in the figure is the symmetric of that with respect to a vertical line, so in each element the coordinate $x$ measured from $y_G$ changes sign and the $y$ remains the same. Therefore the $x y$ product changes sign in each element, and
$$
I_{x_G y_G} = +\frac{b^2 h^2}{72} = 18\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The value $162\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the product of inertia about the axes for the bottom left vertex.
