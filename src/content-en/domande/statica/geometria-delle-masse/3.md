- $x_G = 15.58\,\mathrm{cm}$, $y_G = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $x_G = 14.25\,\mathrm{cm}$, $y_G = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $x_G = 15\,\mathrm{cm}$, $y_G = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $x_G = 12.38\,\mathrm{cm}$, $y_G = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The line $y = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the axis of symmetry of both the rectangle and the hole, hence $y_G = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$. The hole is treated as a part of negative area. The rectangle has area $600\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid at the horizontal coordinate $15\,\mathrm{cm}$, the hole has area $25\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2} = 78.54\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid at the horizontal coordinate $20\,\mathrm{cm}$, so
$$
x_G = \frac{600 \cdot 15 - 78.54 \cdot 20}{600 - 78.54}\,\mathrm{cm} = \frac{7429.2}{521.46}\,\mathrm{cm} = 14.25\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The centroid moves to the left, on the side opposite the hole. The value $15.58\,\mathrm{cm}$ is obtained by adding the hole instead of subtracting it, and $12.38\,\mathrm{cm}$ by dividing by the area of the solid rectangle.
