The section in the figure is formed by two squares on the $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ side with a vertex in common in the origin, one in the first quadrant and the other in the third. What is the product of inertia $I_{xy}$ about axes $x$ and $y$?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,2 0,2;0,0 -2,0 -2,-2 0,-2" frecce="-3.5,0,3.8,0,x;0,-3.5,0,3.8,y" quote="0 2,2 2,2 cm,-0.7;2 0,2 2,2 cm,0.7" riferimento=0 titolo="Two squares of side $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ in the first and third quadrants"}

- $8\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $0\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

Each square has zero product of inertia about its own centroidal axes, because they are axes of symmetry. The square in the first quadrant has area $4\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid $(1, 1)$, the one in the third has centroid $(-1, -1)$. For the **parallel-axis theorem** applied to each
$$
I_{xy} = 4 \cdot 1 \cdot 1 + 4 \cdot (-1) \cdot (-1) = 8\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The section has the center of symmetry in the origin and no axis of symmetry; his product of inertia is positive because both squares stand where $x y > 0$. The value $4\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is the contribution of only one square.
