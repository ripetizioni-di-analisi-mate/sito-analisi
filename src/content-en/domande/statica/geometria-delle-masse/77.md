The parallelogram in the figure has the horizontal sides of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$, the height of $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the oblique sides of $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its second moment of area $I_{x_G}$ about the centroidal axis parallel to its horizontal sides?

::struttura{poligoni="-4.5,-2 1.5,-2 4.5,2 -1.5,2" tratteggi="-4.6,0 5.5,0" etichette="5.9,0.3,x_G" quote="-4.5 -2,1.5 -2,6 cm,0.9;1.5 -2,4.5 2,5 cm,0.9;-6 -2,-6 2,4 cm,1.2" riferimento=0 titolo="Parallelogram with sides $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $4\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

- $62.5\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $32\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $128\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $10.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

Divide the parallelogram into thin strips parallel to its horizontal sides. Each strip has length $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ at every elevation, just as in the rectangle of dimensions $6 \times 4\,\mathrm{cm}$. Corresponding strips lie at the same distance from axis $x_G$. Since $I_{x_G} = \displaystyle\int y^2\,b(y)\,dy$ depends only on the strip width $b(y)$ at each elevation, the parallelogram has the same second moment of area as the rectangle, giving
$$
I_{x_G} = \frac{6 \cdot 4^3}{12} = 32\,\mathrm{cm^4} .
$$
The oblique side of $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ does not enter the calculation; $62.5\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ value uses oblique side instead of height.
