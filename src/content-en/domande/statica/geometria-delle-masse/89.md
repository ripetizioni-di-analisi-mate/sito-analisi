The T-section in the figure has the flange of $8 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the web of $2 \times 8\,\mathrm{cm}$, area $32\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroid $G$ to $6.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the bottom edge. What is the first moment of area of the section about line $r$ through $G$ and inclined at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal?

::struttura{poligoni="-1,0 1,0 1,8 4,8 4,10 -4,10 -4,8 -1,8" punti="0,6.5,G,baricentro" tratteggi="-5,3.61 5,9.39" etichette="-5.3,3.1,r" quote="-4 10,4 10,8 cm,-1.6;-1 0,1 0,2 cm,1;4 0,4 10,10 cm,1.3;-4 8,-4 10,2 cm,-0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="T-section with line $r$ through its centroid at angle $30^\circ$"}

- $208\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $104\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $180.13\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $0\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the first moment of area about any line of the plane, $S_r = A\,d_G$, where $d_G$ is the centroid sign distance from the line. The line $r$ passes through $G$, then $d_G = 0$, and therefore
$$
S_r = 32 \cdot 0 = 0\,\mathrm{cm^3} .
$$
The result does not depend on the inclination of $r$, because the first moment of area is zero about *every* straight for the centroid. The value $208\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ is the first moment of area about the bottom edge, $32 \cdot 6.5$.
