The model $s(t)=4+2t$ is defined for $t\ge0$, with $s$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. Does the object pass through the origin within the model’s domain?

- Yes, at $t=0$.
- Yes, at $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$.
- No. The equation $s(t)=0$ has solution $t=-2\,\mathrm{s}$.
- Yes, at $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

The object passes through the origin when $s(t)=0$, that is, when $4+2t=0$. This gives $2t=-4$, so $t=-2\,\mathrm{s}$. This time lies outside the domain $t\ge 0$, so the object does not pass through the origin within the model’s domain. Indeed, for $t\ge 0$ we have $s(t)\ge 4\,\mathrm{m}$. The answer $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$ results from a sign error, while $s(0)=4\,\mathrm{m}$ shows that at the initial time the object is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from the origin.
