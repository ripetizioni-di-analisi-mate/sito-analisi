The graph shows a constant negative velocity. What is the displacement between $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$?

::grafico{f="-2" xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=-3 ymax=1 nomi="t,v" punti="1,-2;4,-2" area="0;-2;1;4" titolo="Time in seconds and velocity in meters per second."}

- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The graph shows a constant velocity of $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Between $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$ the region is a rectangle below the time axis with base $3\,\mathrm{s}$. The displacement is the signed area:
$$
\Delta s=(-2\,\mathrm{m/s})(3\,\mathrm{s})=-6\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The negative sign indicates motion opposite to the positive direction. The value $6\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance traveled. The value $-8\,\mathrm{m}$ uses the final time $4\,\mathrm{s}$ as the base instead of the elapsed time.
