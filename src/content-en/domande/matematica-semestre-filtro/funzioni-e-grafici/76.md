A graph has velocity in $\mathrm{km/h}$ on the vertical axis and time in hours on the horizontal axis. What is the unit of signed area?

- $\mathrm{km/h}$
- $\mathrm{h/km}$
- $\mathrm{km}$
- $\mathrm{km/h^2}$

## Explanation

The unit of area on the graph is the unit of the vertical coordinate multiplied by the unit of the horizontal coordinate. Here the product is
$$
\frac{\mathrm{km}}{\mathrm{h}}\cdot\mathrm{h}=\mathrm{km},
$$
so the signed area is a displacement measured in kilometers. The unit $\mathrm{km/h^2}$ divides velocity by time and belongs to the slope of the graph, the acceleration. The unit $\mathrm{km/h}$ belongs to the vertical coordinate, while $\mathrm{h/km}$ is its reciprocal.
