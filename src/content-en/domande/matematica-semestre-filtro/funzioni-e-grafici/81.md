A calibration allows only $0\le c\le4$. The model is $A(c)=2c+3$. What range of signals can it produce within this domain?

- $[0,11]$
- $[3,11]$
- $[3,8]$
- $(3,11)$

## Explanation

The slope $2$ is positive, so $A$ increases as $c$ increases and reaches its extreme values at the endpoints of the domain. At $c=0$ we obtain $A(0)=3$, and at $c=4$ we obtain $A(4)=2\cdot 4+3=11$. The endpoints of the domain are included, so $3$ and $11$ are also included and the set of signals is $[3,11]$. The interval $[0,11]$ takes its lower endpoint from the values of $c$ instead of $A$. The interval $[3,8]$ omits the intercept when calculating $A(4)$.
