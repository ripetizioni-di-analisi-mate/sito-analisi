What is the displacement between the two highlighted points on the graph?

::grafico{f="4+4*x/3" xmin=0 xmax=4 ymin=0 ymax=10 nomi="t,v" punti="0,4;3,8" area="4+4*x/3;0;0;3" titolo="Time in seconds and velocity in meters per second."}

- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The highlighted region under the graph is a trapezoid. Its bases are the velocities $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and its height is the elapsed time $3\,\mathrm{s}$. The displacement is its area:
$$
\Delta s=\frac{4\,\mathrm{m/s}+8\,\mathrm{m/s}}{2}\cdot 3\,\mathrm{s}=18\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $24\,\mathrm{m}$ uses only the final velocity as if the graph were horizontal, and $12\,\mathrm{m}$ uses only the initial velocity. The value $6\,\mathrm{m}$ is the area of just the triangle above the rectangle of height $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
