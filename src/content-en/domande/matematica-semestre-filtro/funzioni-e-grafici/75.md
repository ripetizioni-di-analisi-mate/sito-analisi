On a position–time graph, the segment from $t=2$ to $t=5\,\mathrm{s}$ is horizontal at $s=7\,\mathrm{m}$. What does this mean?

- The object moves seven meters.
- The object travels at seven meters per second.
- The object remains at a position of seven meters.
- The object reaches the origin at five seconds.

## Explanation

On a position–time graph the vertical coordinate is position. A horizontal segment at $s=7\,\mathrm{m}$ means that the position stays at $7\,\mathrm{m}$ throughout the interval from $2$ to $5\,\mathrm{s}$. The slope of the segment is zero, so the velocity is zero and the object is at rest. The displacement during the interval is $7\,\mathrm{m}-7\,\mathrm{m}=0$. The answers giving seven meters traveled or seven meters per second interpret the vertical coordinate as a displacement or a velocity.
