A horizontal line passes through $(0,3)$. What is its equation?

- $y=3$
- $x=3$
- $y=3x$
- $y=x+3$

## Explanation

Every point on a horizontal line has the same vertical coordinate, and the horizontal coordinate can take any value. Since $(0,3)$ lies on the line, the common vertical coordinate is $3$ and the equation is $y=3$. The equation $x=3$ describes a vertical line. The equations $y=3x$ and $y=x+3$ have slopes $3$ and $1$ and therefore describe sloping lines. The second passes through $(0,3)$, but its vertical coordinate changes as $x$ changes.
