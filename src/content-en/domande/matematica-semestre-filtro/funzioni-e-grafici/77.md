A tank contains $60\,\mathrm{L}$ of water and drains at a constant rate of $4\,\mathrm{L/min}$. The remaining volume is $V(t)=60-4t$, with $t$ in minutes, until the tank is empty. What is the domain of the model?

- $[0,60]$
- $[0,+\infty)$
- $[15,60]$
- $[0,15]$

## Explanation

The model describes the tank starting from the moment it begins to drain, so $t\ge 0$. The remaining volume cannot be negative, so we must also require $60-4t\ge 0$. Both conditions must hold:
$$
\begin{cases}
t\ge 0\\
60-4t\ge 0
\end{cases}
$$
The second condition is equivalent to $t\le 15$, so the domain is $[0,15]$ in minutes. The formula can be evaluated for any $t$, but beyond $15$ minutes it would give negative volumes. The number $60$ is the initial volume, not a time.
