If $y$ is proportional to $x^2$ and equals $5$ at $x=1$, what is its value at $x=2$?

- $20$
- $9$
- $25$
- $10$

## Explanation

Using $y=kx^2$ and substituting $y=5$ at $x=1$ gives $k=5$. Therefore at $x=2$ we have
$$
y=5\cdot 2^2=20.
$$
Equivalently, doubling $x$ multiplies $y$ by $2^2=4$. The value $10$ applies the factor $2$ as if the relationship were a direct proportion. The value $9$ adds $5$ and $2^2$ instead of multiplying them.
