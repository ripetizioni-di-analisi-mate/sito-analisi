Given $y=3x+2$, calculate $y$ for $x=4$.

- $14$
- $12$
- $15$
- $10$

## Explanation

To find $y$, substitute $x=4$ into the formula $y=3x+2$. First multiply, then add the intercept:
$$
y=3\cdot 4+2=12+2=14.
$$
The value $12$ results from omitting the intercept $2$. The value $10$ comes from subtracting the intercept instead of adding it, that is, calculating $3\cdot 4-2$.
