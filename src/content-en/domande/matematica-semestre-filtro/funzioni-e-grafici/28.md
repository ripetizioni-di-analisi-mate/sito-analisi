An object moves along a straight line from $s=0$ to $s=9\,\mathrm{m}$ and then back to $s=3\,\mathrm{m}$, taking a total of $6\,\mathrm{s}$. What are its average speed and average velocity, in that order?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The distance traveled is the sum of the lengths of the two segments, $9\,\mathrm{m}+6\,\mathrm{m}=15\,\mathrm{m}$, so the average speed is $\dfrac{15\,\mathrm{m}}{6\,\mathrm{s}}=2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The displacement is $3\,\mathrm{m}-0=3\,\mathrm{m}$, so the average velocity is $\dfrac{3\,\mathrm{m}}{6\,\mathrm{s}}=0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The values differ because the object reverses direction. The value $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from adding $9$ and $3$, using the final position in place of the length of the return trip.
