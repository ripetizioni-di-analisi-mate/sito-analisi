What is the vertical intercept of the line through $(2,15)$ with slope $6$?

- $9$
- $6$
- $3$
- $15$

## Explanation

In the equation $y=mx+q$, substitute the point $(2,15)$ and the slope $m=6$ to obtain $15=6\cdot 2+q$. It follows that
$$
q=15-12=3.
$$
The value $9$ comes from subtracting the slope from the vertical coordinate without multiplying it by the horizontal coordinate $2$. The value $15$ is the vertical coordinate of the given point. It equals the intercept only if the horizontal coordinate is zero. The value $6$ is the slope.
