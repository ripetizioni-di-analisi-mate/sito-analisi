An object has velocity $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $10\,\mathrm{s}$ and then $-7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $10\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the total distance traveled, in meters?

- $70$
- $0$
- $140$
- $-140$

## Explanation

On the velocity–time graph the two intervals give rectangles, one above and one below the time axis. Each has unsigned area $(7\,\mathrm{m/s})(10\,\mathrm{s})=70\,\mathrm{m}$. The distance traveled is the sum of the absolute areas, so it equals $70+70=140\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $0$ is the displacement because the signed areas cancel and the object returns to its starting point. Distance is never negative, so $-140$ is ruled out.
