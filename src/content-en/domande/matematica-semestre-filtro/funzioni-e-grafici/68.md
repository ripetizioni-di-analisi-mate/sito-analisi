Three position measurements are $(t,s)=(0,0),(2,5),(4,8)$ in seconds and metres. By how many metres does the middle reading exceed the value on the straight line joining the endpoints?

- $0$
- $2$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

The middle time is halfway between the endpoints. On the line its position would be the mean of 0 and 8, or 4 m; the measured value is 5 m.
