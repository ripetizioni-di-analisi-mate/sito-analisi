In $4\,\mathrm{s}$ an object’s position changes from $-2\,\mathrm{m}$ to $6\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its average velocity over the interval?

- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The displacement is the difference between the final and the initial position.
$$
\Delta s=6\,\mathrm{m}-(-2\,\mathrm{m})=8\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The average velocity is the displacement divided by the elapsed time, so it is $\dfrac{8\,\mathrm{m}}{4\,\mathrm{s}}=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ results from calculating $6-2$, treating $-2$ as $+2$. The value $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ omits division by the elapsed time, while $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ gives the initial position.
