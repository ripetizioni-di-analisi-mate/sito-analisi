The quantity $y$ is directly proportional to $x$ and equals $15$ at $x=5$. What is $y$ at $x=2$?

- $2$
- $15$
- $7.5$
- $6$

## Explanation

In a direct proportion the ratio $\dfrac{y}{x}$ is constant. The known pair gives $k=\dfrac{15}{5}=3$, so the relationship is $y=3x$. At $x=2$ we obtain $y=3\cdot 2=6$. The value $7.5$ results from dividing $15$ by $2$, using the new value of $x$ as the divisor. The value $15$ treats $y$ as constant.
