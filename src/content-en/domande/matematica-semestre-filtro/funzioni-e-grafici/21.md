Two estimates of the same slope, $2.40\,\mathrm{cm/s}$ and $2.52\,\mathrm{cm/s}$, have the same uncertainty $u$. What is the smallest value of $u$ for which the estimates are consistent?

- $0.12\,\mathrm{cm/s}$
- $0.03\,\mathrm{cm/s}$
- $0.24\,\mathrm{cm/s}$
- $0.06\,\mathrm{cm/s}$

## Explanation

The estimates are consistent when the intervals $[2.40-u;\;2.40+u]$ and $[2.52-u;\;2.52+u]$ overlap. This occurs once the upper endpoint of the first reaches the lower endpoint of the second, that is, when $2.40+u\ge 2.52-u$. It follows that $2u\ge 0.12$, so $u\ge 0.06\,\mathrm{cm/s}$. The value $0.12\,\mathrm{cm/s}$ assigns the full difference to one estimate, whereas each interval extends by $u$ toward the other.
