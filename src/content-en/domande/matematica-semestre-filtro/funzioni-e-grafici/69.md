An object moves along a straight line from $s=1\,\mathrm{m}$ to $s=7\,\mathrm{m}$ without reversing direction. It then returns to $s=4\,\mathrm{m}$ without reversing direction again. What is the total distance traveled?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distance traveled is the sum of the lengths of the sections, each traveled without reversing direction. The object travels $7\,\mathrm{m}-1\,\mathrm{m}=6\,\mathrm{m}$ on the outward trip and $7\,\mathrm{m}-4\,\mathrm{m}=3\,\mathrm{m}$ on the return trip. Its total distance is therefore $6+3=9\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $3\,\mathrm{m}$ is the overall displacement $4-1$, which accounts for direction and does not measure the length of the path. The value $11\,\mathrm{m}$ adds the positions $7$ and $4$ instead of the lengths of the sections.
