On a position–time graph the position increases by $150\,\mathrm{cm}$ in $3\,\mathrm{s}$. Express the average slope in meters per second.

- $0.05\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $50\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Before taking the ratio, express the displacement in meters. Since $1\,\mathrm{m}=100\,\mathrm{cm}$, we have $150\,\mathrm{cm}=1.50\,\mathrm{m}$. The average slope is therefore
$$
\frac{1.50\,\mathrm{m}}{3\,\mathrm{s}}=0.50\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
The value $50\,\mathrm{m/s}$ divides centimeters by seconds without converting units and actually corresponds to $50\,\mathrm{cm/s}$. The value $1.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$ omits division by the elapsed time, while $0.05\,\mathrm{m/s}$ gives a conversion error of a factor of $10$.
