An object has velocity $v(t)=6-2t$, with $v$ in meters per second and $t$ in seconds, starting at $t=0$. At what time does it return to its initial position?

- $t=6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $t=9\,\mathrm{s}$
- $t=12\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The velocity is zero when $6-2t=0$, that is, at $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$. Up to that time the region above the axis is a triangle of base $3\,\mathrm{s}$ and height $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with area $9\,\mathrm{m}$. A time $b$ after the reversal, the velocity is $-2b$, so the triangle below the axis has area $\dfrac{b\cdot 2b}{2}=b^2$. The object returns to its initial position when the displacement is zero, that is, when $b^2=9$. Therefore $b=3\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t=6\,\mathrm{s}$. The value $3\,\mathrm{s}$ is the time at which the object reverses direction.
