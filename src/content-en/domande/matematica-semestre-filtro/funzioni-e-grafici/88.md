The model $y=kx$ holds for $x\ge0$ with $k>0$. Which observation rules out this model if the data are exact?

- A value $y>0$ is observed when $x=0$.
- A value $y>0$ is observed when $x=1$.
- $y$ increases as $x$ increases.
- Doubling $x$ is observed to double $y$.

## Explanation

In the model $y=kx$, substituting $x=0$ gives $y=k\cdot 0=0$ for any value of $k$. If the data are exact, observing $y>0$ at $x=0$ therefore contradicts the model and rules it out. The other observations are consistent with $y=kx$ and $k>0$. In fact, at $x=1$ the model gives $y=k>0$, the function is increasing, and doubling $x$ doubles $y$.
