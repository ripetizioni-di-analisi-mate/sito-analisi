The model $m=12+0.8V$ gives the mass in grams of a container holding a volume $V$ of liquid in milliliters. What does $12$ represent?

- The mass of the empty container in grams.
- The density of the liquid in grams per milliliter.
- The volume of the container in milliliters.
- The volume at which the total mass is zero.

## Explanation

At $V=0$ the container holds no liquid and the model gives $m=12+0.8\cdot 0=12$. The intercept $12$ has the same unit as $m$, so it is the mass of the empty container in grams. The coefficient $0.8$ is the slope. It gives the mass added per milliliter and represents the density of the liquid in $\mathrm{g/mL}$. The volume that would make the total mass zero is $V=-15\,\mathrm{mL}$, which has no physical meaning.
