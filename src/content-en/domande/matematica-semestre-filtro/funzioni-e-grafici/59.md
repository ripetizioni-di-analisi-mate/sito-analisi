A gas model uses $p(V)=\dfrac{36}{V}$ for $V>0$. What can we say about $V=0$?

- It gives a pressure of zero.
- It gives a pressure of 36.
- It gives a pressure of one.
- It is excluded because the denominator would be zero.

## Explanation

The domain of the model is $V>0$ because the volume of the gas is positive and $V$ appears in the denominator. At $V=0$ the ratio $\dfrac{36}{V}$ is undefined because division by zero is undefined. Thus the model assigns no pressure to $V=0$. The numerical answers treat $\dfrac{36}{0}$ as if it were equal to $0$, $36$, or $1$.
