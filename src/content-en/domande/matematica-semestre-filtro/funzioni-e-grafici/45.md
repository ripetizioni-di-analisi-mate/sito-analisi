A sensor is calibrated only between $0$ and $8\,\mathrm{min}$, inclusive. Its formula can be evaluated at $t=10\,\mathrm{min}$. Which statement is correct?

- The value at ten minutes is outside the calibration range.
- The calibration covers every positive time.
- The value at ten minutes is verified because the formula gives it.
- The maximum allowed time is ten minutes.

## Explanation

The model’s domain is the calibration interval $[0,8]$ in minutes because the formula has been checked against measurements only within this interval. The time $t=10\,\mathrm{min}$ lies outside it, so the calculated value is an extrapolation that has not been verified. Being able to substitute a number into a formula establishes that the calculation is possible, without establishing the validity of the model. The maximum allowed time is $8\,\mathrm{min}$.
