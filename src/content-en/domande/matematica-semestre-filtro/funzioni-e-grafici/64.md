Two objects have the same vertical coordinate on a position–time graph at time $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$. What follows?

- The two objects have the same velocity at that time.
- Both objects are at rest.
- Both objects started at the origin.
- The two objects are at the same position at that time.

## Explanation

On a position–time graph, the vertical coordinate gives the object’s position at that time. If two graphs have the same vertical coordinate at $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$, the objects are at the same position and therefore meet. Velocity corresponds to the slope of each graph, and the two slopes can differ even at their common point. A single shared vertical coordinate gives no information about the initial positions or whether the objects are at rest.
