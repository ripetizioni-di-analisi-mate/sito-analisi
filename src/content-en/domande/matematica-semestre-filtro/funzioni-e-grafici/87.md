A line has vertical intercept $5$ and meets the horizontal axis at $x=10$. What is its slope?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-2$
- $2$

## Explanation

The line passes through $(0,5)$ because its intercept is $5$, and through $(10,0)$ because it meets the horizontal axis at $x=10$. Its slope is therefore
$$
m=\frac{0-5}{10-0}=-\frac{1}{2}.
$$
The sign is negative because the vertical coordinate decreases as the horizontal coordinate increases. The value $-2$ is the reciprocal ratio, with the horizontal change in the numerator. The value $\dfrac{1}{2}$ has the wrong sign.
