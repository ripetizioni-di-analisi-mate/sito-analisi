An object’s position is $s(t)=3+5t$, with $s$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. The origin is moved by $8\,\mathrm{m}$ in the positive direction. Which position function $s'(t)$ describes the same motion relative to the new origin?

- $s'(t)=5t-5$
- $s'(t)=5t+11$
- $s'(t)=-3t+3$
- $s'(t)=5t-8$

## Explanation

The new origin is $8\,\mathrm{m}$ farther in the positive direction, so every position measured from it is smaller by $8\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore
$$
s'(t)=s(t)-8=3+5t-8=5t-5.
$$
The slope stays $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ because subtracting $8$ from both positions leaves their difference unchanged. Only the intercept changes. The formula $5t+11$ adds $8$ instead of subtracting it, while $-3t+3$ subtracts $8$ from the slope.
