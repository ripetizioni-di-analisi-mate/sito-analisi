An object’s position changes from $5\,\mathrm{m}$ at time $2\,\mathrm{s}$ to $21\,\mathrm{m}$ at time $6\,\mathrm{s}$. Calculate its average velocity.

- $\dfrac{21}{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Average velocity is displacement divided by elapsed time. The displacement is $21\,\mathrm{m}-5\,\mathrm{m}=16\,\mathrm{m}$ and the elapsed time is $6\,\mathrm{s}-2\,\mathrm{s}=4\,\mathrm{s}$, so
$$
v_m=\frac{16\,\mathrm{m}}{4\,\mathrm{s}}=4\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
The value $\dfrac{21}{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from dividing the final position by the elapsed time without subtracting the initial position. The value $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the reciprocal of the required ratio, while $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ reverses the order of subtraction only in the numerator.
