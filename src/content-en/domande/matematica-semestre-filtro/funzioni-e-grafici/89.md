Measurements at times $1$ and $5\,\mathrm{s}$ give positions $2$ and $18\,\mathrm{m}$, respectively. Assuming the graph is linear over the interval, what is the estimated position at $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The line through the two points has slope
$$
m=\frac{18-2}{5-1}\,\mathrm{m/s}=4\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
From $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$ to $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$, the elapsed time is $2\,\mathrm{s}$, so the estimated position is $2\,\mathrm{m}+(4\,\mathrm{m/s})(2\,\mathrm{s})=10\,\mathrm{m}$. This is also the mean of $2$ and $18$ because $3\,\mathrm{s}$ is the midpoint of the interval. The estimate assumes a linear relationship between the measurements. The value $12\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from calculating $4\cdot 3$ without including the position at $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$.
