Two objects have velocities $v_A(t)=2+3t$ and $v_B(t)=8+t$, with $v$ in meters per second and $t$ in seconds. Their lines intersect on the velocity–time graph. Which statement describes that instant?

- The two objects are at the same position at $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$.
- The two objects have the same velocity, $11\,\mathrm{m/s}$, at $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$.
- The two objects have the same acceleration at $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$.
- The two objects have the same velocity, $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$, at $t=11\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

The lines intersect when $2+3t=8+t$, that is, $2t=6$, so the time is $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$. Substitution gives $v_A(3)=2+9=11\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_B(3)=8+3=11\,\mathrm{m/s}$. On a velocity–time graph the vertical coordinate is velocity, so the intersection means that the velocities are equal. The positions depend on the areas under the graphs and on the initial positions, which are not given. The accelerations are the slopes, $3$ and $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and they differ at every instant.
