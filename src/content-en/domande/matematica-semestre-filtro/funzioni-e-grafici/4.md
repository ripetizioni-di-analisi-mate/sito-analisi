At constant density, the volume of an object is multiplied by $3$. By what factor does its mass change?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $9$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

At constant density $\rho$, we have $m=\rho V$, so mass is directly proportional to volume. If the volume becomes $3V_1$, the mass becomes
$$
m_2=\rho\,(3V_1)=3\,\rho V_1=3m_1.
$$
The mass is therefore multiplied by $3$. The factor $\dfrac{1}{3}$ corresponds to an inverse proportion, which relates volume to density at fixed mass. The factor $9$ would give a dependence on the square of the volume, which is absent from $m=\rho V$.
