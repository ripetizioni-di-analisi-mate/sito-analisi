Over a certain interval, a velocity–time graph is horizontal at $v=-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which statement describes the motion?

- Velocity and acceleration are both negative.
- The velocity is negative and the acceleration is zero.
- The object is at rest.
- The velocity is negative and the acceleration is positive.

## Explanation

On a velocity–time graph the vertical coordinate is velocity, so the velocity on this segment is $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and is negative. The slope of the graph is the acceleration. Since the segment is horizontal, its slope is zero and the acceleration is zero. The object therefore moves at constant velocity in the negative direction. The answer giving negative acceleration confuses the sign of the velocity with the sign of its change. An object at rest would give a segment on the time axis.
