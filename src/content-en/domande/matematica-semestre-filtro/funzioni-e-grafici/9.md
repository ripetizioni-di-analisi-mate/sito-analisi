The response of an instrument is $A(c)=(2\,\mathrm{L/mol})c+0.10$ for $0\le c\le 0.20\,\mathrm{mol/L}$. Determine which concentration in the allowed range produces the response $A=0.60$.

- No concentration in the allowed range.
- $0.25\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $0.10\,\mathrm{mol/L}$

## Explanation

We seek the concentration for which $(2\,\mathrm{L/mol})c+0.10=0.60$. Subtracting the baseline response gives $(2\,\mathrm{L/mol})c=0.50$, so
$$
c=\frac{0.50}{2\,\mathrm{L/mol}}=0.25\,\mathrm{mol/L}.
$$
The model’s domain is $0\le c\le 0.20\,\mathrm{mol/L}$, and $0.25\,\mathrm{mol/L}$ falls outside it. Indeed, the maximum response, at $c=0.20\,\mathrm{mol/L}$, is $0.50$. Thus **no concentration in the allowed range** produces the response $0.60$. The value $0.25\,\mathrm{mol/L}$ solves the equation but is outside the calibration range.
