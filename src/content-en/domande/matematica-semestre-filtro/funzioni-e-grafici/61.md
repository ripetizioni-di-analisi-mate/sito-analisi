An object’s velocity changes linearly from $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $-7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $3\,\mathrm{s}$. Which answer gives the average acceleration and describes how the speed changes?

- $a_m=-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and the speed increases.
- $a_m=-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and the speed decreases.
- $a_m=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and the speed increases.
- $a_m=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and the speed decreases.

## Explanation

The change in velocity is $-7\,\mathrm{m/s}-(-1\,\mathrm{m/s})=-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so
$$
a_m=\frac{-6\,\mathrm{m/s}}{3\,\mathrm{s}}=-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
The speed changes from $1$ to $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so it increases. Velocity and acceleration are both negative, and the object moves faster and faster in the negative direction. The value $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ comes from calculating the change as $-1-(-7)$, reversing the subtraction. The speed would decrease if velocity and acceleration had opposite signs.
