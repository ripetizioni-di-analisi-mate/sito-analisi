A table contains $(0,2)$, $(2,6)$, and $(4,10)$. Which affine relationship fits all the data?

- $y=2x$
- $y=4x+2$
- $y=2x-2$
- $y=2x+2$

## Explanation

The point $(0,2)$ has horizontal coordinate zero, so the intercept is $q=2$. The slope between the first two points is $\dfrac{6-2}{2-0}=2$, and the slope between the second and third is $\dfrac{10-6}{4-2}=2$. Since the slopes agree, all three points lie on the line $y=2x+2$. The formula $y=2x$ has the correct slope but passes through the origin. The formula $y=4x+2$ uses the vertical change $4$ as the slope without dividing by the horizontal change $2$.
