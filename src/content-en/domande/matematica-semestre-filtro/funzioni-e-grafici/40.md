A concentration is $10\,\mathrm{mmol/L}$ at $t=0$, $6\,\mathrm{mmol/L}$ at $t=2\,\mathrm{min}$, and $3\,\mathrm{mmol/L}$ at $t=8\,\mathrm{min}$. Over which interval does it decrease faster on average, and by how much per minute?

- Between $2$ and $8\,\mathrm{min}$, by $3\,\mathrm{mmol/L}$ per minute.
- Between $0$ and $2\,\mathrm{min}$, by $4\,\mathrm{mmol/L}$ per minute.
- Between $0$ and $2\,\mathrm{min}$, by $2\,\mathrm{mmol/L}$ per minute.
- Between $2$ and $8\,\mathrm{min}$, by $0.5\,\mathrm{mmol/L}$ per minute.

## Explanation

In the first interval the concentration decreases by $10-6=4\,\mathrm{mmol/L}$ in $2\,\mathrm{min}$, or $2\,\mathrm{mmol/L}$ per minute. In the second it decreases by $6-3=3\,\mathrm{mmol/L}$ in $8-2=6\,\mathrm{min}$, or $0.5\,\mathrm{mmol/L}$ per minute. The decrease is therefore faster between $0$ and $2\,\mathrm{min}$. The answers $3$ and $4\,\mathrm{mmol/L}$ per minute compare the decreases without dividing by their different elapsed times.
