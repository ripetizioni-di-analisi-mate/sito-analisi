Over a time interval the displacement is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the distance traveled is $10\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the unsigned area of the region below the time axis on the velocity–time graph?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Let $A$ and $B$ denote the unsigned areas above and below the time axis. The displacement is the signed sum of the areas and the distance is the sum of their absolute values, so both of the following equations must hold:
$$
\begin{cases}
A-B=2\\
A+B=10
\end{cases}
$$
Subtracting the first equation from the second gives $2B=8$, so $B=4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $A=6\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $6\,\mathrm{m}$ is the area above the axis. The value $8\,\mathrm{m}$ omits division by $2$.
