Which of the following relationships fits the data $(1,12)$, $(2,6)$, and $(3,4)$?

- $y=12x$
- $y=18-6x$
- $y=\dfrac{12}{x}$
- $y=12x^2$

## Explanation

The products of the coordinates are $1\cdot 12=12$, $2\cdot 6=12$, and $3\cdot 4=12$. A constant product characterizes an inverse proportion, so the relationship is $y=\dfrac{12}{x}$. The graph of $y=18-6x$ passes through the first two points, but at $x=3$ it gives $0$ instead of $4$. The functions $y=12x$ and $y=12x^2$ increase for $x>0$, whereas the data decrease as $x$ increases.
