Compare $y_A=2x+1$ and $y_B=x+4$. At which horizontal coordinate does the difference $y_A-y_B$ equal $2$?

- $1$
- $5$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

Subtract the two expressions to find their difference:
$$
y_A-y_B=(2x+1)-(x+4)=x-3.
$$
Setting $x-3=2$ gives $x=5$. Indeed, $y_A(5)=11$ and $y_B(5)=9$ differ by $2$. The value $3$ is the horizontal coordinate of the intersection, where the difference is zero. The value $1$ results from the sign error $x-3=-2$, which corresponds to a difference of $y_B-y_A=2$.
