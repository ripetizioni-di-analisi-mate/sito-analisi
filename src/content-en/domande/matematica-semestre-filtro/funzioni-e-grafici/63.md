The graph of a quadratic relationship $y=kx^2$ passes through $(2,8)$. Which point lies on the same graph?

- $(-2,-8)$
- $(4,16)$
- $(0,2)$
- $(-2,8)$

## Explanation

The point $(2,8)$ gives $k=\dfrac{8}{2^2}=2$, so the relationship is $y=2x^2$. At $x=-2$ we obtain $y=2\cdot(-2)^2=8$, so the point $(-2,8)$ lies on the graph. The graph is symmetric about the vertical axis because $x$ and $-x$ have the same square. The point $(4,16)$ applies a factor of $2$ as in a direct proportion, whereas the actual relationship gives $y=32$. The point $(-2,-8)$ also changes the sign of the vertical coordinate, even though the output is always nonnegative.
