For what value of $k$ does the line $y=kx+1$ meet the line $y=2x+5$ at the point with horizontal coordinate $x=2$?

- $k=2$
- $k=5$
- $k=4$
- $k=3$

## Explanation

The common point has horizontal coordinate $2$ and lies on $y=2x+5$, so its vertical coordinate is $2\cdot 2+5=9$. The same point $(2,9)$ lies on $y=kx+1$, so $2k+1=9$. This gives $2k=8$ and $k=4$. The value $k=5$ results from the sign error $2k=9+1$. With $k=2$ the two lines have equal slopes and different intercepts, so they are parallel and never meet.
