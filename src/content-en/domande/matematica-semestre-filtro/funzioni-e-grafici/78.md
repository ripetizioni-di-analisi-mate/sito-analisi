A straight segment on a position–time graph has a constant positive slope. Which statement is correct?

- The velocity increases at a constant rate.
- The velocity is zero.
- The velocity is positive and constant.
- The acceleration is positive and constant.

## Explanation

On a position–time graph the slope of a segment is the object’s velocity. The segment is straight and has a positive slope, so the velocity is positive and constant. Since the velocity does not change, the acceleration on this segment is zero. The answers giving increasing velocity or positive acceleration interpret the position–time graph as if it were a velocity–time graph, on which a rising straight line indicates constant positive acceleration.
