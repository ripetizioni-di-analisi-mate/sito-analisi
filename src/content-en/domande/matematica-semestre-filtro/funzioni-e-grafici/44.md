The graph consists of the line segment shown. The left endpoint is open and the right endpoint is closed. What is the domain?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=6 linee="1,2 4,5" punti="1,2,vuoto;4,5,pieno" titolo="Open circle at 1 and filled circle at 4."}

- $[1,4]$
- $(1,4]$
- $[1,4)$
- $(1,4)$

## Explanation

The domain is the set of horizontal coordinates of the points on the graph, and the segment runs from horizontal coordinate $1$ to $4$. The open circle at the left means that the point with horizontal coordinate $1$ is absent, so $1$ is excluded. The right endpoint is filled, so $4$ is included. The domain is therefore $(1,4]$. The interval $[1,4)$ reverses the meanings of the two circles. The vertical coordinates $2$ and $5$ do not enter into the domain.
