An empty container has mass $16\,\mathrm{g}$. With $30\,\mathrm{mL}$ of a homogeneous liquid in the container, the total mass is $52\,\mathrm{g}$. Calculate the density of the liquid.

- $1.73\,\mathrm{g/mL}$
- $0.83\,\mathrm{g/mL}$
- $0.53\,\mathrm{g/mL}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{g/mL}$

## Explanation

The scale measures the combined mass of the container and the liquid, so the mass of the liquid is $52\,\mathrm{g}-16\,\mathrm{g}=36\,\mathrm{g}$. Density is the mass of the liquid divided by its volume:
$$
\rho=\frac{36\,\mathrm{g}}{30\,\mathrm{mL}}=1.2\,\mathrm{g/mL}.
$$
The value $1.73\,\mathrm{g/mL}$ comes from dividing the total mass by the volume without subtracting the container’s mass. The value $0.53\,\mathrm{g/mL}$ divides the mass of the empty container by the volume. The value $0.83\,\mathrm{g/mL}$ is the reciprocal ratio $\dfrac{30}{36}$.
