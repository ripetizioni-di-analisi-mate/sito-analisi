The speed of an object of constant mass is initially positive and is multiplied by $3$. By what factor does its kinetic energy change?

- $3$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is $E=\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$. With the mass fixed, the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}m$ is common to both states and cancels in the ratio:
$$
\frac{E_2}{E_1}=\frac{\frac{1}{2}m(3v_1)^2}{\frac{1}{2}mv_1^2}=\frac{9v_1^2}{v_1^2}=9.
$$
Thus the kinetic energy is multiplied by $9$. The value $3$ comes from applying the speed factor to the energy without squaring it. The values $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{1}{9}$ treat the relationship as inverse.
