A line passes through $(1,7)$ and $(11,37)$. What is its slope?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $3$
- $30$
- $2$

## Explanation

The slope is the change in the vertical coordinate divided by the change in the horizontal coordinate:
$$
m=\frac{37-7}{11-1}=\frac{30}{10}=3.
$$
Interchanging the points changes the signs of both numerator and denominator, leaving the slope at $3$. The value $30$ is only the vertical change, without division by the horizontal change. The value $\dfrac{1}{3}$ is the reciprocal ratio, with the horizontal change in the numerator.
