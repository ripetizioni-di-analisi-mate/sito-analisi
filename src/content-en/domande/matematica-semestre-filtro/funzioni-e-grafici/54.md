An instrument gives a background signal $0.4$. With the sample, the total signal is $1.6$. The correct model is $S=0.4+3c$. What is $c$?

- $\dfrac{8}{15}$
- $1.2$
- $0.2$
- $0.4$

## Explanation

Set $0.4+3c=1.6$. Subtracting the background signal gives the signal due to the sample, $3c=1.2$. Dividing by the coefficient $3$ gives
$$
c=\frac{1.2}{3}=0.4.
$$
The value $1.2$ is the sample signal before division by $3$. The value $\dfrac{8}{15}$ uses $3$ as the divisor for the total signal $1.6$ without first subtracting the background.
