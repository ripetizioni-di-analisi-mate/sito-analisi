Which pair of values distinguishes $y=3x$ from $y=3x+2$ at $x=0$?

- $3$ and $3$, respectively.
- $0$ and $0$, respectively.
- $0$ and $2$, respectively.
- $2$ and $0$, respectively.

## Explanation

Substituting $x=0$ makes the term $3x$ zero in both relationships. For $y=3x$ we obtain $y=0$, and for $y=3x+2$ we obtain $y=2$. The values at $x=0$ are the intercepts, $0$ and $2$, showing that the second line does not pass through the origin. The pair $3$ and $3$ gives the slopes, which are equal and therefore do not distinguish the relationships. The pair $2$ and $0$ reverses the required order.
