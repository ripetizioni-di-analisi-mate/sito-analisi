Two lines have slopes $-2$ and $-5$. When $x$ increases by one unit, which line’s vertical coordinate decreases more?

- That of the line with slope $-2$.
- That of the line with slope $-5$.
- Both vertical coordinates decrease by the same amount.
- The answer depends on the intercepts.

## Explanation

The slope is the change in the vertical coordinate when the horizontal coordinate increases by one unit. On the line of slope $-2$, the vertical coordinate changes by $-2$, and on the line of slope $-5$, it changes by $-5$. The decreases therefore have magnitudes $2$ and $5$, so the line with slope $-5$ has the greater decrease. The intercepts determine the values at $x=0$ and do not affect the changes. The number $-2$ is greater than $-5$ but represents the smaller decrease.
