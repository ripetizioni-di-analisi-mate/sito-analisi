A concentration follows the relationship $c(t)=12-1.5t$, with $c$ in $\mathrm{mmol/L}$ and $t$ in minutes, for $0\le t\le 8$. At which times in the domain is the concentration below $3\,\mathrm{mmol/L}$?

- $[0,6)$
- $(6,+\infty)$
- $[6,8]$
- $(6,8]$

## Explanation

The threshold is reached when $12-1.5t=3$, that is, $1.5t=9$, so $t=6\,\mathrm{min}$. The slope is negative, so the concentration decreases with time and is below $3$ after that instant. The time $t=6$ is excluded because $c(6)=3$ is not less than $3$. The time $t=8$ is included because it belongs to the domain and $c(8)=0$. The required set is therefore $(6,8]$. The interval $[0,6)$ reverses the direction of the inequality, while $(6,+\infty)$ extends outside the domain.
