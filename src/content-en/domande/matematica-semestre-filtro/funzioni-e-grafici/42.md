On a graph of total mass $M$ in grams versus liquid volume $V$ in milliliters, the line has slope $0.9$ and intercept $15$. What are the units of the slope and the intercept?

- Slope in $\mathrm{g/mL}$, intercept in $\mathrm{g}$.
- Slope in $\mathrm{g}$, intercept in $\mathrm{g/mL}$.
- Slope in $\mathrm{mL/g}$, intercept in $\mathrm{g}$.
- Slope in $\mathrm{g/mL}$, intercept in $\mathrm{mL}$.

## Explanation

The slope is a change in the vertical coordinate divided by a change in the horizontal coordinate, so its units are those of $M$ divided by those of $V$, giving $\mathrm{g/mL}$. On this graph the slope is the density of the liquid. The intercept is the value of $M$ at $V=0$, so it has the same unit as the vertical coordinate, $\mathrm{g}$, and represents the mass of the empty container. The unit $\mathrm{mL/g}$ results from dividing the horizontal change by the vertical change.
