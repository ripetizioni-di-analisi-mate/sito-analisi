Pressure $p$ and volume $V$ are inversely proportional. At $V=3\,\mathrm{L}$, we have $p=80\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the constant $pV$?

- $\dfrac{80}{3}\,\mathrm{kPa\cdot L}$
- $83\,\mathrm{kPa\cdot L}$
- $240\,\mathrm{kPa\cdot L}$
- $240\,\mathrm{kPa/L}$

## Explanation

For an inverse proportion, the product $pV$ is constant, so we can calculate it from the known pair of values:
$$
pV=(80\,\mathrm{kPa})(3\,\mathrm{L})=240\,\mathrm{kPa\cdot L}.
$$
The unit is the product of the units of the two factors. The answer $240\,\mathrm{kPa/L}$ has the correct numerical value but the units of a ratio. The value $\dfrac{80}{3}\,\mathrm{kPa\cdot L}$ divides the two quantities, as in a direct proportion, while $83$ adds them.
