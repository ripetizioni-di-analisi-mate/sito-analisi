The lines $y=3x-2$ and $y=3x+4$ describe two instruments. Which comparison is correct?

- The second instrument always reads six units higher.
- The lines intersect when $x=0$.
- The second line has a slope six units greater than the first.
- The first instrument always reads six units higher.

## Explanation

The two lines have the same slope $3$, so they are parallel. The difference between their vertical coordinates at the same $x$ is
$$
(3x+4)-(3x-2)=6,
$$
and is independent of $x$. The second line therefore always gives a value six units higher, and the lines never meet. The number $6$ is the difference between the intercepts. The slopes are equal. At $x=0$ the vertical coordinates are $-2$ and $4$, so the lines do not meet at $x=0$.
