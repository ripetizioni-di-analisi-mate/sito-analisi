A line passes through $(2,5)$ and $(5,14)$. What is its vertical coordinate at $x=6$?

- $15$
- $16$
- $18$
- $17$

## Explanation

The slope of the line is
$$
m=\frac{14-5}{5-2}=\frac{9}{3}=3.
$$
As the horizontal coordinate goes from $5$ to $6$, it increases by $1$, so the vertical coordinate increases by $3$, from $14$ to $17$. Using the formula gives $q=5-3\cdot 2=-1$ and $y=3\cdot 6-1=17$. The value $15$ increases the vertical coordinate by $1$ as if the slope were $1$. The value $18$ calculates $3\cdot 6$ without the intercept.
