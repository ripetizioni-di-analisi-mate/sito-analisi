A recorder shows positive velocity for the first 5 s and negative velocity for the next 2 s. The unsigned area of each part is 8 m. What can be inferred without knowing how velocity varies within each part?

- Velocity was constant
- The total distance is 16 m
- The total distance is 8 m
- The turning point was reached after 2 s

## Explanation

When the sign of velocity is known on each interval, distance is the sum of the unsigned areas. The total is $8+8=16$ m.
