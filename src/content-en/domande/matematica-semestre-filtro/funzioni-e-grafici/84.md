Over the same time interval, two objects have straight velocity–time graphs with equal slopes and different initial vertical coordinates. Which quantity is the same for both objects over that interval?

- Initial velocity.
- Final velocity.
- Displacement over the interval.
- Acceleration.

## Explanation

On a velocity–time graph the slope is acceleration. The two lines have the same slope, so the objects have the same acceleration. The different initial vertical coordinates indicate different initial velocities, and the difference in velocity remains constant throughout the interval. Thus the final velocities also differ. The displacements are the signed areas under the graphs, and their difference is represented by a rectangle whose height is the difference between the velocities.
