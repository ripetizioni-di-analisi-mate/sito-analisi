The position is $s(t)=(4\,\mathrm{m/s})t+7\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the initial position?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The initial position is the position at $t=0$. Substituting $t=0$ makes the term $(4\,\mathrm{m/s})t$ zero, so
$$
s(0)=(4\,\mathrm{m/s})\cdot 0+7\,\mathrm{m}=7\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
This is the intercept on the position–time graph. The number $4$ is the coefficient of $t$ and gives the velocity, whose units are $\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $11\,\mathrm{m}$ is the position after $1\,\mathrm{s}$ because $s(1)=4+7$.
