An object has average velocity $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ over $2\,\mathrm{s}$, then average velocity $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ over $1\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its average velocity over the three seconds?

- $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Average velocity is total displacement divided by total elapsed time. The displacements over the two intervals are $(3\,\mathrm{m/s})(2\,\mathrm{s})=6\,\mathrm{m}$ and $(6\,\mathrm{m/s})(1\,\mathrm{s})=6\,\mathrm{m}$, so
$$
v_m=\frac{12\,\mathrm{m}}{3\,\mathrm{s}}=4\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
The value $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the arithmetic mean of the two velocities. It differs from the average velocity because the first interval lasts twice as long as the second and carries more weight.
