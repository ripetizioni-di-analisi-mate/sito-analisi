Two levels are given by $h_A(t)=1+2t$ and $h_B(t)=13-t$, with $h$ in centimeters and $t$ in minutes. Both formulas apply only for $0\le t\le 3$. At what time in this interval are the levels equal?

- $t=4\,\mathrm{min}$
- $t=3\,\mathrm{min}$
- $t=12\,\mathrm{min}$
- At no time in the interval.

## Explanation

The levels are equal when $1+2t=13-t$, that is, $3t=12$, so $t=4\,\mathrm{min}$. This time falls outside the interval $0\le t\le 3$ where the formulas apply. In fact, the difference $h_B(t)-h_A(t)=12-3t$ is still $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ at $t=3\,\mathrm{min}$ and is positive throughout the interval, so the second level is always higher. The answer $t=4\,\mathrm{min}$ accepts the algebraic solution without checking the domain, while $t=12\,\mathrm{min}$ omits division by $3$.
