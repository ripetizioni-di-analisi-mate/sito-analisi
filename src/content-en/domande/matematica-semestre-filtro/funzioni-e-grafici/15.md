At what value of $x$ does the line $y=10-13x$ intersect the horizontal axis?

- $23$
- $\dfrac{10}{13}$
- $-\dfrac{10}{13}$
- $1.3$

## Explanation

A point on the horizontal axis has vertical coordinate zero. Therefore set $10-13x=0$, giving $13x=10$ and
$$
x=\frac{10}{13}.
$$
The value $-\dfrac{10}{13}$ comes from using the wrong sign when moving $13x$ to the right-hand side. The value $23$ adds the two coefficients, while $1.3$ is the reciprocal ratio $\dfrac{13}{10}$.
