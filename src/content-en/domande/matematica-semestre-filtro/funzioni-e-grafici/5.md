For a fixed amount of an ideal gas at constant temperature, $pV=k$. The volume is multiplied by $4$. What is the ratio $\dfrac{p_2}{p_1}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $4$
- $16$
- $1$

## Explanation

For a fixed amount of gas at constant temperature, the product $pV$ remains equal to $k$, so pressure and volume are inversely proportional. With $V_2=4V_1$ we obtain
$$
p_2=\frac{k}{4V_1}=\frac{1}{4}\cdot\frac{k}{V_1}=\frac{p_1}{4}.
$$
Therefore $\dfrac{p_2}{p_1}=\dfrac{1}{4}$. The value $4$ treats the relationship as a direct proportion. The value $16$ squares the factor, as in a quadratic relationship.
