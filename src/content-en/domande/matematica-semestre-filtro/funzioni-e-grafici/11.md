Two objects have positions $s_A(t)=1\,\mathrm{m}+(3\,\mathrm{m/s})t$ and $s_B(t)=13\,\mathrm{m}-(1\,\mathrm{m/s})t$ for $t\ge 0$. Find the time at which they meet.

- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{14}{4}\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The two objects meet when their positions are equal, that is, when $s_A(t)=s_B(t)$. With $t$ in seconds the equation is $1+3t=13-t$. Moving the terms involving $t$ to the left gives $4t=12$, so $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$, which satisfies $t\ge 0$. At that time both objects are at $10\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $6\,\mathrm{s}$ comes from subtracting the speeds instead of adding them, that is, solving $2t=12$.
