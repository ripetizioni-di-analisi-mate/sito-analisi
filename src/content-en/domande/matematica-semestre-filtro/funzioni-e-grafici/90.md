A position–time graph is given by $s(t)=1+t^2$, with $s$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is the average velocity from $t=1$ to $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The endpoint positions are $s(1)=1+1=2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $s(3)=1+9=10\,\mathrm{m}$. The average velocity is the slope of the secant line through the two points on the graph:
$$
v_m=\frac{10\,\mathrm{m}-2\,\mathrm{m}}{3\,\mathrm{s}-1\,\mathrm{s}}=4\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
The value $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from dividing the final position by the elapsed time without subtracting the initial position. The value $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ omits division by the elapsed time.
