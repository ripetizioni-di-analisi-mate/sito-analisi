Given $\vec A=(-1,4)$, calculate $-3\vec A$.

- $(-3,12)$
- $(3,4)$
- $(3,-12)$
- $(-1,-12)$

## Explanation

To multiply a vector by a number, multiply each component by that number:
$$
-3\vec A=\bigl(-3\cdot(-1),\;-3\cdot 4\bigr)=(3,-12).
$$
The factor is negative, so the resulting vector points opposite to $\vec A$ and its magnitude is $3$ times that of $\vec A$. The vector $(-3,12)$ comes from multiplying by $3$ without changing the signs, thus ignoring the sign of the factor.
