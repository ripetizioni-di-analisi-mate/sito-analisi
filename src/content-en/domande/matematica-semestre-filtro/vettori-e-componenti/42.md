A nonzero vector has two negative components. In which interval does its direction angle lie when measured counterclockwise from the positive $x$ axis in the range from $0^\circ$ inclusive to $360^\circ$ exclusive?

- $(0^\circ,90^\circ)$
- $(180^\circ,270^\circ)$
- $(90^\circ,180^\circ)$
- $(270^\circ,360^\circ)$

## Explanation

When the tail of a vector is at the origin, the coordinates of its head are its components. If both are negative, the head lies in the third quadrant, corresponding to angles between $180^\circ$ and $270^\circ$. Indeed, $\cos\theta<0$ and $\sin\theta<0$ throughout that interval, so the components $A\cos\theta$ and $A\sin\theta$ are both negative. In the fourth quadrant, between $270^\circ$ and $360^\circ$, the $x$ component is positive.
