Two downward forces act on a horizontal lever with pivot $O$. The force $12\,\mathrm{N}$ acts $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $O$, and $8\,\mathrm{N}$ acts $0.45\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $O$. Their torques about $O$ cancel. What is the net force?

- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$ downward
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$ downward
- $7.2\,\mathrm{N}$ downward

## Explanation

With the $y$ axis pointing up, the two forces have components $(0,-12)\,\mathrm{N}$ and $(0,-8)\,\mathrm{N}$, so the resultant is
$$
(0,\;-12-8)\,\mathrm{N}=(0,-20)\,\mathrm{N},
$$
that is, $20\,\mathrm{N}$ downward. The torques about $O$ are $(-0.30)\cdot(-12)=3.6\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ and $0.45\cdot(-8)=-3.6\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ and cancel. Torque balance concerns rotation, so force balance must be checked separately. The value $0\,\mathrm{N}$ confuses the two balances.
