What is the cosine of the angle between $(3,4)$ and $(4,0)$?

- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac45$
- $\dfrac{12}{5}$
- $-\dfrac35$

## Explanation

The cosine of the angle between two nonzero vectors is their dot product divided by the product of their magnitudes. Calculating gives
$$
\vec u\cdot\vec v=3\cdot4+4\cdot0=12,\qquad |\vec u|=5,\qquad |\vec v|=4,
$$
so $\cos\theta=\dfrac{12}{5\cdot 4}=\dfrac35$. The value $\dfrac{12}{5}$ comes from dividing by only one magnitude and exceeds $1$, the maximum value of a cosine. The value $\dfrac45$ is the sine of the same angle.
