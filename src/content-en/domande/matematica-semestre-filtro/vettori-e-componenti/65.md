The vector $-2\hat\imath+5\hat\jmath$ is added to $3\hat\imath-\hat\jmath$. What are the Cartesian components of their sum?

- $(-5,6)$
- $(5,4)$
- $(1,4)$
- $(1,6)$

## Explanation

When a vector is written using the unit vectors along the axes, the coefficient of $\hat\imath$ is the $x$ component and the coefficient of $\hat\jmath$ is the $y$ component. Add the coefficients of each unit vector separately:
$$
(-2+3)\,\hat\imath+(5-1)\,\hat\jmath=\hat\imath+4\hat\jmath,
$$
so the sum has components $(1,4)$. The result $(1,6)$ comes from reading $-\hat\jmath$ as $+\hat\jmath$, and $(-5,6)$ from subtracting the vectors instead of adding them.
