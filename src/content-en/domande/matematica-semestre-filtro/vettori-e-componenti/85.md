A vector $\vec A=(5,0)$ is rotated counterclockwise through $90^\circ$. What is the resulting vector?

- $(0,5)$
- $(0,-5)$
- $(-5,0)$
- $(5,5)$

## Explanation

A counterclockwise rotation through $90^\circ$ takes the positive $x$ axis to the positive $y$ axis and preserves magnitude. The vector $(5,0)$ has magnitude $5$ and makes an angle of $0^\circ$ with the $x$ axis, so the rotated vector makes an angle of $90^\circ$ and has components
$$
\bigl(5\cos90^\circ,\;5\sin90^\circ\bigr)=(0,5).
$$
The vector $(0,-5)$ corresponds to a clockwise rotation through $90^\circ$, and $(-5,0)$ to a rotation through $180^\circ$.
