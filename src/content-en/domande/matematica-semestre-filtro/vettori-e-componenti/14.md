A force $\vec F=(3,-8,0)\,\mathrm{N}$ acts at a point with position vector $\vec r=(0.5;\,0;\,0)\,\mathrm{m}$ relative to $O$. Calculate the $z$ component of its torque about $O$ in a right-handed Cartesian coordinate system.

- $4\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The torque about $O$ is $\vec M_O=\vec r\times\vec F$. For vectors in the $xy$ plane, its $z$ component is $r_xF_y-r_yF_x$. Since $r_y=0$, only one term remains:
$$
M_{O,z}=0.5\,\mathrm{m}\cdot(-8\,\mathrm{N})-0\cdot 3\,\mathrm{N}=-4\,\mathrm{N\,m}.
$$
The minus sign indicates clockwise rotation when viewed from the positive side of the $z$ axis. The value $1.5\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ comes from using the $F_x$ component, whose line of action passes through $O$ and which therefore produces no torque.
