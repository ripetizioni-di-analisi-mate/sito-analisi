What is the magnitude of $-3\vec u$ if the magnitude of $\vec u$ is $6$?

- $2$
- $-18$
- $18$
- $6$

## Explanation

Multiplying a vector by a number $k$ multiplies its magnitude by $|k|$ because each squared component contains the factor $k^2$. With $k=-3$, we have
$$
|-3\vec u|=|-3|\cdot|\vec u|=3\cdot 6=18.
$$
The negative factor reverses the direction of the vector while its magnitude remains positive. The value $-18$ assigns a negative sign to the magnitude, which is always nonnegative, and $2$ comes from dividing by $3$.
