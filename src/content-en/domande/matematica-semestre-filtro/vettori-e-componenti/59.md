In the head-to-tail construction of $\vec A+\vec B$, where is the tail of $\vec B$ placed?

- At the tail of $\vec A$.
- At the midpoint of $\vec A$.
- Always at the origin of the coordinate axes.
- At the head of $\vec A$.

## Explanation

In the head-to-tail construction, translate $\vec B$ without changing its magnitude or direction until its tail meets the head of $\vec A$. The sum $\vec A+\vec B$ then runs from the tail of $\vec A$ to the head of $\vec B$. This construction agrees with the component calculation because making displacement $\vec A$ followed by displacement $\vec B$ adds the coordinate changes.
