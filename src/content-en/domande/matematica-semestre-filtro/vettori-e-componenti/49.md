A horizontal lever has a pivot at $O$. A downward force of $15\,\mathrm{N}$ acts $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $O$. What downward force acting $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $O$ gives zero total torque about $O$?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $22.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

With the $x$ axis pointing right and the $y$ axis pointing up, a vertical force acting at $(r_x,0)$ produces a torque whose $z$ component is $r_xF_y$. The first force gives
$$
M_1=(-0.40\,\mathrm{m})\cdot(-15\,\mathrm{N})=6\,\mathrm{N\,m}.
$$
A downward force of magnitude $F$ acting $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right gives $M_2=-0.60\,\mathrm{m}\cdot F$. The condition $M_1+M_2=0$ gives $F=\dfrac{6}{0.60}\,\mathrm{N}=10\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $22.5\,\mathrm{N}$ comes from interchanging the moment arms.
