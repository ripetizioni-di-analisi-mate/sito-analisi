What is the dot product $(3,-2,5)\cdot\hat k$?

- $\sqrt{38}$
- $6$
- $5$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The unit vector along the $z$ axis is $\hat k=(0,0,1)$, so the dot product is
$$
(3,-2,5)\cdot(0,0,1)=3\cdot0+(-2)\cdot0+5\cdot1=5.
$$
The result is the scalar projection of the vector in the positive $z$ direction, its $A_z$ component. The value $-2$ is the component along $\hat\jmath$, $6$ is the sum of the three components, and $\sqrt{38}$ is the vector's magnitude.
