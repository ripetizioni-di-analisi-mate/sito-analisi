A vector has magnitude $10$ and points along the negative $y$ axis. What is its $y$ component?

- $0$
- $-3$
- $-10$
- $10$

## Explanation

The component of a vector parallel to an axis equals its magnitude with the sign given by its direction. The vector has magnitude $10$ and points opposite to the positive $y$ axis, so $A_y=-10$. The $x$ component is zero because the vector is parallel to the $y$ axis. Indeed, $\sqrt{0^2+(-10)^2}=10$ gives the magnitude. The value $10$ ignores the direction indicated by the sign of the component.
