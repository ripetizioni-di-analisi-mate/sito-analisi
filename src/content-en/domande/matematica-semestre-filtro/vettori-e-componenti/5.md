Given $\vec A=(3,-2)$ and $\vec B=(2,4)$, calculate $\vec A\cdot\vec B$.

- $14$
- $16$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Calculate the dot product by multiplying corresponding components and adding the two products:
$$
\vec A\cdot\vec B=3\cdot 2+(-2)\cdot 4=6-8=-2.
$$
The result is a negative scalar because the second product exceeds the first in absolute value. The value $14$ comes from ignoring the sign of the $-2$ component, and $2$ corresponds to $8-6$, the two products with their signs reversed.
