Calculate the magnitude of the vector $(2,-3,6)$.

- $11$
- $49$
- $7$
- $5$

## Explanation

In three dimensions, applying the Pythagorean theorem twice gives the magnitude as the square root of the sum of the squares of the three components:
$$
\sqrt{2^2+(-3)^2+6^2}=\sqrt{4+9+36}=\sqrt{49}=7.
$$
Squaring makes the contribution of component $-3$ positive. The value $49$ comes from omitting the square root, and $11$ from adding the absolute values of the components.
