Two successive displacements are $400\,\mathrm{cm}$ in the positive $x$ direction and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ in the positive $y$ direction. Calculate the magnitude of the resultant displacement in meters.

- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $25\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The two components must be expressed in the same unit, so $400\,\mathrm{cm}=4\,\mathrm{m}$. The displacements are perpendicular, so the resulting displacement is $(4,3)\,\mathrm{m}$. By the Pythagorean theorem its magnitude is
$$
|\vec s|=\sqrt{4^2+3^2}\,\mathrm{m}=\sqrt{25}\,\mathrm{m}=5\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $7\,\mathrm{m}$ is the sum of the magnitudes, which equals the magnitude of the resultant only when the two displacements point in the same direction.
