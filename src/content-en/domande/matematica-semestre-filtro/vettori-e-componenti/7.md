Given $\vec A=(3,0,0)$ and $\vec B=(0,4,0)$ in a right-handed Cartesian coordinate system, calculate $\vec A\times\vec B$.

- $(0,0,-12)$
- $(0,0,12)$
- $(12,0,0)$
- $(0,12,0)$

## Explanation

For two vectors in the $xy$ plane, only the $z$ component of the cross product remains. It is
$$
(\vec A\times\vec B)_z=A_xB_y-A_yB_x=3\cdot 4-0\cdot 0=12.
$$
Therefore $\vec A\times\vec B=(0,0,12)$. This agrees with the right-hand rule: when the fingers curl from the positive $x$ axis toward the positive $y$ axis, the thumb points in the positive $z$ direction. The vector $(0,0,-12)$ is the product $\vec B\times\vec A$.
