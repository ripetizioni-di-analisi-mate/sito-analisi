In right-handed coordinates let $\vec u=(1,2,0)$ and $\vec v=(0,0,3)$. Someone obtains $(6,-3,0)$ for $\vec u\times\vec v$ but suspects they reversed the order. Which check distinguishes that vector from its negative?

- Check that it is perpendicular to $\vec u$
- Check the direction using the right-hand rule from $\vec u$ to $\vec v$
- Check that it is perpendicular to $\vec v$
- Measure its magnitude alone

## Explanation

A vector and its negative have the same magnitude and are perpendicular to the same vectors. Orientation distinguishes them: $\vec u\times\vec v$ has first component $2\cdot3=6$, and the reverse order has first component $-6$.
