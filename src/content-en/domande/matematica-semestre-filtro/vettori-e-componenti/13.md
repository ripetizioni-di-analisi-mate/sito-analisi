Given $\vec A=(2,1,-1)$ and $\vec B=(1,0,3)$ in a right-handed Cartesian coordinate system, calculate $\vec A\times\vec B$.

- $(-3,7,1)$
- $(3,-7,-1)$
- $(3,7,-1)$
- $(3,-7,1)$

## Explanation

In a right-handed coordinate system, the components of the cross product are $A_yB_z-A_zB_y$, $A_zB_x-A_xB_z$, and $A_xB_y-A_yB_x$. With $\vec A=(2,1,-1)$ and $\vec B=(1,0,3)$ this gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
(\vec A\times\vec B)_x&=1\cdot3-(-1)\cdot0=3\\[6pt]
(\vec A\times\vec B)_y&=(-1)\cdot1-2\cdot3=-7\\[6pt]
(\vec A\times\vec B)_z&=2\cdot0-1\cdot1=-1.
\end{aligned}
$$
The check $2\cdot3+1\cdot(-7)+(-1)\cdot(-1)=0$ confirms that the result is perpendicular to $\vec A$. The vector $(-3,7,1)$ is the product $\vec B\times\vec A$.
