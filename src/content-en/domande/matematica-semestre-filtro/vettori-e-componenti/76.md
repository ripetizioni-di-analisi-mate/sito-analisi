If $\vec v=-2\vec u$ and $|\vec u|=3$, what is $\vec u\cdot\vec v$?

- $-18$
- $18$
- $-6$
- $9$

## Explanation

The dot product is linear in each factor, so the number $-2$ can be taken outside:
$$
\vec u\cdot\vec v=\vec u\cdot(-2\vec u)=-2\,(\vec u\cdot\vec u)=-2|\vec u|^2.
$$
The dot product of a vector with itself is its squared magnitude, so the result is $-2\cdot 3^2=-18$. The sign is negative because $\vec u$ and $\vec v$ point in opposite directions. The value $-6$ comes from using the magnitude in place of its square.
