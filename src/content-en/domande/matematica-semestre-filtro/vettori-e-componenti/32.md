A constant force points at an angle of $30^\circ$ above the positive $x$ axis. The displacement is horizontal and to the left. Which force component determines the work, and what is the sign of the work?

- The vertical component. The work is positive.
- The horizontal component. The work is positive.
- The horizontal component. The work is negative.
- The vertical component. The work is zero.

## Explanation

The work done by a constant force is $W=\vec F\cdot\vec s=F_xs_x+F_ys_y$. The displacement is horizontal, so $s_y=0$ and the term containing $F_y$ vanishes. Only the horizontal force component contributes. The force points at an angle of $30^\circ$ above the positive $x$ axis, so $F_x=F\cos30^\circ>0$. The displacement is leftward, so $s_x<0$ and the work $W=F_xs_x$ is negative. Indeed, the angle between the force and displacement is $150^\circ$.
