When a nonzero vector is reflected across the $x$ axis, which property is preserved for every vector?

- Its magnitude.
- Its vertical component.
- Its direction.
- Its directed angle with the $x$ axis.

## Explanation

Reflection across the $x$ axis maps $(A_x,A_y)$ to $(A_x,-A_y)$. The magnitude is unchanged because
$$
\sqrt{A_x^2+(-A_y)^2}=\sqrt{A_x^2+A_y^2}.
$$
The vertical component changes sign whenever it is nonzero. The vector's direction and its directed angle with the $x$ axis also change, with the angle going from $\theta$ to $-\theta$. These properties remain unchanged only for vectors parallel to the $x$ axis.
