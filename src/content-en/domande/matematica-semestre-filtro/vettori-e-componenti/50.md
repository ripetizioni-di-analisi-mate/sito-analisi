Two forces have components $(4,2)$ and $(-1,5)$ in newtons. Which third force makes their resultant zero?

- $(3,7)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(-5,3)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(-3,-7)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(-4,-2)\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The resultant of the three forces is zero if and only if both its components are zero. The two given forces have resultant
$$
(4,2)\,\mathrm{N}+(-1,5)\,\mathrm{N}=(3,7)\,\mathrm{N},
$$
so the third force must be its negative, $(-3,-7)\,\mathrm{N}$. The check $(4-1-3,\;2+5-7)=(0,0)$ confirms the result. The force $(3,7)\,\mathrm{N}$ would double the resultant, and $(-4,-2)\,\mathrm{N}$ cancels only the first force.
