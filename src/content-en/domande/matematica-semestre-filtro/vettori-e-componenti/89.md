A vector of magnitude $10$ is to point in the same direction as $(3,-4)$. What must its components be?

- $(-6,8)$
- $(6,-8)$
- $(3,-4)$
- $(8,-6)$

## Explanation

The required vector points in the direction of $(3,-4)$, so it is a positive multiple of its unit vector. The magnitude of $(3,-4)$ is $\sqrt{9+16}=5$, so
$$
10\cdot\frac{(3,-4)}{5}=2\,(3,-4)=(6,-8).
$$
Indeed, the magnitude is $\sqrt{36+64}=10$. The vector $(-6,8)$ has magnitude $10$ and points in the opposite direction, and $(8,-6)$ has magnitude $10$ and a different direction.
