The vectors $\vec u=(4,1)$ and $\vec v=(3,2)$ span a parallelogram. What happens to its area when $\vec v$ is replaced by $\vec v-2\vec u$?

- It stays the same
- It doubles
- It halves
- It becomes zero

## Explanation

A vector parallel to the base $\vec u$ is subtracted from $\vec v$. The distance between the lines parallel to the base stays the same, so neither base nor height changes.
