What is the area of the parallelogram spanned by the vectors $(1,1,0)$ and $(0,1,1)$?

- $\sqrt3$
- $2$
- $3$
- $1$

## Explanation

The area of the parallelogram is the magnitude of the cross product of its two vectors. With $\vec A=(1,1,0)$ and $\vec B=(0,1,1)$ we have
$$
\vec A\times\vec B=(1\cdot1-0\cdot1,\;0\cdot0-1\cdot1,\;1\cdot1-1\cdot0)=(1,-1,1),
$$
so the area is $\sqrt{1^2+(-1)^2+1^2}=\sqrt3$. The value $2$ is the product of the magnitudes $\sqrt2\cdot\sqrt2$, equal to the area only for perpendicular vectors, and $3$ comes from omitting the square root.
