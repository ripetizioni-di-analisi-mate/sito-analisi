The vector $\vec A=(2,11)$ is resolved into the sum of a component parallel to $\vec B=(3,4)$ and one perpendicular to $\vec B$. What is the perpendicular component?

- $(6,8)$
- $(-1,7)$
- $(4,-3)$
- $(-4,3)$

## Explanation

The magnitude of $\vec B$ is $5$, so its unit vector is $\widehat B=\left(\dfrac35,\dfrac45\right)$. The scalar projection of $\vec A$ in the direction of $\vec B$ is
$$
\vec A\cdot\widehat B=2\cdot\frac35+11\cdot\frac45=\frac{6+44}{5}=10,
$$
so the parallel component is $10\,\widehat B=(6,8)$. The perpendicular component is the difference $\vec A-(6,8)=(-4,3)$, and the check $(-4)\cdot3+3\cdot4=0$ confirms perpendicularity. The vector $(4,-3)$ comes from subtracting in reverse order, and $(-1,7)$ is $\vec A-\vec B$.
