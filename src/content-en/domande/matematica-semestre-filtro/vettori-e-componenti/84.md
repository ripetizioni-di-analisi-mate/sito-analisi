The vector $(2,-6)$ is a multiple of $(-1,3)$. What is the scale factor?

- $2$
- $-3$
- $3$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The vector $(2,-6)$ is a multiple of $(-1,3)$ if there is a number $k$ such that $(2,-6)=k\,(-1,3)=(-k,3k)$. The first component $-k=2$ gives $k=-2$, and the second component confirms it because $3\cdot(-2)=-6$. The factor is negative, so the vectors are parallel and point in opposite directions. The value $2$ ignores the sign, and $-3$ is the ratio of the two components of $(2,-6)$.
