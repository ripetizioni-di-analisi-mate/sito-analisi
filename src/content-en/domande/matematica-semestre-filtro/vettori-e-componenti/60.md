What are the Cartesian components of the vector $3\hat\imath-4\hat k$?

- $(3,-4,0)$
- $(3,-4)$
- $(3,0,-4)$
- $(0,3,-4)$

## Explanation

In $A_x\hat\imath+A_y\hat\jmath+A_z\hat k$ each coefficient is the component along the axis of its corresponding unit vector. The vector $3\hat\imath-4\hat k$ contains no $\hat\jmath$, so the coefficient of $\hat\jmath$ is zero and the components are $(3,0,-4)$. Its magnitude is $\sqrt{9+0+16}=5$. The vector $(3,-4,0)$ assigns the coefficient of $\hat k$ to the $y$ axis, and $(3,-4)$ is a vector in the $xy$ plane.
