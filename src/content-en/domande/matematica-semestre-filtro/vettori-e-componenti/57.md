Three forces with components $(2,1)\,\mathrm{N}$, $(3,-5)\,\mathrm{N}$, and $(-1,1)\,\mathrm{N}$ act on a particle. What is the magnitude of their resultant?

- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Find the resultant by adding the horizontal components and the vertical components separately:
$$
\vec R=(2+3-1,\;1-5+1)\,\mathrm{N}=(4,-3)\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
By the Pythagorean theorem, its magnitude is $\sqrt{4^2+(-3)^2}\,\mathrm{N}=5\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $7\,\mathrm{N}$ comes from adding the absolute values of the two components, $1\,\mathrm{N}$ from adding their signed values, and $25\,\mathrm{N}$ from omitting the square root.
