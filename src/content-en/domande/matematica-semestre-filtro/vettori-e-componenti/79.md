A position vector is measured in meters and a force in newtons. What unit is used for their cross product?

- Newtons per meter.
- Newtons.
- Newton-meters.
- Meters per second.

## Explanation

The cross product $\vec r\times\vec F$ has components of the form $r_xF_y-r_yF_x$. Each term is a length in meters multiplied by a force in newtons, so each component is measured in newtons times meters, or newton-meters ($\mathrm{N\,m}$). This cross product is the torque of the force. The newton-meter has the same dimensions as the joule, and torque is expressed in $\mathrm{N\,m}$ because it is a vector quantity.
