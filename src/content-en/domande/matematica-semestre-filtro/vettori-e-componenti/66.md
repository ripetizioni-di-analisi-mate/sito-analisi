Two nonzero vectors satisfy $\vec u\cdot\vec v>0$. What follows about the smaller angle between them?

- It is less than $90^\circ$.
- It equals $90^\circ$.
- It is greater than $90^\circ$.
- It must equal $0^\circ$.

## Explanation

The dot product of two nonzero vectors is $\vec u\cdot\vec v=|\vec u|\,|\vec v|\cos\theta$, with $\theta$ between $0^\circ$ and $180^\circ$. The magnitudes are positive, so $\cos\theta$ has the same sign as the dot product and is positive. In that interval the cosine is positive only for $0^\circ\le\theta<90^\circ$, so the angle is less than $90^\circ$. Zero, the angle for vectors pointing in the same direction, is one possible value.
