Given $\vec A+\vec B=(6,4)$ and $\vec A-\vec B=(2,-2)$, what is $\vec A$?

- $(2,3)$
- $(8,2)$
- $(4,3)$
- $(4,1)$

## Explanation

Add corresponding sides of the two equations. The vector $\vec B$ appears with opposite signs and cancels:
$$
(\vec A+\vec B)+(\vec A-\vec B)=2\vec A=(6+2,\;4-2)=(8,2).
$$
Dividing by $2$ gives $\vec A=(4,1)$. Subtracting the equations gives $2\vec B=(4,6)$, that is, $\vec B=(2,3)$, and the check $(4,1)+(2,3)=(6,4)$ confirms the result. The vector $(8,2)$ is $2\vec A$.
