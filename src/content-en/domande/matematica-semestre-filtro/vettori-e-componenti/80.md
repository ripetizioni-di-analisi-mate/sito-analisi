An object can rotate in the $xy$ plane, viewed from the positive side of the $z$ axis. A force applied to the object produces a torque with component $M_{O,z}=+3\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. In which direction does the force tend to turn the object about $O$?

- Clockwise.
- In a direction that depends on the magnitude of the force.
- About the $x$ axis.
- Counterclockwise.

## Explanation

The torque $\vec M_O=\vec r\times\vec F$ of a force acting in the $xy$ plane lies along $z$. By the right-hand rule, counterclockwise rotation viewed from the positive side of the $z$ axis corresponds to a thumb pointing in the positive $z$ direction. The $M_{O,z}$ component is positive, so the force tends to turn the object counterclockwise. The force magnitude affects the magnitude of the torque, while the sign of the component determines the direction of rotation.
