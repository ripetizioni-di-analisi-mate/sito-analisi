The sum of two vectors is $(13,0)$, and the first vector is $(5,8)$. What are the components of the second?

- $(-8,8)$
- $(8,-8)$
- $(13,-8)$
- $(8,8)$

## Explanation

Let $\vec B$ denote the second vector. Then $(5,8)+\vec B=(13,0)$. Subtracting $(5,8)$ from both sides component by component gives
$$
\vec B=(13-5,\;0-8)=(8,-8).
$$
The check $(5+8,\;8-8)=(13,0)$ confirms the result. The vector $(8,8)$ comes from ignoring the sign in the second component, and $(-8,8)$ is $(5,8)-(13,0)$, the difference calculated in reverse order.
