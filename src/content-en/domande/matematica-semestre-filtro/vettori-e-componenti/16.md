Two forces of $4\,\mathrm{N}$ and $3\,\mathrm{N}$ and a displacement of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ are given. Which vectors should be added to find the net force?

- The larger force and the displacement.
- Only the two forces.
- The smaller force and the displacement.
- All three vectors.

## Explanation

The net force is the vector sum of the forces acting on the object, so add only the vectors of $4\,\mathrm{N}$ and $3\,\mathrm{N}$. Only quantities of the same kind can be added, and force is measured in newtons while displacement is measured in meters. Adding a force and a displacement is therefore undefined. Displacement enters the calculation of work through the dot product $W=\vec F\cdot\vec s$ of the net force and the displacement.
