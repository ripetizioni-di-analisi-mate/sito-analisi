Two displacements have equal magnitudes and opposite directions. What can be said about the angle their sum makes with the positive $x$ axis?

- The sum makes an angle of $0^\circ$.
- The sum makes an angle of $180^\circ$.
- The sum is the zero vector and its angle is undefined.
- The sum points in the direction of the first displacement.

## Explanation

Denote the first displacement by $\vec s$. The second has the same magnitude and opposite direction, so it is $-\vec s$. Their sum has components
$$
(s_x-s_x,\;s_y-s_y)=(0,0),
$$
and is therefore the zero vector. The zero vector has zero magnitude and all its components are zero, so it points in no particular direction. Its angle with the positive $x$ axis is undefined.
