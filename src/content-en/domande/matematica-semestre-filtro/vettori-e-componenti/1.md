A vector has components $(6,8)\,\mathrm{m}$. Calculate its magnitude.

- $14\,\mathrm{m}$
- $100\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The components $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and $8\,\mathrm{m}$ form the legs of a right triangle whose hypotenuse is the vector. By the Pythagorean theorem, the magnitude is the square root of the sum of the squares of the components:
$$
|\vec A|=\sqrt{6^2+8^2}\,\mathrm{m}=\sqrt{36+64}\,\mathrm{m}=\sqrt{100}\,\mathrm{m}=10\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $14\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from adding the perpendicular components, and $100\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from omitting the square root.
