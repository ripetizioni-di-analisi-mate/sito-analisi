Calculate the scalar projection of $\vec A=(-4,1)$ in the direction of $\vec B=(3,4)$.

- $\dfrac{8}{5}$
- $-8$
- $-\dfrac{8}{5}$
- $-\dfrac{8}{25}$

## Explanation

The scalar projection of $\vec A$ in the direction of $\vec B$ is $\vec A\cdot\widehat B=\dfrac{\vec A\cdot\vec B}{|\vec B|}$. Calculating gives
$$
\vec A\cdot\vec B=(-4)\cdot 3+1\cdot 4=-8,\qquad |\vec B|=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5,
$$
so the projection is $-\dfrac85$. The minus sign indicates that the component of $\vec A$ along $\vec B$ points opposite to $\vec B$. The value $-8$ comes from forgetting to divide by the magnitude, and $-\dfrac{8}{25}$ from dividing by its square.
