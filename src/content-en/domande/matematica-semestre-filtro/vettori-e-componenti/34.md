Two vectors lie in the $xy$ plane and their cross product is nonzero. Why is it sufficient to calculate its $z$ component?

- Because every cross product has only one component.
- Because the product is perpendicular to the $xy$ plane.
- Because the horizontal components of the factors are always zero.
- Because the cross product is a scalar.

## Explanation

The cross product of two nonparallel vectors is perpendicular to both and therefore perpendicular to the plane containing them. The two vectors lie in the $xy$ plane, so the perpendicular direction is along the $z$ axis. Indeed, every term in the $A_yB_z-A_zB_y$ and $A_zB_x-A_xB_z$ components contains $A_z$ or $B_z$, both of which are zero. Thus the $x$ and $y$ components of the product vanish. Only the $A_xB_y-A_yB_x$ component remains.
