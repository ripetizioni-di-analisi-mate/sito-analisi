A force $\vec F=(0,-10)\,\mathrm{N}$ acts at the point $P=(2,0)\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the $z$ component of its torque about $C=(-1,0)\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $-20\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

Calculate the torque about $C$ using the vector from $C$ to the point of application:
$$
\vec r=\overrightarrow{CP}=\bigl(2-(-1),\;0-0\bigr)=(3,0)\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The $z$ component is therefore $M_{C,z}=r_xF_y-r_yF_x=3\cdot(-10)-0\cdot0=-30\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, and its sign indicates clockwise rotation. The value $-20\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ is the torque about the origin because it uses the position $(2,0)$ of $P$. The value $-10\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ comes from adding the coordinates of $P$ and $C$.
