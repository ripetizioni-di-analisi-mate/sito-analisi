A constant force acts in the plane with components $(4,6)\,\mathrm{N}$. The object moves $3\,\mathrm{m}$ in the negative $x$ direction. How much work is done?

- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

A displacement of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ in the negative $x$ direction has components $\vec s=(-3,0)\,\mathrm{m}$. The work is the dot product
$$
W=F_xs_x+F_ys_y=4\cdot(-3)+6\cdot 0=-12\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The vertical force component contributes zero because it is perpendicular to the displacement. The value $-18\,\mathrm{J}$ comes from multiplying the displacement by the $F_y$ component instead of $F_x$, and $12\,\mathrm{J}$ ignores the direction of the displacement.
