A probe moves from position $(1,2,3)\,\mathrm{m}$ to position $(4,6,15)\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the magnitude of the projection of its displacement onto the $xy$ plane?

- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The components of the displacement are the differences of the coordinates:
$$
(4-1,\;6-2,\;15-3)=(3,4,12)\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
Projection onto the $xy$ plane keeps the $x$ and $y$ components and sets the $z$ component to zero. The projection is therefore $(3,4,0)\,\mathrm{m}$ and its magnitude is $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}\,\mathrm{m}=5\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $13\,\mathrm{m}$ is the magnitude of the full displacement, $\sqrt{25+144}\,\mathrm{m}$, and $7\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from adding the two components.
