A vector in the plane has magnitude $5$ and its $x$ component is $-5$. What follows?

- Its $y$ component is $5$.
- Its $y$ component is zero.
- Its $y$ component is $-5$.
- Its $y$ component can be any positive number.

## Explanation

The magnitude satisfies $|\vec A|^2=A_x^2+A_y^2$. With $|\vec A|=5$ and $A_x=-5$, this gives
$$
25=(-5)^2+A_y^2=25+A_y^2,
$$
so $A_y^2=0$ and $A_y=0$. The vector is therefore $(-5,0)$ and points along the negative $x$ axis. When the absolute value of one component equals the magnitude, the other component is zero and the vector is parallel to an axis.
