Which vector in the plane is perpendicular to $\vec u=(8,11)$?

- $(8,0)$
- $(11,-8)$
- $(-8,-11)$
- $(8,11)$

## Explanation

A vector in the plane is perpendicular to $\vec u=(8,11)$ if their dot product is zero. For $(11,-8)$ we have
$$
8\cdot 11+11\cdot(-8)=88-88=0,
$$
so it is perpendicular. For $(8,0)$ the dot product is $64$ and for $(8,11)$ it is $185$. The vector $(-8,-11)$ is the negative of $\vec u$, so it is parallel to it. In general, interchanging the components and reversing the sign of one of them gives a perpendicular vector.
