An elevator descends $6\,\mathrm{m}$. One observer takes upward as positive along the vertical axis, and another takes downward as positive. What displacement components do the two observers record, respectively?

- $-6\,\mathrm{m}$ and $-6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m}$ and $+6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $+6\,\mathrm{m}$ and $-6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $+6\,\mathrm{m}$ and $+6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement is an arrow of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ pointing down. For the first observer, the arrow points opposite to the positive axis direction, so the component is $-6\,\mathrm{m}$. For the second observer, the arrow points in the positive direction, so the component is $+6\,\mathrm{m}$. Thus the sign of the component depends on the reference frame, while the magnitude is the same.
