Calculate the distance between the points $(-1,2)$ and $(3,-1)$.

- $7$
- $3$
- $5$
- $25$

## Explanation

The distance between two points is the magnitude of the vector joining them. Its components are the differences of their coordinates:
$$
\bigl(3-(-1),\;-1-2\bigr)=(4,-3).
$$
By the Pythagorean theorem, the distance is $\sqrt{4^2+(-3)^2}=\sqrt{25}=5$. The value $7$ comes from adding the absolute values of the differences, and $25$ from omitting the square root.
