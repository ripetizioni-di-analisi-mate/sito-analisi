Two vectors $\vec A$ and $\vec B$ are drawn with their tails at the same point. Which vector does the arrow from the head of $\vec B$ to the head of $\vec A$ represent?

- $\vec B-\vec A$
- $\vec A+\vec B$
- $-\vec A-\vec B$
- $\vec A-\vec B$

## Explanation

With the tails at $O$, the head of $\vec A$ is at $O+\vec A$ and the head of $\vec B$ is at $O+\vec B$. The components of an arrow are the coordinates of its head minus those of its tail, so the arrow represents
$$
(O+\vec A)-(O+\vec B)=\vec A-\vec B.
$$
Indeed, in the head-to-tail construction, $\vec B$ followed by this arrow reaches the head of $\vec A$, that is, $\vec B+(\vec A-\vec B)=\vec A$. Following the arrow in the opposite direction gives $\vec B-\vec A$.
