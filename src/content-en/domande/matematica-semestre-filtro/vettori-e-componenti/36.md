The two displacements are shown head to tail. What are the components of the resultant?

::grafico{xmin=-1 xmax=5 ymin=-1 ymax=5 uguale=1 vettori="0,0,3,1,A;3,1,2,4,B" segmenti="0,0,2,4;2,0,2,4;0,4,2,4" punti="2,4" titolo="The second displacement starts where the first ends."}

- $(3,1)$
- $(-1,3)$
- $(2,4)$
- $(5,5)$

## Explanation

In the head-to-tail construction, the resultant runs from the tail of the first displacement to the head of the second. The first displacement has components $(3,1)$ and the second runs from $(3,1)$ to $(2,4)$, so it has components $(2-3,\;4-1)=(-1,3)$. Adding component by component gives
$$
(3,1)+(-1,3)=(2,4),
$$
which are also the coordinates of the final head because the first tail lies at the origin. The vector $(-1,3)$ is the second displacement alone.
