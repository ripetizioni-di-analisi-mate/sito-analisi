In a right-handed Cartesian coordinate system, what is the $z$ component of the cross product $(4,1,0)\times(1,-2,0)$?

- $-9$
- $9$
- $-7$
- $-8$

## Explanation

The cross product of two vectors in the $xy$ plane points along the $z$ axis, with $A_xB_y-A_yB_x$ component
$$
(\vec A\times\vec B)_z=4\cdot(-2)-1\cdot1=-8-1=-9.
$$
The component is negative, so the product points in the negative $z$ direction. The rotation from $(4,1,0)$ to $(1,-2,0)$ through the smaller angle is clockwise when viewed from the positive side of the $z$ axis. The value $9$ corresponds to reversing the order of the factors, and $-8$ comes from omitting the term $A_yB_x$.
