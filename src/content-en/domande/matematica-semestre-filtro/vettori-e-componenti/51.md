The parallelogram spanned by $\vec u$ and $\vec v$ has area $5\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What is the area of the parallelogram spanned by $2\vec u$ and $-3\vec v$?

- $-30\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $25\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The area of the parallelogram spanned by two vectors is the magnitude of their cross product. The cross product is linear in each factor, so
$$
(2\vec u)\times(-3\vec v)=2\cdot(-3)\,(\vec u\times\vec v)=-6\,(\vec u\times\vec v).
$$
The magnitude is multiplied by $|-6|=6$, so the area is $6\cdot 5\,\mathrm{m^2}=30\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The negative factor only reverses the direction of the product. The value $-30\,\mathrm{m^2}$ assigns a negative sign to an area.
