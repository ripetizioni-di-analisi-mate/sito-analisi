Given $\vec A=(2,6)$ and $\vec B=(5,1)$, calculate $\vec A-\vec B$.

- $(7,7)$
- $(-3,5)$
- $(3,-5)$
- $(-3,7)$

## Explanation

The difference $\vec A-\vec B$ is the sum of $\vec A$ and the opposite vector $-\vec B=(-5,-1)$. Subtract the corresponding component of $\vec B$ from each component of $\vec A$:
$$
\vec A-\vec B=(2-5,\;6-1)=(-3,5).
$$
The vector $(3,-5)$ is $\vec B-\vec A$, the difference taken in reverse order, and $(7,7)$ is the sum of the two vectors.
