Two nonzero vectors satisfy $\vec A\cdot\vec B=-|\vec A|\,|\vec B|$. What is the magnitude of their sum if $|\vec A|=7$ and $|\vec B|=3$?

- $10$
- $\sqrt{58}$
- $21$
- $4$

## Explanation

The dot product equals $|\vec A|\,|\vec B|\cos\theta$, so the given equality is equivalent to $\cos\theta=-1$. Therefore the angle between the two vectors is $\theta=180^\circ$, that is, the vectors have the same direction and opposite senses. The magnitude of the sum follows from the formula
$$
|\vec A+\vec B|^2=|\vec A|^2+|\vec B|^2+2\,\vec A\cdot\vec B=49+9-2\cdot21=16,
$$
so $|\vec A+\vec B|=4$. The value $10$ is the magnitude of the sum of two vectors pointing the same way, while $\sqrt{58}$ corresponds to two perpendicular vectors. The value $21$ is the product of the magnitudes.
