Two nonzero vectors satisfy $\vec A\cdot\vec B=-|\vec A|\,|\vec B|$. What is the magnitude of their sum if $|\vec A|=7$ and $|\vec B|=3$?

- $10$
- $\sqrt{58}$
- $21$
- $4$

## Explanation

The equality implies $\cos\theta=-1$, so the vectors point in opposite directions. Their magnitudes subtract, giving $7-3=4$.
