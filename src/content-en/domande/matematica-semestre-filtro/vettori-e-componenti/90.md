Two vectors have magnitudes $|\vec A|=5$ and $|\vec B|=2$, and their cross product has magnitude $|\vec A\times\vec B|=6$. What is the absolute value of their dot product $\vec A\cdot\vec B$?

- $6$
- $10$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the cross product gives the sine of the angle $\theta$ between the vectors:
$$
\sin\theta=\frac{|\vec A\times\vec B|}{|\vec A|\,|\vec B|}=\frac{6}{10}=\frac35.
$$
Since $\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$, we have $|\cos\theta|=\sqrt{1-\dfrac{9}{25}}=\dfrac45$, so $|\vec A\cdot\vec B|=10\cdot\dfrac45=8$. The angle can be acute or obtuse, so the data determine only the absolute value of the dot product. The value $4$ comes from subtracting $6$ from $10$.
