Given $\vec u\times\vec v=(2,-1,4)$, what is $\vec v\times\vec u$?

- $(2,-1,4)$
- $(-2,1,-4)$
- $(-2,-1,4)$
- $(2,1,-4)$

## Explanation

Reversing the order of the factors reverses the direction of the cross product while preserving its magnitude, that is, $\vec v\times\vec u=-(\vec u\times\vec v)$. In the component formula, each component is a difference of two products that change places, for example $v_xu_y-v_yu_x=-(u_xv_y-u_yv_x)$. Therefore
$$
\vec v\times\vec u=-(2,-1,4)=(-2,1,-4).
$$
All three components change sign. In $(-2,-1,4)$ only the first component has changed sign.
