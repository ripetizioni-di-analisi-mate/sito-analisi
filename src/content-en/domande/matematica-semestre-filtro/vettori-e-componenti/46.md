An object undergoes a displacement $\vec s=(5,0)\,\mathrm{m}$ while two constant forces $\vec F_1=(6,3)\,\mathrm{N}$ and $\vec F_2=(-2,4)\,\mathrm{N}$ act on it. What is the total work done by the forces?

- $40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $55\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work done by each constant force is its dot product with the displacement. Since $\vec s=(5,0)\,\mathrm{m}$, only the $x$ component of each force contributes:
$$
W_1=6\cdot5\,\mathrm{J}=30\,\mathrm{J},\qquad W_2=(-2)\cdot5\,\mathrm{J}=-10\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The total work is $30\,\mathrm{J}-10\,\mathrm{J}=20\,\mathrm{J}$, the same result obtained from the resultant $(4,7)\,\mathrm{N}$ because $4\cdot5=20$. The value $40\,\mathrm{J}$ ignores the sign of the work done by the second force, and $30\,\mathrm{J}$ accounts only for the first.
