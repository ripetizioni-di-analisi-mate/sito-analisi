Two vectors have magnitudes $|\vec A|=3$ and $|\vec B|=4$ and make an angle of $60^\circ$. What is the dot product $\vec A\cdot\vec B$?

- $12$
- $6\sqrt3$
- $-6$
- $6$

## Explanation

For two nonzero vectors, the dot product is $|\vec A|\,|\vec B|\cos\theta$, where $\theta$ is the angle between them. Since $\cos60^\circ=\dfrac12$, we obtain
$$
\vec A\cdot\vec B=3\cdot4\cdot\frac12=6.
$$
The value $12$ omits the cosine and applies to vectors pointing in the same direction. The value $6\sqrt3$ comes from using $\sin60^\circ$ in place of $\cos60^\circ$, and $-6$ corresponds to an angle of $120^\circ$.
