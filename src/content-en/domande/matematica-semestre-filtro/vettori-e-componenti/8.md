Given $\vec A=(5,12)$, calculate the unit vector that points in the same direction.

- $\left(\dfrac{12}{13},\dfrac{5}{13}\right)$
- $(5,12)$
- $\left(\dfrac{5}{13},\dfrac{12}{13}\right)$
- $\left(-\dfrac{5}{13},-\dfrac{12}{13}\right)$

## Explanation

To obtain the unit vector pointing in the same direction as a nonzero vector, divide the vector by its magnitude. The magnitude is $|\vec A|=\sqrt{5^2+12^2}=\sqrt{169}=13$, so
$$
\widehat A=\frac{\vec A}{|\vec A|}=\left(\frac{5}{13},\frac{12}{13}\right).
$$
The divisor is positive, so the direction is preserved. Reversing the signs gives the oppositely directed unit vector, and the vector $(5,12)$ has magnitude $13$, which differs from one.
