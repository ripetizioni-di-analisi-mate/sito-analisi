Two forces of magnitude $6\,\mathrm{N}$ each act at the same point and make an angle of $60^\circ$. What is the magnitude of their resultant?

- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\sqrt3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\sqrt2\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The square of the resultant's magnitude is the dot product of the sum with itself:
$$
|\vec F_1+\vec F_2|^2=|\vec F_1|^2+|\vec F_2|^2+2|\vec F_1||\vec F_2|\cos60^\circ=(36+36+36)\,\mathrm{N^2}=108\,\mathrm{N^2}.
$$
Thus the magnitude is $\sqrt{108}\,\mathrm{N}=6\sqrt3\,\mathrm{N}$, approximately $10.4\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $12\,\mathrm{N}$ applies to forces pointing in the same direction, $6\sqrt2\,\mathrm{N}$ applies to perpendicular forces, and $6\,\mathrm{N}$ comes from using a minus sign before twice the dot product, which gives the magnitude of the difference.
