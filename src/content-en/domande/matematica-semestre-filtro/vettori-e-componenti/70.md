A force has components $(6,-8)\,\mathrm{N}$. What are the components of the unit vector $\widehat F$ pointing in its direction?

- $\left(\dfrac35,-\dfrac45\right)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(6,-8)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\left(-\dfrac35,\dfrac45\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac35,-\dfrac45\right)$, dimensionless numbers

## Explanation

Find the unit vector by dividing the force by its magnitude, which is $\sqrt{6^2+(-8)^2}\,\mathrm{N}=10\,\mathrm{N}$:
$$
\widehat F=\frac{(6,-8)\,\mathrm{N}}{10\,\mathrm{N}}=\left(\frac35,-\frac45\right).
$$
The newtons in the numerator and denominator cancel, so the components of the unit vector are dimensionless. The unit vector specifies only the direction. Multiplying it by $10\,\mathrm{N}$ restores the force with its original magnitude. The vector $\left(-\dfrac35,\dfrac45\right)$ is the unit vector in the opposite direction.
