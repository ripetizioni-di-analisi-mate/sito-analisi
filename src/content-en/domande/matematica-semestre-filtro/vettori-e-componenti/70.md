A force has components $(6,-8)\,\mathrm{N}$. What are the components of the unit vector $\widehat F$ pointing in its direction?

- $\left(\dfrac35,-\dfrac45\right)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(6,-8)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\left(-\dfrac35,\dfrac45\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac35,-\dfrac45\right)$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the force is $\sqrt{6^2+(-8)^2}\,\mathrm{N}=10\,\mathrm{N}$, and the unit vector is obtained by dividing the force by this magnitude.
$$
\widehat F=\frac{(6,-8)\,\mathrm{N}}{10\,\mathrm{N}}=\left(\frac35,-\frac45\right).
$$
The newtons in the numerator and denominator cancel, so the components of the unit vector are dimensionless. The unit vector specifies only direction and sense, and the force is obtained by multiplying it by the magnitude $10\,\mathrm{N}$. The vector $\left(-\dfrac35,\dfrac45\right)$ is the unit vector in the opposite direction.
