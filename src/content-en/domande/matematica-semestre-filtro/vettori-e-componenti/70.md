A straight motion has unit direction $\left(\dfrac35,\dfrac45\right)$. Its forward displacement between two times has a horizontal component of 9 m. What is the displacement magnitude in metres?

- $12$
- $9$
- $20$
- $15$

## Explanation

The horizontal component is three fifths of the magnitude. Solving $\dfrac35s=9$ gives $s=15$ m.
