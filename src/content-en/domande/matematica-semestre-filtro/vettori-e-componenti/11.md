Given $\vec A=(2,-4)$ and $\vec B=(t,3)$, find the value of $t$ for which the vectors are perpendicular.

- $-6$
- $6$
- $2$
- $12$

## Explanation

Two nonzero vectors are perpendicular if and only if their dot product is zero. We have
$$
\vec A\cdot\vec B=2\cdot t+(-4)\cdot 3=2t-12,
$$
so the condition $2t-12=0$ gives $t=6$. Indeed, when $t=6$ the vector $\vec B=(6,3)$ satisfies $2\cdot6-4\cdot3=0$. The value $-6$ comes from ignoring the sign of the component $-4$, which makes the second product negative.
