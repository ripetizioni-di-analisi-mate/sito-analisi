For what value of $k$ are the vectors $(2,k)$ and $(3,6)$ parallel?

- $4$
- $9$
- $-1$
- $12$

## Explanation

Two nonzero vectors are parallel if one is a multiple of the other, that is, if there exists a number $c$ such that $(2,k)=c\,(3,6)=(3c,6c)$. The first component $3c=2$ gives $c=\dfrac23$, so
$$
k=6c=6\cdot\frac23=4.
$$
The value $9$ comes from reversing the ratio of the components. The value $-1$ makes the dot product $2\cdot3+6k$ zero and therefore makes the vectors perpendicular.
