What is the vector projection of $(3,4)$ onto the line directed along $(2,0)$?

- $(3,0)$
- $(6,0)$
- $(0,4)$
- $(2,0)$

## Explanation

The vector projection of $\vec A$ onto the line of $\vec B$ is $(\vec A\cdot\widehat B)\,\widehat B$. The vector $(2,0)$ has magnitude $2$, so its unit vector is $\widehat B=(1,0)$. The scalar projection is $(3,4)\cdot(1,0)=3$, so the vector projection is
$$
3\,(1,0)=(3,0).
$$
The vector $(6,0)$ comes from using $(2,0)$ in place of its unit vector, without dividing by its magnitude. The vector $(0,4)$ is the component of $(3,4)$ perpendicular to the line.
