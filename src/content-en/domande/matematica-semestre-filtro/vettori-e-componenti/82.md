An object weighing $20\,\mathrm{N}$ rests on a plane inclined at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal. One axis is chosen along the plane. What is the magnitude of the component of the weight parallel to the plane?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $17.3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The weight is vertical, and the normal to the plane makes the same angle $30^\circ$ with the vertical that the plane makes with the horizontal. Thus the weight makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the plane, and its component along the plane has magnitude
$$
P_\parallel=20\,\mathrm{N}\cdot\cos60^\circ=20\,\mathrm{N}\cdot\sin30^\circ=10\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The value $17.3\,\mathrm{N}$, or $20\cos30^\circ\,\mathrm{N}$, is the component perpendicular to the plane, and $40\,\mathrm{N}$ comes from dividing by $\sin30^\circ$ instead of multiplying.
