A constant force $\vec F=(3,4)\,\mathrm{N}$ acts on an object that follows a closed path of length $12\,\mathrm{m}$ and returns to its starting point. How much work does the force do?

- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $60\,\mathrm{J}$
- $84\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-60\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The object returns to its starting point, so its total displacement is the zero vector, $\vec s=\vec 0$. For a constant force the work is $W=\vec F\cdot\vec s$, which depends only on the total displacement. Therefore
$$
W=3\cdot0+4\cdot0=0\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The value $60\,\mathrm{J}$ comes from multiplying the force magnitude $5\,\mathrm{N}$ by the length of the path, using distance traveled in place of displacement magnitude.
