A constant force has components $(3,-2)\,\mathrm{N}$. The total displacement has components $(-2,4)\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the work done by the force?

- $14\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-14\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work done by a constant force is the dot product of force and displacement, calculated from their components:
$$
W=F_xs_x+F_ys_y=3\cdot(-2)+(-2)\cdot 4=-6-8=-14\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
Both contributions are negative because on each axis the force component points opposite to the displacement component. The value $14\,\mathrm{J}$ ignores the signs, and $2\,\mathrm{J}$ corresponds to $-6+8$, with the sign of the second product reversed.
