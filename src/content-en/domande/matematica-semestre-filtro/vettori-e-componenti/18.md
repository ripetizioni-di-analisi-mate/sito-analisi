A point moves from $P=(-2,3)$ to $Q=(1,-1)$, with coordinates in meters. What is its displacement?

- $(-3,4)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(-1,2)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(3,-4)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(1,-1)\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The components of the displacement from $P$ to $Q$ are the changes in the coordinates, final minus initial:
$$
\overrightarrow{PQ}=\bigl(1-(-2),\;-1-3\bigr)=(3,-4)\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The point moves $3\,\mathrm{m}$ in the positive $x$ direction and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ in the negative $y$ direction. The vector $(-3,4)\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from subtracting in reverse order and describes the displacement from $Q$ to $P$. The vector $(-1,2)\,\mathrm{m}$ is the sum of the coordinates of the two points.
