Two displacements have magnitudes $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Under what condition does the resultant have magnitude $5\,\mathrm{m}$?

- The displacements must point in the same direction.
- The displacements must point in opposite directions along the same line.
- The displacements must start at the same point in the diagram.
- The displacements must be perpendicular.

## Explanation

The square of the resultant's magnitude is the dot product of the sum with itself:
$$
|\vec a+\vec b|^2=|\vec a|^2+|\vec b|^2+2|\vec a||\vec b|\cos\theta=(9+16+24\cos\theta)\,\mathrm{m^2},
$$
where $\theta$ is the angle between the displacements. The value $25\,\mathrm{m^2}$ requires $\cos\theta=0$, that is, $\theta=90^\circ$. For displacements in the same direction the magnitude would be $7\,\mathrm{m}$, and for opposite directions it would be $1\,\mathrm{m}$.
