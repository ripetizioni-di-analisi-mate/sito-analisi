Which unit vector points opposite to $(0,6,-8)$?

- $\left(0,\dfrac35,-\dfrac45\right)$
- $\left(0,-\dfrac35,\dfrac45\right)$
- $(0,-6,8)$
- $\left(0,-\dfrac45,\dfrac35\right)$

## Explanation

The vector $(0,6,-8)$ has magnitude
$$
\sqrt{0^2+6^2+(-8)^2}=\sqrt{100}=10.
$$
The unit vector in the same direction is $\left(0,\dfrac{6}{10},-\dfrac{8}{10}\right)=\left(0,\dfrac35,-\dfrac45\right)$. The unit vector in the opposite direction is obtained by reversing the signs of all the components, giving $\left(0,-\dfrac35,\dfrac45\right)$. The vector $(0,-6,8)$ points in the opposite direction but has magnitude $10$, which differs from one.
