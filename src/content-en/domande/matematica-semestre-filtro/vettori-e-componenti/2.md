Given $\vec A=(4,-2)$ and $\vec B=(-1,5)$, calculate $\vec A+\vec B$.

- $(3,3)$
- $(5,-7)$
- $(3,-7)$
- $(-4,-10)$

## Explanation

Add two vectors component by component, adding the $x$ components together and the $y$ components together:
$$
\vec A+\vec B=\bigl(4+(-1),\;-2+5\bigr)=(3,3).
$$
The $x$ component of $\vec B$ is negative, so it reduces the corresponding component of $\vec A$. The $y$ component of $\vec B$ is positive and greater in absolute value than that of $\vec A$, so the sum has a positive $y$ component. The vector $(5,-7)$ is the difference $\vec A-\vec B$.
