A constant vertical force of $6\,\mathrm{N}$ acts on an object that moves $4\,\mathrm{m}$ horizontally. How much work does this force do?

- $24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $10\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work done by a constant force is $W=Fs\cos\theta$. The force is vertical and the displacement horizontal, so the angle between the vectors is $90^\circ$ and $\cos90^\circ=0$. Therefore
$$
W=6\,\mathrm{N}\cdot 4\,\mathrm{m}\cdot 0=0\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The components give the same result because the dot product of $\vec F=(0,\pm6)\,\mathrm{N}$ and $\vec s=(\pm4,0)\,\mathrm{m}$ is zero. The value $24\,\mathrm{J}$ comes from multiplying the magnitudes without accounting for the angle.
