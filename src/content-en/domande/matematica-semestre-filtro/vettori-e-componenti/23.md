An object weighing $20\,\mathrm{N}$ undergoes a displacement $\vec s=(3,-4)\,\mathrm{m}$, with the $y$ axis pointing up. How much work does gravity do?

- $80\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-80\,\mathrm{J}$
- $100\,\mathrm{J}$
- $60\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The weight acts vertically downward, so $\vec P=(0,-20)\,\mathrm{N}$. Work is the dot product of the force and the displacement:
$$
W=P_xs_x+P_ys_y=0\cdot3+(-20)\cdot(-4)=80\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
Only the vertical component of the displacement contributes because the weight has no horizontal component. The work is positive because the object descends. The value $100\,\mathrm{J}$ is the product of the magnitudes $20\cdot5$, and $60\,\mathrm{J}$ uses the horizontal component of the displacement.
