What changes when the order of two vectors in a dot product is reversed?

- The value always changes sign.
- The value doubles.
- The value stays the same.
- The product becomes a vector.

## Explanation

The dot product is calculated as $\vec A\cdot\vec B=A_xB_x+A_yB_y+A_zB_z$. Reversing the order gives $B_xA_x+B_yA_y+B_zA_z$, and every term equals its counterpart in the first calculation because multiplication of numbers is commutative. Thus $\vec A\cdot\vec B=\vec B\cdot\vec A$ and the value stays the same. The sign change when the factors are interchanged applies to the cross product, and the dot product remains a scalar.
