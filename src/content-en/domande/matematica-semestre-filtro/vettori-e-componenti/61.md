For what value of $k$ are the vectors $(k,-2,1)$ and $(1,3,4)$ perpendicular?

- $-2$
- $6$
- $-10$
- $2$

## Explanation

Two vectors are perpendicular if their dot product is zero. In three dimensions the dot product has three terms:
$$
(k,-2,1)\cdot(1,3,4)=k\cdot1+(-2)\cdot3+1\cdot4=k-6+4=k-2.
$$
The condition $k-2=0$ gives $k=2$. The value $6$ comes from omitting the third term, which appears only in three dimensions, and $-2$ comes from a sign error in solving $k-2=0$.
