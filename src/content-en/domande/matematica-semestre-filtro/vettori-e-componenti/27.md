What is the area of the parallelogram spanned by $(6,0)$ and $(1,9)$?

- $27$
- $60$
- $15$
- $54$

## Explanation

The area of a parallelogram spanned by two vectors is the magnitude of their cross product. For vectors in the plane, only the $z$ component contributes:
$$
|A_xB_y-A_yB_x|=|6\cdot 9-0\cdot 1|=54.
$$
The same value follows from base times height. The first vector lies on the $x$ axis and has length $6$, while the height is the vertical component $9$ of the second vector. The value $27$ is the area of the triangle, half that of the parallelogram.
