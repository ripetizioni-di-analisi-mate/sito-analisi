A force of $20\,\mathrm{N}$ acts at the end of a wrench of length $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the wrench. What is the magnitude of the torque about the bolt at the other end?

- $10\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $8.7\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the torque is the force magnitude times the moment arm, the perpendicular distance from the bolt to the force's line of action. The force makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the wrench, so the moment arm is
$$
b=0.50\,\mathrm{m}\cdot\sin30^\circ=0.25\,\mathrm{m},
$$
and the torque magnitude is $20\,\mathrm{N}\cdot0.25\,\mathrm{m}=5\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. The value $10\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ corresponds to a force perpendicular to the wrench, and $8.7\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ comes from using cosine in place of sine.
