What is the area of the triangle whose two sides from a common vertex are the vectors $(4,1)$ and $(2,3)$?

- $10$
- $7$
- $5$
- $14$

## Explanation

Two sides from a common vertex determine half a parallelogram, so the triangle's area is half the magnitude of the cross product of the two vectors. In the plane, use the $z$ component:
$$
|4\cdot3-1\cdot2|=|12-2|=10,
$$
so the area of the triangle is $\dfrac{10}{2}=5$. The value $10$ is the area of the parallelogram, and $14$ comes from adding the two products instead of subtracting them.
