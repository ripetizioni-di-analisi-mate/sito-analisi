One displacement is $120\,\mathrm{cm}$ to the right and another is $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left. What is the horizontal component of their sum, taking right as positive?

- $0.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-0.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.62\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Express the components in the same unit, so $120\,\mathrm{cm}=1.2\,\mathrm{m}$. Taking right as positive, the first displacement has component $+1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the second $-0.5\,\mathrm{m}$. The component of the sum is
$$
1.2\,\mathrm{m}+(-0.5\,\mathrm{m})=0.7\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $1.7\,\mathrm{m}$ ignores the direction of the second displacement, and $-0.7\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from taking left as positive.
