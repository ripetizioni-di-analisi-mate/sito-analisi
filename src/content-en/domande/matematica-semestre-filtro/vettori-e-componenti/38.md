A vector makes a counterclockwise angle of $135^\circ$ with the positive $x$ axis. What are the signs of its components?

- The $x$ component is negative and the $y$ component positive.
- Both are positive.
- The $x$ component is positive and the $y$ component negative.
- Both are negative.

## Explanation

With the angle $\theta$ measured counterclockwise from the positive $x$ axis, the components are $A\cos\theta$ and $A\sin\theta$. For $\theta=135^\circ$ we have
$$
\cos135^\circ=-\frac{\sqrt2}{2}<0,\qquad \sin135^\circ=\frac{\sqrt2}{2}>0.
$$
Thus the $x$ component is negative and the $y$ component positive. This agrees with the vector's position because $135^\circ$ lies between $90^\circ$ and $180^\circ$ and the vector is in the second quadrant.
