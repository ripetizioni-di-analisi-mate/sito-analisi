A constant force of magnitude $12\,\mathrm{N}$ makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with a displacement of $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Calculate the work done by the force.

- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $36\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $9\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work done by a constant force is the dot product of force and displacement, $W=Fs\cos\theta$, where $\theta$ is the angle between the vectors. Since $\cos 60^\circ=\dfrac12$, we have
$$
W=12\,\mathrm{N}\cdot 3\,\mathrm{m}\cdot\frac12=18\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The angle is acute, so the work is positive. The value $36\,\mathrm{J}$ comes from omitting the cosine, treating the force as if it pointed along the displacement.
