Three masses are in the ratio $2:3:5$ and sum to $50\,\mathrm{g}$. What is the second mass?

- $15\,\mathrm{g}$
- $10\,\mathrm{g}$
- $25\,\mathrm{g}$
- $30\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

According to the ratio $2:3:5$, the combined mass is divided into $2+3+5=10$ equal parts. One part is therefore $\dfrac{50}{10}\,\mathrm{g}=5\,\mathrm{g}$. The second mass corresponds to three parts and is therefore $3\cdot5\,\mathrm{g}=15\,\mathrm{g}$. The other two masses are $10\,\mathrm{g}$ and $25\,\mathrm{g}$, and indeed $10+15+25=50$. The values $10\,\mathrm{g}$ and $25\,\mathrm{g}$ are the first and third masses.
