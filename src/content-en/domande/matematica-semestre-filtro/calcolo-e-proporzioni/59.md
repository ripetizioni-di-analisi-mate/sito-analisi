A mixture is labelled “5% salt” without stating whether this is by mass or mass per volume. What information allows conversion between these conventions?

- The colour of the mixture
- The area of the container
- The mass of the empty container
- The density of the mixture

## Explanation

To convert grams of salt per 100 g of mixture into grams per 100 mL, the mass in that volume must be known. Density supplies precisely that mass-to-volume ratio.
