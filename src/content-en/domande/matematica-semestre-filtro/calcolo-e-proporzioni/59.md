A binary solution containing $4\%$ solute by mass contains $4\,\mathrm{g}$ of solute. What is the mass of the solvent?

- $100\,\mathrm{g}$
- $104\,\mathrm{g}$
- $4\,\mathrm{g}$
- $96\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The base for the mass percentage is the total mass $m$, so $0.04\,m=4\,\mathrm{g}$ and
$$
m=\frac{4\,\mathrm{g}}{0.04}=100\,\mathrm{g}.
$$
The solution is binary, so the solvent mass equals the total mass minus the solute mass, or $100\,\mathrm{g}-4\,\mathrm{g}=96\,\mathrm{g}$. The value $100\,\mathrm{g}$ is the mass of the entire solution. Adding the solute to a mass that already includes it gives $104\,\mathrm{g}$.
