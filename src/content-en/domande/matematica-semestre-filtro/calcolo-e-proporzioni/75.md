Three measurements all equal $8$. All the other measurements equal $14$. How many measurements of $14$ are needed for the overall mean to equal $12$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $9$
- $6$

## Explanation

Let $n$ be the number of measurements of $14$. The sum of all the measurements is $3\cdot8+14n=24+14n$ and the number of measurements is $3+n$, so the condition on the mean gives
$$
\frac{24+14n}{3+n}=12\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 24+14n=36+12n\quad\Longrightarrow\quad n=6.
$$
Indeed, $\dfrac{24+84}{9}=12$. Balancing the deviations from the mean gives the same result. Each measurement of $8$ lies $4$ below the mean and each measurement of $14$ lies $2$ above it, so $3\cdot4=2n$.
