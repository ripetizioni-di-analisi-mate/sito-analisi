Samples of $50\,\mathrm{mL}$ are taken from a $6\,\mathrm{g/L}$ mixture and a $2\,\mathrm{g/L}$ mixture and exchanged. What is the net change in solute mass in the initially more dilute container?

- $+0.40\,\mathrm g$
- $-0.20\,\mathrm g$
- $+0.20\,\mathrm g$
- $+0.10\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The container loses $0.05\cdot2=0.10$ g of solute and receives $0.05\cdot6=0.30$ g. The net gain is $0.20$ g.
