A volume of $18\,\mathrm{mL}$ represents $\dfrac{2}{7}$ of a total volume. What is the total volume?

- $63\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $126\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $36\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $\dfrac{36}{7}\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

Two-sevenths of the total volume $V$ equals $18\,\mathrm{mL}$. One-seventh is therefore $\dfrac{18}{2}\,\mathrm{mL}=9\,\mathrm{mL}$, so
$$
V=7\cdot9\,\mathrm{mL}=18\,\mathrm{mL}\cdot\frac{7}{2}=63\,\mathrm{mL}.
$$
Taking $\dfrac{2}{7}$ of $18\,\mathrm{mL}$ gives $\dfrac{36}{7}\,\mathrm{mL}$ because it treats $18\,\mathrm{mL}$ as the total. Multiplying by $7$ without dividing by $2$ gives $126\,\mathrm{mL}$, while multiplying only by $2$ gives $36\,\mathrm{mL}$.
