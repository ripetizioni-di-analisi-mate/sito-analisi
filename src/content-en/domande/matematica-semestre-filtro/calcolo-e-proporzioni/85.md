A $250\,\mathrm{mL}$ mixture contains $0.015\,\mathrm{L}$ of one component, and the volumes are additive. What is the volume percentage of that component?

- $6\%$
- $0.006\%$
- $0.6\%$
- $60\%$

## Explanation

To compare two volumes, express them in the same unit. Since $1\,\mathrm{L}=1000\,\mathrm{mL}$, the component occupies $0.015\cdot1000\,\mathrm{mL}=15\,\mathrm{mL}$.
$$
\frac{15\,\mathrm{mL}}{250\,\mathrm{mL}}=\frac{6}{100}=6\%.
$$
Dividing $0.015$ by $250$ without converting liters to milliliters gives $0.006\%$. The values $0.6\%$ and $60\%$ arise from a factor-of-$10$ error in the unit conversion or in expressing the fraction in hundredths.
