A $750\,\mathrm{g}$ package costs $4.50$ euros and a $1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$ package of the same product costs $6.60$ euros. Which package has the lower price per kilogram?

- The $750\,\mathrm{g}$ package, because it costs less.
- The two packages have the same price per kilogram.
- The $750\,\mathrm{g}$ package, because it costs $6$ euros per kilogram.
- The $1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$ package, because it costs $5.50$ euros per kilogram.

## Explanation

To compare the packages, find the price per kilogram by dividing the price by the mass in kilograms. Since $750\,\mathrm{g}=0.75\,\mathrm{kg}$, we obtain
$$
\frac{4.50\ \text{euros}}{0.75\,\mathrm{kg}}=6\ \text{euros/kg},\qquad \frac{6.60\ \text{euros}}{1.2\,\mathrm{kg}}=5.50\ \text{euros/kg}.
$$
Therefore the $1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$ package has the lower price per kilogram. The smaller package costs less in total because it contains less, so the comparison uses the price per kilogram.
