Calculate $\dfrac{3}{4}+\dfrac{1}{8}$.

- $\frac{7}{8}$
- $\frac{15}{8}$
- $\frac{3}{8}$
- $\frac{7}{4}$

## Explanation

The least common multiple of the denominators $4$ and $8$ is $8$, so $\dfrac{3}{4}$ can be written as $\dfrac{6}{8}$. Since both terms are now expressed in eighths, add their numerators and keep the denominator.
$$
\frac{3}{4}+\frac{1}{8}=\frac{6}{8}+\frac{1}{8}=\frac{7}{8}.
$$
Adding the numerators $6$ and $1$ while keeping the old denominator $4$ gives $\dfrac{7}{4}$. Multiplying the numerators instead of adding the fractions gives $\dfrac{3}{8}$.
