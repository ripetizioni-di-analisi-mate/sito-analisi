A container already holds $3\,\mathrm{L}$ and receives another $2$ liters each minute. Is its total volume directly proportional to time?

- Yes, because the volume always increases.
- Yes, because two liters flow in each minute.
- Yes, because the total volume doubles every minute.
- No, because the volume at time zero is $3\,\mathrm{L}$.

## Explanation

Two quantities are directly proportional if their ratio is constant, that is, if $V=kt$. In that case the volume at time $0$ is $0$. Here the container already holds $3\,\mathrm{L}$, so after $t$ minutes its volume in liters is $V=3+2t$. The ratio $\dfrac{V}{t}$ therefore changes. For example, it is $5$ for $t=1$. Only the added volume $V-3=2t$ is directly proportional to time.
