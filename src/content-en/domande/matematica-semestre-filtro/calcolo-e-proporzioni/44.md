Different groups of pumps must transfer the same volume of liquid. In which situation is the time required inversely proportional to the number of pumps operating?

- A regulator fixes the total flow rate regardless of how many pumps are operating.
- In every group, all operating pumps have the same constant flow rate, and their individual rates add.
- The pumps share the volume to be transferred, but they run one at a time at the same flow rate.
- The number of pumps doubles while the flow rate of each pump is halved.

## Explanation

The time required to transfer a fixed volume $V$ is $V$ divided by the total flow rate. If all $n$ pumps operate at the same constant rate $q$ and the rates add, the total flow rate is $nq$, so
$$
t=\frac{V}{nq}.
$$
Consequently the product $n\,t$ stays equal to $\dfrac{V}{q}$, and time is inversely proportional to $n$. With a regulator, on the other hand, the total flow rate is independent of $n$.
