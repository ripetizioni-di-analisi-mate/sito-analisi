The ratio of two quantities is 3 to 5. If the second exceeds the first by 16 units, what is their sum?

- 24
- 80
- 64
- 40

## Explanation

The quantities represent $3$ and $5$ equal parts, so the second exceeds the first by $5-3=2$ parts. Since this difference is $16$, one part is $8$. The sum contains $3+5=8$ parts and is therefore $8\cdot8=64$. The two quantities are $24$ and $40$, and indeed $40-24=16$. The values $24$ and $40$ are the individual quantities, while $80$ counts ten parts instead of eight.
