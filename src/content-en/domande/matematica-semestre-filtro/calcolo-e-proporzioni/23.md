After a 20% reduction, a mass is 52 g. What was its original value in grams?

- 52
- 62.4
- 65
- 72

## Explanation

A reduction of $20\%$ corresponds to a factor of $0.80$, so the final mass is $80\%$ of the initial mass $m_0$.
$$
0.80\,m_0=52\,\mathrm{g}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad m_0=\frac{52}{0.80}\,\mathrm{g}=65\,\mathrm{g}.
$$
Indeed, $20\%$ of $65\,\mathrm{g}$ is $13\,\mathrm{g}$, and $65-13=52$. Adding $20\%$ to $52$ gives $62.4$ because it calculates the percentage from the final mass instead of the initial mass.
