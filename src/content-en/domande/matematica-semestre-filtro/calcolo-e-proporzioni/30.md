A portion is $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the total and is divided into three equal parts. What fraction of the total is one part?

- $\dfrac{1}{27}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- 0.25
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

Dividing $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the total into three equal parts means dividing $\dfrac{1}{3}$ by $3$, or multiplying it by $\dfrac{1}{3}$.
$$
\frac{1}{3}:3=\frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{3}=\frac{1}{9}.
$$
The result is a fraction of the total because nine such parts make up the entire total. Confusing one part with the whole portion gives $\dfrac{1}{3}$, while dividing by $3$ one extra time gives $\dfrac{1}{27}$.
