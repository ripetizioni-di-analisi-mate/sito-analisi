In calculating $\dfrac{5}{12}$ of $240\,\mathrm{g}$, the value $\dfrac{5}{12}$ is first rounded to $0.42$. By how much does the result differ from the exact value?

- $0.8\,\mathrm{g}$
- $0\,\mathrm{g}$
- $0.08\,\mathrm{g}$
- $8\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

To obtain the exact value, keep the fraction until the last step.
$$
\frac{5}{12}\cdot240\,\mathrm{g}=5\cdot20\,\mathrm{g}=100\,\mathrm{g}.
$$
Using the rounded value gives $0.42\cdot240\,\mathrm{g}=100.8\,\mathrm{g}$, so the two results differ by $0.8\,\mathrm{g}$. The difference comes from rounding because $0.42$ exceeds $\dfrac{5}{12}$ by $\dfrac{1}{300}$ and $\dfrac{1}{300}\cdot240\,\mathrm{g}=0.8\,\mathrm{g}$. Treating $0.42$ as equal to $\dfrac{5}{12}$ gives $0\,\mathrm{g}$.
