A value grows by $10\%$ in each of two consecutive periods. What is the overall percent increase?

- $21\%$
- $20\%$
- $10\%$
- $1\%$

## Explanation

Each increase of $10\%$ multiplies the value by $1.10$, and the second applies to the value after the first increase. The overall factor is therefore
$$
1.10\cdot1.10=1.21,
$$
which corresponds to an increase of $21\%$ from the initial value. Starting from $100$ gives $110$ and then $121$ because the second increase is $11$. Adding the two percentages as if they used the same base gives $20\%$.
