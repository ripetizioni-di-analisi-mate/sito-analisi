A solution has a total mass of $300\,\mathrm{g}$ and contains $12\,\mathrm{g}$ of solute. Calculate the mass percentage of solute.

- $\frac{2}{5}\%$
- $40\%$
- $96\%$
- $4\%$

## Explanation

The mass percentage compares the solute mass with the total mass of the solution, which is already given here.
$$
\frac{12\,\mathrm{g}}{300\,\mathrm{g}}=0.04=\frac{4}{100}=4\%.
$$
The ratio $0.04$ is written as $4\%$ because a percentage expresses hundredths. The value $96\%$ is the mass percentage of the solvent, the amount needed to reach $100\%$. Shifting the decimal point by one extra place when converting to hundredths gives $40\%$.
