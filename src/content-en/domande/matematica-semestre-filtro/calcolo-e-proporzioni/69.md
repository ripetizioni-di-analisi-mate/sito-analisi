A volume of $12\,\mathrm{mL}$ is transferred in the first $2\,\mathrm{min}$ and another $12\,\mathrm{mL}$ in the next $4\,\mathrm{min}$. What is the average flow rate over the entire interval?

- $3\,\mathrm{mL/min}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mL/min}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{mL/min}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mL/min}$

## Explanation

The average flow rate is the total volume transferred divided by the total time. The total volume is $12+12=24\,\mathrm{mL}$ and the total time is $2+4=6\,\mathrm{min}$, so
$$
\overline{q}=\frac{24\,\mathrm{mL}}{6\,\mathrm{min}}=4\,\mathrm{mL/min}.
$$
The flow rates over the two intervals are $6\,\mathrm{mL/min}$ and $3\,\mathrm{mL/min}$. The value $4.5\,\mathrm{mL/min}$ is their arithmetic mean, which gives equal weight to intervals of different durations.
