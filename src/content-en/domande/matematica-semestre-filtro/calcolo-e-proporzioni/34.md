A rectangular tray has area $360\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. A rectangle covering $\dfrac{5}{6}$ of the width of the tray and $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of its height is filled in. What area is covered?

- $180\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $300\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $216\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $516\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The area of a rectangle is its width times its height. The covered rectangle has $\dfrac{5}{6}$ of the width and $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of the height of the tray, so its area is the product of the two fractions times the total area.
$$
A=\frac{5}{6}\cdot\frac{3}{5}\cdot360\,\mathrm{cm^2}=\frac{1}{2}\cdot360\,\mathrm{cm^2}=180\,\mathrm{cm^2}.
$$
Applying only the width fraction gives $300\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Adding the two fractions instead of multiplying them gives $516\,\mathrm{cm^2}$.
