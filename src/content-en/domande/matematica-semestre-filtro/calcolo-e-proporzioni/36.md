Why is $\dfrac{4}{7}>\dfrac{4}{9}$?

- Both fractions count four parts of the same whole, and a seventh is larger than a ninth.
- A larger denominator gives larger parts.
- The numerators are equal, so the fractions are equal.
- The first denominator is odd.

## Explanation

The fractions have the same numerator $4$, so each represents four parts. Dividing the same whole into $7$ equal parts gives larger parts than dividing it into $9$, since $\dfrac{1}{7}>\dfrac{1}{9}$. Therefore four-sevenths exceeds four-ninths. The cross products give the same ordering because $4\cdot9=36$ is greater than $7\cdot4=28$. A greater denominator therefore gives smaller parts.
