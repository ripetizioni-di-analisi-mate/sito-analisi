The number of reports rises from zero in one week to twelve the next. How can the change from the first week be described correctly?

- The absolute increase is twelve reports. The percent change from the first week is $100\%$.
- The absolute increase is twelve reports. The percent change from the first week is $12\%$.
- The absolute increase is twelve reports. The percent change from the first week is undefined.
- The absolute increase is zero because there were zero reports the first week.

## Explanation

The absolute increase is the difference between the two values, or $12-0=12$ reports. The percent change expresses the ratio of this difference to the initial value as a percentage. Since the initial value is $0$, the ratio $\dfrac{12}{0}$ is undefined, so the percent change is undefined. Both $100\%$ and $12\%$ would assign a value to division by zero. Thus we can describe the change only by its absolute increase.
