Two masses are in the ratio $2:5$. The first is $18\,\mathrm{g}$. What is the second?

- $36\,\mathrm{g}$
- $45\,\mathrm{g}$
- $27\,\mathrm{g}$
- $63\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The ratio $2:5$ means that the first mass corresponds to $2$ equal parts and the second to $5$. Since two parts equal $18\,\mathrm{g}$, one part equals $9\,\mathrm{g}$. The second mass is therefore $5\cdot9\,\mathrm{g}=45\,\mathrm{g}$, and indeed $\dfrac{18}{45}=\dfrac{2}{5}$. The value $63\,\mathrm{g}$ is the combined mass of all seven parts. Doubling the first mass instead of multiplying it by $\dfrac{5}{2}$ gives $36\,\mathrm{g}$.
