A reservoir loses $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of its contents in the morning and $\dfrac{1}{9}$ of its initial contents in the afternoon. What fraction of its initial contents remains?

- $\dfrac{13}{18}$
- $\dfrac{53}{54}$
- $\dfrac{20}{27}$
- $\dfrac{5}{18}$

## Explanation

Both losses are fractions of the same initial contents, so they can be added. With the common denominator $18$, we obtain
$$
\frac{1}{6}+\frac{1}{9}=\frac{3}{18}+\frac{2}{18}=\frac{5}{18}.
$$
The fraction remaining is therefore $1-\dfrac{5}{18}=\dfrac{13}{18}$. The value $\dfrac{20}{27}$ uses the contents remaining after the morning loss as the base for the second loss, although the problem specifies the initial contents. The value $\dfrac{5}{18}$ is the fraction lost.
