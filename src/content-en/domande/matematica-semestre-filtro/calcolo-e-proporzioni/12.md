A solution contains $24\,\mathrm{g}$ of solute, which makes up $6\%$ of its mass. Calculate the total mass of the solution.

- $144\,\mathrm{g}$
- $400\,\mathrm{g}$
- $\dfrac{36}{25}\,\mathrm{g}$
- $424\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The base of the percentage is the total mass $m$, so the given information means that $6\%$ of $m$ equals $24\,\mathrm{g}$.
$$
\frac{6}{100}\,m=24\,\mathrm{g}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad m=24\,\mathrm{g}\cdot\frac{100}{6}=400\,\mathrm{g}.
$$
Multiplying $24$ by $6$ gives $144\,\mathrm{g}$, which interchanges the roles of the part and the percentage. Adding the solute mass to the total mass gives $424\,\mathrm{g}$, but the total already includes the solute.
