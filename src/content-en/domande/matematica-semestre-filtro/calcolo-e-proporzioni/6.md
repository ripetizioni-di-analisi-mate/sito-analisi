An initial quantity of $200$ increases by $20\%$ and then decreases by $20\%$. Calculate the final value.

- $200$
- $192$
- $208$
- $160$

## Explanation

The decrease of $20\%$ is based on the value after the increase, so the two multiplicative factors $1.2$ and $0.8$ apply in succession.
$$
200\cdot1.2=240,\qquad 240\cdot0.8=192.
$$
The first step adds $40$, while the second subtracts $48$, which is $20\%$ of $240$. The value $200$ assumes the two changes cancel, as they would if both percentages used the initial value as their base.
