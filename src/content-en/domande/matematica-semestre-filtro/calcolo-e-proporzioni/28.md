In a set of samples, $40\%$ of all samples were filtered and $30\%$ of all samples passed a test. What can be concluded about the percentage of filtered samples that passed?

- It is $30\%$ because this is the percentage of all samples that passed.
- It is $12\%$ because the two percentages of the total are multiplied.
- It is $75\%$ because $30$ is divided by $40$.
- To find it, we need to know how many filtered samples passed the test.

## Explanation

The required percentage is the number of filtered samples that passed divided by the total number of filtered samples, expressed as a percentage. The data do not determine how many samples have both characteristics. Out of $100$ samples, the number of filtered samples that passed could range from $0$ to $30$, so the percentage could range from $0\%$ to $75\%$. The product of the two fractions, $12\%$, refers to all the samples.
