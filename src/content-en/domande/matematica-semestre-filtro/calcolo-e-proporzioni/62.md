A percentage rises from $18\%$ to $24\%$. By how many percentage points has it increased?

- $18$
- $24$
- $\dfrac{100}{3}$
- $6$

## Explanation

Percentage points measure the difference between two percentages, so the increase is $24-18=6$ percentage points. The change relative to the initial percentage is a different quantity: it divides the difference by $18$, giving $\dfrac{6}{18}=\dfrac{1}{3}$. The value $\dfrac{100}{3}$ expresses this relative change as a percentage and therefore answers a different question. The numbers $18$ and $24$ are the initial and final percentages.
