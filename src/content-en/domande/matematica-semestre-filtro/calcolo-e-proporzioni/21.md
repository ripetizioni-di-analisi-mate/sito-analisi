Which list gives the numbers $-\dfrac{3}{4}$, $-\dfrac{5}{6}$, and $-\dfrac{2}{3}$ in increasing order?

- $-\dfrac{2}{3}<-\dfrac{3}{4}<-\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $-\dfrac{5}{6}<-\dfrac{3}{4}<-\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{4}<-\dfrac{2}{3}<-\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $-\dfrac{5}{6}<-\dfrac{2}{3}<-\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

With the common denominator $12$, the three numbers become
$$
-\frac{3}{4}=-\frac{9}{12},\qquad -\frac{5}{6}=-\frac{10}{12},\qquad -\frac{2}{3}=-\frac{8}{12}.
$$
Of two negative numbers, the one with the larger absolute value is smaller because it lies farther left on the number line. Thus $-\dfrac{5}{6}$ is the smallest and $-\dfrac{2}{3}$ the largest. The ordering $-\dfrac{2}{3}<-\dfrac{3}{4}<-\dfrac{5}{6}$ keeps the order of the corresponding positive fractions without reversing it.
