In a group of 160 people, 40% chose A. One-quarter of those who chose A change their minds. How many people change their choice?

- 64
- 16
- 40
- 80

## Explanation

The number who chose A is $40\%$ of $160$, or $\dfrac{40}{100}\cdot160=64$. One-quarter of them change their minds, so the base for the second calculation is $64$, and $\dfrac{64}{4}=16$ people change their choice. This is $10\%$ of the entire group because $\dfrac{40}{100}\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{1}{10}$. Taking one-quarter of the whole group instead of one-quarter of those who chose A gives $40$.
