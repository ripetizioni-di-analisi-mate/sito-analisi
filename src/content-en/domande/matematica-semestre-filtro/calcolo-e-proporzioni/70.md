Four measurements have a mean of $12$, and two other measurements have a mean of $21$. What is the mean of all six measurements?

- $15$
- $16.5$
- $18$
- $33$

## Explanation

The sum of a group of measurements is its mean times the number of measurements. The sums are therefore $4\cdot12=48$ and $2\cdot21=42$, and the mean of all six measurements is
$$
\overline{x}=\frac{48+42}{4+2}=\frac{90}{6}=15.
$$
This is a weighted average of the two means with weights $4$ and $2$. The value $16.5$ is the arithmetic mean of $12$ and $21$, which ignores the number of measurements in each group.
