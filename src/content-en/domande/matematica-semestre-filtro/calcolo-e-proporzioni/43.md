Divide $-\dfrac{2}{3}$ by $\dfrac{4}{9}$, in that order.

- $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{8}{27}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

Dividing by $\dfrac{4}{9}$ is equivalent to multiplying by its reciprocal $\dfrac{9}{4}$. The quotient is negative because the dividend is negative and the divisor is positive.
$$
-\frac{2}{3}:\frac{4}{9}=-\frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac{9}{4}=-\frac{18}{12}=-\frac{3}{2}.
$$
Multiplying by $\dfrac{4}{9}$ instead of its reciprocal gives $-\dfrac{8}{27}$. The value $\dfrac{3}{2}$ has the correct magnitude but the wrong sign.
