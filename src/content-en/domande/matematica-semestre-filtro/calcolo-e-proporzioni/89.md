What fraction of a $2\,\mathrm{h}\,15\,\mathrm{min}$ interval is $45\,\mathrm{min}$?

- $\dfrac{9}{43}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

To find the ratio of two durations, express them in the same unit. Since $1\,\mathrm{h}=60\,\mathrm{min}$, the interval lasts $2\cdot60+15=135\,\mathrm{min}$.
$$
\frac{45\,\mathrm{min}}{135\,\mathrm{min}}=\frac{45}{135}=\frac{1}{3}.
$$
Reading $2\,\mathrm{h}\,15\,\mathrm{min}$ as $215\,\mathrm{min}$ gives $\dfrac{9}{43}$. Dividing by just the two hours, or $120\,\mathrm{min}$, gives $\dfrac{3}{8}$. Indeed, $45\,\mathrm{min}$ fits into $135\,\mathrm{min}$ exactly three times.
