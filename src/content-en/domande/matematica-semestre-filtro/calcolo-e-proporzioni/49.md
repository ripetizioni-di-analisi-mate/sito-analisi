Which ratio completes the proportion $\dfrac{12}{18}=r$?

- $\dfrac{14}{20}$
- $\dfrac{14}{21}$
- $\dfrac{20}{14}$
- $\dfrac{16}{21}$

## Explanation

A proportion states that two ratios are equal, so $r$ must equal $\dfrac{12}{18}$, which reduces to $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Since $\dfrac{14}{21}$ reduces to $\dfrac{2}{3}$ when its numerator and denominator are divided by $7$, the ratio $\dfrac{14}{21}$ completes the proportion. The cross products confirm this because $12\cdot21=252=18\cdot14$. Adding $2$ to the numerator and denominator of $\dfrac{12}{18}$ gives $\dfrac{14}{20}$, but this changes the value of the fraction.
