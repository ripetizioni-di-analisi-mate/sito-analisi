Calculate $\dfrac{15}{28}\cdot\dfrac{14}{45}$.

- $6$
- $\dfrac{675}{392}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{29}{73}$

## Explanation

In a product of fractions, a factor common to a numerator and a denominator can be canceled even when they belong to different fractions. Since $15=3\cdot5$, $14=2\cdot7$, $28=2\cdot2\cdot7$, and $45=3\cdot3\cdot5$, we have
$$
\frac{15}{28}\cdot\frac{14}{45}=\frac{3\cdot5\cdot2\cdot7}{2\cdot2\cdot7\cdot3\cdot3\cdot5}=\frac{1}{2\cdot3}=\frac{1}{6}.
$$
The value $6$ is the reciprocal of the result. Multiplying each numerator by the denominator of the other fraction, as in division, gives $\dfrac{675}{392}$.
