How many complete $\dfrac{3}{8}\,\mathrm{L}$ portions can be obtained from $\dfrac{7}{4}\,\mathrm{L}$?

- $3$
- $5$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

The number of portions is the available volume divided by the volume of one portion.
$$
\frac{7}{4}:\frac{3}{8}=\frac{7}{4}\cdot\frac{8}{3}=\frac{14}{3}.
$$
Since $\dfrac{14}{3}=4+\dfrac{2}{3}$, there are $4$ whole portions, leaving $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of a portion, or $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{L}$. Rounding the quotient up gives $5$, which counts the incomplete portion as well.
