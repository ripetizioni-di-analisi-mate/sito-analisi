The share of a category rises from $18\%$ to $24\%$. What is the relative increase from its initial share?

- $6\%$
- $25\%$
- $\dfrac{100}{3}\%$
- $75\%$

## Explanation

The relative increase is the increase divided by the initial share. The increase is $24-18=6$ percentage points, so
$$
r=\frac{24-18}{18}=\frac{6}{18}=\frac{1}{3},
$$
which as a percentage is $\dfrac{100}{3}\%$, or about $33.3\%$. The value $6\%$ is the difference in percentage points, which does not account for the base. Dividing by the final share $24$ gives $25\%$, and $75\%$ is the ratio of the two shares.
