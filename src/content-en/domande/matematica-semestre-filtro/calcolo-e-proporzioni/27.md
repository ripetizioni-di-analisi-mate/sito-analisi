Sample A has a mass of $100\,\mathrm{g}$ and sample B a mass of $120\,\mathrm{g}$. A fraction $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of A and a fraction $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of B are removed. Which statement is correct?

- The portion from A is larger because $\dfrac{3}{4}$ is greater than $\dfrac{2}{3}$.
- The portion from B is larger because its mass is $80\,\mathrm{g}$ compared with $75\,\mathrm{g}$.
- The two portions have the same mass.
- The portion from A is larger because its mass is $90\,\mathrm{g}$ compared with $80\,\mathrm{g}$.

## Explanation

A fraction gives a mass only after it is multiplied by the mass of its own sample, so calculate the two portions separately.
$$
\frac{3}{4}\cdot100\,\mathrm{g}=75\,\mathrm{g},\qquad \frac{2}{3}\cdot120\,\mathrm{g}=80\,\mathrm{g}.
$$
The portion from B therefore exceeds the portion from A by $5\,\mathrm{g}$, even though $\dfrac{3}{4}>\dfrac{2}{3}$. Comparing the fractions alone is sufficient when the two samples have the same mass. Applying $\dfrac{3}{4}$ to the mass of B gives $90\,\mathrm{g}$.
