Two places are $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart on a map with scale $1:25\,000$. What is the actual distance between them?

- $15\,\mathrm{km}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{km}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{km}$
- $150\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

The scale $1:25\,000$ means that every actual length is $25\,000$ times the corresponding length on the map, so the actual distance is $6\,\mathrm{cm}\cdot25\,000=150\,000\,\mathrm{cm}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{km}=100\,000\,\mathrm{cm}$, we obtain
$$
150\,000\,\mathrm{cm}=\frac{150\,000}{100\,000}\,\mathrm{km}=1.5\,\mathrm{km}.
$$
The value $15\,\mathrm{km}$ comes from using $1\,\mathrm{km}=10\,000\,\mathrm{cm}$, while $0.15\,\mathrm{km}$ comes from using $1\,\mathrm{km}=1\,000\,000\,\mathrm{cm}$.
