Two discounts, $10\%$ and $20\%$, apply to a list price. The rules specify that each discount is based on the list price. Which description of the final price is correct?

- Reduce the price by $10\%$ and apply the second discount to the reduced price.
- Subtract the arithmetic mean of the two percentages from the list price.
- Subtract only the $20\%$ discount because both discounts use the same base.
- Subtract both $10\%$ and $20\%$ of the list price from that price.

## Explanation

Both discounts use the list price $P$ as their base, so the reductions are $0.10\,P$ and $0.20\,P$. Their sum is $0.30\,P$, giving a final price of $0.70\,P$. Applying the second discount to the reduced price would give $0.90\cdot0.80\,P=0.72\,P$ because the $20\%$ discount would use a smaller base. Subtracting the mean of the percentages would take off only $15\%$.
