A mass decreases by $18\,\mathrm{g}$, which is $12\%$ of its initial mass. What is the mass after the decrease?

- $150\,\mathrm{g}$
- $168\,\mathrm{g}$
- $134.16\,\mathrm{g}$
- $132\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The decrease of $18\,\mathrm{g}$ is $12\%$ of the initial mass $m_0$, so $0.12\,m_0=18\,\mathrm{g}$ and
$$
m_0=\frac{18\,\mathrm{g}}{0.12}=150\,\mathrm{g}.
$$
The mass after the decrease is therefore $150\,\mathrm{g}-18\,\mathrm{g}=132\,\mathrm{g}$, or $88\%$ of the initial mass. The value $150\,\mathrm{g}$ is the mass before the decrease, while $168\,\mathrm{g}$ adds the change instead of subtracting it.
