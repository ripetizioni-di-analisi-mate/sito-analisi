A vehicle travels two straight stretches of equal length in the same direction at different constant speeds. How can its average speed for the entire trip be found from the two speeds?

- Calculate the arithmetic mean because the two distances are equal.
- Calculate a weighted average using the times spent on the two stretches as weights.
- Calculate a weighted average with weights inversely proportional to the times spent on the two stretches.
- Use the speed on the second stretch because it determines the arrival time.

## Explanation

The average speed is the total distance divided by the total time. Let $v_1,v_2$ be the speeds and $t_1,t_2$ the times for the two stretches. The total distance is $v_1t_1+v_2t_2$, so
$$
v_m=\frac{v_1t_1+v_2t_2}{t_1+t_2}.
$$
This is a weighted average with the times as weights. The stretches have equal lengths, so the slower stretch takes longer and carries more weight.
