One pump delivers $0.30\,\mathrm{L/min}$ and another delivers $5\,\mathrm{mL/s}$. Which comparison is correct?

- The second flow rate is greater because $5$ is greater than $0.30$.
- The flow rates are equal because $5\,\mathrm{mL/s}$ is equivalent to $300\,\mathrm{mL/min}$.
- The first flow rate is greater because $300\,\mathrm{mL}$ is more than $5\,\mathrm{mL}$.
- The first flow rate is lower because converting $5\,\mathrm{mL/s}$ to $\mathrm{mL/min}$ requires dividing $5$ by $60$.

## Explanation

To compare two flow rates, use the same volume unit and the same time interval. A minute contains $60$ seconds, so in one minute the second pump delivers
$$
5\,\mathrm{mL/s}\cdot60\,\mathrm{s}=300\,\mathrm{mL}=0.30\,\mathrm{L}.
$$
The two flow rates are therefore equal. Comparing only the numbers $5$ and $0.30$ ignores their units. Dividing $5$ by $60$ reverses the conversion, since more liquid flows in one minute than in one second.
