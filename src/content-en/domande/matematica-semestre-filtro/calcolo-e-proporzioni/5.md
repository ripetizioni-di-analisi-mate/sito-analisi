A quantity increases from $80$ to $100$ in the same units. What is its percent increase?

- $25\%$
- $20\%$
- $125\%$
- $5\%$

## Explanation

The relative change is the increase divided by the initial value $80$ because the increase is measured relative to the starting quantity.
$$
r=\frac{100-80}{80}=\frac{20}{80}=\frac{1}{4}=25\%.
$$
Dividing the increase by the final value $100$ gives $20\%$. The value $125\%$ is the final value divided by the initial value, that is, the multiplicative factor $1.25$ expressed as a percentage.
