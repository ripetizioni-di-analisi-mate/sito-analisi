A liquid exactly fills $8$ bottles of capacity $0.75\,\mathrm{L}$. How many bottles of capacity $0.5\,\mathrm{L}$ would the same amount of liquid fill?

- $5$
- $16$
- $6$
- $12$

## Explanation

The amount of liquid is fixed at $8\cdot0.75\,\mathrm{L}=6\,\mathrm{L}$. The number of bottles times the capacity of one bottle therefore stays equal to $6\,\mathrm{L}$, so the two quantities are inversely proportional.
$$
n=\frac{6\,\mathrm{L}}{0.5\,\mathrm{L}}=12.
$$
The value $5$ comes from the direct proportion $8\cdot\dfrac{0.5}{0.75}=\dfrac{16}{3}$, which would give fewer bottles even though they are smaller. The value $6$ treats the volume in liters as a number of bottles.
