A pump takes $15\,\mathrm{min}$ at a flow rate of $12\,\mathrm{mL/min}$. How long will it take to transfer the same volume at $20\,\mathrm{mL/min}$?

- $9\,\mathrm{min}$
- $12\,\mathrm{min}$
- $25\,\mathrm{min}$
- $5\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

The volume transferred is the flow rate times the time, so it is $12\,\mathrm{mL/min}\cdot15\,\mathrm{min}=180\,\mathrm{mL}$. For a fixed volume, time is inversely proportional to flow rate.
$$
t=\frac{180\,\mathrm{mL}}{20\,\mathrm{mL/min}}=9\,\mathrm{min}.
$$
At a higher flow rate, less time is required. Treating time and flow rate as directly proportional gives $25\,\mathrm{min}$ because $15\cdot\dfrac{20}{12}=25$.
