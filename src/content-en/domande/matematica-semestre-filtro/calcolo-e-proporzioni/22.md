A conveyor belt moves at constant speed and travels $18\,\mathrm{m}$ in $6\,\mathrm{s}$. How many meters does it travel in $25\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $450\,\mathrm{m}$
- $75\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{108}{25}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The ratio of distance to time gives the distance traveled per second, or speed, $v=\dfrac{18\,\mathrm{m}}{6\,\mathrm{s}}=3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Since the speed is constant, distance is directly proportional to time.
$$
s=3\,\mathrm{m/s}\cdot25\,\mathrm{s}=75\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $3\,\mathrm{m}$ treats the speed as a distance. Multiplying $18$ by $25$ without dividing by $6$ gives $450\,\mathrm{m}$, while treating distance and time as inversely proportional gives $\dfrac{108}{25}\,\mathrm{m}$.
