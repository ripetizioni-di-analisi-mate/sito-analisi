A pump delivers $60\,\mathrm{mL}$ during each four-second working period, then pauses for two seconds. It starts pumping at time zero. How many millilitres has it delivered by 16 seconds?

- $120$
- $160$
- $180$
- $240$

## Explanation

The working intervals are 0 to 4, 6 to 10 and 12 to 16 seconds. All three are complete, so the volume is $3\cdot60=180$ mL.
