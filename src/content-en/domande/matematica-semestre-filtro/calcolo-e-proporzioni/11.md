In a binary solution, the ratio of solute mass to solvent mass is $2$ to $7$. The total mass of the solution is $45\,\mathrm{g}$. Calculate the solute mass.

- $10\,\mathrm{g}$
- $7\,\mathrm{g}$
- $\dfrac{90}{7}\,\mathrm{g}$
- $35\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The ratio $2$ to $7$ compares solute with solvent, so the total mass represents $2+7=9$ equal parts. One part is therefore $\dfrac{45}{9}\,\mathrm{g}=5\,\mathrm{g}$. The solute occupies two parts and has mass $10\,\mathrm{g}$, while the solvent has mass $35\,\mathrm{g}$. The value $\dfrac{90}{7}\,\mathrm{g}$ comes from treating $\dfrac{2}{7}$ as a fraction of the total even though the denominator of that ratio is the solvent mass alone.
