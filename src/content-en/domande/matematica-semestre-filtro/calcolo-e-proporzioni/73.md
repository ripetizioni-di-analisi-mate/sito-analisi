One bag contains $2$ balls and another contains $3$. One ball is drawn from each bag. What fraction of all the balls has been drawn?

- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

The number of balls drawn is $1+1=2$ and the total number is $2+3=5$, so the required fraction is
$$
\frac{1+1}{2+3}=\frac{2}{5}.
$$
Here one total count is compared with another, so the numerators are added separately, as are the denominators. The value $\dfrac{5}{6}$ is the sum $\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{3}$, which treats fractions of two different bags as portions of the same whole. The value $\dfrac{1}{6}$ is their product.
