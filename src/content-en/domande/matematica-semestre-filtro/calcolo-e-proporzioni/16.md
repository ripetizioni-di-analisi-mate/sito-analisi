A slider moves $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$ along a straight track in the positive direction and then $\dfrac{3}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$ in the opposite direction. What is its net displacement?

- $\dfrac{23}{20}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{7}{20}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{9}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-\dfrac{7}{20}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The net displacement is the signed sum of the two displacements, so the second takes a minus sign. A common denominator of $5$ and $4$ is $20$.
$$
\Delta x=\left(\frac{2}{5}-\frac{3}{4}\right)\mathrm{m}=\left(\frac{8}{20}-\frac{15}{20}\right)\mathrm{m}=-\frac{7}{20}\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The negative sign gives the direction of the net displacement. The value $\dfrac{23}{20}\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance traveled, obtained by adding the lengths of the two segments. Subtracting the numerators and adding the denominators gives $-\dfrac{1}{9}\,\mathrm{m}$.
