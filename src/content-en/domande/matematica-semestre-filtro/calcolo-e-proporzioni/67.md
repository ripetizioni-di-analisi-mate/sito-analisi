A positive mass undergoes two changes, an increase of $15\%$ and a reduction of $10\%$. Each percentage applies to the most recent mass. What happens if the order is reversed?

- The final mass is greater.
- The final mass is smaller.
- The final mass is the same.
- The mass returns to its initial value.

## Explanation

Each percent change applies to the most recent mass by multiplying it by its own factor. The increase of $15\%$ multiplies it by $1.15$ and the reduction of $10\%$ by $0.90$. The product of two numbers is independent of their order, so
$$
1.15\cdot0.90=0.90\cdot1.15=1.035,
$$
and the final mass is the same in both cases. It exceeds the initial mass by $3.5\%$, so the mass does not return to its starting value.
