In a group of $30$ people, $20\%$ sign up. In another group of $40$ people, $15\%$ sign up. Which comparison is correct?

- Two more people sign up in the first group.
- Two more people sign up in the second group.
- Twice as many people sign up in the second group.
- Six people sign up in each group.

## Explanation

Find the number who sign up in each group by applying the percentage to the number of people in that group.
$$
30\cdot\frac{20}{100}=6,\qquad 40\cdot\frac{15}{100}=6.
$$
Thus six people sign up in each group. The first group has the higher percentage and the second the larger base, and the two effects cancel. Comparing only the percentages would suggest that more people sign up in the first group.
