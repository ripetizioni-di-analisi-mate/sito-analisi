Three measurements have a mean of 10. Two of them are 8 and 15. What is the third?

- 3
- 7
- 13
- 10

## Explanation

The mean of three measurements is their sum divided by $3$, so the three measurements sum to $3\cdot10=30$. The two known measurements sum to $8+15=23$, giving $30-23=7$ for the third. Indeed, $\dfrac{8+15+7}{3}=10$. The value $10$ would assume that the third measurement equals the mean, even though the mean also depends on the other two measurements.
