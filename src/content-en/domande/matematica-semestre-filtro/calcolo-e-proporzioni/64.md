After a reduction of $20\%$, what percent increase from the reduced value restores the initial value?

- $20\%$
- $16\%$
- $25\%$
- $80\%$

## Explanation

A reduction of $20\%$ multiplies the value by $0.8$. Returning to the initial value requires the reciprocal factor because the two factors must have product $1$.
$$
\frac{1}{0.8}=\frac{10}{8}=1.25.
$$
The factor $1.25$ corresponds to an increase of $25\%$. The increase exceeds $20\%$ because the reduced value, a smaller base, is used. For example, $100$ falls to $80$, and $25\%$ of $80$ is exactly $20$.
