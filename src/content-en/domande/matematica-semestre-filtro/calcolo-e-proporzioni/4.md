A mixture of mass $350\,\mathrm{g}$ contains $8\%$ of a component by mass. Calculate the mass of the component.

- $280\,\mathrm{g}$
- $322\,\mathrm{g}$
- $\frac{14}{5}\,\mathrm{g}$
- $28\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The mass percentage uses the total mass of the mixture as its base, so the mass of the component is $8\%$ of $350\,\mathrm{g}$.
$$
m_c=\frac{8}{100}\cdot350\,\mathrm{g}=28\,\mathrm{g}.
$$
The value $322\,\mathrm{g}$ is the mass of the other substances, or $92\%$ of the total. Dividing by $10$ instead of $100$ gives $280\,\mathrm{g}$, and dividing by $1000$ gives $\dfrac{14}{5}\,\mathrm{g}$.
