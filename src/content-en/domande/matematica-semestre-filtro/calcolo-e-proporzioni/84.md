The ratio of A to B is $5:4$. The ratio of B to C is $2:3$. What is the ratio of A to C?

- $5:3$
- $5:6$
- $10:3$
- $6:5$

## Explanation

To combine the two ratios, quantity B must represent the same number of parts in both. In the first ratio B represents $4$ parts, so multiply both terms of the second ratio by $2$.
$$
B:C=2:3=4:6.
$$
Thus A, B, and C are in the ratio $5:4:6$, and the ratio of A to C is $5:6$. The value $5:3$ combines numbers from the two ratios without first equalizing the numbers of parts for B.
