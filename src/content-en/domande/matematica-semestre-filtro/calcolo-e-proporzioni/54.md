A pump transfers $2.4\,\mathrm{L}$ in $8\,\mathrm{min}$ at a constant flow rate. How many liters does it transfer per minute?

- $0.24\,\mathrm{L}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{L}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{L}$
- $3\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

At a constant flow rate, the volume transferred is directly proportional to time, so the volume transferred in one minute is the total volume divided by the duration in minutes.
$$
q=\frac{2.4\,\mathrm{L}}{8\,\mathrm{min}}=0.3\,\mathrm{L/min}.
$$
Thus $0.3\,\mathrm{L}$ flows in one minute, and indeed $8\cdot0.3\,\mathrm{L}=2.4\,\mathrm{L}$ flows in eight minutes. Shifting the decimal point by one place gives $3\,\mathrm{L}$, while dividing by $10$ instead of $8$ gives $0.24\,\mathrm{L}$.
