In a binary solution, the ratio of solute mass to solvent mass is $3:17$. What is the mass percentage of solute in the solution?

- $3\%$
- $15\%$
- $17\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

The ratio $3:17$ compares solute with solvent, so the solution contains $3+17=20$ parts by mass. The base for a mass percentage is the total mass, so the solute mass fraction is
$$
\frac{3}{3+17}=\frac{3}{20}=\frac{15}{100}=15\%.
$$
Treating the number of solute parts as a percentage gives $3\%$, while treating the total number of parts in the same way gives $20\%$. The ratio $\dfrac{3}{17}$ uses only the solvent mass as its base.
