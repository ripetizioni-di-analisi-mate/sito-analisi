Sample A has a mass of $120\,\mathrm{g}$ and sample B a mass of $80\,\mathrm{g}$. The mass of A is what percentage of the mass of B?

- $\dfrac{200}{3}\%$
- $150\%$
- $50\%$
- $40\%$

## Explanation

The percentage compares the mass of A with the base, the mass of B, so divide $120\,\mathrm{g}$ by $80\,\mathrm{g}$.
$$
\frac{120\,\mathrm{g}}{80\,\mathrm{g}}=\frac{3}{2}=\frac{150}{100}=150\%.
$$
The result exceeds $100\%$ because A has a greater mass than the other sample used as the base. Reversing the base and calculating the mass of B relative to the mass of A gives $\dfrac{200}{3}\%$. The value $50\%$ is the percentage by which A exceeds B.
