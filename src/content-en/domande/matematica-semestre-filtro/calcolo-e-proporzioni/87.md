Given $b\ne0$ and $d\ne0$, which equality is equivalent to $\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{c}{d}$?

- $ad=bc$
- $ac=bd$
- $ab=cd$
- $a+d=b+c$

## Explanation

Multiply both sides of $\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{c}{d}$ by $bd$, which is nonzero because $b\ne0$ and $d\ne0$.
$$
\frac{a}{b}\cdot bd=\frac{c}{d}\cdot bd\quad\Longrightarrow\quad ad=bc.
$$
The operation is reversible because dividing $ad=bc$ by $bd$ recovers the proportion. The two equalities are therefore equivalent. For example, $\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{4}{6}$ and $2\cdot6=3\cdot4$, whereas $2\cdot4$ differs from $3\cdot6$.
