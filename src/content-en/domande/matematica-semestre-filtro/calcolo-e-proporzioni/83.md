The first group accounts for $60\%$ of the participants, and $80\%$ of its answers are correct. The second group accounts for $40\%$ of the participants, and $50\%$ of its answers are correct. What is the overall percentage of correct answers if every participant gave one answer?

- $68\%$
- $65\%$
- $70\%$
- $80\%$

## Explanation

Each participant gives one answer, so the overall percentage is the average of the two percentages weighted by the fractions of participants in the two groups.
$$
0.60\cdot0.80+0.40\cdot0.50=0.48+0.20=0.68.
$$
Thus $68\%$ of the answers are correct. Among $100$ participants, for example, the number of correct answers is $48+20=68$. The value $65\%$ is the arithmetic mean of $80\%$ and $50\%$, which would give equal weight to groups of different sizes.
