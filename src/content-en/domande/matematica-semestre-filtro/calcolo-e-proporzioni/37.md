What is $\dfrac{18}{42}$ in lowest terms?

- $\dfrac{3}{14}$
- $\dfrac{6}{7}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{9}{14}$

## Explanation

To reduce a fraction to lowest terms, divide its numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor. Since $18=2\cdot3^2$ and $42=2\cdot3\cdot7$, the greatest common divisor is $2\cdot3=6$.
$$
\frac{18}{42}=\frac{18:6}{42:6}=\frac{3}{7}.
$$
The only positive divisor common to $3$ and $7$ is $1$, so the fraction is in lowest terms. The other answers divide the numerator and denominator by different numbers, changing the value of the fraction.
