Which fraction in lowest terms represents $0.125$?

- $\dfrac{1}{80}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

A decimal with three digits after the decimal point can be written with denominator $1000$, so $0.125=\dfrac{125}{1000}$. The greatest common divisor of $125$ and $1000$ is $125$ because $1000=8\cdot125$.
$$
\frac{125}{1000}=\frac{125:125}{1000:125}=\frac{1}{8}.
$$
Using the denominator $10\,000$ gives $\dfrac{1}{80}$, which counts one extra decimal place. The value $\dfrac{1}{4}$ corresponds to $0.25$.
