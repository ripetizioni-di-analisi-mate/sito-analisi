An initial volume of $160\,\mathrm{mL}$ decreases by $25\%$. The resulting volume then increases by $10\%$. Calculate the final volume.

- $136\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $132\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $120\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $144\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

The second change is calculated from the volume reached after the first, so apply the factors $0.75$ and $1.10$ in succession.
$$
160\,\mathrm{mL}\cdot0.75=120\,\mathrm{mL},\qquad 120\,\mathrm{mL}\cdot1.10=132\,\mathrm{mL}.
$$
The increase is $12\,\mathrm{mL}$, or $10\%$ of $120\,\mathrm{mL}$. Adding the two percentages gives $136\,\mathrm{mL}$, as if both referred to $160\,\mathrm{mL}$. Ignoring the increase gives $120\,\mathrm{mL}$.
