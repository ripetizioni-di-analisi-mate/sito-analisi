Which fraction is greater, $\dfrac{7}{12}$ or $\dfrac{5}{8}$?

- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{7}{12}$
- $\dfrac{12}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The least common multiple of $12$ and $8$ is $24$, so write both fractions in twenty-fourths.
$$
\frac{7}{12}=\frac{14}{24},\qquad \frac{5}{8}=\frac{15}{24}.
$$
Since the denominators are equal and positive, the greater fraction is the one with the greater numerator, namely $\dfrac{5}{8}$. The cross products give the same result because $7\cdot8=56$ is less than $12\cdot5=60$. Choosing $\dfrac{7}{12}$ amounts to comparing only the numerators $7$ and $5$.
