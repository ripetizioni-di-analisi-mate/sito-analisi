A fraction $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of a container’s initial contents is removed. Half of what remains is then removed. What fraction of the initial contents is left?

- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

After the first withdrawal, $1-\dfrac{2}{5}=\dfrac{3}{5}$ of the initial contents remains. The second withdrawal takes half of the remainder, leaving half of $\dfrac{3}{5}$.
$$
\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{3}{5}=\frac{3}{10}.
$$
Taking half of the initial contents in the second step as well gives $\dfrac{1}{10}$, from the calculation $1-\dfrac{2}{5}-\dfrac{1}{2}$. Ignoring the second withdrawal gives $\dfrac{3}{5}$, while subtracting $\dfrac{2}{5}$ from the remainder instead of half of it gives $\dfrac{1}{5}$.
