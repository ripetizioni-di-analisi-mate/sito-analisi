Two groups had different mean scores on a test. The first had $12$ participants and the second $18$. Each participant received one score. How can the mean of all the scores be found from the two group means?

- Use $12$ and $18$ as the weights for the first and second groups, respectively.
- Give the two groups equal weight because they took the same test.
- Use $18$ and $12$ as the weights for the first and second groups, respectively.
- Give more weight to the group with the higher mean score.

## Explanation

The mean of all the scores is their sum divided by the number of participants, $30$. Each group’s total score is its mean times its number of participants, so
$$
\overline{x}=\frac{12\,\overline{x}_1+18\,\overline{x}_2}{12+18}.
$$
This is a weighted average with weights $12$ and $18$. Equal weights would work only for groups of the same size.
