A spring follows the linear model $F=F_0+kx$, with force $F_0=4\,\mathrm{N}$ at zero deformation and spring constant $k=50\,\mathrm{N/m}$. What deformation $x$ corresponds to a measured force $F=19\,\mathrm{N}$?

- $0.38\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.46\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Subtract $F_0$ from both sides, then divide by $k>0$.
$$
kx=F-F_0=15\,\mathrm{N},\qquad x=\frac{15\,\mathrm{N}}{50\,\mathrm{N/m}}=0.3\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
Substitution recovers $4+50\cdot0.3=19$ newtons. The value $0.38\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from dividing $19$ by $50$ without subtracting $F_0$, while $0.46\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from adding $F_0$ instead of subtracting it. The quotient $\dfrac{10}{3}$ is the reciprocal of $\dfrac{k}{F-F_0}$.
