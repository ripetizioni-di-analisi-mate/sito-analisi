A sample with specific heat capacity $c=500\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ releases $1200\,\mathrm{J}$, and its temperature drops by $4\,\mathrm{K}$. According to the model $Q=mc(T_f-T_i)$, what is its mass $m$?

- $-0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $9.6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The sample releases heat, so $Q=-1200\,\mathrm{J}$, and its temperature drops, so $T_f-T_i=-4\,\mathrm{K}$. The product $c(T_f-T_i)$ is nonzero, so we can divide by it.
$$
m=\frac{Q}{c(T_f-T_i)}=\frac{-1200}{500\cdot(-4)}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.6\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
The value $-0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$ assigns a minus sign only to the heat, omitting it from the temperature change. The value $\dfrac{5}{3}$ is the reciprocal of the required quotient.
