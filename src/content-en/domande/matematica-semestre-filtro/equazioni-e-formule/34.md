Two towns are $150\,\mathrm{km}$ apart along a straight road. At the same time, one car leaves the first town at $60\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and another leaves the second town, traveling toward the first at $90\,\mathrm{km/h}$. How far from the first town do they meet?

- $90\,\mathrm{km}$
- $75\,\mathrm{km}$
- $60\,\mathrm{km}$
- $300\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

Measure position from the first town. After $t$ hours, the cars are at $x_1=60t$ and $x_2=150-90t$, and they meet when their positions are equal.
$$
60t=150-90t,\qquad 150t=150,\qquad t=1 .
$$
The cars therefore meet $60\,\mathrm{km}$ from the first town. The value $90\,\mathrm{km}$ is the distance traveled by the second car. The value $300\,\mathrm{km}$ comes from dividing by the difference in speeds, as if the cars were traveling in the same direction.
