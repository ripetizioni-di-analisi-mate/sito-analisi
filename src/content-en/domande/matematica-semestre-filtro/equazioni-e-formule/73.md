Which line has infinitely many points in common with $x-2y=3$?

- $2x-4y=3$
- $x+2y=3$
- $x-2y=-3$
- $-2x+4y=-6$

## Explanation

Two equations describe the same line if one is obtained from the other by multiplying *every* term by the same nonzero number. Multiplying $x-2y=3$ by $-2$ gives
$$
-2x+4y=-6 ,
$$
so the two lines coincide. The equation $2x-4y=3$ doubles the coefficients but leaves the constant term unchanged, so it represents a distinct parallel line. The line $x-2y=-3$ is also parallel and distinct.
