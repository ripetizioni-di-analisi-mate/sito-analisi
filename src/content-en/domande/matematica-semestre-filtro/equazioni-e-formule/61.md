An object moves at speed $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and has kinetic energy $36\,\mathrm{J}$. Given $E_c=\dfrac12mv^2$, what is its mass?

- $1\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Multiply both sides by $2$ and divide by $v^2$, which is positive because $v\ne0$.
$$
m=\frac{2E_c}{v^2}=\frac{2\cdot36\,\mathrm{J}}{36\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}}=2\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
Indeed, $\dfrac12\cdot2\cdot36=36$. The value $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ omits the multiplication by $2$, overlooking the factor $\dfrac12$. The value $12\,\mathrm{kg}$ comes from dividing by $v$ instead of $v^2$, while $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ contains both errors.
