What is the solution set of $x(x-4)=2x$?

- $\{6\}$
- $\{0,6\}$
- $\{0,4\}$
- $\{0,2\}$

## Explanation

Subtract $2x$ from both sides and factor out $x$.
$$
x^2-4x-2x=0,\qquad x(x-6)=0 .
$$
The product is zero when $x=0$ or $x=6$. The set $\{6\}$ comes from immediately dividing both sides by $x$, a factor that is zero precisely at $x=0$, thereby losing the solution zero. The set $\{0,4\}$ comes from omitting the term $2x$ on the right.
