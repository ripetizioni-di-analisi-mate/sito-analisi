For which real values of $x$ is $\sqrt{9x-108}$ defined?

- $x\le12$
- $x>12$
- $x\ge-12$
- $x\ge12$

## Explanation

A real square root is defined when its radicand is nonnegative, so require $9x-108\ge0$. Add $108$ to both sides and divide by $9$, which is positive and therefore preserves the direction of the inequality.
$$
9x\ge108,\qquad x\ge12 .
$$
At $x=12$, the radicand is zero and the root is $0$, so the answer $x>12$ is incomplete. The answer $x\le12$ comes from incorrectly reversing the inequality.
