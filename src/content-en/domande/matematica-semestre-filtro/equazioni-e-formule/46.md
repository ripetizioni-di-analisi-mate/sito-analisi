A stone is dropped from rest from a height of $20\,\mathrm{m}$. Using the model $h=\dfrac12gt^2$ with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how long does it take to reach the ground?

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\sqrt2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Multiply both sides by $2$ and divide by $g>0$ to isolate $t^2$.
$$
t^2=\frac{2h}{g}=\frac{40}{10}\,\mathrm{s^2}=4\,\mathrm{s^2}.
$$
The solutions of $t^2=4$ are $2$ and $-2$, and elapsed time is nonnegative, so $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$. The value $4\,\mathrm{s}$ is $t^2$ before taking the square root. The value $\sqrt2\,\mathrm{s}$ comes from omitting the factor $2$ and calculating $\sqrt{\dfrac{h}{g}}$.
