A square panel has a 1 cm border on every side. Its inner area is $49\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. What is the outer perimeter in centimetres?

- $28$
- $32$
- $36$
- $40$

## Explanation

The inner side is 7 cm. The border adds 1 cm at each end, so the outer side is 9 cm and the perimeter is $4\cdot9$.
