What value can the side length $x$, expressed in meters, have for a square of area $225\,\mathrm{m^2}$?

- $-15$
- $225$
- $15$
- $7.5$

## Explanation

The area of a square is the square of its side length, so the numerical value of the side length satisfies $x^2=225$. This equation has two opposite solutions.
$$
x=15\quad\text{or}\quad x=-15 .
$$
The side length is positive, so the model admits only $x=15$, or $15\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $225$ omits the square root, while $7.5$ comes from dividing $15$ by $2$ without justification.
