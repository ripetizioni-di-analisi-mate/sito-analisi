Three measurements of the same length are $12\,\mathrm{cm}$, $15\,\mathrm{cm}$, and $x\,\mathrm{cm}$, and their mean is $14\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is $x$?

- $-13$
- $42$
- $15$
- $14$

## Explanation

The mean is the sum of the measurements divided by their number, so $x$ satisfies a linear equation. Multiply both sides by $3$ and subtract $27$.
$$
\frac{12+15+x}{3}=14,\qquad 27+x=42,\qquad x=15 .
$$
Indeed, $\dfrac{12+15+15}{3}=14$. The value $42$ is the sum of all three measurements rather than the third measurement itself. The value $-13$ comes from $27+x=14$, which omits the factor of $3$ on the right side.
