In a model of vertical motion, the height in meters is $h(t)=6+4t-2t^2$, where $t$ is the numerical value of the elapsed time in seconds. For what $t\ge0$ is the height zero?

- $1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Set $h(t)=0$ and divide both sides by $-2$.
$$
t^2-2t-3=0,\qquad (t-3)(t+1)=0 .
$$
The algebraic roots are $3$ and $-1$. Elapsed time satisfies $t\ge0$, so the admissible value is $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$, and indeed $h(3)=6+12-18=0$. The root $-1$ fails the model constraint, while $1$ comes from factoring with the signs reversed, $(t+3)(t-1)$.
