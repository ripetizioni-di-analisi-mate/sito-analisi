Solve the system of equations $x+y=12$ and $2x+y=17$.

- $x=7,\ y=5$
- $x=5,\ y=7$
- $x=12,\ y=17$
- $x=5,\ y=-7$

## Explanation

Both equations must hold simultaneously. Subtracting corresponding sides of the first equation from those of the second eliminates $y$.
$$
(2x+y)-(x+y)=17-12,\qquad x=5 .
$$
Substituting into the first equation gives $y=12-5=7$. The pair also satisfies the second, since $2\cdot5+7=17$. The pair $x=7$, $y=5$ satisfies the first equation but fails the second because $2\cdot7+5=19$.
