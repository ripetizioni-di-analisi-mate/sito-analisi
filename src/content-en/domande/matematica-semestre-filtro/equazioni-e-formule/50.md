One indicator reads $4x-1$ and a second reads $2x+7$. At $x=3$, what is the first reading minus the second?

- $-2$
- $2$
- $0$
- $-8$

## Explanation

The difference between the readings is the difference between the two expressions. The minus sign changes the signs of both terms of $2x+7$.
$$
(4x-1)-(2x+7)=2x-8,\qquad 2\cdot3-8=-2 .
$$
Indeed, at $x=3$ the first reading is $11$ and the second is $13$. The value $2$ is the second reading minus the first. The value $-8$ is the constant term in the difference before substituting $x$.
