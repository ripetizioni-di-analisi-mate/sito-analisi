A system gives the equations $y=2x+3$ and $y=2x-1$. What do these equations represent in the plane?

- Two coincident lines.
- Two perpendicular lines.
- Two lines intersecting at a point on the vertical axis.
- Two distinct parallel lines.

## Explanation

The lines have the same slope $2$, so they are parallel. Their intercepts on the $y$-axis are $3$ and $-1$, so they are distinct. A common point would have to satisfy
$$
2x+3=2x-1,\qquad 3=-1 ,
$$
which is false, so the system has no solutions. The lines would coincide only if their intercepts were equal, and they would be perpendicular only if their slopes had product $-1$.
