A triangle has area $30\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Its base is three times its height. Calculate the square of the height.

- $10\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $180\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

Substitute $b=3h$ into the area formula $A=\dfrac{bh}{2}$, then isolate $h^2$ by multiplying by $\dfrac23$.
$$
A=\frac{3h\cdot h}{2}=\frac32\,h^2,\qquad h^2=\frac23\,A=\frac23\cdot30\,\mathrm{cm^2}=20\,\mathrm{cm^2}.
$$
Indeed, $h^2=20$ gives $\dfrac32\cdot20=30$. The value $10\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ comes from $A=3h^2$, which omits the division by $2$ in the area formula.
