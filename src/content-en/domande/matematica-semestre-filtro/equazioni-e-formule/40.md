How many real solutions does $2x^2+4x+5=0$ have?

- One.
- Two.
- None.
- Infinitely many.

## Explanation

The number of real solutions of a quadratic equation depends on the sign of the discriminant. With $a=2$, $b=4$, and $c=5$, we obtain
$$
\Delta=4^2-4\cdot2\cdot5=16-40=-24 .
$$
The discriminant is negative, so the equation **has no real solutions**. Indeed, $2x^2+4x+5=2(x+1)^2+3$ is at least $3$ for every $x$. The answer “Two” comes from calculating $16+40$ with the wrong sign for $-4ac$.
