For what value of $k$ does $(k-2)x=4$ have no real solutions?

- $k=0$
- $k=4$
- $k=-2$
- $k=2$

## Explanation

The equation has the form $ax=b$ with $a=k-2$ and $b=4\ne0$. If $a\ne0$, dividing by $a$ gives the unique solution $x=\dfrac{4}{k-2}$. If $a=0$, however, the equation becomes
$$
0\cdot x=4 ,
$$
which is false for every $x$. The equation therefore has no solutions only for $k=2$. The value $k=-2$ results from a sign error in the condition $k-2=0$.
