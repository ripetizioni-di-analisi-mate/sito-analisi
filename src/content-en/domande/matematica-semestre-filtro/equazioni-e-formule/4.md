In the model $Q=mc(T_f-T_i)$, the given values are $Q=1000\,\mathrm{J}$, $mc=250\,\mathrm{J/K}$, and $T_i=18\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Calculate $T_f$.

- $4\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $14\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $22\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $32\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

Dividing both sides by $mc>0$ gives the temperature change.
$$
T_f-T_i=\frac{Q}{mc}=\frac{1000\,\mathrm{J}}{250\,\mathrm{J/K}}=4\,\mathrm{K}.
$$
A change of $4\,\mathrm{K}$ is equivalent to a change of $4\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, so $T_f=18+4=22\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The heat is positive and the temperature does indeed rise. The value $4\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ is just the change, while $14\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ comes from subtracting the change instead of adding it.
