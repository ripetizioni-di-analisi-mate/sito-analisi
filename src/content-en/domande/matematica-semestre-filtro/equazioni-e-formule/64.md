Adding corresponding sides of two equations in a system gives $0=5$. What does this imply?

- The system has exactly one solution.
- The system has infinitely many solutions.
- Both unknowns equal zero.
- The system is inconsistent.

## Explanation

A solution of the system must satisfy both equations. It must therefore also satisfy the equation obtained by adding corresponding sides. The sum gives $0=5$, which is false for all values of the unknowns. Thus no pair satisfies both equations, and the system is **inconsistent**. For example, $x+y=2$ and $-x-y=3$ add to $0=5$ and represent distinct parallel lines.
