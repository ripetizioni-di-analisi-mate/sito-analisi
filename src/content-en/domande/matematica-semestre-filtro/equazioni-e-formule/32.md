For what value of $x$ is the expression $\dfrac{x+5}{x-8}$ undefined?

- $-5$
- $8$
- $5$
- $-8$

## Explanation

A quotient is defined whenever its denominator is nonzero, so the expression is undefined only where $x-8=0$.
$$
x-8=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x=8 .
$$
At $x=-5$, the numerator is zero and the expression equals $\dfrac{0}{-13}=0$, a well-defined number. The values $5$ and $-8$ come from changing the sign of the constant term in one expression or the other.
