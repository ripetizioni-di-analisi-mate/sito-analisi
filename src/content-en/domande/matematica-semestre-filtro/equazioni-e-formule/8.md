An elapsed time $t\ge0$ satisfies $t^2=16\,\mathrm{s^2}$. What is the admissible value of $t$?

- $-4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The equation $t^2=16$ has two opposite solutions because $4^2=16$ and $(-4)^2=16$.
$$
t=4\,\mathrm{s}\quad\text{or}\quad t=-4\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
The problem requires $t\ge0$ because elapsed time is nonnegative. Therefore $-4\,\mathrm{s}$ is excluded and $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$ remains. The value $16\,\mathrm{s}$ comes from omitting the square root, while $0\,\mathrm{s}$ gives $t^2=0$.
