Solve $5-2(3x-1)=4x-13$.

- $x=\dfrac95$
- $x=2$
- $x=\dfrac85$
- $x=-2$

## Explanation

The factor $-2$ multiplies both $3x$ and $-1$, so $-2(3x-1)=-6x+2$. Then collect the terms containing $x$ on the right and the constants on the left.
$$
7-6x=4x-13,\qquad 20=10x,\qquad x=2 .
$$
At $x=2$, both sides equal $-5$. The value $\dfrac85$ comes from writing $-2$ in place of $+2$, overlooking that $-2$ also multiplies $-1$.
