A rectangle has area $40\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, and one side exceeds the other by $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. How long is the shorter side in centimeters?

- $8$
- $-8$
- $37$
- $5$

## Explanation

Let $x$ be the numerical value of the shorter side in centimeters. The other side measures $x+3$, so the area gives a quadratic equation.
$$
x(x+3)=40,\qquad x^2+3x-40=0,\qquad (x+8)(x-5)=0 .
$$
The algebraic roots are $-8$ and $5$. A side length is positive, so the shorter side measures $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the other side measures $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. The value $8$ is the longer side, while $-8$ fails the condition of the problem.
