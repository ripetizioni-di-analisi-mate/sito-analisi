A sensor records heat exchange using $Q=C(T_f-T_i)$ with constant heat capacity $C>0$. By mistake it adds $3$ degrees to both the final and the initial temperature. How does this affect the calculated $Q$?

- It is too large by $3C$
- The value is still correct
- It is too small by $3C$
- It is too large by $6C$

## Explanation

The error is the same for both readings and cancels in their difference: $(T_f+3)-(T_i+3)=T_f-T_i$. Multiplying by $C$ therefore gives the original $Q$.
