In the model $Q=mc(T_f-T_i)$, the given values are $Q=-720\,\mathrm{J}$, $mc=180\,\mathrm{J/K}$, and $T_f=14\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Calculate $T_i$.

- $10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $18\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $-4\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $14\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

Divide by $mc>0$ to find the change, then solve for $T_i$.
$$
T_f-T_i=\frac{-720}{180}\,\mathrm{K}=-4\,\mathrm{K},\qquad T_i=T_f+4=18\,^\circ\mathrm{C}.
$$
The heat is negative, so the sample cooled and the initial temperature exceeds the final temperature. The value $10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ results from adding $-4$ to $T_f$, treating $-4$ as $T_i-T_f$ instead of $T_f-T_i$.
