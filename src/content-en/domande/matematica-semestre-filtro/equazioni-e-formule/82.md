An ideal gas satisfies $PV=nRT$. Given $PV=1200\,\mathrm{J}$ and $RT=2400\,\mathrm{J/mol}$, calculate $n$.

- $2\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{J/mol}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{mol}$

## Explanation

The product $RT$ is positive, so we can divide both sides by $RT$.
$$
n=\frac{PV}{RT}=\frac{1200\,\mathrm{J}}{2400\,\mathrm{J/mol}}=0.5\,\mathrm{mol}.
$$
Joules cancel in the quotient, leaving moles, the unit for amount of substance. The value $2\,\mathrm{mol}$ is the inverse quotient $\dfrac{RT}{PV}$. The answer $0.5\,\mathrm{J/mol}$ has the correct numerical value but the wrong unit.
