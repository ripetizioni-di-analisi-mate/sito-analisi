A sensor follows the law $y=ax+b$. It reads $y=3$ at $x=1$ and $y=11$ at $x=5$. Calculate $b$.

- $2$
- $-1$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

The two readings give the system $a+b=3$, $5a+b=11$. Subtracting the first equation from the second eliminates $b$.
$$
4a=8,\qquad a=2,\qquad b=3-2=1 .
$$
The law is therefore $y=2x+1$, and indeed at $x=5$ it gives $11$. The value $2$ is the coefficient $a$ rather than the constant term. The value $3$ is the first reading, which equals $b$ only for $x=0$.
