The function $y=ax+b$ takes the value $3$ at $x=1$ and $11$ at $x=5$. What is $b$?

- $2$
- $-1$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

The two conditions give the system
$$
\begin{cases} a+b=3 \\ 5a+b=11 \end{cases}
$$
Subtracting the first equation from the second eliminates $b$.
$$
4a=8,\qquad a=2,\qquad b=3-2=1 .
$$
The function is therefore $y=2x+1$, and indeed at $x=5$ it takes the value $11$. The value $2$ is the coefficient $a$. The value $3$ is the value of $y$ at $x=1$, whereas $b$ is the value of $y$ at $x=0$.
