Solve $x^2-7x+12=0$.

- $x=3\ \text{or}\ x=4$
- $x=-3\ \text{or}\ x=-4$
- $x=0\ \text{or}\ x=7$
- $x=12$

## Explanation

The discriminant is $\Delta=49-48=1$, so the equation has two distinct roots. Two numbers with sum $7$ and product $12$ are $3$ and $4$, so
$$
x^2-7x+12=(x-3)(x-4).
$$
A product is zero when at least one factor is zero, so $x=3$ or $x=4$. The roots $-3$ and $-4$ result from a sign error because in $(x+3)(x+4)$ the coefficient of $x$ is $+7$.
