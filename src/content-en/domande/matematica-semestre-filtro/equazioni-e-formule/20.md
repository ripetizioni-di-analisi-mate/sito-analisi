For what value of $k$ does $x=12$ satisfy $9x+k=162$?

- $6$
- $54$
- $174$
- $-54$

## Explanation

The value $x=12$ is a solution if substituting it into the equation makes both sides equal. Substitution gives a linear equation in the unknown $k$.
$$
9\cdot12+k=162,\qquad 108+k=162,\qquad k=54 .
$$
Here $108$ was subtracted from both sides. The value $-54$ results from a sign error in this subtraction, while $174$ results from adding $12$ to $162$.
