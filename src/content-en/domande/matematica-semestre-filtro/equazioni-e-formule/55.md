Solve $\dfrac{x}{x-1}=2$ over the real numbers.

- $x=1$
- $x=-2$
- $x=\dfrac12$
- $x=2$

## Explanation

The denominator is zero at $x=1$, so the domain is $x\ne1$. On this domain we can multiply both sides by $x-1$, and the factor $2$ multiplies both terms of $x-1$.
$$
x=2(x-1)=2x-2,\qquad x=2 .
$$
The value $2$ belongs to the domain, and indeed $\dfrac{2}{1}=2$. The value $x=1$ is precisely the value excluded from the domain, where the left side is undefined.
