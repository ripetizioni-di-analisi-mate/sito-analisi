What is the real solution set of $(x-9)^2=36$?

- $\{3,15\}$
- $\{-15,-3\}$
- $\{-27,45\}$
- $\{15\}$

## Explanation

A number has square $36$ if and only if it equals $6$ or $-6$, so the expression $x-9$ can take either value.
$$
x-9=6\ \Rightarrow\ x=15,\qquad x-9=-6\ \Rightarrow\ x=3 .
$$
The solutions are therefore $3$ and $15$. The set $\{15\}$ results from considering only the positive square root. The set $\{-27,45\}$ comes from using $36$ in place of $\sqrt{36}=6$, while $\{-15,-3\}$ comes from subtracting $9$ instead of adding it.
