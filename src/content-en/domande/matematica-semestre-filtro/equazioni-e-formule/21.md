A triangle has area $54\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and base $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its height in centimeters?

- $18$
- $54$
- $4.5$
- $9$

## Explanation

The area of the triangle is $A=\dfrac{bh}{2}$. Multiply both sides by $2$ and divide by the base, which is positive.
$$
h=\frac{2A}{b}=\frac{2\cdot54\,\mathrm{cm^2}}{12\,\mathrm{cm}}=9\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
With $b=12$ and $h=9$, the result is again $\dfrac{12\cdot9}{2}=54$. The value $4.5$ comes from dividing the area by the base without multiplying by $2$, as if the figure were a rectangle.
