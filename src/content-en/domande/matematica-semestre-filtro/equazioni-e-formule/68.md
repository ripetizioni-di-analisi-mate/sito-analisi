For which real values of $x$ is $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{4-x}}$ defined?

- $x\le4$
- $x<4$
- $x>4$
- $x\ge4$

## Explanation

Two conditions must hold simultaneously. The radicand must be nonnegative, and the square root in the denominator must be nonzero.
$$
\begin{cases}
4-x\ge0\\
\sqrt{4-x}\ne0
\end{cases}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 4-x>0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x<4 .
$$
The answer $x\le4$ imposes only the restriction on the radicand and includes $x=4$, where the denominator is zero. The answer $x>4$ comes from reversing the inequality.
