In a system of equations, $x+y=15$ and $2x+3y=39$. What is $y$?

- $9$
- $18$
- $15$
- $6$

## Explanation

Multiply the first equation by $2$ so that the coefficient of $x$ agrees with its coefficient in the second equation. Subtracting corresponding sides eliminates $x$.
$$
(2x+3y)-(2x+2y)=39-30,\qquad y=9 .
$$
The first equation gives $x=15-9=6$, and indeed $2\cdot6+3\cdot9=39$. The value $6$ is $x$, while $15$ is the sum of the two unknowns.
