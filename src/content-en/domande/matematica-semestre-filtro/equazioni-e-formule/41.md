How many real solutions does the equation $8(x+11)=8x+88$ have?

- Infinitely many.
- None.
- Two.
- One.

## Explanation

The factor $8$ multiplies both terms of $x+11$, so the left side is $8x+88$. Subtracting $8x$ from both sides gives
$$
88=88 ,
$$
a true equality that no longer contains $x$. In the form $ax=b$, we have $a=0$ and $b=0$, so the equation is an identity and **every real number** is a solution. The answer would be “None” if the constant terms were different.
