Two conductors with positive resistances obey $V=RI$ and carry the same positive current. If $V_2=3V_1$, what is $\dfrac{R_2}{R_1}$?

- $\dfrac13$
- $9$
- $3$
- $1$

## Explanation

From the law $V=RI$ with $I>0$, we obtain $R=\dfrac{V}{I}$ for both conductors. The common current cancels in the ratio.
$$
R_1=\frac{V_1}{I},\qquad R_2=\frac{V_2}{I},\qquad \frac{R_2}{R_1}=\frac{V_2}{V_1}=3 .
$$
At a fixed current, resistance is proportional to voltage. The value $\dfrac13$ is the inverse ratio $\dfrac{R_1}{R_2}$, while $9$ comes from squaring a ratio that appears only to the first power.
