A car travels at a constant speed of $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$. How many meters does it travel in $15\,\mathrm{s}$, according to the relation $s=vt$?

- $1080\,\mathrm{m}$
- $300\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3888\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

To obtain meters from a time in seconds, express the speed in meters per second. Since $1\,\mathrm{km/h}=\dfrac{1000\,\mathrm{m}}{3600\,\mathrm{s}}$, divide by $3.6$.
$$
v=\frac{72}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s}=20\,\mathrm{m/s},\qquad s=20\,\mathrm{m/s}\cdot15\,\mathrm{s}=300\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $1080$ comes from multiplying $72$ by $15$ without converting the speed. The value $3888$ comes from multiplying by $3.6$ instead of dividing.
