The volume of a liquid in liters is given by $V(t)=8-2t$, with $t$ in minutes, until the container is empty. What is the greatest time consistent with $V(t)\ge0$ and $t\ge0$?

- $2\,\mathrm{min}$
- $6\,\mathrm{min}$
- $8\,\mathrm{min}$
- $4\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Both conditions of the model must hold simultaneously.
$$
\begin{cases}
8-2t\ge0\\
t\ge0
\end{cases}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 0\le t\le4 .
$$
The maximum time is therefore $4\,\mathrm{min}$, when $V(4)=0$ and the container is empty. The value $8\,\mathrm{min}$ comes from omitting the division by the coefficient $2$. The value $6\,\mathrm{min}$ comes from subtracting $2$ from $8$.
