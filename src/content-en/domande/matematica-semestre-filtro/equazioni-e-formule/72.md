Solve $(x-2)^2=x^2+8$.

- $x=1$
- $x=-2$
- $x=-3$
- $x=-1$

## Explanation

The square of a binomial includes twice the product of its terms, so $(x-2)^2=x^2-4x+4$. Subtracting $x^2$ from both sides leaves a linear equation.
$$
-4x+4=8,\qquad -4x=4,\qquad x=-1 .
$$
At $x=-1$, both sides equal $9$. The value $1$ uses the wrong sign for the cross term, $+4x$. The value $-2$ comes from writing $-2x$, which omits the factor $2$ in the cross term.
