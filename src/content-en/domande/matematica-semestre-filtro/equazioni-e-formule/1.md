Solve $4x+7=35$.

- $8$
- $\dfrac{21}{2}$
- $-7$
- $7$

## Explanation

Subtract $7$ from both sides to leave the term containing the unknown alone on the left. Since the coefficient $4$ is nonzero, both sides can then be divided by $4$.
$$
4x=35-7=28,\qquad x=\frac{28}{4}=7 .
$$
At $x=7$, the left side equals $28+7=35$, so the equality holds. The value $\dfrac{21}{2}$ results from adding $7$ to $35$ instead of subtracting it.
