Solve $3x^2-12=0$.

- $\{2\}$
- $\{-2,2\}$
- $\{-4,4\}$
- $\{4\}$

## Explanation

Add $12$ to both sides and divide by $3$.
$$
3x^2=12,\qquad x^2=4 .
$$
The real numbers whose square is $4$ are $2$ and $-2$, so the solution set is $\{-2,2\}$. The set $\{2\}$ includes only $\sqrt4$, which denotes the nonnegative value, and omits the negative solution. The sets containing $4$ result from omitting the square root in $x^2=4$.
