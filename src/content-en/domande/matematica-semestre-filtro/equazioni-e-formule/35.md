Which value of $x$ solves $4(x-7)=52$?

- $-6$
- $13$
- $6$
- $20$

## Explanation

The factor $4$ is nonzero, so we can divide both sides by $4$. Then add $7$ to both sides.
$$
x-7=\frac{52}{4}=13,\qquad x=13+7=20 .
$$
At $x=20$, the left side equals $4\cdot13=52$. The value $13$ is the value of the expression $x-7$ before solving for $x$. The value $6$ results from subtracting $7$ from $13$ instead of adding it.
