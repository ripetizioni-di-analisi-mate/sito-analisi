For what value of $k$ does the system of equations $x+2y=3$ and $2x+ky=5$ have no solutions?

- $k=2$
- $k=4$
- $k=-4$
- $k=1$

## Explanation

Multiplying the first equation by $2$ gives $2x+4y=6$. For $k=4$, the second equation becomes $2x+4y=5$, so a solution would have to give $2x+4y$ both the value $6$ and the value $5$. Therefore the system **has no solutions** and the lines are distinct and parallel. For $k\ne4$, the lines intersect at a single point. The value $k=2$ equates the coefficients of $y$ without comparing those of $x$.
