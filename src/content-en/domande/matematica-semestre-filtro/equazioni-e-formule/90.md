In the model $A=bh$, with $h>0$, which formula correctly gives $b$ in terms of $A$ and $h$?

- $b=\dfrac{A}{h}$
- $b=\dfrac{h}{A}$
- $b=Ah$
- $b=A-h$

## Explanation

Since $h$ is positive, both sides of $A=bh$ can be divided by $h$.
$$
\frac{A}{h}=\frac{bh}{h}=b .
$$
A dimensional check confirms the result because dividing an area in $\mathrm{m^2}$ by a length in $\mathrm{m}$ gives a length in $\mathrm{m}$. The formulas $b=\dfrac{h}{A}$ and $b=Ah$ have units of $\mathrm{m^{-1}}$ and $\mathrm{m^3}$, while $A-h$ takes the difference of quantities with different dimensions.
