Which pair satisfies both $2x-y=1$ and $x+2y=8$?

- $(3,2)$
- $(2,3)$
- $(1,1)$
- $(2,-3)$

## Explanation

Solve the first equation to obtain $y=2x-1$, then substitute that expression into the second.
$$
x+2(2x-1)=8,\qquad 5x-2=8,\qquad x=2 .
$$
Consequently, $y=2\cdot2-1=3$. The pair $(2,3)$ satisfies both equations because $4-3=1$ and $2+6=8$. The pair $(3,2)$ interchanges the values of the unknowns and fails the first equation because $6-2=4$. The pair $(1,1)$ satisfies only the first equation.
