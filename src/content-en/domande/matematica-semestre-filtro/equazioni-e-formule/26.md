A normal force of magnitude $60\,\mathrm{N}$ acts uniformly on a surface of area $30\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. What is the pressure $p=\dfrac{F}{A}$?

- $2\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $200\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

To obtain the pressure in pascals, express the area in square meters. One centimeter is $10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}$, so one square centimeter is $10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
$$
A=30\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.003\,\mathrm{m^2},\qquad p=\frac{60\,\mathrm{N}}{0.003\,\mathrm{m^2}}=20\,000\,\mathrm{Pa}.
$$
The value $2\,\mathrm{Pa}$ comes from using the area in square centimeters without converting it. The value $200\,\mathrm{Pa}$ uses the conversion factor $10^{-2}$, omitting the square of the ratio of centimeters to meters.
