For what value of $c$ does the equation $x^2-6x+c=0$ have a double real root?

- $c=3$
- $c=6$
- $c=36$
- $c=9$

## Explanation

A quadratic equation has a double root when its discriminant is zero. With $a=1$ and $b=-6$, we obtain
$$
\Delta=36-4c=0,\qquad c=9 .
$$
At $c=9$, the trinomial is the square $(x-3)^2$ and its double root is $x=3$. The value $c=36$ omits the factor $4$ in the term $4ac$. The value $c=3$ is the root rather than the constant term.
