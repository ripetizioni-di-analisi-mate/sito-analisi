What is $x$ if $\dfrac{x}{5}+\dfrac{x}{10}=24$?

- $80$
- $240$
- $120$
- $40$

## Explanation

The least common multiple of the denominators is $10$, so $\dfrac{x}{5}=\dfrac{2x}{10}$. Multiplying both sides by $10$ gives an equation without denominators.
$$
2x+x=240,\qquad 3x=240,\qquad x=80 .
$$
At $x=80$, the two terms equal $16$ and $8$, and their sum is $24$. The value $240$ results from treating the left side as $\dfrac{x}{10}$, omitting the division by $3$.
