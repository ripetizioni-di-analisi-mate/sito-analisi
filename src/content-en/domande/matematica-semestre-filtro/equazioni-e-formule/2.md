An object has mass $200\,\mathrm{g}$ and density $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Use $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}$ to find its volume.

- $50\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $800\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{50}\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides of $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}$ by $V$ gives $\rho V=m$. Since density is positive, we can divide by $\rho$.
$$
V=\frac{m}{\rho}=\frac{200\,\mathrm{g}}{4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}}=50\,\mathrm{cm^3}.
$$
Dividing grams by grams per cubic centimeter gives cubic centimeters. The value $800$ is the product $m\rho$, which has units of $\mathrm{g^2/cm^3}$, while $\dfrac{1}{50}$ is the quotient with numerator and denominator reversed, $\dfrac{\rho}{m}$.
