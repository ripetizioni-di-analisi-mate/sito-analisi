A substance cools and $T_f<T_i$. If $Q=mc(T_f-T_i)$ with $mc>0$, what is the sign of $Q$?

- Positive.
- Zero.
- Negative.
- The sign depends on the unit of mass.

## Explanation

Since $T_f<T_i$, the change $T_f-T_i$ is negative. The factor $mc$ is positive, and the product of a positive and a negative number is negative.
$$
mc>0,\quad T_f-T_i<0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Q<0 .
$$
In this model, negative heat indicates that the sample releases heat. The sign of $Q$ always agrees with the sign of the temperature change, so it is independent of the units chosen for mass.
