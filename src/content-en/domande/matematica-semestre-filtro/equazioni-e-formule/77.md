A temperature sensor follows the model $U=0.02\,T+0.3$, where $U$ is the numerical value in volts and $T$ the numerical value in degrees Celsius. What temperature corresponds to the reading $U=0.8\,\mathrm{V}$?

- $25\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $55\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $40\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $0.316\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

To find $T$, subtract the constant term and divide by the nonzero coefficient $0.02$.
$$
0.02\,T=0.8-0.3=0.5,\qquad T=\frac{0.5}{0.02}=25 .
$$
Indeed, $0.02\cdot25+0.3=0.8$. The value $55$ comes from adding $0.3$ instead of subtracting it, while $40$ overlooks the constant term. The value $0.316$ comes from substituting $0.8$ for $T$ instead of solving for $T$.
