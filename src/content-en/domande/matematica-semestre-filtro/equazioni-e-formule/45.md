What is the solution of $9x+11=8x+17$?

- $28$
- $3$
- $6$
- $-6$

## Explanation

Subtract $8x$ from both sides, leaving the unknown only on the left, and then subtract $11$.
$$
x+11=17,\qquad x=17-11=6 .
$$
At $x=6$, both sides equal $65$. The value $28$ results from adding $11$ and $17$ instead of subtracting, while $-6$ results from subtracting $17$ from $11$.
