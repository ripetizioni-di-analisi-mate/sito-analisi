For what value of $k$ is the equation $2(x+k)=2x+8$ true for every real $x$?

- $k=2$
- $k=6$
- $k=4$
- $k=8$

## Explanation

The factor $2$ multiplies both terms in $x+k$. Subtracting $2x$ from both sides then eliminates the unknown.
$$
2x+2k=2x+8,\qquad 2k=8 .
$$
The equation is an identity when the remaining equality is true, that is, for $k=4$. Every other value of $k$ gives a false equality, so the equation has no solutions. The value $k=8$ overlooks that $k$ is also multiplied by $2$.
