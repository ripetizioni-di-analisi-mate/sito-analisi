What is the real solution set of $2x^2-3x-2=0$?

- $\left\{-\dfrac12,\ 2\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac12,\ -2\right\}$
- $\{-1,\ 4\}$
- The equation has no real solutions

## Explanation

With $a=2$, $b=-3$, and $c=-2$, the discriminant is positive, so there are two roots.
$$
\Delta=9+16=25,\qquad x=\frac{3\pm5}{4}.
$$
The roots are $x=2$ and $x=-\dfrac12$. The set $\{-1,4\}$ results from dividing by $2$ instead of $2a=4$. The set $\left\{\dfrac12,-2\right\}$ results from using $b$ in place of $-b$. The answer claiming there are no solutions comes from the calculation $\Delta=9-16$, which overlooks the sign of $c$.
