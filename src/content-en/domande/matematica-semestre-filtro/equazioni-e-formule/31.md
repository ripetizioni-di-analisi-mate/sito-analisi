Which operation always preserves the equivalence of an equation on its domain?

- Multiply both sides by $x$.
- Square both sides.
- Add $x^2$ to both sides.
- Divide both sides by $x-1$.

## Explanation

Adding an expression that is defined everywhere to both sides gives an equivalent equation because subtracting the same expression reverses the operation. The expression $x^2$ is defined for every $x$, so this operation is always reversible. Multiplication by $x$ can introduce the solution $x=0$, and division by $x-1$ is undefined for $x=1$. Squaring also makes opposite numbers such as $2$ and $-2$ equal.
