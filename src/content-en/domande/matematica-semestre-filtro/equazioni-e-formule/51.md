Given $p=\dfrac{F}{A}$, what unit is equivalent to the unit of the product $pA$ when $p$ is in pascals and $A$ is in square meters?

- $\mathrm{N/m}$
- $\mathrm{N}$
- $\mathrm{N/m^2}$
- $\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

One pascal equals one newton per square meter, or $1\,\mathrm{Pa}=1\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$. When multiplied by an area in square meters, the powers of meters cancel.
$$
\mathrm{Pa}\cdot\mathrm{m^2}=\frac{\mathrm{N}}{\mathrm{m^2}}\cdot\mathrm{m^2}=\mathrm{N}.
$$
This agrees with $F=pA$ being a force. The unit $\mathrm{N/m^2}$ belongs to $p$ alone, while a joule is a newton times a meter and measures energy.
