Which step introduces the extraneous solution $x=0$ when starting from $x=3$?

- $x^2=3x$
- $2x=6$
- $x+3=6$
- $\dfrac{x}{3}=1$

## Explanation

The equation $x^2=3x$ is obtained by multiplying both sides of $x=3$ by $x$, a factor that is zero at $x=0$. Indeed,
$$
x^2=3x\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x(x-3)=0 ,
$$
and the product is also zero at $x=0$. The other equations follow by multiplying by $2$, adding $3$, or dividing by $3$. These operations are reversible for every real $x$, so they preserve the unique solution $x=3$.
