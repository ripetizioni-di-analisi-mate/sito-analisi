At what point do the lines $y=3x-1$ and $y=x+5$ intersect?

- $(8,3)$
- $(3,8)$
- $(2,7)$
- $(-3,2)$

## Explanation

At a common point the two lines have the same vertical coordinate, so its horizontal coordinate solves the equation obtained by setting the two right sides equal.
$$
3x-1=x+5,\qquad 2x=6,\qquad x=3 .
$$
Substitution into $y=x+5$ gives $y=8$, and indeed $3\cdot3-1=8$ as well. The point $(8,3)$ has its horizontal and vertical coordinates interchanged. The point $(2,7)$ lies only on the second line. The point $(-3,2)$ comes from the sign error $2x=-6$.
