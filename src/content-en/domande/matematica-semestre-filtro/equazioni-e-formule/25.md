For an ideal gas, $pV=nRT$, with $R=8.31\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given $n=2\,\mathrm{mol}$, $p=83.1\,\mathrm{kPa}$, and $V=0.060\,\mathrm{m^3}$, what is the absolute temperature $T$?

- $600\,\mathrm{K}$
- $300\,\mathrm{K}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{K}$
- $150\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

Since $nR>0$, divide both sides by $nR$. Express the pressure in pascals so that $pV$ is in joules.
$$
T=\frac{pV}{nR}=\frac{83\,100\cdot0.060\,\mathrm{J}}{2\cdot8.31\,\mathrm{J/K}}=\frac{4986}{16.62}\,\mathrm{K}=300\,\mathrm{K}.
$$
The value $600\,\mathrm{K}$ comes from omitting the division by $n$, while $0.3\,\mathrm{K}$ leaves the pressure in kilopascals.
