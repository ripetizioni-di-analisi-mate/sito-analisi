After simplifying the residual $R(x)$, the left side minus the right side, you obtain $R(x)=0$ for a candidate value. What one condition remains to be checked for the candidate to be a solution?

- The candidate is an integer.
- The candidate belongs to the original domain.
- The candidate is greater than zero in every equation.
- Both sides equal zero.

## Explanation

On the original domain, simplification leaves the value of the residual unchanged, so $R(x)=0$ there is equivalent to the two sides being equal. However, the simplified expression may also be defined at points outside the domain. For example, $\dfrac{x^2-1}{x+1}-(x-1)$ simplifies to $0$, which also holds at $x=-1$, where the original left side is undefined. It therefore remains to check that the candidate belongs to the original domain.
