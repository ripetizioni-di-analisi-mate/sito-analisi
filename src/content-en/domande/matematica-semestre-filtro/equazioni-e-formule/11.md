A flat surface of area $0.025\,\mathrm{m^2}$ is subject to a uniform pressure of $12\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Use $p=\dfrac{F}{A}$ to find the magnitude $F$ of the normal force.

- $300\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $480\,\mathrm{N}$
- $480000\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides of $p=\dfrac{F}{A}$ by the area gives $F=pA$. Convert the pressure to pascals, or newtons per square meter.
$$
F=12\,000\,\mathrm{Pa}\cdot0.025\,\mathrm{m^2}=300\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The value $0.30\,\mathrm{N}$ results from leaving the pressure in kilopascals. The values $480$ and $480000$ come from the quotient $\dfrac{p}{A}$, which does not have units of force.
