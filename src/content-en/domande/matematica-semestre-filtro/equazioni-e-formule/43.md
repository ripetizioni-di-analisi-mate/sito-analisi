Which expression is equivalent to $-(3-2x)+4(x-1)$?

- $2x-7$
- $6x+7$
- $2x+1$
- $6x-7$

## Explanation

The minus sign in front of $3-2x$ changes the signs of both terms, and the factor $4$ multiplies both terms of $x-1$.
$$
-(3-2x)+4(x-1)=-3+2x+4x-4=6x-7 .
$$
First combine the terms in $x$ and then the constants. The expression $2x-7$ comes from changing the sign of $3$ while leaving the sign of $-2x$ unchanged. The expression $6x+7$ uses the wrong sign for the constants.
