A spring has spring constant $k=200\,\mathrm{N/m}$. In the model $E=\dfrac12kx^2$, the value of $E$ is $1\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the magnitude of the deformation?

- $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.01\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Multiply both sides by $2$ and divide by $k>0$ to isolate $x^2$.
$$
x^2=\frac{2E}{k}=\frac{2}{200}\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.01\,\mathrm{m^2},\qquad |x|=\sqrt{0.01}\,\mathrm{m}=0.10\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
Take the nonnegative square root because the magnitude is required, and indeed $\dfrac12\cdot200\cdot0.01=1$. The value $0.01\,\mathrm{m}$ is $x^2$ before taking the square root.
