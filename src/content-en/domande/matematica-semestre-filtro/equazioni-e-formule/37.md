Temperature in degrees Celsius $C$ and temperature in degrees Fahrenheit $F$ are related by $C=\dfrac59(F-32)$. What value of $F$ corresponds to $C=25$?

- $45$
- $57$
- $102.6$
- $77$

## Explanation

Multiply both sides by $\dfrac95$ and then add $32$.
$$
F-32=\frac95\,C=\frac95\cdot25=45,\qquad F=45+32=77 .
$$
Substitution recovers $\dfrac59\cdot45=25$. The value $45$ equals $F-32$ before adding $32$. The value $57$ comes from adding $32$ to $25$ without the factor $\dfrac95$. The value $102.6$ comes from multiplying $\dfrac95$ by the sum $25+32$, which uses the wrong order of operations.
