An object of mass $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ has kinetic energy $50\,\mathrm{J}$. Use $E_c=\dfrac12mv^2$ to find its speed.

- $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Multiply both sides by $2$ and divide by the positive mass to isolate $v^2$.
$$
v^2=\frac{2E_c}{m}=\frac{100}{4}\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}=25\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}.
$$
The equation $v^2=25$ has solutions $5$ and $-5$. The problem asks for speed, which is nonnegative, so the answer is $v=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is $v^2$ before taking the square root.
