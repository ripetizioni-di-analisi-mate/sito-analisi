Two instrument readings differ by $11$ units. The higher reading is three times the lower reading plus $8$. What is the lower reading?

- $1.5$
- $3$
- $9.5$
- $11$

## Explanation

Let $x$ be the lower reading. The higher reading is then $3x+8$, and the higher reading minus the lower one is $11$.
$$
(3x+8)-x=11,\qquad 2x=3,\qquad x=1.5 .
$$
The higher reading is therefore $3\cdot1.5+8=12.5$, and indeed $12.5-1.5=11$. The value $3$ comes from stopping at $2x=3$ without dividing by the coefficient $2$.
