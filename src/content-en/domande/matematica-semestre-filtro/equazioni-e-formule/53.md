A container has capacity $0.40\,\mathrm{L}$. A balance calculation requires pouring $480\,\mathrm{mL}$ into it. What follows from comparing the two volumes?

- The calculated volume is below the capacity by $80\,\mathrm{mL}$.
- The container holds exactly the calculated volume.
- The calculated volume exceeds the capacity by $80\,\mathrm{mL}$.
- The calculated volume exceeds the capacity by $8\,\mathrm{mL}$.

## Explanation

To compare the volumes, use the same units. One liter is $1000\,\mathrm{mL}$, so
$$
0.40\,\mathrm{L}=400\,\mathrm{mL},\qquad 480\,\mathrm{mL}-400\,\mathrm{mL}=80\,\mathrm{mL}.
$$
The required volume exceeds the capacity by $80\,\mathrm{mL}$, so the container is too small. It would hold exactly the required volume only if its capacity were $0.48\,\mathrm{L}$.
