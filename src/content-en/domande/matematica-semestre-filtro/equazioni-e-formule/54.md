Which values are excluded from the domain of $\dfrac{x+2}{x^2-9}$?

- Only $3$
- Only $-2$
- $-2$, $-3$, and $3$
- $-3$ and $3$

## Explanation

Exclude the values that make the denominator zero. The denominator is a difference of squares.
$$
x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3)=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x=3\ \text{or}\ x=-3 .
$$
The domain is therefore $\mathbb{R}\setminus\{-3,3\}$. The value $-2$ makes the numerator zero, and at that point the quotient is $0$, so the value remains in the domain. The answer “Only $3$” considers just the positive solution of $x^2=9$.
