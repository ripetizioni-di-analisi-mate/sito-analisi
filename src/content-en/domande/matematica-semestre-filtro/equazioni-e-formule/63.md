Two type A tickets and one type B ticket cost $13$ euros. One type A ticket and two type B tickets cost $11$ euros. How much does one type A ticket cost?

- $3$ euros
- $5$ euros
- $4$ euros
- $6$ euros

## Explanation

Let $a$ and $b$ be the prices in euros. The data give the system $2a+b=13$, $a+2b=11$. Multiply the first equation by $2$ and subtract the second to eliminate $b$.
$$
4a+2b-(a+2b)=26-11,\qquad 3a=15,\qquad a=5 .
$$
The first equation then gives $b=13-10=3$, and indeed $5+2\cdot3=11$. The value $3$ euros is the price of a type B ticket.
