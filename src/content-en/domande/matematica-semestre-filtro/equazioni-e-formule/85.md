A sample has volume $0.2\,\mathrm{L}$ and density $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Calculate its mass.

- $0.6\,\mathrm{g}$
- $60\,\mathrm{g}$
- $600\,\mathrm{g}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The density is in grams per cubic centimeter, so express the volume in cubic centimeters. One liter is $1000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, so $0.2\,\mathrm{L}=200\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Multiplying $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}$ by $V$ gives
$$
m=\rho V=3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}\cdot200\,\mathrm{cm^3}=600\,\mathrm{g}.
$$
The value $0.6\,\mathrm{g}$ comes from multiplying $3$ by $0.2$ without converting the volume. The value $60\,\mathrm{g}$ comes from using $1\,\mathrm{L}=100\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.
