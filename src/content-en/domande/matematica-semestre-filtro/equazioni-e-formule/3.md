Solve $\dfrac{x+3}{3}=5$.

- $2$
- $12$
- $18$
- $-12$

## Explanation

The denominator is the nonzero number $3$, so we can multiply both sides by $3$. Then subtract $3$ from both sides.
$$
x+3=3\cdot5=15,\qquad x=15-3=12 .
$$
At $x=12$, the left side equals $\dfrac{15}{3}=5$. The value $2$ comes from subtracting $3$ from $5$ before multiplying by the denominator, while $18$ comes from adding $3$ to $15$ instead of subtracting it.
