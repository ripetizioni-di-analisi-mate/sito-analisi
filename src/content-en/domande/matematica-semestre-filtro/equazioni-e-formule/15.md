Two numbers $x$ and $y$ have sum $19$. Also, $y-x=3$. What is $x$?

- $8$
- $3$
- $19$
- $11$

## Explanation

The data give the system $x+y=19$, $y-x=3$. Subtract the second equation from the first by subtracting corresponding sides to eliminate $y$.
$$
(x+y)-(y-x)=19-3,\qquad 2x=16,\qquad x=8 .
$$
Consequently $y=19-8=11$, and indeed $11-8=3$. The value $11$ is $y$, the larger number, while $3$ is the given difference.
