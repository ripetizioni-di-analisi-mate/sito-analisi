A rectangle has perimeter $30\,\mathrm{cm}$, and its base exceeds its height by $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its height?

- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6.25\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Let $h$ be the numerical value of the height in centimeters. The base measures $h+5$, and the perimeter is twice the sum of the base and height.
$$
2(h+5+h)=30,\qquad 4h+10=30,\qquad h=5 .
$$
The base therefore measures $10\,\mathrm{cm}$, and indeed $2(10+5)=30$. The value $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the base. The value $12.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ comes from $2h+5=30$, which adds the base and height without doubling their sum.
