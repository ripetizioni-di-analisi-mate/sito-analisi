Express $2.7\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ in kilograms per cubic meter.

- $2.7\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $0.0027\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2700\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $27000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{g}=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $1\,\mathrm{cm^3}=10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, substitute for the two units in the quotient. This gives
$$
2.7\,\frac{\mathrm{g}}{\mathrm{cm^3}}=2.7\cdot\frac{10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg}}{10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3}}=2.7\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}=2700\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.
$$
The numerical value increases by a factor of one thousand because the new unit in the denominator is one million times larger and the new unit in the numerator is only one thousand times larger. The value $0.0027\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ comes from inverting the factors and thus dividing by one thousand.
