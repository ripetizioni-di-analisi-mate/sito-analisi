Express $90\,\mathrm{km/h}$ in meters per second.

- $324\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $250\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

One kilometer equals $1000\,\mathrm{m}$ and one hour equals $3600\,\mathrm{s}$. Multiplying by the two conversion factors cancels kilometers and hours and gives
$$
90\,\frac{\mathrm{km}}{\mathrm{h}}\cdot\frac{1000\,\mathrm{m}}{1\,\mathrm{km}}\cdot\frac{1\,\mathrm{h}}{3600\,\mathrm{s}}=\frac{90}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s}=25\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
The value $324\,\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from multiplying by $3.6$ instead of dividing. The value $250\,\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from dividing by $360$ instead of $3600$.
