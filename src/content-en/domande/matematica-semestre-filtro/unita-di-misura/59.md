A bottle contains $40\,\mathrm{mL}$ at $5\,\mathrm{mg/mL}$. An accidental spill removes $10\,\mathrm{mL}$ of the well-mixed solution, then water restores the volume to 40 mL. How many milligrams of solute remain?

- $50$
- $200$
- $120$
- $150$

## Explanation

After the spill $30\cdot5=150$ mg of solute remain. The added water restores the volume without replacing the lost solute.
