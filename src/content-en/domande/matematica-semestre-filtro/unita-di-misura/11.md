A sample of mass $63\,\mathrm{g}$ occupies $25\,\mathrm{mL}$. Calculate its density in kilograms per cubic meter.

- $2.52\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2520\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $25.2\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $25200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{mL}=1\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, the density in grams per cubic centimeter is
$$
\rho=\frac{63\,\mathrm{g}}{25\,\mathrm{cm^3}}=2.52\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}.
$$
A gram equals $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and a cubic centimeter equals $10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, so the conversion factor is $\dfrac{10^{-3}}{10^{-6}}=10^{3}$. Thus the density is $2520\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. The value $2.52\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ comes from stopping at the quotient in grams per cubic centimeter and changing only the unit name.
