An image has rectangular pixels $4\,\mu\mathrm m$ wide and $5\,\mu\mathrm m$ high. How many pixels cover exactly $1\,\mathrm{mm^2}$, ignoring gaps?

- $50$
- $50\,000$
- $500$
- $200\,000$

## Explanation

A square millimetre contains $10^6$ square micrometres. Each pixel covers $4\cdot5=20$ of them, so $\dfrac{10^6}{20}=50\,000$ pixels are needed.
