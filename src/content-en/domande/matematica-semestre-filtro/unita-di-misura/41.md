A runner covers $400\,\mathrm{m}$ in $80\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the runner’s average speed in kilometers per hour?

- $5\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $18\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $180\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

Average speed is distance traveled divided by time, so $v=\dfrac{400\,\mathrm{m}}{80\,\mathrm{s}}=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In one hour, or $3600\,\mathrm{s}$, a speed of $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ gives a distance of $3600\,\mathrm{m}=3.6\,\mathrm{km}$, so $1\,\mathrm{m/s}=3.6\,\mathrm{km/h}$. It follows that
$$
v=5\cdot3.6\,\mathrm{km/h}=18\,\mathrm{km/h}.
$$
The rounded value $1.4\,\mathrm{km/h}$ comes from dividing by $3.6$ instead of multiplying. The value $5\,\mathrm{km/h}$ comes from retaining the numerical value in meters per second and changing only the unit.
