If $x$ is a position, $v$ is a velocity, and $t$ is a time, which sum is dimensionally consistent?

- $x+vt$
- $x+v$
- $x+t$
- $x+vt^2$

## Explanation

Two quantities can be added only if they have the same dimension. The position $x$ has dimension $L$ and the product $vt$ has dimension $LT^{-1}\cdot T=L$, so the sum $x+vt$ is dimensionally consistent and again represents a position. In the sum $x+v$ the second term has dimension $LT^{-1}$. In $x+t$ it has dimension $T$, and in $x+vt^2$ it has dimension $LT$. These three sums therefore combine quantities of different physical kinds.
