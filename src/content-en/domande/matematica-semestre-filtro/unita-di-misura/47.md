A store sells $250\,\mathrm{g}$ of cheese for $3$ euros. How many euros does a kilogram of the same cheese cost?

- $12$
- $0.75$
- $0.012$
- $1.2$

## Explanation

The price per kilogram is the amount paid divided by the mass in kilograms. Since $250\,\mathrm{g}=0.250\,\mathrm{kg}$, we have
$$
\frac{3\ \text{euros}}{0.250\,\mathrm{kg}}=12\ \text{euros/kg}.
$$
The value $0.012$ is the price per gram, obtained by dividing by $250$ without converting the mass. The value $0.75$ comes from multiplying the price by the mass instead of dividing. The value $1.2$ comes from the conversion $250\,\mathrm{g}=2.5\,\mathrm{kg}$, which is off by a factor of ten.
