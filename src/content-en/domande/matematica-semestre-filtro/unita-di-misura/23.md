A fiber has diameter $3.5\,\mu\mathrm{m}$, measured to two significant figures. Which expression in nanometers gives the same value and explicitly shows the two significant figures?

- $3.50\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $3.5\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $3.500\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $3.5\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

One micrometer contains $10^{3}$ nanometers, so the diameter is $3.5\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{nm}$. The prefix factor is exact, so the conversion preserves the number of significant figures. In $3.5\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{nm}$, the coefficient of the power of ten has two digits, as in the original measurement. The expressions $3.50\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{nm}$ and $3.500\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{nm}$ give the same value with three and four significant figures, respectively, implying a precision that the original measurement does not have. The value $3.5\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{nm}$ comes from inverting the factor.
