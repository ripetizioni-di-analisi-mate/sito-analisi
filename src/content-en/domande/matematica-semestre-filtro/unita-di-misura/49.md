A disk has radius $20\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is its area in square centimeters?

- $2\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $40\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $4\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $400\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

First convert the radius to centimeters. Since $1\,\mathrm{cm}=10\,\mathrm{mm}$, we have $r=20\,\mathrm{mm}=2\,\mathrm{cm}$, so
$$
A=\pi r^2=\pi\cdot(2\,\mathrm{cm})^2=4\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}.
$$
The value $400\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ is the area in square millimeters with only the unit name changed. The value $40\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ comes from converting the area with the linear factor $10$ instead of $100$. The value $2\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ comes from omitting the square on the radius.
