In the relation $x=x_0+vt+bt^2$, $x$ and $x_0$ are positions, $v$ is a velocity, and $t$ is a time. What dimension must $b$ have?

- $LT^{-1}$
- $T^{-2}$
- $LT^2$
- $LT^{-2}$

## Explanation

In a sum all terms have the same dimension. The left-hand side $x$ is a position, so $bt^2$ also has dimension $L$. Since $[t^2]=T^2$, we obtain
$$
[b]=\frac{L}{T^{2}}=LT^{-2},
$$
so $b$ has the dimension of acceleration. The term $vt$ also has dimension $LT^{-1}\cdot T=L$, consistent with the other terms. The answer $LT^{-1}$ comes from dividing by $T$ instead of $T^2$. The answer $T^{-2}$ omits length in the numerator.
