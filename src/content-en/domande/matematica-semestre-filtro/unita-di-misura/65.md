Which quantity doubles when two identical samples of the same homogeneous solution are combined at the same temperature and pressure without loss of material?

- The molar concentration.
- The density of the solution.
- The temperature.
- The amount of solute in moles.

## Explanation

An extensive quantity is additive, meaning that for two combined subsystems its value is the sum of their values. When two identical samples are combined, the amount of solute is the sum of two equal amounts, so it doubles. Molar concentration is $\dfrac{n}{V}$, and both $n$ and $V$ double, so the ratio stays the same. The same applies to density, $\dfrac{m}{V}$. Temperature is intensive, so two samples at the same temperature form a mixture at that temperature.
