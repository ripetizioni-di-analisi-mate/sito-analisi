One object travels at $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and another at $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the same direction. By how much does the speed of the first exceed that of the second?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $57\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

To compare the two speeds, express them in the same unit. Since $1\,\mathrm{m/s}=3.6\,\mathrm{km/h}$, the first speed is
$$
\frac{72}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s}=20\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
The difference between the speeds is therefore $20\,\mathrm{m/s}-15\,\mathrm{m/s}=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $57\,\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from subtracting $15$ from $72$ without converting and thus mixing two different units. The value $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the first speed after conversion but before subtraction.
