A container of capacity $2.4\,\mathrm{L}$ is filled at a constant flow rate of $20\,\mathrm{cm^3/s}$. The container is initially empty. How long does it take to fill?

- $12\,\mathrm{s}$
- $120\,\mathrm{s}$
- $48\,\mathrm{s}$
- $240\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Express the volume in cubic centimeters, the volume unit used in the flow rate. Since $1\,\mathrm{L}=1000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, we have $2.4\,\mathrm{L}=2400\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. At constant flow rate, the filling time is volume divided by flow rate, so
$$
t=\frac{2400\,\mathrm{cm^3}}{20\,\mathrm{cm^3/s}}=120\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
The value $12\,\mathrm{s}$ comes from the conversion $2.4\,\mathrm{L}=240\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, which is off by a factor of ten. The value $48\,\mathrm{s}$ comes from using the product $2.4\cdot20$ instead of the quotient.
