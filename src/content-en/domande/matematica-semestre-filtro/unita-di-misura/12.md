A solution of final volume $250\,\mathrm{mL}$ contains $4\,\mathrm{g}$ of a solute with molar mass $80\,\mathrm{g/mol}$. Calculate the molar concentration in moles per liter.

- $0.05\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $0.0125\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $16\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{mol/L}$

## Explanation

Divide the solute mass by its molar mass to find the amount of solute, giving $n=\dfrac{4\,\mathrm{g}}{80\,\mathrm{g/mol}}=0.05\,\mathrm{mol}$. The final volume is $250\,\mathrm{mL}=0.250\,\mathrm{L}$, so
$$
c=\frac{0.05\,\mathrm{mol}}{0.250\,\mathrm{L}}=0.20\,\mathrm{mol/L}.
$$
The value $0.05\,\mathrm{mol/L}$ comes from stopping at the amount of substance without dividing by the volume. The value $0.0125\,\mathrm{mol/L}$ comes from multiplying by the volume instead of dividing.
