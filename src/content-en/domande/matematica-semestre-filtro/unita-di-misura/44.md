Which of the following is the SI base unit of mass?

- $\mathrm{g}$
- $\mathrm{mg}$
- $\mathrm{N}$
- $\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

In the International System of Units, the base unit of mass is the kilogram, symbol $\mathrm{kg}$. It is the only base unit whose name already contains a prefix. The gram and the milligram are submultiples of the kilogram, equal to $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $10^{-6}\,\mathrm{kg}$, respectively. The newton is a derived unit of force because $1\,\mathrm{N}=1\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s^2}$. Therefore the only answer giving the base unit of mass is $\mathrm{kg}$.
