Convert 10 minutes and 13 seconds to seconds.

- $1380$
- $1013$
- $613$
- $790$

## Explanation

One minute equals $60\,\mathrm{s}$, so $10\,\mathrm{min}=600\,\mathrm{s}$. Add the seconds to the part originally expressed in minutes. The duration is therefore
$$
600\,\mathrm{s}+13\,\mathrm{s}=613\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
The value $1013$ comes from treating minutes as hundreds of seconds, using a base-ten conversion instead of a base-sixty conversion. The value $1380$ comes from multiplying the seconds by $60$ as well. The value $790$ comes from interchanging the minutes and seconds.
