To convert a volume from $\mathrm{mm^3}$ to $\mathrm{m^3}$, what operation is performed on its numerical value?

- $\text{Multiply by }10^{-3}$
- $\text{Multiply by }(10^{-3})^3$
- $\text{Multiply by }(10^{-3})^2$
- $\text{Multiply by }(10^{3})^3$

## Explanation

One millimeter equals $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$. Volume is the product of three lengths, so the linear factor occurs three times and $1\,\mathrm{mm^3}=(10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m})^3=10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Therefore multiply the numerical value in cubic millimeters by $(10^{-3})^3$. The factor $10^{-3}$ applies to lengths and the factor $(10^{-3})^2$ to areas. The factor $(10^{3})^3$ applies to the reverse conversion, from cubic meters to cubic millimeters.
