An area was entered as $25\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, but the technician had written $25\,\mathrm{mm^2}$. By what factor must the recorded area be multiplied to correct it?

- $10^{-1}$
- $10$
- $10^2$
- $10^{-2}$

## Explanation

The numerical entry is identical; the unit changes. A square millimetre is one hundredth of a square centimetre, so the corrected area is one hundredth of the recorded area.
