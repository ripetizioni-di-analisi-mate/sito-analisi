An object moves at a constant speed of $7.2\,\mathrm{km/h}$ for $2\,\mathrm{min}$. How far does it travel in meters?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $240\,\mathrm{m}$
- $864\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

To obtain the speed in meters per second, divide the value in kilometers per hour by $3.6$, giving $v=\dfrac{7.2}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s}=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The time is $2\,\mathrm{min}=120\,\mathrm{s}$. At constant speed, distance is speed times time, so
$$
d=2\,\mathrm{m/s}\cdot120\,\mathrm{s}=240\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $864\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from converting the time but leaving the speed in kilometers per hour. The value $4\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from leaving the time in minutes.
