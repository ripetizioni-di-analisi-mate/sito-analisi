A homogeneous solid is divided into two equal pieces without deformation. Which pair of properties describes each piece?

- Half the initial mass and half the initial density.
- The same initial mass and half the initial density.
- Half the initial mass and twice the initial density.
- Half the initial mass and the same density.

## Explanation

When the solid is divided into two equal pieces, each has half the mass and half the volume of the original solid. Density is the ratio of mass to volume, so
$$
\rho_{\mathrm{pezzo}}=\frac{\frac{m}{2}}{\frac{V}{2}}=\frac{m}{V}=\rho.
$$
Mass is extensive and is halved, while density is intensive and stays the same. Answers that halve or double the density treat it as depending only on mass or only on volume.
