Which expression is dimensionless if $x$ and $x_0$ are positive lengths and $t$ is a positive time?

- $\dfrac{x-x_0}{x_0}$
- $x-x_0$
- $\dfrac{x}{t}$
- $x\cdot x_0$

## Explanation

A ratio is dimensionless when its numerator and denominator have the same dimension. The difference $x-x_0$ between two lengths has dimension $L$, and $x_0$ also has dimension $L$, so
$$
\left[\frac{x-x_0}{x_0}\right]=\frac{L}{L}=1.
$$
This ratio is the relative extension, a dimensionless number. The expressions $x-x_0$, $\dfrac{x}{t}$, and $x\cdot x_0$ instead have dimensions $L$, $LT^{-1}$, and $L^2$, so each has a unit of measurement.
