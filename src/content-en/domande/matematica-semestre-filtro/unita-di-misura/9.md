A rectangle has sides $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $150\,\mathrm{mm}$. Calculate its area in square meters.

- $0.45\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.045\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.0045\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

Express both sides in meters so their product is directly in square meters. Since $30\,\mathrm{cm}=0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ and $150\,\mathrm{mm}=0.150\,\mathrm{m}$, the area is
$$
A=0.30\,\mathrm{m}\cdot0.150\,\mathrm{m}=0.045\,\mathrm{m^2}.
$$
The value $0.45\,\mathrm{m^2}$ comes from reading $150\,\mathrm{mm}$ as $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$, which divides by one hundred instead of one thousand. The value $0.0045\,\mathrm{m^2}$ comes from the opposite error on the first side, which is read as $0.03\,\mathrm{m}$.
