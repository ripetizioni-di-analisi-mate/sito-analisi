A sheet has area $12\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. A section of area $800\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ is cut out. What area remains, in square centimeters?

- $0.4\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $11.2\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

Subtract the two areas after expressing them in the same unit. Since $1\,\mathrm{cm}=10\,\mathrm{mm}$, we have $1\,\mathrm{cm^2}=100\,\mathrm{mm^2}$, so the cutout has area $\dfrac{800}{100}\,\mathrm{cm^2}=8\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Therefore the remaining area is
$$
12\,\mathrm{cm^2}-8\,\mathrm{cm^2}=4\,\mathrm{cm^2}.
$$
The value $11.2\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ comes from converting the cutout area with the factor $1000$, as if $0.8\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ held. The value $8\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ is the area of the piece cut out.
