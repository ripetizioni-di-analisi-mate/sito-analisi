An object travels at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its speed in kilometers per minute?

- $0.12\,\mathrm{km/min}$
- $0.002\,\mathrm{km/min}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{km/min}$
- $120\,\mathrm{km/min}$

## Explanation

In one minute, or $60\,\mathrm{s}$, a speed of $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ gives a distance of $2\cdot60=120\,\mathrm{m}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{km}=1000\,\mathrm{m}$, we have $120\,\mathrm{m}=0.12\,\mathrm{km}$, so
$$
v=2\,\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}\cdot\frac{60\,\mathrm{s}}{1\,\mathrm{min}}\cdot\frac{1\,\mathrm{km}}{1000\,\mathrm{m}}=0.12\,\mathrm{km/min}.
$$
The value $0.002\,\mathrm{km/min}$ comes from converting meters and overlooking time. The value $120\,\mathrm{km/min}$ is the distance traveled in meters in one minute with the wrong unit.
