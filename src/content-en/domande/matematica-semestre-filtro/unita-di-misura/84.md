Two instruments measure the same speed. A rounds to the nearest km/h and reads $36$ km/h. B rounds to the nearest tenth of a m/s and reads $10.1$ m/s. Which exact speed is consistent with both readings?

- $10.08\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10.16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9.98\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9.90\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

A speed of $10.08$ m/s is $36.288$ km/h. These round to $10.1$ and $36$ in their respective units. The value $10.16$ m/s is too high for both readings, and the two lower values are too low for B.
