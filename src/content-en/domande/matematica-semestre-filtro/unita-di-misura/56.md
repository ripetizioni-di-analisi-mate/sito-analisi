One sheet gives $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$ and another gives $25\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which comparison is correct?

- The two expressions represent the same length.
- The second length is one hundred times as long as the first.
- The first length is one hundred times as long as the second.
- The lengths have different physical dimensions.

## Explanation

One meter contains $100\,\mathrm{cm}$, so
$$
0.25\,\mathrm{m}=0.25\cdot100\,\mathrm{cm}=25\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
The two expressions therefore represent the same length. In the second expression the numerical value is one hundred times as large because a centimeter is one hundredth of a meter, and the two changes cancel. Both measurements are lengths, so they have the same dimension $L$. Answers that claim one length is one hundred times as large confuse the numerical value with the physical quantity.
