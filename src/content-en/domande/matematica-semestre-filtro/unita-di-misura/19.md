A solution is kept at a temperature of $37\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$. What is this temperature in kelvins?

- $236.15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $37\,\mathrm{K}$
- $273.15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $310.15\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The Celsius and Kelvin scales have different zero points, but an interval of one degree Celsius has the same size as one kelvin. Therefore add $273.15$ to the value in degrees Celsius to obtain the value in kelvins, giving
$$
T_{\mathrm{K}}=t_{\mathrm{C}}+273.15=37+273.15=310.15.
$$
The temperature is therefore $310.15\,\mathrm{K}$. The value $236.15\,\mathrm{K}$ comes from subtracting $37$ instead of adding it. The value $273.15\,\mathrm{K}$ corresponds to $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and comes from ignoring the given temperature.
