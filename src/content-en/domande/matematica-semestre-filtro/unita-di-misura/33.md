Convert $4\,\mu\mathrm{L}$ to $\mathrm{mL}$.

- $4$
- $4000$
- $0.004$
- $0.04$

## Explanation

The prefix micro denotes $10^{-6}$ and the prefix milli denotes $10^{-3}$, so one milliliter contains $10^{3}$ microliters. Since the milliliter is the larger unit, the numerical value decreases. Divide by one thousand, giving
$$
4\,\mu\mathrm{L}=4\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{mL}=0.004\,\mathrm{mL}.
$$
The value $4000$ comes from multiplying by one thousand, the factor for the reverse conversion. The value $0.04$ comes from using the factor $10^{-2}$ instead of $10^{-3}$.
