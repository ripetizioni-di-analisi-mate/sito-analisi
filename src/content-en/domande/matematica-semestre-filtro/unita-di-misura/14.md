In the relation $F=k\ell$, $F$ is the magnitude of a force and $\ell$ is an extension. Find the dimension of $k$, using $M$ for mass, $L$ for length, and $T$ for time.

- $MLT^{-2}$
- $MT^{-2}$
- $ML^2T^{-2}$
- $ML^{-1}T^{-2}$

## Explanation

From $F=k\ell$ we obtain $k=\dfrac{F}{\ell}$, so the dimension of $k$ is the ratio of the dimensions of $F$ and $\ell$. Force is mass times acceleration, so $[F]=MLT^{-2}$, while $[\ell]=L$. It follows that
$$
[k]=\frac{MLT^{-2}}{L}=MT^{-2}.
$$
The answer $MLT^{-2}$ is the dimension of force, before dividing by the extension. The answer $ML^2T^{-2}$ comes from multiplying by $L$ instead of dividing.
