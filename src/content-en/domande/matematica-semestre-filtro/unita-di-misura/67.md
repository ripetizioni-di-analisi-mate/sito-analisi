The specific volume, defined as volume divided by mass, is $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{cm^3/g}$. What is the density?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm^3/g}$
- $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $16\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

Specific volume is $\dfrac{V}{m}$ and density is $\dfrac{m}{V}$, so the quantities are reciprocals of each other. Take the reciprocal of both the numerical value and the unit, giving
$$
\rho=\frac{1}{\frac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{cm^3/g}}=4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}.
$$
The answer $4\,\mathrm{cm^3/g}$ comes from inverting the number while keeping the unit of specific volume. The answer $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ comes from inverting the unit while keeping the number. The value $16\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ comes from dividing $4$ by $\dfrac{1}{4}$.
