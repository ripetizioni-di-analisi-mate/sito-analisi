A force of $30\,\mathrm{N}$ acts perpendicularly to a surface of area $5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. What is the average pressure in pascals?

- $6\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $6\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $600\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $6\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

A pascal is one newton per square meter, so express the area in square meters before dividing. Since $1\,\mathrm{cm^2}=10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, the area is $5\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Therefore
$$
p=\frac{F}{A}=\frac{30\,\mathrm{N}}{5\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}}=6\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}.
$$
The value $6\,\mathrm{Pa}$ comes from dividing $30$ by $5$ without converting the area. The value $600\,\mathrm{Pa}$ comes from using the linear factor $10^{-2}$ instead of $10^{-4}$. The value $6\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm{Pa}$ comes from using the volume conversion factor, $10^{-6}$.
