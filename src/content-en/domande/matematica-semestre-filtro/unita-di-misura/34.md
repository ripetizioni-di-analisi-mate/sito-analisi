A flow rate is $3\,\mathrm{L/min}$. Express it in $\mathrm{mL/s}$.

- $3000$
- $50$
- $180$
- $0.05$

## Explanation

One liter equals $1000\,\mathrm{mL}$ and one minute equals $60\,\mathrm{s}$. Multiplying by both conversion factors cancels liters and minutes, so
$$
3\,\frac{\mathrm{L}}{\mathrm{min}}\cdot\frac{1000\,\mathrm{mL}}{1\,\mathrm{L}}\cdot\frac{1\,\mathrm{min}}{60\,\mathrm{s}}=\frac{3000}{60}\,\mathrm{mL/s}=50\,\mathrm{mL/s}.
$$
The value $3000$ comes from converting liters and overlooking the minutes in the denominator. The value $180$ comes from multiplying by $60$ instead of dividing. The value $0.05$ is the flow rate in liters per second.
