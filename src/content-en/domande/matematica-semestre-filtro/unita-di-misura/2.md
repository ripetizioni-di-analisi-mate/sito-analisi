Express $60\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ in square meters.

- $0.6\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.06\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.00006\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.006\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

One centimeter equals $10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}$. Since an area is the product of two lengths, the linear factor occurs twice, so $1\,\mathrm{cm^2}=(10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m})^2=10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Therefore
$$
60\,\mathrm{cm^2}=60\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.006\,\mathrm{m^2}.
$$
The value $0.6\,\mathrm{m^2}$ comes from using the linear factor $10^{-2}$ without squaring it. The value $0.00006\,\mathrm{m^2}$ comes from using the factor $10^{-6}$, which applies to volumes.
