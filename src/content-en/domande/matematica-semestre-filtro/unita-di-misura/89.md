One-quarter of the volume of a homogeneous solution containing dissolved solute is transferred to another container without evaporation or reaction. Which comparison of the two samples is correct?

- The one-quarter sample has three times the molar concentration of the rest.
- They have the same molar concentration but different amounts of solute.
- The one-quarter sample has one-third the molar concentration of the rest.
- They have the same amount of solute but different concentrations.

## Explanation

The solute in a homogeneous solution is distributed uniformly, so the portion transferred contains one-quarter of the volume and one-quarter of the moles. Concentration is the ratio $\dfrac{n}{V}$. The numerator and denominator decrease in the same proportion, so
$$
\frac{\frac{n}{4}}{\frac{V}{4}}=\frac{n}{V}.
$$
The two portions therefore have the same molar concentration. The amount of solute is extensive, so the portion transferred contains one-quarter of the total amount and the remaining portion contains three-quarters.
