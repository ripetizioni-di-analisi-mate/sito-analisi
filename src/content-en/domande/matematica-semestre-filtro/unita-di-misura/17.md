Convert $2\,\mathrm{mm^3}$ to $\mu\mathrm{L}$.

- $200$
- $2000$
- $2$
- $0.002$

## Explanation

A millimeter is one-tenth of a centimeter, so $1\,\mathrm{mm^3}=(10^{-1}\,\mathrm{cm})^3=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. We also have $1\,\mathrm{cm^3}=1\,\mathrm{mL}=10^{3}\,\mu\mathrm{L}$. Multiplying the two factors gives
$$
1\,\mathrm{mm^3}=10^{-3}\cdot10^{3}\,\mu\mathrm{L}=1\,\mu\mathrm{L},
$$
so $2\,\mathrm{mm^3}=2\,\mu\mathrm{L}$. The value $2000$ comes from confusing a cubic millimeter with a milliliter. The value $0.002$ comes from converting to milliliters and stopping there.
