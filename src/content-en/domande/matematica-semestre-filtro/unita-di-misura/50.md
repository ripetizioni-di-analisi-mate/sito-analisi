Which conversion factor equal to one directly converts $x\,\mathrm{mm}$ to meters and cancels millimeters in the product?

- $\dfrac{1000\,\mathrm{mm}}{1\,\mathrm{m}}$
- $\dfrac{1\,\mathrm{m}}{100\,\mathrm{mm}}$
- $\dfrac{1\,\mathrm{m}}{1000\,\mathrm{mm}}$
- $\dfrac{1000\,\mathrm{m}}{1\,\mathrm{mm}}$

## Explanation

A conversion factor is the ratio of two expressions for the same length, so it equals $1$ and leaves the measurement unchanged. Since $1\,\mathrm{m}=1000\,\mathrm{mm}$, the possible factors are $\dfrac{1\,\mathrm{m}}{1000\,\mathrm{mm}}$ and its reciprocal. The millimeters in the measurement cancel if millimeters are in the denominator, so the required factor is $\dfrac{1\,\mathrm{m}}{1000\,\mathrm{mm}}$. The factor $\dfrac{1\,\mathrm{m}}{100\,\mathrm{mm}}$ differs from $1$ because $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ make one decimeter. The factor $\dfrac{1000\,\mathrm{mm}}{1\,\mathrm{m}}$ gives a result in $\mathrm{mm^2/m}$.
