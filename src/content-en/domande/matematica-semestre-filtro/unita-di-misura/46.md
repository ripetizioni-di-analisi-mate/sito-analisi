Average pressure is the magnitude of the normal force on a surface divided by the area over which it acts. If mass, length, and time are denoted by $M,L,T$, what is the dimension of pressure?

- $MLT^{-2}$
- $ML^{-2}T^{-2}$
- $ML^3T^{-2}$
- $ML^{-1}T^{-2}$

## Explanation

Average pressure is the magnitude of the normal force on a surface divided by the area over which it acts. Force has dimension $MLT^{-2}$ because it is mass times acceleration, and area has dimension $L^2$. The exponents of length are subtracted in the quotient, so
$$
[p]=\frac{MLT^{-2}}{L^{2}}=ML^{-1}T^{-2}.
$$
The answer $MLT^{-2}$ is the dimension of force, before dividing by area. The answer $ML^{3}T^{-2}$ comes from multiplying by area instead of dividing. In SI units the same dimension corresponds to $\mathrm{kg\,m^{-1}\,s^{-2}}$, or the pascal.
