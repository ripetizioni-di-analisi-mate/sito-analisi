Two additive volumes, $0.80\,\mathrm{L}$ and $250\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, are combined. What is the total volume in liters?

- $1.05\,\mathrm{L}$
- $0.825\,\mathrm{L}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{L}$
- $3.30\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

Add the two volumes after expressing them in the same unit. Since $1\,\mathrm{cm^3}=1\,\mathrm{mL}=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{L}$, we have $250\,\mathrm{cm^3}=0.250\,\mathrm{L}$. Therefore the total volume is
$$
0.80\,\mathrm{L}+0.250\,\mathrm{L}=1.05\,\mathrm{L}.
$$
The value $0.825\,\mathrm{L}$ comes from converting $250\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ to $0.025\,\mathrm{L}$ with an extra factor of ten. The value $3.30\,\mathrm{L}$ comes from reading $250\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ as $2.50\,\mathrm{L}$.
