A cube has edge length $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the ratio of its total surface area to its volume, expressed in $\mathrm{m^{-1}}$?

- $300\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $0.03\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $3\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The total surface consists of six square faces, so $S=6\cdot2^2\,\mathrm{cm^2}=24\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, and the volume is $V=2^3\,\mathrm{cm^3}=8\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Their ratio is $\dfrac{24\,\mathrm{cm^2}}{8\,\mathrm{cm^3}}=3\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{cm}=10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}$, we have
$$
1\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}=\frac{1}{10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}}=10^{2}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}},
$$
so the ratio is $300\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. The value $0.03\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$ comes from multiplying by $10^{-2}$, as if centimeters were in the numerator. The value $3\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$ comes from using the area conversion factor.
