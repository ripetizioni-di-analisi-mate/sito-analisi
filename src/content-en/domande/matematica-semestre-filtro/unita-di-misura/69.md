Two samples of different materials have the same positive mass but different volumes. Which has the greater density?

- The one occupying the larger volume.
- They have the same density because they have the same mass.
- The one whose volume is expressed in the unit giving the larger numerical value.
- The one occupying the smaller volume.

## Explanation

Density is $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}$. With the same positive mass in the numerator, the quotient is larger when its denominator is smaller, so the sample occupying the smaller volume has the greater density. For example, $10\,\mathrm{g}$ in $2\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ gives $5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$, while $10\,\mathrm{g}$ in $5\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ gives $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. The comparison concerns physical volumes, so their numerical values can be compared only when both volumes are expressed in the same unit.
