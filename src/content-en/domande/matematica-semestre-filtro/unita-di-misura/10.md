In $12\,\mathrm{s}$, a volume of $180\,\mathrm{mL}$ of liquid is collected. Calculate the average volumetric flow rate in cubic meters per second.

- $1.5\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $1.5\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $1.5\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $2.16\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$

## Explanation

One milliliter equals $10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, so $180\,\mathrm{mL}=1.8\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. The average flow rate is the volume collected divided by the collection time, so
$$
Q=\frac{1.8\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}}{12\,\mathrm{s}}=1.5\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}.
$$
The value $1.5\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ comes from treating a milliliter as one thousandth of a cubic meter. The value $2.16\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ comes from taking the product of volume and time instead of their quotient.
