Two rulers show the same segment as “2.5” and “25”, with their units hidden. If the first ruler is marked in centimetres, what unit must the second use?

- Millimetre
- Metre
- Decimetre
- Micrometre

## Explanation

The numerical reading is ten times larger for the same length, so the unit is ten times smaller. One tenth of a centimetre is one millimetre.
