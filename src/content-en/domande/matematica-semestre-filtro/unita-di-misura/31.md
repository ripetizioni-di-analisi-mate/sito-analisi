A sheet of printer paper is $0.08\,\mathrm{mm}$ thick. What is its thickness in micrometers?

- $80\,\mu\mathrm m$
- $8\,\mu\mathrm m$
- $800\,\mu\mathrm m$
- $0.00008\,\mu\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The prefix milli denotes $10^{-3}$ and the prefix micro denotes $10^{-6}$, so one millimeter contains $\dfrac{10^{-3}}{10^{-6}}=10^{3}$ micrometers. Therefore
$$
0.08\,\mathrm{mm}=0.08\cdot10^{3}\,\mu\mathrm m=80\,\mu\mathrm m.
$$
The micrometer is smaller than the millimeter, so the numerical value increases. The value $8\,\mu\mathrm m$ comes from using the factor $100$, as if a micrometer were one hundredth of a millimeter, and $800\,\mu\mathrm m$ comes from using the factor $10^4$. The value $0.00008\,\mu\mathrm m$ comes from dividing by one thousand instead of multiplying.
