Express $400\,\mathrm{mL}$ in cubic meters.

- $0.0004\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.004\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.000004\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

One milliliter equals one cubic centimeter. Since $1\,\mathrm{cm}=10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}$, the volume conversion factor is the cube of the linear factor, so $1\,\mathrm{mL}=1\,\mathrm{cm^3}=10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Therefore
$$
400\,\mathrm{mL}=400\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3}=0.0004\,\mathrm{m^3}.
$$
The value $0.4\,\mathrm{m^3}$ comes from treating a milliliter as one thousandth of a cubic meter, which confuses the liter with the cubic meter.
