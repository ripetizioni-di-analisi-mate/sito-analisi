Express $125\,\mathrm{mm}$ in meters.

- $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.125\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.0125\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The prefix milli denotes $10^{-3}$, so one millimeter is one thousandth of a meter. Therefore multiply the number of millimeters by $10^{-3}$ to obtain
$$
125\,\mathrm{mm}=125\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}=0.125\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $12.5\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from dividing by $10$ instead of $1000$. The value $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from dividing by $100$, which treats millimeters as centimeters.
