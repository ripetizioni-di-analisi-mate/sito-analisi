A linear axis runs from $10^3$ to $10^4$. What number divides this segment into two equal lengths?

- $10^{3.5}$
- $5\cdot10^3$
- $5.5\cdot10^3$
- $9\cdot10^3$

## Explanation

Equal lengths on a linear axis represent equal differences. The midpoint is $(1000+10000)/2=5500$. Averaging only the exponents would give the midpoint on a logarithmic scale, which uses a different spacing.
