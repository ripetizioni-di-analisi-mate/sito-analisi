Calculate $10^4-10^3$.

- $10$
- $10^{3}$
- $9\cdot10^{3}$
- $-9\cdot10^{4}$

## Explanation

The exponent rules apply to products and quotients, so evaluate the difference by factoring out a common factor. Since $10^4=10\cdot10^3$, factor out $10^3$.
$$
10^4-10^3=10^3(10-1)=9\cdot10^{3}.
$$
A direct check gives $10\,000-1000=9000$. The value $10$ comes from subtracting the exponents as in a quotient. The value $-9\cdot10^4$ comes from factoring out $10^4$ but writing $10^4(1-10)$ instead of $10^4\left(1-10^{-1}\right)$.
