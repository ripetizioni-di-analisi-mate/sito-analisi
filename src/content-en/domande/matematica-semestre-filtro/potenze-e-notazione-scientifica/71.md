For real $x$, which expression equals $\sqrt{x^6}$?

- $|x|^3$
- $x^3$
- $x^4$
- $-x^3$

## Explanation

The radicand $x^6$ is the square of $x^3$ because $\left(x^3\right)^2=x^6$. Since $\sqrt{a^2}=|a|$ for every real $a$, we have
$$
\sqrt{x^6}=\sqrt{\left(x^3\right)^2}=\left|x^3\right|=|x|^3.
$$
The absolute value is needed because the square root is nonnegative, whereas $x^3$ is negative for $x<0$. For example, when $x=-1$ the result is $\sqrt{1}=1$, which differs from $(-1)^3=-1$. The expression $x^4$ comes from subtracting $2$ from the exponent instead of dividing it by $2$.
