Calculate $5^0+(-2)^0$.

- $0$
- $1$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

For any nonzero base, $a^0=1$. Both bases $5$ and $-2$ are nonzero, so $5^0=1$ and $(-2)^0=1$.
$$
5^0+(-2)^0=1+1=2.
$$
The convention is independent of the sign of the base. The value $0$ comes from writing $(-2)^0=-1$, assigning the sign of the base to the power. The value $3$ comes from adding the bases $5$ and $-2$.
