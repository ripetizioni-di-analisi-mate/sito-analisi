A total of $9^4$ items are packed into groups of $27^2$. Without writing the numbers out, which line correctly finds the number of complete groups?

- $3^{8-6}=9$
- $3^{8/6}=3^{4/3}$
- $3^{4+2}=729$
- $3^{8+6}=3^{14}$

## Explanation

Write both quantities as powers of three. The total is $(3^2)^4=3^8$ and the group size is $(3^3)^2=3^6$. Their quotient is $3^{8-6}=9$. Subtracting exponents attached to different bases is an invalid shortcut.
