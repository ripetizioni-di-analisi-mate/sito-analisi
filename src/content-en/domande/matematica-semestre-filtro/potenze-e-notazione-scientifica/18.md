Calculate $\dfrac{9^4}{27^2}$.

- $9$
- $27$
- $1$
- $\dfrac19$

## Explanation

The bases $9$ and $27$ are powers of $3$, so use the rule for a power of a power to rewrite everything in base $3$.
$$
\frac{9^4}{27^2}=\frac{\left(3^2\right)^4}{\left(3^3\right)^2}=\frac{3^{8}}{3^{6}}=3^{2}=9.
$$
The value $27$ comes from writing $\left(3^3\right)^2=3^{5}$, adding the exponents instead of multiplying them. The value $1$ comes from making the same mistake only in the numerator, giving $3^6$, while calculating the denominator $3^6$ correctly. The value $\dfrac19$ comes from dividing the bases and subtracting the exponents, $\left(\dfrac{9}{27}\right)^{4-2}$.
