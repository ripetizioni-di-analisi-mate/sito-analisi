Calculate $\sqrt{(-7)^2}$.

- $-7$
- $49$
- $-49$
- $7$

## Explanation

The square is $(-7)^2=(-7)(-7)=49$, so $\sqrt{(-7)^2}=\sqrt{49}$. By definition, the square root of $49$ is the nonnegative number whose square is $49$, so it equals $7$. In general, $\sqrt{a^2}=|a|$, and here $|-7|=7$. The value $-7$ comes from canceling the square and square root without taking the absolute value. The value $49$ comes from omitting the root.
