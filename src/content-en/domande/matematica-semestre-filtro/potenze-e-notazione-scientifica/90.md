For $a\ne0$, simplify $a^{-2}\cdot(a^3)^2$.

- $a^8$
- $a^{-8}$
- $a^4$
- $1$

## Explanation

When raising a power to a power, multiply the exponents, giving $(a^3)^2=a^{3\cdot2}=a^6$. The two powers now have the same base $a\ne0$, so add their exponents in the product.
$$
a^{-2}\cdot(a^3)^2=a^{-2}\cdot a^{6}=a^{-2+6}=a^{4}.
$$
The value $a^{8}$ comes from subtracting the exponent $-2$ instead of adding it. The value $a^{-8}$ comes from applying the reciprocal to the entire product, that is, calculating $\left(a^{2}\cdot a^{6}\right)^{-1}$.
