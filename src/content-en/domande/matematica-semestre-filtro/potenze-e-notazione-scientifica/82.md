The density of aluminum is $2.7\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Express it in $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.

- $2.7\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2.7\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2.7\cdot10^{-1}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2.7\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Convert the two units separately. We have $1\,\mathrm{g}=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg}$. Also, $1\,\mathrm{cm^3}=\left(10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}\right)^3=10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ because the length conversion factor is cubed.
$$
2.7\,\frac{\mathrm{g}}{\mathrm{cm^3}}=2.7\cdot\frac{10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg}}{10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3}}=2.7\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.
$$
The value $2.7\cdot10^{-3}$ comes from converting grams while leaving the volume unchanged. The value $2.7\cdot10^{6}$ comes from converting the volume while keeping grams. The value $2.7\cdot10^{-1}$ comes from using the factor $10^{-2}$ without cubing it.
