Let $0<x<1$. Which ordering of $x$, $x^2$, and $\sqrt{x}$ is correct?

- $\sqrt{x}<x<x^2$
- $x^2<x<\sqrt{x}$
- $x<x^2<\sqrt{x}$
- $x^2<\sqrt{x}<x$

## Explanation

Multiplying the inequality $x<1$ by the positive number $x$ gives $x^2<x$. Now set $y=\sqrt{x}$. Since $0<x<1$, we also have $0<y<1$, so by the same reasoning $y^2<y$, that is, $x<\sqrt{x}$. It follows that $x^2<x<\sqrt{x}$. For example, when $x=0.25$ we have $x^2=0.0625$ and $\sqrt{x}=0.5$. The ordering $\sqrt{x}<x<x^2$ holds for $x>1$.
