Calculate $\left(\dfrac25\right)^{-2}$.

- $\dfrac4{25}$
- $-\dfrac4{25}$
- $\dfrac52$
- $\dfrac{25}{4}$

## Explanation

The negative exponent indicates a reciprocal, and the reciprocal of $\dfrac25$ is $\dfrac52$. Therefore, square the reciprocal.
$$
\left(\frac25\right)^{-2}=\left(\frac52\right)^{2}=\frac{25}{4}.
$$
The value $\dfrac{4}{25}$ comes from ignoring the sign of the exponent. The value $-\dfrac4{25}$ comes from treating the negative exponent as a minus sign in front of the power. The value $\dfrac52$ comes from taking the reciprocal without squaring.
