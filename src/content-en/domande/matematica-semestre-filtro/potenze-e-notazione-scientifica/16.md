Which exponent $n$ makes $\left(10^9\right)^3=10^n$ true?

- $28$
- $12$
- $19683$
- $27$

## Explanation

The power $\left(10^9\right)^3$ is the product of three factors equal to $10^9$, so multiply the exponents when raising the power to a power.
$$
\left(10^9\right)^3=10^9\cdot10^9\cdot10^9=10^{9+9+9}=10^{27}.
$$
Therefore $n=27$. The value $12$ comes from adding $9$ and $3$ as in the product of two powers. The value $19683=3^9$ comes from raising one exponent to the power of the other instead of multiplying them.
