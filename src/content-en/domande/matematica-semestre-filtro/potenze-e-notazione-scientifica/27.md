To take the cube root of $2.7\cdot10^{-5}$ by hand, you want an exponent divisible by three. Which rewrite preserves the number and achieves that goal?

- $27\cdot10^{-4}$
- $0.27\cdot10^{-6}$
- $270\cdot10^{-8}$
- $27\cdot10^{-6}$

## Explanation

Multiplying the coefficient by ten requires decreasing the exponent by one. This gives $27\cdot10^{-6}$. Now both $27$ and $10^{-6}$ are easy to recognize as cubes, and the cube root is $3\cdot10^{-2}$.
