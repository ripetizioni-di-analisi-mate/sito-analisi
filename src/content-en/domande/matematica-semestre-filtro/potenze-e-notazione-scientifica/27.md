Calculate $\sqrt[3]{2.7\cdot10^{-5}}$ and give the result in scientific notation.

- $3\cdot10^{-6}$
- $3\cdot10^{-3}$
- $9\cdot10^{-2}$
- $3\cdot10^{-2}$

## Explanation

The exponent $-5$ is not a multiple of the index $3$, so transfer a factor of $10$ from the power of ten to the coefficient and write $2.7\cdot10^{-5}=27\cdot10^{-6}$. Now $27=3^3$ and $-6$ is a multiple of $3$.
$$
\sqrt[3]{27\cdot10^{-6}}=\sqrt[3]{27}\cdot10^{\frac{-6}{3}}=3\cdot10^{-2}.
$$
The check gives $\left(3\cdot10^{-2}\right)^3=27\cdot10^{-6}$. The value $3\cdot10^{-3}$ comes from dividing the exponent by $2$ as for a square root. The value $9\cdot10^{-2}$ comes from dividing $27$ by $3$ instead of taking its cube root.
