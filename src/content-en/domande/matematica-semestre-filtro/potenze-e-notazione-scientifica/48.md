An object of mass $m=2.0\cdot10^{-1}\,\mathrm{kg}$ has kinetic energy $E_c=4.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{J}$. Use $E_c=\dfrac12mv^2$ to find its speed $v$.

- $4.0\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.0\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.4\cdot10^{-1}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.0\cdot10^{-1}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Isolate the square of the speed, $v^2=\dfrac{2E_c}{m}$, and calculate it by working with the coefficients and exponents separately.
$$
v^2=\frac{2\cdot4.0\cdot10^{-3}}{2.0\cdot10^{-1}}\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}=4.0\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}.
$$
The exponent $-2$ is even, so $v=\sqrt{4.0}\cdot10^{-1}\,\mathrm{m/s}=2.0\cdot10^{-1}\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $4.0\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is $v^2$ before taking the root. The value $2.0\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from taking only the root of the coefficient. The value $1.4\cdot10^{-1}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from omitting the factor $2$.
