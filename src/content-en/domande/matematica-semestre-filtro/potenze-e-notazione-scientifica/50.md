Calculate $\sqrt[5]{-32}$.

- $2$
- $-2$
- $-4$
- $-8$

## Explanation

The index $5$ is odd, so every real number has a unique real fifth root with the same sign as the radicand. We seek the number whose fifth power is $-32$. Since $2^5=32$ and the exponent is odd, $(-2)^5=-32$. Thus $\sqrt[5]{-32}=-2$. The value $2$ comes from losing the sign of the radicand as if the index were even. The value $-8$ comes from dividing $-32$ by $4$ instead of taking the root.
