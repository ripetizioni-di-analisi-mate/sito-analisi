A model produces the measurement $z=x^3$. The reading is negative. What does this imply about the real value of $x$?

- There is exactly one $x$, and it is positive.
- There is exactly one $x$, and it is negative.
- There are two opposite values of $x$.
- No real $x$ produces the reading.

## Explanation

The cube function is strictly increasing on the whole real line and preserves the sign. Hence $x=\sqrt[3]{z}$ is unique and negative when $z<0$. The restriction on negative radicands applies to roots of even index.
