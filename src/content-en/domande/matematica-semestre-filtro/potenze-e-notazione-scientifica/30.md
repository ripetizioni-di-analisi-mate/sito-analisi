Which number is greatest?

- $10^{10}$
- $5^{20}$
- $4^{30}$
- $3^{40}$

## Explanation

The exponents $10$, $20$, $30$, and $40$ are multiples of $10$, so the rule for a power of a power lets us write each number with exponent $10$.
$$
3^{40}=\left(3^4\right)^{10}=81^{10},\qquad4^{30}=\left(4^3\right)^{10}=64^{10},\qquad5^{20}=\left(5^2\right)^{10}=25^{10}.
$$
For positive bases raised to the same positive exponent, the larger base gives the larger power. Since $81>64>25>10$, the greatest number is $3^{40}$. Choosing $10^{10}$ comes from comparing the bases before making the exponents equal.
