The measurements $10.004$ and $10.001$ are rounded to three significant figures before their difference is calculated. How does the difference of the rounded values compare with the difference of the exact values?

- The difference of the rounded values is $0.01$, while the exact one is zero.
- The difference of the rounded values is $0.1$, while the exact one is $0.03$.
- The difference of the rounded values is zero, while the exact one is $0.003$.
- Both differences equal $0.003$.

## Explanation

Three significant figures keep the digits down to the tenths, and in both measurements the first discarded digit is zero. Hence both measurements round to $10.0$, and the difference of the rounded values is zero. The exact values give instead $10.004-10.001=0.003$. Rounding before the subtraction therefore cancels the whole difference, and this is why intermediate digits are kept until the final result.
