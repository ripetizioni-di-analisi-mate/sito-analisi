A display keeps three significant figures and rounds each reading before subtraction. The exact readings are $10.004$ and $10.001$. How does the display difference compare with the exact difference?

- The display gives $0.01$, while the exact difference is zero.
- The display gives $0.1$, while the exact difference is $0.03$.
- The display difference is zero, while the exact difference is $0.003$.
- Both differences equal $0.003$.

## Explanation

Both readings are displayed as $10.0$ to three significant figures, so subtracting them loses the difference. The exact data give $10.004-10.001=0.003$. A displayed zero does not establish that the original readings were identical.
