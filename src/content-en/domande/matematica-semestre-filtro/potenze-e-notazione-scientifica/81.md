Calculate $\sqrt{\dfrac{49}{81}}$.

- $\dfrac79$
- $\dfrac{49}{9}$
- $\dfrac97$
- $\dfrac7{81}$

## Explanation

The numerator and denominator are positive, so the root of the quotient is the quotient of the roots.
$$
\sqrt{\frac{49}{81}}=\frac{\sqrt{49}}{\sqrt{81}}=\frac79.
$$
A check gives $\left(\dfrac79\right)^2=\dfrac{49}{81}$. The value $\dfrac{7}{81}$ comes from taking the root only of the numerator. The value $\dfrac{49}{9}$ comes from taking the root only of the denominator. The value $\dfrac97$ comes from inverting the fraction as if the exponent were negative.
