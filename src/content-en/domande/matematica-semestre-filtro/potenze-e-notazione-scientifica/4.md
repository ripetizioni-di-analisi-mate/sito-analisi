Calculate $(4\cdot10^4)(5\cdot10^{-2})$ and give the result in scientific notation.

- $2\cdot10^3$
- $2\cdot10^2$
- $2\cdot10^6$
- $2\cdot10^{-3}$

## Explanation

To multiply, multiply the coefficients and add the exponents.
$$
(4\cdot10^4)(5\cdot10^{-2})=20\cdot10^{4-2}=20\cdot10^{2}.
$$
The coefficient $20$ exceeds $10$. Since $20=2\cdot10$, move the factor $10$ into the power of ten. The exponent increases from $2$ to $3$, so the product is $2\cdot10^3$. The value $2\cdot10^2$ comes from writing $2$ in place of $20$ without adjusting the exponent.
