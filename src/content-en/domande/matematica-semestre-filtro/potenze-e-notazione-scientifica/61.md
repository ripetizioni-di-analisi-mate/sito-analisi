Which interval contains $\sqrt{45}$?

- $(5,6)$
- $(6,7)$
- $(7,8)$
- $(8,9)$

## Explanation

Find the consecutive perfect squares between which $45$ lies. Since $6^2=36$ and $7^2=49$, we have $36<45<49$. The square root function is increasing on the nonnegative numbers, so taking square roots preserves the inequalities.
$$
6=\sqrt{36}<\sqrt{45}<\sqrt{49}=7.
$$
Thus $\sqrt{45}$ lies between $6$ and $7$ and is approximately $6.7$. The interval $(5,6)$ is ruled out because $6^2=36<45$. The intervals $(7,8)$ and $(8,9)$ are ruled out because $7^2=49>45$.
