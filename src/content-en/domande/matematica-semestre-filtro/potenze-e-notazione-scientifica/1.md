Calculate $10^{-4}\cdot10^{7}$.

- $10^{-11}$
- $10^{3}$
- $10^{-28}$
- $10^{-3}$

## Explanation

The two powers have the same base $10$, so use the product rule and add the exponents, retaining their signs.
$$
10^{-4}\cdot10^{7}=10^{-4+7}=10^{3}.
$$
The value $10^{-28}$ comes from multiplying the exponents instead of adding them. The value $10^{-11}$ comes from reversing the sign of $7$, that is, calculating $-4-7$.
