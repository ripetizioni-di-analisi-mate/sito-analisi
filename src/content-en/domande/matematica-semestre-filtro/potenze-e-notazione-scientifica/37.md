A model defines $y=-x^2$. The negative reading $x=-3$ is substituted. Which line follows the formula and the order of operations?

- $y=(-(-3))^2=9$
- $y=-(-3)^2=9$
- $y=-(-3)^2=-6$
- $y=-(-3)^2=-9$

## Explanation

Substitute $-3$ in parentheses because it replaces the entire base $x$. In $y=-x^2$ the minus sign outside the power multiplies its result by negative one, so calculate the square first.
$$
y=-(-3)^2=-9.
$$
The value $9$ comes from also squaring the formula's external minus sign. The value $-6$ comes from confusing the square $(-3)^2$ with twice the value, $2\cdot(-3)$.
