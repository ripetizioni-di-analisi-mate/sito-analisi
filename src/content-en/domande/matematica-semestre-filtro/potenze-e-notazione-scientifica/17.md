A model requires the square of $2\cdot10^3$. One line of the calculation gives $\left(2\cdot10^3\right)^2=2\cdot10^6$. What correction is needed?

- Double the base ten as well.
- Add two to the exponent of ten.
- Square the coefficient two as well.
- Reverse the sign of the exponent of ten.

## Explanation

The power of a product is the product of the powers of its factors, so the exponent $2$ applies both to the coefficient $2$ and to $10^3$.
$$
\left(2\cdot10^3\right)^2=2^2\cdot\left(10^3\right)^2=4\cdot10^6.
$$
The line in the model squares only the power of ten, so replace the coefficient $2$ with $2^2=4$. The exponent $6$ is already correct, so changing the exponent or the base ten would be incorrect.
