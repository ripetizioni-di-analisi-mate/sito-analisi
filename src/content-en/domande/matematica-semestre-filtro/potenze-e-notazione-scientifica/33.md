An archive has $2^3$ shelves, each with $5^3$ compartments. Why can the total number of compartments be written as a power of ten?

- The bases add, giving a total of $(2+5)^3$.
- The factors have the same exponent, so the total is $(2\cdot5)^3$.
- The number of shelves must be added to the number of compartments on one shelf.
- The exponents must be added to obtain $10^6$.

## Explanation

Each shelf has the same number of compartments, so the total is $2^3\cdot5^3$. Pair each of the three factors of two with one of the three factors of five. The result has three factors of ten and equals $10^3$ compartments.
