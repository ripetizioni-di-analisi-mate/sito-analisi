Calculate $\dfrac{2^3\cdot5^3}{10^2}$.

- $1$
- $10$
- $100$
- $50$

## Explanation

The two factors in the numerator have the same exponent $3$, so apply the rule for the power of a product, $a^3b^3=(ab)^3$.
$$
\frac{2^3\cdot5^3}{10^2}=\frac{(2\cdot5)^3}{10^2}=\frac{10^3}{10^2}=10^{3-2}=10.
$$
A direct check gives $\dfrac{8\cdot125}{100}=\dfrac{1000}{100}=10$. The value $100$ comes from dividing $1000$ by $10$ instead of by $10^2$. The value $1$ comes from treating the numerator and denominator as the same power.
