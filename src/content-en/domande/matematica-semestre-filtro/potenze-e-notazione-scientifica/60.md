Calculate $(-2)^{-3}$.

- $-\dfrac18$
- $-8$
- $\dfrac18$
- $-\dfrac16$

## Explanation

The base $-2$ is nonzero, so by definition $(-2)^{-3}=\dfrac{1}{(-2)^3}$. The exponent $3$ is odd, so $(-2)^3=-8$.
$$
(-2)^{-3}=\frac{1}{-8}=-\frac18.
$$
The minus sign in the exponent indicates a reciprocal and leaves the sign of the base unchanged. The value $-8$ comes from forgetting the reciprocal. The value $\dfrac18$ comes from losing the sign of the negative base. The value $-\dfrac16$ comes from confusing $(-2)^3$ with $3\cdot(-2)$.
