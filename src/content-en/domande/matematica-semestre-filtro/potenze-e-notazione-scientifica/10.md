In the ratio $\dfrac{6\cdot10^{-8}}{2.4\cdot10^{-5}}$, the numerator has the larger coefficient but the smaller exponent. What is the quotient in scientific notation?

- $2.5\cdot10^{-3}$
- $2.5\cdot10^{-13}$
- $2.5\cdot10^3$
- $4\cdot10^{-4}$

## Explanation

To divide, divide the coefficients and subtract the exponents.
$$
\frac{6\cdot10^{-8}}{2.4\cdot10^{-5}}=\frac{6}{2.4}\cdot10^{-8-(-5)}=2.5\cdot10^{-3}.
$$
The coefficient $2.5$ lies between $1$ and $10$, so the result is already normalized. The value $2.5\cdot10^{-13}$ comes from adding the exponents. The value $4\cdot10^{-4}$ comes from dividing $2.4$ by $6$, reversing the quotient of the coefficients.
