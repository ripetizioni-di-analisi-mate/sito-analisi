Which comparison of $2^{-5}$ and $5^{-2}$ is correct?

- $2^{-5}>5^{-2}$
- $2^{-5}=5^{-2}$
- $2^{-5}$ is negative and $5^{-2}$ is positive.
- $2^{-5}<5^{-2}$

## Explanation

By definition, $2^{-5}=\dfrac{1}{2^5}=\dfrac{1}{32}$ and $5^{-2}=\dfrac{1}{5^2}=\dfrac{1}{25}$. Both numbers are positive because a negative exponent takes the reciprocal without changing the sign. Of two fractions with the same positive numerator, the one with the larger positive denominator is smaller. Since $32>25$, we have $2^{-5}<5^{-2}$. The opposite comparison comes from comparing $2^5$ and $5^2$ without taking their reciprocals.
