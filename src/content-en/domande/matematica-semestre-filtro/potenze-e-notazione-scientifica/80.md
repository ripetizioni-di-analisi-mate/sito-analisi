A data table replaces each positive reading $x$ by $x^{-2}$. If the original rows are in increasing order of $x$ and all readings differ, in what order do the transformed values appear?

- Decreasing only for $x<1$ and increasing for $x>1$.
- In strictly increasing order.
- All equal up to $x=1$, then increasing.
- In strictly decreasing order.

## Explanation

If $0<a<b$, then $a^2<b^2$. Taking positive reciprocals reverses the order, giving $1/a^2>1/b^2$. The same argument applies when the two values lie on opposite sides of one.
