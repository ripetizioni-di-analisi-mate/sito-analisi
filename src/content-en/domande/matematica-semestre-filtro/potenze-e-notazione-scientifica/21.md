Calculate $16^{0.75}$.

- $12$
- $8$
- $2$
- $64$

## Explanation

Write the decimal exponent as a fraction in lowest terms, $0.75=\dfrac{75}{100}=\dfrac34$. The base $16$ is positive, so $x^{\frac{p}{q}}=\left(\sqrt[q]{x}\right)^p$ holds with $p=3$ and $q=4$. Since $2^4=16$, the fourth root of $16$ is $2$.
$$
16^{0.75}=16^{\frac34}=\left(\sqrt[4]{16}\right)^3=2^3=8.
$$
The value $12$ comes from multiplying the base by the exponent, $16\cdot0.75$. The value $2$ comes from taking only the fourth root. The value $64$ comes from taking the square root instead of the fourth root, $\left(\sqrt{16}\right)^3$.
