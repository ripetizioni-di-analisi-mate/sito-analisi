Without calculating its exact value, determine which interval contains $\dfrac{7.6\cdot10^{8}}{3.9\cdot10^{-2}}$.

- Between $10^{6}$ and $10^{7}$
- Between $10^{9}$ and $10^{10}$
- Between $10^{10}$ and $10^{11}$
- Between $10^{-11}$ and $10^{-10}$

## Explanation

Since $3.9<7.6<39$, the quotient of the coefficients lies between $1$ and $10$ and is about $\dfrac{8}{4}=2$. The exponent of the quotient is $8-(-2)=10$.
$$
\frac{7.6\cdot10^{8}}{3.9\cdot10^{-2}}\approx2\cdot10^{10}.
$$
The result therefore lies between $10^{10}$ and $10^{11}$. The interval from $10^{6}$ to $10^{7}$ comes from calculating $8-2$, ignoring the sign of the denominator's exponent. The interval from $10^{-11}$ to $10^{-10}$ corresponds to the reciprocal of the quotient.
