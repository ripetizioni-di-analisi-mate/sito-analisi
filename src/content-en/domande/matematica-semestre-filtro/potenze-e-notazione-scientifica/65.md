Which expression equals $(-3x)^2$ for every real $x$?

- $-9x^2$
- $3x^2$
- $9x^2$
- $9x$

## Explanation

The power of a product is the product of the powers, so the exponent $2$ applies to both factors $-3$ and $x$.
$$
(-3x)^2=(-3)^2\cdot x^2=9x^2.
$$
The factor $(-3)^2$ is positive because the exponent is even. The value $-9x^2$ comes from squaring $3$ while leaving the minus sign outside, as in $-3^2$. The value $3x^2$ comes from squaring only $x$. The value $9x$ comes from squaring only the coefficient.
