Let $x=a\cdot10^{5}$ with $1\le a<10$. For which values of $a$ is the closest power of ten to $x$ on a multiplicative scale equal to $10^{6}$?

- $1\le a<10$
- $5\le a<10$
- $\sqrt{10}<a<10$
- $5.5\le a<10$

## Explanation

The number $x$ lies between $10^5$ and $10^6$. The factor separating $10^5$ from $x$ is $a$, and the factor separating $x$ from $10^6$ is $\dfrac{10}{a}$. The power $10^6$ is closer when the second factor is smaller than the first. Since $a>0$, we have
$$
\frac{10}{a}<a\iff a^2>10\iff a>\sqrt{10}\approx3.16.
$$
The interval $5.5\le a<10$ comes from comparing differences, using the midpoint between $1$ and $10$ on an additive scale.
