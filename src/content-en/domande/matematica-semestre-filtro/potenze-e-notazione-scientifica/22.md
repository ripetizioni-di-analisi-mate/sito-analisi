Round $6047$ to the nearest ten.

- $6000$
- $6040$
- $6050$
- $6047$

## Explanation

The number $6047$ lies between the multiples of ten $6040$ and $6050$. The distances are $6047-6040=7$ and $6050-6047=3$. Since $3<7$, the nearest multiple of ten is $6050$. The ones digit is $7$, greater than $5$, so the rounding rule also increases the tens digit from $4$ to $5$. The value $6040$ comes from truncating the last digit instead of rounding.
