Calculate the geometric mean $\sqrt{ab}$ of $a=2\cdot10^{-3}$ and $b=8\cdot10^{-5}$.

- $4\cdot10^{-8}$
- $4\cdot10^{-4}$
- $1.6\cdot10^{-7}$
- $1.04\cdot10^{-3}$

## Explanation

In the product, multiply the coefficients and add the exponents, giving $ab=16\cdot10^{-8}$. The coefficient $16$ is a perfect square and the exponent $-8$ is even, so take the square roots separately.
$$
\sqrt{ab}=\sqrt{16\cdot10^{-8}}=\sqrt{16}\cdot10^{-4}=4\cdot10^{-4}.
$$
The value $4\cdot10^{-8}$ comes from taking the root of the coefficient alone. The value $1.6\cdot10^{-7}$ is the normalized product $ab$ before taking the root. The value $1.04\cdot10^{-3}$ is the arithmetic mean $\dfrac{a+b}{2}$.
