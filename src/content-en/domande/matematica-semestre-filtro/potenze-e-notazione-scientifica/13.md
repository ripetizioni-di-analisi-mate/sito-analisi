Determine the order of magnitude of $6.4\cdot10^{-6}$ by choosing the nearest power of ten on a multiplicative scale.

- $10^{-6}$
- $10^{-5}$
- $10^{-7}$
- $10^{-4}$

## Explanation

For normalized $x=a\cdot10^n$, the factor between $10^n$ and $x$ is $a$, and the factor between $x$ and $10^{n+1}$ is $\dfrac{10}{a}$. The closer power on a multiplicative scale is the one with the smaller factor. Here $a=6.4$ and $\dfrac{10}{6.4}\approx1.56$, so $10^{-5}$ is closer. In other words, $6.4>\sqrt{10}\approx3.16$. The value $10^{-6}$ comes from looking only at the exponent as written.
