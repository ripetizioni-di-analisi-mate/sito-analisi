Which power of ten is closest to the product $(2.1\cdot10^3)(4.8\cdot10^{-7})$ on a multiplicative scale?

- $10^{-4}$
- $10^{-3}$
- $10^{-2}$
- $10^4$

## Explanation

Calculate the product by multiplying the coefficients and adding the exponents.
$$
(2.1\cdot10^3)(4.8\cdot10^{-7})=10.08\cdot10^{-4}=1.008\cdot10^{-3}.
$$
The normalized coefficient $1.008$ is less than $\sqrt{10}\approx3.16$, so the nearest power of ten on a multiplicative scale is $10^{-3}$. The value $10^{-4}$ comes from reading off the exponent before normalizing the coefficient $10.08$. The value $10^{4}$ comes from reversing the sign of the exponent.
