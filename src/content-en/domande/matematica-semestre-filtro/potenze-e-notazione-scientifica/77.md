Calculate $\sqrt{2.5\cdot10^{-7}}$ and give the result in scientific notation.

- $5\cdot10^{-4}$
- $5\cdot10^{-3}$
- $2.5\cdot10^{-4}$
- $5\cdot10^{-8}$

## Explanation

The exponent $-7$ is odd, so $\sqrt{10^{-7}}$ is not an integer power of ten. Move a factor of $10$ from the power of ten to the coefficient to obtain $2.5\cdot10^{-7}=25\cdot10^{-8}$.
$$
\sqrt{25\cdot10^{-8}}=\sqrt{25}\cdot10^{-4}=5\cdot10^{-4}.
$$
A check gives $\left(5\cdot10^{-4}\right)^2=25\cdot10^{-8}$. The value $5\cdot10^{-3}$ comes from incorrectly rewriting the radicand as $25\cdot10^{-6}$. The value $5\cdot10^{-8}$ comes from taking the root of the coefficient alone.
