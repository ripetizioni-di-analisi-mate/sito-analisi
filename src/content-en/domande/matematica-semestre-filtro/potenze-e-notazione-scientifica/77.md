Only the bound $10^{-8}<A<10^{-7}$ is known for the positive area of a square in square metres. Which interval contains exactly the possible side lengths in metres?

- $10^{-4}<l<\sqrt{10}\cdot10^{-4}$
- $10^{-8}<l<10^{-7}$
- $10^{-4}<l<10^{-3}$
- $10^{-16}<l<10^{-14}$

## Explanation

The square root is increasing on the positive numbers, so taking the square roots of the endpoints preserves the strict inequalities. The lower bound is $10^{-4}$. Since $10^{-7}=10\cdot10^{-8}$, the upper bound is $\sqrt{10}\cdot10^{-4}$.
