Calculate $\left(\dfrac8{27}\right)^{\frac23}$.

- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac{64}{729}$
- $\dfrac49$
- $\dfrac9{4}$

## Explanation

For a positive base, first take the root indicated by the exponent's denominator, then raise it to the power indicated by the numerator. Since $2^3=8$ and $3^3=27$, the cube root of $\dfrac{8}{27}$ is $\dfrac23$.
$$
\left(\frac{8}{27}\right)^{\frac23}=\left(\frac23\right)^{2}=\frac49.
$$
The value $\dfrac23$ comes from taking only the root. The value $\dfrac{64}{729}$ comes from squaring without taking the root. The value $\dfrac94$ comes from inverting the fraction as if the exponent were negative.
