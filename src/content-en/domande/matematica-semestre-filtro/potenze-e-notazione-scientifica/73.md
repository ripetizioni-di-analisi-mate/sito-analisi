In real arithmetic define $x^{2/3}=(\sqrt[3]{x})^2$. Which two different inputs $x$ both give the result $4$?

- $-2$ and $2$
- $0$ and $8$
- $-8$ and $8$
- $-4$ and $4$

## Explanation

A number has square four if it equals either $2$ or $-2$. Here the number being squared is the cube root of $x$. Cubing the two possible values gives $x=8$ or $x=-8$.
