How many significant figures are reported in the measurement $0.03006$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $5$
- $4$

## Explanation

The significant figures begin with the first nonzero digit, $3$. The leading zeros in $0.03006$ only locate the decimal point. The two zeros between $3$ and $6$ are measured digits and therefore count. Thus the significant figures are $3$, $0$, $0$, and $6$, as shown in $3.006\cdot10^{-2}$. The value $2$ comes from excluding the zeros between the nonzero digits. The value $5$ comes from counting the zero immediately after the decimal point as well.
