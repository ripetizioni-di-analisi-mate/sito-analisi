An instrument rounds length to the nearest hundredth of a metre, rounding exact ties upwards. Which positive lengths give a reading of $1.20\,\mathrm m$?

- $1.200\le l<1.210$ metres
- $1.195<l\le1.205$ metres
- $1.19\le l<1.21$ metres
- $1.195\le l<1.205$ metres

## Explanation

The neighbouring displays are $1.19$ and $1.21$. Their midpoints with $1.20$ are $1.195$ and $1.205$. The stated tie rule assigns the first midpoint to $1.20$ and the second to $1.21$. This determines which endpoint to include.
