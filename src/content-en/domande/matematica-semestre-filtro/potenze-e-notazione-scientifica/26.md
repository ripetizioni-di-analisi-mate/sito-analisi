A car's odometer shows six digits and, after $999999$, returns to $000000$. The car starts with the odometer at $000000$ and travels $10^6+27$ kilometres. What number does the odometer show at the end?

- $\mathtt{000026}$
- $\mathtt{100027}$
- $\mathtt{000027}$
- $\mathtt{999999}$

## Explanation

With six digits the odometer shows the numbers from $000000$ to $999999$, that is $10^6$ different values. After $10^6$ kilometres it has therefore shown all these values and is back at $000000$. The next $27$ kilometres bring it to $000027$.
