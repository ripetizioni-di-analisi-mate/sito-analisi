What is the exponent of ten when $30\,000\,000\,000$ is written in scientific notation?

- $11$
- $-10$
- $10$
- $9$

## Explanation

The coefficient must satisfy $1\le a<10$, so put the decimal point immediately after the first digit, $3$. There are ten digits after the $3$, so move the decimal point ten places to the left.
$$
30\,000\,000\,000=3\cdot10^{10}.
$$
The exponent is positive because the number is greater than $1$. The value $11$ comes from counting all the digits, including the first. The value $-10$ comes from assigning a negative exponent to a large number.
