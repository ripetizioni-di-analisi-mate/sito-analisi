A counter displays six decimal digits, from $000000$ to $999999$, and then starts over at zero. It starts at zero and receives exactly $10^6+27$ pulses. What does it display?

- $\mathtt{000026}$
- $\mathtt{100027}$
- $\mathtt{000027}$
- $\mathtt{999999}$

## Explanation

Six positions give $10^6$ different configurations including the initial one. After $10^6$ pulses the counter returns to zero. The remaining twenty-seven pulses bring it to $000027$.
