Two cubes have volumes $V$ and $64V$ with $V>0$. What is the ratio of the larger cube's edge length to the smaller cube's edge length?

- $4$
- $8$
- $16$
- $64$

## Explanation

A cube with edge length $\ell$ has volume $\ell^3$, so the volume ratio is the cube of the edge-length ratio.
$$
\left(\frac{\ell_2}{\ell_1}\right)^3=\frac{64V}{V}=64.
$$
The edge-length ratio is positive and equals $\sqrt[3]{64}=4$ because $4^3=64$. The value $8$ comes from taking the square root instead of the cube root. The value $64$ comes from confusing the volume ratio with the edge-length ratio.
