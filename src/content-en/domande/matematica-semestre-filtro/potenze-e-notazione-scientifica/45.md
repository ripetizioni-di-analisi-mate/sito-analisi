A positive index $y$ is defined by $y=x^{-3/4}$, where $x>0$. How can you recover $x$ from $y$?

- $x=y^{4/3}$
- $x=y^{-3/4}$
- $x=-y^{3/4}$
- $x=y^{-4/3}$

## Explanation

To undo an exponent, multiply it by its reciprocal. Raising $y$ to $-4/3$ makes the exponent of $x$ equal to $(-3/4)(-4/3)=1$. Positivity keeps these power operations unambiguous.
