Calculate $81^{-\frac34}$.

- $27$
- $\dfrac13$
- $-27$
- $\dfrac1{27}$

## Explanation

For a positive base, the power $81^{-\frac34}$ is the reciprocal of the cube of the fourth root of $81$. Since $3^4=81$, we have $\sqrt[4]{81}=3$.
$$
81^{-\frac34}=\frac{1}{\left(\sqrt[4]{81}\right)^3}=\frac{1}{3^3}=\frac{1}{27}.
$$
The value $27$ comes from ignoring the sign of the exponent. The value $-27$ comes from treating the negative exponent as a minus sign in front of the result. The value $\dfrac13$ comes from omitting the cube.
