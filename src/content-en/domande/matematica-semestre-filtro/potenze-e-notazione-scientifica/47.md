A measurement is reported as $0.120\,\mathrm{m}$. Which expression in scientific notation preserves its value and the exact number of significant figures reported?

- $1.2\cdot10^{-1}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.200\cdot10^{-1}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.20\cdot10^{-1}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.20\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The measurement $0.120\,\mathrm{m}$ has three significant figures, $1$, $2$, and the trailing zero, which indicates precision to the nearest thousandth of a meter. The normalized form must preserve both the value and these three figures, so the coefficient is $1.20$. Since $0.120=1.20\cdot10^{-1}$, the exponent is $-1$. The expression $1.2\cdot10^{-1}\,\mathrm{m}$ has the same value but omits the trailing zero. The expression $1.200\cdot10^{-1}\,\mathrm{m}$ has one more digit than the measurement reports.
