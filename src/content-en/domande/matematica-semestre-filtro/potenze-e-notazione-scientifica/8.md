How many significant figures does $0.007200$ have?

- $4$
- $2$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

The significant figures begin with the first nonzero digit, $7$. The preceding zeros only locate the decimal point and do not count. The two trailing zeros after the decimal point indicate the precision of the measurement and do count.
$$
0.007200=7.200\cdot10^{-3}.
$$
The significant figures are therefore $7$, $2$, $0$, and $0$. The value $2$ comes from ignoring the trailing zeros. The value $6$ comes from counting every digit after the decimal point.
