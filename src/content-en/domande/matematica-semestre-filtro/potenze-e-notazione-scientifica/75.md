Let $1\le c<10$. For which values of $c$ does the product $(2\cdot10^4)(c\cdot10^3)$ have exponent $8$ after normalization?

- $1\le c<5$
- $1\le c\le5$
- $6\le c<10$
- $5\le c<10$

## Explanation

Multiply the coefficients and add the exponents to obtain the product $2c\cdot10^{7}$. Since $1\le c<10$, the coefficient $2c$ lies between $2$ and $20$. If $2c<10$, the product is already normalized with exponent $7$. If $2c\ge10$, write $2c=\dfrac{2c}{10}\cdot10$, and the exponent becomes $8$. The condition $2c\ge10$ is equivalent to $c\ge5$, so the required values are $5\le c<10$. On the interval $1\le c<5$ the exponent remains $7$.
