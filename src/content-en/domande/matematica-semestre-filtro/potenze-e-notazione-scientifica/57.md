Which power of $2$ equals $0.125$?

- $2^{3}$
- $2^{-2}$
- $2^{-3}$
- $2^{-1}$

## Explanation

Write the decimal as a fraction and reduce it.
$$
0.125=\frac{125}{1000}=\frac{1}{8}.
$$
Since $8=2^3$, the number is the reciprocal of $2^3$. By the definition of a negative exponent, $\dfrac{1}{2^3}=2^{-3}$. The value $2^{3}=8$ is the reciprocal of the required number and comes from an error in the sign of the exponent. The powers $2^{-1}=0.5$ and $2^{-2}=0.25$ are greater than $0.125$.
