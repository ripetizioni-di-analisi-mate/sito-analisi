A simulator moves from zero in steps of $2^{-3}$. Which positive number can be reached in a whole number of steps?

- $0.30$
- $0.20$
- $0.375$
- $0.45$

## Explanation

The step is $1/8$. A value $v$ is reachable if $8v$ is an integer. For $0.375$ the product is three. Multiplying $0.30$, $0.20$ and $0.45$ by eight gives $2.4$, $1.6$ and $3.6$, respectively.
