Report $9.96\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$ to two significant figures.

- $9.9\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.0\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For two significant figures, keep the first two digits of the coefficient, $9$ and $9$. The first discarded digit is $6$, greater than $5$, so increase the second digit by one. The coefficient becomes $10$. Since $10\cdot10^{-3}=1.0\cdot10^{-2}$, normalize the coefficient and increase the exponent by one.
$$
9.96\cdot10^{-3}\approx10\cdot10^{-3}=1.0\cdot10^{-2}.
$$
The zero in $1.0$ is the second significant figure. The value $9.9\cdot10^{-3}$ comes from truncating instead of rounding. The value $1.0\cdot10^{-3}$ comes from normalizing the coefficient without adjusting the exponent.
