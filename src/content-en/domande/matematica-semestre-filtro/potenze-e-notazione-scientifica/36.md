A table lists a pressure measurement as $3600\,\mathrm{Pa}$ and gives only the value as written. Can you determine how many significant figures were reported?

- No. The trailing zeros are ambiguous as written.
- Yes. There are always four.
- Yes. There are always three.
- Yes. There are always two.

## Explanation

Trailing zeros in a whole number written without a decimal point can be measured digits or can simply locate the hundreds place. The expressions $3.6\cdot10^3$, $3.60\cdot10^3$, and $3.600\cdot10^3$ have the same value and indicate two, three, and four significant figures, respectively. The notation $3600\,\mathrm{Pa}$ is consistent with all three, so the number of significant figures cannot be determined. The answers giving a fixed number treat trailing zeros the same way in every case.
