The sides of a rectangle measure $2.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $3.44\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its area under the significant-figure convention?

- $8.5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $8.7\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $8.6\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The area is the product of the side lengths, $2.5\cdot3.44=8.60$, with unit $\mathrm{cm\cdot cm}=\mathrm{cm^2}$. For a product of measurements, retain as many significant figures as the measurement with the fewest. The side $2.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ has two and the side $3.44\,\mathrm{cm}$ has three, so the area has two. The first discarded digit is $0$, so the area is $8.6\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The value $8.5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ comes from rounding $3.44$ to $3.4$ before multiplying.
