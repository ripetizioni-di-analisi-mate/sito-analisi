Calculate $3\cdot10^{-3}+7\cdot10^{-4}$.

- $10\cdot10^{-7}$
- $3.7\cdot10^{-3}$
- $10\cdot10^{-3}$
- $3.7\cdot10^{-4}$

## Explanation

To add two numbers in scientific notation, express both using the same power of ten. Since $3\cdot10^{-3}=30\cdot10^{-4}$, add the coefficients $30$ and $7$.
$$
3\cdot10^{-3}+7\cdot10^{-4}=37\cdot10^{-4}=3.7\cdot10^{-3}.
$$
The value $10\cdot10^{-7}$ comes from adding both the coefficients and the exponents, treating the sum as a product. The value $3.7\cdot10^{-4}$ comes from normalizing the coefficient $37$ without increasing the exponent.
