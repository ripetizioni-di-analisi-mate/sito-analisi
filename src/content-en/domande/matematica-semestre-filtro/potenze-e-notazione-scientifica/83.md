Round $7.449$ to the nearest tenth in a single step.

- $7.4$
- $7.5$
- $7.44$
- $7.45$

## Explanation

To round to the nearest tenth, look at the first discarded digit, in the hundredths place. In $7.449$ this digit is $4$. Since $4<5$, the tenths digit remains $4$, giving $7.4$. The value $7.5$ comes from rounding first to the nearest hundredth, giving $7.45$, and then to the nearest tenth. After the first step the digit to be discarded is $5$, whereas the original number is less than $7.45$ because $7.449<7.45$.
