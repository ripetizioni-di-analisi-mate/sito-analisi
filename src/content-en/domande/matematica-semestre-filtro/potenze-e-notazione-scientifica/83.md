A program rounds to the nearest thousand before multiplying. For $1490\cdot2$, what is the absolute difference from rounding the exact product to the nearest thousand? The factor 2 is exact and is not rounded.

- $1000$
- $0$
- $490$
- $980$

## Explanation

Rounding first gives $1000\cdot2=2000$. The exact product is $2980$, which rounds to $3000$. The difference is 1000.
