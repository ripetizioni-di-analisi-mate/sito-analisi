Add $0.45\,\mu\mathrm{m}$ and $2\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$, treating the data as exact. Express the sum in meters using scientific notation.

- $6.5\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.45\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6.5\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.7\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Express the two measurements using both the same unit and the same power of ten. Since $1\,\mu\mathrm{m}=10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m}$, we have $0.45\,\mu\mathrm{m}=0.45\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m}=4.5\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$.
$$
4.5\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}+2\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}=6.5\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $2.45\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from adding $0.45$ and $2$ without converting the micrometers. The value $6.5\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from an error of one in the exponent during conversion.
