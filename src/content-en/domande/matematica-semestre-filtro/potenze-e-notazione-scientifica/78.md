There are exactly $12$ objects, and the same measured mass of $2.4\,\mathrm{mg}$ is used for each one. How should their total mass be reported under the significant-figure convention?

- $28.8\,\mathrm{mg}$
- $29\,\mathrm{mg}$
- $28\,\mathrm{mg}$
- $30\,\mathrm{mg}$

## Explanation

The total mass is the number of objects times the mass of each object, $12\cdot2.4\,\mathrm{mg}=28.8\,\mathrm{mg}$. The number $12$ is an exact count, so it does not limit the precision of the result. The measured mass has two significant figures, so the result has two. The first discarded digit is $8$, so $28.8$ rounds to $29\,\mathrm{mg}$. The value $28\,\mathrm{mg}$ comes from truncating instead of rounding. The value $30\,\mathrm{mg}$ comes from retaining only one significant figure.
