Let $x<0$. What are the signs of $x^4$ and $x^{-3}$, respectively?

- Positive and negative.
- Negative and positive.
- Positive and positive.
- Negative and negative.

## Explanation

In the fourth power of a negative number, the four negative factors form two pairs with positive products, so $x^4>0$. By definition, $x^{-3}=\dfrac{1}{x^3}$. The exponent $3$ is odd, so $x^3<0$, and the reciprocal of a negative number is negative. Thus $x^{-3}<0$. The answer “negative and negative” comes from assigning the sign of $x$ to every power. The answer “positive and positive” comes from confusing the sign of the exponent with the sign of the power.
