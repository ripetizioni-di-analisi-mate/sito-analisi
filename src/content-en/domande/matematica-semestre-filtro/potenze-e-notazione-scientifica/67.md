Which expression is equivalent to $\sqrt{72}$?

- $12\sqrt{2}$
- $6\sqrt{3}$
- $6\sqrt{2}$
- $36\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

Factor $72$ to isolate its largest perfect-square factor, $72=36\cdot2$. The root of a product of nonnegative numbers is the product of their roots.
$$
\sqrt{72}=\sqrt{36}\cdot\sqrt2=6\sqrt2.
$$
The value $36\sqrt2$ comes from moving the factor $36$ outside the radical without taking its root. The value $6\sqrt3$ comes from the incorrect factorization $72=36\cdot3$. The value $12\sqrt2$ equals $\sqrt{288}$, whose radicand is four times $72$.
