A periodic signal has period $T=2.5\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{s}$. Calculate its frequency $f=\dfrac{1}{T}$ in scientific notation.

- $4.0\cdot10^{2}\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $4.0\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $2.5\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $4.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The reciprocal of a product is the product of the reciprocals, so calculate $\dfrac{1}{2.5}=0.4$ and $\dfrac{1}{10^{-3}}=10^{3}$ separately.
$$
f=\frac{1}{2.5\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{s}}=0.4\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}=4.0\cdot10^{2}\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
The coefficient $0.4$ is less than $1$. Multiplying it by $10$ decreases the exponent from $3$ to $2$. The value $4.0\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{Hz}$ comes from omitting this adjustment. The value $2.5\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{Hz}$ comes from changing the sign of the exponent without taking the reciprocal of the coefficient.
