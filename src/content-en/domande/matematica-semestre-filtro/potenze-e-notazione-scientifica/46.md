A surface has area $3.0\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Express the area in $\mathrm{m^2}$ using scientific notation.

- $3.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $3.0\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $3.0\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $3.0\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

One centimeter is $10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}$. A square centimeter is the area of a square with side length $1\,\mathrm{cm}$, so square the conversion factor.
$$
1\,\mathrm{cm^2}=\left(10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}\right)^2=10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}.
$$
Therefore $3.0\,\mathrm{cm^2}=3.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The value $3.0\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ comes from using the length conversion factor without squaring it. The value $3.0\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ comes from multiplying by $10^4$ instead of dividing.
