Using the approximation $2^{10}\approx10^{3}$, what estimate do you obtain for $2^{43}$?

- $8\cdot10^{7}$
- $6\cdot10^{12}$
- $8\cdot10^{12}$
- $1\cdot10^{12}$

## Explanation

Decompose the exponent $43$ to isolate a multiple of $10$ by writing $43=3+40$, then apply the product rule and the rule for a power of a power.
$$
2^{43}=2^{3}\cdot\left(2^{10}\right)^4\approx8\cdot\left(10^{3}\right)^4=8\cdot10^{12}.
$$
Evaluating the expression directly gives approximately $8.8\cdot10^{12}$, so the estimate has the correct scale. The value $8\cdot10^{7}$ comes from writing $\left(10^3\right)^4=10^{7}$, adding the exponents. The value $6\cdot10^{12}$ comes from evaluating $2^3$ as $2\cdot3$. The value $1\cdot10^{12}$ comes from omitting the factor $2^3$.
