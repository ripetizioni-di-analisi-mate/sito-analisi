A measured disc radius exceeds the original by a factor of $1.01$. Using both radii in $A=\pi r^2$, by what percentage does the calculated area exceed the original area?

- Exactly $2\%$
- $2.01\%$
- $0.01\%$
- $1.01\%$

## Explanation

The area ratio is $(1.01)^2=1.0201$. Subtracting one leaves a relative excess of $0.0201$, or $2.01\%$. Doubling the small percentage error gives only the first-order approximation.
