Calculate $(6\cdot10^{-4})^2$ and give the result in scientific notation.

- $3.6\cdot10^{-8}$
- $3.6\cdot10^{-7}$
- $3.6\cdot10^{-3}$
- $1.2\cdot10^{-8}$

## Explanation

The square of a product is the product of the squares, so square the coefficient and multiply the exponent by $2$.
$$
\left(6\cdot10^{-4}\right)^2=36\cdot10^{-8}=3.6\cdot10^{-7}.
$$
The coefficient $36$ exceeds $10$, so write $36=3.6\cdot10$ and increase the exponent from $-8$ to $-7$. The value $3.6\cdot10^{-8}$ comes from omitting this adjustment. The value $1.2\cdot10^{-8}$ comes from doubling the coefficient instead of squaring it. The value $3.6\cdot10^{-3}$ comes from squaring only the coefficient.
