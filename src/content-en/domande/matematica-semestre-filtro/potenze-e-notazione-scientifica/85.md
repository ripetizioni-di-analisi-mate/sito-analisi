Which integer $n$ satisfies $4^n=2^{-6}$?

- $3$
- $-3$
- $-6$
- $-12$

## Explanation

Since $4=2^2$, the rule for a power of a power gives $4^n=\left(2^2\right)^n=2^{2n}$. The equation becomes $2^{2n}=2^{-6}$. Two powers with the same base $2$ are equal only if their exponents are equal, so $2n=-6$ and $n=-3$. A check gives $4^{-3}=\dfrac1{64}=2^{-6}$. The value $-6$ comes from leaving the base $4$ as written. The value $-12$ comes from multiplying by $2$ instead of dividing.
