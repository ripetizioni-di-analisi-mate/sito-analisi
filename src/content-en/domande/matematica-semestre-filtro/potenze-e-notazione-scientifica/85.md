A device uses only time intervals of $2^n$ seconds for integer $n$. Which duration between $1/10$ and $1/5$ of a second can it schedule?

- $1/7$ of a second
- $1/8$ of a second
- $1/9$ of a second
- $1/6$ of a second

## Explanation

A fraction below one requires a negative exponent, and the denominator must be an integer power of two. Only $8=2^3$ is an eligible denominator in the given range. The setting is therefore $n=-3$.
