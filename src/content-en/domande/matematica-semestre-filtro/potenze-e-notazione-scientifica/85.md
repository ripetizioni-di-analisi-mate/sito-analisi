Which of the following numbers, all between $\dfrac{1}{10}$ and $\dfrac{1}{5}$, can be written in the form $2^n$ with $n$ an integer?

- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

Since the numbers given are less than $1$, the exponent $n$ must be negative, and then $2^n=\dfrac{1}{2^{-n}}$ has a power of $2$ as its denominator. Among the denominators $7$, $8$, $9$ and $6$ the only power of $2$ is $8=2^3$. Hence the required number is $\dfrac{1}{8}=2^{-3}$, and indeed $\dfrac{1}{10}<\dfrac{1}{8}<\dfrac{1}{5}$.
