A mass is recorded as $2.40\,\mathrm g$ after rounding to the nearest hundredth of a gram. Which uncertainty around the reading represents half the rounding increment?

- $\pm0.05\,\mathrm g$
- $\pm0.005\,\mathrm g$
- $\pm0.01\,\mathrm g$
- $\pm0.001\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

Adjacent display values differ by $0.01$ g. The midpoint is $0.005$ g away from the displayed value.
