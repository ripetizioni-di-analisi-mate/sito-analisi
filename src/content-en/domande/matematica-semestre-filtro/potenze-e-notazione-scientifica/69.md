The measured mass $12.1\,\mathrm{g}$ is subtracted from the measured mass $12.4\,\mathrm{g}$. How many significant figures does the difference have when reported under the decimal-place convention?

- $2$
- $1$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The unrounded difference is $12.4-12.1=0.3\,\mathrm{g}$. When subtracting measurements, retain their last common decimal place, here tenths of a gram, so report $0.3\,\mathrm{g}$. The zero before the decimal point only locates the point, so the difference has one significant figure. The value $3$ comes from applying the product rule, which would retain the three significant figures of the data. In this subtraction, the first two digits cancel.
