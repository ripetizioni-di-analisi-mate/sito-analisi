Which of the following numbers is less than $8\cdot10^{-5}$?

- $8.01\cdot10^{-5}$
- $0.81\cdot10^{-4}$
- $80\cdot10^{-6}$
- $7.99\cdot10^{-5}$

## Explanation

Written as multiples of $10^{-5}$, the numbers are $8.01\cdot10^{-5}$, $0.81\cdot10^{-4}=8.1\cdot10^{-5}$, $80\cdot10^{-6}=8\cdot10^{-5}$ and $7.99\cdot10^{-5}$. Since they all have the same power of ten, it is enough to compare the coefficients with $8$. The only coefficient less than $8$ is $7.99$, so the required number is $7.99\cdot10^{-5}$. The number $80\cdot10^{-6}$ equals $8\cdot10^{-5}$, so it is not less.
