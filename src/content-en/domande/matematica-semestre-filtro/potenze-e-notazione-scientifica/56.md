A sensor records positive readings $x$ at or above $8\cdot10^{-5}$. Which reading is excluded if the criterion is applied to the exact number before rounding?

- $8.01\cdot10^{-5}$
- $0.81\cdot10^{-4}$
- $80\cdot10^{-6}$
- $7.99\cdot10^{-5}$

## Explanation

In units of $10^{-5}$, the threshold is eight. The reading $7.99\cdot10^{-5}$ falls just below it, whereas $0.81\cdot10^{-4}$ has coefficient $8.1$ and $80\cdot10^{-6}$ equals the threshold exactly. Equality is allowed, and $8.01$ also clears the cutoff. If the rejected reading were rounded to one significant figure first, it would appear to meet the threshold.
