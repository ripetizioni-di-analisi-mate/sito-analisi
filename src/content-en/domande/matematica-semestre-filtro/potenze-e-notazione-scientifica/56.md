Which of these positive numbers is greatest?

- $8.4\cdot10^{-5}$
- $9.9\cdot10^{-6}$
- $8.0\cdot10^{-5}$
- $1.2\cdot10^{-4}$

## Explanation

A number $a\cdot10^n$ with $1\le a<10$ lies between $10^n$ and $10^{n+1}$, so to compare positive numbers in normalized form, compare their exponents first. The number $1.2\cdot10^{-4}$ is at least $10^{-4}$, while the other three have exponent $-5$ or $-6$ and are less than $10^{-4}$. Thus $1.2\cdot10^{-4}$ is greatest. Choosing $9.9\cdot10^{-6}$ comes from comparing only the coefficients.
