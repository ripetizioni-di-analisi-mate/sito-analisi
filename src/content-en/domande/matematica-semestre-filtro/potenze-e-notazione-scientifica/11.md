Two exact readings, $6.02\cdot10^4$ and $5.97\cdot10^4$, are close together. What is the excess of the first over the second, expressed in scientific notation?

- $5\cdot10^3$
- $5\cdot10^4$
- $5\cdot10^2$
- $5\cdot10^1$

## Explanation

The two terms share the power $10^4$, so subtract the coefficients.
$$
6.02\cdot10^4-5.97\cdot10^4=0.05\cdot10^4.
$$
The coefficient $0.05$ is less than $1$. Since $0.05=5\cdot10^{-2}$, the exponent decreases by two and the result is $5\cdot10^{2}$. The value $5\cdot10^{3}$ comes from decreasing the exponent by only one. The value $5\cdot10^4$ comes from changing the coefficient without adjusting the exponent.
