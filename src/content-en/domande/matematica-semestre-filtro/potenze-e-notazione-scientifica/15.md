Write $2^{10}+2^{10}$ as a power of $2$.

- $2^{20}$
- $2^{100}$
- $4^{20}$
- $2^{11}$

## Explanation

The two terms are equal, so their sum is twice either one.
$$
2^{10}+2^{10}=2\cdot2^{10}=2^{1}\cdot2^{10}=2^{11}.
$$
In the last step, use the product rule for powers with the same base to add the exponents $1$ and $10$. The value $2^{20}$ comes from adding the exponents of the two terms as though the sum were a product. The value $2^{100}$ comes from multiplying the exponents.
