For which real value of $x$ is the expression $x^{-2}$ not defined?

- $0$
- $1$
- $-1$
- $-2$

## Explanation

By the definition of a power with a negative exponent $x^{-2}=\dfrac{1}{x^2}$, so the expression is defined when its denominator $x^2$ is nonzero. Since $x^2=0$ only for $x=0$, the expression is undefined only at $x=0$. For $x=1$, $x=-1$ and $x=-2$ the expression equals $1$, $1$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}$, respectively. The value $-2$ comes from confusing the exponent with a value of the base.
