How many digits does $2^{10}\cdot5^{12}$ have when written out in full?

- $12$
- $11$
- $22$
- $10$

## Explanation

Group the factors $2$ and $5$ with the same exponent, since $2^{10}\cdot5^{10}=(2\cdot5)^{10}=10^{10}$. Two factors of $5$ remain.
$$
2^{10}\cdot5^{12}=5^{2}\cdot10^{10}=25\cdot10^{10}=2.5\cdot10^{11}.
$$
The number is $25$ followed by ten zeros, so it has $12$ digits. The value $11$ comes from taking the exponent of $2.5\cdot10^{11}$ as the number of digits, whereas a normalized number with exponent $11$ has $12$ digits before its decimal point. The value $10$ comes from ignoring the factor $5^2$.
