A spreadsheet stores a positive reading as $z=1/x$. It needs to return $x^2$ without first recovering $x$. Which formula should it apply to $z$?

- $z^{-2}$
- $z^{1/2}$
- $-z^2$
- $2z^{-1}$

## Explanation

Since $x=z^{-1}$, its square is $(z^{-1})^2=z^{-2}$. The negative sign in the exponent denotes a reciprocal. Putting a minus sign in front of the square would give a negative value for a quantity that is always positive.
