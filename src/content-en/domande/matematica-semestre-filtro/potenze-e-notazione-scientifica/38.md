What is the reciprocal of $3^{-2}$?

- $9$
- $\dfrac19$
- $6$
- $-9$

## Explanation

By definition, $3^{-2}=\dfrac{1}{3^2}=\dfrac19$. The reciprocal of a number $b\ne0$ is the number that, when multiplied by $b$, gives $1$. Since $\dfrac19\cdot9=1$, the reciprocal of $\dfrac19$ is $9$. The rule for a power of a power gives the same result, $\left(3^{-2}\right)^{-1}=3^{2}=9$. The value $\dfrac19$ is $3^{-2}$ itself. The value $-9$ comes from confusing the reciprocal with the additive inverse.
