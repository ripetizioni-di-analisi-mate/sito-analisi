Two measured masses, $1.244\,\mathrm{g}$ and $0.08\,\mathrm{g}$, are added. How should the result be reported under the decimal-place convention?

- $1.32\,\mathrm{g}$
- $1.324\,\mathrm{g}$
- $1.3\,\mathrm{g}$
- $1.33\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The unrounded sum is $1.244+0.08=1.324\,\mathrm{g}$. When adding measurements, retain the last decimal place of the less precise measurement. The first mass is given to the nearest thousandth and the second to the nearest hundredth, so report the result to the nearest hundredth. The first discarded digit is $4$, less than $5$, so write $1.32\,\mathrm{g}$. The value $1.324\,\mathrm{g}$ reports one decimal place beyond the precision of the second measurement.
