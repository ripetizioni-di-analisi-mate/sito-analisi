An object's change in position along an oriented axis is $\Delta x=-3.8\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m}$. What do the two minus signs indicate?

- The first gives the direction of the change. The second indicates that its absolute value is less than $1\,\mathrm{m}$.
- The first indicates that the absolute value is less than $1\,\mathrm{m}$. The second gives the direction of the change.
- The two signs cancel, so the change is positive.
- Both indicate the direction of the change.

## Explanation

The minus sign before the coefficient gives the sign of the number, so $\Delta x<0$ and the final position lies before the initial position on the chosen axis. The exponent $-4$ applies only to the power of ten. Since $10^{-4}=\dfrac{1}{10^4}$ is positive and less than $1$, the absolute value $3.8\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m}=0.00038\,\mathrm{m}$ is less than one meter. The two signs do not cancel because one multiplies the number by negative one and the other belongs to the exponent.
