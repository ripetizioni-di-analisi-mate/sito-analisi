Two masses are reported as $3.80\,\mathrm{g}$ and $3.8\,\mathrm{g}$. What do these expressions indicate?

- The same numerical value, reported to the nearest hundredth in the first case and the nearest tenth in the second.
- The same numerical value, reported to the nearest tenth in both cases.
- Different numerical values, both reported to the nearest hundredth.
- Different numerical values, both reported to the nearest tenth.

## Explanation

The two expressions represent the same number because adding a trailing zero to the decimal part does not change the value. The final zero in $3.80\,\mathrm{g}$ is a measured digit, however, so the first reading is given to the nearest hundredth of a gram and has three significant figures. The expression $3.8\,\mathrm{g}$ ends in the tenths place and has two significant figures. The answers giving different numerical values confuse the stated precision with the value.
