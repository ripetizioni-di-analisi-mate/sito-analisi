The graph plots the particle count $N$ against the volume $V$. One horizontal unit represents $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{L}$, and one vertical unit represents $10^4$ particles. Between $A=(1,2)$ and $B=(3,6)$, what is $\Delta N/\Delta V$ in particles per litre?

::grafico{linee="1,2 3,6" punti="1,2,pieno,A;3,6,pieno,B" xmin=0 xmax=4 ymin=0 ymax=7 nomi="V,N" titolo="Counts in the stated scale units"}

- $2\cdot10^1$
- $2\cdot10^7$
- $2\cdot10^{-7}$
- $4\cdot10^7$

## Explanation

The actual vertical change is $(6-2)\cdot10^4=4\cdot10^4$ particles. The horizontal change is $(3-1)\cdot10^{-3}=2\cdot10^{-3}$ litres. Their ratio is $2\cdot10^{4-(-3)}=2\cdot10^7$ particles per litre.
