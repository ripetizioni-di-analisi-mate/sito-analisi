Calculate $\dfrac{10^{-2}}{10^{-6}}$.

- $10^{-8}$
- $10^{-4}$
- $10^{4}$
- $10^{5}$

## Explanation

When dividing powers with the same base, subtract the denominator's exponent from the numerator's. The denominator's exponent is negative, so the subtraction becomes an addition.
$$
\frac{10^{-2}}{10^{-6}}=10^{-2-(-6)}=10^{-2+6}=10^{4}.
$$
The value $10^{-8}$ comes from adding the exponents as in a product. The value $10^{-4}$ comes from calculating $-6-(-2)$, subtracting in reverse order.
