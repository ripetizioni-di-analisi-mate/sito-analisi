A sample has mass $2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm g$ and the absolute error is $4\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm g$. What is the corresponding relative error as a percentage?

- $0.02\%$
- $2\%$
- $20\%$
- $0.2\%$

## Explanation

Divide absolute error by mass, using the same unit for both. This gives $(4/2)\cdot10^{-5-(-3)}=2\cdot10^{-2}=0.02$. Multiplying that ratio by one hundred gives the percentage value $2\%$.
