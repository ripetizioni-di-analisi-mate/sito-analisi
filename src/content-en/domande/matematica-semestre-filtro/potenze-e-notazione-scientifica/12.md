Which number, written in scientific notation and then cubed, gives $-8\cdot10^{-12}$?

- $2\cdot10^{-4}$
- $-2\cdot10^{-12}$
- $-4\cdot10^{-4}$
- $-2\cdot10^{-4}$

## Explanation

The index $3$ is odd, so the cube root of a negative number exists and is negative. Since $(-2)^3=-8$, we have $\sqrt[3]{-8}=-2$. The exponent $-12$ is a multiple of $3$, so $\sqrt[3]{10^{-12}}=10^{-4}$.
$$
\sqrt[3]{-8\cdot10^{-12}}=-2\cdot10^{-4}.
$$
The value $-2\cdot10^{-12}$ comes from leaving the exponent unchanged. The value $2\cdot10^{-4}$ comes from losing the sign of the radicand.
