A table lists two amounts of substance, $2.4\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{mol}$ and $1.7\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{mol}$. Between which values does the ratio of the second amount to the first lie?

- Between $0.7$ and $0.8$
- Between $7$ and $8$
- Between $70$ and $80$
- Between $0.1$ and $0.2$

## Explanation

Express both amounts using the same power $10^{-6}$. Since $1.7\cdot10^{-5}=17\cdot10^{-6}$, their ratio is the quotient of the coefficients.
$$
\frac{17\cdot10^{-6}}{2.4\cdot10^{-6}}=\frac{17}{2.4}\approx7.1.
$$
Since $2.4\cdot7=16.8<17<19.2=2.4\cdot8$, the ratio lies between $7$ and $8$. The interval from $0.7$ to $0.8$ comes from comparing the coefficients $1.7$ and $2.4$ while ignoring the exponents. The interval from $0.1$ to $0.2$ contains the reciprocal ratio.
