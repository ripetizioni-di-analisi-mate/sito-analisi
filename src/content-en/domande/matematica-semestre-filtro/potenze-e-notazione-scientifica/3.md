Write $0.000045$ in scientific notation.

- $4.5\cdot10^{-7}$
- $4.5\cdot10^{5}$
- $4.5\cdot10^{-6}$
- $4.5\cdot10^{-5}$

## Explanation

In scientific notation, the coefficient $a$ satisfies $1\le a<10$. Since $10^{-5}=0.00001$ and $10^{-4}=0.0001$, we have $10^{-5}\le0.000045<10^{-4}$, so the exponent is $-5$. The coefficient is the number divided by $10^{-5}$.
$$
0.000045=\frac{0.000045}{10^{-5}}\cdot10^{-5}=4.5\cdot10^{-5}.
$$
The value $4.5\cdot10^{-6}$ comes from taking the number of decimal places, six, as the exponent.
