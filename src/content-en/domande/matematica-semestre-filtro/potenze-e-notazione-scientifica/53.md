A counter records $7.2\cdot10^9$ pulses at a constant rate of $2.4\cdot10^6$ pulses per second. How long does the count take?

- $3.0\cdot10^{15}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.7\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.0\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The duration is the number of pulses divided by the number of pulses per second. Divide the coefficients and subtract the exponents.
$$
t=\frac{7.2\cdot10^{9}}{2.4\cdot10^{6}}\,\mathrm{s}=\frac{7.2}{2.4}\cdot10^{9-6}\,\mathrm{s}=3.0\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
The pulses cancel, leaving seconds. The value $3.0\cdot10^{15}\,\mathrm{s}$ comes from adding the exponents. The value $3.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{s}$ comes from subtracting them in reverse order.
