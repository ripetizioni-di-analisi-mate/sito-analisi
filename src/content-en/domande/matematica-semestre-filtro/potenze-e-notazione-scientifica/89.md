Which of the following expressions is not in normalized scientific notation?

- $9.99\cdot10^{-2}$
- $1\cdot10^{0}$
- $0.5\cdot10^{3}$
- $5.0\cdot10^{12}$

## Explanation

An expression $a\cdot10^n$ is normalized when $n$ is an integer and its coefficient satisfies $1\le a<10$. The coefficient $0.5$ is less than $1$, so $0.5\cdot10^3$ is not normalized. Its normalized form is $5\cdot10^2$. The coefficient $1$ meets the condition because the endpoint $1$ is included, and the exponent $0$ is an integer. The coefficients $9.99$ and $5.0$ lie between $1$ and $10$ regardless of the exponent.
