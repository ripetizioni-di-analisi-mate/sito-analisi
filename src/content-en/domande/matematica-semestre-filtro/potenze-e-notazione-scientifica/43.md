You want to store $360\,000$ in the form $c\cdot10^n$, requiring $n$ to be a multiple of three and $1\le c<1000$. Which pair $(c,n)$ meets both conventions?

- $(0.36,\,6)$
- $(36,4)$
- $(360,3)$
- $(3.6,\,5)$

## Explanation

Write the value as $360\cdot1000=360\cdot10^3$. The coefficient is between one and one thousand and the exponent is a multiple of three. The usual scientific form $3.6\cdot10^5$ has the same value but fails the exponent requirement in this problem.
