Which normalized form represents $0.36\cdot10^8$?

- $3.6\cdot10^9$
- $3.6\cdot10^8$
- $3.6\cdot10^7$
- $3.6\cdot10^6$

## Explanation

In scientific notation the coefficient must be at least $1$, and $0.36$ is smaller. Since $0.36=3.6\cdot10^{-1}$, substitute and add the exponents.
$$
0.36\cdot10^8=3.6\cdot10^{-1}\cdot10^{8}=3.6\cdot10^{7}.
$$
The coefficient was multiplied by $10$ and the power of ten divided by $10$, so the value is unchanged. The value $3.6\cdot10^9$ comes from adjusting the exponent in the wrong direction. The value $3.6\cdot10^8$ comes from changing the coefficient without compensating for it.
