Calculate $\left(4^{-1}\right)^2\cdot2^3$ and express the result as a fraction in lowest terms.

- $\dfrac12$
- $2$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac1{32}$

## Explanation

The rule for a power of a power gives $\left(4^{-1}\right)^2=4^{-2}$. Since $4=2^2$, we have $4^{-2}=\left(2^2\right)^{-2}=2^{-4}$. The factors in the product with $2^3$ now have the same base, so add the exponents.
$$
\left(4^{-1}\right)^2\cdot2^3=2^{-4}\cdot2^{3}=2^{-1}=\frac12.
$$
A direct check gives $\dfrac{1}{16}\cdot8=\dfrac12$. The value $\dfrac{1}{32}$ comes from multiplying by $2^{-1}$ instead of $2^3$.
