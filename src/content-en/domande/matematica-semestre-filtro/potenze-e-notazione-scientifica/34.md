For $x>0$, write $\sqrt{x}\cdot\sqrt[3]{x}$ as a power of $x$.

- $x^{\frac16}$
- $x^{\frac56}$
- $x^{\frac25}$
- $x^{\frac15}$

## Explanation

For $x>0$, each root can be written as a fractional power, giving $\sqrt{x}=x^{\frac12}$ and $\sqrt[3]{x}=x^{\frac13}$. The two powers have the same base, so add the exponents.
$$
\sqrt{x}\cdot\sqrt[3]{x}=x^{\frac12+\frac13}=x^{\frac{3+2}{6}}=x^{\frac56}.
$$
The value $x^{\frac16}$ comes from multiplying the exponents instead of adding them. The value $x^{\frac25}$ comes from adding the numerators and denominators of the fractions separately. The value $x^{\frac15}$ comes from adding the indices of the roots.
