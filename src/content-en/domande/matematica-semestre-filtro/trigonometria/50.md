A ramp must make an angle of at most $12^\circ$ with the horizontal. It must rise by $0.90$ m and the available horizontal runs are $3.5$, $4.0$, $4.5$ and $5.0$ m. What is the shortest usable run? Use $\tan12^\circ\approx0.213$.

- $3.5\ \mathrm{m}$
- $4.0\ \mathrm{m}$
- $4.5\ \mathrm{m}$
- $5.0\ \mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For horizontal run $d$ the condition is $0.90/d\le0.213$, requiring $d\gtrsim4.23$ m. Among the available runs, $4.5$ m is the shortest that meets the condition.
