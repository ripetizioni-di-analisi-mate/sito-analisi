The perpendicular components of a displacement have magnitudes $2.4\,\mathrm m$ and $3.2\,\mathrm m$. What is the magnitude of the displacement?

- $5.6\,\mathrm m$
- $0.8\,\mathrm m$
- $4\,\mathrm m$
- $4.8\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The components are perpendicular, so they are the legs of a right triangle with the displacement as the hypotenuse. By the **Pythagorean theorem**,
$$
s=\sqrt{2.4^2+3.2^2}\,\mathrm m=\sqrt{5.76+10.24}\,\mathrm m=\sqrt{16}\,\mathrm m=4\,\mathrm m.
$$
Adding the component magnitudes gives $5.6\,\mathrm m$, the distance traveled along the two segments. Subtracting them gives $0.8\,\mathrm m$.
