In two right triangles the acute angles $\alpha$ and $\beta$ have sines $\dfrac35$ and $\dfrac5{13}$, respectively. Which angle is larger?

- $\alpha$
- $\beta$
- The two angles are equal.
- The hypotenuse lengths must be known.

## Explanation

For acute angles, sine increases as the angle increases, so the angle with the larger sine is larger. Compare the fractions by cross-multiplying:
$$
3\cdot13=39>25=5\cdot5,
$$
so $\dfrac35>\dfrac{5}{13}$ and $\alpha>\beta$. The hypotenuse lengths are unnecessary because trigonometric ratios depend only on the angle and are equal in similar triangles.
