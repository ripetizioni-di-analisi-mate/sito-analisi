Express $45^\circ$ in radians.

- $\dfrac{\pi}{8}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $4\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{4}{\pi}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

A straight angle measures both $180^\circ$ and $\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$, so multiply the degree measure by $\dfrac{\pi}{180}$ to obtain the radian measure. This gives
$$
\theta=45\cdot\frac{\pi}{180}\,\mathrm{rad}=\frac{\pi}{4}\,\mathrm{rad}.
$$
The values $4\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$ and $\dfrac{4}{\pi}\,\mathrm{rad}$ come from inverting the ratio of $45$ to $180$. The value $\dfrac{\pi}{8}\,\mathrm{rad}$ corresponds to $22.5^\circ$.
