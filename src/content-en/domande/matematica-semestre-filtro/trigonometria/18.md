An acute angle $\alpha$ satisfies $\sin\alpha=0.625$. What is the value of $\cos^2\alpha$?

- $0.375$
- $0.609375$
- $1.390625$
- $0.390625$

## Explanation

The identity $\sin^2\alpha+\cos^2\alpha=1$ gives $\cos^2\alpha=1-\sin^2\alpha$. The square of the sine is $0.625^2=0.390625$, so
$$
\cos^2\alpha=1-0.390625=0.609375.
$$
The value $0.375$ results from subtracting the sine from $1$ instead of subtracting its square. The value $0.390625$ is the square of the sine, and adding instead of subtracting gives $1.390625$.
