A segment of length $17\,\mathrm{cm}$ has a horizontal projection of length $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the length of its vertical projection?

- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $25\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $289\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The segment is the hypotenuse and the two projections are the legs. By the **Pythagorean theorem**,
$$
q=\sqrt{17^2-8^2}\,\mathrm{cm}=\sqrt{289-64}\,\mathrm{cm}=\sqrt{225}\,\mathrm{cm}=15\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
Subtracting the lengths without squaring them gives $9\,\mathrm{cm}$. The value $25\,\mathrm{cm}$ is their sum, and $289\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the square of the hypotenuse.
