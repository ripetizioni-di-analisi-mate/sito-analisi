A mass of $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ is on a plane inclined at $60^\circ$ to the horizontal. Using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, calculate the magnitude of the component of weight perpendicular to the plane.

- $30\sqrt3\,\mathrm N$
- $60\,\mathrm N$
- $15\,\mathrm N$
- $30\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The weight has magnitude $mg=6\cdot10\,\mathrm N=60\,\mathrm N$. The inward normal to the plane makes the same angle $\alpha=60^\circ$ with the downward vertical as the plane makes with the horizontal, so the magnitude of the perpendicular component is
$$
mg\cos60^\circ=60\cdot\frac12\,\mathrm N=30\,\mathrm N.
$$
Using sine gives $30\sqrt3\,\mathrm N$, the component parallel to the plane. The value $60\,\mathrm N$ is the full weight.
