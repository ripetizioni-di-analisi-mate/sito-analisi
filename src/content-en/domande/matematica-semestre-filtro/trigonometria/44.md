A light spot on a rotating table traces a circle of radius $r$. A sensor records only its horizontal coordinate. Starting from $(r,0)$, at which two angles in the first turn does the sensor record the value $\dfrac r2$? Take positive angles anticlockwise.

- $30^\circ,\ 150^\circ$
- $60^\circ,\ 120^\circ$
- $120^\circ,\ 240^\circ$
- $60^\circ,\ 300^\circ$

## Explanation

The horizontal coordinate is $r\cos\theta$. A reading of $r/2$ requires cosine $1/2$, at two points symmetric about the horizontal axis. In one turn between $0^\circ$ and $360^\circ$, these occur at $60^\circ$ and $300^\circ$.
