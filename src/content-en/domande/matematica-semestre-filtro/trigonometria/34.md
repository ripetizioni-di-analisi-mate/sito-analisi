The horizontal projection of a rod is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long. The rod makes an acute angle of $60^\circ$ with the vertical. How long is the vertical projection?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\sqrt3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\sqrt3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The rod makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the vertical and therefore $90^\circ-60^\circ=30^\circ$ with the horizontal. Relative to the $30^\circ$ angle, the horizontal projection is the adjacent leg and the vertical projection is the opposite leg, so their ratio is $\tan30^\circ$. This gives
$$
6\tan30^\circ\,\mathrm m=6\cdot\frac{\sqrt3}{3}\,\mathrm m=2\sqrt3\,\mathrm m.
$$
The value $6\sqrt3\,\mathrm m$ comes from using $\tan60^\circ$, which treats the angle measured from the vertical as though it were measured from the horizontal.
