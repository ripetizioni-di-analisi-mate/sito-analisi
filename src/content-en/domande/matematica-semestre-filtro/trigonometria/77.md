What is the principal value of $\arcsin(\sin150^\circ)$ in degrees?

- $150^\circ$
- $-30^\circ$
- $-150^\circ$
- $30^\circ$

## Explanation

The sine of $150^\circ$ equals the sine of its supplementary angle $30^\circ$, so its value is $\dfrac12$. The principal value of the inverse sine is the angle in $[-90^\circ,90^\circ]$ with that sine, so
$$
\arcsin(\sin150^\circ)=\arcsin\frac12=30^\circ.
$$
Canceling the inverse sine and sine as inverse functions gives $150^\circ$, but $150^\circ$ is outside the principal range. The angle $-30^\circ$ has sine $-\dfrac12$.
