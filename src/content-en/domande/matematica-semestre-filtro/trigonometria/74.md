For $\theta=90^\circ$, which statement is correct?

- $\cos\theta=1$ and $\tan\theta=0$.
- $\cos\theta=0$ and $\tan\theta=0$.
- $\cos\theta=0$ and $\tan\theta$ is undefined.
- $\cos\theta=-1$ and $\tan\theta=-1$.

## Explanation

The point on the unit circle corresponding to $90^\circ$ is $(0,1)$, so $\cos90^\circ=0$ and $\sin90^\circ=1$. The tangent is the quotient $\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$, defined only for $\cos\theta\neq0$. Here the denominator is zero, so $\tan90^\circ$ **is undefined**. The statement with $\cos\theta=1$ and $\tan\theta=0$ describes the angle $0^\circ$ and its point $(1,0)$.
