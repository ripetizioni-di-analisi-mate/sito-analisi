Which angle in $[0^\circ,360^\circ)$ has the same direction as $-110^\circ$?

- $110^\circ$
- $70^\circ$
- $290^\circ$
- $250^\circ$

## Explanation

Two measures give the same direction when they differ by an integer multiple of $360^\circ$. Adding a full turn gives
$$
-110^\circ+360^\circ=250^\circ,
$$
which lies in $[0^\circ,360^\circ)$. The direction is in the third quadrant, as expected from a clockwise rotation through $110^\circ$ from the positive $x$-axis. The value $110^\circ$ comes from dropping the sign, and $290^\circ$ comes from adding $180^\circ$ to $110^\circ$.
