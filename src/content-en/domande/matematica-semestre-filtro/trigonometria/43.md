Express the directed rotation $-450^\circ$ in radians, keeping all complete turns.

- $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-\dfrac{5\pi}{4}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-\dfrac{5\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

To convert, multiply by $\dfrac{\pi}{180}$ and retain the sign, which indicates clockwise rotation. This gives
$$
-450\cdot\frac{\pi}{180}\,\mathrm{rad}=-\frac{5\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}.
$$
The rotation includes one full turn, $-2\pi$, plus one-quarter turn, $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, in the same direction. The value $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$ describes the same final direction but removes the complete turn that the problem asks to retain. The value $\dfrac{5\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$ loses the sign.
