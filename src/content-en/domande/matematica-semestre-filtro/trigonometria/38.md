An acute angle increases from $30^\circ$ to $60^\circ$. What is the ratio of the final sine value to the initial sine value?

- $2$
- $\sqrt3$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$
- $\dfrac32$

## Explanation

The exact values are $\sin30^\circ=\dfrac12$ and $\sin60^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$. The ratio of the final value to the initial value is therefore
$$
\frac{\frac{\sqrt3}{2}}{\frac12}=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\cdot2=\sqrt3.
$$
The value $2$ assumes the sine is proportional to the angle. The angle doubles, but $\sin60^\circ\approx0.866$ is less than twice $\sin30^\circ=0.5$.
