Given $\sin25^\circ\approx0.423$ and $\cos25^\circ\approx0.906$, what is $\sin155^\circ$?

- $-0.423$
- $0.906$
- $0.423$
- $-0.906$

## Explanation

The angles $155^\circ$ and $25^\circ$ are supplementary because $155^\circ=180^\circ-25^\circ$. Their points on the unit circle are reflections across the vertical axis, so their horizontal coordinates are opposites and their vertical coordinates are equal. The sine is the vertical coordinate, so
$$
\sin155^\circ=\sin25^\circ\approx0.423.
$$
The value $-0.423$ gives the sine the negative sign that belongs to the cosine in the second quadrant. The value $0.906$ is $\cos25^\circ$.
