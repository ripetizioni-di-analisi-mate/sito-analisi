Given $\cos\theta>0$ and $\tan\theta<0$, in which quadrant does the point corresponding to $\theta$ lie?

- First.
- Second.
- Third.
- Fourth.

## Explanation

The cosine is positive, so the horizontal coordinate is positive and the point lies in the first or fourth quadrant. Since $\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}<0$ and $\cos\theta>0$, the sine is negative. A positive horizontal coordinate and a negative vertical coordinate identify the **fourth quadrant**. The tangent is also negative in the second quadrant, but the cosine there is negative.
