Calculate $\sin 240^\circ$.

- $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$
- $-\dfrac12$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The angle $240^\circ$ lies between $180^\circ$ and $270^\circ$, so it is in the third quadrant. Its reference angle is $240^\circ-180^\circ=60^\circ$. In the third quadrant the vertical coordinate of the point on the unit circle is negative, so
$$
\sin240^\circ=-\sin60^\circ=-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}.
$$
The value $-\dfrac12$ results from using $\cos60^\circ$ in place of $\sin60^\circ$. Overlooking the sign determined by the quadrant gives $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.
