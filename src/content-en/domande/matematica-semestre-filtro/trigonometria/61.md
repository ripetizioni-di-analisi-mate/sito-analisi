A ramp has a horizontal run of $2.4\,\mathrm m$ and a grade of $25\%$, measured as the ratio of rise to horizontal run. Calculate the rise.

- $0.6\,\mathrm m$
- $0.24\,\mathrm m$
- $0.96\,\mathrm m$
- $9.6\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

A grade of $25\%$ means that the ratio of rise to horizontal run is $0.25=\dfrac14$. The rise is therefore
$$
h=0.25\cdot2.4\,\mathrm m=0.6\,\mathrm m.
$$
Indeed, a rise of $0.6\,\mathrm m$ over $2.4\,\mathrm m$ corresponds to $25\,\mathrm{cm}$ of rise per horizontal meter. Dividing the horizontal run by the ratio instead of multiplying gives $9.6\,\mathrm m$.
