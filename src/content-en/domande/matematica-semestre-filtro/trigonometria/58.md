A triangle has sides of lengths $9\,\mathrm{cm}$, $12\,\mathrm{cm}$, and $15\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the sine of its smallest angle?

- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac45$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac53$

## Explanation

Since $9^2+12^2=81+144=225=15^2$, the triangle is right and the side of length $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the hypotenuse. The smallest angle is opposite the shortest side, the leg of length $9\,\mathrm{cm}$. Sine is the opposite leg divided by the hypotenuse, so
$$
\sin\theta=\frac{9}{15}=\frac35.
$$
The value $\dfrac45$ is the cosine of the same angle, and $\dfrac34$ is its tangent.
