Calculate the principal value of $\arccos\left(-\dfrac12\right)$ in degrees.

- $60^\circ$
- $240^\circ$
- $120^\circ$
- $-60^\circ$

## Explanation

The principal value of the inverse cosine is the angle in $[0^\circ,180^\circ]$ with the given cosine. The cosine is negative, so the angle lies in the second quadrant. The reference angle with cosine $\dfrac12$ is $60^\circ$, so
$$
\arccos\left(-\frac12\right)=180^\circ-60^\circ=120^\circ.
$$
The angle $240^\circ$ also has cosine $-\dfrac12$, but lies outside $[0^\circ,180^\circ]$. Moving the minus sign outside the inverse cosine, as one can do for inverse sine, gives $-60^\circ$.
