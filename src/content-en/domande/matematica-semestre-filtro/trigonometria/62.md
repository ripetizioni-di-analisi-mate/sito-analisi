A vector has $A_x=0$ and $A_y<0$. What is its angle in $[0^\circ,360^\circ)$, measured counterclockwise from the positive $x$-axis?

- $0^\circ$
- $270^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $180^\circ$

## Explanation

The horizontal component is zero, so the vector lies on the vertical axis and the ratio $\dfrac{A_y}{A_x}$ is undefined. Its direction is then determined by the sign of $A_y$, which is negative, so it points downward along the negative $y$-axis. A counterclockwise rotation of three-quarters of a turn from the positive $x$-axis reaches this direction, so the angle is $270^\circ$. By contrast, the direction $90^\circ$ corresponds to $A_y>0$.
