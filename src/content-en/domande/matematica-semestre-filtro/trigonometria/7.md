A force of magnitude $16\,\mathrm{N}$ makes an angle of $120^\circ$ with the positive $x$-axis. Calculate the component $F_x$.

- $-8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The horizontal component is $F_x=F\cos\theta$, with $\theta$ measured from the positive $x$-axis. The angle $120^\circ$ lies in the second quadrant with reference angle $180^\circ-120^\circ=60^\circ$, so $\cos120^\circ=-\cos60^\circ=-\dfrac12$. Therefore,
$$
F_x=16\cos120^\circ\,\mathrm{N}=16\cdot\left(-\frac12\right)\mathrm{N}=-8\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The value $8\,\mathrm{N}$ overlooks the negative sign of cosine in the second quadrant.
