Ramp A has a grade of $30\%$. Ramp B rises at an angle of $30^\circ$ to the horizontal. Which ramp rises farther over the same horizontal distance?

- Ramp A.
- They rise by the same amount.
- It depends on the initial elevation.
- Ramp B.

## Explanation

For a given horizontal distance, the rise depends on the ratio of rise to horizontal distance. For ramp A this ratio is $30\%=0.30$. For ramp B it is $\tan30^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}\approx0.577$. Since $0.577>0.30$, **ramp B** rises farther. The two ramps appear equivalent if a grade of $30\%$ is confused with an angle of $30^\circ$. For example, a grade of $100\%$ corresponds to $45^\circ$.
