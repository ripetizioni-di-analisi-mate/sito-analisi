The minute hand of a clock is $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. What is the length of the arc its tip traces in $20$ minutes?

- $4\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $240\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The minute hand completes one revolution in $60$ minutes, so it completes one-third of a revolution in $20$ minutes. The angle swept out is $\dfrac13\cdot2\pi=\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ radians. Using $s=r\theta$ gives
$$
s=12\cdot\frac{2\pi}{3}\,\mathrm{cm}=8\pi\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
The value $4\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$ comes from treating one hour as $\pi$ radians instead of $2\pi$. The value $24\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the entire circumference.
