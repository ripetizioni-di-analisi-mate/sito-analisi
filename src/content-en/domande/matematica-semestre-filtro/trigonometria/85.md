A directed segment has components $(3,4)$. What are the components of a segment pointing in the same direction with magnitude $10$?

- $(30,40)$
- $(8,6)$
- $(5\sqrt2,5\sqrt2)$
- $(6,8)$

## Explanation

The given segment has magnitude $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$, so its direction angle $\theta$ satisfies $\cos\theta=\dfrac35$ and $\sin\theta=\dfrac45$. A segment with the same direction and magnitude $10$ has components
$$
(10\cos\theta,\,10\sin\theta)=\left(10\cdot\frac35,\,10\cdot\frac45\right)=(6,8).
$$
The components double because the magnitude doubles. The pair $(30,40)$ comes from multiplying by $10$ instead of $\dfrac{10}{5}$, and the pair $(8,6)$ interchanges sine and cosine.
