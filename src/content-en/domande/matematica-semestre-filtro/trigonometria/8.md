A circle has radius $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Calculate the length of the arc subtended by $60^\circ$.

- $180\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\pi\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\pi\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{6}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The formula $s=r\theta$ applies when the angle is in radians. Therefore convert the angle, $\theta=60\cdot\dfrac{\pi}{180}=\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, and then multiply by the radius:
$$
s=3\cdot\frac{\pi}{3}\,\mathrm{m}=\pi\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $180\,\mathrm{m}$ results from substituting the numerical degree measure $60$ into the formula. The value $6\pi\,\mathrm{m}$ is the entire circumference.
