On a plane inclined at an acute angle $\theta$, the perpendicular axis points outward from the plane. What is the sign of the component of weight along that axis?

- Positive.
- Zero.
- It changes sign when the parallel axis is reversed.
- Negative.

## Explanation

The weight points vertically downward. Its component perpendicular to the inclined plane has magnitude $mg\cos\theta>0$ and points toward the plane, opposite to the direction chosen as positive. The component is therefore $-mg\cos\theta$ and is **negative** for every acute angle $\theta$. The direction chosen for the axis parallel to the plane determines only the sign of the parallel component.
