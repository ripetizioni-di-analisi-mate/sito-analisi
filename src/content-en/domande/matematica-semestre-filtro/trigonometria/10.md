Calculate $\tan 135^\circ$.

- $1$
- $-1$
- $-\sqrt3$
- $0$

## Explanation

The angle $135^\circ$ lies in the second quadrant and its reference angle is $180^\circ-135^\circ=45^\circ$. In the second quadrant sine is positive and cosine is negative, so
$$
\sin135^\circ=\frac{\sqrt2}{2},\qquad \cos135^\circ=-\frac{\sqrt2}{2}.
$$
Tangent is sine divided by cosine, so $\tan135^\circ=-1$. Ignoring the negative sign of cosine in the second quadrant gives $1$.
