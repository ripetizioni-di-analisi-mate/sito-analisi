In a right triangle the leg opposite angle $\alpha$ has length $21$ and the hypotenuse has length $35$. What is $\sin\alpha$?

- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $0.6$
- $0.8$
- $0.75$

## Explanation

The sine of $\alpha$ is the opposite leg divided by the hypotenuse, so
$$
\sin\alpha=\frac{21}{35}=\frac35=0.6.
$$
The other leg has length $\sqrt{35^2-21^2}=\sqrt{784}=28$, so the cosine is $\dfrac{28}{35}=0.8$. Thus using the adjacent leg gives $0.8$. The value $0.75=\dfrac{21}{28}$ is the tangent, and inverting the ratio gives $\dfrac53$.
