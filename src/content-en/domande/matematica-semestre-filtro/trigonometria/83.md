What are all the solutions of $\sin\theta=\dfrac12$ in $[0^\circ,360^\circ)$?

- $\{30^\circ\}$
- $\{30^\circ,330^\circ\}$
- $\{150^\circ,210^\circ\}$
- $\{30^\circ,150^\circ\}$

## Explanation

In the first quadrant the angle with sine $\dfrac12$ is $30^\circ$. The sine is the vertical coordinate of the point on the unit circle and is positive in the second quadrant as well. The angles $\theta$ and $180^\circ-\theta$ have the same vertical coordinate, so $180^\circ-30^\circ=150^\circ$ is also a solution. The solutions are therefore $30^\circ$ and $150^\circ$. Using the symmetry for cosine gives the set $\{30^\circ,330^\circ\}$, but $\sin330^\circ=-\dfrac12$.
