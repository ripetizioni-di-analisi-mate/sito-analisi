A segment of length $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ makes an acute angle of $30^\circ$ with the vertical. Calculate the length of its horizontal projection.

- $5\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{10}{\sqrt3}\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The $30^\circ$ angle is measured from the vertical. In the right triangle with the segment as hypotenuse, the horizontal projection is the leg opposite that angle, so its length is
$$
10\sin30^\circ\,\mathrm{cm}=10\cdot\frac12\,\mathrm{cm}=5\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
The value $5\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the vertical projection. It comes from treating the angle as measured from the horizontal and therefore using cosine.
