The point on the unit circle corresponding to $\theta$ has a negative horizontal coordinate and a positive vertical coordinate. Which description is correct?

- First quadrant, positive tangent.
- Second quadrant, negative tangent.
- Third quadrant, positive tangent.
- Fourth quadrant, negative tangent.

## Explanation

The horizontal coordinate of the point is $\cos\theta$ and its vertical coordinate is $\sin\theta$. A negative horizontal coordinate and a positive vertical coordinate identify the **second quadrant**. The tangent is the quotient $\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$ of a positive number and a negative number, so it is negative. For example, at $\theta=135^\circ$ we have $\tan\theta=-1$. In the third quadrant both coordinates are negative, so the tangent is positive.
