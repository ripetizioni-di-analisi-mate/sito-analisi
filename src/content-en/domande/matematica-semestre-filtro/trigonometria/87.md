The leg opposite an acute angle $\theta$ has length $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the adjacent leg has length $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which expression gives $\theta$?

- $\arctan\left(\dfrac32\right)$
- $\arcsin\left(\dfrac23\right)$
- $\arctan\left(\dfrac23\right)$
- $\arccos\left(\dfrac23\right)$

## Explanation

The ratio of the opposite leg to the adjacent leg is the tangent, so $\tan\theta=\dfrac23$. The angle is acute and a tangent value determines a unique acute angle, so
$$
\theta=\arctan\frac23.
$$
The expression $\arctan\dfrac32$ gives the other acute angle because it inverts the ratio. The expressions using inverse sine and inverse cosine require a ratio involving the hypotenuse, which has length $\sqrt{13}\,\mathrm{cm}$. Thus $\dfrac23$ differs from both $\sin\theta$ and $\cos\theta$.
