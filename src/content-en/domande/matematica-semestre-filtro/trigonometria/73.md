A right triangle has an acute angle $\alpha<45^\circ$. Which comparison is correct?

- $\sin\alpha<\cos\alpha$
- $\sin\alpha>\cos\alpha$
- $\sin\alpha=\cos\alpha$
- $\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha=1$

## Explanation

The acute angles of the triangle are $\alpha$ and $90^\circ-\alpha$. If $\alpha<45^\circ$, then $90^\circ-\alpha>45^\circ>\alpha$. The shorter side is opposite the smaller angle, so the leg opposite $\alpha$ is shorter than the adjacent leg. Sine and cosine have the same denominator, the hypotenuse, so $\sin\alpha<\cos\alpha$. The equality $\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha=1$ confuses the sum with the sum of the squares. For example, with $\alpha=30^\circ$ the sum is $\dfrac{1+\sqrt3}{2}$.
