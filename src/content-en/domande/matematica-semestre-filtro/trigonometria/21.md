A direction makes an angle of $240^\circ$ measured counterclockwise from the positive $x$-axis. In which quadrant does it lie?

- Second.
- Fourth.
- Third.
- First.

## Explanation

The quadrants are separated by the directions $90^\circ$, $180^\circ$, and $270^\circ$. Since $180^\circ<240^\circ<270^\circ$, the direction lies in the **third quadrant**. Both coordinates of the corresponding point on the unit circle are therefore negative, and indeed $\cos240^\circ=-\dfrac12$ and $\sin240^\circ=-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$. The fourth quadrant, in contrast, contains angles between $270^\circ$ and $360^\circ$ and has a positive horizontal coordinate.
