An object of mass $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ is on a plane inclined at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the uphill direction as positive. Calculate the component of weight along the plane.

- $20\,\mathrm N$
- $-20\sqrt3\,\mathrm N$
- $-20\,\mathrm N$
- $-40\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The weight has magnitude $mg=4\cdot10\,\mathrm{N}=40\,\mathrm{N}$. On the plane inclined at $\alpha=30^\circ$, the magnitude of its component parallel to the plane is
$$
mg\sin\alpha=40\cdot\frac12\,\mathrm{N}=20\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
This component points downhill, opposite the chosen positive direction, so its value is $-20\,\mathrm{N}$. Using cosine gives $-20\sqrt3\,\mathrm{N}$, the component perpendicular to the plane instead.
