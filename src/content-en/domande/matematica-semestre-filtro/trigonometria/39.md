The hypotenuse of a right triangle has length $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and one leg has length $5\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the acute angle adjacent to this leg?

- $45^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $30^\circ$

## Explanation

The leg of length $5\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}$ is adjacent to the required angle $\theta$, so use cosine, the adjacent leg divided by the hypotenuse. This gives
$$
\cos\theta=\frac{5\sqrt3}{10}=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}.
$$
The only acute angle with cosine $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ is $30^\circ$, so $\theta=30^\circ$. Interpreting the ratio as a sine gives $60^\circ$, which would treat the leg as opposite the angle.
