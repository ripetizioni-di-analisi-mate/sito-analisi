A directed segment of magnitude $10$ has direction $225^\circ$ measured counterclockwise from the positive $x$-axis. What are its components?

- $(5\sqrt2,5\sqrt2)$
- $(-5\sqrt2,-5\sqrt2)$
- $(-5\sqrt2,5\sqrt2)$
- $(-5,-5)$

## Explanation

The angle $225^\circ$ lies in the third quadrant and its reference angle is $225^\circ-180^\circ=45^\circ$. In the third quadrant sine and cosine are both negative, so $\cos225^\circ=\sin225^\circ=-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$. The components are
$$
\left(10\cdot\left(-\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\right),\,10\cdot\left(-\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\right)\right)=(-5\sqrt2,-5\sqrt2).
$$
The pair $(-5,-5)$ comes from using $\dfrac12$ instead of $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$ and has magnitude $5\sqrt2$ instead of $10$.
