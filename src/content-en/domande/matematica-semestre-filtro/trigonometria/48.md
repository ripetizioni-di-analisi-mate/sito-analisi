The point $P=(2,-5)$ rotates counterclockwise through $90^\circ$ about the origin. What are its final coordinates?

- $(-5,-2)$
- $(5,2)$
- $(2,5)$
- $(-2,5)$

## Explanation

A counterclockwise rotation through $90^\circ$ about the origin maps $(x,y)$ to $(-y,x)$. Indeed, it maps $(1,0)$ to $(0,1)$ and $(0,1)$ to $(-1,0)$. With $x=2$ and $y=-5$, the result is
$$
(-y,x)=(5,2).
$$
The starting point is in the fourth quadrant and the endpoint is in the first, as expected when a right angle is added to the direction. A clockwise rotation would give $(-5,-2)$.
