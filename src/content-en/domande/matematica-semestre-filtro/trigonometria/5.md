A right triangle has a hypotenuse of length $18\,\mathrm{m}$ and an angle of $60^\circ$. Calculate the length of the leg adjacent to this angle.

- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\sqrt3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The **cosine** of an acute angle is the ratio of the adjacent leg to the hypotenuse, so multiply the hypotenuse by the cosine to find the adjacent leg. Since $\cos60^\circ=\dfrac12$, we obtain
$$
b=18\cos60^\circ\,\mathrm{m}=18\cdot\frac12\,\mathrm{m}=9\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $9\sqrt3\,\mathrm{m}$ is the leg opposite the angle of $60^\circ$, the result obtained using sine. Dividing by the cosine gives $36\,\mathrm{m}$.
