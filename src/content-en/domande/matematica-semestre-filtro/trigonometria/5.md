A right triangle has an $18$ m hypotenuse and a $60^\circ$ acute angle. A drawing of it is enlarged without changing its angles. Which measurement from the enlarged drawing is sufficient on its own to recover the scale factor?

- The radian measure of the $60^\circ$ angle
- The ratio of the opposite leg to the hypotenuse
- The length of the leg adjacent to $60^\circ$
- The size of the other acute angle

## Explanation

The original adjacent leg is $18\cos60^\circ=9$ m long. Dividing the new length by $9$ m in the same units gives the scale factor. Angles and side ratios are unchanged by every enlargement.
