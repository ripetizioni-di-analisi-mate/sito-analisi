A ladder $5\,\mathrm m$ long leans against a vertical wall and makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the horizontal floor. How far from the wall is the foot of the ladder?

- $\dfrac{5\sqrt3}{2}\,\mathrm m$
- $10\,\mathrm m$
- $2.5\,\mathrm m$
- $5\sqrt3\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The ladder, the wall and the floor form a right triangle with the right angle at the foot of the wall, and the ladder is its hypotenuse. The distance of the foot from the wall is the leg adjacent to the $60^\circ$ angle, so it is obtained by multiplying the hypotenuse by the **cosine** of that angle. Since $\cos60^\circ=\dfrac12$, we obtain
$$
d=5\,\mathrm m\cdot\cos60^\circ=5\cdot\frac12\,\mathrm m=2.5\,\mathrm m.
$$
The value $\dfrac{5\sqrt3}{2}\,\mathrm m$ is the height reached on the wall, that is, the opposite leg, which is found with the sine. The value $10\,\mathrm m$ comes from dividing by the cosine instead of multiplying, while $5\sqrt3\,\mathrm m$ comes from multiplying by the tangent.
