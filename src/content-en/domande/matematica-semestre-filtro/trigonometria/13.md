A directed segment has Cartesian components $A_x=-2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $A_y=-2\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{m}$. Calculate its angle in $[0^\circ,360^\circ)$, measured counterclockwise from the positive $x$-axis.

- $60^\circ$
- $240^\circ$
- $120^\circ$
- $300^\circ$

## Explanation

Both components are negative, so the segment points left and downward and its direction lies in the third quadrant. The ratio of the component magnitudes is
$$
\frac{2\sqrt3}{2}=\sqrt3,
$$
so the acute reference angle is $\arctan\sqrt3=60^\circ$. In the third quadrant add a straight angle to obtain $180^\circ+60^\circ=240^\circ$. The value $60^\circ$ is just the reference angle and corresponds to two positive components.
