A vector of magnitude $10$ makes an angle of $180^\circ$ with the positive $x$-axis. Which ordered pair gives its components?

- $(10,0)$
- $(-10,0)$
- $(0,-10)$
- $(0,10)$

## Explanation

The components are $A_x=A\cos\theta$ and $A_y=A\sin\theta$. At $\theta=180^\circ$ the point on the unit circle is $(-1,0)$, so $\cos180^\circ=-1$ and $\sin180^\circ=0$. This gives
$$
(A_x,A_y)=\bigl(10\cdot(-1),\,10\cdot0\bigr)=(-10,0).
$$
The vector therefore lies on the negative $x$-axis. The pair $(10,0)$ corresponds to direction $0^\circ$ and the pair $(0,-10)$ to direction $270^\circ$.
