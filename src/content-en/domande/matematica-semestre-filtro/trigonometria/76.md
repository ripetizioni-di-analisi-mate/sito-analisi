A taut cable of length $25\,\mathrm m$ connects the ground to the top of a vertical pole. The anchor point is $7\,\mathrm m$ from the base of the pole and at the same elevation as the base. How tall is the pole?

- $18\,\mathrm m$
- $32\,\mathrm m$
- $20\,\mathrm m$
- $24\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The pole, ground, and cable form a right triangle with the right angle at the base of the pole. The cable is the hypotenuse and the distance from the anchor to the base is a leg, so by the **Pythagorean theorem**,
$$
h=\sqrt{25^2-7^2}\,\mathrm m=\sqrt{625-49}\,\mathrm m=\sqrt{576}\,\mathrm m=24\,\mathrm m.
$$
Subtracting the lengths without squaring them gives $18\,\mathrm m$, and adding them gives $32\,\mathrm m$.
