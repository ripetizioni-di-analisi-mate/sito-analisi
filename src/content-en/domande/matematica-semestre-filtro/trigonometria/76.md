An interior angle $\theta$ of a triangle is known only to satisfy $\sin\theta=\dfrac35$. Which pair gives both possible values of $\cos\theta$?

- $\left\{0,\dfrac45\right\}$
- $\left\{-\dfrac35,\dfrac35\right\}$
- $\left\{-1,1\right\}$
- $\left\{-\dfrac45,\dfrac45\right\}$

## Explanation

The identity $\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$ gives $\cos^2\theta=\dfrac{16}{25}$. Both the acute angle and its supplement can be interior angles of a triangle.
