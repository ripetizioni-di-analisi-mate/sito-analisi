A directed angle of $4\,\mathrm{rad}$ is measured counterclockwise from the positive $x$-axis. In which quadrant does its terminal side lie?

- First.
- Second.
- Third.
- Fourth.

## Explanation

The quadrants are separated by angles of $\dfrac{\pi}{2}\approx1.57$, $\pi\approx3.14$, and $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\approx4.71$ radians. Since $\pi<4<\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$, the terminal side lies in the **third quadrant**. In degrees the measure is
$$
4\cdot\frac{180^\circ}{\pi}\approx229^\circ,
$$
which lies between $180^\circ$ and $270^\circ$. Interpreting $4\,\mathrm{rad}$ as $4^\circ$ would place it in the first quadrant.
