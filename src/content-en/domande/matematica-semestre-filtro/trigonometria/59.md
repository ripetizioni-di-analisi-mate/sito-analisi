Two ramps have the same positive ratio of rise to horizontal run. One has twice the horizontal run of the other. How do the acute angles of the ramps compare?

- They are equal.
- The angle of the longer ramp is twice as large.
- The angle of the longer ramp is half as large.
- The angles differ by ninety degrees.

## Explanation

The ratio of rise to horizontal run is the tangent of the angle to the horizontal. Both ramps have the same ratio, so their angles have the same tangent. For acute angles, each positive tangent value corresponds to just one angle, so the angles **are equal**. The ramp with twice the run also has twice the rise, and the two triangles are similar. Doubling the angle confuses the length of a ramp with its inclination.
