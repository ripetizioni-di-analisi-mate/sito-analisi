A wheel rotates through $\dfrac{5\pi}{3}$ radians. What fraction of a revolution does this represent?

- $\dfrac56$
- $\dfrac53$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac35$

## Explanation

A full revolution measures $2\pi$ radians, so the fraction of a revolution is the angle of rotation divided by $2\pi$, or
$$
\frac{\frac{5\pi}{3}}{2\pi}=\frac{5\pi}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{2\pi}=\frac56.
$$
In degrees the rotation measures $300^\circ$, which is five-sixths of $360^\circ$. The value $\dfrac53$ comes from dividing by $\pi$, which corresponds to half a turn. The value $\dfrac16$ is the remaining fraction of a turn.
