An acute angle $\alpha$ determines the point $(a,b)$ on the unit circle. Which point corresponds to the angle $-\alpha$?

- $(a,-b)$
- $(-a,b)$
- $(-a,-b)$
- $(b,a)$

## Explanation

The angle $-\alpha$ is measured clockwise from the positive $x$-axis and has the same magnitude as $\alpha$. The two terminal sides are therefore reflections across the $x$-axis. This reflection leaves the horizontal coordinate unchanged and reverses the sign of the vertical coordinate, so the point is $(a,-b)$. Thus $\cos(-\alpha)=\cos\alpha$ and $\sin(-\alpha)=-\sin\alpha$. The point $(-a,b)$ corresponds to $180^\circ-\alpha$ and the point $(-a,-b)$ to $180^\circ+\alpha$.
