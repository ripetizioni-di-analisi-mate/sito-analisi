In a right triangle with hypotenuse $12\,\mathrm{cm}$, the leg opposite the acute angle $\alpha$ has length $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is $\alpha$?

- $30^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $90^\circ$

## Explanation

The sine of $\alpha$ is the opposite leg divided by the hypotenuse, so
$$
\sin\alpha=\frac{6}{12}=\frac12.
$$
The only acute angle with sine $\dfrac12$ is $30^\circ$, so $\alpha=30^\circ$. The value $60^\circ$ is the acute angle whose cosine is $\dfrac12$. This is the other acute angle of the triangle, adjacent to the leg of length $6\,\mathrm{cm}$.
