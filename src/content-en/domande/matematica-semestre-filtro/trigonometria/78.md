Which triple could give the side lengths of a right triangle?

- $4,\ 5,\ 6$
- $9,\ 12,\ 16$
- $6,\ 8,\ 11$
- $8,\ 15,\ 17$

## Explanation

In a right triangle the square of the longest side, the hypotenuse, is the sum of the squares of the other two sides. For the triple $8,\ 15,\ 17$, we have
$$
8^2+15^2=64+225=289=17^2,
$$
so the triangle is right. None of the other triples satisfies the equality. For example, $9^2+12^2=225$, whereas $16^2=256$. That triple would give a right triangle with $15$ in place of $16$.
