On a plane inclined at $60^\circ$, what is the ratio of the magnitude of the component of weight parallel to the plane to that of the perpendicular component?

- $\sqrt3$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$

## Explanation

The component magnitudes are $mg\sin\alpha$ parallel to the plane and $mg\cos\alpha$ perpendicular to it. In the ratio the factor $mg$ cancels, leaving
$$
\frac{mg\sin60^\circ}{mg\cos60^\circ}=\tan60^\circ=\sqrt3.
$$
The value $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}$ is the inverse ratio, perpendicular component to parallel component. The value $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ is the sine alone, without division by the cosine.
