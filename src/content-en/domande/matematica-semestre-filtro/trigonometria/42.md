A force in the first quadrant has horizontal component $5\sqrt3\,\mathrm{N}$ and makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the positive $x$-axis. What is its magnitude?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\sqrt3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\sqrt3\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The horizontal component is $F_x=F\cos30^\circ$, so divide the component by the cosine to find the magnitude:
$$
F=\frac{5\sqrt3\,\mathrm N}{\frac{\sqrt3}{2}}=5\sqrt3\cdot\frac{2}{\sqrt3}\,\mathrm N=10\,\mathrm N.
$$
The magnitude exceeds the component, just as the hypotenuse exceeds either leg. The value $5\,\mathrm N$ is the vertical component $F\sin30^\circ$, and $5\sqrt3\,\mathrm N$ is the horizontal component itself.
