A ray initially makes an angle of $170^\circ$ measured counterclockwise from the positive $x$-axis. It rotates counterclockwise through $110^\circ$. In which quadrant does it end?

- First.
- Second.
- Fourth.
- Third.

## Explanation

Counterclockwise rotations are positive, so add to obtain the final direction:
$$
170^\circ+110^\circ=280^\circ.
$$
Since $270^\circ<280^\circ<360^\circ$, the direction is in the **fourth quadrant**, where cosine is positive and sine is negative. Choosing the third quadrant overlooks the fact that $270^\circ$ separates it from the fourth. The first quadrant corresponds to the difference $170^\circ-110^\circ=60^\circ$, which treats the rotation as clockwise.
