In a right triangle the acute angle $\theta$ satisfies $\tan\theta=\dfrac34$. Calculate $\sin\theta$.

- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac45$
- $\dfrac53$

## Explanation

The tangent is the opposite leg divided by the adjacent leg, so take the leg lengths to be $3k$ and $4k$, with $k>0$. By the Pythagorean theorem, the hypotenuse is
$$
\sqrt{(3k)^2+(4k)^2}=\sqrt{25k^2}=5k.
$$
The sine is the opposite leg divided by the hypotenuse, so $\sin\theta=\dfrac{3k}{5k}=\dfrac35$. The value $\dfrac45$ is the cosine, and $\dfrac34$ is the tangent itself.
