For which angles $\theta$ in the interval $[0^\circ,360^\circ)$ is the tangent equal to $\sqrt3$?

- $\{60^\circ\}$
- $\{60^\circ,120^\circ\}$
- $\{60^\circ,240^\circ\}$
- $\{60^\circ,300^\circ\}$

## Explanation

In the first quadrant the angle with tangent $\sqrt3$ is $60^\circ$. Adding a straight angle changes the signs of both sine and cosine, so their quotient stays the same and the tangent repeats every $180^\circ$. Thus $60^\circ+180^\circ=240^\circ$ is also a solution, and there are no others in the interval. The set $\{60^\circ,120^\circ\}$ comes from using the rule $180^\circ-\theta$, which applies to sine. Indeed, $\tan120^\circ=-\sqrt3$.
