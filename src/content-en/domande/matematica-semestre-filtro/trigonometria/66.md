What is $\sin^2 20^\circ+\sin^2 70^\circ$?

- $2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $0$

## Explanation

The angles $20^\circ$ and $70^\circ$ are complementary, so $\sin70^\circ=\cos(90^\circ-70^\circ)=\cos20^\circ$. Substitution gives
$$
\sin^2 20^\circ+\sin^2 70^\circ=\sin^2 20^\circ+\cos^2 20^\circ=1
$$
by the Pythagorean identity. The approximate values $\sin^2 20^\circ\approx0.117$ and $\sin^2 70^\circ\approx0.883$ confirm the result. The same holds for any pair of complementary angles.
