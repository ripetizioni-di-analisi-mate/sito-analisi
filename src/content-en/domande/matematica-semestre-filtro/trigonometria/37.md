The percent grade of a ramp is one hundred times the ratio of its rise to its horizontal run. What is the percent grade of a ramp that rises at an angle of $30^\circ$?

- $\dfrac{100\sqrt3}{3}\%$
- $50\%$
- $100\sqrt3\%$
- $30\%$

## Explanation

The ratio of rise to horizontal run is the tangent of the angle to the horizontal, $\tan30^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}$. The percent grade is one hundred times this ratio, or
$$
100\cdot\frac{\sqrt3}{3}\%=\frac{100\sqrt3}{3}\%\approx57.7\%.
$$
The value $50\%$ comes from using $\sin30^\circ=\dfrac12$, the ratio of rise to ramp length. The value $30\%$ confuses the angle measure with the grade.
