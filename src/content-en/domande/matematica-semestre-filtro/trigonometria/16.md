A ramp rises $8\,\mathrm m$ over a horizontal distance of $11\,\mathrm m$. What is the tangent of its angle to the horizontal?

- $\dfrac{8}{11}$
- $1.375$
- $\dfrac{8}{19}$
- $\dfrac{11}{19}$

## Explanation

The tangent of the angle to the horizontal is the opposite leg, the rise, divided by the adjacent leg, the horizontal distance. Thus
$$
\tan\alpha=\frac{8\,\mathrm m}{11\,\mathrm m}=\frac{8}{11}.
$$
Inverting the ratio gives $1.375=\dfrac{11}{8}$. The value $\dfrac{8}{19}$ comes from dividing by the sum $8+11$, which differs from the length of the ramp. The ramp is the hypotenuse and has length $\sqrt{8^2+11^2}\,\mathrm m=\sqrt{185}\,\mathrm m$.
