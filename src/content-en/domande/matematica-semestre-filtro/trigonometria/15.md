From a balcony, a car parked on a level road is seen at an angle of depression of $30^\circ$. What is the angle of elevation of the balcony as seen from the car?

- $60^\circ$
- $120^\circ$
- $150^\circ$
- $30^\circ$

## Explanation

The angle of depression is the angle between the line of sight and the horizontal through the balcony. The angle of elevation is the angle between the same line of sight and the horizontal through the car. The two horizontals are parallel, and the line of sight crosses both. Thus the two angles are alternate interior angles and have the same measure, $30^\circ$. The value $60^\circ$ is the angle between the line of sight and the vertical.
