Which angle is larger, $1\,\mathrm{rad}$ or $60^\circ$?

- The angle of $1\,\mathrm{rad}$.
- The two angles are equal.
- The comparison depends on the radius of the circle.
- The angle of $60^\circ$.

## Explanation

Express the two angles in the same unit. Since $60^\circ=\dfrac{\pi}{3}\,\mathrm{rad}$ and $\pi>3$, we have $\dfrac{\pi}{3}>1$, so the angle of $60^\circ$ is larger. In degrees the same comparison follows from
$$
1\,\mathrm{rad}=\frac{180^\circ}{\pi}\approx57.3^\circ.
$$
Approximating $\pi$ by $3$ makes the two angles appear equal. Radian measure is the ratio of arc length to radius, so it is the same on every circle.
