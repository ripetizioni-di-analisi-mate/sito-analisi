The legs opposite and adjacent to an acute angle $\theta$ both have length $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. Calculate $\theta$.

- $30^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $45^\circ$

## Explanation

The **tangent** of $\theta$ is the ratio of the opposite leg to the adjacent leg. Both legs have length $8\,\mathrm{cm}$, so
$$
\tan\theta=\frac{8}{8}=1.
$$
The only acute angle with tangent $1$ is $45^\circ$, so $\theta=45^\circ$. The same result follows because the triangle is isosceles: its two acute angles are equal and sum to $90^\circ$. The value $90^\circ$ measures the right angle between the legs.
