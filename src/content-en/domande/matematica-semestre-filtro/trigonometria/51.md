Which acute angle $\theta$ satisfies $\sin\theta=\cos\theta$?

- $30^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $90^\circ$

## Explanation

The cosine is positive for an acute angle, so both sides of $\sin\theta=\cos\theta$ can be divided by $\cos\theta$. This gives
$$
\tan\theta=1.
$$
The only acute angle with tangent $1$ is $\theta=45^\circ$, the angle in a right triangle with equal legs. At $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$, sine and cosine have the values $\dfrac12$ and $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ in opposite orders, so they are unequal.
