If $\alpha=40^\circ$, which angle has sine equal to $\cos\alpha$ and measures less than $90^\circ$?

- $130^\circ$
- $40^\circ$
- $50^\circ$
- $140^\circ$

## Explanation

The acute angles of a right triangle are complementary, and the leg opposite one is adjacent to the other. It follows that $\cos\alpha=\sin(90^\circ-\alpha)$ for every acute angle $\alpha$. With $\alpha=40^\circ$, we obtain
$$
\cos40^\circ=\sin(90^\circ-40^\circ)=\sin50^\circ.
$$
The required acute angle is therefore $50^\circ$. The value $130^\circ$ results from adding $90^\circ$ instead of subtracting. The angle $140^\circ$ has the same sine as $40^\circ$, and that sine differs from $\cos40^\circ$.
