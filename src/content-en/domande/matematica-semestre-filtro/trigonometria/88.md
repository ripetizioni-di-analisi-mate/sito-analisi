The line of sight to the top of an object makes an acute angle $\alpha$ with the horizontal. The line-of-sight distance is $d$ and the rise is $h$. Which ratio equals $\dfrac hd$?

- $\sin\alpha$
- $\cos\alpha$
- $\tan\alpha$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sin\alpha}$

## Explanation

The line of sight from the observer to the top has length $d$ and is the hypotenuse of the right triangle. The rise $h$ is the leg opposite the angle of elevation $\alpha$. The ratio of the opposite leg to the hypotenuse is the sine, so
$$
\frac hd=\sin\alpha.
$$
To find the tangent, divide $h$ by the horizontal distance, the adjacent leg. The cosine is the horizontal distance divided by $d$.
