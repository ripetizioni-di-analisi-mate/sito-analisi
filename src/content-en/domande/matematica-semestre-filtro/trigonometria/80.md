A vector of magnitude $A$ points down and to the right. The acute angle $\alpha$ is measured from the downward vertical. Which pair gives the components for every $\alpha$, with $x$ pointing right and $y$ pointing up?

- $(A\cos\alpha,-A\sin\alpha)$
- $(-A\sin\alpha,A\cos\alpha)$
- $(A\sin\alpha,-A\cos\alpha)$
- $(A\sin\alpha,A\cos\alpha)$

## Explanation

The angle $\alpha$ is measured from the vertical, so the horizontal component is the leg opposite $\alpha$ and has magnitude $A\sin\alpha$. It points right, so $A_x=A\sin\alpha$. The vertical component is the adjacent leg and has magnitude $A\cos\alpha$. It points downward, opposite the positive $y$-axis, so $A_y=-A\cos\alpha$. The pair $(A\cos\alpha,-A\sin\alpha)$ treats $\alpha$ as if it were measured from the horizontal.
