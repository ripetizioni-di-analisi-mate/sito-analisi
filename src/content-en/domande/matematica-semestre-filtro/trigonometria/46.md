An acute angle satisfies $\sin\theta=\dfrac{5}{13}$. What is $\tan\theta$?

- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{12}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{13}$
- $\dfrac{12}{13}$

## Explanation

The identity $\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$ gives
$$
\cos^2\theta=1-\frac{25}{169}=\frac{144}{169}.
$$
The angle is acute, so its cosine is positive and equals $\dfrac{12}{13}$. The tangent is therefore
$$
\tan\theta=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}=\frac{\frac{5}{13}}{\frac{12}{13}}=\frac{5}{12}.
$$
Inverting the quotient gives $\dfrac{12}{5}$, which calculates $\dfrac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}$. The value $\dfrac{12}{13}$ is the cosine and $\dfrac{5}{13}$ is the sine itself.
