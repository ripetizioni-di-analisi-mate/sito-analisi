A quantity starts at $12$ units and triples every $9\,\mathrm{h}$. How many units are there after $18\,\mathrm{h}$?

- $36$
- $324$
- $108$
- $72$

## Explanation

The elapsed time $18\,\mathrm h$ contains $\dfrac{18}{9}=2$ tripling intervals. In each interval the quantity is multiplied by $3$, so the overall factor over two intervals is $3^2=9$.

$$
Q=12\cdot3^2=12\cdot9=108.
$$

The value $36$ corresponds to just one tripling. The value $72$ comes from multiplying by $3\cdot2$ instead of $3^2$. The value $324$ comes from using $3^3$, which corresponds to three intervals.
