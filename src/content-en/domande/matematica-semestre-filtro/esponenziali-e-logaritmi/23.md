Two light beams start with equal intensities. A filter transmits one third of the first beam, while the second reaches the detector in full. Intensities add. What fraction of the total initial light reaches the detector?

- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac16$

## Explanation

Let $I$ be the intensity of each beam. The detector receives $I/3+I=4I/3$ out of an initial $2I$, giving a fraction of $2/3$. Multiplying all the initial intensity by $1/3$ would incorrectly attenuate the unobstructed beam too.
