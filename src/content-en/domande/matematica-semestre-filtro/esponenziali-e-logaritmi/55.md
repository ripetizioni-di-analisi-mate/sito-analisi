Two sound sources produce levels of $20$ and $50\,\mathrm{dB}$ relative to the same $I_0$, and their intensities add. What fraction of the total intensity comes from the more intense source?

- $\dfrac{1\,000}{1\,001}$
- $\dfrac{50}{70}$
- $\dfrac{1}{1\,001}$
- $\dfrac{10}{11}$

## Explanation

The two levels are relative to the same $I_0$, so the ratios to the reference intensity are

$$
\frac{I_1}{I_0}=10^{2},\qquad \frac{I_2}{I_0}=10^{5}.
$$

The more intense contribution is therefore $10^3$ times the other. The intensities add, so its share is $\dfrac{10^5}{10^2+10^5}=\dfrac{1000}{1001}$. The value $\dfrac{50}{70}$ comes from dividing the levels, which are logarithmic quantities. The value $\dfrac{1}{1001}$ is the share of the less intense contribution.
