A quantity halves every $5\,\mathrm{h}$. What percentage of its initial value remains after $10\,\mathrm{h}$?

- $25\%$
- $50\%$
- $75\%$
- $12.5\%$

## Explanation

The elapsed time comprises $\dfrac{10\,\mathrm h}{5\,\mathrm h}=2$ half-lives. In each interval the quantity is multiplied by $\dfrac12$, so the factors multiply over two intervals.

$$
\frac{N}{N_0}=\left(\frac12\right)^2=\frac14=25\%.
$$

The value $50\%$ is the amount remaining after just one half-life. The value $75\%$ is the fraction lost. The value $12.5\%$ corresponds to three half-lives.
