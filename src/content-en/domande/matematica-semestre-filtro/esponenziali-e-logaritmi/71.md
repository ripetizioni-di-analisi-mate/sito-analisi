Using $\log_{10}(2)\approx0.30$, estimate $\log_{10}(4\cdot10^6)$.

- $6.60$
- $6.30$
- $5.40$
- $24$

## Explanation

The product rule splits the logarithm into two terms. Since $4=2^2$, the power rule gives $\log_{10}4=2\log_{10}2$.

$$
\log_{10}(4\cdot10^6)=2\log_{10}2+6\approx0.60+6=6.60.
$$

The result lies between $6$ and $7$ because the number lies between $10^6$ and $10^7$. The value $6.30$ uses $\log_{10}2$ in place of $\log_{10}4$. The value $24$ comes from multiplying $4$ by $6$.
