A calculator rounds any positive number below $10^{-99}$ down to zero. You need the decimal logarithm of $10^{-70}\cdot10^{-50}$. Which procedure avoids forming the tiny product?

- Add the two logarithms to obtain $-120$.
- Form the product first and take the logarithm of zero.
- Multiply the two logarithms to obtain $3500$.
- Subtract the exponents to obtain $-20$.

## Explanation

Both factors are positive and can be represented. Take their logarithms separately to get $-70$ and $-50$, then add them using the product rule. The result $-120$ can be represented even though the product $10^{-120}$ is lost.
