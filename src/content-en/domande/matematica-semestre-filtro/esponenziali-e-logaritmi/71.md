Let $a$ and $b$ be positive numbers with $\log_{10}a=-70.2$ and $\log_{10}b=-49.5$. Between which powers of ten does the product $ab$ lie?

- $10^{-120}<ab<10^{-119}$
- $10^{-119}<ab<10^{-118}$
- $10^{-21}<ab<10^{-20}$
- $10^{3474}<ab<10^{3475}$

## Explanation

Since $a$ and $b$ are positive, the product rule for logarithms holds, so $\log_{10}(ab)=-70.2+(-49.5)=-119.7$. The number $-119.7$ lies between $-120$ and $-119$. Since the function $y=10^x$ is increasing, it follows that $10^{-120}<ab<10^{-119}$. The interval between $10^{-119}$ and $10^{-118}$ comes from taking $-119$ as the integer immediately below $-119.7$, whereas $-119$ is greater. The exponents $-21$ and $-20$ come from subtracting the logarithms, and so they correspond to the quotient $\dfrac{a}{b}$. The positive exponents come from multiplying the logarithms instead of adding them.
