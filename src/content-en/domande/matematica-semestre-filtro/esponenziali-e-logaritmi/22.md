A sound intensity $I$ corresponds to a level of $-10\,\mathrm{dB}$ relative to $I_0$. Calculate $\dfrac{I}{I_0}$.

- $10$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{100}$
- $-10$

## Explanation

Divide the level by ten to obtain the logarithm of the ratio.

$$
\log_{10}\left(\frac{I}{I_0}\right)=\frac{-10}{10}=-1,\qquad \frac{I}{I_0}=10^{-1}=\frac1{10}.
$$

A negative level thus indicates a positive intensity below the reference. The value $-10$ cannot be an intensity ratio because the intensities are positive. The exponent in $10$ has the wrong sign.
