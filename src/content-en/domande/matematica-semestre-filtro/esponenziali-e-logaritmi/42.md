Two incoherent sources each produce a level of $40\,\mathrm{dB}$ at the measurement point when operating alone. Their intensities add. Using $\log_{10}(2)\approx0.30$, estimate the level when both are active.

- $80\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $46\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $40\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $43\,\mathrm{dB}$

## Explanation

In the model for incoherent sources, the intensities add. Two identical sources therefore give twice the intensity, and the level increases by $10\log_{10}2\,\mathrm{dB}$.

$$
L=40\,\mathrm{dB}+10\log_{10}2\,\mathrm{dB}\approx40\,\mathrm{dB}+3\,\mathrm{dB}=43\,\mathrm{dB}.
$$

The value $80\,\mathrm{dB}$ comes from adding the levels, which are logarithmic measures of the intensities. The value $46\,\mathrm{dB}$ uses a coefficient of twenty. That coefficient applies to the logarithm of an amplitude ratio when the intensity ratio equals its square. The value $40\,\mathrm{dB}$ overlooks the second source.
