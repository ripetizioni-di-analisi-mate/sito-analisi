For integer $n\ge 0$, two quantities have values $A_n=3\cdot2^n$ and $B_n=12\cdot\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^n$. For which $n$ are they equal?

- $n=2$
- $n=3$
- $n=1$
- $n=4$

## Explanation

Set $A_n=B_n$ and write $\left(\dfrac12\right)^n=2^{-n}$. Multiplying both sides by $2^n$ and dividing by $3$ gives

$$
2^n\cdot2^n=\frac{12}{3},\qquad 2^{2n}=4=2^2.
$$

The base-two exponential function is strictly increasing, so $2n=2$ and $n=1$. For $n=1$, both quantities are $6$. The value $n=2$ comes from $2^n=4$, overlooking the fact that the product $2^n\cdot2^n$ equals $2^{2n}$.
