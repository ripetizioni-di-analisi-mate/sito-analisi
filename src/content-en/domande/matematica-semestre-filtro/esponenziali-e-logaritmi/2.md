Calculate $\log_{10}(10^{-5})$.

- $5$
- $-\frac{1}{5}$
- $-5$
- $-6$

## Explanation

By definition, $\log_{10}(10^{-5})$ is the exponent $u$ such that $10^u=10^{-5}$. Since the function $10^u$ is strictly increasing, the only possible exponent is $u=-5$.

$$
\log_{10}(10^{-5})=-5.
$$

The argument $10^{-5}=0.00001$ is positive and less than one, so the logarithm exists and is negative. The value $5$ has the wrong sign. The value $-\dfrac15$ results from confusing the additive inverse with the reciprocal.
