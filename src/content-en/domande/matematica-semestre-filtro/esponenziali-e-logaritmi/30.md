A model has an unknown exponent $x$ and base $3$. All that is known about the measurement is $0<3^x<1$. What can be deduced about $x$ without the exact reading?

- $x>0$
- $x<0$
- $x=0$
- $x<-1$

## Explanation

The function $3^x$ is increasing and equals one at $x=0$. Every value between zero and one comes from a negative exponent. For instance, $x=-1/2$ fits the reading, so the stronger restriction $x<-1$ cannot be inferred.
