A signed measurement is encoded as $L(x)=\log_2(x^2)$ for $x\ne0$. Which formula preserves the value of $L$ even for negative readings?

- $\log_2(2|x|)$
- $2\log_2|x|$
- $2\log_2 x$
- $|\log_2 x|$

## Explanation

Since $x^2=|x|^2$ and $|x|>0$ for $x\ne0$, the power rule applies to the positive base inside the logarithm. This gives $2\log_2|x|$. For example, $x=-4$ gives $4$ in both equivalent formulas.
