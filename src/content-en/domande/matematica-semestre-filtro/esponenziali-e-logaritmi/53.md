Solve $\log_2(x^2)=4$ over the real numbers.

- $x=4$ only
- $x=-4$ or $x=4$
- $x=-16$ or $x=16$
- $x=-2$ or $x=2$

## Explanation

The domain requires $x^2>0$, or $x\ne0$. By the definition of a logarithm, the equation is equivalent to

$$
x^2=2^4=16,\qquad x=-4\quad\text{or}\quad x=4.
$$

Both values are nonzero and give the argument $16$, so both are solutions. The answer with only $x=4$ comes from writing $\log_2(x^2)=2\log_2 x$, which holds only for $x>0$. The answer with $\pm16$ comes from $x=16$, omitting the square.
