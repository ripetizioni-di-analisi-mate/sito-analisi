A measured value is described by $M(t)=30+10\cdot2^{-t}$, where $t$ is the numerical value of time in hours. After how many hours does it reach $M=35$?

- $2\,\mathrm{h}$
- $5\,\mathrm{h}$
- $1\,\mathrm{h}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{h}$

## Explanation

Isolate the time-dependent part by subtracting the background value $30$.

$$
10\cdot2^{-t}=35-30=5,\qquad 2^{-t}=\frac12=2^{-1},\qquad t=1.
$$

Thus the excess above the background halves in one hour, and the value $35$ is reached after $1\,\mathrm h$. The value $\dfrac12\,\mathrm h$ confuses the factor $\dfrac12$ with the time. The value $5\,\mathrm h$ treats the remaining excess as if it were the time.
