Solve $\ln x=2\ln 3-\ln 2$.

- $4$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $3$
- $7$

## Explanation

Write the right-hand side as a single logarithm. The power rule gives $2\ln 3=\ln 9$, and the quotient rule gives $\ln 9-\ln 2=\ln\dfrac92$.

$$
\ln x=\ln\left(\frac92\right),\qquad x=\frac92.
$$

The natural logarithm is one-to-one, so arguments with the same logarithm are equal. The value $4$ computes $2\cdot3-2$ using the arguments without logarithms. The value $7$ comes from $9-2$, treating the difference of the logarithms as the difference of their arguments.
