Two positive readings $A,B$ have been stored as $u=\ln A$ and $v=\ln B$. Which expression recovers the difference $A-B$?

- $u-v$
- $e^u-e^v$
- $e^{u-v}$
- $\ln(u-v)$

## Explanation

Recover each reading by applying the inverse function to its stored value, giving $A=e^u$ and $B=e^v$. Subtracting yields $e^u-e^v$. Subtracting the exponents first would recover the ratio $A/B$.
