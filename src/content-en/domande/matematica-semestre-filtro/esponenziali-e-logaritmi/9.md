Solve $\left(\dfrac13\right)^{2x-1}=27$.

- $x=2$
- $x=-2$
- $x=1$
- $x=-1$

## Explanation

Write the base and the right-hand side as powers of three. Since $\dfrac13=3^{-1}$, the left-hand side becomes $3^{-(2x-1)}=3^{1-2x}$, while $27=3^3$. The function $3^u$ is strictly increasing, so the exponents must be equal.

$$
1-2x=3,\qquad x=-1.
$$

The value $x=2$ comes from $2x-1=3$, which omits the sign change in the exponent due to the base $\dfrac13$.
