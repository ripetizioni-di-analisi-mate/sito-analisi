For every $A>0$, calculate $\log_{10}(0.01A)-\log_{10}(A)$.

- $-2$
- $2$
- $0.01$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The difference of two logarithms with the same base is the logarithm of the quotient, in which the factor $A$ cancels.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\log_{10}(0.01A)-\log_{10}A&=\log_{10}\left(\frac{0.01A}{A}\right)\\[6pt]
&=\log_{10}(10^{-2})=-2.
\end{aligned}
$$

The result therefore does not depend on $A$. The value $2$ has the wrong sign, since an argument less than one gives a negative logarithm for this base. The value $0.01$ is the quotient before taking the logarithm.
