Calculate $\log_2(512)-\log_2(128)$.

- $4$
- $2$
- $9$
- $16$

## Explanation

The difference of two logarithms with the same base is the logarithm of the quotient of their arguments.

$$
\log_2 512-\log_2 128=\log_2\left(\frac{512}{128}\right)=\log_2 4=2.
$$

The same value follows from $\log_2 512=9$ and $\log_2 128=7$. The value $4$ is the quotient of the arguments before taking the logarithm. The value $16$ comes from adding the logarithms $9$ and $7$ instead of subtracting them.
