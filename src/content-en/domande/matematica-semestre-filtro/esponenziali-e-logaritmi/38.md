Solve $9^x=27^{x-1}$.

- $x=-3$
- $x=1$
- $x=\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $x=3$

## Explanation

Both bases are powers of three, since $9=3^2$ and $27=3^3$. The rule for a power raised to a power gives

$$
3^{2x}=3^{3(x-1)}=3^{3x-3}.
$$

The function $3^u$ is strictly increasing, so the exponents are equal, giving $2x=3x-3$ and hence $x=3$. The value $x=1$ comes from writing $3x-1$ instead of $3x-3$, multiplying only the first term of the exponent by three.
