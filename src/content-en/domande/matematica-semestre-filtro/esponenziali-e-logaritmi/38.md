Two instruments return $9^t$ and $27^{t-1}$. Their readings agree at $t=3$. What happens to the ratio of the second reading to the first whenever $t$ increases by one?

- It is multiplied by $18$.
- It is divided by $3$.
- It stays equal to $1$.
- It is multiplied by $3$.

## Explanation

At each step the numerator of the ratio grows by a factor of $27$ and the denominator by a factor of $9$. Their quotient therefore grows by $27/9=3$. Agreement at one instant does not imply agreement one step later.
