To illustrate $\log_a(3)=1/2$, a student draws a square with side length $3$. Which quantity from the diagram should she choose for the base $a$?

- The diagonal, $3\sqrt2$.
- The side length, $3$.
- The area, $9$.
- The perimeter, $12$.

## Explanation

The equation means $a^{1/2}=3$. The area is the positive number whose square root equals the side length, so $a=3^2=9$. The perimeter has a different relationship with the side length.
