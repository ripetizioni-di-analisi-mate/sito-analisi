Calculate $\log_9(3)$.

- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

By definition, $\log_9 3$ is the exponent $u$ such that $9^u=3$. Since $9=3^2$, the equation becomes $3^{2u}=3^1$, so

$$
2u=1,\qquad u=\frac12.
$$

In other words, $9^{\frac12}=\sqrt9=3$. The value $2$ is $\log_3 9$, the logarithm with its base and argument interchanged. The value $\dfrac13$ results from dividing the argument by the base. The value $-\dfrac12$ has the wrong sign, because $9^{-\frac12}=\dfrac13$.
