A quantity decays according to $N(t)=N_0e^{-kt}$ with $k=0.2\,\mathrm{h^{-1}}$. Using $\ln 2\approx0.69$, estimate the half-life.

- $0.14\,\mathrm h$
- $3.45\,\mathrm h$
- $5\,\mathrm h$
- $2.5\,\mathrm h$

## Explanation

The half-life is the time after which $e^{-kT_{\frac12}}=\dfrac12$. Taking the natural logarithm of both sides gives $kT_{\frac12}=\ln 2$.

$$
T_{\frac12}=\frac{\ln 2}{k}\approx\frac{0.69}{0.2\,\mathrm{h^{-1}}}=3.45\,\mathrm h.
$$

The value $0.14\,\mathrm h$ results from multiplying $\ln 2$ by $k$ instead of dividing. The value $5\,\mathrm h$ equals $\dfrac1k$, the time in which the quantity decreases by a factor of $e$. The value $2.5\,\mathrm h$ equals $\dfrac{1}{2k}$.
