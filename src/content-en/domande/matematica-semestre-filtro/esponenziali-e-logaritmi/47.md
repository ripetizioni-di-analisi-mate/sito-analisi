Calculate $\log_2(3)\cdot\log_3(16)$.

- $4$
- $16$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

Use the change-of-base formula to rewrite $\log_3 16$ in base two.

$$
\log_2 3\cdot\log_3 16=\log_2 3\cdot\frac{\log_2 16}{\log_2 3}=\log_2 16=4.
$$

The factor $\log_2 3$ is nonzero, so we may cancel it. The result $4$ follows from $2^4=16$. The value $16$ is the argument of the logarithm instead of its value.
