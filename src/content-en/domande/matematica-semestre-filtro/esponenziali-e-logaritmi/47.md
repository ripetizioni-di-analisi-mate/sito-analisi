A conversion chain contains $\log_2 3$, $\log_3 5$ and $\log_5 2$. Which result checks that the three factors have been multiplied correctly?

- $1$
- $\log_2 30$
- $0$
- $10$

## Explanation

Using change of base, the product becomes $(\ln3/\ln2)(\ln5/\ln3)(\ln2/\ln5)$. The factors cancel in pairs. The chain ends in the same base in which it started and the product is one.
