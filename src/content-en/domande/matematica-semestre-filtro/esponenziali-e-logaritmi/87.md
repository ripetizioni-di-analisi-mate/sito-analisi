Which line is a horizontal asymptote of the graph of $y=2+3e^{-x}$ as $x\to+\infty$?

- $y=5$
- $y=3$
- $y=0$
- $y=2$

## Explanation

As $x\to+\infty$, the exponent $-x$ tends to $-\infty$, so $e^{-x}$ tends to zero. Therefore $3e^{-x}$ also tends to zero and

$$
\lim_{x\to+\infty}\left(2+3e^{-x}\right)=2.
$$

The line $y=2$ is therefore the horizontal asymptote. The value $y=5$ is the vertical coordinate of the point with horizontal coordinate zero, since $e^0=1$. The value $y=0$ is the asymptote of $3e^{-x}$ without the vertical shift. The value $y=3$ confuses the coefficient with the shift.
