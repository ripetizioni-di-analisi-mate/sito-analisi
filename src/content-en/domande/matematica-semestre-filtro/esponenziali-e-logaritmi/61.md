What is the vertical asymptote of the graph of $y=3+\log_2(x-2)$?

- $x=3$
- $x=0$
- $x=2$
- $x=-2$

## Explanation

The logarithm $\log_2(x-2)$ is defined for $x>2$ and tends to $-\infty$ as its argument approaches zero from the right, that is, as $x\to2^+$. The line $x=2$ is therefore a vertical asymptote. The graph of $y=3+\log_2(x-2)$ is obtained by shifting the graph of $y=\log_2 x$ by $2$ to the right and $3$ upward. Only the horizontal shift moves the vertical asymptote. The value $x=3$ applies the vertical shift to the asymptote.
