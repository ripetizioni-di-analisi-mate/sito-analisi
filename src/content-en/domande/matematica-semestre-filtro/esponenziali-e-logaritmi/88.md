For a positive quantity the measured relation is $\log_{10}\left(\dfrac{Q(t)}{Q_0}\right)=-0.25t$, where $t$ is the numerical value of time in hours. What fraction of $Q_0$ remains after eight hours?

- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $100$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{100}$

## Explanation

The logarithm of the ratio is a linear function of time. At eight hours it equals

$$
\log_{10}\left(\frac{Q(8)}{Q_0}\right)=-0.25\cdot8=-2,\qquad \frac{Q(8)}{Q_0}=10^{-2}=\frac{1}{100}.
$$

Convert the logarithm to the ratio by taking a power of ten, because the base-ten logarithm is the inverse of $10^x$. The exponent in $100$ has the wrong sign. The value $\dfrac{1}{10}$ corresponds to four hours. The value $\dfrac12$ confuses the logarithm with the ratio.
