The pH changes from $5$ to $7$. Determine the ratio of final to initial hydrogen ion activity.

- $100$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{100}$

## Explanation

Subtracting the two pH values and using the logarithm of a quotient gives the ratio of the activities.

$$
\frac{a_2}{a_1}=10^{-(\mathrm{pH}_2-\mathrm{pH}_1)}=10^{-(7-5)}=\frac{1}{100}.
$$

The pH has increased, so the hydrogen ion activity has decreased and the ratio is less than one. The value $100$ is the reciprocal ratio, initial to final activity. The value $\dfrac57$ comes from dividing the pH values, which are logarithmic measures of the activities.
