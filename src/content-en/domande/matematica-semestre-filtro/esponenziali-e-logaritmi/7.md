The final sound intensity is $1000$ times the initial intensity. By how many decibels does the sound intensity level increase?

- $3\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $60\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $30\,\mathrm{dB}$

## Explanation

The change in level depends only on the ratio of final to initial intensity because the reference $I_0$ cancels in the difference of the two logarithms.

$$
\Delta L=10\log_{10}\left(\frac{I_2}{I_1}\right)\,\mathrm{dB}=10\log_{10}(10^3)\,\mathrm{dB}=30\,\mathrm{dB}.
$$

The value $3\,\mathrm{dB}$ omits the factor of ten. The value $60\,\mathrm{dB}$ uses the coefficient of twenty, which applies to a distance ratio when intensity follows an inverse-square law.
