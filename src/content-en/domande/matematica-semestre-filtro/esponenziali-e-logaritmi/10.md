Determine the real domain of $\log_2(x-1)+\log_2(7-x)$.

- $(1,7)$
- $[1,7]$
- $(-\infty,1)\cup(7,+\infty)$
- $(1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Each logarithm requires a strictly positive argument, and the sum is defined only where both terms are defined. The two conditions must therefore hold simultaneously.

$$
\begin{cases}
x-1>0\\
7-x>0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad 1<x<7.
$$

The closed interval $[1,7]$ includes the endpoints, where one of the arguments is zero. The interval $(1,+\infty)$ takes only the first term into account. The union $(-\infty,1)\cup(7,+\infty)$ is the set where the arguments have opposite signs.
