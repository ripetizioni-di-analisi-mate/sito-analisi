A sound intensity level increases by $7\,\mathrm{dB}$. Using $\log_{10}2\approx0.30$, estimate the factor by which the intensity is multiplied.

- $7$
- $5$
- $\sqrt5$
- $70$

## Explanation

An increase in level of $\Delta L$ corresponds to the intensity ratio $10^{\frac{\Delta L}{10\,\mathrm{dB}}}$, so here the factor is $10^{0.7}$. Write $0.7=1-0.30$ and use $10^{0.30}\approx2$.

$$
\frac{I_2}{I_1}=10^{0.7}=\frac{10^1}{10^{0.30}}\approx\frac{10}{2}=5.
$$

Indeed, an increase of $10\,\mathrm{dB}$ multiplies the intensity by $10$, while a decrease of $3\,\mathrm{dB}$ divides it by $2$. The value $\sqrt5$ results from dividing the decibel change by twenty instead of ten. The value $7$ confuses decibels with the factor, and $70$ multiplies the decibel change by ten.
