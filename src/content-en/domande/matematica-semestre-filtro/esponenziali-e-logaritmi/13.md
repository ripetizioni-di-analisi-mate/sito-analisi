At a listening point the level rises from $38\,\mathrm{dB}$ to $58\,\mathrm{dB}$ with the same reference. By what factor has the intensity increased?

- $2$
- $100$
- $10\,000$
- $20$

## Explanation

The difference in levels is $58-38=20$ decibels. Since $20=10\log_{10}(I_f/I_i)$, the logarithm of the intensity ratio is $2$. Raising ten to this power gives $I_f/I_i=10^2=100$.
