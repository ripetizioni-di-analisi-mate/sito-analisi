If $0<a<1$ and $x$ is real, which inequality holds for every $x$?

- $a^{x+1}>a^x$
- $a^{x+1}=a^x$
- $a^{x+1}<a^x$
- $a^{x+1}<0$

## Explanation

By the rules for powers, $a^{x+1}=a\cdot a^x$. The power $a^x$ is positive for every real $x$, and multiplying a positive number by a factor $a$ between zero and one gives a smaller number.

$$
a^{x+1}=a\cdot a^x<a^x.
$$

This expresses the fact that an exponential function with a base less than one is strictly decreasing. The inequality $a^{x+1}<0$ is false because every power with a positive base is positive.
