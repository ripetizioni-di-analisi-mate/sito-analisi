The graph of $y=3^x$ contains the point $(4,81)$. Which point belongs to the graph of the inverse function $y=\log_3(x)$?

- $(4,81)$
- $(81,4)$
- $(-4,81)$
- $(81,-4)$

## Explanation

The function $y=\log_3 x$ is the inverse of $y=3^x$, so a point $(x,y)$ on the graph of the exponential corresponds to the point $(y,x)$ on the graph of the logarithm. The point $(4,81)$ therefore gives $(81,4)$. A direct check gives $\log_3 81=4$ because $3^4=81$. The point $(4,81)$ belongs to the graph of the exponential. The vertical coordinate of $(81,-4)$ has the wrong sign because $\log_3 81$ is positive given that $81>1$.
