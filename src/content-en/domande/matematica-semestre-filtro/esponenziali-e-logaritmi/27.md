Which of the following numbers is negative?

- $\log_2 3$
- $\log_{\frac12} 3$
- $\log_{\frac12}\left(\dfrac13\right)$
- $\log_3 5$

## Explanation

The sign of a logarithm depends on both the base and the argument. For a base greater than one, the logarithm is positive for arguments greater than one, so $\log_2 3$ and $\log_3 5$ are positive. With base $\dfrac12$, the logarithm is decreasing and equals zero at $1$. Therefore $\log_{\frac12}3<0$ because the argument is greater than one. In contrast, $\log_{\frac12}\left(\dfrac13\right)$ is positive because the argument is less than one. Indeed, $\log_{\frac12}\left(\dfrac13\right)=\log_2 3$.
