A quantity is $64$ units at time zero and $32$ units after three minutes. If exponential decay continues at the same rate, which third measurement is consistent with the model after three more minutes?

- $0$ units
- $20$ units
- $24$ units
- $16$ units

## Explanation

In exponential decay, equal time intervals give the same factor. Over the first three minutes the factor is

$$
\frac{32}{64}=\frac12,
$$

so in the next three minutes the quantity halves again, becoming $\dfrac{32}{2}=16$ units. The value $0$ units results from a linear decrease that subtracts another $32$ units.
