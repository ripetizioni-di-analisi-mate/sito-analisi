A machine multiplies a signal by $2$ in the first hour and by $8$ in the second. What constant hourly factor would produce the same final value after two hours?

- $16$
- $5$
- $8$
- $4$

## Explanation

Over two hours the actual factor is $2\cdot8=16$. A constant factor $g>0$ must satisfy $g^2=16$, giving $g=4$. The arithmetic mean of the factors would give five, which would multiply the signal by twenty-five over the two hours.
