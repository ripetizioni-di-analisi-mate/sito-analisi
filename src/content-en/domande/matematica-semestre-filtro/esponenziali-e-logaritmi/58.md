A timer is reset two hours after observations start. The original law is $Q(t)=5\cdot3^t$, where $t$ is in hours. Which law uses the time $s$ since the reset?

- $Q=7\cdot3^s$
- $Q=45\cdot3^s$
- $Q=5\cdot9^s$
- $Q=5\cdot3^{s-2}$

## Explanation

When the new timer reads $s=0$, the old time is $t=2$. Thus $t=s+2$. Substitution gives $5\cdot3^{s+2}=(5\cdot9)3^s$. The starting reading changes while the hourly growth factor stays at three.
