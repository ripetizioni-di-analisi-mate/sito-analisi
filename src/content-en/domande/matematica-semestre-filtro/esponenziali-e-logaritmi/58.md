A growth model is given by $Q(t)=Q_0\cdot2^t$, where $t$ is the numerical value of time in hours. The measured value is $Q(2)=40$ units. Calculate $Q_0$.

- $20$ units
- $10$ units
- $80$ units
- $160$ units

## Explanation

In the model $Q(t)=Q_0\cdot2^t$, the initial value is multiplied by $2^2=4$ at time $t=2$. Therefore dividing the measurement by this factor gives $Q_0$.

$$
4Q_0=40,\qquad Q_0=10\ \text{units}.
$$

The value $20$ units results from dividing by $2$, counting only one doubling. The value $160$ units comes from multiplying by $4$ instead of dividing, as if $40$ were the initial value. The value $80$ units results from multiplying by $2$.
