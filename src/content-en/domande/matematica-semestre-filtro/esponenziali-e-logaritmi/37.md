Calculate $\log_2(4)+\log_2(32)$.

- $7$
- $3$
- $10$
- $8$

## Explanation

The arguments are powers of two, so each logarithm equals the exponent. We have $\log_2 4=2$ because $2^2=4$, and $\log_2 32=5$ because $2^5=32$.

$$
\log_2 4+\log_2 32=2+5=7.
$$

The same result follows from the product rule, since $\log_2(4\cdot32)=\log_2 128=7$. The value $10$ is the product of the exponents. The value $3$ is their difference.
