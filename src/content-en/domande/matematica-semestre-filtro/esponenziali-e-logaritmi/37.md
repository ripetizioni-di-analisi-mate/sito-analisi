A classmate claims that $\log_2(u+v)=\log_2 u+\log_2 v$ for all positive $u,v$. Which pair is a counterexample?

- $(1,1)$
- $(3,3/2)$
- $(5,5/4)$
- $(2,2)$

## Explanation

The right side equals $\log_2(uv)$, so equality requires $u+v=uv$. For $(1,1)$ the two sides of the original equation equal $1$ and $0$, respectively. In each of the other three pairs the sum happens to equal the product.
