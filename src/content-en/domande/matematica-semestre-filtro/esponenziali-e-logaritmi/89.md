Calculate $\log_2\sqrt[3]{16}$.

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $12$

## Explanation

A cube root is a power with exponent $\dfrac13$, and $16=2^4$. The power rule gives

$$
\log_2\sqrt[3]{16}=\log_2\left(2^{\frac43}\right)=\frac43\log_2 2=\frac43.
$$

The value $\dfrac34$ confuses the index of the root with the exponent of $2$. The value $\dfrac{16}{3}$ divides the argument by three instead of dividing its logarithm. The value $12$ multiplies the exponent $4$ by the index $3$ instead of dividing.
