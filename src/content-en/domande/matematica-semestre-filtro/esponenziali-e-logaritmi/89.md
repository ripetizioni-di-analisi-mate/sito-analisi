An attenuation law gives $\ln T(d)=-kd$, where $T$ is the transmitted fraction, $d>0$ is thickness and $k>0$. Two layers of thickness $d_1$ and $d_2$ obey the same law with the same $k$. How is the total transmitted fraction obtained?

- $T(d_1)/T(d_2)$
- $T(d_1)+T(d_2)$
- $T(d_1)\,T(d_2)$
- $\dfrac{T(d_1)+T(d_2)}{2}$

## Explanation

Thicknesses add, so $\ln T(d_1+d_2)=-kd_1-kd_2=\ln T(d_1)+\ln T(d_2)$. Applying the exponential gives the product of the transmitted fractions. Only the light that passed through the first layer reaches the second.
