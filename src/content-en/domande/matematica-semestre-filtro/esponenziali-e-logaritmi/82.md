In an idealized model, equal volumes of two dilute solutions of a strong monoprotic acid with pH values of $2$ and $4$ are mixed. Assume complete dissociation and a negligible contribution from water, so the hydronium concentration of the mixture is the mean of the two concentrations. Using $\log_{10}2\approx0.30$, estimate the pH of the mixture.

- $3.00$
- $2.00$
- $2.30$
- $1.70$

## Explanation

The concentrations are $10^{-2}$ and $10^{-4}\,\mathrm{mol/L}$, and their mean is

$$
c=\frac{10^{-2}+10^{-4}}{2}\,\mathrm{mol/L}=5.05\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{mol/L}\approx\frac{10^{-2}}{2}\,\mathrm{mol/L}.
$$

Therefore $\mathrm{pH}\approx-\log_{10}(10^{-2})+\log_{10}2\approx2+0.30=2.30$. The value $3.00$ is the arithmetic mean of the pH values, which does not correspond to the mean of the concentrations. The value $1.70$ subtracts $\log_{10}2$ instead of adding it. The value $2.00$ omits the division by two.
