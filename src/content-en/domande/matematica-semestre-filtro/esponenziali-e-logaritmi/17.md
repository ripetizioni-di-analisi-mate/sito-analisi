An archive contains $70$ cards. To identify one card, you may ask questions with two possible answers, each time splitting the remaining cards into two groups as equal in size as possible. How many questions are needed in the worst case with this strategy?

- $35$
- $6$
- $7$
- $8$

## Explanation

Six binary answers distinguish at most $2^6=64$ possible answer sequences, fewer than the $70$ cards. Seven allow $2^7=128$. With balanced splits, the group sizes in the worst case are $70,35,18,9,5,3,2,1$. This takes seven questions, the smallest integer greater than or equal to $\log_2 70$.
