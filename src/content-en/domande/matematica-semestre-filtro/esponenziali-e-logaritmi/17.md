Calculate $\log_4(512)$.

- $9$
- $4$
- $\dfrac92$
- $5$

## Explanation

The base and argument are powers of two, because $4=2^2$ and $512=2^9$. Setting $u=\log_4 512$, the definition gives $4^u=512$.

$$
2^{2u}=2^9,\qquad 2u=9,\qquad u=\frac92.
$$

The value $9$ equals $\log_2 512$, the logarithm calculated to base two. To change to base four, we must divide by $\log_2 4=2$, as the change-of-base formula shows.
