Let $r$ be a positive real number and let $D=\log_{10}r$. What is $\log_{10}\dfrac{1}{r}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{D}$
- $-D$
- $10-D$
- $D-1$

## Explanation

The reciprocal of $r$ is written as a power with exponent $-1$, that is $\dfrac{1}{r}=r^{-1}$. By the power rule for logarithms $\log_{10}r^{-1}=-\log_{10}r$, so $\log_{10}\dfrac{1}{r}=-D$. The expression $\dfrac{1}{D}$ comes from confusing the logarithm of the reciprocal with the reciprocal of the logarithm. For $r=1$ it is not even defined, because in that case $D=0$.
