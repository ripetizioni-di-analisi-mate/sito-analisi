The pH changes from $6.00$ to $5.70$. Using $\log_{10}(2)\approx0.30$, estimate the ratio of final to initial activity.

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $0.30$
- $10$

## Explanation

The ratio of the activities depends on the difference between the pH values.

$$
\frac{a_2}{a_1}=10^{-(\mathrm{pH}_2-\mathrm{pH}_1)}=10^{-(5.70-6.00)}=10^{0.30}\approx2.
$$

The last step uses $\log_{10}2\approx0.30$. The pH has decreased, so the activity has increased and the ratio is greater than one. The value $\dfrac12$ results from using the wrong sign for the difference in pH. The value $0.30$ is the change in pH, confused with the ratio.
