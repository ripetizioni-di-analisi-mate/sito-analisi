A meter displays a positive ratio $r$ as $D=\log_{10}r$. If a technician swaps the numerator and denominator of the ratio, what will the display show?

- $1/D$ for every $r$.
- $-D$
- $10-D$
- $D-1$

## Explanation

Swapping numerator and denominator changes $r$ to $r^{-1}$. The power rule gives $\log_{10}(r^{-1})=-\log_{10}r$. The formula also works for $r=1$, when the display stays at zero.
