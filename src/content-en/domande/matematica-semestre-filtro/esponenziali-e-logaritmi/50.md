Solve $\log_2(x-1)=\log_2(7-x)$.

- $x=3$
- $x=7$
- $x=1$
- $x=4$

## Explanation

The domain requires both $x-1>0$ and $7-x>0$, which together are equivalent to $1<x<7$. The base-two logarithm is strictly increasing and therefore one-to-one, so equal logarithms have equal arguments.

$$
x-1=7-x,\qquad 2x=8,\qquad x=4.
$$

The calculated value belongs to the domain, and for $x=4$ both arguments equal $3$. The values $1$ and $7$ are the endpoints of the domain, where one argument is zero. The value $3$ is the common value of the two arguments, confused with the unknown.
