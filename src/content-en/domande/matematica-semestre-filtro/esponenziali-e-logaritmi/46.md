The hydrogen ion activity of a solution lies between $10^{-6}$ and $2\cdot10^{-6}$, inclusive. Using $\log_{10}2\approx0.30$, in which interval does the pH of the solution lie?

- $[5.70,\,6.00]$
- $[5.00,\,6.00]$
- $[6.00,\,12.00]$
- $[6.00,\,6.30]$

## Explanation

By definition $\mathrm{pH}=-\log_{10}a_{\mathrm{H^+}}$. Since the decimal logarithm is increasing, the pH decreases as the activity increases, so the smallest activity gives the largest pH. For $a_{\mathrm{H^+}}=10^{-6}$ the pH is $6$. For $a_{\mathrm{H^+}}=2\cdot10^{-6}$ the pH is $6-\log_{10}2\approx5.70$. The pH therefore lies in the interval $[5.70,\,6.00]$.
