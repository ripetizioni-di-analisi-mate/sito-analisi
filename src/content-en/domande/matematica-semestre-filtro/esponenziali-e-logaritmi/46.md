A sensor indicates that the dimensionless hydrogen ion activity lies between $10^{-6}$ and $2\cdot10^{-6}$, inclusive. Using $\log_{10}2\simeq0.30$, which interval gives the full range of possible pH values to the stated precision?

- $[5.70,\,6.00]$
- $[5.00,\,6.00]$
- $[6.00,\,12.00]$
- $[6.00,\,6.30]$

## Explanation

The pH is $-\log_{10}a$ and decreases as the activity $a$ increases. The lower activity gives pH $6$, while the upper one gives $6-\log_{10}2\simeq5.70$. Put these results in increasing order to get the interval.
