In a dilute ideal aqueous solution, $[\mathrm{H_3O^+}]=2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{mol/L}$. Using $\log_{10}(2)\approx0.30$, estimate the pH.

- $4.70$
- $5.30$
- $-4.70$
- $5.00$

## Explanation

Use the logarithm of a product to separate the coefficient from the power of ten.

$$
\log_{10}(2\cdot10^{-5})=\log_{10}2+\log_{10}(10^{-5})\approx0.30-5=-4.70.
$$

The pH is the negative of this logarithm, so it is approximately $4.70$. The result lies between $4$ and $5$ because the concentration lies between $10^{-5}$ and $10^{-4}\,\mathrm{mol/L}$. The value $5.30$ results from subtracting $\log_{10}2$ instead of adding it. The value $5.00$ ignores the coefficient $2$.
