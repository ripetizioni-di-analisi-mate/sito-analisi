A quantity increases by $20\%$ in each time interval, each time relative to its value at the start of that interval. What is the overall percentage increase after three intervals?

- $60\%$
- $44\%$
- $72.8\%$
- $172.8\%$

## Explanation

An increase of $20\%$ corresponds to the factor $q=1.2$. Each interval starts at the already increased value, so the three factors multiply.

$$
q^3=1.2^3=1.728.
$$

Therefore the final value is $172.8\%$ of the initial value and the increase is $72.8\%$. The value $60\%$ results from adding the three percentages as if they referred to the same starting value. The value $172.8\%$ confuses the final value with the increase.
