For which $x$ does $\log_{10}(x+7)=2$ hold?

- $-5$
- $3$
- $107$
- $93$

## Explanation

By the definition of a logarithm, the equation $\log_{10}(x+7)=2$ is equivalent to $x+7=10^2$, and in this form the argument is automatically positive.

$$
x+7=100,\qquad x=93.
$$

The value $107$ results from adding $7$ instead of subtracting it. The value $-5$ comes from $x+7=2$, equating the argument to the logarithm. The value $3$ comes from $x+7=10$, using the exponent $1$ in place of $2$.
