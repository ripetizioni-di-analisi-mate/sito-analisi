Which interval contains the solution of $2^x=5$?

- $1<x<2$
- $3<x<4$
- $2<x<3$
- $4<x<5$

## Explanation

The solution of $2^x=5$ is $x=\log_2 5$. To estimate it, find two powers of two with consecutive integer exponents that bracket $5$.

$$
2^2=4<5<8=2^3.
$$

The function $2^x$ is strictly increasing, so the exponent that gives $5$ lies between $2$ and $3$. The interval $1<x<2$ corresponds to values of $2^x$ between $2$ and $4$. The interval $4<x<5$ confuses the exponent with the value of the power.
