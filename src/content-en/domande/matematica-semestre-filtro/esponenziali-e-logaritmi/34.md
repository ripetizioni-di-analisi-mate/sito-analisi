Let $w=\log_3(6)$. Express $\log_3(18)$ in terms of $w$.

- $3w$
- $w+3$
- $w+1$
- $w-1$

## Explanation

Since $18=3\cdot6$ and the two factors are positive, apply the product rule for logarithms.

$$
\log_3 18=\log_3 3+\log_3 6=1+w.
$$

The term $1$ comes from $\log_3 3=1$, since $3^1=3$. The value $3w$ treats the factor $3$ as a coefficient in front of the logarithm. The value $w+3$ adds the argument $3$ instead of its logarithm. The value $w-1$ corresponds to $\log_3 2$, which is the logarithm of the quotient $\dfrac63$.
