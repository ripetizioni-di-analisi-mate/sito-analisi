A quantity is initially $20$ units and increases by a factor of nine every $2\,\mathrm h$ according to an exponential model that also applies between the ends of those intervals. What is its value after $1\,\mathrm h$?

- $100$ units
- $60$ units
- $90$ units
- $180$ units

## Explanation

In the exponential model, two consecutive one-hour intervals have the same factor $q$, and their product is the factor over two hours.

$$
q^2=9,\qquad q=3.
$$

Take the positive root because the quantity stays positive. Therefore the quantity equals $20\cdot3=60$ units after one hour. The value $100$ units is the midpoint between $20$ and $180$ and corresponds to a linear model. The value $90$ units is obtained by halving $180$.
