Solve $4\cdot3^x=20$.

- $\log_3 20$
- $\log_3 16$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\log_3 5$

## Explanation

Before taking the logarithm, isolate the power by dividing both sides by $4$. This gives an equation of the form $a^x=b$ with $b>0$, whose solution is $x=\log_a b$.

$$
3^x=\frac{20}{4}=5,\qquad x=\log_3 5.
$$

The value $\log_3 20$ comes from taking the logarithm without first removing the coefficient $4$. The value $\log_3 16$ results from subtracting $4$ instead of dividing. The value $\dfrac53$ divides $5$ by the base instead of taking the logarithm.
