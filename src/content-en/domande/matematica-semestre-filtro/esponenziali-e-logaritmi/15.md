Which expression, written with the natural logarithm, is the solution of the equation $4\cdot3^x=20$?

- $\dfrac{\ln 3}{\ln 5}$
- $\ln\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\ln 20}{4\ln 3}$
- $\dfrac{\ln 5}{\ln 3}$

## Explanation

Dividing both sides by $4$ gives the equivalent equation $3^x=5$. Since both sides are positive, the natural logarithm of both sides can be taken, which gives $x\ln 3=\ln 5$. Since $\ln 3\neq0$, it follows that
$$
x=\frac{\ln 5}{\ln 3}.
$$
The expression $\dfrac{\ln 20}{4\ln 3}$ comes from writing $\ln\left(4\cdot3^x\right)=4x\ln 3$, whereas the logarithm of the product is $\ln 4+x\ln 3$.
