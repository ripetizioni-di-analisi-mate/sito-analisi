A spreadsheet provides only $\ln$. Which formula gives the exact solution of $4\cdot3^x=20$?

- $\dfrac{\ln 3}{\ln 5}$
- $\ln(5/3)$
- $\dfrac{\ln 20}{4\ln 3}$
- $\dfrac{\ln 5}{\ln 3}$

## Explanation

First divide by four and then take logarithms. From $3^x=5$ you get $x\ln3=\ln5$. The denominator is the logarithm of the base, and the numerator is the logarithm of the target value.
