The ratio of sound intensity to the reference intensity is $\dfrac{I}{I_0}=10^5$. Calculate $L=10\log_{10}\left(\dfrac{I}{I_0}\right)\,\mathrm{dB}$.

- $5\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $100\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $50\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $100000\,\mathrm{dB}$

## Explanation

The ratio $\dfrac{I}{I_0}=10^5$ is positive and dimensionless, so its base-ten logarithm exists and equals the exponent $5$. The level in decibels is ten times this logarithm.

$$
L=10\log_{10}(10^5)\,\mathrm{dB}=10\cdot5\,\mathrm{dB}=50\,\mathrm{dB}.
$$

The value $5\,\mathrm{dB}$ omits the factor of ten. The value $100000\,\mathrm{dB}$ is the ratio itself, without taking the logarithm. The value $100\,\mathrm{dB}$ uses a coefficient of twenty in place of ten.
