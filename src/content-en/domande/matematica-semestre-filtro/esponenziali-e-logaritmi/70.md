With reference intensity $I_0=10^{-12}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$, a sound has intensity level $80\,\mathrm{dB}$. What is its intensity?

- $10^{-4}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $10^{8}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $10^{-20}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $8\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

Divide the level by ten and switch to exponential form.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\log_{10}\left(\frac{I}{I_0}\right)&=\frac{80}{10}=8,\\[6pt]
I&=10^{8}\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}=10^{-4}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The value $10^{8}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ is the ratio $\dfrac{I}{I_0}$ without multiplying by the reference intensity. The value $10^{-20}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ results from dividing the reference by $10^8$. The value $8\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ multiplies the reference by $8$ without raising ten to a power.
