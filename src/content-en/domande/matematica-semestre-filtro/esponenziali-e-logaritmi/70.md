The expression $10\log_{10}(I/I_0)$ uses two intensities. A data file records $I$ in $\mathrm{mW/m^2}$ and $I_0$ in $\mathrm{W/m^2}$. What preparation makes it valid to use their numerical values?

- Express both in the same unit before forming the ratio.
- Divide the numbers directly because logarithms remove units.
- Take the logarithm of each number and multiply the results.
- Change only the logarithm base from ten to one thousand.

## Explanation

The physical ratio is dimensionless. To obtain it from the file, first convert $I$ to watts per square metre by dividing its numerical value by one thousand, or convert $I_0$ to milliwatts per square metre. Taking the numerical quotient in mixed units would shift the level by $30$ decibels.
