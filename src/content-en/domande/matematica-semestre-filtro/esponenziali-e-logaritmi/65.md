An intensity remains constant while the reference $I_0$ used to calculate its level is doubled. Using $\log_{10}(2)\approx0.30$, how does the calculated level change?

- It increases by approximately $3\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- It decreases by approximately $6\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- It decreases by approximately $3\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- It remains constant.

## Explanation

The calculated level is $L=10\log_{10}\left(\dfrac{I}{I_0}\right)\,\mathrm{dB}$. If the reference becomes $2I_0$ and the intensity remains $I$, the ratio is halved.

$$
\Delta L=10\log_{10}\left(\frac12\right)\,\mathrm{dB}=-10\log_{10}2\,\mathrm{dB}\approx-3\,\mathrm{dB}.
$$

The level therefore decreases by approximately $3\,\mathrm{dB}$. An increase of $3\,\mathrm{dB}$ corresponds to a doubling of the intensity with a fixed reference. The decrease of $6\,\mathrm{dB}$ uses the coefficient of twenty.
