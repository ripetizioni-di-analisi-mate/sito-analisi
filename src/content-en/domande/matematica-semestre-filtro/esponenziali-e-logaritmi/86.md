Solve $4^x-3\cdot2^x+2=0$.

- $x=1$ only
- $x=1$ or $x=2$
- $x=0$ only
- $x=0$ or $x=1$

## Explanation

Since $4^x=(2^x)^2$, set $y=2^x$ with $y>0$ to obtain a quadratic equation.

$$
y^2-3y+2=0,\qquad (y-1)(y-2)=0.
$$

Both solutions $y=1$ and $y=2$ are positive and therefore admissible. Return to the original unknown $x$, so $2^x=1$ gives $x=0$ and $2^x=2$ gives $x=1$. The answer $x=1$ or $x=2$ stops at the values of $y$ without finding $x$. Answers with a single solution omit one of the roots.
