Plot pH on the vertical axis and $z=\log_{10}(a_{\mathrm{H^+}})$ on the horizontal axis. What are the slope and vertical intercept of the line?

- Slope $-1$ and vertical intercept $0$.
- Slope $1$ and vertical intercept $0$.
- Slope $-1$ and vertical intercept $1$.
- Slope $0$ and vertical intercept $-1$.

## Explanation

With $z=\log_{10}(a_{\mathrm{H^+}})$, the definition of pH can be written as

$$
\mathrm{pH}=-z=(-1)\cdot z+0.
$$

This is the equation of a straight line with $z$ on the horizontal axis and pH on the vertical axis, with slope $-1$ and constant term $0$. Thus the line passes through the origin, and the pH drops by one unit for each unit increase in $z$. The slope $1$ results from omitting the minus sign in the definition.
