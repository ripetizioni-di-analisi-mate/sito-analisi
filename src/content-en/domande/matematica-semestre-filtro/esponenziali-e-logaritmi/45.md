A quantity changes from $8$ to $4$ in the first two minutes and from $4$ to $2$ in the next three minutes. The measurements are exact. What follows for an exponential model with a constant factor per minute?

- The model is inconsistent with the data.
- The model has a half-life of two minutes.
- The model has a half-life of three minutes.
- The factor per minute is one-half.

## Explanation

In an exponential model the factor depends only on the length of the interval, so each halving takes the same time. Here the first halving, from $8$ to $4$, takes two minutes, while the second, from $4$ to $2$, takes three. The measurements are exact, so no constant factor per minute describes both. A half-life of either two or three minutes is consistent with only one of the intervals.
