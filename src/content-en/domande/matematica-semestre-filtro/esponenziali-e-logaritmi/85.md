In a model $Q(t)=Q_0a^t$ with $Q_0>0$, only the ratio $Q(3)/Q(1)=16$ is known. Which parameter is uniquely determined, assuming $a>0$?

- $a=4$, while $Q_0$ is undetermined.
- $a=16$ and $Q_0=1$.
- $a=2$ and $Q_0=4$.
- $Q_0=4$, while $a$ is undetermined.

## Explanation

The initial value cancels in the ratio. This leaves $a^{3-1}=16$, which gives $a=4$ for a positive base. Every positive $Q_0$ produces the same ratio, so an absolute reading is needed to determine it.
