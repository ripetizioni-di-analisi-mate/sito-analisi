A measurement has a background value of $8$ units and an initial excess of $16$ units. Only the excess decays, with a half-life of $3\,\mathrm{h}$. What total value is measured after $6\,\mathrm{h}$?

- $12$ units
- $4$ units
- $6$ units
- $16$ units

## Explanation

The background stays constant and only the excess decays. The elapsed time $6\,\mathrm h$ comprises $\dfrac{6}{3}=2$ half-lives, so the excess is multiplied by $\left(\dfrac12\right)^2=\dfrac14$.

$$
16\cdot\frac14=4,\qquad 8+4=12\ \text{units}.
$$

The value $4$ units is the remaining excess alone, excluding the background. The value $6$ units results from halving the initial total of $24$ units twice, applying the decay to the background as well.
