Find the base $a>0$, with $a\ne1$, for which $\log_a(27)=3$.

- $a=3$
- $a=9$
- $a=27$
- $a=\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

By definition, $\log_a 27=3$ means $a^3=27$. The base must be positive, so $a$ is the cube root of $27$.

$$
a^3=27,\qquad a=\sqrt[3]{27}=3.
$$

The value $a=9$ results from dividing $27$ by $3$ instead of taking the cube root. The value $a=27$ confuses the base with the argument. With $a=\dfrac13$ we would have $\log_{\frac13}27=-3$, since $\left(\dfrac13\right)^{-3}=27$.
