The graph plots $y=\log_{10}(Q/R)$ with a constant reference $R$. Two measurements are $A=(0,1)$ and $B=(3,2)$. If the segment is straight, by what factor does $Q$ grow per unit of time?

::grafico{linee="0,1 3,2" punti="0,1,pieno,A;3,2,pieno,B" xmin=-0.5 xmax=4 ymin=0 ymax=3 nomi="t,y" titolo="Readings on the logarithmic scale"}

- $10/3$
- $1/3$
- $\sqrt[3]{10}$
- $10^3$

## Explanation

The vertical coordinate increases by one over three time units, so $Q/R$ increases by a factor of ten. A straight segment gives equal vertical increments in equal times. Thus the one-unit factor $g$ satisfies $g^3=10$.
