Calculate $\log_{10}20+\log_{10}5$.

- $\log_{10}25$
- $1$
- $2$
- $100$

## Explanation

Both arguments are positive, so the sum of the logarithms with the same base is the logarithm of the product.

$$
\log_{10}20+\log_{10}5=\log_{10}(20\cdot5)=\log_{10}100=2.
$$

The result is an integer even though neither term of the sum is an integer. The value $\log_{10}25$ comes from adding the arguments, but the product rule does not apply to sums. The value $100$ is the final argument before taking the logarithm.
