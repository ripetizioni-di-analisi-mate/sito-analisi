Solve $\left(\dfrac12\right)^x>8$.

- $x>-3$
- $x>3$
- $x<3$
- $x<-3$

## Explanation

Since $8=2^3=\left(\dfrac12\right)^{-3}$, both sides are powers with the same base $\dfrac12$. The function $\left(\dfrac12\right)^x$ is strictly decreasing, so the inequality between the exponents is the reverse of the inequality between the powers.

$$
\left(\frac12\right)^x>\left(\frac12\right)^{-3}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x<-3.
$$

The solution $x>-3$ keeps the same direction, as if the base were greater than one. The solutions involving $3$ result from writing $8=\left(\dfrac12\right)^3$.
