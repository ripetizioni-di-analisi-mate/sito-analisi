Two models predict $P(t)=2^t$ and $Q(t)=2^{t+3}$. For which real times does $Q(t)>4P(t)$ hold?

- Only for $t>2$.
- Only for $t<3$.
- For no real $t$.
- For every real $t$.

## Explanation

Since $P(t)>0$ at every time, division preserves the inequality. The ratio $Q(t)/P(t)=2^3=8$ is constant and always greater than four. Thus the inequality has no threshold time.
