Someone proposes computing $\ln((x-1)/(x+2))$ as $\ln(x-1)-\ln(x+2)$. Which test value shows that the rewrite can lose an allowed input?

- $x=0$
- $x=1$
- $x=3$
- $x=-3$

## Explanation

At $x=-3$ the ratio is $(-4)/(-1)=4$, so the original logarithm exists. The two separate logarithms would have negative inputs. The quotient rule requires the numerator and denominator to be individually positive before their real logarithms can be taken separately.
