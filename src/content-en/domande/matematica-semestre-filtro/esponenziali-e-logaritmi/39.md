A plot of $\ln\left(\dfrac{Q(t)}{Q_0}\right)$ against time has slope $-0.9\,\mathrm{h^{-1}}$. What is the same slope expressed in $\mathrm{min^{-1}}$?

- $-54\,\mathrm{min^{-1}}$
- $-0.9\,\mathrm{min^{-1}}$
- $-0.015\,\mathrm{min^{-1}}$
- $-0.15\,\mathrm{min^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The slope has units of inverse time, so its value in inverse hours gives the change in the logarithm over one hour. Since one hour contains sixty minutes, the change in one minute is one-sixtieth as large.

$$
-0.9\,\mathrm{h^{-1}}=-\frac{0.9}{60\,\mathrm{min}}=-0.015\,\mathrm{min^{-1}}.
$$

The value $-54\,\mathrm{min^{-1}}$ results from multiplying by sixty, as one would when converting a duration from hours to minutes. The value $-0.9\,\mathrm{min^{-1}}$ changes the unit without changing the number. The value $-0.15\,\mathrm{min^{-1}}$ divides by six instead of sixty.
