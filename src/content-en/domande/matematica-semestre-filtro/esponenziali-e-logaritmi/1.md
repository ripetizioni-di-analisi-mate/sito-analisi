Solve $2^x=16$.

- $16$
- $4$
- $5$
- $-4$

## Explanation

Since $16=2\cdot2\cdot2\cdot2$, the right-hand side can be written as a power of the same base as the left-hand side, namely $2^4$. The base-two exponential function is strictly increasing, so two powers of two are equal only if their exponents are equal.

$$
2^x=2^4,\qquad x=4.
$$

The value $16$ results from confusing the exponent with the power. The value $-4$ has the wrong sign, because $2^{-4}=\dfrac{1}{16}$.
