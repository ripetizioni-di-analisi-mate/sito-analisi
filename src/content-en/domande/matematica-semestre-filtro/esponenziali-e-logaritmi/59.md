A sample retains the same fraction of its current amount each hour. It loses 12 units between hours one and two and 9 between hours two and three. What fraction does it retain per hour?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac43$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

If the amount is multiplied by $q$, consecutive losses are also multiplied by $q$, since each is a fraction $1-q$ of the amount at the start of its period. Hence $q=\dfrac9{12}$.
