Two factors are written as powers of ten. What single exponent describes their product, that is, what is $\log_{10}(10^3\cdot10^4)$?

- $12$
- $-1$
- $9$
- $7$

## Explanation

The logarithm of a product of positive numbers is the sum of the logarithms of the factors. Each factor is a power of ten, so its base-ten logarithm equals its exponent.

$$
\log_{10}(10^3\cdot10^4)=\log_{10}(10^3)+\log_{10}(10^4)=3+4=7.
$$

We obtain the same result by first writing $10^3\cdot10^4=10^7$. The value $12$ results from multiplying the exponents instead of adding them.
