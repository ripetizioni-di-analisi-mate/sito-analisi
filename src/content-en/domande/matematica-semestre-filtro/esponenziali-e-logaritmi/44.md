The number of bacteria in a culture doubles every $20\,\mathrm{min}$. How long does it take for the number to reach $32$ times its initial value?

- $640\,\mathrm{min}$
- $80\,\mathrm{min}$
- $100\,\mathrm{min}$
- $620\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Each doubling multiplies the number of bacteria by $2$, so after $n$ doublings the factor is $2^n$. Since $32=2^5$, five doublings are required.

$$
2^n=32=2^5,\qquad n=5,\qquad t=5\cdot20\,\mathrm{min}=100\,\mathrm{min}.
$$

The value $640\,\mathrm{min}$ multiplies the factor $32$ by the doubling time. The value $80\,\mathrm{min}$ counts four doublings, which give a factor of $16$. The value $620\,\mathrm{min}$ corresponds to linear growth that adds the initial quantity every $20\,\mathrm{min}$.
