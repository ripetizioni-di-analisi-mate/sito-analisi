A table lists times $t=0,1,3$ minutes and the corresponding readings $2,6,54$. Which check supports a single growth factor per minute?

- $54/2=(6/2)^2$
- $6/2=54/6$
- $6/2=\sqrt{54/6}$
- $6-2=(54-6)/2$

## Explanation

The first ratio covers one minute and equals $3$. The second interval spans two minutes and its total factor is $9$, giving a per-minute factor of $\sqrt9=3$. Comparing $3$ directly with $9$ would treat the two intervals as equal in length.
