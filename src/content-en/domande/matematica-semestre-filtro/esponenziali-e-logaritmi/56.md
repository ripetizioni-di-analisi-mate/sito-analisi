On two references with pH $4$ and $7$, an instrument displays $4.2$ and $7.5$, respectively. An operator wants to correct every display by subtracting one fixed constant. Can this make both reference readings exact?

- No, the two offsets on the pH scale differ.
- Yes, because pH is the negative logarithm of a positive quantity.
- Yes, by subtracting $0.35$ from both.
- Yes, by subtracting $\log_{10}2$ from both.

## Explanation

The first reference requires subtracting $4.2-4=0.2$, while the second requires $7.5-7=0.5$. A single number cannot equal both offsets. In terms of activity, a fixed pH correction would produce the same multiplicative factor at every reading.
