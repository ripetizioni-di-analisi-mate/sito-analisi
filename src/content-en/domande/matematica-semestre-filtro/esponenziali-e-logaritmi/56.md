Two positive activities correspond to pH values of three and five. What pH corresponds to their geometric mean?

- $4$
- $3$
- $5$
- $8$

## Explanation

The activities corresponding to the two pH values are $10^{-3}$ and $10^{-5}$. Their geometric mean is the square root of their product.

$$
\sqrt{10^{-3}\cdot10^{-5}}=\sqrt{10^{-8}}=10^{-4},\qquad \mathrm{pH}=-\log_{10}(10^{-4})=4.
$$

The pH of the geometric mean is therefore the arithmetic mean of the two pH values because a logarithm turns products into sums. The value $8$ is the sum of the pH values, which is the pH of the product of the activities.
