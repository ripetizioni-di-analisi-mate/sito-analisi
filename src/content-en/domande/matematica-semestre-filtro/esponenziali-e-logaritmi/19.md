A probe measures a signal $S$ and a background $B$ in the same unit. Its software displays $\log_{10}((S-B)/R)$, where the reference $R>0$ has that unit too. When can it display a real number?

- $S\ne B$
- $S>B+R$
- $S\ge B$
- $S>B$

## Explanation

The logarithm requires a strictly positive argument. Dividing by a positive reference preserves the sign, so $S-B>0$ is required. A positive difference smaller than $R$ gives a negative but perfectly well-defined display value.
