For which values of $x$ is $\log_{10}(x-3)$ defined?

- $x < 3$
- $x \ge 3$
- $x > -3$
- $x > 3$

## Explanation

The logarithm is defined only for strictly positive arguments, since every real power of ten is positive. We therefore require

$$
x-3>0,\qquad x>3.
$$

The condition $x\ge3$ includes $x=3$, where the argument is zero and the logarithm is undefined. The condition $x>-3$ results from a sign error when moving $3$ to the right-hand side. The condition $x<3$ makes the argument negative.
