Solve $\log_{\frac{1}{2}}(x)\ge 2$ over the real numbers.

- $\left[\dfrac{1}{4},+\infty\right)$
- $\left[0,\dfrac{1}{4}\right]$
- $\left(0,\dfrac{1}{4}\right]$
- $\left(0,\dfrac{1}{4}\right)$

## Explanation

The domain requires $x>0$. Since $\left(\dfrac12\right)^2=\dfrac14$, the right-hand side is $\log_{\frac12}\left(\dfrac14\right)$. The logarithm with base $\dfrac12$ is strictly decreasing, so the inequality between the arguments is reversed.

$$
\begin{cases}
x>0\\
x\le\frac14
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad 0<x\le\frac14.
$$

The interval $\left[\dfrac14,+\infty\right)$ keeps the same direction, as if the base were greater than one. The interval $\left[0,\dfrac14\right]$ includes zero, which is outside the domain.
