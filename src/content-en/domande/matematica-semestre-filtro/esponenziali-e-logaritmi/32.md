On the graph of $y=\log_{1/2}x$, points with a vertical coordinate of at least $2$ are highlighted. Which description correctly locates them relative to $x=1/4$?

- Only at the point whose horizontal coordinate is $1/4$.
- From $1/4$ rightwards.
- Between the vertical axis and $1/4$, including $1/4$.
- On the entire ray to the left of $1/4$, including zero.

## Explanation

The graph passes through $(1/4,2)$ because $(1/2)^2=1/4$. The base is below one, so moving left increases the vertical coordinate as long as the horizontal coordinate stays positive. The required interval is $0<x\le1/4$.
