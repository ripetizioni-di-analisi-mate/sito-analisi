In a model of spherical propagation without absorption, the intensity is inversely proportional to the square of the distance. The distance doubles. Using $\log_{10}(2)\approx0.30$, estimate the change in level.

- $-6\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $6\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{dB}$

## Explanation

Intensity is inversely proportional to the square of the distance, so doubling the distance reduces the intensity to one-quarter of its original value.

$$
\Delta L=10\log_{10}\left(\frac14\right)\,\mathrm{dB}=-20\log_{10}2\,\mathrm{dB}\approx-6\,\mathrm{dB}.
$$

The coefficient of twenty comes from the square of the distance. The value $-3\,\mathrm{dB}$ uses the ratio $\dfrac12$, as if the intensity were inversely proportional to distance. The value $+6\,\mathrm{dB}$ has the wrong sign because the intensity decreases as the distance increases.
