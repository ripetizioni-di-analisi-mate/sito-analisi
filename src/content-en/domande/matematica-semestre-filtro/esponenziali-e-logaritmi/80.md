For $t\ge0$, a quantity follows $Q(t)=20+60e^{-0.5t}$, where $t$ is the numerical value of time in hours. For which values of the threshold $S$ does the equation $Q(t)=S$ have a solution $t\ge0$?

- $20<S\le80$
- $0<S\le80$
- $S\ge20$
- $20\le S\le80$

## Explanation

We have $Q(0)=20+60=80$. The term $60e^{-0.5t}$ is positive, strictly decreasing, and tends to zero as $t\to+\infty$. Thus $Q$ decreases from $80$ and approaches the equilibrium level $20$ without reaching it. The equation therefore has a solution exactly when $20<S\le80$, in which case

$$
e^{-0.5t}=\frac{S-20}{60},\qquad t=2\ln\left(\frac{60}{S-20}\right).
$$

With $S=20$, we would need $e^{-0.5t}=0$, which has no solutions.
