Two signals are added. Their intensities are $2^t$ and $2^{t+1}$ in the same units. What share of the total comes from the second signal, for any $t$?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

The second term equals $2\cdot2^t$, so the total is $3\cdot2^t$. In the ratio of the second intensity to the total, the common factor cancels and leaves $2/3$, independently of $t$.
