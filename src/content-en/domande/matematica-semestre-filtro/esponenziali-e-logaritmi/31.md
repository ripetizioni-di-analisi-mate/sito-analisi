Solve $2^{x+1}+2^x=48$.

- $x=5$
- $x=3$
- $x=16$
- $x=4$

## Explanation

Since $2^{x+1}=2\cdot2^x$, the two terms have the common factor $2^x$, which we can factor out.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2\cdot2^x+2^x=3\cdot2^x&=48,\\[6pt]
2^x&=16=2^4,
\end{aligned}
$$

so $x=4$. The value $x=16$ is the value of the power $2^x$, confused with its exponent. The value $x=3$ results from $2^{x+1}=16$, assigning $16$ to $2^{x+1}$. The value $x=5$ comes from $2^x=32$, dividing $48$ by $\dfrac32$ instead of $3$.
