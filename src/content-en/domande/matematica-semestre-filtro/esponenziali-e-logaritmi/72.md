Given $b>1$ and $\log_b(u)=-2$, in which interval does $u$ lie?

- $u<0$
- $u=1$
- $0<u<1$
- $u>1$

## Explanation

By the definition of a logarithm, $\log_b u=-2$ means

$$
u=b^{-2}=\frac1{b^2}.
$$

Since $b>1$, we have $b^2>1$, so the reciprocal $\dfrac{1}{b^2}$ is positive and less than one. The interval $u<0$ is excluded because the argument of a logarithm is always positive. The value $u=1$ has a logarithm equal to zero. Values $u>1$ would give a positive logarithm, since a logarithm with a base greater than one is increasing.
