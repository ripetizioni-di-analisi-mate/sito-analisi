In a dilute ideal aqueous solution, $[\mathrm{H_3O^+}]=10^{-4}\,\mathrm{mol/L}$. Calculate the pH using the concentration-based approximation.

- $4$
- $-4$
- $10$
- $5$

## Explanation

In the approximation for dilute ideal solutions, pH is the negative of the base-ten logarithm of $\dfrac{c}{c^\circ}$, with $c^\circ=1\,\mathrm{mol/L}$. The ratio equals $10^{-4}$, and its base-ten logarithm is the exponent $-4$.

$$
\mathrm{pH}\approx-\log_{10}(10^{-4})=-(-4)=4.
$$

The value $-4$ omits the minus sign in the definition of pH. The value $10$ equals $14-4$, which gives the pOH of a dilute aqueous solution at 25 °C. The question asks for pH.
