After a $20\%$ loss, a signal must return to its initial value in a single step. By what percentage of the reduced value must it increase?

- $20\%$
- $80\%$
- $25\%$
- $125\%$

## Explanation

The loss leaves a factor of $0.8$. Reversing it requires the reciprocal factor $1/0.8=1.25$, hence a $25\%$ increase. The figure $125\%$ represents the whole final value relative to the reduced value, including the starting amount.
