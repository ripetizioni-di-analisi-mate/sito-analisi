Each hour a quantity loses $20\%$ of its value at the beginning of that hour. What percentage of its initial value remains after two hours?

- $60\%$
- $40\%$
- $64\%$
- $80\%$

## Explanation

Each hour the value is multiplied by $1-0.20=0.80$ because the loss is calculated from the value at the beginning of that hour. Over two hours the factors multiply.

$$
0.80^2=0.64=64\%.
$$

The overall loss is therefore $36\%$. The value $60\%$ results from subtracting $20\%$ of the initial value twice, as if the second loss were based on the same value. The value $80\%$ is the amount remaining after only one hour.
