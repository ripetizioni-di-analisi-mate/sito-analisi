A dilute ideal aqueous solution has $\mathrm{pH}=6$. Estimate $[\mathrm{H_3O^+}]$ in $\mathrm{mol/L}$.

- $10^6\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $10^{-6}\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $10^{-7}\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mol/L}$

## Explanation

The definition $\mathrm{pH}\approx-\log_{10}\left(\dfrac{c}{c^\circ}\right)$ gives $\log_{10}\left(\dfrac{c}{c^\circ}\right)\approx-6$. We switch to exponential form because the base-ten logarithm is the inverse of the base-ten exponential function.

$$
\frac{c}{c^\circ}\approx10^{-6},\qquad c\approx10^{-6}\,\mathrm{mol/L}.
$$

The value $10^{6}\,\mathrm{mol/L}$ omits the minus sign. The value $6\,\mathrm{mol/L}$ confuses pH with concentration. The value $10^{-7}\,\mathrm{mol/L}$ corresponds to a pH of $7$.
