In a group of $50$ readers, $28$ read mysteries, $7$ read both mysteries and fantasy, and $10$ read neither. How many read fantasy?

- $12$
- $22$
- $19$
- $15$

## Explanation

The number who read at least one genre is $50 - 10 = 40$. Of these, $28$ read mysteries, so $40 - 28 = 12$ read **only fantasy**. Add the $7$ who read both genres to obtain $12 + 7 = 19$ fantasy readers. The number $12$ overlooks those who read both, and $22 = 50 - 28$ overlooks those who read neither.
