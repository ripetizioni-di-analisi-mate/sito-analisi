A conference has $60$ participants, including $34$ women. Of the participants, $25$ come from abroad, including $11$ men. The rest come from Italy. How many women come from Italy?

- $15$
- $14$
- $20$
- $9$

## Explanation

Of the $25$ participants from abroad, $11$ are men, so $25 - 11 = 14$ are women. There are $34$ women in all, so the number of **women from Italy** is $34 - 14 = 20$. As a check, $26 - 11 = 15$ men come from Italy, and $20 + 14 + 15 + 11 = 60$. The value $15$ counts men from Italy, and $14$ counts women from abroad.
