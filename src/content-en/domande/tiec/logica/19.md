What is the sixth term of the sequence $13,\ 17,\ 19,\ 23,\ 29,\ \ldots$?

- $31$
- $35$
- $33$
- $37$

## Explanation

The differences $4, 2, 4, 6$ show no pattern, so consider the terms themselves. The numbers $13, 17, 19, 23, 29$ are consecutive **prime numbers** because they are the only primes between $13$ and $29$. The first prime after $29$ is $31$, which is therefore the missing term. The value $35$ repeats the difference $6$, and $33$ adds $4$, but neither result is prime. The value $37$ is prime but skips $31$.
