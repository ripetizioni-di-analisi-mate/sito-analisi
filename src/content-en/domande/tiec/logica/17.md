Which number completes the sequence $3,\ 8,\ 15,\ 24,\ 35,\ \ldots$?

- $46$
- $49$
- $48$
- $47$

## Explanation

The differences between consecutive terms are $5, 7, 9, 11$ and increase by $2$. The next difference is therefore $13$, and the missing term is $35 + 13 = 48$. The same result follows from observing that the terms $2^2 - 1$, $3^2 - 1$, $4^2 - 1$, $5^2 - 1$, and $6^2 - 1$ are **squares minus $1$**, and the next is $7^2 - 1 = 48$. The value $46$ repeats the difference $11$, and $49$ forgets to subtract $1$ from the square.
