After four tests, Sofia's average grade is $6.5$. What grade does she need on the fifth test to average $7$ on all five?

- $7.5$
- $9$
- $8$
- $9.5$

## Explanation

The average is the sum of the grades divided by the number of tests, so after four tests the **sum of the grades** is $4 \cdot 6.5 = 26$. To average $7$ on five tests, the sum must be $5 \cdot 7 = 35$. The fifth grade must therefore be $35 - 26 = 9$. The value $7.5$ makes up half a point for just one test, whereas each of the four tests already taken is half a point short of the new average.
