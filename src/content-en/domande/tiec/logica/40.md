Which Venn diagram represents “All squares are rectangles” and “No rectangle is a triangle”?

- The rectangles' circle inside the squares' circle, with the triangles' circle disjoint from both
- The squares' circle inside the rectangles' circle, with the triangles' circle intersecting only the squares' circle
- The squares' circle inside the rectangles' circle, with the triangles' circle disjoint from both
- Three mutually disjoint circles

## Explanation

“All squares are rectangles” says that every member of the squares' circle also belongs to the rectangles' circle, so the first circle is **contained** in the second. “No rectangle is a triangle” says that the rectangles' and triangles' circles have no points in common. Since the squares lie inside the rectangles, the squares' circle is also disjoint from the triangles' circle. A circle for triangles that intersects the squares' circle must intersect the rectangles' circle as well, contradicting the second statement.
