What is the negation of “Giulio has never been late to a meeting”?

- Giulio has always been late to meetings.
- At least once, Giulio was late to a meeting.
- Giulio has been late to most meetings.
- At least once, Giulio arrived on time for a meeting.

## Explanation

“Never” means “on no occasion,” so the statement works like one beginning with “no.” By the rule for **negating quantifiers**, its negation uses “on at least one occasion,” giving “At least once, Giulio was late to a meeting.” The choices with “always” and “most” say more than the negation. “At least once, Giulio arrived on time” is also true if he was always punctual, so it is consistent with the original statement.
