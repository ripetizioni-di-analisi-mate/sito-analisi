Three partners divide a profit of $7200$ euros in proportion to their investments of $2000$, $3000$, and $4000$ euros. How much does the partner who invested $3000$ euros receive?

- $3000$ euros
- $1600$ euros
- $3200$ euros
- $2400$ euros

## Explanation

The investments are in the ratio $2 : 3 : 4$, so the profit is divided into $2 + 3 + 4 = 9$ equal parts of $\dfrac{7200}{9} = 800$ euros each. The partner who invested $3000$ euros receives **three parts**, or $3 \cdot 800 = 2400$ euros. The amounts $1600$ and $3200$ euros are the other partners' shares, and indeed $1600 + 2400 + 3200 = 7200$.
