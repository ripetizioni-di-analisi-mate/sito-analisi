A form has three compulsory boxes. It can mark each missing entry separately or show only a single “incomplete” warning. Considering every way of leaving at least one box empty, how many different configurations would produce that same warning?

- $3$
- $6$
- $7$
- $8$

## Explanation

Each box can be filled or empty, giving $2^3=8$ configurations. Only the one with all three filled avoids the warning. The other seven consist of three with one empty, three with two empty and one with all three empty.
