What is the negation of “The package arrived, is intact, and contains the invoice”?

- The package did not arrive, is not intact, and does not contain the invoice.
- The package arrived but is not intact and does not contain the invoice.
- The package did not arrive, or it is not intact, or it does not contain the invoice.
- The package did not arrive or it arrived damaged.

## Explanation

The statement is a conjunction of three parts and becomes false as soon as any one of them is false. By **De Morgan's laws**, its negation negates each part and replaces “and” with “or.” “The package did not arrive or it arrived damaged” leaves out a package that arrived intact without the invoice, a case where the original statement is false. The other two answers require several parts to be false at the same time.
