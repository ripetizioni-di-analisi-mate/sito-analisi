“The restaurant is inexpensive but noisy” is true. Which of the following statements is necessarily true?

- The restaurant is not noisy.
- The restaurant is not inexpensive or it is not noisy.
- The restaurant is noisy.
- The restaurant is noisy because it is inexpensive.

## Explanation

The word “but” has the same effect on truth values as “and,” so the statement says that the restaurant is inexpensive and noisy. Since a true **conjunction** has two true parts, the restaurant is noisy. “The restaurant is not inexpensive or it is not noisy” is the negation of the original and therefore false. The statement containing “because” adds a causal relationship that the original does not state.
