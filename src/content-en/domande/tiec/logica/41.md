A mixture consists of $3$ liters of juice containing $20\%$ sugar and $2$ liters of another juice containing $45\%$ sugar. What percentage of the mixture is sugar?

- $32.5\%$
- $35\%$
- $30\%$
- $65\%$

## Explanation

The percentage in the mixture is the **weighted average** of the two percentages, with the volumes in liters as weights. There are $3 \cdot 0.20 + 2 \cdot 0.45 = 0.6 + 0.9 = 1.5$ liters of sugar in $5$ liters of mixture, so the percentage is $\dfrac{1.5}{5} = 30\%$. The value $32.5\%$ is the simple average, which gives the two juices equal weight. The value $35\%$ interchanges the weights, assigning $3$ liters to the sweeter juice.
