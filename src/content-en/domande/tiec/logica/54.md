The letters $A$ and $B$ represent two digits, neither of which is $0$. Suppose $A + A + A = BA$, where $BA$ is the two-digit number with tens digit $B$ and ones digit $A$. What is the number $BA$?

- $51$
- $15$
- $18$
- $25$

## Explanation

The number $BA$ equals $10B + A$, so the equation becomes $3A = 10B + A$, giving $2A = 10B$ and $A = 5B$. Since $A$ is a digit and therefore at most $9$, and $B$ differs from $0$, the only possibility is $B = 1$ and $A = 5$. The number $BA$ is therefore $15$, and indeed $5 + 5 + 5 = 15$. The number $51$ reverses the two digits.
