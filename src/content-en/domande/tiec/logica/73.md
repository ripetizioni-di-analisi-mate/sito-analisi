Which statement is equivalent to “No employee in the sales department works on Saturdays”?

- All employees who do not work on Saturdays are in the sales department.
- Some employees in the sales department do not work on Saturdays.
- Some employees who work on Saturdays are in the sales department.
- No employee who works on Saturdays is in the sales department.

## Explanation

“No $A$ is $B$” says that the circles for $A$ and $B$ have no points in common, and this relation is **symmetric**. Thus “No employee in the sales department works on Saturdays” is equivalent to “No employee who works on Saturdays is in the sales department.” “Some employees in the sales department do not work on Saturdays” follows from the original statement but is not equivalent to it, because it would still be true if another salesperson worked on Saturdays. “Everyone who does not work on Saturdays is in the sales department” concerns other departments, about which the original statement says nothing.
