Which premise, added to “All bakers get up before $5$ a.m.,” allows us to conclude that “All bakers are tired in the evening”?

- Everyone who is tired in the evening gets up before $5$ a.m.
- Some people who get up before $5$ a.m. are tired in the evening.
- Some bakers are tired in the evening.
- Everyone who gets up before $5$ a.m. is tired in the evening.

## Explanation

The conclusion has the form “All $A$ are $C$,” and the given premise is “All $A$ are $B$.” The first of the **valid patterns** requires the second premise “All $B$ are $C$,” or “Everyone who gets up before $5$ a.m. is tired in the evening.” “Everyone who is tired in the evening gets up before $5$ a.m.” says “All $C$ are $B$,” which does not put the bakers inside the circle of people who are tired. The two statements containing “some” guarantee a property for at least one member of a group, so neither supports a conclusion about all bakers.
