In a group of four boys, no two are the same height. Teo is not taller than Ugo, Ugo is not shorter than Vito, Vito is taller than Teo, and Zeno is taller than all the others. Who is shortest?

- Vito
- Teo
- Ugo
- Zeno

## Explanation

Since no heights are equal, “Teo is not taller than Ugo” means $U > T$, and “Ugo is not shorter than Vito” means $U > V$. With $V > T$ and Zeno taller than everyone else, we obtain the **chain** $Z > U > V > T$. Thus Teo is shortest. Choosing Vito reverses the comparison between Vito and Teo.
