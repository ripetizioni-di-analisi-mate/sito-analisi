What is the negation of “All of today's flights departed on time or were canceled”?

- At least one of today's flights did not depart on time and was not canceled.
- None of today's flights departed on time or was canceled.
- At least one of today's flights did not depart on time or was not canceled.
- All of today's flights departed late and none was canceled.

## Explanation

Construct the negation in two steps. By the rule for **negating quantifiers**, “all flights” becomes “at least one flight” that lacks the property “departed on time or was canceled.” By **De Morgan's laws**, the negation of this property is “did not depart on time and was not canceled.” “At least one flight did not depart on time or was not canceled” is true even if every flight departed on time, because a flight that departed on time was not canceled. In that case the original statement is also true, so the statements are not negations of one another.
