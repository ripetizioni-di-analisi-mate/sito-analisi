A parking lot contains cars and motorcycles. There are $40$ vehicles and $130$ wheels in all. How many are motorcycles?

- $15$
- $25$
- $20$
- $10$

## Explanation

With $a$ cars and $m$ motorcycles, $a + m = 40$ and $4a + 2m = 130$ hold. If all $40$ vehicles were motorcycles, they would have $80$ wheels. Each car adds $2$ wheels, so there are $\dfrac{130 - 80}{2} = 25$ cars. There are therefore $40 - 25 = 15$ **motorcycles**. As a check, $25 \cdot 4 + 15 \cdot 2 = 100 + 30 = 130$. The value $25$ counts the cars.
