What is the negation of “Some of this morning's trains are late and crowded”?

- None of this morning's trains is both late and crowded.
- None of this morning's trains is late and none is crowded.
- Some of this morning's trains are neither late nor crowded.
- All of this morning's trains are late or crowded.

## Explanation

The statement says that at least one train has *both* properties. By the rule for **negating quantifiers**, its negation says that no train has both, or “None of this morning's trains is both late and crowded.” “No train is late and none is crowded” says much more. On a morning with one late but empty train and one on-time but crowded train, both this stronger statement and the original are false, so the stronger statement is not the negation.
