What is the negation of “Marta speaks French and Spanish”?

- Marta does not speak French or does not speak Spanish.
- Marta speaks neither French nor Spanish.
- Marta does not speak French but speaks Spanish.
- Marta speaks French or Spanish.

## Explanation

Let $F$ = “Marta speaks French” and $S$ = “Marta speaks Spanish.” The statement is the conjunction $F \land S$, which is false as soon as one part is false. By **De Morgan's laws**, its negation is $\lnot F \lor \lnot S$, or “Marta does not speak French or does not speak Spanish.” If Marta speaks only French, “Marta speaks neither French nor Spanish” is false, and so is the original statement. Thus the two are not negations of each other.
