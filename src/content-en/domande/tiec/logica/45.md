The letters $A$ and $B$ are two digits with $A > B$. The two-digit number $AB$ minus the number $BA$ equals $36$. What is $A - B$?

- $6$
- $3$
- $4$
- $9$

## Explanation

By **place-value notation**, the number $AB$ equals $10A + B$ and $BA$ equals $10B + A$. Their difference is therefore $(10A + B) - (10B + A) = 9A - 9B = 9\,(A - B)$. From $9\,(A - B) = 36$ it follows that $A - B = 4$. For example, $62 - 26 = 36$ and $95 - 59 = 36$, and in both cases the difference between the digits is $4$.
