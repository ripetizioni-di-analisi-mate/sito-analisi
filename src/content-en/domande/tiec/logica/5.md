Livia is wealthier than Marco but less wealthy than Nadia. Olga is less wealthy than Marco, and Piero is wealthier than Nadia. Which statement is false?

- Olga is wealthier than Livia.
- Piero is wealthier than Marco.
- Nadia is wealthier than Olga.
- Marco is less wealthy than Livia.

## Explanation

Use $>$ in the direction that means “wealthier than” to express each comparison. This gives $L > M$, $N > L$, $M > O$, and $P > N$, forming the **chain** $P > N > L > M > O$. Olga is the least wealthy in this chain, so “Olga is wealthier than Livia” is false. The other three statements can be read directly from the chain.
