Which of the following sets of statements is contradictory?

- Ada is taller than Bea, Cia is taller than Bea, and Ada is shorter than Cia.
- Ada is taller than Bea, Bea is taller than Cia, and Cia is taller than Ada.
- Ada is shorter than Bea, Bea is shorter than Cia, and Ada is shorter than Cia.
- Bea is taller than Ada, Bea is taller than Cia, and Cia is taller than Ada.

## Explanation

In “Ada is taller than Bea, Bea is taller than Cia, and Cia is taller than Ada,” the first two statements give $A > B$ and $B > C$, so the **transitive property** gives $A > C$. The third statement says $C > A$, making the set contradictory. The other sets correspond to the orderings $C > A > B$, $C > B > A$, and $B > C > A$ and contain no contradictions.
