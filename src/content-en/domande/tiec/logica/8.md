If $\diamondsuit + \diamondsuit = \star$ and $\star + \diamondsuit = 18$, what is $\star$?

- $6$
- $9$
- $24$
- $12$

## Explanation

The first equation shows that $\star$ is twice $\diamondsuit$, so the second becomes $2\diamondsuit + \diamondsuit = 18$, or $3\diamondsuit = 18$. This gives $\diamondsuit = 6$, and hence $\star = 2 \cdot 6 = 12$. The value $6$ is the value of $\diamondsuit$, and $9$ divides $18$ by two as though the two symbols had equal values.
