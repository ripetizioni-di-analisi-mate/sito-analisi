A travel agency has $80$ customers. Of these, $40$ booked a flight, $35$ a hotel, and $25$ a rental car. The counts are $15$ for a flight and hotel, $10$ for a flight and car, and $8$ for a hotel and car. Of the customers, $5$ booked all three services. How many customers booked none of the services?

- $13$
- $3$
- $31$
- $8$

## Explanation

By the **formula for the union of three sets**, the number of customers who booked at least one service is
$$
40 + 35 + 25 - 15 - 10 - 8 + 5 = 72 .
$$
Thus $80 - 72 = 8$ customers made no bookings. The number $13$ forgets to add back the $5$ customers common to all three services, whom the pairwise intersections subtract three times. The number $3$ adds them back twice.
