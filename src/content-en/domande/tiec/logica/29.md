“The online store ships an order free of charge only if its value exceeds $50$ euros.” Mario placed an order worth $70$ euros. Which conclusion is correct?

- Mario's order ships free of charge.
- It cannot be determined whether Mario's order ships free of charge.
- Mario's order does not ship free of charge.
- Every order worth more than $50$ euros ships free of charge.

## Explanation

The statement “$G$ only if $S$,” with $G$ = “the order ships free of charge” and $S$ = “the order exceeds $50$ euros,” means $G \Rightarrow S$. Thus exceeding $50$ euros is a **necessary condition** for free shipping, and it may not be sufficient. Mario's order makes $S$ true. Inferring $G$ from $S$ would affirm the consequent. The information therefore does not determine whether Mario's order ships free of charge.
