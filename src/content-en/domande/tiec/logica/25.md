Anna earns more than Bice, Carlo earns less than Anna, and Dora earns more than Anna. Which additional information determines the complete ordering of the four salaries?

- Dora earns more than Carlo.
- Bice earns less than Dora.
- Anna earns less than Dora.
- Bice earns more than Carlo.

## Explanation

The information gives $D > A$, $A > B$, and $A > C$. Thus Dora ranks first and Anna second, with Bice and Carlo in the last two places in an **undetermined order**. The only missing comparison is between Bice and Carlo. “Bice earns more than Carlo” settles it, giving $D > A > B > C$. “Dora earns more than Carlo” and “Bice earns less than Dora” already follow by the transitive property. “Anna earns less than Dora” repeats a given fact.
