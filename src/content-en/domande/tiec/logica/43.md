Gianni is $4$ years older than Lara, Lara is $3$ years younger than Mino, and Mino is $2$ years older than Nora. What is the age difference between Gianni and Nora?

- $1$ year
- $3$ years
- $5$ years
- $9$ years

## Explanation

Express all ages in terms of Lara's age $L$. Gianni is $L + 4$ and Mino is $L + 3$, so Nora, who is $2$ years younger than Mino, is $L + 1$. Thus the **difference** between Gianni and Nora is $(L + 4) - (L + 1) = 3$ years, with Gianni older. The value $9 = 4 + 3 + 2$ adds the three age differences without considering their directions.
