What is the negation of “Neither Paolo nor Sara voted”?

- Paolo and Sara both voted.
- Paolo voted but Sara did not.
- At least one of Paolo and Sara voted.
- Paolo did not vote or Sara did not vote.

## Explanation

“Neither Paolo nor Sara voted” is the conjunction $\lnot P \land \lnot S$, where $P$ = “Paolo voted” and $S$ = “Sara voted.” By **De Morgan's laws**, its negation is $P \lor S$, or “At least one of Paolo and Sara voted.” “Paolo and Sara both voted” is false when only Paolo voted, as is the original statement. “Paolo did not vote or Sara did not vote” is true when neither voted, as is the original statement.
