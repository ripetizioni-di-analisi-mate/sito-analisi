The sum of three consecutive integers is $84$. What is the greatest of the three?

- $29$
- $28$
- $30$
- $27$

## Explanation

If $n$ is the smallest integer, the other two are $n + 1$ and $n + 2$. This gives the **equation** $n + (n + 1) + (n + 2) = 84$. Thus $3n + 3 = 84$, or $n = 27$, and the three numbers are $27$, $28$, and $29$. The greatest is therefore $29$. The value $28 = \dfrac{84}{3}$ is the middle number, and $27$ is the smallest.
