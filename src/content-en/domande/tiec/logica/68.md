One euro is worth $1.10$ dollars. How many euros are needed to buy $55$ dollars?

- $60.50$ euros
- $45$ euros
- $50$ euros
- $55$ euros

## Explanation

Euros and dollars are **directly proportional**, with $1$ euro equal to $1.10$ dollars. To convert dollars to euros, divide by $1.10$, so $55$ dollars is worth $\dfrac{55}{1.10} = 50$ euros. As a check, $50 \cdot 1.10 = 55$. The answer $60.50$ euros multiplies by $1.10$ instead of dividing, converting in the opposite direction.
