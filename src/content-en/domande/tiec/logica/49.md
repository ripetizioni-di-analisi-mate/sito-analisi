“All club members subscribe to the newsletter. No subscriber is under age $16$. Some guests at the event are under age $16$.” Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- Some guests at the event are not club members.
- No guest at the event is a club member.
- Some club members are under age $16$.
- All guests under age $16$ subscribe to the newsletter.

## Explanation

The first two premises have the form “All $A$ are $B$, and no $B$ is $C$,” so **no member is under age $16$**. By the third premise, there is at least one guest under age $16$, and this guest cannot be a member. Therefore at least one guest at the event is not a club member. “No guest is a member” does not follow because guests aged $16$ or older could be members. The statements about members under age $16$ and guests who subscribe contradict the premises.
