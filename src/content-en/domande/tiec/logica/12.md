What is the negation of “No hotel guest complained about the service”?

- All the hotel guests complained about the service.
- At least one hotel guest did not complain about the service.
- No hotel guest was satisfied with the service.
- At least one hotel guest complained about the service.

## Explanation

The statement beginning with “no” concerns *every* guest and becomes false as soon as a single guest complains. By the rule for **negating quantifiers**, the negation of “No $A$ is $B$” is “At least one $A$ is $B$,” giving “At least one hotel guest complained about the service.” The statement beginning with “all” says much more. In a hotel where just one guest complained and others did not, it would be false along with the original statement. “At least one guest did not complain” is consistent with the original statement, so it is not its negation.
