“The plane takes off only if the fog clears.” The fog did not clear this morning. Which conclusion is correct?

- It cannot be determined whether the plane took off this morning.
- The plane took off late this morning.
- The plane did not take off this morning.
- The plane took off this morning.

## Explanation

The statement “$A$ only if $N$,” with $A$ = “the plane takes off” and $N$ = “the fog clears,” means $A \Rightarrow N$. Thus the fog clearing is a **necessary condition** for takeoff. Since $N$ was false this morning, the rules of inference imply that $A$ was false too, so the plane did not take off. “It cannot be determined” reads “only if” as “if,” interchanging the antecedent and consequent.
