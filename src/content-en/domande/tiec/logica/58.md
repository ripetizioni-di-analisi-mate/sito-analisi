“No one taking the Saturday class has an annual pass. No annual pass holder pays for admission to the pool.” Which conclusion is correct?

- No one taking the Saturday class pays for admission to the pool.
- Everyone taking the Saturday class pays for admission to the pool.
- Some annual pass holders take the Saturday class.
- The premises do not determine whether people taking the Saturday class pay for admission.

## Explanation

The premises say that the pass holders' circle shares no points with either the circle for the class or the circle for those paying admission. They say nothing about the **relationship between the class and those paying admission**. These two circles can intersect, be disjoint, or lie one inside the other without touching the pass holders' circle. Thus “No one taking the class pays for admission” and “Everyone taking the class pays for admission” are both consistent with the premises, and neither follows. “Some annual pass holders take the Saturday class” contradicts the first premise.
