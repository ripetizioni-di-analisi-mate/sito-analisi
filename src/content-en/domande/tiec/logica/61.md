Six teams, A, B, C, D, E, and F, are ranked without ties. D is first, B is two places after A, C is immediately after B, and E is ahead of A. What is F's position?

- Fourth
- Second
- Fifth
- Sixth

## Explanation

If A is in position $k$, B is in position $k + 2$ and C in position $k + 3$, so $k + 3 \leq 6$ and $k \leq 3$. D occupies position $1$. A cannot be second because E must be ahead of A and cannot be first. Therefore A is **third**, E second, B fifth, and C sixth. The only open position is fourth, which goes to F.
