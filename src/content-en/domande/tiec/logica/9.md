“Showing a college student ID is enough to receive the museum's student discount.” Elena did not receive the student discount. Which conclusion is correct?

- Elena did not show a college student ID.
- Elena is not a college student.
- Elena showed a college student ID.
- It cannot be determined whether Elena showed a college student ID.

## Explanation

The words “is enough” indicate a **sufficient condition**, so the statement means “If you show a student ID, you receive the discount.” Elena did not receive it, and a valid inference from a false consequent is that the antecedent is false. Therefore Elena did not show a student ID. “Elena is not a college student” does not follow, since she might be a student who did not show her ID.
