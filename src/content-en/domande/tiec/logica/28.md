At a company, employees qualify for a bonus if and only if they have at least five years of service and a permanent contract. Which of the following employees does *not* qualify?

- Franco, $5$ years of service, permanent contract
- Elisa, $7$ years of service, permanent contract
- Gino, $9$ years of service, fixed-term contract
- Ilaria, $12$ years of service, permanent contract

## Explanation

The bonus requires a **conjunction**, which is true only when *both* parts are true. Gino has more than five years of service, but his contract is for a fixed term, so the second part is false and he does not qualify. Franco has exactly five years of service. Since “at least five” includes five, he meets both conditions. The same is true of Elisa and Ilaria.
