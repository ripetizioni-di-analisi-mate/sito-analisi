Which number completes the sequence $1,\ 4,\ 5,\ 9,\ 14,\ 23,\ \ldots$?

- $32$
- $46$
- $37$
- $28$

## Explanation

From the third term onward, each term is the **sum of the two preceding terms**, as shown by $1 + 4 = 5$, $4 + 5 = 9$, $5 + 9 = 14$, and $9 + 14 = 23$. The missing term is therefore $14 + 23 = 37$. The value $32 = 23 + 9$ repeats the last difference, and $46$ doubles the last term.
