What is the negation of “At least two of the five projects submitted were approved”?

- None of the five projects submitted was approved.
- At most two of the five projects submitted were approved.
- At least two of the five projects submitted were not approved.
- At most one of the five projects submitted was approved.

## Explanation

The statement says that the number of approved projects is $2$, $3$, $4$, or $5$. The negation describes exactly the remaining cases, with $0$ or $1$ approved, so it is “At most one of the five projects was approved.” “None was approved” leaves out the case of exactly one approval, which makes the original false. “At least two were not approved” can be true when three projects were approved, along with the original statement.
