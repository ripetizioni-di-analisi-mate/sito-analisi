Two trains leave at the same time from two cities $360$ km apart and travel toward each other, one at $70$ km/h and the other at $50$ km/h. How long does it take them to meet?

- $6$ hours
- $3$ hours
- $18$ hours
- $7$ hours and $12$ minutes

## Explanation

Each hour the trains reduce the distance between them by $70 + 50 = 120$ km because they travel toward each other and **their speeds add**. Therefore they meet after $\dfrac{360}{120} = 3$ hours. The answer $18$ hours uses the difference between the speeds, appropriate for trains traveling in the same direction. The answer $6$ hours uses the average speed $60$ km/h as the speed of a single train.
