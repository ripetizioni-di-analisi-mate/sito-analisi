Three friends are Aldo, Bea, and Ciro. Aldo or Bea went to the party. Bea did not go. If Ciro went, then Aldo did not. Who went to the party?

- Only Aldo
- Aldo and Ciro
- Only Ciro
- Aldo and Bea

## Explanation

The first statement is a disjunction. The second says Bea did not go, so Aldo went to the party. The third statement, “If Ciro went, then Aldo did not,” is equivalent to its **contrapositive**, “If Aldo went, then Ciro did not.” Since Aldo went, Ciro did not. Therefore only Aldo went to the party.
