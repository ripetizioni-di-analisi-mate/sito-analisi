Which of the following facts is sufficient to make “On every workday last week Anna rode her bike or walked to work” false?

- Anna never rode her bike to work.
- On Wednesday Anna went to work entirely by bus.
- On Monday Anna rode her bike halfway to work and walked the other half.
- Anna walked every day except Friday, when she rode her bike.

## Explanation

By the rule for **negating quantifiers**, the statement is false if on *at least one* workday Anna neither rode her bike nor walked to work. On Wednesday she traveled entirely by bus, so both parts of the disjunction were false that day and the statement is false. The other three facts do not suffice because each is consistent with Anna either riding her bike or walking every day. For example, if she never rides her bike and always walks, the statement is true.
