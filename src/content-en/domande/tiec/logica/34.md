Five friends sit on five chairs in a row numbered $1$ through $5$ from the left. Ugo sits on chair $3$, Vera sits immediately to Ugo's left, Zoe does not sit at either end, and Tea sits to Sara's left. Who sits on chair $5$?

- Zoe
- Tea
- Vera
- Sara

## Explanation

Ugo is on chair $3$, and Vera is immediately to his left, so she is on chair $2$. Chairs $1$, $4$, and $5$ remain. Zoe cannot sit at an end, so she sits on chair $4$. Tea and Sara occupy chairs $1$ and $5$, and since Tea is to Sara's left, Tea is on $1$ and **Sara on $5$**. Thus the only arrangement is Tea, Vera, Ugo, Zoe, Sara.
