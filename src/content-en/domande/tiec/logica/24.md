A gym has $120$ members. Of these, $85$ take yoga and $70$ take Pilates. What is the least possible number of members who take both classes?

- $15$
- $50$
- $70$
- $35$

## Explanation

The **formula for a union** gives $|Y \cap P| = |Y| + |P| - |Y \cup P| = 155 - |Y \cup P|$. The size of the union cannot exceed the number of members, $120$, so the size of the intersection is at least $155 - 120 = 35$. The value $35$ is attained when every member takes at least one class. The value $70$ is the maximum, attained when everyone taking Pilates also takes yoga.
