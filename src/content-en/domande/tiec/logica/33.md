The diagram shows how many students at a school attend music ($M$), theater ($T$), and sports ($S$) workshops. The number outside the circles counts those who attend none. How many students attend at least two workshops?

::venn{tipo="tre" nomi="M,T,S" elementi="a:9;b:7;c:12;ab:4;ac:5;bc:3;abc:2;u:6" titolo="Students attending music ($M$), theater ($T$), and sports ($S$) workshops."}

- $12$
- $14$
- $18$
- $6$

## Explanation

Students attending **at least two** workshops are in the three regions shared by exactly two circles or in the central region. The regions shared by exactly two contain $4$, $5$, and $3$ students, and the center contains $2$. Thus $4 + 5 + 3 + 2 = 14$ students attend at least two. The number $12$ overlooks the center, which belongs to all three circles and therefore lies in each pair as well. The number $18$ counts the $2$ students in the center three times by adding the full pairwise intersections $6$, $7$, and $5$.
