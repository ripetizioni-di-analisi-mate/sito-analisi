Luca continues $2,6,18,54$ by always multiplying by $3$. Bea uses the same four terms but continues by keeping third differences constant. What fifth term does Bea write?

- $130$
- $162$
- $110$
- $94$

## Explanation

First differences are $4,12,36$, second differences are $8,24$ and the third difference is $16$. The next second difference is $24+16=40$, so the next first difference is $36+40=76$. Bea writes $54+76=130$.
