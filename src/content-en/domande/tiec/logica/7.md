Which number completes the sequence $2,\ 6,\ 18,\ 54,\ \ldots$?

- $162$
- $108$
- $90$
- $216$

## Explanation

The differences $4, 12, 36$ are not constant, so look at the ratios. Each term is **three times** the preceding one because $\dfrac{6}{2} = \dfrac{18}{6} = \dfrac{54}{18} = 3$. The missing term is therefore $54 \cdot 3 = 162$. The value $108$ doubles the preceding term instead of tripling it, and $90$ adds $36$, the last difference.
