In a group of friends, $C$ is the set of dog owners and $G$ the set of cat owners. Which set contains exactly those who own just one of the two types of animals?

- $C \cap G$
- $C \cup G$
- $C \setminus G$
- $(C \cup G) \setminus (C \cap G)$

## Explanation

Someone who owns just one type has at least one animal and therefore belongs to $C \cup G$, but does not own both types and therefore does not belong to $C \cap G$. The required set is thus the **difference** $(C \cup G) \setminus (C \cap G)$. The union $C \cup G$ also includes those who own both, and $C \cap G$ contains only those who own both. The set $C \setminus G$ contains people with a dog and no cat but leaves out those with only a cat.
