The symbols $\heartsuit$ and $\clubsuit$ stand for positive integers. If $\heartsuit \cdot \heartsuit = 36$ and $\heartsuit \cdot \clubsuit = 42$, what is $\heartsuit + \clubsuit$?

- $12$
- $14$
- $7$
- $13$

## Explanation

The symbol $\heartsuit$ is a positive integer whose square equals $36$, so $\heartsuit = 6$. The second equation $6 \cdot \clubsuit = 42$ gives $\clubsuit = 7$. Therefore $\heartsuit + \clubsuit = 6 + 7 = 13$. The value $12$ is $\heartsuit + \heartsuit$, and $7$ is just $\clubsuit$.
