“All the school's pianists can read music. Some choir members cannot read music.” Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- No choir member is a pianist at the school.
- Some of the school's pianists are not choir members.
- Some choir members are pianists at the school.
- Some choir members are not pianists at the school.

## Explanation

The second premise guarantees at least one choir member who cannot read music. By the **contrapositive** of the first premise, anyone who cannot read music is not a pianist at the school, so that singer is not one. Thus some choir members are not pianists at the school. “No choir member is a pianist” does not follow because singers who can read music could be pianists. The premises say nothing about pianists who do not sing in the choir.
