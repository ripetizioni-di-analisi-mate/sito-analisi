“The parking gate rises if and only if the ticket has been paid for.” Today the gate did not rise. Which conclusion is correct?

- The ticket was paid for.
- The ticket was not paid for.
- It cannot be determined whether the ticket was paid for.
- The gate is broken.

## Explanation

The phrase “if and only if” asserts both $S \Rightarrow P$ and $P \Rightarrow S$, where $S$ = “the gate rises” and $P$ = “the ticket has been paid for.” Given the second implication and the fact that $S$ is false, the rules for **valid inferences** show that $P$ is false. Therefore the ticket was not paid for. If the premise were “The gate rises only if the ticket has been paid for,” the fact that the gate did not rise would leave it unclear whether the ticket was paid for. The statement “The gate is broken” does not follow from the information, which relates the gate's rising only to payment.
