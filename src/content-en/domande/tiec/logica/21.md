What is the negation of “Some downtown stores do not accept card payments”?

- No downtown store accepts card payments.
- Some downtown stores accept card payments.
- All downtown stores accept card payments.
- Every downtown store refuses card payments.

## Explanation

The statement says that *at least one* downtown store does not accept cards. It is false only if every downtown store accepts cards, so by the rule for **negating quantifiers**, its negation is “All downtown stores accept card payments.” At a location with both types of stores, “Some stores accept card payments” and the original statement can both be true. “No store accepts” and “Every store refuses” mean the same thing and strengthen the original statement instead of negating it.
