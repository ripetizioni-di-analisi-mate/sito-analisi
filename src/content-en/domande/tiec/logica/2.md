In an apartment building with $48$ households, $30$ have a car, $22$ have a bicycle, and $12$ have both. How many households have exactly one of these two forms of transportation?

- $40$
- $28$
- $18$
- $8$

## Explanation

There are $30 - 12 = 18$ households with a car but no bicycle, and $22 - 12 = 10$ with a bicycle but no car. Thus $18 + 10 = 28$ households have **exactly one** of the two. The number $40 = 30 + 22 - 12$ also counts the $12$ households that have both, and $8 = 48 - 40$ counts those that have neither.
