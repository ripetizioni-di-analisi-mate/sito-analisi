On a test, Dino scored higher than Ezio but lower than Fabio. Gilda scored the same as Ezio, Hilde scored higher than Fabio, and Ivo scored higher than Ezio. Which statement is necessarily true?

- Ivo scored higher than Dino.
- Dino scored lower than Gilda.
- Ivo scored the same as Hilde.
- Gilda scored lower than Fabio.

## Explanation

The information gives the chain $H > F > D > E$ and the equality $G = E$. Thus Gilda scored the same as Ezio, who is below Fabio. By the **transitive property**, Gilda scored lower than Fabio. All we know about Ivo is that he scored higher than Ezio, so his scores relative to Dino and Hilde cannot be determined. “Dino scored lower than Gilda” contradicts $D > E = G$.
