Four painters paint $3$ apartments in $6$ days. Working at the same pace, how many days would $6$ painters need to paint $9$ apartments of the same size?

- $12$
- $18$
- $27$
- $4$

## Explanation

Painting one apartment takes $\dfrac{4 \cdot 6}{3} = 8$ days of work for one painter. Nine apartments therefore take $9 \cdot 8 = 72$ painter-days, which six painters complete in $\dfrac{72}{6} = 12$ days. The value $18$ accounts only for the tripling of the apartments, overlooking the increase in painters. The value $27$ also treats the number of painters as **directly proportional** to the time.
