What is the negation of “Exactly one of the two candidates will be hired”?

- Neither candidate will be hired.
- At least one of the two candidates will not be hired.
- Neither candidate will be hired or both candidates will be hired.
- Both candidates will be hired.

## Explanation

There are three possibilities with two candidates. Neither is hired, exactly one is hired, or both are hired. The given statement is true only when exactly one is hired, so its negation must hold in the other two cases. Thus the negation is “Neither candidate will be hired or both candidates will be hired,” the negation of an **exclusive or**. “Neither will be hired” and “Both will be hired” each describe just one of the two cases. “At least one will not be hired” also holds when exactly one candidate is hired.
