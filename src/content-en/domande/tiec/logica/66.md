“All the contest finalists are under age $30$. All the contest finalists live in Lombardy.” Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- Everyone under age $30$ lives in Lombardy.
- Everyone who lives in Lombardy is under age $30$.
- Some people under age $30$ live in Lombardy.
- Some contest finalists do not live in Lombardy.

## Explanation

These questions assume that each category mentioned has at least one member, so there is at least one finalist. The first premise says that this finalist is under age $30$ and the second that they live in Lombardy. Thus **at least one person** under age $30$ lives in Lombardy. The two conclusions beginning with “everyone” do not follow because the premises say nothing about people who are not finalists. “Some finalists do not live in Lombardy” contradicts the second premise.
