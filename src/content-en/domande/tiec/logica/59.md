One faucet fills a tank in $6$ hours and another fills it in $3$ hours. How long does it take them to fill the tank together?

- $4$ hours and $30$ minutes
- $2$ hours
- $9$ hours
- $3$ hours

## Explanation

In one hour the first faucet fills $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of the tank and the second fills $\dfrac{1}{3}$, so together they fill $\dfrac{1}{6} + \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. **Add the fractions filled in one hour**. The entire tank therefore fills in $2$ hours. The answer $4$ hours and $30$ minutes is the mean of the two times, and $9$ hours is their sum. Both exceed the time for the faster faucet alone, whereas two faucets together take less time than either alone.
