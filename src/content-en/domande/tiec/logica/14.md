On a map with a scale of $1 : 25\,000$, two towns are $8$ cm apart. What is the actual distance between the towns?

- $20$ km
- $2$ km
- $200$ m
- $25$ km

## Explanation

A scale of $1 : 25\,000$ means that $1$ cm on the map corresponds to $25\,000$ cm on the ground, so the actual distance is **directly proportional** to the map distance. The $8$ cm on the map corresponds to $8 \cdot 25\,000 = 200\,000$ cm on the ground. Since $1$ km equals $100\,000$ cm, the actual distance is $2$ km. The value $20$ km divides by $10\,000$ instead of $100\,000$, and the value $200$ m divides by $1000$.
