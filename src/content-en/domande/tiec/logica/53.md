Which number completes the sequence $1,\ 2,\ 6,\ 24,\ 120,\ \ldots$?

- $240$
- $600$
- $144$
- $720$

## Explanation

The ratios of consecutive terms are $2, 3, 4, 5$, so each term is the preceding one times a **number that increases by $1$** at each step. The next multiplier is $6$, giving the missing term $120 \cdot 6 = 720$. The value $240$ doubles the last term, and $600$ repeats the multiplier $5$.
