Account P has more followers than account Q, account R has more followers than Q, and account S has fewer followers than R. No two have the same number of followers. Which statement must be false?

- Q has more followers than R.
- S has more followers than P.
- P has more followers than R.
- S has fewer followers than Q.

## Explanation

The information $R > Q$ makes “Q has more followers than R” necessarily false. The other three statements depend on comparisons **that cannot be determined** from the information. The ordering $R > S > P > Q$ satisfies all the conditions and makes “S has more followers than P” true. The ordering $P > R > S > Q$ makes “P has more followers than R” true, and $R > P > Q > S$ makes “S has fewer followers than Q” true. Thus each of these three statements can be true.
