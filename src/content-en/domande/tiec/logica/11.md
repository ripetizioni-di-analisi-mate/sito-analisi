What is the negation of “If there is a strike tomorrow, the post office will stay closed”?

- If there is no strike tomorrow, the post office will stay open.
- There will be no strike tomorrow and the post office will stay closed.
- There will be a strike tomorrow and the post office will stay open.
- If there is a strike tomorrow, the post office will stay open.

## Explanation

The implication “if $A$, then $B$” is false only when $A$ is true and $B$ is false. By the rule for **negating an implication**, its negation is therefore $A \land \lnot B$, a statement with “and” and no “if.” With $A$ = “there is a strike tomorrow” and $B$ = “the post office stays closed,” this gives “There will be a strike tomorrow and the post office will stay open.” The form of the two answers containing “if” rules them out. “There will be no strike tomorrow and the post office will stay closed” describes a case where the original statement is true.
