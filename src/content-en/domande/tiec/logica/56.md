“If a customer pays by bank transfer, they receive the invoice within two days” and “If a customer receives the invoice within two days, they get a discount on their next order” are both true. Which statement necessarily follows?

- If a customer gets a discount on their next order, they paid by bank transfer.
- If a customer does not pay by bank transfer, they do not get a discount on their next order.
- If a customer receives the invoice within two days, they paid by bank transfer.
- If a customer does not get a discount on their next order, they did not pay by bank transfer.

## Explanation

Let $P$ = “pays by bank transfer,” $F$ = “receives the invoice within two days,” and $S$ = “gets the discount.” The statements give $P \Rightarrow F$ and $F \Rightarrow S$, so chaining them gives $P \Rightarrow S$. The **contrapositive** of $P \Rightarrow S$ is $\lnot S \Rightarrow \lnot P$, or “If a customer does not get the discount, they did not pay by bank transfer.” “If a customer gets the discount, they paid by bank transfer” is the converse $S \Rightarrow P$. “If a customer receives the invoice within two days, they paid by bank transfer” is the converse of $P \Rightarrow F$. “If a customer does not pay by bank transfer, they do not get the discount” is the inverse and is false for a customer who receives the invoice on time by another route.
