Which number replaces the dots in the sequence $4,\ 12,\ 9,\ 27,\ 24,\ \ldots$?

- $21$
- $72$
- $69$
- $48$

## Explanation

The steps are $\times 3$, $-3$, $\times 3$, and $-3$, so **two operations alternate**. The last step, from $27$ to $24$, is a subtraction. The next must therefore be a multiplication by $3$, giving the missing term $24 \cdot 3 = 72$. The value $21$ subtracts $3$ again, and $69 = 24 \cdot 3 - 3$ applies both operations in one step.
