Which number comes after $31$ in the sequence $3,\ 5,\ 8,\ 13,\ 20,\ 31,\ \ldots$?

- $42$
- $51$
- $45$
- $44$

## Explanation

The differences between consecutive terms are $2, 3, 5, 7, 11$, the **prime numbers** in order. The next difference is the prime after $11$, namely $13$, so the missing term is $31 + 13 = 44$. The value $51 = 20 + 31$ uses the sum of the two preceding terms, a rule that works for the first few terms but fails at $20$ because $8 + 13 = 21$. The value $42$ repeats the difference $11$.
