Which number completes the sequence $1,\ -2,\ 4,\ -8,\ 16,\ \ldots$?

- $-32$
- $32$
- $-16$
- $8$

## Explanation

The ratio of each term to its predecessor is always $-2$ because $\dfrac{-2}{1} = \dfrac{4}{-2} = \dfrac{-8}{4} = \dfrac{16}{-8} = -2$. The sequence therefore has a negative **constant ratio**, so the signs alternate. The missing term is $16 \cdot (-2) = -32$. The value $32$ doubles the magnitude without changing the sign, $-16$ changes the sign without doubling the magnitude, and $8$ divides by $2$ instead of multiplying.
