At a school the ratio of students to teachers is $15 : 1$. After $60$ new students and $6$ new teachers arrive, the ratio becomes $14 : 1$. How many teachers were there before they arrived?

- $30$
- $18$
- $24$
- $20$

## Explanation

If there were initially $d$ teachers, there were $15d$ students. After the arrivals there are $15d + 60$ students and $d + 6$ teachers, giving the **equation** $15d + 60 = 14\,(d + 6)$. This gives $15d + 60 = 14d + 84$, or $d = 24$. As a check, the school had $360$ students and $24$ teachers before and $420$ students and $30$ teachers afterward, with $\dfrac{420}{30} = 14$. The value $30$ is the number of teachers after the arrivals.
