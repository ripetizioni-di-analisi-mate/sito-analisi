“If Tommaso wins the scholarship, he moves to Milan.” Tommaso moved to Milan. Which conclusion is correct?

- Tommaso won the scholarship.
- It cannot be determined whether Tommaso won the scholarship.
- Tommaso did not win the scholarship.
- Tommaso moved to Milan for work.

## Explanation

The statement is the implication $B \Rightarrow M$, where $B$ = “Tommaso wins the scholarship” and $M$ = “Tommaso moves to Milan.” We know that $M$ is true. Inferring $B$ from $M$ is **affirming the consequent**, an invalid inference. Tommaso could have moved for another reason or because he won the scholarship, and the given statement is true in either case. Therefore it cannot be determined whether he won the scholarship.
