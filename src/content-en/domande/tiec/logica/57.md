“Not everyone enrolled in the course has paid the fee” is true. Which statement is necessarily true?

- At least one person enrolled in the course has not paid the fee.
- No one enrolled in the course has paid the fee.
- At least one person enrolled in the course has paid the fee.
- Most of the people enrolled in the course have not paid the fee.

## Explanation

“Not everyone enrolled has paid” negates “Everyone enrolled has paid.” By the rule for **negating quantifiers**, it is equivalent to “At least one person enrolled in the course has not paid the fee.” “At least one person has paid” is not guaranteed because “not everyone” includes the case where no one paid. The statements beginning with “no one” and “most” are possible, but the information does not guarantee either one.
