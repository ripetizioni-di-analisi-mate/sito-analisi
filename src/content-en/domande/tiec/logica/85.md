Which of the following statements is *not* a negation of “All the notebooks on the shelf contain lined paper”?

- None of the notebooks on the shelf contain lined paper.
- Not all the notebooks on the shelf contain lined paper.
- At least one notebook on the shelf does not contain lined paper.
- There is a notebook on the shelf that does not contain lined paper.

## Explanation

The original statement becomes false as soon as one notebook on the shelf does not contain lined paper. By the rule for **negating quantifiers**, “Not all the notebooks contain lined paper,” “At least one notebook does not contain lined paper,” and “There is a notebook that does not contain lined paper” express the same negation. “None of the notebooks contain lined paper” is false on a shelf with both lined and graph-paper notebooks, where the original statement is false too. Thus this statement is not the negation.
