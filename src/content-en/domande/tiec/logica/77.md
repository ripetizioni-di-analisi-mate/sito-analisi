Which of the following is a necessary and sufficient condition for an integer to be divisible by $6$?

- Being even
- Being divisible by $12$
- Being divisible by both $2$ and $3$
- Having a digit sum of $6$

## Explanation

Since $6 = 2 \cdot 3$ and its factors $2$ and $3$ are prime, an integer is divisible by $6$ if and only if it is divisible by both $2$ and $3$. This is therefore a **necessary and sufficient condition**. Being even is necessary but not sufficient, as $4$ shows. Being divisible by $12$ is sufficient but not necessary, as $6$ itself shows. Having a digit sum of $6$ is not necessary, as $12$ shows.
