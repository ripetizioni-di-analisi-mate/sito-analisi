For a person to have been born in Piedmont, being born in Turin is

- necessary but not sufficient.
- necessary and sufficient.
- sufficient but not necessary.
- neither necessary nor sufficient.

## Explanation

Turin is in Piedmont, so anyone born in Turin was born in Piedmont, giving the implication $T \Rightarrow P$. Thus being born in Turin is a **sufficient condition** for being born in Piedmont. It is not necessary because someone born in Novara was born in Piedmont without being born in Turin.
