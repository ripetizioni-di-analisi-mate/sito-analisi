The letters $A$ and $B$ represent two digits, neither of which is $0$. The two-digit number $AB$ is $4$ times the sum of its digits, and the ones digit $B$ is $3$ greater than the tens digit $A$. What is the number $AB$?

- $63$
- $36$
- $48$
- $24$

## Explanation

The number $AB$ equals $10A + B$, so the first condition becomes $10A + B = 4\,(A + B)$, giving $6A = 3B$ and $B = 2A$. Applying the second condition $B = A + 3$ gives $2A = A + 3$, so $A = 3$ and $B = 6$. The number is therefore $36$, and indeed $4 \cdot (3 + 6) = 36$. The numbers $48$ and $24$ satisfy $B = 2A$, but their digits differ by $4$ and $2$, respectively. The number $63$ reverses the two digits.
