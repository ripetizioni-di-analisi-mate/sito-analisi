In a class the ratio of boys to girls is $3 : 5$, and there are $12$ boys. How many students are in the class?

- $20$
- $32$
- $17$
- $60$

## Explanation

The ratio $3 : 5$ means the boys account for $3$ equal parts and the girls for $5$, so the class contains $3 + 5 = 8$ parts in all. The $12$ boys account for $3$ parts, so **one part** represents $\dfrac{12}{3} = 4$ students. Thus there are $8 \cdot 4 = 32$ students, including $20$ girls. The value $20$ counts only the girls, and $60 = 12 \cdot 5$ multiplies the number of boys by the girls' number of parts.
