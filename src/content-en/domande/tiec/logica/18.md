The equations $\triangle = 2\,\square$, $\square + 3 = \bigcirc$, and $\triangle + \bigcirc = 18$ hold. What is $\bigcirc$?

- $5$
- $8$
- $10$
- $6$

## Explanation

Express every symbol in terms of $\square$, using $\triangle = 2\,\square$ and $\bigcirc = \square + 3$. The third equation becomes $2\,\square + \square + 3 = 18$, or $3\,\square = 15$, so $\square = 5$. Therefore $\bigcirc = 5 + 3 = 8$, and indeed $\triangle + \bigcirc = 10 + 8 = 18$. The value $10$ is that of $\triangle$, and $5$ is that of $\square$.
