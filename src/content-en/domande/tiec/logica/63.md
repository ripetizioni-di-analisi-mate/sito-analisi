Three $\clubsuit$ objects weigh as much as two $\diamondsuit$ objects, and one $\diamondsuit$ weighs $4$ grams more than one $\clubsuit$. How many grams does one $\clubsuit$ weigh?

- $8$
- $12$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The two pieces of information give $3\clubsuit = 2\diamondsuit$ and $\diamondsuit = \clubsuit + 4$. Substituting the second equation into the first gives $3\clubsuit = 2\clubsuit + 8$, so $\clubsuit = 8$ grams. As a check, $\diamondsuit = 12$ grams and $3 \cdot 8 = 2 \cdot 12 = 24$. The value $12$ is the weight of $\diamondsuit$.
