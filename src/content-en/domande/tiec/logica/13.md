“No reptile has feathers. Some animals in the park are reptiles.” Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- No animal in the park has feathers.
- Some animals in the park have feathers.
- All animals without feathers are reptiles.
- Some animals in the park do not have feathers.

## Explanation

The second premise guarantees at least one animal in the park that is a reptile. By the first premise, that animal has no feathers, so at least one animal in the park has no feathers. The pattern “Some $A$ are $B$, and no $B$ is $C$” is one of the **valid patterns** and gives “Some $A$ are not $C$.” The conclusion “No animal in the park has feathers” does not follow because the park might also have birds. “Some animals in the park have feathers” is possible but not guaranteed.
