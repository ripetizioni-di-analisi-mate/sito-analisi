Each sensor reading is one more than the cube of its position in the series. Its readings so far are $2,9,28,65$. What is the fifth reading?

- $126$
- $125$
- $102$
- $127$

## Explanation

The terms are $1^3 + 1$, $2^3 + 1$, $3^3 + 1$, and $4^3 + 1$, or **cubes plus $1$**. The next term is therefore $5^3 + 1 = 126$. The value $125$ omits the addition of $1$, and $102 = 65 + 37$ repeats the last difference.
