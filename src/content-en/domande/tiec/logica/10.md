The statement “Tomorrow Luca will go to the gym or the pool, but not both” is true. The following day, we learn that Luca went to the pool. Which conclusion is correct?

- Luca did not go to the gym.
- Luca also went to the gym.
- It cannot be determined whether Luca went to the gym.
- Luca went neither to the gym nor to the pool.

## Explanation

The phrase “but not both” makes “or” **exclusive**, meaning that *exactly one* part must be true. Since Luca went to the pool, the second part is true, so the first part must be false. Therefore Luca did not go to the gym. “It cannot be determined” would be correct with an inclusive “or,” which also allows him to visit both places.
