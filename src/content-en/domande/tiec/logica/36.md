For two numbers $a$ and $b$, define $a \star b = 2a - b$. What is $(3 \star 1) \star 4$?

- $8$
- $10$
- $6$
- $14$

## Explanation

First evaluate $3 \star 1 = 2 \cdot 3 - 1 = 5$. Then apply the **definition** again with $a = 5$ and $b = 4$ to obtain $5 \star 4 = 2 \cdot 5 - 4 = 6$. The value $8$ is $3 \star (1 \star 4)$ because $1 \star 4 = -2$ and $3 \star (-2) = 8$, so it uses a different order of operations. The value $10$ forgets to subtract $4$ in the second step.
