Each symbol always stands for the same number. If $\square + \square + \triangle = 14$ and $\square + \triangle + \triangle = 16$, what is $\square + \triangle$?

- $15$
- $10$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

Adding the equations counts each symbol three times, giving $3\,(\square + \triangle) = 30$. Therefore $\square + \triangle = 10$, without having to find the two individual values. As a check, subtracting the first equation from the second gives $\triangle - \square = 2$, from which $\square = 4$ and $\triangle = 6$ follow. The values $4$ and $6$ are the individual symbol values, and $15$ is the mean of $14$ and $16$.
