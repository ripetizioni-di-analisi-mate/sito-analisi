At a restaurant, every customer chooses set menu A or set menu B. Anyone who chooses menu A receives coffee, and anyone who chooses menu B receives coffee. Which statement necessarily follows?

- Anyone who receives coffee chose menu A.
- No customer chooses both menus.
- Anyone who does not choose menu A does not receive coffee.
- Every customer receives coffee.

## Explanation

Every customer falls into at least one of the two cases, menu A or menu B. In each case the customer receives coffee, so **reasoning by cases** shows that every customer receives coffee. “Anyone who receives coffee chose menu A” reverses the first implication and is false for someone who chose only menu B. “Anyone who does not choose menu A does not receive coffee” contradicts the second rule because anyone who does not choose A chooses B. Finally, the “or” is inclusive, so the information does not rule out customers who choose both menus.
