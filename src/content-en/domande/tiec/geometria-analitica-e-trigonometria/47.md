What is the equation of the circle whose diameter has endpoints $A(-1;3)$ and $B(5;-5)$?

- $(x - 3)^2 + (y + 4)^2 = 25$
- $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 25$
- $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 100$
- $(x + 2)^2 + (y - 1)^2 = 25$

## Explanation

The center is the **midpoint** of the diameter, namely $\left(\dfrac{-1 + 5}{2};\dfrac{3 - 5}{2}\right) = (2;-1)$. The diameter has length $\sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = 10$, so the radius is $5$ and $r^2 = 25$. The equation of the circle is therefore $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 25$. The equation with $100$ uses the diameter in place of the radius, the one with $(x + 2)$ and $(y - 1)$ reverses the signs of the center coordinates, and the one with center $(3;-4)$ uses half the coordinate differences instead of the averages.
