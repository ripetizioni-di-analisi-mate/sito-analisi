A rectangular area is to be enclosed with $40$ m of fencing along a straight wall that serves as the fourth side. If $x$ is the length in meters of each of the two sides perpendicular to the wall, the enclosed area is $A = x(40 - 2x)$. What is the maximum area?

- $400$ m$^2$
- $100$ m$^2$
- $150$ m$^2$
- $200$ m$^2$

## Explanation

The graph of the area $A = -2x^2 + 40x$ is a **parabola** with $a = -2 < 0$, so the maximum is the vertical coordinate of its vertex. The horizontal coordinate of the vertex is $x = -\dfrac{40}{2 \cdot (-2)} = 10$ m, and the side parallel to the wall is $40 - 20 = 20$ m long. Thus the maximum area is $10 \cdot 20 = 200$ m$^2$. The value $100$ m$^2$ is the area of a square of side $10$ m, which would be obtained by fencing the fourth side as well, and $400$ m$^2$ is the square of $20$. The value $150$ m$^2$ corresponds to $x = 5$ m or $x = 15$ m.
