On the unit circle, what are the coordinates of the point corresponding to the angle $225^\circ$?

- $\left(-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2};-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)$
- $(-1;-1)$
- $\left(-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2};\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2};-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)$

## Explanation

The angle $225^\circ$ equals $180^\circ + 45^\circ$, so its point is the reflection across the origin of the point for $45^\circ$, which is $\left(\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2};\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)$. The coordinates are therefore $\left(-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2};-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)$, both negative as expected in the third quadrant. The point with positive horizontal coordinate and negative vertical coordinate corresponds to $315^\circ$, and the one with negative horizontal coordinate and positive vertical coordinate corresponds to $135^\circ$. The point $(-1;-1)$ is at distance $\sqrt{2}$ from the origin, so it does not lie on the unit circle.
