Given $\sin 25^\circ \approx 0.42$, what is the approximate value of $\cos 65^\circ$?

- $0.58$
- $-0.42$
- $0.42$
- $0.91$

## Explanation

The angles $25^\circ$ and $65^\circ$ are complementary because $25^\circ + 65^\circ = 90^\circ$. By the relation $\cos x = \sin(90^\circ - x)$ seen with the **special angle values**, $\cos 65^\circ = \sin 25^\circ$, so $\cos 65^\circ \approx 0.42$. The value $0.91$ is approximately $\cos 25^\circ$, which equals $\sqrt{1 - 0.42^2}$ by the Pythagorean identity. The value $0.58$ comes from calculating $1 - 0.42$, and $-0.42$ has the wrong sign because $65^\circ$ is in the first quadrant, where cosine is positive.
