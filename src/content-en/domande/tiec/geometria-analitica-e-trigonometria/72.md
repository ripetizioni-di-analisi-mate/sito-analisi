Approximately how many degrees are in an angle of $1$ radian?

- $360^\circ$
- $0.017^\circ$
- $180^\circ$
- $57^\circ$

## Explanation

To convert from **radians** to degrees, multiply by $\dfrac{180}{\pi}$, so $1$ radian corresponds to $\dfrac{180^\circ}{\pi}$. Since $\pi \approx 3.14$, this is approximately $57^\circ$. The value $180^\circ$ corresponds to $\pi$ radians and $360^\circ$ to $2\pi$ radians. The value $0.017^\circ$ multiplies by $\dfrac{\pi}{180}$, using the conversion in the opposite direction.
