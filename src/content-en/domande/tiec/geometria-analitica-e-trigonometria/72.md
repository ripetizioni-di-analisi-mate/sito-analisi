A wheel of radius $30$ cm rolls $45$ cm on a plane without slipping. Through what angle does it turn, in radians?

- $\dfrac23$
- $3\pi$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac32$

## Explanation

With no slipping, the distance travelled is the arc length $s=r\theta$. The angle in radians is therefore $s/r=45/30=3/2$.
