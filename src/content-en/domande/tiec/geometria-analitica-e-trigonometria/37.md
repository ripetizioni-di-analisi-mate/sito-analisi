For what value are the lines $(a + 2)x + 4y - 1 = 0$ and $3x + 2y + 5 = 0$ parallel?

- $a = -\dfrac{14}{3}$
- $a = 1$
- $a = -8$
- $a = 4$

## Explanation

The first line has slope $-\dfrac{a + 2}{4}$ and the second has slope $-\dfrac{3}{2}$. The lines are **parallel** if their slopes are equal, that is, if $\dfrac{a + 2}{4} = \dfrac{3}{2}$. This gives $a + 2 = 6$, so $a = 4$. The value $a = 1$ equates the coefficients of $x$ without accounting for those of $y$, and $a = -\dfrac{14}{3}$ makes the lines perpendicular. The value $a = -8$ uses the wrong sign in the equality $a + 2 = -6$.
