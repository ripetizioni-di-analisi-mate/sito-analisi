For every angle $x$ with $\cos x \neq 0$, what does the expression $\tan x \cdot \cos x$ equal?

- $\dfrac{\sin x}{\cos^2 x}$
- $\sin x$
- $\cos^2 x$
- $1$

## Explanation

By definition, $\tan x = \dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}$, which is defined because $\cos x \neq 0$. Multiplying by $\cos x$ cancels the denominator, leaving $\sin x$. The expression $\dfrac{\sin x}{\cos^2 x}$ results from dividing by $\cos x$ instead of multiplying. The value $1$ occurs only for angles satisfying $\sin x = 1$, for example $90^\circ$, but there $\cos x = 0$ and the tangent is undefined.
