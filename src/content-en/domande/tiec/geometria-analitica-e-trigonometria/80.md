Which of the following numbers cannot be the sine of an angle?

- $\sqrt{2} - 1$
- $\sqrt{5} - 1$
- $-0.99$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

The sine of an angle is the vertical coordinate of a point on the unit circle, so it lies between $-1$ and $1$. Since $\sqrt{5} \approx 2.24$, the number $\sqrt{5} - 1 \approx 1.24$ is greater than $1$ and cannot be a sine. The other three numbers lie between $-1$ and $1$ because $\sqrt{2} - 1 \approx 0.41$ and $-\dfrac{\pi}{4} \approx -0.79$.
