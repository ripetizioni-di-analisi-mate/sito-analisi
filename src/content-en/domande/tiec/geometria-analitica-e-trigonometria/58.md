Which of the following points lies on the parabola $y = x^2 - 3x + 1$?

- $(2;-3)$
- $(-1;5)$
- $(1;1)$
- $(-2;-1)$

## Explanation

A point lies on a parabola if its vertical coordinate equals the value of the expression evaluated at its horizontal coordinate. For $x = -1$, we obtain $y = 1 + 3 + 1 = 5$, so the point $(-1;5)$ lies on the parabola. At $x = 1$ and $x = 2$, the value is $y = -1$, so $(1;1)$ and $(2;-3)$ do not lie on it. At $x = -2$, we obtain $y = 4 + 6 + 1 = 11$. The value $-1$ results from calculating $-3 \cdot (-2)$ as $-6$.
