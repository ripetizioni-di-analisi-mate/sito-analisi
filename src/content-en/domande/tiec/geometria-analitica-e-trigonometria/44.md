What is the distance from $P(1;2)$ to the line $3x - 4y + 15 = 0$?

- $\dfrac{26}{5}$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{10}{7}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The distance from the point $(x_0;y_0)$ to the line $ax + by + c = 0$ is $\dfrac{|ax_0 + by_0 + c|}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}}$. The numerator is $|3 - 8 + 15| = 10$ and the denominator is $\sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$, so the distance is $2$. The value $10$ omits the denominator, and $\dfrac{10}{7}$ divides by $|a| + |b| = 7$ instead of $\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$. The value $\dfrac{26}{5}$ results from calculating $-4 \cdot 2$ as $+8$.
