Which center and radius does the circle $x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 6y + 1 = 0$ have?

- center $(1;-3)$ and radius $9$
- center $(1;-3)$ and radius $\sqrt{10}$
- center $(-1;3)$ and radius $3$
- center $(1;-3)$ and radius $3$

## Explanation

By the rule for the **center and radius**, with $a = -2$ and $b = 6$ the center is $\left(-\dfrac{a}{2};-\dfrac{b}{2}\right) = (1;-3)$. Under the square root, add the squares of the center coordinates and subtract the constant term $c = 1$, so $r = \sqrt{1 + 9 - 1} = 3$. The center $(-1;3)$ has the wrong signs, the radius $\sqrt{10}$ omits the constant term, and $9$ is the square of the radius.
