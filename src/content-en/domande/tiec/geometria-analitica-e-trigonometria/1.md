What is the distance between $A(-3;-2)$ and $B(2;10)$?

- $13$
- $169$
- $\sqrt{65}$
- $17$

## Explanation

The formula for the **distance between two points** uses the coordinate differences $x_B - x_A = 2 - (-3) = 5$ and $y_B - y_A = 10 - (-2) = 12$. Therefore $\overline{AB} = \sqrt{5^2 + 12^2} = \sqrt{169} = 13$. The value $17$ is the sum of the legs, $5 + 12$, and $169$ results from omitting the square root. The value $\sqrt{65}$ comes from adding the coordinates instead of subtracting them, that is, from $(-3 + 2)^2 + (-2 + 10)^2 = 1 + 64$.
