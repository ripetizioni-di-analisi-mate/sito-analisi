A line passes through $P(2;1)$ and makes an angle with the positive $x$-axis of $135^\circ$, measured counterclockwise. What is its equation?

- $y = -x + 1$
- $y = x - 1$
- $y = -x + 3$
- $y = -x - 3$

## Explanation

The **slope** of a line is the tangent of its angle with the positive $x$-axis, so $m = \tan 135^\circ$. The point for $135^\circ$ on the unit circle is the reflection of the point for $45^\circ$ across the $y$-axis, so $\tan 135^\circ = -\tan 45^\circ = -1$. The line through $P$ with slope $-1$ is $y - 1 = -(x - 2)$, or $y = -x + 3$. The line $y = x - 1$ uses $\tan 45^\circ$, while $y = -x - 3$ and $y = -x + 1$ have the correct slope but do not pass through $P$.
