The hypotenuse of a right triangle has length $12$ and one acute angle measures $30^\circ$. What is the area of the triangle?

- $18$
- $18\sqrt{3}$
- $72$
- $36\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

When **solving a right triangle**, the leg opposite the angle of $30^\circ$ has length $12 \sin 30^\circ = 6$, and the adjacent leg has length $12 \cos 30^\circ = 6\sqrt{3}$. The legs are perpendicular, so the area is their product divided by $2$, giving $\dfrac{6 \cdot 6\sqrt{3}}{2} = 18\sqrt{3}$. The value $36\sqrt{3}$ omits division by $2$, and $18$ sets both legs equal to $6$. The value $72$ multiplies the hypotenuse by one leg.
