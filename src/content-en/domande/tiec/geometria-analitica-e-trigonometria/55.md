On a Cartesian coordinate plane with distances measured in kilometers, an antenna is at $(2;1)$. Its signal reaches points less than $5$ km from the antenna. Which of the following towns receives the signal?

- the town at $(5;4)$
- the town at $(6;5)$
- the town at $(2;7)$
- the town at $(-3;2)$

## Explanation

A town receives the signal if it is **inside** the circle with center $(2;1)$ and radius $5$, that is, if its squared distance from the antenna is less than $25$. For $(5;4)$, we obtain $3^2 + 3^2 = 18 < 25$, so the town receives the signal. For $(-3;2)$, $(6;5)$, and $(2;7)$, the squared distances are $26$, $32$, and $36$, respectively. They exceed $25$, so those towns are outside the circle. Checking only the difference between the horizontal coordinates would suggest $(-3;2)$ because that difference is exactly $5$.
