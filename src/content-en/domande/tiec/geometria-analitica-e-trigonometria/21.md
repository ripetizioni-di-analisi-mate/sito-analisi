The line $3x + 4y - 12 = 0$ forms a triangle with the coordinate axes. What is the area of this triangle?

- $6$
- $5$
- $7$
- $12$

## Explanation

Setting $y = 0$ gives $x = 4$, and setting $x = 0$ gives $y = 3$, so the line crosses the axes at $(4;0)$ and $(0;3)$. The legs of the triangle lie along the axes and have lengths $4$ and $3$, so its area is $\dfrac{4 \cdot 3}{2} = 6$. The value $12$ omits division by $2$, $7$ is the sum of the legs, and $5$ is the length of the hypotenuse.
