What is the value of $\tan\dfrac{\pi}{3}$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
- $\sqrt{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$

## Explanation

The angle $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ corresponds to $60^\circ$. From the **special angle values**, $\sin 60^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ and $\cos 60^\circ = \dfrac{1}{2}$, so $\tan 60^\circ = \dfrac{\sin 60^\circ}{\cos 60^\circ} = \sqrt{3}$. The value $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ is the tangent of $30^\circ$, while $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$ are the sine and cosine of $60^\circ$.
