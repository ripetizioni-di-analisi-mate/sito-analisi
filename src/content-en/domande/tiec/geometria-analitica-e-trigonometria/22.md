What is the equation of the line through $A(-1;4)$ and $B(3;-4)$?

- $2x - y + 6 = 0$
- $2x + y + 2 = 0$
- $x + 2y - 7 = 0$
- $2x + y - 2 = 0$

## Explanation

The slope is $m = \dfrac{-4 - 4}{3 - (-1)} = \dfrac{-8}{4} = -2$. The **line through the two points** is therefore $y - 4 = -2(x + 1)$, or $y = -2x + 2$, written in general form as $2x + y - 2 = 0$. As a check, the point $B$ gives $6 - 4 - 2 = 0$. The lines $x + 2y - 7 = 0$ and $2x - y + 6 = 0$ pass through $A$ but miss $B$ because one inverts the ratio and the other uses the wrong sign for $m$. The line $2x + y + 2 = 0$ has the correct slope but passes through neither point.
