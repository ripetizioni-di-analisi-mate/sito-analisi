Which of the following inequalities is true?

- $\tan 45^\circ < \sin 20^\circ < \cos 20^\circ$
- $\sin 20^\circ < \tan 45^\circ < \cos 20^\circ$
- $\sin 20^\circ < \cos 20^\circ < \tan 45^\circ$
- $\cos 20^\circ < \sin 20^\circ < \tan 45^\circ$

## Explanation

Between $0^\circ$ and $90^\circ$, sine increases and cosine decreases, and at $45^\circ$ they are equal. Since $20^\circ < 45^\circ$, we have $\sin 20^\circ < \sin 45^\circ = \cos 45^\circ < \cos 20^\circ$. Also, $\cos 20^\circ < 1$ because between $0^\circ$ and $90^\circ$ the cosine equals $1$ only at zero, and $\tan 45^\circ = 1$. Therefore $\sin 20^\circ < \cos 20^\circ < \tan 45^\circ$.
