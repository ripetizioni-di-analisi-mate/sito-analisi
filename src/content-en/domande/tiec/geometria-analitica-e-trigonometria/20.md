At what point does the line $2x - 5y + 10 = 0$ intersect the $y$-axis?

- $(-5;0)$
- $(0;-2)$
- $(0;2)$
- $(0;10)$

## Explanation

Points on the $y$-axis have horizontal coordinate $0$, so set $x = 0$ in the equation. This gives $-5y + 10 = 0$, or $y = 2$, and the point is $(0;2)$. The point $(-5;0)$ is the intersection with the $x$-axis, found by setting $y = 0$. The point $(0;-2)$ uses the wrong sign when moving $10$ to the right-hand side, and $(0;10)$ treats the constant term as the vertical intercept without solving for $y$.
