What is the equation of the line through $(3;-2)$ and $(3;5)$?

- $y = -2$
- $x = 3$
- $y = 3$
- $x + y = 1$

## Explanation

The two points have the same horizontal coordinate $3$, so the line through them is vertical. The slope formula does not apply because the denominator $x_2 - x_1$ equals $0$. The vertical line consisting of the points with horizontal coordinate $3$ has equation $x = 3$. The equation $y = 3$ describes a horizontal line, while the lines $x + y = 1$ and $y = -2$ pass through $(3;-2)$ but miss $(3;5)$.
