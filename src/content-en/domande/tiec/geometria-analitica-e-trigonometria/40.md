What is the equation of the line through the origin and the intersection of $x + y = 4$ and $x - y = 2$?

- $y = x - 2$
- $y = 3x$
- $y = -\dfrac{1}{3}x$
- $y = \dfrac{1}{3}x$

## Explanation

Adding the equations $x + y = 4$ and $x - y = 2$ gives $2x = 6$, so $x = 3$, and the first equation gives $y = 1$. The required line passes through the origin and $(3;1)$, so it has slope $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and equation $y = \dfrac{1}{3}x$. The line $y = 3x$ interchanges the horizontal and vertical coordinates of the common point. The line $y = x - 2$ is the second given line, which passes through $(3;1)$ but misses the origin.
