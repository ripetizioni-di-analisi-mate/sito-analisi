At what point do the lines $2x + y = 7$ and $x - y = 2$ intersect?

- $(1;3)$
- $(5;-3)$
- $(3;1)$
- $(3;-1)$

## Explanation

The coordinates of the common point satisfy both equations, so solve the system formed by the two lines. Adding the corresponding sides of the equations eliminates $y$ and gives $3x = 9$, or $x = 3$. The second equation gives $y = x - 2 = 1$, so the lines intersect at $(3;1)$. The point $(5;-3)$ lies on the first line but not the second because $5 - (-3) = 8$. The points $(1;3)$ and $(3;-1)$ do not satisfy the first equation.
