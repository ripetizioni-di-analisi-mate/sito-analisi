The circle $x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 6y + k = 0$ passes through $P(1;1)$. What is its radius?

- $\sqrt{17}$
- $3$
- $17$
- $\sqrt{13}$

## Explanation

Since $P$ lies on the circle, substituting its coordinates gives $1 + 1 - 4 + 6 + k = 0$, or $k = -4$. By the rule for the **center and radius**, the center is $(2;-3)$ and $r^2 = 4 + 9 - (-4) = 17$. The radius is therefore $\sqrt{17}$, and indeed the distance from $P$ to the center is $\sqrt{1 + 16}$. The value $\sqrt{13}$ omits the constant term, $17$ is the square of the radius, and $3$ subtracts $4$ instead of adding it.
