Which of the following lines falls from left to right and crosses the $y$-axis at a point with positive vertical coordinate?

- $3x + 2y + 4 = 0$
- $3x - 2y - 4 = 0$
- $3x + 2y - 4 = 0$
- $3x - 2y + 4 = 0$

## Explanation

Solving for $y$ in $3x + 2y - 4 = 0$ gives $y = -\dfrac{3}{2}x + 2$. The slope $-\dfrac{3}{2}$ is negative, so the line falls from left to right, and the vertical intercept $2$ is positive. The other three lines can be written as $y = \dfrac{3}{2}x - 2$, $y = -\dfrac{3}{2}x - 2$, and $y = \dfrac{3}{2}x + 2$. Therefore each has either a positive slope or a negative vertical intercept.
