The parabola in the figure passes through the marked points, which have integer coordinates. What is its equation?

::grafico{f="-x^2+4*x-3" punti="2,1,pieno;1,0,pieno;3,0,pieno;0,-3,pieno" legenda=0 xmin=-1 xmax=5 ymin=-4 ymax=3 uguale=1 titolo="The marked points are the vertex $(2;1)$ and the intercepts $(1;0)$, $(3;0)$, and $(0;-3)$"}

- $y = -x^2 + 4x - 3$
- $y = -x^2 + 4x - 2$
- $y = x^2 - 4x + 3$
- $y = -x^2 - 4x - 3$

## Explanation

The parabola opens downward, so the coefficient of $x^2$ is negative, ruling out $y = x^2 - 4x + 3$. It crosses the $y$-axis at $(0;-3)$, so the constant term is $-3$, ruling out $y = -x^2 + 4x - 2$. The parabola $y = -x^2 - 4x - 3$ has axis $x = -2$, whereas the vertex in the figure has horizontal coordinate $2$. For $y = -x^2 + 4x - 3$, the axis is $x = 2$ and the **vertex** is $(2;-4 + 8 - 3) = (2;1)$, as shown.
