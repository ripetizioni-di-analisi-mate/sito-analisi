A ball is thrown upward. After $t$ seconds its height above the ground is $h = -5t^2 + 20t$ meters. How long does it take to return to the ground?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$ s
- $20$ s
- $2$ s
- $4$ s

## Explanation

The ball is on the ground when $h = 0$, that is, when $-5t^2 + 20t = 0$. Factoring gives $-5t(t - 4) = 0$, with solutions $t = 0$, the instant of launch, and $t = 4$. Thus the ball returns to the ground after $4$ seconds. The value $2$ s is the horizontal coordinate of the vertex of the **parabola**, the time at which the ball reaches its greatest height. The value $\dfrac{1}{4}$ s divides $5$ by $20$ instead of dividing $20$ by $5$.
