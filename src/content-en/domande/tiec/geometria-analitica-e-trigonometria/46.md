What is the equation of the circle with center $(-3;2)$ and radius $4$?

- $x^2 + y^2 + 6x - 4y - 16 = 0$
- $x^2 + y^2 + 6x - 4y - 3 = 0$
- $x^2 + y^2 + 6x - 4y + 9 = 0$
- $x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 4y - 3 = 0$

## Explanation

The **equation of the circle** with center $(-3;2)$ and radius $4$ is $(x + 3)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 16$. Expanding the squares gives $x^2 + 6x + 9 + y^2 - 4y + 4 = 16$, or $x^2 + y^2 + 6x - 4y - 3 = 0$. The equation with $-6x + 4y$ reverses the signs of the center coordinates, the one with constant term $9$ uses the radius $4$ instead of its square, and the one with constant term $-16$ omits the terms $9$ and $4$ in the expansion.
