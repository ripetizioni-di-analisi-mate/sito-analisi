The figure shows the line through $(-1,-1)$ and $(1,3)$. A printer doubles the width of the drawing on paper, leaves its height unchanged and keeps the same labels on both axes. Which statement about the new drawing is correct?

::grafico{f="2*x+1" punti="-1,-1,pieno;1,3,pieno" legenda=0 xmin=-3 xmax=3 ymin=-3 ymax=5 uguale=1 titolo="Drawing before resizing"}

- The line looks steeper and its coordinate slope becomes $4$
- The vertical intercept doubles
- The upper marked point now has horizontal coordinate $2$
- The line looks less steep, but the ratio of the coordinate changes is still $2$

## Explanation

The horizontal distance on the page doubles while the vertical distance stays the same. The axis labels still give a vertical coordinate difference of $4$ and a horizontal coordinate difference of $2$.
