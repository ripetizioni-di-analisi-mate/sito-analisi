The line in the figure passes through the two marked points. What is the equation of the line perpendicular to it through the origin?

::grafico{f="-2*x+3" punti="0,3,pieno;2,-1,pieno" legenda=0 xmin=-3 xmax=4 ymin=-3 ymax=5 uguale=1 titolo="The marked points are $(0;3)$ and $(2;-1)$"}

- $y = 2x$
- $y = -2x$
- $y = -\dfrac{1}{2}x$
- $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x$

## Explanation

Between the marked points, $x$ increases by $2$ and $y$ decreases by $4$, so the **slope** of the line in the figure is $m = \dfrac{-4}{2} = -2$. Two non-vertical lines are perpendicular if and only if the product of their slopes is $-1$, so the required line has slope $-\dfrac{1}{m} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Since it passes through the origin, its intercept is $0$ and its equation is $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x$. The line $y = -2x$ is parallel to the line in the figure, and the line $y = 2x$ only changes the sign of the slope. The line $y = -\dfrac{1}{2}x$ takes the reciprocal of the slope without changing its sign.
