The line in the figure passes through the two marked points, which have integer coordinates. What is its equation?

::grafico{f="2*x+1" punti="-1,-1,pieno;1,3,pieno" legenda=0 xmin=-3 xmax=3 ymin=-3 ymax=5 uguale=1 titolo="The marked points are $(-1;-1)$ and $(1;3)$"}

- $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x + 1$
- $y = -2x + 1$
- $y = 2x - 1$
- $y = 2x + 1$

## Explanation

Between the marked points, $x$ increases by $2$ and $y$ increases by $4$, so the **slope** is $m = \dfrac{4}{2} = 2$. The line crosses the $y$-axis at $(0;1)$, the midpoint of the marked points. Therefore $q = 1$ and the equation is $y = 2x + 1$. The line $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x + 1$ inverts the ratio, and $y = -2x + 1$ falls from left to right. The line $y = 2x - 1$ crosses the $y$-axis below the origin.
