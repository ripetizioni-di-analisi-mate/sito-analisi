For what value does the parabola $y = x^2 + bx + 3$ pass through $(2;-1)$?

- $b = -8$
- $b = -3$
- $b = -4$
- $b = 4$

## Explanation

The coordinates of the point satisfy the equation of the parabola, so $-1 = 4 + 2b + 3$. This gives $2b = -8$, or $b = -4$. As a check, $y = x^2 - 4x + 3$ at $x = 2$ gives $4 - 8 + 3 = -1$. The value $b = -8$ omits division by $2$, the value $b = 4$ has the wrong sign, and $b = -3$ uses $1$ as the vertical coordinate instead of $-1$.
