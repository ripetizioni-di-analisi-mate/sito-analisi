The parabola $y = ax^2 + bx + c$ has vertex $V(1;-4)$ and passes through $(0;-3)$. What is its equation?

- $y = x^2 - 2x - 4$
- $y = x^2 - 2x - 3$
- $y = 2x^2 - 4x - 3$
- $y = x^2 + 2x - 3$

## Explanation

The point $(0;-3)$ lies on the $y$-axis, so $c = -3$. The horizontal coordinate of the vertex is $-\dfrac{b}{2a} = 1$, so $b = -2a$ and the equation becomes $y = ax^2 - 2ax - 3$. The vertex lies on the parabola, so at $x = 1$ we have $a - 2a - 3 = -4$, giving $a = 1$ and $b = -2$. The parabola $y = x^2 + 2x - 3$ has vertex $(-1;-4)$, and the parabolas $y = 2x^2 - 4x - 3$ and $y = x^2 - 2x - 4$ have vertex $(1;-5)$.
