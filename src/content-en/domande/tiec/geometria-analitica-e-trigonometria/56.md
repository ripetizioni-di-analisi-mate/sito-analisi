What is the distance between the centers of the circles $x^2 + y^2 - 2x = 0$ and $x^2 + y^2 + 6y + 5 = 0$?

- $4$
- $2\sqrt{10}$
- $10$
- $\sqrt{10}$

## Explanation

By the rule for the **center and radius**, the first circle has $a = -2$ and $b = 0$, giving center $(1;0)$. The second has $a = 0$ and $b = 6$, giving center $(0;-3)$. The distance between the centers is $\sqrt{1^2 + 3^2} = \sqrt{10}$. The value $2\sqrt{10}$ uses the coefficients $-2$ and $6$ without dividing by $2$, $4$ adds the coordinate differences, and $10$ omits the square root.
