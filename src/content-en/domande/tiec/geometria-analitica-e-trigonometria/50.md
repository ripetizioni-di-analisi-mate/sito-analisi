Which of the following equations represents a circle?

- $x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 4y + 1 = 0$
- $x^2 + y^2 + 2x + 2y + 5 = 0$
- $x^2 - y^2 + 4x = 0$
- $2x^2 + y^2 - 4 = 0$

## Explanation

An equation $x^2 + y^2 + ax + by + c = 0$ represents a circle if $\dfrac{a^2}{4} + \dfrac{b^2}{4} - c > 0$. For $x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 4y + 1 = 0$, we obtain $1 + 4 - 1 = 4 > 0$, giving the circle with center $(1;-2)$ and radius $2$. For $x^2 + y^2 + 2x + 2y + 5 = 0$, we obtain $1 + 1 - 5 = -3 < 0$, so no point in the plane satisfies the equation. In the equations $2x^2 + y^2 - 4 = 0$ and $x^2 - y^2 + 4x = 0$, the coefficients of $x^2$ and $y^2$ differ, so they do not represent circles.
