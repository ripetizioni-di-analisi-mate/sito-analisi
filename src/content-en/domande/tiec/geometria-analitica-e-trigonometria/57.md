Which statement describes the parabola $y = -2x^2 + 3x + 5$?

- It opens downward and has vertex $(0;5)$.
- It opens downward and intersects the $y$-axis at $(0;-2)$.
- It opens upward and intersects the $y$-axis at $(0;5)$.
- It opens downward and intersects the $y$-axis at $(0;5)$.

## Explanation

The coefficient of $x^2$ is $a = -2 < 0$, so the **concavity** is downward. At $x = 0$ we obtain $y = 5$, so the parabola meets the $y$-axis at $(0;5)$. The point $(0;-2)$ uses the coefficient of $x^2$ as the vertical coordinate instead of the constant term. The point $(0;5)$ is not the vertex because the horizontal coordinate of the vertex is $-\dfrac{b}{2a} = \dfrac{3}{4}$.
