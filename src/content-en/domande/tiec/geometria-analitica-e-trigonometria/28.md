Which of the following lines is parallel to $4x - 6y + 1 = 0$?

- $4x + 6y + 1 = 0$
- $6x - 9y + 2 = 0$
- $3x + 2y = 0$
- $6x - 4y + 1 = 0$

## Explanation

The slope of $ax + by + c = 0$ is $m = -\dfrac{a}{b}$, so the given line has $m = -\dfrac{4}{-6} = \dfrac{2}{3}$. The line $6x - 9y + 2 = 0$ has $m = -\dfrac{6}{-9} = \dfrac{2}{3}$, making it **parallel** to the given line. The other three lines have slopes $\dfrac{3}{2}$, $-\dfrac{2}{3}$, and $-\dfrac{3}{2}$. In particular, the line $3x + 2y = 0$ is perpendicular to the given line because $\dfrac{2}{3} \cdot \left(-\dfrac{3}{2}\right) = -1$.
