For which values of the parameter is the point $P(k;3)$ at a distance of $5$ from $A(1;-1)$?

- $k = -2$ or $k = 4$
- $k = -4$ or $k = 2$
- $k = 1 - \sqrt{41}$ or $k = 1 + \sqrt{41}$
- $k = 4$

## Explanation

By the formula for the **distance between two points**, we need $(k - 1)^2 + (3 + 1)^2 = 25$, so $(k - 1)^2 = 9$. It follows that $k - 1 = 3$ or $k - 1 = -3$, giving $k = 4$ or $k = -2$. The two values correspond to the two points on the line $y = 3$ at distance $5$ from $A$. The answer $k = 4$ loses the negative solution. The pair $k = -4$, $k = 2$ uses the wrong sign in $k - 1$, and the values involving $\sqrt{41}$ add $16$ to $25$ instead of subtracting it.
