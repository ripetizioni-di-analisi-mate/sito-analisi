How is the quadrilateral with vertices $A(0;0)$, $B(4;0)$, $C(5;3)$, and $D(1;3)$ classified?

- a rhombus
- a trapezoid with exactly one pair of parallel sides
- a parallelogram with no right angles and with sides that are not all equal
- a rectangle

## Explanation

The sides $AB$ and $DC$ are horizontal because their endpoints have the same vertical coordinate, so they are parallel. The sides $AD$ and $BC$ have slopes $\dfrac{3 - 0}{1 - 0} = 3$ and $\dfrac{3 - 0}{5 - 4} = 3$, so they too are **parallel** and the quadrilateral is a parallelogram. It is not a rectangle because side $AB$ has slope $0$ and side $AD$ has slope $3$, so these two sides are not perpendicular. It is not a rhombus because $\overline{AB} = 4$ and $\overline{AD} = \sqrt{10}$.
