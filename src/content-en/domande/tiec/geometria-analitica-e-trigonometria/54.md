What is the area of the disk bounded by the circle $x^2 + y^2 + 4x - 2y - 4 = 0$?

- $\pi$
- $9\pi$
- $3\pi$
- $6\pi$

## Explanation

By the rule for the **center and radius**, the center is $(-2;1)$ and $r^2 = 4 + 1 - (-4) = 9$. The area of the disk is $\pi r^2$, so it equals $9\pi$. The value $6\pi$ is the circumference, $2\pi r$ with $r = 3$, and $3\pi$ multiplies $\pi$ by the radius instead of by its square. The value $\pi$ subtracts $4$ instead of adding it, giving $r^2 = 1$.
