Which angles $x$ with $0^\circ \leq x < 360^\circ$ satisfy $\sin x = \dfrac{1}{2}$?

- $30^\circ$ and $150^\circ$
- $30^\circ$ and $330^\circ$
- $60^\circ$ and $120^\circ$
- $30^\circ$ and $210^\circ$

## Explanation

From the **special angle values**, $\sin 30^\circ = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The point for $150^\circ$ on the unit circle is the reflection of the point for $30^\circ$ across the $y$-axis, so the two have the same vertical coordinate. Sine is positive only in the first two quadrants, so the solutions are $30^\circ$ and $150^\circ$. The angles $30^\circ$ and $330^\circ$ have the same *cosine*. The angles $60^\circ$ and $120^\circ$ have sine $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, and $\sin 210^\circ = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.
