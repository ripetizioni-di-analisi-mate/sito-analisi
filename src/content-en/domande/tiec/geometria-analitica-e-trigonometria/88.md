A ladder $4$ m long leans against a vertical wall and makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the floor. How far is the foot of the ladder from the wall?

- $4\sqrt{3}$ m
- $2$ m
- $2\sqrt{3}$ m
- $8$ m

## Explanation

The ladder, wall, and floor form a right triangle with hypotenuse $4$ m. The distance from the wall is the leg adjacent to the angle of $60^\circ$. When **solving a right triangle**, the adjacent leg equals the hypotenuse times the cosine, so the distance is $4\cos 60^\circ = 2$ m. The value $2\sqrt{3}$ m is the height where the ladder meets the wall, found using the sine. The values $8$ m and $4\sqrt{3}$ m are longer than the ladder, so neither can be the length of a leg.
