Which statement holds for an angle of $200^\circ$?

- $\sin 200^\circ < 0$ and $\cos 200^\circ > 0$
- $\sin 200^\circ > 0$ and $\cos 200^\circ < 0$
- $\sin 200^\circ < 0$ and $\cos 200^\circ < 0$
- $\sin 200^\circ > 0$ and $\cos 200^\circ > 0$

## Explanation

The angle $200^\circ$ is between $180^\circ$ and $270^\circ$, so its terminal side lies in the third quadrant. The corresponding point on the unit circle has two negative coordinates, so sine and cosine are both **negative**. A positive sine and negative cosine occur in the second quadrant, and a negative sine and positive cosine occur in the fourth.
