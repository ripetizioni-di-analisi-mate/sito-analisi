What is the value of $\cos 120^\circ$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$

## Explanation

The angle $120^\circ$ lies in the second quadrant, where cosine is negative. Its point on the unit circle is the reflection across the $y$-axis of the point for $60^\circ$, whose coordinates are $\left(\dfrac{1}{2};\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$. Thus the point for $120^\circ$ is $\left(-\dfrac{1}{2};\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$ and $\cos 120^\circ = -\dfrac{1}{2}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{2}$ has the wrong sign, and the values $\pm\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ interchange cosine and sine.
