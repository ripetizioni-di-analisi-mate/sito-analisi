An angle $x$ satisfies $\sin x < 0$ and $\tan x > 0$. In which quadrant does its terminal side lie on the unit circle?

- third quadrant
- fourth quadrant
- second quadrant
- first quadrant

## Explanation

Sine is negative in the third and fourth quadrants, where the point corresponding to the angle has a negative vertical coordinate. Tangent is the quotient $\dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}$, so it is positive when sine and cosine have the same sign. The cosine must therefore also be negative, so the terminal side lies in the **third quadrant**. In the fourth quadrant cosine is positive and tangent is therefore negative, while in the first and second quadrants sine is positive.
