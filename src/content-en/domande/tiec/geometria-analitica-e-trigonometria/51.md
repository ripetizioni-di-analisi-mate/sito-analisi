What is the equation of the circle with center $C(3;-4)$ tangent to the $x$-axis?

- $x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 8y = 0$
- $x^2 + y^2 + 6x - 8y + 9 = 0$
- $x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 8y + 9 = 0$
- $x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 8y + 16 = 0$

## Explanation

A circle is tangent to the $x$-axis if the distance from the center to the $x$-axis equals the radius. The distance from $C(3;-4)$ to the $x$-axis is the absolute value of the vertical coordinate, so $r = 4$. The equation is $(x - 3)^2 + (y + 4)^2 = 16$, or $x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 8y + 9 = 0$, because $9 + 16 - 16 = 9$. The constant term $16$ corresponds to radius $3$, which makes the circle tangent to the $y$-axis. The constant term $0$ corresponds to radius $5$, which makes the circle pass through the origin.
