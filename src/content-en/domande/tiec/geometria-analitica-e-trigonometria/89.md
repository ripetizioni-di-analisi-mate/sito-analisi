In a right triangle the leg opposite an angle of $30^\circ$ has length $6$. What is the perimeter?

- $24$
- $6 + 6\sqrt{3}$
- $18 + 6\sqrt{3}$
- $18 + 2\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

When **solving a right triangle**, the opposite leg equals the hypotenuse times the sine, so $6 = i \sin 30^\circ = \dfrac{i}{2}$ and the hypotenuse has length $12$. The other leg has length $12 \cos 30^\circ = 6\sqrt{3}$, so the perimeter is $6 + 12 + 6\sqrt{3} = 18 + 6\sqrt{3}$. The value $18 + 2\sqrt{3}$ multiplies the known leg by $\tan 30^\circ$ instead of dividing, while $6 + 6\sqrt{3}$ omits the hypotenuse. The value $24$ sets both legs equal to $6$.
