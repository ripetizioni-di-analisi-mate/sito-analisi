A rigid triangular frame has a right angle and a $30^\circ$ angle. A similar frame has three times its perimeter. The shorter leg of the second frame is $18$ cm. What is the hypotenuse of the first?

- $6\ \mathrm{cm}$
- $18\ \mathrm{cm}$
- $12\ \mathrm{cm}$
- $36\ \mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Corresponding sides scale in the same ratio as the perimeters. The shorter leg of the first frame is $18/3=6$ cm. It is opposite $30^\circ$ and half the hypotenuse, so the hypotenuse is $12$ cm.
