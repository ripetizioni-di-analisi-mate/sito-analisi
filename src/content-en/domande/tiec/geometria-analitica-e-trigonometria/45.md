Which of the following pairs consists of perpendicular lines?

- $y = 4x - 1$ and $y = \dfrac{1}{4}x + 2$
- $y = -\dfrac{1}{4}x - 1$ and $y = -\dfrac{1}{4}x + 2$
- $y = 4x - 1$ and $y = -4x + 2$
- $y = 4x - 1$ and $y = -\dfrac{1}{4}x + 2$

## Explanation

Two lines are **perpendicular** if the product of their slopes is $-1$. For the pair $y = 4x - 1$ and $y = -\dfrac{1}{4}x + 2$, the product is $4 \cdot \left(-\dfrac{1}{4}\right) = -1$, so the lines are perpendicular. For the pair containing $\dfrac{1}{4}$, the product is $1$, and for the pair containing $-4$, it is $-16$. The two lines with equal slope $-\dfrac{1}{4}$ are parallel.
