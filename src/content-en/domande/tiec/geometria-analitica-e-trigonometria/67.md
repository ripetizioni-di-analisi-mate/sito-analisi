Which of the following parabolas has no points in common with the $x$-axis?

- $y = -x^2 + x + 1$
- $y = x^2 + x + 1$
- $y = x^2 - 2x + 1$
- $y = x^2 + 3x + 1$

## Explanation

A parabola does not meet the $x$-axis when the equation $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ has no solutions, that is, when $\Delta < 0$. For $y = x^2 + x + 1$, we have $\Delta = 1 - 4 = -3 < 0$, so the parabola does not meet the $x$-axis. For $y = x^2 + 3x + 1$ and $y = -x^2 + x + 1$, we have $\Delta = 5 > 0$, so these parabolas cross the $x$-axis at two points. For $y = x^2 - 2x + 1 = (x - 1)^2$, we have $\Delta = 0$, so the parabola is tangent to the $x$-axis at $(1;0)$.
