Which point on the $x$-axis is equidistant from $A(1;3)$ and $B(5;1)$?

- $(3;0)$
- $(0;-4)$
- $(2;0)$
- $(3;2)$

## Explanation

A point on the $x$-axis has the form $P(x;0)$. Setting the squared distances from $A$ and $B$ equal gives $(x - 1)^2 + 9 = (x - 5)^2 + 1$, or $x^2 - 2x + 10 = x^2 - 10x + 26$. The $x^2$ terms cancel, leaving $8x = 16$, so $x = 2$ and the point is $(2;0)$. The point $(3;2)$ is the midpoint of $AB$, equidistant from the endpoints but not on the $x$-axis, and $(3;0)$ keeps only its horizontal coordinate. The point $(0;-4)$ is the equidistant point on the $y$-axis.
