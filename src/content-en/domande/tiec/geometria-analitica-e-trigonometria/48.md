Which of the following points lies on the circle $(x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 10$?

- $(-1;1)$
- $(2;1)$
- $(1;-2)$
- $(3;0)$

## Explanation

A point lies on a circle if its coordinates satisfy its equation. Substituting $(2;1)$ gives $(2 - 1)^2 + (1 + 2)^2 = 1 + 9 = 10$, so the point lies on the circle. The point $(-1;1)$ gives $4 + 9 = 13 > 10$ and is outside, while $(3;0)$ gives $4 + 4 = 8 < 10$ and is inside. The point $(1;-2)$ is the center, so it does not lie on the circle.
