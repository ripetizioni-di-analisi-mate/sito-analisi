The parabola $y = x^2 - 6x + 2$ passes through $A(1;-3)$. What is the other point on the parabola with vertical coordinate $-3$?

- $(5;-3)$
- $(-1;-3)$
- $(3;-3)$
- $(6;-3)$

## Explanation

The parabola is symmetric about its axis. By the rule for the **vertex and axis**, this is the line $x = -\dfrac{-6}{2} = 3$. The point $A$ has horizontal coordinate $1$ and is therefore $2$ units from the axis, so its reflection has horizontal coordinate $3 + 2 = 5$. Indeed, at $x = 5$ we obtain $25 - 30 + 2 = -3$. The point $(-1;-3)$ is the reflection of $A$ across the $y$-axis, which is not the axis of the parabola. At horizontal coordinate $3$, the value on the parabola is $-7$.
