Which statement describes the triangle with vertices $A(0;0)$, $B(4;2)$, and $C(1;-2)$?

- It has no right angle.
- It has a right angle at $A$.
- It has a right angle at $C$.
- It has a right angle at $B$.

## Explanation

The side $AB$ has slope $\dfrac{2}{4} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, and the side $AC$ has slope $\dfrac{-2}{1} = -2$. Their product is $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot (-2) = -1$, so sides $AB$ and $AC$ are **perpendicular**, with a right angle at $A$. The Pythagorean theorem gives the same result because $\overline{AB}^2 + \overline{AC}^2 = 20 + 5 = 25 = \overline{BC}^2$. The side $BC$ has slope $\dfrac{4}{3}$, whose product with either of the other slopes differs from $-1$.
