How is the triangle with vertices $A(0;0)$, $B(4;0)$, and $C(2;3)$ classified?

- right-angled at $C$
- equilateral
- isosceles, with $\overline{AC} = \overline{BC}$
- scalene

## Explanation

The formula for the **distance between two points** gives $\overline{AB} = 4$, $\overline{AC} = \sqrt{4 + 9} = \sqrt{13}$, and $\overline{BC} = \sqrt{4 + 9} = \sqrt{13}$. Two sides are equal and the third differs, so the triangle is isosceles and not equilateral. It does not have a right angle at $C$ because $\overline{AC}^2 + \overline{BC}^2 = 26$ differs from $\overline{AB}^2 = 16$. The vertex $C$ has horizontal coordinate $2$, the average of $0$ and $4$, which makes it equidistant from $A$ and $B$.
