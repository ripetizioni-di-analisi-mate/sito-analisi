At which points, if any, does the line $y = 2x - 5$ intersect the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 5$?

- only the point $(-2;1)$
- the points $(2;-1)$ and $(1;-3)$
- no points
- only the point $(2;-1)$

## Explanation

Substitute $y = 2x - 5$ into the equation of the circle to obtain $x^2 + 4x^2 - 20x + 25 = 5$, or $5x^2 - 20x + 20 = 0$. Dividing by $5$ gives $x^2 - 4x + 4 = (x - 2)^2 = 0$, which has the single solution $x = 2$. The line is therefore **tangent**, and the common point has vertical coordinate $y = 4 - 5 = -1$. The point $(1;-3)$ lies on the line but not on the circle because $1 + 9 = 10$. The point $(-2;1)$ lies on the circle but not on the line.
