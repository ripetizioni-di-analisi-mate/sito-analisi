In which quadrant is the point $P\left(-\sqrt{2};\, \pi - 4\right)$?

- the third quadrant
- the first quadrant
- the fourth quadrant
- the second quadrant

## Explanation

The horizontal coordinate $-\sqrt{2}$ is negative. Since $\pi \approx 3.14$ is less than $4$, the vertical coordinate $\pi - 4$ is also negative. A point with two negative coordinates lies in the **third quadrant** because the quadrants are numbered counterclockwise starting in the upper right. The second quadrant would require a positive vertical coordinate, that is, $\pi > 4$.
