What is the value of $\cos\dfrac{3\pi}{2} + \sin\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$?

- $1$
- $-1$
- $-2$
- $0$

## Explanation

The angle $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$ corresponds to $270^\circ$, and its terminal side is the negative $y$-axis. The corresponding point on the unit circle is $(0;-1)$, so $\cos\dfrac{3\pi}{2} = 0$ and $\sin\dfrac{3\pi}{2} = -1$. The sum is therefore $-1$. The value $1$ uses the point $(0;1)$, which corresponds to $90^\circ$. The value $-2$ assigns $-1$ to both functions.
