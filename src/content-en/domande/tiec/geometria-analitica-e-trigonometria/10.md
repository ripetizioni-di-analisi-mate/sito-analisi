For the circle $x^2 + y^2 - 4x - 2y + 1 = 0$, which statement describes the line $y = 3$?

- It lies outside the circle.
- It is a secant.
- It passes through the center.
- It is tangent.

## Explanation

By the rule for the **center and radius**, the circle has center $(2;1)$ and radius $\sqrt{4 + 1 - 1} = 2$. The line $y = 3$ is horizontal, so its distance from the center is the difference between the vertical coordinates, $3 - 1 = 2$. This equals the radius, so the line is **tangent**. Omitting the constant term gives radius $\sqrt{5} > 2$ and would incorrectly identify the line as a secant. The line does not pass through the center because the center’s vertical coordinate is $1$.
