At which points do the parabola $y = x^2$ and the line $y = x + 2$ intersect?

- $(1;1)$ and $(-2;4)$
- only at the point $(2;4)$
- $(-1;1)$ and $(2;4)$
- $(-1;0)$ and $(2;0)$

## Explanation

Equating the two expressions for $y$ gives $x^2 = x + 2$, or $x^2 - x - 2 = 0$. The solutions are $x = -1$ and $x = 2$ because $(x + 1)(x - 2) = x^2 - x - 2$. Use the line to find the vertical coordinates, giving the points $(-1;1)$ and $(2;4)$. The points $(1;1)$ and $(-2;4)$ lie on the parabola but not on the line. The points $(-1;0)$ and $(2;0)$ have the correct horizontal coordinates but set the vertical coordinate equal to $0$.
