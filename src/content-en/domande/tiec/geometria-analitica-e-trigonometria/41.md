What is the equation of the perpendicular bisector of the segment with endpoints $A(1;2)$ and $B(5;6)$, the line perpendicular to the segment at its midpoint?

- $y = x + 1$
- $y = -x + 11$
- $y = -x + 3$
- $y = -x + 7$

## Explanation

The **midpoint** of the segment is $M(3;4)$, and the slope of $AB$ is $\dfrac{6 - 2}{5 - 1} = 1$. A line perpendicular to $AB$ has slope $-1$, so the perpendicular bisector is $y - 4 = -(x - 3)$, or $y = -x + 7$. The line $y = x + 1$ is the line $AB$, while $y = -x + 3$ and $y = -x + 11$ are the lines perpendicular to $AB$ through $A$ and $B$, respectively.
