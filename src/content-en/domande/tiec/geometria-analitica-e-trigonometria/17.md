For which of the following angles is the tangent undefined?

- $270^\circ$
- $180^\circ$
- $135^\circ$
- $360^\circ$

## Explanation

The tangent is $\tan x = \dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}$, so it is defined only when $\cos x \neq 0$. The point corresponding to $270^\circ$ on the unit circle is $(0;-1)$, so $\cos 270^\circ = 0$ and $\tan 270^\circ$ **is undefined**. At $180^\circ$ and $360^\circ$, the sine equals $0$, so the tangent equals $0$. At $135^\circ$, we have $\cos 135^\circ = -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \neq 0$, and the tangent equals $-1$.
