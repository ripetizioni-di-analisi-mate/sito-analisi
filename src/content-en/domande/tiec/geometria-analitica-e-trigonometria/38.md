For what value do the lines $x + ky = 3$ and $2x - y = 1$ meet at a point with horizontal coordinate $1$?

- $k = -2$
- $k = 3$
- $k = 1$
- $k = 2$

## Explanation

The common point lies on the second line and has horizontal coordinate $1$, so its vertical coordinate satisfies $2 - y = 1$, giving $y = 1$. The point $(1;1)$ must also lie on the first line, so $1 + k = 3$ and $k = 2$. The value $k = 3$ omits the term $x = 1$ in the first equation, and $k = 1$ confuses the parameter with the vertical coordinate of the point. The value $k = -2$ uses the vertical coordinate $y = -1$, obtained from a sign error in the second equation.
