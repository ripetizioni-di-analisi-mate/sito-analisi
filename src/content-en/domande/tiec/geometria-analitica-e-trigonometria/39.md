Which statement describes the lines $2x - 4y + 3 = 0$ and $x - 2y - 1 = 0$?

- They are parallel and distinct.
- They intersect at $(1;0)$.
- They are perpendicular.
- They coincide.

## Explanation

Both lines have slope $\dfrac{1}{2}$ because $-\dfrac{2}{-4} = -\dfrac{1}{-2} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Multiplying the second equation by $2$ gives $2x - 4y - 2 = 0$, whose constant term differs from $3$, so the lines do not coincide. They are therefore **parallel and distinct**, and the system formed by the two equations has no solutions. The point $(1;0)$ lies on the second line but misses the first because $2 + 3 \neq 0$.
