What is the perimeter of the triangle with vertices $A(1;1)$, $B(5;1)$, and $C(5;4)$?

- $32$
- $6$
- $12$
- $7$

## Explanation

The side $AB$ is horizontal with length $5 - 1 = 4$, and the side $BC$ is vertical with length $4 - 1 = 3$. The formula for the **distance between two points** gives $\overline{AC} = \sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = 5$, so the perimeter is $4 + 3 + 5 = 12$. The value $7$ omits the side $AC$, the value $32$ adds $25$ instead of its square root, and $6$ is the area of the triangle.
