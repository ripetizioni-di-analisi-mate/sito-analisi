What is the equation of the line through the origin and the midpoint of the segment with endpoints $A(2;6)$ and $B(4;-2)$?

- $y = -4x$
- $y = \dfrac{2}{3}x$
- $y = \dfrac{3}{2}x$
- $y = -\dfrac{2}{3}x$

## Explanation

The **midpoint** of $AB$ is $M\left(\dfrac{2 + 4}{2};\dfrac{6 - 2}{2}\right) = (3;2)$. A line through the origin has equation $y = mx$ and passes through $M$ if $2 = 3m$, that is, if $m = \dfrac{2}{3}$. The line is therefore $y = \dfrac{2}{3}x$. The line $y = \dfrac{3}{2}x$ interchanges the horizontal and vertical coordinates of $M$, and $y = -4x$ passes through $(1;-4)$, the point whose coordinates are half the coordinate differences.
