What is the area of the triangle with vertices $A(-2;0)$, $B(4;0)$, and $C(1;5)$?

- $5$
- $15$
- $3\sqrt{34}$
- $30$

## Explanation

The side $AB$ lies on the $x$-axis and has length $4 - (-2) = 6$. The height relative to $AB$ is the distance from $C$ to the $x$-axis, which is its vertical coordinate $5$. Thus the area is $\dfrac{6 \cdot 5}{2} = 15$. The value $30$ omits division by $2$, $5$ uses $4 - 2 = 2$ as the base by dropping the sign of $-2$, and $3\sqrt{34}$ uses the side $AC = \sqrt{34}$ as the height even though it is not perpendicular to $AB$.
