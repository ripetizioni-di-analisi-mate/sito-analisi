For what parameter value does the line $kx + 2y - 4 = 0$ pass through $(3;-1)$?

- $k = \dfrac{2}{3}$
- $k = -2$
- $k = 2$
- $k = 6$

## Explanation

A point lies on a line if its coordinates satisfy the equation, so substitute $x = 3$ and $y = -1$. This gives $3k - 2 - 4 = 0$, hence $3k = 6$ and $k = 2$. The value $k = 6$ omits division by $3$, and $k = -2$ uses the wrong sign when moving $6$ to the right-hand side. The value $k = \dfrac{2}{3}$ results from substituting $y = 1$ instead of $y = -1$.
