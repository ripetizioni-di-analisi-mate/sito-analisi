All of the following parabolas open upward. Which one is the narrowest?

- $y = 3x^2 - 2$
- $y = x^2 + 5$
- $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x^2 + 4x$
- $y = 2x^2 - 7x$

## Explanation

The width of a **parabola** $y = ax^2 + bx + c$ depends on the coefficient $a$. The larger $|a|$ is, the narrower the parabola. The coefficients $b$ and $c$ change the position of the vertex, shifting the parabola without changing its shape. Of $3$, $1$, $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $2$, the largest is $3$, so the narrowest parabola is $y = 3x^2 - 2$. The parabola $y = 2x^2 - 7x$ has the largest coefficient of $x$ in absolute value, and $y = x^2 + 5$ has the largest constant term. Neither of these coefficients affects the width.
