What is $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$ radians in degrees?

- $75^\circ$
- $150^\circ$
- $300^\circ$
- $120^\circ$

## Explanation

To convert from **radians** to degrees, multiply by $\dfrac{180}{\pi}$ because $\pi$ radians corresponds to $180^\circ$. Thus $\dfrac{5\pi}{6} \cdot \dfrac{180}{\pi} = 5 \cdot 30^\circ = 150^\circ$. The value $300^\circ$ pairs $\pi$ with $360^\circ$, and $75^\circ$ pairs it with $90^\circ$. The angle $120^\circ$ measures $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ radians.
