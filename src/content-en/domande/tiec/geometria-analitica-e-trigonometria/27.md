A car’s value decreases linearly over time. It is worth $18\,000$ euros when new, and after $4$ years it is worth $10\,000$ euros. What is it worth $7$ years after purchase?

- $4000$ euros
- $14\,000$ euros
- $2000$ euros
- $8000$ euros

## Explanation

The value is a linear function of time, so its graph is the line through $(0;18\,000)$ and $(4;10\,000)$. The **slope** is $\dfrac{10\,000 - 18\,000}{4} = -2000$ euros per year. Thus after $7$ years the car is worth $18\,000 - 7 \cdot 2000 = 4000$ euros. The value $14\,000$ euros gives the total loss over $7$ years, $2000$ euros is the annual loss, and $8000$ euros is the value after $5$ years.
