The quadrilateral $ABCD$ is a parallelogram with $A(1;1)$, $B(5;2)$, and $C(6;5)$. What is the fourth vertex $D$?

- $(2;4)$
- $\left(\dfrac{7}{2};3\right)$
- $(10;6)$
- $(0;-2)$

## Explanation

In a parallelogram the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ have the same **midpoint**. The midpoint of $AC$ is $M\left(\dfrac{7}{2};3\right)$, so $D$ is the reflection of $B$ across $M$, giving $D(2x_M - x_B;\, 2y_M - y_B) = (7 - 5;\, 6 - 2) = (2;4)$. As a check, $AB$ and $DC$ have the same slope $\dfrac{1}{4}$. The point $(10;6)$ is obtained as $B + C - A$, the point $(0;-2)$ as $A + B - C$, and $\left(\dfrac{7}{2};3\right)$ is the center of the parallelogram.
