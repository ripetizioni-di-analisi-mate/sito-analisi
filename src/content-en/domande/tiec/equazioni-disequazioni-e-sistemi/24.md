What are the solutions of $(2x - 1)^2 = 9$?

- Only $x = 2$
- $x = 5$ and $x = -4$
- $x = 1$ and $x = -2$
- $x = 2$ and $x = -1$

## Explanation

The numbers whose square equals $9$ are $3$ and $-3$, so $2x - 1 = 3$ or $2x - 1 = -3$. The first equation gives $x = 2$ and the second gives $x = -1$. The answer “only $x = 2$” loses the negative square root. The pair $5$ and $-4$ comes from $2x - 1 = \pm 9$, omitting the square root.
