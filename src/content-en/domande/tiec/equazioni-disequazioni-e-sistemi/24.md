The points on a number line at distance $4$ from $c$ have coordinates $-1$ and $7$. Which equation describes exactly those two points?

- $|x+3|=4$
- $|x-4|=3$
- $|x+1|=7$
- $|x-3|=4$

## Explanation

The centre is the midpoint of $-1$ and $7$, namely $3$. The distance from $x$ to the centre is $|x-3|$. Setting it equal to $4$ yields both requested endpoints.
