A machine uses $M(x)=1/(x-1)+1/(x+1)$. Find the product of the two real inputs giving the reading $4/3$, without calculating the inputs separately.

- $-3/2$
- $3/2$
- $1$
- $-1$

## Explanation

For $x\ne\pm1$ the sum becomes $2x/(x^2-1)$ and the equation reduces to $2x^2-3x-2=0$. Its two roots are real, distinct and different from the excluded values. Their product is the constant divided by the leading coefficient, namely $-2/2=-1$.
