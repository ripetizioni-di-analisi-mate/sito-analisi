What are the solutions of $\dfrac{x^2}{x + 2} = \dfrac{4}{x + 2}$?

- Only $x = 2$
- $x = \pm 2$
- Only $x = -2$
- No real number

## Explanation

The **domain restriction** is $x \neq -2$. Multiplying both sides by $x + 2$ gives $x^2 = 4$, a quadratic with no linear term and roots $2$ and $-2$. The value $-2$ makes the denominator zero and must be discarded, so the only solution is $x = 2$. The answer $x = \pm 2$ overlooks the domain restriction.
