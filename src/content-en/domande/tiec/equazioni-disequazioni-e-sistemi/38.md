The common denominator in $\dfrac{x^2}{x+2}=\dfrac{4}{x+2}$ is cancelled. Which annotation must accompany the move to $x^2=4$ to keep the equations equivalent?

- $x\ne-2$
- $x\ne2$
- $x>0$
- $x\ne0$

## Explanation

Cancellation requires $x+2\ne0$. The reduced equation allows $-2$ and $2$, but the former makes the original divisions undefined. Recording the restriction therefore retains only $2$.
