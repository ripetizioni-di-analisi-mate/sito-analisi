How many distinct real roots does $4x^2 + 9 = 12x$ have?

- $2$
- $0$
- $1$
- Infinitely many

## Explanation

In standard form the equation is $4x^2 - 12x + 9 = 0$. Since $b = -12$ is even, calculate $\dfrac{\Delta}{4} = 36 - 36 = 0$. The **discriminant** is zero, so the two roots coincide and there is only one distinct solution. Indeed, the quadratic is the perfect square $(2x - 3)^2$, which is zero only for $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$. The answers $2$ and $0$ would correspond to positive and negative discriminants, respectively.
