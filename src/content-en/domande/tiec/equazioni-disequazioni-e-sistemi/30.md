The roots of $x^2+px+q=0$ are $r$ and $s$. Each is increased by $1$. Which expression gives the product of the new roots in terms of $p$ and $q$?

- $q+p+1$
- $q-p+1$
- $q+1$
- $q-p-1$

## Explanation

Expanding gives $(r+1)(s+1)=rs+r+s+1$. The root–coefficient relations give $rs=q$ and $r+s=-p$. Substituting produces $q-p+1$ without finding either root.
