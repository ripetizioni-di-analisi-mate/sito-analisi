A positive number equals its reciprocal plus $1$. What is the number?

- $\dfrac{1 - \sqrt{5}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{-1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$
- $1 + \sqrt{5}$
- $\dfrac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$

## Explanation

Let $x > 0$ be the number. The condition becomes $x = \dfrac{1}{x} + 1$. Multiplying by $x$ gives $x^2 = 1 + x$, or $x^2 - x - 1 = 0$, with $\Delta = 1 + 4 = 5$. The **quadratic formula** gives $x = \dfrac{1 \pm \sqrt{5}}{2}$. The value with the minus sign is negative, so discard it. The required number is $\dfrac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$. The value $\dfrac{-1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$ solves $x^2 + x - 1 = 0$ with the wrong sign for $b$, and $1 + \sqrt{5}$ omits division by $2a = 2$.
