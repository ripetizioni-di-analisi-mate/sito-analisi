For which real numbers $x$ does the square of $x$ exceed $x$ itself by at least $12$?

- $-3 \leq x \leq 4$
- $x \leq -3 \;\vee\; x \geq 4$
- $x \geq 4$
- $x \leq -4 \;\vee\; x \geq 3$

## Explanation

The condition gives $x^2 \geq x + 12$, or $x^2 - x - 12 \geq 0$. The corresponding equation has roots $4$ and $-3$ because their sum is $1$ and their product is $-12$. The quadratic is positive or zero outside the roots, including the endpoints, so $x \leq -3 \;\vee\; x \geq 4$. The answer $x \geq 4$ loses the negative solutions because at $x = -3$ we have $9 = -3 + 12$. The answer with $-4$ and $3$ reverses the signs of the roots.
