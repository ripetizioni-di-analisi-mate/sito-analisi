The size $x$ of a part, in millimetres, must satisfy $(x-20)^2\leq1/4$. Which tolerance expresses the same specification?

- At most a quarter of a millimetre away from $20$
- At most half a millimetre away from $20$
- Only sizes from $20$ to $20.5$ inclusive
- Only sizes strictly between $19.5$ and $20.5$

## Explanation

The square root of $(x-20)^2$ is $|x-20|$. The condition therefore becomes $|x-20|\leq1/2$. Deviations in either direction are allowed, including their endpoints.
