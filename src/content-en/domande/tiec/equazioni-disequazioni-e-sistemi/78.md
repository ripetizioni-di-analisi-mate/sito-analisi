What are the solutions of the following system?
$$
\begin{cases}
y = x^2 - 2x \\
y = x
\end{cases}
$$

- Only $(3;3)$
- $(0;0)$ and $(1;1)$
- $(0;0)$ and $(-3;-3)$
- $(0;0)$ and $(3;3)$

## Explanation

Substituting $y = x$ into the first equation gives $x = x^2 - 2x$, or $x^2 - 3x = 0$. The quadratic has no constant term and factors as $x(x - 3) = 0$, with roots $x = 0$ and $x = 3$. Since $y = x$, the solutions of the system are $(0;0)$ and $(3;3)$, the points common to the parabola and the line. The answer “only $(3;3)$” loses the solution $x = 0$ by dividing by $x$. The pair $(1;1)$ comes from $x^2 - x = 0$, moving the term $x$ without changing its sign.
