Which inequality describes exactly the real numbers $x$ closer to zero than to $6$ on the number line?

- $x<3$
- $0<x<6$
- $x>3$
- $-3<x<3$

## Explanation

The point equidistant from $0$ and $6$ is their midpoint, $3$. To its left the distance to zero is smaller, including for negative coordinates. The midpoint is excluded because the distances there are equal.
