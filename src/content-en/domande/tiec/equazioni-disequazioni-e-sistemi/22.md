One root of $x^2 + bx - 12 = 0$ is $x = 3$. What is the other root?

- $x = -4$
- $x = 4$
- $x = -1$
- $x = 1$

## Explanation

By the formula for the **product of the roots**, $x_1 \cdot x_2 = \dfrac{c}{a} = -12$. Since one root is $3$, the other is $\dfrac{-12}{3} = -4$. To check, substituting $x = 3$ gives $9 + 3b - 12 = 0$, so $b = 1$, and the sum of the roots of $x^2 + x - 12 = 0$ is $-1 = 3 + (-4)$. The value $4$ overlooks the sign of the product. The values $-1$ and $1$ are the sum of the roots and the coefficient $b$, respectively.
