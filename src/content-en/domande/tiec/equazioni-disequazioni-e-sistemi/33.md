A rectangle has area $48$ cm² and diagonal $10$ cm. What is its perimeter?

- $14$ cm
- $20$ cm
- $4\sqrt{37}$ cm
- $28$ cm

## Explanation

Let $x$ and $y$ be the side lengths in centimeters. By the Pythagorean theorem, $x^2 + y^2 = 100$, and the area gives $xy = 48$. Since $(x + y)^2 = x^2 + y^2 + 2xy = 100 + 96 = 196$ and $x + y > 0$, we have $x + y = 14$, so the perimeter is $2 \cdot 14 = 28$ cm. Indeed, the sides are $6$ and $8$, the roots of $t^2 - 14t + 48 = 0$. The value $14$ is half the perimeter, and $20$ treats the diagonal as the sum of the two sides. The value $4\sqrt{37} = 2\sqrt{148}$ comes from $(x + y)^2 = 100 + 48$, omitting the factor $2$ in the middle term.
