Which number is a solution of $\dfrac{x - 1}{2} - \dfrac{x + 1}{3} = 1$?

- $x = 7$
- $x = 6$
- $x = 11$
- $x = -11$

## Explanation

The common denominator is $6$, so multiplying both sides by $6$ gives $3(x - 1) - 2(x + 1) = 6$. The minus sign before $2(x + 1)$ changes the signs of both terms, so the left side becomes $3x - 3 - 2x - 2 = x - 5$. From $x - 5 = 6$ it follows that $x = 11$. The value $7$ comes from the expansion $-2(x + 1) = -2x + 2$, and $6$ comes from multiplying only the left side by $6$. The value $-11$ comes from multiplying $x - 1$ by $2$ and $x + 1$ by $3$, interchanging the two factors.
