A shop models profit as $G(p)=(p-4)(10-p)$ in terms of price $p$. If the price must be an integer and profit strictly positive, what is the difference between the largest and smallest admissible prices?

- $5$
- $6$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The product is positive for $4<p<10$, where both factors are positive. The extreme admissible integer prices are $5$ and $9$. Their difference is $4$, while there are five admissible prices in total.
