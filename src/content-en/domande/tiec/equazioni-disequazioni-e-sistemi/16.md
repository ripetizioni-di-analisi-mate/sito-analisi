The product of two consecutive natural numbers is $132$. What is the smaller number?

- $12$
- $11$
- $-12$
- $66$

## Explanation

If $n$ is the smaller number, the other is $n + 1$, so the condition becomes $n(n + 1) = 132$, or $n^2 + n - 132 = 0$. We have $\Delta = 1 + 528 = 529 = 23^2$, so the **quadratic formula** gives $n = \dfrac{-1 \pm 23}{2}$, or $n = 11$ and $n = -12$. The number must be a natural number, so discard $-12$. The smaller number is $11$. The value $12$ is the larger of the two numbers. The value $66$ is half of $132$ and solves $n + n = 132$, replacing the product with a sum.
