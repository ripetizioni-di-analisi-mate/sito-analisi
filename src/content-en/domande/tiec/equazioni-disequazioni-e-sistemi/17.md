An accountant knows that an account balance satisfies $s^2=49$, where $s$ is in euros. Which additional fact is sufficient to determine the balance?

- The absolute value of the balance is less than $8$
- The balance is nonzero
- The balance is an integer
- The account is overdrawn

## Explanation

The equation allows $-7$ and $7$. An overdraft selects only the negative value. Each of the other facts applies to both $7$ and $-7$, leaving the choice open.
