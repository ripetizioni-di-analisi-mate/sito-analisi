What are the real solutions of $3x^2 + 12 = 0$?

- $x = \pm 2$
- $x = -2$
- $x = \pm 4$
- None

## Explanation

This quadratic has **no linear term**. Isolating $x^2$ gives $x^2 = -4$. The square of a real number is never negative, so the equation **has no real solutions**. The answers $\pm 2$ and $-2$ take the square root of $4$ while ignoring the sign of $-4$, and $\pm 4$ also omits the square root.
