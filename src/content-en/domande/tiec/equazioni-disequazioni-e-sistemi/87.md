A competition score is $3w-p$, where $w$ is the number of wins and $p$ the number of penalties. Two teams have the same score and the second has two more wins. How many more penalties must it have?

- $6$
- $1$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

Two wins add $3\cdot2=6$ points. To keep the score unchanged, extra penalties must offset exactly those six points. Each penalty subtracts one point, so six more are required.
