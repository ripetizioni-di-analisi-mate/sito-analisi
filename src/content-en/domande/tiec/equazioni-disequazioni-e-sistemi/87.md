The sum of three consecutive integers is $87$. What is the largest of the three?

- $30$
- $29$
- $28$
- $31$

## Explanation

Let $n$ be the middle integer. The three integers are $n - 1$, $n$, and $n + 1$, with sum $3n$. The equation $3n = 87$ gives $n = 29$, so the integers are $28$, $29$, and $30$. The largest is $30$. The value $29$ is the middle integer and $28$ is the smallest.
