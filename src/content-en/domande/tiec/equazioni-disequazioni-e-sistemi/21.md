For which values of the real parameter $k$ does $kx^2 + 2x + 1 = 0$ have no real solutions?

- $k < 1$
- $k \geq 1$
- $k > 1$
- $k > 4$

## Explanation

At $k = 0$ the equation becomes $2x + 1 = 0$, with solution $x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$. For $k \neq 0$ the equation is quadratic, with $\dfrac{\Delta}{4} = 1 - k$, and has no real roots when $1 - k < 0$, or $k > 1$. All these values differ from $0$, so the answer is $k > 1$. At $k = 1$ we get $(x + 1)^2 = 0$, with solution $x = -1$, so $k \geq 1$ is incorrect. The answer $k > 4$ comes from $\Delta = 4 - k$, omitting the factor $4$.
