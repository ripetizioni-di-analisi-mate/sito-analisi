What are the solutions of $x + \dfrac{6}{x} = 5$?

- $x = 2$ and $x = 3$
- $x = -2$ and $x = -3$
- $x = 1$ and $x = 6$
- $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The domain restriction is $x \neq 0$. Multiplying both sides by $x$ gives $x^2 + 6 = 5x$, or $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$. Two numbers with sum $5$ and product $6$ are $2$ and $3$, and both satisfy the restriction. The pair $1$ and $6$ has product $6$ but sum $7$. The value $\dfrac{3}{2}$ comes from $x + 6 = 5x$, multiplying only the fraction by $x$.
