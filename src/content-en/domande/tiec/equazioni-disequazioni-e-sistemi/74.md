Each choice gives the two equations of a system in $x$ and $y$. Which system has no solution?

- $x + y = 3$ and $2x + 2y = 6$
- $x + y = 3$ and $2x + 2y = 5$
- $x + y = 3$ and $x - y = 5$
- $2x - y = 1$ and $4x + 2y = 2$

## Explanation

A system of two linear equations has no solution when the two lines are parallel and distinct. In the system with $x + y = 3$ and $2x + 2y = 5$, doubling the first equation gives $2x + 2y = 6$, which contradicts the second. No pair satisfies both, so the system is **inconsistent**. In the system with $2x + 2y = 6$, the second equation is twice the first, so there are infinitely many solutions. The other two systems have nonzero determinants, $-2$ and $8$, and each has a unique solution.
