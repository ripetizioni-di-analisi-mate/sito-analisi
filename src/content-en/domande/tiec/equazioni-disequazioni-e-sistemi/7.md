A sensor is calibrated so that $x^2+kx+25=0$ has a negative double root. Which value of $k$ satisfies both requirements?

- $-5$
- $10$
- $5$
- $-10$

## Explanation

The discriminant must vanish, giving $k^2-100=0$ and $k=\pm10$. The double root is $-k/2$, negative only for $k>0$. Thus $k=10$ and the root is $-5$.
