The parabola $y = x^2 - 3x - 10$ meets the $x$-axis at two points. What is the length of the segment joining them?

- $7$
- $3$
- $49$
- $5$

## Explanation

Points on the parabola that lie on the $x$-axis satisfy $y = 0$, so their horizontal coordinates are the roots of $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$. We need two numbers with sum $3$ and product $-10$, namely $5$ and $-2$. The points are $(-2;0)$ and $(5;0)$, so the segment has length $5 - (-2) = 7$. The value $3$ comes from subtracting $2$ from $5$ without accounting for the sign, and $49$ is the discriminant of the equation.
