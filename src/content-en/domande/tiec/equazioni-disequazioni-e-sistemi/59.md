After $t$ seconds, the height in meters of an object thrown upward is $h = 20t - 5t^2$. During which time interval is the height greater than $15$ meters?

- $t < 1 \;\vee\; t > 3$
- $1 < t < 3$
- $0 < t < 4$
- $t > 3$

## Explanation

The condition gives the inequality $20t - 5t^2 > 15$. Move all terms to the left, then divide by $-5$. This division **reverses** the inequality, giving $t^2 - 4t + 3 < 0$. The corresponding equation has roots $1$ and $3$, and the quadratic is negative between its roots, so $1 < t < 3$. The answer $0 < t < 4$ gives the interval where the height is positive, solving $h > 0$. The answer $t < 1 \;\vee\; t > 3$ keeps the direction of the inequality.
