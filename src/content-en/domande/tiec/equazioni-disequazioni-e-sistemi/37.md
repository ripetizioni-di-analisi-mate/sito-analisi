Reciprocal ratios $r=(x+1)/x$ and $1/r$ must satisfy $r=2/r$, with $x\ne0,-1$. Which condition on $r$ retains both possibilities for then calculating $x$?

- $r=\sqrt2$ or $r=-\sqrt2$
- $r=1/2$ or $r=-1/2$
- $r=2$ or $r=-2$
- Only $r=\sqrt2$, because it is a ratio

## Explanation

The condition $x\ne-1$ makes $r$ nonzero, so multiplication by $r$ is allowed. This gives $r^2=2$, with both signs possible. Each yields $x=1/(r-1)$, an admissible input.
