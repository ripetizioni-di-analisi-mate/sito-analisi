A fraction $A(x)/B(x)$ is evaluated at a particular $x$. It is defined and positive, and $A(x)<0$. Which sign must $-B(x)$ have?

- Negative
- Zero
- Positive
- Either positive or negative

## Explanation

A positive quotient has a numerator and denominator of the same sign. Since the numerator is negative, $B(x)$ must also be negative. Its opposite $-B(x)$ is therefore positive.
