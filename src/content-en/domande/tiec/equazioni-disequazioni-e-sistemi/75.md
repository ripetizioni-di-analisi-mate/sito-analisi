What is the determinant of the following system?
$$
\begin{cases}
2x + 3y = 7 \\
5x - y = 9
\end{cases}
$$

- $-17$
- $13$
- $17$
- $-13$

## Explanation

For a system with equations $ax + by = c$ and $a'x + b'y = c'$, the determinant is given by $D = ab' - a'b$. Here $a = 2$, $b = 3$, $a' = 5$, and $b' = -1$, so $D = 2 \cdot (-1) - 5 \cdot 3 = -2 - 15 = -17$. Since $D \neq 0$, **Cramer’s rule** gives a unique solution, $(2;1)$. The value $13$ adds the two products instead of subtracting them, and $17$ subtracts them in reverse order. The value $-13$ comes from writing the product $2 \cdot (-1)$ as $2$.
