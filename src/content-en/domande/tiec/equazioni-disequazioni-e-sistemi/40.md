When weighing a parcel a scale always reads $0.3$ kg more than the actual weight. If it reads at most $5$ kg, which equivalent condition follows for the real weight $p$?

- $p<4.7$ kg
- $p\leq4.7$ kg
- $p\leq5.3$ kg
- $p<5.3$ kg

## Explanation

The reading is $p+0.3$, so $p+0.3\leq5$. Subtracting the error gives $p\leq4.7$. Equality is possible because a reading of exactly five is allowed.
