For which values of $x$ does $\dfrac{x}{3} - 1 \geq \dfrac{x}{2}$ hold?

- $x \geq -6$
- $x \leq -6$
- $x \leq 6$
- $x \leq -1$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides by $6$, which is positive, preserves the direction of the inequality and gives $2x - 6 \geq 3x$. Moving terms gives $-x \geq 6$. Multiplying by $-1$ **reverses** the inequality, so $x \leq -6$. To check, $x = -6$ gives $-2 - 1 = -3$ on the left and $-3$ on the right, and $\geq$ is true. The answer $x \geq -6$ keeps the direction of the inequality. The answer $x \leq -1$ multiplies every term by $6$ except $-1$.
