A blend is made by mixing $x$ kg of coffee costing $12$ euros per kg with $6$ kg of coffee costing $18$ euros per kg. The blend costs $14$ euros per kg. What is $x$?

- $12$
- $3$
- $6$
- $9$

## Explanation

The value of the blend is the sum of the values of the two portions, or $12x + 6 \cdot 18 = 12x + 108$ euros. The blend weighs $x + 6$ kg and costs $14$ euros per kg, so its value is also $14(x + 6)$ euros. The equation $12x + 108 = 14x + 84$ gives $2x = 24$, so $x = 12$. The value $3$ comes from reversing the ratio of the quantities. The value $6$ would give two equal portions and an average price of $15$ euros per kg.
