What are the solutions of $\dfrac{x + 2}{x} = \dfrac{x + 5}{x + 2}$?

- $x = \dfrac{4}{5}$
- $x = 4$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{4}$
- No real number

## Explanation

The domain restrictions are $x \neq 0$ and $x \neq -2$. Multiplying both sides by $x(x + 2)$ gives $(x + 2)^2 = x(x + 5)$, or $x^2 + 4x + 4 = x^2 + 5x$. The $x^2$ terms cancel, so the equation is linear and gives $x = 4$, which satisfies the restrictions. The value $\dfrac{4}{5}$ comes from omitting the middle term when expanding $(x + 2)^2$. The value $\dfrac{1}{4}$ comes from the expansion $x(x + 5) = x^2 + 5$.
