Why can one decide that $x^2-4x+7<0$ is impossible without drawing a parabola?

- Its constant term is odd, so the trinomial has no roots
- The left side equals $(x-2)^2+3$
- Its leading coefficient is positive, so every such trinomial is positive
- The left side equals $(x-2)^2-3$

## Explanation

Completing the square gives $(x-2)^2+3$. The square is always nonnegative, so the minimum is $3$. Neither the sign of the leading coefficient alone nor the parity of the constant term would suffice.
