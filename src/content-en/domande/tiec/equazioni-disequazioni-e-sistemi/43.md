What is the solution set of $-4 < 2x - 2 \leq 6$?

- $(-1, 4]$
- $[-1, 4)$
- $(-3, 2]$
- $(-2, 8]$

## Explanation

This compound inequality requires both $2x - 2 > -4$ and $2x - 2 \leq 6$. Adding $2$ to all three expressions gives $-2 < 2x \leq 8$. Dividing by $2$, which is positive, gives $-1 < x \leq 4$. The endpoint $-1$ is excluded and $4$ is included, so the set is $(-1, 4]$. The interval $[-1, 4)$ includes $-1$ and excludes $4$, reversing the open and closed endpoints. The interval $(-3, 2]$ subtracts $2$ instead of adding it, and $(-2, 8]$ omits division by $2$.
