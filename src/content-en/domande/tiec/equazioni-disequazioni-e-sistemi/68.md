On a graph of the domain of $\sqrt{(x+3)/(x-1)}$, the boundary values $-3$ and $1$ must be marked. Which combination of filled and open dots should be used?

- Open at $-3$, filled at $1$
- Both filled
- Filled at $-3$, open at $1$
- Both open

## Explanation

At $x=-3$ the numerator is zero and the denominator is not, so the root equals zero. At $x=1$ the quotient is undefined. A sign analysis places these boundaries at the ends of the intervals $x\leq-3$ and $x>1$.
