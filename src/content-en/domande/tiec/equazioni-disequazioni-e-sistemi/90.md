A group of friends splits a bill of $360$ euros equally. If $2$ more friends joined them, each would pay $6$ euros less. How many friends are in the original group?

- $10$
- $12$
- $6$
- $20$

## Explanation

Let $n$ be the number of friends. Each pays $\dfrac{360}{n}$ euros, whereas with $n + 2$ people the share would be $\dfrac{360}{n + 2}$. The condition gives $\dfrac{360}{n} - \dfrac{360}{n + 2} = 6$. Multiplying by $n(n + 2)$ yields $720 = 6n(n + 2)$, or $n^2 + 2n - 120 = 0$, with solutions $10$ and $-12$. The number of people is positive, so there are $10$ friends. The value $12$ is the number of people in the second case, and $6$ is the difference between the two shares.
