For which real values of $x$ is $\sqrt{6 - 2x}$ defined?

- $x \geq 3$
- $x < 3$
- $x \leq 3$
- $x \leq -3$

## Explanation

A real square root is defined when its radicand is greater than or equal to zero, so solve $6 - 2x \geq 0$. This gives $-2x \geq -6$. Dividing by $-2$ **reverses** the inequality, so $x \leq 3$. At $x = 3$ the radicand equals $0$ and $\sqrt{0} = 0$, so $3$ is included and the answer $x < 3$ is incorrect. The answer $x \geq 3$ keeps the direction of the inequality.
