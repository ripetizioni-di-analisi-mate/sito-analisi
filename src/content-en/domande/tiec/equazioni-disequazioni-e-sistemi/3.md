For which value of the real parameter $k$ does the equation $(k - 2)x = k + 1$ in the unknown $x$ have no solution?

- $k = 2$
- $k = -1$
- $k = 2$ and $k = -1$
- For no value of $k$

## Explanation

The equation has the form $ax = b$, where $a = k - 2$ and $b = k + 1$, and has **no solution** when $a = 0$ and $b \neq 0$. The coefficient $a$ is zero for $k = 2$, and for that value $b = 3 \neq 0$. With $k = 2$ the equation therefore becomes $0 \cdot x = 3$, which has no solution. For $k = -1$, however, it becomes $-3x = 0$, with solution $x = 0$, so $k = -1$ does not make the equation inconsistent.
