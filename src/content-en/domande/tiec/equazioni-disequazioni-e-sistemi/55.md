How many integers satisfy $x^2 - 2x - 15 < 0$?

- $9$
- $8$
- $7$
- $6$

## Explanation

The corresponding equation has roots $5$ and $-3$, with sum $2$ and product $-15$. The quadratic is negative between the roots, so the solutions are $-3 < x < 5$. The integers in this interval are $-2$, $-1$, $0$, $1$, $2$, $3$, and $4$, a total of $7$. The answer $9$ also includes the endpoints $-3$ and $5$, where the quadratic is zero. The answer $8$ gives the interval’s length, $5 - (-3)$, which differs from the number of integers strictly inside it.
