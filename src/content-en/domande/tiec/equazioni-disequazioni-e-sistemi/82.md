For which values of $x$ do $2x + 1 > 5$ and $3 - x > 2$ both hold?

- $1 < x < 2$
- $x < 1 \;\vee\; x > 2$
- For no real $x$
- $x > 2$

## Explanation

The first inequality gives $2x > 4$, or $x > 2$. The second gives $-x > -1$. Multiplying by $-1$ reverses the inequality, so $x < 1$. No number is both greater than $2$ and less than $1$, so the **system has no solutions**. The answer $x < 1 \;\vee\; x > 2$ takes the union of the two sets instead of their intersection. The answer $1 < x < 2$ takes the interval between the two endpoints.
