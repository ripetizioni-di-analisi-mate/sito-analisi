What is the solution of $(x + 3)^2 - (x - 1)^2 = 16$?

- $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$
- No real number
- $x = 2$
- $x = 1$

## Explanation

Expanding the two squares gives $(x^2 + 6x + 9) - (x^2 - 2x + 1) = 16$. The minus sign changes the signs of all the terms in the second square, so the left side simplifies to $8x + 8$. From $8x + 8 = 16$ it follows that $x = 1$. The value $\dfrac{3}{2}$ comes from changing the sign of $x^2$ alone. The answer “No real number” omits the middle terms when expanding the squares, giving $8 = 16$.
