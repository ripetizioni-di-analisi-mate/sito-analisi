What is the solution set of $(x^2 + 1)(x - 3) \leq 0$?

- $x \geq 3$
- $x \leq 3$
- $x \leq -1 \;\vee\; 1 \leq x \leq 3$
- $x < 3$

## Explanation

The factor $x^2 + 1$ is always positive because $x^2 \geq 0$, so it does not change the sign of the product. The product therefore has the same sign as $x - 3$, and the inequality is equivalent to $x - 3 \leq 0$, or $x \leq 3$. At $x = 3$ the product is zero, so $3$ is included and the answer $x < 3$ is incorrect. The answer with $-1$ and $1$ treats $x^2 + 1$ as if it were $x^2 - 1$, which is zero at $\pm 1$.
