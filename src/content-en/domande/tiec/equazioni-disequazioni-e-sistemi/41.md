For which values of $x$ is $2(x + 1) > 2x + 1$ true?

- For no real $x$
- For every real $x$
- For $x > 0$
- For $x > 1$

## Explanation

Expanding the product gives $2x + 2 > 2x + 1$, and moving $2x$ gives $0 \cdot x > -1$. The left side equals $0$ for every $x$, and $0 > -1$ is true, so the inequality holds **for every** real $x$. The answer “for no $x$” confuses this with a false final inequality such as $0 > 1$. The answers $x > 0$ and $x > 1$ divide by the coefficient $0$, which is not allowed.
