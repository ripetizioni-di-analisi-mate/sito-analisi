A father is now three times as old as his son. In $12$ years he will be twice as old as his son. How old is his son now?

- $36$
- $24$
- $12$
- $6$

## Explanation

Let $f$ be the son’s current age. His father is currently $3f$ years old. In $12$ years their ages will be $3f + 12$ and $f + 12$, so the condition gives the equation $3f + 12 = 2(f + 12)$. Expanding gives $3f + 12 = 2f + 24$, so $f = 12$. To check, in $12$ years the father will be $48$ and the son $24$. The values $36$ and $24$ are the father’s current age and the son’s future age, respectively.
