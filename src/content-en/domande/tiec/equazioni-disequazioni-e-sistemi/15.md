Adding the same number to both sides of the equation $x^2+6x=4$ turns the left side into the square of a binomial. Which equation is obtained?

- $(x-3)^2=13$
- $(x+3)^2=7$
- $(x+3)^2=13$
- $(x+6)^2=40$

## Explanation

The square of a binomial beginning with $x^2+6x$ is $(x+3)^2=x^2+6x+9$, because the cross term $2\cdot x\cdot 3$ equals $6x$. So $9$ is added to both sides. The right side becomes $4+9=13$, hence the equation is $(x+3)^2=13$, which has the same solutions as the given one.
