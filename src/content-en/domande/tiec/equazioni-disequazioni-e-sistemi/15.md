A program solves $x^2+6x=4$ by completing the square. Which intermediate line preserves the solutions?

- $(x-3)^2=13$
- $(x+3)^2=7$
- $(x+3)^2=13$
- $(x+6)^2=40$

## Explanation

Half of the coefficient of $x$ is $3$. To form the square of the binomial, add $3^2=9$ to both sides. The right side then becomes $4+9=13$.
