What is the solution of $0.2x + 1.5 = 0.5x - 0.3$?

- $x = -4$
- $x = 6$
- $x = 0.6$
- $x = 60$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides by $10$ clears the decimals and gives $2x + 15 = 5x - 3$. Moving $2x$ to the right and $-3$ to the left gives $18 = 3x$, so $x = 6$. To check, substitute $x = 6$. The left side equals $1.2 + 1.5 = 2.7$ and the right side equals $3 - 0.3 = 2.7$. The value $-4$ comes from moving $1.5$ without changing its sign. The values $0.6$ and $60$ come from dividing $1.8$ by $0.3$ incorrectly.
