A student factors $x^2+x-20$ as $(x-4)(x+5)$. Which step uses this factorisation to solve $x^2+x-20=0$?

- Require both equalities simultaneously
- Set $x-4=0$ or $x+5=0$
- Set $x-4=x+5$
- Always divide by $x-4$ and solve only $x+5=0$

## Explanation

A real product is zero if at least one factor is zero. Each factor therefore gives a root, $4$ or $-5$. Dividing by one factor without distinguishing cases would lose its root.
