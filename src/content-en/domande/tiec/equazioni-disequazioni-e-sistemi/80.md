An adult ticket costs $8$ euros and a reduced ticket $5$. Ten tickets were sold for a reported revenue of $66$. Which check reveals that the figure is impossible?

- Revenue can never exceed $60$
- The number of reduced tickets has to be even
- Every possible revenue must be a multiple of $5$
- The difference between revenue and $50$ must be a multiple of $3$

## Explanation

Ten reduced tickets would bring in $50$. Replacing a reduced ticket with an adult ticket adds $3$ each time. The difference $66-50=16$ is not divisible by $3$, so the count has no integer solution.
