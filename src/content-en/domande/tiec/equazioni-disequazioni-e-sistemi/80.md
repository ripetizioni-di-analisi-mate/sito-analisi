Two real numbers have sum $3$ and product $-10$. What is the larger number?

- $2$
- $-2$
- $-5$
- $5$

## Explanation

By the relationship between roots and coefficients, two numbers with sum $s$ and product $p$ are the roots of $t^2 - st + p = 0$. Here the equation is $t^2 - 3t - 10 = 0$, and the numbers with sum $3$ and product $-10$ are $5$ and $-2$. The larger is therefore $5$. The value $2$ is the larger of the roots of $t^2 + 3t - 10 = 0$, namely $2$ and $-5$. That equation uses the wrong sign in front of the sum. The value $-2$ is the smaller of the two required numbers.
