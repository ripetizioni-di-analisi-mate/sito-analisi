A student earns grades of $5$, $6$, and $7$ on the first three tests. What is the minimum grade needed on the fourth test for the average of all four grades to be at least $6.5$?

- $8$
- $7$
- $7.5$
- $6.5$

## Explanation

Let $x$ be the fourth grade. The average of all four grades is $\dfrac{5 + 6 + 7 + x}{4} = \dfrac{18 + x}{4}$. The requirement gives the inequality $\dfrac{18 + x}{4} \geq 6.5$. Multiplying by $4$ gives $18 + x \geq 26$, so $x \geq 8$ and the minimum grade is $8$. The value $7$ averages the current average $6$ and the fourth grade, giving the first three tests combined the same weight as the fourth.
