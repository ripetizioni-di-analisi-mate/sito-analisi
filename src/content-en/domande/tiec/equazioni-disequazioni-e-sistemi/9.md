For which value of $k$ does the equation $kx + 3 = 2x + k$ in the unknown $x$ have the solution $x = 2$?

- $k = 1$
- $k = 7$
- $k = -1$
- $k = -7$

## Explanation

The number $2$ is a solution if substituting it for $x$ makes the equation true. Substitution gives $2k + 3 = 4 + k$, a linear equation in $k$. Moving $k$ to the left and $3$ to the right gives $k = 1$. To check, with $k = 1$ the equation becomes $x + 3 = 2x + 1$, whose solution is $x = 2$. The value $7$ comes from moving $3$ without changing its sign.
