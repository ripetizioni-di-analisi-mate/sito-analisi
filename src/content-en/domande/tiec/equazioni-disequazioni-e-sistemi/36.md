The average cost per copy in a print shop is $m(n)=3+24/n$ euros, where $n$ is the positive number of copies. A customer wants to pay exactly $4$ euros per copy. Which amount is being spread over the copies when solving $m(n)=4$?

- The entire cost of $3$ euros, at one euro per copy
- A variable cost of $24$ euros for each copy
- A fee of $4$ euros to add to the initial $3$
- The fixed cost of $24$ euros, at one euro per copy

## Explanation

The three-euro charge per copy remains constant. To reach an average of four euros, the extra share must be one euro, so $24/n=1$. The $24$ copies spread the $24$-euro fixed cost into one-euro shares.
