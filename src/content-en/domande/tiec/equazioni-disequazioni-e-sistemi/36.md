How many real solutions does $\dfrac{x}{2} + \dfrac{2}{x} = 1$ have?

- One
- Two
- Infinitely many
- None

## Explanation

The domain restriction is $x \neq 0$, and the common denominator is $2x$. Multiplying both sides by $2x$ gives $x^2 + 4 = 2x$, or $x^2 - 2x + 4 = 0$. The quadratic formula for an even linear coefficient gives $\dfrac{\Delta}{4} = 1 - 4 = -3 < 0$, so the equation **has no real roots**.
