A component works when $(x^2-1)(x^2-9)\leq0$. The technician wants a single condition on magnitude so that opposite inputs are handled together. Which specification should be used?

- $1\leq|x|\leq3$
- $1<|x|<3$
- $|x|\leq1$ or $|x|\geq3$
- $1\leq|x|\leq9$

## Explanation

With $u=x^2$ the product is nonpositive from $1$ to $9$ inclusive. Since $\sqrt{x^2}=|x|$, the magnitude limits are $1$ and $3$. Both directions of input therefore meet the same specification.
