Which real numbers $x$ satisfy $\dfrac{x - 1}{x^2 - 4} \geq 0$?

- $x < -2 \;\vee\; 1 \leq x < 2$
- $x \geq 1$
- $-2 \leq x \leq 1 \;\vee\; x \geq 2$
- $-2 < x \leq 1 \;\vee\; x > 2$

## Explanation

The numerator is positive for $x > 1$, and the denominator $x^2 - 4$ is positive for $x < -2$ and $x > 2$. The zeros $-2$, $1$, and $2$ divide the number line into four intervals. From left to right, the **sign chart** gives the signs of the quotient as negative, positive, negative, and positive. Include the zero of the numerator, $1$, because the inequality uses $\geq$, and exclude the zeros of the denominator, $\pm 2$. The solutions are therefore $-2 < x \leq 1 \;\vee\; x > 2$. The answer $x \geq 1$ considers only the numerator, and the answer containing $-2$ and $2$ includes values where the fraction is undefined.
