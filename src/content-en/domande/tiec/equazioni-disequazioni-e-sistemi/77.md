A model uses $2u+v=11$ and $u+2v=10$. The two variable names are accidentally interchanged in both equations. Which numerical value definitely stays the same?

- $u+v$
- $v$
- $u$
- $u-v$

## Explanation

Interchanging the names swaps the solution components, which here are $4$ and $3$. Their sum is $7$ in either order. The difference changes sign and each component takes the other's value.
