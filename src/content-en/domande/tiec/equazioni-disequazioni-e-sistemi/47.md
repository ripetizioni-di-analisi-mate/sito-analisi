Let $k$ be a negative real number. What is the solution set of $kx > 3$ in the unknown $x$?

- $x > \dfrac{3}{k}$
- $x < \dfrac{3}{k}$
- $x < -\dfrac{3}{k}$
- $x > -\dfrac{3}{k}$

## Explanation

To isolate $x$, divide both sides by $k$. Since $k$ is negative, the **properties of inequalities** require reversing the inequality, so $x < \dfrac{3}{k}$. For example, with $k = -1$ the inequality is $-x > 3$, or $x < -3 = \dfrac{3}{-1}$. The answer $x > \dfrac{3}{k}$ keeps the direction of the inequality. The answers with $-\dfrac{3}{k}$ change the sign of the fraction, which should remain $\dfrac{3}{k}$.
