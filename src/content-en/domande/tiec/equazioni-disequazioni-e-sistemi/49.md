For which values of the parameter $k$ does $x^2 + kx + 4 > 0$ hold for every real $x$?

- $k < -4 \;\vee\; k > 4$
- $-2 < k < 2$
- $-4 < k < 4$
- $-4 \leq k \leq 4$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $x^2$ is positive. By the rule for the **sign of a quadratic**, the quadratic is positive for every $x$ when the discriminant is negative. We have $\Delta = k^2 - 16$, and the condition $k^2 - 16 < 0$ gives $-4 < k < 4$. At $k = 4$ the quadratic is $(x + 2)^2$, which equals zero at $x = -2$. At $k = -4$ it is $(x - 2)^2$, which equals zero at $x = 2$. Therefore exclude the endpoints $\pm 4$. The answer $k < -4 \;\vee\; k > 4$ corresponds to $\Delta > 0$. The answer $-2 < k < 2$ comes from $\Delta = k^2 - 4$, omitting the factor $4$ in front of $ac$.
