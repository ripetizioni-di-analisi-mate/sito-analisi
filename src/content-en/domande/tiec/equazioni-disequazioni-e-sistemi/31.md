A student solves $x^2 = 5x$ by dividing both sides by $x$ and concludes that the only solution is $x = 5$. Which statement is correct?

- The result is correct
- The solutions are $x = \pm\sqrt{5}$
- The student lost the solution $x = -5$
- The student lost the solution $x = 0$

## Explanation

Division by $x$ is valid only for $x \neq 0$, so this approach excludes $x = 0$ from the outset. The quadratic has **no constant term**, so factor out $x$ to get $x^2 - 5x = x(x - 5) = 0$. By the zero-product property, the roots are $x = 0$ and $x = 5$, so the student lost the solution $x = 0$. The value $-5$ is not a solution because $(-5)^2 = 25$, whereas $5 \cdot (-5) = -25$.
