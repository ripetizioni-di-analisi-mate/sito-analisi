The graph shows the parabola $y = ax^2 + bx + c$. For which values of $x$ does $ax^2 + bx + c < 0$ hold?

::grafico{f="-x^2+2*x+3" punti="-1,0,pieno;3,0,pieno" legenda="y = ax^2 + bx + c" xmin=-3 xmax=5 ymin=-6 ymax=5 titolo="The parabola meets the $x$-axis at horizontal coordinates $-1$ and $3$"}

- $-1 < x < 3$
- $x \leq -1 \;\vee\; x \geq 3$
- $x > 3$
- $x < -1 \;\vee\; x > 3$

## Explanation

The quadratic $ax^2 + bx + c$ is negative where the parabola lies below the $x$-axis. The parabola opens downward and meets the $x$-axis at $-1$ and $3$, so it lies below the axis to the left of $-1$ and to the right of $3$. The inequality is strict, so the solutions are $x < -1 \;\vee\; x > 3$. The answer $-1 < x < 3$ describes the points where the parabola lies above the axis. The answer with $\leq$ and $\geq$ includes the points where the quadratic is zero.
