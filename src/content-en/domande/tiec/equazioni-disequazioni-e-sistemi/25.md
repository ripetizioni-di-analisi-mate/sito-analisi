How many real solutions does $x^4 - 5x^2 + 4 = 0$ have?

- $2$
- $1$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

The unknown appears only to the powers $x^4$ and $x^2$, so set $t = x^2$ to obtain the quadratic equation $t^2 - 5t + 4 = 0$. Its roots are $t = 1$ and $t = 4$, since their sum is $5$ and their product is $4$. From $x^2 = 1$ we get $x = \pm 1$, and from $x^2 = 4$ we get $x = \pm 2$. The original equation therefore has **four** real solutions. The answer $2$ counts the solutions in $t$ without returning to the original unknown $x$.
