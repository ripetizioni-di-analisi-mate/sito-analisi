What are the solutions of $(x - 2)(x + 3) = 6$?

- $x = 2$ and $x = -3$
- $x = 8$ and $x = 3$
- $x = 3$ and $x = -4$
- $x = -3$ and $x = 4$

## Explanation

The **zero-product property** applies only when the right side is zero, so first put the equation in standard form. Expanding gives $x^2 + x - 6 = 6$, or $x^2 + x - 12 = 0$. We need two numbers with sum $-1$ and product $-12$, namely $3$ and $-4$. The numbers $2$ and $-3$ make the left side zero, but it must equal $6$. The pair $8$ and $3$ comes from setting each factor equal to $6$.
