One factor in a production model is $x-2$ and the other is $x+3$. Their product must be zero and the first factor must be negative. Which input is allowed?

- $x=3$
- $x=0$
- $x=-3$
- $x=2$

## Explanation

The product can vanish at $x=2$ or $x=-3$. In the first case the first factor is zero and fails the strict condition. In the second it equals $-5$, so both conditions hold.
