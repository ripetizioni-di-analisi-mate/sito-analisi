A booking can be cancelled if at least $48$ hours remain before the event or if the organiser grants an exception. Which condition describes exactly the cases in which cancellation is ruled out?

- At least $48$ hours remain and there is no exception
- Fewer than $48$ hours remain or there is no exception
- At most $48$ hours remain and there is no exception
- Fewer than $48$ hours remain and there is no exception

## Explanation

Both alternative conditions must fail to rule out cancellation. The negation of “at least $48$” is “fewer than $48$”. At exactly the threshold a booking can still be cancelled without an exception.
