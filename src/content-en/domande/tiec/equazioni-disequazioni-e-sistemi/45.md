Which of the following numbers does *not* satisfy $\dfrac{1 - x}{2} < x + 2$?

- $0$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $-1$

## Explanation

Multiplying by $2$ gives $1 - x < 2x + 4$, or $-3 < 3x$, so the solutions are $x > -1$. The numbers $0$, $-\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $2$ are greater than $-1$ and satisfy the inequality. At $x = -1$, however, the left side equals $\dfrac{2}{2} = 1$ and the right side equals $1$, and $1 < 1$ is false. Testing the choices by substitution also gives the answer without solving the inequality.
