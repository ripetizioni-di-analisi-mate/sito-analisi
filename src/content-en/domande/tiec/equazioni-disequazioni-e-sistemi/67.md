For which real numbers $x$ is the fraction $\dfrac{(x - 2)^2}{x + 1}$ positive?

- $x > -1$ and $x \neq 2$
- $x > -1$
- $-1 < x < 2$
- $x < -1 \;\vee\; x > 2$

## Explanation

The numerator $(x - 2)^2$ is a square, so it is positive for $x \neq 2$ and zero for $x = 2$. For $x \neq 2$ the fraction therefore has the sign of its denominator and is positive for $x > -1$. At $x = 2$ the fraction equals $0$, which is not greater than $0$, so exclude $2$. The solutions are $x > -1$ with $x \neq 2$. The answer $x > -1$ includes $2$, and the answer $x < -1 \;\vee\; x > 2$ treats $(x - 2)^2$ as if it were $x - 2$.
