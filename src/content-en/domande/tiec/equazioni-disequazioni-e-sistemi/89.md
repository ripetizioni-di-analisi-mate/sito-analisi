One faucet fills a tank in $6$ hours and a second faucet fills it in $3$ hours. How many hours do they take to fill the tank if both are opened together?

- $4.5$
- $2$
- $9$
- $3$

## Explanation

In one hour the first faucet fills $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of the tank and the second fills $\dfrac{1}{3}$. Together they fill $\dfrac{1}{6} + \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ of the tank per hour. If $x$ is the number of hours, then $\dfrac{x}{2} = 1$, or $x = 2$. The value $4.5$ is the mean of the two times, and $9$ is their sum. Both exceed $3$ hours, whereas the two faucets together take less time than the faster faucet alone.
