Marta spends $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of her monthly salary on rent and $\dfrac{1}{4}$ on groceries, leaving her with $750$ euros. What is her monthly salary?

- $1050$ euros
- $1800$ euros
- about $1286$ euros
- $9000$ euros

## Explanation

Let $x$ be the salary in euros. The fraction remaining is $x - \dfrac{x}{3} - \dfrac{x}{4}$, so solve $x - \dfrac{x}{3} - \dfrac{x}{4} = 750$. Multiplying by the common denominator $12$ gives $12x - 4x - 3x = 9000$, or $5x = 9000$, so $x = 1800$ euros. To check, note that $1800 - 600 - 450 = 750$. The value $1050$ comes from calculating the sum $\dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{1}{4}$ as $\dfrac{2}{7}$. The value $1286$ sets the amount spent equal to $750$, and $9000$ omits division by $5$.
