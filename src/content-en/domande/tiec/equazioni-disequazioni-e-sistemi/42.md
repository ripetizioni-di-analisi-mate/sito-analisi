What is the smallest integer that satisfies $4x + 7 > 2x - 4$?

- $-5$
- $-6$
- $-10$
- $6$

## Explanation

Moving terms gives $2x > -11$. Dividing by $2$, which is positive, gives $x > -\dfrac{11}{2} = -5.5$. The integers greater than $-5.5$ are $-5$, $-4$, $-3$, and so on, so the smallest is $-5$. The value $-6$ is less than $-5.5$ and fails the inequality because $-24 + 7 = -17$ is not greater than $-12 - 4 = -16$. The value $-10$ comes from $x > -11$, omitting division by $2$.
