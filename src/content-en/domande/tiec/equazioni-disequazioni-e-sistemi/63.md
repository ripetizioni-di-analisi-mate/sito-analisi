A procedure looks for the first positive integer $n$ for which $(n-2)(n-5)<0$ and stops immediately when it finds one. Which number does it return?

- $5$
- $4$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

The product is negative strictly between its zeros, that is, between $2$ and $5$. The integer candidates are $3$ and $4$. Searching in order from $1$, the procedure stops at $3$.
