The product $(x - 1)(3 - x)$ is positive for which values of $x$?

- $x < 1 \;\vee\; x > 3$
- $x > 3$
- $1 < x < 3$
- $-3 < x < -1$

## Explanation

The factor $x - 1$ is positive for $x > 1$ and the factor $3 - x$ is positive for $x < 3$. The **sign chart** shows that both are positive for $1 < x < 3$ and have opposite signs outside that interval away from their zeros. The product is therefore positive for $1 < x < 3$. The answer $x < 1 \;\vee\; x > 3$ treats $3 - x$ as if it were $x - 3$. The answer $x > 3$ applies the same rule to make both factors positive, imposing $x > 1$ and $x > 3$.
