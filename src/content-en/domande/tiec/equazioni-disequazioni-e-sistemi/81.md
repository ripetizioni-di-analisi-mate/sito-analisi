In a two-digit number, the tens digit exceeds the ones digit by $2$. The sum of the number and the number obtained by reversing its digits is $110$. What is the original number?

- $46$
- $86$
- $64$
- $42$

## Explanation

Let $a$ be the tens digit and $b$ the ones digit. The original number is $10a + b$, and reversing its digits gives $10b + a$. The conditions in the problem give the system
$$
\begin{cases}
a - b = 2 \\
11a + 11b = 110
\end{cases}
$$
The second equation is equivalent to $a + b = 10$. Adding it to the first gives $2a = 12$, so $a = 6$ and $b = 4$. The number is therefore $64$. In $46$ the tens digit is smaller than the ones digit. The digits of $86$ have the required difference, but $86 + 68 = 154$.
