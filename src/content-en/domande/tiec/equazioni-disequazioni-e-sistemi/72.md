The sign chart of $\dfrac{x-a}{x-b}$ is positive for $x<1$ and $x>4$, negative for $1<x<4$, zero at $x=4$ and undefined at $x=1$. Which pair is consistent with it?

- $a=1,\ b=4$
- $a=4,\ b=1$
- $a=-4,\ b=-1$
- $a=4,\ b=-1$

## Explanation

The zero of the fraction identifies the zero of its numerator and fixes $a=4$. The excluded point comes from the denominator and fixes $b=1$. Between them the factors have opposite signs, as the chart requires.
