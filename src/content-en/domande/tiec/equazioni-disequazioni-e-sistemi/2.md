Which statement about $2(3x - 1) = 6x - 2$ is true?

- It has no solution
- Its only solution is $x = 0$
- Its only solution is $x = \dfrac{1}{3}$
- It is an identity

## Explanation

Expanding the product gives $6x - 2 = 6x - 2$. Moving every term to the left gives $0 \cdot x = 0$, which holds for every value of $x$. The equation is therefore an **identity**, and every real number is a solution. The number $0$ is just one of infinitely many solutions. The answer “It has no solution” confuses $0 \cdot x = 0$ with an equation of the form $0 \cdot x = b$ with $b \neq 0$.
