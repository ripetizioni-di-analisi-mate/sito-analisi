A real solution of $x+y=8$, $xy=12$ is to be recorded as an ordered pair. The first value must be the larger one. Which pair belongs in the record?

- $(12,-4)$
- $(4,4)$
- $(6,2)$
- $(2,6)$

## Explanation

The two numbers are the roots of $t^2-8t+12=(t-2)(t-6)$. Both assignments satisfy the initial system. The order condition selects $x=6$ and $y=2$.
