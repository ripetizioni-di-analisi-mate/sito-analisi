What are the solutions of the following system?
$$
\begin{cases}
x - y = 2 \\
xy = 15
\end{cases}
$$

- Only $(5;3)$
- $(3;5)$ and $(-5;-3)$
- $(5;3)$ and $(-3;-5)$
- $(5;3)$ and $(-5;-3)$

## Explanation

The first equation gives $x = y + 2$. Substituting into the second gives $(y + 2)y = 15$, or $y^2 + 2y - 15 = 0$. Two numbers with sum $-2$ and product $-15$ are $3$ and $-5$, so $y = 3$ or $y = -5$. For $y = 3$ we get $x = 5$, and for $y = -5$ we get $x = -3$. The solutions are therefore $(5;3)$ and $(-3;-5)$. The answer “only $(5;3)$” overlooks the negative solution. The pairs $(3;5)$ and $(-5;-3)$ have product $15$, but for both pairs $x - y = -2$, so neither satisfies the first equation.
