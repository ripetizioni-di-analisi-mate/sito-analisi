The following system has a unique solution $(x;y;z)$. What is $x$?
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y = 5 \\
y + z = 7 \\
x + z = 6
\end{cases}
$$

- $3$
- $2$
- $4$
- $9$

## Explanation

Adding the corresponding sides of all three equations gives $2x + 2y + 2z = 18$, so $x + y + z = 9$. Subtracting the second equation, $y + z = 7$, from this equality gives $x = 9 - 7 = 2$. The same procedure gives $y = 3$ and $z = 4$. Substitution into the three equations confirms the result. The values $3$ and $4$ are the other two unknowns, and $9$ is the sum $x + y + z$.
