A parabola has vertex $(2,-1)$ and opens upwards. How many real solutions does the equation setting its vertical coordinate equal to $-2$ have?

- $0$
- Infinitely many
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The vertex gives the minimum vertical coordinate, $-1$. The horizontal line at $-2$ lies entirely below the parabola. Consequently there is no horizontal coordinate of intersection.
