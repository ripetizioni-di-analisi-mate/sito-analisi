Which of the following equations has real solutions?

- $3x^2 + 5x + 2 = 0$
- $x^2 + x + 1 = 0$
- $2x^2 - 3x + 2 = 0$
- $x^2 + 5 = 0$

## Explanation

A quadratic equation has real roots when its **discriminant** is greater than or equal to zero. For $3x^2 + 5x + 2 = 0$ we get $\Delta = 25 - 24 = 1 > 0$, so there are two real roots, $-1$ and $-\dfrac{2}{3}$. For $x^2 + x + 1 = 0$ we get $\Delta = 1 - 4 < 0$, and for $2x^2 - 3x + 2 = 0$ we get $\Delta = 9 - 16 < 0$. The equation $x^2 + 5 = 0$ requires $x^2 = -5$, which no real number satisfies.
