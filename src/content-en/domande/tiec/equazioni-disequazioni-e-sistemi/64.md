A sign chart for $(x^2-1)/x$ shows critical points at $-1,0,1$. Which left-to-right transition happens specifically at the point where the fraction is undefined?

- Negative to positive across a zero
- Positive to negative across a zero
- No sign change, because the point is excluded
- Positive to negative across an excluded point

## Explanation

The only excluded point is $0$. Near zero the numerator is negative on both sides, while the denominator changes from negative to positive. Thus the fraction goes from positive to negative without taking any value at zero.
