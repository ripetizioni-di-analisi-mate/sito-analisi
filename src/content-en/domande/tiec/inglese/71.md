Complete the sentence: "Could you …… me twenty euros until Monday?"

- lend
- borrow
- lent
- borrowed

## Explanation

The speaker asks the other person to give them money on loan. The verb meaning *give on loan* is *lend*, with the recipient immediately after it, as in *lend me*. The modal *could* takes the base form of the verb, so the correct form is *lend*. *Borrow* means *take on loan* and cannot take *me* as the recipient here. *Lent* and *borrowed* are past forms and cannot follow *could*.
