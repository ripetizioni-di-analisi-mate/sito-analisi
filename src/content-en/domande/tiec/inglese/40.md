Which sentence is the passive form of "The company pays its employees at the end of every month"?

- The employees were paid by the company at the end of every month.
- The employees are paid by the company at the end of every month.
- The employees are paying by the company at the end of every month.
- The employees have paid the company at the end of every month.

## Explanation

In the active sentence the verb *pays* is present simple, and in the passive *be* takes the tense of the active verb. The passive is therefore *are paid*, with the agent introduced by *by*. The sentence with *were paid* places the action in the past. *Are paying* is an active present continuous form, and *have paid the company* reverses the roles because it would mean that the employees pay the company.
