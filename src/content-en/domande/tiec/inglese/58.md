Complete the sentence: "How …… information do we need to complete the application?"

- many
- few
- much
- a lot

## Explanation

*Information* is a **noncount noun**, even though Italian often uses the plural *informazioni*. The expression *How much* asks about the quantity of a noncount noun, so the correct phrase is *How much information*. *Many* and *few* go with plural count nouns. *A lot* requires *of* before a noun and does not form a question with *How*.
