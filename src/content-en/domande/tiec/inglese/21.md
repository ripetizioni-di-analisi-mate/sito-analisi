Complete the sentence: "I …… three chapters of the book so far."

- have been reading
- have read
- am reading
- had read

## Explanation

*So far* means *up to now* and connects what has been done to the present, so a present perfect form is needed. The sentence gives a count of what has been completed, *three chapters*. The **present perfect simple** expresses this result, as in *I have read three chapters*. The present perfect continuous emphasizes the duration of the activity and does not express a count of completed items. *Am reading* does not fit *so far*, and *had read* would require a past reference point.
