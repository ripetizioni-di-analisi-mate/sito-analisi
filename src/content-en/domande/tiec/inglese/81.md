Read the rules at a swimming pool and answer the question:

> Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult at all times. Diving is not permitted in the shallow end.

Which statement is true?

- A ten-year-old child cannot swim without an adult present.
- Children under 12 are not allowed in the pool.
- Diving is forbidden everywhere in the pool.
- Adults must stay in the shallow end.

## Explanation

The first rule requires children under 12 to be accompanied by an adult at all times (*at all times*), so a ten-year-old cannot swim without an adult present. The same rule allows children into the pool as long as they are accompanied. The diving ban applies only to the shallow part (*the shallow end*) rather than the entire pool. The rules do not say where adults must stand.
