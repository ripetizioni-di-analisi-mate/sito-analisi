Complete the sentence: "I'll send you the file as soon as I …… back to the office."

- will get
- got
- would get
- get

## Explanation

After *as soon as*, *when*, *before*, and *until* referring to the future, English uses the **present simple**, even though Italian uses the future tense in *appena tornerò* (“as soon as I get back”). The correct expression is therefore *as soon as I get back*. *Will get* translates the Italian future tense word for word and is incorrect in the time clause. *Got* and *would get* are past and conditional forms that do not fit *I'll send*.
