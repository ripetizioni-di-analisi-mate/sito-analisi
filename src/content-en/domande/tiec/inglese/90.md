Read the text and answer the question:

> Five years ago the library of Sorvano was at risk of closing, because only about 300 people used it every month. The new librarian decided to change its role. Today the library lends not only books but also tools, board games and musical instruments. In the afternoon, university students help schoolchildren with their homework, and on Saturday mornings there are free computer courses for older residents. The number of monthly visitors has risen to around 1,200, and the town council has agreed to keep the library open for at least another ten years.

How has the number of monthly visitors changed?

- It has increased by about 300.
- It has doubled.
- It has fallen to about 300.
- It is now about four times higher.

## Explanation

The passage says that five years ago the library had about $300$ users per month and that it now has about $1\,200$ monthly visitors. Since $1\,200 = 4 \cdot 300$, the number of visitors is about four times the original figure. An increase of about $300$ confuses the original figure with the increase, which is about $900$. Doubling would bring the number to about $600$ visitors, and a decrease to $300$ reverses the direction of the change.
