Complete the sentence: "Your phone battery is always empty by lunchtime. You …… buy a new one."

- should to
- mustn't
- should
- needn't to

## Explanation

The speaker is giving advice in response to a problem, and advice takes the modal *should*. After a modal the verb takes the **base form without *to***, so the correct sentence is *You should buy a new one*. *Should to* and *needn't to* add *to* after the modal. *Mustn't* expresses a prohibition and would mean that buying a new battery is forbidden, which does not fit the problem described.
