Complete the sentence: "The engineer …… designed this bridge has won an international prize."

- which
- whose
- who
- where

## Explanation

The relative refers to a person, *the engineer*, who is the subject of *designed*. For people, the relative is *who*, so the correct phrase is *The engineer who designed this bridge*. *Which* refers to things and animals. *Whose* expresses possession and would require a following noun, while *where* refers to places.
