Complete the sentence: "Have you got …… questions before we start?"

- much
- a little
- any
- every

## Explanation

The sentence is a question, and *questions* is a plural count noun. Questions of this kind use *any*, so the blank takes *any*. *Much* and *a little* go with noncount nouns. *Every* takes a singular noun (*every question*), so it does not fit the plural here.
