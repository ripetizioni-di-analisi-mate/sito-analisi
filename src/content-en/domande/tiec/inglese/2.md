Read the sign in a car park and answer the question:

> Pay at the machine before returning to your vehicle. Tickets must be clearly displayed on the dashboard.

What must drivers do?

- Pay an attendant at the exit.
- Show the ticket at the exit barrier.
- Keep the ticket in their pocket until they leave.
- Leave the ticket where it can be seen inside the car.

## Explanation

*Dashboard* means the panel in front of the driver. *Tickets must be clearly displayed* is a passive modal construction meaning that the tickets must be placed where they can easily be seen. Drivers must therefore leave the ticket visible inside the car. The notice mentions a ticket dispenser (*machine*) but says nothing about an attendant or an exit barrier, so answers that mention them add information. Keeping the ticket in a pocket is the opposite of displaying it.
