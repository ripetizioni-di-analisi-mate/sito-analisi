Complete the sentence: "We'll miss the beginning of the concert unless we …… now."

- will leave
- leave
- don't leave
- left

## Explanation

*Unless* means *if not, except if* and introduces a condition like *if*, so the sentence follows the rules of the **first conditional**. The main clause takes *will* and the condition takes the present simple. The correct form is *unless we leave now* (*if we do not leave now*). *Will leave* is incorrect because the condition does not take *will*. *Don't leave* adds a second negative and reverses the meaning because *unless* already contains the idea of *not*. *Left* is the tense used for the second conditional and does not fit *will*.
