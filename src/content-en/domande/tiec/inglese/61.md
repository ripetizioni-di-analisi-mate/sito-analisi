Complete the sentence: "Nobody spoke …… the film, which lasted …… three hours."

- for / during
- while / for
- during / for
- during / since

## Explanation

*During* is followed by a noun and identifies the period in which something happens, giving *during the film*. *For* indicates duration, giving *lasted for three hours*. *For / during* reverses the prepositions. *While* is a conjunction that requires a clause with a verb (*while we were watching*), and *since* indicates a starting point, whereas *three hours* is a duration.
