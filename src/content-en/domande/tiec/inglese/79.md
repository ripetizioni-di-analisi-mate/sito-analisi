Read the note and answer the question:

> Marco, the plumber is coming at 3 to fix the shower. I won't be back till 6, so can you let him in? The keys are in the usual place. Thanks! Giulia

What does Giulia ask Marco to do?

- Fix the shower before 6.
- Open the door for the plumber.
- Call the plumber at 3.
- Leave the keys for Giulia.

## Explanation

*Let him in* means *open the door for him*. Giulia asks Marco to do this because she will not be back until six, after the plumber arrives at three. Giulia therefore wants Marco to open the door for the plumber. Repairing the shower is the plumber’s job, and the note mentions no phone call at three. The keys are mentioned only to say where they are.
