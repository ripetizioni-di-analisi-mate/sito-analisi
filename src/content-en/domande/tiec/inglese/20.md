Which sentence is correct?

- The guests have just arrived.
- The guests have arrived just.
- The guests just have arrived.
- The guests have just arrive.

## Explanation

In the **present perfect**, the adverb *just* in its time sense means *a moment ago* and normally goes between *have* and the past participle. The correct sentence is therefore *The guests have just arrived* (*the guests arrived a moment ago*). In *have arrived just* the adverb is at the end, while in *just have arrived* it precedes the auxiliary. *Have just arrive* uses the base form instead of the participle *arrived*.
