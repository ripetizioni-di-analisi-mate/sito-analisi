Complete the sentence: "Please send me your application …… Friday at the latest."

- until
- within
- by
- since

## Explanation

*At the latest* (*no later than the time specified*) signals a deadline, and the preposition *by* gives the deadline for completing an action. The correct expression is therefore *by Friday at the latest* (*no later than Friday*). *Until* describes a situation that continues up to a given time, as in *I'll wait until Friday*. It appeals to readers who confuse the Italian *entro* (“by”) with *fino a* (“until”). *Within* is followed by a duration (*within three days*), and *since* indicates a starting point in the past.
