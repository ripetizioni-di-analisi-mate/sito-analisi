Complete the sentence: "I need to buy a present for my sister. Is there a good …… near here where I can find the new novel by her favourite writer?"

- bookshop
- library
- bookcase
- librarian

## Explanation

The speaker wants to buy a gift and therefore needs a store that sells books, a *bookshop*. *Library* is a **false friend** because it resembles the Italian *libreria* (“bookstore”) but means *biblioteca* in Italian, a place where people borrow books rather than buy them. *Bookcase* refers to a piece of furniture for holding books, and *librarian* to a person who works in a library.
