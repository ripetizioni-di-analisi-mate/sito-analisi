Read the sentence: "My neighbours had a loud argument last night and I couldn't sleep." What does "argument" mean here?

- a topic
- a party
- a quarrel
- a lesson

## Explanation

*Argument* is a **false friend**. Besides *a reasoned case*, it can mean *a quarrel*, but it does not mean the Italian *argomento* (“topic”). A noisy dispute between neighbors that keeps someone awake is a quarrel, so the meaning is *a quarrel*. *A topic* is the translation of the Italian *argomento* and appeals to readers who translate by sound. *A party* and *a lesson* do not match any meaning of *argument*.
