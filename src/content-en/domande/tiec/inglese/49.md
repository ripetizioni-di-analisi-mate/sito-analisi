Complete the sentence: "The candidate …… the committee selected will start working in September."

- whose
- whom
- which
- where

## Explanation

The relative refers to a person, *the candidate*, who is the direct object of *selected* because the committee selected the candidate. In formal usage *whom* refers to a person functioning as an object, so the correct phrase is *The candidate whom the committee selected*. *Whose* expresses possession and would require a following noun. *Which* refers to things and animals, and *where* refers to places.
