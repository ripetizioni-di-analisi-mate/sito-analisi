Complete the sentence: "Good news: the missing documents …… in the archive."

- have found
- has been found
- were finding
- have been found

## Explanation

Someone found the documents, so the sentence about them is passive. The **present perfect passive** uses *have been* or *has been* followed by the past participle. The plural subject *the missing documents* takes *have*, so the correct form is *have been found*. *Have found* is active and would mean that the documents found something. *Has been found* does not agree with the plural subject, and *were finding* is an active past continuous form.
