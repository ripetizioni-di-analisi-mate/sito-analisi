Which sentence describes an imaginary situation in the present?

- If I have time, I will grow tomatoes.
- If I had a garden, I would grow tomatoes.
- If I had had a garden, I would have grown tomatoes.
- If you grow tomatoes, you need a lot of sun.

## Explanation

An imaginary present situation takes the **second conditional**, with *if* followed by the past simple and *would* followed by the base form. *If I had a garden, I would grow tomatoes* implies that the speaker has no garden now. The sentence with *If I have time… I will grow* is a first conditional and describes a real future possibility. The sentence with *had had… would have grown* is a third conditional and refers to the past, while the one with *If you grow… you need* is a zero conditional stating a general fact.
