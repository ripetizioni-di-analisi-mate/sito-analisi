Complete the sentence: "He …… to be ill so that he could stay at home."

- expected
- demanded
- attended
- pretended

## Explanation

Acting sick in order to stay home means *feigning illness*. The English verb for the Italian *fingere* is *pretend*, a **false friend** of the Italian *pretendere*. The correct form is therefore *pretended*. *Expected* and *demanded* translate the Italian *pretendere* in its senses *to expect* and *to demand*. Neither fits the purpose given in the sentence. *Attended* means *went to* or *took part in*.
