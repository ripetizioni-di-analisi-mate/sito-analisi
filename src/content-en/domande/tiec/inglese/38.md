Complete the sentence: "The new timetable …… on the website next week."

- will be published
- will publish
- is publishing
- will be publish

## Explanation

Someone will publish the timetable, so the sentence requires the passive, and *next week* places the event in the future. The **future passive** uses *will be* followed by the past participle, so the correct form is *will be published*. *Will publish* and *is publishing* are active and would mean that the timetable publishes something. *Will be publish* uses the base form instead of the participle.
