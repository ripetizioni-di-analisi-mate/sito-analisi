Complete the sentence: "I'm sorry, I …… the documents yet. I'll send them to you this afternoon."

- haven't finished
- don't finish
- haven't finish
- wasn't finishing

## Explanation

*Yet* in a negative sentence conveys the sense *not so far* and goes with the **present perfect** because it connects an action that has not happened so far to the present. The present perfect uses *have* followed by the past participle, so the correct form is *haven't finished*. *Haven't finish* uses the base form in place of the participle. *Don't finish* and *wasn't finishing* do not fit *yet* or the idea that the work will be ready in the afternoon.
