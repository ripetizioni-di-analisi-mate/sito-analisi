Complete the sentence: "Every morning my brother …… the 7\:15 bus to work, but this week he …… because the drivers are on strike."

- is catching / cycles
- catches / is cycling
- catches / cycles
- is catching / is cycling

## Explanation

*Every morning* describes a habit. Habits take the **present simple**, so the first form is *catches*. *This week* describes a temporary situation caused by the strike, so the second form is the **present continuous**, *is cycling*. The combinations with *is catching* use a tense for actions in progress to describe a daily habit. The combination *catches / cycles* presents cycling as a habit, although the sentence says it is only for this week.
