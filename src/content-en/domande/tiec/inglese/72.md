Complete the sentence: "Can you …… me to buy some bread on the way home?"

- remember
- remind
- recall
- memorise

## Explanation

The speaker asks the listener to help them remember something. To *prompt someone to remember something* is to *remind* them, using the pattern *remind someone to do something*. The blank therefore takes *remind*. *Remember* and *recall* both mean *bring something to mind* and do not take the person receiving the reminder as their object. *Memorise* means *learn by heart*.
