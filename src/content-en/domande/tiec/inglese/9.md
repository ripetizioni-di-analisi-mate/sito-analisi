Complete the sentence: "While I …… the report, my colleagues …… lunch in the canteen."

- was writing / was having
- was writing / were having
- am writing / were having
- have written / were having

## Explanation

The sentence describes two actions in progress at the same time in the past, so both take the **past continuous**. The auxiliary agrees with its subject, giving *I was writing* and *my colleagues were having*. The second auxiliary in *Was writing / was having* does not agree with the plural subject *my colleagues*. *Am writing* is in the present and does not fit *were having*. The form *have written* refers to a completed action, whereas *while* introduces an action in progress.
