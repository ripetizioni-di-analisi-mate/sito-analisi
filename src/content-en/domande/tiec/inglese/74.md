Read the sentence: "The museum will close temporarily for renovation." Which expression is closest in meaning to "temporarily"?

- permanently
- for a limited period
- immediately
- partially

## Explanation

*Temporarily* means *for a time*, a limited period after which the museum will reopen. The equivalent expression is therefore *for a limited period*. *Permanently* means *forever* and is the opposite. *Immediately* concerns the time when the closure begins, and *partially* concerns the portion of the museum that is closed. Neither says how long the museum will remain closed.
