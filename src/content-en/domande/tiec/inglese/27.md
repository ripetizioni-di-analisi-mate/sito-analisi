Which sentence is grammatically correct?

- If I had known about the delay, I would have taken another train.
- If I had knew about the delay, I would have taken another train.
- If I would have known about the delay, I would take another train.
- If I had known about the delay, I will take another train.

## Explanation

The sentence describes a past delay the speaker did not know about, so it requires the **third conditional**, with the past perfect in the condition and *would have* followed by the past participle in the main clause. Only *If I had known…, I would have taken…* has both parts correct. The sentence with *If I had knew* uses the past tense *knew* instead of the participle *known*. The sentence with *If I would have known* puts *would* in the condition, and the one with *I will take* combines a past perfect with the main clause of a first conditional.
