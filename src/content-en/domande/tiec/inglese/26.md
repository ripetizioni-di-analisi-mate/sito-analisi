Complete the sentence: "If we lived closer to the city centre, we …… the car every day."

- won't need
- wouldn't need
- wouldn't have needed
- don't need

## Explanation

The condition *If we lived* uses the past simple and describes an unreal present situation, since the family actually lives far from the city center. In the **second conditional**, the main clause takes *would* followed by the base form, so the correct form is *wouldn't need*. *Won't need* and *don't need* belong to the first and zero conditionals, respectively. *Wouldn't have needed* would be the main clause of a third conditional and refers to the past.
