Complete the sentence: "Prices …… rapidly this year, and many families are worried."

- rise
- are raising
- are rising
- raise

## Explanation

*This year* indicates a period that is still in progress, and a change taking place now takes the **present continuous**. The right verb is *rise*, which means *go up, increase on its own* and has no direct object, while *raise* means *make something go up* and requires an object (*The shop raised its prices*). The correct form is therefore *are rising*. *Are raising* and *raise* use the verb that requires an object. The present simple *rise* would suggest that the increase is habitual.
