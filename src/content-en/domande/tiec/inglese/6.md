Which question is grammatically correct?

- Where you bought that jacket?
- Where did you bought that jacket?
- Where did you buy that jacket?
- Where have you bought that jacket yesterday?

## Explanation

Questions in the past simple use *did* followed by the **base form** of the verb, so the form is *Where did you buy…?* The question *Where you bought…?* lacks the auxiliary *did* and retains statement word order. *Where did you bought…?* marks the past twice, on *did* and *bought*. *Where have you bought… yesterday?* uses the present perfect with *yesterday*, which refers to a completed time in the past and requires the past simple.
