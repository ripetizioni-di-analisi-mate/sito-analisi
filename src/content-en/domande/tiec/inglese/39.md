Complete the sentence: "Sorry, you can't use Room 4 at the moment. It …… ."

- is cleaning
- is being cleaned
- has cleaned
- is been cleaned

## Explanation

*At the moment* indicates an action in progress, and the room is being cleaned. The **present continuous passive** uses *is being* or *are being* followed by the past participle, so the correct form is *is being cleaned*. *Is cleaning* and *has cleaned* are active and would mean that the room cleans something. *Is been cleaned* combines *is* with the participle *been*, which does not form a tense.
