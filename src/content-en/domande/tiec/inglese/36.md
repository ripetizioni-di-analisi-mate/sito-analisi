Complete the sentence: "All mobile phones …… switched off during the exam."

- must
- must being
- must be
- must to be

## Explanation

The phones are the things being switched off, so the sentence requires the passive. In the passive a modal is followed by *be* and the past participle, so the correct form is *must be switched off* (*someone must turn them off*). *Must switched off* lacks *be*. *Must being* uses the *-ing* form after the modal, and *must to be* adds *to* after the modal.
