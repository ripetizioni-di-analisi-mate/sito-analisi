Read the sentence: "She turned down the job offer because the salary was too low." Which word is closest in meaning to "turned down"?

- accepted
- reduced
- postponed
- refused

## Explanation

*Turn down*, when referring to an offer, means *refuse*. The reason given in the sentence, that the salary was too low, confirms this. The synonym is therefore *refused*. *Accepted* means the opposite. *Reduced* corresponds to a different use of *turn down*, meaning to *lower* the volume or the heat, which makes no sense with *the job offer*. *Postponed* means *delayed*, or *put off*.
