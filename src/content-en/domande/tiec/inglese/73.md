Complete the sentence: "I'm sorry, I …… a mistake in your invoice. I'll send you a corrected one."

- have done
- have taken
- have made
- have got

## Explanation

The English expression is *make a mistake* (*commit an error*). Some English expressions require *make* and others *do*, regardless of the Italian verb. The correct form is therefore *I have made a mistake*. *Have done* translates the Italian *ho fatto* word for word, but *mistake* takes *make*. Neither *Have taken* nor *have got* combines with *mistake* in an expression meaning *err*.
