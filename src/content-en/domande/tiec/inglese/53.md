Complete the sentence: "For our shop, the Saturday before Christmas is …… day of the year."

- the busiest
- the most busy
- the busier
- busiest than

## Explanation

The comparison is with all the days of the year, so a **superlative** is needed. *Busy* has two syllables and ends in *-y*, so its usual superlative uses *the* and the suffix *-est*. The *y* changes to *i*, giving *the busiest*. *The most busy* uses the construction for longer adjectives. *The busier* is a comparative, and *busiest than* combines a superlative with *than*, which introduces the second term of a comparative.
