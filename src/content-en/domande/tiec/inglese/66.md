Complete the sentence: "We look forward to …… you at the conference next month."

- seeing
- see
- seen
- have seen

## Explanation

In *look forward to*, the word *to* is a preposition, and a verb after a preposition takes the **-ing form**. The correct sentence is therefore *We look forward to seeing you*. *See* treats *to* as an infinitive marker. *Seen* is a past participle and *have seen* uses a participle with an auxiliary. Neither form can follow this preposition.
