Read the text and answer the question:

> In the last ten years, the town of Castelfino has turned several abandoned areas into community gardens. Residents can apply for a small plot, where they grow vegetables, herbs and flowers. There is no rent to pay, but gardeners must look after their plot regularly and share tools with their neighbours. If a plot is left untidy for more than two months, it is given to the next person on the waiting list. The gardens have become popular with retired people, but also with young families who live in flats without a balcony. For many of these families, the garden is the only place where children can play outside safely.

In the text, the word "plot" means

- a small piece of land.
- the story of a book or film.
- a secret plan.
- a tool for gardening.

## Explanation

In the passage, residents apply for a *plot* where they grow vegetables, herbs, and flowers. If they leave it untidy for more than two months, it is assigned to someone else. Therefore, *plot* means a small piece of land. *The storyline of a book* and *a secret plan* are other meanings of the word, but neither fits this context. The equipment is referred to by the word *tools*.
