Complete the sentence: "The shop has been closed …… the beginning of the month."

- for
- ago
- during
- since

## Explanation

With the present perfect, *since* introduces the time when a situation began, while *for* introduces its duration. *The beginning of the month* is a starting point, so the correct preposition is *since*. *For* would require a duration, as in *for two weeks*. *Ago* follows a duration and goes with the past simple (*two weeks ago*), and *during* introduces the period within which something happens.
