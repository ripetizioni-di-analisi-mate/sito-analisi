Complete the sentence: "The police are trying to …… who broke the shop window last night."

- carry on
- give up
- put off
- find out

## Explanation

The police want to learn who broke the shop window. The **phrasal verb** meaning *discover* is *find out*, so the correct sentence is *The police are trying to find out who broke the shop window*. *Carry on* means *continue*, *give up* means *quit*, and *put off* means *postpone*. None of these three takes an indirect question such as *who broke the shop window* in this context.
