Complete the sentence: "According to the timetable, the last train to Bergamo …… at 23\:40."

- leave
- has left
- was leaving
- leaves

## Explanation

Train, flight, and store schedules are fixed, so they take the **present simple** even when they refer to the future. The subject *the last train* is third-person singular, so the verb takes *-s*, giving *leaves*. *Leave* lacks the *-s*. *Has left* would mean that the train has already departed, whereas *according to the timetable* introduces a schedule. The form *was leaving* describes an action in progress in the past.
