"Do you need any help?" the shop assistant asked me. Which is the correct reported speech?

- The shop assistant asked me did I need any help.
- The shop assistant asked me if did I need any help.
- The shop assistant asked me if I needed any help.
- The shop assistant said me if I needed any help.

## Explanation

A question that can be answered *yes* or *no* is reported with *if* (or *whether*). **Indirect questions** use statement word order, *do* drops out, and the tense shifts back, so *Do you need* becomes *I needed*. The sentence with *did I need* keeps direct question word order, and the one with *if did I need* adds an auxiliary after *if*. *Said me* uses *say* with the listener as a direct object, and *say* does not introduce reported questions.
