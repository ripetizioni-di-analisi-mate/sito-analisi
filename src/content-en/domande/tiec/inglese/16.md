Which sentence shows that Tom left BEFORE Anna arrived?

- When Anna arrived, Tom left.
- When Anna arrived, Tom had left.
- When Anna arrived, Tom was leaving.
- When Tom was leaving, Anna arrived.

## Explanation

The **past perfect** describes an action completed before another time in the past, so *When Anna arrived, Tom had left* tells us that Tom was already gone by the time Anna arrived. In *When Anna arrived, Tom left*, the two past simple verbs present the events in sequence, so Tom leaves after Anna arrives. In *Tom was leaving*, the past continuous describes an action in progress, so Tom was on his way out just as Anna arrived. *When Tom was leaving, Anna arrived* has the same meaning with the clauses reversed.
