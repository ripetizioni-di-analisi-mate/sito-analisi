"I can't come to the meeting," said Luca. Which is the correct reported speech?

- Luca said he couldn't came to the meeting.
- Luca told he couldn't come to the meeting.
- Luca said he hadn't could come to the meeting.
- Luca said he couldn't come to the meeting.

## Explanation

In **reported speech** the modal *can* becomes *could*. After *could* the verb stays in the base form, so the correct sentence is *Luca said he couldn't come to the meeting*. *Couldn't came* uses the past form after the modal. *Told he* uses *tell* without the object naming the listener that *tell* requires. *Hadn't could* treats *could* as a participle, but modal verbs have no participles.
