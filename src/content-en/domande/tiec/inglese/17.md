Complete the sentence: "Be careful! That glass is right at the edge of the table. It …… fall."

- is going to
- goes to
- is going
- will to

## Explanation

The prediction is based on evidence visible now, the glass at the edge of the table. Predictions based on present evidence take **be going to** followed by the base form, so the correct form is *is going to fall*. *Goes to fall* lacks the *be going* construction, while *is going fall* lacks *to*, so neither forms a grammatical prediction in this sentence. *Will to* is incorrect because *will* takes the base form without *to*.
