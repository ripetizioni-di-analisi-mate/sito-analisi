Complete the sentence: "Very …… people came to the presentation, so the room looked almost empty."

- few
- little
- a few
- a little

## Explanation

*People* is a plural count noun, so its quantifier could be *few* or *a few*. *Few* without an article has a negative sense (*not many, not enough*), while *a few* has a positive sense (*some*). The nearly empty room signals the negative sense. Also, *very* combines with *few* but not *a few*, so the correct phrase is *Very few people*. *Little* and *a little* go with noncount nouns.
