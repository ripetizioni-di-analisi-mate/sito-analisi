"I saw the director yesterday," she said. Which is the correct reported speech?

- She said she had seen the director the day before.
- She said she had seen the director the next day.
- She said she has seen the director the day before.
- She said she had saw the director the day before.

## Explanation

In **reported speech** the past simple *saw* becomes the past perfect *had seen*, and *yesterday* becomes *the day before* because the day is identified in relation to the time when the words were spoken. The correct sentence is therefore *She said she had seen the director the day before*. *The next day* corresponds to *tomorrow* and moves the meeting to the following day. *Has seen* fails to shift the tense back, and *had saw* uses the past tense *saw* instead of the participle *seen*.
