At a station, Kim asks, “Which platform does the York train leave from?” Complete the clerk’s reply to a colleague: “Kim wants to know which platform ___.”

- does the York train leave from
- the York train leaves from
- the York train does leaves from
- leaves the York train from

## Explanation

An embedded question uses statement word order, giving *the York train leaves*. The preposition *from* stays at the end. The direct question’s auxiliary *does* is no longer needed.
