Complete the question: "…… your brother have to wear a uniform at work?"

- Has
- Does
- Must
- Is

## Explanation

*Have to* behaves like an ordinary verb, so present simple questions use *do* or *does* followed by *have to*. The subject *your brother* is third-person singular, so the auxiliary is *does*, giving *Does your brother have to wear a uniform?* The question *Has your brother have to…?* is incorrect because it treats *have to* as an auxiliary. Starting with *Must* would ask whether the requirement itself is necessary. The ordinary question about a requirement expressed by *have to* takes do-support, and *is* cannot be followed by the base form *have* here.
