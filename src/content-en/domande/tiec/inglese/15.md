Complete the sentence: "This is the most beautiful beach I …… ."

- have ever seen
- have ever saw
- ever see
- am ever seeing

## Explanation

After a superlative such as *the most beautiful*, the comparison covers all experiences up to the present, so the sentence takes the **present perfect** with *ever*. The present perfect uses *have* followed by the past participle, so the correct form is *I have ever seen* (*that I have seen at any point in my life*). *Have ever saw* uses the past tense *saw* instead of the participle *seen*. *Ever see* and *am ever seeing* are present forms and do not express experience accumulated up to now.
