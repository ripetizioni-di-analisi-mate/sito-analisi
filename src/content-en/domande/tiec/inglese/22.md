Read the sentence: "If I were you, I would accept the offer." What is the speaker doing?

- Describing a decision taken in the past.
- Making a promise about the future.
- Asking for permission to accept the offer.
- Giving advice to the listener.

## Explanation

The sentence is a **second conditional**, with the past simple *were* in the condition and *would accept* in the main clause. The speaker imagines being in the listener’s position (*in your place*) and says what they would do. The expression *If I were you* therefore gives advice. The past form *were* signals distance from reality rather than past time, so the sentence does not describe a decision already made. A promise would use *will*, and a request for permission would use *Can I* or *May I*.
