Read the email and answer the question:

> Dear Ms Ricci,
>
> I am writing to ask whether the position of sales assistant advertised on your website is still available. Please find attached my CV. I would be happy to come for an interview at any time next week. I look forward to hearing from you.
>
> Kind regards, Davide Neri

What is the main purpose of the email?

- To complain about a website.
- To offer Ms Ricci a job.
- To confirm the date of an interview.
- To apply for a job.

## Explanation

Davide asks whether the sales assistant position advertised on the website is still open, attaches his résumé (*Please find attached my CV*), and says he is available for an interview. The purpose of the email is therefore **to apply for a job**. The website is mentioned only as the place where the advertisement appeared, and the email contains no complaint. Davide is the one seeking work, so the option about offering Ms. Ricci a job reverses their roles. No interview date has been set, since Davide offers to come *any time next week*.
