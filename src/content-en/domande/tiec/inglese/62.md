Complete the sentence: "The reception is …… the ground floor, and the lifts are …… the end of the corridor."

- in / at
- on / in
- at / on
- on / at

## Explanation

Floors of a building take *on* because the floor is thought of as a surface, giving *on the ground floor*. A precise location such as the end of a corridor takes *at*, giving *at the end of the corridor*. *In the ground floor* uses the preposition for enclosed spaces. *In the end of the corridor* is incorrect for this location, and *at / on* reverses the two prepositions.
