Complete the sentence: "Rome, …… is the capital of Italy, attracts millions of tourists every year."

- that
- which
- where
- who

## Explanation

The clause between commas adds nonessential information about *Rome*, which is already identified by name. **Nonrestrictive relative clauses** do not take *that*. For a thing or place functioning as the subject of the clause, use *which*. The correct form is therefore *Rome, which is the capital of Italy*. *Where* would require a separate subject (*where many people live*) because it identifies the place where something happens. *Who* refers to people.
