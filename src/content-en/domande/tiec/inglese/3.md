Complete the sentence: "I …… what you mean, but I don't agree with you."

- am understanding
- understands
- am understand
- understand

## Explanation

*Understand* is a **stative verb** because it describes a mental state rather than an action. Stative verbs take the present simple even when they refer to the moment of speaking, so the correct form is *I understand*. *Am understanding* puts the stative verb in the *-ing* form. *Understands* uses third-person *-s* with the subject *I*, and *am understand* combines the continuous auxiliary with the base form.
