Read the label on a jar of sauce and answer the question:

> Best before: see lid. Once opened, keep refrigerated and consume within three days.

Which statement is true?

- After opening, the sauce should be kept in the fridge and eaten within three days.
- The sauce must be kept in the fridge before it is opened.
- The date is printed on the bottom of the jar.
- The sauce can be eaten up to three days after the date on the lid.

## Explanation

*Once opened* means *after you open the jar*, *keep refrigerated* means *store in the refrigerator*, and *consume within three days* means *use it up no later than three days afterward*. The refrigeration and three-day instructions therefore apply to the sauce after opening. The label does not say that the jar must be refrigerated before it is opened. *See lid* directs the reader to the top of the jar (*lid*). It does not mention the bottom. The three days are counted from the time the jar is opened.
