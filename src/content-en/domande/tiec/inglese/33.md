Complete the sentence: "Giulia …… my message yesterday. Her phone was switched off all day."

- must have read
- can't have read
- mustn't read
- can't read

## Explanation

The fact that the phone was off all day rules out Giulia’s having read the message. A **deduction that something was impossible in the past** takes *can't have* followed by the past participle, so the correct form is *can't have read* (*it is impossible that she read it*). *Must have read* expresses the opposite deduction, that she certainly read it. *Mustn't read* is a prohibition, and *can't read* refers to the present, whereas *yesterday* places the event in the past.
