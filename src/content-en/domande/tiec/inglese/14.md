Complete the sentence: "The company …… its first shop twenty years ago."

- has opened
- opened
- had opened
- has been opening

## Explanation

*Ago* identifies a specific, completed time in the past, measured back from today, and takes the **past simple**. The correct form is therefore *opened*. *Has opened* and *has been opening* are present perfect forms, which do not go with a completed time in the past. *Had opened* would require another past reference point, and the sentence provides none.
