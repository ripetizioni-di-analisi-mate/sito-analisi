Complete the dialogue: A: "The printer isn't working again." B: "Don't worry, I …… someone from IT."

- 'll call
- call
- would call
- am going call

## Explanation

B decides to call the technician on hearing about the problem. Decisions made at the moment of speaking take **will** followed by the base form. The correct form is therefore *I'll call*. The present simple *Call* would express a habit. *Would call* is a conditional and would require a condition. *Am going call* lacks *to*, which is required in *be going to*.
