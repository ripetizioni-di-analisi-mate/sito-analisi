Complete the sentence: "Saving part of your salary every month is a very …… decision."

- sensitive
- sensible
- comprehensive
- eventual

## Explanation

The sentence describes saving as a reasonable choice. *Reasonable, showing good judgment* is the meaning of *sensible*, a **false friend** of the Italian *sensibile*. The correct word is therefore *sensible*. *Sensitive* means *sensibile* in Italian and appeals to readers who translate by sound. *Comprehensive* means *complete or thorough*, and *eventual* means *final*, something that comes at the end.
