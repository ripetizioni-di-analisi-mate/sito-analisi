Read the sentence: "You needn't bring any food to the party." What does it mean?

- You are not allowed to bring food.
- You must bring some food.
- It is not necessary to bring food.
- You should bring only a little food.

## Explanation

*Needn't* means *there is no need to* and has the same meaning as *don't have to*, so the sentence says that bringing food **is unnecessary**. *You are not allowed to bring food* expresses a prohibition, which would take *mustn't*. *You must bring some food* imposes the opposite obligation. *You should bring only a little food* gives advice about quantity, whereas the original sentence leaves the choice to the listener.
