A: "Would you mind closing the window?" Which reply means that B is happy to close it?

- Not at all, I'll do it now.
- Yes, I do.
- Of course I mind.
- No, I won't close it.

## Explanation

*Would you mind…?* literally asks *Would it bother you…?* because *mind* means *have an objection*. Someone who accepts the request therefore gives a negative answer, *Not at all* (*not in the least, no problem*). The reply that accepts is *Not at all, I'll do it now*. *Yes, I do* means *yes, it bothers me* and is a refusal, although readers may take *yes* as agreement. *Of course I mind* explicitly says the request is unwelcome, and *No, I won't close it* refuses to close the window.
