Which sentence uses the relative clause correctly?

- The laptop who I bought last week has stopped working.
- The laptop what I bought last week has stopped working.
- The laptop I bought last week has stopped working.
- The laptop I bought it last week has stopped working.

## Explanation

When a relative pronoun is the direct object in a defining clause, it can be **omitted**, so *The laptop I bought last week* is correct. *Who* refers to people and does not fit a thing such as a laptop. *What* cannot serve as a relative pronoun after a noun in this construction. In the option with *I bought it*, the pronoun *it* repeats the object already identified by the relative clause, *the laptop*.
