Read the sign at a shop till and answer the question:

> Refunds only with receipt. Sale items cannot be returned.

Which statement is true?

- Sale items can be returned if you have the receipt.
- Customers must show a receipt to enter the shop.
- A customer without a receipt cannot get a refund.
- All items can be exchanged at any time.

## Explanation

*Refund* means *money paid back to a customer*, and *receipt* means *proof of purchase*. The first sentence therefore says that customers can get a refund only by showing a receipt. It follows that a customer without a receipt cannot get a refund. The second sentence says that discounted goods (*sale items*) cannot be returned even with a receipt. The notice concerns refunds rather than admission to the store, and it says nothing about exchanges being available at any time.
