Complete the sentence: "The train was delayed for hours, but we …… arrived home at midnight."

- possibly
- currently
- lately
- eventually

## Explanation

After hours of delay, the travelers reach home at midnight, *in the end*. A word meaning *in the end, finally* is *eventually*. *Eventually* is a **false friend** because it resembles the Italian *eventualmente*, which translates as *possibly* or *if necessary*. *Possibly* would suggest that their arrival was only a possibility, while the sentence reports that it happened. *Currently* means *at present*, and *lately* means *recently*. Neither fits an event completed at midnight.
