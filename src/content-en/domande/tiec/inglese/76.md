Read the notice at a railway station and answer the question:

> Please do not leave bags unattended. Unattended items may be removed and destroyed.

What does the notice mean?

- Passengers must keep their bags with them.
- Passengers can leave their bags at the information desk.
- Lost bags will be returned to their owners.
- Passengers are not allowed to bring bags into the station.

## Explanation

*Unattended* means *left without anyone watching it*, so the first sentence asks passengers not to leave their bags unsupervised. The second explains that unattended items may be removed and destroyed, so the notice asks passengers to keep their bags with them. It mentions no storage service at the information desk. Returning lost bags contradicts *may be removed and destroyed*, and a ban on bringing bags into the station goes beyond the notice, which applies only to bags left on their own.
