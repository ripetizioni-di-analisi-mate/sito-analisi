Complete the sentence: "Yesterday the trains were on strike, so I …… walk to work."

- had to
- must
- have to
- should have

## Explanation

The sentence describes an obligation caused by an external event yesterday, the strike. *Must* has no past-tense form, so **past obligation** takes *had to* followed by the base form. The correct form is therefore *I had to walk*. *Must* and *have to* express present obligation and do not fit *yesterday*. *Should have* would take a past participle and express criticism.
