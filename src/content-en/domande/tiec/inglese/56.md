Complete the sentence: "The football match was cancelled …… the heavy rain."

- although
- due to
- however
- so that

## Explanation

The heavy rain caused the cancellation, and a cause expressed as a noun phrase can be introduced by *due to* (*because of*). The correct sentence is therefore *The match was cancelled due to the heavy rain*. *Although* and *however* express contrast, and neither can introduce this noun phrase to give the reason for the cancellation. *So that* introduces a purpose and takes a clause containing a verb.
