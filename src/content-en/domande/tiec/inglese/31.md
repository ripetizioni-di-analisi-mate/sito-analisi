Complete the sentence: "Take a jacket. It …… get cold later this evening."

- might to
- mustn't
- might
- needn't

## Explanation

The speaker recommends taking a jacket because it may get cold later. A **possibility** is expressed with *may* or *might* followed by the base form, so the correct sentence is *It might get cold*. *Might to* adds *to* after the modal. *Mustn't* expresses a prohibition and *needn't* expresses lack of necessity, neither of which makes sense when referring to the weather here.
