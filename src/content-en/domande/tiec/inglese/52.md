Complete the sentence: "The weather today is even …… than yesterday."

- badder
- worst
- more bad
- worse

## Explanation

*Than yesterday* requires a comparative, and *bad* has the **irregular comparative** *worse*. The correct expression is therefore *even worse than yesterday* (*worse still than it was yesterday*). *Badder* and *more bad* apply the rules for regular adjectives to *bad*. *Worst* is the superlative and does not go with *than*.
