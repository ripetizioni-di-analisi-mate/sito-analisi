Complete the sentence: "We got lost because we didn't take the map. We …… it with us."

- should take
- must have taken
- had to take
- should have taken

## Explanation

The speaker regrets leaving the map behind. **Regret** about something that was not done takes *should have* followed by the past participle, so the correct form is *should have taken* (*it was a mistake not to take it*). *Should take* refers to the present or future. *Must have taken* is a deduction meaning that they certainly took it, which contradicts *we didn't take the map*. *Had to take* expresses a past obligation rather than regret about an action that was not taken.
