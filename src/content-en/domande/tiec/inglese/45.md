"Close the door, please," the teacher said to Leo. Which is the correct reported speech?

- The teacher told Leo close the door.
- The teacher told Leo to close the door.
- The teacher said Leo to close the door.
- The teacher told to Leo to close the door.

## Explanation

An order or request can be reported with *tell* followed by the person addressed and *to* plus the base form of the verb. The correct sentence is therefore *The teacher told Leo to close the door*. The sentence with *told Leo close* lacks *to* before the verb. *Said Leo* uses *say* with the listener as an object without a preposition, while *told to Leo* adds a preposition that *tell* does not take.
