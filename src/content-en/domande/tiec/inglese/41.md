An email begins with "Dear Sir or Madam". Which closing is the most appropriate?

- See you soon,
- Love,
- Cheers,
- Yours faithfully,

## Explanation

*Dear Sir or Madam* opens a formal email to someone whose name is unknown. Formal emails have conventional closings, and in traditional British correspondence the customary closing with this greeting is *Yours faithfully*. *See you soon*, *Love*, and *Cheers* are informal closings used with friends and family.
