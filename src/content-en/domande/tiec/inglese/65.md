Complete the sentence: "Two former students …… a small company that repairs bicycles."

- set off
- took off
- set up
- turned up

## Explanation

Two former students started a company. The expression for *start, establish* is *set up*. The past simple of *set* is *set*, so the form *set up* also works in the past. *Set off* means *leave on a journey*. *Took off* describes a plane leaving the ground, and *turned up* means *showed up*. Neither can take *a company* as an object with the meaning “establish.”
