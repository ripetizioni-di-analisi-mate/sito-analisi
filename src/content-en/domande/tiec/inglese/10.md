Complete the sentence: "How often …… your sister visit your parents?"

- do
- is
- does
- has

## Explanation

*How often* asks about the frequency of a habit, so the question is in the present simple. Present simple questions take *do* or *does*. With a third-person singular subject such as *your sister*, the auxiliary is *does*. *Do* goes with *I*, *you*, *we*, and *they*. *Is* and *has* take different verb forms (*is visiting*, *has visited*) from the base form *visit*.
