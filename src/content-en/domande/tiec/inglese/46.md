Complete the sentence: "This is the café …… we met for the first time."

- where
- which
- who
- whose

## Explanation

The relative introduces information about a place, *the café*, and indicates where the meeting took place. A place takes *where*, so the correct sentence is *This is the café where we met*. *Which* refers to a thing but would need a preposition (*in which*) to identify the location here. *Who* refers to people and *whose* expresses possession.
