Complete the sentence: "We work for a company …… employees can work from home two days a week."

- which
- who
- whose
- what

## Explanation

There is a relationship of possession between *a company* and *employees* because the employees work for the company. The **relative pronoun** expressing possession is *whose* and can also refer to things, so the correct phrase is *a company whose employees can work from home* (*a company that allows its employees to work from home*). *Which* refers to the company but would leave *employees* without a connection to the rest of the sentence. *Who* refers to people and does not indicate possession, while *what* cannot serve as a relative pronoun after a noun in this construction.
