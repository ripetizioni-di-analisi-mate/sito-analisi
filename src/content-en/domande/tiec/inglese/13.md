Complete the question: "You look exhausted. How long …… on this project? Since this morning?"

- are you working
- did you work
- have you been working
- do you work

## Explanation

The question asks how long an activity has been going on. It began this morning and is still in progress, as *How long* and *Since this morning* show. An action that began in the past and continues now, with the emphasis on duration, takes the **present perfect continuous**, so the correct form is *have you been working*. *Are you working* and *do you work* copy the present tense of the Italian *da quanto lavori* (“how long have you been working?”). This use of the present with *since* is incorrect in English. *Did you work* presents the work as finished and does not fit *since*.
