Complete the sentence: "Who will …… your cat while you are on holiday?"

- look for
- look after
- look forward to
- look up

## Explanation

Someone needs to care for the cat during the vacation, and *take care of* is the meaning of *look after*. The blank therefore takes *look after*. *Look for* means *search for* and would suggest that the cat is missing. *Look forward to* means *anticipate with pleasure*, and *look up* means *search for information*, for example in a dictionary.
