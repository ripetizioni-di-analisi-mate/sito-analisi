Complete the sentence: "My new phone is …… my old one, but it is much lighter."

- as expensive than
- so expensive like
- more expensive as
- as expensive as

## Explanation

A comparison of equality uses *as* … *as*, so *as expensive as my old one* means *equal in price to my old phone*. *As expensive than* combines the first *as* with the *than* used in a comparative of greater degree. *So expensive like* copies the Italian expression *così… come* (“as … as”). *More expensive as* combines a comparative of greater degree with the second *as*.
