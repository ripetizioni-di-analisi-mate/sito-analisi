Read the notice on a bank's website and answer the question:

> Due to scheduled maintenance, online banking will be unavailable from 11 p.m. on Saturday to 6 a.m. on Sunday. Card payments will not be affected.

Which statement is true?

- Online banking will be unavailable for the whole weekend.
- Card payments will stop on Saturday night.
- Customers will still be able to pay by card during the maintenance.
- The maintenance was not planned in advance.

## Explanation

The notice says that card payments *will not be affected*, meaning that they will continue as usual, so customers can still pay by card during maintenance. Online service is suspended from 11 p.m. on Saturday to 6 a.m. on Sunday, a total of seven hours rather than the whole weekend. Suspending card payments would reverse the second sentence. *Scheduled* means *planned*, so the maintenance was expected.
