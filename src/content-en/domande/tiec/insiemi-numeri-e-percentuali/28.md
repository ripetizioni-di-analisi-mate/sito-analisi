Which of the following values is closest to $19.6\%$ of $5030$?

- $1102$
- $893$
- $98.6$
- $986$

## Explanation

Since $19.6\%$ is just under $20\%$ and $5030$ is just above $5000$, we can **estimate** the result as $20\%$ of $5000$, or $1000$. The exact calculation gives $0.196 \cdot 5030 \approx 985.9$, and $986$ is the closest choice. The values $1102$ and $893$ differ from the exact result by more than $100$, and $98.6$ has the wrong order of magnitude.
