What is $12.5\%$ of $64$?

- $7.68$
- $0.8$
- $8$
- $56$

## Explanation

The percentage $12.5\%$ is equal to the fraction $\dfrac{12.5}{100} = \dfrac{1}{8}$, so $12.5\%$ of $64$ is one-eighth of $64$, or $8$. Recognizing the fraction makes it possible to do the calculation mentally. The value $7.68$ is $12\%$ of $64$ and leaves out the $0.5\%$. In $0.8$ the decimal point is off by one place, and $56$ is $64$ decreased by $12.5\%$.
