What is the sum $\dfrac{5}{12} + \dfrac{7}{18}$?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{29}{36}$
- $\dfrac{29}{72}$
- $\dfrac{35}{216}$

## Explanation

The least common denominator is $\mathrm{mcm}(12, 18) = 36$ because $12 = 2^2 \cdot 3$ and $18 = 2 \cdot 3^2$. We have $\dfrac{5}{12} = \dfrac{15}{36}$ and $\dfrac{7}{18} = \dfrac{14}{36}$, so the sum is $\dfrac{29}{36}$. The fraction $\dfrac{2}{5} = \dfrac{12}{30}$ adds the numerators and denominators, and $\dfrac{35}{216}$ is the product of the fractions. The fraction $\dfrac{29}{72}$ uses twice the LCM as its denominator without adjusting the numerators.
