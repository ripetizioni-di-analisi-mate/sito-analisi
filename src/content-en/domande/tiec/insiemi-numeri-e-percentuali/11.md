Let $A$ be the set of natural numbers that are multiples of $6$. Which of the following sets is a subset of $A$?

- $\{6, 15, 24\}$
- $\{0, 3, 6\}$
- $\{18, 27, 36\}$
- $\{12, 30, 48\}$

## Explanation

A set is a **subset** of $A$ if *every* one of its elements belongs to $A$. The numbers $12$, $30$, and $48$ are multiples of $6$ because $12 = 6 \cdot 2$, $30 = 6 \cdot 5$, and $48 = 6 \cdot 8$, so $\{12, 30, 48\} \subseteq A$. Each of the other sets contains an element that is not a multiple of $6$, namely $15$, $3$, and $27$, respectively. A single element outside $A$ is enough to show that the set is not contained in $A$.
