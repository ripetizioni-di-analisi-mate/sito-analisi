Let $a$ and $b$ be any real numbers. Which of the following implications is always true?

- $a < b \implies a^2 < b^2$
- $a < b \implies |a| < |b|$
- $a < b \implies 2a < b$
- $a < b \implies 5 - a > 5 - b$

## Explanation

Multiplying $a < b$ by $-1$ reverses the direction of the inequality, giving $-a > -b$. Adding $5$ to both sides preserves the direction, so $5 - a > 5 - b$. Each of the other implications is false for some choice of $a$ and $b$. With $a = -3$ and $b = 2$, we have $a^2 = 9 > 4 = b^2$ and $|a| = 3 > 2 = |b|$. With $a = 1$ and $b = \dfrac{3}{2}$, we have $2a = 2 > b$.
