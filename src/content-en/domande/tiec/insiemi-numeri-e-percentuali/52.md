Which of the following numbers is prime?

- $91$
- $87$
- $51$
- $97$

## Explanation

Any composite number below $121 = 11^2$ has a prime factor smaller than $11$. Thus for these numbers below $100$, it is enough to test divisibility by $2$, $3$, $5$, and $7$. The number $97$ is divisible by none of these primes, so it is **prime**. The others factor as $91 = 7 \cdot 13$, $87 = 3 \cdot 29$, and $51 = 3 \cdot 17$. For $87$ and $51$, the divisibility test reveals the factor $3$ because the digit sums are $15$ and $6$.
