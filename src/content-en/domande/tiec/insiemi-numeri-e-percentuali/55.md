In a class, $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of the students play a sport, half play an instrument, and one-quarter do both. What fraction of the students do neither?

- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The fraction who do at least one activity is given by the formula for the **number of elements in a union**, applied to fractions of the class:
$$
\frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{8 + 6 - 3}{12} = \frac{11}{12} .
$$
The students who do neither therefore make up $1 - \dfrac{11}{12} = \dfrac{1}{12}$ of the class. The fraction $\dfrac{5}{12} = \dfrac{2}{3} - \dfrac{1}{4}$ represents those who only play a sport, and $\dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{1}{4}$ represents those who only play an instrument.
