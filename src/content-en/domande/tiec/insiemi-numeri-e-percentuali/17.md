What is the prime factorization of $540$?

- $2^2 \cdot 3^3 \cdot 5$
- $2^3 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$
- $2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$
- $2^2 \cdot 3^3 \cdot 5^2$

## Explanation

Dividing by primes in increasing order gives $540 = 2 \cdot 270 = 2^2 \cdot 135$, and $135 = 3 \cdot 45 = 3^2 \cdot 15 = 3^3 \cdot 5$. Therefore $540 = 2^2 \cdot 3^3 \cdot 5$. As a check, $4 \cdot 27 \cdot 5 = 540$. The other factorizations give different numbers because $2^3 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5 = 360$, $2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5 = 180$, and $2^2 \cdot 3^3 \cdot 5^2 = 2700$.
