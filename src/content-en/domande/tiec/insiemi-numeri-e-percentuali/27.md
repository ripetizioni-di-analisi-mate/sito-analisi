$30$ is $15\%$ of what number?

- $200$
- $4.5$
- $34.5$
- $450$

## Explanation

If $30$ is $15\%$ of $N$, then $\dfrac{15}{100} \cdot N = 30$, so $N = \dfrac{30 \cdot 100}{15} = 200$. As a check, $10\%$ of $200$ is $20$ and $5\%$ is $10$, giving a sum of $30$. The value $4.5$ is $15\%$ of $30$, which calculates in the opposite direction, and $34.5$ is $30$ increased by $15\%$.
