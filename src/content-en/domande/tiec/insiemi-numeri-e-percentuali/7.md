A rectangular floor measuring $360$ cm by $420$ cm must be covered with identical square tiles without cutting any tiles. What is the greatest possible side length of a tile?

- $30$ cm
- $120$ cm
- $60$ cm
- $2520$ cm

## Explanation

The side of a tile must divide both $360$ and $420$ because whole tiles cover each side of the floor. It must be as large as possible, so it is the **greatest common divisor**. From the factorizations $360 = 2^3 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$ and $420 = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7$, take the common prime factors with the smaller exponents to obtain $2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 = 60$. With a side length of $60$ cm, $6 \cdot 7 = 42$ tiles are needed. Although $30$ cm divides both dimensions, it is not the greatest common divisor. The length $120$ cm does not divide $420$, and $2520$ is the LCM.
