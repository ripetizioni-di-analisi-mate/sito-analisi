Which of the following operations on any two natural numbers always gives a natural number?

- Addition
- Subtraction
- Division
- Taking the arithmetic mean

## Explanation

The sum of two natural numbers is always a natural number. The other operations can give a result outside $\mathbb{N}$. For example, $3 - 5 = -2$ is a negative integer, $3 : 5 = \dfrac{3}{5}$ is not an integer, and the mean of $1$ and $2$ is $1.5$. This is why we introduce $\mathbb{Z}$, in which subtraction always has a result, and $\mathbb{Q}$, in which division by a nonzero number always has a result.
