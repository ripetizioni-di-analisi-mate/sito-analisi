The price of a product increases by $60\%$. What percent discount brings it back to its initial price?

- $37.5\%$
- $60\%$
- $40\%$
- $62.5\%$

## Explanation

After the increase, the price is the initial price times $1.6$. To return to the initial price, multiply by $\dfrac{1}{1.6} = 0.625$. This means paying $62.5\%$ of the higher price, so the discount is $100\% - 62.5\% = 37.5\%$. A price of $100$ euros rises to $160$ euros, and a discount of $60$ euros on $160$ euros corresponds to $\dfrac{60}{160} = 37.5\%$. A $60\%$ discount would bring the price to $160 \cdot 0.4 = 64$ euros because it applies to the higher price.
