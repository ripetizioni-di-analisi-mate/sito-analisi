A mechanical part is accepted if its length $\ell$, in millimeters, satisfies $|\ell - 25| \leq 0.2$. At which of the following lengths would the part be rejected?

- $25.2$ mm
- $24.85$ mm
- $24.75$ mm
- $25.15$ mm

## Explanation

The condition says that the length differs from $25$ mm by at most $0.2$ mm, that is, $24.8 \leq \ell \leq 25.2$. A length of $24.75$ mm differs from $25$ mm by $0.25$ mm, which exceeds $0.2$ mm, so the part **is rejected**. A part measuring $25.2$ mm is accepted because equality is allowed, and $24.85$ mm and $25.15$ mm also lie in the interval.
