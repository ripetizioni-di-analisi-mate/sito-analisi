Of $60$ households, $15$ have only a dog, $10$ have only a cat, and $25$ have neither a dog nor a cat. How many households have a dog?

- $25$
- $15$
- $10$
- $35$

## Explanation

The counts in the four regions of the Venn diagram add up to $60$, so $60 - 15 - 10 - 25 = 10$ households have both a dog and a cat. The households with a dog are those with only a dog plus those with both animals, for a total of $15 + 10 = 25$. The value $15$ counts only households with a dog and no cat, and $10$ counts households with both animals.
