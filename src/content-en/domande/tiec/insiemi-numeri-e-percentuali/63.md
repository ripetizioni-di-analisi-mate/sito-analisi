At a company with $80$ employees, $30$ are college graduates and $6$ are executives. All the executives are college graduates. How many employees are college graduates but not executives?

- $36$
- $24$
- $30$
- $50$

## Explanation

Since every executive is a college graduate, the set $D$ of executives is a **subset** of the set $L$ of graduates. In the Venn diagram, the circle for $D$ lies inside the circle for $L$. Graduates who are not executives form the difference $L \setminus D$, which contains $30 - 6 = 24$ elements. The value $36 = 30 + 6$ treats the two sets as disjoint, and $50$ counts employees who are not college graduates.
