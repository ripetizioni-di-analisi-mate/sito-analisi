A principal of $2500$ euros is invested at an annual simple interest rate of $3\%$. How much interest accrues in $2$ years?

- $75$ euros
- $2650$ euros
- $150$ euros
- $152.25$ euros

## Explanation

With **simple interest**, each year's interest is calculated on the original principal. In one year the interest is $3\%$ of $2500$ euros, or $75$ euros, so the interest in $2$ years is $2 \cdot 75 = 150$ euros. The result of $152.25$ euros includes interest earned in the second year on the first year's interest, as with compound interest. The value $2650$ euros is the accumulated amount, the sum of principal and interest.
