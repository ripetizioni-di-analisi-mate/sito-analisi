Which condition is necessary and sufficient for $|x| = -x$?

- $x \leq 0$
- $x < 0$
- $x \geq 0$
- $x = 0$

## Explanation

By the definition of **absolute value**, $|x| = -x$ when $x < 0$, and for $x = 0$ we have $|0| = 0 = -0$. For $x > 0$, however, $|x| = x$, which differs from $-x$. Hence the equality holds exactly when $x \leq 0$. The condition $x < 0$ excludes $0$, for which the equality is true, and $x \geq 0$ describes the numbers for which $|x| = x$ holds.
