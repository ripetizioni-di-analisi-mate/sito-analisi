A household spends $35\%$ of its monthly income on rent and $25\%$ on food. Its other expenses total $1200$ euros and account for all its remaining income. What is its monthly income?

- $1920$ euros
- $3000$ euros
- $4800$ euros
- $2000$ euros

## Explanation

Rent and food account for $35\% + 25\% = 60\%$ of the income, so other expenses account for $40\%$. If $1200$ euros is $40\%$ of the income $N$, then $N = \dfrac{1200}{0.4} = 3000$ euros. As a check, $35\%$ of $3000$ is $1050$, $25\%$ is $750$, and $1050 + 750 + 1200 = 3000$. The value $1920$ euros increases $1200$ by $60\%$, calculating the percentage on the part instead of the whole, and $2000$ euros treats $1200$ euros as $60\%$ of the income.
