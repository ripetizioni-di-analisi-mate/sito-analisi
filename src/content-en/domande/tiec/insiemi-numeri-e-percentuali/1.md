In a sample of customers at an online store, $40$ paid by credit card at least once, $25$ used a payment app at least once, and $10$ used both methods. How many customers in the sample used at least one of the two methods?

- $55$
- $65$
- $45$
- $75$

## Explanation

Let $C$ and $P$ be the sets of customers who paid by card and by app, respectively. Customers who used at least one method form the union $C \cup P$. By the formula for the **number of elements in a union**, there are $40 + 25 - 10 = 55$ such customers. Subtract the $10$ customers in the intersection once because the sum $40 + 25$ counts them twice. The value $65$ is the sum alone, which works only for disjoint sets, whereas $45$ subtracts the intersection twice.
