What is the product $0.\overline{3} \cdot 0.\overline{6}$?

- $0.\overline{18}$
- $0.\overline{2}$
- $0.18$
- $0.2$

## Explanation

Writing the decimals in their **fractional forms** gives $0.\overline{3} = \dfrac{1}{3}$ and $0.\overline{6} = \dfrac{2}{3}$, so their product is $\dfrac{1}{3} \cdot \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{2}{9}$. The decimal expansion of the fraction $\dfrac{2}{9}$ is $0.222\ldots = 0.\overline{2}$. The number $0.18$ is the product of the truncated decimals $0.3 \cdot 0.6$, and $0.\overline{18}$ repeats the same mistake and also marks the digits as repeating.
