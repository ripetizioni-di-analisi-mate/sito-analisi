Which of $-\dfrac{3}{4}$, $-\dfrac{2}{3}$, $-\dfrac{4}{5}$, and $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ is smallest?

- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

Of two negative numbers, the one with the greater absolute value is smaller because it lies farther to the left on the real number line. The absolute values are $0.75$, approximately $0.667$, $0.8$, and $0.5$. The greatest is $0.8 = \dfrac{4}{5}$, so the smallest number is $-\dfrac{4}{5}$. The answer $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ compares the absolute values without reversing the order.
