What is the fraction $\dfrac{2^3 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5}{2 \cdot 3^3 \cdot 7}$ in lowest terms?

- $\dfrac{10}{21}$
- $\dfrac{20}{63}$
- $\dfrac{5}{21}$
- $\dfrac{20}{21}$

## Explanation

Cancel the prime factors common to the numerator and denominator. Canceling the denominator's factor $2$ against $2^3$ leaves $2^2$ in the numerator. Canceling the numerator's factor $3^2$ against $3^3$ leaves $3$ in the denominator. The factors $5$ and $7$ have nothing to cancel against, so the result is $\dfrac{2^2 \cdot 5}{3 \cdot 7} = \dfrac{20}{21}$. The fraction $\dfrac{10}{21}$ cancels one factor of $2$ too many, and $\dfrac{20}{63}$ leaves $3^2$ in the denominator instead of $3$.
