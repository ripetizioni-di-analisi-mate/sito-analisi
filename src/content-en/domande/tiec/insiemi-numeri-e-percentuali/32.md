Which of the following pairs consists of two disjoint sets?

- The even natural numbers and the natural numbers that are multiples of $5$
- The prime numbers and the even natural numbers
- The natural numbers that are multiples of $4$ and the natural numbers that leave remainder $2$ when divided by $4$
- The positive divisors of $12$ and the positive divisors of $35$

## Explanation

Two sets are **disjoint** if their intersection is empty. A multiple of $4$ divided by $4$ leaves remainder $0$, so no multiple of $4$ leaves remainder $2$, and the two sets are disjoint. Each of the other pairs has at least one common element. The number $10$ is even and a multiple of $5$, the number $2$ is both prime and even, and $1$ divides both $12$ and $35$.
