Two natural numbers have product $1728$ and greatest common divisor $12$. What is their least common multiple?

- $72$
- $144$
- $1716$
- $20\,736$

## Explanation

For two natural numbers, $\mathrm{MCD}(a, b) \cdot \mathrm{mcm}(a, b) = a \cdot b$ holds, so $\mathrm{mcm}(a, b) = \dfrac{1728}{12} = 144$. For example, $36$ and $48$ have product $1728$, GCD $12$, and LCM $144$. The value $20\,736 = 1728 \cdot 12$ multiplies instead of dividing, and $1716$ subtracts the GCD from the product.
