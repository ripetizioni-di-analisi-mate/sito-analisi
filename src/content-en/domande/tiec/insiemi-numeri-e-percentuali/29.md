At a school, let $S$ be the set of students who play a sport and $M$ the set of students who play an instrument. Which set consists of the students who play neither a sport nor an instrument?

- $\overline{S} \cup \overline{M}$
- $S \setminus M$
- $\overline{S \cup M}$
- $\overline{S} \cap M$

## Explanation

The students in question belong neither to $S$ nor to $M$, so they belong to $\overline{S} \cap \overline{M}$. The rule for the **complement of a union** gives $\overline{S} \cap \overline{M} = \overline{S \cup M}$, the set of students outside both circles of the Venn diagram. The set $\overline{S} \cup \overline{M}$ is the complement of $S \cap M$ and also includes students who do just one of the activities. The set $\overline{S} \cap M$ consists of those who play an instrument but do not play a sport.
