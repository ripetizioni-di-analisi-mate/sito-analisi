What percentage of $180$ is $45$?

- $4\%$
- $25\%$
- $40\%$
- $75\%$

## Explanation

Divide the part by the total and multiply by $100$ to obtain $\dfrac{45}{180} \cdot 100 = 25$. Therefore $45$ is $25\%$ of $180$. The same result follows from observing that $180 = 4 \cdot 45$, so $45$ is one-quarter of $180$. The value $4\%$ comes from the inverted ratio $\dfrac{180}{45} = 4$, and $75\%$ is the percentage still needed to reach $180$.
