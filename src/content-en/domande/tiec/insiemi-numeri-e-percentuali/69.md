A class has $12$ boys and $18$ girls. The number of girls is what percentage of the number of boys?

- $60\%$
- $150\%$
- approximately $66.7\%$
- $50\%$

## Explanation

The reference value is the number of boys, so divide the number of girls by $12$ to obtain $\dfrac{18}{12} \cdot 100 = 150\%$. A percentage above $100\%$ means the quantity exceeds the reference value. The value $60\%$ is the percentage of girls among all $30$ students, and approximately $66.7\%$ comes from the inverted ratio $\dfrac{12}{18}$. The value $50\%$ gives the excess of girls over boys relative to the number of boys, or $\dfrac{6}{12}$.
