Which of the following comparisons of $4^{15}$, $8^{9}$, and $16^{7}$ is correct?

- $8^{9} < 16^{7} < 4^{15}$
- $4^{15} < 8^{9} < 16^{7}$
- $16^{7} < 8^{9} < 4^{15}$
- $8^{9} < 4^{15} < 16^{7}$

## Explanation

The three bases are powers of $2$, so the numbers can be written as powers of $2$, giving $4^{15} = 2^{30}$, $8^{9} = 2^{27}$, and $16^{7} = 2^{28}$. Each additional factor of $2$ doubles the number, so of two powers of $2$ the one with the greater exponent is greater. Therefore the order is $8^{9} < 16^{7} < 4^{15}$. The relation $4^{15} < 8^{9} < 16^{7}$ orders the numbers by their bases alone and ignores the exponents.
