What is the smallest natural number greater than $1$ that leaves remainder $1$ when divided by each of $4$, $6$, and $9$?

- $37$
- $36$
- $217$
- $25$

## Explanation

If $n$ leaves remainder $1$ in all three divisions, then $n - 1$ is divisible by $4$, $6$, and $9$, making it a common multiple of all three. Since $n > 1$, the smallest possible value of $n - 1$ is $\mathrm{mcm}(4, 6, 9) = 2^2 \cdot 3^2 = 36$, so $n = 37$. The number $36$ is divisible by all three numbers and leaves remainder $0$. The number $217 = 4 \cdot 6 \cdot 9 + 1$ leaves remainder $1$ but is not the smallest, and $25$ divided by $9$ leaves remainder $7$.
