Which of the following numbers lies between $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\dfrac{3}{4}$?

- $0.65$
- $0.7$
- $0.76$
- $0.\overline{6}$

## Explanation

In decimal form, $\dfrac{2}{3} = 0.666\ldots$ and $\dfrac{3}{4} = 0.75$. The number $0.7$ satisfies $0.666\ldots < 0.7 < 0.75$, so it lies between the two fractions. The number $0.\overline{6}$ equals $\dfrac{2}{3}$, one of the endpoints. The number $0.65$ is smaller than $\dfrac{2}{3}$, and $0.76$ is greater than $\dfrac{3}{4}$.
