On the real line a fraction strictly between $2/3$ and $3/4$ is needed, with positive denominator. What is the smallest possible denominator?

- $4$
- $7$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

For denominator $d$, an integer $n$ must satisfy $2d/3<n<3d/4$. For $d=1,2,3,4,5,6$ no integer is strictly between the bounds. For $d=7$, $n=5$ works since $14/3<5<21/4$.
