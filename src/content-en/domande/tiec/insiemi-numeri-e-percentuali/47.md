At a company, $50$ employees took at least one of two courses, a computer course and a language course. Of these employees, $32$ took the computer course and $12$ took both. How many employees took the language course?

- $18$
- $6$
- $44$
- $30$

## Explanation

The formula $|I \cup L| = |I| + |L| - |I \cap L|$ gives $|L| = |I \cup L| - |I| + |I \cap L|$, so $|L| = 50 - 32 + 12 = 30$. As a check, $20$ employees took only the computer course, $12$ took both, and $18$ took only the language course, and $20 + 12 + 18 = 50$. The value $18$ counts only those who took the language course without the computer course.
