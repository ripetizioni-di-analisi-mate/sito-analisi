In a group of $45$ people, $30$ have a smartphone and $25$ have a tablet. What is the smallest possible number of people who have both devices?

- $0$
- $25$
- $10$
- $5$

## Explanation

At most $45$ people have at least one device, so $|S \cup T| \leq 45$. The formula for the **number of elements in a union** gives $|S \cap T| = 30 + 25 - |S \cup T| \geq 55 - 45 = 10$. The value $10$ is attained when everyone has at least one device, with $20$ people having only a smartphone, $10$ both devices, and $15$ only a tablet. The value $0$ is impossible because $30 + 25 = 55$ exceeds the number of people, and $25$ is the *greatest* possible number who have both devices.
