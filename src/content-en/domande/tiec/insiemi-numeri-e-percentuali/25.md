Which inequality has the interval $[-1, 7]$ as its solution set?

- $|x + 3| \leq 4$
- $|x - 3| \leq 4$
- $|x - 4| \leq 3$
- $|x - 3| \geq 4$

## Explanation

The interval $[-1, 7]$ is centered at its midpoint $\dfrac{-1 + 7}{2} = 3$ and extends $\dfrac{7 - (-1)}{2} = 4$ units in either direction. It contains the numbers at most $4$ units from $3$, so it is the solution set of $|x - 3| \leq 4$. The solution set of $|x + 3| \leq 4$ is the interval $[-7, 1]$, whose center has the opposite sign. The inequality $|x - 4| \leq 3$ interchanges the midpoint and the half-width and has the interval $[1, 7]$ as its solution set.
