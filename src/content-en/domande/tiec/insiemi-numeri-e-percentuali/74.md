Which of the following equalities holds for every real number $a$?

- $|a| = a$
- $|a + 1| = |a| + 1$
- $|-a| = |a|$
- $|-a| = a$

## Explanation

The numbers $a$ and $-a$ are the same distance from $0$ on the real number line, so $|-a| = |a|$ for every $a$. The equality $|a| = a$ holds only for $a \geq 0$, and taking $a = -3$ gives $|a| = 3$. Taking $a = -3$ also shows that the other two equalities are false because $|a + 1| = 2$ whereas $|a| + 1 = 4$, and $|-a| = 3$ differs from $a = -3$.
