What is the twentieth digit after the decimal point in the decimal expansion of $\dfrac{3}{7}$?

- $4$
- $2$
- $8$
- $5$

## Explanation

Division gives $\dfrac{3}{7} = 0.\overline{428571}$, with a repeating block of $6$ digits. The digits repeat every $6$ positions, and $20 = 6 \cdot 3 + 2$, so the twentieth digit is the second digit of the repeating block, $2$. The value $4$ is the first digit of the block, and $8$ results from counting one position too far.
