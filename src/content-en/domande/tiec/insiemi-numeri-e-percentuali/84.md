A company has a $20\%$ market share. The following year its sales in euros stay the same while the total market grows by $25\%$. By how many percentage points does its share change?

- It falls by $5$ points
- It rises by $5$ points
- It stays the same
- It falls by $4$ points

## Explanation

Set the initial market size to $100$, giving the company sales of $20$. The new market size is $125$, so the share is $20/125=16\%$. Going from $20\%$ to $16\%$ is a drop of four percentage points.
