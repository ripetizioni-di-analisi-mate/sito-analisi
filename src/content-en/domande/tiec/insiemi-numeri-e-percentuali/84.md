The annual market for a product is worth $2.4$ million euros. A company's annual sales of the product are $360\,000$ euros. What is the company's market share?

- $1.5\%$
- approximately $6.7\%$
- $0.15\%$
- $15\%$

## Explanation

Market share is the ratio of the company's sales to the value of the market, expressed as a percentage. Since $2.4$ million euros is $2\,400\,000$ euros, we have $\dfrac{360\,000}{2\,400\,000} \cdot 100 = \dfrac{36}{240} \cdot 100 = 15\%$. The value $1.5\%$ is off by a factor of $10$ in converting from millions, and the inverted ratio $\dfrac{2\,400\,000}{360\,000} \approx 6.67$ gives approximately $6.7\%$.
