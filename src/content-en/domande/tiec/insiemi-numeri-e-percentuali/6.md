What are the solutions of the equation $|x - 5| = 3$?

- $x = 8$
- $x = -8 \;\vee\; x = -2$
- $x = -2 \;\vee\; x = 8$
- $x = 2 \;\vee\; x = 8$

## Explanation

The number $|x - 5|$ is the distance between $x$ and $5$ on the real number line, so the equation asks which numbers are $3$ units from $5$. They are $5 - 3 = 2$ and $5 + 3 = 8$. The definition of **absolute value** gives the same result because $x - 5 = 3$ or $x - 5 = -3$. Giving only $x = 8$ leaves out the negative case, whereas $x = -8 \;\vee\; x = -2$ corresponds to the distance from $-5$.
