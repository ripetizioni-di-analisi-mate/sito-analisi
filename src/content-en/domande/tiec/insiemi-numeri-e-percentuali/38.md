Which of the following sets is empty?

- $\{x \in \mathbb{N} : x < 1\}$
- $\{x \in \mathbb{Q} : 3 < x < 4\}$
- $\{x \in \mathbb{N} : 3 < x < 4\}$
- $\{x \in \mathbb{Z} : x^2 = 4\}$

## Explanation

There are no natural numbers strictly between $3$ and $4$ because $4$ is the next natural number after $3$. Thus $\{x \in \mathbb{N} : 3 < x < 4\}$ is the **empty set**. The set of natural numbers less than $1$ contains $0$ because $0 \in \mathbb{N}$. By contrast, there are infinitely many rational numbers between $3$ and $4$, such as $\dfrac{7}{2}$, and the equation $x^2 = 4$ has two integer solutions, $-2$ and $2$.
