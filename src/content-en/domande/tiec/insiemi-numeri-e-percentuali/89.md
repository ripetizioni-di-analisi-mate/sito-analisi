A company's revenue doubles compared with the preceding year. What is the percent increase in revenue?

- $200\%$
- $100\%$
- $50\%$
- $2\%$

## Explanation

If the initial revenue is $V$, the final revenue is $2V$, so the **percent change** is $\dfrac{2V - V}{V} \cdot 100 = 100\%$. The multiplier is $2 = 1 + \dfrac{100}{100}$, which also indicates an increase of $100\%$. The value $200\%$ treats the ratio of final to initial revenue as the change, and $50\%$ divides the increase by the final revenue.
