Which of the following numbers lies between $\sqrt{2}$ and $\sqrt{3}$?

- $1.5$
- $1.8$
- $1.4$
- $1.75$

## Explanation

The numbers to be compared are positive, and squaring positive numbers preserves their order. A positive number $a$ therefore lies between $\sqrt{2}$ and $\sqrt{3}$ if and only if $2 < a^2 < 3$. Since $1.5^2 = 2.25$, the number $1.5$ lies between the two roots. By comparison, $1.4^2 = 1.96$ is smaller than $2$, whereas $1.75^2 = 3.0625$ and $1.8^2 = 3.24$ are greater than $3$. The number $1.75$ exceeds $\sqrt{3} \approx 1.732$ by less than two hundredths.
