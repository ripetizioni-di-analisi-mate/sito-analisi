Two runners start together from the starting line of a track. The first takes $72$ seconds per lap and the second $90$ seconds. When they first return to the starting line together, how many laps has the faster runner completed?

- $4$
- $18$
- $360$
- $5$

## Explanation

The first runner crosses the starting line at multiples of $72$ seconds and the second at multiples of $90$ seconds, so they next meet there after $\mathrm{mcm}(72, 90)$ seconds. From $72 = 2^3 \cdot 3^2$ and $90 = 2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$ we obtain $\mathrm{mcm}(72, 90) = 2^3 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5 = 360$. In $360$ seconds, the faster runner completes $\dfrac{360}{72} = 5$ laps and the other completes $\dfrac{360}{90} = 4$ laps. The value $18$ is the GCD of the two lap times, and $360$ is the time in seconds.
