Which of the following numbers is irrational?

- $\sqrt{12}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{4}{9}}$
- $0.1\overline{2}$
- $\sqrt{0.16}$

## Explanation

A number is rational if it can be written as a fraction of two integers. Both $\sqrt{\dfrac{4}{9}} = \dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\sqrt{0.16} = 0.4 = \dfrac{2}{5}$ are rational. The number $0.1\overline{2}$ is also rational because every repeating decimal has a **fractional form**, in this case $\dfrac{11}{90}$. However, $\sqrt{12} = 2\sqrt{3}$ and $\sqrt{3}$ is irrational, so $\sqrt{12}$ is **irrational**.
