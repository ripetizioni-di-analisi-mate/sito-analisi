At a company, $60\%$ of the employees work in production and $40\%$ work in the offices. College graduates make up $10\%$ of production employees and $25\%$ of office employees. What percentage of all employees are college graduates?

- $16\%$
- $17.5\%$
- $35\%$
- $19\%$

## Explanation

Out of $100$ employees, $60$ work in production and $40$ in the offices. The number of graduates is $10\%$ of $60$, or $6$, plus $25\%$ of $40$, or $10$. That gives $16$ graduates out of $100$, or $16\%$. The value $17.5\%$ is the simple average of $10\%$ and $25\%$ and ignores the different sizes of the departments. The value $19\%$ interchanges the department sizes, and $35\%$ adds the two percentages.
