What is the decimal expansion of the fraction $\dfrac{7}{30}$?

- $0.\overline{23}$
- $0.23$
- $0.\overline{7}$
- $0.2\overline{3}$

## Explanation

The denominator $30 = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$ contains the factor $3$, and the fraction is in lowest terms, so the decimal expansion is **repeating**. Division gives $7 : 30 = 0.2333\ldots = 0.2\overline{3}$, with nonrepeating part $2$ and repeating block $3$. As a check, the **fractional form** of $0.2\overline{3}$ is $\dfrac{23 - 2}{90} = \dfrac{21}{90} = \dfrac{7}{30}$. The number $0.\overline{23}$ equals $\dfrac{23}{99}$, the terminating decimal $0.23$ cuts off the repeating digits, and $0.\overline{7}$ is $\dfrac{7}{9}$.
