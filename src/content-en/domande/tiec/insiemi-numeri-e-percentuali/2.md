At a company with $80$ employees, $35\%$ work remotely and the rest work on site. How many employees work on site?

- $28$
- $52$
- $45$
- $65$

## Explanation

Employees working on site make up $100\% - 35\% = 65\%$ of the total, so there are $0.65 \cdot 80 = 52$ of them. Alternatively, $35\%$ of $80$ is $28$, and $80 - 28 = 52$. The value $28$ counts those working remotely. The value $45$ subtracts $35$ employees instead of $35\%$, and $65$ treats the percentage working on site as a head count.
