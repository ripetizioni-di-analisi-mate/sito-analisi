A gym has $120$ members. Of these, $70$ take yoga, $50$ take Pilates, and $20$ take both classes. How many members take exactly one of the two classes?

- $100$
- $50$
- $120$
- $80$

## Explanation

Starting with the intersection, $70 - 20 = 50$ members take only yoga and $50 - 20 = 30$ take only Pilates. Therefore $50 + 30 = 80$ members take exactly one class. The value $100 = 70 + 50 - 20$ counts everyone taking at least one class, including the $20$ who take both. The value $50$ counts those taking only yoga.
