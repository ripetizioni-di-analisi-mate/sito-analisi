Which values satisfy the inequality $-3x > 12$?

- $x > -4$
- $x > 4$
- $x < -4$
- $x < 4$

## Explanation

Divide both sides by $-3$, which is equivalent to multiplying by the negative number $-\dfrac{1}{3}$. The direction of the inequality therefore reverses, giving $x < -4$. As a check, $x = -5$ gives $-3 \cdot (-5) = 15 > 12$. The answer $x > -4$ fails to reverse the inequality. The answers $x > 4$ and $x < 4$ overlook the negative sign of the coefficient $-3$.
