What is the greatest common divisor of $48$, $72$, and $120$?

- $12$
- $24$
- $8$
- $720$

## Explanation

The factorizations are $48 = 2^4 \cdot 3$, $72 = 2^3 \cdot 3^2$, and $120 = 2^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$. The **GCD** is the product of the factors common to all three numbers, each raised to the smallest exponent, giving $2^3 \cdot 3 = 24$. Both $12$ and $8$ divide all three numbers, but neither is their greatest common divisor, and $720$ is their LCM.
