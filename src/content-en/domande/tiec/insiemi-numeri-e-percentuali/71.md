How many integers from $1$ through $100$ are multiples of $4$ or $6$?

- $41$
- $37$
- $67$
- $33$

## Explanation

There are $25$ multiples of $4$ up to $100$ and $16$ multiples of $6$ because $6 \cdot 16 = 96$. A number is a multiple of both $4$ and $6$ when it is a multiple of $\mathrm{mcm}(4, 6) = 12$, and there are $8$ multiples of $12$ up to $100$. The formula for the **number of elements in a union** therefore gives $25 + 16 - 8 = 33$ integers. The value $37$ takes the multiples of $24 = 4 \cdot 6$ as the intersection, of which there are only $4$. The value $41$ does not subtract the intersection.
