Let $x$ and $y$ be real numbers with $x < y < 0$. Which of the following inequalities is true?

- $\dfrac{1}{x} < \dfrac{1}{y}$
- $-x < -y$
- $x^2 > y^2$
- $x \cdot y < 0$

## Explanation

Multiplying $x < y$ by $-1$ reverses the inequality, giving $-x > -y$, and $-y > 0$ because $y < 0$. Squaring positive numbers preserves their order, so $(-x)^2 > (-y)^2$, or $x^2 > y^2$. For example, $x = -3$ and $y = -1$ give $9 > 1$. The inequality $-x < -y$ fails to reverse the direction. For the same values, $\dfrac{1}{x} = -\dfrac{1}{3}$ is greater than $\dfrac{1}{y} = -1$, and the product $x \cdot y$ of two negative numbers is positive.
