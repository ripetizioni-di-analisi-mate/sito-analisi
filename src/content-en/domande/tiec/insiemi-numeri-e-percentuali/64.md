On the real number line, what is the distance between the points corresponding to $-3.5$ and $2.25$?

- $1.25$
- $-5.75$
- $4.75$
- $5.75$

## Explanation

The distance between two numbers $a$ and $b$ on the real number line is the **absolute value** of their difference, $|a - b|$. Here $|2.25 - (-3.5)| = |2.25 + 3.5| = 5.75$. The value $1.25$ subtracts $3.5$ instead of adding it, overlooking the negative sign of the first number. The value $-5.75$ is a signed difference, whereas distance is never negative.
