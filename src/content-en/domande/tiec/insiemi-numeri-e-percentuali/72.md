Which of the following comparisons of $0.\overline{5}$, $0.5\overline{4}$, and $0.55$ is correct?

- $0.55 < 0.5\overline{4} < 0.\overline{5}$
- $0.5\overline{4} < 0.55 < 0.\overline{5}$
- $0.5\overline{4} < 0.\overline{5} < 0.55$
- $0.\overline{5} < 0.55 < 0.5\overline{4}$

## Explanation

Writing a few more digits gives $0.5\overline{4} = 0.5444\ldots$, $0.55 = 0.5500$, and $0.\overline{5} = 0.5555\ldots$. Compare the digits from left to right. The first digit after the decimal point is $5$ in all three numbers, but the second is $4$ for $0.5\overline{4}$ and $5$ for the other two, so $0.5\overline{4}$ is the smallest. The third digit determines the order of $0.55$ and $0.\overline{5}$. The digits are $0$ and $5$, respectively, so $0.5\overline{4} < 0.55 < 0.\overline{5}$.
