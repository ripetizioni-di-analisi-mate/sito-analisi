Which of the following statements is true?

- $-\dfrac{8}{4} \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $\sqrt{9} \notin \mathbb{N}$
- $0.\overline{3} \notin \mathbb{Q}$
- $\pi \in \mathbb{Q}$

## Explanation

The number $-\dfrac{8}{4}$ equals $-2$, an **integer**, so $-\dfrac{8}{4} \in \mathbb{Z}$ is true. The other statements are false. In fact, $\sqrt{9} = 3$ is a natural number and $0.\overline{3} = \dfrac{1}{3}$ is rational because every repeating decimal has a fractional form. The number $\pi$ is irrational.
