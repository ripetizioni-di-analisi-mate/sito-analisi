Which values satisfy the inequality $|x| < |x - 4|$?

- $x > 2$
- $x < 4$
- $x < 2$
- $0 < x < 4$

## Explanation

The number $|x|$ is the distance from $x$ to $0$, and $|x - 4|$ is the distance from $x$ to $4$. Thus the inequality asks for the numbers closer to $0$ than to $4$. The point equidistant from $0$ and $4$ is their midpoint $2$, and the numbers closer to $0$ are those to the left of $2$, so the solution is $x < 2$. As a check, $x = -10$ gives $10 < 14$, whereas $x = 3$ gives $3 > 1$. The answer $0 < x < 4$ includes $x = 3$, which is not a solution, and excludes $x = -10$, which is.
