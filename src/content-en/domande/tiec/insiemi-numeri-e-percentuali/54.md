Let $A = (-2, 5]$ and $B = [3, 8)$. Which of the following is the set $A \setminus B$?

- $(-2, 3]$
- $[3, 5]$
- $(-2, 3)$
- $(5, 8)$

## Explanation

The set $A \setminus B$ contains the numbers in $A$ that are not in $B$. The numbers in $A$ that also belong to $B$ form the interval $[3, 5]$, so the remaining numbers satisfy $-2 < x < 3$. The number $3$ belongs to $B$ and therefore is excluded from the difference. The interval $[3, 5]$ is the intersection $A \cap B$, and the interval $(5, 8)$ is the difference $B \setminus A$.
