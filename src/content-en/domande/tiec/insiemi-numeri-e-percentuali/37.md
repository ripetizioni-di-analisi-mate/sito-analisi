Which value is closest to $2^{20}$?

- $10^{5}$
- $10^{7}$
- $10^{9}$
- $10^{6}$

## Explanation

Since $2^{10} = 1024$ is approximately $10^3$, we have $2^{20} = \left(2^{10}\right)^2 \approx \left(10^3\right)^2 = 10^6$. The exact value is $2^{20} = 1\,048\,576$, a little over one million. The value $10^{9}$ has the order of magnitude of $2^{30}$, and $10^{5}$ and $10^{7}$ differ from $2^{20}$ by almost one million and almost nine million, respectively.
