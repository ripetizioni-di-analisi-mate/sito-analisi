What is the sum $0.\overline{6} + 0.\overline{3}$?

- $0.9$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{99}{100}$
- $\dfrac{10}{9}$

## Explanation

The rule for the **fractional form** gives $0.\overline{6} = \dfrac{6}{9} = \dfrac{2}{3}$ and $0.\overline{3} = \dfrac{3}{9} = \dfrac{1}{3}$, so their sum is $\dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{1}{3} = 1$. Adding the digits gives $0.\overline{9}$, which equals $1$ because its fractional form is $\dfrac{9}{9}$. The value $0.9$ cuts off the repeating digits, and $\dfrac{99}{100}$ is the sum of the truncated decimals $0.66 + 0.33$.
