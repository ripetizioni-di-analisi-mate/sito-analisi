Which of $0.7$, $\dfrac{2}{3}$, $\dfrac{5}{7}$, and $0.69$ is greatest?

- $0.7$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}$
- $0.69$

## Explanation

To compare the four numbers, write them as decimals to three places, giving $\dfrac{2}{3} \approx 0.667$ and $\dfrac{5}{7} \approx 0.714$. The order is therefore $\dfrac{2}{3} < 0.69 < 0.7 < \dfrac{5}{7}$. The closest comparison is between $\dfrac{5}{7}$ and $0.7 = \dfrac{7}{10}$. The rule for **comparing fractions** gives $5 \cdot 10 = 50 > 7 \cdot 7 = 49$, so $\dfrac{5}{7} > \dfrac{7}{10}$.
