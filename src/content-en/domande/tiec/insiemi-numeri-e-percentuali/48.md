On the real number line, which number is the midpoint of the segment with endpoints $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{3}{4}$?

- $\dfrac{13}{12}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{13}{24}$

## Explanation

The midpoint between two numbers is their arithmetic mean, their sum divided by $2$. The sum is $\dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{4 + 9}{12} = \dfrac{13}{12}$, so the midpoint is $\dfrac{13}{24}$. The value $\dfrac{13}{12}$ omits division by $2$, whereas $\dfrac{4}{7}$ adds the numerators and the denominators. The value $\dfrac{5}{12}$ is the distance between the points.
