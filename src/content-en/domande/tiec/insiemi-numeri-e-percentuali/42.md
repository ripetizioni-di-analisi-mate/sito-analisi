If $-2 \leq x \leq 3$, which inequality does $1 - 2x$ satisfy?

- $-3 \leq 1 - 2x \leq 7$
- $-6 \leq 1 - 2x \leq 4$
- $-5 \leq 1 - 2x \leq 5$
- $-5 \leq 1 - 2x \leq 7$

## Explanation

Multiplying $-2 \leq x \leq 3$ by the negative number $-2$ reverses both inequalities and gives $4 \geq -2x \geq -6$, or $-6 \leq -2x \leq 4$. Adding $1$ to all three parts preserves the order and gives $-5 \leq 1 - 2x \leq 5$. As a check, the expression equals $5$ for $x = -2$ and equals $-5$ for $x = 3$. The chain from $-6$ to $4$ omits the addition of $1$, and the chain from $-3$ to $7$ corresponds to the expression $1 + 2x$.
