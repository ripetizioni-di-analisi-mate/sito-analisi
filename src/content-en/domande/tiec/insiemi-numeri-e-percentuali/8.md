The price of a service rises by $10\%$ and, the following year, by $20\%$. What is the overall percent increase relative to the initial price?

- $30\%$
- $32\%$
- $15\%$
- $3.2\%$

## Explanation

For **successive percent changes**, multiply the two multipliers, $1.1$ for the first increase and $1.2$ for the second. Their product is $1.1 \cdot 1.2 = 1.32$, so the final price is $132\%$ of the initial price and the overall increase is $32\%$. A price of $100$ euros rises to $110$ euros and then to $110 + 22 = 132$ euros. The sum $30\%$ overlooks the fact that the second increase applies to $110$ euros instead of $100$.
