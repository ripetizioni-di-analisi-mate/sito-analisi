The price of a product rises by $20\%$ and the quantity sold falls by $20\%$. What happens to revenue, the product of price and quantity sold?

- It stays the same.
- It decreases by $2\%$.
- It increases by $4\%$.
- It decreases by $4\%$.

## Explanation

The new price is the initial price times $1.2$, and the new quantity is the initial quantity times $0.8$. Revenue is the product of the two quantities, so it is multiplied by $1.2 \cdot 0.8 = 0.96$ and **decreases** by $4\%$. The two changes do not cancel out because the factors multiply and the percentages cannot be added. With a price of $10$ euros and $100$ units sold, revenue changes from $1000$ euros to $12 \cdot 80 = 960$ euros.
