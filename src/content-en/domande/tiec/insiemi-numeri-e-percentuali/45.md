A region's unemployment rate is $8\%$ and increases by $25\%$. What is the new unemployment rate?

- $10\%$
- $33\%$
- $8.25\%$
- $6\%$

## Explanation

The quantity that changes is the rate, initially $8$. Increasing it by $25\%$ means applying the **multiplier** $1.25$. The new rate is therefore $8 \cdot 1.25 = 10$, or $10\%$, an increase of $2$ percentage points. The value $33\%$ adds $25$ percentage points, confusing percent change with percentage points, and $8.25\%$ adds $0.25$ points. The value $6\%$ corresponds to a decrease of $25\%$.
