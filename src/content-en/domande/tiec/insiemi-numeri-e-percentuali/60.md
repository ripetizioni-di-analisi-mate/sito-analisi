For which value of the digit $a$ is the three-digit number $4a5$ divisible by $3$ but not by $9$?

- $1$
- $9$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The digit sum of $4a5$ is $9 + a$. By the **divisibility test for $3$**, the number is divisible by $3$ if $9 + a$ is a multiple of $3$. With $a = 1$ and $a = 4$, the sums $10$ and $13$ are not multiples. A number is divisible by $9$ if its digit sum is a multiple of $9$, so for $a = 9$ the number $495$ is also divisible by $9$. With $a = 3$ we obtain $435$, whose digit sum $12$ is divisible by $3$ but not by $9$.
