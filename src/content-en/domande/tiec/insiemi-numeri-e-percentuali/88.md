The household saving rate rises by $3$ percentage points to $15\%$. What is the percent increase in the rate?

- $3\%$
- $20\%$
- $18\%$
- $25\%$

## Explanation

The rate rose by $3$ **percentage points** to $15\%$, so its initial value was $12\%$. Calculate the **percent change** relative to the initial value to obtain $\dfrac{15 - 12}{12} \cdot 100 = 25\%$. The value $3\%$ confuses percentage points with percent change, and $20\%$ divides by the final value because $\dfrac{3}{15} = 0.2$.
