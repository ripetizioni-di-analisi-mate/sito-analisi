Of the members of an association, $40\%$ are women, and $30\%$ of the women are under age $25$. What percentage of all members are women under age $25$?

- $12\%$
- $70\%$
- $10\%$
- $30\%$

## Explanation

Women under age $25$ make up $30\%$ of the $40\%$ of members who are women, so multiply the two percentages in decimal form to obtain $0.3 \cdot 0.4 = 0.12$, or $12\%$. Out of $100$ members, $40$ are women, and $30\%$ of $40$ is $12$. The value $70\%$ adds percentages that are based on different totals. The value $30\%$ uses the women alone as its reference group.
