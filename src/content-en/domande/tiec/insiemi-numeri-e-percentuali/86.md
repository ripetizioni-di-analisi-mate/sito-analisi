The price of an item is increased by $20\%$ and then discounted by $10\%$, giving a final price of $54$ euros. What was the initial price?

- $45$ euros
- $50$ euros
- $48.60$ euros
- $60$ euros

## Explanation

The two changes correspond to the **multipliers** $1.2$ and $0.9$, so the final price is the initial price times $1.2 \cdot 0.9 = 1.08$. To find the initial price, divide to obtain $\dfrac{54}{1.08} = 50$ euros. As a check, $50$ euros increased by $20\%$ gives $60$ euros, and $60$ euros discounted by $10\%$ gives $54$ euros. The value $45$ euros undoes only the increase because it equals $\dfrac{54}{1.2}$, whereas $60$ euros undoes only the discount.
