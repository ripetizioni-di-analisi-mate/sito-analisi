> The school radio team records interviews on Tuesdays and broadcasts them on Fridays. One student proposed cutting every pause to leave more time for questions. During an edit they noticed that in one answer silence preceded a correction. The guest had said “always”, hesitated, then added “at least in the summer months”. In the edited recording the qualification sounded just as prearranged as the first word. The team decided to shorten only the delays caused by adjusting microphones. A brief hesitation may feel like wasted time to the editor, one member said, yet it may tell a listener how an answer developed.

Which pause would the team keep under its decision?

- The wait while a microphone cable is replaced
- The delay while the microphone is moved closer to the speaker
- The time needed to tighten the microphone stand
- The moment when a guest reconsiders a date before correcting it

## Explanation

The decision distinguishes hesitations that reveal thinking from technical delays. Correcting a date falls in the first group. All three other pauses concern work on a microphone.
