> For one week desk requests were recorded with a red cross if resolved immediately and a black cross if deferred. Halfway through the week the red pen ran out. The clerk continued to record immediately resolved cases with a black circle, always reserving the black cross for deferrals. At the end the supervisor counted only red crosses and announced that quick service had become less frequent in the final days. The clerk showed him the two sets of instructions at the top of the sheet, each with its own effective date. Interpreting the marks meant following the roles assigned to shape and colour each day.

Which marks should be added to find all requests resolved immediately?

- All crosses of both colours
- Black crosses and black circles
- Only marks made before the pen change
- Red crosses and black circles

## Explanation

Before the change, immediate service was marked with a red cross. After it, the same function passed to a black circle. Black crosses indicate deferrals in both parts of the week.
