> “The room seemed larger when the table was in place,” says a character just after the movers have emptied the hall. His companion smiles and looks at the tape measure resting against the bare wall. The narrator adds that they have just measured the width and found the same figure they wrote down the previous evening. Then comes a memory of their meals. The character could easily picture the distance he walked between table and door with a tray in his hands. Once the furniture had gone he lost that reference. In the dialogue the opening statement is true from the speaker's point of view.

Which word in the opening sentence makes the memory compatible with the measurement?

- “Place”
- “Table”
- “Room”
- “Seemed”

## Explanation

The tape measure shows that the width has not changed. The memory concerns the perception of space with a familiar reference. The verb “seemed” places the change in the character's impression.
