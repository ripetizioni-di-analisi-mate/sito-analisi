Read the following passage.

> At an engineering firm with three hundred employees, a third of the workforce will retire within ten years. Most are skilled tradespeople and technicians who know machines and procedures that are not always documented in manuals. To preserve this knowledge, the firm has paired employees nearing retirement with recently hired young employees. For two years, each new hire works alongside the more experienced coworker, asks questions, and records procedures in a shared archive.
>
> Older employees greeted the project with some distrust because they feared being sidelined. Management then explained that mentors would receive an allowance and work reduced hours in the last months before retirement. After that, more employees asked to participate.

According to the passage, how many employees of the engineering firm will retire within ten years?

- $100$
- $30$
- $200$
- $90$

## Explanation

The passage says the firm has three hundred employees and that a third of its workforce will retire within ten years. One third of $300$ is
$$
\frac{300}{3} = 100 .
$$
The value $200$ is the number of employees who will remain. The value $30$ takes one tenth of the workforce instead of one third, and $90$ interprets “a third” as $30\%$.
