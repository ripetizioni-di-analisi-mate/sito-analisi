> A caretaker found two identical umbrellas by the gym window. One was dry and the other was still dripping. Two pupils came to the office, each claiming one. The first said he recognised his own because he had walked to school in the rain. The second said he had left his in the gym the previous week. Before handing them over, the caretaker wanted to check one detail. Someone might have used one of the umbrellas during the morning to cross the courtyard. If so, how wet it was would tell her something about its last use, but not necessarily about the owner who had left it there.

Which distinction explains the caretaker's decision to check?

- The last user of an object may differ from its owner
- Someone who remembers a date always recognises their object
- The courtyard and gym are in separate buildings
- A new umbrella dries faster than an old one

## Explanation

Rain readily explains why a recently used umbrella is wet. It does not identify who brought it into the gym. The possibility of a loan or later use breaks that link.
