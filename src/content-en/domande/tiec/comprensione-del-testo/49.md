> Since the theatre began collecting programmes on the way out to reuse them, audiences have been able to leave them in two identical baskets. Each audience member receives a personal copy and may return only their own. In the first week the basket by the main entrance filled three times and the courtyard basket only once. The head usher did not immediately redistribute them. The courtyard had been open to the public only on Saturday, whereas the main entrance had been used on all four evenings. No volunteer had counted people using either door. She asked staff to watch one evening with both exits open and count both people passing and programmes returned at each door.

What would the proposed count allow staff to compare?

- Each individual audience member's preferred door
- The proportion of exiting people returning programmes at each door
- The number of programmes originally printed
- The quality of the shows on the four evenings

## Explanation

The count relates returns to the number of people passing through each exit. Observing the same evening reduces differences in opportunity between doors. Spectators are not asked which exit they prefer.
