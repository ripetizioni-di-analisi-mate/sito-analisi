> The school of music newsletter reports that several pupils chose the flute after hearing it played in a courtyard. The reporter writes, “For them it was a door.” A photograph of the music room entrance is lying on the editors' table, but the reporter asks them not to use it as their main illustration. The pupils in the interviews talk about first wanting to try the flute. Some already played another instrument, some had never entered the school. The reporter prefers a picture of the musician's hands. The entrance would suggest a physical passage shared by all, whereas the stories share an opportunity.

What does “a door” mean in the reporter's sentence?

- Giving up every instrument previously studied
- A necessary condition for joining the school
- The entrance all pupils used for their first lesson
- An opportunity that sparked a new interest

## Explanation

Hearing the flute sparks the desire to try it. The pupils' earlier experiences differ. What they share is the start of an interest rather than walking through the same doorway.
