> In a factory newsletter an apprentice interviews two colleagues. Asked what helps people learn, the first answers “a good example” and the second “someone who lets you make mistakes”. He titles his piece “Two incompatible methods”. His colleagues ask him to listen again to the end of the recordings. The first says she shows a procedure once and then hands the work to the learner. The second explains that she lets the pupil try after showing how to set up the bench. He changes the title because the opening answers highlighted different moments in practices that could follow the same sequence.

Which pair of actions is common to both accounts?

- Correct every movement before the learner proceeds
- Hand over all work without preparing the bench
- Show something first and then let the learner try
- Allow errors before showing anything at all

## Explanation

Both colleagues describe an initial demonstration. The learner is then given room to act. The difference in emphasis in their opening answers does not make the sequences incompatible.
