> On Saturday the shoe shop accepts repairs all morning. A notice says that soles are repaired on the premises and zips are sent to a nearby workshop every Monday. On-site repairs can be collected after two shop opening days. When work is sent elsewhere, the counter gives a collection date once it returns. On Saturday Alex hands in a boot with a broken zip and immediately counts two days on his calendar. His receipt, however, gives just his telephone number and a blank space beside “collection”. The shopkeeper points to the second part of the notice to explain why the space is still blank.

Under the notice, when will the counter communicate a collection date for the boot?

- When the external repair has come back to the shop
- Two calendar days after the boot was handed in
- When it is sent to the nearby workshop
- After two opening days, as for soles

## Explanation

The boot needs zip work, which is done elsewhere. The two-day rule applies to on-site work. For an external repair the notice ties notification to the item's return.
