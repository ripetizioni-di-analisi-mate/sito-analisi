Read the following passage.

> At an engineering firm with three hundred employees, a third of the workforce will retire within ten years. Most are skilled tradespeople and technicians who know machines and procedures that are not always documented in manuals. To preserve this knowledge, the firm has paired employees nearing retirement with recently hired young employees. For two years, each new hire works alongside the more experienced coworker, asks questions, and records procedures in a shared archive.
>
> Older employees greeted the project with some distrust because they feared being sidelined. Management then explained that mentors would receive an allowance and work reduced hours in the last months before retirement. After that, more employees asked to participate.

Which of the following statements is supported by the passage?

- All employees nearing retirement participate in the project
- After two years of working alongside the older employee, the young employee takes over that person’s position
- The project has reduced the number of retirements
- More employees asked to participate after management explained the benefits for mentors

## Explanation

The passage says management explained that mentors would receive an allowance and work reduced hours, and that “after that, more employees asked to participate.” The correct answer restates this sequence. The passage does not say all employees nearing retirement participate. In fact, it reports initial distrust, so the statement about all employees goes too far. The passage does not say that the younger employee takes over the coworker’s position or that retirements have decreased.
