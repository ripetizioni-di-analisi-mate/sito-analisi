Read the following passage.

> On summer evenings, some squares in the historic center fill with young people drinking and talking outside bars until late at night. For bar owners, these are the most profitable hours of the week. For people living above the bars, they are hours of lost sleep.
>
> Last year, the city ordered the bars to close at one in the morning. Noise in the square decreased, but some young people moved into side streets, where they stayed with drinks bought at all-night stores. This year, the city government changed its approach. Bars may stay open until two, but they must pay for two attendants who ask customers not to linger in the street. After midnight, alcohol sales for takeout are prohibited.

Which statement accurately describes how this year’s rule differs from last year’s?

- Bars close an hour earlier and do not have to pay for attendants
- Bars close an hour later and the all-night stores have been closed
- Bars close at the same time, but after midnight they cannot serve alcohol at tables
- Bars close an hour later, but they must pay for two attendants, and alcohol cannot be sold for takeout after midnight

## Explanation

Last year the bars closed at one. This year they may stay open until two, so they close an hour later. The passage adds two conditions. The bars pay for attendants, and alcohol may not be sold for takeout after midnight. The correct answer combines all three elements. Closing an hour earlier reverses the comparison. The passage does not say the all-night stores have closed, and the prohibition concerns takeout sales, not table service.
