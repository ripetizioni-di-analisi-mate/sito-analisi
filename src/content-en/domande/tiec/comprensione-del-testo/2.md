Read the following passage.

> Some supermarkets have installed refill stations for laundry detergent. Customers bring an empty bottle from home, fill it, and pay by weight. Detergent from a refill station costs less per liter than packaged detergent because the customer does not pay for the bottle or label. Yet sales remain low.
>
> Store managers point to two obstacles. The first is practical. Customers have to remember to bring a bottle, and people who shop on their way home from work rarely have one with them. The second has to do with habit. Many customers choose a familiar brand of detergent, and the refill stations carry few brands. One supermarket tried selling a reusable bottle next to the refill station for $50$ cents. Customers had made $300$ refills in the preceding two months. In the following two months, that number rose by a third.

Which title best fits the passage?

- Detergent refills cost less, but few shoppers choose them
- How to reduce plastic use at home
- A fifty-cent bottle
- Detergent refills cost more than packaged detergent

## Explanation

The passage says that detergent from a refill station costs less per liter, explains why sales remain low, and describes an attempt to increase them. The title about lower prices and the small number of shoppers who choose refills covers all three points and is therefore the best fit. The title about plastic use at home is **too broad**, while the one about the fifty-cent bottle is **too narrow** because it covers only the experiment at the end. The title about the higher price reverses what the passage says. The price per liter is lower.
