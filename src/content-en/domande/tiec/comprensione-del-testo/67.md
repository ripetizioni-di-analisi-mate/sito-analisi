Read the following passage.

> A small coffee roaster in a provincial town needed $40\,000$ euros to buy a larger machine. The bank offered a loan, but the owners felt the interest rate was too high. Instead, they launched an online fundraiser among their customers. Anyone who contributed $50$ euros would receive a kilogram of coffee a month for a year. Anyone who contributed $200$ would also get a guided tour of the roasting facility. In six weeks, the fundraiser surpassed its target, raising $46\,000$ euros from about six hundred supporters.
>
> The owners acknowledge that raising money this way comes at a cost because the coffee they promised has to be produced and shipped. However, the campaign also brought something a loan could not. Many supporters told friends and coworkers about the roaster, and orders through its website increased.

It can be inferred from the passage that:

- the coffee roaster has given up bank loans for good
- some supporters contributed more than $50$ euros
- orders through the website increased because the coffee improved
- the new machine has already been purchased and is in operation

## Explanation

The campaign raised $46\,000$ euros from about six hundred supporters, so the average contribution was
$$
\frac{46\,000}{600} \approx 77 \text{ euros} .
$$
If everyone had contributed at most $50$ euros, the average would not exceed $50$, so someone contributed more. The inference is **supported**. The passage attributes the increase in orders to word of mouth from supporters, not to the quality of the coffee. The text says nothing about the purchase of the machine or future loans.
