Read the following passage.

> Some supermarkets have installed refill stations for laundry detergent. Customers bring an empty bottle from home, fill it, and pay by weight. Detergent from a refill station costs less per liter than packaged detergent because the customer does not pay for the bottle or label. Yet sales remain low.
>
> Store managers point to two obstacles. The first is practical. Customers have to remember to bring a bottle, and people who shop on their way home from work rarely have one with them. The second has to do with habit. Many customers choose a familiar brand of detergent, and the refill stations carry few brands. One supermarket tried selling a reusable bottle next to the refill station for $50$ cents. Customers had made $300$ refills in the preceding two months. In the following two months, that number rose by a third.

Which of the following statements about customers accurately reflects the passage?

- Most customers choose detergent on the basis of the brand
- For many customers, a familiar brand matters when choosing detergent
- People who shop on their way home from work never have a bottle with them
- Customers buy little detergent from refill stations because they think it is lower in quality

## Explanation

The second paragraph says that “many customers choose a familiar brand of detergent,” so the statement about a familiar brand mattering to many customers restates that sentence. The statement about most customers replaces *many* with a larger share that the passage does not specify. Similarly, the passage says that people on their way home from work *rarely* have a bottle with them, so *never* makes the claim stronger than the text allows. The passage says nothing about the quality of the detergent at refill stations. The obstacles it identifies are having to bring a bottle and the limited choice of brands.
