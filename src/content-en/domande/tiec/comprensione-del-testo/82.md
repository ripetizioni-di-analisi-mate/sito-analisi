Read the following passage.

> Each year, some of the fruit and vegetables harvested never reaches supermarket shelves because the produce is irregularly shaped, has blemished skin, or falls outside the required size range. The taste and nutritional properties are the same, but the produce is either sold to juice manufacturers at a very low price or left in the fields.
>
> One farm began selling this fruit directly to consumers in mixed boxes delivered every week, at a $30\%$ discount from the price of its top-grade fruit. At first, the farm worried that customers would see it as lower in quality. The opposite happened. Many subscribers particularly enjoy the surprise of receiving different produce each week. They say they waste less because the box encourages them to cook what they have at home.

In the passage, the sentence “The opposite happened” means that

- the farm stopped delivering boxes each week
- customers asked to receive top-grade fruit only
- customers did not think the fruit was lower in quality and appreciated some of its features
- customers wasted more fruit than before

## Explanation

The preceding sentence reports the farm’s fear that customers would see the fruit as lower in quality. The sentence “The opposite happened” says that this fear did not come true. The next sentence confirms it, explaining that many subscribers appreciate the variety of the produce and waste less. The sentence therefore marks a **contrast** between what the farm feared and what happened. The other three statements describe the very outcomes the farm feared or events the passage does not report.
