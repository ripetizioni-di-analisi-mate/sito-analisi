Read the following passage.

> In many villages of a few hundred residents, the last grocery store has closed, and people must drive to buy bread. In one mountain town, the owner of the last remaining general store decided to hold out by adding services. In addition to buying groceries, customers can now pick up packages ordered online, pay bills, add credit to their phones, and drop off prescriptions for medicines that a pharmacy in the valley delivers the next day.
>
> None of these services brings in much money on its own. Together, however, they bring in people who stop by for a package and leave with bread and a newspaper. The owner says revenue is back to where it was ten years ago. The town has also lowered the rent on her store, which occupies a publicly owned building.

Which statement best summarizes the passage?

- All the grocery stores in mountain towns have closed
- A pharmacy in the valley delivers medicines the next day
- A mountain general store has stayed open by adding services that bring in customers who also buy groceries
- The added services are the store’s main source of income

## Explanation

The passage describes how the only general store in a mountain town added services and explains that, taken together, they attract people who also buy bread and a newspaper. The correct answer covers both paragraphs. The claim about all stores is an exaggeration because the store described is open. The delivery of medicines is **too narrow** a detail. Finally, the passage says that no service brings in much money on its own. There is no line to support the claim that the services are the main source of income.
