> For an outdoor reading the theatre group had one microphone and a music stand. The leaflet invited everyone to sit in a circle “so every voice has equal space”. At rehearsal, however, the microphone stayed in front of the reader opening the evening. A child observed that the chairs were arranged as promised but from the far chair he could hear just one person. The organisers began passing the microphone from hand to hand. In their diary they said they had had to take “space” seriously twice, first as the placement of chairs and then as the opportunity to be heard.

Which action fulfils the second meaning of “space”?

- Arranging the chairs in a circle
- Passing the microphone to whoever speaks
- Putting the stand in front of the first reader
- Leaving the furthest chair empty

## Explanation

The second meaning concerns actually being heard. Arranging the seats addresses only the physical aspect. Sharing the microphone allows other voices to reach the more distant listeners too.
