Read the following passage.

> Opening a franchise store means using an established chain’s name, furnishings, and products in return for an initial fee and a percentage of sales. For someone starting a business, the advantage is clear. Customers recognize the sign right away, and the chain provides training, access to suppliers, and advertising campaigns.
>
> The store owner, however, remains an independent business owner who pays rent, hires staff, and bears the losses if business goes badly. The owner must also follow the chain’s detailed rules on prices, store layout, and often opening hours. Someone choosing this arrangement therefore gives up some freedom in return for less risk at the outset. Before signing, people with experience in the industry suggest talking with other store owners in the same chain and checking the length of the contract carefully.

Which statement expresses the passage’s conclusion about franchising?

- People who open a franchise give up some independence in return for less risk at the outset
- Franchising eliminates business risk
- Franchising pays off only for people who already have retail experience
- Before signing, it is worth checking the length of the contract

## Explanation

The conclusion is introduced by *therefore* in the sentence “Someone choosing this arrangement therefore gives up some freedom in return for less risk at the outset,” which sums up the previously described advantages and constraints. The correct answer restates it. The passage speaks of *less* risk, and the store owner still bears any losses, so risk is not eliminated. The store owner’s experience is not mentioned. The suggestion about the contract is a final detail, **too narrow** to be the conclusion.
