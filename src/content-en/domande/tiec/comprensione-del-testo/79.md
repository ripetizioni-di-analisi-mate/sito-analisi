Read the following passage.

> Bread is one of our largest unnoticed sources of salt. We eat it every day, and it does not taste particularly salty. In one province, bakers agreed to gradually reduce the salt in their dough by $5\%$ every six months for three years. They deliberately chose small reductions because customers do not notice a slow change and grow accustomed to the new taste without realizing it.
>
> At the end of that period, most participants in a blind taste test could not distinguish the new bread from the old. Doctors in the province point out that reducing salt helps keep blood pressure under control and that bread is just one source of salt. Cured meats, cheeses, and prepared foods also contribute.

Which of the following statements is NOT supported by the passage?

- Most people in the taste test could not distinguish the two breads
- Cured meats and cheeses also contribute to salt intake
- Bread with less salt stays fresh for fewer days
- The reductions were gradual so that customers would not notice the change

## Explanation

The passage does not discuss how long bread keeps, so the statement about the number of days it stays fresh has no support, even if the reader finds it plausible. It is a **true but unstated** distractor. The other three statements restate sentences from the text. The taste test is described at the beginning of the second paragraph, cured meats and cheeses appear in the last sentence, and the reason for the gradual reduction appears in the first paragraph, where “customers do not notice a slow change.”
