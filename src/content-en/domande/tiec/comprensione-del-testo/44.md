Read the following passage.

> In a flat farming region with little summer rainfall, many farms still use furrow irrigation. Water flows through furrows between the rows of plants, and a substantial share evaporates or seeps too deep into the soil for the roots to reach. Drip irrigation, by contrast, delivers water through perforated tubing directly to the base of each plant.
>
> A tomato grower compared the two methods on neighboring fields of equal size. In one season, the grower used $5000$ cubic meters of water on the field with furrow irrigation and $40\%$ less on the field with drip irrigation. Although it received less water, the drip-irrigated field produced nearly the same yield. A drip system, however, is expensive to install and requires maintenance because mineral deposits can clog the holes. For this reason, many farms adopt it only when they receive a government subsidy toward the purchase.

Which of the following sentences means the same as “Although it received less water, the drip-irrigated field produced nearly the same yield”?

- The drip-irrigated field received less water and therefore produced nearly the same yield
- The drip-irrigated field received less water. However, it produced nearly the same yield
- The drip-irrigated field produced nearly the same yield because it received less water
- The drip-irrigated field produced nearly the same yield even though it received more water

## Explanation

The clause introduced by *although* expresses a **concession**. A field that receives less water would be expected to produce a smaller yield, yet the main clause says the yield was nearly the same. The connective *however*, used with the indicative in the Italian original, expresses the same contrast between the two facts, so the sentence with *however* has the same meaning. The sentences with *therefore* and *because* present the smaller amount of water as the cause of the yield, turning a concession into a causal relationship. The sentence with *even though* still expresses a concession, but it changes the field that received less water to one that received more.
