> The pool caretaker announces that on Thursday the small pool will close one hour early “to allow the lighting to be tested”. An instructor concludes that nobody will be able to swim in the whole facility that evening. In a message to parents he therefore replaces “small pool” with “swimming centre”. A colleague reminds him that the older group uses the long pool and that closure notices name both pools separately when both are affected. He restores the original wording and adds only his own class time. He has no information supporting either a closure of the other pool or a general change to the weekly timetable.

Which alteration introduced the error into the forwarded message?

- Describing the test as the reason for the closure
- Adding the time of the instructor's own class
- Replacing an exact date with a day of the week
- Applying a statement about one part to the entire facility

## Explanation

The caretaker specifies one pool. The instructor's broader term also includes the long pool without support. The class time is added after the correction and concerns a separate detail.
