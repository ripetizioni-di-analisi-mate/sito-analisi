Read the following passage.

> Some companies that receive many applications have introduced recorded video interviews. Applicants receive a link by email and answer five questions in front of their computer cameras. The questions are the same for everyone, and applicants have two minutes for each answer. Recruiters watch the recordings at their convenience and invite only some applicants to an in-person interview.
>
> This saves the company considerable time. Applicants’ opinions are more divided. Those who live far away avoid a trip, but many find it unnatural to speak to a screen without seeing a listener’s reaction. Some companies let applicants record an answer a second time, while others do not. One recruiter notes that, in the first few weeks, applicants who had practiced with their phones at home appeared more at ease than the others.

According to the passage, how much time do applicants have for each answer in the video interview?

- Five minutes
- Ten minutes
- As much time as they wish
- Two minutes

## Explanation

The first paragraph says that applicants answer five questions and “have two minutes for each answer.” The correct answer restates this detail. Five is the number of questions, and ten minutes is the total time for all five answers. It is the recruiter who watches recordings at a convenient time. The applicant’s time is fixed.
