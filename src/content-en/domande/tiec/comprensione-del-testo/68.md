> The last line of a story says that a postal worker “took the longer route to arrive sooner”. A pupil believes he has found a contradiction. The preceding page explains that on the low bridge along the shorter route a gate opens only on the half hour. The postal worker sees it close from a distance. She knows that the hill route, though longer, will take less time than waiting at the gate. The teacher invites the pupil to use different colours to underline words about the length of a route and words about the duration of the whole journey.

Which fact makes the outcome in the final sentence possible?

- The shorter route involves a long wait at that particular time
- The postal worker's speed always doubles on the hill
- The gate closes the low bridge permanently
- The distance changes on the half hour

## Explanation

Length and duration are different measures. The shorter route currently includes waiting time. The detour can therefore involve a greater distance and still produce an earlier arrival.
