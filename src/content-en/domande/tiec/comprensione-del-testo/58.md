> A small firm produces three copies of each quotation, one for its customer, one for the archive and one for the workshop. The workshop copy gets a blue stamp when the customer accepts the offer. One morning a clerk found an unstamped copy beside a machine that was already on. A colleague assumed the job was urgent. She phoned the office first and discovered that the machine was on for routine maintenance. In her evening report she wrote, “the machine was on, yet authorisation was missing”. She was not saying that running a machine was irregular. She was recording the expectation that had made it easy to mistake equipment being ready for an accepted order.

Under the procedure, which detail records acceptance by the customer?

- The machine already running at the start of the shift
- The copy lying beside the equipment
- The colleague's assessment of urgency
- The blue stamp on the workshop copy

## Explanation

The procedure assigns the stamp a specific function. The location of the copy and the running machine were both compatible with maintenance. The colleague's view was based on these clues and does not record acceptance.
