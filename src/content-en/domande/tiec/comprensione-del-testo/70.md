Read the following passage.

> The municipal museum in a provincial city houses paintings and objects from local history. It used to receive a few thousand visitors a year, almost all school groups or tourists passing through. Two years ago, its directors decided to stay open until $23$\:00 (11 p.m.) on Thursdays, with reduced admission and, on some evenings, a short concert or a book talk in the galleries.
>
> Evening visitors differ considerably from those who come in the morning. Most are between twenty-five and forty and work in the city. Many had never been inside the museum before. Some critics feared that concerts would draw attention away from the art. In response, the directors arranged a half-hour guided tour of a single gallery before each event. Evening receipts covered the costs of the extended hours in the very first year.

According to the passage, who makes up most of the museum’s evening audience?

- School groups accompanied by teachers
- Tourists passing through
- People between twenty-five and forty who work in the city
- People who visit the museum every week

## Explanation

The second paragraph says that “most” evening visitors are between twenty-five and forty and work in the city. The correct answer restates this sentence. School groups and tourists passing through are the usual morning visitors mentioned in the first paragraph. In fact, the passage says many evening visitors had never been inside the museum before, so it does not describe them as regular visitors.
