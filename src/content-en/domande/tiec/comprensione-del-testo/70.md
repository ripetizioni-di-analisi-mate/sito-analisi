> A notice on the counter of a small shop says “last delivery of the day at five”. Clara arrives at ten to five to order a basket and is surprised when the assistant offers delivery the following morning. He shows her the sheet beside the telephone. It lists addresses already supplied and says that orders for the afternoon round must be in by three. Five is the time at which the van finishes its round rather than the last time to add an address. The owner decides to place the information previously shown only by the phone on the counter as well, so that both times are clear.

Which message most directly resolves Clara's misunderstanding?

- The van also operates in the afternoon
- There are no deliveries after five
- For an afternoon delivery, place your order by three
- Ask the assistant to order a basket

## Explanation

Clara already knows the final delivery time. What she lacks is the deadline for getting on the address list. The message explicitly linking afternoon delivery to the three o'clock order deadline supplies the missing fact.
