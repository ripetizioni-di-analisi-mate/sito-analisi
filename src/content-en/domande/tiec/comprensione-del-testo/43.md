> The mountain hostel accepts reservations until five. Beds that have not been claimed are allocated at six to people waiting outside. “Until five” is the deadline for asking for a bed, not for arriving. The booking confirmation gives a second rule: anyone with a reservation must arrive by six or contact the hostel before six with an expected arrival time. A hiker books at four and calls at half past five to say she will arrive at seven. The warden records her call. Another traveller waiting outside asks whether the reserved bed will automatically become available to him at six.

Which answer is consistent with both rules?

- The bed stays reserved only if the second traveller withdraws
- The bed is released because a call after five is always too late
- The bed is released because the hiker was absent at five
- The bed stays reserved because both the request and the notice were timely

## Explanation

The four o'clock booking precedes the five o'clock deadline. The call at half past five precedes the separate six o'clock deadline. The hiker thus also meets the condition for a later arrival.
