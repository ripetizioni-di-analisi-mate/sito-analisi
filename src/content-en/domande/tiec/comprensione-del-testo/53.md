> A pottery company introduced a box for anonymous suggestions. After one month it found twelve slips and published the statement “twelve colleagues have written to us”. Nobody questioned the value of the suggestions. Some dealt with the order of tools, others with the position of lights. The rules, however, allowed staff to write more than once and forbade them to add their names. A representative suggested replacing “colleagues” with “messages” in the bulletin. A colleague objected that they might still come from twelve different people. The representative agreed. Precisely because that was only one possibility, she preferred a term the count could verify.

Which word before “twelve colleagues” would express the colleague's hypothesis without presenting it as established?

- Certainly
- Exactly
- Perhaps
- Necessarily

## Explanation

There are twelve slips, but repeat submissions were allowed. Twelve different authors remain possible without being established. “Perhaps” marks that possibility, while the other words assert certainty about the authors.
