Read the following passage.

> In a hilly area, the local health authority offers some follow-up appointments by video to patients with chronic conditions who live far from hospitals. Patients join from their home computers or tablets. They measure their own blood pressure, weight, and blood glucose using devices supplied by the health authority, and the readings reach the doctor before the call. A video visit replaces the periodic checkup, while the first visit and tests requiring hospital equipment still take place in person.
>
> For patients, the main benefit is avoiding trips that can take half a day by public transportation. The service ran into an unexpected obstacle because many older patients did not know how to use a tablet. The health authority therefore enlisted pharmacies in the local towns, where a pharmacist helps patients connect from a private room.

According to the passage, what obstacle did the service encounter and how was it addressed?

- Many older patients did not know how to use a tablet, and the health authority enlisted local pharmacies
- The readings reached doctors late, and the health authority changed the devices
- Trips to the hospital took too long, and the health authority added bus services
- Doctors refused to conduct video visits, and the health authority made them mandatory

## Explanation

The last paragraph says the service encountered an unexpected obstacle because many older patients did not know how to use a tablet. The health authority therefore enlisted pharmacies, where a pharmacist helps patients connect. The correct answer restates these two sentences. Travel time is the problem the video visit solves, and the health authority did not add any bus services. The passage does not mention delayed readings or doctors refusing to conduct visits.
