Read the following passage.

> In one midsize city, single-person households rose from $24\%$ to $36\%$ of all households over twenty years. These include young people working far from home, people who have separated from their partners, and, most of all, older adults who now live alone.
>
> The housing market has been slow to adapt. Most apartments for sale still have three or four rooms because they were built when families with children were the norm. Two-room apartments, meanwhile, are scarce and command the city’s highest price per square meter. Some developers have begun dividing large older apartments into two smaller units. Doing so requires city approval and expensive work, such as installing a second electrical system and adding a second entrance. It pays off only in buildings where demand for two-room apartments is high.

Which statement best summarizes the passage?

- Older adults living alone make up the majority of the city’s households
- Single-person households have increased, but the supply of small homes is slow and costly to adjust
- Dividing a large apartment in two pays off in every building
- Two-room apartments command the city’s highest price per square meter

## Explanation

The first paragraph describes the growth of single-person households. The second explains that small homes are scarce and that creating more of them is expensive. The correct statement summarizes both paragraphs. The price of two-room apartments is an accurate detail, but it is **too narrow**. The other two statements exaggerate. Older adults living alone are a subset of single-person households, which make up $36\%$ of all households, so they cannot be the majority of households. Also, dividing apartments pays off only where demand for two-room apartments is high.
