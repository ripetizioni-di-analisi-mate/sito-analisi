> An association gave members two kinds of card for a group reading. A green card entitled the holder to speak on stage, a white one to submit a written question. Both granted admission to the hall. The notice said green card holders could give up their turn until the doors opened. One member took this as a request to return her card and free up a seat. The volunteer at the entrance showed her the list. The speaking slot beside her name could be deleted while her seat number stayed the same. The two columns were there precisely to handle the two requests separately.

What does “their turn” refer to in the notice?

- Their opportunity to enter the hall
- Their submission of a written question
- The allocation of their seat
- Their speaking slot on stage

## Explanation

The notice addresses green card holders. Speaking on stage is the specific privilege of that card. The numbered seat remains assigned even if the speaking slot is removed.
