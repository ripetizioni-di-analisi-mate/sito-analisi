Read the following passage.

> In a flat farming region with little summer rainfall, many farms still use furrow irrigation. Water flows through furrows between the rows of plants, and a substantial share evaporates or seeps too deep into the soil for the roots to reach. Drip irrigation, by contrast, delivers water through perforated tubing directly to the base of each plant.
>
> A tomato grower compared the two methods on neighboring fields of equal size. In one season, the grower used $5000$ cubic meters of water on the field with furrow irrigation and $40\%$ less on the field with drip irrigation. Although it received less water, the drip-irrigated field produced nearly the same yield. A drip system, however, is expensive to install and requires maintenance because mineral deposits can clog the holes. For this reason, many farms adopt it only when they receive a government subsidy toward the purchase.

According to the passage, how many cubic meters of water did the grower use on the field with drip irrigation?

- $2000$
- $4960$
- $7000$
- $3000$

## Explanation

The grower used $5000$ cubic meters on the field with furrow irrigation and $40\%$ less on the field with drip irrigation. Since $40\%$ of $5000$ is $2000$, the amount of water used is
$$
5000 - 2000 = 3000 \text{ cubic meters} .
$$
The value $2000$ is only the amount saved, and $4960$ subtracts $40$ cubic meters instead of $40\%$. The value $7000$ corresponds to an increase of $40\%$.
