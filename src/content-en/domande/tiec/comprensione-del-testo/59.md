> For a class dictionary the teacher requested examples in which “weigh” did not mean putting something on scales. Suggestions included “this suitcase weighs very little”, “that promise weighs on me” and “one must weigh one's words”. The group selected the third sentence for the entry it was preparing. Its commentary explained that someone who weighs their words may speak loudly or softly, at length or briefly. What matters is the care taken in choosing words and considering their possible effect. The sentence about the promise went on a separate card, because there the verb expressed the burden of an undertaking that was hard to bear.

Which sentence could replace the chosen example while preserving the meaning of the verb?

- She counted the words remaining on the page
- She answered quietly to avoid being heard
- As she spoke she felt the full burden of the promise
- Before answering she carefully considered each expression

## Explanation

The chosen entry concerns careful consideration of wording. Volume, duration and number of words are not decisive. The burden of a promise belongs to the different use recorded on the second card.
