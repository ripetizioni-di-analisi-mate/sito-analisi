Read the following passage.

> A movie theater in an outlying neighborhood had been closed for six years before a residents’ association reopened it. The building owner accepted a low rent in return for renovations, paid for partly through a fundraiser among residents and partly with a city grant.
>
> The association knows it cannot compete with multiplexes for first-run movies, which reach small theaters weeks later and on difficult financial terms. It therefore placed its bets elsewhere, offering series of older films, afternoon screenings for schools, and evenings when audiences discuss a film with a critic. Tickets cost a little more than half the price of a multiplex ticket. In its first year, the theater sold about $18\,000$ tickets, half of them for school screenings. Evenings with the critic take place in the smaller auditorium, which seats $60$ and is $90\%$ full on average. Film series take place in the main auditorium, which seats $200$ and is $50\%$ full on average.

In the passage, the sentence “It therefore placed its bets elsewhere” means that the association

- wagered money on another activity
- pointed residents toward a different auditorium
- gave up showing movies
- chose to focus on offerings other than first-run movies

## Explanation

The sentence follows the association’s acknowledgment that it cannot compete with multiplexes for first-run movies. It is followed by a list of the offerings it chose, including series of older films, school screenings, and evenings with a critic. *Placing its bets on* something therefore has the **figurative** meaning of relying on it and concentrating one’s efforts on it. The wager takes the expression literally. Pointing toward another room draws on another literal meaning of the Italian verb “puntare,” to point. The association continues to show films, as the film series demonstrate.
