Read the following passage.

> Many prices in stores end in $99$ cents. A pair of shoes costs $49.99$ euros instead of $50$, and a subscription costs $9.99$ euros a month. The difference is only one cent, but people reading a price from left to right often stop at the first digit and register $49$ instead of $50$. Retailers know this. This is why prices ending in $99$ have become almost the rule at sales and in clothing stores.
>
> Not all retailers use them, though. Some high-end stores prefer round prices, such as $120$ euros, because a price ending in $99$ suggests a discount and a budget product, an image these stores want to avoid. In other words, a price communicates more than its amount.

Which statement best expresses the main idea of the passage?

- Prices ending in $99$ save customers a great deal of money
- The way a price is written influences the customer’s impression of a product
- High-end stores sell better products than other stores
- All sale prices end in $99$

## Explanation

The first paragraph explains that a price ending in $99$ looks lower. The second explains that a round price makes a product seem less like a budget item. The last sentence summarizes both by saying that a price communicates more than its amount. The main idea is therefore how the way a price is written affects the customer. The saving is one cent, so the claim that customers save a great deal contradicts the text. The quality of high-end products is not discussed, and the passage says that prices ending in $99$ are *almost* the rule at sales.
