> At the beginning of the year the caretaker of a sports ground asked coaches to choose one evening per team. At Tuesday's meeting three coaches said that they could accept Friday provided they started after seven. The minutes recorded their availability. A provisional schedule appeared on the gate the following morning with no team listed on Friday. A parent accused the caretaker of ignoring a decision. The secretary explained that no evenings had been allocated at the meeting. The minutes recorded times that suited the coaches present. A fourth team that shared the same changing room as the other three still had to be consulted.

Which word best describes the status of the Friday entry in the minutes?

- Availability
- Priority
- Prohibition
- Allocation

## Explanation

The three coaches say Friday would suit them subject to one condition. The secretary confirms that no schedule was agreed. The entry therefore records a possibility the people concerned could accept.
