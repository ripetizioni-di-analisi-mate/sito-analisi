> Membership of a communal oven entitles each person to two baking sessions per month. In the first month the log recorded fifty sessions for forty members. A newsletter called these “thirty unused opportunities”. Two readers understood the phrase as a reproach aimed at thirty absent people. The writer explained that she was counting available slots on the calendar: the forty members had eighty between them. One member could have given up both slots while another had used both. The published log did not list names. She proposed keeping the figure and adding the words “to use the oven” after “opportunities”.

What is the smallest possible number of members who left at least one session unused?

- Forty
- Thirty
- Fifteen
- Ten

## Explanation

Thirty sessions went unused and each person can forgo at most two. Thus at least fifteen people are required. The minimum is attained if fifteen use neither slot and the other twenty-five use both.
