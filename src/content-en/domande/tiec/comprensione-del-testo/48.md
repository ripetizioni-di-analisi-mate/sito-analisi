> A winter photography competition required an unpublished picture. Its rules defined unpublished as never previously shown “to the public, even without payment”. Eva had sent her photograph only to her sister in a private message. Omar had hung his for one morning in the publicly accessible entrance hall of his shop without selling it. Lina had posted hers on a group's freely accessible noticeboard and later removed it. All three confused absence of profit with absence of a public showing. Before they submitted their forms, the organisers asked them to reread the short definition included in the published rules of the competition.

Who meets the requirement on the information supplied?

- Eva and Omar
- Omar and Lina
- All three
- Only Eva

## Explanation

A private message to a sister is not a public display. The shop entrance and the noticeboard were open to everyone. Free viewing or later deletion does not undo the public showing.
