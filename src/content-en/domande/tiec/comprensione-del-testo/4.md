> For two Sundays the village club offered a free ferry to the island. Early each afternoon the pier was crowded, so the treasurer suggested doubling the outward sailings. The skipper showed him her log. Every departure had taken everyone waiting to reach the island. The people lingering on the village quay had just got off the boat and were waiting for their connection to the only bus. A committee member then proposed moving the ferry time. The skipper first wanted to know the drivers' working hours. If the bus could leave earlier, visitors could still spend their full allotted time on the island.

Which piece of information changes the interpretation of the crowd on the pier?

- Those staying on the pier were waiting for the bus after disembarking
- The scheme ran for only two Sundays
- The treasurer preferred to double the departures
- The bus drivers also worked in the afternoon

## Explanation

The treasurer sees the crowd as demand for ferry seats. The log shows that everyone bound for the island had sailed. Identifying what the remaining passengers were waiting for shifts the issue to the connection.
