> A trail notice reads: “After the fountain follow the yellow marks to the gate. Beyond the gate the red marks lead to the viewpoint.” A group approaching from the opposite slope reads only the second sentence, which is also painted on the gatepost. They see red marks on both sides because the return path runs on the other side. One walker observes that “beyond” by itself is not a compass direction. To work out which way to go they must reconstruct the position from which the reader is imagined to approach. The group locates the fountain on its map to orient the instructions correctly.

From the viewpoint implied by the sign, in what order do the landmarks occur?

- Fountain, viewpoint, gate
- Viewpoint, gate, fountain
- Gate, fountain, viewpoint
- Fountain, gate, viewpoint

## Explanation

The first sentence leads from the fountain to the gate. The second continues in the same direction from the gate to the viewpoint. The group's opposite approach does not change the sequence presupposed by the notice.
