> The city choir accepts anyone who turns sixteen by the date of the first meeting. To sing in the final concert one must also attend at least five of the seven rehearsals. Nina turns sixteen on the very day of the first meeting and attends five rehearsals. The conductor puts her in the second group, which sings the same music as the first on a different evening. A friend objects because she thinks the second group is reserved for singers who missed too many rehearsals. The conductor rereads the notice. Once eligibility is established, the only reason for dividing singers into groups is the capacity of the stage.

Which assessment of Nina's situation follows from the notice?

- She meets both requirements and her group is unrelated to attendance
- The second group allows her to perform under an exception to the notice
- She may watch the concert but does not meet the age requirement
- She must attend a sixth rehearsal before singing

## Explanation

“By the date” includes the date itself and “at least five” includes five. Nina meets both thresholds exactly. The final line explains that the division is due to the available space.
