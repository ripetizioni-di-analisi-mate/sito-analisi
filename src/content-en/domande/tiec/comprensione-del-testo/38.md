> Under the rules of a reuse store, members can take an item only after depositing one in working order. Each deposit earns one withdrawal, and rights do not expire at the end of the month. On Saturday Georgia brought two lamps. The first lit up on the test bench, the second did not. The previous Saturday she had brought in a chair and already exchanged it for a stool. She wondered how many items she could take that day. The volunteer explained that she could repair the broken lamp the following week. The corresponding right would arise only when the lamp passed its test.

How many items can Georgia take that Saturday on the facts given?

- None
- One
- Two
- Three

## Explanation

The chair's credit was used for the stool. Only one of the current deposits passes the test. That deposit earns one available withdrawal, while the repair lies in the future.
