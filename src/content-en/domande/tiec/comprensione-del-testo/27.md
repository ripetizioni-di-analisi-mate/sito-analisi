> In a small firm invoices pass through two drawers. They go into the top drawer on arrival. After checking the documents, a clerk moves them to the bottom drawer. Only on Fridays does a second clerk issue payment orders for the invoices there. A supplier calls on Wednesday about an invoice delivered on Monday. The clerk finds it in the bottom drawer and confirms that the paperwork is in order. She does not tell the supplier the date the money will arrive. Between the payment order and the credit to the supplier's account there can be a delay outside the firm's control.

Which is the last stage that has definitely happened by the time of the call?

- The bank has confirmed the credit
- The supplier's account has been credited
- The invoice paperwork has been checked
- Friday's payment order has been issued

## Explanation

Moving to the bottom drawer follows the paperwork check. The invoice arrived on Monday and the call is on Wednesday, before the scheduled payment day. Crediting the account comes later still.
