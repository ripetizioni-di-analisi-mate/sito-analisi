Marta rewrites $\log_3(x-1)+\log_3(x+1)<1$ as $x^2-1<3$ and accepts $x=-3/2$. Which check explains the error and gives every admissible value?

- A sum requires different arguments, leaving $-2<x<1$
- Both original arguments must be positive, leaving $1<x<2$
- The base must exceed $x$, leaving $-2<x<2$
- Only their product must be positive, leaving $1<|x|<2$

## Explanation

The first logarithm already requires $x>1$, which also makes $x+1$ positive. Within this domain the transformation yields $x^2<4$. The intersection is $1<x<2$, and the negative example lies outside the original domain.
