Two departments have output models $A(t)=80\cdot2^t$ and $B(t)=20\cdot4^t$, where $t$ is in years. At what time are their outputs equal?

- $t=4$
- $t=3$
- $t=2$
- $t=1$

## Explanation

The first department initially produces four times as much as the second. Each year the ratio $B/A$ doubles because $4/2=2$. It takes two doublings for that ratio to rise from $1/4$ to $1$.
