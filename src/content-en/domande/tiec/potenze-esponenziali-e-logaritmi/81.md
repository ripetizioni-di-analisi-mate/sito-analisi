Two departments have outputs $A(t) = 80 \cdot 2^{t}$ and $B(t) = 20 \cdot 4^{t}$, where $t$ is in years. In which year are their outputs equal?

- $t = 4$
- $t = 3$
- $t = 2$
- $t = 1$

## Explanation

The outputs are equal when $80 \cdot 2^{t} = 20 \cdot 4^{t}$. Dividing by $20 \cdot 2^{t}$, which is positive, and using $4^{t} = 2^{t} \cdot 2^{t}$, the equation becomes $4 = 2^{t}$. Since $4 = 2^{2}$, we have $2^{t} = 2^{2}$, so $t = 2$. Indeed $A(2) = 80 \cdot 4 = 320$ and $B(2) = 20 \cdot 16 = 320$.
