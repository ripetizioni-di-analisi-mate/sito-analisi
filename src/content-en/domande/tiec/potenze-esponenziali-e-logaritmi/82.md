The equation $2^{x} \cdot 3^{x} = 36$ has the following solution:

- $x = 2$
- $x = 6$
- $x = 1$
- $x = 18$

## Explanation

By the power-of-a-product rule, $2^{x} \cdot 3^{x} = (2 \cdot 3)^{x} = 6^{x}$. The equation becomes $6^{x} = 6^{2}$, so $x = 2$. The result $x = 1$ writes the product as $6^{2x}$ by adding the exponents of powers with different bases. The result $x = 6$ divides $36$ by $6$, and $x = 18$ divides $36$ by $2$.
