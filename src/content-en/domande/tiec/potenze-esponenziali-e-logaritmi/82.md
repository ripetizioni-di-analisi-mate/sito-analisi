An app calculates $2^x$ only for integer values of $x$. Which open interval of outputs contains exactly two possible results?

- $(3,9)$
- $(4,8)$
- $(1,4)$
- $(8,15)$

## Explanation

Between $3$ and $9$ lie $4$ and $8$, consecutive powers of two. The interval from $1$ to $4$ contains only $2$. Neither of the other two open intervals contains a power of two.
