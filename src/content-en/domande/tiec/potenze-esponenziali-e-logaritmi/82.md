In which of the following intervals do exactly two powers of $2$ with integer exponent lie?

- $(3, 9)$
- $(4, 8)$
- $(1, 4)$
- $(8, 15)$

## Explanation

The powers of $2$ with positive integer exponent are $2$, $4$, $8$, $16$, …, while those with zero or negative exponent are less than or equal to $1$. The open interval $(3, 9)$ contains $4$ and $8$, that is exactly two powers. The interval $(4, 8)$ contains none, because the endpoints are excluded, $(1, 4)$ contains only $2$ and $(8, 15)$ contains none. The required interval is therefore $(3, 9)$.
