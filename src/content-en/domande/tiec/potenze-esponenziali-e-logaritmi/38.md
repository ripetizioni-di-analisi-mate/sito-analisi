A message has $n$ spaces, each either white or black. The first and last spaces must match and $n\geq2$. How many different messages can be formed?

- $2^{n-2}$
- $2^{n-1}$
- $2^n-2$
- $2n-2$

## Explanation

You can choose the colours of the first $n-1$ spaces freely. The colour of the first determines the last. The $n-1$ independent choices give $2^{n-1}$ messages.
