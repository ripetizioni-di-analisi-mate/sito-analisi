The number $\dfrac{3^{20}}{9^{9}}$ is equal to:

- $3^{11}$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $3^{38}$

## Explanation

By the power-of-a-power rule, $9^{9} = \left(3^{2}\right)^{9} = 3^{18}$. To divide powers with the same base, subtract the exponents, which gives $\dfrac{3^{20}}{3^{18}} = 3^{2} = 9$. The result $3^{11}$ writes $9^{9}$ as $3^{9}$, while $\dfrac{1}{9}$ subtracts the exponents in the wrong order. The result $3^{38}$ adds the exponents as in a product.
