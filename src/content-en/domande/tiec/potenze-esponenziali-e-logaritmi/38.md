How many sequences of $n$ digits, with $n \geq 2$, are there in which every digit is $0$ or $1$ and the first digit equals the last?

- $2^{n-2}$
- $2^{n-1}$
- $2^{n} - 2$
- $2n - 2$

## Explanation

Each of the first $n - 1$ digits can be chosen in $2$ ways, independently of the others, so these digits can be chosen in $2^{n-1}$ ways. The last digit is instead determined, because it must equal the first. Hence there are $2^{n-1}$ sequences.
