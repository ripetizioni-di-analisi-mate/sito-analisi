A sensor displays only the greatest integer less than or equal to $\log_{10}x$, with $x>0$. If it displays $2$, what is known about $x$?

- $100<x\leq1000$
- $2\leq x<3$
- $10<x\leq100$
- $100\leq x<1000$

## Explanation

A reading of $2$ means $2\leq\log_{10}x<3$. The function $10^t$ is increasing, so both inequality signs are preserved. The lower threshold is included, while $1000$ would already produce a reading of $3$.
