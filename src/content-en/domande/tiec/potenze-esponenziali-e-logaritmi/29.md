A display shows $s=\sqrt{10}-3$. To estimate how many times $s$ fits into $1$, which exact expression is convenient for $1/s$?

- $3-\sqrt{10}$
- $\sqrt{10}+3$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{10}+3}{19}$
- $\sqrt{10}-3$

## Explanation

Multiply the numerator and denominator by the conjugate $\sqrt{10}+3$. The denominator becomes $10-9=1$. The result is slightly greater than $6$, as expected for the reciprocal of this small positive number.
