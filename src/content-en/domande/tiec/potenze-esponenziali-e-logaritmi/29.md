The number $\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{9} \cdot \sqrt{3}}{\sqrt[6]{3}}$ is equal to:

- $\sqrt[3]{9}$
- $3$
- $3\sqrt[3]{3}$
- $9$

## Explanation

Write all the roots as powers of $3$, namely $\sqrt[3]{9} = 3^{\frac{2}{3}}$, $\sqrt{3} = 3^{\frac{1}{2}}$, and $\sqrt[6]{3} = 3^{\frac{1}{6}}$. By the properties of powers, the resulting exponent is $\dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{4 + 3 - 1}{6} = 1$, so the value is $3$. The result $\sqrt[3]{9}$ cancels $\sqrt{3}$ with $\sqrt[6]{3}$ as if they were equal, and $3\sqrt[3]{3} = 3^{\frac{4}{3}}$ adds the exponent $\dfrac{1}{6}$ instead of subtracting it. The result $9$ multiplies and divides the exponents instead of adding and subtracting them.
