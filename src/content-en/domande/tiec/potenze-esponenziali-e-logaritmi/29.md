Which number is equal to $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{10} - 3}$?

- $3 - \sqrt{10}$
- $\sqrt{10} + 3$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{10} + 3}{19}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{10} + 3}{7}$

## Explanation

The numerator and the denominator are multiplied by $\sqrt{10} + 3$, which is not zero, and the value of the quotient does not change. By the product of a sum and a difference, the denominator becomes $\left( \sqrt{10} - 3 \right)\left( \sqrt{10} + 3 \right) = 10 - 9 = 1$. The quotient is therefore equal to the new numerator, hence $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{10} - 3} = \sqrt{10} + 3$.
