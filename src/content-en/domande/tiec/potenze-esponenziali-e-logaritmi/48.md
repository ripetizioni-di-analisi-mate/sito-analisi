A sensor records $12$, $18$ and $27$ units at times $0$, $1$ and $2$. If the ratio of consecutive readings stays constant, which rule describes the readings $L_n$?

- $L_n=12+6n$
- $L_n=18(3/2)^n$
- $L_n=12(3/2)^n$
- $L_n=12(2/3)^n$

## Explanation

Both ratios $18/12$ and $27/18$ equal $3/2$. The reading at time zero fixes the coefficient at $12$. The absolute increase changes from $6$ to $9$, so a linear rule does not fit all three readings.
