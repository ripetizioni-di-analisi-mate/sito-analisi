A colony of bacteria has $12$, $18$ and $27$ thousand individuals after $0$, $1$ and $2$ hours, and the ratio between two consecutive values is constant. Which law gives the number $N(t)$ of thousands of individuals after $t$ hours?

- $N(t) = 12 + 6t$
- $N(t) = 18 \left( \dfrac{3}{2} \right)^{t}$
- $N(t) = 12 \left( \dfrac{3}{2} \right)^{t}$
- $N(t) = 12 \left( \dfrac{2}{3} \right)^{t}$

## Explanation

The ratio between two consecutive values is $\dfrac{18}{12} = \dfrac{27}{18} = \dfrac{3}{2}$, so every hour the number of individuals is multiplied by $\dfrac{3}{2}$. A quantity multiplied by the same factor over equal time intervals follows the exponential law $N(t) = N(0) \left( \dfrac{3}{2} \right)^{t}$. Since $N(0) = 12$, the law is $N(t) = 12 \left( \dfrac{3}{2} \right)^{t}$. The law $N(t) = 12 + 6t$ gives $24$ for $t = 2$, so it does not describe the data.
