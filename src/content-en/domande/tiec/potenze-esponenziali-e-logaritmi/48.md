The graph of the function $y = a^{x}$, with $a > 0$ and $a \neq 1$, passes through the point $\left( 3; \dfrac{8}{27} \right)$. The value of the function at $x = -1$ is:

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$

## Explanation

The point lies on the graph, so $a^{3} = \dfrac{8}{27}$, which gives $a = \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{8}{27}} = \dfrac{2}{3}$. The value at $x = -1$ is $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{-1} = \dfrac{3}{2}$, the reciprocal of the base. The result $\dfrac{2}{3}$ is the base, the value at $x = 1$, and $-\dfrac{2}{3}$ treats the exponent $-1$ as a change of sign. The result $\dfrac{9}{4}$ is the value at $x = -2$.
