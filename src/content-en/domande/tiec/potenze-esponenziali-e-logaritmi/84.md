Two positive numbers $a$ and $b$, with $a > b$, satisfy $\log_{3} a - \log_{3} b = 2$ and $a - b = 8$. What is the value of $b$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $1$
- $8$

## Explanation

By the quotient rule for logarithms, the first condition reads $\log_{3} \dfrac{a}{b} = 2$, so $\dfrac{a}{b} = 3^{2} = 9$ and $a = 9b$. Substituting into the second condition gives $9b - b = 8$, that is $8b = 8$. Hence $b = 1$, and consequently $a = 9$.
