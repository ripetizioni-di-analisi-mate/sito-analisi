The equation $\log_{3} (x + 8) - \log_{3} x = 2$ has the following solution:

- $x = \dfrac{8}{5}$
- $x = -1$
- $x = 1$
- $x = \dfrac{4}{5}$

## Explanation

The domain restrictions are $x + 8 > 0$ and $x > 0$, which together give $x > 0$. By the rule for the **logarithm of a quotient**, the equation becomes $\log_{3} \dfrac{x + 8}{x} = 2$, so $\dfrac{x + 8}{x} = 3^{2} = 9$. This gives $x + 8 = 9x$, or $x = 1$, which satisfies $x > 0$. The result $\dfrac{8}{5}$ comes from $3^{2} = 6$, and $\dfrac{4}{5}$ from a sign error when moving $x$ to the right side. The value $x = -1$ does not satisfy the domain restriction.
