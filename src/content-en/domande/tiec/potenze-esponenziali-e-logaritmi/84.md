The base-three logarithmic coordinates of two positive measurements differ by $2$. The larger measurement exceeds the smaller by $8$ units. What is the smaller measurement?

- $2$
- $4$
- $1$
- $8$

## Explanation

A logarithmic difference of $2$ means a ratio of $3^2=9$. If the smaller measurement is $x$, the larger is $9x$. The absolute gap gives $8x=8$, so $x=1$.
