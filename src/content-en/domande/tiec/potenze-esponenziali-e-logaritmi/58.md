Two curves have equations $y=\log_2 x$ and $y=\log_2(4x)$, with $x>0$. What is the vertical distance between them at the same horizontal coordinate?

- $4$
- $2x$
- $\log_2 x$
- $2$

## Explanation

The product rule gives $\log_2(4x)=\log_2 4+\log_2 x$. Subtracting the ordinates leaves $\log_2 4=2$. The distance is constant for every horizontal coordinate in the domain.
