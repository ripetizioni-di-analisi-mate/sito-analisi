On a scale with base $b>0$, $b\ne1$, an index is defined by $J=\log_b x$. For $x>1$ the index is negative. What follows about the base?

- $0<b<1$
- $1<b<x$
- $b=x$
- $b>x$

## Explanation

The relation means $b^J=x>1$. With a negative exponent, a base greater than one would give a number below one. A positive base below one gives the observed behaviour.
