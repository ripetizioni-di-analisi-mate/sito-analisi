The value of $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} 8$ is:

- $-3$
- $3$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $4$

## Explanation

By the definition of a logarithm, $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} 8$ is the exponent to which $\dfrac{1}{2}$ must be raised to obtain $8$. Since $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{-3} = 2^{3} = 8$, the exponent is $-3$. The value $3$ gives $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{3} = \dfrac{1}{8}$, and $-\dfrac{1}{3}$ switches the base and argument, since $\log_{8} \dfrac{1}{2} = -\dfrac{1}{3}$. The value $4$ multiplies the argument by the base.
