What are the values of the numbers $\sqrt[3]{-64}$ and $\sqrt{(-4)^{2}}$, in this order?

- $-4$ and $-4$
- $-4$ and $4$
- $4$ and $4$
- $4$ and $-4$

## Explanation

The cube root is defined for negative numbers too, and since $(-4)^{3} = -64$ we have $\sqrt[3]{-64} = -4$. In the second number the power is computed first, and it equals $(-4)^{2} = 16$. The square root of $16$ is the non-negative number whose square is $16$, so $\sqrt{(-4)^{2}} = \sqrt{16} = 4$. The two values are therefore $-4$ and $4$.
