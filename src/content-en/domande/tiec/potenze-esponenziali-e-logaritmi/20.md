A worksheet assigns $-4$ both to $\sqrt[3]{-64}$ and to $\sqrt{(-4)^2}$. Which correction distinguishes the cube root from the principal square root?

- Change only the first result to $4$
- Change only the second result to $4$
- Change both results to $4$
- Keep both results at $-4$

## Explanation

The cube of $-4$ is $-64$, so the first result is correct. The square of $-4$ is $16$. The principal square root of $16$ is the nonnegative number $4$.
