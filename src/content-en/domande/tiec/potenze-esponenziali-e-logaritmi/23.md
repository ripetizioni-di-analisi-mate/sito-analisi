How is the number $0.00052$ written in scientific notation?

- $5.2 \cdot 10^{-4}$
- $5.2 \cdot 10^{-5}$
- $5.2 \cdot 10^{-3}$
- $5.2 \cdot 10^{4}$

## Explanation

To go from $0.00052$ to $5.2$ the decimal point moves four places to the right, so $5.2$ is $10^{4}$ times larger than $0.00052$. Hence $0.00052 = \dfrac{5.2}{10^{4}} = 5.2 \cdot 10^{-4}$. The coefficient $5.2$ lies between $1$ and $10$, so $5.2 \cdot 10^{-4}$ is the scientific notation of the number.
