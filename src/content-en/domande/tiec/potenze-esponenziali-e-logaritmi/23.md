The number $\sqrt{0.0009}$ is equal to:

- $0.03$
- $0.3$
- $0.003$
- $0.00045$

## Explanation

Write the radicand as a fraction, $0.0009 = \dfrac{9}{10^{4}}$. By the rule for the **square root of a quotient**, $\sqrt{0.0009} = \dfrac{\sqrt{9}}{\sqrt{10^{4}}} = \dfrac{3}{10^{2}} = 0.03$. As a check, $0.03^{2} = 0.0009$, while $0.3^{2} = 0.09$ and $0.003^{2} = 0.000009$. The result $0.00045$ divides the radicand by $2$ instead of taking its root.
