When the unit of measurement changes, the digit $6$ moves from the hundredths place to the tens place. By what factor has the numerical value of the measurement increased?

- $10^3$
- $10^4$
- $10^{-3}$
- $10^2$

## Explanation

The digit moves three places, from hundredths to tenths, then to units and finally to tens. Its contribution changes from $6\cdot10^{-2}$ to $6\cdot10^1$. The ratio is $10^{1-(-2)}=10^3$.
