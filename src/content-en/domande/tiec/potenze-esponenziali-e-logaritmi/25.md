Marta writes $\sqrt{u+v}=\sqrt u+\sqrt v$ for $u,v\geq0$. For which pairs does the equality actually hold?

- All pairs with $uv=1$
- All pairs with $u+v=1$
- Only those with $u=v$
- Those with $uv=0$

## Explanation

Both sides are nonnegative, so it is enough to compare their squares. The right side has the extra term $2\sqrt{uv}$. It vanishes exactly when at least one of the numbers is zero.
