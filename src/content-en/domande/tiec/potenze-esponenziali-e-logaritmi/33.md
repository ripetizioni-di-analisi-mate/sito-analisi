A table lists the square root of an area of $9\cdot10^{-6}$ square metres as $3\cdot10^{-6}$ metres. Which arithmetic check shows the error?

- The exponent of every length measurement has to be positive
- The square of the proposed measurement is $9\cdot10^{-12}$
- The square of $3$ is $6$
- The proposed measurement is negative

## Explanation

Check the proposed measurement by squaring it. The coefficient becomes $9$, but the exponent doubles from $-6$ to $-12$. The length that gives the stated area is $3\cdot10^{-3}$ metres.
