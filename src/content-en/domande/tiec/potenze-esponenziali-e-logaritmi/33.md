What is the value of $\sqrt{9 \cdot 10^{-6}}$?

- $3 \cdot 10^{-6}$
- $3 \cdot 10^{-3}$
- $9 \cdot 10^{-3}$
- $4.5 \cdot 10^{-3}$

## Explanation

By the rule for the root of a product, $\sqrt{9 \cdot 10^{-6}} = \sqrt{9} \cdot \sqrt{10^{-6}}$, and the first factor equals $3$. Since $\left( 10^{-3} \right)^{2} = 10^{-6}$ and $10^{-3}$ is positive, the second factor equals $10^{-3}$. The number is therefore $3 \cdot 10^{-3}$, and indeed $\left( 3 \cdot 10^{-3} \right)^{2} = 9 \cdot 10^{-6}$.
