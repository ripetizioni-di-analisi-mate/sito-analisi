Which of the following numbers is negative?

- $(-2)^{-4}$
- $(-3)^{-3}$
- $\left( -\dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{6}$
- $(-5)^{0}$

## Explanation

A power with a negative base is positive for an even exponent and negative for an odd exponent. A minus sign in the exponent indicates the reciprocal and does not change the sign of the result. Since $(-3)^{-3} = \dfrac{1}{(-3)^{3}} = -\dfrac{1}{27}$, this number is negative. The exponents $-4$ and $6$ are even, so $(-2)^{-4} = \dfrac{1}{16}$ and $\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{6} = \dfrac{1}{64}$ are positive. Finally, $(-5)^{0} = 1$, since raising any nonzero number to $0$ gives $1$.
