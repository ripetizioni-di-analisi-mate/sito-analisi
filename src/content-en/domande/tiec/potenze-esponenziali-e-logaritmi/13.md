Half of $4^{10}$ is:

- $4^{5}$
- $4^{9}$
- $2^{9}$
- $2^{19}$

## Explanation

By the **power-of-a-power rule**, $4^{10} = \left(2^{2}\right)^{10} = 2^{20}$. Dividing by $2$ removes one factor of $2$, so half the number is $\dfrac{2^{20}}{2} = 2^{19}$. The result $4^{5}$ halves the exponent instead of the number, and $4^{9}$ divides by $4$ instead of $2$. The result $2^{9}$ writes $4^{10}$ as $2^{10}$, overlooking that the exponent must be multiplied by $2$.
