Where does the number $\log_{2} 3$ lie with respect to the numbers $1$, $\dfrac{3}{2}$ and $2$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2} < \log_{2} 3 < 2$
- $1 < \log_{2} 3 < \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\log_{2} 3 = \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $2 < \log_{2} 3 < 3$

## Explanation

Since $2^{1} < 3 < 2^{2}$ and the function $y = \log_{2} x$ is increasing, we have $1 < \log_{2} 3 < 2$. To compare $\log_{2} 3$ with $\dfrac{3}{2}$, one compares $3$ with $2^{\frac{3}{2}}$. Squaring the two positive numbers gives $9$ and $2^{3} = 8$, and since $9 > 8$ we have $3 > 2^{\frac{3}{2}}$. Hence $\log_{2} 3 > \dfrac{3}{2}$, and the number lies between $\dfrac{3}{2}$ and $2$.
