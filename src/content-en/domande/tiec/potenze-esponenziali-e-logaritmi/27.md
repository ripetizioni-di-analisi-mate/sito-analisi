The positive number $x$ that satisfies $x^{\frac{2}{3}} = 9$ is:

- $6$
- $\dfrac{27}{2}$
- $81$
- $27$

## Explanation

Raise both sides to the power $\dfrac{3}{2}$, the reciprocal of $\dfrac{2}{3}$, since $\left(x^{\frac{2}{3}}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}} = x$. The result is $x = 9^{\frac{3}{2}} = \left(\sqrt{9}\right)^{3} = 27$. As a check, $27^{\frac{2}{3}} = \left(\sqrt[3]{27}\right)^{2} = 9$. The result $6$ multiplies $9$ by $\dfrac{2}{3}$, and $\dfrac{27}{2}$ multiplies $9$ by $\dfrac{3}{2}$, while $81$ squares $9$.
