Luca replaces $\log_2(8+8)$ with $\log_2 8+\log_2 8$. What is his error, measured as his result minus the correct value?

- $2$
- $4$
- $-2$
- $0$

## Explanation

The correct value is $\log_2 16=4$. Luca obtains $3+3=6$, an overestimate of $2$. Adding two logarithms corresponds to taking the logarithm of a product.
