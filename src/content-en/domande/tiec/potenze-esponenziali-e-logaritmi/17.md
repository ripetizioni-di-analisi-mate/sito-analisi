A calculator returns $1$ for $a^0$. Which argument justifies the result for every $a\ne0$?

- Every base raised to an even exponent gives $1$
- The exponent is multiplied by the base to give $0$
- The quotient $a^3/a^3$ equals both $1$ and $a^{3-3}$
- A product of zero factors equal to $a$ must equal $a$

## Explanation

The division is valid because $a^3\ne0$. Dividing a quantity by itself gives $1$. The quotient rule assigns the exponent $3-3=0$ to the same result.
