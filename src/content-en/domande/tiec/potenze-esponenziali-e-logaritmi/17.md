Which of the following equations is true?

- $\left(2^{2}\right)^{3} = 2^{2^{3}}$
- $2^{3} + 2^{3} = 2^{6}$
- $2^{3} \cdot 2^{3} = 4^{3}$
- $2^{3} \cdot 3^{3} = 6^{9}$

## Explanation

By the power-of-a-product rule, $2^{3} \cdot 2^{3} = (2 \cdot 2)^{3} = 4^{3}$. Both sides indeed equal $64$. The equation $\left(2^{2}\right)^{3} = 2^{2^{3}}$ is false because its left side is $2^{6} = 64$ and its right side is $2^{8} = 256$. The equation $2^{3} + 2^{3} = 2^{6}$ is also false, since the sum is $16 = 2^{4}$. Finally, $2^{3} \cdot 3^{3} = 6^{3}$, so the exponent $9$ is incorrect.
