Which of the numbers $\dfrac{11}{5}$ and $\sqrt[3]{12}$ are less than $\sqrt{5}$?

- Only $\sqrt[3]{12}$
- Both
- Neither
- Only $\dfrac{11}{5}$

## Explanation

The three numbers are positive, so the order between two of them is the same as between their powers with the same positive exponent. Since $\left( \dfrac{11}{5} \right)^{2} = \dfrac{121}{25}$ and $5 = \dfrac{125}{25}$, we have $\left( \dfrac{11}{5} \right)^{2} < 5$, hence $\dfrac{11}{5} < \sqrt{5}$. To compare $\sqrt[3]{12}$ with $\sqrt{5}$, both are raised to the sixth power, giving $12^{2} = 144$ and $5^{3} = 125$. Since $144 > 125$, we have $\sqrt[3]{12} > \sqrt{5}$. So only $\dfrac{11}{5}$ is less than $\sqrt{5}$.
