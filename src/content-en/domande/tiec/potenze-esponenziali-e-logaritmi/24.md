Which of the following numbers is the greatest?

- $\sqrt[3]{11}$
- $\sqrt[6]{120}$
- $\sqrt[4]{24}$
- $\sqrt{5}$

## Explanation

To compare roots with different indices, raise all of them to the power $12$, a common multiple of the indices $2$, $3$, $4$, and $6$. Raising positive numbers to a positive power preserves their order. The results are $\left(\sqrt{5}\right)^{12} = 5^{6} = 15\,625$, $\left(\sqrt[3]{11}\right)^{12} = 11^{4} = 14\,641$, $\left(\sqrt[6]{120}\right)^{12} = 120^{2} = 14\,400$, and $\left(\sqrt[4]{24}\right)^{12} = 24^{3} = 13\,824$. The greatest of the four results is $15\,625$, so the greatest original number is $\sqrt{5}$. Comparing only the radicands would give $\sqrt[6]{120}$, which is actually smaller than both $\sqrt{5}$ and $\sqrt[3]{11}$.
