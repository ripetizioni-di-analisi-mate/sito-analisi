A slot admits discs at most $\sqrt5$ cm in diameter. Two discs measure $11/5$ cm and $\sqrt[3]{12}$ cm. Which fit when the exact diameters are compared?

- Only the $\sqrt[3]{12}$ cm disc
- Both discs
- Neither disc
- Only the $11/5$ cm disc

## Explanation

The diameters are positive and $(11/5)^2=121/25<5$. For the other disc compare sixth powers: $(\sqrt[3]{12})^6=144$ and $(\sqrt5)^6=125$. The first disc is below the limit and the second exceeds it.
