If $\log_{a} x = 3$ and $\log_{a} y = 2$, what is the value of $\log_{a} \dfrac{x^{2}}{y}$?

- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $1$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

By the quotient and power rules for logarithms, $\log_{a} \dfrac{x^{2}}{y} = 2\log_{a} x - \log_{a} y$. Substituting the given values yields $2 \cdot 3 - 2 = 4$. The result $\dfrac{9}{2}$ evaluates $\dfrac{\left(\log_{a} x\right)^{2}}{\log_{a} y}$, turning the logarithm of the quotient into a quotient of logarithms and the square of the argument into the square of the logarithm. The result $1$ overlooks the exponent $2$, and $6$ overlooks the denominator $y$.
