All that is known is $0<a<b<1$. Which pair is certainly in increasing order?

- $b^3,\ a^3$
- $b^{-1},\ a^{-1}$
- $\sqrt b,\ b$
- $a,\ a^2$

## Explanation

Taking reciprocals reverses the order of two positive numbers. Thus $1/b<1/a$. Squaring a number between zero and one makes it smaller and taking its square root makes it larger, while cubing preserves the order of different bases.
