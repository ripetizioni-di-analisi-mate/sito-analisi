The solutions of the equation $\log_{3} \left( x^{2} - 8x \right) = 2$ are:

- $x = -1 \;\vee\; x = 9$
- $x = 9$
- $x = -1$
- $x = 1 \;\vee\; x = -9$

## Explanation

The domain restriction is $x^{2} - 8x > 0$, or $x(x - 8) > 0$, which is satisfied for $x < 0$ or $x > 8$. By the definition of a logarithm, the equation is equivalent to $x^{2} - 8x = 3^{2} = 9$, or $x^{2} - 8x - 9 = 0$, with solutions $x = 9$ and $x = -1$. Both satisfy the domain restriction since $-1 < 0$ and $9 > 8$, and in both cases the argument equals $9$. The result $x = 9$ discards $x = -1$ because it is negative, although the restriction applies to the argument $x^{2} - 8x$ rather than to $x$. The result $x = 1 \;\vee\; x = -9$ switches the signs of the roots.
