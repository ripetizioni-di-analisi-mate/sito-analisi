What is the inverse function of $y = \log_{2} (x - 1)$?

- $y = 2^{x} + 1$
- $y = 1 + \log_{2} x$
- $y = 2^{x} - 1$
- $y = 2^{x+1}$

## Explanation

By the definition of logarithm, $y = \log_{2} (x - 1)$ is equivalent to $x - 1 = 2^{y}$, that is $x = 2^{y} + 1$. The inverse function is obtained by swapping the names of the two variables, so it is $y = 2^{x} + 1$. For example the given function maps $x = 5$ to $\log_{2} 4 = 2$, and the function $y = 2^{x} + 1$ maps $2$ to $5$.
