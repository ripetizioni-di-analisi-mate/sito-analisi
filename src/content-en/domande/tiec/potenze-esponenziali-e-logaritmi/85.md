The rule $y=\log_2(x-1)$ is used to recover the input $x$ from the output $y$. Which instruction recovers every admissible input?

- $x=1+2^y$
- $x=1+\log_2 y$
- $x=2^y-1$
- $x=2^{y+1}$

## Explanation

The definition of a logarithm gives $x-1=2^y$. Recover the unit shift by adding $1$ after exponentiating. For every real $y$ this gives a value greater than $1$, as the domain requires.
