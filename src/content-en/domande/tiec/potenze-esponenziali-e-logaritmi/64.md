Let $a = \log_{10} 2$. The number $\log_{10} 0.4$ is equal to:

- $2a + 1$
- $2a - 1$
- $a^{2} - 1$
- $1 - 2a$

## Explanation

Write $0.4 = \dfrac{4}{10} = \dfrac{2^{2}}{10}$. By the quotient and power rules for logarithms, $\log_{10} 0.4 = 2\log_{10} 2 - \log_{10} 10 = 2a - 1$. The result is negative, as expected because $0.4 < 1$, and indeed $2a - 1 \approx 2 \cdot 0.30 - 1 = -0.4$. The result $a^{2} - 1$ writes $\log_{10} 4$ as $\left(\log_{10} 2\right)^{2}$, while $2a + 1$ and $1 - 2a$ give one of the two terms the wrong sign.
