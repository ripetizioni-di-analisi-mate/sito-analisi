The number $\sqrt{18} + \sqrt{50}$ is equal to:

- $8\sqrt{2}$
- $\sqrt{68}$
- $15\sqrt{2}$
- $8$

## Explanation

Factor a perfect square out of each radicand by writing $18 = 9 \cdot 2$ and $50 = 25 \cdot 2$. The rule for the **square root of a product** gives $\sqrt{18} = 3\sqrt{2}$ and $\sqrt{50} = 5\sqrt{2}$, so the sum is $8\sqrt{2}$. The result $\sqrt{68}$ adds the radicands, but the square root of their sum differs from the sum of their square roots. The result $15\sqrt{2}$ multiplies the coefficients instead of adding them, and $8$ drops the factor $\sqrt{2}$.
