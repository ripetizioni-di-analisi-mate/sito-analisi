The value of the expression $-3^{2} + (-3)^{2} - (-3)^{3}$ is:

- $45$
- $-27$
- $27$
- $9$

## Explanation

In $-3^{2}$, the exponent applies only to $3$, so $-3^{2} = -9$, while $(-3)^{2} = 9$ because the exponent is even. The cube of $-3$ is negative, $(-3)^{3} = -27$, so subtracting it is equivalent to adding $27$. The expression therefore equals $-9 + 9 + 27 = 27$. The result $45$ treats $-3^{2}$ as $(-3)^{2}$, while $-27$ overlooks that the minus sign in front of $(-3)^{3}$ changes the sign of the cube. The result $9$ evaluates $(-3)^{3}$ as $-9$ by multiplying the base by the exponent.
