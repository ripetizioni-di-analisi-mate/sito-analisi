An archive must find the integer $n$ for which $n<\log_3 20<n+1$. Which check directly identifies the two boundaries?

- $2^3<20<3^3$
- $3^2<20<3^3$
- $3^1<20<3^3$
- $3^2<20<3^4$

## Explanation

Since the base-three logarithm is increasing, two consecutive powers of three must straddle $20$. They are $9$ and $27$, with exponents $2$ and $3$. The other pairs change base or leave an intervening exponent.
