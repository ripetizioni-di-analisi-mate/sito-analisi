The number $\left( \dfrac{3}{4} \right)^{-2} \cdot \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{3}$ is equal to:

- $\dfrac{9}{128}$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$
- $\dfrac{8}{9}$
- $\dfrac{128}{9}$

## Explanation

A negative exponent indicates the reciprocal, so $\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^{-2} = \left(\dfrac{4}{3}\right)^{2} = \dfrac{16}{9}$. Also, $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{3} = \dfrac{1}{8}$, and the product is $\dfrac{16}{9} \cdot \dfrac{1}{8} = \dfrac{2}{9}$. The result $\dfrac{9}{128}$ ignores the minus sign in the exponent, while $\dfrac{128}{9}$ also takes the reciprocal of the second factor as if its exponent were negative too. The result $\dfrac{8}{9}$ multiplies by $\dfrac{1}{2}$ only once.
