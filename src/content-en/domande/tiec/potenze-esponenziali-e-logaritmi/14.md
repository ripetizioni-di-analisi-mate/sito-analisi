Between which two consecutive integers does the number $\log_{3} 20$ lie?

- $1$ and $2$
- $2$ and $3$
- $3$ and $4$
- $6$ and $7$

## Explanation

Since the base $3$ is greater than $1$, the function $y = \log_{3} x$ is increasing, so it is enough to find two consecutive powers of $3$ between which $20$ lies. We have $3^{2} = 9$ and $3^{3} = 27$, hence $3^{2} < 20 < 3^{3}$. Taking $\log_{3}$ of the three members gives $2 < \log_{3} 20 < 3$. The number $\log_{3} 20$ therefore lies between $2$ and $3$.
