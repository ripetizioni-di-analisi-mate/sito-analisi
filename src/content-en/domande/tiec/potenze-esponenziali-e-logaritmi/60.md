If $\log_{a} 16 = -4$, with $a > 0$ and $a \neq 1$, the base $a$ is:

- $2$
- $-2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-4$

## Explanation

By the definition of a logarithm, $\log_{a} 16 = -4$ is equivalent to $a^{-4} = 16$, or $\dfrac{1}{a^{4}} = 16$. Thus $a^{4} = \dfrac{1}{16}$, and since the base is positive, $a = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The value $2$ overlooks the minus sign in the exponent, since $2^{-4} = \dfrac{1}{16}$. The values $-2$ and $-4$ do not satisfy the condition $a > 0$.
