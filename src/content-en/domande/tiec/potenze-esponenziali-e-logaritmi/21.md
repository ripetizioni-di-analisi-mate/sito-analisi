Which fourth root is equal to the product $\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt[4]{8}$?

- $\sqrt[4]{64}$
- $\sqrt[4]{10}$
- $\sqrt[4]{32}$
- $\sqrt[4]{16}$

## Explanation

To multiply two roots, they are brought to the same index. By the invariance property, $\sqrt{2} = \sqrt[4]{2^{2}} = \sqrt[4]{4}$. The two roots now have index $4$, so by the rule for the root of a product $\sqrt[4]{4} \cdot \sqrt[4]{8} = \sqrt[4]{32}$. The product is therefore equal to $\sqrt[4]{32}$.
