A procedure will replace $\sqrt{2}\sqrt[4]{8}$ with a single fourth root before making estimates. Which radicand should it use?

- $64$
- $10$
- $32$
- $16$

## Explanation

To match the indices, write $\sqrt{2}=\sqrt[4]{4}$. The resulting radicands can now be multiplied. The single radicand is therefore $4\cdot8=32$.
