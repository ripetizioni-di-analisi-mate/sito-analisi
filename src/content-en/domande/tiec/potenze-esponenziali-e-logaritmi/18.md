If $3^{a} = 5$, the value of $9^{a+1}$ is:

- $45$
- $225$
- $34$
- $75$

## Explanation

By the product rule for powers with the same base, $9^{a+1} = 9^{a} \cdot 9$. Since $9^{a} = \left(3^{2}\right)^{a} = \left(3^{a}\right)^{2} = 25$, the result is $9^{a+1} = 25 \cdot 9 = 225$. The result $45$ replaces $9^{a}$ with $5$ as if $9^{a}$ and $3^{a}$ were equal. The result $34$ adds $25$ and $9$ instead of multiplying them, while $75$ writes $9^{a+1}$ as $3^{2a+1}$ instead of $3^{2a+2}$.
