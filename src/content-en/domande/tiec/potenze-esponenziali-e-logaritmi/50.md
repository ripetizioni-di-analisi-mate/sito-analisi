A table gives $\log_{10}2\approx0.301$ and $\log_{10}3\approx0.477$. What is the approximate value of $\log_{10}1.5$?

- $0.778$
- $0.176$
- $1.585$
- $-0.176$

## Explanation

Write $1.5$ as $3/2$. The quotient rule calls for subtraction in the order numerator minus denominator. The approximation is $0.477-0.301=0.176$.
