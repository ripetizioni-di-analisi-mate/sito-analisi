A table gives $\log_{10}2\approx0.301$ and $\log_{10}3\approx0.477$. What is the approximate value of $\log_{10}1.5$?

- $0.778$
- $0.176$
- $1.585$
- $-0.176$

## Explanation

Since $1.5 = \dfrac{3}{2}$, by the quotient rule for logarithms $\log_{10} 1.5 = \log_{10} 3 - \log_{10} 2$. The logarithm of the denominator is subtracted from that of the numerator, and the opposite order would give a negative value. Hence $\log_{10} 1.5 \approx 0.477 - 0.301 = 0.176$.
