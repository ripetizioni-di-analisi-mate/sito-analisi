Which value of $x$ solves the equation $2^{x} \cdot 5^{x} = 0.001$?

- $x = 3$
- $x = -2$
- $x = -3$
- $x = -\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

By the rule for the power of a product, $2^{x} \cdot 5^{x} = (2 \cdot 5)^{x} = 10^{x}$. The right side is $0.001 = 10^{-3}$, so the equation becomes $10^{x} = 10^{-3}$. Two powers of $10$ are equal only if they have the same exponent, hence $x = -3$.
