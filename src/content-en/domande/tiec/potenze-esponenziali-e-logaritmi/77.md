An archive occupies $80$ memory units. Two updates each double its size and one compression halves it. Each of the three operations must be performed once. After each operation one checks that the archive occupies at most $250$ units. Which order fails even though its theoretical final size is the same as in the other orders?

- All orders finish without exceeding the limit
- Compression before both doublings
- Both doublings before compression
- Compression between the two doublings

## Explanation

The combined multiplier is $2^2\cdot2^{-1}=2$, so in principle every order takes the size from $80$ to $160$ units. Two immediate doublings, however, pass through $80\cdot2^2=320$, beyond capacity. In the other orders compression happens in time and the maximum size is $160$.
