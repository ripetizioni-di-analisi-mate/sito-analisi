On an axis with coordinate $t=\log_{10}x$, a point moves two units to the left. How does the positive value of $x$ change?

- It is multiplied by $100$
- It is divided by $2$
- It is divided by $100$
- It decreases by $2$

## Explanation

The new coordinate is $t-2$. Returning to the original values gives $10^{t-2}=10^t/100$. The shift is additive on the logarithmic scale and multiplicative on the scale of $x$.
