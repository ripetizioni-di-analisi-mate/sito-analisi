The value of the expression $\log_{10} 0.001 + \log_{10} 10\,000$ is:

- $7$
- $-1$
- $1$
- $10$

## Explanation

We have $0.001 = 10^{-3}$ and $10\,000 = 10^{4}$, so the **special values of logarithms** give $-3$ and $4$ for the two terms. Their sum is $-3 + 4 = 1$. The result $7$ assigns $\log_{10} 0.001$ the value $3$, overlooking that the argument is less than $1$, and $-1$ switches the signs of the two logarithms. The result $10$ writes the sum as $\log_{10} (0.001 \cdot 10\,000) = \log_{10} 10$ and then gives the argument in place of the logarithm.
