If the base-$10$ logarithm of a positive number $x$ decreases by $2$, how does the number $x$ change?

- It is multiplied by $100$
- It is divided by $2$
- It is divided by $100$
- It decreases by $2$

## Explanation

Let $t = \log_{10} x$, that is $x = 10^{t}$. The new number has logarithm $t - 2$, so it equals $10^{t-2}$. By the quotient rule for powers, $10^{t-2} = \dfrac{10^{t}}{10^{2}} = \dfrac{x}{100}$. Hence the number $x$ is divided by $100$.
