Which of the following statements about the number $2^{100}$ is true?

- $10^{29} < 2^{100} < 10^{30}$
- $10^{30} < 2^{100} < 10^{31}$
- $10^{9} < 2^{100} < 10^{10}$
- $10^{50} < 2^{100} < 10^{51}$

## Explanation

By the power-of-a-power rule, $2^{100} = \left(2^{10}\right)^{10} = 1024^{10}$. Since $1024 > 1000$, we have $1024^{10} > 1000^{10} = 10^{30}$, so $2^{100} > 10^{30}$. Also, $1024^{10} = 10^{30} \cdot 1.024^{10}$, and the factor $1.024^{10}$ is approximately $1.27$, which is less than $10$. Therefore $2^{100} < 10^{31}$. The interval from $10^{29}$ to $10^{30}$ overlooks that $1024$ is greater than $1000$. The intervals with exponents $9$ and $50$ divide the exponent $100$ by $10$ or by $2$.
