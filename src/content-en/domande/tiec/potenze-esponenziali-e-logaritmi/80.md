Each year, the value of a machine decreases by $20\%$ of its value the previous year. After how many years is the machine worth $51.2\%$ of its initial value?

- $2$
- $4$
- $3$
- $2.44$

## Explanation

A decrease of $20\%$ multiplies the value by $0.8$ each year, so after $n$ years the initial value has been multiplied by $0.8^{n}$. Solve $0.8^{n} = 0.512$. Since $0.8^{3} = 0.512$, the solution is $n = 3$. The result $2.44$ divides the total loss of $48.8\%$ by $20\%$ as if the percentage decreases could be added. After $2$ years the machine is worth $64\%$ of its initial value, and after $4$ years it is worth $40.96\%$.
