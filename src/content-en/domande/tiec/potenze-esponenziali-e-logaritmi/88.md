Which of the following inequalities holds for every real $x$?

- $3^{x} > 1$
- $3^{x} < 0$
- $3^{-x} \leq 1$
- $\left( \dfrac{1}{3} \right)^{x} > -1$

## Explanation

A power with a positive base is positive for every real exponent, so $\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^{x} > 0$ for every $x$. Since every positive number is greater than $-1$, the inequality $\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^{x} > -1$ **holds for every real $x$**. For the same reason, the inequality $3^{x} < 0$ has no solution. The inequality $3^{x} > 1$ is equivalent to $3^{x} > 3^{0}$, so it holds only for $x > 0$. Finally, $3^{-x} \leq 1$ is equivalent to $-x \leq 0$, or $x \geq 0$. For example, $x = -1$ gives $3 \leq 1$, which is false.
