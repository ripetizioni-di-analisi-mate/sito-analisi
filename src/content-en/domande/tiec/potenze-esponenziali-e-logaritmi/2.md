The value of $\log_{5} \dfrac{1}{25}$ is:

- $2$
- $-2$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-5$

## Explanation

By the **definition of a logarithm**, $\log_{5} \dfrac{1}{25}$ is the exponent to which $5$ must be raised to obtain $\dfrac{1}{25}$. Since $\dfrac{1}{25} = \dfrac{1}{5^{2}} = 5^{-2}$, the exponent is $-2$. The value $2$ overlooks that the argument is the reciprocal of $25$, and $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ switches the base and argument, since $\log_{\frac{1}{25}} 5 = -\dfrac{1}{2}$. The value $-5$ comes from dividing $25$ by $5$ and adding a minus sign.
