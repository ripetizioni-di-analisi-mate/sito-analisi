The number $\dfrac{\left( 4 \cdot 10^{-3} \right)^{2}}{8 \cdot 10^{-9}}$ is equal to:

- $5 \cdot 10^{2}$
- $2 \cdot 10^{6}$
- $2 \cdot 10^{3}$
- $2 \cdot 10^{-15}$

## Explanation

By the power-of-a-product rule, $\left(4 \cdot 10^{-3}\right)^{2} = 4^{2} \cdot 10^{-6} = 16 \cdot 10^{-6}$. The quotient is $\dfrac{16}{8} \cdot 10^{-6 - (-9)} = 2 \cdot 10^{3}$. The result $5 \cdot 10^{2}$ squares only the power of $10$ and leaves the coefficient $4$ unchanged, while $2 \cdot 10^{6}$ squares only the coefficient. The result $2 \cdot 10^{-15}$ adds the exponents instead of subtracting them.
