A square has side length $3\cdot10^{-2}$ km. If its side is converted to metres before calculating the area, what is the area in square metres?

- $9\cdot10^{-1}$
- $6\cdot10^1$
- $9\cdot10^2$
- $9\cdot10^4$

## Explanation

One kilometre is $10^3$ metres, so the side is $3\cdot10^1$ metres. The area is the square of this value. Both the coefficient $3$ and the power of ten are squared.
