The number $\sqrt{2^{10} + 2^{10}}$ is equal to:

- $1024$
- $64$
- $32\sqrt{2}$
- $32$

## Explanation

The sum of two equal powers is twice either one, so $2^{10} + 2^{10} = 2 \cdot 2^{10} = 2^{11}$. Taking the square root halves the exponent and gives $2^{\frac{11}{2}} = 2^{5} \cdot 2^{\frac{1}{2}} = 32\sqrt{2}$. The result $1024$ writes the sum as $2^{20}$ by adding the exponents. The result $64$ takes the square root of each term, but the square root of their sum differs from the sum of their square roots. The result $32$ counts only one of the two powers.
