Two square tiles of side $32$ cm are recast as a single square tile of the same total area. By which factor is the original side multiplied to obtain the new side?

- $2$
- $4$
- $\sqrt{2}$
- $1/\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The new area is twice the area of one tile. Multiplying the side by a positive factor $k$ multiplies area by $k^2$. Thus $k^2=2$ and $k=\sqrt2$.
