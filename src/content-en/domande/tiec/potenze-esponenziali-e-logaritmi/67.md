What is the value of $\dfrac{1}{\log_{2} 6} + \dfrac{1}{\log_{3} 6}$?

- $1$
- $5$
- $\log_{6} 5$
- $2$

## Explanation

The special case of the change-of-base formula gives $\dfrac{1}{\log_{a} b} = \log_{b} a$, so the terms are $\log_{6} 2$ and $\log_{6} 3$. By the product rule for logarithms, their sum is $\log_{6} (2 \cdot 3) = \log_{6} 6 = 1$. The result $\log_{6} 5$ adds the arguments $2$ and $3$ instead of multiplying them, and $5$ is the sum of the bases $2$ and $3$. The result $2$ assigns the value $1$ to each term.
