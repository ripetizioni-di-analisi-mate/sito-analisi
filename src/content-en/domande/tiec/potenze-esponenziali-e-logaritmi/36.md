Which of the following inequalities is true?

- $0.8^{5} > 0.8^{3}$
- $\left( \dfrac{1}{3} \right)^{-2} < \left( \dfrac{1}{3} \right)^{-1}$
- $0.8^{5} < 0.8^{3}$
- $0.5^{-3} < 0.5^{2}$

## Explanation

A power with a base between $0$ and $1$ decreases as the exponent increases, since it involves multiplying more times by a number less than $1$. Since $5 > 3$, we have $0.8^{5} < 0.8^{3}$, and indeed $0.8^{3} = 0.512$ and $0.8^{5} = 0.32768$. For the same reason, $\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^{-2} = 9$ is greater than $\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^{-1} = 3$, and $0.5^{-3} = 8$ is greater than $0.5^{2} = 0.25$.
