A quantity follows $Q(t)=Q_0a^t$ with $Q_0>0$ and $a>1$. What does $\log_a 3$ mean in this model?

- The absolute increase during the third interval
- The growth factor over three intervals
- The value reached after three intervals
- The time required to triple $Q_0$

## Explanation

For the tripling time set $Q(t)=3Q_0$. Dividing by $Q_0>0$ leaves $a^t=3$. The logarithm is the required exponent, and in this model that exponent represents time.
