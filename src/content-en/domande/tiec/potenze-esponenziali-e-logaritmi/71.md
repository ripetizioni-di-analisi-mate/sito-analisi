A detector measures the level $L=10\log_{10}(I/I_0)$. It reads $30$ with only the background source on and $40$ with both the background and a new source on. Assuming intensities add, what is the ratio of the new source’s intensity alone to the background intensity?

- $10$
- $4/3$
- $1$
- $9$

## Explanation

A level increase of $10$ multiplies the total intensity by $10$, since $10\log_{10}(I_\text{total}/I_\text{background})=10$. Subtract the background contribution: $I_\text{new}=10I_\text{background}-I_\text{background}=9I_\text{background}$.
