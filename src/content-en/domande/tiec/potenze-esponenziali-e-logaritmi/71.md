A sound level is defined by $L = 10 \log_{10} \dfrac{I}{I_{0}}$, where $I$ is the sound intensity and $I_{0}$ is a fixed reference value. If the intensity $I$ increases by a factor of $100$, the level $L$:

- increases by $100$
- increases by a factor of $100$
- increases by $2$
- increases by $20$

## Explanation

With the new intensity $100\,I$, the level is $10 \log_{10} \dfrac{100\,I}{I_{0}}$. By the product rule for logarithms, $\log_{10} \dfrac{100\,I}{I_{0}} = \log_{10} 100 + \log_{10} \dfrac{I}{I_{0}} = 2 + \log_{10} \dfrac{I}{I_{0}}$. Multiplying by $10$ gives $L + 20$, so the level **increases by $20$**. The answer giving an increase of $2$ overlooks the factor $10$ in front of the logarithm. The answers giving an increase of $100$ or a value $100$ times as large treat the logarithm as if it were proportional to its argument.
