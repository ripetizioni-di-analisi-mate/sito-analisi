The product $\log_{2} 3 \cdot \log_{3} 4 \cdot \log_{4} 8$ equals:

- $2$
- $3$
- $\log_{2} 96$
- $\log_{24} 96$

## Explanation

Use the **change-of-base formula** to write the factors in base $2$ as $\log_{3} 4 = \dfrac{\log_{2} 4}{\log_{2} 3}$ and $\log_{4} 8 = \dfrac{\log_{2} 8}{\log_{2} 4}$. In the product, the factors $\log_{2} 3$ and $\log_{2} 4$ cancel, leaving $\log_{2} 8 = 3$. The result $\log_{2} 96$ applies the product rule for logarithms to a product of logarithms, although the rule applies to the sum of logarithms with the same base. The result $\log_{24} 96$ multiplies the bases together and the arguments together, and $2$ stops at $\log_{2} 4$.
