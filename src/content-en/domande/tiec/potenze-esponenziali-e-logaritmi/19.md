The number $81^{\frac{3}{4}}$ is equal to:

- $27$
- $\dfrac{243}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{27}$
- $9$

## Explanation

By the definition of a **power with a fractional exponent**, the denominator $4$ calls for the fourth root and the numerator $3$ calls for cubing. Since $3^{4} = 81$, the fourth root of $81$ is $3$, and its cube is $27$. The result $\dfrac{243}{4}$ multiplies the base by the exponent, and $\dfrac{1}{27}$ treats the exponent as negative. The result $9$ is $81^{\frac{1}{2}}$, the square root of $81$.
