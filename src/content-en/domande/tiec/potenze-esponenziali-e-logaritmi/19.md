A cube has volume $V>0$. Which power of $V$ gives the area of one face?

- $V^{\frac{2}{3}}$
- $V^2$
- $V^{\frac{1}{3}}$
- $V^{\frac{3}{2}}$

## Explanation

If the edge length is $s$, the volume is $s^3$, so $s = V^{\frac{1}{3}}$. A face is a square with side length $s$. Its area is $\left( V^{\frac{1}{3}} \right)^{2} = V^{\frac{2}{3}}$.
