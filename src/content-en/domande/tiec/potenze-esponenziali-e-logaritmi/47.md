The graph of the function $y = \left( \dfrac{1}{4} \right)^{x}$ is the reflection across the $y$-axis of the graph of which function?

- $y = -\left( \dfrac{1}{4} \right)^{x}$
- $y = 4^{x}$
- $y = \log_{4} x$
- $y = \log_{\frac{1}{4}} x$

## Explanation

To reflect the graph of a function across the $y$-axis, replace $x$ with $-x$. Since $\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^{-x} = 4^{x}$, the reflected graph is that of $y = 4^{x}$. The graph of $y = -\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^{x}$ is the reflection across the $x$-axis. The graph of $y = \log_{\frac{1}{4}} x$ is the reflection across the line $y = x$, and the graph of $y = \log_{4} x$ is the reflection of $y = 4^{x}$ across the same line.
