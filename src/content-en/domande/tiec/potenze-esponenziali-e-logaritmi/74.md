Which of the following equations has no real solution?

- $5^{x} = 0.2$
- $5^{x} + 2 = 1$
- $5^{-x} = 3$
- $5^{x} - 1 = 0$

## Explanation

The equation $5^{x} + 2 = 1$ is equivalent to $5^{x} = -1$ and **has no solution** because a power with a positive base is always positive. The other three equations have positive right sides, so each has a solution. In fact, $5^{x} = 0.2 = 5^{-1}$ gives $x = -1$, $5^{-x} = 3$ gives $x = -\log_{5} 3$, and $5^{x} = 1$ gives $x = 0$.
