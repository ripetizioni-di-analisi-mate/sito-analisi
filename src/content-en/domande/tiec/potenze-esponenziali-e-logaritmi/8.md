What is the solution of the equation $3^{x+1} - 3^{x-1} = 24$?

- $x = 1$
- $x = 4$
- $x = 1 + \log_{3} 12$
- $x = 2$

## Explanation

The two powers have the same base and their exponents differ by $2$. Factor out the power with the smaller exponent, $3^{x-1}$. Since $3^{x+1} = 3^{2} \cdot 3^{x-1}$, the equation becomes $(9 - 1) \cdot 3^{x-1} = 24$, or $3^{x-1} = 3$. Equating exponents gives $x - 1 = 1$, hence $x = 2$. The result $x = 1$ sets the unknown equal to $1$ instead of setting the exponent $x - 1$ equal to it, and $x = 4$ confuses the value $3$ of the power with its exponent. The result $x = 1 + \log_{3} 12$ writes $3^{x+1}$ as $3 \cdot 3^{x-1}$ as if the exponents differed by $1$.
