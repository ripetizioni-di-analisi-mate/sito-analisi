A bar of length $6$ is cut into positive lengths $x$ and $6-x$. For which cut positions is the index $\log_2 x$ of the first part less than the index of the second?

- $3<x<6$
- $0<x\leq3$
- $0<x<3$
- $x<3$

## Explanation

Both pieces must be positive, so $0<x<6$. The base-two logarithm is increasing and orders the pieces exactly as their lengths do. We need $x<6-x$, with the midpoint excluded.
