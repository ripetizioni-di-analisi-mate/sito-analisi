A signal $S(x)=(1/2)^{x^2}$ must exceed $1/16$. A technician sets the filter to $|x|>2$. Which check gives the correct direction and treats the threshold correctly?

- $S(0)=0$ and $S(\pm2)=1/16$, so use $|x|\geq2$
- $S(0)=1$ and $S(\pm2)=1/16$, so use $|x|>2$
- $S(0)=1$ and $S(\pm2)=1/16$, so use $|x|\leq2$
- $S(0)=1$ and $S(\pm2)=1/16$, so use $|x|<2$

## Explanation

A power with base $1/2$ decreases as the exponent $x^2$ increases. To exceed the value at $x^2=4$ we therefore need $x^2<4$. Equality at the threshold excludes both endpoints.
