The inequality $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{x^{2}} > \dfrac{1}{16}$ holds if and only if:

- $|x| > 2$
- $|x| \geq 2$
- $|x| \leq 2$
- $|x| < 2$

## Explanation

Since $\dfrac{1}{16} = \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{4}$, the inequality reads $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{x^{2}} > \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{4}$. The base $\dfrac{1}{2}$ lies between $0$ and $1$, so the inequality sign reverses and the inequality is equivalent to $x^{2} < 4$. The solutions are therefore $-2 < x < 2$, that is $|x| < 2$. The endpoints $x = \pm 2$ are excluded, because there the two sides are equal.
