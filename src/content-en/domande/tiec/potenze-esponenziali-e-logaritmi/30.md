The number $0.00065 \cdot 10^{8}$, written in scientific notation, is:

- $6.5 \cdot 10^{12}$
- $6.5 \cdot 10^{3}$
- $6.5 \cdot 10^{4}$
- $6.5 \cdot 10^{5}$

## Explanation

To write $0.00065$ in scientific notation, move the decimal point four places to the right, giving $0.00065 = 6.5 \cdot 10^{-4}$. Multiplying by $10^{8}$ gives $6.5 \cdot 10^{-4+8} = 6.5 \cdot 10^{4}$, or $65\,000$. The result $6.5 \cdot 10^{12}$ writes $0.00065$ with exponent $+4$ instead of $-4$. The results $6.5 \cdot 10^{3}$ and $6.5 \cdot 10^{5}$ come from moving the decimal point one place too few or one too many.
