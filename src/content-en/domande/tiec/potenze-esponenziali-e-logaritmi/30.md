Which scientific-notation form is obtained by rounding the number $9.96 \cdot 10^{4}$ to two significant figures?

- $9.9 \cdot 10^{4}$
- $1.0 \cdot 10^{4}$
- $1.0 \cdot 10^{5}$
- $1.1 \cdot 10^{5}$

## Explanation

The first two significant figures of $9.96$ are $9$ and $9$, and the next digit is $6$. Since $6 \geq 5$, the rounding is upwards, so $9.96$ rounded to tenths becomes $10.0$. Hence $9.96 \cdot 10^{4}$ becomes $10 \cdot 10^{4} = 1 \cdot 10^{5}$. With two significant figures, and with the coefficient between $1$ and $10$, the number is written $1.0 \cdot 10^{5}$.
