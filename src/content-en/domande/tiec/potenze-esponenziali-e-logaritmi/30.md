A counter reads $9.96\cdot10^4$. What is its reading rounded to two significant figures in scientific notation?

- $9.9\cdot10^4$
- $1.0\cdot10^4$
- $1.0\cdot10^5$
- $1.1\cdot10^5$

## Explanation

The $6$ rounds $9.96$ up to $10$ at the tenths place. The carry moves the first significant digit to the next power of ten. Writing $1.0\cdot10^5$ explicitly keeps two significant figures.
