An investment of $5000$ euros earns $4\%$ annual compound interest. After $2$ years, the investment is worth:

- $5400$ euros
- $5200$ euros
- $10\,400$ euros
- $5408$ euros

## Explanation

With compound interest, the value of the investment is multiplied by $1.04$ each year, so after $2$ years it is $5000 \cdot 1.04^{2}$. Since $1.04^{2} = 1.0816$, the investment is worth $5000 \cdot 1.0816 = 5408$ euros. The result of $5400$ euros adds the first year's interest of $200$ euros twice, as if interest were never added to the principal. The result of $5200$ euros accounts for only one year, while $10\,400$ euros comes from multiplying by $2$ instead of squaring.
