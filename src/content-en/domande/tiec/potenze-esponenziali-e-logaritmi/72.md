A sensor produces $F(x)=2^{x^2-x}$. Which input, other than $x=3$, gives exactly the same reading as $3$?

- $x=-3$
- $x=2$
- $x=-2$
- $x=0$

## Explanation

At $x=3$ the exponent is $6$. An equal reading requires the same exponent, so $x^2-x-6=0$. The factors are $(x-3)(x+2)$ and the other input is $-2$.
