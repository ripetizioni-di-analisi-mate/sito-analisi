Using the approximation $2^{10} \approx 10^{3}$, the number $2^{40}$ is approximately:

- $10^{7}$
- $10^{120}$
- $10^{4}$
- $10^{12}$

## Explanation

By the **power-of-a-power rule**, $2^{40} = \left(2^{10}\right)^{4}$. Substituting $2^{10} \approx 10^{3}$ gives $2^{40} \approx \left(10^{3}\right)^{4} = 10^{12}$. The actual value is approximately $1.1 \cdot 10^{12}$. The result $10^{7}$ adds the exponents $3$ and $4$ instead of multiplying them, and $10^{120}$ multiplies $3$ by $40$ instead of $4$. The result $10^{4}$ counts the groups of ten factors of $2$ without accounting for the fact that each group is worth approximately $10^{3}$.
