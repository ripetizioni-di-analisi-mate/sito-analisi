How many times as large as $2 \cdot 10^{-5}$ is $4 \cdot 10^{-3}$?

- $200$
- $2$
- $20$
- $2000$

## Explanation

To find the ratio, divide the coefficients and the powers of $10$ separately, giving $\dfrac{4 \cdot 10^{-3}}{2 \cdot 10^{-5}} = 2 \cdot 10^{-3 - (-5)} = 2 \cdot 10^{2}$. The number $4 \cdot 10^{-3}$ is therefore $200$ times as large. The result $2$ overlooks the powers of $10$, while $20$ and $2000$ are off by one in the exponent $-3 - (-5) = 2$.
