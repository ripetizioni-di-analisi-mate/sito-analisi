Which of the following numbers is written in scientific notation?

- $7.2 \cdot 10^{-4}$
- $0.72 \cdot 10^{5}$
- $72 \cdot 10^{3}$
- $10 \cdot 10^{2}$

## Explanation

A number is in **scientific notation** when it has the form $k \cdot 10^{n}$, with $1 \leq k < 10$ and an integer exponent $n$. The coefficient $7.2$ satisfies the condition, and the negative exponent $-4$ is allowed. The coefficient $0.72$ is less than $1$, and the coefficient $72$ is greater than $10$. The coefficient $10$ also fails to satisfy $k < 10$, so $10 \cdot 10^{2}$ is written as $1 \cdot 10^{3}$ in scientific notation.
