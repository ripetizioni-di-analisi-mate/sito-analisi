Two integers $p$ and $q$ satisfy the equation $2^{p} \cdot 8 = 2^{q}$. Which relation links $p$ and $q$?

- $q - p = 3$
- $p - q = 3$
- $q - p = 8$
- $q = 8p$

## Explanation

Since $8 = 2^{3}$, the left side is $2^{p} \cdot 2^{3}$. By the product rule for powers with the same base, $2^{p} \cdot 2^{3} = 2^{p+3}$, so the equation becomes $2^{p+3} = 2^{q}$. Two powers of $2$ are equal only if they have the same exponent, hence $q = p + 3$, that is $q - p = 3$.
