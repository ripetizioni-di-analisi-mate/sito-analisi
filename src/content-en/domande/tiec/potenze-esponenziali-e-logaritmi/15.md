An archive contains $2^p$ identical folders with $8$ files each. If the total number of files is $2^q$, how are the exponents related?

- $q-p=3$
- $p-q=3$
- $q-p=8$
- $q=8p$

## Explanation

The total is the number of folders times the number of files per folder. Since $8=2^3$, this gives $2^q=2^{p+3}$. Adding three to the exponent multiplies the quantity by eight.
