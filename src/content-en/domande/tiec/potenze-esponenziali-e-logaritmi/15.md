The number $\dfrac{6^{5}}{2^{4} \cdot 3^{3}}$ is equal to:

- $18$
- $\dfrac{1}{36}$
- $6$
- $9$

## Explanation

By the **power-of-a-product rule**, $6^{5} = (2 \cdot 3)^{5} = 2^{5} \cdot 3^{5}$. Simplify the powers of $2$ and the powers of $3$ separately to obtain $2^{5-4} \cdot 3^{5-3} = 2 \cdot 9 = 18$. The result $\dfrac{1}{36}$ writes the denominator as $6^{4+3} = 6^{7}$, although a product of powers with different bases and exponents does not reduce to a power of $6$. The result $9$ drops the remaining factor $2$, and $6$ treats the denominator as $6^{4}$.
