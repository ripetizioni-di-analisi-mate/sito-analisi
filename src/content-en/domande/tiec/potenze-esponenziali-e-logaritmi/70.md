The number $\sqrt[6]{27}$ is equal to:

- $\sqrt{3}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $\sqrt[3]{3}$

## Explanation

Write the radicand as a power, $27 = 3^{3}$. To **simplify the radical without changing its value**, divide both the index and the exponent of the radicand by $3$, giving $\sqrt[6]{3^{3}} = \sqrt[2]{3^{1}} = \sqrt{3}$. Using fractional exponents gives the same result, since $\left(3^{3}\right)^{\frac{1}{6}} = 3^{\frac{3}{6}} = 3^{\frac{1}{2}}$. The result $3$ divides the radicand $27$ by $3$ instead of dividing its exponent, and $\dfrac{9}{2}$ divides the radicand by the index. The result $\sqrt[3]{3}$ divides the index by $2$ and the exponent by $3$, two different numbers.
