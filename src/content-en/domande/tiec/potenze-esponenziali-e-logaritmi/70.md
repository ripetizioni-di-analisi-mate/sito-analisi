Which integer is closest to $\sqrt{50}$? To decide without a calculator, compare $50$ with the square of the midpoint between the neighbouring integers.

- $7$
- $6$
- $8$
- $9$

## Explanation

From $7^2=49$ and $8^2=64$ the root lies between $7$ and $8$. The midpoint is $7.5$, whose square is $56.25$. Since $50$ is below this threshold, its root is closer to $7$.
