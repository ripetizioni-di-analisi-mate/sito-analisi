How many digits does the number $2 \cdot 10^{12}$ have when written out in full?

- $13$
- $24$
- $14$
- $12$

## Explanation

The number $10^{12}$ is written as the digit $1$ followed by twelve zeros, so it has thirteen digits. Multiplying by $2$ turns the first digit into $2$ and leaves twelve zeros, because $2 \cdot 1 = 2$ is still a one-digit number. The number $2 \cdot 10^{12} = 2\,000\,000\,000\,000$ therefore has $13$ digits.
