A printer can produce labels numbered from $1$ up to and including $N=2\cdot10^{12}$. How many digits are in the largest number?

- $13$
- $24$
- $14$
- $12$

## Explanation

The number lies between $10^{12}$ and $10^{13}$. The former is a one followed by twelve zeros, which has thirteen digits. The factor of two changes only the leading digit.
