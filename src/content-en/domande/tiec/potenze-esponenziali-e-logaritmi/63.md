The value of $\log_{5} \left( 25\sqrt{5} \right)$ is:

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $1$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

By the rule for the **logarithm of a product**, $\log_{5} \left(25\sqrt{5}\right) = \log_{5} 25 + \log_{5} \sqrt{5}$. The first term equals $2$ because $5^{2} = 25$, and the second equals $\dfrac{1}{2}$ because $\sqrt{5} = 5^{\frac{1}{2}}$. Their sum is $\dfrac{5}{2}$. The result $1$ multiplies $2$ by $\dfrac{1}{2}$ instead of adding them, $3$ assigns $\log_{5} \sqrt{5}$ the value $1$, and $2$ drops the factor $\sqrt{5}$.
