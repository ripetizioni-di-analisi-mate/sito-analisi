Which of the following equations is true for every pair of numbers $a > 0$ and $b > 0$?

- $\log_{10} (a + b) = \log_{10} a \cdot \log_{10} b$
- $\dfrac{\log_{10} a}{\log_{10} b} = \log_{10} (a - b)$
- $\log_{10} (a\,b) = \log_{10} a + \log_{10} b$
- $\left( \log_{10} a \right)^{2} = 2\log_{10} a$

## Explanation

By the rule for the **logarithm of a product**, $\log_{10} (a\,b) = \log_{10} a + \log_{10} b$ for every $a > 0$ and $b > 0$. The equation between $\log_{10} (a + b)$ and $\log_{10} a \cdot \log_{10} b$ is false for $a = b = 10$, since $\log_{10} 20 > 1$ while the product equals $1$. The equation $\left(\log_{10} a\right)^{2} = 2\log_{10} a$ is false for $a = 1000$, since the left side equals $9$ and the right side equals $6$. Finally, for $a = b$, the expression $\log_{10} (a - b) = \log_{10} 0$ is undefined.
