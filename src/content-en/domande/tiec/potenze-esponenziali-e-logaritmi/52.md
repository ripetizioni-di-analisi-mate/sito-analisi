The graph of $f(x)=2^x$ contains the point $(u,7)$. Which point must lie on the graph of the inverse function?

- $(7,u)$
- $(u,-7)$
- $(-u,7)$
- $\left( \dfrac{1}{u}, \dfrac{1}{7} \right)$

## Explanation

The inverse maps an output back to the input that produced it. The relation $2^u=7$ is equivalent to $\log_2 7=u$. Thus the coordinates switch places on the inverse graph.
