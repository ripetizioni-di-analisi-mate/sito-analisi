The number $2^{\log_{2} 5 + 1}$ is equal to:

- $10$
- $6$
- $64$
- $7$

## Explanation

By the product rule for powers with the same base, $2^{\log_{2} 5 + 1} = 2^{\log_{2} 5} \cdot 2^{1}$. A standard logarithm identity gives $2^{\log_{2} 5} = 5$, so the number equals $5 \cdot 2 = 10$. The result $7$ adds $5$ and $2$ instead of multiplying them, and $6$ adds $5$ and the exponent $1$. The result $64$ replaces $\log_{2} 5$ with $5$ to obtain $2^{6}$.
