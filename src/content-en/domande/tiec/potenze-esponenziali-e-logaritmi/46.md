Which of the following statements about the functions $y = 2^{x}$ and $y = 5^{x}$ is true?

- For every real $x$, we have $5^{x} > 2^{x}$
- For $x < 0$, we have $5^{x} > 2^{x}$
- For $x < 0$, we have $2^{x} > 5^{x}$
- The two graphs have no points in common

## Explanation

Both graphs pass through $(0;1)$, since $2^{0} = 5^{0} = 1$. For $x > 0$, the greater base gives the greater power, so $5^{x} > 2^{x}$, while for $x < 0$ the order reverses. For example, for $x = -1$ we have $2^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $5^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{5}$, with $\dfrac{1}{2} > \dfrac{1}{5}$. The statement that $5^{x} > 2^{x}$ for every real $x$ is false for $x \leq 0$, and at $x = 0$ the two values coincide.
