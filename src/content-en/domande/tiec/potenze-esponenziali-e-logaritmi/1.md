To simplify $\dfrac{9^2\cdot3^{-3}}{27}$ without evaluating the powers, which first rewrite gives every factor the same base and preserves the value?

- $\dfrac{3^4\cdot3^{-3}}{3^3}$
- $\dfrac{3^4\cdot3^{-3}}{3^2}$
- $\dfrac{3^4\cdot3^3}{3^3}$
- $\dfrac{3^2\cdot3^{-3}}{3^3}$

## Explanation

The convenient common base is $3$, since $9=3^2$ and $27=3^3$. Squaring $9$ gives $(3^2)^2=3^4$, while the exponent of $3^{-3}$ keeps its negative sign. Combining the powers gives $3^{4-3-3}=3^{-2}$.
