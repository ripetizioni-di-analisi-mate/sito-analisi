The expression $\dfrac{9^{2} \cdot 3^{-3}}{27}$ is equal to:

- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $-9$
- $9$
- $81$

## Explanation

Write all the numbers as powers of $3$, using $9^{2} = \left(3^{2}\right)^{2} = 3^{4}$ and $27 = 3^{3}$. By the **properties of powers**, the resulting exponent is $4 - 3 - 3 = -2$, so the expression equals $3^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{9}$. The result $-9$ treats the negative exponent as a minus sign in front of the number, while $9$ reverses the quotient. The result $81$ treats $3^{-3}$ as $3^{3}$.
