A sheet $80$ cm long is folded in half several times, and each fold halves its length. After how many folds, at least, is the length less than $5$ cm?

- $3$
- $5$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

After $n$ folds the length is $80 \cdot \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{n} = \dfrac{80}{2^{n}}$ centimetres. We look for the smallest integer $n$ such that $\dfrac{80}{2^{n}} < 5$, that is $2^{n} > 16 = 2^{4}$. Since the base $2$ is greater than $1$, the inequality is equivalent to $n > 4$, so at least $5$ folds are needed. After $4$ folds the length is exactly $5$ cm, which is not less than $5$ cm.
