The inequality $5^{x} \leq \dfrac{1}{125}$ holds for:

- $x \geq -3$
- $x \leq -3$
- $x \leq 3$
- $x \leq -\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

Since $\dfrac{1}{125} = 5^{-3}$, the inequality becomes $5^{x} \leq 5^{-3}$. The base $5$ is greater than $1$, so the exponential function is increasing and the inequality sign stays the same when comparing exponents, giving $x \leq -3$. The result $x \geq -3$ reverses the sign even though the base does not require it, $x \leq 3$ drops the minus sign from the reciprocal, and $x \leq -\dfrac{1}{3}$ writes $\dfrac{1}{125}$ as $5^{-\frac{1}{3}}$.
