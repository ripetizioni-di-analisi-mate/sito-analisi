A folding machine halves the width of a strip at each pass, starting at $80$ cm. What is the smallest whole number of passes that makes its width strictly less than $5$ cm?

- $3$
- $5$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The first four folds give $40$, $20$, $10$ and then $5$ cm. Equality at $5$ does not meet the strict threshold. The fifth fold gives $2.5$ cm.
