The index $H(x)=\log_{1/5}x$ drops by one between two readings. What is the ratio of the new value of $x$ to the previous one?

- $1$
- $5$
- $-1$
- $1/5$

## Explanation

If the original index is $h$, the input is $(1/5)^h$. After the drop the input is $(1/5)^{h-1}$. Dividing the latter by the former leaves $(1/5)^{-1}=5$.
