A trader reports the average quantity for two orders of $4$ and $16$ items. A colleague first averages the base-$2$ logarithms and then converts back to quantities. What value does the colleague get?

- $10$
- $8$
- $6$
- $32$

## Explanation

The logarithms are $2$ and $4$. Their mean is $3$, corresponding to $2^3=8$ items. This gives the geometric mean of the two orders, which here differs from the arithmetic mean.
