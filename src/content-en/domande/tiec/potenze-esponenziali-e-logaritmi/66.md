Which of the following numbers is equal to $\log_{7} 11$?

- $\dfrac{\log_{10} 7}{\log_{10} 11}$
- $\log_{10} \dfrac{11}{7}$
- $\dfrac{\log_{10} 11}{\log_{10} 7}$
- $\log_{10} 11 \cdot \log_{10} 7$

## Explanation

By the change-of-base formula with the new base $10$, $\log_{7} 11 = \dfrac{\log_{10} 11}{\log_{10} 7}$. The quotient with the two logarithms swapped is the reciprocal, that is $\log_{11} 7$, which is less than $1$ because $7 < 11$. The number $\log_{10} \dfrac{11}{7}$ equals the difference $\log_{10} 11 - \log_{10} 7$ by the quotient rule for logarithms, so it differs from the quotient of the two logarithms. Hence $\log_{7} 11 = \dfrac{\log_{10} 11}{\log_{10} 7}$.
