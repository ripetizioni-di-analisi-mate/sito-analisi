Two people calculate $\log_7 11$. One uses $\log_{10}11/\log_{10}7$ and the other $\ln 11/\ln 7$. What information is needed to tell whether their exact values agree?

- The numerical value of $\ln 7$
- The difference between $7$ and the natural logarithm base
- No further information, since both are valid changes of base
- The number of digits displayed by the calculators

## Explanation

If $7^y=11$, taking the same logarithm on both sides gives $y\log 7=\log 11$. Any valid base therefore yields the same ratio. Display precision matters only when comparing approximations.
