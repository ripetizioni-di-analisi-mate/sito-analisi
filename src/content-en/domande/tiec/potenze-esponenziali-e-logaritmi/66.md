The number $\dfrac{\log_{10} 81}{\log_{10} 3}$ is equal to:

- $\log_{10} 27$
- $27$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

By the **change-of-base formula**, the quotient $\dfrac{\log_{10} 81}{\log_{10} 3}$ is equal to $\log_{3} 81$. Since $3^{4} = 81$, the quotient equals $4$. Writing $\log_{10} 81 = \log_{10} 3^{4} = 4\log_{10} 3$ gives the same result. The result $\log_{10} 27$ confuses the quotient of two logarithms with the logarithm of the quotient, and $\dfrac{1}{4}$ inverts the ratio.
