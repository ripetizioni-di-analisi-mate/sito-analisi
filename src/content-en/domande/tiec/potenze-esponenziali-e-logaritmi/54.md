The expression $\log_{2} \dfrac{x - 1}{x + 3}$ is defined for:

- $x < -3 \;\vee\; x > 1$
- $-3 < x < 1$
- $x > 1$
- $x \neq -3$

## Explanation

The argument must be positive, so solve the inequality $\dfrac{x - 1}{x + 3} > 0$. The numerator is positive for $x > 1$ and the denominator for $x > -3$. The fraction is positive when the numerator and denominator have the same sign, which occurs for $x < -3$ or $x > 1$. The interval $-3 < x < 1$ is where the fraction is negative. The result $x > 1$ considers only the numerator, while $x \neq -3$ requires only that the fraction be defined.
