When evaluating $\log_2\!\left(\dfrac{x-1}{x+3}\right)$, Sara discards all $x<1$. Which input disproves this exclusion by making the quotient positive?

- $x=-4$
- $x=-3$
- $x=0$
- $x=1$

## Explanation

At $x=-4$ the ratio is $(-5)/(-1)=5$, a valid argument. A negative numerator can therefore work when the denominator is negative too. At $x=-3$ the denominator is zero, at $x=0$ the argument is negative and at $x=1$ the argument is zero.
