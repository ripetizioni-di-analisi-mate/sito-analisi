Which of the following logarithms has a negative value?

- $\log_{\frac{1}{3}} \dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\log_{\frac{1}{3}} \dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\log_{5} 3$
- $\log_{3} \dfrac{4}{5}$

## Explanation

For a base greater than $1$, the logarithm is negative for arguments between $0$ and $1$. For a base between $0$ and $1$, the signs are reversed. The logarithm $\log_{3} \dfrac{4}{5}$ has base $3 > 1$ and argument $\dfrac{4}{5} < 1$, so it is negative. The logarithms $\log_{\frac{1}{3}} \dfrac{4}{5}$ and $\log_{\frac{1}{3}} \dfrac{1}{5}$ have bases and arguments that are both less than $1$, so they are positive. Finally, $\log_{5} 3$ is positive because its base and argument are greater than $1$.
