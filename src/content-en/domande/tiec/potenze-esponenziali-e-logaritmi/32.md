The sum $3 \cdot 10^{5} + 4 \cdot 10^{4}$, written in scientific notation, is:

- $3.4 \cdot 10^{5}$
- $7 \cdot 10^{5}$
- $7 \cdot 10^{9}$
- $7 \cdot 10^{4}$

## Explanation

The properties of powers apply to products and quotients. To add, write both terms using the same power of $10$. Since $4 \cdot 10^{4} = 0.4 \cdot 10^{5}$, their sum is $(3 + 0.4) \cdot 10^{5} = 3.4 \cdot 10^{5}$, or $340\,000$. The result $7 \cdot 10^{5}$ adds the coefficients as if the powers of $10$ were equal, and $7 \cdot 10^{9}$ also adds the exponents as in a product. The result $7 \cdot 10^{4}$ adds the coefficients and keeps the smaller exponent.
