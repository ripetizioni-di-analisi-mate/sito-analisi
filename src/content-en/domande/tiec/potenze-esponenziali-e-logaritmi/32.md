The lengths $d=2\cdot10^{-4}$ metres and $e=8\cdot10^{-4}$ metres are marked on a scale where equal distances represent equal ratios. Which length lies halfway between the marks?

- $4\cdot10^{-4}$ m
- $5\cdot10^{-4}$ m
- $6\cdot10^{-4}$ m
- $3\cdot10^{-4}$ m

## Explanation

The length increases by a factor of $4$ between the endpoints. Each half must multiply it by the same positive factor, which is $\sqrt4=2$. The arithmetic mean would apply to a scale with equal differences.
