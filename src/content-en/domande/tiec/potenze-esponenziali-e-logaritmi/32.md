The positive number $x$ satisfies the equation
$$
\log_{10} x = \frac{\log_{10} \left( 2 \cdot 10^{-4} \right) + \log_{10} \left( 8 \cdot 10^{-4} \right)}{2} .
$$
What is the value of $x$?

- $4 \cdot 10^{-4}$
- $5 \cdot 10^{-4}$
- $1.6 \cdot 10^{-7}$
- $4 \cdot 10^{-8}$

## Explanation

By the product rule for logarithms, the numerator is $\log_{10} \left( 2 \cdot 10^{-4} \cdot 8 \cdot 10^{-4} \right) = \log_{10} \left( 16 \cdot 10^{-8} \right)$. Dividing by $2$ is the same as multiplying by $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so by the power rule for logarithms $\log_{10} x = \log_{10} \left( 16 \cdot 10^{-8} \right)^{\frac{1}{2}}$. Since the logarithm takes different values at different points, $x = \sqrt{16 \cdot 10^{-8}}$. Hence $x = 4 \cdot 10^{-4}$.
