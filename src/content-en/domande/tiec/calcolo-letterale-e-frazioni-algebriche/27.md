What is the value of $103^2 - 97^2$?

- $36$
- $600$
- $1200$
- $12$

## Explanation

The expression is a **difference of squares**, so $103^2 - 97^2 = (103 - 97)(103 + 97)$. The first factor equals $6$ and the second equals $200$, so the result is $6 \cdot 200 = 1200$. The value $36$ is the square of $103 - 97$, obtained by confusing $a^2 - b^2$ with $(a - b)^2$.
