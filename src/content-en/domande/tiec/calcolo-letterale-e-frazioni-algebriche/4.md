For $x \neq 3$, which expression equals the fraction $\dfrac{x^2 - 6x + 9}{3x - 9}$?

- $\dfrac{x - 3}{3}$
- $\dfrac{x + 3}{3}$
- $x - 3$
- $\dfrac{x - 3}{3x}$

## Explanation

The numerator is the **square of a binomial**, $(x - 3)^2$. Factoring $3$ out of the denominator gives $3x - 9 = 3(x - 3)$. To **simplify**, cancel the common factor $x - 3$, leaving $\dfrac{x - 3}{3}$. The result $x - 3$ omits the factor $3$ in the denominator, and $\dfrac{x + 3}{3}$ uses the wrong sign in the square.
