The trinomial $9x^2 + kx + 16$, with $k > 0$, is a perfect square for which value of $k$?

- $12$
- $25$
- $24$
- $48$

## Explanation

The terms $9x^2 = (3x)^2$ and $16 = 4^2$ are squares, so the trinomial can only be the square of $3x + 4$ or $3x - 4$. The middle term must be **twice the product**, $2 \cdot 3x \cdot 4 = 24x$, and $k > 0$ selects the plus sign. Therefore $k = 24$ and the trinomial is $(3x + 4)^2$. The value $12$ is the single product $3 \cdot 4$, while $25$ is the sum $9 + 16$.
