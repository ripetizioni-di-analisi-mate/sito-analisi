The factor $x-1$ occurs exactly twice in $P(x)$ and exactly three times in $Q(x)$. What is its exact multiplicity in $P(x)+Q(x)$?

- $3$
- $5$
- $2$
- $6$

## Explanation

Write $P+Q=(x-1)^2[A(x)+(x-1)B(x)]$ with $A(1)\ne0$. The bracket has value $A(1)$ at $x=1$ and does not vanish. The factor therefore occurs exactly twice in the sum.
