A polynomial $P(x)$ is zero at $x = -2$. Which statement follows?

- $P(x)$ is divisible by $x - 2$
- The constant term of $P(x)$ is $-2$
- $P(x)$ is divisible by $x + 2$
- The remainder when $P(x)$ is divided by $x + 2$ is $-2$

## Explanation

By the criterion for **divisibility by** $x - a$, a polynomial is divisible by $x - a$ if and only if it is zero at $a$. With $a = -2$ the binomial is $x - (-2) = x + 2$, so $P(x)$ is divisible by $x + 2$ and this division has remainder $P(-2) = 0$. The polynomial $x + 2$ is zero at $-2$, has constant term $2$, and is not divisible by $x - 2$, so the other statements do not follow.
