A square frame has an outer side length of $x + 4$ centimeters and encloses a square with side length $x$ centimeters, where $x > 0$. What is the area of the frame in square centimeters?

- $16$
- $4x + 16$
- $8x + 16$
- $8x$

## Explanation

The area of the frame is the difference between the areas of the outer and inner squares, or $(x + 4)^2 - x^2$. The formula for the **square of a binomial** gives $(x + 4)^2 = x^2 + 8x + 16$. Subtracting $x^2$ leaves $8x + 16$. The result $16$ comes from the incorrect expansion $(x + 4)^2 = x^2 + 16$, and $4x + 16$ uses the single product instead of twice the product.
