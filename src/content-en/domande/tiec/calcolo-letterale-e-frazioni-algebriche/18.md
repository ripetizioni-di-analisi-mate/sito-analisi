What is the degree of the polynomial $3x^2y^3 - 5x^4 + xy^2 - 7$?

- $4$
- $3$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

The **degree** of a polynomial is the greatest degree among its terms. The terms have degrees $2 + 3 = 5$, $4$, $1 + 2 = 3$, and $0$, so the polynomial has degree $5$. The value $4$ is the degree in $x$ alone, and $3$ is the degree in $y$ alone.
