Treating $P(x,y)=3xy^2+(y-1)x^2+7$ as a polynomial in $x$ alone, what is its coefficient of degree $2$?

- $3y^2$
- $y$
- $y-1$
- $2(y-1)$

## Explanation

In this reading, $y$ forms part of the coefficients. Collecting powers of $x$ gives $(y-1)x^2+3y^2x+7$. The term with $y^2$ is only of first degree in $x$.
