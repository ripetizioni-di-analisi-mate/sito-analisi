Which expression equals the power $\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}a^2b^3\right)^4$?

- $-\dfrac{1}{16}a^8b^{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}a^6b^7$
- $-2a^8b^{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}a^8b^{12}$

## Explanation

To take the **power of a monomial**, raise the coefficient to the fourth power and multiply each exponent by $4$. The coefficient becomes $\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^4 = \dfrac{1}{16}$, positive because the exponent is even. The exponents become $2 \cdot 4 = 8$ and $3 \cdot 4 = 12$, so the result is $\dfrac{1}{16}a^8b^{12}$. The answer $\dfrac{1}{16}a^6b^7$ adds $4$ to the exponents instead of multiplying them, and $-2a^8b^{12}$ multiplies the coefficient by $4$.
