An investment of $C$ increases each year by a fraction $r$ of the previous year’s value, so after two years it is worth $C(1 + r)^2$. What is the total increase over the two years?

- $2rC$
- $C(2r + r^2)$
- $r^2C$
- $C(1 + 2r + r^2)$

## Explanation

The increase is the final value minus the initial value, or $C(1 + r)^2 - C$. The formula for the **square of a binomial** gives $(1 + r)^2 = 1 + 2r + r^2$, so the increase is $C(1 + 2r + r^2 - 1) = C(2r + r^2)$. The term $r^2C$ is the second year’s growth on the first year’s increase. It is missing from $2rC$. The expression $C(1 + 2r + r^2)$ is the final value of the investment.
