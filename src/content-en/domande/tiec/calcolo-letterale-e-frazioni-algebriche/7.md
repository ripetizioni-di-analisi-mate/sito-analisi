For which values is the fraction $\dfrac{3x}{x^2 + 2x}$ defined?

- $x \neq -2$
- $x \neq 0 \;\wedge\; x \neq -2$
- $x \neq 0$
- $x \neq 0 \;\wedge\; x \neq 2$

## Explanation

Find the **domain restrictions** from the original fraction by factoring its denominator as $x^2 + 2x = x(x + 2)$. The denominator is zero at $x = 0$ and $x = -2$, so the fraction is defined for $x \neq 0$ and $x \neq -2$. Giving only the condition $x \neq -2$ comes from canceling $x$ before finding the restrictions, even though at $x = 0$ the original denominator is zero.
