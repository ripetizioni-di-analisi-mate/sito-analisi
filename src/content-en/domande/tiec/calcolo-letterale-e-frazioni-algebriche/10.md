For $x \neq 0$ and $x \neq -2$, which expression equals the difference $\dfrac{3}{x + 2} - \dfrac{x - 4}{x^2 + 2x}$?

- $\dfrac{2x - 4}{x(x + 2)}$
- $\dfrac{2}{x + 2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{x}$
- $\dfrac{7 - x}{x(x + 2)}$

## Explanation

The denominator $x^2 + 2x$ factors as $x(x + 2)$, the LCM of the denominators. Multiply the numerator $3$ by $x$, so the numerator of the difference is $3x - (x - 4) = 3x - x + 4 = 2x + 4$. Since $2x + 4 = 2(x + 2)$, cancel the factor $x + 2$ to obtain $\dfrac{2}{x}$. The numerator $2x - 4$ applies the minus sign only to $x$, while $7 - x$ omits multiplication of $3$ by $x$.
