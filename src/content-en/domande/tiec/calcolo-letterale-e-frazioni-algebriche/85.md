A table compares the zeros of $N(x)=x^2-4$ with those of $F(x)=N(x)/(x+2)$. Which value is listed as a zero only in the column for $N$?

- $-2$
- $4$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

The numerator vanishes at both values $\pm2$. At $2$ the denominator is nonzero, so $F$ is also zero. At $-2$ the fraction is undefined and the value appears only among the zeros of $N$.
