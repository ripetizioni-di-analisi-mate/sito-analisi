For which values is the fraction $\dfrac{x^2 - 4}{x + 2}$ zero?

- $x = 2$
- $x = 2$ and $x = -2$
- $x = -2$
- no value of $x$

## Explanation

The fraction is defined for $x \neq -2$ because its denominator vanishes at $-2$. A fraction is zero when its numerator is zero and its denominator is nonzero. The numerator $x^2 - 4$ is zero at $x = 2$ and $x = -2$, but the domain restrictions exclude $-2$, so the fraction is zero only at $x = 2$. Simplifying to $\dfrac{x^2 - 4}{x + 2} = x - 2$, which is valid for $x \neq -2$, gives the same result.
