All polynomials in the family $F_k(x)=(x-1)(x+k)+3$ share a point on their graphs for every $k$. Which point is it?

- $(3,1)$
- $(-1,3)$
- $(0,3)$
- $(1,3)$

## Explanation

The parameter appears in a product containing the factor $x-1$. At $x=1$ the product vanishes whatever $k$ is. The remaining constant value $3$ gives the shared point.
