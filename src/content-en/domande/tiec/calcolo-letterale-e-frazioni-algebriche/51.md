Which of the following numbers is a zero of $3x^3 - x^2 - 3x + 1$?

- $3$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $-3$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

A rational zero of a polynomial with integer coefficients, written in lowest terms, has numerator dividing the constant term $1$ and denominator dividing the leading coefficient $3$. The candidates are therefore $\pm 1$ and $\pm \dfrac{1}{3}$, ruling out $3$ and $-3$. Substituting $x = \dfrac{1}{3}$ gives $\dfrac{3}{27} - \dfrac{1}{9} - 1 + 1 = 0$, so $\dfrac{1}{3}$ is a zero. Substituting $x = -\dfrac{1}{3}$ gives $-\dfrac{1}{9} - \dfrac{1}{9} + 1 + 1 = \dfrac{16}{9}$, which is nonzero.
