A workshop table lists hours $h$, pieces $p$ and hourly rate $t$. Which formula gives the labour cost per piece, assuming $p>0$?

- $\dfrac{ht}{p}$
- $\dfrac{h}{pt}$
- $hpt$
- $\dfrac{pt}{h}$

## Explanation

Hours multiplied by the hourly rate gives total labour cost. To get a cost per piece the total must be shared among $p$ pieces. The units of the result are euros per piece.
