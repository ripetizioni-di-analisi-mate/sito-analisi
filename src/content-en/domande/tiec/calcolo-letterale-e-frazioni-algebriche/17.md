What is the sum $2a^2b + 3ab^2 - a^2b$?

- $a^2b + 3ab^2$
- $4a^2b$
- $4a^3b^3$
- $3a^2b + 3ab^2$

## Explanation

The monomials $2a^2b$ and $-a^2b$ are **like terms**, so their sum is $a^2b$. The monomial $3ab^2$ has a different variable part because the exponent $2$ is on $b$, so it stays separate. The sum is therefore $a^2b + 3ab^2$. The result $4a^2b$ treats all three monomials as like terms, and $3a^2b + 3ab^2$ adds $2a^2b$ and $a^2b$ without the minus sign.
