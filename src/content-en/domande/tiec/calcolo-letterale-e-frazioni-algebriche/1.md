To reduce $\dfrac{-18a^5b^2}{6a^2b^2}$ with $a,b\ne0$, which first step correctly separates the three parts of the quotient?

- $\dfrac{-18}{6}\,a^{5+2}b^{2+2}$
- $\dfrac{-18}{6}\,a^{5-2}b^{2-2}$
- $\dfrac{18}{6}\,a^{5-2}b^{2-2}$
- $\dfrac{-18}{6}\,a^{5/2}b^{2/2}$

## Explanation

Divide the coefficients while keeping the sign, and subtract exponents of powers with the same base. The factor in $b$ becomes $b^0=1$ because $b\ne0$. The remaining coefficient is $-3$ and the remaining factor is $a^3$.
