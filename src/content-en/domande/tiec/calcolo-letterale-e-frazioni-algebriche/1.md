What is the expansion of $(2a + 5)^2$?

- $4a^2 + 25$
- $4a^2 + 20a + 25$
- $4a^2 + 10a + 25$
- $2a^2 + 20a + 25$

## Explanation

To find the **square of a binomial**, add the square of the first term, twice the product of the terms, and the square of the second term. The square of $2a$ is $4a^2$, twice the product is $2 \cdot 2a \cdot 5 = 20a$, and the square of $5$ is $25$. The expansion is therefore $4a^2 + 20a + 25$. The result $4a^2 + 25$ omits twice the product, and $4a^2 + 10a + 25$ uses the single product in its place.
