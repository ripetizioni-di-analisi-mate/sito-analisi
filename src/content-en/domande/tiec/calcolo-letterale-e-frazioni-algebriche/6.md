The remainder when $x^3 + kx - 4$ is divided by $x - 2$ is $6$. What is $k$?

- $-2$
- $-9$
- $1$
- $-5$

## Explanation

By the **remainder theorem**, the remainder upon division by $x - 2$ is the value of the polynomial at $2$, namely $8 + 2k - 4 = 4 + 2k$. Set this value equal to $6$ to obtain $2k = 2$ and $k = 1$. The value $-2$ comes from requiring a remainder of $0$, as for divisibility, while $-9$ uses the value of the polynomial at $-2$.
