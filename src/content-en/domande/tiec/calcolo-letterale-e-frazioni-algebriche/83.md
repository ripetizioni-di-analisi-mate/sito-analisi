For $x \neq 1$, which expression equals $x + 1 - \dfrac{x^2}{x - 1}$?

- $-\dfrac{1}{x - 1}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x - 1}$
- $\dfrac{2x^2 - 1}{x - 1}$
- $\dfrac{-x^2 + x + 1}{x - 1}$

## Explanation

Write the polynomial $x + 1$ as a fraction with denominator $x - 1$, so $x + 1 = \dfrac{(x + 1)(x - 1)}{x - 1} = \dfrac{x^2 - 1}{x - 1}$. The difference is therefore $\dfrac{x^2 - 1 - x^2}{x - 1} = -\dfrac{1}{x - 1}$. The result $\dfrac{-x^2 + x + 1}{x - 1}$ puts $x + 1$ over the denominator without multiplying it by $x - 1$, and $\dfrac{2x^2 - 1}{x - 1}$ adds $x^2$ instead of subtracting it.
