In a factorisation of $6x^2+7x-3$ one factor is $3x-1$. What is wrong with proposing $2x-3$ as the other factor?

- The constant terms would give $+3$ instead of $-3$
- Two linear factors cannot produce a trinomial
- The given factor vanishes at $x=3$
- Multiplying the linear terms would give $5x^2$

## Explanation

The proposed constant terms would be $-1$ and $-3$. Their product is $+3$, which already contradicts the polynomial. Using $2x+3$ instead also gives the correct cross terms $9x-2x=7x$.
