The polynomial $3x^3-4x^2+7$ is to be divided by $x-1$ using Ruffini's rule. Which row of coefficients of the dividend is written, from the highest degree to the constant term?

- $3,\ 0,\ -4,\ 7$
- $3,\ -4,\ 0,\ 7$
- $7,\ 0,\ -4,\ 3$
- $3,\ -4,\ 7$

## Explanation

The polynomial has terms of degree $3$ and $2$ and a constant term, while the first-degree term is missing. Ruffini's rule needs one coefficient for each degree, from the third down to degree zero, so $0$ is written in place of the term in $x$. The row is therefore $3,\ -4,\ 0,\ 7$. Without the zero, the constant term $7$ would take the place of the coefficient of $x$ and the division would give a wrong result.
