A program evaluates $P(x)=2x^3-3x^2+5$ using Horner's nested scheme. Which sequence of coefficients, from highest degree to constant, should it receive?

- $2,0,-3,5$
- $2,-3,0,5$
- $5,0,-3,2$
- $2,-3,5$

## Explanation

The powers to consider are $x^3,x^2,x,1$. The coefficient of the missing power $x$ is zero, keeping the other coefficients in their places. The correct sequence reconstructs $((2x-3)x+0)x+5$.
