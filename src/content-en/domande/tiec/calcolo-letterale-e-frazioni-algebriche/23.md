Which numbers $a$ and $b$ make $(x + a)(x - 3) = x^2 + bx - 12$ true for every value of $x$?

- $a = 4$ and $b = -1$
- $a = 4$ and $b = 1$
- $a = -4$ and $b = -7$
- $a = 4$ and $b = 7$

## Explanation

Expanding the left side gives $x^2 + (a - 3)x - 3a$. Two polynomials agree for every $x$ when the coefficients of like powers agree, so $-3a = -12$ and $a - 3 = b$. The first equation gives $a = 4$ and therefore $b = 4 - 3 = 1$. The pair $a = -4$, $b = -7$ uses the wrong sign for the constant term, and $b = 7$ comes from expanding $(x + 4)(x + 3)$.
