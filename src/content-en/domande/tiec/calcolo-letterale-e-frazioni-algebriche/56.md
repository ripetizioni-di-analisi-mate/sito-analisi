A student seeks integers $m,n$ such that $x^2+4x+7=(x+m)(x+n)$. Why must the search fail?

- The coefficient of $x^2$ would have to differ from $1$
- No pair of integer factors of $7$ has sum $4$
- Every trinomial with odd constant term is irreducible
- The two brackets would always give a negative constant term

## Explanation

Expansion requires $mn=7$ and $m+n=4$. The integer pairs, up to order, are $(1,7)$ and $(-1,-7)$. Their sums are $8$ and $-8$, so neither pair satisfies both conditions.
