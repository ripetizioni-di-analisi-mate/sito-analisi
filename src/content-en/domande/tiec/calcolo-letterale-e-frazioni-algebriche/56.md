How does the polynomial $a^2 - 5ab + 6b^2$ factor?

- $(a + 2b)(a + 3b)$
- $(a - 2b)(a - 3b)$
- $(a - b)(a - 6b)$
- $(a - 2b)(a + 3b)$

## Explanation

The polynomial is quadratic in $a$ with leading coefficient $1$. Treat $b$ as a number. Find two terms with **sum** $-5b$ and **product** $6b^2$, namely $-2b$ and $-3b$. Therefore $a^2 - 5ab + 6b^2 = (a - 2b)(a - 3b)$. The product $(a - b)(a - 6b)$ has the correct term $6b^2$, but its middle term is $-7ab$.
