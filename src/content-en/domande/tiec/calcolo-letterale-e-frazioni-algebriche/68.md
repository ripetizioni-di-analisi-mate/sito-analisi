How does the polynomial $a^2 + ab - 2a - 2b$ factor?

- $(a + b)(a - 2)$
- $(a - b)(a + 2)$
- $(a + b)(a + 2)$
- $(a - 2)(a - b)$

## Explanation

The four terms have no nonconstant common factor, so use **factoring by grouping**. Factor $a$ out of the first two terms and $-2$ out of the last two to get $a(a + b) - 2(a + b)$. The binomial $a + b$ is common to both groups, so the polynomial is $(a + b)(a - 2)$. The result $(a + b)(a + 2)$ factors out $2$ instead of $-2$.
