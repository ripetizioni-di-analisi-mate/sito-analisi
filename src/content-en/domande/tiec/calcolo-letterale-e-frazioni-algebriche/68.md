A bill is written as $ac+bc+ad+bd$, where $a,b$ are quantities and $c,d$ are two charges per unit. Which form directly shows the cost of one extra unit of $a$?

- $(a+b)(c+d)$, extra cost $c+d$
- $(a+c)(b+d)$, extra cost $b+d$
- $(a+b)(c+d)$, extra cost $a+b$
- $(a+d)(b+c)$, extra cost $b+c$

## Explanation

Factoring out $c$ and then $d$ gives $(a+b)c+(a+b)d=(a+b)(c+d)$. If $a$ increases by one, the first factor increases by one. The bill therefore rises by the second factor $c+d$.
