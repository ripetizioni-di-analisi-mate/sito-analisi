Which expression equals $(2x^3 - x + 4) - (x^3 - 3x^2 - x + 1)$?

- $x^3 - 3x^2 + 3$
- $x^3 + 3x^2 + 3$
- $x^3 + 3x^2 - 2x + 3$
- $x^3 + 3x^2 + 5$

## Explanation

The minus sign before the second polynomial reverses the sign of *every* term, giving $2x^3 - x + 4 - x^3 + 3x^2 + x - 1$. Combining like terms gives $x^3$ from the cubic terms, $3x^2$ from the quadratic term, $0$ from $-x + x$, and $3$ from $4 - 1$. The result is therefore $x^3 + 3x^2 + 3$. Each other answer leaves the sign of one of $-3x^2$, $-x$, or $1$ unchanged.
