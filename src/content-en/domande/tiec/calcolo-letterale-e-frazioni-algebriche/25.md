What is the constant term of the polynomial $(3x - 2)^3 - (x + 2)^2$?

- $-12$
- $-4$
- $4$
- $-10$

## Explanation

The constant term of a polynomial is its value at $x = 0$, so evaluate at $x = 0$ without expanding. This gives $(-2)^3 - 2^2 = -8 - 4 = -12$. The value $-4$ adds $4$ instead of subtracting it, and $4$ treats the cube of $-2$ as positive.
