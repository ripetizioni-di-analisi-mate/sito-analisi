A polynomial $P$ contains only even powers of $x$, possibly including a constant. If $P(-3)=7$, what can be established?

- $P(3)=7$
- $P(-6)=14$
- $P(0)=7$
- $P(3)=-7$

## Explanation

For every even exponent, powers of $-3$ and $3$ agree. Any constant term is unchanged too. Each term of $P(3)$ therefore matches its counterpart in $P(-3)$.
