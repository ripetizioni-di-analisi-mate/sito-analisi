For values of $x$ where all the fractions are defined, which of the following fractions equals $\dfrac{x - 1}{x + 1}$?

- $\dfrac{x^2 - 1}{x^2 + 1}$
- $\dfrac{x - 2}{x}$
- $\dfrac{2x - 1}{2x + 1}$
- $\dfrac{x^2 - 1}{(x + 1)^2}$

## Explanation

Multiplying the numerator and denominator by the same nonzero factor preserves the value of a rational expression, just as **simplifying** does in reverse. Multiplying both by $x + 1$ gives $\dfrac{(x - 1)(x + 1)}{(x + 1)^2} = \dfrac{x^2 - 1}{(x + 1)^2}$. In the other fractions, the numerator and denominator have not been multiplied by the same factor. Indeed, at $x = 2$ the given fraction equals $\dfrac{1}{3}$, while the others equal $\dfrac{3}{5}$, $0$, and $\dfrac{3}{5}$.
