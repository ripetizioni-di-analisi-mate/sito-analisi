In the product $(x^2 - 3x + 2)(2x^2 + x - 4)$, what is the coefficient of $x^2$?

- $-3$
- $1$
- $-7$
- $3$

## Explanation

A quadratic term arises from every pair of terms, one from each factor, whose degrees add to $2$. The pairs are $x^2 \cdot (-4)$, $(-3x) \cdot x$, and $2 \cdot 2x^2$, giving $-4x^2$, $-3x^2$, and $4x^2$. The coefficient of $x^2$ is therefore $-4 - 3 + 4 = -3$, without expanding the entire product. The values $1$ and $-7$ each omit one of the three pairs.
