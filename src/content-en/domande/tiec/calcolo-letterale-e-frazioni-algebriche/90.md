The expression $\dfrac{x+y}{2}$ has been rewritten as $x+\dfrac{y}{2}$. For which values of $x$ do the two forms agree for every $y$?

- Only $x=2$
- Only $x=0$
- Every real $x$
- No real $x$

## Explanation

Dividing the sum by $2$ halves both terms. Subtracting $y/2$ from both forms leaves $x/2$ and $x$. They agree exactly at $x=0$, regardless of $y$.
