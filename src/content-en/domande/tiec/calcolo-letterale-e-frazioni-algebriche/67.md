After all real linear factors are removed from $x^4-27x$, which monic irreducible factor remains, and what verifies that it is irreducible?

- $x^2-9$, with two opposite roots
- $x^2+3x+9$, with negative discriminant
- $x^2+9$, because it is the quotient on division by $x$
- $x^2-3x+9$, with positive discriminant

## Explanation

Factoring out $x$ gives $x(x^3-27)$. The difference of cubes supplies the factor $x-3$ and the remaining $x^2+3x+9$. Its discriminant is $9-36=-27$, so it has no real zeros.
