What is the complete factorization of $x^4 - 27x$?

- $x(x - 3)(x^2 - 3x + 9)$
- $x(x - 3)(x^2 + 3x + 9)$
- $x(x - 3)^3$
- $x(x + 3)(x^2 - 3x + 9)$

## Explanation

**Factoring out** $x$ gives $x(x^3 - 27)$. The factor $x^3 - 27$ is a **difference of cubes** because $27 = 3^3$, so $x^3 - 27 = (x - 3)(x^2 + 3x + 9)$. Its quadratic factor $x^2 + 3x + 9$ is irreducible, so the factorization is complete. The factor $x^2 - 3x + 9$ appears in the factorization of the sum $x^3 + 27$.
