In the expansion of $(a - 2b + 3c)^2$, what is the coefficient of $bc$?

- $-6$
- $12$
- $9$
- $-12$

## Explanation

In the **square of a trinomial**, the term in $bc$ is twice the product of $-2b$ and $3c$. This cross term is $2 \cdot (-2b) \cdot 3c = -12bc$, with a minus sign because the coefficients of the two terms have opposite signs. The coefficient is therefore $-12$. The value $-6$ omits the factor $2$ in the cross term, and $12$ overlooks the sign of $-2b$.
