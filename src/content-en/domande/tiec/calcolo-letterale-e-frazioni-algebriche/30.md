In expanding the square of $a-2b+3c$, a student includes $-6bc$ just once. Which contribution is missing, and why?

- A $+6bc$, because cross terms cancel
- A $-36b^2c^2$, because every mixed term must be squared
- A $-6abc$, because the first variable must also appear
- Another $-6bc$, from $(3c)(-2b)$ with the factors interchanged

## Explanation

In the product of two equal brackets, the term in $b$ can come from either bracket. In each case it meets $3c$ in the other and gives $-6bc$. The combined contribution is therefore $-12bc$.
