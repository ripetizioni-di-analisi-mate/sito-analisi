What is the degree of the polynomial $(x^3 - 2x + 1)^2\,(x^2 + 5)^3$?

- $10$
- $15$
- $36$
- $12$

## Explanation

The degree of a product is the sum of the degrees of the factors, so the degree of a power is the degree of its base times the exponent. The factor $(x^3 - 2x + 1)^2$ has degree $3 \cdot 2 = 6$ and the factor $(x^2 + 5)^3$ has degree $2 \cdot 3 = 6$. The product therefore has degree $6 + 6 = 12$. The value $36$ multiplies the four numbers $3$, $2$, $2$, and $3$ instead of adding the degrees of the two factors.
