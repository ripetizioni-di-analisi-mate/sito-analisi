Sara checks a proposed identity between two polynomials of degree at most two. Their values agree at $x=0,1,2$. Why do these three checks suffice?

- Every polynomial takes just three different values
- The three inputs happen to be consecutive integers
- Their difference would have three distinct zeros despite degree at most two
- The sum of two quadratic polynomials is always constant

## Explanation

Their difference is either a polynomial of degree at most two or the zero polynomial. A nonzero polynomial of that degree cannot have three distinct zeros. Hence the difference is identically zero.
