A division is handed in as $P(x)=(x^2+1)Q(x)+(x^2-2)$. Which correction makes the remainder lower in degree than the divisor while preserving $P$?

- Use quotient $Q(x)-1$ and remainder $3$
- Use quotient $Q(x)+1$ and remainder $-3$
- Keep $Q(x)$ and take remainder $-2$
- Use quotient $Q(x)+1$ and remainder $-1$

## Explanation

The proposed remainder $x^2-2$ still contains one copy of $x^2+1$. Subtracting that copy leaves $-3$. Moving the copy into the product increases the quotient by one.
