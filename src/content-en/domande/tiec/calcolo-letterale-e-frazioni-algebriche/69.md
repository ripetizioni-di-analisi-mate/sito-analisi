For every integer $n$, which of these numbers divides $n^3 - n$?

- $4$
- $5$
- $9$
- $6$

## Explanation

Factoring out the common factor and using a difference of squares gives $n^3 - n = n(n^2 - 1) = (n - 1)\,n\,(n + 1)$. The number is therefore the product of three consecutive integers. Among three consecutive integers there is always an even number and a multiple of $3$, so their product is divisible by $2 \cdot 3 = 6$. At $n = 2$ the result is $6$, which is divisible by none of $4$, $5$, or $9$.
