How does the polynomial $25x^2 - 49y^4$ factor?

- $(5x - 7y)(5x + 7y)$
- $(5x - 7y^2)(5x + 7y^2)$
- $(5x - 7y^2)^2$
- $(25x - 49y^2)(x + y^2)$

## Explanation

The polynomial is a **difference of squares** because $25x^2 = (5x)^2$ and $49y^4 = (7y^2)^2$. The formula $a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)$ gives $(5x - 7y^2)(5x + 7y^2)$. The proposed result $(5x - 7y)(5x + 7y)$ expands to $25x^2 - 49y^2$ because it uses $y$ instead of $y^2$, the square root of $y^4$. The result $(5x - 7y^2)^2$ also contains the cross term $-70xy^2$.
