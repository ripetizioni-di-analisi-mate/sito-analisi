A square is cut out of a larger square sheet. The remaining area is written as $(u-v)(u+v)$ with $u>v>0$. Which interpretation fits the formula?

- The areas of the squares are $u$ and $v$
- The side lengths of the squares are $u$ and $v$
- The side lengths of the squares are $u-v$ and $u+v$
- The perimeters of the squares are $u$ and $v$

## Explanation

The product is the difference of squares $u^2-v^2$. To obtain the difference in areas, read $u$ and $v$ as side lengths. If they were perimeters, each would first have to be divided by four.
