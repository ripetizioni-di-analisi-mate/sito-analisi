For $x \neq 0$ and $x \neq \pm 1$, which expression equals $\left(1 - \dfrac{1}{x}\right) : \left(x - \dfrac{1}{x}\right)$?

- $\dfrac{x - 1}{x + 1}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x - 1}$
- $x + 1$
- $\dfrac{1}{x + 1}$

## Explanation

Write each expression as a single fraction, $1 - \dfrac{1}{x} = \dfrac{x - 1}{x}$ and $x - \dfrac{1}{x} = \dfrac{x^2 - 1}{x}$. To divide, multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor, which gives $\dfrac{x - 1}{x} \cdot \dfrac{x}{(x - 1)(x + 1)}$. Canceling $x$ and $x - 1$ leaves $\dfrac{1}{x + 1}$. The result $x + 1$ is the reciprocal, obtained by inverting the dividend instead of the divisor.
