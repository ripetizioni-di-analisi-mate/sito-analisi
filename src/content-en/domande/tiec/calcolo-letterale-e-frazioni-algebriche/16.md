A rectangle has side lengths $3a^2b$ and $2ab^3$, where $a$ and $b$ are positive. What is its area?

- $5a^3b^4$
- $6a^2b^3$
- $6a^3b^4$
- $6a^3b^3$

## Explanation

Area is the product of the side lengths, so take the **product of the monomials**. Multiply the coefficients, giving $3 \cdot 2 = 6$, and add the exponents of each variable, giving $a^{2+1} = a^3$ and $b^{1+3} = b^4$. The area is therefore $6a^3b^4$. The result $5a^3b^4$ adds the coefficients instead of multiplying them, and $6a^2b^3$ multiplies the exponents instead of adding them.
