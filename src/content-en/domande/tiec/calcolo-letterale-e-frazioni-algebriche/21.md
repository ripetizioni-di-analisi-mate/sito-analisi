A register records the cost $C(n)=n^2+3n+8$ for $n$ pieces. What is the exact extra cost of the next piece?

- $n+4$
- $n^2+3n+9$
- $2n+3$
- $2n+4$

## Explanation

The extra cost is $C(n+1)-C(n)$. The quadratic term increases by $2n+1$ and the linear term by $3$. The fixed $8$ cancels, leaving $2n+4$.
