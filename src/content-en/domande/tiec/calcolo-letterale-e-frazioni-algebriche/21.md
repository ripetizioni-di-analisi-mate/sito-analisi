What is the value of $P(x) = 2x^3 - x^2 + 3x - 4$ at $x = -1$?

- $-8$
- $0$
- $-4$
- $-10$

## Explanation

Substitute $-1$ for $x$, with $(-1)^3 = -1$ and $(-1)^2 = 1$, to obtain $P(-1) = 2 \cdot (-1) - 1 + 3 \cdot (-1) - 4 = -2 - 1 - 3 - 4 = -10$. The value $-8$ uses $+1$ in place of the term $-x^2$, while $0$ is $P(1)$, the sum of the coefficients.
