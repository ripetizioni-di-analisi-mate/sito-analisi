The polynomial $x^3 + ax^2 + bx - 6$ is divisible by both $x - 1$ and $x + 2$. What are the values of $a$ and $b$?

- $a = 1$ and $b = 4$
- $a = -6$ and $b = 11$
- $a = 2$ and $b = 3$
- $a = 4$ and $b = 1$

## Explanation

A polynomial is **divisible by** $x - 1$ if and only if it is zero at $1$. It is divisible by $x + 2$ if and only if it is zero at $-2$. The first condition gives $1 + a + b - 6 = 0$, or $a + b = 5$, and the second gives $-8 + 4a - 2b - 6 = 0$, or $2a - b = 7$. These conditions form the system
$$
\begin{cases}
a + b = 5 \\
2a - b = 7
\end{cases}
$$
and adding the equations gives $3a = 12$, so $a = 4$ and $b = 1$. To check, $x^3 + 4x^2 + x - 6 = (x - 1)(x + 2)(x + 3)$. The pair $a = -6$, $b = 11$ comes from using $2$ instead of $-2$.
