What is the greatest common divisor of the monomials $8a^3b^2$, $12a^2b^4$, and $20a^4b^3$?

- $4a^4b^4$
- $4a^2b^2$
- $120a^4b^4$
- $2a^2b^2$

## Explanation

For the **greatest common divisor** of monomials, use the GCD of their coefficients and the common variables raised to their smallest exponents. The GCD of $8$, $12$, and $20$ is $4$. The smallest exponent of $a$ is $2$, and that of $b$ is $2$. The GCD is therefore $4a^2b^2$. The monomial $4a^4b^4$ uses the greatest exponents, which are used in the LCM, and $120a^4b^4$ is the LCM itself.
