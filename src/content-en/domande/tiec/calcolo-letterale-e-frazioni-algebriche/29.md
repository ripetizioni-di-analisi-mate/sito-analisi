In the volume of a cube of side $s+t$, where $s,t>0$, which term is the combined volume of the three prisms with edges $s,s,t$?

- $s^3+t^3$
- $(3s)^2t$
- $3s^2t$
- $3st^2$

## Explanation

Each prism has volume $s\cdot s\cdot t=s^2t$. The three arrangements correspond to the three axes along which the edge of length $t$ can run. Their sum gives the term $3s^2t$ in the cube expansion.
