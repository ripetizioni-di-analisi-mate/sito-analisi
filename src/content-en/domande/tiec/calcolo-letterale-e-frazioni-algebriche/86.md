A function is given by $f(x)=\dfrac{(x-1)(x+2)}{x-1}$. Which change to the graph of $y=x+2$ produces exactly the graph of $f$?

- Remove the point $(1,3)$
- Remove the point $(-2,0)$
- Add the point $(1,0)$
- Remove the point $(0,2)$

## Explanation

For $x\ne1$ the shared factor cancels and gives $x+2$. At $x=1$ the original rule divides by zero. Only the point on the line with horizontal coordinate $1$ must therefore be removed.
