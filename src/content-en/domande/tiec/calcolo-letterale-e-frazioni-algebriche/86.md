For $x \neq 2$, which expression equals the fraction $\dfrac{4 - x^2}{x^2 - 4x + 4}$?

- $\dfrac{x + 2}{2 - x}$
- $\dfrac{x + 2}{x - 2}$
- $-1$
- $\dfrac{2 - x}{x + 2}$

## Explanation

The numerator is a **difference of squares**, $4 - x^2 = (2 - x)(2 + x)$, and the denominator is the perfect square $(x - 2)^2$. It also equals $(2 - x)^2$ because the binomials are opposites. Canceling the factor $2 - x$ gives $\dfrac{2 + x}{2 - x}$. The result $\dfrac{x + 2}{x - 2}$ has the opposite sign because it treats $2 - x$ and $x - 2$ as equal. The value $-1$ cancels the individual terms $4$ and $x^2$, although only factors can be canceled.
