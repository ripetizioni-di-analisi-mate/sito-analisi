A rectangular plot has area $(x^2 + 7x + 12)$ square meters and one side of length $(x + 3)$ meters, where $x > 0$. What is the length of the other side in meters?

- $x - 4$
- $x + 10$
- $x + 4$
- $x + 7$

## Explanation

The other side length is the area divided by the known side length, or $\dfrac{x^2 + 7x + 12}{x + 3}$. To factor the quadratic, find two numbers with **sum** $7$ and **product** $12$, namely $3$ and $4$, giving $x^2 + 7x + 12 = (x + 3)(x + 4)$. Cancel the positive factor $x + 3$ to obtain the other side length, $x + 4$. The result $x + 10$ uses synthetic division with $3$ on the left instead of $-3$.
