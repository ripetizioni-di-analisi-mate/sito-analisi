What is the least common denominator of the sum $\dfrac{1}{6x^2y} + \dfrac{1}{4xy^3} - \dfrac{1}{9x^3}$?

- $36x^3y^3$
- $216x^3y^3$
- $36x^6y^4$
- $12x^3y^3$

## Explanation

The least common denominator is the **least common multiple** of the denominators. The LCM of $6$, $4$, and $9$ is $36$. Include every variable that occurs, raised to its greatest exponent, giving $x^3$ and $y^3$. The denominator is therefore $36x^3y^3$. The coefficient $216 = 6 \cdot 4 \cdot 9$ is the product of the coefficients, a common multiple larger than their LCM, and $x^6y^4$ adds the exponents.
