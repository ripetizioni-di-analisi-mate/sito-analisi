What is the product $(3x^2 - y)(3x^2 + y)$?

- $9x^4 + y^2$
- $3x^4 - y^2$
- $9x^4 - y^2$
- $9x^4 - 6x^2y - y^2$

## Explanation

The two factors are the sum and the difference of the same terms $3x^2$ and $y$, so use the **product of a sum and a difference** formula $(a + b)(a - b) = a^2 - b^2$. The square of $3x^2$ is $9x^4$ because the coefficient is squared as well, and the exponent $2$ becomes $4$. The product is therefore $9x^4 - y^2$. The result $3x^4 - y^2$ forgets to square the coefficient $3$.
