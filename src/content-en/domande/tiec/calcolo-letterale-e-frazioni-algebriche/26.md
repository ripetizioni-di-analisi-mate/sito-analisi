An originally square plaza of side $s$ is reshaped into a rectangle. Two opposite sides grow by $d$ and the other two shrink by $d$, with $0<d<s$. How does its area change?

- It increases by $d^2$
- It stays unchanged
- It decreases by $d^2$
- It decreases by $2sd$

## Explanation

The final rectangle has dimensions $s+d$ and $s-d$. Multiplying gives $s^2-d^2$. Compared with the area $s^2$, the loss is $d^2$ square units even though the perimeter is unchanged.
