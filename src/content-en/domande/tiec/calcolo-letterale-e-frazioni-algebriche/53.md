What is the result of factoring the greatest common factor out of $12a^3b^2 - 18a^2b^3 + 6a^2b^2$?

- $6a^2b^2(2a - 3b)$
- $6a^2b^2(2a + 3b + 1)$
- $6a^2b^2(2a - 3b + 1)$
- $3a^2b^2(4a - 6b + 1)$

## Explanation

To **factor out the greatest common factor**, use the GCD of the coefficients, $6$, times the common variables raised to their smallest exponents, $a^2$ and $b^2$. Dividing each term by $6a^2b^2$ gives $2a$, $-3b$, and $1$. The result is therefore $6a^2b^2(2a - 3b + 1)$. The result $6a^2b^2(2a - 3b)$ omits the term $1$, which comes from factoring out the entire term $6a^2b^2$.
