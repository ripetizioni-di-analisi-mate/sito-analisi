The average charge for $x+1$ units is written as $\dfrac{x^2+3x+5}{x+1}$ for nonnegative integers $x$. By how much does it exceed the linear estimate $x+2$?

- $\dfrac{3}{x+1}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x+3}$
- $\dfrac{x+2}{x+1}$
- $3$

## Explanation

Multiplying $(x+1)(x+2)$ gives $x^2+3x+2$. The numerator of the charge therefore contains three units beyond that product. The excess per unit is $3/(x+1)$ and decreases as the number of units grows.
