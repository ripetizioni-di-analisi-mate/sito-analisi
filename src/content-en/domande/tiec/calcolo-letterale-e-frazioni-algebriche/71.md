What is the value of $x^2 - 2xy + y^2$ for $x = 7.3$ and $y = 5.3$?

- $2$
- $8$
- $16$
- $4$

## Explanation

The polynomial is the **square of a binomial**, $x^2 - 2xy + y^2 = (x - y)^2$. With the factored form, we only need to find the difference $x - y = 7.3 - 5.3 = 2$ and square it, without separately squaring the two decimals. The value is therefore $2^2 = 4$. The result $2$ stops at the difference without squaring it.
