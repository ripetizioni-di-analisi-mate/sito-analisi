For $x \neq 3$, what is the sum $\dfrac{x}{x - 3} + \dfrac{3}{3 - x}$?

- $\dfrac{x + 3}{x - 3}$
- $-1$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

The denominators are opposites because $3 - x = -(x - 3)$, so $\dfrac{3}{3 - x} = -\dfrac{3}{x - 3}$. The sum becomes $\dfrac{x - 3}{x - 3}$, which for $x \neq 3$ equals $1$. The result $\dfrac{x + 3}{x - 3}$ treats the two denominators as equal without changing the sign.
