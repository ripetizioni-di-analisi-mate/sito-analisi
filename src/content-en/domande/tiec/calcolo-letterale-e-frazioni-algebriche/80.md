For $x \neq 0$ and $x \neq -5$, what is the product $\dfrac{x^2 - 25}{2x} \cdot \dfrac{4x^2}{x + 5}$?

- $2x(x - 5)$
- $2x(x + 5)$
- $2(x - 5)$
- $2x^2(x - 5)$

## Explanation

The numerator $x^2 - 25$ is a difference of squares, $(x - 5)(x + 5)$. When **multiplying rational expressions**, common factors can be canceled even across fractions, so $x + 5$ cancels and $\dfrac{4x^2}{2x} = 2x$. The product is therefore $2x(x - 5)$. The result $2x(x + 5)$ cancels the factor $x - 5$ instead of $x + 5$, and $2(x - 5)$ cancels $4x^2$ with $2x$ and loses a factor of $x$.
