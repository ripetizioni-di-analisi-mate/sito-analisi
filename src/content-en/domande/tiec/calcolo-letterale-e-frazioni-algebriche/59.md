What is the factored form of $(x + 1)^2 - (2x - 3)^2$?

- $(-x - 2)(3x - 2)$
- $(x - 4)(3x - 2)$
- $(4 - x)(x - 2)$
- $(4 - x)(3x - 2)$

## Explanation

The expression is a **difference of squares** with $a = x + 1$ and $b = 2x - 3$, so it equals $(a - b)(a + b)$. The first factor is $x + 1 - (2x - 3) = x + 1 - 2x + 3 = 4 - x$, with the minus sign applied to both terms of $2x - 3$. The second factor is $x + 1 + 2x - 3 = 3x - 2$. The factor $-x - 2$ leaves the sign of $-3$ unchanged, and $(x - 4)(3x - 2)$ is the opposite of the correct result.
