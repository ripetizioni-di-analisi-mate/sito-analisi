Two segments have lengths $x+1$ and $2x-3$, with $x>3/2$. For which $x$ do squares built on the segments have equal area? Use the factor that can vanish in the domain.

- $x=2$
- $x=3/2$
- $x=2/3$
- $x=4$

## Explanation

The area difference factors as $(4-x)(3x-2)$. On the stated domain the second factor is positive, as expected for a sum of two lengths. Only $4-x$ can vanish, at $x=4$.
