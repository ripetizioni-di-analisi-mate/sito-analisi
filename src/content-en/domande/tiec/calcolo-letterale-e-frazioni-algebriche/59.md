Two segments have lengths $x+1$ and $2x-3$, with $x>\dfrac{3}{2}$. For which value of $x$ do the squares built on the two segments have equal area?

- $x=2$
- $x=\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $x=\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $x=4$

## Explanation

The two squares have equal area when $(2x-3)^2 = (x+1)^2$, that is, when the difference $(2x-3)^2-(x+1)^2$ equals $0$. By the **difference of squares** this expression factors as $\big[(2x-3)-(x+1)\big]\big[(2x-3)+(x+1)\big] = (x-4)(3x-2)$. Since $x>\dfrac{3}{2}$, the factor $3x-2$ is positive, so the product vanishes only when $x-4=0$. Therefore the squares have equal area for $x=4$, where both segments have length $5$.
