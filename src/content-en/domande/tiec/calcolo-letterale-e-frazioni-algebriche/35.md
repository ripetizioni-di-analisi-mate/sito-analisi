Which expression represents $x^4+x^2+1$ as a difference of two squares?

- $(x^2+x)^2-1$
- $(x^2+1)^2-x^2$
- $(x^2+1)^2-1$
- $(x^2-1)^2-x^2$

## Explanation

The square $(x^2+1)^2$ has $2x^2$ as its middle term. Subtracting $x^2$ leaves exactly one such term. The form also reveals the factors $x^2+1-x$ and $x^2+1+x$.
