What is the product $(x - 1)(x + 1)(x^2 + 1)$?

- $x^4 + 1$
- $x^4 - 1$
- $x^4 - 2x^2 + 1$
- $x^3 - 1$

## Explanation

The first two factors form the **product of a sum and a difference**, so $(x - 1)(x + 1) = x^2 - 1$. The expression $(x^2 - 1)(x^2 + 1)$ is another such product with terms $x^2$ and $1$, so it equals $(x^2)^2 - 1 = x^4 - 1$. The result $x^4 - 2x^2 + 1$ is $(x^2 - 1)^2$, obtained by replacing the factor $x^2 + 1$ with $x^2 - 1$.
