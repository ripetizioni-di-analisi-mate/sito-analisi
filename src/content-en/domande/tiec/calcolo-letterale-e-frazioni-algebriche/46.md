Dividing a polynomial $P(x)$ by $x - 3$ gives quotient $x^2 + 2$ and remainder $-1$. What is $P(x)$?

- $x^3 - 3x^2 + 2x - 5$
- $x^3 - 3x^2 + 2x - 7$
- $x^3 + 3x^2 + 2x + 5$
- $x^3 - 3x^2 + 2x - 6$

## Explanation

By **polynomial division**, $P(x) = (x - 3)(x^2 + 2) - 1$. The product equals $x^3 + 2x - 3x^2 - 6$, so $P(x) = x^3 - 3x^2 + 2x - 7$. As a check, the value at $3$ is $27 - 27 + 6 - 7 = -1$, equal to the remainder. The polynomial $x^3 - 3x^2 + 2x - 6$ omits the remainder, and $x^3 - 3x^2 + 2x - 5$ adds it with the wrong sign.
