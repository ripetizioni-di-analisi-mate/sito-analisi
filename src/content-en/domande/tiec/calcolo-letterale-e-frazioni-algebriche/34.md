Given $a + b = 7$ and $ab = 10$, what is $a^2 + b^2$?

- $49$
- $39$
- $69$
- $29$

## Explanation

By the formula for the **square of a binomial**, $(a + b)^2 = a^2 + b^2 + 2ab$, so $a^2 + b^2 = (a + b)^2 - 2ab$. Substitution gives $49 - 2 \cdot 10 = 29$. As a check, the numbers are $2$ and $5$, and $4 + 25 = 29$. The value $49$ omits twice the product, while $39$ subtracts $ab$ instead of $2ab$.
