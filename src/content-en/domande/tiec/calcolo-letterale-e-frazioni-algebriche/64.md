How many distinct monic linear divisors does $x^4-10x^2+9$ have? The count must include divisors with a positive constant term.

- $2$
- $3$
- $8$
- $4$

## Explanation

Treating the expression as a quadratic in $x^2$ gives $(x^2-1)(x^2-9)$. Both factors are differences of squares. The monic linear divisors are $x-1,x+1,x-3,x+3$, all distinct.
