What is the least common multiple of the polynomials $x^2 + x$, $x^2 - 1$, and $x^2 - x$?

- $x^2(x - 1)(x + 1)$
- $x^3(x - 1)^2(x + 1)^2$
- $x(x - 1)(x + 1)$
- $(x - 1)(x + 1)$

## Explanation

The factorizations are $x(x + 1)$, $(x - 1)(x + 1)$, and $x(x - 1)$. The **least common multiple** contains each factor once at its greatest exponent, and here each factor has exponent $1$. The LCM is therefore $x(x - 1)(x + 1)$. The polynomial $x^3(x - 1)^2(x + 1)^2$ is the product of the three polynomials. The result $x^2(x - 1)(x + 1)$ uses the factor $x$ twice because it appears in two polynomials.
