Which expression equals $(x - 3)^2$ for every value of $x$?

- $x^2 - 9$
- $(3 - x)^2$
- $x^2 - 3x + 9$
- $-(3 - x)^2$

## Explanation

The binomials $x - 3$ and $3 - x$ are opposites, so $3 - x = -(x - 3)$. A number and its opposite have the same square, so $(3 - x)^2 = (x - 3)^2$, and both equal $x^2 - 6x + 9$. The expression $-(3 - x)^2$ is instead the opposite of $(x - 3)^2$, and at $x = 0$ it equals $-9$ rather than $9$. The expressions $x^2 - 9$ and $x^2 - 3x + 9$ are incorrect expansions that omit the cross term $-6x$.
