What is the quotient when $x^4 - 16$ is divided by $x - 2$?

- $x^3 - 2x^2 + 4x - 8$
- $x^3 + 2x^2 + 4x + 8$
- $x^3 + 8$
- $x^2 + 4$

## Explanation

The dividend contains only the terms $x^4$ and $-16$, so the coefficients for **synthetic division** are $1$, $0$, $0$, $0$, and $-16$. With $2$ on the left, the resulting numbers are $1$, $2$, $4$, and $8$, followed by the remainder $-16 + 16 = 0$. The quotient has degree $3$, so it is $x^3 + 2x^2 + 4x + 8$. The quotient $x^3 - 2x^2 + 4x - 8$ uses $-2$ on the left, and $x^2 + 4$ is the quotient upon division by $x^2 - 4$.
