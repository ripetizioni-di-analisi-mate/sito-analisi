When dividing $2x^3-7x+3$ by $x+2$, the leading term of the quotient is $2x^2$. After subtracting the product of this term and the divisor, which new leading term must be dealt with next?

- $4x^2$
- $-7x$
- $-4x^2$
- $2x^3$

## Explanation

The product to subtract is $2x^3+4x^2$. The dividend has zero coefficient of $x^2$, so the subtraction leaves $-4x^2-7x+3$. The new highest-degree term is $-4x^2$.
