What are the quotient and remainder when $2x^3 - 7x + 3$ is divided by $x + 2$?

- quotient $2x^2 + 4x + 1$ and remainder $5$
- quotient $2x - 11$ and remainder $25$
- quotient $2x^2 - 4x + 1$ and remainder $1$
- quotient $2x^2 - 4x + 1$ and remainder $-1$

## Explanation

The dividend has no quadratic term, so the coefficients for **synthetic division** are $2$, $0$, $-7$, and $3$. The divisor is $x + 2 = x - (-2)$, so write $-2$ on the left. The resulting numbers are $2$, $-4$, $1$, and finally $3 + 1 \cdot (-2) = 1$, so the quotient is $2x^2 - 4x + 1$ and the remainder is $1$. The quotient $2x - 11$ with remainder $25$ omits the $0$, and the quotient $2x^2 + 4x + 1$ with remainder $5$ uses $2$ on the left.
