A car travels $120$ km on the outward trip at $v$ km/h and $120$ km on the return trip at $(v + 20)$ km/h, with $v > 0$. What is the total travel time in hours?

- $\dfrac{240}{2v + 20}$
- $\dfrac{240}{v(v + 20)}$
- $\dfrac{120(v + 10)}{v(v + 20)}$
- $\dfrac{240(v + 10)}{v(v + 20)}$

## Explanation

The time for each leg is distance divided by speed, so the total time is $\dfrac{120}{v} + \dfrac{120}{v + 20}$. The common denominator is $v(v + 20)$, and the numerator is $120(v + 20) + 120v = 240v + 2400 = 240(v + 10)$. The total time is therefore $\dfrac{240(v + 10)}{v(v + 20)}$. The result $\dfrac{240}{2v + 20}$ adds the numerators and denominators separately, and $\dfrac{120(v + 10)}{v(v + 20)}$ is half the correct result.
