In the expansion of $(a^2 - 2b)^3$, what is the coefficient of $a^2b^2$?

- $12$
- $-12$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

Apply the **cube of a binomial** formula $(A - B)^3 = A^3 - 3A^2B + 3AB^2 - B^3$ with $A = a^2$ and $B = 2b$. The term in $a^2b^2$ is $3AB^2 = 3 \cdot a^2 \cdot 4b^2 = 12a^2b^2$, since $A^2B$ contains $a^4$ rather than $a^2$. The coefficient is positive because the term contains the square of $B$, so the coefficient is $12$. The value $-12$ gives the term the sign of $-2b$. The value $6$ uses $(2b)^2 = 2b^2$, and $4$ omits the coefficient $3$ from the formula.
