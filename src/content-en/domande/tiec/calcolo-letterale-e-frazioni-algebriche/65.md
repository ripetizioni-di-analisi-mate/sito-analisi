In the volume model $V(a,b)=a^3+2ab^2+b^3$, with $a,b>0$, every length is doubled. Which rule explains the behaviour of the whole polynomial?

- $V(2a,2b)=2V(a,b)$, because the coefficient of $ab^2$ is two
- $V(2a,2b)=4V(a,b)$, because there are two variables
- $V(2a,2b)=6V(a,b)$, because there are three terms
- $V(2a,2b)=8V(a,b)$, because every term has total degree three

## Explanation

Even the mixed term contains three length factors, one factor of $a$ and two of $b$. Doubling all of them multiplies every monomial by $2^3=8$. This common multiplier factors out of the sum without changing its coefficients.
