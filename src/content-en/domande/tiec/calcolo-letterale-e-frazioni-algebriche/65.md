Which expression equals the polynomial $8a^3 + 12a^2 + 6a + 1$?

- $(2a + 1)(4a^2 - 2a + 1)$
- $(2a + 1)(4a^2 + 2a + 1)$
- $(2a + 1)(4a^2 + 1)$
- $(2a + 1)^3$

## Explanation

The polynomial has four terms and the first and last are cubes, $8a^3 = (2a)^3$ and $1 = 1^3$. The middle terms are $3 \cdot (2a)^2 \cdot 1 = 12a^2$ and $3 \cdot 2a \cdot 1^2 = 6a$, so the polynomial is the **cube of a binomial**, $(2a + 1)^3$. The product $(2a + 1)(4a^2 - 2a + 1)$ is the sum of cubes $8a^3 + 1$, which lacks the middle terms.
