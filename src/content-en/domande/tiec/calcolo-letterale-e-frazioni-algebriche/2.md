What is the remainder when $x^4 - 3x^2 + x - 2$ is divided by $x - 2$?

- $4$
- $0$
- $-2$
- $8$

## Explanation

By the **remainder theorem**, the remainder upon division by $x - 2$ is the value of the polynomial at $2$. Evaluating gives $16 - 3 \cdot 4 + 2 - 2 = 4$. The value $0$ is the polynomial’s value at $-2$, the remainder upon division by $x + 2$, and $-2$ is the constant term.
