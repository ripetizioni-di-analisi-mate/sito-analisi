Which polynomial is written in descending powers of $x$ with no powers missing?

- $x^3 - 2x^2 + x - 5$
- $x^3 - 2x^2 - 5$
- $-5 + x - 2x^2 + x^3$
- $x^3 + x - 2x^2 - 5$

## Explanation

A polynomial in $x$ is **ordered** by descending powers if the exponents of $x$ decrease from one term to the next. It has **no missing powers** if every power of $x$ appears, from the degree of the polynomial down to the constant term. In $x^3 - 2x^2 + x - 5$ the terms have degrees $3$, $2$, $1$, and $0$ in that order, so the powers are in descending order and none is missing. The powers in $x^3 - 2x^2 - 5$ are in descending order, but the linear term is missing. The other two polynomials include every power, but $-5 + x - 2x^2 + x^3$ is in ascending order and in $x^3 + x - 2x^2 - 5$ the linear term precedes the quadratic term.
