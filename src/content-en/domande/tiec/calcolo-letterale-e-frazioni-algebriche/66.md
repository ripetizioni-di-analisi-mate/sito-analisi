Which of the following polynomials is irreducible over the real numbers?

- $x^2 - x - 2$
- $x^2 + 4x + 4$
- $4x^2 - 1$
- $x^2 + x + 1$

## Explanation

By the factoring criterion for a **quadratic trinomial**, a quadratic with negative discriminant is irreducible over the reals. For $x^2 + x + 1$ we have $\Delta = 1 - 4 = -3 < 0$, so the quadratic is irreducible. The other three factor because $x^2 - x - 2 = (x - 2)(x + 1)$, $x^2 + 4x + 4 = (x + 2)^2$, and $4x^2 - 1 = (2x - 1)(2x + 1)$.
