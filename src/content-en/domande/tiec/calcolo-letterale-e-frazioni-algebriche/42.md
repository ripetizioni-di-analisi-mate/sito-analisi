To find the remainder of $P(x)=x^3+2x^2-x+3$ on division by $x^2-1$, write it as $mx+n$. Which two conditions determine its coefficients without long division?

- $m=P(1)$ and $n=P(-1)$
- $m+n=P(1)$ and $-m+n=P(-1)$
- $m+n=0$ and $m-n=0$
- $m+n=P(0)$ and $-m+n=P(2)$

## Explanation

At $x=\pm1$ the divisor vanishes, so the polynomial and remainder have the same values. Substituting $1$ and $-1$ into $mx+n$ yields the stated conditions. Since $P(1)=P(-1)=5$, they give $m=0$ and $n=5$.
