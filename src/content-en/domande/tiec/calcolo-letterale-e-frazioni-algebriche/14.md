For $x \neq 0$ and $y \neq 0$, which expression equals $(3x^2y)^2 : (-x^3y)$?

- $9xy$
- $-3xy$
- $-9x^7y^3$
- $-9xy$

## Explanation

First calculate the power $(3x^2y)^2 = 9x^4y^2$, squaring the coefficient $3$ as well. In a **quotient of monomials**, divide the coefficients and subtract the exponents, giving $9 : (-1) = -9$, $x^{4-3} = x$, and $y^{2-1} = y$. The result is therefore $-9xy$. The result $-3xy$ omits the square of the coefficient, while $-9x^7y^3$ adds the exponents as in a product.
