What is the complete factorization of $x^3 - 2x^2 - 9x + 18$?

- $(x + 2)(x - 3)(x + 3)$
- $(x - 2)(x - 3)^2$
- $(x - 2)(x + 3)^2$
- $(x - 2)(x - 3)(x + 3)$

## Explanation

To **factor by grouping**, factor $x^2$ out of the first two terms and $-9$ out of the last two, giving $x^2(x - 2) - 9(x - 2) = (x - 2)(x^2 - 9)$. The factor $x^2 - 9$ is a difference of squares, so $x^2 - 9 = (x - 3)(x + 3)$. The complete factorization is therefore $(x - 2)(x - 3)(x + 3)$. The product $(x + 2)(x - 3)(x + 3)$ has constant term $-18$, so the sign of $2$ is incorrect.
