Which of the following simplifications is correct?

- $\dfrac{x^2 + 2x}{x} = x + 2$, for $x \neq 0$
- $\dfrac{x + 4}{4} = x + 1$
- $\dfrac{x^2 + 9}{x + 3} = x + 3$, for $x \neq -3$
- $\dfrac{x^2 - 1}{x - 1} = x - 1$, for $x \neq 1$

## Explanation

Simplify a rational expression by dividing its numerator and denominator by a common **factor**. Since $x^2 + 2x = x(x + 2)$, the factor $x$ cancels, and for $x \neq 0$ this leaves $x + 2$. In $\dfrac{x + 4}{4}$ the number $4$ is a term in the numerator rather than a factor. Indeed, at $x = 4$ the fraction equals $2$ rather than $5$. The polynomial $x^2 + 9$ is irreducible and does not have $x + 3$ as a factor, while $x^2 - 1 = (x - 1)(x + 1)$ gives $x + 1$ rather than $x - 1$.
