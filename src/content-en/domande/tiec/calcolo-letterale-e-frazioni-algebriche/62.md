Which of the following polynomials is a factor of $x^4 - 81$?

- $x^2 + 9$
- $x - 9$
- $x^2 + 3$
- $x + 9$

## Explanation

The polynomial is a **difference of squares** because $x^4 = (x^2)^2$ and $81 = 9^2$, so $x^4 - 81 = (x^2 - 9)(x^2 + 9)$. The factor $x^2 - 9$ factors further as $(x - 3)(x + 3)$, while $x^2 + 9$ is irreducible. The factors are therefore $x - 3$, $x + 3$, and $x^2 + 9$. The binomials $x - 9$ and $x + 9$ use $9$ in place of its square root $3$.
