An expression is factored as $(x-a)^2(x+b)$ with $a\ne-b$. Which feature of the graph identifies the double root?

- At $x=a$ the graph touches the axis without changing sign
- At $x=a$ the function always changes sign
- At $x=-b$ the graph touches the axis without crossing
- At $x=a$ the graph has an excluded point

## Explanation

Near $a$ the factor $x+b$ is nonzero and keeps its sign. The square is positive on both sides of $a$ and zero at $a$. The product therefore touches zero without changing sign there.
