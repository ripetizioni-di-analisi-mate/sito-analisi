What is the factored form of $2x^3 - 12x^2 + 18x$?

- $2x(x + 3)^2$
- $2x(x - 3)^2$
- $2x(x - 3)(x + 3)$
- $x(2x - 3)^2$

## Explanation

**Factoring out the common factor** $2x$ gives $2x(x^2 - 6x + 9)$. The trinomial $x^2 - 6x + 9$ is a perfect square because $x^2$ and $9$ are the squares of $x$ and $3$, and $6x = 2 \cdot x \cdot 3$. The minus sign on the cross term indicates that the binomial is $x - 3$, so the polynomial is $2x(x - 3)^2$. The result $2x(x - 3)(x + 3)$ confuses the square of a binomial with a difference of squares.
