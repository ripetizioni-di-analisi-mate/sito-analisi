For $x \neq -2$ and $x \neq \pm 1$, what is the quotient $\dfrac{x^2 - x}{x + 2} : \dfrac{x^2 - 1}{x^2 + 4x + 4}$?

- $\dfrac{x + 1}{x(x + 2)}$
- $\dfrac{x(x + 2)}{x - 1}$
- $\dfrac{x}{x + 1}$
- $\dfrac{x(x + 2)}{x + 1}$

## Explanation

To divide, multiply the first fraction by the reciprocal of the second, giving $\dfrac{x(x - 1)}{x + 2} \cdot \dfrac{(x + 2)^2}{(x - 1)(x + 1)}$. Use the factorizations $x^2 - x = x(x - 1)$, $x^2 - 1 = (x - 1)(x + 1)$, and $x^2 + 4x + 4 = (x + 2)^2$. Cancel $x - 1$ and one factor of $x + 2$, leaving $\dfrac{x(x + 2)}{x + 1}$. The result $\dfrac{x + 1}{x(x + 2)}$ is the reciprocal, obtained by inverting the dividend instead of the divisor.
