The quotient $\dfrac{x^2-x}{x+2}\div\dfrac{x^2-1}{(x+2)^2}$ simplifies to $\dfrac{x(x+2)}{x+1}$. Which extra inputs would a calculator accept if it checked only the final formula?

- Only $x=-2$
- $x=-1$ and $x=1$
- Only $x=-1$
- $x=-2$ and $x=1$

## Explanation

The original quotient excludes $-2$ from its denominators and $\pm1$ because they make the divisor fraction zero. The reduced form still excludes $-1$ but has values at $-2$ and $1$. Those two inputs therefore require separate checks.
