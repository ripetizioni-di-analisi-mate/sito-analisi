If every variable is nonzero, which of the following quotients is a monomial?

- $15a^4b^2 : (5a^2b^3)$
- $15a^4b^2 : (5a^5b)$
- $15a^4b^2 : (5a^2bc)$
- $15a^4b^2 : (3a^2b)$

## Explanation

A **quotient of monomials** is a monomial only if each variable in the divisor appears in the dividend with an exponent at least as large. In $15a^4b^2 : (3a^2b)$ the exponents in the divisor are smaller than those in the dividend, so the result is the monomial $5a^2b$. A variable remains in the denominator of each other quotient because $b^3$ has a greater exponent than $b^2$, $a^5$ has a greater exponent than $a^4$, and $c$ does not appear in the dividend. Indeed, the results are $\dfrac{3a^2}{b}$, $\dfrac{3b}{a}$, and $\dfrac{3a^2b}{c}$.
