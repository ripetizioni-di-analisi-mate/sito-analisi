What is the sum of the coefficients of the polynomial obtained by expanding $(2x - 1)^5$?

- $1$
- $32$
- $-1$
- $31$

## Explanation

The sum of the coefficients of a polynomial $P(x)$ equals $P(1)$ because at $x = 1$ every power of $x$ equals $1$. Therefore, without expanding, calculate $(2 \cdot 1 - 1)^5 = 1^5 = 1$. The value $32 = 2^5$ is only the leading coefficient, and $-1 = (-1)^5$ is the constant term.
