Which expression equals $(a + b)^2 - (a - b)^2$?

- $4ab$
- $0$
- $2a^2 + 2b^2$
- $2ab$

## Explanation

The formula for the **square of a binomial** gives $(a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$ and $(a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2$. In the difference, the squares $a^2$ and $b^2$ cancel and the cross terms give $2ab - (-2ab) = 4ab$. The result $0$ does not apply the minus sign to $-2ab$, and $2a^2 + 2b^2$ is the *sum* of the two squares.
