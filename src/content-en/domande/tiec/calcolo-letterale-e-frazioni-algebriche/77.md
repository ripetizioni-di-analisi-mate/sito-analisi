Two lamps flash every $x$ and $3x$ seconds, with $x$ a positive integer. A third flashes every $2x$ seconds. If all three flash together now, how many seconds pass before their next simultaneous instantaneous flash?

- $6x$
- $x$
- $6x^3$
- $3x$

## Explanation

Every simultaneous flash must occur at a multiple of all three intervals. In units of $x$, the required times are common multiples of $1,3,2$. The first positive one is $6$, or $6x$ seconds.
