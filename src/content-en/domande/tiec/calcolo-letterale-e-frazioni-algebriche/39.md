It is known that $P(x)$ is divisible by $x-3$ and that $Q(3)=2$. What is the remainder when $P(x)+Q(x)$ is divided by $x-3$?

- $5$
- $3$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

Divisibility gives $P(3)=0$. The remainder of the sum is its value at $3$, namely $P(3)+Q(3)=0+2$. The coefficients of the two polynomials are not needed.
