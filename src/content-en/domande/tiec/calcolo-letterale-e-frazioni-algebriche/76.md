What is the greatest common divisor of $6x^2 - 6$ and $4x^2 - 8x + 4$?

- $2(x - 1)$
- $x - 1$
- $2(x - 1)^2$
- $12(x - 1)^2(x + 1)$

## Explanation

Factoring out common factors and using special product formulas gives $6x^2 - 6 = 6(x - 1)(x + 1)$ and $4x^2 - 8x + 4 = 4(x - 1)^2$. The GCD of the coefficients $6$ and $4$ is $2$, and the common factor $x - 1$ is taken with its smaller exponent, $1$. The **greatest common divisor** is therefore $2(x - 1)$. The polynomial $12(x - 1)^2(x + 1)$ is the LCM, and $x - 1$ omits the numerical coefficients.
