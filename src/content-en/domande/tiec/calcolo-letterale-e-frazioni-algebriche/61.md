A register contains a monic cubic polynomial with distinct zeros $-1,-2,3$. Which coefficient must be zero, even before all the factors are multiplied out?

- The coefficient of $x^2$
- The coefficient of $x^3$
- The constant term
- The coefficient of $x$

## Explanation

The factors are $(x+1)(x+2)(x-3)$. A term in $x^2$ comes from taking one constant and an $x$ from each of the other two factors. The contributions sum to $1+2-3=0$, while the full expansion is $x^3-7x-6$.
