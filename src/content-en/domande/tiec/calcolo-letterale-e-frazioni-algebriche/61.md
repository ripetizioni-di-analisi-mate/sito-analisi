What is the complete factorization of $x^3 - 7x - 6$?

- $(x + 1)(x + 2)(x - 3)$
- $(x - 1)(x - 2)(x + 3)$
- $(x + 1)(x - 2)(x + 3)$
- $(x + 1)^2(x - 6)$

## Explanation

Look for integer zeros among the divisors of $-6$. The number $-1$ is a zero because $-1 + 7 - 6 = 0$. Use **synthetic division** with coefficients $1$, $0$, $-7$, and $-6$, where $0$ stands for the missing quadratic term. The quotient upon division by $x + 1$ is $x^2 - x - 6$. To factor $x^2 - x - 6$, find two numbers with sum $-1$ and product $-6$, giving $(x - 3)(x + 2)$. The complete factorization is therefore $(x + 1)(x + 2)(x - 3)$. The product $(x - 1)(x - 2)(x + 3)$ reverses the sign of the constant in each of the three factors.
