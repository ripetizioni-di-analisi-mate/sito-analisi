What is the total degree of the monomial $-\dfrac{2}{3}a^3b^2c$?

- $5$
- $3$
- $6$
- $1$

## Explanation

The **degree** of a monomial is the sum of the exponents of its variables. The coefficient $-\dfrac{2}{3}$ does not affect it. The variable $c$ has exponent $1$ even though the exponent is not written. The degree is therefore $3 + 2 + 1 = 6$. The value $5$ omits the exponent of $c$, while $3$ and $1$ are the degrees in $a$ and $c$ alone, respectively.
