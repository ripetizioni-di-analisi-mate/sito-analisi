A quadratic has zeros $1$ and $-3$ and satisfies $P(0)=6$. Which coefficient must be placed in front of $(x-1)(x+3)$ to recover $P$?

- $2$
- $-3$
- $-2$
- $6$

## Explanation

The roots determine the two factors but leave a coefficient $k$ undetermined. Setting $x=0$ gives $6=k(-1)(3)=-3k$. The recorded value therefore fixes $k=-2$.
