## Exercise 1

A continuous beam on three supports is fixed at $A$ and $C$ and supported vertically at the intermediate point $B$. The beam is continuous through $B$. Span $AB$ has length $3\,\mathrm m$ and $EI_{AB}=4\,500\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Span $BC$ has length $2\,\mathrm m$ and $EI_{BC}=4\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A counterclockwise couple of $28\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at $B$. Calculate the rotation at $B$ and the two couples the joint transmits to the members.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,5,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello;C,incastro,0" coppie="B,1,C_B" quote="A,B,3 m;B,C,2 m" titolo="Continuous beam with an external couple at B"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
m_{B,AB}&=\frac{4\cdot4\,500}{3}\varphi_B,\\[6pt]
m_{B,BC}&=\frac{4\cdot4\,000}{2}\varphi_B,\\[6pt]
(6\,000+8\,000)\varphi_B&=28.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\varphi_B&=0.002\,\mathrm{rad},\\[6pt]
m_{B,AB}&=12\,\mathrm{kN\,m},\qquad
m_{B,BC}=16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The rotation of the intermediate joint is the only free coordinate. Beam continuity requires both spans to have the same rotation at the joint. Each span transmits a couple proportional to its rotational stiffness. Both couples are counterclockwise. The larger one corresponds to span $BC$.

**Check.** The internal bending moment has end values $-6$ and $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on the first span and $-16$ and $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on the second. The curvature integrals give $\dfrac{3(12-6)}{2\cdot4\,500}=0.002$ and $\dfrac{2(8-16)}{2\cdot4\,000}=-0.002$. They add to zero, as required by the zero rotations at $A$ and $C$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A continuous beam is fixed at ends $A$ and $C$ and supported vertically at $B$. Its two continuous spans each have length $3\,\mathrm m$ and $EI=6\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Only span $AB$ carries the uniform downward load $8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find the rotation of joint $B$.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello;C,incastro,0" carichi="A,B,0.45,0.45,q" quote="A,B,3 m;B,C,3 m" titolo="Continuous beam loaded only on the left span"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
m^{(0)}_{B,AB}&=-\frac{qL^2}{12}
=-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m},\\[6pt]
m_{B,AB}&=-6+8\,000\varphi_B,\\[6pt]
m_{B,BC}&=8\,000\varphi_B.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
0&=-6+16\,000\varphi_B,\\[6pt]
\varphi_B&=0.000375\,\mathrm{rad}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Restraining joint $B$ produces a clockwise end couple in the left span. Since the right span is unloaded, its fixed-end contribution is zero. When the joint rotation is released, both spans develop an additional counterclockwise couple. Together they balance the load contribution, and the resulting rotation is counterclockwise.

**Check.** With the calculated rotation, the external action at $A$ is $6+\dfrac{2\cdot6\,000}{3}\cdot0.000375=7.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The internal moment at $AB$ is therefore $M(x)=-7.5+13.5x-4x^2$ in the units used here because its value at $B$ is $-3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The difference between the curvature integral and the calculated rotation is $\dfrac{1}{6\,000}\int_0^3(-7.5+13.5x-4x^2)\,dx-0.000375=0$ in radians.

</div>
