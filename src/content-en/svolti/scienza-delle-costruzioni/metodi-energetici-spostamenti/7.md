## Exercise 1

A beam of span $5\,\mathrm m$ and flexural rigidity $EI=6\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ has both end rotations restrained. The left end is fixed and the right end moves upward by $5\,\mathrm{mm}$. With no loads on the member, find the external couples and the vertical force at $B$.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,incastro,0" quote="A,B,5 m" frecce="5,0,5,0.9,Δ" etichette="5.17,-0.13,B" titolo="Prescribed displacement at the right end"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\psi&=\frac{0.005}{5}=0.001\,\mathrm{rad},\\[6pt]
m_A=m_B&=-\frac{6EI}{L}\psi
=-7.2\,\mathrm{kN\,m},\\[6pt]
F_B&=-\frac{m_A+m_B}{L}
=2.88\,\mathrm{kN}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The chord rotates counterclockwise. Because both ends have zero rotation, their rotations relative to the chord are negative. The required couples are therefore clockwise. The force at $B$ acts upward, balancing the moments of the two couples.

**Check.** The cubic deflection field satisfying the four end conditions is $w(x)=\Delta\left(3\dfrac{x^2}{L^2}-2\dfrac{x^3}{L^3}\right)$. The external shear force at $B$ is $-EIw^{\prime\prime\prime}(L)$. The residual relative to the result above is $\dfrac{12\cdot6\,000\cdot0.005}{5^3}-2.88=0$, in $\mathrm{kN}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A portal frame has two identical columns of height $3\,\mathrm m$, each with $EI=18\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ and fixed at its base. Assume a crossbeam rigid in its own plane and axially inextensible columns. A horizontal force of $32\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right on the crossbeam. Find the common horizontal displacement of the top joints.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3;D,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,incastro;D,incastro" forze="C,0.88,0,Q" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.9" etichette="2,3.32,rigid crossbeam" titolo="Portal frame with a rigid crossbeam and a horizontal load"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
k_c&=\frac{12EI}{H^3}
=8\,000\,\mathrm{kN/m},\\[6pt]
Q&=2k_c\Delta,\\[6pt]
\Delta&=\frac{32}{2\cdot8\,000}\,\mathrm m
=2\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Because the columns are axially inextensible, the two top joints stay at the same elevation. The rigid crossbeam remains horizontal and prevents rotation at the tops of the columns. Each column deflects with zero rotation at both ends. The resisting forces have equal magnitudes, so each column transmits $16\,\mathrm{kN}$.

**Check.** In each column the internal bending moment varies linearly from a value of magnitude $\dfrac{16\cdot3}{2}=24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ to its negative. The residual between the external work and the strain energy of the columns is $\dfrac{32\cdot0.002}{2}-2\int_0^3\dfrac{(16x-24)^2}{2\cdot18\,000}\,dx=0$, in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

</div>
