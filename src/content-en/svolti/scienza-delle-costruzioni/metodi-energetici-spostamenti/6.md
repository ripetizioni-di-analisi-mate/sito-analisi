## Exercise 1

A beam of length $4\,\mathrm m$ has $EI=4\,800\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Both end translations are restrained. Impose a counterclockwise rotation of $0.005\,\mathrm{rad}$ at $A$ while maintaining zero rotation at $B$. With no other loads, calculate the external end couples.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,incastro,0" quote="A,B,4 m" etichette="0.65,0.53,φ_A" titolo="Prescribed rotation at A with end B fixed"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
m_A&=\frac{4EI}{L}\varphi_A
=\frac{4\cdot4\,800}{4}\cdot0.005\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
=24\,\mathrm{kN\,m},\\[6pt]
m_B&=\frac{2EI}{L}\varphi_A
=12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Since the end displacements are zero, the chord remains horizontal. The first equation contains the direct stiffness coefficient for rotation at $A$. The second contains the carry-over coefficient relating that rotation to the moment at the restrained end. Both couples act counterclockwise.

**Check.** Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives a downward force of $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$. Vertical force equilibrium gives an upward force of $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $A$. Taking moments about $B$ gives $24+12-9\cdot4=0$, in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A beam of length $3\,\mathrm m$ has $EI=4\,500\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. The translations are restrained. Impose a counterclockwise rotation of $0.004\,\mathrm{rad}$ at $A$, with a pin at $B$. With no other loads, calculate the external couple at $A$ and the rotation at $B$.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,3 m" etichette="0.57,0.5,φ_A" titolo="Beam with a hinge at the far end"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
m_B&=\frac{EI}{L}(2\varphi_A+4\varphi_B)=0,\\[6pt]
\varphi_B&=-\frac{\varphi_A}{2}
=-0.002\,\mathrm{rad},\\[6pt]
m_A&=\frac{EI}{L}(4\varphi_A+2\varphi_B)\\[6pt]
&=\frac{3EI}{L}\varphi_A=18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The pin prevents translation at $B$ and permits free rotation. Consequently, the applied couple at $B$ is zero. Eliminate the free rotation from the equation at $A$ to find the required counterclockwise couple. The rotation at $B$ is clockwise.

**Check.** The internal bending moment varies linearly from $-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ to zero. The difference in rotation equals the integral of curvature, giving a residual of $\dfrac{(-18)\cdot3}{2\cdot4\,500}-(-0.002-0.004)=0$ in radians.

</div>
