## Exercise 1

A cantilever of span $2\,\mathrm m$ has $EI=2\,400\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A downward force of $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the tip. Calculate the tip deflection, neglecting shear deformation.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,2 m" forze="B,0,-0.55,P" titolo="Cantilever with a vertical tip force"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
M(x)&=-P(L-x),\\[6pt]
U(P)&=\int_0^L\frac{P^2(L-x)^2}{2EI}\,dx
=\frac{P^2L^3}{6EI},\\[6pt]
v_B&=\frac{\partial U}{\partial P}
=\frac{PL^3}{3EI}
=0.010\,\mathrm m.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The force has a moment arm of $L-x$ about a section a distance $x$ from the fixed end. Squaring the moment in the strain energy expression removes its negative sign. Differentiating with respect to $P$ gives the deflection in the direction of the force, so the result is $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward.

**Check.** Integrating $EIv^{\prime\prime}=P(L-x)$ with $v(0)=v^\prime(0)=0$ gives $v(x)=\dfrac{P}{EI}\left(\dfrac{Lx^2}{2}-\dfrac{x^3}{6}\right)$. At the tip, the residual is $\dfrac{9}{2\,400}\left(\dfrac{2\cdot2^2}{2}-\dfrac{2^3}{6}\right)-0.010=0$, in $\mathrm m$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The cantilever has length $3\,\mathrm m$ and $EI=4\,500\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A downward force $P=6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a clockwise couple $C=3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ act at the tip. Find the tip rotation, taking counterclockwise as positive.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,3 m" forze="B,0,-0.8,P" coppie="B,-1,C" titolo="Downward force and clockwise couple"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
M(x)&=-P(L-x)-C,\\[6pt]
U(C)&=\frac{1}{2EI}
\left(\frac{P^2L^3}{3}+PC L^2+C^2L\right),\\[6pt]
\frac{\partial U}{\partial C}
&=\frac{PL^2}{2EI}+\frac{CL}{EI}
=0.008\,\mathrm{rad}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\varphi_B=-0.008\,\mathrm{rad}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The force and the couple both produce a negative bending moment. The strain energy also contains a cross term equal to twice the product. It accounts for the effect of the force on the rotation conjugate to the couple. Since variable $C$ is positive clockwise, a positive derivative denotes clockwise rotation. The requested rotation takes counterclockwise as positive and is therefore negative.

**Check.** Integrating the curvature from the fixed end to the tip gives the counterclockwise rotation. With the calculated rotation, the residual is $\dfrac{1}{4\,500}\int_0^3[-6(3-x)-3]\,dx+0.008=0$ in radians.

</div>
