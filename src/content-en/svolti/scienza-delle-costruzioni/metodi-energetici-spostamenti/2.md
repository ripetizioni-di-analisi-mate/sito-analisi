## Exercise 1

A cantilever of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ has $EI=2\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A counterclockwise couple of $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is applied gradually at the free end. At full load the rotation in the same direction is $0.015\,\mathrm{rad}$. Calculate the strain energy from the work done by the couple.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,3 m" coppie="B,1,C" titolo="Counterclockwise couple at the tip"}

$$
U=\frac{C\varphi_B}{2}
=\frac{10\cdot0.015}{2}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
=0.075\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The couple increases from zero as the rotation increases. Since the relation is linear, the work is the area of the triangle under the moment–rotation line. The reaction couple does no work because the rotation at the fixed end is zero.

**Check.** The internal bending moment is constant at $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The internal energy integral gives a residual of $\dfrac{10^2\cdot3}{2\cdot2\,000}-0.075=0$, in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A bar with $EA=30\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$ is restrained axially at $A$. Cross sections $B$ and $C$ are distances $2$ and $4\,\mathrm m$, respectively, from $A$. A rightward force of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$ produces a rightward displacement of $1\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $B$. Find the displacement at $C$ when the only load is a rightward force of $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,2 m;B,C,2 m" forze="C,0.7,0,15 kN" titolo="First load case with the load at C"}
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,2 m;B,C,2 m" forze="B,0.95,0,24 kN" titolo="Second load case with the load at B"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
15u_C&=24\cdot1,\\[6pt]
u_C&=\frac{24}{15}\,\mathrm{mm}
=1.6\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For the mutual work of the first system, pair its force at $C$ with the displacement at $C$ produced by the second system. The mutual work of the second system uses the force and displacement at $B$. Both forces act in the same direction, so a positive result indicates a rightward displacement.

**Check.** In the second system, the axial force is $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ only in $AB$. Since $BC$ translates without elongating, the residual is $\dfrac{24\cdot2}{30\,000}-0.0016=0$, in $\mathrm m$.

</div>
