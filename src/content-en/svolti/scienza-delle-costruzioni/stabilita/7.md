## Exercise 1

The column shown is pinned at both ends in the plane of bending. Its length is $5\,\mathrm m$. The flexural rigidity is $EI=500\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. In the unloaded member, the initial imperfection follows the first mode with amplitude $1.5\,\mathrm{mm}$. Apply $P=12\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$. Calculate the total deflection and the additional deflection at midlength.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,-1.1,0,P" quote="A,B,5 m,0.75"  titolo="Compression member and its flexural end restraints"}

The critical load is $P_{\mathrm{cr}}=20\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$. The load ratio is $\dfrac35$, so
$$
\begin{aligned}
v_{\max}&=\dfrac{1.5}{1-\dfrac35}\,\mathrm{mm}
=3.75\,\mathrm{mm},\\[6pt]
\Delta v_{\max}&=3.75\,\mathrm{mm}-1.5\,\mathrm{mm}
=2.25\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Write equilibrium using the total offset because it determines the moment arm of the axial force. The elastic bending response depends on the additional curvature relative to the unloaded shape. Subtract the initial imperfection from the total deflection to obtain the smaller, load-induced increment.

**Check.** At midlength, the additional elastic moment balances the moment of the force. In the units used here, the residual is $20\pi^2\cdot2.25-12\pi^2\cdot3.75=0$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The column shown is pinned at both ends in the plane of bending. Its length is $4\,\mathrm m$. The flexural rigidity is $EI=400\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. The initial imperfection follows the first mode with amplitude $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. Under a concentric compressive force, the total deflection at midlength is $5\,\mathrm{mm}$. Determine the force.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,-0.88,0,P" quote="A,B,4 m,0.75"  titolo="Compression member and its flexural end restraints"}

The critical load $25\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the measured deflection give
$$
1-\dfrac{P}{P_{\mathrm{cr}}}
=\dfrac{2}{5}.
$$
Therefore
$$
P=\dfrac35P_{\mathrm{cr}}=15\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The ratio of the initial deflection to the total deflection equals the denominator in the amplification formula. Since the total deflection exceeds the unloaded offset, one minus this ratio is positive and corresponds to a compressive force below the critical load.

**Check.** The additional deflection is $3\,\mathrm{mm}$. Expressing all lengths in the same units, the curvature balance at midlength gives a residual of $25\pi^2\cdot3-15\pi^2\cdot5=0$.

</div>
