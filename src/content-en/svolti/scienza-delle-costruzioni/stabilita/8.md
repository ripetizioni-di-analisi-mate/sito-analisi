## Exercise 1

The straight member shown has $L=4\,\mathrm m$ and $EI=320\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. The supports restrain transverse displacement. The load line has an eccentricity $e=2\,\mathrm{mm}$ and produces the moment $P(e+v)$. The force is $5\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$. Calculate the midspan deflection.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="0 0.55,0.8,0,P;4 0.55,-0.8,0,P" tratteggi="0,0.55 4,0.55" frecce="2,0,2,0.55,e" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Load line parallel to the axis, with the eccentricity shown out of scale"}

The wave number is $k=\sqrt{\dfrac{P}{EI}}=\dfrac{\pi}{8}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. The symmetric solution for the deflected shape gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
v\left(\dfrac L2\right)
&=e\left[\dfrac{1}{\cos(\dfrac{kL}{2})}-1\right]\\[6pt]
&=2\left[\dfrac1{\cos(\pi/4)}-1\right]\,\mathrm{mm}\\[4pt]
&=2(\sqrt2-1)\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The force already has a moment arm when the member is straight. The midlength displacement increases the moment arm and thus the bending moment. Symmetry requires zero rotation at midlength. The zero-displacement conditions at both ends determine the coefficient of the cosine solution.

**Check.** For the form $v=e\left[\dfrac{\cos(k(x-\dfrac L2))}{\cos(\dfrac{kL}{2})}-1\right]$, we have $v''=-k^2(v+e)$, so $EI v''+P(v+e)=(P-EI k^2)(v+e)=0$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The member shown has $L=6\,\mathrm m$ and $EI=900\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. It is initially straight, and its supports restrain transverse displacement at both ends. The eccentric compressive load produces moment $P(e+v)$, with $e=3\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the load that produces a midlength deflection $3\,\mathrm{mm}$, assuming $0<P<P_{\mathrm{cr}}$.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="0 0.55,1,0,P;6 0.55,-1,0,P" tratteggi="0,0.55 6,0.55" frecce="3,0,3,0.55,e" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="The eccentricity has the same magnitude at both ends"}

Setting the deflection equal to the eccentricity gives
$$
1=\dfrac{1}{\cos(\dfrac{kL}{2})}-1,\qquad
\cos(\dfrac{kL}{2})=\dfrac12.
$$
Since $P<P_{\mathrm{cr}}$, we have $0<\dfrac{kL}{2}<\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. On this interval, $\dfrac{kL}{2}=\dfrac{\pi}{3}$. It follows that
$$
P=EI\left(\dfrac{2\pi}{3L}\right)^2
=\dfrac{100\pi^2}{9}\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first interval of the cosine selects a unique wave number because the cosine decreases strictly between zero and a right angle. Square the wave number and multiply by the flexural rigidity to obtain the load.

**Check.** The ratio to the Euler load $25\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$ is $\dfrac49$. The argument at midlength is therefore $\dfrac{\pi}{2}\sqrt{\dfrac49}=\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, and the resulting deflection is $3(2-1)\,\mathrm{mm}=3\,\mathrm{mm}$.

</div>
