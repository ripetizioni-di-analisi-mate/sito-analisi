## Exercise 1

A rectangular section of width $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $120\,\mathrm{mm}$ is in pure bending with $M_z=4.8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Find the normal stress at each extreme fiber.
::struttura{poligoni="-40,-60 40,-60 40,60 -40,60" frecce="-49.6,0,49.6,0,z;0,-69.6,0,69.6,y" quote="-40 -60,40 -60,80 mm,9.6;40 -60,40 60,120 mm,19.2" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangular section in pure bending"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_z&=\frac{80\cdot120^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}
=11\,520\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4},\\[6pt]
W_z&=\frac{I_z}{60\,\mathrm{mm}}=192\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3},\\[6pt]
\sigma_{\mathrm{upper}}&=-\frac{4.8\cdot10^6}{192\,000}\,\mathrm{MPa}=-25\,\mathrm{MPa},\\[6pt]
\sigma_{\mathrm{lower}}&=\frac{4.8\cdot10^6}{192\,000}\,\mathrm{MPa}=25\,\mathrm{MPa}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The bending moment is positive and puts the top fibers in compression. The section modulus is the same for both extreme fibers because they are equidistant from the centroidal axis. The two stresses therefore have equal magnitudes.

</div>

**Independent check**

The compressive and tensile stress resultants must be equal and opposite. Each stress distribution is triangular over an area of width $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $60\,\mathrm{mm}$, with extreme-fiber stress of magnitude $25\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

$$
N_{\mathrm{upper}}+N_{\mathrm{lower}}
=\frac{80\cdot60}{2}(-25+25)\,\mathrm N=0.
$$

## Exercise 2

A rectangular beam of width $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $160\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries a concentric compressive force of magnitude $32\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a bending moment $M_z=8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Find the normal stresses at the extreme fibers and the elevation of the neutral axis.
::struttura{poligoni="-50,-80 50,-80 50,80 -50,80" frecce="-62,0,62,0,z;0,-92,0,92,y" quote="-50 -80,50 -80,100 mm,12;50 -80,50 80,160 mm,24" riferimento=0 titolo="Cross section under combined compression and uniaxial bending"}

$$
A=16\,000\,\mathrm{mm^2},\qquad
W_z=\frac{100\cdot160^2}{6}\,\mathrm{mm^3}
=\frac{1\,280\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^3}.
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_{\mathrm{upper}}&=-2\,\mathrm{MPa}-18.75\,\mathrm{MPa}
=-20.75\,\mathrm{MPa},\\[6pt]
\sigma_{\mathrm{lower}}&=-2\,\mathrm{MPa}+18.75\,\mathrm{MPa}
=16.75\,\mathrm{MPa}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
0=-2-\frac{18.75}{80}y_0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
y_0=-\frac{128}{15}\,\mathrm{mm}\simeq-8.53\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The axial force adds a uniform compressive stress of $2\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The bending stress varies linearly and equals $-18.75\,\mathrm{MPa}$ at the top fiber. The stress at mid-depth is still compressive, so the fiber at zero stress lies below the centroid.

</div>

**Independent check**

Recover the axial force by integrating the linear stress distribution. Its average ordinate is the arithmetic mean of the ordinates at the two extreme fibers.

$$
\frac{-20.75+16.75}{2}\cdot16\,000\,\mathrm N
-(-32\,000\,\mathrm N)=0.
$$
