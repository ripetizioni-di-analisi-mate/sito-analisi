## Exercise 1

A rectangular section has width $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $z$ and depth $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $y$. The applied moments are $M_z=4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $M_y=1.08\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Find the neutral axis and the extreme normal stresses.
::struttura{poligoni="-30,-50 30,-50 30,50 -30,50" frecce="-37.2,0,37.2,0,z;0,-57.2,0,57.2,y" quote="-30 -50,30 -50,60 mm,7.2;30 -50,30 50,100 mm,14.4" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangular section under two bending moments"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_z&=5\,000\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4},\qquad I_y=1\,800\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4},\\[6pt]
\sigma_x(y,z)&=(-0.8y+0.6z)\,\mathrm{MPa}
\quad (y,z\ \text{in }\mathrm{mm}),\\[6pt]
\sigma_x=0 &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad y=\frac34z.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_{\min}&=\sigma_x(50,-30)=-58\,\mathrm{MPa},\\[6pt]
\sigma_{\max}&=\sigma_x(-50,30)=58\,\mathrm{MPa}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The symmetry axes of the rectangle are principal axes. The moment about $z$ compresses the upper fibers, and the moment about $y$ compresses fibers with negative $z$. Both contributions are compressive at the corner with $y>0$ and $z<0$. The diagonally opposite corner is in tension at a stress of the same magnitude.

</div>

**Independent check**

Recover the resultant moment about $z$ from the calculated stress field. By symmetry the term containing $yz$ integrates to zero.

$$
-\int_A y(-0.8y+0.6z)\,\mathrm dA
-4\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{N\,mm}
=(0.8\cdot5\,000\,000-4\cdot10^6)\,\mathrm{N\,mm}=0.
$$

## Exercise 2

A compressive force of magnitude $72\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts on a rectangular section of width $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $120\,\mathrm{mm}$. Its line of action passes through the point with coordinates $e_y=10\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $e_z=10\,\mathrm{mm}$. Determine whether the entire section is in compression and find the extreme stresses.
::struttura{poligoni="-50,-60 50,-60 50,60 -50,60" frecce="-62,0,62,0,z;0,-72,0,72,y" quote="-50 -60,50 -60,100 mm,12;50 -60,50 60,120 mm,24" punti="10,10,P" riferimento=0 titolo="Point through which the eccentric force acts"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{N}{A}&=-\frac{72\,000}{12\,000}\,\mathrm{MPa}=-6\,\mathrm{MPa},\\[6pt]
\frac{6e_y}{h}&=\frac{60}{120}=0.5,\qquad
\frac{6e_z}{b}=\frac{60}{100}=0.6,\\[6pt]
\sigma_{\max}&=6(-1+0.5+0.6)\,\mathrm{MPa}
=0.6\,\mathrm{MPa},\\[6pt]
\sigma_{\min}&=6(-1-0.5-0.6)\,\mathrm{MPa}
=-12.6\,\mathrm{MPa}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Both eccentricities are positive and the force is compressive. The two bending stresses therefore add in compression at the corner where both coordinates are positive. The stress at the opposite corner is tensile. The line of action lies outside the kern, and part of the section is in tension.

</div>

**Independent check**

The mean value of an affine function over a rectangle equals its value at the centroid. This value also equals the mean of the stresses at two diagonally opposite corners. The resultant force must equal the applied force.

$$
12\,000\frac{0.6-12.6}{2}\,\mathrm N
-(-72\,000\,\mathrm N)=0.
$$
