## Exercise 1

The horizontal beam $AB$ is fixed at $A$ and has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$. Measure coordinate $x$ to the right from $A$. At $B$, a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, force $P$ acts downward with magnitude $8\,\mathrm{kN}$. At $C$, a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, couple $\mathcal M_0$ acts clockwise with magnitude $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Find expressions for $N(x)$, $T(x)$, and $M(x)$ and draw the diagrams for the nonzero internal resultants.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,C,3 m;C,B,2 m" forze="B,0,-1.05,P" coppie="C,-1,M_0" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

The transverse forces have a downward resultant of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$. Their combined moment about $A$ is $-40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

Reaction forces are expressed in kilonewtons and the reaction moment in kilonewton-meters. The equilibrium equations are

$$
\begin{cases}M_A-(40)+(-10)=0\\[5pt]V_A-(8)=0\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_A=8,\quad M_A=50.
$$

With $x$ in meters, axial force and shear force are in kilonewtons, and bending moment is in kilonewton-meters.

For example, on the interval $3<x<5$, equilibrium of the left segment gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
T(x)&=8\\[6pt]M(x)&=8 x - 50 + 10=8 x - 40
\end{aligned}
$$

Repeating the equilibrium calculation on each interval bounded by loads or supports gives the following expressions.

$$
N(x)=0\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$

$$
T(x)=8\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$

$$
M(x)=\begin{cases}8 x - 50 & 0<x<3\\[5pt]8 x - 40 & 3<x<5\end{cases}.
$$
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" diagramma="0,0,3,0,8;3,0,5,0,8" segni=1 titolo="Shear force in kN"}
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" diagramma="0,0,3,0,8*s-50;3,0,5,0,8*s-16" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment in kN·m, plotted on the tension side"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Taking moments about $A$ eliminates the force components applied at $A$. For each interval isolate the segment to the left of the cut. The sum of vertical forces on the isolated segment gives the shear force, while their moments about the cut enter the expression for $M$. A point force contributes to the shear as soon as the cut passes it. The moment contribution increases with the moment arm from the force to the section. The applied couple contributes to the internal moment beyond its point of application, producing a jump opposite in sign to the couple.

</div>

**Independent check.** Taking moments about the point at coordinate $5\,\mathrm{m}$, with counterclockwise positive, gives

$$
-5\cdot(8)+5\cdot(8)-(40)+(-10)+(50)=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

## Exercise 2

The horizontal beam $AB$ is fixed at $A$ and has length $7\,\mathrm{m}$. Measure coordinate $x$ to the right from $A$. At $D$, a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, force $P$ acts downward with magnitude $9\,\mathrm{kN}$. On segment $CB$ between $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x=7\,\mathrm{m}$, a uniform downward load of intensity $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts. Find expressions for $N(x)$, $T(x)$, and $M(x)$ and draw the diagrams for the nonzero internal resultants.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,4,0;B,7,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,C,2 m;C,D,2 m;D,B,3 m" forze="D,0,-1.47,P" carichi="C,B,0.7,0.7,3 kN/m" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

The transverse forces have a downward resultant of $24\,\mathrm{kN}$. Their combined moment about $A$ is $- \dfrac{207}{2}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

Reaction forces are expressed in kilonewtons and the reaction moment in kilonewton-meters. The equilibrium equations are

$$
\begin{cases}M_A-(\frac{207}{2})+(0)=0\\[5pt]V_A-(24)=0\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_A=24,\quad M_A=\frac{207}{2}.
$$

With $x$ in meters, axial force and shear force are in kilonewtons, and bending moment is in kilonewton-meters.

For example, on the interval $2<x<4$, equilibrium of the left segment gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
T(x)&=24 + \left(6 - 3 x\right)=30 - 3 x\\[6pt]M(x)&=24 x - \frac{207}{2} + \left(- \frac{3 x^{2}}{2} + 6 x - 6\right)=- \frac{3 x^{2}}{2} + 30 x - \frac{219}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Repeating the equilibrium calculation on each interval bounded by loads or supports gives the following expressions.

$$
N(x)=0\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$

$$
T(x)=\begin{cases}24 & 0<x<2\\[5pt]30 - 3 x & 2<x<4\\[5pt]21 - 3 x & 4<x<7\end{cases}.
$$

$$
M(x)=\begin{cases}24 x - \frac{207}{2} & 0<x<2\\[5pt]- \frac{3 x^{2}}{2} + 30 x - \frac{219}{2} & 2<x<4\\[5pt]- \frac{3 x^{2}}{2} + 21 x - \frac{147}{2} & 4<x<7\end{cases}.
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,4,0;B,7,0" aste="A-B" diagramma="0,0,2,0,24*s-207/2;2,0,4,0,-3*s^2/2+24*s-111/2;4,0,7,0,-3*s^2/2+9*s-27/2" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment in kN·m, plotted on the tension side"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Taking moments about $A$ eliminates the force components applied at $A$. For each interval isolate the segment to the left of the cut. The sum of vertical forces on the isolated segment gives the shear force, while their moments about the cut enter the expression for $M$. A point force contributes to the shear as soon as the cut passes it. The moment contribution increases with the moment arm from the force to the section. Within a distributed load, integrate only the intensity to the left of the cut. In the moment equation, each elementary force has a moment arm equal to its distance from the section.

</div>
