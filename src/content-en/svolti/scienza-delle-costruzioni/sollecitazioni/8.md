## Exercise 1

The cantilever frame shown is fixed at $A$. Its column $AB$ has height $3\,\mathrm{m}$, and its beam $BC$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. At $C$, vertical force $P=12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward and horizontal force $F=5\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Find $N$, $T$, and $M$ for both members, oriented from $A$ to $B$ and from $B$ to $C$.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="C,0,-0.85,P;C,0.95,0,F" quote="A,B,3 m;B,C,4 m" titolo="Cantilever frame dimensions and loading"}

Overall equilibrium, with reaction forces in kilonewtons and moments in kilonewton-meters, gives

$$
\begin{cases}H_A+5=0\\[5pt]V_A-12=0\\[5pt]M_A-12\cdot 4-5\cdot 3=0\end{cases}\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad H_A=-5,\quad V_A=12,\quad M_A=63.
$$

Let $s$ denote the local coordinate along each member. On $AB$ the positive transverse direction points to the left, and on $BC$ it points upward.

With $s$ in meters, $N$ and $T$ are in kilonewtons and $M$ is in kilonewton-meters. The member equations are

$$
\begin{aligned}AB\quad &N(s)=-12,\qquad T(s)=5,\qquad M(s)=5 s - 63,\\[6pt]BC\quad &N(s)=5,\qquad T(s)=12,\qquad M(s)=12 s - 48.\end{aligned}
$$
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3" aste="A-B;B-C" diagramma="0,0,0,3,-12;0,3,4,3,5" segni=1 titolo="Axial force in kN"}
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3" aste="A-B;B-C" diagramma="0,0,0,3,5;0,3,4,3,12" segni=1 titolo="Shear force in kN"}
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3" aste="A-B;B-C" diagramma="0,0,0,3,5*s-63;0,3,4,3,12*s-48" verso=-1 etichette="0,-0.5," titolo="Moment in kN·m, plotted on the tension side"}

<div class="spiegazione">

On the segment extending to the free end, the internal forces at the cut must balance the vertical and horizontal forces applied at $C$. In the column, the vertical force acts along the member and puts it in compression. The horizontal component acts transversely, and its moment arm decreases as the cut approaches joint $B$. In the horizontal beam, the horizontal force is axial and puts the member in tension, while the vertical force produces shear and bending. The bending moment approaches the same value at the joint along either member because the only actions at $B$ are those transmitted by the rigid connection.

</div>

**Independent check.** Taking moments about $C$ for the whole frame gives

$$
63+(-5)\cdot 3-12\cdot 4=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

## Exercise 2
