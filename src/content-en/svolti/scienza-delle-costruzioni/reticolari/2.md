## Exercise 1

The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$, and $C=(3,4)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $AC$, and $BC$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $B$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A rightward force of $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. Find the support reactions and axial member forces. Model the supports as bilateral restraints.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="C,0.9,0,24 kN" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,3 m;6.45 4,6.45 0,4 m,-0.55" titolo="Horizontal force at the apex"}

Using $\mathrm{kN}$ and $\mathrm m$, overall equilibrium gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
H_A&=-24,\\
6V_B-4\cdot24&=0,\\
V_B&=16,\qquad V_A=-16.
\end{aligned}
$$
Because the chord is horizontal, vertical equilibrium at the two lower joints gives
$$
N_{AC}=-\dfrac54 V_A=20,\qquad N_{BC}=-\dfrac54 V_B=-20.
$$
Equilibrium of joint $A$ then gives
$$
N_{AB}=-H_A-\dfrac35N_{AC}=24-12=12.
$$
Member $AC$ is in tension, $BC$ is in compression, and $AB$ is in tension.

<div class="spiegazione">

The force at elevation $4\,\mathrm m$ produces a clockwise moment about $A$, so the roller reaction must act upward. The resultant external vertical force must remain zero, so the vertical reaction at the pin acts in the opposite direction. These opposite directions account for the opposite signs of the two diagonal forces.

**Check.** The horizontal projection at joint $C$ is $24-\dfrac35(20)+\dfrac35(-20)=24-12-12=0$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$, and $C=(3,4)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $AC$, and $BC$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $B$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. Two forces act at $C$, one of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and one of $48\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Calculate the support reactions and the axial member forces due to these two concurrent forces.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="C,-0.9,0,18 kN;C,0,-0.9,48 kN" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,3 m;6.45 4,6.45 0,4 m,-0.55" titolo="Horizontal and vertical forces concurrent at C"}

Use $\mathrm{kN}$ and $\mathrm m$. The two forces produce moments of opposite signs about $A$, so
$$
\begin{aligned}
6V_B+4\cdot18-3\cdot48&=0,\\
V_B&=12,\qquad V_A=48-12=36,\qquad H_A=18.
\end{aligned}
$$
At joint $C$, write
$$
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac35N_{AC}+\dfrac35N_{BC}-18=0,\\[6pt]
-\dfrac45N_{AC}-\dfrac45N_{BC}-48=0.
\end{cases}
$$
This gives $N_{BC}-N_{AC}=30$ and $N_{AC}+N_{BC}=-60$. Therefore
$$
N_{AC}=-45,\qquad N_{BC}=-15.
$$
At $A$, the result is $N_{AB}=-18-\dfrac35(-45)=9$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The leftward force produces a counterclockwise moment about $A$. It tends to raise the right side relative to the case with the vertical load alone. The roller reaction is therefore less than half the vertical load. Both diagonals are in compression at the apex, but the horizontal component of $AC$ exceeds that of $BC$ and balances the leftward force.

**Check.** The projections at joint $B$ are $-9-\dfrac35(-15)=0$ and $12+\dfrac45(-15)=0$.

</div>
