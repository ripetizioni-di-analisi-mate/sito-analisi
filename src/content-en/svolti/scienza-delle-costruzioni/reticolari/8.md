## Exercise 1

The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$, and $C=(3,4)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $AC$, and $BC$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $B$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A downward force of $64\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. Chord $AB$ has cross-sectional area $180\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and elastic modulus $200\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Calculate its axial force, normal stress, and change in length.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="C,0,-0.9,64 kN" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,3 m;6.45 4,6.45 0,4 m,-0.55" titolo="Vertical load and the lower chord to be analyzed"}

Symmetry gives $V_A=V_B=32\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the vertical loading gives $H_A=0$. Equilibrium of joint $A$ gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{AC}&=-\dfrac54\cdot32\,\mathrm{kN}=-40\,\mathrm{kN},\\[6pt]
N_{AB}&=-\dfrac35(-40\,\mathrm{kN})=24\,\mathrm{kN}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the chord, the normal stress and axial rigidity are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_{AB}&=\dfrac{24\,000}{180}\,\mathrm{MPa}=\dfrac{400}{3}\,\mathrm{MPa},\\[6pt]
EA&=200\,000\cdot180\,\mathrm N=36\,000\,\mathrm{kN}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The change in length is
$$
\Delta L_{AB}=\dfrac{24\cdot6}{36\,000}\,\mathrm m=4\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The chord balances the horizontal component of the diagonal compression. Since the chord force is positive, the average stress is tensile. To calculate the stress, express the force in newtons and the area in square millimeters. For the elongation, express both $N$ and $EA$ in kilonewtons. The intermediate result then has units of length.

**Check.** By symmetry, the diagonal at joint $B$ carries the same force as $AC$. Resolving the force gives $-24-\dfrac35(-40)=0$ and $32+\dfrac45(-40)=0$ in $\mathrm{kN}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$, and $C=(3,4)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $AC$, and $BC$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $B$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A downward force of $48\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. Diagonal $AC$ has cross-sectional area $300\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and elastic modulus $200\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Calculate its axial force, normal stress, and axial change in length.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="C,0,-0.9,48 kN" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,3 m;6.45 4,6.45 0,4 m,-0.55" titolo="Vertical load and the diagonal to be analyzed"}

At joint $C$, symmetry and vertical equilibrium give
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{AC}&=N_{BC},\\[4pt]
-\dfrac85N_{AC}-48\,\mathrm{kN}&=0,\\[4pt]
N_{AC}&=-30\,\mathrm{kN}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The diagonal has length $5\,\mathrm m$. Its normal stress and axial rigidity are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_{AC}&=\dfrac{-30\,000}{300}\,\mathrm{MPa}=-100\,\mathrm{MPa},\\[6pt]
EA&=200\,000\cdot300\,\mathrm N=60\,000\,\mathrm{kN}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Therefore
$$
\Delta L_{AC}=\dfrac{-30\cdot5}{60\,000}\,\mathrm m=-2.5\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

At the top joint, each diagonal in compression supplies an upward force component. The stress is negative, so the longitudinal strain obtained from the elastic constitutive law is also negative. The result measures shortening along the axis of the diagonal. Finding the vertical deflection of joint $C$ would also require compatibility with the other members.

**Check.** The load acts at midspan, so overall equilibrium gives $V_A=24\,\mathrm{kN}$. The vertical component at joint $A$ is $24+\dfrac45(-30)=0$, in $\mathrm{kN}$.

</div>
