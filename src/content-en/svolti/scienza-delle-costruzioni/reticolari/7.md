## Exercise 1

The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, $D=(0,3)$, $E=(4,3)$, and $F=(8,3)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $BE$, $CF$, $DE$, $EF$, $BD$, and $CE$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $C$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. Two forces act at $F$, one of $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and one of $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Calculate the forces in $DE$ and $BD$. Model the supports as bilateral restraints.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,0,3;E,4,3;F,8,3" aste="A-B;B-C;A-D;B-E;C-F;D-E;E-F;B-D;C-E" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="F,1.2,0,16 kN;F,0,-1.2,24 kN" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m;8.56 3,8.56 0,3 m,-0.55" tratteggi="2,-0.6 2,3.6" etichette="2.2,3.6,s" titolo="Concurrent loads at F, at an elevation of 3 m"}

Taking moments about $A$, with distances in $\mathrm m$ and forces in $\mathrm{kN}$, gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
8V_C-8\cdot24-3\cdot16&=0,\\
V_C&=30,\qquad V_A=24-30=-6,\qquad H_A=-16.
\end{aligned}
$$
On the left portion, moment equilibrium about $B$ and vertical force equilibrium give
$$
\begin{aligned}
-4(-6)-3N_{DE}&=0,\\[4pt]
-6-\dfrac35N_{BD}&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $N_{DE}=8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{BD}=-10\,\mathrm{kN}$.

<div class="spiegazione">

Because it acts above the supports, the horizontal force produces a clockwise moment. The reaction at $C$ therefore exceeds the total vertical load, while the pin reaction acts downward. On the left free body, the vertical reaction at $A$ produces a counterclockwise moment. To balance this moment, the top chord force must produce a clockwise moment, so $DE$ is in tension.

**Check.** At $D$ the post is vertical and the joint carries no external load. The horizontal sum is $8+\dfrac45(-10)=0$, in $\mathrm{kN}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, $D=(0,3)$, $E=(4,3)$, and $F=(8,3)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $BE$, $CF$, $DE$, $EF$, $BD$, and $CE$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $C$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A leftward force of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $D$. A downward force of $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $E$. Find $N_{DE}$ and $N_{BD}$, including the force at $D$ on the left free body.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,0,3;E,4,3;F,8,3" aste="A-B;B-C;A-D;B-E;C-F;D-E;E-F;B-D;C-E" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="D,-1.2,0,20 kN;E,0,-1.2,30 kN" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m;8.56 3,8.56 0,3 m,-0.55" tratteggi="3,-0.6 3,3.6" etichette="3.2,3.6,s" titolo="Horizontal force within the isolated part of the truss"}

Using $\mathrm{kN}$ and $\mathrm m$, overall equilibrium gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
8V_C+3\cdot20-4\cdot30&=0,\\
V_C&=\dfrac{15}{2},\qquad V_A=\dfrac{45}{2},\qquad H_A=20.
\end{aligned}
$$
On the left portion, the moment arm of the horizontal force at $D$ about $B$ equals the truss height. Moment equilibrium and vertical force equilibrium give
$$
\begin{aligned}
-4\cdot\dfrac{45}{2}+3\cdot20-3N_{DE}&=0,\\[6pt]
\dfrac{45}{2}-\dfrac35N_{BD}&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Therefore $N_{DE}=-10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{BD}=\dfrac{75}{2}\,\mathrm{kN}$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The force at $D$ acts on the isolated portion, so it enters both the support reaction calculation and the equilibrium equations at the cut. The horizontal forces at $A$ and $D$ act at different elevations. Only the force at $D$ has a nonzero moment about $B$. The diagonal remains the only cut member with a vertical force component.

**Check.** The horizontal force sum at joint $D$ is $-20-10+\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac{75}{2}=0$, in $\mathrm{kN}$.

</div>
