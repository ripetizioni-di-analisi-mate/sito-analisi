## Exercise 1

The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(0,3)$, and $D=(4,3)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $AC$, $BD$, and $CD$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $B$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A rightward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $D$. Determine whether the panel is stable and whether it can equilibrate the specified force.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,0,3;D,4,3" aste="A-B;A-C;B-D;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="D,0.65,0,12 kN" quote="A,B,4 m;4.45 3,4.45 0,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Rectangular panel with four members"}

The four members and the three reaction components give
$$
m+r=4+3=7,\qquad 2j=8.
$$
Consider an infinitesimal horizontal displacement of amplitude $\eta$ at both upper joints. Joints $A$ and $B$ remain stationary. The relative displacement projections are
$$
\delta L_{AB}=\delta L_{AC}=\delta L_{BD}=\delta L_{CD}=0.
$$
The member forces and support reactions therefore do no virtual work during this motion. The applied load, however, does work $12\,\mathrm{kN}\,\eta\ne0$ for $\eta\ne0$. The panel is a mechanism and cannot equilibrate the specified load.

<div class="spiegazione">

Both end posts are vertical, so horizontal motion of their upper ends is perpendicular to their axes. The top chord translates without changing length because its ends have identical displacements. The force at $D$ acts along the free motion and therefore does work.

**Check.** Isolate joints $C$ and $D$ together. The remaining forces are the vertical axial forces in the two posts and the load at $D$. Horizontal equilibrium would require $12\,\mathrm{kN}=0$, confirming that equilibrium is impossible.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(0,3)$, and $D=(4,3)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $AC$, $BD$, $CD$, and $BC$. Joint $A$ is pinned. Joint $B$ has a roller on a vertical surface. A rightward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $D$. Determine whether the triangulated panel is statically determinate and whether it can equilibrate the load.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,0,3;D,4,3" aste="A-B;A-C;B-D;C-D;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" reticolare=1 forze="D,0.65,0,12 kN" quote="A,B,4 m;4.45 3,4.45 0,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Triangulated panel with a horizontal roller reaction"}

Counting the unknowns gives
$$
m+r=5+3=8=2j.
$$
The roller reaction at $B$ acts horizontally. Its line of action passes through $A$, so every support reaction has zero moment about $A$. The force at $D$ produces a moment of
$$
M_A=-3\,\mathrm m\cdot12\,\mathrm{kN}=-36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$
Thus the moment equilibrium equation about $A$ has no solution. The panel is not statically determinate.

<div class="spiegazione">

The diagonal prevents distortion of the panel. The entire panel can nevertheless rotate infinitesimally about the pin. During this rotation $B$ moves vertically, a motion permitted by the roller on the vertical surface. Simply counting reactions would treat this restraint as equivalent to a vertical reaction at $B$. Moment equilibrium distinguishes the two cases.

**Check.** For a counterclockwise virtual rotation $\varphi$, the horizontal displacement at $D$ is $-3\,\mathrm m\,\varphi$. The reactions do no work, while the applied load does work $-36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}\,\varphi$. This work is nonzero when $\varphi\ne0$.

</div>
