## Exercise 1

The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, and $D=(4,3)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $BD$, and $CD$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $C$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A downward force of $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $D$. Find the forces in the vertical member and the two bottom chord members.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,4,3" aste="A-B;B-C;A-D;B-D;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="D,0,-1.2,30 kN" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m;8.56 3,8.56 0,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Roof truss with no load at lower joint B"}

At joint $B$, members $AB$ and $BC$ are horizontal. Resolving the forces vertically gives
$$
N_{BD}=0.
$$
In $\mathrm{kN}$ and $\mathrm m$, the reactions are $H_A=0$ and $V_A=V_C=\dfrac{30}{2}=15$. Both diagonals have length $5\,\mathrm m$, so
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{AD}&=-\dfrac53 V_A=-25,\\[4pt]
N_{CD}&=-\dfrac53 V_C=-25,\\[4pt]
N_{AB}&=-\dfrac45N_{AD}=20,\qquad
N_{BC}=-\dfrac45N_{CD}=20.
\end{aligned}
$$
The vertical is a zero-force member and the bottom chord is in tension.

<div class="spiegazione">

Only the vertical member can supply a vertical force component at joint $B$. Its force can therefore be determined before the support reactions. At the outer lower joints, the diagonals in compression balance the vertical reactions. The horizontal component of each diagonal requires tension in the chord.

**Check.** The horizontal projection at joint $B$ is $-20+20=0$. The vertical projection at $D$ is $-\dfrac35(-25)-\dfrac35(-25)-30=0$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, $D=(0,3)$, $E=(4,3)$, and $F=(8,3)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $BE$, $CF$, $DE$, $EF$, $BD$, and $CE$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $C$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A downward force of $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $E$. Identify the zero-force members at $F$ and $A$. Also calculate the external reactions.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,0,3;E,4,3;F,8,3" aste="A-B;B-C;A-D;B-E;C-F;D-E;E-F;B-D;C-E" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="E,0,-1.2,30 kN" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m;8.56 3,8.56 0,3 m,-0.55" titolo="End panel with no load at F"}

Since the load is vertical, $H_A=0$. Using $\mathrm{kN}$ and $\mathrm m$, overall equilibrium gives
$$
8V_C-4\cdot30=0,\qquad V_C=15,\qquad V_A=15.
$$
At $F$, members $EF$ and $CF$ are perpendicular and no external load acts on the joint. Therefore
$$
-N_{EF}=0,\qquad -N_{CF}=0.
$$
The only members meeting at $A$ are the horizontal member $AB$ and the vertical member $AD$. Therefore
$$
N_{AB}+H_A=0,\qquad N_{AB}=0.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

At joint $F$ each member supplies force along just one axis. With no external force along either axis, both member forces must be zero. A vertical reaction acts at joint $A$, but the horizontal equilibrium equation still has zero external term. Thus $AB$ can also be identified as a zero-force member.

**Check.** Summing moments of all external forces about $C$ gives $-8\cdot15+4\cdot30=0$, in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$. This checks the reaction at $A$ using a different moment center from the one used to calculate it.

</div>
