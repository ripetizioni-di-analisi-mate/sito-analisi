## Exercise 1

The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, $D=(0,3)$, $E=(4,3)$, and $F=(8,3)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $BE$, $CF$, $DE$, $EF$, $BD$, and $CE$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $C$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A downward force of $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $E$. A downward force of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $F$. Isolate the portion to the left of the cut to calculate the forces in $BD$ and $DE$.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,0,3;E,4,3;F,8,3" aste="A-B;B-C;A-D;B-E;C-F;D-E;E-F;B-D;C-E" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="E,0,-1.2,30 kN;F,0,-1.2,15 kN" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m;8.56 3,8.56 0,3 m,-0.55" tratteggi="2,-0.6 2,3.6" etichette="2.2,3.6,s" titolo="Diagonal sloping downward on the left"}

The overall equilibrium equations in $\mathrm{kN}$ and $\mathrm m$ give
$$
8V_C-4\cdot30-8\cdot15=0,\qquad V_C=30,\qquad V_A=15.
$$
The forces in $AB$ and $DE$ are horizontal. The diagonal slopes downward from $D$ to $B$, so vertical equilibrium of the left portion gives
$$
15-\dfrac35N_{BD}=0,\qquad N_{BD}=25\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
Moment equilibrium about $B$ gives
$$
-4\cdot15-3N_{DE}=0,\qquad N_{DE}=-20\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The loads at $E$ and $F$ determine how the reactions are distributed, but both act on the portion that was discarded. On the left portion, the vertical component of $BD$ is the only cut-member force component that can balance the reaction at $A$. Once the diagonal force is known, a moment balance gives the top chord force without first finding the bottom chord force.

**Check.** At $D$, member $AD$ is vertical. The horizontal components of the chord and diagonal forces sum to $-20+\dfrac45(25)=0$, in $\mathrm{kN}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, $D=(0,3)$, $E=(4,3)$, and $F=(8,3)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $BE$, $CF$, $DE$, $EF$, $AE$, and $BF$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $C$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A downward force of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $D$. A downward force of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $E$. Isolate the portion to the right of the cut to calculate the forces in $BF$ and $EF$.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,0,3;E,4,3;F,8,3" aste="A-B;B-C;A-D;B-E;C-F;D-E;E-F;A-E;B-F" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="D,0,-1.2,20 kN;E,0,-1.2,18 kN" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m;8.56 3,8.56 0,3 m,-0.55" tratteggi="6,-0.6 6,3.6" etichette="6.2,3.6,s" titolo="Right-hand section with the diagonal directed from B toward F"}

Overall moment equilibrium about $A$, using $\mathrm{kN}$ and $\mathrm m$, gives
$$
8V_C-4\cdot18=0,\qquad V_C=9.
$$
On the right portion, assume the force in $BF$ acts from $F$ toward $B$. The unit vector is $\left(-\dfrac45,-\dfrac35\right)$. Therefore
$$
9-\dfrac35N_{BF}=0,\qquad N_{BF}=15\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
About $B$, the reaction at $C$ has moment arm $4\,\mathrm m$ and the force in chord $EF$ has moment arm $3\,\mathrm m$. This gives
$$
4\cdot9+3N_{EF}=0,\qquad N_{EF}=-12\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The right portion carries only the vertical roller reaction and the three cut-member forces. Choosing this portion reduces the number of external forces to resolve. Orient the cut-member forces outward from the right portion. The diagonal unit vector points down and to the left, and the top chord unit vector points to the left.

**Check.** At joint $F$, member $CF$ is vertical and no load acts directly on the joint. The horizontal projection is $-(-12)-\dfrac45(15)=0$, in $\mathrm{kN}$.

</div>
