## Exercise 1

The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, $D=(0,3)$, $E=(4,3)$, and $F=(8,3)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $BE$, $CF$, $DE$, $EF$, $BD$, and $CE$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $C$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A downward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $E$. A downward force of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $F$. The dashed cut separates $A,D$ from the rest of the truss. Calculate $N_{AB}$, $N_{BD}$, and $N_{DE}$.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,0,3;E,4,3;F,8,3" aste="A-B;B-C;A-D;B-E;C-F;D-E;E-F;B-D;C-E" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="E,0,-1.2,12 kN;F,0,-1.2,18 kN" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m;8.56 3,8.56 0,3 m,-0.55" tratteggi="2,-0.6 2,3.6" etichette="2.2,3.6,s" titolo="Section with two loads to its right"}

Using units $\mathrm{kN}$ and $\mathrm m$, the support reactions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
H_A&=0,\\
8V_C-4\cdot12-8\cdot18&=0,\\
V_C&=24,\qquad V_A=12+18-24=6.
\end{aligned}
$$
On the left free body, assume the cut-member forces point outward. The unit vector along $BD$ from $D$ toward $B$ has components $\left(\dfrac45,-\dfrac35\right)$. This gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
-4\cdot6-3N_{DE}&=0,\\[4pt]
6-\dfrac35N_{BD}&=0,\\[4pt]
N_{AB}+\dfrac45N_{BD}+N_{DE}&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $N_{DE}=-8\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{BD}=10\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $N_{AB}=0$.

<div class="spiegazione">

Taking moments about $B$ eliminates the forces in $AB$ and $BD$ because both lines of action pass through $B$. The reaction at $A$ produces a clockwise moment. To balance this moment, the top chord pushes joint $D$ to the left and is therefore in compression. The vertical component of the diagonal tension balances $V_A$. The horizontal component of $BD$ balances the top chord force, leaving $AB$ as a zero-force member.

**Check.** Take moments of the same free body about $E$. In $\mathrm{kN\,m}$ the sum is $-4\cdot6+(-4)(-\dfrac35\cdot10)+3\cdot0=-24+24=0$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, $D=(0,3)$, $E=(4,3)$, and $F=(8,3)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $BE$, $CF$, $DE$, $EF$, $BD$, and $CE$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $C$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A downward force of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $D$. A downward force of $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $E$. Calculate the three axial forces in the members crossed by the dashed cut. Check the contribution of the load at joint $D$.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,0,3;E,4,3;F,8,3" aste="A-B;B-C;A-D;B-E;C-F;D-E;E-F;B-D;C-E" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="D,0,-1.2,18 kN;E,0,-1.2,24 kN" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m;8.56 3,8.56 0,3 m,-0.55" tratteggi="2,-0.6 2,3.6" etichette="2.2,3.6,s" titolo="Section with one load on either side"}

Using $\mathrm{kN}$ and $\mathrm m$, overall equilibrium gives
$$
8V_C-4\cdot24=0,\qquad V_C=12,\qquad V_A=18+24-12=30.
$$
The force at $D$ has zero horizontal coordinate and zero moment about $A$. The left free body gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
-4\cdot30+4\cdot18-3N_{DE}&=0,\\[4pt]
30-18-\dfrac35N_{BD}&=0,\\[4pt]
N_{AB}+N_{DE}+\dfrac45N_{BD}&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Therefore $N_{DE}=-16\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{BD}=20\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $N_{AB}=0$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The load at $D$ makes no contribution to the overall moment about $A$, but the moment center used for the cut section is $B$. Since $D$ and $B$ are separated horizontally by $4\,\mathrm m$, the downward force produces a counterclockwise moment on the left free body. The vertical component of the diagonal force balances the difference between the reaction at $A$ and the load at $D$.

**Check.** The sum of the moments about $E$ is $-4\cdot30+4\cdot18+(-4)(-\dfrac35\cdot20)+3\cdot0=-120+72+48=0$, in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

</div>
