## Exercise 1

The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$, and $C=(3,4)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $AC$, and $BC$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $B$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A downward force of $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. Calculate the axial forces in the three members. Take tension as positive.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="C,0,-0.9,40 kN" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,3 m;6.45 4,6.45 0,4 m,-0.55" titolo="Load at the apex of an isosceles triangular truss"}

Use $\mathrm{kN}$ for forces and $\mathrm m$ for distances. Overall equilibrium gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
H_A&=0,\\[4pt]
6V_B-3\cdot40&=0,\\[4pt]
V_B&=20,\qquad V_A=40-20=20.
\end{aligned}
$$
Both diagonals meeting at joint $C$ have length $5\,\mathrm m$. Resolving their forces into components gives
$$
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac35N_{AC}+\dfrac35N_{BC}=0,\\[6pt]
-\dfrac45N_{AC}-\dfrac45N_{BC}-40=0.
\end{cases}
$$
The first equation shows that the two member forces are equal. The second equation and horizontal equilibrium at $A$ give
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{AC}=N_{BC}&=-40\cdot\dfrac58=-25,\\[4pt]
N_{AB}&=-\dfrac35(-25)=15.
\end{aligned}
$$
The bottom chord is in tension and the diagonals are in compression.

<div class="spiegazione">

The vertical components of the two unit vectors pointing away from joint $C$ are both $-\dfrac45$. The load acts downward, so both member forces are negative and their vertical components point upward. At $A$ the diagonal has a leftward force component, so the chord must pull to the right.

**Check.** At joint $B$ the horizontal component gives $-15-\dfrac35(-25)=0$ and the vertical component gives $20+\dfrac45(-25)=0$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(14,0)$, and $C=(5,12)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $AC$, and $BC$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $B$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A downward force of $70\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. Calculate the support reactions and the axial member forces. Take tension as positive.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,14,0;C,5,12" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="C,0,-2.1,70 kN" quote="A,5 0,5 m;5 0,B,9 m;14.98 12,14.98 0,12 m,-0.55" titolo="Triangular truss with the apex shifted to the left"}

Since the load is vertical, $H_A=0$. In $\mathrm{kN}$ and $\mathrm m$, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
14V_B-5\cdot70&=0,\\
V_B&=25,\\
V_A&=70-25=45.
\end{aligned}
$$
The diagonal lengths are $13\,\mathrm m$ and $15\,\mathrm m$. Vertical equilibrium at joints $A$ and $B$ gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
45+\dfrac{12}{13}N_{AC}&=0,\\[4pt]
25+\dfrac{12}{15}N_{BC}&=0,\\[4pt]
N_{AC}&=-\dfrac{195}{4},\qquad N_{BC}=-\dfrac{125}{4}.
\end{aligned}
$$
At $A$, the remaining horizontal equation is
$$
N_{AB}+\dfrac5{13}N_{AC}=0,\qquad N_{AB}=\dfrac{75}{4}.
$$
The diagonals are in compression and the chord is in tension.

<div class="spiegazione">

The load line is closer to $A$, so the vertical reaction there has the greater magnitude. At both supports the chord is horizontal, so each vertical reaction component determines the force in the corresponding diagonal directly. The height-to-length ratios are different, so the compressive forces differ.

**Check.** At joint $C$ the resultant vertical component is $-\dfrac{12}{13}(-\dfrac{195}{4})-\dfrac{12}{15}(-\dfrac{125}{4})-70=45+25-70=0$.

</div>
