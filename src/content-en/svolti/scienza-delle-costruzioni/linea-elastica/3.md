## Exercise 1

A simply supported beam of span $3\,\mathrm m$ and flexural rigidity $EI=9\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a downward point load $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan. Calculate the midspan deflection and the rotation at the left support, taking counterclockwise rotation as positive.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,1.5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,1.5 m;C,B,1.5 m" forze="C,0,-0.78,16 kN"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

$$
0\leq x\leq\frac L2,\qquad
M(x)=\frac{Px}{2},\qquad
v''(x)=-\frac{Px}{2EI}.
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
v'(x)&=-\frac{Px^2}{4EI}+\frac{PL^2}{16EI},\\[6pt]
v(x)&=-\frac{Px^3}{12EI}+\frac{PL^2x}{16EI},\\[6pt]
v\left(\frac L2\right)&=\frac{PL^3}{48EI}=1\,\mathrm{mm},\\[6pt]
\varphi(0)&=-\frac{PL^2}{16EI}=-1.00\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The vertical reactions are equal by symmetry. On the left half, impose zero deflection at $A$ and zero slope at midspan. The downward deflection increases along the left half, so the rotation at the left support is clockwise.

**Check.** Reflecting the solution gives $T(C^-)=8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $T(C^+)=-8\,\mathrm{kN}$. The jump condition from equilibrium gives $-8-8+16=0$, in $\mathrm{kN}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm m$ and flexural rigidity $EI=8\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a downward distributed load $q=6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over its full span. Find the midspan deflection and the rotation at $B$, taking counterclockwise rotation as positive.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m" carichi="A,B,0.55,0.55,6 kN/m"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

$$
M(x)=\frac{qx(L-x)}{2},\qquad
EI\,v''(x)=-\frac{qx(L-x)}{2}.
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
EI\,v'(x)&=q\left(-\frac{Lx^2}{4}+\frac{x^3}{6}+\frac{L^3}{24}\right),\\[6pt]
EI\,v(x)&=q\left(-\frac{Lx^3}{12}+\frac{x^4}{24}+\frac{L^3x}{24}\right),\\[6pt]
v\left(\frac L2\right)&=\frac{5qL^4}{384EI}=2.5\,\mathrm{mm},\\[6pt]
\varphi(L)&=\frac{qL^3}{24EI}=2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The bending moment diagram is parabolic. For the first integration, use the zero slope at midspan. For the second, impose zero displacement at $A$. At $B$ the deflected centerline rises toward the support, so the rotation is counterclockwise.

**Check.** The independent condition of zero displacement at $B$ gives $EI v(L)=qL^4(-\dfrac1{12}+\dfrac1{24}+\dfrac1{24})=0$.

</div>
