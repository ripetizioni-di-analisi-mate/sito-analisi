## Exercise 1

A cantilever of span $4\,\mathrm m$ and flexural rigidity $EI=8\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a downward tip force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a counterclockwise tip couple of $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Find the tip deflection and slope.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;Z,4.850,0,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,4 m" forze="B,0,-1.15,8 kN" coppie="B,1,16 kN m"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

$$
EI\,v''(x)=P(L-x)-\mathcal M.
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
v'(L)&=\frac{PL^2}{2EI}-\frac{\mathcal M L}{EI}=0,\\[6pt]
v(L)&=\frac{PL^3}{3EI}-\frac{\mathcal M L^2}{2EI}
=\frac{16}{3}\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Integrate both contributions using the same fixed-end conditions. The force bends the cantilever downward, while the counterclockwise couple bends it in the opposite direction. At the tip, the slopes cancel and the deflections cancel only partially.

**Check.** The internal tip moment obtained from the deflection is $-EI v^{\prime\prime}(L)=\mathcal M$. The residual relative to the applied couple is $-(-16)-16=0$, in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm m$ carries a downward load $q=3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and a central force $P=12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in the same direction. Let $EI=8\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Support $B$ settles by $4\,\mathrm{mm}$. Calculate the midpoint displacement relative to the original configuration.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" carichi="A,B,0.53,0.53,3 kN/m" forze="C,0,-1.09,12 kN" frecce="4.3,0.1,4.3,-0.7,4 mm"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_q(C)&=\frac{5qL^4}{384EI}=1.25\,\mathrm{mm},\\[6pt]
v_P(C)&=\frac{PL^3}{48EI}=2\,\mathrm{mm},\\[6pt]
v_{\delta}(C)&=\frac{\delta_B}{2}=2\,\mathrm{mm},\\[6pt]
v(C)&=1.25\,\mathrm{mm}+2\,\mathrm{mm}+2\,\mathrm{mm}=5.25\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Calculate the load contributions with stationary supports. Settlement alone tilts the beam without bending it. Its displacement varies linearly and at midspan is half the settlement. The total displacement is downward.

**Check.** The midspan bending moment obtained by adding the curvatures is $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}+\dfrac{PL}{4}=18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Equilibrium of the left half gives $V_A=12\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the residual is $12\cdot2-(3\cdot2)\cdot1-18=0$, in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

</div>
