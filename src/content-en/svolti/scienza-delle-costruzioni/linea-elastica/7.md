## Exercise 1

A simply supported beam of span $5\,\mathrm m$ has flexural rigidity $EI=10\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A downward force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $2\,\mathrm m$ from $A$. Find the curvature expressed as $v^{\prime\prime}$ at the load point and the jump $[v^{\prime\prime\prime}]$ at the same section.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,3 m" forze="C,0,-0.89,10 kN"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

$$
V_A=6\,\mathrm{kN},\quad V_B=4\,\mathrm{kN},\quad
M(C)=V_A\cdot2\,\mathrm m=12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
v''(C^-)&=v''(C^+)=-\frac{12}{10\,000}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}
=-1.2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}},\\[6pt]
[v^{\prime\prime}]_C&=v''(C^+)-v''(C^-)=0\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The applied force changes the shear force while leaving the moment continuous. Because both segments have the same flexural rigidity, the curvature has a unique value at the load point. The third derivative jumps because the shear force changes.

**Check.** Differentiating the moments in the two segments gives $v^{\prime\prime\prime}(C^-)=-\dfrac6{10\,000}$ and $v^{\prime\prime\prime}(C^+)=\dfrac4{10\,000}$ in $\mathrm{m^{-2}}$. The jump in $v^{\prime\prime\prime}$ must be $\dfrac{P}{EI}$. The residual is $\dfrac{4+6-10}{10\,000}=0$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The beam consists of two segments $AC$ and $CB$, each of length $3\,\mathrm m$ and with $EI=9\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. End $A$ is fixed, $C$ is an internal hinge, and $B$ is a simple support. Segment $CB$ carries a downward load $P=12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan. Find the displacement of $C$ and the slopes of the two segments at the hinge.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;D,4.5,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" forze="D,0,-0.9,12 kN" quote="A,C,3 m;C,D,1.5 m;D,B,1.5 m" titolo="Internal hinge between the cantilever and the simply supported beam"}

$$
V_B=V_{C,\mathrm{right}}=6\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
$$
v(C)=\frac{6\cdot3^3}{3\cdot9\,000}\,\mathrm m=6\,\mathrm{mm},\qquad
v'(C^-)=\frac{6\cdot3^2}{2\cdot9\,000}=0.003.
$$
$$
v'(C^+)=\frac{12\cdot3^2}{16\cdot9\,000}-\frac{0.006}{3}
=-0.00125.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The hinge allows $CB$ to be analyzed as a simply supported beam between $C$ and $B$. The right segment transmits a downward force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left segment, which bends as a cantilever. On the right segment, displacement of the hinge adds an upward slope because point $B$ remains stationary. The hinge permits the difference between the slopes.

**Check.** For the complete structure, the reaction moment calculated from the left segment is $6\cdot3=18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The residual in the moment balance about $A$ is $18+6\cdot6-12\cdot4.5=0$.

</div>
