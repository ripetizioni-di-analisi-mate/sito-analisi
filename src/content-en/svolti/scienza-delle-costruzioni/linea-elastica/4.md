## Exercise 1

A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm m$ and flexural rigidity $EI=18\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a downward load $P=24\,\mathrm{kN}$ at a distance $2\,\mathrm m$ from $A$. Calculate the deflection under the load.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,4 m" forze="C,0,-0.92,24 kN"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

$$
V_A=16\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_B=8\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad
M(x)=
\begin{cases}
V_A x,&x\leq a,\\
V_B(L-x),&x\geq a.
\end{cases}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
EI\,v_1(x)&=-\frac{V_A x^3}{6}+c_1 x,\\[6pt]
EI\,v_2(x)&=-\frac{V_B (L-x)^3}{6}+c_2(L-x).
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
2c_1-4c_2=-64\,\mathrm{kN\,m^3},\\
c_1+c_2=96\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}.
\end{cases}
\qquad
c_1=\frac{160}{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2},\quad
c_2=\frac{128}{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}.
$$
$$
v(a)=\frac{256}{3\cdot18\,000}\,\mathrm m
=\frac{128}{27}\,\mathrm{mm}\simeq4.74\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The expressions for $v_1$ and $v_2$ already satisfy the zero-displacement conditions at the supports. Determine the remaining coefficients by requiring continuity of both displacement and slope at the load. The bending moment is continuous, but the shear force changes from positive to negative.

**Check.** The third derivatives of the two solutions give the local force balance $[T]_a+P=-8-16+24=0$, in $\mathrm{kN}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A beam of span $6\,\mathrm m$ and flexural rigidity $EI=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ has bilateral supports. A counterclockwise couple $\mathcal M=18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at a distance $2\,\mathrm m$ from $A$. Calculate the displacement at the section where the couple acts.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,4 m" coppie="C,1,18 kN m"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

$$
V_A=\frac{\mathcal M}{L}=3\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad
V_B=-3\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
$$
M(x)=
\begin{cases}
3x,&x\leq2,\\
3x-18,&x\geq2
\end{cases}
\quad\text{in }\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
EI\,v_1(x)&=-\frac{x^3}{2}+c_1x,\\[6pt]
EI\,v_2(x)&=-\frac{x^3}{2}+c_1x+9(x-2)^2.
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
-108+6c_1+144=0,\qquad
c_1=-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2},\qquad
v(2)=\frac{-4-12}{12\,000}\,\mathrm m=-\frac43\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The equations give the numerical values in consistent units. The couple reduces the moment by $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ to the right of the section, thereby increasing the curvature by $\dfrac{18}{EI}$. The added quadratic term produces this increase and has zero value and slope at $x=2$. The displacement is upward. The support at $B$ must be bilateral because its vertical reaction acts downward.

**Check.** The one-sided limits of $EI\,v^{\prime\prime}$ at $2$ are $-6$ and $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The jump condition from equilibrium gives $12-(-6)-18=0$, in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

</div>
