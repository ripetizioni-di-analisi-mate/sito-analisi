## Exercise 1

A cantilever of span $5\,\mathrm m$ carries a downward tip load $P=10\,\mathrm{kN}$. Up to $x=2\,\mathrm m$ the rigidity is $EI_1=10\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Beyond that point it is $EI_2=5\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Calculate the tip deflection.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,2,0;Z,5.850,0,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,3 m" forze="B,0,-0.9,10 kN" etichette="0.85,0.28,EI_1;3.25,0.28,EI_2" titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
v(L)&=\int_0^2\frac{10(5-s)^2}{10\,000}\,\mathrm ds
+\int_2^5\frac{10(5-s)^2}{5\,000}\,\mathrm ds\\[6pt]
&=\frac{10}{10\,000}\cdot\frac{125-27}{3}\,\mathrm m
+\frac{10}{5\,000}\cdot\frac{27}{3}\,\mathrm m\\[6pt]
&=\frac{152}{3}\,\mathrm{mm}\simeq50.67\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Equilibrium alone determines the bending moment. The curvature in each segment depends on its flexural rigidity. The factor $5-s$ converts each increment of slope into its contribution to tip displacement.

**Check.** Direct integration gives $v(2)=\dfrac{13}{1500}\,\mathrm m$ and $v^\prime(2)=0.008$ at the junction. The solution for the second segment adds $3v^\prime(2)+\dfrac{10\cdot3^3}{3\cdot5\,000}\,\mathrm m$. The residual is $\dfrac{13}{1500}+0.024+0.018-\dfrac{152}{3000}=0$ in meters.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A cantilever of span $4\,\mathrm m$ has $EI(x)=16\,000(1+\dfrac{x}{L})\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A downward load $P=8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a counterclockwise couple of $64\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ act at the tip. Find the deflected shape and the tip deflection.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;Z,4.850,0,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,4 m" forze="B,0,-1.15,8 kN" coppie="B,1,64 kN m" etichette="1.6,0.31,increasing EI" titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

$$
M(x)=64\,\mathrm{kN\,m}-P(L-x)
=32\left(1+\frac{x}{L}\right)\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$
$$
v''(x)=-\frac{32}{16\,000}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}
=-0.002\,\mathrm{m^{-1}},
\qquad
v(x)=-(0.001\,\mathrm{m^{-1}})x^2.
$$
$$
v(L)=-16\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The couple dominates throughout the span, so the bending moment is positive. The moment and the rigidity increase by the same factor, so the curvature is constant. The fixed-end conditions make both integration constants zero. The negative sign indicates that the tip moves upward.

**Check.** The quantity $EI v^{\prime\prime}$ calculated from the deflection is linear. Its derivative is $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$. The shear force obtained at the end section satisfies $-(-8)-8=0$, in $\mathrm{kN}$.

</div>
