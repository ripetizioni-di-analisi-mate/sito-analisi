## Exercise 1

A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm m$ has $EI=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A downward force of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $2\,\mathrm m$ from $A$. Find the location and magnitude of the maximum deflection.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,4 m" forze="C,0,-0.92,18 kN"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
EI\,v_1(x)&=-2x^3+40x,\qquad 0\le x\le 2,\\[6pt]
EI\,v_2(x)&=-(6-x)^3+32(6-x),\qquad 2\le x\le 6.
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
EI\,v_1'(2)&=40-6\cdot2^2=16,\\[6pt]
EI\,v_2'(x)&=3(6-x)^2-32,\\[6pt]
x_{\max}&=\left(6-\frac{4\sqrt6}{3}\right)\mathrm m\simeq2.73\,\mathrm m,\\[6pt]
v(x_{\max})&=\frac{64\sqrt6}{27}\,\mathrm{mm}\simeq5.81\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The expressions use consistent units, with $x$ evaluated numerically in meters. The reactions are $12$ and $6\,\mathrm{kN}$. Integrating the two linear moment expressions and imposing zero displacement at the supports leaves two undetermined coefficients of the linear terms. Displacement and slope continuity give these coefficients as $40$ and $32$. The slope is still positive at the load point. The bending moment is positive throughout the interior, so the slope decreases. Its zero in the second segment locates the maximum deflection.

**Check.** The maximum moment calculated from the two second derivatives is $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Summing moments on the right segment about $C$ gives $6\cdot4-24=0$, in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A cantilever of span $4\,\mathrm m$ and flexural rigidity $EI=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a downward tip force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a counterclockwise tip couple of $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Find the point of maximum downward deflection.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;Z,4.850,0,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,4 m" forze="B,0,-1.1,12 kN" coppie="B,1,30 kN m"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

$$
EI\,v''(x)=12(4-x)-30=18-12x.
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
EI\,v'(x)&=18x-6x^2,\\[6pt]
EI\,v(x)&=9x^2-2x^3,\\[6pt]
x_{\max}&=3\,\mathrm m,\quad v(3\,\mathrm m)=2.25\,\mathrm{mm},\\[6pt]
v(4\,\mathrm m)&=\frac43\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the polynomial expressions, $x$ is the numerical value in meters. The force and the couple produce curvatures of opposite signs. Integrating with zero slope and deflection at the fixed end gives a positive slope between zero and three meters and a negative slope between three and four meters. The deflection increases up to $x=3\,\mathrm m$ and decreases beyond it.

**Check.** Moment equilibrium of the entire cantilever about the fixed end requires a reaction moment of $12\cdot4-30=18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The fixed-end curvature gives a reaction moment of $EI\,v^{\prime\prime}(0)=18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, so the residual is $18-48+30=0$.

</div>
