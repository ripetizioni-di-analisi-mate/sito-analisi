## Exercise 1

A cantilever of span $2.5\,\mathrm m$ and flexural rigidity $EI=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a uniformly distributed downward load $q=8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Calculate the deflection and slope at the free end.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2.5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,2.5 m" carichi="A,B,0.45,0.45,8 kN/m"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

$$
EI\,v''(x)=\frac{q(L-x)^2}{2}.
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
v'(x)&=\frac{q}{2EI}\left(L^2x-Lx^2+\frac{x^3}{3}\right),\\[6pt]
v(x)&=\frac{q}{2EI}\left(\frac{L^2x^2}{2}-\frac{Lx^3}{3}+\frac{x^4}{12}\right),\\[6pt]
v(L)&=\frac{qL^4}{8EI}\simeq3.26\,\mathrm{mm},\\[6pt]
v'(L)&=\frac{qL^3}{6EI}\simeq1.74\cdot10^{-3}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The resultant of the load on the end segment is $q(L-x)$, and its moment arm is half the segment length. The moment is negative because the top fibers are in tension. The kinematic conditions at the fixed end set both integration constants to zero.

**Check.** The bending moment at the fixed end obtained from the deflection is $-EI v^{\prime\prime}(0)=-25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The positive reaction moment has the same magnitude and the opposite sign. Overall moment equilibrium gives $25-(8\cdot2.5)\cdot1.25=0$, in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A cantilever of span $3\,\mathrm m$ with $EI=9\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ is loaded over its full length. The load intensity increases linearly from zero at $A$ to $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. Calculate the tip deflection and slope.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,3 m" carichi="A,B,0,0.76,3 kN/m"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

$$
q(x)=kx,\quad k=1\,\mathrm{kN/m^2},\qquad EI\,v^{(4)}(x)=kx.
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
EI\,v^{\prime\prime\prime}(x)&=k\left(\frac{x^2}{2}-\frac{L^2}{2}\right),\\[6pt]
EI\,v''(x)&=k\left(\frac{x^3}{6}-\frac{L^2x}{2}+\frac{L^3}{3}\right),\\[6pt]
EI\,v'(x)&=k\left(\frac{x^4}{24}-\frac{L^2x^2}{4}+\frac{L^3x}{3}\right),\\[6pt]
EI\,v(x)&=k\left(\frac{x^5}{120}-\frac{L^2x^3}{12}+\frac{L^3x^2}{6}\right).
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
v(L)=\frac{11kL^5}{120EI}=2.475\,\mathrm{mm},\qquad
v'(L)=\frac{kL^4}{8EI}=1.125\cdot10^{-3}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Use the fourth-order beam equation. Zero shear and moment at the tip determine the first two constants. Zero displacement and slope at the fixed end determine the remaining two. Since the load intensity is linear, the deflection is a fifth-degree polynomial.

**Check.** The moment of the triangular load resultant about $A$ is $-\dfrac{3\cdot3}{2}\cdot2=-9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The fixed-end moment calculated from $EI\,v^{\prime\prime}(0)$ is $\dfrac{kL^3}{3}=9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The residual for overall equilibrium is $9-9=0$.

</div>
