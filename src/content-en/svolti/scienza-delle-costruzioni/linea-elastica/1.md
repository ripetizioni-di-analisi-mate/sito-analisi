## Exercise 1

A cantilever of span $2\,\mathrm m$ and flexural rigidity $EI=4\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a downward tip load $P=6\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the tip deflection and rotation, taking counterclockwise rotation as positive.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;Z,2.850,0,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,2 m" forze="B,0,-0.66,6 kN"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

$$
M(x)=-P(L-x),\qquad
EI\,v''(x)=P(L-x).
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
v'(x)&=\frac{P}{EI}\left(Lx-\frac{x^2}{2}\right),\\[6pt]
v(x)&=\frac{P}{EI}\left(\frac{Lx^2}{2}-\frac{x^3}{6}\right),\\[6pt]
v(L)&=\frac{6\cdot2^3}{3\cdot4\,000}\,\mathrm m=4\,\mathrm{mm},\\[6pt]
\varphi(L)&=-\frac{6\cdot2^2}{2\cdot4\,000}\,\mathrm{rad}=-3\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

On the segment to the right of the cut, the tip force produces a bending moment that puts the top fibers in tension. Integrate using $v(0)=v^\prime(0)=0$. Both integration constants are then zero. The slope of $v$ is positive, so the section rotates clockwise.

**Check.** Using the calculated deflection, the shear force immediately inside the free end is $-EI\,v^{\prime\prime\prime}(L^-)=P$. The residual relative to the applied force is $-4\,000(-\dfrac6{4\,000})-6=0$, in $\mathrm{kN}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A cantilever of span $5\,\mathrm m$ with $EI=10\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a downward force $P=15\,\mathrm{kN}$ at a distance $3\,\mathrm m$ from the fixed end. Find the deflection at the load point and at the free end.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,C,3 m;C,B,2 m" forze="C,0,-0.92,15 kN"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

$$
M(x)=
\begin{cases}
-P(a-x),&0\leq x\leq a,\\
0,&a<x\leq L,
\end{cases}
\qquad a=3\,\mathrm m.
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
v(a)&=\frac{Pa^3}{3EI}=13.5\,\mathrm{mm},\\[6pt]
v'(a)&=\frac{Pa^2}{2EI}=6.75\cdot10^{-3},\\[6pt]
v(L)&=v(a)+(L-a)v'(a)=27\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Integrate over the loaded segment as for a cantilever of length $a$. Beyond the applied force the bending moment is zero, so the deflected centerline is straight. Continuity determines both the initial elevation and the slope of this straight segment.

**Check.** On $[a,L]$, integrating the curvature gives $v(L)-v(a)-(L-a)v^\prime(a)=0$. The residual is $0.027-0.0135-2\cdot0.00675=0$ in meters.

</div>
