## Exercise 1

A right triangle has vertices $(z,y)=(0,0),(60,0),(0,40)$ in millimeters. Calculate its second moment about the horizontal line through its centroid.
::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 60,0 0,40" frecce="0,0,74,0,z;0,0,0,53,y" quote="0 0,60 0,60 mm,8;0 0,0 40,40 mm,-9" titolo="Right triangle with its base on the z axis"}

$$
b(y)=60\left(1-\frac{y}{40}\right).
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=\frac{60\cdot40}{2}=1\,200\,\mathrm{mm^2},\\[6pt]
y_G&=\frac{\int_0^{40}y\,b(y)\,dy}{A}
=\frac{40}{3}\,\mathrm{mm},\\[6pt]
I_z&=\int_0^{40}y^2 b(y)\,dy
=320\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4},\\[6pt]
I_{z,G}&=I_z-Ay_G^2=\frac{320\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The width $b(y)$ decreases linearly from $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ to zero. This same width appears in the integrals for the first and second moments of area. The first integral gives the centroid height. The second measures heights from the lower edge. Subtract the parallel-axis term to obtain the centroidal second moment.

**Check.** For vertical strips, the height of the sloping side is $40\left(1-\dfrac{z}{60}\right)$. The residual relative to the calculated second moment is

$$
\int_0^{60}\int_0^{40(1-\frac{z}{60})}
\left(y-\frac{40}{3}\right)^2dy\,dz
-\frac{320\,000}{3}=0,
$$

in $\mathrm{mm^4}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An angle section has a horizontal leg of width $70\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$. On the left the vertical leg reaches height $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and has width $10\,\mathrm{mm}$. The base is at height zero. Calculate $I_{z,G}$.
::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 70,0 70,10 10,10 10,60 0,60" frecce="0,0,86,0,z;0,0,0,74,y" quote="0 0,70 0,70 mm,8;0 0,0 60,60 mm,-9;0 60,10 60,10 mm,-22;70 0,70 10,10 mm,9" titolo="Angle section with a longer horizontal leg"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=700+500=1\,200\,\mathrm{mm^2},\\[6pt]
y_G&=\frac{700\cdot5+500\cdot35}{1\,200}\,\mathrm{mm}
=17.5\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_{z,G}&=\frac{70\cdot10^3}{12}+700(5-17.5)^2\\[6pt]
&\quad+\frac{10\cdot50^3}{12}+500(35-17.5)^2\\[6pt]
&=372\,500\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The horizontal leg includes the full thickness at the base. The second rectangle excludes the overlapping square and extends from height $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ to $60\,\mathrm{mm}$, so it is centered at height $35\,\mathrm{mm}$. After finding the mean height of the entire area, transfer both rectangles' second moments to the centroidal axis.

**Check.** The angle can also be formed by subtracting a rectangle measuring $60\times50\,\mathrm{mm}$ above the base from the outer rectangle measuring $70\times60\,\mathrm{mm}$. The difference between this calculation and the result obtained is

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\frac{70\cdot60^3-60\cdot50^3}{12}\\[6pt]
&\quad+4\,200(30-17.5)^2
-3\,000(35-17.5)^2-372\,500=0,
\end{aligned}
$$

in $\mathrm{mm^4}$.

</div>
