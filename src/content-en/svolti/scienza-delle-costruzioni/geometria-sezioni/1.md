## Exercise 1

A rectangular hollow section has outer dimensions $80\times120\,\mathrm{mm}$ and a centered opening of dimensions $60\times100\,\mathrm{mm}$. The second dimension is vertical. Find the smaller principal second moment of area.
::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-40,-60 40,-60 40,60 -40,60" fori="-30,-50 30,-50 30,50 -30,50" frecce="0,0,55,0,z;0,0,0,75,y" quote="-40 -60,40 -60,80 mm,8;40 -60,40 60,120 mm,26;-30 -50,30 -50,60 mm,-7;-30 -50,-30 50,100 mm,10" titolo="Rectangular hollow section with uniform wall thickness"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_z&=\frac{80\cdot120^3-60\cdot100^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}\\[6pt]
&=6.52\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4},\\[6pt]
I_y&=\frac{120\cdot80^3-100\cdot60^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}\\[6pt]
&=3.32\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By double symmetry the center of the opening coincides with the centroid of the section. The axes through that center parallel to the sides are therefore principal axes. To find $I_z$, cube the vertical dimension. To find $I_y$, cube the horizontal dimension. Subtract the contribution of the opening for each axis and compare the two results. The smaller principal second moment is $I_y$.

**Check.** Calculate $I_y$ by adding the contributions of the two vertical walls of width $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $120\,\mathrm{mm}$ and the two remaining horizontal strips of width $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$. The centroid of each vertical wall is a distance $35\,\mathrm{mm}$ from $y$. The sum of these contributions minus the result found above is

$$
2\left(\frac{120\cdot10^3}{12}+1\,200\cdot35^2\right)
+2\,\frac{10\cdot60^3}{12}
-3\,320\,000=0,
$$

in $\mathrm{mm^4}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The T-section shown has a web $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide and $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ high. A flange $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide and $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ thick is centered above it. Find the centroid height, the second moment about the horizontal centroidal axis, and the section moduli at the two extreme fibers.
::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-10,0 10,0 10,80 50,80 50,100 -50,100 -50,80 -10,80" frecce="0,0,65,0,z;0,0,0,114,y" quote="-50 100,50 100,100 mm,-23;-10 0,10 0,20 mm,9;10 0,10 80,80 mm,10;50 80,50 100,20 mm,9" titolo="Vertical web and top flange of the T-section"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=20\cdot80+100\cdot20=3\,600\,\mathrm{mm^2},\\[6pt]
y_G&=\frac{1\,600\cdot40+2\,000\cdot90}{3\,600}\,\mathrm{mm}\\[6pt]
&=\frac{610}{9}\,\mathrm{mm}\simeq67.78\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_z&=\frac{20\cdot80^3}{12}
 +1\,600\left(40-\frac{610}{9}\right)^2\\[6pt]
 &\quad+\frac{100\cdot20^3}{12}
 +2\,000\left(90-\frac{610}{9}\right)^2\\[6pt]
 &=\frac{28\,280\,000}{9}\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
W_{\mathrm{upper}}&=\frac{I_z}{100\,\mathrm{mm}-y_G}
=\frac{2\,828\,000}{29}\,\mathrm{mm^3}
\simeq97\,517\,\mathrm{mm^3},\\[6pt]
W_{\mathrm{lower}}&=\frac{I_z}{y_G}
=\frac{2\,828\,000}{61}\,\mathrm{mm^3}
\simeq46\,361\,\mathrm{mm^3}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Measure heights from the bottom of the web. The web centroid is at half its height. The flange centroid is $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ above the flange–web junction. The area-weighted average of the two heights therefore uses $40$ and $90\,\mathrm{mm}$. Transfer the second moments of the two parts to the common horizontal axis using the **parallel-axis theorem**. By symmetry this is a principal axis. The bottom fiber is farther from $G$ and therefore has the smaller section modulus.

**Check.** Integrating horizontal strips about the axis at the base gives the second moment about that axis. Subtracting the transfer term for the entire section and the previously obtained $I_z$ gives a residual of

$$
\begin{aligned}
&20\int_0^{80}y^2\,dy+100\int_{80}^{100}y^2\,dy\\[6pt]
&\qquad-3\,600\left(\frac{610}{9}\right)^2
-\frac{28\,280\,000}{9}=0,
\end{aligned}
$$

in $\mathrm{mm^4}$.

</div>
