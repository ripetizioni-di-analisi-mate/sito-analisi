## Exercise 1

A U-section has overall width $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $60\,\mathrm{mm}$. Its centered notch, open at the top, is $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide and $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ deep. Calculate the centroid height above the lower edge.
::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-40,0 40,0 40,60 30,60 30,10 -30,10 -30,60 -40,60" frecce="0,0,57,0,z;0,0,0,74,y" quote="-40 0,40 0,80 mm,9;40 0,40 60,60 mm,9;-30 60,30 60,60 mm,-22;30 10,30 60,50 mm,-11" titolo="U-section with 10 mm base and side walls"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=80\cdot60-60\cdot50
=1\,800\,\mathrm{mm^2},\\[6pt]
S_z&=(80\cdot60)\cdot30-(60\cdot50)\cdot35\\[6pt]
&=39\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3},\\[6pt]
y_G&=\frac{S_z}{A}=\frac{65}{3}\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The notch extends from height $10$ to height $60\,\mathrm{mm}$. Its centroid is at $35\,\mathrm{mm}$, and its contribution must be subtracted. The outer rectangle is centered at $30\,\mathrm{mm}$. Divide the net first moment by the remaining area. Symmetry places the centroid on the vertical centerline.

**Check.** Divide the U-section into its base and the two walls above the base. About the height just found, the sum of the first moments of these three parts is

$$
800\left(5-\frac{65}{3}\right)
+2\cdot500\left(35-\frac{65}{3}\right)=0,
$$

in $\mathrm{mm^3}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A rectangular plate measuring $120\times80\,\mathrm{mm}$ has two rectangular openings with sides parallel to the axes. The first measures $20\times20\,\mathrm{mm}$ and is centered at $(z,y)=(30,30)\,\mathrm{mm}$. The second measures $20\times30\,\mathrm{mm}$ and is centered at $(90,50)\,\mathrm{mm}$. The origin is at the lower left corner. Find both centroid coordinates.
::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 120,0 120,80 0,80" fori="20,20 40,20 40,40 20,40;80,35 100,35 100,65 80,65" frecce="0,0,137,0,z;0,0,0,95,y" punti="30,30,C_1,centro;90,50,C_2,centro" quote="0 80,120 80,120 mm,-8;120 0,120 80,80 mm,9" titolo="Plate with two openings of different areas"}

$$
A=9\,600-400-600=8\,600\,\mathrm{mm^2}.
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
z_G&=\frac{9\,600\cdot60-400\cdot30-600\cdot90}{8\,600}\,\mathrm{mm}\\[6pt]
&=\frac{2\,550}{43}\,\mathrm{mm}\simeq59.30\,\mathrm{mm},\\[6pt]
y_G&=\frac{9\,600\cdot40-400\cdot30-600\cdot50}{8\,600}\,\mathrm{mm}\\[6pt]
&=\frac{1\,710}{43}\,\mathrm{mm}\simeq39.77\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The outer rectangle contributes its area at its center $(60,40)\,\mathrm{mm}$. Subtract each opening at its own center. The second opening is larger and lies above and to the right of the outer rectangle's center. The centroid of the remaining area therefore moves to the left and downward relative to that center.

**Check.** Integrate the horizontal widths of the plate minus the two openings, using their actual height intervals. The first moment about the horizontal axis through $G$ gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
&120\int_0^{80}\left(y-\frac{1\,710}{43}\right)dy\\[6pt]
&\quad-20\int_{20}^{40}\left(y-\frac{1\,710}{43}\right)dy\\[6pt]
&\quad-20\int_{35}^{65}\left(y-\frac{1\,710}{43}\right)dy=0,
\end{aligned}
$$

in $\mathrm{mm^3}$.

</div>
