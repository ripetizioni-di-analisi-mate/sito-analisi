## Exercise 1

An annulus has outer radius $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ and inner radius $30\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the polar moment of area and the second moment about a diameter.
::struttura{riferimento=0 cerchi="0,0,50;0,0,30,foro" frecce="0,0,67,0,z;0,0,0,67,y;0,0,40,30,R;0,0,-18,-24,r" titolo="Annulus with concentric inner and outer radii"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_p&=\frac{\pi}{2}(50^4-30^4)\,\mathrm{mm^4}\\[6pt]
&=2\,720\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4},\\[6pt]
I_y&=I_z=\frac{I_p}{2}
=1\,360\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Since the hole is concentric, the center remains the centroid. Subtract the polar moment of the inner disk from that of the outer disk. Since every diameter has the same second moment, $I_y$ and $I_z$ are equal. Each therefore equals half the polar moment.

**Check.** In polar coordinates write $y=\rho\sin\alpha$. For the $z$ axis, direct integration of $y^2$ gives a residual of

$$
\int_{30}^{50}\rho^3\,d\rho
\int_0^{2\pi}\sin^2\alpha\,d\alpha
-1\,360\,000\pi=0,
$$

in $\mathrm{mm^4}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A rectangle of width $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ is centered at the origin. A square hole of side $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ is centered at $(z,y)=(0,20)\,\mathrm{mm}$. Calculate the second moment of the remaining material about its horizontal centroidal axis.
::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-50,-40 50,-40 50,40 -50,40" fori="-10,10 10,10 10,30 -10,30" frecce="0,0,65,0,z;0,0,0,55,y" punti="0,20,C,centro" quote="-50 -40,50 -40,100 mm,8;50 -40,50 40,80 mm,27;10 10,10 30,20 mm,9" titolo="Hollow section with a hole above the horizontal centerline"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=8\,000-400=7\,600\,\mathrm{mm^2},\\[6pt]
y_G&=\frac{-400\cdot20}{7\,600}\,\mathrm{mm}
=-\frac{20}{19}\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_z&=\frac{100\cdot80^3-20\cdot20^3}{12}
-400\cdot20^2\\[6pt]
&=\frac{12\,280\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4},\\[6pt]
I_{z,G}&=I_z-Ay_G^2\\[6pt]
&=\frac{232\,840\,000}{57}\,\mathrm{mm^4}
\simeq4.085\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Removing area above the centerline gives the centroid of the remaining area a negative height. To find the second moment about the original centerline, subtract both the hole's centroidal second moment and its parallel-axis contribution from the value for the solid rectangle. Finally transfer the net second moment to the actual centroidal axis, where it is smaller.

**Check.** Integrate the squared distance from the axis through $G$ over all horizontal material strips, subtracting the portion occupied by the hole. The residual is

$$
\begin{aligned}
&100\int_{-40}^{40}\left(y+\frac{20}{19}\right)^2dy\\[6pt]
&\quad-20\int_{10}^{30}\left(y+\frac{20}{19}\right)^2dy
-\frac{232\,840\,000}{57}=0,
\end{aligned}
$$

in $\mathrm{mm^4}$.

</div>
