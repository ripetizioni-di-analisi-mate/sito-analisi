## Exercise 1

A rectangular cross section of width $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ extends from $y=-15\,\mathrm{mm}$ to $y=45\,\mathrm{mm}$. Calculate the first moment about $z$.
::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-25,-15 25,-15 25,45 -25,45" frecce="0,0,42,0,z;0,0,0,60,y" quote="-25 45,25 45,50 mm,-23;25 -15,25 0,15 mm,8;25 0,25 45,45 mm,8" titolo="The reference axis cuts the rectangle 15 mm above its lower edge"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=50\cdot60=3\,000\,\mathrm{mm^2},\\[6pt]
y_G&=\frac{-15+45}{2}\,\mathrm{mm}=15\,\mathrm{mm},\\[6pt]
S_z&=Ay_G=45\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The centroid lies halfway between the heights of the horizontal edges. Because the mean height is positive, the area above the axis contributes more than the area below it. The area times the mean height gives the first moment.

**Check.** Integrate the contributions below and above the axis separately. The difference from the calculated value is

$$
50\int_{-15}^0y\,dy+50\int_0^{45}y\,dy-45\,000=0,
$$

in $\mathrm{mm^3}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A stepped section has width $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ between heights $0$ and $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ and width $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ between heights $20$ and $50\,\mathrm{mm}$. Both parts are centered on the same vertical axis. Find $S_z$ for only the portion with $y\geq10\,\mathrm{mm}$.
::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-30,0 30,0 30,20 10,20 10,50 -10,50 -10,20 -30,20" tratteggi="-33,10 33,10" frecce="0,0,46,0,z;0,0,0,64,y" quote="-30 0,30 0,60 mm,8;-10 50,10 50,20 mm,-23;30 0,30 10,10 mm,9;30 0,30 20,20 mm,24;10 20,10 50,30 mm,8" titolo="The dashed line marks the height that bounds the portion considered"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{z,1}&=60\cdot(20-10)\cdot\frac{10+20}{2}
=9\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3},\\[6pt]
S_{z,2}&=20\cdot(50-20)\cdot\frac{20+50}{2}
=21\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3},\\[6pt]
S_z&=30\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The requested portion includes a strip of the base and the entire upper rectangle. The strip starts at height $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ and is centered at $15\,\mathrm{mm}$. The upper rectangle is centered at height $35\,\mathrm{mm}$. Both heights are positive, so the contributions add.

**Check.** The entire section has first moment $60\cdot20\cdot10+20\cdot30\cdot35=33\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$. Subtracting the excluded strip and the calculated first moment gives

$$
33\,000-60\cdot10\cdot5-30\,000=0,
$$

in $\mathrm{mm^3}$.

</div>
