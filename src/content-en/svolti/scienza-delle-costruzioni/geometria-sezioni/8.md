## Exercise 1

A rectangular section is $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide and $90\,\mathrm{mm}$ high. Calculate its radii of gyration about the two principal axes parallel to the sides.
::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-15,-45 15,-45 15,45 -15,45" frecce="0,0,34,0,z;0,0,0,61,y" quote="-15 -45,15 -45,30 mm,9;15 -45,15 45,90 mm,27" titolo="Rectangle with axes along its two centerlines"}

$$
A=30\cdot90=2\,700\,\mathrm{mm^2}.
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_z&=\sqrt{\frac{30\cdot90^3}{12\cdot2\,700}}\,\mathrm{mm}
=15\sqrt3\,\mathrm{mm},\\[6pt]
\rho_y&=\sqrt{\frac{90\cdot30^3}{12\cdot2\,700}}\,\mathrm{mm}
=5\sqrt3\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The axes parallel to the sides are principal because they coincide with lines of symmetry. In the ratio of second moment to area, the dimension parallel to the axis cancels. The radius of gyration is proportional to the perpendicular dimension. The radius about $z$ is therefore three times the radius about $y$.

**Check.** Integrate to calculate the polar moment, then subtract the sum of the two second moments reconstructed from the radii of gyration. The residual is

$$
\int_{-15}^{15}\int_{-45}^{45}(y^2+z^2)\,dy\,dz
-2\,700\big[(15\sqrt3)^2+(5\sqrt3)^2\big]=0,
$$

in $\mathrm{mm^4}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An I-section has overall depth $100\,\mathrm{mm}$. Its flanges are $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide and $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ thick. The centered web is $12\,\mathrm{mm}$ thick and $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ high between the flanges. Calculate the smaller principal radius of gyration.
::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-30,-50 30,-50 30,-40 6,-40 6,40 30,40 30,50 -30,50 -30,40 -6,40 -6,-40 -30,-40" frecce="0,0,48,0,z;0,0,0,65,y" quote="-30 50,30 50,60 mm,-23;30 -50,30 50,100 mm,28;-6 -40,-6 40,80 mm,-34" titolo="Doubly symmetric I-section"}

$$
A=2\cdot60\cdot10+12\cdot80
=2\,160\,\mathrm{mm^2}.
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_y&=\frac{2\cdot10\cdot60^3+80\cdot12^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}
=371\,520\,\mathrm{mm^4},\\[6pt]
I_z&=\frac{60\cdot100^3-48\cdot80^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}
=2\,952\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\rho_{\min}=\rho_y
=\sqrt{\frac{371\,520}{2\,160}}\,\mathrm{mm}
=2\sqrt{43}\,\mathrm{mm}\simeq13.11\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For the vertical axis, add the web and flange contributions. For the horizontal axis, subtract the second moments of the two voids from the second moment of the outer rectangle. The removed portions are centered at middepth, so their contributions refer to the same axis. Since $I_y<I_z$, taking the square root after dividing by the common area gives the smaller radius about the vertical axis.

**Check.** Verify the smaller radius by calculating $I_y$ through subtraction of the voids from the outer rectangle. At horizontal distances between $6$ and $30\,\mathrm{mm}$, a strip of height $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ is removed on each side. The residual is

$$
100\int_{-30}^{30}z^2\,dz
-2\cdot80\int_6^{30}z^2\,dz
-2\,160(2\sqrt{43})^2=0,
$$

in $\mathrm{mm^4}$.

</div>
