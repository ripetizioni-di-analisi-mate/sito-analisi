## Exercise 1

A plate with $D=4\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$ and $\nu=\dfrac14$ has deflection $w=0.003(x^2-2xy-y^2)$, with $x,y$ and $w$ in meters. Calculate the three moment resultants per unit length.
::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,3.4 0,3.4" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,1.7,y" punti="3,1.7,P" etichette="4.4,1.55,M_x;3.35,2.45,M_y" riferimento=0 titolo="Directions of the bending moment resultants at P"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
w_{,xx}&=0.006\,\mathrm{m^{-1}},\\[6pt]
w_{,yy}&=-0.006\,\mathrm{m^{-1}},\\[6pt]
w_{,xy}&=-0.006\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
M_x&=-4\,000(0.006-0.0015)\,\mathrm{kN}=-18\,\mathrm{kN},\\[6pt]
M_y&=-4\,000(-0.006+0.0015)\,\mathrm{kN}=18\,\mathrm{kN},\\[6pt]
M_{xy}&=-4\,000(0.75)(-0.006)\,\mathrm{kN}=18\,\mathrm{kN}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Differentiating twice in one direction eliminates the mixed term and the square of the other coordinate. Taking the mixed derivative eliminates both squared terms. Through the Poisson effect, the bending moment resultant also includes a contribution from the curvature in the perpendicular direction. The twisting moment relation contains the factor $1-\nu$.

</div>

**Independent check.** The Laplacian of the deflection is zero. The trace of the moment tensor is proportional to the Laplacian and gives the following residual in kilonewtons.

$$
(-18)+18=0.
$$

## Exercise 2

At one point, $M_x=-16\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M_y=8\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $M_{xy}=0$. The plate has $D=8\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$ and $\nu=\dfrac14$. Find the two normal curvatures.
::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,3.4 0,3.4" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,1.7,y" punti="3,1.7,P" etichette="4.4,1.55,M_x;3.35,2.45,M_y" riferimento=0 titolo="Prescribed moments at point P"}

$$
\begin{cases}
w_{,xx}+\frac14 w_{,yy}=0.002\\[6pt]
\frac14 w_{,xx}+w_{,yy}=-0.001
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{15}{16}w_{,yy}&=-0.0015\,\mathrm{m^{-1}},\\[6pt]
w_{,yy}&=-0.0016\,\mathrm{m^{-1}},\\[6pt]
w_{,xx}&=0.0024\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Dividing both moments by $-D$ gives two linear combinations of the normal curvatures. Subtracting one-quarter of the first equation from the second eliminates the curvature in the $x$ direction. Substitute the result into the first equation. Since $D(1-\nu)$ is nonzero, a zero twisting moment also requires the mixed derivative to vanish.

</div>

**Independent check.** The difference between the moments gives $M_x-M_y=-D(1-\nu)(w_{,xx}-w_{,yy})$. With these curvatures, the residual in kilonewtons is

$$
(-16-8)+8\,000\cdot0.75\cdot(0.0024+0.0016)=0.
$$
