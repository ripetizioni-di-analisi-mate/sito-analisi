## Exercise 1

A strip in cylindrical bending is simply supported along two lines a distance $L=4\,\mathrm m$ apart. The uniform downward load is $q=6\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$ and the flexural rigidity is $D=2\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$. Calculate the bending moment and downward deflection at midspan.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" riferimento=0 frecce="-0.45,-0.85,0.05,-0.85,x;-0.45,-0.85,-0.45,-1.25,z" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Simply supported strip with a four-meter span"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
M_x(x)&=12x-3x^2 \quad \text{in }\mathrm{kN},\\[6pt]
M_x(2)&=12\,\mathrm{kN},\\[6pt]
w(2)&=\frac{5\cdot6\cdot4^4}{384\cdot2\,000}\,\mathrm m\\[6pt]
&=10\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The reaction at each support is $\dfrac{qL}{2}$ per unit width. Taking moments about the cut, the load to the left has resultant $qx$ per unit width and a moment arm $\dfrac x2$. The deflection formula for the simply supported strip follows by integrating $w^{\prime\prime}=-\dfrac{M_x}{D}$ with zero deflection at both supports.

</div>

**Independent check.** With $x$ in meters, the result is $w(x)=\dfrac{64x-8x^3+x^4}{8\,000}$ in meters. Symmetry requires zero slope at the center. Substituting $x=2$ gives

$$
w^{\prime}(2)=\frac{64-24\cdot2^2+4\cdot2^3}{8\,000}=0.
$$

## Exercise 2

A plate strip is clamped at $x=0$ and free at $x=L=2\,\mathrm m$. The uniform load is $q=8\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$ and the flexural rigidity is $D=2\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$. Calculate the fixed-end moment and the tip deflection.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.3,0.3,q" riferimento=0 frecce="-0.45,-0.85,0.05,-0.85,x;-0.45,-0.85,-0.45,-1.25,z" quote="A,B,2 m" titolo="Clamped strip loaded over its entire span"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
M_x(x)&=-\frac q2(L-x)^2,\\[6pt]
M_x(0)&=-16\,\mathrm{kN},\\[6pt]
w(x)&=\frac{qx^2}{24D}\bigl(6L^2-4Lx+x^2\bigr),\\[6pt]
w(L)&=\frac{qL^4}{8D}=8\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Isolate the portion between the cut and the free edge. The load on that portion has moment arm $\dfrac{L-x}{2}$ and produces a negative moment. To find the curvature, divide the negative of the moment by $D$. Integrate twice and use $w(0)=w^{\prime}(0)=0$ to determine the integration constants.

</div>

**Independent check.** Check transverse equilibrium by integrating the load over the entire strip. The shear at the fixed end, obtained by differentiating the moment, is $16\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. The residual, in kilonewtons per meter, is

$$
M_x^{\prime}(0)-qL=16-8\cdot2=0.
$$
