## Exercise 1

A rectangular plate simply supported on all four edges carries a uniform load $q=9\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$. Find the coefficients $q_{11}$, $q_{13}$, and $q_{21}$.
::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,6 0,6" tratteggi="-0.2,0 -0.2,6;3.2,0 3.2,6;0,-0.2 3,-0.2;0,6.2 3,6.2" frecce="0,0,1.5,0,x;0,0,0,2,y" quote="0 0,3 0,a;3 0,3 6,b" punti="1.5,3,C" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangular plate with side b parallel to y"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
q_{11}&=\frac{16q}{\pi^2}=144\,\mathrm{kN/m^2},\\[6pt]
q_{13}&=\frac{16q}{3\pi^2}=48\,\mathrm{kN/m^2},\\[6pt]
q_{21}&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For each mode with two odd indices, integrate the sines over an odd number of half-waves. This gives a factor equal to the product of the reciprocals of the two indices. For $q_{21}$, the sine along $x$ has two half-waves that cancel, so the integral along $x$ is zero.

</div>

**Independent check.** As an independent check on the first mode, recalculate the projection in each direction. The integrals are $\dfrac{2a}{\pi}$ and $\dfrac{2b}{\pi}$. The residual of the projection, in kilonewtons per square meter, is

$$
\frac{4}{ab}\,9\pi^2\frac{2a}{\pi}\frac{2b}{\pi}-144=0.
$$

## Exercise 2

For a uniformly loaded square plate simply supported on all four edges, compare the contributions of modes $(1,3)$ and $(3,3)$ to the deflection at the center. Express each as a signed ratio to the contribution of mode $(1,1)$.
::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,4 0,4" tratteggi="-0.2,0 -0.2,4;4.2,0 4.2,4;0,-0.2 4,-0.2;0,4.2 4,4.2" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,2,y" quote="0 0,4 0,a;4 0,4 4,a" punti="2,2,C" riferimento=0 titolo="Square plate simply supported on all four sides"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{W_{13}}{W_{11}}&=\frac13\left(\frac2{10}\right)^2=\frac1{75},\\[6pt]
\frac{W_{33}}{W_{11}}&=\frac19\left(\frac2{18}\right)^2=\frac1{729},\\[6pt]
\frac{w_{13}(C)}{w_{11}(C)}&=-\frac1{75},\qquad
\frac{w_{33}(C)}{w_{11}(C)}=\frac1{729}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Under uniform loading, the load coefficient decreases as the product of the indices increases. To obtain the deflection, also divide by the square of the sum of the squared spatial frequencies. The third sine harmonic equals $-1$ at the center. Thus the product of the sines is negative for mode $(1,3)$ and positive for mode $(3,3)$.

</div>
