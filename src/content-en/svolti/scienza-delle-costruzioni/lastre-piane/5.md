## Exercise 1

In one region of a plate, $M_x=10-3x$, $M_y=4+2y$, and $M_{xy}=1+x-2y$, with coordinates in meters and moment resultants in kilonewtons. Calculate $Q_x$, $Q_y$, and the load $q$ required by equilibrium.
::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,3.4 0,3.4" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,1.7,y" tratteggi="1.6,0 1.6,3.4;0,2.3 6,2.3" etichette="3.8,2.7,cut normal to y;2.8,1.35,cut normal to x" riferimento=0 titolo="Two orthogonal cuts in the plate region"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_x&=(-3-2)\,\mathrm{kN/m}=-5\,\mathrm{kN/m},\\[6pt]
Q_y&=(1+2)\,\mathrm{kN/m}=3\,\mathrm{kN/m},\\[6pt]
q&=-\partial_x Q_x-\partial_y Q_y=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To obtain $Q_x$, add the derivative of $M_x$ with respect to $x$ and that of the twisting moment with respect to $y$. To obtain $Q_y$, add the derivative of $M_{xy}$ with respect to $x$ and that of $M_y$ with respect to $y$. Both shear resultants are constant, so their divergence vanishes.

</div>

**Independent check.** Check the outward shear flux across a rectangle with sides $2\,\mathrm m$ in the $x$ direction and $3\,\mathrm m$ in the $y$ direction. The outward normals on opposite edges point in opposite directions. The sum of the outward shear forces, in kilonewtons, is

$$
3(-5)+3(5)+2(3)+2(-3)=0.
$$

## Exercise 2

A plate with $D=3\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$ has prescribed deflection $w=A(x^2+y^2)^2$, with $A=10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$. Calculate the required uniform load and the shear resultants on the rectangle with opposite vertices $(0,0)$ and $(2,1)$, with coordinates in meters.
::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,1 0,1" frecce="0,0,0.75,0,x;0,0,0,0.55,y" quote="0 0,2 0,2 m;2 1,2 0,1 m" riferimento=0 titolo="Integration region with sides measuring two meters and one meter"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta w&=16A(x^2+y^2),\\[6pt]
\Delta^2w&=64A,\\[6pt]
q&=64DA=19.2\,\mathrm{kN/m^2},\\[6pt]
Q_x&=-32DAx,\\[6pt]
Q_y&=-32DAy.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Expand the square. The second derivatives of the pure terms give $12x^2$ and $12y^2$. The mixed term contributes another $4x^2$ and $4y^2$ to the Laplacian. With $D$ constant, substituting the moment–curvature relations gives $Q_x=-D\partial_x\Delta w$ and the corresponding expression for $Q_y$.

</div>

**Independent check.** The flux vanishes on edges $x=0$ and $y=0$. On the opposite edges, the outward shear resultants are $-19.2$ and $-9.6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. The integral equilibrium equation for the region gives the following residual in kilonewtons.

$$
(-19.2)\cdot1+(-9.6)\cdot2+19.2\cdot2=0.
$$
