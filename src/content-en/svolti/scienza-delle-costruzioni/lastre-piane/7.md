## Exercise 1

A square plate of side $3\,\mathrm m$ and flexural rigidity $D=3\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$ is simply supported on all four edges. The load is the first sinusoidal mode, with amplitude $8\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$. Calculate the deflection at the center.
::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,4 0,4" tratteggi="-0.2,0 -0.2,4;4.2,0 4.2,4;0,-0.2 4,-0.2;0,4.2 4,4.2" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,2,y" quote="0 0,4 0,a;4 0,4 4,a" punti="2,2,C" riferimento=0 titolo="Square plate simply supported on all four sides"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha_1^2+\beta_1^2&=\frac{2\pi^2}{9}\,\mathrm{m^{-2}},\\[6pt]
W_{11}&=\frac{8}{3\,000\left(\dfrac{2\pi^2}{9}\right)^2}\,\mathrm m\\[6pt]
&=\frac{54}{\pi^4}\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The load is a product of two sines, with one half-wave along each side. The simply supported edges admit the same product for the deflection. At the center, both sines equal one, so the deflection equals the amplitude.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A simply supported rectangular plate has sides $a=4\,\mathrm m$ in the $x$ direction and $b=2\,\mathrm m$ in the $y$ direction. Given $D=2\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$ and $q_{21}=\pi^4\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$, calculate $w$ at points $A=(1,1)$, $C=(2,1)$, and $B=(3,1)$ for mode $(2,1)$.
::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,2 0,2" tratteggi="-0.2,0 -0.2,2;4.2,0 4.2,2;0,-0.2 4,-0.2;0,2.2 4,2.2" frecce="0,0,1.4,0,x;0,0,0,1,y" quote="0 0,4 0,4 m;4 0,4 2,2 m" punti="1,1,A;2,1,C;3,1,B" riferimento=0 titolo="Plate simply supported on all four sides, with A, C, and B on its centerline"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha_2=\beta_1&=\frac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}},\\[6pt]
W_{21}&=\frac{\pi^4}{2\,000\left(\dfrac{\pi^2}{2}\right)^2}\,\mathrm m=2\,\mathrm{mm},\\[6pt]
w_A&=2\,\mathrm{mm},\qquad w_C=0,\qquad w_B=-2\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The second sine harmonic along $x$ has two half-waves of opposite signs. Coordinate $x=2$ lies on a nodal line of the mode. On the transverse midline $y=1$, the second sine equals one, so the displacement signs depend only on the coordinate in the first direction. The negative displacement at $B$ is upward.

</div>

**Independent check.** Reflection $x\mapsto a-x$ reverses the sign of the load and preserves the boundary conditions. Check the calculated displacements for antisymmetry. The residual is

$$
w_A+w_B=2-2=0\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
