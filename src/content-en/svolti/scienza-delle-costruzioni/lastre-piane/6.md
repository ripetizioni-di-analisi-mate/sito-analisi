## Exercise 1

Near $x=0$, a plate has deflection $w=0.001x^2+0.0005x^3$ with $x$ and $w$ in meters. The flexural rigidity is $D=3\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$. Check the kinematic conditions at the clamped edge and find $M_{nn}$ and $V_n$ on the left edge.
::struttura{poligoni="0,0 5,0 5,3 0,3" frecce="0,0,1.5,0,x;0,0,0,1.5,y;0,1.6,-1,1.6,n" riferimento=0 titolo="Left edge with its outward normal pointing in the negative x direction"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
w(0)&=0,\qquad \partial_nw(0)=0,\\[6pt]
M_{nn}(0)&=-D\cdot0.002\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}=-6\,\mathrm{kN},\\[6pt]
Q_x(0)&=-D\cdot0.003\,\mathrm{m^{-2}}=-9\,\mathrm{kN/m},\\[6pt]
V_n(0)&=-Q_x(0)=9\,\mathrm{kN/m}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

On the left, the outward normal derivative is the negative of the derivative in the $x$ direction. Both prescribed quantities vanish there for the given deflection field, satisfying the kinematic conditions. The normal bending moment equals $M_x$ because the normal enters the tensor component twice. The normal shear resultant has the opposite sign to $Q_x$. The twisting moment is zero, so the effective shear equals the normal shear resultant.

</div>

**Independent check.** The load vanishes in the region next to the edge because the deflection field is cubic. The shear is constant, so the change in moment from $x=0$ to $x=1\,\mathrm m$ must be $-9\,\mathrm{kN}$. The residual in kilonewtons is

$$
\bigl[-3\,000(0.002+0.003)+6\bigr]+9=0.
$$

## Exercise 2

On the right edge $x=a$, the local moment fields are $M_x=-6(x-a)$ and $M_{xy}=3y$, with coordinates in meters and moments in kilonewtons. Check the natural free-edge conditions along the edge, excluding its endpoints.
::struttura{poligoni="0,0 5,0 5,3 0,3" tratteggi="5,0 5,3" frecce="0,0,1.8,0,x;0,0,0,1.6,y;5,1.5,5.8,1.5,n" riferimento=0 titolo="Right edge with its outward normal pointing in the positive x direction"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
M_{nn}(a,y)&=M_x(a,y)=0,\\[6pt]
Q_x(a,y)&=(-6+3)\,\mathrm{kN/m}=-3\,\mathrm{kN/m},\\[6pt]
V_n(a,y)&=Q_x(a,y)+\partial_y M_{xy}(a,y)=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

On the right edge, the outward normal points in the $+x$ direction. The normal bending moment vanishes because the factor $x-a$ equals zero. The shear resultant obtained from equilibrium is nonzero, but the derivative of the twisting moment cancels it in the effective shear. Thus both natural conditions hold in the interior of the edge.

</div>

**Independent check.** On an edge segment of length $2\,\mathrm m$, the contribution of $Q_x$ can be checked using the difference between the endpoint values of $M_{xy}$. The integral residual, in kilonewtons, is

$$
(-3)\cdot2+(3\cdot2-3\cdot0)=0.
$$
