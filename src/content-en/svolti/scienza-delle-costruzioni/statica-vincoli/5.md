## Exercise 1

On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$. The unit normal to the roller surface at $B$ is $\left(- \frac{3}{5},\frac{4}{5}\right)$. A downward force of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at coordinate $3\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Find the external reactions.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,306.8699" quote="0 0,3 0,3 m;3 0,5 0,2 m" forze="3 0,0,-1,P_1" titolo="Inclined roller and load position"}

The equilibrium equations are the two force balances and the moment balance about $A$. Use kilonewtons and meters.

$$
\begin{cases}
H_{A} - \frac{3 R_{B}}{5}=0 \\[6pt]
\frac{4 R_{B}}{5} + V_{A} - 20=0 \\[6pt]
4 R_{B} - 60=0
\end{cases}
$$
The moment equation gives the third unknown. The two force equations give the remaining unknowns.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{B}&=15\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
H_{A}&=9\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_{A}&=8\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
H_{A}=9\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=8\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad R_{B}=15\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The roller is frictionless, so its reaction acts along the specified normal. Only the vertical component has a nonzero moment arm about $A$. The horizontal component of the roller reaction acts to the left, so the pin reacts to the right.

To check the result, take moments about $B$ for the entire structure. Substituting the reactions gives

$$
40 - 5 V_{A}=40-5\cdot8=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

</div>

## Exercise 2

On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $6\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$. The unit normal to the roller surface at $B$ is $\left(\frac{3}{5},\frac{4}{5}\right)$. A force with components $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward acts at coordinate $2\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Find the external reactions.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,233.1301" quote="0 0,2 0,2 m;2 0,6 0,4 m" forze="2 0,-0.1667,-1,P_1" titolo="Inclined roller and load position"}

The equilibrium equations are the two force balances and the moment balance about $A$. Use kilonewtons and meters.

$$
\begin{cases}
H_{A} + \frac{3 R_{B}}{5} - 4=0 \\[6pt]
\frac{4 R_{B}}{5} + V_{A} - 24=0 \\[6pt]
\frac{24 R_{B}}{5} - 48=0
\end{cases}
$$
The moment equation gives the third unknown. The two force equations give the remaining unknowns.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{B}&=10\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
H_{A}&=-2\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_{A}&=16\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
H_{A}=-2\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=16\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad R_{B}=10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The line of action of the applied horizontal force passes through $A$. The inclined roller also contributes a horizontal component to the force balance. The pin balances the sum of this component and the applied horizontal force component.

To check the result, take moments about $B$ for the entire structure. Substituting the reactions gives

$$
96 - 6 V_{A}=96-6\cdot16=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

</div>
