## Exercise 1

On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. The overhang beyond $B$ ends at $D$ and has length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. The supports can resist force in either direction. A downward force of $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at coordinate $6\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Find the external reactions.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;D,6,0" aste="A-B;B-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,4 0,4 m;4 0,6 0,2 m" forze="6 0,0,-1,P_1" titolo="Span, overhang, and applied loads"}

The equilibrium equations are the two force balances and the moment balance about $A$. Use kilonewtons and meters.

$$
\begin{cases}
H_{A}=0 \\[6pt]
V_{A} + V_{B} - 16=0 \\[6pt]
4 V_{B} - 96=0
\end{cases}
$$
The moment equation gives the third unknown. The two force equations give the remaining unknowns.

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_{B}&=24\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
H_{A}&=0\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_{A}&=-8\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
H_{A}=0\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=-8\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{B}=24\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The force acts beyond the last support. Its moment arm about $A$ exceeds that of $V_B$, so $V_B$ exceeds the applied force. Vertical equilibrium requires a downward reaction at $A$.

To check the result, take moments about $B$ for the entire structure. Substituting the reactions gives

$$
- 4 V_{A} - 32=-4\cdot\left(-8\right)-32=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

</div>

## Exercise 2

On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. The overhang beyond $B$ ends at $D$ and has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$. The supports can resist force in either direction. A downward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at coordinate $2\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Between coordinates $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and $8\,\mathrm{m}$, a downward load has uniform intensity $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find the external reactions.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;D,8,0" aste="A-B;B-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,2 0,2 m;2 0,5 0,3 m;5 0,8 0,3 m" forze="2 0,0,-1,P_1" carichi="5 0,8 0,0.75,0.75,q" titolo="Span, overhang, and applied loads"}

The distributed load has a resultant of magnitude $Q$ acting at coordinate $x_Q$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q&=\frac{4+4}{2}\cdot3=12\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
x_Q&=\frac{\int_{5}^{8}xq(x)\,\mathrm{d}x}{Q}=\frac{13}{2}\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
The equilibrium equations are the two force balances and the moment balance about $A$. Use kilonewtons and meters.

$$
\begin{cases}
H_{A}=0 \\[6pt]
V_{A} + V_{B} - 24=0 \\[6pt]
5 V_{B} - 102=0
\end{cases}
$$
The moment equation gives the third unknown. The two force equations give the remaining unknowns.

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_{B}&=\frac{102}{5}\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
H_{A}&=0\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_{A}&=\frac{18}{5}\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
H_{A}=0\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=\frac{18}{5}\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{B}=\frac{102}{5}\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The resultant of the uniform load acts at the midpoint of the overhang. The force to the left of $B$ and the load on the overhang produce opposite moments about $B$. Comparing the products of force and moment arm gives the direction of the reaction at $A$.

To check the result, take moments about $B$ for the entire structure. Substituting the reactions gives

$$
18 - 5 V_{A}=18-5\cdot\frac{18}{5}=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

</div>
