## Exercise 1

On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $6\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Between coordinates $0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $6\,\mathrm{m}$, the intensity of a downward vertical load varies linearly from $0\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find the external reactions.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,6 0,6 m" carichi="0 0,6 0,0,0.75,q" titolo="Distributed load and beam supports"}

The distributed load has a resultant of magnitude $Q$ acting at coordinate $x_Q$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q&=\frac{0+6}{2}\cdot6=18\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
x_Q&=\frac{\int_{0}^{6}xq(x)\,\mathrm{d}x}{Q}=4\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
The equilibrium equations are the two force balances and the moment balance about $A$. Use kilonewtons and meters.

$$
\begin{cases}
H_{A}=0 \\[6pt]
V_{A} + V_{B} - 18=0 \\[6pt]
6 V_{B} - 72=0
\end{cases}
$$
The moment equation gives the third unknown. The two force equations give the remaining unknowns.

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_{B}&=12\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
H_{A}&=0\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_{A}&=6\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
H_{A}=0\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=6\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{B}=12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The load increases to the right, so its resultant acts one third of the base length from the right edge. Its line of action is closer to $B$, so the reaction at $B$ is larger.

To check the result, take moments about $B$ for the entire structure. Substituting the reactions gives

$$
36 - 6 V_{A}=36-6\cdot6=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

</div>

## Exercise 2

On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $8\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Between coordinates $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $8\,\mathrm{m}$, the intensity of a downward vertical load varies linearly from $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find the external reactions.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,2 0,2 m;2 0,8 0,6 m" carichi="2 0,8 0,0.1875,0.75,q" titolo="Distributed load and beam supports"}

The distributed load has a resultant of magnitude $Q$ acting at coordinate $x_Q$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q&=\frac{2+8}{2}\cdot6=30\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
x_Q&=\frac{\int_{2}^{8}xq(x)\,\mathrm{d}x}{Q}=\frac{28}{5}\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
The equilibrium equations are the two force balances and the moment balance about $A$. Use kilonewtons and meters.

$$
\begin{cases}
H_{A}=0 \\[6pt]
V_{A} + V_{B} - 30=0 \\[6pt]
8 V_{B} - 168=0
\end{cases}
$$
The moment equation gives the third unknown. The two force equations give the remaining unknowns.

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_{B}&=21\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
H_{A}&=0\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_{A}&=9\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
H_{A}=0\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=9\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{B}=21\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The trapezoidal loading is equivalent to a rectangle and a triangle of increasing intensity. The rectangular portion has resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at coordinate $5\,\mathrm{m}$. The triangular portion has resultant $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ at coordinate $6\,\mathrm{m}$. Their combined moment equals the moment of the single resultant.

To check the result, take moments about $B$ for the entire structure. Substituting the reactions gives

$$
72 - 8 V_{A}=72-8\cdot9=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

</div>
