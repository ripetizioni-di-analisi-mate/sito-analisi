## Exercise 1

On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The beam is fixed at $A$. A force with components $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward acts at coordinate $3\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Find the external reactions.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="0 0,3 0,3 m;3 0,4 0,1 m" forze="3 0,0.6,-1,P_1" titolo="Fixed support and loads on the cantilever"}

The equilibrium equations are the two force balances and the moment balance about $A$. Use kilonewtons and meters.

$$
\begin{cases}
H_{A} + 6=0 \\[6pt]
V_{A} - 10=0 \\[6pt]
M_{A} - 30=0
\end{cases}
$$
The moment equation gives the third unknown. The two force equations give the remaining unknowns.

$$
\begin{aligned}
M_{A}&=30\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \\[6pt]
H_{A}&=-6\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_{A}&=10\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
H_{A}=-6\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=10\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad M_{A}=30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The fixed support restrains two translations and one rotation, giving two unknown force components and an unknown reaction couple. The vertical force produces a clockwise moment about $A$. The reaction couple is therefore counterclockwise.

To check the result, take moments about $B$ for the entire structure. Substituting the reactions gives

$$
M_{A} - 4 V_{A} + 10=1\cdot30-4\cdot10+10=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

</div>

## Exercise 2

On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The beam is fixed at $A$. A downward force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at coordinate $4\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Between coordinates $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $6\,\mathrm{m}$, a downward load has uniform intensity $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. A counterclockwise couple of magnitude $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at $B$. Find the external reactions.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="0 0,2 0,2 m;2 0,4 0,2 m;4 0,6 0,2 m" forze="4 0,0,-1,P_1" carichi="2 0,6 0,0.75,0.75,q" coppie="B,1," etichette="6.54,0.48,M_a" titolo="Fixed support and loads on the cantilever"}

The distributed load has a resultant of magnitude $Q$ acting at coordinate $x_Q$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q&=\frac{3+3}{2}\cdot4=12\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
x_Q&=\frac{\int_{2}^{6}xq(x)\,\mathrm{d}x}{Q}=4\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
The equilibrium equations are the two force balances and the moment balance about $A$. Use kilonewtons and meters.

$$
\begin{cases}
H_{A}=0 \\[6pt]
V_{A} - 20=0 \\[6pt]
M_{A} - 68=0
\end{cases}
$$
The moment equation gives the third unknown. The two force equations give the remaining unknowns.

$$
\begin{aligned}
M_{A}&=68\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \\[6pt]
H_{A}&=0\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_{A}&=20\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
H_{A}=0\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=20\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad M_{A}=68\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The point force and the resultant of the uniform load act at coordinate $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Both produce clockwise moments about $A$. The applied couple is counterclockwise and reduces the moment the fixed support must resist.

To check the result, take moments about $B$ for the entire structure. Substituting the reactions gives

$$
M_{A} - 6 V_{A} + 52=1\cdot68-6\cdot20+52=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

</div>
