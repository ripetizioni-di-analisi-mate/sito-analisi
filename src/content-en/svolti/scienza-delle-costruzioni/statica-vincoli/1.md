## Exercise 1

On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $6\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A force with components $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward acts at coordinate $2\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Between coordinates $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $6\,\mathrm{m}$, a downward load has uniform intensity $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find the external reactions.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,2 0,2 m;2 0,3 0,1 m;3 0,6 0,3 m" forze="2 0,0.75,-1,P_1" carichi="3 0,6 0,0.75,0.75,q" titolo="Distributed load and beam supports"}

The distributed load has a resultant of magnitude $Q$ acting at coordinate $x_Q$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q&=\frac{3+3}{2}\cdot3=9\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
x_Q&=\frac{\int_{3}^{6}xq(x)\,\mathrm{d}x}{Q}=\frac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
The equilibrium equations are the two force balances and the moment balance about $A$. Use kilonewtons and meters.

$$
\begin{cases}
H_{A} + 9=0 \\[6pt]
V_{A} + V_{B} - 21=0 \\[6pt]
6 V_{B} - \frac{129}{2}=0
\end{cases}
$$
The moment equation gives the third unknown. The two force equations give the remaining unknowns.

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_{B}&=\frac{43}{4}\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
H_{A}&=-9\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_{A}&=\frac{41}{4}\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
H_{A}=-9\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=\frac{41}{4}\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{B}=\frac{43}{4}\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The horizontal component passes through $A$, so its moment about $A$ is zero. The resultant of the uniform load acts at the midpoint of the loaded interval. The horizontal pin reaction acts to the left, and both vertical reactions act upward.

To check the result, take moments about $B$ for the entire structure. Substituting the reactions gives

$$
\frac{123}{2} - 6 V_{A}=\frac{123}{2}-6\cdot\frac{41}{4}=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

</div>

## Exercise 2

On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A force with components $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward acts at coordinate $1\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. A clockwise couple of magnitude $5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at $B$. Find the external reactions.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,1 0,1 m;1 0,5 0,4 m" forze="1 0,-0.4,-1,P_1" coppie="B,-1," etichette="5.45,0.4,M_a" titolo="Supports and concentrated loads"}

The equilibrium equations are the two force balances and the moment balance about $A$. Use kilonewtons and meters.

$$
\begin{cases}
H_{A} - 4=0 \\[6pt]
V_{A} + V_{B} - 10=0 \\[6pt]
5 V_{B} - 15=0
\end{cases}
$$
The moment equation gives the third unknown. The two force equations give the remaining unknowns.

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_{B}&=3\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
H_{A}&=4\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_{A}&=7\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
H_{A}=4\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=7\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{B}=3\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The applied couple has a clockwise moment about every point. The vertical force acts near $A$, so the vertical reaction at the left support is larger. The applied horizontal component acts to the left, so the pin reacts to the right.

To check the result, take moments about $B$ for the entire structure. Substituting the reactions gives

$$
35 - 5 V_{A}=35-5\cdot7=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

</div>
