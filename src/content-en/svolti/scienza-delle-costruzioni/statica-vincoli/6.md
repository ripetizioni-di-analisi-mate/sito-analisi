## Exercise 1

Frame $ACD$ is fixed at $A=(0,0)$. The coordinates of $C$ and $D$, in meters, are $(0,3)$ and $(4,3)$. A force with components $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward acts at point $(4,3)$. Find the external reactions.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,3;D,4,3" aste="A-C;C-D" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,C,3 m;C,D,4 m,0.8" forze="4 3,0.6667,-1,P_1" titolo="Frame geometry and loading"}

The equilibrium equations are the two force balances and the moment balance about $A$. Use kilonewtons and meters.

$$
\begin{cases}
H_{A} + 6=0 \\[6pt]
V_{A} - 9=0 \\[6pt]
M_{A} - 54=0
\end{cases}
$$
The moment equation gives the third unknown. The two force equations give the remaining unknowns.

$$
\begin{aligned}
M_{A}&=54\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \\[6pt]
H_{A}&=-6\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_{A}&=9\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
H_{A}=-6\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=9\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad M_{A}=54\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The horizontal force acts at a positive elevation. Its moment arm equals the frame height, and it produces a clockwise moment. The vertical component also produces a clockwise moment about $A$. The fixed-end couple balances the sum of the two moments.

To check the result, take moments about $O=(4,0)$ for the entire structure. Substituting the reactions gives

$$
M_{A} - 4 V_{A} - 18=1\cdot54-4\cdot9-18=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

</div>

## Exercise 2

The rigid portal frame $ACDB$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. The supports can resist force in either direction. The joint coordinates, in meters, are $A=(0,0)$, $C=(0,4)$, $D=(6,4)$, and $B=(6,0)$. A force with components $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward acts at point $(2,4)$. Find the external reactions.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,0,4;D,6,4" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,4 m;C,D,6 m,0.8" forze="2 4,-0.75,-1,P_1" titolo="Portal frame with an off-axis load"}

The equilibrium equations are the two force balances and the moment balance about $A$. Use kilonewtons and meters.

$$
\begin{cases}
H_{A} - 9=0 \\[6pt]
V_{A} + V_{B} - 12=0 \\[6pt]
6 V_{B} + 12=0
\end{cases}
$$
The moment equation gives the third unknown. The two force equations give the remaining unknowns.

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_{B}&=-2\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
H_{A}&=9\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_{A}&=14\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
H_{A}=9\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=14\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{B}=-2\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The leftward horizontal component produces a counterclockwise moment about $A$. The vertical component produces a clockwise moment. The net moment of the applied loads is counterclockwise, so the reaction at $B$ acts downward.

To check the result, take moments about $B$ for the entire structure. Substituting the reactions gives

$$
84 - 6 V_{A}=84-6\cdot14=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

</div>
