## Exercise 1

On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A counterclockwise couple of magnitude $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at $B$. The roller can resist a reaction in either direction. Find the external reactions.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,4 0,4 m" coppie="B,1," etichette="4.36,0.32,M_a" titolo="Supports and concentrated loads"}

The equilibrium equations are the two force balances and the moment balance about $A$. Use kilonewtons and meters.

$$
\begin{cases}
H_{A}=0 \\[6pt]
V_{A} + V_{B}=0 \\[6pt]
4 V_{B} + 12=0
\end{cases}
$$
The moment equation gives the third unknown. The two force equations give the remaining unknowns.

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_{B}&=-3\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
H_{A}&=0\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_{A}&=3\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
H_{A}=0\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=3\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{B}=-3\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The applied couple has zero resultant force. The two vertical reactions therefore form a couple of the opposite sign. The reaction at $B$ acts downward, as the bilateral roller permits.

To check the result, take moments about $B$ for the entire structure. Substituting the reactions gives

$$
12 - 4 V_{A}=12-4\cdot3=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

</div>

## Exercise 2

On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $6\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A downward force of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at coordinate $2\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. A clockwise couple of magnitude $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at $B$. Find the external reactions.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,2 0,2 m;2 0,6 0,4 m" forze="2 0,0,-1,P_1" coppie="B,-1," etichette="6.54,0.48,M_a" titolo="Supports and concentrated loads"}

The equilibrium equations are the two force balances and the moment balance about $A$. Use kilonewtons and meters.

$$
\begin{cases}
H_{A}=0 \\[6pt]
V_{A} + V_{B} - 18=0 \\[6pt]
6 V_{B} - 60=0
\end{cases}
$$
The moment equation gives the third unknown. The two force equations give the remaining unknowns.

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_{B}&=10\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
H_{A}&=0\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_{A}&=8\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
H_{A}=0\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=8\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{B}=10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The clockwise couple adds to the clockwise moment of the force about $A$. The roller balances this moment, and the pin balances the remainder of the vertical force. A couple has the same moment about any point.

To check the result, take moments about $B$ for the entire structure. Substituting the reactions gives

$$
48 - 6 V_{A}=48-6\cdot8=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

</div>
