## Exercise 1

On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $7\,\mathrm{m}$. The beam is fixed at $A$, with an internal hinge at $C$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Segment $AC$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. A downward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at coordinate $5\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Find the external reactions.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,7,0;C,4,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" quote="0 0,4 0,4 m;4 0,5 0,1 m;5 0,7 0,2 m" cerniere="C" forze="5 0,0,-1,P_1" titolo="Internal hinge and span dimensions"}

On segment $CB$, assume $X_C$ acts to the right and $Y_C$ acts upward. Taking moments about $C$ gives the following system in kilonewtons and meters.

$$
\begin{cases}
X_{C}=0 \\[6pt]
V_{B} + Y_{C} - 12=0 \\[6pt]
3 V_{B} - 12=0
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
X_{C}&=0\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
Y_{C}&=8\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_{B}&=4\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$
On segment $AC$ the hinge force components reverse direction. The equilibrium equations, with moments taken about $A$, give the fixed-support reactions.

$$
\begin{cases}
H_{A}=0 \\[6pt]
V_{A} - 8=0 \\[6pt]
M_{A} - 32=0
\end{cases}
$$

$$
H_{A}=0\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=8\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad M_{A}=32\,\mathrm{kN\,m},\qquad V_{B}=4\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

On segment $CB$, the forces at $C$ pass through the moment center, so the moment equation gives the roller reaction directly. The internal hinge exerts an upward force on the suspended span and a downward force on the fixed segment.

To check the result, take moments about $B$ for the entire structure. Substituting the reactions gives

$$
M_{A} - 7 V_{A} + 24=1\cdot32-7\cdot8+24=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

</div>

## Exercise 2

On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $10\,\mathrm{m}$. The beam is fixed at $A$, with an internal hinge at $C$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Segment $AC$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. A force with components $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward acts at coordinate $8\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Between coordinates $0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a downward load has uniform intensity $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find the external reactions.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,10,0;C,4,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" quote="0 0,4 0,4 m;4 0,8 0,4 m;8 0,10 0,2 m" cerniere="C" forze="8 0,0.3333,-1,P_1" carichi="0 0,4 0,0.75,0.75,q" titolo="Internal hinge and span dimensions"}

The distributed load has a resultant of magnitude $Q$ acting at coordinate $x_Q$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q&=\frac{3+3}{2}\cdot4=12\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
x_Q&=\frac{\int_{0}^{4}xq(x)\,\mathrm{d}x}{Q}=2\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
On segment $CB$, assume $X_C$ acts to the right and $Y_C$ acts upward. Taking moments about $C$ gives the following system in kilonewtons and meters.

$$
\begin{cases}
X_{C} + 6=0 \\[6pt]
V_{B} + Y_{C} - 18=0 \\[6pt]
6 V_{B} - 72=0
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
X_{C}&=-6\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
Y_{C}&=6\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_{B}&=12\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$
On segment $AC$ the hinge force components reverse direction. The equilibrium equations, with moments taken about $A$, give the fixed-support reactions.

$$
\begin{cases}
H_{A} + 6=0 \\[6pt]
V_{A} - 18=0 \\[6pt]
M_{A} - 48=0
\end{cases}
$$

$$
H_{A}=-6\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=18\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad M_{A}=48\,\mathrm{kN\,m},\qquad V_{B}=12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The hinge balances the horizontal force on the suspended span because the roller reaction is vertical. The fixed segment experiences the opposite horizontal force. The uniform load on that segment affects the fixed-support reactions and lies outside the free body $CB$.

To check the result, take moments about $B$ for the entire structure. Substituting the reactions gives

$$
M_{A} - 10 V_{A} + 132=1\cdot48-10\cdot18+132=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

</div>
