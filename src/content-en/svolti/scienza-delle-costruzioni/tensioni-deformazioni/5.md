## Exercise 1

An isotropic elastic sheet in plane stress has $E=180\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\nu=0.20$, $\sigma_x=90\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=45\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=15\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the normal strains and engineering shear strain.
::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" forze="1 0,0.62,0,σ_x;-1 0,-0.62,0;0 1,0,0.62,σ_y;0 -1,0,-0.62" frecce="1.16,-0.56,1.16,0.56,τ_xy;-1.16,0.56,-1.16,-0.56;-0.56,1.16,0.56,1.16;0.56,-1.16,-0.56,-1.16" titolo="Element with positive normal and shear stresses"}


$$
G=\frac{180\,000}{2(1+0.20)}\,\mathrm{MPa}
=75\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}.
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\varepsilon_x&=\frac{90-0.20\cdot45}{180\,000}=450\cdot10^{-6},\\
\varepsilon_y&=\frac{45-0.20\cdot90}{180\,000}=150\cdot10^{-6},\\
\varepsilon_z&=-\frac{0.20(90+45)}{180\,000}=-150\cdot10^{-6},\\
\gamma_{xy}&=\frac{15}{75\,000}=200\cdot10^{-6}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The broad faces are traction-free. Since the sum of the in-plane stresses is positive, the Poisson effect reduces the thickness. The shear strain follows from the shear constitutive law and depends on $G$.

**Check.** The three-dimensional volumetric relation gives $\varepsilon_x+\varepsilon_y+\varepsilon_z=\dfrac{(1-2\nu)(\sigma_x+\sigma_y+\sigma_z)}{E}$. In microstrain the residual is $450+150-150-\dfrac{0.60\cdot135\cdot10^6}{180\,000}=0$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A sheet in plane stress has measured strains $\varepsilon_x=400\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_y=100\cdot10^{-6}$, and $\gamma_{xy}=-250\cdot10^{-6}$. Assuming isotropic linear elasticity with $E=150\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\nu=0.25$, calculate $\sigma_x$, $\sigma_y$, and $\tau_{xy}$.
::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,2 0,2" frecce="0,0,3.8,0,x;0,0,0,2.8,y" punti="1.5,1,P,pieno" riferimento=0 titolo="Point in the planar region under study"}


$$
\frac{E}{1-\nu^2}=160\,000\,\mathrm{MPa},\qquad
G=60\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}.
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_x&=160\,000(400+0.25\cdot100)\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{MPa}=68\,\mathrm{MPa},\\[6pt]
\sigma_y&=160\,000(100+0.25\cdot400)\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{MPa}=32\,\mathrm{MPa},\\[6pt]
\tau_{xy}&=60\,000(-250)\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{MPa}=-15\,\mathrm{MPa}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Invert the two coupled equations to obtain the normal stresses. Strain measured in either direction contributes to both stresses. The engineering shear strain is negative and therefore has the same sign as the shear stress.

**Check.** The direct constitutive law gives $\dfrac{68-0.25\cdot32}{150\,000}-400\cdot10^{-6}=0$ and $\dfrac{32-0.25\cdot68}{150\,000}-100\cdot10^{-6}=0$. In shear it gives $\dfrac{-15}{60\,000}+250\cdot10^{-6}=0$.

</div>
