## Exercise 1

An isotropic, linearly elastic solid is in plane strain. Given $\sigma_x=96\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=48\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $E=180\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\nu=0.25$, find $\sigma_z$ and the in-plane normal strains.
::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2.8,0 2.8,1.8 0,1.8" linee="2.8,0 3.6,0.65 3.6,2.45 0.8,2.45 0,1.8;2.8,1.8 3.6,2.45" frecce="2.8,1.8,4.1,2.86,z" etichette="1.25,0.95,ε_z = 0" titolo="Element with strain along z restrained"}


$$
\sigma_z=0.25(96+48)\,\mathrm{MPa}=36\,\mathrm{MPa}.
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\varepsilon_x&=\frac{96-0.25(48+36)}{180\,000}
=\frac1{2\,400},\\[6pt]
\varepsilon_y&=\frac{48-0.25(96+36)}{180\,000}
=\frac1{12\,000}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Strain along $z$ is restrained, so an out-of-plane reaction stress develops. The Poisson contribution along $x$ includes both the stress along $y$ and the stress along $z$. The in-plane strains are approximately $416.67$ and $83.33$ microstrain.

**Check.** The volumetric strain is $\frac1{2\,400}+\frac1{12\,000}=\frac1{2\,000}$. The volumetric constitutive relation gives $\dfrac{(1-2\cdot0.25)(96+48+36)}{180\,000}-\dfrac1{2\,000}=0$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Under plane strain, $\varepsilon_x=500\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_y=-200\cdot10^{-6}$, and $\gamma_{xy}=150\cdot10^{-6}$. The material is isotropic and linearly elastic, with $E=160\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\nu=0.25$. Find $\sigma_x$, $\sigma_y$, $\sigma_z$, and $\tau_{xy}$.
::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2.8,0 2.8,1.8 0,1.8" linee="2.8,0 3.6,0.65 3.6,2.45 0.8,2.45 0,1.8;2.8,1.8 3.6,2.45" frecce="2.8,1.8,4.1,2.86,z" etichette="1.25,0.95,ε_z = 0" titolo="Element with strain along z restrained"}


$$
G=64\,000\,\mathrm{MPa},\qquad
\lambda=\frac{E\nu}{(1+\nu)(1-2\nu)}=64\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}.
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_z&=64\,000(500-200)\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{MPa}=19.2\,\mathrm{MPa},\\
\sigma_x&=2\cdot64\,000\cdot500\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{MPa}+19.2\,\mathrm{MPa}=83.2\,\mathrm{MPa},\\
\sigma_y&=2\cdot64\,000(-200)\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{MPa}+19.2\,\mathrm{MPa}=-6.4\,\mathrm{MPa},\\
\tau_{xy}&=64\,000\cdot150\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{MPa}=9.6\,\mathrm{MPa}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first-order volume change is the sum of the in-plane normal strains because $\varepsilon_z=0$. The volumetric term is therefore common to all three normal stresses. Component $\sigma_y$ is compressive because the direct contribution along $y$ exceeds the volumetric term in magnitude.

**Check.** The direct constitutive law gives $\dfrac{-6.4-0.25(83.2+19.2)}{160\,000}+200\cdot10^{-6}=0$. The out-of-plane condition gives $\dfrac{19.2-0.25(83.2-6.4)}{160\,000}=0$.

</div>
