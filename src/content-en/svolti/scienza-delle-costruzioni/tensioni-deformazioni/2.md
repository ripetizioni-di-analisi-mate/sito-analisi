## Exercise 1

In a planar region, $\sigma_x=(18+2x)\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=12\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=-3y\,\mathrm{MPa}$, where $x,y$ are the numerical values of the coordinates in meters. Find the body force per unit volume required for equilibrium.
::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,2 0,2" frecce="0,0,3.8,0,x;0,0,0,2.8,y" punti="1.5,1,P,pieno" riferimento=0 titolo="Point in the planar region under study"}


$$
\begin{aligned}
\partial_x\sigma_x+\partial_y\tau_{xy}&=(2-3)\,\mathrm{MPa/m},\\
\partial_x\tau_{xy}+\partial_y\sigma_y&=(0+0)\,\mathrm{MPa/m},\\[6pt]
\mathbf b&=(1,0)\,\mathrm{MN/m^3}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The normal stress increases to the right, while the shear stress decreases upward. Their combined effect is a net contact force along $-x$. For equilibrium the body force points toward $+x$.

**Check.** For a unit cube the faces normal to $x$ contribute $2\,\mathrm{MN}$ along $x$, and those normal to $y$ contribute $-3\,\mathrm{MN}$. Adding the body force gives $2-3+1=0$ in $\mathrm{MN}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In the region $0<x<3\,\mathrm m$, prescribe $\sigma_x=(6+4x)\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=(8-2y)\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=(5x+cy)\,\mathrm{MPa}$, where $x,y$ are the numerical coordinates in meters. Given $b_x=0$, find $c$ and $b_y$. Then determine the traction on the boundary $x=3\,\mathrm m$ at $y=1\,\mathrm m$.
::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,2 0,2" frecce="0,0,3.8,0,x;0,0,0,2.8,y;3,1,3.7,1,n" riferimento=0 titolo="Outward normal at the right boundary"}


$$
\begin{cases}
4+c=0,\\
5-2+b_y=0
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
c=-4,\qquad b_y=-3\,\mathrm{MN/m^3}.
$$
$$
\mathbf t(\mathbf e_x)
=\begin{pmatrix}6+4\cdot3\\ 5\cdot3-4\cdot1\end{pmatrix}\mathrm{MPa}
=(18,11)\,\mathrm{MPa}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Express $b_y$ in $\mathrm{MN/m^3}$ in the system of equations. Equilibrium in the $x$ direction determines the coefficient $c$, while equilibrium in the $y$ direction determines the body force. The boundary normal is $\mathbf e_x$, so its traction is the first column of the stress tensor at the specified point.

**Check.** On the unit prism between $x=1$ and $x=2$ and between $y=0$ and $y=1$, the variation in $\tau_{xy}$ across the faces normal to $x$ contributes $5\,\mathrm{MN}$ along $y$. The variation in $\sigma_y$ on the other two faces contributes $-2\,\mathrm{MN}$. Including the body force gives the residual $5-2-3=0$ in $\mathrm{MN}$.

</div>
