## Exercise 1

A rectangular strain-gauge rosette measures $350\cdot10^{-6}$, $260\cdot10^{-6}$, and $-50\cdot10^{-6}$ at $0^\circ$, $45^\circ$, and $90^\circ$, respectively. Find the engineering shear strain and shear stress, given $G=75\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$ for the isotropic elastic material.
::struttura{poligoni="-0.2,-0.2 3.2,-0.2 3.2,2.8 -0.2,2.8" frecce="0,0,2.6,0,0°;0,0,1.75,1.75,45°;0,0,0,2.35,90°" titolo="Orientations of the three strain-gauge grids"}


$$
\begin{aligned}
\gamma_{xy}&=(2\cdot260-350+50)\cdot10^{-6}=220\cdot10^{-6},\\[6pt]
\tau_{xy}&=75\,000\cdot220\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{MPa}=16.5\,\mathrm{MPa}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two grids aligned with the axes give the Cartesian normal strains. The inclined grid also measures the shear contribution. Subtracting the mean of the axial readings from its reading gives half the engineering shear strain.

**Check.** The reconstructed strain tensor has off-diagonal component $110\cdot10^{-6}$. The direction at $45^\circ$ gives a residual of $\dfrac12(350+2\cdot110-50)-260=0$ in microstrain.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A rectangular strain-gauge rosette reads $\varepsilon_0=200\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_{45}=125\cdot10^{-6}$, and $\varepsilon_{90}=50\cdot10^{-6}$. Its orientation is shown in the figure. Under a new load, the reading at $45^\circ$ changes from $125$ to $185$ microstrain, while the other two readings stay the same. Determine the final principal strains and the direction of the larger one.
::struttura{poligoni="-0.2,-0.2 3.2,-0.2 3.2,2.8 -0.2,2.8" frecce="0,0,2.6,0,0°;0,0,1.75,1.75,45°;0,0,0,2.35,90°" titolo="Orientations of the three strain-gauge grids"}


$$
\gamma_{xy}=(2\cdot185-200-50)\cdot10^{-6}=120\cdot10^{-6}.
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\varepsilon}&=125\cdot10^{-6},&
d_{\varepsilon}&=75\cdot10^{-6},\\
R_{\varepsilon}&=\sqrt{75^2+60^2}\cdot10^{-6}=15\sqrt{41}\cdot10^{-6},\\[6pt]
\varepsilon_1&=(125+15\sqrt{41})\cdot10^{-6},&
\varepsilon_2&=(125-15\sqrt{41})\cdot10^{-6},\\
\theta_1&=\frac12\arctan(\frac45)\simeq19.33^\circ.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The average of the readings at $0^\circ$ and $90^\circ$ is unchanged. Changing the reading at $45^\circ$ changes only the off-diagonal term of the strain tensor, which is half of $\gamma_{xy}$. In the final state both the sine and cosine of twice the principal angle are positive.

**Check.** The product of the principal strains, in microstrain squared, must equal the determinant of the tensor. The residual is $(125^2-15^2\cdot41)-(200\cdot50-60^2)=0$.

</div>
