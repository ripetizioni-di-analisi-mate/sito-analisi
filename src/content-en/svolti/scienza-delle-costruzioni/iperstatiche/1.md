## Exercise 1

A cantilever of length $4\,\mathrm m$ with $EI=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a downward force $P=24\,\mathrm{kN}$ at a distance $2\,\mathrm m$ from the fixed end. Use the principle of virtual work to find the tip deflection, neglecting shear deformation.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="C,0,-0.9,P" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Force P at midspan"}

A downward unit load at the tip gives $m=-(4-x)$ throughout the cantilever. With $x$ in meters, the actual bending moment is $M=-24(2-x)$ for $0\le x\le2$ and zero on the remaining segment.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_B&=\frac{24}{12\,000}\int_0^2(2-x)(4-x)\,\mathrm dx\\[5pt]
&=\frac{24}{12\,000}\left[8x-3x^2+\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^2\,\mathrm m\\[5pt]
&=\frac{1}{75}\,\mathrm m\simeq13.33\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The unit load points in the positive direction of deflection. The two moments have the same sign between the fixed end and the load, so their product is positive. Beyond the load, the beam remains straight because the actual moment vanishes. The tip continues downward along the tangent at midspan.

</div>

**Independent check**

Integrate $EI v''=24(2-x)$ on the first segment and $v''=0$ on the second to find the deflected shape. The conditions at $A$ give $v(2)=\dfrac{2}{375}\,\mathrm m$ and $v'(2)=\dfrac1{250}$. The residual, in meters, is

$$
\frac{2}{375}+2\frac1{250}-\frac1{75}=0.
$$

## Exercise 2

A cantilever of length $5\,\mathrm m$ with $EI=10\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a downward tip load $20\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the rotation of the section a distance $3\,\mathrm m$ from the fixed end, taking counterclockwise as positive.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0,-1.0,P" quote="A,C,3 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Tip load and section C located 3 m from the fixed end"}

A counterclockwise unit couple at $C$ produces $m=1$ between $A$ and $C$ and $m=0$ between $C$ and $B$. The actual moment is $M=-20(5-x)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\varphi_C
&=-\frac{20}{10\,000}\int_0^3(5-x)\,\mathrm dx\\[5pt]
&=-\frac{20}{10\,000}\left(15-\frac92\right)\,\mathrm{rad}\\[5pt]
&=-0.021\,\mathrm{rad}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The unit couple defines counterclockwise rotation as positive. The actual bending moment is negative wherever the unit-load bending moment is nonzero. The result is negative, indicating clockwise rotation.

</div>

**Independent check**

Integrate the curvature of the downward deflection curve by finding the area of the trapezoid between $A$ and $C$. Before division by $EI$, the two ordinates are $100\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Since counterclockwise rotation is $-v'$, the residual is

$$
-0.021+\frac{100+40}{2\cdot10\,000}\cdot3=0.
$$
