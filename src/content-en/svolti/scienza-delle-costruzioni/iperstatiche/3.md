## Exercise 1

A beam of length $4\,\mathrm m$ is fixed at $A$ and supported vertically at $B$. Its flexural rigidity is constant, and it carries a uniform downward load $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find the vertical reactions and the moment reaction at the fixed end.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.65,0.65,q" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Propped cantilever under a uniform load"}

Remove the prop and take $R_B$ as positive upward. For the primary structure, $\delta_0=-\dfrac{qL^4}{8EI}$ and $f_{11}=\dfrac{L^3}{3EI}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R_B &=\frac{3qL}{8}=\frac{3\cdot12\cdot4}{8}\,\mathrm{kN}=18\,\mathrm{kN},\\[5pt]
V_A &=48\,\mathrm{kN}-18\,\mathrm{kN}=30\,\mathrm{kN},\\[5pt]
M_A &=\left(48\cdot2-18\cdot4\right)\mathrm{kN\,m}=24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The load deflects the free end downward, while the upward reaction raises it. Compatibility requires the two contributions to cancel. Vertical force equilibrium then gives the vertical reaction at the fixed end. Taking moments about $A$ gives the counterclockwise reaction couple there.

</div>

**Independent check**

Take moments about $B$. The reaction at $B$ has zero moment arm, and the resultant of the uniform load acts at midspan. The residual, in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$, is

$$
24-30\cdot4+48\cdot2=0.
$$

## Exercise 2

A propped cantilever of length $4\,\mathrm m$ and constant flexural rigidity carries a downward load $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ at a distance $1\,\mathrm m$ from the fixed end. Find the reaction of the stationary prop and the reactions at $A$.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,1,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-0.9,P" quote="A,C,1 m;C,B,3 m" titolo="Point load near the fixed end"}

In the primary structure, an upward unit force at the tip gives $m=4-x$ and $M_0=-24(1-x)$ for $0\le x\le1$. For $x>1$, we have $M_0=0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
EI\,\delta_0&=-24\int_0^1(1-x)(4-x)\,\mathrm dx=-44\,\mathrm{kN\,m^3},\\[5pt]
EI\,f_{11}&=\int_0^4(4-x)^2\,\mathrm dx=\frac{64}{3}\,\mathrm{m^3},\\[5pt]
R_B&=\frac{44}{\frac{64}{3}}\,\mathrm{kN}=\frac{33}{16}\,\mathrm{kN}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
V_A=\frac{351}{16}\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad
M_A=\left(24-4\frac{33}{16}\right)\mathrm{kN\,m}
=\frac{63}{4}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The actual force is near the fixed end, and its bending moment diagram extends only over the first meter. The unit-load moment diagram extends over the entire cantilever, so the flexibility integral covers a greater length. The prop reaction acts upward and the couple at $A$ is counterclockwise.

</div>

**Independent check**

Take moments about $B$. The point load has a moment arm of $3\,\mathrm m$ about that point. The residual, in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$, is

$$
\frac{63}{4}-4\frac{351}{16}+24\cdot3=0.
$$
