## Exercise 1

A continuous prismatic beam of total span $6\,\mathrm m$ is simply supported at its ends $A$ and $C$ and at midspan $B$. The beam carries a uniform downward load $8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Calculate the reaction at $B$.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;C,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.7,0.7,q" quote="A,B,3 m;B,C,3 m" titolo="Two equal spans under a uniform load"}

Remove the middle support, leaving a simply supported beam of span $L=6\,\mathrm m$. The actual bending moment is $M_0=\dfrac q2x(L-x)$. An upward unit force at midspan produces the unit-load moment $m=-\dfrac x2$ on the left half of the beam. By symmetry, double the integrals over the half-span.

$$
\begin{aligned}
EI\,\delta_0&=2\int_0^{\frac L2}\frac q2x(L-x)\left(-\frac x2\right)\mathrm dx
=-\frac{5qL^4}{384},\\[5pt]
EI\,f_{11}&=2\int_0^{\frac L2}\frac{x^2}{4}\,\mathrm dx=\frac{L^3}{48},\\[5pt]
R_B&=\frac{5qL}{8}=30\,\mathrm{kN}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the primary structure, the uniform load deflects the midpoint downward. The upward unit force produces a bending moment of the opposite sign, so the product gives a negative displacement. A positive reaction at the middle support is needed to cancel this displacement. By symmetry, the end reactions are equal and each is $9\,\mathrm{kN}$.

</div>

**Independent check**

Integrate the curvature on the first span using moment $M(x)=9x-4x^2$, with $x$ in meters. Condition $v(A)=0$ determines the displacement integration constant. Symmetry requires $v'(B)=0$, which gives $EI\,v'(A)=\dfrac92\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. The resulting deflection at $B$ is

$$
v(B)=\frac{1}{EI}\left(\frac92\cdot3-\frac32\cdot3^3+\frac{3^4}{3}\right)=0.
$$

## Exercise 2

A cantilever of length $4\,\mathrm m$ is propped at $B$. The flexural rigidity is $K$ over the first $2\,\mathrm m$ and $\dfrac K2$ over the last $2\,\mathrm m$. The uniform load acts downward with intensity $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find the reaction of the stationary prop.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.65,0.65,q" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Rigidity K along AC and half that value along CB"}

Let $w=4-x$ be the distance from the tip. Weight each product by the flexibility of the corresponding segment. With $w$ in meters,

$$
\begin{aligned}
Kf_{11}&=2\int_0^2w^2\,\mathrm dw+\int_2^4w^2\,\mathrm dw=24\,\mathrm{m^3},\\[5pt]
K\delta_0&=-6\left(2\int_0^2w^3\,\mathrm dw+\int_2^4w^3\,\mathrm dw\right)
=-408\,\mathrm{kN\,m^3},\\[5pt]
R_B&=\frac{408}{24}\,\mathrm{kN}=17\,\mathrm{kN}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The half nearest the tip is more flexible, so both products on that segment have twice the weight. The reaction is smaller than for the same structure with uniform flexural rigidity. Equilibrium at $A$ leaves $V_A=31\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M_A=28\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

</div>
