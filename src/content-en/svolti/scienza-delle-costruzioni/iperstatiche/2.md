## Exercise 1

A bar fixed at $A$ consists of two consecutive segments of lengths $2\,\mathrm m$ and $1.5\,\mathrm m$, with axial rigidities $12\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $18\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$, respectively. A tensile force of $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at tip $C$. Find the rightward tip displacement.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,3.5,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="C,0.65,0,P" quote="A,B,2 m;B,C,1.5 m" titolo="Segments AB and BC with different axial rigidities"}

A rightward unit force produces $n=1$ in both segments. The actual force produces $N=24\,\mathrm{kN}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
u_C&=\frac{24\cdot2}{12\,000}\,\mathrm m
+\frac{24\cdot1.5}{18\,000}\,\mathrm m\\[5pt]
&=4\,\mathrm{mm}+2\,\mathrm{mm}=6\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The joint transmits the same force to both segments because no external axial load acts at the joint. The stiffer segment elongates less per unit length. Since the two segments are in series, their elongations add.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A cantilever of length $3\,\mathrm m$ has $EA=15\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $EI=9\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. The tip loads are $25\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Find the projection of the tip displacement onto the unit vector whose components are $\dfrac35$ to the right and $\dfrac45$ downward.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0.65,0,H;B,0,-0.65,P" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Horizontal component H and vertical component P"}

The rightward and downward components of the unit load are $\dfrac35$ and $\dfrac45$. Hence $n=\dfrac35$ and $m=-\dfrac45(3-x)$, while $N=25\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M=-8(3-x)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\delta&=\frac{25}{15\,000}\frac35\cdot3\,\mathrm m
+\frac{8}{9\,000}\frac45\int_0^3(3-x)^2\,\mathrm dx\,\mathrm m\\[5pt]
&=0.003\,\mathrm m+0.0064\,\mathrm m=9.4\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The inclined virtual force produces both axial force and bending. The first integral gives the contribution of axial elongation to the projected displacement. The second gives the contribution of vertical deflection. Both contributions are positive in the directions chosen.

</div>

**Independent check**

The beam displacement formulas give $u_B=\dfrac{25\cdot3}{15\,000}\,\mathrm m=5\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $v_B=\dfrac{8\cdot3^3}{3\cdot9\,000}\,\mathrm m=8\,\mathrm{mm}$. The residual in the projected displacement, in millimeters, is

$$
\frac35\cdot5+\frac45\cdot8-9.4=0.
$$
