## Exercise 1

A beam of span $4\,\mathrm m$ is fixed at $A$ and supported at $B$. It carries a uniform downward load $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and has $EI=16\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Calculate the vertical reaction at $B$ with the support stationary and again when $B$ settles by $1\,\mathrm{mm}$.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.65,0.65,q" quote="A,B,4 m" frecce="4.55,0.12,4.55,-0.28,s" titolo="Settlement of prop B"}

Taking deflection as positive downward, the total tip displacement of the released cantilever must equal the settlement $s$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{qL^4}{8EI}-\frac{R_B L^3}{3EI}&=s,\\[5pt]
R_B&=\frac{3qL}{8}-\frac{3EI}{L^3}s,\\[5pt]
R_B(0)&=18\,\mathrm{kN},\\[5pt]
R_B(0.001\,\mathrm m)&=17.25\,\mathrm{kN}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The load deflects the free end downward. The upward reaction reduces that deflection. If the support settles, a smaller reduction in deflection is required, so the reaction decreases. Because the reaction still acts upward, the beam can remain in contact with a unilateral support.

</div>

**Independent check**

With support settlement, vertical force equilibrium and moment equilibrium about $A$ give $V_A=30.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M_A=27\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The residual in the moment balance about $B$ is

$$
27-30.75\cdot4+48\cdot2=0
$$

in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

## Exercise 2

A continuous beam on three supports has two spans of length $3\,\mathrm m$, with $EI=9\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ and no applied load. The downward settlements at $A$, $B$, and $C$ are $2$, $5$, and $4\,\mathrm{mm}$, respectively. The supports can exert reactions in either direction. Calculate the vertical reaction at $B$.
::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;C,carrello" quote="A,B,3 m;B,C,3 m" frecce="0.42,0.1,0.42,-0.23,s_A;3.42,0.1,3.42,-0.34,s_B;6.42,0.1,6.42,-0.3,s_C" titolo="Prescribed settlements at the three supports"}

In the primary structure supported at the ends, the chord moves down by $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ at midspan. Point $B$ must therefore move down a further $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ relative to the chord. The vertical flexibility at midspan is $\dfrac{L^3}{48EI}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{rel}}&=-0.002\,\mathrm m,\\[5pt]
R_B&=\frac{-0.002\cdot48\cdot9\,000}{6^3}\,\mathrm{kN}
=-4\,\mathrm{kN}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The difference in the end settlements rotates the chord without bending the simply supported beam. Only the additional displacement of $B$ relative to that chord contributes. The reaction at $B$ must pull downward to impose the additional deflection. The bilateral restraint can supply this reaction.

</div>

**Independent check**

For the beam of span $6\,\mathrm m$ with only the downward central force, equilibrium gives an end reaction of $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ at each support. Taking moments about $C$ gives the residual

$$
-2\cdot6+4\cdot3=0
$$

in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
