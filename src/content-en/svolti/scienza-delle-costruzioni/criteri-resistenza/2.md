## Exercise 1

The principal stresses in descending order are $\sigma_1=120\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\sigma_3=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Calculate the von Mises equivalent stress.
::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_1;0,-1.03,0,-1.8,;0,1.03,0,1.8,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = 30 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}&=\sqrt{\frac{(\sigma_1-\sigma_2)^2+(\sigma_2-\sigma_3)^2+(\sigma_3-\sigma_1)^2}{2}} \\[6pt]
&=\sqrt{\frac{90^2+0^2+\left(-90\right)^2}{2}} \\[6pt]
&=90\,\mathrm{MPa}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each principal stress difference is squared. Since von Mises yielding depends on the deviatoric part of the stress, adding the same stress in all three directions leaves the result unchanged.

The check uses the traces of the stress tensor and its square. The invariant formula gives the same squared equivalent stress.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta&=\frac{3\operatorname{tr}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}^2)-(\operatorname{tr}\boldsymbol{\sigma})^2}{2}-\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}^2 \\[6pt]
&=\frac{3\cdot16200-180^2}{2}-8100=0\,\mathrm{MPa^2}
\end{aligned}
$$

</div>

## Exercise 2

The principal stresses in descending order are $\sigma_1=115\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=25\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\sigma_3=-35\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Calculate the von Mises equivalent stress.
::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_1;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = 25 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}&=\sqrt{\frac{(\sigma_1-\sigma_2)^2+(\sigma_2-\sigma_3)^2+(\sigma_3-\sigma_1)^2}{2}} \\[6pt]
&=\sqrt{\frac{90^2+60^2+\left(-150\right)^2}{2}} \\[6pt]
&=30 \sqrt{19}\,\mathrm{MPa}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each principal stress difference is squared. Since von Mises yielding depends on the deviatoric part of the stress, adding the same stress in all three directions leaves the result unchanged.

The check uses the traces of the stress tensor and its square. The invariant formula gives the same squared equivalent stress.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta&=\frac{3\operatorname{tr}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}^2)-(\operatorname{tr}\boldsymbol{\sigma})^2}{2}-\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}^2 \\[6pt]
&=\frac{3\cdot15075-105^2}{2}-17100=0\,\mathrm{MPa^2}
\end{aligned}
$$

</div>
