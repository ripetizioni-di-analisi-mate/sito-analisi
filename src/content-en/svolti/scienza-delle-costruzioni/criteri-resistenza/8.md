## Exercise 1

The element is in plane stress with $\sigma_x=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=0\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The uniaxial yield strength is $f_y=250\,\mathrm{MPa}$. All stresses are scaled by one load multiplier. Calculate the factor of safety using the von Mises criterion.
::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_x;1.18,-0.55,1.18,0.55,τ_xy;-1.18,0.55,-1.18,-0.55,;-0.55,1.18,0.55,1.18,;0.55,-1.18,-0.55,-1.18," titolo="Stress components in the xy plane"}

The center of the in-plane circle is $m$ and its radius is $R$. Its two horizontal intercepts are the in-plane principal stresses. Include zero as the out-of-plane principal stress.

$$
\begin{aligned}
m&=\frac{60+0}{2}=30\,\mathrm{MPa} \\[6pt]
R&=\sqrt{\left(\frac{60-0}{2}\right)^2+40^2}=50\,\mathrm{MPa} \\[6pt]
(\sigma_1,\sigma_2,\sigma_3)&=\left(80,0,-20\right)\,\mathrm{MPa}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}&=\sqrt{\frac{(\sigma_1-\sigma_2)^2+(\sigma_2-\sigma_3)^2+(\sigma_3-\sigma_1)^2}{2}} \\[6pt]
&=\sqrt{\frac{80^2+20^2+\left(-100\right)^2}{2}} \\[6pt]
&=20 \sqrt{21}\,\mathrm{MPa}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\eta_V&=\frac{\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}}{f_y}=\frac{20 \sqrt{21}}{250}=\frac{2 \sqrt{21}}{25}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
n&=\frac{1}{\eta}=\frac{1}{\frac{2 \sqrt{21}}{25}}=\frac{25 \sqrt{21}}{42}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Shear contributes even when one normal stress is zero. The equivalent stress measures the magnitude of the combined state. The yield strength divided by the equivalent stress gives the multiplier at which yielding begins.

Apply the multiplier to the stresses and compare the scaled equivalent stress with the uniaxial strength.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}(n\sigma)-f_y&=250-250=0\,\mathrm{MPa}
\end{aligned}
$$

</div>

## Exercise 2

The element is in plane stress with $\sigma_x=72\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=-24\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=36\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The uniaxial yield strength is $f_y=240\,\mathrm{MPa}$. All stresses are scaled by one load multiplier. Calculate the factor of safety using the von Mises criterion.
::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_x;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_y;1.18,-0.55,1.18,0.55,τ_xy;-1.18,0.55,-1.18,-0.55,;-0.55,1.18,0.55,1.18,;0.55,-1.18,-0.55,-1.18," titolo="Stress components in the xy plane"}

The center of the in-plane circle is $m$ and its radius is $R$. Its two horizontal intercepts are the in-plane principal stresses. Include zero as the out-of-plane principal stress.

$$
\begin{aligned}
m&=\frac{72+\left(-24\right)}{2}=24\,\mathrm{MPa} \\[6pt]
R&=\sqrt{\left(\frac{72-\left(-24\right)}{2}\right)^2+36^2}=60\,\mathrm{MPa} \\[6pt]
(\sigma_1,\sigma_2,\sigma_3)&=\left(84,0,-36\right)\,\mathrm{MPa}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}&=\sqrt{\frac{(\sigma_1-\sigma_2)^2+(\sigma_2-\sigma_3)^2+(\sigma_3-\sigma_1)^2}{2}} \\[6pt]
&=\sqrt{\frac{84^2+36^2+\left(-120\right)^2}{2}} \\[6pt]
&=12 \sqrt{79}\,\mathrm{MPa}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\eta_V&=\frac{\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}}{f_y}=\frac{12 \sqrt{79}}{240}=\frac{\sqrt{79}}{20}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
n&=\frac{1}{\eta}=\frac{1}{\frac{\sqrt{79}}{20}}=\frac{20 \sqrt{79}}{79}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Shear contributes even when one normal stress is zero. The equivalent stress measures the magnitude of the combined state. The yield strength divided by the equivalent stress gives the multiplier at which yielding begins.

Apply the multiplier to the stresses and compare the scaled equivalent stress with the uniaxial strength.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}(n\sigma)-f_y&=240-240=0\,\mathrm{MPa}
\end{aligned}
$$

</div>
