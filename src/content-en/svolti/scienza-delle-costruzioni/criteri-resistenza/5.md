## Exercise 1

The principal stresses in descending order are $\sigma_1=24\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=0\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\sigma_3=-48\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The tensile strength is $f_t=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the magnitude of the compressive strength is $f_c=120\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Calculate the Rankine utilization ratio.
::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_1;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = 0 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\eta_R&=\max\left(\frac{\sigma_1}{f_t},-\frac{\sigma_3}{f_c},0\right) \\[6pt]
&=\max\left(\frac{4}{5},\frac{2}{5},0\right)=\frac{4}{5}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Rankine compares the largest tensile and compressive stresses separately with their respective strengths. The larger ratio governs. The utilization ratio is dimensionless because it compares stresses expressed in the same unit.

Divide both ratios by the utilization ratio obtained. The ratio for the governing mode should become one.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max\left(1,\frac{1}{2},0\right)-1&=0
\end{aligned}
$$

</div>

## Exercise 2

The principal stresses in descending order are $\sigma_1=12\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=-15\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\sigma_3=-72\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The tensile strength is $f_t=35\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the magnitude of the compressive strength is $f_c=90\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Calculate the Rankine utilization ratio.
::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_1;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = -15 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\eta_R&=\max\left(\frac{\sigma_1}{f_t},-\frac{\sigma_3}{f_c},0\right) \\[6pt]
&=\max\left(\frac{12}{35},\frac{4}{5},0\right)=\frac{4}{5}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Rankine compares the largest tensile and compressive stresses separately with their respective strengths. The larger ratio governs. The utilization ratio is dimensionless because it compares stresses expressed in the same unit.

Divide both ratios by the utilization ratio obtained. The ratio for the governing mode should become one.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max\left(\frac{3}{7},1,0\right)-1&=0
\end{aligned}
$$

</div>
