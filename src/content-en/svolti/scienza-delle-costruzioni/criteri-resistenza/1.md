## Exercise 1

The principal stresses, in descending order, are $\sigma_1=90\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\sigma_3=-30\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the Tresca equivalent stress.
::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_1;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = 20 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,T}}&=\sigma_1-\sigma_3 \\[6pt]
&=90-\left(-30\right)=120\,\mathrm{MPa}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two extreme principal stresses determine the diameter of the largest Mohr circle. Its radius is the maximum shear stress. The equivalent stress is twice this value because the reference test is uniaxial tension.

As a check, resolve the traction on the plane at forty-five degrees between the extreme principal directions. Subtract the square of its normal component from the squared magnitude of the traction.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\left\|\boldsymbol t\right\|^2-\sigma_n^2&=\frac{90^2+\left(-30\right)^2}{2}-30^2 \\[6pt]
4\left(\left\|\boldsymbol t\right\|^2-\sigma_n^2\right)-\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,T}}^2&=4\cdot3600-14400=0\,\mathrm{MPa^2}
\end{aligned}
$$

</div>

## Exercise 2

The principal stresses, in descending order, are $\sigma_1=-35\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=-80\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\sigma_3=-145\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the Tresca equivalent stress.
::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.8,0,-1.03,0,;1.8,0,1.03,0,σ_1;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = -80 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,T}}&=\sigma_1-\sigma_3 \\[6pt]
&=-35-\left(-145\right)=110\,\mathrm{MPa}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two extreme principal stresses determine the diameter of the largest Mohr circle. Its radius is the maximum shear stress. The equivalent stress is twice this value because the reference test is uniaxial tension.

As a check, resolve the traction on the plane at forty-five degrees between the extreme principal directions. Subtract the square of its normal component from the squared magnitude of the traction.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\left\|\boldsymbol t\right\|^2-\sigma_n^2&=\frac{\left(-35\right)^2+\left(-145\right)^2}{2}-\left(-90\right)^2 \\[6pt]
4\left(\left\|\boldsymbol t\right\|^2-\sigma_n^2\right)-\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,T}}^2&=4\cdot3025-12100=0\,\mathrm{MPa^2}
\end{aligned}
$$

</div>
