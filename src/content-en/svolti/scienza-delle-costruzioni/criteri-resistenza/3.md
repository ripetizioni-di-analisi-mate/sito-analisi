## Exercise 1

The element is in plane stress with $\sigma_x=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$. First find the principal stresses, then calculate the von Mises equivalent stress.
::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_x;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_y;1.18,-0.55,1.18,0.55,τ_xy;-1.18,0.55,-1.18,-0.55,;-0.55,1.18,0.55,1.18,;0.55,-1.18,-0.55,-1.18," titolo="Stress components in the xy plane"}

The center of the in-plane circle is $m$ and its radius is $R$. Its two horizontal intercepts are the in-plane principal stresses. Include zero as the out-of-plane principal stress.

$$
\begin{aligned}
m&=\frac{40+\left(-10\right)}{2}=15\,\mathrm{MPa} \\[6pt]
R&=\sqrt{\left(\frac{40-\left(-10\right)}{2}\right)^2+60^2}=65\,\mathrm{MPa} \\[6pt]
(\sigma_1,\sigma_2,\sigma_3)&=\left(80,0,-50\right)\,\mathrm{MPa}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}&=\sqrt{\frac{(\sigma_1-\sigma_2)^2+(\sigma_2-\sigma_3)^2+(\sigma_3-\sigma_1)^2}{2}} \\[6pt]
&=\sqrt{\frac{80^2+50^2+\left(-130\right)^2}{2}} \\[6pt]
&=10 \sqrt{129}\,\mathrm{MPa}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The out-of-plane principal stress is zero. Order it together with the two in-plane values. All three principal stress differences then enter the von Mises calculation.

The check uses the traces of the stress tensor and its square. The invariant formula gives the same squared equivalent stress.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta&=\frac{3\operatorname{tr}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}^2)-(\operatorname{tr}\boldsymbol{\sigma})^2}{2}-\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}^2 \\[6pt]
&=\frac{3\cdot8900-30^2}{2}-12900=0\,\mathrm{MPa^2}
\end{aligned}
$$

</div>

## Exercise 2

The element is in plane stress with $\sigma_x=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=-15\,\mathrm{MPa}$. First find the principal stresses, then calculate the von Mises equivalent stress.
::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_x;0,-1.03,0,-1.8,;0,1.03,0,1.8,σ_y;1.18,0.55,1.18,-0.55,τ_xy;-1.18,-0.55,-1.18,0.55,;0.55,1.18,-0.55,1.18,;-0.55,-1.18,0.55,-1.18," titolo="Stress components in the xy plane"}

The center of the in-plane circle is $m$ and its radius is $R$. Its two horizontal intercepts are the in-plane principal stresses. Include zero as the out-of-plane principal stress.

$$
\begin{aligned}
m&=\frac{100+60}{2}=80\,\mathrm{MPa} \\[6pt]
R&=\sqrt{\left(\frac{100-60}{2}\right)^2+\left(-15\right)^2}=25\,\mathrm{MPa} \\[6pt]
(\sigma_1,\sigma_2,\sigma_3)&=\left(105,55,0\right)\,\mathrm{MPa}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}&=\sqrt{\frac{(\sigma_1-\sigma_2)^2+(\sigma_2-\sigma_3)^2+(\sigma_3-\sigma_1)^2}{2}} \\[6pt]
&=\sqrt{\frac{50^2+55^2+\left(-105\right)^2}{2}} \\[6pt]
&=5 \sqrt{331}\,\mathrm{MPa}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The out-of-plane principal stress is zero. Order it together with the two in-plane values. All three principal stress differences then enter the von Mises calculation.

The check uses the traces of the stress tensor and its square. The invariant formula gives the same squared equivalent stress.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta&=\frac{3\operatorname{tr}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}^2)-(\operatorname{tr}\boldsymbol{\sigma})^2}{2}-\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}^2 \\[6pt]
&=\frac{3\cdot14050-160^2}{2}-8275=0\,\mathrm{MPa^2}
\end{aligned}
$$

</div>
