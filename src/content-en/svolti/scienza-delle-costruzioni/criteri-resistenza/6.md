## Exercise 1

The principal stresses in descending order are $\sigma_1=16\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=2\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\sigma_3=-36\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The tensile strength is $f_t=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the magnitude of the compressive strength is $f_c=120\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Calculate the Coulomb–Mohr utilization ratio.
::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_1;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = 2 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\eta_{CM}&=\frac{\sigma_1}{f_t}-\frac{\sigma_3}{f_c} \\[6pt]
&=\frac{16}{40}+\frac{36}{120}=\frac{7}{10}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The extreme principal stresses determine which branch of the criterion applies. If the extreme stresses have opposite signs, the tensile and compressive contributions are both positive. For a branch on which all stresses have the same sign, the corresponding extreme stress governs.

Scale the stress state by the reciprocal of the calculated ratio. Use the new extreme stresses to obtain the contributions at failure.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\max(n\sigma_1,0)}{f_t}+\frac{\max(-n\sigma_3,0)}{f_c}-1&=\frac{4}{7}+\frac{3}{7}-1=0
\end{aligned}
$$

</div>

## Exercise 2

The principal stresses in descending order are $\sigma_1=15\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=-30\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\sigma_3=-75\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The tensile strength is $f_t=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the magnitude of the compressive strength is $f_c=150\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Calculate the Coulomb–Mohr utilization ratio.
::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_1;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = -30 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\eta_{CM}&=\frac{\sigma_1}{f_t}-\frac{\sigma_3}{f_c} \\[6pt]
&=\frac{15}{30}+\frac{75}{150}=1
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The extreme principal stresses determine which branch of the criterion applies. If the extreme stresses have opposite signs, the tensile and compressive contributions are both positive. For a branch on which all stresses have the same sign, the corresponding extreme stress governs.

Scale the stress state by the reciprocal of the calculated ratio. Use the new extreme stresses to obtain the contributions at failure.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\max(n\sigma_1,0)}{f_t}+\frac{\max(-n\sigma_3,0)}{f_c}-1&=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{2}-1=0
\end{aligned}
$$

</div>
