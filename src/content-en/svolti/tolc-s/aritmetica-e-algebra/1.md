## Exercise 1

Calculate $\left(\dfrac56-\dfrac14\right)\div\dfrac78$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac56-\frac14&=\frac{10-3}{12}=\frac7{12},\\[6pt]
\frac7{12}\div\frac78&=\frac7{12}\cdot\frac87=\frac23.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The subtraction requires parts of the same size, so the common denominator $12$ is used. The divisor $\dfrac78$ is nonzero, allowing division to be replaced by multiplication by its reciprocal. The common factors $7$ cancel, and $\dfrac8{12}$ reduces to $\dfrac23$. The quotient exceeds $\dfrac7{12}$ because the divisor is positive and less than one. This comparison also checks the direction of the change produced by division.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A mixture contains concentrate and water in the ratio $2$ to $5$. Find the liters of each component in $21\,\mathrm L$ of mixture, assuming the volumes add.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2k+5k&=21,\\
k&=3,\\
V_c&=2\cdot3=6\,\mathrm L,\\
V_a&=5\cdot3=15\,\mathrm L.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The ratio compares the volume of concentrate with that of water; the total consists of seven equal parts. The letter $k$ denotes the volume of one part, measured in liters. Dividing the total by seven gives $k=3$. The components occupy two and five parts, respectively, giving $6$ and $15$ liters. Their sum is $21$ liters, and the ratio $\dfrac6{15}=\dfrac25$ matches the required ratio. The fraction of concentrate in the total is instead $\dfrac27$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A price of $120$ euros is discounted by $25\%$ and then increased by $10\%$. Calculate the final price and the overall percentage change.

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_1&=120\cdot0.75=90,\\
P_2&=90\cdot1.10=99,\\
\frac{P_2-120}{120}\cdot100\%&=-17.5\%.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The discount leaves $75\%$ of the initial price, so the price is multiplied by $0.75$. The subsequent increase applies to the discounted $90$ euros and adds $9$ euros, bringing the price to $99$ euros. The absolute decrease is $21$ euros; dividing by the initial $120$ euros gives a decrease of $17.5\%$. The overall factor $0.75\cdot1.10=0.825$ confirms that $82.5\%$ of the original price remains.

</div>
