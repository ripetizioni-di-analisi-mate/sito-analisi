## Exercise 1

Which figure is a rotated letter R rather than its mirror image?

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="lettera:R r90 | lettera:R specchio | lettera:R specchio r90 | lettera:R specchio r180 | lettera:R specchio r-90"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

| figure | rotation that returns the stem to vertical | the bowl of the R is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | rotation through $90°$ counterclockwise | on the right |
| B | no rotation | on the left |
| C | rotation through $90°$ counterclockwise | on the left |
| D | rotation through $180°$ | on the left |
| E | rotation through $90°$ clockwise | on the left |

<div class="spiegazione">

The letter R has its vertical stem on the left and its curved part, the bowl, on the upper right of the stem. For each figure, rotate the stem back to vertical and check which side the bowl is on. Rotating figure A $90°$ counterclockwise makes the stem vertical and puts the bowl at the upper right, so A is the rotated R. In the other four figures the bowl is on the left after rotating the stem back to vertical. They are therefore **mirror images**, and by the result on rotated and reflected figures no rotation can make them coincide with the R. Figure D can be mistaken for an R rotated through $180°$ because its bowl is at the bottom. However, an R rotated through $180°$ has its stem on the right, while D has its stem on the left. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Four figures show the same letter in different orientations, and one shows its mirror image. Which figure is the mirror image?

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="lettera:G r90 | lettera:G r180 | lettera:G r-90 | lettera:G specchio r90 | lettera:G"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

| figure | rotation that returns the letter to upright | result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | $90°$ counterclockwise | G |
| B | $180°$ | G |
| C | $90°$ clockwise | G |
| D | $90°$ counterclockwise | mirror image of G |
| E | none | G |

<div class="spiegazione">

Figure E is an upright G and serves as a reference. The G opens to the right, and its inner horizontal stroke extends from the lower end of the opening toward the center. Rotating A by $90°$ counterclockwise, B by $180°$, and C by $90°$ clockwise makes each coincide with E, so they are the same letter **rotated**. Rotating D by $90°$ counterclockwise puts its opening on the left, so D is a mirror image of the G. Figures A and D have the same general orientation. To distinguish them, check which side the opening is on relative to the inner stroke. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The figure shows the design cut into a rubber stamp as seen by someone looking directly at the rubber. The stamp is pressed onto a sheet of paper. Which of figures A–E could be the imprint left on the paper?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="lettera:F; punto se" titolo="The design on the rubber stamp"}

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="lettera:F; punto se | lettera:F specchio; punto so | lettera:F r180; punto no | lettera:F r90; punto so | lettera:F specchio; punto se"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

| figure | transformation relative to the design on the rubber |
| --- | --- |
| A | none |
| B | reflection across the vertical axis |
| C | rotation through $180°$ |
| D | rotation through $90°$ clockwise |
| E | the letter is reflected but the dot is not |

<div class="spiegazione">

When the stamp touches the paper, the part that was on the right when looking at the rubber appears on the left when looking at the paper. Thus the imprint is a **mirror image** of the design, possibly rotated depending on how the stamp is held. Figure B is the design reflected across the vertical axis: the strokes of the F extend to the left, and the dot moves from the bottom right corner to the bottom left. Figures A, C, and D show the original design or a rotation of it, so they are not mirror images. In figure E the F is reflected but the dot remains in its original position. This result cannot be obtained by rotating or reflecting the design. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
