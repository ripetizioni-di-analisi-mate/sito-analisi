## Exercise 1

An illuminated sign displays one figure at a time according to a fixed rule. Which figure appears in place of the question mark?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="squadra | squadra grigio r90 | squadra pieno r180 | squadra r-90 | ?" titolo="The first four figures on the sign"}

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="squadra pieno | squadra grigio r90 | squadra | squadra grigio r180 | squadra grigio"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

| figure | orientation | fill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | $0°$ | unfilled |
| 2 | $90°$ clockwise | gray |
| 3 | $180°$ | solid |
| 4 | $270°$ clockwise | unfilled |
| 5 | $360°$, equivalent to $0°$ | gray |

<div class="spiegazione">

Two features change in this sequence, and each follows an **independent rule**. The right triangle rotates $90°$ clockwise at each step, so after four steps it returns to its original orientation. Its fill cycles through unfilled, gray, and solid and then starts over. The fifth figure therefore has the same fill as the second, gray. The missing figure is thus a gray right triangle in the same position as the first figure. Figure C has the correct orientation but the wrong fill, and figure B has the correct fill but repeats the orientation of the second figure. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Which figure continues the sequence?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="triangolo; punto no | quadrato grigio; punto ne | pentagono; punto se | ?"}

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="esagono; punto so | esagono grigio; punto so | esagono grigio; punto no | pentagono grigio; punto so | esagono grigio; punto se"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

| figure | sides of the polygon | fill | position of the dot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | $3$ | unfilled | top left |
| 2 | $4$ | gray | top right |
| 3 | $5$ | unfilled | bottom right |
| 4 | $6$ | gray | bottom left |

<div class="spiegazione">

There are three rules, one for each feature. The number of sides increases by one at each step, so a hexagon follows the pentagon. The fill alternates between unfilled and gray, so the fourth figure is gray like the second. The dot moves from each corner to the next one clockwise, so after the bottom right corner it occupies the bottom left corner. The figure meeting all three rules is a gray hexagon with the dot at the bottom left. Each other option violates just one rule: A has the wrong fill, C and E have the dot in the wrong position, and D has the wrong number of sides. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Which figure continues the sequence?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="punto no; cerchio s | punto n; cerchio e | punto ne; cerchio n | punto e; cerchio o | ?"}

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="punto se; cerchio n | punto e; cerchio s | punto se; cerchio s | punto s; cerchio se | punto se; cerchio so"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

| figure | solid dot | unfilled circle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | top left | bottom middle |
| 2 | top middle | middle right |
| 3 | top right | top middle |
| 4 | middle right | middle left |
| 5 | bottom right | bottom middle |

<div class="spiegazione">

The cell contains two figures, each moving around the eight positions on the edge according to its own rule. The solid dot advances one position clockwise at each step, so after the middle of the right side it occupies the bottom right corner. The unfilled circle advances two positions *counterclockwise*: from the bottom middle it moves to the middle of the right side, skipping the bottom right corner. From the middle of the left side the circle skips the bottom left corner and reaches the bottom middle. Thus, in the fifth figure the dot is at the bottom right and the circle at the bottom middle. Figure A moves the circle clockwise, figure B leaves the dot in place, and figure E moves the circle only one position. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
