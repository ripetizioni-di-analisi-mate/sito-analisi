## Exercise 1

The net shown is folded into a cube. Which face, among figures A–E, is opposite the face with the dot?

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema="xx..|.xx.|..xx" celle="punto | cerchio | croce pieno | quadrato grigio | anello | x" titolo="The net to be folded"}

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="cerchio | croce pieno | quadrato grigio | anello | x"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

| four faces in a Z pattern | first and last faces, which are opposite |
| --- | --- |
| dot, circle, solid cross, gray square | dot and gray square |
| circle, solid cross, gray square, ring | circle and ring |
| solid cross, gray square, ring, diagonal cross | solid cross and diagonal cross |

<div class="spiegazione">

The net forms a staircase, with two faces side by side, a step down, two more faces, and so on. The dot and circle are side by side. The solid cross is below the circle and is in line with the gray square after the step down. These four faces form a Z, so by the rule for **opposite faces**, the first and last are opposite. The dot is therefore opposite the gray square. Each face of a cube has exactly one opposite face, so each of the other four faces shares an edge with the dot's face. They include the solid cross, which touches the dot's face only at a vertex in the net. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A company makes dice with symbols arranged according to the net shown. Which of cubes A–E can be obtained by folding the net?

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema=".x..|xxxx|.x.." celle="anello | punto | croce pieno | quadrato | cerchio grigio | x" titolo="The net of the die"}

::figura{tipo="cubi" celle="anello / punto / croce pieno | anello / cerchio grigio / x | anello / croce pieno / punto | punto / quadrato / croce pieno | croce pieno / punto / anello"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

| pairs of opposite faces | cubes eliminated |
| --- | --- |
| ring and diagonal cross | B |
| dot and square | D |
| solid cross and gray circle | none |

| cube | visible faces in clockwise order around the vertex | order in the net |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | ring, solid cross, dot | same |
| C | ring, dot, solid cross | reversed |
| E | solid cross, ring, dot | reversed |

<div class="spiegazione">

In the Latin cross net the middle column contains the ring, solid cross, and diagonal cross, so the ring and diagonal cross are opposite. In the middle row the dot, solid cross, square, and gray circle lie in a straight line, so the dot is opposite the square and the solid cross is opposite the gray circle. Cube B shows the ring with the diagonal cross, and cube D shows the dot with the square, so both are eliminated. To check the remaining cubes, use the rule for **faces around a vertex**. In the net, the ring, solid cross, and dot meet at the top left corner of the solid cross. Their clockwise order is ring, solid cross, dot. In cube A, moving clockwise around the vertex nearest the viewer gives the ring on top, the solid cross on the right, and the dot in front, the same order. In C and E, reading the same three faces in the same way gives ring, dot, solid cross, which is the reverse order. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Which of the arrangements in figures A–E folds into a cube when the squares are folded along their common edges?

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema="xx..|xxxx" celle="- | - | - | - | - | -" titolo="Figure A"}

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema="xxxxx|..x.." celle="- | - | - | - | - | -" titolo="Figure B"}

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema="x.x.|xxxx" celle="- | - | - | - | - | -" titolo="Figure C"}

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema="xx..|xx..|.xx." celle="- | - | - | - | - | -" titolo="Figure D"}

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema="x...|xxx.|..xx" celle="- | - | - | - | - | -" titolo="Figure E"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

| net | feature | folds into a cube? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | $2 \times 2$ block and two faces on the same side of the row of four | no |
| B | five faces in a straight row | no |
| C | two faces on the same side of the row of four | no |
| D | $2 \times 2$ block | no |
| E | none of the three configurations | yes |

<div class="spiegazione">

Check the three **arrangements that do not fold into a cube**. A and D each contain a $2 \times 2$ block of four squares, which would force four faces to meet at a vertex of the folded cube. Only three faces meet at each vertex. B has five squares in a straight row, so the fifth face overlaps the first. In C the two squares outside the row of four are both above the row, so they end up on the same face of the cube. E contains none of the three configurations. When it is folded, each face of the cube is covered by a single square. The three squares in the second row become the left, front, and right walls. The top square becomes the top face, the square below the right wall becomes the bottom face, and the last square becomes the back wall. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
