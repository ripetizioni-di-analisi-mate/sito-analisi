## Exercise 1

In each row of the matrix, the third figure is obtained from the first two by the same rule. Which figure completes the matrix?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="linea medio n r90; linea medio o | linea medio o; linea medio e | linea medio n r90; linea medio o; linea medio e | linea medio s r90; linea r45 | linea medio s r90; linea medio e | linea medio s r90; linea r45; linea medio e | linea medio o; linea r-45 | linea medio n r90; linea r-45 | ?"}

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="linea medio o; linea medio n r90 | linea r-45 | linea medio o; linea medio n r90; linea r-45; linea medio e | linea medio o; linea medio n r90; linea r-45 | linea medio o; linea medio n r90; linea r45"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

| row | element common to the first two | present in the third figure? | operation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | left side | yes | union |
| 2 | bottom side | yes | union |
| 3 | diagonal | | union |

<div class="spiegazione">

In the first row the third figure contains all the segments from the first two. The left side appears in both and also in the third. The same happens with the bottom side in the second row. Therefore, the operation is **union**. Symmetric difference would remove the common elements. In the third row the first figure contains the left side and the diagonal, and the second contains the top side and the same diagonal. The missing figure therefore contains the left side, the top side, and the diagonal sloping down to the right. Figure A shows the symmetric difference, B contains only the common elements, and E has the diagonal running the other way. Figure C adds the right side, which does not appear in the third row. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A panel has nine lights in three rows and three columns. Dots indicate the lights that are on. In each row of the matrix the third configuration is obtained from the first two using the same rule. Which configuration completes the matrix?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="punto no; punto ne; punto piccolo c | punto piccolo c; punto so | punto no; punto ne; punto so | punto n; punto s | punto n; punto e; punto o | punto s; punto e; punto o | punto no; punto se; punto e | punto se; punto o | ?" titolo="The lights that are on in the eight known configurations"}

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="punto no; punto se; punto e; punto o | punto no; punto e; punto o | punto se | punto no; punto o | punto ne; punto e; punto o"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

| row | on in the first | on in the second | on in the third |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | top left, top right, center | center, bottom left | top left, top right, bottom left |
| 2 | top, bottom | top, right, left | bottom, right, left |
| 3 | top left, bottom right, right | bottom right, left | top left, right, left |

<div class="spiegazione">

In the first row the center light is on in the first two configurations and off in the third. The other lights, each on in only one of the first two, stay on. The same happens with the top light in the second row. The rule is therefore **symmetric difference**: a light is on in the third configuration if it is on in *exactly one* of the first two. In the third row the bottom right light is on in both the first and second configurations, so it goes out. The top left, right, and left lights stay on. Figure A is the union because it keeps the common light on, and figure C contains only the common light. Figure D omits the right light, and E moves the first light to the top right. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Which figure completes the matrix?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="linea medio n r90; linea medio o | linea medio e; linea medio s r90 | linea medio n r90; linea medio e; linea r45 | linea medio n r90; linea r45 | linea medio s r90; linea r-45 | linea medio e; linea medio s r90; linea r45 | linea medio o; linea r45 | linea medio e; linea r-45 | ?"}

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="linea medio n r90; linea medio s r90; linea medio e; linea r45 | linea medio n r90; linea medio s r90; linea medio e | linea medio o; linea medio e | linea medio n r90; linea medio s r90; linea r45 | linea medio n r90; linea medio s r90"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

| column | first figure | second figure | third figure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | top, left | top, diagonal $/$ | left, diagonal $/$ |
| 2 | right, bottom | bottom, diagonal $\backslash$ | right, diagonal $\backslash$ |
| 3 | top, right, diagonal $/$ | right, bottom, diagonal $/$ | ? |

<div class="spiegazione">

Neither operation works across the rows. In the first row both the union and the symmetric difference of the first two figures contain four sides, while the third figure contains two sides and a diagonal. The pattern runs down the columns. In the first column the top side appears in the first two figures and is absent from the third, and in the second column the same happens to the bottom side. The third figure of each column is therefore the **symmetric difference** of the first two. In the third column the right side and diagonal are common to the first two figures, so they cancel, leaving the top and bottom sides. Figure A is the union, and figures B and D eliminate only one of the two common elements. Figure C shows the correct sides rotated through $90°$. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
