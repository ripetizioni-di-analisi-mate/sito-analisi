## Exercise 1

An illuminated panel in a station displays the figures shown in sequence. Which figure appears in place of the question mark?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="freccia pieno | freccia pieno r45 | freccia pieno r90 | freccia pieno r135 | ?" titolo="The first four figures on the panel"}

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="freccia pieno r135 | freccia pieno r-135 | freccia pieno r180 | freccia pieno r90 | freccia pieno r-45"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

| feature | from cell 1 to cell 4 | fifth cell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| shape and fill | solid arrow throughout | solid arrow |
| orientation | $0°$, $45°$, $90°$, $135°$ clockwise | $180°$, pointing down |

<div class="spiegazione">

The shape and fill do not change, so the rule concerns only orientation. From one cell to the next the arrow undergoes a clockwise **rotation** through $45°$. By the fifth cell it has therefore turned through $135° + 45° = 180°$ from its first position and points down. Figure A repeats the fourth cell and figure D goes back one step. Figure B has rotated through $225°$, one step too far, and figure E is the first step of a counterclockwise rotation. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Which figure continues the sequence?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="punto medio no | punto medio ne | punto medio se | punto medio so | ?" titolo="The dot changes position in the cell"}

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="punto medio no | punto medio o | punto medio so | punto medio s | punto medio se"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

| cell | position of the dot | clockwise steps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | top left | |
| 2 | top right | $2$ |
| 3 | bottom right | $2$ |
| 4 | bottom left | $2$ |
| 5 | top left | $2$ |

<div class="spiegazione">

The dot moves around the eight positions on the edge of the cell: the corners and the midpoints of the sides. From the first cell to the second it moves from the top left corner to the top right corner, skipping the top middle position. It therefore advances two positions clockwise. The same **shift** takes it from the second cell to the third and from the third to the fourth. In the fifth cell it advances two more positions from the bottom left corner and returns to the top left corner. Figure B moves forward only one position, and figure C repeats the fourth cell. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The first four figures of a sequence are shown, and the sequence continues according to the same rule. Which figure is tenth in the sequence?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="squadra grigio | squadra grigio r90 | squadra grigio r180 | squadra grigio r-90 | ?" titolo="The first four figures in the sequence, with a question mark for the tenth"}

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="squadra grigio | squadra grigio r180 | squadra grigio r-90 | squadra grigio r90 | squadra pieno r90"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

$$
10 - 1 = 9 \ \text{steps},
\qquad
9 = 2 \cdot 4 + 1
$$

| steps beyond the full turns | orientation |
| --- | --- |
| $0$ | same as the first figure |
| $1$ | rotated $90°$ clockwise |
| $2$ | rotated through $180°$ |
| $3$ | rotated $90°$ counterclockwise |

<div class="spiegazione">

From one figure to the next the right triangle rotates $90°$ clockwise, so after four steps it returns to its original position. The rule has **period** $4$. The number of steps from the first figure to the tenth is $9$. Since $9 = 2 \cdot 4 + 1$, the triangle makes two full turns and one extra step, so the tenth figure coincides with the second. Figure B comes from dividing $10$ by $4$ instead of dividing $9$ by it and getting a remainder of $2$. Figure E has the correct orientation but a different fill, while the fill in the sequence stays gray. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
