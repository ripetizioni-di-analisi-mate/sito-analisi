## Exercise 1

Today is Tuesday. What day of the week will it be in $45$ days?

- A) Thursday
- B) Friday
- C) Saturday
- D) Wednesday
- E) Monday

$$
45 = 6 \cdot 7 + 3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Every $7$ days the weekday repeats, so only the remainder when $45$ is divided by $7$ matters. The remainder is $3$. By the **remainder rule for weekdays**, advance three places from Tuesday, through Wednesday and Thursday to Friday. Option A counts one place too few. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A reimbursement request was logged on a Monday, and the office must respond within $60$ days. What day of the week is the sixtieth day after the request was logged?

- A) Thursday
- B) Saturday
- C) Monday
- D) Wednesday
- E) Friday

$$
60 = 8 \cdot 7 + 4
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The sixtieth day after a Monday is found by advancing $60$ days. Since $56$ days make $8$ full weeks, there are $4$ days left to count. From Monday these are Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday. Option A uses a remainder of $3$, which results from counting the original Monday as day one. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

March 1, 2027 is a Monday. What day of the week is December 25, 2027?

- A) Friday
- B) Sunday
- C) Saturday
- D) Thursday
- E) Monday

$$
\begin{aligned}
n &= 30 + 30 + 31 + 30 + 31 + 31 + 30 + 31 + 30 + 25 \\[6pt]
&= 299 \\[6pt]
299 &= 42 \cdot 7 + 5
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Count the days between the two dates. After March 1, $30$ days remain in March. The full months from April through November have $30$, $31$, $30$, $31$, $31$, $30$, $31$ and $30$ days, respectively. Finally there are $25$ days in December. The total is $299$, which leaves a remainder of $5$ on division by $7$. The weekday advances five places from Monday to Saturday. The interval does not include February, so leap years do not affect the calculation. Option B uses a remainder of $6$ from counting all $31$ days of March, including March 1. Option A counts one day too few. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
