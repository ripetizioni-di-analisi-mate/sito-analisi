## Exercise 1

Roberto is the brother of Sofia's mother. Elena is Roberto's daughter. How is Elena related to Sofia's mother?

- A) Her cousin
- B) Her sister
- C) Her niece
- D) Her aunt
- E) Her daughter

$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
\text{Sofia’s mother} & \text{siblings} & \text{Roberto} \\
\downarrow & & \downarrow \\
\text{Sofia} & \text{first cousins} & \text{Elena}
\end{array}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Roberto is the brother of Sofia's mother, so they are on the same level of the family tree. Elena is Roberto's daughter and therefore the daughter of the brother of Sofia's mother. From Elena's point of view, Sofia's mother is her aunt. Hence Elena is the **niece** of Sofia's mother. Option A, cousin, describes the relationship between Elena and Sofia and answers the question from Sofia's perspective. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Luca is $12$ years old, and his father is $40$. In how many years will his father be twice Luca's age?

- A) $14$
- B) $16$
- C) $8$
- D) $28$
- E) $20$

$$
40 + x = 2\,(12 + x)
\iff
40 + x = 24 + 2x
\iff
x = 16
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In $x$ years, the father will be $40 + x$ years old and Luca will be $12 + x$. The required condition gives $40 + x = 2\,(12 + x)$, hence $x = 16$. As a check, in $16$ years their ages will be $56$ and $28$, and $56$ is twice $28$. The age difference stays at $28$ years, as in any problem **comparing ages**. Option D, $28$, is exactly that difference. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Giulia says, “Pietro's mother is my mother's only daughter.” How is Pietro related to Giulia?

- A) Her son
- B) Her nephew
- C) Her brother
- D) Her cousin
- E) Her uncle

$$
\begin{array}{c}
\text{Giulia’s mother} \\
\downarrow \\
\text{Giulia} = \text{only daughter} \\
\downarrow \\
\text{Pietro}
\end{array}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Giulia is a daughter of her mother. Since her mother has only one daughter, that daughter is Giulia herself. Thus the statement says that Giulia is Pietro's mother, so Pietro is Giulia's **son**. Option B, nephew, treats “my mother's only daughter” as Giulia's sister. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
