## Exercise 1

At 7:00 an employee sets the office clock to the correct time. It gains $2$ minutes per hour at a constant rate. What does the clock show at 19:00 that day?

- A) 19:24
- B) 19:12
- C) 18:36
- D) 19:22
- E) 19:48

$$
19 - 7 = 12 \ \text{hours},
\qquad
12 \cdot 2 = 24 \ \text{minutes}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

From 7:00 to 19:00, $12$ actual hours elapse. The clock gains $2$ minutes each actual hour, so it is $24$ minutes fast and reads 19:24. Option C subtracts the minutes, as if the clock were *losing* time, and option B counts $6$ hours. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A clock was set correctly at 8:00 and loses $5$ minutes per hour at a constant rate. What is the actual time when the clock reads 11:40?

- A) 11:55
- B) 11:58
- C) 11:20
- D) 12:00
- E) 12:05

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{minutes shown} &= 3 \cdot 60 + 40 = 220 \\[6pt]
\text{actual hours} &= \frac{220}{55} = 4
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The clock loses $5$ minutes per hour, so its hands advance $55$ minutes in each actual hour. From 8:00 to 11:40 the clock has recorded $220$ minutes. The number of actual hours elapsed is therefore $\dfrac{220}{55} = 4$, and the actual time is 12:00. Adding $5$ minutes for each of the $3$ full hours *shown* on the clock gives 11:55, option A. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

An analog clock loses $3$ minutes per day at a constant rate. Today at noon it shows the correct time. How many days will pass before it next shows the correct time?

- A) $480$
- B) $20$
- C) $240$
- D) $120$
- E) $360$

$$
12 \ \text{hours} = 720 \ \text{minutes},
\qquad
\frac{720}{3} = 240
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The hands of an analog clock repeat their positions every $12$ hours, so the clock next shows the **correct time** when the accumulated delay is $12$ hours, or $720$ minutes. At a loss of $3$ minutes a day, this takes $240$ days. Option A, $480$, applies to a $24$-hour digital clock. Option B gives the time needed to lose just one hour. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
