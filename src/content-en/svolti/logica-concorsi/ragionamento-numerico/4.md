## Exercise 1

A train leaves at 8:47 and arrives at 13:22 the same day. How long is the trip?

- A) $4$ h $35$ min
- B) $5$ h $15$ min
- C) $4$ h $25$ min
- D) $4$ h $45$ min
- E) $5$ h $35$ min

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{from 8:47 to 9:00} &= 13 \ \text{min} \\[6pt]
\text{from 9:00 to 13:00} &= 4 \ \text{h} \\[6pt]
\text{from 13:00 to 13:22} &= 22 \ \text{min}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To find the **elapsed time**, count up to the next full hour first. From 8:47 to 9:00 there are $13$ minutes, then $4$ hours to 13:00, and finally $22$ minutes. The trip therefore lasts $4$ hours and $13 + 22 = 35$ minutes. Option B treats the times as decimals, calculating $13.22 - 8.47 = 4.75$ and reading it as $4$ hours and $75$ minutes, or $5$ hours and $15$ minutes. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A night shift at a barracks begins at 21:50 and lasts $8$ hours and $25$ minutes. When does it end?

- A) 5:15
- B) 6:35
- C) 7:15
- D) 6:05
- E) 6:15

$$
\begin{aligned}
21{:}50 + 8 \ \text{h} &= 5{:}50 \\[6pt]
5{:}50 + 25 \ \text{min} &= 6{:}15
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Adding $8$ hours to 21:50 takes us past 24:00. Subtracting $24$ from the hours gives 5:50 the next day. Adding $25$ minutes gives $50 + 25 = 75$ minutes past the hour, or one hour and $15$ minutes, so the shift ends at 6:15. Option A, 5:15, subtracts $60$ from the $75$ minutes without adding the corresponding hour. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A flight leaves Rome at 23:40 Rome time and lasts $11$ hours and $35$ minutes. Suppose Tokyo is $7$ hours ahead of Rome. What is the local arrival time in Tokyo?

- A) 11:15
- B) 18:15
- C) 4:15
- D) 19:15
- E) 17:15

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{arrival, Rome time} &= 23{:}40 + 11 \ \text{h} \ 35 \ \text{min} = 11{:}15 \\[6pt]
\text{arrival, Tokyo time} &= 11{:}15 + 7 \ \text{h} = 18{:}15
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

First add the flight duration in the departure **time zone**. Adding $11$ hours to 23:40 gives 10:40 the next day. Another $35$ minutes brings the time to 11:15 in Rome. Tokyo is $7$ hours ahead, so the plane lands at 18:15 local time. Option A gives the arrival time on a clock in Rome. Option C subtracts the time difference instead of adding it. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
