## Exercise 1

What is the smaller of the two angles formed by the hands of a clock at 9:30?

- A) $90°$
- B) $105°$
- C) $120°$
- D) $75°$
- E) $255°$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{hour hand} &= 30 \cdot 9 + 0.5 \cdot 30 = 285° \\[6pt]
\text{minute hand} &= 6 \cdot 30 = 180° \\[6pt]
\alpha &= 285° - 180° = 105°
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

At 9:30 the minute hand points to $6$, which is $180°$ clockwise from $12$. The hour hand is half an hour past $9$, so its position is $270° + 15° = 285°$. The formula for the **angle between the hands** gives $|30 \cdot 9 - 5.5 \cdot 30| = |270 - 165| = 105°$. Option A, $90°$, leaves the hour hand at $9$, and option E gives the larger angle, $360° - 105°$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An office closes to the public at 14:20. What is the smaller of the two angles formed by the hands of its wall clock at that time?

- A) $60°$
- B) $70°$
- C) $40°$
- D) $50°$
- E) $310°$

$$
\alpha = \left| 30 \cdot 2 - 5.5 \cdot 20 \right| = \left| 60 - 110 \right| = 50°
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

On the dial, 14:00 corresponds to $2$ because $14 - 12 = 2$, so use $h = 2$ in the formula. The hour hand is at $60° + 20 \cdot 0.5° = 70°$ and the minute hand at $20 \cdot 6° = 120°$, making an angle of $50°$. Option A, $60°$, leaves the hour hand at $2$. Option E uses $h = 14$ and gives $|420 - 110| = 310°$, the larger angle between the hands, because $360° - 310° = 50°$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

At 15:00 the hands of a clock form a right angle. How many minutes will it take for them to coincide for the first time after 15:00?

- A) $16 + \dfrac{4}{11}$ minutes
- B) $15$ minutes
- C) $16$ minutes
- D) $17 + \dfrac{3}{11}$ minutes
- E) $18$ minutes

$$
5.5 \cdot m = 90
\iff
m = \frac{90}{5.5} = \frac{180}{11} = 16 + \frac{4}{11}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

At 15:00 the hour hand is at $90°$ and the minute hand is at $0°$. The minute hand gains $6° - 0.5° = 5.5°$ per minute on the hour hand, so it catches up when it closes the initial gap of $90°$. This takes $\dfrac{180}{11}$ minutes, or $16$ minutes and $\dfrac{4}{11}$ of a minute, approximately $16$ minutes and $22$ seconds. Option B, $15$ minutes, treats the hour hand as stationary, although in $15$ minutes it moves $7.5°$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
