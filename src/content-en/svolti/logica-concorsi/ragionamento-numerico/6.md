## Exercise 1

People are waiting in line at a service counter. Carlo is seventh from the front and twelfth from the back. How many people are in line?

- A) $18$
- B) $19$
- C) $17$
- D) $5$
- E) $20$

$$
n = 7 + 12 - 1 = 18
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Adding the two positions counts everyone in the line, with Carlo counted among both the first seven and the last twelve. The formula for **positions in a line** therefore subtracts $1$, giving $18$ people. Option B, $19$, is the sum $7 + 12$, and option D is the difference $12 - 7$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Andrea is taller than Bianca. Carla is shorter than Bianca. Davide is taller than Andrea. Carla is shorter than Elisa. Elisa is shorter than Bianca. No two people have the same height. Who is third tallest?

- A) Andrea
- B) Davide
- C) Carla
- D) Elisa
- E) Bianca

$$
\text{Davide} > \text{Andrea} > \text{Bianca} > \text{Elisa} > \text{Carla}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Use the **transitive property** to put the names in order from tallest to shortest. Davide is taller than Andrea, who is taller than Bianca, so the first three are Davide, Andrea and Bianca. Elisa is shorter than Bianca, and Carla is shorter than Elisa, so the last two are Elisa and Carla. Bianca is third tallest. Option A, Andrea, is the second tallest and can result from using the first statement without placing Davide. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Four colleagues at an office all earn different salaries. Franco earns more than Gino, Ilaria earns less than Gino, and Luisa earns more than Ilaria. Which of the following statements must be true?

- A) Franco earns more than Luisa.
- B) Luisa earns more than Gino.
- C) Luisa earns more than everyone else.
- D) Ilaria earns less than everyone else.
- E) Gino has the second-highest salary.

| From highest salary | Case 1 | Case 2 | Case 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | Luisa | Franco | Franco |
| 2nd | Franco | Luisa | Gino |
| 3rd | Gino | Gino | Luisa |
| 4th | Ilaria | Ilaria | Ilaria |

<div class="spiegazione">

Franco earns more than Gino and Gino earns more than Ilaria, so by the **transitive property** Franco earns more than Ilaria. Luisa also earns more than Ilaria. The other three all earn more than Ilaria, who therefore ranks last in every case. Luisa's position relative to Franco and Gino is undetermined. The table shows the three orders consistent with the information. Each of options A, B, C and E is false in at least one case. For example, case 3 is a counterexample to option B. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
