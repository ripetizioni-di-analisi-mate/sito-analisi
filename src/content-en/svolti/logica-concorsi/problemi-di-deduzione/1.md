## Exercise 1

Anna, Bruno, and Carla work in three different municipal offices: the civil registry, the records office, and the tax office. Anna does not work in the records office. Bruno does not work in the tax office. Carla works in neither the civil registry nor the tax office. Which of the following statements is true?

- A) Anna works in the civil registry
- B) Bruno works in the records office
- C) Anna works in the tax office
- D) The information does not determine which office Anna works in
- E) Carla works in the records office and Anna in the civil registry

| | civil registry | records office | tax office |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Anna | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |
| Bruno | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Carla | ✗ | ✓ | ✗ |

<div class="spiegazione">

Carla's row has an ✗ for the civil registry and the tax office, so by the **row and column rule** Carla works in the records office. Bruno's row then gets an ✗ in the records office column. The second clue rules out the tax office for Bruno, so he works in the civil registry, leaving the tax office for Anna. Option A comes from assigning Anna to the civil registry because it is the first available office in her row, before completing the column. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Giulia, Marco, and Sara have three different occupations (lawyer, pharmacist, teacher) and live in three different cities (Florence, Naples, Rome). The teacher lives in Rome. Giulia lives in Naples. Sara is not the pharmacist and does not live in Rome. Who is the pharmacist, and in which city does that person live?

- A) Sara, in Florence
- B) Marco, in Rome
- C) Giulia, in Florence
- D) Giulia, in Naples
- E) Marco, in Florence

| | occupation | city |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Giulia | pharmacist | Naples |
| Marco | teacher | Rome |
| Sara | lawyer | Florence |

<div class="spiegazione">

Giulia lives in Naples and Sara does not live in Rome, so Sara lives in Florence and Marco in Rome. The teacher lives in Rome. By the **contrapositive** of this clue, anyone who does not live in Rome is not the teacher. Thus neither Giulia nor Sara is the teacher, so Marco is. Sara is not the pharmacist, so she is the lawyer. The pharmacist is Giulia, who lives in Naples. Option B identifies Marco as the person who lives in Rome but overlooks the fact that the teacher lives there. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Elena, Franca, and Ilaria take vacations in three different months (June, July, August) at three different destinations (the seaside, the mountains, a lake). The person going to the seaside leaves in July. Elena leaves before Ilaria. Franca goes to the mountains. Ilaria does not go to the lake. Which statement is true?

- A) Elena leaves in July
- B) Ilaria leaves in August
- C) Franca leaves in June
- D) Elena goes to the seaside
- E) Franca leaves in August

| | month | destination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Elena | June | lake |
| Franca | August | mountains |
| Ilaria | July | seaside |
