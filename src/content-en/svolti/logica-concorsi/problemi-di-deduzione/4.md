## Exercise 1

Ada, Bruno, Carlo, and Dora sit around a square table, one on each side, facing the center. Bruno is opposite Ada. Carlo is on Ada's right. Which statement is true?

- A) Carlo is opposite Bruno
- B) Dora is on Bruno's right
- C) Dora is on Ada's right
- D) Carlo is on Bruno's right
- E) Dora is on Bruno's left

$$
\begin{array}{cccc}
\text{seat } 0 & \text{seat } 1 & \text{seat } 2 & \text{seat } 3 \\
\text{Ada} & \text{Carlo} & \text{Bruno} & \text{Dora}
\end{array}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Place Ada in seat $0$ and number the seats counterclockwise, so the seat to the right of someone in seat $k$ is seat $k + 1$. Bruno sits opposite Ada, so he occupies seat $2$. Carlo is on Ada's right, so he occupies seat $1$. Thus Dora sits in seat $3$, on Bruno's right, while seat $1$ on his left belongs to Carlo. Option D reverses left and right when viewing Bruno from the other side of the table. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Elio, Fausto, Gaia, Ivana, Leo, and Marta sit around a round table with six equally spaced seats, facing the center. Gaia is opposite Elio. Leo is on Gaia's right. Marta is not next to Elio. Fausto is not next to Leo. Who sits opposite Marta?

- A) Leo
- B) Fausto
- C) Ivana
- D) Elio
- E) The information is insufficient to determine this

<div class="spiegazione">

Place Elio in seat $0$ and number the seats counterclockwise. Gaia, opposite him, is in seat $3$, and Leo, on her right, is in seat $4$. This leaves seats $1$, $2$, and $5$. Seats $1$ and $5$ are next to Elio, so Marta must occupy seat $2$. Seat $5$ is next to Leo's seat $4$, so Fausto occupies $1$ and Ivana occupies $5$. Opposite seat $2$ is seat $2 + 3 = 5$, occupied by **Ivana**. Option A puts Marta in seat $1$, next to Elio, ignoring the third clue. Opposite seat $1$ is Leo's seat $4$. The correct answer is **C**.

## Exercise 3

Three men (Andrea, Cesare, Ettore) and three women (Bianca, Delia, Flavia) sit around a round table with six seats facing the center, with men and women alternating. Bianca is opposite Andrea. Cesare sits on Bianca's right. Delia is not next to Cesare. Who sits on Ettore's left?

- A) Bianca
- B) Flavia
- C) Andrea
- D) The information is insufficient to determine this
- E) Delia

$$
\begin{array}{cccccc}
0 & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 \\
\text{Andrea} & \text{Delia} & \text{Ettore} & \text{Bianca} & \text{Cesare} & \text{Flavia}
\end{array}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Place Andrea in seat $0$ and number the seats counterclockwise. Bianca is opposite him in seat $3$, and Cesare sits on her right in seat $4$. Men and women alternate, so the men occupy the even-numbered seats $0$, $2$, and $4$, placing Ettore in seat $2$. The remaining women occupy seats $1$ and $5$. Seat $5$ is next to Cesare, so Delia must occupy seat $1$ and Flavia seat $5$. To Ettore's left is seat $2 - 1 = 1$, occupied by **Delia**. Option A is the person to Ettore's *right*. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
