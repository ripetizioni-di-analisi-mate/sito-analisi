## Exercise 1

Four store employees, Pia, Quinto, Rosa, and Silvio, each work one weekend shift: Saturday morning, Saturday afternoon, Sunday morning, or Sunday afternoon. Quinto works on Sunday. Rosa and Quinto both work in the afternoon. Rosa and Silvio work on different days. Pia does not work in the afternoon. Who works on Sunday morning?

- A) Silvio
- B) Pia
- C) Rosa
- D) Quinto
- E) The information is insufficient to determine this

| | morning | afternoon |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saturday | Pia | Rosa |
| Sunday | Silvio | Quinto |

<div class="spiegazione">

Quinto works on Sunday and in the afternoon, so his shift is Sunday afternoon. Rosa works in the afternoon and Sunday afternoon is already taken, so she works Saturday afternoon. Silvio works on a different day from Rosa, so he works on Sunday. The only Sunday shift left is the morning. Pia is left with Saturday morning, consistent with the final clue. Option B assigns Sunday morning to Pia because she is the only one explicitly ruled out of the afternoon, without using the clue about Rosa and Silvio. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Five office employees, Fabio, Gemma, Ilio, Lina, and Mauro, each cover one afternoon when the office is open, Monday through Friday of the same week. Gemma covers Monday. Mauro works the day after Lina. Fabio works neither Tuesday nor Friday. Ilio works on a day later than Mauro's. On which day does Mauro cover the afternoon opening?

- A) Tuesday
- B) Thursday
- C) Wednesday
- D) Friday
- E) The information is insufficient to determine this

$$
\begin{array}{ccccc}
\text{Mon} & \text{Tue} & \text{Wed} & \text{Thu} & \text{Fri} \\
\text{Gemma} & \text{Lina} & \text{Mauro} & \text{Fabio} & \text{Ilio}
\end{array}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Fabio works on Wednesday or Thursday. If Fabio worked on Wednesday, Lina and Mauro would have to take the consecutive days Thursday and Friday, leaving no later day for Ilio. Therefore, Fabio works on Thursday. Lina and Mauro then take Tuesday and Wednesday because Friday is isolated, and Ilio works on Friday. Thus Mauro works on **Wednesday**. Option A puts Mauro *before* Lina, reversing the clue. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Three security guards, Aldo, Bruno, and Ciro, cover the six nights from Monday through Saturday, one guard per night and two nights per guard. No one works on consecutive nights. Aldo works on Monday, Bruno on Friday, Ciro does not work on Tuesday, and Aldo does not work on Saturday. Which statement must be true?

- A) Ciro works on Wednesday
- B) Bruno works on Tuesday
- C) Aldo works on Thursday
- D) Ciro works on Thursday
- E) Aldo works on Wednesday

| Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aldo | Bruno | Aldo | Ciro | Bruno | Ciro |
| Aldo | Bruno | Ciro | Aldo | Bruno | Ciro |

<div class="spiegazione">

Aldo cannot work on Tuesday because he works on Monday and cannot work consecutive nights. Ciro cannot work on Tuesday either, so Bruno works that night. Bruno now has his two nights, Tuesday and Friday. Neither Aldo nor Bruno works on Saturday, so Ciro does. Wednesday and Thursday go to Aldo and Ciro in either order, and both satisfy the clues, as the table shows. A statement is **necessarily true** only if it holds in both solutions. Only option B does. Each of options A, C, D, and E is false in one of the two solutions. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
