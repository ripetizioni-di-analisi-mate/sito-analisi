## Exercise 1

Aurelio, Beatrice, and Corrado work at three counters numbered $1$, $2$, and $3$, one person per counter. Each uses a different colored folder: gray, red, or white. The folder at counter $1$ is red. Corrado works at counter $3$. Beatrice does not have the gray folder. Which additional piece of information determines each person's counter and folder color?

- A) Aurelio works at counter $2$
- B) Aurelio does not have the gray folder
- C) The gray folder is at counter $3$
- D) Beatrice has the white folder
- E) Beatrice works at counter $1$

| counter $1$ | counter $2$ | counter $3$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aurelio, red | Beatrice, white | Corrado, gray |
| Beatrice, red | Aurelio, gray | Corrado, white |
| Beatrice, red | Aurelio, white | Corrado, gray |

<div class="spiegazione">

The original clues allow the three solutions in the table. An **additional piece of information** settles the question if it leaves exactly one. Option D rules out the second and third rows, in which Beatrice has the red folder, leaving only the first. Each other option leaves two solutions. For example, E leaves the second and third rows, which differ in Aurelio's and Corrado's folder colors. Option C looks only at the first row, where the gray folder is at counter $3$, overlooking that the same holds in the third row. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Alessio, Bruno, Cinzia, Debora, and Ettore run a race. Everyone finishes and there are no ties. Cinzia finishes before Alessio, and Alessio before Ettore. Bruno finishes immediately after Debora. Debora does not finish first. Which statement must be true?

- A) Alessio finishes second
- B) Ettore finishes fifth
- C) Cinzia finishes first
- D) Bruno finishes before Alessio
- E) Debora finishes third

| $1$ | $2$ | $3$ | $4$ | $5$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cinzia | Alessio | Debora | Bruno | Ettore |
| Cinzia | Alessio | Ettore | Debora | Bruno |
| Cinzia | Debora | Bruno | Alessio | Ettore |

<div class="spiegazione">

Debora cannot be first by the last clue. Bruno finishes after Debora, and Alessio and Ettore finish after Cinzia, so none of these three can be first either. Thus Cinzia finishes **first** in every order consistent with the clues. The three solutions in the table give a **counterexample** to each other option. In the third row Alessio is fourth, in the second Ettore is third, in the first Bruno finishes after Alessio, and in the second Debora is fourth. Option A takes the first solution found to be the only one. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Elisa, Franco, Guido, and Ilenia are taking four business trips to four different cities: Bari, Genoa, Turin, and Venice. Elisa goes to neither Bari nor Genoa. Franco does not go to Turin. Guido goes to either Bari or Turin. Ilenia does not go to Venice. Which statement cannot be true?

- A) Elisa goes to Turin and Franco to Genoa
- B) Franco goes to Bari
- C) Ilenia goes to Turin
- D) Guido goes to Turin
- E) Elisa goes to Venice and Guido to Bari

| Elisa | Franco | Guido | Ilenia |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Turin | Venice | Bari | Genoa |
| Venice | Bari | Turin | Genoa |
| Venice | Genoa | Bari | Turin |
| Venice | Genoa | Turin | Bari |

<div class="spiegazione">

Suppose A is true. Guido, who goes to Bari or Turin, must go to Bari because Elisa goes to Turin. That leaves Venice for Ilenia, contrary to the last clue. Option A therefore leads to a contradiction and **cannot be true**. For each other option it is enough to find a solution in which it holds. The table's second row makes B and D true, and the third makes C and E true. Option C stops at the first solution, in which Ilenia goes to Genoa, and confuses “false in this solution” with “cannot be true.” The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
