## Exercise 1

Everyone in a village is either a truth-teller, who always tells the truth, or a liar, who always lies. Davide says, “Elena and I are both liars.” Elena says nothing. Which statement is true?

- A) Davide and Elena are both truth-tellers
- B) Davide and Elena are both liars
- C) Davide is a truth-teller and Elena is a liar
- D) Davide is a liar and Elena is a truth-teller
- E) The information does not determine Elena's type

| Davide | Elena | “both liars” | consistent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| truth-teller | truth-teller | F | no |
| truth-teller | liar | F | no |
| liar | truth-teller | F | yes |
| liar | liar | T | no |

<div class="spiegazione">

If Davide were a truth-teller, his statement would be true and Davide would be a liar, a contradiction. By **reasoning by assumption and contradiction**, Davide is therefore a liar and his statement is false. Since Davide is a liar, “both liars” is false only if Elena is a truth-teller. Option B takes Davide's statement as true, and option E assumes Elena's type is undetermined because she says nothing. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Every islander is either a truth-teller, who always tells the truth, or a liar, who always lies. Alberto says, “Bruno is a truth-teller.” Bruno says, “Carla is a liar.” Carla says, “Alberto and Bruno are the same type.” Who is a truth-teller?

- A) Only Carla
- B) Only Alberto
- C) Alberto and Bruno
- D) Bruno and Carla
- E) None of the three

| | type | statement | truth value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alberto | liar | “Bruno is a truth-teller” | F |
| Bruno | liar | “Carla is a liar” | F |
| Carla | truth-teller | “Alberto and Bruno are the same type” | T |

<div class="spiegazione">

Alberto says “Bruno is a truth-teller,” so by one of the **recurring statements**, Alberto and Bruno are the same type. Carla's statement is therefore true, making her a truth-teller. Bruno says Carla is a liar, so Bruno's statement is false and he is a liar. Alberto is the same type as Bruno, so he is also a liar. Option D takes Bruno to be a truth-teller on Alberto's word without checking that this would make Carla a liar. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

During a training course, three coworkers, Fabio, Gina, and Ugo, play a logic game. Each is secretly assigned the role of a truth-teller, who always tells the truth, or a liar, who always lies. Fabio says, “Exactly one of the three of us is a liar.” Gina says, “Fabio is a liar.” Ugo says, “Gina and I are the same type.” Who are the liars?

- A) Only Fabio
- B) Fabio and Ugo
- C) Only Ugo
- D) Gina and Ugo
- E) All three

| assumption about Ugo | Fabio | Gina | Ugo | liars | Fabio's statement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| truth-teller | liar | truth-teller | truth-teller | $1$ | T, contradiction |
| liar | liar | truth-teller | liar | $2$ | F, consistent |

<div class="spiegazione">

Ugo says “Gina and I are the same type.” By one of the **recurring statements**, Gina is therefore a truth-teller regardless of Ugo's type. Gina says Fabio is a liar, so Fabio is a liar and his statement is false. If Ugo were a truth-teller, Fabio would be the only liar, and Fabio's statement would be true, a contradiction. Thus Ugo is a liar, and the liars are **Fabio and Ugo**. Option A stops after determining that Fabio is lying, without checking that his statement would become true if he were the only liar. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
