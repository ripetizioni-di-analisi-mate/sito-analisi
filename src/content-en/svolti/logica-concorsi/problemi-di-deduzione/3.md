## Exercise 1

Luca, Matteo, Nicola, and Paolo order four different drinks at a café: coffee, tea, juice, and water. Luca has neither coffee nor tea. Nicola has either juice or water. Paolo has either coffee or tea. Matteo does not have coffee. Who has coffee?

- A) Nicola
- B) Matteo
- C) Luca
- D) Paolo
- E) The information is insufficient to determine this

| | coffee | tea | juice | water |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Luca | ✗ | ✗ | | |
| Matteo | ✗ | | | |
| Nicola | ✗ | ✗ | | |
| Paolo | ✓ | | ✗ | ✗ |

<div class="spiegazione">

The coffee column has an ✗ for Luca, Matteo, and Nicola, so by the **row and column rule** Paolo has the coffee. He therefore does not have the tea. Neither Luca nor Nicola has tea, so Matteo has it. Luca and Nicola are left with juice and water, and the clues do not determine which one has which drink. There are two solutions, but both assign the coffee to Paolo. Option E comes from noticing the two solutions and concluding that no question has a definite answer. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Rita, Sonia, Teo, and Ugo work in a four-story building, one on each floor, in four different roles: secretary, accountant, technician, and manager. The manager works on the fourth floor. Rita works on the floor immediately above the accountant. Teo is the technician and does not work on the first floor. Ugo works on the first floor. Sonia is not the manager. Who is the accountant, and on which floor does that person work?

- A) Sonia, on the third floor
- B) Ugo, on the first floor
- C) Sonia, on the second floor
- D) Rita, on the third floor
- E) Sonia, on the fourth floor

| floor | person | role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $4$ | Rita | manager |
| $3$ | Sonia | accountant |
| $2$ | Teo | technician |
| $1$ | Ugo | secretary |

<div class="spiegazione">

The manager works on the fourth floor. Teo is the technician, Sonia is not the manager, and Ugo works on the first floor. Thus Rita is the manager and works on the fourth floor. Since Rita works immediately above the accountant, the accountant is on the third floor. Teo is not on the first floor, where Ugo works, or the fourth, where Rita works. Since Teo is the technician, he is not on the third floor either, where the accountant works. Thus Teo is on the second floor and Sonia on the third. Sonia is therefore the accountant and Ugo is the secretary. Option C correctly identifies Sonia as the accountant but overlooks the clue about Rita when determining her floor. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Alba, Bice, Dario, and Enzo arrive at work at 8\:00, 8\:15, 8\:30, and 8\:45, one at each time, traveling by four different means: car, train, bicycle, and bus. The cyclist arrives at 8\:00. Dario arrives before Enzo and Enzo before Bice. Alba takes the train. The driver arrives at 8\:45. Alba arrives after the person who takes the bus. Who takes the bus and when does that person arrive?

- A) Dario, at 8\:15
- B) Enzo, at 8\:30
- C) Bice, at 8\:30
- D) Dario, at 8\:00
- E) Enzo, at 8\:15

| time | person | means of transportation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8\:00 | Dario | bicycle |
| 8\:15 | Enzo | bus |
| 8\:30 | Alba | train |
| 8\:45 | Bice | car |

<div class="spiegazione">

Alba takes the train, so she arrives neither at 8\:00, the cyclist's time, nor at 8\:45, the driver's time. If she arrived at 8\:15, the only earlier arrival would be the cyclist at 8\:00, contradicting the clue that Alba arrives after the person who takes the bus. Therefore, Alba arrives at 8\:30. The other three arrive in the order Dario, Enzo, Bice, so Dario arrives at 8\:00, Enzo at 8\:15, and Bice at 8\:45. Thus Dario cycles, Bice drives, and Enzo takes the bus. Option D assigns the bus to the first arrival without using the clue about the cyclist. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
