## Exercise 1

The statements “All trainees attended the safety module” and “Some trainees did not attend the safety module” are

- A) contradictory, because they can neither both be true nor both be false
- B) contrary, because they cannot both be true but can both be false
- C) subcontrary, because they can both be true but cannot both be false
- D) equivalent, because they are true in exactly the same situations
- E) such that the second follows from the first

| situation | all attended | some did not attend |
| --- | --- | --- |
| all trainees attended | T | F |
| some attended and some did not | F | T |
| no trainee attended | F | T |

<div class="spiegazione">

The statements have the forms “All $A$ are $B$” and “Some $A$ are not $B$.” In each row of the table, one is true and the other false, so the statements are **contradictory**. In other words, the second is the negation of the first. Option B describes the pair “All $A$ are $B$” and “No $A$ is $B$,” which are both false in the middle row. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The statement “No tax office employee works on Saturdays” is false. Which of the following must be true?

- A) All tax office employees work on Saturdays
- B) Some tax office employees do not work on Saturdays
- C) No one who works on Saturdays is a tax office employee
- D) At least one tax office employee works on Saturdays
- E) Everyone who works on Saturdays is a tax office employee

| given statement | All | None | Some do | Some do not |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| “No $T$ works on Saturdays” is false | ? | F | T | ? |

<div class="spiegazione">

If a statement is false, its negation is true. By the **rules for negating the four forms**, the negation of “No $T$ is $S$” is “Some $T$ are $S$,” that is, “At least one tax office employee works on Saturdays.” Option A is tempting if we confuse negation with contrariety, but “it is not true that nobody works on Saturdays” does not imply that everyone does, since just one employee might work on Saturdays. Option C is equivalent to the given statement and is therefore false. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

In a hospital department, “Some doctors are residents” is true. Which of the following statements *cannot* be true?

- A) Some doctors are not residents
- B) All doctors are residents
- C) Some residents are doctors
- D) Some residents are not doctors
- E) No doctor is a resident

| given statement | All | None | Some do | Some do not |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| “Some $M$ are $S$” is true | ? | F | T | ? |

<div class="spiegazione">

The statements “Some doctors are residents” and “No doctor is a resident” are **contradictory**, so if the first is true, the second is false. Option A is true in a department where some doctors are residents and others are not. Option B is true in a department where all of them are. Option C is always true because the categories can be interchanged in an affirmative statement with “some.” Option D concerns residents who are not doctors, about whom the given statement says nothing. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
