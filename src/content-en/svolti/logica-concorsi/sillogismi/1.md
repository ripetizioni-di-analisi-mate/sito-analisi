## Exercise 1

In a hospital, the statement “No night-shift nurse is a manager” is true. Which of the following statements is equivalent to it?

- A) Some night-shift nurses are not managers
- B) No manager is a night-shift nurse
- C) All night-shift nurses are managers
- D) All managers are night-shift nurses
- E) Some managers are not night-shift nurses

::venn{tipo="disgiunti" zone="a,b" nomi="notte,dirigenti" etichetteNomi="night nurses,managers" titolo="No night-shift nurse is a manager. The two categories have no members in common"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The statement has the form “No $A$ is $B$,” so the two circles in the diagram are disjoint. Switching the names of the two categories leaves the diagram unchanged. The statement therefore works in either direction and is equivalent to **No $B$ is $A$**. If at least one nurse works the night shift, option A follows from the given statement. It is not equivalent to it, because it is also true in a hospital where some night-shift nurses are managers and others are not. Option E has the same defect. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Which of the following statements is equivalent to “Not all applicants passed the preliminary screening test”?

- A) No applicant passed the preliminary screening test
- B) Some applicants passed the preliminary screening test
- C) Most applicants did not pass the preliminary screening test
- D) At least one applicant did not pass the preliminary screening test
- E) Exactly one applicant did not pass the preliminary screening test

$$
\text{not all } C \text{ are } S
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{at least one } C \text{ is not } S
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

“Not all applicants passed the test” negates a universal statement. By the **rules for negating the four forms**, the negation of “All $C$ are $S$” is “Some $C$ are not $S$,” that is, “At least one applicant did not pass the test.” Option A is tempting if we negate “all” with “none.” With $100$ applicants, $60$ of whom passed, the given statement is true and option A is false. Options C and E give information about the number of applicants that the statement does not provide. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

An office regulation states, “Only employees with at least five years of service can request a transfer.” Which of the following statements is equivalent?

- A) All employees who can request a transfer have at least five years of service
- B) All employees with at least five years of service can request a transfer
- C) Some employees with at least five years of service can request a transfer
- D) Some employees who can request a transfer have less than five years of service
- E) An employee can request a transfer if and only if they have at least five years of service

::venn{tipo="incluso" zone="a,ab" nomi="≥5 a.,trasferimento" etichetteNomi="≥5 yr,transfer" titolo="Everyone who can request a transfer is in the category of employees with at least five years of service"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The word “only” identifies the broader category, so everyone who can request a transfer must belong to the group of employees with at least five years of service. The statement therefore has the form “**All $A$ are $B$**,” where $A$ stands for “people who can request a transfer” and $B$ for “people with at least five years of service.” Option B reverses the statement, but the regulation does not guarantee that everyone with at least five years of service can request a transfer, because it may impose other conditions. Option E asserts both directions and therefore says more than the given statement. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
