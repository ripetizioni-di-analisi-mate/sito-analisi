## Exercise 1

“All inspectors are officials” and “All officials have passed a competitive exam” are true. Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- A) Everyone who has passed a competitive exam is an inspector
- B) Some officials are not inspectors
- C) All inspectors have passed a competitive exam
- D) All officials are inspectors
- E) Some people who have passed a competitive exam are not officials

::venn{tipo="tre" zone="abc,bc,c" nomi="ispett.,funz.,concorso" etichetteNomi="insp.,officials,exam" titolo="The colored regions can contain members. The inspectors’ circle lies inside the officials’ circle, which lies inside the circle of people who have passed a competitive exam"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The first premise makes the regions for inspectors outside the officials empty. The second makes the regions for officials outside the group who have passed a competitive exam empty. In the diagram, the only region for inspectors that can contain members lies inside all three circles, so every inspector has passed a competitive exam. This is the first of the **valid forms**, the chain “All $A$ are $B$, all $B$ are $C$, so all $A$ are $C$.” Options A and D reverse a statement containing “all.” Options B and E can be false, for example, in an agency where all officials are inspectors and everyone who passed the exam is an official. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

“All the town’s librarians have degrees” and “No one with a degree works at the switchboard” are true. Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- A) No one with a degree is one of the town’s librarians
- B) None of the town’s librarians works at the switchboard
- C) Some of the town’s librarians work at the switchboard
- D) Everyone who does not work at the switchboard is one of the town’s librarians
- E) Everyone with a degree is one of the town’s librarians

::venn{tipo="tre" zone="ab,b,c" nomi="bibliot.,laureati,centralino" etichetteNomi="libr.,grads,switchboard" titolo="The librarians are in the graduates’ circle, and the graduates have no members in common with the switchboard staff"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Since the librarians belong to the category of graduates and no graduate works at the switchboard, no librarian works at the switchboard. This is the second of the **valid forms**: “All $A$ are $B$, no $B$ is $C$, so no $A$ is $C$.” Option C is always false when the premises are true, because no librarian works at the switchboard. Option A is false as soon as the town has one librarian, since that librarian has a degree. Option D reverses the conclusion just obtained: “No librarian works at the switchboard” does not imply that everyone who does not work there is a librarian. Option E reverses the first premise. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

“All night-shift officers have a category C driver’s license” and “No one with a category C driver’s license is on vacation in August” are true. Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- A) Everyone who is not on vacation in August is a night-shift officer
- B) No one with a category C driver’s license is a night-shift officer
- C) Everyone on vacation in August has a category C driver’s license
- D) Some night-shift officers are on vacation in August
- E) No one on vacation in August is a night-shift officer

::venn{tipo="tre" zone="ac,b,c" nomi="notte,ferie,patente C" etichetteNomi="night officers,vacation,C license" titolo="The night-shift officers belong to the category C license holders, who have no members in common with the people on vacation in August"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The premises have the same form as in the preceding exercise, so the **valid forms** show that no night-shift officer is on vacation in August. The conclusion works in either direction because a statement with “no” is equivalent to the statement obtained by interchanging the categories. Therefore, no one on vacation in August is a night-shift officer. Under the premises, option D is always false, and B is false as soon as there is a night-shift officer, because that officer has a category C license. Option C is false in every situation where someone is on vacation, because anyone on vacation lies outside the group of category C license holders. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
