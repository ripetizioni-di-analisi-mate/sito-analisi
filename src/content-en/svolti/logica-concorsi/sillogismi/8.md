## Exercise 1

At a company, “All sales department employees have a company car” is true. Paolo is an employee of the company and does not have a company car. Which of the following must be true?

- A) Paolo does not work in the sales department
- B) Paolo works in the sales department
- C) Everyone who has a company car works in the sales department
- D) Some sales department employees do not have a company car
- E) Paolo is the only employee of the company without a company car

::venn{tipo="incluso" zone="a,ab,u" nomi="auto,vendite" etichetteNomi="car,sales" elementi="u:Paolo" titolo="Sales employees belong to the group of people with company cars, and Paolo lies outside this group"}

<div class="spiegazione">

If Paolo worked in the sales department, the premise tells us he would have a company car. Since he does not have one, he does not work in sales. This is the second of the **syllogisms about an individual**. In the diagram, Paolo lies outside the circle for company cars, so he also lies outside the sales circle contained within it. Option B is always false. Option C reverses the premise, and D is its contradictory. Option E adds information the premise does not give. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

At a gym, “Everyone enrolled in the Pilates class submitted a medical certificate” and “No one who submitted a medical certificate is over $80$ years old” are true. Mr. Bruni is $83$. Which of the following must be true?

- A) Mr. Bruni is enrolled in the Pilates class
- B) Mr. Bruni submitted a medical certificate
- C) Everyone over $80$ is enrolled in the Pilates class
- D) Mr. Bruni is not enrolled in the Pilates class
- E) Some people enrolled in the Pilates class are over $80$

::venn{tipo="tre" zone="ab,b,c" nomi="pilates,certif.,over 80" etichetteNomi="Pilates,cert.,over 80" elementi="c:Bruni" titolo="Everyone over $80$ lies outside the group who submitted a certificate and therefore also outside the Pilates class"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Mr. Bruni is over $80$, so the second premise tells us that he did not submit a medical certificate. Since everyone enrolled in Pilates submitted one and Mr. Bruni did not, the **syllogisms about an individual** show that he is not enrolled in the Pilates class. Options A, B, and E are always false under the premises. Option C is false because Mr. Bruni is over $80$ and is not enrolled. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

At an agency, the following statements are true. “All counter staff have a training certificate,” “No one with a training certificate works in the archives,” and “Some records office employees are counter staff.” Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- A) No records office employee works in the archives
- B) Some records office employees work in the archives
- C) Some records office employees do not work in the archives
- D) Everyone with a training certificate works at the counter
- E) Some people who work in the archives work at the counter

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{all counter staff have the certificate}\\[4pt]
&\text{no one with the certificate works in the archives}\\[4pt]
\Longrightarrow\ &\text{no member of the counter staff works in the archives}\\[4pt]
&\text{some records office employees work at the counter}\\[4pt]
\Longrightarrow\ &\text{some records office employees do not work in the archives}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

With three premises, first combine two and then combine the resulting conclusion with the third. By the **valid forms**, the first two give “No member of the counter staff works in the archives.” With the third, apply the form “Some $A$ are $B$, no $B$ is $C$, so some $A$ are not $C$.” The records office employee who works at the counter does not work in the archives, so some records office employees do not work in the archives. Option A is tempting if we treat “some” as “all,” but other records office employees may work in the archives. Option E is always false. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
