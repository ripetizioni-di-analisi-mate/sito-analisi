## Exercise 1

Read the following passage:

“The Valdorso public library lends books and films to registered residents. Books may be kept for $30$ days and renewed once unless another user has placed a hold on the book. Films may be kept for $7$ days and cannot be renewed. Anyone who returns a borrowed item late may not borrow other items for as many days as the item was overdue.”

Based solely on the passage, which of the following statements is true?

- A) People who do not live in Valdorso can also borrow books.
- B) A film can be renewed once.
- C) Borrowing books is free.
- D) Someone who returns a film $3$ days late cannot borrow a book for $3$ days.
- E) A borrowed book can never be kept for longer than $30$ days.

| option | wording in the passage | assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | lends “to registered residents” | cannot be inferred |
| B | films “cannot be renewed” | false |
| C | none | cannot be inferred |
| D | $n$ days late, no borrowing for $n$ days | true |
| E | books may be “renewed once” | false |

<div class="spiegazione">

The last sentence says that anyone who returns *a borrowed item* late may not borrow *other items* for as many days as the item was overdue. The film is a borrowed item and the book is another item, so option D follows from the passage and is **true**. Option B contradicts the statement about films. Option E contradicts the renewal rule, since a book with no hold from another user can be renewed and kept beyond the original loan period. The passage does not say whether renewal brings the total to $60$ days. Option C might agree with what the reader knows about public libraries, but the passage says nothing about fees, so it **cannot be inferred**. The text, which mentions only residents, does not confirm option A. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Read the following passage:

“Lake Versa lies at an elevation of $2100$ meters and freezes almost every winter. A layer of ice up to half a meter thick forms on the surface, while the water at the bottom remains liquid. The fish spend the winter there, eating much less than in summer. Since 2010 the town has prohibited walking on the lake ice, after two hikers who were walking on the ice fell into the water.”

Based solely on the passage, which of the following statements cannot be inferred?

- A) In winter the water at the bottom of the lake remains liquid.
- B) Before 2010 many hikers walked on the lake ice.
- C) Lake Versa never freezes.
- D) The fish in the lake eat more in winter than in summer.
- E) Walking on the lake ice is prohibited today.

| option | wording in the passage | assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | “the water at the bottom remains liquid” | true |
| B | “two hikers … fell into the water” | cannot be inferred |
| C | “freezes almost every winter” | false |
| D | “eating much less than in summer” | false |
| E | “since 2010 the town has prohibited” | true |

<div class="spiegazione">

The passage says that two hikers fell into the water before the ban, so at least two people walked on the ice. It does not say how many there were in total. Statement B, with the word *many*, therefore **cannot be inferred**. General knowledge about mountain lakes cannot tell us the number of hikers either, because the question concerns this particular lake. Option E is true because the prohibition is described as still in force and the passage gives no end date. Options C and D change a word or phrase in the passage, replacing “almost every winter” with “never” and “less” with “more,” so both contradict it. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Read the following passage:

“In 2023, the Montalto transit agency, which previously had no electric buses, replaced $40$ of its $100$ buses with electric vehicles and assigned them to routes through the historic center. That same year, residents of the center made fewer complaints about traffic noise. The agency expects to have $70$ electric buses by 2026 if it receives regional funding.”

Based solely on the passage, which of the following statements is true?

- A) In 2023, most of Montalto’s buses were electric.
- B) Residents made fewer complaints because of the electric buses.
- C) By 2026, the agency will have $70$ electric buses.
- D) All Montalto bus routes pass through the historic center.
- E) In 2023 electric buses ran on routes through the historic center.

| option | wording in the passage | assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | $40$ electric buses out of $100$ | false |
| B | both events occurred in the same year | cannot be inferred |
| C | “if it receives regional funding” | cannot be inferred |
| D | none | cannot be inferred |
| E | electric buses assigned to routes through the center | true |

<div class="spiegazione">

The passage says the $40$ electric buses were assigned to routes through the historic center in 2023, so statement E is **true**. Option A is false: before 2023 the agency had no electric buses, and $40$ buses out of $100$ are fewer than half. Option B appeals to someone who reads two events in the same year as cause and effect, but the passage does not say complaints decreased *because of* the electric buses, and other factors may explain the decrease. Option C turns a forecast subject to a condition into a certainty. Option D concerns routes outside the center, about which the passage says nothing. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
