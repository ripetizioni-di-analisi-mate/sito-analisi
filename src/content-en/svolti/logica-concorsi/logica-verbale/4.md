## Exercise 1

Which connective makes the following sentence coherent? “The registry counter closes at 12:30, ___ anyone arriving at 13:00 cannot collect the certificate.”

- A) because
- B) although
- C) so
- D) even though
- E) however

| part | content | relationship |
| --- | --- | --- |
| first | the counter closes at 12:30 | fact |
| second | someone arriving at 13:00 cannot collect the certificate | consequence of the fact |

<div class="spiegazione">

Anyone arriving at 13:00 finds the counter already closed, so the second part of the sentence follows from the first. The relationship is **consequence**, which is expressed by *so*. Option A presents the consequence as if it were the cause, reversing the relationship. Option E would introduce something contrary to what the first part leads us to expect, whereas being unable to collect the certificate is exactly what we would expect. Options B and D express concession, but closing at 12:30 does not lead us to expect the opposite of what the other part of the sentence says. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Which connective makes the following sentence coherent? “___ she had studied very little, Giulia passed the written test.”

- A) Since
- B) Though
- C) So
- D) Because
- E) However

| part | content | what it leads us to expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| subordinate clause | Giulia had studied very little | that Giulia would not pass the test |
| main clause | Giulia passed the test | the opposite of what was expected |

<div class="spiegazione">

Having studied little leads us to expect a poor result, yet the main clause says Giulia passed the test. The subordinate clause leads us to expect the opposite of the main clause, which remains true, so the relationship is **concession**. The concessive connective among the options is *though* (Italian “sebbene,” followed by the subjunctive “avesse studiato”). Option A would present insufficient study as the reason for her success. The original Italian in option D, “perché” with the subjunctive, takes on the sense of “so that,” which makes no sense here. Options C and E do not introduce an initial subordinate clause. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Which pair of connectives makes the following sentence coherent? “The training course was mandatory for all employees. ___, Marco did not attend ___ he was on vacation that week.”

- A) so – because
- B) in fact – so
- C) therefore – however
- D) however – because
- E) although – in fact

| blank | parts being linked | relationship | connective |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| first | mandatory course / Marco did not attend | contrast | however |
| second | Marco did not attend / he was on vacation | cause | because |

<div class="spiegazione">

A course that is mandatory for everyone leads us to expect Marco to attend, yet the sentence says he did not. The first relationship is therefore **contrast**, expressed by *however*. Being on vacation explains Marco’s absence, so the second relationship is **cause**, expressed by *because*. Options A and C begin with a connective of consequence, but Marco’s absence does not follow from the requirement to attend. Option B puts *so* second, presenting the vacation as an effect of the absence. Option E uses *although* (Italian “benché,” which requires the subjunctive) and leaves the sentence without a main clause. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
