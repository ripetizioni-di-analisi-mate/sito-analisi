## Exercise 1

Which word does not belong in the group?

- A) chair
- B) wardrobe
- C) fork
- D) table
- E) bed

| word | is a piece of furniture |
| --- | --- |
| chair | yes |
| wardrobe | yes |
| fork | no, it is a piece of cutlery |
| table | yes |
| bed | yes |

<div class="spiegazione">

The **criterion** “is a piece of furniture” applies to a chair, wardrobe, table, and bed, whereas a fork is a piece of cutlery. The criterion singles out one item, so *fork* is the odd one out. A criterion such as “you can sit or lie on it” applies to the chair and the bed but not to the other three objects. It therefore splits the group into two and three and identifies no odd one out. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Which verb does not belong in the group?

- A) start
- B) begin
- C) launch
- D) embark on
- E) conclude

| verb | means “to start something” |
| --- | --- |
| start | yes |
| begin | yes |
| launch | yes |
| embark on | yes |
| conclude | no, it means “to bring to an end” |

<div class="spiegazione">

The verbs start, begin, launch, and embark on are **synonyms** here because all describe the act of starting something, as in “launch a project” or “embark on a journey.” The verb “conclude” has the opposite meaning, so it is the odd one out. The criterion concerns meaning, and only “conclude” indicates an end to the activity. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Which pair of words does not belong in the group?

- A) dentist – clinic
- B) cook – ladle
- C) teacher – school
- D) sales associate – store
- E) nurse – hospital

| pair | connecting sentence |
| --- | --- |
| dentist – clinic | a dentist works in a clinic |
| cook – ladle | a cook works with a ladle |
| teacher – school | a teacher works in a school |
| sales associate – store | a sales associate works in a store |
| nurse – hospital | a nurse works in a hospital |

<div class="spiegazione">

With five pairs, write a **connecting sentence** for each. In four pairs, the second word is the place where the person named by the first word works, so the relationship is between a person and their workplace. In “cook – ladle,” the second word names a tool the cook uses, so the relationship is different. All the pairs concern occupations, so their common theme does not distinguish any one pair. The **criterion** that singles out the odd pair is the type of relationship. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
