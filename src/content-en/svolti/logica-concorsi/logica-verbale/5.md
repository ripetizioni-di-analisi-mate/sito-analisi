## Exercise 1

Which word makes the following sentence coherent? “Since the road was ___, the town barred heavy vehicles from using it.”

- A) straight
- B) wide
- C) well lit
- D) paved
- E) damaged

| word | does it give a reason to bar heavy vehicles? |
| --- | --- |
| straight | no |
| wide | no |
| well lit | no |
| paved | no |
| damaged | yes |

<div class="spiegazione">

The connective *since* introduces the **cause** of the restriction, so the missing word must describe a road that needs protection from heavy vehicles. The weight of trucks can worsen the condition of a damaged road, which gives a reason for the restriction. The other four words describe features that make the road suitable for traffic, so each would present a favorable quality as the reason for barring traffic. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Which expression makes the following sentence coherent? “Although the store stayed open on Sunday as well, sales for the week were ___.”

- A) poor
- B) high
- C) double their previous level
- D) rising
- E) better than usual

| part | content | what it leads us to expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| subordinate clause with *although* | the store stayed open an extra day | higher sales |
| main clause | ___ sales | the opposite of the expectation |

<div class="spiegazione">

The connective *although* introduces a **concession**, a fact that leads us to expect the opposite of the main clause. An extra day of opening leads us to expect higher sales, so the main clause must say that sales were low. The only option with this meaning is *poor*. Options B, C, D, and E all match the expectation, so they would make the relationship causal and require *since* in place of *although*. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Which pair of words makes the following sentence coherent? “Under the new schedule, buses on route 5 run every $10$ minutes instead of every $20$ minutes. Service is therefore more ___. ___, many commuters have left their cars at home.”

- A) frequent – although
- B) slow – therefore
- C) frequent – therefore
- D) infrequent – in fact
- E) expensive – because

| blank | what it requires | word |
| --- | --- | --- |
| first | a quality that follows from buses running every $10$ minutes | frequent |
| second | a connective of consequence introducing the effect on commuters | therefore |

<div class="spiegazione">

The first blank follows *therefore*, so the missing word expresses a consequence of the initial information. Buses running every $10$ minutes instead of every $20$ minutes make the service more *frequent*. “Slow,” “infrequent,” and “expensive” do not follow from those numbers. The second blank links the more frequent service to the commuters’ decision, which is its effect. It therefore calls for a connective of **consequence** such as *therefore*. In option A, the first word is correct, but *although* expresses concession and its Italian equivalent, “benché,” requires the subjunctive. With two missing words, an option is correct only if both words are correct. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
