## Exercise 1

An announcement states, “Only candidates on the professional register who hold a language certificate are admitted to the selection process.” Valeria is on the register but has no language certificate. Which statement must be true?

- A) Valeria is admitted to the selection process.
- B) Anyone on the register who holds the certificate is admitted to the selection process.
- C) A candidate who is not on the register may be admitted if they hold the certificate.
- D) Whether Valeria is admitted to the selection process cannot be determined.
- E) Valeria is not admitted to the selection process.

|  | statement |
| --- | --- |
| announcement | admitted $\Rightarrow$ registered and certified |
| contrapositive | not registered or no certificate $\Rightarrow$ not admitted |
| given fact | Valeria has no certificate |
| conclusion | Valeria is not admitted |

<div class="spiegazione">

The word “only” makes the two requirements **necessary conditions**, so the announcement translates as “if a candidate is admitted, then they are on the register and hold the certificate.” Valeria does not hold the certificate, so for her the consequent of the implication is false. By **modus tollens**, she is not admitted. Missing even one requirement is enough. Choice B treats the requirements as sufficient, while the announcement states only that they are necessary. Choice D would be correct if Valeria met both requirements. Choice C contradicts the announcement because someone who is not on the register is never admitted. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The rules for a company tournament state, “Only people who have been employees for at least one year and belong to the recreation club may participate.” Roberto takes part in the tournament. Which statement must be true?

- A) Roberto has been an employee for at least one year, but whether he belongs to the club is unknown.
- B) Roberto meets just one of the two requirements.
- C) Every employee who has been with the company for at least one year and belongs to the club takes part.
- D) Roberto has been an employee for at least one year and belongs to the club.
- E) Anyone who does not take part in the tournament has not been an employee for at least one year.

|  | statement |
| --- | --- |
| rule | participates $\Rightarrow$ at least one year of employment and club membership |
| given fact | Roberto participates |
| conclusion (modus ponens) | Roberto has at least one year of employment and is a club member |

<div class="spiegazione">

The rule translates as “if a person takes part, then they have been an employee for at least one year and belong to the club.” Roberto takes part, so by **modus ponens** he meets both requirements. Choice A acknowledges only one of them, and B contradicts the conclusion. Choice C is the converse of the rule because it treats the requirements as sufficient. Choice E comes from the inverse and is false because someone may meet both requirements and choose not to take part. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A call for bids states, “A company may be awarded the contract only if it has been in business for at least three years and submits a bid bond.” Elisa's company has been in business for five years. Franco's company submits a bond but has been in business for two years. Gina's company is awarded the contract. Which statement must be true?

- A) Elisa's company is awarded the contract.
- B) Franco's company is not awarded the contract, and Gina's company submitted a bid bond.
- C) Elisa's company submitted a bid bond.
- D) Gina's company has been in business for at least three years, and Franco's company is awarded the contract.
- E) Franco's company is awarded the contract.

| company | at least three years | bid bond | awarded the contract |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Elisa | yes (given) | ? | ? |
| Franco | no (given) | yes (given) | no |
| Gina | yes | yes | yes (given) |

<div class="spiegazione">

The rule makes both requirements **necessary conditions** for being awarded the contract. Franco's company fails the three-year requirement, so by modus tollens it is not awarded the contract. Gina's company is awarded the contract, so by modus ponens it has been in business for at least three years and submitted a bid bond. Together these conclusions give choice B. For Elisa's company, only its time in business is known. We do not know whether it submitted a bond, so neither A nor C is certain. Choices D and E say that Franco's company is awarded the contract, contrary to the conclusion. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
