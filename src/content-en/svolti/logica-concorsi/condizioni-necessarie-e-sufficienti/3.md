## Exercise 1

A company's internal rules state, “Travel expenses are reimbursed only if a receipt is submitted.” Which statement is equivalent?

- A) If a receipt is submitted, the expenses are reimbursed.
- B) Submitting a receipt is enough to obtain reimbursement.
- C) If the expenses are not reimbursed, no receipt was submitted.
- D) The expenses are reimbursed if and only if a receipt is submitted.
- E) If no receipt is submitted, the expenses are not reimbursed.

$$
E = \text{the expenses are reimbursed},
\qquad
S = \text{a receipt is submitted}
$$

| choice | implication |
| --- | --- |
| A | $S \Rightarrow E$ |
| B | $S \Rightarrow E$ |
| C | not $E \Rightarrow$ not $S$ |
| D | $E \Rightarrow S$ and $S \Rightarrow E$ |
| E | not $S \Rightarrow$ not $E$ |

<div class="spiegazione">

What follows “only if” is a **necessary condition**, so the rule translates as “if the expenses are reimbursed, then a receipt was submitted.” Its contrapositive is “if no receipt is submitted, the expenses are not reimbursed,” choice E. Choices A and B make the receipt sufficient. Choice C is the inverse, which is equivalent to the converse, “if a receipt is submitted, the expenses are reimbursed.” Even with a receipt, reimbursement might be denied, for instance because the expense was not authorized. Choice D adds the converse. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The airport board reads, “The flight departs on time unless there is fog.” Which statement is equivalent?

- A) If the flight departs on time, there is no fog.
- B) If there is fog, the flight does not depart on time.
- C) If the flight does not depart on time, there is fog.
- D) The flight departs on time only if there is no fog.
- E) If the flight does not depart on time, there is no fog.

| choice | implication | relation to the given statement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | on time $\Rightarrow$ no fog | converse |
| B | fog $\Rightarrow$ not on time | inverse |
| C | not on time $\Rightarrow$ fog | contrapositive |
| D | on time $\Rightarrow$ no fog | converse |
| E | not on time $\Rightarrow$ no fog | attempted contrapositive without negating “there is no fog” |

<div class="spiegazione">

The **translation of “unless”** takes “$A$ unless $B$” to “if not $B$, then $A$.” Thus the board says “if there is no fog, the flight departs on time.” In the Italian expression “a meno che non,” the word “non” is redundant and does not negate the condition. The contrapositive interchanges the two statements and negates both, giving “if the flight does not depart on time, there is fog,” choice C. Choices A and D state the same thing, the converse, and B is the inverse. The board says nothing about what happens in fog, and a flight may depart on time even in fog. Choice E arises from failing to negate “there is no fog” when forming the contrapositive. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Which statement does *not* have the same meaning as the other four?

- A) If the sign is displayed, the office is closed.
- B) If the office is closed, the sign is displayed.
- C) The office is closed only if the sign is displayed.
- D) If the sign is not displayed, the office is not closed.
- E) The office is not closed or the sign is displayed.

$$
K = \text{the office is closed},
\qquad
C = \text{the sign is displayed}
$$

| $K$ | $C$ | A: $C \Rightarrow K$ | B: $K \Rightarrow C$ | C: $K \Rightarrow C$ | D: not $C \Rightarrow$ not $K$ | E: not $K$ or $C$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T | T | T | T | T | T | T |
| T | F | T | F | F | F | F |
| F | T | F | T | T | T | T |
| F | F | T | T | T | T | T |

<div class="spiegazione">

Statement B is the implication “if $K$, then $C$.” Statement C means the same thing because what follows “only if” is the consequent. Statement D is the **contrapositive** of B and is therefore equivalent to B. Statement E is true when the office is open or the sign is displayed and is false only when the office is closed and the sign is not displayed, which is exactly when B is false. Indeed, columns B through E are identical, whereas column A differs in the middle two rows. Statement A is the converse of B. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
