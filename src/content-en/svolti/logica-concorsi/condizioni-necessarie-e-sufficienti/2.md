## Exercise 1

If the statement “If an employee works the night shift, they receive a shift allowance” is true, which of the following must be true?

- A) If an employee does not receive the allowance, they do not work the night shift.
- B) If an employee receives the allowance, they work the night shift.
- C) If an employee does not work the night shift, they do not receive the allowance.
- D) Some employee works the night shift without receiving the allowance.
- E) Every employee works the night shift.

$$
N = \text{works the night shift},
\qquad
R = \text{receives the allowance}
$$

| statement | form | equivalent to $N \Rightarrow R$? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $N \Rightarrow R$ | given implication | yes |
| not $R \Rightarrow$ not $N$ | contrapositive | yes |
| $R \Rightarrow N$ | converse | no |
| not $N \Rightarrow$ not $R$ | inverse | no |

<div class="spiegazione">

Choice A is the **contrapositive** of the given statement, so it is true whenever that statement is true. A night-shift employee receives the allowance, so an employee who does not receive it cannot be working the night shift. Choices B and C are the converse and the inverse. The statement says nothing about people who receive the allowance for other reasons, such as working on holidays. Choice D describes precisely the case ruled out by the statement. Choice E concerns all employees, while the rule concerns only night-shift workers. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

If the statement “If it is not raining, Sara bikes to work” is true, which of the following must be true?

- A) If Sara bikes to work, it is not raining.
- B) If it is raining, Sara does not bike to work.
- C) If Sara does not bike to work, it is not raining.
- D) If Sara does not bike to work, it is raining.
- E) Sara bikes to work if and only if it is not raining.

| statement | form |
| --- | --- |
| not $P \Rightarrow B$ | given implication |
| not $B \Rightarrow P$ | contrapositive |
| $B \Rightarrow$ not $P$ | converse |
| $P \Rightarrow$ not $B$ | inverse |

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $P$ stand for “it is raining” and $B$ for “Sara bikes.” The given statement is then “if not $P$, then $B$.” To form the **contrapositive**, interchange the antecedent and consequent and negate both. The negation of “it is not raining” is “it is raining,” so the contrapositive is “if Sara does not bike to work, then it is raining,” choice D. Choice C fails to negate “it is not raining.” Choices A and B are the converse and the inverse. Neither follows from the given statement because Sara may bike to work even in the rain. Choice E contains the converse. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A department has this rule: “If Marco works the Sunday shift, he does not work on Monday.” Which statement must be true?

- A) If Marco does not work the Sunday shift, he works on Monday.
- B) If Marco works on Monday, he did not work the Sunday shift.
- C) If Marco does not work on Monday, he worked the Sunday shift.
- D) If Marco does not work the Sunday shift, he does not work on Monday.
- E) If Marco works on Monday, he worked the Sunday shift.

| Sunday | Monday | the rule is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| on shift | does not work | satisfied |
| on shift | works | violated |
| off shift | does not work | satisfied |
| off shift | works | satisfied |

<div class="spiegazione">

Exactly one row of the table violates the rule: Marco works the Sunday shift and also works on Monday. Thus if Marco works on Monday, that row is ruled out and “off shift” is the only remaining possibility for Sunday. This gives choice B, the **contrapositive** of the rule, because the negation of the consequent “does not work on Monday” is “works on Monday.” The fourth row disproves choice E: Marco works on Monday without having worked the Sunday shift. The rows in which he did not work the Sunday shift disprove choices A, C, and D, because in those rows he may or may not work on Monday. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
