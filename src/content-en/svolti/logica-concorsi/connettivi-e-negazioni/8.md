## Exercise 1

If "It is not true that Carla or Ettore signed the minutes" is true, which of the following must be true?

- A) Carla signed the minutes and Ettore did not
- B) Neither Carla nor Ettore signed the minutes
- C) Exactly one of Carla and Ettore signed the minutes
- D) If Carla did not sign the minutes, Ettore did
- E) Carla did not sign the minutes and Ettore did

$$
\lnot (C \lor E) \equiv \lnot C \land \lnot E
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $C$ = "Carla signed the minutes" and $E$ = "Ettore signed the minutes." The statement negates the disjunction $C \lor E$, so by **De Morgan's laws** it is equivalent to $\lnot C \land \lnot E$, that is, "Neither Carla nor Ettore signed the minutes." Options A, C, and E assert that at least one of the two signed, so they are false. Option D is equivalent to $C \lor E$, which is precisely the statement being negated. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Both statements "It is not true that Mirko and Luisa are both on vacation next week" and "Mirko is on vacation next week" are true. Which of the following must be true?

- A) Luisa is on vacation next week
- B) Neither Mirko nor Luisa is on vacation next week
- C) It is impossible to determine whether Luisa is on vacation next week
- D) Luisa is not on vacation next week
- E) If Luisa is not on vacation next week, Mirko is not on vacation

$$
\lnot (M \land L) \equiv \lnot M \lor \lnot L,
\qquad
M \text{ true}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\lnot L \text{ true}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $M$ = "Mirko is on vacation" and $L$ = "Luisa is on vacation." By **De Morgan's laws**, the first statement is equivalent to $\lnot M \lor \lnot L$, so at least one of the two propositions $\lnot M$ and $\lnot L$ is true. Since Mirko is on vacation, $\lnot M$ is false, so $\lnot L$ is true, that is, Luisa is not on vacation. Option C would be correct without the second statement because the first alone does not determine whether Luisa is on vacation. Option E has a true antecedent and a false consequent, so it is false. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The following statements are true:

1. The project is assigned to Elena or Fabio.
2. If the project is assigned to Elena, Giada coordinates the team.
3. Giada does not coordinate the team.

Which of the following must be true?

- A) The project is assigned to Elena
- B) The project is assigned to neither Elena nor Fabio
- C) The project is assigned to both Elena and Fabio
- D) The project is assigned to Fabio if and only if it is assigned to Elena
- E) The project is assigned to Fabio and not to Elena

| step | statement used | conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | $E \Rightarrow G$ and $\lnot G$ | $\lnot E$ (contrapositive) |
| 2 | $E \lor F$ and $\lnot E$ | $F$ |
| 3 | $\lnot E$ and $F$ | $F \land \lnot E$ |

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $E$ = "The project is assigned to Elena," $F$ = "The project is assigned to Fabio," and $G$ = "Giada coordinates the team." The second statement is equivalent to its **contrapositive** $\lnot G \Rightarrow \lnot E$. Since Giada does not coordinate the team, the project is not assigned to Elena. The first statement is a true disjunction with $E$ false, so $F$ is true. Thus the project is assigned to Fabio and not to Elena. Option D is false because $F$ and $E$ have different values. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
