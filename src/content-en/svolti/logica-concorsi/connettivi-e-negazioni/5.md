## Exercise 1

Which of the following statements is equivalent to "If the application is complete, it is registered"?

- A) If the application is registered, it is complete
- B) If the application is incomplete, it is not registered
- C) The application is complete and is registered
- D) The application is complete or is registered
- E) If the application is not registered, it is incomplete

| $C$ | $P$ | $C \Rightarrow P$ | $P \Rightarrow C$ | $\lnot C \Rightarrow \lnot P$ | $\lnot P \Rightarrow \lnot C$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T | T | T | T | T | T |
| T | F | F | T | T | F |
| F | T | T | F | F | T |
| F | F | T | T | T | T |

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $C$ = "The application is complete" and $P$ = "The application is registered." Option E exchanges the antecedent and the consequent and negates them, so it is the **contrapositive**, which is equivalent to the original implication. Indeed, the columns for $C \Rightarrow P$ and $\lnot P \Rightarrow \lnot C$ in the table agree. Option A is the converse and option B is the inverse, and both differ from the given statement in the second and third rows. Option C is false when the application is incomplete, a case in which the given statement is true. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Which of the following statements is equivalent to "If Sara takes the 7\:10 train, she arrives at the office before 9\:00"?

- A) Sara takes the 7\:10 train or arrives at the office before 9\:00
- B) If Sara arrives at the office before 9\:00, she took the 7\:10 train
- C) Sara does not take the 7\:10 train or arrives at the office before 9\:00
- D) Sara does not take the 7\:10 train and arrives at the office before 9\:00
- E) If Sara does not take the 7\:10 train, she does not arrive at the office before 9\:00

$$
T \Rightarrow U \equiv \lnot T \lor U
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $T$ = "Sara takes the 7\:10 train" and $U$ = "Sara arrives at the office before 9\:00." Expressing an **implication as a disjunction** shows that $T \Rightarrow U$ is equivalent to $\lnot T \lor U$, that is, to option C. Both statements are false in the same single case, when Sara takes the train and does not arrive before 9\:00. In option A the antecedent is not negated, so A is true even when Sara takes the train and arrives late. Options B and E are the converse and the inverse. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Which of the following statements is equivalent to "If there is no strike, the train departs on time"?

- A) If there is a strike, the train does not depart on time
- B) If the train departs on time, there is no strike
- C) There is no strike and the train departs on time
- D) If the train does not depart on time, there is a strike
- E) Either there is a strike or the train departs on time, but not both

$$
\lnot S \Rightarrow P \equiv \lnot P \Rightarrow \lnot \lnot S \equiv \lnot P \Rightarrow S
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $S$ = "There is a strike" and $P$ = "The train departs on time," so the statement is $\lnot S \Rightarrow P$. The **contrapositive** exchanges the antecedent and the consequent and negates them. Since the negation of $\lnot S$ is $S$, this gives $\lnot P \Rightarrow S$, that is, "If the train does not depart on time, there is a strike." Option A is the inverse and option B is the converse. Option E excludes the case in which there is a strike and the train still departs on time, which the given statement allows. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
