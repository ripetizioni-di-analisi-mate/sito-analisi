## Exercise 1

Which of the following propositions is a tautology?

- A) $A \land \lnot A$
- B) $A \Rightarrow \lnot A$
- C) $A \lor \lnot A$
- D) $A \lor B$
- E) $A \Leftrightarrow \lnot A$

| $A$ | $B$ | $A \land \lnot A$ | $A \Rightarrow \lnot A$ | $A \lor \lnot A$ | $A \lor B$ | $A \Leftrightarrow \lnot A$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T | T | F | F | T | T | F |
| T | F | F | F | T | T | F |
| F | T | F | T | T | T | F |
| F | F | F | T | T | F | F |

<div class="spiegazione">

A **tautology** is true in every row of the truth table. Proposition $A \lor \lnot A$ is always true because one of the two propositions $A$ and $\lnot A$ is always true. Options A and E are contradictions because they are false in every row. Option B is true only when $A$ is false, and option D is false when $A$ and $B$ are both false, so neither is a tautology. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Let $M$ = "The museum is open" and $B$ = "The ticket office is open." Which of the following statements is false in every case, that is, a contradiction?

- A) The museum is open or it is not open
- B) If the museum is open, the museum is open
- C) The museum is open and the ticket office is not open
- D) If the museum is open, the museum is not open
- E) The statements “If the museum is open, the ticket office is open” and “The museum is open and the ticket office is not open” are both true

| $M$ | $B$ | $M \Rightarrow B$ | $M \land \lnot B$ | $(M \Rightarrow B) \land M \land \lnot B$ | $M \Rightarrow \lnot M$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T | T | T | F | F | F |
| T | F | F | T | F | F |
| F | T | T | F | F | T |
| F | F | T | F | F | T |

<div class="spiegazione">

A **contradiction** is false in every row. Statement E is the conjunction of $M \Rightarrow B$ and $M \land \lnot B$, which is the negation of $M \Rightarrow B$. A proposition and its negation are never both true, so their conjunction is always false. Option D may look contradictory, but $M \Rightarrow \lnot M$ is true when the museum is closed because its antecedent is false. Options A and B are tautologies, and option C is true in one case. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Which of the following propositions is a tautology?

- A) $(A \land B) \Rightarrow (A \lor B)$
- B) $(A \lor B) \Rightarrow (A \land B)$
- C) $(A \Rightarrow B) \Rightarrow (B \Rightarrow A)$
- D) $\lnot (A \lor B) \Leftrightarrow (\lnot A \lor \lnot B)$
- E) $(A \Rightarrow B) \land \lnot B$

| $A$ | $B$ | $A \land B$ | $A \lor B$ | $(A \land B) \Rightarrow (A \lor B)$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T | T | T | T | T |
| T | F | F | T | T |
| F | T | F | T | T |
| F | F | F | F | T |

<div class="spiegazione">

An implication is false only when its antecedent is true and its consequent is false. If $A \land B$ is true, then $A$ is true, so $A \lor B$ is also true. Thus option A is never false and is a **tautology**. Option B is false when $A$ is true and $B$ is false. Option C asserts that an implication implies its converse and is false when $A$ is false and $B$ is true. Option D confuses one of **De Morgan's laws** with the other and is false, for example, when $A$ is true and $B$ is false. Option E is false when $B$ is true. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
