## Exercise 1

In a class of $40$ students, $22$ play soccer, $15$ swim, and $8$ do both. How many students do neither?

- A) $3$
- B) $29$
- C) $11$
- D) $8$
- E) $18$

$$
|C \cup N| = 22 + 15 - 8 = 29,
\qquad
40 - 29 = 11
$$

::venn{zone="u" nomi="C,N" elementi="a:14;ab:8;b:7;u:11" titolo="Students who play soccer ($C$) and swim ($N$): $14$ play only soccer, $8$ do both, $7$ only swim, and $11$ do neither"}

<div class="spiegazione">

By the **union formula**, $22 + 15 - 8 = 29$ students play at least one sport because the $8$ students who play both are counted among both the $22$ and the $15$. The number who play neither is therefore $40 - 29 = 11$. Choice A, namely $40 - 22 - 15 = 3$, subtracts the $8$ students in the overlap twice, while choice B counts those who play at least one sport. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A library has $130$ members. Last year, $75$ members borrowed novels, $50$ borrowed nonfiction books, and $20$ borrowed neither novels nor nonfiction books. How many members borrowed novels but no nonfiction books?

- A) $75$
- B) $15$
- C) $35$
- D) $110$
- E) $60$

$$
|R \cup S| = 130 - 20 = 110,
\qquad
|R \cap S| = 75 + 50 - 110 = 15,
\qquad
75 - 15 = 60
$$

::venn{zone="a" nomi="R,S" elementi="a:60;ab:15;b:35;u:20" titolo="Members who borrowed novels ($R$) and nonfiction books ($S$): the shaded region contains those who borrowed novels but no nonfiction books"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The number of members who borrowed novels or nonfiction books is $130 - 20 = 110$. The **union formula** gives the size of the overlap: $75 + 50 - 110 = 15$ members borrowed both novels and nonfiction books. The $75$ readers of novels include these $15$ members, so $75 - 15 = 60$ borrowed novels but no nonfiction books. Choice A includes people who borrowed both novels and nonfiction books, and choice B gives the size of the overlap. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

In an office of $36$ employees, twice as many commute by train as by bus. Exactly $6$ employees use both forms of transportation and $3$ use neither. How many use only the train?

- A) $26$
- B) $20$
- C) $13$
- D) $7$
- E) $22$

$$
2x + x - 6 = 36 - 3
\iff
3x = 39
\iff
x = 13
$$
$$
|T| = 2 \cdot 13 = 26,
\qquad
26 - 6 = 20
$$

::venn{zone="a" nomi="T,A" elementi="a:20;ab:6;b:7;u:3" titolo="Employees who commute by train ($T$) and by bus ($A$): $20$ take only the train, $6$ use both, $7$ take only the bus, and $3$ use neither"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $x$ be the number of employees who take the bus. Then $2x$ take the train. The number who use at least one form of transportation is $36 - 3 = 33$. The **union formula** gives $2x + x - 6 = 33$, so $x = 13$. Thus $26$ employees take the train. Subtracting the $6$ who also take the bus gives $20$ who take only the train. As a check, $20 + 6 + 7 + 3 = 36$. Choice A counts everyone who takes the train. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
