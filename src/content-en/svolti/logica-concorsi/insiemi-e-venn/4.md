## Exercise 1

An office has $60$ employees, of whom $25$ are men. There are $34$ full-time employees and $12$ women who work part-time. How many men work part-time?

- A) $14$
- B) $13$
- C) $11$
- D) $26$
- E) $23$

| | Full-time | Part-time | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Men | $11$ | $14$ | $25$ |
| Women | $23$ | $12$ | $35$ |
| Total | $34$ | $26$ | $60$ |

<div class="spiegazione">

Enter the given values and totals in the **two-way table**. There are $60 - 34 = 26$ part-time employees, and $12$ of them are women, so $26 - 12 = 14$ men work part-time. The other entries follow in the same way, and each row and column adds up to its total. Choice D is the total number of part-time employees. Choice C is the number of men who work full-time. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A total of $120$ candidates take the preliminary test for a competitive exam, and $70$ of them have a university degree. Of the $90$ candidates who pass, $60$ have a degree. How many candidates without a degree fail the test?

- A) $30$
- B) $10$
- C) $50$
- D) $20$
- E) $60$

| | Pass | Fail | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Degree | $60$ | $10$ | $70$ |
| No degree | $30$ | $20$ | $50$ |
| Total | $90$ | $30$ | $120$ |

<div class="spiegazione">

There are $120 - 70 = 50$ candidates without a degree. Of the $90$ who pass, $60$ have a degree, so $90 - 60 = 30$ do not. Therefore, $50 - 30 = 20$ candidates without a degree fail the test. The total in the “Fail” column confirms the result because $10 + 20 = 30 = 120 - 90$. Choice A is the number of candidates without a degree who pass, the entry next to the one we need. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

At a company, $40\%$ of the employees are men. Of the men, $30\%$ work remotely, as do $20\%$ of the women. What percentage of all employees work remotely?

- A) $50\%$
- B) $25\%$
- C) $24\%$
- D) $12\%$
- E) $30\%$

| | Remote | On-site | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Men | $12$ | $28$ | $40$ |
| Women | $12$ | $48$ | $60$ |
| Total | $24$ | $76$ | $100$ |

<div class="spiegazione">

Suppose there are $100$ employees. Then $40$ are men and $60$ are women. Among those working remotely are $30\%$ of $40$, or $12$ men, and $20\%$ of $60$, or $12$ women. Therefore, $24$ out of $100$ employees work remotely, or $24\%$. Choice B averages the two percentages, which would give the right answer only if the numbers of men and women were equal. Choice A adds percentages calculated for different groups. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
