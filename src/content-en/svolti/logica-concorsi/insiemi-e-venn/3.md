## Exercise 1

In the universal set $U = \{1, 2, 3, \ldots, 12\}$, let $A$ be the set of even numbers and $B$ the set of multiples of $3$. Which of the following is the set $\overline{A \cup B}$?

- A) $\{1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11\}$
- B) $\{2, 3, 4, 8, 9, 10\}$
- C) $\{1, 5, 7, 11\}$
- D) $\{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11\}$
- E) $\{6, 12\}$

::venn{zone="u" nomi="A,B" elementi="a:2,4,8,10;ab:6,12;b:3,9;u:1,5,7,11" titolo="Numbers from $1$ to $12$: $1$, $5$, $7$, and $11$ lie outside both the even numbers ($A$) and the multiples of $3$ ($B$)"}

<div class="spiegazione">

By **De Morgan’s law**, $\overline{A \cup B} = \overline{A} \cap \overline{B}$, so we need the numbers that are neither even nor multiples of $3$. The odd numbers are $1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11$, of which $3$ and $9$ are multiples of $3$. This leaves $1$, $5$, $7$, and $11$. Choice A gives $\overline{A}$ alone, choice D gives $\overline{A \cap B}$, and choice B is the set of elements that belong to just one of the two sets. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

At a company, $P$ is the set of employees who have a driver’s license and $L$ is the set of employees who speak a foreign language. The set $\overline{P \cap L}$ consists of employees who:

- A) have no driver’s license and speak no foreign language
- B) have a driver’s license and speak no foreign language
- C) speak a foreign language and have no driver’s license
- D) have no driver’s license or speak no foreign language
- E) have a driver’s license or speak a foreign language, but not both

::venn{zone="a,b,u" nomi="P,L" titolo="The complement of $P \cap L$ consists of the three regions outside the overlap of the two circles"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The set $P \cap L$ contains the employees who have *both* characteristics. An employee belongs to its complement if they lack at least one characteristic. Thus, by **De Morgan’s law**, $\overline{P \cap L} = \overline{P} \cup \overline{L}$, the set of employees who have no driver’s license or speak no foreign language. Choice A describes $\overline{P \cup L}$, the region outside both circles, and arises from keeping “and” when negating an “and” statement. Choice E excludes employees who have neither characteristic, although they belong to $\overline{P \cap L}$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A recruitment notice lists two requirements: a category B driver’s license and a certificate of English proficiency. Of the $50$ applicants, $30$ have the license, $25$ have the certificate, and $12$ meet both requirements. How many applicants are excluded because they lack at least one of the two requirements?

- A) $7$
- B) $43$
- C) $12$
- D) $31$
- E) $38$

$$
|\overline{P \cap C}| = |U| - |P \cap C| = 50 - 12 = 38
$$

::venn{zone="a,b,u" nomi="P,C" elementi="a:18;ab:12;b:13;u:7" titolo="Applicants with the license ($P$) and the certificate ($C$): all are excluded except the $12$ in the overlap"}

<div class="spiegazione">

An applicant is eligible only if they meet both requirements, that is, if they belong to $P \cap C$. Those excluded form the **complement** of $P \cap C$, so their number is $50 - 12 = 38$. The diagram confirms this because $18 + 13 + 7 = 38$. Choice A counts only those who meet neither requirement. Choice D counts those who meet exactly one and leaves out those who meet neither. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
