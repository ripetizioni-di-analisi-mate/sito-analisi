## Exercise 1

A survey of $100$ households finds that $45$ have a dog, $40$ have a cat, and $30$ have a goldfish. Of these, $15$ have a dog and a cat, $10$ have a dog and a goldfish, $12$ have a cat and a goldfish, and $5$ have all three pets. The pairwise counts include households with all three pets. How many households have none of the three?

- A) $17$
- B) $22$
- C) $83$
- D) $56$
- E) $27$

$$
|C \cup G \cup P| = 45 + 40 + 30 - 15 - 10 - 12 + 5 = 83,
\qquad
100 - 83 = 17
$$

::venn{tipo="tre" nomi="C,G,P" elementi="a:25;b:18;c:13;ab:10;ac:5;bc:7;abc:5;u:17" titolo="Households with a dog ($C$), a cat ($G$), and a goldfish ($P$), with the regions filled in from the center outward"}

<div class="spiegazione">

By the **formula for the union of three sets**, $83$ households have at least one of the pets, so $100 - 83 = 17$ have none. The diagram confirms the count because the seven interior regions add up to $25 + 18 + 13 + 10 + 5 + 7 + 5 = 83$. Choice B is obtained by omitting the central count $|C \cap G \cap P| = 5$. Choice E is obtained by subtracting it instead of adding it. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

At a sports center with $70$ members, $38$ use the gym, $20$ use the pool, and $24$ take yoga classes. Of the members, $10$ use the gym and the pool, $12$ use the gym and take yoga, $6$ use the pool and take yoga, and $4$ participate in all three activities. The pairwise counts include everyone who participates in all three. How many members take yoga only?

- A) $24$
- B) $6$
- C) $10$
- D) $14$
- E) $2$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{gym and yoga, no pool} &= 12 - 4 = 8 \\[6pt]
\text{pool and yoga, no gym} &= 6 - 4 = 2 \\[6pt]
\text{yoga only} &= 24 - 8 - 2 - 4 = 10
\end{aligned}
$$

::venn{tipo="tre" zone="c" nomi="Pal,Pis,Y" elementi="a:20;b:8;c:10;ab:6;ac:8;bc:2;abc:4;u:12" titolo="Gym, pool, and yoga participants: the shaded region contains the members who take yoga only"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Fill in the diagram **from the center outward**. There are $4$ members in the center, so the gym-and-yoga and pool-and-yoga regions that exclude the third activity contain $8$ and $2$ members, respectively. The yoga circle contains $24$ members, so $24 - 8 - 2 - 4 = 10$ take yoga only. Choice B, namely $24 - 12 - 6 = 6$, subtracts the pairwise counts without first removing the center, thus subtracting twice the $4$ members who participate in all three activities. Choice D, namely $24 - 8 - 2 = 14$, is obtained by failing to subtract the $4$ members in the center. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Of $50$ participants in a training course, $28$ took the computing module, $22$ the law module, and $20$ the accounting module. There were $10$ who took computing and law, $8$ who took computing and accounting, $7$ who took law and accounting, and $2$ who did not take any module. The pairwise counts include those who took all three modules. How many participants took all three?

- A) $5$
- B) $3$
- C) $2$
- D) $1$
- E) $8$

$$
50 - 2 = 28 + 22 + 20 - 10 - 8 - 7 + x
\iff
48 = 45 + x
\iff
x = 3
$$

::venn{tipo="tre" zone="abc" nomi="I,D,C" elementi="a:13;b:8;c:8;ab:7;ac:5;bc:4;abc:3;u:2" titolo="Participants in the computing ($I$), law ($D$), and accounting ($C$) modules: the $3$ who took all three are in the center"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The unknown is the central count $x = |I \cap D \cap C|$. The number who took at least one module is $50 - 2 = 48$. By the **formula for the union of three sets**, $48 = 45 + x$, so $x = 3$. Filling in the diagram from the center outward gives nonnegative counts in every region. Their sum, including the $2$ people outside the circles, is $50$. Choice A results from forgetting the $2$ people who took no modules and writing $50 = 45 + x$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
