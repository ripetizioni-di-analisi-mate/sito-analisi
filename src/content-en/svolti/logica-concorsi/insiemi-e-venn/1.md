## Exercise 1

Let $A = \{2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12\}$ and $B = \{3, 6, 9, 12\}$. Which of the following is the set $A \cap B$?

- A) $\{2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12\}$
- B) $\{6, 12\}$
- C) $\{2, 4, 8, 10\}$
- D) $\{6\}$
- E) $\{3, 9\}$

::venn{zone="ab" nomi="A,B" elementi="a:2,4,8,10;ab:6,12;b:3,9" titolo="The elements common to $A$ and $B$ are $6$ and $12$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The **intersection** $A \cap B$ contains the elements that belong to both $A$ and $B$. Of the elements of $B$, the numbers $3$ and $9$ do not belong to $A$, while $6$ and $12$ do, so $A \cap B = \{6, 12\}$. Choice A is the union $A \cup B$, and choice C is the difference $A \setminus B$. Choice D omits $12$, the last element in both lists. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Seven coworkers work at an office: Anna, Bruno, Carla, Dario, Elena, Fabio, and Giulia. Anna, Carla, Elena, and Giulia work the morning shift. Bruno, Carla, and Giulia work the Saturday shift. Which coworkers work neither the morning shift nor the Saturday shift?

- A) Bruno
- B) Anna and Elena
- C) Carla and Giulia
- D) Dario and Fabio
- E) Bruno, Dario, and Fabio

::venn{zone="u" nomi="M,S" elementi="a:An,El;ab:Ca,Gi;b:Br;u:Da,Fa" titolo="Coworkers, identified by the first two letters of their names, who work the morning shift ($M$) and the Saturday shift ($S$): Dario and Fabio are outside both sets"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The universal set consists of the seven coworkers. Anyone who works neither the morning shift nor the Saturday shift is outside $M$ and outside $S$ and therefore belongs to the **complement of the union** $\overline{M \cup S}$. The union $M \cup S$ contains Anna, Bruno, Carla, Elena, and Giulia, leaving Dario and Fabio. Choice E is the complement of $M$ alone and therefore also includes Bruno, who works the Saturday shift. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

In the universal set $U = \{1, 2, 3, \ldots, 10\}$, consider $A = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$, $B = \{4, 5, 6, 7, 8\}$, and the set $C$ of even numbers in $U$. Which of the following is the set $(A \cap B) \setminus C$?

- A) $\{5\}$
- B) $\{4, 6\}$
- C) $\{4, 5, 6\}$
- D) $\{2, 4, 5, 6\}$
- E) $\{5, 7\}$

$$
A \cap B = \{4, 5, 6\},
\qquad
C = \{2, 4, 6, 8, 10\},
\qquad
(A \cap B) \setminus C = \{5\}
$$

::venn{tipo="tre" zone="ab" nomi="A,B,C" elementi="a:1,3;b:7;c:10;ab:5;ac:2;bc:8;abc:4,6;u:9" titolo="The shaded region contains the elements of both $A$ and $B$ that do not belong to $C$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

First compute the intersection $A \cap B = \{4, 5, 6\}$. The **difference** then removes the elements that belong to $C$. Since $4$ and $6$ are even, only $5$ remains. Choice B is $(A \cap B) \cap C$, which contains the elements to be removed instead of the ones that remain. Choice C is the intersection $A \cap B$ before taking the difference. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
