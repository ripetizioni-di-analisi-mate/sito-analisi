## Exercise 1

Compute
$$
\int \left( 4x^3 - 2x + \frac{3}{x} \right) dx .
$$

$$
\int \left( 4x^3 - 2x + \frac{3}{x} \right) dx
= 4 \int x^3\, dx - 2 \int x\, dx + 3 \int \frac{1}{x}\, dx
= x^4 - x^2 + 3 \ln|x| + c
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By **linearity** the integral of the sum is the sum of the integrals and multiplicative constants come out of the integral sign. From the table of elementary integrals, the integral of $x^3$ is $\dfrac{x^4}{4}$ and the integral of $x$ is $\dfrac{x^2}{2}$, therefore the first two terms give $x^4$ and $-x^2$. The integral of $\dfrac{1}{x}$ is $\ln|x|$, hence the third term gives $3 \ln|x|$. The three constants of integration are combined into the *single* constant $c$. Differentiating $x^4 - x^2 + 3 \ln|x|$ gives back the integrand, therefore the result is **correct**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Compute
$$
\int x\, e^{x^2}\, dx .
$$

$$
\int x\, e^{x^2}\, dx
= \frac{1}{2} \int 2x\, e^{x^2}\, dx
= \frac{1}{2}\, e^{x^2} + c
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The derivative of the exponent $x^2$ is $2x$ and the factor $x$ appears in the integrand, therefore the integral is **almost immediate** of the form $\displaystyle\int f'\, e^{f}\, dx$ with $f(x) = x^2$. One multiplies and divides by $2$ to reconstruct *exactly* the factor $f'(x) = 2x$; at that point the formula gives $e^{x^2}$, multiplied by the coefficient $\dfrac{1}{2}$ left outside. The check by differentiation gives $\dfrac{1}{2}\, e^{x^2} \cdot 2x = x\, e^{x^2}$, which is the integrand.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Compute
$$
\int \frac{e^x}{1 + e^{2x}}\, dx .
$$

$$
\int \frac{e^x}{1 + e^{2x}}\, dx
= \int \frac{e^x}{1 + (e^x)^2}\, dx
= \arctan(e^x) + c
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Since $e^{2x} = (e^x)^2$, the denominator can be written as $1 + (e^x)^2$. The numerator $e^x$ is the **derivative** of $e^x$, therefore the integral has the form $\displaystyle\int \dfrac{f'}{1 + f^2}\, dx$ with $f(x) = e^x$, whose result is $\arctan f(x)$. Differentiating $\arctan(e^x)$ gives $\dfrac{e^x}{1 + e^{2x}}$, hence the result is **correct**.

</div>
