## Exercise 1

Find the values of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ for which the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y = 3 \\[4pt]
2x - y = 0 \\[4pt]
x + ky = 5
\end{cases}
$$
is consistent and, for those values, solve it.

$$
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 1 \\
2 & -1
\end{vmatrix}
= -3 \neq 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{rk} A = 2 \ \text{ for every } k
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\det (A \mid b) =
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 1 & 3 \\
2 & -1 & 0 \\
1 & k & 5
\end{vmatrix}
&= 1(-5 - 0) - 1(10 - 0) + 3(2k + 1) \\[6pt]
&= 6k - 12
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) =
\begin{cases}
3 & \text{if } k \neq 2 \\[4pt]
2 & \text{if } k = 2
\end{cases}
$$
$$
\text{for } k = 2
\qquad
\begin{cases}
x + y = 3 \\[4pt]
2x - y = 0
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
3x = 3, \quad x = 1, \quad y = 2
$$

::grafico{f="3-x;2*x;(5-x)/2" punti="1,2,pieno" etichette="2.6,-1.4,x + y = 3;0.3,-2.2,2x − y = 0;2.7,2.6,x + 2y = 5" xmin=-1.5 xmax=4.5 ymin=-2.5 ymax=4 uguale=1 titolo="For $k = 2$ the lines $x + y = 3$, $2x - y = 0$ and $x + 2y = 5$ all pass through the point $(1, 2)$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The system has three equations in two unknowns, so $\operatorname{rk} A \leq 2$, and the minor of the first two rows equals $-3$, therefore $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$ for every $k$. The augmented matrix is square of order $3$: if $\det (A \mid b) \neq 0$, its rank is $3$ and by the **Rouché-Capelli theorem** the system is inconsistent. The expansion along the first row gives $6k - 12$, which vanishes only for $k = 2$. For $k = 2$ the minor of $A$ already found is nonzero, so $\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 2$; the system is consistent and, since the common rank equals the number of unknowns, the solution is unique. The first two equations, added, give $3x = 3$, so $x = 1$ and $y = 2$; the third is satisfied, because $1 + 2 \cdot 2 = 5$. The system **has the unique solution** $(1, 2)$ for $k = 2$ and is inconsistent for every other value.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Discuss, as $k \in \mathbb{R}$ varies, the system in the unknowns $x_1, x_2, x_3, x_4$
$$
\begin{cases}
x_1 + x_2 + x_3 + x_4 = 1 \\[4pt]
x_1 + 2x_2 + 3x_3 + 4x_4 = 2 \\[4pt]
2x_1 + 3x_2 + 4x_3 + k x_4 = 4
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\left(\begin{array}{cccc|c}
1 & 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\
1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 2 \\
2 & 3 & 4 & k & 4
\end{array}\right)
\xrightarrow[R_3 \to R_3 - 2R_1]{R_2 \to R_2 - R_1}
\left(\begin{array}{cccc|c}
1 & 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\
0 & 1 & 2 & 3 & 1 \\
0 & 1 & 2 & k - 2 & 2
\end{array}\right)
$$
$$
\xrightarrow{R_3 \to R_3 - R_2}
\left(\begin{array}{cccc|c}
1 & 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\
0 & 1 & 2 & 3 & 1 \\
0 & 0 & 0 & k - 5 & 1
\end{array}\right)
$$
$$
\text{for } k = 5
\qquad
(0 \ \ 0 \ \ 0 \ \ 0 \mid 1)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{rk} A = 2, \ \operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 3
$$
$$
\text{for } k \neq 5
\qquad
x_3 = t, \quad
x_4 = \frac{1}{k - 5}, \quad
x_2 = 1 - 2t - \frac{3}{k - 5} = \frac{k - 8}{k - 5} - 2t
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
x_1 &= 1 - x_2 - x_3 - x_4 \\[6pt]
&= 1 - \frac{k - 8}{k - 5} + 2t - t - \frac{1}{k - 5} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{2}{k - 5} + t
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
(x_1, x_2, x_3, x_4) =
\left( \frac{2}{k - 5} + t, \ \frac{k - 8}{k - 5} - 2t, \ t, \ \frac{1}{k - 5} \right),
\qquad t \in \mathbb{R}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The system has three equations and four unknowns, so the determinant of $A$ is not defined and the ranks are computed by reduction to echelon form. The first two operations do not involve the parameter; after $R_3 \to R_3 - R_2$ the last row is $(0 \ 0 \ 0 \ \ k - 5 \mid 1)$. For $k = 5$ this row becomes $(0 \ 0 \ 0 \ 0 \mid 1)$, which corresponds to the equation $0 = 1$; hence $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$, $\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 3$ and the system is **inconsistent**. For $k \neq 5$ the matrix has three pivots, in the columns of $x_1$, $x_2$ and $x_4$, therefore both ranks equal $3$ and the system has $\infty^{4 - 3} = \infty^1$ solutions, with $x_3 = t$ free. The third equation, $(k - 5)x_4 = 1$, can be divided by $k - 5$ precisely because $k \neq 5$; the other unknowns follow by back substitution.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Discuss, as $k \in \mathbb{R}$ varies, the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y - z = 1 \\[4pt]
x + 2y + z = k \\[4pt]
2x + 3y = k^2 - 1
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\det A =
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 1 & -1 \\
1 & 2 & 1 \\
2 & 3 & 0
\end{vmatrix}
= 0 \ \text{ for every } k,
\qquad
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 1 \\
1 & 2
\end{vmatrix}
= 1 \neq 0
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 1 & 1 \\
1 & 2 & k \\
2 & 3 & k^2 - 1
\end{vmatrix}
&= \bigl( 2(k^2 - 1) - 3k \bigr) - \bigl( (k^2 - 1) - 2k \bigr) + (3 - 4) \\[6pt]
&= k^2 - k - 2 = (k - 2)(k + 1)
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\text{for } k = 2
\qquad
z = t
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\begin{cases}
x + y = 1 + t \\[4pt]
x + 2y = 2 - t
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
(x, y, z) = (3t, \ 1 - 2t, \ t)
$$
$$
\text{for } k = -1
\qquad
z = t
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\begin{cases}
x + y = 1 + t \\[4pt]
x + 2y = -1 - t
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
(x, y, z) = (3 + 3t, \ -2 - 2t, \ t)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The third row of $A$ is the sum of the first two, so $\det A = 0$ for *every* value of $k$ and Cramer's rule never applies. The minor of the first two rows and columns equals $1$, therefore $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$. In the augmented matrix there are two bordered minors of this minor: the one with the column of $z$ is $\det A = 0$, the one with the column of constant terms equals $(k - 2)(k + 1)$. If $k \neq 2$ and $k \neq -1$, then $\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 3 \neq \operatorname{rk} A$ and the system is **inconsistent**. If $k = 2$ or $k = -1$, both bordered minors are zero and by the **bordered minors theorem** $\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 2$, so the system has $\infty^1$ solutions. To solve it, the first two equations are kept and we set $z = t$; subtracting the first from the second gives $y = k - 1 - 2t$, and the first gives $x = 1 + t - y = 2 - k + 3t$, which for $k = 2$ and $k = -1$ yields the solutions shown.

</div>
