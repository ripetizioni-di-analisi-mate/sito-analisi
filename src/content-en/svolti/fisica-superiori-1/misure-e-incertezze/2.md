## Exercise 1

An analogue ruler's smallest division is $0.7\,\mathrm{{cm}}$. Using half a division as the reading uncertainty, what uncertainty should be assigned?

::grafico{assi=0 xmin=-0.2 xmax=1.6 ymin=-0.5 ymax=1.4 linee="0,0 1.4,0;0,-0.12 0,0.12;0.7,-0.12 0.7,0.12;1.4,-0.12 1.4,0.12" punti="0.35,0,pieno,P" etichette="0,0.42,0 cm;0.7,0.42,0.7 cm;1.4,0.42,1.4 cm" titolo="P is midway across the first ruler division"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta l &= \dfrac{0.7}2\\[6pt]
&= 0.35\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The problem specifies half of the smallest division for one reading. Divide the division size by two and keep its unit. The smallest division is the interval between two marks, so distinguish the scale step from the uncertainty assigned when using it. Thus $\Delta l=\dfrac{0.7}2=0.35\,\mathrm{cm}$.

Half a division is the distance from a mark to the midpoint of the adjacent gap. For a reading of $3.50\,\mathrm{cm}$, for example, the rule gives the interval from $3.15$ to $3.85\,\mathrm{cm}$. Its width is $0.70\,\mathrm{cm}$, one division. The value $0.35\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the radius applied on each side of the reading.

</div>

## Exercise 2

There are $10$ equal intervals between $0$ and $45\,\mathrm N$ on a scale. What is the value of each division?

$$
\dfrac{45}{10} = 4.5\,\mathrm N
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The scale span is the final value minus the initial value. Divide this span by the number of equal intervals. The marks bound those intervals, so counting the marks at both endpoints as well would give one too many. One division equals $\dfrac{45}{10}=4.5\,\mathrm N$.

The sixth mark after zero is at $6\cdot4.5=27\,\mathrm N$. The tenth is at $10\cdot4.5=45\,\mathrm N$, as required by the dial's upper limit. Counting the initial mark as well gives eleven positions. This check separates the number of positions on the scale from the number of steps between them.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A measurement is $l=(17.0\pm0.7)\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the lower endpoint of the associated interval?

$$
l_{\min}=(17.0-0.7)\,\mathrm{cm}=16.3\,\mathrm{cm}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The central value is halfway along the symmetric interval. The absolute uncertainty gives the distance from the centre to each endpoint. For the lower endpoint, subtract it from the centre to obtain the position further left on the scale. The upper endpoint would be obtained by adding it. The lower endpoint is $l_{\min}=(17.0-0.7)\,\mathrm{cm}=16.3\,\mathrm{cm}$.

The other endpoint is $17.0+0.7=17.7\,\mathrm{cm}$. The entire range from $16.3$ to $17.7\,\mathrm{cm}$ has width $1.4\,\mathrm{cm}$, twice $0.7\,\mathrm{cm}$. This check confirms that the subtraction used the stated radius and that the reported value lies to the left of the centre on the scale.

</div>
