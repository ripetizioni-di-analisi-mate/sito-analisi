## Exercise 1

Two opposite vertical forces of $20\,\mathrm N$ act along lines $0.25\,\mathrm m$ apart. The left one is upward. With anticlockwise positive, what is the couple's moment?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-2,-2 -2,2;2,-2 2,2" vettori="-2,0,-2,1.5,F;2,0,2,-1.5,F" etichette="0,0.3,0.25 m" xmin=-3 xmax=3 ymin=-2.5 ymax=2.5 titolo="The couple produces a clockwise moment about any reference point"}

$$
M_c=-Fd=-20\cdot0.25\,\mathrm{N\,m}=-5\,\mathrm{N\,m}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The left force lifts that side while the right force lowers it, so the tendency is clockwise. Choosing a point on the left line eliminates that force's moment and the right force acts a quarter-metre away. The total resultant is still zero.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A system has zero resultant and a clockwise moment of $12\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. To balance it an anticlockwise couple is added with its lines $0.4\,\mathrm m$ apart. What must each force be?

$$
F=\frac{12\,\mathrm{N\,m}}{0.4\,\mathrm m}=30\,\mathrm N.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The second couple has zero resultant, preserving the force balance. It must produce a moment of magnitude twelve and direction opposite to the original. Dividing by the distance between the lines gives thirty newtons for each force.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A rigid body's force system has vertical resultant $8\,\mathrm N$ and zero moment about O. Which single correction restores both balances?

An opposite $8\,\mathrm N$ force along a line through O.

<div class="spiegazione">

Cancel the resultant without creating a new moment. The opposing force cancels the resultant. For zero moment, its line of action must contain O. A lateral displacement would create a moment arm and spoil the second balance.

</div>
