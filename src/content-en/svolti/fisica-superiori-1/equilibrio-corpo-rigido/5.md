## Exercise 1

A light $6\,\mathrm m$ beam is supported at ends A and B. A load of $90\,\mathrm N$ is $2\,\mathrm m$ from A. What is the vertical reaction difference $R_A-R_B$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="0,0 6,0;-0.25,-0.5 0,0 0.25,-0.5;-0.2,-0.65 0.2,-0.65;5.75,-0.5 6,0 6.25,-0.5;5.8,-0.65 6.2,-0.65" vettori="2,0,2,-2.4,P;0,0,0,1.6,R_A;6,0,6,0.8,R_B" etichette="0,-1,A;6,-1,B;3,0.38,6 m" xmin=-1 xmax=7.2 ymin=-3 ymax=2.4 titolo="The load is 2 m from A. Taking moments about A eliminates the moment of R_A"}

$$
\begin{aligned}R_B&=\frac{90\cdot2}{6}\,\mathrm N=30\,\mathrm N\\[6pt]R_A-R_B&=(90-30)-30\,\mathrm N=30\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The moments about A contain only the load and reaction B. Reaction A supplies the rest of the weight support, or sixty newtons. Subtracting shows that the support closer to the load carries thirty newtons more.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A uniform beam $4\,\mathrm m$ long weighs $40\,\mathrm N$ and rests on supports at its ends A and B. Two downward loads act with magnitudes $60\,\mathrm N$ at $1\,\mathrm m$ from A and $20\,\mathrm N$ at $3\,\mathrm m$ from A. What is the vertical reaction $R_B$?

$$
R_B=\frac{40\cdot2+60\cdot1+20\cdot3}{4}\,\mathrm N=50\,\mathrm N.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Apply the beam's own weight at its midpoint. All three loads give clockwise moments about A, so add their weight–distance products. Reaction B counters the moment four metres away. The vertical balance gives seventy newtons at A and checks the total load of one hundred and twenty.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A light $5\,\mathrm m$ beam supports $100\,\mathrm N$ between its supports. B can supply a reaction of at most $40\,\mathrm N$. What is the greatest distance from A to the load?

$$
100a\leq40\cdot5\quad\Longrightarrow\quad a_{\max}=2\,\mathrm m.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Moving the weight away from A increases its moment about A and therefore the B reaction required. The limit at B determines the largest arm. Then A carries sixty newtons and the load is still between the supports.

</div>
