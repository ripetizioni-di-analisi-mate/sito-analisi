## Exercise 1

A horizontal bar receives $10\,\mathrm N$ downward $0.2\,\mathrm m$ left of O and $5\,\mathrm N$ downward $0.3\,\mathrm m$ right of O. With anticlockwise positive, what is the total moment?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-2,0 3,0" vettori="-2,0,-2,-2,10 N;3,0,3,-1,5 N" punti="0,0,pieno,O" etichette="-1,0.4,0.2 m;1.6,0.4,0.3 m" xmin=-3.1 xmax=4 ymin=-2.7 ymax=1.6 titolo="Both forces point down. Their positions relative to O give opposite signs to their moments"}

$$
\sum M_O=(10\cdot0.2-5\cdot0.3)\,\mathrm{N\,m}=+0.5\,\mathrm{N\,m}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

On the left, the downward push tends to lift the right end and makes a positive anticlockwise moment. On the right, a downward force has the opposite effect. Subtract the products, leaving a small anticlockwise excess.

As a check, use the point of application of the $5\,\mathrm N$ force as the reference. That force has zero lever arm and the other acts $0.5\,\mathrm m$ to the left, so the moment about the new point is $+5\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. It differs by $4.5\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ from the moment about O, as expected for a downward resultant of $15\,\mathrm N$ and a shift of $0.3\,\mathrm m$ to the right.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A nonzero force is moved along its own line of action without changing sense or magnitude. Reference point O is fixed. How does the moment change?

$$
\lvert M_O\rvert=Fb.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The moment arm is measured to the line, which stays the same. The tendency to turn also depends on the line and the sense of the force, neither of which changes. Moving a force along that line preserves both its force and moment contributions to the rigid body.

For example, a horizontal force of $20\,\mathrm N$ to the right on the line $y=0.40\,\mathrm m$ has moment $-8\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ about the origin regardless of the horizontal coordinate of its point of application. Moving it to $y=0.50\,\mathrm m$ would change the moment to $-10\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ because that is a different line of action.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A bar receives $9\,\mathrm N$ upward $0.4\,\mathrm m$ right of O and $6\,\mathrm N$ downward $0.1\,\mathrm m$ left of O. What is the total moment with anticlockwise positive?

$$
\sum M_O=[(0.4)(9)+(-0.1)(-6)]\,\mathrm{N\,m}=+4.2\,\mathrm{N\,m}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The forces oppose each other but are also on opposite sides of the pivot. Both tend to turn the bar the same way. Check signs with $M=xF_y$ using a negative coordinate on the left.

The force on the right contributes $+3.6\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ and the force on the left $+0.6\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. The latter contribution is positive because both the coordinate and the force component are negative. In the force sum, by contrast, the vectors have opposite signs and give $9-6=3\,\mathrm N$ upwards.

</div>
