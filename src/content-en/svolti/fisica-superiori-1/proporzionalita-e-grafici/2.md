## Exercise 1

Two positive quantities are inversely proportional. When $x=7$, an observation gives $y=9$. What is the constant $c$ in the rule $y=\dfrac{c}{x}$?

::grafico{f="63/x" xmin=3 xmax=14 ymin=0 ymax=21 punti="7,9,pieno,P" titolo="The curve contains the positive pairs with product 63"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
c &= xy\\[6pt]
&= 7\cdot9\\[6pt]
&= 63
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The product is constant when two positive quantities are inversely proportional. Multiply the first and second coordinates to find that product. The factors change for other pairs but must give the same value. Their ratio would instead characterize a direct-proportionality prediction. Therefore $c=xy=7\cdot9=63$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The law is $y=\dfrac{42}x$, with $x>0$. What is $y$ when $x=3$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
y &= \dfrac{42}3\\[6pt]
&= 14
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the inverse law the new horizontal value goes in the denominator. The numerator is the constant product preserved for every allowed pair. Divide it by the given horizontal value. Multiplying the result by the horizontal value recovers the constant product and checks the calculation. The calculation gives $y=\dfrac{42}3=14$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The law is $xy=84$ with $x,y>0$. What value of $x$ corresponds to $y=4$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
x &= \dfrac{84}4\\[6pt]
&= 21
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The equation gives the constant product and one of its factors. Since the supplied vertical value is positive, division by it does not lose any solutions. It gives the horizontal factor which, multiplied by the vertical one, recovers the required product. Therefore $x=\dfrac{84}4=21$.

</div>
