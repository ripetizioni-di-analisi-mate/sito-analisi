## Exercise 1

A table contains the pairs $(1;7), (2;14), (3;21)$. Which model describes all of the data?

- $y=7x$
- $y=7\div x$
- $y=x+7$
- $y=7x^2$

::grafico{f="7*x" xmin=0 xmax=4 ymin=0 ymax=29 punti="1,7,pieno;2,14,pieno;3,21,pieno" titolo="All pairs lie on the same line through the origin"}

$$
y = 7x
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For direct proportionality, every vertical value must be its horizontal value times the same number. Divide each vertical value by its own horizontal value to obtain the same coefficient. The resulting law passes through each listed pair, and the origin belongs to its model line. All ratios are $7$, so the points satisfy $y=7x$.

Dividing $7$ by $1$, $14$ by $2$ and $21$ by $3$ always gives seven. Each measured point can therefore be reconstructed by multiplying its horizontal coordinate by seven. The line through the origin is the compatible law among the choices. Experiments with uncertain readings would also require checking the deviations from the line.

</div>

## Exercise 2

On a graph of mass in grams against volume in cubic centimetres, a line has numerical slope $7$. What is the unit of this slope?

- $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\mathrm{cm^3/g}$
- $\mathrm{g\,cm^3}$
- $\mathrm g$

$$
\dfrac{\mathrm g}{\mathrm{cm}^3}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Take differences of vertical values in grams and horizontal values in cubic centimetres. Dividing the first difference by the second gives the slope. The units undergo the same division. In this graph the ratio gives the increase in mass per unit volume. The unit is $\dfrac{\mathrm g}{\mathrm{cm}^3}$.

Slope seven means an increase of $1\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ corresponds along the line to an increase of $7\,\mathrm g$. Swapping the axes would reverse the division, placing cubic centimetres in the numerator. The required unit therefore depends on the quantity assigned to each axis.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Each horizontal grid square represents $7\,\mathrm{cm}$ and each vertical square represents $9\,\mathrm N$. A line rises 3 squares while going right 2 squares. What is its slope in $\mathrm{N/cm}$?

::grafico{f="27*x/14" xmin=0 xmax=20 ymin=0 ymax=40 punti="0,0,pieno;14,27,pieno,P" segmenti="0,0,14,0;14,0,14,27" nomi="x (cm),F (N)" titolo="Three vertical squares are 27 N. Two horizontal squares are 14 cm"}

$$
\dfrac{3\cdot9}{2\cdot7} = \dfrac{27}{14}\,\mathrm{N/cm}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Find the vertical increase in newtons by multiplying the number of squares by the vertical scale. Find the horizontal increase in centimetres using its own scale. The ratio of the two physical changes gives the slope. Using the ratio of squares alone would omit the different scales. The slope is $\dfrac{3\cdot9}{2\cdot7}=\dfrac{27}{14}\,\mathrm{N/cm}$.

Dividing the counts alone gives $\dfrac32$, which is not yet a force change per centimetre. Two horizontal intervals represent fourteen centimetres, and three vertical intervals represent twenty-seven newtons. Both parts of the ratio must use the labelled units, and the sign is positive because the line rises to the right.

</div>
