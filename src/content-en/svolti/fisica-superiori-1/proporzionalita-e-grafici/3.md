## Exercise 1

A line passes through $(1;8)$ and $(4;35)$. What is its slope?

::grafico{f="9*x-1" xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=-2 ymax=46 punti="1,8,pieno,P;4,35,pieno,Q" segmenti="1,8,4,8;4,8,4,35" titolo="Find the slope from the changes between P and Q"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= \dfrac{35-8}{4-1}\\[6pt]
&= 9
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Slope is the increase in vertical value divided by the increase in horizontal value. Subtract the first vertical value from the second and keep the same order for the horizontal values. This describes the same move in both directions. A positive result describes an increasing line and gives its rise per horizontal unit. The calculation gives $m=\dfrac{35-8}{4-1}=9$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The line $y=7x+q$ passes through the point $(2;23)$. What is its intercept $q$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
q &= (23)-7\cdot2\\[6pt]
&= 9
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The given vertical value contains the contribution that grows with the horizontal coordinate and the fixed contribution. Moving the proportional part to the other side of the equation leaves only the intercept. The model uses it as the value at zero. Adding back the proportional part at the given coordinate checks it. Substituting the point gives $q=(23)-7\cdot2=9$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A graph is given by $y=63-7x$. At what value of $x$ does it meet the horizontal axis?

$$
x = 9
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The vertical coordinate vanishes on the horizontal axis. Set the expression for that coordinate to zero. The constant term is positive and the slope is negative, so a positive horizontal coordinate is needed to offset the initial term. Substitution into the expression gives a zero vertical value. Setting $0=63-7x$ gives $x=9$.

</div>
