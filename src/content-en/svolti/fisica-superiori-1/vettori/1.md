## Exercise 1

Two vectors have horizontal components $u_x=7\,\mathrm N$ and $v_x=9\,\mathrm N$. What is the horizontal component of their sum?

::grafico{assi=0 xmin=-1 xmax=18 ymin=-0.7 ymax=1.8 vettori="0,0,7,0,uₓ;7,0,16,0,vₓ" punti="0,0,pieno;7,0,pieno;16,0,pieno" etichette="3.5,0.45,7 N;11.5,0.45,9 N" titolo="Only the horizontal projections are known. The two contributions add along their line"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
(u+v)_x &= u_x+v_x\\[6pt]
&= 7+9\\[6pt]
&= 16\,\mathrm N
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Add the two horizontal components to find the resultant's horizontal component. Both are positive, so the projection of the resultant points in the positive direction along the horizontal axis. The vertical components would be needed to find the resultant's vertical component or magnitude. Thus $(u+v)_x=u_x+v_x=7+9=16\,\mathrm N$.

To draw the arrows along the horizontal axis, start the second seven units from the first arrow's tail. The final tip would be at sixteen. Finding the whole resultant would still require the vertical components. In particular, its magnitude would be sixteen only if the vertical sum were zero.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Two vectors have vertical components $u_y=7\,\mathrm N$ and $v_y=-9\,\mathrm N$. What is the vertical component of their sum?

$$
\begin{aligned}
(u+v)_y &= 7+(-9)\\[6pt]
&= -2\,\mathrm N
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two terms are signed components. Add the negative component with its sign, cancelling part of the positive one. A negative balance means the vertical result points opposite the positive axis, while its magnitude along that axis is the absolute value. The calculation gives $(u+v)_y=7+(-9)=-2\,\mathrm N$.

Two newtons is the part of the downward component that the upward one does not cancel. Adding the magnitudes would give sixteen newtons, which would apply if both contributions pointed the same way. Since the question asks for a signed component, the answer keeps the minus sign and shows which direction prevails.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Given $u_x=7\,\mathrm m$ and $v_x=-9\,\mathrm m$, what is the $x$ component of $u-v$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
(u-v)_x &= 7-(-9)\\[6pt]
&= 16\,\mathrm m
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Subtracting a vector means adding its opposite. On the horizontal axis alone, reverse the sign of the second vector's component and add it to the first. The originally negative component becomes positive. Adding the second vector's component to the result checks that the first component is recovered. Therefore $(u-v)_x=7-(-9)=16\,\mathrm m$.

With a common tail, the horizontal component of $v$ puts its tip nine metres to the left. The tip of $u$ is seven metres to the right. An arrow from the tip of $v$ to that of $u$ therefore advances sixteen metres. The algebraic subtraction $7-(-9)$ gives the same value.

</div>
