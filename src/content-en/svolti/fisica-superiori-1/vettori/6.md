## Exercise 1

Which description fits vectors $u=(7;0)\,\mathrm N$ and $v=(0;7)\,\mathrm N$?

- Perpendicular with equal magnitudes
- Equal
- Opposite
- Parallel and pointing the same way

::grafico{uguale=1 xmin=-1 xmax=8 ymin=-1 ymax=8 vettori="0,0,7,0,u;0,0,0,7,v" nomi="x (N),y (N)" punti="7,0,pieno;0,7,pieno" titolo="Both arrows are 7 N long along perpendicular axes"}

Perpendicular with equal magnitudes.

<div class="spiegazione">

The first vector has only a nonzero horizontal component. The second has only a nonzero vertical component. Their lines are perpendicular. The nonzero components have equal sizes, which give their respective magnitudes. Equal magnitudes can therefore occur for differently oriented arrows. Both have magnitude $7\,\mathrm N$ and their directions differ.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Vector $u$ has components $(8;-10)\,\mathrm N$. What are the components of $-u$?

- $(-8;10)\,\mathrm N$
- $(8;10)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-8;-10)\,\mathrm N$
- $(10;-8)\,\mathrm N$

$$
(-8;10)\,\mathrm N
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The opposite arrow has the same length but points the other way along the same line. This reverses the effect along each axis, so both signs change. Each pair of corresponding components adds to zero, confirming that the vectors cancel. Changing both signs gives $(-8;10)\,\mathrm N$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A vector has components $(8;10)\,\mathrm m$. What components does the vector have after multiplication by 3?

- $(24;30)\,\mathrm m$
- $(11;13)\,\mathrm m$
- $(24;10)\,\mathrm m$
- $(8;30)\,\mathrm m$

$$
(3\cdot8;3\cdot10) = (24;30)\,\mathrm m
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The same multiplier acts on both components, keeping horizontal and vertical distinct. The positive scalar triples the arrow's length while preserving its sense. Their ratio therefore stays the same, confirming the same tilt in the plane. Its components become $(3\cdot8;3\cdot10)=(24;30)\,\mathrm m$.

</div>
