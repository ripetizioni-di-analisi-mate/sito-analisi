## Exercise 1

In the Cartesian plane, $A=(7;9)\,\mathrm m$ and $B=(28;18)\,\mathrm m$. What are the components of the vector from A to B?

- $(21;9)\,\mathrm m$
- $(-21;-9)\,\mathrm m$
- $(35;27)\,\mathrm m$
- $(28;18)\,\mathrm m$

::grafico{uguale=1 xmin=0 xmax=32 ymin=0 ymax=24 vettori="7,9,28,18" punti="7,9,pieno;28,18,pieno,B" etichette="6.2,10.0,A;17.5,14.45,AB" segmenti="7,9,28,9;28,9,28,18" nomi="x (m),y (m)" titolo="Displacement uses the horizontal and vertical differences from A to B"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
AB &= (28-7;18-9)\\[6pt]
&= (21;9)\,\mathrm m
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The vector describes the difference between the finishing point and starting point. Subtract A's horizontal coordinate from B's and repeat for the vertical coordinate. The results are changes, rather than the final position's coordinates. Adding them to A's coordinates reaches B exactly. Therefore $AB=(28-7;18-9)=(21;9)\,\mathrm m$.

Check the direction by adding the calculated components to the initial coordinates. Along $x$ this gives $7+21=28$ and along $y$ it gives $9+9=18$. This reveals subtraction in the wrong order, which would give the return vector from B to A. Both checks must reproduce the same destination.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A resultant force has components $R_x=-7\,\mathrm N$ and $R_y=9\,\mathrm N$. Which way does it point?

- Left and up
- Right and up
- Left and down
- Right and down

Left and up.

<div class="spiegazione">

With right and up positive, the negative horizontal component points left. The positive vertical component points up. Both are nonzero, so the resultant is in the second quadrant. Along the same line but in the opposite sense, both signs would change. The positive vertical component $9\,\mathrm N$ points up.

With the tail at the origin, the tip is in the quadrant with negative horizontal coordinate and positive vertical coordinate. The larger absolute value of the vertical component does not remove the horizontal part, so the arrow is diagonal. With a vertical axis pointing downwards, the second component would become negative without changing the physical direction.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A vector has magnitude $35\,\mathrm N$. Its positive $x$ component is three fifths of the magnitude. What is the component?

$$
\begin{aligned}
u_x &= \dfrac35\cdot35\\[6pt]
&= 21\,\mathrm N
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The horizontal component is the arrow's signed projection. It is positive and accounts for three parts of every five of the full magnitude, as specified. Multiply the magnitude by three fifths. The result is smaller than the magnitude, as expected for a projection onto one axis. The calculation gives $u_x=\dfrac35\cdot35=21\,\mathrm N$.

The force stays in newtons because $\dfrac35$ is a dimensionless ratio. The calculation gives three groups of seven newtons. It also satisfies the geometric bound, since twenty-one is less than thirty-five. Knowing the magnitude and the horizontal component alone does not determine the sign of any vertical component.

</div>
