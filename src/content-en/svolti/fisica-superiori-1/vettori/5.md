## Exercise 1

In the Cartesian plane, $A=(7;9)\,\mathrm m$ and $B=(28;18)\,\mathrm m$. What are the components of the vector from A to B?

- $(21;9)\,\mathrm m$
- $(-21;-9)\,\mathrm m$
- $(35;27)\,\mathrm m$
- $(28;18)\,\mathrm m$

::grafico{uguale=1 xmin=0 xmax=32 ymin=0 ymax=24 vettori="7,9,28,18,AB" punti="7,9,pieno,A;28,18,pieno,B" segmenti="7,9,28,9;28,9,28,18" nomi="x (m),y (m)" titolo="Displacement uses the horizontal and vertical differences from A to B"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
AB &= (28-7;18-9)\\[6pt]
&= (21;9)\,\mathrm m
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The vector describes the difference between the finishing point and starting point. Subtract A's horizontal coordinate from B's and repeat for the vertical coordinate. The results are changes, rather than the final position's coordinates. Adding them to A's coordinates reaches B exactly. Therefore $AB=(28-7;18-9)=(21;9)\,\mathrm m$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A resultant force has components $R_x=-7\,\mathrm N$ and $R_y=9\,\mathrm N$. Which way does it point?

- Left and up
- Right and up
- Left and down
- Right and down

Left and up.

<div class="spiegazione">

With right and up positive, the negative horizontal component points left. The positive vertical component points up. Both are nonzero, so the resultant is in the second quadrant. Along the same line but in the opposite sense, both signs would change. The positive vertical component $9\,\mathrm N$ points up.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A vector has magnitude $35\,\mathrm N$. Its positive $x$ component is three fifths of the magnitude. What is the component?

$$
\begin{aligned}
u_x &= \dfrac35\cdot35\\[6pt]
&= 21\,\mathrm N
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The horizontal component is the arrow's signed projection. It is positive and accounts for three parts of every five of the full magnitude, as specified. Multiply the magnitude by three fifths. The result is smaller than the magnitude, as expected for a projection onto one axis. The calculation gives $u_x=\dfrac35\cdot35=21\,\mathrm N$.

</div>
