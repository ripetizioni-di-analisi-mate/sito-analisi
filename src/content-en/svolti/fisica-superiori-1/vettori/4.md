## Exercise 1

Two collinear forces of $21$ and $14\,\mathrm N$ both point right. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

::grafico{assi=0 xmin=-2 xmax=39 ymin=-0.8 ymax=1.9 vettori="0,0,21,0,F₁;21,0,35,0,F₂" punti="0,0,pieno;21,0,pieno;35,0,pieno" etichette="10.5,0.54,21 N;28,0.54,14 N" titolo="The tip-to-tail construction adds the full lengths of both rightward forces"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
R &= 21+14\\[6pt]
&= 35\,\mathrm N
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Both forces lie on the same line. Taking right as positive, both components are positive and add. Since the result is positive, its magnitude equals its component's numerical value and the arrow still points right. Two aligned vectors do not require a right triangle. Thus $R=21+14=35\,\mathrm N$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Two collinear forces are $35\,\mathrm N$ to the right and $14\,\mathrm N$ to the left. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

$$
35-14 = 21\,\mathrm N
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Assign a positive sign to the rightward force and a negative sign to the leftward one. The resultant component is a difference of the magnitudes. The rightward force is larger, so the balance points right. Its magnitude is the absolute value of this balance in the same force unit. The magnitude is $35-14=21\,\mathrm N$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

On a rightward-oriented axis, the successive displacements are $28\,\mathrm m$ right, $14\,\mathrm m$ left, and $7\,\mathrm m$ right. What is the signed net displacement?

$$
\begin{aligned}
s &= 28-14+7\\[6pt]
&= 21\,\mathrm m
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first and final contributions increase the coordinate, while the middle one decreases it. Adding all three with these signs gives the displacement from start to finish. Total distance would be larger because every section adds positively. The question asks for the signed change in position. Therefore $s=28-14+7=21\,\mathrm m$.

</div>
