## Exercise 1

Vector $u$ has components $(7;-9)\,\mathrm N$. What are the components of $-u$?

- $(-7;9)\,\mathrm N$
- $(7;9)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-7;-9)\,\mathrm N$
- $(9;-7)\,\mathrm N$

::grafico{uguale=1 xmin=-10 xmax=10 ymin=-11 ymax=11 vettori="0,0,7,-9,u;0,0,-7,9,−u" nomi="x (N),y (N)" punti="7,-9,pieno;-7,9,pieno" titolo="Reversing both signs sends the tip to the opposite quadrant"}

$$
(-7;9)\,\mathrm N
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The opposite arrow has the same length but points the other way along the same line. This reverses the effect along each axis, so both signs change. Each pair of corresponding components adds to zero, confirming that the vectors cancel. Changing both signs gives $(-7;9)\,\mathrm N$.

Check by adding the two arrows. The horizontal contribution is $7-7=0$ and the vertical one is $-9+9=0$. Reversing only the horizontal sign would leave both vertical components negative. With a common tail, the correct tips lie on opposite sides of the origin and at the same distance.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A vector has components $(7;9)\,\mathrm m$. What components does the vector have after multiplication by 3?

- $(21;27)\,\mathrm m$
- $(10;12)\,\mathrm m$
- $(21;9)\,\mathrm m$
- $(7;27)\,\mathrm m$

$$
(3\cdot7;3\cdot9) = (21;27)\,\mathrm m
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The same multiplier acts on both components, keeping horizontal and vertical distinct. The positive scalar triples the arrow's length while preserving its sense. Their ratio therefore stays the same, confirming the same tilt in the plane. Its components become $(3\cdot7;3\cdot9)=(21;27)\,\mathrm m$.

Imagine joining three copies of the same arrow tip to tail. Each advances seven metres and rises nine, so both changes triple together. Since the factor is positive, the direction stays the same. The ratio $\dfrac{27}{21}=\dfrac97$ checks this feature of the answer.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A vector $u=(7;-9)\,\mathrm N$ is multiplied by $-2$. What is the result?

- $(-14;18)\,\mathrm N$
- $(14;-18)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-7;9)\,\mathrm N$
- $(5;-11)\,\mathrm N$

$$
-2u = (-14;18)\,\mathrm N
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Multiplication by minus two acts once on the horizontal component and once on the vertical. The initially positive component becomes negative and the other becomes positive. This is a half turn of the arrow together with doubling its length. The multiplier is dimensionless, so components remain in newtons. The calculation gives $-2u=(-14;18)\,\mathrm N$.

The initial magnitude is $\sqrt{7^2+(-9)^2}\,\mathrm N=\sqrt{130}\,\mathrm N$. The final magnitude is $\sqrt{(-14)^2+18^2}\,\mathrm N=2\sqrt{130}\,\mathrm N$, since both squares are four times the original values. The factor $-2$ reverses the direction and doubles the length. The signs show an arrow pointing left and up.

</div>
