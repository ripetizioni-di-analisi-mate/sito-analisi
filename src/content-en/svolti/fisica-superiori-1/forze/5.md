## Exercise 1

A belt moves right faster than a parcel sliding on it. In which direction does the belt exert friction on the parcel?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-3,0 4,0" poligoni="-0.8,0 0.8,0 0.8,0.7 -0.8,0.7" vettori="0,0.35,2,0.35,f_d" xmin=-4 xmax=5 ymin=-1 ymax=2 titolo="The body slides left relative to the belt. Friction on the body points right"}

$$
v_{\mathrm{pacco/nastro}}=v_{\mathrm{pacco/terra}}-v_{\mathrm{nastro/terra}}<0.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To find the direction, consider the relative motion of the surfaces. With rightward positive, the parcel's velocity relative to the belt is negative, so the parcel slides left relative to the contact. Friction from the belt opposes that sliding and points right.

</div>

## Exercise 2

For the same contact at the same normal load, the static limit is $16\,\mathrm N$ and the kinetic friction is $12\,\mathrm N$. By what percentage is the kinetic coefficient smaller than the static coefficient?

$$
\frac{\mu_s-\mu_d}{\mu_s}=\frac{16-12}{16}=0.25=25\%.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The ratio of the coefficients can be read from the forces because the normal reaction is shared. The decrease is four newtons out of sixteen. The denominator is the force associated with the static reference. Dividing by twelve would measure a percentage change relative to a different reference.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A block stays at rest while two horizontal cords pull with equal magnitudes in opposite directions. The table is rough. What value must static friction have?

$$
T-T+f_x=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad f_x=0.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two horizontal components of tension already cancel. A nonzero horizontal friction component would spoil the force balance. The roughness permits static friction when it is needed, but this situation requires zero friction.

</div>
