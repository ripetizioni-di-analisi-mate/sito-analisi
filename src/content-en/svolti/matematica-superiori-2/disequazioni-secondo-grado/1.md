## Exercise 1

Solve $x^2-8x+15\le0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^2-8x+15&=(x-3)(x-5),\\
(x-3)(x-5)\le0 &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x\in[3,5].
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{f="x^2-8*x+15" xmin=1.1 xmax=6.9 ymin=-1.8 ymax=7.6 nomi="x,y" punti="3,0,pieno;5,0,pieno;4,-1,pieno" segmenti="4,0,4,-1" titolo="The parabola lies below the axis between $3$ and $5$. Both zeros are included."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The roots $3$ and $5$ divide the number line into three intervals. For $x<3$, both factors are negative and the product is positive. Between $3$ and $5$, $x-3$ is positive and $x-5$ is negative. Beyond $5$ both are positive. The requirement of a nonpositive value selects the middle interval and the points where the product is zero. As a check, $4$ gives $(4-3)(4-5)=-1$, whereas $2$ gives $3$. The vertex also has horizontal coordinate $4$, halfway between the two roots.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve $x^2\le 3x+10$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^2-3x-10&\le0,\\
(x+2)(x-5)&\le0,\\
x&\in[-2,5].
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Subtract $3x+10$ from both sides so the comparison becomes a sign problem. The factors $x+2$ and $x-5$ give the roots $-2$ and $5$. They have opposite signs between the roots, so the product is negative there and zero at the endpoints. In the original inequality, $-2$ gives $4\le4$ and $5$ gives $25\le25$. The value $6$ instead gives the false statement $36\le28$, checking the outer interval. The roots have sum $3$ and product $-10$, agreeing with the coefficients of the quadratic.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve $2x^2-x-3\le0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x^2-x-3&=(x+1)(2x-3),\\
x+1=0&\iff x=-1,\\
2x-3=0&\iff x=\frac32,\\
x&\in\left[-1,\frac32\right].
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The factor $2x-3$ is zero at $\dfrac32$ because the coefficient of $x$ is $2$. The other factor vanishes at $-1$. Ordering the roots identifies the middle interval, on which one factor is positive and the other negative. At $x=0$, for instance, the product is $(1)(-3)=-3$. Outside that interval the factors have the same sign. Since $\le$ allows equality, include both $-1$ and $\dfrac32$. To find the factors, split $-x$ into $2x-3x$ and then take out the common factor $x+1$.

</div>
