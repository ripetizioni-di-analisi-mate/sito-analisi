## Exercise 1

Solve $x^2-8x+15>0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^2-8x+15&=(x-3)(x-5),\\
x&\in(-\infty,3)\cup(5,+\infty).
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Find the values where both factors have the same sign. Below $3$ they are both negative, so their product is positive. Above $5$ they are both positive. Between the two roots their signs differ. The roots themselves are excluded because $>$ requires a value greater than zero. As a check, choosing $x=6$ gives $3\cdot1=3$, while $x=4$ gives $1\cdot(-1)=-1$. The two outer intervals remain separate. The solution is the complement of $[3,5]$, the interval on which the quadratic is nonpositive.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve $x^2+4>4x$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^2-4x+4&>0,\\
(x-2)^2&>0,\\
x&\in\mathbb R\setminus\{2\}.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{f="(x-2)^2" xmin=-0.33 xmax=4.32 ymin=-0.75 ymax=5.68 punti="1,1,pieno;2,0,pieno;3,1,pieno" segmenti="2,0,2,1.2" titolo="The vertex at $(2,0)$ is the only point excluded by the strict inequality."}

<div class="spiegazione">

Subtracting $4x$ gives a perfect square. The square equals zero only at $x=2$, where the original comparison becomes $8>8$. At every other point the square is positive. For example, it equals $1$ at both $x=1$ and $x=3$. The graph touches the axis once and stays on the same side of the axis to the left and right. The strict sign excludes the point, splitting the solution into two half-lines. The discriminant $(-4)^2-4\cdot1\cdot4=0$ gives the same double root.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve $x^2+2x+3>0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^2+2x+3&=(x+1)^2+2,\\
(x+1)^2+2&\ge2>0,\\
x&\in\mathbb R.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first two terms can be completed to a square by adding the square of $1$. The constant is $3$, so two units remain beyond that square. The quadratic has minimum value $2$, attained at $x=-1$. Even its lowest value is positive, so every real number satisfies the comparison. The discriminant $\Delta=2^2-4\cdot1\cdot3=-8$ confirms that there are no real roots. The positive leading coefficient keeps the entire graph above the axis. The values at $0$ and $-2$ agree because each point is one unit from $-1$.

</div>
