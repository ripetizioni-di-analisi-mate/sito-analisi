## Exercise 1

A triangle has area $45\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ and base $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the corresponding altitude.

$$
A=\frac{bh}2\implies h=\frac{2A}b
$$

$$
h=\frac{2\cdot45}{5}=18
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Start from the triangle area formula. To isolate the altitude, double the area and divide by the base. Dividing an area by a length gives a length.

The given value $45\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ is the area of one copy of the triangle. Doubling it gives the product of base $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and altitude.

The result is $18$ cm.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A triangle has area $72\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ and base $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the corresponding altitude.

$$
A=\frac{bh}2\implies h=\frac{2A}b
$$

$$
h=\frac{2\cdot72}{6}=24
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Start from the triangle area formula. To isolate the altitude, double the area and divide by the base. Dividing an area by a length gives a length.

Substituting the resulting altitude into the original formula, half the product with $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ must give $72\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ again. This also checks the unit of length.

The result is $24$ cm.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A triangle has base $80\,\mathrm{cm}$ and area $0.24\,\mathrm m^2$. Find its altitude in meters.

$$
\begin{aligned}
b&=0.8\,\mathrm m \\[5pt]
h&=\frac{2\cdot 0.24}{0.8}=0.6\,\mathrm m
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Before using the formula, express the base in meters so the quotient divides square meters by meters. Doubling the area gives the product of the base and altitude. Dividing by the base isolates the altitude.

</div>
