## Exercise 1

In triangle $ABC$, $D$ lies on $AB$, $E$ lies on $AC$, and $DE\parallel BC$. Given $\dfrac{AD}{AB}=\dfrac13$ and $BC=15$ cm, find $DE$.

$$
\triangle ADE\sim\triangle ABC
$$

$$
\frac{DE}{BC}=\frac{AD}{AB}=\frac13
$$

$$
DE=\frac{15}3=5
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="0,9 0,0 15,0 0,9;0,6 5,6" punti="0,9,pieno,A;0,0,pieno,B;15,0,pieno,C;0,6,pieno,D;5,6,pieno,E" xmin=-1 xmax=16 ymin=-1 ymax=10 titolo="In the first exercise, $DE$ is one third of $BC$, so its length is $5$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The parallel sides give congruent corresponding angles, so the triangles are similar. The ratio of the shorter side to the longer side is one third. The segment parallel to the base is therefore also one third of the base.

The vertex correspondence follows the order of the two triangles. In particular, $DE$ and the base of the larger triangle are matching sides.

The result is $5$ cm.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In triangle $ABC$, $D$ lies on $AB$, $E$ lies on $AC$, and $DE\parallel BC$. Given $\dfrac{AD}{AB}=\dfrac13$ and $BC=18$ cm, find $DE$.

$$
\triangle ADE\sim\triangle ABC
$$

$$
\frac{DE}{BC}=\frac{AD}{AB}=\frac13
$$

$$
DE=\frac{18}3=6
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The parallel sides give congruent corresponding angles, so the triangles are similar. The ratio of the shorter side to the longer side is one third. The segment parallel to the base is therefore also one third of the base.

The base of the smaller triangle is the interior segment. To find it, compare the triangle bases in the same order, smaller to larger.

The result is $6$ cm.

</div>

## Exercise 3

In triangle $ABC$, segment $DE$ is parallel to $BC$, with $D\in AB$ and $E\in AC$. Given $AD=4\,\mathrm{cm}$, $DB=2\,\mathrm{cm}$, and $BC=9\,\mathrm{cm}$, find $DE$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
AB&=4+2=6 \\[5pt]
\frac{DE}{BC}&=\frac{AD}{AB}=\frac46 \\[5pt]
DE&=9\cdot\frac46=6\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Segment $DB$ is the remaining part of side $AB$. The side corresponding to $AD$ in the larger triangle is all of $AB$, so first add the two segments. The same fraction of $BC$ gives $DE$.

</div>
