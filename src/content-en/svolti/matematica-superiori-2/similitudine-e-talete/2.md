## Exercise 1

Two similar triangles have corresponding sides of lengths $4$ and $12$ cm. Another side of the first triangle measures $7$ cm. Find the length of the corresponding side in the second.

$$
k=\frac{12}{4}=3
$$

$$
l_2=3\cdot7=21
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Corresponding sides all have the same ratio. The first pair gives a factor of three. Multiply the other side by that factor as well.

Compare the two sides that occupy the same position in the triangles. The length $7$ is in the first triangle, so multiply it by the scale factor from the first figure to the second.

The result is $21$ cm.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Two similar triangles have corresponding sides of lengths $5$ and $15$ cm. Another side of the first triangle measures $8$ cm. Find the length of the corresponding side in the second.

$$
k=\frac{15}{5}=3
$$

$$
l_2=3\cdot8=24
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Corresponding sides all have the same ratio. The first pair gives a factor of three. Multiply the other side by that factor as well.

The ratio of the resulting side to $8$ must equal the ratio of $15$ to $5$. The two ratios use the same order.

The result is $24$ cm.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Let $ABC\sim DEF$. Given $AB=8\,\mathrm{cm}$, $DE=6\,\mathrm{cm}$, and $EF=9\,\mathrm{cm}$, find $BC$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{DE}{AB}&=\frac{EF}{BC} \\[5pt]
BC&=\frac{9\cdot8}6=12\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The vertex correspondence pairs $B$ with $E$ and $C$ with $F$. The length $EF=9$ is in the second triangle. To return to the first, divide by the factor $\dfrac68$.

</div>
