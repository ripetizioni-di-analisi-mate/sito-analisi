## Exercise 1

A triangle has base $8$ and altitude $6$. A similar triangle is enlarged by factor $3/2$. Find both areas.

$$
\begin{aligned}
    A_1&=\frac{8\cdot 6}{2}=24,\\
    (b_2,h_2)&=(12,9),\qquad A_2=\frac{12\cdot9}{2}=54,\\
    \frac{A_2}{A_1}&=\frac94=\left(\frac32\right)^2.
    \end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The enlarged triangle has a longer base and a greater altitude by the same factor. The area product therefore contains two factors of $3/2$. The diagram shows the perpendicular altitudes to the bases, which remain perpendicular under enlargement. Find the new area either from the new dimensions or by multiplying $24$ by $9/4$. Using $3/2$ only once would account for the change in just one dimension.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In triangle $ABC$ of area $75$, segment $DE$ joins sides $AB$ and $AC$, is parallel to $BC$, and satisfies $AD/AB=2/5$. Find the areas of the smaller triangle and the trapezoid.

$$
\begin{aligned}
    A_{ADE}&=\left(\frac25\right)^2\cdot75=12,\\
    A_{DBCE}&=75-12=63.
    \end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The parallel lines and shared angle make the two triangles similar. The ratio from lengths in the larger triangle to those in the smaller is $2/5$, so the area fraction is $4/25$. The trapezoid is the piece left after cutting, so its fraction is $1-4/25=21/25$. The diagram’s smaller triangle has height $4$ and the full height is $10$, matching the ratio. The two areas must total $75$, and indeed $12+63=75$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Every length in a similar reduction decreases by $20\%$. By what percentage does the area decrease?

$$
\begin{aligned}
    k&=1-\frac{20}{100}=\frac45,\\
    \frac{A_2}{A_1}&=\frac{16}{25}=\frac{64}{100},\\
    1-\frac{64}{100}&=36\%.
    \end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

A $20\%$ reduction leaves $80\%$ of each length. Squaring the remaining fraction leaves $64\%$ of the original area. The question asks for the percentage lost, which is the complement to one hundred percent. For a concrete check, start with a square of side $10$ and area $100$. Its new side is $8$ and its new area is $64$. The missing $36$ represents the percentage loss in the same way for any similar figure.

</div>
