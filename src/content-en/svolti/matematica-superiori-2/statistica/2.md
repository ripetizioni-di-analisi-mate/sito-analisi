## Exercise 1

The values $2$, $5$, and $8$ occur $1$, $2$, and $3$ times. Find the mean using counts and check it using relative frequencies.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bar x&=\frac{2\cdot1+5\cdot2+8\cdot3}{6}=6,\\[5pt]
&=2\cdot\frac16+5\cdot\frac13+8\cdot\frac12=6.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{xmin=-0.5 xmax=9.5 ymin=-0.35 ymax=4.1 nomi="x,n" poligoni="1.65,0 2.35,0 2.35,1 1.65,1;4.65,0 5.35,0 5.35,2 4.65,2;7.65,0 8.35,0 8.35,3 7.65,3" etichette="1.89,1.26,1;4.89,2.29,2;7.89,3.27,3" titolo="The bar at eight carries three times the weight of the bar at two"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The first calculation sums the contributions of all six observations and divides by six. The second already divides by six in the **weights**, which are fractions of the total. The results agree because each contribution was divided by the same denominator. The mean exceeds $5$ because the three observations at $8$ outweigh the one at $2$, which has the same deviation from $5$ in the opposite direction.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Group $A$ has two measurements equal to $1$ and six equal to $5$. Group $B$ has three equal to $1$ and nine equal to $5$. Compare their means and relative frequencies.

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_A(1)&=\frac{2}{8}=\frac{1}{4},\qquad f_B(1)=\frac{3}{12}=\frac{1}{4},\\[5pt]
f_A(5)&=\frac{6}{8}=\frac{3}{4},\qquad f_B(5)=\frac{9}{12}=\frac{3}{4},\\[5pt]
\bar x_A&=\bar x_B=1\cdot\frac14+5\cdot\frac34=4.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Group $B$ is larger, but in both groups one quarter of the measurements equal $1$ and three quarters equal $5$. The values and the relative weights are the same, so the means agree. The mean is closer to $5$ because it has three times the weight of $1$. The sums can also be reconstructed as $32$ and $48$. They differ, but each is four times its own number of measurements.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A grouped table has two times in $[0,4)$ minutes and three in $[4,8)$. Estimate the mean from the midpoints and find two compatible lists with different means.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bar x_{\rm est}&=\frac{2\cdot2+3\cdot6}{5}=\frac{22}{5},\\[5pt]
\bar x_A&=\frac{1+1+4+4+4}{5}=\frac{14}{5},\\[5pt]
\bar x_B&=\frac{3+3+7+7+7}{5}=\frac{27}{5}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The midpoints are $2$ and $6$ minutes. Assigning each to every observation in its class gives a mean of $\dfrac{22}{5}$ minutes. To see that this is an **estimate**, choose durations closer to the left ends and then closer to the right ends of the classes. In both lists two values are below $4$ and three are at least $4$ but below $8$. The grouped table is the same even though the total duration changes.

</div>
