## Exercise 1

Solve $x^{2} - 16=0$.

$$
x^2=16
$$

$$
x=\pm\sqrt{16}=\pm4
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Isolate the square of the unknown. The right side is positive, so there are two opposite roots. Both are solutions because their squares are equal.

The polynomial is zero when the square of $x$ equals $16$. Since the principal root is $4$, solve $|x|=4$. The solution set contains two numbers, each at distance $4$ from zero.

The result is $\{-4;\,4\}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve $3x^2-18=0$ over the real numbers.

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x^2&=18\\[6pt]
x^2&=6\\[6pt]
|x|&=\sqrt6.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The coefficient of $x^2$ is $3$, so divide both sides by $3$. Since $\sqrt{x^2}=|x|$, look for the values at distance $\sqrt6$ from zero on the number line. They are $-\sqrt6$ and $\sqrt6$. Squaring either gives $6$, and substitution into the left side gives $3\cdot6-18=0$.

</div>
