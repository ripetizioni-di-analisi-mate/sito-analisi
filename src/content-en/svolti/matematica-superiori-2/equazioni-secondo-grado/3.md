## Exercise 1

Solve $x^2-10x+24=0$ using the formula. Where is the axis of symmetry relative to the roots?

::grafico{f="x^2-10*x+24" xmin=-0.3 xmax=8.2 ymin=-2 ymax=11 punti="4,0,pieno;6,0,pieno;5,-1,pieno" segmenti="5,-1,5,3" titolo="The roots 4 and 6 are symmetric about x = 5"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
         \Delta&=(-10)^2-4\cdot24=4\\[6pt]
         x&=\frac{10\pm2}{2},\qquad S=\{4,6\}.
         \end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The linear coefficient is $-10$, so the formula has $+10$. The discriminant is $4$ and its square root is $2$. The two choices of sign give $4$ and $6$. The completed square $(x-5)^2-1$ shows that the axis is $x=5$, halfway between the zeroes. We can also check using Viète’s relations: $4+6=10$ and $4\cdot6=24$, so $(x-4)(x-6)$ recovers all the coefficients.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Construct a quadratic equation with roots $-2$ and $7$ and coefficient of $x^2$ equal to $2$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
         S&=-2+7=5,\qquad P=(-2)\cdot7=-14,\\[6pt]
         2(x+2)(x-7)&=2(x^2-5x-14),\\[6pt]
         2x^2-10x-28&=0.
         \end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The negative root requires factor $x+2$ and the positive root factor $x-7$. Their product is initially monic. Multiplying by $2$ preserves the roots and gives the required coefficient. The sum and product also check the signs: the linear coefficient is $-2\cdot5=-10$ and the constant is $2\cdot(-14)=-28$. Once both roots and the leading coefficient are fixed, the product of the factors determines the entire polynomial.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve $2x^2+x-4=0$ exactly and check the signs of the two solutions.

$$
\begin{aligned}
         \Delta&=1-4\cdot2\cdot(-4)=33\\[6pt]
         x&=\frac{-1\pm\sqrt{33}}{4}.
         \end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Since $33>1$, its square root is greater than $1$. The numerator with the plus sign is positive and the one with the minus sign is negative. The denominator $4$ preserves those signs. Viète’s relation gives the same check: the product is $-4:2=-2$, so the real roots have opposite signs. To verify exactly, rewrite the equation as $(4x+1)^2=33$. The values found make $4x+1$ equal $\pm\sqrt{33}$.

</div>
