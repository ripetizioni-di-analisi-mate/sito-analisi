## Exercise 1

Solve $x^{2} - 4 x=0$.

$$
x(x-4)=0
$$

$$
x=0\quad\text{or}\quad x=4
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Factor out $x$. A product is zero when at least one factor is zero. Factoring preserves the zero root, which dividing by $x$ would lose.

Substituting $x=0$ makes every term zero. Substituting $x=4$ makes the first term $16$ and the second $-16$. Factoring includes $x=0$, which division by $x$ would exclude.

The result is $\{0;\,4\}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve $3x^2+2x=0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x^2+2x&=x(3x+2)\\[6pt]
x(3x+2)=0&\iff x=0\ \text{or}\ 3x+2=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
x=0 \qquad\text{or}\qquad x=-\frac23.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The factor $x$ occurs in both terms. After factoring it out, the product is zero when at least one factor is zero. Solving $3x+2=0$ gives the negative root $-\dfrac23$. The value $0$ makes the common factor zero, so it also belongs to the solution set.

</div>
