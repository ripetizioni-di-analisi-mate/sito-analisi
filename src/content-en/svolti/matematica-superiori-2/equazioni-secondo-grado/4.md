## Exercise 1

Solve $x^{2} - 8 x + 16=0$.

$$
\Delta=64-4\cdot16=0
$$

$$
(x-4)^2=0\iff x=4
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The discriminant is zero, so the two roots given by the formula coincide. The trinomial is the square of a binomial. The solution set contains one distinct value.

Expanding $(x-4)^2$ gives $x^2-8x+16$. The square is zero only if $x-4=0$. The factor $x-4$ appears twice in the product, and the solution set contains just $4$.

The result is $\{4\}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve $4x^2+12x+9=0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
4x^2+12x+9&=(2x+3)^2\\[6pt]
(2x+3)^2=0&\iff 2x+3=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
x=-\frac32.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first and last terms are the squares of $2x$ and $3$. Twice their product is $2\cdot2x\cdot3=12x$, so the trinomial is the square of $2x+3$. The square is zero only when $2x+3=0$, giving the double root $-\dfrac32$.

</div>
