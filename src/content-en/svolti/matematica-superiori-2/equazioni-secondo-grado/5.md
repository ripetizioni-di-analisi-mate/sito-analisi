## Exercise 1

Show in two ways that $x^2+2x+5=0$ has no real roots.

$$
\begin{aligned}
         \Delta&=2^2-4\cdot5=-16,\\[6pt]
         x^2+2x+5&=(x+1)^2+4\ge4.
         \end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The discriminant is negative, so the quantity under the radical has no real square root. Completing the square gives a direct check. Whatever $x$ is, the term $(x+1)^2$ is at least zero, so adding $4$ leaves the polynomial strictly positive. The lowest value is $4$, attained at $x=-1$. The vertex calculation also tells us how far the closest point of the parabola lies above the horizontal axis.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The graph of $y=-2x^2+4x-7$ lies entirely on one side of the horizontal axis. Which side, and at what minimum distance?

::grafico{f="-2*x^2+4*x-7" xmin=-2.1 xmax=4.1 ymin=-12 ymax=1.4 punti="1,-5,pieno,V" segmenti="1,-5,1,0" titolo="The vertex lies five units below the axis"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
         -2x^2+4x-7&=-2(x-1)^2-5\\[6pt]
         -2(x-1)^2-5&\le-5.
         \end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Factor $-2$ out of the first two terms and complete the square in $x-1$. The negative coefficient makes $-2(x-1)^2$ at most zero. The parabola stays below the axis. Its maximum is $-5$ at $x=1$, so the vertex is five units from the horizontal axis. The discriminant $16-56=-40$ confirms that there are no roots, and the completed square also supplies the required distance.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Can two real numbers have sum $4$ and product $6$? Explain without finding the numbers.

$$
\begin{aligned}
         t^2-4t+6&=0,\\[6pt]
         \Delta&=16-24=-8,\\[6pt]
         (u-v)^2&=(u+v)^2-4uv=16-24=-8.
         \end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

If the numbers existed, they would be roots of the monic polynomial whose linear coefficient is the negative of the sum and whose constant is the product. The negative discriminant rules out real roots. The same calculation works without the polynomial. With $u+v=4$ and $uv=6$, their squared difference would have to be negative, which is impossible over the reals. At a fixed sum the product is greatest when the numbers coincide. Here that maximum would be $2\cdot2=4$.

</div>
