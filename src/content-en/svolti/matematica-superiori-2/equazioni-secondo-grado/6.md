## Exercise 1

Solve $\dfrac{x^{2} - 10 x + 24}{x-4}=0$.

$$
x\ne4
$$

$$
(x-4)(x-6)=0
$$

$$
x=6\quad\text{is allowed}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

A fraction equals zero when its numerator is zero and its denominator is nonzero. The first root of the numerator makes the denominator zero and is excluded. The second is in the domain.

The denominator excludes $x=4$. For $x\ne4$, the fraction simplifies to $x-6$, whose only zero is $6$. At that point the original denominator is $2$.

The result is $\{6\}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve $\dfrac{x+3}{x-1}=\dfrac{8}{x(x-1)}$.

$$
x\ne0,\qquad x\ne1
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
x(x+3)&=8\\[6pt]
x^2+3x-8&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
x_1=\frac{-3-\sqrt{41}}2,\qquad x_2=\frac{-3+\sqrt{41}}2.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The common denominator is $x(x-1)$, which is nonzero on the domain. Multiplying both sides by this product leaves $x(x+3)=8$. Neither root is $0$ or $1$. To check, the polynomial $x^2+3x-8$ equals $-8$ at $x=0$ and $-4$ at $x=1$. Both roots therefore solve the original equation.

</div>
