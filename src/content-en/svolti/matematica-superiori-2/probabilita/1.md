## Exercise 1

An urn contains $3$ red balls and $6$ blue balls. A ball is drawn uniformly at random. Find the probability that it is red.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N&=3+6=9,\\
P(R)&=\frac39=\frac13.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The nine individual balls are the elementary outcomes. Since each is equally likely to be selected, event $R$ consists of three outcomes of probability $\dfrac19$ each. The probabilities add because a single draw selects only one ball.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A bag contains $4$ yellow tokens, $5$ black tokens, and $3$ white tokens. What is the probability of getting a black or white token in a uniform draw?

$$
\begin{aligned}
N&=4+5+3=12,\\
n_{A\cup B}&=5+3=8,\\
P(A\cup B)&=\frac8{12}=\frac23.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $A$ denote black and $B$ white. A token can have only one of the three colors. The five black tokens and three white tokens give eight distinct favorable outcomes. The denominator remains twelve because the draw can also give a yellow token. That color has probability $\dfrac13$, which adds to the result to make one.

</div>

## Exercise 3

An urn contains $6$ blue balls and an unknown number of red balls. If the probability of a red ball in a uniform draw is $\dfrac13$, how many red balls are there?

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{r}{r+6}&=\frac13,\\
3r&=r+6,\\
r&=3.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $r$ be the number of red balls. The denominator is $r+6$ and includes the red balls. A one-third probability means the total is three times the red count. With $r=3$, nine balls include three favorable outcomes, which gives the stated probability.

</div>
