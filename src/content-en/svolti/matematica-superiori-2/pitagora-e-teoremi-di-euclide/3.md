## Exercise 1

A rectangular display measures $18$ inches high and $24$ inches wide. What is its diagonal length?

$$
\begin{aligned}
d^2&=18^2+24^2=324+576=900,\\[6pt]
d&=30\,\mathrm{in}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The diagonal joins opposite corners and splits the rectangle into two right triangles. Each has the display's height and width as its legs. The right angle follows from the definition of a rectangle. Both measurements use the same unit, and the square root returns inches. The diagonal is longer than $24$ inches and shorter than $18+24=42$ inches. Its square is $900$ square inches, so the diagonal is exactly $30$ inches.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A rectangle has one side of length $5$ cm and a diagonal of $13$ cm. Find the other side and the area.

$$
\begin{aligned}
b&=\sqrt{13^2-5^2}=12\,\mathrm{cm},\\[6pt]
\mathcal A&=5\cdot12=60\,\mathrm{cm}^2 .
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The side of length $5$ cm is a leg of the right triangle formed with the diagonal. For the missing side, subtract its square from the diagonal's square and take the square root. The diagonal is not a factor in the rectangle area formula, which uses adjacent sides. Multiplying $5$ cm by $12$ cm gives square units. Each triangle formed by cutting along the diagonal takes up half the rectangle.

</div>

## Exercise 3

In the coordinate plane, find the distance between $P=(-1,3)$ and $Q=(4,-2)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x&=4-(-1)=5,\\[6pt]
\Delta y&=-2-3=-5,\\[6pt]
PQ&=\sqrt{5^2+(-5)^2}=5\sqrt2 .
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Build a right triangle with one horizontal leg and one vertical leg. The second difference is negative because the vertical coordinate decreases from $P$ to $Q$. The vertical leg length is its absolute value. Squaring still contributes $25$. Since the two lengths agree, $PQ$ is also the diagonal of a square with side length $5$. The square-diagonal formula confirms the distance $5\sqrt2$.

Taking differences from $Q$ to $P$ instead of $P$ to $Q$ reverses their signs and leaves the squares unchanged.

</div>
