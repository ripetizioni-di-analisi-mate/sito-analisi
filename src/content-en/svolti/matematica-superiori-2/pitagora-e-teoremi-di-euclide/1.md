## Exercise 1

Find the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $12\,\mathrm{cm}$.

::grafico{uguale=1 assi=0 poligoni="0,0 12,0 0,9" linee="0.5,0 0.5,0.5 0,0.5" etichette="6.0,-0.5,12;-0.6,4.5,9" xmin=-1.5 xmax=13 ymin=-1.5 ymax=10 titolo="The two marked sides form the right angle"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
c^2 &= 9^2+12^2 \\[5pt]
&= 81+144=225 \\[5pt]
c&=\sqrt{225}=15\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The unknown side is opposite the angle formed by the two legs. By the **Pythagorean theorem**, its square is the sum of the squares of the known lengths. The square of $9$ is $81$ and the square of $12$ is $144$. Their sum is $225$. Take the positive square root because the hypotenuse is a length. The value $15$ is greater than both legs, as expected for the side opposite the right angle.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The end of a ramp is $2\,\mathrm m$ above the ground. Its horizontal projection measures $7\,\mathrm m$. Find the length of the ramp.

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 7,0 7,2" linee="6.7,0 6.7,0.3 7,0.3" etichette="3.5,-0.3,7 m;7.6,1,2 m" xmin=-0.7 xmax=8.2 ymin=-0.9 ymax=3 titolo="Horizontal projection and rise of the ramp"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
L^2&=7^2+2^2=53 \\[5pt]
L&=\sqrt{53}\,\mathrm m
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The horizontal distance and the rise form the right angle, so the sloping path is the hypotenuse. Its square is $53$, which lies between $7^2$ and $8^2$. The ramp is therefore between $7$ and $8$ meters long. The expression $\sqrt{53}$ gives the exact length.

</div>
