## Exercise 1

Solve the system $\begin{cases}2x+y=10\\x-y=-1\end{cases}$.

$$
y=x+1
$$

$$
2x+x+1=10\iff 3x=9
$$

$$
x=3,\qquad y=3+1=4
$$

::grafico{f="10-2*x;x+1" punti="3,4,pieno,P" xmin=-1 xmax=6 ymin=-1 ymax=10 titolo="The solution of the first system is $(3;4)$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The second equation gives $y$ in terms of $x$. Substituting into the first equation gives a single equation in $x$. The value obtained is then substituted into the expression for $y$, and the resulting pair satisfies both equations.

The coefficient of $y$ in the second equation is $-1$, so $y$ can be isolated without introducing fractions. In the first equation, both $2x$ and $x$ now involve the same unknown. The check of the pair gives $2\cdot 3+4=10$ and $3-4=-1$, which recover the two constant terms.

The result is $\left(3;\,4\right)$.

The vertical projection of the intersection gives $x=3$. Both formulas give its vertical coordinate, $10-2\cdot3=4$ and $3+1=4$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve $\begin{cases}x+3y=8\\2x-y=9\end{cases}$ by substitution. Give two ways to isolate a variable without introducing fractions and choose which to substitute.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=8-3y,\qquad y=2x-9,\\[6pt]
2(8-3y)-y&=9,\\[6pt]
16-7y&=9\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y=1,\\[6pt]
x&=8-3\cdot1=5.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first row has coefficient one on $x$, and the second has coefficient negative one on $y$. Either one isolates a variable without denominators. Choosing the first, the expression $8-3y$ is multiplied by two in the second row. This gives $(5;1)$. Starting with the other expression makes the first row $x+3(2x-9)=8$ and gives the same $x$. The checks in the original rows are $5+3=8$ and $10-1=9$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve $\begin{cases}2x-y=4\\x+2y=3\end{cases}$ by substitution.

$$
y=2x-4
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
x+2(2x-4)&=3\\[6pt]
5x-8&=3\\[6pt]
5x&=11.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
x=\frac{11}{5},\qquad y=2\cdot\frac{11}{5}-4=\frac25.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The coefficient of $y$ in the first equation is $-1$, so $y=2x-4$ follows without division by an integer greater than one. The whole expression $2x-4$ is substituted for $y$ in the second equation. The factor $2$ therefore multiplies both $2x$ and $-4$. To check the result, the second equation gives $\dfrac{11}5+2\cdot\dfrac25=3$ and the first gives $2\cdot\dfrac{11}5-\dfrac25=4$. The solution is $\left(\dfrac{11}5;\,\dfrac25\right)$.

</div>
