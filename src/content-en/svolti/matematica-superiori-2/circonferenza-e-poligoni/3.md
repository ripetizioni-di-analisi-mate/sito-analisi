## Exercise 1

From an exterior point $P$, tangents touch a circle at $S,T$. Given $OP=17$ cm and radius $8$ cm, find each tangent segment's length.

$$
\begin{aligned}
PT^2&=OP^2-OT^2=17^2-8^2=225,\\[6pt]
PT&=PS=15\,\mathrm{cm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In triangle $OTP$, the right angle is at $T$ because the radius is perpendicular to the tangent. The given distance $OP$ is therefore the hypotenuse and $OT$ is a leg. Subtract the square of the known leg and use the positive root to obtain a length. The other triangle $OSP$ shares the hypotenuse length and has an equal radius, so the second tangent segment is as long as the first. Check that $8^2+15^2=17^2$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A circle has radius $4$ cm. Three lines are at distances of $2$ cm, $4$ cm, and $6$ cm from the centre. How many intersections does each line have with the circle?

$$
\begin{aligned}
2<4&\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \#=2,\\[6pt]
4=4&\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \#=1,\\[6pt]
6>4&\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \#=0 .
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For each line, start from the point nearest the centre, the foot of the perpendicular. When the distance is smaller than the radius, two points are exactly one radius from the centre, one on either side of the foot. When the distance equals the radius, the foot is the only contact. At a greater distance all points are outside. With distance $2$ cm, for example, each intersection is $\sqrt{4^2-2^2}=2\sqrt3$ cm from the foot.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A quadrilateral $ABCD$ is circumscribed about a circle. Sides $AB$, $BC$, and $CD$ measure $10$, $8$, and $7$ cm. Find $DA$ and the perimeter.

$$
\begin{aligned}
AB+CD&=BC+DA,\\[6pt]
DA&=10+7-8=9\,\mathrm{cm},\\[6pt]
p&=10+8+7+9=34\,\mathrm{cm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two tangent pieces from any vertex are equal in length. Each side combines one piece from each of its endpoints. Adding opposite sides collects exactly one piece from each of the four vertices. The other pair of opposite sides has the same total, $17$ cm. Subtract $8$ cm to find the missing side, and double $17$ cm to get the perimeter. The fact that every side is tangent is what justifies the equality.

</div>
