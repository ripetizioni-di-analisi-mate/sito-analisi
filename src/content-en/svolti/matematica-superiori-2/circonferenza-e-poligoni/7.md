## Exercise 1

What is the measure of each interior angle of a regular polygon with $6$ sides?

$$
\Sigma=(6-2)\cdot180^\circ
$$

$$
\alpha=\frac{(6-2)\cdot180^\circ}{6}=120^\circ
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

A convex polygon can be divided into two fewer triangles than its number of sides. This determines the sum of the interior angles. In a regular polygon, the angles are equal, so divide the sum by the number of angles.

Drawing the diagonals from one vertex of a polygon with $6$ sides gives a triangle for every side except the two meeting at that vertex.

The result is $120$ degrees.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is the measure of each interior angle of a regular polygon with $7$ sides?

$$
\Sigma=(7-2)\cdot180^\circ
$$

$$
\alpha=\frac{(7-2)\cdot180^\circ}{7}=\dfrac{900}{7}^\circ
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

A convex polygon can be divided into two fewer triangles than its number of sides. This determines the sum of the interior angles. In a regular polygon, the angles are equal, so divide the sum by the number of angles.

In a regular polygon, the sum of the interior angles is split into $7$ equal parts. Dividing gives the size of each angle.

The result is $\dfrac{900}{7}$ degrees.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Each interior angle of a regular polygon is $156^\circ$. Find its number of sides.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\beta&=180^\circ-156^\circ=24^\circ \\[5pt]
N&=\frac{360^\circ}{24^\circ}=15
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The adjacent exterior angle measures $24^\circ$ because it forms a straight angle with the interior angle. One trip around the polygon accumulates a total turn of $360^\circ$. Dividing by $24^\circ$ gives the number of turns, which equals the number of sides.

</div>
