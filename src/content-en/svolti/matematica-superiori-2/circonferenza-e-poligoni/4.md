## Exercise 1

A chord is at distance $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center of a circle with radius $15\,\mathrm{cm}$. How long is the chord?

$$
\frac{AB}2=\sqrt{225-81}=12
$$

$$
AB=2\cdot12=24
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The perpendicular from the center to a chord bisects it. The radius, the distance to the chord, and the half-chord form a right triangle. Use the Pythagorean theorem, then double the half-chord.

Distance $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ is measured to the midpoint of the chord. The half-chord is the leg perpendicular to that segment.

The result is $24$ cm.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A chord is at distance $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center of a circle with radius $20\,\mathrm{cm}$. How long is the chord?

$$
\frac{AB}2=\sqrt{400-144}=16
$$

$$
AB=2\cdot16=32
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The perpendicular from the center to a chord bisects it. The radius, the distance to the chord, and the half-chord form a right triangle. Use the Pythagorean theorem, then double the half-chord.

The two triangles on either side of the perpendicular segment have the same hypotenuse $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the same distance $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. This is why each contains half the chord.

The result is $32$ cm.

</div>
