## Exercise 1

An inscribed angle measures $25^\circ$. What is the measure of the central angle that intercepts the same arc?

$$
\beta=2\cdot25^\circ=50^\circ
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The central angle is twice an inscribed angle intercepting the same arc. Double the given measure. Both measures remain in degrees.

Halving the central angle just found recovers the given measure $25^\circ$.

The result is $50$ degrees.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An inscribed angle measures $30^\circ$. What is the measure of the central angle that intercepts the same arc?

$$
\beta=2\cdot30^\circ=60^\circ
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The central angle is twice an inscribed angle intercepting the same arc. Double the given measure. Both measures remain in degrees.

Twice $30^\circ$ gives the measure of the intercepted arc. The vertex on the circle lies on the other arc.

The result is $60$ degrees.

</div>

## Exercise 3

An inscribed angle measures $105^\circ$. Find the measure of the other arc cut off by its sides.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha&=2\cdot105^\circ=210^\circ \\[5pt]
\gamma&=360^\circ-210^\circ=150^\circ
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Twice $105^\circ$ gives the intercepted arc, which is the larger one. The two arcs make up the full turn. Subtract from $360^\circ$ to obtain the requested measure.

</div>
