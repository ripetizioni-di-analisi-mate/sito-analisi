## Exercise 1

Solve $x^{4} - 41 x^{2} + 400=0$.

$$
t=x^2\ge0
$$

$$
(t-16)(t-25)=0
$$

$$
x^2=16\quad\text{or}\quad x^2=25
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The equation contains only even powers and is biquadratic. Setting $t=x^2$ gives a quadratic equation with two positive roots. Each gives two opposite values of $x$.

To find $x$, note that $16$ and $25$ are positive. Their principal square roots are $4$ and $5$, respectively. Each of these values and its opposite has the required square.

The result is $\{-5;\,-4;\,4;\,5\}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve $x^4+x^2-6=0$ over $\mathbb R$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t&=x^2,\qquad t\ge0\\[6pt]
t^2+t-6&=(t+3)(t-2).
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
t=2\implies x=-\sqrt2 \quad\text{or}\quad x=\sqrt2.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The equation in $t$ has roots $-3$ and $2$. Since $t$ is the square of $x$, exclude $-3$. The value $2$ is positive and gives two opposite real numbers. Both give $x^2=2$ and $x^4=4$, so the polynomial equals $4+2-6=0$.

</div>
