## Exercise 1

Solve $\begin{cases}x+y=9\\xy=20\end{cases}$.

$$
y=9-x
$$

$$
x(9-x)=20
$$

$$
(x-4)(x-5)=0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Solve the sum equation for $y$ and substitute into the product. The two roots of the quadratic give two ordered pairs that differ only in the order of their coordinates. Both have the required sum and product.

The two values are distinct, so both orders must be considered. Swapping the coordinates leaves the sum $9$ and the product $20$ unchanged. Each pair therefore satisfies both conditions.

The result is $\{\left(4;\,5\right);\, \left(5;\,4\right)\}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve $\begin{cases}x+y=6\\xy=9\end{cases}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t^2-6t+9&=0\\[6pt]
(t-3)^2&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The values $x$ and $y$ are the roots of the monic trinomial whose roots have sum $6$ and product $9$. The trinomial is the square of $t-3$, so both coordinates are $3$. Swapping the coordinates leaves the pair $(3;3)$ unchanged. It satisfies $3+3=6$ and $3\cdot3=9$.

</div>
