## Exercise 1

Solve $\dfrac{x^{3} - 16 x}{x-4}=0$.

$$
x\ne4
$$

$$
x(x-4)(x+4)=0
$$

$$
x=0\quad\text{or}\quad x=-4
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

First exclude the zero of the denominator. The numerator factors into three terms, giving three candidates. The positive value lies outside the domain, so it is discarded. The other two roots are valid.

The numerator is $x(x-4)(x+4)$. Division by $x-4$ is allowed on the domain and gives $x(x+4)$. Both roots $-4$ and $0$ are allowed by the original restriction.

The result is $\{-4;\,0\}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve $\dfrac{x^3-4x}{x^2-4}=0$.

$$
x\ne-2,\qquad x\ne2
$$

$$
\frac{x^3-4x}{x^2-4}
=\frac{x(x-2)(x+2)}{(x-2)(x+2)}
=x \qquad (x\ne-2,\ x\ne2).
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The denominator is zero at $-2$ and $2$, so exclude those values before simplifying. On the domain, the numerator is the denominator multiplied by $x$, and the fraction equals $x$. Its only zero is $0$, which is admissible because the original denominator there is $-4$.

</div>
