## Exercise 1

In a geometric progression with positive terms we have $a_2 = 6$ and $a_5 = 162$. Find the common ratio, the first term, the eighth term and the sum of the first eight terms.

$$
\frac{a_5}{a_2} = q^3
\iff
q^3 = \frac{162}{6} = 27
\iff
q = 3
$$
$$
a_1 = \frac{a_2}{q} = 2,
\qquad
a_8 = a_1\,q^{7} = 2 \cdot 2187 = 4374
$$
$$
S_8 = a_1\,\frac{1 - q^{8}}{1 - q} = 2 \cdot \frac{1 - 6561}{1 - 3} = 6560
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In a **geometric progression**, to go from $a_2$ to $a_5$ one multiplies by the common ratio three times, so the ratio $\dfrac{a_5}{a_2}$ equals $q^3$. From $q^3 = 27$ we get $q = 3$, the only real cube root. The first term is obtained by dividing $a_2$ by the common ratio, and the eighth with the formula $a_n = a_1\,q^{n-1}$. The **sum of the terms** is computed with the formula for the geometric sum; as a check, $2 + 6 + 18 + 54 + 162 + 486 + 1458 + 4374 = 6560$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

At the end of every month, $200$ euros are deposited, for $24$ months, into an account that yields $0.5\%$ per month with compound interest. Compute the balance immediately after the last deposit.

$$
S = 200 + 200 \cdot 1.005 + 200 \cdot 1.005^{2} + \dots + 200 \cdot 1.005^{23}
$$
$$
S = 200 \cdot \frac{1.005^{24} - 1}{1.005 - 1} \approx 200 \cdot 25.4320 \approx 5086.39 \text{ euros}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each deposit stays in the account for a different number of months. The last one stays in the account zero months, the second-to-last one month and the first $23$ months, because it is made at the end of the first month. A deposit that stays $k$ months is multiplied by $1.005^{k}$, so the balance is the sum of $24$ terms of a **geometric progression** with first term $200$ and common ratio $1.005$. With the formula for the **sum of the terms**, written as $a_1\,\dfrac{q^n - 1}{q - 1}$ so that numerator and denominator are positive, the balance is about $5086.39$ euros. The deposits total $4800$ euros, so the interest amounts to about $286$ euros.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A machine bought for $20\,000$ euros loses $15\%$ of its value every year. Find after how many years its value falls below $5000$ euros.

$$
V_n = 20\,000 \cdot 0.85^{\,n}
$$
$$
20\,000 \cdot 0.85^{\,n} < 5000
\iff
0.85^{\,n} < 0.25
\iff
n > \frac{\ln 0.25}{\ln 0.85} \approx 8.53
$$
$$
V_8 \approx 5449.81,
\qquad
V_9 \approx 4632.34
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Losing $15\%$ means keeping $85\%$, so every year the value is multiplied by $0.85$ and the values form a **geometric progression** with common ratio $0.85 < 1$, which tends to $0$ by the limit of the geometric sequence. In the inequality one takes the logarithm of both sides; then one divides by $\ln 0.85$, which is negative, and the direction of the inequality is reversed. This gives $n > 8.53$, and the first natural number that satisfies the condition is $9$. Therefore the value falls below $5000$ euros **after 9 years**, as confirmed by $V_8$ and $V_9$.

</div>
