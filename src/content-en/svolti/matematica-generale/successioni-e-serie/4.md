## Exercise 1

Compute
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \frac{3n^2 - n + 2}{5n^2 + 4} .
$$

$$
\frac{3n^2 - n + 2}{5n^2 + 4}
= \frac{n^2 \left( 3 - \dfrac{1}{n} + \dfrac{2}{n^2} \right)}{n^2 \left( 5 + \dfrac{4}{n^2} \right)}
= \frac{3 - \dfrac{1}{n} + \dfrac{2}{n^2}}{5 + \dfrac{4}{n^2}}
\to \frac{3}{5}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Numerator and denominator diverge to $+\infty$, so the quotient takes the indeterminate form $\dfrac{\infty}{\infty}$. In both one factors out the highest power of $n$, which is $n^2$, and cancels it. The terms $\dfrac{1}{n}$, $\dfrac{2}{n^2}$ and $\dfrac{4}{n^2}$ tend to $0$, therefore by the **rules for computing limits** the quotient tends to $\dfrac{3}{5}$, the ratio of the coefficients of the highest-degree terms.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Compute
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \frac{n^3 + 1}{2n^2 - n}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \frac{4n + 1}{n^2 + 3} .
$$

$$
\frac{n^3 + 1}{2n^2 - n}
= \frac{n^3 \left( 1 + \dfrac{1}{n^3} \right)}{n^2 \left( 2 - \dfrac{1}{n} \right)}
= n \cdot \frac{1 + \dfrac{1}{n^3}}{2 - \dfrac{1}{n}}
\to +\infty
$$
$$
\frac{4n + 1}{n^2 + 3}
= \frac{n \left( 4 + \dfrac{1}{n} \right)}{n^2 \left( 1 + \dfrac{3}{n^2} \right)}
= \frac{1}{n} \cdot \frac{4 + \dfrac{1}{n}}{1 + \dfrac{3}{n^2}}
\to 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Here too one factors out the respective highest power in numerator and denominator. In the first quotient the numerator has degree $3$ and the denominator degree $2$, so a factor $n$ that diverges remains, multiplied by a factor that tends to $\dfrac{1}{2}$; the product **diverges** to $+\infty$. In the second the degree of the denominator is greater, the factor $\dfrac{1}{n}$ that tends to $0$ remains, and the quotient **tends** to $0$. In general, the limit of a quotient of polynomials is infinite, finite and nonzero, or zero according to whether the degree of the numerator is greater than, equal to or less than that of the denominator.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Compute
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \left( \sqrt{n^2 + 3n} - n \right) .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sqrt{n^2 + 3n} - n
&= \frac{\left( \sqrt{n^2 + 3n} - n \right)\left( \sqrt{n^2 + 3n} + n \right)}{\sqrt{n^2 + 3n} + n} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{n^2 + 3n - n^2}{\sqrt{n^2 + 3n} + n} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{3n}{n \left( \sqrt{1 + \dfrac{3}{n}} + 1 \right)} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{3}{\sqrt{1 + \dfrac{3}{n}} + 1}
\to \frac{3}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two terms both diverge to $+\infty$, so the difference is in the indeterminate form $+\infty - \infty$. One multiplies and divides by the sum $\sqrt{n^2 + 3n} + n$, so the numerator becomes the difference of squares $n^2 + 3n - n^2$, which equals $3n$ and contains no roots. In the denominator one takes $n$ out of the root, since $\sqrt{n^2 + 3n} = n\,\sqrt{1 + \dfrac{3}{n}}$ for $n > 0$. After cancelling $n$, the root tends to $1$, and the limit is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Compute
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \frac{2^n + 3^n}{3^{\,n+1}} .
$$

$$
\frac{2^n + 3^n}{3^{\,n+1}}
= \frac{3^n \left( \left( \dfrac{2}{3} \right)^{n} + 1 \right)}{3 \cdot 3^n}
= \frac{1}{3} \left( \left( \frac{2}{3} \right)^{n} + 1 \right)
\to \frac{1}{3}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

With powers of different bases, one factors out the one that grows fastest, that is, $3^n$, and writes $3^{n+1} = 3 \cdot 3^n$. After cancelling, what remains is the sequence $\left( \dfrac{2}{3} \right)^{n}$, which by the **limit of the geometric sequence** tends to $0$ because the base lies between $-1$ and $1$. The limit is therefore $\dfrac{1}{3}$.

</div>
