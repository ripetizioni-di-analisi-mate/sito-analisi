## Exercise 1

Compute
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \frac{3n^2 - n + 2}{5n^2 + 4} .
$$

$$
\frac{3n^2 - n + 2}{5n^2 + 4}
= \frac{n^2 \left( 3 - \dfrac{1}{n} + \dfrac{2}{n^2} \right)}{n^2 \left( 5 + \dfrac{4}{n^2} \right)}
= \frac{3 - \dfrac{1}{n} + \dfrac{2}{n^2}}{5 + \dfrac{4}{n^2}}
\to \frac{3}{5}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Both the numerator and the denominator tend to $+\infty$, so the quotient has the indeterminate form $\dfrac{\infty}{\infty}$. Factoring out and canceling the highest power of $n$, namely $n^2$, leaves terms $\dfrac{1}{n}$, $\dfrac{2}{n^2}$, and $\dfrac{4}{n^2}$ that tend to $0$. By the **limit laws**, the quotient therefore tends to $\dfrac{3}{5}$, the ratio of the leading coefficients.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Compute
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \frac{n^3 + 1}{2n^2 - n}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \frac{4n + 1}{n^2 + 3} .
$$

$$
\frac{n^3 + 1}{2n^2 - n}
= \frac{n^3 \left( 1 + \dfrac{1}{n^3} \right)}{n^2 \left( 2 - \dfrac{1}{n} \right)}
= n \cdot \frac{1 + \dfrac{1}{n^3}}{2 - \dfrac{1}{n}}
\to +\infty
$$
$$
\frac{4n + 1}{n^2 + 3}
= \frac{n \left( 4 + \dfrac{1}{n} \right)}{n^2 \left( 1 + \dfrac{3}{n^2} \right)}
= \frac{1}{n} \cdot \frac{4 + \dfrac{1}{n}}{1 + \dfrac{3}{n^2}}
\to 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Again, factor the highest power out of the numerator and denominator separately. In the first quotient the numerator has degree $3$ and the denominator has degree $2$. A factor $n$ that tends to infinity remains, multiplied by a factor that tends to $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so the product **diverges** to $+\infty$. In the second quotient the denominator has the higher degree. The remaining factor $\dfrac{1}{n}$ tends to $0$, so the quotient **tends** to $0$. In general, the limit of a quotient of polynomials is infinite, finite and nonzero, or zero according as the numerator has greater, equal, or lower degree than the denominator.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Compute
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \left( \sqrt{n^2 + 3n} - n \right) .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sqrt{n^2 + 3n} - n
&= \frac{\left( \sqrt{n^2 + 3n} - n \right)\left( \sqrt{n^2 + 3n} + n \right)}{\sqrt{n^2 + 3n} + n} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{n^2 + 3n - n^2}{\sqrt{n^2 + 3n} + n} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{3n}{n \left( \sqrt{1 + \dfrac{3}{n}} + 1 \right)} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{3}{\sqrt{1 + \dfrac{3}{n}} + 1}
\to \frac{3}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Both terms tend to $+\infty$, so the difference has the indeterminate form $+\infty - \infty$. Multiplying and dividing by $\sqrt{n^2 + 3n} + n$ gives the difference of squares $n^2 + 3n - n^2$ in the numerator. This is $3n$, with no radical. In the denominator the factor $n$ can be taken out of the square root because $\sqrt{n^2 + 3n} = n\,\sqrt{1 + \dfrac{3}{n}}$ for $n > 0$. After canceling $n$, the remaining square root tends to $1$, so the limit is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Compute
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \frac{2^n + 3^n}{3^{\,n+1}} .
$$

$$
\frac{2^n + 3^n}{3^{\,n+1}}
= \frac{3^n \left( \left( \dfrac{2}{3} \right)^{n} + 1 \right)}{3 \cdot 3^n}
= \frac{1}{3} \left( \left( \frac{2}{3} \right)^{n} + 1 \right)
\to \frac{1}{3}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Among the powers with different bases, $3^n$ grows fastest. Write $3^{n+1} = 3 \cdot 3^n$ and factor out the common power. After canceling it, the only limit left to evaluate is that of $\left( \dfrac{2}{3} \right)^{n}$. By the **limit law for geometric sequences**, it tends to $0$ because the base lies strictly between $-1$ and $1$. The limit is therefore $\dfrac{1}{3}$.

</div>
