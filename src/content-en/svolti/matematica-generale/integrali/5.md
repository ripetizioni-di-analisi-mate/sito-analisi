## Exercise 1

Compute
$$
\int \frac{x^2 + 3x}{x + 1}\,dx .
$$

$$
x^2 + 3x = (x + 1)(x + 2) - 2
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\frac{x^2 + 3x}{x + 1} = x + 2 - \frac{2}{x + 1}
$$
$$
\int \left( x + 2 - \frac{2}{x + 1} \right) dx = \frac{x^2}{2} + 2x - 2 \ln \lvert x + 1 \rvert + c
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The denominator is of **first degree** and the numerator has a higher degree, so we divide $x^2 + 3x$ by $x + 1$. The quotient is $x + 2$ and the remainder is the value of the numerator at $x = -1$, that is, $1 - 3 = -2$; indeed $(x + 1)(x + 2) - 2 = x^2 + 3x$. Dividing by $x + 1$ gives the sum of a polynomial and a term $\dfrac{-2}{x + 1}$. The polynomial is integrated term by term and the term with $x + 1$ in the denominator has antiderivative $-2 \ln \lvert x + 1 \rvert$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Compute
$$
\int \frac{5x - 1}{x^2 - x - 2}\,dx .
$$

$$
x^2 - x - 2 = (x - 2)(x + 1)
$$
$$
\frac{5x - 1}{(x - 2)(x + 1)} = \frac{A}{x - 2} + \frac{B}{x + 1}
\iff
5x - 1 = A\,(x + 1) + B\,(x - 2)
$$
$$
x = 2: \ 9 = 3A \iff A = 3,
\qquad
x = -1: \ -6 = -3B \iff B = 2
$$
$$
\int \frac{5x - 1}{x^2 - x - 2}\,dx = \int \left( \frac{3}{x - 2} + \frac{2}{x + 1} \right) dx = 3 \ln \lvert x - 2 \rvert + 2 \ln \lvert x + 1 \rvert + c
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The denominator is of second degree, with discriminant $1 + 8 = 9 > 0$ and roots $2$ and $-1$, and the numerator has a lower degree, so it is decomposed into **partial fractions** with two constants $A$ and $B$. Bringing the right-hand side to the common denominator gives an equality between the numerators that must hold for every $x$. Choosing $x = 2$ makes the term with $B$ vanish and gives $A = 3$; choosing $x = -1$ makes the term with $A$ vanish and gives $B = 2$. Each partial fraction has a logarithm as antiderivative. As a check, $3\,(x + 1) + 2\,(x - 2) = 5x - 1$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Compute
$$
\int \frac{2x + 1}{x^2 - 4x + 4}\,dx .
$$

$$
x^2 - 4x + 4 = (x - 2)^2,
\qquad
2x + 1 = 2\,(x - 2) + 5
$$
$$
\frac{2x + 1}{(x - 2)^2} = \frac{2\,(x - 2) + 5}{(x - 2)^2} = \frac{2}{x - 2} + \frac{5}{(x - 2)^2}
$$
$$
\int \left( \frac{2}{x - 2} + 5\,(x - 2)^{-2} \right) dx = 2 \ln \lvert x - 2 \rvert - \frac{5}{x - 2} + c
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The denominator is the square of $x - 2$, so it has the **double root** $2$, and the partial fractions have denominators $x - 2$ and $(x - 2)^2$. The constants are found by writing the numerator in terms of $x - 2$. Since $2x = 2\,(x - 2) + 4$, we have $2x + 1 = 2\,(x - 2) + 5$, therefore $A = 2$ and $B = 5$. The first fraction has a logarithm as antiderivative. The second is the power $(x - 2)^{-2}$, which by the power formula in composite form has antiderivative $\dfrac{(x - 2)^{-1}}{-1} = -\dfrac{1}{x - 2}$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Compute
$$
\int_2^3 \frac{x^3}{x^2 - 1}\,dx .
$$

$$
x^3 = x\left( x^2 - 1 \right) + x
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\frac{x^3}{x^2 - 1} = x + \frac{x}{x^2 - 1}
$$
$$
\frac{x}{x^2 - 1} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{2x}{x^2 - 1}
$$
$$
\int \frac{x^3}{x^2 - 1}\,dx = \frac{x^2}{2} + \frac{1}{2} \ln \left\lvert x^2 - 1 \right\rvert + c
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_2^3 \frac{x^3}{x^2 - 1}\,dx
&= \left[ \frac{x^2}{2} + \frac{1}{2} \ln\left( x^2 - 1 \right) \right]_2^3 \\[6pt]
&= \left( \frac{9}{2} + \frac{1}{2} \ln 8 \right) - \left( 2 + \frac{1}{2} \ln 3 \right)
= \frac{5}{2} + \frac{1}{2} \ln \frac{8}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The numerator has degree $3$, greater than the degree of the denominator, so the **division** is performed first, which gives quotient $x$ and remainder $x$. The term $\dfrac{x}{x^2 - 1}$ could be decomposed into partial fractions, but the numerator is half the derivative of the denominator, therefore the integral is basic in the **composite form** $\dfrac{g'}{g}$. On the interval $[2, 3]$ the denominator $x^2 - 1$ is positive, so the integrand is continuous and the absolute value can be removed. With the fundamental formula and the property $\ln 8 - \ln 3 = \ln \dfrac{8}{3}$ we obtain the result, about $2.99$.

</div>
