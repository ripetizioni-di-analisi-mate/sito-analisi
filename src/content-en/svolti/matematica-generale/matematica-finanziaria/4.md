## Exercise 1

Determine after how long a principal of $2500$ euros, invested at $3.5\%$ per year, becomes $3000$ euros, under compound interest and under simple interest.

$$
2500 \cdot 1.035^{t} = 3000
\iff
1.035^{t} = 1.2
\iff
t = \frac{\ln 1.2}{\ln 1.035} \approx 5.30 \text{ years}
$$
$$
0.30 \cdot 12 \approx 3.6 \text{ months}
$$
$$
2500\,(1 + 0.035\,t) = 3000
\iff
0.035\,t = 0.2
\iff
t = \frac{0.2}{0.035} \approx 5.71 \text{ years}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Under **compound interest** one divides by the principal and obtains $1.035^{t} = 1.2$; the unknown is in the exponent, so one takes the logarithm of both sides and uses $\ln 1.035^{t} = t \ln 1.035$. The time is about $5.30$ years; the decimal part, multiplied by $12$, gives about $3.6$ months, therefore $5$ years and almost $4$ months are needed.

Under **simple interest** the accumulated value is linear in $t$, and the equation is linear. The time, about $5.71$ years, is longer than under compound interest, because for durations longer than a year the simple accumulated value is smaller than the compound one and therefore reaches $3000$ euros later.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A principal of $6000$ euros becomes $7000$ euros in $4$ years. Determine the annual rate of the transaction under compound interest and under simple interest.

$$
6000\,(1 + i)^{4} = 7000
\iff
(1 + i)^{4} = \frac{7}{6}
\iff
i = \left( \frac{7}{6} \right)^{\frac{1}{4}} - 1 \approx 3.93\%
$$
$$
6000\,(1 + 4\,i) = 7000
\iff
4\,i = \frac{1}{6}
\iff
i = \frac{1}{24} \approx 4.17\%
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Under compound interest the rate appears in the base of the power, and the exponent $4$ is known, so one takes the fourth root of both sides, which are positive, and subtracts $1$. This gives a rate of about $3.93\%$.

Under simple interest the total interest, $1000$ euros, equals $C\,i\,t = 6000 \cdot 4\,i$, so $i = \dfrac{1000}{24\,000} = \dfrac{1}{24}$. The **simple rate** is greater than the compound rate, because under simple interest the interest does not earn interest and a higher rate is therefore needed to obtain the same accumulated value. As a check, $6000 \cdot 1.0393^{4} \approx 7000$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A principal of $5000$ euros is invested at $4\%$ per year compound and a principal of $4000$ euros at $7\%$ per year compound. Determine after how long the two accumulated values are equal, and the common value.

$$
5000 \cdot 1.04^{t} = 4000 \cdot 1.07^{t}
\iff
\left( \frac{1.07}{1.04} \right)^{t} = \frac{5}{4}
$$
$$
t = \frac{\ln 1.25}{\ln 1.07 - \ln 1.04} \approx \frac{0.223144}{0.028438} \approx 7.85 \text{ years}
$$
$$
M = 5000 \cdot 1.04^{t} \approx 6801.82 \text{ euros}
$$

::grafico{f="5000*1.04^x+0*sqrt(x);4000*1.07^x+0*sqrt(x)" punti="7.847,6801.8,pieno" segmenti="7.847,0,7.847,6801.8" xmin=-2 xmax=11 ymin=-1000 ymax=9000 nomi="t,M" legenda="M = 5000 \cdot 1.04^{t};M = 4000 \cdot 1.07^{t}" titolo="The graphs of the two accumulated values intersect at $t \approx 7.85$ years; afterwards, the principal at $7\%$ has the larger accumulated value"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The accumulated values are equal when $5000 \cdot 1.04^{t} = 4000 \cdot 1.07^{t}$. Both sides are divided by $4000 \cdot 1.04^{t}$, which is positive, and the two powers with the same exponent are combined into $\left( \dfrac{1.07}{1.04} \right)^{t}$. Taking the logarithm, and using $\ln \dfrac{1.07}{1.04} = \ln 1.07 - \ln 1.04$, one obtains $t \approx 7.85$ years.

Before this date the larger accumulated value is that of the principal of $5000$ euros, which is larger at time $0$; afterwards, it is that of the principal at $7\%$, because the ratio $\left( \dfrac{1.07}{1.04} \right)^{t}$ is increasing. As a check, with the same value of $t$ also $4000 \cdot 1.07^{t} \approx 6801.82$ euros.

</div>
