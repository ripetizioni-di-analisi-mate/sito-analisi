## Exercise 1

Fill in the French amortization schedule of a loan of $5000$ euros repaid in $4$ annual installments in arrears at the rate of $6\%$, and compute the total interest paid.

$$
a_{\overline{4}|0.06} = \frac{1 - 1.06^{-4}}{0.06} \approx 3.465106,
\qquad
R = \frac{5000}{3.465106} \approx 1442.96 \text{ euros}
$$

| $k$ | $R_k$ | $Q_k$ | $I_k$ | $S_k$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $0$ | | | | $5000.00$ |
| $1$ | $1442.96$ | $1142.96$ | $300.00$ | $3857.04$ |
| $2$ | $1442.96$ | $1211.53$ | $231.42$ | $2645.51$ |
| $3$ | $1442.96$ | $1284.23$ | $158.73$ | $1361.28$ |
| $4$ | $1442.96$ | $1361.28$ | $81.68$ | $0.00$ |

$$
I_1 + I_2 + I_3 + I_4 = 4 \cdot 1442.96 - 5000 \approx 771.83 \text{ euros}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In **French amortization** the installment is constant and the debt is the present value of the installments, so $R = \dfrac{S}{a_{\overline{4}|0.06}}$. Each row of the schedule is filled in in the same order. First one computes the interest portion on the outstanding balance of the previous row, $I_k = 0.06\,S_{k-1}$; then the principal portion by difference, $Q_k = R - I_k$; finally the new outstanding balance, $S_k = S_{k-1} - Q_k$. For the first row one has $I_1 = 0.06 \cdot 5000 = 300$, $Q_1 = 1142.96$ and $S_1 = 3857.04$.

Two checks confirm the schedule. The principal portions grow with common ratio $1.06$, for example $1142.96 \cdot 1.06 \approx 1211.53$, and their sum is $5000$ euros, so the final outstanding balance is **zero**. The total interest is the installments paid minus the principal repaid, about $771.83$ euros. The amounts are rounded to the cent, therefore in some rows $Q_k + I_k$ differs from the installment by one cent.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Fill in the Italian amortization schedule of a loan of $8000$ euros repaid in $5$ annual installments at the rate of $4\%$, and compute the total interest paid.

$$
Q = \frac{8000}{5} = 1600 \text{ euros}
$$

| $k$ | $R_k$ | $Q_k$ | $I_k$ | $S_k$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $0$ | | | | $8000$ |
| $1$ | $1920$ | $1600$ | $320$ | $6400$ |
| $2$ | $1856$ | $1600$ | $256$ | $4800$ |
| $3$ | $1792$ | $1600$ | $192$ | $3200$ |
| $4$ | $1728$ | $1600$ | $128$ | $1600$ |
| $5$ | $1664$ | $1600$ | $64$ | $0$ |

$$
I_1 + \dots + I_5 = 320 + 256 + 192 + 128 + 64 = 960 \text{ euros}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In **Italian amortization** the principal portion is constant, equal to the debt divided by the number of installments, so the outstanding balance decreases by $1600$ euros every year. The interest portion is $4\%$ of the outstanding balance at the beginning of the year, and the installment is the sum of the two portions.

The interest portions decrease by $0.04 \cdot 1600 = 64$ euros every year, and the same holds for the installments, which form a decreasing **arithmetic progression**. The total interest can also be computed with the formula for the sum of the terms of an arithmetic progression, $\dfrac{5\,(320 + 64)}{2} = 960$ euros.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A mortgage of $100\,000$ euros is repaid by French amortization in $25$ annual installments in arrears at $4\%$. Compute the installment, the outstanding balance after the payment of the tenth installment, the principal and interest portions of the eleventh installment, and the first installment in which the principal portion exceeds the interest portion.

$$
R = \frac{100\,000}{a_{\overline{25}|0.04}} \approx \frac{100\,000}{15.622080} \approx 6401.20 \text{ euros}
$$
$$
S_{10} = R\,a_{\overline{15}|0.04} \approx 6401.20 \cdot 11.118387 \approx 71\,170.98 \text{ euros}
$$
$$
I_{11} = 0.04 \cdot S_{10} \approx 2846.84 \text{ euros},
\qquad
Q_{11} = R - I_{11} \approx 3554.36 \text{ euros}
$$
$$
Q_1 = R - 0.04 \cdot 100\,000 \approx 2401.20,
\qquad
Q_k > I_k
\iff
Q_k > \frac{R}{2}
\iff
1.04^{\,k-1} > \frac{3200.60}{2401.20} \approx 1.332918
$$
$$
k - 1 > \frac{\ln 1.332918}{\ln 1.04} \approx 7.33
\iff
k \geq 9
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The installment is obtained by dividing the debt by $a_{\overline{25}|0.04}$. The **outstanding balance** after $10$ installments is computed with the prospective method, that is, as the value at time $10$ of the $15$ installments still to be paid, and in this way there is no need to fill in the first ten rows of the schedule. As a check with the retrospective method, $100\,000 \cdot 1.04^{10} - R\,s_{\overline{10}|0.04} \approx 71\,170.98$ euros.

The eleventh interest portion is computed on the outstanding balance after the tenth installment, and the principal portion is the difference from the installment. The same value is obtained from the geometric progression of the principal portions, $Q_{11} = Q_1 \cdot 1.04^{10} \approx 3554.36$.

Since $Q_k + I_k = R$, the principal portion exceeds the interest portion exactly when it exceeds half the installment. The principal portions grow with common ratio $1.04$, therefore one solves the inequality with the logarithm, which is an increasing function and hence preserves the direction of the inequality. One obtains $k - 1 > 7.33$, so the first installment with $Q_k > I_k$ is the **ninth**; indeed $Q_8 \approx 3159.81 < 3200.60$ and $Q_9 \approx 3286.20 > 3200.60$.

</div>
