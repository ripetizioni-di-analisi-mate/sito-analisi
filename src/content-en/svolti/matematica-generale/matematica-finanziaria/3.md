## Exercise 1

Compute the present value and the accumulated value of an annuity immediate of $8$ annual payments of $1500$ euros at the annual rate of $3\%$.

$$
a_{\overline{8}|0.03} = \frac{1 - 1.03^{-8}}{0.03} \approx 7.019692,
\qquad
V = 1500\,a_{\overline{8}|0.03} \approx 10\,529.54 \text{ euros}
$$
$$
s_{\overline{8}|0.03} = \frac{1.03^{8} - 1}{0.03} \approx 8.892336,
\qquad
M = 1500\,s_{\overline{8}|0.03} \approx 13\,338.50 \text{ euros}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The payments are constant and are made at the end of each year starting from the first, so the annuity is an **annuity immediate**, and the period of the payments coincides with that of the rate. The present value is the payment multiplied by $a_{\overline{8}|0.03}$, the accumulated value is the payment multiplied by $s_{\overline{8}|0.03}$.

As a check, the accumulated value must be the present value carried to the end of the eighth year, and indeed $10\,529.54 \cdot 1.03^{8} \approx 13\,338.50$. The present value is less than the sum of the payments, $12\,000$ euros, because each discounted payment is worth less than its amount; the accumulated value is greater, because each payment earns interest until the end.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A rent of $600$ euros a month is paid at the beginning of each month for one year. At the annual rate of $6\%$, compute the present value of the twelve payments.

$$
i_{12} = 1.06^{\frac{1}{12}} - 1 \approx 0.0048676
$$
$$
a_{\overline{12}|i_{12}} = \frac{1 - (1 + i_{12})^{-12}}{i_{12}} = \frac{1 - 1.06^{-1}}{i_{12}} \approx 11.628800
$$
$$
V = 600\,(1 + i_{12})\,a_{\overline{12}|i_{12}} \approx 600 \cdot 1.0048676 \cdot 11.628800 \approx 7011.24 \text{ euros}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The payments are monthly and the rate is annual, so first one computes the **equivalent monthly rate**, because the annuity formulas require the rate to refer to the same period as the payments. In the computation of $a_{\overline{12}|i_{12}}$ one uses the fact that $(1 + i_{12})^{12} = 1.06$, which avoids raising an approximate number to the twelfth power.

The rent is paid at the beginning of each month, so the annuity is an **annuity due**, and its present value is that of the annuity immediate multiplied by $1 + i_{12}$. The present value of about $7011.24$ euros is the sum that, paid in a single amount at the beginning of the year, is equivalent to the twelve payments.

</div>

## Exercise 3

One wants to have $20\,000$ euros at the end of the tenth year by depositing a constant sum $R$ at the beginning of each of the $10$ years into an account at $2\%$ per year compound. Compute $R$.

$$
s_{\overline{10}|0.02} = \frac{1.02^{10} - 1}{0.02} \approx 10.949721
$$
$$
R\,(1.02)\,s_{\overline{10}|0.02} = 20\,000
\iff
R = \frac{20\,000}{1.02 \cdot 10.949721} \approx 1790.72 \text{ euros}
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-0.5,0 10.6,0" vettori="0,0,0,1.6,R;1,0,1,1.6,R;2,0,2,1.6,R;3,0,3,1.6,R;4,0,4,1.6,R;5,0,5,1.6,R;6,0,6,1.6,R;7,0,7,1.6,R;8,0,8,1.6,R;9,0,9,1.6,R" unicolore=1 punti="10,0,pieno" etichette="0,-0.5,0;1,-0.5,1;2,-0.5,2;5,-0.5,5;9,-0.5,9;10,-0.5,10;10,0.6,M" xmin=-1 xmax=11 ymin=-1 ymax=2.2 titolo="The ten deposits are made at times $0, 1, \dots, 9$, and the accumulated value $M = 20\,000$ euros is required at time $10$, one year after the last deposit"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The deposits are made at the beginning of each year, so they form an **annuity due**, and the balance required at the end of the tenth year is its accumulated value, $R\,(1 + i)\,s_{\overline{10}|i}$. As the diagram shows, the last deposit is made at time $9$ and earns interest for one year, while the first earns interest for $10$ years.

The equation is linear in $R$, and dividing gives a payment of about $1790.72$ euros. As a check, the ten deposits add up to about $17\,907$ euros, and the difference up to $20\,000$ euros is due to the interest.

</div>

## Exercise 4

A debt of $15\,000$ euros is repaid with annual payments in arrears of $2500$ euros at the rate of $5\%$. Determine how many full payments are needed and the amount of the last, reduced payment, made one year after the last full payment.

$$
15\,000 = 2500\,\frac{1 - 1.05^{-n}}{0.05}
\iff
1.05^{-n} = 1 - \frac{15\,000 \cdot 0.05}{2500} = 0.7
$$
$$
n = -\frac{\ln 0.7}{\ln 1.05} \approx 7.31
$$
$$
2500\,a_{\overline{7}|0.05} \approx 2500 \cdot 5.786373 \approx 14\,465.93 \text{ euros}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
X &= (15\,000 - 14\,465.93) \cdot 1.05^{8} \\[6pt]
&\approx 534.07 \cdot 1.477455 \\[6pt]
&\approx 789.06 \text{ euros}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The debt is the present value of the payments, so one imposes $15\,000 = 2500\,a_{\overline{n}|0.05}$ and solves for $1.05^{-n}$. The number $\dfrac{15\,000 \cdot 0.05}{2500} = 0.3$ is less than $1$, because the payment exceeds the interest of the first year, $750$ euros; therefore the problem has a solution, and taking the logarithm gives $n \approx 7.31$.

The number of payments is not an integer, so $7$ **full payments** are made and an eighth, **reduced payment** $X$ at time $8$. The seven full payments cover a present value of about $14\,465.93$ euros; the remaining part of the debt, about $534.07$ euros in present value, must be paid at time $8$, therefore it is multiplied by $1.05^{8}$. The last payment is therefore about $789.06$ euros.

</div>
