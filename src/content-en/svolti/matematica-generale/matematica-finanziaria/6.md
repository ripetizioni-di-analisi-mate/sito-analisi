## Exercise 1

An annuity of $6$ annual payments of $2000$ euros has its first payment in $4$ years. Compute its present value at the rate of $4\%$ per year compound.

$$
a_{\overline{6}|0.04} = \frac{1 - 1.04^{-6}}{0.04} \approx 5.242137,
\qquad
2000\,a_{\overline{6}|0.04} \approx 10\,484.27 \text{ euros}
$$
$$
V = 1.04^{-3} \cdot 10\,484.27 \approx 0.888996 \cdot 10\,484.27 \approx 9320.48 \text{ euros}
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-0.4,0 9.6,0" vettori="4,0,4,1.4,R;5,0,5,1.4,R;6,0,6,1.4,R;7,0,7,1.4,R;8,0,8,1.4,R;9,0,9,1.4,R" unicolore=1 punti="0,0,pieno;3,0,pieno" etichette="0,-0.45,0;1,-0.45,1;2,-0.45,2;3,-0.45,3;4,-0.45,4;9,-0.45,9;0,0.45,V;3,0.45,R·a₆" xmin=-1 xmax=10 ymin=-1 ymax=2 titolo="The six payments are made at times $4, 5, \dots, 9$: at time $3$ they form an annuity immediate, which is then brought back to time $0$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The first payment is made at time $4$, so the annuity is an annuity immediate **deferred** by $p = 3$ years; indeed at time $3$ begins the period at whose end the first payment is made. At time $3$ the payments form an annuity immediate of $6$ payments, which at that date is worth $2000\,a_{\overline{6}|0.04}$.

This value is brought back to time $0$ by multiplying it by the discount factor for $3$ years, $1.04^{-3}$, and one obtains a present value of about $9320.48$ euros. With $p = 4$, that is, with the time of the first payment in place of the deferment, the formula would give the value of six payments made at times $5, \dots, 10$, hence a present value smaller than the correct one.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A property produces an annual rent of $9000$ euros with no end date. At the rate of $4.5\%$ per year, compute the value of the property if the rent is paid at the end of each year and if it is paid at the beginning. Then compute the value if the rent is paid in semiannual installments in arrears of $4500$ euros.

$$
V = \frac{9000}{0.045} = 200\,000 \text{ euros},
\qquad
V = \frac{9000 \cdot 1.045}{0.045} = 209\,000 \text{ euros}
$$
$$
i_2 = 1.045^{\frac{1}{2}} - 1 \approx 0.0222524
$$
$$
V = \frac{4500}{i_2} \approx \frac{4500}{0.0222524} \approx 202\,225.24 \text{ euros}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The rent has no end date, so it is a **perpetuity**, and the value of the property is its present value. If the rent is paid in arrears the value is $\dfrac{R}{i}$; if it is paid in advance each payment is made one year earlier, and the value is multiplied by $1 + i$.

With semiannual installments the period of the annuity is the half-year, therefore one uses the **semiannual rate equivalent** to $4.5\%$ per year, and the semiannual installment is divided by this rate. The value is greater than $200\,000$ euros, because half of the annual rent is collected six months earlier than in the case of annual payments in arrears.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A foundation wants to establish a perpetual scholarship of $3000$ euros per year, with the first disbursement in $5$ years. At the rate of $2.5\%$ per year, compute the sum to be set aside today. Then compute the annual scholarship that can be funded by setting aside $100\,000$ euros today, with the same first due date.

$$
V = 1.025^{-4} \cdot \frac{3000}{0.025} = 1.025^{-4} \cdot 120\,000 \approx 0.905951 \cdot 120\,000 \approx 108\,714.08 \text{ euros}
$$
$$
100\,000 = 1.025^{-4} \cdot \frac{R}{0.025}
\iff
R = 100\,000 \cdot 0.025 \cdot 1.025^{4} \approx 2759.53 \text{ euros}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The disbursements begin at time $5$ and never end, so they form a perpetuity immediate **deferred** by $4$ years. At time $4$ the perpetuity is worth $\dfrac{3000}{0.025} = 120\,000$ euros, and this sum is discounted for $4$ years.

In the second part the present value is known and the payment is unknown. The equation is linear in $R$, and one obtains a scholarship of about $2759.53$ euros per year. As a check, $100\,000$ euros accumulated for $4$ years become about $110\,381$ euros, and $2.5\%$ of this sum is exactly the payment found; indeed a perpetuity with payment $R$ is funded with the principal $\dfrac{R}{i}$, which produces the interest $R$ every year without decreasing.

</div>
