## Exercise 1

Solve the equation $2^{x + 1} = 3^{x}$.

$$
(x + 1) \ln 2 = x \ln 3
\iff
x\,(\ln 3 - \ln 2) = \ln 2
\iff
x = \frac{\ln 2}{\ln 3 - \ln 2} = \frac{\ln 2}{\ln \frac{3}{2}} \approx 1.71
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two sides are exponentials with different bases, so the exponents cannot be compared directly. Take the natural **logarithm** of both sides, which are positive, and by the property $\ln a^{k} = k \ln a$ the exponents become factors. This gives a first-degree equation in $x$; factoring out $x$ and dividing by $\ln 3 - \ln 2 = \ln \dfrac{3}{2}$, which is positive, yields $x \approx 1.71$. As a check, $2^{2.71} \approx 6.5$ and $3^{1.71} \approx 6.5$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A capital of $2000$ euros, invested at compound interest, is worth $2662$ euros after $3$ years. Find the annual rate.

$$
2000\,(1 + i)^{3} = 2662
\iff
(1 + i)^{3} = 1.331
$$
$$
1 + i = \sqrt[3]{1.331} = 1.1
\iff
i = 0.1 = 10\%
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

At compound interest the capital is multiplied each year by $1 + i$, where $i$ is the annual rate, so after $3$ years it is multiplied by $(1 + i)^{3}$. Dividing by $2000$ gives $(1 + i)^{3} = 1.331$. Here the unknown is the base, not the exponent, therefore we take the cube root of both sides and the logarithm is not needed. Since $1.1^{3} = 1.331$, the **annual rate** is $10\%$. As a check, $2000 \cdot 1.1 = 2200$, then $2420$, then $2662$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve the inequality $\ln(x - 1) + \ln(x + 1) < \ln 3$.

$$
\begin{cases}
x - 1 > 0 \\[4pt]
x + 1 > 0
\end{cases}
\iff
x > 1
$$
$$
\ln\big( (x - 1)(x + 1) \big) < \ln 3
\iff
x^2 - 1 < 3
\iff
-2 < x < 2
$$
$$
1 < x < 2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

First impose that the arguments of the two logarithms be positive, conditions that must hold together and give $x > 1$. By the property of the **logarithm** of a product, the left-hand side becomes $\ln(x^2 - 1)$. The logarithm is increasing, so the inequality between the logarithms is equivalent to the inequality between the arguments, $x^2 - 1 < 3$, which gives $-2 < x < 2$. Intersecting with the condition $x > 1$, the solutions are $1 < x < 2$.

</div>
