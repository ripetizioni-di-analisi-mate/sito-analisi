## Exercise 1

Find the linear function whose graph passes through the points $(2, 7)$ and $(6, -1)$, and find the point where the graph crosses the $x$-axis.

$$
m = \frac{-1 - 7}{6 - 2} = \frac{-8}{4} = -2
$$
$$
f(x) = 7 - 2\,(x - 2) = -2x + 11
$$
$$
f(x) = 0
\iff
x = \frac{11}{2}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the formula for the **line through two points**, the slope is the ratio between the change in the $y$-coordinates and the change in the $x$-coordinates, so it equals $-2$. The line passes through $(2, 7)$ with slope $-2$, and expanding gives $f(x) = -2x + 11$. As a check, $f(6) = -12 + 11 = -1$. The slope is negative, therefore the function is decreasing, and every increase of $1$ in the variable makes $f$ decrease by $2$. The graph crosses the $x$-axis where $f(x) = 0$, that is, at the point $\left( \dfrac{11}{2},\ 0 \right)$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A firm incurs a cost of $3800$ euros to produce $200$ pieces and of $6500$ euros to produce $500$. Assuming the cost is linear in the quantity, find the fixed cost, the variable unit cost and the cost of $350$ pieces.

$$
m = \frac{6500 - 3800}{500 - 200} = \frac{2700}{300} = 9
$$
$$
C(q) = 3800 + 9\,(q - 200) = 2000 + 9q
$$
$$
C(350) = 2000 + 9 \cdot 350 = 5150 \text{ euros}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The cost is linear, so its graph is the line passing through the points $(200, 3800)$ and $(500, 6500)$. The slope is the change in cost divided by the change in quantity. Since $300$ more pieces cost $2700$ euros more, each additional piece costs $9$ euros, which is the **variable unit cost**. The intercept is the cost for $q = 0$, that is, the **fixed cost**, and it equals $2000$ euros. The cost of $350$ pieces is $5150$ euros. As a check, $C(200) = 2000 + 1800 = 3800$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Write the equation of the line that passes through the point $(-2, 5)$ and is parallel to the line $y = 3x - 1$, and that of the line through the same point perpendicular to $y = 3x - 1$.

$$
\text{parallel:}\qquad m = 3,
\qquad
y = 5 + 3\,(x + 2) = 3x + 11
$$
$$
\text{perpendicular:}\qquad m' = -\frac{1}{3},
\qquad
y = 5 - \frac{1}{3}\,(x + 2) = -\frac{1}{3}\,x + \frac{13}{3}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Two non-vertical lines are parallel when they have the same **slope**, so the first line has slope $3$ and passes through $(-2, 5)$. Two lines are perpendicular when the product of their slopes is $-1$, therefore the second has slope $-\dfrac{1}{3}$. In both cases the equation is written in the form $y = y_1 + m\,(x - x_1)$ with $(x_1, y_1) = (-2, 5)$ and then expanded. As a check, substituting $x = -2$ both equations give $y = 5$.

</div>
