## Exercise 1

Calculate $(2 - 3i)(4 + i) - (1 + 2i)^2$ and write the result in algebraic form.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(2 - 3i)(4 + i) &= 8 + 2i - 12i - 3i^2 \\[6pt]
&= 8 - 10i + 3 \\[6pt]
&= 11 - 10i
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1 + 2i)^2 &= 1 + 4i + 4i^2 \\[6pt]
&= 1 + 4i - 4 \\[6pt]
&= -3 + 4i
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
(11 - 10i) - (-3 + 4i) = (11 + 3) + (-10 - 4)\,i = 14 - 14i
$$

The result is $14 - 14i$, with real part $14$ and imaginary part $-14$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The expression contains a product and a square, and the order of operations is the same as for real numbers. Therefore, calculate the product and square first, then take the difference.

Expand the product $(2 - 3i)(4 + i)$ using the **distributive property**, multiplying each term of the first binomial by each term of the second. This gives the four terms $8$, $2i$, $-12i$, and $-3i^2$. Since $i^2 = -1$, the term $-3i^2$ equals $+3$. Adding the real terms $8$ and $3$ and the terms containing $i$, namely $2i$ and $-12i$, gives $11 - 10i$.

To find the square $(1 + 2i)^2$, use the identity $(a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$ with $a = 1$ and $b = 2i$. The square of $2i$ is $4i^2 = -4$, so the result is $1 - 4 + 4i = -3 + 4i$.

In the last line, subtract $-3 + 4i$ by changing the sign of both its parts. By the subtraction rule, subtract the real parts, $11 - (-3) = 14$, and the imaginary parts, $-10 - 4 = -14$, separately. The result $14 - 14i$ is in **algebraic form** because the real and imaginary parts are separate.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Calculate $i^{2026} + i^{-3}$ and the sum $S = 1 + i + i^2 + i^3 + \dots + i^{10}$.

$$
2026 = 4 \cdot 506 + 2
\qquad
\Longrightarrow
\qquad
i^{2026} = i^2 = -1
$$
$$
i^{-3} = \frac{1}{i^3} = \frac{1}{-i} = \frac{i}{-i \cdot i} = \frac{i}{1} = i
$$
$$
i^{2026} + i^{-3} = -1 + i
$$
$$
1 + i + i^2 + i^3 = 1 + i - 1 - i = 0
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
S &= \left( 1 + i + i^2 + i^3 \right) + i^4\left( 1 + i + i^2 + i^3 \right) + i^8 + i^9 + i^{10} \\[6pt]
&= 0 + 0 + 1 + i - 1 \\[6pt]
&= i
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus $i^{2026} + i^{-3} = -1 + i$ and $S = i$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The powers of $i$ repeat in cycles of four because $i^4 = 1$. For this reason, **$i^n$ depends only on the remainder** when $n$ is divided by $4$. Dividing $2026$ by $4$ gives quotient $506$ and remainder $2$, so $i^{2026} = i^2 = -1$.

A power with a negative exponent is the reciprocal of the corresponding power with a positive exponent, just as for real numbers. Since $i^3 = -i$, we must calculate $\dfrac{1}{-i}$. To eliminate $i$ from the denominator, multiply the numerator and denominator by $i$. The denominator becomes $-i^2 = 1$, so $i^{-3} = i$. The required sum is therefore $-1 + i$.

For the sum $S$, observe that four consecutive powers of $i$ sum to $0$ because $1$ and $-1$ cancel, as do $i$ and $-i$. The sum $S$ contains the eleven powers from $i^0$ to $i^{10}$. The first eight form two groups of four consecutive powers, each with sum $0$. The second group is the first multiplied by $i^4 = 1$. The remaining terms are $i^8 = 1$, $i^9 = i$, and $i^{10} = -1$, and their sum is $i$. Thus **$S = i$**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Find the real numbers $x$ and $y$ such that $(x + 2i)(1 - i) = 7 + y\,i$. Then plot the numbers $z = x + 2i$ and $w = 1 - i$ and their sum in the complex plane.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x + 2i)(1 - i) &= x - x\,i + 2i - 2i^2 \\[6pt]
&= (x + 2) + (2 - x)\,i
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
(x + 2) + (2 - x)\,i = 7 + y\,i
\iff
\begin{cases}
x + 2 = 7 \\[4pt]
2 - x = y
\end{cases}
\iff
\begin{cases}
x = 5 \\[4pt]
y = -3
\end{cases}
$$
$$
z = 5 + 2i ,
\qquad
w = 1 - i ,
\qquad
z + w = 6 + i
$$

::grafico{vettori="0,0,5,2,z;0,0,1,-1,w;0,0,6,1,z + w" segmenti="5,2,6,1;1,-1,6,1" punti="6,1,pieno" xmin=-1 xmax=7 ymin=-2 ymax=3 uguale=1 nomi="Re,Im" titolo="The vectors $z = 5 + 2i$ and $w = 1 - i$ and their sum $z + w = 6 + i$, the diagonal of the parallelogram"}

Thus $x = 5$ and $y = -3$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The unknowns $x$ and $y$ are real numbers, so an equality between two complex numbers is equivalent to two equalities between real numbers. To apply this rule, first write the left-hand side in algebraic form.

Expand the product $(x + 2i)(1 - i)$ using the distributive property. The term $-2i^2$ equals $+2$ because $i^2 = -1$. Then collect the terms without $i$, namely $x$ and $2$, and the coefficients of $i$, namely $-x$ and $2$. Thus the real part of the product is $x + 2$ and the imaginary part is $2 - x$. Both are real numbers because $x$ is real.

By the rule for **equality of two complex numbers**, the real parts of the two sides must be equal and their imaginary parts must be equal. Both conditions must hold at the same time, so we write them as a system. The first equation gives $x = 5$, and substituting into the second gives $y = 2 - 5 = -3$.

With $x = 5$, the number $z$ is $5 + 2i$, represented by the point $(5, 2)$, while $w = 1 - i$ is the point $(1, -1)$. Adding components gives the sum, so $z + w = 6 + i$. In the figure, the vector $z + w$ is the diagonal of the parallelogram with sides $z$ and $w$, by the **parallelogram rule**.

</div>
