## Exercise 1

A right prism has an isosceles triangular base $ABC$ with base $AB = 10$ and equal sides $AC = BC = 13$. The prism has height $8$. Find its lateral surface area, total surface area, and volume.

$$
CM = \sqrt{AC^2 - AM^2} = \sqrt{169 - 25} = \sqrt{144} = 12
$$
$$
B = \frac{AB \cdot CM}{2} = \frac{10 \cdot 12}{2} = 60
$$
$$
2p = 10 + 13 + 13 = 36
$$
$$
A_l = 2p \cdot h = 36 \cdot 8 = 288
$$
$$
A_t = A_l + 2B = 288 + 120 = 408
$$
$$
V = B \cdot h = 60 \cdot 8 = 480
$$

Segment $CM$ is the altitude of the triangular base"}

The lateral surface area is $288$, the total surface area is $408$, and the volume is $480$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The prism formulas require the area and perimeter of the triangular base. To find the area, we need the altitude of the triangle to base $AB$. In an isosceles triangle this altitude meets the base at its midpoint $M$, so $AM = 5$. Triangle $AMC$ has a right angle at $M$ and hypotenuse $AC = 13$, and the Pythagorean theorem gives $CM = 12$. The base area is therefore $B = 60$.

The base perimeter is the sum of the three sides, $2p = 36$. The prism is **right**, so its lateral faces are rectangles of height $8$ and the lateral surface area is the base perimeter times the height, namely $288$.

Add the areas of the two bases, congruent triangles of area $60$, to the lateral surface area to obtain a total surface area of $408$. The volume is the base area times the height, or $480$. The formula $V = B \cdot h$ holds for any prism. The formula for lateral surface area as perimeter times height requires a right prism, since only then are the lateral faces rectangles with height equal to the height of the prism.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A regular hexagonal prism has base edge $4$ and height $10$. Find its total surface area and volume.

$$
B = 6 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \cdot 4^2 = 6 \cdot 4\sqrt{3} = 24\sqrt{3}
$$
$$
A_l = 2p \cdot h = 24 \cdot 10 = 240
$$
$$
A_t = A_l + 2B = 240 + 48\sqrt{3}
$$
$$
V = B \cdot h = 24\sqrt{3} \cdot 10 = 240\sqrt{3}
$$

The total surface area is $240 + 48\sqrt{3}$, approximately $323.1$, and the volume is $240\sqrt{3}$, approximately $415.7$.

<div class="spiegazione">

A **regular** prism is a right prism whose bases are regular polygons. Here the base is a regular hexagon of side $4$.

Joining the center of a regular hexagon to its vertices divides it into six equilateral triangles whose sides are as long as a side of the hexagon. The area of an equilateral triangle of side $l$ is $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\,l^2$ because its height is $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,l$. With $l = 4$, each triangle has area $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \cdot 16 = 4\sqrt{3}$, so the hexagon has area $24\sqrt{3}$.

The base perimeter is $6 \cdot 4 = 24$. The prism is right, so its lateral surface area is the perimeter times the height, or $240$. Equivalently, the lateral faces are six rectangles with sides $4$ and $10$, each of area $40$.

The total surface area is the lateral surface area plus the areas of the two hexagonal bases, namely $240 + 2 \cdot 24\sqrt{3} = 240 + 48\sqrt{3}$. The volume is the base area times the height, or $240\sqrt{3}$. To find the approximate values, use $\sqrt{3} \approx 1.732$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The base of a right prism is a rhombus with diagonals of lengths $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $16\,\mathrm{cm}$. The prism has lateral surface area $400\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. Find its height, total surface area, and volume.

$$
l = \sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{36 + 64} = 10
$$
$$
2p = 4 \cdot 10 = 40
$$
$$
h = \frac{A_l}{2p} = \frac{400}{40} = 10
$$
$$
B = \frac{12 \cdot 16}{2} = 96
$$
$$
A_t = A_l + 2B = 400 + 192 = 592
$$
$$
V = B \cdot h = 96 \cdot 10 = 960
$$

The height is $10\,\mathrm{cm}$, the total surface area is $592\,\mathrm{cm}^2$, and the volume is $960\,\mathrm{cm}^3$.

<div class="spiegazione">

Here the unknown is the height, so solve the lateral surface area formula for the height. This requires the base perimeter, so we need the side length of the rhombus.

The diagonals of a rhombus are perpendicular bisectors of each other. Thus the rhombus splits into four congruent right triangles with legs $6$ and $8$, half the diagonal lengths, and hypotenuse equal to the side of the rhombus. The Pythagorean theorem gives a side length of $10$, so the base perimeter is $40$.

The prism is right, so $A_l = 2p \cdot h$. Dividing the lateral surface area by the perimeter gives $h = 10$.

The area of the rhombus is half the product of its diagonals, or $96$. The total surface area is the lateral surface area plus the areas of the two bases, $400 + 192 = 592$, and the volume is the base area times the height, or $960$. The lengths are in centimeters, so the total surface area is in square centimeters and the volume in cubic centimeters.

</div>
