## Exercise 1

A principal of $2000$ euros is invested for $5$ years at an annual interest rate of $3\%$. Calculate the final amount under simple interest and under compound interest, and compare the two results.

$$
i = 0.03
$$
$$
M_5^{\text{simple}} = 2000 \cdot (1 + 5 \cdot 0.03) = 2000 \cdot 1.15 = 2300
$$
$$
M_5^{\text{comp}} = 2000 \cdot 1.03^5 \approx 2000 \cdot 1.159274 \approx 2318.55
$$
$$
2318.55 - 2300 = 18.55
$$

::grafico{punti="0,0,pieno;1,6,pieno;2,12,pieno;3,18,pieno;4,24,pieno;5,30,pieno;1,6,vuoto;2,12.18,vuoto;3,18.5454,vuoto;4,25.1018,vuoto;5,31.8548,vuoto" xmin=0 xmax=6 ymin=0 ymax=35 nomi="n,I" titolo="Interest earned, in tens of euros, on $2000$ euros at $3\%$. Filled points show simple interest and open points show compound interest"}

The accumulated amount is $2300$ euros with simple interest and approximately $2318.55$ euros with compound interest, which yields $18.55$ euros more.

<div class="spiegazione">

Write the interest rate of $3\%$ as a decimal, $i = 0.03$. With **simple interest**, the annual interest is always calculated on the initial principal and equals $2000 \cdot 0.03 = 60$ euros. After $5$ years the interest is $300$ euros, and the accumulated amount is $2300$ euros, as given by the formula $M_n = C\,(1 + n\,i)$. The year-end amounts, $2000, 2060, 2120, \ldots$, form an arithmetic progression with common difference $60$.

With **compound interest**, each year's accumulated amount is the previous year's amount multiplied by $1 + i = 1.03$ because the interest already earned also earns interest. The amounts therefore form a geometric progression with common ratio $1.03$, and after $5$ years the accumulated amount is $M_5 = 2000 \cdot 1.03^5$. A calculator gives approximately $1.159274$ for the power. Multiplying by $2000$ and rounding to the nearest cent gives $2318.55$ euros.

The difference between the two amounts is $18.55$ euros. In the first year the amounts are equal because in both cases the interest is calculated on the initial principal alone. From the second year onward, compound interest yields more, and the difference increases each year, as the graph shows.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A principal is invested at an annual rate of $4\%$ with compound interest. After how many years does the accumulated amount exceed twice the initial principal?

$$
C \cdot 1.04^n > 2C
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
1.04^n > 2
$$
$$
n \ln 1.04 > \ln 2
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
n > \frac{\ln 2}{\ln 1.04} \approx 17.67
$$
$$
1.04^{17} \approx 1.948 < 2,
\qquad
1.04^{18} \approx 2.026 > 2
$$

The accumulated amount exceeds twice the principal after $18$ years.

<div class="spiegazione">

After $n$ years the accumulated amount is $C \cdot 1.04^n$ by the **compound interest** formula. Require this amount to exceed $2C$ and divide both sides by $C$, which is positive. The direction of the inequality is preserved, giving $1.04^n > 2$. The result is independent of the initial principal.

This is an exponential inequality with base greater than $1$. It can be solved using logarithms, as shown in the section on logarithms. The natural logarithm is an increasing function, so taking it on both sides preserves the direction of the inequality, and the power rule for logarithms gives $n \ln 1.04 > \ln 2$. The number $\ln 1.04$ is positive because $1.04 > 1$, so dividing by $\ln 1.04$ again preserves the direction of the inequality.

A calculator gives $\dfrac{\ln 2}{\ln 1.04} \approx 17.67$. The number of years is an integer, so the first value that satisfies the inequality is $n = 18$. The values of $1.04^{17}$ and $1.04^{18}$ confirm the result because the first is less than $2$ and the second is greater than $2$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

At the end of each year for $4$ years, a deposit of $500$ euros is made into an account that earns $2\%$ per year in compound interest. What is the account balance immediately after the fourth deposit?

$$
V = 500 + 500 \cdot 1.02 + 500 \cdot 1.02^2 + 500 \cdot 1.02^3
$$
$$
a_1 = 500, \qquad q = 1.02, \qquad n = 4
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
V &= 500 \cdot \frac{1.02^4 - 1}{1.02 - 1} \\[6pt]
&= 500 \cdot \frac{1.08243216 - 1}{0.02} \\[6pt]
&= 500 \cdot 4.121608 \approx 2060.80
\end{aligned}
$$

After the fourth deposit the account balance is approximately $2060.80$ euros.

<div class="spiegazione">

Each deposit remains in the account for a different number of years, so calculate its value at the end of the fourth year separately. The fourth deposit has just been made and is worth $500$ euros. The third has been in the account for one year and is worth $500 \cdot 1.02$, the second for two years and is worth $500 \cdot 1.02^2$, and the first for three years and is worth $500 \cdot 1.02^3$, by the **compound interest** formula.

Read from the most recent deposit to the oldest, the four values form a geometric progression with first term $500$ and common ratio $1.02$. The account balance is their sum. Use the formula for the **sum of the first n terms** of a geometric progression, written as $S_n = a_1 \cdot \dfrac{q^n - 1}{q - 1}$ by reversing the signs of the numerator and denominator so that both are positive.

The power $1.02^4$ equals $1.08243216$ exactly, so the numerator is $0.08243216$. The denominator is $0.02$ and the quotient is $4.121608$. Multiplying by $500$ gives $2060.804$, which rounds to $2060.80$ euros to the nearest cent. The deposits total $2000$ euros, so the interest earned is approximately $60.80$ euros.

</div>
