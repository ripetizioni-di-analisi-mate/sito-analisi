## Exercise 1

An ideal source with $\mathcal{E} = 12\,\mathrm{V}$ powers resistor $R_1 = 2.0\,\Omega$ in series with the parallel pair $R_2 = 6.0\,\Omega$ and $R_3 = 3.0\,\Omega$. Calculate equivalent resistance, source current and the currents through $R_2$ and $R_3$.

![Circuit nodes with a 2-ohm resistor in series with a 6- and 3-ohm parallel pair.](/figure/fisica-semestre-filtro/elettricita-e-magnetismo/circuito-en.svg)

$$
R_{23} = \dfrac{R_2 R_3}{R_2 + R_3} ,
\qquad
R_{\text{eq}} = R_1 + R_{23} ,
\qquad
I = \dfrac{\mathcal{E}}{R_{\text{eq}}} ,
\qquad
\Delta V_{23} = R_{23}\, I ,
\qquad
I_k = \dfrac{\Delta V_{23}}{R_k}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{23} &= \dfrac{6.0 \cdot 3.0}{9.0}\,\Omega = 2.0\,\Omega ,
\qquad
R_{\text{eq}} = 4.0\,\Omega ,
\qquad
I = \dfrac{12}{4.0}\,\mathrm{A} = 3.0\,\mathrm{A} \\[6pt]
\Delta V_{23} &= 2.0 \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{V} = 6.0\,\mathrm{V} ,
\qquad
I_2 = \dfrac{6.0}{6.0}\,\mathrm{A} = 1.0\,\mathrm{A} ,
\qquad
I_3 = \dfrac{6.0}{3.0}\,\mathrm{A} = 2.0\,\mathrm{A}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Resistors $R_2$ and $R_3$ connect to the same two nodes, so they are **parallel** and can be replaced by $R_{23}$. Resistor $R_1$ carries the same current as that equivalent group, so it is in series and resistances add. Ohm’s law for the equivalent resistance gives source current.

The parallel pair has voltage $R_{23}I=6.0\,\mathrm V$; the remaining $6.0\,\mathrm V$ drops across $R_1$. Each parallel resistor therefore has $6.0\,\mathrm V$, giving the two branch currents. As a check, $I_2+I_3=3.0\,\mathrm A=I$, consistent with charge conservation at the node; the lower resistance carries the larger current.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The parallel pair $R_1 = 4.0\,\Omega$ and $R_2 = 12\,\Omega$ is in series with $R_3 = 5.0\,\Omega$; that branch is in parallel with $R_4 = 8.0\,\Omega$. The whole network is powered by an ideal source with $\mathcal{E} = 24\,\mathrm{V}$. Calculate equivalent resistance and current through each resistor.

![A 5-ohm resistor in series with a 4- and 12-ohm parallel pair, beside an 8-ohm branch.](/figure/fisica-semestre-filtro/elettricita-e-magnetismo/rete-doppia-en.svg)

$$
R_{12} = \dfrac{R_1 R_2}{R_1 + R_2} ,
\qquad
R_{123} = R_{12} + R_3 ,
\qquad
R_{\text{eq}} = \dfrac{R_{123} R_4}{R_{123} + R_4}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{12} &= \dfrac{4.0 \cdot 12}{16}\,\Omega = 3.0\,\Omega ,
\qquad
R_{123} = 8.0\,\Omega ,
\qquad
R_{\text{eq}} = \dfrac{8.0 \cdot 8.0}{16}\,\Omega = 4.0\,\Omega \\[6pt]
I &= \dfrac{24}{4.0}\,\mathrm{A} = 6.0\,\mathrm{A} ,
\qquad
I_4 = \dfrac{24}{8.0}\,\mathrm{A} = 3.0\,\mathrm{A} ,
\qquad
I_3 = \dfrac{24}{8.0}\,\mathrm{A} = 3.0\,\mathrm{A} \\[6pt]
\Delta V_{12} &= R_{12}\, I_3 = 9.0\,\mathrm{V} ,
\qquad
I_1 = \dfrac{9.0}{4.0}\,\mathrm{A} \approx 2.3\,\mathrm{A} ,
\qquad
I_2 = \dfrac{9.0}{12}\,\mathrm{A} = 0.75\,\mathrm{A}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Reduce the network starting with the innermost group. The parallel pair $R_1,R_2$ is equivalent to $3.0\,\Omega$. That group carries the same current as $R_3$, making a series branch of $8.0\,\Omega$. This branch and $R_4$ span the source terminals, so they are parallel; two equal resistances in parallel give half either value, $4.0\,\Omega$.

Both branches receive the full source voltage $\mathcal E$ and have equal resistance, so each carries $3.0\,\mathrm A$. Within the composite branch, **voltage division** splits $24\,\mathrm V$ into $15\,\mathrm V$ across $R_3$ and $9.0\,\mathrm V$ across $R_{12}$. Ohm’s law then gives currents in $R_1$ and $R_2$, which add to $3.0\,\mathrm A$. The unrounded value of $I_1$ is $2.25\,\mathrm A$.

</div>
