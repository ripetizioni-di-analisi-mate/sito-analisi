## Exercise 1

Initially uncharged capacitors $C_1 = 2.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ and $C_2 = 3.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ are connected to a $\Delta V = 12\,\mathrm{V}$ source, first in parallel and then, after discharging, in series. For each connection, find equivalent capacitance and the charge and voltage of each capacitor.

$$
\text{parallel:} \quad C_{\text{eq}} = C_1 + C_2 , \qquad \Delta V_1 = \Delta V_2 = \Delta V , \qquad Q_i = C_i\,\Delta V
$$
$$
\text{series:} \quad C_{\text{eq}} = \dfrac{C_1 C_2}{C_1 + C_2} , \qquad Q_1 = Q_2 = C_{\text{eq}}\,\Delta V , \qquad \Delta V_i = \dfrac{Q_1}{C_i}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{parallel:} \quad C_{\text{eq}} &= 5.0\,\mu\mathrm{F} , \qquad Q_1 = 2.0 \cdot 12\,\mu\mathrm{C} = 24\,\mu\mathrm{C} , \qquad Q_2 = 3.0 \cdot 12\,\mu\mathrm{C} = 36\,\mu\mathrm{C} \\[6pt]
\text{series:} \quad C_{\text{eq}} &= \dfrac{2.0 \cdot 3.0}{5.0}\,\mu\mathrm{F} = 1.2\,\mu\mathrm{F} , \qquad Q_1 = Q_2 = 1.2 \cdot 12\,\mu\mathrm{C} \approx 14\,\mu\mathrm{C} \\[6pt]
\Delta V_1 &= \dfrac{14.4}{2.0}\,\mathrm{V} = 7.2\,\mathrm{V} , \qquad \Delta V_2 = \dfrac{14.4}{3.0}\,\mathrm{V} = 4.8\,\mathrm{V}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In parallel, plates joined to the same wire share a potential, so both capacitors receive the source voltage and **parallel capacitances** add. Each charge is proportional to its capacitance, and total positive-plate charge $60\,\mu\mathrm C$ equals $C_{\mathrm{eq}}\Delta V$.

In series, the inner plates form an isolated neutral conductor, so capacitor charge magnitudes are equal and **series capacitance** follows by adding reciprocals. Equivalent capacitance $1.2\,\mu\mathrm F$ is smaller than both individual values. The smaller capacitor has the larger voltage, and the two voltages sum to $12\,\mathrm V$, as required by potential differences along the series path.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In the illustrated network, $C_1 = 4.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ is in series with the parallel pair $C_2 = 2.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ and $C_3 = 6.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}$. The network is connected to $\Delta V = 24\,\mathrm{V}$ and the capacitors are initially uncharged. Calculate equivalent capacitance and the charge and voltage of each capacitor.

![A 4-microfarad capacitor in series with a 2- and 6-microfarad parallel pair.](/figure/fisica-semestre-filtro/elettricita-e-magnetismo/rete-condensatori-en.svg)

$$
C_{23} = C_2 + C_3 , \qquad C_{\text{eq}} = \dfrac{C_1 C_{23}}{C_1 + C_{23}} , \qquad Q_1 = C_{\text{eq}}\,\Delta V
$$
$$
\Delta V_1 = \dfrac{Q_1}{C_1} , \qquad \Delta V_{23} = \dfrac{Q_1}{C_{23}} , \qquad Q_2 = C_2\,\Delta V_{23} , \qquad Q_3 = C_3\,\Delta V_{23}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
C_{23} &= (2.0 + 6.0)\,\mu\mathrm{F} = 8.0\,\mu\mathrm{F} \\[6pt]
C_{\text{eq}} &= \dfrac{4.0 \cdot 8.0}{12}\,\mu\mathrm{F} \approx 2.7\,\mu\mathrm{F} \\[6pt]
Q_1 &= \dfrac{8}{3} \cdot 24\,\mu\mathrm{C} = 64\,\mu\mathrm{C} \\[6pt]
\Delta V_1 &= \dfrac{64}{4.0}\,\mathrm{V} = 16\,\mathrm{V} , \qquad \Delta V_{23} = \dfrac{64}{8.0}\,\mathrm{V} = 8.0\,\mathrm{V} \\[6pt]
Q_2 &= 2.0 \cdot 8.0\,\mu\mathrm{C} = 16\,\mu\mathrm{C} , \qquad Q_3 = 6.0 \cdot 8.0\,\mu\mathrm{C} = 48\,\mu\mathrm{C}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Capacitors $C_2$ and $C_3$ connect to the same two nodes, so they are parallel and can be replaced by $C_{23}=8.0\,\mu\mathrm F$. This equivalent capacitor is in series with $C_1$ because the intermediate node between $C_1$ and the equivalent group is isolated and initially neutral. Network capacitance is therefore $\dfrac{8}{3}\,\mu\mathrm F$.

Now work back through the network. Charge supplied by the source is that of the equivalent capacitor and, by the series constraint, also the magnitude on $C_1$ and on the parallel group. Use charge to obtain voltages, which sum to $24\,\mathrm V$. Finally, $C_2$ and $C_3$ share $8.0\,\mathrm V$, and their charges $16\,\mu\mathrm C$ and $48\,\mu\mathrm C$ add back to $64\,\mu\mathrm C$, providing a check.

</div>
