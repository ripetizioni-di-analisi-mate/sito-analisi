## Exercise 1

Find the equation of the perpendicular bisector of the segment with endpoints $A=(0,0)$ and $B=(6,8)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
PA^2&=PB^2\\
x^2+y^2&=(x-6)^2+(y-8)^2\\
x^2+y^2&=x^2+y^2-12x-16y+100\\
12x+16y&=100\\
3x+4y&=25.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

A point on the perpendicular bisector must be equally far from both endpoints. Distances are nonnegative, so their squares can be equated without changing the solution set. The squared terms cancel because the same coefficients of $x^2$ and $y^2$ occur in each squared distance. A linear equation remains. As a check, the midpoint $(3,4)$ satisfies $3\cdot3+4\cdot4=25$. Also the perpendicular bisector has slope $-\dfrac34$ while the segment has slope $\dfrac43$, and the product of the two slopes is $-1$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Find the locus of points equidistant from $F=(2,0)$ and the line $x=-2$. Determine the vertex and axis.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sqrt{(x-2)^2+y^2}&=|x+2|\\
(x-2)^2+y^2&=(x+2)^2\\
x^2-4x+4+y^2&=x^2+4x+4\\
y^2&=8x .
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
V=(0,0),\qquad y=0.
$$

::grafico{curve="t^2/8,t,-6,6" xmin=-4 xmax=6 ymin=-5 ymax=5 uguale=1 asintoti="x=-2" punti="0,0,pieno,V;2,0,pieno,F;2,4,pieno,P;-2,4,pieno,H" segmenti="2,0,2,4;-2,4,2,4" legenda="y^2=8x" titolo="At $P=(2,4)$ the distances from focus $F$ and from the directrix, measured to $H$, are both $4$."}

<div class="spiegazione">

Distance from the vertical line $x=-2$ is measured horizontally, so it is the absolute difference between the abscissas. Distance from the focus instead comes from the two-point distance formula. Both sides are nonnegative, so squaring is reversible. The resulting equation is $x=\dfrac{y^2}{8}$, so every point has $x\geq0$ and the minimum is $0$ at $y=0$. Reversing the sign of $y$ leaves the abscissa unchanged, so the axis is horizontal. At $(2,4)$ the distance from the focus is $4$, and the distance from the directrix is also $|2+2|=4$.

</div>
