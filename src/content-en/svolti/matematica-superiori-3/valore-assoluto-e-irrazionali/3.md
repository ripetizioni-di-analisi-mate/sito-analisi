## Exercise 1

Solve $|x-1|>3$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x-1<-3 &\iff x<-2,\\[4pt]
x-1>3 &\iff x>4,\\[4pt]
S&=(-\infty,-2)\cup(4,+\infty).
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The allowed points are more than three units from $1$. On the number line, they are before $-2$ or after $4$. A value only needs to satisfy one of the two comparisons, so take the union.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve $|x^2-1|\geq1$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^2-1\leq-1 &\iff x^2\leq0 \iff x=0,\\[4pt]
x^2-1\geq1 &\iff x^2\geq2,\\[4pt]
S&=(-\infty,-\sqrt2]\cup\{0\}\cup[\sqrt2,+\infty).
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The branch $x^2-1\leq-1$ is possible only at $0$ because a square is nonnegative. The branch $x^2-1\geq1$ requires a distance from zero of at least $\sqrt2$. Keep the isolated point $0$ because its image equals $|-1|=1$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve $|x-2|>x$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x<2&\implies 2-x>x \iff x<1,\\[4pt]
x\geq2&\implies x-2>x \iff -2>0,\\[4pt]
S&=(-\infty,1).
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For $x<2$, $x-2$ is negative and we use its opposite. The condition $x<1$ already lies within that branch, so it requires no further restriction there. For $x\geq2$, use $x-2$, which is always less than $x$. The point $1$ gives equality and is excluded.

</div>
