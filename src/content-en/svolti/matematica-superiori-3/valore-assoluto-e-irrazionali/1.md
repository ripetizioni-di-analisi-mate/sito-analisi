## Exercise 1

Solve $|x-1|=3$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x-1=3 &\iff x=4,\\[4pt]
x-1=-3 &\iff x=-2,\\[4pt]
S&=\{-2,4\}.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{f="abs(x-1);3" xmin=-4 xmax=6 ymin=-1 ymax=6 punti="-2,3,pieno;4,3,pieno" titolo="The two points at height $3$ have x-coordinates $-2$ and $4$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Moving three units to the right of $1$ gives $4$. Moving three units to the left gives $-2$. These are the only points at distance $3$ from the center, because a real displacement with absolute value $3$ is either $3$ or $-3$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve $|3x+2|=4$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x+2=4 &\iff x=\frac23,\\[4pt]
3x+2=-4 &\iff x=-2,\\[4pt]
S&=\left\{-2,\frac23\right\}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The expression $3x+2$ must be $4$ units from zero. Subtract $2$ and divide each case by $3$ to get the two x-coordinates. On the number line, rewriting $|3x+2|=3|x+\dfrac23|$ shows that both solutions are $\dfrac43$ units from $-\dfrac23$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve $|x+1|=2x-1$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x-1\geq0&\iff x\geq\frac12,\\[4pt]
x\geq\frac12&\implies x+1>0,\\[4pt]
x+1=2x-1&\iff x=2,\\[4pt]
S&=\{2\}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The right-hand side must be nonnegative, so work only where $x\geq\dfrac12$. On this ray, $x+1$ is positive and its absolute value equals $x+1$. The solution $2$ satisfies the constraint and gives $|2+1|=2\cdot2-1=3$.

</div>
