## Exercise 1

Evaluate $\log_2 24-\log_2 3$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\log_2 24-\log_2 3
 &=\log_2\frac{24}{3}\\[4pt]
 &=\log_2 8\\[4pt]
 &=3.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Both arguments are positive and the logarithms have the same base, so the difference is the logarithm of the quotient. The quotient is $24$ divided by $3$ because the term being subtracted becomes the denominator. This gives $8=2^3$, so the requested value is $3$. Reversing the quotient would give the reciprocal, which corresponds to subtracting in the opposite order.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Write $2\log_3x-\dfrac12\log_3 y+\log_3 9$ as a single logarithm for $x,y>0$. Evaluate it at $x=2$ and $y=16$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2\log_3x-\frac12\log_3 y+\log_3 9
 &=\log_3(x^2)-\log_3(\sqrt y)+\log_3 9\\[4pt]
 &=\log_3\frac{9x^2}{\sqrt y},\\[6pt]
\log_3\frac{9\cdot2^2}{\sqrt{16}}
 &=\log_3 9=2.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

First interpret each coefficient as an exponent on its argument. The $y$ term is subtracted, so $\sqrt y$ goes in the denominator. The term $\log_3 9$ is added, so its argument multiplies $x^2$. At the given values, $x^2=4$ and $\sqrt y=4$. Their quotient is $1$, leaving the logarithm of $9$.

</div>
