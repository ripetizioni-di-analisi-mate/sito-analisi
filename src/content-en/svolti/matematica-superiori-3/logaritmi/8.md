## Exercise 1

A culture starts with $120$ cells and the model predicts that it doubles every $3$ hours. Find the exact time at which the model reaches $500$ cells and the first whole hour with at least $500$ cells.

$$
\begin{aligned}
120\cdot2^{\frac t3}&=500,\\[4pt]
2^{\frac t3}&=\frac{25}{6},\\[4pt]
t&=3\log_2\frac{25}{6}\approx6{,}18.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
6<t<7 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad n=7.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Time $t$ is measured in hours, so after $t$ hours there have been $\dfrac t3$ doubling periods. Dividing the threshold by the initial population gives the required growth factor. The logarithm gives the number of periods, which is multiplied by $3$ to convert back to hours. The model is increasing and the threshold falls after the sixth hour and before the seventh, so the first whole-hour observation that qualifies is at $7$ hours.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A signal has intensity $I(t)=64\left(\dfrac12\right)^{\frac t2}$ for $t\ge0$, where $t$ is time in hours. Find when the intensity is strictly less than $8$ and the first whole hour that meets the requirement.

$$
\begin{aligned}
64\left(\frac12\right)^{\frac t2}<8
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\left(\frac12\right)^{\frac t2}<\frac18,\\[4pt]
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\left(\frac12\right)^{\frac t2}<\left(\frac12\right)^3,\\[4pt]
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\frac t2>3.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
t>6 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad n=7.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The ratio of the threshold to the initial intensity is $\dfrac18$, the factor after three halvings. For base $\dfrac12$, a smaller power value corresponds to a larger exponent. The resulting condition is therefore $t>6$. At the sixth hour the intensity is exactly $8$, so the first whole hour with intensity below $8$ is the seventh.

</div>
