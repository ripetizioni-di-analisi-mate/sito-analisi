## Exercise 1

A culture starts with $120$ cells and the model predicts that it doubles every $3$ hours. Find the exact time at which the model reaches $500$ cells and the first whole hour with at least $500$ cells.

$$
\begin{aligned}
120\cdot2^{\frac t3}&=500,\\[4pt]
2^{\frac t3}&=\frac{25}{6},\\[4pt]
t&=3\log_2\frac{25}{6}\approx6{,}18.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
6<t<7 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad n=7.
$$

$$
N(6)=480<500,\qquad N(7)=480\sqrt[3]2>500.
$$

::grafico{f="120*2^(x/3);500" xmin=0 xmax=7.5 ymin=0 ymax=710 punti="6,480,pieno,A;7,604.7621,pieno,B" segmenti="6,300,6,480;7,300,7,604.7621" legenda="120\cdot 2^{\frac{x}{3}};500" nomi="t,N" titolo="The curve crosses the threshold of $500$ between hourly samples $A$ and $B$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The model is $N(t)=120\cdot2^{\frac t3}$, with $t$ measured in hours. The exponent counts the elapsed three-hour periods. Dividing by $120$ shows that reaching $500$ requires a factor of $\dfrac{25}{6}$. The base-$2$ logarithm gives the number of periods and multiplying by $3$ converts this number to hours.

For the second question use the increasing model. After two periods, at $t=6$, the value is $480$, below the threshold. An extra hour multiplies it by $\sqrt[3]2$. Since $\left(\dfrac{25}{24}\right)^3<2$, this factor exceeds $\dfrac{25}{24}$ and hence $N(7)>500$.

The graph distinguishes the two results. The curve crosses the threshold at $t=3\log_2\dfrac{25}{6}$, just after the sixth hour. The first reading at a whole hour that reaches the threshold occurs at $t=7$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A signal has intensity $I(t)=64\left(\dfrac12\right)^{\frac t2}$ for $t\ge0$, where $t$ is time in hours. Find when the intensity is strictly less than $8$ and the first whole hour that meets the requirement.

$$
\begin{aligned}
64\left(\frac12\right)^{\frac t2}<8
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\left(\frac12\right)^{\frac t2}<\frac18,\\[4pt]
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\left(\frac12\right)^{\frac t2}<\left(\frac12\right)^3,\\[4pt]
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\frac t2>3.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
t>6 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad n=7.
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
I(6)&=64\left(\frac12\right)^3=8,\\[4pt]
I(7)&=8\left(\frac12\right)^{\frac12}=\frac{8}{\sqrt2}<8.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{f="64*0.5^(x/2);8" xmin=0 xmax=8.8 ymin=-1 ymax=68 punti="6,8,pieno,P;7,5.65685425" segmenti="6,0,6,8" legenda="64\left(\frac12\right)^{\frac{x}{2}};8" nomi="t,I" titolo="Point $P$ gives equality. The portion to its right meets the strict threshold"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Compare the intensity with its initial value. The threshold is $\dfrac8{64}=\dfrac18$ of the starting value and corresponds to applying the factor $\dfrac12$ three times. The exponential with base less than $1$ decreases, so falling below the threshold requires going beyond three half-life periods. This gives $\dfrac t2>3$, or $t>6$.

Equality at $t=6$ marks the instant when the intensity reaches $8$, but the question asks for a strict inequality. The first allowed whole hour is therefore the seventh. Check first at the sixth hour and then at the seventh.

The factor over one hour is $\left(\dfrac12\right)^{\frac12}$, because one hour is half the halving period. Going from hour $6$ to hour $7$ therefore drops below the threshold without waiting for another full period.

</div>
