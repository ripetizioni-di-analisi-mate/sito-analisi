## Exercise 1

A quantity of $80$ units doubles every $3$ hours. Find the value predicted after $6$ hours.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N(t)&=80\cdot 2^{\frac t3},\\[4pt]
N(6)&=80\cdot2^{\frac63}\\[4pt]
 &=80\cdot 4=320.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The factor $2$ applies once every $3$ hours, so the exponent in the model is the time in hours divided by $3$. Two doubling periods elapse in $6$ hours. The value goes from $80$ to $160$ after the first period and from $160$ to $320$ after the second.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A quantity decays according to $N(t)=N_0q^t$, where $q>0$ is the daily factor. After $2$ days, $64\%$ of the initial quantity remains. Find the daily percentage decrease.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{N(2)}{N_0}&=q^2=0{,}64,\\[4pt]
q&=\sqrt{0{,}64}=0{,}8,\\[4pt]
1-q&=0{,}2=20\%.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The ratio after two days is the product of two equal daily factors. Taking the positive square root gives the factor $0{,}8$. Each day, $80\%$ of the previous day's value remains. The amount lost each day is $20\%$ of that value.

</div>
