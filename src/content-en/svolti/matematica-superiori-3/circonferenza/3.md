## Exercise 1

Determine which of $P(4,4)$, $Q(1,4)$ and $R(6,2)$ lie on the circle with centre $C(1,0)$ and radius $5$. Classify the other two points as well.

$$
\begin{aligned}
CP^2&=(4-1)^2+4^2=25,\\
CQ^2&=(1-1)^2+4^2=16<25,\\
CR^2&=(6-1)^2+2^2=29>25.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{curve="1+5*cos(t),5*sin(t),0,6.283185307" xmin=-5 xmax=8 ymin=-6 ymax=6 uguale=1 punti="1,0,pieno,C;4,4,pieno,P;1,4,pieno,Q;6,2,pieno,R" legenda="(x-1)^2+y^2=25" titolo="The points $P$, $Q$ and $R$ lie on the circle, inside it and outside it, respectively."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The distance formula subtracts $1$ from the $x$-coordinate because the centre is away from the origin. All three squared distances are compared with $5^2=25$. Thus $P$ lies on the circle, $Q$ is inside, and $R$ is outside. We can also locate $R$ by starting from $(6,0)$, the right endpoint of the horizontal diameter. Keeping the same $x$-coordinate and changing the $y$-coordinate adds a positive square, so the distance exceeds the radius.

</div>

## Exercise 2

For which $t\in\mathbb R$ does the point $S(t,3)$ lie in the closed disk bounded by $(x+2)^2+(y-1)^2=20$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
(t+2)^2+(3-1)^2&\leq 20,\\
(t+2)^2 &\leq 16,\\
-4\leq t+2&\leq 4,\\
-6\leq t&\leq 2.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The point $S$ moves along the horizontal line $y=3$, always at vertical distance $2$ from the centre. That leaves at most $16$ for the square of the horizontal difference. Since $(t+2)^2\leq16$ is equivalent to $|t+2|\leq4$, the $x$-coordinate can be at most $4$ units from $-2$. The endpoints $-6$ and $2$ are included because points on the circle satisfy the condition. All points strictly between them are inside.

</div>
