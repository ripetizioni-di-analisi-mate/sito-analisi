## Exercise 1

The measurements $2$, $4$ and $7$ occur $3$, $2$ and $5$ times, respectively. Find the mean from the frequency table.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N&=3+2+5=10,\\
S&=3\cdot2+2\cdot4+5\cdot7=49,\\[4pt]
\bar x&=\frac{49}{10}=4{,}9.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The table lists three distinct values but summarises ten measurements. The value $7$ contributes five times, so its row adds $35$ to the sum. Dividing by ten distributes the sum across all observed measurements.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In a histogram of durations, classes $[0,3)$ and $[3,7)$ have heights $4$ and $2$, measured in observations per minute of interval width. How many observations does each class represent?

$$
\begin{aligned}
n_1&=(3-0)\cdot4=12,\\
n_2&=(7-3)\cdot2=8,\\
N&=12+8=20.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Height is a frequency density and width is measured in minutes. Their product gives the count. The second interval spans more of the horizontal axis, but it contains fewer observations because its area is smaller.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A table lists $8$ times in $[0,4)$ and $12$ times in $[4,10)$, in minutes. Estimate the mean duration using the class midpoints.

$$
\begin{aligned}
c_1&=\frac{0+4}{2}=2,\qquad c_2=\frac{4+10}{2}=7,\\[4pt]
\widehat{\bar x}&=\frac{8\cdot2+12\cdot7}{8+12}\\[4pt]
&=\frac{100}{20}=5\,\mathrm{min}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Represent every duration in the first class by $2$ minutes, and each duration in the second by $7$ minutes. These representative values have weights $8$ and $12$. The estimated mean is $5$ minutes. It equals the actual mean if each class mean equals its midpoint.

</div>
