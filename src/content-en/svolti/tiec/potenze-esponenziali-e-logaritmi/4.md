## Exercise 1

The number $0.00042$ written in scientific notation is:

- A) $4.2 \cdot 10^{-4}$
- B) $4.2 \cdot 10^{-3}$
- C) $4.2 \cdot 10^{4}$
- D) $0.42 \cdot 10^{-4}$

$$
0.00042 = 4.2 \cdot 0.0001 = 4.2 \cdot 10^{-4}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In **scientific notation**, there is just one digit before the decimal point, and it must be nonzero. Thus the coefficient is $4.2$. To go from $0.00042$ to $4.2$, move the decimal point $4$ places to the right. The exponent is therefore $-4$. Choice B counts $3$ places by counting only the zeros after the decimal point. Choice C has a positive exponent, which would give a large number. Choice D equals $0.000042$ and has a coefficient less than $1$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A company has annual revenue of $3.6 \cdot 10^{8}$ euros and $4.5 \cdot 10^{3}$ employees. Its average revenue per employee is:

- A) $8 \cdot 10^{5}$ euros
- B) $1.25 \cdot 10^{-5}$ euros
- C) $8 \cdot 10^{6}$ euros
- D) $8 \cdot 10^{4}$ euros

$$
\frac{3.6 \cdot 10^{8}}{4.5 \cdot 10^{3}} = \frac{3.6}{4.5} \cdot 10^{8-3} = 0.8 \cdot 10^{5} = 8 \cdot 10^{4}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Revenue per employee is total revenue divided by the number of employees. Divide the coefficients, $\dfrac{3.6}{4.5} = 0.8$, and divide the powers of $10$, $10^{8-3} = 10^{5}$. The coefficient $0.8$ is less than $1$, so move the decimal point one place to the right and **decrease the exponent by $1$**. The result is $8 \cdot 10^{4}$ euros, or $80\,000$ euros. Choice A writes $8$ in place of $0.8$ without adjusting the exponent. Choice C adjusts the exponent in the wrong direction. Choice B reverses the quotient. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Which of the following inequalities is true?

- A) $3^{24} > 2^{40}$
- B) $2^{40} > 3^{24}$
- C) $4^{20} > 2^{40}$
- D) $8^{12} < 2^{30}$

$$
2^{40} = \left( 2^{5} \right)^{8} = 32^{8},
\qquad
3^{24} = \left( 3^{3} \right)^{8} = 27^{8}
$$
$$
32 > 27
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
2^{40} > 3^{24}
$$
$$
4^{20} = 2^{40},
\qquad
8^{12} = 2^{36} > 2^{30}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To **compare the powers**, rewrite $2^{40}$ and $3^{24}$ with the same exponent. Since $40 = 5 \cdot 8$ and $24 = 3 \cdot 8$, this gives $32^{8}$ and $27^{8}$. With the same exponent, the greater base gives the greater power, so $2^{40} > 3^{24}$. Choice A picks the power with the greater base without checking the exponents. Choice C is false because $4^{20} = \left(2^2\right)^{20} = 2^{40}$, so the two numbers are equal. Choice D is false because $8^{12} = 2^{36}$ is greater than $2^{30}$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
