## Exercise 1

The value of $\log_{3} 81$ is:

- A) $27$
- B) $3$
- C) $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- D) $4$

$$
3^{4} = 81
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\log_{3} 81 = \log_{3} 3^{4} = 4
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the **definition of a logarithm**, $\log_3 81$ is the exponent to which $3$ must be raised to obtain $81$. Since $3 \cdot 3 \cdot 3 \cdot 3 = 81$, the exponent is $4$. Choice A divides $81$ by $3$ as if a logarithm were a quotient. Choice C switches the base and the argument, since $\log_{81} 3 = \dfrac{1}{4}$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The value of the expression $\log_{2} \dfrac{1}{8} + \log_{5} 1 + \log_{4} 2$ is:

- A) $-\dfrac{5}{2}$
- B) $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
- C) $-1$
- D) $\dfrac{7}{2}$

$$
\log_{2} \frac{1}{8} = \log_{2} 2^{-3} = -3,
\qquad
\log_{5} 1 = 0,
\qquad
\log_{4} 2 = \frac{1}{2}
$$
$$
-3 + 0 + \frac{1}{2} = -\frac{5}{2}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Evaluate each logarithm using its **special values**. Since $\dfrac{1}{8} = 2^{-3}$, the first logarithm equals $-3$. The logarithm of $1$ equals $0$ in any base, since $5^0 = 1$. Finally, $4^{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{4} = 2$, so $\log_4 2 = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The sum is $-\dfrac{5}{2}$. Choice B assigns $\log_5 1$ the value $1$. Choice C assigns $\log_4 2$ the value $2$. Choice D drops the minus sign in $\log_2 \dfrac{1}{8}$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

An investment doubles in value every $5$ years. The time it takes for $3000$ euros to grow to $48\,000$ euros is:

- A) $16$ years
- B) $4$ years
- C) $20$ years
- D) $80$ years

$$
\frac{48\,000}{3000} = 16,
\qquad
\log_{2} 16 = 4
$$
$$
4 \cdot 5 = 20 \text{ years}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The value of the investment must increase by a factor of $\dfrac{48\,000}{3000} = 16$. Each $5$-year period multiplies its value by $2$, so the number of periods $n$ satisfies $2^n = 16$, or $n = \log_2 16 = 4$. Four periods of $5$ years amount to $20$ years. Choice B gives the number of doublings without multiplying by the length of a period. Choice D multiplies the factor $16$ by $5$ as if the investment grew by a fixed amount. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
