## Exercise 1

The equation $9^{x - 1} = 27^{x}$ has the following solution:

- A) $x = 2$
- B) $x = -1$
- C) no real solution
- D) $x = -2$

$$
\begin{aligned}
9^{x-1} = 27^{x}
&\iff 3^{2(x-1)} = 3^{3x} \\[6pt]
&\iff 2x - 2 = 3x \\[6pt]
&\iff x = -2
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The bases $9 = 3^2$ and $27 = 3^3$ are powers of $3$, so **rewrite both sides with the same base**. By the power-of-a-power rule, the left side becomes $3^{2(x-1)}$, and the exponent $2$ multiplies the entire expression $x - 1$. Equating exponents gives $2x - 2 = 3x$, or $x = -2$. Choice B multiplies only $x$ by $2$, giving $2x - 1 = 3x$. Choice A makes a sign error in the last step. As a check, $9^{-3} = 3^{-6}$ and $27^{-2} = 3^{-6}$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An investment of $2000$ euros earning $10\%$ annual compound interest grows to $2662$ euros after $n$ years. The value of $n$ is:

- A) $3$
- B) $1.331$
- C) $3.31$
- D) $4$

$$
2000 \cdot 1.1^{n} = 2662
\iff
1.1^{n} = \frac{2662}{2000} = 1.331
$$
$$
1.1^{2} = 1.21,
\qquad
1.1^{3} = 1.331
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
n = 3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

With **compound interest**, the value of the investment is multiplied by $1.1$ each year, so after $n$ years its value is $2000 \cdot 1.1^{n}$. Dividing by $2000$ gives the exponential equation $1.1^{n} = 1.331$, whose right side is the power $1.1^{3}$. Therefore $n = 3$. Choice B gives the factor $1.331$, the value of the power instead of its exponent. Choice C uses simple interest, giving $2000 \cdot 0.1 \cdot n = 662$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The solutions of the equation $4^{x} - 2^{x+1} - 8 = 0$ are:

- A) $x = 2$ or $x = -1$
- B) $x = 4$
- C) $x = 2$
- D) no real solution

$$
4^{x} = \left( 2^{x} \right)^{2},
\qquad
2^{x+1} = 2 \cdot 2^{x},
\qquad
t = 2^{x} > 0
$$
$$
t^{2} - 2t - 8 = 0
\iff
(t - 4)(t + 2) = 0
\iff
t = 4
\;\vee\;
t = -2
$$
$$
2^{x} = 4 \iff x = 2,
\qquad
2^{x} = -2 \ \text{no solution}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The equation contains $4^{x} = \left(2^x\right)^2$ and $2^{x+1} = 2 \cdot 2^{x}$. Using the **change of variable** $t = 2^{x}$ gives the quadratic equation $t^{2} - 2t - 8 = 0$, with solutions $t = 4$ and $t = -2$. Since $2^{x}$ is always positive, discard $t = -2$, while $2^{x} = 4$ gives $x = 2$. Choice A turns $t = -2$ into $x = -1$, but $2^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, which differs from $-2$. Choice B stops at the variable $t$ without returning to $x$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
