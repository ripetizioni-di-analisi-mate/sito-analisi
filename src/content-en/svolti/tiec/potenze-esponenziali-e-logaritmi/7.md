## Exercise 1

The inequality $2^{3x - 1} > 32$ holds for:

- A) $x < 2$
- B) $x > \dfrac{4}{3}$
- C) $x > 2$
- D) $x > 11$

$$
2^{3x-1} > 2^{5}
\iff
3x - 1 > 5
\iff
3x > 6
\iff
x > 2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Write $32 = 2^{5}$ so that both sides are powers of the same base $2$. Since the base is greater than $1$, the exponential function is increasing, and **the inequality sign stays the same** when comparing the exponents. From $3x - 1 > 5$ it follows that $x > 2$. Choice A reverses the sign unnecessarily. Choice B moves $-1$ to the right without changing its sign, giving $3x > 4$. Choice D compares the exponent with $32$ instead of $5$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The inequality $\left( \dfrac{1}{3} \right)^{x - 2} > 9$ holds for:

- A) $x > 0$
- B) $x < 4$
- C) $x > 4$
- D) $x < 0$

$$
9 = 3^{2} = \left( \frac{1}{3} \right)^{-2}
$$
$$
\left( \frac{1}{3} \right)^{x-2} > \left( \frac{1}{3} \right)^{-2}
\iff
x - 2 < -2
\iff
x < 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To obtain the same base, write $9$ as a power of $\dfrac{1}{3}$. Since $\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^{-2} = 3^{2} = 9$, the exponent is $-2$. The base $\dfrac{1}{3}$ lies between $0$ and $1$, so the exponential function is decreasing and **the inequality sign reverses** when comparing the exponents. From $x - 2 < -2$ it follows that $x < 0$. As a check, $x = -1$ gives $\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^{-3} = 27 > 9$. Choice A fails to reverse the inequality sign. Choice B writes $9 = \left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^{2}$, which actually equals $\dfrac{1}{9}$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A machine loses half its value each year. Purchased for $64\,000$ euros, it is worth $64\,000 \cdot \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{t}$ euros after $t$ years. Its value is less than $4000$ euros for:

- A) $t < 4$
- B) $t > 4$
- C) $t > 16$
- D) $t > 8$

$$
64\,000 \cdot \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{t} < 4000
\iff
\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{t} < \frac{4000}{64\,000} = \frac{1}{16}
$$
$$
\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{t} < \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{4}
\iff
t > 4
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Dividing both sides by $64\,000$ gives $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{t} < \dfrac{1}{16}$, and $\dfrac{1}{16} = \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{4}$. The base $\dfrac{1}{2}$ is less than $1$, so **the inequality sign reverses** and the inequality is equivalent to $t > 4$. This fits the situation since a value that halves each year decreases over time. After $4$ years the machine is worth exactly $4000$ euros, then less. Choice A fails to reverse the sign. Choice C compares $t$ with $16$ instead of the exponent $4$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
