## Exercise 1

In this model, $R = 3 \log_{2} (x + 1)$ gives revenue in thousands of euros when $x$ is the number of hundreds of units sold. For what value of $x$ does revenue reach $12$ thousand euros?

- A) $15$
- B) $16$
- C) $4095$
- D) $3$

$$
3 \log_{2} (x + 1) = 12
\iff
\log_{2} (x + 1) = 4
$$
$$
x + 1 = 2^{4} = 16
\iff
x = 15
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Divide by $3$ to obtain $\log_2 (x + 1) = 4$. By the **definition of a logarithm**, the argument equals the power $2^{4}$, so $x + 1 = 16$ and $x = 15$. The domain restriction $x + 1 > 0$ is satisfied, and the value makes sense in the model because it corresponds to $1500$ units. Choice B gives the value of $x + 1$. Choice C omits division by $3$, since $2^{12} - 1 = 4095$. Choice D treats the logarithm as a factor and solves $3(x + 1) = 12$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The solutions of the equation $\log_{3} (x + 1) + \log_{3} (x - 1) = 1$ are:

- A) $x = 2$ or $x = -2$
- B) $x = 2$
- C) $x = -2$
- D) $x = \sqrt{2}$

$$
\begin{cases}
x + 1 > 0 \\
x - 1 > 0
\end{cases}
\iff
x > 1
$$
$$
\log_{3} \left[ (x + 1)(x - 1) \right] = 1
\iff
x^{2} - 1 = 3
\iff
x^{2} = 4
\iff
x = 2
\;\vee\;
x = -2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **domain restrictions** require both arguments to be positive, so $x > 1$. By the product rule for logarithms, the equation becomes $\log_3 \left(x^2 - 1\right) = 1$, or $x^2 - 1 = 3^1 = 3$, with solutions $x = 2$ and $x = -2$. Only $x = 2$ satisfies $x > 1$, so it is the only solution. Choice A keeps $x = -2$, for which $\log_3 (-1)$ is undefined. Choice D sets $x^2 - 1 = 1$, overlooking that the right side becomes $3^{1}$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The inequality $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} (x - 3) > -1$ holds for:

- A) $x > 5$
- B) $x < 5$
- C) $3 < x < \dfrac{7}{2}$
- D) $3 < x < 5$

$$
-1 = \log_{\frac{1}{2}} \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{-1} = \log_{\frac{1}{2}} 2
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
x - 3 > 0 \\
x - 3 < 2
\end{cases}
\iff
\begin{cases}
x > 3 \\
x < 5
\end{cases}
\iff
3 < x < 5
$$

::grafico{f="log(x-3)/log(0.5);-1" xmin=-0.5 xmax=10 ymin=-3.5 ymax=3.5 punti="5,-1,vuoto;4,0,pieno" segmenti="5,0,5,-1" asintoti="x=3" legenda="y = \log_{\frac{1}{2}} (x - 3);y = -1" titolo="The graph of $y = \log_{\frac{1}{2}} (x - 3)$ is defined for $x > 3$ and lies above the line $y = -1$ for $3 < x < 5$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Write $-1$ as a logarithm with base $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Since $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{-1} = 2$, it follows that $-1 = \log_{\frac{1}{2}} 2$. The base is less than $1$, so **the inequality sign reverses** and the inequality gives $x - 3 < 2$. The solutions must also satisfy the domain restriction $x - 3 > 0$. Solving the system therefore gives $3 < x < 5$. Choice A fails to reverse the inequality sign. Choice B overlooks the domain restriction. Choice C evaluates $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{-1}$ as $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
