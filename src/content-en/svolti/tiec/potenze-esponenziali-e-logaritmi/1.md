## Exercise 1

The expression $\dfrac{2^{5} \cdot 2^{-2}}{2^{4}}$ is equal to:

- A) $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- B) $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- C) $2$
- D) $8$

$$
\frac{2^{5} \cdot 2^{-2}}{2^{4}} = 2^{5 + (-2) - 4} = 2^{-1} = \frac{1}{2}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

All three powers have the same base, $2$. By the **properties of powers**, add the exponents in the numerator and subtract the exponent in the denominator. The resulting exponent is $5 - 2 - 4 = -1$. Since $2^{-1}$ is the reciprocal of $2$, its value is $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Choice A treats the negative exponent as a minus sign in front of the number. Choice D comes from adding $+2$ instead of $-2$, since $2^{5+2-4} = 2^3 = 8$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The number $3^{10} + 3^{10} + 3^{10}$ is equal to:

- A) $3^{30}$
- B) $9^{10}$
- C) $3^{11}$
- D) $9^{30}$

$$
3^{10} + 3^{10} + 3^{10} = 3 \cdot 3^{10} = 3^{1 + 10} = 3^{11}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

All three terms are equal, so their sum is $3$ times $3^{10}$. The product $3 \cdot 3^{10}$ has two factors with the same base. Add the exponents to obtain $3^{11}$. Choice A applies the product rule to a sum, although it applies only to products. Choice B adds the bases and gives $9^{10} = 3^{20}$, a much larger number. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The number of customers at an online store doubles every year. The store has $4^{5}$ customers at the beginning of this year. In $6$ years, if growth continues at the same rate, the number of customers will be:

- A) $2^{16}$
- B) $4^{11}$
- C) $2^{60}$
- D) $8^{5}$

$$
4^{5} = \left( 2^{2} \right)^{5} = 2^{10}
$$
$$
2^{10} \cdot 2^{6} = 2^{10 + 6} = 2^{16}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Doubling every year for $6$ years means multiplying by $2$ six times, or multiplying by $2^{6}$. Since this factor has base $2$, write the initial number as a power of $2$ as well. The **power-of-a-power rule** gives $4^{5} = 2^{10}$. Multiplying powers with the same base then gives $2^{16}$. Choice B adds $6$ to the exponent of $4$ as if the number were multiplied by $4$ each year. Choice C multiplies the exponents $10$ and $6$ instead of adding them. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
