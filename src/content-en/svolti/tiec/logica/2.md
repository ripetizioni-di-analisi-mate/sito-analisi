## Exercise 1

In a class of $30$ students, $18$ study Spanish, $15$ study German, and $7$ study both languages. How many students study neither language?

- A) $3$
- B) $4$
- C) $7$
- D) $11$

$$
|S \cup T| = 18 + 15 - 7 = 26,
\qquad
30 - 26 = 4
$$

::venn{zone="u" nomi="S,T" elementi="a:11;ab:7;b:8;u:4" titolo="Of the students studying Spanish ($S$) or German ($T$), $11$ study only Spanish, $7$ both, and $8$ only German. Another $4$ study neither language."}

<div class="spiegazione">

By the **formula for a union**, $18 + 15 - 7 = 26$ students study at least one language because the $7$ who study both are counted among the $18$ and among the $15$. Therefore $30 - 26 = 4$ students study neither language. Choice D, $11$, counts those who study only Spanish. Choice A, $18 + 15 - 30 = 3$, compares the sum of the two groups with the total without accounting for the $7$ students they have in common. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Of $50$ supermarket customers, $32$ bought bread, $27$ bought milk, and $6$ bought neither. How many bought both bread and milk?

- A) $9$
- B) $12$
- C) $15$
- D) $17$

$$
|P \cup L| = 50 - 6 = 44,
\qquad
|P \cap L| = 32 + 27 - 44 = 15
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The number of customers who bought at least one product is $50 - 6 = 44$. The **formula for a union** $|P \cup L| = |P| + |L| - |P \cap L|$ gives $|P \cap L| = 32 + 27 - 44 = 15$. As a check, $32 - 15 = 17$ customers bought bread only, $27 - 15 = 12$ bought milk only, and $17 + 15 + 12 + 6 = 50$. Choice A, $32 + 27 - 50 = 9$, overlooks the $6$ customers who bought neither item. Choices B and D count customers who bought just one product. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A company has $60$ employees and offers courses in Excel, English, and accounting. Of the employees, $26$ take Excel, $20$ English, and $17$ accounting. The counts are $8$ for Excel and English, $7$ for Excel and accounting, $5$ for English and accounting, and $3$ for all three courses. How many employees take exactly one course?

- A) $32$
- B) $23$
- C) $46$
- D) $14$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Excel only} &= 26 - (8 - 3) - (7 - 3) - 3 = 14 \\[6pt]
\text{English only} &= 20 - (8 - 3) - (5 - 3) - 3 = 10 \\[6pt]
\text{accounting only} &= 17 - (7 - 3) - (5 - 3) - 3 = 8
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
14 + 10 + 8 = 32
$$

::venn{tipo="tre" nomi="E,I,C" elementi="a:14;b:10;c:8;ab:5;ac:4;bc:2;abc:3;u:14" titolo="Employees taking Excel ($E$), English ($I$), and accounting ($C$), with the regions filled in from the center outward."}

<div class="spiegazione">

Fill in the diagram **from the center outward**. Place the $3$ employees taking all three courses in the center. Since $8$ employees take Excel and English, $8 - 3 = 5$ take just these two. Likewise, $7 - 3 = 4$ take just Excel and accounting and $5 - 3 = 2$ just English and accounting. Subtracting the regions already filled from each total leaves $14$ taking only Excel, $10$ only English, and $8$ only accounting. Thus $32$ employees take exactly one course. Choice B, $11 + 7 + 5 = 23$, subtracts the pairwise intersections without first removing the $3$ employees common to all three. Choice C counts those taking at least one course and choice D those taking none. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
