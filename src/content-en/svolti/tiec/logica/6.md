## Exercise 1

Four identical printers print $600$ pages in $5$ minutes. How many pages do six printers of the same type print in $8$ minutes?

- A) $960$
- B) $1440$
- C) $900$
- D) $3600$

$$
\frac{600}{4 \cdot 5} = 30 \ \text{pages per printer per minute}
$$
$$
6 \cdot 8 \cdot 30 = 1440
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The number of pages is **directly proportional** to both the number of printers and the time. One printer prints $\dfrac{600}{20} = 30$ pages per minute, so six printers print $6 \cdot 8 \cdot 30 = 1440$ pages in eight minutes. The same result follows by multiplying $600$ by $\dfrac{6}{4}$ and by $\dfrac{8}{5}$. Choice A accounts only for time because $600 \cdot \dfrac{8}{5} = 960$, and choice C accounts only for the number of printers because $600 \cdot \dfrac{6}{4} = 900$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The ages of a father and his son are in the ratio $7 : 2$. In $10$ years, the ratio will be $9 : 4$. How old is the father now?

- A) $45$
- B) $10$
- C) $28$
- D) $35$

$$
\text{father} = 7k, \qquad \text{son} = 2k
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
(7k + 10) : (2k + 10) &= 9 : 4 \\[6pt]
4\,(7k + 10) &= 9\,(2k + 10) \\[6pt]
28k + 40 &= 18k + 90 \\[6pt]
k &= 5
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\text{father} = 35, \qquad \text{son} = 10
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The ratio $7 : 2$ means that the ages are $7k$ and $2k$ for some number $k$ to be determined. In $10$ years, their ages will be $7k + 10$ and $2k + 10$. By the fundamental property of proportions, the product of the extremes equals the product of the means. Solving the **equation** gives $k = 5$, so the father is $35$ years old and the son is $10$. As a check, in $10$ years they will be $45$ and $20$, and $\dfrac{45}{20} = \dfrac{9}{4}$. Choice A is the father's age in $10$ years and choice B is the son's age. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A store sells type A coffee at $12$ euros per kg and type B coffee at $18$ euros per kg. In what ratio should the amounts of A and B be mixed to produce a blend costing $14$ euros per kg?

- A) $1 : 2$
- B) $2 : 3$
- C) $2 : 1$
- D) $3 : 1$

$$
\frac{12\,a + 18\,b}{a + b} = 14
$$
$$
12\,a + 18\,b = 14\,a + 14\,b
\iff
4\,b = 2\,a
\iff
a = 2\,b
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

With $a$ kg of coffee A and $b$ kg of coffee B, the price per kilogram of the blend is the **weighted average** of the two prices. Setting it equal to $14$ gives $a = 2b$, so the ratio of A to B is $2 : 1$. This agrees with the fact that $14$ euros is closer to $12$ than to $18$, so the blend contains more of the cheaper coffee. Choice A reverses the ratio and would give a blend costing $16$ euros per kg. Choice B is the ratio of the two prices, which does not give the ratio of the amounts. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
