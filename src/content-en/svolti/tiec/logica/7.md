## Exercise 1

Which number completes the sequence $2,\ 5,\ 11,\ 23,\ 47,\ \ldots$?

- A) $94$
- B) $95$
- C) $71$
- D) $96$

$$
2 \cdot 2 + 1 = 5, \quad 5 \cdot 2 + 1 = 11, \quad 11 \cdot 2 + 1 = 23, \quad 23 \cdot 2 + 1 = 47
$$
$$
47 \cdot 2 + 1 = 95
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The differences between consecutive terms are $3, 6, 12, 24$, doubling at each step. Each term is therefore twice the preceding term plus $1$, following the rule of **two operations in succession**. The next term is $47 \cdot 2 + 1 = 95$. Using the differences also gives $47 + 48 = 95$. Choice A doubles without adding $1$, and choice C repeats the difference $24$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Which number completes the sequence $3,\ 8,\ 6,\ 11,\ 9,\ 14,\ \ldots$?

- A) $12$
- B) $17$
- C) $19$
- D) $11$

$$
3 \xrightarrow{+5} 8 \xrightarrow{-2} 6 \xrightarrow{+5} 11 \xrightarrow{-2} 9 \xrightarrow{+5} 14 \xrightarrow{-2} 12
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The sequence goes up and down, suggesting an **alternating sequence**. Alternately add $5$ and subtract $2$. The last step, from $9$ to $14$, is an addition. The next step is therefore a subtraction, giving the missing term $14 - 2 = 12$. Choice C adds $5$ again, and choice B adds $3$, the net effect of two steps. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Which number completes the sequence $2,\ 30,\ 4,\ 27,\ 8,\ 24,\ 16,\ \ldots$?

- A) $32$
- B) $18$
- C) $20$
- D) $21$

$$
\text{odd-numbered positions: } 2,\ 4,\ 8,\ 16 \quad (\times 2)
$$
$$
\text{even-numbered positions: } 30,\ 27,\ 24,\ 21 \quad (-3)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The terms alternate between small and large numbers, suggesting an **interleaved** sequence. The odd-numbered terms $2, 4, 8, 16$ double, whereas the even-numbered terms $30, 27, 24$ decrease by $3$. The missing term is eighth, an even position, so it is given by $24 - 3 = 21$. Choice A is the next odd-numbered term and belongs in ninth place. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
