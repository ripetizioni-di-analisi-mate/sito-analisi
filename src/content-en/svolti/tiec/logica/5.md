## Exercise 1

Anna is taller than Bruno, Carla is shorter than Bruno, and Dario is taller than Anna. Which of the following statements is true?

- A) Bruno is taller than Dario.
- B) Carla is taller than Anna.
- C) Anna is shorter than Bruno.
- D) Dario is taller than Carla.

$$
A > B, \qquad B > C, \qquad D > A
$$
$$
D > A > B > C
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Write each statement with $>$ pointing in the same direction to mean “taller than.” The statement “Carla is shorter than Bruno” becomes $B > C$. By the **transitive property**, the three relations form the chain $D > A > B > C$, so Dario is tallest and Carla shortest. In particular, Dario is taller than Carla. Choices A, B, and C each reverse a comparison in the chain. For example, C directly contradicts the given statement “Anna is taller than Bruno.” The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Marco earns more than Luca and less than Sara. Paola earns more than Luca. Which of the following statements is necessarily true?

- A) Paola earns more than Marco.
- B) Sara earns more than Luca.
- C) Paola earns less than Sara.
- D) Luca earns more than Paola.

$$
S > M > L, \qquad P > L
$$

| Possible ordering | A | B | C | D |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $S > M > P > L$ | F | T | T | F |
| $P > S > M > L$ | T | T | F | F |

<div class="spiegazione">

The information gives the chain $S > M > L$ and the comparison $P > L$, but Paola's position relative to Marco and Sara **cannot be determined**. By the transitive property, $S > M$ and $M > L$ imply $S > L$, so B is true in every possible ordering. Choices A and C depend on Paola's position. For each choice, the table gives a possible ordering in which it is false, so neither is necessarily true. Choice D contradicts $P > L$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Five contestants finish a race without any ties. They are Elena, Fabio, Giulia, Hugo, and Ivo. Giulia finishes before Elena, and Elena before Fabio. Ivo finishes immediately after Fabio. Hugo finishes neither first nor last. Which statement is necessarily true?

- A) Elena finishes second.
- B) Hugo finishes third.
- C) Fabio finishes fourth.
- D) Hugo finishes before Elena.

| Position | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First arrangement | Giulia | Hugo | Elena | Fabio | Ivo |
| Second arrangement | Giulia | Elena | Hugo | Fabio | Ivo |

<div class="spiegazione">

Giulia, Elena, and Fabio finish in that order, and Ivo follows immediately after Fabio, so their order is Giulia, Elena, Fabio, Ivo, with Fabio and Ivo together. Hugo must be inserted into this sequence. He cannot be first, last, or between Fabio and Ivo, so he must be between Giulia and Elena or between Elena and Fabio. There are therefore two **possible arrangements**, and in both Fabio is fourth and Ivo fifth. Choices A, B, and D are true in only one of the two arrangements, so they are not necessarily true. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
