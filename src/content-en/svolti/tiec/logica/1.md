## Exercise 1

What is the negation of “All the students in the class passed the English exam”?

- A) No student in the class passed the English exam.
- B) Some students in the class passed the English exam.
- C) At least one student in the class did not pass the English exam.
- D) Only some students in the class passed the English exam.

$$
\lnot\,(\text{all are } B) \equiv \text{at least one is not } B
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The statement refers to *all* the students, so it becomes false as soon as one student fails to pass. By the rule for **negating quantifiers**, its negation is therefore “At least one student in the class did not pass the English exam.” Choice A replaces “all” with “none.” In a class where half the students passed, both the original statement and choice A are false. Since a statement and its negation always have opposite truth values, A is not the negation. Choice D excludes the case where no one passed, which makes the original statement false, so D is not its negation either. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is the negation of “The store is open on Sundays or offers home delivery”?

- A) The store is not open on Sundays or does not offer home delivery.
- B) The store is open on Sundays and offers home delivery.
- C) The store is open on Sundays but does not offer home delivery.
- D) The store is not open on Sundays and does not offer home delivery.

$$
\lnot (A \lor C) \equiv \lnot A \land \lnot C
$$

| $A$ | $C$ | $A \lor C$ | $\lnot A \land \lnot C$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T | T | T | F |
| T | F | T | F |
| F | T | T | F |
| F | F | F | T |

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $A$ = “the store is open on Sundays” and $C$ = “the store offers home delivery.” The statement is the disjunction $A \lor C$, which is false only when both parts are false. By **De Morgan's laws**, the negation is therefore $\lnot A \land \lnot C$, or “The store is not open on Sundays and does not offer home delivery.” Choice A negates the two parts but keeps “or,” so it also holds for a store that opens on Sundays and does not deliver, a case in which the original statement is true. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

What is the negation of “Every branch of the bank has at least one employee who speaks German”?

- A) At least one branch of the bank has no employees who speak German.
- B) No branch of the bank has an employee who speaks German.
- C) Every branch of the bank has at least one employee who does not speak German.
- D) At least one branch of the bank has an employee who does not speak German.

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\lnot\,(\text{every branch has at least one employee who speaks German}) \\[6pt]
&\equiv \text{at least one branch } \lnot\,(\text{has at least one employee who speaks German}) \\[6pt]
&\equiv \text{at least one branch has no employees who speak German}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The statement contains two quantifiers. Negate one at a time. Negating “every branch” gives “at least one branch.” Then, by the rule for **negating quantifiers**, negating “has at least one employee who speaks German” gives “has no employees who speak German.” The negation is therefore choice A. Choice D negates only the employee's property and leaves the inner quantifier in place. A branch with one employee who speaks German and another who does not makes D true without disproving the original statement. Choice B says much more than the negation because it concerns every branch. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
