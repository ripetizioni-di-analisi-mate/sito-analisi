## Exercise 1

Read the text and answer the question:

> Many small towns are trying to attract young families. In Valdoro, a town in the mountains, the local council has opened a shared office in the old railway station. For a monthly fee, residents can rent a desk, use a fast internet connection and book a meeting room. The idea came from a group of residents who worked for companies in the city but did not want to commute every day. Since the office opened two years ago, eight families have moved to the town, and a bakery that had closed has reopened. Not everyone is enthusiastic, however. Some older residents complain that house prices have risen and that the new arrivals rarely take part in local events. The mayor admits that the project cannot solve every problem, but she believes that a town without young people has no future.

What is the main purpose of the shared office in Valdoro?

- A) To give tourists a place to work during their holidays.
- B) To replace the old railway station.
- C) To provide meeting rooms for the local council.
- D) To allow people to work for distant companies without travelling every day.

The relevant sentence is *The idea came from a group of residents who worked for companies in the city but did not want to commute every day.*

<div class="spiegazione">

The passage says that the idea for a shared office came from residents who worked for companies in the city and did not want a daily commute. The purpose of the office is therefore to let people work for companies based elsewhere without traveling each day, as option D says. Option A introduces tourists, whom the passage does not mention. Option B confuses location with purpose, since the office is *in* the old station. Option C takes a true detail, the meeting rooms, and attributes it to the town government, whereas it is residents who book them. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In the text about the town of Valdoro, which statement is true?

- A) The bakery opened for the first time two years ago.
- B) Some residents think that the newcomers are not involved in community life.
- C) All the older residents are against the project.
- D) House prices have fallen since the office opened.

The relevant sentence is *Some older residents complain that house prices have risen and that the new arrivals rarely take part in local events.*

<div class="spiegazione">

The passage reports that some older residents complain that the newcomers seldom participate in local events (*rarely take part in local events*). Option B therefore restates information from the text correctly. Option A reads *reopened* as *opened*, whereas the text says that the bakery *had closed* and then reopened. Option C changes *some* to *all*. Option D reverses the information because house prices have gone up (*have risen*). The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

In the text about the town of Valdoro, the word "commute" is closest in meaning to:

- A) change job
- B) exchange something for something else
- C) travel regularly between home and work
- D) work from home

*Commute* means to travel regularly between home and work, here every day.

<div class="spiegazione">

In the passage, *commute* appears in *did not want to commute every day* and refers to people who live in Valdoro and work for companies in the city. The context therefore indicates the daily journey between home and work, the meaning of the Italian *fare il pendolare*. Option B appeals to readers who associate *commute* with the Italian *commutare* (“to switch or convert”). Option D describes precisely what the shared office makes possible, which is the opposite of commuting. Option A has no connection to the context. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
