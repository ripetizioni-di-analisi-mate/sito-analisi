## Exercise 1

Read the notice on a door in a shop and answer the question:

> STAFF ONLY. Customers are kindly asked to use the main entrance.

What does the notice mean?

- A) Customers cannot enter the shop.
- B) Staff must use the main entrance.
- C) Only employees may use this door.
- D) The main entrance is closed to customers.

*Staff only* means the area is reserved for employees. *Customers are kindly asked to use the main entrance* means that customers should enter through the main entrance.

<div class="spiegazione">

*Staff* refers to the people who work in the store, and *staff only* means *reserved for employees*. The second sentence asks customers to use another door, the main entrance. The door with the sign is therefore reserved for employees, as option C says. Option A goes further than the text because customers can enter through the main entrance. Option B directs the request to employees when it is addressed to customers, and option D reverses the meaning of the notice. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Read the text message and answer the question:

> Hi Tom, my train is running 20 minutes late. Don't wait for me at the station, I'll take a taxi to the office. See you there! Anna

According to the message, which statement is true?

- A) Anna will go directly to the office.
- B) Anna wants Tom to meet her at the station.
- C) Anna will arrive at the office by train.
- D) Anna's train has been cancelled.

*Don't wait for me at the station* means Tom should not wait for Anna at the station. *I'll take a taxi to the office* means Anna will travel to work by cab. *See you there* means they will meet at the office.

<div class="spiegazione">

Anna writes that she will take a taxi from the station to the office and that the two of them will meet there (*See you there*). Anna will therefore go directly to the office, as option A says. Option B contradicts *Don't wait for me at the station*. Option C mixes up the means of transportation, since Anna will travel from the station to the office by taxi. Option D turns a delay into a cancellation, whereas *running late* means *behind schedule*. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Read the email and answer the question:

> Dear customer, your order 4521 has been dispatched. Please note that deliveries to islands may take up to two extra working days. If you are not at home, the courier will leave a card with instructions to collect the parcel from the nearest pick-up point.

According to the email, what happens if the customer is not at home when the courier arrives?

- A) The parcel will be left at the door.
- B) The courier will try again the next day.
- C) The order will be sent back.
- D) The customer can collect the parcel at a nearby pick-up point.

*The courier will leave a card with instructions to collect the parcel from the nearest pick-up point* means that the delivery driver will leave a notice explaining how to pick up the package at the closest collection point.

<div class="spiegazione">

The answer is in the last sentence of the email, which begins with *If you are not at home*. The courier will leave a notice (*card*) explaining how to pick up (*collect*) the package at the nearest collection point, so the customer can get it at a nearby pickup location. Option B describes a common delivery practice that the email does not mention. The text does not support options A or C. The sentence about islands concerns delivery times and does not answer this question. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
