## Exercise 1

Complete the sentence: "The new bridge …… in 2015."

- A) built
- B) has been built
- C) was building
- D) was built

The bridge receives the action, so the sentence takes the passive, *be* + past participle. *In 2015* is a completed time in the past, so *be* takes the past simple, giving *was built*.

<div class="spiegazione">

The bridge is the thing being built, so the sentence requires the **passive voice**, formed with *be* followed by the past participle. The year 2015 is a completed time in the past, so *be* takes the past simple and the correct form is *was built*. Option A is active and would mean that the bridge built something. Option B is a correctly formed passive, but the present perfect does not go with a specific past date. Option C is active and in the past continuous. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

"I am tired," said Laura. Which is the correct reported speech?

- A) Laura said she was tired.
- B) Laura said that I was tired.
- C) Laura told that she was tired.
- D) Laura said me she was tired.

*am* → *was*, *I* → *she*. The listener is not a direct object of *say*, but is a direct object of *tell*.

<div class="spiegazione">

In **reported speech** with a past reporting verb, the present simple *am* becomes the past simple *was*. The pronoun *I* becomes *she* because the person reporting is talking about Laura. Option B keeps *I* and attributes the tiredness to the person reporting. Options C and D misuse *tell* and *say* because *tell* takes an object naming the listener (*told me*), while *say* cannot take the listener as its direct object. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

"We will finish the report tomorrow," the manager said. Which is the correct reported speech?

- A) The manager said they finished the report the following day.
- B) The manager said they would finish the report the following day.
- C) The manager told they would finish the report the following day.
- D) The manager said they would have finished the report the following day.

*will* → *would*; *we* → *they*; *tomorrow* → *the following day*.

<div class="spiegazione">

In **reported speech**, *will* becomes *would* and *tomorrow* becomes *the following day* because the day is identified in relation to the time when the original words were spoken. Option B makes all the changes correctly. Option A uses the past simple *finished*, which would mean that the report was actually finished. Option C uses *told* without an object naming the listener. Option D uses *would have finished*. Its perfect form would report a different statement or suggest an unrealized possibility. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
