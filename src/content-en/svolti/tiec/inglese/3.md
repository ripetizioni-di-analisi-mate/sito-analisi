## Exercise 1

Complete the sentence: "If it …… tomorrow, we will cancel the trip."

- A) will rain
- B) rains
- C) rained
- D) would rain

The main clause with *will* signals the first conditional. The *if*-clause takes the present simple, *rains*.

<div class="spiegazione">

The main clause *we will cancel the trip* contains *will*, and the condition concerns tomorrow, so it is a future possibility. This is therefore the **first conditional**, in which the *if*-clause takes the present simple, as in *If it rains tomorrow*. Option A copies the Italian future tense (*se pioverà*, “if it rains”), but the *if*-clause of the first conditional does not take *will*. *Rained* and *would rain* are forms used in the second conditional and do not fit *will*. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Complete the sentence: "If I …… more free time, I would learn Spanish."

- A) have
- B) will have
- C) had
- D) would have

The main clause with *would* + base form signals the second conditional. The *if*-clause takes the past simple, *had*.

<div class="spiegazione">

The main clause *I would learn Spanish* uses *would* followed by the base form. The situation is unreal in the present because the speaker actually has little free time. In the **second conditional**, the *if*-clause takes the past simple, so the correct form is *had* (*if I had more free time, I would learn Spanish*). *Have* and *will have* are forms used in the first conditional. *Would have* does not belong in the *if*-clause here. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Complete the sentence: "If they had left home earlier, they …… the flight."

- A) wouldn't miss
- B) won't miss
- C) hadn't missed
- D) wouldn't have missed

The past perfect in the *if*-clause signals the third conditional. The main clause takes *would have* + past participle, giving *wouldn't have missed*.

<div class="spiegazione">

The *if*-clause contains the past perfect *had left*, so it describes a past possibility that did not materialize, since they actually left late. In the **third conditional**, the main clause takes *would have* followed by the past participle, so the correct form is *wouldn't have missed* (*they would not have missed the flight*). Option A belongs to the second conditional and refers to the present, while option B belongs to the first conditional. Option C uses the past perfect in the main clause as well, where a conditional form is required. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
