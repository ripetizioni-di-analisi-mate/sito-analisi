## Exercise 1

How does the quadratic $x^2 - x - 12$ factor?

- A) $(x + 4)(x - 3)$
- B) $(x - 6)(x + 2)$
- C) $(x - 4)(x + 3)$
- D) $(x - 4)(x - 3)$

$$
m + n = -1,
\qquad
m \cdot n = -12
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
m = -4,\ n = 3
$$
$$
x^2 - x - 12 = (x - 4)(x + 3)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The quadratic has leading coefficient $1$, so find two numbers with **sum** $-1$ and **product** $-12$. The product is negative, so the numbers have opposite signs. The sum is negative, so the number with greater absolute value must be negative. The numbers are therefore $-4$ and $3$. Choice A reverses the signs and expands to $x^2 + x - 12$. Choice B gives product $-12$ but sum $-4$. Choice D gives product $+12$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Which expression equals the polynomial $8a^3 - 27$?

- A) $(2a - 3)(4a^2 + 6a + 9)$
- B) $(2a - 3)(4a^2 - 6a + 9)$
- C) $(2a - 3)(4a^2 + 12a + 9)$
- D) $(2a - 3)^3$

$$
8a^3 - 27 = (2a)^3 - 3^3
$$
$$
(2a)^3 - 3^3 = (2a - 3)\left((2a)^2 + 2a \cdot 3 + 3^2\right) = (2a - 3)(4a^2 + 6a + 9)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The polynomial is a **difference of cubes** because $8a^3 = (2a)^3$ and $27 = 3^3$. By the formula $x^3 - y^3 = (x - y)(x^2 + xy + y^2)$, the quadratic factor is $4a^2 + 6a + 9$, with the single product $6a$ and a plus sign in the middle. Choice B uses the sign for a sum of cubes. Choice C uses twice the product, $12a$. Choice D is the cube of a binomial, whose expansion also contains terms in $a^2$ and $a$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A company’s profit in thousands of euros when it produces $x$ thousand items is $P(x) = -x^3 + 7x^2 - 14x + 8$. Given that $P(1) = 0$, what is the complete factorization of $P(x)$?

- A) $-(x + 1)(x - 2)(x - 4)$
- B) $(x - 1)(x - 2)(x - 4)$
- C) $-(x - 1)(x + 2)(x + 4)$
- D) $-(x - 1)(x - 2)(x - 4)$

$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc|c}
 & -1 & 7 & -14 & 8 \\
1 & & -1 & 6 & -8 \\ \hline
 & -1 & 6 & -8 & 0
\end{array}
$$
$$
P(x) = (x - 1)(-x^2 + 6x - 8) = -(x - 1)(x^2 - 6x + 8) = -(x - 1)(x - 2)(x - 4)
$$

::grafico{f="-x^3+7*x^2-14*x+8+0*sqrt(x)" punti="1,0,pieno;2,0,pieno;4,0,pieno" xmin=-0.5 xmax=5.2 ymin=-7 ymax=9 nomi="x,P" legenda="P = -(x - 1)(x - 2)(x - 4)" titolo="The profit $P(x) = -(x - 1)(x - 2)(x - 4)$ is zero at $x = 1$, $x = 2$, and $x = 4$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Since $P(1) = 0$, the polynomial is divisible by $x - 1$. **Synthetic division** gives the quotient $-x^2 + 6x - 8$. Factor out the minus sign and factor $x^2 - 6x + 8$ using the numbers $-2$ and $-4$, whose sum is $-6$ and whose product is $8$. The leading term confirms the sign because $-x \cdot x \cdot x = -x^3$. Choice B omits the minus sign. Choices A and C have zeros with incorrect signs. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
