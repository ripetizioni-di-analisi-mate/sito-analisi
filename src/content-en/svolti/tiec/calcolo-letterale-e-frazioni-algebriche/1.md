## Exercise 1

What is the product $(-2a^2b)^3 \cdot \dfrac{1}{4}ab^2$?

- A) $2a^7b^5$
- B) $-2a^7b^5$
- C) $-\dfrac{3}{2}a^7b^5$
- D) $-2a^6b^6$

$$
(-2a^2b)^3 = (-2)^3 a^6 b^3 = -8a^6b^3
$$
$$
-8a^6b^3 \cdot \frac{1}{4}ab^2 = -\frac{8}{4}\,a^{6+1}b^{3+2} = -2a^7b^5
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To take the **power of a monomial**, cube the coefficient and multiply each exponent by $3$, giving $(-2a^2b)^3 = -8a^6b^3$. The sign stays negative because the cube of a negative number is negative. In a **product of monomials**, multiply the coefficients and add the exponents to obtain $-2a^7b^5$. Choice A uses the wrong sign for the cube. Choice C comes from the calculation $(-2)^3 = -6$, and choice D multiplies the exponents instead of adding them. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What does $(x - 2)(x + 3) - x(x + 1)$ simplify to?

- A) $-6$
- B) $2x - 6$
- C) $-2x - 6$
- D) $2x^2 + 2x - 6$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x - 2)(x + 3) - x(x + 1) &= x^2 + 3x - 2x - 6 - x^2 - x \\[6pt]
&= x^2 + x - 6 - x^2 - x \\[6pt]
&= -6
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **product of polynomials** $(x - 2)(x + 3)$ equals $x^2 + x - 6$. The minus sign before $x(x + 1) = x^2 + x$ changes the signs of both terms, so subtract both $x^2$ and $x$. The quadratic and linear terms cancel, leaving $-6$. Choice B changes the sign only of $x^2$. Choice D adds instead of subtracting. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A company sells $x$ units of a product at a price of $(50 - x)$ euros per unit and incurs a total cost of $(10x + 300)$ euros. Which polynomial gives its profit, defined as revenue minus cost?

- A) $-x^2 + 40x + 300$
- B) $-x^2 + 60x - 300$
- C) $x^2 + 40x - 300$
- D) $-x^2 + 40x - 300$

$$
R(x) = x(50 - x) = 50x - x^2
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
R(x) - C(x) &= 50x - x^2 - (10x + 300) \\[6pt]
&= 50x - x^2 - 10x - 300 \\[6pt]
&= -x^2 + 40x - 300
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Revenue is the price per unit times the number of units sold, so $R(x) = x(50 - x) = 50x - x^2$. Subtract the entire cost. The minus sign before the polynomial $10x + 300$ therefore changes the sign of **every** term, giving a profit of $-x^2 + 40x - 300$. At $x = 20$ the result is $-400 + 800 - 300 = 100$ euros, equal to the revenue $600$ minus the cost $500$. Choice A applies the minus sign only to $10x$. Choice C writes the revenue as $50x + x^2$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
