## Exercise 1

A bill of $(x^2 + 4x - 12)$ euros, where $x$ is an integer greater than $2$, is split equally among $(x + 6)$ people. How much does each person pay in euros?

- A) $x + 2$
- B) $x - 6$
- C) $x - 2$
- D) $x + 6$

$$
x^2 + 4x - 12 = (x + 6)(x - 2)
$$
$$
\frac{x^2 + 4x - 12}{x + 6} = \frac{(x + 6)(x - 2)}{x + 6} = x - 2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each person’s share is the fraction $\dfrac{x^2 + 4x - 12}{x + 6}$, whose denominator is positive because $x > 2$. To factor the quadratic in the numerator, find two numbers with sum $4$ and product $-12$, namely $6$ and $-2$. To **simplify**, cancel the common factor $x + 6$, leaving $x - 2$. With $x = 4$ the bill is $20$ euros for $10$ people, or $2$ euros per person, in agreement with $x - 2$. Choice A uses the numbers $6$ and $2$, whose product is $+12$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Which choice gives the simplified form of $\dfrac{x^2 - 4x + 4}{x^2 - 4}$ with its domain restrictions?

- A) $\dfrac{x - 2}{x + 2}$ for $x \neq -2$
- B) $\dfrac{x - 2}{x + 2}$ for $x \neq \pm 2$
- C) $x - 1$ for $x \neq \pm 2$
- D) $\dfrac{x + 2}{x - 2}$ for $x \neq \pm 2$

$$
\frac{x^2 - 4x + 4}{x^2 - 4} = \frac{(x - 2)^2}{(x - 2)(x + 2)}
$$
$$
\text{Domain restrictions}
\qquad
\begin{cases}
x \neq 2 \\
x \neq -2
\end{cases}
$$
$$
\frac{(x - 2)^2}{(x - 2)(x + 2)} = \frac{x - 2}{x + 2}
$$

::grafico{f="(x-2)/(x+2)" punti="2,0,vuoto" asintoti="x=-2;y=1" xmin=-8 xmax=7 ymin=-6 ymax=8 nomi="x,y" legenda="y = \dfrac{x - 2}{x + 2}" titolo="The graph of $\dfrac{x^2 - 4x + 4}{x^2 - 4}$ is the graph of $\dfrac{x - 2}{x + 2}$ with the point $(2;0)$ removed"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Find the **domain restrictions** from the original fraction. The denominator $(x - 2)(x + 2)$ is zero at $x = 2$ and $x = -2$, so both values must be excluded. The numerator is the perfect square $(x - 2)^2$, so cancel one factor of $x - 2$ to obtain $\dfrac{x - 2}{x + 2}$. Choice A finds the restrictions after simplifying and loses the restriction at $x \neq 2$. Choice C cancels the terms $x^2$, which cannot be canceled because they are not factors. Choice D inverts the fraction. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

What is the least common multiple of the polynomials $x^2 - 9$, $x^2 + 6x + 9$, and $2x + 6$?

- A) $2(x - 3)(x + 3)^2$
- B) $(x - 3)(x + 3)^2$
- C) $x + 3$
- D) $2(x - 3)(x + 3)^4$

$$
x^2 - 9 = (x - 3)(x + 3),
\qquad
x^2 + 6x + 9 = (x + 3)^2,
\qquad
2x + 6 = 2(x + 3)
$$
$$
\text{lcm} = 2(x - 3)(x + 3)^2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Factor the three polynomials using a difference of squares, a perfect-square trinomial, and a common factor, respectively. The **least common multiple** contains every factor occurring in any of them, each included once at its greatest exponent, giving $2$, $x - 3$, and $(x + 3)^2$. Choice C is the greatest common divisor. Choice B omits the numerical factor $2$, and choice D multiplies together all occurrences of $x + 3$ instead of taking the greatest exponent. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
