## Exercise 1

What is the expansion of $(3x - 2y)^2$?

- A) $9x^2 - 4y^2$
- B) $9x^2 - 6xy + 4y^2$
- C) $9x^2 - 12xy + 4y^2$
- D) $9x^2 - 12xy - 4y^2$

$$
(3x - 2y)^2 = (3x)^2 + 2 \cdot 3x \cdot (-2y) + (-2y)^2 = 9x^2 - 12xy + 4y^2
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,3 0,3;3,3 5,3 5,5 3,5" linee="0,0 5,0 5,5 0,5 0,0;3,0 3,5;0,3 5,3" etichette="1.5,1.5,9x²;4,4,4y²;4,1.5,6xy;1.5,4,6xy;1.5,-0.5,3x;4,-0.5,2y" xmin=-1 xmax=6 ymin=-1.2 ymax=5.8 titolo="In the square with side length $3x + 2y$, the two rectangles each have area $6xy$, so together they contribute $12xy$. In the square of $3x - 2y$, this term has a minus sign"}

<div class="spiegazione">

To find the **square of a binomial**, add the square of the first term, twice the product of the two terms, and the square of the second term. Twice the product is $2 \cdot 3x \cdot (-2y) = -12xy$, while the square of $-2y$ is positive, giving $4y^2$. Choice A omits twice the product. Choice B uses only the single product $6xy$, and choice D gives $4y^2$ the sign of $-2y$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is the expansion of $(x + y - 1)^2$?

- A) $x^2 + y^2 + 1 + 2xy - 2x - 2y$
- B) $x^2 + y^2 + 1$
- C) $x^2 + y^2 + 1 + 2xy + 2x + 2y$
- D) $x^2 + y^2 - 1 + 2xy - 2x - 2y$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x + y - 1)^2 &= x^2 + y^2 + (-1)^2 + 2 \cdot x \cdot y + 2 \cdot x \cdot (-1) + 2 \cdot y \cdot (-1) \\[6pt]
&= x^2 + y^2 + 1 + 2xy - 2x - 2y
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **square of a trinomial** is the sum of its three squared terms and twice the product of each of the three pairs. Each square has a positive coefficient, giving $(-1)^2 = 1$. Each cross term has the sign of the product of its two terms, so $2xy$ has a positive coefficient, whereas $-2x$ and $-2y$ have negative coefficients. Choice B keeps only the squares. Choice C uses the wrong signs for the cross terms involving $-1$, and choice D writes $(-1)^2 = -1$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A company’s revenue increases each year by a fraction $r$ of the previous year’s revenue. After three years it has therefore been multiplied by $(1 + r)^3$. What is the expansion of $(1 + r)^3$?

- A) $1 + r^3$
- B) $1 + 3r$
- C) $1 + 2r + r^2$
- D) $1 + 3r + 3r^2 + r^3$

$$
(1 + r)^3 = 1^3 + 3 \cdot 1^2 \cdot r + 3 \cdot 1 \cdot r^2 + r^3 = 1 + 3r + 3r^2 + r^3
$$
$$
r = 0.1
\quad \Longrightarrow \quad
1 + 0.3 + 0.03 + 0.001 = 1.331
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the **cube of a binomial** with $a = 1$ and $b = r$, the coefficients are $1$, $3$, $3$, and $1$, so $(1 + r)^3 = 1 + 3r + 3r^2 + r^3$. With annual growth of $10\%$, revenue is multiplied by $1.331$, an increase of $33.1\%$ over three years. Choice B keeps only the first two terms and adds three increases of $10\%$. Choice C squares the binomial, and choice A raises each term of the sum to the power separately. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
