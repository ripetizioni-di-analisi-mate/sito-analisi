## Exercise 1

What is the sum $\dfrac{2}{x - 1} + \dfrac{3}{x + 1}$ for $x \neq \pm 1$?

- A) $\dfrac{5}{2x}$
- B) $\dfrac{5x + 1}{x^2 - 1}$
- C) $\dfrac{5}{x^2 - 1}$
- D) $\dfrac{5x - 1}{x^2 - 1}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{2}{x - 1} + \frac{3}{x + 1} &= \frac{2(x + 1) + 3(x - 1)}{(x - 1)(x + 1)} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{2x + 2 + 3x - 3}{x^2 - 1} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{5x - 1}{x^2 - 1}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The denominators have no nonconstant common factors, so their least common denominator is their product $(x - 1)(x + 1) = x^2 - 1$. When **adding rational expressions**, multiply each numerator by the factor missing from its denominator. Multiply $2$ by $x + 1$ and $3$ by $x - 1$. The numerator is therefore $5x - 1$. Choice A adds the numerators and denominators separately. Choice C adds only the numerators, and choice B multiplies each numerator by its own denominator. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is the product $\dfrac{x^2 - 9}{x^2 + 2x} \cdot \dfrac{x + 2}{x - 3}$ for $x \neq 0$, $x \neq -2$, and $x \neq 3$?

- A) $\dfrac{x - 3}{x}$
- B) $\dfrac{x + 3}{x^2}$
- C) $\dfrac{x + 3}{x}$
- D) $x + 3$

$$
\frac{x^2 - 9}{x^2 + 2x} \cdot \frac{x + 2}{x - 3} = \frac{(x - 3)(x + 3)}{x(x + 2)} \cdot \frac{x + 2}{x - 3}
$$
$$
\frac{(x - 3)(x + 3)}{x(x + 2)} \cdot \frac{x + 2}{x - 3} = \frac{x + 3}{x}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Factor the numerators and denominators to obtain $x^2 - 9 = (x - 3)(x + 3)$ and $x^2 + 2x = x(x + 2)$. When **multiplying rational expressions**, common factors can be canceled even across fractions, so cancel $x - 3$ and $x + 2$. This leaves $\dfrac{x + 3}{x}$ with the stated restrictions. Choice A cancels the factor $x + 3$ instead of $x - 3$. Choice D omits the factor $x$ in the denominator. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A budget of $600$ euros buys items costing $x$ euros each, with $x > 0$. If the price increases by $2$ euros and the full budget is spent in each case, the number of items purchased decreases by $\dfrac{600}{x} - \dfrac{600}{x + 2}$. Which expression is equivalent to this decrease?

- A) $\dfrac{600}{x(x + 2)}$
- B) $\dfrac{1200}{x(x + 2)}$
- C) $-\dfrac{1200}{x(x + 2)}$
- D) $\dfrac{1200}{x^2 + 2}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{600}{x} - \frac{600}{x + 2} &= \frac{600(x + 2) - 600x}{x(x + 2)} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{600x + 1200 - 600x}{x(x + 2)} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{1200}{x(x + 2)}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The common denominator is $x(x + 2)$, so multiply the first numerator by $x + 2$ and the second by $x$. In the **difference of rational expressions**, the $600x$ terms cancel, leaving $1200$. At $x = 10$ the number of items drops from $60$ to $50$, and indeed $\dfrac{1200}{10 \cdot 12} = 10$. Choice A does not multiply $600$ by $2$. Choice C reverses the order of subtraction, and choice D incorrectly expands the product $x(x + 2)$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
