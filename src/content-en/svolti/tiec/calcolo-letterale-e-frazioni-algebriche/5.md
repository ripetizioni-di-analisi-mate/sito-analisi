## Exercise 1

What is the factored form of $3x^3 - 12x$?

- A) $3x(x - 2)^2$
- B) $3x(x - 2)(x + 2)$
- C) $x(3x - 2)(3x + 2)$
- D) $3x(x - 4)(x + 4)$

$$
3x^3 - 12x = 3x(x^2 - 4) = 3x(x - 2)(x + 2)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The terms share the factor $3x$, so first **factor out the common factor** to obtain $3x(x^2 - 4)$. The factor $x^2 - 4$ is a **difference of squares** because $4 = 2^2$, so it factors as $(x - 2)(x + 2)$. Choice A confuses a difference of squares with the square of a binomial. Choice D uses $4$ instead of its square root $2$, and choice C expands to $9x^3 - 4x$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

How does the polynomial $ax - 2a + 3x - 6$ factor?

- A) $(x + 2)(a - 3)$
- B) $(x - 2)(a - 3)$
- C) $(x - 3)(a + 2)$
- D) $(x - 2)(a + 3)$

$$
\begin{aligned}
ax - 2a + 3x - 6 &= a(x - 2) + 3(x - 2) \\[6pt]
&= (x - 2)(a + 3)
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The four terms have no common factor other than constants, so try **factoring by grouping**. Factor $a$ out of the first two terms and $3$ out of the last two. Both groups then contain $x - 2$, which can also be factored out. Check the result by expanding to get $(x - 2)(a + 3) = ax + 3x - 2a - 6$. Choice B gives the constant term $+6$ when expanded because it uses $-3$ in the second group. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A company sells $q$ items for $(40 - 2q)$ euros each and spends $10$ euros to produce each item. Which expression gives its profit $q(40 - 2q) - 10q$ in factored form?

- A) $2q(15 - q)$
- B) $2q(15 - 2q)$
- C) $2q(25 - q)$
- D) $q(30 - q)$

$$
q(40 - 2q) - 10q = 40q - 2q^2 - 10q = 30q - 2q^2
$$
$$
30q - 2q^2 = 2q(15 - q)
$$

::grafico{f="30*x-2*x^2+0*sqrt(x)" punti="0,0,pieno;15,0,pieno;7.5,112.5,pieno" segmenti="7.5,0,7.5,112.5" xmin=-2 xmax=18 ymin=-20 ymax=135 nomi="q,P" legenda="P = 2q(15 - q)" titolo="The profit $P = 2q\,(15 - q)$ is zero at $q = 0$ and $q = 15$ and positive between those values"}

<div class="spiegazione">

First expand and combine like terms to obtain $30q - 2q^2$. The two terms share $2q$, so **factoring out the common factor** gives $2q(15 - q)$ because $\dfrac{30q}{2q} = 15$ and $\dfrac{-2q^2}{2q} = -q$. The factored form shows that profit is zero at $q = 0$ and $q = 15$. Choice C adds the cost instead of subtracting it. Choice D omits the coefficient $2$ of $q^2$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
