## Exercise 1

The cost in thousands of euros of producing $x$ thousand items is $C(x) = x^3 - 6x^2 + 15x + 10$. What is the remainder when $C(x)$ is divided by $x - 2$?

- A) $-52$
- B) $10$
- C) $72$
- D) $24$

$$
C(2) = 2^3 - 6 \cdot 2^2 + 15 \cdot 2 + 10 = 8 - 24 + 30 + 10 = 24
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the **remainder theorem**, the remainder upon division by $x - 2$ is the value of the polynomial at $2$, so there is no need to perform the division. Evaluating gives $C(2) = 24$. In this context, producing $2000$ items costs $24\,000$ euros. Choice A gives $C(-2)$, which corresponds to the divisor $x + 2$. Choice B gives the constant term $C(0)$, and choice C uses the wrong sign for $-6 \cdot 2^2$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The polynomial $x^3 + kx^2 - 4x + 3$ is divisible by $x - 3$ if and only if $k$ has which value?

- A) $2$
- B) $-2$
- C) $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- D) $-18$

$$
P(3) = 27 + 9k - 12 + 3 = 18 + 9k
$$
$$
18 + 9k = 0
\iff
k = -2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

A polynomial is **divisible by** $x - 3$ if and only if it is zero at $3$. Substitute $x = 3$ to get $P(3) = 18 + 9k$, then require $18 + 9k = 0$, giving $k = -2$. To check, $27 - 18 - 12 + 3 = 0$. Choice C sets $P(-3) = 0$, which tests divisibility by $x + 3$. Choice D stops at $9k = -18$ without dividing by $9$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Which of the following binomials divides the polynomial $P(x) = 2x^3 - 3x^2 - 11x + 6$?

- A) $x + 3$
- B) $x - 2$
- C) $2x - 1$
- D) $x - 1$

$$
P(-3) = -54 - 27 + 33 + 6 = -42,
\qquad
P(2) = 16 - 12 - 22 + 6 = -12
$$
$$
P\!\left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = \frac{2}{8} - \frac{3}{4} - \frac{11}{2} + 6 = \frac{1 - 3 - 22 + 24}{4} = 0,
\qquad
P(1) = 2 - 3 - 11 + 6 = -6
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The binomial $2x - 1$ is zero at $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and equals $2\left(x - \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$, so it divides $P(x)$ if and only if $P\!\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right) = 0$. For each choice, evaluate $P$ at the zero of the binomial, using the criterion for **divisibility by** $x - a$. Only $P\!\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$ is zero, so $2x - 1$ is a divisor. The other three values are nonzero. The zeros of $P$ are $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $3$, and $-2$, and choice A reverses the sign of the zero $3$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
