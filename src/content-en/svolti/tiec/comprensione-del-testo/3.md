## Passage

> In many mountain towns, the number of births has fallen steadily over the past twenty years, and the effects are most visible in the schools. In one town in the Apennines that had three elementary schools twenty years ago, only one remains today. Children of different ages study together in the same classroom. The other two buildings have been sold. One now houses a hostel and the other an artisan’s workshop.
>
> A school closure has effects beyond the building. Families with young children in the outlying villages must take them into town each day. Some have chosen to move down to the valley, where the school is close by and local services stay open longer. Those who remain pay for school bus transportation, a cost the town covers only in part.
>
> Over the past three years, however, the decline in enrollment has stopped. Some young couples who work remotely for city-based companies have bought homes in town, attracted by low prices and the quiet. The mayor said that if elementary school enrollment remains stable for another two years, the town will ask the regional government to fund a new school cafeteria.

## Exercise 1

It can be inferred from the passage that:

- A) the young couples who moved to town have children enrolled in elementary school
- B) the town has fewer residents than twenty years ago
- C) the hostel mainly serves hikers
- D) twenty years ago the town had more elementary schools than it has today

| option | situation consistent with the passage in which the option is false | assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | the couples have no school-age children | unsupported |
| B | the incoming couples offset the number who left | unsupported |
| C | the hostel mainly accommodates students on school trips | unsupported |
| D | none, because there were three schools and today there is one | supported |

<div class="spiegazione">

Apply the **inference test** by looking, for each option, for a situation in which the passage is true and the option false. There is no such situation for D because the passage says there were three elementary schools twenty years ago and only one remains today. Option D is therefore supported. Option B appeals to readers who connect fewer births with a falling population, but the population also depends on who leaves and who arrives, and the passage gives no population figures. Options A and C add facts: the text does not say whether the couples have children or who stays at the hostel. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Which of the following statements is NOT supported by the passage?

- A) Families pay part of the cost of the school bus
- B) Some families moved down to the valley because of the cost of the school bus
- C) The town’s school now has classes with children of different ages
- D) School enrollment was falling before the past three years

| option | line from the passage | assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | “Those who remain pay for school bus transportation … the town covers only in part” | supported |
| B | the reasons given are the nearby school and longer opening hours for services | unsupported |
| C | “Children of different ages study together in the same classroom” | explicit |
| D | “Over the past three years, however, the decline in enrollment has stopped” | supported |

<div class="spiegazione">

The question is negative, so three options have a supporting line and the answer is the only one that does not. The passage gives the nearby school and longer opening hours for services as the reasons for moving, whereas it mentions the bus cost in connection with those who stay. Option B therefore assigns a **cause** the text does not give. Option A follows from the sentence about the school bus: if the town pays only part of the cost, families pay the rest. Option D follows from the sentence with *however*, since a decline that “has stopped” over the past three years was underway before then. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

It can be inferred from the mayor’s statement that:

- A) the regional government has already promised to fund the new cafeteria
- B) the new cafeteria will be built within two years
- C) the request for regional funding depends on what happens to enrollment over the next two years
- D) elementary school enrollment has risen in the past three years

| option | comparison with the statement | assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | the town “will ask,” so a request has not yet been made | unsupported |
| B | the town will request funding *after* the two years | unsupported |
| C | “if elementary school enrollment remains stable for another two years, the town will ask” | supported |
| D | the decline “has stopped,” meaning enrollment is stable | unsupported |

<div class="spiegazione">

The mayor’s statement takes the form “if something happens, the town will make a request.” Thus the request to the regional government depends on enrollment over the next two years, as option C says. Option A is unsupported because the passage describes a future request and mentions no existing commitment from the region. Option B assumes the cafeteria will already have been built when the passage places only the funding request after the two years. Option D is **exaggerated**: a decline that has stopped means enrollment is stable rather than rising, as the word “stable” in the mayor’s statement confirms. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
