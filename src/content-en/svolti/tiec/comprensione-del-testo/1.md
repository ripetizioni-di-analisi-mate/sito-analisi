## Passage

> In recent years, many small towns have opened shared workspaces, known as *coworking spaces*, in former schools or public offices that stood empty. The idea is to offer people who work remotely a desk, a fast connection, and a meeting room a few minutes from home, without a daily commute into the city.
>
> In one such town, halfway between two provincial capitals, the coworking space opened two years ago with twenty workstations. At first, most users were freelancers, such as graphic designers and consultants. Today half the users are employees of city-based companies who work from home three days a week and prefer not to be isolated. A monthly pass costs less than a tank of gas, and the town offers it at half price to people under thirty.
>
> Local officials report an effect they had not anticipated. The café on the town square, which had been at risk of closing, has won back customers at lunchtime, and a stationery store has extended its hours. A few problems remain, however. The workstations are almost always occupied on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, while on Fridays the room is often half empty. The town has not yet decided whether to add workstations or spread attendance more evenly through a booking system.

## Exercise 1

According to the passage, who used the coworking space in the early days after it opened?

- A) Employees of city-based companies
- B) Mainly freelancers
- C) Young people under thirty
- D) University students

| option | wording in the passage | assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | “Today half the users are employees” | refers to the present |
| B | “At first, most users were freelancers” | explicit |
| C | “at half price to people under thirty” | refers to the discount |
| D | none | not stated |

<div class="spiegazione">

The question asks for **explicit information**, so look for the sentence about the early days. The second paragraph says that at first most users of the coworking space were freelancers, so option B restates that sentence. Option A appeals to a reader in a hurry because it names today’s users instead of the early users. Option C repeats the statement about the discount, which sets a price without identifying who used the space. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The passage states that:

- A) the town has decided to increase the number of workstations
- B) the pass is free for people under thirty
- C) the coworking space in the town described is in a former school
- D) the café on the town square has won back customers at lunchtime

| option | wording in the passage | assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | “has not yet decided whether to add workstations” | contradicted |
| B | “at half price to people under thirty” | contradicted |
| C | “in former schools or public offices,” referring to towns in general | not stated for this town |
| D | “The café on the town square … has won back customers at lunchtime” | explicit |

<div class="spiegazione">

Option D restates the third paragraph’s sentence about the café, so it is explicit. Option A contradicts the last sentence because the town has not yet decided whether to add workstations. Option B turns a fifty percent discount into a free pass. Option C is the trickiest distractor: the first paragraph mentions former schools, but it is discussing small towns in general. The passage does not identify the building used in this particular town, so option C has no **line to support it**. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

According to the passage, which statement about the employees who now use the coworking space is correct?

- A) They work from home three days a week and prefer not to be isolated
- B) They make up the majority of coworking space users
- C) They use the coworking space mainly on Fridays
- D) Their companies provide their passes

| option | wording in the passage | assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | “work from home three days a week and prefer not to be isolated” | explicit |
| B | “half the users” | half is not a majority |
| C | “on Fridays the room is often half empty,” without saying who uses it | not stated |
| D | none | not stated |

<div class="spiegazione">

The second paragraph says that the employees work from home three days a week and prefer not to be isolated, so option A gives explicit information. Option B is **exaggerated**: “half” of the users is not a majority, which requires more than half. Option C is wrong because the passage says only that the room is often half empty on Fridays and does not associate that day with any group of users. Option D is plausible, but the passage does not say who pays for the pass. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
