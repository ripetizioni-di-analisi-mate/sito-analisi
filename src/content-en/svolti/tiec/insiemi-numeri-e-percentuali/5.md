## Exercise 1

Three companies hold shares of $\dfrac{3}{8}$, $\dfrac{2}{5}$, and $\dfrac{7}{20}$ of a market. Which list orders the shares from smallest to largest?

- A) $\dfrac{3}{8} < \dfrac{2}{5} < \dfrac{7}{20}$
- B) $\dfrac{7}{20} < \dfrac{2}{5} < \dfrac{3}{8}$
- C) $\dfrac{7}{20} < \dfrac{3}{8} < \dfrac{2}{5}$
- D) $\dfrac{2}{5} < \dfrac{3}{8} < \dfrac{7}{20}$

$$
\frac{3}{8} = \frac{15}{40},
\qquad
\frac{2}{5} = \frac{16}{40},
\qquad
\frac{7}{20} = \frac{14}{40}
$$
$$
\frac{14}{40} < \frac{15}{40} < \frac{16}{40}
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="13.4,0 16.8,0;16.6,0.12 16.8,0 16.6,-0.12;14,-0.08 14,0.08;15,-0.08 15,0.08;16,-0.08 16,0.08" punti="14,0,pieno;15,0,pieno;16,0,pieno" etichette="13.8,-0.3,0.35;14.75,-0.3,0.375;15.85,-0.3,0.4" xmin=13.2 xmax=17 ymin=-0.5 ymax=0.3 titolo="The three shares on the real number line are $\dfrac{7}{20} = 0.35$, $\dfrac{3}{8} = 0.375$, and $\dfrac{2}{5} = 0.4$."}

<div class="spiegazione">

To **compare the fractions**, write them with the same denominator. The least common multiple of $8$, $5$, and $20$ is $40$. With this denominator, their numerators are $15$, $16$, and $14$. Of two fractions with the same positive denominator, the one with the smaller numerator is smaller, so the order is $\dfrac{7}{20} < \dfrac{3}{8} < \dfrac{2}{5}$. Their decimal forms $0.35$, $0.375$, and $0.4$ give the same order. Choice B places $\dfrac{3}{8}$ after $\dfrac{2}{5}$ because it compares $0.375$ and $0.4$ as if they were the integers $375$ and $4$. Choice D gives the shares in descending order. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Given $a = 2\sqrt{3}$, $b = 3\sqrt{2}$, and $c = \sqrt{17}$, which of the following statements is true?

- A) $a < c < b$
- B) $a < b < c$
- C) $c < a < b$
- D) $b < a < c$

$$
a = \sqrt{4 \cdot 3} = \sqrt{12},
\qquad
b = \sqrt{9 \cdot 2} = \sqrt{18},
\qquad
c = \sqrt{17}
$$
$$
\sqrt{12} < \sqrt{17} < \sqrt{18}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To **compare the roots**, move each outside factor under the radical by squaring it. This gives $2\sqrt{3} = \sqrt{12}$ and $3\sqrt{2} = \sqrt{18}$. Square roots of positive numbers preserve the order of their radicands, and $12 < 17 < 18$, so $a < c < b$. Choice D orders the numbers by their radicands $2$, $3$, and $17$, ignoring the outside factors. Choice B places $\sqrt{17}$ after $3\sqrt{2}$ because $17$ is the largest radicand. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Which of the following statements is true?

- A) $2^{30} < 10^{9} < 3^{20}$
- B) $3^{20} < 2^{30} < 10^{9}$
- C) $2^{30} < 3^{20} < 10^{9}$
- D) $10^{9} < 2^{30} < 3^{20}$

$$
2^{30} = \left(2^{10}\right)^3 = 1024^3 > 1000^3 = 10^{9}
$$
$$
2^{30} = \left(2^3\right)^{10} = 8^{10} < 9^{10} = \left(3^2\right)^{10} = 3^{20}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To **compare the powers**, write them with the same exponent. Since $2^{10} = 1024$ is greater than $1000 = 10^3$, cubing gives $2^{30} > 10^9$. Also, $2^{30} = 8^{10}$ and $3^{20} = 9^{10}$. With the same exponent, the power with the larger base is larger, so $2^{30} < 3^{20}$. The complete order is therefore $10^9 < 2^{30} < 3^{20}$. Choices A and C put $10^9$ after $2^{30}$ because $10$ is the largest base, without taking the exponents into account. Choice B reverses the order of $2^{30}$ and $3^{20}$ because the exponent $30$ is larger than $20$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
