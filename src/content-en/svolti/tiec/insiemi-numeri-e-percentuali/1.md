## Exercise 1

A company uses $A$ for the set of months in which it exceeded its sales target and $B$ for the set of months in which costs stayed below budget:
$$
A = \{\text{Jan}, \text{Feb}, \text{Mar}, \text{Jun}, \text{Sep}\},
\qquad
B = \{\text{Feb}, \text{Apr}, \text{Jun}, \text{Sep}, \text{Dec}\} .
$$
Which set contains the months in which the company exceeded its sales target *and* kept costs below budget?

- A) $\{\text{Feb}, \text{Jun}, \text{Sep}\}$
- B) $\{\text{Jan}, \text{Feb}, \text{Mar}, \text{Apr}, \text{Jun}, \text{Sep}, \text{Dec}\}$
- C) $\{\text{Jan}, \text{Mar}\}$
- D) $\{\text{Apr}, \text{Dec}\}$

$$
A \cap B = \{\text{Feb}, \text{Jun}, \text{Sep}\}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The question asks which months belong to both sets, so we need the **intersection** $A \cap B$. Looking through $A$, the months that also appear in $B$ are February, June, and September. Choice B is the union $A \cup B$, which contains the months in which at least one condition holds. Choice C is the set difference $A \setminus B$, and choice D is $B \setminus A$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Let $A = \{x \in \mathbb{N} : x \text{ is a divisor of } 12\}$ and $B = \{x \in \mathbb{N} : x \text{ is even and } 1 \leq x \leq 10\}$. Which of the following is the set $A \setminus B$?

- A) $\{8, 10\}$
- B) $\{2, 4, 6\}$
- C) $\{1, 3, 12\}$
- D) $\{1, 3, 8, 10, 12\}$

$$
A = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12\},
\qquad
B = \{2, 4, 6, 8, 10\}
$$
$$
A \setminus B = \{1, 3, 12\}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

First list the elements of the two sets. The divisors of $12$ are $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, $6$, and $12$. The even numbers from $1$ to $10$ are $2$, $4$, $6$, $8$, and $10$. The **set difference** $A \setminus B$ contains the elements of $A$ that do not belong to $B$, so remove $2$, $4$, and $6$ from $A$. This leaves $1$, $3$, and $12$. The number $12$ remains because, although even, it is greater than $10$ and therefore does not belong to $B$. Choice A is $B \setminus A$, choice B is the intersection, and choice D is the symmetric difference. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

In the universal set $U = \{1, 2, 3, \dots, 10\}$, let $A$ be the set of multiples of $2$ and $B$ the set of multiples of $3$. What is the complement of $A \cup B$?

- A) $\{6\}$
- B) $\{1, 5, 7\}$
- C) $\{1, 3, 5, 7, 9\}$
- D) $\{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10\}$

$$
A = \{2, 4, 6, 8, 10\},
\qquad
B = \{3, 6, 9\}
$$
$$
A \cup B = \{2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10\},
\qquad
\overline{A \cup B} = \{1, 5, 7\}
$$

::venn{zone="u" nomi="A,B" universo=1 elementi="a:2,4,8,10;ab:6;b:3,9;u:1,5,7" titolo="In the universal set $U = \{1, 2, \dots, 10\}$, the shaded region outside both $A$ and $B$ is the complement of $A \cup B$."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The union $A \cup B$ contains the numbers that are multiples of $2$ or of $3$. The **complement** of $A \cup B$ contains the numbers in $U$ that are multiples of neither $2$ nor $3$, namely $1$, $5$, and $7$. The rule $\overline{A \cup B} = \overline{A} \cap \overline{B}$ gives the same result because the odd numbers in $U$ are $1$, $3$, $5$, $7$, and $9$, and those that are not multiples of $3$ are $1$, $5$, and $7$. Choice C is the complement of $A$ alone, and choice D is the complement of $A \cap B = \{6\}$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
