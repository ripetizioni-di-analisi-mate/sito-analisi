## Exercise 1

What is $15\%$ of $240$?

- A) $24$
- B) $36$
- C) $16$
- D) $3.6$

$$
\frac{15}{100} \cdot 240 = \frac{3600}{100} = 36
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the definition of a **percentage**, $15\%$ of $240$ is found by multiplying $240$ by $\dfrac{15}{100}$. To calculate mentally, $10\%$ of $240$ is $24$, and $5\%$ is half that amount, or $12$, so $15\%$ is $24 + 12 = 36$. Choice A gives only $10\%$, choice C gives the quotient $\dfrac{240}{15}$, and choice D is one-tenth of the result. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A jacket costs $160$ euros and is discounted by $35\%$. What is the sale price?

- A) $56$ euros
- B) $125$ euros
- C) $104$ euros
- D) $96$ euros

$$
160 \cdot \left( 1 - \frac{35}{100} \right) = 160 \cdot 0.65 = 104
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

A $35\%$ discount leaves $65\%$ of the price to be paid, so apply the **multiplier** $0.65$ to the price. Alternatively, the discount is $0.35 \cdot 160 = 56$ euros, so the sale price is $160 - 56 = 104$ euros. Choice A gives the amount of the discount instead of the sale price. Choice B subtracts $35$ euros instead of $35\%$, and choice D corresponds to a $40\%$ discount. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A company's annual revenue rises from $80\,000$ euros to $92\,000$ euros. What is the percent increase in revenue?

- A) $12\%$
- B) $13\%$
- C) $115\%$
- D) $15\%$

$$
\frac{92\,000 - 80\,000}{80\,000} \cdot 100 = \frac{12\,000}{80\,000} \cdot 100 = 15\%
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To find the **percent change**, divide the increase by the *initial* value. The increase is $12\,000$ euros on an initial $80\,000$, and $\dfrac{12\,000}{80\,000} = \dfrac{15}{100}$, so revenue increased by $15\%$. Choice B divides by the final value, since $\dfrac{12\,000}{92\,000}$ is approximately $0.13$. Choice C is the ratio of final to initial revenue, $\dfrac{92\,000}{80\,000} = 1.15$, expressed as a percentage. Choice A confuses the increase in thousands of euros with the percent increase. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
