## Exercise 1

The price of a product increases by $25\%$ and then decreases by $20\%$. How does the final price compare with the initial price?

- A) It has increased by $5\%$.
- B) It has decreased by $5\%$.
- C) It has decreased by $4\%$.
- D) It is unchanged.

$$
1.25 \cdot 0.8 = 1
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,4 100,4 100,5.4 0,5.4;0,2 125,2 125,3.4 0,3.4;0,0 100,0 100,1.4 0,1.4" etichette="30,4.5,100 euros (initial price);35,2.5,125 euros (after +25%);30,0.5,100 euros (after −20%)" xmin=-5 xmax=135 ymin=-1 ymax=6.5 titolo="A price of $100$ euros rises by $25\%$ and then falls by $20\%$, giving $100 \cdot 1.25 \cdot 0.8 = 100$ euros."}

<div class="spiegazione">

For **successive percent changes**, multiply the two multipliers, $1.25$ for the increase and $0.8$ for the decrease. The product is $1$, so the final price equals the initial price. A price of $100$ euros rises to $125$ euros and then falls to $125 \cdot 0.8 = 100$ euros. Indeed, $20\%$ of $125$ is $25$, the same amount as the increase. Choice A adds the two signed percentages. Choice C results from an increase and a decrease both equal to $20\%$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

After a $12\%$ increase, a subscription costs $56$ euros. What was its price before the increase?

- A) $49.28$ euros
- B) $44$ euros
- C) $50$ euros
- D) $62.72$ euros

$$
V \cdot 1.12 = 56
\iff
V = \frac{56}{1.12} = \frac{5600}{112} = 50
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The new price is the initial price $V$ times the multiplier $1.12$. Therefore, to **find the initial value**, divide $56$ by $1.12$ to obtain $50$ euros. As a check, $12\%$ of $50$ is $6$, and $50 + 6 = 56$. Choice A subtracts $12\%$ from $56$, calculating the percentage on the final price instead of the initial price. Choice B subtracts $12$ euros, and choice D applies a second increase of $12\%$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The interest rate on a mortgage changes from $2.5\%$ to $3\%$. Which statement is true?

- A) The rate has risen by $0.5$ percentage points, an increase of $20\%$.
- B) The rate has increased by $0.5\%$.
- C) The rate has risen by $20$ percentage points.
- D) The rate has risen by $0.5$ percentage points, an increase of about $16.7\%$.

$$
3 - 2.5 = 0.5 \ \text{percentage points}
$$
$$
\frac{3 - 2.5}{2.5} \cdot 100 = \frac{0.5}{2.5} \cdot 100 = 20\%
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The difference between the rates, $3 - 2.5 = 0.5$, is measured in **percentage points**. To obtain the percent change in the rate, divide by the initial value, giving $\dfrac{0.5}{2.5} \cdot 100 = 20\%$. Choice B confuses percentage points with percent change, and choice C confuses them the other way around. Choice D divides by the final value, since $\dfrac{0.5}{3} \approx 0.167$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
