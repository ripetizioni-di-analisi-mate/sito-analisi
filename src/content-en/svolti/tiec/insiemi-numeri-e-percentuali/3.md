## Exercise 1

In a class of $30$ students, $18$ study English, $15$ study French, and $7$ study both languages. How many students study neither language?

- A) $4$
- B) $7$
- C) $11$
- D) $8$

$$
|I \cup F| = 18 + 15 - 7 = 26
$$
$$
30 - 26 = 4
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $I$ and $F$ denote the sets of students who study English and French, respectively. By the formula for the **number of elements in a union**, $18 + 15 - 7 = 26$ students study at least one of the languages. Subtract the $7$ students who study both languages once because the sum $18 + 15$ counts them twice. Therefore $30 - 26 = 4$ students study neither language. Choice C is the number who study English only, $18 - 7 = 11$, and choice D is the number who study French only, $15 - 7 = 8$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Of $200$ supermarket customers, $120$ bought product $P$, $90$ bought product $Q$, and $30$ bought neither. How many customers bought both products?

- A) $10$
- B) $40$
- C) $70$
- D) $50$

$$
|P \cup Q| = 200 - 30 = 170
$$
$$
|P \cap Q| = |P| + |Q| - |P \cup Q| = 120 + 90 - 170 = 40
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $P$ and $Q$ be the sets of customers who bought each of the two products. The number who bought at least one product is $200 - 30 = 170$. The formula $|P \cup Q| = |P| + |Q| - |P \cap Q|$ gives $|P \cap Q| = 120 + 90 - 170$, so $40$ customers bought both products. As a check, $80$ customers bought only $P$, $50$ bought only $Q$, and $80 + 40 + 50 + 30 = 200$. Choice A, $120 + 90 - 200 = 10$, overlooks the $30$ customers who bought neither product. Choice D is the number of customers who bought only $Q$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The finite sets $A$ and $B$ satisfy $|A| = 25$, $|B| = 18$, and $|A \cup B| = 35$. How many elements does $A \setminus B$ contain?

- A) $7$
- B) $10$
- C) $17$
- D) $8$

$$
|A \cap B| = 25 + 18 - 35 = 8
$$
$$
|A \setminus B| = |A| - |A \cap B| = 25 - 8 = 17
$$

::venn{zone="a" nomi="A,B" elementi="a:17;ab:8;b:10" titolo="With $|A| = 25$, $|B| = 18$, and $|A \cup B| = 35$, the intersection contains $8$ elements. The shaded difference $A \setminus B$ contains $25 - 8 = 17$ elements."}

<div class="spiegazione">

First find the size of the intersection using the formula for the **number of elements in a union**, which gives $|A \cap B| = |A| + |B| - |A \cup B| = 8$. The difference $A \setminus B$ contains the elements of $A$ outside the intersection, so it has $25 - 8 = 17$ elements. As a check, the region $B \setminus A$ contains $18 - 8 = 10$ elements, and the three regions together give $17 + 8 + 10 = 35$, which is $|A \cup B|$. Choice A is the difference $|A| - |B|$, which equals $|A \setminus B|$ only when $B$ is contained in $A$. Choice B is $|B \setminus A|$, and choice D is $|A \cap B|$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
