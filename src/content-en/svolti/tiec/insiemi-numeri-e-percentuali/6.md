## Exercise 1

Let $a$, $b$, and $c$ be real numbers with $a < b$ and $c < 0$. Which of the following inequalities is always true?

- A) $a c > b c$
- B) $a c < b c$
- C) $a + c > b + c$
- D) $\dfrac{a}{c} < \dfrac{b}{c}$

$$
a < b, \quad c < 0
\implies
a c > b c
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the **properties of inequalities**, multiplying both sides of $a < b$ by a negative number reverses the direction of the inequality, so $a c > b c$. Indeed, $a = 1$, $b = 2$, and $c = -1$ give $-1 > -2$. Choice B fails to reverse the inequality. Choice C is false because adding the same number to both sides preserves the direction and gives $a + c < b + c$. Choice D is false for the same reason as choice B because dividing by $c$ is equivalent to multiplying by the negative number $\dfrac{1}{c}$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A company considers a department's monthly cost $c$, in euros, acceptable if it differs from the budget of $12\,000$ euros by at most $600$ euros, that is, if $|c - 12\,000| \leq 600$. Which condition describes the acceptable costs?

- A) $c \leq 12\,600$
- B) $11\,400 \leq c \leq 12\,600$
- C) $11\,400 < c < 12\,600$
- D) $c \leq 11\,400 \;\vee\; c \geq 12\,600$

$$
|c - 12\,000| \leq 600
\iff
12\,000 - 600 \leq c \leq 12\,000 + 600
$$
$$
11\,400 \leq c \leq 12\,600
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **absolute value** $|c - 12\,000|$ is the distance between the cost $c$ and the budget. A distance of at most $600$ euros means that $c$ lies between $12\,000 - 600$ and $12\,000 + 600$, inclusive, because the inequality is $\leq$. Choice A considers only amounts above budget. Choice C excludes the endpoints even though they satisfy $|c - 12\,000| = 600 \leq 600$, and choice D describes costs *outside* the tolerance. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Which values satisfy the inequality $|x - 3| \geq 2$?

- A) $1 \leq x \leq 5$
- B) $x \geq 5$
- C) $x \leq -5 \;\vee\; x \geq -1$
- D) $x \leq 1 \;\vee\; x \geq 5$

$$
|x - 3| \geq 2
\iff
x - 3 \leq -2 \;\vee\; x - 3 \geq 2
$$
$$
x \leq 1 \;\vee\; x \geq 5
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **absolute value** $|x - 3|$ is the distance between $x$ and $3$ on the real number line. The numbers at least $2$ units from $3$ lie to the left of $3 - 2 = 1$ or to the right of $3 + 2 = 5$, with both endpoints included, so the solution is $x \leq 1 \;\vee\; x \geq 5$. As a check, $x = 0$ gives $|0 - 3| = 3 \geq 2$ and belongs to the solution set, whereas $x = 3$ gives $0$ and does not. Choice A solves the opposite inequality $|x - 3| \leq 2$, choice B leaves out the region on the left, and choice C uses the wrong sign and therefore measures distance from $-3$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
