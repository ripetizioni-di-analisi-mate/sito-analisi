## Exercise 1

Which fraction represents $0.\overline{36}$?

- A) $\dfrac{9}{25}$
- B) $\dfrac{4}{11}$
- C) $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- D) $\dfrac{11}{4}$

$$
0.\overline{36} = \frac{36}{99} = \frac{4 \cdot 9}{11 \cdot 9} = \frac{4}{11}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The decimal has no nonrepeating part, and its repeating block $36$ has two digits. Therefore the rule for its **fractional form** gives a denominator of $99$ and a numerator of $36$. Dividing both by $9$ gives $\dfrac{4}{11}$. As a check, $4 : 11 = 0.3636\ldots$. Choice A is the terminating decimal $0.36 = \dfrac{36}{100}$, obtained by cutting off the repeating digits. Choice C is $\dfrac{36}{90}$ and uses the wrong denominator. Choice D is the reciprocal of the result. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Which of the following equals $1.2\overline{3}$?

- A) $\dfrac{41}{33}$
- B) $\dfrac{37}{33}$
- C) $\dfrac{41}{30}$
- D) $\dfrac{37}{30}$

$$
1.2\overline{3} = \frac{123 - 12}{90} = \frac{111}{90} = \frac{37}{30}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The repeating block $3$ and the nonrepeating part $2$ each have one digit, so the rule for the **fractional form** gives a denominator of $90$. The numerator is the number written without the decimal point, $123$, minus the number formed by the digits preceding the repeating block, $12$. This gives $\dfrac{111}{90}$, which reduces to $\dfrac{37}{30}$ on dividing by $3$. Choice A treats all the digits after the decimal point as repeating, because $\dfrac{123}{99} = \dfrac{41}{33}$. Choice C leaves out the subtraction in the numerator, because $\dfrac{123}{90} = \dfrac{41}{30}$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A partner is entitled to a share equal to $0.1\overline{6}$ of a profit of $48\,000$ euros. How much does the partner receive?

- A) $7680$ euros
- B) $8160$ euros
- C) $8000$ euros
- D) $7920$ euros

$$
0.1\overline{6} = \frac{16 - 1}{90} = \frac{15}{90} = \frac{1}{6}
$$
$$
\frac{1}{6} \cdot 48\,000 = 8000
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The nonrepeating part is $1$ and the repeating block is $6$, so the **fractional form** is $\dfrac{16 - 1}{90} = \dfrac{15}{90}$, which reduces to $\dfrac{1}{6}$. The partner's share is therefore one-sixth of the profit, or $8000$ euros. The other choices replace the repeating decimal with a terminating decimal. Choice A uses $0.16$, choice B uses $0.17$, and choice D uses $0.165$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
