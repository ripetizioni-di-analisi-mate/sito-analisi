## Exercise 1

What is the slope of the line $4x - 2y + 6 = 0$?

- A) $-2$
- B) $4$
- C) $2$
- D) $\dfrac{1}{2}$

$$
4x - 2y + 6 = 0
\iff
2y = 4x + 6
\iff
y = 2x + 3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Solving for $y$ gives **slope-intercept form**, $y = 2x + 3$, so the slope is the coefficient of $x$, namely $2$. The formula $m = -\dfrac{a}{b} = -\dfrac{4}{-2} = 2$ gives the same value. Choice A omits the minus sign in the formula, choice B confuses $m$ with the coefficient $a$, and choice D interchanges $a$ and $b$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is the equation of the line through $A(1;3)$ and $B(3;7)$?

- A) $y = 2x + 1$
- B) $y = 2x + 3$
- C) $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x + \dfrac{5}{2}$
- D) $y = -2x + 5$

$$
m = \frac{7 - 3}{3 - 1} = \frac{4}{2} = 2
$$
$$
y - 3 = 2\,(x - 1)
\iff
y = 2x + 1
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The slope is the difference between the vertical coordinates divided by the difference between the horizontal coordinates, so $m = 2$. To find the **line through two points**, use the slope $2$ and the point $A$ to write $y - 3 = 2(x - 1)$, or $y = 2x + 1$. As a check, at $x = 3$ we obtain $y = 7$, the vertical coordinate of $B$. Rule out choice B because at $x = 1$ it gives $y = 5$, so its line does not pass through $A$. The lines in choices C and D pass through $A$ but miss $B$ because one inverts the ratio and the other uses the wrong sign for $m$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A company’s total cost $C$ is a linear function of the quantity produced, $q$. Producing $100$ units costs $1300$ euros, and producing $180$ units costs $1700$ euros. What are the fixed costs, that is, the cost at $q = 0$?

- A) $500$ euros
- B) $800$ euros
- C) $1300$ euros
- D) $400$ euros

$$
m = \frac{1700 - 1300}{180 - 100} = \frac{400}{80} = 5
$$
$$
C - 1300 = 5\,(q - 100)
\iff
C = 5q + 800
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The cost is linear, so its graph is the **line through the two points** $(100;1300)$ and $(180;1700)$. The slope is $5$ euros per unit, the cost of each additional unit. The vertical intercept is the cost at $q = 0$, so the fixed costs are $800$ euros. Choice A gives the variable cost of the first $100$ units, $5 \cdot 100 = 500$ euros, and choice D gives the increase in cost from $100$ to $180$ units. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
