## Exercise 1

What is the midpoint of the segment with endpoints $A(-3;5)$ and $B(7;-1)$?

- A) $(2;2)$
- B) $(5;-3)$
- C) $(4;4)$
- D) $(-5;3)$

$$
x_M = \frac{-3 + 7}{2} = \frac{4}{2} = 2,
\qquad
y_M = \frac{5 + (-1)}{2} = \frac{4}{2} = 2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The coordinates of the **midpoint** are the averages of the corresponding endpoint coordinates. The average of the horizontal coordinates is $2$, and the average of the vertical coordinates is $2$, so $M = (2;2)$. Choice B comes from dividing the *differences* between the coordinates by $2$ instead of dividing their sums. Choice C adds the coordinates without dividing by $2$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

On a Cartesian coordinate plane with distances measured in kilometers, a warehouse is at $M(2;1)$ and a store is at $N(8;9)$. A courier charges $1.50$ euros per kilometer of straight-line distance. What is the cost of delivery from the warehouse to the store?

- A) $21$ euros
- B) $15$ euros
- C) $150$ euros
- D) $10$ euros

$$
\overline{MN} = \sqrt{(8 - 2)^2 + (9 - 1)^2} = \sqrt{36 + 64} = \sqrt{100} = 10
$$
$$
10 \cdot 1.50 = 15
$$

::grafico{punti="2,1,pieno,M;8,9,pieno,N" linee="2,1 8,9" segmenti="2,1,8,1;8,1,8,9" etichette="5,0.4,6;8.5,5,8" xmin=-1 xmax=10 ymin=-1 ymax=10 uguale=1 nomi="km,km" titolo="The warehouse $M(2;1)$ and the store $N(8;9)$ are $\sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = 10$ km apart"}

<div class="spiegazione">

To find the **distance between two points**, take the coordinate differences $6$ and $8$. The distance formula gives $\sqrt{100} = 10$ km, so the cost is $10 \cdot 1.50 = 15$ euros. Choice A adds the legs, $6 + 8 = 14$ km, giving the length of the route along the two sides parallel to the axes. Choice C omits the square root. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The segment $AB$ has endpoint $A(-3;4)$ and midpoint $M(1;-2)$. What is the other endpoint $B$?

- A) $(-1;1)$
- B) $(4;-6)$
- C) $(-7;10)$
- D) $(5;-8)$

$$
\frac{-3 + x_B}{2} = 1 \iff x_B = 2 + 3 = 5
$$
$$
\frac{4 + y_B}{2} = -2 \iff y_B = -4 - 4 = -8
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The point $M$ is the **midpoint** of $AB$, so its horizontal coordinate is the average of $-3$ and $x_B$, and its vertical coordinate is the average of $4$ and $y_B$. Solving the two equations gives $B = (5;-8)$, or $B = (2x_M - x_A;\, 2y_M - y_A)$. Choice A is the midpoint of $A$ and $M$, obtained by using the formula in the wrong direction. Choice B gives the difference $M - A$. As a check, the average of $-3$ and $5$ is $1$, and the average of $4$ and $-8$ is $-2$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
