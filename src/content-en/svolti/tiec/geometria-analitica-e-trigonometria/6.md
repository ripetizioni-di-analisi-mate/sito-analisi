## Exercise 1

What is the vertex of the parabola $y = x^2 - 4x + 1$?

- A) $(4;1)$
- B) $(-2;-3)$
- C) $(2;-3)$
- D) $(-2;13)$

$$
x_V = -\frac{b}{2a} = -\frac{-4}{2} = 2
$$
$$
y_V = 2^2 - 4 \cdot 2 + 1 = -3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The horizontal coordinate of the **vertex** is $-\dfrac{b}{2a}$, with $a = 1$ and $b = -4$, so it equals $2$. Substituting $x = 2$ into the equation gives the vertical coordinate, $-3$. Choice A uses $-\dfrac{b}{a}$, omitting the $2$ in the denominator. Choices B and D use the wrong sign for the horizontal coordinate. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

At which points does the parabola $y = -x^2 + 2x + 3$ intersect the $x$-axis?

- A) $(1;0)$ and $(-3;0)$
- B) $(-1;0)$ and $(3;0)$
- C) $(0;3)$ and $(0;-1)$
- D) At no point.

$$
-x^2 + 2x + 3 = 0
\iff
x^2 - 2x - 3 = 0
\iff
(x - 3)(x + 1) = 0
$$
$$
x = -1 \;\vee\; x = 3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Points on the $x$-axis have vertical coordinate $0$, so to find the **intercepts of the parabola**, solve $-x^2 + 2x + 3 = 0$. Multiplying by $-1$ gives $x^2 - 2x - 3 = 0$, with solutions $-1$ and $3$. Choice A uses the wrong sign in factoring, and choice C places the solutions on the $y$-axis. Opening downward does not prevent the parabola from crossing the axis because its vertex $(1;4)$ is above the $x$-axis. This rules out choice D. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A company’s weekly profit from selling $q$ units is $P = -2q^2 + 160q - 1200$ euros. What is its maximum profit?

- A) $2000$ euros
- B) $40$ euros
- C) $8400$ euros
- D) $3200$ euros

$$
q_V = -\frac{160}{2 \cdot (-2)} = 40
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
P(40) &= -2 \cdot 1600 + 160 \cdot 40 - 1200 \\[6pt]
&= -3200 + 6400 - 1200 \\[6pt]
&= 2000
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The profit graph is a **parabola** with $a = -2 < 0$, so it opens downward and reaches its maximum at the vertex. The horizontal coordinate of the vertex is $q = 40$ units. Substitution gives a maximum profit of $2000$ euros. Choice B gives the number of units that maximizes profit, choice D omits the $1200$ euros in fixed costs, and choice C calculates $-2 \cdot 1600$ with a plus sign. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
