## Exercise 1

Which center and radius does the circle $x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 4y - 12 = 0$ have?

- A) center $(-3;2)$ and radius $5$
- B) center $(3;-2)$ and radius $5$
- C) center $(3;-2)$ and radius $\sqrt{13}$
- D) center $(6;-4)$ and radius $8$

$$
a = -6, \quad b = 4, \quad c = -12
$$
$$
C\left( -\frac{-6}{2} ; -\frac{4}{2} \right) = (3;-2),
\qquad
r = \sqrt{9 + 4 + 12} = \sqrt{25} = 5
$$

::grafico{curve="3+5*cos(t),-2+5*sin(t),0,6.29" punti="3,-2,pieno,C" segmenti="3,-2,8,-2" etichette="5.5,-1.6,r = 5" legenda="0" xmin=-3 xmax=9 ymin=-8 ymax=4 uguale=1 titolo="The circle $x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 4y - 12 = 0$ has center $(3;-2)$ and radius $5$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

By the rule for the **center and radius**, the coordinates of the center are the negatives of half the coefficients of $x$ and $y$, so the center is $(3;-2)$. Under the square root, add the squares of these coordinates and subtract the constant term. Since $c = -12$, we obtain $9 + 4 + 12 = 25$, so the radius is $5$. Choice A gives the center coordinates with the wrong signs, choice C omits the constant term, and choice D uses the coefficients without dividing by $2$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is the equation of the circle with center $(1;-2)$ that passes through $P(4;2)$?

- A) $x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 4y - 20 = 0$
- B) $x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 4y = 0$
- C) $x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 4y - 25 = 0$
- D) $x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 4y - 20 = 0$

$$
r^2 = (4 - 1)^2 + (2 + 2)^2 = 9 + 16 = 25
$$
$$
(x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 25
\iff
x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 4y - 20 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The radius is the distance from the center to a point on the circle, so $r^2 = 25$. The **equation of the circle** is $(x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 25$. Expanding the squares gives $x^2 - 2x + 1 + y^2 + 4y + 4 = 25$, with constant term $1 + 4 - 25 = -20$. Choice A reverses the signs associated with the center, choice B uses $r = 5$ instead of $r^2 = 25$, and choice C omits the terms $1$ and $4$ in the expansion. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

On a Cartesian coordinate plane with distances measured in kilometers, a store offers free delivery within $3$ km of its warehouse, located at $(2;-1)$. For what value of the parameter does $x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 2y + k = 0$ describe the boundary of the free delivery area?

- A) $k = 4$
- B) $k = 2$
- C) $k = -4$
- D) $k = -9$

$$
r^2 = \frac{(-4)^2}{4} + \frac{2^2}{4} - k = 4 + 1 - k
$$
$$
4 + 1 - k = 9
\iff
k = -4
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The boundary is the circle with center $(2;-1)$ and radius $3$. The center determines the coefficients $-4$ and $2$ in the equation, and the formula for the **center and radius** gives the squared radius as $4 + 1 - k$. Setting this equal to $9$ gives $k = -4$. As a check, $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 9$ expands to $x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 2y - 4 = 0$. Choice B uses the radius $3$ instead of its square, and choice D sets $k = -r^2$ while omitting $4 + 1$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
