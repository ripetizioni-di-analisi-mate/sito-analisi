## Exercise 1

What is the equation of the line perpendicular to $y = 3x - 2$ that passes through the origin?

- A) $y = 3x$
- B) $y = -3x$
- C) $y = \dfrac{1}{3}x$
- D) $y = -\dfrac{1}{3}x$

$$
m_1 = 3
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
m_2 = -\frac{1}{3}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Two lines are **perpendicular** if the product of their slopes is $-1$, so the required slope is $-\dfrac{1}{3}$. The line passes through the origin, so its vertical intercept is $0$ and its equation is $y = -\dfrac{1}{3}x$. Choice A gives the parallel line, choice B changes only the sign, and choice C takes the reciprocal without changing the sign. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is the equation of the line parallel to $2x + y - 5 = 0$ that passes through $P(1;4)$?

- A) $y = 2x + 2$
- B) $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x + \dfrac{7}{2}$
- C) $y = -2x + 6$
- D) $y = -2x + 5$

$$
2x + y - 5 = 0
\iff
y = -2x + 5
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
m = -2
$$
$$
y - 4 = -2\,(x - 1)
\iff
y = -2x + 6
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The given line has slope $-2$, and **parallel** lines have the same slope. The line of slope $-2$ through $P(1;4)$ has equation $y - 4 = -2(x - 1)$, or $y = -2x + 6$. As a check, at $x = 1$ we obtain $y = 4$. Choice D gives the original line, which does not pass through $P$. Choice A uses the wrong sign for $m$, and choice B gives the perpendicular line through $P$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A company sells a product for $12$ euros per unit. The cost of producing $q$ units is $8q + 600$ euros. On a graph with $q$ on the horizontal axis and euros on the vertical axis, at what point do the revenue and cost lines intersect?

- A) $(150;1800)$
- B) $(30;360)$
- C) $(50;600)$
- D) $(150;600)$

$$
\begin{cases}
y = 12q \\
y = 8q + 600
\end{cases}
\iff
\begin{cases}
12q = 8q + 600 \\
y = 12q
\end{cases}
\iff
\begin{cases}
q = 150 \\
y = 1800
\end{cases}
$$

::grafico{f="12*x+0*sqrt(x);8*x+600+0*sqrt(x)" punti="150,1800,pieno" segmenti="150,0,150,1800" nomi="q,€" legenda="R = 12q;C = 8q + 600" xmin=-25 xmax=225 ymin=-250 ymax=2250 titolo="Revenue and cost are equal at the break-even point $(150;1800)$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

To find the **intersection of two lines**, solve the system formed by their equations. Setting revenue equal to cost gives $4q = 600$, so the quantity is $q = 150$ units and the corresponding revenue is $12 \cdot 150 = 1800$ euros. As a check, the cost is $8 \cdot 150 + 600 = 1800$ euros. Choice B adds $12$ and $8$ instead of subtracting, and choice D gives the correct horizontal coordinate but uses only the fixed costs for the vertical coordinate. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
