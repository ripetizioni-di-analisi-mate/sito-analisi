## Exercise 1

What are the solutions of $5x^2 - 20x = 0$?

- A) $x = 4$
- B) $x = 0$ and $x = -4$
- C) $x = -2$ and $x = 2$
- D) $x = 0$ and $x = 4$

$$
\begin{aligned}
5x(x - 4) &= 0 \\[6pt]
x = 0 \quad &\vee \quad x - 4 = 0 \\[6pt]
x = 0 \quad &\vee \quad x = 4
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

There is **no constant term**, so factor out $5x$. By the **zero-product property**, the product $5x(x - 4)$ is zero when $x = 0$ or $x - 4 = 0$, which gives $x = 4$. Choice A divides both sides by $x$, losing the solution $x = 0$. Choice C treats the equation as if it had no linear term and solves $5x^2 = 20$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What are the solutions of $2x^2 - 5x - 3 = 0$?

- A) $x = -3$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$
- B) $x = 3$ and $x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$
- C) $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$ and $x = 1$
- D) $x = 6$ and $x = -1$

$$
\Delta = (-5)^2 - 4 \cdot 2 \cdot (-3) = 25 + 24 = 49
$$
$$
x_{1,2} = \frac{5 \pm 7}{4}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
x_1 = -\frac{1}{2}, \quad x_2 = 3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

With $a = 2$, $b = -5$, and $c = -3$, calculating the discriminant gives $\Delta = 25 + 24 = 49$ because $-4ac = -4 \cdot 2 \cdot (-3) = +24$. Since $\Delta > 0$, there are two roots and the **quadratic formula** gives $x = \dfrac{5 \pm 7}{4}$, or $3$ and $-\dfrac{1}{2}$. Their sum and product confirm the result because $3 - \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{5}{2} = -\dfrac{b}{a}$ and $3 \cdot \left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right) = -\dfrac{3}{2} = \dfrac{c}{a}$. Choice A reverses the signs by using $-b = -5$. Choice C comes from $\Delta = 25 - 24 = 1$, with the wrong sign for $c$. Choice D divides by $2$ instead of $2a = 4$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A principal of $2000$ euros is invested at an annual compound interest rate $r$, so the principal is multiplied by $1 + r$ each year. After two years the investment is worth $2420$ euros. What is the annual rate $r$?

- A) $21\%$
- B) $10.5\%$
- C) $10\%$
- D) $10\%$ or $-210\%$

$$
\begin{aligned}
2000\,(1 + r)^2 &= 2420 \\[6pt]
(1 + r)^2 &= 1.21 \\[6pt]
1 + r = 1.1 \quad &\vee \quad 1 + r = -1.1 \\[6pt]
r = 0.1 \quad &\vee \quad r = -2.1
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

After two years the principal has been multiplied twice by $1 + r$, giving $2000\,(1 + r)^2$. Dividing by $2000$ gives $(1 + r)^2 = 1.21$, a **quadratic with no linear term** in the unknown $1 + r$, with solutions $1 + r = \pm 1.1$. The root $r = -2.1$ would multiply the principal by $-1.1$ each year, giving a negative balance after the first year. That makes no sense in this problem, so discard it. The rate is therefore $r = 0.1 = 10\%$. Choice A gives the total growth over two years, $\dfrac{420}{2000} = 21\%$. Choice B divides $21\%$ by two as if the interest were simple. Choice D keeps the root that makes no sense in context. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
