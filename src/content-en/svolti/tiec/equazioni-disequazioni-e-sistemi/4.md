## Exercise 1

The equation
$$
\frac{3}{x} = \frac{5}{x + 2}
$$
has which solution?

- A) $x = -3$
- B) $x = 1$
- C) $x = 3$
- D) no real number

$$
\text{Domain restrictions: }
\begin{cases}
x \neq 0 \\
x \neq -2
\end{cases}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
3(x + 2) &= 5x \\[6pt]
3x + 6 &= 5x \\[6pt]
6 &= 2x \\[6pt]
x &= 3
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The denominators $x$ and $x + 2$ must be nonzero, so the **domain restrictions** are $x \neq 0$ and $x \neq -2$. Multiplying both sides by $x(x + 2)$ gives $3(x + 2) = 5x$, or $6 = 2x$, so $x = 3$. The value $3$ satisfies the restrictions and is the solution. Indeed, $\dfrac{3}{3} = 1$ and $\dfrac{5}{5} = 1$. Choice B comes from the expansion $3(x + 2) = 3x + 2$. Choice A moves $6$ across the equals sign without changing its sign, giving $3x - 5x = 6$ and therefore $x = -3$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The total cost of producing $q$ items, with $q > 0$, is $C = 1500 + 12q$ euros. The average cost per item is $\dfrac{C}{q}$. An average cost of $15$ euros occurs at which value of $q$?

- A) $500$
- B) $100$
- C) $125$
- D) about $56$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1500 + 12q}{q} &= 15 \\[6pt]
1500 + 12q &= 15q \\[6pt]
1500 &= 3q \\[6pt]
q &= 500
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

This is a rational equation with the unknown $q$ in the denominator. The domain restriction $q \neq 0$ is already satisfied because the problem states that $q > 0$. Multiplying both sides by $q$ gives $1500 + 12q = 15q$, so $3q = 1500$ and therefore $q = 500$ items. To check, note that $\dfrac{1500 + 6000}{500} = \dfrac{7500}{500} = 15$. Choice B divides only the fixed costs by $15$ and ignores the cost of $12$ euros per item. Choice C divides the fixed costs by $12$. Choice D comes from $\dfrac{1500}{q} - 12 = 15$, with the wrong sign. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The equation
$$
\frac{x}{x - 2} - \frac{4}{x + 2} = \frac{8}{x^2 - 4}
$$
has which solutions?

- A) $x = 0$ and $x = 2$
- B) only $x = 2$
- C) no real number
- D) only $x = 0$

$$
x^2 - 4 = (x - 2)(x + 2),
\qquad
\text{Domain restrictions: }
\begin{cases}
x \neq 2 \\
x \neq -2
\end{cases}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
x(x + 2) - 4(x - 2) &= 8 \\[6pt]
x^2 + 2x - 4x + 8 - 8 &= 0 \\[6pt]
x^2 - 2x &= 0 \\[6pt]
x(x - 2) &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
x = 0 \quad \vee \quad x = 2 \ \text{(outside the domain)}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The denominator $x^2 - 4$ factors as $(x - 2)(x + 2)$, which is also the common denominator, so the **domain restrictions** are $x \neq 2$ and $x \neq -2$. Multiplying both sides by $(x - 2)(x + 2)$ gives $x(x + 2) - 4(x - 2) = 8$, or $x^2 - 2x = 0$, a quadratic with no constant term and roots $0$ and $2$. The value $x = 2$ makes the denominators zero and is discarded, so the original equation has the **unique solution** $x = 0$. Choice A overlooks the restrictions. Choice C also discards $x = 0$, even though it satisfies them. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
