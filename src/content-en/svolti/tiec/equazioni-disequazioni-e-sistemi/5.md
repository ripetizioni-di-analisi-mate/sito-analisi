## Exercise 1

Which values satisfy $2 - 3x \geq 8$?

- A) $x \geq -2$
- B) $x \leq -2$
- C) $x \leq 2$
- D) $x < -2$

$$
\begin{aligned}
-3x &\geq 8 - 2 \\[6pt]
-3x &\geq 6 \\[6pt]
x &\leq -2
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Moving $2$ to the right gives $-3x \geq 6$. Now divide both sides by $-3$, which is negative. By the **properties of inequalities**, the direction reverses, giving $x \leq -2$. As a check, $x = -3$ gives $2 + 9 = 11 \geq 8$, whereas $x = 0$ gives $2 \geq 8$, which is false. Choice A keeps the direction of the inequality. Choice D excludes $-2$, which is a solution because $2 + 6 = 8$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A car rental agency offers two plans. Plan A charges a flat fee of $40$ euros plus $0.25$ euros per kilometer. Plan B charges $0.45$ euros per kilometer with no flat fee. If $k$ is the number of kilometers driven, when does plan A cost strictly less than plan B?

- A) $k < 200$
- B) $k > 160$
- C) $k > 200$
- D) $k \geq 200$

$$
\begin{aligned}
40 + 0.25\,k &< 0.45\,k \\[6pt]
40 &< 0.20\,k \\[6pt]
k &> 200
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For $k$ kilometers, plan A costs $40 + 0.25\,k$ euros and plan B costs $0.45\,k$ euros. We want the first cost to be less than the second. Moving $0.25\,k$ to the other side gives $40 < 0.20\,k$. Dividing by $0.20$, which is positive, preserves the direction of the inequality, so $k > 200$. At $k = 200$ both plans cost $90$ euros. Choice D includes $200$ and therefore fails the requirement “strictly less.” Choice B divides the flat fee only by the per-kilometer charge for plan A. Choice A reverses the inequality. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The system of inequalities
$$
\begin{cases}
3x - 6 > 0 \\
x^2 - 16 \leq 0
\end{cases}
$$
has which solution set?

- A) $(2, 4]$
- B) $(2, 4)$
- C) $[4, +\infty)$
- D) $[-4, +\infty)$

$$
3x - 6 > 0 \iff x > 2
$$
$$
x^2 - 16 \leq 0 \iff -4 \leq x \leq 4
$$
$$
(2, +\infty) \cap [-4, 4] = (2, 4]
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first inequality gives $x > 2$. For the second, the corresponding equation $x^2 = 16$ has roots $-4$ and $4$. We need the values where the quadratic is negative or zero. By the **sign of the quadratic**, these are the values between the roots, including the endpoints, or $-4 \leq x \leq 4$. The solutions of the system must satisfy *both* inequalities, so take the intersection $2 < x \leq 4$. Choice B excludes $4$, which satisfies the second inequality because $16 - 16 = 0$. Choice C takes values outside the roots instead of between them. Choice D gives the union of the two sets instead of their intersection. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
