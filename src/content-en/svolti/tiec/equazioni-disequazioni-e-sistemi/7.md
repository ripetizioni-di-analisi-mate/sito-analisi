## Exercise 1

The total cost of producing $q$ items, with $q > 0$, is $C = 800 + 6q$ euros. For which quantities is the average cost per item $\dfrac{C}{q}$ less than $8$ euros?

- A) $q > 400$
- B) $0 < q < 400$
- C) $q > 100$
- D) $q \geq 400$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{800 + 6q}{q} &< 8 \\[6pt]
800 + 6q &< 8q \qquad (q > 0) \\[6pt]
800 &< 2q \\[6pt]
q &> 400
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

This is a rational inequality, but the denominator $q$ is positive by assumption. By the **properties of inequalities**, multiplying both sides by a positive number preserves the direction of the inequality, giving $800 + 6q < 8q$, or $q > 400$. At $q = 400$ the average cost is $\dfrac{800 + 2400}{400} = 8$ euros, which is not less than $8$, so choice D incorrectly includes $400$. Choice C divides only the fixed costs by $8$ and ignores the $6$ euros per item. Choice B reverses the inequality. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Which values satisfy $x(x + 2)(x - 3) < 0$?

- A) $-2 < x < 0 \;\vee\; x > 3$
- B) $x < -3 \;\vee\; 0 < x < 2$
- C) $-2 < x < 3$
- D) $x < -2 \;\vee\; 0 < x < 3$

$$
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
x & (-\infty, -2) & (-2, 0) & (0, 3) & (3, +\infty) \\ \hline
x & - & - & + & + \\
x + 2 & - & + & + & + \\
x - 3 & - & - & - & + \\ \hline
x(x + 2)(x - 3) & - & + & - & +
\end{array}
$$

::grafico{f="x*(x+2)*(x-3)" punti="-2,0,vuoto;0,0,vuoto;3,0,vuoto" xmin=-3.5 xmax=4.5 ymin=-14 ymax=12 legenda="y = x(x + 2)(x - 3)" titolo="The product $x(x + 2)(x - 3)$ is negative for $x < -2$ and $0 < x < 3$, where the graph lies below the $x$-axis"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The zeros of the three factors are $0$, $-2$, and $3$. They divide the number line into four intervals. In the **sign chart**, each factor is positive to the right of its zero and negative to the left. The sign of the product in each column follows from the sign rule for multiplication. The product is negative in the first and third columns. Since the inequality is strict, the solutions are $x < -2 \;\vee\; 0 < x < 3$. Choice A takes the columns where the product is positive. Choice B changes the signs of the zeros, using $2$ and $-3$ instead of $-2$ and $3$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The inequality
$$
\frac{x + 4}{x - 2} \geq 3
$$
holds for which values?

- A) $x \leq 5$
- B) $2 \leq x \leq 5$
- C) $2 < x \leq 5$
- D) $x < 2 \;\vee\; x \geq 5$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{x + 4}{x - 2} - 3 &\geq 0 \\[6pt]
\frac{x + 4 - 3(x - 2)}{x - 2} &\geq 0 \\[6pt]
\frac{10 - 2x}{x - 2} &\geq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
x & (-\infty, 2) & (2, 5) & (5, +\infty) \\ \hline
10 - 2x & + & + & - \\
x - 2 & - & + & + \\ \hline
\dfrac{10 - 2x}{x - 2} & - & + & -
\end{array}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The sign of $x - 2$ varies with $x$, so we cannot simply multiply through by the denominator. Move $3$ to the left and write all terms over the denominator $x - 2$ to obtain $\dfrac{10 - 2x}{x - 2} \geq 0$. The numerator is positive for $x < 5$ and the denominator is positive for $x > 2$. The **sign chart** shows that the quotient is positive on the middle interval. Include the zero of the numerator, $5$, because the inequality uses $\geq$. Always exclude the zero of the denominator, $2$. The solutions are therefore $2 < x \leq 5$. Choice A multiplies by $x - 2$ without checking its sign. Choice B includes $2$, where the fraction is undefined. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
