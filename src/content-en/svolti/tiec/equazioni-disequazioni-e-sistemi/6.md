## Exercise 1

Which values satisfy $x^2 - 2x - 8 \leq 0$?

- A) $x \leq -2 \;\vee\; x \geq 4$
- B) $-4 \leq x \leq 2$
- C) $-2 < x < 4$
- D) $-2 \leq x \leq 4$

$$
x^2 - 2x - 8 = 0
\iff
x = \frac{2 \pm 6}{2}
\iff
x = -2 \;\vee\; x = 4
$$
$$
x^2 - 2x - 8 \leq 0
\iff
-2 \leq x \leq 4
$$

::grafico{f="x^2-2*x-8" area="0;x^2-2*x-8;-2;4" punti="-2,0,pieno;4,0,pieno" xmin=-4 xmax=6 ymin=-10 ymax=10 legenda="y = x^2 - 2x - 8" titolo="The parabola $y = x^2 - 2x - 8$ lies on or below the $x$-axis for $-2 \leq x \leq 4$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The corresponding equation has $\Delta = 4 + 32 = 36$ and roots $-2$ and $4$. The coefficient of $x^2$ is positive, so the parabola opens upward. The **sign of the quadratic** is therefore negative between the roots. The inequality is non-strict, so the endpoints are included and the solutions are $-2 \leq x \leq 4$. Choice A takes values outside the roots. Choice B changes the signs of the roots. Choice C excludes the endpoints, where the quadratic is zero. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Which values satisfy $-x^2 + 6x - 9 \geq 0$?

- A) every real $x$
- B) no real $x$
- C) only $x = 3$
- D) every $x \neq 3$

$$
\begin{aligned}
-x^2 + 6x - 9 &\geq 0 \\[6pt]
x^2 - 6x + 9 &\leq 0 \\[6pt]
(x - 3)^2 &\leq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
(x - 3)^2 \leq 0 \iff x = 3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The coefficient of $x^2$ is negative, so multiply both sides by $-1$ and reverse the inequality to get $x^2 - 6x + 9 \leq 0$. The quadratic is the perfect square $(x - 3)^2$, with $\Delta = 0$. A square is never negative and is zero only for $x = 3$, so the inequality holds **only** for $x = 3$. Choice D solves $(x - 3)^2 > 0$, which reverses the direction and makes the inequality strict. Choice B overlooks the fact that the sign $\leq$ includes the zero of the quadratic. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A company that produces and sells $q$ units of a product earns a profit of $P = -q^2 + 40q - 300$ euros. For which quantities is the profit positive?

- A) $q < 10 \;\vee\; q > 30$
- B) $10 < q < 30$
- C) $q > 10$
- D) $0 < q < 40$

$$
\begin{aligned}
-q^2 + 40q - 300 &> 0 \\[6pt]
q^2 - 40q + 300 &< 0
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\frac{\Delta}{4} = 400 - 300 = 100,
\qquad
q = 20 \pm 10
\iff
q = 10 \;\vee\; q = 30
$$
$$
q^2 - 40q + 300 < 0 \iff 10 < q < 30
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Profit is positive when $-q^2 + 40q - 300 > 0$. Multiplying by $-1$ reverses the inequality, so solve $q^2 - 40q + 300 < 0$. The **quadratic formula for an even linear coefficient** gives $10$ and $30$ as the roots of the corresponding equation. The quadratic is negative between its roots, so profit is positive for $10 < q < 30$. To check, $q = 20$ gives $P = -400 + 800 - 300 = 100 > 0$. Choice A keeps the direction of the inequality after multiplying by $-1$. Choice D solves $-q^2 + 40q > 0$, omitting the term $-300$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
