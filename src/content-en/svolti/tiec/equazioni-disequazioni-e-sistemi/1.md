## Exercise 1

What is the solution of $3(x - 2) - 2(x + 1) = 4 - x$?

- A) $x = 4$
- B) $x = 12$
- C) $x = 6$
- D) $x = -6$

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x - 6 - 2x - 2 &= 4 - x \\[6pt]
x - 8 &= 4 - x \\[6pt]
2x &= 12 \\[6pt]
x &= 6
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Expand the products to get $3(x - 2) = 3x - 6$ and $-2(x + 1) = -2x - 2$, since the minus sign before $2$ changes the signs of both terms in $x + 1$. The left side simplifies to $x - 8$. **Moving terms across the equals sign** takes $-x$ to the left as $+x$ and $-8$ to the right as $+8$, so $2x = 12$ and $x = 6$. To check, substitute $x = 6$. The left side equals $12 - 14 = -2$ and the right side equals $4 - 6 = -2$. Choice A comes from expanding as $-2(x + 1) = -2x + 2$, with the wrong sign on the second term. Choice B omits division by $2$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An online store discounts the list price of a product by $15\%$ and then charges $6$ euros for shipping. A customer pays $74$ euros in total. What was the list price?

- A) $80$ euros
- B) $78.20$ euros
- C) $94.12$ euros
- D) $87.06$ euros

$$
\begin{aligned}
0.85\,x + 6 &= 74 \\[6pt]
0.85\,x &= 68 \\[6pt]
x &= \frac{68}{0.85} = 80
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $x$ be the list price in euros. A discount of $15\%$ leaves $85\%$ of the price, or $0.85\,x$. Shipping adds $6$ euros, so the total is $0.85\,x + 6$. Setting this equal to $74$ and moving $6$ to the other side gives $0.85\,x = 68$, so $x = 80$ euros. Choice B multiplies $68$ by $1.15$, treating the discount as a markup on the amount paid. Choice C adds the shipping charge instead of subtracting it. Choice D ignores shipping and divides $74$ by $0.85$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The equation
$$
\frac{2}{x - 3} + 1 = \frac{x - 1}{x - 3}
$$
holds for which values?

- A) every real $x$
- B) every $x \neq 3$
- C) no value of $x$
- D) $x = 3$

$$
\text{Domain restrictions: } x \neq 3
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
2 + (x - 3) &= x - 1 \\[6pt]
x - 1 &= x - 1 \\[6pt]
0 \cdot x &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The unknown appears in a denominator, so this is a rational equation, and its **domain restriction** is $x - 3 \neq 0$, or $x \neq 3$. Multiplying both sides by $x - 3$ gives $2 + x - 3 = x - 1$, or $0 \cdot x = 0$. The resulting polynomial equation is an identity, so the original equation holds for every number that satisfies the restriction, that is, **every number except** $3$. Choice A overlooks the domain restriction. Choice C confuses an identity with an equation that has no solution. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
