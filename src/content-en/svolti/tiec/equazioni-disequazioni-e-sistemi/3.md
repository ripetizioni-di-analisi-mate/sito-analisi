## Exercise 1

What is the sum of the roots of $2x^2 - 6x - 5 = 0$?

- A) $3$
- B) $-3$
- C) $-\dfrac{5}{2}$
- D) $6$

$$
\Delta = 36 + 40 = 76 > 0
$$
$$
x_1 + x_2 = -\frac{b}{a} = -\frac{-6}{2} = 3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The discriminant is positive, so the equation has two real roots and we can use the formula for the **sum of the roots**. With $a = 2$ and $b = -6$, the sum is $-\dfrac{b}{a} = 3$, without having to find the roots themselves, which involve $\sqrt{76}$. Choice B omits the minus sign in the formula. Choice C gives the product $\dfrac{c}{a}$, and choice D omits division by $a$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

For which value does $x^2 - 6x + k = 0$ have a repeated real root?

- A) $k = 36$
- B) $k = 3$
- C) $k = -9$
- D) $k = 9$

$$
\frac{\Delta}{4} = (-3)^2 - 1 \cdot k = 9 - k
$$
$$
9 - k = 0
\iff
k = 9
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two roots coincide when the **discriminant** is zero. Since $b = -6$ is even, use $\dfrac{\Delta}{4} = \left(\dfrac{b}{2}\right)^2 - ac = 9 - k$, which is zero for $k = 9$. With $k = 9$ the equation becomes $x^2 - 6x + 9 = (x - 3)^2 = 0$, with double root $x = 3$. Choice A comes from $\Delta = 36 - k$, writing the discriminant without the factor $4$. Choice C has the wrong sign. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A company sells $q$ units of a product at $50 - q$ euros each, so its revenue is $R = q(50 - q)$ euros. What are the sum and product of the quantities $q_1$ and $q_2$ that give revenue of $600$ euros?

- A) sum $-50$ and product $600$
- B) sum $50$ and product $600$
- C) sum $50$ and product $-600$
- D) sum $600$ and product $50$

$$
\begin{aligned}
q(50 - q) &= 600 \\[6pt]
50q - q^2 - 600 &= 0 \\[6pt]
q^2 - 50q + 600 &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\Delta = 2500 - 2400 = 100 > 0,
\qquad
q_1 + q_2 = 50,
\qquad
q_1 \cdot q_2 = 600
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Write the equation in standard form. Moving all terms to the left and multiplying by $-1$ gives $q^2 - 50q + 600 = 0$. The discriminant is $100 > 0$, so there are two distinct quantities. By the formulas relating the **sum and product** of the roots to the coefficients, their sum is $-\dfrac{-50}{1} = 50$ and their product is $\dfrac{600}{1} = 600$. Indeed, the quantities are $q_1 = 20$ and $q_2 = 30$, and in both cases the revenue is $20 \cdot 30 = 600$ euros. Choice A omits the minus sign in the sum. Choice C comes from the equation $q^2 - 50q - 600 = 0$, moving $600$ across the equals sign without changing its sign. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
