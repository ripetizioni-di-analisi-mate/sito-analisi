## Exercise 1

The closed loop shown consists of branches $1\!-\!2$ ($400\,\mathrm{m}$, $250\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.022$), $2\!-\!3$ ($300\,\mathrm{m}$, $200\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.023$), $4\!-\!3$ ($400\,\mathrm{m}$, $200\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.023$) and $1\!-\!4$ ($300\,\mathrm{m}$, $250\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.022$). At node $1$ the inflow is $100\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Withdrawals at nodes $2$, $3$ and $4$ are $30$, $40$ and $30\,\mathrm{L/s}$, respectively. Start with $Q_{12} = 60\,\mathrm{L/s}$, $Q_{23} = 30\,\mathrm{L/s}$, $Q_{14} = 40\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and $Q_{43} = 10\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Use the Hardy Cross method with stopping criterion $|\Delta Q| < 0.5\,\mathrm{L/s}$ to find the discharges, then find the nodal piezometric heads assuming $h_1 = 100.00\,\mathrm{m}$.

::schema{nodi="1,4,16;2,20,16;3,20,4;4,4,4" condotte="1-2,0.4,;2-3,0.4,;4-3,0.4,;1-4,0.4," frecce="0.5,16,3.2,16,;20.9,16,23.5,16,;20.9,4,23.5,4,;4,3.1,4,0.6," etichette="12,23,1-2: 400 m - 250 mm;12,21.6,2-3: 300 m - 200 mm;12,20.2,4-3: 400 m - 200 mm;12,18.8,1-4: 300 m - 250 mm;1.8,17.4,100 L/s;25.8,16,30 L/s;25.8,4,40 L/s;4,-0.7,30 L/s" titolo="Closed loop with one inflow and three withdrawals"}

$$
r_{12} = \frac{8 \cdot 0.022 \cdot 400}{9.81 \cdot \pi^2 \cdot 0.250^5} = 744.6\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5} ,
\qquad
r_{23} = \frac{8 \cdot 0.023 \cdot 300}{9.81 \cdot \pi^2 \cdot 0.200^5} = 1782\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}
$$
$$
r_{43} = \frac{8 \cdot 0.023 \cdot 400}{9.81 \cdot \pi^2 \cdot 0.200^5} = 2376\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5} ,
\qquad
r_{14} = \frac{8 \cdot 0.022 \cdot 300}{9.81 \cdot \pi^2 \cdot 0.250^5} = 558.4\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}
$$

First iteration, taking $1 \to 2 \to 3 \to 4 \to 1$ as the positive loop direction.

| Branch | $r$ ($\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$) | $Q$ (L/s) | $r\,Q\,\lvert Q\rvert$ (m) | $r\,\lvert Q\rvert$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $1\!-\!2$ | $744.6$ | $+60.00$ | $+2.681$ | $44.68$ |
| $2\!-\!3$ | $1782$ | $+30.00$ | $+1.603$ | $53.45$ |
| $3\!-\!4$ | $2376$ | $-10.00$ | $-0.238$ | $23.76$ |
| $4\!-\!1$ | $558.4$ | $-40.00$ | $-0.893$ | $22.34$ |
| Total | | | $+3.153$ | $144.23$ |

$$
\Delta Q = -\frac{\sum r\,Q\,\lvert Q\rvert}{2\sum r\,\lvert Q\rvert} = -\frac{3.153}{2 \cdot 144.23}\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = -0.01093\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = -10.93\,\mathrm{L/s}
$$

Second iteration using the corrected discharges.

| Branch | $r$ ($\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$) | $Q$ (L/s) | $r\,Q\,\lvert Q\rvert$ (m) | $r\,\lvert Q\rvert$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $1\!-\!2$ | $744.6$ | $+49.07$ | $+1.793$ | $36.54$ |
| $2\!-\!3$ | $1782$ | $+19.07$ | $+0.648$ | $33.98$ |
| $3\!-\!4$ | $2376$ | $-20.93$ | $-1.041$ | $49.72$ |
| $4\!-\!1$ | $558.4$ | $-50.93$ | $-1.449$ | $28.44$ |
| Total | | | $-0.049$ | $148.68$ |

$$
\Delta Q = \frac{0.049}{2 \cdot 148.68}\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = +0.00016\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = +0.16\,\mathrm{L/s} < 0.5\,\mathrm{L/s}
$$
$$
Q_{12} = 49.2\,\mathrm{L/s} ,
\quad
Q_{23} = 19.2\,\mathrm{L/s} ,
\quad
Q_{43} = 20.8\,\mathrm{L/s} ,
\quad
Q_{14} = 50.8\,\mathrm{L/s}
$$
$$
h_2 = 100.00 - 744.6 \cdot 0.0492^2 = 98.20\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
h_4 = 100.00 - 558.4 \cdot 0.0508^2 = 98.56\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
h_3 = 98.20 - 1782 \cdot 0.0192^2 = 97.54\,\mathrm{m}
$$

Check the head at junction $3$ along the other path.
$$
h_3 = h_4 - 2376 \cdot 0.0208^2 = 98.56 - 1.03 = 97.53\,\mathrm{m}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The network has four branches and four junctions. It has three independent junction equations and one independent loop, since $4 - 4 + 1 = 1$. There are four unknown discharges, so the three junction equations must be supplemented by the loop equation, which is quadratic and is solved iteratively.

The initial distribution satisfies continuity at every junction. At $2$, the inflow is $60\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and the outflow consists of $30\,\mathrm{L/s}$ toward junction $3$ plus the local withdrawal of $30\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The same balance holds at the other junctions. With clockwise taken as positive, the discharges in branches $3\!-\!4$ and $4\!-\!1$ are negative because the actual flows run from $4$ to $3$ and from $1$ to $4$.

At the first iteration, the sum $\sum r\,Q\,|Q|$ is $+3.153\,\mathrm{m}$. The clockwise flow path dissipates more head than the counterclockwise path, so some flow must be shifted from the former to the latter. Adding the correction $\Delta Q = -10.93\,\mathrm{L/s}$ to all four signed discharges preserves the junction balances.

At the second iteration, the sum has fallen to $-0.049\,\mathrm{m}$ and the correction to $0.16\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The correction is below the specified tolerance, so iteration can stop. The final velocities range from $0.61$ to $1.03\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

As a check, calculate the head at junction $3$ along path $1\!-\!4\!-\!3$ rather than $1\!-\!2\!-\!3$. The two results agree to within one hundredth of a meter, confirming that the signed head losses around the loop sum to zero to this accuracy.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Node $A$ receives $150\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The withdrawals are $40\,\mathrm{L/s}$ at $C$, $30\,\mathrm{L/s}$ at $D$, $40\,\mathrm{L/s}$ at $E$ and $40\,\mathrm{L/s}$ at $F$. <div class="spiegazione">

The network has seven branches and six junctions, giving $7 - 6 + 1 = 2$ independent loops. Branch $B\!-\!E$ belongs to both.

The initial distribution satisfies the junction equations. At junction $B$ the inflow is $100\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and the outflows are $70$ toward $C$ and $30$ toward $E$. At junction $E$ the inflow is $20 + 30 = 50\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and the outflows are $10$ toward $F$ and a withdrawal of $40$. The remaining junctions balance in the same way.

Correct the loops one at a time. For the shared branch $B\!-\!E$, loop II uses the discharge just updated in loop I. Its table therefore shows $-23.29\,\mathrm{L/s}$ instead of $-30\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Using updated values immediately speeds convergence. The shared branch receives two corrections in each iteration, one from each loop, with the sign determined by the direction in which that loop is traversed.

After the third iteration both corrections are smaller than $0.5\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and iteration can stop. The final discharges satisfy all junction balances. For example, junction $E$ receives $30.06 + 32.25 = 62.31\,\mathrm{L/s}$, sends $22.31\,\mathrm{L/s}$ toward $F$, and supplies a local withdrawal of $40\,\mathrm{L/s}$.

Calculating the head at junction $E$ along paths $A\!-\!B\!-\!E$ and $A\!-\!D\!-\!E$ gives the same value, confirming that the equation for loop I is satisfied.

</div>
