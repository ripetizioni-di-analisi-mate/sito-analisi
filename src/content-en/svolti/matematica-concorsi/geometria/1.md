## Exercise 1

What is the supplement of the complement of a $28^\circ$ angle?

- A) $62^\circ$
- B) $118^\circ$
- C) $152^\circ$
- D) $28^\circ$
- E) $90^\circ$

$$
90^\circ - 28^\circ = 62^\circ,
\qquad
180^\circ - 62^\circ = 118^\circ
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Work from the given angle outward. The **complement** of $28^\circ$ is $90^\circ - 28^\circ = 62^\circ$, and the **supplement** of $62^\circ$ is $180^\circ - 62^\circ = 118^\circ$. Choice A gives only the complement. Choice C gives the supplement of $28^\circ$, skipping the first step. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A straight road crosses two parallel railroad tracks. Two same-side interior angles formed by the road and the tracks measure $(2x + 20)^\circ$ and $(3x - 40)^\circ$. What is $x$?

- A) $60$
- B) $36$
- C) $32$
- D) $40$
- E) $44$

$$
(2x + 20) + (3x - 40) = 180
\iff
5x - 20 = 180
\iff
x = 40
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The tracks are parallel lines and the road is a transversal. **Same-side interior angles** are supplementary, so their measures add up to $180^\circ$. It follows that $5x = 200$, or $x = 40$. The angles measure $100^\circ$ and $80^\circ$, which add up to $180^\circ$. Choice A sets the two angles equal as if they were alternate interior angles. Choice C comes from a sign error in the equation $5x + 20 = 180$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Each interior angle of a regular polygon measures $140^\circ$. How many sides does the polygon have?

- A) $7$
- B) $8$
- C) $9$
- D) $10$
- E) $12$

$$
180^\circ - 140^\circ = 40^\circ,
\qquad
n = \frac{360^\circ}{40^\circ} = 9
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The shortest calculation uses the **exterior angles**. Each exterior angle is supplementary to its interior angle, so it measures $40^\circ$. The exterior angles of a convex polygon add up to $360^\circ$. Since the polygon is regular, the number of sides is $\dfrac{360}{40} = 9$. As a check, the **sum of the interior angles** of a polygon with $9$ sides is $7 \cdot 180^\circ = 1260^\circ$, and $\dfrac{1260^\circ}{9} = 140^\circ$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
