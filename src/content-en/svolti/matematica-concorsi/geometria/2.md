## Exercise 1

The legs of a right triangle measure $9$ cm and $12$ cm. How long is its hypotenuse?

- A) $21$ cm
- B) $15$ cm
- C) $\sqrt{63}$ cm
- D) $225$ cm
- E) $13$ cm

$$
c = \sqrt{9^2 + 12^2} = \sqrt{81 + 144} = \sqrt{225} = 15
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the **Pythagorean theorem**, the square of the hypotenuse is the sum of the squares of the legs, so $c^2 = 225$ and $c = 15$ cm. The same result follows without calculation by noting that $9$ and $12$ are $3 \cdot 3$ and $3 \cdot 4$. The side lengths are therefore a multiple of the triple $(3, 4, 5)$, and the hypotenuse is $3 \cdot 5$. Choice A gives the sum of the legs, while choice D gives the square of the hypotenuse without taking the square root. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A ladder $2.6$ m long leans against a vertical wall, with its foot $1$ m from the base of the wall. The floor is level. How high up the wall does the ladder reach?

- A) $1.6$ m
- B) $3.6$ m
- C) $2.8$ m
- D) $5.76$ m
- E) $2.4$ m

$$
h = \sqrt{2.6^2 - 1^2} = \sqrt{6.76 - 1} = \sqrt{5.76} = 2.4
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 1,0 0,2.4" linee="-0.4,0 2,0;0,0 0,3;1,0 0,2.4;0,0.25 0.25,0.25 0.25,0" etichette="0.35,-0.3,1 m;-0.35,1.15,h;0.6,1.4,2.6 m" xmin=-0.8 xmax=2.2 ymin=-0.6 ymax=3.2 titolo="The ladder, wall, and floor form a right triangle with hypotenuse $2.6$ m and one leg of length $1$ m"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The wall is vertical and the floor is horizontal, so they form a right triangle with the ladder as its hypotenuse. The height $h$ is a leg. By the **Pythagorean theorem**, subtract the square of the known leg from the square of the hypotenuse to obtain $h = 2.4$ m. The side lengths are the triple $(5, 12, 13)$ multiplied by $0.2$. Choice C is approximately $\sqrt{6.76 + 1}$. It adds the squares instead of subtracting them, giving a leg longer than the hypotenuse, which is impossible. Choice D is $h^2$. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A rectangle has a diagonal of $5$ cm and a base of $1.4$ cm. What is its perimeter?

- A) $12.4$ cm
- B) $6.2$ cm
- C) $6.72$ cm
- D) $12.8$ cm
- E) $9.6$ cm

$$
h = \sqrt{5^2 - 1.4^2} = \sqrt{25 - 1.96} = \sqrt{23.04} = 4.8
$$
$$
2p = 2 \cdot (1.4 + 4.8) = 2 \cdot 6.2 = 12.4
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The diagonal divides the rectangle into two right triangles whose legs are the base and height. By the **Pythagorean theorem**, the height is $4.8$ cm. The perimeter is twice the sum of the base and height, or $12.4$ cm. Choice B gives the semiperimeter. Choice C gives the area $1.4 \cdot 4.8 = 6.72$. Choice D uses the diagonal in place of the height. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
