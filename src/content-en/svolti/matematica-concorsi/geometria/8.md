## Exercise 1

What is the distance between points $A(-1, 2)$ and $B(5, 10)$?

- A) $14$
- B) $100$
- C) $4\sqrt{5}$
- D) $8$
- E) $10$

$$
\overline{AB} = \sqrt{(5 - (-1))^2 + (10 - 2)^2} = \sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{100} = 10
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **distance between two points** follows from applying the Pythagorean theorem to a triangle whose legs are parallel to the axes. The leg lengths are the coordinate differences, $5 - (-1) = 6$ and $10 - 2 = 8$, so the distance is $10$, as in the triple $(6, 8, 10)$. Choice C comes from the calculation $5 - 1 = 4$, overlooking the minus sign in $-1$. Choice A gives the sum of the legs. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is the midpoint of the segment with endpoints $A(3, -4)$ and $B(7, 10)$?

- A) $(5, 7)$
- B) $(2, 7)$
- C) $(5, 3)$
- D) $(10, 6)$
- E) $(4, 14)$

$$
M = \left( \frac{3 + 7}{2},\ \frac{-4 + 10}{2} \right) = \left( \frac{10}{2},\ \frac{6}{2} \right) = (5, 3)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The coordinates of the **midpoint** are the averages of the corresponding endpoint coordinates. The horizontal coordinate is $\dfrac{3 + 7}{2} = 5$, and the vertical coordinate is $\dfrac{-4 + 10}{2} = 3$. Choice A ignores the sign of $-4$. Choice D adds the coordinates without dividing by $2$. Choice B calculates half the differences in place of the averages. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A taxi fare consists of a fixed charge and a constant rate per kilometer. A $4$ km trip costs $11$ euros, and a $10$ km trip costs $20$ euros. What does a $16$ km trip cost?

- A) $44$ euros
- B) $32$ euros
- C) $24$ euros
- D) $29$ euros
- E) $26$ euros

$$
m = \frac{20 - 11}{10 - 4} = \frac{9}{6} = 1.5,
\qquad
q = 11 - 1.5 \cdot 4 = 5
$$
$$
y = 1.5 \cdot 16 + 5 = 24 + 5 = 29
$$

::grafico{f="1.5*x+5" xmin=-2 xmax=18 ymin=-4 ymax=32 nomi="km,€" legenda="y = 1.5\,x + 5" punti="4,11,pieno;10,20,pieno;16,29,pieno" segmenti="16,0,16,29" titolo="Taxi fare as a function of distance in kilometers. The line passes through $(4, 11)$ and $(10, 20)$ and crosses the vertical axis at $5$, the fixed charge"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The graph of the fare $y$ as a function of distance $x$ in kilometers is a **line** $y = mx + q$, where $m$ is the rate per kilometer and $q$ is the fixed charge. The slope is the change in fare divided by the change in distance, giving $m = 1.5$ euros per kilometer. Substituting the point $(4, 11)$ gives $q = 5$ euros. Therefore a $16$ km trip costs $29$ euros. Choices A and B treat fare and distance as directly proportional, which the fixed charge rules out. Choice C omits the fixed charge. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
