## Exercise 1

A triangle has side lengths $6$ cm, $8$ cm, and $10$ cm. The shortest side of a similar triangle measures $9$ cm. What is the perimeter of the second triangle?

- A) $33$ cm
- B) $54$ cm
- C) $48$ cm
- D) $16$ cm
- E) $36$ cm

$$
k = \frac{9}{6} = \frac{3}{2},
\qquad
2p_1 = 6 + 8 + 10 = 24
$$
$$
2p_2 = 24 \cdot \frac{3}{2} = 36
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The shortest sides of the two triangles correspond, so the **scale factor** is $k = \dfrac{9}{6} = \dfrac{3}{2}$. The perimeters have the same ratio as the sides, so the second perimeter is $24 \cdot \dfrac{3}{2} = 36$ cm. Its sides measure $9$, $12$, and $15$ cm. Choice A adds $3$ cm to each side, treating scaling as addition instead of multiplication. Choice B multiplies by $k^2$, the factor for areas. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A triangle has area $24\ \text{cm}^2$. Each side of a similar triangle is $1.5$ times as long as the corresponding side of the first triangle. What is its area?

- A) $36\ \text{cm}^2$
- B) $54\ \text{cm}^2$
- C) $81\ \text{cm}^2$
- D) $16\ \text{cm}^2$
- E) $48\ \text{cm}^2$

$$
k = 1.5,
\qquad
k^2 = 2.25,
\qquad
24 \cdot 2.25 = 54
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **ratio of the areas** of similar figures is $k^2$, since an area is a product of two lengths, each multiplied by $k$. With $k = 1.5$, we have $k^2 = 2.25$, so the second triangle has area $24 \cdot 2.25 = 54\ \text{cm}^2$. Choice A multiplies by $k$, and choice C multiplies by $k^3$, the volume factor. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A company increases the edge length of a cube-shaped package by $20\%$. By what percentage does its volume increase?

- A) $20\%$
- B) $60\%$
- C) $44\%$
- D) $72.8\%$
- E) $172.8\%$

$$
k = 1.2,
\qquad
k^3 = 1.2^3 = 1.728
$$
$$
1.728 - 1 = 0.728 = 72.8\%
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The enlarged cube is similar to the original with scale factor $k = 1.2$. By the rule for the **ratio of the volumes** of similar figures, the volume ratio is $k^3$. Since $1.2^3 = 1.728$, the volume is multiplied by $1.728$, an increase of $72.8\%$. Choice B adds $20\%$ three times, although successive percentage factors multiply, as discussed in the topic on percentages. Choice C gives the increase in the area of a face. Choice E confuses the new volume, which is $172.8\%$ of the original volume, with the increase. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
