## Exercise 1

How long is the diagonal of a square with side length $7$ cm?

- A) $14$ cm
- B) $7\sqrt{2}$ cm
- C) $7\sqrt{3}$ cm
- D) $49$ cm
- E) $\dfrac{7\sqrt{2}}{2}$ cm

$$
d = l\sqrt{2} = 7\sqrt{2}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The diagonal divides the square into two isosceles right triangles with legs $7$ cm long. By the **Pythagorean theorem**, $d = \sqrt{49 + 49} = \sqrt{98} = 7\sqrt{2}$. The diagonal is about $9.9$ cm long, between the side length $7$ and the sum of the two side lengths $14$, as expected for the hypotenuse. Choice C gives the space diagonal of a cube with edge length $7$, and choice E gives half the diagonal of the square. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is the altitude of an equilateral triangle with side length $6$ cm?

- A) $3\sqrt{3}$ cm
- B) $6\sqrt{3}$ cm
- C) $9\sqrt{3}$ cm
- D) $3\sqrt{2}$ cm
- E) $6$ cm

$$
h = \sqrt{6^2 - 3^2} = \sqrt{36 - 9} = \sqrt{27} = 3\sqrt{3}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The altitude of an equilateral triangle meets the base at its midpoint, dividing the triangle into two right triangles with hypotenuse $6$ and shorter leg $3$. By the **Pythagorean theorem**, the altitude is $\sqrt{27} = 3\sqrt{3}$, as the formula $h = \dfrac{l\sqrt{3}}{2}$ also shows. Choice C, $9\sqrt{3}$, is the area of the triangle, while choice E is its side length. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A kite is attached to a taut string $50$ m long anchored to the ground. The string makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the level ground. Ignoring any curvature of the string, how high is the kite above the ground?

- A) $25$ m
- B) $25\sqrt{2}$ m
- C) $25\sqrt{3}$ m
- D) $50\sqrt{3}$ m
- E) $\dfrac{50\sqrt{3}}{3}$ m

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{leg opposite } 30^\circ &= \frac{50}{2} = 25 \\[6pt]
h = \text{leg opposite } 60^\circ &= 25\sqrt{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-0.5,0 4,0;0,0 2.5,4.33;0.6,0 0.59,0.09 0.57,0.18 0.54,0.26 0.5,0.34 0.44,0.41 0.37,0.47 0.3,0.52;2.5,0 2.5,0.25 2.25,0.25" segmenti="2.5,0,2.5,4.33" punti="2.5,4.33,pieno" etichette="0.7,0.25,60°;0.75,2.3,50 m;2.65,2.1,h" xmin=-0.7 xmax=4.2 ymin=-0.5 ymax=4.8 titolo="The taut string, the vertical line down from the kite, and the ground form a right triangle with angles of $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The string, the vertical line down from the kite, and the ground form a right triangle with hypotenuse $50$ m and acute angles of $60^\circ$ and $30^\circ$. This triangle is half of an equilateral triangle with side length $50$ m. Therefore, the leg opposite the $30^\circ$ angle is $25$ m, and the leg opposite the $60^\circ$ angle, which is the height, is $25\sqrt{3} \approx 43.3$ m. Choice A gives the horizontal distance from the anchor point to the point directly below the kite, interchanging the two legs. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
