## Exercise 1

Two cars leave at $8{:}00$ from two cities $270$ km apart and travel toward each other on the same road at constant speeds of $70$ km/h and $65$ km/h. At what time do the cars meet?

- A) $9{:}30$
- B) $10{:}00$
- C) $11{:}51$
- D) $12{:}09$
- E) $10{:}30$

$$
t = \frac{270}{70 + 65} = \frac{270}{135} = 2\ \text{h}
$$
$$
8{:}00 + 2\ \text{h} = 10{:}00
$$

::grafico{f="70*x+0*sqrt(x)+0*sqrt(3.85-x);270-65*x+0*sqrt(x)+0*sqrt(4.15-x)" punti="2,140,pieno" segmenti="2,0,2,140" xmin=-0.5 xmax=4.5 ymin=-40 ymax=360 nomi="t,s" legenda="s = 70\,t;s = 270 - 65\,t" titolo="Distance $s$ in km from the first city as a function of the number of hours $t$ since $8{:}00$. The cars meet when $t = 2$."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The cars are traveling toward each other, so the distance between them decreases by $70 + 65 = 135$ km each hour. To find the **time until they meet**, divide the initial distance by the sum of their speeds. This gives $2$ hours, so the cars meet at $10{:}00$. To check, the cars have traveled $140$ km and $130$ km, for a total of $270$ km. Options C and D give the times at which each car would reach the other city if it traveled alone. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A truck leaves at $7{:}30$ and travels at a constant speed of $60$ km/h. At $9{:}00$, a car leaves the same point and follows the truck along the same road at a constant speed of $90$ km/h. At what time does the car catch up with the truck?

- A) $10{:}00$
- B) $10{:}30$
- C) $9{:}36$
- D) $11{:}00$
- E) $12{:}00$

$$
d = 60 \cdot 1.5 = 90\ \text{km}
$$
$$
t = \frac{90}{90 - 60} = 3\ \text{h},
\qquad
9{:}00 + 3\ \text{h} = 12{:}00
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By $9{:}00$, the truck has been traveling for an hour and a half, giving it a head start of $90$ km. The car gains $90 - 60 = 30$ km per hour, so the **catch-up time** is $3$ hours. Count those $3$ hours from the time the car leaves. The car catches the truck at $12{:}00$. Option B adds the $3$ hours to the truck's departure time. Option C adds the speeds, using the formula for vehicles traveling toward each other. To check, by $12{:}00$ the truck has traveled $60 \cdot 4.5 = 270$ km and the car has traveled $90 \cdot 3 = 270$ km. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A sales representative travels the $240$ km between two cities at an average speed of $80$ km/h on the outbound trip and $120$ km/h on the return trip. What is the average speed for the entire round trip?

- A) $100$ km/h
- B) $104$ km/h
- C) $96$ km/h
- D) $92$ km/h
- E) $98$ km/h

$$
t_{\text{outbound}} = \frac{240}{80} = 3\ \text{h},
\qquad
t_{\text{return}} = \frac{240}{120} = 2\ \text{h}
$$
$$
v = \frac{480}{3 + 2} = 96\ \text{km/h}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Average speed is total distance divided by total time. The total distance is $480$ km and the total time is $5$ hours, so the average speed is $96$ km/h. The formula for **average speed on a round trip**, $\dfrac{2 \cdot 80 \cdot 120}{200} = 96$, gives the same value. Option A takes the arithmetic mean of the two speeds. This is incorrect because the slower outbound trip takes longer than the return trip. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
