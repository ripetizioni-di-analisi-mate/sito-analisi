## Exercise 1

One painter can paint an apartment in $10$ hours and another can paint it in $15$ hours. How long will they take to paint it together if each works at a constant rate?

- A) $12.5$ hours
- B) $25$ hours
- C) $5$ hours
- D) $6$ hours
- E) $8$ hours

$$
\frac{1}{10} + \frac{1}{15} = \frac{3}{30} + \frac{2}{30} = \frac{5}{30} = \frac{1}{6}
$$
$$
T = 6\ \text{h}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In one hour the first painter paints $\dfrac{1}{10}$ of the apartment and the second paints $\dfrac{1}{15}$. To find the **time needed to finish a job together**, add their work rates. Together they paint $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of the apartment each hour, so they finish in $6$ hours. The result must be less than $10$ hours, the faster painter's time. This rules out options A and B, which give the mean and the sum of the individual times. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A faucet fills a tank in $6$ hours and a drain empties it in $10$ hours, both at constant rates. If the tank starts empty and the faucet and drain are opened together, how long does it take to fill?

- A) $15$ hours
- B) $3$ hours and $45$ minutes
- C) $16$ hours
- D) $8$ hours
- E) $4$ hours

$$
\frac{1}{6} - \frac{1}{10} = \frac{5}{30} - \frac{3}{30} = \frac{2}{30} = \frac{1}{15}
$$
$$
T = 15\ \text{h}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each hour the faucet fills $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of the tank and the drain empties $\dfrac{1}{10}$ of it. With the **faucet and drain open together**, subtract the drain's rate. The tank gains $\dfrac{1}{15}$ of its capacity each hour and fills in $15$ hours. The drain slows the filling process, so the result must exceed $6$ hours. Option B adds the rates as if the drain supplied water, since $\dfrac{1}{6} + \dfrac{1}{10} = \dfrac{4}{15}$, and $\dfrac{15}{4}$ h equals $3$ hours and $45$ minutes. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

An empty pool has two inlets that would fill it separately in $4$ hours and $12$ hours, and a drain that would empty it in $6$ hours. All three operate at constant rates. Someone leaves all three open by mistake. How long does the pool take to fill?

- A) $2$ hours
- B) $6$ hours
- C) $3$ hours
- D) $22$ hours
- E) $12$ hours

$$
\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{12} - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{3}{12} + \frac{1}{12} - \frac{2}{12} = \frac{2}{12} = \frac{1}{6}
$$
$$
T = 6\ \text{h}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each hour the two inlets fill $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $\dfrac{1}{12}$ of the pool, respectively, while the drain empties $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of it. Add the inlet rates and subtract the drain rate. The pool gains $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of its capacity each hour and fills in $6$ hours. Option C gives the time with only the two inlets running and the drain closed. Option A adds the drain rate as well. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
