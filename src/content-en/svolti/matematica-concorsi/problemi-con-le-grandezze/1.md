## Exercise 1

A length of $3.25$ km, expressed in centimeters, is

- A) $3250$ cm
- B) $32\,500$ cm
- C) $325\,000$ cm
- D) $3\,250\,000$ cm
- E) $0.0000325$ cm

$$
3.25\ \text{km} = 3.25 \cdot 100\,000\ \text{cm} = 325\,000\ \text{cm}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

From kilometers to centimeters there are five steps down the metric scale (km, hm, dam, m, dm, cm). To **convert a length** over these five steps, multiply by $100\,000$ and move the decimal point five places to the right. A centimeter is smaller than a kilometer, so the numerical value must increase. This immediately rules out option E. Options A, B, and D result from counting too few or too many steps. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The sum $2.4\ \text{kg} + 350\ \text{g} + 5\ \text{hg}$, expressed in kilograms, is

- A) $3.25$ kg
- B) $2.8$ kg
- C) $7.75$ kg
- D) $2.755$ kg
- E) $32.5$ kg

$$
350\ \text{g} = 0.35\ \text{kg},
\qquad
5\ \text{hg} = 0.5\ \text{kg}
$$
$$
2.4 + 0.35 + 0.5 = 3.25\ \text{kg}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Measurements must be in the same unit before you add them, so convert everything to kilograms. Moving from grams to kilograms takes three steps up the scale, so $350$ g equals $0.35$ kg. A hectogram is $100$ g, or one-tenth of a kilogram, so $5$ hg equals $0.5$ kg. Options B, C, and D result from interpreting $5$ hg as $50$ g, $5$ kg, and $5$ g, respectively. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A farm bottles $99$ liters of oil in bottles that hold $33$ cl each. How many bottles does it need?

- A) $3$
- B) $30$
- C) $3000$
- D) $330$
- E) $300$

$$
99\ \text{l} = 9900\ \text{cl}
$$
$$
\frac{9900}{33} = 300
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To find the number of bottles, divide the total amount by the capacity of one bottle. First express both quantities in the same unit. Moving from liters to centiliters takes two steps down the scale, so $99$ liters equals $9900$ cl. Dividing $9900$ by $33$ gives $300$ bottles. To check, each bottle holds about a third of a liter, so it takes about three bottles per liter, and $3 \cdot 99 \approx 300$. Options B and C differ from the result by a factor of $10$. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
