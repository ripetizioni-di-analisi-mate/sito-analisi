## Exercise 1

A duration of $0.35$ hours is equivalent to

- A) $21$ minutes
- B) $35$ minutes
- C) $30$ minutes
- D) $20$ minutes
- E) $25$ minutes

$$
0.35 \cdot 60 = 21\ \text{min}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

An hour contains $60$ minutes, so to convert decimal hours to minutes, multiply by $60$. This gives $0.35 \cdot 60 = 21$ minutes. Option B reads the decimal digits as minutes, as though an hour contained $100$ minutes. To check, $0.35$ h is a little more than a third of an hour, or a little more than $20$ minutes. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A train leaves at $9{:}47$ and arrives at $13{:}22$ the same day. How long is the trip?

- A) $3$ h $45$ min
- B) $4$ h $35$ min
- C) $3$ h $35$ min
- D) $3$ h $25$ min
- E) $4$ h $25$ min

$$
13\ \text{h}\ 22\ \text{min} = 12\ \text{h}\ 82\ \text{min}
$$
$$
12\ \text{h}\ 82\ \text{min} - 9\ \text{h}\ 47\ \text{min} = 3\ \text{h}\ 35\ \text{min}
$$

::grafico{linee="9.783,0 13.367,0" punti="9.783,0,pieno;10,0,pieno;13.367,0,pieno" segmenti="9.783,0.6,10,0.6;10,0.6,13.367,0.6;9.783,0.3,9.783,0.9;10,0.3,10,0.9;13.367,0.3,13.367,0.9" etichette="9.4,-0.8,9:47;10,-1.5,10:00;12.9,-0.8,13:22;9.6,1.2,13 min;11.2,1.2,3 h 22 min" assi=0 xmin=9 xmax=13.8 ymin=-2 ymax=2 titolo="The trip from $9{:}47$ to $13{:}22$ consists of $13$ minutes to reach $10{:}00$, then another $3\ \text{h}\ 22\ \text{min}$ to reach $13{:}22$."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The minutes to be subtracted, $47$, exceed the $22$ minutes available. Regroup one hour as $60$ minutes and write the arrival time as $12$ h $82$ min. The difference between the hours is $3$ and the difference between the minutes is $35$, so the trip takes $3$ h $35$ min. Option B calculates the hours as $13 - 9 = 4$ and overlooks the hour converted to minutes. To check, from $9{:}47$ to $10{:}00$ is $13$ minutes and from $10{:}00$ to $13{:}22$ is $3$ h $22$ min, for a total of $3$ h $35$ min. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

An employee works from $8{:}15$ to $12{:}45$ and from $14{:}00$ to $17{:}06$ each day. How much time does the employee work in five days?

- A) $36$ h $48$ min
- B) $37$ h $48$ min
- C) $38$ h $36$ min
- D) $38$ h
- E) $40$ h

$$
12\ \text{h}\ 45\ \text{min} - 8\ \text{h}\ 15\ \text{min} = 4\ \text{h}\ 30\ \text{min}
$$
$$
17\ \text{h}\ 06\ \text{min} - 14\ \text{h} = 3\ \text{h}\ 06\ \text{min}
$$
$$
4\ \text{h}\ 30\ \text{min} + 3\ \text{h}\ 06\ \text{min} = 7\ \text{h}\ 36\ \text{min}
$$
$$
5 \cdot 7\ \text{h}\ 36\ \text{min} = 35\ \text{h}\ 180\ \text{min} = 38\ \text{h}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Add the two shifts to find the time worked in a day. They last $4$ h $30$ min and $3$ h $06$ min, for a total of $7$ h $36$ min. Multiplying the hours and minutes separately by $5$ gives $35$ h and $180$ min. Since $180$ minutes equals $3$ hours, the total is $38$ h. Option A writes $7$ h $36$ min as $7.36$ h, leading to $5 \cdot 7.36 = 36.8$ h, or $36$ h $48$ min. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
