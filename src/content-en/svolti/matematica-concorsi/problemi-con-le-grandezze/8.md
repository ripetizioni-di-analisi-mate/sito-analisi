## Exercise 1

Aluminum has a density of $2.7\ \text{g/cm}^3$. What is the mass of a piece of aluminum with a volume of $250\ \text{cm}^3$?

- A) $675$ g
- B) $92.6$ g
- C) $67.5$ g
- D) $6750$ g
- E) $252.7$ g

$$
m = d \cdot V = 2.7 \cdot 250 = 675\ \text{g}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

**Density** is the ratio of mass to volume, so mass equals density times volume. The units are consistent because the density is in grams per cubic centimeter and the volume is in cubic centimeters. The mass is therefore $675$ g. Option B divides the volume by the density instead of multiplying. Options C and D differ from the result by a factor of $10$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A tourist exchanges $4500$ yen for euros at a rate of $1$ euro $= 150$ yen. The currency exchange office charges a fee of $2\%$ of the amount in euros. How much does the tourist receive?

- A) $30$ euros
- B) $29.40$ euros
- C) $30.60$ euros
- D) $28$ euros
- E) $29.98$ euros

$$
\frac{4500}{150} = 30\ \text{euros}
$$
$$
30 \cdot 0.98 = 29.40\ \text{euros}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The exchange rate gives the number of yen per euro, so to **convert currencies** from yen to euros, divide by $150$. This gives $30$ euros. The $2\%$ fee is deducted from this amount, so the tourist receives $98\%$ of $30$ euros, or $29.40$ euros. Option A ignores the fee, option C adds it instead of subtracting it, and option D subtracts $2$ euros instead of $2\%$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A car uses an average of $6.5$ liters of fuel per $100$ km. Fuel costs $1.85$ euros per liter. How much does fuel cost for a $480$ km trip?

- A) $5.77$ euros
- B) $577.20$ euros
- C) $57.72$ euros
- D) $31.20$ euros
- E) $16.86$ euros

$$
\frac{480}{100} \cdot 6.5 = 4.8 \cdot 6.5 = 31.2\ \text{l}
$$
$$
31.2 \cdot 1.85 = 57.72\ \text{euros}
$$

At $6.5$ liters per $100$ km and $1.85$ euros per liter, a trip of $480$ km costs $57.72$ euros."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The problem involves a **chain of ratios**. Apply one ratio at a time. The trip is $4.8$ times as long as $100$ km, so the car uses $4.8 \cdot 6.5 = 31.2$ liters. Each liter costs $1.85$ euros, so the total cost is $57.72$ euros. Option D gives the number of liters instead of the cost, and option E divides the liters by the price. To estimate, about $30$ liters at about $2$ euros per liter cost about $60$ euros. Only option C is close to this value. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
