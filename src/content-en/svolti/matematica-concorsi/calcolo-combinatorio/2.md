## Exercise 1

Six candidates must attend interviews one at a time. In how many different orders can they be called?

- A) $36$
- B) $120$
- C) $720$
- D) $21$
- E) $5040$

$$
P_6 = 6! = 6 \cdot 5 \cdot 4 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1 = 720
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The interview order is an arrangement of all $6$ candidates, so count the **permutations of distinct objects** with $6$ objects. There are $6$ choices for the first candidate, $5$ remaining candidates for the second, and so on down to the last. There are therefore $6! = 720$ orders. Option B, $120 = 5!$, and option E, $5040 = 7!$, are the factorials of the neighboring integers. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

How many anagrams does the Italian word LIBRO have, including arrangements that do not form words?

- A) $25$
- B) $24$
- C) $60$
- D) $720$
- E) $120$

$$
P_5 = 5! = 5 \cdot 4 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1 = 120
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

LIBRO has $5$ distinct letters, and an anagram is an arrangement of those $5$ letters. Its **anagrams** are therefore the permutations of $5$ distinct objects, giving $5! = 120$. The count includes LIBRO itself, which is also an arrangement of its letters. Option B, $24 = 4!$, omits one factor from the product. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Six people, including Anna and Bruno, line up at a service counter. In how many lineups do Anna and Bruno stand next to each other?

- A) $120$
- B) $240$
- C) $720$
- D) $480$
- E) $48$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{arrangements} &= 5! \cdot 2! \\[6pt]
&= 120 \cdot 2 \\[6pt]
&= 240
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 xmin=-0.5 xmax=13.5 ymin=-0.6 ymax=2.8 poligoni="0,0 3.4,0 3.4,1.3 0,1.3;4.2,0 5.7,0 5.7,1.3 4.2,1.3;6.4,0 7.9,0 7.9,1.3 6.4,1.3;8.6,0 10.1,0 10.1,1.3 8.6,1.3;10.8,0 12.3,0 12.3,1.3 10.8,1.3" etichette="0.3,0.5,Anna, Bruno;4.45,0.5,other;6.65,0.5,other;8.85,0.5,other;11.05,0.5,other;0.2,1.9,block (2! orders)" titolo="Anna and Bruno form a block. Together with the other $4$ people, the block makes $5$ items that can be arranged in $5!$ ways. Within the block the two can stand in $2!$ orders."}

<div class="spiegazione">

Since Anna and Bruno must stand together, treat them as a single block. The block and the other $4$ people make $5$ items that can be arranged in $5! = 120$ ways. Within the block there are $2! = 2$ orders, since Anna can stand before or after Bruno. By the **multiplication principle**, there are $120 \cdot 2 = 240$ lineups. Option A leaves out the two orders inside the block. Option D, $480 = 720 - 240$, counts the lineups in which Anna and Bruno are *not* next to each other. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
