## Exercise 1

How many anagrams does the Italian word NONNA have, including arrangements that do not form words?

- A) $20$
- B) $120$
- C) $60$
- D) $10$
- E) $40$

$$
\frac{5!}{3!} = \frac{120}{6} = 20
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The word NONNA has $5$ letters. N appears $3$ times, while O and A each appear once. Its anagrams are **permutations with repeated objects**. Divide $5!$ by $3!$, the number of ways to rearrange the three identical Ns without changing the word. This gives $20$ anagrams. Option B, $5! = 120$, treats the three Ns as distinct letters. Option C, $\dfrac{5!}{2!}$, divides by the wrong factorial. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

On a shelf, $4$ identical blue jars and $3$ identical red jars are arranged in a row. How many different arrangements are possible?

- A) $5040$
- B) $144$
- C) $12$
- D) $35$
- E) $210$

$$
\frac{7!}{4! \cdot 3!} = \frac{7 \cdot 6 \cdot 5}{3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = \frac{210}{6} = 35
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 xmin=-0.5 xmax=12.5 ymin=-0.6 ymax=2.2 poligoni="0,0 1.4,0 1.4,1.4 0,1.4;1.7,0 3.1,0 3.1,1.4 1.7,1.4;3.4,0 4.8,0 4.8,1.4 3.4,1.4;5.1,0 6.5,0 6.5,1.4 5.1,1.4;6.8,0 8.2,0 8.2,1.4 6.8,1.4;8.5,0 9.9,0 9.9,1.4 8.5,1.4;10.2,0 11.6,0 11.6,1.4 10.2,1.4" etichette="0.5,0.5,B;2.2,0.5,R;3.9,0.5,B;5.6,0.5,B;7.3,0.5,R;9,0.5,B;10.7,0.5,R" titolo="One arrangement of $4$ blue jars (B) and $3$ red jars (R) in $7$ positions. An arrangement is determined by the $3$ positions of the red jars, which can be chosen in $\dbinom{7}{3} = 35$ ways."}

<div class="spiegazione">

There are $7$ jars, with $4$ identical jars in one group and $3$ identical jars in the other. The formula for **permutations with repeated objects** gives $\dfrac{7!}{4! \cdot 3!}$ distinct arrangements. Canceling $4!$ against the last four factors of $7!$ leaves $\dfrac{7 \cdot 6 \cdot 5}{6} = 35$. The same result comes from choosing which $3$ of the $7$ positions the red jars occupy. Option E, $210 = \dfrac{7!}{4!}$, divides only by the number of ways to rearrange the blue jars. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

How many anagrams does the Italian word ANAGRAMMA have, including arrangements that do not form words?

- A) $15\,120$
- B) $181\,440$
- C) $7560$
- D) $362\,880$
- E) $3780$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{9!}{4! \cdot 2!}
&= \frac{9 \cdot 8 \cdot 7 \cdot 6 \cdot 5}{2} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{15\,120}{2} \\[6pt]
&= 7560
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

ANAGRAMMA has $9$ letters. A appears $4$ times, M appears $2$ times, and N, G, and R each appear once. For **permutations with repeated objects**, divide $9!$ by $4! \cdot 2!$. Canceling $4!$ against the final factors of $9!$ leaves the product $9 \cdot 8 \cdot 7 \cdot 6 \cdot 5 = 15\,120$. Dividing by $2$ gives $7560$. Option A overlooks the repeated M. Option E divides by $2$ twice. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
