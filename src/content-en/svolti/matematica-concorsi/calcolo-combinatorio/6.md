## Exercise 1

An office with $9$ employees must select $3$ of them for an internal committee, all with the same role. How many different committees can be formed?

- A) $504$
- B) $27$
- C) $729$
- D) $84$
- E) $168$

$$
\binom{9}{3} = \frac{9 \cdot 8 \cdot 7}{3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = \frac{504}{6} = 84
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The three members have the same role, so their selection order does not matter, and one person cannot be chosen twice. Count the **combinations without repetition** of $3$ of the $9$ employees for a committee of $3$, given by the **binomial coefficient** $\dbinom{9}{3} = 84$. Option A, $D_{9,3} = 504$, counts each committee $3! = 6$ times, once per ordering of its members. Option C, $9^3$, also allows repetition. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

At the end of a meeting, each of the $12$ participants shakes hands once with each of the others. How many handshakes are there in all?

- A) $132$
- B) $144$
- C) $66$
- D) $24$
- E) $78$

$$
\binom{12}{2} = \frac{12 \cdot 11}{2} = 66
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each handshake involves two people. Anna shaking hands with Bruno is the same as Bruno shaking hands with Anna. The handshakes are therefore the **combinations without repetition** of $2$ of the $12$ participants, with $2$ people per handshake, giving $\dfrac{12 \cdot 11}{2} = 66$. Option A, $12 \cdot 11 = 132$, counts each handshake twice, once for each participant. Option E, $\dbinom{13}{2}$, counts one extra participant. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A convex polygon has $35$ diagonals. How many sides does it have?

- A) $7$
- B) $10$
- C) $9$
- D) $12$
- E) $8$

$$
\frac{n \, (n - 3)}{2} = 35
\iff
n \, (n - 3) = 70
$$
$$
n = 10
\implies
n \, (n - 3) = 10 \cdot 7 = 70
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

A polygon with $n$ vertices has $\dbinom{n}{2} - n = \dfrac{n \, (n - 3)}{2}$ **diagonals**, because $\dbinom{n}{2}$ segments join pairs of vertices and $n$ of them are sides. We therefore seek $n$ such that $n \, (n - 3) = 70$. The quickest method is to test the options. With $n = 10$ we get $10 \cdot 7 = 70$. With $n = 9$ there are $27$ diagonals, and with $n = 12$ there are $54$. A polygon has as many sides as vertices, so it has $10$ sides. Option A, $7$, is the value of $n - 3$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
