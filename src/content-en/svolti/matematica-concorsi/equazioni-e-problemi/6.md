## Exercise 1

The solution of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2y = 11 \\
3x - 2y = 1
\end{cases}
$$
is given by which choice?

- A) $x = 4$, $y = 3$
- B) $x = 1$, $y = 5$
- C) $x = 5$, $y = 3$
- D) $x = 3$, $y = 4$
- E) $x = -3$, $y = -4$

$$
(x + 2y) + (3x - 2y) = 11 + 1
\iff
4x = 12
\iff
x = 3
$$
$$
3 + 2y = 11
\iff
y = 4
$$

::grafico{f="(11-x)/2;(3*x-1)/2" punti="3,4,pieno" segmenti="3,0,3,4;0,4,3,4" xmin=-1 xmax=7 ymin=-2 ymax=10 legenda="x + 2y = 11;3x - 2y = 1" titolo="The lines $x + 2y = 11$ and $3x - 2y = 1$ intersect at $(3, 4)$, the solution of the system"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The term $2y$ has opposite signs in the two equations. Using **elimination**, add the two equations and $y$ cancels. This gives $x = 3$. Substitution into the first equation gives $y = 4$. Checking the second equation gives $9 - 8 = 1$. Choices B and C satisfy only the first equation, since $3 \cdot 1 - 10 = -7$ and $15 - 6 = 9$, so neither solves the system. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A yard contains chickens and rabbits, with $35$ heads and $110$ legs in all. How many rabbits are there?

- A) $15$
- B) $20$
- C) $17$
- D) $25$
- E) $10$

$$
\begin{cases}
g + c = 35 \\
2g + 4c = 110
\end{cases}
$$
$$
2g + 4c - 2\,(g + c) = 110 - 70
\iff
2c = 40
\iff
c = 20
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

With $g$ chickens and $c$ rabbits, counting heads gives $g + c = 35$ and counting legs gives $2g + 4c = 110$ because each chicken has $2$ legs and each rabbit has $4$. Subtract the first equation multiplied by $2$ from the second to eliminate $g$ and get $c = 20$, so there are $15$ chickens. To check, $2 \cdot 15 + 4 \cdot 20 = 30 + 80 = 110$. Choice A gives the number of chickens. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

At a café, $3$ croissants and $2$ cappuccinos cost $6.40$ euros, while $2$ croissants and $3$ cappuccinos cost $6.60$ euros. How much does a cappuccino cost?

- A) $1.20$ euros
- B) $2.60$ euros
- C) $0.20$ euros
- D) $1.30$ euros
- E) $1.40$ euros

$$
\begin{cases}
3x + 2y = 6.40 \\
2x + 3y = 6.60
\end{cases}
$$
$$
5x + 5y = 13
\iff
x + y = 2.60
$$
$$
(2x + 3y) - 2\,(x + y) = 6.60 - 5.20
\iff
y = 1.40
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $x$ be the price of a croissant and $y$ the price of a cappuccino. The two bills give a **linear system**. Adding the equations gives $5x + 5y = 13$, so one croissant and one cappuccino together cost $2.60$ euros. Subtracting twice this equation from the second gives $y = 1.40$, so $x = 1.20$. Substitution gives $3.60 + 2.80 = 6.40$ and $2.40 + 4.20 = 6.60$. Choice A gives the price of a croissant, choice B the combined price, choice C the difference between the prices, and choice D half of $2.60$ euros. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
