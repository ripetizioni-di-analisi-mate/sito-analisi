## Exercise 1

Three consecutive natural numbers have a sum of $87$. What is the largest?

- A) $28$
- B) $29$
- C) $31$
- D) $30$
- E) $33$

$$
x + (x + 1) + (x + 2) = 87
\iff
3x + 3 = 87
\iff
x = 28
$$
$$
x + 2 = 30
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Write three **consecutive numbers** as $x$, $x + 1$, and $x + 2$. Their sum is $3x + 3$. The equation $3x + 3 = 87$ gives $x = 28$, so the numbers are $28$, $29$, and $30$. A shortcut gives the middle number directly as $\dfrac{87}{3} = 29$. Choices A and B give the smallest and middle numbers, respectively. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Two coworkers have a total of $62$ unused vacation days, and the first has $14$ more than the second. How many unused vacation days does the second coworker have?

- A) $38$
- B) $48$
- C) $31$
- D) $20$
- E) $24$

$$
x + (x + 14) = 62
\iff
2x = 48
\iff
x = 24
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $x$ be the second coworker's number of days. The first has $x + 14$. Their sum gives $2x + 14 = 62$, or $2x = 48$, so the second coworker has **24 days**. The sum-and-difference formulas give the same result since $\dfrac{62 - 14}{2} = 24$. Choice A gives the first coworker's days, choice B gives $2x$, and choice C splits the total in half, ignoring the difference. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Three times a number minus $8$ equals half the number plus $12$. What is the number?

- A) $20$
- B) $8$
- C) $\dfrac{8}{5}$
- D) $4$
- E) $40$

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x - 8 &= \frac{x}{2} + 12 \\[6pt]
6x - 16 &= x + 24 \\[6pt]
5x &= 40 \\[6pt]
x &= 8
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

**Translating the problem into algebra** gives three times the number minus $8$, or $3x - 8$, and half the number plus $12$, or $\dfrac{x}{2} + 12$. Multiply every term by $2$ to clear the denominators and rearrange to get $5x = 40$, so $x = 8$. Substitution gives $24 - 8 = 16$ and $4 + 12 = 16$. Choice E gives $5x$, and choice C moves $-8$ and $12$ without changing their signs, giving $\dfrac{5}{2}\,x = 4$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
