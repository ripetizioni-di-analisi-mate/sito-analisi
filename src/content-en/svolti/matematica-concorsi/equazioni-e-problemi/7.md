## Exercise 1

What are the solutions of $x^2 - 2x - 15 = 0$?

- A) $x = 3$ and $x = -5$
- B) $x = 5$ and $x = -3$
- C) $x = 10$ and $x = -6$
- D) $x = 5$ and $x = 3$
- E) the equation has no real solutions

$$
\Delta = (-2)^2 - 4 \cdot 1 \cdot (-15) = 4 + 60 = 64
$$
$$
x = \frac{2 \pm 8}{2}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x_1 = 5, \qquad x_2 = -3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The coefficients are $a = 1$, $b = -2$, and $c = -15$, so the discriminant is $64$. It is positive, so there are two solutions. The **quadratic formula** gives $x = \dfrac{2 \pm 8}{2}$, or $5$ and $-3$. We can also find the solutions from their **sum and product** by looking for two numbers with sum $2$ and product $-15$. Choice A reverses the signs by using $b$ in place of $-b$, and choice C omits division by $2a = 2$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What are the solutions of $2x^2 = 18x$?

- A) $x = 9$
- B) $x = 3$ and $x = -3$
- C) $x = 0$ and $x = 9$
- D) $x = 0$ and $x = -9$
- E) $x = 9$ and $x = -9$

$$
2x^2 - 18x = 0
\iff
2x\,(x - 9) = 0
\iff
x = 0 \ \text{ or } \ x = 9
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Move all terms to the left and factor out $2x$ to write the equation as a product equal to zero. By the **zero-product property**, the product equals zero if $2x = 0$ or $x - 9 = 0$, so the solutions are $0$ and $9$. Choice A divides both sides by $x$, losing the solution $x = 0$, and choice B treats the equation as if it were $2x^2 = 18$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A rectangular flower bed has perimeter $40\ \text{m}$ and area $96\ \text{m}^2$. How long is its longer side?

- A) $8\ \text{m}$
- B) $20\ \text{m}$
- C) $16\ \text{m}$
- D) $12\ \text{m}$
- E) $24\ \text{m}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
x\,(20 - x) &= 96 \\[6pt]
x^2 - 20x + 96 &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\Delta = 400 - 384 = 16,
\qquad
x = \frac{20 \pm 4}{2}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x_1 = 12, \qquad x_2 = 8
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 12,0 12,8 0,8" linee="0,0 12,0 12,8 0,8 0,0" etichette="5.2,-1.2,12 m;12.5,3.8,8 m;4.2,3.8,96 m²" xmin=-1 xmax=15 ymin=-2.5 ymax=9.5 titolo="The sides of the flower bed measure $12\ \text{m}$ and $8\ \text{m}$, so half its perimeter is $12 + 8 = 20\ \text{m}$ and its area is $12 \cdot 8 = 96\ \text{m}^2$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The lengths of two adjacent sides of a rectangle add up to half the perimeter, or $20\ \text{m}$. If one side has length $x$, the other has length $20 - x$. The area gives a quadratic equation with discriminant $16$. The solutions $12$ and $8$ are the side lengths. They are the two numbers with **sum** $20$ and **product** $96$. Choice B gives half the perimeter, and choice A gives the shorter side. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
