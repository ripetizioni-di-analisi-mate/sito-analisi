## Exercise 1

What are the solutions of the inequality $3 - 4x > 2x - 9$?

- A) $x > 2$
- B) $x < -2$
- C) $x > -2$
- D) $x < 12$
- E) $x < 2$

$$
-4x - 2x > -9 - 3
\iff
-6x > -12
\iff
x < 2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Rearranging the terms as for an equation gives $-6x > -12$. By the **rules for inequalities**, dividing both sides by the negative number $-6$ reverses the sign, giving $x < 2$. Substituting $x = 0$ gives $3 > -9$, which is true, as expected since $0 < 2$. Choice A divides by $-6$ without reversing the sign, and choice D reverses the sign without dividing by $6$. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

One courier charges a flat fee of $10$ euros plus $2$ euros per package. A second courier charges $4$ euros per package with no flat fee. What is the smallest number of packages for which the first courier costs less?

- A) $6$
- B) $5$
- C) $3$
- D) $7$
- E) $10$

$$
10 + 2n < 4n
\iff
10 < 2n
\iff
n > 5
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For $n$ packages, the first courier charges $10 + 2n$ euros and the second charges $4n$ euros. The first costs less when the **inequality** $10 + 2n < 4n$ holds. Dividing by $2$ gives $n > 5$. Since the number of packages is an integer, the minimum is $6$. For $5$ packages, both couriers charge $20$ euros, so choice B is the boundary value, where the first courier costs the same. For $6$ packages, the first charges $22$ euros and the second $24$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

One phone plan costs $12$ euros per month plus $0.05$ euros per minute. Another costs $20$ euros per month plus $0.01$ euros per minute. Above what number of minutes per month does the second plan cost less?

- A) $160$
- B) $400$
- C) $200$
- D) $250$
- E) $320$

$$
20 + 0.01\,m < 12 + 0.05\,m
\iff
8 < 0.04\,m
\iff
m > 200
$$

::grafico{f="12+0.05*x;20+0.01*x" punti="200,22,pieno" segmenti="200,0,200,22" xmin=0 xmax=400 ymin=0 ymax=40 nomi="min,€" legenda="y = 12 + 0.05\,m;y = 20 + 0.01\,m" titolo="Monthly cost of each plan as a function of the number of minutes $m$. The lines meet at $m = 200$, where both plans cost $22$ euros. For $m > 200$, the second plan costs less"}

<div class="spiegazione">

For $m$ minutes, the second plan costs less if $20 + 0.01\,m < 12 + 0.05\,m$. Rearranging gives $0.04\,m > 8$. Dividing by the positive number $0.04$ preserves the inequality sign, giving $m > 200$. At $200$ minutes, both plans cost $22$ euros. Choice A divides $8$ by $0.05$, and choice B divides $20$ by $0.05$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
