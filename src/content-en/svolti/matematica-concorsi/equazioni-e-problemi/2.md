## Exercise 1

What is the solution of the equation $7x - 4 = 3x + 16$?

- A) $3$
- B) $5$
- C) $20$
- D) $2$
- E) $-5$

$$
7x - 3x = 16 + 4
\iff
4x = 20
\iff
x = 5
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the **addition property of equality**, move $3x$ to the left and $-4$ to the right, changing both signs. This gives $4x = 20$. By the division property of equality, divide both sides by $4$ to get $x = 5$. Substitution gives $35 - 4 = 31$ and $15 + 16 = 31$. Choice C gives the right-hand side of $4x = 20$, stopping one step early. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is the solution of the equation $\dfrac{x - 1}{3} - \dfrac{x - 4}{4} = 2$?

- A) $40$
- B) $-6$
- C) $8$
- D) $16$
- E) $24$

$$
\begin{aligned}
4\,(x - 1) - 3\,(x - 4) &= 24 \\[6pt]
4x - 4 - 3x + 12 &= 24 \\[6pt]
x + 8 &= 24 \\[6pt]
x &= 16
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The least common multiple of the denominators is $12$, so multiply every term by $12$, including the right-hand side $2$, which becomes $24$. The minus sign before $3\,(x - 4)$ changes the signs of both terms in the expansion, so $-4$ becomes $+12$. This gives $x + 8 = 24$, or $x = 16$. Choice A writes $-12$ in place of $+12$, and choice B leaves $2$ on the right without multiplying it by $12$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A taxi charges a flat fee of $5$ euros plus $1.20$ euros per kilometer. A ride costs $23$ euros. How many kilometers did the taxi travel?

- A) $15$
- B) $18$
- C) $12$
- D) $20$
- E) $25$

$$
5 + 1.20\,x = 23
\iff
1.20\,x = 18
\iff
x = \frac{18}{1.2} = 15
$$

::grafico{f="5+1.2*x" punti="15,23,pieno" segmenti="15,0,15,23;0,23,15,23" xmin=0 xmax=20 ymin=0 ymax=30 nomi="km,€" legenda="y = 5 + 1.2x" titolo="The fare $y = 5 + 1.2\,x$ for a ride of $x$ kilometers reaches $23$ euros when $x = 15$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The fare is the flat fee plus the price per kilometer times the distance. For $x$ kilometers, the **linear equation** is $5 + 1.20\,x = 23$. Subtracting $5$ from both sides leaves $1.20\,x = 18$, and dividing by $1.2$ gives $x = 15$. Substitution gives $5 + 18 = 23$ euros. Choice B takes the variable portion of the fare in euros as the number of kilometers. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
