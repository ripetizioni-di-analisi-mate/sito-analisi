## Exercise 1

Three brothers divide an inheritance of $90\,000$ euros. The second receives $6000$ euros less than the first, and the third receives half as much as the first. What is the first brother's share?

- A) $33\,600$ euros
- B) $36\,000$ euros
- C) $32\,400$ euros
- D) $30\,000$ euros
- E) $38\,400$ euros

$$
\begin{aligned}
x + (x - 6000) + \frac{x}{2} &= 90\,000 \\[6pt]
\frac{5}{2}\,x &= 96\,000 \\[6pt]
x &= 38\,400
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Express the second and third shares in terms of the first, so the unknown $x$ is the first brother's share. Adding the three shares gives $\dfrac{5}{2}\,x - 6000 = 90\,000$, so $\dfrac{5}{2}\,x = 96\,000$ and $x = 38\,400$ euros. The other two shares are $32\,400$ and $19\,200$ euros, and all three sum to $90\,000$. Choice C gives the second brother's share. Choice A writes the second share as $x + 6000$, since $\dfrac{5}{2}\,x = 84\,000$ gives $x = 33\,600$, and choice B ignores the $6000$ euros, since $90\,000 \cdot \dfrac{2}{5} = 36\,000$. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A merchant takes in $720$ euros in $24$ banknotes. Some are $50$-euro notes and the rest are $20$-euro notes. How many $50$-euro notes are there?

- A) $16$
- B) $8$
- C) $12$
- D) $14$
- E) $10$

$$
\begin{aligned}
50x + 20\,(24 - x) &= 720 \\[6pt]
30x + 480 &= 720 \\[6pt]
x &= 8
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

If the number of $50$-euro notes is $x$, the number of $20$-euro notes is $24 - x$. The value of each group is the number of notes times their denomination. The total receipts give the equation $30x + 480 = 720$, so $x = 8$. The number of $20$-euro notes is therefore $16$, as $8 \cdot 50 + 16 \cdot 20 = 400 + 320 = 720$ confirms. Choice A gives the number of $20$-euro notes, and choice C splits the notes into two equal groups. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Three people divide an inheritance. The first receives $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of the inheritance, the second receives $\dfrac{1}{3}$, and the third receives the remaining $12\,000$ euros. What is the total inheritance?

- A) $20\,000$ euros
- B) $3200$ euros
- C) $45\,000$ euros
- D) $36\,000$ euros
- E) $30\,000$ euros

$$
1 - \frac{2}{5} - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{15 - 6 - 5}{15} = \frac{4}{15}
$$
$$
\frac{4}{15}\,E = 12\,000
\iff
E = 12\,000 \cdot \frac{15}{4} = 45\,000
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Since each share is a fraction of the total, let $E$ be the entire inheritance. The fraction left for the third person is $\dfrac{4}{15}$, so $12\,000$ euros is $\dfrac{4}{15}$ of the inheritance. Dividing by $4$ and multiplying by $15$ gives $E = 45\,000$ euros. To check, the first two shares are $18\,000$ and $15\,000$ euros, and $18\,000 + 15\,000 + 12\,000 = 45\,000$. Choice B gives $\dfrac{4}{15}$ of $12\,000$, and choice A considers only the first fraction, since $12\,000$ divided by $1 - \dfrac{2}{5}$ gives $20\,000$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
