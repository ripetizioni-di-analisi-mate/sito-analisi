## Exercise 1

A father is $34$ years old and his son is $6$. In how many years will the father be three times as old as his son?

- A) $8$
- B) $16$
- C) $12$
- D) $6$
- E) $4$

$$
34 + x = 3\,(6 + x)
\iff
34 + x = 18 + 3x
\iff
2x = 16
\iff
x = 8
$$

::grafico{f="34+x;18+3*x" punti="8,42,pieno" segmenti="8,0,8,42" xmin=0 xmax=12 ymin=0 ymax=60 nomi="x,y" legenda="y = 34 + x;y = 18 + 3x" titolo="In $x$ years, the father's age will be $y = 34 + x$ and three times his son's age will be $y = 18 + 3x$. The two quantities are equal when $x = 8$, and both then equal $42$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

In $x$ years, the father will be $34 + x$ and his son $6 + x$ because both ages increase by $x$. Setting the father's age equal to three times his son's age gives $2x = 16$, so $x = 8$. **Checking the answer choices** gives the same result. In $8$ years, the father will be $42$ and his son $14$, and $3 \cdot 14 = 42$. Choice B gives $2x$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A mother is currently four times as old as her daughter. In $5$ years, she will be three times as old as her daughter. How old is the mother now?

- A) $10$ years
- B) $45$ years
- C) $40$ years
- D) $35$ years
- E) $50$ years

$$
4f + 5 = 3\,(f + 5)
\iff
4f + 5 = 3f + 15
\iff
f = 10
$$
$$
4f = 40
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $f$ be the daughter's current age. The mother is now $4f$ years old. In $5$ years, their ages will be $4f + 5$ and $f + 5$, and the equation gives $f = 10$. The mother is therefore **40 years old**. In $5$ years, she will be $45$ and her daughter $15$, and $45 = 3 \cdot 15$. Choice A gives the daughter's age, and choice B gives the mother's age in $5$ years. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Two brothers' ages add up to $30$ years. Three years ago, the older brother was twice as old as the younger one. How old is the younger brother now?

- A) $12$ years
- B) $19$ years
- C) $8$ years
- D) $11$ years
- E) $13$ years

$$
\begin{aligned}
(30 - x) - 3 &= 2\,(x - 3) \\[6pt]
27 - x &= 2x - 6 \\[6pt]
3x &= 33 \\[6pt]
x &= 11
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

If the younger brother is now $x$, the older brother is $30 - x$. Three years ago, their ages were $27 - x$ and $x - 3$ because we subtract $3$ from each. The equation gives $x = 11$. The younger brother is therefore **11 years old** and the older brother is $19$. Three years ago, they were $8$ and $16$, and $16$ is twice $8$. Choice B gives the older brother's age, and choice C gives the younger brother's age three years ago. Choice A subtracts $3$ only from the younger brother's age, since the equation $30 - x = 2\,(x - 3)$ has solution $x = 12$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
