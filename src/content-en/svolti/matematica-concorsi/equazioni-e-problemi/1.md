## Exercise 1

A supplier delivers $3a$ crates on Monday, $2a$ crates on Tuesday, and $a + 4$ crates on Wednesday. How many crates does the supplier deliver over the three days?

- A) $5a + 4$
- B) $10a$
- C) $6a + 4$
- D) $6a$
- E) $7a + 4$

$$
3a + 2a + (a + 4) = (3 + 2 + 1)\,a + 4 = 6a + 4
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The monomials $3a$, $2a$, and $a$ have the same variable part, so they are **like terms**. Add them by adding their coefficients $3$, $2$, and $1$. The number $4$ contains no $a$, so it remains a separate term. Choice A omits the coefficient $1$ of $a$, and choice B adds $4$ to the coefficients as if it were a like term. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is the expansion of $(2x - 3)^2$?

- A) $4x^2 - 12x + 9$
- B) $4x^2 - 9$
- C) $4x^2 + 9$
- D) $4x^2 - 6x + 9$
- E) $2x^2 - 12x + 9$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(2x - 3)^2 &= (2x)^2 - 2 \cdot 2x \cdot 3 + 3^2 \\[6pt]
&= 4x^2 - 12x + 9
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Use the **square of a binomial** formula $(a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2$ with $a = 2x$ and $b = 3$. The square of $2x$ is $4x^2$ because both the coefficient and the variable are squared. Twice the product is $2 \cdot 2x \cdot 3 = 12x$. Choice C omits twice the product, choice D drops the factor $2$ from that term, and choice E fails to square the coefficient $2$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Which expression equals $(x + 2)^2 - (x - 2)^2$?

- A) $0$
- B) $8$
- C) $2x^2 + 8$
- D) $4x$
- E) $8x$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x + 2)^2 - (x - 2)^2 &= (x^2 + 4x + 4) - (x^2 - 4x + 4) \\[6pt]
&= x^2 + 4x + 4 - x^2 + 4x - 4 \\[6pt]
&= 8x
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{f="(x+2)^2;(x-2)^2" punti="1,9,pieno;1,1,pieno" segmenti="1,0,1,9" xmin=-4 xmax=4 ymin=0 ymax=16 legenda="y = (x + 2)^2;y = (x - 2)^2" titolo="When $x = 1$, the two squares equal $9$ and $1$, so their difference is $8 = 8x$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Expand both squares using **special product formulas**. The minus sign before the second expansion changes the signs of all three of its terms, so $x^2$ and $4$ cancel, leaving $4x + 4x = 8x$. Choice A omits the cross term from both expansions, choice B changes only the sign of $x^2$, and choice C adds the squares instead of subtracting them. To check, when $x = 1$, the expression equals $9 - 1 = 8$, and $8x$ also equals $8$. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
