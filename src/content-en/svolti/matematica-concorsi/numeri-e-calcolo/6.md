## Exercise 1

Which of the following numbers lies between $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $0.35$?

- A) $0.33$
- B) $0.3\overline{5}$
- C) $0.3$
- D) $0.353$
- E) $0.34$

$$
\frac{1}{3} = 0.333\ldots,
\qquad
0.3\overline{5} = 0.3555\ldots
$$
$$
0.3 < 0.33 < 0.333\ldots < 0.34 < 0.35 < 0.353 < 0.3555\ldots
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The fraction $\dfrac{1}{3}$ equals the **repeating decimal** $0.\overline{3}$. To compare decimals, compare the digits one at a time from the left. All the choices have $3$ in the tenths place, so compare the following digits. The numbers $0.3$ and $0.33$ are less than $0.333\ldots$, while $0.353$ and $0.3555\ldots$ are greater than $0.35$. The only number between the two endpoints is $0.34$. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An office splits a cost of $100$ euros equally among $7$ employees. Rounded to the nearest cent, what is each employee’s share?

- A) $14.28$ euros
- B) $14.3$ euros
- C) $14.29$ euros
- D) $1.43$ euros
- E) $14.20$ euros

$$
\frac{100}{7} = 14.285\,714\ldots
$$
$$
14.28\,|\,5\ldots \longrightarrow 14.29
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The division gives a repeating decimal, so the share must be rounded. When **rounding** to hundredths, look at the thousandths digit, which is $5$. The hundredths digit therefore increases by $1$, and the share is $14.29$ euros. Choice A uses **truncation**, which drops the following digits without looking at them. Choice B rounds to tenths instead of hundredths. Choice E comes from the division $100 = 7 \cdot 14 + 2$, writing the remainder $2$ after the decimal point. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Which fraction equals the number $0.1\overline{2}$?

- A) $\dfrac{11}{90}$
- B) $\dfrac{4}{33}$
- C) $\dfrac{2}{15}$
- D) $\dfrac{11}{99}$
- E) $\dfrac{3}{25}$

$$
0.1\overline{2} = \frac{12 - 1}{90} = \frac{11}{90}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The number has a nonrepeating part $1$ and a repeating block $2$, each one digit long. By the rule for finding the **fractional equivalent**, the numerator is the number written without the decimal point through the end of the first repeating block, $12$, minus the number formed by the preceding digits, $1$. For the denominator, use one $9$ for the repeating digit and one $0$ for the nonrepeating digit, giving $90$. The fraction is therefore $\dfrac{11}{90}$, already in lowest terms. As a check, dividing $11$ by $90$ gives $0.1222\ldots$. Choice B is the fraction $\dfrac{12}{99}$, which treats all digits as repeating. Choice C is $\dfrac{12}{90}$, which omits the subtraction of the nonrepeating part. Choice E corresponds to the terminating decimal $0.12$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
