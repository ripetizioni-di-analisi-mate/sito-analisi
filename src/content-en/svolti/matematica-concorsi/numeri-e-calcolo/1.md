## Exercise 1

What is the value of the expression $18 - 3 \cdot (4 - 6)^2$?

- A) $6$
- B) $60$
- C) $24$
- D) $30$
- E) $-18$

$$
\begin{aligned}
18 - 3 \cdot (4 - 6)^2 &= 18 - 3 \cdot (-2)^2 \\[6pt]
&= 18 - 3 \cdot 4 \\[6pt]
&= 18 - 12 = 6
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the **order of operations**, evaluate the expression in parentheses first, then the power, then the product, and finally the subtraction. Since $4 - 6 = -2$ and $(-2)^2 = 4$, the product is $3 \cdot 4 = 12$, so the result is $18 - 12 = 6$. Choice B, $60$, comes from performing the subtraction $18 - 3$ first. Choice C omits the square, while choice D uses $(-2)^2 = -4$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is the value of the expression $(-2)^3 - (-3)^2 + (-1)^5 \cdot 4$?

- A) $-3$
- B) $-13$
- C) $-21$
- D) $-5$
- E) $21$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(-2)^3 - (-3)^2 + (-1)^5 \cdot 4 &= -8 - 9 + (-1) \cdot 4 \\[6pt]
&= -8 - 9 - 4 \\[6pt]
&= -21
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

A power with a negative base is negative if the exponent is odd and positive if it is even. Thus $(-2)^3 = -8$, $(-3)^2 = 9$, and $(-1)^5 = -1$. The term $-(-3)^2$ therefore equals $-9$, and the product $(-1) \cdot 4$ equals $-4$ by the **rules of signs**. The sum of the three terms is $-21$. Choice A comes from writing $(-3)^2 = -9$ and choice B from writing $(-1)^5 = 1$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A checking account has an initial balance of $350$ euros. During the month, three installments of $120$ euros each and two bills of $40$ euros each are debited, and a refund of $85$ euros is credited. What is the balance at the end of the month?

- A) $275$ euros
- B) $-5$ euros
- C) $5$ euros
- D) $-175$ euros
- E) $75$ euros

$$
\begin{aligned}
350 - 3 \cdot 120 - 2 \cdot 40 + 85 &= 350 - 360 - 80 + 85 \\[6pt]
&= -10 - 80 + 85 \\[6pt]
&= -5
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{nomi="n,euro" linee="0,350 1,-10 2,-90 3,-5" punti="0,350,pieno;1,-10,pieno;2,-90,pieno;3,-5,pieno" etichette="0.1,370,350;1.1,-45,−10;2.1,-60,−90;3.1,15,−5" xmin=0 xmax=4 ymin=-100 ymax=400 titolo="Balance in euros: $350$ initially (stage $0$), $-10$ after the installments (stage $1$), $-90$ after the bills (stage $2$), and $-5$ after the refund (stage $3$)"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Subtract debits and add credits. The installments total $3 \cdot 120 = 360$ euros and the bills total $2 \cdot 40 = 80$ euros. Since the debits exceed the initial balance plus the refund, the final balance is **negative** and equals $-5$ euros. Choice A counts just one installment and one bill, choice C gives the wrong sign, and choice D subtracts the refund as well. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
