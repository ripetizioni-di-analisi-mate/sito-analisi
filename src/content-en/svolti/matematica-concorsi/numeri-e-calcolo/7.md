## Exercise 1

What is the value of the expression $\dfrac{9^2 \cdot 3^5}{27^3}$?

- A) $27$
- B) $9$
- C) $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- D) $1$
- E) $0$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{9^2 \cdot 3^5}{27^3} &= \frac{(3^2)^2 \cdot 3^5}{(3^3)^3} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{3^4 \cdot 3^5}{3^9} \\[6pt]
&= 3^{4 + 5 - 9} = 3^0 = 1
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Since $9 = 3^2$ and $27 = 3^3$, all the bases can be written as powers of $3$. By the **properties of powers**, raising a power to a power multiplies the exponents, multiplying powers adds them, and dividing powers subtracts them. The final exponent is $4 + 5 - 9 = 0$, and any nonzero base raised to the zero power equals $1$. Choice E confuses the exponent $0$ with the value of the power. Choice A comes from writing $(3^3)^3 = 3^6$, adding the exponents instead of multiplying them, which gives a final exponent of $4 + 5 - 6 = 3$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is the value of the expression $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{-2} - 2^{-1}$?

- A) $-\dfrac{1}{18}$
- B) $\dfrac{7}{4}$
- C) $\dfrac{17}{4}$
- D) $\dfrac{11}{4}$
- E) $\dfrac{14}{9}$

$$
\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{-2} = \left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^{2} = \frac{9}{4},
\qquad
2^{-1} = \frac{1}{2}
$$
$$
\frac{9}{4} - \frac{1}{2} = \frac{9}{4} - \frac{2}{4} = \frac{7}{4}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

A power with a **negative exponent** equals the reciprocal of the base raised to the corresponding positive exponent. Thus $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{-2} = \dfrac{9}{4}$ and $2^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, and the difference is $\dfrac{7}{4}$. Choice A ignores the sign of the exponent in the first term, giving $\dfrac{4}{9} - \dfrac{1}{2}$. Choice C comes from writing $2^{-1} = -2$ on the assumption that a negative exponent makes the result negative. Choice D adds the two terms instead of subtracting them. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

An office’s digital archive has a capacity of $2 \cdot 10^{12}$ bytes. Each scanned document occupies $4 \cdot 10^{6}$ bytes. What is the maximum number of documents that the archive can hold?

- A) $5 \cdot 10^{6}$
- B) $5 \cdot 10^{7}$
- C) $8 \cdot 10^{18}$
- D) $2 \cdot 10^{-6}$
- E) $5 \cdot 10^{5}$

$$
\frac{2 \cdot 10^{12}}{4 \cdot 10^{6}} = \frac{2}{4} \cdot 10^{12 - 6} = 0.5 \cdot 10^{6}
$$
$$
0.5 \cdot 10^{6} = 5 \cdot 10^{-1} \cdot 10^{6} = 5 \cdot 10^{5}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Divide the archive’s capacity by the space occupied by one document. In **scientific notation**, divide the coefficients $2$ and $4$ and the powers of $10$ separately, obtaining $0.5 \cdot 10^{6}$. Since $0.5$ is less than $1$, write $0.5 = 5 \cdot 10^{-1}$, and the result becomes $5 \cdot 10^{5}$, or $500\,000$ documents. Choice A replaces $0.5$ with $5$ without adjusting the exponent, and choice B adjusts the exponent in the wrong direction. Choice C is the product of the two quantities. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
