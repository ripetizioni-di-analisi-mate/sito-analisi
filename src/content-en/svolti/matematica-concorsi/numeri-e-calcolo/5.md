## Exercise 1

A total of $840$ candidates take a written exam, and $\dfrac{5}{12}$ of them pass. How many candidates pass?

- A) $490$
- B) $350$
- C) $70$
- D) $2\,016$
- E) $420$

$$
\frac{5}{12} \cdot 840 = \frac{840}{12} \cdot 5 = 70 \cdot 5 = 350
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To calculate a **fraction of a number**, divide by the denominator and multiply the result by the numerator. One twelfth of the candidates is $70$, so five twelfths is $350$. Choice C is just one twelfth, and choice A counts the candidates who do not pass. Choice D is greater than $840$ and can be ruled out immediately because $\dfrac{5}{12}$ is a proper fraction. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A driver has covered $\dfrac{5}{8}$ of a trip, a distance of $225$ km. How many kilometers remain?

- A) $360$
- B) $225$
- C) $84.375$
- D) $135$
- E) $140.625$

$$
\frac{225}{5} = 45,
\qquad
45 \cdot 8 = 360
$$
$$
360 - 225 = 135
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The problem gives a part and asks us to **find the whole**. If $\dfrac{5}{8}$ of the trip is $225$ km, one eighth is $45$ km and the full trip is $360$ km long. The remaining $\dfrac{3}{8}$ of the trip is $3 \cdot 45 = 135$ km. Choice A is the full length of the trip, which answers a different question. Choices C and E come from taking $\dfrac{3}{8}$ and $\dfrac{5}{8}$ of $225$ instead of the full distance. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A sum of money is divided among three people. Anna receives $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of the total, Bruno receives $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of what remains, and Carlo receives the rest, which is $480$ euros. What was the original sum?

- A) $1\,200$ euros
- B) $1\,800$ euros
- C) $800$ euros
- D) $960$ euros
- E) $720$ euros

$$
1 - \frac{2}{5} = \frac{3}{5},
\qquad
\frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{5} = \frac{1}{5}
$$
$$
\frac{3}{5} - \frac{1}{5} = \frac{2}{5},
\qquad
\frac{480}{2} \cdot 5 = 1\,200
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="3,0 5,0 5,1 3,1" linee="0,0 5,0 5,1 0,1 0,0;1,0 1,1;2,0 2,1;3,0 3,1;4,0 4,1" etichette="0.2,0.4,Anna;1.2,0.4,Anna;2.1,0.4,Bruno;3.1,0.4,Carlo;4.1,0.4,Carlo;1.5,1.4,total divided into 5 equal shares;5.3,0.4,2 shares = 480 euros" xmin=-0.5 xmax=9 ymin=-0.4 ymax=2 titolo="Anna receives $\dfrac{2}{5}$, Bruno receives $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of $\dfrac{3}{5}$, or $\dfrac{1}{5}$, and Carlo receives the last $2$ shares out of $5$, worth $480$ euros"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Bruno’s fraction applies to what remains after Anna receives her share, so these are **successive fractions**. After Anna’s share, $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of the sum remains. Bruno receives one third of that, or $\dfrac{1}{5}$ of the original sum. Carlo therefore receives $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of the sum, which is $480$ euros, so the original sum is $1\,200$ euros. As a check, Anna receives $480$ euros, Bruno $240$ euros, and Carlo $480$ euros, totaling $1\,200$ euros. Choice B comes from subtracting $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the entire sum, leaving $\dfrac{4}{15}$ for Carlo. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
