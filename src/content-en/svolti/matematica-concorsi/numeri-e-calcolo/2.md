## Exercise 1

Which of the following fractions is greatest?

- A) $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- B) $\dfrac{7}{12}$
- C) $\dfrac{11}{18}$
- D) $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- E) $\dfrac{3}{5}$

$$
\operatorname{mcm}(8, 12, 18, 3, 5) = 360
$$
$$
\frac{5}{8} = \frac{225}{360},
\qquad
\frac{7}{12} = \frac{210}{360},
\qquad
\frac{11}{18} = \frac{220}{360},
\qquad
\frac{2}{3} = \frac{240}{360},
\qquad
\frac{3}{5} = \frac{216}{360}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To **compare fractions**, rewrite them with the same denominator, the LCM of the denominators, and compare the numerators. The greatest numerator is $240$, so the greatest fraction is $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Decimals give the same result because $\dfrac{2}{3} = 0.\overline{6}$ is greater than $\dfrac{5}{8} = 0.625$, the greatest of the other values. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A ribbon $\dfrac{15}{4}$ m long is cut into pieces $\dfrac{3}{8}$ m long. How many pieces are there?

- A) $10$
- B) $\dfrac{45}{32}$
- C) $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- D) $8$
- E) $5$

$$
\frac{15}{4} \cdot \frac{8}{3} = \frac{5 \cdot 2}{1 \cdot 1} = 10
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 1,0 1,1 0,1" linee="0,0 10,0 10,1 0,1 0,0;1,0 1,1;2,0 2,1;3,0 3,1;4,0 4,1;5,0 5,1;6,0 6,1;7,0 7,1;8,0 8,1;9,0 9,1" etichette="0.2,0.4,⅜;1.2,0.4,⅜;2.2,0.4,⅜;3.2,0.4,⅜;4.2,0.4,⅜;5.2,0.4,⅜;6.2,0.4,⅜;7.2,0.4,⅜;8.2,0.4,⅜;9.2,0.4,⅜;3.5,1.5,ribbon = 10 pieces" xmin=-0.5 xmax=10.5 ymin=-0.4 ymax=2 titolo="The $\dfrac{15}{4}$ m ribbon contains $10$ pieces measuring $\dfrac{3}{8}$ m each"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The number of pieces is the length of the ribbon divided by the length of one piece. By the rules for **operations with fractions**, multiply the first fraction by the reciprocal of the second, namely $\dfrac{8}{3}$, and cancel common factors between $15$ and $3$ and between $8$ and $4$. The result is $10$ pieces. In decimal form, the ribbon is $3.75$ m long and each piece is $0.375$ m long. Choice B is the product of the two fractions, and choice C reverses the division. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

What is the value of the expression $\dfrac{3}{4} - \dfrac{2}{3} \cdot \dfrac{9}{8} + \dfrac{5}{6}$?

- A) $\dfrac{89}{96}$
- B) $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- C) $-\dfrac{5}{6}$
- D) $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- E) $\dfrac{19}{12}$

$$
\frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{9}{8} = \frac{1 \cdot 3}{1 \cdot 4} = \frac{3}{4}
$$
$$
\frac{3}{4} - \frac{3}{4} + \frac{5}{6} = \frac{5}{6}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the **order of operations**, evaluate the product $\dfrac{2}{3} \cdot \dfrac{9}{8}$ before subtracting. Canceling common factors between $3$ and $9$ and between $2$ and $8$ gives a product of $\dfrac{3}{4}$, so the first two terms cancel and $\dfrac{5}{6}$ remains. Choice A comes from performing the subtraction $\dfrac{3}{4} - \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{1}{12}$ before the multiplication. Choice E adds only the first and last terms. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
