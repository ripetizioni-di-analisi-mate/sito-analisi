## Exercise 1

What is the value of the expression $\sqrt{50} + \sqrt{18} - \sqrt{8}$?

- A) $\sqrt{60}$
- B) $10\sqrt{2}$
- C) $6\sqrt{2}$
- D) $4\sqrt{2}$
- E) $6$

$$
\sqrt{50} = \sqrt{25 \cdot 2} = 5\sqrt{2},
\qquad
\sqrt{18} = \sqrt{9 \cdot 2} = 3\sqrt{2},
\qquad
\sqrt{8} = \sqrt{4 \cdot 2} = 2\sqrt{2}
$$
$$
5\sqrt{2} + 3\sqrt{2} - 2\sqrt{2} = 6\sqrt{2}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the **properties of roots**, write each radicand as a perfect square times $2$ and take the square root of that perfect square outside the radical. The three radicals become multiples of $\sqrt{2}$, so add and subtract their coefficients to obtain $6\sqrt{2}$. Choice A comes from adding the radicands, which is not valid because the square root of a sum is not the sum of the square roots. Choice B adds $\sqrt{8}$ rather than subtracting it. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A square plot of land has an area of $200\ \text{m}^2$. Approximately how long is each side?

- A) $100$ m
- B) $50$ m
- C) $44.7$ m
- D) $14.1$ m
- E) $1.41$ m

$$
14^2 = 196 < 200 < 225 = 15^2
$$
$$
\sqrt{200} = \sqrt{100 \cdot 2} = 10\sqrt{2} \approx 10 \cdot 1.414 = 14.14
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 14.14,0 14.14,14.14 0,14.14" linee="0,0 14.14,0 14.14,14.14 0,14.14 0,0" etichette="3.2,7,area 200 m²;3,-1.6,side ≈ 14.1 m" xmin=-2 xmax=16 ymin=-3 ymax=16 titolo="The square of area $200\ \text{m}^2$ has side length $\sqrt{200} \approx 14.1$ m, between $14$ and $15$ m"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The side length of a square is the square root of its area. Since $200$ lies between the perfect squares $196$ and $225$, **estimating the square root** places the side length between $14$ and $15$ m. The only compatible choice is $14.1$ m. A calculation using $\sqrt{200} = 10\sqrt{2}$ confirms the value $14.14$ m. Choice A divides the area by $2$, and choice B divides it by $4$ as though the area were the perimeter. Choices C and E are the square roots of $2\,000$ and $2$ and result from putting the decimal point in the wrong place. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Approximately what is the value of the expression $\dfrac{0.48 \cdot 6\,020}{0.0199}$?

- A) $145\,000$
- B) $14\,500$
- C) $1\,450\,000$
- D) $57.5$
- E) $1\,450$

$$
\frac{0.48 \cdot 6\,020}{0.0199} \approx \frac{0.5 \cdot 6\,000}{0.02} = \frac{3\,000}{0.02} = 150\,000
$$
$$
\frac{0.48 \cdot 6\,020}{0.0199} = \frac{2\,889.6}{0.0199} \approx 145\,206
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Choices A, B, C, and E contain the same digits and differ by powers of $10$, so an **estimate** of the **order of magnitude** is enough to identify the result. Rounding the numbers to $0.5$, $6\,000$, and $0.02$ gives approximately $150\,000$ because dividing by $0.02$ is equivalent to multiplying by $50$. Only $145\,000$ is close to the estimate, and the exact calculation gives $145\,206$. Choice D multiplies by $0.0199$ instead of dividing. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
