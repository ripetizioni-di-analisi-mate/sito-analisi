## Exercise 1

The table shows the $40$ employees in an office by sex and employment status.

| | Men | Women | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full-time | $14$ | $11$ | $25$ |
| Part-time | $10$ | $5$ | $15$ |
| Total | $24$ | $16$ | $40$ |

What percentage of all the employees work full-time?

- A) $25\%$
- B) $35\%$
- C) $56\%$
- D) $37.5\%$
- E) $62.5\%$

$$
\frac{25}{40} \cdot 100\% = 62.5\%
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The number of full-time employees is the first row total, $25$, and the reference total is the grand total, $40$. The percentage is therefore $\dfrac{25}{40} = 62.5\%$. Choice A treats the count $25$ as a percentage. Choice B counts only the men working full-time, and choice D is the percentage of part-time employees. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Some cells in a two-way table for $230$ participants in a course are illegible.

| | In person | Remote | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | $45$ | ? | $110$ |
| Afternoon | ? | $70$ | $120$ |
| Total | $95$ | ? | $230$ |

How many participants attended remotely?

- A) $65$
- B) $70$
- C) $135$
- D) $115$
- E) $230$

$$
110 - 45 = 65
$$
$$
65 + 70 = 135
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each row total is the sum of its cells. In the morning row, the total is $110$ and $45$ participants attended in person, so $65$ attended remotely. The total of the “Remote” column is therefore $65 + 70 = 135$. As a check, the totals row also gives $230 - 95 = 135$. Choice A counts only the morning cell, and choice B counts only the afternoon cell. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

For $200$ candidates taking a competitive exam, the table records whether each candidate took a preparation course and whether the candidate passed the test.

| | Took course | No course | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admitted | $52$ | $58$ | $110$ |
| Not admitted | $28$ | $62$ | $90$ |
| Total | $80$ | $120$ | $200$ |

*Among* the candidates who took the course, what percentage were admitted?

- A) $26\%$
- B) $55\%$
- C) approximately $47.3\%$
- D) $65\%$
- E) $35\%$

::grafico{poligoni="0.6,0 0.6,52 1,52 1,0;1,0 1,28 1.4,28 1.4,0;2,0 2,58 2.4,58 2.4,0;2.4,0 2.4,62 2.8,62 2.8,0" etichette="0.8,56,A;1.2,32,N;2.2,62,A;2.6,66,N;1,-8,took course;2.4,-8,no course" xmin=0 xmax=3.4 ymin=0 ymax=72 nomi="c,n" legenda=0 titolo="Admitted (A) and not admitted (N). The counts are $52$ and $28$ for candidates who took the course and $58$ and $62$ for those who did not"}


$$
\frac{52}{80} \cdot 100\% = 65\%
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The word “among” identifies the reference group as the candidates who took the course. Their number is the total of the first column, $80$. Of these candidates, $52$ were admitted, so the percentage is $\dfrac{52}{80} = 65\%$. Choice A divides by the grand total $200$. Choice C divides by the total number admitted, $110$, answering a different question: what percentage of those admitted took the course? Choice B gives the percentage admitted among all candidates, and choice E gives the percentage not admitted among those who took the course. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
