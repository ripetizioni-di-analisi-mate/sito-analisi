## Exercise 1

The histogram shows wait times for $25$ people at a service counter, grouped into $10$-minute intervals. Each interval includes its left endpoint and excludes its right endpoint. What percentage waited at least $30$ minutes?

- A) $32\%$
- B) $20\%$
- C) $8\%$
- D) $12\%$
- E) $68\%$

::grafico{poligoni="0,0 0,3 10,3 10,0;10,0 10,6 20,6 20,0;20,0 20,8 30,8 30,0;30,0 30,5 40,5 40,0;40,0 40,3 50,3 50,0" xmin=0 xmax=50 ymin=0 ymax=10 nomi="t,n" legenda=0 titolo="Number $n$ of people by wait-time interval $t$, in minutes. There are $3$ with a wait of $0$ to under $10$ minutes, $6$ from $10$ to under $20$, $8$ from $20$ to under $30$, $5$ from $30$ to under $40$, and $3$ from $40$ to under $50$"}

$$
5 + 3 = 8,
\qquad
\frac{8}{25} \cdot 100\% = 32\%
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The people who waited at least $30$ minutes fall in the last two intervals of the **histogram**, for a total of $5 + 3 = 8$. Out of $25$ people, this gives $\dfrac{8}{25} = 32\%$. Choice C treats the count $8$ as a percentage. Choice D counts only the last interval, and choice E gives the percentage who waited less than $30$ minutes. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A town divides its budget of $2\,400\,000$ euros among four areas as shown in the pie chart. The sector for culture has an angle of $54°$. How much money is allocated to culture?

- A) $600\,000$ euros
- B) $360\,000$ euros
- C) $1\,296\,000$ euros
- D) $240\,000$ euros
- E) $36\,000$ euros

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 curve="3*cos(t),3*sin(t),0,6.29" segmenti="0,0,0,3;0,0,2.427,1.763;0,0,0,-3;0,0,-3,0" etichette="0.02,1.59,Culture, 54°;0.85,-0.96,Education, 126°;-2.32,-1.44,Transportation, 90°;-2.18,1.24,Social services, 90°" xmin=-3.4 xmax=3.4 ymin=-3.4 ymax=3.4 legenda=0 titolo="Distribution of the town budget. The angle of each sector is proportional to the amount allocated"}

$$
\frac{54}{360} = \frac{3}{20} = 15\%
$$
$$
\frac{3}{20} \cdot 2\,400\,000 = 360\,000
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In a **pie chart**, the angle of a sector is proportional to its share of the total, and a full circle measures $360°$. Culture therefore receives $\dfrac{54}{360} = \dfrac{3}{20}$ of the budget, or $15\%$, amounting to $360\,000$ euros. Choice A corresponds to a sector of $90°$, or one-quarter of the budget. Choice C multiplies by $\dfrac{54}{100}$, confusing degrees with percentage points. Choice E is one-tenth of the correct result. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The graph shows membership at a municipal gym from January (month $1$) through June (month $6$). Which month saw the greatest percent increase in membership relative to the previous month?

- A) February
- B) March
- C) May
- D) June
- E) April

::grafico{linee="1,40 2,50 3,30 4,45 5,63 6,70" punti="1,40,pieno;2,50,pieno;3,30,pieno;4,45,pieno;5,63,pieno;6,70,pieno" etichette="0.8,44,40;1.8,54,50;2.8,34,30;3.8,49,45;4.8,67,63;5.8,74,70" xmin=0 xmax=7 ymin=0 ymax=100 nomi="t,n" legenda=0 titolo="Number $n$ of gym members in month $t$. The monthly counts are $40$, $50$, $30$, $45$, $63$, and $70$"}

$$
\text{February: } \frac{10}{40} = 25\%,
\qquad
\text{April: } \frac{15}{30} = 50\%
$$
$$
\text{May: } \frac{18}{45} = 40\%,
\qquad
\text{June: } \frac{7}{63} \approx 11\%
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **line graph** shows membership rising in February, April, May, and June and falling in March, so eliminate March. To find the percent increase, divide the increase by the previous month's value. The largest absolute increase is in May, with $18$ more members, but from a base of $45$, this is $40\%$. April gains $15$ members from a base of $30$, or $50\%$. April therefore has the largest percent increase. Choice C compares only the slopes of the segments. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
