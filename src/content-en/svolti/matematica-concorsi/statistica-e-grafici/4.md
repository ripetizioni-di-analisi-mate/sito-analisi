## Exercise 1

What is the median of $7$, $3$, $9$, $4$, $12$, $5$, and $8$?

- A) $4$
- B) $7$
- C) $\dfrac{48}{7}$
- D) $8$
- E) $9$

$$
3, \quad 4, \quad 5, \quad \mathbf{7}, \quad 8, \quad 9, \quad 12
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To find the **median**, sort the data in increasing order. There are $7$ observations, an odd number, so the median is the observation in position $\dfrac{7 + 1}{2} = 4$, which is $7$ in the ordered list. Choice A is the fourth value in the *unsorted* list. Choice C is the arithmetic mean, and choice E is the range $12 - 3$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

On six days, the wait times at a post office counter were $34$, $28$, $45$, $31$, $52$, and $40$ minutes. What is the median wait time?

- A) $34$ minutes
- B) $38$ minutes
- C) $40$ minutes
- D) $37$ minutes
- E) $\dfrac{115}{3}$ minutes

$$
28, \quad 31, \quad \mathbf{34}, \quad \mathbf{40}, \quad 45, \quad 52
$$
$$
\frac{34 + 40}{2} = 37
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

There are six observations, an even number, so the middle values in the ordered list are the third and fourth, $34$ and $40$. The median is their mean, or $37$ minutes. Choice B averages the third and fourth values of the unsorted list, $\dfrac{45 + 31}{2}$, and choice E is the arithmetic mean of all six times. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A store recorded the shoe size purchased by each of $40$ customers.

| Shoe size | $38$ | $39$ | $40$ | $41$ | $42$ | $43$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customers | $3$ | $5$ | $11$ | $10$ | $7$ | $4$ |

Which of the following statements is true?

- A) The mode is $41$ and the median is $40$
- B) The mode is $11$ and the median is $41$
- C) The mode is $40$ and the median is $41$
- D) The mode is $40$ and the median is $40$
- E) The mode is $40$ and the median is $40.5$

::grafico{poligoni="37.7,0 37.7,3 38.3,3 38.3,0;38.7,0 38.7,5 39.3,5 39.3,0;39.7,0 39.7,11 40.3,11 40.3,0;40.7,0 40.7,10 41.3,10 41.3,0;41.7,0 41.7,7 42.3,7 42.3,0;42.7,0 42.7,4 43.3,4 43.3,0" xmin=37.5 xmax=43.8 ymin=0 ymax=13 nomi="n,c" legenda=0 titolo="Number of customers by shoe size. The counts are $3$, $5$, $11$, $10$, $7$, and $4$ for sizes $38$ through $43$, respectively"}


$$
\text{cumulative frequencies: } 3, \quad 8, \quad 19, \quad 29, \quad 36, \quad 40
$$
$$
\text{middle positions: } 20 \text{ and } 21
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **mode** is the size with the highest frequency, $40$, which was purchased by $11$ customers. The value $11$ is the frequency rather than the mode, so choice B is incorrect. There are $40$ customers, so the median is the mean of the twentieth and twenty-first observations. The cumulative frequencies show that sizes up to $40$ account for the first nineteen customers and sizes up to $41$ for the first twenty-nine. Both the twentieth and twenty-first observations therefore equal $41$, so the median is $41$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
