## Exercise 1

The daily low temperatures in a mountain town over a week, in degrees Celsius, were $-3$, $2$, $0$, $-1$, $4$, $5$, and $-2$. What is the range of the temperatures?

- A) $2$ °C
- B) $8$ °C
- C) $5$ °C
- D) $7$ °C
- E) $\dfrac{5}{7}$ °C

::grafico{linee="1,-3 2,2 3,0 4,-1 5,4 6,5 7,-2" punti="1,-3,pieno;2,2,pieno;3,0,pieno;4,-1,pieno;5,4,pieno;6,5,pieno;7,-2,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=8 ymin=-5 ymax=7 nomi="g,T" legenda=0 titolo="Daily low temperatures $T$ in degrees Celsius on the seven days $g$ of the week"}


$$
5 - (-3) = 5 + 3 = 8
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **range** is the largest value, $5$, minus the smallest, $-3$. Subtracting a negative number is equivalent to adding its opposite, so the range is $8$ °C. Choice A subtracts $3$ instead of $-3$. Choice C is simply the maximum, and choice E is the mean. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is the population variance of the numbers $3$, $5$, $7$, $9$, and $11$?

- A) $8$
- B) $2\sqrt{2}$
- C) $2.4$
- D) $40$
- E) $0$

$$
\bar{x} = \frac{3 + 5 + 7 + 9 + 11}{5} = 7
$$
$$
(-4)^2 + (-2)^2 + 0^2 + 2^2 + 4^2 = 16 + 4 + 0 + 4 + 16 = 40
$$
$$
\sigma^2 = \frac{40}{5} = 8
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **population variance** is the mean of the squared deviations from the mean. The mean is $7$, the deviations are $-4$, $-2$, $0$, $2$, and $4$, and their squares add up to $40$, so the variance is $8$. Choice B is the standard deviation $\sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2}$. Choice D gives the sum of squares without dividing by $5$. Choice E is the sum of the deviations, which is always zero. Choice C uses absolute deviations instead of squared deviations. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The salaries at a company have a mean of $2000$ euros and a standard deviation of $300$ euros. If every salary increases by $10\%$, what are the new mean and standard deviation, respectively?

- A) $2200$ euros and $300$ euros
- B) $2010$ euros and $310$ euros
- C) $2200$ euros and $363$ euros
- D) $2200$ euros and $330$ euros
- E) $2000$ euros and $330$ euros

$$
2000 \cdot 1.1 = 2200,
\qquad
300 \cdot 1.1 = 330
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

An increase of $10\%$ multiplies every salary by $1.1$. By the rule for the **mean of transformed data**, the mean is also multiplied by $1.1$ and becomes $2200$ euros. Each deviation from the mean is multiplied by $1.1$, so the standard deviation becomes $300 \cdot 1.1 = 330$ euros. Choice A treats the increase as adding the same amount to each salary, which would leave the standard deviation unchanged. Choice C multiplies by $1.1^2$, the factor for the variance. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
