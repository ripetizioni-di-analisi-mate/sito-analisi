## Exercise 1

What is the arithmetic mean of $3$, $6$, $8$, $11$, and $17$?

- A) $8$
- B) $9$
- C) $45$
- D) $10$
- E) $11.25$

$$
\bar{x} = \frac{3 + 6 + 8 + 11 + 17}{5} = \frac{45}{5} = 9
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **arithmetic mean** is the sum of the values divided by their number. Their sum is $45$ and there are $5$ values, so the mean is $9$, between the minimum $3$ and maximum $17$. Choice A gives the middle value, the median. Choice C gives the sum. Choice D averages only the two extremes, $\dfrac{3 + 17}{2}$. Choice E divides by $4$ instead of $5$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In the first six months of the year, an employee worked $4$, $0$, $6$, $3$, $5$, and $0$ hours of overtime. What was the mean number of overtime hours per month?

- A) $4.5$
- B) $3.6$
- C) $3.5$
- D) $3$
- E) $18$

::grafico{poligoni="0.7,0 0.7,4 1.3,4 1.3,0;1.7,0 1.7,0 2.3,0 2.3,0;2.7,0 2.7,6 3.3,6 3.3,0;3.7,0 3.7,3 4.3,3 4.3,0;4.7,0 4.7,5 5.3,5 5.3,0;5.7,0 5.7,0 6.3,0 6.3,0" xmin=0 xmax=7.6 ymin=0 ymax=7 nomi="m,h" legenda=0 titolo="Overtime hours over the six months were $4$, $0$, $6$, $3$, $5$, and $0$"}


$$
\bar{x} = \frac{4 + 0 + 6 + 3 + 5 + 0}{6} = \frac{18}{6} = 3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Months without overtime have a value of $0$ and count as observations, so divide the sum $18$ by $6$. The mean is therefore $3$ hours per month. Choice A divides by only the $4$ months with overtime. Choice B divides by $5$, overlooking one of the two zeros, and choice C gives the median. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Five packages of a product weigh $1996$, $2004$, $2007$, $1993$, and $2010$ grams. What is the mean weight of a package?

- A) $2000$ g
- B) $2001$ g
- C) $2010$ g
- D) $2004$ g
- E) $2002$ g

$$
-4 + 4 + 7 - 7 + 10 = 10,
\qquad
\frac{10}{5} = 2
$$
$$
\bar{x} = 2000 + 2 = 2002
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

All the values are close to $2000$, so use $2000$ as a **reference value**. The deviations from $2000$ are $-4$, $4$, $7$, $-7$, and $10$, with sum $10$ and mean $2$. Since subtracting $2000$ from every observation also subtracts $2000$ from the mean, the mean weight is $2000 + 2 = 2002$ grams. Choice D is the median. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
