## Exercise 1

In a survey of $20$ office employees, $4$ said they bike to work. What is the relative frequency of employees who bike to work?

- A) $4$
- B) $0.4$
- C) $0.2$
- D) $0.25$
- E) $0.04$

$$
f = \frac{4}{20} = \frac{1}{5} = 0.2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **relative frequency** is the absolute frequency, $4$, divided by the total number of respondents, $20$. The ratio is $\dfrac{1}{5} = 0.2$, so $20\%$ of the employees bike to work. Choice A is the absolute frequency. Choice D divides by the $16$ employees who do not bike to work instead of by the total. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An office received $160$ applications. The relative frequency of rejected applications is $0.2$. How many applications were rejected?

- A) $32$
- B) $20$
- C) $128$
- D) $16$
- E) $800$

$$
0.2 \cdot 160 = 32
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Relative frequency is absolute frequency divided by the total, so multiply the relative frequency by the total to find the absolute frequency. The number of rejected applications is therefore $0.2 \cdot 160 = 32$. Choice C, $128$, is the number of applications approved. Choice E divides by $0.2$ instead of multiplying. The result must be less than $160$ because it is part of the total. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The chart shows the scores earned by $25$ candidates on a written test. What percentage of candidates scored *at most* $7$?

- A) $28\%$
- B) $60\%$
- C) $40\%$
- D) $32\%$
- E) $15\%$

::grafico{poligoni="4.7,0 4.7,2 5.3,2 5.3,0;5.7,0 5.7,6 6.3,6 6.3,0;6.7,0 6.7,7 7.3,7 7.3,0;7.7,0 7.7,6 8.3,6 8.3,0;8.7,0 8.7,4 9.3,4 9.3,0" xmin=0 xmax=10 ymin=0 ymax=8 nomi="v,n" legenda=0 titolo="Number $n$ of candidates with score $v$. There are $2$ with score $5$, $6$ with score $6$, $7$ with score $7$, $6$ with score $8$, and $4$ with score $9$"}

$$
2 + 6 + 7 = 15,
\qquad
\frac{15}{25} \cdot 100\% = 60\%
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Candidates scoring at most $7$ scored $5$, $6$, or $7$. Their number is therefore the **cumulative frequency** for the score $7$, which is $2 + 6 + 7 = 15$. Out of $25$ candidates, this gives $\dfrac{15}{25} = 60\%$. Choice A counts only those scoring $7$. Choice D counts only scores below $7$, and choice E treats the count $15$ as a percentage. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
