## Exercise 1

A candidate took five tests and scored $24$ on three of them and $29$ on two of them. What is the mean of the five scores?

- A) $26$
- B) $26.5$
- C) $27$
- D) $25$
- E) $130$

$$
\bar{x} = \frac{24 \cdot 3 + 29 \cdot 2}{5} = \frac{72 + 58}{5} = \frac{130}{5} = 26
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The score $24$ occurs three times and the score $29$ twice, so calculate the **weighted mean** with weights $3$ and $2$. The sum of the five scores is $130$, giving a mean of $26$. Choice B averages the two values $24$ and $29$ once each and ignores the fact that $24$ occurs more often. The correct mean is indeed closer to $24$. Choice E gives the sum. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The table shows the number of children in each of $20$ families.

| Children | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Families | $5$ | $8$ | $5$ | $2$ |

What is the mean number of children per family?

- A) $1.5$
- B) $5$
- C) $1.2$
- D) $1.6$
- E) $24$

::grafico{poligoni="-0.3,0 -0.3,5 0.3,5 0.3,0;0.7,0 0.7,8 1.3,8 1.3,0;1.7,0 1.7,5 2.3,5 2.3,0;2.7,0 2.7,2 3.3,2 3.3,0" xmin=-1 xmax=4 ymin=0 ymax=9 nomi="k,n" legenda=0 titolo="Number $n$ of families by number of children. There are $5$ families with no children, $8$ with one, $5$ with two, and $2$ with three"}


$$
\bar{x} = \frac{0 \cdot 5 + 1 \cdot 8 + 2 \cdot 5 + 3 \cdot 2}{20} = \frac{24}{20} = 1.2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Multiply each number of children by its frequency, which acts as its weight. The sum of the products is the total number of children, $24$. Divide by the number of families, $20$, to obtain a mean of $1.2$. Choice A is the unweighted mean of the values $0$, $1$, $2$, and $3$. Choice B is the mean of the frequencies. Choice D divides by only the $15$ families with children. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

On a competitive exam, $20$ candidates at the Milan test site earned a mean score of $6.5$, and $30$ candidates at the Naples site earned a mean score of $7$. What was the mean score of all $50$ candidates?

- A) $6.75$
- B) $7$
- C) $6.7$
- D) $6.85$
- E) $6.8$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bar{x} &= \frac{6.5 \cdot 20 + 7 \cdot 30}{50} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{130 + 210}{50} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{340}{50} = 6.8
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The mean of the two groups combined is the **weighted mean** of their two means, with the number of candidates in each group as its weight. The total scores are $130$ in Milan and $210$ in Naples, so the overall mean is $\dfrac{340}{50} = 6.8$. The Naples group is larger, so the overall mean is closer to $7$ than to $6.5$. Choice A is the simple mean of the two means, and choice C switches the group weights. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
