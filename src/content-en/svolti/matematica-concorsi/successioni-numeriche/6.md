## Exercise 1

A geometric sequence has first term $2$ and common ratio $3$. What is its sixth term?

- A) $1458$
- B) $243$
- C) $162$
- D) $972$
- E) $486$

$$
a_6 = a_1 \cdot q^{5} = 2 \cdot 3^5 = 2 \cdot 243 = 486
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the formula for the **general term of a geometric sequence**, multiply the first term by the common ratio $6 - 1 = 5$ times, giving $a_6 = 2 \cdot 3^5 = 486$. Listing the terms gives the same result: $2, 6, 18, 54, 162, 486$. Choice A uses the exponent $6$ and gives the seventh term, choice C gives the fifth term, and choice B omits the first term. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is the sum of the first eight terms of the geometric sequence $3, 6, 12, 24, \dots$?

- A) $768$
- B) $384$
- C) $765$
- D) $381$
- E) $1530$

$$
S_8 = a_1 \cdot \frac{q^8 - 1}{q - 1} = 3 \cdot \frac{2^8 - 1}{2 - 1} = 3 \cdot 255 = 765
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The sequence has $a_1 = 3$ and common ratio $q = 2$. The formula for the **sum of the terms of a geometric sequence** gives $3 \cdot 255 = 765$. The rule for a common ratio of $2$ provides a check. The eighth term is $3 \cdot 2^7 = 384$, and the sum is twice the last term minus the first, or $768 - 3 = 765$. Choice B is the eighth term, choice D is the sum of the first seven terms, and choice A omits subtracting $1$ from $2^8$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A machine is purchased for $24\,000$ euros. Each year it loses $25\%$ of the value it had at the start of that year. What is the machine worth after three years?

- A) $13\,500$ euros
- B) $6000$ euros
- C) $7593.75$ euros
- D) $10\,125$ euros
- E) $18\,000$ euros

$$
q = 1 - \frac{25}{100} = \frac{3}{4}
$$
$$
24\,000 \cdot \left( \frac{3}{4} \right)^{3} = 24\,000 \cdot \frac{27}{64} = 10\,125
$$

::grafico{f="24000*(3/4)^x" punti="0,24000,pieno;1,18000,pieno;2,13500,pieno;3,10125,pieno" xmin=-1 xmax=5 ymin=-3000 ymax=27000 nomi="t,euro" titolo="Value of the machine after $t$ years. Each year its value is $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of the previous year’s value, giving $24\,000$, $18\,000$, $13\,500$, and $10\,125$ euros"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Each year the value is multiplied by $\dfrac{3}{4}$, so the end-of-year values form a **geometric sequence** with common ratio $\dfrac{3}{4}$. The value goes from $24\,000$ to $18\,000$, then to $13\,500$, and finally to $10\,125$ euros. Choice B subtracts $25\%$ of the *initial* value each year, or $6000$ euros, but each year’s loss is based on that year’s value, as with successive percentage changes in the chapter on proportions and percentages. Choices E, A, and C give the values after one, two, and four years. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
