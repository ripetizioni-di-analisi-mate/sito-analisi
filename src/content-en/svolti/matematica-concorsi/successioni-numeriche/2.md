## Exercise 1

What is the next term in the sequence $5, 15, 45, 135, \dots$?

- A) $405$
- B) $270$
- C) $225$
- D) $540$
- E) $675$

$$
\frac{15}{5} = \frac{45}{15} = \frac{135}{45} = 3
$$
$$
135 \cdot 3 = 405
$$

::grafico{f="5*3^(x-1)" punti="1,5,pieno;2,15,pieno;3,45,pieno;4,135,pieno;5,405,vuoto" xmin=-1 xmax=6 ymin=-50 ymax=450 nomi="n,aₙ" titolo="The terms $5, 15, 45, 135$ and the required term $405$ (open circle). Each term is three times the preceding one"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The differences $10, 30, 90$ grow more and more rapidly, so calculate the **ratios** of consecutive terms. All the ratios equal $3$, so each term is three times its predecessor and the next term is $135 \cdot 3 = 405$. Choice C, $225$, repeats the last difference $90$, and choice B multiplies by $2$ instead of $3$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is the next term in the sequence $2, -6, 18, -54, \dots$?

- A) $-162$
- B) $162$
- C) $-108$
- D) $108$
- E) $486$

$$
\frac{-6}{2} = \frac{18}{-6} = \frac{-54}{18} = -3
$$
$$
-54 \cdot (-3) = 162
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The terms change sign at each step, so the sequence has **alternating signs** and the ratio of consecutive terms is negative. The ratio is $-3$, so the next term is $-54 \cdot (-3) = 162$, which is positive because the product of two negative numbers is positive. Choice A has the correct absolute value but the wrong sign. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The numbers of visits to an online service recorded in consecutive hours are $640, 960, 1440, 2160$. If the ratio of each value to the preceding one stays the same, how many visits will there be in the next hour?

- A) $2880$
- B) $4320$
- C) $3600$
- D) $3240$
- E) $2700$

$$
\frac{960}{640} = \frac{1440}{960} = \frac{2160}{1440} = \frac{3}{2}
$$
$$
2160 \cdot \frac{3}{2} = 3240
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Reducing $\dfrac{960}{640}$ by the common factor $320$ gives $\dfrac{3}{2}$, and the other two ratios have the same value. Each value is therefore the preceding one multiplied by $\dfrac{3}{2}$, an increase of half its value, so the next number of visits is $2160 + 1080 = 3240$. Choice A, $2880$, repeats the last difference $720$. Choice C adds the last two values, a rule that does not fit the preceding terms because it gives $640 + 960 = 1600$ instead of $1440$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
