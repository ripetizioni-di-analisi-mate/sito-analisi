## Exercise 1

An arithmetic sequence has first term $7$ and common difference $5$. What is its twentieth term?

- A) $107$
- B) $100$
- C) $102$
- D) $140$
- E) $97$

$$
a_{20} = a_1 + 19\,d = 7 + 19 \cdot 5 = 7 + 95 = 102
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To find the **general term of an arithmetic sequence**, start with $a_1 = 7$ and add the common difference $5$ a total of $20 - 1 = 19$ times, giving $a_{20} = 102$. Choice A, $107$, adds the common difference $20$ times instead of $19$, and choice E adds it $18$ times. Choice B, $20 \cdot 5 = 100$, omits the first term. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The fourth term of an arithmetic sequence is $19$ and its tenth term is $43$. What is its twenty-fifth term?

- A) $99$
- B) $107$
- C) $100$
- D) $103$
- E) $95$

$$
d = \frac{43 - 19}{10 - 4} = \frac{24}{6} = 4
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
a_1 &= a_4 - 3\,d = 19 - 12 = 7 \\[6pt]
a_{25} &= 7 + 24 \cdot 4 = 103
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

From the fourth term to the tenth there are $6$ steps and a total increase of $43 - 19 = 24$. Each step therefore adds a common difference of $d = 4$. The first term is $3$ steps before the fourth, so $a_1 = 19 - 12 = 7$, and the **general term** gives $a_{25} = 7 + 24 \cdot 4 = 103$. Alternatively, start with $a_{10}$ and add the common difference $15$ times to obtain $43 + 60 = 103$. Choices A and B are the twenty-fourth and twenty-sixth terms. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A worker saves $50$ euros in the first month and $10$ euros more each month than in the preceding month. How much does the worker save in all over twelve months?

- A) $1920$ euros
- B) $1200$ euros
- C) $2520$ euros
- D) $1320$ euros
- E) $1260$ euros

$$
a_{12} = 50 + 11 \cdot 10 = 160
$$
$$
S_{12} = \frac{(50 + 160) \cdot 12}{2} = \frac{210 \cdot 12}{2} = 1260
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The amounts saved each month form an arithmetic sequence with $a_1 = 50$ and $d = 10$, so the worker saves $160$ euros in the twelfth month. By the formula for the **sum of the terms of an arithmetic sequence**, the total is the number of months times the average of the first and last amounts, or $12 \cdot 105 = 1260$ euros. Choice C omits division by $2$, choice A multiplies the last amount by $12$, and choice D uses $50 + 12 \cdot 10 = 170$ euros as the last amount. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
