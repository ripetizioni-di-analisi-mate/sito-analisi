## Exercise 1

Using the $21$-letter Italian alphabet, what is the next letter in the sequence A, D, G, L, …?

- A) N
- B) O
- C) M
- D) P
- E) Q

$$
\text{A} = 1,
\quad
\text{D} = 4,
\quad
\text{G} = 7,
\quad
\text{L} = 10
$$
$$
10 + 3 = 13 = \text{O}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Replace each letter with its **position in the alphabet**, using the Italian alphabet here. This gives the sequence $1, 4, 7, 10$, which increases by $3$. The next term is $13$, and the thirteenth letter of the Italian alphabet is O. Choice C, M, is the thirteenth letter of the English alphabet, which also contains J and K. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Using the $26$-letter English alphabet, what is the next letter in the sequence Z, W, T, Q, …?

- A) N
- B) O
- C) M
- D) P
- E) K

$$
\text{Z} = 26,
\quad
\text{W} = 23,
\quad
\text{T} = 20,
\quad
\text{Q} = 17
$$
$$
17 - 3 = 14 = \text{N}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the English alphabet, these letters occupy positions $26, 23, 20, 17$, so the sequence moves back $3$ places at each step. The next term is $14$, which corresponds to N in the English alphabet. Choice D, P, is the fourteenth letter of the Italian alphabet. Choice B moves back $2$ places instead of $3$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The files in an archive have codes that follow a pattern: B3, E6, H12, M24, … Using the $21$-letter Italian alphabet, what is the next code?

- A) P36
- B) O48
- C) P48
- D) N48
- E) P30

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{letters:}\quad 2,\ 5,\ 8,\ 11 \ \longrightarrow\ 14 = \text{P} \\[6pt]
&\text{numbers:}\quad 3,\ 6,\ 12,\ 24 \ \longrightarrow\ 48
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In a **mixed sequence**, the letters and numbers follow separate rules. The letters B, E, H, M occupy positions $2, 5, 8, 11$ in the Italian alphabet, increasing by $3$. The next letter is therefore in position $14$ and is P. The numbers double, so the next number is $48$. Choice A adds $12$ instead of doubling, and choice D looks up position $14$ in the English alphabet. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
