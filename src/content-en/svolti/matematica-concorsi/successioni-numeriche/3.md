## Exercise 1

What is the next term in the sequence $1, 10, 3, 9, 5, 8, 7, \dots$?

- A) $9$
- B) $6$
- C) $7$
- D) $8$
- E) $11$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{odd-numbered positions:}\quad 1,\ 3,\ 5,\ 7 \\[6pt]
&\text{even-numbered positions:}\quad 10,\ 9,\ 8,\ ?
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
8 - 1 = 7
$$

::grafico{punti="1,1,pieno;3,3,pieno;5,5,pieno;7,7,pieno;2,10,pieno;4,9,pieno;6,8,pieno;8,7,vuoto" linee="1,1 3,3 5,5 7,7;2,10 4,9 6,8 8,7" xmin=-1 xmax=9 ymin=-1 ymax=11 nomi="n,aₙ" titolo="The odd-position terms increase by $2$ and the even-position terms decrease by $1$. The eighth term, shown as an open circle, continues the second sequence"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The terms rise and fall, so the sequence is **interleaved**. Separate the odd-position terms from the even-position terms. The former are $1, 3, 5, 7$ and increase by $2$, while the latter are $10, 9, 8$ and decrease by $1$. The required term is in eighth position, an even position, so it continues the second sequence and equals $7$. Choice A, $9$, continues the sequence in odd-numbered positions instead. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is the next term in the sequence $3, 7, 15, 31, 63, \dots$?

- A) $126$
- B) $95$
- C) $125$
- D) $129$
- E) $127$

$$
3 \cdot 2 + 1 = 7,
\qquad
7 \cdot 2 + 1 = 15,
\qquad
15 \cdot 2 + 1 = 31
$$
$$
63 \cdot 2 + 1 = 127
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The ratios of consecutive terms are a little greater than $2$, so check whether each term is twice the preceding one plus a constant. The rule “multiply by $2$ and add $1$” holds for all the given terms, so the next term is $63 \cdot 2 + 1 = 127$. The differences $4, 8, 16, 32$ give the same result. They double at each step, so the next difference is $64$. Choice A omits the addition of $1$, and choice B repeats the last difference $32$. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A quiz contestant starts with $5$ points. Each correct answer doubles the score and each incorrect answer reduces it by $3$ points. The contestant gives alternating correct and incorrect answers. The scores after the first five answers are $10, 7, 14, 11, 22$. What is the score after the seventh answer?

- A) $38$
- B) $19$
- C) $44$
- D) $41$
- E) $35$

$$
5 \xrightarrow{\ \cdot 2\ } 10 \xrightarrow{\ -3\ } 7 \xrightarrow{\ \cdot 2\ } 14 \xrightarrow{\ -3\ } 11 \xrightarrow{\ \cdot 2\ } 22
$$
$$
22 - 3 = 19,
\qquad
19 \cdot 2 = 38
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The odd-numbered answers are correct and the even-numbered ones incorrect, so the two **operations** “multiply by $2$” and “subtract $3$” alternate. The fifth answer is correct, so the sixth is incorrect and brings the score to $22 - 3 = 19$. The seventh is correct and brings the score to $19 \cdot 2 = 38$. Choice B, $19$, gives the score after the sixth answer, and choice C doubles $22$ without accounting for the alternation. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
