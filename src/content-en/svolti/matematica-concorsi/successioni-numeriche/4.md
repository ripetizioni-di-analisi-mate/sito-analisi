## Exercise 1

What is the next term in the sequence $3, 8, 15, 24, 35, \dots$?

- A) $46$
- B) $48$
- C) $47$
- D) $49$
- E) $45$

$$
3 = 2^2 - 1,
\quad
8 = 3^2 - 1,
\quad
15 = 4^2 - 1,
\quad
24 = 5^2 - 1,
\quad
35 = 6^2 - 1
$$
$$
7^2 - 1 = 48
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each term is a **perfect square** minus $1$, starting with $2^2$. The next term therefore corresponds to $7^2 - 1 = 48$. The differences $5, 7, 9, 11$ give the same result. They are consecutive odd numbers, so the next difference is $13$ and $35 + 13 = 48$. Choice D, $49$, gives the square $7^2$ without subtracting $1$. Choice A repeats the last difference $11$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

New enrollments in a course during its first six weeks were $2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21$. If the number of new students each week continues to be the sum of the new enrollments in the preceding two weeks, how many new students enroll in the eighth week?

- A) $34$
- B) $68$
- C) $47$
- D) $55$
- E) $89$

$$
a_7 = 13 + 21 = 34
$$
$$
a_8 = 21 + 34 = 55
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

This is the rule for the **Fibonacci sequence** because each term is the sum of the two preceding ones. The figure for the seventh week is $13 + 21 = 34$, so the figure for the eighth is $21 + 34 = 55$. Choice A, $34$, gives the seventh week and stops one step too early. Choice E, $89$, gives the ninth week. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

What is the next term in the sequence $0, 7, 26, 63, 124, \dots$?

- A) $215$
- B) $185$
- C) $216$
- D) $209$
- E) $217$

$$
0 = 1^3 - 1,
\quad
7 = 2^3 - 1,
\quad
26 = 3^3 - 1,
\quad
63 = 4^3 - 1,
\quad
124 = 5^3 - 1
$$
$$
6^3 - 1 = 216 - 1 = 215
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each term is a **perfect cube** minus $1$, because $8$, $27$, $64$, and $125$ are the cubes of $2$, $3$, $4$, and $5$. The next term is therefore $6^3 - 1 = 215$. Using differences takes longer. The differences are $7, 19, 37, 61$ and the second differences are $12, 18, 24$, increasing by $6$. The next second difference is therefore $30$, the next first difference is $61 + 30 = 91$, and the next term is $124 + 91 = 215$. Choice B repeats the last difference $61$, and choice C gives the cube $6^3$ without subtracting $1$. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
