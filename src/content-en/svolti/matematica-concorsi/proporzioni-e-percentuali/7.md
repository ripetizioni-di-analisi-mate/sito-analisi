## Exercise 1

After a discount of $30\%$, a customer pays $56$ euros. What was the price before the discount?

- A) $72.80$ euros
- B) $75$ euros
- C) $86$ euros
- D) $80$ euros
- E) $186.67$ euros

$$
P \cdot 0.7 = 56
\iff
P = \frac{56}{0.7} = 80
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The discounted price is the initial price $P$ times the factor $0.7$, so to recover $P$, divide by $0.7$. To check, $30\%$ of $80$ is $24$, and $80 - 24 = 56$. Choice A adds $30\%$ to $56$, applying the percentage to the final price instead of the original price. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A service costs $61$ euros including VAT at $22\%$. How much VAT was paid?

- A) $13.42$ euros
- B) $8.58$ euros
- C) $12.20$ euros
- D) $10$ euros
- E) $11$ euros

$$
\frac{61}{1.22} = 50,
\qquad
61 - 50 = 11
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To **remove VAT**, divide the final price by the factor $1.22$. The taxable amount is $50$ euros and VAT is $11$ euros. To check, $22\%$ of $50$ is indeed $11$. Choice A gives $22\%$ of $61$, which applies the tax rate to the price including VAT instead of the taxable amount. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

After an increase of $25\%$, an item costs $75$ euros. What percentage discount on $75$ euros would restore the original price?

- A) $25\%$
- B) $30\%$
- C) $15\%$
- D) $20\%$
- E) $18.75\%$

$$
P = \frac{75}{1.25} = 60
$$
$$
\frac{75 - 60}{75} \cdot 100\% = \frac{15}{75} \cdot 100\% = 20\%
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,4 60,4 60,5.4 0,5.4;0,2 75,2 75,3.4 0,3.4;0,0 60,0 60,1.4 0,1.4" etichette="30,4.5,60 euros (initial);37,2.5,75 euros (after +25%);30,0.5,60 euros (after −20%)" xmin=-5 xmax=85 ymin=-1 ymax=6.5 titolo="After an increase of $25\%$, the price is $75$ euros. Returning to $60$ euros requires subtracting $15$ euros, or $\dfrac{15}{75} = 20\%$ of $75$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

To recover the original price, divide $75$ by the factor $1.25$ to get $60$ euros. To return to $60$ euros, subtract $15$ euros from $75$. The base for the **percentage change** is the new starting value, $75$. The required discount is $20\%$, corresponding to the multiplier $0.8 = \dfrac{1}{1.25}$. Choice A assumes an increase and a discount by the same percentage cancel each other out. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
