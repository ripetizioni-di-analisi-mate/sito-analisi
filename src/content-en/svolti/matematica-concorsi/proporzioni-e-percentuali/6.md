## Exercise 1

The price of a product rises by $10\%$ and then falls by $10\%$. How does the final price compare with the original price?

- A) It stays the same
- B) It decreases by $0.1\%$
- C) It increases by $1\%$
- D) It decreases by $2\%$
- E) It decreases by $1\%$

$$
1.1 \cdot 0.9 = 0.99
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,4 100,4 100,5.4 0,5.4;0,2 110,2 110,3.4 0,3.4;0,0 99,0 99,1.4 0,1.4" etichette="50,4.5,100 euros (initial price);55,2.5,110 euros (after +10%);50,0.5,99 euros (after −10%)" xmin=-5 xmax=120 ymin=-1 ymax=6.5 titolo="A price of $100$ euros after an increase of $10\%$ followed by a decrease of $10\%$ is $100 \cdot 1.1 \cdot 0.9 = 99$ euros"}

<div class="spiegazione">

For **successive percentage changes**, multiply the factors, $1.1$ for the increase and $0.9$ for the decrease. Their product is $0.99$, so the final price is $99\%$ of the original price, or $1\%$ lower. A price of $100$ euros rises to $110$ euros and then falls to $99$ euros. Choice A adds the two signed percentages. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An item is discounted first by $20\%$ and then by $15\%$. What is the overall discount?

- A) $35\%$
- B) $17.5\%$
- C) $30\%$
- D) $32\%$
- E) $3\%$

$$
0.8 \cdot 0.85 = 0.68,
\qquad
1 - 0.68 = 0.32
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The second discount applies to the already discounted price, so multiply the two factors. After both discounts, the customer pays $68\%$ of the price, giving an overall discount of $32\%$. The overall discount is always smaller than the sum $20\% + 15\% = 35\%$ shown in choice A. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A town has $40\,000$ residents and its population grows by $5\%$ each year. How many residents will it have after two years?

- A) $44\,000$
- B) $48\,000$
- C) $42\,000$
- D) $44\,200$
- E) $44\,100$

$$
40\,000 \cdot 1.05^{2} = 40\,000 \cdot 1.1025 = 44\,100
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each year the population is multiplied by $1.05$. The second year's growth applies to the population after the first increase. There are $42\,000$ residents after the first year and $42\,000 \cdot 1.05 = 44\,100$ after the second, for an overall increase of $10.25\%$. Choice A adds the two percentages and takes $10\%$ of the initial population as the increase. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
