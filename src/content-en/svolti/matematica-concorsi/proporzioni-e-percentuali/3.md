## Exercise 1

A pair of shoes costs $120$ euros and is discounted by $25\%$. What is the discounted price?

- A) $95$ euros
- B) $85$ euros
- C) $100$ euros
- D) $30$ euros
- E) $90$ euros

$$
120 \cdot \left( 1 - \frac{25}{100} \right) = 120 \cdot 0.75 = 90
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="90,0 120,0 120,2 90,2" linee="0,0 120,0 120,2 0,2 0,0;90,0 90,2" etichette="40,0.8,pay 90 euros;93,2.6,discount 30" xmin=-5 xmax=125 ymin=-1 ymax=4 titolo="A price of $120$ euros with a discount of $25\%$. The shaded discount is $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of the price, or $30$ euros"}

<div class="spiegazione">

A discount of $25\%$ leaves $75\%$ of the price to pay, so multiply the price by the **multiplier** $0.75$. Alternatively, $25\%$ is one-quarter and one-quarter of $120$ is $30$, so the discounted price is $120 - 30 = 90$ euros. Choice D, $30$ euros, gives the amount saved. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An item costs $80$ euros plus VAT at $22\%$. What is the price including VAT?

- A) $97.60$ euros
- B) $102$ euros
- C) $17.60$ euros
- D) $96$ euros
- E) $98.20$ euros

$$
80 \cdot 1.22 = 97.60
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

VAT is a **percentage increase** of $22\%$ on the taxable amount, so multiply the taxable amount by $1.22$ to find the final price. The VAT is $80 \cdot 0.22 = 17.60$ euros, given in choice C. Adding it to the taxable amount gives $97.60$ euros. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The price of a subscription rises from $40$ to $46$ euros. What is the percentage increase?

- A) $6\%$
- B) $13\%$
- C) $15\%$
- D) $46\%$
- E) $87\%$

$$
\frac{46 - 40}{40} \cdot 100\% = \frac{6}{40} \cdot 100\% = 15\%
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To find the **percentage change**, divide the increase by the *initial* value. The increase is $6$ euros on a base of $40$, or $15\%$. Choice B divides by the final value, since $\dfrac{6}{46}$ is approximately $13\%$. Choice A confuses the increase in euros with the percentage increase. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
