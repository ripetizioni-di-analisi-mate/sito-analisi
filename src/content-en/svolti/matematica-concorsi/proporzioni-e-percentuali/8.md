## Exercise 1

What is $15\%$ of $360$?

- A) $54$
- B) $36$
- C) $45$
- D) $63$
- E) $5.4$

$$
10\% \text{ of } 360 = 36,
\qquad
5\% \text{ of } 360 = 18,
\qquad
36 + 18 = 54
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 54,0 54,2 0,2" linee="0,0 360,0 360,2 0,2 0,0;36,0 36,2" etichette="10,0.8,36;41,2.6,18;180,0.8,360 (100%)" xmin=-10 xmax=370 ymin=-1 ymax=4 titolo="Finding $15\%$ of $360$. First, $10\%$ is $36$, and $5\%$ is half as much, or $18$. The shaded part is worth $54$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Break $15\%$ into $10\%$ plus $5\%$. Moving the decimal point gives $10\%$ of $360$, which is $36$. Then $5\%$ is half as much, or $18$. Thus $15\%$ is $54$. Choice B gives only $10\%$, and choice E is one-tenth of the result. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is $16\%$ of $25$?

- A) $0.4$
- B) $4$
- C) $40$
- D) $6.25$
- E) $2.5$

$$
16\% \text{ of } 25 = 25\% \text{ of } 16 = \frac{16}{4} = 4
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Since $p\%$ of $q$ equals $\dfrac{p \cdot q}{100}$, we can **switch the numbers**. Then $25\%$ of $16$ is one-quarter of $16$, or $4$, which is easy to calculate mentally. The formula gives the same value, since $\dfrac{16 \cdot 25}{100} = \dfrac{400}{100} = 4$. Choices A and C differ from the result by a factor of $10$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

What is the approximate value of $19.8\%$ of $4012$?

- A) $79.44$
- B) $765.10$
- C) $7943.76$
- D) $821.46$
- E) $794.38$

$$
19.8\% \text{ of } 4012 \approx 20\% \text{ of } 4000 = 800
$$
$$
0.198 \cdot 4012 = 794.376
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The exact calculation takes longer, so **estimate** by rounding the percentage to $20\%$ and the number to $4000$. The estimate is $800$. Since $19.8$ falls below $20$ by $1\%$, while $4012$ exceeds $4000$ by only $0.3\%$, the exact value is slightly less than $800$. The closest choice is $794.38$, which agrees with the rounded exact result. Choices A and C are off by a factor of $10$. The correct answer is **E**.

</div>
