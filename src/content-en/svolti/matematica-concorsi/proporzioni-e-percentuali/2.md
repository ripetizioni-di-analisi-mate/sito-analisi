## Exercise 1

What is $35\%$ of $240$?

- A) $68$
- B) $72$
- C) $84$
- D) $96$
- E) $8.4$

$$
\frac{35}{100} \cdot 240 = 0.35 \cdot 240 = 84
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the definition of a **percentage**, to find $35\%$ of $240$, multiply $240$ by $\dfrac{35}{100}$. For a mental calculation, $10\%$ of $240$ is $24$, so $30\%$ is $72$. Then $5\%$ is half of $10\%$, or $12$, so $35\%$ is $72 + 12 = 84$. Choice B, $72$, gives only $30\%$. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In a class of $25$ students, $9$ passed the exam on their first attempt. What percentage passed on their first attempt?

- A) $9\%$
- B) $25\%$
- C) $36\%$
- D) $34\%$
- E) $16\%$

$$
\frac{9}{25} = \frac{36}{100} = 36\%
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,4 5,4 5,5 0,5;0,3 4,3 4,4 0,4" linee="0,0 5,0 5,5 0,5 0,0;0,1 5,1;0,2 5,2;0,3 5,3;0,4 5,4;1,0 1,5;2,0 2,5;3,0 3,5;4,0 4,5" xmin=-1 xmax=6 ymin=-1 ymax=6 titolo="A class of $25$ students, with one student per square. The $9$ shaded squares represent the students who passed, $\dfrac{9}{25} = \dfrac{36}{100}$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

A percentage expresses the ratio of the part to the whole as a fraction with denominator 100. Since $25 \cdot 4 = 100$, multiplying the numerator and denominator by $4$ gives $\dfrac{9 \cdot 4}{25 \cdot 4} = \dfrac{36}{100}$, or $36\%$. Choice E, $16\%$, treats the difference $25 - 9$ as a percentage, which has no meaning here. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The number $42$ is $28\%$ of what number?

- A) $11.76$
- B) $117.6$
- C) $150$
- D) $168$
- E) $70$

$$
\frac{28}{100} \cdot N = 42
\iff
N = \frac{42 \cdot 100}{28} = 150
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The unknown is the total $N$, and $42$ is the part. The proportion $42 : N = 28 : 100$ gives $N = \dfrac{42 \cdot 100}{28} = 150$. The result must exceed $42$ because $42$ is only a part of $N$. Choice A, $11.76$, gives $28\%$ of $42$ and can be ruled out immediately. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
