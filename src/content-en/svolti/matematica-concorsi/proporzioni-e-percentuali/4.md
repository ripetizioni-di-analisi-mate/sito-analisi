## Exercise 1

Four identical faucets fill a tank in $6$ hours. How long would three faucets of the same type take?

- A) $4.5$ hours
- B) $8$ hours
- C) $7$ hours
- D) $9$ hours
- E) $5$ hours

$$
4 \cdot 6 = 3 \cdot x
\iff
x = \frac{24}{3} = 8
$$

::grafico{f="24/x+0*sqrt(x)" punti="4,6,pieno;3,8,pieno" segmenti="4,0,4,6;3,0,3,8" xmin=-2 xmax=8 ymin=-2 ymax=18 nomi="n,ore" legenda="\text{hours} = \dfrac{24}{n}" titolo="Time in hours to fill the tank as a function of the number of faucets $n$. With $4$ faucets, it takes $6$ hours. With $3$ faucets, it takes $8$ hours"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Fewer faucets take more time, so the number of faucets and the time are **inversely proportional**, and their product is constant. One faucet would need $4 \cdot 6 = 24$ hours to fill the tank, so three faucets take $8$ hours. Choice A, $4.5$ hours, treats the quantities as directly proportional. It can be ruled out because it is less than $6$. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Five notebooks cost $7.50$ euros. How much do twelve notebooks of the same kind cost?

- A) $18$ euros
- B) $15$ euros
- C) $20$ euros
- D) $16.50$ euros
- E) $21$ euros

$$
5 : 7.50 = 12 : x
\iff
x = \frac{7.50 \cdot 12}{5} = 18
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The price is **directly proportional** to the number of notebooks, so use the rule of three with the values in the same ratio. Alternatively, one notebook costs $\dfrac{7.50}{5} = 1.50$ euros and twelve cost $12 \cdot 1.50 = 18$ euros. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A car takes $2$ hours and $40$ minutes to complete a trip at an average speed of $90$ km/h. How long would the same trip take at an average speed of $120$ km/h?

- A) $3$ hours and $33$ minutes
- B) $2$ hours and $10$ minutes
- C) $2$ hours
- D) $1$ hour and $50$ minutes
- E) $2$ hours and $30$ minutes

$$
2\ \text{h}\ 40\ \text{min} = \frac{8}{3}\ \text{h},
\qquad
90 \cdot \frac{8}{3} = 240\ \text{km}
$$
$$
t = \frac{240}{120} = 2\ \text{h}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For a fixed distance, speed and time are **inversely proportional**. Express the time in hours. Since $40$ minutes is $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of an hour, the distance is $90 \cdot \dfrac{8}{3} = 240$ km. At $120$ km/h, the trip therefore takes $2$ hours. Choice A, which exceeds the initial time, uses direct variation. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>
