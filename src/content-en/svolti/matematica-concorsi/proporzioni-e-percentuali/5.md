## Exercise 1

Three partners contributed $2000$, $3000$, and $5000$ euros and divide a profit of $1200$ euros in direct proportion to their contributions. How much does the partner who contributed $3000$ euros receive?

- A) $400$ euros
- B) $360$ euros
- C) $300$ euros
- D) $240$ euros
- E) $600$ euros

$$
2000 : 3000 : 5000 = 2 : 3 : 5,
\qquad
2 + 3 + 5 = 10
$$
$$
\frac{1200}{10} = 120,
\qquad
120 \cdot 3 = 360
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The partners' contributions are in the ratio $2$ to $3$ to $5$. Using **direct proportional division**, divide the profit into $10$ equal parts of $120$ euros each. The second partner receives $3$ parts, or $360$ euros. The other two receive $240$ and $600$ euros, and the shares sum to $1200$. Choice A, $400$ euros, splits the profit equally. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An amount of $520$ euros is divided in inverse proportion to $2$, $3$, and $4$. What is the largest share?

- A) $231.11$ euros
- B) $160$ euros
- C) $120$ euros
- D) $240$ euros
- E) $260$ euros

$$
\frac{1}{2} = \frac{6}{12},
\qquad
\frac{1}{3} = \frac{4}{12},
\qquad
\frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{12}
$$
$$
6 + 4 + 3 = 13,
\qquad
\frac{520}{13} = 40,
\qquad
40 \cdot 6 = 240
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For **inverse proportional division**, use shares proportional to the reciprocals. With a common denominator of $12$, their numerators are $6$, $4$, and $3$, so divide the total into $13$ parts of $40$ euros each. The three shares are $240$, $160$, and $120$ euros. The largest goes to the smallest number, $2$. Choice A is the largest share in *direct* proportional division, since $\dfrac{520}{9} \cdot 4 \approx 231.11$. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Two positive numbers are in the ratio $3 : 7$, and their difference is $48$. What is the larger number?

- A) $84$
- B) $112$
- C) $70$
- D) $36$
- E) $48$

$$
x = 3k,
\qquad
y = 7k,
\qquad
7k - 3k = 4k = 48
\iff
k = 12
$$
$$
y = 7 \cdot 12 = 84
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The ratio $3 : 7$ means the two numbers consist of $3$ and $7$ equal parts, respectively, each of value $k$. Their difference corresponds to $7 - 3 = 4$ parts, so one part is worth $12$ and the numbers are $36$ and $84$. To check, $84 - 36 = 48$ and $\dfrac{36}{84} = \dfrac{3}{7}$. Choice E, $48$, is the difference itself. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>
