## Exercise 1

What is the missing term in the proportion $7 : x = 21 : 15$?

- A) $3$
- B) $5$
- C) $9$
- D) $45$
- E) $7$

$$
21 \cdot x = 7 \cdot 15
\iff
x = \frac{7 \cdot 15}{21} = 5
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The missing term is a mean. By the **cross-product property**, the product of the means $x \cdot 21$ equals the product of the extremes $7 \cdot 15 = 105$, so $x = \dfrac{105}{21} = 5$. To check, divide both terms of $21 : 15$ by $3$ to get $7 : 5$, equal to the first ratio. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In the proportion $(x + 2) : 5 = 12 : 10$, what is $x$?

- A) $4$
- B) $6$
- C) $8$
- D) $2$
- E) $10$

$$
10\,(x + 2) = 5 \cdot 12
\iff
x + 2 = 6
\iff
x = 4
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The product of the extremes is $10\,(x + 2)$ and the product of the means is $5 \cdot 12 = 60$. Setting them equal gives $x + 2 = 6$, so $x = 4$. Choice B, $6$, is the value of $x + 2$, one step short of the solution. The correct answer is **A**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

On a map with scale $1 : 50\,000$, two towns are $6$ cm apart. What is the actual distance between them?

- A) $300$ m
- B) $3$ km
- C) $30$ km
- D) $12$ km
- E) $0.6$ km

$$
6 \cdot 50\,000\ \text{cm} = 300\,000\ \text{cm} = 3\,000\ \text{m} = 3\ \text{km}
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,3 6,3 6,4 0,4;0,0 6,0 6,1 0,1" etichette="2.4,3.4,6 cm on the map;1.6,0.4,3 km on the ground" xmin=-1 xmax=7 ymin=-1 ymax=5.5 titolo="At a scale of $1 : 50\,000$, $6$ cm on the map represents $300\,000$ cm on the ground, or $3$ km"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The **scale** means that each centimeter on the map represents $50\,000$ cm on the ground, so $6$ cm represents $300\,000$ cm. Since $1$ km $= 100\,000$ cm, the distance is $3$ km. Choices A and C differ from the result by a factor of $10$ because of unit conversion errors. The correct answer is **B**.

</div>
