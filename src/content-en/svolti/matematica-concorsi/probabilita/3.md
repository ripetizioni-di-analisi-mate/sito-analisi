## Exercise 1

A fair coin is flipped $3$ times. What is the probability of getting tails at least once?

- A) $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- B) $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- C) $\dfrac{7}{8}$
- D) $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- E) $\dfrac{3}{4}$

$$
P(\text{no tails}) = P(TTT) = \frac{1}{2^3} = \frac{1}{8}
$$
$$
P(\text{at least one tail}) = 1 - \frac{1}{8} = \frac{7}{8}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The complement of “at least one tail” is “no tails.” This happens only for the all-heads sequence $TTT$ out of the $2^3 = 8$ equally likely sequences. By the **complement rule**, subtract $\dfrac{1}{8}$ from $1$ to get $\dfrac{7}{8}$. Option A gives the probability of the complement. Option B gives the probability of getting tails *exactly* once. The correct answer is **C**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Two fair six-sided dice are rolled. What is the probability that the two numbers are different?

- A) $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- B) $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- C) $\dfrac{35}{36}$
- D) $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- E) $\dfrac{11}{12}$

$$
\text{matching numbers}:\ (1,1),\ (2,2),\ \ldots,\ (6,6) \quad (6 \text{ outcomes out of } 36)
$$
$$
P(\text{different numbers}) = 1 - \frac{6}{36} = \frac{30}{36} = \frac{5}{6}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The complement of “different numbers” is “matching numbers,” with $6$ favorable outcomes out of the $36$ ordered pairs. The probability of matching numbers is $\dfrac{1}{6}$, so by the **complement rule**, the probability of different numbers is $\dfrac{5}{6}$. Another way to see this is that whatever the first die shows, the second must show one of the other $5$ numbers. Option C subtracts only one outcome from $36$, as if $(6, 6)$ were the only matching pair. The correct answer is **A**.

## Exercise 3

An employee reviews three case files. Each file has a $10\%$ probability of containing an error, independently of the other files. What is the probability that at least one of the three files contains an error?

- A) $0.3$
- B) $0.729$
- C) $0.001$
- D) $0.271$
- E) $0.243$

$$
P(\text{no errors}) = 0.9 \cdot 0.9 \cdot 0.9 = 0.729
$$
$$
P(\text{at least one error}) = 1 - 0.729 = 0.271
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each file is error-free with probability $1 - 0.1 = 0.9$. The files are independent, so by the **multiplication rule**, the probability that all three are error-free is $0.9^3 = 0.729$. The event “at least one error” is the complement of “no errors,” so its probability is $1 - 0.729 = 0.271$. Option A adds the three probabilities of $10\%$. But “an error in the first file” and “an error in the second file” can both occur, so their probabilities cannot simply be added. Option B gives the probability of the complement. Option E gives the probability of *exactly* one error. The correct answer is **D**.

</div>
