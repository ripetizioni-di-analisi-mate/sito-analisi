## Exercise 1

Replace debts of $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and $484\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years with a single payment in one year. The annual compound interest rate is $10\%$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
X&=400q^{1-0}+484q^{1-2} \\[6pt]
&=400q+484q^{-1} \\[6pt]
&=400\cdot1.1+\frac{484}{1.1} \\[6pt]
&=440+440=880\,\mathrm{EUR} \\[6pt]
V_{\mathrm{orig}}(0)&=400+\frac{484}{1.21}=800,\qquad V_{\mathrm{new}}(0)=\frac{880}{1.1}=800
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The replacement debt is due at time $1$, so value both original debts on that date. Their exponents are $1-0=1$ for today's debt and $1-2=-1$ for the debt in year two. Moving the first debt forward one year increases it to $440$ euros. Moving the second back one year reduces it to $440$ euros. The values coincide because $484=400\cdot1.21$, so the second debt is the accumulated value of $400$ euros over two years. Add the two amounts now that they refer to the same due date. The replacement payment is **880 euros**. At time zero the original debts are worth $400+\dfrac{484}{1.21}=800$ euros and the single payment is worth $\dfrac{880}{1.1}=800$ euros, confirming equivalence.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A supplier is owed $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in six months, $420\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in twelve months, and $441\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in eighteen months. Find the equivalent single payment at twelve months using an effective compound interest rate of $5\%$ per half-year.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(t_1,t_2,t_3)&=(1,2,3)\quad\text{in half-years},\qquad T=2 \\[6pt]
q&=1.05 \\[6pt]
X&=600q^{2-1}+420q^{2-2}+441q^{2-3} \\[6pt]
&=600\cdot1.05+420+\frac{441}{1.05} \\[6pt]
&=630+420+420=1470\,\mathrm{EUR}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Since the rate is semiannual, measure time in half-years. Six, twelve, and eighteen months are one, two, and three half-years, and the replacement payment is due at the end of half-year $T=2$. The one-period factor is $q=1.05$. The payment in month twelve already falls on the **focal date** and enters at face value. Accumulate the first payment for one period and discount the third for one period. The total is **1,470 euros**. The direction depends on the payment dates even when the time unit changes from years to months. To check, value everything at time $3$. The original debts are worth $600q^2+420q+441=1543.5$ euros and the single replacement payment accumulates to $1470q=1543.5$ euros.

</div>
