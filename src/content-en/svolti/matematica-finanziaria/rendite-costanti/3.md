## Exercise 1

Find the account balance immediately after the third of three year-end deposits of $200$ euros at an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$. The initial balance is zero.

$$
\begin{aligned}
M(3)&=200q^{3-1}+200q^{3-2}+200q^{3-3} \\[6pt]
&=200\cdot1.1^2+200\cdot1.1+200 \\[6pt]
&=200\cdot1.21+200\cdot1.1+200 \\[6pt]
&=242+220+200=662\,\mathrm{EUR}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
s_{\overline 3|0.1}&=\frac{1.1^3-1}{0.1}=\frac{1.331-1}{0.1}=3.31, \\[6pt]
M(3)&=200\cdot3.31=662\,\mathrm{EUR}, \\[6pt]
M(3)-3\cdot200&=662-600=62\,\mathrm{EUR}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The balance after the third deposit is $662$ euros.

<div class="spiegazione">

The equal deposits occur at times $1$, $2$, and $3$, so they form an ordinary annuity with $R=200$ euros and $n=3$. The balance is required immediately after the last deposit. Apply the **accumulated value of level deposits**, $M=R\,s_{\overline n|i}$.

The valuation date is time $3$. The first deposit is made at time $1$ and remains in the account for two years, contributing $200\cdot1.21=242$ euros. The second earns interest for one year and contributes $220$ euros. The last deposit is made on the valuation date and contributes $200$ euros.

Since $i=0.1\neq0$, the closed-form expression gives the factor $s_{\overline 3|0.1}=\dfrac{1.331-1}{0.1}=3.31$. Multiplying it by $200$ again gives $662$ euros. The balance exceeds the $600$ euros deposited by $62$ euros, reflecting the interest earned on the first two deposits, $42$ euros on the first and $20$ euros on the second.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Deposit $250$ euros at the beginning of each of the first three years in an account earning an effective annual interest rate of $20\%$. Find the balance at the end of year four, assuming a zero initial balance and no other transactions.

$$
\begin{aligned}
M(2)&=250(1.2^2+1.2+1) \\[6pt]
&=250(1.44+1.2+1) \\[6pt]
&=250\cdot3.64=910\,\mathrm{EUR}, \\[6pt]
M(4)&=M(2)\cdot1.2^2 \\[6pt]
&=910\cdot1.44=1310.40\,\mathrm{EUR}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
s_{\overline 3|0.2}&=\frac{1.2^3-1}{0.2}=\frac{0.728}{0.2}=3.64, \\[6pt]
M(3)&=250\cdot q\,s_{\overline 3|0.2}=250\cdot1.2\cdot3.64=1092\,\mathrm{EUR}, \\[6pt]
M(4)&=1092\cdot1.2=1310.40\,\mathrm{EUR}.
\end{aligned}
$$

At the end of the fourth year the balance is $1310.40$ euros.

<div class="spiegazione">

The three deposits are made at times $0,1,2$ because the first year begins at time $0$. The fourth year ends at time $4$. First find the balance at time $2$, when all deposits have been made. The first has earned interest for two years and the second for one year, so their factors are $1.44$ and $1.2$. The third has just been deposited. At the date of the last deposit the three amounts have the same accumulation pattern as an ordinary annuity, giving $M(2)=250\,s_{\overline 3|0.2}=910$ euros.

From time $2$ to time $4$ the funds remain invested with no further transactions. Accumulate the entire balance of $910$ euros for two years. Alternatively, use the annuity-due factor $q\,s_{\overline 3|0.2}$ to obtain the accumulated value at time $3$, one period after the last deposit. One more year of interest gives the same balance of $1310.40$ euros. Total deposits are $750$ euros, so the interest earned is $560.40$ euros.

</div>
