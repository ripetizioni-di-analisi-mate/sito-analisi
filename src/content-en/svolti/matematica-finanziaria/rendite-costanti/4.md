## Exercise 1

Two annual payments of $1331$ euros are due at times $3$ and $4$. Find the value of the entire stream at time $1$ and at time zero at an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$.

$$
v^2=\frac{100}{121}, \qquad v^3=\frac{1000}{1331}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
V(1)&=1331(v^2+v^3) \\[6pt]
&=1331\cdot\frac{100}{121}+1331\cdot\frac{1000}{1331} \\[6pt]
&=1100+1000=2100\,\mathrm{EUR}, \\[6pt]
V(0)&=2100v=\frac{21000}{11}\approx1909.09\,\mathrm{EUR}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
V(2)&=1331\,a_{\overline 2|0.1}=1331\cdot\frac{210}{121}=2310\,\mathrm{EUR}, \\[6pt]
V(0)&=v^2\,V(2)=\frac{100}{121}\cdot2310=\frac{21000}{11}\,\mathrm{EUR}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The value at time $1$ is $2100$ euros and the value at time zero is approximately $1909.09$ euros.

<div class="spiegazione">

Measure the time between the valuation date and each payment date. At time $1$ the first payment is two years away and the second is three years away, so the exponents of $v$ are $2$ and $3$. Since $1331=11^3$, both products are whole numbers, $1100$ and $1000$ euros.

The same sum can be found by valuing the ordinary annuity at time $2$, one period before the first payment, where it is worth $1331\,a_{\overline 2|0.1}=2310$ euros, and discounting it for one more year. Viewed from time zero, this is a **deferred ordinary annuity** with a deferral of $m=2$ periods because its payments fall at times $m+1=3$ and $m+2=4$. Its present value is therefore $v^2R\,a_{\overline 2|0.1}$, the result of discounting $2310$ euros for two years.

Once the entire stream has been valued at time $1$, multiplying by another factor of $v$ gives its value at time zero. Both approaches give $\dfrac{21000}{11}$ euros, or $1909.09$ euros to the nearest cent.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Three equal payments of $121$ euros are deferred, with the first due at time $2$. Find the value of the entire stream at time $4$, including the payment on that date, at an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(t_1,t_2,t_3)&=(2,3,4), \\[6pt]
V(4)&=121q^{4-2}+121q^{4-3}+121q^{4-4} \\[6pt]
&=121\cdot1.21+121\cdot1.1+121 \\[6pt]
&=146.41+133.10+121 \\[6pt]
&=400.51\,\mathrm{EUR}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
s_{\overline 3|0.1}&=\frac{1.1^3-1}{0.1}=3.31, \\[6pt]
V(4)&=121\,s_{\overline 3|0.1}=121\cdot3.31=400.51\,\mathrm{EUR}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The value at time $4$ is $400.51$ euros.

<div class="spiegazione">

The three payment dates are consecutive, starting at time $2$ and ending at time $4$. The valuation date is the date of the last payment. Accumulate the first two payments for two years and one year, using $1.21$ and $1.1$, respectively. Add the final payment at face value.

At time zero the stream is an ordinary annuity deferred for one period, with present value $v\,R\,a_{\overline 3|0.1}$. To value it at time $4$, multiply by $q^4$. Since $q^4v=q^3$ and $q^3a_{\overline 3|0.1}=s_{\overline 3|0.1}$, this gives the accumulated value $121\,s_{\overline 3|0.1}$. The factor $3.31$ again gives $400.51$ euros.

The deferral from time zero determines the payment dates. The exponent in each accumulation factor is the time from the corresponding payment date to time $4$.

</div>
