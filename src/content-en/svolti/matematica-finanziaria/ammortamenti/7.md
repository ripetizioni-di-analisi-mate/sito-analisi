## Exercise 1

An equal-principal loan of $4200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ has two annual installments and an interest rate of $5\%$. Find the interest-only and principal-only values at inception using the same valuation rate of $5\%$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_1=C_2&=\frac{4200}{2}=2100 \\[6pt]
I_1&=0.05\cdot4200=210,\qquad I_2=0.05\cdot2100=105\\[6pt]
U &= \frac{210}{1.05}+\frac{105}{1.05^2}
   =200+\frac{105}{1.1025}\\[6pt]
  &\approx 200+95.238\approx295.24\,\mathrm{EUR}\\[6pt]
N &= \frac{2100}{1.05}+\frac{2100}{1.05^2}
   =2000+\frac{2100}{1.1025}\\[6pt]
  &\approx 2000+1904.762\approx3904.76\,\mathrm{EUR}\\[6pt]
U+N &= 4200\,\mathrm{EUR}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Both principal portions are equal. In the first year interest accrues on $4200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and in the second only on the remaining $2100\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Discount the interest portions for the interest-only value and the principal portions for the principal-only value. In both sums the portion due at the end of year two is discounted for two periods. The two-year discount factor is $1.05^2=1.1025$, so at inception the second year's interest is worth approximately $95.24\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the second principal portion approximately $1904.76\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Since the valuation and contractual rates are equal, the two values sum to the original principal of $4200\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Indeed, their sum $U+N$ is the value of the full installments, which is $D_0$ at the contractual rate by the equation of value for the loan.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A level-payment loan of $2100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at an annual interest rate of $10\%$ is repaid in two installments of $1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Find the interest-only and principal-only values at an annual valuation rate of $21\%$. Verify their sum by valuing the full installments.

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_1 &= 0.10\cdot2100=210,\qquad C_1=1210-210=1000\\[6pt]
D_1 &= 2100-1000=1100\\[6pt]
I_2 &= 0.10\cdot1100=110,\qquad C_2=1210-110=1100.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
U&=\frac{210}{1.21}+\frac{110}{1.4641}
   \approx173.554+75.131\\[6pt]
 &\approx248.69\,\mathrm{EUR}\\[6pt]
N&=\frac{1000}{1.21}+\frac{1100}{1.4641}
   \approx826.446+751.315\\[6pt]
 &\approx1577.76\,\mathrm{EUR}\\[6pt]
V&=\frac{1210}{1.21}+\frac{1210}{1.4641}
   \approx1000+826.446\\[6pt]
 &\approx1826.45\,\mathrm{EUR}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The contract sets the installments using the rate of $10\%$. Use that rate to determine the interest portions and obtain the principal portions by subtraction. Use $21\%$ to discount each portion to the initial date. Divide the first-year portions by $1.21$ and the second-year portions by $1.21^2=1.4641$. Since discounting is linear in the amounts, the interest-only and principal-only values sum to the value of the full installments. To three decimal places, $248.685+1577.761=1826.446\,\mathrm{EUR}$, which agrees with discounting the two installments of $1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The market value is below the original principal because the valuation rate exceeds the rate used to set the installments.

</div>
