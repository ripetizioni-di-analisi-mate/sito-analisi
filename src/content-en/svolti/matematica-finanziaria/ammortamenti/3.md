## Exercise 1

A loan of $4800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is repaid in four annual installments with equal principal portions at an interest rate of $5\%$. Find the third installment and the total interest.

$$
\begin{aligned}
C &= \frac{4800}{4}=1200 \\[6pt]
D_2 &= 4800-2\cdot1200=2400 \\[6pt]
R_3 &= 1200+0.05\cdot2400=1320\,\mathrm{EUR} \\[6pt]
I_{\mathrm{tot}} &= 0.05(4800+3600+2400+1200) \\[6pt]
                 &= 0.05\cdot12000=600\,\mathrm{EUR}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_1&=240,\quad I_2=180,\quad I_3=120,\quad I_4=60 \\[6pt]
R_1&=1440,\quad R_2=1380,\quad R_3=1320,\quad R_4=1260.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Under equal-principal amortization the principal portion is $C=\dfrac{D_0}{n}$. Each of the four payments therefore repays $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ of principal. Two principal portions have been repaid by the beginning of year three, leaving a balance of $2400\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Add the interest of $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ on that balance to the third principal portion. The four opening balances are $4800$, $3600$, $2400$, and $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The corresponding interest charges are $240$, $180$, $120$, and $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so each annual installment is $0.05\cdot1200=60\,\mathrm{EUR}$ smaller than the previous one. Total interest is the sum of interest on the four opening balances. The first balance is the amount advanced and the last is a single principal portion, because only $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ remains at the beginning of the final year. The formula $iD_0\dfrac{n+1}{2}=0.05\cdot4800\cdot\dfrac{5}{2}=600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ gives the same total.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In a four-period equal-principal loan, the first installment is $1440\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the last $1320\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Find the original principal and the effective interest rate per period.

$$
\begin{aligned}
iC &= \frac{1440-1320}{3}=40 \\[6pt]
C &= 1320-40=1280 \\[6pt]
D_0 &= 4\cdot1280=5120\,\mathrm{EUR}\\[6pt]
i &= \frac{40}{1280}=0.03125=3.125\%.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_1 &= 0.03125\cdot5120=160 \\[6pt]
R_1 &= 1280+160=1440\,\mathrm{EUR}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

There are three changes from the first installment to the fourth. The opening balance falls by the same principal portion every period, so interest decreases by $iC$ each time. Thus $R_1-R_4=3iC$. Divide the difference between the first and last installments by three to get $40\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Exactly one principal portion remains at the beginning of the last period, so $40\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is also the interest in the last installment. Subtract it from that installment to get $C$. The original principal is four times $C$. Divide the last interest charge by the balance on which it accrues to find the rate. The opening balances are $5120$, $3840$, $2560$, and $1280\,\mathrm{EUR}$, giving installments of $1440$, $1400$, $1360$, and $1320\,\mathrm{EUR}$, in agreement with the data. In particular, the first installment is $1280$ plus $0.03125\cdot5120=160\,\mathrm{EUR}$.

</div>
