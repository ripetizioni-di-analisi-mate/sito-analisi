## Exercise 1

Construct the amortization schedule for a loan of $3300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ to be repaid in two equal annual installments at an effective interest rate of $20\%$. Find the total interest.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{1.2}+\frac{1}{1.2^2}
  &= \frac{30}{36}+\frac{25}{36}=\frac{55}{36} \\[6pt]
3300 &= \frac{R}{1.2}+\frac{R}{1.2^2}
      = \frac{55}{36}R \\[6pt]
R &= 3300\cdot\frac{36}{55}=2160.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_1 &= 0.20\cdot3300=660,\qquad C_1=2160-660=1500 \\[6pt]
D_1 &= 3300-1500=1800 \\[6pt]
I_2 &= 0.20\cdot1800=360,\qquad C_2=2160-360=1800.
\end{aligned}
$$

| Year-end | Opening balance | Interest | Principal portion | Payment | Closing balance |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 3300 | 660 | 1500 | 2160 | 1800 |
| 2 | 1800 | 360 | 1800 | 2160 | 0 |

$$
I_1+I_2=660+360=1020\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The interest rate is constant and the two installments are equal and paid in arrears, so the **level-payment theorem** applies, $R=\dfrac{D_0}{a_{\overline 2|0.2}}$. The annuity factor is the sum of the one- and two-year discount factors. With common denominator $36$ these are $\dfrac{30}{36}$ and $\dfrac{25}{36}$.

The present values of both installments must add up to the amount borrowed at time zero. In the first year the balance is still $3300\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so interest is $660\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The first installment therefore repays $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ of principal. In the second year interest accrues only on the remaining $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The second installment pays off the debt, and the principal portions satisfy $1500+1800=3300\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Total interest, $1020\,\mathrm{EUR}$, equals the installments paid, $2\cdot2160=4320\,\mathrm{EUR}$, less the original loan because the principal portions repay exactly the $3300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ advanced.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A principal of $1655\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is repaid in three equal semiannual installments in arrears. The nominal annual interest rate compounded twice yearly is $20\%$. Construct the amortization schedule and find the total interest.

$$
\begin{aligned}
i&=\frac{20\%}{2}=10\% \\[6pt]
\frac{1}{1.1}+\frac{1}{1.1^2}+\frac{1}{1.1^3}
  &= \frac{1.21+1.1+1}{1.331}=\frac{3310}{1331} \\[6pt]
1655 &= R\left(\frac{1}{1.1}+\frac{1}{1.1^2}
                     +\frac{1}{1.1^3}\right)\\[6pt]
R &= 1655\cdot \frac{1331}{3310}
   = 665.50.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_1 &= 0.10\cdot1655=165.50,\qquad C_1=665.50-165.50=500 \\[6pt]
I_2 &= 0.10\cdot1155=115.50,\qquad C_2=665.50-115.50=550 \\[6pt]
I_3 &= 0.10\cdot605=60.50,\qquad C_3=665.50-60.50=605.
\end{aligned}
$$

| Half-year end | Opening balance | Interest | Principal portion | Payment | Closing balance |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 1655 | 165.50 | 500 | 665.50 | 1155 |
| 2 | 1155 | 115.50 | 550 | 665.50 | 605 |
| 3 | 605 | 60.50 | 605 | 665.50 | 0 |

$$
I_{\mathrm{tot}}=3\cdot665.50-1655=341.50\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Divide the nominal annual rate by two to obtain the effective semiannual rate. With common denominator $1.1^3=1.331$ the first discount factor is $\dfrac{1.21}{1.331}$, the second $\dfrac{1.1}{1.331}$, and the third $\dfrac{1}{1.331}$. Since $1655=\dfrac{3310}{2}$, the installment is $\dfrac{1331}{2}=665.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The payments fall one, two, and three half-years after the loan is issued, so the discount exponents count half-years. In each row, interest accrues on the previous row's closing balance. For example, in the second half-year the balance of $1155\,\mathrm{EUR}$ earns $115.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in interest. A payment of $665.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ therefore repays $550\,\mathrm{EUR}$ of principal. The last payment pays off the loan, and the interest column sums to $3R-D_0$.

</div>
