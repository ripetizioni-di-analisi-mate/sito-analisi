## Exercise 1

Find the interest and accumulated value of $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ invested at $2.5\%$ simple annual interest for seven months.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t&=\frac{7}{12}\,\mathrm{years}\\[6pt]
Ci&=1800\cdot 0.025=45\,\mathrm{EUR}\\[6pt]
I&=Cit=45\cdot\frac{7}{12}=26.25\,\mathrm{EUR}\\[6pt]
M&=1800+26.25=1826.25\,\mathrm{EUR}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
M=C(1+it)=1800\left(1+\frac{7}{480}\right)=1800\cdot\frac{487}{480}=1826.25\,\mathrm{EUR}
$$

The outflow of $1800$ euros occurs at time $0$ and the inflow of $1826.25$ euros occurs seven months later."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The rate $i=0.025$ is annual, so express the term in years. Since a year has twelve months, seven months is $t=\dfrac{7}{12}$ of a year.

Under **simple interest**, interest always accrues on the original principal, which remains $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ throughout the term. The product $Ci=45\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the interest for one full year. Since interest is proportional to time, the deposit earns $\dfrac{7}{12}$ of that amount in seven months, or $26.25\,\mathrm{EUR}$. This agrees with the formula $I=Cit$.

Add interest to the original principal to obtain the accumulated value $1826.25\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Alternatively, multiply the principal by the accumulation factor $1+it$, where $it=0.025\cdot\dfrac{7}{12}=\dfrac{7}{480}$. This second calculation checks the result using $M=C(1+it)$ directly.

On the depositor's timeline the initial deposit is an outflow and the repayment seven months later is an inflow. Over those seven months the deposit earns **interest of $26.25\,\mathrm{EUR}$** and reaches an **accumulated value of $1826.25\,\mathrm{EUR}$**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In how many days does $6000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ grow to $6060\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at $4\%$ simple annual interest? Use the actual number of days and a $360$-day year.

$$
\begin{aligned}
I&=M-C=6060-6000=60\,\mathrm{EUR}\\[6pt]
Ci&=6000\cdot0.04=240\,\mathrm{EUR}\\[6pt]
t&=\frac{I}{Ci}=\frac{60}{240}=\frac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{year}\\[6pt]
N&=360\,t=360\cdot\frac{1}{4}=90\,\mathrm{days}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The amount $6060\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the accumulated value and includes both principal and interest. Subtracting the original principal gives the interest required, $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$.

At $4\%$ per year, $6000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ earns $Ci=240\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in a full year. Under simple interest, $I=Cit$, so dividing the required interest by the annual interest gives the term. The ratio $\dfrac{60}{240}$ equals $\dfrac{1}{4}$, one quarter of a year.

Actual/360 uses the actual number of days and a $360$-day year. The term in years is $t=\dfrac{N}{360}$, where $N$ is the actual number of days. Solving for $N$ gives $N=360\,t$. For $t=\dfrac{1}{4}$ the result is $N=90$.

To check, the interest earned in $90$ days under the same convention is $6000\cdot0.04\cdot\dfrac{90}{360}=60\,\mathrm{EUR}$, exactly the amount required. The principal therefore reaches $6060\,\mathrm{EUR}$ **after $90$ days**.

</div>
