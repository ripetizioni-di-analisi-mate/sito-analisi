## Exercise 1

A bond is bought for $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. After one year the holder receives a coupon of $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and immediately sells the bond. Only one payment of $1155\,\mathrm{EUR}$ remains, due one year later. At sale the market requires an effective annual yield of $10\%$. Calculate the realized return.

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_0&=1000,\qquad W_1=60,\\[6pt]
P_1&=\frac{1155}{1.1}=1050,\\[6pt]
P_1+W_1&=1050+60=1110,\\[6pt]
h&=\frac{P_1+W_1-P_0}{P_0}=\frac{1110-1000}{1000}=0.11.
\end{aligned}
$$

The realized return is $11\%$.

<div class="spiegazione">

Use the definition of the **realized return** $h=\dfrac{P_H+W_H-P_0}{P_0}$ with a horizon of $H=1$ year. The sale occurs immediately after the coupon payment, so the coupon is included in $W_1$ and the price $P_1$ reflects only the remaining payment of $1155$ euros.

The resale price is determined on the sale date using the market yield at that time. The seller has just received the coupon, so the buyer pays only for the remaining future cash flow. The seller has the sale proceeds and the coupon at the same time. The capital gain is $50$ euros and the coupon adds $60$ euros, giving $110$ euros on an initial investment of $1000$ euros. Since the horizon is one year, the equivalent effective annual return $(1+h)^{\frac{1}{H}}-1$ equals $h$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A bond bought for $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ pays two annual coupons of $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and repays $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ with the second. The first coupon is reinvested at an effective rate of $4\%$ for one year. Calculate the realized effective annual return if the bond is held to maturity.

$$
\begin{aligned}
50\cdot1.04^{2-1}&=52,\\[6pt]
W_2&=52+50=102,\\[6pt]
M+W_2&=1000+102=1102,\\[6pt]
1+y_{\mathrm{real}}&=\left(\frac{1102}{1000}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}=\sqrt{1.102}\approx1.049762,\\[6pt]
y_{\mathrm{real}}&=\sqrt{1.102}-1\approx0.049762.
\end{aligned}
$$

The realized effective annual return is approximately $4.9762\%$.

<div class="spiegazione">

To compare terminal wealth with the initial price, value all receipts at the end of year two. A coupon $C_k$ received at time $t_k$ and reinvested at rate $r$ contributes $C_k(1+r)^{H-t_k}$. With $C_1=50$, $t_1=1$, $H=2$, and $r=0.04$, the first coupon is worth $52$ euros at maturity. The second coupon and principal are due in year two, so they enter at their full amounts without compounding. Since the bond is held to redemption, the principal repaid, $M=1000$ euros, takes the place of a sale price.

The overall growth factor is $1+h=1.102$. The equivalent effective annual return is $(1+h)^{\frac{1}{H}}-1$, so for $H=2$ take the square root. The yield to maturity is $5\%$ because the bond was purchased at par. The realized return is lower because reinvestment produced $52$ euros instead of the $52.50$ euros that reinvesting at the yield to maturity would have produced.

</div>
