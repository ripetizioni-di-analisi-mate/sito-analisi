## Exercise 1

A contract pays $220$ euros at time $1$, $363$ euros at time $2$, and $1464.10$ euros at time $4$. Find its value at time $0$ at an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$.

$$
q=1.1,\qquad q^2=1.21,\qquad q^4=1.4641
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_0&=220v+363v^2+1464.10v^4\\[6pt]
&=\frac{220}{1.1}+\frac{363}{1.21}+\frac{1464.10}{1.4641}\\[6pt]
&=200+300+1000=1500.
\end{aligned}
$$

The present value is $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$.

<div class="spiegazione">

Apply the formula for the **value of a variable annuity**, $V_0=\sum R_kv^{t_k}$, with $v=\dfrac{1}{1.1}$. In each term the exponent of $v$ is the payment date, measured in years from today.

The third payment falls at time $4$, so its discount factor is $v^4$. Having no payment at time $3$ is equivalent to scheduling a payment of zero on that date. The exponent $4$ therefore stays the same even though this is only the third payment in the list.

Successive multiplication gives the powers of $1.1$, so $1.1^2=1.21$ and $1.1^4=1.21^2=1.4641$. The first two quotients equal $200$ and $300$ euros because $220=200\cdot1.1$ and $363=300\cdot1.21$. The third payment was already $1000$ euros multiplied by $1.1^4$, making its present value exactly $1000$ euros.

The discounted amounts all refer to today and can therefore be added. The contract is worth **$1500$ euros at time $0$**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An account receives deposits of $800$ euros at time $1$, $500$ euros at time $2$, and $300$ euros at time $4$. The effective annual interest rate is $5\%$. Find the accumulated value at time $4$ immediately after the last deposit.

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=4.6 ymin=0 ymax=1000 nomi="t,euro" vettori="1,0,1,800;2,0,2,500;4,0,4,300" unicolore=1 etichette="1.13,820,800;2.13,520,500;4.13,320,300" titolo="Deposits of $800$, $500$, and $300$ euros at times $1$, $2$, and $4$, measured in years."}

$$
q=1.05,\qquad q^2=1.1025,\qquad q^3=1.157625
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
M_4&=800q^3+500q^2+300\\[6pt]
&=800\cdot1.157625+500\cdot1.1025+300\\[6pt]
&=926.10+551.25+300=1777.35.
\end{aligned}
$$

The accumulated value is $1777.35\,\mathrm{EUR}$.

<div class="spiegazione">

Find the accumulated value at time $T=4$ using $M_T=\sum R_kq^{T-t_k}$. The exponent of $q$ is the time each deposit spends in the account. The deposit at time $1$ remains invested for $4-1=3$ years, earns three years of interest, and has accumulation factor $q^3$. The deposit of $500$ euros at time $2$ earns interest for $4-2=2$ years. The final $300$ euros are deposited at time $4$, the valuation date itself, so their factor is $q^0=1$.

The products $800\cdot1.157625=926.10$ and $500\cdot1.1025=551.25$ are exact to the cent. The sum of the three contributions gives an accumulated value of **$1777.35$ euros**.

To check the exponents, find the present value of the three deposits and multiply by $1.05^4$, using $M_4=q^4V_0$.

</div>
