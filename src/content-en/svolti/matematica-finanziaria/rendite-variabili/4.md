## Exercise 1

A perpetuity pays $420$ euros two years from today. Each subsequent annual payment is $2\%$ larger than the previous one. Find the present value at an effective annual interest rate of $5\%$.

::grafico{assi=0 unicolore=1 legenda=0 xmin=-0.5 xmax=6.4 ymin=-0.45 ymax=1.75 linee="-0.3,0 5.5,0;5.38,0.06 5.5,0 5.38,-0.06;0,-0.04 0,0.04;1,-0.04 1,0.04;2,-0.04 2,0.04;3,-0.04 3,0.04;4,-0.04 4,0.04;5,-0.04 5,0.04" vettori="2,0,2,1.319;3,0,3,1.346;4,0,4,1.373;5,0,5,1.4" etichette="1.88,1.439,420;2.76,1.466,428.40;3.76,1.493,436.97;4.76,1.52,445.71;-0.04,-0.2,0;0.96,-0.2,1;1.96,-0.2,2;2.96,-0.2,3;3.96,-0.2,4;4.96,-0.2,5;5.55,-0.03,t (years);5.55,0.7,…" titolo="The first payments of the perpetuity, in euros. The first is due at time $2$, and each payment is $2\%$ larger than the previous one in percentage terms. The dots indicate that the payments continue indefinitely."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
i-g&=0.05-0.02=0.03>0,\\[6pt]
V_1&=\frac{420}{0.03}=14000,\\[6pt]
V_0&=\frac{14000}{1.05}=\frac{40000}{3}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
V_0=\frac{R_1v^m}{i-g}=\frac{420}{0.03\cdot1.05}\qquad(m=1)
$$

The present value is approximately $13333.33\,\mathrm{EUR}$.

<div class="spiegazione">

This is a **growing perpetuity** whose first payment is $R_1=420$ euros and whose growth rate is $g=2\%$. Since the valuation rate exceeds the growth rate, $i-g=0.03>0$, the theorem applies. The present values form a convergent geometric series with common ratio $\rho=\dfrac{1.02}{1.05}<1$.

The formula $\dfrac{R_1}{i-g}$ gives the value one period before the first payment. Since the first payment is due at time $2$, the quotient of $\dfrac{420}{0.03}=14000$ euros gives the value at time $1$.

To obtain the value at time $0$, discount for one year by dividing by $1.05$, giving $\dfrac{40000}{3}$ euros. The deferral is $m=1$, so the same operation can be written using the theorem's formula $\dfrac{R_1v^m}{i-g}$.

In a direct sum, the first present value would be $420v^2$, with each subsequent term obtained by multiplying by $\rho$. The present value is **approximately $13333.33$ euros**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A perpetuity is worth $5000$ euros today, pays its first installment one year from now, and increases by $3\%$ per payment. The effective annual interest rate is $8\%$. Find the remaining value at time $1$ immediately after the first payment is received.

$$
\begin{aligned}
5000&=\frac{R_1}{0.08-0.03}=\frac{R_1}{0.05},\\[6pt]
R_1&=5000\cdot0.05=250,\\[6pt]
R_2&=250\cdot1.03=257.50,\\[6pt]
V_1^{+}&=\frac{257.50}{0.08-0.03}=5150.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
5000\cdot1.08-250=5400-250=5150
$$

The remaining value is $5150\,\mathrm{EUR}$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The interest rate $i=8\%$ exceeds the growth rate $g=3\%$, so the perpetuity has a finite value given by $\dfrac{R_1}{i-g}$. Solve this equation with $V_0=5000$ euros to find the first payment, $R_1=250$ euros.

Immediately after that payment, the next payment of $R_2=257.50$ euros is due one year after time $1$. Subsequent payments continue to grow by $3\%$ and the stream continues indefinitely. At time $1$, apply the perpetuity formula using the next payment $R_2$ to obtain $5150$ euros.

Alternatively, accumulate $5000$ euros for one year and subtract the $250$ euros just received. Immediately before that payment, the annuity is worth $5000\cdot1.08=5400$ euros. Its remaining value exceeds its initial value by $3\%$ because each remaining payment equals the corresponding initial payment multiplied by $1.03$. The remaining value is **$5150$ euros**.

</div>
