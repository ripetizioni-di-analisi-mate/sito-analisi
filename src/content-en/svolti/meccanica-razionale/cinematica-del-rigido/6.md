## Exercise 1

A rigid body moves in space. At the instant considered its point $A$, coinciding with the origin, has velocity $\vec{v}_A = v\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{k})$ and the angular velocity of the body is $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\hat{k}$, with $v > 0$ and $\omega > 0$. Classify the state of motion, determine the Mozzi axis, the minimum velocity and the pitch.


::struttura{linee="0,0 3,0;0,0 1.35,0.96;0,0 0,3" tratteggi="0.9,-0.9 0.9,2.6" punti="0,0,A;0.9,0.64,P*,centro" frecce="0,0,1.5,1.5,v_A;0.9,0.64,0.9,1.74,v_min;-1,0.6,-1,1.8,ω" etichette="3.1,-0.05,x;1.45,1.05,y;0.05,3.1,z;1.1,2.4,Mozzi axis" riferimento=0 titolo="Axonometric view: the Mozzi axis is the line $x = 0$, $y = \dfrac{v}{\omega}$ parallel to $\vec{\omega}$"}

$$
I = \vec{\omega} \cdot \vec{v}_A = \omega\,v \neq 0
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
\text{helical state of motion}
$$
$$
P^* = A + \frac{\vec{\omega} \wedge \vec{v}_A}{|\vec{\omega}|^2}
= \frac{\omega v\,(\hat{k} \wedge \hat{\imath}) + \omega v\,(\hat{k} \wedge \hat{k})}{\omega^2}
= \frac{v}{\omega}\;\hat{\jmath}
$$

Mozzi axis, the line through $P^*$ parallel to $\vec{\omega}$:
$$
\begin{cases}
x = 0 \\[4pt]
y = \dfrac{v}{\omega}
\end{cases}
$$
$$
\vec{v}_{\min} = \frac{I}{|\vec{\omega}|^2}\;\vec{\omega} = v\,\hat{k} ,
\qquad
p = \frac{I}{|\vec{\omega}|^2} = \frac{v}{\omega}
$$

Check by the fundamental formula applied to $P^*$:
$$
\vec{v}_{P^*} = \vec{v}_A + \vec{\omega} \wedge (P^* - A)
= v\,\hat{\imath} + v\,\hat{k} + \omega\,\hat{k} \wedge \frac{v}{\omega}\,\hat{\jmath}
= v\,\hat{\imath} + v\,\hat{k} - v\,\hat{\imath}
= v\,\hat{k}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **kinematic invariant** is the scalar product of the angular velocity by the velocity of any point of the body. Here it equals $\omega v$, different from zero, therefore no point has zero velocity and the state of motion is helical: the body turns about an axis and at the same time advances along it.

The point $P^*$ of the axis is computed by the formula of Mozzi's theorem. In the product $\vec{\omega} \wedge \vec{v}_A$ the term with $\hat{k} \wedge \hat{k}$ vanishes and $\omega v\,\hat{\jmath}$ remains, which divided by $|\vec{\omega}|^2 = \omega^2$ gives $\dfrac{v}{\omega}\,\hat{\jmath}$. The Mozzi axis is the line through $P^*$ parallel to $\hat{k}$, hence it is the vertical line with equations $x = 0$ and $y = \dfrac{v}{\omega}$.

The minimum velocity is the component of $\vec{v}_A$ along $\vec{\omega}$, that is, $v\,\hat{k}$: the rotation does not change it, because $\vec{\omega} \wedge (P - P^*)$ is always orthogonal to $\vec{\omega}$. The pitch $p = \dfrac{v}{\omega}$ is the advance along the axis for every radian of rotation.

The check computes the velocity of $P^*$ directly by the fundamental formula: the component along $\hat{\imath}$ cancels and only $v\,\hat{k}$ remains, parallel to $\vec{\omega}$ as Mozzi's theorem requires.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A rigid body has, at the instant considered, angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,(2\,\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath} + 2\,\hat{k})$, and its point $A$, coinciding with the origin, has velocity $\vec{v}_A = v\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k})$. Classify the state of motion, determine the Mozzi axis, the minimum velocity and the pitch.


::struttura{linee="0,0 3,0;0,0 1.35,0.96;0,0 0,2.8" tratteggi="-2.55,-0.68 1.33,3.52" punti="0,0,A;-1,1,P*,centro" frecce="0,0,1.45,1.32,v_A;-1,1,-0.17,1.9,v_min" etichette="3.1,-0.05,x;1.45,1.05,y;0.05,2.9,z;2.2,2.9,Mozzi axis" riferimento=0 titolo="Axonometric view: the Mozzi axis passes through $P^*$ with the direction of $\vec{\omega}$"}

$$
|\vec{\omega}|^2 = \omega^2\,(4 + 1 + 4) = 9\,\omega^2 ,
\qquad
I = \vec{\omega} \cdot \vec{v}_A = \omega v\,(2 - 1 + 2) = 3\,\omega v \neq 0
$$
$$
\vec{\omega} \wedge \vec{v}_A = \omega v\,\big( (-1)\cdot 1 - 2 \cdot 1,\;\; 2 \cdot 1 - 2 \cdot 1,\;\; 2 \cdot 1 - (-1)\cdot 1 \big)
= \omega v\,\big( -3\,\hat{\imath} + 3\,\hat{k} \big)
$$
$$
P^* = A + \frac{\vec{\omega} \wedge \vec{v}_A}{|\vec{\omega}|^2}
= \frac{\omega v\,(-3\,\hat{\imath} + 3\,\hat{k})}{9\,\omega^2}
= -\,\frac{v}{3\omega}\;\hat{\imath} + \frac{v}{3\omega}\;\hat{k}
$$

Mozzi axis, in parametric form with $t \in \mathbb{R}$:
$$
\begin{cases}
x = -\dfrac{v}{3\omega} + 2t \\[6pt]
y = -\,t \\[6pt]
z = \dfrac{v}{3\omega} + 2t
\end{cases}
$$
$$
\vec{v}_{\min} = \frac{I}{|\vec{\omega}|^2}\;\vec{\omega} = \frac{3\omega v}{9\omega^2}\;\omega\,(2\,\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath} + 2\,\hat{k}) = \frac{v}{3}\,\big( 2\,\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath} + 2\,\hat{k} \big)
$$
$$
|\vec{v}_{\min}| = \frac{|I|}{|\vec{\omega}|} = \frac{3\omega v}{3\omega} = v ,
\qquad
p = \frac{I}{|\vec{\omega}|^2} = \frac{v}{3\omega}
$$

Check by the fundamental formula applied to $P^*$:
$$
P^* - A = \frac{v}{3\omega}\,(-1,\, 0,\, 1) ,
\qquad
\vec{\omega} \wedge (P^* - A) = \frac{v}{3}\,\big[ (2,-1,2) \wedge (-1,0,1) \big] = \frac{v}{3}\,(-1,\, -4,\, -1)
$$
$$
\vec{v}_{P^*} = v\,(1,1,1) + \frac{v}{3}\,(-1,-4,-1) = \frac{v}{3}\,(2,-1,2) = \vec{v}_{\min}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The invariant is positive, hence the state of motion is **helical** and the body advances in the sense of $\vec{\omega}$.

The cross product $\vec{\omega} \wedge \vec{v}_A$ is computed component by component: the first equals $(-1)(1) - (2)(1) = -3$, the second $(2)(1) - (2)(1) = 0$, the third $(2)(1) - (-1)(1) = 3$. Divided by $9\omega^2$ it gives the point $P^*$.

The Mozzi axis is the line through $P^*$ with the direction of $\vec{\omega}$, that is, $(2, -1, 2)$; the parametric equations are written by adding $t$ times that direction to the coordinates of $P^*$.

The minimum velocity is the projection of $\vec{v}_A$ on $\vec{\omega}$, and its magnitude is obtained at once as $\dfrac{|I|}{|\vec{\omega}|}$. The pitch equals $\dfrac{v}{3\omega}$.

The check computes $\vec{\omega} \wedge (P^* - A)$ with $P^* - A$ proportional to $(-1, 0, 1)$, obtaining $\dfrac{v}{3}\,(-1, -4, -1)$; added to $\vec{v}_A$ it gives back exactly $\vec{v}_{\min}$, hence the point found belongs to the axis.

</div>

## Exercise 3

For a rigid body moving in space the velocities of three points are known, measured in $\mathrm{m/s}$:
$$
\vec{v}_A = (0,\, -2,\, 2) ,
\qquad
\vec{v}_B = (-2,\, 0,\, 1) ,
\qquad
\vec{v}_C = (2,\, -3,\, 2) ,
$$
with $A = (0,0,0)$, $B = (1,1,0)$ and $C = (0,0,1)$ in meters. Determine the angular velocity, the kinematic invariant, the type of state of motion and the Mozzi axis.

::struttura{linee="0,0 2.6,0;0,0 1.35,0.96;0,0 0,2.4" tratteggi="0.05,-1.32 2.9,2.64" punti="0,0,A;1.45,0.32,B;0,1,C;1,0,Q,centro" frecce="0,0,-0.54,0.82,v_A;1.45,0.32,0.61,0.74,v_B;0,1,0.39,1.62,v_C" etichette="2.7,-0.05,x;1.45,1.05,y;0.05,2.5,z;2,2.3,axis" riferimento=0 titolo="Axonometric view: the velocities of the three points and the axis of instantaneous rotation through $Q = (1,0,0)$"}

Having set $\vec{\omega} = (p, q, r)$, from the fundamental formula between $A$ and $B$:
$$
\vec{v}_B - \vec{v}_A = \vec{\omega} \wedge (B - A)
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
(-2,\, 2,\, -1) = (p,q,r) \wedge (1,1,0) = (-r,\; r,\; p - q)
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
-r = -2 \\[4pt]
r = 2 \\[4pt]
p - q = -1
\end{cases}
$$

From the fundamental formula between $A$ and $C$:
$$
\vec{v}_C - \vec{v}_A = \vec{\omega} \wedge (C - A)
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
(2,\, -1,\, 0) = (p,q,r) \wedge (0,0,1) = (q,\; -p,\; 0)
$$
$$
q = 2 ,
\qquad
p = 1 ,
\qquad
\vec{\omega} = (1,\, 2,\, 2)\;\mathrm{rad/s} ,
\qquad
|\vec{\omega}| = 3\;\mathrm{rad/s}
$$
$$
I = \vec{\omega} \cdot \vec{v}_A = 0 - 4 + 4 = 0
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
\text{rotational state of motion}
$$
$$
P^* = A + \frac{\vec{\omega} \wedge \vec{v}_A}{|\vec{\omega}|^2}
= \frac{(2 \cdot 2 - 2 \cdot (-2),\;\; 2 \cdot 0 - 1 \cdot 2,\;\; 1 \cdot (-2) - 2 \cdot 0)}{9}
= \left( \frac{8}{9},\, -\frac{2}{9},\, -\frac{2}{9} \right)\mathrm{m}
$$

Axis of instantaneous rotation:
$$
\begin{cases}
x = \dfrac{8}{9} + t \\[6pt]
y = -\dfrac{2}{9} + 2t \\[6pt]
z = -\dfrac{2}{9} + 2t
\end{cases}
$$

Check: the point $Q = (1, 0, 0)$ is obtained for $t = \dfrac{1}{9}$ and must have zero velocity.
$$
\vec{v}_Q = \vec{v}_A + \vec{\omega} \wedge (Q - A) = (0,-2,2) + (1,2,2) \wedge (1,0,0) = (0,-2,2) + (0,2,-2) = (0,0,0)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The velocities of two points differ by $\vec{\omega} \wedge (B-A)$, therefore every pair of points gives three scalar equations in the three unknowns $p$, $q$, $r$. The pair $A$, $B$ determines $r$ and the relation $p - q = -1$; the pair $A$, $C$ determines $q$ and $p$. The value $r = 2$ appears in two equations with the same result, and this confirms that the data are compatible with a rigid motion.

The invariant turns out to be **zero**, hence there exists a line of points at rest and the state of motion is rotational. Note that equiprojectivity is satisfied as well: $(\vec{v}_B - \vec{v}_A) \cdot (B-A) = -2 + 2 + 0 = 0$.

The axis of instantaneous rotation is found by the same formula of Mozzi's theorem; since $I = 0$, its points have zero velocity instead of velocity parallel to $\vec{\omega}$.

The check chooses the point $Q = (1,0,0)$ of the axis, obtained for $t = \dfrac{1}{9}$, and computes its velocity by the fundamental formula: it turns out to be zero, as it must be for a point of the axis of instantaneous rotation. The point $Q$ is more convenient than $P^*$ because it has integer coordinates, and the axis can be described as the line through $(1,0,0)$ with direction $(1,2,2)$.

</div>
