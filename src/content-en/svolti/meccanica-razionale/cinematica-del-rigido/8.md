## Exercise 1

The homogeneous disk in the figure, of radius $R$, is hinged to the ground at its center $O$ and turns in the vertical plane with angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k}$. An inextensible string is wound on the rim, stretched along the vertical line through the rightmost point $Q$ of the rim, and supports a body $S$ that translates vertically. Determine the velocity and the acceleration of $S$ in terms of $\dot{\vartheta}$ and $\ddot{\vartheta}$.

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,1.4" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" nodi="O,0,0,-;Q,1.4,0,-;S,1.4,-2.5,-" catene="Q-S" punti="0,0,O;1.4,0,Q" linee="1.1,-2.5 1.7,-2.5 1.7,-3.1 1.1,-3.1 1.1,-2.5" coppie="-0.6 0.6,1,ϑ" etichette="1.9,-2.8,S" quote="0 0,1.4 0,R,-0.35" titolo="Pulley of radius $R$ with an inextensible string and the body $S$ hanging"}

The point $Q$ of the rim and the body $S$ are connected by a stretched vertical inextensible string, hence they have the same vertical component of the velocity:
$$
\vec{v}_Q = \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge (R\,\hat{\imath}) = R\dot{\vartheta}\;\hat{\jmath}
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
\vec{v}_S = R\dot{\vartheta}\;\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
\vec{a}_S = R\ddot{\vartheta}\;\hat{\jmath}
$$

Check by the length of the string: the point of tangency is fixed at height $0$, hence, denoting by $y_S$ the height of $S$ and by $L$ the length of the stretched portion,
$$
y_S = -\,L ,
\qquad
L = L_0 - R\vartheta
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
\dot{y}_S = R\dot{\vartheta}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The disk turns about the fixed hinge $O$, therefore the velocity of the point $Q$ is computed by the fundamental formula and equals $R\dot{\vartheta}$, directed vertically because $OQ$ is horizontal.

The string is **inextensible** and stretched, hence its two endpoints have the same component of velocity along the string; the stretched portion is vertical and the body $S$ translates vertically, therefore $\vec{v}_S$ coincides with $\vec{v}_Q$. Differentiating again one obtains the acceleration.

The check starts from the length of the string: the stretched portion shortens by $R\,d\vartheta$ when the disk turns by $d\vartheta$ in the counterclockwise sense, because that arc winds onto the rim. The height of $S$ therefore increases by $R\,d\vartheta$, and differentiating one recovers $\dot{y}_S = R\dot{\vartheta}$. With $\dot{\vartheta} > 0$ the body **rises**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The system in the figure moves in the vertical plane: the disk of radius $R$ and center $G$ rolls without slipping on the fixed horizontal guide; at the center $G$ the rod $GB$ of length $\ell$ is hinged, and its endpoint $B$ is constrained by a slider to run on the fixed vertical guide passing through $O$. Let $\beta$ denote the angle between the rod and the vertical. Determine the angular velocities of the disk and of the rod and the velocity of $B$ in terms of $\dot{s}$, where $s$ is the abscissa of $G$.

::struttura{cerchi="1.5,1,1" linee="-0.4,0 4.4,0;0,-0.4 0,4.2" aste="G-B" nodi="G,1.5,1,-;B,0,3.6" vincoli="B,carrello,180" punti="1.5,1,G,baricentro;1.5,0,C,centro;0,0,O;1.5,3.6,C_a,centro" tratteggi="1.5,1 1.5,3.6;0,3.6 1.5,3.6" etichette="1.15,1.9,β" quote="0 0,1.5 0,s,0.4" titolo="Rolling disk with the rod $GB$ hinged at its center and the endpoint $B$ on a vertical guide"}

$$
G - O = s\,\hat{\imath} + R\,\hat{\jmath} ,
\qquad
B - O = (R + \ell\cos\beta)\,\hat{\jmath} ,
\qquad
s = \ell\sin\beta
$$
$$
\dot{s} = \ell\dot{\beta}\cos\beta
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
\dot{\beta} = \frac{\dot{s}}{\ell\cos\beta}
$$
$$
\vec{\omega}_{\text{disk}} = -\,\frac{\dot{s}}{R}\;\hat{k} ,
\qquad
\vec{\omega}_{\text{rod}} = \dot{\beta}\;\hat{k} = \frac{\dot{s}}{\ell\cos\beta}\;\hat{k}
$$
$$
\vec{v}_B = -\,\ell\dot{\beta}\sin\beta\;\hat{\jmath} = -\,\dot{s}\tan\beta\;\hat{\jmath}
$$

Instantaneous center of rotation of the rod, intersection of the vertical line through $G$ with the horizontal line through $B$:
$$
C_{\text{rod}} - O = \ell\sin\beta\;\hat{\imath} + (R + \ell\cos\beta)\,\hat{\jmath}
$$

Check by the distance from the instantaneous center of rotation:
$$
|G - C_{\text{rod}}| = \ell\cos\beta ,
\qquad
|\vec{\omega}_{\text{rod}}|\,|G - C_{\text{rod}}| = \frac{|\dot{s}|}{\ell\cos\beta}\,\ell\cos\beta = |\dot{s}| = |\vec{v}_G|
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The disk rolls without slipping, hence its center stays at height $R$ and its angular velocity equals $-\dfrac{\dot{s}}{R}$, with the sign corresponding to the clockwise rotation when the disk advances to the right.

The rod has one endpoint at $G$ and the other on the vertical guide, therefore its horizontal projection is $\ell\sin\beta$ and must coincide with $s$. This relation reduces the system to **a single degree of freedom**. Differentiating it one obtains $\dot{\beta}$ in terms of $\dot{s}$. The direction going from $G$ toward $B$ forms with the $x$ axis the angle $\dfrac{\pi}{2} + \beta$, whose derivative is $\dot{\beta}$, therefore $\vec{\omega}_{\text{rod}} = \dot{\beta}\,\hat{k}$, and an increasing $\beta$ corresponds to a counterclockwise rotation.

The velocity of $B$ is obtained by differentiating its height. It is directed downward when $\dot{s} > 0$ and $0 < \beta < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, since the angle $\beta$ increases and the height of $B$ decreases. The magnitude tends to infinity for $\beta \to \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, because in that configuration the rod is horizontal and $\cos\beta$ vanishes.

The instantaneous center of rotation of the rod is constructed from the directions of the velocities of the two endpoints: $\vec{v}_G$ is horizontal and $\vec{v}_B$ is vertical, hence the center is the fourth vertex of the rectangle having $G$ and $B$ as opposite vertices. The check verifies that the product of the angular velocity of the rod by the distance $|G - C_{\text{rod}}| = \ell\cos\beta$ gives back $|\vec{v}_G| = |\dot{s}|$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The system in the figure is formed by the disk of radius $R$ and center $G$, which rolls without slipping on the fixed horizontal guide, and by the rod $GB$ of length $\ell$, hinged at $G$ and free to turn in the vertical plane. Let $s$ denote the abscissa of $G$ and $\vartheta$ the angle between the rod and the downward vertical. Determine the velocity of $B$, the angular velocities of the two bodies and the instantaneous center of rotation of the rod.

::struttura{cerchi="2,1.4,1.4" linee="-0.3,0 5.4,0" aste="G-B" nodi="G,2,1.4,-;B,3.46,0.18" punti="2,1.4,G,baricentro;2,0,C,centro;3.46,0.18" tratteggi="2,1.4 2,0.25" etichette="2.3,0.8,ϑ" frecce="2,1.4,2.9,1.4,v_G" quote="0 0,2 0,s,0.4" titolo="Rolling disk with the rod $GB$ hinged at its center: two degrees of freedom, $s$ and $\vartheta$"}

$$
G - O = s\,\hat{\imath} + R\,\hat{\jmath} ,
\qquad
B - O = (s + \ell\sin\vartheta)\,\hat{\imath} + (R - \ell\cos\vartheta)\,\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
\vec{v}_B = \big( \dot{s} + \ell\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta \big)\,\hat{\imath} + \ell\dot{\vartheta}\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
|\vec{v}_B|^2 = \dot{s}^{\,2} + \ell^2\dot{\vartheta}^{\,2} + 2\,\ell\,\dot{s}\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta
$$
$$
\vec{\omega}_{\text{disk}} = -\,\frac{\dot{s}}{R}\;\hat{k} ,
\qquad
\vec{\omega}_{\text{rod}} = \dot{\vartheta}\;\hat{k}
$$

Instantaneous center of rotation of the rod, with $\dot{\vartheta} \neq 0$:
$$
C_{\text{rod}} = G + \frac{\hat{k} \wedge \vec{v}_G}{\dot{\vartheta}} = G + \frac{\dot{s}}{\dot{\vartheta}}\;\hat{\jmath}
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
C_{\text{rod}} - O = s\,\hat{\imath} + \left( R + \frac{\dot{s}}{\dot{\vartheta}} \right)\hat{\jmath}
$$

Check by the velocity of $B$ computed from the center:
$$
B - C_{\text{rod}} = \ell\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} - \left( \ell\cos\vartheta + \frac{\dot{s}}{\dot{\vartheta}} \right)\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge (B - C_{\text{rod}}) = \dot{\vartheta}\left( \ell\cos\vartheta + \frac{\dot{s}}{\dot{\vartheta}} \right)\hat{\imath} + \ell\dot{\vartheta}\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath} = \vec{v}_B
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The system has **two degrees of freedom**, because the abscissa of the center and the angle of the rod are assigned independently: no constraint ties the endpoint $B$ to a guide. The position of $B$ is obtained by adding to $G$ the vector of the rod, and differentiating one finds $\vec{v}_B$.

The square of the magnitude is obtained by expanding the squares of the two components and using the fundamental identity; the mixed term $2\ell\dot{s}\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta$ is the one that couples the two degrees of freedom, and it is the same one that appears in the kinetic energy of the system.

The angular velocity of the disk follows from the rolling without slipping, that of the rod from the angle $\vartheta$: the two are independent, as the number of degrees of freedom requires.

The instantaneous center of rotation of the rod lies on the vertical line through $G$, because $\vec{v}_G$ is horizontal, at distance $\dfrac{|\dot{s}|}{|\dot{\vartheta}|}$ from $G$; it does not coincide with the contact point $C$ of the disk, which is the instantaneous center of rotation of the disk and not of the rod.

The check recomputes $\vec{v}_B$ by the formula $\vec{v}_B = \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge (B - C_{\text{rod}})$ and gives back the expression already found by differentiation.

</div>
