## Exercise 1

The rigid rod $AB$ in the figure, of length $\ell$, has the endpoint $A$ on a smooth horizontal guide and the endpoint $B$ on a smooth vertical guide; the two guides meet at $O$. The rod forms the angle $\vartheta$ with the horizontal, with $0 < \vartheta < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Determine the angular velocity of the rod, the instantaneous center of rotation and the velocity of the midpoint $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,2.6,0;B,0,1.5" aste="A-B" linee="-0.4,0 3.4,0;0,-0.4 0,2.4" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello,180" punti="2.6,0;0,1.5;1.3,0.75,M;0,0,O" etichette="2.1,0.2,ϑ" riferimento=0 titolo="Rod with its endpoints on two orthogonal guides, inclined at $\vartheta$ to the horizontal"}

$$
A - O = \ell\cos\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} ,
\qquad
B - O = \ell\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
\vec{v}_A = -\,\ell\dot{\vartheta}\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} ,
\qquad
\vec{v}_B = \ell\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath}
$$

Angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\hat{k}$ from the fundamental formula, with $B - A = \ell\,(-\cos\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath})$:
$$
\begin{cases}
0 = -\,\ell\dot{\vartheta}\sin\vartheta - \omega\ell\sin\vartheta \\[4pt]
\ell\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta = -\,\omega\ell\cos\vartheta
\end{cases}
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
\vec{\omega} = -\,\dot{\vartheta}\;\hat{k}
$$

Instantaneous center of rotation:
$$
C = A + \frac{\hat{k} \wedge \vec{v}_A}{\omega}
= A + \frac{-\,\ell\dot{\vartheta}\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath}}{-\dot{\vartheta}}
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
C - O = \ell\cos\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} + \ell\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
|M - C| = \frac{\ell}{2} ,
\qquad
|\vec{v}_M| = |\omega|\,|M - C| = \frac{\ell\,|\dot{\vartheta}|}{2}
$$

Check by direct computation:
$$
M - O = \frac{\ell\cos\vartheta}{2}\;\hat{\imath} + \frac{\ell\sin\vartheta}{2}\;\hat{\jmath} ,
\qquad
\vec{v}_M = \frac{\ell\dot{\vartheta}}{2}\,\big( -\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath} \big) ,
\qquad
|\vec{v}_M| = \frac{\ell\,|\dot{\vartheta}|}{2}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The angle $\vartheta$ locates the configuration by itself, therefore the system has one degree of freedom and the positions of the two endpoints are written at once as functions of $\vartheta$. Differentiating with respect to time one obtains the two velocities, horizontal that of $A$ and vertical that of $B$, as the guides impose.

The fundamental formula written between $A$ and $B$ gives two scalar equations in the single unknown $\omega$, and they are compatible: both give $\omega = -\dot{\vartheta}$. The minus sign is read off the drawing, because an increasing $\vartheta$ corresponds to a **clockwise** rotation of the rod.

The instantaneous center of rotation is found by the formula $C = A + \dfrac{\hat{k} \wedge \vec{v}_A}{\omega}$, or, more quickly, as the intersection of the vertical line through $A$ with the horizontal line through $B$, that is, of the lines orthogonal to the two velocities. The point $C$ closes the rectangle $OACB$, hence $|O - C| = \ell$ for every value of $\vartheta$.

The triangle $ACB$ is right-angled at $C$, because $CA$ is vertical and $CB$ is horizontal, and the midpoint of the hypotenuse of a right triangle is as far from the three vertices as half the hypotenuse, therefore $|M - C| = \dfrac{\ell}{2}$ and the velocity of $M$ has magnitude $\dfrac{\ell\,|\dot{\vartheta}|}{2}$, constant in magnitude when $\dot{\vartheta}$ is constant. The direct computation of the derivative of $M - O$ confirms the result and shows that $\vec{v}_M$ is orthogonal to $M - C$, that is, tangent to the circle with center $O$ and radius $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ on which $M$ moves.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The rod $AB$ in the figure moves in the vertical plane: the endpoint $A$ slides on a smooth horizontal guide and the rod passes inside a fixed sleeve hinged at the point $D$, placed at distance $h$ from the guide on the vertical line through $O$. The rod forms the angle $\vartheta$ with the horizontal, with $0 < \vartheta < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Determine the angular velocity of the rod, the velocity of $A$ and the instantaneous center of rotation.

::struttura{nodi="A,-1.73,0;B,0.77,4.33" aste="A-B" linee="-3,0 1.6,0;0.03,3.45 0.37,3.25 -0.03,2.55 -0.37,2.75 0.03,3.45" punti="-1.73,0;0.77,4.33;0,3,D;-1.73,4,C,centro;0,0,O" vincoli="A,carrello" tratteggi="-1.73,0 -1.73,4;0,3 -1.73,4;0,0 0,3" quote="0 0,0 3,h,0.5" etichette="-1.05,0.3,ϑ" riferimento=0 titolo="Rod sliding in the fixed sleeve $D$, with the endpoint $A$ on a horizontal guide ($\vartheta = 60^\circ$ in the drawing)"}

$$
A - O = -\,\frac{h}{\tan\vartheta}\;\hat{\imath}
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
\vec{v}_A = \frac{h\,\dot{\vartheta}}{\sin^2\vartheta}\;\hat{\imath}
$$
$$
\vec{\omega} = \dot{\vartheta}\;\hat{k}
$$

Instantaneous center of rotation, intersection of the vertical line through $A$ with the perpendicular to the rod through $D$:
$$
C - O = -\,\frac{h}{\tan\vartheta}\;\hat{\imath} + \frac{h}{\sin^2\vartheta}\;\hat{\jmath}
$$

Check by the formula for the center:
$$
C = A + \frac{\hat{k} \wedge \vec{v}_A}{\dot{\vartheta}}
= A + \frac{1}{\dot{\vartheta}}\,\frac{h\dot{\vartheta}}{\sin^2\vartheta}\;\hat{\jmath}
= A + \frac{h}{\sin^2\vartheta}\;\hat{\jmath}
$$

Check on the velocity of the point of the rod that is at $D$:
$$
\vec{v}_D = \vec{v}_A + \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge (D - A) ,
\qquad
D - A = \frac{h}{\tan\vartheta}\;\hat{\imath} + h\;\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
\vec{v}_D = \left( \frac{h\dot{\vartheta}}{\sin^2\vartheta} - h\dot{\vartheta} \right)\hat{\imath} + \frac{h\dot{\vartheta}}{\tan\vartheta}\;\hat{\jmath}
= \frac{h\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta}{\sin^2\vartheta}\,\big( \cos\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath} \big)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The line of the rod passes through $D$ and cuts the guide at $A$. From the right triangle with legs $h$ and $|x_A|$ one obtains $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{h}{|x_A|}$, and since $A$ lies to the left of $O$ it turns out that $x_A = -\dfrac{h}{\tan\vartheta}$. Differentiating and using $\dfrac{d}{dt}\cot\vartheta = -\dfrac{\dot{\vartheta}}{\sin^2\vartheta}$ one obtains the velocity of $A$, directed to the right when $\vartheta$ increases.

The angle that the rod forms with the $x$ axis is precisely $\vartheta$, therefore the angular velocity is $\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k}$, counterclockwise for $\dot{\vartheta} > 0$.

The instantaneous center of rotation lies on the line through $A$ orthogonal to $\vec{v}_A$, which is the vertical line through $A$. The sleeve forces the point of the rod that is at $D$ to move along the rod itself, hence its velocity has the direction of the rod and the line through $D$ orthogonal to that velocity is the perpendicular to the rod through $D$. The intersection of the two lines gives $C$, and the computation by the formula for the center confirms the ordinate $\dfrac{h}{\sin^2\vartheta}$.

The last check computes $\vec{v}_D$ by the fundamental formula and shows that it is parallel to the unit vector $\cos\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath}$ of the rod, as the constraint of the sleeve requires.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The slider-crank system in the figure is formed by the crank $OA$, of length $r$, hinged to the ground at $O$, and by the connecting rod $AB$, of length $2r$, hinged at $A$ and with the endpoint $B$ on a slider that runs on the horizontal guide through $O$. The crank turns with constant angular velocity $\Omega\,\hat{k}$. Determine the velocity of $B$ and the angular velocity of the connecting rod at the instant at which the crank is vertical.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,0,1.6;B,2.77,0" aste="O-A;A-B" linee="-0.6,0 3.6,0" vincoli="O,cerniera;B,carrello" punti="0,0;0,1.6;2.77,0" quote="A,B,2r,0.6" etichette="-0.35,0.8,r" coppie="0.45 0.8,1,Ω" titolo="Slider-crank in the configuration with the crank vertical: $OA = r$, $AB = 2r$"}

$$
A - O = r\,\hat{\jmath} ,
\qquad
\vec{v}_A = \Omega\,\hat{k} \wedge r\,\hat{\jmath} = -\,\Omega r\;\hat{\imath}
$$
$$
B - O = \sqrt{4r^2 - r^2}\;\hat{\imath} = \sqrt{3}\,r\;\hat{\imath} ,
\qquad
B - A = \sqrt{3}\,r\;\hat{\imath} - r\,\hat{\jmath}
$$

Having set $\vec{v}_B = v_B\,\hat{\imath}$ and $\vec{\omega}_{AB} = \omega_{AB}\,\hat{k}$, the fundamental formula for the connecting rod gives
$$
\begin{cases}
v_B = -\,\Omega r + \omega_{AB}\,r \\[4pt]
0 = \omega_{AB}\,\sqrt{3}\,r
\end{cases}
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
\omega_{AB} = 0 ,
\qquad
v_B = -\,\Omega r
$$

Check by equiprojectivity:
$$
(\vec{v}_B - \vec{v}_A) \cdot (B - A) = (v_B + \Omega r)\,\sqrt{3}\,r = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The crank turns about the fixed hinge $O$, therefore the velocity of $A$ is obtained from the fundamental formula with pole $O$ and $\vec{v}_O = \vec{0}$: it equals $\Omega r$ in magnitude and is orthogonal to $OA$, hence horizontal and directed to the left.

The slider forces $B$ to stay on the guide, therefore $y_B = 0$ and the length of the connecting rod gives $x_B = \sqrt{3}\,r$. The velocity of $B$ is horizontal.

The fundamental formula applied to the connecting rod between $A$ and $B$ gives two scalar equations. The second one, that is, the equation of the vertical components, contains only $\omega_{AB}$ and gives $\omega_{AB} = 0$; substituting in the first one obtains $v_B = -\Omega r$, equal to the velocity of $A$.

The state of motion of the connecting rod is therefore **translational**: all its points have the same velocity and the instantaneous center of rotation does not exist. The same result is read off the geometric construction, because the line through $A$ orthogonal to $\vec{v}_A$ and the line through $B$ orthogonal to $\vec{v}_B$ are both vertical, hence parallel and distinct. The check by equiprojectivity is immediate, since the two velocities are equal.

</div>
