## Exercise 1

The system in the figure is formed by two equal rods $OA$ and $AB$, both of length $\ell$, hinged to each other at $A$; the rod $OA$ is hinged to the ground at $O$ and the endpoint $B$ runs on a slider along the horizontal guide passing through $O$. The rod $OA$ forms the angle $\vartheta$ with the horizontal, with $0 < \vartheta < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Determine the angular velocity of the rod $AB$, the velocity of $B$ and the instantaneous center of rotation of $AB$.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1.73,1;B,3.46,0" aste="O-A;A-B" linee="-0.5,0 4.2,0" vincoli="O,cerniera;B,carrello" punti="0,0;1.73,1;3.46,0" etichette="0.75,0.18,ϑ" riferimento=0 titolo="Two equal rods hinged at $A$, with $B$ on a slider on the guide through $O$"}

$$
A - O = \ell\cos\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} + \ell\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath}
$$

The triangle $OAB$ is isosceles on $OB$, because $|OA| = |AB| = \ell$, hence
$$
B - O = 2\ell\cos\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} ,
\qquad
\vec{v}_B = -\,2\ell\dot{\vartheta}\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\imath}
$$
$$
B - A = \ell\cos\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} - \ell\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath}
$$

Fundamental formula for the rod $AB$, with $\vec{v}_A = \ell\dot{\vartheta}\,(-\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath})$ and $\vec{\omega}_{AB} = \omega_{AB}\,\hat{k}$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-\,2\ell\dot{\vartheta}\sin\vartheta = -\,\ell\dot{\vartheta}\sin\vartheta + \omega_{AB}\,\ell\sin\vartheta \\[4pt]
0 = \ell\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta + \omega_{AB}\,\ell\cos\vartheta
\end{cases}
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
\vec{\omega}_{AB} = -\,\dot{\vartheta}\;\hat{k}
$$

Instantaneous center of rotation of $AB$, intersection of the vertical line through $B$ with the line $OA$:
$$
C - O = 2\ell\cos\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} + 2\ell\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath}
$$

Check by the distance from $C$:
$$
|B - C| = 2\ell\sin\vartheta ,
\qquad
|\omega_{AB}|\,|B-C| = 2\ell\,|\dot{\vartheta}|\sin\vartheta = |\vec{v}_B|
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two rods have the same length and $B$ lies on the line passing through $O$, therefore the triangle $OAB$ is isosceles and the foot of the perpendicular from $A$ falls at the midpoint of $OB$. From this $x_B = 2\ell\cos\vartheta$, and differentiating one obtains $\vec{v}_B$, directed to the left when $\vartheta$ increases.

The **internal hinge** at $A$ requires the point $A$ to have the same velocity as a point of $OA$ and as a point of $AB$, therefore one computes $\vec{v}_A$ from the rotation of $OA$ about $O$ and uses it as a datum for the rod $AB$. The system of the two components is compatible and gives $\omega_{AB} = -\dot{\vartheta}$: the two rods turn with opposite angular velocities, as the symmetry of the figure with respect to the vertical line through the midpoint of $OB$ shows.

The instantaneous center of rotation of $AB$ lies on the line through $B$ orthogonal to $\vec{v}_B$, that is, on the vertical line through $B$, and on the line through $A$ orthogonal to $\vec{v}_A$, which is the line $OA$ extended. The intersection is the point $C$ with $|OC| = 2\ell$.

The check verifies the magnitude of $\vec{v}_B$ starting from the distance $|B - C| = 2\ell\sin\vartheta$, which is the height of $C$ above the guide; the product with $|\omega_{AB}|$ gives back the value already computed by differentiation.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The slider-crank system in the figure has the crank $OA$ of length $r$, hinged to the ground at $O$, and the connecting rod $AB$ of length $\ell > r$, with $B$ on a slider on the horizontal guide through $O$. Let $\vartheta$ denote the angle of the crank with the $x$ axis and $\varphi$ the angle that the connecting rod forms with the $x$ axis, measured below the horizontal. Determine the relation between $\dot{\vartheta}$ and $\dot{\varphi}$, the angular velocity of the connecting rod and the velocity of $B$.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1,1.73;B,4.46,0" aste="O-A;A-B" linee="-0.5,0 5.2,0" vincoli="O,cerniera;B,carrello" punti="0,0;1,1.73;4.46,0" tratteggi="1,1.73 1,0" etichette="0.45,0.2,ϑ;3.75,0.14,φ" riferimento=0 titolo="Slider-crank: $OA = r$, $AB = \ell$, angles $\vartheta$ and $\varphi$"}

Closure constraint of the mechanism:
$$
\begin{cases}
x_B = r\cos\vartheta + \ell\cos\varphi \\[4pt]
r\sin\vartheta = \ell\sin\varphi
\end{cases}
$$

Differentiating the second equation:
$$
r\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta = \ell\dot{\varphi}\cos\varphi
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
\dot{\varphi} = \frac{r\cos\vartheta}{\ell\cos\varphi}\;\dot{\vartheta}
$$

Angular velocity of the connecting rod and velocity of $B$:
$$
\vec{\omega}_{AB} = -\,\dot{\varphi}\;\hat{k} = -\,\frac{r\cos\vartheta}{\ell\cos\varphi}\,\dot{\vartheta}\;\hat{k}
$$
$$
\dot{x}_B = -\,r\dot{\vartheta}\sin\vartheta - \ell\dot{\varphi}\sin\varphi
= -\,r\dot{\vartheta}\left( \sin\vartheta + \frac{\cos\vartheta\sin\varphi}{\cos\varphi} \right)
= -\,\frac{r\,\sin(\vartheta + \varphi)}{\cos\varphi}\;\dot{\vartheta}
$$

Check in the configuration $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$:
$$
\dot{\varphi} = 0 ,
\qquad
\sin\left( \frac{\pi}{2} + \varphi \right) = \cos\varphi
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
\dot{x}_B = -\,r\dot{\vartheta}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The mechanism has one degree of freedom: once $\vartheta$ has been chosen, the angle $\varphi$ is determined by the condition that $B$ lie on the guide, that is, that the two rods have the same height at the start and at the end. The two equations of the system are the horizontal projection and the vertical projection of the polygonal path $O \to A \to B$.

The second equation relates $\varphi$ to $\vartheta$; differentiating it one obtains the relation between the velocities. The ratio $\dfrac{\dot{\varphi}}{\dot{\vartheta}}$ depends on the configuration and vanishes when $\cos\vartheta = 0$, that is, when the crank is vertical.

The connecting rod forms the angle $-\varphi$ with the $x$ axis, therefore its angular velocity is $-\dot{\varphi}\,\hat{k}$. The velocity of $B$ is obtained by differentiating the first equation and substituting $\dot{\varphi}$; factoring out $\dfrac{r\dot{\vartheta}}{\cos\varphi}$ and using the addition formula $\sin\vartheta\cos\varphi + \cos\vartheta\sin\varphi = \sin(\vartheta+\varphi)$ one arrives at the compact expression.

The check is the comparison with the configuration with the crank vertical, solved in the sheet on the instantaneous center of rotation: there $\omega_{AB} = 0$ and $\vec{v}_B = -\Omega r\,\hat{\imath}$ had been found, and the general formulas give the same values. The result holds for every ratio $\dfrac{\ell}{r}$, because in that configuration $\cos\vartheta = 0$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The double pendulum in the figure is formed by the rods $OA$ and $AB$, of length $\ell$ each, hinged to the ground at $O$ and to each other at $A$, free to turn in the vertical plane. Let $\vartheta$ and $\psi$ denote the angles that the two rods form with the downward vertical. Compute the velocity of $B$ and determine the instantaneous center of rotation of the rod $AB$.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1.2,-1.6;B,3.1,-2.22" aste="O-A;A-B" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" punti="0,0;1.2,-1.6;3.1,-2.22" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2;1.2,-1.6 1.2,-3" etichette="0.25,-1.1,ϑ;1.72,-2.35,ψ" riferimento=0 titolo="Double pendulum: two degrees of freedom, the angles $\vartheta$ and $\psi$ with the vertical"}

$$
A - O = \ell\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} - \ell\cos\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath} ,
\qquad
B - A = \ell\sin\psi\;\hat{\imath} - \ell\cos\psi\;\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
\vec{v}_B = \ell\,\big( \dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta + \dot{\psi}\cos\psi \big)\;\hat{\imath} + \ell\,\big( \dot{\vartheta}\sin\vartheta + \dot{\psi}\sin\psi \big)\;\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
|\vec{v}_B|^2 = \ell^2\,\big[ \dot{\vartheta}^{\,2} + \dot{\psi}^{\,2} + 2\,\dot{\vartheta}\dot{\psi}\cos(\vartheta - \psi) \big]
$$

Angular velocities of the two rods:
$$
\vec{\omega}_{OA} = \dot{\vartheta}\;\hat{k} ,
\qquad
\vec{\omega}_{AB} = \dot{\psi}\;\hat{k}
$$

Instantaneous center of rotation of $AB$, with $\dot{\psi} \neq 0$:
$$
C = A + \frac{\hat{k} \wedge \vec{v}_A}{\dot{\psi}}
= A - \frac{\ell\dot{\vartheta}}{\dot{\psi}}\,\big( \sin\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} - \cos\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath} \big)
$$

Check in the case $\vartheta = \psi$ and $\dot{\vartheta} = \dot{\psi}$:
$$
|\vec{v}_B| = 2\ell\,|\dot{\vartheta}| ,
\qquad
C = A - \ell\,\big( \sin\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} - \cos\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath} \big) = O
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The double pendulum has **two degrees of freedom**, because the angles $\vartheta$ and $\psi$ can be assigned independently. The position of $B$ is obtained by adding the two vectors $A - O$ and $B - A$, and differentiating one obtains $\vec{v}_B$.

The square of the magnitude simplifies by factoring out $\ell^2$ and using the subtraction formula $\cos\vartheta\cos\psi + \sin\vartheta\sin\psi = \cos(\vartheta - \psi)$. The expression found is the one that appears in the kinetic energy of the double pendulum in the topic on Lagrange's equations.

Each rod forms with the $x$ axis the angle $\vartheta - \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $\psi - \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ respectively, hence the derivatives of these angles give the two angular velocities, independent of each other.

The instantaneous center of rotation of the rod $AB$ is computed by the formula $C = A + \dfrac{\hat{k} \wedge \vec{v}_A}{\omega_{AB}}$, where $\vec{v}_A$ is the velocity of the point $A$ as a point of the rod $OA$; the velocity $\vec{v}_A$ is orthogonal to $OA$, therefore $\hat{k} \wedge \vec{v}_A$ has the direction of $OA$ and the point $C$ lies on the line $OA$, as the geometric construction requires.

The check is made in the case in which the two rods stay aligned and turn together: the system behaves as a single rod of length $2\ell$ hinged at $O$, therefore $|\vec{v}_B| = 2\ell\,|\dot{\vartheta}|$ and the instantaneous center of rotation of $AB$ coincides with the fixed hinge $O$. Both general formulas give back these values.

</div>
