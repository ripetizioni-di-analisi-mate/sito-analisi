## Exercise 1

A disk of radius $R$ and center $G$ rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide, with $G - O = s\,\hat{\imath} + R\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $s = R\varphi$, where $\varphi$ is the rotation angle of the disk. Determine the fixed centrode and the moving centrode of the motion.

::struttura{cerchi="2.5,1,1" linee="-0.3,0 5.2,0" punti="2.5,1,G,baricentro;2.5,0,C,centro" tratteggi="2.5,1 2.5,-0.6" etichette="4.2,0.2,fixed centrode" titolo="The instantaneous center of rotation is always the contact point: the fixed centrode is the guide"}

The instantaneous center of rotation is the contact point, hence in the fixed frame
$$
C - O = s\;\hat{\imath} ,
\qquad \text{that is} \qquad
y_C = 0 .
$$
The **fixed centrode** is therefore the horizontal guide.

Frame attached to the disk, with origin at $G$ and axes rotated by the angle $-\varphi$ with respect to the fixed ones:
$$
\hat{e}_1 = \cos\varphi\;\hat{\imath} - \sin\varphi\;\hat{\jmath} ,
\qquad
\hat{e}_2 = \sin\varphi\;\hat{\imath} + \cos\varphi\;\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
C - G = -\,R\,\hat{\jmath}
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
\xi_C = (C-G) \cdot \hat{e}_1 = R\sin\varphi ,
\qquad
\eta_C = (C-G) \cdot \hat{e}_2 = -\,R\cos\varphi
$$
$$
\xi_C^2 + \eta_C^2 = R^2 .
$$
The **moving centrode** is therefore the circle with center $G$ and radius $R$, that is, the rim of the disk.

Check by the theorem on the rolling of the fixed centrode and the moving centrode:
$$
\text{arc of moving centrode} = R\,\Delta\varphi = \Delta s = \text{arc of fixed centrode}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The disk rolls without slipping on a fixed guide, therefore the contact point has zero velocity and is the instantaneous center of rotation. As time varies that point travels along the guide, hence the fixed centrode is the line $y = 0$.

For the moving centrode the attached frame is needed. The disk turns in the clockwise sense by the angle $\varphi$, hence its attached axes are obtained by rotating the fixed ones by $-\varphi$; the components of $C - G$ in this basis are computed by two scalar products. Eliminating $\varphi$ between the two expressions one obtains the equation of a circle of radius $R$ centered at the origin of the attached frame, that is, at $G$: the moving centrode coincides with the rim of the disk.

The result can also be read off without computations: the point of the body that is from time to time the instantaneous center of rotation is the point of the rim that touches the guide, and the set of these points is the rim itself.

The check is the verification of the theorem by which the moving centrode rolls without slipping on the fixed centrode: in an interval in which the angle varies by $\Delta\varphi$ the arc of moving centrode traveled is $R\,\Delta\varphi$ and the displacement of the contact along the fixed centrode is $\Delta s = R\,\Delta\varphi$. The two arcs coincide.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A rod $AB$ of length $\ell$ has the endpoint $A$ on a fixed straight guide and the endpoint $B$ on a second fixed straight guide; the two guides meet at $O$ and form the angle $\alpha$, with $0 < \alpha < \pi$. Determine the fixed centrode and the moving centrode of the motion of the rod.

::struttura{nodi="A,2,0;B,1,1.73" aste="A-B" linee="-0.5,0 2.6,0;-0.3,-0.52 1.6,2.77;2.15,0.58 2.12,0.88 2,1.15 1.82,1.39 1.58,1.58 1.3,1.69 1,1.73 0.7,1.69 0.42,1.58 0.18,1.39 0,1.15 -0.12,0.88 -0.15,0.58 -0.12,0.28 0,0 0.18,-0.24 0.42,-0.42 0.7,-0.54 1,-0.58 1.3,-0.54 1.58,-0.42 1.82,-0.24 2,0 2.12,0.28 2.15,0.58" tratteggi="2.23,-0.6 2.28,-0.35 2.31,-0.1 2.3,0.16 2.27,0.41 2.21,0.66 2.13,0.9 2.02,1.13 1.88,1.34 1.72,1.54 1.54,1.72 1.34,1.88 1.13,2.02 0.9,2.13 0.66,2.21 0.41,2.27 0.16,2.3 -0.1,2.31 -0.35,2.28 -0.6,2.23;2,0 2,1.15;1,1.73 2,1.15" punti="2,0;1,1.73;2,1.15,C,centro;0,0,O" etichette="0.45,0.25,α;-0.9,2.1,fixed centrode;1.1,-1,moving centrode" riferimento=0 titolo="Two guides meeting at $O$ with angle $\alpha$: fixed centrode of radius $\dfrac{\ell}{\sin\alpha}$ and moving centrode of radius $\dfrac{\ell}{2\sin\alpha}$"}

Directions of the velocities and position of the center:
$$
\vec{v}_A \parallel OA ,
\qquad
\vec{v}_B \parallel OB
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
\widehat{OAC} = \frac{\pi}{2} ,
\qquad
\widehat{OBC} = \frac{\pi}{2}
$$

The points $A$ and $B$ see the segment $OC$ under a right angle, hence $O$, $A$, $B$, $C$ belong to the circle with diameter $OC$, of radius $\rho$. In it the chord $AB$ subtends the inscribed angle $\widehat{AOB} = \alpha$, therefore
$$
\ell = 2\rho\sin\alpha
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
\rho = \frac{\ell}{2\sin\alpha} ,
\qquad
|O - C| = 2\rho = \frac{\ell}{\sin\alpha} .
$$

The **fixed centrode** is therefore the circle with center $O$ and radius $\dfrac{\ell}{\sin\alpha}$, and the **moving centrode** is the circle of radius $\dfrac{\ell}{2\sin\alpha}$ passing through $A$ and through $B$, which in the plane attached to the rod are fixed points.

Check by coordinates: having set $|OA| = a$ and $|OB| = b$, with the $x$ axis along the first guide,
$$
A - O = a\,\hat{\imath} ,
\qquad
B - O = b\cos\alpha\;\hat{\imath} + b\sin\alpha\;\hat{\jmath} ,
\qquad
C - O = a\,\hat{\imath} + \frac{b - a\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}\;\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
|O-C|^2 = a^2 + \frac{(b - a\cos\alpha)^2}{\sin^2\alpha} = \frac{a^2 + b^2 - 2ab\cos\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha} = \frac{\ell^2}{\sin^2\alpha}
$$

Check in the case $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$:
$$
|O-C| = \ell ,
\qquad
\rho = \frac{\ell}{2}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each endpoint slides along its own guide, hence $\vec{v}_A$ has the direction of $OA$ and $\vec{v}_B$ that of $OB$. The instantaneous center of rotation lies on the line through $A$ orthogonal to $\vec{v}_A$ and on the line through $B$ orthogonal to $\vec{v}_B$, therefore the angles $\widehat{OAC}$ and $\widehat{OBC}$ are right.

Two points that see a segment under a right angle lie on the circle having that segment as diameter, hence $A$ and $B$ lie on the circle with diameter $OC$. The **chord theorem** applied to that circle gives $AB = 2\rho\,\sin\widehat{AOB}$, and the angle $\widehat{AOB}$ is the angle $\alpha$ between the guides, which stays constant during the motion. It follows that $\rho$ is constant, and with it the diameter $|O-C|$.

The distance of $C$ from $O$ is constant, therefore in the fixed plane $C$ describes a circle with center $O$ and radius $\dfrac{\ell}{\sin\alpha}$. In the plane attached to the rod the points $A$ and $B$ are fixed and $C$ always lies on the circle through $A$ and $B$ of radius $\dfrac{\ell}{2\sin\alpha}$, which is therefore the moving centrode.

The check by coordinates computes the ordinate of $C$ by imposing that it lie on the perpendicular to $OB$ through $B$, and the square of the distance reduces, by the law of cosines in the triangle $OAB$, to $\dfrac{\ell^2}{\sin^2\alpha}$. With orthogonal guides one recovers the circle of radius $\ell$ and the one of radius $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$, that is, the **Cardano circles**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A rod slides inside a fixed sleeve hinged at $D$, at height $h$ above a horizontal guide, and has the endpoint $A$ on the guide; the rod forms the angle $\vartheta$ with the horizontal, with $0 < \vartheta < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Determine the fixed centrode and the moving centrode of the motion of the rod.

::struttura{nodi="A,-1.73,0;B,0.77,4.33" aste="A-B" linee="-3.4,0 1.8,0;0.03,3.45 0.37,3.25 -0.03,2.55 -0.37,2.75 0.03,3.45" vincoli="A,carrello" punti="-1.73,0;0,3,D;-1.73,4,C,centro;0,0,O" tratteggi="-1.73,0 -1.73,4;0,3 -1.73,4;-3.2,6.41 -2.6,5.25 -2,4.33 -1.4,3.65 -0.8,3.21 0,3 0.8,3.21 1.4,3.65" quote="0 0,0 3,h,0.5" etichette="-1.05,0.3,ϑ;-2.9,6,fixed centrode" riferimento=0 titolo="Rod in the fixed sleeve $D$: the center $C$ describes the parabola $y = h + \dfrac{x^2}{h}$"}

Instantaneous center of rotation, intersection of the vertical line through $A$ with the perpendicular to the rod through $D$:
$$
x_C = -\,\frac{h}{\tan\vartheta} ,
\qquad
y_C = \frac{h}{\sin^2\vartheta}
$$

Elimination of the parameter, with $\dfrac{1}{\sin^2\vartheta} = 1 + \dfrac{1}{\tan^2\vartheta}$:
$$
y_C = h\left( 1 + \frac{x_C^2}{h^2} \right) = h + \frac{x_C^2}{h} .
$$
The **fixed centrode** is therefore the parabola with vertex $(0, h)$ and vertical axis.

Frame attached to the rod, with origin at $A$ and first unit vector directed along the rod:
$$
\hat{e}_1 = \cos\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath} ,
\qquad
\hat{e}_2 = -\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
C - A = \frac{h}{\sin^2\vartheta}\;\hat{\jmath}
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
\xi_C = \frac{h}{\sin\vartheta} ,
\qquad
\eta_C = \frac{h\cos\vartheta}{\sin^2\vartheta}
$$

Elimination of the parameter, from $\sin\vartheta = \dfrac{h}{\xi_C}$ and $\cos\vartheta = \dfrac{h\,\eta_C}{\xi_C^2}$:
$$
\frac{h^2\eta_C^2}{\xi_C^4} + \frac{h^2}{\xi_C^2} = 1
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
\xi_C^4 = h^2\,\big( \xi_C^2 + \eta_C^2 \big) .
$$
The **moving centrode** is therefore the quartic with equation $\xi^4 = h^2\,(\xi^2 + \eta^2)$.

Check on the distance $AC$:
$$
|C - A| = \frac{h}{\sin^2\vartheta} ,
\qquad
\sqrt{\xi_C^2 + \eta_C^2} = \frac{h}{\sin\vartheta}\sqrt{1 + \frac{\cos^2\vartheta}{\sin^2\vartheta}} = \frac{h}{\sin^2\vartheta}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The point $A$ moves on the guide, hence its velocity is horizontal and the center lies on the vertical line through $A$, of abscissa $x_A = -\dfrac{h}{\tan\vartheta}$. The point of the rod that is inside the sleeve has velocity directed along the rod, because the sleeve prevents it from coming out; the line through $D$ orthogonal to this velocity is then the perpendicular to the rod through $D$, and the intersection with the vertical line through $A$ gives $C$. The computation of its ordinate is carried out in the right triangle with vertices $A$, $D$ and $C$, in which $|AD| = \dfrac{h}{\sin\vartheta}$ and the angle at $A$, between the rod and the vertical $AC$, equals $\dfrac{\pi}{2} - \vartheta$; since $\cos\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2} - \vartheta \right) = \sin\vartheta$, it follows that $|AC| = \dfrac{|AD|}{\sin\vartheta} = \dfrac{h}{\sin^2\vartheta}$.

For the fixed centrode one eliminates $\vartheta$ between the two coordinates using $1 + \cot^2\vartheta = \dfrac{1}{\sin^2\vartheta}$, and one obtains a parabola with vertex at the point $(0, h)$, that is, at the sleeve itself, since when the rod is vertical the instantaneous center of rotation falls at $D$. The fixed centrode and the moving centrode are in general arbitrary curves, and only in particular cases do they turn out to be circles or lines.

For the moving centrode one projects $C - A$ on the attached unit vectors and eliminates $\vartheta$ by obtaining the sine and the cosine from the two coordinates; the fundamental identity gives the required equation, a curve of the fourth degree.

The check compares the distance $|C - A|$ computed in the fixed frame with the one computed from the coordinates in the attached frame: the two expressions coincide, as they must, because the distance between two points does not depend on the frame.

</div>
