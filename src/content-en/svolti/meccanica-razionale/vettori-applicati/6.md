## Exercise 1

In the plane $Oxy$ four vectors parallel to $\hat{\jmath}$ are given, with coefficients $2F$, $F$, $3F$ and $2F$, applied respectively at the vertices $P_1 = O$, $P_2$, $P_3$ and $P_4$ of the rectangle in the figure, with $P_2 - O = 3a\,\hat{\imath}$, $P_3 - O = 3a\,\hat{\imath} + 2a\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $P_4 - O = 2a\,\hat{\jmath}$. Determine the resultant and the center of the system.

::struttura{tratteggi="0,0 3,0 3,2 0,2 0,0" punti="0,0;3,0;3,2;0,2;1.5,1.25,C,centro" forze="0 1,0,1,;3 0.5,0,0.5,;3 3.5,0,1.5,;0 3,0,1," reazioni="1.5 3.45,0,2.2," etichette="0.2,1.02,2F;3.2,0.52,F;3.2,3.52,3F;0.2,3.02,2F;1.75,3.4,R;-0.5,-0.28,P_1;3.2,-0.28,P_2;3.25,1.72,P_3;-0.55,1.72,P_4" titolo="The four parallel vectors and the resultant applied at the center $C$"}

$$
c = 2F + F + 3F + 2F = 8F
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\vec{R} = 8F\,\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
x_C = \frac{2F \cdot 0 + F \cdot 3a + 3F \cdot 3a + 2F \cdot 0}{8F} = \frac{12\,a F}{8F} = \frac{3a}{2}
$$
$$
y_C = \frac{2F \cdot 0 + F \cdot 0 + 3F \cdot 2a + 2F \cdot 2a}{8F} = \frac{10\,a F}{8F} = \frac{5a}{4}
$$

Check with additivity, grouping the two vectors applied on the side $x = 0$ and the two applied on the side $x = 3a$:
$$
c' = 4F , \quad C' - O = a\,\hat{\jmath} ;
\qquad
c'' = 4F , \quad C'' - O = 3a\,\hat{\imath} + \frac{3a}{2}\,\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
C - O = \frac{4F\,\big( a\,\hat{\jmath} \big) + 4F\,\Big( 3a\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{3a}{2}\,\hat{\jmath} \Big)}{8F} = \frac{3a}{2}\,\hat{\imath} + \frac{5a}{4}\,\hat{\jmath}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The four vectors are parallel and the sum of the coefficients differs from zero, hence the system is equivalent to the resultant $8F\,\hat{\jmath}$ applied at the **center of the system**.

The coordinates of the center are the averages of the coordinates of the points of application, weighted with the coefficients. The denominator is always the sum of the coefficients; in the numerator each point enters with its own weight.

In the check the system is split into two groups. The first group is formed by the vectors of coefficients $2F$ at $P_1$ and $2F$ at $P_4$, which have total coefficient $4F$ and center at the midpoint of the side, of ordinate $a$. The second group is formed by the vectors of coefficients $F$ at $P_2$ and $3F$ at $P_3$, with total coefficient $4F$ and center of ordinate $\dfrac{0 \cdot F + 2a \cdot 3F}{4F} = \dfrac{3a}{2}$. The center of the two centers, weighted with $4F$ and $4F$, coincides with the one computed directly, as the property of **additivity** states.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In the plane $Oxy$ four vectors parallel to the unit vector $\hat{u}$ are given, with coefficients $F$, $2F$, $2F$ and $F$, applied at the points $P_1 = O$, $P_2 - O = 2a\,\hat{\imath}$, $P_3 - O = 2a\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$ and $P_4 - O = 2a\,\hat{\jmath}$, as the diagram in the figure shows. Determine the center of the system and check that the line of action of the resultant passes through the center both for $\hat{u} = \hat{\jmath}$ and for $\hat{u} = \hat{\imath}$.

::struttura{tratteggi="0,0 2,0 2,2 0,2 0,0" punti="0,0;2,0;2,2;0,2;1.333,1,C,centro" forze="0 0.7,0,0.7,;2 1.4,0,1.4,;2 3.4,0,1.4,;0 2.7,0,0.7," etichette="0.2,0.72,F;2.2,1.42,2F;2.2,3.42,2F;0.2,2.72,F;-0.45,-0.25,P_1;2.2,-0.25,P_2;2.25,1.75,P_3;-0.5,1.75,P_4" titolo="The four vectors parallel to $\hat{\jmath}$ and the center $C$ of the system"}

$$
c = F + 2F + 2F + F = 6F ,
\qquad
x_C = \frac{2F \cdot 2a + 2F \cdot 2a}{6F} = \frac{4a}{3} ,
\qquad
y_C = \frac{2F \cdot 2a + F \cdot 2a}{6F} = a
$$
With $\hat{u} = \hat{\jmath}$ one has $\vec{R} = 6F\,\hat{\jmath}$ and
$$
M_O = F \cdot 0 + 2F \cdot 2a + 2F \cdot 2a + F \cdot 0 = 8\,a F
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x\,R_y = M_O
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x = \frac{8\,a F}{6F} = \frac{4a}{3} .
$$
With $\hat{u} = \hat{\imath}$ one has $\vec{R} = 6F\,\hat{\imath}$ and
$$
M_O = -\,\big( F \cdot 0 + 2F \cdot 0 + 2F \cdot 2a + F \cdot 2a \big) = -\,6\,a F
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
-\,y\,R_x = M_O
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
y = \frac{6\,a F}{6F} = a .
$$

Check with the moment about $C$ for an oblique direction, $\hat{u} = \dfrac{\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}}{\sqrt{2}}$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M_C &= \frac{F}{\sqrt{2}}\,\Big[ \Big( -\frac{4a}{3} \Big)(1) - (-a)(1) \Big]
+ \frac{2F}{\sqrt{2}}\,\Big[ \Big( 2a - \frac{4a}{3} \Big)(1) - (-a)(1) \Big] \\[6pt]
&\quad + \frac{2F}{\sqrt{2}}\,\Big[ \Big( 2a - \frac{4a}{3} \Big)(1) - (2a - a)(1) \Big]
+ \frac{F}{\sqrt{2}}\,\Big[ \Big( -\frac{4a}{3} \Big)(1) - (2a - a)(1) \Big] \\[6pt]
&= \frac{a F}{\sqrt{2}}\,\Big( -\frac{1}{3} + \frac{10}{3} - \frac{2}{3} - \frac{7}{3} \Big) = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The center is computed with the formula for parallel vectors and does not depend on the unit vector $\hat{u}$: only the coefficients and the points of application appear in the formula.

With the vectors parallel to $\hat{\jmath}$ each moment about $O$ equals $x_i\,c_i$, because the component $v_x$ vanishes; the line of action of the resultant is the vertical line of abscissa $\dfrac{4a}{3}$, which passes through $C$. With the vectors parallel to $\hat{\imath}$ each moment equals $-\,y_i\,c_i$; the line of action is the horizontal line of ordinate $a$, which passes through $C$ as well.

The two lines of action therefore meet at $C$: rotating all the vectors by the same amount about their respective points of application, the line of action of the resultant rotates about the center.

The check computes the moment about $C$ for a third direction, the bisector of the first quadrant, and uses the formula $M = (x_i - x_C)\,v_y - (y_i - y_C)\,v_x$ with $v_x = v_y = \dfrac{c_i}{\sqrt{2}}$. The sum of the four contributions vanishes, hence in this case too the line of action of the resultant passes through $C$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

In the plane $Oxy$ three vectors parallel to $\hat{\jmath}$ are given, with coefficients $2F$, $-F$ and $\mu F$ where $\mu$ is a real parameter, applied at the points $P_1 = O$, $P_2 - O = 2a\,\hat{\imath}$ and $P_3 - O = 4a\,\hat{\imath}$, as the diagram in the figure shows. Determine the value of $\mu$ for which the center of the system is the point of abscissa $6a$, and the value of $\mu$ for which the center does not exist, specifying to which system it reduces in that case.

::struttura{nodi="P_1,0,0;P_2,2,0;P_3,4,0" aste="P_1-P_3" forze="0 1.3,0,1.3,;2 -0.8,0,-0.8,;4 1.6,0,1.6," etichette="0.22,1.32,2F;2.22,-0.82,F;4.22,1.62,μF" quote="P_1,P_2,2a,1.6;P_2,P_3,2a,1.6" titolo="The three parallel vectors, with the coefficient $\mu$ of the third one as parameter"}

$$
c = 2F - F + \mu F = (1 + \mu)\,F
$$
$$
x_C = \frac{2F \cdot 0 - F \cdot 2a + \mu F \cdot 4a}{(1 + \mu)\,F} = \frac{(4\mu - 2)\,a}{1 + \mu}
$$
$$
\frac{(4\mu - 2)\,a}{1 + \mu} = 6a
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
4\mu - 2 = 6\mu + 6
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mu = -\,4
$$
$$
c = 0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mu = -\,1 ,
\qquad
M_O = 2F \cdot 0 - F \cdot 2a - F \cdot 4a = -\,6\,a F ,
$$
therefore for $\mu = -\,1$ the system is equivalent to a **couple** of moment $-\,6\,a F\,\hat{k}$.

Check for $\mu = -\,4$ with the moment about the point of abscissa $6a$:
$$
\vec{R} = (1 - 4)\,F\,\hat{\jmath} = -\,3F\,\hat{\jmath} ,
\qquad
M = (0 - 6a) \cdot 2F + (2a - 6a) \cdot (-F) + (4a - 6a) \cdot (-\,4F) = -\,12\,a F + 4\,a F + 8\,a F = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The sum of the coefficients depends on $\mu$ and the center is defined only when this sum differs from zero. The abscissa of the center is obtained from the weighted average of the abscissas; the ordinates are all zero, therefore the center belongs to the $x$ axis.

The equation $x_C = 6a$ gives $\mu = -\,4$: the third coefficient is negative and the center falls outside the segment containing the three points of application. This is consistent with the property of the center, which guarantees membership of the convex set of the points of application only when all the coefficients are positive.

For $\mu = -\,1$ the sum of the coefficients vanishes, hence the resultant vanishes and the center is not defined. The resultant moment equals $-\,6\,a F$ and is the same about every pole, therefore by the **reduction theorem** the system is equivalent to a clockwise couple of magnitude $6\,a F$.

The check examines the case $\mu = -\,4$ by computing the resultant moment about the point found. The value is zero, hence that point belongs to the line of action of the resultant, as it must be for the center of a system of parallel vectors.

</div>
