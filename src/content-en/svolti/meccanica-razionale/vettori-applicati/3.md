## Exercise 1

In space two couples are given. The first is formed by $(A, F\,\hat{\jmath})$ and $(B, -F\,\hat{\jmath})$, the second by $(C, F\,\hat{k})$ and $(D, -F\,\hat{k})$, with $A - O = a\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$, $B - A = 2a\,\hat{\imath}$, $C - A = 2a\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $D - B = 2a\,\hat{\jmath}$, as the diagram in the figure shows. Determine the moment of each couple and the system equivalent to the two couples together.

::struttura{frecce="0,0,-0.7,-0.504,x;0,0,1.4,0,y;0,0,0,1.4,z" punti="0.5,-0.36,A;-0.5,-1.08,B;2.5,-0.36,C;1.5,-1.08,D" forze="1.6 -0.36,1.1,0,;-1.6 -1.08,-1.1,0,;2.5 0.74,0,1.1,;1.5 -2.18,0,-1.1," tratteggi="0.5,-0.36 -0.5,-1.08;2.5,-0.36 1.5,-1.08" etichette="0.12,0.18,O;1.65,-0.6,F;-1.7,-1.32,F;2.65,0.78,F;1.62,-2.22,F" riferimento=0 titolo="The two couples: the first in the horizontal plane, the second with its vectors parallel to $\hat{k}$"}

$$
\vec{M}_1 = (A - B) \wedge F\,\hat{\jmath} = (-\,2a\,\hat{\imath}) \wedge F\,\hat{\jmath} = -\,2\,a F\,\hat{k}
$$
$$
\vec{M}_2 = (C - D) \wedge F\,\hat{k} = (-\,2a\,\hat{\imath}) \wedge F\,\hat{k} = 2\,a F\,\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
\vec{R} = \vec{0} ,
\qquad
\vec{M} = \vec{M}_1 + \vec{M}_2 = 2\,a F\,\hat{\jmath} - 2\,a F\,\hat{k} ,
\qquad
|\vec{M}| = 2\,a F\sqrt{2}
$$

Check by direct computation about the pole $O$ and about the pole $E$ with $E - O = a\,(\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath} + 3\hat{k})$:
$$
\vec{M}_O = \underbrace{a\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}) \wedge F\,\hat{\jmath}}_{A} + \underbrace{a\,(3\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}) \wedge (-F\,\hat{\jmath})}_{B} + \underbrace{a\,(\hat{\imath} + 3\hat{\jmath}) \wedge F\,\hat{k}}_{C} + \underbrace{a\,(3\hat{\imath} + 3\hat{\jmath}) \wedge (-F\,\hat{k})}_{D}
$$
$$
\vec{M}_O = a F\,\hat{k} - 3\,a F\,\hat{k} + a F\,(3\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath}) - a F\,(3\hat{\imath} - 3\hat{\jmath}) = 2\,a F\,\hat{\jmath} - 2\,a F\,\hat{k}
$$
$$
\vec{M}_E = \vec{M}_O + (O - E) \wedge \vec{R} = \vec{M}_O + \vec{0} = 2\,a F\,\hat{\jmath} - 2\,a F\,\hat{k}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The moment of a couple is computed with the formula $(A - B) \wedge \vec{v}$, in which $A$ is the point of application of the vector $\vec{v}$ and $B$ that of the opposite vector. For the first couple $A - B = -\,2a\,\hat{\imath}$ and $\vec{v} = F\,\hat{\jmath}$, hence the moment is directed along $\hat{\imath} \wedge \hat{\jmath} = \hat{k}$ with a negative sign. For the second couple the product $\hat{\imath} \wedge \hat{k} = -\hat{\jmath}$ gives a moment directed along $\hat{\jmath}$ with a positive sign.

Both couples have arm $2a$ and magnitude $F$, therefore both moments have magnitude $2\,a F$; what changes are the directions, which are orthogonal to the planes of the two couples. Couples are composed by adding their moments, since the resultant and the resultant moment of a system are additive.

The system of the four forces has zero resultant, hence by the **reduction theorem** it is equivalent to a single couple, whose moment $2\,a F\,(\hat{\jmath} - \hat{k})$ has magnitude $2\,a F\sqrt{2}$ and lies in the plane $Oyz$.

The check recomputes the resultant moment by adding the four moments about $O$, without using the formula for the couple, and obtains the same vector. The second check uses an arbitrary pole $E$: since $\vec{R} = \vec{0}$, by the **change of pole formula** the moment does not change.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The rigid bracket in the figure is formed by the vertical post $AB$ of length $2\ell$ and by the horizontal arm $BC$ of length $\ell$, with $A$ at the origin. At $C$ the vector $-F\,\hat{\jmath}$ acts and at $B$ the vector $2F\,\hat{\imath}$. Reduce the system at $A$, that is determine the equivalent vector applied at $A$ and the equivalent couple, and then reduce it to a single applied vector.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,2;C,1,2" aste="A-B;B-C" forze="C,0,-1,F;B,1,0,2F" quote="A,B,2ℓ,1.5;B,C,ℓ,0.5" titolo="The bracket with the two vectors applied at $B$ and at $C$"}

$$
\vec{R} = 2F\,\hat{\imath} - F\,\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
M_A = \underbrace{\ell \cdot (-F) - 2\ell \cdot 0}_{\text{vector at } C} + \underbrace{0 \cdot 0 - 2\ell \cdot 2F}_{\text{vector at } B} = -F\ell - 4F\ell = -\,5F\ell
$$
The system is equivalent to the vector $\vec{R}$ applied at $A$ together with the couple of moment $-\,5F\ell\,\hat{k}$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,2;C,1,2" aste="A-B;B-C" reazioni="A,1,-0.5,R" coppie="0.6 0.8,-1,ℳ_t" titolo="The system reduced at $A$: the vector $\vec{R}$ and the transfer couple $\mathcal{M}_t = -\,5F\ell$"}

$$
x\,R_y - y\,R_x = M_A
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
-F\,x - 2F\,y = -\,5F\ell
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x + 2y = 5\ell
$$
$$
d = \frac{|M_A|}{|\vec{R}|} = \frac{5F\ell}{F\sqrt{5}} = \sqrt{5}\,\ell
$$

Check with Varignon's theorem: the two lines of action meet at $C$, of coordinates $(\ell, 2\ell)$, and the point $C$ satisfies the equation of the line found, since $\ell + 4\ell = 5\ell$. Moreover
$$
M_C = \underbrace{0}_{\text{vector at } C} + \underbrace{(0 - \ell) \cdot 0 - (2\ell - 2\ell) \cdot 2F}_{\text{vector at } B} = 0 .
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The reduction at $A$ is obtained by the **transfer** of each vector to $A$: every vector brings with it the transfer couple equal to its own moment about $A$, and the couples add up. The vector applied at $C$ has vertical component $-F$ and horizontal arm $\ell$, hence it gives $-F\ell$; the vector applied at $B$ has horizontal component $2F$ and vertical arm $2\ell$, hence it gives $-\,4F\ell$, with the minus sign because a force pointing right applied above the pole tends to produce a clockwise rotation.

The system is planar with a nonzero resultant, therefore it reduces to a single vector applied along the **line of action of the resultant**, of equation $x + 2y = 5\ell$. The distance of this line from $A$ equals $\sqrt{5}\,\ell$, that is the ratio of the magnitude of the moment to the magnitude of the resultant.

The check uses **Varignon's theorem**. The vector applied at $C$ has a vertical line of action through $C$; the vector applied at $B$ has a horizontal line of action at height $2\ell$, which contains $C$ as well. The two lines therefore meet at $C$, and by the theorem the system is equivalent to the resultant applied at $C$. Consequently the line of action of the resultant passes through $C$, as the substitution in the equation confirms, and the resultant moment about $C$ vanishes.

</div>

## Exercise 3

On a rigid body there act the vector $\vec{v} = F\,(3\hat{\imath} + 4\hat{\jmath})$ applied at $O$ and a couple of moment $\mathcal{M} = -\,10\,F a\,\hat{k}$, with $a > 0$, as the diagram in the figure shows. Determine the equivalent system formed by a single applied vector, specifying the line of action and its distance from $O$.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0" forze="1.8 2.4,1.8,2.4,v" coppie="2.2 0.4,-1,ℳ" punti="0,0" titolo="The vector applied at $O$ and the clockwise couple of moment $\mathcal{M} = -\,10\,F a$"}

$$
\vec{R} = F\,(3\hat{\imath} + 4\hat{\jmath}) ,
\qquad
|\vec{R}| = 5F ,
\qquad
M_O = 0 + (-\,10\,F a) = -\,10\,F a
$$
$$
x\,R_y - y\,R_x = M_O
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
4F\,x - 3F\,y = -\,10\,F a
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
4x - 3y = -\,10\,a
$$
$$
d = \frac{|M_O|}{|\vec{R}|} = \frac{10\,F a}{5F} = 2a
$$

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0" reazioni="-0.7 2.4,1.8,2.4,R" tratteggi="-4,-2 1.1,4.8;0,0 -1.6,1.2" punti="0,0;-1.6,1.2" etichette="-0.65,0.45,d" titolo="The equivalent system: the vector $\vec{R}$ on the line $4x - 3y = -\,10\,a$, at distance $d = 2a$ from $O$"}

Check with the pole $K$ of coordinates $\left( -\dfrac{5a}{2},\ 0 \right)$, which belongs to the line found:
$$
M_K^{\,\text{(original)}} = \left[ 0 - \left( -\frac{5a}{2} \right) \right] \cdot 4F - (0 - 0) \cdot 3F + (-\,10\,F a) = 10\,F a - 10\,F a = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The resultant coincides with the vector $\vec{v}$ alone, since the couple has zero resultant. The moment about $O$ is instead the sum of the moment of $\vec{v}$, which vanishes because $\vec{v}$ is applied at $O$, and of the moment of the couple, equal to $-\,10\,F a$ about every pole.

The passage from one vector and one couple to a single vector is the **transfer** run backwards. One looks for the point at which to apply $\vec{R}$ so that its moment about $O$ equals $M_O$. The set of these points is the line $4x - 3y = -\,10\,a$, parallel to $\vec{R}$ and at distance $2a$ from $O$. The couple is clockwise, therefore the line lies on the side where $\vec{R}$ produces a clockwise moment about $O$, that is to the left of the line through $O$ parallel to $\vec{R}$.

The check chooses the point $K$ at which the line meets the $x$ axis and computes the moment of the initial system about $K$. The vector $\vec{v}$, applied at $O$ to the right of $K$, has counterclockwise moment $10\,F a$, which the clockwise couple cancels; the resultant moment about $K$ is therefore zero, as it must be for a point of the line of action of the resultant.

</div>
