## Exercise 1

On the rigid rectangular plate $OBCE$ in the figure, with sides $3a$ and $2a$, there act the applied vectors $\vec{v}_1 = -F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at $E$, $\vec{v}_2 = 2F\,\hat{\imath}$ at $O$ and $\vec{v}_3 = -2F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at $C$. The frame has its origin at $O$ and its axes along the sides. Reduce the system: compute the resultant, the resultant moment about $O$ and the line of action of the resultant.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,2 0,2" nodi="O,0,0;B,3,0;C,3,2;E,0,2" forze="E,0,-1,v_1;O,1,0,v_2;C,0,-1.6,v_3" quote="O,B,3a,-3.4;B,C,2a,1" titolo="The plate and the three applied vectors"}

$$
\vec{R} = 2F\,\hat{\imath} - F\,\hat{\jmath} - 2F\,\hat{\jmath} = 2F\,\hat{\imath} - 3F\,\hat{\jmath} ,
\qquad
|\vec{R}| = F\sqrt{4 + 9} = F\sqrt{13}
$$
$$
M_O = \underbrace{0}_{\vec{v}_1} + \underbrace{0}_{\vec{v}_2} + \underbrace{3a \cdot (-2F)}_{\vec{v}_3} = -\,6\,a F
$$
$$
x\,R_y - y\,R_x = M_O
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
-\,3F\,x - 2F\,y = -\,6\,a F
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
3x + 2y = 6a
$$
$$
d = \frac{|M_O|}{|\vec{R}|} = \frac{6\,a F}{F\sqrt{13}} = \frac{6\sqrt{13}}{13}\,a
$$

Check with the pole $C$, comparing the initial system with the resultant applied at the point $(2a, 0)$ of the line found:
$$
M_C^{\,\text{(original)}} = -\,6\,a F + (x_O - x_C)\,R_y - (y_O - y_C)\,R_x = -\,6\,a F + (-3a)(-3F) + 2a \cdot 2F = 7\,a F
$$
$$
M_C^{\,\text{(resultant)}} = (2a - 3a)\,(-3F) - (0 - 2a)\,(2F) = 3\,a F + 4\,a F = 7\,a F
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The resultant is the sum of the three vectors and does not depend on the points of application. The resultant moment about $O$ is computed instead by adding the three moments. The vector $\vec{v}_1$ has a vertical line of action through $E$, that is the $y$ axis, which contains $O$; hence its moment about $O$ vanishes by the **theorem on the moment and the arm**. The vector $\vec{v}_2$ is applied at $O$, therefore its moment vanishes as well. The moment of $\vec{v}_3$ remains, which has arm $3a$ with respect to $O$ and tends to produce a clockwise rotation.

The system is planar, hence the scalar invariant vanishes and, since $\vec{R} \neq \vec{0}$, the system is equivalent to the resultant alone applied along a line. The **line of action of the resultant** is obtained by requiring that the moment about $O$ of $\vec{R}$ applied at the point $(x, y)$ equal $M_O$. The line $3x + 2y = 6a$ is parallel to $\vec{R}$, as one checks by observing that the vector $(2, -3)$ satisfies the homogeneous equation $3x + 2y = 0$; its distance from $O$ equals $\dfrac{6\sqrt{13}}{13}\,a$.

In the check the moment about $C$ is computed in two ways. The first starts from $M_O$ and uses the **change of pole formula**. The second computes the moment of the resultant alone applied at the point $(2a, 0)$, which belongs to the line found because $3 \cdot 2a + 2 \cdot 0 = 6a$. The two values coincide, therefore the reduced system is **equivalent** to the initial one.

</div>

## Exercise 2

On the rigid frame in the figure, formed by the horizontal rod $AB$ of length $3a$ and by the vertical post $AE$ of length $2a$, there act $F\,\hat{\imath}$ at $A$, $-F\,\hat{\imath}$ at $E$, $2F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at the point $C$ with $C - A = a\,\hat{\imath}$ and $-2F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at $B$. Reduce the system.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,1,0;E,0,2" aste="A-B;A-E" forze="A,1,0,F;E,-1,0,F;C,0,1.2,2F;B,0,-1.2,2F" quote="A,C,a,1.6;C,B,2a,1.6;A,E,2a,0.9" titolo="The frame and the four applied vectors, opposite in pairs"}

$$
\vec{R} = F\,\hat{\imath} - F\,\hat{\imath} + 2F\,\hat{\jmath} - 2F\,\hat{\jmath} = \vec{0}
$$
$$
M_O = \underbrace{0}_{F\hat{\imath} \text{ at } A} + \underbrace{-\,2a \cdot (-F)}_{-F\hat{\imath} \text{ at } E} + \underbrace{a \cdot 2F}_{2F\hat{\jmath} \text{ at } C} + \underbrace{3a \cdot (-2F)}_{-2F\hat{\jmath} \text{ at } B} = 2\,a F + 2\,a F - 6\,a F = -\,2\,a F
$$
The system is equivalent to a **couple** of moment $\vec{M} = -\,2\,a F\,\hat{k}$, that is clockwise, of magnitude $2\,a F$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;E,0,2" aste="A-B;A-E" coppie="1.6 1,-1,ℳ" titolo="The equivalent system: a single couple of moment $\mathcal{M} = -\,2\,a F$"}

Check with the pole $D$ of coordinates $(2a, 3a)$, at which the moment of a couple must stay the same:
$$
M_D = \underbrace{-\,(0 - 3a)\,F}_{F\hat{\imath} \text{ at } A} + \underbrace{-\,(2a - 3a)\,(-F)}_{-F\hat{\imath} \text{ at } E} + \underbrace{(a - 2a)\,2F}_{2F\hat{\jmath} \text{ at } C} + \underbrace{(3a - 2a)\,(-2F)}_{-2F\hat{\jmath} \text{ at } B} = 3\,a F - a F - 2\,a F - 2\,a F = -\,2\,a F
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The resultant vanishes, because the four vectors are opposite in pairs. By the **reduction theorem** the system is then equivalent to a couple, or else it is balanced; the decision is made by computing the resultant moment.

In the computation of $M_O$ the vector applied at $A$ has zero moment, because $A$ coincides with the pole. The vector $-F\,\hat{\imath}$ applied at $E$ has arm $2a$ and component $v_x = -F$, hence it contributes $-(y_E - y_A)\,v_x = -\,2a \cdot (-F) = 2\,a F$. The two vertical vectors have arms $a$ and $3a$ and contribute $a \cdot 2F$ and $3a \cdot (-2F)$.

The result can also be read as the sum of the two couples present. The horizontal couple, formed by $F\,\hat{\imath}$ at $A$ and $-F\,\hat{\imath}$ at $E$, has arm $2a$ and counterclockwise moment $2\,a F$; the vertical couple, formed by $2F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at $C$ and $-2F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at $B$, has arm $2a$ and clockwise moment $-\,4\,a F$. Their sum equals $-\,2\,a F$, in agreement with the direct computation.

The check computes the moment about a pole outside the frame. Since the resultant vanishes, by the **change of pole formula** the resultant moment must be the same about every pole, and the computation confirms it.

</div>

## Exercise 3

On the rigid rod $AB$ in the figure, of length $3\ell$ and placed along the $x$ axis with $A$ at the origin, there act the vector $-F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at the point of abscissa $\ell$, the vector $-2F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at the point of abscissa $2\ell$ and a couple of moment $\mathcal{M} = 3F\ell\,\hat{k}$. Reduce the system to a single applied vector and determine its line of action.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" forze="1 0,0,-1.2,F;2 0,0,-1.9,2F" coppie="2.6 0,1,ℳ" quote="A,1 0,ℓ,1.1;1 0,2 0,ℓ,1.1;2 0,B,ℓ,1.1" titolo="The rod with the two vertical vectors and the counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 3F\ell$"}

$$
\vec{R} = -F\,\hat{\jmath} - 2F\,\hat{\jmath} = -\,3F\,\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
M_A = \underbrace{\ell \cdot (-F)}_{\text{first vector}} + \underbrace{2\ell \cdot (-2F)}_{\text{second vector}} + \underbrace{3F\ell}_{\text{couple}} = -F\ell - 4F\ell + 3F\ell = -\,2F\ell
$$
$$
x\,R_y = M_A
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
-\,3F\,x = -\,2F\ell
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x = \frac{2\ell}{3}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" reazioni="0.667 0,0,-1.9,R" quote="A,0.667 0,x_C,1.1" titolo="The equivalent system: the single vector $\vec{R} = -\,3F\,\hat{\jmath}$ applied at the point of abscissa $\dfrac{2\ell}{3}$"}

Check with the pole $B$:
$$
M_B^{\,\text{(original)}} = (\ell - 3\ell)(-F) + (2\ell - 3\ell)(-2F) + 3F\ell = 2F\ell + 2F\ell + 3F\ell = 7F\ell
$$
$$
M_B^{\,\text{(reduced)}} = \Big( \frac{2\ell}{3} - 3\ell \Big)(-\,3F) = 7F\ell
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The couple contributes the zero vector to the resultant, because the resultant of a couple vanishes; therefore $\vec{R}$ contains only the two vertical vectors. In the moment about $A$ the couple enters instead with its own moment $3F\ell$, the same about every pole by the **theorem on the moment of a couple**.

The system is planar and has a resultant different from the zero vector, hence it is equivalent to a single vector applied along the **line of action of the resultant**. Since $R_x = 0$, the equation $x\,R_y - y\,R_x = M_A$ reduces to $x\,R_y = M_A$ and gives the vertical line of abscissa $\dfrac{2\ell}{3}$.

The point found lies between $A$ and the first vector, whereas without the couple the resultant would pass through the point of abscissa $\dfrac{5\ell}{3}$: the counterclockwise couple moves the line of action towards $A$, because for a downward load getting closer to $A$ reduces the clockwise moment.

The check compares the moments about $B$. In the initial system the two vectors lie to the left of $B$ and tend to produce counterclockwise rotations, to which the couple is added; in the reduced system there is the vector $\vec{R}$ alone with arm $3\ell - \dfrac{2\ell}{3} = \dfrac{7\ell}{3}$. The two values coincide, therefore the two systems are **equivalent**.

</div>
