## Exercise 1

The applied vectors $(P_1, \vec{v}_1)$ and $(P_2, \vec{v}_2)$ are given, with $P_1 - O = a\,\hat{\jmath}$, $\vec{v}_1 = F\,\hat{k}$, $P_2 - O = 2a\,\hat{\imath}$ and $\vec{v}_2 = F\,\hat{\jmath}$, as the diagram in the figure shows. Compute the resultant, the resultant moment about $O$ and the scalar invariant; establish to which minimal system it is equivalent and determine the central axis and the minimum moment.

::struttura{frecce="0,0,-1,-0.72,x;0,0,2,0,y;0,0,0,2,z" punti="1.2,0,P_1;-1.2,-0.864,P_2" forze="1.2 1.2,0,1.2,;-0.3 -0.864,0.9,0," etichette="0.16,-0.34,O;1.35,1.28,v_1;-0.72,-1.22,v_2" riferimento=0 titolo="The two applied vectors, with skew lines of action"}

$$
\vec{R} = F\,(\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) ,
\qquad
|\vec{R}| = F\sqrt{2}
$$
$$
\vec{M}_O = \underbrace{a\,\hat{\jmath} \wedge F\,\hat{k}}_{(P_1, \vec{v}_1)} + \underbrace{2a\,\hat{\imath} \wedge F\,\hat{\jmath}}_{(P_2, \vec{v}_2)} = a F\,\hat{\imath} + 2\,a F\,\hat{k}
$$
$$
I = \vec{R} \cdot \vec{M}_O = F\,(0 + 0 + 2\,a F) = 2\,a F^2 \neq 0
$$
$$
Q - O = \frac{\vec{R} \wedge \vec{M}_O}{|\vec{R}|^2}
= \frac{F\,(\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) \wedge a F\,(\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{k})}{2F^2}
= \frac{a F^2\,(2\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} - \hat{k})}{2F^2}
= a\,\hat{\imath} + \frac{a}{2}\,\hat{\jmath} - \frac{a}{2}\,\hat{k}
$$
$$
\vec{M}_Q = \frac{I}{|\vec{R}|^2}\,\vec{R} = \frac{2\,a F^2}{2F^2}\,F\,(\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) = a F\,(\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) ,
\qquad
|\vec{M}_Q| = a F\sqrt{2}
$$

::struttura{frecce="0,0,-1,-0.72,x;0,0,2,0,y;0,0,0,2,z" punti="1.2,0,P_1;-1.2,-0.864,P_2;0,-1.032,,centro" forze="1.2 1.2,0,1.2,;-0.3 -0.864,0.9,0," tratteggi="-1.2,-2.232 0.8,-0.232" etichette="0.16,-0.34,O;1.35,1.28,v_1;-0.72,-1.22,v_2;0.42,-1.3,Q;-1.0,-2.5,central axis" riferimento=0 titolo="The central axis: the line through $Q$ parallel to $\vec{R}$, about whose points the moment has minimum magnitude"}

Check by direct computation of the moment about $Q$:
$$
P_1 - Q = -\,a\,\hat{\imath} + \frac{a}{2}\,\hat{\jmath} + \frac{a}{2}\,\hat{k}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
(P_1 - Q) \wedge F\,\hat{k} = \frac{a F}{2}\,\hat{\imath} + a F\,\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
P_2 - Q = a\,\hat{\imath} - \frac{a}{2}\,\hat{\jmath} + \frac{a}{2}\,\hat{k}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
(P_2 - Q) \wedge F\,\hat{\jmath} = -\,\frac{a F}{2}\,\hat{\imath} + a F\,\hat{k}
$$
$$
\vec{M}_Q = a F\,\hat{\jmath} + a F\,\hat{k}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two lines of action are skew. The first is parallel to $\hat{k}$ and passes through the point of coordinates $(0, a, 0)$, the second is parallel to $\hat{\jmath}$ and passes through the point of coordinates $(2a, 0, 0)$. The resultant moment is computed with the products $\hat{\jmath} \wedge \hat{k} = \hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\imath} \wedge \hat{\jmath} = \hat{k}$.

The **scalar invariant** differs from zero, hence by the reduction theorem the system is not equivalent to a single applied vector, and the equivalent minimal system is formed by the vector $\vec{R}$ applied along a line and by a couple whose moment is parallel to $\vec{R}$.

The point $Q$ is obtained from the formula for the **central axis**. In the cross product one finds the components $(\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) \wedge (\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{k}) = \hat{\jmath} \wedge \hat{\imath} + 2\,\hat{\jmath} \wedge \hat{k} + \hat{k} \wedge \hat{\imath} = -\hat{k} + 2\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}$. The central axis is the line through $Q$ parallel to $\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}$, and about its points the moment equals $a F\,(\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k})$, with magnitude $a F\sqrt{2}$, smaller than the magnitude $a F\sqrt{5}$ of the moment about $O$.

The check recomputes the moment about $Q$ by adding the two moments, without using the formula for the minimum moment, and obtains the same vector. The result is parallel to $\vec{R}$, as it must be for a point of the central axis.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The three applied vectors $(O, F\,\hat{\imath})$, $(A, F\,\hat{\jmath})$ and $(B, F\,\hat{k})$ are given, with $A - O = a\,\hat{\imath}$ and $B - O = a\,\hat{\jmath}$, as the diagram in the figure shows. Determine the central axis and the minimum moment.

::struttura{frecce="0,0,-1.1,-0.792,x;0,0,2.2,0,y;0,0,0,2.2,z" punti="0,0;-0.7,-0.504,A;1.4,0,B" forze="-0.6 -0.432,-0.6,-0.432,;0.5 -0.504,1.2,0,;1.4 1.2,0,1.2," etichette="0.2,-0.34,O;-0.9,-0.28,v_1;0.62,-0.92,v_2;1.55,1.26,v_3" riferimento=0 titolo="The three vectors applied at $O$, $A$ and $B$, parallel to the three coordinate axes"}

$$
\vec{R} = F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) ,
\qquad
|\vec{R}|^2 = 3F^2
$$
$$
\vec{M}_O = \underbrace{\vec{0}}_{(O, F\hat{\imath})} + \underbrace{a\,\hat{\imath} \wedge F\,\hat{\jmath}}_{(A, F\hat{\jmath})} + \underbrace{a\,\hat{\jmath} \wedge F\,\hat{k}}_{(B, F\hat{k})} = a F\,\hat{k} + a F\,\hat{\imath} = a F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{k})
$$
$$
I = \vec{R} \cdot \vec{M}_O = F\,a F\,(1 + 0 + 1) = 2\,a F^2 \neq 0
$$
$$
\vec{R} \wedge \vec{M}_O = F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) \wedge a F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{k}) = a F^2\,(\hat{\imath} - \hat{k})
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
Q - O = \frac{a F^2\,(\hat{\imath} - \hat{k})}{3F^2} = \frac{a}{3}\,(\hat{\imath} - \hat{k})
$$
$$
\vec{M}_Q = \frac{2\,a F^2}{3F^2}\,F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) = \frac{2\,a F}{3}\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) ,
\qquad
|\vec{M}_Q| = \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\,a F
$$

Check by direct computation of the moment about $Q$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec{M}_Q &= \Big( -\frac{a}{3}\,\hat{\imath} + \frac{a}{3}\,\hat{k} \Big) \wedge F\,\hat{\imath}
+ \Big( \frac{2a}{3}\,\hat{\imath} + \frac{a}{3}\,\hat{k} \Big) \wedge F\,\hat{\jmath}
+ \Big( -\frac{a}{3}\,\hat{\imath} + a\,\hat{\jmath} + \frac{a}{3}\,\hat{k} \Big) \wedge F\,\hat{k} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{a F}{3}\,\hat{\jmath} + \Big( \frac{2\,a F}{3}\,\hat{k} - \frac{a F}{3}\,\hat{\imath} \Big) + \Big( a F\,\hat{\imath} + \frac{a F}{3}\,\hat{\jmath} \Big) \\[6pt]
&= \frac{2\,a F}{3}\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k})
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The vector applied at $O$ has zero moment about $O$, because the point of application coincides with the pole. The other two give the moments $a F\,\hat{k}$ and $a F\,\hat{\imath}$, with the products $\hat{\imath} \wedge \hat{\jmath} = \hat{k}$ and $\hat{\jmath} \wedge \hat{k} = \hat{\imath}$.

The invariant differs from zero, therefore the equivalent minimal system is a vector applied along the **central axis** together with a couple of moment $\vec{M}_Q$, parallel to $\vec{R}$. In the product $\vec{R} \wedge \vec{M}_O$ one uses $\hat{\imath} \wedge \hat{\imath} = \vec{0}$, $\hat{\jmath} \wedge \hat{\imath} = -\hat{k}$, $\hat{\jmath} \wedge \hat{k} = \hat{\imath}$, $\hat{k} \wedge \hat{\imath} = \hat{\jmath}$ and $\hat{\imath} \wedge \hat{k} = -\hat{\jmath}$, and the terms in $\hat{\jmath}$ cancel out.

The central axis is the line through the point of coordinates $\left( \dfrac{a}{3},\ 0,\ -\dfrac{a}{3} \right)$ parallel to $\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}$. The magnitude of the moment about its points equals $\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\,a F \approx 1.15\,a F$, whereas about $O$ it equals $a F\sqrt{2} \approx 1.41\,a F$: the moment about the central axis is the smallest possible.

The check adds the three moments about $Q$ and recovers the same vector, parallel to $\vec{R}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The vectors $(P_1, F\,\hat{\jmath})$ with $P_1 - O = a\,\hat{\imath}$ and $(P_2, F\,\hat{\imath})$ with $P_2 - O = c\,\hat{k}$ are given, where $c \geq 0$ is a parameter, as the diagram in the figure shows. Determine for which values of $c$ the system is equivalent to a single applied vector and write the central axis in that case; then compute the central axis and the minimum moment for $c = a$.

::struttura{frecce="0,0,-1,-0.72,x;0,0,2,0,y;0,0,0,2,z" punti="-0.7,-0.504,P_1;0,1.4,P_2" forze="0.5 -0.504,1.2,0,;-0.6 0.968,-0.6,-0.432," tratteggi="0,0 0,1.4" etichette="0.2,-0.34,O;0.65,-0.92,v_1;-0.95,1.12,v_2;-0.22,0.68,c" riferimento=0 titolo="The two applied vectors: the height $c$ of the point $P_2$ is the parameter"}

$$
\vec{R} = F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}) ,
\qquad
\vec{M}_O = \underbrace{a\,\hat{\imath} \wedge F\,\hat{\jmath}}_{(P_1, \vec{v}_1)} + \underbrace{c\,\hat{k} \wedge F\,\hat{\imath}}_{(P_2, \vec{v}_2)} = a F\,\hat{k} + c F\,\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
I = \vec{R} \cdot \vec{M}_O = F\,(0 + c F + 0) = c F^2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
I = 0 \ \Longleftrightarrow\ c = 0
$$
For $c = 0$:
$$
\vec{R} \wedge \vec{M}_O = F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}) \wedge a F\,\hat{k} = a F^2\,(-\hat{\jmath} + \hat{\imath})
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
Q - O = \frac{a}{2}\,(\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath}) ,
$$
and the central axis is the line through $Q$ parallel to $\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}$, along which the moment vanishes. For $c = a$:
$$
\vec{M}_O = a F\,(\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) ,
\qquad
\vec{R} \wedge \vec{M}_O = F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}) \wedge a F\,(\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) = a F^2\,(\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k})
$$
$$
Q - O = \frac{a}{2}\,(\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) ,
\qquad
\vec{M}_Q = \frac{a F^2}{2F^2}\,F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}) = \frac{a F}{2}\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}) ,
\qquad
|\vec{M}_Q| = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,a F
$$

Check for $c = 0$ with Varignon's theorem: the two lines of action are the line through $P_1$ parallel to $\hat{\jmath}$ and the $x$ axis, which meet at $P_1$; hence the system is equivalent to $(P_1, \vec{R})$ and the central axis passes through $P_1$. With $\lambda = \dfrac{a}{2F}$ in the equation of the central axis one obtains
$$
Q - O + \lambda\,\vec{R} \ \text{ with } \ \lambda\,\vec{R} = \frac{a}{2}\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\frac{a}{2}\,(\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath}) + \frac{a}{2}\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}) = a\,\hat{\imath} = P_1 - O .
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The invariant equals $c F^2$, hence it depends only on the height of the second point of application and vanishes if and only if $c = 0$. For $c = 0$ both lines of action lie in the plane $Oxy$, therefore the system is planar and reduces to a single applied vector; for $c > 0$ the lines are skew and the reduction to a single vector is not possible.

The formula for the central axis applies in both cases, because it requires only $\vec{R} \neq \vec{0}$. For $c = 0$ the moment about the points of the axis vanishes, and the central axis coincides with the line of action of the single equivalent vector. For $c = a$ the minimum moment has magnitude $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,a F$, that is $\dfrac{|I|}{|\vec{R}|} = \dfrac{a F^2}{F\sqrt{2}}$.

The check examines the case $c = 0$ with **Varignon's theorem**. The two lines of action meet at $P_1$, hence the system is equivalent to the resultant applied at $P_1$ and the central axis must contain $P_1$. The substitution $\lambda = \dfrac{a}{2F}$ in the parametric equation of the axis gives exactly $P_1$, and this confirms the result.

</div>
