## Exercise 1

On a rigid cube of side $a$, with three edges on the coordinate axes and one vertex at $O$, there act the applied vectors $(A, F\,\hat{\imath})$, $(B, F\,\hat{\jmath})$ and $(C, F\,\hat{k})$, with $A - O = a\,\hat{\jmath}$, $B - O = a\,\hat{k}$ and $C - O = a\,\hat{\imath}$, as the diagram in the figure shows. Reduce the system to the equivalent minimal system and determine the central axis.

::struttura{frecce="0,0,-1.15,-0.828,x;0,0,2.3,0,y;0,0,0,2.3,z" tratteggi="0.0,0.0 -0.7,-0.504 0.7,-0.504 1.4,0.0 0.0,0.0;0.0,1.4 -0.7,0.896 0.7,0.896 1.4,1.4 0.0,1.4;-0.7,-0.504 -0.7,0.896;0.7,-0.504 0.7,0.896;1.4,0.0 1.4,1.4;0.0,0.0 0.0,1.4;0.0,0.0 0.7,0.896" punti="1.4,0,A;0,1.4,B;-0.7,-0.504,C" forze="0.8 -0.432,-0.6,-0.432,;1.2 1.4,1.2,0,;-0.7 0.696,0,1.2," etichette="0.2,-0.34,O;0.95,-0.5,v_1;1.35,1.46,v_2;-0.6,0.78,v_3" riferimento=0 titolo="The cube with the three vectors applied at the vertices $A$, $B$ and $C$"}

$$
\vec{R} = F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) ,
\qquad
|\vec{R}|^2 = 3F^2
$$
$$
\vec{M}_O = \underbrace{a\,\hat{\jmath} \wedge F\,\hat{\imath}}_{(A, \vec{v}_1)} + \underbrace{a\,\hat{k} \wedge F\,\hat{\jmath}}_{(B, \vec{v}_2)} + \underbrace{a\,\hat{\imath} \wedge F\,\hat{k}}_{(C, \vec{v}_3)} = -\,a F\,\hat{k} - a F\,\hat{\imath} - a F\,\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
I = \vec{R} \cdot \vec{M}_O = F\,(-\,a F)\,(1 + 1 + 1) = -\,3\,a F^2 \neq 0
$$
$$
\vec{R} \wedge \vec{M}_O = F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) \wedge \big[ -\,a F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) \big] = \vec{0}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
Q = O
$$
$$
\vec{M}_O = \frac{I}{|\vec{R}|^2}\,\vec{R} = \frac{-\,3\,a F^2}{3F^2}\,F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) = -\,a F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) ,
\qquad
|\vec{M}_O| = \sqrt{3}\,a F
$$
The central axis is the line through $O$ parallel to $\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}$, that is the diagonal of the cube issuing from $O$.

Check with the pole $D$, the opposite vertex, with $D - O = a\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k})$, which belongs to the diagonal:
$$
\vec{M}_D = \vec{M}_O + (O - D) \wedge \vec{R} = -\,a F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) - a\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) \wedge F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) = -\,a F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k})
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The three moments are obtained with $\hat{\jmath} \wedge \hat{\imath} = -\hat{k}$, $\hat{k} \wedge \hat{\jmath} = -\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\imath} \wedge \hat{k} = -\hat{\jmath}$, hence the resultant moment is parallel to $\vec{R}$ and oppositely oriented.

The invariant differs from zero, therefore the equivalent minimal system is an applied vector plus a couple. The product $\vec{R} \wedge \vec{M}_O$ vanishes precisely because $\vec{M}_O$ is parallel to $\vec{R}$; it follows that $Q = O$, that is **the origin already belongs to the central axis** and the moment about $O$ is already the minimum moment.

The central axis is the diagonal of the cube through $O$. The system is therefore equivalent to the vector $\vec{R}$ applied along that diagonal together with a couple of moment $-\,a F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k})$, that is to a force and a couple with the same axis.

The check computes the moment about a second point of the diagonal. The additional term of the change of pole formula contains the cross product of two parallel vectors, hence it vanishes, and the moment stays the same. The result confirms that the diagonal is the central axis.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The three applied vectors parallel to $\hat{\jmath}$ $(P_1, F\,\hat{\jmath})$, $(P_2, \lambda F\,\hat{\jmath})$ and $(P_3, -\,2F\,\hat{\jmath})$ are given, with $P_1 - O = a\,\hat{\imath}$, $P_2 - O = a\,\hat{k}$, $P_3 - O = a\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $\lambda$ a real parameter, as the diagram in the figure shows. Classify the system as $\lambda$ varies and determine, in the cases in which it exists, the single equivalent vector with its point of application.

::struttura{frecce="0,0,-1.1,-0.792,x;0,0,2.2,0,y;0,0,0,2.2,z" punti="-0.7,-0.504,P_1;0,1.4,P_2;1.4,0,P_3" forze="0.3 -0.504,1,0,;1 1.4,1,0,;0.4 0,-1,0," etichette="0.2,-0.34,O;0.42,-0.92,F;1.15,1.6,λF;0.6,0.26,2F" riferimento=0 titolo="The three vectors parallel to $\hat{\jmath}$, with the coefficient $\lambda$ of the second one as parameter"}

$$
\vec{R} = F\,(1 + \lambda - 2)\,\hat{\jmath} = F\,(\lambda - 1)\,\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
\vec{M}_O = \underbrace{a\,\hat{\imath} \wedge F\,\hat{\jmath}}_{(P_1, \vec{v}_1)} + \underbrace{a\,\hat{k} \wedge \lambda F\,\hat{\jmath}}_{(P_2, \vec{v}_2)} + \underbrace{a\,\hat{\jmath} \wedge (-\,2F\,\hat{\jmath})}_{(P_3, \vec{v}_3)} = a F\,\hat{k} - \lambda\,a F\,\hat{\imath}
$$
$$
I = \vec{R} \cdot \vec{M}_O = F\,(\lambda - 1)\,\hat{\jmath} \cdot \big( -\,\lambda\,a F\,\hat{\imath} + a F\,\hat{k} \big) = 0
\qquad \text{for every } \lambda
$$

- For $\lambda = 1$ the resultant vanishes and $\vec{M}_O = a F\,(\hat{k} - \hat{\imath}) \neq \vec{0}$: the system is equivalent to a **couple** of moment $a F\,(\hat{k} - \hat{\imath})$ and magnitude $a F\sqrt{2}$.
- For $\lambda \neq 1$ the resultant differs from the zero vector and the invariant vanishes, hence the system is equivalent to a **single applied vector** $\big( C, F\,(\lambda - 1)\,\hat{\jmath} \big)$, with $C$ the center of the parallel vectors:
$$
C - O = \frac{F\,(P_1 - O) + \lambda F\,(P_2 - O) - 2F\,(P_3 - O)}{F\,(\lambda - 1)}
= \frac{a\,\hat{\imath} + \lambda\,a\,\hat{k} - 2a\,\hat{\jmath}}{\lambda - 1} .
$$

Check for $\lambda \neq 1$ with the equation of the central axis:
$$
\vec{R} \wedge \vec{M}_O = F\,(\lambda - 1)\,\hat{\jmath} \wedge a F\,(-\lambda\,\hat{\imath} + \hat{k}) = a F^2\,(\lambda - 1)\,(\lambda\,\hat{k} + \hat{\imath})
$$
$$
Q - O = \frac{a F^2\,(\lambda - 1)\,(\hat{\imath} + \lambda\,\hat{k})}{F^2\,(\lambda - 1)^2} = \frac{a\,(\hat{\imath} + \lambda\,\hat{k})}{\lambda - 1} ,
$$
and the point $Q$ differs from $C$ only in the component along $\hat{\jmath}$, hence it belongs to the line through $C$ parallel to $\vec{R}$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The three vectors are parallel to one another, hence the moment of the third one vanishes, because its position vector is parallel to the vector itself. The resultant moment is orthogonal to $\hat{\jmath}$, and the resultant is parallel to $\hat{\jmath}$; therefore the scalar invariant vanishes for every value of $\lambda$, as happens in every system of parallel vectors.

The classification follows from the **reduction theorem**. With $\lambda = 1$ the sum of the coefficients vanishes, the center is not defined and the system reduces to a couple; its moment is the same about every pole. With $\lambda \neq 1$ the system reduces to the vector $\vec{R}$ alone applied at the **center of the parallel vectors**.

The center is obtained from the average of the points of application weighted with the coefficients. For $\lambda = 3$, for example, one finds $C - O = \dfrac{a\,\hat{\imath} + 3a\,\hat{k} - 2a\,\hat{\jmath}}{2}$ and the resultant equals $2F\,\hat{\jmath}$.

The check recomputes the point with the equation of the **central axis**, which does not use the formula for the center. The two points differ only along $\hat{\jmath}$, that is along the common direction of the vectors, therefore they determine the same line, and the central axis is the line of action of the single equivalent vector, as the theorem predicts when the invariant vanishes.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The vectors $(O, F\,\hat{\imath})$, $(P_2, F\,\hat{\jmath})$ with $P_2 - O = a\,\hat{k}$ and $(P_3, \lambda F\,\hat{k})$ with $P_3 - O = a\,\hat{\imath}$ are given, where $\lambda$ is a real parameter, as the diagram in the figure shows. Determine for which value of $\lambda$ the system is equivalent to a single applied vector and write the central axis in that case.

::struttura{frecce="0,0,-1.1,-0.792,x;0,0,2.2,0,y;0,0,0,2.2,z" punti="0,0;0,1.4,P_2;-0.7,-0.504,P_3" forze="-0.6 -0.432,-0.6,-0.432,;1.2 1.4,1.2,0,;-0.7 0.696,0,1.2," etichette="0.2,-0.3,O;-0.9,-0.28,F;1.35,1.46,F;-0.62,0.78,λF" riferimento=0 titolo="The three vectors applied at $O$, $P_2$ and $P_3$, with the coefficient $\lambda$ of the third one as parameter"}

$$
\vec{R} = F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \lambda\,\hat{k}) ,
\qquad
|\vec{R}|^2 = F^2\,(2 + \lambda^2)
$$
$$
\vec{M}_O = \underbrace{\vec{0}}_{(O, F\hat{\imath})} + \underbrace{a\,\hat{k} \wedge F\,\hat{\jmath}}_{(P_2, F\hat{\jmath})} + \underbrace{a\,\hat{\imath} \wedge \lambda F\,\hat{k}}_{(P_3, \lambda F\hat{k})} = -\,a F\,\hat{\imath} - \lambda\,a F\,\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
I = \vec{R} \cdot \vec{M}_O = F \cdot (-\,a F) + F \cdot (-\,\lambda\,a F) + \lambda F \cdot 0 = -\,a F^2\,(1 + \lambda)
$$
$$
I = 0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\lambda = -\,1
$$
With $\lambda = -\,1$:
$$
\vec{R} = F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} - \hat{k}) ,
\qquad
\vec{M}_O = a F\,(-\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}) ,
\qquad
|\vec{R}|^2 = 3F^2
$$
$$
\vec{R} \wedge \vec{M}_O = F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} - \hat{k}) \wedge a F\,(-\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}) = a F^2\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + 2\hat{k})
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
Q - O = \frac{a}{3}\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + 2\hat{k})
$$
The central axis is the line through $Q$ parallel to $\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} - \hat{k}$, and the system is equivalent to the vector $\vec{R}$ alone applied at $Q$.

Check by direct computation of the moment about $Q$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec{M}_Q &= \Big( -\frac{a}{3}\,\hat{\imath} - \frac{a}{3}\,\hat{\jmath} - \frac{2a}{3}\,\hat{k} \Big) \wedge F\,\hat{\imath}
+ \Big( -\frac{a}{3}\,\hat{\imath} - \frac{a}{3}\,\hat{\jmath} + \frac{a}{3}\,\hat{k} \Big) \wedge F\,\hat{\jmath}
+ \Big( \frac{2a}{3}\,\hat{\imath} - \frac{a}{3}\,\hat{\jmath} - \frac{2a}{3}\,\hat{k} \Big) \wedge (-F\,\hat{k}) \\[6pt]
&= \Big( \frac{a F}{3}\,\hat{k} - \frac{2\,a F}{3}\,\hat{\jmath} \Big)
+ \Big( -\frac{a F}{3}\,\hat{k} - \frac{a F}{3}\,\hat{\imath} \Big)
+ \Big( \frac{2\,a F}{3}\,\hat{\jmath} + \frac{a F}{3}\,\hat{\imath} \Big) = \vec{0}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The vector applied at $O$ does not contribute to the moment about $O$. The other two give $\hat{k} \wedge \hat{\jmath} = -\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\imath} \wedge \hat{k} = -\hat{\jmath}$, therefore the resultant moment is parallel to the plane $Oxy$ and depends on $\lambda$ through the component along $\hat{\jmath}$ alone.

The invariant equals $-\,a F^2\,(1 + \lambda)$ and vanishes for $\lambda = -\,1$. By the **reduction theorem**, since the resultant never vanishes, with $\lambda = -\,1$ the system is equivalent to a single applied vector, whereas for every other value of $\lambda$ a couple is needed as well.

In the product $\vec{R} \wedge \vec{M}_O$ the components are computed one by one: the first is $1 \cdot 0 - (-1) \cdot 1 = 1$, the second is $(-1) \cdot (-1) - 1 \cdot 0 = 1$, the third is $1 \cdot 1 - 1 \cdot (-1) = 2$.

The check computes the moment about $Q$ by adding the three contributions and obtains the zero vector, as it must be on a central axis with vanishing invariant. The system is therefore equivalent to the **vector $\vec{R}$ alone applied at $Q$**.

</div>
