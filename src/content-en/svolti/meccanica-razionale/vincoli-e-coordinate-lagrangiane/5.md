## Exercise 1

The rod $AB$ in the figure, of length $\ell$, has the end $A$ on a smooth vertical guide and the end $B$ on a smooth horizontal guide, meeting at $O$. Write the virtual displacements of $A$, of $B$ and of the midpoint $G$ as functions of $\delta\vartheta$, and verify that they are compatible with the rigidity constraint of the rod.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,2.4;B,3.2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,180;B,carrello,270" linee="0,-0.4 0,3.6;-0.4,0 4.2,0" punti="1.6,1.2,G,baricentro" frecce="0,2.4,0,3.2,δA;3.2,0,2.35,0,δB;1.6,1.2,1.12,1.84,δG" quote="A,B,ℓ,-0.9" etichette="2.75,0.25,ϑ" titolo="Virtual displacements of the rod with its ends on two orthogonal guides"}

$$
A - O = \ell\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath},
\qquad
B - O = \ell\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath},
\qquad
G - O = \frac{\ell}{2}\,\big( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} \big)
$$
$$
\delta A = \ell\cos\vartheta\,\delta\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath},
\qquad
\delta B = -\ell\sin\vartheta\,\delta\vartheta\,\hat{\imath},
\qquad
\delta G = \frac{\ell}{2}\,\delta\vartheta\,\big( -\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} \big)
$$
$$
B - A = \ell\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} - \ell\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath},
\qquad
\delta B - \delta A = -\ell\,\delta\vartheta\,\big( \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} \big)
$$

Check of the rigidity constraint:
$$
\delta\big( |B - A|^2 \big) = 2\,(B - A) \cdot (\delta B - \delta A) = -2\ell^2\,\delta\vartheta\,\big( \cos\vartheta\sin\vartheta - \sin\vartheta\cos\vartheta \big) = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The system has one degree of freedom and the Lagrangian coordinate is the angle $\vartheta$ of the rod with the horizontal guide. The virtual displacements are obtained by differentiating the positions with respect to $\vartheta$ and multiplying by the arbitrary $\delta\vartheta$, as the definition of virtual displacement establishes.

The virtual displacement of $A$ is vertical and that of $B$ is horizontal, hence each is directed along its own guide, as must happen because the two ends stay on the guides. For $\delta\vartheta > 0$ the point $A$ rises, since the coefficient $\ell\cos\vartheta$ is positive for $0 < \vartheta < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, and the point $B$ moves closer to $O$, since the coefficient $-\ell\sin\vartheta$ is negative. The virtual displacement of $G$ is orthogonal to $G - O$, in agreement with the fact that the midpoint travels along a circle of center $O$.

The constraints are fixed, hence the possible displacements coincide with the virtual ones up to the factor $dt$, and it is enough to replace $\delta\vartheta$ with $\dot{\vartheta}\,dt$.

The check verifies the rigidity constraint in the form given by the **theorem on virtual displacements**: the function $f = |B - A|^2 - \ell^2$ has gradient proportional to $B - A$, therefore the condition to be satisfied is $(B - A) \cdot (\delta B - \delta A) = 0$. The computation **gives zero** for every $\vartheta$ and for every $\delta\vartheta$, hence the displacements written respect the rigidity of the rod.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The material point $P$ in the figure is threaded on a smooth straight guide that rotates in the plane $Oxy$ about the fixed point $O$ with assigned constant angular velocity $\omega$; at the instant $t = 0$ the guide coincides with the $x$ axis. Choose the Lagrangian coordinate, write the virtual displacement and the possible displacement of $P$, compute their difference and give its values at the instant $t = 0$ for $s = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\omega = 3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;P,2.6,1.5,-" linee="-0.5,-0.29 4.5,2.6" tratteggi="0,0 3.4,0" vincoli="O,cerniera" punti="2.6,1.5,P,pieno" frecce="2.6,1.5,3.47,2,δP;2.6,1.5,2.1,2.37,sω dt" coppie="1.2 0.69,1,ω" etichette="1.15,0.25,ωt" titolo="Point on a rotating guide: the possible displacement adds to the virtual displacement the term due to the rotation of the guide"}

$$
q = s = |P - O|,
\qquad
P - O = s\,\big( \cos(\omega t)\,\hat{\imath} + \sin(\omega t)\,\hat{\jmath} \big)
$$
$$
\delta P = \delta s\,\big( \cos(\omega t)\,\hat{\imath} + \sin(\omega t)\,\hat{\jmath} \big)
$$
$$
dP = ds\,\big( \cos(\omega t)\,\hat{\imath} + \sin(\omega t)\,\hat{\jmath} \big) + s\,\omega\,dt\,\big( -\sin(\omega t)\,\hat{\imath} + \cos(\omega t)\,\hat{\jmath} \big)
$$
$$
dP - \delta P \Big|_{\delta s \,=\, ds} = s\,\omega\,dt\,\big( -\sin(\omega t)\,\hat{\imath} + \cos(\omega t)\,\hat{\jmath} \big)
$$
$$
t = 0:
\qquad
\delta P = \delta s\,\hat{\imath},
\qquad
dP = ds\,\hat{\imath} + 6\,dt\,\hat{\jmath}
$$

Check with the case of a guide at rest:
$$
\omega = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
dP - \delta P \Big|_{\delta s \,=\, ds} = \vec{0}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The guide is a **moving** constraint, since its equation $x\sin(\omega t) - y\cos(\omega t) = 0$ contains time explicitly. The point has two coordinates and the constraint imposes one relation between them, therefore one degree of freedom remains; as Lagrangian coordinate one takes the distance $s$ of $P$ from $O$, measured along the guide.

The virtual displacement is obtained by differentiating the position with respect to $s$ with time frozen, hence it is directed along the guide in the position that the guide occupies at the instant considered. The possible displacement is obtained instead by differentiating the position with respect to all the variables, $s$ and $t$, therefore it also contains the term $\dfrac{\partial P}{\partial t}\,dt$, which is orthogonal to the guide and equals $s\,\omega\,dt$ in magnitude.

The difference between the two displacements, computed for the same advance along the guide, **is the term due to the rotation of the guide**. At the instant $t = 0$ the guide coincides with the $x$ axis, hence the virtual displacement is horizontal, whereas the possible displacement also has a vertical component of $6\,dt$, with $s\,\omega = 2 \cdot 3 = 6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

The check considers the case $\omega = 0$, in which the guide is at rest and the constraint is fixed; then the difference vanishes and the two displacements coincide, as the **theorem on virtual displacements** establishes for fixed constraints.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The double pendulum in the figure is formed by the rods $OA$ and $AB$, of lengths $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$, hinged to each other at $A$, with $OA$ hinged to the fixed point $O$. The Lagrangian coordinates are the angles $\vartheta_1$ and $\vartheta_2$ that the two rods form with the downward vertical. Write the virtual displacements of $A$ and of $B$ and verify that they respect the two rigidity constraints.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1,-1.73;B,2.03,-2.96" aste="O-A;A-B" cerniere="A" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.3;1,-1.73 1,-3.3" frecce="1,-1.73,1.87,-1.23,δA;2.03,-2.96,2.73,-2.48,δB" etichette="0.32,-1.05,ϑ_1;1.3,-2.6,ϑ_2" titolo="Virtual displacements of the double pendulum: $\delta A$ is orthogonal to $OA$ and $\delta B - \delta A$ is orthogonal to $AB$"}

$$
A - O = \ell_1\,\big( \sin\vartheta_1\,\hat{\imath} - \cos\vartheta_1\,\hat{\jmath} \big),
\qquad
B - A = \ell_2\,\big( \sin\vartheta_2\,\hat{\imath} - \cos\vartheta_2\,\hat{\jmath} \big)
$$
$$
\delta A = \ell_1\,\delta\vartheta_1\,\big( \cos\vartheta_1\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta_1\,\hat{\jmath} \big)
$$
$$
\delta B = \ell_1\,\delta\vartheta_1\,\big( \cos\vartheta_1\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta_1\,\hat{\jmath} \big) + \ell_2\,\delta\vartheta_2\,\big( \cos\vartheta_2\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta_2\,\hat{\jmath} \big)
$$

Check of the two rigidity constraints:
$$
(A - O) \cdot \delta A = \ell_1^2\,\delta\vartheta_1\,\big( \sin\vartheta_1\cos\vartheta_1 - \cos\vartheta_1\sin\vartheta_1 \big) = 0
$$
$$
(B - A) \cdot (\delta B - \delta A) = \ell_2^2\,\delta\vartheta_2\,\big( \sin\vartheta_2\cos\vartheta_2 - \cos\vartheta_2\sin\vartheta_2 \big) = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The system has two degrees of freedom, hence the virtual displacements depend on the two arbitrary parameters $\delta\vartheta_1$ and $\delta\vartheta_2$. The virtual displacement of $A$ is obtained by differentiating $A - O$ with respect to $\vartheta_1$, the only coordinate on which it depends, and multiplying by $\delta\vartheta_1$; the result is orthogonal to $A - O$, as it must be for a point that moves on a circle of center $O$.

The virtual displacement of $B$ has two contributions, one for each coordinate: the first is the displacement that $B$ undergoes when the rod $OA$ alone rotates, and it coincides with $\delta A$ since in that case the rod $AB$ translates; the second is the displacement due to the rotation of the rod $AB$ alone about $A$. The sum of the two is the general form of the virtual displacement of a system with two degrees of freedom.

In the particular case $\delta\vartheta_2 = \delta\vartheta_1$ the two rods rotate through the same angle and the system behaves as a single rigid body; in the case $\delta\vartheta_1 = 0$ the point $A$ stays still and the second rod alone moves.

The check verifies the two constraints in the form given by the **theorem on virtual displacements**. The first constraint, $|A - O| = \ell_1$, requires $(A - O) \cdot \delta A = 0$; the second, $|B - A| = \ell_2$, requires $(B - A) \cdot (\delta B - \delta A) = 0$. Both scalar products **turn out to be zero** for every choice of $\delta\vartheta_1$ and $\delta\vartheta_2$.

</div>
