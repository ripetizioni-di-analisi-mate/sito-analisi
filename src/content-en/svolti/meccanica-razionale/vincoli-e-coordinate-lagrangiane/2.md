## Exercise 1

The mechanism in the figure, called a slider-crank, is formed by the crank $OA$ of length $r$, hinged to the fixed point $O$, and by the connecting rod $AB$ of length $\ell = 2r$, hinged at $A$ to the crank and with the end $B$ constrained by a roller support to the horizontal guide through $O$. The plane is vertical and the constraints are smooth and bilateral. Write the constraint equations, determine the number of degrees of freedom, express the position of $B$ as a function of the angle of the crank and compute the stroke of the connecting-rod end.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,0.77,0.92;B,2.99,0" aste="O-A;A-B" cerniere="A" vincoli="O,cerniera;B,carrello,270" linee="-0.6,0 3.8,0" quote="A,B,ℓ = 2r,-0.7" etichette="0.3,0.6,r;0.55,0.25,ϑ;2.6,0.25,φ" titolo="Slider-crank with $\ell = 2r$: the crank rotates about $O$ and the connecting-rod end $B$ slides on the guide"}

$$
\begin{cases}
x_A^2 + y_A^2 = r^2 \\[4pt]
(x_B - x_A)^2 + (y_B - y_A)^2 = \ell^2 \\[4pt]
y_B = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
n = 4 - 3 = 1,
\qquad
\vartheta = \text{angle of the crank with the } x \text{ axis}
$$
$$
A - O = r\,\big( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} \big)
$$
$$
x_B = r\cos\vartheta + \sqrt{\ell^2 - r^2\sin^2\vartheta} = r\,\Big( \cos\vartheta + \sqrt{3 + \cos^2\vartheta} \Big)
$$
$$
\sin\varphi = \frac{r}{\ell}\,\sin\vartheta = \frac{1}{2}\,\sin\vartheta
$$
$$
x_B(0) = 3r,
\qquad
x_B\Big( \frac{\pi}{2} \Big) = \sqrt{3}\,r,
\qquad
x_B(\pi) = r,
\qquad
c = x_B(0) - x_B(\pi) = 2r
$$

Check of the constraint $|B - A| = \ell$ for $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$:
$$
|B - A|^2 = \big( \sqrt{3}\,r - 0 \big)^2 + \big( 0 - r \big)^2 = 3r^2 + r^2 = 4r^2 = \ell^2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The mechanism is described by the two points $A$ and $B$, hence by four coordinates. The crank imposes $|A - O| = r$, the connecting rod imposes $|B - A| = \ell$ and the roller support imposes $y_B = 0$; these three constraint equations are independent, therefore the system has $4 - 3 = 1$ degree of freedom.

The angle $\vartheta$ of the crank is chosen as Lagrangian coordinate, since the crank makes complete turns and $\vartheta$ identifies the configuration without ambiguity. The position of $A$ follows from the first constraint equation. In order to find $x_B$ one substitutes $y_B = 0$ and the coordinates of $A$ into the second equation, which becomes $(x_B - r\cos\vartheta)^2 + r^2\sin^2\vartheta = \ell^2$; from it one obtains $x_B - r\cos\vartheta = \pm\sqrt{\ell^2 - r^2\sin^2\vartheta}$, and the positive sign is taken because the connecting-rod end lies to the right of the crank. With $\ell = 2r$ the radicand equals $4r^2 - r^2\sin^2\vartheta = r^2\,(3 + \cos^2\vartheta)$.

The angle $\varphi$ of the connecting rod with the guide is not a second Lagrangian coordinate. The vertical projection of the connecting rod must span the height of $A$, hence $\ell\sin\varphi = r\sin\vartheta$ and $\varphi$ turns out to be a function of $\vartheta$. Since $\ell = 2r$, the angle $\varphi$ never exceeds $30^\circ$.

The **stroke** of the connecting-rod end is the distance between the two extreme positions, which are reached when crank and connecting rod are aligned, that is, for $\vartheta = 0$ and $\vartheta = \pi$; it equals $c = 2r$, twice the crank radius, as in reciprocating engines.

The check verifies the length of the connecting rod in the configuration $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, in which $A = (0, r)$ and $B = (\sqrt{3}\,r, 0)$, and the distance turns out to be $2r = \ell$, hence the expression of $x_B$ is correct.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In the plane $Oxy$ the point $P_1$ is constrained to the smooth circular guide of center $O$ and radius $a$, and the point $P_2$ is connected to $P_1$ by a rigid rod of negligible mass and length $\ell$, hinged to $P_1$ and free to rotate, as in the figure. Write the constraint equations, verify that they are independent in the configuration $P_1 = (a, 0)$, $P_2 = (a + \ell, 0)$, determine the number of degrees of freedom and choose the Lagrangian coordinates.

::struttura{linee="2.00,0.00 1.97,0.35 1.88,0.68 1.73,1.00 1.53,1.29 1.29,1.53 1.00,1.73 0.68,1.88 0.35,1.97 0.00,2.00 -0.35,1.97 -0.68,1.88 -1.00,1.73 -1.29,1.53 -1.53,1.29 -1.73,1.00 -1.88,0.68 -1.97,0.35 -2.00,0.00 -1.97,-0.35 -1.88,-0.68 -1.73,-1.00 -1.53,-1.29 -1.29,-1.53 -1.00,-1.73 -0.68,-1.88 -0.35,-1.97 -0.00,-2.00 0.35,-1.97 0.68,-1.88 1.00,-1.73 1.29,-1.53 1.53,-1.29 1.73,-1.00 1.88,-0.68 1.97,-0.35 2.00,-0.00" nodi="O,0,0,-;P,1.29,1.53,-;Q,3.3,2.2,-" aste="P-Q" punti="0,0,O,pieno;1.29,1.53,P_1,pieno;3.3,2.2,P_2,pieno" tratteggi="0,0 1.29,1.53;1.29,1.53 3.2,1.53;0,0 2.3,0" etichette="0.62,0.22,ϑ;1.85,1.72,φ;0.35,1.05,a;2.3,2.1,ℓ" titolo="Point $P_1$ on a circular guide of radius $a$ and rod $P_1P_2$ of length $\ell$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
f_1 = x_1^2 + y_1^2 - a^2 = 0 \\[4pt]
f_2 = (x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2 - \ell^2 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
J = \begin{pmatrix}
2x_1 & 2y_1 & 0 & 0 \\[4pt]
-2(x_2 - x_1) & -2(y_2 - y_1) & 2(x_2 - x_1) & 2(y_2 - y_1)
\end{pmatrix}
$$
$$
J\big|_{(a,\,0,\,a+\ell,\,0)} = \begin{pmatrix}
2a & 0 & 0 & 0 \\[4pt]
-2\ell & 0 & 2\ell & 0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\operatorname{rank} J = 2
$$
$$
n = 2 \cdot 2 - 2 = 2
$$
$$
P_1 - O = a\,\big( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} \big),
\qquad
P_2 - O = \big( a\cos\vartheta + \ell\cos\varphi \big)\,\hat{\imath} + \big( a\sin\vartheta + \ell\sin\varphi \big)\,\hat{\jmath}
$$

Check of the constraint equations on the parametrization:
$$
f_1 = a^2\cos^2\vartheta + a^2\sin^2\vartheta - a^2 = 0,
\qquad
f_2 = \ell^2\cos^2\varphi + \ell^2\sin^2\varphi - \ell^2 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two points have four coordinates, and the constraints are two: the circular guide forces $P_1$ to be at distance $a$ from $O$, the rod forces $P_2$ to be at distance $\ell$ from $P_1$. Both equations contain the positions alone, hence the constraints are holonomic, bilateral and fixed.

The Jacobian matrix has two rows, one per constraint, and four columns, one per coordinate; the first row contains the derivatives of $f_1$, which does not depend on $P_2$, therefore the last two columns of the first row are zero. In the assigned configuration the two rows turn out to be $(2a,\,0,\,0,\,0)$ and $(-2\ell,\,0,\,2\ell,\,0)$, which are linearly independent since the second has third component $2\ell \neq 0$ while the first has third component zero. The rank therefore equals $2$ and the **theorem on the degrees of freedom** applies, hence the system has $n = 4 - 2 = 2$ degrees of freedom.

As Lagrangian coordinates one takes the angle $\vartheta$ that identifies $P_1$ on the circular guide and the angle $\varphi$ that the rod forms with the $x$ axis. The two angles are independent, since to each pair of values there corresponds one and only one admissible configuration.

The check substitutes the parametrization into the constraint equations, which turn out to be satisfied identically in $\vartheta$ and in $\varphi$, hence the choice of the coordinates **is correct**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

In space a rigid rod $AB$ of length $\ell$, regarded as a segment and identified by its ends, has the end $A$ constrained to the spherical surface of center $O$ and radius $a$, as in the figure, while the end $B$ is free. The constraint is smooth and bilateral. Determine the number of degrees of freedom and choose the Lagrangian coordinates.

::struttura{linee="1.50,0.00 1.46,0.33 1.35,0.65 1.17,0.94 0.94,1.17 0.65,1.35 0.33,1.46 0.00,1.50 -0.33,1.46 -0.65,1.35 -0.94,1.17 -1.17,0.94 -1.35,0.65 -1.46,0.33 -1.50,0.00 -1.46,-0.33 -1.35,-0.65 -1.17,-0.94 -0.94,-1.17 -0.65,-1.35 -0.33,-1.46 -0.00,-1.50 0.33,-1.46 0.65,-1.35 0.94,-1.17 1.17,-0.94 1.35,-0.65 1.46,-0.33 1.50,-0.00" nodi="O,0,0,-;A,0.94,1.17,-;B,3.1,2.3,-" aste="A-B" punti="0,0,O,pieno;0.94,1.17,A,pieno;3.1,2.3,B,pieno" tratteggi="0,0 0.94,1.17" etichette="0.25,0.85,a;2.1,1.95,ℓ" titolo="Rod with the end $A$ on a spherical surface of radius $a$: the figure shows a cross section of it"}

$$
\begin{cases}
f_1 = x_A^2 + y_A^2 + z_A^2 - a^2 = 0 \\[4pt]
f_2 = (x_B - x_A)^2 + (y_B - y_A)^2 + (z_B - z_A)^2 - \ell^2 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
n = 3 \cdot 2 - 2 = 4
$$
$$
A - O = a\,\big( \sin\vartheta\cos\varphi\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\sin\varphi\,\hat{\jmath} + \cos\vartheta\,\hat{k} \big)
$$
$$
B - A = \ell\,\big( \sin\alpha\cos\beta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\alpha\sin\beta\,\hat{\jmath} + \cos\alpha\,\hat{k} \big)
$$

Check with the count for rigid bodies:
$$
n = 5 - 1 = 4
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The system is described by the two ends of the rod, hence by six coordinates. The constraints are two: the spherical surface forces $A$ to be at distance $a$ from $O$, the rigidity forces $B$ to be at distance $\ell$ from $A$. The two equations are independent, since the first does not contain the coordinates of $B$ while the second contains them with derivatives not all zero; therefore the system has $n = 6 - 2 = 4$ degrees of freedom.

As Lagrangian coordinates one takes two angles for the position of $A$ on the spherical surface, the colatitude $\vartheta$ and the longitude $\varphi$, and two angles for the direction of the rod, the colatitude $\alpha$ and the longitude $\beta$ of the unit vector of $B - A$. The four coordinates are independent, since the position of $A$ on the sphere and the direction of the rod are chosen separately.

The check uses the count for rigid bodies. A rod regarded as a segment has $5$ degrees of freedom in space, because the rotation about its own axis does not change the position of its points; the constraint that brings the end $A$ onto the spherical surface is a single scalar condition, therefore $5 - 1 = 4$ degrees of freedom remain. The two counts **give the same number**.

</div>
