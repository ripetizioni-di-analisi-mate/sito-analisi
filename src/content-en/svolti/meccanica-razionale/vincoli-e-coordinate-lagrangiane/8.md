## Exercise 1

The material point $P$ in the figure is connected to the fixed point $O$ by an inextensible and flexible string of length $\ell$ and negligible mass, and it moves in the vertical plane $Oxy$. Write the constraint equation, distinguish the ordinary configurations from the boundary ones together with the number of degrees of freedom in each case, write the reaction of the string and verify that the constraint is ideal.

::struttura{linee="2.00,0.00 1.97,0.35 1.88,0.68 1.73,1.00 1.53,1.29 1.29,1.53 1.00,1.73 0.68,1.88 0.35,1.97 0.00,2.00 -0.35,1.97 -0.68,1.88 -1.00,1.73 -1.29,1.53 -1.53,1.29 -1.73,1.00 -1.88,0.68 -1.97,0.35 -2.00,0.00 -1.97,-0.35 -1.88,-0.68 -1.73,-1.00 -1.53,-1.29 -1.29,-1.53 -1.00,-1.73 -0.68,-1.88 -0.35,-1.97 -0.00,-2.00 0.35,-1.97 0.68,-1.88 1.00,-1.73 1.29,-1.53 1.53,-1.29 1.73,-1.00 1.88,-0.68 1.97,-0.35 2.00,-0.00" nodi="O,0,0,-;P,1.29,-1.53,-" catene="O-P" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" punti="0,0,O,pieno;1.29,-1.53,P,pieno" reazioni="1.29 -1.53,-0.62,0.74,Φ" frecce="1.29,-1.53,2.07,-0.88,δP₁;1.29,-1.53,0.39,-1.43,δP₂" etichette="0.3,-1.1,ℓ" titolo="Point hanging from a string: on the boundary $|P - O| = \ell$ the virtual displacements form a half-plane"}

$$
f(P) = \ell^2 - |P - O|^2 \geq 0
$$
$$
|P - O| < \ell:
\qquad
\text{ordinary configuration},
\qquad
n = 2,
\qquad
\vec{\Phi} = \vec{0}
$$
$$
|P - O| = \ell:
\qquad
\text{boundary configuration},
\qquad
n = 1,
\qquad
\vec{\Phi} = -\frac{T}{\ell}\,(P - O),
\qquad
T \geq 0
$$
$$
\operatorname{grad} f = -2\,(P - O)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{grad} f \cdot \delta P \geq 0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
(P - O) \cdot \delta P \leq 0
$$

Check of the ideal character of the constraint:
$$
\delta L^{(v)} = \vec{\Phi} \cdot \delta P = -\frac{T}{\ell}\,(P - O) \cdot \delta P \geq 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The string is inextensible, hence it prevents $P$ from moving away from $O$ beyond the length $\ell$, and it is flexible, hence it does not prevent $P$ from coming closer. The constraint is therefore written as an inequality and is **unilateral**.

In the configurations in which the string is slack, that is, $|P - O| < \ell$, the inequality is strict, hence the point moves freely in the plane, has two degrees of freedom and the string exerts no force. In the boundary configurations, in which the string is taut, the point is forced to stay on the circle of center $O$ and radius $\ell$ as long as the string remains taut, therefore it has a single degree of freedom.

The reaction of the string is directed along the string and pulls the point toward $O$, hence it is opposite to $P - O$; its magnitude $T$ is the tension of the string, non-negative because the string pulls and does not push. The condition $T = 0$ signals that the string is about to become slack.

The virtual displacements in the boundary configuration are obtained from the linearized inequality. The gradient of the constraint function equals $-2\,(P - O)$, therefore the condition $\operatorname{grad} f \cdot \delta P \geq 0$ becomes $(P - O) \cdot \delta P \leq 0$. The displacements tangent to the circle and those directed inward are admitted, whereas those directed outward **are not virtual**; the set of the virtual displacements is therefore a half-plane.

The check shows that the virtual work of the reaction is non-negative. The product $(P - O) \cdot \delta P$ is less than or equal to zero and the coefficient $-\dfrac{T}{\ell}$ is non-positive, hence the product of the two is non-negative. The constraint therefore satisfies the definition of ideal constraint in the form with the inequality.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The homogeneous rod $OA$ in the figure, of length $\ell$, is hinged to the fixed point $O$ and rotates in a vertical plane; below it, at depth $d < \ell$, there is a horizontal floor on which the end $A$ can rest. Determine the interval of the admissible values of the Lagrangian coordinate, recognize the boundary configurations and verify that the support is an ideal constraint. Carry out the computations for $d = \dfrac{\ell}{2}$.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,2.6,-1.5,-" aste="O-A" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" linee="-0.8,-1.5 4,-1.5" tratteggi="0,0 3.2,0;0,0 0,-1.5" punti="2.6,-1.5,A,pieno" reazioni="2.6 -1.5,0,1,Φ_n" quote="0 0,0 -1.5,d,0.9" etichette="1.2,-0.4,ϑ" titolo="Rod hinged at $O$ with the end $A$ resting on the floor: boundary configuration"}

$$
A - O = \ell\,\big( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} \big),
\qquad
y_A \geq -d
$$
$$
\ell\sin\vartheta \geq -d
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\sin\vartheta \geq -\frac{d}{\ell}
$$
$$
d = \frac{\ell}{2}:
\qquad
\sin\vartheta \geq -\frac{1}{2}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
-\frac{\pi}{6} \leq \vartheta \leq \frac{7\pi}{6}
$$
$$
\vartheta = -\frac{\pi}{6}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\vartheta = \frac{7\pi}{6}:
\qquad
\text{boundary configurations},
\qquad
\vec{\Phi} = \Phi_n\,\hat{\jmath},
\qquad
\Phi_n \geq 0
$$
$$
\delta A = \ell\,\delta\vartheta\,\big( -\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} \big),
\qquad
\delta L^{(v)} = \Phi_n\,\ell\cos\vartheta\,\delta\vartheta
$$

Check of the sign of the virtual work in the two boundary configurations:
$$
\vartheta = -\frac{\pi}{6},
\;\;
\delta\vartheta \geq 0:
\qquad
\delta L^{(v)} = \Phi_n\,\ell\,\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\delta\vartheta \geq 0
$$
$$
\vartheta = \frac{7\pi}{6},
\;\;
\delta\vartheta \leq 0:
\qquad
\delta L^{(v)} = -\Phi_n\,\ell\,\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\delta\vartheta \geq 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The rod is hinged at $O$, hence its configuration depends on the angle $\vartheta$ alone that it forms with the $x$ axis, hence the system has one degree of freedom. The floor imposes that the ordinate of $A$ not fall below $-d$, and the ordinate of $A$ equals $\ell\sin\vartheta$; whence the inequality $\sin\vartheta \geq -\dfrac{d}{\ell}$, which is the unilateral constraint expressed in the Lagrangian coordinate.

With $d = \dfrac{\ell}{2}$ the inequality becomes $\sin\vartheta \geq -\dfrac{1}{2}$, satisfied for $\vartheta$ between $-\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ and $\dfrac{7\pi}{6}$. The rod **cannot make complete turns**: it is blocked by the floor in two configurations symmetric with respect to the downward vertical, one on the right and one on the left.

In the configurations interior to the interval the floor does not touch the rod and its reaction is zero; in the two boundary configurations the end $A$ rests on the floor, which is smooth and unilateral, therefore it exerts a vertical reaction directed upward, with $\Phi_n \geq 0$.

The virtual displacements of $A$ are orthogonal to the rod, since $A$ travels along a circle of center $O$. In the configuration $\vartheta = -\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ only the $\delta\vartheta \geq 0$ are admitted, because a negative $\delta\vartheta$ would bring $A$ below the floor; in the configuration $\vartheta = \dfrac{7\pi}{6}$ only the $\delta\vartheta \leq 0$ are admitted, for the same reason.

The check computes the virtual work of the reaction in the two boundary configurations. In both the product $\cos\vartheta\,\delta\vartheta$ turns out to be non-negative, because the sign of $\cos\vartheta$ and the admitted sign of $\delta\vartheta$ coincide; the virtual work of the reaction is therefore non-negative and **the constraint is ideal**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The double pendulum in the figure is formed by the rods $OA$ and $AB$ hinged at $A$, with Lagrangian coordinates $\vartheta_1$ and $\vartheta_2$, angles with the downward vertical. A stop mounted in the hinge $A$ prevents the two rods from forming an angle between them greater than $\beta$ in absolute value. Write the constraint, describe the set of the admissible configurations, determine the degrees of freedom in the ordinary configurations and in the boundary ones and write the virtual displacements admitted on the boundary. Carry out the computations for $\beta = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1,-1.73;B,3,-1.73" aste="O-A;A-B" cerniere="A" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.3;1,-1.73 2.73,-4.73" etichette="0.32,-1.05,ϑ_1;2.0,-2.3,β" titolo="Double pendulum with a stop: the two rods cannot form an angle greater than $\beta$"}

$$
|\vartheta_2 - \vartheta_1| \leq \beta
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\begin{cases}
\beta - \vartheta_2 + \vartheta_1 \geq 0 \\[4pt]
\beta + \vartheta_2 - \vartheta_1 \geq 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
\beta = \frac{\pi}{3}:
\qquad
\vartheta_1 - \frac{\pi}{3} \leq \vartheta_2 \leq \vartheta_1 + \frac{\pi}{3}
$$

::grafico{f="x+1.047;x-1.047" area="x+1.047;x-1.047;-3;3" xmin=-3 xmax=3 ymin=-3 ymax=3 uguale=1 nomi="ϑ₁,ϑ₂" titolo="Admissible configurations for $\beta = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$: the strip between the lines $\vartheta_2 = \vartheta_1 \pm \dfrac{\pi}{3}$"}

$$
|\vartheta_2 - \vartheta_1| < \beta:
\qquad
n = 2,
\qquad
\delta\vartheta_1,\; \delta\vartheta_2 \text{ arbitrary}
$$
$$
\vartheta_2 - \vartheta_1 = \beta:
\qquad
n = 1,
\qquad
\delta\vartheta_2 - \delta\vartheta_1 \leq 0
$$
$$
\vartheta_2 - \vartheta_1 = -\beta:
\qquad
n = 1,
\qquad
\delta\vartheta_2 - \delta\vartheta_1 \geq 0
$$

Check with the case $\beta = 0$:
$$
\vartheta_2 = \vartheta_1,
\qquad
\delta\vartheta_2 = \delta\vartheta_1,
\qquad
n = 1
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The stop limits the relative angle between the two rods, which equals $\vartheta_2 - \vartheta_1$. The condition $|\vartheta_2 - \vartheta_1| \leq \beta$ is equivalent to the two inequalities written as a system, to be satisfied together; each one is a unilateral constraint.

In the plane of the Lagrangian coordinates the set of the admissible configurations is the strip between the lines $\vartheta_2 = \vartheta_1 + \beta$ and $\vartheta_2 = \vartheta_1 - \beta$, parallel to the bisector. With $\beta = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ the strip has half-width $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ measured vertically.

In the configurations interior to the strip neither of the two inequalities is satisfied with equality, therefore the stop does not touch the rods and the system has two degrees of freedom with $\delta\vartheta_1$ and $\delta\vartheta_2$ arbitrary. In the configurations on the boundary the stop is in contact, therefore only the virtual displacements that bring the system back inside the strip or that keep it on the boundary are admitted, and $\delta\vartheta_2 - \delta\vartheta_1$ has a forced sign. As long as the stop stays in contact the relative angle is fixed, therefore the system has **a single degree of freedom**, which is the overall rotation; the virtual displacements, instead, form a half-plane.

The check considers the limit case $\beta = 0$, in which the stop locks the two rods aligned. Then the strip reduces to the bisector, the two inequalities become the single equation $\vartheta_2 = \vartheta_1$ and the system behaves as a single rod hinged at $O$, with a single degree of freedom.

</div>
