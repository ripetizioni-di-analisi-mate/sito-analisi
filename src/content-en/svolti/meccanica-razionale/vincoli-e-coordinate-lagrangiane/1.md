## Exercise 1

The system in the figure is formed by the rods $AB$ and $BC$, both of length $\ell$, lying in a vertical plane. The rod $AB$ is hinged at the fixed point $A$, the two rods are hinged to each other at $B$ and the end $C$ is constrained by a roller support to the horizontal guide through $A$. The constraints are smooth and bilateral. Determine the number of degrees of freedom, choose a Lagrangian coordinate and write the positions of $B$, of $C$ and of the midpoints $G_1$ and $G_2$ of the two rods.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,2.6;C,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello,270" linee="-0.6,0 5.2,0" quote="A,B,ℓ,-0.7;B,C,ℓ,-0.7" etichette="0.75,0.33,ϑ" titolo="Two equal rods hinged at $B$, with a hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $C$"}

$$
n = 3 \cdot 2 - (2 + 2 + 1) = 1
$$
$$
\vartheta = \text{angle that } AB \text{ forms with the guide},
\qquad
0 < \vartheta < \pi
$$
$$
B - A = \ell\,\big( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} \big),
\qquad
C - A = 2\ell \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath}
$$
$$
G_1 - A = \frac{\ell}{2}\,\big( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} \big),
\qquad
G_2 - A = \frac{3\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \frac{\ell}{2}\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
\vec{v}_B = \ell\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\big( -\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} \big),
\qquad
\vec{v}_C = -2\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath}
$$

Check of the constraint $|C - B| = \ell$:
$$
|C - B|^2 = \big( 2\ell\cos\vartheta - \ell\cos\vartheta \big)^2 + \big( \ell\sin\vartheta \big)^2 = \ell^2\cos^2\vartheta + \ell^2\sin^2\vartheta = \ell^2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The system is formed by two plane rigid bodies, hence without constraints it would have $3 \cdot 2 = 6$ parameters. The hinge at $A$ imposes two conditions, because it blocks the two components of the displacement of the point $A$ of the first rod; the internal hinge at $B$ imposes two more, because it forces the second end of the first rod and the first end of the second one to coincide; the roller support at $C$ imposes one, because it blocks the vertical displacement only. There remains $6 - 5 = 1$ degree of freedom, therefore one parameter is enough to identify the configuration.

The angle $\vartheta$ of the rod $AB$ with the guide, measured counterclockwise, is chosen as Lagrangian coordinate, since with it the position of $B$ is written immediately. The point $C$ lies on the guide, hence it has zero ordinate, and its abscissa is obtained from the condition $|C - B| = \ell$. The triangle $ABC$ has the two sides $AB$ and $BC$ equal, therefore it is isosceles and the foot of the altitude drawn from $B$ is the midpoint of $AC$; the abscissa of $B$ equals $\ell\cos\vartheta$, hence $x_C = 2\ell\cos\vartheta$.

The midpoints are obtained as the half-sums of the ends, and the velocities are obtained by differentiating the positions with respect to time, with $\vartheta$ a function of time. The velocity of $C$ is horizontal, as it must be for a point that slides on the guide. The configuration depends on $\vartheta$ alone, and every value of $\vartheta$ in $(0, \pi)$ gives an admissible configuration, hence the count of the degrees of freedom **is confirmed** by the explicit parametrization.

In the check the condition $|C - B| = \ell$ turns out to be satisfied for every value of $\vartheta$, hence the positions written do respect the constraint of the second rod.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The rod $AB$ in the figure, of length $\ell$, has the end $A$ constrained to slide on a smooth vertical guide and the end $B$ constrained to slide on a smooth horizontal guide; the two guides meet at the point $O$ and the plane is vertical. Determine the number of degrees of freedom, choose a Lagrangian coordinate and study the trajectory of the midpoint $G$ of the rod.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,2.4;B,3.2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,180;B,carrello,270" linee="0,-0.4 0,3.4;-0.4,0 4.2,0" punti="1.6,1.2,G,baricentro" quote="A,B,ℓ,-0.8" etichette="2.75,0.25,ϑ" titolo="Rod with the end $A$ on a vertical guide and the end $B$ on a horizontal guide"}

$$
n = 3 - 2 = 1
$$
$$
A - O = \ell\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath},
\qquad
B - O = \ell\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath},
\qquad
G - O = \frac{\ell}{2}\,\big( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} \big)
$$
$$
|G - O|^2 = \frac{\ell^2}{4}\,\big( \cos^2\vartheta + \sin^2\vartheta \big) = \frac{\ell^2}{4}
$$
$$
\vec{v}_A = \ell\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath},
\qquad
\vec{v}_B = -\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath},
\qquad
\vec{v}_G = \frac{\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}}{2}\,\big( -\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} \big)
$$

Check with the orthogonality between velocity and radius:
$$
\vec{v}_G \cdot (G - O) = \frac{\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}}{4}\,\big( -\sin\vartheta\cos\vartheta + \cos\vartheta\sin\vartheta \big) = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The rod is a plane rigid body, hence it starts from three parameters. The vertical guide imposes $x_A = 0$ and the horizontal guide imposes $y_B = 0$; these two conditions are independent, therefore there remains $3 - 2 = 1$ degree of freedom.

The angle $\vartheta$ that the rod forms with the horizontal guide is chosen as Lagrangian coordinate. The end $B$ lies on the horizontal guide at distance $\ell\cos\vartheta$ from $O$, since $\ell\cos\vartheta$ is the projection of the rod on the horizontal; the end $A$ lies on the vertical guide at distance $\ell\sin\vartheta$ from $O$, which is the projection of the rod on the vertical. The midpoint has coordinates equal to the half-sums, therefore its distance from $O$ equals $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ for every value of $\vartheta$.

The distance $|G - O|$ **is constant**, hence the midpoint of the rod travels along the circle of center $O$ and radius $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$. The result is also read on the right triangle $AOB$, of which $OG$ is the median to the hypotenuse, and the median to the hypotenuse of a right triangle equals half the hypotenuse.

The check confirms the result by another route, since the velocity of $G$ turns out to be orthogonal to the vector $G - O$, and a point whose velocity is always orthogonal to the position vector moves on a circle of center $O$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The system in the figure, in a vertical plane, is formed by a homogeneous disk of radius $R$ that rolls without slipping on the fixed horizontal guide $y = 0$ and by a rod $CB$ of length $\ell$, hinged to the disk at its center $C$ and free to rotate about it. Determine the number of degrees of freedom, choose the Lagrangian coordinates and write the position and the velocity of the end $B$.

::struttura{nodi="C,2,1,-;B,4.4,2.1" aste="C-B" cerchi="2,1,1" linee="-0.5,0 6,0" punti="2,1,C,baricentro;2,0,H,centro" coppie="2 1,1,ϑ" tratteggi="2,1 4.6,1" etichette="3.35,1.28,φ" titolo="Disk rolling without slipping with a rod hinged at its center"}

$$
n = 3 \cdot 2 - (2 + 2) = 2
$$
$$
\vartheta = \text{rotation angle of the disk},
\qquad
\varphi = \text{angle of the rod with the horizontal}
$$
$$
C - O = -R\,\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + R\,\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
B - O = \big( -R\,\vartheta + \ell\cos\varphi \big)\,\hat{\imath} + \big( R + \ell\sin\varphi \big)\,\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
\vec{v}_B = \big( -R\,\dot{\vartheta} - \ell\,\dot{\varphi}\sin\varphi \big)\,\hat{\imath} + \ell\,\dot{\varphi}\cos\varphi\,\hat{\jmath}
$$

Check with the velocity of the contact point:
$$
\vec{v}_H = \vec{v}_C + \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge (H - C) = -R\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\imath} + \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge (-R\,\hat{\jmath}) = \big( -R\,\dot{\vartheta} + R\,\dot{\vartheta} \big)\,\hat{\imath} = \vec{0}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The system is formed by two plane rigid bodies, hence it starts from $3 \cdot 2 = 6$ parameters. The disk that rolls without slipping on the fixed guide is subject to two conditions, the contact with the guide and the absence of slipping; the internal hinge at $C$ imposes two more. There remain $6 - 4 = 2$ degrees of freedom.

As Lagrangian coordinates one takes the rotation angle $\vartheta$ of the disk, positive counterclockwise, and the angle $\varphi$ that the rod forms with the horizontal. The center of the disk keeps the constant height $R$, because the disk stays in contact with the guide; its abscissa is obtained from the **theorem on pure rolling**, which gives $x_C + R\,\vartheta = \text{constant}$, and the origin of time is chosen so that the constant is zero.

The position of $B$ is obtained by adding to the position of $C$ the vector $\ell\,(\cos\varphi\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\varphi\,\hat{\jmath})$, which is the vector $B - C$ along the rod. The velocity is obtained by differentiating with respect to time, and it contains a term due to the advance of the disk and a term due to the rotation of the rod, since the two angles are independent and therefore both derivatives appear.

The check verifies the rolling condition on the chosen parametrization. The velocity of the point of the disk in contact with the guide **turns out to be zero**, hence the relation $x_C = -R\,\vartheta$ does describe a rolling without slipping.

</div>
