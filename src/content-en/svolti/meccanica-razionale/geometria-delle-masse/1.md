## Exercise 1

The system in the figure consists of two homogeneous rigid rods welded at a right angle: the rod $OA$, horizontal, has length $2\ell$ and mass $2m$; the rod $AB$, vertical, has length $\ell$ and mass $m$. Determine the center of mass of the system.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,2,0;B,2,1" aste="O-A;A-B" quote="O,A,2ℓ,0.5;A,B,ℓ,0.6" etichette="0.85,0.3,2m;2.3,0.55,m" frecce="-0.5,0,2.9,0,x;0,-0.5,0,1.6,y" riferimento=0 titolo="Two homogeneous rods welded at $A$, of masses $2m$ and $m$"}

$$
M = 2m + m = 3m
$$
$$
G_1 = (\ell,\ 0) ,
\qquad
G_2 = \left( 2\ell,\ \frac{\ell}{2} \right)
$$
$$
x_G = \frac{2m \cdot \ell + m \cdot 2\ell}{3m} = \frac{4\ell}{3} ,
\qquad
y_G = \frac{2m \cdot 0 + m \cdot \dfrac{\ell}{2}}{3m} = \frac{\ell}{6}
$$

Check with the position of $G$ on the segment $G_1 G_2$:
$$
G - G_1 = \left( \frac{4\ell}{3} - \ell,\ \frac{\ell}{6} \right) = \frac{1}{3}\left( \ell,\ \frac{\ell}{2} \right) = \frac{1}{3}\,(G_2 - G_1)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The frame $(O; x, y)$ is chosen with the $x$ axis along the horizontal rod and the $y$ axis along the vertical rod, because in this way the coordinates of the two ends are immediate and the problem is plane.

Each rod is homogeneous, hence its center of mass is the midpoint, by the **material symmetry** with respect to the plane orthogonal to the rod at the midpoint. The rod $OA$ has ends $(0,0)$ and $(2\ell, 0)$, therefore $G_1 = (\ell, 0)$; the rod $AB$ has ends $(2\ell, 0)$ and $(2\ell, \ell)$, therefore $G_2 = \left(2\ell, \dfrac{\ell}{2}\right)$.

By the **theorem on the center of mass of a composite system** the system is treated as two material points of masses $2m$ and $m$ placed at $G_1$ and $G_2$. The two coordinates are computed separately with the weighted average, and one gets $G = \left(\dfrac{4\ell}{3},\ \dfrac{\ell}{6}\right)$.

The check verifies the position of $G$ on the segment joining the two partial centers of mass. The same theorem guarantees that $G - G_1 = \dfrac{M_2}{M_1 + M_2}\,(G_2 - G_1)$, and here $\dfrac{M_2}{M_1 + M_2} = \dfrac{m}{3m} = \dfrac{1}{3}$; the direct computation of the difference gives exactly one third of $G_2 - G_1$. The center of mass **lies closer to $G_1$**, because the horizontal rod has twice the mass.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The system in the figure consists of three equal homogeneous rigid rods, each of length $\ell$ and mass $m$, welded so as to form the polygonal line $A O C D$ with $A = (0, \ell)$, $O = (0,0)$, $C = (\ell, 0)$ and $D = (\ell, \ell)$. Determine the center of mass of the system.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,1;O,0,0;C,1,0;D,1,1" aste="A-O;O-C;C-D" punti="0,0.5,G_1;0.5,0,G_2;1,0.5,G_3" quote="O,C,ℓ,0.75" tratteggi="0.5,-0.25 0.5,1.3" riferimento=0 titolo="Three equal rods arranged in a U, with the dashed axis of material symmetry $x = \dfrac{\ell}{2}$"}

$$
M = 3m
$$
$$
G_1 = \left( 0,\ \frac{\ell}{2} \right) ,
\qquad
G_2 = \left( \frac{\ell}{2},\ 0 \right) ,
\qquad
G_3 = \left( \ell,\ \frac{\ell}{2} \right)
$$
$$
x_G = \frac{m \cdot 0 + m \cdot \dfrac{\ell}{2} + m \cdot \ell}{3m} = \frac{\ell}{2} ,
\qquad
y_G = \frac{m \cdot \dfrac{\ell}{2} + m \cdot 0 + m \cdot \dfrac{\ell}{2}}{3m} = \frac{\ell}{3}
$$

Check with the material symmetry with respect to the plane $x = \dfrac{\ell}{2}$:
$$
x_G = \frac{\ell}{2}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The frame has the origin at the vertex $O$ of the polygonal line and the axes along the two rods that meet there, so that all the coordinates of the notable points are read off the drawing.

The three rods are homogeneous, hence their centers of mass are the midpoints of the respective segments. Since the three masses are equal, the weighted average reduces to the arithmetic mean of the three positions, and one gets $G = \left(\dfrac{\ell}{2},\ \dfrac{\ell}{3}\right)$.

The check uses symmetry. The plane $x = \dfrac{\ell}{2}$, orthogonal to the plane of the drawing, interchanges the rod $AO$ with the rod $CD$ and maps the rod $OC$ onto itself preserving the masses, hence it is a **plane of material symmetry**; by the theorem on material symmetry the center of mass belongs to it, therefore $x_G = \dfrac{\ell}{2}$, as the computation gives. The ordinate, on the other hand, is not obtained by symmetry, because the system is not symmetric with respect to a horizontal plane, and the value $y_G = \dfrac{\ell}{3}$ is smaller than $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ because the lower rod lowers the center of mass.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The straight rod $AB$ in the figure has length $\ell$ and variable linear density
$$
\lambda(s) = \lambda_0 \left( 1 + \frac{s}{\ell} \right) ,
$$
where $s$ is the abscissa measured from $A$ towards $B$ and $\lambda_0$ is a positive constant. Determine the mass of the rod and the position of its center of mass.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" quote="A,B,ℓ,0.5" punti="2.222,0,G,baricentro" frecce="0,0.5,1.6,0.5,s" etichette="0.6,0.85,λ(s)" riferimento=0 titolo="Non-homogeneous rod with linear density $\lambda(s) = \lambda_0\left(1 + \dfrac{s}{\ell}\right)$"}

$$
M = \int_0^{\ell} \lambda(s)\,ds = \lambda_0 \int_0^{\ell} \left( 1 + \frac{s}{\ell} \right) ds = \lambda_0 \left( \ell + \frac{\ell}{2} \right) = \frac{3}{2}\,\lambda_0 \ell
$$
$$
\int_0^{\ell} s\,\lambda(s)\,ds = \lambda_0 \int_0^{\ell} \left( s + \frac{s^2}{\ell} \right) ds = \lambda_0 \left( \frac{\ell^2}{2} + \frac{\ell^2}{3} \right) = \frac{5}{6}\,\lambda_0 \ell^2
$$
$$
s_G = \frac{1}{M} \int_0^{\ell} s\,\lambda(s)\,ds = \frac{\dfrac{5}{6}\,\lambda_0 \ell^2}{\dfrac{3}{2}\,\lambda_0 \ell} = \frac{5}{9}\,\ell
$$

Check with the homogeneous case, that is with the constant density $\lambda = \lambda_0$:
$$
M = \lambda_0 \ell ,
\qquad
s_G = \frac{1}{\lambda_0 \ell} \cdot \lambda_0\,\frac{\ell^2}{2} = \frac{\ell}{2}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The rod is a one-dimensional continuous system, hence the mass element is $dm = \lambda(s)\,ds$ and the sums in the definition of center of mass become integrals between $0$ and $\ell$. The abscissa $s$ measured from the end $A$ is chosen as the coordinate, because the density is given as a function of it.

The mass is obtained by integrating the density over the whole rod. The integral of $1$ gives $\ell$ and the integral of $\dfrac{s}{\ell}$ gives $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$, hence $M = \dfrac{3}{2}\,\lambda_0 \ell$: the rod weighs one and a half times the homogeneous rod of density $\lambda_0$.

The numerator of the formula for the center of mass is the moment of the mass about $A$. Dividing by the mass one gets $s_G = \dfrac{5}{9}\,\ell$, hence the center of mass **is shifted towards $B$** with respect to the midpoint, because the density grows from $A$ to $B$ and the heavier part of the rod lies towards $B$.

The check repeats the computation with the constant density $\lambda_0$, which is the limiting case in which the term $\dfrac{s}{\ell}$ is suppressed: the center of mass returns to the midpoint, as it must be for a homogeneous rod. One checks moreover that $\dfrac{5}{9}\,\ell$ lies between $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ and $\ell$, that is between the midpoint and the heavier end.

</div>
