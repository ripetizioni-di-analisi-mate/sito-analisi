## Exercise 1

The homogeneous square plate in the figure has mass $m$ and side $\ell$, and occupies the square $[0, \ell] \times [0, \ell]$ of the plane $Oxy$. Determine the principal moments of inertia relative to the pole $O$ and the directions of the principal axes.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,2 0,2" frecce="-1.7,0,3.4,0,x;0,-1.7,0,3.4,y;0,0,2.9,2.9,ξ;0,0,-1.7,1.7,η" punti="0,0," etichette="-0.32,-0.42,O" quote="0 0,2 0,ℓ,0.65" riferimento=0 titolo="Square plate with the pole $O$ at a vertex and the principal axes $\xi$ and $\eta$"}

$$
\sigma = \frac{m}{\ell^2}
$$
$$
I_{xx} = \sigma \int_0^{\ell} \int_0^{\ell} y^2\,dy\,dx = \frac{m\ell^2}{3} ,
\qquad
I_{yy} = \frac{m\ell^2}{3} ,
\qquad
I_{xy} = \sigma \int_0^{\ell} \int_0^{\ell} xy\,dy\,dx = \frac{m\ell^2}{4}
$$
$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
I_{xx} - I & -I_{xy} \\
-I_{xy} & I_{yy} - I
\end{pmatrix}
= 0
\quad \Longrightarrow \quad
I^2 - \frac{2}{3}\,m\ell^2\,I + \frac{7}{144}\,m^2\ell^4 = 0
$$
$$
I_1 = \frac{m\ell^2}{12} ,
\qquad
I_2 = \frac{7\,m\ell^2}{12} ,
\qquad
I_3 = I_{zz} = \frac{2\,m\ell^2}{3}
$$
$$
(I_{xx} - I_1)\,v_x - I_{xy}\,v_y = 0
\quad \Longrightarrow \quad
\frac{m\ell^2}{4}\,v_x = \frac{m\ell^2}{4}\,v_y
\quad \Longrightarrow \quad
\vec{u}_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\,(1, 1, 0)
$$

Check with the material symmetry with respect to the diagonal and with the trace:
$$
I_{u_1} = \frac{m\ell^2}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{2} + \frac{m\ell^2}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{2} - 2 \cdot \frac{m\ell^2}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \frac{m\ell^2}{12} ,
\qquad
I_1 + I_2 = \frac{2\,m\ell^2}{3} = I_{xx} + I_{yy}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The plate lies in the plane $Oxy$, hence the products $I_{xz}$ and $I_{yz}$ vanish and the $z$ axis is a principal axis with moment $I_{zz} = I_{xx} + I_{yy}$. The problem therefore reduces to the $2 \times 2$ matrix of the moments in the plane.

The integrals over the square separate. The moment $I_{xx}$ is the product of the integral of $y^2$ between $0$ and $\ell$, which equals $\dfrac{\ell^3}{3}$, by the length $\ell$ and by $\sigma$, and one gets $\dfrac{m\ell^2}{3}$; by symmetry $I_{yy}$ has the same value. The product $I_{xy}$ is the product of two integrals equal to $\dfrac{\ell^2}{2}$, hence it equals $\dfrac{m\ell^2}{4}$.

The principal moments are the eigenvalues of the matrix, that is the roots of the characteristic equation $I^2 - (I_{xx} + I_{yy})\,I + \big( I_{xx}I_{yy} - I_{xy}^2 \big) = 0$. The constant term equals $\dfrac{m^2\ell^4}{9} - \dfrac{m^2\ell^4}{16} = \dfrac{7\,m^2\ell^4}{144}$, and the discriminant gives the square root $\dfrac{m\ell^2}{2}$; it follows that $I_1 = \dfrac{m\ell^2}{12}$ and $I_2 = \dfrac{7m\ell^2}{12}$.

The principal direction relative to $I_1$ is obtained from the first row of the homogeneous system, which gives $v_x = v_y$: the axis $\xi$ **is the diagonal issuing from $O$**. The axis $\eta$ is the line through $O$ orthogonal to it, because the eigenvectors of a symmetric matrix relative to distinct eigenvalues are orthogonal.

The first check verifies the value $I_1$ with the formula $I_u = \vec{u} \cdot \mathbb{I}_O\,\vec{u}$ applied to the unit vector of the diagonal, and confirms $\dfrac{m\ell^2}{12}$. The result could have been foreseen: the diagonal is an axis of material symmetry of the square, hence by the theorem on principal axes from symmetry it is a principal axis relative to each of its points, $O$ included. The second check compares the sum of the two eigenvalues with the trace of the matrix, and the two values coincide.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The homogeneous plate in the figure has mass $m$ and occupies the isosceles right triangle with vertices $O = (0,0)$, $A = (\ell, 0)$ and $B = (0, \ell)$ of the plane $Oxy$. Determine the principal moments of inertia relative to the pole $O$ and the directions of the principal axes.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2,0 0,2" frecce="-1.7,0,3.2,0,x;0,-1.7,0,3.2,y;0,0,2.4,2.4,ξ;0,0,-1.7,1.7,η" punti="0,0," etichette="-0.32,-0.42,O" quote="0 0,2 0,ℓ,0.65;0 2,0 0,ℓ,0.65" riferimento=0 titolo="Isosceles triangular plate with the pole $O$ at the vertex of the right angle and the principal axes $\xi$ and $\eta$"}

$$
\sigma = \frac{2m}{\ell^2}
$$
$$
I_{xx} = \frac{m\ell^2}{6} ,
\qquad
I_{yy} = \frac{m\ell^2}{6} ,
\qquad
I_{xy} = \frac{m\ell^2}{12}
$$
$$
I^2 - \frac{m\ell^2}{3}\,I + \frac{m^2\ell^4}{48} = 0
\quad \Longrightarrow \quad
I_1 = \frac{m\ell^2}{12} ,
\qquad
I_2 = \frac{m\ell^2}{4} ,
\qquad
I_3 = I_{zz} = \frac{m\ell^2}{3}
$$
$$
\vec{u}_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\,(1, 1, 0) ,
\qquad
\vec{u}_2 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\,(-1, 1, 0)
$$

Check with the material symmetry with respect to the bisector of the first quadrant:
$$
I_{u_1} = \frac{m\ell^2}{6} \cdot \frac{1}{2} + \frac{m\ell^2}{6} \cdot \frac{1}{2} - 2 \cdot \frac{m\ell^2}{12} \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \frac{m\ell^2}{12} ,
\qquad
I_1 + I_2 = \frac{m\ell^2}{3} = I_{xx} + I_{yy}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The moments and the product of inertia are obtained from the formulas derived for the right triangular plate with legs $a$ and $b$, that is $I_{xx} = \dfrac{mb^2}{6}$, $I_{yy} = \dfrac{ma^2}{6}$ and $I_{xy} = \dfrac{mab}{12}$, by setting $a = b = \ell$.

The characteristic equation has the coefficient $I_{xx} + I_{yy} = \dfrac{m\ell^2}{3}$ and the constant term $I_{xx}I_{yy} - I_{xy}^2 = \dfrac{m^2\ell^4}{36} - \dfrac{m^2\ell^4}{144} = \dfrac{m^2\ell^4}{48}$. The discriminant gives the square root $\dfrac{m\ell^2}{6}$, hence the principal moments in the plane are $I_1 = \dfrac{m\ell^2}{12}$ and $I_2 = \dfrac{m\ell^2}{4}$. The third principal moment is $I_{zz} = \dfrac{m\ell^2}{3}$, relative to the axis orthogonal to the plate.

Since $I_{xx} = I_{yy}$, the first row of the homogeneous system for $I_1$ gives $v_x = v_y$, hence the axis $\xi$ is the bisector of the first quadrant and the axis $\eta$ is the line through $O$ orthogonal to it.

The check relies on symmetry. The reflection with respect to the bisector of the first quadrant interchanges the vertices $A$ and $B$ and maps the triangle onto itself, hence that line **is an axis of material symmetry** and by the theorem on principal axes from symmetry it is principal with respect to $O$. The computation of $I_{u_1}$ with the formula for the moment of inertia about an axis confirms the value $\dfrac{m\ell^2}{12}$, and the sum of the eigenvalues in the plane coincides with the trace of the $2 \times 2$ block.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The homogeneous rectangular plate in the figure has mass $m$ and occupies the rectangle $[0, 2\ell] \times [0, \ell]$ of the plane $Oxy$. Determine the principal moments of inertia relative to the pole $O$ and the angle that the principal axis of smallest moment makes with the $x$ axis.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,1.5 0,1.5" frecce="-1.7,0,4,0,x;0,-1.7,0,3,y;0,0,3.46,1.44,ξ;0,0,-0.9,2.17,η" punti="0,0,O" quote="0 0,3 0,2ℓ,0.65;3 0,3 1.5,ℓ,0.6" etichette="1.35,0.33,α" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangular plate with the pole $O$ at a vertex; the principal axis $\xi$ makes the angle $\alpha$ with the $x$ axis"}

$$
I_{xx} = \frac{m\ell^2}{3} ,
\qquad
I_{yy} = \frac{m\,(2\ell)^2}{3} = \frac{4\,m\ell^2}{3} ,
\qquad
I_{xy} = \frac{m\,(2\ell)\,\ell}{4} = \frac{m\ell^2}{2}
$$
$$
I^2 - \frac{5}{3}\,m\ell^2\,I + \frac{7}{36}\,m^2\ell^4 = 0
$$
$$
I_{1,2} = \frac{I_{xx} + I_{yy}}{2} \pm \sqrt{\left( \frac{I_{xx} - I_{yy}}{2} \right)^2 + I_{xy}^2}
= \frac{5\,m\ell^2}{6} \pm \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,m\ell^2
$$
$$
I_1 = \frac{(5 - 3\sqrt{2})\,m\ell^2}{6} \approx 0.126\,m\ell^2 ,
\qquad
I_2 = \frac{(5 + 3\sqrt{2})\,m\ell^2}{6} \approx 1.540\,m\ell^2
$$
$$
\tan 2\alpha = \frac{2\,I_{xy}}{I_{yy} - I_{xx}} = \frac{m\ell^2}{m\ell^2} = 1
\quad \Longrightarrow \quad
\alpha = 22.5^\circ
$$

Check with the computation of $I_u$ in the direction found, with $\cos 22.5^\circ \approx 0.9239$ and $\sin 22.5^\circ \approx 0.3827$:
$$
I_u = \frac{m\ell^2}{3}\,(0.9239)^2 + \frac{4\,m\ell^2}{3}\,(0.3827)^2 - 2 \cdot \frac{m\ell^2}{2} \cdot 0.9239 \cdot 0.3827 \approx 0.126\,m\ell^2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The values of $I_{xx}$, $I_{yy}$ and $I_{xy}$ are read off the formulas for the rectangular plate referred to a vertex, that is $\dfrac{mb^2}{3}$, $\dfrac{ma^2}{3}$ and $\dfrac{mab}{4}$, with $a = 2\ell$ and $b = \ell$.

The characteristic equation has the coefficient $I_{xx} + I_{yy} = \dfrac{5m\ell^2}{3}$ and the constant term $\dfrac{4\,m^2\ell^4}{9} - \dfrac{m^2\ell^4}{4} = \dfrac{7\,m^2\ell^4}{36}$. Solving with the formula for the principal moments one obtains the square root $\sqrt{\dfrac{m^2\ell^4}{4} + \dfrac{m^2\ell^4}{4}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,m\ell^2$, hence $I_{1,2} = \dfrac{5m\ell^2}{6} \pm \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,m\ell^2$.

The angle that the principal axis makes with the $x$ axis is obtained from the relation $\tan 2\alpha = \dfrac{2I_{xy}}{I_{yy} - I_{xx}}$, which comes from the homogeneous system of the eigenvectors. Here the ratio equals $1$, hence $2\alpha = 45^\circ$ and $\alpha = 22.5^\circ$; the other principal axis makes the angle $112.5^\circ$ with the $x$ axis.

The pole $O$ is a vertex of the rectangle and no line through $O$ is an axis of material symmetry, because the diagonal of a non-square rectangle is not one. The principal axes **are therefore not obtained by symmetry** and the computation of the eigenvalues is necessary.

The check substitutes the unit vector of the direction found into the formula $I_u = I_{xx}u_x^2 + I_{yy}u_y^2 - 2I_{xy}u_xu_y$. The three terms equal about $0.2845\,m\ell^2$, $0.1953\,m\ell^2$ and $-0.3536\,m\ell^2$, and their sum is about $0.126\,m\ell^2$, that is the smallest principal moment $I_1$. The angle $\alpha = 22.5^\circ$ therefore locates the axis of smallest moment.

</div>
