## Exercise 1

The material point $A$ of mass $2m$ slides on a smooth fixed horizontal guide; a rigid rod of negligible mass and length $\ell$ is hinged to it, which carries at its other endpoint the material point $P$ of mass $m$. The system moves in the vertical plane, as in the diagram in the figure. Write the equations of motion and a first integral.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0,-;A,2.4,0;P,3.662,-1.802" catene="A-P" vincoli="A,carrello,90" linee="-0.6,0 6,0" tratteggi="2.4,0 2.4,-2.3" forze="3.662 -1.802,0,-0.65,mg" etichette="2.68,-1.3,ϑ;1.7,0.45,2m;4.0,-2.05,m" quote="0 0,A,x,0.75" titolo="Slider on a horizontal guide with the pendulum hinged at $A$"}

The system has two degrees of freedom and the abscissa $x$ of $A$ and the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the downward vertical are taken as Lagrangian coordinates.

$$
P - O = (x + \ell\sin\vartheta)\,\hat{\imath} - \ell\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} ,
\qquad
\vec{v}_P = (\dot{x} + \ell\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta)\,\hat{\imath} + \ell\dot{\vartheta}\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
v_P^2 = \dot{x}^2 + \ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + 2\,\ell\,\dot{x}\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta
$$
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,(2m)\,\dot{x}^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,v_P^2 = \frac{3}{2}\,m\,\dot{x}^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + m\,\ell\,\dot{x}\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta
$$
$$
U = -m\,g\,y_P = m\,g\,\ell\cos\vartheta
$$
$$
\frac{\partial T}{\partial \dot{x}} = 3\,m\,\dot{x} + m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta ,
\qquad
\frac{\partial T}{\partial x} = 0 ,
\qquad
\frac{\partial U}{\partial x} = 0
$$
$$
\frac{\partial T}{\partial \dot{\vartheta}} = m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta} + m\,\ell\,\dot{x}\cos\vartheta ,
\qquad
\frac{\partial T}{\partial \vartheta} = -m\,\ell\,\dot{x}\,\dot{\vartheta}\sin\vartheta ,
\qquad
\frac{\partial U}{\partial \vartheta} = -m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
3\,m\,\ddot{x} + m\,\ell\,\ddot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta - m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}^2\sin\vartheta = 0 \\[6pt]
m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} + m\,\ell\,\ddot{x}\cos\vartheta + m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
p_x = \frac{\partial T}{\partial \dot{x}} = 3\,m\,\dot{x} + m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta = \text{const.}
$$

Check with the first cardinal equation projected on the horizontal. The external forces are the weights, which are vertical, and the reaction of the smooth guide, which is vertical too, hence
$$
\frac{d Q_x}{dt} = 0 ,
\qquad
Q_x = 2\,m\,\dot{x} + m\,(\dot{x} + \ell\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta) = 3\,m\,\dot{x} + m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta ,
$$
which is the first integral found with Lagrange's method.

<div class="spiegazione">

The point $A$ moves on a line and the rod can rotate about $A$, therefore the system has **two degrees of freedom**. The natural coordinates are the abscissa of the slider and the angle of the rod, since the position of $P$ is written at once with them.

The velocity of $P$ is the sum of the dragging velocity of the slider and of the velocity due to the rotation of the rod. The mixed term $2\,\ell\,\dot{x}\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta$ appears in the square of the magnitude, which is twice the product of the two horizontal components.

The rod has negligible mass, hence only the two material points appear in the kinetic energy. Only the weight of $P$ appears in the potential, given that the height of $A$ remains constant.

The coordinate $x$ is **cyclic**: the Lagrangian contains $\dot{x}$ but not $x$, because the kinetic energy depends on the abscissa only through its derivative and the potential does not depend on $x$. The equation of index $x$ therefore reduces to $\dfrac{d p_x}{dt} = 0$, and the kinetic momentum $p_x$ is a first integral.

In the second equation the term $-m\,\ell\,\dot{x}\,\dot{\vartheta}\sin\vartheta$, which comes from $\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \vartheta}$, cancels with an equal term produced by the total derivative with respect to time. For this reason the equation in $\vartheta$ turns out to be simpler than the first one.

In the check $p_x$ is recognized as the horizontal component of the linear momentum of the system: $2\,m\,\dot{x}$ for the slider and $m\,(\dot{x} + \ell\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta)$ for the point $P$. External forces with a horizontal component are absent, therefore the first cardinal equation projected on the $x$ axis gives the conservation of $Q_x$: the result of Lagrange's method coincides with that of the cardinal equations.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The material point $A$ of mass $2m$ slides on a smooth fixed horizontal guide; the uniform rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $2\ell$, is hinged to it and moves in the vertical plane, as in the diagram in the figure. Write the equations of motion and a first integral.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0,-;A,2.4,0;B,4.006,-2.294" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,90" linee="-0.6,0 6,0" tratteggi="2.4,0 2.4,-2.6" punti="3.203,-1.147,G,baricentro" forze="3.203 -1.147,0,-0.65,mg" etichette="2.7,-1.5,ϑ;1.7,0.45,2m" quote="0 0,A,x,0.75;A,B,2ℓ,-0.7" titolo="Slider on a horizontal guide with the uniform rod hinged at $A$"}

The system has two degrees of freedom and the abscissa $x$ of $A$ and the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the downward vertical are taken as Lagrangian coordinates.

$$
G - O = (x + \ell\sin\vartheta)\,\hat{\imath} - \ell\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} ,
\qquad
v_G^2 = \dot{x}^2 + \ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + 2\,\ell\,\dot{x}\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta
$$
$$
I_G = \frac{1}{12}\,m\,(2\ell)^2 = \frac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2
$$
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,(2m)\,\dot{x}^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,v_G^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,I_G\,\dot{\vartheta}^2
= \frac{3}{2}\,m\,\dot{x}^2 + \frac{2}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + m\,\ell\,\dot{x}\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta
$$
$$
U = -m\,g\,y_G = m\,g\,\ell\cos\vartheta
$$
$$
\mathbb{A}(\vartheta) =
\begin{pmatrix}
3\,m & m\,\ell\cos\vartheta \\[4pt]
m\,\ell\cos\vartheta & \dfrac{4}{3}\,m\,\ell^2
\end{pmatrix}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
3\,m\,\ddot{x} + m\,\ell\,\ddot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta - m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}^2\sin\vartheta = 0 \\[6pt]
\dfrac{4}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} + m\,\ell\,\ddot{x}\cos\vartheta + m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
p_x = 3\,m\,\dot{x} + m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta = \text{const.}
$$

Check of the determinant of the kinetic matrix:
$$
\det \mathbb{A} = 4\,m^2\,\ell^2 - m^2\,\ell^2\cos^2\vartheta = m^2\,\ell^2\,\big( 4 - \cos^2\vartheta \big) > 0 ,
$$
hence the system is solved for the accelerations in every configuration, as the theorem on the kinetic matrix guarantees.

<div class="spiegazione">

The system differs from the one of the previous exercise by the replacement of the material point with a uniform rod, which has distributed mass and therefore a moment of inertia of its own.

The center of mass of the rod is its midpoint, which is at distance $\ell$ from $A$; its position and its velocity therefore have the same form as those of the point $P$ of the previous exercise. The term $\dfrac{1}{2}\,I_G\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$ of the relative rotational motion is added to the kinetic energy, by König's theorem.

The coefficient of $\dot{\vartheta}^2$ goes from $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\ell^2$ to $\dfrac{2}{3}\,m\,\ell^2$, whereas the mixed term and the coefficient of $\dot{x}^2$ remain the same: the mass of the rod is concentrated around the center of mass, hence the translational part does not change.

The kinetic matrix is read from the coefficients of the kinetic energy, recalling that the mixed term is $a_{12}\,\dot{x}\,\dot{\vartheta}$ with $a_{12} = a_{21}$. Its determinant is positive for every $\vartheta$, because $\cos^2\vartheta \le 1 < 4$.

Here too $x$ is **cyclic** and the conjugate kinetic momentum is the horizontal component of the linear momentum; its conservation follows from the absence of horizontal external forces, as in the previous exercise.

The check of the determinant is the verification that the system is actually solvable for the accelerations. If the determinant vanished in some configuration, the coordinates chosen would not be independent in that configuration, and the computation would have to be revised.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The double pendulum in the figure consists of two rigid rods of negligible mass and length $\ell$: the first one is hinged at the fixed point $O$ and carries at the endpoint $A$ the material point of mass $2m$, the second one is hinged at $A$ and carries at the endpoint $B$ the material point of mass $m$. All the hinges are smooth and the system moves in the vertical plane. Write the equations of motion.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,0.803,-1.722;B,2.359,-2.812" catene="O-A;A-B" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" cerniere="A" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.0;0.803,-1.722 0.803,-3.2" etichette="0.17,-1.02,ϑ_1;1.02,-2.45,ϑ_2;0.42,-1.95,2m;2.62,-2.9,m" titolo="Double pendulum with two material points of mass $2m$ and $m$"}

The system has two degrees of freedom and the angles $\vartheta_1$ and $\vartheta_2$ that the two rods form with the downward vertical are taken as Lagrangian coordinates.

$$
A - O = \ell\sin\vartheta_1\,\hat{\imath} - \ell\cos\vartheta_1\,\hat{\jmath} ,
\qquad
B - O = \big( \ell\sin\vartheta_1 + \ell\sin\vartheta_2 \big)\,\hat{\imath} - \big( \ell\cos\vartheta_1 + \ell\cos\vartheta_2 \big)\,\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
v_A^2 = \ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1^2 ,
\qquad
v_B^2 = \ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1^2 + \ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_2^2 + 2\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1\,\dot{\vartheta}_2\cos(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)
$$
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,(2m)\,v_A^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,v_B^2
= \frac{3}{2}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_2^2 + m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1\,\dot{\vartheta}_2\cos(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)
$$
$$
U = -2\,m\,g\,y_A - m\,g\,y_B = m\,g\,\ell\,\big( 3\cos\vartheta_1 + \cos\vartheta_2 \big)
$$
$$
\frac{\partial T}{\partial \dot{\vartheta}_1} = 3\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1 + m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_2\cos(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)
$$
$$
\frac{\partial T}{\partial \vartheta_1} = -m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1\,\dot{\vartheta}_2\sin(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2) ,
\qquad
\frac{\partial U}{\partial \vartheta_1} = -3\,m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta_1
$$
$$
\frac{\partial T}{\partial \dot{\vartheta}_2} = m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_2 + m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1\cos(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)
$$
$$
\frac{\partial T}{\partial \vartheta_2} = m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1\,\dot{\vartheta}_2\sin(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2) ,
\qquad
\frac{\partial U}{\partial \vartheta_2} = -m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta_2
$$
Dividing both equations by $m\,\ell$ gives
$$
\begin{cases}
3\,\ell\,\ddot{\vartheta}_1 + \ell\cos(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)\,\ddot{\vartheta}_2 + \ell\sin(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)\,\dot{\vartheta}_2^2 + 3\,g\sin\vartheta_1 = 0 \\[6pt]
\ell\,\ddot{\vartheta}_2 + \ell\cos(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)\,\ddot{\vartheta}_1 - \ell\sin(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)\,\dot{\vartheta}_1^2 + g\sin\vartheta_2 = 0
\end{cases}
$$

Check with the second cardinal equation, pole at the fixed point $O$. The angular momentum of the system and the moment of the external forces are
$$
K_O = m\,\ell^2\,\Big[ 3\,\dot{\vartheta}_1 + \dot{\vartheta}_2 + \cos(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)\,\big( \dot{\vartheta}_1 + \dot{\vartheta}_2 \big) \Big] ,
\qquad
M_O^{(e)} = -m\,g\,\ell\,\big( 3\sin\vartheta_1 + \sin\vartheta_2 \big) ,
$$
and adding the two Lagrange equations, multiplied by $m\,\ell$, gives
$$
m\,\ell^2\Big[ 3\,\ddot{\vartheta}_1 + \ddot{\vartheta}_2 + \cos(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)\big( \ddot{\vartheta}_1 + \ddot{\vartheta}_2 \big) + \sin(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)\big( \dot{\vartheta}_2^2 - \dot{\vartheta}_1^2 \big) \Big] = M_O^{(e)} ,
$$
that is $\dfrac{d K_O}{dt} = M_O^{(e)}$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The two rods can rotate independently, hence the system has **two degrees of freedom** and the angles $\vartheta_1$ and $\vartheta_2$ determine its configuration.

The position of $B$ is obtained by adding the two vectors $A - O$ and $B - A$, each of which has magnitude $\ell$ and direction determined by the respective angle. Differentiating gives $\vec{v}_B$; in the square of the magnitude the double product is simplified with the subtraction formula for the cosine, $\cos\vartheta_1\cos\vartheta_2 + \sin\vartheta_1\sin\vartheta_2 = \cos(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)$.

The kinetic energy contains the mixed term $m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1\,\dot{\vartheta}_2\cos(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)$, which depends on the configuration: the two equations turn out to be coupled both in the accelerations and in the squares of the velocities.

The derivatives of $T$ with respect to $\vartheta_1$ and to $\vartheta_2$ differ by the sign, because the difference $\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2$ grows with the first angle and decreases with the second. For this reason the terms in $\dot{\vartheta}^2$ appear with opposite signs in the two equations.

In the potential the point $A$ weighs twice as much and its height appears a second time in the position of $B$; adding the two contributions the coefficient of $\cos\vartheta_1$ becomes $3$.

In the check the two Lagrange equations are added. The sum corresponds to the virtual displacement with $\delta\vartheta_1 = \delta\vartheta_2$, in which the system rotates rigidly about $O$; the result is the second cardinal equation with pole at the fixed point, $\dfrac{d K_O}{dt} = M_O^{(e)}$. The angular momentum and the moment of the external forces are computed directly from the positions and the velocities of the two points, without using the Lagrangian, therefore the check is independent and verifies the coefficients of the mixed terms.

</div>
