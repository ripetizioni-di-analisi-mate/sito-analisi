## Exercise 1

The system in the figure consists of the uniform rod $OA$, of mass $m$ and length $2\ell$, and of the material point $P$ of mass $m$ welded at the endpoint $A$. The rod is hinged at $O$ by a smooth fixed hinge and moves in the vertical plane. Write the equation of motion.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1.2,-2.078,-" aste="O-A" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.4" punti="0.6,-1.039,G,baricentro;1.2,-2.078,A ≡ P" forze="0.6 -1.039,0,-0.8,mg;1.2 -2.078,0,-0.8,mg" etichette="0.3,-1.08,ϑ" quote="O,A,2ℓ,-0.6" titolo="Rod hinged at $O$ with the material point $P$ welded at $A$"}

The system has one degree of freedom and the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the downward vertical, positive counterclockwise, is taken as Lagrangian coordinate.

$$
I_O = \frac{1}{3}\,m\,(2\ell)^2 + m\,(2\ell)^2 = \frac{16}{3}\,m\,\ell^2
$$
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,I_O\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 = \frac{8}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2
$$
$$
y_G = -\ell\cos\vartheta ,
\qquad
y_A = -2\ell\cos\vartheta
$$
$$
U = -m\,g\,y_G - m\,g\,y_A = 3\,m\,g\,\ell\cos\vartheta
$$
$$
\frac{d}{dt}\frac{\partial T}{\partial \dot{\vartheta}} = \frac{16}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} ,
\qquad
\frac{\partial T}{\partial \vartheta} = 0 ,
\qquad
Q_\vartheta = \frac{\partial U}{\partial \vartheta} = -3\,m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta
$$
$$
\frac{16}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = -3\,m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
\ddot{\vartheta} = -\frac{9\,g}{16\,\ell}\sin\vartheta
$$

Check with the second cardinal equation, pole at the hinge $O$:
$$
I_O\,\ddot{\vartheta} = M_O^{(e)} = -m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta - m\,g\,(2\ell)\sin\vartheta = -3\,m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The rod rotates about the fixed axis through the hinge, hence the configuration is determined by the angle $\vartheta$ alone and the system has **one degree of freedom**. The act of motion is a rotation about that axis, therefore the kinetic energy is written with the moment of inertia with respect to the pole $O$.

The moment of inertia is the sum of that of the rod, $\dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,(2\ell)^2$ with respect to an endpoint, and that of the material point, $m\,(2\ell)^2$, because $P$ is at distance $2\ell$ from the axis. The two values add up since the moment of inertia is additive, as is seen in the topic on the geometry of masses.

The only active forces are the two weights, which are conservative, hence the load admits the potential $U = -m\,g\,y_G - m\,g\,y_A$. The center of mass of the rod is at distance $\ell$ from $O$ and the point $P$ at distance $2\ell$, therefore the two heights are $-\ell\cos\vartheta$ and $-2\ell\cos\vartheta$.

The kinetic energy does not depend on $\vartheta$, given that the moment of inertia with respect to the fixed axis is constant; therefore $\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \vartheta} = 0$ and Lagrange's equation reduces to $I_O\,\ddot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{\partial U}{\partial \vartheta}$.

The motion is that of a **compound pendulum** of reduced length $\dfrac{16}{9}\,\ell$: the angle is governed by the same equation as a simple pendulum of that length. The minus sign says that the moment of the weight brings the system back towards the configuration with $\vartheta = 0$.

In the check the second cardinal equation is written with pole at the hinge. The pole is chosen at $O$ because the reaction of the hinge is applied at $O$ and has zero moment with respect to that point, hence it does not appear in the equation. The two weights have clockwise, that is negative, moment, with arms $\ell\sin\vartheta$ and $2\ell\sin\vartheta$; the cardinal equation coincides with Lagrange's one.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The uniform rod $OA$, of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, is hinged at $O$ by a smooth fixed hinge and moves in the vertical plane. A spring of elastic constant $k$ and zero rest length connects the endpoint $A$ to the fixed point $D$, which lies on the downward vertical through $O$ at distance $\ell$ from it, as the diagram in the figure shows. Write the equation of motion.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1.4,-1.96" aste="O-A" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" linee="0,-2.4 0.156,-2.162 0.474,-2.44 0.576,-2.03 0.894,-2.308 0.996,-1.898 1.4,-1.96" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.75" punti="0.7,-0.98,G,baricentro;0,-2.4,D" forze="0.7 -0.98,0,-0.75,mg" etichette="0.3,-1.05,ϑ;0.86,-2.56,k" quote="O,A,ℓ,-0.55" titolo="Rod hinged at $O$ with the spring between the endpoint $A$ and the fixed point $D$"}

The system has one degree of freedom and the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the downward vertical is taken as Lagrangian coordinate.

$$
A - O = \ell\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} - \ell\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} ,
\qquad
D - O = -\ell\,\hat{\jmath}
$$
$$
|A - D|^2 = \ell^2\sin^2\vartheta + \ell^2\,(1 - \cos\vartheta)^2 = 2\,\ell^2\,(1 - \cos\vartheta)
$$
$$
T = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 = \frac{1}{6}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2
$$
$$
U = -m\,g\,y_G - \frac{1}{2}\,k\,|A - D|^2 = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\cos\vartheta + k\,\ell^2\,(\cos\vartheta - 1)
$$
$$
Q_\vartheta = \frac{\partial U}{\partial \vartheta} = -\left( \frac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell + k\,\ell^2 \right) \sin\vartheta
$$
$$
\frac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = -\left( \frac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell + k\,\ell^2 \right) \sin\vartheta
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
\ddot{\vartheta} = -\frac{3\,(m\,g + 2\,k\,\ell)}{2\,m\,\ell}\sin\vartheta
$$

Check with the second cardinal equation, pole at $O$. The elastic force applied at $A$ is $\vec{F} = -k\,(A - D)$, with
$$
A - D = \ell\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \ell\,(1 - \cos\vartheta)\,\hat{\jmath} ,
$$
$$
M_O^{(F)} = (A - O) \wedge \vec{F} \cdot \hat{k} = -k\,\ell^2 \big[ \sin\vartheta\,(1 - \cos\vartheta) + \cos\vartheta\sin\vartheta \big] = -k\,\ell^2\sin\vartheta
$$
$$
I_O\,\ddot{\vartheta} = -\frac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta - k\,\ell^2\sin\vartheta
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The system is a rigid body with a fixed axis, hence it has one degree of freedom and the kinetic energy is $\dfrac{1}{2}\,I_O\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$ with $I_O = \dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2$, moment of inertia of the rod with respect to one of its endpoints.

The length of the spring is obtained from the square of the distance between $A$ and $D$, in which the identity $\sin^2\vartheta + \cos^2\vartheta = 1$ appears: expanding the square gives $|A - D|^2 = 2\,\ell^2\,(1 - \cos\vartheta)$. The spring has zero rest length, therefore its potential is $-\dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,|A - D|^2$, which depends on the configuration alone.

The two active forces, the weight and the elastic force, are conservative, hence the load is conservative and the Lagrangian component is obtained by differentiating $U$ with respect to $\vartheta$. Both contributions are proportional to $\sin\vartheta$ and have the same sign, therefore they add up.

The equation has the same form as that of the compound pendulum, with the coefficient increased by the elastic term, hence the **restoring** moment towards the configuration $\vartheta = 0$ is larger; in that configuration $A$ coincides with $D$ and the spring has zero length. For $k \to 0$ one recovers $\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{3\,g}{2\,\ell}\sin\vartheta$, that is the equation of the compound pendulum without the spring.

In the check the elastic force is written as a vector and its moment with respect to $O$ is computed with the cross product. The moment of the weight is $-\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta$ and that of the elastic force is $-k\,\ell^2\sin\vartheta$, therefore the second cardinal equation with pole at the hinge gives the same equation of motion.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The rigid bracket $AOB$ in the figure consists of two uniform rods $OA$ and $OB$, each of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, welded orthogonally at $O$; the bracket is hinged at $O$ by a smooth fixed hinge and moves in the vertical plane. Write the equation of motion and compute the angular velocity with which the bracket passes through the configuration $\vartheta = 0$, knowing that it starts from rest with $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,0.98,-1.7;B,1.7,0.98" aste="O-A;O-B" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.1" punti="0.49,-0.85,G_1,baricentro;0.85,0.49,G_2,baricentro" forze="0.49 -0.85,0,-0.7,mg;0.85 0.49,0,-0.7,mg" etichette="0.26,-1.05,ϑ" quote="O,A,ℓ,-0.5;O,B,ℓ,-0.5" titolo="Bracket formed by two equal rods welded orthogonally and hinged at $O$"}

The system has one degree of freedom and the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod $OA$ and the downward vertical is taken as Lagrangian coordinate; the rod $OB$ is obtained from $OA$ by a counterclockwise rotation of $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

$$
I_O = \frac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2 + \frac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2 = \frac{2}{3}\,m\,\ell^2
$$
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,I_O\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 = \frac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2
$$
$$
y_{G_1} = -\frac{\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta ,
\qquad
y_{G_2} = \frac{\ell}{2}\sin\vartheta
$$
$$
U = -m\,g\,y_{G_1} - m\,g\,y_{G_2} = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\,(\cos\vartheta - \sin\vartheta)
$$
$$
\frac{2}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = \frac{\partial U}{\partial \vartheta} = -\frac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\,(\sin\vartheta + \cos\vartheta)
$$
$$
\ddot{\vartheta} = -\frac{3\,g}{4\,\ell}\,(\sin\vartheta + \cos\vartheta)
$$

The constraints are fixed and the load is conservative, hence the mechanical energy $E = T - U$ is constant:
$$
\frac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 - \frac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\,(\cos\vartheta - \sin\vartheta)
= 0 - \frac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\,\left( \cos\frac{\pi}{2} - \sin\frac{\pi}{2} \right) = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell
$$
$$
\frac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell = m\,g\,\ell
\qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad
|\dot{\vartheta}| = \sqrt{\frac{3\,g}{\ell}}
$$

Check with the second cardinal equation, pole at $O$:
$$
I_O\,\ddot{\vartheta} = -m\,g\,\frac{\ell}{2}\sin\vartheta - m\,g\,\frac{\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The bracket is a single rigid body with a fixed axis, hence it has one degree of freedom. The moment of inertia with respect to the axis through $O$ is the sum of the moments of inertia of the two rods, each of which is hinged at one of its endpoints, therefore $I_O = \dfrac{2}{3}\,m\,\ell^2$.

The heights of the two centers of mass are read on the figure. The center of mass of $OA$ is at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ from $O$ along the direction of the rod, which forms the angle $\vartheta$ with the downward vertical, hence $y_{G_1} = -\dfrac{\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta$. The rod $OB$ is rotated by $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ with respect to $OA$, therefore its direction forms the angle $\vartheta + \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ with the downward vertical and the height of its center of mass is $-\dfrac{\ell}{2}\cos\left(\vartheta + \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right) = \dfrac{\ell}{2}\sin\vartheta$.

Both $\sin\vartheta$ and $\cos\vartheta$ therefore appear in the equation of motion: the weight of the rod $OB$ gives a moment that does not vanish at $\vartheta = 0$, because in that configuration $OB$ is horizontal.

For the second question the equation of motion is not needed. The constraints are fixed and the active forces are conservative, hence the mechanical energy is conserved. The value of $E$ in the initial configuration, in which $\dot{\vartheta} = 0$, is set equal to the value in the configuration $\vartheta = 0$, and $\dot{\vartheta}$ is obtained. The result is $|\dot{\vartheta}| = \sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{\ell}}$ and the bracket passes through $\vartheta = 0$ rotating **clockwise**, because the motion goes from $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ to $\vartheta = 0$ and hence $\dot{\vartheta} < 0$.

In the check the second cardinal equation is written with pole at the hinge: the weight of $OA$ has arm $\dfrac{\ell}{2}\sin\vartheta$ and the weight of $OB$ has arm $\dfrac{\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta$, both with clockwise moment for $0 < \vartheta < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The equation coincides with the one obtained with Lagrange's method.

</div>
