## Exercise 1

What is the coefficient of $x^2y$ in the sum $8x^2y+16x^2y-2x^2y$?

$$
8+16-2=22
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The three monomials have the same variable part, so they are like terms. Only their coefficients are added. The variable part is unchanged.

The result is $22$.

If $x=2$ and $y=1$, the variable part equals $4$. The values of the three addends also have a common factor of $4$, so their sum is $(8+16-2)\cdot4=88$. The required coefficient is the multiplier of $x^2y$, so it equals $22$ for any values of $x$ and $y$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Simplify the sum $(9 x^{2} + 2 x + 1)+(8 x - 3)$.

$$
(9 x^{2} + 2 x + 1)+(8 x - 3)=9 x^{2} + 10 x - 2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Remove the grouping symbols preceded by a plus sign. Combine the quadratic terms, the linear terms, and the constant terms separately. Each sum retains its own variable part.

The result is $9 x^{2} + 10 x - 2$.

The second addend has no quadratic term. The coefficient of $x^2$ therefore remains $9$, while the contribution to the coefficient of $x$ is $2+8=10$. At $x=1$, the first polynomial is $12$ and the second is $5$. The simplified polynomial also evaluates to $9+10-2=17$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Simplify $(10 x^{2} + 3 x + 1)-(2 x^{2} - x + 10)$.

$$
(10 x^{2} + 3 x + 1)-(2 x^{2} - x + 10)=8 x^{2} + 4 x - 9
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Subtracting a polynomial changes the sign of each of its terms. After changing the signs, combine like terms. The constant term of the polynomial being subtracted changes sign too.

The result is $8 x^{2} + 4 x - 9$.

Before collecting like terms, write the additive inverse of the second polynomial as $-2x^2+x-10$. The positive sign of $x$ comes from the product $(-1)(-x)$. Setting $x=0$ compares only the constant terms. Their difference is $1-10=-9$.

</div>
