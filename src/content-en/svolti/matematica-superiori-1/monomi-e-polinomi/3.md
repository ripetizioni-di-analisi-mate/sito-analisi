## Exercise 1

Square $-\dfrac32x^2y$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left(-\frac32 x^2y\right)^2
&=\left(-\frac32\right)\left(-\frac32\right)x^{2+2}y^{1+1}\\[6pt]
&=\frac94 x^4y^2.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The square has two identical factors. The factors can be reordered to calculate the two coefficients together and the powers of $x$ and $y$ separately. The product of the coefficients is $\dfrac94$ because both the numerator and the denominator are squared. At $x=1$ and $y=-2$, the original monomial is $3$ and its square is $9$. The expanded form gives the same value through the product $\dfrac94\cdot4$. The expanded form is positive when both variables are nonzero. When either variable is zero, both the base and its square vanish.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Find the cube of $-\dfrac23xy^2$ and compare the result at $x=1$ and $x=-1$, with $y=1$.

::grafico{f="-8*x^3/27" xmin=-1.65 xmax=1.65 ymin=-1.5 ymax=1.5 punti="-1,0.296296296,pieno,A;1,-0.296296296,pieno,B" legenda="y=-\dfrac8{27}x^3" titolo="Cube values when the second variable is fixed at $1$"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left(-\frac23xy^2\right)^3
&=\left(-\frac23\right)^3x^{1\cdot3}y^{2\cdot3}\\[6pt]
&=-\frac8{27}x^3y^6.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The coefficient is multiplied by itself three times. Since there are three negative factors, the resulting coefficient is negative. Setting $y=1$ leaves a multiple of $x^3$. The value is $-\dfrac8{27}$ at $x=1$ and $\dfrac8{27}$ at $x=-1$, because $(-1)^3=-1$. The graph for $y=1$ shows the two values. The coefficient of the monomial keeps the same sign, while the sign of the numerical value depends on $x$. If $y$ is fixed at $-1$, the factor $y^6$ is still one. The resulting values are therefore the same as those shown in the graph.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Simplify $\left(\dfrac{x^2y}{2}\right)^2-\dfrac{3x^4y^2}{8}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left(\frac{x^2y}{2}\right)^2-\frac{3x^4y^2}{8}
&=\frac14 x^4y^2-\frac38 x^4y^2\\[6pt]
&=\left(\frac28-\frac38\right)x^4y^2\\[6pt]
&=-\frac18 x^4y^2.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The common variable part can be identified after calculating the power. The square of $x^2y$ is $x^4y^2$, so the two terms belong to the same group of like terms. To subtract $\dfrac38$ from $\dfrac14$, write the first coefficient as $\dfrac28$. A negative coefficient remains. At $x=2$ and $y=1$, the first power is $4$ and the subtracted monomial is $6$. The difference is $-2$, as is the product of $-\dfrac18$ and $16$. Both powers in the variable part have even exponents. The simplified monomial is therefore negative when both variables are nonzero and equals zero if either is zero.

</div>
