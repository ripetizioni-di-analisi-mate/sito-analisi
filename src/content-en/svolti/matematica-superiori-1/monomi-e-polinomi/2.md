## Exercise 1

Calculate $(8x^6y)(-2xy^2)$ and check the degree of the product.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(8x^6y)(-2xy^2)
&=(8\cdot(-2))\,x^{6+1}y^{1+2}\\[6pt]
&=-16x^7y^3.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The factors can be reordered because multiplication is commutative. First group the numerical factors, whose product is $-16$. Then count seven factors of $x$ and three factors of $y$. The product has degree $7+3=10$. The same number follows from the two original monomials before multiplying them. The first has $6+1=7$ variable factors, and the second has $1+2=3$. All of these factors remain because the two coefficients being multiplied are nonzero.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Expand $-2x(3x^2-x+4)$ and explain the sign of the quadratic term.

$$
\begin{aligned}
-2x(3x^2-x+4)
&=(-2x)(3x^2)+(-2x)(-x)+(-2x)(4)\\[6pt]
&=-6x^3+2x^2-8x.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the distributive property, the factor $-2x$ multiplies all three terms. The quadratic term comes from $(-2x)(-x)$. The two negative signs give a positive coefficient, while $x\cdot x$ gives $x^2$. The constant term $4$ of the second factor gives the linear term $-8x$. The final constant term is zero because every product contains at least one factor of $x$. At $x=1$, for example, both the original and expanded expressions equal $-12$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Expand $(2x+3)(x-1)$. For $x>1$, interpret the same result as a difference of areas.

::struttura{linee="0,0 7,0 7,2 0,2 0,0" poligoni="0,0 7,0 7,1 0,1" riferimento=0 etichette="3.5,-0.35,2x+3;-0.42,0.5,x-1;-0.42,1.5,1" titolo="The shaded region remains after removing the strip"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
(2x+3)(x-1)
&=(2x+3)x-(2x+3)\\[6pt]
&=2x^2+3x-2x-3\\[6pt]
&=2x^2+x-3.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first product uses the entire base $2x+3$ and height $x$. The second subtracts a strip with the same base and height one. Removing the strip subtracts both the contribution $2x$ and the contribution $3$. This is why the final linear coefficient is $3-2=1$. In the drawing $x=2$, so the base is $7$ and the remaining part has height one and area $7$. The geometric model requires $x>1$. The identity obtained by distribution holds for every real number.

</div>
