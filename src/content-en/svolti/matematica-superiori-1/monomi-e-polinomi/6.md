## Exercise 1

A rectangle has sides $8x$ and $8y$, where $x,y>0$. Which expression gives its perimeter?

$$
P=8x+8y+8x+8y=16 x + 16 y
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The perimeter is the sum of the four side lengths. Opposite sides of a rectangle have equal length. Each of the two lengths therefore occurs twice.

The result is $16 x + 16 y$.

The result can also be read in the form $2(8x+8y)$. The factor $2$ multiplies the sum of a width and a height. For $x=1$ and $y=2$, the sides have lengths $8$ and $16$, so the perimeter is $2(8+16)=48$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A rectangle has width $9x$ and height $2x+3$, where $x>0$. What is its area?

$$
A=9x(2x+3)=18 x^{2} + 27 x
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The area of the rectangle is its width times its height. Apply the distributive property to the product of the monomial and the binomial. Each term contributes to the same area.

The result is $18 x^{2} + 27 x$.

A segment dividing the height into $2x$ and $3$ creates two rectangles of width $9x$. Their areas are $18x^2$ and $27x$. When $x=1$, the full rectangle has width $9$ and height $5$, so its area is $45$, equal to the sum $18+27$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A rectangle has width $x+8$ and height $x+2$, where $x>0$. Express its area as a polynomial.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,3 0,3;3,0 11,0 11,3 3,3;0,3 3,3 3,5 0,5;3,3 11,3 11,5 3,5" riferimento=0 etichette="1.5,-0.4,x;7,-0.4,8;-0.45,1.5,x;-0.45,4,2" titolo="Four areas in the product of two binomials"}

$$
(x+8)(x+2)=x^2+2x+8x+16=x^{2} + 10 x + 16
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each term of the first factor multiplies both terms of the second. This gives four products. The two linear terms are like terms and can be added.

The result is $x^{2} + 10 x + 16$.

When $x>0$, the factors are the dimensions of a rectangle. Dividing the width into lengths $x$ and $8$ and the height into lengths $x$ and $2$ produces four regions. The contributions $2x$ and $8x$ combine to give $10x$ because they multiply the same variable.

</div>
