## Exercise 1

Find the perimeter of a rectangle with side lengths $5x+1$ and $2x$ for $x>0$.

::struttura{linee="0,0 11,0 11,4 0,4 0,0" riferimento=0 etichette="5.5,-0.53,5x+1;-0.6,2,2x" titolo="The two side lengths used in the perimeter calculation"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
P&=2(5x+1)+2(2x)\\[6pt]
 &=10x+2+4x\\[6pt]
 &=14x+2.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Opposite sides are equal. Two sides have length $5x+1$, and the other two have length $2x$. The constant contribution $1$ occurs on two sides, giving the constant term $2$. The variable contributions are $10x$ and $4x$, so they can be added. The figure uses $x=2$. The sides then have lengths $11$ and $4$, and the perimeter is $30$. The simplified formula gives the same result, $14\cdot2+2=30$. If $x$ increases by one, the first side length grows by $5$ and the second grows by $2$. The perimeter therefore increases by $2(5+2)=14$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A square of side length $x$ is removed from one corner of a rectangle with side lengths $3x+1$ and $2x+2$. For $x>0$, find the area remaining.

::struttura{linee="0,0 7,0 7,6 0,6 0,0" poligoni="0,0 7,0 7,4 5,4 5,6 0,6" riferimento=0 etichette="3.5,-0.46,3x+1;-0.58,3,2x+2;6,3.7,x;4.75,5,x" titolo="The removed square occupies the upper right corner"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=(3x+1)(2x+2)-x^2\\[6pt]
 &=6x^2+6x+2x+2-x^2\\[6pt]
 &=5x^2+8x+2.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The square fits inside the rectangle because $x$ is smaller than both side lengths when $x>0$. Calculate the area of the entire rectangle and subtract just the square's area, which is $x^2$. The linear contributions arise from products of a variable length and a constant length. In the figure $x=2$, so the rectangle has area $7\cdot6=42$ and the square has area $4$. The shaded region has area $38$, in agreement with $5x^2+8x+2$. The same area can be found by dividing the remaining shape into a strip across the full base and a smaller rectangle beside the cutout.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A rectangle has perimeter $18x+14$ and one side of length $4x+3$, with $x>0$. Find the other side and the area.

$$
\begin{aligned}
b&=\frac{18x+14}{2}-(4x+3)\\[6pt]
 &=9x+7-4x-3=5x+4,\\[6pt]
A&=(4x+3)(5x+4)\\[6pt]
 &=20x^2+16x+15x+12\\[6pt]
 &=20x^2+31x+12.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The semiperimeter contains each side length once. Dividing the perimeter by two gives $9x+7$, from which the known side length can be subtracted. The resulting side length is positive for $x>0$. The area follows by multiplying the two lengths. The contributions $16x$ and $15x$ come from two different pairs of factors and can be added because they have the same variable part. At $x=1$ the sides are $7$ and $9$, the perimeter is $32$ and the area is $63$. Both formulas give those values.

</div>
