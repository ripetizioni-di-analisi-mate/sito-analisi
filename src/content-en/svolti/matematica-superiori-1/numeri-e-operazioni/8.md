## Exercise 1

There is a mistake in the step $8-(-24+3)=8+24+3$. Find it and check the result in two ways.

$$
\begin{aligned}
8-(-24+3)&=8+24-3=29,\\[6pt]
8-(-24+3)&=8-(-21)=8+21=29.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Subtracting the expression in parentheses means adding its opposite. So the factor $-1$ applies to both terms. The term $-24$ becomes $+24$ and the term $+3$ becomes $-3$. The stated step leaves the second sign unchanged and gives $35$. Evaluating the parentheses first gives a subtraction of $-21$, which is an addition of $21$. Both calculations describe the same change: add $24$ units and remove $3$, for a net increase of $21$ from the starting value $8$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The proposed answers to $\dfrac{10}{9}:\dfrac23$ are $\dfrac53$ and $\dfrac{20}{27}$. Which is correct? Check by multiplying.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{10}{9}:\frac23&=\frac{10}{9}\cdot\frac32=\frac53,\\[6pt]
\frac53\cdot\frac23&=\frac{10}{9},\\[6pt]
\frac{20}{27}\cdot\frac23&=\frac{40}{81}\ne\frac{10}{9}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The quotient is the number that, multiplied by the divisor $\dfrac23$, gives back the dividend $\dfrac{10}{9}$. Dividing by $\dfrac23$ is therefore the same as multiplying by $\dfrac32$. The check confirms $\dfrac53$. The other number, $\dfrac{20}{27}$, comes from multiplying the dividend by the divisor directly. An estimate also rules it out. Since $\dfrac23<1$, the check must reduce the value of the quotient. So the quotient has to exceed $\dfrac{10}{9}$, whereas $\dfrac{20}{27}$ is less than one.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Compare $\dfrac36+\dfrac12:\dfrac34$ with $\left(\dfrac36+\dfrac12\right):\dfrac34$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac36+\frac12:\frac34
&=\frac12+\frac12\cdot\frac43\\[6pt]
&=\frac12+\frac23=\frac76,\\[6pt]
\left(\frac36+\frac12\right):\frac34
&=1:\frac34=\frac43.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the first calculation, division takes precedence over addition and applies only to the second term. The term $\dfrac36$ reduces to $\dfrac12$ and is added to the quotient $\dfrac23$. In the second calculation, the two halves are added first, and their sum is divided by $\dfrac34$. The difference between the results comes from the first half. In the first case its value remains $\dfrac12$, while in the second this half is also multiplied by $\dfrac43$ and becomes $\dfrac23$. The increase is $\dfrac23-\dfrac12=\dfrac16$, which is exactly the difference between $\dfrac43$ and $\dfrac76$.

</div>
