## Exercise 1

Calculate $8-(-24+3)$.

$$
8-(-24+3)=8+24-3=29
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

First calculate the sum $-24+3=-21$. The difference $8-(-21)$ is equivalent to $8+21=29$. It is also possible to distribute the factor $-1$ across the two terms, giving $8+24-3$. Both methods give $29$ because they reverse the sign of the entire sum being subtracted.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Calculate $\dfrac{10}{9}:\dfrac23$.

$$
\frac{10}{9}:\frac23=\frac{10}{9}\cdot\frac32=\frac{5}{3}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Multiplying the divisor $\dfrac23$ by its reciprocal $\dfrac32$ gives $1$. The quotient can therefore be calculated by multiplying the dividend by $\dfrac32$. Canceling common factors in $\dfrac{10}{9}\cdot\dfrac32$ leaves $\dfrac53$. The check is $\dfrac53\cdot\dfrac23=\dfrac{10}{9}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Express $3+\dfrac2{8}$ as a single fraction.

$$
3+\frac2{8}=\frac{24+2}{8}=\frac{13}{4}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The number $3$ equals twenty-four eighths, so adding two eighths gives twenty-six eighths. Dividing the numerator and denominator by $2$ gives $\dfrac{13}{4}$. Another check starts with $\dfrac28=\dfrac14$. Since $3=\dfrac{12}{4}$, the sum is one quarter above three.

</div>
