## Exercise 1

A bottle contains $\dfrac{8}3$ liters. If the same amount is added, how many liters does it contain?

$$
\frac{8}3+\frac{8}3=\frac{16}3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Both amounts are measured in liters. Each amount is eight thirds of a liter, so the total is sixteen thirds of a liter. This is five whole liters and another third of a liter because $16=5\cdot3+1$. Thus the total lies between five and six liters.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Make packs of 4 red pens and 3 blue pens using $36$ red pens and $27$ blue pens. After giving away 2 packs, how many pens are left?

$$
\frac{36}4=9,\qquad(9-2)\cdot7=49
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Both supplies make nine packs because $36:4=9$ and $27:3=9$. Seven packs remain after two are given away. Each contains seven pens, so $49$ pens remain. The result can also be checked by color. There are $28$ red pens and $21$ blue pens left, giving the same total, $49$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Which integer makes the equation $-8+z=9$ true?

$$
z=9+8=17
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Interpret $z$ as the change from the initial value $-8$ to the final value $9$. From $-8$, travel eight units to the right to reach zero and another nine to reach $9$. The total change is therefore $17$. Substitution gives $-8+17=9$.

</div>
