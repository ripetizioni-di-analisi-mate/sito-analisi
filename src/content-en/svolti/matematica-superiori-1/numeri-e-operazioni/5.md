## Exercise 1

What is the greatest common divisor of $48$ and $72$?

$$
\gcd(48,72)=24
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The prime factorizations are $48=2^4\cdot3$ and $72=2^3\cdot3^2$. A common divisor can contain at most three factors of $2$ and one factor of $3$ because these are the smaller exponents in the two factorizations. Their product is $2^3\cdot3=24$. Indeed, $48:24=2$ and $72:24=3$, and $2$ and $3$ are relatively prime.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Two signals repeat every $36$ and $54$ seconds, respectively, and start together. How many seconds pass before they next occur together?

$$
\operatorname{lcm}(36,54)=108
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each coincidence requires a whole number of periods to have elapsed for both signals. The first positive multiples of $36$ are $36$, $72$, and $108$, while those of $54$ begin with $54$ and $108$. The first common value is $108\,\mathrm{s}$. At that point the first signal has completed three periods and the second has completed two.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Make packs of 4 red pens and 3 blue pens using $40$ red pens and $30$ blue pens. After giving away 2 packs, how many pens are left?

$$
\frac{40}4=10,\qquad(10-2)\cdot7=56
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The red pens make $40:4=10$ packs and the blue pens make $30:3=10$, so both supplies run out together. Eight packs remain after two are given away. Each pack contains seven pens, so the required number is $8\cdot7=56$. Subtracting the fourteen pens given away from the original seventy gives the same result.

</div>
