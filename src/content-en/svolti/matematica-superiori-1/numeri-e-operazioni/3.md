## Exercise 1

Calculate $20-18:3\cdot2$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
20-18:3\cdot2&=20-(18:3)\cdot2\\[6pt]
&=20-6\cdot2\\[6pt]
&=20-12=8.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Multiplication and division precede subtraction. They have the same precedence, so they are evaluated from left to right. Eighteen divided by three gives six, and the following multiplication gives twelve. Thus twelve is subtracted from twenty. The expression $20-18:(3\cdot2)$ would give $20-3=17$ because its divisor is the product of three and two. In the given expression the divisor is just three. Comparing the two expressions makes explicit which value is subtracted from $20$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Calculate $3\cdot9-2(9+1)$.

::grafico{xmin=-0.6 xmax=10.7 ymin=-0.5 ymax=7.15 assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,3.35 9,3.35 9,6.35 0,6.35;0,0 9,0 9,2 0,2;9,0 10,0 10,2 9,2" linee="0,4.35 9,4.35;0,5.35 9,5.35;0,1 10,1;9,0 9,2" etichette="4.0,6.72,3 × 9;4.12,2.35,2 × 9;9.05,2.35,2 × 1" titolo="Two rows of nine and two units are removed from three rows of nine"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
3\cdot9-2(9+1)&=27-2\cdot10\\[6pt]
&=27-20\\[6pt]
&=7.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The second product has the entire sum $9+1$ as a factor, and that sum equals $10$. The first product equals $27$ independently of the calculation of the second. In the figure, the lower rectangle can be split into a rectangle with two rows of nine and a strip with two units. Removing the first two rows from the upper rectangle with three rows of nine leaves one row. Removing the strip's two units as well gives $9-2=7$. This illustrates distributivity and confirms the difference $27-20$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Calculate $\left(\dfrac34-\dfrac56\right):\left(-\dfrac29\right)+\dfrac12$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac34-\frac56&=\frac9{12}-\frac{10}{12}
=-\frac1{12}\\[6pt]
\left(-\frac1{12}\right):\left(-\frac29\right)
&=\left(-\frac1{12}\right)\left(-\frac92\right)\\[6pt]
&=\frac38\\[6pt]
\frac38+\frac12&=\frac38+\frac48=\frac78.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Evaluate the first group in twelfths. Three quarters occupies nine parts and five sixths occupies ten, leaving one part with a negative sign. The divisor is negative too. Since it is nonzero, we can multiply by its reciprocal, $-\dfrac92$. The product is positive and equals $\dfrac38$. The final half is added to the quotient once it has been written in eighths. As a check, the product $\dfrac38\cdot\left(-\dfrac29\right)=-\dfrac1{12}$ gives exactly the original dividend.

</div>
