## Exercise 1

Calculate $\dfrac{9}{24}+\dfrac{1}{3}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac9{24}+\frac13&=\frac9{24}+\frac8{24} \\[6pt]
&=\frac{17}{24}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The second term is one third. In twenty-fourths, one third is eight parts because $24:3=8$. Each of the first term’s nine parts is now the same size as each of the second term’s eight parts, so add the numerators. The result is greater than $\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{12}{24}$ because its numerator is $17$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Calculate $\dfrac{9}{18}\cdot\dfrac13$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac9{18}\cdot\frac13&=\frac9{54} \\[6pt]
&=\frac16
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first factor, $\dfrac{9}{18}$, equals $\dfrac12$ because the numerator and denominator can be divided by $9$. The product is therefore one third of one half. Reducing $\dfrac9{54}$ gives the same value, $\dfrac16$, because $54=9\cdot6$. The result is smaller than either factor, as it must be when multiplying two positive numbers less than $1$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Calculate $\dfrac{11}{10}:\dfrac23$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{11}{10}:\frac23&=\frac{11}{10}\cdot\frac32 \\[6pt]
&=\frac{33}{20}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The quotient is the number that gives $\dfrac{11}{10}$ when multiplied by $\dfrac23$. The reciprocal $\dfrac32$ is the factor that turns $\dfrac23$ into $1$, so multiply the dividend by $\dfrac32$. Checking in the opposite direction gives $\dfrac{33}{20}\cdot\dfrac23=\dfrac{11}{10}$. The quotient is larger than the dividend because $\dfrac32>1$.

</div>
