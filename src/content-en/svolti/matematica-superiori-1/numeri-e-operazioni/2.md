## Exercise 1

Calculate $\dfrac9{24}+\dfrac13$.

::grafico{xmin=-1 xmax=25 ymin=-0.45 ymax=2.15 assi=0 poligoni="0,0.27 9,0.27 9,0.99 0,0.99;9,0.27 17,0.27 17,0.99 9,0.99" linee="0,0.27 24,0.27 24,0.99 0,0.99 0,0.27;1,0.27 1,0.99;2,0.27 2,0.99;3,0.27 3,0.99;4,0.27 4,0.99;5,0.27 5,0.99;6,0.27 6,0.99;7,0.27 7,0.99;8,0.27 8,0.99;9,0.27 9,0.99;10,0.27 10,0.99;11,0.27 11,0.99;12,0.27 12,0.99;13,0.27 13,0.99;14,0.27 14,0.99;15,0.27 15,0.99;16,0.27 16,0.99;17,0.27 17,0.99;18,0.27 18,0.99;19,0.27 19,0.99;20,0.27 20,0.99;21,0.27 21,0.99;22,0.27 22,0.99;23,0.27 23,0.99" etichette="4.27,1.47,9;12.77,1.47,8;20.27,1.47,7" titolo="Nine twenty-fourths plus eight twenty-fourths leave seven parts empty"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac9{24}+\frac13
&=\frac9{24}+\frac{1\cdot8}{3\cdot8}\\[6pt]
&=\frac9{24}+\frac8{24}\\[6pt]
&=\frac{17}{24}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The unit in the first addend is divided into twenty-four parts. To use the same parts for the second addend, observe that each third contains eight, since $24:3=8$. So eight twenty-fourths are added to nine. The result is greater than one half and less than three quarters, which correspond to twelve and eighteen twenty-fourths. One way to check the choice of denominator is to reduce the first addend to $\dfrac38$. The least common multiple of $8$ and $3$ is $24$, so the reduced fraction leads to the same partition.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Calculate $\dfrac9{18}\cdot\dfrac13$.

::grafico{xmin=-0.45 xmax=2.45 ymin=-0.38 ymax=3.6 uguale=1 assi=0 poligoni="0,0 1,0 1,1 0,1" linee="0,0 2,0 2,3 0,3 0,0;1,0 1,3;0,1 2,1;0,2 2,2" titolo="One third of the first column occupies one of the six cells"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac9{18}\cdot\frac13
&=\frac12\cdot\frac13\\[6pt]
&=\frac16.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Dividing the numerator and denominator of the first factor by $9$ gives $\dfrac12$. The product then represents one third of one half. In a rectangle divided into two columns and three rows, one column is half the area. One of the three cells in that column is therefore one sixth of the full rectangle. A check computes the product $\dfrac9{54}$ directly and reduces it by $9$. The result is smaller than each factor because the operation takes a part of each positive quantity.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Calculate $\dfrac{11}{10}:\dfrac23$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{11}{10}:\frac23
&=\frac{11}{10}\cdot\frac32\\[6pt]
&=\frac{33}{20}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The required quotient is the number that gives $\dfrac{11}{10}$ when multiplied by $\dfrac23$. The reciprocal $\dfrac32$ is defined because the divisor is nonzero. Multiplying by that reciprocal undoes multiplication by $\dfrac23$, whose product with $\dfrac32$ equals $1$. This gives $\dfrac{33}{20}$. The inverse check is $\dfrac{33}{20}\cdot\dfrac23=\dfrac{11}{10}$. A bound can also be predicted. The quotient is greater than the dividend because $\dfrac32>1$, but less than twice the dividend because $\dfrac32<2$.

</div>
