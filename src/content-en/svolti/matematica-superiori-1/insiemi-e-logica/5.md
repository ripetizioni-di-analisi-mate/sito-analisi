## Exercise 1

Let $A=\left\{8, 9\right\}$ and $B=\left\{10, 11\right\}$. Which pair belongs to $A\times B$?

- $(8;10)$
- $(10;8)$
- $(8;9)$
- $(10;11)$

$$
(8;10)\in A\times B
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first component must belong to $A$ and the second to $B$. The pair $(8;10)$ meets both conditions. The pair $(8;9)$ fails the second condition because $9$ belongs to $A$. The other two pairs fail the first because $10$ does not belong to $A$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The domain is $A=\left\{9, 10, 11\right\}$ and the codomain is $B=\{1;2;3\}$. Which relation, written as a set of ordered pairs, is a function from $A$ to $B$?

- $\{(9;1),(9;2),(10;2),(11;3)\}$
- $\{(9;1),(10;2),(11;2)\}$
- $\{(9;1),(10;2)\}$
- $\{(9;1),(10;2),(11;4)\}$

$$
f:\ 9\mapsto1,\quad10\mapsto2,\quad11\mapsto2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each of the three domain elements must occur in the first position of exactly one pair. The chosen relation maps $9$ to $1$, $10$ to $2$, and $11$ to $2$. The image $2$ can occur twice because it comes from distinct elements of the domain. A pair with second component $4$ would violate the specified codomain.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The function $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ is defined by $f(x)=2x+10$. What is the image of $-8$?

$$
f(-8)=2(-8)+10=-6
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The image of $-8$ is the value found by substituting it for $x$ in the formula. First multiply $-8$ by $2$ to obtain $-16$, then add $10$. The result is $-6$. Since $-8$ is real, it is in the stated domain and can be substituted.

</div>
