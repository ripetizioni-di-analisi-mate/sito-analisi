## Exercise 1

Let $A=\{-1;2\}$ and $B=\{1;3;4\}$. Plot $A\times B$ and give a pair in $B\times A$ that lies outside $A\times B$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
A\times B&=\{(-1;1);(-1;3);(-1;4)\}\\[5pt]
&\qquad{}\cup\{(2;1);(2;3);(2;4)\},\\[5pt]
(3;2)&\in B\times A,\qquad (3;2)\notin A\times B.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{xmin=-2 xmax=3 ymin=0 ymax=5 punti="-1,1;-1,3;-1,4;2,1;2,3;2,4" titolo="The two first coordinates from $A$ generate three points each"}

<div class="spiegazione">

First fix $-1$ and pair it with each of the three elements of $B$. Then repeat with $2$. The plot shows two columns with three points each, for a total of $2\cdot3=6$. To read $B\times A$, choose a first coordinate in $B$ and a second in $A$. The pair $(3;2)$ meets both requirements. To belong to $A\times B$ as well, its first coordinate would have to belong to $A$, but $3\notin A$. **Coordinate order** determines membership.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Let $A=\{0;1;2\}$ and $B=\{1;2;3\}$. Write the relation $R$ consisting of the pairs $(a;b)$ in $A\times B$ with $a+b=3$. Is it a function from $A$ to $B$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
a+b=3&\ \Longrightarrow\ b=3-a,\\[5pt]
0&\mapsto3,\qquad1\mapsto2,\qquad2\mapsto1,\\[5pt]
R&=\{(0;3);(1;2);(2;1)\}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The equation forces a single choice of $b$ once we fix $a$. We still need to **check both sets**. Test all first coordinates in $A$ and verify that the second coordinates, $3$, $2$, and $1$, belong to $B$. Each element of $A$ thus occurs exactly once and $R$ is a function with image $\{1;2;3\}$. If $3$ were added to $A$ with the same $B$, it would require the new value $0$, which lies outside the codomain.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Let $A=\{-1;0;1\}$ and $B=\{0;1;2\}$. Turn the relation $R=\{(-1;1);(0;0);(0;2);(1;1)\}$ into a function by removing exactly one pair. Find all possibilities and their images.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R\setminus\{(0;2)\}&=\{(-1;1);(0;0);(1;1)\},& f(A)&=\{0;1\},\\[5pt]
R\setminus\{(0;0)\}&=\{(-1;1);(0;2);(1;1)\},& g(A)&=\{1;2\}.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{xmin=-2 xmax=2 ymin=-1 ymax=3 punti="-1,1;0,0;0,2;1,1" titolo="The two points at first coordinate $0$ are the candidates for removal"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Count the pairs by **first coordinate**. The first coordinates $-1$ and $1$ already have exactly one point each. The issue is at $0$, where the two plotted points assign different outputs. Removing either point there solves the problem. Removing any other point would leave an input without an image while keeping two images for $0$. In either function the inputs $-1$ and $1$ both give $1$, so $1$ occurs once in the image set.

</div>
