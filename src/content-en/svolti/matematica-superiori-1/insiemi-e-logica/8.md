## Exercise 1

Set $A$ has $8$ elements and set $B$ has $9$ elements. How many ordered pairs are in $A\times B$?

$$
|A\times B|=8\cdot9=72
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each of the eight elements of $A$ can come first. For each choice there are nine independent options for the second element, one for each element of $B$. This gives eight groups of nine pairs, or $72$ pairs. Changing the first element gives a different pair even when the second stays the same.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In a group of $40$ students, $24$ study English, $16$ study French, and $8$ study both languages. How many study at least one of the two languages?

$$
24+16-8=32
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The English group includes the $8$ students who study both languages, and the French group includes them again. So there are $24-8=16$ students who study only English and $16-8=8$ who study only French. Including the $8$ shared students, the union has $16+8+8=32$ members. The remaining $8$ of the $40$ students study neither English nor French.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The domain is $A=\left\{10, 11, 12\right\}$ and the codomain is $B=\{1;2;3\}$. Which relation, written as a set of ordered pairs, is a function from $A$ to $B$?

- $\{(10;1),(10;2),(11;2),(12;3)\}$
- $\{(10;1),(11;2),(12;2)\}$
- $\{(10;1),(11;2)\}$
- $\{(10;1),(11;2),(12;4)\}$

$$
f:\ 10\mapsto1,\quad11\mapsto2,\quad12\mapsto2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the correct relation, the first components $10$, $11$, and $12$ appear once each. Both second components, $1$ and $2$, belong to $B$. The definition allows $3$ to be absent from the images. The function must be defined on the whole domain, while its range may be a proper subset of the codomain.

</div>
