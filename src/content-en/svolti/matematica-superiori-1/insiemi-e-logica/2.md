## Exercise 1

In the universal set $U=\left\{8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13\right\}$, let $A=\left\{8, 9, 10\right\}$. What is the complement of $A$ relative to $U$?

$$
U\setminus A=\{11;12;13\}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The complement is taken relative to the universal set $U$. Remove the elements of $A$, namely $8$, $9$, and $10$, from $U$. This leaves $11$, $12$, and $13$. To check, the sets are disjoint and their union gives all six elements of $U$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In a class of $54$ students, $36$ swim, play tennis, or do both. How many do neither?

$$
54-36=18
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The $36$ students counted do at least one sport, so they are in the union of the two groups. The other students are exactly those outside that union. The total is $54$, so $54-36=18$ students are outside it. The size of the union is all that is needed to find the size of its complement.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Let $U=\left\{10, 11, 12, 13\right\}$, $A=\left\{10, 12\right\}$, and $B=\left\{11, 12\right\}$. What is $U\setminus(A\cup B)$?

$$
U\setminus(A\cup B)=\{13\}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

First find the union. The element $10$ is in $A$, $11$ is in $B$, and $12$ is in both, so the union has three elements. Removing them from $U$ leaves only $13$. Indeed, $13$ is in the universal set and in neither of the two sets.

</div>
