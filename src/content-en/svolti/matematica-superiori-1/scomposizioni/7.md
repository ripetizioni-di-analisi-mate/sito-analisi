## Exercise 1

The polynomial $P(x)=(x+8)(x-1)$ is already factored. What is $P(-8)$?

$$
P(-8)=(-8+8)(-8-1)=0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Substituting the opposite of the number into the first factor makes it zero. A product is zero if one factor is zero. The value can be found directly from the factored polynomial.

The result is $0$.

Substituting $x=-8$ into the first factor gives $-8+8=0$. It gives $-9$ in the second factor. The product is zero because one factor is zero.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What value of $x$ makes the factor $x+9$ zero in the factorization $(x+9)(x+2)$?

::grafico{f="(x+9)*(x+2)" xmin=-11 xmax=0 ymin=-15 ymax=20 punti="-9,0,pieno;-2,0,pieno" titolo="Zeros of the two polynomial factors"}

$$
x+9=0,\qquad x=-9
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To make the given factor zero, the sum of x and the number must be zero. The value sought is the opposite of that number. Substitute it in the factor to check that it vanishes.

The result is $-9$.

To solve $x+9=0$, subtract $9$ from both sides. The result $-9$ zeros the given factor. The second factor vanishes at $-2$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

For what value of $c$ does the identity $x^2+11x+c=(x+8)(x+3)$ hold?

$$
(x+8)(x+3)=x^2+11x+24,\quad c=24
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Expand the product of the two binomials. The constant term comes from multiplying their constant terms. The identity requires c to equal that product.

The result is $24$.

At $x=0$, only the product of the constants $8$ and $3$ remains. The terms in $x$ come from $8x$ and $3x$, so the coefficient $11$ agrees with the given factors.

</div>
