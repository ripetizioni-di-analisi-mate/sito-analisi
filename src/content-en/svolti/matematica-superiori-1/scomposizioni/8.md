## Exercise 1

For $x\ne0$, what quotient results when $8x$ is factored out of $8x^2+24x$?

$$
\frac{8x^2+24x}{8x}=x+3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Factoring out a term means dividing each term by that factor. The first term gives x and the second gives three. The remaining polynomial is their sum.

The result is $x + 3$.

Check the calculation by multiplying by $8x$ again. This gives the terms $8x^2$ and $24x$ separately, so the remaining factor is $x+3$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Factor $x^2y+9xy^2$.

$$
x^2y+9xy^2=xy(x+9y)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Both terms contain an x and a y. After taking out their product, the first term leaves x and the second leaves $9 y$. Multiply the common factor by the result to verify the factorization.

The result is $x y \left(x + 9 y\right)$.

Multiplying $xy$ by $x$ recovers $x^2y$. Multiplying it by $9y$ recovers $9xy^2$. These two products check the individual terms.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Factor $xy+10x+2y+20$.

$$
xy+10x+2y+20=x(y+10)+2(y+10)=(x+2)(y+10)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Group the first two terms and the last two terms. Factor each group to leave the same binomial. Then factor out the common binomial.

The result is $\left(x + 2\right) \left(y + 10\right)$.

The group $xy+10x$ gives $x(y+10)$. The group $2y+20$ gives $2(y+10)$. Expanding the final product recovers $xy$, the two mixed terms $10x$ and $2y$, and the constant $20$.

</div>
