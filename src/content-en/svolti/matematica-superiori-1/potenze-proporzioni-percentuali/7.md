## Exercise 1

On a map with scale $1:50000$, a distance measures $24$ cm. What distance does this represent in kilometers?

$$
24\cdot50000\,\mathrm{cm}=12\,\mathrm{km}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The scale uses the same unit for the map and actual distances. Multiplying $24$ centimeters by $50000$ gives $1200000$ centimeters on the ground. Since $1\,\mathrm{km}=100000\,\mathrm{cm}$, divide by $100000$ to get $12\,\mathrm{km}$. Converting $12\,\mathrm{km}$ back gives $24$ centimeters on the map.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Two people split $40$ euros in the ratio $2:3$. How many euros does the second person receive?

$$
\frac{40}{2+3}=8,\qquad3\cdot8=24
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

There are five shares in total, so each share is worth $40:5=8$ euros. The second person receives three shares, or $24$ euros, and the first receives $16$ euros. The ratio of $16$ to $24$ simplifies to $2:3$, as required.

</div>

## Exercise 3

In the proportion $2:3=20:x$, what is $x$?

$$
2x=3\cdot20,\qquad x=30
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The ratio of the first term to the second must be the same on both sides. To go from $2$ to $20$, multiply by $10$, so apply the same factor to $3$. This gives $30$. Checking the cross products gives $2\cdot30=3\cdot20=60$.

</div>
