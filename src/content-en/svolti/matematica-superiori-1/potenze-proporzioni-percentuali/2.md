## Exercise 1

Calculate $-8^2$, where the minus sign is outside the base of the power.

$$
-8^2=-(8\cdot8)=-64
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The minus sign is outside the power, so square the positive number $8$. Taking the opposite of $64$ gives $-64$. In $\left(-8\right)^2$, the base is the negative number $-8$, so the product of two negative factors would be positive.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Calculate $9^{-2}$.

$$
9^{-2}=\frac1{9^2}=\frac{1}{81}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The exponent $-2$ gives the reciprocal of $9^2$. This reciprocal is defined because the square of $9$ is nonzero. Since $9^2=81$, the result is $\dfrac1{81}$. To check, the exponents in $9^2\cdot9^{-2}$ must add to zero, and the product is indeed $81\cdot\dfrac1{81}=1$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

What is the ratio of $10\cdot10^9$ to $10$?

$$
\frac{10\cdot10^9}{10}=10^9=1000000000
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The required factor is the number by which $10$ must be multiplied to get $10\cdot10^9$. Dividing by $10$ cancels the common factor and leaves $10^9$. Its decimal representation has nine zeros. The result gives a multiplicative factor, whereas subtraction would measure the difference between the quantities.

</div>
