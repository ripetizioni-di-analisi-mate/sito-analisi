## Exercise 1

Evaluate $-8^2$ and $(-8)^2$ and place the results on the number line.

$$
\begin{aligned}
-8^2&=-(8\cdot8)=-64,\\[6pt]
(-8)^2&=(-8)\cdot(-8)=64.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{xmin=-80 xmax=80 ymin=-0.6 ymax=1.45 assi=0 vettori="-78,0,78,0" punti="-64,0,pieno;0,0,pieno;64,0,pieno" etichette="-64,-0.29,−64;0,-0.29,0;64,-0.29,64" titolo="The values $-64$ and $64$ are placed symmetrically about zero"}

<div class="spiegazione">

In the first case the base is $8$. Evaluate the square and then take its opposite, so the result lies to the left of zero. In the second case the minus sign is part of the base. Since both factors are negative, the product is positive and lies to the right of zero. Both values are $64$ units from the origin. This symmetry follows from the base having absolute value $8$ in both calculations.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Evaluate $\left(-\dfrac32\right)^{-3}$ and check the result by multiplication.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left(-\frac32\right)^3&=-\frac{27}{8},\\[6pt]
\left(-\frac32\right)^{-3}&=\frac{1}{-\frac{27}{8}}
=-\frac8{27},\\[6pt]
\left(-\frac{27}{8}\right)\left(-\frac8{27}\right)&=1.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The base is nonzero, so the negative power is defined. The $3$ calls for three equal factors, and the odd exponent gives a negative result. After taking the cube, take the reciprocal. This interchanges numerator and denominator and preserves the sign. The result has absolute value less than $1$ because the original cube has absolute value greater than $1$. Their product equals $1$, as required of two reciprocals.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Evaluate $\dfrac{(8\cdot10^{-4})(3\cdot10^6)}{6\cdot10^2}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{(8\cdot10^{-4})(3\cdot10^6)}{6\cdot10^2}
&=\frac{8\cdot3}{6} \cdot 10^{-4+6-2}\\[6pt]
&=4\cdot10^0\\[6pt]
&=4.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The quotient separates the coefficients from the powers of ten, since all factors in the denominator are nonzero. The coefficients give $\dfrac{24}{6}=4$. The powers in the numerator have total exponent $-4+6=2$, which matches the denominator. Their ratio is therefore $10^0=1$. The result depends only on the ratio of the coefficients. As a check, the numerator can be written as $2400$ and the denominator as $600$.

</div>
