## Exercise 1

$8$ notebooks cost $24$ euros. What is the cost of $40$ notebooks at the same unit price?

$$
\frac{24}{8}=3,\qquad 3\cdot40=120
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

One notebook costs $24:8=3$ euros. At that fixed unit price, $40$ notebooks cost $40\cdot3=120$ euros. Another calculation uses $40=5\cdot8$. Five sets of eight therefore cost $5\cdot24=120$ euros.

</div>

## Exercise 2

It takes 3 identical machines $36$ hours to finish a job. How many hours will 6 machines take to finish the same job at the same rate per machine?

$$
3\cdot36=6t,\qquad t=18
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Three machines running for $36$ hours do $3\cdot36=108$ hours of work at the rate of a single machine. All six machines work at the same time and at the same rate, so each one does a sixth of those hours. The duration is therefore $108:6=18$ hours. The product $6\cdot18$ confirms that the total work is the same.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A recipe for 4 people requires $16$ hectograms of flour. How many hectograms are needed for 7 people if the proportions stay the same?

$$
16\cdot\frac74=28
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Keeping the proportions means keeping the same amount per person. The initial amount per person is $16:4=4$ hectograms, so the total for seven people is $7\cdot4=28$ hectograms. Going from four to seven people also adds three individual portions, giving $16+3\cdot4=28$.

</div>
