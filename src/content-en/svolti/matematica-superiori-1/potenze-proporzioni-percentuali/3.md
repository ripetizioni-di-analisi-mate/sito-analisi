## Exercise 1

Solve the proportion $2:3=16:x$ and check the value in the original proportion.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac23&=\frac{16}{x}, &x&\ne0,\\[6pt]
2x&=3\cdot16=48,\\[6pt]
x&=24.
\end{aligned}
\qquad
\frac{16}{24}=\frac23.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The fraction containing the unknown requires $x\ne0$. In this domain both sides can be multiplied by $3x$, since the product is nonzero. This gives $2x=48$, whose only solution is $x=24$. The calculation can also be read as scaling the original pair. The first term goes from $2$ to $16$ with factor $8$, so the second must go from $3$ to $24$. The result lies in the domain and the fractions agree.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Divide $45$ euros in the ratio $2:3$ and state what fraction of the total the second person receives.

$$
\begin{aligned}
q&=\frac{45}{2+3}=9,\\[6pt]
A&=2q=18,\qquad B=3q=27,\\[6pt]
\frac B{A+B}&=\frac{27}{45}=\frac35.
\end{aligned}
$$

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,0.8 0,0.8;2,0 5,0 5,0.8 2,0.8" linee="1,0 1,0.8;2,0 2,0.8;3,0 3,0.8;4,0 4,0.8" etichette="1,1.15,18 €;3.5,1.15,27 €" titolo="Five shares of 9 euros make up the total of 45 euros"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The shares are $2q$ and $3q$, where $q$ is the value of one part. The equal parts make up the total, so five parts are worth $45$ euros and one is worth $9$ euros. The second person receives $27$ euros. The ratio between the two amounts is $\dfrac{18}{27}=\dfrac23$, and the ratio of the second amount to the total is $\dfrac{27}{45}=\dfrac35$. The denominator changes according to the quantity being used for comparison.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A recipe for $4$ people calls for $20$ hectograms of flour. Find the amount for $7$ people using the same portion per person.

$$
\begin{aligned}
k&=\frac{20}{4}=5,\\[6pt]
F(7)&=7\cdot5=35,\\[6pt]
\frac{F(7)}{7}&=\frac{20}{4}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Since everyone receives the same portion, the ratio of total flour to number of people is constant. The value $k=5$ is the portion in hectograms per person, and multiplying it by seven gives $35$ hectograms. On the graph both measurements lie on the line $F=5p$ through the origin. The point with horizontal coordinate $0$ has vertical coordinate $0$, since there are no portions to prepare. Comparing the two ratios confirms that individual portions are the same.

</div>
