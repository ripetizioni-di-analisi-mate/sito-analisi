## Exercise 1

In the proportion $2:3=16:x$, what is $x$?

$$
2x=3\cdot16,\qquad x=24
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Write the proportion as $\dfrac23=\dfrac{16}{x}$, where $x\ne0$. Multiplying by $3x$ gives $2x=3\cdot16=48$. Dividing both sides by $2$ gives $x=24$. This meets the condition on the denominator and confirms the equality because $\dfrac{16}{24}$ reduces to $\dfrac23$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Two people split $45$ euros in the ratio $2:3$. How many euros does the second person receive?

$$
\frac{45}{2+3}=9,\qquad3\cdot9=27
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Split the amount into shares with equal value. The ratio $2:3$ requires five shares. Each is worth $45:5=9$ euros, so three shares are worth $27$ euros. The first person receives $18$ euros, and dividing both amounts by $9$ gives the ratio $2:3$ again.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A recipe for 4 people requires $20$ hectograms of flour. How many hectograms are needed for 7 people if the proportions stay the same?

$$
20\cdot\frac74=35
$$

::grafico{f="5*x" xmin=-0.5 xmax=8 ymin=-1 ymax=41 punti="4,20,pieno;7,35,pieno" titolo="The amount of flour increases by 5 hectograms per person"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The amount per person is $20:4=5$ hectograms. Keeping that amount per person, seven people need $7\cdot5=35$ hectograms. On the graph, the two points have the same ratio of vertical to horizontal coordinate, so they lie on the line $y=5x$. Dividing $35$ by $7$ also gives the amount per person.

</div>
