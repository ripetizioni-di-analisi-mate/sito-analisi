## Exercise 1

Expand $(4x+3)(4x-3)$ and explain what happens to the mixed terms.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(4x+3)(4x-3)&=16x^2-12x+12x-9\\[6pt]
&=(4x)^2-3^2=16x^2-9 .
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The distributive expansion gives one mixed product with $-3$ and one with $3$. Their sum is zero because they share the factor $4x$. The remaining products come from $(4x)(4x)$ and $3\cdot(-3)$. The roots $x=\pm\dfrac34$, visible in the two factors, also appear as intercepts of the expanded graph. Between these values the product is negative because the first factor is positive and the second one negative.

Outside the interval between the zeros, the factors have the same sign. The factored form therefore also shows where the product is positive.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Calculate $47\cdot53$ using the midpoint of the factors. Give their common distance from that midpoint.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{47+53}{2}&=50, & \dfrac{53-47}{2}&=3,\\[6pt]
47\cdot53&=(50-3)(50+3)\\[6pt]
&=2500-9=2491 .
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Find the base $50$ by taking the mean of the factors. Half their difference gives $3$, the distance of each one from the mean. The factors now have the form required for the product of a sum and difference. The value is $9$ below $50^2$. A larger common offset reduces the product by the square of the offset. For example, $46\cdot54$ would be $16$ below $2500$.

A choice such as $49$ leaves unequal offsets. Writing $(49-2)(49+4)$ would leave a total mixed contribution of $98$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Remove a square with side length $5$ cm from one corner of a square with side length $13$ cm. Rearrange the remaining region into a rectangle and find its area.

$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=13^2-5^2\\[6pt]
&=(13-5)(13+5)\\[6pt]
&=8\cdot18=144\ \mathrm{cm}^2 .
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

A cut separates the L-shaped region into rectangles of dimensions $13\times8$ and $8\times5$. Turn the second rectangle so the sides of length $8$ line up. The rearranged rectangle has one side $8$ cm long and the other $13+5=18$ cm long. The difference of squares becomes the product of two positive lengths, the difference between the original sides, $8$, and their sum, $18$.

Each region keeps its area in the rearrangement. The direct check $169-25=144$ also uses only the original squares' side lengths.

</div>
