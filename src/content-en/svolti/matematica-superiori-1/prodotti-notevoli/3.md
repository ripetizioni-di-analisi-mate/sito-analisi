## Exercise 1

What is the expansion of $(x+8)^3$?

$$
(x+8)^3=x^3+24x^2+192x+512
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To cube a binomial, multiply its square by the binomial itself. The quadratic and linear terms each receive three equal contributions. The coefficients follow the pattern one, three, three, and one.

The result is $x^{3} + 24 x^{2} + 192 x + 512$.

To find the coefficient of $x^2$, choose $8$ from just one of the three factors. The three possible choices give $24x^2$. To find the coefficient of $x$, choose $8$ from two factors, giving $3\cdot64=192$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Expand $(x-9)^3$.

$$
(x-9)^3=x^3-27x^2+243x-729
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Use the cube of a sum with a negative second term. Terms containing an odd power of that number are negative. The term containing its square is positive.

The result is $x^{3} - 27 x^{2} + 243 x - 729$.

The quadratic term contains one power of $-9$ and equals $-27x^2$. The linear term contains $(-9)^2$ and equals $243x$. When $x=9$, the four terms add to zero, as does the original expression.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Expand $(x+y+10)^2$.

$$
(x+y+10)^2=x^2+y^2+100+2xy+20x+20y
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The square of a sum of three terms contains the three squares and three doubled products. Each pair occurs once among the doubled products. Include all these contributions.

The result is $x^{2} + 2 x y + 20 x + y^{2} + 20 y + 100$.

The three pairs are $x,y$, $x,10$, and $y,10$. Their doubled products are $2xy$, $20x$, and $20y$. At $y=0$, only $x^2+20x+100$ remains, which is the square of $x+10$ expanded.

</div>
