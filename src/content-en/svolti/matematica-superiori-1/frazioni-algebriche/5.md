## Exercise 1

For $x\ne 8$ and $x\ne -8$, calculate $\dfrac{x-8}{x+8}\cdot\dfrac{x+8}{2(x-8)}$.

$$
\frac{(x-8)(x+8)}{2(x+8)(x-8)}=\frac12
$$

::grafico{f="0.5" xmin=-11 xmax=11 ymin=-1 ymax=2 punti="-8,0.5,vuoto;8,0.5,vuoto" titolo="The constant product retains the two excluded values"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The product of the denominators is $2(x+8)(x-8)$. The numerator has the same two linear factors, which can be canceled throughout the given domain. The only denominator left is $2$.

For a numerical check, choose $x=0$, which satisfies both restrictions. The factors are $-1$ and $-\dfrac12$, whose product is $\dfrac12$. The graph is horizontal and the open circles indicate the exclusions from the two different fractions.

</div>

## Exercise 2

For $x\ne 9$ and $x\ne -9$, calculate $\dfrac{x-9}{x+9}:\dfrac{2(x-9)}{x+9}$.

$$
\frac{x-9}{x+9}\cdot\frac{x+9}{2(x-9)}=\frac12
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The divisor fraction has denominator $x+9$ and numerator $2(x-9)$. Both are nonzero, so the reciprocal is defined. In the product, the two linear factors cancel, leaving $\dfrac12$.

On the stated domain the divisor is twice the dividend, which is itself nonzero. The quotient must therefore be $\dfrac12$. This observation also checks the order of division, since reversing the fractions would give a quotient of $2$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Find the domain and simplify $\dfrac{x+4}{x-1}:\dfrac{x-2}{x+1}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
D&=\mathbb R\setminus\{-1,1,2\}\\[6pt]
\frac{x+4}{x-1}:\frac{x-2}{x+1}
&=\frac{(x+4)(x+1)}{(x-1)(x-2)}\qquad (x\in D).
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The values $1$ and $-1$ come from the two original denominators. The value $2$ makes the numerator of the divisor zero while its denominator is defined, so it must also be excluded. In the product by the reciprocal, the factor $x+1$ appears in the numerator. That change of position might suggest that $-1$ is admissible. The original divisor shows that its quotient is undefined there. At $x=-4$, the denominators are nonzero and the dividend is zero, so the quotient is zero.

</div>
