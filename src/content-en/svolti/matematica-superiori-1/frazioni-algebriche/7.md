## Exercise 1

For $x\ne-8$, simplify $\dfrac{-x-8}{x+8}$.

$$
\frac{-(x+8)}{x+8}=-1
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The numerator is the additive inverse of the entire denominator. Factor minus one out of the numerator. The common nonzero factor cancels, leaving a negative sign.

The result is $-1$.

The numerator is $-(x+8)$. Cancel the factor $x+8$ within the domain to leave $-1$. At $x=0$, direct substitution gives $\dfrac{-8}{8}=-1$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

For $x\ne -9$, calculate $\dfrac{x^2-81}{(x+9)^2}$.

$$
\frac{(x-9)(x+9)}{(x+9)^2}=\frac{x-9}{x+9}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Factor the numerator into a sum and a difference. The denominator contains two copies of the sum factor. Cancel only one common copy and keep the remaining factor.

The result is $\dfrac{x - 9}{x + 9}$.

The numerator has one copy of the factor $x+9$. The denominator has two, so one remains after cancellation. At $x=9$, the new numerator is zero and the denominator is $18$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

For $x\ne 10$, simplify $\dfrac{x^2-100}{x-10}$.

$$
\frac{(x-10)(x+10)}{x-10}=x+10
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Factor the numerator as a difference of squares. The common factor can be canceled because it is nonzero on the stated domain. The original condition still applies.

The result is $x + 10$.

The numerator factors as $(x-10)(x+10)$. The second factor remains after cancellation. The value $x=-10$ is allowed and gives zero, while $x=10$ is still excluded.

</div>
