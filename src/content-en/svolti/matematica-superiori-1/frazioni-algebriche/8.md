## Exercise 1

For $x\ne8$, rewrite $1+\dfrac{8}{x-8}$ as a single fraction.

$$
1+\frac{8}{x-8}=\frac{x-8+8}{x-8}=\frac x{x-8}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Write one as the denominator divided by itself. Add the two numerators. Minus $8$ and plus $8$ cancel.

The result is $\dfrac{x}{x - 8}$.

Rewrite the number $1$ as $\dfrac{x-8}{x-8}$. Adding the second numerator $8$ gives $x-8+8=x$. The denominator remains $x-8$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

For $x\ne9$, calculate $\dfrac{2x}{x-9}-2$.

$$
\frac{2x-2(x-9)}{x-9}=\frac{18}{x-9}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Rewrite two using the denominator of the first fraction. Then subtract the entire new numerator. The terms containing x cancel.

The result is $\dfrac{18}{x - 9}$.

The term being subtracted becomes $\dfrac{2(x-9)}{x-9}$. The minus sign also applies to its constant term $-18$. The result is therefore $\dfrac{18}{x-9}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

For $x\ne 10$ and $x\ne -10$, calculate $\dfrac{x-10}{x+10}:\dfrac{2(x-10)}{x+10}$.

$$
\frac{x-10}{x+10}\cdot\frac{x+10}{2(x-10)}=\frac12
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The divisor is defined and nonzero on the specified domain. Replace division by multiplication by the reciprocal of the divisor. The common factors cancel, leaving the ratio of the coefficients.

The result is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.

The divisor has denominator $x+10$ and numerator $2(x-10)$. The given conditions allow us both to take its reciprocal and to cancel the two common factors in the product. The ratio of the coefficients $\dfrac12$ remains.

</div>
