## Exercise 1

Triangles $ABC$ and $DEF$ are congruent with correspondence $A\leftrightarrow D$, $B\leftrightarrow E$, and $C\leftrightarrow F$. If $BC=13$ cm and $AB=10$ cm, what is the length of $EF$?

$$
EF=BC=13
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The vertex correspondence determines the side correspondence. Endpoints B and C match E and F, respectively. Side EF is therefore congruent to BC.

The result is $13\,\mathrm{cm}$.

The $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ measurement is for $AB$. It corresponds to $DE$ because $A$ matches $D$ and $B$ matches $E$. The question asks for $EF$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In triangle $ABC$, it is given that $AB=AC=13$ cm. The goal is to prove $\widehat B=\widehat C$. What is the conclusion?

- $AB=AC=13\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $AB=BC$
- $\widehat A=90^\circ$
- $\widehat B=\widehat C$

<div class="spiegazione">

The hypothesis is the given information, in this case the equality of the two sides. The conclusion is the statement to be proved. Here the conclusion is that the angles opposite the congruent sides are equal.

The result is $\widehat B=\widehat C$.

The common side length belongs to the hypotheses. In a proof diagram, the bisector of the angle at $A$ can be introduced to derive the required equality from congruent triangles.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The segments $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at $O$. It is given that $AO=OC$ and $BO=OD$. Prove that $AB=CD$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\widehat{AOB}&=\widehat{COD} \\[6pt]
\triangle AOB&\cong\triangle COD \\[6pt]
AB&=CD
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The angles $\widehat{AOB}$ and $\widehat{COD}$ are vertical angles, so they are congruent. Each is the included angle between the two sides known to match. Apply SAS to triangles $AOB$ and $COD$, with $A$ corresponding to $C$ and $B$ to $D$. It follows that $AB$ and $CD$ have equal lengths.

</div>
