## Exercise 1

In triangles $ABC$ and $DEF$, $AB=DF=6$ cm, $BC=FE=4$ cm, and $\widehat{ABC}=\widehat{DFE}=48^\circ$. Which criterion applies, and what side matches $AC$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
B&\leftrightarrow F,\qquad A\leftrightarrow D,\qquad C\leftrightarrow E,\\[6pt]
ABC&\cong DFE,\\[6pt]
AC&=DE.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two given sides meet at $B$ in the first triangle and at $F$ in the second. The angles at those vertices are congruent and included between the given sides, so SAS applies. Starting with $B\leftrightarrow F$, the side of length $6$ cm matches $A$ with $D$. The side of length $4$ cm matches $C$ with $E$. The data determine the vertex order, which gives $AC=DE$.

An angle at $A$ or $D$ would adjoin only one of the given sides of its triangle.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In triangles $ABC$ and $PQR$, $AC=QP$, $\widehat A=\widehat Q=40^\circ$, and $\widehat C=\widehat P=56^\circ$. Establish congruence and find the angles at $B$ and $R$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
A&\leftrightarrow Q,\qquad C\leftrightarrow P,\\[6pt]
ABC&\cong QRP,\\[6pt]
\widehat B=\widehat R&=180^\circ-40^\circ-56^\circ=84^\circ.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The given angles are at the ends of congruent sides $AC$ and $QP$. Their different measures determine how those endpoints match. ASA gives congruence, and the remaining vertex $B$ therefore matches $R$. To find the missing angle, use the interior angle sum in either triangle. The result is positive and completes the given measures in both drawings.

The interior sum gives the third angle without the side length, while congruence still uses the matching side.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The sides of $ABC$ are $AB=5$, $BC=7$, and $AC=8$. In $PQR$, $PQ=7$, $QR=8$, and $RP=5$. Write the congruence in the right order and identify the angle matching $\widehat{BAC}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
AB&=RP,\quad BC=PQ,\quad AC=RQ,\\[6pt]
ABC&\cong RPQ,\\[6pt]
\widehat{BAC}&=\widehat{PRQ}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

SSS gives the correspondence from the equal side lengths. In $ABC$, vertex $B$ is shared by the sides of lengths $5$ and $7$. In $PQR$ those sides meet at $P$. That leaves $A\leftrightarrow R$ and $C\leftrightarrow Q$. Replacing each letter in $\widehat{BAC}$ with its match gives $\widehat{PRQ}$, with $R$ in the centre. That is the vertex where the sides of lengths $5$ and $8$ meet.

Also check the side opposite the middle letter. It has length $8$ in both triangles.

</div>
