## Exercise 1

The complement of an angle measures three times the angle. Find the angle's measure and the measure of its supplement.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x+3x&=90^\circ,& x&=\dfrac{45^\circ}{2},\\[6pt]
180^\circ-x&=\dfrac{315^\circ}{2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The given angle is one of the four equal parts in the one-to-three split of a right angle. It therefore measures $\dfrac{45^\circ}{2}$, and its complement takes three of those parts, or $\dfrac{135^\circ}{2}$. Use a straight angle to find the supplement. As a check, the supplement exceeds the complement by $90^\circ$, because the same angle is subtracted from $180^\circ$ and from $90^\circ$.

The computed supplement exceeds a right angle, as it does whenever the original angle is acute.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Two intersecting lines form vertical angles with measures $(3x+12)^\circ$ and $(5x-30)^\circ$. Find all four angle measures.

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x+12&=5x-30,\\[6pt]
2x&=42,\qquad x=21,\\[6pt]
\alpha=\gamma&=75^\circ,\qquad \beta=\delta=105^\circ.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Vertical angles have the same measure, so the two formulas describing them must agree. The number $x$ is an input for both expressions. Indeed, $3\cdot21+12=75$ and $5\cdot21-30=75$. Each adjacent angle adds to $180^\circ$ with one of the vertical angles, so it measures $105^\circ$. The four measures add to $360^\circ$ around the point of intersection, as required.

The vertical-angle condition gives an equality. A sum of one straight angle would describe a linear pair instead.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Rays $OA,OC,OD,OB$ occur in that order within straight angle $\widehat{AOB}$. Given $\widehat{AOC}=112^\circ$ and $\widehat{DOB}=36^\circ$, what is the measure of each half of $\widehat{COD}$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
\widehat{COD}&=180^\circ-112^\circ-36^\circ=32^\circ,\\[6pt]
\dfrac{\widehat{COD}}{2}&=16^\circ.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The order of the rays ensures that the three sectors do not overlap inside the straight angle. The required angle is the sector left after removing those adjoining the ends of the straight angle. Halve the result of that subtraction. If $OE$ is the interior bisector, adding $16^\circ$ to $112^\circ$ gives $\widehat{AOE}=128^\circ$. The other side of the same ray contains $16^\circ+36^\circ=52^\circ$, which completes the straight angle.

Reading the rays in order also shows on which side of the sector each given measure lies.

</div>
