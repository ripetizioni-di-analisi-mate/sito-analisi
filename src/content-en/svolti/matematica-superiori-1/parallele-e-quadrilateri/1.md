## Exercise 1

Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One angle measures $58^\circ$. What is the measure of its corresponding angle on the other line?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 xmin=-1.5 xmax=5.5 ymin=-0.7 ymax=3.2 linee="-1,0 5,0;-1,2 5,2;-0.31,-0.5 1.88,3" punti="0,0;1.2497,2" etichette="0.75,0.30,α;2,2.3,β" titolo="Angles α and β are corresponding angles"}

$$
\beta=58^\circ
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Corresponding angles are congruent when a transversal crosses parallel lines. The angles have the same relative position at the two intersections. Their measures therefore agree.

The result is $58^\circ$.

An angle in the same position therefore measures $58^\circ$ at the other intersection too. Its adjacent angle on the same line measures $122^\circ$ and forms a different pair.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. An interior angle measures $64^\circ$. What is the measure of its alternate interior angle?

$$
\beta=64^\circ
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Alternate interior angles lie between the lines and on opposite sides of the transversal. When the lines are parallel, these angles are congruent. The unknown measure therefore equals the known measure.

The result is $64^\circ$.

The interior region lies between the parallel lines. Of the two interior angles at the other intersection, take the one on the opposite side of the transversal. The one on the same side would be supplementary and would measure $116^\circ$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. An interior angle measures $89^\circ$. What is the measure of its same-side interior angle?

$$
\beta=180^\circ-89^\circ=91^\circ
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Same-side interior angles are supplementary when the lines are parallel. Their measures sum to one hundred eighty degrees. Subtract the known angle from this total.

The result is $91^\circ$.

The required angle is on the same side of the transversal as the given angle. Indeed, $89^\circ+91^\circ=180^\circ$. The pair contains one acute angle and one obtuse angle.

</div>
