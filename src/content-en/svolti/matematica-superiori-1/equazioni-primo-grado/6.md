## Exercise 1

Find $x$ in the proportion $x:4=8:2$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{x}{4}&=\frac{8}{2}=4 \\[6pt]
x&=4\cdot 4=16
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the ratio on the right, $8$ is four times $2$. The proportion requires the same quotient on the left, so $x$ is four times $4$. The value $16$ satisfies the equality because $\dfrac{16}{4}=\dfrac82$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve the formula $s=3t+9$ for $t$, then find its value when $s=27$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
s-9 &= 3t \\[6pt]
t &= \frac{s-9}{3} \\[6pt]
t &= \frac{27-9}{3}=6
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The constant term is $9$. Subtracting it from both sides gives the product $3t$. Division by $3$ is possible for every $s$ and gives the rearranged formula. Substituting $27$ gives $t=6$, which makes $3\cdot6+9=27$ in the original formula.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve $P=2b+2h$ for $h$. Then find $h$ when $P=34\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $b=11\,\mathrm{cm}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
P-2b&=2h \\[6pt]
h&=\frac{P-2b}{2}=\frac P2-b \\[6pt]
h&=(17-11)\,\mathrm{cm}=6\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The perimeter includes two copies of the base and two copies of the height. Subtracting the contribution of both bases and then dividing by $2$ isolates the height. The form $\dfrac P2-b$ amounts to subtracting the base from half the perimeter. The given data leave a height of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$.

</div>
