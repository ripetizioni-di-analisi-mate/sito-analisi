## Exercise 1

Solve $3x+8=32$ and explain which changes preserve the equality.

::grafico{xmin=-1 xmax=34 ymin=-0.25 ymax=2.8 assi=0 poligoni="0,1.3 24,1.3 24,1.8 0,1.8;24,1.3 32,1.3 32,1.8 24,1.8;0,0.19 24,0.19 24,0.73 0,0.73" linee="0,1.3 32,1.3 32,1.8 0,1.8 0,1.3;24,1.3 24,1.8;0,0.19 24,0.19 24,0.73 0,0.73 0,0.19;8,0.19 8,0.73;16,0.19 16,0.73" etichette="11,2.2,3x;27.4,2.2,8;3.7,0.94,x;11.7,0.94,x;19.7,0.94,x;31,1,32;23,-0.09,24" titolo="Above, 3x + 8 equals 32. Below, the three x terms remain and total 24"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
         3x+8-8&=32-8\\[6pt]
         3x&=24\\[6pt]
         x&=8.
         \end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The part to isolate is the product $3x$. Subtracting $8$ from both sides removes the constant and preserves equality. The diagram compares the two levels before and after removing the same amount. There are $24$ units left, divided into three equal parts, so each is worth $8$. Reverse the calculation by multiplying by three and adding eight. The check in the original expression is $3\cdot8+8=32$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve $5x-9=2x+18$. What does the point of intersection represent?

::grafico{f="5*x-9;2*x+18" xmin=-0.7 xmax=12.6 ymin=-5.1 ymax=57 punti="9,36,pieno,P" titolo="The lines for the two sides intersect at (9, 36)"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
         5x-9&=2x+18\\[6pt]
         3x&=27\\[6pt]
         x&=9.
         \end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Subtract $2x$ from both sides so that the difference between the terms in $x$ is $3x$. Adding $9$ gives $3x=27$. The solution is $9$, and the original sides equal $5\cdot9-9=36$ and $2\cdot9+18=36$. The lines therefore intersect at $(9,36)$. The horizontal coordinate answers the question about $x$. The vertical coordinate records the common value. Their different slopes mean there is exactly one intersection.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve $-2x+10=30$. Predict the sign of the solution before dividing.

$$
\begin{aligned}
         -2x&=30-10=20\\[6pt]
         x&=\frac{20}{-2}=-10.
         \end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Subtracting $10$ from both sides leaves $-2x=20$. The product must be positive and one factor is $-2$, so the other factor is negative. Division by $-2$ confirms the prediction. Substituting gives $(-2)(-10)+10=30$. Alternatively, multiply the entire reduced equation by $-1$ to obtain $2x=-20$. This form makes it clear that a positive value of $x$ is incompatible with the given equation.

</div>
