## Exercise 1

Solve $3x+8=32$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x &= 32-8 = 24 \\[6pt]
x &= \frac{24}{3}=8
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

On the left, the multiple $3x$ is added to the constant term $8$. Subtracting $8$ from both sides preserves the equality and gives $3x=24$. Since $3\ne0$, dividing both sides by $3$ preserves the solution. On the original left-hand side, $3\cdot8+8=32$, so $8$ is the required value.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve $5x-9=2x+18$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
5x-2x-9 &= 18 \\[6pt]
3x &= 18+9=27 \\[6pt]
x &= \frac{27}{3}=9
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The unknown appears on both sides. Subtracting $2x$ from both sides leaves $3x$ on the left. Adding $9$ to both sides removes the term $-9$, and dividing by $3$ isolates the unknown. At $9$, the left-hand side is $45-9=36$ and the right-hand side is $18+18=36$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve $-2x+10=30$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
-2x &= 30-10=20 \\[6pt]
x &= \frac{20}{-2}=-10
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Subtracting $10$ from both sides removes the term $+10$. The product of $-2$ and the unknown remains. The quotient must be negative for the product to equal $20$. In the original equation, $(-2)(-10)+10=20+10=30$.

</div>
