## Exercise 1

A mother is now three times as old as her son. In $8$ years she will be twice his age. How old is her son now?

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x+8 &= 2(x+8) \\[6pt]
3x+8 &= 2x+16 \\[6pt]
x &= 8
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $x$ be the son's current age, so the mother's current age is $3x$. After $8$ years, both will be $8$ years older. Twice the son's age refers to twice his entire future age $x+8$. Their ages today are $8$ and $24$. In $8$ years they will be $16$ and $32$, as required.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Admission costs $5$ euros and each activity costs $3$ euros. A visitor pays $59$ euros in total. How many activities does the visitor buy?

$$
\begin{aligned}
5+3x &= 59 \\[6pt]
3x &= 59-5=54 \\[6pt]
x &= \frac{54}{3}=18
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The admission fee is paid once. The term $3x$ represents the total cost of $x$ activities. Subtracting admission leaves $54$ euros, the cost of $18$ activities at $3$ euros each. Indeed, $5+3\cdot18=59$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A rectangle has perimeter $46\,\mathrm{cm}$. Its base is $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ longer than its height. Find its side lengths.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2(h+5)+2h &= 46 \\[6pt]
4h+10 &=46 \\[6pt]
h &=\frac{46-10}{4}=9 \\[6pt]
b &= 9+5=14
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $h$ be the numerical value of the height in centimeters. The base is then $h+5$. The perimeter includes two sides of each length, so the extra $5$ adds $10$ centimeters to the perimeter. The height is $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the base is $14\,\mathrm{cm}$. They differ by $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the perimeter is $2(9+14)\,\mathrm{cm}=46\,\mathrm{cm}$.

</div>
