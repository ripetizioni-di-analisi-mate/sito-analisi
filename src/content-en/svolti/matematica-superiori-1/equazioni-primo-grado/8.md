## Exercise 1

Check that $x=8$ solves $5x-8=2x+16$ and determine whether it is unique.

$$
\begin{aligned}
5\cdot8-8 &= 32 \\[6pt]
2\cdot8+16 &= 32 \\[6pt]
5x-8=2x+16 &\iff 3x=24
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Substituting $8$ verifies the equality. To determine whether there are other solutions, subtract $2x$ and add $8$ on both sides. The equivalent equation is $3x=24$ and the coefficient $3$ is nonzero. Division gives a single value, so the checked solution is unique.

</div>

## Exercise 2

For which values of $x$ do the two sides of $4x-6=x+3$ differ by $3$, with the left-hand side greater?

$$
\begin{aligned}
(4x-6)-(x+3)&=3 \\[6pt]
3x-9&=3 \\[6pt]
x&=4
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The required difference uses the left-hand side as the quantity from which to subtract. Subtract the whole expression $x+3$ from $4x-6$. The new right-hand side is $3$ because the question requires a positive difference of $3$. At $x=4$, the two sides are $10$ and $7$, so the condition is satisfied.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Classify the equation $(k-4)x=2k-8$ as the real parameter $k$ varies.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(k-4)x&=2(k-4) \\[6pt]
(k-4)(x-2)&=0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The coefficient $k-4$ is zero only when $k=4$. In that case the original equation becomes $0x=0$, and every real number is a solution. When $k\ne4$, dividing by $k-4$ gives $x=2$. Substituting $2$ also gives $2k-8$ on the left-hand side.

</div>
