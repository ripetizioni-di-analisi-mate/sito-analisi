## Exercise 1

What is the mode of the data $8, 9, 9, 9, 11, 11$?

<div class="spiegazione">

The value $8$ occurs once, $9$ occurs three times, and $11$ occurs twice. Therefore the largest frequency is $3$ and corresponds to $9$. The mode is the value with the largest frequency. In this distribution, it is $9$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

What is the range of the data $10, 17, 11, 13$?

$$
R=17-10=7
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The minimum is $10$ and the maximum is $17$. The range is the distance between them, $17-10=7$. The intermediate values $11$ and $13$ are already inside this interval. Replacing them with other values inside the interval would leave the range unchanged.

</div>

## Exercise 3

What is the median of the ordered data $10, 10, 11, 11, 30$, which include a value much larger than the others?

$$
\operatorname{median}=11
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The third of five ordered values is the middle one, so the median is $11$. In this example the maximum is $30$. The maximum makes the range $30-10=20$. Because it is last in the list, it leaves the third value unchanged. The two measures describe different aspects of the data.

</div>
