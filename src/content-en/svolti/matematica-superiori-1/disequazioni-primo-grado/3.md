## Exercise 1

Find the interval where both $x>8$ and $x\le12$ hold.

::grafico{assi=0 xmin=6 xmax=14 ymin=-0.7 ymax=0.8 linee="6.2,0 13.5,0" vettori="13.4,0,13.8,0" area="0.07;-0.07;8;12" punti="8,0,vuoto;12,0,pieno" etichette="8,-0.32,8;12,-0.32,12;13.65,-0.32,x" titolo="From 8 excluded to 12 included"}

$$
(8,+\infty)\cap(-\infty,12]=(8,12]
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The intersection contains the numbers that satisfy both inequalities. The first condition excludes $8$. The value $12$ satisfies both, so it is included. The rest of the interval consists of all numbers between $8$ and $12$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve $19\le2x+1<23$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\quad18&\le2x<22 \\[6pt]
9&\le x<11
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The middle expression must satisfy both comparisons. Subtract $1$ from all three expressions and divide each by the positive number $2$. The solution is $[9,11)$. Indeed, $x=9$ gives $2x+1=19$, while $x=11$ gives $23$, which the upper bound excludes.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Find the common solutions of $2x+1\le7$ and $5-x<2$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x+1\le7&\iff x\le3 \\[6pt]
5-x<2&\iff x>3 \\[6pt]
(-\infty,3]\cap(3,+\infty)&=\varnothing
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first inequality allows $3$ and every smaller value. The second allows only values strictly greater than $3$ because division by $-1$ reverses the sign. The two rays have the same boundary, but no point belongs to both.

</div>
