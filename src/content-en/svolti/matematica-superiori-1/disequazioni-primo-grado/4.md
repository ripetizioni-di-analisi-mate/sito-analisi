## Exercise 1

How many integers satisfy $8<x\le14$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
\{x\in\mathbb Z:8<x\le14\}&=\{9,10,11,12,13,14\} \\[6pt]
14-9+1&=6
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first admissible integer is $9$ because $8$ is excluded. The largest is $14$, which satisfies the second comparison by equality. The difference $14-9$ counts five steps between integers. Adding one includes the starting number.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A buyer has $29$ euros. Each ticket costs $3$ euros. What is the greatest number of tickets the buyer can purchase?

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x&\le29 \\[6pt]
x&\le\frac{29}{3}=9+\frac23 \\[6pt]
x_{\max}&=9
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The cost of $x$ tickets is $3x$. The number $x$ must be a nonnegative integer, so choose the largest integer that satisfies the bound. Nine tickets cost $27$ euros and leave $2$ euros. Ten cost $30$ euros and exceed the budget.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A pack contains $4$ pencils. How many packs are needed to have at least $41$ pencils?

$$
\begin{aligned}
4x&\ge41 \\[6pt]
x&\ge\frac{41}{4}=10+\frac14 \\[6pt]
x_{\min}&=11
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Ten packs give $40$ pencils, one short of the target. Eleven packs contain $44$ pencils and meet the requirement. The problem asks for a minimum above a threshold, so select the first integer that reaches or exceeds the real bound.

</div>
