## Exercise 1

Solve $2x+9>2x+10$.

$$
2x+9>2x+10\ \iff\ 9>10
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Subtracting $2x$ from both sides leaves a numerical comparison that is the same for every $x$. Since $9>10$ is false, the solution is $\varnothing$. In fact, the original right-hand side exceeds the left-hand side by $1$ for every real number.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve $3x-10\le3x-9$.

$$
3x-10\le3x-9\ \iff\ -10\le-9
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The expression $3x$ appears on both sides and can be subtracted without changing the inequality. Since $-10$ is less than $-9$, the resulting comparison is always true. The solution set is therefore $\mathbb R$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Find the real values of $a$ for which $2x+a\le2x+4$ is true for every $x$.

$$
2x+a\le2x+4\ \iff\ a\le4
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Here $a$ is the parameter. Subtracting $2x$ gives a condition that depends only on $a$. When $a\le4$, every $x$ satisfies the inequality, including the case $a=4$, which gives equality. When $a>4$, the inequality has no solutions.

</div>
