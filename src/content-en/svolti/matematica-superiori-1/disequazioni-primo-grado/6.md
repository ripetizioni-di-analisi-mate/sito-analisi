## Exercise 1

Find the real numbers satisfying at least one of $x<-8$ and $x>8$. Write the result as a complement as well.

$$
\begin{aligned}
S&=(-\infty,-8)\cup(8,+\infty),\\[6pt]
&=\mathbb R\setminus[-8,8].
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two conditions allow values to the left of $-8$ and to the right of $8$, respectively. Either condition is enough, so take the union of the half-lines. Both comparisons are strict, so $-8$ and $8$ are excluded along with the values between them. The excluded set is $[-8,8]$, which can be removed from $\mathbb R$ to describe the same solution. For example, $-9$ and $9$ are included, while $0$ satisfies neither condition.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Find the union of $[-2,4)$ and $(1,6]$. Determine which intervals contain $1$ and $4$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=[-2,4),\qquad B=(1,6],\\[6pt]
A\cap B&=(1,4),\\[6pt]
A\cup B&=[-2,6],\\[6pt]
1&\in A,\quad 1\notin B,\quad 4\notin A,\quad 4\in B.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The intervals overlap between $1$ and $4$, with both endpoints excluded from the overlap. For the union each value needs to belong to one interval. The number $1$ is in the first and $4$ is in the second, so both remain. Of the outer endpoints, $-2$ belongs to the first interval and $6$ to the second. Since the overlap leaves no gap, the union fills the entire stretch from $-2$ to $6$, including both endpoints.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Find the part shared by the condition $x\le-2$ or $x\ge4$ and the interval $-3<x<5$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(-\infty,-2]\cap(-3,5)&=(-3,-2],\\[6pt]
[4,+\infty)\cap(-3,5)&=[4,5),\\[6pt]
S&=(-3,-2]\cup[4,5).
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Apply the constraint $-3<x<5$ to each half-line separately. On the left, keep values above $-3$ up to and including $-2$. On the right, keep values from $4$ inclusive to $5$ exclusive. Both inner endpoints satisfy the two constraints and are included. The second constraint excludes the outer endpoints. The result still has two pieces because, for example, $0$ meets the second constraint but meets neither of the alternatives.

</div>
