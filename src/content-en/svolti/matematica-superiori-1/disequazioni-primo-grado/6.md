## Exercise 1

Find the real numbers that satisfy at least one of $x<-8$ and $x>8$.

$$
(-\infty,-8)\cup(8,+\infty)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first condition selects numbers to the left of $-8$. The second selects numbers to the right of $8$. Belonging to either ray is sufficient, so take their union. The number $0$ is excluded, and the endpoints are open.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Find the union of the intervals $[-2,4)$ and $(1,6]$.

$$
[-2,4)\cup(1,6]=[-2,6]
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The intervals overlap on $(1,4)$. Their union therefore covers every number between $-2$ and $6$. The first interval already contains $1$, and the second contains $4$. Each outer endpoint belongs to its respective interval.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Find the common solutions of the condition $x\le-2$ or $x\ge4$ and the condition $-3<x<5$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bigl((-\infty,-2]\cup[4,+\infty)\bigr)&\cap(-3,5) \\[6pt]
&=(-3,-2]\cup[4,5)
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Restrict each ray to the interval $(-3,5)$. The left ray keeps values greater than $-3$ and at most $-2$. The right ray keeps values from $4$ included up to $5$ excluded. The two pieces remain separate.

</div>
