## Exercise 1

Solve $2x+8>24$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x&>24-8=16 \\[6pt]
x&>8
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Subtract $8$ from both sides. Dividing by $2$ preserves the inequality sign because $2$ is positive. The solution is $(8,+\infty)$. At $x=8$, the left-hand side is $24$, so the strict comparison excludes $8$. At $x=9$, it gives $26>24$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve $5x-9\ge2x+18$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
5x-2x&\ge18+9 \\[6pt]
3x&\ge27 \\[6pt]
x&\ge9
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Subtract $2x$ from both sides to collect the terms containing the unknown. Then add $9$ to isolate $3x$. Dividing by $3$ gives $[9,+\infty)$. At $x=9$, both original sides equal $36$. The symbol $\ge$ includes equality.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve $-3x\le30$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(-3)x&\le30 \\[6pt]
x&\ge\frac{30}{-3}=-10
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Dividing by $-3$ reverses the inequality sign. In fact, as $x$ increases, the product $-3x$ decreases. The value $x=-10$ gives $30$, so it belongs to $[-10,+\infty)$. At $x=-11$, the product is $33$ and the inequality is false.

</div>
