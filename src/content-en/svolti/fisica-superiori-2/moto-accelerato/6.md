## Exercise 1

A vehicle travels at $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and brakes with a constant acceleration of magnitude $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Before braking, it maintains its initial velocity through a reaction time of $1\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the total distance before it stops?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=4.0 ymin=-1 ymax=17 f="15-5*max(0,x-1.0)" punti="1.0,15,pieno,R;4.0,0,pieno,F" nomi="t (s),v (m/s)" titolo="F marks the stop. The graph has negative slope during braking."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_a &= \frac{v_0}{b}=\frac{15}{5}\,\mathrm{s}\simeq 3\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
s_r &= v_0t_r=15\cdot 1\,\mathrm{m}=15\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
s_f &= \frac{15^2}{2\cdot 5}\,\mathrm{m}\simeq 22.5\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
s_{\mathrm{tot}} &= s_r+s_f \simeq 37.5\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Choose the direction of travel as positive. Velocity decreases by $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ each second and reaches zero after $3\,\mathrm{s}$. During the reaction the velocity stays at $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so the graph has a rectangle of area $15\,\mathrm{m}$. Add the braking triangle’s area of $22.5\,\mathrm{m}$. The entire duration includes reaction and braking, while the second phase covers less distance than it would at the initial speed.

</div>

## Exercise 3

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=5.25 ymin=-1 ymax=29 f="27-6*max(0,x-0.75)" punti="0.75,27,pieno,R;5.25,0,pieno,F" nomi="t (s),v (m/s)" titolo="F marks the stop. The graph has negative slope during braking."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_a &= \frac{v_0}{b}=\frac{27}{6}\,\mathrm{s}\simeq 4.5\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]

s_f &= \frac{27^2}{2\cdot 6}\,\mathrm{m}\simeq 60.75\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
s_{\mathrm{tot}} &= s_r+s_f \simeq 81\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
