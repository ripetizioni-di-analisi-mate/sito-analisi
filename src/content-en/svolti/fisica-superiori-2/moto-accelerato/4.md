## Exercise 1

A vehicle travels at $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and brakes with a constant acceleration of magnitude $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. How long does it take to brake to rest?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=6.0 ymin=-1 ymax=20 f="18-3*max(0,x-0.0)" punti="0.0,18,pieno,R;6.0,0,pieno,F" nomi="t (s),v (m/s)" titolo="F marks the stop. The graph has negative slope during braking."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_a &= \frac{v_0}{b}=\frac{18}{3}\,\mathrm{s}\simeq 6\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Choose the direction of travel as positive. Velocity decreases by $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ each second and reaches zero after $6\,\mathrm{s}$. The required time is the first instant at which the velocity reaches zero. The equation describes the braking interval only.

</div>
