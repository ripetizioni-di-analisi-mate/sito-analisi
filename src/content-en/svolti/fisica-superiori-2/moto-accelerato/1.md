## Exercise 1

A cart's velocity changes from $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $3\,\mathrm{s}$. Find its average acceleration.

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=3.0 ymin=-1.0 ymax=9.0 f="2+(2.0)*x" punti="0,2.000000,pieno,I;3.000000,8.000000,pieno,F" nomi="t (s),v (m/s)" titolo="The secant line through the two velocity readings."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta v &= 8-(2)= 6\,\mathrm{m/s} \\[6pt]
a_m &= \frac{6}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\simeq 2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Subtract the initial velocity $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from the final velocity $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The change is $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Divide it by the duration $3\,\mathrm{s}$. The quotient is in metres per second per second. Its sign indicates the direction of change. If the velocity changes linearly, the slope of the line through the two readings is also the acceleration at each instant.

</div>
