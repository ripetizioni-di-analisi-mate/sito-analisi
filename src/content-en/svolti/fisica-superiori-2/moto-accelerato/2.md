## Exercise 1

An object has initial velocity $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and constant acceleration $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the signed velocity after $4\,\mathrm{s}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta v &= (2)\cdot 4 = 8\,\mathrm{m/s} \\[6pt]
v_f &= 3+(8)=11\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The acceleration gives a change of $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ each second. Over $4\,\mathrm{s}$ the total change is $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Adding it to the initial velocity gives $11\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The same velocity is the ordinate at the end of the velocity–time line.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An object has initial velocity $-10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and constant acceleration $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the signed velocity after $2\,\mathrm{s}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta v &= (3)\cdot 2 = 6\,\mathrm{m/s} \\[6pt]
v_f &= -10+(6)=-4\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The acceleration gives a change of $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ each second. Over $2\,\mathrm{s}$ the total change is $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Adding it to the initial velocity gives $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The same velocity is the ordinate at the end of the velocity–time line.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A stopwatch shows $2\,\mathrm s$ when a trolley passes with velocity $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At $5\,\mathrm s$ its velocity is $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Assume constant acceleration from $2$ to $6\,\mathrm s$. Find when the trolley reverses and its velocity at $6\,\mathrm s$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a &= \frac{6-(-3)}{5-2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\\[6pt]
v(t)&=-3+3(t-2)\\[6pt]
v(t)=0&\ \Longrightarrow\ t=3\,\mathrm s\\[6pt]
v(6)&=-3+3(6-2)=9\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two readings determine a straight velocity-time line because the acceleration is constant. Use the first reading as the reference. Times in the question are stopwatch readings, so the elapsed time in the last equation is counted from two seconds.

To find the reversal, locate the zero and check the directions on either side. Before that instant the line has negative values, and afterwards it has positive values. The reversal falls between the two readings. The requested velocity at six seconds still belongs to the interval in which acceleration is constant.

</div>
