## Exercise 1

An object has initial velocity $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and constant acceleration $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the signed velocity after $4\,\mathrm{s}$.

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=4.0 ymin=-1.0 ymax=12.0 f="3+(2)*x" punti="0,3.000000,pieno,I;4.000000,11.000000,pieno,F" nomi="t (s),v (m/s)" titolo="I is the initial velocity, and F the final velocity."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta v &= (2)\cdot 4 = 8\,\mathrm{m/s} \\[6pt]
v_f &= 3+(8)=11\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The acceleration gives a change of $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ each second. Over $4\,\mathrm{s}$ the total change is $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Adding it to the initial velocity gives $11\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The same velocity is the ordinate at the end of the velocity–time line.

</div>
