## Exercise 1

An object is released from rest $20\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground. Neglecting air resistance and taking $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, when does it reach the ground?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=2.0 ymin=-1.0 ymax=21.0 f="20-10*x^2/2" punti="0,20.000000,pieno,I;2.000000,0.000000,pieno,F" nomi="t (s),y (m)" titolo="Height reaches zero at the ground."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
y &= 20-\frac{10}{2}t^2=0 \\[6pt]
t &= \sqrt{\frac{2\cdot 20}{10}}\,\mathrm{s}\simeq 2\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Take height as positive upward. The acceleration is therefore negative, and height starts at $20\,\mathrm{m}$ and then decreases. Impact occurs at zero height. The equation gives two opposite roots; choose the positive one because motion is observed after release.

</div>
