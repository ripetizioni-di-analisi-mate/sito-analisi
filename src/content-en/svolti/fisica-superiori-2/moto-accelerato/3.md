## Exercise 1

A cart has initial velocity $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and constant acceleration $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its signed displacement in $3\,\mathrm{s}$?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=3.0 ymin=-1.0 ymax=9.0 f="2+(2)*x" punti="0,2.000000,pieno,I;3.000000,8.000000,pieno,F" nomi="t (s),v (m/s)" titolo="The signed area between the line and the time axis is displacement."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x &= v_0t+\frac12at^2 \\[6pt]
&= (2)\cdot 3+\frac{(2)\cdot 3^2}{2}\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
&= 15\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The initial-velocity term contributes $6\,\mathrm{m}$. Acceleration changes the velocity linearly, giving the signed area of a triangle of base $3\,\mathrm{s}$. This second contribution is $9\,\mathrm{m}$. Add them for the displacement. As a check, multiply the mean of the two endpoint velocities, $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, by the duration.

</div>
