## Exercise 1

A sledge moves at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $6\,\mathrm{s}$ and then at $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $4\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the total displacement?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=10 ymin=-4 ymax=5 linee="0,3 6,3;6,-2 10,-2" nomi="t (s),v (m/s)" titolo="The rectangle areas measure the displacements on the two legs."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x_1 &= 3\cdot 6 = 18\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
\Delta x_2 &= -2\cdot 4 = -8\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
\Delta x &= (18)+(-8) = 10\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The displacement on the first leg is $18\,\mathrm{m}$ and on the second it is $-8\,\mathrm{m}$. Their signed sum is $10\,\mathrm{m}$. The distance is the sum of the magnitudes, giving $26\,\mathrm{m}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A sledge moves at $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $4\,\mathrm{s}$ and then at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $9\,\mathrm{s}$. How far does it travel in total?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=13 ymin=-5 ymax=4 linee="0,-3 4,-3;4,2 13,2" nomi="t (s),v (m/s)" titolo="The rectangle areas measure the displacements on the two legs."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x_1 &= -3\cdot 4 = -12\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
\Delta x_2 &= 2\cdot 9 = 18\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
d &= \lvert -12\rvert + \lvert 18\rvert = 30\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The displacement on the first leg is $-12\,\mathrm{m}$ and on the second it is $18\,\mathrm{m}$. Their signed sum is $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The distance is the sum of the magnitudes, giving $30\,\mathrm{m}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A sledge moves at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $3\,\mathrm{s}$ and then at $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $6\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the signed average velocity?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=9 ymin=-7 ymax=6 linee="0,4 3,4;3,-5 9,-5" nomi="t (s),v (m/s)" titolo="The rectangle areas measure the displacements on the two legs."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x_1 &= 4\cdot 3 = 12\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
\Delta x_2 &= -5\cdot 6 = -30\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
\Delta x &= (12)+(-30) = -18\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
v_m &= \frac{-18}{9}\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq -2\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The displacement on the first leg is $12\,\mathrm{m}$ and on the second it is $-30\,\mathrm{m}$. Their signed sum is $-18\,\mathrm{m}$. The distance is the sum of the magnitudes, giving $42\,\mathrm{m}$. For the required average, divide the displacement by $9\,\mathrm{s}$, the duration of the whole interval.

</div>
