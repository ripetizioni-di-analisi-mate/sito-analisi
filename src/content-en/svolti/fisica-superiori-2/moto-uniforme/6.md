## Exercise 1

Two cyclists travel in the same direction at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The slower cyclist is $36\,\mathrm{m}$ ahead of the faster one. How long until they meet?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=14.4 ymin=-38 ymax=66.8 f="0+4*x;-36+7*x" punti="12.000000,48.000000,pieno,I" legenda="A;B" nomi="t (s),x (m)" titolo="At point I the two coordinates agree."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_I &= \frac{36}{7-4}\,\mathrm{s} \simeq 12\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The initial gap is $36\,\mathrm{m}$ and shrinks by $3\,\mathrm{m}$ each second because both cyclists move in the same direction. Their relative speed is therefore the difference of their speeds. At the meeting the first cyclist's lead is zero.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Two cyclists travel in the same direction at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The slower cyclist is $28\,\mathrm{m}$ ahead of the faster one.The first mentioned starts from coordinate $31\,\mathrm{m}$ in the positive direction. What is the meeting coordinate?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=8.4 ymin=1 ymax=63.8 f="31+3*x;3+7*x" punti="7.000000,52.000000,pieno,I" legenda="A;B" nomi="t (s),x (m)" titolo="At point I the two coordinates agree."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_I &= \frac{28}{7-3}\,\mathrm{s} \simeq 7\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
x_I &= 31+3\cdot\frac{28}{4} = 52\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The initial gap is $28\,\mathrm{m}$ and shrinks by $4\,\mathrm{m}$ each second because both cyclists move in the same direction. Their relative speed is therefore the difference of their speeds. At the meeting the first cyclist's lead is zero. The first object travels $21\,\mathrm{m}$, so add that distance to its initial position. Its endpoint is $52\,\mathrm{m}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Two cyclists start from the same point on a straight line in the same direction. The first leaves at time zero at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The second leaves $6\,\mathrm{s}$ later at $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What does the common clock read when they meet again?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=15 ymin=0 ymax=68 f="4*x;7*max(0,x-6)" punti="6,0,pieno,B;14,56,pieno,I" legenda="A;B" nomi="t (s),x (m)" titolo="The intersection is at 14 s, after the second cyclist sets off."}

$$
\begin{aligned}d_0&=4\cdot6\,\mathrm{m}=24\,\mathrm{m}\\[6pt]\Delta t&=\frac{24}{7-4}\,\mathrm{s}=8\,\mathrm{s}\\[6pt]t&=6\,\mathrm{s}+8\,\mathrm{s}=14\,\mathrm{s}.\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

When the second cyclist starts, the first has covered 24 m. The second gains 3 m each second because the speeds differ by 3 m/s. It therefore takes 8 s to close the gap after the second cyclist starts. The common clock includes the initial six seconds too. At the calculated time both are 56 m from the starting point.

</div>
