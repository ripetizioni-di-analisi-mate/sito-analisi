## Exercise 1

Two objects $140\,\mathrm{m}$ apart set off towards each other at speeds $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How long until they meet?

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_I &= \frac{140}{4+3}\,\mathrm{s} = 20\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first object closes the gap by $4\,\mathrm{m}$ per second and the second closes it by a further $3\,\mathrm{m}$ per second. Add the speeds because the objects move towards each other. At the calculated time the gap is zero.

Take the first starting point as the origin. At the calculated time the first body reaches $4\cdot20=80\,\mathrm m$. The second starts at $140\,\mathrm m$ and moves towards smaller coordinates, reaching $140-3\cdot20=80\,\mathrm m$. Agreement between the two position equations also checks the directions.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Two objects $42\,\mathrm{m}$ apart set off towards each other at speeds $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.The first mentioned starts from coordinate $-6\,\mathrm{m}$ in the positive direction. What is the meeting coordinate?

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_I &= \frac{42}{4+2}\,\mathrm{s} = 7\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
x_I &= -6+4\cdot\frac{42}{6} = 22\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first object closes the gap by $4\,\mathrm{m}$ per second and the second closes it by a further $2\,\mathrm{m}$ per second. Add the speeds because the objects move towards each other. At the calculated time the gap is zero. The first object travels $28\,\mathrm{m}$, so add that distance to its initial position. Its endpoint is $22\,\mathrm{m}$.

The second body starts at $-6+42=36\,\mathrm m$. In the seven seconds before meeting, its coordinate decreases by $2\cdot7=14\,\mathrm m$ and also reaches $22\,\mathrm m$. The distances covered add to the initial forty-two metres. The coordinate calculation retains the origin specified in the problem.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Trolley A starts at $x=0$ at $t=0$ with velocity $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Trolley B remains at rest at $x=42\,\mathrm m$ until $t=3\,\mathrm s$, then moves towards A at $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. When and at which coordinate do they meet?

$$
\begin{aligned}
x_A(3\,\mathrm s)&=12\,\mathrm m,\qquad x_B(3\,\mathrm s)=42\,\mathrm m,\\[4pt]
4t&=42-2(t-3)\qquad (t\geq3),\\[4pt]
6t&=48,\qquad t=8\,\mathrm s,\\[4pt]
x_A(8\,\mathrm s)&=4\cdot8\,\mathrm m=32\,\mathrm m.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=10 ymin=0 ymax=46 f="4*x;42-2*max(0,x-3)" punti="3,42,pieno;8,32,pieno,I" segmenti="3,12,3,42" nomi="t (s),x (m)" legenda="A;B" titolo="B stays stationary until 3 s. I marks the positions coinciding at 8 s."}

<div class="spiegazione">

While B waits, A reaches at most twelve metres, so it does not reach B's coordinate in that stage. B's position equation after starting uses the time elapsed since three seconds, while A's uses the full clock time. Equating the positions gives eight seconds, which lies in the second stage. To check, the gap when B starts is $42-12=30\,\mathrm m$. It closes at $4+2=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in five seconds. B moves ten metres back to $32\,\mathrm m$, the same point as A.

</div>
