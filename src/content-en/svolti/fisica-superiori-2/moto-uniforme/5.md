## Exercise 1

Two objects $140\,\mathrm{m}$ apart set off towards each other at speeds $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How long until they meet?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=24.0 ymin=-2 ymax=142 f="0+4*x;140-3*x" punti="20.000000,80.000000,pieno,I" legenda="A;B" nomi="t (s),x (m)" titolo="At point I the two coordinates agree."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_I &= \frac{140}{4+3}\,\mathrm{s} \simeq 20\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first object closes the gap by $4\,\mathrm{m}$ per second and the second closes it by a further $3\,\mathrm{m}$ per second. Add the speeds because the objects move towards each other. At the calculated time the gap is zero.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Two objects $42\,\mathrm{m}$ apart set off towards each other at speeds $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.The first mentioned starts from coordinate $-6\,\mathrm{m}$ in the positive direction. What is the meeting coordinate?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=8.4 ymin=-8 ymax=38 f="-6+4*x;36-2*x" punti="7.000000,22.000000,pieno,I" legenda="A;B" nomi="t (s),x (m)" titolo="At point I the two coordinates agree."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_I &= \frac{42}{4+2}\,\mathrm{s} \simeq 7\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
x_I &= -6+4\cdot\frac{42}{6} = 22\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first object closes the gap by $4\,\mathrm{m}$ per second and the second closes it by a further $2\,\mathrm{m}$ per second. Add the speeds because the objects move towards each other. At the calculated time the gap is zero. The first object travels $28\,\mathrm{m}$, so add that distance to its initial position. Its endpoint is $22\,\mathrm{m}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Two objects $105\,\mathrm{m}$ apart set off towards each other at speeds $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.The first mentioned starts from coordinate $12\,\mathrm{m}$ in the positive direction. What is the meeting coordinate?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=8.4 ymin=10 ymax=119 f="12+10*x;117-5*x" punti="7.000000,82.000000,pieno,I" legenda="A;B" nomi="t (s),x (m)" titolo="At point I the two coordinates agree."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_I &= \frac{105}{10+5}\,\mathrm{s} \simeq 7\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
x_I &= 12+10\cdot\frac{105}{15} = 82\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first object closes the gap by $10\,\mathrm{m}$ per second and the second closes it by a further $5\,\mathrm{m}$ per second. Add the speeds because the objects move towards each other. At the calculated time the gap is zero. The first object travels $70\,\mathrm{m}$, so add that distance to its initial position. Its endpoint is $82\,\mathrm{m}$.

</div>
