## Exercise 1

An object of mass $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ is where the acceleration due to gravity is $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the magnitude of its weight?

$$
\begin{aligned}
P &= mg=4\cdot 10\,\mathrm{N} = 40\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Weight is the gravitational force on the object. If it were the only force, the object would accelerate with magnitude $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Apply the second law to that situation to express weight as mass times gravitational acceleration. The value $40\,\mathrm{N}$ is in newtons, while the mass remains $4\,\mathrm{kg}$. The direction is vertically down. Changing location could change weight while mass stays the same.

</div>
