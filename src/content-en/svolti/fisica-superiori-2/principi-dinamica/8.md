## Exercise 1

A block initially at rest is pushed with $3\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right. Maximum static friction is $8\,\mathrm{N}$ and the block stays at rest. What is the actual friction magnitude?

::grafico{xmin=-3.2 xmax=3.3 ymin=-3.05 ymax=3.05 linee="-3,-0.56 3,-0.56" vettori="0,0,2.5000,0.0000,F₁;0,0,-2.5000,0.0000,f" uguale=1 assi=0 nomi="x,y" titolo="The horizontal net force is zero."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_{Rx}&=3-0-f_s=0 \\[6pt]
f_s&=3\,\mathrm{N}<8\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

At rest the horizontal net force is zero. The known pushes add with sign to $3\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right. The contact must supply exactly that magnitude to the left. Since $3\,\mathrm{N}$ is below the limit $8\,\mathrm{N}$, rest is consistent. The limit specifies the contact's capacity. Force balance selects the actual value.

</div>
