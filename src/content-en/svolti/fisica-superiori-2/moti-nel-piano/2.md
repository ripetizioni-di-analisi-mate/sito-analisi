## Exercise 1

A person walks $6\,\mathrm{m}$ east and then $8\,\mathrm{m}$ north. What is the magnitude of the resultant displacement?

::grafico{xmin=-2 xmax=8 ymin=-2 ymax=10 vettori="0,0,6,0,X;6,0,6,8,Y;0,0,6,8,R" uguale=1 nomi="x (m),y (m)" titolo="The legs give the signed components. Arrow R is the resultant vector."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\lvert\Delta\vec r\rvert &= \sqrt{(6)^2+(8)^2}\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 10\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The east–west and north–south displacements are perpendicular. Take east and north as positive and place the two legs tip to tail. The resultant displacement joins the first point to the last. Its length by Pythagoras is $10\,\mathrm{m}$. The distance travelled along the two legs is $14\,\mathrm{m}$. The quantities answer different questions about the route.

</div>

## Exercise 3
