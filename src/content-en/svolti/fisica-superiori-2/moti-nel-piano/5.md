## Exercise 1

A wheel makes 12 revolutions in $4\,\mathrm{s}$ at constant frequency. What is the frequency in hertz?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=4 ymin=0 ymax=13.200000000000001 f="3.0*x" punti="4,12,pieno,F" nomi="t (s),N (turns)" titolo="The slope gives revolutions per second."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
f &= \frac{N}{\Delta t} = \frac{12}{4}\,\mathrm{Hz} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 3\,\mathrm{Hz}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The duration used is $4\,\mathrm{s}$. Hertz counts revolutions in one second, so divide the number of revolutions by that duration. This gives $3\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Multiplying that figure by $4\,\mathrm{s}$ recovers the 12 observed revolutions. The period would instead be duration divided by revolutions.

</div>
