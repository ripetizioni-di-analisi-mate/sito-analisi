## Exercise 1

A platform rotates at constant frequency $2\,\mathrm{Hz}$. How long does it take to complete one revolution?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=0.515 ymin=-1.2 ymax=1.2 f="cos(2*PI*2.0*x)" punti="0,1,pieno,I;0.50000000,1,pieno,F" nomi="t (s),x/r" titolo="The horizontal projection returns to its initial value after one period."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac1{2}\,\mathrm{s}\simeq 0.5\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
\Delta t &= 1\cdot \frac1{2}\,\mathrm{s}\simeq 0.5\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The point makes 2 revolutions per second. The time for one revolution is the reciprocal $0.5\,\mathrm{s}$. With constant frequency, each revolution takes the same time. Multiply the period by 1 for the requested number of revolutions. Duration times frequency recovers that count.

</div>
