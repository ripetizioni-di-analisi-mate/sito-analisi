## Exercise 1

A point moves in a circle of radius $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$ at frequency $2\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Find its tangential speed.

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=0.325 ymin=0 ymax=4.39822971502571 f="12.566370614359172*x" punti="0.25,3.1415927,pieno,P" nomi="r (m),v (m/s)" titolo="At fixed frequency speed is proportional to radius."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
v &= 2\pi r f=2\pi\cdot 0.25\cdot 2\,\mathrm{m/s} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 3.1416\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The distance in one revolution is the circumference $2\pi r$. The point makes 2 revolutions each second, so multiply that length by the frequency. Instantaneous velocity points along the tangent and has magnitude $3.1416\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Another point at twice the radius on the same disk would make the same number of revolutions per second and travel twice the distance in each revolution.

</div>
