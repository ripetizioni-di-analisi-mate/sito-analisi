## Exercise 1

A thin diverging lens has focal length $f=-6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a real object at $p=18\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the signed image distance?

::grafico{assi=0 linee="-20.076923,0 11.076923,0;0,-9.75 0,9.75" vettori="-18,0,-18,3;-18,3,0,3;-18,3,0,0;0,3,9,7.5;0,0,9,-1.5" segmenti="0,3,-4.5,0.75;0,0,-4.5,0.75;-4.5,0,-4.5,0.75" punti="-6,0,pieno,F;6,0,pieno,F′;-4.5,0.75,vuoto,I;-18,3,pieno,O" xmin=-22.153846 xmax=13.153846 ymin=-10.725 ymax=10.725 titolo="Virtual image I lies 4.5 cm from the centre on the object side."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
q &= \frac{f p}{- f + p} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(-6\right) \left(18\right)}{- \left(-6\right) + \left(18\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq -4.5\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The focal length $f=-6\,\mathrm{cm}$ is negative and object distance $p=18\,\mathrm{cm}$ is positive. The difference of reciprocals is negative and the result lies on the object side. Output rays do not cross that point. The image is virtual and its distance magnitude is less than the focal-length magnitude.

In the diagram the point located by the extensions is closer to the lens than the object. With $p=18\,\mathrm{cm}$, the magnification magnitude is one quarter. The virtual image is therefore upright and reduced.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An object $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall is $p=24\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a diverging lens with $f=-8\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find image distance and height.

$$
\begin{aligned} q&=-6\,\mathrm{cm}\\M&=-\frac{-6}{24}=0.25\\ h_i&=0.25\cdot3\,\mathrm{cm}=0.75\,\mathrm{cm}.\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The image is virtual because q is negative. Magnification is positive, so the top of the object corresponds to the top of the image. A ratio of one quarter gives a height of three quarters of a centimetre.

The value $q=-6\,\mathrm{cm}$ lies between the focus on the object side and the centre. Multiply the original height by the positive magnification to find the height and also the direction of the image top.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A diverging lens has an object at $p=36\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a virtual image at $q=-9\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the focal length.

$$
\frac1f=\left(\frac1{36}-\frac{1}{9}\right)\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}=-\frac1{12}\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}},\qquad f=-12\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Use the negative value of the virtual image. Its reciprocal has four times the magnitude of the positive object reciprocal. The signed sum is still negative, as expected of a diverging lens. The final reciprocal gives minus twelve centimetres.

To check, put $f=-12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $p=36\,\mathrm{cm}$ back into the lens equation. The numerator for $q$ is negative, the denominator is $48\,\mathrm{cm}$, and the quotient returns $-9\,\mathrm{cm}$.

</div>
