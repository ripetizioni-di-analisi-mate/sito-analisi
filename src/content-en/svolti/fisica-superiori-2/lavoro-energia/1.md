## Exercise 1

A constant force of $20\,\mathrm{N}$ makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with a straight-line displacement of $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Find its work.

::grafico{xmin=-1 xmax=4 ymin=-0.7 ymax=3 vettori="0,0,3,0,s;0,0,1.250000,2.165064,F" uguale=1 assi=0 nomi="x,y" titolo="Arrow s follows displacement. The angle with F determines the projection."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_\parallel &= 20\cos60^\circ\,\mathrm{N}\simeq 10\,\mathrm{N} \\[6pt]
W &= F_\parallel s \simeq 30\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Work uses the component of force parallel to displacement. The angle is measured between these vectors, so use cosine. The signed component is $10\,\mathrm{N}$. Multiplying by $3\,\mathrm{m}$ gives $30\,\mathrm{J}$. The perpendicular component has no displacement in its direction and contributes zero. The sign of the result agrees with the projection's direction relative to the motion.

</div>
