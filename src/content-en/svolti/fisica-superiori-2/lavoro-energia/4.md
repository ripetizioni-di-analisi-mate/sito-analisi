## Exercise 1

A body of mass $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ is at signed height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ relative to the zero level. What is its gravitational potential energy with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

::grafico{xmin=-1.0 xmax=5.8 ymin=-1.0 ymax=97.0 f="20*x" punti="4,80,pieno,P;0,0,pieno,O" nomi="h (m),U (J)" titolo="O marks the zero-energy level."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
U_g &= mgh=2\cdot 10\cdot(4)\,\mathrm{J}=80\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Mass times $g$ gives the weight magnitude $20\,\mathrm{N}$. Multiply again by the signed height $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The result $80\,\mathrm{J}$ is relative to the chosen zero level. A height below it gives a negative value without implying negative kinetic energy. Shifting the reference adds the same constant to each point’s energy, while the difference between two positions is unchanged.

</div>
