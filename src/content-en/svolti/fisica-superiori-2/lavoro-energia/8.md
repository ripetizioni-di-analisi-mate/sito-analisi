## Exercise 1

A machine takes in $500\,\mathrm{J}$ and supplies $350\,\mathrm{J}$ of useful energy. What is its efficiency as a percentage?

$$
\begin{aligned}
\eta &= \frac{350}{500} = 0.7 \\[6pt]
\eta_\% &= 70\,\%
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Put useful energy in the numerator and input energy for the same process in the denominator. The quotient is a dimensionless fraction. Multiply by one hundred to express it as a percentage. The difference between input and useful output is $150\,\mathrm{J}$. As a check, the useful and non-useful percentages of the same input add to one hundred percent.

</div>
