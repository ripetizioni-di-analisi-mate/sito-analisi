## Exercise 1

A pinhole camera has its screen $11\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the pinhole. An object $120\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall is $220\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the pinhole. What is the magnitude of the image height?

::grafico{assi=0 linee="-220,0 11,0;-220,0 -220,120;11,-12 11,12" vettori="-220,120,11,-6" punti="-220,120,pieno,O;0,0,pieno,F;11,-6,pieno,I" xmin=-235.4 xmax=22 ymin=-17.6 ymax=132 titolo="The top, 120 cm above the axis, reaches 6 cm below the axis on the screen."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
h &= \frac{H q}{p} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(120\right) \left(11\right)}{\left(220\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 6\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The object, aperture and screen define two similar triangles. Their horizontal ratio is $11\,\mathrm{cm}$ divided by $220\,\mathrm{cm}$. The same factor multiplies the height $120\,\mathrm{cm}$. The image is much smaller because the screen is about twenty times closer to the aperture than the object. Its magnitude is six centimetres, below the axis for the top of the object.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An object of height $50\,\mathrm{cm}$ is $200\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the pinhole. How far away should the screen be to form a $7.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ image?

$$
q=p\frac{|h_i|}{h_o}=200\frac{7.5}{50}\,\mathrm{cm}=30\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Screen distance and projected height are proportional when the object and pinhole stay fixed. An image fifteen per cent as high as the object needs a screen at fifteen per cent of the object distance. The projected image remains inverted.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A screen stays $24\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the pinhole. The top of a $60\,\mathrm{cm}$ object is initially $240\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the aperture plane along the axis. It moves closer along the axis to $160\,\mathrm{cm}$. How much does the image-height magnitude grow?

$$
\begin{aligned}|h_{i,1}|&=60\cdot\frac{24}{240}\,\mathrm{cm}=6\,\mathrm{cm}\\|h_{i,2}|&=60\cdot\frac{24}{160}\,\mathrm{cm}=9\,\mathrm{cm}\\ \Delta |h_i|&=3\,\mathrm{cm}.\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The projection grows when the object approaches the pinhole because object distance is in the denominator. With the screen fixed, the height ratio is the inverse ratio of object distances. Image magnitude grows by three centimetres, with its top still below the axis.

</div>
