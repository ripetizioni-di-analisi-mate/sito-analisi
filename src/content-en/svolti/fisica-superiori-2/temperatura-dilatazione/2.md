## Exercise 1

A probe reads $305.15\,\mathrm K$. What Celsius temperature corresponds to the reading?

::grafico{f="x+273.15" punti="32,305.15,pieno,A" segmenti="32,280,32,305.15;4,305.15,32,305.15" xmin=4 xmax=44 ymin=280 ymax=318 nomi="t_C (°C),T (K)" titolo="The horizontal coordinate of A is the Celsius value corresponding to 305.15 K."}

$$
t_C=(305.15-273.15)\,{}^\circ\mathrm C=32\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The absolute reading is above $273.15\,\mathrm K$, the temperature of zero Celsius. The difference gives the distance above zero on the Celsius scale. On the diagram, start at the reading on the vertical axis and find the corresponding value on the horizontal axis. Substituting $32+273.15$ recovers the initial reading.

On the graph, the horizontal guide selects the point at $305.15\,\mathrm K$. From there, move vertically down to the Celsius axis. Following the guides in reverse order performs the inverse operation, which adds the difference between the two origins.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A device indicates a temperature between $247.15$ and $263.15\,\mathrm K$. Rewrite the range in degrees Celsius.

$$
\begin{aligned} t_{C,\min}&=(247.15-273.15)\,{}^\circ\mathrm C=-26\,{}^\circ\mathrm C\\[6pt] t_{C,\max}&=(263.15-273.15)\,{}^\circ\mathrm C=-10\,{}^\circ\mathrm C. \end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Both absolute values are below $273.15\,\mathrm K$, so both Celsius values are negative. Subtract the same constant from each endpoint. The lower initial reading still gives the lower endpoint. As a check, $-10$ is greater than $-26$ and their difference is $16$.

As a second check, the midpoint of the absolute interval is $255.15\,\mathrm K$. Converting it gives $-18\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. It is also the mean of $-26$ and $-10$, because conversion shifts every value by the same amount and preserves the midpoint.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A liquid that stays in the same phase starts at $278.15\,\mathrm K$ and cools by $12\,\mathrm K$. Find its final Celsius temperature.

$$
\begin{aligned} T_f&=(278.15-12)\,\mathrm K=266.15\,\mathrm K\\[6pt] t_{C,f}&=(266.15-273.15)\,{}^\circ\mathrm C=-7\,{}^\circ\mathrm C. \end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Cooling specifies a negative change, $\Delta T=-12\,\mathrm K$. Subtract $12$ kelvins from the initial absolute temperature. The result is still positive as an absolute value, but it lies below $273.15\,\mathrm K$. The Celsius reading is therefore negative. Converting the initial temperature first would give $5\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, from which we would subtract twelve degrees Celsius.

The difference between the initial and final values is $12\,\mathrm K$, both directly and after conversion. The reading goes from $5$ to $-7\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Crossing zero Celsius describes the position on the scale. Identifying any phase change would require knowing the substance.

</div>
