## Exercise 1

The mRNA tract 5′-AUG GAA UUU GGC-3′ is read from the first base shown. A variant replaces GAA with UAA. The meanings are given: AUG = methionine, GAA = glutamate, UUU = phenylalanine, GGC = glycine, UAA = stop. Classify the variant and compare the products before the stop.

Normal sequence → Met–Glu–Phe–Gly in the tract shown.

Variant sequence → Met–stop.

The substitution is **nonsense**; the translated tract before the stop contains only methionine.

<div class="spiegazione">

Compare triplets while retaining the specified frame, because the variant substitutes one base without adding or removing any. The first codon remains AUG and produces methionine. The second becomes UAA, which encodes no amino acid and terminates translation. UUU and GGC remain in the sequence, but that ribosome does not reach them after the stop.

The classification concerns the change from a sense codon to a stop codon. A missense variant would replace one amino acid with another; a synonymous variant would preserve the amino acid. The question describes the product expected from codon reading: actual protein amount and stability can also depend on RNA surveillance and cellular degradation.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In 5′-AUG GAA UUU GGC-3′, the first G of the second codon is deleted. Rewrite the tract grouped from the same first base and determine whether the frame changes. Compare this with deletion of the entire GAA codon.

One-base deletion → 5′-AUG AAU UUG GC-3′. The final two bases shown do not form a complete codon within the available tract.

Deletion of GAA → 5′-AUG UUU GGC-3′.

The first case causes a **frameshift**; the second preserves the downstream frame.

![Diagram of the data and relationships considered](/figure/biologia-semestre-filtro/genetica-mutazioni/cornice-en.svg)

<div class="spiegazione">

After removing the specified base, join the remaining sequence and regroup it in threes from the beginning. The first AUG is unaffected; subsequent bases are instead grouped into AAU and UUG. The frame changes because one base is not a whole number of triplets. The final two bases do not necessarily mark the actual end of the mRNA: the problem shows only a tract.

Removing three bases, the entire GAA, makes UUU begin exactly where GAA began, still followed by GGC. Loss of one amino acid can nevertheless alter function. The comparison therefore distinguishes frame preservation from preservation of biological activity, which requires additional information about the protein.

</div>
