## Exercise 1

During elongation, the P site contains a tRNA carrying a five-amino-acid chain; the A site contains the tRNA with the sixth. Describe the chain’s position after peptide bond formation and after one translocation, and the fate of the tRNA initially in P.

![Diagram of the data and reasoning](/figure/biologia-semestre-filtro/flusso-informazione/ribosoma-en.svg)

After peptide bond formation, the chain has six amino acids and is attached to the **A-site** tRNA. The tRNA initially in P has donated the chain and is uncharged.

After translocation, the chain-bearing tRNA moves from A to **P**; the uncharged tRNA moves from P towards E and can exit. The ribosome advances by one codon, or three nucleotides along mRNA.

<div class="spiegazione">

Peptide bond formation and translocation are separate events. The first changes which tRNA carries the chain; the second changes tRNA positions relative to ribosomal sites. This distinction avoids immediately placing the newly extended chain in P. Relative movement along mRNA is one triplet, preserving the frame for the next A-site entry. The exiting tRNA is uncharged because its former amino acid is now part of the transferred chain, and a synthetase can recharge it.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In a simplified model, a protein contains 25 amino acids. Initiator tRNA is already in the P site. Each subsequent amino acid consumes two GTP molecules, one for tRNA delivery and selection and one for translocation. Excluding initiation, termination and tRNA charging, calculate peptide bonds formed and GTP consumed during elongation.

The first residue is already present. The subsequent entries number

$$n_{\mathrm{c}}=25-1=24.$$

Each entry adds one residue and forms one peptide bond, so there are **24** bonds. With the specified consumption:

$$n_{\mathrm{GTP}}=2\cdot24=48.$$

The result concerns only the accounting defined in the question.

<div class="spiegazione">

A linear chain of twenty-five residues contains twenty-four links between adjacent residues. This can be checked independently of the ribosome by counting intervals between amino acids. GTP molecules are instead counted through the post-initiation cycles, explicitly using the model’s cost. The result is not the total energetic cost of producing the protein: tRNA charging uses ATP, and initiation, termination and other processes also have costs. The exclusions make the calculation well-defined and prevent confusion between ATP and GTP molecules.

</div>
