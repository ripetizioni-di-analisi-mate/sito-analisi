## Exercise 1

An mRNA is read from the first AUG in the sequence 5′-AUG GAA UUU UGA-3′. In the standard code, AUG = methionine, GAA = glutamate, UUU = phenylalanine and UGA = stop. Derive the incorporated chain and the anticodon of the GAA-recognising tRNA, written 5′ → 3′ and ignoring wobble.

The sequence is read as AUG, GAA, UUU and UGA. The first three codons add amino acids; the last causes release.

**Chain: methionine–glutamate–phenylalanine.**

The codon 5′-GAA-3′ pairs with 3′-CUU-5′. In the required direction, the anticodon is therefore **5′-UUC-3′**.

<div class="spiegazione">

The codon meanings are supplied, so translation requires retaining the frame and distinguishing the stop from amino acids. The twelve nucleotides contain four codons, but the chain has three residues because the fourth is a termination signal. For the anticodon, first write the antiparallel complement and then orient it 5′ → 3′. The UUC anticodon must not itself be interpreted as an amino acid codon: in this problem it is a tRNA sequence recognising GAA.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The original sequence is 5′-AUG GCU AAA UAA-3′, read from its first nucleotide. A C is inserted after AUG. Rewrite the complete new codons in the shown segment and determine whether the original UAA stop remains in the same frame. The subsequent sequence is unavailable.

The unspaced sequence changes from AUGGCUAAAUAA to AUGCGCUAAAUAA. Grouping from the first nucleotide gives:

**AUG | CGC | UAA | AUA | A**.

The new third codon is UAA and causes an early stop in the standard code. The original UAA no longer retains its grouping. The final A of the shown segment does not form a complete codon.

<div class="spiegazione">

A one-base insertion shifts downstream grouping because codons are triplets. As a check, the mutated sequence contains thirteen bases, arranged into four complete triplets plus one remaining base. The first AUG remains unchanged because insertion occurs after it. UAA appearing in the third group allows a conclusion about termination even without knowing subsequent bases. Translation must not be continued beyond that stop in the adopted standard model, nor should the incomplete final triplet be filled arbitrarily.

</div>
