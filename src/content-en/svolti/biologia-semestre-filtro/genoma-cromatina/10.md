## Exercise 1

Three lines show less RNA from gene G. A has a new promoter insertion; B retains the sequence but has a nucleosome moved onto the regulatory site; C retains sequence and accessibility but degrades RNA faster. Associate each line with the documented mechanism.

| Line | Documented mechanism |
|---|---|
| A | Genetic sequence variation |
| B | Chromatin reorganisation |
| C | Altered RNA stability |

<div class="spiegazione">

The final observation, less RNA, is shared but does not identify the cause alone. A contains new DNA bases and junctions, so the change is genetic even though it affects regulation. B changes particle arrangement with an unchanged sequence, influencing site access. C may produce RNA at the same rate yet accumulate less because it removes it faster. Distinguishing the cases requires sequence, chromatin, RNA-production and RNA-degradation measurements. Classifying B as a chromatin change does not yet demonstrate epigenetic memory; that requires following persistence through divisions.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In a 500,000-bp artificial genome, 10,000 bp directly encode proteins, 40,000 are assigned to RNA genes and regulatory regions, and the remainder is unclassified in the experiment. The categories are defined without overlap. Calculate percentages and state the limit of functional interpretation.

$$\begin{aligned}p_{\mathrm{protein}}&=\frac{10000}{500000}\cdot100=2\%\\[6pt]p_{\mathrm{RNA+reg}}&=\frac{40000}{500000}\cdot100=8\%\\[6pt]p_{\mathrm{unclassified}}&=100-2-8=90\%.\end{aligned}$$

The unclassified 90% is not equivalent to 90% lacking function.

<div class="spiegazione">

The experiment defines separate categories, so their percentages can be added without double counting. The known total is 10%, and its complement to 100% gives the unclassified 90%. That term describes a limitation of available data, not demonstrated absence of function. The 8% assigned to RNA and regulation already shows that function is not equivalent to protein coding. Assigning roles to the remaining DNA requires specific experiments; percentage calculation cannot replace them. The final check $2+8+90=100$ verifies only the length balance for the stated artificial genome.

</div>
