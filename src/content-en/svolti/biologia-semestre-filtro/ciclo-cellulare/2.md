## Exercise 1

Two extracts contain equal amounts of cyclin B and CDK1. In the first, CDK1 retains inhibitory phosphorylation; in the second, Cdc25 has removed it. With other factors equal, which extract should show greater mitotic activity? Propose a direct measurement.

The second extract is compatible with greater complex activity because Cdc25 removed inhibitory phosphorylation. Phosphorylation of a validated CDK1 substrate can be compared under controlled conditions, including a kinase-inhibited control. A blot measuring cyclin B alone would confirm abundance but would not directly measure catalysis.

<div class="spiegazione">

The question separates how many complexes may be present from how many are functionally active. A regulatory protein can change activity without changing abundance. The inhibitor control helps attribute the measured signal to the kinase of interest rather than another kinase in the extract. The prediction remains conditional on other regulators being equal, which real experiments must check.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A mutant cyclin B binds CDK1 normally but is not degraded at the end of mitosis. The spindle checkpoint has been satisfied. Which regulatory step is directly impaired, and why does correct chromosome alignment alone not solve the problem?

The directly impaired step is the **reduction in mitotic activity associated with cyclin B destruction**, required for normal mitotic exit. Satisfying the checkpoint permits activation of the degradation pathway but does not make a substrate degradable if it lacks the required recognition signal. Permission to initiate exit and execution of proteolysis are distinct steps.

<div class="spiegazione">

The checkpoint is not a switch that automatically completes every subsequent event. It assesses conditions and removes inhibition, while downstream steps retain their own requirements. This mutation affects the regulatory substrate, so CDK1 activity can persist despite correct chromosome attachment. It does not imply that every other APC/C substrate, such as securin, is also non-degradable.

</div>
