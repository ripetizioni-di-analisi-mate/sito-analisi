## Exercise 1

In a diploid human culture, fluorescence proportional to DNA is 100 units in G1. One group shows 200 units. How many chromosomes and DNA molecules does a cell in this group contain before anaphase? Is this measurement sufficient to distinguish G2 from M?

The intensity ratio is $\dfrac{200}{100}=2$: DNA has doubled relative to G1. The cell therefore has $4C$ but retains two chromosome sets, or $2n$.

| Quantity | G1 | After S, before anaphase |
|---|---:|---:|
| Chromosomes | 46 | 46 |
| DNA molecules | 46 | 92 |
| DNA content | $2C$ | $4C$ |

The measurement is compatible with **either G2 or M** before separation. Chromosome condensation can help distinguish them.

![DNA content during the cycle and in each daughter cell](/figure/biologia-semestre-filtro/ciclo-cellulare/ciclo-en.svg)

<div class="spiegazione">

Replication produces sister chromatids, not another set of homologues. Two counts must be separated: a duplicated chromosome remains one unit until sister centromeres separate, whereas each chromatid already contains a DNA molecule. Fluorescence measures total quantity, not DNA organisation. Assigning every 200-unit cell directly to mitosis would add a conclusion that the measurement does not support.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A population contains 60 cells with $2C$, 25 with DNA content between $2C$ and $4C$, and 15 with $4C$. A short EdU pulse labels only the 25 intermediate cells. What does this show, and can G1 be concluded to last 60% of the cycle?

EdU is incorporated into newly synthesised DNA: the 25 labelled cells are performing **DNA synthesis**, consistent with S phase. The 60 cells at $2C$ may include G1 and G0; the 15 at $4C$ may include G2 and M. The fraction $\dfrac{60}{100}=60\%$ describes the sample but does not establish a G1 duration equal to 60% of the cycle.

<div class="spiegazione">

A population snapshot is not the timeline of one cell. Inferring durations requires a model of culture growth, cell-age distribution, possible quiescence and cell loss. Even in a proliferating culture, different ages need not be equally represented. The synthesis marker adds information about the ongoing process but does not remove these sampling limitations.

</div>
