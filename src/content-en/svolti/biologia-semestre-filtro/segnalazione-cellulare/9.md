## Exercise 1

In a linear EGFR → SOS → Ras → Raf → MEK → ERK model, active ERK is measured. EGFR blockade eliminates the signal; Ras active independently of SOS restores it. A subsequent MEK inhibitor eliminates active ERK again. Interpret the three results, assuming selective interventions and no alternative pathways.

![Ordering Ras-MAPK pathways](/figure/biologia-semestre-filtro/segnalazione-cellulare/rtk-en.svg)

1. Receptor blockade interrupts upstream activation.
2. Independently active Ras bypasses EGFR and SOS and restores downstream signalling.
3. MEK blockade again interrupts transmission to ERK even with active Ras.

The model places **MEK downstream of Ras** and makes it necessary for that response.

<div class="spiegazione">

Rescue locates a defect relative to the introduced component. An active Ras form can replace the command received from SOS, but cannot replace a downstream kinase that has been inactivated. The second inhibition demonstrates this distinction in the model. The conclusions depend on selectivity and linearity: an artificially active protein might also stimulate other branches in a real cell. Controls for Ras-GTP, MEK and ERK phosphorylation, and viability help verify that the observed signal actually follows the stated route.

</div>

## Exercise 2

After EGF removal, the Ras-GTP fraction falls from 60% to 10% in cells A but remains at 55% in cells B. Cells B express a Ras variant with reduced hydrolysis. Explain signal persistence and state why this measurement alone does not establish complete tumour transformation.

The decrease is 50 percentage points in A and 5 percentage points in B. Reduced GTP hydrolysis slows Ras return to the GDP-bound state, making the **persistence of the active state** in B consistent. The finding characterises one pathway property, not every property of the cell.

<div class="spiegazione">

Nucleotide hydrolysis is a termination mechanism. The variant may therefore retain a high Ras-GTP fraction even after removal of upstream stimulation. The comparison concerns percentages of active Ras, so the direct difference is expressed in percentage points; an increase in total Ras molecules need not be assumed. Persistent activation may favour growth signals, but proliferation, survival, genome integrity and tissue interactions depend on many controls. Describing transformation requires further functional and molecular data beyond this single marker.

</div>
