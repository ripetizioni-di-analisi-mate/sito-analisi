## Exercise 1

Three Ras samples initially contain the GDP-bound state. Sample A receives a GEF; sample B receives only a GAP; sample C receives a GEF but has no available GTP. All other required components are present. Identify the sample in which Ras-GTP accumulation is directly favoured and explain the other two cases.

| Sample | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| A | The GEF promotes GDP release and binding of available GTP. |
| B | The GAP accelerates hydrolysis when GTP is bound; it does not replace nucleotide exchange. |
| C | GDP may be released, but the GTP needed to form Ras-GTP is missing. |

The requested accumulation is favoured in **sample A**.

<div class="spiegazione">

The distinction concerns two different reactions. A GEF facilitates nucleotide exchange; it does not add a phosphate to GDP already bound. Forming Ras-GTP therefore requires GTP to be available after GDP release. A GAP instead acts on the GTP-bound state, promoting return to GDP through hydrolysis. In sample B, starting from GDP makes clear why adding only a GAP does not provide an activation mechanism. The experiment separately controls the protein regulator and nucleotide, avoiding attribution of substrate-independent activation to the mere presence of a GEF.

</div>

## Exercise 2

After a pulse, 160 Gα molecules are active. In a discrete model, half of the molecules still active hydrolyse GTP in each interval; the receptor activates no new molecules. Calculate how many remain active after three intervals. Compare this with a variant in which only one quarter of the active molecules hydrolyse GTP per interval.

![G-protein cycles](/figure/biologia-semestre-filtro/segnalazione-cellulare/gpcr-en.svg)

$$
\begin{aligned}
N_1&=160\cdot\frac12=80,\\
N_2&=80\cdot\frac12=40,\\
N_3&=40\cdot\frac12=20.
\end{aligned}
$$

For the variant:
$$
160\left(\frac34\right)^3=67{,}5.
$$
The second result is a model mean: approximately 68 molecules. The variant retains a **larger active fraction**.

<div class="spiegazione">

The fraction remaining active is applied each time to the residual number, not the initial value. Half remains per interval in the normal sample; three quarters remains in the variant because only one quarter hydrolyses GTP. The noninteger value expresses a population mean, whereas a single-cell count would be an integer. The absence of new activation isolates termination. In a cell continuously exposed to ligand, the active number would also depend on the rate at which the receptor reactivates G proteins.

</div>
