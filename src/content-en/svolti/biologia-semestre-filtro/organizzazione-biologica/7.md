## Exercise 1

An enzyme made of one 80-amino-acid chain loses activity after treatment. Analysis still shows an 80-residue chain with the original sequence, but an altered conformation. Distinguish denaturation from proteolysis and explain why preserved sequence does not guarantee activity.

![Levels of protein organisation](/figure/biologia-semestre-filtro/organizzazione-biologica/proteins-en.svg)

Primary structure is preserved. Altered conformation and functional loss are consistent with **denaturation**. Extensive proteolysis would have produced shorter fragments by hydrolysing peptide bonds.

The active site may depend on bringing residues distant in sequence close together in space; the new folding may prevent that arrangement.

<div class="spiegazione">

The covalent chain provides the sequence, while interactions between its groups support three-dimensional organisation. Preserving the first level does not imply preserving all subsequent levels. An active site requires appropriate residue positions and chemical properties, so conformational change may reduce substrate binding or catalysis. The findings alone cannot establish whether the damage is reversible: treatment would need to be removed and recovery of structure and activity measured. The case therefore distinguishes classification of the observed change from the still untested prediction of renaturation.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A functional protein contains four linear chains, each with 50 amino acids. The chains associate through noncovalent interactions. Calculate the total number of peptide bonds and identify the structural level changed if the complex dissociates into intact subunits.

A linear chain of 50 residues contains $50-1=49$ peptide bonds. The four chains therefore contain

$$
4\cdot49=196
$$

peptide bonds. Dissociation changes **quaternary structure**. If the subunits remain intact, their total number of peptide bonds remains 196.

<div class="spiegazione">

Counting 200 residues and subtracting one would give 199 bonds, but that would describe a single continuous chain. There are four open chains here, so each chain contributes one interruption of linear connectivity. An alternative check is $200-4=196$. The interactions between subunits, specified as noncovalent, add no peptide bonds to the count. Quaternary refers to relationships between chains and also applies when the number of subunits differs from four. Dissociation may impair function while preserving the sequence and folding of individual chains, depending on how they cooperated in the complex.

</div>
