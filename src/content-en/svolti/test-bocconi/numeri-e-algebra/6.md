## Exercise 1

The rental cost $C$ is $C=(F+rt)(1+s)-v$. The fixed charge is $F$, the hourly rate is $r$, the duration is $t>0$, the relative surcharge is $s\ge0$ and the voucher is worth $v$. Rearrange the formula for $r$ and evaluate it for $C=50\,\mathrm{EUR}$, $F=12\,\mathrm{EUR}$, $t=4\,\mathrm{h}$, $s=\dfrac14$ and $v=5\,\mathrm{EUR}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
C+v&=(F+rt)(1+s),\\[6pt]
\frac{C+v}{1+s}-F&=rt,\\[6pt]
r&=\frac{\dfrac{C+v}{1+s}-F}{t}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
r=\frac{\dfrac{50+5}{\dfrac54}-12}{4}\,\mathrm{EUR/h}
=8\,\mathrm{EUR/h}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

First add the voucher to the final cost to recover the total with the surcharge. Since $s\ge0$, the factor $1+s$ is positive. Dividing by this factor recovers the fixed charge plus the cost for the hours rented. After subtracting the fixed charge, division by $t>0$ gives the cost of one hour. A check gives $(12+8\cdot4)\cdot\dfrac54-5=50$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A $60\,\mathrm{m}$ fence encloses three sides of a rectangle built against a wall. The side along the wall has length $l$ and the two perpendicular sides have length $x$. The area is $400\,\mathrm{m}^2$, and the side along the wall is at least twice the width. Find $x$ and $l$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
l+2x&=60,\\[6pt]
x(60-2x)&=400,\\[6pt]
x^2-30x+200&=0,\\[6pt]
(x-10)(x-20)&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

For $x=10$ we obtain $l=40$. For $x=20$ we obtain $l=20$. Only $x=10\,\mathrm{m}$ and $l=40\,\mathrm{m}$ satisfy $l\ge2x$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The fence covers both widths and the side opposite the wall, which has the same length as the side along the wall. The area therefore gives a quadratic equation in $x$. Both roots produce positive dimensions, so the additional constraint determines the answer. For the first pair, $40\ge2\cdot10$. For the second, $20<2\cdot20$.

</div>
