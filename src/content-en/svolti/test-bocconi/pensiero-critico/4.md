## Exercise 1

Four deliveries $F,G,H,K$ take place one at a time. $G$ is immediately before $H$. $K$ is after $H$. $F$ is not the first delivery. Determine the set of possible positions for $K$.

| Positions of the block $G,H$ | Feasible order |
| --- | --- |
| 1 and 2 | $G,H,F,K$ or $G,H,K,F$ |
| 2 and 3 | None |
| 3 and 4 | None |

<div class="spiegazione">

If the block occupied positions 2 and 3, $K$ would have to be fourth, so $F$ would be first. That violates the constraint on $F$. If it occupied positions 3 and 4, no position after $H$ would remain for $K$. When the block occupies 1 and 2, both orders in the table meet all the constraints. $K$ can therefore be third or fourth.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Four people $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ staff two stations, an information desk and a coat check. Each person works at one station only and each station has exactly two people. $A$ works at the desk, $B$ and $D$ work at different stations, and $C$ and $D$ work together. Determine the two pairs.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Information desk} &= \{A,B\}, \\
\text{Coat check} &= \{C,D\}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

$C$ and $D$ fill one station because they work together and each station has two people. The desk already has $A$, so the pair $C,D$ must be at the coat check. The only available place is at the desk, so it goes to $B$. The allocation also meets the condition that $B$ and $D$ work separately.

</div>
