## Exercise 1

At a festival, tickets purchased online give access to the fast lane. Complimentary tickets issued to speakers also give access to the fast lane. A staff member who is not a speaker receives a complimentary ticket online. A speaker receives a complimentary ticket from the accreditation desk. Determine what follows for each ticket.

<div class="spiegazione">

The first ticket is complimentary, and the staff member is outside the group of speakers. Both rules therefore leave the outcome open. Allowing access with that ticket and denying it are both consistent with the rules. The second rule applies to the speaker because her ticket is complimentary. The method of delivery is not a condition of this rule, so her ticket gives access to the fast lane.

</div>

## Exercise 2

For returns submitted from 15 March onwards, a shop requires proof of purchase except for defective items. During the same period, it accepts a photo of the label as an alternative to proof of purchase for those items. Two customers request a return with a photo but no proof of purchase. The first returns a defective item on 14 March, and the second returns an intact item on 18 March. Determine whether the photo meets the requirement in each case.

| Case | Does the rule from 15 March apply? | Document requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 14 March, defective | No | Cannot be determined |
| 18 March, intact | Yes | Proof of purchase |

<div class="spiegazione">

14 March precedes the effective date, so the stated rule does not determine the requirement for the first customer. The earlier conditions could allow a photo or require proof of purchase. 18 March falls within the specified period and the item is intact, so the exception does not apply. For the second customer the photo alone does not meet the requirement.

</div>
