## Exercise 1

> Students may watch the demonstration by entering through the north door. Visitors who want to try the equipment must pick up a pass at the desk by the south entrance. Both entrances close ten minutes before the start.

State the requirement for a visitor who wants to use the equipment.

A visitor who wants to use the equipment must **pick up a pass at the desk by the south entrance**.

<div class="spiegazione">

The requested activity is *using the equipment*. The first sentence concerns people attending as spectators. The second mentions trying the equipment and specifies a pass for that activity. The closing time applies to both groups, so it limits when they can enter while leaving the conditions for participation distinct.

</div>

## Exercise 2

> Projects submitted by 12 June will be assessed in the first round. Additions to projects already submitted may arrive by 15 June if the administration has requested them. Projects first submitted after 12 June can enter only the next call, even if the upload form remains open on the days reserved for additions.

A school submits a project for the first time on 14 June. Determine which call it can enter and explain what the 15 June date applies to.

The project can enter **only the next call**. The 15 June date is the deadline for requested additions to projects already submitted.

<div class="spiegazione">

The phrase *for the first time* places the submission among new projects. Their deadline for the first round is 12 June, so 14 June is too late. The 15 June date concerns a different set of documents, the additions to projects already submitted. The form remains open to accept those additions.

</div>
