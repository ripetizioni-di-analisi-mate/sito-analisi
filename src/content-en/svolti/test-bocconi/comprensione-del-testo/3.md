## Exercise 1

> After checking that the seal closed properly, the researcher fitted the lid to the container. The researcher then filled it with water to the three-litre mark to check for leaks.

Identify what *it* refers to.

*It* refers to **the container**.

<div class="spiegazione">

*Lid* and *container* are both singular nouns, so the pronoun could grammatically refer to either. In the second sentence, the object is filled with water up to a mark. The container is the vessel that can hold that volume. The lid allows the researcher to close it.

</div>

## Exercise 2

> Booking requests were concentrated in the week before the festival. By then, the organisers had little room to add another performance. The venue was already booked for the next production, and two of the actors had commitments in other cities.

Explain what *room* means in this passage.

*Room* means **the practical flexibility to change the schedule**.

<div class="spiegazione">

The phrase *to add another performance* identifies the action for which the organisers have little room. The venue booking and the actors' commitments reduce the available options. The word therefore refers to freedom to make a change. The passage uses no revenue or cost figures to define a financial margin.

</div>
