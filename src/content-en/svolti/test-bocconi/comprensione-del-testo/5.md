## Exercise 1

> The group had chosen the room with a projector to show photographs from the trip. The projector broke down shortly before the meeting. The group used the room anyway because a participant had brought printed photographs for everyone to discuss.

Explain the link between the breakdown and the final sentence.

The breakdown **creates an obstacle**, and the final sentence explains how the group managed to hold the meeting in the room as planned.

<div class="spiegazione">

The opening links the choice of room to the use of the projector. Following the breakdown, the group's original way of showing the photographs is unavailable. The prints give participants another way to discuss them. The word *because* therefore introduces the reason the group could keep the room without a projector.

</div>

## Exercise 2

> The apple trees along the trail were in flower before the date in the nursery's logbook. The first inspection, however, took place three days later than the visit recorded in the logbook. This year, as in the past, the technicians would need to make visits closer together to pinpoint when flowering began.

Explain what the word *however* does in the second sentence.

*However* introduces **a qualification about how precise the comparison can be**.

<div class="spiegazione">

The first sentence compares the current flowering with the date in the logbook. The second points out that the inspections took place at different times, so the two observations have to be compared with care. The final sentence explains what closer inspections would allow the technicians to do. The connective therefore links the comparison to a limitation of the method used to collect the observations.

</div>
