## Exercise 1

> Everyone in a team of four must bring valid identification to take part in the events reserved for teams of that size. Sara's team arrives with valid identification for three members. The fourth member brings only a copy of the entry form, which is outside the list of accepted documents.

Assuming that no one obtains any other identification, determine whether the team meets the condition for participation in the reserved event.

The team **fails to meet the identification requirement** for the reserved event.

<div class="spiegazione">

The requirement covers all four members of the team. The passage says there are three valid identity documents and specifies what the fourth member brings, a copy of the entry form. The passage places that copy outside the list of accepted identification. Since the stated assumption rules out any other documents, one of the four required documents is missing.

</div>

## Exercise 2

> A company replaced some of its vehicles with smaller ones. Over the next three months, total expenditure on fuel fell. Deliveries outside the city also decreased over the same period. The report gives the number of deliveries and total spending, but gives no mileage figures for the different vehicle types.

Determine whether the passage supports attributing the full fall in expenditure to the new vehicles.

The passage documents an overall saving. **The amount attributable to the new vehicles remains undetermined**.

<div class="spiegazione">

The figures describe total spending, so they combine different journeys. Deliveries outside the city decreased over the same period and may have reduced the distance travelled. Separating the two effects would require comparing similar journeys. The passage therefore shows a change in spending while leaving its causes open.

</div>
