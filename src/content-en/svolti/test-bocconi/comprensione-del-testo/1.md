## Exercise 1

> Restaurants in the town centre trialled returnable containers for takeaway meals. At first, many containers came back several days late. Since customers have been able to return them to any participating restaurant, many are returned by the following day.

Identify the role of the second sentence.

The second sentence **describes the problem at the start** of the trial.

<div class="spiegazione">

The first sentence introduces the trial, so the reader already knows which containers the passage goes on to discuss. The second specifies that the containers often came back late. The third introduces a change and reports that many containers now come back by the following day. The initial delay is therefore the problem that helps make sense of the final observation.

</div>

## Exercise 2

> To read the inscriptions on the stone plaques in the entrance hall, conservators usually had to light each carving from the side. They therefore photographed the plaques with a lamp moved to several positions. On one inscription, however, such a large area had worn away that two words remained unreadable even in the side-lit photographs.
>
> The catalogue will print the two words in square brackets with a question mark, following the convention for uncertain readings.

Explain the relationship between the paragraphs.

The second paragraph **explains how the catalogue will show the uncertainty** identified in the first.

<div class="spiegazione">

The first paragraph starts with the photographic method and identifies a specific limit. The phrase *the two words* in the second paragraph refers to the exact words that remained unreadable. Their presentation in the catalogue follows the convention mentioned. The passage therefore moves from a physical obstacle to the way the result will be reported.

</div>
