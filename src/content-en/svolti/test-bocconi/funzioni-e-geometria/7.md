## Exercise 1

Determine whether the line $y=5$ intersects the circle $(x+1)^2+(y-2)^2=25$. If so, find the points of intersection.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x+1)^2+(5-2)^2&=25,\\
(x+1)^2&=16,\\
x+1&=\pm4.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
P_1=(-5,5),\qquad P_2=(3,5).
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The center is $(-1,2)$ and the radius is $5$. The horizontal line is $|5-2|=3$ units from the center, so the radius exceeds that distance and there are two intersections. Substituting $y=5$ shows that each $x$-coordinate is $4$ units away from $-1$, giving the two points.

</div>

## Exercise 2

For what value of $c$ is the line $y=4x+c$ tangent to the parabola $y=x^2+2x+3$? Find the point of tangency as well.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^2+2x+3&=4x+c,\\
x^2-2x+3-c&=0,\\
(x-1)^2&=c-2.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
c=2,\qquad x=1,\qquad y=4+2=6.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The line has finite slope, so tangency corresponds to a double root of the intersection equation. After completing the square, the left side is zero only at $x=1$. If $c>2$, the right side is positive and there are two roots. If $c<2$, the right side is negative and there are no real roots. The only value that produces tangency is $c=2$, at $(1,6)$.

</div>
