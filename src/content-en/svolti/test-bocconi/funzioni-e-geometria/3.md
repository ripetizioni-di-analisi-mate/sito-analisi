## Exercise 1

Find the vertex and intercepts of $f(x)=2|x+1|-6$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(-1)&=-6,\\
f(x)=0&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad |x+1|=3\\
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x=-4\ \text{or}\ x=2,\\
f(0)&=2-6=-4.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{f="2*abs(x+1)-6" xmin=-5.5 xmax=3.5 ymin=-7 ymax=3 punti="-4,0,pieno;-1,-6,pieno;0,-4,pieno;2,0,pieno" titolo="The vertex is $(-1,-6)$ and the intercepts are $(-4,0)$, $(2,0)$ and $(0,-4)$."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The absolute value is the distance from $-1$, so it has its minimum at $x=-1$. The factor $2>0$ preserves the location of this minimum. To reach a $y$-coordinate of zero, the distance from $-1$ must be $3$. The two corresponding inputs are on opposite sides of the vertex. For the vertical intercept, set $x=0$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Find the inputs for which $f(x)=(x-2)^{-2}+1$ is at most $5$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
1+\frac{1}{(x-2)^2}&\leq 5,\\
\frac{1}{(x-2)^2}&\leq 4,\\
(x-2)^2&\geq \frac14,\\
|x-2|&\geq \frac12.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
x\in\left(-\infty,\frac32\right]\cup\left[\frac52,+\infty\right).
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The input $2$ is excluded because the negative power requires a nonzero denominator. On the domain $(x-2)^2$ is positive, so multiplication by this factor preserves the direction of the inequality. The final condition requires a distance of at least $\dfrac12$ from $2$. Equality is allowed at both endpoints.

</div>
