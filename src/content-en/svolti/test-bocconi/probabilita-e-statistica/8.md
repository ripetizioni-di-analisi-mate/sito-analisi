## Exercise 1

Four deliveries take $3,5,5,11$ minutes. Find the mean, median, mode, and range.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bar x&=\frac{3+5+5+11}{4}=6\,\mathrm{min},\\[6pt]
\operatorname{Med}&=\frac{5+5}{2}=5\,\mathrm{min},\\[6pt]
\operatorname{Mode}&=5\,\mathrm{min},\\[6pt]
R&=11-3=8\,\mathrm{min}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The mean distributes the total of $24$ minutes across the four deliveries. Since the data are in order and the number of observations is even, the median is the mean of the two middle measurements. The value $5$ also has the highest frequency, so it is the mode. The range measures the distance between the extremes.

</div>

## Exercise 2

On a test, scores of $1,3,5$ occur $2,4,2$ times, respectively. Find the descriptive variance of the scores. Each score $x$ is then transformed into $y=3+2x$. What is the standard deviation of the new scores?

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bar x&=\frac{2\cdot1+4\cdot3+2\cdot5}{8}=3,\\[6pt]
\sigma_x^2&=\frac{2(1-3)^2+4(3-3)^2+2(5-3)^2}{8}=2,\\[6pt]
\sigma_y&=2\sqrt2.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each value is weighted by how often it occurs. After finding the mean, multiply each squared deviation by its frequency and divide by eight. The transformation doubles each deviation from the new mean, since $y-\bar y=2(x-\bar x)$. The original standard deviation is $\sqrt2$, so the final one is $2\sqrt2$.

</div>
