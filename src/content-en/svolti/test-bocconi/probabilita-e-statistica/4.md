## Exercise 1

Of $72$ club members, $26$ take a photography course, $34$ take an editing course, and $12$ take both. A member is selected uniformly at random. What is the probability that they take neither course?

::venn{tipo="due" universo=1 ambiente="Ω" zone="u" elementi="a:14;ab:12;b:22;u:24" titolo="Number of members in each region. The outside region represents members who take neither course."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
|A\cup B|&=26+34-12=48,\\[6pt]
P((A\cup B)^c)&=\frac{72-48}{72}=\frac13.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $A$ and $B$ be the events that the member takes photography and editing, respectively. Adding the two counts includes the $12$ members taking both courses twice, so one copy is subtracted. This leaves $48$ members taking at least one course. The complement contains $24$. All $72$ members are equally likely, so the denominator is $72$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Two fair dice are rolled independently. Find the probability that the absolute difference between their results is at most $3$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
|\Omega|&=6\cdot6=36,\\[6pt]
|\{\text{absolute difference at least }4\}|&=2\cdot3=6,\\[6pt]
P(\text{absolute difference at most }3)&=1-\frac{6}{36}=\frac56.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The $36$ outcomes are equally likely ordered pairs. The pairs whose first value is smaller and whose difference is at least $4$ are $(1,5),(1,6),(2,6)$. Each gives a reversed pair. Since the difference is an integer, the complement of the event of interest has six outcomes. Subtract its probability from $1$.

</div>
