## Exercise 1

At a repair shop, $25\%$ of customers book online. Of these, $16\%$ reschedule. The remaining $75\%$ book by phone, and $4\%$ of them reschedule. Find the probability that a uniformly selected customer reschedules.

$$
P(S)=\frac{25}{100}\cdot\frac{16}{100}
     +\frac{75}{100}\cdot\frac4{100}
     =\frac4{100}+\frac3{100}=\frac7{100}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Online and phone bookings are disjoint groups. Each group's contribution is its share of all customers multiplied by the rescheduling frequency within that group. The two contributions give seven expected reschedulings per hundred customers, so the probability is $7\%$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Three lines $A,B,C$ produce $50\%,30\%,20\%$ of the batches, respectively. The probabilities of an additional inspection for those lines are $10\%,20\%,25\%$, respectively. A uniformly selected batch required an additional inspection. What is the probability that it came from $B$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
P(A\cap S)&=\frac{50}{100}\cdot\frac{10}{100}=\frac5{100},\\[6pt]
P(B\cap S)&=\frac{30}{100}\cdot\frac{20}{100}=\frac6{100},\\[6pt]
P(C\cap S)&=\frac{20}{100}\cdot\frac{25}{100}=\frac5{100},\\[6pt]
P(B\mid S)&=\frac{6}{5+6+5}=\frac38.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Event $S$ means the batch requires inspection. For every hundred batches, the expected numbers requiring an additional inspection from the three lines are five, six, and five. Since the observed batch required inspection, it belongs to the group of $16$ expected batches. Of these, $6$ come from $B$, so **Bayes' rule** gives $\dfrac38$.

</div>
