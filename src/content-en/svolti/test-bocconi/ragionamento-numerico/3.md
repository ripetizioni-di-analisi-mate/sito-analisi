## Exercise 1

The chart shows bicycles delivered by a repair shop over four weeks. The horizontal axis indicates the week number. Find the difference in deliveries between the weeks with the highest and lowest totals.

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=36 poligoni="0.7,0 1.3,0 1.3,18 0.7,18;1.7,0 2.3,0 2.3,26 1.7,26;2.7,0 3.3,0 3.3,16 2.7,16;3.7,0 4.3,0 4.3,30 3.7,30" etichette="1,20,18;2,28,26;3,18,16;4,32,30" titolo="Bicycles delivered in weeks 1 to 4"}

$$
30-16=14.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each bar's height represents a weekly count. The maximum is $30$ in week four. The minimum is $16$ in week three. Subtract the minimum from the maximum because the question asks for the difference between the two counts. The difference is $14$ bicycles.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The chart shows the cumulative number of site inspections completed by two teams at the end of each week. Determine which team completed more inspections during week three and by how many.

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=4 ymin=0 ymax=84 curve="t,16+32*(t-1)-7*(t-2+abs(t-2)),1,3;t,20+20*(t-1)+4*(t-2+abs(t-2)),1,3" punti="1,16,pieno;2,48,pieno;3,66,pieno;1,20,pieno;2,40,pieno;3,68,pieno" legenda="\mathrm{A};\mathrm{B}" titolo="Cumulative inspections at the end of each week. A records 16, 48 and 66. B records 20, 40 and 68."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
66-48 &= 18 \quad \text{(team A)} \\
68-40 &= 28 \quad \text{(team B)} \\
28-18 &= 10
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To isolate the inspections completed during week three, subtract each team's cumulative total at the end of week two. Team B moves from $40$ to $68$, completing $28$ inspections in the period. Team A completes $18$. The difference is $10$ more inspections for team B.

</div>
