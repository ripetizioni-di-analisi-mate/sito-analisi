## Exercise 1

The share of subscribers choosing an annual subscription rises from $18\%$ to $24\%$. Calculate the increase in percentage points and the increase relative to the original share.

$$
\begin{aligned}
24-18 &= 6 \\
\frac{24-18}{18} &= \frac{1}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Both shares are already expressed per hundred, so the difference is $6$ percentage points. The relative increase uses the original $18\%$ as its base. Six points is one third of that base, giving a relative increase of $33\dfrac{1}{3}\%$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A bicycle hire service increases its fleet from $400$ to $600$ bicycles. The number of electric bicycles rises from $90$ to $120$. Calculate the relative change in the number of electric bicycles and the change in their share of the fleet in percentage points.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{120-90}{90} &= \frac{1}{3} \\
\frac{90}{400} &= 22.5\% \\
\frac{120}{600} &= 20\% \\
20-22.5 &= -2.5
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The number of electric bicycles rises by $30$. Dividing by the original $90$ gives relative growth of $33\dfrac{1}{3}\%$. To find each share, divide the electric bicycle count by the total fleet in the same period. The fleet grows enough for the share to fall by $2.5$ percentage points.

</div>
