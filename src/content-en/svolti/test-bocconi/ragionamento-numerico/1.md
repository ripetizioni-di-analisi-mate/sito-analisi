## Exercise 1

In its May accounts, the Vega cinema box office reports takings of $1.8$ thousand euros and refunds of $240\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The cinema was open for $22$ days that month. Calculate the May takings after refunds.

$$
\begin{aligned}
1.8\cdot 1{,}000 &= 1{,}800\,\mathrm{EUR} \\
1{,}800 - 240 &= 1{,}560\,\mathrm{EUR}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The column heading expresses takings in thousands of euros, so one unit in the column equals $1{,}000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Refunds are already expressed in euros. Both quantities now have the same unit and refer to the same period. After subtraction, $1{,}560\,\mathrm{EUR}$ remains. The number of days would be relevant to the average daily takings.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The North depot shipped $3.6$ tonnes of material on $24$ pallets. The South depot shipped $2{,}400\,\mathrm{kg}$ on $30$ pallets. Calculate how many kilograms more the North depot shipped per pallet on average.

$$
\begin{aligned}
3.6\cdot 1{,}000 &= 3{,}600\,\mathrm{kg} \\
\frac{3{,}600}{24} &= 150\,\mathrm{kg} \\
\frac{2{,}400}{30} &= 80\,\mathrm{kg} \\
150-80 &= 70\,\mathrm{kg}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

One tonne equals $1{,}000\,\mathrm{kg}$, so the North depot shipped $3{,}600\,\mathrm{kg}$. The requested quantity is the mean load per pallet, so divide each load by the number of pallets in the same shipment. The difference between the two averages is $70\,\mathrm{kg}$ per pallet.

</div>
