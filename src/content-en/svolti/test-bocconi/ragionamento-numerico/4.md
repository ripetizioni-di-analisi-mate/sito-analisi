## Exercise 1

On the morning shift, three staff each work for two hours and pack $90$ parcels. On the evening shift, four staff each work for two hours and pack $112$ parcels. Identify the shift with more parcels packed per person-hour and find the difference between the rates.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{90}{3\cdot 2} &= 15 \\
\frac{112}{4\cdot 2} &= 14 \\
15-14 &= 1
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

One hour worked by one employee equals one person-hour. Adding up the staff hours gives $6$ for the morning shift and $8$ for the evening shift. Dividing each parcel count by its person-hours gives comparable rates. The morning shift packed **one more parcel per person-hour**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A supplier reports $24$ complaints relating to orders in the first week of June. There were $2{,}400$ orders in June and $640$ in the first week. Each complaint concerns a different order. Calculate the percentage of first-week orders that received a complaint.

$$
\frac{24}{640}\cdot 100=\frac{15}{4}=3.75.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The numerator counts first-week orders. The denominator with the same scope is therefore $640$. Multiplying the ratio by $100$ gives the percentage. Orders that received a complaint made up $3.75\%$ of first-week orders.

</div>
