## Exercise 1

Alba's annual production has a base value of $160$ items and a current index of $125$. Corallo's has a base value of $200$ items and a current index of $110$. Determine which produces more and calculate the difference.

$$
\begin{aligned}
160\cdot\frac{125}{100} &= 200 \quad \text{(Alba)} \\
200\cdot\frac{110}{100} &= 220 \quad \text{(Corallo)} \\
220-200 &= 20
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each index expresses a ratio to its own series' base. Taking $125\%$ of $160$ gives $200$, while taking $110\%$ of $200$ gives $220$. Corallo produces $20$ more items than Alba.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A quoted price receives a $20\%$ discount. A $10\%$ surcharge is then applied to the discounted price. The amount payable is $132\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Find the original price.

$$
\begin{aligned}
0.8\cdot 1.1 &= 0.88 \\
0.88P &= 132\,\mathrm{EUR} \\
P &= \frac{132}{0.88}=150\,\mathrm{EUR}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $P$ be the price before either change. The discount leaves $80\%$ of $P$. The $10\%$ surcharge applies to that new amount, resulting in $88\%$ of $P$. Dividing the payable amount by $0.88$ gives $150\,\mathrm{EUR}$. A direct check gives $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ after the discount and $132\,\mathrm{EUR}$ after the surcharge.

</div>
