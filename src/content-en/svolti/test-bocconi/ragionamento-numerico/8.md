## Exercise 1

A process requires approval of at least $74\%$ of $145$ forms. So far $106$ forms have been approved. Find how many more approvals are needed.

$$
\begin{aligned}
0.74\cdot 145 &= 107.3 \\
\lceil 107.3\rceil &= 108 \\
108-106 &= 2
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The numerical threshold is $107.3$, while the forms are counted in whole numbers. At least $108$ approvals are therefore needed. Compared with the $106$ already recorded, $2$ more approvals are required.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A car travels between $25.6$ and $26.4\,\mathrm{km}$ in between $28$ and $32$ minutes. Determine whether its average speed is at least $45\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and whether it can reach $60\,\mathrm{km/h}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
45\cdot\frac{32}{60} &= 24\,\mathrm{km} \\
60\cdot\frac{28}{60} &= 28\,\mathrm{km} \\
25.6 &> 24 \\
26.4 &< 28
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Over the maximum duration of $32$ minutes, reaching $45\,\mathrm{km/h}$ would require only $24\,\mathrm{km}$. The actual distance is at least $25.6\,\mathrm{km}$, so the average speed exceeds $45\,\mathrm{km/h}$. Even over the minimum duration of $28$ minutes, travelling at $60\,\mathrm{km/h}$ would require $28\,\mathrm{km}$. The actual distance is at most $26.4\,\mathrm{km}$, so the speed stays below $60\,\mathrm{km/h}$.

</div>
