## Exercise 1

An apartment complex has $46$ units. The table groups them by the number of bedrooms and whether they have a terrace. Calculate the fraction of units with a terrace that have two bedrooms.

| Unit | With terrace | Without terrace | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| One bedroom | 9 | 11 | 20 |
| Two bedrooms | ? | 13 | 26 |
| Total | 22 | 24 | 46 |

$$
\begin{aligned}
26-13 &= 13 \\
\frac{13}{22} &= \frac{\text{units with a terrace and two bedrooms}}{\text{units with a terrace}}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Of the $26$ two-bedroom units, $13$ have no terrace. The other $13$ occupy the cell that meets both conditions. There are $22$ units with a terrace, the marginal total of the chosen column. The requested fraction is therefore $\dfrac{13}{22}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Of the $92$ subscribers to a service, $58$ used the mobile app, $47$ used the web portal and $24$ used both. Calculate how many subscribers used neither channel.

$$
\begin{aligned}
58+47-24 &= 81 \\
92-81 &= 11
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The sum $58+47$ counts each subscriber who used both channels twice. Subtract the $24$ subscribers in the overlap to obtain the number who used at least one channel. The complement within the $92$ subscribers contains $11$ people.

</div>
