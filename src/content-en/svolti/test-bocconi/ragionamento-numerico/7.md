## Exercise 1

Fifteen interviews in group A last an average of $12$ minutes each. Ten interviews in group B last an average of $22$ minutes each. Calculate the mean duration across the $25$ interviews.

$$
\begin{aligned}
15\cdot 12+10\cdot 22 &= 180+220=400\,\mathrm{min} \\
\frac{400}{25} &= 16\,\mathrm{min}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Multiplying a group's size by its mean recovers the total duration of its interviews. Add the group durations and divide by the total number of interviews. Each interview has the same weight, so the larger group contributes more to the mean of $16$ minutes.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Two plants produce $160$ components in total. The first plant's pass rate in testing is $70\%$, and the second plant's is $90\%$. The combined rate is exactly $85\%$. Calculate how many components the first plant produces.

$$
\begin{aligned}
0.7x+0.9(160-x) &= 0.85\cdot 160 \\
144-0.2x &= 136 \\
0.2x &= 8 \\
x &= 40
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $x$ be the number of components produced by the first plant. The second plant makes $160-x$. Add the numbers of components passing at the two plants and set the total equal to $85\%$ of $160$. The first plant makes $40$ components. With this split, $28$ components from the first plant and $108$ from the second pass, for a total of $136$.

</div>
