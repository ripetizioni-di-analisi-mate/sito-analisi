## Exercise 1

A string of length $L=0.96\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at both ends has wave speed $u=120\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the frequency of the third harmonic.

$$
\lambda_3=\frac{2L}{3}=\frac{2\cdot0.96}{3}\,\mathrm{m}=0.64\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

$$
f_3=\frac{u}{\lambda_3}=\frac{120}{0.64}\,\mathrm{Hz}=187.5\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The ends are nodes. The third harmonic has three antinodes, so three intervals between successive nodes fill the string. Each occupies $0.32\,\mathrm{m}$ and is half a wavelength, so $\lambda_3=0.64\,\mathrm{m}$. Divide the wave speed by that length to find the frequency. The result is three times the fundamental because the wave speed is the same and the wavelength is one third as large.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A string of length $L=1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at both ends has wave speed $u=120\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the fundamental frequency, the third-harmonic frequency, and the third-harmonic wavelength.

$$
f_1=\frac{u}{2L}=\frac{120}{2\cdot1.20}\,\mathrm{Hz}=50\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$

$$
f_3=3f_1=150\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$

$$
\lambda_3=\frac{u}{f_3}=\frac{120}{150}\,\mathrm{m}=0.80\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the fundamental, the string contains only one interval between nodes and therefore half a wavelength. Dividing the speed by $2L$ gives $f_1$. In the same medium the third harmonic has three times the frequency and one third the wavelength. As a check, adding three intervals of $\dfrac{0.80}{2}=0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ gives the full string length.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ string fixed at both ends with $u=120\,\mathrm{m/s}$ shows three internal nodes in a single standing mode. Find the mode frequency and the three node positions.

::grafico{f="sin(4*pi*x/1.2)" xmin=0 xmax=1.2 ymin=-1.2 ymax=1.3 nomi="x,y/A" punti="0,0,pieno;0.3,0,pieno;0.6,0,pieno;0.9,0,pieno;1.2,0,pieno" titolo="Five nodes, including the two ends, enclose four intervals. Horizontal coordinates in meters."}

$$
n=3+1=4,\qquad \frac{\lambda_4}{2}=\frac{L}{4}=0.30\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

$$
f_4=\frac{120}{0.60}\,\mathrm{Hz}=200\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$

$$
x_1=0.30\,\mathrm{m},\quad x_2=0.60\,\mathrm{m},\quad x_3=0.90\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Including the end nodes gives five nodes. They enclose four equal intervals, each $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ long, so this is the fourth mode. The distance to the next node is half a wavelength, and the three internal nodes lie at the first three multiples of that distance from $x=0$. The fourth multiple reaches the right end, providing a check.

</div>
