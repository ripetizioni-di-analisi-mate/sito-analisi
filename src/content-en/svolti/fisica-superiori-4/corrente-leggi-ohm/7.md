## Exercise 1

A device draws an average of $0.96\,\mathrm{kW}$ for $2.5\,\mathrm{h}$. Find its energy use in kilowatt-hours.

$$
E=P_{\mathrm{avg}}\Delta t=0.96\cdot2.5\,\mathrm{kWh}=2.4\,\mathrm{kWh}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Average power is the equivalent constant power over the same interval. Power is already in kilowatts and time in hours, so the product directly gives kilowatt-hours. A $1\,\mathrm{kW}$ appliance over the same time would use $2.5\,\mathrm{kWh}$, slightly more than this result.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A device draws $1.2\,\mathrm{kW}$ for $2.5\,\mathrm{h}$. Find its energy use in kilowatt-hours and in joules.

$$
E=1.2\cdot2.5\,\mathrm{kWh}=3\,\mathrm{kWh}.
$$

$$
E=3\cdot3.6\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{J}=1.08\cdot10^7\,\mathrm{J}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The initial product uses kilowatts and hours. To express the same energy in joules, multiply by the number of joules per kilowatt-hour. This factor converts both kilowatts to watts and hours to seconds. The result also equals $1200\,\mathrm{J/s}$ for $9000\,\mathrm{s}$.

</div>
