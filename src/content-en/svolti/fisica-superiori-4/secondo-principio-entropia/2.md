## Exercise 1

A heat engine absorbs $Q_h=1440\,\mathrm{J}$ per cycle and has an efficiency of $\eta=0.28$. Calculate the work it produces in one cycle.

::grafico{f="0.28*x" punti="1440,403.2,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=1900 ymin=0 ymax=600 nomi="Qₕ,W (J)" titolo="Work per cycle at fixed efficiency. The marked point corresponds to $Q_h=1440\,\mathrm{J}$."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\eta&=\frac{W}{Q_h},\\[6pt]
W&=\eta Q_h=0.28\cdot1440\,\mathrm{J}=403.2\,\mathrm{J}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The efficiency expresses how much work is delivered per joule absorbed. Here the engine delivers $0.28\,\mathrm{J}$ of work for each joule that enters. Therefore, given the total energy absorbed, multiply $1440\,\mathrm{J}$ by the fraction $0.28$. The product is $403.2\,\mathrm{J}$, less than $1440\,\mathrm{J}$ as expected for an efficiency below one.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A heat engine absorbs $Q_h=1800\,\mathrm{J}$ per cycle and has an efficiency of $\eta=0.28$. Calculate the work produced and the heat rejected to the cold reservoir.

$$
\begin{aligned}
W &=\eta Q_h=0.28\cdot1800\,\mathrm{J}=504\,\mathrm{J},\\[6pt]
Q_c &=Q_h-W=(1800-504)\,\mathrm{J}=1296\,\mathrm{J}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The engine obtains work from the energy absorbed in the proportion given by $\eta$. It therefore produces $504\,\mathrm{J}$ of work per cycle. By the first law the remaining share of the heat absorbed is rejected to the cold reservoir, because the working fluid accumulates no energy from one cycle to the next. The rejected fraction is $1-\eta=0.72$ and $0.72\cdot1800\,\mathrm{J}=1296\,\mathrm{J}$, in agreement with the subtraction. For the working fluid, the signed heat during rejection is $-1296\,\mathrm{J}$.

</div>
