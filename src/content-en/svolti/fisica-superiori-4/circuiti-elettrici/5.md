## Exercise 1

A current of $5\,\mathrm{mA}$ enters a node, and a current of $9\,\mathrm{mA}$ leaves. For the third branch, take the outward direction as positive. Find the signed current and its actual direction.

::schema{riferimento=0 punti="0,0,A,pieno" linee="-3,0 3,0;0,0 0,-2" frecce="-2.8,0.65,-0.9,0.65,5 mA;0.85,0.65,2.7,0.65,9 mA;0.55,-0.2,0.55,-1.8,I_3" titolo="The arrows show the directions used in the balance at A"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
5\,\mathrm{mA}&=9\,\mathrm{mA}+I_3,\\[6pt]
I_3&=-4\,\mathrm{mA}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

**Kirchhoff's current law** sets the incoming current equal to the outgoing current. Put $I_3$ on the outgoing side because that is the direction of its reference arrow. The calculation shows that $I_3$ contributes negatively to that side. The actual $4\,\mathrm{mA}$ therefore enters the node and adds to the other $5\,\mathrm{mA}$ entering it. The incoming total equals the outgoing $9\,\mathrm{mA}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

One loop contains opposing ideal sources of $15\,\mathrm V$ and $9\,\mathrm V$ in series with $2\,\Omega$ and $4\,\Omega$ resistors. The positive direction crosses the $9\,\mathrm V$ source from its negative to its positive terminal. Find the signed current and the power absorbed by the smaller source.

$$
\begin{aligned}
9\,\mathrm V-15\,\mathrm V-(6\,\Omega)I &= 0,\\[6pt]
I &= \frac{-6\,\mathrm V}{6\,\Omega}=-1\,\mathrm A,\\[6pt]
P_{9} &=(9\,\mathrm V)(1\,\mathrm A)=9\,\mathrm W.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the reference direction, the $9\,\mathrm V$ source raises the potential and the $15\,\mathrm V$ source lowers it. By **Kirchhoff's voltage law**, their algebraic sum must balance the resistive drops along the same traversal. The sign of $I$ shows that the $15\,\mathrm V$ source drives the actual current. Therefore current enters the smaller source at its positive terminal and it absorbs $9\,\mathrm W$. The resistors dissipate $6\,\mathrm W$, and the larger source supplies the $15\,\mathrm W$ required by the balance.

</div>
