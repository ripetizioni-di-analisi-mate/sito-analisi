## Exercise 1

A gas increases its internal energy by $336\,\mathrm{J}$ and does $180\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. Calculate the heat exchanged.

::grafico{linee="2,1 5,1 5,4 2,4 2,1" vettori="0.25,2.15,1.95,2.15,Q;5.05,2.15,6.75,2.15,W" etichette="3.5,2.35,ΔU" xmin=0 xmax=7 ymin=0 ymax=5 assi=0 titolo="The balance divides the energy received as heat between increased internal energy and work."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q&=\Delta U+W \\[6pt]
&=(336+180)\,\mathrm{J}=516\,\mathrm{J}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the equation $\Delta U=Q-W$, heat is the unknown. Move the term $-W$ to the left-hand side and add the **increase in internal energy** to **the work done by the gas**. The result is positive. A total of $516\,\mathrm{J}$ entered as heat, of which $180\,\mathrm{J}$ then passed to the surroundings as work. The remaining $336\,\mathrm{J}$ matches the measured change in internal energy.

</div>

## Exercise 2

During compression, the surroundings do $640\,\mathrm{J}$ of work on the gas. The internal energy increases by $420\,\mathrm{J}$. Calculate the heat exchanged and the final internal energy if the initial value is $500\,\mathrm{J}$ in the chosen reference.

$$
\begin{aligned}
W&=-640\,\mathrm{J}, \\[6pt]
Q&=\Delta U+W=(420-640)\,\mathrm{J} \\[6pt]
&=-220\,\mathrm{J}, \\[6pt]
U_f&=U_i+\Delta U=(500+420)\,\mathrm{J} \\[6pt]
&=920\,\mathrm{J}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The work is done on the gas, so energy enters the system as work and $W$ is negative. Compression supplies $640\,\mathrm{J}$, of which $420\,\mathrm{J}$ increases the internal energy. The gas therefore **releases** the remaining $220\,\mathrm{J}$ as heat. To find $U_f$, add only the increase in internal energy to $U_i$, since that increase already includes the effects of both transfers.

</div>
