## Exercise 1

Two initially uncharged capacitors of $3$ and $6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ are connected in series to a $12\,\mathrm V$ source. Find the equivalent capacitance, the charge magnitude, and the voltages.

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_{\mathrm{eq}}&=\frac{3\cdot6}{3+6}\,\mathrm{\mu F}=2\,\mathrm{\mu F}, \\[6pt]
Q&=C_{\mathrm{eq}}\Delta V=24\,\mathrm{\mu C}, \\[6pt]
\Delta V_1&=\frac{24}{3}\,\mathrm V=8\,\mathrm V,\qquad \Delta V_2=\frac{24}{6}\,\mathrm V=4\,\mathrm V.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The node common to both capacitors is isolated and initially neutral. The charges on its two plates therefore have equal magnitudes and opposite signs. The quantity $24\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ is the magnitude for each capacitor. Dividing that charge by the respective capacitance gives a greater drop across the $3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor. As a check, the two voltages add to $12\,\mathrm V$ and the equivalent capacitance is smaller than either one.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A $2\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor is in series with a group consisting of $3$ and $5\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ in parallel. The network is initially uncharged and is connected to $20\,\mathrm V$. Find the voltage across the group and the charge magnitude on the $5\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor.

::grafico{linee="0.5,2 1.2,2;1.2,1.4 1.2,2.6;1.6,1.4 1.6,2.6;1.6,2 2.4,2;2.4,1 2.4,3;2.4,3 3.3,3;2.4,1 3.3,1;3.3,2.55 3.3,3.45;3.7,2.55 3.7,3.45;3.3,0.55 3.3,1.45;3.7,0.55 3.7,1.45;3.7,3 4.6,3;3.7,1 4.6,1;4.6,1 4.6,3;4.6,2 5.5,2" etichette="1.4,3,2 μF;3.5,3.9,3 μF;3.5,0.18,5 μF" punti="2.4,2,pieno,A;4.6,2,pieno,B" xmin=0 xmax=6 ymin=0 ymax=4.5 assi=0 titolo="The 3- and 5-microfarad elements are on branches between A and B."}

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_{\mathrm{par}}&=(3+5)\,\mathrm{\mu F}=8\,\mathrm{\mu F}, \\[6pt]
C_{\mathrm{eq}}&=\frac{2\cdot8}{2+8}\,\mathrm{\mu F}=1.6\,\mathrm{\mu F}, \\[6pt]
Q_{\mathrm{ser}}&=1.6\cdot20\,\mathrm{\mu C}=32\,\mathrm{\mu C}, \\[6pt]
\Delta V_{\mathrm{par}}&=\frac{32}{8}\,\mathrm V=4\,\mathrm V, \\[6pt]
Q_5&=5\cdot4\,\mathrm{\mu C}=20\,\mathrm{\mu C}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The $3$ and $5\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitors share both nodes, so they reduce to a capacitance of $8\,\mathrm{\mu F}$. The equivalent capacitor of the group is in series with the $2\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ one, so it has the common series charge magnitude. This gives $4\,\mathrm V$ across the group. Inside it, both capacitors have that voltage and the charge divides according to capacitance. As a check, $12+20=32$ microcoulombs gives the charge of the whole group.

</div>
