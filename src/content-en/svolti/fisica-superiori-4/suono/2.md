## Exercise 1

A source and a microphone are at the same point. A pulse directed toward a wall returns after $\Delta t=0.28\,\mathrm s$. With $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$, find the distance $d$ to the wall.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2d&=u\Delta t
=(340\,\mathrm{m/s})(0.28\,\mathrm s)
=95.2\,\mathrm m, \\[6pt]
d&=\frac{95.2\,\mathrm m}{2}
=47.6\,\mathrm m.
\end{aligned}
$$

The pulse reaches wall P and returns to S."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The timer starts at emission and stops when the pulse returns. The sound first travels $d$ to the wall and then another $d$ to the microphone. Since the speed is constant, the product $u\Delta t$ gives the full path length $2d$. The required distance is half of that.

As a check, the outward trip takes $\dfrac{47.6}{340}\,\mathrm s=0.14\,\mathrm s$. The return takes the same time, so the predicted delay is $0.28\,\mathrm s$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A reflected pulse returns to the emission point after $0.35\,\mathrm s$. With $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$, find the total path length and the distance to the wall. Also determine when the pulse reaches the wall.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\ell&=u\Delta t=340\cdot0.35\,\mathrm m=119\,\mathrm m, \\[6pt]
d&=\frac{\ell}{2}=59.5\,\mathrm m, \\[6pt]
t_{\mathrm r}&=\frac{d}{u}=\frac{59.5}{340}\,\mathrm s
=0.175\,\mathrm s.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The path length $\ell$ includes two equal segments. The reflection point therefore divides the route into two segments of $59.5\,\mathrm m$. The sound has the same speed on both segments, so reflection takes place halfway through the measured time.

A microphone at the wall would detect the outward wavefront at $0.175\,\mathrm s$. The microphone next to the source detects the reflected wavefront at the end of the second segment.

</div>
