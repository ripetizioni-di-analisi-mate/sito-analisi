## Exercise 1

A sound has frequency $f=400\,\mathrm{Hz}$ in a medium where its speed is $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the wavelength.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda &= \frac{u}{f} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{340\,\mathrm{m/s}}{400\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}} \\[6pt]
&= 0.85\,\mathrm m .
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{f="cos(2*pi*(x-0.2)/0.85)" xmin=0 xmax=1.28 ymin=-1.3 ymax=1.35 nomi="x,Δp (Pa)" punti="0.2,1,pieno;1.05,1,pieno" segmenti="0.2,0,0.2,1;1.05,0,1.05,1" legenda="\Delta p(x)" titolo="The two pressure maxima are separated by one wavelength, $0.85\,\mathrm m$."}

<div class="spiegazione">

In one second the wavefront travels $340\,\mathrm m$ and the source completes $400$ oscillations. During each oscillation the front advances by one wavelength. Therefore divide the speed by the frequency. The factor $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$ cancels, leaving meters.

In the instantaneous profile, points in the same state of compression are maxima of the curve. The two marked maxima at $0.20\,\mathrm m$ and $1.05\,\mathrm m$ are exactly $0.85\,\mathrm m$ apart.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A $500\,\mathrm{Hz}$ sound travels through the same medium at $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the wavelength and the period measured by a stationary microphone.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{1}{500\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}}
=0.0020\,\mathrm s, \\[6pt]
\lambda &= uT
=(340\,\mathrm{m/s})(0.0020\,\mathrm s)
=0.68\,\mathrm m .
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The microphone remains at a fixed point and measures the time between two compressions. Frequency gives the number of compressions arriving in one second, so the interval is the reciprocal of the frequency, or $2.0\,\mathrm{ms}$. Between arrivals, a front advances by $uT$, the spatial separation between compressions.

Compared with a $400\,\mathrm{Hz}$ tone in the same medium, the period is shorter by a factor of $\dfrac{400}{500}$. The speed is the same, so the wavelength is reduced by the same factor.

</div>
