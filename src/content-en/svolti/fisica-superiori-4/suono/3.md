## Exercise 1

An isotropic point source emits sound power $P=0.48\,\mathrm W$. In a free field, with no absorption or reflections, find the intensity at $r=3\,\mathrm m$ and at $r=6\,\mathrm m$.

$$
I=\frac{P}{A}
\qquad
A=4\pi r^2
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_3&=4\pi(3\,\mathrm m)^2=36\pi\,\mathrm{m^2}\simeq113.1\,\mathrm{m^2}, \\[6pt]
I_3&=\frac{P}{A_3}=\frac{0.48\,\mathrm W}{36\pi\,\mathrm{m^2}} \\[6pt]
&\simeq4.24\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}, \\[6pt]
I_6&=\frac{P}{4\pi(6\,\mathrm m)^2}=\frac{0.48\,\mathrm W}{144\pi\,\mathrm{m^2}} \\[6pt]
&\simeq1.06\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}=\frac{I_3}{4}.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{f="9/x^2" xmin=2.65 xmax=8 ymin=0 ymax=1.35 nomi="r,I/I₃" punti="3,1,pieno;6,0.25,pieno" segmenti="6,0,6,0.25;3,0,3,1" legenda="I(r)/I_3" titolo="$I_3$ is the intensity at $3\,\mathrm m$. At $6\,\mathrm m$ the ratio is $\dfrac14$."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The points at distance $3\,\mathrm m$ form a sphere centered on the source. Since the source is isotropic, each square meter of the sphere receives the same share of power. The intensity is therefore the ratio of the power to the area $4\pi r^2$ of the sphere, and is measured in $\mathrm{W/m^2}$. The area is $36\pi\,\mathrm{m^2}\simeq113.1\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the quotient $\dfrac{0.48}{113.1}$ gives **$4.24\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$**.

The graph uses this value as a reference. At $6\,\mathrm m$ the radius is doubled and the area, proportional to $r^2$, is four times larger, that is, $144\pi\,\mathrm{m^2}$, while the power is the same. The intensity is therefore $\dfrac{I_3}{4}\simeq1.06\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$, and the same value is obtained by dividing $0.48\,\mathrm W$ by $144\pi\,\mathrm{m^2}$.

The power that passes through the sphere of radius $3\,\mathrm m$ and the power that passes through the sphere of radius $6\,\mathrm m$ are both $0.48\,\mathrm W$, because in a free field there is no absorption. The product $I\cdot A$ therefore remains constant, which explains the dependence on $\dfrac{1}{r^2}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An isotropic point source emits $0.60\,\mathrm W$ in a free field, with no absorption or reflections. Find the intensity and the level at $3\,\mathrm m$, with $I_0=10^{-12}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. By how much does the level change at $6\,\mathrm m$?

$$
I=\frac{P}{4\pi r^2}
\qquad
\beta=10\log_{10}\frac{I}{I_0}\,\mathrm{dB}
\qquad
\beta_2-\beta_1=10\log_{10}\frac{I_2}{I_1}\,\mathrm{dB}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
I_3&=\frac{0.60}{36\pi}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}
\simeq 5.31\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}, \\[6pt]
\frac{I_3}{I_0}&=5.31\cdot10^{9}, \\[6pt]
\beta_3&=10\log_{10}(5.31\cdot10^9)\,\mathrm{dB}
=10\,(\log_{10}5.31+9)\,\mathrm{dB}
\simeq 97.25\,\mathrm{dB}, \\[6pt]
\frac{I_6}{I_3}&=\left(\frac{3}{6}\right)^2=\frac14, \\[6pt]
\beta_6-\beta_3&=10\log_{10}\!\left(\frac14\right)\,\mathrm{dB}
\simeq-6.02\,\mathrm{dB}, \\[6pt]
\beta_6&\simeq97.25\,\mathrm{dB}-6.02\,\mathrm{dB}=91.23\,\mathrm{dB}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The ratio of power to spherical surface gives the physical intensity at the point, $5.31\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. To compute the level, divide that intensity by $I_0$ and take the logarithm of the dimensionless ratio. Since $\log_{10}(5.31\cdot10^9)=\log_{10}5.31+9\simeq0.725+9=9.725$, multiplying by $10$ gives **$97.25\,\mathrm{dB}$**.

For the second microphone the quantity of interest is the change, which follows from the difference between levels referred to the same $I_0$, because $I_0$ cancels in the ratio. Since the power stays fixed and the radius doubles, the area is four times larger and the intensity is one quarter of the initial one, as the ratio $\left(\dfrac{3}{6}\right)^2$ shows. The logarithm of one quarter is negative and equals about $-0.602$, so the level decreases by $6.02\,\mathrm{dB}$.

The unit $\mathrm{dB}$ goes with the difference between levels, while the factor of one quarter refers to the intensities. At $6\,\mathrm m$ the level is **$91.23\,\mathrm{dB}$**.

</div>
