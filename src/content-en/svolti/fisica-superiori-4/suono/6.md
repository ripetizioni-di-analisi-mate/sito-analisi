## Exercise 1

An ideal pipe of length $L=0.60\,\mathrm m$ is open at both ends. With $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and negligible end corrections, find the fundamental frequency.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_1&=2L=1.20\,\mathrm m, \\[6pt]
f_1&=\frac{u}{\lambda_1}=\frac{340}{1.20}\,\mathrm{Hz}
\simeq283.3\,\mathrm{Hz}.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{curve="t,cos(pi*t/0.6),0,0.6" xmin=-0.035 xmax=0.68 ymin=-1.3 ymax=1.3 nomi="x,s/sₘₐₓ" punti="0,1,pieno;0.3,0,pieno;0.6,-1,pieno" legenda="s(x)/s_{\max}" titolo="The fundamental has a displacement node at $x=0.30\,\mathrm m$ and an antinode at each end."}

<div class="spiegazione">

At both openings the air can move along the pipe, so the displacement profile has an antinode at each end. The fundamental is the mode with the fewest interior nodes. It has one node at the midpoint and two antinodes a half wavelength apart. From $L=\dfrac{\lambda_1}{2}$, it follows that $\lambda_1=2L$. Dividing the sound speed by this wavelength gives the frequency. The opposite signs at the two ends of the graph represent displacements in opposite directions at the same instant.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An ideal pipe open at both ends is $0.75\,\mathrm m$ long. Sound travels at $340\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting end corrections, find the fundamental and third-harmonic frequencies. How many interior displacement nodes does the third harmonic have?

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_1&=\frac{340}{2\cdot0.75}\,\mathrm{Hz}
\simeq226.7\,\mathrm{Hz}, \\[6pt]
\lambda_3&=\frac{2L}{3}=0.50\,\mathrm m, \\[6pt]
f_3&=\frac{340}{0.50}\,\mathrm{Hz}
=680\,\mathrm{Hz}.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{curve="t,cos(4*pi*t),0,0.75" xmin=-0.034 xmax=0.81 ymin=-1.3 ymax=1.3 nomi="x,s/sₘₐₓ" punti="0.125,0,pieno;0.375,0,pieno;0.625,0,pieno" legenda="s(x)/s_{\max}" titolo="The three interior displacement nodes of the third mode are marked."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The third mode fits three half wavelengths inside the pipe. Each half-wave segment between successive antinodes contains a displacement node. There are therefore three interior nodes, with the first $\dfrac{\lambda_3}{4}=0.125\,\mathrm m$ from the left end. They are separated by $\dfrac{\lambda_3}{2}=0.25\,\mathrm m$, so the other two are at $0.375\,\mathrm m$ and $0.625\,\mathrm m$.

The exact third-harmonic frequency is three times the fundamental. Use the exact ratio without multiplying the already rounded value of $f_1$.

</div>
