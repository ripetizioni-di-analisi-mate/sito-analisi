## Exercise 1

A converging lens has focal length $f=0.12\,\mathrm m$. A real object is at distance $p=0.288\,\mathrm m$. Find the position of the image.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{q}&=\frac{1}{f}-\frac{1}{p}
            =\frac{p-f}{fp}\\[6pt]
q&=\frac{0.12\cdot0.288}{0.288-0.12}\,\mathrm m
  \simeq0.206\,\mathrm m.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Start from the lens equation and isolate the reciprocal of $q$. The denominator $p-f$ is positive because the object lies beyond the front focus. Thus $q$ is positive and the image forms on the side opposite the object. Since $p>2f$, the image must be between $f$ and $2f$. Indeed, $0.12\,\mathrm m<q<0.24\,\mathrm m$. The screen therefore belongs about $20.6\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the lens on the exit side.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A converging lens has focal length $f=0.12\,\mathrm m$ and an object at distance $p=0.36\,\mathrm m$. Calculate the image position and the signed magnification.

$$
\begin{aligned}
q&=\frac{0.12\cdot0.36}{0.36-0.12}\,\mathrm m
  =0.18\,\mathrm m\\[6pt]
M&=-\frac{0.18}{0.36}=-0.50.
\end{aligned}
$$

The central ray and the ray bent toward the focus meet $0.18\,\mathrm m$ from the lens."}

<div class="spiegazione">

At $p=3f$, the denominator $p-f$ is $2f$, so the image distance is $\dfrac{3f}{2}$. The ray through the optical center slopes down from the tip of the object and continues below the axis on the image side. The two triangles along this ray have bases $p$ and $q$, so the ratio of the absolute heights is $\dfrac{q}{p}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. The sign of $M$ indicates that the tip of the image is below the axis.

</div>
