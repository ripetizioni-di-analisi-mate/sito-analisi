## Exercise 1

A converging lens has $f=0.20\,\mathrm m$. An object is at $p=0.064\,\mathrm m$. Find the signed image distance and identify the side where it appears.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{q}&=\frac{1}{0.20}-\frac{1}{0.064}
            =-10.625\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}\\[6pt]
q&\simeq-0.0941\,\mathrm m.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Since the object is inside the focal length, $\dfrac{1}{p}$ is greater than $\dfrac{1}{f}$. Their difference is therefore negative in the form $\dfrac{1}{q}=\dfrac{1}{f}-\dfrac{1}{p}$. The value $q\simeq-0.0941\,\mathrm m$ places the virtual image about $9.41\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the lens on the object side. The distances to compare in space are $|q|$ and $p$. Since $|q|>p$, the image appears farther from the lens than the object is.

</div>

## Exercise 2

For a converging lens with $f=0.20\,\mathrm m$, a real object is at $p=0.080\,\mathrm m$. Find $q$ and $M$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
q&=\frac{0.20\cdot0.080}{0.080-0.20}\,\mathrm m
  =-\frac{2}{15}\,\mathrm m\simeq-0.133\,\mathrm m\\[6pt]
M&=-\frac{q}{p}=\frac{5}{3}\simeq+1.67.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-5.6,0 5.6,0;0,-2 0,3" unicolore=1 segmenti="0,1.5,-3.3333,2.5;0,0,-3.3333,2.5;-3.3333,0,-3.3333,2.5" vettori="-2,0,-2,1.5;-2,1.5,0,1.5;0,1.5,5,0;-2,1.5,0,0;0,0,2.5,-1.875" punti="-5,0,pieno,F₁;5,0,pieno,F₂" etichette="-4.2,2.8,virtual image;-2.1,-0.6,object" xmin=-6 xmax=6 ymin=-2.3 ymax=3.4 titolo="The image appears on the object side. The dashed extensions intersect at $q\simeq-0.133\,\mathrm m$."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The central ray and the ray bent toward the rear focus diverge after the lens. In the diagram, dashed lines extend them backward to their intersection. That intersection gives $q<0$. Virtual images from a converging lens with the object inside the focal length have $M>1$. In this case an object $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ high would give an upright image $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ high because $3\cdot\dfrac{5}{3}=5$.

</div>
