## Exercise 1

A converging lens has $f=0.20\,\mathrm m$. An object is at $p=0.064\,\mathrm m$. Calculate the signed image distance and identify the side on which it appears.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{q}&=\frac{1}{f}-\frac{1}{p}\\[6pt]
&=\left(\frac{1}{0.20}-\frac{1}{0.064}\right)\mathrm{m^{-1}}\\[6pt]
&=(5.000-15.625)\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}=-10.625\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}\\[6pt]
q&=\frac{1}{-10.625\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}}\simeq-0.0941\,\mathrm m\\[6pt]
M&=-\frac{q}{p}=\frac{0.0941}{0.064}\simeq+1.47.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

We apply the lens equation with $f>0$ and $p>0$. Since $p=0.064\,\mathrm m$ is less than $f=0.20\,\mathrm m$, the object is inside the focus and $\dfrac{1}{p}$ is greater than $\dfrac{1}{f}$. The difference $\dfrac{1}{f}-\dfrac{1}{p}$ is therefore negative and $q<0$. A negative value of $q$ indicates a virtual image, formed by the backward extensions of the rays on the object side, at $|q|\simeq9.41\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the lens. Since $|q|>p$, the image appears farther from the lens than the object. The magnification $M=-\dfrac{q}{p}$ is positive and greater than $1$, so the image is upright and enlarged by a factor of about $1.47$. An object inside the focal length of a converging lens therefore has a virtual, upright, enlarged image, about $9.41\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the lens on the object side. This is how a magnifying glass works. With the object exactly at the focus, $p=0.20\,\mathrm m$, the denominator $p-f$ would vanish and the image would form at infinity.

</div>

## Exercise 2

For a converging lens with $f=0.20\,\mathrm m$, a real object $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall is at $p=0.080\,\mathrm m$. Calculate $q$, $M$, and the image height.

$$
\begin{aligned}
q&=\frac{fp}{p-f}=\frac{0.20\cdot0.080}{0.080-0.20}\,\mathrm m\\[6pt]
&=\frac{0.016}{-0.12}\,\mathrm m=-\frac{2}{15}\,\mathrm m\simeq-0.133\,\mathrm m\\[6pt]
M&=-\frac{q}{p}=\frac{0.133}{0.080}=\frac{5}{3}\simeq+1.67\\[6pt]
h'&=Mh=\frac{5}{3}\cdot3\,\mathrm{cm}=5\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$

The dashed extensions meet at $q\simeq-0.133\,\mathrm m$."}

<div class="spiegazione">

Here too $p=0.080\,\mathrm m$ is less than $f=0.20\,\mathrm m$, so the denominator $p-f$ is negative and $q<0$. The ray parallel to the axis is deflected toward the rear focus, while the central ray continues undeviated. After the lens the two rays diverge and do not meet. In the figure the dashed lines extend them backward to their meeting point, and this intersection, at $q=-\dfrac{2}{15}\,\mathrm m$ on the object side, is the virtual image. The virtual images of a converging lens with the object inside the focus have $M>1$. Since $M=\dfrac{5}{3}$ is the ratio of the heights, an object $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall has an upright image $\dfrac{5}{3}\cdot3\,\mathrm{cm}=5\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall. The image is therefore virtual, upright, and enlarged, about $13.3\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the lens.

</div>
