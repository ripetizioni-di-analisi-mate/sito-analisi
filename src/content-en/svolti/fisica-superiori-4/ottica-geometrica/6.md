## Exercise 1

A diverging lens has focal length $f=-0.15\,\mathrm m$. The real object is at distance $p=0.24\,\mathrm m$. Calculate $q$ and state whether the image lies between the lens and the front focus.

$$
\begin{aligned}
q&=\frac{fp}{p-f}\\[6pt]
&=\frac{(-0.15)\cdot0.24}{0.24-(-0.15)}\,\mathrm m\\[6pt]
&=\frac{-0.036}{0.39}\,\mathrm m\simeq-0.0923\,\mathrm m\\[6pt]
|q|&=9.23\,\mathrm{cm}<|f|=15\,\mathrm{cm}\\[6pt]
M&=-\frac{q}{p}=\frac{0.0923}{0.24}\simeq0.385.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-9.2,0 5.4,0;0,-2 0,2" unicolore=1 vettori="-8,0,-8,1.5;-8,1.5,0,1.5;0,1.5,3,2.4;-8,1.5,3,-0.5625" segmenti="0,1.5,-5,0;-3.077,0,-3.077,0.577" punti="-5,0,pieno,F;5,0,pieno,F′;-3.077,0.577,vuoto" etichette="-7.6,2.1,object;-4.4,-0.55,virtual image" xmin=-9.6 xmax=5.8 ymin=-1.4 ymax=2.8 titolo="Diverging lens with $f=-0.15\,\mathrm m$ and object at $0.24\,\mathrm m$. The ray parallel to the axis diverges and its dashed extension passes through $F$. The virtual image lies between the lens and the focus. Heights are enlarged relative to distances."}

<div class="spiegazione">

We apply the lens equation with $f=-0.15\,\mathrm m$, negative because the lens is diverging, and $p=0.24\,\mathrm m$, positive because the object is real. The sign of $f$ appears in both the numerator and the denominator, where $p-f=p+|f|=0.39\,\mathrm m$ is positive. The numerator $fp$ is negative, so $q<0$: the image is virtual and lies on the object side, $9.23\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the lens. Since $9.23\,\mathrm{cm}<15\,\mathrm{cm}$, the image lies between the lens and the front focus. In the figure the ray parallel to the axis diverges after the lens and its extension passes through $F$. This extension meets the central ray at the image. The magnification $M=-\dfrac{q}{p}\simeq0.385$ is positive and less than $1$, so the image is upright and reduced.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An object is at $p=0.30\,\mathrm m$ from a diverging lens of focal length $f=-0.15\,\mathrm m$. Calculate $q$ and $M$, and determine the image height if the object is $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall.

$$
\begin{aligned}
q&=\frac{fp}{p-f}=\frac{(-0.15)\cdot0.30}{0.30-(-0.15)}\,\mathrm m\\[6pt]
&=\frac{-0.045}{0.45}\,\mathrm m=-0.10\,\mathrm m\\[6pt]
M&=-\frac{q}{p}=-\frac{-0.10}{0.30}=\frac{1}{3}\\[6pt]
h'&=Mh=\frac{1}{3}\cdot3\,\mathrm{cm}=1\,\mathrm{cm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The rays leaving a diverging lens move away from the axis and do not meet beyond the lens. Only their extensions meet, on the entrance side, so $q$ is negative and the image is virtual. In the denominator $p-f=0.30\,\mathrm m+0.15\,\mathrm m=0.45\,\mathrm m$, while the numerator is $-0.045\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The distance $|q|=10\,\mathrm{cm}$ is less than $|f|=15\,\mathrm{cm}$, as happens for every real object in front of a diverging lens. The ratio $-\dfrac{q}{p}$ is positive and less than one, so the image is upright and reduced. Since $M$ is a ratio of heights and has no unit, it can be multiplied directly by the height in centimeters. The factor $\dfrac{1}{3}$ turns an object $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall into an image $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall. The result agrees with the formula valid for every diverging lens, $|q|=\dfrac{|f|\,p}{p+|f|}=\dfrac{0.15\cdot0.30}{0.45}\,\mathrm m=0.10\,\mathrm m$.

</div>
