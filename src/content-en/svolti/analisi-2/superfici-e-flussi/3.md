## Exercise 1

Compute the area of the lateral surface of the cone obtained by rotating about the $z$-axis the segment with equations $x = t$, $z = 2t$ for $t \in [0, 3]$.

$$
g(t) = t, \qquad h(t) = 2t, \qquad \sqrt{g'(t)^2 + h'(t)^2} = \sqrt{1 + 4} = \sqrt{5}
$$
$$
\operatorname{Area} = 2\pi \int_0^3 t \, \sqrt{5} \; dt
= 2\pi \sqrt{5} \cdot \frac{9}{2}
= 9 \sqrt{5}\, \pi
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The curve generating the surface is the segment $x = g(t) = t$, $z = h(t) = 2t$, with $g \geq 0$, so the **formula for the area of a surface of revolution** applies. The derivatives are $g' = 1$ and $h' = 2$, therefore the factor $\sqrt{g'^2 + h'^2} = \sqrt{5}$ is constant. The integral reduces to $2\pi\sqrt{5} \int_0^3 t \, dt$, and since $\int_0^3 t \, dt = \dfrac{9}{2}$, the area equals $9\sqrt{5}\,\pi$. The **same value** is obtained from the elementary formula $\pi R L$ for the lateral surface of the cone, with base radius $R = 3$ and slant height $L = \sqrt{9 + 36} = 3\sqrt{5}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Compute the area of the spherical cap
$$
\Sigma = \bigl\{ (x, y, z) : x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 4,\; z \geq 1 \bigr\} .
$$

$$
\mathbf{r}(\varphi, \theta) = (2 \sin\varphi \cos\theta,\; 2 \sin\varphi \sin\theta,\; 2 \cos\varphi),
\qquad
d\sigma = 4 \sin\varphi \; d\varphi \, d\theta
$$
$$
z \geq 1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
2 \cos\varphi \geq 1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
0 \leq \varphi \leq \frac{\pi}{3}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Area}
&= \int_0^{2\pi} \!\! \int_0^{\pi/3} 4 \sin\varphi \; d\varphi \, d\theta \\[6pt]
&= 8\pi \, \bigl[ -\cos\varphi \bigr]_0^{\pi/3}
= 8\pi \left( 1 - \frac{1}{2} \right) = 4\pi
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{xmin=-2.8 xmax=3.2 ymin=-2.4 ymax=2.6 uguale=1 nomi="x,z" curve="2*cos(t),2*sin(t),0.5236,2.618;2*cos(t),2*sin(t),2.618,6.8068;0.5*cos(t),0.5*sin(t),0.5236,1.5708" segmenti="-2.6,1,2.6,1;0,0,1.7321,1" punti="-1.7321,1;1.7321,1" etichette="2.15,1.15,z = 1;0.12,0.62,φ" titolo="Cross-section with the plane $y = 0$: the cap is the part of the sphere with $z \geq 1$, that is, with colatitude $\varphi$ between $0$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The cap is a portion of the sphere of radius $2$, so we use the **spherical parametrization**, whose area element is $d\sigma = R^2 \sin\varphi \, d\varphi \, d\theta = 4 \sin\varphi \, d\varphi \, d\theta$. The condition $z \geq 1$ is translated into the parameters: since $z = 2\cos\varphi$, the inequality is equivalent to $\cos\varphi \geq \dfrac{1}{2}$, that is, $\varphi \in \left[0, \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right]$, while $\theta$ runs over the *full* turn. The integral in $\theta$ gives $2\pi$ and the one in $\varphi$ gives $1 - \cos\dfrac{\pi}{3} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, so the area is $4\pi$. The result is **consistent** with the formula $2\pi R h$ for the area of a cap, with $R = 2$ and height $h = 2 - 1 = 1$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Compute the area of the portion of the cylinder $x^2 + y^2 = 9$ lying between the plane $z = 0$ and the plane $z = x + 5$.

$$
\mathbf{r}(\theta, t) = (3 \cos\theta,\; 3 \sin\theta,\; t), \qquad d\sigma = 3 \; d\theta \, dt
$$
$$
0 \leq t \leq 3\cos\theta + 5, \qquad 3\cos\theta + 5 > 0 \ \text{ for every } \theta
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Area}
&= \int_0^{2\pi} \!\! \int_0^{3\cos\theta + 5} 3 \; dt \, d\theta \\[6pt]
&= 3 \int_0^{2\pi} \bigl( 3\cos\theta + 5 \bigr) \, d\theta \\[6pt]
&= 3 \, \bigl( 0 + 10\pi \bigr) = 30\pi
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{xmin=-0.6 xmax=7 ymin=-0.6 ymax=9 nomi="θ,t" f="3*cos(x)+5+0*sqrt(x*(6.2832-x))" area="3*cos(x)+5;0;0;6.2832" segmenti="6.2832,0,6.2832,8" titolo="The parameter domain of the portion of the cylinder: $0 \leq \theta \leq 2\pi$ and $0 \leq t \leq 3\cos\theta + 5$; the function $3\cos\theta + 5$ lies between $2$ and $8$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The cylinder of radius $3$ is parametrized by the angle $\theta$ and the height $t$, and the area element is $d\sigma = R \, d\theta \, dt = 3 \, d\theta \, dt$, since for the cylinder the norm of the normal vector is the **radius**. On the cylinder $x = 3\cos\theta$, so the upper plane cuts the surface at height $t = 3\cos\theta + 5$; since $3\cos\theta + 5 \geq 2 > 0$ for every $\theta$, the upper height stays *above* the plane $z = 0$ and the parameter region is $0 \leq t \leq 3\cos\theta + 5$ with $\theta \in [0, 2\pi]$. The inner integral in $t$ gives the length $3\cos\theta + 5$ of the generating line. In the integral in $\theta$ the term $3\cos\theta$ has **zero integral** over the period, therefore what remains is $5 \cdot 2\pi = 10\pi$, and the area equals $30\pi$.

</div>
