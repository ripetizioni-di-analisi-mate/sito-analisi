## Exercise 1

Solve graphically the inequality
$$
(x - y)(x^2 + y^2 - 4) > 0 .
$$

$$
\begin{gathered}
F(x, y) = x - y \\[6pt]
G(x, y) = x^2 + y^2 - 4
\end{gathered}
$$
$$
F > 0 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad y < x
$$
$$
G > 0 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x^2 + y^2 > 2^2
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
y = x \\[4pt]
x^2 + y^2 = 4
\end{cases}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
x^2 + x^2 &= 4 \\[6pt]
x^2 &= 2 \\[6pt]
x &= \pm\sqrt{2}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\left( \sqrt{2}, \sqrt{2} \right), \qquad \left( -\sqrt{2}, -\sqrt{2} \right)
$$

$$
\def\arraystretch{1.35}
\begin{array}{l|l|c|c|c}
\text{points} & \text{line} & F & G & F \cdot G \\ \hline
\text{exterior} & \text{below} & + & + & + \\
\text{exterior} & \text{above} & - & + & - \\
\text{interior} & \text{below} & + & - & - \\
\text{interior} & \text{above} & - & - & +
\end{array}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
y < x \\[4pt]
x^2 + y^2 > 4
\end{cases}
\ \text{or} \
\begin{cases}
y > x \\[4pt]
x^2 + y^2 < 4
\end{cases}
$$

::grafico{regione="y<x; x^2+y^2>4 | y>x; x^2+y^2<4" xmin=-4 xmax=4 ymin=-4 ymax=4 uguale=1 punti="1.4142,1.4142,vuoto;-1.4142,-1.4142,vuoto" titolo="$(x - y)(x^2 + y^2 - 4) > 0$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The left-hand side is the product of the two factors $F$ and $G$, so we study the sign of each factor and apply the **rule of signs**. The factor $F = x - y$ vanishes on the line $y = x$ and is positive where $y < x$, that is, below the line. The factor $G = x^2 + y^2 - 4$ vanishes on the circle with center the origin and radius $2$ and is positive where the square of the distance from the origin is greater than $4$, that is, at the exterior points. The line and the circle meet at $\left( \sqrt{2}, \sqrt{2} \right)$ and $\left( -\sqrt{2}, -\sqrt{2} \right)$ and divide the plane into four regions, in each of which the two factors have constant sign. The product is positive where the two factors have the same sign, so the solutions are the **exterior points of the circle that lie below the line and the interior points that lie above the line**. The symbol is $>$, hence the line and the circle are **excluded** and dashed, and their two intersection points do not belong to the solutions either.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve graphically the inequality
$$
\frac{y - x^2}{x - 1} \geq 0 .
$$

$$
\begin{gathered}
N(x, y) = y - x^2 \\[6pt]
D(x, y) = x - 1
\end{gathered}
$$
$$
N > 0 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad y > x^2
$$
$$
D > 0 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x > 1
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
y = x^2 \\[4pt]
x = 1
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad (1, 1)
$$

$$
\def\arraystretch{1.35}
\begin{array}{l|l|c|c|c}
\text{parabola} & \text{line} & N & D & \dfrac{N}{D} \\[6pt] \hline
\text{above} & \text{to the right} & + & + & + \\
\text{above} & \text{to the left} & + & - & - \\
\text{below} & \text{to the right} & - & + & - \\
\text{below} & \text{to the left} & - & - & +
\end{array}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
y \geq x^2 \\[4pt]
x > 1
\end{cases}
\ \text{or} \
\begin{cases}
y \leq x^2 \\[4pt]
x < 1
\end{cases}
$$

::grafico{regione="y>=x^2; x>1 | y<=x^2; x<1" xmin=-3 xmax=4 ymin=-2 ymax=5 uguale=1 punti="1,1,vuoto" titolo="$\dfrac{y - x^2}{x - 1} \geq 0$: parabola included, line $x = 1$ excluded"}

<div class="spiegazione">

A quotient is greater than or equal to zero where numerator and denominator have the same sign and where the numerator vanishes, provided that the denominator is different from zero. The numerator $N = y - x^2$ vanishes on the parabola $y = x^2$, which has its vertex at the origin, and is positive where $y > x^2$, that is, above the parabola. The denominator $D = x - 1$ vanishes on the vertical line $x = 1$ and is positive to the right of the line. The parabola and the line meet at the point $(1, 1)$ and divide the plane into four regions. By the **rule of signs** the quotient is positive **above the parabola to the right of the line and below the parabola to the left of the line**. On the parabola the quotient equals $0$, so the parabola is included and drawn as a solid line. On the line $x = 1$ the denominator vanishes and the quotient is not defined, so the line is **excluded** and dashed. For the same reason the point $(1, 1)$ of the parabola does not belong to the solutions and is drawn as a hollow point.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve graphically the inequality
$$
|x - 1| + |y| < 2 .
$$

We distinguish four cases, according to the sign of $x - 1$ and of $y$.

- $\begin{cases} x \geq 1 \\ y \geq 0 \\ x - 1 + y < 2 \end{cases} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \begin{cases} x \geq 1 \\ y \geq 0 \\ y < 3 - x \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} x \geq 1 \\ y < 0 \\ x - 1 - y < 2 \end{cases} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \begin{cases} x \geq 1 \\ y < 0 \\ y > x - 3 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} x < 1 \\ y \geq 0 \\ 1 - x + y < 2 \end{cases} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \begin{cases} x < 1 \\ y \geq 0 \\ y < x + 1 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} x < 1 \\ y < 0 \\ 1 - x - y < 2 \end{cases} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \begin{cases} x < 1 \\ y < 0 \\ y > -x - 1 \end{cases}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
3 - x = x - 3 &\quad\Longrightarrow\quad (3, 0) \\[6pt]
3 - x = x + 1 &\quad\Longrightarrow\quad (1, 2) \\[6pt]
x + 1 = -x - 1 &\quad\Longrightarrow\quad (-1, 0) \\[6pt]
x - 3 = -x - 1 &\quad\Longrightarrow\quad (1, -2)
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
C(1, 0): \quad |1 - 1| + |0| = 0 < 2
$$

::grafico{regione="abs(x-1)+abs(y)<2" xmin=-3 xmax=5 ymin=-4 ymax=4 uguale=1 punti="1,0,pieno,C" titolo="$|x - 1| + |y| < 2$: square with center $C(1, 0)$, sides excluded"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The absolute value $|x - 1|$ equals $x - 1$ for $x \geq 1$ and $1 - x$ for $x < 1$, while $|y|$ equals $y$ for $y \geq 0$ and $-y$ for $y < 0$, so we distinguish four cases. Each case is a system, formed by the two conditions on the signs and by the inequality written without absolute values, and the region is the **union of the regions of the four cases**. In each system the variable $y$ is isolated, so by the isolated variable rule we take the points below the lines $y = 3 - x$ and $y = x + 1$ where $y \geq 0$ and the points above the lines $y = x - 3$ and $y = -x - 1$ where $y < 0$. Equating the expressions of two lines at a time we find the intersection points $(3, 0)$, $(1, 2)$, $(-1, 0)$ and $(1, -2)$. The region is therefore the **interior of the square** that has these four vertices and center $C(1, 0)$, the point at which the left-hand side equals $0$. The symbol is $<$, hence the four sides are **excluded** and dashed.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Solve graphically the inequality
$$
xy\,(x^2 + y^2 - 1) \leq 0 .
$$

$$
\begin{gathered}
F(x, y) = xy \\[6pt]
G(x, y) = x^2 + y^2 - 1
\end{gathered}
$$
$$
F = 0 \ \Longleftrightarrow\ x = 0 \ \text{ or } \ y = 0
$$
$$
F > 0
\ \Longleftrightarrow\
\begin{cases}
x > 0 \\[4pt]
y > 0
\end{cases}
\ \text{or} \
\begin{cases}
x < 0 \\[4pt]
y < 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
G > 0 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x^2 + y^2 > 1^2
$$

$$
\def\arraystretch{1.35}
\begin{array}{l|l|c|c|c}
\text{quadrants} & \text{points} & F & G & F \cdot G \\ \hline
\text{I and III} & \text{interior} & + & - & - \\
\text{I and III} & \text{exterior} & + & + & + \\
\text{II and IV} & \text{interior} & - & - & + \\
\text{II and IV} & \text{exterior} & - & + & -
\end{array}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
xy \geq 0 \\[4pt]
x^2 + y^2 \leq 1
\end{cases}
\ \text{or} \
\begin{cases}
xy \leq 0 \\[4pt]
x^2 + y^2 \geq 1
\end{cases}
$$

::grafico{regione="x*y>=0; x^2+y^2<=1 | x*y<=0; x^2+y^2>=1" xmin=-3 xmax=3 ymin=-3 ymax=3 uguale=1 titolo="$xy\,(x^2 + y^2 - 1) \leq 0$: axes and circle included"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The left-hand side is the product of the factor $F = xy$ and the factor $G = x^2 + y^2 - 1$. The factor $F$ vanishes on the two coordinate axes, is positive where $x$ and $y$ have the same sign, that is, in the first and third quadrants, and is negative in the second and fourth. The factor $G$ vanishes on the circle with center the origin and radius $1$, is negative at the interior points and is positive at the exterior points. The axes and the circle divide the plane into eight regions, and in each of them the two factors have constant sign. By the **rule of signs** the product is negative where the two factors have opposite signs, so it is negative at the **interior points of the first and third quadrants and at the exterior points of the second and fourth**. The symbol is $\leq$, so we add to the solutions the points at which the product equals $0$, that is, the two whole axes and the circle. All the boundaries are **included** and drawn as solid lines.

</div>
