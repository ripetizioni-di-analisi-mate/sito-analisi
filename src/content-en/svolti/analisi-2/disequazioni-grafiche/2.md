## Exercise 1

Solve graphically the inequality
$$
y \leq 4 - x^2 .
$$

$$
y - 4 = -x^2
$$
$$
V(0, 4), \qquad a = -1 < 0
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
y = 0: \quad & 4 - x^2 = 0 \\[6pt]
& x^2 = 4 \\[6pt]
& x = \pm 2
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{regione="y<=4-x^2" xmin=-4 xmax=4 ymin=-3 ymax=5 uguale=1 punti="0,4,pieno,V" titolo="$y \leq 4 - x^2$: vertex $V(0, 4)$, intersections with the $x$-axis at $-2$ and $2$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The variable $x$ appears squared and $y$ to the first degree, so the boundary is the parabola with vertical axis $y = 4 - x^2$. The equation is written in the form $y - 4 = -x^2$, so the boundary is the parabola $y = -x^2$ translated so that the vertex is $V(0, 4)$. The coefficient of $x^2$ is $-1$, negative, hence the parabola opens downward. Setting $y = 0$ we obtain $x = \pm 2$, so the parabola meets the $x$-axis at the points $(-2, 0)$ and $(2, 0)$. The variable $y$ is isolated with the symbol $\leq$, hence by the **isolated variable rule** the solutions are the points **below the parabola**. The symbol includes equality, so the parabola is **included** and is drawn as a solid line.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve graphically the inequality
$$
y > x^2 + 2x - 3 .
$$

$$
a = 1, \qquad b = 2, \qquad c = -3
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
x_V &= -\frac{b}{2a} = -\frac{2}{2 \cdot 1} = -1 \\[6pt]
y_V &= (-1)^2 + 2 \cdot (-1) - 3 = -4
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
y = 0: \quad & x^2 + 2x - 3 = 0 \\[6pt]
& (x + 3)(x - 1) = 0 \\[6pt]
& x_1 = -3, \quad x_2 = 1
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
x = 0: \quad y = -3
$$

::grafico{regione="y>x^2+2*x-3" xmin=-5 xmax=3 ymin=-5 ymax=3 uguale=1 punti="-1,-4,pieno,V" titolo="$y > x^2 + 2x - 3$: vertex $V(-1, -4)$, intersections with the $x$-axis at $-3$ and $1$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The variable $x$ appears squared and $y$ to the first degree, so the boundary is the parabola with vertical axis $y = x^2 + 2x - 3$. The coefficient of $x^2$ is $a = 1$, positive, hence the parabola opens upward. The $x$-coordinate of the vertex is $x_V = -\dfrac{b}{2a} = -1$ and, substituting this value into the equation, we find $y_V = -4$, so the vertex is $V(-1, -4)$. The trinomial $x^2 + 2x - 3$ factors into the product $(x + 3)(x - 1)$, hence the parabola meets the $x$-axis at the points $(-3, 0)$ and $(1, 0)$. For $x = 0$ we have $y = -3$, so the parabola meets the $y$-axis at the point $(0, -3)$. The variable $y$ is isolated with the symbol $>$, hence by the **isolated variable rule** the solutions are the points **above the parabola**. The symbol is strict, so the parabola is **excluded** and is drawn as a dashed line.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve graphically the inequality
$$
2y + x^2 - 4x \geq 0 .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
2y &\geq -x^2 + 4x \\[6pt]
y &\geq -\frac{1}{2} x^2 + 2x
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
-\frac{1}{2} x^2 + 2x &= -\frac{1}{2} \left( x^2 - 4x \right) \\[6pt]
&= -\frac{1}{2} \left[ (x - 2)^2 - 4 \right] \\[6pt]
&= -\frac{1}{2} (x - 2)^2 + 2
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
y \geq -\frac{1}{2} (x - 2)^2 + 2, \qquad V(2, 2)
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
y = 0: \quad & x^2 - 4x = 0 \\[6pt]
& x (x - 4) = 0 \\[6pt]
& x_1 = 0, \quad x_2 = 4
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{regione="2*y+x^2-4*x>=0" xmin=-2 xmax=6 ymin=-4 ymax=4 uguale=1 punti="2,2,pieno,V" titolo="$2y + x^2 - 4x \geq 0$, that is, $y \geq -\dfrac{1}{2} (x - 2)^2 + 2$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

In the inequality $x^2$ appears and $y^2$ does not, so the boundary is a parabola with vertical axis and the variable to isolate is $y$. We move $x^2$ and $-4x$ to the right-hand side and divide by $2$, which is positive, hence the direction stays the same and we obtain $y \geq -\dfrac{1}{2} x^2 + 2x$. By **completing the square** the right-hand side becomes $-\dfrac{1}{2} (x - 2)^2 + 2$, so the vertex is $V(2, 2)$. The coefficient of $x^2$ is negative, hence the parabola opens downward. Setting $y = 0$ in the equation $2y + x^2 - 4x = 0$ we obtain $x^2 - 4x = 0$, that is, $x(x - 4) = 0$, so the parabola meets the $x$-axis at the points $(0, 0)$ and $(4, 0)$. The variable $y$ is isolated with the symbol $\geq$, hence the solutions are the points **above the parabola**. The symbol includes equality, so the parabola is **included** and is drawn as a solid line.

</div>
