## Exercise 1

A crate of mass $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ is initially at rest on a horizontal surface. The coefficient of static friction is $0.40$ and a horizontal force of $12\,\mathrm{N}$ pulls the crate to the right. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Determine the maximum static friction, decide whether the crate can remain at rest, and find the actual frictional force.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N&=mg=50\,\mathrm{N},\\[6pt]
F_{s,\max}&=\mu_sN\\[6pt]
&=0.40\cdot50\,\mathrm{N}=20\,\mathrm{N},\\[6pt]
F&=12\,\mathrm{N}<20\,\mathrm{N},\\[6pt]
F_s&=F=12\,\mathrm{N}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The pull is horizontal, so it does not affect vertical equilibrium and the normal force equals the weight in magnitude. The product $\mu_sN$ gives the maximum friction available. To remain at rest, the crate requires a frictional force of $12\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left, equal and opposite to the pull. This value is below the maximum, so equilibrium is possible. The actual frictional force adjusts to the required value and has magnitude $12\,\mathrm{N}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A crate of mass $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ is initially at rest on a horizontal surface with coefficients of friction $\mu_s=0.40$ and $\mu_d=0.30$. A constant horizontal force of $25\,\mathrm{N}$ pulls it to the right. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Determine whether the crate starts to slide and calculate its acceleration once sliding begins.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N&=mg=50\,\mathrm{N},\\[6pt]
F_{s,\max}&=\mu_sN=20\,\mathrm{N},\\[6pt]
25\,\mathrm{N}&>20\,\mathrm{N},\\[6pt]
F_d&=\mu_dN=15\,\mathrm{N},\\[6pt]
a&=\frac{F-F_d}{m}\\[6pt]
&=\frac{25\,\mathrm{N}-15\,\mathrm{N}}{5\,\mathrm{kg}}\\[6pt]
&=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The maximum static friction is $20\,\mathrm{N}$, so it cannot balance the pull of $25\,\mathrm{N}$ and the crate **starts to slide**. Once it is sliding, the coefficient of kinetic friction applies and gives a frictional force of $15\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left. The net force is therefore $10\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right. Dividing by the mass gives the acceleration once the crate is moving.

</div>
