## Exercise 1

A cart starts at $x_0=-2\,\mathrm{m}$ with velocity $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and constant acceleration $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Calculate its position and displacement after $4\,\mathrm{s}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=x_0+v_0t+\frac{1}{2}at^2\\[6pt]
&=-2\,\mathrm{m}+3\,\mathrm{m/s}\cdot4\,\mathrm{s}
+\frac{1}{2}\cdot2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\cdot(4\,\mathrm{s})^2\\[6pt]
&=-2\,\mathrm{m}+12\,\mathrm{m}+16\,\mathrm{m}\\[6pt]
&=26\,\mathrm{m},\\[6pt]
\Delta x&=x-x_0=26\,\mathrm{m}-(-2\,\mathrm{m})=28\,\mathrm{m}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Since the acceleration is constant, we can use the **position equation**. Time is squared in the acceleration term, so that contribution is $16\,\mathrm{m}$. The initial position is negative and enters the sum with its sign. To find the displacement, subtract the initial position from the final position. The cart starts two meters on the negative side of the origin, so it travels $28\,\mathrm{m}$ to reach $x=26\,\mathrm{m}$. Its velocity remains positive over the interval, so the distance traveled equals the displacement.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A small conveyor belt starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at $0.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $5\,\mathrm{s}$. An object moves with the belt without slipping. Calculate the object's displacement and final velocity.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x&=v_0t+\frac{1}{2}at^2\\[6pt]
&=\frac{1}{2}\cdot0.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\cdot(5\,\mathrm{s})^2\\[6pt]
&=10\,\mathrm{m},\\[6pt]
v&=v_0+at\\[6pt]
&=0+0.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\cdot5\,\mathrm{s}\\[6pt]
&=4\,\mathrm{m/s}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The object stays in contact with the belt without slipping, so it moves with the belt. Starting from rest gives $v_0=0$, so the term $v_0t$ vanishes. The displacement is therefore half the product of the acceleration and the square of the time. As a check, the average velocity is $\dfrac{0+4}{2}=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Multiplying it by five seconds again gives $10\,\mathrm{m}$.

</div>
