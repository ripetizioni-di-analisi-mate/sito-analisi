## Exercise 1

An object is launched vertically upward with velocity $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglect air resistance and use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ to calculate the time of ascent, the maximum height above the launch point, and the time to return to the same height along an unobstructed path.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_s&=\frac{v_0}{g}=\frac{20\,\mathrm{m/s}}{10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}}=2\,\mathrm{s},\\[6pt]
h_{\max}&=\frac{v_0^2}{2g}\\[6pt]
&=\frac{(20\,\mathrm{m/s})^2}{2\cdot10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}}=20\,\mathrm{m},\\[6pt]
t_r&=\frac{2v_0}{g}=4\,\mathrm{s}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

With the axis positive upward, the acceleration is $-g$. The velocity is zero at the highest point, so $0=v_0-gt_s$ gives the ascent time. Substituting this time into the equation for height gives the maximum height. Descending to the initial height takes as long as ascending because the acceleration remains constant and air resistance is neglected. The total time is therefore four seconds.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An object is launched upward with velocity $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Place the origin at the launch point and take upward as positive. Neglect air resistance and use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ to find the height and velocity after $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and determine whether the object is rising or falling. The flight path is unobstructed.

$$
\begin{aligned}
y&=v_0t-\frac{1}{2}gt^2\\[6pt]
&=15\,\mathrm{m/s}\cdot2\,\mathrm{s}
-\frac{1}{2}\cdot10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\cdot(2\,\mathrm{s})^2\\[6pt]
&=10\,\mathrm{m},\\[6pt]
v&=v_0-gt\\[6pt]
&=15\,\mathrm{m/s}-10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\cdot2\,\mathrm{s}\\[6pt]
&=-5\,\mathrm{m/s}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The height is positive, so the object is still above the launch point. Its velocity is negative, so it is moving downward. The ascent time is $\dfrac{15}{10}=1.5\,\mathrm{s}$, less than the given two seconds, which confirms that the object has already passed its highest point. Position and velocity describe different aspects of the motion and can therefore have different signs at the same instant.

</div>
