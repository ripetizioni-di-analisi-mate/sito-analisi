## Exercise 1

A vehicle travels at $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$. After a signal, it maintains this velocity for $0.8\,\mathrm{s}$, then brakes with constant acceleration $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ until it stops. Calculate the distance traveled between the signal and the stop. The axis points in the initial direction of motion.

$$
\begin{aligned}
s_1&=v_0t_1\\[6pt]
&=18\,\mathrm{m/s}\cdot0.8\,\mathrm{s}=14.4\,\mathrm{m},\\[6pt]
t_2&=-\frac{v_0}{a}=6\,\mathrm{s},\\[6pt]
s_2&=\frac{v_0}{2}t_2\\[6pt]
&=9\,\mathrm{m/s}\cdot6\,\mathrm{s}=54\,\mathrm{m},\\[6pt]
s_{\mathrm{tot}}&=s_1+s_2=68.4\,\mathrm{m}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the first interval the velocity is constant, so multiplying velocity by time gives the distance. Braking begins after this stage, and the initial velocity for braking is still $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In the second interval we use the average velocity during uniform braking. The vehicle continues moving in the same direction until it stops, so the total distance is the sum of the two distances. These values describe the given model with constant acceleration during braking.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A cart initially moves at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. It accelerates at $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $3\,\mathrm{s}$, then maintains the velocity it has reached for another $4\,\mathrm{s}$. Calculate the total distance traveled.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_1&=v_0+at_1\\[6pt]
&=4\,\mathrm{m/s}+2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\cdot3\,\mathrm{s}=10\,\mathrm{m/s},\\[6pt]
s_1&=v_0t_1+\frac{1}{2}at_1^2\\[6pt]
&=12\,\mathrm{m}+9\,\mathrm{m}=21\,\mathrm{m},\\[6pt]
s_2&=v_1t_2=10\,\mathrm{m/s}\cdot4\,\mathrm{s}=40\,\mathrm{m},\\[6pt]
s_{\mathrm{tot}}&=21\,\mathrm{m}+40\,\mathrm{m}=61\,\mathrm{m}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the first interval the acceleration is constant, so the equations for accelerated motion give both the final velocity and the displacement. The final velocity in the first interval is the constant velocity in the second. The time $t_2=4\,\mathrm{s}$ is the duration of this second stage. The total time elapsed since the start is seven seconds. The velocity is always positive, so the distance is the sum of the displacements.

</div>
