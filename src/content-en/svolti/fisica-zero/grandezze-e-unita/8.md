## Exercise 1

Write $72\,000\,\mathrm{m}$ and $0.00084\,\mathrm{m}$ in scientific notation.

$$
\begin{aligned}
72\,000\,\mathrm{m}&=7.2\cdot10\,000\,\mathrm{m}\\[6pt]
&=7.2\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{m},\\[6pt]
0.00084\,\mathrm{m}&=\frac{8.4}{10\,000}\,\mathrm{m}\\[6pt]
&=8.4\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The coefficient must be at least $1$ and less than $10$. In the first case, choose $7.2$ and multiply by $10\,000=10^4$. In the second, choose $8.4$ and divide by $10\,000$, which is the same as multiplying by $10^{-4}$. Both expressions preserve the original value.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A wire has a diameter of $3.2\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m}$. Express it in decimal form in meters, then in millimeters.

$$
\begin{aligned}
d&=\frac{3.2}{10\,000}\,\mathrm{m}\\[6pt]
&=0.00032\,\mathrm{m},\\[6pt]
d&=0.00032\cdot1000\,\mathrm{mm}\\[6pt]
&=0.32\,\mathrm{mm}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The letter $d$ denotes the diameter, the length of a line segment that crosses the circular cross section through its center. The exponent $-4$ indicates division by $10\,000$, so the decimal form is $0.00032\,\mathrm{m}$. A meter contains one thousand millimeters, so the diameter equals $0.32\,\mathrm{mm}$.

</div>
