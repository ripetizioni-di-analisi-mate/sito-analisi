## Exercise 1

Express $0.46\,\mathrm{m^2}$ in square centimeters and $850\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ in square meters.

$$
\begin{aligned}
1\,\mathrm{m^2}&=100\cdot100\,\mathrm{cm^2}\\[6pt]
&=10\,000\,\mathrm{cm^2},\\[6pt]
0.46\,\mathrm{m^2}&=4600\,\mathrm{cm^2},\\[6pt]
850\,\mathrm{cm^2}&=\frac{850}{10\,000}\,\mathrm{m^2}\\[6pt]
&=0.085\,\mathrm{m^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

A square meter is the area of a square with sides one meter long. In centimeters, each side measures $100$, so the area is $100\cdot100=10\,000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The area conversion factor is therefore $10\,000$. Multiply by this factor in the first case and divide by it in the second.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A rectangular panel has a base of $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ and a height of $45\,\mathrm{cm}$. Calculate its area in square meters.

$$
\begin{aligned}
h&=\frac{45}{100}\,\mathrm{m}=0.45\,\mathrm{m},\\[6pt]
A&=b\,h\\[6pt]
&=1.2\,\mathrm{m}\cdot0.45\,\mathrm{m}\\[6pt]
&=0.54\,\mathrm{m^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The base $b$ is given in meters, so the height $h$ is also expressed in meters. Applying the formula for the **area of a rectangle** means multiplying $1.2$ by $0.45$. The product of the units is $\mathrm{m}\cdot\mathrm{m}=\mathrm{m^2}$, so the result is an area.
