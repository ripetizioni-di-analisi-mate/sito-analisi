## Exercise 1

A recording lasts $3\,\mathrm{min}$ and $25\,\mathrm{s}$. Express its duration in seconds.

$$
\begin{aligned}
1\,\mathrm{min}&=60\,\mathrm{s},\\[6pt]
t&=3\cdot60\,\mathrm{s}+25\,\mathrm{s}\\[6pt]
&=180\,\mathrm{s}+25\,\mathrm{s}\\[6pt]
&=205\,\mathrm{s}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each minute contains sixty seconds, so three minutes contain $3\cdot60=180$ seconds. The recording lasts another twenty-five seconds, which are added to the first one hundred eighty. The **total duration** is $205\,\mathrm{s}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Express $1.25\,\mathrm{h}$ in hours and minutes, then in seconds.

$$
\begin{aligned}
1.25\,\mathrm{h}&=1\,\mathrm{h}+0.25\,\mathrm{h},\\[6pt]
0.25\,\mathrm{h}&=0.25\cdot60\,\mathrm{min}\\[6pt]
&=15\,\mathrm{min},\\[6pt]
t&=1.25\cdot3600\,\mathrm{s}\\[6pt]
&=4500\,\mathrm{s}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The decimal part $0.25$ represents a quarter of an hour. Since an hour contains sixty minutes, a quarter of an hour contains fifteen minutes. The duration is therefore one hour and fifteen minutes. One hour equals $3600\,\mathrm{s}$, so direct conversion gives $1.25\cdot3600=4500\,\mathrm{s}$.

</div>
