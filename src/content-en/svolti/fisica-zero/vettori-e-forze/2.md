## Exercise 1

Two forces act on the same particle along the same line. One is $9\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right and the other is $4\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left. Taking right as positive, calculate the component and magnitude of the resultant and state which way it points.

$$
\begin{aligned}R_x&=F_{1x}+F_{2x}\\[6pt]&=9\,\mathrm{N}-4\,\mathrm{N}\\[6pt]&=5\,\mathrm{N}\\[6pt]R&=|R_x|=5\,\mathrm{N}\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{vettori="0,1,4.5,1,F1;0,-1,-2,-1,F2;0,-3,2.5,-3,R" assi=0 uguale=1 xmin=-3 xmax=6 ymin=-4 ymax=3 titolo="The forces and their resultant are shown on separate lines. Each unit in the drawing represents $2\,\mathrm{N}$."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The forces are collinear, so we add their **signed components**. The rightward force has component $+9\,\mathrm{N}$, and the leftward force has component $-4\,\mathrm{N}$. The sum is positive, so the resultant points right and has magnitude $5\,\mathrm{N}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Three successive displacements along a line are $6\,\mathrm{m}$ east, $11\,\mathrm{m}$ west, and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ east. Calculate the component and magnitude of the overall displacement. The positive direction is east.

$$
\begin{aligned}R_x&=A_x+B_x+C_x\\[6pt]&=6\,\mathrm{m}-11\,\mathrm{m}+2\,\mathrm{m}\\[6pt]&=-3\,\mathrm{m}\\[6pt]R&=|-3\,\mathrm{m}|=3\,\mathrm{m}\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The eastward displacements have positive components, and the westward displacement has a negative component. First add $6$ and $-11$ to get $-5$, then add $2$ to get $-3$. The negative component indicates that the endpoint lies west of the starting point. The overall displacement therefore **has a magnitude of 3 meters and points west**.

</div>
