## Exercise 1

Two displacement vectors have components $A_x=4\,\mathrm{m}$, $A_y=1\,\mathrm{m}$, $B_x=-1\,\mathrm{m}$, and $B_y=3\,\mathrm{m}$. Calculate the components and magnitude of their sum.

$$
\begin{aligned}R_x&=A_x+B_x=(4-1)\,\mathrm{m}=3\,\mathrm{m}\\[6pt]R_y&=A_y+B_y=(1+3)\,\mathrm{m}=4\,\mathrm{m}\\[6pt]R&=\sqrt{R_x^2+R_y^2}\\[6pt]&=\sqrt{9+16}\,\mathrm{m}=5\,\mathrm{m}\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Add the horizontal components together and then the vertical components. This gives $R_x=3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $R_y=4\,\mathrm{m}$. Since both are positive, the vector sum points up and to the right. The two components are perpendicular, so the Pythagorean theorem gives a magnitude of $5\,\mathrm{m}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Two forces acting at the same point have components $F_{1x}=-8\,\mathrm{N}$, $F_{1y}=2\,\mathrm{N}$, $F_{2x}=2\,\mathrm{N}$, and $F_{2y}=-10\,\mathrm{N}$. Calculate the components and magnitude of the resultant.

$$
\begin{aligned}R_x&=F_{1x}+F_{2x}=(-8+2)\,\mathrm{N}=-6\,\mathrm{N}\\[6pt]R_y&=F_{1y}+F_{2y}=(2-10)\,\mathrm{N}=-8\,\mathrm{N}\\[6pt]R&=\sqrt{(-6)^2+(-8)^2}\,\mathrm{N}\\[6pt]&=\sqrt{36+64}\,\mathrm{N}=10\,\mathrm{N}\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{vettori="0,0,-6,-8,R;0,0,-6,0,Rx;0,0,0,-8,Ry" segmenti="-6,0,-6,-8;0,-8,-6,-8" uguale=1 xmin=-9 xmax=3 ymin=-11 ymax=3 titolo="Components of the resultant in newtons. Its magnitude is $10\,\mathrm{N}$."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The sum of the horizontal components is $-6\,\mathrm{N}$, and the sum of the vertical components is $-8\,\mathrm{N}$. The resultant therefore points down and to the left. Squaring each negative component gives a positive product, so the magnitude is the square root of $100\,\mathrm{N^2}$, or $10\,\mathrm{N}$.

</div>
