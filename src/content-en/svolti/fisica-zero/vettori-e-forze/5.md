## Exercise 1

There are five equal intervals between the $1\,\mathrm{N}$ and $2\,\mathrm{N}$ marks on a spring scale. The pointer is on the third mark past $1\,\mathrm{N}$. Determine the reading.

$$
\begin{aligned}d&=\frac{F_b-F_a}{n}\\[6pt]&=\frac{2\,\mathrm{N}-1\,\mathrm{N}}{5}=0.2\,\mathrm{N}\\[6pt]F&=F_a+3d\\[6pt]&=1\,\mathrm{N}+3\cdot0.2\,\mathrm{N}\\[6pt]&=1.6\,\mathrm{N}\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The symbol $d$ denotes the value of one scale interval. The difference between the numbered marks is $1\,\mathrm{N}$ and is divided into five equal parts, so each part represents $0.2\,\mathrm{N}$. Three intervals represent $0.6\,\mathrm{N}$. Adding this to the starting value gives a reading of $1.6\,\mathrm{N}$. This is the value indicated on the scale. The instrument uncertainty is included with the reading when requested.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A spring scale has a capacity of $10\,\mathrm{N}$. There are eight equal intervals between $4\,\mathrm{N}$ and $6\,\mathrm{N}$. The pointer is five intervals past $4\,\mathrm{N}$. Calculate the reading and check that it is within the scale's capacity.

$$
\begin{aligned}d&=\frac{6\,\mathrm{N}-4\,\mathrm{N}}{8}\\[6pt]&=0.25\,\mathrm{N}\\[6pt]F&=4\,\mathrm{N}+5\cdot0.25\,\mathrm{N}\\[6pt]&=5.25\,\mathrm{N}\\[6pt]5.25\,\mathrm{N}&<10\,\mathrm{N}\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The difference between the numbered values is $2\,\mathrm{N}$. Dividing by the eight intervals gives $0.25\,\mathrm{N}$ per interval. Five intervals represent $1.25\,\mathrm{N}$, so the reading is $5.25\,\mathrm{N}$. This value is below the **capacity** of $10\,\mathrm{N}$, so it lies within the stated measuring range.

</div>
