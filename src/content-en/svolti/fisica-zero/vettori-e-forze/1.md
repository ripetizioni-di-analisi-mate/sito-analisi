## Exercise 1

A force points horizontally to the right and has magnitude $8\,\mathrm{N}$. The scale is $S=2\,\mathrm{N/cm}$, so each centimeter on the page represents $2\,\mathrm{N}$. Calculate the length of the arrow and give the component of the force, taking right as positive.

$$
\begin{aligned}\ell&=\frac{F}{S}\\[6pt]&=\frac{8\,\mathrm{N}}{2\,\mathrm{N/cm}}\\[6pt]&=4\,\mathrm{cm}\\[6pt]F_x&=+8\,\mathrm{N}\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **scale** assigns $2\,\mathrm{N}$ to each centimeter. To represent $8\,\mathrm{N}$, divide $8$ by $2$ to obtain an arrow $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. The tip points to the right. Since the force points in the positive direction of the axis, its component is positive. The length of the drawing is expressed in centimeters, while the component of the force is expressed in newtons.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An arrow $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ long represents a westward displacement at a scale of $S=4\,\mathrm{m/cm}$. Calculate the magnitude of the displacement and its component, taking east as positive.

$$
\begin{aligned}A&=S\ell\\[6pt]&=4\,\mathrm{m/cm}\cdot3\,\mathrm{cm}\\[6pt]&=12\,\mathrm{m}\\[6pt]A_x&=-12\,\mathrm{m}\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each centimeter represents $4\,\mathrm{m}$, so three centimeters represent $12\,\mathrm{m}$. This value is the **magnitude** of the displacement. West is opposite to the positive direction, east, so the component is $-12\,\mathrm{m}$. The sign indicates which way the arrow points. The magnitude remains positive.

</div>
