## Exercise 1

An object exerts a force of $180\,\mathrm{N}$ perpendicular to a surface of area $0.030\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Calculate the average pressure.

$$
\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{F}{A}\\[6pt]&=\frac{180\,\mathrm{N}}{0.030\,\mathrm{m^2}}\\[6pt]&=6000\,\mathrm{Pa}.\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The force is perpendicular to the surface, so the average pressure is found by dividing the force by the area. Both quantities are already in SI units. The quotient $180:0.030$ equals $6000$, so the pressure is $6000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. If the force is distributed uniformly, this is also the pressure at every point.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A rectangular base with sides $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ supports a perpendicular force of $100\,\mathrm{N}$. Calculate the average pressure on the base.

$$
\begin{aligned}a&=0.10\,\mathrm{m},\qquad b=0.20\,\mathrm{m},\\[6pt]A&=ab=0.020\,\mathrm{m^2},\\[6pt]p&=\frac{F}{A}\\[6pt]&=\frac{100}{0.020}\,\mathrm{Pa}=5000\,\mathrm{Pa}.\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Convert both side lengths to meters, then calculate the area of the rectangle as the product $ab$. The value $0.020\,\mathrm{m^2}$ is equivalent to $200\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The **average pressure** is the ratio of the $100\,\mathrm{N}$ force to this area. The result depends on the contact area as well as the force.

</div>
