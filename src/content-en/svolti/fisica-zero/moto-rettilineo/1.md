## Exercise 1

A cart moves from position $x_i=-4\,\mathrm{m}$ to position $x_f=7\,\mathrm{m}$ along a straight track without changing direction. Calculate the displacement and the distance traveled.

$$
\begin{aligned}\Delta x&=x_f-x_i\\[6pt]&=7\,\mathrm{m}-(-4\,\mathrm{m})\\[6pt]&=11\,\mathrm{m}\\[6pt]s&=|\Delta x|=11\,\mathrm{m}\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **displacement** is the final position minus the initial position. Subtracting $-4$ is equivalent to adding $4$, so $7-(-4)=11$. The positive sign indicates that the final position lies in the positive direction from the initial position. The cart moves in the same direction throughout, so the distance traveled equals the magnitude of the displacement.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A person starts at position $2\,\mathrm{m}$, reaches $10\,\mathrm{m}$, and returns to $4\,\mathrm{m}$ along the same straight line. Calculate the overall displacement and the distance traveled.

$$
\begin{aligned}\Delta x&=x_f-x_i\\[6pt]&=4\,\mathrm{m}-2\,\mathrm{m}=2\,\mathrm{m}\\[6pt]s&=|10-2|\,\mathrm{m}+|4-10|\,\mathrm{m}\\[6pt]&=8\,\mathrm{m}+6\,\mathrm{m}\\[6pt]&=14\,\mathrm{m}\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Only the initial position $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the final position $4\,\mathrm{m}$ enter the displacement calculation, so the displacement is $2\,\mathrm{m}$. The **distance traveled** includes both legs. The first leg is $8\,\mathrm{m}$ long and the return leg is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long, so their lengths add up to $14\,\mathrm{m}$. The final position is close to the initial position even though the total path is longer.

</div>
