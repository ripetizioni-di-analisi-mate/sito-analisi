## Exercise 1

At $t=0$ a cart is at $x_0=-3\,\mathrm{m}$. It moves with constant velocity $v=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for at least $7\,\mathrm{s}$. Write its position equation and calculate its position at $t=7\,\mathrm{s}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}x(t)&=x_0+vt\\[6pt]&=-3\,\mathrm{m}+(2\,\mathrm{m/s})t\\[6pt]x(7\,\mathrm{s})&=-3\,\mathrm{m}+2\,\mathrm{m/s}\cdot7\,\mathrm{s}\\[6pt]&=11\,\mathrm{m}\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **position equation** adds the initial position to the displacement $vt$. In $7\,\mathrm{s}$ the displacement is $14\,\mathrm{m}$, so the final position is $-3+14=11\,\mathrm{m}$. The origin lies between the initial and final positions. The displacement and the final position have different values because the initial position is nonzero.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An object starts at $x_0=30\,\mathrm{m}$ at time $t=0$ and maintains velocity $v=-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $5\,\mathrm{s}$. Calculate the final position, displacement, and distance traveled.

$$
\begin{aligned}x&=x_0+vt\\[6pt]&=30\,\mathrm{m}-4\,\mathrm{m/s}\cdot5\,\mathrm{s}\\[6pt]&=10\,\mathrm{m}\\[6pt]\Delta x&=vt=-20\,\mathrm{m}\\[6pt]s&=|\Delta x|=20\,\mathrm{m}\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{f="30-4*x" punti="0,30;5,10" xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=35 nomi="t,x" legenda=0 titolo="The position decreases from $30\,\mathrm{m}$ to $10\,\mathrm{m}$ in $5\,\mathrm{s}$."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The negative velocity indicates that the coordinate decreases. In five seconds it decreases by $20\,\mathrm{m}$, so it goes from $30\,\mathrm{m}$ to $10\,\mathrm{m}$. The object keeps moving in the same direction and travels $20\,\mathrm{m}$. Its final position is still positive even though its velocity is negative.

</div>
