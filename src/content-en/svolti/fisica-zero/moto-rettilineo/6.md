## Exercise 1

The position-time graph for uniform motion passes through $(0\,\mathrm{s},2\,\mathrm{m})$ and $(5\,\mathrm{s},12\,\mathrm{m})$. Calculate the velocity, write the position equation, and find the position at $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}v&=\frac{x_2-x_1}{t_2-t_1}\\[6pt]&=\frac{12\,\mathrm{m}-2\,\mathrm{m}}{5\,\mathrm{s}-0\,\mathrm{s}}\\[6pt]&=2\,\mathrm{m/s}\\[6pt]x(t)&=2\,\mathrm{m}+(2\,\mathrm{m/s})t\\[6pt]x(3\,\mathrm{s})&=2\,\mathrm{m}+6\,\mathrm{m}=8\,\mathrm{m}\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{f="2+2*x" punti="0,2;3,8;5,12" xmin=0 xmax=6 ymin=0 ymax=15 nomi="t,x" legenda=0 titolo="Time in seconds and position in meters. All three points satisfy the same position equation."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The straight line represents uniform motion, so the average velocity between the two points equals the velocity of the object. The position increases by $10\,\mathrm{m}$ in $5\,\mathrm{s}$, so the velocity is $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The point at $t=0$ gives $x_0=2\,\mathrm{m}$. Substituting these values into the position equation and then setting $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$ gives $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The graph of uniform motion is a straight line through $(0\,\mathrm{s},18\,\mathrm{m})$ and $(4\,\mathrm{s},6\,\mathrm{m})$. Determine the velocity and the time at which the object passes the origin, assuming that its motion remains uniform.

$$
\begin{aligned}v&=\frac{6\,\mathrm{m}-18\,\mathrm{m}}{4\,\mathrm{s}}\\[6pt]&=-3\,\mathrm{m/s}\\[6pt]0&=x_0+vt\\[6pt]t&=-\frac{x_0}{v}\\[6pt]&=-\frac{18\,\mathrm{m}}{-3\,\mathrm{m/s}}\\[6pt]&=6\,\mathrm{s}\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The position decreases by $12\,\mathrm{m}$ in four seconds, so the velocity is negative and equals $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Passing the origin corresponds to $x=0$. We therefore set $0=18-3t$ in the position equation, with positions in meters and time in seconds, and obtain $t=6\,\mathrm{s}$. Since the problem assumes that the motion remains uniform, we can still use the equation after $4\,\mathrm{s}$.

</div>
