## Exercise 1

Find the sums
$$
(+6) + (+9) , \qquad (-4) + (-11) , \qquad (+12) + (-7) , \qquad (-15) + (+6) .
$$

$$
(+6) + (+9) = +(6 + 9) = +15
$$
$$
(-4) + (-11) = -(4 + 11) = -15
$$
$$
(+12) + (-7) = +(12 - 7) = +5
$$
$$
(-15) + (+6) = -(15 - 6) = -9
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the first two sums the numbers have **like signs**. In this case we add the absolute values and keep the common sign. Therefore $(+6) + (+9) = +15$ because $6 + 9 = 15$, and $(-4) + (-11) = -15$ because $4 + 11 = 15$.

In the last two sums the numbers have **unlike signs**. We subtract the smaller absolute value from the greater, and the result takes the sign of the number with the greater absolute value. In $(+12) + (-7)$, the greater absolute value is $12$, so the sum is positive and equals $12 - 7 = 5$. In $(-15) + (+6)$, the greater absolute value is $15$ and belongs to a negative number, so the sum is negative and equals $-(15 - 6) = -9$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Calculate
$$
(-8) + (+8) + (-5) .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(-8) + (+8) + (-5)
&= 0 + (-5) \\[6pt]
&= -5
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first two addends, $-8$ and $+8$, are **opposites** because they have the same absolute value and different signs. The sum of two opposites is $0$. Adding $0$ to a number gives the number itself, so the result is $-5$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Calculate
$$
(+7) + (-12) + (+9) + (-3) + (-6) .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& (+7) + (-12) + (+9) + (-3) + (-6) \\[6pt]
={} & [(+7) + (+9)] + [(-12) + (-3) + (-6)] \\[6pt]
={} & (+16) + (-21) \\[6pt]
={} & -5
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the **commutative** and **associative** properties of addition, the addends can be rearranged and regrouped without changing the sum. We therefore group the positive numbers together and the negative numbers together. The positive numbers all have the same sign, so they give $+(7 + 9) = +16$. The negative numbers also have the same sign, and they give $-(12 + 3 + 6) = -21$.

Two numbers with opposite signs remain. The greater absolute value is $21$ and belongs to the negative number, so the sum is negative and equals $-(21 - 16) = -5$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Calculate
$$
(-14) + (+23) + (-9) + (+5) + (-11) + (+2) .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& (-14) + (+23) + (-9) + (+5) + (-11) + (+2) \\[6pt]
={} & [(+23) + (+5) + (+2)] + [(-14) + (-9) + (-11)] \\[6pt]
={} & (+30) + (-34) \\[6pt]
={} & -4
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

As in the previous exercise, we group the positive addends together and the negative addends together. The positive numbers give $23 + 5 + 2 = 30$, that is, $+30$. The negative numbers give $14 + 9 + 11 = 34$ in absolute value, so their sum is $-34$.

The numbers $+30$ and $-34$ have opposite signs. The greater absolute value is $34$, so the sum has the sign $-$ and absolute value $34 - 30 = 4$. The result is therefore $-4$.

</div>
