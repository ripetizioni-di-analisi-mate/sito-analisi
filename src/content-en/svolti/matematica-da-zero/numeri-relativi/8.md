## Exercise 1

An elevator starts on floor $-2$, where the garage is located. It goes up $7$ floors and then down $3$ floors. Find the floor where the elevator ends up.

$$
(-2) + (+7) + (-3) = (+5) + (-3) = +2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Floors above the ground floor are positive, and basement floors are negative. Going up $7$ floors corresponds to adding $+7$, and going down $3$ floors corresponds to adding $-3$.

The sum $(-2) + (+7)$ has addends with opposite signs, and $+7$ has the greater absolute value, so the sum equals $+5$. Then $(+5) + (-3) = +2$ because $5 - 3 = 2$ and the positive number has the greater absolute value. The elevator is therefore on floor $+2$, the **second floor**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

At the beginning of the month, Luca has a balance of $+340$ euros in his checking account. During the month he receives a salary of $1200$ euros and pays $650$ euros in rent, $85$ euros for a utility bill, and $310$ euros for groceries. Find the balance at the end of the month.

$$
\begin{aligned}
& 340 + 1200 - 650 - 85 - 310 \\[6pt]
={} & (340 + 1200) - (650 + 85 + 310) \\[6pt]
={} & 1540 - 1045 \\[6pt]
={} & 495
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Money entering the account is represented by positive numbers, and money leaving it by negative numbers. The final balance is therefore the algebraic sum of the opening balance $+340$, the deposit $+1200$, and the three withdrawals $-650$, $-85$, and $-310$.

The positive terms give $340 + 1200 = 1540$. The negative terms give $-(650 + 85 + 310)$. Since $650 + 85 = 735$ and $735 + 310 = 1045$, the withdrawals total $-1045$ euros. Finally, $1540 - 1045 = 495$, and the **balance** at the end of the month is $+495$ euros.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A store's account has a balance of $-120$ euros. For $4$ days the store pays a supplier each day, with a debit of $75$ euros per day. Over the same period it takes in a total of $280$ euros. Find the final balance and determine whether it is greater or less than the initial balance.

$$
4 \cdot (-75) = -300
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
(-120) + (-300) + (+280)
&= (-420) + (+280) \\[6pt]
&= -140
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
-140 < -120
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each debit of $75$ euros is a negative number, $-75$. Together the four debits equal $4 \cdot (-75)$, and by the **rule of signs** the product of two factors with opposite signs is negative. Since $4 \cdot 75 = 300$, the debits total $-300$ euros.

The final balance is the sum of the initial balance, debits, and receipts. The numbers $-120$ and $-300$ have the same sign and give $-420$. Then $-420$ and $+280$ have opposite signs, and $-420$ has the greater absolute value, so their sum is $-(420 - 280) = -140$. The **final balance** is therefore $-140$ euros.

Of two negative numbers, the one with the greater absolute value is smaller. Since $140 > 120$, we have $-140 < -120$, so the final balance is **less than** the initial balance, and the store's debt has increased by $20$ euros.

</div>
