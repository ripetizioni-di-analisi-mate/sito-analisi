## Exercise 1

Calculate
$$
5 - 3 \cdot (-4) + (-18) : (+6) .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
5 - 3 \cdot (-4) + (-18) : (+6)
&= 5 - (-12) + (-3) \\[6pt]
&= 5 + 12 - 3 \\[6pt]
&= 14
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the order of operations, we multiply and divide before subtracting and adding. The product $3 \cdot (-4)$ has factors with opposite signs and equals $-12$. The quotient $(-18) : (+6)$ involves numbers with opposite signs and equals $-3$.

Then we remove the parentheses. The sign $-$ before $-12$ changes it to $+12$, and the sign $+$ before $-3$ leaves $-3$. In the algebraic sum $5 + 12 - 3$, the positive terms give $17$, and $17 - 3 = 14$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Calculate
$$
(-2) \cdot [\, 7 - (3 - 9) \,] : (-13) .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(-2) \cdot [\, 7 - (3 - 9) \,] : (-13)
&= (-2) \cdot [\, 7 - (-6) \,] : (-13) \\[6pt]
&= (-2) \cdot [\, 7 + 6 \,] : (-13) \\[6pt]
&= (-2) \cdot 13 : (-13) \\[6pt]
&= (-26) : (-13) \\[6pt]
&= +2
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

First we evaluate the expression $3 - 9$. The numbers $3$ and $-9$ have opposite signs, and $-9$ has the greater absolute value, so $3 - 9 = -6$. Then we subtract $-6$, which means adding its opposite $+6$, so $7 - (-6) = 7 + 6 = 13$.

A multiplication and a division remain, which we perform from left to right. The product $(-2) \cdot 13$ has factors with opposite signs and equals $-26$. The quotient $(-26) : (-13)$ involves numbers with the same sign and equals $+2$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Calculate
$$
[\, (-15) : (+3) - (-2) \cdot (-4) \,] \cdot (-1) + (-6) \cdot (+2) .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& [\, (-15) : (+3) - (-2) \cdot (-4) \,] \cdot (-1) + (-6) \cdot (+2) \\[6pt]
={} & [\, (-5) - (+8) \,] \cdot (-1) + (-12) \\[6pt]
={} & [\, -5 - 8 \,] \cdot (-1) - 12 \\[6pt]
={} & (-13) \cdot (-1) - 12 \\[6pt]
={} & 13 - 12 \\[6pt]
={} & 1
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the expression $(-15) : (+3) - (-2) \cdot (-4)$, division and multiplication come before subtraction. The quotient $(-15) : (+3)$ involves numbers with opposite signs and equals $-5$. The product $(-2) \cdot (-4)$ has factors with the same sign and equals $+8$. We also evaluate the product $(-6) \cdot (+2) = -12$, whose factors have opposite signs.

Subtracting $+8$ becomes adding $-8$, so $(-5) - (+8) = -5 - 8 = -13$. Multiplying $-13$ by $-1$ gives its opposite $+13$. Finally, $13 - 12 = 1$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Calculate
$$
\{\, [\, (-3)^2 - 4 \cdot (+2) \,]^3 - (-2)^2 \cdot (-5) \,\} : (-3) .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \{\, [\, (-3)^2 - 4 \cdot (+2) \,]^3 - (-2)^2 \cdot (-5) \,\} : (-3) \\[6pt]
={} & \{\, [\, 9 - 8 \,]^3 - 4 \cdot (-5) \,\} : (-3) \\[6pt]
={} & \{\, 1^3 - (-20) \,\} : (-3) \\[6pt]
={} & \{\, 1 + 20 \,\} : (-3) \\[6pt]
={} & 21 : (-3) \\[6pt]
={} & -7
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

First we evaluate the powers whose bases are numbers. The base $-3$ has an even exponent, so $(-3)^2 = +9$. The base $-2$ also has an even exponent, so $(-2)^2 = +4$. The product $4 \cdot (+2)$ equals $8$, so the expression $(-3)^2 - 4 \cdot (+2)$ equals $9 - 8 = 1$.

This expression is cubed, and $1^3 = 1 \cdot 1 \cdot 1 = 1$. The product $4 \cdot (-5)$ has factors with opposite signs and equals $-20$. We then subtract $-20$, which means adding $+20$, so $1 - (-20) = 1 + 20 = 21$.

Finally, the quotient $21 : (-3)$ involves numbers with opposite signs, and $21 : 3 = 7$. The expression therefore equals $-7$.

</div>
