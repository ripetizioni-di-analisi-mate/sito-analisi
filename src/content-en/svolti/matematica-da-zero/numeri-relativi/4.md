## Exercise 1

Calculate
$$
(-6) \cdot (+7) , \qquad (-9) \cdot (-4) , \qquad (+36) : (-9) , \qquad (-45) : (-5) .
$$

$$
(-6) \cdot (+7) = -(6 \cdot 7) = -42
$$
$$
(-9) \cdot (-4) = +(9 \cdot 4) = +36
$$
$$
(+36) : (-9) = -(36 : 9) = -4
$$
$$
(-45) : (-5) = +(45 : 5) = +9
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In each operation, the absolute value of the result is the product or quotient of the absolute values, and the **rule of signs** determines its sign.

In the product $(-6) \cdot (+7)$, the factors have opposite signs, so the product is negative and equals $-42$ because $6 \cdot 7 = 42$. In the product $(-9) \cdot (-4)$, the factors have the same sign, so the product is positive and equals $+36$.

The quotient follows the same rule. In $(+36) : (-9)$, the numbers have opposite signs and $36 : 9 = 4$, so the quotient is $-4$. In $(-45) : (-5)$, the numbers have the same sign and $45 : 5 = 9$, so the quotient is $+9$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Calculate
$$
(-2) \cdot (+3) \cdot (-5) \cdot (-1) .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(-2) \cdot (+3) \cdot (-5) \cdot (-1)
&= (-6) \cdot (-5) \cdot (-1) \\[6pt]
&= (+30) \cdot (-1) \\[6pt]
&= -30
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

There are three negative factors, namely $-2$, $-5$, and $-1$. Three is odd, so by the rule for a **product of several factors** the result is negative. The absolute value is $2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 1 = 30$, so the product equals $-30$.

Multiplying two factors at a time confirms the sign. The factors in $(-2) \cdot (+3)$ have opposite signs, so the product is $-6$. Then $(-6) \cdot (-5) = +30$ because the factors have the same sign. Finally, $(+30) \cdot (-1) = -30$ because multiplying by $-1$ gives the opposite of $+30$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Calculate
$$
(-48) : (+4) : (-3) .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(-48) : (+4) : (-3)
&= (-12) : (-3) \\[6pt]
&= +4
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

We divide in the order in which the operations appear, from left to right. In the first division, $(-48) : (+4)$, the numbers have opposite signs and $48 : 4 = 12$, so the quotient is $-12$.

In the second division, $(-12) : (-3)$, the numbers have the same sign and $12 : 3 = 4$, so the result is $+4$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Calculate
$$
(-4) \cdot (-5) : (-10) \cdot (+7) : (-2) .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& (-4) \cdot (-5) : (-10) \cdot (+7) : (-2) \\[6pt]
={} & (+20) : (-10) \cdot (+7) : (-2) \\[6pt]
={} & (-2) \cdot (+7) : (-2) \\[6pt]
={} & (-14) : (-2) \\[6pt]
={} & +7
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Multiplication and division have the same precedence, so we perform them one at a time from left to right. The product $(-4) \cdot (-5)$ has factors with the same sign and equals $+20$. The quotient $(+20) : (-10)$ involves numbers with opposite signs and equals $-2$.

Next, the product $(-2) \cdot (+7)$ has factors with opposite signs and equals $-14$. Finally, the quotient $(-14) : (-2)$ involves numbers with the same sign and equals $+7$.

We can also determine the sign of the result by counting the negative numbers. There are four: $-4$, $-5$, $-10$, and $-2$. Four is even, and division follows the same rule of signs as multiplication, so the result is **positive**.

</div>
