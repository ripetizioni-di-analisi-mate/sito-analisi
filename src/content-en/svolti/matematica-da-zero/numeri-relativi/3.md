## Exercise 1

Find the differences
$$
(+4) - (+10) , \qquad (-6) - (+3) , \qquad (+2) - (-9) , \qquad (-7) - (-12) .
$$

$$
(+4) - (+10) = (+4) + (-10) = -6
$$
$$
(-6) - (+3) = (-6) + (-3) = -9
$$
$$
(+2) - (-9) = (+2) + (+9) = +11
$$
$$
(-7) - (-12) = (-7) + (+12) = +5
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To subtract an integer, we add the **opposite** of the second number. In each difference, we therefore change the operation sign from $-$ to $+$, write the opposite of the second number, and then add.

In the first difference, the opposite of $+10$ is $-10$. The numbers $+4$ and $-10$ have opposite signs, and $-10$ has the greater absolute value, so the sum is $-(10 - 4) = -6$. In the second, the opposite of $+3$ is $-3$, and the numbers $-6$ and $-3$ have the same sign, so the sum is $-(6 + 3) = -9$.

In the third, the opposite of $-9$ is $+9$, and $(+2) + (+9) = +11$. In the fourth, the opposite of $-12$ is $+12$. The numbers $-7$ and $+12$ have opposite signs, and $+12$ has the greater absolute value, so the sum is $+(12 - 7) = +5$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Remove the parentheses and evaluate the algebraic sum
$$
(-5) - (+8) + (+3) - (-6) + (-1) .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& (-5) - (+8) + (+3) - (-6) + (-1) \\[6pt]
={} & -5 - 8 + 3 + 6 - 1 \\[6pt]
={} & (3 + 6) - (5 + 8 + 1) \\[6pt]
={} & 9 - 14 \\[6pt]
={} & -5
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

We write the first number with its sign and without parentheses, giving $-5$. For the others we use the equalities for an **algebraic sum**. A $+$ sign in front leaves the sign of the number unchanged, so $+(+3) = +3$ and $+(-1) = -1$. A $-$ sign in front changes the sign of the number, so $-(+8) = -8$ and $-(-6) = +6$.

In the algebraic sum $-5 - 8 + 3 + 6 - 1$, the positive terms are $3$ and $6$, and their sum is $9$. The negative terms are $-5$, $-8$, and $-1$, which together give $-14$. Finally, $9 - 14 = -5$ because the negative number has the greater absolute value.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Calculate
$$
12 - (7 - 15) - (4 + 9 - 20) .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& 12 - (7 - 15) - (4 + 9 - 20) \\[6pt]
={} & 12 - 7 + 15 - 4 - 9 + 20 \\[6pt]
={} & (12 + 15 + 20) - (7 + 4 + 9) \\[6pt]
={} & 47 - 20 \\[6pt]
={} & 27
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The expressions $7 - 15$ and $4 + 9 - 20$ are preceded by the sign $-$. By the rule for a **minus sign before an algebraic sum**, we remove the parentheses and reverse the sign of every term inside. Thus $-(7 - 15)$ becomes $-7 + 15$, and $-(4 + 9 - 20)$ becomes $-4 - 9 + 20$.

In the resulting algebraic sum, the positive terms are $12$, $15$, and $20$, and their sum is $47$. The negative terms are $-7$, $-4$, and $-9$, and together they give $-20$. Therefore the result is $47 - 20 = 27$.

We get the same result by evaluating the two expressions first, $7 - 15 = -8$ and $4 + 9 - 20 = -7$, so $12 - (-8) - (-7) = 12 + 8 + 7 = 27$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Calculate
$$
-3 - [\, 5 - (8 - 2) + (-4) \,] + (6 - 10) .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& -3 - [\, 5 - (8 - 2) + (-4) \,] + (6 - 10) \\[6pt]
={} & -3 - [\, 5 - 6 - 4 \,] + (-4) \\[6pt]
={} & -3 - (-5) - 4 \\[6pt]
={} & -3 + 5 - 4 \\[6pt]
={} & -2
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

First we evaluate the expressions in parentheses. We get $8 - 2 = 6$ and $6 - 10 = -4$. In the expression $5 - (8 - 2) + (-4)$, the term $-(8 - 2)$ therefore becomes $-6$ and the term $+(-4)$ becomes $-4$.

This leaves the algebraic sum $5 - 6 - 4$. The positive term is $5$ and the negative terms give $-10$, so the sum equals $5 - 10 = -5$. The sign $-$ before $-5$ gives $+5$, and the sign $+$ before $-4$ gives $-4$.

This leaves the algebraic sum $-3 + 5 - 4$. The positive term is $5$ and the negative terms give $-7$, so the result is $5 - 7 = -2$.

</div>
