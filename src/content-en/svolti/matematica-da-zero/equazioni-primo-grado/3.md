## Exercise 1

Solve the equation
$$
\frac{x}{4} + \frac{x}{6} = 5 .
$$

$$
\operatorname{mcm}(4, 6) = 12
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
12 \cdot \frac{x}{4} + 12 \cdot \frac{x}{6} &= 12 \cdot 5 \\[6pt]
3x + 2x &= 60 \\[6pt]
5x &= 60 \\[6pt]
x &= 12
\end{aligned}
$$

The equation has solution $x = 12$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The denominators are $4$ and $6$. Since $4 = 2^2$ and $6 = 2 \cdot 3$, their least common multiple is $2^2 \cdot 3 = 12$. By the **second principle**, we multiply every term on both sides by $12$, including the $5$ that has no denominator.

We have $12 \cdot \dfrac{x}{4} = 3x$ because $12 : 4 = 3$, and $12 \cdot \dfrac{x}{6} = 2x$ because $12 : 6 = 2$. On the right-hand side, $12 \cdot 5 = 60$. The equation without fractions is $5x = 60$, so $x = 12$.

Checking gives $\dfrac{12}{4} + \dfrac{12}{6} = 3 + 2 = 5$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve the equation
$$
\frac{x - 2}{4} = \frac{x + 1}{6} .
$$

$$
\operatorname{mcm}(4, 6) = 12
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
3\,(x - 2) &= 2\,(x + 1) \\[6pt]
3x - 6 &= 2x + 2 \\[6pt]
3x - 2x &= 2 + 6 \\[6pt]
x &= 8
\end{aligned}
$$

The equation has solution $x = 8$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The least common multiple of the denominators is again $12$. Multiplying the left-hand side by $12$ gives $3\,(x - 2)$ because $12 : 4 = 3$. Multiplying the right-hand side by $12$ gives $2\,(x + 1)$ because $12 : 6 = 2$. The factors $3$ and $2$ each multiply both terms of their respective numerators $x - 2$ and $x + 1$.

The distributive property gives $3x - 6 = 2x + 2$. Using the **transposition rule**, we move $2x$ to the left-hand side and $-6$ to the right, changing the sign of both terms, to obtain $x = 8$.

Checking gives $\dfrac{8 - 2}{4} = \dfrac{6}{4} = \dfrac{3}{2}$ and $\dfrac{8 + 1}{6} = \dfrac{9}{6} = \dfrac{3}{2}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve the equation
$$
\frac{2x + 1}{3} - \frac{x - 1}{2} = 1 - \frac{x}{6} .
$$

$$
\operatorname{mcm}(3, 2, 6) = 6
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
2\,(2x + 1) - 3\,(x - 1) &= 6 - x \\[6pt]
4x + 2 - 3x + 3 &= 6 - x \\[6pt]
x + 5 &= 6 - x \\[6pt]
x + x &= 6 - 5 \\[6pt]
2x &= 1 \\[6pt]
x &= \frac{1}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

The equation has solution $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The denominators are $3$, $2$, and $6$. The number $6$ is a multiple of both $3$ and $2$, so the least common multiple is $6$. We multiply each term by $6$. The first term becomes $2\,(2x + 1)$, the second becomes $3\,(x - 1)$, the number $1$ becomes $6$, and the last term becomes $x$.

In the second term, the minus sign before the fraction changes the sign of both terms of $3\,(x - 1) = 3x - 3$, so we write $-3x + 3$. The left-hand side becomes $4x - 3x + 2 + 3 = x + 5$.

Using the **transposition rule**, we move $-x$ to the left-hand side and $5$ to the right to obtain $2x = 1$. Dividing both sides by $2$ yields $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

To check, when $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ we have $2x + 1 = 2$ and $x - 1 = -\dfrac{1}{2}$. The first fraction therefore equals $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and the second equals $-\dfrac{1}{2} : 2 = -\dfrac{1}{4}$. The left-hand side is therefore $\dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{8}{12} + \dfrac{3}{12} = \dfrac{11}{12}$. On the right-hand side, $\dfrac{x}{6} = \dfrac{1}{12}$, so the right-hand side equals $1 - \dfrac{1}{12} = \dfrac{11}{12}$.

</div>
