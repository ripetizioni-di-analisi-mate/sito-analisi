## Exercise 1

The area of a triangle is $A = \dfrac{b \cdot h}{2}$, where $b$ is the base and $h$ is the height. Rearrange the formula to solve for the height $h$ and calculate $h$ for a triangle with $A = 30$ cm² and $b = 12$ cm.

$$
\begin{aligned}
A &= \frac{b \cdot h}{2} \\[6pt]
2A &= b \cdot h \\[6pt]
h &= \frac{2A}{b}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
h = \frac{2 \cdot 30}{12} = \frac{60}{12} = 5
$$

The height of the triangle is $h = 5$ cm.

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 12,0 4,5" linee="0,0 12,0 4,5 0,0;4,0 4,5;4,0.6 4.6,0.6 4.6,0" etichette="5.3,-0.9,b = 12 cm;4.3,2.3,h = 5 cm;5.6,1.2,A = 30 cm²" xmin=-1 xmax=13 ymin=-1.6 ymax=6 titolo="The triangle has area $A = 30$ cm² and base $b = 12$ cm, so its height is $h = \dfrac{2 \cdot 30}{12} = 5$ cm"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The quantity to find is $h$, which we treat as an unknown. In the formula, $h$ is multiplied by $b$ and divided by $2$. By the **second principle**, we multiply both sides by $2$ to obtain $2A = b \cdot h$, with no denominator.

The base $b$ is a length, so it is nonzero. Dividing both sides by $b$ gives the rearranged formula $h = \dfrac{2A}{b}$.

Substituting $A = 30$ and $b = 12$ gives $h = \dfrac{60}{12} = 5$. Checking gives $\dfrac{12 \cdot 5}{2} = \dfrac{60}{2} = 30$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The perimeter of a rectangle with base $b$ and height $h$ is $2p = 2\,(b + h)$. Rearrange the formula to solve for $h$ and calculate $h$ for a rectangle with perimeter $34$ cm and base $10$ cm.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2p &= 2\,(b + h) \\[6pt]
p &= b + h \\[6pt]
h &= p - b
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
p = \frac{34}{2} = 17,
\qquad
h = 17 - 10 = 7
$$

The height of the rectangle is $h = 7$ cm.

<div class="spiegazione">

The symbol $2p$ denotes the perimeter, so $p$ is the semiperimeter, that is, half the perimeter. Dividing both sides by $2$, by the **second principle**, gives $p = b + h$.

By the **first principle**, we subtract $b$ from both sides to obtain the rearranged formula $h = p - b$. In other words, the height is the semiperimeter minus the base.

The perimeter is $34$ cm, so the semiperimeter is $17$ cm. The height is therefore $17 - 10 = 7$ cm. Checking gives $2 \cdot (10 + 7) = 2 \cdot 17 = 34$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The area of a trapezoid is $A = \dfrac{(B + b) \cdot h}{2}$, where $B$ and $b$ are its two bases and $h$ is its height. Rearrange the formula to solve for the shorter base $b$ and calculate $b$ for a trapezoid with $A = 40$ cm², $h = 4$ cm, and $B = 12$ cm.

$$
\begin{aligned}
A &= \frac{(B + b) \cdot h}{2} \\[6pt]
2A &= (B + b) \cdot h \\[6pt]
\frac{2A}{h} &= B + b \\[6pt]
b &= \frac{2A}{h} - B
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
b = \frac{2 \cdot 40}{4} - 12 = \frac{80}{4} - 12 = 20 - 12 = 8
$$

The shorter base of the trapezoid is $b = 8$ cm.

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 12,0 10,4 2,4" linee="0,0 12,0 10,4 2,4 0,0;2,0 2,4;2,0.6 2.6,0.6 2.6,0" etichette="5.3,-0.9,B = 12 cm;4.7,4.6,b = 8 cm;2.3,1.9,h = 4 cm;6,1.7,A = 40 cm²" xmin=-1 xmax=13 ymin=-1.6 ymax=5.6 titolo="The trapezoid has bases $B = 12$ cm and $b = 8$ cm and height $h = 4$ cm, so its area is $A = \dfrac{(12 + 8) \cdot 4}{2} = 40$ cm²"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The unknown $b$ appears in the sum $B + b$, which is multiplied by $h$ and divided by $2$. We first undo the operations applied to the whole sum. Multiplying both sides by $2$ gives $2A = (B + b) \cdot h$.

The height $h$ is nonzero, so by the **second principle** we divide both sides by $h$, leaving $\dfrac{2A}{h} = B + b$. Finally, by the **first principle**, we subtract $B$ from both sides to obtain $b = \dfrac{2A}{h} - B$.

Using the given values, $\dfrac{2 \cdot 40}{4} = 20$, so $b = 20 - 12 = 8$. Checking gives $\dfrac{(12 + 8) \cdot 4}{2} = \dfrac{80}{2} = 40$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

A water bill includes a fixed charge of $12$ euros plus $0.25$ euros for each cubic meter of water used. The total cost is $C = 12 + 0.25\,m$, where $m$ is the amount used in cubic meters. Rearrange the formula to solve for $m$ and calculate how much water a family uses if its bill is $87$ euros.

$$
\begin{aligned}
C &= 12 + 0.25\,m \\[6pt]
C - 12 &= 0.25\,m \\[6pt]
m &= \frac{C - 12}{0.25}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
m = \frac{87 - 12}{0.25} = \frac{75}{0.25} = 75 \cdot 4 = 300
$$

The family used $300$ cubic meters of water.

<div class="spiegazione">

By the **first principle**, we subtract the fixed charge from both sides to obtain $C - 12 = 0.25\,m$. The number $0.25$ is nonzero, so by the **second principle** we divide both sides by $0.25$ to obtain the rearranged formula.

When $C = 87$, we have $87 - 12 = 75$. The number $0.25$ equals $\dfrac{1}{4}$, and dividing by $\dfrac{1}{4}$ is equivalent to multiplying by its reciprocal $4$. Therefore $m = 75 \cdot 4 = 300$.

Checking gives $12 + 0.25 \cdot 300 = 12 + 75 = 87$.

</div>
