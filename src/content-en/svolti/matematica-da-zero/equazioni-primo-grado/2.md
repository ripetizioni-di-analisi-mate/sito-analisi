## Exercise 1

Solve the equation $3\,(x - 4) = x + 2$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
3\,(x - 4) &= x + 2 \\[6pt]
3x - 12 &= x + 2 \\[6pt]
3x - x &= 2 + 12 \\[6pt]
2x &= 14 \\[6pt]
x &= 7
\end{aligned}
$$

The equation has solution $x = 7$.

::grafico{f="3*x-12;x+2" punti="7,9,pieno" segmenti="7,0,7,9" xmin=-1 xmax=10 ymin=-14 ymax=20 legenda="y = 3x - 12;y = x + 2" titolo="The lines $y = 3x - 12$ and $y = x + 2$ intersect at $(7, 9)$, so the equation $3\,(x - 4) = x + 2$ has solution $x = 7$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The first step is to expand the product. By the distributive property, $3$ multiplies both terms of $x - 4$, so $3\,(x - 4) = 3x - 12$.

Using the **transposition rule**, we move $x$ to the left-hand side, where it becomes $-x$, and $-12$ to the right-hand side, where it becomes $+12$. This gives $2x = 14$, and dividing both sides by $2$ yields $x = 7$.

Checking gives $3 \cdot (7 - 4) = 3 \cdot 3 = 9$ on the left-hand side and $7 + 2 = 9$ on the right, so the solution is correct.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve the equation $5\,(x + 1) - 2\,(3x - 4) = 2x - 5$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
5\,(x + 1) - 2\,(3x - 4) &= 2x - 5 \\[6pt]
5x + 5 - 6x + 8 &= 2x - 5 \\[6pt]
-x + 13 &= 2x - 5 \\[6pt]
-x - 2x &= -5 - 13 \\[6pt]
-3x &= -18 \\[6pt]
x &= 6
\end{aligned}
$$

The equation has solution $x = 6$.

<div class="spiegazione">

We expand the two products using the distributive property. The first gives $5x + 5$. In the second, the factor is $-2$, which multiplies both $3x$ and $-4$, so the product is $-6x + 8$ because $(-2) \cdot (-4) = 8$ by the sign rule.

On the left-hand side, we combine like terms. The terms containing $x$ give $5x - 6x = -x$, and the numbers give $5 + 8 = 13$, so the left-hand side is $-x + 13$.

Using the **transposition rule**, we move $2x$ to the left-hand side and $13$ to the right, changing the sign of both terms. This gives $-3x = -18$. Dividing both sides by $-3$ yields $x = \dfrac{-18}{-3} = 6$ because the quotient of two negative numbers is positive.

Checking gives $5 \cdot (6 + 1) - 2 \cdot (18 - 4) = 35 - 28 = 7$ on the left-hand side and $2 \cdot 6 - 5 = 12 - 5 = 7$ on the right.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve the equation $(x + 2)(x - 3) = x^2 + 4$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x + 2)(x - 3) &= x^2 + 4 \\[6pt]
x^2 - 3x + 2x - 6 &= x^2 + 4 \\[6pt]
x^2 - x - 6 &= x^2 + 4 \\[6pt]
-x &= 4 + 6 \\[6pt]
-x &= 10 \\[6pt]
x &= -10
\end{aligned}
$$

The equation has solution $x = -10$.

<div class="spiegazione">

We expand a product of two polynomials by multiplying each term of the first by each term of the second. This gives $x \cdot x = x^2$, $x \cdot (-3) = -3x$, $2 \cdot x = 2x$, and $2 \cdot (-3) = -6$. The terms $-3x$ and $2x$ are like terms and combine to give $-x$.

The term $x^2$ appears on both sides. By the **first principle**, we subtract it from both sides, so the equation is linear. Moving $-6$ to the right-hand side gives $-x = 10$, and multiplying both sides by $-1$ yields $x = -10$.

Checking gives $(-10 + 2)(-10 - 3) = (-8) \cdot (-13) = 104$ on the left-hand side and $(-10)^2 + 4 = 100 + 4 = 104$ on the right.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Solve the equation $(2x - 1)^2 - 4\,(x + 1)(x - 1) = 9$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(2x - 1)^2 - 4\,(x + 1)(x - 1) &= 9 \\[6pt]
4x^2 - 4x + 1 - 4\,(x^2 - 1) &= 9 \\[6pt]
4x^2 - 4x + 1 - 4x^2 + 4 &= 9 \\[6pt]
-4x + 5 &= 9 \\[6pt]
-4x &= 4 \\[6pt]
x &= -1
\end{aligned}
$$

The equation has solution $x = -1$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The equation contains two special products. By the **square of a binomial** formula, we have $(2x - 1)^2 = (2x)^2 - 2 \cdot 2x \cdot 1 + 1^2 = 4x^2 - 4x + 1$. By the **product of a sum and a difference** formula, we have $(x + 1)(x - 1) = x^2 - 1$.

The factor $-4$ multiplies both terms of $x^2 - 1$, giving $-4x^2 + 4$. The terms $4x^2$ and $-4x^2$ cancel, and the numbers give $1 + 4 = 5$. The left-hand side reduces to $-4x + 5$.

Moving $5$ to the right-hand side gives $-4x = 9 - 5 = 4$. Dividing both sides by $-4$ yields $x = -1$.

To check, $(2 \cdot (-1) - 1)^2 = (-3)^2 = 9$, whereas $(x + 1)(x - 1)$ with $x = -1$ equals $0 \cdot (-2) = 0$. The left-hand side therefore equals $9 - 0 = 9$, the same as the right-hand side.

</div>
