## Exercise 1

Solve the equation $4x - 9 = 11$ and check the solution.

$$
\begin{aligned}
4x - 9 &= 11 \\[6pt]
4x &= 11 + 9 \\[6pt]
4x &= 20 \\[6pt]
x &= \frac{20}{4} \\[6pt]
x &= 5
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
4 \cdot 5 - 9 = 20 - 9 = 11
$$

The equation has solution $x = 5$.

::grafico{f="4*x-9;11" punti="5,11,pieno" segmenti="5,0,5,11" xmin=-1 xmax=8 ymin=-12 ymax=16 legenda="y = 4x - 9;y = 11" titolo="The lines $y = 4x - 9$ and $y = 11$ intersect at $(5, 11)$, so the equation $4x - 9 = 11$ has solution $x = 5$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The left-hand side contains the term $-9$, which we move to the right-hand side using the **transposition rule**. As the term crosses the $=$ sign, its sign changes, so it becomes $+9$, and the right-hand side becomes $11 + 9 = 20$.

In the equation $4x = 20$, the number $4$ multiplies $x$. By the **second principle**, we divide both sides by $4$, which is nonzero, to obtain $x = \dfrac{20}{4} = 5$.

To check, substitute $5$ for $x$ on the left-hand side. This gives $4 \cdot 5 - 9 = 20 - 9 = 11$, which equals the right-hand side, so $x = 5$ is the solution.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve the equation $7x + 2 = 3x - 10$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
7x + 2 &= 3x - 10 \\[6pt]
7x - 3x &= -10 - 2 \\[6pt]
4x &= -12 \\[6pt]
x &= \frac{-12}{4} \\[6pt]
x &= -3
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
7 \cdot (-3) + 2 = -21 + 2 = -19,
\qquad
3 \cdot (-3) - 10 = -9 - 10 = -19
$$

The equation has solution $x = -3$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The unknown $x$ appears on both sides. Using the **transposition rule**, we move $3x$ to the left-hand side, where it becomes $-3x$, and $+2$ to the right-hand side, where it becomes $-2$. On the left we have $7x - 3x = 4x$, and on the right we have $-10 - 2 = -12$.

We divide both sides by $4$. The quotient of a negative number and a positive number is negative by the sign rule, so $\dfrac{-12}{4} = -3$.

To check, the product $7 \cdot (-3)$ equals $-21$, and adding $2$ gives $-19$. On the right-hand side, $3 \cdot (-3) = -9$, and subtracting $10$ again gives $-19$. The two sides are equal, so $x = -3$ is the solution.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve the equation $5 - 2x = 3x + 8 - x$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
5 - 2x &= 3x + 8 - x \\[6pt]
5 - 2x &= 2x + 8 \\[6pt]
-2x - 2x &= 8 - 5 \\[6pt]
-4x &= 3 \\[6pt]
x &= \frac{3}{-4} \\[6pt]
x &= -\frac{3}{4}
\end{aligned}
$$

The equation has solution $x = -\dfrac{3}{4}$.

<div class="spiegazione">

Before moving any terms, we combine the like terms on the right-hand side. The terms $3x$ and $-x$ both contain $x$, so $3x - x = 2x$, and the right-hand side becomes $2x + 8$.

Using the **transposition rule**, we move $2x$ to the left-hand side, where it becomes $-2x$, and $5$ to the right-hand side, where it becomes $-5$. On the left we have $-2x - 2x = -4x$, and on the right we have $8 - 5 = 3$.

We divide both sides by $-4$. A positive number divided by a negative number gives a negative result, so $x = -\dfrac{3}{4}$.

To check, the left-hand side equals $5 - 2 \cdot \left( -\dfrac{3}{4} \right) = 5 + \dfrac{6}{4} = \dfrac{10}{2} + \dfrac{3}{2} = \dfrac{13}{2}$. The right-hand side, already simplified, equals $2 \cdot \left( -\dfrac{3}{4} \right) + 8 = -\dfrac{3}{2} + \dfrac{16}{2} = \dfrac{13}{2}$. The two values are equal, so the solution is correct.

</div>
