## Exercise 1

Calculate the perimeter and area of a rectangle with base $9$ cm and height $5$ cm.

$$
2p = 2 \cdot (9 + 5) = 2 \cdot 14 = 28\ \text{cm}
$$
$$
A = 9 \cdot 5 = 45\ \text{cm}^2
$$
::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 9,0 9,5 0,5" segmenti="1,0,1,5;2,0,2,5;3,0,3,5;4,0,4,5;5,0,5,5;6,0,6,5;7,0,7,5;8,0,8,5;0,1,9,1;0,2,9,2;0,3,9,3;0,4,9,4" etichette="4.1,-0.8,9 cm;-1.5,2.4,5 cm" xmin=-2.6 xmax=9.8 ymin=-1.6 ymax=5.8 titolo="The rectangle with base $9$ cm and height $5$ cm contains $5$ rows of $9$ squares with side length $1$ cm, giving $45\ \text{cm}^2$"}


<div class="spiegazione">

The rectangle has two sides of $9$ cm and two sides of $5$ cm. Its perimeter is the sum of its four side lengths, so add the base and height, $9 + 5 = 14$, and multiply by $2$. The perimeter is therefore $28$ cm.

The area of the rectangle is the product of its base and height, or $9 \cdot 5 = 45$. In simple terms, the rectangle contains $5$ rows of $9$ squares with side length $1$ cm. The area is therefore $45\ \text{cm}^2$. It is measured in square centimeters because it is the product of two lengths in centimeters.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A square has perimeter $36$ cm. Find its side length and its area.

$$
l = \frac{2p}{4} = \frac{36}{4} = 9\ \text{cm}
$$
$$
A = l^2 = 9^2 = 81\ \text{cm}^2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

A square has four equal sides, so its perimeter is $4$ times the side length. The side length is therefore the perimeter divided by $4$. Since $4 \cdot 9 = 36$, it measures $9$ cm.

The area of the square is the side length multiplied by itself, or $9 \cdot 9 = 81$. Its area is therefore $81\ \text{cm}^2$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A rectangle has area $48\ \text{cm}^2$ and base $8$ cm. Find its height and perimeter.

$$
h = \frac{A}{b} = \frac{48}{8} = 6\ \text{cm}
$$
$$
2p = 2 \cdot (8 + 6) = 2 \cdot 14 = 28\ \text{cm}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The area of the rectangle is base times height, so $8 \cdot h = 48$. Its height is therefore the number that, when multiplied by $8$, gives $48$, that is, the quotient $\dfrac{48}{8} = 6$. Its height is $6$ cm.

Once the base and height are known, the perimeter is twice their sum. Since $8 + 6 = 14$ and $2 \cdot 14 = 28$, the perimeter is $28$ cm.

</div>

## Exercise 4

A rectangular rug measures $250$ cm by $80$ cm. Find its area in square meters and its perimeter in meters.

$$
A = 250 \cdot 80 = 20\,000\ \text{cm}^2
$$
$$
A = \frac{20\,000}{10\,000}\ \text{m}^2 = 2\ \text{m}^2
$$
$$
250\ \text{cm} = 2.5\ \text{m}, \qquad 80\ \text{cm} = 0.8\ \text{m}
$$
$$
2p = 2 \cdot (2.5 + 0.8) = 2 \cdot 3.3 = 6.6\ \text{m}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The area in square centimeters is the product $250 \cdot 80$. Since $25 \cdot 8 = 200$ and the two factors have a total of two trailing zeros, the product is $20\,000\ \text{cm}^2$. A square meter is the area of a square with side length $100$ cm, so it contains $100 \cdot 100 = 10\,000\ \text{cm}^2$. To convert to square meters, therefore, divide by $10\,000$. The area is $2\ \text{m}^2$.

We get the same result by first converting the lengths to meters. From centimeters to meters there are two steps in the sequence of length units, so divide by $100$ to obtain $2.5$ m and $0.8$ m. The product $2.5 \cdot 0.8$ equals $2$ because $25 \cdot 8 = 200$ and the two factors have a total of two decimal places. The perimeter is twice $2.5 + 0.8 = 3.3$, or $6.6$ m.

</div>
