## Exercise 1

Find the circumference and area of a disk of radius $6$ cm. Give the results in terms of $\pi$ and then using the approximation $\pi \approx 3.14$.

$$
C = 2\pi r = 2 \cdot \pi \cdot 6 = 12\pi\ \text{cm}
$$
$$
C \approx 12 \cdot 3.14 = 37.68\ \text{cm}
$$
$$
A = \pi r^2 = \pi \cdot 6^2 = 36\pi\ \text{cm}^2
$$
$$
A \approx 36 \cdot 3.14 = 113.04\ \text{cm}^2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The circumference is $2\pi r$, or $\pi$ times the diameter. The diameter is $2 \cdot 6 = 12$ cm, so the circumference is $C = 12\pi$ cm. Using the approximate value of $\pi$ gives the calculation $12 \cdot 3.14$. Since $12 \cdot 3 = 36$ and $12 \cdot 0.14 = 1.68$, the result is $C \approx 37.68$ cm.

The area of the disk is $\pi$ times the square of the radius. The square of the radius is $6 \cdot 6 = 36$, so $A = 36\pi\ \text{cm}^2$. Since $36 \cdot 3 = 108$ and $36 \cdot 0.14 = 5.04$, the area is approximately $113.04\ \text{cm}^2$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A disk has diameter $18$ cm. Find its area in terms of $\pi$ and using $\pi \approx 3.14$.

$$
r = \frac{d}{2} = \frac{18}{2} = 9\ \text{cm}
$$
$$
A = \pi \cdot 9^2 = 81\pi\ \text{cm}^2
$$
$$
A \approx 81 \cdot 3.14 = 254.34\ \text{cm}^2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The area formula uses the radius, which is half the diameter, so the radius is $r = 9$ cm. The square of the radius is $9 \cdot 9 = 81$, so the area is $81\pi\ \text{cm}^2$.

For an approximate value, calculate $81 \cdot 3.14$. Since $81 \cdot 3 = 243$ and $81 \cdot 0.14 = 11.34$, the sum is $243 + 11.34 = 254.34$. The area is approximately $254.34\ \text{cm}^2$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A disk has area $49\pi\ \text{cm}^2$. Find the radius and circumference in terms of $\pi$ and using $\pi \approx 3.14$.

$$
\pi \cdot r^2 = 49\pi
$$
$$
r^2 = 49
$$
$$
r = \sqrt{49} = 7\ \text{cm}
$$
$$
C = 2 \cdot \pi \cdot 7 = 14\pi \approx 14 \cdot 3.14 = 43.96\ \text{cm}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The area of the disk is $\pi r^2$, so $\pi \cdot r^2 = 49\pi$. Dividing both sides by $\pi$ gives $r^2 = 49$. The radius is a length and therefore positive. The positive number whose square is $49$ is $7$. The radius is therefore $7$ cm.

The circumference is $2\pi r = 2 \cdot 7 \cdot \pi = 14\pi$ cm. Since $14 \cdot 3 = 42$ and $14 \cdot 0.14 = 1.96$, the length is approximately $43.96$ cm.

</div>
