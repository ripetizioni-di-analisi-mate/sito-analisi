## Exercise 1

Calculate $5a^2b - 2a^2b + 4a^2b$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
5a^2b - 2a^2b + 4a^2b &= (5 - 2 + 4)\,a^2b \\[6pt]
&= 7a^2b
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The three monomials have the same variable part $a^2b$, so they are **like terms**. The sum of like monomials is a monomial with the same variable part and a coefficient equal to the sum of their coefficients.

The coefficients are $5$, $-2$, and $4$. Their sum is $5 - 2 + 4 = 7$, so the result is $7a^2b$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Calculate the product $\left( -\dfrac{2}{3}x^2y \right) \cdot \left( \dfrac{9}{4}xy^3 \right)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\left( -\frac{2}{3}x^2y \right) \cdot \left( \frac{9}{4}xy^3 \right) \\[6pt]
&= -\frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{9}{4} \cdot x^2 \cdot x \cdot y \cdot y^3 \\[6pt]
&= -\frac{18}{12}\,x^{2 + 1}y^{1 + 3} \\[6pt]
&= -\frac{3}{2}\,x^3y^4
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the rule for the **product of monomials**, multiply the coefficients together and, separately, multiply powers of the same variable.

The product of the coefficients is $-\dfrac{2}{3} \cdot \dfrac{9}{4} = -\dfrac{18}{12}$, with a minus sign because one factor is negative and the other positive. The numerator and denominator are both divisible by $6$, so the fraction simplifies to $-\dfrac{3}{2}$. For the variables, add the exponents: $x^2 \cdot x = x^{2 + 1} = x^3$ because $x$ has exponent $1$, and $y \cdot y^3 = y^{1 + 3} = y^4$. The product is therefore $-\dfrac{3}{2}x^3y^4$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Calculate the powers $\left( -3a^2b^3 \right)^2$ and $\left( -2xy^2 \right)^3$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left( -3a^2b^3 \right)^2 &= (-3)^2 \cdot \left( a^2 \right)^2 \cdot \left( b^3 \right)^2 \\[6pt]
&= 9a^4b^6
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\left( -2xy^2 \right)^3 &= (-2)^3 \cdot x^3 \cdot \left( y^2 \right)^3 \\[6pt]
&= -8x^3y^6
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the rule for the **power of a monomial**, raise the coefficient to the given power and multiply the exponent of each variable by the exponent of the power.

In the first power the coefficient is $-3$, and $(-3)^2 = 9$ is positive because the exponent is even. The exponents of the variables are multiplied by $2$, so $a^2$ becomes $a^4$ and $b^3$ becomes $b^6$.

In the second power, $(-2)^3 = (-2) \cdot (-2) \cdot (-2) = -8$, which is negative because the exponent is odd. The variable $x$ has exponent $1$, so it becomes $x^3$, and $y^2$ becomes $y^{2 \cdot 3} = y^6$. The result is therefore $-8x^3y^6$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

For $a \neq 0$ and $b \neq 0$, simplify the expression $\dfrac{\left( -2a^2b \right)^3}{4a^3b^2} + 5a^3b$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\left( -2a^2b \right)^3}{4a^3b^2} + 5a^3b &= \frac{-8a^6b^3}{4a^3b^2} + 5a^3b \\[6pt]
&= -2a^{6 - 3}b^{3 - 2} + 5a^3b \\[6pt]
&= -2a^3b + 5a^3b \\[6pt]
&= 3a^3b
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

As in numerical expressions, first evaluate the power, then divide, and finally add. The power $\left( -2a^2b \right)^3$ equals $-8a^6b^3$ because $(-2)^3 = -8$, $\left( a^2 \right)^3 = a^6$, and $b$ becomes $b^3$.

The divisor $4a^3b^2$ is nonzero because $a \neq 0$ and $b \neq 0$. By the rule for the **quotient of monomials**, divide the coefficients, $\dfrac{-8}{4} = -2$, and subtract the exponents of each variable, giving $a^{6 - 3} = a^3$ and $b^{3 - 2} = b$. The quotient is therefore $-2a^3b$.

Finally, $-2a^3b$ and $5a^3b$ are like monomials. Their sum has coefficient $-2 + 5 = 3$, so the expression equals $3a^3b$.

</div>
