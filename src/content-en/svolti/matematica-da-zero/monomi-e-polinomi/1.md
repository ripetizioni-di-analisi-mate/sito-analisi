## Exercise 1

Evaluate the expression $2a + 3b$ for $a = 4$ and $b = 5$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2a + 3b &= 2 \cdot 4 + 3 \cdot 5 \\[6pt]
&= 8 + 15 \\[6pt]
&= 23
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,1.6 0,1.6;4,0 8,0 8,1.6 4,1.6" linee="0,0 23,0 23,1.6 0,1.6 0,0;4,0 4,1.6;8,0 8,1.6;13,0 13,1.6;18,0 18,1.6" etichette="1.8,0.55,a;5.8,0.55,a;10.2,0.55,b;15.2,0.55,b;20.2,0.55,b" xmin=-1 xmax=24 ymin=-1 ymax=3 titolo="The segment consists of $2$ pieces of length $a = 4$ and $3$ pieces of length $b = 5$, so its length is $2a + 3b = 8 + 15 = 23$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

To find the **value** of the expression, substitute $4$ for $a$ and $5$ for $b$. The multiplication sign is omitted between a number and a letter, so $2a$ means $2 \cdot a$. After substitution, the dot is written again, and $2a$ becomes $2 \cdot 4$.

Multiplication comes before addition. We have $2 \cdot 4 = 8$ and $3 \cdot 5 = 15$, so the expression equals $8 + 15 = 23$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Evaluate the polynomial $x^2 - 3x + 2$ for $x = -1$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^2 - 3x + 2 &= (-1)^2 - 3 \cdot (-1) + 2 \\[6pt]
&= 1 + 3 + 2 \\[6pt]
&= 6
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The number $-1$ is negative, so in place of each $x$ we write $(-1)$. This keeps the minus sign inside the power and inside the product.

First evaluate the power. The square $(-1)^2$ is $(-1) \cdot (-1)$, which equals $1$ because the product of two negative numbers is positive. Next, calculate the product $3 \cdot (-1) = -3$. With the minus sign in front, this gives $+3$ because the opposite of $-3$ is $3$. The remaining additions are $1 + 3 + 2$, so the **value** of the polynomial is $6$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Evaluate the expression $3xy - y^2$ for $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $y = -4$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
3xy - y^2 &= 3 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot (-4) - (-4)^2 \\[6pt]
&= -6 - 16 \\[6pt]
&= -22
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The term $3xy$ is the product of three numbers, $3$, $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $-4$. We have $3 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{2}$ and then $\dfrac{3}{2} \cdot (-4) = -\dfrac{12}{2} = -6$, with a minus sign by the sign rule.

In the term $y^2$, write $(-4)^2$ because the entire number, including its sign, is squared. Therefore $(-4)^2 = (-4) \cdot (-4) = 16$, and the minus sign in front gives $-16$. The expression therefore equals $-6 - 16 = -22$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Determine the values of the variables for which $\dfrac{a^2 - b}{a + b}$ is defined, and evaluate the expression for $a = -3$ and $b = 1$.

$$
a + b \neq 0
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{a^2 - b}{a + b} &= \frac{(-3)^2 - 1}{-3 + 1} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{9 - 1}{-2} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{8}{-2} = -4
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Division by zero is undefined, so the denominator $a + b$ must be nonzero. The expression is therefore defined for all values of $a$ and $b$ such that $a + b \neq 0$, that is, $b \neq -a$.

For $a = -3$ and $b = 1$, the denominator is $-3 + 1 = -2$, which is nonzero, so the calculation is possible. In the numerator, $(-3)^2 = 9$ because the square of a negative number is positive, and $9 - 1 = 8$. The quotient of $8$ by $-2$ is negative by the sign rule, and the expression equals $-4$.

</div>
