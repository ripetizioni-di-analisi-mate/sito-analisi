## Exercise 1

Simplify the polynomial $3x - 2x^2 + 5 + x^2 - 7x + x^3$, write it in descending order, and state its degree.

$$
\begin{aligned}
&3x - 2x^2 + 5 + x^2 - 7x + x^3 \\[6pt]
&= x^3 + \left( -2x^2 + x^2 \right) + (3x - 7x) + 5 \\[6pt]
&= x^3 - x^2 - 4x + 5
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To simplify the polynomial, group like terms, those with the same variable part, writing them in descending order of degree as you go. The term $x^3$ is the only one of degree $3$. The terms $-2x^2$ and $x^2$ are like terms, and their sum has coefficient $-2 + 1 = -1$, so it equals $-x^2$. The terms $3x$ and $-7x$ give $(3 - 7)\,x = -4x$. The constant term $5$ stays the same.

The simplified polynomial **in descending order** is $x^3 - x^2 - 4x + 5$. Its highest-degree term is $x^3$, so the polynomial has degree $3$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Calculate $\left( 4a^2 - 3a + 2 \right) - \left( a^2 - 5a + 6 \right)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\left( 4a^2 - 3a + 2 \right) - \left( a^2 - 5a + 6 \right) \\[6pt]
&= 4a^2 - 3a + 2 - a^2 + 5a - 6 \\[6pt]
&= 3a^2 + 2a - 4
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first polynomial has an implied plus sign in front, so its terms retain their signs. The second polynomial is preceded by a minus sign, which reverses the sign of *each* of its terms. Therefore $a^2$ becomes $-a^2$, $-5a$ becomes $+5a$, and $6$ becomes $-6$.

Next, combine like terms. For $a^2$ we have $4 - 1 = 3$, for $a$ we have $-3 + 5 = 2$, and for the constants we have $2 - 6 = -4$. The difference is therefore $3a^2 + 2a - 4$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Calculate $2x\,(3x - 4) - x\,(x + 5)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x\,(3x - 4) - x\,(x + 5) &= 6x^2 - 8x - x^2 - 5x \\[6pt]
&= 5x^2 - 13x
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Expand each product of a monomial and a polynomial using the **distributive property**. The first gives $2x \cdot 3x = 6x^2$ and $2x \cdot (-4) = -8x$. In the second, the monomial is $-x$, including the minus sign in front, so $-x \cdot x = -x^2$ and $-x \cdot 5 = -5x$.

Next, combine like terms, giving $6x^2 - x^2 = 5x^2$ and $-8x - 5x = -13x$. The result is $5x^2 - 13x$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Calculate $-3ab\left( a^2 - 2ab + b^2 \right) + 2a^2b\,(a - b)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
&-3ab\left( a^2 - 2ab + b^2 \right) + 2a^2b\,(a - b) \\[6pt]
&= -3a^3b + 6a^2b^2 - 3ab^3 + 2a^3b - 2a^2b^2 \\[6pt]
&= -a^3b + 4a^2b^2 - 3ab^3
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the first product, the monomial $-3ab$ multiplies each of the three terms. This gives $-3ab \cdot a^2 = -3a^3b$, then $-3ab \cdot (-2ab) = +6a^2b^2$, which is positive because both coefficients are negative, and finally $-3ab \cdot b^2 = -3ab^3$. The second product gives $2a^2b \cdot a = 2a^3b$ and $2a^2b \cdot (-b) = -2a^2b^2$.

There are two pairs of like terms. The terms in $a^3b$ have coefficients $-3$ and $2$ with sum $-1$, so they give $-a^3b$. The terms in $a^2b^2$ have coefficients $6$ and $-2$ with sum $4$. The term $-3ab^3$ has no like terms and stays the same. The result is therefore $-a^3b + 4a^2b^2 - 3ab^3$.

</div>
