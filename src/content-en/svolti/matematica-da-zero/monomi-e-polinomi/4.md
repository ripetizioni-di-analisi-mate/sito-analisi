## Exercise 1

Calculate $(x + 4)(x - 3)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x + 4)(x - 3) &= x \cdot x + x \cdot (-3) + 4 \cdot x + 4 \cdot (-3) \\[6pt]
&= x^2 - 3x + 4x - 12 \\[6pt]
&= x^2 + x - 12
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,3 0,3" linee="0,0 10,0 10,3 0,3 0,0;6,0 6,3" etichette="2.6,1.2,x²−3x;7.6,1.2,4x−12;2.8,-0.6,x;7.8,-0.6,4;-1,1.2,x−3" xmin=-2 xmax=11 ymin=-1 ymax=4 titolo="The rectangle with side lengths $x + 4$ and $x - 3$ is divided into two parts with areas $x(x - 3)$ and $4(x - 3)$. For $x = 6$, its sides measure $10$ and $3$, and the two areas are $18$ and $12$, for a total of $30 = 6^2 + 6 - 12$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

By the rule for the **product of polynomials**, multiply each term of the first binomial by each term of the second. The four products are $x \cdot x = x^2$, $x \cdot (-3) = -3x$, $4 \cdot x = 4x$, and $4 \cdot (-3) = -12$.

The terms $-3x$ and $4x$ are like terms and their sum is $(-3 + 4)\,x = x$. The product is therefore $x^2 + x - 12$. For $x = 1$ the original product equals $5 \cdot (-2) = -10$, and the result also equals $1 + 1 - 12 = -10$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Calculate $(2a - 3b)(a + 5b)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(2a - 3b)(a + 5b) &= 2a \cdot a + 2a \cdot 5b - 3b \cdot a - 3b \cdot 5b \\[6pt]
&= 2a^2 + 10ab - 3ab - 15b^2 \\[6pt]
&= 2a^2 + 7ab - 15b^2
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Multiply each term of $2a - 3b$ by each term of $a + 5b$. The term $2a$ gives $2a^2$ and $10ab$. The term $-3b$ gives $-3b \cdot a = -3ab$, because $ba = ab$, and $-3b \cdot 5b = -15b^2$.

The terms $10ab$ and $-3ab$ are like terms with sum $(10 - 3)\,ab = 7ab$. The product is therefore $2a^2 + 7ab - 15b^2$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Calculate $(x + 2)\left( x^2 - 3x + 1 \right)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
&(x + 2)\left( x^2 - 3x + 1 \right) \\[6pt]
&= x^3 - 3x^2 + x + 2x^2 - 6x + 2 \\[6pt]
&= x^3 - x^2 - 5x + 2
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Multiplying a binomial by a trinomial gives $2 \cdot 3 = 6$ products. The term $x$ multiplies the three terms of the trinomial, giving $x^3$, $-3x^2$, and $x$. The term $2$ multiplies the same three terms, giving $2x^2$, $-6x$, and $2$.

Combine like terms. For $x^2$ we have $-3 + 2 = -1$, giving $-x^2$. For $x$ we have $1 - 6 = -5$, giving $-5x$. The product is $x^3 - x^2 - 5x + 2$ and its degree is $3$, the sum of the degrees $1$ and $2$ of the factors.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Calculate $(x - 1)(x + 2)(x + 3)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x - 1)(x + 2) &= x^2 + 2x - x - 2 \\[6pt]
&= x^2 + x - 2
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\left( x^2 + x - 2 \right)(x + 3) \\[6pt]
&= x^3 + 3x^2 + x^2 + 3x - 2x - 6 \\[6pt]
&= x^3 + 4x^2 + x - 6
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

With three factors, first multiply the first two and then multiply the result by the third, by the associative property of multiplication. The product $(x - 1)(x + 2)$ has four terms, $x^2$, $2x$, $-x$, and $-2$, and the two linear terms give $2x - x = x$. The result is $x^2 + x - 2$.

Next, multiply $x^2 + x - 2$ by $x + 3$. The term $x^2$ gives $x^3$ and $3x^2$, the term $x$ gives $x^2$ and $3x$, and the term $-2$ gives $-2x$ and $-6$. Combining like terms gives $3x^2 + x^2 = 4x^2$ and $3x - 2x = x$, so the product is $x^3 + 4x^2 + x - 6$. For $x = 1$ the first factor is $0$, and indeed the result also equals $1 + 4 + 1 - 6 = 0$.

</div>
