## Exercise 1

For $x \neq 0$, calculate the quotient $\left( 10x^4 - 15x^3 + 5x^2 \right) : \left( 5x^2 \right)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{10x^4 - 15x^3 + 5x^2}{5x^2} &= \frac{10x^4}{5x^2} - \frac{15x^3}{5x^2} + \frac{5x^2}{5x^2} \\[6pt]
&= 2x^2 - 3x + 1
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The divisor $5x^2$ is nonzero because $x \neq 0$. By the rule for **dividing a polynomial by a monomial**, divide each term of the polynomial by $5x^2$.

Calculate each quotient using the rule for dividing monomials. The first gives $\dfrac{10}{5} = 2$ and $x^{4 - 2} = x^2$. The second gives $\dfrac{15}{5} = 3$ and $x^{3 - 2} = x$, with the minus sign from the term $-15x^3$. In the third, the term $5x^2$ equals the divisor, so the quotient is $1$. The result is therefore $2x^2 - 3x + 1$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

For $a \neq 0$ and $b \neq 0$, calculate the quotient $\left( 12a^3b^2 - 6a^2b^3 + 9a^2b^2 \right) : \left( -3a^2b \right)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\frac{12a^3b^2 - 6a^2b^3 + 9a^2b^2}{-3a^2b} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{12a^3b^2}{-3a^2b} + \frac{-6a^2b^3}{-3a^2b} + \frac{9a^2b^2}{-3a^2b} \\[6pt]
&= -4ab + 2b^2 - 3b
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The divisor $-3a^2b$ is nonzero because $a \neq 0$ and $b \neq 0$, and its coefficient is negative. By the sign rule, each quotient therefore has the opposite sign to the term being divided.

In the first quotient, $\dfrac{12}{-3} = -4$, $a^{3 - 2} = a$, and $b^{2 - 1} = b$, giving $-4ab$. In the second, $\dfrac{-6}{-3} = 2$, the variable $a$ has the same exponent in the two monomials and disappears, and $b^{3 - 1} = b^2$, giving $2b^2$. In the third, $\dfrac{9}{-3} = -3$, the variable $a$ disappears, and $b^{2 - 1} = b$, giving $-3b$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

For $x \neq 0$, simplify the expression $\left( 12x^4 - 8x^3 + 4x^2 \right) : \left( 4x^2 \right) - (x - 1)^2$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\frac{12x^4 - 8x^3 + 4x^2}{4x^2} - (x - 1)^2 \\[6pt]
&= 3x^2 - 2x + 1 - \left( x^2 - 2x + 1 \right) \\[6pt]
&= 3x^2 - 2x + 1 - x^2 + 2x - 1 \\[6pt]
&= 2x^2
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Divide term by term because the divisor $4x^2$ is a nonzero monomial. This gives $\dfrac{12x^4}{4x^2} = 3x^2$, then $\dfrac{8x^3}{4x^2} = 2x$ with the minus sign, and $\dfrac{4x^2}{4x^2} = 1$.

The second term is the **square of a binomial** and equals $x^2 - 2x + 1$. Its expansion is written as $\left( x^2 - 2x + 1 \right)$ because the minus sign in front applies to all its terms. With the minus sign it becomes $-x^2 + 2x - 1$.

Finally, combine like terms: $3x^2 - x^2 = 2x^2$, while $-2x$ and $2x$ are opposites, as are $1$ and $-1$. The expression therefore equals $2x^2$.

</div>
