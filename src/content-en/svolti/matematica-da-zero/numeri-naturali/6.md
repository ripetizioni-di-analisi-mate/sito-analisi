## Exercise 1

Evaluate $18 + 12 : 3 - 2 \cdot 5$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
18 + 12 : 3 - 2 \cdot 5
&= 18 + 4 - 10 \\[6pt]
&= 22 - 10 \\[6pt]
&= 12
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

There are no powers in the expression, so the **order of operations** requires division and multiplication first. We have $12 : 3 = 4$ and $2 \cdot 5 = 10$.

An addition and a subtraction remain, which we perform from left to right. First $18 + 4 = 22$, then $22 - 10 = 12$. The **value** of the expression is therefore $12$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Evaluate $48 : 6 \cdot 2 - 3 \cdot 4 + 7$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
48 : 6 \cdot 2 - 3 \cdot 4 + 7
&= 8 \cdot 2 - 12 + 7 \\[6pt]
&= 16 - 12 + 7 \\[6pt]
&= 4 + 7 \\[6pt]
&= 11
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first term, $48 : 6 \cdot 2$, contains division followed by multiplication. The two operations have the same precedence, so they are performed from left to right: first $48 : 6 = 8$, then $8 \cdot 2 = 16$. In the second term we calculate $3 \cdot 4 = 12$.

The expression becomes $16 - 12 + 7$. Here again the operations have the same precedence, so we work from left to right, obtaining $16 - 12 = 4$ and then $4 + 7 = 11$. The **value** of the expression is therefore $11$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Evaluate $2^4 \cdot 3 - 5^2 + 36 : 3^2$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2^4 \cdot 3 - 5^2 + 36 : 3^2
&= 16 \cdot 3 - 25 + 36 : 9 \\[6pt]
&= 48 - 25 + 4 \\[6pt]
&= 23 + 4 \\[6pt]
&= 27
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Powers take precedence over all the other operations, so they are evaluated first. We have $2^4 = 16$, $5^2 = 25$, and $3^2 = 9$.

Next we find the product, $16 \cdot 3 = 48$, and the quotient, $36 : 9 = 4$. In the division, the exponent $2$ applies only to $3$, so we divide $36$ by $9$. This leaves $48 - 25 + 4$, which gives $23 + 4 = 27$ when evaluated from left to right. The **value** of the expression is therefore $27$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Evaluate $100 - 4 \cdot 3^2 + 2^3 \cdot 5 : 10$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
100 - 4 \cdot 3^2 + 2^3 \cdot 5 : 10
&= 100 - 4 \cdot 9 + 8 \cdot 5 : 10 \\[6pt]
&= 100 - 36 + 40 : 10 \\[6pt]
&= 100 - 36 + 4 \\[6pt]
&= 64 + 4 \\[6pt]
&= 68
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

First evaluate the powers, $3^2 = 9$ and $2^3 = 8$. Then carry out the multiplications and divisions. The product $4 \cdot 9$ equals $36$. In the term $8 \cdot 5 : 10$, the two operations have the same precedence, so we work from left to right, obtaining $8 \cdot 5 = 40$ and then $40 : 10 = 4$.

This leaves $100 - 36 + 4$. Working from left to right gives $100 - 36 = 64$ and $64 + 4 = 68$, so the **value** of the expression is $68$.

</div>
