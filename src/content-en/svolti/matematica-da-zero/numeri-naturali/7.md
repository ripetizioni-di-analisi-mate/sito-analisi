## Exercise 1

Evaluate $(15 - 3 \cdot 2) \cdot 4 - (20 + 4) : 6$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(15 - 3 \cdot 2) \cdot 4 - (20 + 4) : 6
&= (15 - 6) \cdot 4 - 24 : 6 \\[6pt]
&= 9 \cdot 4 - 24 : 6 \\[6pt]
&= 36 - 4 \\[6pt]
&= 32
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

First evaluate the two expressions enclosed in parentheses. In the first, $15 - 3 \cdot 2$, multiplication comes before subtraction, so we have $3 \cdot 2 = 6$ and then $15 - 6 = 9$. The second gives $20 + 4 = 24$.

The expression becomes $9 \cdot 4 - 24 : 6$. Multiplication and division come before subtraction, giving $9 \cdot 4 = 36$ and $24 : 6 = 4$. Finally, $36 - 4 = 32$, so the **value** of the expression is $32$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Evaluate $[(2^3 + 4) : 3 + 5] \cdot 2 - 3^2$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
[(2^3 + 4) : 3 + 5] \cdot 2 - 3^2
&= [(8 + 4) : 3 + 5] \cdot 2 - 9 \\[6pt]
&= [12 : 3 + 5] \cdot 2 - 9 \\[6pt]
&= [4 + 5] \cdot 2 - 9 \\[6pt]
&= 9 \cdot 2 - 9 \\[6pt]
&= 18 - 9 = 9
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the expression $2^3 + 4$ inside the parentheses, first evaluate the power, $2^3 = 8$, and then the sum, $8 + 4 = 12$. In the same step, also evaluate the power $3^2 = 9$ outside the grouping symbols, since powers precede the other operations and this calculation does not depend on the grouping symbols.

The expression inside the square brackets becomes $12 : 3 + 5$. Division precedes addition, so we have $12 : 3 = 4$ and then $4 + 5 = 9$. This leaves $9 \cdot 2 - 9$, that is, $18 - 9 = 9$. The **value** of the expression is therefore $9$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Evaluate $\{ [5 \cdot (9 - 6) + 3^2] : 4 + 2 \} \cdot 3 - 10$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\{ [5 \cdot (9 - 6) + 3^2] : 4 + 2 \} \cdot 3 - 10
&= \{ [5 \cdot 3 + 9] : 4 + 2 \} \cdot 3 - 10 \\[6pt]
&= \{ [15 + 9] : 4 + 2 \} \cdot 3 - 10 \\[6pt]
&= \{ 24 : 4 + 2 \} \cdot 3 - 10 \\[6pt]
&= \{ 6 + 2 \} \cdot 3 - 10 \\[6pt]
&= 8 \cdot 3 - 10 \\[6pt]
&= 24 - 10 = 14
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Begin with the expression inside the parentheses, $9 - 6 = 3$, and at the same time evaluate the power $3^2 = 9$. The expression inside the square brackets becomes $5 \cdot 3 + 9$. Multiplication precedes addition, so we have $5 \cdot 3 = 15$ and then $15 + 9 = 24$.

The expression inside the braces becomes $24 : 4 + 2$. First divide, $24 : 4 = 6$, and then add, $6 + 2 = 8$. This leaves the expression $8 \cdot 3 - 10$, that is, $24 - 10 = 14$. The **value** of the expression is therefore $14$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Evaluate $2^2 \cdot \{ 50 - [3 \cdot (4 + 2^2) - 4] : 5 \} : 8$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2^2 \cdot \{ 50 - [3 \cdot (4 + 2^2) - 4] : 5 \} : 8
&= 4 \cdot \{ 50 - [3 \cdot (4 + 4) - 4] : 5 \} : 8 \\[6pt]
&= 4 \cdot \{ 50 - [3 \cdot 8 - 4] : 5 \} : 8 \\[6pt]
&= 4 \cdot \{ 50 - [24 - 4] : 5 \} : 8 \\[6pt]
&= 4 \cdot \{ 50 - 20 : 5 \} : 8 \\[6pt]
&= 4 \cdot \{ 50 - 4 \} : 8 \\[6pt]
&= 4 \cdot 46 : 8 \\[6pt]
&= 184 : 8 = 23
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two occurrences of the power $2^2$, one outside and one inside the grouping symbols, both equal $4$. The expression inside the parentheses therefore gives $4 + 4 = 8$. The expression inside the square brackets becomes $3 \cdot 8 - 4$, and since multiplication precedes subtraction, we have $3 \cdot 8 = 24$ and then $24 - 4 = 20$.

The expression inside the braces becomes $50 - 20 : 5$. Division precedes subtraction, so we have $20 : 5 = 4$ and then $50 - 4 = 46$. This leaves the expression $4 \cdot 46 : 8$, with a multiplication and a division of equal precedence, which are performed from left to right. We have $4 \cdot 46 = 184$ and then $184 : 8 = 23$ because $8 \cdot 23 = 184$. The **value** of the expression is therefore $23$.

</div>
