## Exercise 1

Calculate $36 + 58 + 64 + 42$ by grouping the addends conveniently.

$$
\begin{aligned}
36 + 58 + 64 + 42
&= (36 + 64) + (58 + 42) \\[6pt]
&= 100 + 100 \\[6pt]
&= 200
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The ones digits of $36$ and $64$ give $6 + 4 = 10$, and the same is true of $58$ and $42$ because $8 + 2 = 10$. Each pair therefore gives a sum ending in $0$, so we add these pairs first. By the **commutative property**, we switch the order of $58$ and $64$, and by the **associative property**, we group the addends in pairs.

We obtain $36 + 64 = 100$ and $58 + 42 = 100$, so the sum is $100 + 100 = 200$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Calculate $4 \cdot 13 \cdot 25$ and $5 \cdot 17 \cdot 2 \cdot 3$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
4 \cdot 13 \cdot 25
&= 13 \cdot (4 \cdot 25) \\[6pt]
&= 13 \cdot 100 \\[6pt]
&= 1\,300
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
5 \cdot 17 \cdot 2 \cdot 3
&= (5 \cdot 2) \cdot (17 \cdot 3) \\[6pt]
&= 10 \cdot 51 \\[6pt]
&= 510
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the first product, the factors $4$ and $25$ give $100$, and multiplication by $100$ is straightforward. By the commutative property, we move $13$ to the first position, and by the associative property, we calculate $4 \cdot 25 = 100$ first. The product is therefore $13 \cdot 100 = 1\,300$.

In the second product, the factors $5$ and $2$ give $10$. By the commutative and associative properties, we group $5$ with $2$ and $17$ with $3$. We have $5 \cdot 2 = 10$ and $17 \cdot 3 = 51$, so the product is $10 \cdot 51 = 510$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Use the distributive property to calculate $15 \cdot 21$ and $8 \cdot 99$ mentally.

$$
\begin{aligned}
15 \cdot 21
&= 15 \cdot (20 + 1) \\[6pt]
&= 15 \cdot 20 + 15 \cdot 1 \\[6pt]
&= 300 + 15 = 315
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
8 \cdot 99
&= 8 \cdot (100 - 1) \\[6pt]
&= 8 \cdot 100 - 8 \cdot 1 \\[6pt]
&= 800 - 8 = 792
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 21,0 21,15 0,15" linee="0,0 21,0 21,15 0,15 0,0;20,-0.8 20,15.8" etichette="5.5,7,15 · 20 = 300;14.2,16.4,15 · 1 = 15;8.5,-1.9,20;20.1,-1.9,1" xmin=-1 xmax=26 ymin=-3 ymax=18 titolo="The rectangle with sides $15$ and $21$ splits into two rectangles, one with sides $15$ and $20$ and the other with sides $15$ and $1$, giving $15 \cdot 21 = 300 + 15 = 315$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Write the factor $21$ as $20 + 1$. By the **distributive property**, the product of $15$ and the sum $20 + 1$ is the sum of the products $15 \cdot 20$ and $15 \cdot 1$. We have $15 \cdot 20 = 300$ because $15 \cdot 2 = 30$ and we then multiply by $10$. Also, $15 \cdot 1 = 15$ because $1$ is the multiplicative identity. Therefore $15 \cdot 21 = 315$.

Write the factor $99$ as $100 - 1$. The distributive property applies to differences as well. We have $8 \cdot 100 = 800$ and $8 \cdot 1 = 8$, so $8 \cdot 99 = 800 - 8 = 792$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Calculate by factoring out the common factor: $23 \cdot 7 + 23 \cdot 3$ and $45 \cdot 12 - 45 \cdot 2$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
23 \cdot 7 + 23 \cdot 3
&= 23 \cdot (7 + 3) \\[6pt]
&= 23 \cdot 10 = 230
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
45 \cdot 12 - 45 \cdot 2
&= 45 \cdot (12 - 2) \\[6pt]
&= 45 \cdot 10 = 450
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the first expression, the two products share the factor $23$. Read from right to left, the distributive property lets us **factor out** this factor, so $23 \cdot 7 + 23 \cdot 3$ equals $23$ times the sum $7 + 3$. Since $7 + 3 = 10$, the value is $23 \cdot 10 = 230$.

In the second expression, the common factor is $45$ and the two products are separated by a minus sign. Factoring out $45$ gives $45 \cdot (12 - 2) = 45 \cdot 10 = 450$. Without factoring, the calculation would have been $540 - 90 = 450$ because $45 \cdot 12 = 540$ and $45 \cdot 2 = 90$, giving the same result with longer multiplications.

</div>
