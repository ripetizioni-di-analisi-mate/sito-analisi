## Exercise 1

Factor the trinomial $x^2 + 8x + 15$.

$$
\begin{cases}
a + b = 8 \\
a \cdot b = 15
\end{cases}
\qquad
a = 3 , \quad b = 5
$$
$$
x^2 + 8x + 15 = (x + 3)(x + 5)
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,2 0,2;2,0 7,0 7,2 2,2" linee="0,0 7,0 7,5 0,5 0,0;2,0 2,5;0,2 7,2" etichette="0.55,0.8,x²;4.2,0.8,5x;0.5,3.3,3x;4.2,3.3,15;0.55,-0.7,x;4.2,-0.7,5;-0.8,0.8,x;-0.8,3.3,3" xmin=-1.5 xmax=8 ymin=-1.4 ymax=5.8 titolo="For $x = 2$, the rectangle with side lengths $x + 5 = 7$ and $x + 3 = 5$ has area $x^2 + 5x + 3x + 15 = x^2 + 8x + 15 = 35$. The term $15$ is the product $3 \cdot 5$, and the coefficient $8$ is the sum $3 + 5$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The coefficient of $x^2$ is $1$, so we look for two numbers with sum $8$ and product $15$. The product is positive and the sum is positive, so both numbers are positive. The positive integer pairs with product $15$ are $1$ and $15$, with sum $16$, and $3$ and $5$, with sum $8$.

The pair with sum $8$ consists of $3$ and $5$. By the rule for **factoring a trinomial using a sum and a product**, we have $x^2 + 8x + 15 = (x + 3)(x + 5)$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Factor the trinomial $x^2 - 9x + 14$.

$$
\begin{cases}
a + b = -9 \\
a \cdot b = 14
\end{cases}
\qquad
a = -2 , \quad b = -7
$$
$$
x^2 - 9x + 14 = (x - 2)(x - 7)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The product $14$ is positive, so the two numbers have the same sign. Their sum $-9$ is negative, so both numbers are negative. The negative integer pairs with product $14$ are $-1$ and $-14$, with sum $-15$, and $-2$ and $-7$, with sum $-9$.

Choose the pair $-2$ and $-7$. The factors are $x + (-2) = x - 2$ and $x + (-7) = x - 7$, so the trinomial equals $(x - 2)(x - 7)$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Factor the trinomial $x^2 + 2x - 24$.

$$
\begin{cases}
a + b = 2 \\
a \cdot b = -24
\end{cases}
\qquad
a = 6 , \quad b = -4
$$
$$
x^2 + 2x - 24 = (x + 6)(x - 4)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The product $-24$ is negative, so the two numbers have opposite signs. The sum $2$ is positive, so the positive number has the larger absolute value. The integer pairs with product $-24$ in which the positive number has larger absolute value are $24$ and $-1$, $12$ and $-2$, $8$ and $-3$, and $6$ and $-4$, with sums $23$, $10$, $5$, and $2$.

The pair with sum $2$ consists of $6$ and $-4$. The trinomial therefore equals $(x + 6)(x - 4)$. For $x = 1$ the trinomial equals $1 + 2 - 24 = -21$ and the product equals $7 \cdot (-3) = -21$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Factor the trinomial $3x^2 - 3x - 60$.

$$
3x^2 - 3x - 60 = 3\,(x^2 - x - 20)
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
a + b = -1 \\
a \cdot b = -20
\end{cases}
\qquad
a = 4 , \quad b = -5
$$
$$
3x^2 - 3x - 60 = 3\,(x + 4)(x - 5)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The coefficient of $x^2$ is $3$, which also divides $3$ and $60$. **Factoring out the common factor** $3$ gives $3\,(x^2 - x - 20)$ because $\dfrac{60}{3} = 20$. In the trinomial $x^2 - x - 20$, the coefficient of $x^2$ is $1$ and the coefficient of $x$ is $-1$.

Look for two numbers with sum $-1$ and product $-20$. The product is negative, so the numbers have opposite signs, and the sum is negative, so the negative number has the larger absolute value. The pair $4$ and $-5$ has product $-20$ and sum $-1$. The original trinomial therefore equals $3\,(x + 4)(x - 5)$.

</div>
