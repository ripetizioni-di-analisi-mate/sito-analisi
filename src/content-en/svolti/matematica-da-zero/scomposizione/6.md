## Exercise 1

Determine the domain restrictions of the fraction
$$
\frac{x + 2}{x^2 - 25} .
$$

$$
x^2 - 25 = (x + 5)(x - 5)
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
x \neq -5 \\
x \neq 5
\end{cases}
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-6.5,0 6.5,0;6.3,0.1 6.5,0 6.3,-0.1;-6,-0.08 -6,0.08;-5,-0.08 -5,0.08;-4,-0.08 -4,0.08;-3,-0.08 -3,0.08;-2,-0.08 -2,0.08;-1,-0.08 -1,0.08;0,-0.08 0,0.08;1,-0.08 1,0.08;2,-0.08 2,0.08;3,-0.08 3,0.08;4,-0.08 4,0.08;5,-0.08 5,0.08;6,-0.08 6,0.08" punti="-5,0,vuoto;5,0,vuoto" etichette="-6.15,-0.4,−6;-5.15,-0.4,−5;-4.15,-0.4,−4;-3.15,-0.4,−3;-2.15,-0.4,−2;-1.15,-0.4,−1;-0.05,-0.4,0;0.95,-0.4,1;1.95,-0.4,2;2.95,-0.4,3;3.95,-0.4,4;4.95,-0.4,5;5.95,-0.4,6;6.45,0.25,x" xmin=-6.9 xmax=6.9 ymin=-0.7 ymax=0.5 titolo="For the fraction $\dfrac{x + 2}{x^2 - 25}$, the values $x = -5$ and $x = 5$ are excluded"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The domain restrictions require the denominator to be nonzero. The denominator $x^2 - 25$ is a **difference of squares** because $25 = 5^2$, so it equals $(x + 5)(x - 5)$.

By the **zero product property**, the denominator equals zero when either factor equals zero. The factor $x + 5$ is zero for $x = -5$, and the factor $x - 5$ is zero for $x = 5$. Both factors must be nonzero at the same time, so *both* values are excluded. The numerator does not affect the restrictions because a zero numerator gives a fraction equal to zero, which is defined.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Simplify the fraction
$$
\frac{x^2 - 16}{x^2 + 4x} .
$$

$$
\frac{x^2 - 16}{x^2 + 4x} = \frac{(x + 4)(x - 4)}{x\,(x + 4)}
\qquad
\begin{cases}
x \neq 0 \\
x \neq -4
\end{cases}
$$
$$
\frac{(x + 4)(x - 4)}{x\,(x + 4)} = \frac{x - 4}{x}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The numerator is a **difference of squares** with bases $x$ and $4$. Factoring $x$ out of the denominator leaves $x + 4$. The denominator $x\,(x + 4)$ equals zero for $x = 0$ and $x = -4$, so these two values are excluded.

The factor $x + 4$ appears in both the numerator and the denominator and is nonzero for all allowed values. By the equivalent fractions property, dividing numerator and denominator by $x + 4$ gives the fraction $\dfrac{x - 4}{x}$, with the restrictions $x \neq 0$ and $x \neq -4$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Simplify the fraction
$$
\frac{x^2 - 6x + 9}{x^2 - 9} .
$$

$$
\frac{x^2 - 6x + 9}{x^2 - 9} = \frac{(x - 3)^2}{(x + 3)(x - 3)}
\qquad
\begin{cases}
x \neq -3 \\
x \neq 3
\end{cases}
$$
$$
\frac{(x - 3)^2}{(x + 3)(x - 3)} = \frac{x - 3}{x + 3}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The numerator is the **square of the binomial** $x - 3$ because $9 = 3^2$ and twice the product, $2 \cdot x \cdot 3 = 6x$, appears with a minus sign. The denominator is a difference of squares. The denominator equals zero for $x = -3$ and $x = 3$, so both values are excluded.

The numerator contains the factor $x - 3$ twice and the denominator once. Dividing numerator and denominator by $x - 3$ gives $\dfrac{x - 3}{x + 3}$. The restriction $x \neq 3$ still holds even though the simplified fraction is defined for $x = 3$, because the original fraction with $x = 3$ becomes $\dfrac{0}{0}$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Simplify the fraction
$$
\frac{2x^2 - 2x - 12}{x^3 - 27} .
$$

$$
2x^2 - 2x - 12 = 2\,(x^2 - x - 6) = 2\,(x + 2)(x - 3)
$$
$$
x^3 - 27 = (x - 3)(x^2 + 3x + 9)
$$
$$
x^2 + 3x + 9 = \left( x + \frac{3}{2} \right)^2 + \frac{27}{4}
$$
$$
\frac{2\,(x + 2)(x - 3)}{(x - 3)(x^2 + 3x + 9)} = \frac{2\,(x + 2)}{x^2 + 3x + 9}
\qquad
x \neq 3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Factoring $2$ out of the numerator leaves the trinomial $x^2 - x - 6$. Two numbers with sum $-1$ and product $-6$ are $2$ and $-3$, so **factoring the trinomial using a sum and a product** gives $(x + 2)(x - 3)$. The denominator is a **difference of cubes** because $27 = 3^3$.

The factor $x^2 + 3x + 9$ is the quadratic factor in a difference of cubes. It can be written as $\left( x + \dfrac{3}{2} \right)^2 + \dfrac{27}{4}$ because $\left( x + \dfrac{3}{2} \right)^2 = x^2 + 3x + \dfrac{9}{4}$ and $\dfrac{9}{4} + \dfrac{27}{4} = \dfrac{36}{4} = 9$. The square is greater than or equal to $0$, so the factor is greater than or equal to $\dfrac{27}{4}$ and is never zero. The denominator is therefore zero only for $x = 3$, and the only domain restriction is $x \neq 3$.

Canceling the common factor $x - 3$ gives the fraction $\dfrac{2\,(x + 2)}{x^2 + 3x + 9}$.

</div>
