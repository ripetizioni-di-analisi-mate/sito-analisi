## Exercise 1

Calculate
$$
\frac{1}{x - 3} + \frac{2}{x + 3} .
$$

$$
\begin{cases}
x \neq 3 \\
x \neq -3
\end{cases}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{x - 3} + \frac{2}{x + 3} &= \frac{(x + 3) + 2\,(x - 3)}{(x - 3)(x + 3)} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{x + 3 + 2x - 6}{(x - 3)(x + 3)} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{3x - 3}{(x - 3)(x + 3)} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{3\,(x - 1)}{(x - 3)(x + 3)}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The denominators $x - 3$ and $x + 3$ equal zero for $x = 3$ and $x = -3$, so these values are excluded. The denominators have no common factor, so their $\operatorname{mcm}$, the **common denominator**, is the product $(x - 3)(x + 3)$.

Multiply the numerator and denominator of the first fraction by $x + 3$ and those of the second by $x - 3$. The resulting numerator is $x + 3 + 2x - 6$, which equals $3x - 3$. Factoring out $3$ gives the result $\dfrac{3\,(x - 1)}{(x - 3)(x + 3)}$, which cannot be simplified because the numerator and denominator have no common factors.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Calculate
$$
\frac{2}{x^2 - 1} - \frac{1}{x^2 - x} .
$$

$$
x^2 - 1 = (x + 1)(x - 1) ,
\qquad
x^2 - x = x\,(x - 1)
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
x \neq 0 \\
x \neq 1 \\
x \neq -1
\end{cases}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{2}{(x + 1)(x - 1)} - \frac{1}{x\,(x - 1)} &= \frac{2x - (x + 1)}{x\,(x + 1)(x - 1)} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{x - 1}{x\,(x + 1)(x - 1)} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{1}{x\,(x + 1)}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first denominator is a **difference of squares**, and $x$ can be factored out of the second. The factors of the denominators equal zero for $x = -1$, $x = 1$, and $x = 0$, so these three values are excluded. The $\operatorname{mcm}$ of the denominators contains each factor once and equals $x\,(x + 1)(x - 1)$.

The first denominator does not contain the factor $x$, so the numerator $2$ becomes $2x$. The second does not contain $x + 1$, so the numerator $1$ becomes $x + 1$, preceded by the minus sign. The numerator is therefore $2x - x - 1 = x - 1$. The factor $x - 1$ is common to the numerator and denominator and cancels, giving the result $\dfrac{1}{x\,(x + 1)}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Calculate
$$
\frac{x^2 - 9}{2x} \cdot \frac{4x^2}{x^2 + 3x} .
$$

$$
\begin{cases}
x \neq 0 \\
x \neq -3
\end{cases}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{x^2 - 9}{2x} \cdot \frac{4x^2}{x^2 + 3x} &= \frac{(x + 3)(x - 3)}{2x} \cdot \frac{4x^2}{x\,(x + 3)} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{4x^2\,(x - 3)}{2x \cdot x} \\[6pt]
&= 2\,(x - 3)
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The denominator $2x$ equals zero for $x = 0$, and the denominator $x^2 + 3x = x\,(x + 3)$ equals zero for $x = 0$ and $x = -3$. The domain restrictions are therefore $x \neq 0$ and $x \neq -3$.

Before multiplying, factor everything. The factor $x + 3$ appears in the numerator of the first fraction and the denominator of the second, so it cancels. This leaves $4x^2\,(x - 3)$ in the numerator and $2x \cdot x = 2x^2$ in the denominator. Since $\dfrac{4x^2}{2x^2} = 2$, the product equals $2\,(x - 3)$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Calculate
$$
\frac{x^2 + 5x + 6}{x^2 - 1} : \frac{x + 3}{x - 1} .
$$

$$
\begin{cases}
x \neq 1 \\
x \neq -1 \\
x \neq -3
\end{cases}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{x^2 + 5x + 6}{x^2 - 1} : \frac{x + 3}{x - 1} &= \frac{(x + 2)(x + 3)}{(x + 1)(x - 1)} \cdot \frac{x - 1}{x + 3} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{x + 2}{x + 1}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The denominators $x^2 - 1 = (x + 1)(x - 1)$ and $x - 1$ give the restrictions $x \neq 1$ and $x \neq -1$. In division, the second fraction is inverted, so its numerator $x + 3$ also becomes a denominator and must be nonzero. This gives the condition $x \neq -3$.

The numerator $x^2 + 5x + 6$ is a **trinomial that can be factored using a sum and a product** because $2 + 3 = 5$ and $2 \cdot 3 = 6$, so it equals $(x + 2)(x + 3)$. Multiply the first fraction by the **reciprocal** of the second and cancel the common factors $x + 3$ and $x - 1$. The quotient is therefore $\dfrac{x + 2}{x + 1}$.

</div>
