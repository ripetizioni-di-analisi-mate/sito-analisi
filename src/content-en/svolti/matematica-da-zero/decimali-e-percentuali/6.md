## Exercise 1

Find $35\%$ of $240$ and $4\%$ of $75$.

$$
\frac{35}{100} \cdot 240 = \frac{35 \cdot 240}{100} = \frac{8400}{100} = 84
$$
$$
\frac{4}{100} \cdot 75 = \frac{300}{100} = 3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To find a **percentage of a number**, multiply the number by the fraction with denominator $100$. In the first case, $35 \cdot 240$ is calculated as $35 \cdot 24 \cdot 10$. Since $35 \cdot 24 = 35 \cdot 20 + 35 \cdot 4 = 700 + 140 = 840$, the product is $8400$. Dividing by $100$ gives $84$, so $35\%$ of $240$ is $84$.

In the second case, $4 \cdot 75 = 300$ and $\dfrac{300}{100} = 3$. Thus $4\%$ of $75$ is $3$. The result can also be checked by observing that $4\%$ equals $\dfrac{4}{100} = \dfrac{1}{25}$ and $\dfrac{75}{25} = 3$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Write $0.08$, $1.25$ and $\dfrac{3}{8}$ as percentages.

$$
0.08 = \frac{8}{100} = 8\%
\qquad
1.25 = \frac{125}{100} = 125\%
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
30 : 8 &= 3 \quad \text{with remainder } 6 \\[6pt]
60 : 8 &= 7 \quad \text{with remainder } 4 \\[6pt]
40 : 8 &= 5 \quad \text{with remainder } 0
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\frac{3}{8} = 0.375 = \frac{37.5}{100} = 37.5\%
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

A **percentage** is a fraction with denominator $100$, so to write a number as a percentage, express it in hundredths. The number $0.08$ equals $8$ hundredths, so it is $8\%$. The number $1.25$ equals $125$ hundredths, so it is $125\%$. A percentage greater than $100\%$ represents more than one whole.

For the fraction $\dfrac{3}{8}$, first find its decimal form by dividing $3$ by $8$. The $3$ whole units equal $30$ tenths, and $30 : 8$ gives $3$ tenths with remainder $6$ because $8 \cdot 3 = 24$. The remaining $6$ tenths equal $60$ hundredths, and $60 : 8$ gives $7$ hundredths with remainder $4$ because $8 \cdot 7 = 56$. The remaining $4$ hundredths equal $40$ thousandths, and $40 : 8 = 5$ with remainder $0$, so $\dfrac{3}{8} = 0.375$. Multiplication by $100$ moves the decimal point two places to the right and gives $37.5$, so $\dfrac{3}{8}$ is $37.5\%$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Find what percentage of $160$ is represented by $24$, and what percentage of $12$ is represented by $9$.

$$
24 : 160 = p : 100
\qquad
p = \frac{24 \cdot 100}{160} = \frac{2400}{160} = 15
$$
$$
9 : 12 = p : 100
\qquad
p = \frac{9 \cdot 100}{12} = \frac{900}{12} = 75
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The part, total and percentage satisfy the proportion $P : N = p : 100$. In the first case, the part is $P = 24$ and the total is $N = 160$. The unknown term $p$ is a mean, so it equals the product of the extremes $24 \cdot 100 = 2400$ divided by the other mean $160$. Since $\dfrac{2400}{160} = \dfrac{240}{16} = 15$, the number $24$ is $15\%$ of $160$.

In the second case, $P = 9$ and $N = 12$, so $p = \dfrac{900}{12} = 75$ because $12 \cdot 75 = 900$. The number $9$ is therefore $75\%$ of $12$. This agrees with the fractions because $\dfrac{9}{12} = \dfrac{3}{4}$ and $\dfrac{3}{4}$ corresponds to $75\%$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

If $15\%$ of a number is $27$, find the number.

$$
27 : N = 15 : 100
$$
$$
N = \frac{27 \cdot 100}{15} = \frac{2700}{15} = 180
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Here the part $P = 27$ and the percentage $p = 15$ are known, while the total $N$ is unknown. In the proportion relating **part, percentage and total**, the term $N$ is a mean, so it equals the product of the extremes $27 \cdot 100 = 2700$ divided by the other mean $15$.

To carry out the division $\dfrac{2700}{15}$, first divide by $5$ to obtain $540$, then by $3$ to obtain $180$. The required number is therefore $180$. As a check, $10\%$ of $180$ is $18$, and $5\%$ is half that, or $9$. Therefore $15\%$ of $180$ is $18 + 9 = 27$.

</div>
