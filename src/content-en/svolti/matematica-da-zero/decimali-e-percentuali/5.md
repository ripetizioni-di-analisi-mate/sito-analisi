## Exercise 1

Determine which of the following equalities are proportions.
$$
4 : 6 = 10 : 15 ,
\qquad
3 : 7 = 9 : 20 .
$$

$$
4 \cdot 15 = 60
\qquad
6 \cdot 10 = 60
$$
$$
3 \cdot 20 = 60
\qquad
7 \cdot 9 = 63
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the **cross-product property**, four numbers form a proportion if and only if the product of the extremes equals the product of the means.

In the first equality, the extremes are $4$ and $15$ with product $60$, and the means are $6$ and $10$ with product $60$. The products are equal, so $4 : 6 = 10 : 15$ **is a proportion**. Indeed, $\dfrac{4}{6}$ and $\dfrac{10}{15}$ both simplify to $\dfrac{2}{3}$.

In the second equality, the product of the extremes is $3 \cdot 20 = 60$ and the product of the means is $7 \cdot 9 = 63$. The products differ, so $3 : 7 = 9 : 20$ **is not a proportion**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve the proportion
$$
3 : 12 = 5 : x .
$$

$$
3 \cdot x = 12 \cdot 5
$$
$$
x = \frac{12 \cdot 5}{3} = \frac{60}{3} = 20
$$

::grafico{f="4*x" punti="3,12,pieno;5,20,pieno" segmenti="3,0,3,12;0,12,3,12;5,0,5,20;0,20,5,20" xmin=-1 xmax=7 ymin=-4 ymax=28 nomi="x,y" legenda="y = 4x" titolo="The points $(3, 12)$ and $(5, 20)$ lie on the line $y = 4x$ through the origin because $\dfrac{12}{3} = \dfrac{20}{5} = 4$, so $3 : 12 = 5 : 20$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The unknown term $x$ is in the last position, so it is an **extreme**. By the cross-product property, the product of the extremes $3 \cdot x$ equals the product of the means $12 \cdot 5 = 60$. Therefore $x$ is the number that, when multiplied by $3$, gives $60$, namely $x = \dfrac{60}{3} = 20$.

To check the result, compare the ratios. We have $\dfrac{3}{12} = \dfrac{1}{4}$ and $\dfrac{5}{20} = \dfrac{1}{4}$, so the proportion $3 : 12 = 5 : 20$ is correct.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve the proportion
$$
2.4 : x = 0.6 : 1.5 .
$$

$$
0.6 \cdot x = 2.4 \cdot 1.5
$$
$$
2.4 \cdot 1.5 = 3.6
$$
$$
x = \frac{3.6}{0.6} = \frac{36}{6} = 6
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The unknown term is in the second position, so it is a **mean**. By the cross-product property, the product of the means $x \cdot 0.6$ equals the product of the extremes $2.4 \cdot 1.5$.

To calculate $2.4 \cdot 1.5$, multiply $24 \cdot 15 = 360$. The factors have two decimal places in all, so the product is $3.60 = 3.6$. Then $x$ is found by dividing $3.6$ by $0.6$. Multiplying the dividend and divisor by $10$ gives the division $\dfrac{36}{6} = 6$, so $x = 6$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Solve the proportion
$$
\frac{2}{3} : x = \frac{4}{9} : 6 .
$$

$$
\frac{4}{9} \cdot x = \frac{2}{3} \cdot 6
$$
$$
\frac{2}{3} \cdot 6 = \frac{12}{3} = 4
$$
$$
x = 4 : \frac{4}{9} = 4 \cdot \frac{9}{4} = 9
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Here again, $x$ is a **mean**, so the product of the means $x \cdot \dfrac{4}{9}$ equals the product of the extremes $\dfrac{2}{3} \cdot 6$. This product equals $\dfrac{2 \cdot 6}{3} = \dfrac{12}{3} = 4$.

The number $x$ is therefore the quotient $4 : \dfrac{4}{9}$. Dividing by a fraction is equivalent to multiplying by its reciprocal, as shown in the chapter on fractions, so $x = 4 \cdot \dfrac{9}{4} = \dfrac{36}{4} = 9$.

Check the result using the ratios. The first ratio is $\dfrac{2}{3} : 9 = \dfrac{2}{3} \cdot \dfrac{1}{9} = \dfrac{2}{27}$ and the second is $\dfrac{4}{9} : 6 = \dfrac{4}{9} \cdot \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{4}{54} = \dfrac{2}{27}$. They are equal, so $x = 9$ is the solution.

</div>
