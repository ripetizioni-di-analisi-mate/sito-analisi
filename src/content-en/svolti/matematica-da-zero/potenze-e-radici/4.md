## Exercise 1

Write $45\,000\,000$ and $0.000078$ in scientific notation and determine the order of magnitude of each.

$$
45\,000\,000 = 4.5 \cdot 10^7
$$
$$
0.000078 = 7.8 \cdot 10^{-5}
$$
$$
4.5 < 5 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \text{order of magnitude } 10^7
$$
$$
7.8 \geq 5 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \text{order of magnitude } 10^{-5+1} = 10^{-4}
$$

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="0,0 10.6,0;1,-0.18 1,0.18;2,-0.12 2,0.12;3,-0.12 3,0.12;4,-0.12 4,0.12;5,-0.18 5,0.18;6,-0.12 6,0.12;7,-0.12 7,0.12;8,-0.12 8,0.12;9,-0.12 9,0.12;10,-0.18 10,0.18" segmenti="5,-0.5,5,0.9" punti="4.5,0,pieno" etichette="0.75,-0.75,10⁷;4.4,-0.75,5 · 10⁷;9.75,-0.75,10⁸;3.2,0.45,45 000 000" xmin=-0.5 xmax=11 ymin=-1.3 ymax=1.3 titolo="The number $45\,000\,000 = 4.5 \cdot 10^7$ lies between $10^7$ and $10^8$, below the threshold $5 \cdot 10^7$, so its order of magnitude is $10^7$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

In $45\,000\,000$, the decimal point is after the last zero. To obtain a number with a single digit before the point, namely $4.5$, move the point $7$ places to the left. Multiplying by $10^7$ moves the decimal point back $7$ places to the right, so $45\,000\,000 = 4.5 \cdot 10^7$.

In $0.000078$ the first nonzero digit is $7$, in the fifth place after the decimal point. To obtain $7.8$, move the decimal point $5$ places to the right, so the exponent is $-5$ and $0.000078 = 7.8 \cdot 10^{-5}$.

To find the **order of magnitude**, look at the number in front of the power of $10$. In the first case $4.5$ is less than $5$, so the order of magnitude is $10^7$. In the second case $7.8$ is greater than $5$, so the exponent increases by $1$ and the order of magnitude is $10^{-4}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Calculate $\left( 4 \cdot 10^6 \right) \cdot \left( 3.5 \cdot 10^{-2} \right)$ and write the result in scientific notation.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left( 4 \cdot 10^6 \right) \cdot \left( 3.5 \cdot 10^{-2} \right)
&= (4 \cdot 3.5) \cdot 10^{6 + (-2)} \\[6pt]
&= 14 \cdot 10^4 \\[6pt]
&= 1.4 \cdot 10 \cdot 10^4 \\[6pt]
&= 1.4 \cdot 10^5
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The commutative and associative properties of multiplication allow the factors to be reordered. Therefore multiply $4$ and $3.5$ together to obtain $14$, then multiply the powers of $10$ together. For the **product of powers with the same base**, add the exponents. Since $6 + (-2) = 4$, this gives $10^4$.

The number $14$ has two digits before the decimal point, so $14 \cdot 10^4$ is not in scientific notation. Since $14 = 1.4 \cdot 10$, we have $14 \cdot 10^4 = 1.4 \cdot 10^{1+4} = 1.4 \cdot 10^5$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Calculate
$$
\frac{9 \cdot 10^{-3}}{1.5 \cdot 10^4}
$$
and write the result in scientific notation.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{9 \cdot 10^{-3}}{1.5 \cdot 10^4}
&= \frac{9}{1.5} \cdot \frac{10^{-3}}{10^4} \\[6pt]
&= 6 \cdot 10^{-3-4} \\[6pt]
&= 6 \cdot 10^{-7}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Separate the quotient into two parts, the quotient of the coefficients and the quotient of the powers of $10$. We have $\dfrac{9}{1.5} = 6$ because $1.5 \cdot 6 = 9$.

For the **quotient of powers with the same base**, subtract the exponents. Since $-3 - 4 = -7$, this gives $10^{-7}$. The number $6$ lies between $1$ and $10$, so the result $6 \cdot 10^{-7}$ is already in scientific notation.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Calculate
$$
\frac{\left( 2 \cdot 10^3 \right)^2 \cdot 6 \cdot 10^{-5}}{8 \cdot 10^{-4}}
$$
and write the result in scientific notation.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\left( 2 \cdot 10^3 \right)^2 \cdot 6 \cdot 10^{-5}}{8 \cdot 10^{-4}}
&= \frac{2^2 \cdot 10^6 \cdot 6 \cdot 10^{-5}}{8 \cdot 10^{-4}} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{4 \cdot 6}{8} \cdot \frac{10^{6 + (-5)}}{10^{-4}} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{24}{8} \cdot \frac{10^{1}}{10^{-4}} \\[6pt]
&= 3 \cdot 10^{1-(-4)} \\[6pt]
&= 3 \cdot 10^{5}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the **power of a product** rule, square each factor of $2 \cdot 10^3$. We have $2^2 = 4$ and, by the power of a power rule, $\left( 10^3 \right)^2 = 10^{6}$.

In the numerator the coefficients give $4 \cdot 6 = 24$ and the powers of $10$ give $10^{6 + (-5)} = 10^1$. Then divide by the denominator. The quotient of the coefficients is $\dfrac{24}{8} = 3$. For the powers of $10$ the exponent is $1 - (-4) = 1 + 4 = 5$. The result is therefore $3 \cdot 10^5$, or $300\,000$.

</div>
