## Exercise 1

Calculate
$$
\frac{5^4 \cdot 5^3}{5^5} .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{5^4 \cdot 5^3}{5^5}
&= \frac{5^{4+3}}{5^5} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{5^7}{5^5} \\[6pt]
&= 5^{7-5} \\[6pt]
&= 5^2 = 25
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the numerator, the powers $5^4$ and $5^3$ have the same base. By the rule for the **product of powers with the same base**, add the exponents, and since $4 + 3 = 7$, the numerator is $5^7$.

The quotient $\dfrac{5^7}{5^5}$ also involves powers with the same base, so by the rule for the **quotient of powers with the same base**, subtract the exponents. Since $7 - 5 = 2$, the result is $5^2 = 5 \cdot 5 = 25$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Calculate
$$
\frac{\left( 2^3 \right)^4}{2^5 \cdot 2^4} .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\left( 2^3 \right)^4}{2^5 \cdot 2^4}
&= \frac{2^{3 \cdot 4}}{2^{5+4}} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{2^{12}}{2^9} \\[6pt]
&= 2^{12-9} \\[6pt]
&= 2^3 = 8
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The numerator is a power raised to another power. By the **power of a power** rule, multiply the exponents, and since $3 \cdot 4 = 12$, the numerator is $2^{12}$.

In the denominator, the two powers have the same base, so add their exponents. Since $5 + 4 = 9$, the denominator is $2^9$. In the quotient, subtract the exponents, giving $12 - 9 = 3$, so the result is $2^3 = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 8$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Calculate
$$
\frac{\left( 3^2 \right)^4 \cdot 3^5}{\left( 3^3 \right)^3 \cdot 3^2} .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\left( 3^2 \right)^4 \cdot 3^5}{\left( 3^3 \right)^3 \cdot 3^2}
&= \frac{3^8 \cdot 3^5}{3^9 \cdot 3^2} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{3^{8+5}}{3^{9+2}} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{3^{13}}{3^{11}} \\[6pt]
&= 3^{13-11} \\[6pt]
&= 3^2 = 9
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

First apply the **power of a power** rule to the two factors that require it. Since $2 \cdot 4 = 8$, we have $\left( 3^2 \right)^4 = 3^8$, and since $3 \cdot 3 = 9$, we have $\left( 3^3 \right)^3 = 3^9$.

The numerator and denominator now contain products of powers with base $3$, so add the exponents. The sums are $8 + 5 = 13$ in the numerator and $9 + 2 = 11$ in the denominator.

In the quotient $\dfrac{3^{13}}{3^{11}}$, subtract the exponents. Since $13 - 11 = 2$, the result is $3^2 = 9$.

</div>
