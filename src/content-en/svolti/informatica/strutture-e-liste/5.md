## Exercise 1

Use the stack of capacity $5$ from the chapter. Starting empty, push $4$, $9$ and $3$, then pop twice and push $6$. Find the removed values and the final state.

| Stage | Occupied prefix, from the base | `used` |
|---|---|---|
| After the initial pushes | $\langle 4,9,3\rangle$ | $3$ |
| After the first pop | $\langle 4,9\rangle$ | $2$ |
| After the second pop | $\langle 4\rangle$ | $1$ |
| Final state | $\langle 4,6\rangle$ | $2$ |

The values popped are $3$ and $9$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The three initial pushes use cells at indices $0$, $1$ and $2$ in order. With `used` equal to $3$, the first pop decrements it to $2$ and reads the cell at index $2$, which contains $3$. The second pop decrements it to $1$ and reads $9$. Only the cell at index $0$ remains occupied. Pushing $6$ writes to the first free cell at index $1$ and brings the count to $2$.

To check, apply the LIFO discipline directly to arrival order. The last two elements inserted before the pops are $9$ and $3$, so they are popped in the order $3$, $9$. After the final push only $4$ and $6$ are present, and $6$ arrived last. The independent count $3-2+1=2$ confirms the final occupancy.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A stack of capacity $5$ holds $\langle 1,2,3,4,5\rangle$ from the base. Attempt to push $8$, pop one element, and then attempt to push $9$. Determine the outcomes and the final top.

| Operation | Outcome | `used` afterwards |
|---|---|---|
| Push $8$ | Failure | $5$ |
| Pop | Success, value $5$ | $4$ |
| Push $9$ | Success | $5$ |

The final top is $9$.

<div class="spiegazione">

Before the first attempt the number of elements equals capacity. The push guard therefore returns `false` before any write. The pop finds five occupied cells and decrements `used` to $4$. It then reads the cell at index $4$, which still holds $5$. The second push attempt now finds a free cell and writes $9$ at index $4$. The final increment brings occupancy back to capacity.

As a check, follow the last cell alone. It holds $5$ through the pop, since the first attempt fails. It becomes logically free when `used` falls to $4$. With the second push it holds $9$ and returns to the occupied prefix. The cell at the base remains unchanged.

</div>
