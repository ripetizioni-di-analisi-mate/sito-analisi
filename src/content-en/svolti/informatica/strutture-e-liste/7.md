## Exercise 1

A circular queue has capacity $6$, `first` equal to $5$ and `used` equal to $4$. Remove one element and insert another. Give the read index, the write index and the occupied positions at the end.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{First} &=(5+1)\bmod6=0,\\[6pt]
\text{Write index} &=(0+3)\bmod6=3 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The read uses index $5$. The final sequence of occupied indices is $\langle 0,1,2,3\rangle$.

<div class="spiegazione">

Before removal, start at $5$ and follow four positions modulo capacity. These are $\langle 5,0,1,2\rangle$. Removal reads the first and leaves three elements, with the first position at index $0$. The new element has logical position $3$, after the three positions still occupied. Its physical position is therefore $3$, and incrementing the length returns the count to $4$.

To check, remove the first index from the initial list and append the candidate $3$. The order agrees with four consecutive indices computed from `first` modulo $6$. None of the remaining indices is $3$, so the write uses a free cell and preserves the three occupied cells.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A full circular queue of capacity $4$ has `first` equal to $2$ and values $\langle 12,15,18,20\rangle$ in logical order. Attempt to insert $27$, perform two removals and try to insert $27$ again. Find the removed values, the successful write index and the final state.

| Stage | `first` | `used` | Logical values |
|---|---|---|---|
| After the initial attempt | $2$ | $4$ | $\langle 12,15,18,20\rangle$ |
| After two removals | $0$ | $2$ | $\langle 18,20\rangle$ |
| After the second insertion | $0$ | $3$ | $\langle 18,20,27\rangle$ |

The values removed are $12$ and $15$. The successful write uses index $2$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The first attempt finds `used` equal to capacity and leaves the fields unchanged. The occupied cells in order are $\langle 2,3,0,1\rangle$. The removals read indices $2$ and $3$ in turn and advance `first` to $0$. Length becomes $2$, so there is space to insert a new value. The next logical position is $2$ and the physical index is $(0+2)\bmod4=2$.

The check compares sequences and occupancies. Dropping the prefix $\langle 12,15\rangle$ from the initial values leaves $\langle 18,20\rangle$. Appending $27$ produces the result in the table. The cell at index $2$ is exactly the cell freed by the first removal, so the computed index agrees with the available space.

</div>
