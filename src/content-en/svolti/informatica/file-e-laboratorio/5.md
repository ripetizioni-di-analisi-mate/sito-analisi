## Exercise 1

Accept lines containing exactly one integer from $-5$ to $12$. The four lines are `0`, `14`, `-5` and `13 q`. List the accepted readings in order and compute their sum.

The two accepted integers are $0$ and $-5$. Their sum is $0-5=-5$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The value $0$ satisfies the bound and occupies its whole line. The value $14$ exceeds the upper limit. The value $-5$ is accepted because the interval includes its lower endpoint. The fourth line is rejected both because the parsed value is out of range and because a character remains after the digits. Check the sum by removing the two rejected lines and adding the remaining numbers directly. The reading order is preserved.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In the C locale, a successful call executes `fprintf(file, "%d;%d\n", 7, -25)`. Reconstruct the characters emitted and the return value.

The output is `"7;-25\n"`. The count is $1+1+3+1=6$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The first specifier writes one digit, the format delimiter occupies one character, and the second specifier writes the sign and two digits. The newline is one more character. The function returns the total number of characters transmitted. For a separate check, enumerate the output characters as a digit, delimiter, minus sign, two digits and newline. Those items cover every position.

</div>
