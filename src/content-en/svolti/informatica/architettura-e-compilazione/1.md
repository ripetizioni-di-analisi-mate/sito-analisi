## Exercise 1

A device’s registers occupy every byte in the region shown in the figure. Addresses run from `0x180` inclusive to `0x1C0` exclusive. Each register occupies $4$ consecutive bytes. Compute the number of registers and the address of the last byte.

![Address range from 0x180 inclusive to 0x1C0 exclusive, with four bytes per register.](/immagini/informatica/architettura-e-compilazione/dati-svolto-en.png)

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{bytes in the range} &= (1\mathrm{C}0)_{16}-(180)_{16}
  = (40)_{16} = 64\\[6pt]
\text{registers} &= \frac{64}{4}=16 .
\end{aligned}
$$

The last byte has address `0x1BF`.

<div class="spiegazione">

The range is **half-open** because it includes the starting address and stops before the ending address. The difference between the two addresses therefore counts all the bytes without adding one. The registers occupy disjoint groups of four bytes, so divide the capacity by four. The last register starts after the first fifteen groups, at distance $15\cdot4=60$ from address `0x180`.

As a check, the fourth byte of the last register is at distance $60+3=63$, giving address `0x1BF`. The next address is `0x1C0`, the excluded endpoint.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A machine addresses bytes with $15$-bit words. RAM occupies two disjoint regions, from `0x0800` inclusive to `0x2000` exclusive and from `0x4000` inclusive to `0x4800` exclusive. Compute the number of bytes of RAM and the fraction of the entire address space they occupy.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{first region} &= (1800)_{16}=6144\\[6pt]
\text{second region} &= (0800)_{16}=2048\\[6pt]
\text{bytes of RAM} &= 6144+2048=8192\\[6pt]
\text{fraction} &= \frac{8192}{2^{15}}=\frac{1}{4}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Calculate the length of each range separately because the regions are disjoint. The first spans `0x2000` minus `0x0800`, so it contains `0x1800` bytes. The second spans `0x4800` minus `0x4000`. The denominator of the fraction counts all addresses that can be expressed with $15$ bits.

To check the total, count the positions outside RAM. There are $2048$ positions before the first region, $8192$ between the two regions and $14336$ from the end of the second region to the end of the address space. The sum $24576$ leaves $32768-24576=8192$ occupied positions, the same as the direct calculation.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A machine addresses bytes with $16$-bit addresses. ROM occupies addresses from `0x0000` inclusive to `0x2000` exclusive, RAM those from `0x8000` inclusive to `0xC000` exclusive and a device those from `0xFF00` inclusive to `0xFF40` exclusive. Each device register occupies $4$ consecutive bytes and the registers cover the whole device region. Compute the capacity of each region and the number of expressible addresses that belong to no region. Then determine which register contains byte `0xFF2E` and at which position.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{ROM} &= (2000)_{16}=8192\\[6pt]
\text{RAM} &= (\mathrm{C}000)_{16}-(8000)_{16}=(4000)_{16}=16384\\[6pt]
\text{device} &= (\mathrm{FF}40)_{16}-(\mathrm{FF}00)_{16}=(40)_{16}=64\\[6pt]
\text{unassigned} &= 2^{16}-(8192+16384+64)=65536-24640=40896
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\mathrm{FF}2\mathrm{E})_{16}-(\mathrm{FF}00)_{16} &= (2\mathrm{E})_{16}=46\\[6pt]
46 &= 11\cdot4+2 .
\end{aligned}
$$

The device has $\dfrac{64}{4}=16$ registers, numbered $0$ to $15$. Byte `0xFF2E` is at position $2$ of register $11$, which spans `0xFF2C` to `0xFF2F`.

<div class="spiegazione">

The three regions are half-open ranges, so the capacity of each is the difference between the excluded and the included endpoint. There are $2^{16}=65536$ addresses of $16$ bits in total. Since the regions are disjoint, the assigned addresses are the sum of the three capacities and the others have no memory behind them.

To find the register, compute the distance of the byte from the start of the device. The registers are disjoint groups of four bytes starting at `0xFF00`, so the quotient on division by $4$ gives the register number and the remainder gives the position of the byte within it. The quotient $11$ denotes the twelfth register, because numbering starts at zero. Its first byte is at distance $11\cdot4=44=(2\mathrm{C})_{16}$ from the start, hence at address `0xFF2C`.

As a check, count the free positions range by range. There are $(6000)_{16}=24576$ addresses between ROM and RAM, $(3\mathrm{F}00)_{16}=16128$ between RAM and the device and $(\mathrm{C}0)_{16}=192$ after the device. The sum is $40896$, the same as the direct calculation.

</div>
