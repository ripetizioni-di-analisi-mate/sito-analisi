## Exercise 1

Write $0.0234375$ in normalized binary form with four significant digits, including the leading $1$. Determine whether the format represents it exactly when any integer exponent is allowed.

$$
\begin{aligned}
0.0234375 &= \frac{3}{128} \\[6pt]
              &= 2^{-6}+2^{-7} \\[6pt]
              &= (1.100)_2\cdot 2^{-6} .
\end{aligned}
$$

The format represents the value **exactly**.

<div class="spiegazione">

The value is the sum of the powers of $2$ given by $2^{-6}$ and $2^{-7}$. Factoring out $2^{-6}$ leaves the significand $1+\dfrac{1}{2}$, whose binary digits are $1.1$. Add two trailing zeros to reach the required four digits. The zeros contribute nothing, so the value is unchanged.

As a check, the significand $1.100$ has value $\dfrac{12}{8}$. Multiplying it by $2^{-6}=\dfrac{1}{64}$ gives $\dfrac{12}{512}=\dfrac{3}{128}$. The error is zero.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Round $(100.01)_2$ to a normalized format with four significant digits and unrestricted integer exponents. Round to the nearest value. If both candidates are equally close, choose the one whose last bit is $0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(100.01)_2 &= (1.0001)_2\cdot 2^2=4.25 \\[6pt]
(1.000)_2\cdot 2^2&=4 \\
(1.001)_2\cdot 2^2&=4.5 .
\end{aligned}
$$

The result is $(1.000)_2\cdot 2^2$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The precision allows a leading $1$ and three fractional digits, so the significand $1.0001$ lies between $1.000$ and $1.001$. For exponent $2$, consecutive values are $2^{2-3}=\dfrac{1}{2}$ apart. The discarded bit has weight $\dfrac{1}{4}$ in the original number, so the value lies halfway between the candidates. The candidate $1.000$ ends in $0$, so the ties-to-even rule selects it.

As a check, $4.25-4=\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $4.5-4.25=\dfrac{1}{4}$. On the grid with spacing $\dfrac{1}{2}$, the candidates are the multiples with coefficients $8$ and $9$. Choosing $4$ gives the even coefficient.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Round $(1111)_2$ to a normalized format with three significant digits and unrestricted integer exponents. Round to nearest with ties to even and write the result in normalized form.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1111)_2 &= (1.111)_2\cdot 2^3 = 15 \\[6pt]
(1.11)_2\cdot 2^3 &= 14 \\
(1.00)_2\cdot 2^4 &= 16 .
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\bigl((1.11)_2+(0.01)_2\bigr)\cdot 2^3
  = (10.00)_2\cdot 2^3
  = (1.00)_2\cdot 2^4 .
$$

The result is $(1.00)_2\cdot 2^4$.

<div class="spiegazione">

For exponent $3$, the last retained bit has weight $2^{3-2}=2$. The significand $1.11$ is the largest allowed at that exponent and represents $14$. Increasing the value by one step gives $16$, the first value at exponent $4$. Since $15$ lies at a distance of $1$ from both candidates, choose the candidate whose last bit is even. Incrementing the significand $1.11$ propagates the carry and gives $10.00$. Moving the binary point one place to the left divides the significand by $2$, so increase the exponent from $3$ to $4$ to preserve the value.

To verify the tie, express the candidates in units of the spacing on the lower side, which is $2$. This gives $14=7\cdot2$ and $16=8\cdot2$. The second coefficient is even, and the normalized form $(1.00)_2\cdot2^4$ has value $16$.

</div>
