## Exercise 1

A monatomic ideal gas goes through the cycle $ABCDA$ formed by two isochoric and two isobaric processes, with $A$ ($1.00\,\mathrm{L}$; $1.00 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$), $B$ ($1.00\,\mathrm{L}$; $3.00 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$), $C$ ($3.00\,\mathrm{L}$; $3.00 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$), $D$ ($3.00\,\mathrm{L}$; $1.00 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$). Compute the heat exchanged in each process, the total work, the heat absorbed and the heat released.

::grafico{vettori="1,1,1,3;1,3,3,3;3,3,3,1;3,1,1,1" area="3;1;1;3" punti="1,1,pieno,A;1,3,pieno,B;3,3,pieno,C;3,1,pieno,D" xmin=0 xmax=4 ymin=0 ymax=4 uguale=1 nomi="V,p" titolo="The cycle $ABCDA$ with $V$ in liters and $p$ in $10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$; it is traversed clockwise and the work is the area of the square"}

$$
Q_{\text{isochoric}} = n\,c_V\,\Delta T = \frac{3}{2}\,V\,\Delta p, \qquad Q_{\text{isobaric}} = n\,c_p\,\Delta T = \frac{5}{2}\,p\,\Delta V
$$
$$
Q_{AB} = \frac{3}{2} \cdot 1.00 \cdot 10^{-3} \cdot 2.00 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{J} = 300\,\mathrm{J}
$$
$$
Q_{BC} = \frac{5}{2} \cdot 3.00 \cdot 10^5 \cdot 2.00 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{J} = 1500\,\mathrm{J}
$$
$$
Q_{CD} = \frac{3}{2} \cdot 3.00 \cdot 10^{-3} \cdot (-2.00 \cdot 10^5)\,\mathrm{J} = -900\,\mathrm{J}
$$
$$
Q_{DA} = \frac{5}{2} \cdot 1.00 \cdot 10^5 \cdot (-2.00 \cdot 10^{-3})\,\mathrm{J} = -500\,\mathrm{J}
$$
$$
L = (p_B - p_A)(V_C - V_B) = 2.00 \cdot 10^5 \cdot 2.00 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{J} = 400\,\mathrm{J}
$$
$$
Q_{\text{in}} = 300\,\mathrm{J} + 1500\,\mathrm{J} = 1800\,\mathrm{J}, \qquad Q_{\text{out}} = 900\,\mathrm{J} + 500\,\mathrm{J} = 1400\,\mathrm{J}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The number of moles is not given, but it is not needed: by the equation of state $nR\,\Delta T = \Delta(pV)$, which on an isochoric process equals $V\,\Delta p$ and on an isobaric process $p\,\Delta V$. Therefore the heat of an **isochoric process** is $\dfrac{c_V}{R}\,V\,\Delta p$ and that of an **isobaric process** is $\dfrac{c_p}{R}\,p\,\Delta V$, with $\dfrac{c_V}{R} = \dfrac{3}{2}$ and $\dfrac{c_p}{R} = \dfrac{5}{2}$ for a monatomic gas. The signs follow from the increments: the gas absorbs heat when it warms up, in $AB$ and $BC$, and releases it in $CD$ and $DA$.

The cycle is traversed clockwise, therefore the total work is positive and equal to the area of the enclosed square. As a check, the sum of the heats is $300 + 1500 - 900 - 500 = 400\,\mathrm{J}$, equal to the work, as the first law predicts with $\Delta U = 0$. The gas **absorbs $1800\,\mathrm{J}$ and releases $1400\,\mathrm{J}$**, and the difference is the work produced.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A monatomic ideal gas goes through the following cycle: from $A$ ($1.00\,\mathrm{L}$; $4.00 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$) a quasi-static isothermal expansion up to $B$ with $V_B = 4.00\,\mathrm{L}$; from $B$ an isobaric compression down to $C$ with $V_C = 1.00\,\mathrm{L}$; from $C$ an isochoric heating back to $A$. Compute the work and the heat of each process and the total work.

::grafico{f="4/x+0*sqrt((x-1)*(4-x))" vettori="4,1,1,1;1,1,1,4;2.3,1.739,2.5,1.6" area="4/x;1;1;4" punti="1,4,pieno,A;4,1,pieno,B;1,1,pieno,C" xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=5 uguale=1 nomi="V,p" titolo="The cycle $ABCA$ with $V$ in liters and $p$ in $10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$: isothermal $AB$, isobaric $BC$, isochoric $CA$; the shaded area is the total work"}

$$
p_B = p_A\,\frac{V_A}{V_B} = 1.00 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}, \qquad p_C = p_B
$$
$$
L_{AB} = Q_{AB} = p_A V_A \ln\frac{V_B}{V_A} = 400 \ln 4\,\mathrm{J} \approx 555\,\mathrm{J}
$$
$$
L_{BC} = p_B\,(V_C - V_B) = -300\,\mathrm{J}, \qquad Q_{BC} = \frac{5}{2}\,p_B\,(V_C - V_B) = -750\,\mathrm{J}
$$
$$
L_{CA} = 0, \qquad Q_{CA} = \frac{3}{2}\,V_A\,(p_A - p_C) = \frac{3}{2} \cdot 1.00 \cdot 10^{-3} \cdot 3.00 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{J} = 450\,\mathrm{J}
$$
$$
L = L_{AB} + L_{BC} + L_{CA} = (555 - 300)\,\mathrm{J} \approx 255\,\mathrm{J}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The pressure at $B$ follows from Boyle's law, because $A$ and $B$ lie on the same isotherm, and $C$ has the pressure of $B$ because $BC$ is isobaric. Along the **isotherm** the internal energy does not change, therefore the heat absorbed equals the work, which is $nRT \ln\dfrac{V_B}{V_A}$ with $nRT = p_A V_A = 400\,\mathrm{J}$.

Along the isobaric process the gas is compressed, therefore the work is negative and the gas releases heat; along the isochoric process the work is zero and the gas absorbs the heat that brings it back to the temperature of $A$. The cycle is traversed clockwise and the total work, $255\,\mathrm{J}$, is the area between the isotherm and the isobaric segment. As a check, the sum of the heats is $555 - 750 + 450 = 255\,\mathrm{J}$, equal to the total work, as the first law predicts in a cycle. The gas **absorbs about $1.00 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$** in the processes $AB$ and $CA$ and releases $750\,\mathrm{J}$ in the isobaric process.

</div>

## Exercise 3

$1.00\,\mathrm{mol}$ of monatomic ideal gas is in the state $A$ with $T_A = 600\,\mathrm{K}$ and $V_A = 10.0\,\mathrm{L}$. The gas undergoes a quasi-static adiabatic expansion up to the state $B$ with $T_B = 300\,\mathrm{K}$, then a quasi-static isothermal compression down to the state $C$ with $V_C = V_A$, and finally returns to $A$ with an isochoric heating. Compute the volume $V_B$, the pressures in the three states, the work and the heat of each process and the total work.

::grafico{f="24.94/x+0*sqrt((x-10)*(28.29-x));4.988*(10/x)^(5/3)+0*sqrt((x-10)*(28.29-x))" vettori="10,2.494,10,4.988;16,2.279,17.5,1.963;19,1.313,17.5,1.425" punti="10,4.988,pieno,A;28.28,0.882,pieno,B;10,2.494,pieno,C" xmin=0 xmax=32 ymin=0 ymax=6 nomi="V,p" titolo="The cycle $ABCA$ with $V$ in liters and $p$ in $10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$: adiabatic $AB$ (dashed), isothermal $BC$ (solid line), isochoric $CA$"}

$$
T V^{\gamma - 1} = \text{constant} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad V_B = V_A \left( \frac{T_A}{T_B} \right)^{\frac{1}{\gamma - 1}} = 10.0 \cdot 2^{\frac{3}{2}}\,\mathrm{L} \approx 28.3\,\mathrm{L}
$$
$$
p_A = \frac{nRT_A}{V_A} \approx 4.99 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}, \qquad p_B = \frac{nRT_B}{V_B} \approx 8.82 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{Pa}, \qquad p_C = \frac{nRT_B}{V_A} \approx 2.49 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}
$$
$$
Q_{AB} = 0, \qquad L_{AB} = n\,c_V\,(T_A - T_B) = 1.00 \cdot \frac{3}{2} \cdot 8.314 \cdot 300\,\mathrm{J} \approx 3.74 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}
$$
$$
Q_{BC} = L_{BC} = nRT_B \ln\frac{V_A}{V_B} = 8.314 \cdot 300 \cdot \ln\frac{1}{2^{\frac{3}{2}}}\,\mathrm{J} \approx -2.59 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}
$$
$$
L_{CA} = 0, \qquad Q_{CA} = n\,c_V\,(T_A - T_B) \approx 3.74 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}
$$
$$
L = L_{AB} + L_{BC} = (3741 - 2593)\,\mathrm{J} \approx 1.15 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For the monatomic gas $\gamma = \dfrac{5}{3}$, therefore $\gamma - 1 = \dfrac{2}{3}$ and the exponent $\dfrac{1}{\gamma - 1}$ equals $\dfrac{3}{2}$. From the relation of the **adiabatic process** $TV^{\gamma - 1} = \text{constant}$ the volume at $B$ is obtained: the temperature is halved, therefore the volume increases by the factor $2^{\frac{3}{2}} \approx 2.83$. The pressures follow from the equation of state, with the volumes in cubic meters.

In the adiabatic process $Q = 0$ and the work equals the decrease in internal energy, $n\,c_V\,(T_A - T_B)$. In the isothermal process at $300\,\mathrm{K}$ the gas is compressed, therefore the work is negative and the gas releases an equal amount of heat. In the isochoric process the work is zero and the heat absorbed brings the temperature back from $300\,\mathrm{K}$ to $600\,\mathrm{K}$; it has the same value as the adiabatic work, because the two processes connect the same two temperatures.

The total work is positive, as expected for a cycle traversed clockwise. As a check, the sum of the heats, $0 - 2593 + 3741 = 1148\,\mathrm{J}$, coincides with the total work, therefore the cycle **produces about $1.15\,\mathrm{kJ}$** of work while absorbing $3.74\,\mathrm{kJ}$ in the isochoric process.

</div>
