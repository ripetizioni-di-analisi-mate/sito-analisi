## Exercise 1

A glyceraldehyde Fischer projection has CHO above, $\mathrm{CH_2OH}$ below, OH right and H left. Determine priorities, assign R/S and D/L, and explain what information is needed to state optical sign.

The ranking is
$$\mathrm{OH}>\mathrm{CHO}>\mathrm{CH_2OH}>\mathrm H.$$
The carbon-starting groups compare as O,O,H and O,H,H. The $1\to2\to3$ path is anticlockwise, but horizontal H points forwards: reversing gives **R**. Right-side OH in this representation identifies **D**. Optical sign requires experimental information under appropriate conditions.

![D/R glyceraldehyde on the left and L/S on the right](/figure/chimica-semestre-filtro/carbonio-idrocarburi-stereochimica/fischer-en.svg)

<div class="spiegazione">

Formal oxygen duplication in the carbonyl is a CIP comparison device and does not change molecular formula. Once priorities are assigned, Fischer spatial conventions must be applied: forgetting that H points forwards would incorrectly give S. The D series instead uses a specified configurational reference. D and R coincide here for this glyceraldehyde, but the example does not universally equate D with R in other structures. The figure also shows the mirror image, where exchanging H and OH gives L and S. Both structural assignments can be made without a polarimetric measurement.

</div>

## Exercise 2

List the configurational stereoisomers of 2,3-dichlorobutane and explain why the count is not simply four. Classify RR/SS and RR/meso. Distinguish a meso form from a racemate.

Initial assignments are RR, SS, RS and SR. Symmetry makes RS and SR descriptions of the same species, so
$$N=2^2-1=3.$$
RR and SS are enantiomers. RR and meso are diastereoisomers. The meso form is one achiral species; an equimolar RR/SS mixture is instead a racemate.

![Three distinct Fischer projections and identity of meso RS/SR](/figure/chimica-semestre-filtro/carbonio-idrocarburi-stereochimica/meso-en.svg)

<div class="spiegazione">

The molecule’s two ends are identical methyl groups. Comparing the whole structure while allowing rotations and equivalent numbering from the other end establishes RS/SR identity. This reduces the combinatorial maximum of four without removing local stereogenic centres. RR has SS as its mirror and does not become meso through conformational rotation. The meso/racemate distinction remains even when both give zero net optical rotation: in the first, each molecule is achiral; in the second, two chiral species compensate in the measurement. The result therefore requires a symmetry check as well as centre counting.

</div>
