## Exercise 1

Classify three changes: A, propanone and an enol with the same formula but H in a different position; B, two enolate formulas with fixed nuclei and charge drawn on C or O; C, uric acid transferring a proton to solvent to form urate. Identify the balances distinguishing these cases.

| Case | Description | Total charge and protons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Tautomerism | Unchanged within the species |
| B | Resonance | Unchanged; nuclei fixed |
| C | Deprotonation | One fewer H and more negative charge |

<div class="spiegazione">

In case A, connectivity changes: a proton moves between an alpha carbon and oxygen, together with redistribution of double bonds. These are distinguishable species in equilibrium, although one often predominates. Catalysis can accelerate interconversion without automatically making their amounts equal.

Case B does not describe two molecules in equilibrium. Resonance contributors represent delocalization within one species; interpreting them as proton movement would introduce a change absent from the data.

In case C, the proton leaves the species and charge changes. Uric acid and urate can also have tautomers, but that possibility does not remove the acid-base character of this particular step. The same distinction helps with cytosine: tautomerization can alter hydrogen-bond donors and acceptors without changing total formula or adding a proton from outside.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Ideally, $0.200\,\mathrm{mol}$ of ethanal undergoes complete aldol addition, after which all product is dehydrated. Assume no side reactions. Use $M(\text{ethanal})=44.0$, $M(\text{but-2-enal})=70.0$ and $M(\mathrm{H_2O})=18.0$, in $\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$. Find final amounts and masses.

$$
2\,\mathrm{CH_3CHO}\longrightarrow\mathrm{CH_3CH(OH)CH_2CHO}
\longrightarrow\mathrm{CH_3CH{=}CHCHO}+\mathrm{H_2O}.
$$

The products are $0.100\,\mathrm{mol}$ of but-2-enal and the same amount of water: $7.00\,\mathrm g$ and $1.80\,\mathrm g$ respectively.

![Four-carbon aldol-addition balance followed by water loss](/figure/chimica-semestre-filtro/gruppi-funzionali/aldolica-en.svg)

<div class="spiegazione">

Each aldol product incorporates two ethanal molecules. Organic moles are halved, but carbons are conserved: two C2 units become one C4 unit. The donor's alpha carbon bonds to the acceptor carbonyl carbon, which becomes the OH-bearing center.

Dehydration does not change the number of organic molecules, but removes one water molecule per beta-hydroxyaldehyde and creates the conjugated double bond. The one-to-one factor therefore applies to the second stage, while the two-to-one factor concerns the first.

The initial mass is 8.80 g and the final sum is also 8.80 g. Omitting water would make mass conservation appear violated. The exercise imposes ideal conversion: a real mixture may contain remaining reactant or other products requiring additional data.

</div>
