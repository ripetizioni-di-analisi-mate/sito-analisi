## Exercise 1

An isolated glucan has 12 residues and a single tree structure without cycles or protein attached to its root. It has three binary α$(1\to6)$ branches, with all other links α$(1\to4)$. Count total links, α$(1\to4)$ links and nonreducing ends. Explain why it remains a homopolymer.

![Local α(1→6) linkage beside α(1→4) segments.](/figure/chimica-semestre-filtro/biomolecole/branch-en.svg)

$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{links}}&=12-1=11,\\[4pt]
N_{\alpha(1\to4)}&=11-3=8,\\[4pt]
N_{\mathrm{ends}}&=1+3=4.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

A connected tree is constructed by adding each new residue through one link. Starting with the first residue, the other eleven therefore require **eleven bonds**. Three are specified as α$(1\to6)$; subtracting them leaves eight α$(1\to4)$. The count does not depend on whether the drawn structure is linear or branched, provided no cycles occur.

An unbranched chain has one nonreducing end. Each bifurcation adds one terminal path and increases the count by one: three branches give four ends. The root retains one free anomeric centre under the stated assumptions. The polymer still contains only glucose and therefore remains a homopolymer: branching changes connections, not monomer identity. As a check, $8+3=11$ recovers the total. The figure shows only the local branch type; the counts refer to the 12 residues in the question.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A GAG alternates glucuronate and N-acetylglucosamine with β$(1\to3)$ and β$(1\to4)$ bonds and contains no sulfate. A fragment contains six complete disaccharides. Identify it and calculate its charge in a model where every glucuronate contributes $-1$ and all other groups are neutral. Explain its affinity for water and cations.

$$
N_{\mathrm{GlcA}}=6,
\qquad N_{\mathrm{GlcNAc}}=6,
\qquad q=6(-1)+6(0)=-6.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The composition, linkage sequence and absence of sulfate identify **hyaluronan**. Each disaccharide carries one glucuronate with a carboxylate, so six repeats give six negative charges. N-Acetyl is an amide and is not counted as positive ammonium; absence of sulfate does not make the polymer neutral.

The charges can interact electrostatically with cations and orient water molecules. Other hydroxyl groups contribute hydrogen bonds, allowing the chain to create a strongly hydrated environment. This connects chemical composition to matrix properties without attributing all hydration to one group. As a check, the fragment contains twelve residues but only six carboxylate groups: using the total monosaccharide count would incorrectly double the charge. It is a heteropolysaccharide because two different units alternate; regular repetition does not make it a homopolymer.

</div>
