## Exercise 1

The Gerber beam shown consists of beam $AC$, with a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$, together with beam $CD$, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, connected at $C$ and supported by a roller at $D$. They can act a moving force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a uniform load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ of variable length, downward. Calculate the minimum value and maximum value of the bending moment at section $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,D,4 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with moving loads $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

$$
\eta_{M_B}(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
0 & \text{if } 0 \le \xi \le 4 \\[4pt]
-(\xi - 4) & \text{if } 4 \le \xi \le 6 \\[4pt]
-\dfrac{10 - \xi}{2} & \text{if } 6 \le \xi \le 10
\end{cases}
\qquad
\eta_{\min} = \eta_{M_B}(6) = -2\,\mathrm{m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" diagramma="0,0,4,0,0;4,0,6,0,(-1)*s;6,0,10,0,-2+(1/2)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for the moment at $B$ ($\mathrm{m}$), zero over $AB$ and negative from $B$ to $D$"}

$$
\mathcal{A}^- = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot (-2) + \frac{1}{2} \cdot 4 \cdot (-2) = -2 - 4 = -6\,\mathrm{m^2} ,
\qquad
\mathcal{A}^+ = 0
$$
$$
M_{B,\min} = P\,\eta_{\min} + q\,\mathcal{A}^- = 20 \cdot (-2) + 5 \cdot (-6) = -40 - 30 = -70\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
M_{B,\max} = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" carichi="B,D,0.6,0.6,q" forze="6 0.6,0,-1.8,P" titolo="Load positions for $M_{B,\min}$, with $P$ at $C$ and $q$ extending from $B$ to $D$"}

$$
\text{check (minimum configuration):} \quad
\begin{aligned}
&\text{beam } CD\text{, moments about } D\text{:} \quad Y_C \cdot 4 - 20 \cdot 2 = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; Y_C = 10\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
&\text{segment } BC\text{, moments about } B\text{:} \quad -M_B - 10 \cdot 1 - (Y_C + P) \cdot 2 = 70 - 10 - 60 = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The moment on the $B$ support belongs to the supporting segment. For the **Müller–Breslau theorem** a hinge is inserted in $B$: the span $AB$ remains still, the overhang rotates around $B$ with $\varphi_d = -1$ and brings $C$ to $-2\,\mathrm{m}$, the suspended segment rotates about $D$. The line is zero on the span and negative elsewhere, so the moment at $B$ can only stretch the upper fibers or be zero.

For the **most unfavorable position** theorem the force goes to the minimum ordinate point, the hinge $C$, and the variable length load on all negative ordinate segments, from $B$ to $D$. The negative area is the sum of the two triangles that have the common vertex in $C$. You get $M_{B,\min} = -70\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension. The maximum is $0$ and you have with the loads on the span $AB$ only or with the beam unloaded, because the line has no positive ordinate.

Check recalculates the minimum with balance. The suspended segment, with the uniform load of resultant $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $D$, transmits the force $Y_C = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting downward at $C$; the force $P$ applied in the hinge is attributed to the supporting segment. On the segment $BC$, to the right of $B$, act the internal moment (clockwise if positive), the uniform load of the resultant $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$ and the two forces in $C$ $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

For the Gerber beam shown, the same as in the previous exercise, with the same $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ moving loads of variable length, calculate the maximum and minimum value of the bending moment at section $S$ in the middle of span $AB$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,B,2 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,D,4 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam showing section $S$ at the midpoint of span $AB$"}

$$
\eta_{M_S}(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{\xi}{2} & \text{if } 0 \le \xi \le 2 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4 - \xi}{2} & \text{if } 2 \le \xi \le 6 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{10 - \xi}{4} & \text{if } 6 \le \xi \le 10
\end{cases}
\qquad
\eta_{\max} = \eta_{M_S}(2) = 1\,\mathrm{m} ,
\quad
\eta_{\min} = \eta_{M_S}(6) = -1\,\mathrm{m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,(1/2)*s;2,0,4,0,1+(-1/2)*s;4,0,6,0,(-1/2)*s;6,0,10,0,-1+(1/4)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for the moment at $S$ ($\mathrm{m}$), positive over $AB$ and negative from $B$ to $D$"}

$$
\mathcal{A}^+ = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 4 \cdot 1 = 2\,\mathrm{m^2} ,
\qquad
\mathcal{A}^- = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot (-1) + \frac{1}{2} \cdot 4 \cdot (-1) = -1 - 2 = -3\,\mathrm{m^2}
$$
$$
M_{S,\max} = 20 \cdot 1 + 5 \cdot 2 = 30\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
M_{S,\min} = 20 \cdot (-1) + 5 \cdot (-3) = -35\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" forze="2 0.6,0,-1.8,P" titolo="Load positions for $M_{S,\max}$, with $P$ at $S$ and $q$ covering span $AB$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" carichi="B,D,0.6,0.6,q" forze="6 0.6,0,-1.8,P" titolo="Load positions for $M_{S,\min}$, with $P$ at $C$ and $q$ extending from $B$ to $D$"}

$$
\text{check (minimum configuration, beam } AC\text{, moments about } B\text{):} \quad
-V_A \cdot 4 - 10 \cdot 1 - 30 \cdot 2 = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; V_A = -17.5\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_S - V_A \cdot 2 = -35 + 35 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

With the hinge $S$ the segment $AS$ rotates around $A$ and the segment $SC$ around $B$; with the relative unit rotation the hinge $S$ rises to $\dfrac{2 \cdot 2}{4} = 1\,\mathrm{m}$. The segment $SC$ continues past $B$ in a straight line, then $C$ drops by $1\,\mathrm{m}$, and the suspended segment rotates about $D$. In the span the line is that of the simply supported beam; the overhang and the suspended beam give a negative branch.

The maximum is achieved by force at $S$ and uniform load on $AB$ span only; the minimum with force at $C$ and load from $B$ to $D$. It results $M_{S,\max} = 30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension, and $M_{S,\min} = -35\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension: the section in span should be checked for moments of both signs.

The check recalculates the minimum. As in the preceding exercise, the suspended beam transfers force $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the supporting beam at $C$, where force $P$ also acts. The total is $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$; the load on the overhang has resultant $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$. Moment equilibrium of beam $AC$ about $B$ gives $V_A = -17.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward, and the segment $AS$ gives $M_S = V_A \cdot 2 = -35\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. For maximum balance gives $V_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M_S = 20 \cdot 2 - 10 \cdot 1 = 30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

</div>
