## Exercise 1

The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. On it can act a moving force $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a uniform load $q = 8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ of variable length, both downward. Calculate the maximum value and minimum value of the bending moment at section $S$ at midspan, and the positions of the loads that produce them.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,3,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,S,3 m,0.9;S,B,3 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam with section $S$ at the midpoint of the span; moving loads $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $q = 8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

$$
\eta_M(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{\xi}{2} & \text{if } 0 \le \xi \le 3 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{6 - \xi}{2} & \text{if } 3 \le \xi \le 8
\end{cases}
\qquad
\eta_{\max} = \eta_M(3) = \frac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
\eta_{\min} = \eta_M(8) = -1\,\mathrm{m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,3,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,3,0,(1/2)*s;3,0,6,0,3/2+(-1/2)*s;6,0,8,0,(-1/2)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for the moment at $S$ ($\mathrm{m}$), positive over $AB$ and negative over $BC$"}

$$
\mathcal{A}^+ = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot \frac{3}{2} = \frac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{m^2} ,
\qquad
\mathcal{A}^- = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot (-1) = -1\,\mathrm{m^2}
$$
$$
M_{S,\max} = P\,\eta_{\max} + q\,\mathcal{A}^+ = 30 \cdot \frac{3}{2} + 8 \cdot \frac{9}{2} = 45 + 36 = 81\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
M_{S,\min} = P\,\eta_{\min} + q\,\mathcal{A}^- = 30 \cdot (-1) + 8 \cdot (-1) = -38\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,3,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" forze="3 0.6,0,-1.6,P" titolo="Load positions for $M_{S,\max}$, with $P$ at $S$ and $q$ over the entire span $AB$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,3,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="B,C,0.6,0.6,q" forze="C,0,-1.6,P" titolo="Load positions for $M_{S,\min}$, with $P$ at $C$ and $q$ covering only overhang $BC$"}

$$
\text{check (minimum configuration):} \quad
\begin{aligned}
&\text{rotation about } B\text{:} \quad -V_A \cdot 6 - 16 \cdot 1 - 30 \cdot 2 = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; V_A = -\frac{38}{3}\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
&\text{segment } AS\text{, moments about } S\text{:} \quad M_S - V_A \cdot 3 = -38 - \left(-\frac{38}{3}\right) \cdot 3 = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The moment influence lines in $S$ is that of the simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, with $\dfrac{3 \cdot 3}{6} = \dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ vertex, prolonged in a straight line on the overhang, where it becomes negative. The right branch is $0$ in $B$ and $-1\,\mathrm{m}$ in $C$.

For the **most unfavorable position** theorem the force goes to the point where the ordinate is maximum for the maximum, i.e. in $S$, and to the point where it is minimum for the minimum, i.e. in $C$. The load of variable length goes on all and only the positive ordinate segments for the maximum, that is, on the span $AB$, and on the negative ordinate segments for the minimum, that is, on the overhang. A load on the overhang in the maximum configuration would reduce the moment, because it would add a negative contribution to it.

You get $M_{S,\max} = 81\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension, and $M_{S,\min} = -38\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension. The check recalculates the minimum with the balance: the resultant of the load on the overhang is $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$, $V_A$ is negative, i.e. directed downward, and the segment $AS$ gives its own $M_S = -38\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. For maximum balance gives $V_A = 39\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M_S = 39 \cdot 3 - 24 \cdot 1.5 = 81\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

On the simply supported beam shown, of span $8\,\mathrm{m}$, a moving force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ of variable length can act. Calculate the maximum value and the minimum value of the shear at section $S$ to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the hinge $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,B,6 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam showing section $S$; moving loads $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

$$
\eta_T(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac{\xi}{8} & \text{if } 0 \le \xi < 2 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{8 - \xi}{8} & \text{if } 2 < \xi \le 8
\end{cases}
\qquad
\eta_T(2^+) = \frac{3}{4} ,
\qquad
\eta_T(2^-) = -\frac{1}{4}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,(-1/8)*s;2,0,8,0,3/4+(-1/8)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for shear at $S$, negative over $AS$ and positive over $SB$"}

$$
\mathcal{A}^+ = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot \frac{3}{4} = \frac{9}{4}\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
\mathcal{A}^- = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot \left(-\frac{1}{4}\right) = -\frac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
T_{S,\max} = 20 \cdot \frac{3}{4} + 6 \cdot \frac{9}{4} = 15 + 13.5 = 28.5\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
T_{S,\min} = 20 \cdot \left(-\frac{1}{4}\right) + 6 \cdot \left(-\frac{1}{4}\right) = -5 - 1.5 = -6.5\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="S,B,0.6,0.6,q" forze="2.05 0.6,0,-1.6,P" titolo="Load positions for $T_{S,\max}$, with $P$ immediately to the right of $S$ and $q$ on segment $SB$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,S,0.6,0.6,q" forze="1.95 0.6,0,-1.6,P" titolo="Load positions for $T_{S,\min}$, with $P$ immediately to the left of $S$ and $q$ on segment $AS$"}

$$
\text{check (maximum configuration, segment } AS\text{):} \quad
V_A = \frac{20 \cdot 6 + 36 \cdot 3}{8} = 28.5\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A - T_S = 28.5 - 28.5 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The influence line for the shear has the jump of $1$ in $S$: the maximum ordinate, $\dfrac{3}{4}$, is with the force immediately to the right of $S$, the minimum, $-\dfrac{1}{4}$, with the force immediately to the left. The value in $S$ is not defined, because the shear itself jumps under a concentrated force; then the limits from the right and the left are considered.

The variable length load produces the maximum when occupying the section $SB$, where the line is positive, and the minimum when occupying the section $AS$. The areas are triangles: $\dfrac{9}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$ and $-\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$. You get $T_{S,\max} = 28.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $T_{S,\min} = -6.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The shear in a section close to the $A$ support is predominantly positive, because most of the line lies on the positive side.

The check uses the balance with loads at the maximum position: the force is at $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$ and the resultant of the uniform load, $36\,\mathrm{kN}$, at $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$. On the segment $AS$ no loads act, so vertical force equilibrium gives $T_S = V_A$. For the minimum, $V_B = \dfrac{20 \cdot 2 + 12 \cdot 1}{8} = 6.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, and on the unloaded segment $SB$ we have $T_S = -V_B = -6.5\,\mathrm{kN}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The beam shown, of length $9\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from end $E$ and the roller support at $B$ to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from end $F$. A moving force $P = 25\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a uniform load $q = 10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ of variable length can act. Calculate the maximum value and the minimum value of the reaction $V_B$ and tell what the minimum sign indicates.

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;A,2,0;B,7,0;F,9,0" aste="E-F" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="E,A,2 m,0.9;A,B,5 m,0.9;B,F,2 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with two overhangs; moving loads $P = 25\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $q = 10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

$$
\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \frac{\xi - 2}{5}
\quad (0 \le \xi \le 9) ,
\qquad
\eta_{\max} = \eta_{V_B}(9) = \frac{7}{5} ,
\qquad
\eta_{\min} = \eta_{V_B}(0) = -\frac{2}{5}
$$

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;A,2,0;B,7,0;F,9,0" aste="E-F" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,-2/5+(1/5)*s;2,0,7,0,(1/5)*s;7,0,9,0,1+(1/5)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for $V_B$, negative over overhang $EA$"}

$$
\mathcal{A}^+ = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 7 \cdot \frac{7}{5} = \frac{49}{10}\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
\mathcal{A}^- = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot \left(-\frac{2}{5}\right) = -\frac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
V_{B,\max} = 25 \cdot \frac{7}{5} + 10 \cdot \frac{49}{10} = 35 + 49 = 84\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_{B,\min} = 25 \cdot \left(-\frac{2}{5}\right) + 10 \cdot \left(-\frac{2}{5}\right) = -10 - 4 = -14\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;A,2,0;B,7,0;F,9,0" aste="E-F" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,F,0.6,0.6,q" forze="F,0,-1.6,P" titolo="Load positions for $V_{B,\max}$, with $P$ at $F$ and $q$ extending from $A$ to $F$"}

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;A,2,0;B,7,0;F,9,0" aste="E-F" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="E,A,0.6,0.6,q" forze="E,0,-1.6,P" titolo="Load positions for $V_{B,\min}$, with $P$ at $E$ and $q$ covering only overhang $EA$"}

$$
\text{check (moments about } A\text{):} \quad
\begin{cases}
\text{maximum:} & V_B \cdot 5 - 25 \cdot 7 - 70 \cdot 3.5 = 420 - 175 - 245 = 0 \\[4pt]
\text{minimum:} & V_B \cdot 5 + 25 \cdot 2 + 20 \cdot 1 = -70 + 50 + 20 = 0
\end{cases}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The beam is one body, so the line of $V_B$ is a straight line over the entire length: it equals $0$ under the $A$, $1$ under $B$, $\dfrac{7}{5}$ in $F$ and $-\dfrac{2}{5}$ in $E$. The maximum is with the force at the end $F$ and the uniform load from $A$ to $F$; the minimum with the force at $E$ and the load on the left overhang only.

It results in **$V_{B,\max} = 84\,\mathrm{kN}$, Upward**, and **$V_{B,\min} = -14\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $14\,\mathrm{kN}$ down**. The negative minimum indicates that, with the loads on the $EA$ overhang, the beam tends to rise in $B$: the roller support must be bilateral, that is, capable of holding the beam down as well, or the beam must have sufficient weight to avoid detachment.

The check writes the rotation around $A$ in the two configurations. At most the resultant load is $70\,\mathrm{kN}$ $3.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and the force is at $7\,\mathrm{m}$; in the minimum the resultant is $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $1\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $A$ and the force to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left, with counterclockwise moments.

</div>
