## Exercise 1

The Gerber beam shown consists of cantilever $AC$, fixed at $A$ and of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, and beam $CB$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, connected to the cantilever through hinge $C$ and supported by a roller at $B$. Draw the influence lines for $V_B$, for support moment $M_A$, and for the bending moment at section $S$, a distance $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,1.5,0;C,3,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="5 0,0,-1.6,1" quote="A,S,1.5 m,1.2;S,C,1.5 m,1.2;C,B,4 m,1.2;A,5 0,ξ,0.7" titolo="Gerber beam with supporting cantilever $AC$ and suspended beam $CB$; section $S$ is at the midpoint of the cantilever"}

$$
V_B\!: \quad AC \text{ stationary} ,
\quad
CB \text{ rotates about } C ,
\qquad
\eta_{V_B}(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
0 & \text{if } 0 \le \xi \le 3 \\[4pt]
\dfrac{\xi - 3}{4} & \text{if } 3 \le \xi \le 7
\end{cases}
$$

$$
M_A\!: \quad \varphi_{AC} = 1 ,
\quad
v(C) = 3\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
\eta_{M_A}(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
\xi & \text{if } 0 \le \xi \le 3 \\[4pt]
\dfrac{3}{4}\,(7 - \xi) & \text{if } 3 \le \xi \le 7
\end{cases}
$$

$$
M_S\!: \quad \varphi_{SC} = -1 ,
\quad
v(C) = -1.5\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
\eta_{M_S}(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
0 & \text{if } 0 \le \xi \le 1.5 \\[4pt]
-(\xi - 1.5) & \text{if } 1.5 \le \xi \le 3 \\[4pt]
-\dfrac{3}{8}\,(7 - \xi) & \text{if } 3 \le \xi \le 7
\end{cases}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,3,0,0;3,0,7,0,(1/4)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for $V_B$, zero over the supporting cantilever"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,3,0,(1)*s;3,0,7,0,3+(-3/4)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for $M_A$ ($\mathrm{m}$), reaching its maximum at $C$ with $3$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,1.5,0;C,3,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,1.5,0,0;1.5,0,3,0,(-1)*s;3,0,7,0,-3/2+(3/8)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for the moment at $S$ ($\mathrm{m}$), reaching its minimum at $C$ with $-1.5$"}

$$
\text{check (load at } \xi = 5\,\mathrm{m}\text{, moments about } A\text{):} \quad
M_A + V_B \cdot 7 - 1 \cdot 5 = \frac{3}{2} + \frac{1}{2} \cdot 7 - 5 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The cantilever $AC$ is the supporting segment, because fixed support makes it statically determinate by itself; beam $CB$ is the suspended beam because it rests on the cantilever at $C$ and on the ground support at $B$. The **Müller–Breslau theorem** is used for each size.

Removing the roller support at $B$, the cantilever gets fixed and then stops, while $CB$ revolves about hinge $C$; with $v(B) = 1$ the influence line is zero along $AC$ and rises linearly from $0$ to $1$ over $CB$, as the theorem on **Gerber beam influence lines** predicts for a response of the suspended beam.

For $M_A$ the fixed support is replaced with a hinge. The cantilever rotates about $A$ through the counterclockwise angle $\varphi = 1$, so $v(\xi) = \xi$ and point $C$ rises by $3\,\mathrm{m}$; the beam $CB$ rotates around $B$ and the line drops linearly from $3$ to $0$. For $M_S$ a hinge is inserted in $S$: the segment $AS$ remains fixed, the segment $SC$ rotates around $S$ with $\varphi_s - \varphi_d = 0 - \varphi_d = 1$, i.e. $\varphi_d = -1$, and $C$ drops by $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$. On $AS$ the line is zero, because with the load between $A$ and $S$ the right part of $S$ is unloaded, since the suspended segment has $V_B = 0$, and therefore the internal actions in $S$ are zero.

A load on the suspended span gives a direct equilibrium check. At $C$ the suspended span transmits a downward force $\dfrac{7 - \xi}{4}$ to the cantilever, producing $M_A = 3 \cdot \dfrac{7 - \xi}{4}$ and $M_S = -1.5 \cdot \dfrac{7 - \xi}{4}$. The displayed check takes moments for the whole beam about $A$ with the load at $\xi = 5\,\mathrm{m}$. There the influence lines give $V_B = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $M_A = \dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The Gerber beam shown consists of beam $AC$, with a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$, together with beam $CD$, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, connected at $C$ and supported by a roller at $D$. Trace the influence lines of $V_A$, $V_B$ and the bending moment at section $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0;D,11,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" forze="9 0,0,-2,1" quote="A,B,5 m,1.2;B,C,2 m,1.2;C,D,4 m,1.2;A,9 0,ξ,0.7" titolo="Gerber beam with an overhanging supporting beam $AC$ and a suspended beam $CD$"}

$$
V_A\!: \quad AC \text{ rotates about } B ,
\quad
\varphi = -\frac{1}{5} ,
\quad
v(C) = -\frac{2}{5} ;
\qquad
V_B\!: \quad AC \text{ rotates about } A ,
\quad
\varphi = \frac{1}{5} ,
\quad
v(C) = \frac{7}{5}
$$

$$
\eta_{V_A}(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{5 - \xi}{5} & \text{if } 0 \le \xi \le 7 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{2}{5} \cdot \dfrac{11 - \xi}{4} & \text{if } 7 \le \xi \le 11
\end{cases}
\qquad
\eta_{V_B}(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{\xi}{5} & \text{if } 0 \le \xi \le 7 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{7}{5} \cdot \dfrac{11 - \xi}{4} & \text{if } 7 \le \xi \le 11
\end{cases}
$$

$$
M_B\!: \quad AB \text{ stationary} ,
\quad
\varphi_{BC} = -1 ,
\quad
v(C) = -2\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
\eta_{M_B}(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
0 & \text{if } 0 \le \xi \le 5 \\[4pt]
-(\xi - 5) & \text{if } 5 \le \xi \le 7 \\[4pt]
-\dfrac{11 - \xi}{2} & \text{if } 7 \le \xi \le 11
\end{cases}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0;D,11,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" diagramma="0,0,5,0,1+(-1/5)*s;5,0,7,0,(-1/5)*s;7,0,11,0,-2/5+(1/10)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for $V_A$ with ordinate $-\dfrac{2}{5}$ at $C$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0;D,11,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" diagramma="0,0,5,0,(1/5)*s;5,0,7,0,1+(1/5)*s;7,0,11,0,7/5+(-7/20)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for $V_B$ with ordinate $\dfrac{7}{5}$ at $C$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0;D,11,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" diagramma="0,0,5,0,0;5,0,7,0,(-1)*s;7,0,11,0,-2+(1/2)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for the moment at $B$ ($\mathrm{m}$), reaching its minimum at $C$ with $-2$"}

$$
\text{check (load at } \xi = 9\,\mathrm{m}\text{, beam } AC\text{):} \quad
Y_C = \frac{11 - 9}{4} = \frac{1}{2} ,
\qquad
V_A + V_B - Y_C = -\frac{1}{5} + \frac{7}{10} - \frac{1}{2} = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The beam $AC$ is the supporting: the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$ bind it to the ground in statically determinate mode. Beam $CD$ is the suspended beam. All three quantities belong to the supporting beam. Over the suspended beam their influence lines are therefore straight segments from the ordinate at $C$ to zero under $D$.

For $V_A$, the vertical component of the pin at $A$ is removed: the beam $AC$ rotates around $B$ and, with $v(A) = 1$, rotates towards $\dfrac{1}{5}$ clockwise, so the overhang drops and $C$ lowers by $\dfrac{2}{5}$. For $V_B$, remove the roller. Beam $AC$ then rotates about $A$ and point $C$ rises by $\dfrac{7}{5}$. In both cases the beam $CD$, hinged at $C$ and supported at $D$, rotates around $D$.

For $M_B$, a hinge is inserted into $B$. The segment $AB$ has a pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, so it stays still; the $BC$ overhang rotates about $B$ with $\varphi_d = -1$ and $C$ dropping by $2\,\mathrm{m}$. The line is zero over span $AB$ and negative elsewhere. At support $B$, any load on the overhang or the suspended beam produces a moment that puts the upper fibers in tension.

Check uses load at $\xi = 9\,\mathrm{m}$. At $C$ the suspended beam transmits a downward force $Y_C = \dfrac{1}{2}$, and the influence lines give $V_A = -\dfrac{2}{5} \cdot \dfrac{2}{4} = -\dfrac{1}{5}$ and $V_B = \dfrac{7}{5} \cdot \dfrac{2}{4} = \dfrac{7}{10}$; the vertical translation of only the beam $AC$ is satisfied.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The Gerber beam shown consists of three bodies: beam $AC$, with a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $1\,\mathrm{m}$; the beam $CE$, connected in $C$, with $C$ $D$ to $4\,\mathrm{m}$ roller support and overhang $DE$ of length $1\,\mathrm{m}$; the beam $EF$, connected in $E$ and supported with a $F$ to $3\,\mathrm{m}$ roller support from $E$. Trace the influence lines of $V_D$ and the bending moment at section $D$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,5,0;D,9,0;E,10,0;F,13,0" aste="A-C;C-E;E-F" cerniere="C;E" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello;F,carrello" forze="7 0,0,-2.2,1" quote="A,B,4 m,1.2;B,C,1 m,1.2;C,D,4 m,1.2;D,E,1 m,1.2;E,F,3 m,1.2;A,7 0,ξ,0.7" titolo="Three-body Gerber beam comprising $AC$, $CE$, and $EF$"}

$$
V_D\!: \quad AC \text{ stationary} ,
\quad
CE \text{ rotates about } C ,
\quad
v(E) = \frac{5}{4} ,
\qquad
\eta_{V_D}(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
0 & \text{if } 0 \le \xi \le 5 \\[4pt]
\dfrac{\xi - 5}{4} & \text{if } 5 \le \xi \le 10 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{5}{4} \cdot \dfrac{13 - \xi}{3} & \text{if } 10 \le \xi \le 13
\end{cases}
$$

$$
M_D\!: \quad AC \text{ and } CD \text{ stationary} ,
\quad
\varphi_{DE} = -1 ,
\quad
v(E) = -1\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
\eta_{M_D}(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
0 & \text{if } 0 \le \xi \le 9 \\[4pt]
-(\xi - 9) & \text{if } 9 \le \xi \le 10 \\[4pt]
-\dfrac{13 - \xi}{3} & \text{if } 10 \le \xi \le 13
\end{cases}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,5,0;D,9,0;E,10,0;F,13,0" aste="A-C;C-E;E-F" cerniere="C;E" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello;F,carrello" diagramma="0,0,4,0,0;4,0,5,0,0;5,0,9,0,(1/4)*s;9,0,10,0,1+(1/4)*s;10,0,13,0,5/4+(-5/12)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for $V_D$, zero over $AC$ and reaching its maximum at $E$ with $\dfrac{5}{4}$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,5,0;D,9,0;E,10,0;F,13,0" aste="A-C;C-E;E-F" cerniere="C;E" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello;F,carrello" diagramma="0,0,4,0,0;4,0,5,0,0;5,0,9,0,0;9,0,10,0,(-1)*s;10,0,13,0,-1+(1/3)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for the moment at $D$ ($\mathrm{m}$), nonzero only from $D$ to $F$"}

$$
\text{check (load at } \xi = 11.5\,\mathrm{m}\text{, beam } CE\text{, moments about } C\text{):} \quad
Y_E = \frac{13 - 11.5}{3} = \frac{1}{2} ,
\qquad
V_D \cdot 4 - Y_E \cdot 5 = \frac{5}{8} \cdot 4 - \frac{1}{2} \cdot 5 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The support order is $AC$, then $CE$, then $EF$: the beam $AC$ is statically bound to the ground; $CE$ beam leans to $AC$ in $C$ and to the ground in $D$; the beam $EF$ leans to $CE$ in $E$ and to the ground in $F$. A size of a body has no influence lines over all the bodies that carry it.

For $V_D$ you remove the roller support at $D$. The beam $AC$ remains stationary, so $CE$ revolves about hinge $C$ and, with $v(D) = 1$, end $E$ rises by $\dfrac{5}{4}$, because it is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$ versus $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $D$. The beam $EF$ rotates around $F$ and the line drops from $\dfrac{5}{4}$ to $0$.

For $M_D$, a hinge is inserted into $D$. The segment $CD$ has in $C$ the hinge with the beam $AC$, stationary, and in $D$ the roller support, so it is also stationary; the segment $DE$ rotates around $D$ with $\varphi_d = -1$ and $E$ drops by $1\,\mathrm{m}$; the beam $EF$ rotates about $F$. The moment on the $D$ stand depends only on the loads to the right of $D$, which puts the upper fibers in tension.

The check uses the load at $\xi = 11.5\,\mathrm{m}$, on the beam $EF$. The force transmitted in $E$ is $Y_E = \dfrac{1}{2}$, the line gives $V_D = \dfrac{5}{4} \cdot \dfrac{1.5}{3} = \dfrac{5}{8}$, and moment equilibrium of beam $CE$ about $C$ is satisfied. Similarly $M_D = -Y_E \cdot 1 = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$, equal to $\eta_{M_D}(11.5) = -\dfrac{1.5}{3}$.

</div>
