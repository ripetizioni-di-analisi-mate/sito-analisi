## Exercise 1

For the simply supported beam shown, of span $8\,\mathrm{m}$, derive by the equilibrium method the influence lines of the bending moment and the shear at section $S$, to $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from the hinge $A$. Then calculate $M_S$ and $T_S$ produced from a force $P = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,3,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="5.2 0,0,-2,1" quote="A,S,3 m,1.4;S,B,5 m,1.4;A,5.2 0,ξ,0.8" titolo="Simply supported beam showing section $S$ and a unit load at $\xi$"}

$$
\eta_{V_A}(\xi) = \frac{8 - \xi}{8} ,
\qquad
\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \frac{\xi}{8}
$$

$$
\xi > 3 \;\;(\text{segment } AS)\!: \quad
\begin{cases}
M = V_A \cdot 3 = \dfrac{3}{8}\,(8 - \xi) \\[8pt]
T = V_A = \dfrac{8 - \xi}{8}
\end{cases}
\qquad
\xi < 3 \;\;(\text{segment } SB)\!: \quad
\begin{cases}
M = V_B \cdot 5 = \dfrac{5}{8}\,\xi \\[8pt]
T = -V_B = -\dfrac{\xi}{8}
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\eta_M(3) = \frac{5 \cdot 3}{8} = \frac{15}{8} = 1.875\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
\lim_{\xi \to 3^-} \eta_T = -\frac{3}{8} ,
\qquad
\lim_{\xi \to 3^+} \eta_T = \frac{5}{8}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,3,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,3,0,(5/8)*s;3,0,8,0,15/8+(-3/8)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for the moment at $S$ ($\mathrm{m}$), with peak ordinate $\dfrac{15}{8}$ at the section"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,3,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,3,0,(-1/8)*s;3,0,8,0,5/8+(-1/8)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for shear at $S$, with a jump of $1$ from $-\dfrac{3}{8}$ to $\dfrac{5}{8}$"}

$$
M_S = P\,\eta_M(6) = 16 \cdot \frac{3}{8} \cdot 2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
T_S = P\,\eta_T(6) = 16 \cdot \frac{2}{8} = 4\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$$
\text{check (segment } AS\text{, moments about } S\text{):} \quad
V_A = 16 \cdot \frac{2}{8} = 4\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_S - V_A \cdot 3 = 12 - 4 \cdot 3 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The reactions with the load at $\xi$ are those of the simply supported beam, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$. For $M$ and $T$ in $S$, the balance of the segment on which the load *not* is located is used, because on that segment only a reaction acts and the internal actions in $S$.

If the load is to the right of $S$, only $V_A$ acts on the segment $AS$: moment equilibrium about $S$ gives $M = V_A \cdot 3$ and the vertical force equilibrium gives $T = V_A$, because on the left segment the positive shear acts downward. If the load is to the left of $S$, the segment $SB$ is used, on which only $V_B$ to $5\,\mathrm{m}$ acts from $S$: the moment equals $V_B \cdot 5$, while the positive shear acts upward on the right segment and then $T = -V_B$.

The moment line is a triangle with the $\dfrac{ab}{L} = \dfrac{15}{8}\,\mathrm{m}$ vertex below the section. The shear line is formed by two parallel segments, of $-\dfrac{1}{8}$ slope, with the jump of $1$ to $S$ provided by the **Statically determinate beam influence lines** theorem.

The force $P$ in $\xi = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ is to the right of $S$, so right branches are used. You get $M_S = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, which puts the lower fibers in tension, and $T_S = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$. The check calculates $V_A$ with the rotation around $B$ and writes moment equilibrium of segment $AS$ about $S$: the counterclockwise moment $M_S$ balances the clockwise moment of $V_A$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Get the moment and shear influence lines at section $S$ of the span, at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, with the ordinate at the notable points.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="4.5 0,0,-2,1" quote="A,S,2 m,1.4;S,B,4 m,1.4;B,C,2 m,1.4;A,4.5 0,ξ,0.8" titolo="Overhanging beam showing section $S$ in the span and a unit load at $\xi$"}

$$
\eta_{V_A}(\xi) = \frac{6 - \xi}{6} ,
\qquad
\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \frac{\xi}{6}
\qquad (0 \le \xi \le 8)
$$

$$
\eta_M(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
V_B \cdot 4 = \dfrac{2}{3}\,\xi & \text{if } 0 \le \xi \le 2 \\[8pt]
V_A \cdot 2 = \dfrac{6 - \xi}{3} & \text{if } 2 \le \xi \le 8
\end{cases}
\qquad
\eta_T(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
-V_B = -\dfrac{\xi}{6} & \text{if } 0 \le \xi < 2 \\[8pt]
V_A = \dfrac{6 - \xi}{6} & \text{if } 2 < \xi \le 8
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\eta_M(2) = \frac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m} ,
\quad
\eta_M(6) = 0 ,
\quad
\eta_M(8) = -\frac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{m} ;
\qquad
\eta_T(2^-) = -\frac{1}{3} ,
\quad
\eta_T(2^+) = \frac{2}{3} ,
\quad
\eta_T(8) = -\frac{1}{3}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,(2/3)*s;2,0,6,0,4/3+(-1/3)*s;6,0,8,0,(-1/3)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for the moment at $S$ ($\mathrm{m}$), positive in the span and negative on the overhang"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,(-1/6)*s;2,0,6,0,2/3+(-1/6)*s;6,0,8,0,(-1/6)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for shear at $S$, with a jump of $1$ at $S$ and ordinate $-\dfrac{1}{3}$ at $C$"}

$$
\text{check (load at } C\text{, segment } SC\text{, moments about } S\text{):} \quad
-M + V_B \cdot 4 - 1 \cdot 6 = \frac{2}{3} + \frac{4}{3} \cdot 4 - 6 = \frac{2 + 16 - 18}{3} = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The beam with the overhang is one body, so the reactions have the same expressions as the simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, also valid for $6 \le \xi \le 8$. For the moment and shear at $S$ the segment that does not carry the load is used, as in the previous exercise: the segment $SC$, on which only $V_B$ to $4\,\mathrm{m}$ acts from $S$, if the load is left of $S$; the segment $AS$, on which only $V_A$ to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ acts from $S$, if the load is to the right.

The right branch $\dfrac{6 - \xi}{3}$ also applies to the overhang, where it becomes negative: a load on the overhang lifts the beam into $A$, so $V_A < 0$ and the section $S$ has the upper fibers in tension. For the same reason the shear returns negative on the overhang, with ordinate $-\dfrac{1}{3}$ in $C$. The $S$ shear jump equals $\dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{1}{3} = 1$.

The check is done with the unit load at $C$, calculating $M$ from the balance of the segment $SC$, which has not been used for right branches. On this segment act the internal moment, clockwise moment if positive because it is the right segment, the $V_B = \dfrac{4}{3}$ to $4\,\mathrm{m}$ reaction from $S$ and the load $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $S$. With $M = \eta_M(8) = -\dfrac{2}{3}$ the sum of the moments around $S$ is zero.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The beam shown has a pin at $A$, the roller support at $B$ and two overhangs: $EA$ along $2\,\mathrm{m}$ on the left and $BF$ along $2\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right. Get the moment and shear influence lines in the section $D$ of the left overhang, at $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $E$, and in the section $G$ of the right overhang, at $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $F$.

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;D,1,0;A,2,0;B,7,0;G,8,0;F,9,0" aste="E-F" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="4.5 0,0,-2,1" quote="E,D,1 m,1.4;D,A,1 m,1.4;A,B,5 m,1.4;B,G,1 m,1.4;G,F,1 m,1.4;E,4.5 0,ξ,0.8" titolo="Beam with two overhangs and sections $D$ and $G$ on the overhangs; $\xi$ is measured from $E$"}

$$
\text{section } D\!: \quad
\eta_M(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
-(1 - \xi) & \text{if } 0 \le \xi \le 1 \\
0 & \text{if } 1 \le \xi \le 9
\end{cases}
\qquad
\eta_T(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
-1 & \text{if } 0 \le \xi < 1 \\
0 & \text{if } 1 < \xi \le 9
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\text{section } G\!: \quad
\eta_M(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
0 & \text{if } 0 \le \xi \le 8 \\
-(\xi - 8) & \text{if } 8 \le \xi \le 9
\end{cases}
\qquad
\eta_T(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
0 & \text{if } 0 \le \xi < 8 \\
1 & \text{if } 8 < \xi \le 9
\end{cases}
$$

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;D,1,0;A,2,0;B,7,0;G,8,0;F,9,0" aste="E-F" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,1,0,-1+(1)*s;1,0,2,0,0;2,0,7,0,0;7,0,9,0,0" segni=1 scala=1.5 titolo="Influence line for the moment at $D$ ($\mathrm{m}$), nonzero only on segment $ED$"}

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;D,1,0;A,2,0;B,7,0;G,8,0;F,9,0" aste="E-F" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,1,0,-1;1,0,2,0,0;2,0,7,0,0;7,0,9,0,0" segni=1 scala=1.5 titolo="Influence line for shear at $D$, equal to $-1$ on segment $ED$"}

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;D,1,0;A,2,0;B,7,0;G,8,0;F,9,0" aste="E-F" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,0;2,0,7,0,0;7,0,8,0,0;8,0,9,0,(-1)*s" segni=1 scala=1.5 titolo="Influence line for the moment at $G$ ($\mathrm{m}$), nonzero only on segment $GF$"}

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;D,1,0;A,2,0;B,7,0;G,8,0;F,9,0" aste="E-F" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,0;2,0,7,0,0;7,0,8,0,0;8,0,9,0,1" segni=1 scala=1.5 titolo="Influence line for shear at $G$, equal to $1$ on segment $GF$"}

$$
\text{check (load at } E\text{, segment } DF\text{):} \quad
\begin{aligned}
&V_A = \frac{7 - 0}{5} = \frac{7}{5} , \qquad V_B = \frac{0 - 2}{5} = -\frac{2}{5} \\[6pt]
&\text{vertical translation:} \quad T + V_A + V_B = -1 + \frac{7}{5} - \frac{2}{5} = 0 \\[6pt]
&\text{rotation about } D\text{:} \quad -M + V_A \cdot 1 + V_B \cdot 6 = 1 + \frac{7}{5} - \frac{12}{5} = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For a section on one overhang it is convenient the segment that goes from the section to the free end, because it contains no constraints. For section $D$ this segment is $ED$, on the left. If the load does not stand on $ED$, only the internal actions in $D$ act on the segment, which are therefore zero. If the load is in $\xi < 1$, on the left segment the positive shear acts downward and the positive moment counterclockwise; vertical force equilibrium gives $-1 - T = 0$, i.e. $T = -1$. The downward load to the left of $D$ has about $D$ counterclockwise moment $1 \cdot (1 - \xi)$, so moment equilibrium about $D$ gives $M = -(1 - \xi)$.

For section $G$, the segment $GF$ is used, on the right, and the result of the theorem on **Overhang section** is found: $M = -(\xi - 8)$ and $T = 1$ with the load on $GF$. The two overhangs give moments of the same sign, because in both the load puts the upper fibers in tension, and cuts of opposite sign, because the load stands to the left of the section in the first case and to the right in the second.

Loads between $D$ and $F$ do not affect internal actions in $D$, and those between $E$ and $G$ do not affect $G$. The check considers the load at $E$ and the balance of the other segment, $DF$, on which the reactions $V_A$ and $V_B$ calculated with rotation around $B$ and $A$ act: with $T = -1$ and $M = -1$ the vertical translation and rotation around $D$ are satisfied. On the right segment the positive shear acts upward and the positive moment is clockwise moment.

</div>
