## Exercise 1

For the simply supported beam shown, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, derive by the equilibrium method the influence lines of the reactions $V_A$ and $V_B$. Then calculate the reactions produced by a downward force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ placed $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="1.8 0,0,-1.3,1" quote="A,1.8 0,ξ,1;A,B,5 m,1.6" titolo="Simply supported beam of span $5\,\mathrm{m}$ with a unit load at $\xi$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\
V_A + V_B - 1 = 0 \\
V_B \cdot 5 - 1 \cdot \xi = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \frac{\xi}{5} ,
\qquad
\eta_{V_A}(\xi) = 1 - \frac{\xi}{5} = \frac{5 - \xi}{5}
\qquad (0 \le \xi \le 5)
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,5,0,1+(-1/5)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for $V_A$, given by $\eta_{V_A}(\xi) = \dfrac{5 - \xi}{5}$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,5,0,(1/5)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for $V_B$, given by $\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \dfrac{\xi}{5}$"}

$$
V_A = P\,\eta_{V_A}(2) = 20 \cdot \frac{3}{5} = 12\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_B = P\,\eta_{V_B}(2) = 20 \cdot \frac{2}{5} = 8\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$$
\text{check (moments about } B\text{):} \quad -V_A \cdot 5 + P \cdot 3 = -12 \cdot 5 + 20 \cdot 3 = -60 + 60 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The beam is a rigid body with the pin at $A$ (two scalar constraints) and the roller support at $B$ (one), so it is statically determinate. With the unit load in the generic $\xi$ position the three equilibrium equations are written: horizontal translation, vertical translation and rotation around $A$. The $A$ reference point is chosen because $H_A$ and $V_A$ pass through it, and the moment equation contains the only unknown $V_B$. The load is vertical, so $H_A = 0$ in each position and its influence line is zero.

For **static method** the influence lines are the reactions written as a function of $\xi$. Both are linear: $\eta_{V_A}$ equals $1$ with the load above $A$ and $0$ with the load above $B$, $\eta_{V_B}$ the opposite. They are drawn with the positive ordinate above the axis.

For the force $P$ just multiply $P$ by the ordinate of the line at the point of application, $\xi = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. So **$V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, Upward**, and **$V_B = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, Upward**. Check using the previously unused moment equation about $B$. The upward force $V_A$ a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$ produces a clockwise moment. The downward force $P$ a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$ produces a counterclockwise moment. Their sum is zero.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The beam shown has a pin at $A$, the roller support at $B$ and two $2\,\mathrm{m}$, $CA$ and $BD$ long overhangs. Find the $V_A$ and $V_B$ influence lines, indicating the ordinate in the $C$ and $D$ extremes. Then calculate the reactions produced by an $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward force applied at $D$.

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,2,0;B,8,0;D,10,0" aste="C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="4.5 0,0,-1.6,1" quote="C,A,2 m,1.6;A,B,6 m,1.6;B,D,2 m,1.6;C,4.5 0,ξ,0.9" titolo="Beam with two overhangs and a unit load at coordinate $\xi$ measured from $C$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
V_A + V_B - 1 = 0 \\
V_B \cdot 6 - 1 \cdot (\xi - 2) = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \frac{\xi - 2}{6} ,
\qquad
\eta_{V_A}(\xi) = \frac{8 - \xi}{6}
\qquad (0 \le \xi \le 10)
$$

$$
\eta_{V_A}(0) = \frac{8}{6} = \frac{4}{3} ,
\quad
\eta_{V_A}(10) = -\frac{2}{6} = -\frac{1}{3} ,
\qquad
\eta_{V_B}(0) = -\frac{1}{3} ,
\quad
\eta_{V_B}(10) = \frac{8}{6} = \frac{4}{3}
$$

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,2,0;B,8,0;D,10,0" aste="C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,4/3+(-1/6)*s;2,0,8,0,1+(-1/6)*s;8,0,10,0,(-1/6)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for $V_A$, ranging from $\dfrac{4}{3}$ at $C$ to $-\dfrac{1}{3}$ at $D$"}

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,2,0;B,8,0;D,10,0" aste="C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,-1/3+(1/6)*s;2,0,8,0,(1/6)*s;8,0,10,0,1+(1/6)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for $V_B$, ranging from $-\dfrac{1}{3}$ at $C$ to $\dfrac{4}{3}$ at $D$"}

$$
V_A = F\,\eta_{V_A}(10) = 12 \cdot \left(-\frac{1}{3}\right) = -4\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_B = F\,\eta_{V_B}(10) = 12 \cdot \frac{4}{3} = 16\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$$
\text{check (moments about } D\text{):} \quad -V_A \cdot 8 - V_B \cdot 2 = -(-4) \cdot 8 - 16 \cdot 2 = 32 - 32 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The coordinate $\xi$ is measured from end $C$, so the hinge $A$ is in $\xi = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the roller support at $B$ at $\xi = 8\,\mathrm{m}$. Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives only $V_B$; the moment arm of the load about $A$ is $\xi - 2$, which is negative when the load is on the $CA$ overhang, and the sign alone gives the right direction of the moment. The vertical force equilibrium then gives $V_A$.

The beam with the overhangs is one rigid body, therefore, for the **Statically determinate beam influence lines** theorem, the expressions are valid over the entire length from $C$ to $D$: the two lines are straight that extend beyond the supports. On the overhang opposed to a support the influence lines of his reaction becomes negative. With the load at $D$ the reaction at $A$ is directed downward, because the beam tends to rotate around $B$ and rise up in $A$.

For $F$ in $D$ you multiply $F$ by the ordinate ones in $\xi = 10\,\mathrm{m}$. You get **$V_A = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ down**, and **$V_B = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$, Upward**. In the check, rotation around $D$, the force $F$ does not appear because it is applied at $D$; $V_A$ and $V_B$ have $8\,\mathrm{m}$ and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ arms, and an upward force to the left of the reference point has clockwise moment.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The cantilever shown, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at $A$ and free in $B$. Find the influence lines of vertical reaction $V_A$ and reaction moment $M_A$. Then calculate the reactions produced by the $P_1 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $1\,\mathrm{m}$ forces from $A$ and $P_2 = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $B$, both downward.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="1.2 0,0,-0.8,1" quote="A,1.2 0,ξ,0.6;A,B,3 m,1" titolo="Cantilever of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ with a unit load at $\xi$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\
V_A - 1 = 0 \\
M_A - 1 \cdot \xi = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\eta_{V_A}(\xi) = 1 ,
\qquad
\eta_{M_A}(\xi) = \xi
\qquad (0 \le \xi \le 3)
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,3,0,1" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for $V_A$, constant and equal to $1$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,3,0,(1)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for $M_A$ ($\mathrm{m}$), given by $\eta_{M_A}(\xi) = \xi$"}

$$
V_A = P_1 \cdot 1 + P_2 \cdot 1 = 8 + 5 = 13\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_A = P_1 \cdot 1 + P_2 \cdot 3 = 8 + 15 = 23\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

$$
\text{check (moments about } B\text{):} \quad M_A - V_A \cdot 3 + P_1 \cdot 2 = 23 - 39 + 16 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Fixed support exhibits three reactions, $H_A$, $V_A$ and moment $M_A$, taken positive counterclockwise; the cantilever is statically determinate. With the unit load at $\xi$ the vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = 1$ and moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $M_A = \xi$, because the downward load to the right of $A$ has clockwise moment $-1 \cdot \xi$ and the reaction moment must balance it.

$V_A$'s influence line is constant: the vertical reaction of fixed support carries all the load, in any position. The $M_A$'s grows linearly from fixed support to the free end and is measured in meters, because it is a moment produced by a unit force.

The two forces add the products of the intensities for the ordinate at the application points, $\xi = 1\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\xi = 3\,\mathrm{m}$. So **$V_A = 13\,\mathrm{kN}$, Upward**, and **$M_A = 23\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise**. In the check, rotation around the free end $B$, $P_2$ does not appear, $V_A$ upward to $3\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$ has clockwise moment and $P_1$ downward to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$ has counterclockwise moment.

</div>
