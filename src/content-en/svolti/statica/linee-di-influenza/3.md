## Exercise 1

The simply supported beam shown, of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, carries downward forces $P_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, $P_2 = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $P_3 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at distances $1\,\mathrm{m}$, $3\,\mathrm{m}$, and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from pin $A$. Use the influence lines to calculate reactions $V_A$ and $V_B$ and the bending moment at section $S$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="1 0,0,-1.2,P_1;3 0,0,-1.6,P_2;4 0,0,-1.2,P_3" quote="A,1 0,1 m,0.8;1 0,S,1 m,0.8;S,3 0,1 m,0.8;3 0,4 0,1 m,0.8;4 0,B,2 m,0.8" titolo="Simply supported beam under three forces, $P_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, $P_2 = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $P_3 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

$$
\eta_{V_A}(\xi) = \frac{6 - \xi}{6} ,
\qquad
\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \frac{\xi}{6} ,
\qquad
\eta_M(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{4}{6}\,\xi & \text{if } 0 \le \xi \le 2 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{2}{6}\,(6 - \xi) & \text{if } 2 \le \xi \le 6
\end{cases}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,(2/3)*s;2,0,6,0,4/3+(-1/3)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for the moment at $S$ ($\mathrm{m}$); ordinates $\dfrac{2}{3}$, $1$, and $\dfrac{2}{3}$ under the three forces"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_A &= 12 \cdot \frac{5}{6} + 18 \cdot \frac{3}{6} + 6 \cdot \frac{2}{6} = 10 + 9 + 2 = 21\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_B &= 12 \cdot \frac{1}{6} + 18 \cdot \frac{3}{6} + 6 \cdot \frac{4}{6} = 2 + 9 + 4 = 15\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
M_S &= 12 \cdot \frac{2}{3} + 18 \cdot 1 + 6 \cdot \frac{2}{3} = 8 + 18 + 4 = 30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\text{check (moments about } A\text{):} \quad V_B \cdot 6 - 12 \cdot 1 - 18 \cdot 3 - 6 \cdot 4 = 90 - 12 - 54 - 24 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

By the theorem on **response to a load from its influence line**, for several point loads we sum the products of each force and the influence-line ordinate at its point of application. The reaction influence lines are those of a simply supported beam; the influence line for the moment at $S$ is triangular, with a peak ordinate of $\dfrac{ab}{L} = \dfrac{2 \cdot 4}{6} = \dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$. The force $P_1$ stands to the left of $S$, so the left $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\xi$ branch is used for it; $P_2$ and $P_3$ forces are on the right, and the right branch $\dfrac{6 - \xi}{3}$ is used for them.

The results are **$V_A = 21\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$, both upward**, and $M_S = 30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, which puts the lower fibers in tension. The sum of reactions is $36\,\mathrm{kN}$, equal to the sum of forces. The check uses moment equilibrium about $A$ with the newly found reactions. The moment is also controlled with the segment $AS$, on which $V_A$ and $P_1$: $M_S = 21 \cdot 2 - 12 \cdot 1 = 30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ act.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The simply supported beam shown, of span $8\,\mathrm{m}$, carries a uniform $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load over all the span and a force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from the hinge $A$. Calculate with the influence lines the moment and shear at section $S$ $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.8,0.8,q" forze="5 0.8,0,-1.6,P" quote="A,S,2 m,0.9;S,5 0,3 m,0.9;5 0,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam under uniform load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

$$
\eta_M(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{3}{4}\,\xi & \text{if } 0 \le \xi \le 2 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{1}{4}\,(8 - \xi) & \text{if } 2 \le \xi \le 8
\end{cases}
\qquad
\eta_T(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac{\xi}{8} & \text{if } 0 \le \xi < 2 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{8 - \xi}{8} & \text{if } 2 < \xi \le 8
\end{cases}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,(3/4)*s;2,0,8,0,3/2+(-1/4)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for the moment at $S$ ($\mathrm{m}$), with area $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 8 \cdot \dfrac{3}{2} = 6\,\mathrm{m^2}$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,(-1/8)*s;2,0,8,0,3/4+(-1/8)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for shear at $S$, with area $-\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{9}{4} = 2\,\mathrm{m}$"}

$$
\mathcal{A}_M = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 8 \cdot \frac{3}{2} = 6\,\mathrm{m^2} ,
\qquad
\eta_M(5) = \frac{8 - 5}{4} = \frac{3}{4}\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
M_S = q\,\mathcal{A}_M + P\,\eta_M(5) = 5 \cdot 6 + 10 \cdot \frac{3}{4} = 30 + 7.5 = 37.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\mathcal{A}_T = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot \left(-\frac{1}{4}\right) + \frac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot \frac{3}{4} = -\frac{1}{4} + \frac{9}{4} = 2\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
\eta_T(5) = \frac{3}{8}
$$
$$
T_S = q\,\mathcal{A}_T + P\,\eta_T(5) = 5 \cdot 2 + 10 \cdot \frac{3}{8} = 10 + 3.75 = 13.75\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$$
\text{check (segment } AS\text{):} \quad
\begin{aligned}
&V_A = \frac{5 \cdot 8 \cdot 4 + 10 \cdot 3}{8} = 23.75\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
&\text{vertical translation:} \quad V_A - 5 \cdot 2 - T_S = 23.75 - 10 - 13.75 = 0 \\[6pt]
&\text{rotation about } S\text{:} \quad M_S - V_A \cdot 2 + 5 \cdot 2 \cdot 1 = 37.5 - 47.5 + 10 = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The uniform load is with the area of the influence lines on the loaded segment, here the whole beam; the concentrated force with the ordinate in its point. The moment line is a base triangle $8\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $\dfrac{2 \cdot 6}{8} = \dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$. The shear line has a negative triangle of base $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and a positive triangle of base $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $\dfrac{3}{4}$; the area with a sign is the sum of the two. Areas are measured in $\mathrm{m^2}$ for the moment being and in $\mathrm{m}$ for shear, so multiplied by $q$ in $\mathrm{kN/m}$ give $\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $\mathrm{kN}$.

You get $M_S = 37.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, which puts the lower fibers in tension, and $T_S = 13.75\,\mathrm{kN}$. The check calculates $V_A$ with the rotation around $B$, in which the uniform load has resulted $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$ and the force is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$; then writes the balance of the segment $AS$, on which $V_A$ acts, the load $q$ on the first $2\,\mathrm{m}$ (the resultant $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $S$) and the internal actions, with the positive shear down and the positive counterclockwise moment.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. It carries a force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and a uniform $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load on the overhang. Calculate with the $V_A$, $V_B$ and the bending moment lines at section $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="2 0,0,-1.5,P" carichi="B,C,0.7,0.7,q" quote="A,2 0,2 m,0.9;2 0,B,3 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam with $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in the span and $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the overhang"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,5,0,1+(-1/5)*s;5,0,7,0,(-1/5)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for $V_A$, given by $\eta_{V_A}(\xi) = \dfrac{5 - \xi}{5}$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,5,0,(1/5)*s;5,0,7,0,1+(1/5)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for $V_B$, given by $\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \dfrac{\xi}{5}$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,5,0,0;5,0,7,0,(-1)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for the moment at $B$ ($\mathrm{m}$), zero over the span"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{A}_{V_A}(BC) &= \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot \left(-\frac{2}{5}\right) = -\frac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{m} ,
&\qquad
V_A &= 10 \cdot \frac{3}{5} + 4 \cdot \left(-\frac{2}{5}\right) = 6 - 1.6 = 4.4\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
\mathcal{A}_{V_B}(BC) &= \frac{1 + \frac{7}{5}}{2} \cdot 2 = \frac{12}{5}\,\mathrm{m} ,
&\qquad
V_B &= 10 \cdot \frac{2}{5} + 4 \cdot \frac{12}{5} = 4 + 9.6 = 13.6\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
\mathcal{A}_{M_B}(BC) &= \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot (-2) = -2\,\mathrm{m^2} ,
&\qquad
M_B &= 10 \cdot 0 + 4 \cdot (-2) = -8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\text{check (vertical force balance):} \quad V_A + V_B - P - q \cdot 2 = 4.4 + 13.6 - 10 - 8 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The three lines are those of the beam with overhang: the lines of reactions extend in a straight line on the overhang, while the line of the moment at $B$ is zero on the span and equals $-(\xi - 5)$ on the overhang, by the theorem on **Overhang section**. The load $q$ occupies the section $BC$, so for each size the area of the line is calculated on the overhang alone: a triangle for $V_A$ and for $M_B$, a trapezoid for $V_B$. The force $P$ multiplies by the ordinate at $\xi = 2\,\mathrm{m}$.

It results in **$V_A = 4.4\,\mathrm{kN}$, Upward**, **$V_B = 13.6\,\mathrm{kN}$, Upward**, and $M_B = -8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, which puts the upper fibers in tension. The load on the overhang reduces $V_A$ and increases $V_B$, and the force in span does not give moment at $B$. The value of $M_B$ coincides with the moment of the resultant load on the overhang, $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$. The check uses vertical translation, which does not enter the calculation with the influence lines.

</div>

## Exercise 4

The simply supported beam shown has span $6\,\mathrm{m}$. A downward force $P = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from pin $A$, and a counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Use the influence lines to calculate reactions $V_A$ and $V_B$ and the bending moment at section $S$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="1 0,0,-1.5,P" coppie="4 0,1,ℳ" quote="A,1 0,1 m,0.9;1 0,S,1 m,0.9;S,4 0,2 m,0.9;4 0,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam with force $P = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ and counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$"}

$$
\eta_{V_A}' = -\frac{1}{6} ,
\qquad
\eta_{V_B}' = \frac{1}{6} ,
\qquad
\eta_M'(4) = -\frac{1}{3}
\qquad (\text{slopes at the applied couple})
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_A &= P\,\eta_{V_A}(1) - \mathcal{M}\,\eta_{V_A}'(4) = 9 \cdot \frac{5}{6} - 12 \cdot \left(-\frac{1}{6}\right) = 7.5 + 2 = 9.5\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_B &= P\,\eta_{V_B}(1) - \mathcal{M}\,\eta_{V_B}'(4) = 9 \cdot \frac{1}{6} - 12 \cdot \frac{1}{6} = 1.5 - 2 = -0.5\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
M_S &= P\,\eta_M(1) - \mathcal{M}\,\eta_M'(4) = 9 \cdot \frac{2}{3} - 12 \cdot \left(-\frac{1}{3}\right) = 6 + 4 = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\text{check (moments about } A\text{):} \quad V_B \cdot 6 - P \cdot 1 + \mathcal{M} = -0.5 \cdot 6 - 9 + 12 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Force is treated as in previous exercises. For the couple the theorem on **response to a load from its influence line** gives the contribution $-\mathcal{M}\,\eta_X'(\xi_0)$, where $\eta_X'$ is the slope of the line at the point where the couple is applied. Reaction lines have constant slope, $-\dfrac{1}{6}$ and $\dfrac{1}{6}$. The couple is to the right of $S$, on the $\dfrac{6 - \xi}{3}$ branch of the current line, which has $-\dfrac{1}{3}$ slope.

You get **$V_A = 9.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, Upward**, **$V_B = -0.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $0.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ down**, and $M_S = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, which puts the lower fibers in tension. The counterclockwise couple alone would lift the beam up into $B$ and push it down into $A$; the force $P$, close to $A$, almost completely compensates for the effect in $B$. In check the couple enters with its value, because the moment of a couple does not depend on the reference point. The moment at $S$ is controlled with the segment $AS$: $M_S = 9.5 \cdot 2 - 9 \cdot 1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

</div>
