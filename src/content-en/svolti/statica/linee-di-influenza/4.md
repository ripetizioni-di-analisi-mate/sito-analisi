## Exercise 1

The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Trace with Müller–Breslau theorem the influence lines of $V_B$ and $V_A$, and check with equilibrium the ordinate at $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="3 0,0,-1.5,1" quote="A,B,4 m,1.2;B,C,2 m,1.2;A,3 0,ξ,0.7" titolo="Overhanging beam with a unit load at $\xi$"}

$$
V_B\!: \quad \text{remove the roller at } B ,
\qquad
v(x) = \varphi\,x ,
\qquad
v(4) = 1 \;\Longrightarrow\; \varphi = \frac{1}{4}
$$
$$
\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \frac{\xi}{4} ,
\qquad
\eta_{V_B}(4) = 1 ,
\qquad
\eta_{V_B}(6) = \frac{3}{2}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera" tratteggi="0,0 6,1.5" frecce="4,0,4,1,1" punti="0,0,C_1,centro" titolo="Mechanism for $V_B$ in which the beam rotates about $A$ with $v(B) = 1$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,4,0,(1/4)*s;4,0,6,0,1+(1/4)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for $V_B$ with ordinate $\dfrac{3}{2}$ at $C$"}

$$
V_A\!: \quad \text{the pin support at } A \text{ becomes a roller against a vertical surface} ,
\qquad
v(x) = \varphi\,(x - 4) ,
\qquad
v(0) = 1 \;\Longrightarrow\; \varphi = -\frac{1}{4}
$$
$$
\eta_{V_A}(\xi) = -\frac{\xi - 4}{4} = \frac{4 - \xi}{4} ,
\qquad
\eta_{V_A}(0) = 1 ,
\qquad
\eta_{V_A}(6) = -\frac{1}{2}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,carrello,180;B,carrello" tratteggi="0,1 6,-0.5" frecce="0,0,0,1,1" punti="4,0,C_1,centro" titolo="Mechanism for $V_A$ in which the beam rotates about $B$ with $v(A) = 1$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,4,0,1+(-1/4)*s;4,0,6,0,(-1/4)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for $V_A$ with ordinate $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ at $C$"}

$$
\text{check (load at } C\text{):} \quad
\begin{cases}
\text{rotation about } A\!: & V_B \cdot 4 - 1 \cdot 6 = \dfrac{3}{2} \cdot 4 - 6 = 0 \\[8pt]
\text{rotation about } B\!: & -V_A \cdot 4 - 1 \cdot 2 = -\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right) \cdot 4 - 2 = 0
\end{cases}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For **Müller–Breslau theorem** the influence lines of a reaction is the diagram of the vertical displacements of the mechanism that is obtained by removing the corresponding constraint, with unit displacement in the positive direction of the reaction. Removing the roller support at $B$ remains the only pin at $A$: the beam becomes a one-degree mechanism of freedom that rotates about $A$, the center of absolute rotation $C_1$. The displacement of a point is $\varphi\,x$, and requiring an upward displacement $v(B) = 1$ determines the counterclockwise rotation $\varphi = \dfrac{1}{4}$.

For $V_A$ the vertical component is removed from the hinge. In $A$ horizontal displacement is prevented, i.e. a roller support on a vertical plane, and the center of rotation lies on the horizontal line for $A$; $B$ roller support puts center on vertical for $B$. The two lines meet in $B$, so the beam rotates around $B$, and the $v(A) = 1$ condition gives $\varphi = -\dfrac{1}{4}$, clockwise. The overhang, to the right of the center, lowers.

The two lines are read directly from the mechanisms: $\eta_{V_B} = \dfrac{\xi}{4}$ and $\eta_{V_A} = \dfrac{4 - \xi}{4}$. The check writes the balance to the rotation with the unit load at $C$: the ordinate $\dfrac{3}{2}$ and $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ satisfy it, and the static method then gives the same result.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The beam shown, of length $10\,\mathrm{m}$, has $A$ to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ roller support from end $E$ and $B$ to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ hinge from end $F$. Draw with Müller–Breslau theorem the influence line for the bending moment at section $S$ $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, with the ordinate at $E$, $S$ and $F$.

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;A,2,0;S,4,0;B,8,0;F,10,0" aste="E-F" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera" forze="6 0,0,-2,1" quote="E,A,2 m,1.2;A,S,2 m,1.2;S,B,4 m,1.2;B,F,2 m,1.2;E,6 0,ξ,0.7" titolo="Beam with two overhangs and section $S$; $\xi$ is measured from $E$"}

$$
v_1(x) = \varphi_1\,(x - 2) ,
\qquad
v_2(x) = \varphi_2\,(x - 8)
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
\varphi_1 \cdot 2 = \varphi_2 \cdot (-4) \\
\varphi_1 - \varphi_2 = 1
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\varphi_1 = \frac{2}{3} ,
\quad
\varphi_2 = -\frac{1}{3}
$$
$$
\eta_M(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{2}{3}\,(\xi - 2) & \text{if } 0 \le \xi \le 4 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{1}{3}\,(\xi - 8) & \text{if } 4 \le \xi \le 10
\end{cases}
\qquad
\eta_M(0) = -\frac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m} ,
\quad
\eta_M(4) = \frac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m} ,
\quad
\eta_M(10) = -\frac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;A,2,0;S,4,0;B,8,0;F,10,0" aste="E-S;S-F" cerniere="S" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera" tratteggi="0,-1.3333 4,1.3333 10,-0.6667" punti="4,1.3333,S',pieno;2,0,C_1,centro;8,0,C_2,centro" titolo="Mechanism with a hinge at $S$ in which $ES$ rotates about $A$ and $SF$ about $B$, with $\varphi_1 - \varphi_2 = 1$"}

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;A,2,0;S,4,0;B,8,0;F,10,0" aste="E-F" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera" diagramma="0,0,2,0,-4/3+(2/3)*s;2,0,4,0,(2/3)*s;4,0,8,0,4/3+(-1/3)*s;8,0,10,0,(-1/3)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for the moment at $S$ ($\mathrm{m}$)"}

$$
\text{check (load at } E\text{, segment } SF\text{):} \quad
V_B = \frac{0 - 2}{6} = -\frac{1}{3} ,
\qquad
-M + V_B \cdot 4 = \frac{4}{3} - \frac{4}{3} = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For the moment at $S$ you insert a pin at $S$. The segment $ES$ has only one constraint, roller support at $A$, and the segment $SF$ has a pin at $B$; with the internal hinge you get a tie of two bodies with a degree of freedom. The segment $SF$ rotates about $B$. The segment $ES$ has the center on the vertical through $A$ and, by the kinematic ties theorem, aligned with $B$ and the hinge $S$, which stand on the axis: the center $C_1$ is therefore $A$.

The two conditions to be met together are the congruence in $S$, where the two segment must have the same displacement, and the relative unit rotation $\varphi_1 - \varphi_2 = 1$, which is the conjugate displacement of the moment. The left segment rotates counterclockwise and the right segment clockwise, so the $S$ goes up and the extremes of the overhangs go down.

For **Müller–Breslau theorem**, the displacement diagram is the influence lines. The vertex equals $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$, as $\dfrac{ab}{L}$ of the span with $a = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, $b = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, $L = 6\,\mathrm{m}$; the overhangs give negative ordinate, **$-\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ in $E$** and **$-\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ in $F$**. The check uses the static method with the load at $E$: on the segment $SF$ only $V_B$ acts, calculated with the rotation around $A$, and the rotation around $S$ is satisfied with $M = -\dfrac{4}{3}$, the clockwise internal moment if positive on the right segment.

</div>

## Exercise 3

For the beam shown, the same as the previous exercise, with the roller support at $A$, the pin at $B$ and the section $S$ to $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from end $E$, trace with Müller–Breslau theorem the influence line for the shear at $S$.

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;A,2,0;S,4,0;B,8,0;F,10,0" aste="E-F" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera" forze="6 0,0,-2,1" quote="E,A,2 m,1.2;A,S,2 m,1.2;S,B,4 m,1.2;B,F,2 m,1.2;E,6 0,ξ,0.7" titolo="Beam with two overhangs showing shear section $S$"}

$$
v_1(x) = \varphi\,(x - 2) ,
\qquad
v_2(x) = \varphi\,(x - 8) ,
\qquad
v_2(4) - v_1(4) = -4\,\varphi - 2\,\varphi = -6\,\varphi = 1
\;\Longrightarrow\;
\varphi = -\frac{1}{6}
$$
$$
\eta_T(\xi) =
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac{\xi - 2}{6} & \text{if } 0 \le \xi < 4 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{\xi - 8}{6} & \text{if } 4 < \xi \le 10
\end{cases}
\qquad
\eta_T(0) = \frac{1}{3} ,
\quad
\eta_T(4^-) = -\frac{1}{3} ,
\quad
\eta_T(4^+) = \frac{2}{3} ,
\quad
\eta_T(10) = -\frac{1}{3}
$$

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;A,2,0;S,4,0;B,8,0;F,10,0" aste="E-S;S-F" interni="S,doppio-pendolo,0" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera" tratteggi="0,0.3333 4,-0.3333;4,0.6667 10,-0.3333" punti="2,0,C_1,centro;8,0,C_2,centro" titolo="Mechanism with a parallel-link connection at $S$ in which the two segments rotate through the same angle and $v_d - v_s = 1$"}

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;A,2,0;S,4,0;B,8,0;F,10,0" aste="E-F" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera" diagramma="0,0,2,0,1/3+(-1/6)*s;2,0,4,0,(-1/6)*s;4,0,8,0,2/3+(-1/6)*s;8,0,10,0,(-1/6)*s" segni=1 titolo="Influence line for shear at $S$, consisting of two parallel segments with a jump of $1$ at $S$"}

$$
\text{check (load at } F\text{, segment } ES\text{):} \quad
V_A = \frac{8 - 10}{6} = -\frac{1}{3} ,
\qquad
V_A - T = -\frac{1}{3} - \left(-\frac{1}{3}\right) = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For shear, a parallel-link support is inserted in $S$ with pin-ended links parallel to the axis, which leaves the two segment with relative vertical sliding and prevents relative rotation. The two segment therefore have the same rotation $\varphi$, and each rotates around its own constraint: $ES$ around $A$, $SF$ around $B$. The conjugate displacement of the shear is $v_d - v_s$, and the $v_d - v_s = 1$ condition gives $\varphi = -\dfrac{1}{6}$, clockwise.

The influence line is formed by two parallel segments, which cancel out below $A$ and below $B$ and present in $S$ the jump of $1$, from $-\dfrac{1}{3}$ to $\dfrac{2}{3}$. On the two overhangs the ordinate are $\dfrac{1}{3}$ in $E$ and $-\dfrac{1}{3}$ in $F$: a load at $E$ produces in $S$ a positive shear, a load at $F$ a negative shear.

The check uses the static method with the load at $F$. On the segment $ES$ only $V_A$ acts, calculated with rotation around $B$, and shear at $S$, downward if positive; the vertical translation of the segment is satisfied with $T = \eta_T(10) = -\dfrac{1}{3}$.

</div>
