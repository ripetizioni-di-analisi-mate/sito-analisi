## Exercise 1

Beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$ on a surface inclined at angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal, with $\tan\alpha = \dfrac{3}{4}$. The roller reaction acts upward and to the left. A vertical force $F = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at midspan. Find the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,306.87" forze="3 0,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,3 m" titolo="Beam with a roller on an inclined surface, $\tan\alpha = \dfrac{3}{4}$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" reazioni="A,1.3,0,H_A;A,0,1.3,V_A;B,-0.9,1.2,R_B" forze="3 0,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,3 m" titolo="Free-body diagram showing $R_B$ normal to the roller's support surface"}

$$
\sin\alpha = \frac{3}{5} = 0.6,
\qquad
\cos\alpha = \frac{4}{5} = 0.8
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A - R_B\sin\alpha = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + R_B\cos\alpha - F = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,R_B\cos\alpha - 3\,F = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
R_B = \frac{3 \cdot 40}{6 \cdot 0.8}\,\mathrm{kN} = 25\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
H_A = 0.6 \cdot 25 = 15\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 40 - 0.8 \cdot 25 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
-6\,V_A + 3\,F = -120 + 120 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The roller support only prevents normal shift to the support surface, so its $R_B$ reaction is normal to the plane and forms the angle $\alpha$ with the vertical. From the values of $\tan\alpha$ are obtained $\sin\alpha$ and $\cos\alpha$ with the right triangle of legs $3$ and $4$ and hypotenuse $5$. The reaction has horizontal component $R_B\sin\alpha$ to the left and vertical component $R_B\cos\alpha$ upward.

The first equation is horizontal force balance, the second vertical force balance, the third moment equilibrium about $A$. About $A$ the horizontal component of $R_B$ has line of action along the axis of the beam and null moment, so the rotation contains only the vertical component, with a moment arm of $6\,\mathrm{m}$.

It results in $R_B = 25\,\mathrm{kN}$, directed as assumed. The hinge exerts $H_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the right** and $V_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ upwards. The load is vertical, but the inclination of the support surface at $B$ produces a horizontal reaction at $A$. The vertical components are equal, $20\,\mathrm{kN}$, because the force is in the middle. In the check $B$, the reaction $R_B$ and the reaction $H_A$ have zero moment, because their lines of action pass through $B$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The beam $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and is supported at $B$ by an inclined two-force link of $45^\circ$, whose pin-ended link ranges from $B$ down and to the left. A uniform $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load acts on the entire beam. Calculate support reactions and tell if the two-force link is compressed or stretched.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,pendolo,225" carichi="A,B,0.4,0.4,q" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Beam with a pin and a two-force link inclined at $45^\circ$"}

$$
Q = q \cdot 4 = 20\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
x_Q = 2\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + \dfrac{R_B}{\sqrt{2}} = 0 \\[8pt]
V_A + \dfrac{R_B}{\sqrt{2}} - Q = 0 \\[8pt]
4 \cdot \dfrac{R_B}{\sqrt{2}} - 2\,Q = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
\frac{R_B}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{2 \cdot 20}{4}\,\mathrm{kN} = 10\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
R_B = 10\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 14.14\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
H_A = -10\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
-4\,V_A + 2\,Q = -40 + 40 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two-force link is a pin-ended link hinged to the ends and transmits a direct force along its axis. The reaction $R_B$ is assumed to be positive when directed from the ground towards the beam, i.e. upwards and rightwards. In this direction its components are both $\dfrac{R_B}{\sqrt{2}}$, because $\cos 45^\circ = \sin 45^\circ = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$.

Equations are horizontal force balance, vertical force equilibrium and moment equilibrium about $A$, in which only the vertical component of $R_B$ appears. $R_B = 10\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN}$ is positive, so the pin-ended link pushes the beam up and right and the two-force link is **compressed**.

The horizontal component of $R_B$ pushes the beam to the right and the hinge balances it with $H_A = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**; $V_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ is upward. The vertical load is divided in half between the two constraints, because it is symmetrical with respect to the middle.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The beam $AB$ shown, of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, has at $A$ a roller support that moves along an inclined plane with $\tan\alpha = \dfrac{3}{4}$, whose reaction is directed upwards and to the right, and at $B$ a hinge. Loads are: an $F = 50\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $3\,\mathrm{m}$ force from $A$, directed downwards and rightwards, with horizontal $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ component and vertical $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ component; a uniform $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load on the last $4\,\mathrm{m}$; an $\mathcal{M} = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ to $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ clockwise couple from $A$. Calculate support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,233.13;B,cerniera" forze="3 0,1.2,-1.6,F" carichi="4 0,B,0.8,0.8,q" coppie="1.5 0,-1,ℳ" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,4 0,1 m;4 0,B,4 m" titolo="Beam with a roller on an incline at $A$ under an inclined force, a uniform load, and a couple"}

$$
Q = q \cdot 4 = 20\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
x_Q = 6\,\mathrm{m} \text{ from } A
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
0.6\,R_A + 30 + H_B = 0 \\[4pt]
0.8\,R_A - 40 - Q + V_B = 0 \\[4pt]
-8 \cdot 0.8\,R_A + 5 \cdot 40 + 2\,Q - \mathcal{M} = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
R_A = \frac{200 + 40 - 16}{6.4}\,\mathrm{kN} = \frac{224}{6.4}\,\mathrm{kN} = 35\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_B = 40 + 20 - 28 = 32\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
H_B = -21 - 30 = -51\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $A$:
$$
8\,V_B - 3 \cdot 40 - 6\,Q - \mathcal{M} = 256 - 120 - 120 - 16 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The support surface at $A$ has $\sin\alpha = 0.6$ and $\cos\alpha = 0.8$, so the reaction $R_A$, normal on the plane, has $0.6\,R_A$ components to the right and $0.8\,R_A$ components upward. The hinge is at $B$, so the reference point of the rotation is $B$, and in the third equation only the vertical component of $R_A$ appears, with a moment arm of $8\,\mathrm{m}$ and a clockwise moment.

Equations are horizontal force equilibrium, vertical force equilibrium, and moment equilibrium about $B$. About $B$ the vertical component of $F$ has $5\,\mathrm{m}$ arm and the resultant $Q$ has $2\,\mathrm{m}$ arm; both stand to the left of $B$ and are directed downwards, so they have counterclockwise moment. The horizontal component of $F$ has line of action along the axis of the beam and null moment. The couple is clockwise and enters with the minus sign.

It results in $R_A = 35\,\mathrm{kN}$, directed as assumed; $V_B = 32\,\mathrm{kN}$ upwards; $H_B = -51\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $51\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**, because the hinge balances both the horizontal component of $F$ and that of $R_A$, both directed to the right. In the check with respect to $A$ the arms are $8\,\mathrm{m}$ for $V_B$, $3\,\mathrm{m}$ for the vertical component of $F$ and $6\,\mathrm{m}$ for $Q$.

</div>
