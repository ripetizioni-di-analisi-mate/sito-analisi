## Exercise 1

Find the support reactions of simply supported beam $AB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, with a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A vertical force $F = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="2 0,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,2 0,2 m;2 0,B,4 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under force $F = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" reazioni="A,1.3,0,H_A;A,0,1.3,V_A;B,0,1.3,V_B" forze="2 0,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,2 0,2 m;2 0,B,4 m" titolo="Free-body diagram"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - F = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_B - 2\,F = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
V_B = \frac{2\,F}{6} = \frac{2 \cdot 30}{6}\,\mathrm{kN} = 10\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = F - V_B = 30 - 10 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check by taking moments about $B$.
$$
-6\,V_A + 4\,F = -6 \cdot 20 + 4 \cdot 30 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The beam is one rigid body with three scalar restraints. The pin at $A$ exerts reaction components $H_A$ and $V_A$, and the roller at $B$ exerts only the vertical reaction $V_B$. The **free-body diagram** shows all three reactions in the positive coordinate directions.

The first equation balances horizontal forces, the second balances vertical forces, and the third takes moments about $A$. Choose $A$ as the reference point because the lines of action of $H_A$ and $V_A$ pass through $A$. Only the reaction $V_B$ enters the moment equation, with moment arm $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and a counterclockwise moment. Force $F$ has moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and produces a clockwise moment, so its term is negative.

The load is vertical, so $H_A = 0$. Both reactions, $V_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$, act **upward**. The support closer to the load carries the larger share. In the check, $V_A$ has moment arm $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and produces a clockwise moment about $B$, while $F$ has moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and produces a counterclockwise moment. Their moments sum to zero.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The beam shown has length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, a pin at $A$, and a roller at $B$. A uniform load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the entire span, and a vertical force $F = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Find the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.8,0.8,q" forze="6 0,0,-2,F" quote="A,6 0,6 m;6 0,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under uniform load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and force $F = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

$$
Q = q \cdot 8 = 5 \cdot 8 = 40\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
x_Q = 4\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - Q - F = 0 \\[4pt]
8\,V_B - 4\,Q - 6\,F = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
V_B = \frac{4\,Q + 6\,F}{8} = \frac{160 + 120}{8}\,\mathrm{kN} = 35\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 40 + 20 - 35 = 25\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
-8\,V_A + 4\,Q + 2\,F = -200 + 160 + 40 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The uniform load replaces with its **resultant**, equal to the area of the load diagram, $Q = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$, applied at the midpoint of the beam. The substitution is valid because the written equations are those of the entire beam, on which the whole load acts.

The three equations are horizontal force equilibrium, vertical force equilibrium and moment equilibrium about $A$. About $A$ the resultant $Q$ has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $F$ force has a moment arm of $6\,\mathrm{m}$; both have clockwise moment. Rotation gives $V_B = 35\,\mathrm{kN}$, then vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = 25\,\mathrm{kN}$, both **upward**; $H_A$ is zero, because there are no horizontal loads.

The reaction at $B$ is greater than at $A$ because the force $F$ is closer to $B$. In the check $B$, the arms are $8\,\mathrm{m}$ for $V_A$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ for $Q$ and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ for $F$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Beam $AB$ of length $10\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the first $6\,\mathrm{m}$ of the span. A force $F = 25\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward and to the right a distance $8\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, with horizontal component $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and vertical component $15\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,10,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,6 0,0.8,0.8,q" forze="8 0,2,-1.5,F" quote="A,6 0,6 m;6 0,8 0,2 m;8 0,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with a uniform load on $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and inclined force $F = 25\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,10,0" aste="A-B" reazioni="A,2,0,H_A;A,0,2,V_A;B,0,2,V_B" forze="3 0,0,-2.4,Q;8 0,2,0,20 kN;8 0,0,-1.5,15 kN" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,8 0,5 m;8 0,B,2 m" titolo="Free-body diagram with resultant $Q$ and the components of $F$"}

$$
Q = q \cdot 6 = 4 \cdot 6 = 24\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
x_Q = 3\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + 20 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - Q - 15 = 0 \\[4pt]
10\,V_B - 3\,Q - 8 \cdot 15 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
V_B = \frac{72 + 120}{10}\,\mathrm{kN} = 19.2\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 24 + 15 - 19.2 = 19.8\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
H_A = -20\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
-10\,V_A + 7\,Q + 2 \cdot 15 = -198 + 168 + 30 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The resultant uniform load is $Q = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and passes through the midpoint of the *loaded segment*, a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The tilted force breaks down into the $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ components to the right and $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ components downward, applied at the same point.

The first equation is horizontal force balance, in which only $H_A$ appears, because the roller support at $B$ exerts only a vertical force. The second is vertical force balance, the third moment equilibrium about $A$. The horizontal component of $F$ has line of action along the axis of the beam, which passes through $A$, so it does not appear in the rotation.

$H_A = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**, opposite the horizontal component of the load; $V_A = 19.8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 19.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ are upwards. In the check $B$ the arms are $10\,\mathrm{m}$ for $V_A$, $7\,\mathrm{m}$ for $Q$ and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ for the vertical component of $F$.

</div>
