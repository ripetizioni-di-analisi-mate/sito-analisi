## Exercise 1

The $ACB$ L-shaped beam in the figure is formed by the column $AC$, of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and the crossbeam $CB$, of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, welded at $C$. It has a $A$ footing and horizontal roller support at the end $B$ of the crossbeam. Crossbeam carries a uniform $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load; in the $C$ node acts a horizontal force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right. Calculate support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,4;B,5,4" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="C,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="C,1.3,0,F" quote="A,C,4 m,1;C,B,5 m,-1.1" titolo="L-shaped beam with a uniform load on its crossbeam and a horizontal force at $C$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,4;B,5,4" aste="A-C;C-B" reazioni="A,1.2,0,H_A;A,0,1.2,V_A;B,0,1.2,V_B" forze="2.5 4,0,-1.2,Q;C,1.3,0,F" quote="A,C,4 m,-1;C,B,5 m,0.8" titolo="Free-body diagram"}

$$
Q = q \cdot 5 = 20\,\mathrm{kN} \text{ in } \left(2.5\,\mathrm{m};\ 4\,\mathrm{m}\right)
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + F = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - Q = 0 \\[4pt]
5\,V_B - 2.5\,Q - 4\,F = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
V_B = \frac{2.5 \cdot 20 + 4 \cdot 10}{5}\,\mathrm{kN} = \frac{90}{5}\,\mathrm{kN} = 18\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 20 - 18 = 2\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
H_A = -10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
-5\,V_A + 4\,H_A + 2.5\,Q = -10 - 40 + 50 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The column and crossbeam are welded, so the L-shaped beam is one rigid body with a hinge and a roller Support, and the unknowns are $H_A$, $V_A$ and $V_B$. Equations are horizontal force equilibrium, vertical force equilibrium, and moment equilibrium about $A$.

To write the rotation, the **arm** of each force is used about $A$. For vertical forces is the horizontal distance from $A$: $5\,\mathrm{m}$ for $V_B$ and $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ for $Q$. For horizontal force $F$ is the elevation of its line of action, $4\,\mathrm{m}$; Heading right over $A$, it has clockwise moment.

It results in $V_B = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ upwards, $H_A = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**. The moment of horizontal force increases the reaction at $B$ by $\dfrac{40}{5} = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and decreases by the same amount that at $A$, which with the load $q$ alone would equal $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ each. In the check $B$, the reaction $H_A$, applied $4\,\mathrm{m}$ lower than $B$, has $4\,H_A$ moment, and the force $F$ has zero moment arm because its line of action passes through $B$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Frame $ACDB$ has columns $AC$ and $BD$ of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and beam $CD$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, welded together at the joints. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. The beam carries a uniform load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, and at joint $C$ a horizontal force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Calculate the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,4;D,6,4;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="C,D,0.6,0.6,q" forze="C,1.5,0,F" quote="A,C,4 m,1.2;C,D,6 m,-1.2" titolo="Portal frame with a uniform load on its crossbeam and a horizontal force at $C$"}

$$
Q = q \cdot 6 = 30\,\mathrm{kN} \text{ in } \left(3\,\mathrm{m};\ 4\,\mathrm{m}\right)
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + F = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - Q = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_B - 3\,Q - 4\,F = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
V_B = \frac{90 + 48}{6}\,\mathrm{kN} = 23\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 30 - 23 = 7\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
H_A = -12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $D$:
$$
-6\,V_A + 4\,H_A + 3\,Q = -42 - 48 + 90 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The portal frame is a single rigid body, because the three segments are welded at the nodes; hinge at $A$ and roller support at $B$ give three unknowns. Equations are horizontal force balance, which contains only $H_A$, vertical force balance and moment equilibrium about $A$.

About $A$ the reaction $V_B$ has a moment arm of $6\,\mathrm{m}$, the resultant $Q$ has a moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and the horizontal $F$ force has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$, equal to the height of the crossbeam. The horizontal force has clockwise moment, like the load.

It results in $V_B = 23\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A = 7\,\mathrm{kN}$ upwards and $H_A = -12\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**. All horizontal force is balanced by the hinge, because roller support exerts only a vertical force. Verification uses the $D$ reference point, which does not stand on the lines of action of reactions; about $D$ the reaction $V_A$ has $6\,\mathrm{m}$ arm, the reaction $H_A$ has $4\,\mathrm{m}$ arm, the resultant $Q$ has $3\,\mathrm{m}$ arm, while $V_B$ and $F$ have zero arm.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The bent cantilever $ACB$ shown consists of column $AC$ of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at its base $A$ and horizontal arm $CB$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The arm carries a uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and, at its tip $B$, a vertical force $F_1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$. At joint $C$ a horizontal force $F_2 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Calculate the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,3;B,4,3" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro" carichi="C,B,0.4,0.4,q" forze="B,0,-1,F_1;C,1,0,F_2" quote="A,C,3 m,1;C,B,4 m,-0.9" titolo="L-shaped cantilever fixed at its base"}

$$
Q = q \cdot 4 = 12\,\mathrm{kN} \text{ in } \left(2\,\mathrm{m};\ 3\,\mathrm{m}\right)
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + F_2 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A - Q - F_1 = 0 \\[4pt]
M_A - 2\,Q - 4\,F_1 - 3\,F_2 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
H_A = -8\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 12 + 10 = 22\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
M_A = 24 + 40 + 24 = 88\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
M_A - 4\,V_A + 3\,H_A + 2\,Q = 88 - 88 - 24 + 24 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Fixed foot support prevents the two translation and rotation, then exerts $H_A$, $V_A$ and $M_A$; equations are horizontal force balance, vertical force balance and moment equilibrium about $A$, in which only the reactive moment appears.

About $A$ all loads have clockwise moment. The resultant $Q$ and $F_1$ force are vertical, with $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ arms given by horizontal distance from $A$; the force $F_2$ is horizontal, with a moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ given by the elevation of $C$.

It results in $H_A = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**, $V_A = 22\,\mathrm{kN}$ upwards and $M_A = 88\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ **counterclockwise**. In the check $B$ the reaction $V_A$ has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and a clockwise moment, the reaction $H_A$ has a moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and, directed to the left below $B$, clockwise moment; $Q$ has a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and counterclockwise moment; $F_1$ and $F_2$ have lines of action for $B$.

</div>
