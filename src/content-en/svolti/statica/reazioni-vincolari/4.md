## Exercise 1

The simply supported beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, with a hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, is loaded only by a clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ applied a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Calculate support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="2 0,-1,ℳ" quote="A,2 0,2 m;2 0,B,4 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_B - \mathcal{M} = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
V_B = \frac{\mathcal{M}}{6} = \frac{18}{6}\,\mathrm{kN} = 3\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = -V_B = -3\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
-6\,V_A - \mathcal{M} = 18 - 18 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The couple has zero resultant, so it does not appear in the two translation equations; vertical force balance says that the vertical reactions are opposite. In the moment equation about $A$ the couple enters with its moment, negative because it is clockwise moment, and the moment arm does not intervene, because the moment of an **couple** is the same as with each reference point.

It results in $V_B = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ **upward** and $V_A = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ **downward**. The two reactions form a counterclockwise couple of a moment arm of $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and $3 \cdot 6 = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ moment, equal to and opposite to the applied one. The result does not depend on the distance of $\mathcal{M}$ from $A$, so the same couple at another point in the beam would give the same reactions.

In the $B$ test the reaction $V_A$ has $6\,\mathrm{m}$ arm; with $V_A = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$ the term $-6\,V_A$ is $+18$, and the couple enters again with $-18$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The simply supported beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, with a hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, carries a triangular load that grows linearly from $0$ at $A$ to $q_0 = 9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. Calculate support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0,0.9,q_0" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with a triangular load varying from $0$ to $q_0 = 9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

$$
Q = \frac{q_0 \cdot 6}{2} = \frac{9 \cdot 6}{2} = 27\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
x_Q = \frac{2}{3} \cdot 6 = 4\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - Q = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_B - 4\,Q = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
V_B = \frac{4 \cdot 27}{6}\,\mathrm{kN} = 18\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 27 - 18 = 9\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
-6\,V_A + 2\,Q = -54 + 54 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The load equals $q(x) = \dfrac{q_0\,x}{6}$, with $x$ measured from $A$. Its **resultant** is the area of the triangle, $\dfrac{q_0 \cdot 6}{2} = 27\,\mathrm{kN}$, and passes through the centroid of the triangle, which stands at one third of the length from the side of the maximum value, i.e. $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The same value is obtained from the integral $x_Q = \dfrac{1}{Q}\displaystyle\int_0^6 x\,\dfrac{9\,x}{6}\,dx = \dfrac{108}{27} = 4\,\mathrm{m}$.

Equations are horizontal force equilibrium, vertical force equilibrium, and moment equilibrium about $A$. It results in $V_B = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$, both **upward**; $B$ support, on the side where the load is most intense, carries two thirds of the load.

In the check against $B$ the resultant has a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and counterclockwise moment, $V_A$ has a moment arm of $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and a clockwise moment.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and fixed at $A$, carries a trapezoidal load that decreases linearly from $8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$ to $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$; in the free end $B$ a counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is applied. Calculate support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.8,0.2,q" coppie="B,1,ℳ" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Cantilever with a trapezoidal load varying from $8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and a couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$"}

$$
Q_1 = 2 \cdot 3 = 6\,\mathrm{kN} \ \text{ at } 1.5\,\mathrm{m} \text{ from } A,
\qquad
Q_2 = \frac{(8 - 2) \cdot 3}{2} = 9\,\mathrm{kN} \ \text{ at } 1\,\mathrm{m} \text{ from } A
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A - Q_1 - Q_2 = 0 \\[4pt]
M_A - 1.5\,Q_1 - 1 \cdot Q_2 + \mathcal{M} = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
V_A = 6 + 9 = 15\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
M_A = 9 + 9 - 10 = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
M_A - 3\,V_A + 1.5\,Q_1 + 2\,Q_2 + \mathcal{M} = 8 - 45 + 9 + 18 + 10 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The trapezoid breaks down into a rectangle of $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ height, resulting in $Q_1$ at the midpoint, and into a triangle with maximum value $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$, resulting in $Q_2$ at one-third the length of $A$. Alternatively, the unique result $Q = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ is calculated, with $x_Q = \dfrac{6 \cdot 1.5 + 9 \cdot 1}{15} = 1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.

Equations are horizontal force equilibrium, vertical force equilibrium, and rotation around fixed support $A$. The resulting two have clockwise moment about $A$, the **couple** $\mathcal{M}$ is counterclockwise and enters with the plus sign. It results in $V_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ **upward** and $M_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ **counterclockwise**. Without the couple the reactive moment would be $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and the couple balances a part of it.

In the check $B$, the reaction $V_A$ has a moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and a clockwise moment; $Q_1$ has a moment arm of $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and $Q_2$ has a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$, both with counterclockwise moment because they stand to the left of $B$ and are directed downwards.

</div>
