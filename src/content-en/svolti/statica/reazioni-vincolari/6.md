## Exercise 1

Member $AB$ in the figure is a cover flap ranging from $A = (0, 0)$ to $B = (4\,\mathrm{m}, 3\,\mathrm{m})$, with a hinge at $A$ and a horizontal-level roller support at $B$. Self-weight is $g = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per rod length unit; snow equals $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per horizontal projection unit. Calculate support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.4,0.4,g;0 3.9,4 3.9,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.5;4 0,B,3 m,-0.5" titolo="Roof member under self-weight $g$ per unit member length and snow load $q$ per unit horizontal projection"}

$$
\ell = \sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = 5\,\mathrm{m},
\qquad
G = g\,\ell = 2 \cdot 5 = 10\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
Q = q \cdot 4 = 6 \cdot 4 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - G - Q = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_B - 2\,G - 2\,Q = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
V_B = \frac{2 \cdot 10 + 2 \cdot 24}{4}\,\mathrm{kN} = 17\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 10 + 24 - 17 = 17\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
-4\,V_A + 2\,G + 2\,Q = -68 + 20 + 48 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Both loads are vertical, but they are specified per unit length along different lines. The **self-weight** acts over the member length $\ell = 5\,\mathrm{m}$ and has resultant $g\,\ell = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$. The **snow load** acts over the horizontal projection of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and has resultant $q \cdot 4 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$, equal to $q\,\ell\cos\alpha$ because $\cos\alpha = \dfrac{4}{5}$. Both loads are uniform, so their resultants pass through the midpoint of the member, at horizontal coordinate $2\,\mathrm{m}$.

Equations are horizontal force equilibrium, vertical force equilibrium, and moment equilibrium about $A$. For vertical forces the moment arm about $A$ is the horizontal distance: $4\,\mathrm{m}$ for $V_B$ and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ for the resulting two.

The loads are vertical and their resultant passes through the midpoint of the horizontal projection, so the vertical reactions are equal: $V_A = V_B = 17\,\mathrm{kN}$, **upward**, and $H_A = 0$. In the check $B$, the arms are $4\,\mathrm{m}$ for $V_A$ and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ for the resulting arms.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The same $AB$ sloping roof member in the figure, from $A = (0, 0)$ to $B = (4\,\mathrm{m}, 3\,\mathrm{m})$, with a hinge at $A$ and a horizontal roller support at $B$, is hit by wind: a uniform $p = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ pressure perpendicular to the rod, per unit length, directed downwards and to the right. Calculate support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.4,0.4,p,306.87" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.6;4 0,B,3 m,-0.5" titolo="Roof member under wind load $p$ perpendicular to its axis"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,3" aste="A-B" reazioni="A,1,0,H_A;A,0,1,V_A;B,0,1,V_B" forze="2 1.5,0.75,-1,P" quote="A,4 0,4 m,-0.5;4 0,B,3 m,-0.5" titolo="Free-body diagram showing resultant wind force $P$ at the midpoint"}

$$
P = p\,\ell = 4 \cdot 5 = 20\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
P_x = P\sin\alpha = 20 \cdot 0.6 = 12\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
P_y = -P\cos\alpha = -20 \cdot 0.8 = -16\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + 12 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - 16 = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_B - 2 \cdot 16 - 1.5 \cdot 12 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
V_B = \frac{32 + 18}{4}\,\mathrm{kN} = 12.5\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 16 - 12.5 = 3.5\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
H_A = -12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
-4\,V_A + 3\,H_A + 2 \cdot 16 + 1.5 \cdot 12 = -14 - 36 + 32 + 18 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The wind resultant is $P = p\,\ell = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$, applied at the midpoint $(2\,\mathrm{m}, 1.5\,\mathrm{m})$ and perpendicular to the rod. The rod forms with the horizontal the angle $\alpha$ with $\sin\alpha = 0.6$ and $\cos\alpha = 0.8$, then the perpendicular forms the angle $\alpha$ with the vertical, so $P$ has horizontal component $P\sin\alpha$ to the right and vertical component $P\cos\alpha$ downward.

Equations are horizontal force equilibrium, vertical force equilibrium, and moment equilibrium about $A$. About $A$ the vertical component of $P$ has $2\,\mathrm{m}$ arm, the horizontal component has $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ arm, i.e. the point of application dimension; both have clockwise moment.

It results in $H_A = -12\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**, and $V_A = 3.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_B = 12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upwards. The moment of the horizontal component of the wind about $A$ increases the reaction at $B$ and reduces that at $A$, which with the vertical component alone would equal $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ each. In the $B$ test, the reaction $H_A$, applied $3\,\mathrm{m}$ lower than $B$, has $3\,\mathrm{m}$ arm; since $H_A$ acts to the left, its moment is clockwise and the term $3\,H_A$ equals $-36$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The $AB$ ramp in the figure goes from $A = (0, 0)$ to $B = (3\,\mathrm{m}, 4\,\mathrm{m})$; has a pin at the base $A$ and at $B$ a roller support that moves along a vertical wall, with horizontal reaction directed to the left. Self-weight is $g = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per unit length; at point $P$ which is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ away from $A$ along the rod acts a vertical force $F = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$. Calculate support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;P,1.8,2.4;B,3,4" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" carichi="A,B,0.4,0.4,g" forze="P,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,P,3 m,0.5;P,B,2 m,0.5" titolo="Ramp supported against a vertical wall, under self-weight $g$ and force $F$"}

$$
\ell = 5\,\mathrm{m},
\qquad
G = g\,\ell = 10\,\mathrm{kN} \text{ in } \left(1.5\,\mathrm{m};\ 2\,\mathrm{m}\right),
\qquad
P = \left(\frac{3}{5} \cdot 3\,\mathrm{m};\ \frac{4}{5} \cdot 3\,\mathrm{m}\right) = \left(1.8\,\mathrm{m};\ 2.4\,\mathrm{m}\right)
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A - R_B = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A - G - F = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,R_B - 1.5\,G - 1.8\,F = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
R_B = \frac{15 + 27}{4}\,\mathrm{kN} = 10.5\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
H_A = 10.5\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 10 + 15 = 25\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
4\,H_A - 3\,V_A + 1.5\,G + 1.2\,F = 42 - 75 + 15 + 18 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The wall is vertical, so roller support at $B$ exerts a horizontal $R_B$ reaction, assumed positive to the left. The resultant of the self-weight is $G = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the midpoint of the ramp; find point $P$ by multiplying the components of the unit vector along the member, $\left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$, by distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$.

Equations are horizontal force equilibrium, vertical force equilibrium, and moment equilibrium about $A$. The reaction $R_B$ is horizontal and applied to the $4\,\mathrm{m}$ dimension, so its arm about $A$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$; Heading left over $A$, it has counterclockwise moment. Vertical forces $G$ and $F$ have moment arms $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and $1.8\,\mathrm{m}$, respectively, and both produce clockwise moments.

It results in $R_B = 10.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, $H_A = 10.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the right** and $V_A = 25\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The two horizontal reactions are opposite and form a counterclockwise couple, which balances the clockwise moment of vertical loads; all vertical load is carried by the hinge, because the smooth wall exerts only a horizontal force. In the check against $B$ the reaction $H_A$ has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and counterclockwise moment, $V_A$ has a moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and a clockwise moment, $G$ and $F$ have $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ moment arms and counterclockwise moment.

</div>
