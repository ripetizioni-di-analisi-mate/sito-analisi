## Exercise 1

Find the support reactions of cantilever $AB$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, fixed at $A$ and loaded at its free end $B$ by a vertical force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0,-0.8,F" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Cantilever under force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at its free end"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" reazioni="A,0.7,0,H_A;A,0,0.7,V_A" coppie="A,1,M_A" forze="B,0,-0.8,F" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Free-body diagram showing fixed-support reactions $H_A$, $V_A$, and moment $M_A$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A - F = 0 \\[4pt]
M_A - 3\,F = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
V_A = F = 12\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
M_A = 3\,F = 3 \cdot 12 = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
M_A - 3\,V_A = 36 - 36 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Fixed support prevents the two translation and rotation, so it exerts three reactions: $H_A$, $V_A$ and the reactive moment $M_A$, assumed counterclockwise. The three unknowns are all applied at $A$.

Equations are horizontal force equilibrium, vertical force equilibrium, and moment equilibrium about $A$. In the moment equation about $A$ the forces $H_A$ and $V_A$ have zero moment arm, so only $M_A$ appears; $F$ force has a moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and a clockwise moment.

The result is $V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ **upward** and $M_A = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ **counterclockwise**, because fixed support balances the clockwise load clockwise moment. In the check $B$ the force $F$ does not appear, while $V_A$ has a moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and a clockwise moment; the reactive moment $M_A$, like any couple, enters with the same value as with each reference point.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Cantilever $AB$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at $A$. A uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts along its entire length, and a vertical force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at free end $B$. Find the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.4,0.4,q" forze="B,0,-1,F" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Cantilever under uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

$$
Q = q \cdot 4 = 6 \cdot 4 = 24\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
x_Q = 2\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A - Q - F = 0 \\[4pt]
M_A - 2\,Q - 4\,F = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
V_A = 24 + 10 = 34\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
M_A = 2 \cdot 24 + 4 \cdot 10 = 48 + 40 = 88\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
M_A - 4\,V_A + 2\,Q = 88 - 136 + 48 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The resultant of the uniform load is $Q = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$, applied at the midpoint of the cantilever. Equations are horizontal force balance, vertical force equilibrium and rotation around fixed support $A$, in which only the reactive moment appears.

About $A$ the resultant $Q$ has a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $F$ force has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$; both have clockwise moment. The reactive moment is then the sum of the two moments, $M_A = 88\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ **counterclockwise**, and the vertical reaction is the sum of the loads, $V_A = 34\,\mathrm{kN}$ **upward**.

In the check against $B$ the reaction $V_A$ has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and a clockwise moment, the resultant $Q$ has a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and counterclockwise moment, the force $F$ has zero moment arm.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The cantilever shown has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$. Its right end $B$ is fixed, and its left end $A$ is free. A uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the first $3\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$, while a counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and a rightward horizontal force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ act at $A$. Find the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="B,incastro,0" carichi="A,3 0,0.5,0.5,q" coppie="A,1,ℳ" forze="A,1.2,0,F" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,2 m" titolo="Right-fixed cantilever with a partially distributed load, couple $\mathcal{M}$, and axial force $F$"}

$$
Q = q \cdot 3 = 4 \cdot 3 = 12\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
\text{at } 1.5\,\mathrm{m} \text{ from } A, \text{ that is, at } 3.5\,\mathrm{m} \text{ from } B
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_B + F = 0 \\[4pt]
V_B - Q = 0 \\[4pt]
M_B + 3.5\,Q + \mathcal{M} = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
H_B = -8\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
M_B = -3.5 \cdot 12 - 20 = -62\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $A$:
$$
M_B + 5\,V_B - 1.5\,Q + \mathcal{M} = -62 + 60 - 18 + 20 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Fixed support is at $B$, so the reactions are $H_B$, $V_B$ and $M_B$, assumed positive according to the axes and counterclockwise; the rotation reference point is $B$. The first equation is horizontal force balance, the second vertical force balance, the third moment equilibrium about $B$.

The resultant $Q$ stands to the left of $B$, at $3.5\,\mathrm{m}$, and is directed downwards, so it tends to rotate the cantilever counterclockwise around $B$ and its moment is positive. The **couple** $\mathcal{M}$ enters the rotation with its value, regardless of where it is applied. The force $F$ has line of action along the cantilever axis, which passes through $B$, so it has zero moment.

It results in $H_B = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, $V_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $M_B = -62\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, i.e. $62\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ **in clockwise**. The minus sign indicates that the reactive moment has the opposite direction to the assumed one. The load and couple applied both have counterclockwise moment about $B$, so fixed support balances them with a clockwise moment. In the check against $A$ the reaction $V_B$ has a moment arm of $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and counterclockwise moment, $Q$ has a moment arm of $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and a clockwise moment.

</div>
