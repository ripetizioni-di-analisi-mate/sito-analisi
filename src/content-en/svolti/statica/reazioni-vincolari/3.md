## Exercise 1

Beam $AC$ has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and an overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. A vertical force $P = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and a vertical force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at end $C$. Find the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="3 0,0,-2,P;C,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,3 m;B,C,2 m" titolo="Overhanging beam under two point forces"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - P - F = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_B - 3\,P - 8\,F = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
V_B = \frac{3 \cdot 18 + 8 \cdot 12}{6}\,\mathrm{kN} = \frac{54 + 96}{6}\,\mathrm{kN} = 25\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 18 + 12 - 25 = 5\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
-6\,V_A + 3\,P - 2\,F = -30 + 54 - 24 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The beam is a rigid body with a hinge and roller support, so the unknowns are $H_A$, $V_A$ and $V_B$. Equations are horizontal force equilibrium, vertical force equilibrium, and moment equilibrium about $A$, where the lines of action of $H_A$ and $V_A$ meet.

About $A$ both forces have clockwise moment, with $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $8\,\mathrm{m}$ arms; $F$'s arm is greater than the distance between the supports, because $F$ lies on the overhang. It results in $V_B = 25\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$, both **upward**.

The force on the overhang loads the $B$ mount more than the load itself and unloads the $A$ mount. To check moments about $B$, force $P$ acts to the left of $B$ and produces a counterclockwise moment, while $F$ acts to the right of $B$ and produces a clockwise moment.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Beam $AC$ of length $9\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$ a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. A uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts along the entire beam, and a vertical force $F = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at end $C$ of the overhang. Find the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,9,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.8,0.8,q" forze="C,0,-2,F" quote="A,B,6 m;B,C,3 m" titolo="Overhanging beam under uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and force $F = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

$$
Q = q \cdot 9 = 4 \cdot 9 = 36\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
x_Q = 4.5\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - Q - F = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_B - 4.5\,Q - 9\,F = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
V_B = \frac{4.5 \cdot 36 + 9 \cdot 20}{6}\,\mathrm{kN} = \frac{162 + 180}{6}\,\mathrm{kN} = 57\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 36 + 20 - 57 = -1\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
-6\,V_A + 1.5\,Q - 3\,F = 6 + 54 - 60 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,9,0" aste="A-C" reazioni="A,0,-1.5,V_A;B,0,2.5,V_B" forze="4.5 0,0,-2.5,Q;C,0,-2,F" quote="A,B,6 m;B,C,3 m" titolo="Beam forces with the reactions shown in their actual directions. Reaction $V_A$ acts downward"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The resultant uniform load, $Q = 36\,\mathrm{kN}$, passes through the midpoint of the entire beam, a distance $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Equations are horizontal force equilibrium, vertical force equilibrium, and moment equilibrium about $A$.

The rotation gives $V_B = 57\,\mathrm{kN}$ upwards, and vertical force balance gives $V_A = -1\,\mathrm{kN}$. **minus sign** indicates that the reaction at $A$ equals $1\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is directed **downward**. About $B$ the force $F$ and load on the overhang have clockwise moment, while the load between $A$ and $B$ has counterclockwise moment. The clockwise moment $20 \cdot 3 + 12 \cdot 1.5 = 78\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ exceeds the counterclockwise moment $24 \cdot 3 = 72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The beam therefore tends to lift at $A$, where the pin holds it down.

To check moments about $B$, resultant $Q$ acts a distance $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$ and produces a counterclockwise moment, whereas $F$ has moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and produces a clockwise moment; with $V_A = -1\,\mathrm{kN}$ the term $-6\,V_A$ is $+6$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Beam $CD$ of length $10\,\mathrm{m}$ has two overhangs. A roller on a horizontal surface at $A$ lies a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$, and a pin at $B$ lies a distance $8\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$. The entire beam carries a uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. A vertical force $F_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$, and a force $F_2 = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward and to the left at $D$, with horizontal component $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and vertical component $9\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,2,0;B,8,0;D,10,0" aste="C-D" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera" carichi="C,D,0.8,0.8,q" forze="C,0,-2,F_1;D,-1.6,-1.2,F_2" quote="C,A,2 m;A,B,6 m;B,D,2 m" titolo="Beam with two overhangs under a uniform load and two forces"}

$$
Q = q \cdot 10 = 40\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
x_Q = 5\,\mathrm{m} \text{ from } C
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_B - 12 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - Q - F_1 - 9 = 0 \\[4pt]
-6\,V_A + 3\,Q + 8\,F_1 - 2 \cdot 9 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
V_A = \frac{3 \cdot 40 + 8 \cdot 12 - 18}{6}\,\mathrm{kN} = \frac{198}{6}\,\mathrm{kN} = 33\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_B = 40 + 12 + 9 - 33 = 28\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
H_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $A$:
$$
6\,V_B - 3\,Q + 2\,F_1 - 8 \cdot 9 = 168 - 120 + 24 - 72 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The hinge is at $B$, so the reference point of rotation is $B$; $H_B$ and $V_B$ lines of action pass through $B$, so only $V_A$ appears in the equation. The first equation is horizontal force balance, which contains only $H_B$; the second is vertical force equilibrium; the third moment equilibrium about $B$.

About $B$ the reaction $V_A$, to $6\,\mathrm{m}$ on the left, has clockwise moment-frame; the resultant $Q$, $3\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left, and $F_1$ force, $8\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left, are directed downwards and have counterclockwise moment; the vertical component of $F_2$, at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right, has clockwise moment. The horizontal component of $F_2$ has line of action along the beam axis, which passes through $B$.

It results in $V_A = 33\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 28\,\mathrm{kN}$ upwards and $H_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the right**, opposite the horizontal component of $F_2$. In the check $A$ the arms are $6\,\mathrm{m}$ for $V_B$, $3\,\mathrm{m}$ for $Q$, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ for $F_1$ and $8\,\mathrm{m}$ for the vertical component of $F_2$.

</div>
