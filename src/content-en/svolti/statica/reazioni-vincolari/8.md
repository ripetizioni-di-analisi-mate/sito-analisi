## Exercise 1

The beam $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, is bound at $A$ with a parallel-link support at horizontal links, which prevents horizontal force equilibrium and rotation, and at $B$ with a roller support on a horizontal plane. They act a uniform $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load over the entire beam, a vertical $F_1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ force in the middle and a horizontal $F_2 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ force at $B$, directed to the left. Calculate support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.4,0.4,q" forze="2 0,0,-1,F_1;B,-1,0,F_2" quote="A,2 0,2 m;2 0,B,2 m" titolo="Beam with a parallel-link support at $A$ and a roller at $B$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" reazioni="A,1,0,H_A;B,0,1,V_B" coppie="A,1,M_A" forze="2 0,0,-1.2,Q + F_1;B,-1,0,F_2" quote="A,2 0,2 m;2 0,B,2 m" titolo="Free-body diagram showing force $H_A$ and moment $M_A$ at the parallel-link support"}

$$
Q = q \cdot 4 = 12\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
x_Q = 2\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A - F_2 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_B - Q - F_1 = 0 \\[4pt]
M_A + 4\,V_B - 2\,Q - 2\,F_1 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
H_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_B = 12 + 10 = 22\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
M_A = 24 + 20 - 88 = -44\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
M_A + 2\,Q + 2\,F_1 = -44 + 24 + 20 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Parallel-link support to horizontal links exerts a horizontal force, with any line of action, i.e. a force $H_A$ applied at $A$ and a moment $M_A$; does not exert vertical forces. Roller support at $B$ only exercises $V_B$. The three unknowns are therefore $H_A$, $M_A$ and $V_B$.

Equations are horizontal force equilibrium, vertical force equilibrium, and moment equilibrium about $A$. Each of the two translations contains only one unknown. All vertical component of the load goes to roller support, because parallel-link support has no vertical reaction, and all horizontal component goes to parallel-link support. The rotation then gives $M_A$.

It results in $H_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, $V_B = 22\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $M_A = -44\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, i.e. $44\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ **in clockwise**. The reactive moment is timed because the reaction $V_B$, with a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$, has about $A$ a counterclockwise moment greater than the load clockwise moment, applied to $2\,\mathrm{m}$. In the $B$ test, the reaction $V_B$ and the force $F_2$ have zero moment arm.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The canopy shown consists of a horizontal beam $AB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, pinned to the wall at $A$. A tie rod $CD$ is pinned to the beam at $C$, a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and to the wall at $D$, a height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$. The beam carries a uniform load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and a vertical point load $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at its tip $B$. Find the support reactions at $A$ and the tension in the tie rod.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,6,0;D,0,3" aste="A-B;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;D,cerniera,180" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" forze="B,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,C,4 m;C,B,2 m;A,D,3 m,0.9" titolo="Canopy pinned at $A$ and supported by tie rod $CD$"}

$$
\overline{CD} = \sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = 5\,\mathrm{m},
\qquad
\text{unit vector from } C \text{ to } D = \left(-\frac{4}{5};\ \frac{3}{5}\right),
\qquad
Q = q \cdot 6 = 30\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A - \dfrac{4}{5}\,N = 0 \\[8pt]
V_A + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N - Q - F = 0 \\[8pt]
4 \cdot \dfrac{3}{5}\,N - 3\,Q - 6\,F = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
N = \frac{90 + 60}{2.4}\,\mathrm{kN} = 62.5\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
H_A = 0.8 \cdot 62.5 = 50\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 30 + 10 - 37.5 = 2.5\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $D$:
$$
3\,H_A - 3\,Q - 6\,F = 150 - 90 - 60 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The $CD$ tie rod is a hinged rod at the two ends and loaded only in the hinges, so it is an **two-force link** and exerts a direct force along the line $CD$ on the beam. It is denoted by $N$ and assumed to be positive when pulling the beam towards $D$, i.e. when the tie rod is stretched; Its components are $\dfrac{4}{5}\,N$ to the left and $\dfrac{3}{5}\,N$ upward. The beam thus has three unknowns: $H_A$, $V_A$ and $N$.

Use horizontal and vertical force equilibrium and take moments about $A$. To find the moment about $A$, resolve the tie force into components at $C$. The horizontal component acts along the beam axis and has zero moment arm. The vertical component has moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and produces a counterclockwise moment. The applied loads act clockwise, with moment arms $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $6\,\mathrm{m}$.

The calculation gives $N = 62.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The positive sign means that the tie rod is **in tension**. The pin exerts the horizontal reaction $H_A = 50\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, balancing the horizontal component of the tie force, and the vertical reaction $V_A = 2.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. The tie force at $C$ and the reaction $H_A$ act in opposite directions, so segment $AC$ of the beam is in compression. To check equilibrium, take moments about $D$, which lies on the line of action of the tie force. About $D$, the reaction $H_A$ acts to the right at a point $3\,\mathrm{m}$ lower and produces a counterclockwise moment. The moment arm of $V_A$ is zero.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The beam $AB$ shown, of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, is bound at $A$ with a parallel-link support at vertical links, which prevents vertical force equilibrium and rotation, and at $B$ with a two-force inclined link of $45^\circ$, whose pin-ended link goes from $B$ down and to the right. They act a uniform $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load over the entire beam and a horizontal $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ force to the right at the end $A$. Calculate support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo;B,pendolo,315" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="A,1.2,0,F" quote="A,B,5 m" titolo="Beam with a vertical parallel-link support and an inclined two-force link"}

$$
Q = q \cdot 5 = 20\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
x_Q = 2.5\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac{R_B}{\sqrt{2}} + F = 0 \\[8pt]
V_A + \dfrac{R_B}{\sqrt{2}} - Q = 0 \\[8pt]
M_A + 5 \cdot \dfrac{R_B}{\sqrt{2}} - 2.5\,Q = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
R_B = 8\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 11.31\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 20 - 8 = 12\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
M_A = 50 - 40 = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
M_A - 5\,V_A + 2.5\,Q = 10 - 60 + 50 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Parallel-link support at vertical links exerts a vertical force with any line of action, i.e. $V_A$ and the moment $M_A$; does not exert horizontal forces. The two-force link at $B$ exerts a force $R_B$ along the pin-ended link, assumed positive when directed from the ground towards the beam, i.e. up and left, and its components are $\dfrac{R_B}{\sqrt{2}}$ to the left and $\dfrac{R_B}{\sqrt{2}}$ upward.

The first equation is horizontal force balance, and the only reaction with a horizontal component is $R_B$, so this equation immediately gives $R_B$, and it is the choice that leaves an unknown by equation. Then vertical force balance gives $V_A$ and taking moments about $A$ gives $M_A$. The force $F$ has line of action along the axis of the beam and does not appear in the rotation.

It results in $R_B = 8\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN}$, positive, so the two-force link pushes the beam and is **compressed**; $V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upwards and $M_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ **counterclockwise**. The horizontal load, through the tilted two-force link, also produces a vertical component of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$, at $B$, which reduces the vertical reaction at $A$. In the check against $B$ the reaction $R_B$ has zero moment, $V_A$ has a moment arm of $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and a clockwise moment, $Q$ has a moment arm of $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and counter-clockwise moment.

</div>
