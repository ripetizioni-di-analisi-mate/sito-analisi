## Exercise 1

The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, with $AB = 6\,\mathrm{m}$. At point $C$, at $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, an inclined $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ force acts, with components $P_x = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $P_y = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$ (downwards). Calculate with the principle of virtual work the axial force and the shear force at point $D$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and the axial force at point $E$, a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,2,0;C,4,0;E,5,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,1.2,-0.9,P" quote="A,D,2 m,0.9;D,C,2 m,0.9;C,E,1 m,0.9;E,B,1 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam under inclined force $P$ at $C$ ($P_x = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, $P_y = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$)"}

Axial force at $D$: parallel-link axial support at $D$. The section $AD$ remains stationary and the section $DB$ moves horizontally by $\delta w_d$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,2,0;C,4,0;E,5,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" interni="D,doppio-pendolo,90" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,1.2,-0.9,P" frecce="2.5,0.5,3.7,0.5,δw_d" titolo="With an axial release using parallel links at $D$, only segment $DB$ moves, translating horizontally"}

$$
\Delta w = \delta w_s - \delta w_d = -\delta w_d ,
\qquad
\delta L = P_x\,\delta w_d = 8\,\delta w_d
$$
$$
N_D\,\Delta w + \delta L = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
N_D = \frac{8\,\delta w_d}{\delta w_d} = 8\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Axial force at $E$: the section $EB$ moves horizontally and does not carry loads, so
$$
\delta L = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
N_E = 0
$$

$D$ shear force: parallel-link support in $D$, the $AD$ and $DB$ segments rotate $\delta\varphi$ around $A$ and $B$.
$$
\delta v_s = 2\,\delta\varphi ,
\qquad
\delta v_d = (2 - 6)\,\delta\varphi = -4\,\delta\varphi ,
\qquad
\Delta v = -6\,\delta\varphi
$$
$$
\delta u_C = 0 ,
\qquad
\delta v_C = (4 - 6)\,\delta\varphi = -2\,\delta\varphi ,
\qquad
\delta L = (-6) \cdot (-2\,\delta\varphi) = 12\,\delta\varphi
$$
$$
T_D = -\frac{12\,\delta\varphi}{-6\,\delta\varphi} = 2\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Verification: from the horizontal translation of the whole $H_A = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$ beam and from rotation about $B$, $-6\,V_A + 6 \cdot 2 = 0$, i.e. $V_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$; $AD$ segment balance:
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + N_D = -8 + 8 = 0 \\
V_A - T_D = 2 - 2 = 0
\end{cases}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For the axial force an **parallel-link axial support** is inserted, which prevents relative rotation and perpendicular sliding to the axis and leaves the sliding along the axis free. The segment $AD$ has the pin at $A$ and can only rotates about $A$. Parallel-link support imposes the same rotation on the segment $DB$, which would move $B$ vertically; the roller support prevents it, so neither segment rotates. The horizontal translation of $DB$ remains, which the roller support allows. In this displacement only the horizontal component of the force does work, and the force at $D$ is **$N_D = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, in tension**: the component $P_x$ pulls the segment $DC$ to the right and the pin at $A$ holds it back. At section $E$ the section that it translates does not carry loads, therefore $N_E = 0$. For shear force the two segments rotate about $A$ and $B$; all points of the axis stand at the center elevation and have zero horizontal displacement, so only the vertical component does work, and $T_D = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$. The check system writes the two translation equations of the segment $AD$, horizontal and vertical, which must be valid together.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In the frame shown, columns $AD$ and $EB$ have height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and beam $DE$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, with $A = (0, 0)$, $D = (0, 4)$, $E = (4, 4)$, and $B = (4, 0)$. A pin supports the frame at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface supports it at $B$. At $D$ a horizontal force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right, and the beam carries a uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. The dashed inner fibers are the reference fibers. Use the principle of virtual work to calculate the axial force in column $EB$ and the bending moment in the beam immediately to the right of joint $D$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;E,4,4;B,4,0" aste="A-D;D-E;E-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="D,1.5,0,F" carichi="D,E,0.5,0.5,q" tratteggi="0.15,0 0.15,3.85 3.85,3.85 3.85,0" quote="A,D,4 m,-0.8;D,E,4 m,-1.1" titolo="Portal frame with $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $D$ and $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on its crossbeam. Dashed lines indicate the inner reference fibers"}

Axial force in the column $EB$: parallel-link axial support at $S = (4, 2)$. The upper portion $ADES$ rotates about $A$ through angle $\delta\varphi$; the lower portion $SB$ rotates through the same angle. The roller prevents vertical displacement at $B$, so this portion rotates about $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;E,4,4;S,4,2,-;B,4,0" aste="A-D;D-E;E-B" interni="S,doppio-pendolo,0" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="D,1.5,0,F" carichi="D,E,0.5,0.5,q" etichette="4.5,2,S" punti="0,0,C_1,centro;4,0,C_2,centro" titolo="With an axial sliding connection at $S$ we have $C_1 = A$ and $C_2 = B$, and the two bodies rotate through the same angle"}

$$
\text{in}\ S:\quad \delta v_s = \delta\varphi\,(4 - 0) = 4\,\delta\varphi ,
\qquad
\delta v_d = \delta\varphi\,(4 - 4) = 0
$$
$$
\delta w_s = -\delta v_s = -4\,\delta\varphi ,
\qquad
\delta w_d = 0 ,
\qquad
\Delta w = \delta w_s - \delta w_d = -4\,\delta\varphi
$$
$$
\delta u_D = -\delta\varphi\,(4 - 0) = -4\,\delta\varphi ,
\qquad
\delta v(2) = 2\,\delta\varphi
$$
$$
\delta L = 10 \cdot (-4\,\delta\varphi) - 12 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi = -64\,\delta\varphi
$$
$$
N_{EB}\,\Delta w + \delta L = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
N_{EB} = -\frac{-64\,\delta\varphi}{-4\,\delta\varphi} = -16\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Moment in crossbeam at $D$: hinge at $D$. The column $AD$ rotates about $A$; the body $DEB$ has the center on the vertical through $B$ and on the line $AD$, parallel, then transposes horizontally.
$$
\delta\varphi_s = \delta\varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\delta\varphi_d = 0 ,
\qquad
\Delta\varphi = \delta\varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\delta u_D = -4\,\delta\varphi_1
$$
$$
\delta L = 10 \cdot (-4\,\delta\varphi_1) + 0 = -40\,\delta\varphi_1
$$
$$
M_D = -\frac{-40\,\delta\varphi_1}{\delta\varphi_1} = 40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Verification: from the horizontal translation of the entire $H_A = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$ frame portal; from rotation about $A$, $4\,V_B - 10 \cdot 4 - 12 \cdot 2 = 0$, then $V_B = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the column $EB$ is compressed by $16\,\mathrm{kN}$. Rotation of the column $AD$ around $D$:
$$
-(0 - 4) \cdot H_A + M_D = 4 \cdot (-10) + 40 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The column $EB$ has the vertical axis and runs from $E$ towards $B$, so the displacement along the axis is $\delta w = -\delta v$, and the segment that comes first, the one on the $E$ side, plays the role of the left segment. Axial parallel-link support requires the two bodies to rotate the same: the top rotates about the hinge $A$, the bottom around $B$, because the roller $B$ support blocks its vertical displacement and the horizontal displacement coincides with that of the top. The point $S$ rises as the point of the top and stays at the same elevation as the point of the bottom, so the two parts separate and the column stretches into $S$ of $\delta w_d - \delta w_s = 4\,\delta\varphi$. The work of the loads is negative for $\delta\varphi > 0$, and the force is **$N_{EB} = -16\,\mathrm{kN}$, in compression**. For the moment, a hinge is inserted at $D$: the column $AD$ rotates about $A$ and the rest of the portal frame moves horizontally, because its center is the point at infinity of the vertical. Along the $A$, $D$, $E$, $B$ path the inner fibers stand to the right of the travel direction, like the lower fibers of a beam traveled from left to right, so the same rule of signs applies. The vertical load on the crossbeam does no work, because the crossbeam moves horizontally, and **$M_D = 40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the inner fibers stretched**. The check uses the balance to the rotation of the column $AD$ around $D$, with the $M_D$ counterclockwise moment on the face of the column.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The inclined beam in the figure goes from the hinge $A = (0, 0)$ to the roller at $B = (4, 3)$, which moves on a horizontal plane; the beam is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long and forms with the horizontal the angle $\alpha$ with $\cos\alpha = \dfrac{4}{5}$ and $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{3}{5}$. It carries a uniform vertical load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per unit length of the horizontal projection. Calculate with the principle of virtual work the axial force at section $S = (1;\ 0.75)$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,1,0.75;B,4,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.8;4 0,B,3 m,0.8" linee="0,0 4,0" titolo="Inclined beam with $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ applied per unit horizontal projection"}

Parallel-link axial support at $S$. Both segments rotate through the same angle $\delta\varphi$; segment $AS$ rotates about $A$, and segment $SB$ undergoes the same motion plus translation $\delta w$ along the axis with unit vector $\vec{t} = \left( \dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5} \right)$.
$$
\delta v_B = 4\,\delta\varphi + \frac{3}{5}\,\delta w = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\delta w = -\frac{20}{3}\,\delta\varphi ,
\qquad
\Delta w = \delta w_s - \delta w_d = -\delta w = \frac{20}{3}\,\delta\varphi
$$
$$
\delta L_{AS} = -5 \cdot 0.5\,\delta\varphi = -2.5\,\delta\varphi
$$
$$
\delta L_{SB} = -15 \cdot \left( 2.5\,\delta\varphi + \frac{3}{5}\,\delta w \right) = -15 \cdot (2.5 - 4)\,\delta\varphi = 22.5\,\delta\varphi
$$
$$
N_S \cdot \frac{20}{3}\,\delta\varphi + 20\,\delta\varphi = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
N_S = -3\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Verification: from the horizontal $H_A = 0$ translation, from rotation about $B$, $-4\,V_A + 20 \cdot 2 = 0$, then $V_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$; $AS$ section translation along the axis, with the load $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ of the $0$ to $1\,\mathrm{m}$ projection:
$$
N_S + (V_A - 5) \cdot \frac{3}{5} = -3 + 5 \cdot \frac{3}{5} = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The parallel-link axial support leaves only the relative translation free along the axis of the beam, so the motion of the segment $SB$ is that of the segment $AS$ plus an $\delta w\,\vec{t}$ translation. $B$ roller support requires that $B$'s vertical displacement is zero; $AS$'s rotation would bring $B$ up by $4\,\delta\varphi$, and the translation along the axis must compensate it with its vertical component $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\delta w$. The load is divided into two parts, $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ with centroid in $x = 0.5$ and $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ with centroid in $x = 2.5$. The vertical displacement of the second part is the sum of the two contributions, $2.5\,\delta\varphi - 4\,\delta\varphi$. Total work is $20\,\delta\varphi$ and force is **$N_S = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$, in compression**. In verifying the net vertical force on the $AS$, $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ section upwards, it has $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ component along the axis, towards $B$, which the compressive force balances.

</div>
