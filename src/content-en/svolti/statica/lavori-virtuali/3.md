## Exercise 1

The simply supported beam shown has span $AB = 6\,\mathrm{m}$, a pin at $A$, and a roller at $B$. A downward force $F = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at point $C$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Use virtual work to find the bending moments at $C$ and at point $D$, a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.4,F" quote="A,C,2 m,0.9;C,D,2 m,0.9;D,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam with $F = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

Moment at $D$: hinge at $D$, segment $AD$ around $A$, segment $DB$ around $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" cerniere="D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.4,F" tratteggi="0,0 4,-0.6 6,0" titolo="With a hinge at $D$ the two segments rotate about $A$ and $B$. Dashed lines show the displacement for $\delta\varphi_s < 0$"}

$$
\delta v_D = 4\,\delta\varphi_s = (4 - 6)\,\delta\varphi_d
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\delta\varphi_d = -2\,\delta\varphi_s
$$
$$
\Delta\varphi = \delta\varphi_s - \delta\varphi_d = 3\,\delta\varphi_s ,
\qquad
\delta L = -18 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi_s = -36\,\delta\varphi_s
$$
$$
M_D\,\Delta\varphi + \delta L = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
M_D = -\frac{-36\,\delta\varphi_s}{3\,\delta\varphi_s} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Moment at $C$: hinge at $C$, at the point where force acts.
$$
\delta v_C = 2\,\delta\varphi_s = (2 - 6)\,\delta\varphi_d
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\delta\varphi_d = -\frac{1}{2}\,\delta\varphi_s
$$
$$
\Delta\varphi = \delta\varphi_s + \frac{1}{2}\,\delta\varphi_s = \frac{3}{2}\,\delta\varphi_s ,
\qquad
\delta L = -18 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi_s = -36\,\delta\varphi_s
$$
$$
M_C = -\frac{-36\,\delta\varphi_s}{\dfrac{3}{2}\,\delta\varphi_s} = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Verification: from the rotation of the whole beam around $A$, $6\,V_B - 18 \cdot 2 = 0$, then $V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$; rotation of the segment $DB$ around $D$:
$$
-M_D + V_B \cdot 2 = -12 + 6 \cdot 2 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To find the bending moment at a section, introduce a **hinge** there. The beam becomes a two-body mechanism in which the moment does work $M\,\Delta\varphi$, where $\Delta\varphi$ is the rotation of the left segment minus the rotation of the right segment. The left segment has a fixed pin at $A$ and rotates about $A$. The right segment has a roller at $B$ and its internal hinge lies on line $AB$, so its center is $B$. Relate the two rotations by requiring the same vertical displacement at the hinge when calculated from either segment. For $\delta\varphi_s < 0$, the hinge moves downward and the force does positive work. The downward corner in the displacement diagram corresponds to tension in the lower fibers. Indeed, **$M_D = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $M_C = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$** are positive. When the force acts at the hinge, use the hinge displacement to calculate its work: this displacement is the same for both segments. The moment reaches its maximum at $C$ because the diagram peaks under the concentrated force. To check, take moments for segment $DB$ about $D$. The moment at $D$ acts clockwise on the left face of $DB$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ with $AB = 5\,\mathrm{m}$, and an overhang $BC = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. A uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on $AB$, and a downward force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. Use virtual work to find the bending moment over support $B$ and at midpoint $E$ of $AB$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;E,2.5,0;B,5,0;C,7,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="C,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,E,2.5 m,0.9;E,B,2.5 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam with $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $AB$ and $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

Moment at $B$: with the pin at $B$ the section $AB$ stays still and the overhang rotates about $B$.
$$
\delta\varphi_s = 0 ,
\qquad
\delta\varphi_d = \delta\varphi ,
\qquad
\Delta\varphi = -\delta\varphi ,
\qquad
\delta v_C = 2\,\delta\varphi
$$
$$
M_B \cdot (-\delta\varphi) - 10 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
M_B = -20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Moment at $E$: hinge at $E = (2.5;\ 0)$, segment $AE$ around $A$, segment $EC$ around $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;E,2.5,0;B,5,0;C,7,0" aste="A-C" cerniere="E" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="C,0,-1.5,F" tratteggi="0,0 2.5,-0.75 5,0 7,0.6" titolo="With a hinge at $E$ the overhang rises as $E$ moves down. Dashed lines show the displacement for $\delta\varphi_s < 0$"}

$$
2.5\,\delta\varphi_s = (2.5 - 5)\,\delta\varphi_d
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\delta\varphi_d = -\delta\varphi_s ,
\qquad
\Delta\varphi = 2\,\delta\varphi_s
$$
$$
\delta v(1.25) = 1.25\,\delta\varphi_s ,
\qquad
\delta v(3.75) = -\delta\varphi_s\,(3.75 - 5) = 1.25\,\delta\varphi_s ,
\qquad
\delta v_C = -\delta\varphi_s\,(7 - 5) = -2\,\delta\varphi_s
$$
$$
\delta L = -15 \cdot 1.25\,\delta\varphi_s - 15 \cdot 1.25\,\delta\varphi_s + 10 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi_s = -17.5\,\delta\varphi_s
$$
$$
M_E = -\frac{-17.5\,\delta\varphi_s}{2\,\delta\varphi_s} = 8.75\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Verification: from the rotation of the whole beam around $B$, $-5\,V_A + 30 \cdot 2.5 - 10 \cdot 2 = 0$, then $V_A = 11\,\mathrm{kN}$; rotation of the segment $AE$ around $E$:
$$
-V_A \cdot 2.5 + 15 \cdot 1.25 + M_E = -27.5 + 18.75 + 8.75 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

With a hinge at support $B$, segment $AB$ retains its pin at $A$ and its roller at $B$. It is therefore statically determinate and remains stationary. Only the overhang moves, rotating about $B$. The relative rotation is $\Delta\varphi = 0 - \delta\varphi$, and the load on $AB$ does no work. This gives $M_B = -20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The negative sign indicates tension in the upper fibers, as expected over the support of a loaded overhang. For section $E$, divide the distributed load into two halves because they act on different bodies. Each half has resultant $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ at its own midpoint, at $x = 1.25$ and $x = 3.75$, respectively. As $E$ moves down, both halves move down while the overhang moves up. Force $F$ therefore reduces the moment in the span. The result is **$M_E = 8.75\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension**. To check, take moments for segment $AE$ about $E$, with $M_E$ acting counterclockwise on the right face of $AE$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The simply supported beam shown has span $AB = 8\,\mathrm{m}$. A downward force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and a counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at $D$, a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Use virtual work to find the bending moment immediately to the left and right of $D$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.6,F" coppie="D,1,ℳ" quote="A,C,2 m,0.9;C,D,4 m,0.9;D,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam with $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$ and counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $D$"}

Hinge at $D$, segment $AD$ around $A$, segment $DB$ around $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" cerniere="D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.6,F" coppie="D,1,ℳ" tratteggi="0,0 6,-0.9 8,0" titolo="With a hinge at $D$, $\delta\varphi_d = -3\,\delta\varphi_s$. Dashed lines show the displacement for $\delta\varphi_s < 0$"}

$$
6\,\delta\varphi_s = (6 - 8)\,\delta\varphi_d
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\delta\varphi_d = -3\,\delta\varphi_s ,
\qquad
\Delta\varphi = 4\,\delta\varphi_s
$$

Immediately to the left of $D$ the couple acts on the right segment:
$$
\delta L = -12 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi_s + 24\,\delta\varphi_d = -24\,\delta\varphi_s - 72\,\delta\varphi_s = -96\,\delta\varphi_s
$$
$$
M_{D}^{-} = -\frac{-96\,\delta\varphi_s}{4\,\delta\varphi_s} = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Immediately to the right of $D$ the couple acts on the left segment:
$$
\delta L = -12 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi_s + 24\,\delta\varphi_s = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
M_{D}^{+} = 0
$$

Verification: from the rotation of the whole beam around $B$, $-8\,V_A + 12 \cdot 6 + 24 = 0$, then $V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$; rotation around $D$ of the segment $AD$ without the couple:
$$
-V_A \cdot 6 + 12 \cdot 4 + M_{D}^{-} = -72 + 48 + 24 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the section where a concentrated couple acts the bending moment has a jump, so the section immediately to the left and the one immediately to the right of the couple are distinguished. Inserting the hinge immediately to the left of $D$, the couple stays on the right section and does the $\mathcal{M}\,\delta\varphi_d$ work; by inserting it immediately to the right, the couple stays on the left section and does the $\mathcal{M}\,\delta\varphi_s$ work. The rotations are the same in the two cases, because the geometry of the mechanism does not change. You get **$M_D^- = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $M_D^+ = 0$**: the counterclockwise couple decreases the moment of $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ by going from left to right. The null value on the right is also explained by the equilibrium: the reaction at $B$ is $V_B = F - V_A = 0$, so the segment $DB$ is unloaded. The check uses the moment equilibrium around $D$ of the segment $AD$ that ends just before the couple.

</div>
