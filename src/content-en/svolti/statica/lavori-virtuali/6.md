## Exercise 1

The three-hinged portal frame in the figure has the fixed $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (8, 0)$ hinges and the internal hinge $C = (4, 4)$ in the center of the crossbeam $DE$, with $D = (0, 4)$ and $E = (8, 4)$ (coordinates in meters). At $G = (2, 4)$ the force $F = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward, at $D$ the force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Calculate with the principle of virtual work the reactions $H_B$ and $V_B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;G,2,4;C,4,4;E,8,4;B,8,0" aste="A-D;D-C;C-E;E-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="G,0,-1.6,F;D,1.6,0,P" quote="D,G,2 m,-0.6;G,C,2 m,-0.6;C,E,4 m,-0.6;E,B,4 m,0.7" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with $F = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $G$ and horizontal force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $D$"}

$H_B$ thrust: in $B$ you leave the horizontal displacement (roller support) free. $C_1 = A$, $C_{12} = C$; $C_2$ lies on the vertical through $B$ and on the line $AC$, $y = x$, then $C_2 = (8, 8)$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;G,2,4;C,4,4;E,8,4;B,8,0" aste="A-D;D-C;C-E;E-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reazioni="B,1.6,0,H_B" forze="G,0,-1.6,F;D,1.6,0,P" tratteggi="0,0 8,8;8,0 8,8" punti="8,8,C_2,centro" titolo="With a roller at $B$, body $CEB$ rotates about $C_2 = (8, 8)$"}

$$
\text{body 1:}\quad \delta u = -4\,\delta\varphi_1 \ \text{and}\ \delta v = 4\,\delta\varphi_1 \ \text{in}\ C ,
\qquad
\text{body 2:}\quad \delta u = 4\,\delta\varphi_2 \ \text{and}\ \delta v = -4\,\delta\varphi_2 \ \text{in}\ C
$$
$$
\delta\varphi_2 = -\delta\varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\delta u_B = -\delta\varphi_2\,(0 - 8) = -8\,\delta\varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\delta v_G = 2\,\delta\varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\delta u_D = -4\,\delta\varphi_1
$$
$$
H_B \cdot (-8\,\delta\varphi_1) - 20 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi_1 + 10 \cdot (-4\,\delta\varphi_1) = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
H_B = -\frac{80}{8} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$V_B$ reaction: in $B$ the vertical displacement is left free. $C_2$ lies on the horizontal through $B$ and on the line $AC$, then $C_2 = A = C_1$: the two bodies rotate together around $A$ of $\delta\varphi$.
$$
\delta v_B = 8\,\delta\varphi ,
\qquad
\delta v_G = 2\,\delta\varphi ,
\qquad
\delta u_D = -4\,\delta\varphi
$$
$$
V_B \cdot 8\,\delta\varphi - 20 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi + 10 \cdot (-4\,\delta\varphi) = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
V_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check with the rotation of the body $CEB$ around the hinge $C$, on which only reactions at $B$ act:
$$
(8 - 4) \cdot V_B - (0 - 4) \cdot H_B = 4 \cdot 10 + 4 \cdot (-10) = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the portal frame, the load points are not all at the same elevation, so horizontal displacements, $\delta u = -\delta\varphi\,(y - y_C)$, are also needed, which depend on the vertical distance from the center. Leaving the horizontal displacement of $B$ free, the center of the right body is found with the first theorem of kinematic chains, on the line that passes through $A$ and the hinge $C$; the displacement of $C$ calculated with the two bodies gives $\delta\varphi_2 = -\delta\varphi_1$. The point $B$ stands $8\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C_2$, so it moves horizontally by $-8\,\delta\varphi_1$. The thrust is **$H_B = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left**. Leaving the vertical displacement of $B$, the center $C_2$ coincides with $C_1 = A$; The hinge $C$ has the same displacement on the two bodies, which rotates about the same point, so the rotations are equal and the portal frame rotates like a single body. You get **$V_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward**. The check uses the auxiliary null moment equation in the hinge $C$ for the right body.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The three-hinged portal frame in the figure has the pin support $A = (0, 0)$ at the base of the column $AD$, of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and the pin support $B = (6, 2)$ at the base of the column $EB$, shorter, with $E = (6, 4)$; the internal hinge $C = (3, 4)$ splits the crossbeam $DE$ in half. The $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ uniform load acts on the crossbeam. Calculate with the principle of virtual work the reactions $H_A$ and $V_A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;C,3,4;E,6,4;B,6,2" aste="A-D;D-C;C-E;E-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" carichi="D,E,0.6,0.6,q" quote="D,C,3 m,-1.2;C,E,3 m,-1.2;A,D,4 m,0.7;E,B,2 m,0.7" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with supports at different elevations and $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the crossbeam"}

Half the load has resultant $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ (body $1$) and $x = 4.5\,\mathrm{m}$ (body $2$).

$H_A$ reaction: $A$ leaves horizontal displacement free. $C_2 = B$; $C_1$ lies on the vertical through $A$ and on the line $BC$, then $C_1 = (0, 6)$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;C,3,4;E,6,4;B,6,2" aste="A-D;D-C;C-E;E-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera" reazioni="A,1.5,0,H_A" carichi="D,E,0.6,0.6,q" tratteggi="0,0 0,6;6,2 0,6" punti="0,6,C_1,centro" titolo="With a roller at $A$, body $ADC$ rotates about $C_1 = (0, 6)$ on line $BC$"}

$$
C:\quad \text{body 1}\ (\delta u, \delta v) = (2\,\delta\varphi_1,\ 3\,\delta\varphi_1) ,
\qquad
\text{body 2}\ (\delta u, \delta v) = (-2\,\delta\varphi_2,\ -3\,\delta\varphi_2)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\delta\varphi_2 = -\delta\varphi_1
$$
$$
\delta u_A = -\delta\varphi_1\,(0 - 6) = 6\,\delta\varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\delta v(1.5) = 1.5\,\delta\varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\delta v(4.5) = -\delta\varphi_1\,(4.5 - 6) = 1.5\,\delta\varphi_1
$$
$$
H_A \cdot 6\,\delta\varphi_1 - 18 \cdot 1.5\,\delta\varphi_1 - 18 \cdot 1.5\,\delta\varphi_1 = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
H_A = \frac{54}{6} = 9\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

To find reaction $V_A$, release the vertical displacement at $A$. Point $C_1$ lies both on the horizontal through $A$ and on line $BC$, with $y = 2 + \dfrac{2}{3}\,(6 - x)$, so $C_1 = (9, 0)$.
$$
C:\quad \text{body 1}\ (\delta u, \delta v) = (-4\,\delta\varphi_1,\ -6\,\delta\varphi_1) ,
\qquad
\text{body 2}\ (\delta u, \delta v) = (-2\,\delta\varphi_2,\ -3\,\delta\varphi_2)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\delta\varphi_2 = 2\,\delta\varphi_1
$$
$$
\delta v_A = (0 - 9)\,\delta\varphi_1 = -9\,\delta\varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\delta v(1.5) = (1.5 - 9)\,\delta\varphi_1 = -7.5\,\delta\varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\delta v(4.5) = 2\,\delta\varphi_1\,(4.5 - 6) = -3\,\delta\varphi_1
$$
$$
V_A \cdot (-9\,\delta\varphi_1) - 18 \cdot (-7.5\,\delta\varphi_1) - 18 \cdot (-3\,\delta\varphi_1) = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
V_A = \frac{135 + 54}{9} = 21\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check with the rotation of the entire portal frame around $B$; the total load is $36\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $(3, 4)$:
$$
(0 - 6) \cdot V_A - (0 - 2) \cdot H_A + 36 \cdot (6 - 3) = -126 + 18 + 108 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Supports are at different altitudes, and the rotation centers of the mechanism do not stand on the axis of the beam. For $H_A$ the right body rotates about the pin support $B$, and the center of the left body lies on the vertical through $A$, because the roller support leaves $A$ only the horizontal displacement, and on the line through $B$ and the hinge $C$. The line $BC$ goes up by $2\,\mathrm{m}$ every $3\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left and meets the axis $x = 0$ in $y = 6$. The displacement of $C$ calculated with the two bodies gives $\delta\varphi_2 = -\delta\varphi_1$, and the two halves of the load have the same vertical displacement. The result is **$H_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$, to the right**. For $V_A$ the center of the left body is the point $(9, 0)$, to the right of the whole structure: all points of the body $1$ stand to the left of the center and lower for $\delta\varphi_1 > 0$. **$V_A = 21\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward** follows. Verification writes the moment equilibrium around $B$, which was not used in the calculation in calculations; $H_A$ has $2\,\mathrm{m}$ arm about $B$ because $A$ stands $2\,\mathrm{m}$ lower.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The three-hinged portal frame shown has fixed pins $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (6, 0)$, columns of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and internal hinge $C = (2, 4)$ on crossbeam $DE$, with $D = (0, 4)$ and $E = (6, 4)$. At $D$, force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. At $E$, couple $\mathcal{M} = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts clockwise. Use virtual work to find the reactions at $B$ and then those at $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;C,2,4;E,6,4,-;B,6,0" aste="A-D;D-C;C-E;E-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="D,1.4,0,P" coppie="E,-1,ℳ" etichette="6.35,3.6,E" quote="D,C,2 m,-0.6;C,E,4 m,-0.6;E,B,4 m,0.8" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $D$ and clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $E$"}

$H_B$ thrust: roller support at $B$; $C_2$ lies on vertical $x = 6$ and line $AC$, $y = 2x$, then $C_2 = (6, 12)$.
$$
C:\quad \text{body 1}\ (\delta u, \delta v) = (-4\,\delta\varphi_1,\ 2\,\delta\varphi_1) ,
\qquad
\text{body 2}\ (\delta u, \delta v) = (8\,\delta\varphi_2,\ -4\,\delta\varphi_2)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\delta\varphi_2 = -\frac{1}{2}\,\delta\varphi_1
$$
$$
\delta u_B = -\delta\varphi_2\,(0 - 12) = 12\,\delta\varphi_2 = -6\,\delta\varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\delta u_D = -4\,\delta\varphi_1
$$
$$
H_B \cdot (-6\,\delta\varphi_1) + 12 \cdot (-4\,\delta\varphi_1) + (-24) \cdot \left( -\frac{1}{2}\,\delta\varphi_1 \right) = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
H_B = \frac{-48 + 12}{6} = -6\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$V_B$ reaction: vertical displacement is free in $B$; $C_2$ lies on the horizontal $y = 0$ and the line $AC$, then $C_2 = A$ and the portal frame rotates like a single body around $A$.
$$
\delta v_B = 6\,\delta\varphi ,
\qquad
\delta u_D = -4\,\delta\varphi
$$
$$
V_B \cdot 6\,\delta\varphi + 12 \cdot (-4\,\delta\varphi) + (-24)\,\delta\varphi = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
V_B = \frac{48 + 24}{6} = 12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Reactions at $A$ from translation equations:
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + P + H_B = 0 \\
V_A + V_B = 0
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
H_A = -12 + 6 = -6\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = -12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check with the body $ADC$ rotation around the hinge $C$:
$$
(0 - 2) \cdot V_A - (0 - 4) \cdot H_A - (4 - 4) \cdot P = -2 \cdot (-12) + 4 \cdot (-6) - 0 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The couple does the $\mathcal{M}\,\delta\varphi$ work with the rotation of the body on which it acts, here the right body; the clockwise couple equals $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. For thrusting the center $C_2$ stands $12\,\mathrm{m}$ above $B$, on the line connecting $A$ with the hinge $C$, and the displacement of $C$ on the two bodies gives $\delta\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,\delta\varphi_1$. The force $P$ and the couple perform opposite-sign jobs, and you get **$H_B = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left**. For $V_B$ the center of the right body coincides with $A$, the center of the left body, and the two rotations are the same: the portal frame rotates about $A$, and **$V_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward**. Reactions at $A$ follow from the two translation equations to be met together, horizontal and vertical: **$H_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward**. Vertical reactions form a counterclockwise couple of $72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, which balances the clockwise moment of $P$ about the base, $48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and the applied couple. Verification writes the null moment in the hinge $C$ for the left body; the force $P$ has line of action for $C$ and gives no moment.

</div>
