## Exercise 1

The Gerber beam shown consists of segment $AC$, with a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$, and segment $CD$, connected to the first by internal hinge $C$ and supported by a roller at $D$. The horizontal coordinates are $A = 0$, $B = 6\,\mathrm{m}$, $C = 8\,\mathrm{m}$, and $D = 12\,\mathrm{m}$. Two downward forces act: $F_1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $E$, a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and $F_2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $G$, a distance $10\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Use virtual work to find the reactions $R_D$, $R_B$, and $V_A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;E,3,0;B,6,0;C,8,0;G,10,0;D,12,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" forze="E,0,-2,F_1;G,0,-2,F_2" quote="A,E,3 m,1.1;E,B,3 m,1.1;B,C,2 m,1.1;C,G,2 m,1.1;G,D,2 m,1.1" titolo="Gerber beam with $F_1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $E$ and $F_2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $G$"}

To find reaction $R_D$, remove the roller at $D$. Segment $AC$ remains stationary, and segment $CD$ rotates about $C$ through angle $\delta\varphi_2$.
$$
\delta v_G = 2\,\delta\varphi_2 ,
\qquad
\delta v_D = 4\,\delta\varphi_2
$$
$$
R_D \cdot 4\,\delta\varphi_2 - 12 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi_2 = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
R_D = 6\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

To find reaction $R_B$, remove the roller at $B$. Then $C_1 = A$ and $C_{12} = C$. Point $C_2$ lies on the vertical through $D$ and on line $AC$, so $C_2 = D$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;E,3,0;B,6,0;C,8,0;G,10,0;D,12,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" reazioni="B,0,1.6,R_B" forze="E,0,-2,F_1;G,0,-2,F_2" tratteggi="0,0 8,1.6 12,0" titolo="With the roller at $B$ removed, segment $AC$ rotates about $A$ and segment $CD$ about $D$. Dashed lines show the displacement for $\delta\varphi_1 > 0$"}

$$
\delta v_C = 8\,\delta\varphi_1 = (8 - 12)\,\delta\varphi_2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\delta\varphi_2 = -2\,\delta\varphi_1
$$
$$
\delta v_E = 3\,\delta\varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\delta v_B = 6\,\delta\varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\delta v_G = (10 - 12)\,\delta\varphi_2 = 4\,\delta\varphi_1
$$
$$
R_B \cdot 6\,\delta\varphi_1 - 10 \cdot 3\,\delta\varphi_1 - 12 \cdot 4\,\delta\varphi_1 = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
R_B = \frac{30 + 48}{6} = 13\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$V_A$ reaction: $A$ remains a roller on a vertical surface; $C_1$ lies on the horizontal through $A$ and on the vertical through $B$, then $C_1 = B$, and $C_2 = D$.
$$
\delta v_C = (8 - 6)\,\delta\varphi_1 = (8 - 12)\,\delta\varphi_2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\delta\varphi_2 = -\frac{1}{2}\,\delta\varphi_1
$$
$$
\delta v_A = -6\,\delta\varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\delta v_E = -3\,\delta\varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\delta v_G = (10 - 12)\,\delta\varphi_2 = \delta\varphi_1
$$
$$
V_A \cdot (-6\,\delta\varphi_1) - 10 \cdot (-3\,\delta\varphi_1) - 12\,\delta\varphi_1 = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
V_A = \frac{30 - 12}{6} = 3\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check vertical force equilibrium of the whole beam:
$$
V_A + R_B + R_D - F_1 - F_2 = 3 + 13 + 6 - 10 - 12 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **principle of virtual work for rigid body systems** is applied to the entire beam, without disassembling it, because the forces that the two segments exchange in the hinge $C$ perform zero overall work. By removing the roller $D$ support, the segment $AC$ retains its two constraints and stays still, so $C$ is fixed and the segment $CD$ rotates about $C$: only $F_2$ does work. By removing the roller support at $B$ both segments move, with the centers at $A$ and $D$, and the displacement of $C$ calculated with the two segments binds the rotations. For $\delta\varphi_1 > 0$, the hinge $C$ rises and the segment $CD$ rotates clockwise around $D$, so $G$ also rises and $F_2$ does negative work, like $F_1$. For $V_A$ the segment $AC$ rotates about $B$: the point $E$, to the left of $B$, lowers when $A$ lowers, while $C$ rises. The results are **$R_D = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, $R_B = 13\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$, all upwards**; $H_A = 0$, because the loads are vertical. The vertical translation of the entire beam, not used in calculations, gives zero.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The Gerber beam in the figure is formed by the section $AB$, framed at $A$, and the section $BD$, connected to the first by the internal hinge $B$ and resting on a roller support at $C$; the horizontal coordinates are $A = 0$, $B = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, $C = 8\,\mathrm{m}$, $D = 10\,\mathrm{m}$. They act the force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting downwards at $E$, at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and the uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $BD$. Calculate with the principle of virtual work $R_C$, the fixed support moment $M_A$ and the reaction $V_A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;E,2,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,10,0" aste="A-B;B-D" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;C,carrello" forze="E,0,-1.8,F" carichi="B,D,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,E,2 m,1;E,B,2 m,1;B,C,4 m,1;C,D,2 m,1" titolo="Fixed-end Gerber beam with $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $E$ and $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $BD$"}

The load on $BD$ has resultant $Q = 4 \cdot 6 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 7\,\mathrm{m}$.

To find reaction $R_C$, remove the roller. The fixed segment remains stationary, and $BD$ rotates about $B$.
$$
R_C \cdot 4\,\delta\varphi_2 - 24 \cdot 3\,\delta\varphi_2 = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
R_C = 18\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Moment $M_A$: fixed support becomes a hinge; $C_1 = A$, $C_{12} = B$, $C_2 = C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;E,2,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,10,0" aste="A-B;B-D" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" coppie="A,1,M_A" forze="E,0,-1.8,F" carichi="B,D,0.6,0.6,q" tratteggi="0,0 4,1.2 8,0 10,-0.6" titolo="With a hinge at $A$, segment $AB$ rotates about $A$ and segment $BD$ about $C$. Dashed lines show the displacement for $\delta\varphi_1 > 0$"}

$$
4\,\delta\varphi_1 = (4 - 8)\,\delta\varphi_2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\delta\varphi_2 = -\delta\varphi_1
$$
$$
\delta v_E = 2\,\delta\varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\delta v(7) = (7 - 8)\,\delta\varphi_2 = \delta\varphi_1
$$
$$
M_A\,\delta\varphi_1 - 10 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi_1 - 24\,\delta\varphi_1 = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
M_A = 44\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

To find reaction $V_A$, replace the fixed support with a pair of parallel horizontal links. Segment $AB$ translates vertically by $\delta v$, and $BD$ rotates about $C$.
$$
\delta v_B = \delta v = (4 - 8)\,\delta\varphi_2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\delta\varphi_2 = -\frac{\delta v}{4} ,
\qquad
\delta v(7) = (7 - 8)\,\delta\varphi_2 = \frac{\delta v}{4}
$$
$$
V_A\,\delta v - 10\,\delta v - 24 \cdot \frac{\delta v}{4} = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
V_A = 10 + 6 = 16\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

It checks with the vertical translation of the entire beam and with the rotation of the section $AB$ around $B$, where $AB$ receives from $BD$ the force $Q - R_C = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downwards:
$$
V_A + R_C - F - Q = 16 + 18 - 10 - 24 = 0 ,
\qquad
M_A - V_A \cdot 4 + F \cdot 2 = 44 - 64 + 20 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The section $AB$ is the supporting beam, fixed and therefore statically determinate by itself; the section $BD$ is the beam suspended span, which rests on $AB$ in the hinge $B$. By removing the roller $C$ support, the supporting member remains stationary and the range rotates about $B$. For fixed support moment you put a hinge at $A$: the $C_1 = A$ and $C_{12} = B$ centers stand on the axis of the beam, so $C_2$ is the roller $C$ support, and the hinge $B$ rising rotates $BD$ clockwise around $C$. The center of the load, in $x = 7$, stands to the left of $C$ and also rises. The result is **$M_A = 44\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise**. For $V_A$, parallel-link support leaves the section $AB$ with only vertical translation, and the range rotates about $C$ with $B$ moving by $\delta v$. You get **$V_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward**, and **$R_C = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward**. The second verification equation uses the rotation of the supporting member around the hinge $B$, where the force transmitted by the suspended span has zero moment arm.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The Gerber beam in the figure consists of three segments: $AC$, with a hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $B$; $CE$, connected to $AC$ by the internal hinge $C$ and resting on a roller support at $D$; $EG$, connected to $CE$ by the internal hinge $E$ and resting on a roller support at $G$. The axes are $A = 0$, $B = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, $C = 6\,\mathrm{m}$, $D = 10\,\mathrm{m}$, $E = 12\,\mathrm{m}$, $G = 16\,\mathrm{m}$. They act the uniform $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load on $AB$ and the force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ down in $x = 14\,\mathrm{m}$. Calculate with the principle of virtual work the reactions of $B$ and $D$ roller supports.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0;E,12,0;G,16,0" aste="A-C;C-E;E-G" cerniere="C;E" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello;G,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.7,0.7,q" forze="14 0,0,-2.4,P" quote="A,B,4 m,1.2;B,C,2 m,1.2;C,D,4 m,1.2;D,E,2 m,1.2;E,14 0,2 m,1.2;14 0,G,2 m,1.2" titolo="Three-segment Gerber beam with $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $AB$ and $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $x = 14\,\mathrm{m}$"}

To find reaction $R_B$, remove the roller at $B$. The centers are $C_1 = A$, $C_2 = D$, and $C_3 = G$ because each center lies both on the vertical through its roller and on the beam axis.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0;E,12,0;G,16,0" aste="A-C;C-E;E-G" cerniere="C;E" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello;G,carrello" reazioni="B,0,1.8,R_B" carichi="A,B,0.7,0.7,q" forze="14 0,0,-2.4,P" tratteggi="0,0 6,1.8 10,0 12,-0.9 16,0" titolo="With the roller at $B$ removed, the three segments rotate about $A$, $D$, and $G$. Dashed lines show the displacement for $\delta\varphi_1 > 0$"}

$$
\delta v_C = 6\,\delta\varphi_1 = (6 - 10)\,\delta\varphi_2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\delta\varphi_2 = -\frac{3}{2}\,\delta\varphi_1
$$
$$
\delta v_E = (12 - 10)\,\delta\varphi_2 = -3\,\delta\varphi_1 = (12 - 16)\,\delta\varphi_3
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\delta\varphi_3 = \frac{3}{4}\,\delta\varphi_1
$$
$$
\delta v(2) = 2\,\delta\varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\delta v_B = 4\,\delta\varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\delta v(14) = (14 - 16)\,\delta\varphi_3 = -\frac{3}{2}\,\delta\varphi_1
$$
$$
R_B \cdot 4\,\delta\varphi_1 - 12 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi_1 - 12 \cdot \left( -\frac{3}{2}\,\delta\varphi_1 \right) = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
R_B = \frac{24 - 18}{4} = 1.5\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

To find reaction $R_D$, remove the roller at $D$. Segment $AC$ remains stationary, $CE$ rotates about $C$, and $EG$ rotates about $G$.
$$
\delta v_E = 6\,\delta\varphi_2 = (12 - 16)\,\delta\varphi_3
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\delta\varphi_3 = -\frac{3}{2}\,\delta\varphi_2
$$
$$
\delta v_D = 4\,\delta\varphi_2 ,
\qquad
\delta v(14) = (14 - 16)\,\delta\varphi_3 = 3\,\delta\varphi_2
$$
$$
R_D \cdot 4\,\delta\varphi_2 - 12 \cdot 3\,\delta\varphi_2 = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
R_D = 9\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check with the static method, from the rightmost section to the left. $EG$ rotation around $E$: $4\,R_G - 12 \cdot 2 = 0$, then $R_G = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $EG$ receives at $E$ the force $12 - 6 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upwards, which on $CE$ becomes $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downwards. $CE$ rotation around $C$:
$$
R_D \cdot 4 - 6 \cdot 6 = 9 \cdot 4 - 36 = 0
$$
The force that $CE$ receives at $C$ is $6 - 9 = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ down, so on $AC$ it acts at $C$ a force of $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ up. $AC$ rotation around $A$:
$$
R_B \cdot 4 - 12 \cdot 2 + 3 \cdot 6 = 6 - 24 + 18 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In a chain of several segments each absolute center is found with the theorems of the kinematic chains: $C_2$ lies on the vertical through $D$ and on the line through $C_1$ and $C_{12}$, that is, on the axis of the beam, and the same applies to $C_3$. The rotations bind from one segment to the next, with the $C$ and $E$ hinges moving calculated on the two segments they connect. For $\delta\varphi_1 > 0$ the hinge $C$ gets up, the hinge $E$ gets down and the section in $x = 14$ also gets down, so the force $P$ does positive work, of opposite sign to that of the load on $AB$. The two equations give **$R_B = 1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $R_D = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$, both upwards**. A load on the third segment modifies the reaction at $B$ through two hinges, and the static method requires disassembling the beam and solving the three segments one after the other, while with virtual work an equation is all it takes. The check runs right down the static road and finds both values.

</div>
