## Exercise 1

The cantilever shown has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and is fixed at $A$. A uniform load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over its full length, and a downward force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at its free end $B$. Use virtual work to find the fixed-support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.45,0.45,q" forze="B,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8" titolo="Cantilever with $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$"}

The distributed load has resultant $Q = 5 \cdot 4 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$.

Moment $M_A$: fixed support becomes a hinge and cantilever rotates about $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera" coppie="A,1,M_A" carichi="A,B,0.45,0.45,q" forze="B,0,-1.2,F" tratteggi="0,0 4,-0.8" titolo="Introducing a hinge at $A$ lets the cantilever rotate about $A$. Dashed lines show the displacement for $\delta\varphi < 0$"}

$$
M_A\,\delta\varphi - 20 \cdot 2\,\delta\varphi - 8 \cdot 4\,\delta\varphi = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
M_A = 40 + 32 = 72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

$V_A$ reaction: fixed support becomes a parallel-link support with horizontal links and the cantilever translates vertically of $\delta v$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,180" reazioni="A,0,1,V_A" carichi="A,B,0.45,0.45,q" forze="B,0,-1.2,F" tratteggi="0,-0.6 4,-0.6" titolo="With a parallel-link support at $A$ the cantilever translates vertically without rotating"}

$$
V_A\,\delta v - 20\,\delta v - 8\,\delta v = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
V_A = 28\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$H_A$ reaction: with a parallel-link support to vertical links the cantilever moves horizontally; no load has horizontal component, so $H_A = 0$.

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
M_A - V_A \cdot 4 + Q \cdot 2 = 72 - 112 + 40 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The fixed support supplies three reaction components. To find each one, release only its corresponding displacement. For the moment $M_A$, replace the fixed support with a pin. The cantilever rotates about $A$, and the work of $M_A$, positive counterclockwise, is $M_A\,\delta\varphi$. The downward loads move down when $\delta\varphi < 0$, so their work is negative for $\delta\varphi > 0$. The support moment is therefore **$M_A = 72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise**. For $V_A$, use a pair of horizontal parallel links to prevent rotation and horizontal translation. The beam can move only vertically, so every point has the same displacement $\delta v$ and the loads do work equal to their sum times $\delta v$. This gives **$V_A = 28\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward**. Check by taking moments about the free end $B$, where force $F$ has zero moment arm.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The cantilever shown has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and is fixed at $A$. At end $B$, a force of magnitude $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward and to the left, with components $F_x = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $F_y = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$. A clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at midpoint $C$. Use virtual work to find the fixed-support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,1.5,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,-0.6,-0.8,F" coppie="C,-1,ℳ" quote="A,C,1.5 m,1.1;C,B,1.5 m,1.1" titolo="Cantilever under inclined force $F$ at $B$ ($F_x = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$, $F_y = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$) and clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$"}

$H_A$ reaction: parallel-link support to vertical links, horizontal $\delta u$ translation.
$$
H_A\,\delta u + F_x\,\delta u = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
H_A = -F_x = 6\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$V_A$ reaction: parallel-link support to horizontal links, vertical translation $\delta v$.
$$
V_A\,\delta v + F_y\,\delta v = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
V_A = -F_y = 8\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Moment $M_A$: hinge at $A$, rotation $\delta\varphi$ around $A$.
$$
\delta u_B = -\delta\varphi\,(0 - 0) = 0 ,
\qquad
\delta v_B = 3\,\delta\varphi
$$
$$
M_A\,\delta\varphi + (-6) \cdot 0 + (-8) \cdot 3\,\delta\varphi + (-12)\,\delta\varphi = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
M_A = 24 + 12 = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Check moment equilibrium about $B$:
$$
M_A - V_A \cdot 3 - \mathcal{M} = 36 - 8 \cdot 3 - 12 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Rigid translations give the force equilibrium equations directly. The inclined force does work $F_x\,\delta u$ in a horizontal translation and $F_y\,\delta v$ in a vertical translation. The resulting reactions **$H_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward** balance the components of the force. During rotation about $A$, point $B$ is level with the center, so $\delta u_B = 0$. Only $F_y$ does work. With counterclockwise positive, the applied clockwise couple is $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and its work is $-12\,\delta\varphi$. This gives **$M_A = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise**. For the check, take moments about $B$ to eliminate the inclined force. The line of action of $H_A$ passes through $B$, giving zero moment, whereas $V_A$ acts clockwise with moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The beam shown has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, a pin at $A$, and a roller at $B$ on a surface inclined at $45^\circ$. The roller reaction acts upward and to the left. A uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the entire beam, and a horizontal force $F = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the left at $B$. Use virtual work to find the reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,315" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" forze="B,-1.4,0,F" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with an inclined support at $B$ under $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and horizontal force $F = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$"}

Set $A = (0, 0)$; distributed load has resultant $Q = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$. The reaction of the roller support is $R_B$ with $\vec{n} = \left( -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right)$ direction.

$R_B$ reaction: removes the roller support, rotation around $A$.
$$
\delta\vec{s}_B = (0,\ 6\,\delta\varphi) ,
\qquad
\vec{n} \cdot \delta\vec{s}_B = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \cdot 6\,\delta\varphi = 3\sqrt{2}\,\delta\varphi
$$
$$
R_B \cdot 3\sqrt{2}\,\delta\varphi - 24 \cdot 3\,\delta\varphi - 5 \cdot 0 = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
R_B = \frac{72}{3\sqrt{2}} = 12\sqrt{2} \approx 16.97\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$H_A$ reaction: $A$ remains a roller on a horizontal surface; center $C$ lies on the vertical through $A$ and the normal on the plane in $B$, $C = (0, 6)$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello,315" reazioni="A,1.3,0,H_A" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" forze="B,-1.4,0,F" tratteggi="0,0 0,6 6,0;0.6,0 6.6,0.6" punti="0,6,C,centro" titolo="Releasing $A$ horizontally lets the beam rotate about $C = (0, 6)$. Dashed lines show the displacement for $\delta\varphi > 0$"}

$$
\delta u = -\delta\varphi\,(y - 6) ,
\qquad
\delta v = \delta\varphi\,x
$$
$$
\delta u_A = 6\,\delta\varphi ,
\qquad
\delta u_B = 6\,\delta\varphi ,
\qquad
\delta v(3) = 3\,\delta\varphi
$$
$$
H_A \cdot 6\,\delta\varphi - 5 \cdot 6\,\delta\varphi - 24 \cdot 3\,\delta\varphi = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
H_A = \frac{72 + 30}{6} = 17\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$V_A$ reaction: $A$ remains a roller on a vertical surface; the center lies on the horizontal through $A$ and the normal in $B$, so it's $B$.
$$
\delta v_A = -6\,\delta\varphi ,
\qquad
\delta v(3) = -3\,\delta\varphi ,
\qquad
\delta u_B = 0
$$
$$
V_A \cdot (-6\,\delta\varphi) - 24 \cdot (-3\,\delta\varphi) = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Components of $R_B$ and verification with horizontal translation:
$$
H_B = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,R_B = -12\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_B = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,R_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
H_A - F + H_B = 17 - 5 - 12 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The work of the inclined roller reaction is $R_B$ times the component of the displacement of $B$ along the reaction, the direction restrained by the roller. During rotation about $A$, point $B$ moves vertically. Its displacement component along the reaction is $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ times the vertical displacement. This gives $R_B = 12\sqrt{2} \approx 16.97\,\mathrm{kN}$, with components $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. For $H_A$, both rollers remain in place. The center of rotation is where the normals to the two tracks intersect, at point $C = (0, 6)$ above $A$. The center lies $6\,\mathrm{m}$ above the beam, so every point on the beam has the same horizontal displacement $6\,\delta\varphi$ and force $F$ does work. This gives **$H_A = 17\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right**. For $V_A$, the center of rotation $B$ remains stationary, so $F$ does no work and the result is **$V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward**. Check using horizontal translation, which none of the three calculations used.

</div>
