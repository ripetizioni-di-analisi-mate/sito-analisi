## Exercise 1

The three-hinged frame shown has external hinges $A$ and $E$ at the same elevation, columns $AB$ and $DE$ of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and a beam $BD$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ with an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. The beam carries a uniform load $q = 10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Calculate the support reactions and the force at hinge $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4;D,6,4;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" carichi="B,D,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,C,3 m,-1;C,D,3 m,-1" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with load $q = 10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the crossbeam"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4,-;C2,6,4,-;D,9,4;E,9,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C2-D;D-E" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" forze="1.5 4,0,-1.3,30 kN;7.5 4,0,-1.3,30 kN;C,-1,0,X_C;C,0,-1,Y_C" reazioni="A,1,0,H_A;A,0,1,V_A;E,1,0,H_E;E,0,1,V_E;C2,1,0,X_C;C2,0,1,Y_C" etichette="2.8,4.3,C;6.15,4.3,C" punti="3,4;6,4" titolo="Free-body diagrams of bodies $ABC$ and $CDE$, showing the load resultant on each half of the crossbeam"}

Whole structure and auxiliary equation for part $CDE$:
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + H_E = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_E - 60 \cdot 3 = 0 \\[4pt]
-6\,V_A + 60 \cdot 3 = 0 \\[4pt]
3\,V_E + 4\,H_E - 30 \cdot 1.5 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_E = V_A = 30\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad H_E = \frac{45 - 90}{4} = -11.25\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad H_A = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Force in the hinge, from the balance of the body $ABC$:
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A - X_C = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A - 30 - Y_C = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
X_C = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_C = 0
$$

Check, moment equilibrium of body $ABC$ about $C$:
$$
-3\,V_A + 4\,H_A + 30 \cdot 1.5 = -90 + 45 + 45 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The structure has two bodies and three hinges, one internal and two external, hence $s = 6 = 3n$; the $A$, $C$, $E$ hinges are not aligned and the portal frame is statically determinate. There are four external reactions. The equations of the system are the horizontal translation of the whole structure, the rotation around $A$, the rotation around $E$ and the **auxiliary equation** into $C$ for the $CDE$ part. The springings are at the same elevation, so horizontal reactions do not appear in rotations around $A$ and $E$, and vertical reactions are those of a simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ with the resultant $60\,\mathrm{kN}$ in half.

In the auxiliary equation the $CDE$ part is subject to $V_E$, with a moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ about $C$ and counterclockwise moment, to $H_E$, applied $4\,\mathrm{m}$ under $C$ (with $H_E$ positive to right the moment is counterclockwise), and to the resultant of the load on the right half, $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$, with clockwise moment. $H_E = -11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ follows. Springings push inwards: $H_A = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $H_E = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left; $V_A = V_E = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ up.

On the body $ABC$, the hinge exerts forces $-X_C$ and $-Y_C$. The force equilibrium gives $X_C = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $Y_C = 0$: the body $ABC$ pushes the body $CDE$ to the right, and the crossbeam is in compression into $C$. The vertical force at $C$ is zero because the structure and load are symmetrical compared to the vertical through $C$. The check uses the rotation of $ABC$ around $C$, which has not been written.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The three-hinged frame shown has columns of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a beam of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. At joint $B$ a horizontal force $F = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Calculate the support reactions and the force at hinge $C$. Check that the force on body $CDE$ at $C$ acts along line $CE$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,8,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" forze="B,1.6,0,F" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,C,4 m,-0.8;C,D,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with horizontal force $F = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at joint $B$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,8,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" forze="B,1.6,0,F" reazioni="A,1.2,0,H_A;A,0,1.2,V_A;E,1.2,0,H_E;E,0,1.2,V_E" tratteggi="4,4 8,0" titolo="Free-body diagram of the entire structure; the dashed line is $CE$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + H_E + F = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_E = 0 \\[4pt]
8\,V_E - 4\,F = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_E + 4\,H_E = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_E = \frac{F}{2} = 10\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad V_A = -10\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad H_E = -10\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad H_A = -10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Force in the hinge, from the balance of the body $CDE$:
$$
\begin{cases}
X_C + H_E = 0 \\[4pt]
Y_C + V_E = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
X_C = 10\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_C = -10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check, rotation of the whole structure around $E$:
$$
-8\,V_A - 4\,F = 80 - 80 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The equations are, in order, the horizontal translation and the vertical translation of the whole structure, the rotation around $A$ and the auxiliary equation in $C$ for the $CDE$ part. In the rotation around $A$ the force $F$, applied at $4\,\mathrm{m}$ elevation, has clockwise moment $4F$, and the reactions in $A$ and $H_E$ have zero moment arm. In the auxiliary equation the $CDE$ part has no loads, and the reactions $V_E$ and $H_E$ both have $4\,\mathrm{m}$ arm about $C$.

The results are: $V_E = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $V_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, $H_A = H_E = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left in both pin supports, which divide the force $F$ equally. Vertical reactions form a counterclockwise couple of a moment arm of $8\,\mathrm{m}$, which balances the clockwise couple formed by $F$ and horizontal reactions.

The body $CDE$ is unloaded and is only connected to the rest by the $C$ and $E$ hinges. For the **unloaded member hinged at the two extremes** theorem, the forces it receives are directed along the line $CE$, which drops by $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right every $4\,\mathrm{m}$. In fact the force at $C$ has components $X_C = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $Y_C = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$, in the $-1$ ratio, which is the slope of the line $CE$; the reaction at $E$, of components $-10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, is the opposite force.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The three-hinged frame shown has columns of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a beam of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. Segment $BC$ carries a uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. At a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $C$ a vertical force $P = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Body $CDE$ carries a clockwise couple at joint $D$ of magnitude $\mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Calculate the support reactions and the force at hinge $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,8,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" carichi="B,C,0.6,0.6,q" forze="6 4,0,-1.6,P" coppie="D,-1" etichette="7.35,4.6,M" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,C,4 m,-1;C,6 4,2 m,-1;6 4,D,2 m,-1" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $BC$, force $P = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$, and clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $D$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,8,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" forze="2 4,0,-1.4,24 kN;6 4,0,-1.4,P" coppie="D,-1" etichette="7.35,4.6,M" reazioni="A,1.2,0,H_A;A,0,1.2,V_A;E,1.2,0,H_E;E,0,1.2,V_E" titolo="Free-body diagram of the entire structure showing resultant $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ of the distributed load"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + H_E = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_E - 24 - P = 0 \\[4pt]
8\,V_E - 24 \cdot 2 - 6\,P - \mathcal{M} = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_E + 4\,H_E - 2\,P - \mathcal{M} = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
V_E = \frac{48 + 96 + 8}{8} = 19\,\mathrm{kN}, \qquad V_A = 40 - 19 = 21\,\mathrm{kN}, \qquad H_E = \frac{32 + 8 - 76}{4} = -9\,\mathrm{kN}, \qquad H_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Force in the hinge, from the balance of the body $CDE$:
$$
\begin{cases}
X_C + H_E = 0 \\[4pt]
Y_C + V_E - P = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
X_C = 9\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_C = 16 - 19 = -3\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check, moment equilibrium of body $ABC$ about $C$:
$$
-4\,V_A + 4\,H_A + 24 \cdot 2 = -84 + 36 + 48 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The four equations are the horizontal translation and vertical translation of the whole structure, the rotation around $A$ and the auxiliary equation in $C$ for the $CDE$ part. The couple $\mathcal{M}$ is clockwise, so it enters with the minus sign in each moment equation, with the same value whatever the reference point. The text assigns it to the body $CDE$, so it appears in the auxiliary equation written for that part; in the auxiliary equation for part $ABC$ would not appear.

The resultant of the distributed load, $6 \cdot 4 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$, acts at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ horizontally. In the rotation around $A$ the reactions in $A$ and the reaction $H_E$ have zero arm, so the only unknown $V_E$ remains. In the auxiliary equation $V_E$ and $H_E$ have $4\,\mathrm{m}$ moment arm about $C$ and counterclockwise moment, while $P$ has a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and clockwise moment. The results are: $V_A = 21\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_E = 19\,\mathrm{kN}$ up, $H_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ right, $H_E = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ left.

The body $CDE$ receives at $C$ a force of $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward; Opposite forces act on the body $ABC$. In the check, written for the body $ABC$ around $C$, the reaction $V_A$ has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and clockwise moment, $H_A$ has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and counterclockwise moment, and the resultant load, at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $C$, has counterclockwise moment.

</div>
