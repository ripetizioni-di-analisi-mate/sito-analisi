## Exercise 1

The three-hinged arch shown has the parabolic axis, the springings $A$ and $B$ at the same elevation, the span $L = 12\,\mathrm{m}$ and the $f = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ rise, with the crown $C$ at midspan. The arch carries the load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, uniform on the horizontal projection. Calculate the support reactions, thrust and force in the hinge $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,6,4;B,12,0" aste="0 0-1 1.222;1 1.222-2 2.222;2 2.222-3 3;3 3-4 3.556;4 3.556-5 3.889;5 3.889-6 4;6 4-7 3.889;7 3.889-8 3.556;8 3.556-9 3;9 3-10 2.222;10 2.222-11 1.222;11 1.222-12 0" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" carichi="0 4.6,12 4.6,0.9,0.9,q" quote="A,6 0,6 m,0.8;6 0,B,6 m,0.8;6 0,C,4 m,-0.6" titolo="Three-hinged parabolic arch with $L = 12\,\mathrm{m}$, $f = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, and load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ distributed over the horizontal projection"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,6,4;B,12,0" aste="0 0-1 1.222;1 1.222-2 2.222;2 2.222-3 3;3 3-4 3.556;4 3.556-5 3.889;5 3.889-6 4;6 4-7 3.889;7 3.889-8 3.556;8 3.556-9 3;9 3-10 2.222;10 2.222-11 1.222;11 1.222-12 0" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="3 3,0,-1.6,30 kN;9 3,0,-1.6,30 kN" reazioni="A,1.6,0,H_A;A,0,1.6,V_A;B,1.6,0,H_B;B,0,1.6,V_B" titolo="Free-body diagram of the arch showing the load resultant on each half"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + H_B = 0 \\[4pt]
12\,V_B - 60 \cdot 6 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - 60 = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_B + 4\,H_B - 30 \cdot 3 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_A = V_B = 30\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad H_B = \frac{90 - 180}{4} = -22.5\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad H_A = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
H = \frac{qL^2}{8f} = \frac{5 \cdot 144}{8 \cdot 4} = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Force in the hinge, from the balance of the $CB$ half:
$$
\begin{cases}
X_C + H_B = 0 \\[4pt]
Y_C + V_B - 30 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
X_C = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_C = 0
$$

Check, rotation of half $AC$ around $C$:
$$
-6\,V_A + 4\,H_A + 30 \cdot 3 = -180 + 90 + 90 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The load is uniform on the horizontal projection, so on each half of the arch the resultant equals $5 \cdot 6 = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is applied on the vertical in the middle of the projection, to $3\,\mathrm{m}$ by the crown; the shape of the axis does not intervene. The equations are the horizontal translation, the rotation around $A$, the vertical translation and the **auxiliary equation** in $C$ by half $CB$. In the rotation around $A$ the horizontal reactions have zero arm, because the springings are at the same elevation.

In the auxiliary equation the reaction $V_B$ has a moment arm of $6\,\mathrm{m}$ about $C$ and counterclockwise moment; the reaction $H_B$ is applied $4\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C$; the resultant of the load on the right half has clockwise moment. $H_B = -22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ is obtained, and the value coincides with the formula of the **thrust** of the arch with uniform load. The reactions are: $V_A = V_B = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ up, $H_A = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ right and $H_B = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ left, i.e. the $H = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ thrust directed inward of the arch in both springings.

Half $AC$ exerts a rightward horizontal force of $22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ on half $CB$. Symmetry of the arch and load makes the vertical force at the crown zero. The two halves push each other in a crown with a force equal to the thrust.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The three-hinged arch shown consists of two straight $AC$ and $CB$ members, with the springings $A$ and $B$ at the same elevation, the span $L = 12\,\mathrm{m}$ and the crown $C$ at midspan, at $f = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ elevation. At the midpoint of the member $AC$, the vertical force $P = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Calculate support reactions with equilibrium equations, check thrust with the equivalent simply supported beam and verify that the reaction at $B$ is directed along the line $BC$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,6,4;B,12,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="3 2,0,-1.6,P" quote="A,3 0,3 m,0.8;3 0,6 0,3 m,0.8;6 0,B,6 m,0.8;6 0,C,4 m,-0.6" titolo="Three-hinged arch with straight members and force $P = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the midpoint of $AC$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,6,4;B,12,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="3 2,0,-1.6,P" reazioni="A,1.5,0,H_A;A,0,1.5,V_A;B,1.5,0,H_B;B,0,1.5,V_B" titolo="Free-body diagram of the arch"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + H_B = 0 \\[4pt]
12\,V_B - 3\,P = 0 \\[4pt]
-12\,V_A + 9\,P = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_B + 4\,H_B = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad V_A = 18\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad H_B = -\frac{6 \cdot 6}{4} = -9\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad H_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Simply supported beam equivalent:
$$
M_C^0 = V_A^0 \cdot 6 - P \cdot 3 = 18 \cdot 6 - 24 \cdot 3 = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m},
\qquad
H = \frac{M_C^0}{f} = \frac{36}{4} = 9\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Reaction direction in $B$:
$$
\frac{V_B}{H_B} = \frac{6}{-9} = -\frac{2}{3},
\qquad
\text{slope of } BC = \frac{4 - 0}{6 - 12} = -\frac{2}{3}
$$

Check, rotation of half $AC$ around $C$:
$$
-6\,V_A + 4\,H_A + 3\,P = -108 + 36 + 72 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The equations are the horizontal translation, the rotation around $A$, the rotation around $B$ and the auxiliary equation in $C$ by half $CB$. The force $P$ is located at $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and $9\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$ horizontally, so vertical reactions divide the load as in a simply supported beam: three-quarters to $A$ and one-quarter to $B$. Half $CB$ is unloaded, so only reactions in $B$ appear in the auxiliary equation.

The results are: $V_A = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ up, $H_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ right and $H_B = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ left. The **three-hinged arch reaction theorem** gives the same thrust value: the equivalent simply supported beam's bending moment in the section under the crown is $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and divided by the rise gives $9\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The $CB$ half is connected to the rest only by the $B$ and $C$ hinges and is unloaded, so for the **unloaded member hinged at the two extremes** theorem the reaction at $B$ is directed along the line $BC$. The ratio of the reaction components is equal to the slope of $BC$, as it must be. The check uses the rotation of the $AC$ half around $C$: $P$ is located $3\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $C$ and has counterclockwise moment.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The three-hinged arch shown has the parabolic axis, the springings $A$ and $B$ at the same elevation, the span $L = 16\,\mathrm{m}$ and the $f = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ rise, with the crown $C$ at midspan. The left half carries the load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, uniform on the horizontal projection; on the right half, at the point of $12\,\mathrm{m}$ coordinate from $A$, the vertical force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Calculate support reactions and force in the hinge $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,8,4;B,16,0" aste="0 0-1 0.938;1 0.938-2 1.75;2 1.75-3 2.438;3 2.438-4 3;4 3-5 3.438;5 3.438-6 3.75;6 3.75-7 3.938;7 3.938-8 4;8 4-9 3.938;9 3.938-10 3.75;10 3.75-11 3.438;11 3.438-12 3;12 3-13 2.438;13 2.438-14 1.75;14 1.75-15 0.938;15 0.938-16 0" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" carichi="0 4.6,8 4.6,0.9,0.9,q" forze="12 3,0,-2,P" quote="A,8 0,8 m,0.8;8 0,12 0,4 m,0.8;12 0,B,4 m,0.8;8 0,C,4 m,-0.6" titolo="Parabolic arch with $L = 16\,\mathrm{m}$, $f = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the left half, and force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + H_B = 0 \\[4pt]
16\,V_B - 32 \cdot 4 - 12\,P = 0 \\[4pt]
-16\,V_A + 32 \cdot 12 + 4\,P = 0 \\[4pt]
8\,V_B + 4\,H_B - 4\,P = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\begin{aligned}
V_B &= \frac{128 + 144}{16} = 17\,\mathrm{kN}, \qquad V_A = \frac{384 + 48}{16} = 27\,\mathrm{kN}, \\[6pt]
H_B &= \frac{48 - 136}{4} = -22\,\mathrm{kN}, \qquad H_A = 22\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

Check using the equivalent simply supported beam:
$$
M_C^0 = 27 \cdot 8 - 32 \cdot 4 = 88\,\mathrm{kN\,m},
\qquad
H = \frac{88}{4} = 22\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Force in the hinge, from the balance of the $CB$ half:
$$
\begin{cases}
X_C + H_B = 0 \\[4pt]
Y_C + V_B - P = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
X_C = 22\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_C = 12 - 17 = -5\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check, rotation of half $AC$ around $C$:
$$
-8\,V_A + 4\,H_A + 32 \cdot 4 = -216 + 88 + 128 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The distributed load on the left half has resultant $4 \cdot 8 = 32\,\mathrm{kN}$, on the $4\,\mathrm{m}$ vertical. The equations are the horizontal translation, the rotation around $A$, the rotation around $B$ and the auxiliary equation in $C$ by half $CB$. The last one features $V_B$, with $8\,\mathrm{m}$, $H_B$ moment arm, applied $4\,\mathrm{m}$ under the crown, and the force $P$, which is located $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $C$ and has clockwise moment.

The results are: $V_A = 27\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 17\,\mathrm{kN}$ up, $H_A = 22\,\mathrm{kN}$ right and $H_B = 22\,\mathrm{kN}$ left. The thrust equals the bending moment in the equivalent simply supported beam directly below the crown divided by the rise. The load is not symmetrical compared to the vertical through $C$, and in crown the vertical force is different from zero. The half $AC$ exerts on the half $CB$ a force of $22\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. To check equilibrium of half $AC$, take moments about $C$. Force $V_A$ produces a clockwise moment with arm $8\,\mathrm{m}$. Force $H_A$ produces a counterclockwise moment with arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and the load resultant, a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $C$, also produces a counterclockwise moment.

</div>
