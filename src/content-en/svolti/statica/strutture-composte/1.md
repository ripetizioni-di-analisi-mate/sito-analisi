## Exercise 1

The Gerber beam shown is formed by the cantilever $AC$, fixed at $A$, and the beam $CB$, connected to the cantilever by the hinge $C$ and resting on a roller support at $B$. The section $CB$ carries the uniform $q = 10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load. Calculate the support reactions and the force transmitted by the hinge $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="C,B,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,C,2 m,0.8;C,B,4 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam with cantilever $AC$ and segment $CB$ under load $q = 10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

$$
n = 2, \qquad 3n = 6, \qquad s = 3 + 2 + 1 = 6
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C1,2,0,-;C2,3,1.5,-;B,7,1.5" aste="A-C1;C2-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="5 1.5,0,-1.4,40 kN;C1,0,-1,Y_C" reazioni="C2,0,1,Y_C;C2,1,0,X_C;B,0,1,V_B;A,1,0,H_A;A,0,1,V_A" coppie="A,1,M_A" etichette="1.85,0.3,C;2.75,1.8,C" punti="2,0;3,1.5" titolo="Free-body diagrams of suspended segment $CB$ above and cantilever $AC$ below, showing load resultant $qL = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

$CB$ suspended segment:
$$
\begin{cases}
X_C = 0 \\[4pt]
Y_C + V_B - 40 = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_B - 40 \cdot 2 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_B = 20\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_C = 20\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad X_C = 0
$$

Cantilever $AC$, with forces $-X_C$ and $-Y_C$ in $C$:
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A - X_C = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A - Y_C = 0 \\[4pt]
M_A - 2\,Y_C = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
H_A = 0, \quad V_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad M_A = 40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Check, rotation of the entire beam around $B$:
$$
M_A - 6\,V_A + 40 \cdot 2 = 40 - 120 + 80 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The structure has two bodies, so six degrees of freedom; Fixed support takes out three degrees of freedom, internal hinge Two and roller Support One. The count is satisfied and the constraints are well arranged, because the cantilever is fixed by the fixed support and the section $CB$ is supported on two distinct points, $C$ and $B$.

Cantilever $AC$ is the supporting segment because its fixed support alone holds it in place; segment $CB$ is suspended because it rests on the cantilever at hinge $C$. For the **order of calculation** of the Gerber beam, we start from the suspended segment. The uniform load on $4\,\mathrm{m}$ has resultant $10 \cdot 4 = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$, applied in the middle of the segment, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$. The three equations of the segment $CB$ are horizontal translation, vertical translation and rotation around $C$; the $C$ reference point eliminates $X_C$ and $Y_C$, so the third equation contains only $V_B$.

On the cantilever the hinge exerts the opposite force to that which acts on the suspended segment, i.e. $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ down in $C$. In the rotation around $A$ this force has a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and clockwise moment, so $M_A$ is counterclockwise. The results are: $V_B = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $V_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $M_A = 40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise, $H_A = 0$; The hinge transmits a vertical force of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$. In the check, $V_A$ produces a clockwise moment about $B$ with arm $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The load resultant, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$, produces a counterclockwise moment.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The Gerber beam shown has the cantilever $AC$ fixed at $A$, the internal hinge $C$ and the segment $CD$ resting on a roller support at $B$, with the overhang $BD$. The cantilever carries the uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$; at the free end $D$ acts the vertical force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Calculate support reactions and force in the hinge $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,5,0;D,7,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.6,0.6,q" forze="D,0,-1.6,F" quote="A,C,3 m,0.8;C,B,2 m,0.8;B,D,2 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam with load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the cantilever and force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the overhang tip"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C1,3,0,-;C2,4,1.5,-;B,6,1.5;D,8,1.5" aste="A-C1;C2-D" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,C1,0.5,0.5,q" forze="D,0,-1.3,F;C1,0,-1,Y_C" reazioni="C2,0,1,Y_C;B,0,1,V_B;A,0,1,V_A;A,1,0,H_A" coppie="A,1,M_A" etichette="2.85,0.3,C;3.75,1.8,C" punti="3,0;4,1.5" titolo="Free-body diagrams of suspended segment $CD$ and cantilever $AC$"}

$CD$ suspended segment (horizontal force equilibrium gives $X_C = 0$):
$$
\begin{cases}
Y_C + V_B - F = 0 \\[4pt]
2\,V_B - 4\,F = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_B = 2\,F = 24\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_C = F - V_B = -12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Cantilever $AC$, with the resultant $6 \cdot 3 = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and the force $-Y_C = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting upward at $C$:
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A - 18 - Y_C = 0 \\[4pt]
M_A - 18 \cdot 1.5 - 3\,Y_C = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_A = 18 - 12 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad M_A = 27 - 36 = -9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Check, rotation of the entire beam around $B$:
$$
M_A - 5\,V_A + 18 \cdot 3.5 - F \cdot 2 = -9 - 30 + 63 - 24 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Segment $CD$ is suspended, so analyze it first. In the rotation around $C$ the reaction $V_B$ has a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the force $F$ has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$, so the roller support must react with twice as much $F$. Therefore, $V_B = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$, greater than the applied force, and vertical force equilibrium requires $Y_C = -12\,\mathrm{kN}$: the cantilever holds the downward-suspended segment. The overhang tends to rotate the section $CD$ around $B$ by raising end $C$.

By action and reaction on the cantilever acts in $C$ an upward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, which in the equations appears as $-Y_C$. About $A$, the distributed load produces a clockwise moment $18 \cdot 1.5 = 27\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and the force at $C$ produces a counterclockwise moment $12 \cdot 3 = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The second prevails, so fixed support reacts with a clockwise moment. The results are: $V_B = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $M_A = -9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, i.e. $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise, $H_A = 0$. The hinge transmits a vertical force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, down on the suspended segment and up on the cantilever. In the check the resultant of the distributed load is $3.5\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$ and the force $F$ to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $B$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The Gerber beam shown has the segment $AC$ bound by a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, with the $BC$ overhang, and the segment $CD$ connected in $C$ by an internal hinge and resting on a roller support at $D$. The section $AC$ carries the uniform $q = 8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load; at the midpoint $E$ of the segment $CD$ acts a force $F$ of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, inclined downward and leftwards, with $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ components to the left and $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Calculate support reactions and force in the hinge $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;E,8,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.6,0.6,q" forze="E,-1.2,-1.6,F" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,C,2 m,0.8;C,E,2 m,0.8;E,D,2 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam with load $q = 8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on segment $AC$ and inclined force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $E$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C1,6,0,-;C2,8,2.2,-;E,10,2.2;D,12,2.2" aste="A-C1;C2-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C1,0.5,0.5,q" forze="E,-0.9,-1.2,F;C1,-1,0,X_C;C1,0,-1,Y_C" reazioni="C2,1,0,X_C;C2,0,1,Y_C;D,0,1,V_D;A,1,0,H_A;A,0,1,V_A;B,0,1,V_B" etichette="5.85,0.3,C;7.75,2.5,C" punti="6,0;8,2.2" titolo="Free-body diagrams of suspended segment $CD$ and supporting segment $AC$"}

$CD$ suspended segment:
$$
\begin{cases}
X_C - 6 = 0 \\[4pt]
Y_C + V_D - 8 = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_D - 8 \cdot 2 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
X_C = 6\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad V_D = 4\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_C = 4\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$AC$ supporting segment, with the resultant $8 \cdot 6 = 48\,\mathrm{kN}$ $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and forces $-X_C$, $-Y_C$ in $C$:
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A - X_C = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - 48 - Y_C = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_B - 48 \cdot 3 - 6\,Y_C = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
H_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad V_B = \frac{144 + 24}{4} = 42\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad V_A = 52 - 42 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check, rotation of the entire beam around $D$:
$$
-10\,V_A - 6\,V_B + 48 \cdot 7 + 8 \cdot 2 = -100 - 252 + 336 + 16 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Segment $AC$ is a simply supported beam with an overhang. It is statically determinate on its own and therefore serves as the supporting segment; segment $CD$ is suspended because without the hinge its only support would be roller $D$. Analyze the suspended segment first. The force $F$ has assigned components, so you don't need to break it down with angles. In the horizontal translation of the segment $CD$ the only available horizontal reaction is $X_C$, because the roller support at $D$ reacts vertically; therefore the horizontal component of $F$ is transmitted all to the hinge, $X_C = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right on the suspended segment. Moment equilibrium about $C$ gives $V_D$, and the vertical force equilibrium gives $Y_C$.

On the supporting segment the forces of the hinge have opposite direction: $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. The horizontal force thus comes to the $A$ support pin, which is the only constraint that can balance it. In the rotation around $A$ the force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $C$ has a moment arm of $6\,\mathrm{m}$; the horizontal component passes through $A$ and has zero moment. The results are: $H_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, $V_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $V_B = 42\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $V_D = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. In the check, the reactions $V_A$ and $V_B$, left of $D$, have clockwise moment; the resultant of the distributed load, at $7\,\mathrm{m}$ from $D$, and the vertical component of $F$, at $2\,\mathrm{m}$, have counterclockwise moment, while the horizontal component of $F$ and the reaction $H_A$ lie on the beam axis and have zero moment.

</div>
