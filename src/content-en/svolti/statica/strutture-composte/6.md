## Exercise 1

The beam shown is formed by the section $AC$, fixed at $A$, and the section $CB$, resting on a roller support at $B$; the two segments are connected in $C$ by an internal parallel-link connection with pin-ended links parallel to the beam axis. The whole beam carries the uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Calculate support reactions and actions transmitted by parallel-link support.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" interni="C,doppio-pendolo,0" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,C,4 m,0.8;C,B,3 m,0.8" titolo="Beam with an internal parallel-link connection at $C$ and load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

$$
n = 2, \qquad 3n = 6, \qquad s = 3 + 2 + 1 = 6
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C1,4,0,-;C2,8,0,-;B,11,0" aste="A-C1;C2-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="2 0,0,-1.2,24 kN;9.5 0,0,-1.2,18 kN;C1,-0.9,0,X_C" coppie="C1,-1;C2,1,M_C;A,1,M_A" reazioni="C2,0.9,0,X_C;B,0,1,V_B;A,1,0,H_A;A,0,1,V_A" etichette="3.95,-0.6,C;3.35,0.65,M_C;7.85,0.45,C" punti="4,0;8,0" titolo="Free-body diagrams of the two segments: the parallel-link connection transmits horizontal force $X_C$ and couple $M_C$, with opposite senses on the two segments"}

$CB$:
$$
\begin{cases}
X_C = 0 \\[4pt]
V_B - 18 = 0 \\[4pt]
M_C + 3\,V_B - 18 \cdot 1.5 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_B = 18\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad M_C = 27 - 54 = -27\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Segment $AC$, with $-X_C$ force and $-M_C$ couple at $C$:
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A - X_C = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A - 24 = 0 \\[4pt]
M_A - 24 \cdot 2 - M_C = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
H_A = 0, \quad V_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad M_A = 48 - 27 = 21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Check, rotation of the whole beam around $B$, with the resultant $6 \cdot 7 = 42\,\mathrm{kN}$ $3.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$:
$$
M_A - 7\,V_A + 42 \cdot 3.5 = 21 - 168 + 147 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Internal parallel-link connection with pin-ended links parallel to the axis prevents horizontal relative displacement and relative rotation of the two segments, and leaves vertical relative displacement free. Therefore it transmits a horizontal force $X_C$ and a couple $M_C$, and **does not transmit vertical forces**. $X_C$ and $M_C$ indicate the force and couple that the segment $AC$ exerts on the section $CB$, positive to the right and counterclockwise; on the segment $AC$ act opposite force and couple. The count gives $3 + 2 + 1 = 6$ scalar constraints for two bodies.

It starts with the segment $CB$, which in addition to the unknowns of parallel-link support has only one external reaction, $V_B$. For the **auxiliary equation of parallel-link support**, the vertical translation of the segment $CB$ contains only $V_B$ and the load, so the roller support carries all the load of the segment, $6 \cdot 3 = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$. The rotation around $C$ then gives $M_C = -27\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, i.e. the couple that the segment $AC$ exerts on the segment $CB$ is clockwise.

The segment $AC$ receives the opposite couple, counterclockwise of $27\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and no vertical force. So the vertical reaction of fixed support balances only the load of the segment $AC$, and the moment of fixed support balances the difference between the clockwise moment of the load, $48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and the counterclockwise couple received. The results are: $V_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $M_A = 21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise, $H_A = 0$; parallel-link support transmits a couple of $27\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and no force.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The three-hinged arch shown consists of two straight $AC$ and $CB$ members, with the crown $C$ at midspan; the span is $12\,\mathrm{m}$ and the rise $3\,\mathrm{m}$. The springing $A$ is a hinge, the springing $B$ is a roller support on a horizontal surface, and the two springings are connected by the tie $AB$, hinged at the two extremes. The arch carries the load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, uniform on the horizontal projection. Calculate support reactions and force in the tie.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,6,3;B,12,0" aste="A-C;C-B;A-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="0 3.6,12 3.6,0.7,0.7,q" quote="A,6 0,6 m,0.8;6 0,B,6 m,0.8;6 0,C,3 m,-0.6" titolo="Three-hinged arch with tie $AB$ and load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ distributed over the horizontal projection"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,6,3;B,12,0" aste="A-C;C-B;A-1.5 0;10.5 0-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="3 1.5,0,-1.4,24 kN;9 1.5,0,-1.4,24 kN" reazioni="A,1.3,0,H_A;A,0,1.3,V_A;B,0,1.3,V_B;2.9 0,1.4,0,N;9.1 0,-1.4,0,N" titolo="Free-body diagram of the arch cut through the tie: the tie is in tension and pulls the springings inward"}

$$
n = 2, \qquad 3n = 6, \qquad s = 2 + 1 + 2 + 1 = 6
$$

Whole structure (tie is internal):
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
12\,V_B - 48 \cdot 6 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - 48 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
H_A = 0, \quad V_B = 24\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad V_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Half $CB$ around $C$, with the force $-N$ of the tie in $B$:
$$
6\,V_B - 3\,N - 24 \cdot 3 = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N = \frac{144 - 72}{3} = 24\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad N = \frac{qL^2}{8f} = \frac{4 \cdot 144}{8 \cdot 3} = 24\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Force in the hinge $C$, from the balance of the $CB$ half:
$$
\begin{cases}
X_C - N = 0 \\[4pt]
Y_C + V_B - 24 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
X_C = 24\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_C = 0
$$

Check, rotation of the half $AC$ around $C$, with the force $N$ of the tie in $A$:
$$
-6\,V_A + 3\,(H_A + N) + 24 \cdot 3 = -144 + 72 + 72 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The tie is a **internal two-force link**: an unloaded member pinned at both ends, connecting the two halves of the arch and transmitting only a force along its own axis. It counts as a scalar constraint between the two halves, which is added to the hinge $A$, $B$ roller Support and $C$ hinge. The force $N$ is positive in tension: a in tension tie pulls the springing $A$ to the right and the springing $B$ to the left.

For the whole structure the tie is internal, so its forces do not appear in the global equations. The only external horizontal reaction is $H_A$, because the roller support at $B$ reacts vertically; because the loads are vertical, $H_A = 0$. Vertical reactions are those of the equivalent simply supported beam. To calculate $N$, the half $CB$ is isolated, on which $V_B$ acts, the force of the tie in $B$, the load and the force at $C$. Rotation around $C$ eliminates force at $C$; the force of the tie, directed to the left and applied $3\,\mathrm{m}$ under $C$, has clockwise moment $3N$.

The tie absorbs the thrust that the springings of a untied arch should provide, and in fact $N$ coincides with the formula of the thrust of the arch with uniform load. The results are: $V_A = V_B = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $H_A = 0$, **in tension** tie with $N = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$; in crown the two halves exchange a horizontal force of $24\,\mathrm{kN}$. In the check the tie force at $A$ is directed to the right, $3\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C$, and has counterclockwise moment.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The frame shown has columns of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and a beam of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$ with an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. The left column base $A$ is pinned. The right base $E$ rests on a roller on a horizontal surface, and tie $AE$ connects the two bases. At joint $B$ a horizontal force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Segment $BC$ carries a uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Calculate the support reactions, the force in the tie, and the force at hinge $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,8,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;A-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="B,1.5,0,F" carichi="B,C,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,C,4 m,-1;C,D,4 m,-1" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with tie $AE$, force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$, and load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $BC$"}

Whole structure:
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + F = 0 \\[4pt]
8\,V_E - 4\,F - 24 \cdot 2 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_E - 24 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
H_A = -10\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad V_E = \frac{40 + 48}{8} = 11\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad V_A = 13\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$CDE$ body around $C$, with the force $-N$ of the tie in $E$:
$$
4\,V_E - 4\,N = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N = V_E = 11\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Force in the hinge, from the balance of the body $CDE$:
$$
\begin{cases}
X_C - N = 0 \\[4pt]
Y_C + V_E = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
X_C = 11\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_C = -11\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check, moment equilibrium of body $ABC$ about $C$, with the force $N$ of tie in $A$:
$$
-4\,V_A + 4\,(H_A + N) + 24 \cdot 2 = -52 + 4 + 48 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The external reactions are three, $H_A$, $V_A$ and $V_E$, and are calculated with the equations of the whole structure, in which the tie does not appear because it is internal. The force $F$ is balanced all by the hinge $A$, the only external support that reacts horizontally, then $H_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. In the rotation around $A$ the force $F$, at $4\,\mathrm{m}$ elevation, and the resultant of the load, $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, have clockwise moment. You get $V_E = 11\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A = 13\,\mathrm{kN}$, both up.

The axial force in the tie is derived from the balance of the body $CDE$ at the rotation around $C$. On this body act $V_E$, with a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and counterclockwise moment, the force of the tie in $E$, directed to the left if the tie is in tension and applied $4\,\mathrm{m}$ under $C$, with clockwise moment, and the force at $C$, which has zero moment. The following is $N = 11\,\mathrm{kN}$: the tie is **in tension**.

The body $CDE$ is unloaded and receives forces only in $C$ and $E$, where the roller support and tie act together. As in the proof of the theorem on the unloaded member hinged at the two extremes, the force at $C$ and the resultant forces in $E$ are therefore opposite and directed along $CE$. In fact, in $E$ the tie and roller support give the force of $-11\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $11\,\mathrm{kN}$ components, and the body $ABC$ exerts on $CDE$ in $C$ the force of $X_C = 11\,\mathrm{kN}$ components to the right and $Y_C = -11\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $11\,\mathrm{kN}$ down. In the check the horizontal forces in $A$, i.e. $H_A$ and the force $N$ of the tie, has resultant $1\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, applied $4\,\mathrm{m}$ under $C$.

</div>
