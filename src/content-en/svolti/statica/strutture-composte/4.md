## Exercise 1

The Gerber beam shown consists of three segments: $AC$, bound by a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$; $CE$, connected to $AC$ by the internal hinge $C$ and resting on a roller support at $D$; $EF$, connected to $CE$ by the internal hinge $E$ and resting on a roller support at $F$. The whole beam carries the uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Calculate support reactions and forces in the $C$ and $E$ hinges.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0;D,12,0;E,14,0;F,18,0" aste="A-C;C-E;E-F" cerniere="C;E" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello;F,carrello" carichi="A,F,0.8,0.8,q" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,D,4 m,0.9;D,E,2 m,0.9;E,F,4 m,0.9" titolo="Three-segment Gerber beam with uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over its entire length"}

$$
n = 3, \qquad 3n = 9, \qquad s = 2 + 1 + 2 + 1 + 2 + 1 = 9
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C1,8,0,-;C2,9.5,2.2,-;D,13.5,2.2;E1,15.5,2.2,-;E2,17,4.4,-;F,21,4.4" aste="A-C1;C2-E1;E2-F" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="4 0,0,-1.2,48 kN;12.5 2.2,0,-1.2,36 kN;19 4.4,0,-1.2,24 kN;C1,0,-0.9,Y_C;E1,0,-0.9,Y_E" reazioni="A,0,1,V_A;A,1,0,H_A;B,0,1,V_B;C2,0,1,Y_C;D,0,1,V_D;E2,0,1,Y_E;F,0,1,V_F" etichette="7.8,0.35,C;9.3,2.55,C;15.3,2.55,E;16.8,4.75,E" punti="8,0;9.5,2.2;15.5,2.2;17,4.4" titolo="Three isolated segments, from bottom to top: supporting segment $AC$, segment $CE$ supported by $AC$, and segment $EF$ supported by $CE$"}

$EF$:
$$
\begin{cases}
Y_E + V_F - 24 = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_F - 24 \cdot 2 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_E = 12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Segment $CE$, with force $-Y_E$ in $E$:
$$
\begin{cases}
Y_C + V_D - 36 - Y_E = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_D - 36 \cdot 3 - 6\,Y_E = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_D = \frac{108 + 72}{4} = 45\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_C = 48 - 45 = 3\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Segment $AC$, with force $-Y_C$ in $C$:
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - 48 - Y_C = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_B - 48 \cdot 4 - 8\,Y_C = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_B = \frac{192 + 24}{6} = 36\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad V_A = 51 - 36 = 15\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check, rotation of the whole beam around $F$, with the resultant $6 \cdot 18 = 108\,\mathrm{kN}$ $9\,\mathrm{m}$ from $F$:
$$
-18\,V_A - 12\,V_B - 6\,V_D + 108 \cdot 9 = -270 - 432 - 270 + 972 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The beam has three bodies and nine scalar constraints: the pin support and the two internal ones give two each, the three roller supports one each. Segment $AC$ is the supporting beam, a simply supported beam with an overhang. Segment $CE$ has only one roller and rests on $AC$ at $C$, so it is suspended from $AC$; in turn carries the segment $EF$, which only has the roller support at $F$. For the **order of calculation** it starts from the segment that does not support any other segment, namely $EF$, then it moves to $CE$ and finally to $AC$. Horizontal segment translation gives zero horizontal forces in the hinges and $H_A = 0$, because there are no horizontal loads.

For each segment the distributed load replaces itself with the resultant of the segment itself: $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ on $EF$, $36\,\mathrm{kN}$ on $CE$, $48\,\mathrm{kN}$ on $AC$, each in half of its own segment. The segment $EF$ is calculated as a simply supported beam in $E$ and $F$, and the force $Y_E = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ it receives from $CE$ becomes a load of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ down at end $E$ of $CE$. In the rotation of $CE$ around $C$ the resultant of the load has a moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and this force has a moment arm of $6\,\mathrm{m}$. Similarly $Y_C = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ becomes a load of $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ down at the end of the $AC$ overhang, at $8\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.

The reactions are all upward: $V_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_B = 36\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_D = 45\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, with $H_A = 0$; their sum is $108\,\mathrm{kN}$, equal to the total load. Hinges transmit vertical forces of $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $C$ and $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $E$. In the check the reactions, all left of $F$, have clockwise moment, and the resultant of the total load has counterclockwise moment.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The Gerber beam shown has the section $AB$ fixed at $A$, the section $BD$ connected to $AB$ by the internal hinge $B$ and resting on a roller support at $C$, and the section $DE$ connected to $BD$ by the internal hinge $D$ and resting on a roller support at $E$. At the midpoint of $AB$ the force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward; the section $BE$ carries the uniform $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load; at section $DE$, at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $D$, the clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is applied. Calculate the support reactions and forces in the hinges.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,7,0;D,8,0;E,12,0" aste="A-B;B-D;D-E" cerniere="B;D" vincoli="A,incastro,180;C,carrello;E,carrello" forze="1.5 0,0,-1.4,F" carichi="B,E,0.6,0.6,q" coppie="10 0,-1,M" quote="A,B,3 m,0.9;B,C,4 m,0.9;C,D,1 m,0.9;D,10 0,2 m,0.9;10 0,E,2 m,0.9" titolo="Three-segment Gerber beam with force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$, load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $BE$, and clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on $DE$"}

Segment $DE$, with the resultant $4 \cdot 4 = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $D$:
$$
\begin{cases}
Y_D + V_E - 16 = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_E - 16 \cdot 2 - \mathcal{M} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_E = \frac{32 + 8}{4} = 10\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_D = 6\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Segment $BD$, with the resultant $4 \cdot 5 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$ and the force $-Y_D$ in $D$:
$$
\begin{cases}
Y_B + V_C - 20 - Y_D = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_C - 20 \cdot 2.5 - 5\,Y_D = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_C = \frac{50 + 30}{4} = 20\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_B = 26 - 20 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Segment $AB$, with force $-Y_B$ in $B$:
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A - F - Y_B = 0 \\[4pt]
M_A - 1.5\,F - 3\,Y_B = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad M_A = 15 + 18 = 33\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Check, rotation of the whole beam around $E$, with the resultant $4 \cdot 9 = 36\,\mathrm{kN}$ $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $E$:
$$
M_A - 12\,V_A + 10.5\,F - 5\,V_C + 36 \cdot 4.5 - \mathcal{M} = 33 - 192 + 105 - 100 + 162 - 8 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

segment $AB$ is fixed and therefore acts as the supporting segment; the segment $BD$ has only $C$ roller support and rests on $AB$ in $B$; the segment $DE$ has only $E$ roller support and leans on $BD$ in $D$. The order of calculation is therefore $DE$, $BD$, $AB$. Constraint count gives $3 + 2 + 1 + 2 + 1 = 9 = 3n$.

The couple $\mathcal{M}$ acts on the segment $DE$, so it only enters the moment equation of that segment and the global equation; its clockwise moment is $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about any reference point, because it is clockwise. The distributed load on $BE$ is divided between the two segments: $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ on $DE$ and $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ on $BD$, each applied in the middle of its segment. The forces of the hinges are transmitted to the next segment with the opposite direction: the section $BD$ receives at $D$ a force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, with a moment arm of $5\,\mathrm{m}$ about $B$; the cantilever $AB$ receives at $B$ a force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ down, with a moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ about $A$.

Reactions are: $V_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_C = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_E = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $M_A = 33\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise, $H_A = 0$. The sum of vertical reactions, $46\,\mathrm{kN}$, is equal to the sum of $F$ and distributed load. In the check around $E$ the reactions have clockwise moment, $F$ and the resultant of the distributed load have counterclockwise moment, and the couple enters with their own clockwise moment.

</div>
