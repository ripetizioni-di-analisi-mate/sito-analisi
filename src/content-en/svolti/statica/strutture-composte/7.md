## Exercise 1

The three-hinged arch shown is formed by the straight $AC$ and $CB$ members. Compared to the springing $A$, the crown $C$ is located $4\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ higher, the springing $B$ is located $10\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ higher. At the midpoint of $AC$ the vertical force $P_1 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts, at the midpoint of $CB$ the vertical force $P_2 = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$, both downward. Calculate support reactions and force in the hinge $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,4;B,10,2" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="2 2,0,-1.6,P_1;7 3,0,-1.6,P_2" quote="A,10 0,10 m,0.8;10 0,B,2 m,0.8;A,0 4,4 m,-0.8" tratteggi="0,0 10,0;0,4 4,4" titolo="Three-hinged arch with springings at different elevations and forces $P_1 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $P_2 = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,4;B,10,2" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="2 2,0,-1.6,P_1;7 3,0,-1.6,P_2" reazioni="A,1.4,0,H_A;A,0,1.4,V_A;B,1.4,0,H_B;B,0,1.4,V_B" titolo="Free-body diagram of the arch"}

Rotation of the whole structure around $A$ and auxiliary equation for half $CB$ around $C$:
$$
\begin{cases}
10\,V_B - 2\,H_B - 2\,P_1 - 7\,P_2 = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_B + 2\,H_B - 3\,P_2 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\begin{cases}
10\,V_B - 2\,H_B = 160 \\[4pt]
6\,V_B + 2\,H_B = 48
\end{cases}
$$
$$
16\,V_B = 208 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad V_B = 13\,\mathrm{kN}, \qquad H_B = \frac{48 - 78}{2} = -15\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Translations of the whole structure:
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + H_B = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - P_1 - P_2 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
H_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad V_A = 40 - 13 = 27\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Force in the hinge, from the balance of the $CB$ half:
$$
\begin{cases}
X_C + H_B = 0 \\[4pt]
Y_C + V_B - P_2 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
X_C = 15\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_C = 3\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check, rotation of half $AC$ around $C$:
$$
-4\,V_A + 4\,H_A + 2\,P_1 = -108 + 60 + 48 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Springings are at different elevations, so the horizontal $H_B$ reaction has $2\,\mathrm{m}$ arm about $A$ and appears in the moment equation around $A$ along with $V_B$. In the auxiliary equation for half $CB$ around $C$ the same two unknowns appear: $V_B$ has $6\,\mathrm{m}$ arm, and $H_B$, applied $2\,\mathrm{m}$ under $C$, has $2\,\mathrm{m}$ arm. The two equations form a system in $V_B$ and $H_B$; By adding them member to member you delete $H_B$.

In the rotation around $A$ the reaction $H_B$, positive to the right and applied $2\,\mathrm{m}$ over $A$, has clockwise moment, so it appears with the minus sign; the forces $P_1$ and $P_2$ have $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $7\,\mathrm{m}$ arms and clockwise moment. In the auxiliary equation $P_2$ is located $3\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $C$. Translations of the whole structure then give the reactions in $A$.

The results are: $V_A = 27\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 13\,\mathrm{kN}$ up, $H_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ right and $H_B = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ left. The half $AC$ exerts on the half $CB$ a force of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. To check equilibrium of half $AC$, take moments about $C$. Force $V_A$ produces a clockwise moment. Force $H_A$, applied a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C$, and force $P_1$, applied a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $C$, each produce a counterclockwise moment.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The three-hinged frame shown has columns of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a beam of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. Column $AB$ carries a uniform horizontal load of $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to the right over its full height. Calculate the support reactions and the force at hinge $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4;D,6,4;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,3 kN/m,0" quote="A,E,6 m,0.8;E,D,4 m,0.8" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with a uniform horizontal load of $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on column $AB$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4;D,6,4;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" forze="0 2,1.2,0,12 kN" reazioni="A,1.2,0,H_A;A,0,1.2,V_A;E,1.2,0,H_E;E,0,1.2,V_E" tratteggi="3,4 6,0" titolo="Free-body diagram showing resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ of the horizontal load; the dashed line is $CE$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + H_E + 12 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_E = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_E - 12 \cdot 2 = 0 \\[4pt]
3\,V_E + 4\,H_E = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_E = 4\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad V_A = -4\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad H_E = -3\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad H_A = -9\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Force in the hinge, from the balance of the body $CDE$:
$$
\begin{cases}
X_C + H_E = 0 \\[4pt]
Y_C + V_E = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
X_C = 3\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_C = -4\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check, moment equilibrium of body $ABC$ about $C$:
$$
-3\,V_A + 4\,H_A + 12 \cdot 2 = 12 - 36 + 24 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The horizontal load has resultant $3 \cdot 4 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, applied in the middle of the column, at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ elevation. The equations are the two translation and rotation around $A$ of the whole structure and the auxiliary equation in $C$ for the body $CDE$. In the rotation around $A$ the resultant has a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and clockwise moment. The body $CDE$ is unloaded, so only $V_E$, with $3\,\mathrm{m}$ arm, and $H_E$, applied $4\,\mathrm{m}$ under $C$, appear in the auxiliary equation.

The results are: $H_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, $H_E = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, $V_E = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The hinge $A$, close to the load, absorbs the largest part of it. Vertical reactions form a counterclockwise couple that balances the clockwise moment of the horizontal load.

The body $CDE$ is unloaded and hinged at $C$ and $E$, so for the **unloaded member hinged at the two extremes** theorem the force it receives at $C$ is directed along $CE$. The line $CE$ drops by $4\,\mathrm{m}$ every $3\,\mathrm{m}$, and in fact $\dfrac{Y_C}{X_C} = -\dfrac{4}{3}$. This observation gives immediate check of the $\dfrac{H_E}{V_E} = -\dfrac{3}{4}$ ratio.

</div>

## Exercise 3

In the three-hinged frame shown, column $AB$ has height $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and column $DE$ has height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ because hinge $E$ is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ higher than hinge $A$. Beam $BD$ has length $8\,\mathrm{m}$ and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. The beam carries a uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and at joint $B$ a horizontal force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Calculate the support reactions and the force at hinge $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,6;C,4,6;D,8,6;E,8,2" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" carichi="B,D,0.6,0.6,q" forze="B,1.5,0,F" quote="A,B,6 m,0.8;E,D,4 m,0.8;B,C,4 m,-1;C,D,4 m,-1" tratteggi="0,2 8,2" titolo="Portal frame with columns of unequal height, load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, and force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

Rotation of the whole structure around $A$ and auxiliary equation for body $CDE$ around $C$:
$$
\begin{cases}
8\,V_E - 2\,H_E - 6\,F - 32 \cdot 4 = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_E + 4\,H_E - 16 \cdot 2 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\begin{cases}
8\,V_E - 2\,H_E = 164 \\[4pt]
V_E + H_E = 8
\end{cases}
$$
$$
8\,V_E - 2\,(8 - V_E) = 164 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad V_E = 18\,\mathrm{kN}, \qquad H_E = -10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Translations of the whole structure:
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + H_E + F = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_E - 32 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
H_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad V_A = 14\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Force in the hinge, from the balance of the body $CDE$:
$$
\begin{cases}
X_C + H_E = 0 \\[4pt]
Y_C + V_E - 16 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
X_C = 10\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_C = -2\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check, moment equilibrium of body $ABC$ about $C$:
$$
-4\,V_A + 6\,H_A + 16 \cdot 2 = -56 + 24 + 32 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The distributed load has resultant $4 \cdot 8 = 32\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the entire crossbeam, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ horizontally, and $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ on each half. The hinge $E$ is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ higher than $A$, so in the rotation around $A$ the reaction $H_E$ has a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and, if positive to the right, clockwise moment. In the same equation the force $F$, at elevation $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and the resultant of the load have clockwise moment, and $V_E$ has counterclockwise moment.

In the auxiliary equation for the body $CDE$ the hinge $E$ is located $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C$, so $V_E$ and $H_E$ have the same counterclockwise moment arm and moment; the resultant of the right half load, at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$, has clockwise moment. A system of two equations is obtained in $V_E$ and $H_E$, which is solved by substitution by deriving $H_E$ from the second.

The results are: $V_A = 14\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_E = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ up, $H_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ right and $H_E = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ left. The body $ABC$ exerts on the body $CDE$ a force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. In the check for the body $ABC$ around $C$, $H_A$ is applied $6\,\mathrm{m}$ under $C$ and has counterclockwise moment, $F$ is at the elevation of $C$ and has zero moment, the resultant of the load of the left half has counterclockwise moment.

</div>
