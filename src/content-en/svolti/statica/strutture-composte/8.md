## Exercise 1

The Gerber beam shown has the cantilever $AC$ fixed at $A$ and the section $CB$, connected to the cantilever by the hinge $C$ and resting on a roller support at $B$. In the hinge $C$, the vertical force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward; the section $CB$ carries the uniform load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Calculate the support reactions and the force transmitted by the hinge in the two cases: (a) $P$ applied to the $C$ extreme of the segment $CB$; (b) $P$ applied at end $C$ of the cantilever.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.6,P" carichi="C,B,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,C,3 m,0.8;C,B,4 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam with force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at hinge $C$ and load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $CB$"}

Case (a), $CB$ segment with $P$ and the resultant $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ load:
$$
\begin{cases}
Y_C + V_B - P - 20 = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_B - 20 \cdot 2 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_C = 30\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
V_A - Y_C = 0 \\[4pt]
M_A - 3\,Y_C = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_A = 30\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad M_A = 90\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Case (b), $CB$ segment with only distributed load and cantilever with $P$:
$$
\begin{cases}
Y_C + V_B - 20 = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_B - 20 \cdot 2 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_C = 10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
V_A - P - Y_C = 0 \\[4pt]
M_A - 3\,P - 3\,Y_C = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_A = 30\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad M_A = 60 + 30 = 90\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Check, rotation of the entire beam around $B$ (equal in both cases):
$$
M_A - 7\,V_A + 4\,P + 20 \cdot 2 = 90 - 210 + 80 + 40 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

A force applied at an internal hinge must be assigned to one of the two connected bodies. The exercise considers both possibilities. In either case, taking moments for segment $CB$ about $C$ gives $V_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ because force $P$, applied at $C$, has zero moment arm about $C$. The vertical force balance for segment $CB$ does change. In case (a), force $P$ acts on this segment and the cantilever must exert $Y_C = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ to support it. In case (b), force $P$ acts on the cantilever and the hinge transmits only the portion of the distributed load that the roller does not carry, $Y_C = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The external reactions are the same in the two cases, as the observation on **load applied in the hinge** states: the equations of the whole structure contain $P$ at the same point, whatever the body that receives it. In case (a) the cantilever receives at $C$ a force of $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward; in case (b) it receives $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ transmitted by the segment $CB$, still $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ throughout. The results are: $V_A = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $M_A = 90\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise, $H_A = 0$; the vertical force in the hinge is $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ in case (a) and $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in case (b).

</div>

## Exercise 2

The three-hinged frame shown has columns of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a beam of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. At hinge $C$, a downward force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts on body $ABC$. Body $CDE$ carries a counterclockwise couple at joint $D$ of magnitude $\mathcal{M} = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Calculate the support reactions and the force at hinge $C$. How would the hinge force change if $P$ acted on body $CDE$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,8,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" forze="C,0,-1.6,P" coppie="D,1" etichette="7.35,4.6,M" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,C,4 m,-0.8;C,D,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$ and counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ applied at $D$ to body $CDE$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,8,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" forze="C,0,-1.6,P" coppie="D,1" etichette="7.35,4.6,M" reazioni="A,1.2,0,H_A;A,0,1.2,V_A;E,1.2,0,H_E;E,0,1.2,V_E" titolo="Free-body diagram of the entire structure"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + H_E = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_E - P = 0 \\[4pt]
8\,V_E - 4\,P + \mathcal{M} = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_E + 4\,H_E + \mathcal{M} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_E = \frac{80 - 16}{8} = 8\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad H_E = \frac{-32 - 16}{4} = -12\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad H_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Force in the hinge, from the balance of the body $CDE$ (without $P$):
$$
\begin{cases}
X_C + H_E = 0 \\[4pt]
Y_C + V_E = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
X_C = 12\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_C = -8\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

With $P$ applied to the body $CDE$:
$$
\begin{cases}
X_C + H_E = 0 \\[4pt]
Y_C + V_E - P = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
X_C = 12\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_C = 20 - 8 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check, rotation of the whole structure around $E$:
$$
-8\,V_A + 4\,P + \mathcal{M} = -96 + 80 + 16 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The equations are the two translation and rotation around $A$ of the whole structure and the auxiliary equation in $C$ for the body $CDE$. The force $P$ is applied at $C$, so it has zero moment arm about $C$ and does not appear in the auxiliary equation, to whichever body is assigned. The couple $\mathcal{M}$ is counterclockwise and applied to the body $CDE$, so it enters with the plus sign in the rotation of the whole structure and in the auxiliary equation for $CDE$.

The results are: $V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_E = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ up, $H_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ right and $H_E = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ left. Without the couple the structure and load would be symmetrical and the vertical reactions would equal $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ each; the counterclockwise couple takes $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $V_E$ and adds them to $V_A$, because the vertical reactions must form a clockwise couple of $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on a moment arm of $8\,\mathrm{m}$.

The force in the hinge depends on where $P$ is applied. With $P$ on the body $ABC$ the body $CDE$ receives at $C$ a force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward; with $P$ on the body $CDE$ the horizontal component does not change and the vertical one becomes $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, because now the body $ABC$ must support a part of $P$ through the hinge. The external reactions remain the same. In the check around $E$ the reaction $V_A$ has clockwise moment, $P$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $E$, has counterclockwise moment, and horizontal reactions have zero moment arm.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The frame shown has three bodies. Body $ABC$ consists of column $AB$ of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ pinned to the ground at $A$ and beam segment $BC$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Beam $CD$ has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and is pinned at $C$ to body $ABC$ and at $D$ to column $DE$. Column $DE$ has height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and is fixed to the ground at $E$. Beam $CD$ carries a uniform load $q = 8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and at joint $B$ a horizontal force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Calculate the support reactions and the forces at hinges $C$ and $D$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4;D,6,4;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C;D" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,incastro" carichi="C,D,0.6,0.6,q" forze="B,1.4,0,F" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,C,3 m,-1;C,D,3 m,-1" titolo="Three-body frame with internal hinges $C$ and $D$, a fixed support at $E$, load $q = 8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $CD$, and force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

$$
n = 3, \qquad 3n = 9, \qquad s = 2 + 2 + 2 + 3 = 9
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C1,3,4,-;C2,5,6.5,-;D1,8,6.5,-;D2,10,4,-;E,10,0" aste="A-B;B-C1;C2-D1;D2-E" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,incastro" forze="6.5 6.5,0,-1.2,24 kN;B,1.4,0,F;C1,-1,0,X_C;C1,0,-1,Y_C;D1,-1,0,X_D;D1,0,-1,Y_D" reazioni="C2,1,0,X_C;C2,0,1,Y_C;D2,1,0,X_D;D2,0,1,Y_D;A,1.2,0,H_A;A,0,1.2,V_A;E,1.2,0,H_E;E,0,1.2,V_E" coppie="E,1,M_E" etichette="2.8,4.4,C;4.8,6.9,C;8.1,6.9,D;10.2,4.4,D" punti="3,4;5,6.5;8,6.5;10,4" titolo="Three isolated bodies: $X_C$ and $Y_C$ act on beam $CD$, and $X_D$ and $Y_D$ on column $DE$; equal and opposite forces act on the other bodies"}

$CD$ beam, with the resultant $8 \cdot 3 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ in half:
$$
\begin{cases}
X_C - X_D = 0 \\[4pt]
-3\,Y_C + 24 \cdot 1.5 = 0 \\[4pt]
-3\,Y_D - 24 \cdot 1.5 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
Y_C = 12\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad Y_D = -12\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad X_D = X_C
$$

Body $ABC$, with forces $-X_C$ and $-Y_C$ in $C$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-4\,F + 4\,X_C - 3\,Y_C = 0 \\[4pt]
H_A + F - X_C = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A - Y_C = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
X_C = \frac{24 + 36}{4} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad H_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Column $DE$, with forces $X_D = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $Y_D = -12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $D$:
$$
\begin{cases}
H_E + X_D = 0 \\[4pt]
V_E + Y_D = 0 \\[4pt]
M_E - 4\,X_D = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
H_E = -15\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad V_E = 12\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad M_E = 60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

Check, rotation of the whole structure around $A$:
$$
M_E + 6\,V_E - 4\,F - 24 \cdot 4.5 = 60 + 72 - 24 - 108 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The frame has three bodies and nine scalar constraints: the $A$, $C$, $D$ hinges give two each and the fixed $E$ support three. The unknowns are nine: five external reactions and four components of the forces in the hinges. $X_C$, $Y_C$ are the components of the force that the body $ABC$ exerts on the beam $CD$, and $X_D$, $Y_D$ are those of the force that the beam $CD$ exerts on the column $DE$; $-X_D$ and $-Y_D$ act on the beam in $D$. You **disassemble** the structure and you choose the order of the bodies so that you have few unknowns by equation.

It starts with the beam $CD$, which has only the four unknowns of the hinges. The rotation around $D$ eliminates forces in $D$ and, since $C$ and $D$ are at the same elevation, $X_C$ also remains: $Y_C$, with a moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Similarly moment equilibrium about $C$ gives $Y_D$, and the horizontal force equilibrium gives $X_D = X_C$. To find $X_C$, consider body $ABC$ and take moments about $A$. Force $F$ produces clockwise moment $4F$. Force $-X_C$ at $C$, acting leftward at elevation $4\,\mathrm{m}$, produces counterclockwise moment $4X_C$. Force $-Y_C$, acting downward a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, produces clockwise moment $3Y_C$. $ABC$ body translation gives $H_A$ and $V_A$. Finally the column $DE$ receives at $D$ the force of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, and the fixed support balances it; the horizontal force at $4\,\mathrm{m}$ elevation above $E$ has clockwise moment $60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, so $M_E$ is counterclockwise.

The results are: $H_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, $V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $H_E = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, $V_E = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $M_E = 60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise. Hinges transmit to the beam a horizontal compression force of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ and support each $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ of the load. In the check around $A$ enter the moment of fixed support, $V_E$ with a moment arm of $6\,\mathrm{m}$, $F$ with a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and the resultant of the load with a moment arm of $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$; reactions in $A$ and $H_E$, applied at the elevation of $A$, have zero moment.

</div>
