## Exercise 1

The Gerber beam shown is fixed at $A$, it has an internal pin at $C$ $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and a roller support at $B$ $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$; carries the uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire length. Get shear and moment with the differential equilibrium equations, using the condition in the hinge, reactions and diagrams.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,C,4 m,1;C,B,2 m,1" titolo="Gerber beam with a fixed support at $A$, a hinge at $C$, and uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

$$
T(x) = c_1 - 3x ,
\qquad
M(x) = c_2 + c_1 x - \frac{3}{2}\,x^2
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
M(4) = c_2 + 4\,c_1 - 24 = 0 \\
M(6) = c_2 + 6\,c_1 - 54 = 0
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
c_1 = 15\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
c_2 = -36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
T(x) = 15 - 3x ,
\qquad
M(x) = -36 + 15x - \frac{3}{2}\,x^2
$$
$$
V_A = T(0) = 15\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_A = -M(0) = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
V_B = -T(6) = 3\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
T(5) = 0 ,
\qquad
M(5) = -36 + 75 - 37.5 = 1.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check, moment balance for part } CB \text{ about } C: \quad V_B \cdot 2 - q \cdot 2 \cdot 1 = 6 - 6 = 0
$$
$$
\text{check, moments about } A: \quad M_A + V_B \cdot 6 - q \cdot 6 \cdot 3 = 36 + 18 - 54 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" diagramma="0,0,6,0,15-3*s" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" diagramma="0,0,6,0,-36+15*s-1.5*s^2" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$), zero at hinge $C$ and at $B$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The structure has two bodies, $AC$ and $CB$, hence $3 \cdot 2 = 6$ degrees of freedom, and six scalar constraints: three of fixed support, two of internal hinge and one of roller support. It is statically determinate. The load is uniform and there are no forces concentrated between $A$ and $B$, so, with the equations indefinite, a single couple of constants describes the whole beam, because the internal hinge unloaded does not produce shear or moment jumps.

Fixed support does not impose conditions. The two **boundary conditions** are the zero moment in the **internal hinge** $C$, which transmits no moment, and the zero moment in the $B$ end support. The system gives $c_1 = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $c_2 = -36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The reactions are **$V_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $M_A = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise and $V_B = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward**. The segment $CB$ is the suspended segment, and its reactions are $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $C$ and $B$, as in a simply supported beam of span $2\,\mathrm{m}$, and the maximum moment at $x = 5\,\mathrm{m}$ is $\dfrac{3 \cdot 2^2}{8} = 1.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. On the segment $AC$ the moment is negative and equals $-36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at the fixed end. The verifications are the rotation of only the $CB$ part around $C$ and the rotation of the whole beam around $A$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The Gerber beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller support at $B$ $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, an internal pin at $C$ $8\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and a roller support at $D$ $12\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$; carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire length. Get shear and moment for segments, reactions and maximum moments.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0;D,12,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" cerniere="C" carichi="A,D,0.9,0.9,q" quote="A,B,6 m,1;B,C,2 m,1;C,D,4 m,1" titolo="Gerber beam with overhang $BC$ and suspended beam $CD$ under uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

$$
\text{segment } AB: \qquad T_1(x) = a - 2x ,
\qquad
M_1(x) = a x - x^2
$$
$$
\text{segment } BD: \qquad T_2(x) = b - 2x ,
\qquad
M_2(x) = c + b x - x^2
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
M_2(8) = c + 8b - 64 = 0 \\
M_2(12) = c + 12b - 144 = 0 \\
M_1(6) = M_2(6): \quad 6a - 36 = c + 6b - 36
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
b = 20\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
c = -96\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
a = 4\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
T_1(x) = 4 - 2x ,
\qquad
M_1(x) = 4x - x^2 ,
\qquad
T_2(x) = 20 - 2x ,
\qquad
M_2(x) = -96 + 20x - x^2
$$
$$
V_A = T_1(0) = 4\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_B = T_2(6) - T_1(6) = 8 - (-8) = 16\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_D = -T_2(12) = 4\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
M_1(2) = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
M(6) = 24 - 36 = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
M_2(10) = -96 + 200 - 100 = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check, vertical force balance:} \quad V_A + V_B + V_D - q \cdot 12 = 4 + 16 + 4 - 24 = 0
$$
$$
\text{check, moments about } A: \quad V_B \cdot 6 + V_D \cdot 12 - q \cdot 12 \cdot 6 = 96 + 48 - 144 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0;D,12,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" cerniere="C" diagramma="0,0,6,0,4-2*s;6,0,12,0,8-2*s" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$), with a jump of $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0;D,12,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" cerniere="C" diagramma="0,0,6,0,4*s-s^2;6,0,12,0,-12+8*s-s^2" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$), zero at $A$, $C$, and $D$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The structure has two bodies, $AC$ and $CD$, and six scalar constraints: two of the pin at $A$, one for each roller support and two of the Inner hinge; is statically determinate. The reaction at $B$ is a concentrated force, therefore the segments $AB$ and $BD$ are distinguished; the pin at $C$ is unloaded and does not interrupt the segment $BD$. The $M(0) = 0$ condition is already in the writing of $M_1$.

The three remaining conditions form the system: zero moment in the **internal hinge** $C$, zero moment in the $D$ end support and moment continuity in $B$, where the shear has an unknown jump. The first two equations contain only $b$ and $c$ and resolve first; The third gives $a$. It is the same order as the calculation of the Gerber beam with the balance, which starts from the suspended segment $CD$.

The reactions are **$V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_B = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_D = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward**. The shear vanishes in $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x = 10\,\mathrm{m}$, where the moment has two positive maximums of $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$; on the $B$ support, where the shear changes sign with the jump, the moment has the minimum $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The verifications are vertical translation and rotation around $A$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The Gerber beam shown is fixed at $A$, it has an internal pin at $C$ $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and a roller support at $B$ $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$; brings only the downward force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $E$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Get shear and moment, reactions and diagrams.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;E,4,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" forze="E,0,-1.3,F" quote="A,C,3 m,1;C,E,1 m,1;E,B,1 m,1" titolo="Gerber beam with force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ on suspended beam $CB$"}

$$
\text{segment } AE: \qquad T_1(x) = a ,
\qquad
M_1(x) = c + a x
$$
$$
\text{segment } EB: \qquad T_2(x) = a - 10 ,
\qquad
M_2(x) = c + a x - 10\,(x - 4)
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
M_1(3) = c + 3a = 0 \\
M_2(5) = c + 5a - 10 = 0
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
a = 5\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
c = -15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
V_A = T_1(0) = 5\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_A = -M(0) = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
V_B = -T_2(5) = 5\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
M(0) = -15\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
M(3) = 0 ,
\qquad
M(4) = -15 + 20 = 5\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
M(5) = 0
$$
$$
\text{check, moments about } A: \quad M_A + V_B \cdot 5 - F \cdot 4 = 15 + 25 - 40 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;E,4,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" diagramma="0,0,4,0,5;4,0,5,0,-5" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;E,4,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" diagramma="0,0,4,0,-15+5*s;4,0,5,0,5-5*s" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$), piecewise linear and zero at the hinge"}

<div class="spiegazione">

There are no distributed loads, so in each segment the shear is constant and the moment linear; the force at $E$ divides the beam into sections $AE$ and $EB$ and, for the **rule of jumps**, the shear decreases by $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ crossing it. The moment is continuous in $E$, so both segments have the same constant $c$. Fixed support does not give conditions; conditions are the zero moment in the **internal hinge** $C$ and $B$ mount.

The reactions are **$V_A = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $M_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise and $V_B = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward**. The force acts at the midpoint of suspended beam $CB$, so $C$ and $B$ share it equally; $5\,\mathrm{kN}$'s force transmitted in $C$ acts on the cantilever $AC$, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, and produces at the fixed end the moment $-5 \cdot 3 = -15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The moment diagram is a broken one: from $-15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $A$ it rises with $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ slope, passes through zero in the hinge, reaches $5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ below the force, where it has a corner, and returns to zero in $B$. The maximum of $|M|$ is at the fixed end. The check is the rotation around $A$.

</div>
