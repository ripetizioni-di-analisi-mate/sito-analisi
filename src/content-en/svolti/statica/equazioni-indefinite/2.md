## Exercise 1

The cantilever shown is fixed at $A$, has a span of $L = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and carries the uniform $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load. Get with the differential equilibrium equations the shear and bending moment, the reactions of fixed support, and draw diagrams.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.45,0.45,q" quote="A,B,4 m,1" titolo="Cantilever under uniform load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

$$
T(x) = c_1 - 5x ,
\qquad
M(x) = c_2 + c_1 x - \frac{5}{2}\,x^2
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
T(4) = c_1 - 20 = 0 \\
M(4) = c_2 + 4\,c_1 - 40 = 0
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
c_1 = 20\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
c_2 = -40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
T(x) = 20 - 5x = 5\,(4 - x) ,
\qquad
M(x) = -40 + 20x - \frac{5}{2}\,x^2 = -\frac{5}{2}\,(4 - x)^2
$$
$$
V_A = T(0) = 20\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M(0^+) = -M_A
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
M_A = -M(0) = 40\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
H_A = 0
$$
$$
\text{check, moments about } B: \quad -V_A \cdot 4 + M_A + q \cdot 4 \cdot 2 = -20 \cdot 4 + 40 + 5 \cdot 4 \cdot 2 = -80 + 40 + 40 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,4,0,20-5*s" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,4,0,-2.5*(4-s)^2" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$), drawn on the side of the upper fibers"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The cantilever is a rigid body with fixed support at $A$, which is a triple constraint, and is statically determinate. It places the origin at the fixed end with the axis $x$ towards the free end $B$. The **differential equilibrium equations** give, as in any segment with uniform load, a linear shear and a parabolic moment.

Fixed support does not impose conditions on features, because it transmits any shear and moment. The two conditions are then written at the unloaded free end $B$, where shear and moment are zero. Constants are obtained from the system, and expressions are written compactly with the factor $4 - x$, i.e. the distance of the section from the free end.

Reactions are derived from jumps in $A$. The shear goes from $0$ to $V_A$, and the moment goes from $0$ to $-M_A$, because the $M_A$ reactive couple is counterclockwise. You get **$V_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $M_A = 40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise**. The moment is negative throughout the cantilever, so it tends the higher fibers, and its absolute value is maximum at the fixed end. The shear vanishes only in the free end, where the moment equals zero; therefore in this structure the maximum of $|M|$ is at the end of the segment. The check is the rotation around $B$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The cantilever shown is fixed at $A$ and has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$. It carries a triangular load that decreases from $q_0 = 12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at the fixed end to zero at the free end $B$. Integrate the differential equilibrium equations starting at the free end to determine the shear force, bending moment, and fixed-end reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.6,0,q_0" quote="A,B,3 m,1" titolo="Cantilever under a triangular load of intensity $q_0 = 12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at the fixed end"}

$$
q(x) = q_0\left(1 - \frac{x}{L}\right) = 12\left(1 - \frac{x}{3}\right) = 4\,(3 - x)
$$
$$
T(x) = T(3) + \int_x^3 q(t)\,dt = 0 + \int_x^3 4\,(3 - t)\,dt = 2\,(3 - x)^2
$$
$$
M(x) = M(3) - \int_x^3 T(t)\,dt = 0 - \int_x^3 2\,(3 - t)^2\,dt = -\frac{2}{3}\,(3 - x)^3
$$
$$
V_A = T(0) = 2 \cdot 9 = 18\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_A = -M(0) = \frac{2}{3} \cdot 27 = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check, moments about } A: \quad M_A - \frac{q_0 L}{2} \cdot \frac{L}{3} = 18 - 18 \cdot 1 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,3,0,2*(3-s)^2" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$), a parabola with a horizontal tangent at $B$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,3,0,-2/3*(3-s)^3" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$), a cubic curve"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The load intensity decreases linearly from $q_0$ at $x = 0$ to zero at $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$. Factoring gives $q(x) = 4\,(3 - x)$. Since the boundary conditions are known at the free end, use the **integral form** of the equilibrium equations between an arbitrary section $x$ and the tip $B$. From $T(3) - T(x) = -\displaystyle\int_x^3 q\,dt$ and $T(3) = 0$, the shear at $x$ equals the resultant of the load to the right of the section. Similarly, $M(3) - M(x) = \displaystyle\int_x^3 T\,dt$ together with $M(3) = 0$ gives the bending moment.

The shear is a parabola and the moment a cubic, as the degree of the curves for a first degree load predicts. In the free end the load is zero, so the shear derivative, $-q$, vanishes, and the shear diagram has horizontal tangent in $B$.

The reactions are **$V_A = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $M_A = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise**. The first value is the resultant of the load, $\dfrac{q_0 L}{2}$; the second is the moment of the resultant about $A$, because the resultant is applied in the triangle centroid, to $\dfrac{L}{3} = 1\,\mathrm{m}$ by fixed support. The check just writes this rotation around $A$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The cantilever shown is fixed to the right, in $B$, and is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long; carries the uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and, in the free end $A$, the force $F = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. With the origin in $A$, derive shear and moment with the differential equilibrium equations, the fixed support reactions, and draw diagrams.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="B,incastro,0" carichi="A,B,0.45,0.45,q" forze="A,0,-1,F" quote="A,B,4 m,1" titolo="Cantilever fixed at $B$ with uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and force $F = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the free end"}

$$
T(0^+) = -F = -5\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M(0) = 0
$$
$$
T(x) = -5 - 3x ,
\qquad
M(x) = \int_0^x T(t)\,dt = -5x - \frac{3}{2}\,x^2
$$
$$
T(4) = -17\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M(4) = -20 - 24 = -44\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
V_B = -T(4) = 17\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M(4^+) - M(4^-) = -M_B
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
0 - (-44) = -M_B ,
\qquad
M_B = -44\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check, vertical force balance:} \quad V_B - F - q \cdot 4 = 17 - 5 - 12 = 0
$$
$$
\text{check, moments about } A: \quad V_B \cdot 4 + M_B - q \cdot 4 \cdot 2 = 68 - 44 - 24 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="B,incastro,0" diagramma="0,0,4,0,-5-3*s" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="B,incastro,0" diagramma="0,0,4,0,-5*s-1.5*s^2" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$), drawn on the side of the upper fibers"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The free end is left and the origin is placed there, so boundary conditions are written in $x = 0$. In $A$ the force $F$ acts downward, so for the **rule of jumps** the shear goes from $0$, out of the beam, to $T(0^+) = -F$. The moment at $A$ is zero, because at the end no couple is applied.

The shear starts from $-5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and decreases with $-q$ slope; the moment is the integral of the shear from $0$ to $x$, i.e. the area of the shear diagram, and is negative throughout the cantilever. The vertical reaction is the leap of the shear at the fixed end. The shear goes from $-17\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $0$, then **$V_B = 17\,\mathrm{kN}$, Upward**.

The moment switches from $-44\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ to $0$, and the moment jump under an $M_B$ counterclockwise couple is $-M_B$. You get $M_B = -44\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, that is **a reaction moment of $44\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise**. About the cantilever fixed on the left the sign of the reaction moment changes, while the diagram remains on the side of the upper fibers. The two verifications are vertical translation and rotation around $A$, both not used in the calculation.

</div>
