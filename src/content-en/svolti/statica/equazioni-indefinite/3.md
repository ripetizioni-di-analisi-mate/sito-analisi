## Exercise 1

The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the vertical forces $F_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $C$ and $F_2 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $D$, down. To gain the shear and the moment with the differential equilibrium equations and the rule of jumps, reactions and the maximum moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.5,F_1;D,0,-1,F_2" quote="A,C,2 m,1;C,D,2 m,1;D,B,2 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with $F_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $F_2 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

$$
T = a \text{ on } AC ,
\qquad
T = a - 12 \text{ on } CD ,
\qquad
T = a - 18 \text{ on } DB
$$
$$
M(0) = 0 ,
\qquad
M(6) = \int_0^6 T\,dx = 2a + 2\,(a - 12) + 2\,(a - 18) = 6a - 60 = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
a = 10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
T = 10\,\mathrm{kN} \text{ on } AC ,
\qquad
T = -2\,\mathrm{kN} \text{ on } CD ,
\qquad
T = -8\,\mathrm{kN} \text{ on } DB
$$
$$
M(2) = 10 \cdot 2 = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
M(4) = 20 - 2 \cdot 2 = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
M(6) = 16 - 8 \cdot 2 = 0
$$
$$
V_A = T(0^+) = 10\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_B = -T(6^-) = 8\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_{\max} = M(2) = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check, moments about } B: \quad -V_A \cdot 6 + F_1 \cdot 4 + F_2 \cdot 2 = -60 + 48 + 12 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,10;2,0,4,0,-2;4,0,6,0,-8" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$), piecewise constant with jumps of $-F_1$ and $-F_2$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,10*s;2,0,4,0,20-2*s;4,0,6,0,16-8*s" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$), piecewise linear with corners at $C$ and $D$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Between the supports there are no distributed loads, so in each of the three $AC$, $CD$, $DB$ sections the shear is constant and the moment linear. $a$ is the shear of the first segment. For the **rule of jumps** under each downward-directed force the shear decreases in the value of the force, and so the shear of the other two segments are obtained as a function of the unknown $a$ alone.

The moment is zero in $A$ and $B$. By the integral form of the differential equilibrium equations $M(6) - M(0)$ is the area of the shear diagram, and by imposing that it is zero you get $a = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$. The moment values in $C$ and $D$ are obtained by adding up the areas of the rectangles of the shear diagram, proceeding from the left.

Reactions are the shear in the supports, **$V_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward**. The shear changes sign to $C$, with a jump, so the moment grows to $C$ and decreases after, so the maximum is **$M_{\max} = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ under the force $F_1$**. In $C$ and $D$ the moment diagram has corners, because the shear, which is the slope, is discontinuous. The check is the rotation around $B$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $8\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over all span and downward force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $C$, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Get shear and moment for segments, reactions and maximum moment, and draw diagrams.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.7,0.7;C,B,0.7,0.7,q" forze="C,0,-2,F" quote="A,C,2 m,1;C,B,6 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam under uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

$$
\text{segment } AC: \qquad T_1(x) = a - 3x ,
\qquad
M_1(x) = a x - \frac{3}{2}\,x^2
$$
$$
\text{segment } CB: \qquad T_2(x) = T_1(x) - 12 = a - 12 - 3x ,
\qquad
M_2(x) = b + (a - 12)\,x - \frac{3}{2}\,x^2
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
M_2(2) = M_1(2): \quad b + 2a - 24 - 6 = 2a - 6 \\
M_2(8) = 0: \quad b + 8a - 96 - 96 = 0
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
b = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
a = 21\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
T_1(x) = 21 - 3x ,
\qquad
M_1(x) = 21x - \frac{3}{2}\,x^2 ,
\qquad
T_2(x) = 9 - 3x ,
\qquad
M_2(x) = 24 + 9x - \frac{3}{2}\,x^2
$$
$$
V_A = T_1(0) = 21\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_B = -T_2(8) = -(9 - 24) = 15\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
T_1(2^-) = 15\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
T_2(2^+) = 3\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
T_2(x) = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x = 3\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
M(2) = 42 - 6 = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
M_{\max} = M_2(3) = 24 + 27 - 13.5 = 37.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check, moments about } A: \quad V_B \cdot 8 - F \cdot 2 - q \cdot 8 \cdot 4 = 120 - 24 - 96 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,21-3*s;2,0,8,0,3-3*s" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$), with a jump of $-12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$ and a zero at $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,21*s-1.5*s^2;2,0,8,0,36+3*s-1.5*s^2" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$), with a maximum at $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The force at $C$ divides the beam into two sections, both with uniform load, so in each the shear is linear and the moment parabolic. The $M(0) = 0$ condition is already used in writing $M_1$. For **rule of jumps** the shear decreases by $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ through $C$, and for this $T_2$ is written as $T_1 - 12$; the moment is continuous in $C$ and zero in $B$. The two remaining conditions form the system, hence $a = 21\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $b = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The reactions are **$V_A = 21\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward**. The shear to the left of $C$ equals $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ and to the right $3\,\mathrm{kN}$; the shear remains positive, so the moment continues to grow beyond force. The shear vanishes in the second segment, in $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$, and there, since $q > 0$, the moment has the **maximum $37.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$**.

The moment under force, $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, is less than the maximum. The diagram has in $C$ a corner, with the slope falling from $15$ to $3\,\mathrm{kN}$, and between $C$ and the maximum point is still increasing. The check is the rotation around $A$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $5\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and, in $C$ $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, an inclined force of magnitude $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ with horizontal component $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and vertical component $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Find axial force, shear and moment with the differential equilibrium equations and the rule of jumps, and reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.5,0.5;C,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="C,0.96,-0.72,F" quote="A,C,2 m,1;C,B,3 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and an inclined force at $C$ with components $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $6\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

$$
N(5) = 0 ,
\qquad
N(2^+) - N(2^-) = -8
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
N = 8\,\mathrm{kN} \text{ on } AC ,
\qquad
N = 0 \text{ on } CB
$$
$$
H_A:\ N(0^+) - 0 = -H_A
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
H_A = -8\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
T_1(x) = a - 2x ,
\qquad
M_1(x) = a x - x^2 ,
\qquad
T_2(x) = a - 6 - 2x ,
\qquad
M_2(x) = b + (a - 6)\,x - x^2
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
M_2(2) = M_1(2): \quad b + 2a - 12 - 4 = 2a - 4 \\
M_2(5) = 0: \quad b + 5a - 30 - 25 = 0
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
b = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
a = 8.6\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
T_1(x) = 8.6 - 2x ,
\qquad
T_2(x) = 2.6 - 2x ,
\qquad
M_1(x) = 8.6\,x - x^2 ,
\qquad
M_2(x) = 12 + 2.6\,x - x^2
$$
$$
V_A = T_1(0) = 8.6\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_B = -T_2(5) = 7.4\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
T_1(2^-) = 4.6\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
T_2(2^+) = -1.4\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_{\max} = M(2) = 17.2 - 4 = 13.2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check, moments about } B: \quad -V_A \cdot 5 + 6 \cdot 3 + q \cdot 5 \cdot 2.5 = -43 + 18 + 25 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,8;2,0,5,0,0" segni=1 titolo="Axial force diagram $N$ ($\mathrm{kN}$), showing tension in segment $AC$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,8.6-2*s;2,0,5,0,-1.4-2*s" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$), changing sign across the jump at $C$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,8.6*s-s^2;2,0,5,0,13.2-1.4*s-s^2" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$), with its maximum under the force"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The inclined force breaks down into the two given components, which are treated separately. There are no distributed axial loads, so for the equation $\dfrac{dN}{dx} = -p$ the axial force is constant at times. The roller support at $B$ has vertical reaction and does not transmit horizontal forces, so $N(5) = 0$. Crossing $C$ the axial force jumps by $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$, and proceeding to the left you get **$N = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ tension on $AC$**. The jump to $A$ gives the horizontal reaction, $H_A = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. **$8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left**.

For the shear and the moment we proceed as with a vertical force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, with two segments with uniform load, the $-6\,\mathrm{kN}$ jump of the shear at $C$, the continuous moment at $C$ and zero in the supports. Vertical reactions are **$V_A = 8.6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 7.4\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward**.

The shear is positive until $C$ and negative after, and changes sign with the jump; in the first segment it remains greater than $4.6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and in the second less than $-1.4\,\mathrm{kN}$, so it does not cancel at any internal point. The maximum moment is therefore **under the force, $13.2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$**. The check is the rotation around $B$, in which the horizontal component of the force does not appear because its line of action passes through $B$.

</div>
