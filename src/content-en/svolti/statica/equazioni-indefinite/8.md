## Exercise 1

The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the distributed axial load $p = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, directed to the right, over all the span, and the horizontal force $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left in $C$, at midspan. Find the axial force with the differential equilibrium equations and the horizontal reaction at $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" frecce="0.1,0.45,0.55,0.45;0.85,0.45,1.3,0.45;1.6,0.45,2.05,0.45;2.35,0.45,2.80,0.45;3.1,0.45,3.55,0.45;3.85,0.45,4.3,0.45;4.6,0.45,5.05,0.45;5.35,0.45,5.8,0.45" etichette="0.3,0.75,p" forze="C,-1.4,0,F" quote="A,C,3 m,1;C,B,3 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with axial load $p = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to the right and force $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left at $C$"}

$$
\frac{dN}{dx} = -p = -2 ,
\qquad
N(6) = 0
$$
$$
\text{segment } CB: \qquad N(x) = N(6) + \int_x^6 2\,dt = 2\,(6 - x) ,
\qquad
N(3^+) = 6\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
N(3^+) - N(3^-) = -F_x = -(-4) = 4
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
N(3^-) = 2\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{segment } AC: \qquad N(x) = N(3^-) + \int_x^3 2\,dt = 2 + 2\,(3 - x) = 8 - 2x ,
\qquad
N(0) = 8\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
N(0^+) - 0 = -H_A
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
H_A = -8\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{check, horizontal force balance:} \quad H_A + p \cdot 6 - F = -8 + 12 - 4 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,3,0,8-2*s;3,0,6,0,6-2*s" segni=1 titolo="Axial force diagram $N$ ($\mathrm{kN}$), showing tension and a jump of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The axial load $p$ is positive to the right, and for the first **differential equilibrium equation** the axial force decreases by $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ for each meter traveled to the right. The roller support at $B$ has vertical reaction and does not transmit horizontal forces, so the boundary condition is $N(6) = 0$. Integrating from $B$ to the left results in the axial force of section $CB$, equal to the resultant axial load to the right of the section, because the beam part to the right of the section is in equilibrium under the action of that load and the axial force.

The force at $C$ is directed to the left, i.e. $F_x = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$; for the **rule of jumps** the axial force increases by $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ crossing $C$ to the right, then to the left of the $C$ equals $2\,\mathrm{kN}$. In the section $AC$ the axial force grows to the left until $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $A$. Axial force is positive everywhere, so all beam is **in tension**.

The jump to $A$ gives the horizontal reaction, **$H_A = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left**. There are no vertical loads, so the vertical reactions are zero, and so are shear and moment. Check is horizontal translation.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The cantilever shown is fixed at $A$ and has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. A downward force $F = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the free end $B$. Secondary cantilevers attached along its side apply a uniform counterclockwise distributed couple $m = 2\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$ over the full length. Use the differential equilibrium equations to obtain the shear force, bending moment, and fixed-end reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0,-1,F" coppie="1 0,1;2 0,1,m;3 0,1" quote="A,B,4 m,1" titolo="Cantilever with force $F = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$ and distributed counterclockwise couple $m = 2\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$"}

$$
\frac{dT}{dx} = -q = 0 ,
\qquad
T(4^-) = F = 3\,\mathrm{kN}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
T(x) = 3
$$
$$
\frac{dM}{dx} = T - m = 3 - 2 = 1 ,
\qquad
M(4) = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
M(x) = x - 4
$$
$$
V_A = T(0) = 3\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_A = -M(0) = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check, moments about } A: \quad M_A + m \cdot 4 - F \cdot 4 = 4 + 8 - 12 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,4,0,3" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,4,0,s-4" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$), with slope $T - m = 1\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

There are no distributed transverse loads, so the shear is constant; In the free end, the force $F$ acts downward, and for the **rule of jumps** $T(4^-) = F$. The shear equals $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ throughout the cantilever.

The distributed couple enters the third **differential equilibrium equation**, $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T - m$, so the slope of the moment is $T - m = 1\,\mathrm{kN}$, less than the shear. With the $M(4) = 0$ condition in the free end, free of concentrated couples, you get $M(x) = x - 4$. Without the distributed couple the moment at the fixed end would be $-F \cdot 4 = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$; the counterclockwise couple, of total moment $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, reduces it in magnitude to $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The reactions are **$V_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $M_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise**. The check is the rotation around $A$, in which the distributed couple contributes with its total $m \cdot 4$ moment, independent of the reference point.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and the uniform distributed couple $m = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$ counterclockwise. Get shear and moment with the differential equilibrium equations, reactions, position and maximum moment value.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" coppie="1.5 0,1;3 0,1,m;4.5 0,1" quote="A,B,6 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and distributed counterclockwise couple $m = 4\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$"}

$$
T(x) = c_1 - 2x ,
\qquad
\frac{dM}{dx} = T - m = c_1 - 4 - 2x ,
\qquad
M(x) = c_2 + (c_1 - 4)\,x - x^2
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
M(0) = c_2 = 0 \\
M(6) = c_2 + 6\,(c_1 - 4) - 36 = 0
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
c_2 = 0 ,
\qquad
c_1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
T(x) = 10 - 2x ,
\qquad
M(x) = 6x - x^2
$$
$$
V_A = T(0) = 10\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_B = -T(6) = 2\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\frac{dM}{dx} = 0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
T = m
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
10 - 2x = 4 ,
\qquad
x = 3\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
M_{\max} = 18 - 9 = 9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check, moments about } A: \quad V_B \cdot 6 - q \cdot 6 \cdot 3 + m \cdot 6 = 12 - 36 + 24 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,6,0,10-2*s" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$), zero at $x = 5\,\mathrm{m}$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,6,0,6*s-s^2" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$), with a maximum at $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ where $T = m$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The shear is obtained as always from $\dfrac{dT}{dx} = -q$. In the derivative of the moment instead appears the distributed couple, $\dfrac{dM}{dx} = T - m$, and integrating you get a parabolic moment with two constants, fixed by the **boundary conditions** $M(0) = 0$ and $M(6) = 0$.

The reactions are **$V_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward**. Without the distributed couple they would both be $6\,\mathrm{kN}$; the $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ total counterclockwise moment pair is balanced by a pair of reactions, $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward in $A$ and $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward in $B$.

The moment is $6x - x^2$, identical to that of the beam with the load $q$ alone. In this simply supported beam the uniform distributed pair modifies the shear but not the moment, because in each section its contribution $-4x$ is compensated by that of the reaction pair, $+4x$. The moment is stationary where $T = m$, i.e. in $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$, and not where the shear vanishes, which is $x = 5\,\mathrm{m}$. The **maximum is $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at midspan**. The check is the rotation around $A$.

</div>
