## Exercise 1

The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries only the couple $\mathcal{M} = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, counterclockwise, applied at $C$ $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Get shear and moment with the differential equilibrium equations and the rule of jumps, reactions, and draw diagrams.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="C,1,ℳ" quote="A,C,2 m,1;C,B,4 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $C$"}

$$
T(x) = a \quad \text{along the entire beam}
$$
$$
M_1(x) = a x \quad (0 \le x < 2) ,
\qquad
M_2(x) = a x - 18 \quad (2 < x \le 6)
$$
$$
M_2(6) = 6a - 18 = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
a = 3\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
V_A = T(0) = 3\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_B = -T(6) = -3\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
M(2^-) = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
M(2^+) = 6 - 18 = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check, moments about } A: \quad V_B \cdot 6 + \mathcal{M} = -3 \cdot 6 + 18 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,6,0,3" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$), constant and continuous across the applied couple"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,3*s;2,0,6,0,-12+3*s" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$), with a jump of $-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $C$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

There are no forces between the supports, so the shear has derived zero anywhere. Under one couple the shear is continuous, so only one $a$ constant is valid along the entire beam. The moment is linear with $a$ slope and, for the **rule of jumps**, crossing the $\mathcal{M}$ counterclockwise couple decreases by $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The $M(0) = 0$ condition is already in the writing of $M_1$, and the $M(6) = 0$ condition gives $a = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The reactions are **$V_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ up and $V_B = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ down**. The two reactions form a moment $3 \cdot 6 = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise couple, which balances the applied couple.

The moment has the two parallel branches, with the same slope $3\,\mathrm{kN}$, and a jump of $-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $C$. It equals $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ immediately to the left of $C$, with the lower fibers in tension, and $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ immediately to the right, with the upper fibers in tension. The maximum value of $|M|$ is **$12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, right of couple**, at the end of a segment. The check is the rotation around $A$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The cantilever shown is fixed at $A$ and has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. It carries a uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and, at its free end $B$, a counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Determine the shear force, bending moment, and fixed-end reactions, and locate the section where the bending moment is zero.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.45,0.45,q" coppie="B,1,ℳ" quote="A,B,4 m,1" titolo="Cantilever with uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $B$"}

$$
T(4) = 0 ,
\qquad
M(4^-) = \mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
T(x) = \int_x^4 q\,dt = 3\,(4 - x) ,
\qquad
M(x) = M(4^-) - \int_x^4 T\,dt = 12 - \frac{3}{2}\,(4 - x)^2
$$
$$
M(x) = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
(4 - x)^2 = 8 ,
\qquad
x = 4 - 2\sqrt{2} \approx 1.17\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
V_A = T(0) = 12\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M(0) = 12 - 24 = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
M_A = -M(0) = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check, moments about } B: \quad -V_A \cdot 4 + M_A + \mathcal{M} + q \cdot 4 \cdot 2 = -48 + 12 + 12 + 24 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,4,0,3*(4-s)" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,4,0,12-1.5*(4-s)^2" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$), changing sign at $x = 4 - 2\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The boundary conditions are written in the free end $B$. There no forces act, so $T(4) = 0$. Instead, the $\mathcal{M}$ counterclockwise couple acts, and for the **rule of jumps** the moment switches from $M(4^-)$ to $0$ with an $-\mathcal{M}$ jump; therefore $M(4^-) = \mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The shear is the resultant of the load to the right of the section, and the moment is obtained from the integral form starting from $B$.

The moment equals $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $B$, with the lower fibers in tension, and decreases going towards fixed support, where it equals $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension. It vanishes at section $x = 4 - 2\sqrt{2} \approx 1.17\,\mathrm{m}$, called **null point** of the moment, when the fibers in tension pass from one side of the beam to the other.

The reactions are **$V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $M_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise**, because under the reactive couple the moment jumps from $0$ to $-M_A$. The shear is positive, so the moment grows from $A$ to $B$ throughout the segment, and the extreme values are at the ends. The check is the rotation around $B$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and couple $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise, applied in the $A$ support. Get shear and moment, reactions and maximum moment, and draw diagrams.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" coppie="A,-1,ℳ" quote="A,B,6 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $A$"}

$$
T(x) = c_1 - 4x ,
\qquad
M(x) = c_2 + c_1 x - 2x^2
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
M(0^+) = -(-12) = 12: \quad c_2 = 12 \\
M(6) = 0: \quad c_2 + 6\,c_1 - 72 = 0
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
c_2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
c_1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
T(x) = 10 - 4x ,
\qquad
M(x) = 12 + 10x - 2x^2
$$
$$
V_A = T(0) = 10\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_B = -T(6) = -(10 - 24) = 14\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
T(x) = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x = 2.5\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
M_{\max} = 12 + 25 - 12.5 = 24.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check, moments about } B: \quad -V_A \cdot 6 - 12 + q \cdot 6 \cdot 3 = -60 - 12 + 72 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,6,0,10-4*s" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,6,0,12+10*s-2*s^2" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$), starting at $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at point $A$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The couple is applied in the support, so the boundary condition in $A$ is not $M = 0$. A clockwise couple corresponds to $\mathcal{M} = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in the counterclockwise convention, and for the **rule of jumps** the moment passes from $0$, left of the beam, to $M(0^+) = -\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. In $B$ there are no couples and the moment is zero. The two conditions together give the constants.

The reactions are **$V_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 14\,\mathrm{kN}$, upward**. Without the couple they would both be $12\,\mathrm{kN}$; the clockwise couple adds the reaction $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ pair in $B$ upward and $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $A$ downward, of $2 \cdot 6 = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise moment.

The shear vanishes in $x = 2.5\,\mathrm{m}$, to the left of the midspan, and there the moment has the **maximum $24.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$**. The moment diagram is the parabola of the simply supported beam, $12x - 2x^2$, added to the line $12 - 2x$ due to the couple, which equals $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $A$ and zero in $B$. The check is the rotation around $B$.

</div>
