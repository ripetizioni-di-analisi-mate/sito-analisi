## Exercise 1

The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller support at $B$ $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and an overhang of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the free end $C$; carries the uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire length. Get shear and moment, reactions, maximum moment in span, moment on $B$ support and zero point of moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.75,0.75,q" quote="A,B,6 m,1;B,C,2 m,1" titolo="Overhanging beam under uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

$$
\text{overhang } BC,\ 6 < x \le 8: \qquad T_2(x) = \int_x^8 3\,dt = 3\,(8 - x) ,
\qquad
M_2(x) = -\frac{3}{2}\,(8 - x)^2
$$
$$
M(6) = -\frac{3}{2} \cdot 4 = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{span } AB,\ 0 \le x < 6: \qquad T_1(x) = a - 3x ,
\qquad
M_1(x) = a x - \frac{3}{2}\,x^2
$$
$$
M_1(6) = 6a - 54 = -6
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
a = 8\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
V_A = T_1(0) = 8\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_B = T_2(6^+) - T_1(6^-) = 6 - (-10) = 16\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
T_1(x) = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x = \frac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
M_{\max} = 8 \cdot \frac{8}{3} - \frac{3}{2} \cdot \frac{64}{9} = \frac{32}{3} \approx 10.67\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
M_1(x) = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x\left(8 - \frac{3}{2}\,x\right) = 0 ,
\qquad
x = \frac{16}{3} \approx 5.33\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\text{check, moments about } A: \quad V_B \cdot 6 - q \cdot 8 \cdot 4 = 96 - 96 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,6,0,8-3*s;6,0,8,0,6-3*s" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$), with a jump of $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,6,0,8*s-1.5*s^2;6,0,8,0,-1.5*(2-s)^2" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$), positive in the span and negative at the support and along the overhang"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The $B$ support divides the beam into two sections, the span $AB$ and the $BC$ overhang. On the overhang the conditions are known in the free end, $T(8) = 0$ and $M(8) = 0$, so shear and moment are obtained as in a cantilever, integrating from $C$ to the left. The moment on the support equals $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and puts the upper fibers in tension.

The support reaction at $B$ is a point force. The shear therefore jumps there, while the bending moment remains continuous. Within the span the moment is zero at $A$ and equals $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $B$. This condition gives $a = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$. The jumps in the shear diagram give the two reactions, **$V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$, both upward**.

Within the span the shear vanishes at $x = \dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$, where the moment reaches its **maximum span value $\dfrac{32}{3} \approx 10.67\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$**. The moment then passes through zero at the **point of contraflexure $x = \dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$**. From there to the free end the top fibers are in tension. The shear also changes sign in the jump at $B$, where the moment reaches a local minimum, $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The maximum value of $|M|$ along the beam occurs within the span. Check by taking moments about $A$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, distant $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and two equal overhangs of $2\,\mathrm{m}$, with free extremes $C$ and $D$; carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire length of $10\,\mathrm{m}$. With the origin in $C$, derive shear and moment, the reactions, the moment on the supports and at midspan and the zero points of the moment.

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,2,0;B,8,0;D,10,0" aste="C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="C,D,0.8,0.8,q" quote="C,A,2 m,1;A,B,6 m,1;B,D,2 m,1" titolo="Beam with two overhangs under uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

$$
\text{overhang } CA: \qquad T(x) = -2x ,
\qquad
M(x) = -x^2 ,
\qquad
M(2) = -4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{span } AB: \qquad T(x) = T(2^+) - 2\,(x - 2) ,
\qquad
M(x) = -4 + T(2^+)\,(x - 2) - (x - 2)^2
$$
$$
M(8) = -4 + 6\,T(2^+) - 36 = -4
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
T(2^+) = 6\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
T(x) = 10 - 2x ,
\qquad
M(x) = -x^2 + 10\,(x - 2)
\quad (2 \le x \le 8)
$$
$$
V_A = T(2^+) - T(2^-) = 6 - (-4) = 10\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
M(5) = -25 + 30 = 5\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
M(x) = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x^2 - 10x + 20 = 0 ,
\qquad
x = 5 \pm \sqrt{5}
$$
$$
\text{check, moments about } A: \quad V_B \cdot 6 - q \cdot 10 \cdot 3 = 60 - 60 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,2,0;B,8,0;D,10,0" aste="C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,-2*s;2,0,8,0,6-2*s;8,0,10,0,4-2*s" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$), with jumps of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the supports"}

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,2,0;B,8,0;D,10,0" aste="C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,-s^2;2,0,8,0,-4+6*s-s^2;8,0,10,0,-(2-s)^2" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$), with zeros at $x = 5 \pm \sqrt{5}\,\mathrm{m}$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The left overhang has the free end $C$ in the origin, with $T(0) = 0$ and $M(0) = 0$, so shear and moment are written immediately; in $A$ the moment equals $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. In the span the shear starts from the unknown value $T(2^+)$, after the jump due to the reaction, while the moment is continuous in $A$. The right overhang is equal to the left one, so the moment at $B$ is also $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and this condition determines $T(2^+) = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The jumps in shear give the reactions, **$V_A = V_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward**. They are equal by symmetry of the beam and its loading. Shear vanishes at midspan, $x = 5\,\mathrm{m}$, where the moment reaches its maximum $5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. At the supports the moment is $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The **points of contraflexure** are the roots of $M(x) = 0$ within the span, $x = 5 - \sqrt{5} \approx 2.76\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x = 5 + \sqrt{5} \approx 7.24\,\mathrm{m}$. The bottom fibers are in tension between these points and the top fibers are in tension outside them. Check by taking moments about $A$, with the load resultant $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting at midspan, $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller support at $B$ $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and an overhang unloaded of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the free end $C$, where the force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward; the span $AB$ carries the uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find shear and moment, reactions and the maximum positive and negative moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" forze="C,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,B,5 m,1;B,C,2 m,1" titolo="Overhanging beam with uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the span and force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

$$
\text{overhang } BC: \qquad T(7^-) = F = 6\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
T(x) = 6 ,
\qquad
M(x) = -6\,(7 - x) ,
\qquad
M(5) = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{span } AB: \qquad T(x) = a - 4x ,
\qquad
M(x) = a x - 2x^2 ,
\qquad
M(5) = 5a - 50 = -12
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
a = 7.6\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
V_A = T(0) = 7.6\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_B = T(5^+) - T(5^-) = 6 - (7.6 - 20) = 18.4\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
T(x) = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x = 1.9\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
M_{\max} = 7.6 \cdot 1.9 - 2 \cdot 1.9^2 = 14.44 - 7.22 = 7.22\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
M_{\min} = M(5) = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
M(x) = 0 \text{ in } x = 3.8\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\text{check, moments about } B: \quad -V_A \cdot 5 + q \cdot 5 \cdot 2.5 - F \cdot 2 = -38 + 50 - 12 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,5,0,7.6-4*s;5,0,7,0,6" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,5,0,7.6*s-2*s^2;5,0,7,0,-12+6*s" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$), linear over the unloaded overhang"}

<div class="spiegazione">

There is no distributed load on the overhang, so its shear is constant and its moment varies linearly. The downward force $F$ at the free end gives $T(7^-) = F$ by the **jump condition**, while the bending moment at $C$ is zero. Moving left gives the moment $-F \cdot 2 = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at support $B$.

Within the span the moment is zero at $A$ and equals $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $B$ because the support reaction causes no jump in bending moment. This condition gives the shear at $A$ and hence the reactions **$V_A = 7.6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 18.4\,\mathrm{kN}$, both upward**.

Shear vanishes at $x = 1.9\,\mathrm{m}$, where the bending moment reaches its **positive maximum $7.22\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$**. The point of contraflexure is at $x = 3.8\,\mathrm{m}$. The **largest negative moment, $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$**, occurs at support $B$, where the shear changes sign in a jump. The largest value of $|M|$ therefore occurs at that support. Check by taking moments about $B$.

</div>
