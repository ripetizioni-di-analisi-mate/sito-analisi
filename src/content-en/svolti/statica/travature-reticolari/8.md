## Exercise 1

The roof truss shown has span $8\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $3\,\mathrm{m}$. The horizontal tie $ABCDE$ has joints spaced at $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Rafter segments $AF$, $FG$, $GH$, and $HE$ have slope $\dfrac{3}{4}$, with $F = (2;\ 1.5)$, $G = (4, 3)$, and $H = (6;\ 1.5)$. The king post is $CG$, the intermediate verticals are $BF$ and $DH$, and the diagonal struts are $FC$ and $HC$. There is a pin at $A$ and a roller at $E$. Downward forces of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ act at joints $F$, $G$, and $H$. Find the axial force in every member.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,4,0;D,6,0;E,8,0;F,2,1.5;G,4,3;H,6,1.5" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;A-F;F-G;G-H;H-E;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="F,0,-1.2,P;G,0,-1.2,P;H,0,-1.2,P" quote="A,B,2 m,1.1;B,C,2 m,1.1;C,D,2 m,1.1;D,E,2 m,1.1;8 0,8 3,3 m,0.9" titolo="Roof truss with three forces $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the rafters"}

Reactions, by symmetry: $H_A = 0$, $V_A = V_E = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Unloaded members: in the joints $B$ and $D$ the tie members are aligned and there are no loads, therefore $N_{BF} = N_{DH} = 0$, $N_{AB} = N_{BC}$, $N_{CD} = N_{DE}$.

$A$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{AB} = (1, 0)$ and $\hat{e}_{AF} = \left(\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{AB} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AF} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AF} + 18 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AF} = -30\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{AB} = 24\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$F$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{FA} = \left(-\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$, $\hat{e}_{FG} = \left(\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$, $\hat{e}_{FC} = \left(\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$ and $N_{BF} = 0$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AF} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{FG} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{FC} = 0 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AF} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{FG} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{FC} - 12 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\begin{cases}
N_{FG} + N_{FC} = -30 \\[4pt]
N_{FG} - N_{FC} = -10
\end{cases}
$$
$$
N_{FG} = -20\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{FC} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

By symmetry: $N_{HE} = -30\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{GH} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{HC} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{CD} = N_{DE} = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Joint $G$, vertical translation, with $\hat{e}_{GF} = \left(-\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$, $\hat{e}_{GH} = \left(\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$, $\hat{e}_{GC} = (0, -1)$:
$$
-\frac{3}{5}\,N_{FG} - \frac{3}{5}\,N_{GH} - N_{CG} - 12 = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{CG} = 12 + 12 - 12 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Verify at joint $C$, with $\hat{e}_{CF} = \left(-\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$ and $\hat{e}_{CH} = \left(\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
-N_{BC} + N_{CD} - \frac{4}{5}\,N_{FC} + \frac{4}{5}\,N_{HC} &= -24 + 24 + 8 - 8 = 0 \\[6pt]
\frac{3}{5}\,N_{FC} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{HC} + N_{CG} &= -6 - 6 + 12 = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The truss has $n = 8$ joints and $a = 13$ members. With $v = 3$ it satisfies $2n = a + v = 16$. It is a simple truss because it can be built from triangle $ABF$ by adding joints $C$, $G$, $H$, $D$, and $E$, each connected by two noncollinear members. The truss is therefore statically determinate. The rafter segments and diagonal struts have length $\sqrt{2^2 + 1.5^2} = 2.5\,\mathrm{m}$, giving direction cosines $\pm\dfrac{4}{5}$ and $\pm\dfrac{3}{5}$.

In the joints $B$ and $D$ of the tie the intermediate verticals are **unloaded**, because the two members of the tie are aligned and the joint is not loaded. At joint $A$ the vertical equation contains only the strut, which is in compression with $30\,\mathrm{kN}$; the horizontal one gives the tie **in tension** with $24\,\mathrm{kN}$: the tie prevents the struts from opening.

At joint $F$, the unknowns are the upper $FG$ strut and the diagonal $FC$ strut diagonal strut. The two equations, divided by $\dfrac{4}{5}$ and $\dfrac{3}{5}$, respectively, give the sum and difference of the two axial forces, and therefore $N_{FG} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{FC} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$: the diagonal strut is **in compression**, because it transmits to the base of the king post part of the load applied at $F$. By symmetry, the right members are obtained. At joint $G$, the two in compression struts push the joint upward with $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ each; the load balances $12$ and the king post pulls the remaining $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ down, so it is **in tension**: the king post supports the tie in $C$.

Joint $C$ has not been used. Its two equations give $0$, and vertical equilibrium shows that the tensile force in the king post balances the vertical components of the compressive forces in the two struts.

**Results**: $N_{AB} = N_{BC} = N_{CD} = N_{DE} = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{CG} = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension); $N_{AF} = N_{HE} = -30\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{FG} = N_{GH} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{FC} = N_{HC} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression); $N_{BF} = N_{DH} = 0$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The roof truss shown, equal to that of the previous exercise, brings the forces $P_1 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $F$, $P_2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $G$ and $P_3 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $H$, all down. Calculate the reactions and, with a section of Ritter, the axial forces in the $FG$ strut, in the diagonal $FC$ strut diagonal strut and in the tie $BC$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,4,0;D,6,0;E,8,0;F,2,1.5;G,4,3;H,6,1.5" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;A-F;F-G;G-H;H-E;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="F,0,-1.4,P_1;G,0,-1.2,P_2;H,0,-1.2,P_3" tratteggi="3,-0.6 3,3.4" etichette="3.12,3.3,s" quote="A,B,2 m,1.1;B,C,2 m,1.1;C,D,2 m,1.1;D,E,2 m,1.1;8 0,8 3,3 m,0.9" titolo="Roof truss under unequal loads, with section $s$ cutting members $FG$, $FC$, and $BC$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
8\,V_E - 24 \cdot 2 - 12 \cdot 4 - 12 \cdot 6 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_E - 48 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_E = \frac{168}{8} = 21\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 27\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Left portion (joints $A$, $B$, $F$): $N_{FG}$ at $F$ along unit vector $\left(\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$, $N_{FC}$ at $F$ along unit vector $\left(\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$, and $N_{BC}$ at $B$ along unit vector $(1, 0)$. Ritter points: $F$ for $BC$, $C$ for $FG$, $A$ for $FC$, because the $FG$ strut line passes through $A$.

Rotation around $F = (2;\ 1.5)$:
$$
-27 \cdot 2 + 1.5\,N_{BC} = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{BC} = \frac{54}{1.5} = 36\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Rotation around $C = (4, 0)$; the moment of $N_{FG}$ applied at $F$ is $(-2) \cdot \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{FG} - 1.5 \cdot \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{FG} = -2.4\,N_{FG}$:
$$
-27 \cdot 4 + 24 \cdot 2 - 2.4\,N_{FG} = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{FG} = -\frac{60}{2.4} = -25\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Rotation around $A = (0, 0)$; the moment of $N_{FC}$ applied at $F$ is $2 \cdot \left(-\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{FC}\right) - 1.5 \cdot \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{FC} = -2.4\,N_{FC}$:
$$
-24 \cdot 2 - 2.4\,N_{FC} = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{FC} = -\frac{48}{2.4} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Verify at joint $F$, after extracting $N_{AF} = -\dfrac{5}{3} \cdot 27 = -45\,\mathrm{kN}$ force from joint $A$ and with $N_{BF} = 0$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
-\frac{4}{5}\,N_{AF} + \frac{4}{5}\,N_{FG} + \frac{4}{5}\,N_{FC} &= 36 - 20 - 16 = 0 \\[6pt]
-\frac{3}{5}\,N_{AF} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{FG} - \frac{3}{5}\,N_{FC} - 24 &= 27 - 15 + 12 - 24 = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Reactions are obtained from the whole roof truss; the largest load is in $F$, close to $A$, so $V_A$ exceeds $V_E$.

The section $s$, vertical to $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$, cuts the $FG$ strut, the diagonal $FC$ strut diagonal strut and the tie $BC$. The chords are not parallel, so all three of Ritter's points are proper. The Ritter point of $BC$ is $F$, where $FG$ and $FC$ meet; $FG$'s is $C$, where $FC$ and $BC$ meet. The Ritter point of the diagonal strut diagonal strut is the intersection of the line of the strut with that of the tie: the $FG$ strut lies on the line $y = \dfrac{3}{4}\,x$, which passes through $A$, and the tie lies on the axis $x$, so the point is $A$.

In the rotation around $F$ the load $P_1$ has zero moment arm and remains $V_A$, with $2\,\mathrm{m}$ arm: the tie is **in tension** with $36\,\mathrm{kN}$. For $FG$ and $FC$ the moment of the force of the member is calculated as the moment of the force applied at $F$, with the components; in both cases the arm equals $2.4\,\mathrm{m}$, which is the distance of $C$ from the line of the strut and also the distance of $A$ from the line of the diagonal strut. The strut is **in compression** with $25\,\mathrm{kN}$. In the rotation around $A$ the reaction at $A$ has zero moment arm and only counts the load $P_1$, so the force in the diagonal strut diagonal strut depends only on $P_1$: the diagonal strut diagonal strut is in compression with $20\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Check by the method of joints. At joint $A$ we obtain the force in strut $AF$, and joint $F$, with the short king post $BF$ unloaded, is in equilibrium with the calculated forces.

**Results**: $V_A = 27\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_E = 21\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $H_A = 0$; $N_{BC} = 36\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension), $N_{FG} = -25\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{FC} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression).

</div>

## Exercise 3

The roof truss shown is the same as in Exercise 1. Three downward forces of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ act at joints $F$, $G$, and $H$. A rightward horizontal force $Q = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at joint $F$ represents wind thrust on the left roof slope. Calculate reactions and axial forces in all members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,4,0;D,6,0;E,8,0;F,2,1.5;G,4,3;H,6,1.5" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;A-F;F-G;G-H;H-E;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="F,0,-1.2,P;G,0,-1.2,P;H,0,-1.2,P;F,1.2,0,Q" quote="A,B,2 m,1.1;B,C,2 m,1.1;C,D,2 m,1.1;D,E,2 m,1.1;8 0,8 3,3 m,0.9" titolo="Roof truss with three forces $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and horizontal thrust $Q = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $F$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + 8 = 0 \\[4pt]
8\,V_E - 12 \cdot 2 - 12 \cdot 4 - 12 \cdot 6 - 8 \cdot 1.5 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_E - 36 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
H_A = -8\,\mathrm{kN},
\quad
V_E = \frac{156}{8} = 19.5\,\mathrm{kN},
\quad
V_A = 16.5\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Unloaded members: $N_{BF} = N_{DH} = 0$, $N_{AB} = N_{BC}$, $N_{CD} = N_{DE}$.

$A$ joint:
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{AB} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AF} - 8 = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AF} + 16.5 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AF} = -27.5\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{AB} = 22 + 8 = 30\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$F$ joint:
$$
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AF} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{FG} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{FC} + 8 = 0 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AF} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{FG} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{FC} - 12 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\begin{cases}
N_{FG} + N_{FC} = -37.5 \\[4pt]
N_{FG} - N_{FC} = -7.5
\end{cases}
$$
$$
N_{FG} = -22.5\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{FC} = -15\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$E$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{ED} = (-1, 0)$ and $\hat{e}_{EH} = \left(-\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{DE} - \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{HE} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{HE} + 19.5 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{HE} = -32.5\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{DE} = N_{CD} = 26\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$H$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{HG} = \left(-\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$, $\hat{e}_{HE} = \left(\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$, $\hat{e}_{HC} = \left(-\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$ and $N_{DH} = 0$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{GH} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{HE} - \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{HC} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{GH} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{HE} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{HC} - 12 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\begin{cases}
N_{GH} + N_{HC} = -32.5 \\[4pt]
N_{GH} - N_{HC} = -12.5
\end{cases}
$$
$$
N_{GH} = -22.5\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{HC} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$G$ joint, vertical translation:
$$
-\frac{3}{5}\,N_{FG} - \frac{3}{5}\,N_{GH} - N_{CG} - 12 = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{CG} = 13.5 + 13.5 - 12 = 15\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Checks ($G$ joint horizontally, $C$ joint horizontally and vertically):
$$
\begin{aligned}
-\frac{4}{5}\,N_{FG} + \frac{4}{5}\,N_{GH} &= 18 - 18 = 0 \\[6pt]
-N_{BC} + N_{CD} - \frac{4}{5}\,N_{FC} + \frac{4}{5}\,N_{HC} &= -30 + 26 + 12 - 8 = 0 \\[6pt]
\frac{3}{5}\,N_{FC} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{HC} + N_{CG} &= -9 - 6 + 15 = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The $Q$ thrust is balanced horizontally by the hinge alone, then $H_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**. In the rotation around $A$ the force $Q$, to the right at $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ elevation, has clockwise moment $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, which is added to that of vertical loads; for this $V_E$ exceeds $V_A$, while without wind the two reactions would be the same.

The intermediate verticals remain unloaded, because the joints $B$ and $D$ are not loaded. It proceeds from joint $A$ to $F$ and joint $E$ to $H$; in the joints $F$ and $H$ the two equations, divided by $\dfrac{4}{5}$ and $\dfrac{3}{5}$, give the sum and difference of the axial forces in the strut and the diagonal strut. At joint $A$, the hinge pulls the joint to the left with $8\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the tie in the first section is more in tension than in the second: $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $AB$ and $BC$, $26\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $CD$ and $DE$. The diagonal $FC$ strut diagonal strut, on the wind side, is more in compression than the diagonal $HC$ strut diagonal strut. The joint $G$ gives the king post, in tension with $15\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The unused equations are the horizontal equation of the joint $G$ and the two equations of the joint $C$, and they equal $0$. At joint $C$, the difference in force between the two sections of the tie, $4\,\mathrm{kN}$, is balanced by the horizontal components of the two diagonal struts.

**Results**: $H_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, $V_A = 16.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_E = 19.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $N_{AB} = N_{BC} = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{CD} = N_{DE} = 26\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{CG} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension); $N_{AF} = -27.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{FG} = N_{GH} = -22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{HE} = -32.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{FC} = -15\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{HC} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression); $N_{BF} = N_{DH} = 0$.

</div>
