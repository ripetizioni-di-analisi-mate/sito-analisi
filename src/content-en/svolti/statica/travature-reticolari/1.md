## Exercise 1

The truss shown is formed by the $AB$, $AC$ and $BC$ members, with $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (6, 0)$ and $C = (3, 4)$ in meters. It is supported by a pin at $A$ and a horizontal-plane roller support at $B$, and is loaded into $C$ by the vertical force $P = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Calculate support reactions and axial forces in the three members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.5,P" quote="A,B,6 m,1.1;6 0,6 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Triangular truss with force $P = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

$$
n = 3, \quad a = 3, \quad v = 3
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
2n = 6 = a + v
$$

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_B - 40 \cdot 3 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - 40 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
H_A = 0, \quad V_B = 20\,\mathrm{kN}, \quad V_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$A$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{AB} = (1, 0)$ and $\hat{e}_{AC} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{AB} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AC} + H_A = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AC} + V_A = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AC} = -\frac{5}{4} \cdot 20 = -25\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{AB} = -\frac{3}{5}\,(-25) = 15\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Joint $B$, with $\hat{e}_{BA} = (-1, 0)$ and $\hat{e}_{BC} = \left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$, vertical translation:
$$
\frac{4}{5}\,N_{BC} + V_B = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{BC} = -\frac{5}{4} \cdot 20 = -25\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Verify at joint $C$, with $\hat{e}_{CA} = \left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ and $\hat{e}_{CB} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
-\frac{3}{5}\,N_{AC} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{BC} &= 15 - 15 = 0 \\[6pt]
-\frac{4}{5}\,N_{AC} - \frac{4}{5}\,N_{BC} - 40 &= 20 + 20 - 40 = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The truss is a triangle, i.e. an **simple truss**, and it is supported by a hinge and a roller support whose vertical reaction does not pass by $A$; the three constraints are well arranged, so the truss is statically determinate, as the $2n = a + v$ count confirms.

The reactions are derived from the equilibrium equations of the whole truss: the horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A = 0$ because there are no horizontal forces, moment equilibrium about $A$ gives only $V_B$ because $H_A$ and $V_A$ pass through $A$, and the vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A$. The load is at midspan, so the two vertical reactions equal $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ each, upward.

The members $AC$ and $BC$ are $\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5\,\mathrm{m}$ long, so the cosine conductors are $\dfrac{3}{5}$ and $\dfrac{4}{5}$, with the sign given by the direction going from the joint to the other end of the member. At joint $A$ there are two unknowns and the members are not aligned, so the two equations of the joint determine them. The vertical equation contains only $N_{AC}$, because $AB$ is horizontal, and gives $N_{AC} = -25\,\mathrm{kN}$: the member $AC$ is **in compression**. The horizontal equation then gives $N_{AB} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$: the chord $AB$ is **in tension**. At joint $B$, only one unknown remains, $N_{BC}$, which is obtained by vertical translation; the result is equal to $N_{AC}$ for structure and load symmetry.

Reactions were derived from global equilibrium, so the joint $C$ provides two unused equations, which equal $0$ with the axial forces found.

**Results**: $V_A = V_B = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $H_A = 0$; $N_{AB} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension), $N_{AC} = N_{BC} = -25\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression).

</div>

## Exercise 2

The truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)$ and $C = (0, 3)$ in meters, connected by the members $AB$, $AC$ and $BC$. In $A$ there is a hinge and in $B$ a horizontal-level roller support. At joint $C$, the horizontal force $P_1 = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and the vertical force $P_2 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward act. Calculate axial forces in members and support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,0,3" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,1,0,P_1;C,0,-1.2,P_2" quote="A,B,4 m,1.1;0 0,0 3,3 m,-1.7" titolo="Right-triangle truss with forces $P_1$ and $P_2$ at joint $C$"}

$C$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{CA} = (0, -1)$ and $\hat{e}_{CB} = \left(\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{BC} + 15 = 0 \\[8pt]
-N_{AC} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{BC} - 20 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{BC} = -\frac{75}{4} = -18.75\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{AC} = -20 + \frac{3}{5} \cdot 18.75 = -8.75\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$B$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{BA} = (-1, 0)$ and $\hat{e}_{BC} = \left(-\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{AB} - \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{BC} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{BC} + V_B = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AB} = -\frac{4}{5}\,(-18.75) = 15\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_B = \frac{3}{5} \cdot 18.75 = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$A$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{AB} = (1, 0)$ and $\hat{e}_{AC} = (0, 1)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{AB} + H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
N_{AC} + V_A = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
H_A = -15\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 8.75\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check with the rotation of the whole truss around $A$:
$$
4\,V_B - 15 \cdot 3 = 4 \cdot 11.25 - 45 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The truss is a bound triangle with a well-arranged hinge and roller support, so it is statically determinate ($2n = 6 = a + v$). This time we start from the joint $C$: in it two non-aligned members concur and act only known loads, therefore the two axial forces are obtained without knowing the reactions. The member $BC$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long and, taken from $C$ towards $B$, has direction cosines $\dfrac{4}{5}$ and $-\dfrac{3}{5}$; the member $CA$, taken from $C$ towards $A$, points down.

The horizontal equation of the joint $C$ contains only $N_{BC}$, because $AC$ is vertical; $N_{BC} = -18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ follows, so the member $BC$ is **in compression**. The force $P_1$ pushes the joint $C$ to the right, and only the member $BC$ has a horizontal component that can balance it. The vertical equation then gives $N_{AC} = -8.75\,\mathrm{kN}$, compression.

At joint $B$ the unknowns are $N_{AB}$ and the reaction $V_B$; Because roller support only reacts vertically, the horizontal equation gives $N_{AB} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension) and the vertical equation gives $V_B$. The joint $A$ finally provides the two components of the hinge reaction: $H_A = -15\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, equal to and opposite to $P_1$, and $V_A = 8.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The reactions were obtained from the joints, so the equilibrium equations of the whole truss are free and serve as a check: the rotation around $A$, in which $P_2$ has zero moment arm and $P_1$ has $3\,\mathrm{m}$ arm, gives $0$.

**Results**: $N_{AB} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension), $N_{AC} = -8.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{BC} = -18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression); $H_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, $V_A = 8.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (6, 0)$, $C = (3, 4)$ and $D = (9, 4)$ in meters and the members $AB$, $AC$, $BC$, $CD$ and $BD$. It is supported by a pin at $A$ and a horizontal-level roller support at $B$; at joint $D$, to overhang beyond the $B$ support, the vertical $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ force acts downward. Calculate axial forces in all members and support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4;D,9,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C;C-D;B-D" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="D,0,-1.5,P" quote="A,B,6 m,1.1;C,D,6 m,-0.8;9 0,9 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Truss with joint $D$ overhanging support $B$"}

$$
n = 4, \quad a = 5, \quad v = 3
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
2n = 8 = a + v
$$

$D$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{DC} = (-1, 0)$ and $\hat{e}_{DB} = \left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{CD} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{BD} = 0 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{BD} - 30 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{BD} = -37.5\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{CD} = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$C$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{CA} = \left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$, $\hat{e}_{CB} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ and $\hat{e}_{CD} = (1, 0)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AC} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{BC} + N_{CD} = 0 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AC} - \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{BC} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AC} = -N_{BC},
\qquad
\frac{6}{5}\,N_{BC} = -22.5
$$
$$
N_{BC} = -18.75\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{AC} = 18.75\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$B$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{BA} = (-1, 0)$, $\hat{e}_{BC} = \left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ and $\hat{e}_{BD} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{AB} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{BC} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{BD} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{BC} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{BD} + V_B = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\begin{aligned}
N_{AB} &= 11.25 - 22.5 = -11.25\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
V_B &= 15 + 30 = 45\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

$A$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{AB} = (1, 0)$ and $\hat{e}_{AC} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{AB} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AC} + H_A = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AC} + V_A = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
H_A = 11.25 - 11.25 = 0,
\qquad
V_A = -15\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check with the rotation of the whole truss around $A$:
$$
6\,V_B - 30 \cdot 9 = 6 \cdot 45 - 270 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The truss is constructed from the triangle $ABC$ by adding the joint $D$ with the members $CD$ and $BD$, which are not aligned; is therefore an **simple truss** and, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, is statically determinate, as the $2n = a + v = 8$ count confirms.

It starts from the joint $D$, where only two members meet and the known load acts. The vertical equation contains only $N_{BD}$ and gives $N_{BD} = -37.5\,\mathrm{kN}$; The horizontal one gives $N_{CD} = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The joint $D$ is hung from a horizontal **in tension** member and supported by an **in compression** inclined member, as in a cantilever. At joint $C$ two unknowns remain: the vertical equation says that $AC$ and $BC$ have opposite axial forces, because $C$ is not vertically loaded and the two members have the same inclination; The horizontal one fixes the value. At joint $B$, the unknowns left are $N_{AB}$ and $V_B$, which are obtained one by equation. At joint $A$, $H_A$ and $V_A$ are located.

The reaction $V_A$ is negative, so it is directed **downward**: the load is beyond the $B$ support and the truss tends to rise in $A$, like a beam with overhang. For the same reason $V_B = 45\,\mathrm{kN}$ exceeds the load. The reactions were derived from the joints, so the rotation of the whole truss around $A$ is an unused equation, and equals $0$.

**Results**: $N_{AB} = -11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression), $N_{AC} = 18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension), $N_{BC} = -18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression), $N_{CD} = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension), $N_{BD} = -37.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression); $H_A = 0$, $V_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ down, $V_B = 45\,\mathrm{kN}$ up.

</div>
