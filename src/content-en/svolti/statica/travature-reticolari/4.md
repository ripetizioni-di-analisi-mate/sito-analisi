## Exercise 1

The cantilever truss shown is attached to the wall with a hinge $A = (0, 0)$ and a roller support at $B = (0, 3)$, which moves along the vertical wall. The other joints are $C = (4, 3)$, $D = (4, 0)$ and $E = (8, 0)$, in meters; The members are $AB$, $BC$, $CE$, $AD$, $DE$, $CD$, $AC$. At the free end $E$ the vertical force $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Calculate axial forces in members and support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3;D,4,0;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-E;A-D;D-E;C-D;A-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera,180;B,carrello,180" forze="E,0,-1.6,P" quote="A,D,4 m,1.1;D,E,4 m,1.1;0 0,0 3,3 m,-1.5" titolo="Cantilever truss with force $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the free end"}

Unloaded members:
$$
\text{joint } B: \ N_{AB} = 0;
\qquad
\text{joint } D: \ N_{CD} = 0, \ N_{AD} = N_{DE}
$$

$E$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{ED} = (-1, 0)$ and $\hat{e}_{EC} = \left(-\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{DE} - \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{CE} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{CE} - 30 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{CE} = 50\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{DE} = -40\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{AD} = -40\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$C$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{CB} = (-1, 0)$, $\hat{e}_{CA} = \left(-\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$, $\hat{e}_{CE} = \left(\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$ and $N_{CD} = 0$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{BC} - \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AC} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{CE} = 0 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AC} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{CE} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AC} = -50\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{BC} = 40 + 40 = 80\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Constrained joints:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{joint } B: \quad & N_{BC} + H_B = 0 &&\Longrightarrow\quad H_B = -80\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
\text{joint } A: \quad & N_{AD} + \frac{4}{5}\,N_{AC} + H_A = 0 &&\Longrightarrow\quad H_A = 40 + 40 = 80\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
\text{joint } A: \quad & N_{AB} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{AC} + V_A = 0 &&\Longrightarrow\quad V_A = 30\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

Check with the rotation of the whole truss around $A$:
$$
-3\,H_B - 30 \cdot 8 = 240 - 240 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The count gives $2n = 10$ and $a + v = 7 + 3 = 10$; the truss is built from the triangle $ABC$ by adding $D$ with the members $AD$ and $CD$ and $E$ with the members $DE$ and $CE$, so it is simple. The reaction of the roller support is horizontal and its line does not pass by $A$, so the constraints are well arranged and the truss is statically determinate.

At joint $B$ the vertical member $AB$, the horizontal member $BC$ and the horizontal reaction of the roller support, directed along $BC$; for the **zero-force member rule** the member $AB$ is unloaded, as shown by the vertical equation of the joint. At joint $D$ the $AD$ and $DE$ aligned members and the vertical $CD$, without loads, so $CD$ is also unloaded.

It starts from the free end $E$, where there are two unknowns and no need for reactions. The vertical equation gives the diagonal $CE$ **in tension** with $50\,\mathrm{kN}$; the horizontal equation gives the bottom chord **in compression** with $40\,\mathrm{kN}$. At joint $C$ the vertical equation says that $AC$ and $CE$ have opposite stresses, so $AC$ is in compression with $50\,\mathrm{kN}$; the horizontal equation gives the top chord $BC$ in tension with $80\,\mathrm{kN}$. As in any cantilever loaded downward, the top chord is in tension and the lower one in compression. The joints $B$ and $A$ finally provide the reactions: the roller support pulls the top chord towards the wall with $80\,\mathrm{kN}$ ($H_B$ negative, that is, to the left) and the hinge pushes the bottom chord with $80\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right.

Reactions were obtained from joints, so the equations of the whole truss are free. In the rotation around $A$ the reaction $H_B$ has a moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and the load has a moment arm of $8\,\mathrm{m}$, and the sum of the moments is zero.

**Results**: $N_{AB} = N_{CD} = 0$; $N_{BC} = 80\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{CE} = 50\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension); $N_{AD} = N_{DE} = -40\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{AC} = -50\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression); $H_B = 80\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, $H_A = 80\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, $V_A = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The cantilever truss shown is connected to the wall with a pin at $B = (0, 4)$ and a two-force horizontal link in $A = (0, 0)$. The other joints are $C = (3, 4)$, $D = (6, 4)$ and $E = (3, 0)$, in meters, and the members are $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, $AE$, $CE$, $AC$, $ED$. In the joints $C$ and $D$, two forces of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ act downward. Calculate axial forces in members and support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4;D,6,4;E,3,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;A-E;C-E;A-C;E-D" reticolare=1 vincoli="B,cerniera,180;A,pendolo,180" forze="C,0,-1.4,P;D,0,-1.4,P" quote="B,C,3 m,-0.8;C,D,3 m,-0.8;A,E,3 m,1.1;0 0,0 4,4 m,-1.7" titolo="Cantilever truss with a pin at $B$, a horizontal link at $A$, and two forces $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

$D$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{DC} = (-1, 0)$ and $\hat{e}_{DE} = \left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{CD} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{ED} = 0 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{ED} - 20 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{ED} = -25\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{CD} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$E$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{EA} = (-1, 0)$, $\hat{e}_{EC} = (0, 1)$ and $\hat{e}_{ED} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{AE} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{ED} = 0 \\[8pt]
N_{CE} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{ED} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AE} = -15\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{CE} = 20\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$C$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{CB} = (-1, 0)$, $\hat{e}_{CD} = (1, 0)$, $\hat{e}_{CE} = (0, -1)$ and $\hat{e}_{CA} = \left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{BC} + N_{CD} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AC} = 0 \\[8pt]
-N_{CE} - \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AC} - 20 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AC} = -\frac{5}{4} \cdot 40 = -50\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{BC} = 15 + 30 = 45\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Joint $A$, with $\hat{e}_{AB} = (0, 1)$, $\hat{e}_{AE} = (1, 0)$, $\hat{e}_{AC} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ and the reaction $R_A$ of the horizontal two-force link:
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{AE} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AC} + R_A = 0 \\[8pt]
N_{AB} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AC} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
R_A = 15 + 30 = 45\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{AB} = 40\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$B$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{BA} = (0, -1)$ and $\hat{e}_{BC} = (1, 0)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{BC} + H_B = 0 \\[4pt]
-N_{AB} + V_B = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
H_B = -45\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_B = 40\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check with the rotation of the whole truss around $B$:
$$
4\,R_A - 20 \cdot 3 - 20 \cdot 6 = 180 - 60 - 120 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The truss has $n = 5$ joints, $a = 7$ members and $v = 3$ scalar constraints (the hinge equals $2$, the two-force link $1$), hence $2n = a + v = 10$. It is constructed from the triangle $ABC$ by adding $E$ with the members $AE$ and $CE$ and then $D$ with the members $CD$ and $ED$; the line of the two-force link, horizontal for $A$, does not pass by $B$, so the constraints are well arranged and the truss is statically determinate.

It proceeds from the free end towards the wall, choosing each time a joint with two unknowns: $D$, then $E$, then $C$. In $D$ the diagonal $ED$ is **in compression** and the chord $CD$ **in tension**. In $E$, where there are no loads, the in compression diagonal pushes the joint down and left; vertical $CE$ pulls it upward and is in tension with force $20\,\mathrm{kN}$, while bottom chord $AE$ is in compression. In $C$ the in tension vertical pulls the joint down, so the diagonal $AC$ must balance $20 + 20 = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$ vertically, and is in compression with $50\,\mathrm{kN}$.

At joint $A$ the horizontal equation contains the reaction of the two-force link, which pushes the joint to the right with $45\,\mathrm{kN}$; the vertical equation gives the member $AB$ along the wall, in tension with $40\,\mathrm{kN}$. At joint $B$ the hinge holds the top chord with $45\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and supports the truss with $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, equal to the sum of the loads. The check uses the rotation of the whole truss around $B$: the two-force link has $4\,\mathrm{m}$ arm and the loads $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $6\,\mathrm{m}$.

**Results**: $N_{BC} = 45\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{CD} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{CE} = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{AB} = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension); $N_{AE} = -15\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{AC} = -50\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{ED} = -25\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression); $R_A = 45\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, $H_B = 45\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, $V_B = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The cantilever truss shown has three $3\,\mathrm{m}$ panels and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ height. It is attached to the wall with a hinge $B = (0, 4)$ and a roller support at $A = (0, 0)$, which moves along the vertical wall. At joints $C$, $D$ of the top chord act two forces of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and at the end $E$ a force of $24\,\mathrm{kN}$, all downward. Calculate with the Ritter method the axial forces in the $BC$, $AC$ and $AG$ members of the panel near the wall, and then the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4;D,6,4;E,9,4;G,3,0;H,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;A-G;G-H;C-G;D-H;A-C;G-D;H-E" reticolare=1 vincoli="B,cerniera,180;A,carrello,180" forze="C,0,-1.4,P_1;D,0,-1.4,P_1;E,0,-1.8,P_2" tratteggi="1.5,-0.8 1.5,5.2" etichette="1.65,5.1,s" quote="A,G,3 m,1.1;G,H,3 m,1.1;6 0,9 0,3 m,1.1;9 0,9 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Cantilever truss with $P_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$ and $D$, $P_2 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $E$, and section $s$ in the first panel"}

Portion to the right of the section (joints $C$, $D$, $E$, $G$, $H$), with cut-member forces directed toward the section: $N_{BC}$ at $C$ along unit vector $(-1, 0)$, $N_{AC}$ at $C$ along unit vector $\left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$, and $N_{AG}$ at $G$ along unit vector $(-1, 0)$.

Rotation around $A = (0, 0)$, Ritter point of $BC$:
$$
4\,N_{BC} - 12 \cdot 3 - 12 \cdot 6 - 24 \cdot 9 = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{BC} = \frac{324}{4} = 81\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Rotation around $C = (3, 4)$, Ritter point of $AG$:
$$
-4\,N_{AG} - 12 \cdot 3 - 24 \cdot 6 = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AG} = -\frac{180}{4} = -45\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Vertical translation:
$$
-\frac{4}{5}\,N_{AC} - 12 - 12 - 24 = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AC} = -\frac{5}{4} \cdot 48 = -60\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Reactions, from whole truss:
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + H_B = 0 \\[4pt]
V_B - 48 = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,H_A - 324 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
H_A = 81\,\mathrm{kN},
\quad
H_B = -81\,\mathrm{kN},
\quad
V_B = 48\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Verify at joint $A$, horizontal translation, with $\hat{e}_{AG} = (1, 0)$ and $\hat{e}_{AC} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$:
$$
N_{AG} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{AC} + H_A = -45 - 36 + 81 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The count gives $2n = 14$ and $a + v = 11 + 3 = 14$, and the truss is constructed by triangles starting with $ABC$; the roller support reacts horizontally with line of action that does not pass through $B$, so the truss is statically determinate.

Section $s$ cuts the top chord $BC$, diagonal $AC$ and bottom chord $AG$. One studies the right part, which contains all the loads and no constraints: in a cantilever it is convenient, because Ritter equations do not require reactions. The $AC$ and $AG$ lines pass through $A$, $BC$ Ritter point; $BC$ and $AC$ lines pass through $C$, $AG$'s Ritter point.

In the rotation around $A$ the loads have $3$, $6$ and $9\,\mathrm{m}$ arms and $324\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ overall clockwise moment; the top chord, in tension, pulls the joint $C$ towards the wall with a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and counterclockwise moment, therefore $N_{BC} = 81\,\mathrm{kN}$ of **tension**. In the rotation around $C$ the load at $C$ has zero moment arm; tension force of $AG$, to the left and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ under $C$, would have clockwise moment, like loads, so the bottom chord is **in compression** with $45\,\mathrm{kN}$. The chords are parallel, so the diagonal $AC$ is derived from the vertical translation: the whole sum of the loads, $48\,\mathrm{kN}$, passes into the diagonal, which is in compression with $60\,\mathrm{kN}$.

For reactions moment equilibrium about $B$ gives only $H_A$, because $H_B$ and $V_B$ pass through $B$; the loads have the same horizontal arms compared to $B$ as compared to $A$. The roller support pushes the truss to the right with $81\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the hinge holds it to the left with $81\,\mathrm{kN}$. The check is the horizontal equilibrium of the joint $A$, which contains the three results $N_{AG}$, $N_{AC}$ and $H_A$, obtained with different equations.

**Results**: $N_{BC} = 81\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension), $N_{AC} = -60\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{AG} = -45\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression); $H_A = 81\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, $H_B = 81\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, $V_B = 48\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.

</div>
