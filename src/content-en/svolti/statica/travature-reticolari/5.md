## Exercise 1

The Warren truss shown has basic $3\,\mathrm{m}$ triangles and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ height, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $D$. In the joints $B$ and $C$ of the bottom chord the forces $P_1 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $P_2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ act downward. Calculate axial forces in all members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,1.5,2;G,4.5,2;H,7.5,2" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;E-G;G-H;A-E;E-B;B-G;G-C;C-H;H-D" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="B,0,-1.3,P_1;C,0,-1.3,P_2" quote="A,B,3 m,1.2;B,C,3 m,1.2;C,D,3 m,1.2;9 0,9 2,2 m,1" titolo="Warren truss with forces $P_1 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$ and $P_2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
9\,V_D - 24 \cdot 3 - 12 \cdot 6 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_D - 36 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_D = 16\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

The diagonals have direction cosines $\pm\dfrac{3}{5}$ horizontally and $\pm\dfrac{4}{5}$ vertically.

$A$ joint:
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{AB} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AE} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AE} + 20 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AE} = -25\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{AB} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$E$ joint:
$$
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AE} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{EB} + N_{EG} = 0 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AE} - \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{EB} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{EB} = 25\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{EG} = -15 - 15 = -30\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$B$ joint:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{AB} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{EB} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{BG} + N_{BC} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{EB} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{BG} - 24 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{BG} = \frac{5}{4}\,(24 - 20) = 5\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{BC} = 15 + 15 - 3 = 27\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$G$ joint:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{EG} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{BG} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{GC} + N_{GH} = 0 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{BG} - \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{GC} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{GC} = -5\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{GH} = -30 + 3 + 3 = -24\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$D$ joint:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{CD} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{HD} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{HD} + 16 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{HD} = -20\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{CD} = 12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$H$ joint, vertical translation:
$$
-\frac{4}{5}\,N_{CH} - \frac{4}{5}\,N_{HD} = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{CH} = 20\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Checks ($H$ joint horizontally, $C$ joint horizontally and vertically):
$$
\begin{aligned}
-N_{GH} - \frac{3}{5}\,N_{CH} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{HD} &= 24 - 12 - 12 = 0 \\[6pt]
-N_{BC} - \frac{3}{5}\,N_{GC} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{CH} + N_{CD} &= -27 + 3 + 12 + 12 = 0 \\[6pt]
\frac{4}{5}\,N_{GC} + \frac{4}{5}\,N_{CH} - 12 &= -4 + 16 - 12 = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The truss has $n = 7$ joints and $a = 11$ members; with $v = 3$ you have $2n = 14 = a + v$, and being simple and bound with hinge and roller support is statically determinate. Reactions are calculated from the whole truss; loads are closer to $A$, so $V_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ exceeds $V_D = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The **joint method** proceeds from the left: in $A$ there are two unknowns, then in $E$, $B$ and $G$ there are always two. In each joint the vertical equation contains only one unknown diagonal, because the chords are horizontal, and it is best to solve it first. At joint $E$, without loads, the two diagonals have opposite axial forces; at joint $B$ the diagonal $BG$ brings up the difference between the load $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the vertical component $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ of diagonal $EB$. After $G$ it is best to start from the other support, the joint $D$, and close with the joint $H$.

The equations of the joints are $14$ and the unknowns $11 + 3$; Three reactions have been derived from global equilibrium, so there are three unused equations that serve as check. All three equal $0$.

The diagonal $BG$ rises to the right as $AE$, but is in tension. In the vertical section that cuts it, the sum of the vertical forces on the left is $V_s = 20 - 24 = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, negative, and for the stress theorem in chords and diagonals a diagonal rising has $-\dfrac{V_s}{\sin\alpha} = \dfrac{4}{\frac{4}{5}} = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ force, in accordance with the joint method.

**Results**: $N_{AB} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{BC} = 27\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{CD} = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{EB} = 25\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{BG} = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{CH} = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension); $N_{EG} = -30\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{GH} = -24\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{AE} = -25\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{GC} = -5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{HD} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression); $V_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_D = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The Warren truss shown, equal to that of the previous exercise, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $D$, brings three forces of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ down into the $E$, $G$, $H$ joints of the top chord and, at joint $E$, the horizontal force $Q = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right. Calculate reactions and axial forces in all members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,1.5,2;G,4.5,2;H,7.5,2" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;E-G;G-H;A-E;E-B;B-G;G-C;C-H;H-D" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="E,0,-1.3,P;G,0,-1.3,P;H,0,-1.3,P;E,1.3,0,Q" quote="A,B,3 m,1.2;B,C,3 m,1.2;C,D,3 m,1.2;9 0,9 2,2 m,1" titolo="Warren truss with three forces $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and horizontal force $Q = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $E$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + 9 = 0 \\[4pt]
9\,V_D - 20 \cdot 1.5 - 20 \cdot 4.5 - 20 \cdot 7.5 - 9 \cdot 2 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_D - 60 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
H_A = -9\,\mathrm{kN},
\quad
V_D = \frac{288}{9} = 32\,\mathrm{kN},
\quad
V_A = 28\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$A$ joint:
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{AB} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AE} - 9 = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AE} + 28 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AE} = -35\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{AB} = 21 + 9 = 30\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$E$ joint:
$$
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AE} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{EB} + N_{EG} + 9 = 0 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AE} - \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{EB} - 20 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{EB} = \frac{5}{4} \cdot 8 = 10\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{EG} = -21 - 6 - 9 = -36\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$B$ joint:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{AB} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{EB} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{BG} + N_{BC} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{EB} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{BG} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{BG} = -10\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{BC} = 30 + 6 + 6 = 42\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$G$ joint:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{EG} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{BG} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{GC} + N_{GH} = 0 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{BG} - \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{GC} - 20 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{GC} = \frac{5}{4}\,(8 - 20) = -15\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{GH} = -36 - 6 + 9 = -33\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$D$ joint:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{CD} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{HD} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{HD} + 32 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{HD} = -40\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{CD} = 24\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$H$ joint, vertical translation:
$$
-\frac{4}{5}\,N_{CH} - \frac{4}{5}\,N_{HD} - 20 = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{CH} = \frac{5}{4}\,(32 - 20) = 15\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Checks ($H$ joint horizontally, $C$ joint horizontally and vertically):
$$
\begin{aligned}
-N_{GH} - \frac{3}{5}\,N_{CH} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{HD} &= 33 - 9 - 24 = 0 \\[6pt]
-N_{BC} - \frac{3}{5}\,N_{GC} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{CH} + N_{CD} &= -42 + 9 + 9 + 24 = 0 \\[6pt]
\frac{4}{5}\,N_{GC} + \frac{4}{5}\,N_{CH} &= -12 + 12 = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The horizontal $Q$ force is balanced by the single-hinge, because the roller support reacts vertically: $H_A = -9\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**. In the rotation around $A$ the force $Q$ is applied at elevation $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and is directed to the right, so it has clockwise moment $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and increases $V_D$.

At joint $A$ the reaction $H_A$ enters the horizontal equation: the chord $AB$ is in tension with $30\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $21\,\mathrm{kN}$ due to the in compression $AE$ diagonal plus the $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ with which the hinge pulls the joint to the left. At joint $E$ the force $Q$ enters the horizontal equation and makes the chord $EG$ more in compression. At joint $B$, without loads, the two diagonals have opposite stresses, and the diagonal $BG$ is **in compression**. Continue with the joint $G$ and restart from the $D$ support, closing with the vertical equation of the joint $H$.

The three remaining equations (horizontal in $H$, horizontal and vertical in $C$) equal $0$ with the axial forces found. In particular, at joint $C$, which is not loaded, the two diagonals $GC$ and $CH$ have opposite axial forces, as the vertical equation predicts.

**Results**: $H_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, $V_A = 28\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_D = 32\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $N_{AB} = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{BC} = 42\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{CD} = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{EB} = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{CH} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension); $N_{AE} = -35\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{EG} = -36\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{BG} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{GC} = -15\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{GH} = -33\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{HD} = -40\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression).

</div>
