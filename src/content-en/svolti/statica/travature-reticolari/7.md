## Exercise 1

The triangular truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (8, 0)$ and $C = (4, 3)$, in meters. In $A$ there is a hinge; at $B$ there is a roller on an inclined surface, with reaction $R_B$ in the direction of unit vector $\left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$. In $C$ the vertical force $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Calculate reactions and axial forces in members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0;C,4,3" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,306.87" forze="C,0,-1.6,P" quote="A,B,8 m,1.3;0 0,0 3,3 m,-0.9" titolo="Triangular truss with a roller on an inclined surface at $B$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A - \dfrac{3}{5}\,R_B = 0 \\[8pt]
8 \cdot \dfrac{4}{5}\,R_B - 30 \cdot 4 = 0 \\[8pt]
V_A + \dfrac{4}{5}\,R_B - 30 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
R_B = \frac{120}{6.4} = 18.75\,\mathrm{kN},
\quad
H_A = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN},
\quad
V_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$C$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{CA} = \left(-\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$ and $\hat{e}_{CB} = \left(\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AC} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{BC} = 0 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AC} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{BC} - 30 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AC} = N_{BC} = -25\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Joint $A$, horizontal translation:
$$
N_{AB} + \frac{4}{5}\,N_{AC} + H_A = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AB} = 20 - 11.25 = 8.75\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Verify at joint $B$, with $\hat{e}_{BA} = (-1, 0)$ and $\hat{e}_{BC} = \left(-\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
-N_{AB} - \frac{4}{5}\,N_{BC} - \frac{3}{5}\,R_B &= -8.75 + 20 - 11.25 = 0 \\[6pt]
\frac{3}{5}\,N_{BC} + \frac{4}{5}\,R_B &= -15 + 15 = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The roller support reaction is perpendicular to the support surface and has two components, $-\dfrac{3}{5}\,R_B$ horizontally and $\dfrac{4}{5}\,R_B$ vertically. Its line does not pass by $A$, so the truss, a triangle, is statically determinate. In the rotation around $A$ the horizontal component of $R_B$ passes through $A$, because $B$ lies on the axis $x$, and only counts the vertical component, with $8\,\mathrm{m}$ arm. $R_B = 18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ follows; the other two equations give $H_A = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $V_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The hinge must push to the right because the inclined roller support pushes the truss to the left.

The joint $C$ has two unknowns and resolves without reactions: by symmetry of the triangle the two members have the same force, $-25\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. they are **in compression**. The chord $AB$ force is derived from the horizontal force equilibrium of the joint $A$: the in compression diagonal pushes $A$ left with $20\,\mathrm{kN}$, the hinge pushes it right with $11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the chord pulls the remaining $8.75\,\mathrm{kN}$, so it is **in tension**. With a horizontal roller support the chord would carry all $20\,\mathrm{kN}$: part of the thrust of the inclined members is absorbed by the hinge.

The joint $B$ has not been used and its two equations equal $0$.

**Results**: $R_B = 18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ perpendicular to the plane, up and left; $H_A = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, $V_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $N_{AC} = N_{BC} = -25\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression), $N_{AB} = 8.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension).

</div>

## Exercise 2

The Warren truss shown has base triangles $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $2\,\mathrm{m}$. In $A$ there is a hinge; at $D$ there is a roller on an inclined surface. Its reaction $R_D$ acts along unit vector $\left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$, parallel to diagonal $HD$. At joint $G$, an inclined $F = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ force acts, of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ components to the right and $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ components downward. Calculate reactions and axial forces in all members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,1.5,2;G,4.5,2;H,7.5,2" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;E-G;G-H;A-E;E-B;B-G;G-C;C-H;H-D" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello,306.87" forze="G,0.9,-1.2,F" quote="A,B,3 m,1.3;B,C,3 m,1.3;C,D,3 m,1.3;9 0,9 2,2 m,1.2" titolo="Warren truss with an inclined roller support at $D$ and inclined force $F$ at $G$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + 18 - \dfrac{3}{5}\,R_D = 0 \\[8pt]
9 \cdot \dfrac{4}{5}\,R_D - 24 \cdot 4.5 - 18 \cdot 2 = 0 \\[8pt]
V_A + \dfrac{4}{5}\,R_D - 24 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
R_D = \frac{144}{7.2} = 20\,\mathrm{kN},
\quad
H_A = 12 - 18 = -6\,\mathrm{kN},
\quad
V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$D$ joint: the reaction is directed along $HD$, then $N_{CD} = 0$ and, in the direction of $HD$,
$$
N_{HD} + R_D = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{HD} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$A$ joint:
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{AB} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AE} - 6 = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AE} + 8 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AE} = -10\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{AB} = 6 + 6 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$E$ and $H$ joints, without loads:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{joint } E: \quad & N_{EB} = -N_{AE} = 10\,\mathrm{kN}, \qquad N_{EG} = \frac{3}{5}\,N_{AE} - \frac{3}{5}\,N_{EB} = -12\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
\text{joint } H: \quad & N_{CH} = -N_{HD} = 20\,\mathrm{kN}, \qquad N_{GH} = -\frac{3}{5}\,N_{CH} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{HD} = -24\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

$C$ and $B$ joints, without loads and with $N_{CD} = 0$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{joint } C: \quad & N_{GC} = -N_{CH} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}, \qquad N_{BC} = -\frac{3}{5}\,N_{GC} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{CH} = 24\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
\text{joint } B: \quad & N_{BG} = -N_{EB} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

Checks ($B$ joint horizontally, $G$ joint horizontally and vertically):
$$
\begin{aligned}
-N_{AB} - \frac{3}{5}\,N_{EB} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{BG} + N_{BC} &= -12 - 6 - 6 + 24 = 0 \\[6pt]
-N_{EG} - \frac{3}{5}\,N_{BG} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{GC} + N_{GH} + 18 &= 12 + 6 - 12 - 24 + 18 = 0 \\[6pt]
-\frac{4}{5}\,N_{BG} - \frac{4}{5}\,N_{GC} - 24 &= 8 + 16 - 24 = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The inclined force breaks down into the $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ components to the right and $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ components downward, and the same is done with the roller support reaction. In the rotation around $A$ the horizontal component of the force, applied at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ elevation, has $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise moment and the vertical component has $108\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise moment; the reaction $R_D$ counts with the vertical component only. It is located $R_D = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$, $H_A = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**, and $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.

At joint $D$ the chord $CD$, diagonal $HD$ and reaction $R_D$, parallel to $HD$ concur: for the **zero-force member rule**, which also applies when a force is directed along one of the members, chord $CD$ is unloaded and diagonal $HD$ carries all the reaction, in compression with $20\,\mathrm{kN}$.

At joints $E$, $H$, $C$ and $B$ there are no loads, so in each the two diagonals have opposite axial forces, and the horizontal equation gives the chord. We proceed from $A$ to $E$ and from $D$ to $H$, then $C$ and $B$.

The three remaining equations, horizontal in $B$ and the two of the joint $G$, equal $0$; at joint $G$ the two components of force $F$ appear.

**Results**: $R_D = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$, $H_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ left, $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ up; $N_{CD} = 0$; $N_{AB} = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{BC} = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{EB} = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{CH} = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension); $N_{AE} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{EG} = -12\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{BG} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{GC} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{GH} = -24\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{HD} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression).

</div>

## Exercise 3

The cantilever truss shown is pinned to the wall at $A = (0, 0)$ and supported at joint $D = (4, 3)$ by an inclined link whose reaction $R_D$ acts along unit vector $\left(-\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$. The other joints are $B = (4, 0)$ and $C = (8, 0)$, and the members are $AB$, $BC$, $BD$, $CD$, $AD$. At joint $B$, a force $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts and at joint $C$, a force $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts, both downward. Calculating axial forces in members and reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,4,3" aste="A-B;B-C;B-D;C-D;A-D" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera,180;D,pendolo,143.13" forze="4 -1.3,0,-1.3,;C,0,-1.6,P_2" etichette="4.25,-1.1,P_1" quote="A,B,4 m,1.9;B,C,4 m,1.9;8 0,8 3,3 m,0.9" titolo="Cantilever truss with a pin at $A$, an inclined link at $D$, and forces $P_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $P_2 = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

$C$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{CB} = (-1, 0)$ and $\hat{e}_{CD} = \left(-\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{BC} - \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{CD} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{CD} - 18 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{CD} = 30\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{BC} = -24\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$B$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{BA} = (-1, 0)$, $\hat{e}_{BC} = (1, 0)$ and $\hat{e}_{BD} = (0, 1)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{AB} + N_{BC} = 0 \\[4pt]
N_{BD} - 12 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AB} = -24\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{BD} = 12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$D$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{DA} = \left(-\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$, $\hat{e}_{DB} = (0, -1)$ and $\hat{e}_{DC} = \left(\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AD} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{CD} - \dfrac{4}{5}\,R_D = 0 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AD} - N_{BD} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{CD} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,R_D = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\begin{aligned}
R_D &= 30 - N_{AD} \\[6pt]
-\frac{6}{5}\,N_{AD} &= 12
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
N_{AD} = -10\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
R_D = 40\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$A$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{AB} = (1, 0)$ and $\hat{e}_{AD} = \left(\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{AB} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AD} + H_A = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AD} + V_A = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
H_A = 24 + 8 = 32\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check with the rotation of the whole truss around $A$; the two-force link reaction at $D$ has $(-32, 24)\,\mathrm{kN}$ components:
$$
4 \cdot 24 - 3 \cdot (-32) - 12 \cdot 4 - 18 \cdot 8 = 96 + 96 - 48 - 144 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The truss has $n = 4$ joints, $a = 5$ members and $v = 3$ scalar constraints (hinge and two-force links), then $2n = 8 = a + v$. It is simple, formed by the triangles $ABD$ and $BCD$, and the line of the two-force link, which passes through $D$ with $-\dfrac{3}{4}$ slope, does not pass through $A$: the truss is statically determinate.

It starts from end $C$, where two members meet. The vertical equation gives the diagonal $CD$ **in tension** with $30\,\mathrm{kN}$, which supports the load at $C$; the horizontal one gives the bottom chord $BC$ **in compression** with $24\,\mathrm{kN}$. At joint $B$ the members $AB$ and $BC$ are aligned, so they have the same force, and the vertical $BD$ carries up the load of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, and is in tension.

At joint $D$ the unknowns are $N_{AD}$ and the two-force link reaction. From the horizontal equation, divided by $\dfrac{4}{5}$, $R_D = 30 - N_{AD}$ is obtained; substituting in the vertical equation you get $N_{AD} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$, compression, and $R_D = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$. The reaction is positive, so the two-force link pulls the joint $D$ towards the wall and is an **tie**. The joint $A$ finally gives the components of the hinge reaction.

The check uses the rotation of the whole truss around $A$, which is not needed: the reaction of the two-force link has counterclockwise moment $192\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and the two loads have clockwise moment $48 + 144 = 192\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

**Results**: $N_{CD} = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{BD} = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension); $N_{AB} = N_{BC} = -24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{AD} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression); $R_D = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$ (two-force link in tension), $H_A = 32\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, $V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.

</div>
