## Exercise 1

The Pratt truss shown has four $2\,\mathrm{m}$ panels and $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ height, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $E$. At joints $B$, $C$, $D$ of the bottom chord are hung the forces $P_1 = 36\,\mathrm{kN}$, $P_2 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $P_3 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward. Calculate by the Ritter method the axial forces in the $FG$, $FC$ and $BC$ members of the second panel.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,4,0;D,6,0;E,8,0;F,2,1.5;G,4,1.5;H,6,1.5" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="2 -1.1,0,-1.1,;4 -1.1,0,-1.1,;6 -1.1,0,-1.1," etichette="2.2,-0.9,P_1;4.2,-0.9,P_2;6.2,-0.9,P_3" quote="A,B,2 m,1.6;B,C,2 m,1.6;C,D,2 m,1.6;D,E,2 m,1.6;8 0,8 1.5,1.5 m,0.8" titolo="Pratt truss with three forces suspended from the bottom chord"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
8\,V_E - 36 \cdot 2 - 24 \cdot 4 - 12 \cdot 6 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_E - 72 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_E = \frac{240}{8} = 30\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 42\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;F,2,1.5;C,4,0" aste="A-B;A-F;B-F;F-2.9 1.5;F-2.9 0.825;B-2.9 0" reticolare=1 forze="2 -1.1,0,-1.1,;3.8 1.5,0.9,0,;3.62 0.285,0.72,-0.54,;3.8 0,0.9,0," reazioni="A,0,1.1,V_A" etichette="2.2,-0.9,P_1;3.35,1.75,N_FG;3.1,0.4,N_FC;3.35,-0.3,N_BC" punti="2,1.5,,centro;4,0,,centro" tratteggi="2.6,-0.6 2.6,2.1" titolo="Portion to the left of the section, with the cut members assumed to be in tension; the Ritter point for $FG$ is $C$, and that for $BC$ is $F$"}

Rotation of the left side around $F$, Ritter point of $BC$:
$$
-42 \cdot 2 + 1.5\,N_{BC} = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{BC} = \frac{84}{1.5} = 56\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Rotation of the left side around $C$, Ritter point of $FG$:
$$
-42 \cdot 4 + 36 \cdot 2 - 1.5\,N_{FG} = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{FG} = -\frac{96}{1.5} = -64\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Vertical force equilibrium of the left portion, with $\hat{e}_{FC} = \left(\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$:
$$
42 - 36 - \frac{3}{5}\,N_{FC} = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{FC} = \frac{5}{3} \cdot 6 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Verify with $A$ and $B$ joints, with $\hat{e}_{AF} = \left(\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$:
$$
\frac{3}{5}\,N_{AF} + 42 = 0 \ \Rightarrow\ N_{AF} = -70\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{AB} = -\frac{4}{5}\,N_{AF} = 56\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{BC} - N_{AB} = 56 - 56 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The truss is a simple Pratt with hinge and roller support, so statically determinate. The reactions are calculated with the whole truss: moment equilibrium about $A$ gives only $V_E$ and the vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A$.

The vertical section in the second panel cuts three members: the top chord $FG$, the diagonal $FC$ and the bottom chord $BC$. The $FG$ and $FC$ lines meet in $F$, which is the **Ritter's Point** of $BC$; $FC$ and $BC$ lines meet in $C$, $FG$'s Ritter point. It studies the left side, which contains the joints $A$, $B$, $F$ and on which $V_A$ and $P_1$ act. The forces of the cut members are assumed to be tension, i.e. directed from the side towards the section.

In the rotation around $F$ appear $V_A$, with $2\,\mathrm{m}$ arm and clockwise, and $N_{BC}$, with $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ arm; $P_1$ force has zero moment arm because its line passes through $F$. It follows that $N_{BC} = 56\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. the bottom chord is **in tension**. In the rotation around $C$ appear $V_A$ and $P_1$ and the top chord, which has a moment arm of $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and clockwise moment if it is in tension; the negative result says $FG$ is **in compression** with $64\,\mathrm{kN}$. The two chords are parallel, so the force in the diagonal is derived from the vertical translation: the diagonal brings the difference between the reaction and the load to the left of the section, and since it drops to the right it is **in tension**, as in every Pratt.

The joint method is used as a check. The joint $A$ gives the chord $AB$; the joint $B$ has two horizontal members aligned and the vertical, so the horizontal equation says $N_{BC} = N_{AB}$, and the value $56\,\mathrm{kN}$ found with Ritter is confirmed.

**Results**: $N_{FG} = -64\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression), $N_{FC} = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension), $N_{BC} = 56\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension).

</div>

## Exercise 2

The Howe truss shown has four panels of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ height; the diagonals $BG$ and $DG$ go up towards the midspan. It is supported by a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $E$; $F$, $G$, $H$ joints of the top chord act the forces $P_1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$, $P_2 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $P_3 = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$, downward. Calculate axial forces in the $GH$, $DG$ and $CD$ members of the third panel.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;B-G;D-G" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="F,0,-1.5,P_1;G,0,-1.5,P_2;H,0,-1.5,P_3" tratteggi="7.5,-0.8 7.5,5.6" etichette="7.65,5.5,s" quote="A,B,3 m,1.3;B,C,3 m,1.3;C,D,3 m,1.3;D,E,3 m,1.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,1" titolo="Howe truss with three forces on the top chord and section $s$ in the third panel"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
12\,V_E - 10 \cdot 3 - 20 \cdot 6 - 30 \cdot 9 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_E - 60 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_E = \frac{420}{12} = 35\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 25\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Right portion (joints $D$, $E$, $H$), with the cut-member forces directed toward the section: $N_{GH}$ at $H$ along unit vector $(-1, 0)$, $N_{CD}$ at $D$ along unit vector $(-1, 0)$, and $N_{DG}$ at $D$ along unit vector $\left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$.

Rotation around $D$, Ritter point of $GH$:
$$
35 \cdot 3 + 4\,N_{GH} = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{GH} = -\frac{105}{4} = -26.25\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Rotation around $G$, Ritter point of $CD$:
$$
35 \cdot 6 - 30 \cdot 3 - 4\,N_{CD} = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{CD} = \frac{120}{4} = 30\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Vertical translation:
$$
35 - 30 + \frac{4}{5}\,N_{DG} = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{DG} = -\frac{5}{4} \cdot 5 = -6.25\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check vertical force equilibrium of the left portion, where $N_{DG}$ acts at $G$ along unit vector $\left(\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$:
$$
V_A - P_1 - P_2 - \frac{4}{5}\,N_{DG} = 25 - 10 - 20 + 5 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Reactions are derived from whole truss; the largest load is close to $E$, so $V_E = 35\,\mathrm{kN}$ exceeds $V_A = 25\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Section $s$ cuts the top chord $GH$, diagonal $DG$ and bottom chord $CD$. It is advisable to study the right side, on which only $V_E$ and $P_3$ act. The Ritter point of $GH$ is $D$, intersection of the lines of $DG$ and $CD$; the Ritter point of $CD$ is $G$, intersection of the lines of $GH$ and $DG$. On the right side tension forces are directed to the left, that is, towards the section.

In the rotation around $D$ the force $P_3$ has zero moment arm, because $H$ lies on the vertical line through $D$; $V_E$ remains, with counterclockwise moment $105\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and $N_{GH}$, which with the moment $4\,\mathrm{m}$ arm has counterclockwise moment $4\,N_{GH}$. The top chord is **in compression** with $26.25\,\mathrm{kN}$. In the rotation around $G$ tension force of $CD$, to the left and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ below $G$, has clockwise moment, and the bottom chord results **in tension** with $30\,\mathrm{kN}$. For the diagonal the Ritter point is at infinity, because the chords are parallel, and the vertical translation is written: $V_E$ exceeds $P_3$ by $5\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the diagonal, which on the right side pulls upward if it is in tension, must push downward. The diagonal $DG$ is therefore **in compression** with $6.25\,\mathrm{kN}$, like the diagonals of a Howe.

The check uses the left side, which was not used in the calculation in the calculation: for it too the vertical translation is satisfied.

**Results**: $N_{GH} = -26.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression), $N_{DG} = -6.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression), $N_{CD} = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension).

</div>

## Exercise 3

The Warren truss shown has basic $3\,\mathrm{m}$ triangles and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ height, a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $D$. At joint $G$ the vertical force $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward and at joint $H$ the horizontal force $Q = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right. Calculate reactions and, with a section of Ritter, axial forces in the $EG$, $BG$ and $BC$ members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,1.5,2;G,4.5,2;H,7.5,2" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;E-G;G-H;A-E;E-B;B-G;G-C;C-H;H-D" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="G,0,-1.4,P;H,1.4,0,Q" tratteggi="3.75,-0.7 3.75,2.8" etichette="3.9,2.7,s" quote="A,B,3 m,1.2;B,C,3 m,1.2;C,D,3 m,1.2;9 0,9 2,2 m,1" titolo="Warren truss with a vertical force at $G$ and a horizontal force at $H$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + 9 = 0 \\[4pt]
9\,V_D - 30 \cdot 4.5 - 9 \cdot 2 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_D - 30 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
H_A = -9\,\mathrm{kN},
\quad
V_D = \frac{153}{9} = 17\,\mathrm{kN},
\quad
V_A = 13\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Left portion (joints $A$, $B$, $E$): $N_{EG}$ at $E$ along unit vector $(1, 0)$, $N_{BG}$ at $B$ along unit vector $\left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$, and $N_{BC}$ at $B$ along unit vector $(1, 0)$.

Rotation around $B = (3, 0)$, Ritter point of $EG$:
$$
-3\,V_A - 2\,N_{EG} = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{EG} = -\frac{3 \cdot 13}{2} = -19.5\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Rotation around $G = (4.5;\ 2)$, Ritter point of $BC$:
$$
-4.5\,V_A + 2\,H_A + 2\,N_{BC} = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{BC} = \frac{58.5 + 18}{2} = 38.25\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Vertical translation:
$$
V_A + \frac{4}{5}\,N_{BG} = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{BG} = -\frac{5}{4} \cdot 13 = -16.25\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Verify by joint method, $A$ and $E$ joints:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{joint } A: \quad \frac{4}{5}\,N_{AE} + 13 = 0 &\ \Rightarrow\ N_{AE} = -16.25\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
\text{joint } E: \quad -\frac{4}{5}\,N_{AE} - \frac{4}{5}\,N_{EB} = 0 &\ \Rightarrow\ N_{EB} = 16.25\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
\text{joint } E: \quad -\frac{3}{5}\,N_{AE} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{EB} + N_{EG} &= 9.75 + 9.75 - 19.5 = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Reactions are derived from whole truss. The horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A = -9\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**, because the roller support reacts only vertically. In the rotation around $A$ the force $Q$, horizontal and applied at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ elevation, has clockwise moment $9 \cdot 2 = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, which is added to that of $P$.

Section $s$ between $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x = 4.5\,\mathrm{m}$ cuts the top chord $EG$, the diagonal $BG$ and the bottom chord $BC$. The lines of $BG$ and $BC$ both pass through $B$, which is then the Ritter point of $EG$; $EG$ and $BG$ lines meet in $G$, $BC$'s Ritter point. In the rotation around $B$ the reactions in $A$ give only the moment of $V_A$, because $H_A$ acts on the line $AB$; the top chord, in tension, would have clockwise moment with a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$, and the negative result indicates that it is **in compression**. In the rotation around $G$ also $H_A$ has arm, $2\,\mathrm{m}$; is directed to the left and is applied under $G$, so it has clockwise moment, like $V_A$. The bottom chord is **in tension**. The diagonal $BG$ is derived from the vertical translation, because the chords are parallel: to the left of the section there is only $V_A$ upward, and the diagonal, which rises to the right, must push downward, so it is **in compression**.

The check remakes the chord $EG$ with the method of joints, starting from $A$ and passing through $E$, and finds the same value.

**Results**: $H_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, $V_A = 13\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_D = 17\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $N_{EG} = -19.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression), $N_{BG} = -16.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression), $N_{BC} = 38.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension).

</div>
