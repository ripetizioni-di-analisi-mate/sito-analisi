## Exercise 1

The truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)$, $C = (8, 0)$, $D = (0, 3)$, $E = (4, 3)$ in meters and the members $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $DE$, $BE$, $AE$, $EC$. It is supported by a pin at $A$ and a horizontal-level roller support at $C$. In $D$ the horizontal force $Q = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right and in $E$ the vertical force $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Check that the truss is statically determinate, locate the unloaded members and calculate by the Ritter method the axial forces in the $DE$, $AE$ and $AB$ members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,0,3;E,4,3" aste="A-B;B-C;A-D;D-E;B-E;A-E;E-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" forze="D,1.4,0,Q;E,0,-1.4,P" tratteggi="2,-0.8 2,3.8" etichette="2.15,3.7,s" quote="A,B,4 m,1.1;B,C,4 m,1.1;8 0,8 3,3 m,0.9" titolo="Truss with horizontal force $Q$ at $D$ and vertical force $P$ at $E$; section $s$ cuts $DE$, $AE$, and $AB$"}

$$
n = 5, \quad a = 7, \quad v = 3
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
2n = 10 = a + v
$$

Reactions:
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + 10 = 0 \\[4pt]
8\,V_C - 30 \cdot 4 - 10 \cdot 3 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_C - 30 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
H_A = -10\,\mathrm{kN},
\quad
V_C = \frac{150}{8} = 18.75\,\mathrm{kN},
\quad
V_A = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Unloaded members:
$$
\text{joint } D: \ N_{AD} = 0;
\qquad
\text{joint } B: \ N_{BE} = 0, \ N_{AB} = N_{BC}
$$

Portion to the right of the section (joints $B$, $C$, $E$): $N_{DE}$ at $E$ along unit vector $(-1, 0)$, $N_{AE}$ at $E$ along unit vector $\left(-\dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$, and $N_{AB}$ at $B$ along unit vector $(-1, 0)$.
$$
\begin{cases}
8 \cdot 18.75 - 30 \cdot 4 + 3\,N_{DE} = 0 \\[6pt]
4 \cdot 18.75 - 3\,N_{AB} = 0 \\[6pt]
18.75 - 30 - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AE} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\begin{aligned}
N_{DE} &= -\frac{30}{3} = -10\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
N_{AB} &= \frac{75}{3} = 25\,\mathrm{kN} \\[6pt]
N_{AE} &= -\frac{5}{3} \cdot 11.25 = -18.75\,\mathrm{kN}
\end{aligned}
$$

Check at joint $D$, horizontal translation:
$$
N_{DE} + 10 = -10 + 10 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The count gives $2n = a + v = 10$, condition *necessary*. To conclude that the truss is statically determinate we observe that it is **simple**: we start from the triangle $ADE$, we add $B$ with the members $AB$ and $BE$, not aligned, and then $C$ with the members $BC$ and $EC$. The truss is therefore a rigid body, and the pin at $A$ with the roller support at $C$, whose vertical reaction does not pass through $A$, are well-arranged constraints.

Reactions come from whole truss; in the rotation around $A$ the force $Q$, directed to the right at elevation $3\,\mathrm{m}$, has clockwise moment. At joint $D$ the vertical member $AD$ and horizontal member $DE$ concur, and the force $Q$ is directed along $DE$: the vertical equation of the joint contains only $N_{AD}$, which is therefore **null**. At joint $B$ the members $AB$ and $BC$ are aligned and the vertical $BE$ is unloaded.

Section $s$ cuts $DE$, $AE$ and $AB$; The right side is studied, on which $V_C$ and $P$ act. The Ritter point of $DE$ is $A$, where the lines of $AE$ and $AB$ meet: the first equation is the rotation around $A$, and gives $DE$ **in compression** with $10\,\mathrm{kN}$. The Ritter point of $AB$ is $E$: the second equation is the rotation around $E$, where the force $P$ has zero moment arm, and gives $AB$ **in tension** with $25\,\mathrm{kN}$. The members $DE$ and $AB$ are parallel, so the diagonal is obtained from the vertical translation, third equation: $AE$ is in compression with $18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The check is the horizontal equilibrium of the joint $D$: the chord $DE$, in compression with $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, pushes the joint to the left and balances $Q$.

**Results**: statically determinate truss; $AD$ and $BE$ unloaded members; $N_{DE} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{AE} = -18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression), $N_{AB} = 25\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension); $H_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, $V_A = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_C = 18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The truss shown has two panels measuring $3\,\mathrm{m}$ by $4\,\mathrm{m}$, with the joints $A$, $B$, $C$ on the bottom chord and $D$, $E$, $G$ on the top chord, and the members $AB$, $BC$, $DE$, $EG$, $AD$, $BE$, $CG$, $AE$; it is supported by a pin at $A$ and a horizontal roller support at $C$. Classify the truss. Then the diagonal $BG$ is added: verify that the new truss is statically determinate and calculate the axial forces in the members when two forces of $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ down act in the joints $E$ and $G$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,0,4;E,3,4;G,6,4" aste="A-B;B-C;D-E;E-G;A-D;B-E;C-G;A-E" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="A,B,3 m,1.1;B,C,3 m,1.1;6 0,6 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Two-panel truss with a diagonal in the first panel only"}

$$
n = 6, \quad a = 8, \quad v = 3
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
2n - (a + v) = 12 - 11 = 1 = l - i
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,0,4;E,3,4;G,6,4" aste="A-B;B-C;D-E;E-G;A-D;B-E;C-G;A-E;B-G" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" forze="E,0,-1.5,P;G,0,-1.5,P" quote="A,B,3 m,1.1;B,C,3 m,1.1;6 0,6 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Truss with added diagonal $BG$ and forces $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $E$ and $G$"}

With the diagonal $BG$: $a = 9$ and $2n = 12 = a + v$.

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_C - 30 \cdot 3 - 30 \cdot 6 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_C - 60 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_C = 45\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Unloaded members and $C$ joint:
$$
\text{joint } D: \ N_{AD} = N_{DE} = 0;
\qquad
\text{joint } C: \ N_{BC} = 0, \quad N_{CG} + 45 = 0 \ \Rightarrow\ N_{CG} = -45\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$G$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{GE} = (-1, 0)$, $\hat{e}_{GC} = (0, -1)$ and $\hat{e}_{GB} = \left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{EG} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{BG} = 0 \\[8pt]
-N_{CG} - \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{BG} - 30 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{BG} = \frac{5}{4} \cdot 15 = 18.75\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{EG} = -11.25\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$A$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{AB} = (1, 0)$, $\hat{e}_{AE} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ and $N_{AD} = 0$:
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{AB} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AE} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AE} + 15 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AE} = -18.75\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{AB} = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Joint $B$, vertical translation, with $\hat{e}_{BE} = (0, 1)$ and $\hat{e}_{BG} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$:
$$
N_{BE} + \frac{4}{5}\,N_{BG} = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{BE} = -15\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Checks ($B$ joint horizontally, $E$ joint horizontally and vertically):
$$
\begin{aligned}
-N_{AB} + N_{BC} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{BG} &= -11.25 + 0 + 11.25 = 0 \\[6pt]
-\frac{3}{5}\,N_{AE} - N_{DE} + N_{EG} &= 11.25 - 0 - 11.25 = 0 \\[6pt]
-\frac{4}{5}\,N_{AE} - N_{BE} - 30 &= 15 + 15 - 30 = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For the first truss the count gives $2n - (a + v) = 1$, so for the $2n = a + v$ condition it equals $l - i = 1$ and the truss is **unstable**. The first $ABED$ panel consists of the $ABE$ and $ADE$ triangles and is rigid without extra members; the second $BCGE$ panel is a panel without a diagonal. The mechanism is as follows: the $ABED$ rigid panel rotates about hinge $A$, the joint $B$ moves vertically and the joint $D$ horizontally; the joint $C$ remains stationary, because the horizontal member $BC$ does not stretch, and the quadrilateral $BCGE$ deforms with $CG$ rotating around $C$. The members are not in excess in any panel, therefore $i = 0$ and $l = 1$.

Adding the diagonal $BG$ the second panel becomes the couple of triangles $BCG$ and $BEG$, and the truss is **simple**: from the triangle $ABE$ are added $D$ ($AD$, $DE$, $G$, $EG$, $BG$) and $C$ ($BC$, $CG$ members). With the hinge and roller support well arranged it is statically determinate, and the count gives $2n = a + v = 12$.

At joint $D$, two non-aligned members meet without loads, so $AD$ and $DE$ are **zero-force members**. At joint $C$ the reaction $V_C$ is vertical, i.e. directed along the $CG$ strut: the horizontal equation gives $N_{BC} = 0$ and the vertical one gives the strut in compression with $45\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. the whole reaction. At joint $G$, the vertical pushes up with $45\,\mathrm{kN}$, the load removes $30$ and the diagonal $BG$ pulls down the remaining $15\,\mathrm{kN}$: it is **in tension** with $18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$. The joints $A$ and $B$ complete the calculation, and the three unused equations equal $0$.

**Results**: the first truss is unstable ($l = 1$, $i = 0$); with the diagonal $BG$ is statically determinate, with $V_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_C = 45\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $H_A = 0$; $N_{AD} = N_{DE} = N_{BC} = 0$; $N_{AB} = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{BG} = 18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension); $N_{AE} = -18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{BE} = -15\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{EG} = -11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{CG} = -45\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression).

</div>

## Exercise 3

The Warren truss shown has base triangles $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $2\,\mathrm{m}$. It is supported by a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $D$ that moves along a vertical plane, then reacts horizontally. Classify the truss. It then replaces the roller support with one on a horizontal plane: with the downward force $P = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ at joint $G$, calculate the reactions and axial forces in the $AE$, $AB$, $EB$ and $EG$ members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,1.5,2;G,4.5,2;H,7.5,2" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;E-G;G-H;A-E;E-B;B-G;G-C;C-H;H-D" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello,0" forze="G,0,-1.3,P" quote="A,B,3 m,1.2;B,C,3 m,1.2;C,D,3 m,1.2" titolo="Warren truss with a roller at $D$ on a vertical surface and force $P = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $G$"}

First truss:
$$
n = 7, \quad a = 11, \quad v = 3
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
2n = 14 = a + v,
\qquad
\text{rotation about } A: \ -18 \cdot 4.5 = -81 \neq 0
$$

Travature with roller support on horizontal plane in $D$:
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
9\,V_D - 18 \cdot 4.5 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_D - 18 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_D = 9\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$A$ joint:
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{AB} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AE} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AE} + 9 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AE} = -11.25\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{AB} = 6.75\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$E$ joint:
$$
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AE} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{EB} + N_{EG} = 0 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AE} - \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{EB} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{EB} = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{EG} = -6.75 - 6.75 = -13.5\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check $N_{EG}$ with the rotation of the left part of a section between $B$ and $G$ around the point of Ritter $B = (3, 0)$:
$$
-3 \cdot V_A - 2\,N_{EG} = -27 + 27 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The truss is a simple Warren, so internally rigid, and the count gives $2n = a + v$. However, counting is only a necessary condition. The external reactions are the two components of the pin at $A$ and the horizontal reaction of the roller support at $D$, whose line is the axis $x$ and passes through $A$. All three lines of action pass through $A$, so the constraints are **ill-disposed**: the truss can rotate an infinitesimal rotation around $A$, and $D$ moves vertically, a direction that roller support does not prevent. Therefore $l = 1$, and from the $l - i = 2n - (a + v) = 0$ relationship follows $i = 1$: the truss is unstable and statically indeterminate. The balance with force at $G$ is impossible, because in the rotation around $A$ the reactions have zero moment and the load has $-81\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ moment. The static indeterminacy is seen in the horizontal translation, which contains $H_A$ and the roller support reaction and is not enough to determine them separately.

With horizontal roller support the reaction at $D$ is vertical and its line does not pass through $A$, so the truss is statically determinate. The load is at midspan and the reactions equal $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ each. At joint $A$ the diagonal $AE$ is **in compression** and chord $AB$ **in tension**; at joint $E$, without loads, the two diagonals have opposite stresses, and the top chord is in compression with $13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The check independently obtains $N_{EG}$ with a section of Ritter: the Ritter point of $EG$ is $B$, where the lines of $BG$ and $BC$ meet, and on the left side only $V_A$ acts, with $3\,\mathrm{m}$ arm.

**Results**: with roller support on a vertical plane the truss is unstable and statically indeterminate ($l = i = 1$); with horizontal roller support is statically determinate, with $V_A = V_D = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $H_A = 0$; $N_{AE} = -11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{EG} = -13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression), $N_{AB} = 6.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{EB} = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension).

</div>
