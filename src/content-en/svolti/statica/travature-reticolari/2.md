## Exercise 1

The Pratt truss shown has four $3\,\mathrm{m}$ panels and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ height, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $E$. At joint $C$ of the bottom chord is hung the downward force $P = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$. Locate the unloaded members and calculate the axial forces in all the members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="6 -1.6,0,-1.6," etichette="6.3,-1.3,P" quote="A,B,3 m,2.3;B,C,3 m,2.3;C,D,3 m,2.3;D,E,3 m,2.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Pratt truss with force $P = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ suspended from joint $C$"}

Unloaded members:
$$
\text{joint } B: \ N_{BF} = 0, \ N_{BC} = N_{AB};
\qquad
\text{joint } D: \ N_{DH} = 0, \ N_{CD} = N_{DE};
\qquad
\text{joint } G: \ N_{CG} = 0, \ N_{GH} = N_{FG}
$$

Reactions, by symmetry:
$$
H_A = 0,
\qquad
V_A = V_E = \frac{24}{2} = 12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$A$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{AB} = (1, 0)$ and $\hat{e}_{AF} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{AB} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AF} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AF} + 12 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AF} = -15\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{AB} = 9\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$F$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{FA} = \left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$, $\hat{e}_{FG} = (1, 0)$, $\hat{e}_{FC} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ and $N_{BF} = 0$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AF} + N_{FG} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{FC} = 0 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AF} - \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{FC} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{FC} = 15\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{FG} = -9 - 9 = -18\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

By symmetry: $N_{DE} = N_{CD} = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{EH} = -15\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{HC} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{GH} = -18\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Verify at joint $C$, with $\hat{e}_{CF} = \left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ and $\hat{e}_{CH} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
-N_{BC} + N_{CD} - \frac{3}{5}\,N_{FC} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{HC} &= -9 + 9 - 9 + 9 = 0 \\[6pt]
\frac{4}{5}\,N_{FC} + \frac{4}{5}\,N_{HC} + N_{CG} - 24 &= 12 + 12 + 0 - 24 = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Before the calculation we look for the joints without loads in which three members of which two are aligned. Horizontal members $AB$ and $BC$ and vertical $BF$ meet at joint $B$; for the **zero-force member rule** the vertical is unloaded and the two horizontal members have the same force. The same reasoning applies at joint $D$ to vertical $DH$ and at joint $G$ to vertical $CG$, with collinear members $FG$ and $GH$. Of the thirteen members, ten remain to be calculated, and the symmetry of the left half is sufficient.

At joint $A$ the vertical equation contains only the $AF$ end diagonal, which is **in compression** with $15\,\mathrm{kN}$; $AB$ chord is **in tension** with $9\,\mathrm{kN}$. At joint $F$, the unknowns are $N_{FG}$ and $N_{FC}$, because $N_{BF} = 0$. The vertical equation says that the two inclined members $FA$ and $FC$ have opposite stresses, so the diagonal $FC$ is **in tension** with $15\,\mathrm{kN}$, as in any Pratt truss; the horizontal equation gives the top chord in compression with $18\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The joint $C$ was not used. Vertical check shows how the load hanging in $C$ rises along the two diagonals in tension, each with vertical component $12\,\mathrm{kN}$.

**Results**: $BF$, $CG$, $DH$ unloaded members; $N_{AB} = N_{BC} = N_{CD} = N_{DE} = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{FC} = N_{HC} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension); $N_{AF} = N_{EH} = -15\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{FG} = N_{GH} = -18\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression); $V_A = V_E = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ up.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The truss shown has the $12\,\mathrm{m}$ long chords at $4\,\mathrm{m}$ distance, four panels of $3\,\mathrm{m}$, the end verticals $AF$ and $EL$ and the diagonals $AG$, $BH$, $HD$, $IE$. It is supported by a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $E$; at joints $G$, $H$, $I$ of the top chord act three vertical forces of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Locate unloaded members and calculate axial forces in other members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,0,4;G,3,4;H,6,4;I,9,4;L,12,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;H-I;I-L;A-F;B-G;C-H;D-I;E-L;A-G;B-H;H-D;I-E" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="G,0,-1.5,P;H,0,-1.5,P;I,0,-1.5,P" quote="A,B,3 m,1.3;B,C,3 m,1.3;C,D,3 m,1.3;D,E,3 m,1.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,1.2" titolo="Truss with end verticals and three forces $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the top chord"}

Unloaded members:
$$
\text{joint } F: \ N_{AF} = N_{FG} = 0;
\qquad
\text{joint } L: \ N_{EL} = N_{IL} = 0;
\qquad
\text{joint } C: \ N_{CH} = 0, \ N_{BC} = N_{CD}
$$

Reactions, by symmetry:
$$
H_A = 0,
\qquad
V_A = V_E = \frac{3 \cdot 20}{2} = 30\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$A$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{AB} = (1, 0)$, $\hat{e}_{AG} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ and $N_{AF} = 0$:
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{AB} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AG} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AG} + 30 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AG} = -37.5\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{AB} = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$G$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{GH} = (1, 0)$, $\hat{e}_{GA} = \left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$, $\hat{e}_{GB} = (0, -1)$ and $N_{FG} = 0$:
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{GH} - \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AG} = 0 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AG} - N_{BG} - 20 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{GH} = -22.5\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{BG} = 30 - 20 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$B$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{BA} = (-1, 0)$, $\hat{e}_{BC} = (1, 0)$, $\hat{e}_{BG} = (0, 1)$ and $\hat{e}_{BH} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-N_{AB} + N_{BC} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{BH} = 0 \\[8pt]
N_{BG} + \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{BH} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{BH} = -12.5\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{BC} = 22.5 + 7.5 = 30\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

By symmetry: $N_{DE} = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{CD} = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{IE} = -37.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{HI} = -22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{DI} = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{HD} = -12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Verify at joint $H$, with $\hat{e}_{HB} = \left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ and $\hat{e}_{HD} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
-N_{GH} + N_{HI} - \frac{3}{5}\,N_{BH} + \frac{3}{5}\,N_{HD} &= 22.5 - 22.5 + 7.5 - 7.5 = 0 \\[6pt]
-\frac{4}{5}\,N_{BH} - \frac{4}{5}\,N_{HD} - N_{CH} - 20 &= 10 + 10 - 0 - 20 = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Joint $F$ is a corner of the truss. Only vertical $AF$ and chord $FG$ meet there. They are noncollinear, and no external load acts at the joint. For the **zero-force member rule** both members are unloaded; The same is true at joint $L$. At joint $C$ of the bottom chord the aligned members $BC$ and $CD$ and the vertical $CH$, without loads, then the vertical $CH$ is unloaded. Five out of seventeen members have zero force for this load condition; However, they are necessary, because removing one of their trusses would become unstable.

With $N_{AF} = 0$ the joint $A$ has two unknowns, $N_{AB}$ and $N_{AG}$; the vertical equation gives the diagonal $AG$ **in compression** with $37.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. At joint $G$ the chord $FG$ is unloaded, so the horizontal equation immediately gives $N_{GH}$ and the vertical one gives the $BG$, **in tension** vertical with $10\,\mathrm{kN}$: the diagonal $AG$ carries $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ up, the load takes $20$ off and the vertical transmits the remaining $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ down. At joint $B$, the vertical equation gives the diagonal $BH$, in compression, and the horizontal one the chord $BC$. The diagonals go up towards the midspan and are in compression, as in the Howe scheme.

The joint $H$ has not been used and provides check: the vertical components of the two in compression diagonals, $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ each, balance the load at $H$.

**Results**: $AF$, $FG$, $CH$, $IL$, $EL$ unloaded members; $N_{AB} = N_{DE} = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{BC} = N_{CD} = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{BG} = N_{DI} = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension); $N_{AG} = N_{IE} = -37.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{GH} = N_{HI} = -22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{BH} = N_{HD} = -12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression).

</div>

## Exercise 3

The Warren truss shown has base triangles $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $2\,\mathrm{m}$. It is backed with a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $D$; the joints $I$ and $L$ form one overhang beyond the $D$ support. In the joints $E$ and $G$, two forces of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ act downward. Locate unloaded members, calculate reactions and axial forces in $AB$, $AE$, $EB$ and $EG$ members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;L,12,0;E,1.5,2;G,4.5,2;H,7.5,2;I,10.5,2" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-L;E-G;G-H;H-I;A-E;E-B;B-G;G-C;C-H;H-D;D-I;I-L" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="E,0,-1.3,P;G,0,-1.3,P" quote="A,B,3 m,1.2;B,C,3 m,1.2;C,D,3 m,1.2;D,L,3 m,1.2;12 0,12 2,2 m,1" titolo="Warren truss with an unloaded overhang and two forces $P = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $E$ and $G$"}

Unloaded members:
$$
\text{joint } L: \ N_{DL} = N_{IL} = 0
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\text{joint } I: \ N_{HI} = N_{DI} = 0
$$

Reactions:
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
9\,V_D - 18 \cdot 1.5 - 18 \cdot 4.5 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_D - 36 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V_D = \frac{108}{9} = 12\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
V_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$A$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{AB} = (1, 0)$ and $\hat{e}_{AE} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
N_{AB} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AE} = 0 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AE} + 24 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{AE} = -30\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{AB} = 18\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

$E$ joint, with $\hat{e}_{EA} = \left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$, $\hat{e}_{EB} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ and $\hat{e}_{EG} = (1, 0)$:
$$
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{AE} + \dfrac{3}{5}\,N_{EB} + N_{EG} = 0 \\[8pt]
-\dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{AE} - \dfrac{4}{5}\,N_{EB} - 18 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
N_{EB} = \frac{5}{4}\,(24 - 18) = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN},
\qquad
N_{EG} = -18 - 4.5 = -22.5\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

Check with the rotation of the whole truss around $D$:
$$
-9\,V_A + 18 \cdot 7.5 + 18 \cdot 4.5 = -216 + 135 + 81 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The diagonals are $\sqrt{1.5^2 + 2^2} = 2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ long, so they have direction cosines $\pm\dfrac{3}{5}$ and $\pm\dfrac{4}{5}$. End $L$ of the overhang is a joint without loads in which two non-aligned members, $DL$ and $IL$, meet: for the **zero-force member rule** they are both unloaded. After removing these two members, only $HI$ and $DI$ remain at joint $I$, which are also not aligned and without loads, so they are also unloaded. Overhang does not carry loads and therefore does not contribute to balance; its members serve when the overhang is loaded.

Reactions are obtained from the whole truss: moment equilibrium about $A$ gives only $V_D$, with the $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$ arms of the two loads, and vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A$. At joint $A$, the vertical equation contains only the diagonal $AE$, which is **in compression** with $30\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the horizontal equation gives the chord $AB$ **in tension** with $18\,\mathrm{kN}$. At joint $E$ the vertical equation contains only $N_{EB}$, since $N_{AE}$ is known and $EG$ is horizontal: the diagonal $AE$ carries $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ up, the load absorbs $18$ and the difference drops along $EB$, which is in tension. The horizontal equation gives the top chord $EG$ in compression.

Reactions were calculated with rotation around $A$ and vertical translation, so rotation around $D$ is an unused equation, and equals $0$.

**Results**: $DL$, $IL$, $HI$, $DI$ unloaded members; $V_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_D = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $H_A = 0$; $N_{AB} = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{EB} = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension), $N_{AE} = -30\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{EG} = -22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression).

</div>
