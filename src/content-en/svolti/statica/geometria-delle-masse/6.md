## Exercise 1

For the L-section in the figure, with the $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $10\,\mathrm{cm}$-thick $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ flanges, determine the central principal axes and the principal moments of inertia.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 8,0 8,2 2,2 2,10 0,10" frecce="0,0,9.5,0,x;0,0,0,11.5,y" quote="0 0,8 0,8 cm,0.9;0 0,0 10,10 cm,-0.9;8 0,8 2,2 cm,0.7;0 10,2 10,2 cm,-0.7" riferimento=0 titolo="L-section with leg lengths $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

$$
A_1 = 16\,\mathrm{cm^2},\ (4, 1);
\quad
A_2 = 16\,\mathrm{cm^2},\ (1, 6);
\qquad
G = \left( \frac{4 + 1}{2},\ \frac{1 + 6}{2} \right) = (2.5;\ 3.5)\,\mathrm{cm}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
I_{x_G} &= \left( \frac{8 \cdot 2^3}{12} + 16 \cdot 2.5^2 \right) + \left( \frac{2 \cdot 8^3}{12} + 16 \cdot 2.5^2 \right) = 105.33 + 185.33 = 290.67\,\mathrm{cm^4} \\[6pt]
I_{y_G} &= \left( \frac{2 \cdot 8^3}{12} + 16 \cdot 1.5^2 \right) + \left( \frac{8 \cdot 2^3}{12} + 16 \cdot 1.5^2 \right) = 121.33 + 41.33 = 162.67\,\mathrm{cm^4} \\[6pt]
I_{x_G y_G} &= 16 \cdot 1.5 \cdot (-2.5) + 16 \cdot (-1.5) \cdot 2.5 = -120\,\mathrm{cm^4}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
C = \frac{290.67 + 162.67}{2} = 226.67\,\mathrm{cm^4},
\qquad
D = \frac{290.67 - 162.67}{2} = 64\,\mathrm{cm^4},
\qquad
\sqrt{D^2 + I_{x_G y_G}^2} = \sqrt{64^2 + 120^2} = 136\,\mathrm{cm^4}
$$
$$
I_1 = 226.67 + 136 = 362.67\,\mathrm{cm^4},
\qquad
I_2 = 226.67 - 136 = 90.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}
$$
$$
\tan 2\alpha_0 = -\frac{2 \cdot (-120)}{128} = \frac{15}{8},
\qquad
2\alpha_0 = 61.93^\circ,
\qquad
\alpha_0 = 30.96^\circ
$$
$$
I_\xi(\alpha_0) = 226.67 + 64 \cdot \frac{8}{17} + 120 \cdot \frac{15}{17} = 226.67 + 136 = 362.67\,\mathrm{cm^4} = I_1
$$
$$
\text{check:}\quad
I_1 + I_2 = 453.33 = I_{x_G} + I_{y_G},
\qquad
I_1 I_2 = \frac{1088}{3} \cdot \frac{272}{3} = 32\,881.8 = I_{x_G} I_{y_G} - I_{x_G y_G}^2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two sides have the same area, so centroid is the midpoint of their centroids. The centroidal moments are found with the **parallel-axis theorem**, with the distances $(1.5;\ -2.5)\,\mathrm{cm}$ for the horizontal flange and $(-1.5;\ 2.5)\,\mathrm{cm}$ for the vertical part. The product of inertia is different from zero, so the $x_G$, $y_G$ axes are not main.

The theorem on **direction of the principal axes and principal moments** gives the two values $C \pm \sqrt{D^2 + I_{x_G y_G}^2}$ and the angle $\alpha_0$, with $\cos 2\alpha_0 = \dfrac{8}{17}$ and $\sin 2\alpha_0 = \dfrac{15}{17}$. To determine which axis has the maximum moment you replace $\alpha_0$ in the rotation formula: you get $I_1$, so the rotated $\xi$ axis of $30.96^\circ$ counterclockwise about $x_G$ is the axis of the maximum moment, and the perpendicular $\eta$ axis is that of the minimum. The check uses two quantities that do not change with rotation: the sum $I_{x_G} + I_{y_G}$ and the quantity $I_{x_G} I_{y_G} - I_{x_G y_G}^2$, which for the principal axes equals $I_1 I_2$. The principal axes are rotated by $\alpha_0 = 30.96^\circ$, with $I_1 = 362.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_2 = 90.67\,\mathrm{cm^4}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The Z-section in the figure is $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ high, with $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ thick web and flanges and $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ long flanges throughout. Determine the main centroidal axes and the principal moments of inertia.

::struttura{poligoni="-7,-7 1,-7 1,5 7,5 7,7 -1,7 -1,-5 -7,-5" frecce="-9,0,9.5,0,x;0,-8.5,0,9,y" quote="-1 7,7 7,8 cm,-1;-7 -7,1 -7,8 cm,1;7 -7,7 7,14 cm,4;7 5,7 7,2 cm,0.7;-1 0,1 0,2 cm,3.4" riferimento=0 titolo="Z-section of height $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ with web and flange thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_{x_G} &= \frac{2 \cdot 10^3}{12} + 2\left( \frac{8 \cdot 2^3}{12} + 16 \cdot 6^2 \right) = 166.67 + 1162.67 = 1329.33\,\mathrm{cm^4} \\[6pt]
I_{y_G} &= \frac{10 \cdot 2^3}{12} + 2\left( \frac{2 \cdot 8^3}{12} + 16 \cdot 3^2 \right) = 6.67 + 458.67 = 465.33\,\mathrm{cm^4} \\[6pt]
I_{x_G y_G} &= 16 \cdot 3 \cdot 6 + 16 \cdot (-3) \cdot (-6) = 576\,\mathrm{cm^4}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
C = 897.33\,\mathrm{cm^4},
\qquad
D = 432\,\mathrm{cm^4},
\qquad
\sqrt{432^2 + 576^2} = 720\,\mathrm{cm^4}
$$
$$
I_1 = 897.33 + 720 = 1617.33\,\mathrm{cm^4},
\qquad
I_2 = 897.33 - 720 = 177.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}
$$
$$
\tan 2\alpha_0 = -\frac{2 \cdot 576}{864} = -\frac{4}{3},
\qquad
2\alpha_0 = -53.13^\circ,
\qquad
\alpha_0 = -26.57^\circ
$$
$$
I_\xi(\alpha_0) = 897.33 + 432 \cdot \frac{3}{5} - 576 \cdot \left( -\frac{4}{5} \right) = 897.33 + 259.2 + 460.8 = 1617.33\,\mathrm{cm^4} = I_1
$$
$$
\text{check:}\quad
I_1 + I_2 = 1794.67 = I_{x_G} + I_{y_G},
\qquad
I_1 I_2 = 286\,807.1 = 1329.33 \cdot 465.33 - 576^2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The section is symmetrical with respect to the center of the web, so the centroid is that center and the axes in the figure are centroidal. excluding the portions shared with the flanges, the web has height $10\,\mathrm{cm}$; flanges are rectangles of $8 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ with centers at $(3, 6)\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $(-3, -6)\,\mathrm{cm}$. With the **parallel-axis theorem** you find the centroidal moments; the product of inertia is positive because the flanges are in the first and third quadrants.

The theorem on **direction of the principal axes and principal moments** gives $2\alpha_0 = -53.13^\circ$, with $\cos 2\alpha_0 = \dfrac{3}{5}$ and $\sin 2\alpha_0 = -\dfrac{4}{5}$. The rotation formula with this angle returns $I_1$, so the maximum moment axis is rotated by $26.57^\circ$ in clockwise about $x_G$, and the minimum axis is perpendicular to it. Verification controls the sum and product of the principal moments. The principal axes are rotated by $\alpha_0 = -26.57^\circ$, with $I_1 = 1617.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_2 = 177.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The isosceles right triangle triangle in the figure has the legs of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. Determine the axes and principal moments centroidal, the main radii of gyration and the centroidal inertia ellipse.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 0,6" frecce="0,0,7.5,0,x;0,0,0,7.5,y" quote="0 0,6 0,6 cm,0.7;0 0,0 6,6 cm,-0.7" riferimento=0 titolo="Isosceles right triangle with legs of length $6\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

$$
A = \frac{6 \cdot 6}{2} = 18\,\mathrm{cm^2},
\qquad
G = (2, 2)\,\mathrm{cm}
$$
$$
I_{x_G} = I_{y_G} = \frac{6 \cdot 6^3}{36} = 36\,\mathrm{cm^4},
\qquad
I_{x_G y_G} = -\frac{6^2 \cdot 6^2}{72} = -18\,\mathrm{cm^4}
$$
$$
\alpha_0 = 45^\circ,
\qquad
I_\xi = 36 - (-18) \cdot \sin 90^\circ = 54\,\mathrm{cm^4} = I_1,
\qquad
I_\eta = 36 + (-18) \cdot \sin 90^\circ = 18\,\mathrm{cm^4} = I_2
$$
$$
\rho_\xi = \sqrt{\frac{54}{18}}\,\mathrm{cm} = \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{cm} = 1.73\,\mathrm{cm},
\qquad
\rho_\eta = \sqrt{\frac{18}{18}}\,\mathrm{cm} = 1\,\mathrm{cm}
$$
$$
\frac{\xi^2}{\rho_\eta^2} + \frac{\eta^2}{\rho_\xi^2} = 1
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\xi^2 + \frac{\eta^2}{3} = 1 \quad (\mathrm{cm^2})
$$

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 0,6" tratteggi="2.71,2.71 2.54,2.86 2.37,3 2.18,3.12 2,3.22 1.82,3.31 1.63,3.37 1.46,3.4 1.29,3.41 1.14,3.4 1,3.37 0.88,3.31 0.78,3.22 0.69,3.12 0.63,3 0.6,2.86 0.59,2.71 0.6,2.54 0.63,2.37 0.69,2.18 0.78,2 0.88,1.82 1,1.63 1.14,1.46 1.29,1.29 1.46,1.14 1.63,1 1.82,0.88 2,0.78 2.18,0.69 2.37,0.63 2.54,0.6 2.71,0.59 2.86,0.6 3,0.63 3.12,0.69 3.22,0.78 3.31,0.88 3.37,1 3.4,1.14 3.41,1.29 3.4,1.46 3.37,1.63 3.31,1.82 3.22,2 3.12,2.18 3,2.37 2.86,2.54 2.71,2.71;0,0 2,2;2,2 4,0" frecce="2,2,4.6,4.6,ξ;2,2,-0.2,4.2,η" punti="2,2,G,baricentro" riferimento=0 titolo="Principal axes $\xi$ and $\eta$ and the centroidal inertia ellipse, with semiaxis $\rho_\eta = 1\,\mathrm{cm}$ along $\xi$ and semiaxis $\rho_\xi = \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{cm}$ along $\eta$"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{check:}\quad
I_\eta &= \frac{6\sqrt{2} \cdot (3\sqrt{2})^3}{36} = \frac{6 \cdot 54 \cdot 2}{36} = 18\,\mathrm{cm^4} , \\[6pt]
I_\xi &= 2 \cdot \frac{3\sqrt{2} \cdot (3\sqrt{2})^3}{12} = 2 \cdot \frac{324}{12} = 54\,\mathrm{cm^4}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The centroid of the right triangle with the legs on the axes is $\left( \dfrac{b}{3}, \dfrac{h}{3} \right) = (2, 2)\,\mathrm{cm}$. Moments about the centroidal axes parallel to the legs and the product of inertia, with the right angle at the bottom left, are read between the **second moments of area of rectangle, triangle, circle and crown**. Since $I_{x_G} = I_{y_G}$ and $I_{x_G y_G} \neq 0$, the principal axes are bisectors, $\alpha_0 = 45^\circ$; the rotation formula with $2\alpha_0 = 90^\circ$ gives $I_\xi = 54\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, so the axis to $45^\circ$, which passes through the vertex of the right angle, is the axis of the maximum.

That axis is the axis of symmetry of the isosceles triangle, so it is also principal for the **product of inertia and symmetry** theorem. The check calculates the two moments directly. About the axis $\eta$, parallel to the hypotenuse, the triangle has base $6\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $3\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$, and $\dfrac{b h^3}{36}$ is used. About the axis $\xi$, which is the height relative to the hypotenuse, the triangle is divided into two rectangular triangles with a leg of $3\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$ on the axis and the other of $3\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$ perpendicular, and for each equals $\dfrac{b h^3}{12}$.

The main **radii of gyration** are $\rho_\xi = \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $\rho_\eta = 1\,\mathrm{cm}$. The **centroidal inertia ellipse** has the $\rho_\eta = 1\,\mathrm{cm}$ semi-axis along $\xi$ and the $\rho_\xi = 1.73\,\mathrm{cm}$ semi-axis along $\eta$: it is stretched parallel to the hypotenuse, in the direction in which the area of the triangle is most distributed.

</div>
