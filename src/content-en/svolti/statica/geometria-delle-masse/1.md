## Exercise 1

Find the centroid of the T-section shown. Its web measures $2 \times 12\,\mathrm{cm}$, and its flange measures $12 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,0 1,0 1,12 6,12 6,14 -6,14 -6,12 -1,12" frecce="-7.5,0,7.5,0,x;0,-0.8,0,15.4,y" quote="-6 14,6 14,12 cm,-2.2;-1 0,1 0,2 cm,1.2;6 0,6 14,14 cm,1.4;-6 12,-6 14,2 cm,-0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="T-section with web dimensions $2 \times 12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and flange dimensions $12 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

$$
x_G = 0
$$
$$
A_1 = 2 \cdot 12 = 24\,\mathrm{cm^2},\quad y_1 = 6\,\mathrm{cm};
\qquad
A_2 = 12 \cdot 2 = 24\,\mathrm{cm^2},\quad y_2 = 12 + 1 = 13\,\mathrm{cm}
$$
$$
y_G = \frac{A_1 y_1 + A_2 y_2}{A_1 + A_2} = \frac{24 \cdot 6 + 24 \cdot 13}{48}\,\mathrm{cm} = \frac{456}{48}\,\mathrm{cm} = 9.5\,\mathrm{cm}
$$
$$
\text{check:}\quad 24 \cdot (6 - 9.5) + 24 \cdot (13 - 9.5) = -84 + 84 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Axis $y$ is an axis of symmetry. By the theorem on the **centroid of a symmetric area**, the centroid lies on this axis and $x_G = 0$. To find $y_G$, take the bottom edge as the $x$ axis and measure heights from it. Split the section into web $A_1$, extending from $0$ to $12\,\mathrm{cm}$, and flange $A_2$, extending from $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ to $14\,\mathrm{cm}$. Their centroids are the centers of the two rectangles. The **composite-area centroid** formula gives $y_G = 9.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The parts have equal areas, so the section centroid is halfway between their centroids, at the mean of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $13\,\mathrm{cm}$.

Check the result by taking the first moment of area about the calculated centroidal axis. Multiply each area by the signed distance of its centroid from that axis and add. The sum is zero, as required about any centroidal axis. The section centroid is $G = (0;\ 9.5)\,\mathrm{cm}$, a distance $4.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ below the top edge.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Find the centroid of the L-section shown. Its horizontal leg measures $14 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$, and its vertical leg has overall height $16\,\mathrm{cm}$ and thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 14,0 14,2 2,2 2,16 0,16" frecce="0,0,16,0,x;0,0,0,18,y" quote="0 0,14 0,14 cm,1;0 0,0 16,16 cm,-1;14 0,14 2,2 cm,0.9;0 16,2 16,2 cm,-0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="L-section with leg lengths $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $16\,\mathrm{cm}$ and thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

$$
A_1 = 14 \cdot 2 = 28\,\mathrm{cm^2},\quad (x_1, y_1) = (7, 1)\,\mathrm{cm};
\qquad
A_2 = 2 \cdot 14 = 28\,\mathrm{cm^2},\quad (x_2, y_2) = (1, 9)\,\mathrm{cm}
$$
$$
x_G = \frac{28 \cdot 7 + 28 \cdot 1}{56}\,\mathrm{cm} = \frac{224}{56}\,\mathrm{cm} = 4\,\mathrm{cm},
\qquad
y_G = \frac{28 \cdot 1 + 28 \cdot 9}{56}\,\mathrm{cm} = \frac{280}{56}\,\mathrm{cm} = 5\,\mathrm{cm}
$$
$$
\text{check:}\quad
A_1' = 2 \cdot 16 = 32\,\mathrm{cm^2},\ (1, 8);
\quad
A_2' = 12 \cdot 2 = 24\,\mathrm{cm^2},\ (8, 1);
\quad
x_G = \frac{32 \cdot 1 + 24 \cdot 8}{56} = 4\,\mathrm{cm},
\quad
y_G = \frac{32 \cdot 8 + 24 \cdot 1}{56} = 5\,\mathrm{cm}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The section has no axis of symmetry, so both centroid coordinates are needed. Take axes $x$ and $y$ along the outer edges of the two legs so that all coordinates are positive. Use the entire horizontal leg $A_1$, extending over $0 \leq x \leq 14\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $0 \leq y \leq 2\,\mathrm{cm}$, and the part of the vertical leg above it, $A_2$, with height $16 - 2 = 14\,\mathrm{cm}$ and center at elevation $2 + 7 = 9\,\mathrm{cm}$. The formula for the **centroid of a composite area** gives $G = (4, 5)\,\mathrm{cm}$.

Check the result using a different subdivision. Take the full vertical leg of height $16\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the part of the horizontal leg to its right, of length $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. This gives the same result. The centroid $G = (4, 5)\,\mathrm{cm}$ lies outside the material, in the angle between the two legs.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Find the centroid of the asymmetric I-section shown. Its bottom flange measures $14 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$, its web $2 \times 16\,\mathrm{cm}$, and its top flange $6 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$.

::struttura{poligoni="-7,0 7,0 7,2 1,2 1,18 3,18 3,20 -3,20 -3,18 -1,18 -1,2 -7,2" frecce="-8.5,0,8.5,0,x;0,-0.8,0,21.3,y" quote="-7 0,7 0,14 cm,1.1;-3 20,3 20,6 cm,-2.2;7 0,7 20,20 cm,1.4;1 2,1 18,16 cm,2.6;-7 0,-7 2,2 cm,-0.9;-3 18,-3 20,2 cm,-0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="Asymmetric I-section of height $20\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

$$
x_G = 0
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_1 &= 14 \cdot 2 = 28\,\mathrm{cm^2}, & y_1 &= 1\,\mathrm{cm} \\[4pt]
A_2 &= 2 \cdot 16 = 32\,\mathrm{cm^2}, & y_2 &= 2 + 8 = 10\,\mathrm{cm} \\[4pt]
A_3 &= 6 \cdot 2 = 12\,\mathrm{cm^2}, & y_3 &= 18 + 1 = 19\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
y_G = \frac{28 \cdot 1 + 32 \cdot 10 + 12 \cdot 19}{28 + 32 + 12}\,\mathrm{cm} = \frac{28 + 320 + 228}{72}\,\mathrm{cm} = \frac{576}{72}\,\mathrm{cm} = 8\,\mathrm{cm}
$$
$$
\text{check:}\quad 28 \cdot (1 - 8) + 32 \cdot (10 - 8) + 12 \cdot (19 - 8) = -196 + 64 + 132 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The vertical axis passing through the center of the web is an axis of symmetry, so $x_G = 0$ by the theorem on **centroid of a symmetrical figure**. Heights are measured from the bottom edge. The section is divided into the bottom flanges, web and top flanges; the web goes from elevation $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ to elevation $18\,\mathrm{cm}$, so its centroid is at $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. From the formula of **centroid of a composite figure** you get $y_G = 8\,\mathrm{cm}$.

The centroid is below half the height, which is at $10\,\mathrm{cm}$, because the bottom flange has an area more than twice that of the upper one. The sum of the first moments of area of the parts about the centroidal axis is zero, as the theorem on the first moment of area about a centroidal axis requires. The centroid is $G = (0, 8)\,\mathrm{cm}$.

</div>
