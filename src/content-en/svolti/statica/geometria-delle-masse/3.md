## Exercise 1

Calculate the centroidal second moments of area $I_{x_G}$ and $I_{y_G}$ of the T-section shown. Its web measures $2 \times 12\,\mathrm{cm}$, and its flange measures $12 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,0 1,0 1,12 6,12 6,14 -6,14 -6,12 -1,12" frecce="-7.5,0,7.5,0,x;0,-0.8,0,15.4,y" quote="-6 14,6 14,12 cm,-2.2;-1 0,1 0,2 cm,1.2;6 0,6 14,14 cm,1.4;-6 12,-6 14,2 cm,-0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="T-section with web dimensions $2 \times 12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and flange dimensions $12 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

$$
y_G = \frac{24 \cdot 6 + 24 \cdot 13}{48}\,\mathrm{cm} = 9.5\,\mathrm{cm},
\qquad
d_1 = 6 - 9.5 = -3.5\,\mathrm{cm},
\qquad
d_2 = 13 - 9.5 = 3.5\,\mathrm{cm}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
I_{x_G} &= \left( \frac{2 \cdot 12^3}{12} + 24 \cdot 3.5^2 \right) + \left( \frac{12 \cdot 2^3}{12} + 24 \cdot 3.5^2 \right) \\[6pt]
&= (288 + 294) + (8 + 294) = 884\,\mathrm{cm^4}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
I_{y_G} = \frac{12 \cdot 2^3}{12} + \frac{2 \cdot 12^3}{12} = 8 + 288 = 296\,\mathrm{cm^4}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{check:}\quad I_{\text{base}} &= \frac{2 \cdot 12^3}{3} + \left( \frac{12 \cdot 2^3}{12} + 24 \cdot 13^2 \right) = 1152 + 4064 = 5216\,\mathrm{cm^4} , \\[6pt]
I_{x_G} + A\,y_G^2 &= 884 + 48 \cdot 9.5^2 = 884 + 4332 = 5216\,\mathrm{cm^4}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Follow the **Procedure for the Compound Sections**. The axis $y$ is symmetry axis, therefore $x_G = 0$, and the centroid's elevation, measured from the bottom edge, is $9.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The web ($A_1 = 24\,\mathrm{cm^2}$) has the centroid $3.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ below the axis $x_G$ and the $3.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ flange ($A_2 = 24\,\mathrm{cm^2}$) above. For each rectangle the second moment of area about its horizontal centroidal axis is $\dfrac{b h^3}{12}$, with $h$ the vertical dimension: $2 \cdot 12^3$ for the web, $12 \cdot 2^3$ for the flange. The **parallel-axis theorem** adds for each part the area for the square of the distance from the axis $x_G$.

The axis $y_G$ coincides with the axis $y$ and passes through the centroids of both rectangles, so in $I_{y_G}$ there are no parallel-axis terms; now the dimension perpendicular to the axis is horizontal. The check calculates the second moment of area about the bottom edge directly, with $\dfrac{b h^3}{3}$ for the web, which has the base on the axis, and with the transport of the flange, and compares it with that obtained by carrying $I_{x_G}$: the two values coincide. The second moments of area are $I_{x_G} = 884\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{y_G} = 296\,\mathrm{cm^4}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Find $I_{x_G}$ and $I_{y_G}$ for the I-section shown. It has overall height $20\,\mathrm{cm}$, flanges of dimensions $10 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$, and web thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$.

::struttura{poligoni="-5,0 5,0 5,2 1,2 1,18 5,18 5,20 -5,20 -5,18 -1,18 -1,2 -5,2" frecce="-7,10,6.6,10,x_G;0,-1,0,21.3,y_G" quote="-5 0,5 0,10 cm,1.1;5 0,5 20,20 cm,4.5;-1 6,1 6,2 cm,-0.8;-5 18,-5 20,2 cm,-0.9;-5 0,-5 2,2 cm,-0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="Symmetric I-section of height $20\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_{x_G} &= 2\left( \frac{10 \cdot 2^3}{12} + 20 \cdot 9^2 \right) + \frac{2 \cdot 16^3}{12} \\[6pt]
&= 2\,(6.67 + 1620) + 682.67 = 3936\,\mathrm{cm^4}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
I_{y_G} = 2 \cdot \frac{2 \cdot 10^3}{12} + \frac{16 \cdot 2^3}{12} = 333.33 + 10.67 = 344\,\mathrm{cm^4}
$$
$$
\text{check:}\quad I_{x_G} = \frac{10 \cdot 20^3}{12} - 2 \cdot \frac{4 \cdot 16^3}{12} = 6666.67 - 2730.67 = 3936\,\mathrm{cm^4}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The section has two axes of symmetry, so the centroid is the center of the section, at $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the bottom edge, and the centroidal axes are the axes of symmetry. Flanges have area $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ each and centroid to $10 - 1 = 9\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis $x_G$; the web is $20 - 2 \cdot 2 = 16\,\mathrm{cm}$ high and has centroid on the axis. With the **parallel-axis theorem** lies $I_{x_G} = 3936\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, and the parallel-axis terms of the flanges, $1620\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ each, are the major part of it: it is the reason why the steel beams have this shape.

About $y_G$ all three parts have the centroid on the axis, so $I_{y_G}$ is the sum of the proper moments, much smaller. The check uses the section as the solid rectangle of $10 \times 20\,\mathrm{cm}$ minus the two empty rectangles of $4 \times 16\,\mathrm{cm}$ at the sides of the web, which have the centroid on the axis $x_G$. The second moments of area are $I_{x_G} = 3936\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{y_G} = 344\,\mathrm{cm^4}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Find the centroid and centroidal second moments of area of the channel section shown. The section is $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ high, its web is $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ thick, and its flanges have overall width $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 8,0 8,2 2,2 2,10 8,10 8,12 0,12" frecce="0,0,10,0,x;0,0,0,14,y" quote="0 0,8 0,8 cm,1;0 0,0 12,12 cm,-1;0 12,2 12,2 cm,-0.9;8 0,8 2,2 cm,0.9;8 10,8 12,2 cm,0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="Channel section of height $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ with web and flange thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

$$
y_G = 6\,\mathrm{cm},
\qquad
x_G = \frac{24 \cdot 1 + 2 \cdot 12 \cdot 5}{48}\,\mathrm{cm} = \frac{144}{48}\,\mathrm{cm} = 3\,\mathrm{cm}
$$
$$
I_{x_G} = \frac{2 \cdot 12^3}{12} + 2\left( \frac{6 \cdot 2^3}{12} + 12 \cdot 5^2 \right) = 288 + 2\,(4 + 300) = 896\,\mathrm{cm^4}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
I_{y_G} &= \left( \frac{12 \cdot 2^3}{12} + 24 \cdot (1 - 3)^2 \right) + 2\left( \frac{2 \cdot 6^3}{12} + 12 \cdot (5 - 3)^2 \right) \\[6pt]
&= (8 + 96) + 2\,(36 + 48) = 272\,\mathrm{cm^4}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{check:}\quad I_{x_G} &= \frac{8 \cdot 12^3}{12} - \frac{6 \cdot 8^3}{12} = 1152 - 256 = 896\,\mathrm{cm^4} , \\[6pt]
I_y &= \frac{12 \cdot 2^3}{3} + 2 \int_2^8 x^2 \cdot 2\,dx = 32 + 672 = 704\,\mathrm{cm^4} = I_{y_G} + A\,x_G^2 = 272 + 48 \cdot 9
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The horizontal line at elevation $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ is an axis of symmetry, so $y_G = 6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and remains to be found $x_G$, measured from the outer edge of the web. The whole web ($2 \times 12\,\mathrm{cm}$, area $24\,\mathrm{cm^2}$) has the centroid at $x = 1\,\mathrm{cm}$; each flange, excluding the part already counted in the web, is a rectangle of $6 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ with the centroid at $x = 2 + 3 = 5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The **centroid of a composite figure** is then at $x_G = 3\,\mathrm{cm}$, out of the web, towards the flanges.

For $I_{x_G}$ the web has the centroid on the axis, while the flanges are distant $6 - 1 = 5\,\mathrm{cm}$. For $I_{y_G}$ all parts are transported with the **parallel-axis theorem**: the web is $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis $y_G$ and the flanges equally, on the other side. In $I_{x_G}$ verification, the difference between the solid rectangle of $8 \times 12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the void of $6 \times 8\,\mathrm{cm}$ is obtained, centered on the axis; $I_y$ with respect to the outer edge of the web is calculated directly, with the vertical strips of the flanges, and coincides with $I_{y_G} + A\,x_G^2$. The results are $G = (3, 6)\,\mathrm{cm}$, $I_{x_G} = 896\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{y_G} = 272\,\mathrm{cm^4}$.

</div>
