## Exercise 1

The three $AC$, $BC$ and $DC$ rods in the figure are hinged to the ground at $A$, $B$ and $D$ and connected to each other in the $C$ node by a single hinge. Calculate the degree of kinematic instability and the degree of static indeterminacy.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,3,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-C;D-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" quote="A,D,3 m,0.9;D,B,3 m,0.9;B,6 4,4 m,0.6" tratteggi="3,4 6,4"}

$$
n = 3 ,
\qquad
s = 3 \cdot 2 + 2\,(3 - 1) = 10 ,
\qquad
3n - s = -1
$$
$$
\text{bodies } AC \text{ and } BC : \quad C_1 = A ,
\quad
C_2 = B ,
\quad
C_{12} = C ,
\qquad
A ,\ B ,\ C \ \text{noncollinear}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{stationary}
$$
$$
\text{body } DC : \quad C_3 = D ,
\quad
C_3 = C
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{stationary}
$$
$$
l = 0 ,
\qquad
i = l - (3n - s) = 1
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad
\begin{cases}
-4\varphi_1 = -4\varphi_2 \\
3\varphi_1 = -3\varphi_2 \\
-4\varphi_1 = -4\varphi_3 \\
3\varphi_1 = 0
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi_1 = \varphi_2 = \varphi_3 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Each rod is a body, so $3n = 9$. The three pin supports equal $2$ scalar constraints each. the pin at $C$ connects three bodies and, by the theorem on **hinge connecting multiple bodies**, it equals $2\,(3 - 1) = 4$ scalar constraints: it requires that the displacement of $C$ in member $BC$ and that in member $DC$ are equal to that in member $AC$. In all $s = 10$ and $3n - s = -1$, so the structure has at least one excess constraint.

To find $l$ we first consider the $AC$ and $BC$ rods, which with the $A$, $B$ and $C$ hinges form a three-hinged arch; the three points are not aligned, so the two rods are stationary. The $C$ node is then stationary, and member $DC$ has two distinct centers, $D$ and $C$; So $DC$ is also stationary. You have $l = 0$ and $i = 1$, so the structure is **statically indeterminate to degree one**, because two non-aligned rods would be enough to fix the $C$ node.

In the check the displacements of $C$ calculated in the three rods, which rotates about $A$, $B = (6, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $D = (3, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ are equal: in member $AC$ the displacement is $\varphi_1\,(-4, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$, in member $BC$ is $\varphi_2\,(-4, -3)\,\mathrm{m}$ and in member $DC$ is $\varphi_3\,(-4, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$. The four equations are the four conditions of the pin at $C$, and the only solution is the null one.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The roof truss shown consists of struts $AC$ and $CB$ and tie $AB$, pinned together at all three vertices. At $A$ the hinge joining the $AC$ strut and tie is fixed to the ground; at $B$ the hinge that joins the $CB$ strut and the tie rests on a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. Determine whether the roof truss is statically determinate.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,3;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B;A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" cerniere="C" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9;B,6 3,3 m,0.6" tratteggi="3,3 6,3"}

$$
n = 3 ,
\qquad
s = \underbrace{2 \cdot 2}_{A} + \underbrace{2}_{C} + \underbrace{2 + 1}_{B} = 9 ,
\qquad
3n - s = 0
$$
$$
\text{body } 3 \ (AB) : \quad C_3 = A ,
\quad
C_3 \in r_B : \ x = 6\,\mathrm{m}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{stationary}
$$
$$
\text{bodies } 1 \ (AC) \text{ and } 2 \ (CB) : \quad C_1 = A ,
\quad
C_2 = B ,
\quad
C_{12} = C ,
\qquad
\text{noncollinear}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{stationary}
$$
$$
l = 0 ,
\qquad
i = 0
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad
\begin{cases}
-3\varphi_1 = -3\varphi_2 \\
3\varphi_1 = (3 - 6)\,\varphi_2
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi_1 = \varphi_2 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The pin support $A$ connects to the ground two bodies, the strut $AC$ and the chain, and by the theorem on the **hinge connecting multiple bodies** equals $2 \cdot 2 = 4$ scalar constraints. At $B$ there is an internal hinge between the $CB$ strut and the tie, which equals $2$, and the roller support, which equals $1$ and is attributed to the tie; the hinge $C$ between the two dots equals $2$. In all $s = 9 = 3n$.

The tie has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ with the vertical reaction, which does not pass through $A$, so it is stationary. The point $B$ is then stationary, and the two struts, hinged at $A$ and $B$ and between them at $C$, form a three-hinged arch with the non-collinear hinges: they too are stationary. The roof truss is **statically determinate**. The same result reads: the triangle of three hinged rods is a rigid body, bound to the ground by a well-arranged hinge and roller support.

In verification the tie is already stationary, so the reference points of the struts, $A$ and $B$, have zero displacement. The two equations equal the horizontal and vertical displacements of $C = (3, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$ calculated in the two struts: $u_C = -3\varphi_1$ and $v_C = 3\varphi_1$ in the strut $AC$, $u_C = -3\varphi_2$ and $v_C = (3 - 6)\,\varphi_2$ in the strut $CB$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The frame in the figure consists of three bodies: the column $AD$ with the crossbeam $DC$, the crossbeam $CE$ with the column $EB$ and the $FC$ central pillar. The three bodies are connected in the $C$ node by a single hinge. At $A$ and $B$ there are two pin supports and at $F$ a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. Determine whether the frame is statically determinate.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;C,4,4;E,8,4;B,8,0;F,4,0" aste="A-D;D-C;C-E;E-B;F-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera;F,carrello" cerniere="C" quote="A,F,4 m,0.9;F,B,4 m,0.9;B,E,4 m"}

$$
n = 3 ,
\qquad
s = 2 + 2 + 2\,(3 - 1) + 1 = 9 ,
\qquad
3n - s = 0
$$
$$
\text{bodies } 1 \text{ and } 2 : \quad A ,\ B ,\ C \ \text{noncollinear}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{stationary} ,
\qquad
C \ \text{stationary}
$$
$$
\text{body } 3 \ (FC) : \quad C_3 = C = (4, 4)\,\mathrm{m} ,
\quad
C_3 \in r_F : \ x = 4\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
C \in r_F
$$
$$
l = 1 ,
\qquad
i = 1
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad
u_F = -\varphi_3\,(0 - 4\,\mathrm{m}) = 4\varphi_3\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
v_F = \varphi_3\,(4 - 4)\,\mathrm{m} = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The restraint count gives $s = 9 = 3n$. The pin at $C$ connects three bodies and supplies $4$ simple restraints. The two outer bodies form a three-hinged arch with noncollinear hinges $A$, $B$ and $C$, so they hold joint $C$ fixed. For column $FC$, hinge $C$ therefore fixes the center of rotation at $C$. The roller at $F$ requires the center to lie on the vertical through $F$. This line coincides with the column axis and passes through $C$. The conditions are compatible, so the column can rotate about $C$ while its base $F$ moves horizontally along the roller track. The frame has **both a mechanism and a redundant restraint**, with $l = i = 1$. The roller reaction passes through the pin and cannot prevent this rotation.

The excess constraint is also recognized from the static point of view. The vertical reaction of the roller support at $F$ and the equal and opposite force that the hinge $C$ transmits to the pillar, directed along its axis, are in equilibrium without loads. On the two outer bodies the force at $C$ is balanced by the reactions of the $A$ and $B$ hinges, and all these forces form the reaction system that gives $i = 1$. With a roller moving on a vertical plane at $F$ the reaction would be horizontal, it would not pass through $C$ and the frame would be statically determinate.

In verifying the displacement of $F$ in the rotation of the pillar around $C$ is horizontal, so roller support, which only prevents vertical displacement, allows it for every value of $\varphi_3$.

</div>
