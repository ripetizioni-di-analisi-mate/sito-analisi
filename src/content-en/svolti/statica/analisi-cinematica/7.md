## Exercise 1

The beam shown, of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$ whose support surface is inclined of the angle $\alpha$ on the horizontal, so the reaction of the roller support forms the angle $\alpha$ with the vertical. Figure $\alpha = 30^\circ$. Write the kinematic matrix as a function of $\alpha$ and determine for which values of $\alpha$, between $0^\circ$ and $90^\circ$, the beam is unstable.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,240" tratteggi="5,0 5,2;5,0 6,1.732" etichette="5.4,1.55,α" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9"}

$$
\hat n_B = (\sin\alpha,\ \cos\alpha) ,
\qquad
u_B = u_A ,
\qquad
v_B = v_A + 5\varphi
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
u_A = 0 \\
v_A = 0 \\
\sin\alpha\,u_A + \cos\alpha\,(v_A + 5\varphi) = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ \sin\alpha & \cos\alpha & 5\cos\alpha \end{pmatrix}
$$
$$
\det A = 5\cos\alpha
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\det A = 0 \iff \alpha = 90^\circ
$$
$$
\alpha = 90^\circ : \quad
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} ,
\qquad
r = 2 ,
\qquad
l = i = 1
$$
$$
\text{check for } \alpha = 30^\circ : \quad
\det A = 5 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \approx 4.33 \neq 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

You choose the reference point at $A$, so the parameters are $u_A$, $v_A$ and $\varphi$. The hinge gives the first two lines. The roller support requires that the displacement of $B$ in the direction of the reaction is zero; the reaction makes an angle $\alpha$ with the vertical, so its unit vector is $(\sin\alpha, \cos\alpha)$. The displacement of $B$ has components $u_A$ and $v_A + 5\varphi$ because $B$ is a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of the reference point. The determinant is calculated by developing along the first line and equals $5\cos\alpha$. The beam is **statically determinate** for $0^\circ \le \alpha < 90^\circ$ and is unstable for $\alpha = 90^\circ$, when the support surface is vertical.

The result coincides with that of the body theorem with three scalar constraints: for $\alpha = 90^\circ$ the reaction of the roller support is horizontal and its line of action, the axis of the beam, passes through the hinge $A$. In that case the third line coincides with the first and the rank is $r = 2$, so $l = 3n - r = 1$ and $i = s - r = 1$.

The check calculates the determinant for the angle of the figure: it is different from zero, so the drawn beam is statically determinate.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The beam shown, of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, is supported by a two-force link at $A$, with the tilted axis of $45^\circ$ descending to the left, by a two-force link at $B$, with the tilted axis of $45^\circ$ descending to the right, and by a roller support at $D$, at a distance $a$ from $A$, moving on a horizontal plane. Write the kinematic matrix and find the value $a$ for which the beam is unstable.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-D;D-B" vincoli="A,pendolo,225;B,pendolo,315;D,carrello" quote="A,D,a,0.9;A,B,8 m,1.6"}

$$
\begin{cases}
u_A + v_A = 0 \\
-u_A + (v_A + 8\varphi) = 0 \\
v_A + a\,\varphi = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ -1 & 1 & 8 \\ 0 & 1 & a \end{pmatrix}
$$
$$
\det A = 1 \cdot (a - 8) - 1 \cdot (-a - 0) + 0 = 2a - 8
$$
$$
\det A = 0 \iff a = 4\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad
r_A \cap r_B = (4, 4)\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
r_D : \ x = a
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
(4, 4)\,\mathrm{m} \in r_D \iff a = 4\,\mathrm{m}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

With the reference point at $A$, the two-force link at $A$ requires that the displacement of $A$ along the axis, direction $(1, 1)$, is zero, i.e. $u_A + v_A = 0$. The two-force link at $B$ has the $(-1, 1)$ directional axis and imposes $-u_B + v_B = 0$, with $u_B = u_A$ and $v_B = v_A + 8\varphi$. Roller support imposes $v_D = v_A + a\varphi = 0$. The lines are written without the $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ factor of the unit vectors, which does not change the rank. The determinant, developed along the first line, is $2a - 8$ and cancels for $a = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, with the roller support at the midpoint. For every other position of the roller support the beam is **statically determinate**; for $a = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ is unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$.

Verification uses the body theorem with three scalar constraints. The axes of the two two-force links meet in $(4, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$, and the vertical for the roller support only passes through this point if $a = 4\,\mathrm{m}$: is the same condition found with the determinant.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The three-hinged arch in the figure is formed by the straight $AC$ and $CB$ rods, connected by the internal hinge $C$, and has the pin supports at $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (8, 2)\,\mathrm{m}$. The hinge $C$ with horizontal coordinate $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $h$ elevation. Write the constraints conditions, reduce them to a system in the rotations and find the value of $h$ for which the arch is unstable.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,3;B,8,2" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" tratteggi="4,0 4,3;0,0 8,0" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.9;4 0,8 0,4 m,0.9;4 0,C,h,0.5;8 0,B,2 m,0.6"}

$$
u_1 = v_1 = 0 ,
\qquad
u_2 = v_2 = 0
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
-\varphi_1\,h = -\varphi_2\,(h - 2) \\
\varphi_1 \cdot 4 = \varphi_2\,(4 - 8)
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\begin{pmatrix} -h & h - 2 \\ 4 & 4 \end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix} \varphi_1 \\ \varphi_2 \end{pmatrix}
= \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}
$$
$$
\det = -4h - 4\,(h - 2) = 8 - 8h
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\det = 0 \iff h = 1\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad
(x_C - x_A)(y_B - y_A) - (y_C - y_A)(x_B - x_A) = 4 \cdot 2 - h \cdot 8 = 8 - 8h
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

You choose the reference points in the pin supports, $A$ for the body $1$ and $B$ for the body $2$; the pin supports cancel the displacements of the reference points, and the six conditions remain two, those of the internal hinge. The horizontal displacement of $C$ is $-\varphi_1\,(y_C - y_A) = -\varphi_1 h$ in the body $1$ and $-\varphi_2\,(y_C - y_B) = -\varphi_2\,(h - 2)$ in the body $2$; The vertical one equals $\varphi_1\,(x_C - x_A) = 4\varphi_1$ and $\varphi_2\,(x_C - x_B) = -4\varphi_2$. The homogeneous system in rotations has non-zero solutions if and only if the determinant is zero, i.e. for $h = 1\,\mathrm{m}$. For $h = 1\,\mathrm{m}$ the arch is unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$; for every other value is **statically determinate**, and particularly so is the arch of the figure, with $h = 3\,\mathrm{m}$.

In the check, the alignment of the three hinges is checked, as required by the three-hinged arch theorem. The expression is the same as the determinant, so the two conditions coincide: for $h = 1\,\mathrm{m}$ the hinge $C$ lies on the segment $AB$, in half.

</div>
