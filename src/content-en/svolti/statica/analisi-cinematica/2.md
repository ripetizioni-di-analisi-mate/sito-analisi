## Exercise 1

The beam shown has length $8\,\mathrm{m}$ and rests on three rollers on horizontal surfaces at $A$, $B$, and $C$. Find its degrees of kinematic instability and static indeterminacy and describe its possible motions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello;C,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m"}

$$
n = 1 ,
\qquad
s = 3 ,
\qquad
3n - s = 0
$$
$$
r_A : \ x = 0 ,
\qquad
r_B : \ x = 4\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
r_C : \ x = 8\,\mathrm{m}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
C_1 = Y_\infty
$$
$$
l = 1 ,
\qquad
i = l - (3n - s) = 1
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad
A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 4 \\ 0 & 1 & 8 \end{pmatrix} ,
\qquad
\det A = 0 ,
\qquad
(u_A, v_A, \varphi) = (u, 0, 0) :
\quad
\begin{cases}
v_A = 0 \\
v_A + 4\varphi = 0 \\
v_A + 8\varphi = 0
\end{cases}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The count gives $s = 3n$, but the lines of action of the three reactions are vertical and therefore parallel: they have in common the point at infinity in the vertical direction, $Y_\infty$. For the body theorem with three scalar constraints the constraints are **ill-disposed**. The center $C_1 = Y_\infty$ corresponds to a horizontal translation, which none of the three roller supports prevent. The possible displacements depend only on the parameter $u$, then $l = 1$, and the relationship $3n - s = l - i$ gives $i = 1$. The beam is **unstable and statically indeterminate**: it moves horizontally and, for vertical loads, the three vertical reactions are not determined by the two equilibrium equations that contain them.

In verification the kinematic matrix, with the reference point at $A$, has the first column null, because no constraint involves $u_A$; the determinant is zero and the rank is $2$. The $(u, 0, 0)$ shift, a horizontal translation, meets all three conditions of roller supports.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The beam shown has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, a pin at $A$, and a roller at $B$ on a vertical surface. Is the beam statically determinate? If it is unstable, describe its motion.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" quote="A,B,5 m"}

$$
n = 1 ,
\qquad
s = 2 + 1 = 3 ,
\qquad
3n - s = 0
$$
$$
C_1 = A ,
\qquad
C_1 \in r_B : \ y = 0 ,
\qquad
A \in r_B
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
l = 1 ,
\qquad
i = 1
$$
$$
u_B = 0 ,
\qquad
v_B = 5\varphi
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} ,
\qquad
\det A = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The roller moves along a vertical surface, so its reaction is horizontal. Its line of action $r_B$ coincides with the beam axis and passes through pin $A$. The pin requires $C_1 = A$ and the roller requires $C_1$ to lie on $r_B$. These conditions are compatible because $A$ lies on $r_B$, so the beam can rotate about $A$. During this rotation $B$ moves vertically by $v_B = 5\varphi$, parallel to the roller guide, which therefore offers no resistance. The supports are **improperly arranged**, giving $l = 1$ and, by $3n - s = l - i$, $i = 1$. From the force balance, the two horizontal reactions $H_A$ and $R_B$ have the same line of action and the supports cannot balance a general vertical load.

In verification, with the reference point at $A$, the row of the roller support is $u_B = u_A - \varphi \cdot 0 = 0$, equal to the first line; the determinant is zero and the rank is $2$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The beam shown has length $8\,\mathrm{m}$ and is supported by a two-force link at $A$ sloping downward to the left at $45^\circ$ and another at $B$ sloping downward to the right at $45^\circ$. A roller at midpoint $D$ runs on a horizontal surface. Determine whether the beam is statically determinate. If it is unstable, locate its center of rotation.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,4,0;B,8,0" aste="A-D;D-B" vincoli="A,pendolo,225;B,pendolo,315;D,carrello" quote="A,D,4 m;D,B,4 m"}

$$
r_A : \ y = x ,
\qquad
r_B : \ y = 8\,\mathrm{m} - x ,
\qquad
r_D : \ x = 4\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
r_A \cap r_B = (4, 4)\,\mathrm{m} \in r_D
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
C_1 = (4, 4)\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
l = i = 1
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad
\det \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ -1 & 1 & 8 \\ 0 & 1 & 4 \end{pmatrix}
= 1 \cdot (4 - 8) - 1 \cdot (-4 - 0) + 0 = 0
$$
$$
u_A = 4\varphi ,
\quad
v_A = -4\varphi :
\qquad
u_A + v_A = 0 ,
\qquad
-u_A + (v_A + 8\varphi) = 0 ,
\qquad
v_A + 4\varphi = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The lines of action of the reactions of the two-force links are their axes: for $A$ the slope line $1$, for $B$ the slope line $-1$. The two lines meet at point $(4, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$, which lies on the vertical through $D$, line of action of the roller support reaction. The three lines are therefore **concurrent** and, by the body theorem with three scalar constraints, the beam is unstable: it can rotates about $C_1 = (4, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$. In this rotation each bound point moves perpendicular to the line that connects it with $C_1$, that is, perpendicular to the axis of its constraint. You have $l = 1$ and, since $s = 3n$, $i = 1$. Moving the roller support to a point other than the midpoint, the vertical for the roller support would no longer pass through $(4, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$ and the beam would become statically determinate.

In verification the reference point is at $A$. The two-force links require that $A$ and $B$ shifts along the $(1, 1)$ and $(-1, 1)$ directions are zero, with $u_B = u_A$ and $v_B = v_A + 8\varphi$; roller support imposes $v_D = v_A + 4\varphi = 0$. The determinant is zero. The rotation $\varphi$ around $(4, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$ shifts the $A$ reference point of $u_A = -\varphi\,(0 - 4) = 4\varphi$ and $v_A = \varphi\,(0 - 4) = -4\varphi$, and these values meet all three conditions.

</div>

## Exercise 4

The beam shown has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, a parallel-link support with horizontal links at $A$, and a roller at $B$ on a vertical surface. Classify the beam.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,180;B,carrello,0" quote="A,B,6 m"}

$$
n = 1 ,
\qquad
s = 2 + 1 = 3 ,
\qquad
3n - s = 0
$$
$$
\text{parallel-link support: } C_1 = X_\infty ,
\qquad
r_B : \ y = 0 ,
\qquad
X_\infty \in r_B
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
l = i = 1
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} ,
\qquad
\det A = 0 ,
\qquad
(u_A, v_A, \varphi) = (0, v, 0)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Parallel-link support with horizontal links prevents rotation and horizontal translation, so the beam can only move vertically and the center is the point at infinity in the horizontal direction $X_\infty$. The vertical-level roller support has the horizontal reaction, parallel to the pin-ended links, and its horizontal line of action contains $X_\infty$. The two conditions are compatible, so vertical translation is possible: this is the case of parallel-link support and roller support with the parallel reaction to pin-ended links, one of the cases of **Geometrically dependent constraints**. The beam is **unstable and statically indeterminate**, with $l = i = 1$: two scalar constraints prevent horizontal translation and one rotation, while none prevents vertical translation.

In verification, with the reference point at $A$, the row of the roller at $u_B = u_A = 0$ coincides with the first line, so the rank is $2$; $(0, v, 0)$ displacement meets the three conditions.

</div>
