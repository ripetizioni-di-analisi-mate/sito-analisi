## Exercise 1

The portal frame in the figure is formed by the body $1$, with the column $AD$ and the crossbeam section $DC$, and by the body $2$, with the crossbeam section $CE$ and the column $EB$. At $A$ and $B$ there are two pin supports and at $C$ an internal hinge. Determine whether the portal frame is statically determinate.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;C,3,4;E,6,4;B,6,0" aste="A-D;D-C;C-E;E-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" quote="D,C,3 m,-0.7;C,E,3 m,-0.7;B,E,4 m"}

$$
n = 2 ,
\qquad
s = 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 ,
\qquad
3n - s = 0
$$
$$
C_1 = A = (0, 0) ,
\qquad
C_2 = B = (6, 0)\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
C_{12} = C = (3, 4)\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
(x_C - x_A)(y_B - y_A) - (y_C - y_A)(x_B - x_A) = 3 \cdot 0 - 4 \cdot 6 = -24\,\mathrm{m^2} \neq 0
$$
$$
A ,\ B ,\ C \ \text{noncollinear}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
l = 0 ,
\qquad
i = 0
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad
\begin{cases}
-4\varphi_1 = -4\varphi_2 \\
3\varphi_1 = (3 - 6)\,\varphi_2
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi_1 = \varphi_2 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The portal frame has two bodies, connected to the ground by two hinges and to each other by an internal hinge, so it is an **three-hinged arch** and $s = 6 = 3n$. For the three-hinges arch theorem the structure is statically determinate if and only if $A$, $B$ and $C$ are not collinear. To control alignment, the vector product of the vectors $\overrightarrow{AC}$ and $\overrightarrow{AB}$ is calculated, whose component $z$ is the written expression: it is zero if and only if the three points stand on a line. It equals $-24\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so the dots are not aligned and the portal frame is **statically determinate**.

In the check the reference points are $A$ for the body $1$ and $B$ for the body $2$, and the pin supports cancel the displacements of the reference points. The rotations remain. The internal hinge requires that $C$ have the same displacement as the point of the two bodies: the first equation equals the horizontal shifts, $u_C = -\varphi_1 \cdot 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $u_C = -\varphi_2 \cdot 4\,\mathrm{m}$, the second vertical ones, $v_C = \varphi_1 \cdot 3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $v_C = \varphi_2 \cdot (3 - 6)\,\mathrm{m}$. The first gives $\varphi_1 = \varphi_2$, the second $\varphi_1 = -\varphi_2$, so both rotations are zero.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The frame in the figure, formed by the column $AD$, the crossbeam $DE$ and the column $EC$ with the rigid joints, has a pin support at $A$. The foot $C$ of the right column is connected with an internal hinge at the end of the beam $CB$, which has a pin support at $B$. Determine whether the structure is statically determinate and, if it is unstable, find the ratio between the rotations of the two bodies.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;E,5,4;C,5,0;B,8,0" aste="A-D;D-E;E-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" quote="A,C,5 m;C,B,3 m;C,E,4 m,-0.7"}

$$
n = 2 ,
\qquad
s = 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 ,
\qquad
3n - s = 0
$$
$$
C_1 = A = (0, 0) ,
\qquad
C_2 = B = (8, 0)\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
C_{12} = C = (5, 0)\,\mathrm{m}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{collinear on } y = 0
$$
$$
\varphi_1\,\overrightarrow{AC} = \varphi_2\,\overrightarrow{BC}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi_1 \cdot 5\,\mathrm{m} = \varphi_2 \cdot (-3\,\mathrm{m})
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi_2 = -\frac{5}{3}\,\varphi_1
$$
$$
l = 1 ,
\qquad
i = l - (3n - s) = 1
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad
v_C = \varphi_1 \cdot 5\,\mathrm{m}
\quad\text{and}\quad
v_C = \varphi_2 \cdot (5 - 8)\,\mathrm{m} = -\frac{5}{3}\,\varphi_1 \cdot (-3\,\mathrm{m}) = 5\varphi_1\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
u_C = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The structure is a three-hinged arch, with the frame as body $1$ and the beam $CB$ as body $2$, although it does not look like it. The fixed $A$ and $B$ and the internal hinge $C$ all stand on the line $y = 0$, so for the **three-hinged arch** theorem the structure is unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$. The frame rotates about $A$ and the beam around $B$; the point $C$ moves vertically in both bodies, because it lies on the horizontal line of the two centers.

The ratio of rotations is derived from the **first theorem of kinematic chains**. The point $C$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ to the right and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$ to the left, so the two bodies rotate in opposite directions and $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{5}{3}\,\varphi_1$: the beam rotates more than the frame, because it is shorter.

In the check the vertical displacement of $C$ calculated by the frame rotation coincides with that calculated by the beam rotation, and the horizontal displacement is zero in both bodies, because $C$ stands at the same elevation as the centers. The shift is therefore compatible with internal hinge.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The portal frame in the figure has feet at different altitudes: the pin support $A$ is in the origin and the pin support $B$ is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ higher, at the base of the right column. The internal hinge $C$ is on the crossbeam, at $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from the left-hand stand. Determine whether the portal frame is statically determinate.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,5;C,3,5;E,7,5;B,7,2" aste="A-D;D-C;C-E;E-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" quote="D,C,3 m,-0.7;C,E,4 m,-0.7;B,E,3 m;A,D,5 m,-0.7"}

$$
n = 2 ,
\qquad
s = 6 ,
\qquad
A = (0, 0) ,
\quad
B = (7, 2)\,\mathrm{m} ,
\quad
C = (3, 5)\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
(x_C - x_A)(y_B - y_A) - (y_C - y_A)(x_B - x_A) = 3 \cdot 2 - 5 \cdot 7 = -29\,\mathrm{m^2} \neq 0
$$
$$
l = 0 ,
\qquad
i = 0
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad
\begin{cases}
-5\varphi_1 + 3\varphi_2 = 0 \\
3\varphi_1 + 4\varphi_2 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
\det \begin{pmatrix} -5 & 3 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix} = -20 - 9 = -29 \neq 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Even with feet at different altitudes the portal frame is a three-hinged arch, because the shape of the bodies does not affect the result. The coordinates of the three hinges are calculated: $B$ is at the base of the right column, $7\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$, and $C$ is on the crossbeam, at the elevation of $5\,\mathrm{m}$. The vector product expression is $-29\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so the hinges are not collinear and the portal frame is **statically determinate**.

In verification, with the reference points at $A$ and $B$, the displacements of $C$ in the two bodies are equal. For the horizontal, $u_C = -\varphi_1 \cdot 5\,\mathrm{m}$ in body $1$ and $u_C = -\varphi_2 \cdot (5 - 2)\,\mathrm{m}$ in body $2$; for vertical, $v_C = \varphi_1 \cdot 3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $v_C = \varphi_2 \cdot (3 - 7)\,\mathrm{m}$. The two equations of the system are these equalities; the determinant is different from zero, so the rotations are zero. The determinant coincides with the expression of the alignment, because the two conditions describe the same property.

</div>

## Exercise 4

The frame in the figure, formed by the column $AD$, the crossbeam $DE$ and the column $EC$ with the rigid joints, has a pin support at $A$. Point $C$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground, is pinned to straight strut $CB$. The strut is pinned at $B$ on raised ground, a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of and $1\,\mathrm{m}$ above $C$. Determine whether the structure is statically determinate.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,5;E,6,5;C,6,2;B,9,3" aste="A-D;D-E;E-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" tratteggi="6,2 9,2" quote="D,E,6 m,-0.7;E,C,3 m;A,D,5 m,-0.7;C,9 2,3 m;9 2,B,1 m,0.5"}

$$
n = 2 ,
\qquad
s = 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 ,
\qquad
3n - s = 0
$$
$$
A = (0, 0) ,
\quad
C = (6, 2)\,\mathrm{m} ,
\quad
B = (9, 3)\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
(x_C - x_A)(y_B - y_A) - (y_C - y_A)(x_B - x_A) = 6 \cdot 3 - 2 \cdot 9 = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{collinear}
$$
$$
\varphi_1\,\overrightarrow{AC} = \varphi_2\,\overrightarrow{BC}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi_1 \begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} = \varphi_2 \begin{pmatrix} -3 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi_2 = -2\varphi_1
$$
$$
l = 1 ,
\qquad
i = 1
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad
\vec{s}_C = \varphi_1 \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix}\mathrm{m}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\vec{s}_C = \varphi_2 \begin{pmatrix} -(2 - 3) \\ 6 - 9 \end{pmatrix}\mathrm{m} = -2\varphi_1 \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix}\mathrm{m} = \varphi_1 \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix}\mathrm{m}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The frame and strut form a three-hinged arch, with the fixed $A$ and $B$ hinges and the internal hinge $C$. The vector product of $\overrightarrow{AC}$ and $\overrightarrow{AB}$ is zero, so the three hinges stand on the line $y = \dfrac{x}{3}$ and, by the **three-hinged arch** theorem, the structure is unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$. The frame rotates about $A$ and the strut around $B$ in the opposite direction, with $\varphi_2 = -2\varphi_1$ from the first theorem of kinematic chains.

The same result is achieved by observing that the strut, straight and hinged at the two extremes without intermediate loads, behaves as a two-force link to the frame. The frame then has a hinge $A$ and a two-force link at $C$ whose axis, the line $CB$, passes through $A$: this is the case with the hinge and roller support with the line of action passing through the hinge, one of the cases of **Geometrically dependent constraints**.

In the check, the displacement of $C$ is calculated with the formulas $u = -\varphi\,(y_C - y_O)$ and $v = \varphi\,(x_C - x_O)$, once with the center $A$ and the rotation $\varphi_1$ and once with the center $B$ and the rotation $\varphi_2 = -2\varphi_1$. The two shifts coincide, so the mechanism is compatible with the internal hinge.

</div>
