## Exercise 1

The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at $A$ and carries the downward force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in the free end $B$. Calculate reactions and plot diagrams of $T$ and $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0,-0.9,F" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Cantilever with force $F$ at the free end"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A - F = 0 \\[4pt]
M_A - 3\,F = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
T = -R^{\mathrm{right}}_y = F = 8\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M(x) = M^{\mathrm{right}}_S = -F\,(3 - x) = -8\,(3 - x)
$$
$$
M(0) = -24\,\mathrm{kN\,m} , \qquad M(3) = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,3,0,8" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,3,0,8*s-24" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_B = M_A - V_A \cdot 3 = 24 - 24 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Fixed support gives three reactions: $H_A$, $V_A$ and the moment $M_A$, assumed counterclockwise. The horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A = 0$, the vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = F$ upward and moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $M_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, positive and therefore counterclockwise.

The portion to the right of the section, which contains only $F$ and no reaction, is convenient for the characteristics of the stress. Using **section rules** on the right portion, the shear is the negative of the vertical resultant, so $T = F$. The bending moment is the moment of $F$ about the section, positive counterclockwise. Force $F$ acts downward to the right of the section, so its moment is clockwise and $M = -F\,(3 - x)$. The moment is negative on the whole cantilever, so the **upper fibers** are in tension, and the diagram is drawn above the axis. The maximum value in magnitude is at the fixed end, $|M(0)| = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, equal to the reaction moment $M_A$.

The check uses moment equilibrium about $B$: the $M_A$ counterclockwise reaction moment balances the clockwise moment of $V_A$, which has a moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and fixed at $A$, carries the uniform $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load over the entire length and the force $F = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward in the free end $B$. Calculate reactions and plot diagrams of $T$ and $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.4,0.4,q" forze="B,0,-1.1,F" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Cantilever with uniform load $q$ and end force $F$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A - 4q - F = 0 \\[4pt]
M_A - 4q \cdot 2 - F \cdot 4 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_A = 12 + 5 = 17\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_A = 24 + 20 = 44\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
T(x) = q\,(4 - x) + F = 3\,(4 - x) + 5
$$
$$
M(x) = -\frac{q\,(4 - x)^2}{2} - F\,(4 - x) = -\frac{3\,(4 - x)^2}{2} - 5\,(4 - x)
$$
$$
T(0) = 17\,\mathrm{kN} , \quad T(4) = 5\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(0) = -44\,\mathrm{kN\,m} , \quad M(4) = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,4,0,-3*s+17" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,4,0,-1.5*s^2+17*s-44" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_B = M_A - V_A \cdot 4 + 12 \cdot 2 = 44 - 68 + 24 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The resultant load is $4q = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is applied $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The vertical translation and rotation around $A$ give $V_A$ and $M_A$, each with only one unknown.

The right-hand side of the section $x$ carries the load $q\,(4 - x)$, with a moment arm of $\dfrac{4 - x}{2}$, and the force $F$, with a moment arm of $4 - x$. For **section rules** the shear is the sum of the two vertical loads and is linear, and the moment is the sum of their sign changed clockwise moments, that is, a parabola. The shear is positive on the whole cantilever and the negative moment, so the upper fibers are in tension. At the fixed end the shear equals $V_A$ and the moment equals $-M_A$, because the left part of a section close to fixed support contains only the reactions. The $M$ diagram has in $B$ the slope $T(4) = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$, due to the force $F$.

The check uses moment equilibrium about $B$: the reaction moment $M_A$ and the counterclockwise moment of the load result balance the clockwise moment of $V_A$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and fixed at $A$, carries a triangular load that equals zero in $A$ and $q_0 = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in $B$, and at the end $B$ the horizontal force $P = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ directed to the right. Plot diagrams of $N$, $T$ and $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0,0.6,q_0" forze="5.2 0,1.2,0,P" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Cantilever under a triangular load and axial force $P$"}

$$
q(x) = \frac{3}{4}\,x ,
\qquad
\text{resultant } \frac{3 \cdot 4}{2} = 6\,\mathrm{kN} \text{ in } x = \frac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + P = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A - 6 = 0 \\[4pt]
M_A - 6 \cdot \dfrac{8}{3} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
H_A = -6\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
N = P = 6\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
T(x) = \frac{q(x) + q_0}{2}\,(4 - x) = \frac{3\,(16 - x^2)}{8}
$$
$$
M(x) = -\left[ q(x)\,\frac{(4 - x)^2}{2} + \frac{q_0 - q(x)}{2}\,(4 - x) \cdot \frac{2\,(4 - x)}{3} \right] = -\frac{(4 - x)^2\,(8 + x)}{8}
$$
$$
T(0) = 6\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(0) = -16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,4,0,6" segni=1 titolo="Axial force diagram $N$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,4,0,-0.375*s^2+6" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,4,0,-0.125*s^3+6*s-16" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_B = M_A - V_A \cdot 4 + 6 \cdot \frac{4}{3} = 16 - 24 + 8 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The load grows linearly from $A$ to $B$; its resultant is the area of the triangle, $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, and it switches to two-thirds of the length from $A$. The force $P$ is directed along the axis, so it only enters the horizontal translation, which gives $H_A = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left.

The portion to the right of the section is used. The force $P$ pulls it to the right, so for the **section rules** $N = P = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the cantilever is **in tension** over the entire length. The load on the right side is a trapezoid, with the heights $q(x)$ in the section and $q_0$ in $B$; The shear is its area. For the moment being, the trapezoid is divided into a rectangle of height $q(x)$, with the centroid in the middle of the segment, and a triangle of height $q_0 - q(x)$, with the centroid two-thirds of the segment from the section. Both the resultants have clockwise moment about the section, so the bending moment is negative and the upper fibers are in tension; simplifying results in a cubic polynomial. The fixed support features $T(0) = V_A$ and $M(0) = -M_A$.

The check uses moment equilibrium about $B$: the resultant of the load is $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$; $P$ and $H_A$ have line of action on the axis and do not give moment.

</div>

## Exercise 4

The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at the right end $B$ and has the free end in $A$. It brings the downward force $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ into $A$ and the uniform $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load on the section $CB$, with $AC = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. Plot diagrams of $T$ and $M$ and calculate the fixed support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="B,incastro,0" carichi="C,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="A,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,3 m" titolo="Cantilever fixed at the right, with force $F$ at the free end and load $q$ on $CB$"}

$$
\text{segment } AC,\ 0 < x < 2: \qquad T = -F = -4\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = -F x = -4x
$$
$$
\text{segment } CB,\ 2 < x < 5: \qquad T(x) = -4 - 2\,(x - 2) , \qquad M(x) = -4x - (x - 2)^2
$$
$$
T(5) = -10\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(5) = -20 - 9 = -29\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_B = 0 \\[4pt]
V_B - F - 3q = 0 \\[4pt]
M_B + F \cdot 5 + 3q \cdot 1.5 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_B = -20 - 9 = -29\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="B,incastro,0" diagramma="0,0,2,0,-4;2,0,5,0,-2*s-4" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="B,incastro,0" diagramma="0,0,2,0,-4*s;2,0,5,0,-s^2-4*s-8" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_A = M_B + V_B \cdot 5 - 6 \cdot 3.5 = -29 + 50 - 21 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The free end is on the left, so for the characteristics of the stress it is convenient to **left side**, which contains no reactions; calculation does not require knowing the fixed support reactions first. In the segment $AC$, only $F$ acts, and for practical $T = -F$ rules, because the force is directed downward. The force stands to the left of the section and is directed downward, so its moment about the section is counterclockwise and equals $Fx$; for rule $M = -M^{\mathrm{left}}_S$ you get $M = -Fx$. In the segment $CB$, the load $2\,(x - 2)$ is added, with $\dfrac{x - 2}{2}$ arm. The moment is negative everywhere, with the upper fibers in tension out, and it grows in form until fixed support.

The reactions are then obtained with the equilibrium equations, taking the moments about $B$: the force $F$ has a moment arm of $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and the resultant of the load, $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, has a moment arm of $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$, and both have counterclockwise moment about $B$. You get $M_B = -29\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, i.e. an **clockwise** reaction moment of $29\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and $V_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. Values are consistent with diagrams: with the right part of a section close to $B$ you have $T = -V_B$ and $M = M_B$.

The check uses moment equilibrium about $A$, resulting in the load $3.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.

</div>
