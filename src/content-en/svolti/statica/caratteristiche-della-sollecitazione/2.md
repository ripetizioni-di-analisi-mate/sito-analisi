## Exercise 1

The beam $AB$ shown, of length $L = 8\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ and carries the uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ all over the span. Plot the shear and bending moment diagrams and calculate the maximum moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.8,0.8,q" quote="A,B,8 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under uniform load $q$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - qL = 0 \\[4pt]
L\,V_B - qL \cdot \dfrac{L}{2} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_A = V_B = \frac{qL}{2} = \frac{3 \cdot 8}{2} = 12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
T(x) = V_A - q x = 12 - 3x ,
\qquad
M(x) = V_A\,x - q x \cdot \frac{x}{2} = 12x - \frac{3x^2}{2}
$$
$$
T(x) = 0 \iff x = 4\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
M_{\max} = M(4) = 48 - 24 = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m} = \frac{qL^2}{8}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,8,0,-3*s+12" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,8,0,-1.5*s^2+12*s" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_B = -V_A \cdot 8 + qL \cdot 4 = -96 + 96 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The resultant of the load is $qL = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is applied at midspan. Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $V_B$ and vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A$; the two values are equal, because the beam and load are symmetrical.

On the left side of an $x$ horizontal coordinate section $V_A$ acts and the segment load from $0$ to $x$, resulting in $qx$ applied in the middle of the segment, i.e. with $\dfrac{x}{2}$ arm about the section. For **section rules** the shear is linear and the moment is a parabola, as the theorem on the course in a segment with uniform load predicts. The moment is maximum where the shear vanishes, i.e. at midspan, and equals $M_{\max} = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, the value $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$ of the simply supported beam with uniform load. The moment is positive along the entire beam, so the lower fibers are in tension.

The check uses moment equilibrium about $B$: the clockwise moment of $V_A$ and the counterclockwise moment of the load resultant, which stands at $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$, have the same magnitude.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and supported at both ends, carries the uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on only half $AC$, with $AC = 3\,\mathrm{m}$. Plot diagrams of $T$ and $M$ and calculate the maximum moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,C,3 m;C,B,3 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with a uniform load over half $AC$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - 12 = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_B - 12 \cdot 1.5 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_B = \frac{18}{6} = 3\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = 12 - 3 = 9\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{segment } AC,\ 0 < x < 3: \qquad T(x) = 9 - 4x , \qquad M(x) = 9x - 2x^2
$$
$$
\text{segment } CB,\ 3 < x < 6: \qquad T = -V_B = -3\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = 3\,(6 - x)
$$
$$
T(x) = 0 \iff x = \frac{9}{4} = 2.25\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
M_{\max} = 9 \cdot \frac{9}{4} - 2 \cdot \frac{81}{16} = \frac{81}{8} = 10.125\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,3,0,-4*s+9;3,0,6,0,-3" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,3,0,-2*s^2+9*s;3,0,6,0,-3*s+9" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_B = -V_A \cdot 6 + 12 \cdot 4.5 = -54 + 54 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The resultant load is $4 \cdot 3 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is applied at the midpoint of $AC$, $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $V_B$, vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A$; the major reaction is that of the support closest to the load.

The load ends in $C$, so the beam has two segments. In the segment $AC$ the left side contains $V_A$ and the load $4x$ with $\dfrac{x}{2}$ arm, and the shear is linear; in the segment $CB$ the right part contains only $V_B$, so the shear is constant and the moment linear. In $C$ the shear equals $-3\,\mathrm{kN}$ on both sides, because in $C$ there are no concentrated forces, and the diagram of $M$ switches from the parabola to the line with the same tangent. The shear vanishes in $x = 2.25\,\mathrm{m}$, inside the loaded segment, and there the moment has the **maximum $M_{\max} = \dfrac{81}{8}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$**, for observation on the maximum moment where the shear vanishes. The value $M_C = 9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is obtained with both expressions.

The check uses moment equilibrium about $B$, resulting in the load $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The beam $AB$ shown, $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long and supported at both ends, brings the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over all span and downward force $F = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $C$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Trace the diagrams of $T$ and $M$ and locate the maximum moment section.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="C,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,C,4 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under uniform load $q$ and force $F$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - 12 - 9 = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_B - 12 \cdot 3 - 9 \cdot 4 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_B = \frac{72}{6} = 12\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = 21 - 12 = 9\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{segment } AC,\ 0 < x < 4: \qquad T(x) = 9 - 2x , \qquad M(x) = 9x - x^2
$$
$$
\text{segment } CB,\ 4 < x < 6: \qquad T(x) = -12 + 2\,(6 - x) = -2x , \qquad M(x) = 12\,(6 - x) - (6 - x)^2 = 36 - x^2
$$
$$
T(4^-) = 1\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad T(4^+) = -8\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M_{\max} = M_C = 36 - 16 = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,4,0,-2*s+9;4,0,6,0,-2*s-8" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,4,0,-s^2+9*s;4,0,6,0,-s^2-8*s+20" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_C = -V_A \cdot 4 + 8 \cdot 2 - 4 \cdot 1 + V_B \cdot 2 = -36 + 16 - 4 + 24 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The resultant distributed load equals $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is applied at midspan, $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $V_B$ and the vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A$.

In the segment $AC$, the left side is used, with $V_A$ and the load $2x$; in the segment $CB$ the right side, with $V_B$ and the load $2\,(6 - x)$, which has $\dfrac{6 - x}{2}$ arm. In $C$ the shear drops by $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ and goes from $1\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$. In the segment $AC$ the shear remains positive, so the moment grows to $C$; in the segment $CB$ the shear is negative and the moment decreases. The **change sign to $C$ with a jump** shear and in no segment cancel; therefore the moment is maximum at $C$ and equals $M_{\max} = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The two expressions of $M$ give the same value in $C$, because the moment is continuous under a concentrated force.

The check uses moment equilibrium about $C$, with the load divided into two sections: $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in the center of $AC$, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $C$, and $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in the center of $CB$, $1\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $C$.

</div>

## Exercise 4

The beam $AB$ shown, of length $L = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ and supported at both ends, carries a triangular load that equals zero in $A$ and $q_0 = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in $B$. Plot diagrams of $T$ and $M$ and calculate the maximum moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0,1,q_0" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under a triangular load, zero at $A$ and equal to $q_0$ at $B$"}

$$
q(x) = \frac{q_0}{L}\,x = x ,
\qquad
\text{resultant } \frac{q_0 L}{2} = 18\,\mathrm{kN} \text{ in } x = \frac{2}{3}\,L = 4\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - 18 = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_B - 18 \cdot 4 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
T(x) = 6 - \frac{x^2}{2} ,
\qquad
M(x) = 6x - \frac{x^2}{2} \cdot \frac{x}{3} = 6x - \frac{x^3}{6}
$$
$$
T(x) = 0 \iff x = \sqrt{12} = 2\sqrt{3} \approx 3.46\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
M_{\max} = 12\sqrt{3} - \frac{24\sqrt{3}}{6} = 8\sqrt{3} \approx 13.86\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,6,0,-0.5*s^2+6" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,6,0,-0.16666667*s^3+6*s" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_B = -V_A \cdot 6 + 18 \cdot 2 = -36 + 36 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The load grows linearly from $A$ to $B$, then $q(x) = \dfrac{q_0}{L}\,x$, which with the data equals $x$ (in $\mathrm{kN/m}$, with $x$ in meters). The resultant is the area of the triangle and passes through its centroid, two thirds of the span from $A$. Reactions follow by rotation around $A$ and vertical translation; the major reaction is in $B$, where the load is most intense.

The left part of a section $x$ carries a base triangle $x$ and height $q(x) = x$: its resultant equals $\dfrac{x^2}{2}$ and stands at two-thirds of $x$ from $A$, i.e. at $\dfrac{x}{3}$ from the section. For **section rules** the shear is second grade and the third grade moment. The shear vanishes in $x = 2\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{m}$, beyond the midspan and then on the side of the most intense load; There the moment has the **maximum $8\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m} \approx 13.86\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$**. In $B$ the shear equals $6 - 18 = -12\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $-V_B$, in accordance with the right part of a section close to $B$.

The check uses moment equilibrium about $B$: the resultant load stands at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$ and has counterclockwise moment equal to the clockwise moment of $V_A$.

</div>
