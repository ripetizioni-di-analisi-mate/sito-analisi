## Exercise 1

The beam $AB$ shown, of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and supported at both ends (pin at $A$, roller support at $B$), leads to $C$, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, the concentrated clockwise couple of moment $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Plot diagrams of $T$ and $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="C,-1,ℳ" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,3 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under clockwise couple $\mathcal{M}$ at $C$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B = 0 \\[4pt]
5\,V_B - \mathcal{M} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_B = \frac{10}{5} = 2\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = -2\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
T = V_A = -2\,\mathrm{kN} \quad \text{along the entire beam}
$$
$$
\text{segment } AC,\ 0 < x < 2: \qquad M(x) = -2x
$$
$$
\text{segment } CB,\ 2 < x < 5: \qquad M(x) = V_B\,(5 - x) = 10 - 2x
$$
$$
M(2^-) = -4\,\mathrm{kN\,m} , \qquad M(2^+) = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,-2;2,0,5,0,-2" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,-2*s;2,0,5,0,-2*s+6" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_C = -V_A \cdot 2 + V_B \cdot 3 - \mathcal{M} = 4 + 6 - 10 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The couple is clockwise, so in the rotation around $A$ it appears with the minus sign. A couple has no result, so the two vertical reactions form a counterclockwise couple that balances it: $V_B = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $V_A = -2\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ **downward**.

On the beam no forces act, so the shear is constant and equal to $V_A$ over the entire length. The moment is linear in the two segments with the same slope $-2\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the two segments of the diagram are parallel. For the **jump in bending moment** theorem, a clockwise couple causes the $\mathcal{M}$ diagram to rise; Therefore in $C$ the moment goes from $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension, to $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension. The maximum value in magnitude is $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, immediately to the right of $C$.

The check uses moment equilibrium about $C$: the two reactions have counterclockwise moment, $4$ and $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and the couple has clockwise moment of $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The beam $AB$ shown, $8\,\mathrm{m}$ long and supported at both ends, brings the downward force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $C$, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and the $\mathcal{M} = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise couple at $D$, $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Plot diagrams of $T$ and $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-2,F" coppie="D,1,ℳ" quote="A,C,2 m;C,D,4 m;D,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with force $F$ at $C$ and counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M}$ at $D$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - F = 0 \\[4pt]
8\,V_B - 2\,F + \mathcal{M} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_B = \frac{16 - 16}{8} = 0 ,
\qquad
V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{segment } AC,\ 0 < x < 2: \qquad T = 8\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = 8x
$$
$$
\text{segment } CD,\ 2 < x < 6: \qquad T = 0 , \qquad M = 8x - 8\,(x - 2) = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{segment } DB,\ 6 < x < 8: \qquad T = 0 , \qquad M = 16 - \mathcal{M} = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,8;2,0,6,0,0;6,0,8,0,0" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,8*s;2,0,6,0,16;6,0,8,0,0" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_C = -V_A \cdot 2 + \mathcal{M} + V_B \cdot 6 = -16 + 16 + 0 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Taking moments about $A$, the clockwise moment of $F$ and the counterclockwise moment of the couple have the same magnitude $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Therefore $V_B = 0$, and support $A$ carries the entire force.

In the segment $AC$ the left side contains only $V_A$. In the segment $CD$ you add $F$, which cancels the shear; the moment, which has zero slope, is constant and equals $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and the segment $CD$ is in **pure bending**. In $D$ the counterclockwise couple drops the moment of $\mathcal{M}$, so in the segment $DB$ the moment is zero, according to the right side, on which only $V_B = 0$ acts. The moment is positive or zero along the entire beam, with the lower fibers in tension between $A$ and $D$.

The check uses moment equilibrium about $C$, where the force $F$ gives no moment.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and fixed at $A$, brings the downward force $F = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $C$, at half the length, and the $\mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise couple into the free end $B$. Calculate reactions and plot diagrams of $T$ and $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="C,0,-1.2,F" coppie="B,1,ℳ" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Cantilever with force $F$ at midspan and couple $\mathcal{M}$ at the tip"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A - F = 0 \\[4pt]
M_A - 2\,F + \mathcal{M} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_A = 6 - 8 = -2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{segment } CB,\ 2 < x < 4: \qquad T = 0 , \qquad M = M^{\mathrm{right}}_S = \mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{segment } AC,\ 0 < x < 2: \qquad T = F = 3\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = 8 - 3\,(2 - x) = 2 + 3x
$$
$$
M(0) = 2\,\mathrm{kN\,m} = -M_A
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,2,0,3;2,0,4,0,0" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" diagramma="0,0,2,0,3*s+2;2,0,4,0,8" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_B = M_A - V_A \cdot 4 + F \cdot 2 + \mathcal{M} = -2 - 12 + 6 + 8 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Fixed support gives $H_A$, $V_A$ and $M_A$. Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives the clockwise moment of $F$, with a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$, and the counterclockwise moment of the couple, which is the same as any reference point. You get $M_A = -2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, i.e. the reaction moment is **clockwise**, of $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The right side is used. In the segment $CB$ only the couple acts on it, then $T = 0$ and, by the section rules, $M = \mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, constant and positive: the counterclockwise couple at the end puts the lower fibers in tension. In the segment $AC$ you add $F$, which has clockwise moment $3\,(2 - x)$ about the section; the moment decreases towards fixed support and equals $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $A$, equal to $-M_A$. The moment is positive on the whole cantilever; Therefore, unlike the cantilever with an extreme force, the **Lower Fibers** are in tension here.

The check uses moment equilibrium about $B$, which contains all reactions and loads.

</div>

## Exercise 4

The beam $AC$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on $AB$ and the clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts in the free end $C$ of the overhang. Plot diagrams of $T$ and $M$ and calculate the maximum positive moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" coppie="C,-1,ℳ" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,2 m" titolo="Overhanging beam with a uniform load on the span and a clockwise couple at the tip"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - 8 = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_B - 8 \cdot 2 - \mathcal{M} = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_B = \frac{22}{4} = 5.5\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = 8 - 5.5 = 2.5\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{segment } AB,\ 0 < x < 4: \qquad T(x) = 2.5 - 2x , \qquad M(x) = 2.5\,x - x^2
$$
$$
\text{segment } BC,\ 4 < x < 6: \qquad T = 0 , \qquad M = -\mathcal{M} = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
T = 0 \iff x = 1.25\,\mathrm{m} , \qquad M_{\max} = 3.125 - 1.5625 = \frac{25}{16} \approx 1.56\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,4,0,-2*s+2.5;4,0,6,0,0" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,4,0,-s^2+2.5*s;4,0,6,0,-6" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_B = -V_A \cdot 4 + 8 \cdot 2 - \mathcal{M} = -10 + 16 - 6 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The clockwise couple at the end of the overhang appears in the rotation around $A$ with the minus sign, as the moment of the load; $V_B = 5.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ follows, while the vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = 2.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, both upward.

In the overhang the right part contains only the couple, so $T = 0$ and the moment is constant and equal to $-\mathcal{M}$, with the upper fibers in tension. In the segment $AB$ the left part contains $V_A$ and the load $2x$, and the shear vanishes in $x = 1.25\,\mathrm{m}$, where the moment has the **$\dfrac{25}{16}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ positive maximum**, then the moment decreases and in $B$ it equals $10 - 16 = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, the same value as the overhang. In $B$ the shear rises from $-5.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to zero, with a jump equal to $V_B$, while the moment is continuous and has a corner. The moment vanishes in $x = 2.5\,\mathrm{m}$.

The check uses moment equilibrium about $B$, with the resultant load, $8\,\mathrm{kN}$, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$.

</div>
