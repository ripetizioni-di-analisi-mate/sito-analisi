## Exercise 1

The beam $AB$ shown, of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ and carries a downward load at $C$, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, of magnitude $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$. Plot the shear and bending moment diagrams.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,3 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under force $F$ at $C$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - F = 0 \\[4pt]
5\,V_B - 2\,F = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_B = \frac{2 \cdot 10}{5} = 4\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = 10 - 4 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{segment } AC,\ 0 < x < 2: \qquad T = V_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = V_A\,x = 6x
$$
$$
\text{segment } CB,\ 2 < x < 5: \qquad T = -V_B = -4\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = V_B\,(5 - x) = 4\,(5 - x)
$$
$$
M_C = 6 \cdot 2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,6;2,0,5,0,-4" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,6*s;2,0,5,0,-4*s+12" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_C = -V_A \cdot 2 + V_B \cdot 3 = -6 \cdot 2 + 4 \cdot 3 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Reactions are obtained with equilibrium equations: the horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A = 0$, because there are no horizontal loads; moment equilibrium about $A$ gives the only unknown $V_B$, because $H_A$ and $V_A$ pass through $A$; the vertical force equilibrium then gives $V_A$. Both reactions are directed upward.

The force $F$ divides the beam into two segments. In the segment $AC$, the portion to the left of the section is used, on which only $V_A$ acts; Therefore, by **section rules**, the shear is $V_A$ and the bending moment is the clockwise moment of $V_A$, which has a moment arm of $x$. In the segment $CB$, the right side is convenient, on which only $V_B$ acts with a moment arm of $5 - x$, so the shear is $-V_B$ and the moment is $V_B\,(5 - x)$. In $C$ the shear drops from $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$, a drop of $F$, as the **jump in shear** predicts under a concentrated force. The moment is positive along the entire beam, so the lower fibers are in tension; has a corner in $C$, where it reaches its maximum, $M_C = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The check uses moment equilibrium about $C$, an equation not used for reactions: the moments of $V_A$, clockwise, and $V_B$, counterclockwise, have the same magnitude.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The beam $AB$ shown, of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$ and supported at both ends (pin at $A$, roller support at $B$), carries the vertical forces $F_1 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $C$ and $F_2 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $D$, directed downward, with $AC = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $AD = 6\,\mathrm{m}$. Plot diagrams of $T$ and $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.5,F_1;D,0,-2,F_2" quote="A,C,2 m;C,D,4 m;D,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under forces $F_1$ and $F_2$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - F_1 - F_2 = 0 \\[4pt]
8\,V_B - 2\,F_1 - 6\,F_2 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_B = \frac{2 \cdot 6 + 6 \cdot 10}{8} = \frac{72}{8} = 9\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = 16 - 9 = 7\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{segment } AC,\ 0 < x < 2: \qquad T = 7\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = 7x
$$
$$
\text{segment } CD,\ 2 < x < 6: \qquad T = 7 - 6 = 1\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = 7x - 6\,(x - 2) = x + 12
$$
$$
\text{segment } DB,\ 6 < x < 8: \qquad T = -V_B = -9\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = 9\,(8 - x)
$$
$$
M_C = 14\,\mathrm{kN\,m} , \qquad M_D = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,7;2,0,6,0,1;6,0,8,0,-9" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,7*s;2,0,6,0,s+14;6,0,8,0,-9*s+18" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_D = -V_A \cdot 6 + F_1 \cdot 4 + V_B \cdot 2 = -42 + 24 + 18 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $V_B$, because in it appear the moments of the two forces, with moment arms $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and that of $V_B$, with moment arm $8\,\mathrm{m}$; vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A$. The two forces locate three segments, in each of which there are no loads; Therefore, for the **trend in the segments** theorem, the shear is constant and the moment is linear.

In the first two segments the left side is used: in the segment $CD$ on it act $V_A$ upward and $F_1$ downward, then $T = 7 - 6 = 1\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the clockwise moment about the section is $7x - 6\,(x - 2)$, because $F_1$ has a moment arm of $x - 2$. In the segment $DB$, the right side is shorter, with only the $V_B$. The shear drops $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $C$ and $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $D$. The shear is positive up to $D$ and negative after, so the moment grows up to $D$ and then decreases; therefore the maximum is $M_D = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension.

With the left side you get the same value, $M_D = 7 \cdot 6 - 6 \cdot 4 = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, as the theorem on the calculation by sections establishes. Check by taking moments about $D$. The clockwise moment of $V_A$ equals the sum of the counterclockwise moments of $F_1$ and $V_B$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The beam $AB$ shown, of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ on a horizontal surface. In $C$, at $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, the inclined $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ force acts, of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ components to the left and $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ components downward. Plot diagrams of $N$, $T$ and $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,-1.2,-1.6,F" quote="A,C,6 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under inclined force $F$ at $C$"}

$$
F_x = -6\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad F_y = -8\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + F_x = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B + F_y = 0 \\[4pt]
8\,V_B + 6\,F_y = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
H_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_B = \frac{6 \cdot 8}{8} = 6\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = 8 - 6 = 2\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{segment } AC,\ 0 < x < 6: \qquad N = -H_A = -6\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad T = 2\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = 2x
$$
$$
\text{segment } CB,\ 6 < x < 8: \qquad N = 0 , \qquad T = -6\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = 6\,(8 - x)
$$
$$
M_C = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,6,0,-6;6,0,8,0,0" segni=1 titolo="Axial force diagram $N$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,6,0,2;6,0,8,0,-6" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,6,0,2*s;6,0,8,0,-6*s+12" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_B = -V_A \cdot 8 - F_y \cdot 2 = -2 \cdot 8 + 8 \cdot 2 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The force breaks down into the $F_x$ and $F_y$ components, with the sign according to the axes. The roller support at $B$ only gives a vertical reaction, so the horizontal component of $F$ is balanced by $H_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, directed to the right. Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $V_B$, because $F_x$ has line of action on the beam axis and zero moment about $A$.

In the segment $AC$ the left side contains $H_A$, which pushes the beam to the right while $F_x$ pushes it to the left; therefore, by the section rules, $N = -H_A = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the segment $AC$ is **in compression**. In the segment $CB$ the right part contains only $V_B$, vertical, then $N = 0$. The jump of $N$ to $C$ equals $-F_x = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the jump of the shear equals $F_y = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$, as the theorem on **jump in shear** provides. The moment grows linearly to $M_C = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and returns to zero in $B$; the horizontal component of the force does not contribute at the moment, because it is applied on the axis.

The check uses moment equilibrium about $B$: $V_A$ has clockwise moment and the vertical component of $F$, at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$ to its left, has counterclockwise moment of the same magnitude.

</div>
