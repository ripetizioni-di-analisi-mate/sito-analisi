## Exercise 1

The beam $AC$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$; in the free end $C$ of the overhang the force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Calculate reactions and plot diagrams of $T$ and $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,2 m" titolo="Overhanging beam with force $F$ at the tip"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - F = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_B - 6\,F = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_B = \frac{6 \cdot 8}{4} = 12\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = 8 - 12 = -4\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{segment } AB,\ 0 < x < 4: \qquad T = V_A = -4\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = V_A\,x = -4x
$$
$$
\text{segment } BC,\ 4 < x < 6: \qquad T = F = 8\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = -F\,(6 - x) = -8\,(6 - x)
$$
$$
M_B = -16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,4,0,-4;4,0,6,0,8" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,4,0,-4*s;4,0,6,0,8*s-16" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_C = -V_A \cdot 6 - V_B \cdot 2 = 24 - 24 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $V_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, greater than $F$, because the roller support acts as the moment center for the force applied on the overhang. The vertical force equilibrium gives $V_A = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. the reaction at $A$ is **$4\,\mathrm{kN}$ down**, and the hinge holds the beam that tends to rise.

In the segment $AB$ the left part contains only $V_A$, so the shear is $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the moment is $V_A\,x$, negative. In the overhang the right side contains only $F$. In $B$ the shear rises from $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $8\,\mathrm{kN}$, a drop of $V_B$. The moment is negative along the entire beam, with the upper fibers in tension out, and has the maximum magnitude value on the support, $M_B = -16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, which is the moment of $F$ about $B$. The diagram has a corner in $B$.

The check uses moment equilibrium about $C$: $V_A$, downward, $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$, has counterclockwise moment, and $V_B$, upward, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$, has clockwise moment of the same magnitude.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The beam $CD$ shown, $10\,\mathrm{m}$ long, rests on a hinge $A$ and $B$ roller support, with two $CA$ and $BD$ $2\,\mathrm{m}$ long overhangs each, and carries the uniform $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load over the entire length. Trace the diagrams of $T$ and $M$ and find the sections where the moment vanishes.

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,2,0;B,8,0;D,10,0" aste="C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="C,D,0.8,0.8,q" quote="C,A,2 m;A,B,6 m;B,D,2 m" titolo="Beam with two overhangs under a uniform load"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - 20 = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_B - 20 \cdot 3 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_A = V_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{segment } CA,\ 0 < x < 2: \qquad T(x) = -2x , \qquad M(x) = -x^2
$$
$$
\text{segment } AB,\ 2 < x < 8: \qquad T(x) = 10 - 2x , \qquad M(x) = 10\,(x - 2) - x^2 = -x^2 + 10x - 20
$$
$$
\text{segment } BD,\ 8 < x < 10: \qquad T(x) = 2\,(10 - x) , \qquad M(x) = -(10 - x)^2
$$
$$
M_A = M_B = -4\,\mathrm{kN\,m} , \qquad M(5) = 5\,\mathrm{kN\,m} , \qquad M(x) = 0 \iff x = 5 \pm \sqrt{5}
$$

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,2,0;B,8,0;D,10,0" aste="C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,-2*s;2,0,8,0,-2*s+6;8,0,10,0,-2*s+4" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,2,0;B,8,0;D,10,0" aste="C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,2,0,-s^2;2,0,8,0,-s^2+6*s-4;8,0,10,0,-s^2+4*s-4" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_B = -V_A \cdot 6 + 20 \cdot 3 = -60 + 60 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The resultant load is $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and is applied at $x = 5\,\mathrm{m}$, halfway between the supports; moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $V_B$ and vertical translation $V_A$, equal in symmetry.

The supports divide the beam into three segments. In the $CA$ overhang the left side contains only the load $2x$, with $\dfrac{x}{2}$ arm; in the segment $AB$ you add $V_A$, with $x - 2$ arm; In the $BD$ overhang, the right side is convenient, which contains only the load $2\,(10 - x)$. The shear goes up by $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $A$ and $B$. In the overhangs the moment is negative, with the upper fibers in tension, and on the supports it equals $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. In the segment $AB$ the shear vanishes at midspan, where the moment has the **$5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ positive maximum**; the moment vanishes where $-x^2 + 10x - 20 = 0$, i.e. in $x = 5 - \sqrt{5} \approx 2.76\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x = 5 + \sqrt{5} \approx 7.24\,\mathrm{m}$. In these two sections the fibers in tension pass from the upper downward edge.

The check uses moment equilibrium about $B$, resulting in the load $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The beam $AC$ shown, of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The uniform $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load acts on the segment $AB$ and the downward force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts in the free end $C$. Plot the diagrams of $T$ and $M$, calculate the positive maximum moment and the section where the moment vanishes.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" forze="C,0,-1.8,F" quote="A,B,6 m;B,C,2 m" titolo="Overhanging beam with a uniform load on the span and a force at the tip"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - 24 - 6 = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_B - 24 \cdot 3 - 6 \cdot 8 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_B = \frac{120}{6} = 20\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = 30 - 20 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{segment } AB,\ 0 < x < 6: \qquad T(x) = 10 - 4x , \qquad M(x) = 10x - 2x^2
$$
$$
\text{segment } BC,\ 6 < x < 8: \qquad T = F = 6\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = -6\,(8 - x)
$$
$$
T = 0 \iff x = 2.5\,\mathrm{m} , \qquad M_{\max} = 25 - 12.5 = 12.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
M_B = 60 - 72 = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m} , \qquad M(x) = 0 \iff x = 5\,\mathrm{m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,6,0,-4*s+10;6,0,8,0,6" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" diagramma="0,0,6,0,-2*s^2+10*s;6,0,8,0,6*s-12" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_B = -V_A \cdot 6 + 24 \cdot 3 - F \cdot 2 = -60 + 72 - 12 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The resultant load, $24\,\mathrm{kN}$, is applied $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$; moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $V_B$ and vertical translation $V_A$, both upward.

In the segment $AB$ the left part contains $V_A$ and the load $4x$, so the shear is linear and vanishes in $x = 2.5\,\mathrm{m}$, where for observation on the **maximum moment** the moment has the maximum positive $12.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. In the overhang the right part contains only $F$, so the shear is constant and the moment linear. In $B$ the shear goes from $10 - 24 = -14\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, with an $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ jump equal to $V_B$, and the moment equals $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ with both expressions. The moment vanishes where $10x - 2x^2 = 0$ with $x > 0$, i.e. in $x = 5\,\mathrm{m}$. Between $A$ and this section the lower fibers are in tension, from here to $C$ the upper ones.

The check uses moment equilibrium about $B$: $V_A$ and $F$ have clockwise moment, the resultant of the counterclockwise moment load.

</div>
