## Exercise 1

The Gerber beam shown consists of cantilever $AC$, fixed at $A$ and of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, and beam $CB$. An internal hinge $C$ connects the beam to the cantilever, and roller $B$ supports it, with $CB = 4\,\mathrm{m}$. A downward force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $D$, the midpoint of $CB$. Calculate the reactions and draw the diagrams for $T$ and $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;D,5,0;B,7,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" forze="D,0,-1.6,F" quote="A,C,3 m;C,D,2 m;D,B,2 m" titolo="Gerber beam comprising cantilever $AC$ and beam $CB$ under force $F$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - F = 0 \\[4pt]
M_A - 5\,F + 7\,V_B = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_B - 2\,F = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_A = 40 - 28 = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{segment } AD,\ 0 < x < 5: \qquad T = V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = -M_A + V_A\,x = 4x - 12
$$
$$
\text{segment } DB,\ 5 < x < 7: \qquad T = -V_B = -4\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = 4\,(7 - x)
$$
$$
M(0) = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m} , \qquad M_C = 0 , \qquad M_D = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;D,5,0;B,7,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" diagramma="0,0,3,0,4;3,0,5,0,4;5,0,7,0,-4" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;D,5,0;B,7,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" diagramma="0,0,3,0,4*s-12;3,0,5,0,4*s;5,0,7,0,-4*s+8" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_D = M_A - V_A \cdot 5 + V_B \cdot 2 = 12 - 20 + 8 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The structure has two bodies, so $6$ degrees of freedom, and fixed support, internal hinge and roller support give $3 + 2 + 1 = 6$ scalar constraints, so it is statically determinate. To the three equilibrium equations of the entire beam (horizontal translation, vertical translation, rotation around $A$) is added the **auxiliary equation** of the hinge: the segment $CB$ is in moment equilibrium around $C$, and only $F$ and $V_B$ act on it. The auxiliary equation immediately gives $V_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$; the others give $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $M_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise.

Once the reactions are known, the beam is studied as a single beam. The only concentrated force is in $D$, so the segments are two. In the segment $AD$ the left part contains $V_A$ and the reaction moment $M_A$, counterclockwise, which enters with the minus sign; the bending moment is linear, it equals $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ to fixed support, with the upper fibers in tension, and vanishes in $C$, as the observation on the **zero moment in the internal hinge** requires. The right side is used in the segment $DB$. The maximum positive moment is $M_D = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, the value $\dfrac{FL}{4}$ of a simply supported beam of span $L = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ with the force at midspan, because the section $CB$ is supported at $C$ and $B$.

The check uses the rotation of the entire beam around $D$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The Gerber beam shown is formed by the beam $AC$, with a pin at $A$, a roller support at $B$ and the $BC$ overhang, and the beam $CD$, connected in $C$ with an internal hinge and resting on the roller support at $D$. The distances are $AB = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, $BC = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, $CD = 3\,\mathrm{m}$, and the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on the entire beam. Calculate reactions and plot diagrams of $T$ and $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,9,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" cerniere="C" carichi="A,D,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,2 m;C,D,3 m" titolo="Gerber beam under a uniform load over its entire length"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,9,0" aste="A-D" cerniere="C" reazioni="A,1.2,0,H_A;A,0,1.2,V_A;B,0,1.2,V_B;D,0,1.2,V_D" forze="3 0,0,-1.5,12 kN;7.5 0,0,-1.5,6 kN" titolo="Free-body diagram showing the load resultants on segments $AC$ and $CD$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
3\,V_D - 6 \cdot 1.5 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B + V_D - 18 = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_B + 9\,V_D - 18 \cdot 4.5 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_D = 3\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_B = \frac{81 - 27}{4} = 13.5\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = 1.5\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{segment } AB,\ 0 < x < 4: \qquad T(x) = 1.5 - 2x , \qquad M(x) = 1.5\,x - x^2
$$
$$
\text{segment } BD,\ 4 < x < 9: \qquad T(x) = 15 - 2x , \qquad M(x) = 3\,(9 - x) - (9 - x)^2 = (x - 6)(9 - x)
$$
$$
M_B = -10\,\mathrm{kN\,m} , \qquad M_C = 0 , \qquad M(0.75) = \frac{9}{16}\,\mathrm{kN\,m} , \qquad M(7.5) = 2.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,9,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" cerniere="C" diagramma="0,0,4,0,-2*s+1.5;4,0,6,0,-2*s+7;6,0,9,0,-2*s+3" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,9,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" cerniere="C" diagramma="0,0,4,0,-s^2+1.5*s;4,0,6,0,-s^2+7*s-10;6,0,9,0,-s^2+3*s" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_D = -V_A \cdot 9 - V_B \cdot 5 + 18 \cdot 4.5 = -13.5 - 67.5 + 81 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The structure has two bodies and $2 + 1 + 2 + 1 = 6$ scalar constraints, so it is statically determinate. Start with the suspended beam $CD$. Taking moments about $C$ for segment $CD$ alone, on which load $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts with moment arm $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and reaction $V_D$ also acts, gives $V_D = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$. Moment equilibrium of the beam about $A$ gives $V_B$, and the vertical translation $V_A$; All reactions are upward.

The load is uniform across the beam and the only concentrated forces are the reactions, so the segments are $AB$ and $BD$; The hinge $C$ does not separate two segments, because no concentrated loads act in it. The left side is used in the segment $AB$; the shear vanishes in $x = 0.75\,\mathrm{m}$, where the moment has a small positive maximum, $\dfrac{9}{16}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. In the segment $BD$, the right side is convenient, with $V_D$ and the load $2\,(9 - x)$. The moment equals $-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on the $B$ mount, vanishes in the hinge $C$ and has the maximum positive $2.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in the middle of $CD$, where the shear vanishes. In $B$ the shear goes from $-6.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $7\,\mathrm{kN}$, with a jump equal to $V_B$.

The check uses the rotation of the whole beam around $D$, an equation not used for reactions.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The Gerber beam shown consists of cantilever $AC$, fixed at $A$ and of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$, and beam $CD$, connected through internal hinge $C$, supported by roller $B$, and extending to overhang $BD$. The distances are $CB = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $BD = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. A uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on $CB$, and a downward force $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $D$. Calculate the reactions, draw the diagrams for $T$ and $M$, and determine the maximum positive bending moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0;D,8,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" carichi="C,B,0.6,0.6,q" forze="D,0,-1.6,F" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,4 m;B,D,2 m" titolo="Gerber beam with a uniform load over $CB$ and a force at the overhang tip"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_B - 12 \cdot 2 - 4 \cdot 6 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - 12 - 4 = 0 \\[4pt]
M_A + 6\,V_B - 12 \cdot 4 - 4 \cdot 8 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
V_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_A = 80 - 72 = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{segment } AC,\ 0 < x < 2: \qquad T = 4\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = 4x - 8
$$
$$
\text{segment } CB,\ 2 < x < 6: \qquad T(x) = 4 - 3\,(x - 2) , \qquad M(x) = 4x - 8 - \frac{3\,(x - 2)^2}{2}
$$
$$
\text{segment } BD,\ 6 < x < 8: \qquad T = F = 4\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = -4\,(8 - x)
$$
$$
T = 0 \iff x = \frac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{m} , \qquad M_{\max} = \frac{40}{3} - 8 - \frac{8}{3} = \frac{8}{3} \approx 2.67\,\mathrm{kN\,m} , \qquad M_B = -8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0;D,8,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" diagramma="0,0,2,0,4;2,0,6,0,-3*s+4;6,0,8,0,4" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0;D,8,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" diagramma="0,0,2,0,4*s-8;2,0,6,0,-1.5*s^2+4*s;6,0,8,0,4*s-8" verso=-1 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_B = M_A - V_A \cdot 6 + 12 \cdot 2 - F \cdot 2 = 8 - 24 + 24 - 8 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The structure has two bodies and $3 + 2 + 1 = 6$ scalar constraints, so it is statically determinate. The auxiliary equation is the rotation around $C$ of the segment $CD$, on which $V_B$ acts, the load $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ with a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the force $F$ with a moment arm of $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and gives $V_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$. They follow $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $M_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise. The cantilever $AC$ carries the force $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ transmitted by the hinge into $C$, so it works like a cantilever with a force at its extreme.

The load starts in $C$, so the segments are $AC$, $CB$ and $BD$. In the segment $AC$ the moment is linear, it equals $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ to fixed support and vanishes in the **hinge $C$**. In the segment $CB$ you add the load $3\,(x - 2)$, and the shear vanishes in $x = \dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$, where the moment has the $\dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ positive maximum, and the moment drops to $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on the $B$ mount. In the overhang the right side contains only $F$. In $B$ the shear goes from $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $4\,\mathrm{kN}$, with a jump equal to $V_B$.

The check uses the rotation of the entire beam around $B$.

</div>
