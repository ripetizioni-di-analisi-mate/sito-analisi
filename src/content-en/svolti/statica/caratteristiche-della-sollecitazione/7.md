## Exercise 1

The L-shaped cantilever shown consists of column $AB$ of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at its base $A$ and beam $BC$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, rigidly connected to the column. At its free end $C$ a vertical force $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward and a horizontal force $P = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. The dashed fibers on the inside of the L are the reference fibers. Draw the diagrams for $N$, $T$, and $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,3,3" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="C,0,-1,F;3.9 3,0.9,0," etichette="3.75,3.3,P" tratteggi="0.12,0 0.12,2.88 3,2.88" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.7;B,C,3 m,0.7" titolo="L-shaped cantilever with forces $F$ and $P$ at its free end; dashed lines indicate the reference fibers"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + P = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A - F = 0 \\[4pt]
M_A - 3\,F - 3\,P = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
H_A = -2\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_A = 12 + 6 = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{column } AB,\ 0 < s < 3: \qquad N = -V_A = -4\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad T = -H_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(s) = -M_A + 2s = -18 + 2s
$$
$$
\text{crossbeam } BC,\ 0 < x < 3: \qquad N = P = 2\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad T = F = 4\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = -F\,(3 - x) = -4\,(3 - x)
$$
$$
M(A) = -18\,\mathrm{kN\,m} , \qquad M(B) = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,3,3" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro" diagramma="0,3,0,0,-4;0,3,3,3,2" segni=1 riferimento=0 titolo="Axial force diagram $N$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,3,3" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro" diagramma="0,0,0,3,2;0,3,3,3,4" segni=1 titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,3,3" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro" diagramma="0,0,0,3,2*s-18;0,3,3,3,4*s-12" verso=-1 riferimento=0 titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_C = M_A + H_A \cdot 3 - V_A \cdot 3 = 18 - 6 - 12 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The fixed support supplies $H_A$, $V_A$ and $M_A$. About $A$, force $F$ has a horizontal moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$, and force $P$ has a vertical moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Both forces produce clockwise moments, so the reaction moment $M_A = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts counterclockwise. The horizontal reaction $H_A = -2\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the left.

It runs through the structure from $A$ to $C$; the dotted fibers stand to the right of the travel direction, as the definition of **reference fibers** requires. In the column, with $s$ measured by $A$, the part preceding the section is used, i.e. the lower section with the fixed support reactions; for observation on $\alpha = 90^\circ$ inclined members we have $N = -V_A$ and $T = -H_A$. The moment about the section of $M_A$ and $H_A$, which has a moment arm of $s$, equals $18 - 2s$, then $M = -18 + 2s$. The column is in compression, and the negative moment indicates that the **the outer fibers** are in tension to the left of the column. In crossbeam it is convenient the part that follows the section, with only forces in $C$: $P$ pulls the crossbeam, which is in tension, $F$ gives the shear and the moment, negative, with the upper fibers in tension.

In $B$ the moment equals $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ both at the top of the column and at the beginning of the crossbeam, with the outer fibers in tension in both, as the theorem on **Hard joint** provides. In the joint the axle force of the column, $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$, becomes the shear of the crossbeam changed by sign, and the shear of the column, $2\,\mathrm{kN}$, becomes the axle force of the crossbeam, because the two members are orthogonal.

The check uses moment equilibrium about $C$: $H_A$, left to $3\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C$, and $V_A$, up to $3\,\mathrm{m}$ left of $C$, have clockwise moments that balance $M_A$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In the frame shown, columns $AB$ and $DC$ have height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and beam $BC$ has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The frame has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $D$. A downward force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $E$, the midpoint of the beam, and a rightward horizontal force $P = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $B$. Taking the inner fibers as the reference fibers, draw the diagrams for $N$, $T$, and $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;E,3,4;C,6,4;D,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="E,0,-1.4,F;B,1.2,0,P" tratteggi="0.15,0 0.15,3.85 5.85,3.85 5.85,0" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,E,3 m,0.7;E,C,3 m,0.7" titolo="Portal frame with vertical force $F$ and horizontal force $P$; dashed lines indicate the reference fibers"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + P = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_D - F = 0 \\[4pt]
6\,V_D - 3\,F - 4\,P = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
H_A = -3\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_D = \frac{24 + 12}{6} = 6\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{column } AB,\ 0 < s < 4: \qquad N = -V_A = -2\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad T = -H_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(s) = 3s
$$
$$
\text{crossbeam } BE,\ 0 < x < 3: \qquad N = 0 , \qquad T = V_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = 12 + 2x

$$
\text{crossbeam } EC,\ 3 < x < 6: \qquad N = 0 , \qquad T = -V_D = -6\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(x) = 6\,(6 - x)
$$
$$
\text{column } CD: \qquad N = -V_D = -6\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad T = 0 , \qquad M = 0
$$
$$
M_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m} , \qquad M_E = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_E = H_A \cdot 4 - V_A \cdot 3 + V_D \cdot 3 = -12 - 6 + 18 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, because the roller support at $D$ only gives a vertical reaction. In the rotation around $A$ the force $F$ has a moment arm of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and the force $P$, applied at $B$, has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$; both have clockwise moment. $V_D = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ follow, upward.

You go through the portal frame from $A$ to $D$, with the fibers dashed inside. In column $AB$ the part preceding the section contains $H_A$ and $V_A$; the column is in compression by $V_A$ and, since $H_A$ has a moment arm of $s$, the moment is $3s$, positive, with **in tension the inner fibers**. In crossbeam the part preceding the section contains $H_A$, $V_A$ and $P$: the two horizontal forces balance, then $N = 0$; the clockwise moment of clockwise moment about the section is that of $H_A$, with a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$, plus that of $V_A$, with a moment arm of $x$, i.e. $12 + 2x$. After $E$ the part following the section, with the $V_D$ alone, agrees. In column $CD$, the reaction $V_D$ acts along the axis, so the column is only in compression.

In $B$ the moment equals $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at the top of the column and at the beginning of the crossbeam, as the theorem on **Hard joint** provides; the maximum is $M_E = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, below the force $F$, where the diagram has a corner. In $C$ the moment is zero.

Check by taking moments about $E$, where $F$ has zero moment. Force $H_A$, acting leftward a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ below $E$, and $V_A$ produce clockwise moments. The moment of $V_D$ is counterclockwise.

</div>

## Exercise 3

In the frame shown, column $AB$ has height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and is pinned at $A$. Beam $BC$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Column $CD$ has height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and rests on a roller at $D$ on a horizontal surface. A uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on the beam, and a rightward horizontal force $P = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $B$. Taking the inner fibers as the reference fibers, draw the diagrams for $N$, $T$, and $M$ and calculate the maximum bending moment in the beam.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,4,1" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" carichi="B,C,0.5,0.5,q" forze="B,1,0,P" tratteggi="0.13,0 0.13,3.87 3.87,3.87 3.87,1" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,C,4 m,-1.2;D,C,3 m,0.7" titolo="Frame with columns of unequal height, a uniform load on the crossbeam, and horizontal force $P$"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + P = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_D - 12 = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_D - 12 \cdot 2 - 4\,P = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
H_A = -4\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_D = \frac{24 + 16}{4} = 10\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{column } AB,\ 0 < s < 4: \qquad N = -2\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad T = 4\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad M(s) = 4s
$$
$$
\text{crossbeam } BC,\ 0 < x < 4: \qquad N = 0 , \qquad T(x) = 2 - 3x , \qquad M(x) = 16 + 2x - \frac{3x^2}{2}
$$
$$
\text{column } CD: \qquad N = -10\,\mathrm{kN} , \qquad T = 0 , \qquad M = 0
$$
$$
T = 0 \iff x = \frac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{m} , \qquad M_{\max} = 16 + \frac{4}{3} - \frac{2}{3} = \frac{50}{3} \approx 16.67\,\mathrm{kN\,m} , \qquad M_C = 16 + 8 - 24 = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,4,1" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" diagramma="0,4,0,0,-2;0,4,4,4,0;4,1,4,4,-10" segni=1 titolo="Axial force diagram $N$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,4,1" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" diagramma="0,0,0,4,4;0,4,4,4,-3*s+2;4,4,4,1,0" segni=1 numeri=0 etichette="-0.55,2,4;0.12,4.42,2;3.55,3.05,−10" titolo="Shear diagram $T$ ($\mathrm{kN}$)"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,4,1" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" diagramma="0,0,0,4,4*s;0,4,4,4,-1.5*s^2+2*s+16;4,4,4,1,0" verso=-1 scala=0.03 numeri=0 etichette="-0.35,3.7,16;0.75,3.25,16.67" titolo="Bending moment diagram $M$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$)"}

$$
\text{check:} \quad \sum M_C = H_A \cdot 4 - V_A \cdot 4 + 12 \cdot 2 = -16 - 8 + 24 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the rotation around $A$ counts the horizontal distance of $D$ from $A$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$, because $V_D$ is vertical; the height of column $CD$ does not enter the equations. The force $P$ has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$. You get $H_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, $V_D = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.

You walk the frame from $A$ to $D$, with the fibers dashed inside. Moving up column $AB$ from the base, the portion before the section contains $H_A$ and $V_A$; Therefore, for observation on the inclined members with $\alpha = 90^\circ$, $N = -V_A$ and $T = -H_A$, and the moment is $4s$, with the inner fibers in tension; $B$ is $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. In crossbeam the force $P$ balances $H_A$, then $N = 0$; the moment starts from $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, as the theorem on **Hard joint** requires, and has the shear $2 - 3x$, which vanishes in $x = \dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$, where the moment has the **maximum $\dfrac{50}{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$**. In $C$ the moment vanishes, because the column $CD$ only carries the vertical reaction $V_D$, directed along its axis, and is therefore only in compression.

Check by taking moments about $C$. Force $V_D$ passes through $C$, and the line of action of $P$ passes through $C$, leaving the clockwise moments of $H_A$ and $V_A$ and the counterclockwise moment of the load.

</div>
