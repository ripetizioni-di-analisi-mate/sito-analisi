## Exercise 1

Beam $AB$ has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and carries a downward force $F_1 = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ at a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, an upward force $F_2 = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$, and a counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Find the resultant and the resultant moment about $A$. Use the change-of-reference-point formula to find the resultant moment about $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" forze="2 0,0,-1.5,F_1;B,0,1.5,F_2" coppie="4 0,1,ℳ" quote="A,2 0,2 m,1.9;2 0,4 0,2 m,1.9;4 0,B,2 m,1.9" titolo="Beam under forces $F_1$ and $F_2$ and concentrated couple $\mathcal{M}$"}

$$
R_x = 0 ,
\qquad
R_y = -9 + 3 = -6\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
M_A = 2 \cdot (-9) + 6 \cdot 3 + 6 = -18 + 18 + 6 = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
M_B = M_A + (x_A - x_B)\,R_y - (y_A - y_B)\,R_x = 6 + (0 - 6) \cdot (-6) - 0 = 42\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad M_B = (2 - 6) \cdot (-9) + (6 - 6) \cdot 3 + 6 = 36 + 0 + 6 = 42\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The forces are vertical, so the **resultant** has only the vertical component, sum of the components with the sign: $-9\,\mathrm{kN}$ by $F_1$, directed downwards, and $+3\,\mathrm{kN}$ by $F_2$. About $A$, force $F_1$ has moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and produces a clockwise moment. Force $F_2$ has moment arm $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and produces a counterclockwise moment. The couple contributes with its $\mathcal{M} = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ moment by the theorem on **moment of a couple**, which does not depend on the reference point or where the couple is drawn.

The **change of reference point formula** gives $M_B$ with only $M_A$ and $R_y$, because $A$ and $B$ stand on the axis $x$ and the term with $R_x$ is zero. The check calculates $M_B$ directly: the force $F_1$, down to $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$, has counterclockwise moment $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, the force $F_2$ is applied at $B$ and has zero moment. The system has **resultant of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ down**, $M_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $M_B = 42\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, both counterclockwise.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The rectangular plate $ABCD$ shown has points $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)$, $C = (4, 3)$, and $D = (0, 3)$, with coordinates in meters. A rightward force $F_1 = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $A$, a downward force $F_2 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$, and a leftward force $F_3 = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $D$. A clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ also acts on the plate. Find the resultant and its moments about $A$ and about the plate center $G = (2;\, 1.5)$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,3 0,3" nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,4,3;D,0,3" forze="A,1.3,0,F_1;C,0,-1.3,F_2;-1.3 3,-1.3,0,F_3" coppie="2 1.5,-1,ℳ" punti="2,1.5,G" quote="A,B,4 m,0.7;B,C,3 m,0.7" titolo="Plate under three forces and a clockwise couple"}

$$
R_x = 5 - 3 = 2\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
R_y = -6\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
M_A &= \underbrace{0}_{F_1} + \underbrace{4 \cdot (-6) - 3 \cdot 0}_{F_2} + \underbrace{0 \cdot 0 - 3 \cdot (-3)}_{F_3} - 8 \\[6pt]
&= -24 + 9 - 8 = -23\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
M_G = M_A + (x_A - x_G)\,R_y - (y_A - y_G)\,R_x = -23 + (-2) \cdot (-6) - (-1.5) \cdot 2 = -23 + 12 + 3 = -8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{check:} \quad M_G &= \underbrace{-(0 - 1.5) \cdot 5}_{F_1} + \underbrace{(4 - 2) \cdot (-6)}_{F_2} + \underbrace{-(3 - 1.5) \cdot (-3)}_{F_3} - 8 \\[6pt]
&= 7.5 - 12 + 4.5 - 8 = -8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The **resultant** is obtained by adding the horizontal components, $+5\,\mathrm{kN}$ for $F_1$ and $-3\,\mathrm{kN}$ for $F_3$, and the vertical components, where only $F_2$ appears. At the moment about $A$ the force $F_1$ has zero moment, because it is applied in the reference point. The force $F_2$, down to $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $A$, has clockwise moment $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$; the force $F_3$, to the left at $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$, has counterclockwise moment $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The clockwise couple enters with $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The $G$ reference point has $(2;\, 1.5)$ coordinates, so the $x_A - x_G = -2$ and $y_A - y_G = -1.5$ differences appear in the **change of reference point formula**. In verification each force has its own moment arm about $G$: $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ for the two horizontal forces and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ for $F_2$. The two horizontal forces both tend to rotate the plate counterclockwise around $G$, because $F_1$ pushes to the right below the center and $F_3$ pushes to the left above the center. The system has **resultant $\vec{R} = (2, -6)\,\mathrm{kN}$**, with $M_A = -23\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $M_G = -8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, both times.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The frame shown has joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (0, 4)$, $C = (5, 4)$, and $D = (5, 0)$, with coordinates in meters. A force $F_1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right at $B$, a force $F_2 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward at midpoint $E$ of beam $BC$, and a force $F_3 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$, downward and to the right, parallel to vector $(3, -4)$. Find the resultant and the resultant moments about $A$ and $D$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;E,2.5,4;C,5,4,-;D,5,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" forze="B,1.3,0,F_1;E,0,-1.3,F_2;5.78 2.96,0.78,-1.04,F_3" quote="B,E,2.5 m,-0.8;E,C,2.5 m,-0.8;C,D,4 m,0.8" etichette="4.72,3.65,C" titolo="Frame under force $F_1$ at $B$, force $F_2$ at $E$, and inclined force $F_3$ at $C$"}

$$
F_{3,x} = 10 \cdot \frac{3}{5} = 6\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
F_{3,y} = 10 \cdot \frac{-4}{5} = -8\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
R_x = 10 + 6 = 16\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
R_y = -20 - 8 = -28\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
M_A &= \underbrace{-4 \cdot 10}_{F_1} + \underbrace{2.5 \cdot (-20)}_{F_2} + \underbrace{5 \cdot (-8) - 4 \cdot 6}_{F_3} \\[6pt]
&= -40 - 50 - 64 = -154\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
M_D = M_A + (x_A - x_D)\,R_y - (y_A - y_D)\,R_x = -154 + (0 - 5) \cdot (-28) - 0 = -14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{check:} \quad M_D &= \underbrace{-4 \cdot 10}_{F_1} + \underbrace{(2.5 - 5) \cdot (-20)}_{F_2} + \underbrace{0 \cdot (-8) - 4 \cdot 6}_{F_3} \\[6pt]
&= -40 + 50 - 24 = -14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The vector $(3, -4)$ has magnitude $5$, so the components of $F_3$ are $\dfrac{3}{5}$ and $-\dfrac{4}{5}$ of its intensity. The **resultant** adds the horizontal components of $F_1$ and $F_3$ and the vertical components of $F_2$ and $F_3$. At the moment about $A$ the force $F_1$, to the right at $4\,\mathrm{m}$ over $A$, has clockwise moment; the force $F_2$, down to $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $A$, also has clockwise moment. For $F_3$ the formula of the **moment of a force** is used in components, with $x_C = 5\,\mathrm{m}$ and $y_C = 4\,\mathrm{m}$.

The moment about $D$ is achieved with the **change of reference point formula**. Points $A$ and $D$ have the same vertical coordinate, so only $R_y$ enters the formula. The check recalculates $M_D$ force by force. About $D$ the force $F_2$ stands to the left of the reference point and has counterclockwise moment, and the vertical component of $F_3$ has zero moment arm, because $C$ lies on the vertical line through $D$. The two calculations coincide. The system has **resultant $\vec{R} = (16, -28)\,\mathrm{kN}$**, $\sqrt{16^2 + 28^2} = 32.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ intensity, and $M_A = -154\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $M_D = -14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ times, times.

</div>
