## Exercise 1

On the beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, the downward force $F_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, the downward force $F_2 = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, the force $F_3 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ up at $B$ and the $\mathcal{M} = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise couple act. Reduce the system to a single force, determining the line of action.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" forze="1 0,0,-1.5,F_1;4 0,0,-1.5,F_2;B,0,1.2,F_3" coppie="2.5 0,1,ℳ" quote="A,1 0,1 m,1.6;1 0,4 0,3 m,1.6;4 0,B,2 m,1.6" titolo="Beam under three vertical forces and a couple"}

$$
R_x = 0 ,
\qquad
R_y = -12 - 18 + 6 = -24\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
M_A = 1 \cdot (-12) + 4 \cdot (-18) + 6 \cdot 6 + 6 = -12 - 72 + 36 + 6 = -42\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
x\,R_y = M_A
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x = \frac{M_A}{R_y} = \frac{-42}{-24} = 1.75\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad M_B = (1 - 6) \cdot (-12) + (4 - 6) \cdot (-18) + 0 + 6 = 60 + 36 + 6 = 102\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
(1.75 - 6) \cdot (-24) = 102\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The resultant is nonzero, so **reduction of a planar force system** gives a single equivalent force equal to that resultant. All forces are vertical, so $R_x = 0$ and the resultant also acts vertically. Find its horizontal coordinate by requiring the resultant applied at $(x, 0)$ to give the same moment about $A$ as the original system. About $A$ the two downward forces act clockwise, while the force at $B$ and the applied couple act counterclockwise.

The couple contributes no resultant force, but its moment changes the position of the line of action. Without it the moment would be $-48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and the line of action would pass $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. For a check, compare the original moment about $B$ with that of the resultant acting $1.75\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, or $4.25\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$. The values agree. The system is equivalent to **a downward force of $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting $1.75\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

On the rectangular plate in the figure, with $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)$, $C = (4, 3)$ and $D = (0, 3)$ in meters, act the force $F_1 = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right at $A$, the force $F_2 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting upwards at $B$ and the force $F_3 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$, directed to the left and down, parallel to the vector $(-3, -4)$. Reduce the system to one force: determine intensity and direction of the resultant, the equation of its line of action and the distance of this line from $A$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,3 0,3" nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,4,3;D,0,3" forze="A,1.3,0,F_1;B,0,1.3,F_2;C,-0.78,-1.04,F_3" quote="A,B,4 m,1.6;B,C,3 m,0.7" titolo="Plate under forces $F_1$ and $F_2$ and inclined force $F_3$"}

$$
F_{3,x} = 10 \cdot \frac{-3}{5} = -6\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
F_{3,y} = 10 \cdot \frac{-4}{5} = -8\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
R_x = 4 - 6 = -2\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
R_y = 6 - 8 = -2\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
R = \sqrt{2^2 + 2^2} = 2\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN} = 2.83\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
M_A = \underbrace{0}_{F_1} + \underbrace{4 \cdot 6}_{F_2} + \underbrace{4 \cdot (-8) - 3 \cdot (-6)}_{F_3} = 24 - 32 + 18 = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
x\,R_y - y\,R_x = M_A
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
-2x + 2y = 10
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
y = x + 5
$$
$$
d = \frac{|M_A|}{R} = \frac{10}{2\sqrt{2}}\,\mathrm{m} = 3.54\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad M_B = \underbrace{0}_{F_1} + \underbrace{0}_{F_2} + \underbrace{0 \cdot (-8) - 3 \cdot (-6)}_{F_3} = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
\underbrace{(0 - 4) \cdot (-2) - (5 - 0) \cdot (-2)}_{\vec{R} \text{ in } (0,\,5)} = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The components of $F_3$ follow from unit vector $\Big( -\dfrac{3}{5}, -\dfrac{4}{5} \Big)$ because vector $(-3, -4)$ has magnitude $5$. The resultant has equal and negative components, so it is directed to the left and down, at $45^\circ$ below the horizontal. At the moment about $A$ the force $F_1$ is applied in the reference point, $F_2$ has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and counterclockwise moment, and for $F_3$ the formula is used in components with $C$ coordinates.

By **reduction of a planar force system**, the resultant can act at any point $(x, y)$ where $\vec{R}$ produces moment $M_A$ about $A$. These points lie on line $y = x + 5$, which is parallel to $\vec{R}$ as required. The line passes through $(0, 5)$ and $(-5, 0)$, so the resultant may act at either point, even outside the plate. For a check, apply the resultant at $(0, 5)$ and compare its moment about $B$ with that of the original system. The equivalent load is **a force of $2.83\,\mathrm{kN}$ directed down and to the left at $45^\circ$, with line of action $y = x + 5$**. This line lies $3.54\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

On the rectangular plate in the figure, with $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)$, $C = (4, 3)$ and $D = (0, 3)$ in meters, act the force $F_1 = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right at $A$, the force $F_2 = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left at $D$, the force $F_3 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward at $B$ and the force $F_4 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at point $E = (1, 0)$. Reduce the system to the simplest form.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,3 0,3" nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,4,3;D,0,3;E,1,0" forze="A,1.3,0,F_1;-1.3 3,-1.3,0,F_2;B,0,1.3,F_3;1 -1.3,0,-1.3,F_4" quote="A,E,1 m,2;E,B,3 m,2;B,C,3 m,0.7" titolo="Four forces on a plate, forming two equal and opposite pairs"}

$$
R_x = 5 - 5 = 0 ,
\qquad
R_y = 8 - 8 = 0
$$
$$
M_A = \underbrace{0}_{F_1} + \underbrace{-3 \cdot (-5)}_{F_2} + \underbrace{4 \cdot 8}_{F_3} + \underbrace{1 \cdot (-8)}_{F_4} = 15 + 32 - 8 = 39\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad M_C = \underbrace{-(0 - 3) \cdot 5}_{F_1} + \underbrace{0}_{F_2} + \underbrace{0}_{F_3} + \underbrace{(1 - 4) \cdot (-8)}_{F_4} = 15 + 24 = 39\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The resultant force is zero but the moment about $A$ is nonzero. By **reduction of a planar force system**, the system is equivalent to a couple whose moment is the same about any point. This also follows directly from the forces. The opposite forces $F_1$ and $F_2$ act on horizontal lines $3\,\mathrm{m}$ apart, forming a counterclockwise couple of moment $5 \cdot 3 = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The opposite forces $F_3$ and $F_4$ act on vertical lines $3\,\mathrm{m}$ apart, forming a counterclockwise couple of moment $8 \cdot 3 = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. By the theorem on the **moment of a couple**, the two moments add.

About $A$, force $F_2$ acts to the left at a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$ and produces a counterclockwise moment. Force $F_4$ acts downward a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $A$ and produces a clockwise moment. Recalculating about $C$ gives the same moment, as the change-of-reference-point formula predicts when $\vec{R} = \vec{0}$. The system is equivalent to **a counterclockwise couple of moment $39\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$**.

</div>

## Exercise 4

Beam $AB$ has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and carries a uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on segment $AC$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. A downward force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at point $E$ a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. A horizontal force $H = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right at $B$, and a counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at $C$. Reduce this system to a single force and find where its line of action intersects the beam axis.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;E,5,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" carichi="A,C,0.5,0.5,q" forze="E,0,-1.5,F;7.3 0,1.3,0,H" coppie="C,1,ℳ" quote="A,C,4 m,0.8;C,E,1 m,0.8;E,B,1 m,0.8" titolo="Beam under a uniform load, vertical and horizontal forces, and a couple"}

$$
Q = q \cdot 4 = 16\,\mathrm{kN} \;\text{ in }\; x = 2\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
R_x = 5\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
R_y = -16 - 8 = -24\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
R = \sqrt{5^2 + 24^2} = \sqrt{601}\,\mathrm{kN} = 24.52\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
M_A = 2 \cdot (-16) + 5 \cdot (-8) - 0 \cdot 5 + 12 = -32 - 40 + 12 = -60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
x\,R_y - y\,R_x = M_A
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
-24x - 5y = -60
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
24x + 5y = 60
$$
$$
y = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x = \frac{60}{24} = 2.5\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad M_B = (2 - 6) \cdot (-16) + (5 - 6) \cdot (-8) + 0 + 12 = 64 + 8 + 12 = 84\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
(2.5 - 6) \cdot (-24) - (0 - 0) \cdot 5 = 84\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Replace the uniform load by its resultant using the rules for **standard distributed loads**. It acts downward with magnitude $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the midpoint of $AC$. The horizontal force $H$ acts along the beam axis through $A$, so its moment about $A$ is zero and it contributes only to $R_x$. The couple contributes only to the moment.

The resultant has components $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. By **reduction of a planar force system**, its line of action is $24x + 5y = 60$. This line meets the beam axis $y = 0$ at a distance $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, so the resultant can be applied there. Its angle with the horizontal has tangent $\dfrac{24}{5}$ and is therefore $78.2^\circ$. Check by comparing the moment about $B$ for the original system and for the resultant applied at $(2.5;\, 0)$. The equivalent load is **a force of magnitude $24.52\,\mathrm{kN}$, with components $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, acting on the beam $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$**.

</div>
