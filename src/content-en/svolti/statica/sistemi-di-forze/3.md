## Exercise 1

Beam $AB$ of length $7\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a uniform load $q_1 = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on segment $AC$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and a triangular load on segment $CB$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$. The triangular load increases from zero at $C$ to $q_2 = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. Find the combined resultant, its line of action, and the moment of the loads about $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,7,0" aste="A-B" carichi="A,C,0.5,0.5,q_1;C,B,0,0.75,q_2" quote="A,C,4 m,0.8;C,B,3 m,0.8" titolo="Uniform load $q_1$ on $AC$ and triangular load varying from $0$ to $q_2$ on $CB$"}

$$
Q_1 = q_1 \cdot 4 = 16\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
x_1 = \frac{4}{2} = 2\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
Q_2 = \frac{q_2 \cdot 3}{2} = 9\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
x_2 = 4 + \frac{2}{3} \cdot 3 = 6\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
Q = Q_1 + Q_2 = 25\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
x_Q = \frac{Q_1 x_1 + Q_2 x_2}{Q} = \frac{16 \cdot 2 + 9 \cdot 6}{25} = \frac{86}{25} = 3.44\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
M_A = -Q_1 x_1 - Q_2 x_2 = -32 - 54 = -86\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad M_B = (2 - 7) \cdot (-16) + (6 - 7) \cdot (-9) = 80 + 9 = 89\,\mathrm{kN\,m} = (3.44 - 7) \cdot (-25)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two segments have loads of different shape, so the result of each with the **significant loads** is calculated first. The uniform load has resultant the same area as the rectangle, $4 \cdot 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, applied in the middle of the segment. The resultant of the triangular load equals the area of the triangle. It acts two thirds of the way from the unloaded end $C$, a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$, where the load is greatest.

The resulting two are parallel downward forces, so for **center of parallel forces** the overall result is their sum and passes through the average of the weighted horizontal coordinates with the intensities. The moment about $A$ is clockwise because all the loads act downward to the right of $A$. As a check, the moment is calculated about $B$ in two ways, with the two resultants partial and with the overall result, and the two calculations give $89\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The resultant is **$25\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward**, to **$3.44\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$**, and the moment of loads about $A$ is **$-86\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Beam $AB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a trapezoidal load of intensity $q_1 = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$ and $q_2 = 9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. Use the trapezoidal-load formula to find the resultant and its position. Check by splitting the load into uniform and triangular parts.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" carichi="A,B,0.3,0.9,q" quote="A,B,6 m,0.8" etichette="-0.15,0.55,q_1;6.15,1.15,q_2" titolo="Trapezoidal load varying from $q_1 = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $q_2 = 9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

$$
Q = \frac{(q_1 + q_2)\,L}{2} = \frac{(3 + 9) \cdot 6}{2} = 36\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
x_Q = \frac{(q_1 + 2q_2)\,L}{3\,(q_1 + q_2)} = \frac{(3 + 18) \cdot 6}{3 \cdot 12} = \frac{126}{36} = 3.5\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
Q_r = 3 \cdot 6 = 18\,\mathrm{kN} \;\text{ in }\; x = 3\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
Q_t = \frac{(9 - 3) \cdot 6}{2} = 18\,\mathrm{kN} \;\text{ in }\; x = 4\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
Q_r + Q_t = 36\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
\frac{18 \cdot 3 + 18 \cdot 4}{36} = \frac{126}{36} = 3.5\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad M_B = (3 - 6) \cdot (-18) + (4 - 6) \cdot (-18) = 54 + 36 = 90\,\mathrm{kN\,m} = (3.5 - 6) \cdot (-36)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The trapezoidal load formula is from the **Resultant of a distributed load** applied to $q(x) = q_1 + (q_2 - q_1)\dfrac{x}{L}$: the intensity is the area of the trapezoid and the line of action passes through its centroid. The line of action stands to the right of the center, on the side where the load is most intense.

For control the trapezoid breaks down into a rectangle $q_1 = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ height and a triangle growing from zero at $A$ to $q_2 - q_1 = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. The rectangle has resultant the middle, the triangle two thirds of the span from $A$; the two parts have the same resultant, $18\,\mathrm{kN}$, so the center lies halfway between $3$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The check compares the moment against $B$ of the two partial results with that of the overall result. The load is equivalent to **$36\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, $3.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The distributed load on beam $AB$ of length $7\,\mathrm{m}$ increases linearly from zero at $A$ to $q_0 = 8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at point $C$, a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, then decreases linearly to zero at $B$. Find the resultant, its position, and the moment of the load about $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,7,0" aste="A-B" carichi="A,C,0,1,q_0;C,B,1,0," quote="A,C,3 m,0.8;C,B,4 m,0.8" titolo="Triangular load with peak intensity $q_0$ at $C$"}

$$
Q_1 = \frac{8 \cdot 3}{2} = 12\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
x_1 = \frac{2}{3} \cdot 3 = 2\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
Q_2 = \frac{8 \cdot 4}{2} = 16\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
x_2 = 3 + \frac{1}{3} \cdot 4 = \frac{13}{3}\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
Q = 12 + 16 = 28\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
x_Q = \frac{12 \cdot 2 + 16 \cdot \dfrac{13}{3}}{28} = \frac{\dfrac{280}{3}}{28} = \frac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{m} = 3.33\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
M_A = -Q\,x_Q = -28 \cdot \frac{10}{3} = -93.33\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad M_B = (2 - 7) \cdot (-12) + \Big( \frac{13}{3} - 7 \Big) \cdot (-16) = 60 + \frac{128}{3} = \frac{308}{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m} = \Big( \frac{10}{3} - 7 \Big) \cdot (-28)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The load diagram is a triangle with the base vertices at $A$ and $B$ and the top vertex above $C$. It is divided into two rectangular triangles, $AC$ and $CB$, and for each the **triangular load** is used: the resulting area is the area and passes to a third of the base from the extreme in which the load is maximum, that is, from $C$. In the first segment the point is $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$ to $A$, in the second a distance $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$ to $B$.

The resulting two are composed with the **center of parallel forces**. The axis $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ is the average of the three vertices' axes, $\dfrac{0 + 3 + 7}{3}$, as it must be, because the line of action passes through the centroid of the diagram and the centroid of a triangle is the average of the vertices. The check compares the moment with the calculated $B$ with the two partial results and the overall result. The load is equivalent to **$28\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, $3.33\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$**, with moment about $A$ of **$-93.33\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise**.

</div>
