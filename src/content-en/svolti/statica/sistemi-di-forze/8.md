## Exercise 1

On the rectangular plate in the figure, with $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)$, $C = (4, 3)$ and $D = (0, 3)$ in meters, the force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ down at point $E = (2, 3)$ and the force $H = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right at $D$ act. Determine a horizontal $H_A$ force and a vertical $V_A$ force applied at $A$ and a vertical $V_B$ force applied at $B$ which, added to the given forces, form a balanced system.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,3 0,3" nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,4,3;D,0,3;E,2,3" forze="E,0,-1.3,F;D,1.3,0,H" reazioni="A,1.2,0,H_A;A,0,1.2,V_A;B,0,1.2,V_B" quote="A,B,4 m,1.8;B,C,3 m,0.7" titolo="Plate with given forces $F$ and $H$ and unknown forces $H_A$, $V_A$, and $V_B$ shown in a second color"}

$$
\begin{cases}
H_A + 4 = 0 \\[4pt]
V_A + V_B - 10 = 0 \\[4pt]
4\,V_B + 2 \cdot (-10) - 3 \cdot 4 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
$$
H_A = -4\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_B = \frac{20 + 12}{4} = 8\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
V_A = 10 - 8 = 2\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad M_B = \underbrace{(0 - 4) \cdot 2}_{V_A} + \underbrace{(2 - 4) \cdot (-10)}_{F} + \underbrace{-(3 - 0) \cdot 4}_{H} + \underbrace{0}_{H_A,\ V_B} = -8 + 20 - 12 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The unknown forces assume positive according to the axes, $H_A$ to the right and $V_A$, $V_B$ upward. For the definition of **balanced system** the resultant and moment about a reference point must be null, and in the plane these conditions are the three equations of the system: horizontal force equilibrium, vertical force equilibrium and moment equilibrium about $A$. The $A$ reference point is the point of application of two unknowns, so the equation of moments contains only the $V_B$. In the moment $V_B$ equation it has a moment arm of $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and counterclockwise moment, $F$ has a moment arm of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and a clockwise moment, and $H$, to the right at $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$, has clockwise moment.

Each equation contains only one unknown, once $V_B$ is known, and the system resolves in order. The negative sign of $H_A$ indicates that the force has opposite to the assumed one. The check uses the equation of moments about $B$, which has not been used, and finds zero. The forces sought are **$H_A = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left**, **$V_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$, Upward**, and **$V_B = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, Upward**. They are the reactions that a hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ exert on the plate, as seen in the argument about support reactions.

</div>

## Exercise 2

On member $AB$ in the figure, with $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (6, 0)$ in meters, the load $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downwards at point $C = (3, 0)$. At $B$ you want to apply a force with line of action tilted by $45^\circ$, which rises to the left, and at $A$ a directional force to be determined. Find intensity and towards the two forces for the three forces to form a balanced system.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" forze="C,0,-1.5,P" tratteggi="6.8,-0.8 2,4" quote="A,C,3 m,0.8;C,B,3 m,0.8" etichette="5.15,0.3,45°" titolo="Member under load $P$ with the prescribed line of action of the force at $B$"}

$$
\text{line of } P:\; x = 3 ,
\qquad
\text{line of action of the force at } B:\; y = 6 - x
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
K = (3, 3)
$$
$$
\vec{F}_A = s\,(1, 1) ,
\qquad
\vec{F}_B = t\,(-1, 1)
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
s - t = 0 \\[4pt]
s + t - 12 = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
s = t = 6\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\vec{F}_A = (6, 6)\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
\vec{F}_B = (-6, 6)\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
F_A = F_B = 6\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{kN} = 8.49\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad M_A = \underbrace{3 \cdot (-12)}_{P} + \underbrace{6 \cdot 6 - 0 \cdot (-6)}_{F_B} + \underbrace{0}_{F_A} = -36 + 36 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The lines of action of $P$ and force at $B$ are known and meet at point $K = (3, 3)$. For the **three forces in equilibrium** theorem, the line of action of the force at $A$ also passes through $K$, so the force at $A$ has the direction of the segment $AK$, i.e. the vector $(1, 1)$. The two unknown forces are then written with only one parameter each, $s$ and $t$, positive if the forces have the direction of the indicated vectors.

The two translation equations, horizontal and vertical, determine $s$ and $t$; the equation of moments about $K$ is already satisfied, because the three lines pass by $K$. The two parameters are positive, so the force at $A$ is directed right and up and the force at $B$ to the left and up, both at $45^\circ$. The figure is symmetrical about the vertical through $C$, and the two forces have the same intensity. The check uses the equation of moments about $A$, which has not been written. The forces are **$8.49\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $A$, up right**, and **$8.49\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$, up left**, both at $45^\circ$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

On the rectangular plate in the figure, with $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (6, 0)$, $C = (6, 4)$ in meters and the fourth vertex in $(0, 4)$, the force $F = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ down at point $D = (3, 4)$ and the force $H = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ right at point $E = (0, 2)$ act. Determine the intensities of three forces that balance the system: vertical $X_1$ applied at $A$, $X_2$ applied at $B$ and parallel to vector $(-3, 4)$, horizontal $X_3$ applied at $C$. Write three equations of moment, each with only one unknown.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,4 0,4" nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,6,4;D,3,4;E,0,2" forze="D,0,-1.4,F;E,1.3,0,H" tratteggi="0,-1 0,8.6;6.75,-1 -0.45,8.6;6,4 7.6,4;0,4 6,4" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9;B,C,4 m,0.8" titolo="Plate with the given forces and the lines of action of the three unknown forces shown dashed"}

$$
K_1 = (0, 4) ,
\qquad
K_2 = (3, 4) ,
\qquad
K_3 = (0, 8)
$$
$$
\vec{X}_1 = (0,\, X_1) ,
\qquad
\vec{X}_2 = \Big( -\frac{3}{5} X_2,\, \frac{4}{5} X_2 \Big) ,
\qquad
\vec{X}_3 = (X_3,\, 0)
$$
$$
M_{K_1} = \underbrace{3 \cdot (-20)}_{F} + \underbrace{-(2 - 4) \cdot 6}_{H} + \underbrace{6 \cdot \frac{4}{5} X_2 - (0 - 4) \cdot \Big( -\frac{3}{5} X_2 \Big)}_{X_2} = -48 + \frac{12}{5}\,X_2 = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
X_2 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
M_{K_2} = \underbrace{-(2 - 4) \cdot 6}_{H} + \underbrace{(0 - 3)\,X_1}_{X_1} = 12 - 3\,X_1 = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
X_1 = 4\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
M_{K_3} = \underbrace{3 \cdot (-20)}_{F} + \underbrace{-(2 - 8) \cdot 6}_{H} + \underbrace{-(4 - 8)\,X_3}_{X_3} = -24 + 4\,X_3 = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
X_3 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad R_x = 6 - \frac{3}{5} \cdot 20 + 6 = 0 ,
\qquad
R_y = -20 + 4 + \frac{4}{5} \cdot 20 = 0
$$
$$
M_A = \underbrace{3 \cdot (-20)}_{F} + \underbrace{-2 \cdot 6}_{H} + \underbrace{6 \cdot 16}_{X_2} + \underbrace{-4 \cdot 6}_{X_3} = -60 - 12 + 96 - 24 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The three lines of action unknown are the vertical $x = 0$ for $A$, the line through $B$ parallel to $(-3, 4)$ and the horizontal $y = 4$ for $C$. You take as reference points the points where you meet two by two: $K_1$, common to the first and third lines; $K_2$, common to the second and third, because the line through $B$ reaches $y = 4$ after a shift of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left; $K_3$, common to the first and second, because the line through $B$ reaches $x = 0$ going up by $8\,\mathrm{m}$. For the **moment and moment arm** theorem two of the three unknowns have zero moment about each reference point, and the equation of moments contains only one unknown. The three lines do not pass by the same point, so the three reference points are not aligned.

The three moment equations are satisfied, and the resultant, which verification calculates component by component, is zero; So the system is **balanced**. In the equation with respect to $K_2$ the force $F$ does not appear, because $D$ coincides with $K_2$. The check also writes the equation of moments about $A$, with $X_2$ broken down into components $-12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $16\,\mathrm{kN}$. The three intensities are positive, so the forces have the assumed directions: **$X_1 = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward**, **$X_2 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ up and left**, **$X_3 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right**.

</div>
