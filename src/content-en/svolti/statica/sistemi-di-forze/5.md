## Exercise 1

The L-shaped cantilever shown consists of column $AB$ of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and horizontal arm $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. At $C$, force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward, and at $B$, force $H = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Transfer the two forces to $A$: find the force at $A$ and the couple that together form an equivalent system.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,2,3" aste="A-B;B-C" forze="C,0,-1.2,F;B,1.3,0,H" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.8;B,C,2 m,-0.8" titolo="L-shaped cantilever under vertical force $F$ at $C$ and horizontal force $H$ at $B$"}

$$
R_x = 4\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
R_y = -10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\mathcal{M}_t = M_A = \underbrace{2 \cdot (-10)}_{F} + \underbrace{-3 \cdot 4}_{H} = -20 - 12 = -32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad M_C^{\text{(original)}} = \underbrace{0}_{F} + \underbrace{(0 - 2) \cdot 0 - (3 - 3) \cdot 4}_{H} = 0
$$
$$
M_C^{\text{(in } A)} = \underbrace{(0 - 2) \cdot (-10) - (0 - 3) \cdot 4}_{\vec{R} \text{ in } A} + \mathcal{M}_t = 20 + 12 - 32 = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For the **parallel transport** each force is carried at $A$ with its own transport couple, equal to the moment of force about the $A$. The force $F$, down to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $A$, gives the clockwise couple $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$; the force $H$, to the right at $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$, gives the clockwise couple $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. At $A$, the two forces carried add up to the resultant, and the two couples add up to a couple of $-32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

As a check, the moments of the two systems are compared with a reference point other than $A$; choose $C$. In the original $F$ system it is applied at $C$ and the line of action of $H$ passes through $C$, so the moment is zero. In the transported system the force applied at $A$ has counterclockwise moment $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about $C$, which the couple cancels. For the **Equivalence with respect to each reference point** the comparison confirms the result. The system is equivalent to **a force $A$ with $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ components to the right and $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ components downward, plus a clockwise couple of $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$**; are the actions that fixed support at $A$ must balance.

</div>

## Exercise 2

On the beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, the force $F_1 = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ down at point $C$ a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and the force $F_2 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at point $D$ a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, directed right and down, parallel to the vector $(3, -4)$, act. Reduce the system to one force and one couple at $A$, then to one force and one couple at $B$, and finally to only one force.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,5,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" forze="C,0,-1.5,F_1;D,0.9,-1.2,F_2" quote="A,C,2 m,0.8;C,D,3 m,0.8;D,B,1 m,0.8" titolo="Beam under vertical force $F_1$ and inclined force $F_2$"}

$$
F_{2,x} = 10 \cdot \frac{3}{5} = 6\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
F_{2,y} = 10 \cdot \frac{-4}{5} = -8\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
R_x = 6\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
R_y = -16 - 8 = -24\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
M_A = 2 \cdot (-16) + 5 \cdot (-8) = -72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
M_B = M_A + (x_A - x_B)\,R_y = -72 + (0 - 6) \cdot (-24) = -72 + 144 = 72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
x\,R_y = M_A
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x = \frac{-72}{-24} = 3\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad M_B = (2 - 6) \cdot (-16) + (5 - 6) \cdot (-8) = 64 + 8 = 72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The system reduced to a point is formed by the result applied at that point and the couple equal to the resultant moment about the point, for the **parallel transport** of each force. The resultant is the same in the two points; Change the couple. The horizontal component of $F_2$ has the line of action on the beam axis, so it has zero moment about $A$ and $B$. The moment about $B$ is obtained from that about $A$ with the **change of reference point formula**, and the check recalculates force by force: about $B$ both vertical components stand to the left of the reference point and have counterclockwise moment.

To switch to a single force the **reduction of a planar force system** is used: the resultant applied at point $(x, 0)$ of the axis must have moment $-72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about $A$, and the term with $R_x$ is zero because the point lies on the axis. The system is equivalent to **$\vec{R} = (6, -24)\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $A$ with a clockwise couple of $72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$**, or **same force at $B$ with a counterclockwise couple of $72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$**, or **$\vec{R}$ force only applied on $3\,\mathrm{m}$ beam from $A$**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

On the beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, the system formed by the force $F_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ down at point $C$ a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, the force $F_2 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ up at point $D$ a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and the clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts. Determine two vertical forces, applied at $A$ and $B$, which form an equivalent system.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" forze="C,0,-1.5,F_1;D,0,1.2,F_2" coppie="5 0,-1,ℳ" quote="A,C,2 m,1.7;C,D,2 m,1.7;D,B,2 m,1.7" titolo="System to be replaced by two vertical forces at $A$ and $B$"}

$$
R_y = -12 + 6 = -6\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_A = 2 \cdot (-12) + 4 \cdot 6 - 6 = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
F_A + F_B = R_y \\[4pt]
6\,F_B = M_A
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\begin{cases}
F_A + F_B = -6 \\[4pt]
6\,F_B = -6
\end{cases}
$$
$$
F_B = -1\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
F_A = -6 - (-1) = -5\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad M_B = (2 - 6) \cdot (-12) + (4 - 6) \cdot 6 - 6 = 48 - 12 - 6 = 30\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
(0 - 6) \cdot F_A = (-6) \cdot (-5) = 30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The unknowns are the vertical $F_A$ and $F_B$ components, positive upwards. The new system is **equivalent** to that given if it has the same resultant and the same moment as about a reference point; the forces are vertical, so the equality of horizontal components is already met and two conditions remain. The reference point is chosen at $A$, where the force $F_A$ has zero moment, so the equation of moments contains only the $F_B$, with moment arm $6\,\mathrm{m}$.

The two forces are negative, that is, directed downwards. For the **Equivalence with respect to each reference point** the check compares the moments about $B$ of the two systems; in the new system counts only $F_A$, down to $6\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$. The system is equivalent to **$5\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at $A$ and $1\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at $B$**. The reactions of a simply supported beam at $A$ and $B$ under this system are the opposite forces, as seen in the argument on support reactions.

</div>
