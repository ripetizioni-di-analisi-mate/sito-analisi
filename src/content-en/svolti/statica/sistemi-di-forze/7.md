## Exercise 1

The beam $AB$ shown, of length $L = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, carries the parabolic load
$$
q(x) = q_0\,\frac{x^2}{L^2} ,
\qquad
q_0 = 9\,\mathrm{kN/m} ,
$$
with $x$ measured from $A$. Calculate the resultant, its line of action and the moment of load about $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" carichi="0 0,0.5 0,0.000,0.017,;0.5 0,1 0,0.017,0.068,;1 0,1.5 0,0.068,0.152,;1.5 0,2 0,0.152,0.270,;2 0,2.5 0,0.270,0.422,;2.5 0,3 0,0.422,0.608,;3 0,3.5 0,0.608,0.827,;3.5 0,4 0,0.827,1.080,q_0" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8" titolo="Parabolic load $q(x) = q_0\,\dfrac{x^2}{L^2}$, zero at $A$ and equal to $q_0$ at $B$"}

$$
Q = \int_0^L q_0\,\frac{x^2}{L^2}\,dx = \frac{q_0}{L^2} \cdot \frac{L^3}{3} = \frac{q_0 L}{3} = \frac{9 \cdot 4}{3} = 12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\int_0^L x\,q(x)\,dx = \frac{q_0}{L^2} \cdot \frac{L^4}{4} = \frac{q_0 L^2}{4} = \frac{9 \cdot 16}{4} = 36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
x_Q = \frac{36}{12} = 3\,\mathrm{m} = \frac{3}{4}\,L ,
\qquad
M_A = -Q\,x_Q = -36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{check:} \quad M_B &= \int_0^L (x - L)\,\big( -q(x) \big)\,dx = \frac{q_0}{L^2}\int_0^L (L x^2 - x^3)\,dx \\[6pt]
&= \frac{q_0}{L^2} \cdot \frac{L^4}{12} = \frac{9 \cdot 16}{12} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m} = (3 - 4) \cdot (-12)
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For **Resultant of a distributed load** the intensity is the integral of $q$ on the loaded segment, i.e. the area of the diagram, and the line of action horizontal coordinate is the ratio of the integral of $x\,q(x)$ to the intensity. Integrals are of powers, and are calculated with the primitives $\dfrac{x^3}{3}$ and $\dfrac{x^4}{4}$. The resultant parabolic load is one third that of a uniform $q_0$ load, and the line of action is three-quarters of the span, closer to $B$ than the triangular load, because the load focuses more towards $B$.

The moment about $A$ is the moment of the resultant, down to $3\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $A$. For moment verification against $B$ is calculated by adding up the moments of the elementary forces $q(x)\,dx$, each with vertical component $-q(x)\,dx$ and moment arm $x - L$, and compares with the moment of the resultant. The load is equivalent to **$12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$**, with moment about $A$ of **$-36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The beam $AB$ shown, of length $L = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, carries the load
$$
q(x) = q_0 \sin\frac{\pi x}{2L} ,
\qquad
q_0 = 10\,\mathrm{kN/m} ,
$$
which is zero at $A$ and equals $q_0$ at $B$. Calculate the resultant, its line of action and load moments about $A$ and $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" carichi="0 0,0.5 0,0.000,0.195,;0.5 0,1 0,0.195,0.383,;1 0,1.5 0,0.383,0.556,;1.5 0,2 0,0.556,0.707,;2 0,2.5 0,0.707,0.831,;2.5 0,3 0,0.831,0.924,;3 0,3.5 0,0.924,0.981,;3.5 0,4 0,0.981,1.000,q_0" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8" titolo="Sinusoidal load $q(x) = q_0 \sin\dfrac{\pi x}{2L}$ over one-quarter of a period"}

$$
k = \frac{\pi}{2L} = \frac{\pi}{8}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}
$$
$$
Q = \int_0^L q_0 \sin(kx)\,dx = q_0 \left[ -\frac{\cos(kx)}{k} \right]_0^L = \frac{q_0}{k} = \frac{2 q_0 L}{\pi} = \frac{80}{\pi}\,\mathrm{kN} = 25.46\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\int_0^L x \sin(kx)\,dx = \left[ -\frac{x\cos(kx)}{k} + \frac{\sin(kx)}{k^2} \right]_0^L = \frac{1}{k^2} = \frac{64}{\pi^2}\,\mathrm{m^2}
$$
$$
\int_0^L x\,q(x)\,dx = \frac{640}{\pi^2}\,\mathrm{kN\,m} = 64.85\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
x_Q = \frac{\dfrac{640}{\pi^2}}{\dfrac{80}{\pi}} = \frac{8}{\pi}\,\mathrm{m} = 2.55\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
M_A = -Q\,x_Q = -64.85\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
M_B = (x_Q - L)\,(-Q) = \Big( 4 - \frac{8}{\pi} \Big) \cdot \frac{80}{\pi} = 37.01\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad M_B = \int_0^L (L - x)\,q(x)\,dx = L\,Q - \int_0^L x\,q(x)\,dx = \frac{320}{\pi} - \frac{640}{\pi^2} = 101.86 - 64.85 = 37.01\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The sine argument is $kx$ with $k = \dfrac{\pi}{2L}$, so in $x = L$ it is $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, and at that point the cosine is $0$ and the sine is $1$. The **Resultant of a distributed load** is obtained with the primitive $-\dfrac{\cos(kx)}{k}$. The integral of $x\sin(kx)$ is calculated by parts, deriving $x$ and integrating $\sin(kx)$; in the resulting primitive the term with cosine is zero in both extremes, the one with sine is zero in $0$, and remains $\dfrac{1}{k^2}$.

The line of action is at $2.55\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, beyond the middle, because the load grows towards $B$. Moments about $A$ and $B$ are calculated with the resultant: about $A$ the resultant, down to the right of the reference point, has clockwise moment; about $B$ stands to the left of the reference point and has counterclockwise moment. The check calculates $M_B$ directly from the integral of the elementary forces moments, which have $L - x$ moment arm about $B$. The load is equivalent to **$25.46\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, $2.55\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$**, with $M_A = -64.85\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $M_B = 37.01\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The $AB$ inclined rod in the figure ranges from $A = (0, 0)$ to $B = (4, 3)$, in meters. It carries the $q_s = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ self-weight per unit rod length and the $p = 1\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ wind pressure, perpendicular to the rod and applied on the lower face, then directed up and left. Calculate the result of the two loads and their moment about $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,3" aste="A-B" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q_s;A,B,0.35,0.35,p,126.87" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.6;4 0,B,3 m,0.6" titolo="Inclined member under self-weight $q_s$ and wind pressure $p$ normal to the member"}

$$
\ell = \sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = 5\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
E = (2;\, 1.5)\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
Q_s = q_s\,\ell = 2 \cdot 5 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}\;\text{ downward} ,
\qquad
Q_p = p\,\ell = 1 \cdot 5 = 5\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
\hat{n} = \Big( -\frac{3}{5},\, \frac{4}{5} \Big) ,
\qquad
\vec{Q}_p = 5\,\hat{n} = (-3, 4)\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
R_x = -3\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
R_y = -10 + 4 = -6\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
R = \sqrt{3^2 + 6^2} = 6.71\,\mathrm{kN}
$$
$$
M_A = \underbrace{2 \cdot (-10)}_{Q_s} + \underbrace{2 \cdot 4 - 1.5 \cdot (-3)}_{Q_p} = -20 + 12.5 = -7.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad M_B = M_A + (x_A - x_B)\,R_y - (y_A - y_B)\,R_x = -7.5 + (-4) \cdot (-6) - (-3) \cdot (-3) = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
M_B = \underbrace{(2 - 4) \cdot (-10)}_{Q_s} + \underbrace{(2 - 4) \cdot 4 - (1.5 - 3) \cdot (-3)}_{Q_p} = 20 - 8 - 4.5 = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For **loads on an inclined rod** the self-weight, given per unit rod length, has resultant vertical $q_s\,\ell$, and wind pressure has resultant $p\,\ell$ perpendicular to the rod; both are applied at the midpoint $E$. The rod has the direction of the vector $(4, 3)$, so the perpendicular unit vectors are $\pm\Big( -\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5} \Big)$, and the wind, which pushes the lower face up and left, has the direction $\hat{n}$ with the positive vertical component.

The resulting two pass through the midpoint, so by **Varignon's theorem** the overall result also passes through the midpoint. To find the moment about $A$, use the components at the midpoint. The weight produces clockwise moment $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The wind produces counterclockwise moment $12.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, equal to $Q_p$ times moment arm $\dfrac{\ell}{2} = 2.5\,\mathrm{m}$, because it acts perpendicular to the member. The check calculates $M_B$ with the change of reference point formula and directly; the value is opposite to $M_A$, because $A$ and $B$ are symmetrical with respect to the midpoint, so the resultant passes. Loads are equivalent to **$\vec{R} = (-3, -6)\,\mathrm{kN}$, of $6.71\,\mathrm{kN}$, applied at the midpoint of the rod**, with **$M_A = -7.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise**.

</div>

## Exercise 4

The cover flap in the figure is schematized with the $AB$ inclined rod, from $A = (0, 0)$ to $B = (8, 6)$ in meters. It carries the self-weight $q_s = 0.4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per unit rod length, the snow load $q_n = 1\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per unit horizontal projection length and the force $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting downwards at $B$. Determine the resultant and the point of the rod for which it passes its line of action.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,6" aste="A-B" carichi="A,B,0.3,0.3,q_s;0 7.4,8 7.4,0.6,0.6,q_n" tratteggi="0,7.4 8,7.4" forze="8 4.6,0,-1.4,F" quote="A,8 0,8 m,0.6;8 0,B,6 m,0.6" titolo="Sloping roof member under self-weight $q_s$ along its length, snow load $q_n$ on its horizontal projection, and force $F$ at $B$"}

$$
\ell = \sqrt{8^2 + 6^2} = 10\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
a = 8\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
Q_s = q_s\,\ell = 0.4 \cdot 10 = 4\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
Q_n = q_n\,a = 1 \cdot 8 = 8\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
x_{Q_s} = x_{Q_n} = 4\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
R_y = -4 - 8 - 4 = -16\,\mathrm{kN} ,
\qquad
M_A = 4 \cdot (-4) + 4 \cdot (-8) + 8 \cdot (-4) = -80\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$
$$
x = \frac{M_A}{R_y} = \frac{-80}{-16} = 5\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad
y = \frac{6}{8} \cdot 5 = 3.75\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad M_B = (4 - 8) \cdot (-4) + (4 - 8) \cdot (-8) + 0 = 16 + 32 = 48\,\mathrm{kN\,m} ,
\qquad
(5 - 8) \cdot (-16) = 48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

For **loads on an inclined member**, the self-weight resultant equals $q_s$ times the member length, $10\,\mathrm{m}$, and the snow-load resultant equals $q_n$ times the horizontal projection, $8\,\mathrm{m}$. Both resultants act vertically through the midpoint of the member, whose horizontal coordinate is $4\,\mathrm{m}$. All forces are vertical, so by **reduction of a planar system**, the resultant acts on the vertical line with horizontal coordinate $\dfrac{M_A}{R_y}$. The point on the member with this horizontal coordinate has vertical coordinate $\dfrac{6}{8}\,x$ because the member lies on the line $y = \dfrac{3}{4}\,x$.

The check compares the moment with $B$ of the three loads with that of the resultant; $F$ force is applied at $B$ and has zero moment. Loads are equivalent to **$16\,\mathrm{kN}$ down, with line of action meeting the rod at point $(5;\, 3.75)\,\mathrm{m}$**.

</div>
