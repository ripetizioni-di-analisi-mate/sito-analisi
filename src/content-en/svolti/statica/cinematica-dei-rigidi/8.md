## Exercise 1

The arch in the figure is formed by the $AC$ and $CB$ rods, connected by the internal hinge $C$; at $A$ there is a pin support and at $B$ a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. On the structure act a horizontal force $H$ at $C$, to the right, and a vertical force $F$ at the midpoint $K$ of the rod $CB$, downwards. For $\varphi_1 = 0.003\,\mathrm{rad}$ calculate the displacements of the application points of forces in the direction and sense of forces, and the displacement of the roller support.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,2;K,7,1;B,10,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,1.6,0,H;K,0,-1.6,F" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.9;4 0,B,6 m,0.9;C,4 0,2 m,-0.6" titolo="Two-member arch with a horizontal force at $C$ and a vertical force at $K$"}

$$
C_1 = A = (0, 0) ,
\qquad
C_{12} = C = (4, 2) ,
\qquad
\begin{cases}
x = 10 \\[2pt]
y = \dfrac{x}{2}
\end{cases}
\ \Longrightarrow \
C_2 = (10, 5)
$$
$$
\varphi_1 \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} = \varphi_2 \begin{pmatrix} 4 - 10 \\ 2 - 5 \end{pmatrix}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi_2 = -\frac{2}{3}\,\varphi_1 = -0.002\,\mathrm{rad}
$$
$$
u_C = -\varphi_1\,(y_C - 0) = -0.003 \cdot 2\,\mathrm{m} = -6\,\mathrm{mm}
$$
$$
v_K = \varphi_2\,(x_K - 10) = -0.002 \cdot (7 - 10)\,\mathrm{m} = 6\,\mathrm{mm}
$$
$$
u_B = -\varphi_2\,(0 - 5)\,\mathrm{m} = -0.002 \cdot 5\,\mathrm{m} = -10\,\mathrm{mm}
$$
$$
s_H = u_C = -6\,\mathrm{mm} ,
\qquad
s_F = -v_K = -6\,\mathrm{mm}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad u_C^{(2)} = -\varphi_2\,(2 - 5)\,\mathrm{m} = -0.002 \cdot 3\,\mathrm{m} = -6\,\mathrm{mm} = u_C^{(1)}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The center $C_2$ lies on the vertical for roller support and on the line $AC$, of $\dfrac{1}{2}$ slope, then $C_2 = (10, 5)$. The point $C$ is inside the segment $C_1 C_2$ and divides it into two parts of lengths $\overline{C_1 C} = 2\sqrt{5}\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\overline{C C_2} = 3\sqrt{5}\,\mathrm{m}$, in the $2 : 3$ ratio, so the rod $CB$ rotates in opposite direction with an amplitude equal to $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of that of $AC$: with $\varphi_1 = 0.003\,\mathrm{rad}$ you have $\varphi_2 = -0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$.

The displacement in the direction and sense of a force, denoted here by $s_H$ and $s_F$, is the component of the displacement of its point of application along the force's unit vector. The force $H$ is horizontal to the right, so it counts $u_C$; the force $F$ is vertical downwards, so it counts $-v_K$. The point $C$ moves to the left by $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ and the point $K$ rises to $6\,\mathrm{mm}$, therefore **both points move $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ in the opposite direction to that of the force**. These quantities are used to write the virtual work of the loads, for which reference is made to the argument on the principle of virtual work. The roller support moves $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left. The check recalculates $u_C$ as the point of member $CB$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The frame in the figure is formed by the body $1 = ABC$, with a pin support at $A$, and the body $2 = CD$, connected to the first by the internal hinge $C$ and supported at $D$ by a two-force vertical link. They act a horizontal force $H$ at $B$, to the right, a vertical force $F$ at the midpoint $M$ of $CD$, downwards, and a counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M}$ on the body $2$. For $\varphi_1 = 0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$ calculate the displacement of $B$ in the direction of $H$, that of $M$ in the direction of $F$ and the rotation of the body on which the couple acts.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,3,3;M,4.5,3;D,6,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,pendolo" forze="B,1.2,0,H;M,0,-1.2,F" coppie="5.2 3,1,ℳ" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.8;B,C,3 m,0.7;C,D,3 m,0.7" titolo="Frame with a vertical link at $D$"}

$$
C_1 = A = (0, 0) ,
\qquad
C_{12} = C = (3, 3) ,
\qquad
\begin{cases}
x = 6 & \text{(axis of the link)} \\
y = x & \text{(line } C_1 C_{12})
\end{cases}
\ \Longrightarrow \
C_2 = (6, 6)
$$
$$
\varphi_1 \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} = \varphi_2 \begin{pmatrix} 3 - 6 \\ 3 - 6 \end{pmatrix}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1 = -0.002\,\mathrm{rad}
$$
$$
s_H = u_B = -\varphi_1\,(3 - 0)\,\mathrm{m} = -6\,\mathrm{mm}
$$
$$
v_M = \varphi_2\,(4.5 - 6)\,\mathrm{m} = -0.002 \cdot (-1.5)\,\mathrm{m} = 3\,\mathrm{mm} ,
\qquad
s_F = -v_M = -3\,\mathrm{mm}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad v_D = \varphi_2\,(6 - 6) = 0 ,
\qquad
u_D = -\varphi_2\,(3 - 6)\,\mathrm{m} = -6\,\mathrm{mm} = u_B
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two-force vertical link at $D$ allows $D$ only horizontal shifts, so $C_2$ lies on the vertical $x = 6$; for the first theorem of kinematic chains also lies on line $AC$, and the intersection is $C_2 = (6, 6)$. The hinge $C$ is the midpoint of $C_1 C_2$, therefore $\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1$.

The point $B$ moves to the left by $6\,\mathrm{mm}$, in the opposite direction to $H$. The point $M$ rises by $3\,\mathrm{mm}$, then moves by $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ in the opposite direction to $F$. A couple acts on a rigid body and its work depends on the rotation of that body, which here is $\varphi_2 = -0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$, clockwise, opposite to the counterclockwise direction of $\mathcal{M}$. The check checks the condition of the two-force link and that $D$, at the same elevation as $B$ and the hinge $C$, has the same horizontal displacement as $B$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The portal frame in the figure is formed by the body $1 = ABD$ and the body $2 = DEF$, connected by the internal hinge $D$; at $A$ there is a pin support and at $F$ a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. The roller support moves $21\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right. Calculate the rotations of the two bodies, the horizontal displacement of $B$ and the vertical displacement of the midpoint $M$ of the segment $DE$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;D,3,3;M,5,3;E,7,3;F,7,0" aste="A-B;B-D;D-E;E-F" cerniere="D" vincoli="A,cerniera;F,carrello" frecce="7,-0.9,8.3,-0.9,u_F" quote="B,D,3 m,-0.7;D,E,4 m,-0.7;A,B,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Portal frame with prescribed displacement at roller $F$"}

$$
C_1 = A = (0, 0) ,
\qquad
C_{12} = D = (3, 3) ,
\qquad
\begin{cases}
x = 7 \\
y = x
\end{cases}
\ \Longrightarrow \
C_2 = (7, 7)
$$
$$
\varphi_1 \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} = \varphi_2 \begin{pmatrix} 3 - 7 \\ 3 - 7 \end{pmatrix}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi_2 = -\frac{3}{4}\,\varphi_1
$$
$$
u_F = -\varphi_2\,(0 - 7)\,\mathrm{m} = 7\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_2 = 21\,\mathrm{mm}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi_2 = 0.003\,\mathrm{rad} ,
\qquad
\varphi_1 = -\frac{4}{3}\,\varphi_2 = -0.004\,\mathrm{rad}
$$
$$
u_B = -\varphi_1\,(3 - 0)\,\mathrm{m} = 0.004 \cdot 3\,\mathrm{m} = 12\,\mathrm{mm}
$$
$$
v_M = \varphi_2\,(5 - 7)\,\mathrm{m} = 0.003 \cdot (-2)\,\mathrm{m} = -6\,\mathrm{mm}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad \vec{s}_D^{\,(1)} = \big( -\varphi_1 \cdot 3,\ \varphi_1 \cdot 3 \big)\,\mathrm{m} = (12, -12)\,\mathrm{mm} ,
\qquad
\vec{s}_D^{\,(2)} = \big( -\varphi_2\,(3 - 7),\ \varphi_2\,(3 - 7) \big)\,\mathrm{m} = (12, -12)\,\mathrm{mm}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The center $C_2$ lies on the vertical for the roller support and on the line $AD$, the bisector $y = x$, then $C_2 = (7, 7)$. The hinge $D$ is internal to the segment $C_1 C_2$ and is $3\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C_1$ and $4\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C_2$, therefore $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{3}{4}\varphi_1$.

In this exercise the data is a shift and the chain parameter is derived from it. The roller at $F$ stands $7\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C_2$, then $u_F = 7\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_2$, and moving $21\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right gives $\varphi_2 = 0.003\,\mathrm{rad}$ counterclockwise; the column $AB$ rotates in clockwise with $\varphi_1 = -0.004\,\mathrm{rad}$. The point $B$ moves $12\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right and the point $M$ drops by $6\,\mathrm{mm}$. The check recalculates the displacement of the hinge $D$ with the two bodies and gets the same vector.

</div>
