## Exercise 1

The portal frame in the figure is formed by the body $1 = ABD$ and the body $2 = DCE$, connected by the internal hinge $D$; at $A$ there is a pin support and at $E$ a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. Draw, as a function of $\varphi_1$, the horizontal displacement diagram of the columns and that of the vertical displacements of the crossbeam.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;D,4,3;C,6,3;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-D;D-C;C-E" cerniere="D" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" quote="B,D,4 m,-0.7;D,C,2 m,-0.7;C,E,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Portal frame with an internal hinge on its crossbeam"}

$$
C_1 = A = (0, 0) ,
\qquad
C_{12} = D = (4, 3)
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
x = 6 & \text{(vertical line through } E) \\[2pt]
y = \dfrac{3}{4}\,x & \text{(line } C_1 C_{12})
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
C_2 = (6;\ 4.5)
$$
$$
\varphi_1 \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} = \varphi_2 \begin{pmatrix} 4 - 6 \\ 3 - 4.5 \end{pmatrix}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi_2 = -2\,\varphi_1
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{column } AB &: \quad u = -\varphi_1\,y , & u_B &= -3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 \\[4pt]
\text{column } CE &: \quad u = -\varphi_2\,(y - 4.5) = 2\varphi_1\,(y - 4.5) , & u_C &= -3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 , \quad u_E = -9\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 \\[4pt]
\text{crossbeam } BD &: \quad v = \varphi_1\,x , & v_D &= 4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 \\[4pt]
\text{crossbeam } DC &: \quad v = -2\varphi_1\,(x - 6) , & v_C &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad v_D^{(2)} = -2\varphi_1\,(4 - 6)\,\mathrm{m} = 4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 = v_D^{(1)}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;D,4,3;C,6,3;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-D;D-C;C-E" cerniere="D" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" diagramma="0,3,0,0,s-3;6,3,6,0,-3-2*s" segni=1 numeri=0 etichette="-0.7,2.9,−3;5.45,3.15,−3;4.45,-0.1,−9" titolo="Horizontal displacement diagram $\dfrac{u}{\varphi_1}$ ($\mathrm{m}$) of the columns, positive to the right"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;D,4,3;C,6,3;E,6,0" aste="A-B;B-D;D-C;C-E" cerniere="D" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" diagramma="0,3,4,3,s;4,3,6,3,4-2*s" segni=1 titolo="Vertical displacement diagram $\dfrac{v}{\varphi_1}$ ($\mathrm{m}$) of the crossbeam, positive upward"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The center $C_2$ lies on the vertical for the roller support and on the line $AD$, then $C_2 = (6;\ 4.5)$, above the crossbeam. For the first theorem of the $\varphi_2 = -2\varphi_1$ kinematic chains, because $D$ is internal to the segment $C_1 C_2$ and is twice as far from $C_1$ as from $C_2$.

By the theorem on **vertical and horizontal displacements of a body**, columns, verticals, the diagram of $u$ as a function of $y$, and crossbeam, horizontal, that of $v$ as a function of $x$, is convenient. The column $AB$ rotates about $A$, so $u$ is zero at $A$ and equals $-3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$ at $B$. The column $CE$ rotates about $C_2$, which stands $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ above $C$: therefore $u$ equals $-3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$ at $C$ and **$-9\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$ at $E$ support roller**, the most moved point. The crossbeam ends $B$ and $C$ have the same horizontal displacement as hinge $D$: points $B$ and $D$ of body $1$ have the same vertical coordinate, as do points $C$ and $D$ of body $2$. The diagram of $v$ on the crossbeam is continuous at $D$, and verification controls it.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The frame in the figure is formed by the column $AB$ (body $1$), with a pin support at $A$, and the body $2 = BCD$, connected to the column by the internal hinge $B$ and resting at $D$ on a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. Find the centers of rotation and draw the horizontal displacement diagram as a function of $\varphi_1$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,6,4;D,6,1" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" quote="B,C,6 m,-0.7;A,B,4 m,-0.8;C,D,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Frame with an internal hinge at the top of column $AB$"}

$$
C_1 = A = (0, 0) ,
\qquad
C_{12} = B = (0, 4)
$$
$$
C_2 \in C_1 C_{12} \ (x = 0) ,
\qquad
C_2 \in \text{vertical line through } D \ (x = 6)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
C_2 = \text{point at infinity in the vertical direction} ,
\quad
\varphi_2 = 0
$$
$$
u_B = -\varphi_1\,(4 - 0)\,\mathrm{m} = -4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\vec{s}_2 = (u_B, 0) = (-4, 0)\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{column } AB &: \quad u = -\varphi_1\,y \quad (0 \le y \le 4\,\mathrm{m}) \\[4pt]
\text{column } CD &: \quad u = -4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 \quad (1\,\mathrm{m} \le y \le 4\,\mathrm{m})
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad v_D = v_B = \varphi_1\,(0 - 0) = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,6,4;D,6,1" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" diagramma="0,4,0,0,s-4;6,4,6,1,-4" segni=1 numeri=0 etichette="-1.6,3.9,−4;4.8,4.15,−4;4.45,0.9,−4" titolo="Horizontal displacement diagram $\dfrac{u}{\varphi_1}$ ($\mathrm{m}$), showing translation of body $BCD$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The pin support gives $C_1 = A$ and the internal hinge gives $C_{12} = B$, so for the first theorem of the $C_2$ kinematic chains it lies on line $AB$, the vertical $x = 0$. The roller $D$ support runs horizontally, so $C_2$ also sits on the vertical $x = 6$. The two lines are **parallel**, so $C_2$ is the point at infinity in the vertical direction and the body $2$ moves horizontally.

The displacement of the body $2$ is that of the hinge $B$, which as a point of the column $AB$ moves $-4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$ horizontally and zero vertically. The diagram of $u$ is triangular on the $AB$ and constant, $-4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$, on the $CD$; the vertical displacement diagram of the crossbeam is zero. The check checks that the roller at $D$ does not move vertically.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The frame in the figure is formed by the body $1 = ABC$, with the inclined column $AB$ and the crossbeam section $BC$, and the body $2 = CDE$, connected by the internal hinge $C$; at $A$ there is a pin support and at $E$ a roller support that moves along a horizontal plane. Draw diagrams of vertical and horizontal displacements as a function of $\varphi_1$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,4;C,4,4;D,8,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" quote="A,2 0,2 m;2 0,8 0,6 m;B,C,2 m,-0.7;C,D,4 m,-0.7;D,E,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Frame with an inclined column"}

$$
C_1 = A = (0, 0) ,
\qquad
C_{12} = C = (4, 4) ,
\qquad
\begin{cases}
x = 8 \\
y = x
\end{cases}
\ \Longrightarrow \
C_2 = (8, 8)
$$
$$
\varphi_1 \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} = \varphi_2 \begin{pmatrix} 4 - 8 \\ 4 - 8 \end{pmatrix}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{body } 1 &: \quad v = \varphi_1\,x & &(0 \le x \le 4\,\mathrm{m}) \\[4pt]
&\phantom{:} \quad u = -\varphi_1\,y & &(0 \le y \le 4\,\mathrm{m}) \\[4pt]
\text{body } 2 &: \quad v = -\varphi_1\,(x - 8) & &(4\,\mathrm{m} \le x \le 8\,\mathrm{m}) \\[4pt]
&\phantom{:} \quad u = \varphi_1\,(y - 8) & &(0 \le y \le 4\,\mathrm{m})
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\vec{s}_B = (-4, 2)\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\vec{s}_C = (-4, 4)\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\vec{s}_D = (-4, 0)\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 ,
\qquad
\vec{s}_E = (-8, 0)\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad \vec{s}_C^{\,(2)} = \big( \varphi_1\,(4 - 8),\ -\varphi_1\,(4 - 8) \big) = (-4, 4)\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 = \vec{s}_C^{\,(1)}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,4;C,4,4;D,8,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" linee="0,-2 8,-2" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2;4,4 4,-2;8,0 8,-2" diagramma="0,-2,4,-2,s;4,-2,8,-2,4-s" segni=1 titolo="Vertical displacement diagram $\dfrac{v}{\varphi_1}$ ($\mathrm{m}$) along the horizontal reference line"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,4;C,4,4;D,8,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" linee="-2.5,4 -2.5,0" tratteggi="0,0 -2.5,0;2,4 -2.5,4" diagramma="-2.5,4,-2.5,0,s-4;8,4,8,0,-4-s" segni=1 numeri=0 etichette="-4.0,3.9,−4;6.85,4.22,−4;5.45,-0.1,−8" titolo="Horizontal displacement diagram $\dfrac{u}{\varphi_1}$ ($\mathrm{m}$), shown on the vertical reference line for body $1$ and on column $DE$ for body $2$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The center $C_2$ lies on the vertical for the roller support and on the line $AC$, the bisector $y = x$, then $C_2 = (8, 8)$. The hinge $C$ is the midpoint of $C_1 C_2$, therefore $\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1$.

The column $AB$ is tilted, and on it $u$ and $v$ both vary. For the **vertical and horizontal displacements of a body** theorem, $v$ depends only on $x$ and $u$ only on $y$, so diagrams are drawn on projections: that of $v$ on a horizontal line, where the body $1$ occupies the $0 \le x \le 4\,\mathrm{m}$ range and the body $2$ the $4\,\mathrm{m} \le x \le 8\,\mathrm{m}$ range; $u$'s on a vertical line, where both bodies occupy the $0 \le y \le 4\,\mathrm{m}$ range. For the body $2$ the vertical reference line is the column $DE$ itself. The $v$ diagram has the maximum $4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$ in the hinge $C$; $u$ diagram of body $2$ ranges from $-4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$ at $D$ to **$-8\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$ at $E$ support roller**. The check recalculates the displacement of the hinge $C$ with the body $2$.

</div>
