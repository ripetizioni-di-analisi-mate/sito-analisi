## Exercise 1

The beam shown is formed by the body $1 = DB$, with a pin support at $A$ and the overhang $DA$, and by the body $2 = BC$, connected to the first by the internal hinge $B$ and bound at $C$ by a parallel-link support with the horizontal links. Find the rotation centers and vertical displacements of $D$, $B$ and $C$ as a function of $\varphi_1$.

::struttura{nodi="D,0,0;A,2,0;B,5,0;C,8,0" aste="D-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,doppio-pendolo,0" quote="D,A,2 m;A,B,3 m;B,C,3 m" titolo="Two-body beam with a parallel-link connection with horizontal links"}

$$
C_1 = A = (2, 0) ,
\qquad
C_{12} = B = (5, 0) ,
\qquad
C_2 = \text{point at infinity in the horizontal direction}
$$
$$
\varphi_2 = 0 ,
\qquad
v_B = \varphi_1\,(x_B - x_A) = 3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1
$$
$$
v_D = \varphi_1\,(0 - 2)\,\mathrm{m} = -2\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 ,
\qquad
v_C = v_B = 3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad u_C = u_B = -\varphi_1\,(y_B - y_A) = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="D,0,0;A,2,0;B,5,0;C,8,0" aste="D-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,doppio-pendolo,0" diagramma="0,0,5,0,s-2;5,0,8,0,3" titolo="Vertical displacement diagram $\dfrac{v}{\varphi_1}$ ($\mathrm{m}$)"}

<div class="spiegazione">

Parallel-link support with horizontal links prevents the body $2$ from rotating and moving horizontally, so the body $2$ can only move vertically and its $C_2$ center is the **Misplaced point of horizontal direction**. The first theorem of kinematic chains, with a center at infinity, requires that the line $C_1 C_{12}$ have the horizontal direction. The line $AB$ is the axis of the beam, horizontal, so the condition is met and the chain moves with one degree of freedom.

The body $1$ rotates about $A$: the hinge $B$ rises by $3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$ and the tip of overhang $D$ drops by $2\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$. The body $2$ moves with the displacement of $B$, so all its points, including $C$, rise by $3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$. The vertical displacement diagram is an inclined segment on the body $1$, which cancels out under $A$, and a horizontal segment on the body $2$. The check checks that $C$ does not move horizontally, as parallel-link support requires. The body $1$ points on the axis have $u = 0$ because they stand on the horizontal of the center $A$, and the body $2$ moves with the displacement of $B$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The frame in the figure is formed by the column $AB$ (body $1$), bound at $A$ by a parallel-link support with the pin-ended links vertical, and by the body $2 = BDE$, connected to the first by the internal hinge $B$ and bound at $E$ by a roller support that moves along a vertical plane. Find the rotation centers and displacements of $B$, $D$ and $E$ as a function of the rotation $\varphi_2$ of the body $2$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;D,5,4;E,5,0" aste="A-B;B-D;D-E" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo;E,carrello,0" quote="B,D,5 m,-0.7;A,B,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Frame with a parallel-link support at $A$ and a roller on a vertical surface at $E$"}

$$
C_1 = \text{point at infinity in the vertical direction} ,
\qquad
C_{12} = B = (0, 4)
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
y = 0 & \text{(normal to the sliding plane through } E) \\
x = 0 & \text{(line through } C_{12} \text{ in the direction of } C_1)
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
C_2 = (0, 0)
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec{s}_B &= \big( -\varphi_2\,(4 - 0),\ \varphi_2\,(0 - 0) \big) = (-4, 0)\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_2 \\[4pt]
\vec{s}_D &= \big( -\varphi_2\,(4 - 0),\ \varphi_2\,(5 - 0) \big) = (-4, 5)\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_2 \\[4pt]
\vec{s}_E &= \big( -\varphi_2\,(0 - 0),\ \varphi_2\,(5 - 0) \big) = (0, 5)\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_2
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\varphi_1 = 0 ,
\qquad
\vec{s}_A = \vec{s}_B = (-4, 0)\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_2
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad v_A = 0 , \qquad u_E = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Parallel-link support with vertical links prevents the column $AB$ from rotating and moving vertically, so the body $1$ can only move horizontally and **$C_1$ is the point at infinity in the vertical direction**. The internal hinge gives $C_{12} = B$. For the first theorem of the kinematic chains $C_2$ lies on the line through $B$ in the direction of $C_1$, i.e. on the vertical $x = 0$. The roller $E$ support moves along a vertical surface, so $C_2$ also lies on normal for $E$, the horizontal $y = 0$. The intersection is $C_2 = (0, 0)$, the point where $A$ is located, but it belongs to body $1$ and not body $2$.

The body $2$ rotates about $(0, 0)$ and the body $1$ moves horizontally with the displacement of $B$, $u = -4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_2$. For $\varphi_2 > 0$ the column $AB$ moves to the left and the roller $E$ support goes up by $5\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_2$. The check checks the two external supports: $A$ does not move vertically and $E$ does not move horizontally.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The beam shown is formed by the body $1 = AB$, with a pin support at $A$, and the body $2 = BC$, with a roller support at $C$ that moves along a horizontal plane. The two bodies are connected at $B$ by an internal parallel-link connection with the horizontal links. Find the centers of rotation, the ratio of rotations to the vertical displacement jump at $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,7,0" aste="A-B;B-C" interni="B,doppio-pendolo,0" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,3 m" titolo="Two-body beam joined by an internal parallel-link connection with horizontal links"}

$$
C_1 = A = (0, 0) ,
\qquad
C_{12} = \text{point at infinity in the horizontal direction}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi_2 = \varphi_1
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
x = 7 & \text{(vertical line through } C) \\
y = 0 & \text{(line through } C_1 \text{ in the direction of } C_{12})
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
C_2 = C = (7, 0)
$$
$$
v_B^{(1)} = \varphi_1\,(4 - 0)\,\mathrm{m} = 4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 ,
\qquad
v_B^{(2)} = \varphi_2\,(4 - 7)\,\mathrm{m} = -3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1
$$
$$
\Delta v_B = v_B^{(2)} - v_B^{(1)} = -7\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad u_B^{(2)} - u_B^{(1)} = -\varphi_2\,(0 - 0) + \varphi_1\,(0 - 0) = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,7,0" aste="A-B;B-C" interni="B,doppio-pendolo,0" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" diagramma="0,0,4,0,s;4,0,7,0,s-3" titolo="Vertical displacement diagram $\dfrac{v}{\varphi_1}$ ($\mathrm{m}$), showing two parallel segments with a jump at $B$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The internal parallel-link connection prevents the relative rotation of the two bodies and their relative translation along the pin-ended links, so $\varphi_2 = \varphi_1$ and the relative center $C_{12}$ is the point at infinity in the horizontal direction.

The center $C_2$ lies on the vertical for the roller support and, for the first theorem of kinematic chains, on the line by $C_1$ in the direction of $C_{12}$, i.e. on the axis of the beam; then $C_2 = C$. The two bodies rotate through the same angle about different centers, so the two segments in the vertical displacement diagram are parallel. At $B$ the extreme of the body $1$ moves up by $4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$ and that of the body $2$ drops by $3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$: the parallel-link support allows vertical relative sliding, which equals $7\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$. The check checks that the relative horizontal displacement at $B$ is zero, as horizontal links require.

</div>
