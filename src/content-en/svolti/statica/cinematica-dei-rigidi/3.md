## Exercise 1

The beam shown consists of bodies $1 = AB$ and $2 = BD$ joined by internal hinge $B$. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $C$ on a horizontal surface. Segment $CD$ is an overhang. Find the absolute and relative centers of rotation, the rotation ratio, and the vertical displacements of $B$ and $D$ in terms of $\varphi_1$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,8,0" aste="A-B;B-D" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="A,B,3 m;B,C,3 m;C,D,2 m" titolo="Two-body beam with an overhang"}

$$
C_1 = A = (0, 0) ,
\qquad
C_{12} = B = (3, 0) ,
\qquad
C_2 = C = (6, 0)
$$
$$
\varphi_1\,\overrightarrow{C_1 C_{12}} = \varphi_2\,\overrightarrow{C_2 C_{12}}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi_1 \cdot 3\,\mathrm{m} = \varphi_2 \cdot (3 - 6)\,\mathrm{m}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1
$$
$$
v_B = \varphi_1\,(x_B - x_{C_1}) = 3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 ,
\qquad
v_D = \varphi_2\,(x_D - x_{C_2}) = -\varphi_1 \cdot 2\,\mathrm{m} = -2\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad v_B \text{ with body } 2 = \varphi_2\,(3 - 6)\,\mathrm{m} = -\varphi_1 \cdot (-3)\,\mathrm{m} = 3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The pin support at $A$ cancels the displacement of $A$, then $C_1 = A$; the internal hinge at $B$ gives the same shift to the two bodies at $B$, then $C_{12} = B$. The roller $C$ support only allows horizontal shifts, so $C_2$ lies on the vertical through $C$. For the **first theorem of kinematic chains** $C_2$ it also lies on the line $C_1 C_{12}$, which is the axis of the beam, and therefore $C_2 = C$.

The vector relationship of the first theorem gives $\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1$: the two bodies **rotate in opposite directions**, because $C_{12}$ is internal to the segment $C_1 C_2$. For $\varphi_1 > 0$ the hinge $B$ goes up by $3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$ and the tip of overhang $D$ moves down by $2\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$. The check recalculates the displacement of $B$ as the point of the body $2$ and finds the same value.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The portal frame shown consists of body $1$ (column $AB$ and crossbeam segment $BD$) and body $2$ (crossbeam segment $DE$ and column $EF$). It has a pin support at $A$, an internal hinge at $D$, and a roller on a horizontal surface at $F$. Locate the centers of rotation, find the rotation ratio, and express the displacements of $B$, $D$, $E$, and $F$ in terms of $\varphi_1$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;D,4,3;E,8,3;F,8,0" aste="A-B;B-D;D-E;E-F" cerniere="D" vincoli="A,cerniera;F,carrello" quote="B,D,4 m,-0.7;D,E,4 m,-0.7;E,F,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Portal frame with an internal hinge in its crossbeam"}

$$
C_1 = A = (0, 0) ,
\qquad
C_{12} = D = (4, 3)
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
x = 8 & \text{(vertical line through } F) \\[2pt]
y = \dfrac{3}{4}\,x & \text{(line } C_1 C_{12})
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
C_2 = (8, 6)
$$
$$
\varphi_1 \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} = \varphi_2 \begin{pmatrix} 4 - 8 \\ 3 - 6 \end{pmatrix}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec{s}_B &= \big( -\varphi_1 \cdot 3,\ \varphi_1 \cdot 0 \big) = (-3, 0)\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 \\[4pt]
\vec{s}_D &= \big( -\varphi_1 \cdot 3,\ \varphi_1 \cdot 4 \big) = (-3, 4)\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 \\[4pt]
\vec{s}_E &= \big( -\varphi_2\,(3 - 6),\ \varphi_2\,(8 - 8) \big) = (-3, 0)\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 \\[4pt]
\vec{s}_F &= \big( -\varphi_2\,(0 - 6),\ 0 \big) = (-6, 0)\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad \vec{s}_D \text{ with body } 2 = \big( -\varphi_2\,(3 - 6),\ \varphi_2\,(4 - 8) \big) = (-3, 4)\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;D,4,3;E,8,3;F,8,0" aste="A-B;B-D;D-E;E-F" cerniere="D" vincoli="A,cerniera;F,carrello" punti="8,6,C_2,centro" etichette="-0.9,0.4,C_1;3.4,3.6,C_12" tratteggi="0,0 8.4,6.3;8,0 8,6.5" titolo="Center $C_2$ lies on the vertical through $F$ and on line $C_1 C_{12}$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The pin support gives $C_1 = A$ and the internal hinge gives $C_{12} = D$. The roller $F$ support moves horizontally, so $C_2$ lies on the vertical through $F$; for the **first theorem of kinematic chains** also lies on the line through $C_1$ and $C_{12}$, of $\dfrac{3}{4}$ slope. The two conditions together give $C_2 = (8, 6)$, above the roller support.

The point $D$ is the midpoint of the segment $C_1 C_2$, then $\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1$. The displacements are calculated with the formulas of the center: the points of the body $1$ around $A$, those of the body $2$ around $C_2$. All points of the crossbeam have vertical coordinate $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and the same horizontal displacement $-3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$; the roller at $F$, which stands $6\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C_2$, moves by $-6\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$, i.e. to the left for $\varphi_1 > 0$. The check recalculates the displacement of $D$ as the point of the body $2$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

The arch shown consists of straight members $AC$ and $CB$ joined by internal hinge $C$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a two-force link at $B$ sloping downward to the right at $45^\circ$. Locate the centers of rotation, find the rotation ratio, and calculate the displacements of $C$ and $B$ for $\varphi_1 = 0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,3;B,8,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,pendolo,315" quote="A,3 0,3 m,0.9;3 0,B,5 m,0.9;C,3 0,3 m,-0.7" titolo="Two-member arch with a pin support and a two-force link"}

$$
C_1 = A = (0, 0) ,
\qquad
C_{12} = C = (3, 3)
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
y = x & \text{(line } C_1 C_{12}) \\[2pt]
y = -(x - 8) & \text{(axis of the link)}
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
C_2 = (4, 4)
$$
$$
\varphi_1 \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} = \varphi_2 \begin{pmatrix} 3 - 4 \\ 3 - 4 \end{pmatrix}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi_2 = -3\,\varphi_1 = -0.003\,\mathrm{rad}
$$
$$
\vec{s}_C = \big( -\varphi_1 \cdot 3\,\mathrm{m},\ \varphi_1 \cdot 3\,\mathrm{m} \big) = (-3, 3)\,\mathrm{mm}
$$
$$
\vec{s}_B = \big( -\varphi_2\,(0 - 4)\,\mathrm{m},\ \varphi_2\,(8 - 4)\,\mathrm{m} \big) = (-12, -12)\,\mathrm{mm}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad \vec{s}_C \text{ with body } 2 = \big( -\varphi_2\,(3 - 4)\,\mathrm{m},\ \varphi_2\,(3 - 4)\,\mathrm{m} \big) = (-3, 3)\,\mathrm{mm}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The center $C_2$ of member $CB$ is on the line of the two-force link, because the two-force link only allows $B$ perpendicular shifts to the pin-ended link, and for the **first theorem of kinematic chains** it is on the line $C_1 C_{12}$, the $y = x$ bisector. The intersection is $C_2 = (4, 4)$, a point outside member $CB$.

The relative center $C$ stands between $C_1$ and $C_2$, so the two rods rotate in opposite directions; $C$ is also $3\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C_1$ and $\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C_2$, therefore $|\varphi_2| = 3\,|\varphi_1|$. The displacement of $B$ has magnitude $|\varphi_2| \cdot \overline{C_2 B} = 0.003 \cdot 4\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 17\,\mathrm{mm}$ and is perpendicular to the pin-ended link, down to the left. The check recalculates the displacement of the hinge $C$ with the body $2$.

</div>
