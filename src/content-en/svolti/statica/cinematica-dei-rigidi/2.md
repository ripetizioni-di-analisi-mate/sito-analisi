## Exercise 1

Beam $AB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ has a roller at $A$ on a horizontal surface and a two-force link at $B$ inclined at $45^\circ$. Locate the center of rotation. For rotation $\varphi = 0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$, find the displacements of $A$, $B$, and midpoint $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,pendolo,315" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Beam with a roller at $A$ and a two-force link inclined at $45^\circ$ at point $B$"}

$$
\text{roller at } A: \ x = 0 ,
\qquad
\text{link at } B: \ y = -(x - 6)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
C = (0, 6)\,\mathrm{m}
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,pendolo,315" punti="0,6,C,centro" tratteggi="0,0 0,6.5;6,0 -0.4,6.4" titolo="Center $C$ is the intersection of the vertical through $A$ and the link axis"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec{s}_A &: \quad u_A = -\varphi\,(0 - 6)\,\mathrm{m} = 6\,\mathrm{mm} , \quad v_A = \varphi\,(0 - 0) = 0 \\[4pt]
\vec{s}_B &: \quad u_B = -\varphi\,(0 - 6)\,\mathrm{m} = 6\,\mathrm{mm} , \quad v_B = \varphi\,(6 - 0)\,\mathrm{m} = 6\,\mathrm{mm} \\[4pt]
\vec{s}_M &: \quad u_M = 6\,\mathrm{mm} , \quad v_M = \varphi\,(3 - 0)\,\mathrm{m} = 3\,\mathrm{mm}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad \vec{s}_B \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix} = \frac{6 - 6}{\sqrt{2}}\,\mathrm{mm} = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The roller support allows $A$ to move only horizontally, so the center lies on the normal to the support surface for $A$, the line $x = 0$. The two-force link allows $B$ to move only perpendicular to the pin-ended link, so the center lies on the line of the pin-ended link, which passes through $B$ with $-1$ slope. For the **center from the directions of two-point shifts** theorem, the center is the intersection of the two lines, $C = (0, 6)\,\mathrm{m}$, $6\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$.

The displacements are written with the rotation center formulas. All points of the beam have vertical coordinate $0$ and therefore the same horizontal displacement $6\,\mathrm{mm}$; the vertical displacement grows linearly with the horizontal distance from the center. The check checks that the $B$ move is perpendicular to the pin-ended link, i.e. that the two-force link is respected.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The L-shaped member $ADB$ has points $A = (0, 0)$, $D = (0, 3)$, and $B = (4, 3)$, with coordinates in meters. A roller at $A$ moves along a horizontal surface, and a two-force link at $B$ slopes downward to the left at $45^\circ$. Locate the center of rotation and determine the displacements of $A$, $D$, and $B$ for a clockwise rotation $\varphi = -0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,3;B,4,3" aste="A-D;D-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,pendolo,225" quote="A,D,3 m,-0.8;D,B,4 m,-0.7" titolo="L-shaped member with a roller at $A$ and a link at $B$"}

$$
\text{roller at } A: \ x = 0 ,
\qquad
\text{link at } B: \ y - 3 = x - 4
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
C = (0, -1)\,\mathrm{m}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec{s}_A &: \quad u_A = -\varphi\,\big( 0 - (-1) \big)\,\mathrm{m} = 0.002 \cdot 1\,\mathrm{m} = 2\,\mathrm{mm} , \quad v_A = 0 \\[4pt]
\vec{s}_D &: \quad u_D = -\varphi\,(3 + 1)\,\mathrm{m} = 8\,\mathrm{mm} , \quad v_D = \varphi\,(0 - 0) = 0 \\[4pt]
\vec{s}_B &: \quad u_B = -\varphi\,(3 + 1)\,\mathrm{m} = 8\,\mathrm{mm} , \quad v_B = \varphi\,(4 - 0)\,\mathrm{m} = -8\,\mathrm{mm}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad \vec{s}_B \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = \frac{8 - 8}{\sqrt{2}}\,\mathrm{mm} = 0
$$

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,3;B,4,3" aste="A-D;D-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,pendolo,225" punti="0,-1,C,centro" tratteggi="0,0 0,-1.4;4,3 -0.4,-1.4" frecce="0,3,0.8,3,s_D;4,3,4.8,2.2,s_B" titolo="Center $C$ lies below the roller, outside the L-shaped member. Arrows indicate the displacement directions for $\varphi < 0$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The center lies on the vertical through $A$, normal on the support surface of the roller support, and on the line of the two-force link, which passes through $B$ with $1$ slope. The intersection is $C = (0, -1)\,\mathrm{m}$, under roller support. The center of rotation can stay out of the body, and shifts are calculated as if the L-shaped member were extended to $C$.

With $\varphi < 0$, the L-shaped member rotates clockwise about $C$. The $D$ and $B$ points stand $4\,\mathrm{m}$ above the center, then move to the right of $|\varphi| \cdot 4\,\mathrm{m} = 8\,\mathrm{mm}$; the point $A$, $1\,\mathrm{m}$ above the center, moves to the right by $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. The point $D$ lies on the vertical of the center and has no vertical displacement, while **$B$ drops by $8\,\mathrm{mm}$**. The check checks that the $B$ shift is perpendicular to the pin-ended link, direction $(1, 1)$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Beam $AB$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ has a roller at $A$ on a surface inclined at $45^\circ$ and a two-force link at $B$ parallel to the normal to that surface. The horizontal displacement of $A$ is $u_A = 5\,\mathrm{mm}$. Locate the center of rotation and find the displacement of every point of the beam.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,225;B,pendolo,225" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Beam with a roller on a surface inclined at $45^\circ$ at point $A$ and a link at $B$"}

$$
\text{roller at } A: \ y = x ,
\qquad
\text{link at } B: \ y = x - 4
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
\vec{s}_A \cdot (1, 1) = u_A + v_A = 0 \\
\vec{s}_B \cdot (1, 1) = u_B + v_B = 0
\end{cases}
\qquad
u_A = 5\,\mathrm{mm}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
v_A = -5\,\mathrm{mm}
$$
$$
\varphi = 0 ,
\qquad
\vec{s}_P = (5, -5)\,\mathrm{mm} \quad \text{at every point } P
$$
$$
\text{check:} \quad u_B + v_B = 5\,\mathrm{mm} - 5\,\mathrm{mm} = 0
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The roller support at $A$ has the reaction, and therefore the normal to the support surface, in the direction $(1, 1)$; the center therefore lies on the line through $A$ of $1$ slope. The pin-ended link of the two-force link at $B$ has the same direction, and the center also lies on the line through $B$ of $1$ slope. The two lines are **parallel and distinct**, so the center is the point at infinity of the direction $(1, 1)$ and the beam moves with zero rotation.

The translation occurs perpendicular to the direction $(1, 1)$, i.e. along the support surface of the roller support. The system writes the two constraint conditions, which must be valid together: the displacement of $A$ and that of $B$ have zero component along $(1, 1)$. With $u_A = 5\,\mathrm{mm}$ the first gives $v_A = -5\,\mathrm{mm}$, and since the beam moves **each point moves $5\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right and $5\,\mathrm{mm}$ down**. Verification checks the condition of the two-force link at $B$.

</div>
