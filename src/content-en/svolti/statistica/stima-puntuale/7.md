## Exercise 1

Independent observations $2,3,7$ come from $U(0,\theta)$ with $\theta>0$. Use a density positive at the endpoints. Determine the maximum likelihood estimate.

$$
f_\theta(x)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac1\theta & \text{if } 0\le x\le\theta,\\[8pt]
0 & \text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
L(\theta)&=f_\theta(2)\,f_\theta(3)\,f_\theta(7),\\[6pt]
m&=\max(2,3,7)=7,
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
L(\theta)&=0 \quad \text{for } 0<\theta<7,\\[6pt]
L(\theta)&=\frac{1}{\theta^{3}} \quad \text{for } \theta\ge 7,\\[6pt]
\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm d\theta}\,\theta^{-3}&=-\frac{3}{\theta^4}<0,
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\widehat\theta=7,\qquad L(7)=\frac{1}{343}.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The likelihood is the product of three densities evaluated at the observations. Each factor is $\dfrac1\theta$ if the observation belongs to $[0,\theta]$ and zero otherwise. The largest observation, seven, imposes the tightest constraint. For an upper endpoint below seven its density factor is zero and the whole likelihood vanishes.

For endpoints at least seven all three data values are in the support, giving density product $\theta^{-3}$. Its derivative $-3\theta^{-4}$ is negative, so it decreases as $\theta$ increases and the smallest admissible value maximizes it. The endpoint convention admits seven, which attains maximum $L(7)=\dfrac{1}{343}$. The maximum likelihood estimate is therefore the **observed maximum**, $\widehat\theta=7$. With the open-interval convention $(0,\theta)$ the likelihood at $7$ would be zero and its supremum unattained. The endpoint convention determines whether a maximum exists.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Three observations $3,4,5$ are assumed uniform on $[\theta,\theta+3]$ with density positive at the endpoints. Find the set of maximum likelihood estimates.

$$
\begin{cases}
\theta\le3,\\[4pt]
\theta+3\ge5
\end{cases}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
2\le\theta\le3.
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
L(\theta)&=\left(\frac13\right)^3=\frac{1}{27} \quad \text{for } \theta\in[2,3],\\[6pt]
L(\theta)&=0 \quad \text{for } \theta<2 \text{ or } \theta>3.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\operatorname*{arg\,max}_{\theta\in\mathbb R}L(\theta)=[2,3].
$$

::grafico{curve="t,0,0,2;t,1/27,2,3;t,0,3,5" xmin=-0.35 xmax=5.55 ymin=-0.01 ymax=0.055 nomi="θ,L" punti="2,0,vuoto;3,0,vuoto;2,0.037037,pieno;3,0.037037,pieno" etichette="2.5,0.046,1⁄27" segmenti="2,0,2,0.037037;3,0,3,0.037037" legenda=0 titolo="All values of θ from 2 through 3, including both endpoints, give the same likelihood."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The support has length $3$, giving density $\dfrac13$ on $[\theta,\theta+3]$. Its lower endpoint must be at most the observed minimum, $\theta\le3$. Its upper endpoint must reach the observed maximum, $\theta+3\ge5$, giving $\theta\ge2$. Together these constraints also include the middle observation $4$.

Every compatible parameter gives three equal factors of one-third, so the likelihood is $\dfrac{1}{27}$ throughout $[2,3]$ and zero outside. An entire interval attains the maximum, and every $\theta\in[2,3]$ is a **maximum likelihood estimate**. The interval has width $3-(5-3)=1$, the difference between the support length and the observed range.

</div>
