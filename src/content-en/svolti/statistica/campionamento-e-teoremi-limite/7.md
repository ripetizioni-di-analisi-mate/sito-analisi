## Exercise 1

The preparation time for an order has mean $24\,\mathrm{min}$ and standard deviation $8\,\mathrm{min}$. For $64$ i.i.d. orders, approximate $P(\bar X_{64}<23\,\mathrm{min})$ using the central limit theorem. Use $\Phi(-1)\approx0.1587$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
E[\bar X_{64}]&=24\,\mathrm{min},\\[6pt]
SE(\bar X_{64})&=\frac{8}{\sqrt{64}}\,\mathrm{min}=\frac88\,\mathrm{min}
=1\,\mathrm{min},\\[6pt]
\bar X_{64}&\approx N(24,1),\\[6pt]
z&=\frac{23-24}{1}=-1,\\[6pt]
P(\bar X_{64}<23\,\mathrm{min})
&\approx\Phi(-1)\approx0.1587.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The distribution of individual preparation times is unspecified, but the times are i.i.d. with finite positive variance. The **central limit theorem** applies, so for $64$ orders approximate the sample-mean distribution by a normal distribution centered at $24\,\mathrm{min}$. Its standard deviation is the standard error $\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}$, namely $\dfrac{8}{8}=1\,\mathrm{min}$.

Divide the deviation of the threshold from the center, $23-24=-1\,\mathrm{min}$, by the standard error of the mean. Its standardized value is $-1$, negative because the threshold lies below the center. The event $\bar X_{64}<23$ becomes $Z<-1$ for the standardized variable. The normal distribution function gives left-tail area. Continuity of the approximating normal gives the same area for strict or inclusive inequalities. The mean time for $64$ orders is below $23\,\mathrm{min}$ with probability **approximately $0.1587$**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The masses of $100$ i.i.d. packages have mean $250\,\mathrm{g}$ and standard deviation $20\,\mathrm{g}$. Approximate $P(248\,\mathrm{g}<\bar X_{100}<253\,\mathrm{g})$. Use $\Phi(1.5)\approx0.9332$ and $\Phi(-1)\approx0.1587$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
SE(\bar X_{100})&=\frac{20}{\sqrt{100}}\,\mathrm{g}=\frac{20}{10}\,\mathrm{g}=2\,\mathrm{g},\\[6pt]
z_1&=\frac{248-250}{2}=\frac{-2}{2}=-1,\\[6pt]
z_2&=\frac{253-250}{2}=\frac32=1.5,\\[6pt]
P(248<\bar X_{100}<253)
&\approx P(-1<Z<1.5)\\[6pt]
&=\Phi(1.5)-\Phi(-1)\\[6pt]
&\approx0.9332-0.1587=0.7745.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The masses are i.i.d. with finite variance. By the **central limit theorem**, the mean of $100$ packages is approximately normal with center $250\,\mathrm{g}$ and standard error $2\,\mathrm{g}$. Standardize the cutoffs separately because their distances from the mean differ. The lower one is one standard error below the center and the upper one is one and a half above.

The interval for the mean corresponds to the interval from $-1$ to $1.5$ for the standardized variable. Subtract the area to the left of the lower cutoff from the area to the left of the upper cutoff. This difference is the approximating normal area. The mean falls between $248\,\mathrm{g}$ and $253\,\mathrm{g}$ with probability **approximately $0.7745$**.

</div>
