## Exercise 1

A library records loans of books and magazines. Branch A has 18 book loans and 12 magazine loans. Branch B has 9 book loans and 21 magazine loans. Find the marginal count for each type of loan and the grand total.

| Branch | Books | Magazines | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | 18 | 12 | 30 |
| B | 9 | 21 | 30 |
| Total | 27 | 33 | 60 |

$$
\begin{aligned}
n_{\cdot,\mathrm{books}}&=18+9=27\\[6pt]
n_{\cdot,\mathrm{magazines}}&=12+21=33\\[6pt]
n&=27+33=60\\[6pt]
n_{\mathrm{A},\cdot}+n_{\mathrm{B},\cdot}&=(18+12)+(9+21)=30+30=60.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Arrange the data in a two-way table with branches in the rows and types of loans in the columns. Each loan belongs to exactly one branch and one type, so it appears in exactly one cell.

The marginal total for each type includes both branches because $n_{\cdot j}=\sum_i n_{ij}$ sums down the column. Adding the two counts in the books column gives **27**. The same calculation for the magazines column gives **33**.

The two column totals add to 60 because each loan appears in just one column. The row totals are 30 for each branch and also add to 60 because each loan appears in just one row. The **grand total is therefore 60**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A table of 70 shipments has rows North, Central, and South, and columns On time and Late. In the North, 18 shipments were on time and 4 were late. In the Central region, 14 were on time. In the South, 9 were late. In total, 50 shipments were on time. Complete the table and find the marginal relative frequency of the Central region.

$$
\begin{aligned}
n_{\mathrm{South,p}}&=50-18-14=18\\[6pt]
n_{\cdot,\mathrm{rit}}&=70-50=20\\[6pt]
n_{\mathrm{Center,late}}&=20-4-9=7
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
n_{\mathrm{North},\cdot}&=18+4=22\\[6pt]
n_{\mathrm{Center},\cdot}&=14+7=21\\[6pt]
n_{\mathrm{South},\cdot}&=18+9=27\\[6pt]
f_{\mathrm{Center},\cdot}&=\frac{21}{70}=\frac{3}{10}=0.30.
\end{aligned}
$$

| Region | On time | Late | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| North | 18 | 4 | 22 |
| Central | 14 | 7 | 21 |
| South | 18 | 9 | 27 |
| Total | 50 | 20 | 70 |

<div class="spiegazione">

The on-time column has total 50 and two known cells, 18 and 14. Its total determines the missing entry in the first column: 18 on-time shipments in the South.

The grand total also gives the total number of late shipments because each shipment belongs to exactly one of the two columns. This gives $70-50=20$. Subtracting the late shipments already recorded for North and South leaves 7 in the Central region.

With all cells known, summing across the rows gives row totals of 22, 21, and 27. Their sum is 70 because each shipment belongs to exactly one region. The marginal relative frequency of the Central region is its share of the whole table, so divide its row total by 70. The Central region accounts for $30\%$ of shipments.

</div>
