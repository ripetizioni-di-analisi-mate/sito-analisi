## Exercise 1

Find the mean of the four response times $12,18,11,15$ minutes and their total duration.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T&=(12+18+11+15)\,\mathrm{min}=56\,\mathrm{min}\\[6pt]
\bar x&=\frac{56}{4}\,\mathrm{min}=14\,\mathrm{min}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
x_i-\bar x&:\quad 12-14=-2,\quad 18-14=4,\quad 11-14=-3,\quad 15-14=1\\[6pt]
\sum_i(x_i-\bar x)&=-2+4-3+1=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=20.5 nomi="i,xᵢ" punti="1,12;2,18;3,11;4,15" linee="0.4,14 4.6,14" segmenti="1,12,1,14;2,14,2,18;3,11,3,14;4,14,4,15" etichette="4.65,14.6,14" titolo="The four times in the order recorded. The horizontal line marks the mean $14$. The vertical segments show the deviations $-2$, $4$, $-3$, and $1$, which sum to zero."}

<div class="spiegazione">

Add the four times, measured in the same units, to find the total $T$. Each observation has equal weight, so divide the total by four. The result, $14\,\mathrm{min}$, is the time each observation would have if the four times were equal and their total remained $56\,\mathrm{min}$.

The total time requested is $T=56\,\mathrm{min}$. It can also be recovered as $4\cdot14\,\mathrm{min}$, the number of observations times the mean.

As a check, calculate the deviations from the mean. The times of $11$ and $12$ minutes are a combined five minutes below the mean, whereas those of $15$ and $18$ minutes are a combined five minutes above. The deviations therefore sum to zero, as deviations from the mean do for every data set. A nonzero sum would indicate an error in the total or the division.

The mean response time is **$14\,\mathrm{min}$**, and the total time is **$56\,\mathrm{min}$**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The calculated mean mass for eight shipments was $11\,\mathrm{kg}$. A mass of $21\,\mathrm{kg}$ had been entered as $13\,\mathrm{kg}$. Find the corrected mean.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T_{\rm registrato}&=8\cdot11\,\mathrm{kg}=88\,\mathrm{kg}\\[6pt]
T_{\rm corretto}&=(88-13+21)\,\mathrm{kg}=96\,\mathrm{kg}\\[6pt]
\bar x_{\rm corretta}&=\frac{96}{8}\,\mathrm{kg}=12\,\mathrm{kg}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bar x_{\rm corretta}-\bar x_{\rm registrata}
&=\frac{21-13}{8}\,\mathrm{kg}\\[6pt]
&=\frac{8}{8}\,\mathrm{kg}=1\,\mathrm{kg}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Recover the total used in the calculation by multiplying the recorded mean by the number of observations. That total includes $13\,\mathrm{kg}$, so subtract the incorrect value before adding the correct one.

This is a replacement and there are still eight shipments. Divide the new total by eight to obtain the corrected mean $12\,\mathrm{kg}$.

The second calculation gives the same result without reconstructing the total. The total increases by $21-13=8\,\mathrm{kg}$. Since there are still eight observations, spreading the increase across them raises the mean by $\dfrac{8}{8}\,\mathrm{kg}=1\,\mathrm{kg}$. Thus $\bar x_{\rm corretta}=11+1=12\,\mathrm{kg}$, in agreement with the first calculation.

The other seven masses are not needed because their contribution to the total is unchanged. The corrected mean mass for the eight shipments is **$12\,\mathrm{kg}$**.

</div>
