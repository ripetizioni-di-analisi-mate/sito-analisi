## Exercise 1

The time of a reading, in hours, is uniformly distributed between $8$ and $14$. Calculate the probability that the reading is taken after $12$ and before $16$.

$$
X\sim U(8,14),\qquad f_X(x)=\frac{1}{14-8}=\frac16\quad\text{for }8<x<14.
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(12,16)\cap(8,14)&=(12,14),\\[6pt]
P(12<X<16)&=\int_{12}^{14}\frac16\,dx+\int_{14}^{16}0\,dx\\[6pt]
&=\frac{14-12}{14-8}=\frac26=\frac13.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{linee="0,0 8,0;8,0.166667 14,0.166667;14,0 16.6,0" area="1/6;0;12;14" segmenti="8,0,8,0.166667;12,0,12,0.166667;14,0,14,0.166667" xmin=-0.6 xmax=16.6 ymin=-0.035 ymax=0.225 nomi="x,f_X" legenda=0 titolo="Only the portion from 12 noon to 2 p.m. lies in the support and contributes to the requested probability."}

<div class="spiegazione">

By the definition of the **uniform distribution on an interval**, the density is the reciprocal of the support length, namely $\dfrac16\,\mathrm{h}^{-1}$, between $8$ and $14$, and zero elsewhere. The model therefore assigns probability only to times between $8$ and $14$.

The requested interval runs from $12$ to $16$ and splits into two parts. Between $12$ and $14$ the density is $\dfrac16$. Between $14$ and $16$ it is zero, so this segment contributes nothing. Only the two hours preceding $14$ count, namely the intersection $(12,14)$.

The density is constant on the support, so the integral is length times height. Divide the length that contributes, $2$ hours, by the six hours in total. The probability that the reading is taken after $12$ and before $16$ is $\dfrac13$.

Using the full width of the requested interval would include the times from $14$ to $16$, which have zero probability, and give the incorrect result $\dfrac46$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Let $X\sim U(0,12)$. Given $X>4$, calculate the probability that $6<X<9$ or $X>11$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=\{6<X<9\}\cup\{X>11\},\qquad A\subseteq\{X>4\},\\[6pt]
P(A)&=\frac{(9-6)+(12-11)}{12}=\frac{3+1}{12}=\frac4{12},\\[6pt]
P(X>4)&=\frac{12-4}{12}=\frac8{12},\\[6pt]
P(A\mid X>4)&=\frac{P(A\cap\{X>4\})}{P(X>4)}=\frac{P(A)}{P(X>4)}\\[6pt]
&=\frac{\frac4{12}}{\frac8{12}}=\frac48=\frac12.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The conditional probability is the probability of the intersection divided by the probability of the conditioning event, which here is $P(X>4)=\dfrac8{12}>0$. Let $A$ denote the event of interest.

The two intervals forming $A$ are disjoint, so their probabilities add. The first has length $3$. The second contributes only from $11$ to $12$ because the density is zero beyond $12$. Its contributing length is $1$. Since the density equals $\dfrac1{12}$ on the support, we obtain $P(A)=\dfrac4{12}$.

Every point in $A$ exceeds $6$ and therefore exceeds $4$. Thus the union is contained in $\{X>4\}$, and the intersection in the conditional probability is $A$ itself.

The common factor $\dfrac1{12}$ cancels in the ratio. Four units of length are favorable out of the eight allowed by the condition, giving conditional probability $\dfrac12$. Equivalently, given $X>4$, the variable is uniform on $(4,12)$.

</div>
