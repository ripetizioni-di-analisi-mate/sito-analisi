## Exercise 1

Let $X\sim N(70,16)$. Using $\Phi(1.5)=0.9332$, calculate $P(X<64)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mu&=70,\qquad \sigma=\sqrt{16}=4,\\[6pt]
P(X<64)&=P\!\left(\frac{X-70}{4}<\frac{64-70}{4}\right)\\[6pt]
       &=P\!\left(Z<-\frac64\right)=\Phi(-1.5)\\[6pt]
       &=1-\Phi(1.5)=1-0.9332=0.0668.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{f="exp(-x*x/2)/sqrt(2*PI)" area="exp(-x*x/2)/sqrt(2*PI);0;-4.2;-1.5" segmenti="-1.5,0,-1.5,0.12952" etichette="-1.5,0.17,−1.5" xmin=-4.2 xmax=4.2 ymin=-0.045 ymax=0.46 nomi="z,f_Z" legenda="f_Z(z)" titolo="The shaded tail to the left of $z=-1.5$ corresponds to $X<64$ and has area $0.0668$."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The second normal parameter is the variance, so standardization uses its square root $\sigma=4$ in the denominator. By the **normal standardization theorem**, $Z=\dfrac{X-70}{4}$ is standard normal. Subtracting $70$ and dividing by $4>0$ preserves the direction of the inequality, so event $\{X<64\}$ equals $\{Z<-1.5\}$.

The threshold is six units below the mean, or one and a half standard deviations, so its standardized value is negative. The table entry is for the positive threshold $1.5$. By symmetry of the standard normal density, the area to the left of $-1.5$ equals the area to the right of $1.5$, found by subtracting the table entry from one.

The probability that $X$ is less than $64$ is therefore $0.0668$, or approximately $6.7\%$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

The weight $X$ of a part, in grams, follows $N(100,25)$. A part meets specifications if its weight is between $95$ and $110\,\mathrm{g}$. Using $\Phi(1)\approx0.84134$ and $\Phi(2)\approx0.97725$, calculate the probability of rejection.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma&=\sqrt{25}=5\,\mathrm{g},\\[6pt]
z_{95}&=\frac{95-100}{5}=-1,\qquad
z_{110}=\frac{110-100}{5}=2,\\[6pt]
P(\text{deviation})
 &=P(X<95)+P(X>110)\\[6pt]
 &=\Phi(-1)+1-\Phi(2)\\[6pt]
 &=\bigl(1-\Phi(1)\bigr)+\bigl(1-\Phi(2)\bigr)\\[6pt]
 &\approx(1-0.84134)+(1-0.97725)\\[6pt]
 &=0.15866+0.02275=0.18141.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{f="exp(-x*x/2)/sqrt(2*PI)" area="exp(-x*x/2)/sqrt(2*PI);0;-4.5;-1|exp(-x*x/2)/sqrt(2*PI);0;2;4.5" segmenti="-1,0,-1,0.24197;2,0,2,0.053991" xmin=-4.5 xmax=4.5 ymin=-0.05 ymax=0.46 nomi="z,f_Z" legenda="f_Z(z)" titolo="Items below 95 grams or above 110 grams are rejected. Add the area to the left of $z=-1$ to the area to the right of $z=2$."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The variance is $25\,\mathrm{g}^2$, so the standard deviation is $5\,\mathrm{g}$. A part is rejected if its weight is below the lower limit or above the upper limit. These tails are disjoint, so add their probabilities.

After **standardization**, the limits become $-1$ and $2$ because they are one and two standard deviations from the mean, respectively. The distances differ, so the tail areas differ. Use symmetry for the left tail, $\Phi(-1)=1-\Phi(1)=0.15866$. For the right tail, subtract the cumulative distribution function at $2$ from one, giving $0.02275$.

The sum gives a rejection probability of approximately $0.181$. Thus about $18\%$ of parts are rejected, most below $95\,\mathrm{g}$ because $0.15866$ exceeds $0.02275$. Taking the complement of the probability of meeting specifications, $\Phi(2)-\Phi(-1)\approx0.81859$, gives the same value.

</div>
