## Exercise 1: A requested early arrival

Read: “The train leaves at 8\:15. Please arrive ten minutes early.” What arrival time is requested?

- At 8\:05.
- At 8\:15.
- At 8\:25.
- At 8\:10.
- At 7\:15.

<div class="spiegazione">

**Answer: At 8\:05.**

*Ten minutes early* requires arrival ten minutes before departure. Subtracting ten minutes from 8\:15 gives 8\:05. 8\:25 would be ten minutes later; 8\:10 would allow only five minutes of early arrival.

Separate the event time, 8\:15, from the instruction to the traveler, ten minutes earlier. Subtraction concerns minutes on the same day and does not cross an hour boundary: fifteen minus ten gives five. The requested 8\:05 arrival does not change the train's departure time, which remains that stated in the first sentence.

</div>

## Exercise 2: The order of an instruction

Read: “Weigh the empty container. Then add the powder and weigh the container again.” What happens immediately before the second weighing?

- Adding the powder.
- Emptying the container again.
- Washing the scale.
- Writing a label.
- Removing the powder.

<div class="spiegazione">

**Answer: Adding the powder.**

The first weighing concerns the empty container. *Then* introduces adding the powder, followed by the second weighing. The other operations are not requested; removing the powder would undo the change distinguishing the second measurement from the first.

The actions form the sequence “weigh empty container → add powder → weigh again.” The second use of *weigh* keeps the same container but changes the mass of its contents. To find the immediately preceding action, locate the step between the weighings. No outside laboratory procedure is needed: the order is fully expressed in the instructions.

</div>

## Exercise 3: A condition for a copy

Read: “Submit the form online. A printed copy is required only if the upload fails.” The upload succeeded. What can be concluded?

- These instructions do not require a printed copy in this case.
- A printed copy is compulsory in every case.
- Printing any copy is forbidden.
- A successful upload becomes valid only after a printed copy arrives.
- A printed copy must be submitted before the first upload attempt.

<div class="spiegazione">

**Answer: These instructions do not require a printed copy in this case.**

*Only if* limits the copy requirement to upload failure. Since uploading succeeded here, the requirement does not apply. This conclusion is not a ban on printing: the text concerns whether a copy is required, not a general prohibition.

Start from the given fact: uploading succeeded. The necessary condition for the message to require a copy, upload failure, did not occur. Conversely, *only if* does not establish that every upload failure alone is sufficient to require a copy. The result is therefore a conclusion about the absence of obligation. Choosing “Printing is forbidden” would add a ban absent from the notice; choosing an always-compulsory copy would erase the restriction introduced by *only if*.

</div>
