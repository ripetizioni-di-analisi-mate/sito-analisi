## Exercise 1

A train starts at coordinate $100\,\mathrm m$ and travels with $v=-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What coordinate does it reach after $10\,\mathrm s$?

::grafico{f="100-12*x" xmin=0 xmax=11 ymin=-37 ymax=106 nomi="t,x" punti="0,100,pieno;10,-20,pieno" titolo="The line crosses the spatial origin before 10 s"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=x_0+vt\\[4pt]&=100-12\cdot10\\[4pt]
&= -20\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The positive axis points against the motion. In ten seconds the train is displaced by $-120\,\mathrm m$ from its starting coordinate of $100\,\mathrm m$. It therefore passes the origin just after eight seconds and its final coordinate is negative. Distance travelled is still $120\,\mathrm m$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A particle is at $x=9\,\mathrm m$ at $t=3\,\mathrm s$ and moves with constant velocity $v=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Where is it at $t=8\,\mathrm s$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=x_1+v(t-t_1)\\[4pt]&=9+2(8-3)\\[4pt]
&= 19\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The $9\,\mathrm m$ is the position at $3\,\mathrm s$. Starting from that datum, the duration is $8-3=5\,\mathrm s$. Over this interval the particle advances $10\,\mathrm m$. Using all eight seconds would wrongly include motion before the given starting position.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A cart moves for $4\,\mathrm s$ at $+3\,\mathrm{m/s}$, then for $5\,\mathrm s$ at $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. It starts at $x=6\,\mathrm m$. What is its final coordinate?

::grafico{segmenti="0,6,4,18;4,18,9,8" xmin=0 xmax=10 ymin=0 ymax=21 nomi="t,x" punti="0,6,pieno;4,18,pieno;9,8,pieno" titolo="The second stage starts from the coordinate reached in the first"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
x_f&=6+3\cdot4+(-2)\cdot5\\[4pt]
&= 8\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The cart first advances $12\,\mathrm m$ and reaches $18\,\mathrm m$. The second stage starts there and subtracts $10\,\mathrm m$. The overall change is $+2\,\mathrm m$ from the start, while the requested coordinate also includes the initial $6\,\mathrm m$.

</div>
