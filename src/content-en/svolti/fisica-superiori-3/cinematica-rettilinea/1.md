## Exercise 1

A train starts at coordinate $100\,\mathrm m$ and travels with $v=-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What coordinate does it reach after $10\,\mathrm s$?

::grafico{f="100-12*x" xmin=0 xmax=10.9 ymin=-40 ymax=110 nomi="t (s),x (m)" punti="0,100,pieno;10,-20,pieno" etichette="0.34,101.6,100 m;8.31,-26.9,−20 m" titolo="The line crosses the spatial origin before 10 s"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=x_0+vt\\[4pt]&=100-12\cdot10\\[4pt]
&= -20\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The positive axis points against the motion. In ten seconds the train is displaced by $-120\,\mathrm m$ from its starting coordinate of $100\,\mathrm m$. It therefore passes the origin just after eight seconds and its final coordinate is negative. Distance travelled is still $120\,\mathrm m$.

The origin crossing satisfies $100-12t=0$, so $t=\dfrac{25}{3}\,\mathrm s$. It occurs before the requested reading. The negative final position is consistent with the crossing. The train has covered $120\,\mathrm m$ and ends twenty metres on the negative side of the origin.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A particle is at $x=9\,\mathrm m$ at $t=3\,\mathrm s$ and moves with constant velocity $v=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Where is it at $t=8\,\mathrm s$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=x_1+v(t-t_1)\\[4pt]&=9+2(8-3)\\[4pt]
&= 19\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The $9\,\mathrm m$ is the position at $3\,\mathrm s$. Starting from that datum, the duration is $8-3=5\,\mathrm s$. Over this interval the particle advances $10\,\mathrm m$. Using all eight seconds would wrongly include motion before the given starting position.

Using the same clock, the law is $x(t)=9+2(t-3)=3+2t$ in SI units. The intercept is $3\,\mathrm m$ if the model is extended to $t=0$. Substituting time eight gives the final coordinate again without treating the first reading as time zero.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Two sensors use the same axis to track a trolley in uniform motion. A reads $x=8\,\mathrm m$ when its clock shows $1\,\mathrm s$. B reads $x=20\,\mathrm m$ when its clock shows $11\,\mathrm s$. B's clock is $4\,\mathrm s$ ahead. Find the velocity and the position when A's clock shows $10\,\mathrm s$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_{2,A}&=(11-4)\,\mathrm s=7\,\mathrm s,\\[4pt]
v&=\frac{20-8}{7-1}\,\mathrm{m/s}=2\,\mathrm{m/s},\\[4pt]
x(10\,\mathrm s)&=[8+2(10-1)]\,\mathrm m=26\,\mathrm m.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Express both readings on A's clock. At eleven seconds on B, A reads seven. Six seconds therefore pass between the readings, while the trolley travels twelve metres in the positive direction. The constant velocity gives $x(t)=8+2(t-1)$, with $t$ on A's clock and values in SI units. The model also reproduces B's reading. Subtracting the readings of two different clocks directly would give ten seconds and make the slope artificially small. The position at ten seconds extrapolates the stated uniform motion.

</div>
