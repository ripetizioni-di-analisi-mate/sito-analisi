## Exercise 1

An object has $v_0=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $a=-4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its velocity after $2\,\mathrm s$ if the same acceleration persists beyond the reversal?

::grafico{curve="t,6-4*t,0,2" punti="1.5,0,pieno;2,-2,pieno" etichette="1.452,-0.85,1.5" xmin=0 xmax=2.4 ymin=-4 ymax=7 nomi="t (s),v (m/s)" titolo="Velocity crosses zero before 2 s"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=v_0+at=6-4\cdot2\\[4pt]
&= -2\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The change in velocity is $-8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Its magnitude exceeds the initial speed, so the body passes through $v=0$ at $1.5\,\mathrm s$ and moves in the negative direction. The requested quantity is signed velocity and retains that sign.

The crossing of zero satisfies $6-4t=0$, so it occurs at $1.5\,\mathrm s$. Final velocity is negative because the model continues for another $0.5\,\mathrm s$ at the same acceleration. A model holding the trolley still after the stop would give a different later velocity.

</div>

## Exercise 2

In a constant-acceleration stage, velocity rises from $2$ to $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$ over $6\,\mathrm s$. What distance is covered?

$$
\begin{aligned}
s&=\frac{v_0+v_f}{2}t=\frac{2+14}{2}\cdot6\\[4pt]
&= 48\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The velocity–time graph is a straight line above the axis. Its trapezium area uses the mean of the two endpoint velocities as its average height. This is $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and covers forty-eight metres in six seconds.

As a check, acceleration is $\dfrac{14-2}{6}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. In the position equation the initial velocity contributes $12\,\mathrm m$ and acceleration $36\,\mathrm m$. Their sum agrees with the trapezium area. Velocities are positive throughout, so distance and displacement have the same value.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A trolley starts at $x=5\,\mathrm m$ with $v_0=-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and maintains $a=+2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for the first $4\,\mathrm s$. When does it pass $x=2\,\mathrm m$? Find the velocity on each pass.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x(t)&=5-4t+t^2,\\[4pt]
x(t)=2&\iff t^2-4t+3=0\iff(t-1)(t-3)=0,\\[4pt]
v(1\,\mathrm s)&=(-4+2)\,\mathrm{m/s}=-2\,\mathrm{m/s},\\[4pt]
v(3\,\mathrm s)&=(-4+6)\,\mathrm{m/s}=+2\,\mathrm{m/s}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The position equation has two positive roots and both belong to the stated interval. The first pass occurs while the trolley moves towards smaller coordinates. The second follows the change of sign of velocity. The reversal is at $t=2\,\mathrm s$, where position reaches its minimum of $1\,\mathrm m$. The equation $v^2=v_0^2+2a\Delta x$ confirms the same speed at both passages, because displacement from the start is $-3\,\mathrm m$ in each case. The time of each event determines the direction.

</div>
