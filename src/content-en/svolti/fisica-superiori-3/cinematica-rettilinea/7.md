## Exercise 1

An object falls from rest from $20\,\mathrm m$. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. How many seconds pass before it reaches the ground?

::grafico{curve="t,20-5*t^2,0,2" punti="0,20,pieno;2,0,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=2.2 ymin=-1 ymax=22 nomi="t,y" titolo="The parabola reaches the ground at 2 s"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
t&=\sqrt{\frac{2h}{g}}=\sqrt{\frac{2\cdot20}{10}}\\[4pt]
&= 2\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The vertical axis points up and height is $y=20-5t^2$. Contact occurs when height reaches zero. Of the two roots, only the positive one describes an event after release. The graph ends at contact because the model changes after impact.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An object is dropped from a tower. It reaches the ground $3\,\mathrm s$ after the release is recorded. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. How high is the tower?

$$
\begin{aligned}
h&=\tfrac12gt^2=\tfrac12\cdot10\cdot3^2\\[4pt]
&= 45\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The interval starts at release, so initial velocity is zero. The fall increases speed to $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Average speed is $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for the three seconds, giving $45\,\mathrm m$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

From a height of $30\,\mathrm m$, a sphere is given an initial velocity of $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ downwards. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. When does it hit the ground?

$$
\begin{aligned}
30&=5t+5t^2\\[4pt] t&=\frac{-5+\sqrt{25+600}}{10}\\[4pt]
&= 2\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Taking downwards as positive, the initial term $5t$ adds to the acceleration term. The equation factors as $(t-2)(t+3)=0$. The physical time is two seconds, which gives ten metres from the initial term and twenty from the acceleration term.

</div>
