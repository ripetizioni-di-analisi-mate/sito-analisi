## Exercise 1

A runner goes from $x=0$ to $x=16\,\mathrm m$ and back to $x=4\,\mathrm m$ in a total of $8\,\mathrm s$. What is the average speed?

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_{\mathrm{rap}}&=\frac{16+(16-4)}{8}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The route includes $16\,\mathrm m$ outward and $12\,\mathrm m$ on the return. Add both lengths as positive and divide by the total time. Average velocity would be $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ because it would use only the final displacement of four metres.

The displacement is only $4\,\mathrm m$, showing the distinction. Its average velocity is $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the positive direction. The return leg reduces displacement but adds to the total distance run, which enters the average speed.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A sensor records $x=10\,\mathrm m$ at $t=1\,\mathrm s$ and $x=-5\,\mathrm m$ at $t=6\,\mathrm s$. Find the average velocity.

::grafico{segmenti="1,10,6,-5" punti="1,10,pieno;6,-5,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=7 ymin=-8 ymax=12 nomi="t (s),x (m)" titolo="The secant through the two readings has slope −3 m/s"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_m&=\frac{x_f-x_i}{t_f-t_i}\\[4pt]&=\frac{-5-10}{6-1}\\[4pt]
&= -3\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The final coordinate is fifteen metres below the first. Five seconds pass between the readings. The negative sign describes the net displacement direction even if the motion was not uniform.

With the origin moved $10\,\mathrm m$ forward, the readings would be zero and $-15\,\mathrm m$. The secant's slope would be unchanged. The two recordings determine the overall ratio, whereas an instantaneous velocity would require closer readings or a model.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A trolley is at $2\,\mathrm m$ at $t=0$, at $8\,\mathrm m$ at $t=2\,\mathrm s$ and at $6\,\mathrm m$ at $t=5\,\mathrm s$. Its only reversal is between the last two readings, at maximum position $M$. Its average speed over the five seconds is $2.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find $M$ and its signed average velocity.

$$
\begin{aligned}
d&=(2.4\cdot5)\,\mathrm m=12\,\mathrm m,\\[4pt]
d&=(M-2\,\mathrm m)+(M-6\,\mathrm m),\\[4pt]
M&=\frac{12+2+6}{2}\,\mathrm m=10\,\mathrm m,\\[6pt]
v_m&=\frac{6-2}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s}=0.8\,\mathrm{m/s}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Average speed determines the total distance travelled. With one reversal, the outward leg is the distance from $2\,\mathrm m$ to $M$ and the inward leg runs from $M$ to $6\,\mathrm m$. The intermediate reading of $8\,\mathrm m$ is before the maximum, as the result confirms. The outward leg is eight metres and the return four, recovering the total of twelve metres. To calculate average velocity the two lengths have opposite signs. This leaves four metres of positive displacement over five seconds.

</div>
