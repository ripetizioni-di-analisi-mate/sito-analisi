## Exercise 1

A wheel of radius $0.40\,\mathrm m$ rolls without slipping with $\left\lvert\omega\right\rvert=5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Find the speed of its centre.

::grafico{uguale=1 assi=0 curve=".4*cos(t),.4+.4*sin(t),0,6.283185" linee="-.6,0 1.05,0" vettori="0,.4,.3,.4,v_CM;0,.8,.6,.8,v" punti="0,0,pieno,C" xmin=-.65 xmax=1.1 ymin=-.2 ymax=1.05 titolo="Contact point C is instantaneously at rest"}

$$
v_{CM}=\left\lvert\omega\right\rvert{}R,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_{CM}&=2 \cdot \frac{1}{5} \cdot 5\\[4pt]&=2\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

At contact, translation and rotation cancel. The centre speed equals the tangential speed of the rim about the centre.

On fixed ground the bottom point receives forward translational velocity and backward rotational velocity. Without slipping those magnitudes must agree. Here $0.40\cdot5=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ makes contact-point velocity zero, confirming the centre speed.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A roller of radius $0.25\,\mathrm m$ advances at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ without slipping. Find the speed of its top point.

$$
v_{\mathrm{top}}=v_{CM}+\left\lvert\omega\right\rvert{}R,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_{\mathrm{top}}&=2 \cdot 3\\[4pt]&=6\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

At the top, both contributions point forward. Their magnitudes agree, giving twice the centre speed.

The constraint gives $\omega=\dfrac{3}{0.25}=12\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ in magnitude. At the top, rotation adds another $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the centre's direction. At the bottom those contributions instead cancel, consistent with no slip.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A wheel rolls without slipping on fixed ground and moves forward at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the speed of the frontmost rim point level with the centre?

$$
v=\sqrt{v_{CM}^2+(\omega R)^2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v&=\sqrt{4^{2} + 4^{2}}\\[4pt]&\simeq5.65685\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Rotation gives a vertical component at that point. Total velocity sums two perpendicular contributions of equal magnitude.

For a wheel moving right, rotation is clockwise. At the front its velocity relative to the centre is therefore vertically downwards with magnitude four metres per second. The centre's translation is horizontal to the right. These arrows are perpendicular, giving $4\sqrt2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ rather than eight.

</div>
