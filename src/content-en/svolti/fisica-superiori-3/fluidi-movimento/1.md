## Exercise 1

The average normal speed across a section of $0.004\,\mathrm{m^2}$ is $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find flow rate in litres per second.

::grafico{assi=0 linee="-2,1 2,1;-2,-1 2,-1;0,-1 0,1" vettori="-1.6,0,1.5,0,v" xmin=-2.5 xmax=2.7 ymin=-1.5 ymax=1.7 titolo="The indicated velocity is normal to the section"}

$$
Q=1000Av,\qquad
\begin{aligned}Q&=4 \cdot \frac{1}{1000} \cdot 2 \cdot 1000\\[4pt]&=8\,\mathrm{L/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Area times speed gives eight thousandths of a cubic metre per second. Each cubic metre contains one thousand litres.

In one second a $2\,\mathrm m$ long slice of liquid crosses the section. Its volume is $0.004\,\mathrm{m^2}\cdot2\,\mathrm m=0.008\,\mathrm{m^3}$, or $8\,\mathrm L$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A pipe has a circular section of diameter $0.10\,\mathrm m$. With a mean normal speed of $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ find flow rate.

$$
Q=\pi(d/2)^2v,\qquad
\begin{aligned}Q&=\pi \cdot \left(1 \cdot \frac{1}{20}\right)^{2} \cdot 2\\[4pt]&\simeq0.015708\,\mathrm{m^3/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Area uses radius, half of diameter. Squaring would make a calculation with diameter instead of radius four times too large.

The section area is $0.0025\pi\,\mathrm{m^2}$. With $v=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ the product is $0.005\pi\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, about $15.7\,\mathrm{L/s}$ if expressed in litres per second.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Velocity is uniform across an area of $0.020\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and makes $60^\circ$ to its normal. The speed is $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find volume flow crossing the face.

$$
Q=Av\cos\theta,\qquad
\begin{aligned}Q&=2 \cdot \frac{1}{100} \cdot 4 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\\[4pt]&=0.04\,\mathrm{m^3/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Only motion in the normal direction crosses the face. The speed to multiply by area is two metres per second.

The normal component is $4\cos60^\circ=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the tangential component runs along the face. As a check, tilting the flow by $60^\circ$ halves the rate compared with perpendicular flow at the same speed.

</div>
