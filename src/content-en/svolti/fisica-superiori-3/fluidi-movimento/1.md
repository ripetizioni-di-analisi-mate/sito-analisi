## Exercise 1

The average normal speed across a section of $0.004\,\mathrm{m^2}$ is $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find flow rate in litres per second.

::grafico{assi=0 linee="-2,1 2,1;-2,-1 2,-1;0,-1 0,1" vettori="-1.6,0,1.5,0,v" xmin=-2.5 xmax=2.7 ymin=-1.5 ymax=1.7 titolo="The indicated velocity is normal to the section"}

$$
\begin{aligned}Q&=Av=(0.004\cdot2)\,\mathrm{m^3/s}\\&=0.008\,\mathrm{m^3/s}=8\,\mathrm{L/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The drawing identifies the cross section and its normal direction. A slice of liquid $2\,\mathrm m$ long crosses the plane in one second. Multiply that length by area to find the volume passing in one second. The product is in cubic metres, so flow rate is initially in cubic metres per second. Each cubic metre contains a thousand litres. Converting only the volume unit gives the required eight litres per second. If the stated speed were the centreline reading of a nonuniform profile, the normal mean across the section would be needed first.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A pipe has a circular section of diameter $0.10\,\mathrm m$. With a mean normal speed of $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ find flow rate.

$$
\begin{aligned}r&=\frac d2=0.05\,\mathrm m,\\ A&=\pi r^2=0.0025\pi\,\mathrm{m^2},\\Q&=Av=0.005\pi\,\mathrm{m^3/s}\simeq0.0157\,\mathrm{m^3/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The section is a disk. Its diameter is given, so halve it before finding its area. Multiply that area by the stated normal mean speed. Keep $\pi$ until the final step and round once. To check the scale, the rate is about $15.7\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Using the diameter as the radius would make the section four times too large and would overestimate the flow rate by the same factor. Changing speed in the same tube, by contrast, would change the rate linearly.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A uniform flow of speed $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ crosses a flat face of area $0.020\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The measured outward flow rate is $0.040\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Find the angle between the velocity and the outward normal.

$$
\begin{aligned}v_n&=\frac{Q}{A}=\frac{0.040}{0.020}\,\mathrm{m/s}=2\,\mathrm{m/s},\\ \cos\theta&=\frac{v_n}{v}=\frac24=\frac12,\qquad \theta=60^\circ.\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The rate measures flow through the face. Dividing volume per second by surface area gives the normal component, while the stated speed also includes motion along the face. The ratio of the two gives the cosine of the angle to the normal. Since the flow is outward, the cosine is positive and the angle is acute. As a check, perpendicular flow at the same speed would give $0.080\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The actual reading is half that value, as expected from the cosine. The angle to the plane of the face would be $30^\circ$.

</div>
