## Exercise 1

A pump keeps $4\,\mathrm{L/s}$ for $10\,\mathrm s$. Find transported volume.

$$
V=Qt,\qquad
\begin{aligned}V&=4 \cdot 10\\[4pt]&=40\,\mathrm L.\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Flow rate is volume per second. Four litres pass ten times over the stated duration.

On a graph of $Q$ against $t$ the volume is the area of a rectangle $4\,\mathrm{L/s}$ tall and $10\,\mathrm s$ wide. Multiplying the units, $(\mathrm{L/s})\cdot\mathrm s$, leaves litres.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A pump delivers $4\,\mathrm{L/s}$ for $10\,\mathrm s$ and then $2\,\mathrm{L/s}$ for $5\,\mathrm s$. What is the total volume?

::grafico{linee="0,4 10,4;10,2 15,2" area="4;0;0;10|2;0;10;15" xmin=0 xmax=17 ymin=0 ymax=5.3 nomi="t (s),Q (L/s)" titolo="Add the areas of the two rectangles"}

$$
V=Q_1t_1+Q_2t_2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}V&=2 \cdot 5 + 4 \cdot 10\\[4pt]&=50\,\mathrm L.\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The durations differ. Adding the two rectangular areas on the flow rate–time graph gives the correct volume.

The first interval gives $40\,\mathrm L$ and the second $10\,\mathrm L$. Dividing their sum by $15\,\mathrm s$ gives an average of about $3.33\,\mathrm{L/s}$, closer to the rate maintained for longer.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A tank holds $15\,\mathrm L$ and receives $3\,\mathrm{L/s}$ while losing $1\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Find its volume after $8\,\mathrm s$.

$$
V_f=V_i+(Q_{\mathrm{in}}-Q_{\mathrm{out}})t,\qquad
\begin{aligned}V_f&=15 + \left(3 - 1\right) \cdot 8\\[4pt]&=31\,\mathrm L.\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Retain the initial volume in the balance. Net flow keeps two litres each second, adding to the initial amount.

In the same interval $24\,\mathrm L$ enter and $8\,\mathrm L$ leave. The tank gains $16\,\mathrm L$, consistent with the incoming rate being greater than the outgoing rate.

</div>
