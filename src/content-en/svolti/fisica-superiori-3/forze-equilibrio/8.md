## Exercise 1

A uniform $4\,\mathrm m$ plank weighs $80\,\mathrm N$. Two supports are at $x=0.50\,\mathrm m$ and $x=3.50\,\mathrm m$ from the left end. A $60\,\mathrm N$ vertical load acts at the right end. Find both reactions and check that the supports remain in contact.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N_B\cdot3.00\,\mathrm m&=(80\cdot1.50+60\cdot3.50)\,\mathrm{N\cdot m},\\
N_B&=110\,\mathrm N,\qquad N_A=(80+60-110)\,\mathrm N=30\,\mathrm N.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The uniform plank’s weight acts at its midpoint. Choosing the first support as pivot removes the torque of its reaction. Measure the lever arms from that support. The load lies beyond the second support, giving it a longer lever arm than the support separation. Once the right reaction is known, use vertical balance. Both positive values are compatible with two supports that push.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A uniform horizontal beam of length $2\,\mathrm m$ weighs $40\,\mathrm N$ and is hinged at the left. A $60\,\mathrm N$ load acts $1.50\,\mathrm m$ from the hinge. A cable attached at the right end pulls upwards and leftwards at $30^\circ$ above horizontal. Find the tension and the hinge-reaction components.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T\sin30^\circ\cdot2\,\mathrm m&=(40\cdot1+60\cdot1.50)\,\mathrm{N\cdot m},\qquad T=130\,\mathrm N,\\
R_x&=130\cos30^\circ\,\mathrm N=65\sqrt3\,\mathrm N,\qquad R_y=(40+60-65)\,\mathrm N=35\,\mathrm N.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The ideal hinge supplies a reaction with two components and transmits no torque. With the pivot at the hinge, the horizontal component of cable force also has zero lever arm because its line of action lies along the beam. Torque balance gives the vertical cable component and hence the full tension. After that, include the horizontal cable component in force balance: the hinge has to cancel it. Both hinge components are positive with right and up taken as positive.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A uniform $4\,\mathrm m$ plank weighing $60\,\mathrm N$ is supported at $x=1\,\mathrm m$ and $x=3\,\mathrm m$. At $x=4\,\mathrm m$ an added vertical load $Q$ acts. Find the largest $Q$ before the plank loses contact at one support and the other support reaction at the threshold.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(60\,\mathrm N-Q)\cdot1\,\mathrm m-2\,\mathrm m\cdot N_A&=0,\\
N_A&=(60\,\mathrm N-Q)/2,\qquad Q_{\max}=60\,\mathrm N,\qquad N_B=120\,\mathrm N.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

About the right support, the plank weight and added load have equal lever arms and opposite torques. The left reaction decreases as the right load increases. At the threshold it vanishes, and the right support alone carries both weights. A greater load would give a negative left reaction under the contact assumption, which cannot occur without fastening. This identifies the tipping threshold before extending the equation beyond its valid range.

</div>
