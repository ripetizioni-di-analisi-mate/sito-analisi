## Exercise 1

Two carts of $2$ and $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ are connected on a smooth track. A $24\,\mathrm N$ horizontal pull acts on the $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ front cart. Find the common acceleration.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=\frac{24}{2+4}\\[4pt]
&= 4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{assi=0 poligoni="-2,0 -1,0 -1,0.8 -2,0.8;0.4,0 1.9,0 1.9,0.8 0.4,0.8" segmenti="-1,0.4,0.4,0.4;-2.4,0,3.5,0" vettori="1.9,0.4,3.1,0.4,F" etichette="-1.5,0.4,2;1.1,0.4,4" xmin=-2.5 xmax=3.6 ymin=-0.6 ymax=1.3 titolo="The external pull accelerates both masses, labelled in kilograms"}

<div class="spiegazione">

For the two-cart system tension cancels because it acts between members of the chosen system. The only horizontal external force supplies twenty-four newtons to the full six kilograms. A taut connection gives both carts the same acceleration.

A check on the rear cart gives a tension of $2\cdot4\,\mathrm N=8\,\mathrm N$. On the cart pulled directly, subtracting that tension from $24\,\mathrm N$ leaves $16\,\mathrm N$. Dividing by its mass recovers the common acceleration.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Two carts, $A$ of mass $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $B$ of mass $5\,\mathrm{kg}$, are connected by a negligible-mass spring on a smooth track. At one instant the external horizontal resultant is $+7\,\mathrm N$, and $A$ accelerates at $+4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ along the same axis. Find the acceleration of $B$ and of the centre of mass.

$$
\begin{aligned}
7\,\mathrm N&=3\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}+5\,\mathrm{kg}\,a_B,\\
a_B&=\frac{7-12}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=-1\,\mathrm{m/s^2},\qquad
a_{\mathrm{CM}}=\frac78\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Include the spring in the system so the connector forces are internal. The external resultant is the sum of each mass times its own acceleration. The first contribution is larger than the total resultant, so the second must have the opposite sign. The spring allows the two carts to accelerate differently.

The centre of mass accelerates to the right even though $B$ accelerates to the left. Check the signed contributions: twelve newtons to the right and five to the left give the required seven.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Connected carts of total mass $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ are pulled rightwards by $35\,\mathrm N$. A total external resistance of $15\,\mathrm N$ points left. Find their acceleration.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=\frac{35-15}{10}\\[4pt]
&= 2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Include both external forces with their opposite directions. Internal tensions do not change the balance for the two carts together. The resultant is twenty newtons and the accelerated inertia is the full ten kilograms.

The distribution of resistance between carts is unnecessary for the system’s external resultant. It would matter for finding tension by isolating one cart. The positive acceleration follows from comparing the magnitudes of the external forces.

</div>
