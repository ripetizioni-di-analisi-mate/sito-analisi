## Exercise 1

A drone moves $24\,\mathrm m$ east and $-18\,\mathrm m$ along the north-pointing axis in $6\,\mathrm s$. What is the magnitude of its mean velocity?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,24,-18,Δr" segmenti="0,0,24,0;24,0,24,-18" etichette="9.1,0.58,Δx 24 m;18.4,-9.2,Δy −18 m" xmin=-2 xmax=26 ymin=-20 ymax=2 uguale=1 nomi="x (m),y (m)" titolo="Displacement forms a right triangle with its components"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_m&=\sqrt{\left(\frac{24}{6}\right)^2+\left(\frac{-18}{6}\right)^2}\\[4pt]
&= 5\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Mean velocity is $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ east and $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ south. Divide the two signed displacements by the same elapsed time. The directions are perpendicular, so add their squares.

The displacement magnitude is $\sqrt{24^2+18^2}\,\mathrm m=30\,\mathrm m$. Dividing this magnitude by the elapsed time gives the same result. The calculation uses the starting and ending positions, so a detour during the flight could increase the distance travelled while leaving the average velocity unchanged.

</div>

## Exercise 2

An object has speed $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_x=-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find $v_y$ if it rises and the vertical axis points upward.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_y&=+\sqrt{13^2-(-5)^2}\\[4pt]
&= 12\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Subtract the squared horizontal contribution from the squared speed, regardless of its sign. Two possibilities of magnitude twelve remain. Rising motion imposes the positive sign on an upward axis.

The vector points left and upwards, so it inclines to the left of the vertical. Its magnitude checks out through the Pythagorean triple $5$, $12$, $13$. The negative horizontal component still determines the quadrant even though it leaves the magnitude check unchanged.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A trolley follows $x(t)=2-3t$ and $y(t)=1+2t^2$ in SI units, with $t\geq0$. Find its trajectory, then determine the velocity components and magnitude at $t=1\,\mathrm s$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t&=\frac{2-x}{3}, \qquad y=1+\frac{2(2-x)^2}{9},\quad x\leq2,\\[6pt]
v_x(1)&=-3\,\mathrm{m/s},\\[4pt]
\frac{y(1+\delta)-y(1)}{\delta}&=4+2\delta,\qquad v_y(1)=4\,\mathrm{m/s},\\[6pt]
|\vec v(1)|&=\sqrt{(-3)^2+4^2}\,\mathrm{m/s}=5\,\mathrm{m/s}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The horizontal coordinate decreases by three metres per second, so the trolley follows the branch $x\leq2$ in the direction of decreasing $x$. Find the slope of $y(t)$ at one second by dividing the vertical displacement by a short interval $\delta$. The term $2\delta$ vanishes as the interval approaches zero. The velocity points left and upwards. The trajectory provides a check on the direction. At $x=-1$ its tangent slope is $-\dfrac43$, the same as $\dfrac{v_y}{v_x}$.

</div>
