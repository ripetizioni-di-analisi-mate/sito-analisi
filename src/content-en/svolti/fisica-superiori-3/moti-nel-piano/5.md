## Exercise 1

A rotor completes $30$ revolutions per minute. What is its angular speed?

$$
\begin{aligned}
\omega&=2\pi f=2\pi\cdot\frac{30}{60}\\[4pt]
&= \pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Divide the revolutions by sixty to express them per second. Each revolution includes $2\pi$ radians. Multiplying half a revolution per second by the radians in one turn gives $\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

A point sweeps a quarter revolution at constant rate in $0.5\,\mathrm s$. What is $\omega$?

::grafico{curve="2*cos(t),2*sin(t),0,1.5707963268" segmenti="0,0,2,0;0,0,0,2" xmin=-0.5 xmax=2.6 ymin=-0.5 ymax=2.6 uguale=1 titolo="The arc spans one quarter of the circle"}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\omega&=\frac{(2\pi)/4}{0.5}\\[4pt]
&= \pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

A quarter revolution is $\pi/2$ radians. The given duration applies to that quarter only, so divide its angle by half a second. At the same rate a full turn would take two seconds.

</div>

## Exercise 3

A disc rotates uniformly at $\omega=4\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. How much time passes between successive crossings of one mark past a fixed sensor?

$$
\begin{aligned}
T&=\frac{2\pi}{4\pi}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 0.5\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

To return to the sensor, the mark must rotate through one complete turn. Divide $2\pi$ by angular speed. Half a second is consistent with two turns per second.

</div>
