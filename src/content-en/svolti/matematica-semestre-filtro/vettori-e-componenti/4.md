## Exercise 1

Given $\vec A=(2,0,0)$ and $\vec B=(0,3,0)$ in a right-handed Cartesian coordinate system, calculate $\vec A\times\vec B$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\vec A\times\vec B)_x&=A_yB_z-A_zB_y=0\cdot0-0\cdot3=0\\[6pt]
(\vec A\times\vec B)_y&=A_zB_x-A_xB_z=0\cdot0-2\cdot0=0\\[6pt]
(\vec A\times\vec B)_z&=A_xB_y-A_yB_x=2\cdot3-0\cdot0=6\\[6pt]
\vec A\times\vec B&=(0,0,6).
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Calculate the components of the cross product using the formula for a right-handed coordinate system, keeping the factors in the given order. Both vectors have zero $z$ component, so they lie in the $xy$ plane. Every term in the $x$ and $y$ components therefore contains a zero factor, leaving only the component along $z$.

The $z$ component is $2\cdot3-0\cdot0=6$. Check using the geometric definition. The vector $\vec A$ points along the positive $x$ axis and $\vec B$ along the positive $y$ axis, so the angle between them is $90^\circ$ and $\sin90^\circ=1$. The cross product therefore has magnitude $|\vec A|\,|\vec B|=2\cdot3=6$, the area of the rectangle with sides $2$ and $3$.

By the **right-hand rule**, curl the fingers of your right hand from $\vec A$ toward $\vec B$ through the right angle, going from the positive $x$ axis to the positive $y$ axis. Your thumb points along the positive $z$ axis, in agreement with the sign of the component. Reversing the factors would give $\vec B\times\vec A=(0,0,-6)$.

The cross product is $\vec A\times\vec B=(0,0,6)$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Given $\vec A=(3,4)$, calculate the unit vector that points in the same direction.

$$
\begin{aligned}
|\vec A|&=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=\sqrt{25}=5\\[6pt]
\widehat A&=\frac{\vec A}{|\vec A|}
=\left(\frac35,\frac45\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
|\widehat A|=\sqrt{\frac{9}{25}+\frac{16}{25}}=\sqrt{\frac{25}{25}}=1.
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

A **unit vector** is a vector of magnitude one. To obtain the unit vector in the direction of $\vec A$, divide $\vec A$ by its magnitude. This division is possible because $\vec A$ is nonzero.

The Pythagorean theorem gives the magnitude $5$. Then divide each component by $5$. The components of the unit vector are therefore $\dfrac35$ and $\dfrac45$.

The divisor $5$ is positive, so each component keeps its sign. The unit vector therefore points in the same direction as $\vec A$, as the figure shows. Both arrows lie on the same ray from the origin. Calculating the magnitude of $\widehat A$ confirms that it equals $1$. Indeed, its head lies on the circle of radius $1$ centered at the origin.

The required unit vector is $\widehat A=\left(\dfrac35,\dfrac45\right)$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Given $\vec A=(1,2,-1)$ and $\vec B=(3,0,2)$ in a right-handed Cartesian coordinate system, calculate $\vec A\times\vec B$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\vec A\times\vec B)_x&=A_yB_z-A_zB_y=2\cdot2-(-1)\cdot0=4\\[6pt]
(\vec A\times\vec B)_y&=A_zB_x-A_xB_z=(-1)\cdot3-1\cdot2=-5\\[6pt]
(\vec A\times\vec B)_z&=A_xB_y-A_yB_x=1\cdot0-2\cdot3=-6\\[6pt]
\vec A\times\vec B&=(4,-5,-6)
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\vec A\times\vec B)\cdot\vec A&=4\cdot1+(-5)\cdot2+(-6)\cdot(-1)=4-10+6=0\\[6pt]
(\vec A\times\vec B)\cdot\vec B&=4\cdot3+(-5)\cdot0+(-6)\cdot2=12-12=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Use the component formula for the cross product in a right-handed coordinate system. Each component uses the other two coordinates, and the first factor in the first term is a component of $\vec A$. Interchanging $\vec A$ and $\vec B$ would reverse the signs of all three components.

For the $x$ component, subtract the product $(-1)\cdot0=0$, leaving $2\cdot2=4$. For the $y$ component, start with $(-1)\cdot3=-3$ and subtract $1\cdot2=2$ to get $-5$. For the $z$ component, the first product is zero. Subtracting $2\cdot3=6$ therefore gives $-6$.

The cross product is perpendicular to both factors, so its dot products with $\vec A$ and $\vec B$ must be zero. Using the three calculated components gives zero in both checks, as expected. The magnitude of the result, $\sqrt{16+25+36}=\sqrt{77}$, is the area of the parallelogram spanned by $\vec A$ and $\vec B$.

The cross product is $\vec A\times\vec B=(4,-5,-6)$.

</div>
