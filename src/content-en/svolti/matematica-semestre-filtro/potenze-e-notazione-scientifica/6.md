## Exercise 1

Find the two consecutive multiples of $10^4$ between which the quotient $\dfrac{3.6\cdot10^8}{1.9\cdot10^4}$ lies.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{3.6\cdot10^8}{1.9\cdot10^4}
&=\frac{3.6}{1.9}\cdot\frac{10^8}{10^4}\\[6pt]
&=\frac{3.6}{1.9}\cdot10^4,
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
1\cdot1.9 &<3.6<2\cdot1.9,\\[6pt]
1 &<\frac{3.6}{1.9}<2,\\[6pt]
10^4 &<\frac{3.6\cdot10^8}{1.9\cdot10^4}<2\cdot10^4.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Separate the power of ten so that only the coefficient needs to be compared. The quotient of the powers is $10^{8-4}=10^4$, so the number is $\dfrac{3.6}{1.9}$ times $10^4$. It remains to find the two consecutive integers between which $\dfrac{3.6}{1.9}$ lies.

The numerator $3.6$ is greater than $1.9$ and less than $3.8$, since $3.8=2\cdot1.9$. Dividing these inequalities by the positive value $1.9$ gives a coefficient between one and two. Dividing by a positive number preserves the direction of the inequalities, so $1<\dfrac{3.6}{1.9}<2$.

Multiplying all three parts by the positive number $10^4$ preserves the inequalities and gives the final chain. The quotient therefore lies between **$10^4$ and $2\cdot10^4$**.

The value $3.6$ is close to $3.8$, so the quotient is near the upper bound. Evaluating the quotient directly gives approximately $1.89\cdot10^4$, within the interval just found.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Determine which power of ten is closest to $3.2\cdot10^{-7}$ on a multiplicative scale.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{3.2\cdot10^{-7}}{10^{-7}}&=3.2,\\[6pt]
\frac{10^{-6}}{3.2\cdot10^{-7}}&=\frac{10}{3.2}=3.125,
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
3.2^2&=10.24>10,\\[6pt]
3.2&>\sqrt{10},\\[6pt]
10^{-7+1}&=10^{-6}.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{f="x;10/x" xmin=0 xmax=6.4 ymin=0 ymax=7 punti="3.16227766,3.16227766,vuoto" segmenti="3.2,0,3.2,3.2" nomi="a,fattore" legenda="a;\dfrac{10}{a}" titolo="To the right of $a=\sqrt{10}$, the line $a$ lies above the hyperbola $\dfrac{10}{a}$. At $a=3.2$ the two factors are $3.2$ and $3.125$."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The adjacent powers are $10^{-7}$ and $10^{-6}$. The number is normalized with coefficient $a=3.2$, so it lies between these two powers.

On a multiplicative scale, distance from a power is measured by the factor separating the number and the power. The number is $3.2$ times $10^{-7}$, and $10^{-6}$ is $\dfrac{10}{3.2}=3.125$ times the number. The smaller factor is $3.125$, so the nearer power is $10^{-6}$.

The boundary between the two choices occurs at a coefficient of $\sqrt{10}$. Indeed, the two factors $a$ and $\dfrac{10}{a}$ are equal when $a^2=10$. To determine which side of this boundary $3.2$ lies on, compare the squares without approximating the root. Since $3.2^2=10.24$ exceeds $10$ and squaring is increasing for positive numbers, $3.2>\sqrt{10}$.

On the graph the horizontal coordinate $3.2$ is just to the right of the intersection of the curves, where the line $a$ lies above the hyperbola $\dfrac{10}{a}$. The order of magnitude is **$10^{-6}$**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Bound $\sqrt{7.3\cdot10^{-5}}$ between two consecutive multiples of $10^{-4}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
7.3\cdot10^{-5}&=7.3\cdot10\cdot10^{-6}\\[6pt]
&=73\cdot10^{-6},
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
8.5^2=72.25 &<73<73.96=8.6^2,\\[6pt]
8.5 &<\sqrt{73}<8.6,\\[6pt]
8.5\cdot10^{-3}&<\sqrt{7.3\cdot10^{-5}}<8.6\cdot10^{-3},\\[6pt]
85\cdot10^{-4}&<\sqrt{7.3\cdot10^{-5}}<86\cdot10^{-4}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Rewrite the radicand with an even exponent to isolate the root of $73$. Since $10^{-5}=10\cdot10^{-6}$, the radicand is $73\cdot10^{-6}$, and the rule for the root of a product gives $\sqrt{73\cdot10^{-6}}=\sqrt{73}\cdot10^{-3}$.

The number $73$ is not a perfect square, so bound its square root between two values. Since $8^2=64$ and $9^2=81$, the root lies between $8$ and $9$. Checking tenths next gives $8.5^2=72.25$ and $8.6^2=73.96$.

Since squaring is increasing for nonnegative numbers, the squares of $8.5$ and $8.6$ give the two bounds. As $73$ lies between the squares, $\sqrt{73}$ lies between $8.5$ and $8.6$.

Multiplying both values by $10^{-3}$ gives consecutive multiples of $10^{-4}$, since $8.5\cdot10^{-3}=85\cdot10^{-4}$ and $8.6\cdot10^{-3}=86\cdot10^{-4}$. The root therefore lies between **$85\cdot10^{-4}$ and $86\cdot10^{-4}$**.

</div>
