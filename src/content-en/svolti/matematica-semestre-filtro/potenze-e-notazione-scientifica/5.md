## Exercise 1

Calculate $81^{\frac{3}{4}}$.

$$
81=3\cdot3\cdot3\cdot3=3^4
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
81^{\frac{3}{4}}
&=\left(\sqrt[4]{81}\right)^3\\[6pt]
&=\left(\sqrt[4]{3^4}\right)^3\\[6pt]
&=3^3\\[6pt]
&=27.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
81^{\frac{3}{4}}=\left(3^4\right)^{\frac{3}{4}}=3^{4\cdot\frac{3}{4}}=3^3=27
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The base $81$ is positive, so the fractional power is defined and equals $x^{\frac{p}{q}}=\left(\sqrt[q]{x}\right)^p$ with $p=3$ and $q=4$.

The denominator four gives the index of the root. To calculate it, factor the base into primes. Repeated division by $3$ gives $81=3^4$. Since $3^4=81$ and $3$ is positive, the fourth root is $3$. The index four is even, so of the two numbers $3$ and $-3$, both of whose fourth powers equal $81$, the root is the nonnegative one.

The numerator three gives the power to which the result must be raised. We have $3^3=3\cdot3\cdot3=27$.

The second calculation uses the factorization $81=3^4$ and the rule for a power of a power. The product of the exponents is $4\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}=3$, which again gives $3^3=27$. Taking the root before raising it to a power keeps the intermediate numbers small because $81^3=531\,441$. The power is **$27$**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Calculate $32^{-\frac{2}{5}}$ and express the result as a fraction in lowest terms.

$$
32=2\cdot2\cdot2\cdot2\cdot2=2^5
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
32^{-\frac{2}{5}}
&=\frac{1}{32^{\frac{2}{5}}}\\[6pt]
&=\frac{1}{(\sqrt[5]{32})^2}\\[6pt]
&=\frac{1}{(\sqrt[5]{2^5})^2}\\[6pt]
&=\frac{1}{2^2}\\[6pt]
&=\frac{1}{4}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The exponent is negative, so take the reciprocal of the corresponding positive power. By the definition of a negative exponent, $32^{-\frac{2}{5}}$ is the number that gives one when multiplied by $32^{\frac{2}{5}}$, namely $\dfrac{1}{32^{\frac{2}{5}}}$. Thus the minus sign in the exponent indicates a reciprocal, and the result is positive.

The positive exponent $\dfrac{2}{5}$ has denominator $5$ and numerator $2$, so take the fifth root and square it. The base $32$ is positive, so these operations are defined.

The fifth root of $32$ is $2$, since the prime factorization gives $32=2^5$. Squaring the root gives $4$. Its reciprocal is therefore one-fourth.

The numerator and denominator of $\dfrac{1}{4}$ are relatively prime, so the fraction is in lowest terms. As a check, the result lies between $0$ and $1$, as it must for a base greater than one raised to a negative exponent. The power is **$\dfrac{1}{4}$**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Calculate $\sqrt[3]{-12.5\cdot10^{-8}}$ and write the result in scientific notation.

$$
-12.5\cdot10^{-8}=-12.5\cdot10\cdot10^{-9}=-125\cdot10^{-9}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sqrt[3]{-12.5\cdot10^{-8}}
&=\sqrt[3]{-125\cdot10^{-9}}\\[6pt]
&=\sqrt[3]{-125}\cdot\sqrt[3]{10^{-9}}\\[6pt]
&=\sqrt[3]{-125}\cdot10^{-3}\\[6pt]
&=-5\cdot10^{-3}.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{f="x^3" xmin=-6.5 xmax=6.5 ymin=-150 ymax=150 punti="-5,-125,pieno,P" segmenti="-5,0,-5,-125;-5,-125,0,-125" legenda="y=x^3" titolo="The point $P$ on the graph of $y=x^3$ with vertical coordinate $-125$ has horizontal coordinate $-5$, so $\sqrt[3]{-125}=-5$."}

<div class="spiegazione">

The odd index allows a negative radicand. Indeed, every real number has a unique real cube root with the same sign as the radicand, and the root of a product can be split into the product of the roots without sign restrictions.

Rewrite the radicand with exponent $-9$, a multiple of three, and calculate the cube root of $-125$. To go from $10^{-8}$ to $10^{-9}$, divide the power of ten by ten. The coefficient must therefore be multiplied by ten, giving $-125$. With exponent $-8$, the root of the power would have exponent $-\dfrac{8}{3}$, which is not an integer.

The cube root of $10^{-9}$ is $10^{-3}$ because $\left(10^{-3}\right)^3=10^{-9}$. The result is negative because the cube of $-5$ equals $-125$. The graph of $y=x^3$ is increasing and takes each real value exactly once. Its only point with vertical coordinate $-125$ is $P$, with horizontal coordinate $-5$.

The absolute value of the coefficient is $5$, between $1$ and $10$, so the expression is normalized. The root is **$-5\cdot10^{-3}$**.

</div>
