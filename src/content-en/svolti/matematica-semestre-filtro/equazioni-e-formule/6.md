## Exercise 1

Solve the system of equations $x+y=9$ and $2x+y=13$.

$$
\begin{cases}
x+y=9\\
2x+y=13
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(2x+y)-(x+y)&=13-9 \\[6pt]
2x-x+y-y&=4 \\[6pt]
x&=4 \\[6pt]
4+y&=9 \\[6pt]
y&=9-4=5 .
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{f="9-x;13-2*x" xmin=-1 xmax=8 ymin=-2 ymax=14 punti="4,5,pieno" segmenti="4,0,4,5;0,5,4,5" legenda="x+y=9;2x+y=13" titolo="The lines $x+y=9$ and $2x+y=13$ intersect at $(4,5)$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The two equations contain $y$ with the same coefficient $1$, so we use the **elimination method**. Keep the first equation and replace the second by the difference between the second and the first, subtracting their left sides and their right sides. This operation is reversible because adding the first equation back recovers the second. The new system therefore has the same solutions.

On subtracting the left sides, $2x-x=x$ and $y-y=0$, so $y$ drops out. Subtracting the right sides gives $13-9=4$, so $x=4$.

Then substitute $x=4$ into the first equation, giving $4+y=9$. Subtracting $4$ gives $y=5$. The pair $(4,5)$ satisfies both equations because $4+5=9$ and $2\cdot4+5=13$.

In the figure, each equation represents a line in the plane consisting of all the pairs that satisfy it. The solution is their common point. The system **has the unique solution** $(x,y)=(4,5)$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Two solutions of the same solute, with concentrations of $4\,\mathrm{g/L}$ and $10\,\mathrm{g/L}$, are mixed. Their volumes are additive, and the solute is conserved. The final volume is $0.5\,\mathrm{L}$ and the mass of solute is $3.8\,\mathrm{g}$. Calculate the volume of each solution.

$$
\begin{cases}
x+y=0.5\\
4x+10y=3.8
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(4x+10y)-4(x+y)&=3.8-4\cdot0.5 \\[6pt]
6y&=3.8-2=1.8 \\[6pt]
y&=\frac{1.8}{6}=0.3 \\[6pt]
x&=0.5-0.3=0.2 .
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Let $x$ and $y$ be the numerical values of the volumes in liters, in the order given in the problem. The volumes are additive, so their sum is the final volume, giving $x+y=0.5$.

The mass of solute in a solution equals its concentration times its volume. The first solution therefore contributes $4x$ grams and the second contributes $10y$ grams. Since the solute is conserved, the sum of these masses equals the final mass of solute, giving $4x+10y=3.8$.

To eliminate $x$, subtract four times the first equation from the second. On the left, $4x-4x=0$ and $10y-4y=6y$. On the right, $3.8-2=1.8$. Dividing by $6$ gives $y=0.3$, and the first equation then gives $x=0.2$.

Both volumes are positive, so the solution is admissible in this problem. As a check, the solute mass is $4\cdot0.2+10\cdot0.3=0.8+3=3.8$ grams. The amounts needed are **0.2 liters of the first solution and 0.3 liters of the second**.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Find the solution set of the system of equations $2x-3y=5$ and $4x-6y=8$.

$$
\begin{cases}
2x-3y=5\\
4x-6y=8
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
2(2x-3y)&=2\cdot5 \\[6pt]
4x-6y&=10 \\[6pt]
(4x-6y)-2(2x-3y)&=8-10 \\[6pt]
0&=-2 \\[6pt]
S&=\varnothing.
\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{f="(2*x-5)/3;(2*x-4)/3" xmin=-2 xmax=6 ymin=-4 ymax=4 legenda="2x-3y=5;4x-6y=8" titolo="The lines $y=\dfrac{2x-5}{3}$ and $y=\dfrac{2x-4}{3}$ have the same slope and no points in common"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The coefficients in the second equation, $4$ and $-6$, are twice those in the first, $2$ and $-3$. Multiplying both sides of the first equation by $2$ gives $4x-6y=10$, so the first equation requires the expression $4x-6y$ to equal $10$. The second requires the same expression to equal $8$.

Subtracting twice the first equation from the second cancels both the $x$ and $y$ terms, leaving the equality $0=-2$, which is false. Since the operation is reversible, the original system is equivalent to one containing a false equality. Therefore no pair $(x,y)$ satisfies both equations.

Solving for $y$ gives the lines $y=\dfrac{2x-5}{3}$ and $y=\dfrac{2x-4}{3}$ in the plane. They have the same slope $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and different intercepts, so they are distinct parallel lines. The system is **inconsistent**, and its solution set is empty.

</div>
