## Exercise 1

Solve the equation
$$
x - 5\sqrt{x} + 6 = 0 .
$$

$$
t = \sqrt{x} , \qquad t \geq 0 , \qquad x = t^2
$$
$$
t^2 - 5t + 6 = 0 \iff (t - 2)(t - 3) = 0 \iff t = 2 \ \vee \ t = 3
$$
$$
\sqrt{x} = 2 \iff x = 4 , \qquad \sqrt{x} = 3 \iff x = 9
$$

Solutions: $x = 4 \ \vee \ x = 9$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The equation contains $x$ and $\sqrt{x}$, and for $x \geq 0$ one has $x = \left(\sqrt{x}\right)^2$. With the **auxiliary unknown** $t = \sqrt{x}$ the equation therefore becomes a quadratic equation in $t$. The root exists only for $x \geq 0$ and its value is nonnegative, therefore the new unknown is subject to the condition $t \geq 0$.

The trinomial $t^2 - 5t + 6$ factors as $(t - 2)(t - 3)$, because $2$ and $3$ have sum $5$ and product $6$. The roots $t = 2$ and $t = 3$ are both positive, so they satisfy the condition $t \geq 0$ and are accepted.

One then returns to the unknown $x$. Each of the equations $\sqrt{x} = 2$ and $\sqrt{x} = 3$ is of the form $\sqrt{A} = B$ with $B$ a positive constant; by the **solving system** the condition $B \geq 0$ is satisfied and squaring gives $x = 4$ and $x = 9$.

The check confirms both values. For $x = 4$ the left-hand side equals $4 - 5 \cdot 2 + 6 = 0$; for $x = 9$ it equals $9 - 5 \cdot 3 + 6 = 0$. The equation therefore has **two solutions**.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve the equation
$$
x + 5 - \sqrt{x + 5} = 6 .
$$

$$
t = \sqrt{x + 5} , \qquad t \geq 0 , \qquad x + 5 = t^2
$$
$$
t^2 - t - 6 = 0 \iff (t - 3)(t + 2) = 0 \iff t = 3 \ \vee \ t = -2
$$
$$
t = -2 \ \text{does not satisfy} \ t \geq 0
$$
$$
\sqrt{x + 5} = 3 \iff x + 5 = 9 \iff x = 4
$$

Solution: $x = 4$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The radicand $x + 5$ also appears outside the root, so the substitution $t = \sqrt{x + 5}$ is used, for which $x + 5 = t^2$. Since a square root is not negative, the new unknown must satisfy $t \geq 0$.

The equation becomes $t^2 - t = 6$, that is, $t^2 - t - 6 = 0$. The trinomial factors as $(t - 3)(t + 2)$, because $-3$ and $2$ have sum $-1$ and product $-6$; the roots are $t = 3$ and $t = -2$.

The root $t = -2$ is negative, so it does not satisfy the condition $t \geq 0$ and **is discarded**, because no real number has square root equal to $-2$. What remains is $\sqrt{x + 5} = 3$, which by the solving system for $\sqrt{A} = B$ with $B = 3 > 0$ is equivalent to $x + 5 = 9$, that is, to $x = 4$.

The check gives $4 + 5 - \sqrt{9} = 9 - 3 = 6$, equal to the right-hand side. Without the condition $t \geq 0$ one would arrive at the equation $\sqrt{x + 5} = -2$, which has no solutions; squaring it would give the value $x = -1$, for which the left-hand side equals $4 - 2 = 2$, different from $6$. The equation therefore has **the single solution** $x = 4$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve the inequality
$$
x - 3\sqrt{x} - 4 < 0 .
$$

$$
t = \sqrt{x} , \qquad t \geq 0 , \qquad x = t^2
$$
$$
t^2 - 3t - 4 < 0 \iff (t - 4)(t + 1) < 0 \iff -1 < t < 4
$$
$$
\begin{cases}
t \geq 0 \\
-1 < t < 4
\end{cases}
\iff 0 \leq t < 4
$$
$$
0 \leq \sqrt{x} < 4 \iff
\begin{cases}
x \geq 0 \\
x < 16
\end{cases}
$$

Solutions: $0 \leq x < 16$.

::grafico{f="x-3*sqrt(x)-4" xmin=-2 xmax=20 ymin=-8 ymax=4 punti="0,-4,pieno;16,0,vuoto;2.25,-6.25,pieno" titolo="The graph of $y = x - 3\sqrt{x} - 4$, defined for $x \geq 0$, lies below the $x$-axis for $0 \leq x < 16$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The inequality is defined for $x \geq 0$. With the substitution $t = \sqrt{x}$, subject to the condition $t \geq 0$, the left-hand side becomes the trinomial $t^2 - 3t - 4$, which factors as $(t - 4)(t + 1)$.

The trinomial has a positive leading coefficient, so it is negative between the roots $-1$ and $4$. Combining $-1 < t < 4$ with the condition $t \geq 0$ in a system gives $0 \leq t < 4$, because negative values of $t$ do not correspond to any value of $\sqrt{x}$.

Returning to $x$, the condition $\sqrt{x} \geq 0$ is true on the whole domain, while $\sqrt{x} < 4$ is an inequality of the form $\sqrt{A} < B$ with $B = 4 > 0$. Its **solving system** gives $x \geq 0$ and $x < 16$, so the solutions are $0 \leq x < 16$.

The point $x = 16$ is excluded because the left-hand side equals $16 - 12 - 4 = 0$ and the inequality is strict. The minimum value of the left-hand side is attained for $t = \dfrac{3}{2}$, that is, for $x = \dfrac{9}{4}$, and equals $-\dfrac{25}{4}$, as the graph shows.

</div>
