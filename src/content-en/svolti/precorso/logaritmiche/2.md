## Exercise 1

Solve the equation
$$
\log_2 (x + 3) = 4 .
$$

$$
\log_2 (x + 3) = 4 \iff x + 3 = 2^{4} = 16 \iff x = 13
$$

The domain condition $x + 3 > 0$ is satisfied, since $x + 3 = 16 > 0$.

Solution: $x = 13$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The logarithm to base $2$ of $x + 3$ equals $4$, so by the **definition of logarithm** the argument is the power $2^{4} = 16$. What remains is the linear equation $x + 3 = 16$, with solution $x = 13$. The domain condition is **automatically satisfied**, because the argument turns out to be equal to $16$, which is positive.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve the equation
$$
\log_3 (2x - 1) = 2 .
$$

$$
\log_3 (2x - 1) = 2 \iff 2x - 1 = 3^{2} = 9 \iff x = 5
$$

The domain condition $2x - 1 > 0$ is satisfied, since $2x - 1 = 9 > 0$.

Solution: $x = 5$.

<div class="spiegazione">

By the **definition of logarithm**, the argument $2x - 1$ is equal to the base $3$ raised to the value of the logarithm, that is $3^{2} = 9$. The linear equation $2x - 1 = 9$ gives $2x = 10$, so $x = 5$. The check **confirms the result**, because $\log_3 (2 \cdot 5 - 1) = \log_3 9 = 2$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve the equation
$$
\log_{\frac{1}{2}} (x + 1) = -3 .
$$

$$
\log_{\frac{1}{2}} (x + 1) = -3 \iff x + 1 = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{-3} = 2^{3} = 8 \iff x = 7
$$

The domain condition $x + 1 > 0$ is satisfied, since $x + 1 = 8 > 0$.

Solution: $x = 7$.

::grafico{f="log(x+1)/log(0.5);-3" xmin=-1.5 xmax=8.5 ymin=-4 ymax=3 asintoti="x=-1" punti="7,-3,pieno" titolo="The line $y = -3$ meets the decreasing graph of $y = \log_{\frac{1}{2}} (x + 1)$ at $x = 7$"}

<div class="spiegazione">

The base of the logarithm is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and the method does *not* change: by the **definition of logarithm** the argument equals $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{-3}$. The negative exponent replaces the fraction with its **reciprocal**, so $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{-3} = 2^{3} = 8$. From the equation $x + 1 = 8$ it follows that $x = 7$; the check confirms the result, because $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} 8 = -3$ since $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{-3} = 8$.

</div>
