## Exercise 1

Simplify
$$
\frac{2^5 \cdot 2^3}{2^6} .
$$

$$
\frac{2^5 \cdot 2^3}{2^6} = \frac{2^{5+3}}{2^6} = 2^{8-6} = 2^2 = 4
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the numerator the two powers have the same base, therefore the product is the power with the **sum of the exponents**, that is, $2^8$. The quotient of powers with the same base is the power with the **difference of the exponents**, hence the expression equals $2^2 = 4$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Simplify
$$
\frac{6^4 \cdot 2^{-3}}{3^4} .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{6^4 \cdot 2^{-3}}{3^4}
&= \frac{6^4}{3^4} \cdot 2^{-3} \\[6pt]
&= \left(\frac{6}{3}\right)^{4} \cdot 2^{-3} \\[6pt]
&= 2^4 \cdot 2^{-3} = 2^{4-3} = 2
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The powers $6^4$ and $3^4$ have the same exponent, therefore their quotient is the **power of the quotient** of the bases, that is, $\left(\dfrac{6}{3}\right)^4 = 2^4$. At this point the product $2^4 \cdot 2^{-3}$ has a common base, hence the exponents are added and the result is $2^1 = 2$.

The negative exponent indicates the **reciprocal**: $2^{-3} = \dfrac{1}{8}$, and the direct check confirms the result, because $2^4 \cdot \dfrac{1}{8} = \dfrac{16}{8} = 2$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Simplify, for $a, b \neq 0$,
$$
\left(\frac{a^3 b^2}{a b^4}\right)^{2} \cdot \frac{b^5}{a^4} .
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left(\frac{a^3 b^2}{a b^4}\right)^{2} \cdot \frac{b^5}{a^4}
&= \left(a^{3-1}\, b^{2-4}\right)^{2} \cdot \frac{b^5}{a^4} \\[6pt]
&= \left(a^{2}\, b^{-2}\right)^{2} \cdot \frac{b^5}{a^4} \\[6pt]
&= a^{4}\, b^{-4} \cdot \frac{b^5}{a^4} \\[6pt]
&= a^{4-4}\, b^{-4+5} = a^0\, b^1 = b
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

Inside the fraction one applies the **quotient of powers with the same base**, separately for $a$ and for $b$: the exponents are subtracted and $a^2$ and $b^{-2}$ remain. The **power of a power** multiplies the exponents, therefore the square of the simplified fraction is $a^4 b^{-4}$.

In the final product the powers of $a$ and those of $b$ are combined by adding the exponents: for $a$ one obtains $4 - 4 = 0$ and for $b$ one obtains $-4 + 5 = 1$. Since $a \neq 0$, $a^0 = 1$ holds, hence the expression reduces to $b$.

</div>
