## Exercise 1

Simplify
$$
\sqrt{72} + \sqrt{50} - \sqrt{8} .
$$

$$
\sqrt{72} = \sqrt{36 \cdot 2} = 6\sqrt{2}, \qquad
\sqrt{50} = \sqrt{25 \cdot 2} = 5\sqrt{2}, \qquad
\sqrt{8} = \sqrt{4 \cdot 2} = 2\sqrt{2}
$$

$$
\sqrt{72} + \sqrt{50} - \sqrt{8} = (6 + 5 - 2)\sqrt{2} = 9\sqrt{2}
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In each radicand the largest perfect square is separated: $72 = 36 \cdot 2$, $50 = 25 \cdot 2$ and $8 = 4 \cdot 2$. The root of a product of factors greater than or equal to zero is the product of the roots, therefore the square comes out of the radical sign and each term becomes a multiple of $\sqrt{2}$.

The three terms contain the same factor $\sqrt{2}$, hence they are collected like similar monomials: the coefficients are added with their signs and one obtains $(6 + 5 - 2)\sqrt{2} = 9\sqrt{2}$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Determine for which real $x$ the following expression is defined:
$$
\frac{1}{\sqrt{x - 2}} + \sqrt{7 - x} .
$$

$$
\begin{cases}
x - 2 > 0 \\
7 - x \geq 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\begin{cases}
x > 2 \\
x \leq 7
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
2 < x \leq 7
$$

The expression is defined for $2 < x \leq 7$.

<div class="spiegazione">

The first root has an even index, therefore it requires a radicand greater than or equal to zero; since the root is in the denominator, it must also be different from zero, and the two requirements together are equivalent to $x - 2 > 0$. The second root has an even index and is in the numerator, therefore the condition $7 - x \geq 0$ is enough.

The two conditions must hold together, hence they are combined in a system. From the first it follows that $x > 2$ and from the second it follows that $x \leq 7$; the intersection of the two sets is the interval $2 < x \leq 7$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Simplify
$$
\sqrt[3]{\sqrt{64}}
\qquad \text{and, for } a \geq 0, \qquad
\sqrt{\sqrt[3]{a^{12}}} .
$$

$$
\sqrt[3]{\sqrt{64}} = \sqrt[6]{64} = \sqrt[6]{2^6} = 2
$$

$$
\sqrt{\sqrt[3]{a^{12}}} = \sqrt[6]{a^{12}} = a^{\frac{12}{6}} = a^2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The root of a root is the root with the product of the indices, therefore in both cases the indices $3$ and $2$ are multiplied and the overall index is $6$. In the first case the radicand is rewritten as a power: $64 = 2^6$, hence the sixth root of $2^6$ equals $2$.

In the second case the radicand is the power $a^{12}$ and the base $a$ is greater than or equal to zero, therefore the root is rewritten as a power with rational exponent: $\sqrt[6]{a^{12}} = a^{\frac{12}{6}}$. The exponent $\dfrac{12}{6}$ simplifies to $2$, hence the result is $a^2$.

</div>
