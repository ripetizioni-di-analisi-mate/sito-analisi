## Exercise 1

Expand $(3x - 2)^2$ and $(x + 2)^3$.

$$
(3x - 2)^2 = (3x)^2 - 2 \cdot 3x \cdot 2 + 2^2 = 9x^2 - 12x + 4
$$

$$
(x + 2)^3 = x^3 + 3 \cdot x^2 \cdot 2 + 3 \cdot x \cdot 2^2 + 2^3 = x^3 + 6x^2 + 12x + 8
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the square of the binomial the bases are $A = 3x$ and $B = 2$, so the squares are $9x^2$ and $4$ and the double product is $2 \cdot 3x \cdot 2 = 12x$; the binomial contains a minus sign, therefore the double product appears with a negative sign.

In the cube of the binomial the bases are $A = x$ and $B = 2$. The four terms of the expansion are $A^3 = x^3$, $3A^2 B = 3 \cdot x^2 \cdot 2 = 6x^2$, $3AB^2 = 3 \cdot x \cdot 4 = 12x$ and $B^3 = 8$; the binomial is a sum, so all the signs are positive.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Factor $4x^2 - 25$ and $8x^3 - 27$.

$$
4x^2 - 25 = (2x)^2 - 5^2 = (2x - 5)(2x + 5)
$$

$$
8x^3 - 27 = (2x)^3 - 3^3 = (2x - 3)\left( 4x^2 + 6x + 9 \right)
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The binomial $4x^2 - 25$ is the difference of the squares of $2x$ and $5$, so it factors as the product of the difference and the sum of the bases.

The binomial $8x^3 - 27$ is the difference of the cubes of $A = 2x$ and $B = 3$, therefore the formula $A^3 - B^3 = (A - B)(A^2 + AB + B^2)$ applies: the first factor is $2x - 3$, the second is $A^2 + AB + B^2 = 4x^2 + 6x + 9$. The second factor is a false square with no real roots, so the factorization is complete.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Factor $2x^3 - 8x$ and $3x^3 y + 6x^2 y^2 + 3x y^3$.

$$
2x^3 - 8x = 2x\,(x^2 - 4) = 2x\,(x - 2)(x + 2)
$$

$$
3x^3 y + 6x^2 y^2 + 3x y^3 = 3xy\,(x^2 + 2xy + y^2) = 3xy\,(x + y)^2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

In the first polynomial all the terms contain the factor $2x$, so the first step is factoring out the common factor $2x$. The remaining factor $x^2 - 4$ is a difference of squares, therefore it factors as $(x - 2)(x + 2)$ and the factorization is complete.

In the second polynomial the common factor is $3xy$, and factoring it out leaves $x^2 + 2xy + y^2$. This trinomial contains the squares of $x$ and $y$ and the double product $2xy$ with a positive sign, so it is the square of the binomial $x + y$.

</div>
