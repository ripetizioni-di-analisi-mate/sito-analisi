## Exercise 1

Solve the inequality
$$
x^3 - 9x \leq 0 .
$$

$$
x^3 - 9x = x\left( x^2 - 9 \right) = x\,(x - 3)(x + 3)
$$

$$
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
 & x < -3 & -3 < x < 0 & 0 < x < 3 & x > 3 \\ \hline
x + 3 & - & + & + & + \\
x & - & - & + & + \\
x - 3 & - & - & - & + \\ \hline
x\,(x+3)(x-3) & - & + & - & +
\end{array}
$$

$$
x \leq -3 \ \text{ or } \ 0 \leq x \leq 3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The polynomial factors by factoring out $x$ and using the difference of squares, so the zeros are $-3$, $0$ and $3$ and the real line splits into four intervals. Each first-degree factor is negative before its own zero and positive after it, therefore the chart is filled in row by row.

The sign of the product on each interval is the product of the signs in the column: the product is negative for $x < -3$ and for $0 < x < 3$. The direction of the inequality is $\leq$, so the zeros of the polynomial belong to the solutions and the intervals are taken closed: $x \leq -3$ or $0 \leq x \leq 3$.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve the inequality
$$
(x - 1)^2 (x + 3) < 0 .
$$

$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
 & x < -3 & -3 < x < 1 & x > 1 \\ \hline
(x - 1)^2 & + & + & + \\
x + 3 & - & + & + \\ \hline
(x-1)^2 (x+3) & - & + & +
\end{array}
$$

$$
x < -3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The left-hand side is already factored and its zeros are $1$, with multiplicity two, and $-3$. The factor $(x - 1)^2$ is a square, so it is positive at every point other than $1$ and zero at $1$: in the chart its row always carries the plus sign, and the sign of the product depends only on the factor $x + 3$.

The product is negative only for $x < -3$. The direction is the strict $<$, therefore the zeros $-3$ and $1$ are excluded; the point $x = 1$ lies outside the interval found anyway, so the solutions are $x < -3$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve the inequality
$$
x^4 - 5x^2 + 4 < 0 .
$$

$$
x^4 - 5x^2 + 4 = \left( x^2 - 1 \right)\left( x^2 - 4 \right) = (x + 2)(x + 1)(x - 1)(x - 2)
$$

$$
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
 & x < -2 & -2 < x < -1 & -1 < x < 1 & 1 < x < 2 & x > 2 \\ \hline
x + 2 & - & + & + & + & + \\
x + 1 & - & - & + & + & + \\
x - 1 & - & - & - & + & + \\
x - 2 & - & - & - & - & + \\ \hline
\text{product} & + & - & + & - & +
\end{array}
$$

$$
-2 < x < -1 \ \text{ or } \ 1 < x < 2
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The polynomial is a special trinomial in the variable $x^2$, since $(-1) + (-4) = -5$ and $(-1) \cdot (-4) = 4$, so it factors as $\left( x^2 - 1 \right)\left( x^2 - 4 \right)$; the two differences of squares lead to four first-degree factors, with zeros $-2$, $-1$, $1$ and $2$.

The chart has one row per factor and five columns. The product is negative in the columns with an odd number of minus signs, that is, on the intervals $(-2, -1)$ and $(1, 2)$. The direction is the strict $<$, so the zeros are excluded and the solutions are $-2 < x < -1$ or $1 < x < 2$.

</div>
