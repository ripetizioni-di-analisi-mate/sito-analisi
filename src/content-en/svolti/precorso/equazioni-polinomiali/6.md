## Exercise 1

Solve the system
$$
\begin{cases}
2x - 1 > 0 \\[2pt]
3 - x \geq 0
\end{cases}
$$

$$
2x - 1 > 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x > \frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
3 - x \geq 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x \leq 3
$$

$$
\frac{1}{2} < x \leq 3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The two inequalities are of first degree and are solved separately: the first by isolating $x$ after adding $1$ to both sides and dividing by $2$, the second by adding $x$ to both sides.

The solutions of the system are the points that satisfy both conditions, so the intersection of the half-line $x > \dfrac{1}{2}$ with the half-line $x \leq 3$. The two half-lines have in common the interval $\dfrac{1}{2} < x \leq 3$, with the first endpoint excluded and the second included, in accordance with the directions of the two inequalities.

</div>

## Exercise 2

Solve the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 - 5x + 6 \geq 0 \\[2pt]
x + 2 > 0
\end{cases}
$$

$$
x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0: \qquad \Delta = 25 - 24 = 1, \qquad x = \frac{5 \pm 1}{2}, \qquad x_1 = 2, \ x_2 = 3
$$

$$
x^2 - 5x + 6 \geq 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x \leq 2 \ \text{ or } \ x \geq 3
$$

$$
x + 2 > 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x > -2
$$

$$
-2 < x \leq 2 \ \text{ or } \ x \geq 3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first inequality is of second degree with positive discriminant and roots $2$ and $3$; the leading coefficient is positive, so the trinomial is positive outside the interval between the roots and zero at the roots, and the direction $\geq$ includes the roots. The second inequality is of first degree and gives $x > -2$.

The intersection is read on the real line: from the part $x \leq 2$ the stretch with $x > -2$ survives, that is, $-2 < x \leq 2$; the half-line $x \geq 3$ is entirely contained in $x > -2$, so all of it remains. The solutions of the system are $-2 < x \leq 2$ or $x \geq 3$.

</div>

## Exercise 3

Solve the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 - 9 \leq 0 \\[4pt]
\dfrac{x + 1}{x - 2} \geq 0
\end{cases}
$$

$$
x^2 - 9 \leq 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad -3 \leq x \leq 3
$$

$$
\frac{x + 1}{x - 2} \geq 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x \leq -1 \ \text{ or } \ x > 2
$$

$$
-3 \leq x \leq -1 \ \text{ or } \ 2 < x \leq 3
$$

<div class="spiegazione">

The first inequality is equivalent to $x^2 \leq 9$ and its solutions are the closed interval $[-3, 3]$, since the trinomial $x^2 - 9$ has roots $\pm 3$ and is negative between the roots. The second is a rational inequality: the numerator is positive for $x > -1$ and the denominator for $x > 2$, so the quotient is positive where the signs agree, that is, for $x < -1$ and for $x > 2$. The direction $\geq$ includes the zero of the numerator $x = -1$ and excludes $x = 2$, which makes the denominator vanish.

The intersection of the two sets is obtained on the real line: from the interval $[-3, 3]$ the stretch $-3 \leq x \leq -1$, common to the half-line $x \leq -1$, and the stretch $2 < x \leq 3$, common to the half-line $x > 2$, survive. These two parts are the solutions of the system.

</div>
