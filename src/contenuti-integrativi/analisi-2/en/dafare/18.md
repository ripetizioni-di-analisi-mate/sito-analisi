Compute $e^{xA}$ for $A=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$.

:::soluzione
$$
e^{xA}=\begin{pmatrix}\cos x&-\sin x\\\sin x&\cos x\end{pmatrix}.
$$
:::
