Solve the initial value problem for $Y'=AY$, $A=\begin{pmatrix}4&1\\1&4\end{pmatrix}$, $Y(0)=(3,1)$.

:::soluzione
$$
Y(x)=2e^{5x}(1,1)+e^{3x}(1,-1).
$$
:::
