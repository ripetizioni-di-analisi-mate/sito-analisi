titolo: Exponential with a nilpotent matrix
teoria: [esponenziale-di-matrice, calcolo-esponenziale-matrice]
parole: [matrix exponential, fundamental matrix, diagonalization, nilpotent matrices]
---

Compute $e^{xA}$ for $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$.

:::soluzione
$$
e^{xA}=e^{2x}\begin{pmatrix}1&x\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$
:::
