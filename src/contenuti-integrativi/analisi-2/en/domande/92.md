Solve the initial value problem for $Y'=AY$, $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-2\end{pmatrix}$, $Y(2)=(1,4)$.

- $Y(x)=(e^x,4e^{-2x})$
- $Y(x)=(e^{x-2},4e^{-2(x-2)})$
- $Y(x)=(e^{x-2},4e^{2(x-2)})$
- $Y(x)=(e^{-2(x-2)},4e^{x-2})$

## Explanation

The diagonal entries are $1$ and $-2$. The initial point is $x=2$, so the initial values are multiplied by $e^{x-2}$ and $e^{-2(x-2)}$.
