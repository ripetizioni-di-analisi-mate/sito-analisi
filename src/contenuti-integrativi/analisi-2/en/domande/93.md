Compute $e^{xA}$ for $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$.

- $e^{xA}=e^{2x}I$
- $e^{xA}=\begin{pmatrix}e^{2x}&x\\0&e^{2x}\end{pmatrix}$
- $e^{xA}=e^{2x}\begin{pmatrix}1&x\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$
- $e^{xA}=e^{2x}\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\x&1\end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

Write $A=2I+N$. Since $N^2=0$, we have $e^{xN}=I+xN$, so $e^{xA}=e^{2x}(I+xN)$.
