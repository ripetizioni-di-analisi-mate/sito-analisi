Compute $e^{xA}$ for $A=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$.

- $e^{xA}=\begin{pmatrix}\cos x&\sin x\\-\sin x&\cos x\end{pmatrix}$
- $e^{xA}=\begin{pmatrix}e^x&0\\0&e^{-x}\end{pmatrix}$
- $e^{xA}=\begin{pmatrix}1&-x\\x&1\end{pmatrix}$
- $e^{xA}=\begin{pmatrix}\cos x&-\sin x\\\sin x&\cos x\end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

Since $A^2=-I$, the even powers in the series give $I\cos x$ and the odd powers give $A\sin x$.
