Solve the initial value problem for $Y'=AY$, $A=\begin{pmatrix}4&1\\1&4\end{pmatrix}$, $Y(0)=(3,1)$.

- $Y(x)=2e^{5x}(1,1)+e^{3x}(1,-1)$
- $Y(x)=e^{5x}(1,1)+2e^{3x}(1,-1)$
- $Y(x)=2e^{3x}(1,1)+e^{5x}(1,-1)$
- $Y(x)=e^{5x}(3,1)$

## Explanation

The coefficients along $(1,1)$ and $(1,-1)$ are $2$ and $1$. Their eigenvalues are $5$ and $3$.
