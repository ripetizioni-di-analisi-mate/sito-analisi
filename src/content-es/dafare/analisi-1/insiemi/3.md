Dados $A = \{x \in \mathbb{R} : |x| < 2\}$ y $B = \{x \in \mathbb{R} : x^2 \geq 1\}$, calcula $A \cap B$ y $A \setminus B$.

:::soluzione
$$
A \cap B = (-2, -1] \cup [1, 2), \qquad A \setminus B = (-1, 1)
$$
:::
