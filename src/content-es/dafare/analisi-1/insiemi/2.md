Dados $A = (-3, 2]$ y $B = [0, 4)$, calcula $A \cap B$, $A \cup B$, $A \setminus B$ y el complemento $B^{c}$ respecto de $\mathbb{R}$.

:::soluzione
$$
A \cap B = [0, 2], \qquad A \cup B = (-3, 4), \qquad A \setminus B = (-3, 0), \qquad B^{c} = (-\infty, 0) \cup [4, +\infty)
$$
:::
