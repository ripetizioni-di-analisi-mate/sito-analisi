"I can't come to the meeting," said Luca. Which is the correct reported speech?

- Luca said he couldn't came to the meeting.
- Luca told he couldn't come to the meeting.
- Luca said he hadn't could come to the meeting.
- Luca said he couldn't come to the meeting.

## Spiegazione

Nel **discorso indiretto** il modale *can* diventa *could*, e dopo *could* il verbo resta nella forma base. Quindi la frase corretta è *Luca said he couldn't come to the meeting*. *Couldn't came* mette il passato dopo il modale. *Told he* usa *tell* senza il complemento di persona, che *tell* richiede. *Hadn't could* tratta *could* come un participio, mentre i modali non hanno participio.
