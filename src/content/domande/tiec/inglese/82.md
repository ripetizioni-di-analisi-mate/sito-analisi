Read the text and answer the question:

> Buying second-hand clothes used to be something many young people avoided. Today, however, apps that let users sell the clothes they no longer wear are becoming more and more popular. Sellers take a few photos, write a short description and decide the price themselves. Buyers, on the other hand, can find good-quality items for much less than in a shop. Supporters say that these apps help the environment, because fewer new clothes need to be produced. Critics point out that many users buy more than they need simply because prices are low, and that every parcel has to be transported. The real effect on the environment, they argue, depends on how people use the apps.

What is the text mainly about?

- How to take good photos of clothes to sell online.
- Why young people no longer buy clothes in shops.
- The pollution caused by clothes factories.
- The popularity of apps for second-hand clothes and the debate about their effects.

## Spiegazione

Il brano descrive la diffusione delle app per vendere vestiti usati e poi riporta le opinioni di chi le sostiene e di chi le critica per i loro effetti sull'ambiente. Quindi l'argomento principale è la diffusione di queste app insieme al dibattito sui loro effetti. Le foto sono un dettaglio del funzionamento, troppo particolare per essere il tema. Il brano non dice che i giovani hanno smesso di comprare nei negozi, e le fabbriche di vestiti non sono l'argomento del testo.
