Read the notice in a second-hand shop. “We have stopped putting prices on individual books. Pick up a basket at the door. One payment covers the whole basketful. The colour of the handle sets that payment. Please return empty baskets to the door.” A customer puts two books back on the shelf before paying and keeps the same basket with one book left inside. What happens to the price?

- It falls by the price marked on the two books.
- It automatically falls to one third of the old total.
- It stays the same because the customer still uses the same basket.
- It doubles because the basket is nearly empty.

## Spiegazione

Il prezzo è associato al manico del cesto e vale per tutto il contenuto. Le singole copie non hanno più un prezzo indicato, quindi togliere due libri lasciando lo stesso cesto non modifica il totale.
