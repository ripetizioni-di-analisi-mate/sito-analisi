"I saw the director yesterday," she said. Which is the correct reported speech?

- She said she had seen the director the day before.
- She said she had seen the director the next day.
- She said she has seen the director the day before.
- She said she had saw the director the day before.

## Spiegazione

Nel **discorso indiretto** il past simple *saw* diventa il past perfect *had seen*, e *yesterday* diventa *the day before*, perché il giorno si conta rispetto al momento in cui la frase è stata detta. La frase corretta è quindi *She said she had seen the director the day before*. *The next day* corrisponde a *tomorrow* e sposta l'incontro nel giorno successivo. *Has seen* non arretra il tempo, e *had saw* usa il passato *saw* al posto del participio *seen*.
