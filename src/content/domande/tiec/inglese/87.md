Read the text and answer the question:

> Last year Lumetta, a small design company with forty employees, decided to test a four-day working week for six months. Employees had Fridays off, and their salaries did not change. At the end of the trial, the managers compared the results with the previous year. The number of completed projects was almost the same, while sick days had fallen by a third. Most employees said they felt less tired and had more time for their families. However, the trial also created some problems: clients sometimes complained that they could not reach anyone on Fridays. The company has now decided to keep the four-day week, but one person from each team will work on Fridays, taking turns.

What has the company decided after the trial?

- To return to the five-day week for everyone.
- To let only the managers work on Fridays.
- To repeat the trial for another six months.
- To keep the four-day week, with one person per team working on Fridays in turn.

## Spiegazione

L'ultima frase dice che l'azienda *has now decided to keep the four-day week, but one person from each team will work on Fridays, taking turns*. Quindi la settimana corta resta e il venerdì lavora a turno una persona per gruppo. Il ritorno ai cinque giorni per tutti contraddice *keep the four-day week*. I dirigenti non sono indicati come quelli che lavorano il venerdì, e una nuova prova non è nominata.
