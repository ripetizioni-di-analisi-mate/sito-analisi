Complete the sentence: "While I …… the report, my colleagues …… lunch in the canteen."

- was writing / was having
- was writing / were having
- am writing / were having
- have written / were having

## Spiegazione

La frase descrive due azioni in corso nello stesso momento del passato, quindi entrambe vanno al **past continuous**. L'ausiliare si accorda con il soggetto: *I was writing* e *my colleagues were having*. *Was writing / was having* sbaglia l'accordo con il soggetto plurale *my colleagues*. *Am writing* è un presente e non si accorda con *were having*; *have written* indica un'azione conclusa, mentre *while* introduce un'azione in corso.
