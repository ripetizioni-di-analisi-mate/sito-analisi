Which sentence is grammatically correct?

- If I had known about the delay, I would have taken another train.
- If I had knew about the delay, I would have taken another train.
- If I would have known about the delay, I would take another train.
- If I had known about the delay, I will take another train.

## Spiegazione

La frase parla di un ritardo passato che chi parla non conosceva, quindi serve il **periodo ipotetico del terzo tipo**: past perfect nella condizione e *would have* con il participio nella principale. Solo *If I had known…, I would have taken…* rispetta entrambe le parti. La frase con *If I had knew* usa il passato *knew* al posto del participio *known*. La frase con *If I would have known* mette *would* nella condizione, e quella con *I will take* unisce il past perfect a una principale del primo tipo.
