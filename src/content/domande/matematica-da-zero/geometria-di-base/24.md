Nel triangolo in figura gli angoli in $A$ e in $B$ misurano $52^\circ$ e $71^\circ$. Quanto misura l’angolo in $C$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 6,0 4.16,5.33" linee="0.90,0.00 0.89,0.10 0.88,0.20 0.85,0.30 0.81,0.39 0.76,0.48 0.70,0.57 0.63,0.64 0.55,0.71;5.71,0.85 5.58,0.80 5.46,0.72 5.36,0.63 5.27,0.52 5.20,0.40 5.14,0.27 5.11,0.14 5.10,0.00" etichette="-0.45,-0.45,A;6.1,-0.45,B;4.16,5.65,C;1.1,0.3,52°;4.55,0.45,71°" xmin=-1 xmax=7 ymin=-1 ymax=6.3 legenda=0}

- $57^\circ$
- $123^\circ$
- $109^\circ$
- $128^\circ$

## Spiegazione

Gli angoli interni di un triangolo sommano $180^\circ$. L’angolo in $C$ misura quindi $180^\circ - (52^\circ + 71^\circ) = 180^\circ - 123^\circ = 57^\circ$. Il valore $123^\circ$ è la somma dei due angoli noti, e $128^\circ$ è $180^\circ - 52^\circ$, che non tiene conto dell’angolo in $B$.
