Read the message. «The seminar starts at half past two. Doors open a quarter of an hour earlier.» When do the doors open?

- At a quarter past two.
- At half past two.
- At three o’clock.
- At a quarter to three.
- At two o’clock.

## Spiegazione

«Half past two» corrisponde alle due e mezza. Togliendo un quarto d’ora si arriva alle due e un quarto.
