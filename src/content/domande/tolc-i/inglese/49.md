Read the notice. «At weekends this bus stops at the museum on request. Tell the driver when you get on.» What should a passenger do to get off at the museum on Saturday?

- Show a museum ticket when getting off.
- Ask the driver when boarding.
- Ask another passenger to open the doors.
- Board only at the museum.
- Wait for the driver to make a regular museum stop.

## Spiegazione

«On request» significa su richiesta. Il cartello precisa che la richiesta va fatta al conducente quando si sale.
