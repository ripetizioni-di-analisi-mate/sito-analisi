What does the notice mean? «The rear entrance is for staff only. Visitors should use the main entrance.»

- Staff must enter at the front.
- The rear entrance is closed to everyone.
- Visitors need to enter at the front.
- Both entrances are only for staff.
- Visitors should wait outside the rear entrance.

## Spiegazione

Il cartello riserva l’accesso posteriore al personale e indica l’ingresso principale ai visitatori.
