Read the notice. «No cash payments after six. Card payments are accepted until closing.» Which payment is possible after six and before closing?

- Paying with cash only.
- Paying only with coins.
- Paying by card only before six.
- Paying by card.
- No payment is possible.

## Spiegazione

Il limite delle sei riguarda il contante. Il cartello indica che le carte sono accettate fino alla chiusura.
