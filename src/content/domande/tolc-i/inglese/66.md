Read the email. «Your first appointment was moved from Tuesday to Wednesday. The follow-up is still on Friday.» Which appointment changed?

- An appointment scheduled for Thursday.
- The Friday appointment.
- The first appointment.
- Neither appointment.
- Both appointments.

## Spiegazione

La prima visita è stata spostata da martedì a mercoledì. «Still on Friday» conferma che il controllo rimane venerdì.
