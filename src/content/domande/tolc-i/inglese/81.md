Read the email. «Your order has arrived. We can hold it at the shop until Thursday. From Friday it will be sent back to the warehouse.» What should the customer do to collect it from the shop?

- Collect it by Thursday.
- Wait until Friday.
- Place a new order before visiting.
- Collect it only on Friday morning.
- Go directly to the warehouse on Thursday.

## Spiegazione

Il negozio tiene disponibile l’ordine fino a giovedì compreso. Da venerdì è previsto il reso al magazzino.
