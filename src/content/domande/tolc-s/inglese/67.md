Choose the meaning of «The technician will look into the problem tomorrow».

- The technician will investigate the problem.
- The technician will solve the problem before tomorrow.
- The technician will explain how the problem was solved.
- The technician will create a new problem.
- The technician will ignore the problem.

## Spiegazione

*Look into* significa esaminare o indagare. La frase indica che l’esame avverrà domani e lascia aperto il suo esito.
