Read the note. «Please leave the small bottles on the shelf. The larger ones must be taken downstairs.» What does *ones* refer to?

- Bottles.
- Stairs.
- Rooms.
- Labels.
- Shelves.

## Spiegazione

*Ones* sostituisce il sostantivo plurale *bottles*. L’aggettivo *larger* distingue le bottiglie grandi da quelle piccole.
