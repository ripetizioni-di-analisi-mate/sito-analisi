Read the email. «We have moved the seminar from Room 4 to Room 7. It will still start at ten.» What has changed?

- The speaker.
- The duration.
- The room.
- The date and start time.
- The start time.

## Spiegazione

*From Room 4 to Room 7* indica lo spostamento. *Still start at ten* conferma che l’ora di inizio è rimasta la stessa.
