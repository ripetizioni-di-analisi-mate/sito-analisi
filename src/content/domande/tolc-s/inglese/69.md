Read the note. «I reserved two seats, but Ben cannot come. You may use the spare seat.» What does *spare* mean here?

- Already occupied by Ben.
- Reserved for a different day.
- Available because it is not needed.
- Broken and unusable.
- More expensive than usual.

## Spiegazione

Ben non può partecipare e uno dei due posti prenotati rimane libero. *Spare* qui significa disponibile in più.
