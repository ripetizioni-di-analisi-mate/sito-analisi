Complete the sentence. Eva's sample weighs two grams and Niko's also weighs two grams. Eva's sample is ___ Niko's.

- not as heavy as
- as heavy as
- heavier than
- twice as heavy as
- the heaviest of

## Spiegazione

I campioni hanno lo stesso peso. La struttura *as* + aggettivo + *as* esprime uguaglianza nella proprietà confrontata.
