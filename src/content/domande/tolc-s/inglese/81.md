Choose the correct question about yesterday's class.

- Was Max bring his notes?
- Did Max brought his notes?
- Did Max bring his notes?
- Does Max brought his notes?
- Did Max brings his notes?

## Spiegazione

Per chiedere di un’azione avvenuta ieri si usa *did* prima del soggetto. L’ausiliare indica già il passato, quindi segue *bring*.
