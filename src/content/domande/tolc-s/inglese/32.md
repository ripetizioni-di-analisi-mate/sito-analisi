Read the note. «I left the library books at the front desk because the return box was full.» Why did the writer use the front desk?

- The front desk was closed.
- The library was about to open.
- The return box was missing.
- There was no room in the return box.
- The books could only be used at the desk.

## Spiegazione

La frase introdotta da *because* indica il motivo. Il contenitore per i resi era pieno, perciò i libri sono stati lasciati al banco.
