Choose the meaning of «The meeting has been put off until Friday».

- The meeting has been moved to an earlier day.
- The meeting has been postponed to Friday.
- The meeting has been cancelled permanently.
- The meeting has already ended.
- The meeting takes place every Friday.

## Spiegazione

*Put off* significa rinviare. *Until Friday* specifica la nuova data senza indicare una cadenza settimanale.
