Read the instruction. «Submit the online form by noon. Bring a printed copy only if the website shows an error.» The form was submitted successfully. What should you do?

- You should wait for the website to show an error.
- You must print the form in every case.
- You must submit the form after noon.
- You must complete a different form on paper.
- You can attend without bringing a printed copy.

## Spiegazione

L’espressione *only if* limita la richiesta della copia ai casi in cui il sito mostra un errore. Qui l’invio è riuscito.
