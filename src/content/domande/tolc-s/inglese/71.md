Read the message. «You can collect your coat before five or ask us to keep it until tomorrow.» What can someone do if they cannot arrive before five?

- Collect it only after buying a ticket.
- Ask for the coat to be kept until tomorrow.
- Collect the coat after five today.
- Pay to have a new coat delivered.
- Leave the coat there for the entire week.

## Spiegazione

Il messaggio offre la possibilità di chiedere che il cappotto venga conservato fino a domani. È l’alternativa al ritiro prima delle cinque.
