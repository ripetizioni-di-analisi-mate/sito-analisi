Read the sign. «Use the stairs in case of fire. The lift may be used at other times.» What does the sign say about the lift?

- It is reserved for staff at all times.
- It can never be used.
- It is the only way to reach the stairs.
- It is permitted when there is no fire.
- It must be used in a fire.

## Spiegazione

*At other times* rimanda ai momenti diversi dall’emergenza descritta. In quei momenti *may be used* esprime un permesso.
