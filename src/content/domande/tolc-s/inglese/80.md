Read the notice. «The café is closed for repairs on Wednesday. It will open as usual on Thursday.» When can customers next visit after Tuesday?

- On Wednesday afternoon.
- The following Monday.
- Only on Friday.
- On Wednesday morning.
- On Thursday.

## Spiegazione

L’avviso indica la chiusura per l’intera giornata di mercoledì e la riapertura giovedì secondo il solito orario.
