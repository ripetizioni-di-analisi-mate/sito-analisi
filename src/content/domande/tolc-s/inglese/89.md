Choose the sentence that means «There is no obligation to print the form».

- You mustn't print the form.
- You have to print the form.
- You must print the form.
- You don't have to print the form.
- You can't print the form.

## Spiegazione

*Don't have to* esprime assenza di obbligo. *Mustn't* e *can't* qui indicherebbero invece un divieto o l’impossibilità di stampare.
