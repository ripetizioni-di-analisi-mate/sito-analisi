Complete the sentence. We have three routes. The blue one is eight kilometres long, the green one is six, and the red one is five. The red route is the ___ of the three.

- less long than
- longest
- more short
- shorter than
- shortest

## Spiegazione

Il percorso rosso ha la lunghezza minore dell’intero gruppo. Il superlativo di *short* è *shortest*.
