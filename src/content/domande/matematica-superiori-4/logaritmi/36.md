Quanto vale $\log_4 2 + \log_{\frac{1}{2}} 4$?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Spiegazione

Poiché $4^{\frac{1}{2}} = 2$, si ha $\log_4 2 = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

Con la base reciproca vale $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} 4 = -\log_2 4 = -2$.

Quindi la somma vale $\dfrac{1}{2} - 2 = -\dfrac{3}{2}$.
