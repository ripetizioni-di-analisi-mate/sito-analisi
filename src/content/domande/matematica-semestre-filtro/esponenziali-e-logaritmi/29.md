Determinare il valore approssimato di $x$ se $\log_{10}(x)=-2{,}30$, usando $\log_{10}(2)\approx0{,}30$.

- $2\cdot10^{-2}$
- $5\cdot10^{-2}$
- $5\cdot10^{-3}$
- $2\cdot10^{-3}$

## Spiegazione

$10^{-2{,}30}=10^{-2}\cdot 10^{-0{,}30}\approx10^{-2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=5\cdot10^{-3}$.
