Quanto vale $2-\left(\dfrac34+\dfrac56\right)$?

- $\dfrac{13}{12}$
- $\dfrac{19}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{7}{12}$

## Spiegazione

La parentesi vale $\dfrac9{12}+\dfrac{10}{12}=\dfrac{19}{12}$. L’intero $2$ equivale a $\dfrac{24}{12}$, quindi la differenza è $\dfrac5{12}$.
