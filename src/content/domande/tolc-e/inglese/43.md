Read the instruction. «Pay online before you arrive. At the door, show the confirmation on your phone.» What should you show at the door?

- The organiser's phone number.
- An unsigned paper form.
- Cash for the full price.
- The original advertisement.
- The payment confirmation on your phone.

## Spiegazione

L'istruzione distingue il pagamento anticipato dalla verifica all'ingresso. Al personale si mostra la conferma sul telefono.
