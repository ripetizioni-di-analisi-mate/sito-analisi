Read the message. «The talk begins in Room 12 as planned. The workshop that follows it will take place in Room 18.» Which statement about the talk is supported?

- The talk has moved to Room 18.
- The talk will happen after the workshop.
- The room for the talk has stayed the same.
- The workshop has been cancelled.
- Both events now take place in Room 18.

## Spiegazione

As planned conferma la sede iniziale del talk. Room 18 riguarda l'attività successiva.
