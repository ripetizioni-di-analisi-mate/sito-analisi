Read the notice. «The first bus is cancelled. The next bus leaves from the same stop twenty minutes later.» What stays the same?

- The number of departures.
- The departure time.
- The driver, according to the notice.
- The fact that the first bus will run.
- The departure stop.

## Spiegazione

Same stop indica che la fermata resta uguale. Twenty minutes later modifica l'orario.
