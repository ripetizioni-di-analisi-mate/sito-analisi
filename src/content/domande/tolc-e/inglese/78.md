What does «affordable» mean in «We are looking for affordable accommodation»?

- It costs an amount we can pay.
- It is difficult to find on a map.
- It is only available for groups.
- It has no kitchen or bathroom.
- It has already been booked.

## Spiegazione

Affordable descrive un prezzo che rientra nelle proprie possibilità. Non significa necessariamente gratuito.
