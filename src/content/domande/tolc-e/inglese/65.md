Read the sign. «You may leave your bike here during the day. Please remove it before the gates close at eight.» What must cyclists do?

- Collect their bikes before eight.
- Leave their bikes overnight.
- Bring their bikes only after closing.
- Move their bikes inside after eight.
- Pay before leaving a bike there.

## Spiegazione

Before the gates close fissa un limite. Le biciclette vanno ritirate prima delle otto.
