Read the notice. «The lift is working again, but the west entrance remains closed. Please use the south entrance.» Which statement is correct?

- Visitors should enter from the west.
- The lift is still out of service.
- The south entrance is under repair.
- Both entrances are open.
- Visitors should enter from the south.

## Spiegazione

L'ultima frase dà l'istruzione per l'accesso. La riparazione dell'ascensore non comporta la riapertura dell'ingresso ovest.
