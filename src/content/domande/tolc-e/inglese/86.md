Read the message. «I am already on the train. I left your notebook at reception before I left.» Where is the notebook?

- On the train.
- At the writer's home.
- In the writer's bag.
- In the station café.
- At reception.

## Spiegazione

Left your notebook at reception indica dove il quaderno è stato lasciato prima della partenza.
