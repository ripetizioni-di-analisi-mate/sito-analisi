Read the message. «I missed the start of the lecture because the bus was delayed. Could you send me the notes from the first ten minutes?» What does the writer ask for?

- A copy of the bus timetable.
- Notes from the opening part of the lecture.
- A seat near the door for the next lecture.
- A recording of the whole course.
- Notes from the final part of the lecture.

## Spiegazione

From the first ten minutes precisa quale parte è mancata. La richiesta riguarda gli appunti relativi all'inizio.
