Read the email. «I can meet in person on Wednesday afternoon. If that does not suit you, I am free for a video call on Thursday morning.» What is offered as the alternative?

- A meeting in person on Wednesday morning.
- A video call on Wednesday afternoon.
- A video call on Thursday morning.
- A face-to-face meeting on Thursday morning.
- A meeting by phone on Friday.

## Spiegazione

La frase introdotta da if offre l'alternativa. Sia la modalità, video, sia il momento, giovedì mattina, cambiano.
