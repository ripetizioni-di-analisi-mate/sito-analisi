Read the notice. «The help desk closes at four today. For urgent problems after four, call the number on your student card.» What should you do for an urgent problem at five?

- Call the number on the student card.
- Wait at the desk until the next person arrives.
- Call the number printed on the front door.
- Send your card to the office by post.
- Visit the help desk before it opens tomorrow.

## Spiegazione

La seconda frase indica una procedura alternativa per le urgenze dopo le quattro. Il numero è sulla tessera.
