What does «out of stock» mean in «This item is currently out of stock»?

- It is the cheapest item in the shop.
- There are no units available to sell.
- It is sold only to members.
- The price is valid for a short time.
- The shop cannot accept card payments.

## Spiegazione

Out of stock riguarda la disponibilità a magazzino. L'articolo è temporaneamente esaurito.
