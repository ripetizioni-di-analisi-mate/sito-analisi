What does «due» mean in «The application is due on Friday»?

- It opens only after Friday.
- It can be ignored until next month.
- It must be submitted by Friday.
- It was submitted last Friday.
- It must be checked every Friday.

## Spiegazione

Due indica il momento previsto per la consegna. Friday è la scadenza dell'iscrizione.
