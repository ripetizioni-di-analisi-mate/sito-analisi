Read the notice. «The library will accept returns through the outside box while the counter is closed.» What can readers do during the closure?

- Renew their cards at the counter.
- Return books using the outside box.
- Collect reservations at the outside box.
- Borrow books from the outside box.
- Ask the counter staff to reopen immediately.

## Spiegazione

Returns riguarda la restituzione. La cassetta esterna consente quel servizio anche con lo sportello chiuso.
