Read the note. «There are two draft schedules. Please use the one marked “revised”, as the other still includes the old dates.» Which schedule should you use?

- A new schedule made from the old dates.
- The schedule marked “revised”.
- Both schedules at the same time.
- The one that has no label.
- The schedule with the old dates.

## Spiegazione

Marked revised significa contrassegnato come aggiornato. La motivazione è che l'altra bozza contiene ancora date vecchie.
