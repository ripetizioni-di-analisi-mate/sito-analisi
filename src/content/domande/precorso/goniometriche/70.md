Quali valori in $\left[0, 2 \pi\right)$ soddisfano $6\sin\left(x\right)\geq 3$?

- $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{7 \pi}{6}\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{6}\right]$
- $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{6}\right)$
- $\left[\dfrac{7 \pi}{6}, \dfrac{11 \pi}{6}\right]$

## Spiegazione

Il seno vale $\dfrac{1}{2}$ agli estremi $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ e $\dfrac{5 \pi}{6}$. Tra questi due angoli rimane almeno pari a quel valore, quindi $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{6}\right]$.
