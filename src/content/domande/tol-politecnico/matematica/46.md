Quali valori di $x$ in $[0,\pi]$ soddisfano $2\cos^2x-1=0$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{3 \pi}{4}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{6}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{4}\right\}$

## Spiegazione

Serve $\cos x=\pm\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$. Nell’intervallo indicato, i due segni si ottengono per $\dfrac\pi4$ e $\dfrac{3\pi}4$.
