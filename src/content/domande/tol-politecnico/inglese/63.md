**An office move**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

The reception desk will move to the first floor next week. Deliveries should still come through the side gate, where a porter will direct them. Visitors with appointments should use the main entrance and follow the temporary signs.

**Deliveries will continue to arrive `___`.**

- without asking the porter where to go
- through a different building
- through the main entrance
- through the side gate
- at the visitors’ reception upstairs

## Spiegazione

Il testo mantiene il cancello laterale come accesso per le consegne.
