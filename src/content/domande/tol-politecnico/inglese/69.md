**Bicycle hire**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Take a helmet from the rack if you need one. You can return the bicycle at any Dock Street station before sunset. Helmets must go back to this desk, `___` you leave the bicycle somewhere else. The desk closes at six in the evening.

- as long you
- despite you
- unless of
- even if
- because of you

## Spiegazione

La bici può restare altrove e l’obbligo per il casco vale anche in quel caso. “Even if” esprime proprio questa concessione.
