**A borrowed coat**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Ella: I’ve left your coat with the neighbour. I meant to bring it yesterday, but I only got back late last night.

Tom: Thanks. I won’t need it until the weekend. I’ll collect it when I get home from work.

**Ella did not bring the coat yesterday because `___`.**

- Tom was leaving for the weekend
- the coat was still at the shop
- Tom had already collected it
- she got back late
- the neighbour could not find it

## Spiegazione

Ella attribuisce il ritardo al proprio rientro a tarda sera.
