**Email from a tutor**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Hi Sam, Thursday’s workshop `___` from Room 4 to Room 9. The start time is still ten in the morning. Please bring your draft. If you have already sent it to me, a copy on your phone will do. See you there, Nina.

- had move
- has moved
- did moving
- were moving
- have move

## Spiegazione

Il soggetto “workshop” è singolare. Il present perfect usa “has” seguito dal participio “moved” per una modifica già avvenuta con effetto attuale.
