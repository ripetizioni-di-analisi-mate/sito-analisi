**The meeting reminder**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

We need to reach a decision about the budget tomorrow. The first twenty minutes are for questions about the figures. If you have an alternative proposal, send it tonight `___` everyone can read it before the meeting. We’ll vote after the discussion.

- despite
- in place of
- such that of
- although of
- so that

## Spiegazione

“So that” introduce lo scopo dell’invio anticipato, permettere a tutti di leggere prima della riunione.
