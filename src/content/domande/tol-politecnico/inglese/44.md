**At the repair desk**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

A customer brings in a lamp that sometimes goes out. The assistant can test it today, but any parts will `___`. If the switch is the problem, the repair should be ready next Tuesday. The assistant will phone before ordering anything that costs more than fifteen pounds.

- have been order
- have to be ordered
- have to ordering
- have to order themselves
- have to be ordering

## Spiegazione

I ricambi sono l’oggetto dell’ordine. “Have to be ordered” esprime la necessità con una forma passiva.
