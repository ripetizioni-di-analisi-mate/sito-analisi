**Lost and found**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

I left a grey scarf somewhere on the train. It might be on the seat beside the door. If you find it, please hand it to the conductor rather than leave it at the next station. I’ve already told the railway company which train I was on.

**The railway company already knows `___`.**

- who will drive the next train
- who found the scarf
- where the writer bought the scarf
- exactly where the scarf is
- which train the writer was on

## Spiegazione

L’ultima frase dice che la compagnia è stata informata del treno su cui si trovava l’autore.
