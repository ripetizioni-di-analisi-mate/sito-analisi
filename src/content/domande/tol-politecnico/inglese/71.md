**A delayed order**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Your order is ready except for the blue notebook. We can send the remaining items today and the notebook next week at no extra cost. If you prefer one delivery, reply before midday and we’ll hold everything until the notebook arrives.

**To receive only one parcel, the customer must reply `___`.**

- at the end of next week
- after the first parcel is sent
- before midday
- only when the shop closes
- sometime next month

## Spiegazione

Il messaggio richiede una risposta prima di mezzogiorno per trattenere tutto fino all’arrivo del quaderno.
