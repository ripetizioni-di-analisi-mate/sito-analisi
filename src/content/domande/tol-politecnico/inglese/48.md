**Café sign**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

The lunch menu comes with soup or a small salad. You can swap either for chips for one pound extra. We have `___` tomato soup, but the vegetable soup is still available. Please order at the counter before taking a seat.

- kept away from
- looked forward to
- come up with
- run out of
- put up with

## Spiegazione

“Run out of” significa aver esaurito una scorta. La zuppa di pomodoro è terminata.
