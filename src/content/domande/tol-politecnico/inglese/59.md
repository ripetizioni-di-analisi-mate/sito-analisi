**A neighbour’s plants**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

While I’m away, could you water the plants on the balcony every `___` day? The pots in the hallway need no water until I return. If there is heavy rain, skip the balcony watering that day and check the soil the next morning.

- second of
- further of
- more
- alternating of
- other

## Spiegazione

“Every other day” indica una frequenza di un giorno sì e uno no.
