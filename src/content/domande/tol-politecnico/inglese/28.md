**Café sign**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

The lunch menu comes with soup or a small salad. You can swap either for chips for one pound extra. We have run out of tomato soup, but the vegetable soup is still available. Please order at the counter before taking a seat.

**A customer who wants chips instead of the salad will pay `___`.**

- only for the salad
- one pound less
- nothing for lunch
- twice the price of the meal
- one pound more

Sostituire la zuppa o l’insalata con le patatine costa una sterlina in più.
