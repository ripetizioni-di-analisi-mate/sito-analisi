**A spare seat**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank. Select `---` if no word is needed.

Lena: I have two tickets for the early show. Pat can’t come now. Would you like the spare?

Mo: I finish work at six. What time does it start?

Lena: At a quarter past six, but the theatre is just across from `___` your office.

Mo: In that case, count me in.

- of
- to
- `---`
- in
- the

## Spiegazione

“Your” determina già “office”. La frase è completa come “across from your office”.
