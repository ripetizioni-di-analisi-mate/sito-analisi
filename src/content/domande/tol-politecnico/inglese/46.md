**A missed connection**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Maya: I missed the bus after my train. The next one leaves in forty minutes.

Leo: I’m near the station. I can pick you up in fifteen.

Maya: Thanks. I’ll wait `___` the bakery on the station side of the road. I have a blue bag.

- during
- among
- since
- by
- throughout

## Spiegazione

“By the bakery” indica un punto di attesa vicino al panificio.
