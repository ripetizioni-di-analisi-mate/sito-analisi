**A borrowed coat**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Ella: I’ve left your coat with the neighbour. I `___` bring it yesterday, but I only got back late last night.

Tom: Thanks. I won’t need it until the weekend. I’ll collect it when I get home from work.

- was used to
- would rather of
- put off to
- looked forward to
- meant to

## Spiegazione

“Meant to bring” indica che Ella intendeva portare il cappotto, anche se poi non l’ha fatto.
