**The wrong size**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Hi Alex, I ordered a table for the kitchen, but the long side is wider than the space by the window. The shop will collect it on Thursday. I’ve asked them to send the smaller one only after I check `___` it can go through the hallway.

- instead
- apart
- during
- despite
- whether

## Spiegazione

“Whether” introduce il dubbio da verificare, cioè se il tavolo passerà nel corridoio.
