**At the repair desk**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

A customer brings in a lamp that sometimes goes out. The assistant can test it today, but any parts will have to be ordered. If the switch is the problem, the repair should be ready next Tuesday. The assistant will phone before ordering anything that costs more than fifteen pounds.

**If the switch is the problem, the lamp should be ready `___`.**

- next Tuesday
- before any test is possible
- on the day the customer first bought it
- at closing time today
- later today
