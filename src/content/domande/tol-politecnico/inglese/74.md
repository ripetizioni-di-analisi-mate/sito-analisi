**Exchange at a shop**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

The jacket fits, but I’d prefer a darker colour. I bought it here last week. I’ve brought the receipt, and the label is still attached. If you don’t have the dark blue one in this size, I’d rather keep this jacket than take a larger one.

**The jacket is the right size, but the customer would like `___`.**

- a lighter colour
- a refund for another item
- a different receipt
- a larger size
- a darker colour

## Spiegazione

Il cliente dice “the jacket fits” e chiede un colore più scuro.
