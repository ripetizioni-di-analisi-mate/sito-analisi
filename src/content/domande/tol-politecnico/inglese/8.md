**A key for the cleaner**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

I’ll be out when the cleaner arrives. Could you let him in and leave the front door key on the hall table? He has his own key for the back door, but it won’t work until the lock has been repaired. The repair is planned for tomorrow.

**The cleaner’s own key will work again after `___`.**

- the back door lock has been repaired
- the writer returns from work
- he moves the hall table
- he puts his key in a bowl
- the front door has been painted

## Spiegazione

Il problema riguarda la serratura posteriore, che sarà riparata.
