**At the swimming pool**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

The small pool is reserved for lessons until noon. Swimmers who have booked the large pool can use it from nine. If the large pool reaches its limit, people without a booking will be asked to wait in the lobby `___` by the water.

- even though of
- provided to
- rather than
- because of to
