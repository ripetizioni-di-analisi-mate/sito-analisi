**The hall next door**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Our evening class will meet in the hall next door this week. The usual room will be painted, and the new hall has no projector. Please read the two pages sent yesterday so that we can discuss the pictures without showing them on a screen.

**The class will meet in the hall next door because `___`.**

- the pages have been lost
- the class has been cancelled
- the usual room will be painted
- the usual room has too many projectors
- the lesson took place yesterday

## Spiegazione

L’aula consueta deve essere tinteggiata, quindi per una settimana si usa la sala accanto.
