**Parcel delivery**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

We tried to deliver your parcel today. It `___` to the post office on Mill Road tomorrow morning. You can collect it from noon with photo ID. If someone goes in your place, they need their own ID and the code on this card.

- will be taken
- must taken
- will take
- is take
- has taken itself

## Spiegazione

Il pacco sarà portato all’ufficio postale. “Will be taken” è il futuro passivo.
