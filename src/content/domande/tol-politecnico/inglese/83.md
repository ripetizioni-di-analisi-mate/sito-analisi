**A change of mind**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

I was going to take the highway, but the traffic report says there’s a long queue near the bridge. The road by the river is longer on the map, yet it should be quicker this morning. Let’s meet outside the station at the same time as planned.

**Although the route has changed, the meeting will still take place `___`.**

- an hour earlier
- on the following morning
- at the original time
- after the station closes
- as soon as the bridge is repaired

## Spiegazione

“At the same time as planned” conferma l’orario stabilito.
