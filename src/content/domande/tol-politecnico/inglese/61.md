**Parcel delivery**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

We tried to deliver your parcel today. It will be taken to the post office on Mill Road tomorrow morning. You can collect it from noon with photo ID. If someone goes `___` your place, they need their own ID and the code on this card.

- at
- in
- by
- over
- off

## Spiegazione

“In your place” significa che un’altra persona agisce al posto del destinatario.
