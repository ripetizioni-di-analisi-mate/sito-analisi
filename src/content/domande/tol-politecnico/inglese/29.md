**A spare seat**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Lena: I have two tickets for the early show. Pat can’t come now. Would you like the spare?

Mo: I finish work at six. What time does it start?

Lena: At a quarter past six, but the theatre is just across from your office.

Mo: In that case, count me in.

**By saying “count me in”, Mo means that `___`.**

- they will ask Pat to come instead
- they need Lena to count the seats
- they decline the offer
- they accept the offer
- they want the theatre to close early

## Spiegazione

“Count me in” segnala che Mo parteciperà.
