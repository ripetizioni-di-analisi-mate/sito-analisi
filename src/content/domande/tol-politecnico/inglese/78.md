**An office move**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

The reception desk will move to the first floor next week. Deliveries should `___` come through the side gate, where a porter will direct them. Visitors with appointments should use the main entrance and follow the temporary signs.

- continued
- still to
- still
- ongoing
- yet to

## Spiegazione

“Still” mantiene la disposizione per le consegne anche dopo il trasferimento della reception.
