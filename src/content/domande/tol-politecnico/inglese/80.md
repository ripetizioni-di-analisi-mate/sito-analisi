**Camera loan**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

You may borrow a camera for up to three days. Memory cards are available separately and must be returned in their cases. Before you leave, take one photograph at the desk `___` a member of staff can check that the camera works.

- so that
- although of
- in spite to
- by the time of
- even after of

## Spiegazione

La fotografia viene fatta affinché un addetto possa verificare il funzionamento. “So that” introduce lo scopo.
