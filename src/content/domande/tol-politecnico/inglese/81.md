**A borrowed coat**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Ella: I’ve left your coat with the neighbour. I meant to bring it yesterday, but I only got back late last night.

Tom: Thanks. I won’t need it until the weekend. I’ll collect it when I get home from work.

**Tom will collect the coat when `___`.**

- Ella comes back from work
- he finishes last night’s journey
- he goes to work this morning
- the weekend ends
- he gets home from work

## Spiegazione

Tom dice che passerà a ritirarlo quando tornerà dal lavoro.
