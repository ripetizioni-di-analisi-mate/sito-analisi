**Museum route**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

The west staircase is being repaired. To reach the upper gallery, `___` the yellow signs from the shop and take the lift. Visitors who only want to see the ground-floor exhibition may go straight through the glass doors.

- belong to
- consist of
- wait across
- follow
- pass around of

## Spiegazione

“Follow the signs” significa seguire la segnaletica che guida dal negozio all’ascensore.
