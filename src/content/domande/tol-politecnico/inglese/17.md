**A neighbour’s plants**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

While I’m away, could you water the plants on the balcony every other day? The pots in the hallway need no water until I return. If there is heavy rain, skip the balcony watering that day and check the soil the next morning.

**On a day with heavy rain, the reader should `___`.**

- skip watering the balcony plants
- water the balcony plants twice
- move all the pots inside
- ask the writer to return immediately
- water every plant in the hallway

## Spiegazione

In caso di pioggia intensa il messaggio chiede di saltare l’annaffiatura del balcone.
