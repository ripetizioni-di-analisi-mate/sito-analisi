**Weekend help**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

We need two volunteers to set out chairs on Friday afternoon and four to welcome guests on Saturday evening. Anyone who helps on Friday can watch Saturday’s performance for free. If you can only come on Saturday, please arrive an hour `___` the doors open.

- after of
- since to
- before
- during at
- until at

## Spiegazione

La presenza è richiesta un’ora prima che aprano le porte. “Before” stabilisce l’ordine dei due eventi.
