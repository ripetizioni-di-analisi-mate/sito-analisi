**The hall next door**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Our evening class will meet in the hall next door this week. The usual room will be painted, and the new hall has no projector. Please read the two pages sent yesterday `___` we can discuss the pictures without showing them on a screen.

- even though of
- so that
- in spite to
- because of to
- although of

## Spiegazione

“So that” introduce lo scopo della lettura anticipata, discutere le immagini anche senza proiettore.
