**Bicycle hire**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Take a helmet from the rack if you need one. You can return the bicycle at `___` Dock Street station before sunset. Helmets must go back to this desk, even if you leave the bicycle somewhere else. The desk closes at six in the evening.

- either of
- any
- every of
- each of
- none

## Spiegazione

“Any” prima di “station” permette di scegliere liberamente una stazione della rete indicata.
