**Library notice**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

The main library will be closed on Monday `___` the lights are replaced. Books due that day may be returned on Tuesday without a late fee. If you need a quiet place to study on Monday, the study room on King Street will be open as usual.

- until to
- without
- during
- while
- by

## Spiegazione

Qui la sostituzione delle luci avviene durante la chiusura. “While” introduce l’evento contemporaneo.
