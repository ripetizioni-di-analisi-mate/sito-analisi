Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

The choir’s rehearsal is open to visitors for the first half hour. After that, the doors will close while the singers work on a new piece. You may leave at any point during the open part, but please use the side door so that you do not cross in front of the singers.

**Visitors should use the side door so that they avoid `___`.**

- hearing the rehearsal at all
- finding the new piece
- going into the building on time
- walking in front of the singers
- leaving before the doors close
