**At the swimming pool**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

The small pool is reserved for lessons until noon. Swimmers who have booked the large pool can use it from nine. If the large pool reaches its limit, people without a booking will be asked to wait in the lobby rather than by the water.

**When the large pool is full, someone without a booking should wait `___`.**

- in the lobby
- in an ongoing lesson
- at the side of the large pool
- inside the small pool
- by the water

## Spiegazione

La sala di ingresso è il luogo di attesa previsto quando la vasca è piena.
