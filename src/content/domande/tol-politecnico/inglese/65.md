**A delayed order**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Your order is ready `___` the blue notebook. We can send the remaining items today and the notebook next week at no extra cost. If you prefer one delivery, reply before midday and we’ll hold everything until the notebook arrives.

- ahead of
