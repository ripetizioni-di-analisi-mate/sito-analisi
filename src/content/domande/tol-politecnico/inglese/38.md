**A key for the cleaner**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

I’ll be out when the cleaner arrives. Could you let him `___` and leave the front door key on the hall table? He has his own key for the back door, but it won’t work until the lock has been repaired. The repair is planned for tomorrow.

- in
- into
- to
- at
- for

## Spiegazione

“Let him in” significa far entrare il cleaner. Qui la particella “in” completa il verbo senza indicare un luogo.
