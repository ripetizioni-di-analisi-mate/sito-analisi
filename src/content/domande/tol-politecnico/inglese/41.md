**A missed connection**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Maya: I `___` the bus after my train. The next one leaves in forty minutes.

Leo: I’m near the station. I can pick you up in fifteen.

Maya: Thanks. I’ll wait by the bakery on the station side of the road. I have a blue bag.

- missed
- lost to
- forgot of
- failed on
- left behind to

## Spiegazione

In questo contesto “miss a bus” significa perdere l’autobus perché è già partito.
