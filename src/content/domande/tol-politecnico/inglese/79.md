**The wrong size**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Hi Alex, I ordered a table for the kitchen, but the long side is wider `___` the space by the window. The shop will collect it on Thursday. I’ve asked them to send the smaller one only after I check whether it can go through the hallway.

- than
- to
- of
- at
- as

## Spiegazione

Il comparativo “wider” richiede “than” prima del termine di paragone.
