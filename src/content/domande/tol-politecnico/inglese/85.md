**An office move**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

The reception desk will move to the first floor next week. Deliveries should still come through the side gate, where a porter will direct them. Visitors with appointments should use the main entrance and follow the temporary signs.

**Visitors with appointments should enter through the main entrance and `___`.**

- go to the side gate
- look for an entrance outside the building
- avoid all signs
- follow the temporary signs
- wait for delivery drivers

## Spiegazione

Le indicazioni temporanee guidano i visitatori con appuntamento a partire dall’ingresso principale.
