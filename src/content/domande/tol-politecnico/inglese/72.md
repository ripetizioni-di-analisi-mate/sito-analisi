**Shared flat message**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

I have an online interview at half past two tomorrow. Could you use headphones in the living room `___` three? The kitchen is fine, because I’ll keep my door closed. After the interview I’ll put the spare key back in the blue bowl.

- by to
- for at
- since to
- during at
- until

## Spiegazione

“Until three” indica fino a quando va mantenuta la situazione richiesta, cioè l’uso delle cuffie.
