**Food collection**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

We collect unopened packets of rice, pasta and flour at the hall on Sunday. Please leave fresh food at home, as the hall has no fridge. If a packet is damaged, tell a volunteer before placing it on the shelves.

**Fresh food is unsuitable for the collection because `___`.**

- the hall has no fridge
- the volunteers collect only drinks
- the shelves have been removed
- the hall closes before Sunday
- rice has to be bought on Sunday

## Spiegazione

La ragione dichiarata è che nel locale non c’è un frigorifero.
