**The box in the hall**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

A note on the door reads, “Please put the signed forms in the red box. Keep unsigned forms with you. If the box is full, give your signed form to an assistant instead. We’ll empty the box at the end of the meeting.”

**If the box is full, a signed form can be given `___`.**

- to the person who will empty it tomorrow
- to an assistant
- only to the next meeting’s organiser
- to nobody until the end of the next day
- to the first person leaving the meeting

## Spiegazione

L’assistente è l’alternativa indicata quando la scatola è piena.
