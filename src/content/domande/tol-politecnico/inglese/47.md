**Camera loan**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

You may borrow a camera for `___` three days. Memory cards are available separately and must be returned in their cases. Before you leave, take one photograph at the desk so that a member of staff can check that the camera works.

- after of
- owing to
- ahead at
- during to
- up to

## Spiegazione

“Up to” indica la durata massima del prestito, tre giorni.
