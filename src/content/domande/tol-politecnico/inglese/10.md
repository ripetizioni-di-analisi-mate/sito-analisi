**Email from a tutor**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Hi Sam, Thursday’s workshop has moved from Room 4 to Room 9. The start time is still ten in the morning. Please bring your draft. If you have already sent it `___` me, a copy on your phone will do. See you there, Nina.

- to
- over
- on
- from
- into

## Spiegazione

Con “send something to someone” la preposizione “to” introduce il destinatario.
