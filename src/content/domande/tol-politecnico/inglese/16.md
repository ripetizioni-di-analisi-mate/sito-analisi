**Camera loan**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

You may borrow a camera for up to three days. Memory cards are available separately and must be returned in their cases. Before you leave, take one photograph at the desk so that a member of staff can check that the camera works.

**The borrower tests the camera by taking `___`.**

- all memory cards outside
- a photograph at the desk
- a camera back after one week
- a receipt to the entrance
- a case to the office

L’avviso richiede una fotografia al banco prima di uscire.
