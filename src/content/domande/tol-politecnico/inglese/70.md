**The hall next door**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Our evening class will meet in the hall next door this week. The usual room will be painted, and the new hall has no projector. Please read the two pages sent yesterday so that we can discuss the pictures without showing them on a screen.

**Reading the pages ahead of time helps the class because the temporary hall `___`.**

- will be used only to store screens
- has no doors
- has no projector
- is being painted during the lesson
- is too far away to reach

## Spiegazione

La nuova sala non dispone di un proiettore, perciò gli studenti devono vedere prima le immagini.
