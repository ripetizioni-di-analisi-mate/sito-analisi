**A missed connection**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Maya: I missed the bus after my train. The next one leaves in forty minutes.

Leo: I’m near the station. I can pick you up in fifteen.

Maya: Thanks. I’ll wait by the bakery on the station side of the road. I have a blue bag.

**Leo can arrive `___` before the next bus leaves.**

- forty minutes
- fifteen minutes
- twenty-five minutes
- fifty-five minutes
- five minutes

## Spiegazione

Leo può arrivare tra quindici minuti. L’autobus parte tra quaranta, quindi la differenza è venticinque.
