**Weekend help**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

We need two volunteers to `___` chairs on Friday afternoon and four to welcome guests on Saturday evening. Anyone who helps on Friday can watch Saturday’s performance for free. If you can only come on Saturday, please arrive an hour before the doors open.

- set out
- give up
- turn down
- call off
- put off

## Spiegazione

Qui “set out” significa sistemare le sedie prima dell’evento.
