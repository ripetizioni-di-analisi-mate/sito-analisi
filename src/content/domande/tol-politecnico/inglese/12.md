**Exchange at a shop**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

The jacket fits, but I’d `___` a darker colour. I bought it here last week. I’ve brought the receipt, and the label is still attached. If you don’t have the dark blue one in this size, I’d rather keep this jacket than take a larger one.

- preferred
- prefer
- prefers
- preferring
- to prefer

## Spiegazione

“I’d prefer” significa “I would prefer”. Il modale “would” è seguito dalla forma base del verbo.
