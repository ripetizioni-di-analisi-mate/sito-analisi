**Exchange at a shop**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

The jacket fits, but I’d prefer a darker colour. I bought it here last week. I’ve brought the receipt, and the label is still attached. If you don’t have the dark blue one in this size, I’d `___` keep this jacket than take a larger one.

- rather
- prefer of
- quite to
- quite of
- instead to

## Spiegazione

“Would rather” seguito dalla forma base “keep” indica che il cliente preferisce tenere la giacca.
