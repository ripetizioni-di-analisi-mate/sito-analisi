**Computer room**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

Computers by the windows can be used without booking for twenty minutes at a time. For longer work, book a desk on the second floor. You may save files to your own drive, as the computers remove local files `___` they restart.

- because of
- whenever
- never they

## Spiegazione

“Whenever” indica che l’eliminazione si ripete ogni volta che i computer si riavviano.
