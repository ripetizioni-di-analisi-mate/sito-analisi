**A change of mind**

Complete the text with the word or phrase that fits the blank.

I was going to take the highway, but the traffic report says there’s a long queue near the bridge. The road by the river is longer on the map, `___` it should be quicker this morning. Let’s meet outside the station at the same time as planned.

- although of
- yet
- unless
- so that of
- because of

## Spiegazione

“Yet” segnala il contrasto fra la distanza più lunga e il tempo previsto più breve.
