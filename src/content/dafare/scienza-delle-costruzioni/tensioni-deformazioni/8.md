Una lamina elastica lineare isotropa è in stato piano di tensione. Siano $E=200\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\nu=0{,}25$, $\sigma_x=120\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$ e $\tau_{xy}=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Trovare $\varepsilon_x$, $\varepsilon_y$, $\varepsilon_z$ e $\gamma_{xy}$.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" reazioni="1 0,0.55,0,σ_x;-1 0,-0.55,0;0 1,0,0.55,σ_y;0 -1,0,-0.55" frecce="1.13,-0.65,1.13,0.58,τ_xy;-1.13,0.65,-1.13,-0.58;-0.55,1.13,0.58,1.13;0.55,-1.13,-0.58,-1.13" titolo="Lamina con facce ortogonali a z libere"}

:::soluzione
$\varepsilon_x=525\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_y=150\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_z=-225\cdot10^{-6}$ e $\gamma_{xy}=375\cdot10^{-6}$.
:::
