"Where do you live?" he asked me. Which is the correct reported speech?

- A) He asked me where did I live.
- B) He said me where I lived.
- C) He asked me where do you live.
- D) He asked me where I lived.

:::soluzione
Risposta D: He asked me where I lived.
:::
