Risolvi $\sin(3x)>0$ in $[0,2\pi)$.

:::soluzione
$\left(0, \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) \cup \left(\dfrac{2 \pi}{3}, \pi\right) \cup \left(\dfrac{4 \pi}{3}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{3}\right)$
:::
