Sapendo che $\cos\alpha = \dfrac{7}{9}$ con $\dfrac{3\pi}{2} < \alpha < 2\pi$, calcola $\sin\dfrac{\alpha}{2}$, $\cos\dfrac{\alpha}{2}$ e $\tan\dfrac{\alpha}{2}$.

:::soluzione
$\sin\dfrac{\alpha}{2} = \dfrac{1}{3}$, $\cos\dfrac{\alpha}{2} = -\dfrac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$, $\tan\dfrac{\alpha}{2} = -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4}$.
:::
