Sapendo che $\cos\alpha = -\dfrac{1}{3}$ con $\pi < \alpha < \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$, calcola $\sin 2\alpha$, $\cos 2\alpha$ e $\tan 2\alpha$.

:::soluzione
$\sin 2\alpha = \dfrac{4\sqrt{2}}{9}$, $\cos 2\alpha = -\dfrac{7}{9}$, $\tan 2\alpha = -\dfrac{4\sqrt{2}}{7}$.
:::
