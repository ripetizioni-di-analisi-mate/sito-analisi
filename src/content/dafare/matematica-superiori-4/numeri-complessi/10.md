Dati $z = 6\left( \cos\dfrac{5\pi}{12} + i\sin\dfrac{5\pi}{12} \right)$ e $w = 2\left( \cos\dfrac{\pi}{12} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{12} \right)$, calcola $z w$ e $\dfrac{z}{w}$ in forma algebrica.

:::soluzione
$z w = 12i$ e $\dfrac{z}{w} = \dfrac{3}{2} + \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\,i$.
:::
