Read the note. «The plant grew more slowly in the shade. However, it had the same number of leaves as the plant in sunlight.» What does *however* introduce?

- A definition of sunlight.
- The reason the plant was put in the shade.
- A contrast between growth rate and leaf count.
- A second way to measure growth rate.
- A time limit for the observation.

:::soluzione

**Risposta corretta C.** Il secondo periodo introduce un risultato che può sorprendere alla luce del primo. La crescita era più lenta e il numero di foglie era uguale.

:::
