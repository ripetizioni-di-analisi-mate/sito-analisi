Which question fits the answer «No, the computer room closes at six»?

- Does the computer room closes at seven?
- Does the computer room close at seven?
- Do the computer room close at seven?
- Is the computer room close at seven?
- Does close the computer room at seven?

:::soluzione

**Risposta corretta B.** La domanda chiede di un orario abituale. Con il soggetto singolare si usa *does* prima del soggetto e *close* resta alla forma base.

:::
