Choose the meaning of *borrow* in «Can I borrow your pen for a minute?»

- Keep it without returning it.
- Take it temporarily with permission.
- Write down its name.
- Buy it at a lower price.
- Give it to someone as a gift.

:::soluzione

**Risposta corretta B.** Chi parla chiede di ricevere temporaneamente la penna. *Borrow* si riferisce a chi prende in prestito, mentre *lend* si riferisce a chi presta.

:::
