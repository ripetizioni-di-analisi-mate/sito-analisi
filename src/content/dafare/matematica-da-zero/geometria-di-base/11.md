Esprimi $3{,}2\ \text{m}^2$ in centimetri quadrati e $45\,000\ \text{cm}^2$ in metri quadrati.

:::soluzione
$3{,}2\ \text{m}^2 = 32\,000\ \text{cm}^2$ e $45\,000\ \text{cm}^2 = 4{,}5\ \text{m}^2$.
:::
