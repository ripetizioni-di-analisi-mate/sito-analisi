---

Leggi: «I have lost my key, so I cannot open the door now». Quale informazione comunica la frase?

- The key was lost but has already been found.
- The speaker loses a key every morning.
- The door was opened before the key was lost.
- The missing key will be returned tomorrow.
- The key is missing and this prevents entry now.

:::soluzione

Risposta corretta: E. The key is missing and this prevents entry now.

:::
