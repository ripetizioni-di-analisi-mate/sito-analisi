Nel messaggio «You can borrow books from the library», quale luogo indica *library*?

- A place where people can read and borrow books.
- A shop whose main activity is selling books.
- A private book collection that is closed to all readers.
- A workshop whose only activity is repairing damaged books.
- A company whose main activity is printing books for sale.

:::soluzione

Risposta corretta: A. A place where people can read and borrow books.

:::
