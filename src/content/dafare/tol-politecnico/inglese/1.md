**Shop notice.** “Customers collecting online orders should use the side door. All other customers should enter from Market Street.” Complete the sentence. Someone collecting an online order should use `___`.

:::soluzione

“the side door”. Il primo periodo si applica proprio al ritiro di un ordine online.

:::
