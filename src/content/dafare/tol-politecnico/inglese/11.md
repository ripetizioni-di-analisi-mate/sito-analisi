**At the station.** “Can I help you carry that bag?” “Yes, please. I need to get it upstairs.” Complete the sentence. By saying “Yes, please”, the second person `___` the offer.

:::soluzione

“accepts”. “Yes, please” accetta l’aiuto offerto per portare la borsa al piano di sopra.

:::
