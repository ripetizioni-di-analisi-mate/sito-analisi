Dati $A = \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ e $B = \{2, 3, 5\}$, determinare $A \cup B$, $A \cap B$, $A \setminus B$ e $B \setminus A$.

:::soluzione
$$
A \cup B = \{0, 1, 2, 3, 5\}, \qquad A \cap B = \{2, 3\}, \qquad A \setminus B = \{0, 1\}, \qquad B \setminus A = \{5\}
$$
:::
