Risolvere
$$
\sqrt{2}\, \sin x + 1 = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
x = -\frac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi
\quad \text{oppure} \quad
x = \frac{5\pi}{4} + 2k\pi,
\qquad k \in \mathbb{Z}
$$
:::
