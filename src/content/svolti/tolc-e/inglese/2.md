Complete the sentence. Visitors are not allowed to touch the exhibits. They ___ keep their hands off the objects.

- may not
- must
- must to
- don't have to
- should to

## Risoluzione

**Risposta corretta.** must

Il divieto di toccare gli oggetti equivale all’obbligo di tenere le mani lontane. Dopo *must* si usa la forma base *keep*.
