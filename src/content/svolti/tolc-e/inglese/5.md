Choose the sentence that means the team started in April and still meets regularly.

- The team meets regularly before April.
- The team met regularly until April.
- The team was meeting regularly last April.
- The team has met regularly for April.
- The team has met regularly since April.

## Risoluzione

**Risposta corretta.** The team has met regularly since April.

*Since April* indica il momento iniziale. Il present perfect collega l’attività iniziata ad aprile al presente.
