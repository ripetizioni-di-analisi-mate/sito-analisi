Complete the sentence. The first message ___ before the second one arrived.

- had send
- did sent
- had been sent
- was send
- has been send

## Risoluzione

**Risposta corretta.** had been sent

Il primo messaggio era già stato inviato al momento dell’arrivo del secondo. *Had been sent* è il past perfect passivo e usa il participio *sent*.
