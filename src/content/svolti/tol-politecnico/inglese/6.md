## Esercizio 1

**At a desk.** “Would you mind sending the receipt by email? I need to show it to my manager later.” Complete the sentence. The speaker wants to receive the receipt `___`.

<div class="spiegazione">

“by email”. *Would you mind sending …?* formula una richiesta cortese. La frase sul responsabile spiega perché la ricevuta servirà più tardi.

</div>

## Esercizio 2

**A conversation.** “There is a free table outside.” “Thanks. I would rather `___` wait for one inside. It’s rather cold today.” Choose from “to”, “of”, “with”, “an” and `---`.

<div class="spiegazione">

`---`. Dopo *would rather* si usa direttamente la forma base *wait*. La persona preferisce attendere un tavolo all’interno perché fa freddo.

</div>
