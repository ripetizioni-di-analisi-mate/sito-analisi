## Esercizio 1

**Station notice.** “Platform 3 is closed today. The train to the airport leaves from platform 1. Trains to Green Hill still leave from platform 2.” Complete the sentence. An airport passenger should wait `___`.

<div class="spiegazione">

“on platform 1”. L’avviso distingue la banchina chiusa, quella per l’aeroporto e quella per Green Hill. La seconda frase riguarda direttamente la destinazione chiesta.

</div>

## Esercizio 2

**Community centre.** “The computer room opens at eight. The café opens at nine, but drinks are available from the machine in the hall.” Complete the sentence. At half past eight, someone can get a drink from `___`.

<div class="spiegazione">

“the machine in the hall”. La sala computer è già aperta, mentre il bar apre più tardi. L’ultima frase indica il distributore come alternativa.

</div>
