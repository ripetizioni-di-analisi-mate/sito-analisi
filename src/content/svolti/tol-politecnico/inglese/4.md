## Esercizio 1

**A message.** “I found the forms under the shelf, but the envelope was empty. I left them on your desk.” Complete the sentence. The writer left `___` on the desk.

<div class="spiegazione">

“the forms”. *Them* è plurale e riprende i moduli. *The shelf* e *the envelope* sono singolari e hanno altri ruoli nella frase.

</div>

## Esercizio 2

**A journey.** “The bus stopped earlier than expected because the street was blocked, so we walked the last part.” Complete the sentence. The bus stopped early because `___`.

<div class="spiegazione">

“the street was blocked”. *Because* introduce la ragione della fermata. Il tratto a piedi è la conseguenza introdotta da *so*.

</div>
